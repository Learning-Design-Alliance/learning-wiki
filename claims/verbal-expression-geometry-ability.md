### Wood 1914

Wood, B. D., & Bell, J. C. (1914). Solution of problems in geometry. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Wood+Bell+Solution+of+Problems+in+Geometry

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results discussion of Table I gradings of verbal expression (good group average 92, poor group 42). The authors state the "difference in ability to use good English is far greater" than the imagery difference, and offer a causal interpretation they explicitly label their own opinion.

> "The difference in ability to use good English is far greater, and, in the opinion of the authors, is intimately associated causally, with the lack of stability and lack of clarity in the visual manifestations."
