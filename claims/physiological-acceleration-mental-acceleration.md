# Physiologically accelerated children are mentally accelerated: height correlates with mental age +0.53 for 49 girls with age held constant

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` For 49 girls, partial correlation (with age constant) between height and mental age is +0.53, between height and weight +0.57, and between height and ar-ray +0.52, indicating physiologically accelerated girls are mentally accelerated. [→ Bird T. Baldwin 1922](#bird-t-baldwin-1922)

## Evidence

### Bird T. Baldwin 1922

Bird T. Baldwin. (1922). The Relation Between Mental and Physical Growth. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. XIII, No. 1. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/13/1/193

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Partial-correlation analysis of height, weight, carpal-bone area and Stanford-Binet mental age for 49 girls examined at Iowa City. The printed effect size is r = +0.53 for height and mental age with age held constant (the author reports "For height and mental age the coefficient is +0.53").

> "The coefficient for the group of 49 girls by the method of partial correlation (with age constant) is between height and weight +0.57; between height and ar-ray +0.52. For height and mental age the coefficient is +0.53."
