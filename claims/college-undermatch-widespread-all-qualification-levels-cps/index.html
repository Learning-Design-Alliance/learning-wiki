Roderick 2011¶

Roderick, M., Coca, V., & Nagaoka, J. (2011). Potholes on the Road to College: High School Effects in Shaping Urban Students' Participation in College Application, Four-year College Enrollment, and College Match. Sociology of Education, 84(3), 178-211. https://doi.org/10.1177/0038040711411280

Descriptive cross-tabulation (Table 2, N=4,317) comparing students' college access selectivity (from ACT, GPA, AP/IB) to actual enrollment selectivity using Barron's ratings. Only 38 percent of very-selective-access students enrolled in a very selective college (a match).

"Graduates with access to selective colleges were actually less likely to match or overmatch (27 percent) than graduates with access to very selective colleges."
