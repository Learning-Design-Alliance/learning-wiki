Evidence¶

Vincent Hoogerheide 2016¶

Vincent Hoogerheide, Sofie M. M. Loyens, Tamara van Gog. (2016). Learning from video modeling examples: does gender matter? Instructional Science, 44, 69-86. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-015-9360-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

2x2 ANOVA on judgment of learning and its accuracy in the 2x2 experiment: no significant gender main effects or interaction, and one-sample t-tests showed accuracy "not statistically different from zero" in all four conditions. Equivalence across genders was not tested.

"One sample t-tests showed that for all four combinations of the 2 9 2 design, judgment of learning accuracy was not statistically different from zero, ts [ .10, indicating that male and female students were highly accurate in predicting their performance."
