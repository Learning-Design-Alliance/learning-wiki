Evidence¶

Zhou 2020¶

Zhou, W., Kwok, V. P. Y., Su, M., Luo, J., & Tan, L. H. (2020). Children's neurodevelopment of reading is affected by China's language input system in the information era. npj Science of Learning, 5:3. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0062-0

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Regression analysis with pinyin typing group as covariate alongside sex, age, grade, nonverbal IQ, parental education, head motion, offline reading time, total device time, and character recognition score. The article reports that effects on brain activations, gray matter volume of the left middle frontal gyrus, and functional connectivity strengths all remained significant after this regression.

"After regressing out the effect of these possible confounding variables, we found that the following effects of pinyin typing on neurodevelopment remained signiﬁcant: brain activations in the left middle frontal gyrus"
