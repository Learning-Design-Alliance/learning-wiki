Evidence¶

Dirk Ifenthaler 2020¶

Dirk Ifenthaler, Jane Yin-Kim Yau. (2020). Utilising learning analytics to support study success in higher education: a systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09788-z

q2 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of interventions tabulated in Table 1 of the review, extending a prior review of 11 publications that found visual signals and dashboard features dominant. The review states the key publications "only exhibited a few intervention strategies" and effects may be biased by other variables.

"For example, alerts to teachers enable them to give more individual attention to students (Darlington 2017; Dawson et al. 2017; Gkontzis et al. 2018; Lu et al. 2017; Yang et al. 2017). Other interventions focus on facilitating peer-to-peer communication (Cohen 2017; Seidel and Kutieleh 2017), as well as on recommendations for adaptive learning materials, prior knowledge building, reduction of test anxiety, or student–teacher perceptions."

Related Claims¶

  • SAGLET's real-time learning analytics provided alerts that enabled the teacher to monitor groups and give appropriate feedback — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Real-time AI alerts can expand teachers' situational awareness but in some cases overload teachers' attention or draw focus away from their own observation flow — related
  • Guiding dashboard support enabled teachers to perform more orchestration actions, more targeted interactions, and more class announcements than mirroring support — related
  • Guiding support stimulated targeted interactions: teachers posted more messages to groups (14 vs 4) and performed more dashboard interventions than under mirroring — related
  • Mirroring dashboards improved teacher situational awareness while alerting-and-guiding dashboards reduced teacher workload and frustration — related
  • Teachers used student performance and progress data at individual, class, and school levels, often presented via color-coded dashboard visualizations — related
