Evidence¶

Ajredini 2013¶

Ajredini, F., Izairi, N., Zajkov, O. (2013). Real Experiments versus PhET Simulations for Understanding Electrostatic Charging. European J of Physics Education, 5(1). https://ejpe.phywe.de

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Interpretation of a student's answer to situation d) (Figure 13b), where a carelessly drawn sketch was paired with text explaining that the spheres will decline to equal distances. The authors warn that missing accompanying explanation can mislead the teacher's judgment.

"This case shows that drawing is not enough. The lack of additional information, obtained in a form of text or discussion students-to-teacher, could bring the teacher to a wrong conclusion about the students’ knowledge."
