## Evidence

### De Clercq 2021

De Clercq, Mikaël, Helen Watt, & Paul Richardson. (2021). Profiles of Teachers’ Striving and Wellbeing: Evolution and relations with context factors, retention and professional engagement. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000702

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Longitudinal latent transition profile analysis of 414 Australian secondary and primary teachers followed from early to mid-career; the article reports that "28% of teachers were movers over time". No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "this study used latent transition profile analysis (LTPA) to examine changes in the striving and wellbeing profiles among teachers from their early until mid-career. Five profiles were identified (Sparing, Good health, Ambitious, Burnout and Wornout) among a longitudinal sample of 414 Australian secondary and primary teachers. LTPA showed that 28% of teachers were movers over time."
