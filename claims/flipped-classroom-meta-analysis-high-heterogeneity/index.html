Evidence¶

Zhang 2021¶

Zhang, Q., Cheung, E.S.T., & Cheung, C.S.T. (2021). The impact of flipped classroom on college students’ academic performance: A meta-analysis based on 20 experimental studies. Science Insights Education Frontiers, 8(2):1059-1080. https://doi.org/10.15354/sief.21.re019

q3 · i?

Heterogeneity test of the 20 included studies (Table 1) using the Q and I² statistics per the article's stated thresholds (fixed model if p > 0.10, random model if p ≤ 0.10). The high I² value led the authors to adopt the random-effects model for all pooled analyses.

"The test r e-sults of sample heterogeneity showed that Q = 108.377, p = 0.000 (p ≤ 0.01), I 2 = 75.087% (I 2 > 50%), indicating that there is a large heterogeneity between samples."
