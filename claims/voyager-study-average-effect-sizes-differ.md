## Evidence

### Voyager Universal Literacy System® — WWC Intervention Report 2007

Voyager Universal Literacy System® — WWC Intervention Report. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/538

`q2 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

WWC-computed study-level averages from Appendix A3.1: effect size 0.45 (8 schools, 398 students) versus effect size 0.10 (4 schools, 213 students), both not statistically significant. Findings were inconsistent across the two studies.

> "Across all constructs in the alphabetics domain, the average effect size in Frechtling, Zhang, & Silverstein (2006) was positive and large enough to be considered substantively important according to the WWC criteria (that is, at least 0.25). The average effect size for Hecht (2003) was positive, but not large enough to be considered substantively important."
