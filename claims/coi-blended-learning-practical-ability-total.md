## Evidence

### Liu Weichu 2021

Liu Weichu, Wang Jun, Zhang Han, Yu Changfeng, Liu Shuai, Zhang Cen, Yu Jingya, Liu Qiao, Yang Bing. (2021). Determining the effects of blended learning using the community of inquiry on nursing students' learning gains in sudden patient deterioration module. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.914

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t test on the NCEE checklist total in the two-group quasi-experimental trial, with printed means of 64.48 versus 60.53 and Cohen d = 0.53 favouring the CoI-based blended learning group.

> "The total score of practical ability in the CoI‐based blended learning group was 64.48 (SD = 7.44), while that in the control group was 60.53 (SD = 9.06), and the difference was statistically significant between two groups (t = 3.64, p < .001, Cohen d = 0.53)."
