## Evidence

### Leanne Doughty and Marcos D. Caballero 2014

Leanne Doughty and Marcos D. Caballero. (2014). Rubric Design for Separating the Roles of Open-Ended Assessments. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.014](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.014)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of student written answers to the CCMI differential-equation question, collected in situ during CCMI validation at three universities. The authors infer from answer categories that some students represent force descriptions mathematically but cannot translate them to a differential equation.

> "Although students who do not write a second order differential equation receive a zero score for their answers, some had the ability to represent the force descriptions in a mathematical expression(s), but could not translate that to a differential equation correctly."
