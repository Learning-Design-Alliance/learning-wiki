## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The largest self-concept/self-attribution correlations occur between measures specific to the same academic subject. [→ Marsh 1983](#marsh-1983)

## Evidence

### Marsh 1983

Marsh, Herbert W. (1983). Relationships Among Dimensions of Self-attribution, Dimensions of Self-concept and Academic Achievements. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED243914

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Table 3) in the 559-student sample. These content-matched correlations were "substantially larger than the other coefficients", supporting content specificity of both constructs; the content specificity of attributions was clear for ability attributions but not for effort and external causes.

> "The largest correlations between the self-concept and self - attribution scores, also as predicted, occur betWeen the two reading specific scores and between the two math specific scores."
