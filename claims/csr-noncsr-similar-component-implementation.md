# CSR and non-CSR schools implemented a similar number of legislatively specified components in both 2003 and 2007

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` By 2007, CSR schools implemented fewer than five components on average, with no significant differences from non-CSR schools in the number of components implemented at either elementary or middle school levels. [→ Orland 2010](#orland-2010)

## Evidence

### Orland 2010

Orland, M., Hoffman, A., & Vaughn, III, E. S. (2010). Evaluation of the Comprehensive School Reform Program Implementation and Outcomes: Fifth-Year Report. U.S. Department of Education. https://www.ed.gov/about/offices/list/opepd/ppss/reports.html#title

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey-based descriptive analysis of principals and teachers in 436 CSR and 382 matched non-CSR schools. The report finds that "by 2007, CSR schools, on average, implemented fewer than five components five years after receiving their CSR awards." Both school types increased components from 2003 to 2007.
