### Lerch S.P. 2025

Lerch S.P., Huwendiek S., Nendaz M., Klöppel S., Pinilla S. (2025). Perceptions of Ad Hoc Entrustment, the Need for Supervision, and Coping Strategies in Clinical Residents: A Qualitative Study. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006019

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semistructured interviews with 12 Swiss geriatric psychiatry residents (interviews January–March 2022) identified this as a main theme. Residents reported that "entrustment decisions in the workplace as a default, often covert process" shaped daily work, with some tasks delegated by default at rotation start without assessment.

> "Residents described entrustment decisions in the workplace as a default, often covert process and as an explicit, ad hoc delegation of clinical tasks (e.g., during ward rounds)."
