### Whiting Penny F 2015

Whiting Penny F, Davenport Clare, Jameson Catherine, Burke Margaret, Sterne Jonathan A C, Hyde Chris, Ben-Shlomo Yoav. (2015). How well do health professionals interpret diagnostic information? A systematic review. BMJ Open. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4521525/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review narrative synthesis of six single-group studies assessing accuracy-definition ability. The review reports that health professionals were less likely to identify the correct definition of likelihood ratios than of sensitivity and specificity, with proportions correctly identifying sensitivity ranging from 76% to 88% and specificity from 80% to 88%.

> "6 assessed ability to define accuracy metrics: health professionals were less likely to identify the correct definition of likelihood ratios than of sensitivity and specificity."
