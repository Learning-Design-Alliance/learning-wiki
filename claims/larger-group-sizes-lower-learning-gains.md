## Evidence

### Tan Kate M 2015

Tan Kate M, Flack Felicity S, Bear Natasha L, Allen Judy A. (2015). An evaluation of a data linkage training workshop for research ethics committees. BMC Medical Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12910-015-0007-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-hoc comparison across workshops: the second NSW workshop, which had more participants (27 registered) and a wider range of prior knowledge, showed the decreases. The authors also report that "Participants who attended trainings of larger group sizes reported lower mean differences than those who were in smaller groups."

> "Results from one of the training workshops showed a substantial number of participants reporting a decrease in knowledge/understanding (29%) in at least one of the eight impact statements following the training."
