## Subclaims
`q2 i2` The OIF group produced significantly fewer errors per T-Unit than the EF group in revised compositions, with partial eta squared = .63. [→ Akbarzadeh 2014](#akbarzadeh-2014)

## Evidence

### Akbarzadeh 2014

Akbarzadeh, R., Saeidi, M., & Chehreh, M. (2014). The effect of oral interactive feedback on the accuracy and complexity of EFL learners’ writing performance: Uptake and retention. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 2(2), 105-126. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on revised compositions (uptake) with pre-test as covariate, comparing the OIF group (M = .26) and EF group (M = 1.08) errors per T-Unit; "F = 80.67, p <.05". The article reports the effect size as .63 (partial eta squared).

> "The results of the comparison of the revised compositions based on ANCOVA statistical test indicated that there was a significant difference between the scores in the OIF group (M = .26, SD = .06) and EF group (M = 1.08, SD = .06), F = 80.67, p <.05."
