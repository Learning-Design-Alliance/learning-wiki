## Evidence

### Tanmay Sinha and Manu Kapur 2021

Tanmay Sinha and Manu Kapur. (2021). When Problem Solving Followed by Instruction Works: Evidence for Productive Failure. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211019105

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression within the three-level meta-analytic model on 166 comparisons, using a synthesized PF fidelity score (0–7 criteria as a percentage). The score "was a significant predictor of the effect size (β = 0.0065, p < .001, 95% CI [0.0044, 0.0087])"; the β is unstandardized, so no standardized effect size is reported.

> "The overall PF fidelity score (computed as a percentage) was a significant predictor of the effect size (β = 0.0065, p < .001, 95% CI [0.0044, 0.0087])."
