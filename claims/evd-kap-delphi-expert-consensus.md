## Evidence

### Chen Xiaohan 2025

Chen Xiaohan, Chi Yunbo, Tian Yangyang, Bi Yuange, Yu Hongyuan, Zhang Jing, Zhou Jing. (2025). Construction of a Questionnaire on Knowledge, Attitude, and Practices in Nursing Care of Adults With External Ventricular Drainage: Reliability and Validity Assessment. Journal of Nursing Care Quality. https://doi.org/10.1097/NCQ.0000000000000873

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Delphi expert consultation with 15 experts (14 nursing, 1 medical) from 8 provinces over two rounds. The article reports authority coefficients "were 0.92 and 0.93, respectively", and Kendall's W of 0.249 and 0.252, both P < .001.

> "During the 2 rounds of correspondence, the familiarity levels (Cs) were 0.88 and 0.91, the coefficients of judgment basis (Ca) were 0.95 and 0.96, and the authority coefficients (Cr) were 0.92 and 0.93, respectively."
