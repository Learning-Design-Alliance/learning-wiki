## Evidence

### Jorm Anthony F 2010

Jorm Anthony F, Kitchener Betty A, Sawyer Michael G, Scales Helen, Cvetkovski Stefan. (2010). Mental health first aid training for high school teachers: a cluster randomized trial. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-244X-10-51

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized trial teacher stigma items analyzed individually because principal components could not be replicated. Odds ratios printed for two personal stigma items; no standardized effect size printed.

> "Trained teachers were less likely than untrained ones to see depression as due to personal weakness (OR = 3.07, P = 0.024 at post-test and OR = 2.47, P = 0.077 at follow-up) and they were also less likely to be reluctant to disclose depression to others (OR = 3.79, P = 0.012 at post-test and OR = 3.42, P = 0.029 at follow-up)."
