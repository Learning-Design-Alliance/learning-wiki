### Chen 2025

Chen, Z.; Singh, C. (2025). Opportunities and Challenges in Harnessing Digital Technology for Effective Teaching and Learning. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.3390/higheredu4010006](https://doi.org/10.3390/higheredu4010006)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

Interviews with 37 students during fall 2020 COVID-19 online instruction. Students reported that 'the most important factors that were tied to student satisfaction and performance were those related to human connections with instructors and peers.' Short instructor check-ins at the start of class had an outsized impact on morale; the social aspects 'reduced procrastination, improved time- management and self-regulation, and increased student attention and engagement'.

> "the most important factors that were tied to student satisfaction and performance were those related to human connections with instructors and peers."
