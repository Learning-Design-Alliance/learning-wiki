Interactive Associations between Physical Activity and Sleep Duration in Relation to Adolescent Academic Achievement. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315604

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The interaction model on the YRBSS sample tested both main effects and the omnibus interaction; the interaction was significant at "F(9,22) = 2.56, p = 0.042". Figure 3 depicts the sleep–achievement relationship by level of physical activity engagement.

> "Results from our physical activity by sleep ANOVA model revealed significant main effects of sleep (F(3,34) = 20.81, p < 0.001) and physical activity (F(3,31) = 3.53, p < 0.001) as well as a significant sleep by physical activity interaction (F(9,22) = 2.56, p = 0.042)."
