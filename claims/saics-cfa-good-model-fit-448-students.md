## Evidence

### Thomas K. F. Chiu 2025

Thomas K. F. Chiu, Murat Çoban, Ismaila Temitayo Sanusi, Musa Adekunle Ayanwale. (2025). Validating student AI competency self-efficacy (SAICS) scale and its framework. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10512-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Confirmatory factor analysis of the 32-item SAICS scale with 448 students aged 12–17 from six Hong Kong secondary schools, assessed on a 5-point Likert scale. The article reports "a good model fit" with the printed indices and concludes the measurement model "has high validity and reliability scores (RQ2)."

> "the fitness indices of the measured items revealed a good model fit regarding the measurement model's goodness-of-fit: x2/df = 2.39 (< 5.0); RMSEA = 0.056 (< 0.08); SRMR = 0.03 (< 0.05); PNFI = 0.81 (> 0.50); CFI = 0.95 (> 0.90)"
