## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Detlev Leutner · Johanna Biele 2025

Detlev Leutner · Johanna Biele. (2025). Without Integration, Everything Is Nothing: A Meta-Analysis of the Effectiveness of Instructional Support for Drawing-to-Learn. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10067-7

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis in the random-effects meta-analysis of comprehension outcomes. Facilitation of integration was a "significant moderator", Q(1) = 7.90, p = 0.005; supported drawing expected to facilitate integration yielded g = 0.35, p = 0.048, versus g = − 0.15, p = 0.186, without facilitation.

> "turned out to be a significant moderator of the effect of drawing support on students' comprehension, Q(1) = 7.90, p = 0.005, with an aggregated effect size of g = 0.35, p = 0.048, for facilitation, and of g = − 0.15, p = 0.186, for no facilitation of integration"
