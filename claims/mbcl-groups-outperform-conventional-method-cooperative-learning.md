## Evidence

### Zhou C 2021

Zhou C, Lewis M. (2021). A mobile technology‐based cooperative learning platform for undergraduate biology courses in common college classrooms. Biochem Mol Biol Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.21496

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A fall 2019 comparison experiment in General Biology I Lecture and Genetics: two in-class activities per class, half the groups using each method, methods crossed across activities. Figure 6c reports statistically higher average earned points for MBCL groups (*P < 0.05; **P < 0.01); no effect size is printed.

> "Thus, the data indicate that when compared to the traditional method of student‐instructor communication during in‐class group studies, the MBCL platform can yield better students' learning gains in cooperative learning activities."
