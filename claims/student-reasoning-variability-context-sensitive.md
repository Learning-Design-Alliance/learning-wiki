### Ying Cao and Bárbara M. Brizuela 2016

Ying Cao and Bárbara M. Brizuela. (2016). High school students' representations and understandings of electric fields. Physical Review Physics Education Research 12, 020102. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.12.020102

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Case study analysis showed Xinmiao's representation of multi-charge interaction changed when task framing shifted from filling four frames to showing simultaneous forces, reflecting contextual sensitivity of student ideas.

> "This clip of the interview showed that Xinmiao's representation of the interaction between multiple charges was affected by multiple local factors: his understanding of the interaction, his understanding of the task requirement, and the interviewer's request he received."
