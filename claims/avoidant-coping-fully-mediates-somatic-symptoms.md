# Avoidant coping fully mediates the relationship between resilience resources and somatic health symptoms

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Resilience resources had a significant indirect effect on somatic health symptoms (β = -.13, p < .001) with no significant direct effect, indicating full mediation by avoidant coping. [→ Fullerton Dayna J. 2021](#fullerton-dayna-j-2021)
`q3 i?` Avoidant coping directly predicted greater somatic health symptoms (β = .29, p < .001). [→ Fullerton Dayna J. 2021 (2)](#fullerton-dayna-j-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Fullerton Dayna J. 2021

Fullerton Dayna J., Zhang Lisa M., Kleitman Sabina. (2021). An integrative process model of resilience in an academic context: Resilience resources, coping strategies, and positive adaptation. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0246000. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246000

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Indirect-effects test in the path analysis of 306 undergraduates; the article reports "As resilience resources did not have a significant direct effect on somatic health symptoms, this indicates that the relationship was fully mediated by avoidant coping", with printed indirect effect β = -.13, p < .001.

> "As resilience resources did not have a significant direct effect on somatic health symptoms, this indicates that the relationship was fully mediated by avoidant coping, the only coping factor which predicted this outcome."

### Fullerton Dayna J. 2021 (2)

Fullerton Dayna J., Zhang Lisa M., Kleitman Sabina. (2021). An integrative process model of resilience in an academic context: Resilience resources, coping strategies, and positive adaptation. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0246000. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246000

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct-effect path in the same model; the article reports "Avoidant coping moderately predicted greater somatic health symptoms (β = .29, p < .001)". Somatic symptoms were self-reported, so the article interprets this as tendency to report complaints rather than objective symptoms.

> "Avoidant coping moderately predicted greater somatic health symptoms (β = .29, p < .001)."
