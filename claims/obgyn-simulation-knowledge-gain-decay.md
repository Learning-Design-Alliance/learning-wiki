## Evidence

### Ogunyemi D 2020

Ogunyemi D, Haltigin C, Vallie S, Ferrari TM, Hayashi S. (2020). Evolution of an obstetrics and gynecology interprofessional simulation-based education session for medical and nursing students. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000022562

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre- and immediate-post survey analysis of the 2014 obstetrics simulation cohort in the Reproductive Sciences Course, with a 4-month follow-up showing a score of 20 (7.46). The article reports the "post-simulation mean score increasing to 29.57 (5.15), P<.001".

> "For the knowledge questions on obstetrics and FHR, students obtained a mean pre-score for correct answers of 12.82 (SD = 6.02), with post-simulation mean score increasing to 29.57 (5.15), P<.001."

### Ogunyemi D 2020 (2)

Ogunyemi D, Haltigin C, Vallie S, Ferrari TM, Hayashi S. (2020). Evolution of an obstetrics and gynecology interprofessional simulation-based education session for medical and nursing students. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000022562

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

2015 cohort of MS2 students completing knowledge surveys pre, post, and 8 months after the interprofessional simulation; the article states the 8-month score was "not significantly different from baseline".

> "By 8 months it was not significantly different from baseline and was significantly lower than the immediate post simulation scores."
