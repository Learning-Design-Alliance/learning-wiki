## Evidence

### Desimone and Hill 2017

Laura M. Desimone, Kirsten Lee Hill. (2017). Inside the Black Box: Examining Mediators and Moderators of a Middle School Science Intervention. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373717697842

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

SEM results: contrary to expectations, CS participation was negatively associated with teacher content knowledge (a 0.25 SD difference, p = .04; no standardized effect size printed). The article offers possible explanations including control-condition content-intensive PD or "unhappy random assignment," untestable because no content knowledge pretest was administered.

> "teachers in the CS condition had levels of content knowledge that are 0.25 standard deviations lower than teachers in the control condition (p  = .04)"

### Desimone and Hill 2017 (2)

Laura M. Desimone, Kirsten Lee Hill. (2017). Inside the Black Box: Examining Mediators and Moderators of a Middle School Science Intervention. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373717697842

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Same SEM analysis of 10,281 students and 124 teachers: the printed standardized coefficient of 0.08 (p = .001) is below the small-effect threshold, so impact is coded 0. Table 3 reports the indirect effect of content knowledge as −0.018*.

> "content knowledge is positively related to student achievement, such that for every one standard deviation increase in content knowledge, there is a corresponding 0.08 standard deviation increase in student achievement (p = .001)"
