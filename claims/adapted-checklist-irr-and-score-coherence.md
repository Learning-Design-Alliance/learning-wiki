Reliability analysis within the same RCT: two trained raters scored each session live after three calibration sessions; scores were averaged across raters. The correlation r = 0.855 is a printed effect-size-like coefficient indicating strong coherence.

> "IRR was good, with an ICC of 0.84 for the objective score and 0.76 for the subjective score. Additionally, the correlation between the 2 types of scores was 0.855 (95% CI 0.745‐0.920), indicating good internal coherence between checklist-based performance and global assessments."
