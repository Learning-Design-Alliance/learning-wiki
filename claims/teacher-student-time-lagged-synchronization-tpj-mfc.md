## Evidence

### X. Feng 2025

X. Feng, X. Xu, Z. Meng, J. Jiang, M. Pei, Y. Zheng, C. Lu. (2025). A Rapid Cortical Learning Process Supporting Students’ Knowledge Construction During Real Classroom Teaching. Advanced Science. https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202416610

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Time-lagged teacher–student neural synchronization analysis (lags 6–14 s, step 2 s) with FDR correction across channel pairs during the LH lecturing phase. The reported result is the Figure 8B caption pattern; no effect size is printed.

> "the teacher's brain activity in the right TPJ (CH17 and 19) positively correlated with the student's brain activity in the MFC (CH 8 and CH 5) when the teacher's brain activity preceded that of the student by 8–10 s, with a peak correlation at a time lag of 8 s."
