Evaluating 3D-printed models of coronary anomalies: a survey among clinicians and researchers at a university hospital in the UK. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025227

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feasibility evaluation of 3D printing coronary artery anomalies from cardiac CT data at a UK university hospital. Eight cases were segmented, reconstructed, and printed in-house using a Formlabs Form 2 SLA printer with white resin. The article reports that "All models were reconstructed and printed successfully" with accurate anatomical detail.

> "All models were reconstructed and printed successfully, with accurate details showing coronary anatomy (eg, anomalous coronary artery, coronary roofing or coronary aneurysm in a patient with Kawasaki syndrome)."
