## Evidence

### Tanner 2025

Tanner, S., Krausen, K., Garcia, B., & Brown, A. (2025). Understanding the At-risk indicator: Nevada's Pupil-Centered Funding Plan. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677573

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-year statewide stability analysis of all Nevada students, comparing GRAD-score identification with a hypothetical direct-certification measure. "a 12 percent loss under direct certification compared to 27 percent using the GRAD score and 9 percent gain under direct certification compared to 30 percent using the GRAD score." Total net change was –3.6 percent under GRAD and –4.0 percent under direct certification; mobility and program eligibility shifts also contributed.

> "a 12 percent loss under direct certification compared to 27 percent using the GRAD score and 9 percent gain under direct certification compared to 30 percent using the GRAD score."
