## Evidence

### Kashiwabara Tomoka 2022

Kashiwabara Tomoka, Fujiwara Takeo, Doi Satomi, Yamaoka Yui. (2022). Association between Hope for the Future and Academic Performance in Adolescents: Results from the K-CHILD Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191911890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Conditional logistic regression on PS-matched K-CHILD pairs for dichotomized study time. Note the article's Table 4 and abstract assign OR = 1.89 (95% CI 1.37 to 2.61) to studying except in class and OR = 1.45 (95% CI 1.19 to 1.75) to reading books; the Results text sentence swaps the two ORs, an internal inconsistency reported here as printed.

> "Having hope for the future was also associated with studying except in class (OR = 1.45, 95% CI = 1.19 to 1.75) and reading books (OR = 1.89, 95% CI = 1.37 to 2.61)."
