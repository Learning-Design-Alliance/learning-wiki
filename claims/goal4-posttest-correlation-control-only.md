### Adelson de Araujo 2025

Adelson de Araujo, Pantelis M. Papadopoulos, Susan McKenney, Ton de Jong. (2025). Investigating the Impact of a Collaborative Conversational Agent on Dialogue Productivity and Knowledge Acquisition. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00469-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Correlation analysis of the four FGPD counts against average post-test scores per student; in the aggregate data Goal 4 correlated with acquisition (ρ = .230, p = .016) while other goals showed |ρ| < 0.1; per condition the correlation held "only in the control condition" (ρ = 0.414, p =.002).

> "When checking per condition, Goal 4 appears to be significantly positively correlated to knowledge acquisition, but only in the control condition (ρ = 0.414, p =.002)."
