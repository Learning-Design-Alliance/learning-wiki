# A multicultural education programme showed no significant effect on high school students' attitude towards national integration

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After a multicultural education treatment, the experimental group's attitude towards national integration did not differ significantly from the control group's at the 0.01 level. [→ Perveen 2014](#perveen-2014)

## Evidence

### Perveen 2014

Shaheen Perveen. (2014). Study of the Effectiveness of Multi-cultural Education on the Attitude Towards National Integration of High School Students. li-manager's Journal of Psychology, Educational, Vol. 8 No. 2, August-October 2014. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Study+of+the+Effectiveness+of+Multi-cultural+Education+on+the+Attitude+towards+National+Integration%22+Perveen

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled-group pre/post comparison of 200 high school students in Marathwada, using the researcher's Attitude toward National Integration Scale and t-test. The article reports the t-value was "less than 1.96" and insignificant at the 0.01 level; table means were 67.38 (control) and 68.91 (experimental).

> "The obtained t - value is less than 1.96 for attitude towards national integration and hence is insignificant at 0.01 level. Hence the null hypothesis is accepted."
