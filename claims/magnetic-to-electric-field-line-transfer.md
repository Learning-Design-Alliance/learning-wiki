`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Mandi used her middle school knowledge of magnetic field lines to reason that electric field lines should start at positive charges and end at negative charges, producing curved lines resembling canonical field lines.

> "Because magnetic field lines start at the North pole and end at the South pole, electric field lines should start from the positive charge and end at the negative charge."
