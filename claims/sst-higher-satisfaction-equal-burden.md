## Evidence

### Meng Jing 2025

Meng Jing, Wang Li, Jin Danni, Li Zihan, Tao Qingqing, Ying Lan. (2025). Situational simulation teaching can effectively enhance the clinical thinking ability of residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08248-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-aspect satisfaction questionnaire (5-point scale) administered in the same RCT (Table 9). Teaching methods scored 4.81 ± 0.40 versus 4.25 ± 0.73 (t = 5.095, P < 0.001); no effect sizes are printed.

> "Residents in the intervention group reported significantly higher satisfaction with the course content, course resources, classroom interaction, and teaching methods compared to the control group (P < 0.05)."

### Meng Jing 2025 (2)

Meng Jing, Wang Li, Jin Danni, Li Zihan, Tao Qingqing, Ying Lan. (2025). Situational simulation teaching can effectively enhance the clinical thinking ability of residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08248-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result from the same satisfaction survey: learning burden ratings were 4.16 ± 0.77 (intervention) versus 4.33 ± 0.64 (control), t = 1.320, P = 0.189. No effect size is printed, so equivalence was not established.

> "There was no statistically significant difference in the learning burden between the two groups (P > 0.05), as shown in Table 9."
