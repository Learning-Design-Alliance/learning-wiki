## Evidence

### Şengör K 2025

Şengör K, Alptekin HM, Kara Ö, Görücü R, Ayoğlu T, Akyüz N. (2025). Assessment tool for surgical hand washing skills: a scale development study. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-03192-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Test-retest reliability analysis with 30 participants re-assessed after a 15-day interval, evaluated with ICC, Pearson correlation, and independent samples t-tests. The article reports combined-total ICC of 0.900 and Pearson r of 0.951 (all p < 0.001), and no statistically significant mean difference between test and retest (p > 0.05); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The ICC values for the scores obtained from Observer 1, Observer 2, and the combined total were 0.906, 0.847, and 0.900, respectively. Corresponding Pearson correlation coefficients were 0.901, 0.875, and 0.951 (all p < 0.001)"
