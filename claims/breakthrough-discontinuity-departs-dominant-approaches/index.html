Conceptual field note defining the discontinuity factor, drawing on research on technological discontinuities and architectural innovation. The article states the factor "Introduces new models that depart from dominant approaches and challenge existing constraints." Kindergarten is given as the example of a play-based, developmentally informed departure from formal instruction or no schooling.
