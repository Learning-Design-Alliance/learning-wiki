# In a randomized trial of physiotherapy students, AI-supported PBL produced greater two-week knowledge retention than traditional PBL, with a very large within-group effect from post-test to follow-up (Cohen's d = 3.14)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Physiotherapy students in the AI-PBL group showed a very large continued improvement in theoretical knowledge scores from immediate post-test to the two-week retention test (d = 3.14), while the traditional PBL group's consolidation gain was smaller (d = 0.77). [→ Ergezen Sahin Gizem 2025](#ergezen-sahin-gizem-2025)
`q2 i2` Between groups, the AI-PBL group outperformed the traditional PBL group at each time point; the between-group difference was small at post-test (Cohen's d ≈ 0.54, p = 0.031) and became large at two-week follow-up (Cohen's d > 1.70). [→ Ergezen Sahin Gizem 2025 (2)](#ergezen-sahin-gizem-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Ergezen Sahin Gizem 2025

Ergezen Sahin Gizem, Aras Bayram Gulay, Sanchez Sierra Alberto, Akdemir Simay, Kurc Dogukan, Tarakci Devrim, Tunali Ayse Nur. (2025). Effects of artificial intelligence based physiotherapy educational approach in developing clinical reasoning skills: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07926-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within-group analyses from the two-arm randomized trial (AI-PBLG N = 19, PBLG N = 16 analyzed). The AI-PBLG's "very large effect" from post-test to follow-up (ΔM = 17.6, d = 3.14) contrasted with the PBLG's smaller consolidation gain of d = 0.77 reported in the same table.

> "A very large effect was also observed between post and 2-week follow-up (ΔM = 17.6, d = 3.14). PBLG also showed significant gains between pre and post (ΔM = 13.8, p < 0.001, d = 1.73)"

### Ergezen Sahin Gizem 2025 (2)

Ergezen Sahin Gizem, Aras Bayram Gulay, Sanchez Sierra Alberto, Akdemir Simay, Kurc Dogukan, Tarakci Devrim, Tunali Ayse Nur. (2025). Effects of artificial intelligence based physiotherapy educational approach in developing clinical reasoning skills: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07926-w

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparisons of the 20-item theoretical knowledge exam at three time points in the same randomized trial; the difference was "small at post-test (Cohen’s d ≈ 0.54, p = 0.031)" and grew to over 1.70 at two-week follow-up.

> "Between-group comparisons showed that the AI-PBL group (N = 19) consistently outperformed the traditional PBL group (N = 16) at each time point (PT1−2, PT1−3,PT2−3). The between-group difference was small at post-test (Cohen’s d ≈ 0.54, p = 0.031) but became large at 2-week follow-up (Cohen’s d > 1.70)."
