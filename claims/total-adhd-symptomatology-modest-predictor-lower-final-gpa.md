## Evidence

### Henning Colin 2021

Henning Colin, Summerfeldt Laura J., Parker James D. A. (2021). ADHD and Academic Success in University Students: The Important Role of Impaired Attention. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547211036758

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation models using a single latent ADHD variable from CAARS scores predicted official final GPAs of degree completers, fit separately by gender with ADFG estimation; the article reports good fit (e.g., men MFI = 0.99, SRMR = 0.04). Parameters ranged from −0.14 to −0.21.

> "As is evident from Figure 1a, results of the models indicated total ADHD symptomatology was a significant, modest predictor (parameter ranging from −0.14 to −0.21) of lower final GPAs."
