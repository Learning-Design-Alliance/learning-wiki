### He 2022

He, W. (2022). MAP Growth item parameter drift study. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-item-parameter-drift-study/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Conclusion drawn in the report's Conclusion and Discussion section from the full sequence of analyses: re-estimation showing negligible average difficulty change, Robust Z flagging, and rescoring showing negligible average score impact. The report adds that both old and new calibration procedures yield comparable parameter estimates.

> "Nevertheless, both the item-level and test-level analysis results indicate that the MAP Growth measurement scales are remaining stable. This conclusion is consistent with what past studies (e.g., Kingsbury, 2003) have found."
