Evidence¶

Victoria Menzies 2015¶

Victoria Menzies, Clare Collyer, Dimitra Kokotsaki, Catherine Hewitt, Kirsty Younger, Andy Wiggins and Carole Torgerson. (2015). SHINE in Secondaries: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581429

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pilot quasi-experiment using a two-cut-point regression discontinuity design with tie-breaker randomisation in four schools (613 pupils pre-tested). The report states the evaluation showed "a very small impact on literacy outcomes across all four schools" on Progress in English and Progress in Maths tests; no effect size value is printed in the supplied text.
