Varpula Jaakko 2026¶

Varpula Jaakko, Askola Riitta, Kahsay Desale Tewelde, Axelin Anna, Virtanen Laura, Maijala Riikka, Peltonen Laura‐Maria. (2026). Enhancing Nurse Leaders Evidence‐Based Practice Implementation Leadership Competence. A Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis of Educational Interventions. Worldviews on Evidence‐Based Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/wvn.70164

q2 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r1

Meta-analysis of two studies measuring implementation climate with the EBP Work Environment Scale and Implementation Climate Scale, pooled between baseline and post-intervention. The review reports "a SMD of 1.60 (95% CI: 1.06, 2.15; p < 0.0001)" favoring the intervention.

"Similarly, two studies evaluated the effect of the intervention on implementation climate, which demonstrated significant improvement with a SMD of 1.60 (95% CI: 1.06, 2.15; p < 0.0001), favoring the intervention."
