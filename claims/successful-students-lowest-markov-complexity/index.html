Evidence¶

Jakub Kuzilek 2023¶

Jakub Kuzilek, Zdenek Zdrahal, Jonas Vaclavek, Viktor Fuglik, Jan Skocilas, Annika Wolff. (2023). First-Year Engineering Students' Strategies for Taking Exams. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00303-4

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational analysis of exam-attempt logs of 311 students, with Markov chain statistics per performance group reported in Table 5: nodes 48/84/114, edges 83/134/206, complexity "0.1844 0.2896 0.7474" for Successful, Passing and Failing groups respectively.

"We can observe that the number of nodes is the lowest for the students in the Successful group and the highest in the performance group of Failing students. The same observation can be made for the graph edges and the complexity"
