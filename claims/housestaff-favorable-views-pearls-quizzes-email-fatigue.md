## Evidence

### Matos Jason 2017

Matos Jason, Petri Camille R., Mukamal Kenneth J., Vanka Anita. (2017). Spaced education to improve competency in medical knowledge. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0181418

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-study Likert-scale and free-text survey of housestaff from the study period; 27 respondents. "73% of responders felt the addition of the monthly Clinical Pearls helped them learn", but many cited email burden and wanted immediate feedback.

> "A total of 27 housestaff responded to the post-study survey (S1 Fig). Of these, 73% of responders felt the addition of the monthly Clinical Pearls helped them learn. However, 56% wished for immediate feedback to question responses and 48% felt that the reinforcement program with reminder messages involved too many emails."
