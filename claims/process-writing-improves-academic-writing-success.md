# The process writing approach significantly improved academic writing success among first-year preschool teaching students relative to traditional instruction

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taught with the process writing approach scored significantly higher on posttest academic essays than control-group students taught traditionally, after controlling for pretest scores. [→ Bayat 2014](#bayat-2014)

## Evidence

### Bayat 2014

Nihat BAYAT. (2014). The Effect of the Process Writing Approach on Writing Success and Anxiety. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 14(3). https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2014.3.1720

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A pretest-posttest control group quasi-experiment with 74 first-year preschool teaching students; the experimental group (n=38) used process writing for 10 weeks while the control group (n=36) used the traditional method. ANCOVA on essays scored by two experts favored the experimental group, "F(1,73)=5,818; p=.018".

> "The analysis showed a statistically significant difference between the experimental group and control group participants' mean academic writing scores on the posttest adjusted according to pretest academic writing scores (F(1,73)=5,818; p=.018)."
