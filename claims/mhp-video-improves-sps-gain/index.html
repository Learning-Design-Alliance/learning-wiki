Evidence¶

Wilujeng 2020¶

Wilujeng, I., Suryadarma, I. G. P., Ertika, & Dwandaru, W. S. B. (2020). Local Potential Integrated Science Video to Improve SPS and Concept Mastery. International Journal of Instruction, 13(4), 197-214. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13413a

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent control group study with 28 students per class; SPS measured by non-test observation of observing and communicating skills. The experimental class posttest average rose from 58.57 to 75.96, and "the g scores of the experimental and control groups are 0.42 and 0.22".

"Moreover, the g scores of the experimental and control groups are 0.42 and 0.22, which are categorized as medium and low, respectively. The improvement of students’ SPS in the experimental group is higher than the control group."
