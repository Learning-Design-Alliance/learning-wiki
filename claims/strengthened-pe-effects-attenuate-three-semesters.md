## Evidence

### Zhang Yunting 2019

Zhang Yunting, Ma Xiaochen, Zhao Jin, Shen Hong, Jiang Fan. (2019). The Effect of Strengthened Physical Education on Academic Achievements in High School Students: A Quasi-Experiment in China. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16234688

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Three-semester follow-up difference-in-difference analysis in the fully adjusted model on the same cohort. The article reports the Chinese gain "persisted, although with a mitigated effect size" of 0.27 SD, English turning negative at −0.23 SD, and math still negative at −0.18 SD (p = 0.04).

> "In the fully adjusted model, the positive association between strengthened PE session and Chinese scores persisted, although with a mitigated effect size of (0.27 SD, 95% CI: 0.06 to 0.48, p = 0.01). The positive association found in English scores became negative (−0.23 SD, 95% CI: −0.38 to −0.09.47, p = 0.00)."
