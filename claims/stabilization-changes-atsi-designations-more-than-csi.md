# Stabilization changed ATSI designations more than CSI designations, with the median lowest-performing subgroup larger under stabilized indicators

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 164 schools designated for ATSI using either unstabilized or stabilized test-based indicators, 95 (57 percent) were designated under both; 42 of 137 schools designated using unstabilized data (31 percent) would not be designated after stabilization, and 27 schools would be newly designated. [→ Rosendahl 2024](#rosendahl-2024)
`q2 i?` The median size of the lowest performing subgroup in ATSI-designated schools was 294 students using stabilized test-based indicators versus 238 using unstabilized indicators, consistent with stabilization reining in error-driven outliers among small groups. [→ Rosendahl 2024 (2)](#rosendahl-2024-2)
