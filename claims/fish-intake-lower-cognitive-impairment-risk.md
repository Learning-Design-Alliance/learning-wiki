# Higher fish intake is associated with lower risk of cognitive impairment in older adults

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In AREDS, participants in the highest fish intake quartile had lower odds of cognitive impairment than those in the lowest quartile (OR = 0.36 for 3MS, P = .001). [→ Keenan Tiarnán D. 2020](#keenan-tiarnan-d-2020)
`q3 i2` In AREDS2, participants in the highest fish intake quartile had lower odds of cognitive impairment than those in the lowest quartile (OR = 0.50 for TICS-M, P < .0001). [→ Keenan Tiarnán D. 2020 (2)](#keenan-tiarnan-d-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Keenan Tiarnán D. 2020

Keenan Tiarnán D., Agrón Elvira, Mares Julie A., Clemons Traci E., van Asten Freekje, Swaroop Anand, Chew Emily Y.; AREDS and AREDS2 Research Groups. (2020). Adherence to a Mediterranean diet and cognitive function in the Age-Related Eye Disease Studies 1 & 2. Alzheimer's & Dementia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8328271/

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional component analysis in AREDS examining fish intake quartiles and cognitive impairment. The article reports OR = 0.36 (P = .001; 3MS) and OR = 0.54 (P = .001; composite) for quartile 4 versus quartile 1.

> "In AREDS, the odds ratios for cognitive impairment, in quartile 4 (vs 1), were 0.36 (P = .001; 3MS) Figure 3A and 0.54 (P = .001; composite) Figure 3B."

### Keenan Tiarnán D. 2020 (2)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional component analysis in AREDS2 examining fish intake quartiles and cognitive impairment. The article reports OR = 0.50 (P < .0001; TICS-M) and OR = 0.54 (P = .001; composite) for quartile 4 versus quartile 1.

> "In AREDS2, the equivalent odds ratios were 0.50 (P < .0001; TICS-M) Figure 3E and 0.54 (P = .001; composite) Figure 3F"
