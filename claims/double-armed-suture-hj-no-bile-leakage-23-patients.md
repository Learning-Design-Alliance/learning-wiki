Kawashima Mampei, Nakamura Yoshiharu, Matsushita Akira, Hamaguchi Akira, Ono Takashi, Haruna Takahiro, Yoshimori Daigo, Irie Toshiyuki, Ga Ryo, Aoki Yuto, Oshiro Yukio, Yoshida Hiroshi. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective single-center analysis of 23 consecutive patients undergoing the technique during minimally invasive pancreaticoduodenectomy. The article reports "No bile leakage was observed" and one stricture managed without reoperation; no effect size is printed.

> "Laparoscopic hepaticojejunostomy was successfully completed in all patients without intraoperative conversion. No bile leakage was observed. An anastomotic stricture occurred in 1 patient (4.3%)."
