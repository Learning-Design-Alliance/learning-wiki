Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Electronics lab instructors' approaches to troubleshooting instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.113.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.113.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of 20 electronics instructors from 18 institutions; in the purpose-of-electronics theme, almost all instructors named troubleshooting as a key learning goal. One called it "one of the most important things they learn in electronics."

> "In terms of learning goals, almost all of the instructors in our study said that developing students' ability to troubleshoot is an important learning goal of the electronics course"
