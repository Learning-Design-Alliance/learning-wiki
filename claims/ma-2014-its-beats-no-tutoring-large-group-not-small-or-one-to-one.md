The letter reports the Ma et al. (2014) meta-analysis of step-based ITSs: "positive results for step-based ITSs" against no tutoring and large-group teacher-led instruction, with no differences against small-group or one-to-one tutoring. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Ma et al. (2014) found similar positive results for step-based ITSs both when compared to a no tutoring condition and when compared to large group human teacher led-instruction, but no differences when compared to small group human tutoring or to one –to–one tutoring"
