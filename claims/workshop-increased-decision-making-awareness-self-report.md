Getting lost to find insights - a quasi-experimental evaluation of outdoor experiential learning as a method to teach clinical decision-making and decision science concepts. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07533-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of post-course 5-point Likert survey responses from the same 41 students. Means ranged from 3.51 to 4.12 across seven items, with the highest for appreciating how workshop decision-making correlates to the clinical environment (mean: 4.12 ± 0.60). No effect sizes were reported.

> "students agreed that the workshop was valuable (mean: 3.63 ± 0.58), would improve their future clinical practice (mean: 3.63 ± 0.58), increased awareness of their decision-making process (mean: 3.68 ± 0.57)"
