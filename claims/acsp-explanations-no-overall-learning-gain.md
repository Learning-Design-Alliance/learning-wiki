# Providing access to explanations of adaptive hints does not significantly change percentage learning gains overall (d = .12)

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` In the controlled ACSP study, percentage learning gains did not differ significantly between the explanation condition (M=.49) and the control condition (M=.45), with a small printed effect size d=.12. [→ Cristina Conati 2019](#cristina-conati-2019)

## Evidence

### Cristina Conati 2019

Cristina Conati, Oswald Barral, Vanessa Putnam, Lea Rieger. (2019). Toward Personalized XAI: A Case Study in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. Preprint submitted to the JAI – Special Issue on Explainable Artificial Intelligence. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1912.04464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1912.04464)

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Controlled between-subjects study comparing 24 explanation-condition users who accessed explanations against 17 control users. The unpaired t-test on percentage learning gains showed "no statistically significant difference", with control M=.45, explanation M=.49, t(25.7)=.36, p=.7, d=.12.

> "A two sample unpaired t-test, between the PLGs of the control and explanation group showed no statistically significant difference in the scores for participants in the control (M=.45) and the explanation (M=.49) conditions (t(25.7) = .36, p=.7, d=.12)."
