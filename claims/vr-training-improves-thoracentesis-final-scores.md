## Evidence

### Han Shaoyu 2026

Han Shaoyu, Tian Zhen, Cui Bingjun, Wu Lang, Chen Zhixiang. (2026). Virtual reality-based training improves thoracentesis skills in medical interns: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09801-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In this single-center parallel-group RCT, 20 medical interns were randomized 1:1 to VR-based or mannequin-based training. At the week 4 final assessment the VR group scored 278.4 ± 9.8 versus 252.1 ± 10.3, with Cohen's d = 2.60 (95% CI: 1.35 to 3.85).

> "The VR group significantly outperformed the control group (VR: 278.4 ± 9.8, control: 252.1 ± 10.3; p < 0.001). The between-group difference at week 4 corresponded to a large effect size (Cohen's d = 2.60, 95% CI: 1.35 to 3.85)."
