## Evidence

### Kvintova Jana 2024

Kvintova Jana, Novotny Jan Sebastian, Liu Hongyang, Vachova Lucie, Kantor Jiri. (2024). Path analysis reveals cross-country differences between Czech and Chinese university students in effect of internet and smartphone addiction, mental health, and personality traits on academic achievement in the post-pandemic era. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02069-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted R-squared values from the final SEM models (Table 1): Czech IAD adj. R2 = 0.524 via five variables (Extraversion, mobile phone addiction, sex, stress, conscientiousness); Chinese IAD adj. R2 = 0.155 via anxiety and mobile phone addiction.

> "The models explained most of the internet addiction of Czech students (52.4%), but only 15.5% in the case of Chinese students."

### Kvintova Jana 2024 (2)

Kvintova Jana, Novotny Jan Sebastian, Liu Hongyang, Vachova Lucie, Kantor Jiri. (2024). Path analysis reveals cross-country differences between Czech and Chinese university students in effect of internet and smartphone addiction, mental health, and personality traits on academic achievement in the post-pandemic era. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02069-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted R-squared from Table 1: AASP 0.255 (Chinese) vs 0.127 (Czech); coping with study demands showed the reverse pattern (32.1% Czech vs 9.9% Chinese).

> "study performance itself was better explained by the observed variables for Chinese students (25.5% vs. 12.7% in the Chinese and Czech samples, respectively)."
