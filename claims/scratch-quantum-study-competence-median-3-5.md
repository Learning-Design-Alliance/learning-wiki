## Evidence

### Damian Rovara 2026

Damian Rovara, Robert Wille. (2026). Teaching Quantum Design Automation with Block-Based Programming. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.18206](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.18206)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

User study with N=24 post-graduate computer science and electrical engineering students, deployed in supervised and take-home formats, who completed design-automation exercises and a questionnaire. "The median total score of all participants was 3.5 out of 4 points"; per-question pass rates ranged from 68.8% (Q2-4 resource estimation) to 100% (Q2-1). No effect size printed.

> "The median total score of all participants was 3.5 out of 4 points, with a mean of 3.40 and a standard deviation of 0.61."
