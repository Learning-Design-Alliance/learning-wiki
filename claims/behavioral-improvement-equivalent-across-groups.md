## Evidence

### Graziano 2016

Graziano, P. A., & Hart, K. (2016). Beyond behavior modification: Benefits of social–emotional/self-regulation training for preschoolers with behavior problems. Journal of School Psychology, 58, 91–111. http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.jsp.2016.07.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed models of parent and teacher BASC-2 externalizing scores in the randomized trial found "no time by group interaction" for EBP, indicating similar behavioral improvement across all three intervention groups.
