## Evidence

### Melguizo-Ibáñez 2026

Melguizo-Ibáñez, E., Zurita-Ortega, F., Tadeu, P., Ubago-Jiménez, J. L., & Alonso Vargas, J. M. (2026). Classroom Active Breaks as a Physical-Educational Intervention for Executive Functions and Mathematical Outcomes: a Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10146-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 7 mathematical-achievement studies (n = 2483): the outcome-averaged model was significant while the primary RVE model was not; the article attributes the difference to reduced precision "when within-study dependence is considered".

> "The averaged model was statistically significant (g = 0.22, 95% CI [0.06, 0.39], Z = 2.67, p = 0.008), whereas the RVE model did not reach significance (g = 0.23, 95% CI [0.05, 0.41], t(6) = 1.28, p = 0.25)."
