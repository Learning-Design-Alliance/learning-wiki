# Pre-reflective students initially reacted negatively to the lecture-free course but the restructure fostered growth in their reflective judgment

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The restructured, lecture-free collaborative course was initially viewed negatively by pre-reflective students, yet fostered growth in reflective judgment for them. [→ Carr 1997](#carr-1997)

## Evidence

### Carr 1997

Carr, K. M. (1997). A Constructivist Approach To Reflective Judgment and Science Literacy in Introductory College Science Instruction. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED414179

`q2 · i?`

In the case study's discussion of pre-reflective functioning students, the author reports the decentralization of cognitive authority fostered disequilibrium, which "in some cases led to positive resolution and awareness of growth."

> "the restructured, lecture-free collaborativecourse was initiallyviewed negatively by pre- reflective, students. It was evident in the experience described in this study that the decentralization of cognitive authority fostereddisequilibrium for pre- reflective students"

### Carr 1997 (2)

Carr, K. M. (1997). A Constructivist Approach To Reflective Judgment and Science Literacy in Introductory College Science Instruction. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED414179

`q2 · i?`

The author's interpretation of case data: activities were stressful but "well supported by rich peer interaction and modeling," while instructors communicating at a reflective level were somewhat less effective at mentoring pre-reflective students.

> "The restructured course was effective at fostering growth in reflective judgment for pre-reflective functioning science students. The activities, while at times stressful, were well supported by rich peer interaction and modeling."
