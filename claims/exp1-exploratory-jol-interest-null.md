## Evidence

### Manuela Glaser 2025

Manuela Glaser, Laura Hug, Stephan Werner, Stephan Schwan. (2025). Spatial versus normal audio guides in exhibitions: Cognitive mechanisms and effects on learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10424-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory two-tailed test in Experiment 1 (n = 48) on a single confidence item rated 0–100 percent. No significant difference between the spatial and normal audio conditions.

> "There was no significant difference in judgement of learning between spatial audio settings (M = 48.00, SD = 13.84) and normal audio settings (M = 43.48, SD = 16.95), U = 241.50, p = .334, r = .14."

### Manuela Glaser 2025 (2)

Manuela Glaser, Laura Hug, Stephan Werner, Stephan Schwan. (2025). Spatial versus normal audio guides in exhibitions: Cognitive mechanisms and effects on learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10424-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory two-tailed tests in Experiment 1 (n = 48) on six-point Likert interest scales for inventors and inventions; both printed effect sizes (d = .14 and d = .06) were negligible and non-significant.

> "No significant differences between the conditions regarding interest in further information about the inventors, t(46) =  − .50, p = .623, d = .14, and their inventions, t(46) = .20, p = .844, d = .06, were found."
