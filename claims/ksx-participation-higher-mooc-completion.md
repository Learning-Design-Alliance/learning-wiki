## Evidence

### Heusler Andreas 2019

Heusler Andreas, Molitor Dominik, Spann Martin. (2019). Knowledge Stock Exchanges increase student success in Massive Open Online Courses. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0223064

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Field study of a Coursera management MOOC with 61,893 registered students; 992 also joined the KSX. Completion was "64.9 percent" for KSX joiners versus 6.4% for MOOC-only students; the authors flag self-selection because groups were non-randomized.

> "Students that additionally joined the KSX showed significantly higher completion rates of 64.9 percent. However, this may be driven by self-selection effects related to non-randomized groups"
