q2 · i? · review · r2

In the Priority 3 section on assessment, the report cites research showing that 'grade retention and “down tracking” actually undermine achievement,' recommending instead formative assessment with actionable feedback and tailored acceleration strategies. No effect size is printed.

"While many districts and educators may feel pressure to address learning loss by holding students back or tracking them for remedial instruction, research shows that grade retention and “down tracking” actually undermine achievement."
