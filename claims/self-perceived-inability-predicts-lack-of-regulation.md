## Evidence

### Loyens 2008

Loyens, S. M. M., Rikers, R. M. J. P., & Schmidt, H. G. (2008). Relationships between students' conceptions of constructivist learning and their regulation and processing strategies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9065-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM path analysis of the ILS model (N = 98); Table 3 reports a standardized estimate of .49 (p < .01) for the path from conceptions of inability to learn to ILS lack of regulation, a "large positive relationship with lack of regulation."

> "Conceptions of self-perceived inability to learn had a large positive relationship with lack of regulation. This implies that students who express high levels of doubt with regard to their learning process are at risk for adopting an inadequate regulation strategy."

### Loyens 2008 (2)

Loyens, S. M. M., Rikers, R. M. J. P., & Schmidt, H. G. (2008). Relationships between students' conceptions of constructivist learning and their regulation and processing strategies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9065-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the saturated alternative ILS model with direct paths added, the direct path from self-perceived inability to deep processing was significant, with standardized regression weight = -.35 (unstandardized = -3.00). The authors caution this renewed model should be tested in a new, independent sample.

> "The regression coefﬁcients of the alternative model showed a signiﬁcant ( p\ .001), negative relationship between self-perceived inability to learn and deep processing (unstandardized regression weight =- 3.00; standardized regression weight =- .35; standard error = .80)."
