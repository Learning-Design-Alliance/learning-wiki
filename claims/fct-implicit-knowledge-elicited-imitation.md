## Subclaims
`q3 i3` On the elicited imitation test, the FCT group significantly outperformed the control group at immediate posttest (p = .003, d = 1.11), improving more than 28% from pretest to posttest. [→ Broszkiewicz 2011](#broszkiewicz-2011)
`q3 i?` The CPA group only approached a significant difference from the control group on the elicited imitation posttest (p = .06, d = 0.66), and the two experimental groups did not differ significantly from each other. [→ Broszkiewicz 2011](#broszkiewicz-2011)

## Evidence

### Broszkiewicz 2011

Broszkiewicz, A. (2011). The effect of focused communication tasks on instructed acquisition of English past counterfactual conditionals. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 1(3), 335-363. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136449

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Elicited imitation tasks measuring implicit knowledge across pretest, posttest and delayed posttest in the three-group quasi-experiment. The FCT group improved from 54.79% to 83.13% (F = 14.89, p = .002); at delayed posttest both experimental groups remained significantly above the CG (FCT p = .001, CPA p = .02).

> "In comparison with the CG, the results achieved by the group instructed by means of focused communication tasks was highly significant ( p = .003) and the effect size was large ( d = 1.11)"
