## Evidence

### Sykes EA 2021

Sykes EA, Lemke M, Potter D, Li T, Mir ZM, Sheahan G, Wu V, Zevin B. (2021). Evaluating bowel enterotomy closures in simulated deep body cavities using the reversing half-hitch alternating post and square knots: a randomized controlled trial. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.016719

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Flat-surface repair time was compared between randomized groups of 10 each. The article reports "no difference" in repair time (19 [SD 2] v. 19 [SD 2] min, p = 0.45); no effect size was printed.

> "There was no difference between the square knot and RHAP groups in time required to repair the cadaveric porcine small bowel enterotomy on a flat surface (19 [SD 2] v. 19 [SD 2] min, p = 0.45)"

### Sykes EA 2021 (2)

Sykes EA, Lemke M, Potter D, Li T, Mir ZM, Sheahan G, Wu V, Zevin B. (2021). Evaluating bowel enterotomy closures in simulated deep body cavities using the reversing half-hitch alternating post and square knots: a randomized controlled trial. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.016719

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Blinded burst-pressure testing on the same day assessed flat-surface repairs. Pressures were "statistically similar" (128 [SD 40] v. 101 [SD 35] mm Hg, p = 0.31); no effect size was printed.

> "The mean burst pressures for enterotomy repairs on a flat surface were statistically similar for the square and RHAP knot groups (128 [SD 40] v. 101 [SD 35] mm Hg, p = 0.31)"
