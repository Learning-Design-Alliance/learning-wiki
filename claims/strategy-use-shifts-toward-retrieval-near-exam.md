Descriptive analyses of daily strategy use across the four course weeks (Table 4) showed both groups mainly re-reading, summarizing, or watching videos early, then "incorporated more retrieval-oriented strategies such as self-testing and explaining content" near the exam. The II group used self-testing from week 1, controls from week 3.
