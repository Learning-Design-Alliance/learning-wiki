Evidence¶

Ngo Hazel 2025¶

Ngo Hazel, Sokolovic Nina, Hu Julia, Jenkins Jennifer. (2025). A systematic review identifying effective teaching methods and their combinations for increasing empathy in physicians: pairwise and network meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07917-x

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression of measurement type in the pairwise meta-analysis (Table 2: objective k = 72, SMD .67; self-report k = 10, SMD .12; Q = 14.64, p = .00). The article reports "larger average effects on objective measures (d = 0.72, 95% CI: 0.51, 0.93) compared to self-report measures". The review prioritized objective measures when studies reported multiple types.

"A statistically significant effect was found for measurement type with larger average effects on objective measures (d = 0.72, 95% CI: 0.51, 0.93) compared to self-report measures (d = 0.12, 95% CI: − 0.10, 0.34)."
