### Eldhose Anupa 2026

Eldhose Anupa, Mathew Rohini Ann, Kumar Aniket, Shanthi Margaret. (2026). Quick response-code linked video-based objective structured practical examination: Bridging gaps in pharmacology education. Indian Journal of Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.4103/ijp.ijp_1351_25

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pretest–posttest study of 2nd-year MBBS students; paired t-test comparison of OSPE checklist scores before and after video access. The article reports "a significant improvement in student performance" with P < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "The posttest scores of 17.54 ± 2.18 (mean ± standard deviation [SD]) demonstrated a significant improvement in student performance compared to the pretest scores of 11.95 ± 6.69 (mean ± SD), with a P < 0.001"
