`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Figure-reported data from the MLH "Hackathon Data" talk at Hackcon IV, presented in the diversity and inclusion breakout group's results. Figure 1 compares the percentage of US population, EECS majors, and MLH hackathon participants per group; the report notes MLH hackathons "had a higher percentage of female participants on average than EECS degree programs did". No effect size is printed.

> "Notably, MLH hackathons had a higher percentage of female participants on average than EECS degree programs did. One possible explanation for this might be that among EECS majors, MLH hackathons were more likely to attract women, but MLH attributed it to the participation of non -EECS majors."
