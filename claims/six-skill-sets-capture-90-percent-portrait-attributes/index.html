Evidence¶

synthesis-analysis-1¶

Mills, K., Weisgrau, J., Burke, Q., Lee, K., Solorzano, T. and Coenraad, M. (2024, March). Shifting Education with Learning Pathways: Becoming Your Portrait of a Graduate. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/205

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Document analysis of 69 publicly available district Portraits (31 from League of Innovative Schools), coded inductively. The authors report being "surprised to discover that most districts identified a similar set of qualities" — the six Skill Sets capture "90 percent of the attributes" they coded.

"Overall, we identified six Skill Sets that capture 90 percent of the attributes articulated by school districts across the country"
