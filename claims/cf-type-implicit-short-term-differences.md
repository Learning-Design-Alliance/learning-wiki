# On the immediate posttest of implicit L2 knowledge, direct CF and metalinguistic CF with rule explanations outperformed the control group while metalinguistic code CF did not.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The DR, MS and MN groups scored significantly higher than the CO group on the immediate dictogloss writing posttest with large effect sizes, while the MC group did not. [→ Jang 2020](#jang-2020)

## Evidence

### Jang 2020

Jang, Sung-Soo. (2020). The efficacy of different types of metalinguistic information in L2 written corrective feedback. English Teaching, 75(4), 33-56. https://doi.org/10.15858/engtea.75.4.202012.33

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Tukey post-hoc results for dictogloss writing accuracy, the measure of implicit knowledge, in the four-session experiment. The study reports the groups "other than the MC group outperformed the CO group on the immediate posttest with large effect sizes".

> "All the experimental groups other than the MC group outperformed the CO group on the immediate posttest with large effect sizes (d = 1.527 for DR; d = 1.573 for MS; d = 1.064 for MN)."
