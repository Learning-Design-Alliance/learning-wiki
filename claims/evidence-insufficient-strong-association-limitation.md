### Bulfone Giampiera 2025

`q1 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Authors' stated limitation in the Study limitations section: only a few studies met meta-analysis inclusion criteria, just two were longitudinal with a two-year follow-up, and academic achievement was measured inconsistently across studies (e.g., GPA scales of 1 to 10 versus 1 to 4).

> "Due to these limitations, the current statistical evidence is insufficient to clarify a strong association between substance or alcohol use and poor academic performance."
