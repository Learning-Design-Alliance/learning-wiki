# Manipulating data elements in the ISNL supported a PSMT's transition from an algorithmic scheme to a whole set unit scheme for mode

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Engagement with constructing and visualizing mathematical models of datasets allowed Alex to shift from a step-by-step algorithmic scheme to considering the whole data set as a unit before finding the mode. [→ Md Amiruzzaman 2020](#md-amiruzzaman-2020)

## Evidence

### Md Amiruzzaman 2020

Md Amiruzzaman, Karl W. Kosko, Stefanie R. Amiruzzaman. (2020). Preservice Mathematics Teachers Understanding of Mode. Proceedings of the 39th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.10304](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.10304)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' interpretation of episode 4: Alex initially considered his algorithm before the nature of the data, but by the end of the episode demonstrated attention to elements of the data as part of a dataset. In the whole set unit scheme, "Alex considered the whole data set as a unit before finding the mode."

> "Such engagement presse d Alex to consider a different way of determining mode, and allowed a transition from an algorithmic scheme to a whole set unit scheme"
