Vijay Kanabar, PhD, Anatoly Temkin, PhD. (2015). Computer Science Programs, Goals, Student Learning Outcomes and their Assessment. CSECS 2015, Boston, MA. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.07793](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.07793)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article quotes an assessment handbook (Skidmore) distinguishing direct from indirect assessment, arguing that indirect measures give immediate feedback but that "indirect assessment does not provide reliable evidence that learning objectives have been achieved". This is an asserted methodological position, not a tested result.

> "Unfortunately indirect assessment does not provide reliable evidence that learning objectives have been achieved. The use of surveys and focus groups may lead to improvements in a program but do not directly provide evidence of student learning."
