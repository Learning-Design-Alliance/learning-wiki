### Sommer GM 2021

Sommer GM, Broschewitz J, Huppert S, Sommer CG, Jahn N, Jansen-Winkeln B, Gockel I, Hau HM. (2021). The role of virtual reality simulation in surgical training in the light of COVID-19 pandemic: Visual spatial ability as a predictor for improved surgical performance: a randomized trial. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000027844

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-arm randomized trial of 60 medical students; the training group (n=30) completed 48 VR simulator tasks over nine days while the control group performed two tasks on days 1 and 9. The article reports "significant improvements in surgical performance in five of the seven analyzed parameters" in the training group, analyzed with the Mann–Whitney U test; no effect size is printed.
