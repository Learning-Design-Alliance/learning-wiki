Geyer 1921¶

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The author's interpretive comparison of cited form-reliability figures (Otis 0.74-0.94; Snarr 0.79 with 306 pupils; Colvin averaging 0.83; Brown halves 0.76) against the between-test correlations reported earlier in the article.

"Comparing this agreement with the wide variation cited above leads one to doubt somewhat that the different tests, though all called "general intelligence tests," are really measuring the same element in the pupil's endowment."
