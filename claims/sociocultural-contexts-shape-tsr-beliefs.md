### Robinson 2022 (2)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument that stereotypes and a predominantly White teaching force may lead teachers to hold biases affecting the value and self-efficacy of TSRs with stigmatized students, potentially contributing to documented racial match effects on student outcomes. No new data reported.

> "they may have lower relational efficacy with students who do not share their ethnic-racial backgrounds"
