Evidence¶

Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse 2011¶

Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse. (2011). CSCL in Asia. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning 6:1–7. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-011-9112-1

q2 · i? · review · r1

In an illustrative study of students in India, Manu Kapur applied Lag-sequential Analysis, a statistical technique yielding transition probabilities that one category of event will follow another, similar to Hidden Markov Modeling.

"Kapur 's LsA temporal analysis revealed which groups ordered their problem-solving moves more effectively , providing insight into desirable group knowledge-building processes."
