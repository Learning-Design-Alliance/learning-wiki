## Evidence

### Arakawa Hiromi 2020

Arakawa Hiromi, Anme Tokie. (2020). The effect of an experiential learning program on motivations and activity involvement among dementia supporters in Japan. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244337

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental interventional study with 37 intervention and 44 control participants assigned by session number; Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on VAS motivation. The intervention group showed "a significant increase in motivation after the training session, after two weeks, and after three months."

> "The Wilcoxon signed-rank test (Table 2) revealed that, among the intervention group participants, there was a significant increase in motivation after the training session, after two weeks, and after three months."
