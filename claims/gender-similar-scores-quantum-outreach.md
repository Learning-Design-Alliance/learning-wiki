Preliminary analysis of the IQWs satisfaction questionnaire (564 responses) shows similar scores across gender categories. The authors note this is "quite good considering the known difficulties in involving girls in STEM activities".

> "figure 2(a) shows that females (51% of participants), males (37%) and those who chose not to answer the question on gender (21%) score similarly, which is quite good considering the known difficulties in involving girls in STEM activities."
