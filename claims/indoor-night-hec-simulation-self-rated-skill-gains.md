## Evidence

### Pietsch Urs 2020

Pietsch Urs, Lischke Volker, Sollid Stephen J. M., Prückner Stephan, Theiler Lorenz, Greif Robert, Albrecht Roland. (2020). Efficacy and efficiency of indoor nighttime human external cargo mission simulation in a high-fidelity training Centre. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-020-00755-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective observational study of 22 HEMS crew members (6 HEMS-TC, 16 MCM) using an established Pre-Post-Training Self-Evaluation Questionnaire; "Textbook knowledge and CRM questions improved in all domains" with Likert means such as 3.1 (0.61) before vs 1.9 (0.57) after for keeping track in darkness.

> "Textbook knowledge and CRM questions improved in all domains between the pre-course and post-course questionnaire (Table 5)."
