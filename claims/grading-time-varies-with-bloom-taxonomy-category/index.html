Rebecka Weegar · Peter Idestam-Almquist 2024¶

Rebecka Weegar · Peter Idestam-Almquist. (2024). Reducing Workload in Short Answer Grading Using Machine Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00322-1

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive analysis of timed grading from the retake exam, averaging grading time per answer across the knowledge dimension of Bloom's revised taxonomy. For the cognitive process dimension, applying was fastest at 1.82 seconds, remembering averaged 10.34 seconds, and understanding averaged 13.99 seconds.

"The least time for grading was required for the procedural knowledge, 1.82 seconds on average. More time was required for an answer describing factual knowledge with 7.95 seconds on average, and the most for conceptual knowledge with 14.73 seconds on average."
