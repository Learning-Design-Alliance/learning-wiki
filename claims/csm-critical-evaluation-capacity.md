## Evidence

### Gürsoy 2016

Gürsoy, E., Kesner, J. E., & Salihoglu, U. M. (2016). Clinical Supervision Model in Teaching Practice: Does it Make a Difference in Supervisors' Performance?. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 41(11). https://doi.org/10.14221/ajte.2016v41n11.5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Open-ended questionnaire item on whether TTs would recommend their US: 98% of experimental and 82% of control TTs said yes, but the control group had more undecided respondents (10%) and was, per the article, "inefficient when justifying their claim". The article interprets this as limited knowledge of quality supervision.

> "Although both the overwhelming majority in both groups answered “yes”, nearly all of the control group did so (98%).  Moreover, the control group had a greater percentage of their respondents who were “undecided” ."
