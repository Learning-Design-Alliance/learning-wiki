## Subclaims
`q3 i3` At the class level, students' aggregated test anxiety positively predicted aggregated math achievement, a pattern the authors describe as unexpected. [→ Lee 2025](#lee-2025)

## Evidence

### Lee 2025

Lee, H. J., Kim, S., & Bong, M. (2025). Social antecedents of children's mindsets, motivation and achievement in math: Investigating parental beliefs and perceived teacher beliefs. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 849–870. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12764

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Between-class path from the multilevel path analysis of 507 students in 28 classrooms: "The class‐level anxiety related positively to the class‐level math achievement" with β = .56 (i = 3), p = .002, contrary to the negative student-level pattern.

> "The class‐level anxiety related positively to the class‐level math achievement (β = .56, p = .002), which was unexpected."
