## Evidence

### Mingxin Liu 2024

Mingxin Liu, Tsuyoshi Okuhara, XinYi Chang, Ritsuko Shirabe, Yuriko Nishiie, Hiroko Okada, Takahiro Kiuchi. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT Across Different Versions in Medical Licensing Examinations Worldwide: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/60807

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Qualitative comparison of 9 studies that tested both original-language and English-translated questions, with 8 reporting comparative results (Table 2). The review reports GPT-4's "accuracy improvement ranged from 0.17% to 8.65%", while GPT-3.5's accuracy was more than 20% higher in English in 2 studies.

> "Overall, for GPT-4, translating the original language into English had a limited effect on improving the performance. The accuracy improvement ranged from 0.17% to 8.65%, with 6 studies showing an accuracy increase of less than 5%."
