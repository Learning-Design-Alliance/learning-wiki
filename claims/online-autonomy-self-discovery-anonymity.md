Synthesis of qualitative accounts under the autonomy theme: adolescents sought out spaces and content that validated their experiences, with the review reporting that "the anonymity created a judgement free space". One participant stated they could say whatever they wanted without worrying about being judged.

> "Evidence indicated that participants used platforms such as Tumblr to journal their challenges, as the anonymity created a judgement free space."
