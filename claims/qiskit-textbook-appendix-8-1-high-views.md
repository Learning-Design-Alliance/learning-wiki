### Wootton 2021

Wootton, J. R., Harkins, F., Bronn, N. T., Carrera Vazquez, A., Phan, A., & Asfaw, A. T. (2021). Teaching quantum computing with an interactive textbook. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1109/qce52317.2021.00058](https://doi.org/10.1109/qce52317.2021.00058)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of page views and referrals for Section 8.1 in late 2020 (Figs. 2 and 3). The authors report a "notable peak" in views and attribute referrals to the Chapter 1 link and to IBM-related domains recommending it for online events. No effect size is reported.

> "Another notable peak in Fig. (2) is that for 8.1, the appendix on linear algebra. Most referrals to this come from the introduction to Chapter 1, which links to the appendix"
