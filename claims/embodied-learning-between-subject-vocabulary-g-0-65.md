## Evidence

### Xiaoxuan Li · Julia Chatain · Fan Wang · Manu Kapur 2026

Xiaoxuan Li · Julia Chatain · Fan Wang · Manu Kapur. (2026). From Body to Word: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis of Embodied Vocabulary Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10194-9

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level meta-analytic model of 190 between-subject effect sizes from 52 studies of embodied vocabulary learning. The pooled effect was "moderate (Hedges’ g = 0.65, 95% CI [0.45, 0.85], p < .001)", with 83.23% of variance at Levels 2+3.

> "For between-subject studies ( k = 190), the pooled effect size was moderate (Hedges’ g = 0.65, 95% CI [0.45, 0.85], p < .001), with most heterogeneity attributable to between-study and within-study variance"
