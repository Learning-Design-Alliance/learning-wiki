## Evidence

### Sasayama Kiriko 2023

Sasayama Kiriko, Momoi Yasuko, Gilmour Stuart, Ota Erika. (2023). Development and feasibility of an oral health e-learning program for long-term Japanese overseas workers: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03361-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square comparison of fluoride use checklist behaviours in a pilot RCT (n=36 intervention, n=46 control). Table 3 reports OR 5.66 (2.15 to 14.92) for attention to fluoride concentration, rising from 13.9% to 61.1% in the intervention group versus 6.5% to 21.7% in controls.

> "At three months after the intervention, the intervention group showed a more positive impact than the control group with regard to "Paying attention to the concentration of fluoride when choosing a toothpaste" and "Not eating or drinking for two hours after brushing"."

### Sasayama Kiriko 2023 (2)

Sasayama Kiriko, Momoi Yasuko, Gilmour Stuart, Ota Erika. (2023). Development and feasibility of an oral health e-learning program for long-term Japanese overseas workers: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03361-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same chi-square comparison as printed in Table 3 of the pilot RCT: not eating or drinking for two hours after brushing rose from 30.6% to 61.1% in the intervention group versus 17.4% to 26.1% in controls, OR 4.45 (1.74 to 11.39).

> "At three months after the intervention, the intervention group showed a more positive impact than the control group with regard to "Paying attention to the concentration of fluoride when choosing a toothpaste" and "Not eating or drinking for two hours after brushing"."

### Sasayama Kiriko 2023 (3)

Sasayama Kiriko, Momoi Yasuko, Gilmour Stuart, Ota Erika. (2023). Development and feasibility of an oral health e-learning program for long-term Japanese overseas workers: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03361-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 3 of the pilot RCT reports, under a chi-square comparison, brushing with fluoride toothpaste at least twice a day OR 0.74 (0.25 to 2.2) and rinsing mouth only once after brushing OR 2.03 (0.81 to 5.09); these two items are not among those the text names as showing a more positive impact.

> "The χ-square test was used to compare the control group with the intervention group"
