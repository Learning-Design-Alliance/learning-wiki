> "We obtained and examined students’ grade point averages (GPA) upon enterin g the mechanics course, SAT Reasoning Test (SAT) scores, and the grades earned in the mechanics course; we found no signiﬁcant diﬀerence in the distributions of any of these metrics u sing a rank-sum test."
