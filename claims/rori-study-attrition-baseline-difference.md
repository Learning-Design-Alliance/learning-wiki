# Students who dropped out before endline had lower baseline math scores than completers, though age and gender did not differ

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 637 baseline students, 160 did not complete the endline; dropouts scored lower at baseline (M = 18.53, SD = 7.70) than completers (M = 22.26, SD = 7.57), with no significant differences in age or gender. [→ Owen Henkel 2024](#owen-henkel-2024)

## Evidence

### Owen Henkel 2024

Owen Henkel, Hannah Horne-Robinson, Nessie Kozhakhmetova, Amanda Lee. (2024). Effective and Scalable Math Support: Experimental Evidence on the Impact of an AI- Math Tutor in Ghana. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-64315-6_34](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-64315-6_34)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Attrition analysis of the 637 baseline participants (336 treatment, 301 control), of whom 477 completed both tests. Age (t(635) = 0.88, p = 0.38) and gender (χ² = 0.766, p = 0.38) showed no significant differences; the baseline-score difference is reported descriptively with no test statistic or effect size.

> "The mean raw baseline score of those that completed both tests was 22.26 (SD = 7.57) and the mean score of those that dropped out was 18.53 (SD = 7.70)."
