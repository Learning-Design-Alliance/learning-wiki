The article's literature review reports on the Marsh et al. (2020) synthesis of PISA data: "the effect of school-average achievement on ASC was negative in all but one of the 191 samples" and significant in 181. No standardized effect size is printed for the pooled BFLPE.

> "Marsh et al. (2020) reviewed findings based on four Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA) data collections with 1.25 million students. The results show that the effect of school-average achievement on ASC was negative in all but one of the 191 samples representing different countries/regions and significantly so in 181 samples."
