## Evidence

### Alsadi 2025

Alsadi, M., Alosta, M. R., Othman, E. H., Obiedallah, H., & Nashwan, A. J. (2025). Optimising Educational Practices in Simulation‐Based Nursing Education: Results of A National Survey. Nursing Open, 12(4), e70172. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70172

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive results from the 16-item NLN Educational Practices Questionnaire completed by the same 966 students; the article reports "the highest scores (M = 3.47 ± 1.17)" for active learning and "the lowest scores" for diverse way of learning. The printed High Expectations mean (4.44) exceeds the scale's stated 5.0 maximum context and the item-level mean of 3.44, an internal inconsistency.

> "The subscale of attitudes and opportunities to have an “Active Learning” experience using simulation had the highest scores (M = 3.47 ± 1.17). Nursing students have “High Expectations” (M = 4.44 ± 1.05) and perceived good levels of “Collaboration” (M = 3.38 ± 0.97). The lowest scores were for the “Diverse Way of Learning” (M = 3.18 ± 1.05)."
