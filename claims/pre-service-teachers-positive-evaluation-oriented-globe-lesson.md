`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation questionnaire administered after the Teams laboratory lesson; the article states all 425 participants filled it in. Descriptive questionnaire percentages; no inferential statistics reported.

> "100% of the students found the lesson interesting and even the 73% of them found it very interesting.
