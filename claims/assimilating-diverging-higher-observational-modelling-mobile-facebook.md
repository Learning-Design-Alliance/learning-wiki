## Evidence

### Chen 2015

Chen, Y. (2015). Linking Learning Styles and Learning on Mobile Facebook. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 16(2). https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/1973

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A composite score from six observational modelling items on the Facebook Use Questionnaire was compared across learning style groups after the nine-week treatment. "Results of Scheffé’s post hoc test showed that students in the Assimilating group" and Diverging group scored significantly more highly than Converging and Accommodating groups.

> "Results of Scheffé’s post hoc test showed that students in the Assimilating group (M=26.11, s=2.50) and the Diverging group (M=25.42, s=3.31) scored significantly more highly than those in the Converging (M=19.29, s=3.13) and the Accomodating group (M=17.65, s=4.09)."
