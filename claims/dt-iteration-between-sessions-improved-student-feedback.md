Guo Yan, Wang Xiaomei, Gao Yang, Yin Haiyan, Ma Qun, Chen Ting. (2024). Flipped online teaching of histology and embryology with design thinking: design, practice and reflection. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05373-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Test-phase iteration in the EDIPT cycle: questionnaire results triggered a redesign that "did not depend on students as thoroughly as it had the last time". Informal discussion quotes show students shifting from "Teacher almost let go" to perceiving teacher support and group feedback.

> "Three days after the questionnaires, the teaching team adjusted the flipped classroom teaching design scheme for the Female Reproductive System course according to the questionnaire results, and arranged the pre-class tasks one week prior to the class, which differed from the previous class."
