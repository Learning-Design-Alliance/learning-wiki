## Evidence

### Diego Ramírez 2023

Diego Ramírez, Eugenio J. Guzmán-Lavín, Javier Pulgar, Cristian Candia. (2023). Affinity-Based Groups in Secondary Education: Increased Stability at the Expense of Collaboration. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2309.15212](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2309.15212)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression models (9-11) show grade level as a positive significant predictor (p < 0.01) of the number of original teammates retained; the text reports the coefficient at "0.58∗∗ 0.59∗∗ 0.61∗∗" across the three models.

> "Tenth-grade participants exhibited a stronger inclination to continue collaborating with their first-semester teammates compared to those in the 9th grade."

> "it is worth noting that 10th grade students display statistically higher levels of team cooperation, compared to their younger counterparts."
