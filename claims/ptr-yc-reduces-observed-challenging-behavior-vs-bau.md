## Subclaims
`q4 i2` In an RCT, directly observed challenging behavior decreased significantly more from pretest to posttest for the PTR-YC group than for the BAU group, with a small interaction effect. [→ Glen Dunlap 2018](#glen-dunlap-2018)

## Evidence

### Glen Dunlap 2018

Glen Dunlap, Phillip Strain, Janice K. Lee, Jaclyn Joseph, and Nancy Leech. (2018). A Randomized Controlled Evaluation of Prevent-Teach-Reinforce for Young Children. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121417724874

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Momentary time sampling of the same video samples in the classroom-level RCT; mixed ANOVA on challenging behavior showed a significant time-by-group interaction, "F(1, 150) = 6.19, p = .014"; the article identifies a small effect size (ηp² = .040) for this interaction analysis.

> "Results specific to directly observed levels of challenging behavior revealed a statistically interaction effect between time and group, F(1, 150) = 6.19, p = .014. Table 1 presents the means and standard deviations for both groups at pre and post, and Figure 4 graphically represents the interaction between time and group."
