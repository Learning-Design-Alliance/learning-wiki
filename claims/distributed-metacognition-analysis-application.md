Cognitive-ethnographic excerpt analysis of the analysis phase, in which an observer comments on peers' NEWS-scorecard use and the facilitator propagates the evaluation back to the performing students, who publicly confirm the gap and articulate its clinical significance.

> "Excerpt 3 offers an example of the socially distributed nature of metacognitive work. Rather than being an individual performance, the facilitator activates the observer (student C), asking for his external perspective, identifying a critical gap and its rationale"
