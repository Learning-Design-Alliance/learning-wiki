### Bartley 2019

Bartley, J. E., Riedel, M. C., Salo, T., Boeving, E. R., Bottenhorn, K. L., Bravo, E. I., Odean, R., Nazareth, A., Laird, R. W., Sutherland, M. T., Pruden, S. M., Brewe, E. & Laird, A. R. (2019). Brain activity links performance in science reasoning with conceptual approach. npj Science of Learning 4:20. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0059-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Phase-wise whole-brain GLM analyses of the three sequential FCI phases (scenario view, question presentation, answer selection) in 107 students. Meta-analytic decoding associated Phase I with visuospatial attention and memory retrieval, Phase II with switching and visual short-term memory, and Phase III with DMN-related terms such as mentalizing.

> "These network transitions from fronto-temporo-parietal (Phase I) to dorsal attention (DAN; Phase II) followed by default mode cooperation (Phase III) point to the potentially important role V5–DMN–CEN interactions may have within physics reasoning processes."
