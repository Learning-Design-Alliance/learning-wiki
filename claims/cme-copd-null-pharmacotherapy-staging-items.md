## Evidence

### Drexel Carole 2011

Drexel Carole, Jacobson Anne, Hanania Nicola A, Whitfield Ben, Katz Jay, Sullivan Thomas. (2011). Measuring the impact of a live, case-based, multiformat, interactive continuing medical education program on improving clinician knowledge and competency in evidence-based COPD care. International Journal of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease. https://doi.org/10.2147/COPD.S18257

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Case 2 pharmacotherapy item in the matched comparison. The article reports the groups were "equally likely" to choose the guideline-recommended inhaled glucocorticosteroid option, 64% versus 66%; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "Participants and nonparticipants were equally likely to select guideline-recommended pharmacotherapy for COPD patients with repeated exacerbations (64% versus 66%, Table 4, Case 2)."

### Drexel Carole 2011 (2)

Drexel Carole, Jacobson Anne, Hanania Nicola A, Whitfield Ben, Katz Jay, Sullivan Thomas. (2011). Measuring the impact of a live, case-based, multiformat, interactive continuing medical education program on improving clinician knowledge and competency in evidence-based COPD care. International Journal of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease. https://doi.org/10.2147/COPD.S18257

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Case 2 GOLD staging item in the matched comparison. The article's text describes participants as more likely to stage correctly (58% versus 44%), but the printed test for this item is P = 0.161, so the difference was not statistically significant.

> "participants were more likely than nonparticipants to determine disease severity correctly (58% versus 44%, Table 4, Case 2) according to GOLD severity criteria."
