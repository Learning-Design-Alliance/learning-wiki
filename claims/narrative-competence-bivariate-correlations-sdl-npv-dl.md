Bivariate correlation analysis (Table 3) in the same survey: narrative competence correlated with self-directed learning at r = 0.603, professional values at r = 0.504, and digital literacy at r = 0.460 (all P < 0.01).

> "Pearson correlation analysis revealed significant positive correlations between nursing students’ narrative competence (including its dimensions) and the total scores as well as individual dimensions of digital literacy, nursing professional values, and self-directed learning (P < 0.01)."
