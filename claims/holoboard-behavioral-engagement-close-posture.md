### Jiangtao Gong 2021

HoloBoard: a Large-format Immersive Teaching Board based on pseudo HoloGraphics. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Video-coding analysis of sampled clips from the two classes, coded with a scheme covering affective states, posture, and behavior; inter-rater Kappa was greater than 0.89. The article reports "significantly longer duration of close posture (p = .002)" and positive behavior (p = .019) in the HoloBoard group; only p-values are printed, no effect size.

> "Mann-Whitney test indicated students in the HoloBoard group demonstrated significantly longer duration of close posture (p = .002) and positive behavior (p = .019) in the HoloBoard group than students in the normal group."
