Linear mixed-effects model on prompting self-efficacy (0–10 scale) from 106 observations across 53 students in the randomized session-controlled study; both groups also showed a main effect of Time (B = 2.187, p < 0.001).

"The interaction between Time and Condition was statistically significant (𝐵 = 1.349, 95% CI [0.139, 2.559],𝑝 = 0.029), indicating that students in the experimental group reported significantly higher prompting self-efficacy gains than those in the control group."
