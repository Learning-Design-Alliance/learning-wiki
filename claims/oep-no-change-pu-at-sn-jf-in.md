## Evidence

### Hengtao Tang 2021

Hengtao Tang, Yu-Ju Lin, & Yingxiao Qian. (2021). Improving K-12 Teachers' Acceptance of Open Educational Resources by Open Educational Practices: A Mixed Methods Inquiry. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10046-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired samples t-tests in the quantitative phase (N = 54) on the two factors meeting normality assumptions found no significant pre-post change in PU or SN; equivalence was not tested.

> "The results (see Table 1) suggested no significant changes on teachers' PU [t(53) = 0.25, p = .81], and SN [t(53) = 1.61, p = .11]."

### Hengtao Tang 2021 (2)

Hengtao Tang, Yu-Ju Lin, & Yingxiao Qian. (2021). Improving K-12 Teachers' Acceptance of Open Educational Resources by Open Educational Practices: A Mixed Methods Inquiry. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10046-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests in the quantitative phase (N = 54) found no significant change for AT (p = .66), JF (p = .54), or IN (p = 1.00); the IN median increased descriptively but was not significant.

> "Additionally, though participants' IN was not significantly improved, the median of this factor increased after the OEP experience."
