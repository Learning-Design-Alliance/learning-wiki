Evidence¶

New York Charter Schools: Remote Instruction During COVID Crisis (Spring 2020) – Results for All Authorizers 2020¶

New York Charter Schools: Remote Instruction During COVID Crisis (Spring 2020) – Results for All Authorizers. (2020). Survey slide report of New York State charter schools, May 2020. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/report-3/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive survey of engagement practices during remote instruction in New York charter schools. The report states that "about two-thirds of schools took ‘above and beyond’ steps" for students not regularly active, alongside the most commonly cited connection strategies: community events (184 schools), regular multi-mean communication (174), and existing structures like advisory or SEL programs (128).

"For students who were not regularly active in school, about two-thirds of schools took ‘above and beyond’ steps, such as conducting home visits, providing wifi hotspots, or reaching out to individual students’ extended networks of friends/family."
