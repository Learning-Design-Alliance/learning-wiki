## Evidence

### Daniel Howard and Mariel Meier 2021

Daniel Howard and Mariel Meier (2021). Meeting Laboratory Course Learning Goals Remotely via Custom Home Experiment Kits. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1119/5.0021600](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0021600)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-semester survey item SU35: students "overwhelmingly agreed" that freeloaders were eliminated because "all students had to perform all of the experiments individually" and extend results individually in the group project.

> ""freeloader s" (students who contributed little to group projects) were eliminated by the fact that all students had to perform all of the experiments individually and also extend those results individually in their group project assignment (SU35)."
