Pairwise A/B comparison tests with human evaluators (Table 4) on clips from the same script and gender, plus gender comparisons of the same model. The paper reports "female voices are preferred across all models", with comments noting smoother, more natural delivery.

"Table 4 shows a clear preference for bothXTTSandOrpheusoverCSM.XTTSis favored overCSM 43.3% vs 30.0%, andOrpheusshows an even stronger advantage at 53.2% vs 34.0%, align- ing withCSM's lower MOS scores and frequent truncations."
