## Evidence

### Mostafavi and Vahdany 2016

Fatemeh Mostafavi, Fereidoon Vahdany. (2016). The Effect of Explicit Affective Strategy Training on Iranian EFL learners' Oral Language Proficiency and Anxiety Reduction. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 7(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.4p.197

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing post-test oral scores of the experimental group (mean 4.53, SD 1.63) and control group (mean 3.56, SD 1.52), each n=30, after the experimental group received "six weeks of affective strategy instruction". The difference was significant (p<0.05); no effect size was printed.

> "The results of independent samples t-test for the post-test scores in tables 4.9, and 4.10 indicated that there was a significant difference between the two groups in their post- test (p<0.05)."
