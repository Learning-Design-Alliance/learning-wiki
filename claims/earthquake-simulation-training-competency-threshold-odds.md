## Evidence

### Farahmandnia Iman 2025

Farahmandnia Iman, Karimiankakolaki Zohreh. (2025). Effectiveness of the educational-operational intervention of the crisis management in earthquake simulation conditions in prehospital emergency personnel: a randomized controlled trial study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-13376-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The outcome is the Jahanbakhsh crisis-management questionnaire (14 Likert items, scored 14–70), and the authors list "reliance on questionnaire-based assessment rather than field performance measures" as a limitation; the intervention also bundled theoretical instruction, simulation, team exercises, films and video debriefing. Reported in the Results section alongside the post-test comparison, trained participants' odds of "achiev[ing] competency thresholds" were OR = 3.40 (95% CI: 1.8–6.2) relative to controls.

> "Participants receiving the training were 3.4 times more likely (95% CI: 1.8–6.2) to achieve competency thresholds than controls."
