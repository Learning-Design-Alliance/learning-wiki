## Evidence

### Slomowitz R. F. 2025

Slomowitz R. F., Narayan A. J., & McGrath L. M. (2025). Resilience research in learning disabilities: Guiding principles from developmental psychopathology. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102090

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

This paper reports, citing Torppa (2025) from the same special issue, a study where risk was based on parental math skills and potential resilience factors were home numeracy environment (HNE) types. The paper states the protective HNE types were not also promotive (main effects not significant) and some protective effects were developmentally sensitive.

> "Torppa (2025) identified that certain types of HNE activities (i.e., explicit, instructional math-related activities, such as counting objects, printing numbers, etc.) served as protective factors for math performance in 1st-2nd grade."
