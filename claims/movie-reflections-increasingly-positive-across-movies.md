## Evidence

### Karadeniz 2016

Karadeniz, O., & İncirci, A. (2016). A Study of Developing Multicultural Awareness in Teacher Education: Watch, Write and Talk a Movie. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(34), 81-89. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1126694

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Content analysis of 61 trainee teachers' written MES responses after each of three movies, counting positive and negative sentences. The article reports "the number of positive sentences increases and oppositely negative sentences decreases after each movie activity." Only one researcher coded the writings, limiting reliability.

> "It is detected that the number of positive sentences increases and oppositely negative sentences decreases after each movie activity."
