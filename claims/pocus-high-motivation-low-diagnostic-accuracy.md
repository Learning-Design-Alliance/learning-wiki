## Evidence

### Bansbach Joachim 2024

Bansbach Joachim, Bentele Michael, Bollinger Matthias, Bentele Stefanie, Langenhan Ronny, Gerber Bianka, Trifunovic-Koenig Milena, Bushuven Stefan. (2024). Self-assessment and learning motivation in emergency point-of-care ultrasound: an online pilot investigation in German physicians. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-024-01154-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive results from the cross-sectional multicenter online survey, in which participants completed the SIMS adapted to POCUS training. The article reports "intrinsic motivation (M = 6.1, SD = 0.87)" and identified regulation M = 6.40, with extrinsic motivation and amotivation notably low.

> "The overall motivation to learn POCUS competencies was high among participants, with particularly strong levels of intrinsic motivation (M = 6.1, SD = 0.87) and identified regulation (M = 6.40, SD = 0.68). Extrinsic motivation (M = 2.3, SD = 1.1) and amotivation (M = 1.6, SD = 0.8) were notably low."
