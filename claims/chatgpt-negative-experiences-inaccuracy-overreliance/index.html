Annamalai N, Nasor M, Din Eak A and Ayoub Alkubaisy AA (2026) Students’ experiences of using ChatGPT for English language learning: a qualitative study in a Malaysian higher education institution. Front. Psychol. 17:1875727. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1875727

Inductively coded negative themes from the same 25-student interview corpus: participants reported "inaccurate or misleading feedback reduces their confidence in the tool and requires extra effort to verify information". Qualitative; no effect size printed.

Annamalai N 2026 (2)¶

Annamalai N, Nasor M, Din Eak A and Ayoub Alkubaisy AA (2026) Students’ experiences of using ChatGPT for English language learning: a qualitative study in a Malaysian higher education institution. Front. Psychol. 17:1875727. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1875727

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Participants also reported "the lack of emotional connection and dependence on technology for language learning" and that there is "no human interaction". The authors state ChatGPT "serves as an AI that scaﬀolds, not a substitute for, an instructor."

"In addition, users have observed that the drawbacks of engaging with ChatGPT include the “absence of emotional response and depending on technology for learning” (G5)"
