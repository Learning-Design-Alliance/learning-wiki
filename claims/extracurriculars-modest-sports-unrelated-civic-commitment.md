## Evidence

### Susan E. Sporte and Joseph E. Kahne 2007

Susan E. Sporte and Joseph E. Kahne. (2007). Educating for Democracy: Lessons from Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research Research Brief. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/educating-democracy-lessons-chicago

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal analysis of the Chicago survey data compared extracurricular participation with classroom civic learning factors. The authors report that "the impact of participating in after-school clubs is quite modest" and that sports team participation was unrelated to civic commitment development.

> "our data suggest that the impact of participating in after-school clubs is quite modest, and, in line with previous research, our data also indicate that participating on sports teams is unrelated to the development of commitment to civic participation"
