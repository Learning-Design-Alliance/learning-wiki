## Evidence

### Wahabi HA 2019

Wahabi HA, Esmaeil SA, Bahkali KH, Titi MA, Amer YS, Fayed AA, Jamal A, Zakaria N, Siddiqui AR, Semwal M, Car LT, Posadzki P, Car J. (2019). Medical Doctors' Offline Computer-Assisted Digital Education: Systematic Review by the Digital Health Education Collaboration. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/12998

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

One economic evaluation (Evers checklist assessed) compared self-debriefing versus instructor debriefing using net benefit regression, reporting incremental net benefit and a cost-effectiveness acceptability curve, drawing on data from an included RCT.

> "The study concluded that digital learning was cost-effective if the intervention cost was less than or equal to Can $200 in the 2012 rate."

### Wahabi HA 2019 (2)

Wahabi HA, Esmaeil SA, Bahkali KH, Titi MA, Amer YS, Fayed AA, Jamal A, Zakaria N, Siddiqui AR, Semwal M, Car LT, Posadzki P, Car J. (2019). Medical Doctors' Offline Computer-Assisted Digital Education: Systematic Review by the Digital Health Education Collaboration. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/12998

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's secondary-outcome search found no randomized trial evidence on adverse effects of OCDE, so this outcome remains untested in the included evidence base.

> "We did not find any RCT that compared the adverse effects of OCDE with other interventions."
