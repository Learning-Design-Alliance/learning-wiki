A validation check on a subsample of 120 students compared self-reported continuous assessment scores against transcript and LMS records. The high correlation supports the validity of self-reported academic performance as the primary outcome.

> "To address concerns regarding the self-reported internal assessment scores, a subsample of 120 students was cross-validated with actual transcript and LMS records. The Pearson correlation between self-reported scores and verified records was r = .87, p < .001, indicating high concordance."
