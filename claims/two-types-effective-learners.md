## Evidence

### Renkl 1997

Renkl, A. (1997). Learning from Worked-Out Examples: A Study on Individual Differences. Cognitive Science, 21(1), 1-29. https://www.cogsci.org/journal/21-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A cluster analysis (Ward procedure) on self-explanation variables from 36 learners yielded a four-cluster solution. Clusters 1 and 2 both performed well but differed in style: Cluster 1 emphasized anticipative computation while Cluster 2 emphasized principle-based explanations and goal-operator explication. "there were two types of effective learners, labeled anticipative reasoners and principle-based explohers."
