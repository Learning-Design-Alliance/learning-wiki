## Evidence

### Bowman K 2023

Bowman K, Cadman T, Goncalves Soares A, Robinson O, Hughes A, Heron J, Segal AB, Huerta MC, Howe LD. (2023). Mediators of the association between childhood body mass index and educational attainment: Analysis of a UK prospective cohort study. Pediatric Obesity. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijpo.13014

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sensitivity analyses repeating the individual-mediator models using overt and relational bullying summary scores separately, plus ethnicity-restricted and SEN-excluded samples. The article reports results were "broadly similar" to the main analysis and "we did not find any statistical evidence for mediation by either component".

> "However, we did not find any statistical evidence for mediation by either component in either females or males."
