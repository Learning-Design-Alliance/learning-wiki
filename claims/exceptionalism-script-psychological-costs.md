The review reports, citing Anguiano & Gutiérrez Nájera (2015), interviews with Latinx undergraduates from undocumented backgrounds at an elite PWI for whom acceptance reflected 'deserving' status; 'their hard work and success came at a psychological cost like feelings of isolation and self-doubt.'

> "Once there, however, their hard work and success came at a psychological cost like feelings of isolation and self-doubt, also known as racial opportunity costs (Chambers et al., 2014)."
