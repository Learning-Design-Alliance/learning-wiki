### Nicole P. Vogt & Amy Smith Muise 2015

Nicole P. Vogt & Amy Smith Muise. (2015). An Online Tutor for Astronomy: The GEAS Self-Review Library. [doi:10.1080/2331186x.2015.1037990](https://doi.org/10.1080/2331186x.2015.1037990)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

An observational analysis of the distance cohort split into a 'super-studier' group (29% of students, completing between 340 and 754 quizzes over the semester) and the rest, comparing curves of accuracy against questions answered. Because homework credit is guaranteed at 20 quizzes per week, extra work is ungraded.

> "Beyond this line, however, we observe that the normal group continue to study but shows no strong increase in accuracy, while the super-studiers answer twice as many questions beyond the nominal goal and continue to increase in accuracy over this interval."
