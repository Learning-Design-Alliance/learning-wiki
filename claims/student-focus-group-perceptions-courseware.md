# Students in focus groups were positive about adaptive courseware but reported alignment, access, and reliability concerns

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Focus group students reported roughly four hours of weekly courseware use and were largely positive, but almost half cited course-courseware misalignment, about a third experienced glitches, and about one in eight had device or internet access difficulty. ## Evidence

### Digital Promise 2020

Digital Promise. (2020). Every Learner Everywhere and Lighthouse Institutions: First-Year Experiences. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/abf8db78-dc7a-4f4f-b06a-b00d1bda63e8 [doi:10.51388/20.500.12265/99](https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/99)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Online focus groups with 34 students from five institutions (about three-quarters students of color), coded in nVivo. Students reported "an average of approximately four hours per week" of use; concerns also included software crashes and desire for more instructor interaction.

> "Almost half of the students had examples of situations where the courseware and the other content in the course were not aligned."
