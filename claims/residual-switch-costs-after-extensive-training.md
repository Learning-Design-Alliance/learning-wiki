### Zhao Xin 2020

Zhao Xin, Wang Haien, Maes Joseph H. R. (2020). Training and transfer effects of extensive task-switching training in students. Psychological Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-018-1059-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Trial Type × CSI ANOVA on the final (21st) training session in the trained group; the significant trial-type effect shows a remaining switch cost after extensive training.

> "Of primary importance, the effect of trial type was highly significant and reflected faster responding on non-switch (M = 439.27, SD = 88.42) than switch (M = 457.62, SD = 100.82) trials, and did not interact with the CSI factor."
