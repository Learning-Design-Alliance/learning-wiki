Imlach, A.-R., Ward, D. D., Stuart, K. E., Summers, M. J., Valenzuela, M. J., King, A. E., Saunders, N. L., Summers, J., Srikanth, V. K., Robinson, A. & Vickers, J. C. (2017). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the initial regression (N = 181), all LSNS subscales (family, neighbours, friends) and HADS anxiety and depression were non-significant (Table 2, all p ≥ 0.367). This contrasts with previous young-adult research the authors cite, where these factors influenced performance.

> "Other predictors that did not demonstrate associations with academic performance were IQ and psychosocial factors (i.e., social networks, symptoms of depression and anxiety)."
