## Evidence

### Drovandi Aaron 2026

Drovandi Aaron, Sheader Elizabeth, Kingston Margaret. (2026). Integrated Team-Based Learning in a UK Undergraduate Medical Programme. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70470

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of assessment outcomes between the TBL cohort (n = 433) and two previous PBL cohorts (n = 507 and n = 416) using independent sample t-tests. Findings show the TBL cohort performed significantly worse than both PBL cohorts for the Semester 2 tests (all p < 0.001).

> "The TBL cohort (2023/2024 academic year) performed significantly better than the 2021/2022 PBL cohort, though significantly worse than the 2022/2023 PBL cohort for the Semester 1 Test, and significantly worse than both PBL cohorts for the Semester 2 tests (all p < 0.001)."
