`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bivariate and Mantel-Haenszel stratified analyses of 156 pediatric AKI cases with 15 in-hospital deaths. The article reports unadjusted ORs with CIs and p-values for each predictor, and adjusted ORs (4.14 and 3.99) for severe dehydration and hypernatremia after stratifying by hospital.

> "Significant independent predictors of mortality included hypovolemic shock (OR 8.73, 95% CI 2.13–35.76, p = 0.008), severe dehydration (OR 5.29, 95% CI 1.57–17.86, p = 0.009), and hypernatremia (OR 4.18, 95% CI 1.02–17.06, p = 0.045)"
