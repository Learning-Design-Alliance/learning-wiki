The article asserts these benefits of high-quality early care without citing specific studies or effect sizes. It stresses access to affordable infant/toddler care is critical because many American infants and toddlers spend part of each week in out-of-home care settings.

"High-quality early care has been shown to increase school readiness, improve math and language ability, help reduce the incidence of cognitive and social difficulties, and decrease the likelihood of a child repeating a grade or being placed in special education."
