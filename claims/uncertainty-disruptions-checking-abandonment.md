## Evidence

### Gamborg 2023

Gamborg, M. L., Mylopoulos, M., Mehlsen, M., Paltved, C., & Musaeus, P. (2023). Exploring adaptive expertise in residency: the (missed) opportunity of uncertainty. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-023-10241-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Narrative analysis of observed resident-actor interactions in the cognitive ethnography found a frequent interplay between disruptions and uncertainty; disruption of contextual learning often triggered uncertainty, mainly when nurses or supervising physicians interrupted residents' line of thought. No effect size reported.

> "By doing a narrative analysis of interactions between the residents and actors, we frequently observed an interplay between disruptions and uncertainty."

### Gamborg 2023 (2)

Gamborg, M. L., Mylopoulos, M., Mehlsen, M., Paltved, C., & Musaeus, P. (2023). Exploring adaptive expertise in residency: the (missed) opportunity of uncertainty. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-023-10241-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Figure 2 illustrates the interaction between observed disruptions from different actors and residents' behaviours indicating uncertainty, with checking behaviour marking abandonment of adaptive practices. This was a qualitative observational finding with no effect size reported.

> "As demonstrated in Fig. 2, checking behaviour was often indicative of uncertainty and the 'abandonment' of adaptive practices."
