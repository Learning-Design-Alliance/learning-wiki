## Evidence

### Freeman S. 2010

Freeman S., Parks J.W. (2010). How Accurate Is Peer Grading? CBE Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.10-03-0017

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In spring 2005, half the class graded as individuals and half in structured heterogeneous groups of four; the professional grader re-graded 96 and 94 individual exams and all 43 group exams per exercise. Differences from professional scores did not differ significantly (week 5: p = 0.64; week 10: p = 0.15); equivalence was not tested.

> "Unpaired t tests indicate no difference between individual versus group grading, on either exercise evaluated (Table 2)."
