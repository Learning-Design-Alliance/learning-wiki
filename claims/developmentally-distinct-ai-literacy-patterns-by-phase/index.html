Cross-case comparison of the six sector-specific scenarios in the discussion; the authors argue these variations challenge AI literacy frameworks that "universally support AI literacy for all students" and call for context-specific, developmentally appropriate strategies.

"primary contexts foregrounded socio-emotional competencies and collaborative learning; secondary contexts emphasised self-regulation mechanisms and real-time data interpretation; technical college contexts centred authentic industry partnerships"
