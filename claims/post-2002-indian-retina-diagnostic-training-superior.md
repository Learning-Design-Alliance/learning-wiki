## Evidence

### Gogate Parikshit 2019

Gogate Parikshit, Biswas Partha, Das Taraprasad, Nirmalan Praveen, Natarajan Sundaram. (2019). Ophthalmology residency training in India: Comparing feedback about how the training equips ophthalmologists to combat retinal diseases. READS report #6. Indian Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4103/ijo.IJO_1960_18

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey with a prevalidated questionnaire sent to 4512 ophthalmologists; 851 valid responses (320 group 1, 531 group 2). The abstract's results state "all P < 0.001" for the listed diagnostic skills, "but there was large variation between the training institutions".

> "Group 2 residents had received superior training in indirect ophthalmoscopy, slit lamp biomicroscopy using + 78 and + 90D lens, optical coherence tomography, fundus photography, and fluorescein angiography (all P < 0.001), but there was large variation between the training institutions."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

> "The results show that except for direct ophthalmoscopy, the ophthalmologists trained after 2002 reported superior exposure to skills in retinal diagnostics and laser."
