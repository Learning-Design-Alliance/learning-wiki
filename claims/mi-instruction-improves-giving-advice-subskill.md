## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Participants showed a statistically significant pre-post gain on the giving-advice component (t = 3.874, p ≤ .001, effect size 0.843). [→ Salem 2013](#salem-2013)

## Evidence

### Salem 2013

Salem, A. A. M. S. (2013). The Impact of Multiple Intelligences-Based Instruction on Developing Speaking Skills of the Pre-Service Teachers of English. English Language Teaching, 6(9), 53-66. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n9p53

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

One-group pre-post quasi-experiment (n = 60). Giving-advice pretest M = 10.20 (SD = 3.150), posttest M = 11.68 (SD = 0.596), t = 3.874, p ≤ .001, effect size 0.843, i.e. "a statistically significant difference between mean scores of the subjects of the study on the pre and posttest on the giving advices component."
