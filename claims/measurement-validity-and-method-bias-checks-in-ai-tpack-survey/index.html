Bai X and Hsieh T-S (2026) AI teaching innovation behavior among college teachers: a structural equation modeling analysis based on the AI-TPACK framework, teaching self-efficacy, professional identity, and AI literacy. Front. Psychol. 17:1834827. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1834827

Common method bias assessment on the 898 responses used Harman's single-factor test and a common latent factor approach. The article reports the first unrotated factor "accounted for 33.31% of the total variance, which was below the recommended threshold of 40%."

Bai X and Hsieh T-S 2026 (2)¶

Bai X and Hsieh T-S (2026) AI teaching innovation behavior among college teachers: a structural equation modeling analysis based on the AI-TPACK framework, teaching self-efficacy, professional identity, and AI literacy. Front. Psychol. 17:1834827. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1834827

Measurement model assessment in PLS-SEM evaluated reliability and validity for all eleven constructs. The article reports "all composite reliability (CR) values exceeded 0.70, and all average variance extracted (AVE) values exceeded 0.50," and notes several TPACK-dimension correlations were very small with a few trivially negative coefficients.

"all composite reliability (CR) values exceeded 0.70, and all average variance extracted (AVE) values exceeded 0.50, indicating satisfactory internal consistency reliability and convergent validity."
