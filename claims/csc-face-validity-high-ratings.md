## Evidence

### Daniel Ghods-Esfahani 2026

Daniel Ghods-Esfahani, Fatemeh Ramazani and Justin Lui. (2026). Validating the Calgary Simulation Curriculum: A Retrospective Review of Face and Content Validity of a Surgical Simulation Curriculum in Otolaryngology—Head and Neck Surgery. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1177/19160216261443996

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Retrospective analysis of resident feedback surveys on 5-point Likert scales collected across annual iterations (2022-2024) of the CSC. Sessions with preparatory materials and learning objectives "demonstrated uniformly high ratings across all evaluated domains"; responses were reported as medians with median absolute deviations, with no effect sizes printed.

> "In contrast, sessions supported by structured learning objectives and preparatory materials in subsequent iterations demonstrated uniformly high ratings across all evaluated domains."

### Daniel Ghods-Esfahani 2026 (2)

Daniel Ghods-Esfahani, Fatemeh Ramazani and Justin Lui. (2026). Validating the Calgary Simulation Curriculum: A Retrospective Review of Face and Content Validity of a Surgical Simulation Curriculum in Otolaryngology—Head and Neck Surgery. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1177/19160216261443996

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Participation analysis across the three iterations of the CSC among University of Calgary OHNS residents in postgraduate years 1 to 5. "A total of 25 residents provided feedback across 3 annual iterations of the CSC," and all residents who participated completed the survey.

> "A total of 25 residents provided feedback across 3 annual iterations of the CSC. Ten out of 10 eligible residents participated in the first iteration, 7 out of 8 eligible residents participated in the second, and 8 out of 8 eligible residents participated in the third."
