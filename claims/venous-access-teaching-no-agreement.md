`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of three procedure-teaching studies in the review: Bertran et al. found video-lesson residents obtained better results for central venous catheter positioning; Lian et al. found the traditional method significantly better for vascular access; Chenkin et al. found web tutorial and in-person lecture equivalent.

> "Regarding studies on ultrasound-guided procedures, there was no agreement among the results."
