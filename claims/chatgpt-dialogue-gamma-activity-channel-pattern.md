# Gamma-band EEG activity during dialogue differs by channel: teacher-dialogue students show stronger gamma at F3, F4, C3 and C4, while ChatGPT-dialogue students show significantly higher gamma at P4 and POz

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Gamma-band spectral power was higher for teacher-dialogue students at F3, F4, C3 and C4 (e.g., F3 13.57 vs 8.06), but ChatGPT-dialogue students showed significantly higher gamma at P4 (p = 0.047) and POz (p = 0.012); other channels showed no significant difference. ## Evidence

### Jiayue Zhang 2024

Jiayue Zhang, Yiheng Liu, Wenqi Cai, Lanlan Wu, Yali Peng, Jingjing Yu, Senqing Qi, Taotao Long, Bao Ge. (2024). Investigation of the effectiveness of applying ChatGPT in Dialogic Teaching Using Electroencephalography. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.16687](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.16687)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The EEG gamma-band analysis across nine electrode channels compared the two dialogue conditions. The article reports a mixed channel pattern, with "a difference between P4 and Poz channels (p < 0.05)" favouring the ChatGPT group and no significant differences at the other listed channels.

> "there is no more significant difference between F3, F4, Fz, C3, C4, Cz and P3 channels, and there is a difference between P4 and Poz channels (p < 0.05), and the students in EG group clearly show higher Gamma band activity"
