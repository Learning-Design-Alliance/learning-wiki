`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same latent-variable SEM on the 247 remote workers (Conceptual Model (a)) in Mplus 8. The article interprets that "with the increase in the avoidance of abusive ICT adoption, the perception of control increases and demands decreases in remote workers."

> "the avoidance of abusive ICT adoption was significantly and positive related to control (β: 0.47, t = 7.28, p < 0.001) and negatively associated with perceptions of demands (β: −0.38, t = −5.40, p < 0.001)"
