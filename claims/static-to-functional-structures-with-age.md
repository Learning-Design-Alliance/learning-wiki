## Evidence

### Kelley 1916

Kelley, Truman Lee. (1916). A constructive ability test. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. VII, No. 1. https://www.google.com/search?q=Kelley+1916+A+Constructive+Ability+Test+Journal+of+Educational+Psychology

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative observation from questioning children during building and examining photographed products across the author's test administrations. The article states that "in the main the building of adolescents is of this latter type" of functional structures, and that eight-year-old boys build structures of both types or combine both.

> "Another characteristic of the products of young children is that their buildings are usually representations of things as they are statical-ly—their ideas frequently do not involve the idea of motion; for example, such a child built the wagon shown in Plate XI, with wheels pegged to the body and incapable of movement."
