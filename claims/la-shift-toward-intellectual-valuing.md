### Amy D. Robertson 2013

Amy D. Robertson. (2013). Valuing Student Ideas: Morally, Instrumentally, and Intellectually. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1312.1446](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1312.1446)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of LA teaching reflections across Fall 2012 and Winter 2013. The author reports LAs foregrounding moral and instrumental value in Fall 2012/early Winter 2013 and intellectual value in mid-to-late Winter 2013, describing "a shift within LAs’ ways of valuing."

> "This shift was not from a view of student ideas as value-less to a view of student ideas as valuable; it was a shift within LAs’ ways of valuing toward a more expansive view."
