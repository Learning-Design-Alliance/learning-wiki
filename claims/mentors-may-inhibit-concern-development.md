## Evidence

### Olsen 1990

Olsen, Dwayne G.; Heyse, Kathy L. (1990). Development and Concerns of First-year and Reentry Teachers with and without Mentors. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED323188

`q2 · i?`

Discussion-section comparison across the study's four volunteer groups, contrasting mentored and unmentored teachers' final-interview concern levels. The authors quote Fuller and Bown's conclusion that the research question must be what kinds of interventions by what interveners elicit what responses from which subjects.

> "the question of whether the mentors inhibited the development of their mentees must be raised since seven of nineteen teachers without mentors (37%) reached the "impact" level in the third interview while cnly four of twenty first-year and reentry teachers with mentors (20%) identified "impact" concerns"
