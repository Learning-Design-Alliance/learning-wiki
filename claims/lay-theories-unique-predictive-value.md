## Evidence

### Limeri Lisa B. 2023

Limeri Lisa B., Carter Nathan T., Lyra Franchesca, Martin Joel, Mastronardo Halle, Patel Jay, Dolan Erin L. (2023). Undergraduate Lay Theories of Abilities: Mindset, universality, and brilliance beliefs uniquely predict undergraduate educational outcomes. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.22-12-0250

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Structural equation models in the phase-4 sample of 1396 undergraduates predicted six psychosocial and academic outcomes from the three second-order lay theory factors plus demographics, using a conservative p ≤ 0.01 threshold. The authors report "each lay theory contributed unique predictive power to at least one outcome."

> "After controlling for gender, race/ethnicity, and generation, and the other lay theories, each lay theory contributed unique predictive power to at least one outcome."
