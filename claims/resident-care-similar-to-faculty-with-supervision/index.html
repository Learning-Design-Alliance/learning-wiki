Evidence¶

van der Leeuw Renée M 2012¶

van der Leeuw Renée M, Lombarts Kiki MJMH, Arah Onyebuchi A, Heineman Maas Jan. (2012). A systematic review of the effects of residency training on patient outcomes. BMC Medicine. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3391170/

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis of 43 studies comparing faculty and residents, mostly in surgical disciplines. The review reports "no statistically significant differences between faculty and residents" on mortality, morbidity and length of stay in 31 studies; equivalence was not formally tested.

"Thirty-one studies reported no statistically significant differences between faculty and residents on a wide variety of outcomes including mortality, morbidity and length of stay [52,54,56-58,61,63,67-69,73-90,92-94]."

van der Leeuw Renée M 2012 (2)¶

van der Leeuw Renée M, Lombarts Kiki MJMH, Arah Onyebuchi A, Heineman Maas Jan. (2012). A systematic review of the effects of residency training on patient outcomes. BMC Medicine. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3391170/

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Same synthesis of faculty-versus-resident comparisons. The review reports that despite similar patient outcomes, "12 of these 31 studies did report a significantly longer operation time for residents", indicating a time cost of training.

"Although patient outcomes were similar, 12 of these 31 studies did report a significantly longer operation time for residents [61,67,69,74,77,78,81,83,84,87-89]."
