# Comparing highly alignable model buildings helps 5- to 7-year-olds place a diagonal brace in a repair task better than comparing surface-dissimilar buildings

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Children who saw the highly alignable (HA) training pair were more likely than those who saw the low-alignable (LA) pair to place a diagonal brace in the repair task. [→ Zheng 2025](#zheng-2025)

## Evidence

### Zheng 2025

Zheng, Y. S., Goldwater, M., & Gentner, D. (2025). Structural Alignment and Linguistic Contrast Help Children Learn a Key Principle of Spatial Construction. Cognitive Science, 49(12), e70149. https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.70149

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1, a 2x2 between-subjects lab study with 64 children (M = 6.4 years), logistic regression on repair-task placement showed a significant alignment effect (odds ratio = 2.31, p = .003); means were "M HA = 0.78" versus 0.41 for LA.

> "Children who saw the HA pair were more likely to place a diagonal brace in the repair task than those who saw the LA pair ( M HA = 0.78, SD HA = 0.42; M LA = 0.41, SD LA = 0.50)."
