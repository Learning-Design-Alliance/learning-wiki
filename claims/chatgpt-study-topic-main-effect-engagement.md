## Evidence

### Gaoxia Zhu 2023

Gaoxia Zhu, Xiuyi Fan, Chenyu Hou, Tianlong Zhong, Peter Seow, Annabel Chen Shen-Hsing, Preman Rajalingam, Low Kin Yew, Tan Lay Poh. (2023). Embrace Opportunities and Face Challenges: Using ChatGPT in Undergraduate Students' Collaborative Interdisciplinary Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2305.18616](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2305.18616)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Factorial ANOVA in the Results section of the quasi-experiment with 130 undergraduates in four tutorials. Main effects of topic were significant for all three dependent measures; a post hoc Tukey test showed scores were "consistently higher for the AI & Internet topic" (η² = .05, .02, .06).

> "the factorial ANOVA results indicate significant main effects of topics on collaborative interdisciplinary problem-solving (F(1, 202) = 13.19, p < .01, 𝜂2 = .05), cognitive engagement (F(1, 202) = 5.91, p < .05, 𝜂2 = .02), and physical engagement (F(1, 202) = 15.15, p < .001, 𝜂2 = .06). A post hoc Tukey test shows students' collaborative interdisciplinary learning, physical engagement, and cognitive engagement were consistently higher for the AI & Internet topic."
