### Mary Jane Brundage and Chandralekha Singh 2024

Mary Jane Brundage and Chandralekha Singh. (2024). Development and validation of a conceptual multiple-choice survey instrument to assess student understanding of introductory thermodynamics. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.07906](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.07906)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability test splitting the survey into 48- and 52-question parts with 22 common items, randomly administered in-class at one university. The article reports 58.5% vs. 58.8% on common items and 57.6% vs. 55.0% on all administered questions.

> "The performances of calculus-based introductory physics students who were administered the two version on the common 22 questions were 58.5% vs. 58.8%, which is similar"
