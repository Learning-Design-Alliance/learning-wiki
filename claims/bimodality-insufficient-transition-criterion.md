Analytical argument illustrated in Figures 7 and 8: a genuine sudden jump and a rapid acceleration in a continuous developmental model can both yield bimodal distributions, so the remaining catastrophe flags must be checked to discriminate the two sources of bimodality.

> "Both a discontinuous and a continuous accelerated development may lead to a bimodal distribution (see Figure 8). Consequently, bimodality in itself is not sensitive to this important distinction."
