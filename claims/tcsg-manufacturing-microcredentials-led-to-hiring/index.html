Georgia case study based on administrator interviews and program records of the summer 2021 pilot. In the last course week manufacturing companies interviewed participants; two were offered jobs. The article prints the cohort description but no wage or effect-size figures for this finding.

"Although those companies had the practice of not hiring anyone under the age of 21, according to Executive Director, Keith Fletcher, they made an exception to this practice due to the observed skills and certifications."
