q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis of disadvantaged pupils (EverFSM) in the two-year RCT headline results. The report states these pupils made "1 additional month's progress in maths, no additional progress in reading".

"Children ever eligible for Free School Meals (FSM) made 1 additional month's progress in maths, no additional progress in reading, and small gains in 'teamwork' and 'social responsibility' compared to ever FSM children in the other schools."
