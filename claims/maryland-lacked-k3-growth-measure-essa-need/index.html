Lisa Dragoset 2019¶

Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education, Evaluation, and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/measuring-school-performance-for-early-elementary-grades-in-maryland

q1 · i? · associational · r?

The report's introduction states the motivation: "MSDE lacks a measure of how well its schools are supporting the academic growth" of students from kindergarten to grade 3, motivating the K-3 growth measure developed in the study.

"Yet, as is the case for nearly every state, MSDE lacks a measure of how well its schools are supporting the academic growth of its youngest students, from kindergarten to grade 3."
