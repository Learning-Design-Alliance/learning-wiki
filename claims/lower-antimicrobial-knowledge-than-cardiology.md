## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students scored significantly better on clinical questions about cardiovascular medication management (mean 64%) than on antibiotic prescribing questions (mean 45%). [→ Weier N 2017](#weier-n-2017)

## Evidence

### Weier N 2017

Weier N, Thursky K, Zaidi STR. (2017). Antimicrobial knowledge and confidence amongst final year medical students in Australia. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182460

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Clinical-case knowledge test within the cross-sectional survey; answers were grouped by subject area and compared collectively. The printed means and SDs support the cardiology-versus-antibiotic contrast, significant at p<0.005; no effect size printed.

> "Students on average scored better on questions related to knowledge in medication management of cardiovascular diseases (mean 64% SD = 32.56), compared to scores in questions related to antibiotic prescribing (mean 45% SD = 15.75). These results were significantly different at p<0.005."
