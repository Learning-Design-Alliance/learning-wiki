## Evidence

### Chen 2025

Chen, Z.; Singh, C. (2025). Opportunities and Challenges in Harnessing Digital Technology for Effective Teaching and Learning. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.3390/higheredu4010006](https://doi.org/10.3390/higheredu4010006)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In an introductory physics course administered in 2020 and 2021, students completed a survey on their intention and plan to obtain extra credits for one homework sequence, with no obligation to follow the plan. Comparing average extra credit points per homework sequence with the previous semester, the authors 'were surprised to ﬁnd that students who answered the survey acquired signiﬁcantly more extra credit points on that particular homework sequence' (Figure 4C).
