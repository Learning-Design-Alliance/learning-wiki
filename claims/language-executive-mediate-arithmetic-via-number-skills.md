# Early language and executive skills at age 3–4 predict preschool counting and number knowledge, which mediate their influence on later arithmetic

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Three indirect pathways from early language and executive skills through counting and number knowledge to arithmetic were statistically reliable in both groups. [→ Moll K. 2015](#moll-k-2015)
`q3 i?` The model shows complete mediation: direct effects of language and executive function on arithmetic do not approach significance after mediated relationships are accounted for. [→ Moll K. 2015 (2)](#moll-k-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Moll K. 2015

Moll K., Snowling M. J., Göbel S. M., Hulme C. (2015). Early language and executive skills predict variations in number and arithmetic skills in children at family-risk of dyslexia and typically developing controls. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.03.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped indirect effects in the multi-group path model. The language→counting→arithmetic pathway was significant in both groups (FR .090, p = .019; TD .082, p = .013); executive function→number knowledge→arithmetic and language→number knowledge→arithmetic were also reliable in both groups.

> "Three of these indirect effects are statistically reliable in both groups: (1) language → counting → arithmetic: FR-group standardized effect = .090; p = .019; TD-group standardized effect = .082; p = .013"

### Moll K. 2015 (2)

Moll K., Snowling M. J., Göbel S. M., Hulme C. (2015). Early language and executive skills predict variations in number and arithmetic skills in children at family-risk of dyslexia and typically developing controls. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.03.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

After mediated pathways were included, direct paths from executive function and language to arithmetic were non-significant in both groups (e.g., TD executive function→arithmetic standardized effect = .075, p = .388), indicating complete mediation through verbal number skills.

> "this model shows complete mediation. Although language and executive function at t1 are significantly correlated with arithmetic skills in both groups the direct effects of these variables on later arithmetic skill"
