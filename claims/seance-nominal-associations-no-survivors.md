`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' stated interpretive stance on the corrected null: primary SEANCE features were tested with Bonferroni correction (adjusted α=.0009) and exploratory features with FDR, and "we report all uncorrected associations (𝑝<. 05) as hypothesis-generating findings". Readers are told to interpret accordingly.

> "Given the formative nature of this study, we report all uncorrected associations (𝑝<. 05) as hypothesis-generating findings, consistent with the goal of characterizing the signal landscape rather than making confirmatory claims."
