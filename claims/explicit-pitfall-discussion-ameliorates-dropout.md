## Evidence

### L. M. Rebull 2018

L. M. Rebull, T. Roberts, W. Laurence, M. T. Fitzgerald, D. A. French, V. Gorjian, and G. K. Squires. (2018). Motivations of Educators for Participating in NITARP, an Authentic Astronomy Research Experience Professional Development Program. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010148)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Program-organizer observation from running NITARP: after the Bootcamp added a forthright discussion of how overwhelming the program can be, the authors report that "Explicitly point-ing out potential pitfalls seems to have ameliorated issues of broken teams." This is an author interpretation, not a tested comparison; no effect size is printed.

> "Explicitly point-ing out potential pitfalls seems to have ameliorated issues of broken teams and educators 'giving up' entirely."
