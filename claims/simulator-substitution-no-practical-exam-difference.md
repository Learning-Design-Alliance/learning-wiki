## Evidence

### Silva Jack Philip 2016

Silva Jack Philip, Plescia Trevor, Molina Nathan, Tonelli Ana Claudia de Oliveira, Langdorf Mark, Fox John Christian. (2016). Randomized study of effectiveness of computerized ultrasound simulators for an introductory course for residents in Brazil. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2016.13.16

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized course, 41 residents completed the 16-point practical exam requiring six minutes of live-model scanning of five windows; the live-model-plus-simulator group scored "8.7±4.8 (n=22)" and the live-model-only group 8.7±3.9 (n=19), P>0.05. No effect size was printed.

> "The mean score for the LM+ S group was 8.7±4.8 (n=22) while the mean score for the LM group was also 8.7±3.9 (n=19) (P>0.05) (Fig. 2)."
