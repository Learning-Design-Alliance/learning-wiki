Center on School Turnaround at WestEd 2018¶

Center on School Turnaround at WestEd. (2018). Thought Leadership Forum brief: Formative assessment and agency as drivers of instructional transformation. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED592990

q2 · i? · design · r1

The brief reports Hattie's meta-analytical synthesis of "hundreds of studies," with Table 1 printing effect sizes: Lesson Study 0.90, Formative Evaluation of Teaching 0.90, Discussion of Learning 0.82, Providing Feedback 0.80. The brief attributes these figures to Hattie's visible learning research rather than computing them itself.

"Hattie reviewed and synthesized hundreds of studies to identify which instructional factors have the greatest impact on student learning/outcomes."
