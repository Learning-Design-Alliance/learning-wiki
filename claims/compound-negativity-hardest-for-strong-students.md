`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretive argument (type e) grounded in their re-analysis of upper-division studies: "even very strong students struggle most" in compound contexts, which they argue should be viewed as a culmination of reasoning with and about sign rather than a skill at the level of simpler cases. No effect size is printed.

> "Because of the many possible combinations of multiple signed quantities appearing in an expression, each compound context poses a unique challenge. It is in these compound contexts that even very strong students struggle most."
