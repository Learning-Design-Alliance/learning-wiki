## Evidence

### Aadithreya Varman 2024

Aadithreya Varman, Varman N V Arulmozhi, Balakumar Dinesh. (2024). Assessing the surgical competency of novice surgeons by using a three-dimensional heads-up display microscope. Indian Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4103/IJO.IJO_2677_23

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-surgeon comparison measuring BCVA recorded at postoperative day 30 across 400 surgeries: mean logMAR was 0.28 for TM and 0.26 for 3D surgeries with "No significant difference was observed in BCVA between the two groups" (P = 0.586).

> "The overall mean BCVA in logMAR units was 0.27 (0.42); in TM surgeries, it was 0.28 (0.43) units, and for 3D surgeries, it was 0.26 (0.41) units. No significant difference was observed in BCVA between the two groups (P = 0.586)"
