Evidence¶

Pears 2016¶

Pears, K. C., Kim, H. K., Fisher, P., & Yoerger, K. (2016). Increasing pre-kindergarten early literacy skills in children with developmental disabilities and delays. Journal of School Psychology, 57, 15-27. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsp.2016.05.004

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Benchmark analysis of the PLRS critical score in the same RCT sample. The article reports the percentages descriptively and states "not a significant difference"; no test statistic or effect size is printed, so equivalence was not established.

"For the PLRS, we examined how many children met the critical score for their age in each group. In the KITS group, 61% of the children met this benchmark whereas in the SAU group only 52% did (not a significant difference)."
