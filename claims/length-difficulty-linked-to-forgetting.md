## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Students' length-related difficulty reports centered on forgetting what was heard at the beginning by the time the text ends, which the author links to lack of note-taking skill. [→ İlhan 2018](#ilhan-2018)

## Evidence

### İlhan 2018

İlhan, B. (2018). Length in listening texts as a single determiner of difficulty for comprehension. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(3), 336-346. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1193259

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interpretation of Theme 4 reflection excerpts (e.g., "as the listening is long, I forget them towards the end"). Analysis of students' answer papers found "no form of efficient note taking was detected". This is the author's interpretation, not a tested result.

> "From these two excerpts,  it can be understood that students associate length with remembering the content. This might be due to students’ lack of note-taking skill which is an effective listening strategy."
