# Shape recognition through Edukoi sonification is above chance but less robust than colour recognition

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students identified geometric shapes through sound at 45% average correct, significantly above the 33% chance level but modest (d = 0.52). [→ Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino 2024](#lucrezia-guiotto-nai-fovino-2024)
`q3 i3` Students recognised spiral vs elliptical shapes of real galaxies through sound at 64% average, above chance (d = 1.15). [→ Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino 2024 (2)](#lucrezia-guiotto-nai-fovino-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino 2024

Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino, Anita Zanella, Luca Di Mascolo, Michele Ginolfi, Nicolò Carpita, Francesco Trovato Manuncola, Massimo Grassi. (2024). Evaluating the effectiveness of sonification in science education using Edukoi. Personal and Ubiquitous Computing. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00779-024-01809-5

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Behavioural shape task in T1 with 109 students who completed all eighteen trials; d = 0.52, average correct-response rate was 45% against 33% chance, using circle, square, and equilateral triangle stimuli.

> "We computed a one-sample t-test against chance (i.e. 33%) and it was significant: t (108) = 3.83, p < 0.001, d = 0.52, BF = 90.39."

### Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino 2024 (2)

Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino, Anita Zanella, Luca Di Mascolo, Michele Ginolfi, Nicolò Carpita, Francesco Trovato Manuncola, Massimo Grassi. (2024). Evaluating the effectiveness of sonification in science education using Edukoi. Personal and Ubiquitous Computing. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00779-024-01809-5

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Behavioural task in T2: 126 students explored real galaxy images from ESO and NASA archives in reversed order and classified each as spiral or elliptical; d = 1.15 and average performance was 64%.

> "Students recognised the shape above chance: t(125) = 9.13, p < 0.0001, d = 1.15, BF = 4.177327e + 12, and a small performance difference was observed among the grades: F(2, 123) = 3.88, p = 0.0232"
