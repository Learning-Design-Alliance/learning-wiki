Evidence¶

Yuan-Hsuan Lee and Jiun-Yu Wu 2026¶

Yuan-Hsuan Lee and Jiun-Yu Wu. (2026). Differential engagement with generative artificial intelligence in higher education: Gender, motivation, and achievement trajectories. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00615-8

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

ANOVA validation of the k-means four-cluster solution (95 students tested; two lacked pretest scores) confirmed clear separation on both clustering variables, with printed effect sizes η2 = .77 for prior knowledge and η2 = .72 for final grades.

"Results supported significant differences among the four clusters with large effect sizes ( F = 102.10, p < .01, η2 = .77 for prior knowledge; F = 78.29, p < .01, η2 = .72 for final course grades)"

Yuan-Hsuan Lee and Jiun-Yu Wu 2026 (2)¶

Yuan-Hsuan Lee and Jiun-Yu Wu. (2026). Differential engagement with generative artificial intelligence in higher education: Gender, motivation, and achievement trajectories. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00615-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The four profiles were labeled from standardized means: Consistent Achievers (n = 27, high-high), Growing Achievers (n = 30, low prior knowledge to high final grades), Declining Performers (n = 19, higher start to low finish), and Struggling Performers (n = 19, low-low).

"Students who began with low prior knowledge ( M = − 0.37) but reached high final grades (M = 0.69) demonstrated substantial improvement and effective learning gains, earning the label Growing Achievers (n = 30 in purple squares)."
