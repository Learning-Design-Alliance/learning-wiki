## Evidence

### Nieminen 2007

Nieminen, T. A. (2007). Project-based assessment for graduate coursework in physics. Australian Institute of Physics (AIP) 17th National Congress 2006: Refereed Papers. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0702046](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0702046)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Author's observational assessment of the 2005-2006 trials. Students handed in written reports and made in-class oral presentations; performance "varied from good through to superb", and presentations improved relative to earlier assignments in the same course.

> "Written reports varied from seven pages through to over 30, with 15 pages being typical, not including attached listings of computer code. Thus, these assessment tasks also provide a signiﬁcant degree of practice in written scientiﬁc communication"
