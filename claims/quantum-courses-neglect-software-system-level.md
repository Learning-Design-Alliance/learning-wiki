The authors' own narrative review of existing quantum computing courses (university courses, paid online offerings, high-school and undergraduate efforts) motivated the series. This is an author observation, not a systematic audit; no counts of courses reviewed are printed.

> "However, we notice that most of the courses only offer general introduction to quantum computing, neglecting the important role of software and system level support. Hence, we decided to hold lectures to introduce the software and system level support for quantum computing."
