## Evidence

### Yun 2008

Yun, J. H. (2008). The Effects of Hypertext Glosses on L2 Vocabulary Acquisition: A Meta-Analysis. Doctoral dissertation, University of Kansas. https://kuscholarworks.ku.edu

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The dissertation reports, citing Lomicka (1998), a study of 12 native English speakers in undergraduate French classes randomly assigned to no-gloss, single-gloss, or multiple-gloss conditions, in which "statistical differences between three groups were not found". The apparent advantage is descriptive only.

> "statistical differences between three groups were not found through think-aloud protocol data even though the students appeared to learn more vocabulary when they chose from a variety of assistive multiple annotations"
