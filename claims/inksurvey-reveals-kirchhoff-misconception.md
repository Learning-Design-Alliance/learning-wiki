F.V. Kowalski and S.E. Kowalski. (2013). The Effect of Student Learning Styles on the Learning Gains Achieved When Interactive Simulations Are Coupled with Real-Time Formative Assessment via Pen-Enabled Mobile Technology. [doi:10.1109/fie.2012.6462281](https://doi.org/10.1109/fie.2012.6462281)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational result from the Results section: after running the phasor circuits simulation, students' open-format InkSurvey submissions revealed this misconception; the instructor, "reading the earliest submissions on InkSurvey", coaxed them and students revised scaffolding submissions.

> "Typically, students did not notice that at each point in time on the animation, the voltage across the source equals the sum of the voltages across the inductor and resistor."
