# Under mirror-image conditions, superficial locations in a 3D laparoscopic training model take longer to access than deep locations with either hand

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Touching superficial spots required significantly more time than touching deep spots with the right hand (P<0.001) and with the left hand (P<0.001). [→ Inagaki Yuriko MD 2024](#inagaki-yuriko-md-2024)

## Evidence

### Inagaki Yuriko MD 2024

Inagaki Yuriko MD, Kawai Kazushige MD, PhD, Sonoda Hirofumi MD, PhD, Anzai Hiroyuki MD, PhD, Nagai Yuzo MD, PhD, Abe Shinya MD, PhD, Yokoyama Yuichiro MD, PhD, Ozawa Tsuyoshi MD, PhD, Kishikawa Junko MD, PhD, Emoto Shigenobu MD, PhD, Murono Koji MD, PhD, Sasaki Kazuhito MD, PhD, Nozawa Hiroaki MD, PhD, Ishihara Soichiro MD, PhD. (2024). Differences in the Difficulty of Accessing Various 3-Dimensional Locations Under Mirror-Image Conditions During Laparoscopic Surgery. Surgical Laparoscopy, Endoscopy & Percutaneous Techniques. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLE.0000000000001087

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experimental simulation in which 11 surgeons touched 25 points on a 3D model in a laparoscopic box trainer under mirror-image conditions. Right-hand time-points were "0.27 to 0.34" for deep versus "0.35 to 0.58" for superficial spots, a statistically significant difference (P<0.001).

> "When the right hand was used, the time-points were in the range of 0.27 to 0.34 and 0.35 to 0.58 for touching the deep and superficial spots, respectively, recording a longer time to reach the superficial spots (P<0.001)."

### Inagaki Yuriko MD 2024 (2)

Inagaki Yuriko MD, Kawai Kazushige MD, PhD, Sonoda Hirofumi MD, PhD, Anzai Hiroyuki MD, PhD, Nagai Yuzo MD, PhD, Abe Shinya MD, PhD, Yokoyama Yuichiro MD, PhD, Ozawa Tsuyoshi MD, PhD, Kishikawa Junko MD, PhD, Emoto Shigenobu MD, PhD, Murono Koji MD, PhD, Sasaki Kazuhito MD, PhD, Nozawa Hiroaki MD, PhD, Ishihara Soichiro MD, PhD. (2024). Differences in the Difficulty of Accessing Various 3-Dimensional Locations Under Mirror-Image Conditions During Laparoscopic Surgery. Surgical Laparoscopy, Endoscopy & Percutaneous Techniques. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLE.0000000000001087

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same box-trainer simulation, left-hand trials: deep spots took "0.28 to 0.42" and superficial spots "0.43 to 0.63", with the superficial areas significantly slower (P<0.001). The authors attribute this to needing to adjust both depth and 2-dimensional positioning at superficial spots.

> "With the left hand, reaching the deep and superficial spots required 0.28 to 0.42 and 0.43 to 0.63, respectively, indicating more time required for accessing the superficial areas (P<0.001)."
