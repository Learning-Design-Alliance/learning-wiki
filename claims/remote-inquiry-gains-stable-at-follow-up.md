# Learning gains from remote inquiry with virtual and video experiments remain relatively stable from post-test to eight-week follow-up, with a slight decline

> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium
> **Currency** · newest study 2023 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Conceptual understanding declined slightly but remained relatively stable from post-test to follow-up test approximately eight weeks later, independent of condition. [→ Flegr 2023](#flegr-2023)

## Evidence

### Flegr 2023

Flegr, S., Kuhn, J., & Scheiter, K. (2023). How to foster STEM learning during Covid-19 remote schooling: Combining virtual and video experiments. Learning and Instruction, 86, 101778. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101778

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Post-to-follow-up mixed ANOVA in the randomized experiment, based on only n = 72 of the original 154 participants due to missing follow-up data. Students performed "slightly worse in the follow-up test" (partial η2 = 0.09); no condition effect or interaction emerged.

> "The mixed ANOVA revealed a significant main effect of time, F(1, 69) = 6.94; MSE = 1109.68; p = .01; partial η2 = 0.09 (medium effect), suggesting that students performed slightly worse in the follow-up test (M = 57.42%, SE = 2.15) than in the immediate post-test (M = 62.89%, SE = 2.30)."
