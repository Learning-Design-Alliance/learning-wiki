`q2 i?` Pre-intervention groups A and B, described as random groups of presumably similar linguistic competence, showed a minor positive correlation of 0.26428556, while A and C (the same control subjects) correlated 0.8253401 and B and D correlated 0.80017335. [→ Al-Ahdal 2015](#al-ahdal-2015)

### Al-Ahdal 2015

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational check within the same quasi-experiment, computed on the sum totals of the five rating parameters for each subject. The article interprets the high A-C correlation as statistical proof that A and C are the same set of subjects.

> "A and B which are random groups of presum ably similar linguistic competence, show a minor positive correlation of 0.26428556 and groups A and C show an extremely high correlation of 0.8253401."
