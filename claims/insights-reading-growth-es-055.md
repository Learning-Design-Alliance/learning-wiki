### INSIGHTS into Children's Temperament 2010

INSIGHTS into Children's Temperament. (2010). Program description and evidence summary. https://www.evidencebasedprograms.org/programs/insights-into-childrens-temperament/

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

A cluster randomized controlled trial in 22 urban elementary schools during 2010-11 with 435 kindergarten and first-grade students compared INSIGHTS to an attention-control supplemental reading program. Reading was measured with the Woodcock-Johnson III; the article reports students "grew significantly faster than those in the control group in reading achievement (effect size = +0.55)".
