## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the optional ARCS survey (n=76), repeated-measures ANOVA showed a significant difference among the four components (F3, 225=13.15; P<.001), with confidence (mean 3.78, SD 0.87) significantly lower than attention, relevance, and satisfaction. [→ Chartash David 2026](#chartash-david-2026)

## Evidence

### Chartash David 2026

Evaluation of a Problem-Based Learning Program’s Effect on Artificial Intelligence Ethics Among Japanese Medical Students: Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/84535

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Optional postprogram ARCS survey completed by 76 (64.4%) of the 118 participants, rated on a 5-point Likert scale; attention mean 4.01 (SD 0.62), relevance 4.20 (SD 0.71), confidence 3.78 (SD 0.87), satisfaction 4.03 (SD 0.75).

> "Additionally, post hoc tests with Bonferroni correction revealed that the mean score for confidence was significantly lower than the scores for attention, relevance, and satisfaction."
