# Participants' engagement (think-aloud word counts) did not differ significantly between texts or assessment points

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Word counts on the think-aloud protocols did not differ significantly between the own-side and other-side texts, between assessment points, or in their interaction. [→ Iordanou 2025](#iordanou-2025)

## Evidence

### Iordanou 2025

Iordanou, K., Fotiou, C., Manoli, A., & Zembylas, M. (2025). Examining myside bias on a controversial historical event after engagement in dialogic argumentation: Insights from a think aloud study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102209

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed-effects models on think-aloud protocol word counts (own-side M = 351, SD = 140.89; other-side M = 341, SD = 139.22) from 30 participants at pre and 24 at post, with random intercepts for participants and school. No significant text, time, or interaction effects; equivalence was not tested.

> "Participants’ engagement did not differ significantly between the two texts (“own” vs “other”) (β = 9.08, SE = 18.09, t = -.50, p =.62), and between the two assessment points (β = 16.07, SE = 18.81, t = -.85, p = .40). No significant interaction was found between the texts and assessment points (β = 16.24, SE = 36.17, t = -.45, p = .65)."
