Post-lab interviews with four previously ChatGPT-naive students found they "stated after the lab that they would use such tools in the future" for short, simple explanations of difficult content.

> "The students who had no previous experience with using AI chatbots in physics, Benjamin, Beatrice, Caroline, and Daniel, stated after the lab that they would use such tools in the future when they are looking to get short and simple explanations of things they struggle to understand."
