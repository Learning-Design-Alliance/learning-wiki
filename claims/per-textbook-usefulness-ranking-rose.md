# Switching to a PER-based textbook raised students' average usefulness ranking of the textbook from 2.1 to 3.2 on a 1–5 scale

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After replacing Tipler and Llewellyn with portions of Knight's introductory physics textbook, average student ranking of textbook usefulness rose from 2.1 to 3.2, while rankings of other course aspects did not change significantly. [→ S. B. McKagan 2007](#s-b-mckagan-2007)

## Evidence

### S. B. McKagan 2007

S. B. McKagan, K. K. Perkins and C. E. Wieman. (2007). Reforming a large lecture modern physics course for engineering majors using a PER-based design. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.1063/1.2508685](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2508685)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-term survey comparison across the two reformed semesters (Fa05 with Tipler and Llewellyn, Sp06 with Knight). Students had complained weekly about the first text and top students stopped reading it; complaints were almost absent in the second semester.

> "the average student rank- ing of the usefulness of the textbook for their learning on a scale of 1 (not useful) to 5 (a great deal) went up from 2.1 to 3.2. The usefulness rankings for other aspects of the course did not change signiﬁcantly between the two semesters"
