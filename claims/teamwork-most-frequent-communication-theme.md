Documentary content analysis of 16 course syllabi identified 467 quotes related to communication competencies. The study reports that Theme 2 was "the most frequent, comprising 46.9% of all identified quotes (n = 219)".

> "Theme 2 “Communicates appropriately and in a timely manner with all personnel involved in patient care and working effectively as a member or leader of a health care or other professional group” was the most frequent, comprising 46.9% of all identified quotes (n = 219)."
