## Evidence

### Chen Yingshi 2025

Chen Yingshi, Feng Rixuan, Liu Mina, Yang Yang, Liu Huan, Zheng Wei, Zhao Yang, Luo Chenling. (2025). Effect of nursing simulation teaching information system based on HIS in comprehensive training course for senior nursing undergraduates: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07015-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Faculty-scored group performance (nursing assessment, health education, skill operation, cooperation, communication; maximum 15 points) for all 114 intervention completers, compared with the Mann–Whitney U test. No effect size was printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "The control group had an average score of 12.80 ± 1.23 points, while the experimental group scored 12.84 ± 0.92 points. The difference in the case workshop scores between the two groups was not statistically significant (Z = -0.036, p = 0.971)."
