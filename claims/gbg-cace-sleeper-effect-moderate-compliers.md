## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In the moderate compliance CACE model, a small but statistically significant intervention effect on reading emerged at 1-year follow-up (β = 0.93; Δ = 0.10, p < .01), absent at post-test. [→ Ashworth Emma 2020](#ashworth-emma-2020)

## Evidence

### Ashworth Emma 2020

Ashworth Emma, Panayiotou Margarita, Humphrey Neil, Hennessey Alexandra. (2020). Game On—Complier Average Causal Effect Estimation Reveals Sleeper Effects on Academic Attainment in a Randomized Trial of the Good Behavior Game. Prevention Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-019-01074-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel mixture CACE models in the cluster RCT, with compliance defined as classrooms above the 50th percentile of cumulative dosage (1030 min), showed "a small but statistically significant intervention effect was observed in the moderate compliance model" at T3 only; the printed standardized treatment effect is β = 0.93, with Δ = 0.10.

> "However, at T3, a small but statistically significant intervention effect was observed in the moderate compliance model (β = 0.93; Δ = 0.10, p < .01). No effect was observed in the high compliance model (β = − 0.25, p > .05)."
