## Evidence

### Hazlett 2025

Hazlett, Z. S., Jimenez, P. C., & Knight, J. K. (2025). Self-Testing and Follow-Through of Learning Strategies Supports Student Success. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-04-0128

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Separate stepwise generalized linear regressions for each of three semesters (S22 n = 155, F22 n = 94, S23 n = 187) predict course score from self-testing, Pre-GCA, gender, class standing, and race. Table 4 prints unstandardized coefficients (e.g., self-testing β = 0.11 in S22); every 10% increase in practice completion predicted a 1.1% course-score increase in S22. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "For all three semesters, Pre-GCA score and amount of self-testing significantly predicted course score, but to different degrees each semester."
