Shi 2025¶

Shi, H., Bi, R., Lin, X., & Dai, Y. (2025). Decreasing Digital Distraction in College Students: Associated Online Learning Strategies Identified by Unsupervised Data Mining Approaches. https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/P85S3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

ARM single-antecedent rules from the 530-student survey. Two help-seeking (HS) rules from the SRLQ showed negative association with distraction; e.g., disagreement with sharing problems with classmates online had phi = -0.129, odds ratio 0.590, chi-square p = .003 in Table 2.

"Interestingly, some rules (see HS rules in Table 2) revealed that students experiencing fewer distractions reported less frequent peer-related help-seeking behaviors, such as sharing problems with classmates or seeking help from knowledgeable peers."
