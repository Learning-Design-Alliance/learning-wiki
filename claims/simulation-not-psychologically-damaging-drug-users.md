`q2 i?` Although the simulation elicited challenging emotions and retriggered past experiences, participants described it as comfortable and affirming, refuting the idea that it is psychologically damaging for this population. [→ Whittall Jonathan P. 2022](#whittall-jonathan-p-2022)

## Evidence

### Whittall Jonathan P. 2022

Whittall Jonathan P., Orkin Aaron M., Handford Curtis, Klaiman Michelle, Leece Pamela, Charles Mercy, Wright Amy, Turner Suzanne, Morrison Laurie J., Strike Carol, Campbell Douglas M. (2022). Resuscitation simulation among people who are likely to witness opioid overdose: Experiences from the SOONER Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0270829

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of their qualitative findings; most participants reported feeling anxious, stressed or nervous during simulation but valued the emotional activation and described the experience as positive and empowering.

> "Findings from this study refute the idea that exposing individuals with previous overdose experience to a contextualized simulation is psychologically damaging."
