## Evidence

### Piya-amornphan N 2020

Piya-amornphan N, Santiworakul A, Cetthakrikul S, Srirug P. (2020). Physical activity and creativity of children and youths. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-020-2017-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis comparison of TCT-DP scores across the three age groups (n = 521, 487, 439) found scores "increased in accordance with the age group", medians 20, 21 and 24, p < 0.001; only medians and ranges are printed, no effect size.

> "The total TCT-DP scores which indicated creativity ability in the present study ranged between 9 and 69 points, and the TCT-DP score increased in accordance with the age group (Table1)."
