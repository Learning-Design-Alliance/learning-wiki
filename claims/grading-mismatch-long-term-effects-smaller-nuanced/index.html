McKenzie 2025¶

McKenzie, R. & Allensworth, E. (2025). Variability in the Grades Teachers Give: Teacher Grade Value-Add, Mismatch, and Long-Term Effects. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/00028312251345094

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Long-term outcome regressions (Table 5) on the CPS sub-sample, mismatch coefficients. The article reports that mismatch effects are smaller than persistent effects and that positive mismatch is associated with a higher graduating core GPA; the abstract characterizes the mismatch-outcome relationship as nuanced. No standardized effect size is printed for this finding.
