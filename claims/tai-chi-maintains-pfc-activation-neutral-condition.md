## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, L., Li, Y., Wang, L., Zhang, X., Sun, Y., He, S. & Zhu, W. (2026). Effects of Tai Chi on attentional bias in men with methamphetamine use disorder. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00426-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

fNIRS analysis during the dot-probe task in the RCT; ANCOVA with FDR correction showed significant group differences across six prefrontal ROIs, e.g. L-VLPFC: F = 12.579, pFDR = 0.006, ηp2 = 0.249; R-FPA: F = 10.940, pFDR = 0.006, ηp2 = 0.224.

> "participants were better able to maintain a certain level of activation, with moderate to large effect sizes in the mean oxy-Hb beta across various brain regions of the prefrontal cortex while performing a dot-probe task (when the probe dot was consistent with a neutral stimulus) , compared with the control group."
