## Evidence

### Luminica Topale 2016

Luminica Topale. (2016). The strategic use of lecture recordings to facilitate an active and self-directed learning approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0723-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional electronic survey of 281 first- and second-year medical students at an international medical school, drawn from 2,322 invitations via a convenience sample. Respondents' self-reported reasons for non-participation show "their attendance had not been negatively influenced by the availability of recorded lectures."

> "Nearly 88 % of the participants reported that their attendance had not been negatively influenced by the availability of recorded lectures."
