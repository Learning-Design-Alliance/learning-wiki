Related Claims¶

  • Teachers prefer to start ILS design from empty pedagogical structures rather than domain content — related
  • Teachers who participated in ILSs with KAM practices were far more likely to be involved in an implemented ILS (42–53%) than the dataset average (16.27%) — related
  • ILS adoption rate increases across knowledge maturation stages, from 3.38% for individually created ILSs to 43.94% for published ILSs — related
  • The higher the maturation level of an Inquiry Learning Space (create, share, co-create, publish), the higher its probability of being implemented in a classroom — related
  • Re-used Inquiry Learning Spaces were about four times more likely to be implemented than those created from scratch, and scaffolding increased the implementation rate — related
  • ILS reuse and edition as a proxy for knowledge appropriation relates to about four times higher adoption (19.77% vs 4.51%) — related
  • Expert scaffolding is associated with ILS adoption, but the fading stage shows a comparatively low rate (8.27%) and was a non-significant predictor — related
  • Logistic regression shows all traced KAM practices except fading are significant positive predictors of adoption, with maturation strongest (odds ratios up to 12.36) and model AUC = 0.79 — related
