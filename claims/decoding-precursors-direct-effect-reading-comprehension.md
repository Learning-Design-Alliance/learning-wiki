## Evidence

### Jessica Massonnié 2018

Jessica Massonnié, Maryse Bianco, Laurent Lima, Pascal Bressoux. (2018). Longitudinal predictors of reading comprehension in French at first grade: Unpacking the oral comprehension component of the simple view. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.005

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal SEM (SEM 2) on 556 French first-graders tested whether T1 decoding precursors (phonemic awareness, letter knowledge, RAN) predicted T2 reading comprehension beyond T2 decoding skills; the direct path was "significant (β = .31)" and the model accounted for 82% of reading comprehension variance.

> "the direct path from T1 decoding precursors to T2 reading comprehension proved to be significant (β = .31; for the overall model: χ2(272) = 651.26, p < .001, CFI = .94, TLI = .93, SRMR = .07, RMSEA = .06, 90% confidence interval [.05, .06]; Δ χ2(1) = 4.79, p < .05)."
