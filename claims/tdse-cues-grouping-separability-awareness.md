## Evidence

### Bahar Modir and Eleanor C. Sayre 2016

Bahar Modir and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2016). “Pulling out” as a procedural resource when solving partial differential equations. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1607.07493](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1607.07493)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The authors' interpretation from observing group problem solving: the right and left sides of the TDSE cue Alex to group one side as a function of time and the other as a function of x, yet awareness of the separability condition varies across students.

> "The separated terms of partial derivatives, with respect to time and space, in the TDSE cue students to group the equation based on two separated terms. However, not all of the students are aware that this is only correct if the wave function is separated into a function of only space and a function of only time."
