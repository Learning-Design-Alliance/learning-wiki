`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney tests on Form and Meaning Recall in the Delayed Test two weeks after instruction, comparing each treatment group with the control group (CT N=16). Delayed recall means were low for CT (2.5 Repeating) versus PBI (6) and TBI (7.933).

> "The same result is noticed in Delayed Test, with the dominance of PBI (W=48, p=0.0011) and TBI (W=24, p=4.679e-5) for Repeating chunks."
