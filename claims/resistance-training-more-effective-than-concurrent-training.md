Printed effect sizes: resistance training SMD = 0.26 (95% CI 0.10–0.42) versus concurrent training SMD = 0.11 (95% CI −0.05 to 0.28); intervention type explained R2 = 0.40 of between-study variance. The concurrent training CI crosses zero.

> "Resistance training significantly improved cognition and academic outcomes (SMD 0.26, 95% CI 0.10–0.42), while the result for concurrent training was less effective (SMD 0.11, 95% CI − 0.05 to 0.28)."
