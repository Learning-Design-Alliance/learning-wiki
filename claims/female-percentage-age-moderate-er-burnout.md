## Evidence

### Ioana Alexandra Iuga · Oana Alexandra David 2024

Ioana Alexandra Iuga · Oana Alexandra David. (2024). Emotion Regulation and Academic Burnout Among Youth: a Quantitative Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09930-w

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regressions on continuous moderators: female percentage significantly moderated ER difficulties–overall burnout and –emotional exhaustion; mean age significantly moderated ER difficulties–overall burnout. Study quality did not significantly influence any relationship.

> "The proportion of female participants in the study sample significantly influences the relationship between ER difficulties and overall burnout (β, -0.0055, SE = 0.001, p < 0.001), as well as the emotional exhaustion dimension (β, -0.0049, SE = 0.002, p < 0.01). Mean age significantly influences the relationship between ER difficulties and overall burnout (β, -0.0184, SE = 0.006, p < 0.01)."
