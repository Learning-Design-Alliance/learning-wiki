Veli Batdı & Hacer Batdı 2015¶

Veli Batdı & Hacer Batdı. (2015). Effect of Creative Drama on Academic Achievement: A Meta-analytic and Thematic Analysis. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.6.0156

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analytic moderator analysis of implementation period across five groups: the 9–18-week group showed the highest descriptive ES (2.55) and unspecified the lowest (1.23), but no significant between-group difference (QB = 7.63, df = 4, p > .05).

"the lowest effect size of 1.23 was observed in the case of unspecified IP , whereas the highest effect size of 2.55 occurred in the 9–18-week-period group. However, a statistically significant difference in terms of the between- group effect ( QB = 7.63, df = 4, p > .05) was not found."
