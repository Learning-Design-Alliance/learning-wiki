# Emotionally enriched AI feedback shows no meaningful advantage in behavioural engagement with feedback; control students spent slightly longer on feedback during creation

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Omar Alsaiari 2025

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Hatim Lahza, Jason M. Lodge, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2025). Emotionally Enriched Feedback via Generative AI. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U test on time between receiving feedback and subsequent submission/moderation during creation tasks, with outliers removed: control median 76.545 s (N=707) vs experimental 70.6865 s (N=696), "statistically significant (p = 0.026) with a small effect size (|r| = 0.059)".

> "In the creation task, the control group exhibited a median engagement time of (M dn= 76.545, N = 707), while the experimental group had a median time of (M dn= 70.6865, N = 696). This difference was statistically significant (p = 0.026) with a small effect size (|r| = 0.059)."

### Omar Alsaiari 2025 (2)

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Hatim Lahza, Jason M. Lodge, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2025). Emotionally Enriched Feedback via Generative AI. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U test on engagement time during moderation tasks in the same RCT: medians 3.450 s (control, N=2709) and 3.464 s (experimental, N=2671), "no statistically significant difference ( p = 0.920)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "For the moderation task, the engagement times were nearly identical, with the control group at (M dn= 3.450, N = 2709) and the experimental group at ( M dn= 3.464, N = 2671), showing no statistically significant difference ( p = 0.920)."

### Omar Alsaiari 2025 (3)

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Hatim Lahza, Jason M. Lodge, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2025). Emotionally Enriched Feedback via Generative AI. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three survey items adapted from Kay (2011) measuring engagement showed medians of 3.29 (control, N=50) and 3.25 (experimental, N=51), "no significant differences" (p = 0.823); no effect size printed.

> "further results on engagement from the survey data revealed no significant differences between the control group ((M dn= 3.29, N = 50) and the experimental group ((M dn= 3.25, N = 51), (p = 0.823))."
