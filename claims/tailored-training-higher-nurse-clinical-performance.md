## Evidence

### Kazemi-Arpanahi 2025

Kazemi-Arpanahi, H. et al. (2025). Effectiveness of tailored educational content on improving nurses’ clinical performance in cardiac surgery wards: a mixed-methods study. Scientific Reports 15, 23616. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-08728-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Case–control comparison of 30 trained and 60 untrained C-ICU nurses after 6 months of hospital activity, assessed with a 200-item checklist by eight blinded nurse assessors and analyzed with the Mann–Whitney test. The article reports "a statistically significant difference between the two groups (p < 0.001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "the trained group exhibited a mean total score of 883 (± 36.23), whereas the untrained group showed a mean score of 566.45 (± 39.95), indicating a statistically significant difference between the two groups (p < 0.001)"
