# Maladaptive motivational profiles are associated with less time on task and lower cognitive task achievement

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Profiles with more maladaptive motivational beliefs showed less time on task (log data) and lower performance on learning-to-learn cognitive tasks. [→ Nyman 2025](#nyman-2025)

## Evidence

### Nyman 2025

Nyman, L., Koivuhovi, S., Greiff, S., Hotulainen, R., Little, T. D., & Vainikainen, M.-P. (2025). A person-centered approach to action-control beliefs of students with special educational needs and their relation to student performance and time on task. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001000

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Profile differences in outcomes were examined using student-reported GPA, learning-to-learn cognitive task scores, and log-file TOT in the national assessment sample. The Educational Impact statement reports "less time on task and poorer cognitive performance, as indicated by the log data."

> "more maladaptive patterns of motivational beliefs were associated with less time on task and poorer cognitive performance, as indicated by the log data."
