`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's reconciliation of 870 associations across measures and contexts reports that "consistency in patterns of association improved" for dimensions such as supporting play, while instructional support showed low support even against cognitive-academic outcomes.

> "The consistency in patterns of association improved for some dimensions of interaction quality (e.g. supporting play), with other dimensions showing low support even when they aligned with the outcome (e.g. instructional support with cognitive-academic outcomes)."
