## Evidence

### Linton Debra L. 2014

Linton Debra L., Farmer Jan Keith, Peterson Ernie. (2014). Is Peer Interaction Necessary for Optimal Active Learning? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.13-10-0201

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Deidentified essays coded by two raters with rubrics; item frequencies compared by chi-square. "In each of those 17 cases, the cooperative section showed a significantly higher percentage of students including that item." Concepts included conservation of matter, cellular respiration, photosynthesis, stomata, and fitness.

> "Fifty-nine separate chi-square analyses were run on individual items from the exam essay rubrics. Seventeen items were found to have significant differences between the cooperative and individual sections. In each of those 17 cases, the cooperative section showed a significantly higher percentage of students including that item in their essay answer than did the individual section."
