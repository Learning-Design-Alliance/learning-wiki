Park 2025¶

Park, A.T., Colantonio, J., Delgado Reyes, L., Sharp, S.D.S., Koepp, A.E., Bonawitz, E., & Mackey, A.P. (2025). Question asking practice fosters aspects of curiosity in science content in young children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00384-5

Preregistered quantile-regression moderation of science learning: WISC Vocabulary x Condition interaction (b = -1.80, p = .028) with a main effect of vocabulary (b = 3.21, p < .001); the article reports this "equalized" children's learning outcomes. No standardized effect size printed.
