### Han Mingyu 2025

Han Mingyu, Shi Wei, Yu Ling, Yuan Tianming, Zhang Yuanyuan. (2025). Stamp collecting game improves internship performance and experience in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07764-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Instructor proficiency ratings in the pilot RCT (Table 3): resuscitation 3.80 vs 4.14 (p < 0.010); teaching rounds 3.88 vs 4.09 (p = 0.139). Authors attribute the null to "the limited duration of the rotation".

> "Additionally, in Neonatal Resuscitation Training, the experimental group exhibited significantly greater proficiency than the control group, whereas no significant proficiency difference was observed between the two groups during Teaching Rounds."
