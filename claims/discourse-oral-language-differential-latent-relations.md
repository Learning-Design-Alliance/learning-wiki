Grace Kim Young-Suk & Graham Steve. (2022). Expanding the Direct and Indirect Effects Model of Writing (DIEW): Reading-Writing Relations, and Dynamic Relations As a Function of Measurement/Dimensions of Written Composition. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000564

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Latent-variable correlations from confirmatory factor analysis in the 350-child Grade 2 sample showed discourse oral language "fairly strongly related to writing quality (.62) and reading comprehension (.68)" but unrelated to productivity (r = .09, p = .21), a negligible correlation, supporting dimension-dependent relations.

> "Discourse oral language was fairly strongly related to writing quality (.62) and reading comprehension (.68) whereas it was not related to writing productivity (.09, p = .21)."
