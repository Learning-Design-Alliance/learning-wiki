### Buljan Ivan 2019

Buljan Ivan, Tokalić Ružica, Marušić Matko, Marušić Ana. (2019). Health numeracy skills of medical students:cross-sectional and controlled before-and-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1902-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled before-and-after study, objective NUMi scores across three measurements; the non-intervention group improved more than the intervention group. The authors interpret this as possibly due to the first-year curriculum and frequent exposure to numeracy testing.

> "the non-intervention group showed greater increase in objective numeracy results (Mddiff = 2.0, 95% CI = 1.0 to 3.0) compared to the intervention group (Mddiff = 1.0, 95% CI = 0.0 to 1.0)."
