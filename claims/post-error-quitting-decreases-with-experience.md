### Johansson 2024

Johansson, A. M., Savi, A. O., van der Maas, H. L. J., & Hofman, A. D. (2024). Three Strikes and Who is Out? Individual Differences in Error-Induced Quitting. https://github.com/ann1ejohansson/three-strikes

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory analysis comparing the probability of quitting after a correct versus an incorrect response across session count, separated by difficulty level and new versus existing users, displayed in Figure 3 with 95% confidence ribbons.

> "This exploratory analysis revealed two important trends in our data: (1) post-error quitting decreases with more playing experience, while post-correct quitting does not, (2) post-error quitting is more likely while playing easy and difficulty levels, but not post-correct quitting."
