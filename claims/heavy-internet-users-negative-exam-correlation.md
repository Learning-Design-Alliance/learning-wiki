Within-cluster correlational analysis for Cluster 3 (n = 56 per Table II) from the survey and assessment data; the significant negative correlation "r = −0.28; p = 0.04" suggests lookup time costs, per the authors' interpretation. Correlation is not causal.

> "For the heavy internet users (Cluster 3), the usage of internet resources other than AI during online exams (OnlInt) is significantly negatively correlated with the scores on the exams (OnlExams) (r = −0.28; p = 0.04), which may indicate that relying on the internet, the students were not able to quickly enough find what they needed"
