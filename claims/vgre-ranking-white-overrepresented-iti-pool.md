# Ranking qualified applicants by verbal GRE produced a statistically significant racial/ethnic imbalance, with White/non-Hispanic applicants overrepresented in the interview-invited pool

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` When the qualified applicant pool was ranked by vGRE, the racial/ethnic distributions of applicants invited to interview versus waitlisted differed significantly, with White/non-Hispanic applicants more represented in the ITI pool (70.2% vs 56.4%). [→ Eikenberry Megan PT 2025](#eikenberry-megan-pt-2025)

## Evidence

### Eikenberry Megan PT 2025

Eikenberry Megan PT, DPT, EdD; Scott Kylie PT, DPT, EdD; González Jaime PT, DPT; Subialka Josh PT, DPT, DSc; Frankland Lacey PT, DPT; Ayala Patrice PT, DPT; Kargela Mark PT, DPT; Russell Byron PT, PhD. Journal of Physical Therapy Education. https://doi.org/10.1097/JTE.0000000000000413

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square test of homogeneity on 435 qualified applicants in the 2020–2021 cycle ranked by vGRE, comparing ITI versus WLI racial/ethnic frequencies. Post hoc z-tests showed White applicants at 70.2% ITI versus 56.4% WLI, "P = .003"; Asian, Hispanic, and Other URM differences were not significant after Bonferroni adjustment.

> "The multinomial distributions of race and ethnicity (White, Asian, Hispanic, and Other Underrepresented Minorities) were not equal between the ITI and WLI groups, χ2(3) = 11.892, P = .008"
