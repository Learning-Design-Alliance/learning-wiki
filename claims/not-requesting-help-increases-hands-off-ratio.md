## Evidence

### Høyer Christian B 2011

Høyer Christian B, Christensen Erika F, Eika Berit. (2011). Standards of resuscitation during inter-hospital transportation: the effects of structured team briefing or guideline review - A randomised, controlled simulation study of two micro-interventions. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-19-15

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis within the simulation study of the association between requesting the driver's help and hands-off ratio (time without chest compressions over time in cardiac arrest). Help was not requested in 15% of cases (7/46). The association is correlational, not a manipulated effect.

> "Never calling for help from the driver was associated with a statistically significant increase in hands-off ratio, rising from 39% if help was requested to 54% if not (p < 0.01)."
