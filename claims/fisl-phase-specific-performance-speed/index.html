Evidence¶

Qi Liu 2025¶

Qi Liu, Siyu Zhu, Can Liu, Yanmiao Yang, Chunmei Lan, Xinwei Song, Benjamin Becker, Keith M. Kendrick, Xinqi Zhou & Weihua Zhao. (2025). Neural signals gradually shift towards inferior frontal gyrus and long-term effects following facial imitation-based social learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00372-9

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Repeated measures correlation on onset-time of successful imitation in the same 80-participant FISL course: the article reports "a signiﬁcant negative correlation between the onset-time of successful imitation of positive emotion and the number of repetitive learning", with no correlations for negative emotions (ps > 0.065).

"a signiﬁcant negative correlation between the onset-time of successful imitation of positive emotion and the number of repetitive learning during the overall period of FISLs (rrm(879) = -0.105,pFDR = 0.003"

Qi Liu 2025 (2)¶

Qi Liu, Siyu Zhu, Can Liu, Yanmiao Yang, Chunmei Lan, Xinwei Song, Benjamin Becker, Keith M. Kendrick, Xinqi Zhou & Weihua Zhao. (2025). Neural signals gradually shift towards inferior frontal gyrus and long-term effects following facial imitation-based social learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00372-9

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Repeated measures correlations between positive imitation AUC and repetition number: significant overall and "primarily to the early phase FISLs", non-significant for late-phase sessions, supporting a phase-specific dissociation between performance gains and speed gains.

"due pri- marily to the early phase FISLs ( rrm(399) = 0.170, pFDR = 0.003, 95% CI = [0.073, 0.236]) but not the late phase ones ( rrm(399) = 0.055, p = 0.271, 95% CI = [ −0.043, 0.152])"
