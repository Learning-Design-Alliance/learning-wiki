## Evidence

### Pyung-Gang Jung 2017

Pyung-Gang Jung, Kristen L. McMaster, and Robert C. delMas. (2017). Effects of Early Writing Intervention Delivered Within a Data-Based Instruction Framework. Exceptional Children, 83(3), 281–297. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402916667586

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up independent t test for the at-risk subgroup (n = 17 analyzed) on the average of the three standardized residual WJ III subtest scores, with Holm-Bonferroni adjustment. No significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "No statistically significant mean difference was found between conditions for students at risk, t (17) = −0.78, p = .45."
