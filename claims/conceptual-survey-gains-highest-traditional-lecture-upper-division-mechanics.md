## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre/post gains on an instructor-constructed ten-question conceptual survey were 0.74 for TL, 0.62 for SD, and 0.64 for PI, with the TL semester highest. [→ H. Vincent Kuo 2012](#h-vincent-kuo-2012)

## Evidence

### H. Vincent Kuo 2012

H. Vincent Kuo, Patrick B. Kohl, and Lincoln D. Carr. (2012). Socratic Dialogs and Clicker use in an Upper-Division Mechanics Course. [doi:10.1063/1.3680038](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680038)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Three-semester quasi-experimental comparison in one intermediate mechanics course with the same instructor, using matched pre/post samples on an instructor-constructed survey (N = 57, 54, 49). The gains were "0.74, 0.62, and 0.64 for TL (2008), SD (2009), and PI (2010), respectively."

> "The pre/post gain for the three different engagement styles are 0.74, 0.62, and 0.64 for TL (2008), SD (2009), and PI (2010), respectively."
