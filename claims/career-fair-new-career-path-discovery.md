Yes/No responses to survey question #14 among the 86 participants. Themes from the 46 affirmative respondents included technical careers, government/defense roles, and management and consulting, with one participant noting "I could work as a product manager for Google, which is certainly a career path I never considered."

> "approximately half of the participants, 46 (53.49%), indicated that they learned about new career paths or technologies by attending the career fair."
