## Evidence

### Enrique Barra 2023

Enrique Barra, Sonsoles López-Pernas, Aldo Gordillo, Alejandro Pozo, Andres Muñoz-Arcentales, Javier Conde. (2023). Empowering Database Learning through Remote Educational Escape Rooms. IEEE Internet Computing. https://doi.org/10.1109/MIC.2023.3333199

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Performance data automatically recorded by the Escapp web platform for 185 teams (114 pairs, 71 solo students). The authors consider the difficulty well-balanced because almost half the teams succeeded before time ran out.

> "89 teams solved the last puzzle and so succeeded in the escape room, the mean time to solve the complete escape room for these teams was 93.4 minutes (SD =  17.3)."

### Enrique Barra 2023 (2)

Enrique Barra, Sonsoles López-Pernas, Aldo Gordillo, Alejandro Pozo, Andres Muñoz-Arcentales, Javier Conde. (2023). Empowering Database Learning through Remote Educational Escape Rooms. IEEE Internet Computing. https://doi.org/10.1109/MIC.2023.3333199

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Hint-usage analysis from Escapp logs (Table 2): puzzle 3 was solved by 89 teams with 3.44 hints obtained on average (SD 3.3), versus 1.92 for puzzle 1 and 1.18 for puzzle 2, supporting the authors' reading that the final puzzle was hardest.

> "The number of hints obtained and failed to obtain show that the last puzzle was the most complicated one where the students needed more help as it included developing code for an application and not only doing queries."
