## Evidence

### Josephine C. Meyer 2024

Josephine C. Meyer, Gina Passante, and Bethany Wilcox. (2024). Disparities in access to US quantum information education. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010131)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Poisson regression of QIS degree and certificate program counts (Table IV, no overdispersion) found programs "disproportion- ately found at private R1 institutions", with significant disparities among public institutions by state urbanization index and Pell grant percentage.

> "Once again, QIS degree programs are disproportion- ately found at private R1 institutions, and significant dispar- ities are seen among public institutions as to state urbaniza- tion index and percentage of undergraduates receiving a need- based Pell grant."

### Josephine C. Meyer 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The catalog search of institutions with sufficient QIS coursework identified "a total of 45 degree and certificate programs across 35 of the 456 institutions surveyed" as of August-September 2022.

> "We identified a total of 45 degree and certificate programs across 35 of the 456 institutions surveyed."
