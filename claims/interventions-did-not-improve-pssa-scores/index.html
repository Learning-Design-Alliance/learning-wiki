q3 · i? · causal · r2

PSSA scores were collected during the intervention year, capturing roughly the first two-thirds to three-quarters of the interventions. Table 3 shows statistically significant negative PSSA reading and math impacts for the fifth-grade cohort (-27.3 and -28.8 for all interventions combined); the report states the interventions "lowered the reading and mathematics scores" for fifth graders.

"For students in the third-grade cohort, we did not detect significant impacts of the four interventions combined on reading and mathematics test scores from the Pennsylvania System of School Assessment"

  • Four supplemental reading comprehension curricula had no positive impact on fifth-grade students' test scores, with negative impacts in some cases — related
  • Four remedial reading interventions combined improved phonemic decoding, word reading accuracy and fluency, and reading comprehension for third-grade struggling readers one year after the interventions ended, though not on all measures — related
  • The four supplemental reading comprehension curricula had no statistically significant impacts on students one year after the end of their implementation (Cohort 1 follow-up in sixth grade). — related
  • After one school year of implementation (first cohort), the curricula produced no statistically significant positive impacts, and four statistically significant negative impacts were observed. — related
  • For fifth-grade struggling readers, the four interventions combined improved phonemic decoding on one measure but produced a small reduction in oral reading fluency one year after the interventions — related
  • Younger struggling readers benefited more: the interventions generally helped third-grade cohort students more than fifth-grade cohort students — related
