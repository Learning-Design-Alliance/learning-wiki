## Evidence

### Rosendahl 2024

Rosendahl, M., Gill, B., & Starling, J. E. (2024). Stabilizing school performance indicators in New Jersey to reduce the effect of random error (REL 2025–009). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/stabilizing-school-performance-indicators-new-jersey-reduce-effect-random-error

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive correlational analysis of NJDOE aggregated school subgroup–indicator data (2015/16–2021/22, excluding 2019/20 and 2020/21), grouped by subgroup, indicator, and year. The article reports "average correlation –0.28" with group size and "average correlation 0.52" with absolute z-score, and about 82 percent of stabilized z-scores changed by less than 0.5 standard deviation.

> "The amount of adjustment was negatively correlated with student group size (average correlation –0.28) and positively correlated with the absolute value of the z-score (average correlation 0.52) for all subgroup, indicator, and year combinations, as expected."
