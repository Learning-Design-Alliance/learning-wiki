### Kavas Mustafa Volkan; Demirören Meral; Koşan Ayşen Melek Aytuğ; Karahan Süleyman Tuna; Yalim Neyyire Yasemin 2015

Kavas Mustafa Volkan; Demirören Meral; Koşan Ayşen Melek Aytuğ; Karahan Süleyman Tuna; Yalim Neyyire Yasemin. (2015). Turkish students' perceptions of professionalism at the beginning and at the end of medical education: a cross-sectional qualitative study. Medical Education Online. [doi:10.3402/meo.v20.26614](https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.26614)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Across all focus groups, students described learning professionalism by imitating residents and faculty, e.g. a G6 intern who 'did whatever she did' in an out-patient clinic. The discussion adds that 'The influence of role models is virtually continuous', though unsupervised, and that the process should be formalized institutionally.

> "By observing the practitioners' behaviors, medical students comprehend how a certain moral position is realized and learn about the various dimensions of their future professional lives"
