## Evidence

### Knox S 2015

Knox S, Cullen W, Dunne CP. (2015). A national study of Continuous Professional Competence (CPC) amongst pre-hospital practitioners. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-1197-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey item on sanctions for CPC non-compliance. 39% of Paramedics/APs agreed with downgrading registration for failure to meet CPC requirements while 23% disagreed; the abstract reports 78% of EMTs believed persistent failure should mandate denial of registration.

> "39 % (307) of Paramedics/APs agreed with the suggestion that those who fail to meet CPC requirements should be allowed to register only at the level below their current registration, but 23 % (179) did not support that proposition."
