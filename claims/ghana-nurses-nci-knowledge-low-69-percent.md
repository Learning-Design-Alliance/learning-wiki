Salu Samuel, Okyere Joshua, Charles-Unadike Veronica Okwuchi, Ananga Mark Kwame. (2023). Nurses’ knowledge on nosocomial infections preventive measures and its associated factors in Ghana: a cross-sectional study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-023-09942-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 237 nurses in the Hohoe Municipality, Ghana (July–August 2021). A composite knowledge score from eleven items classified nurses scoring below the mean as not knowledgeable; "164 (69.2%) of the participants were not knowledgeable".

> "The findings from this study revealed that 164 (69.2%) of the participants were not knowledgeable on the preventive measures of NCIs (Fig. 1)."
