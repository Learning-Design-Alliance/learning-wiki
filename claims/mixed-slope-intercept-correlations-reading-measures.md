# Kindergarten-to-fifth-grade slope-intercept correlations varied across reading measures, showing cumulative, compensatory, and stable patterns for different measures

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i2`–`i3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` WID-decoding showed a significant positive intercept-slope correlation (r = .42), indicating a cumulative growth pattern. [→ Little 2021](#little-2021)
`q3 i2` WA-decoding and TOSREC-reading comprehension showed significant negative intercept-slope correlations (r = −.21 and −.48), indicating compensatory growth patterns. [→ Little 2021 (2)](#little-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Little 2021

Little, C. W., Lonigan, C. J., & Phillips, B. M. (2021). Differential Patterns of Growth in Reading and Math Skills during Elementary School. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(3), 462-476. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000635

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Univariate latent-growth-curve model across all available K-5 time points for WID-decoding in the 921-child sample. The study reports r = .42, p < .001 for the intercept-slope correlation, "indicating a cumulative growth pattern" of decoding on initial decoding status.

> "The correlation between intercept and slope for WID- decoding was significant and positive (r = .42, p < .001), indicating a cumulative growth pattern in which decoding at kindergarten was positively associated with how developmental changes in decoding performance occurred through fifth grade"

### Little 2021 (2)

Little, C. W., Lonigan, C. J., & Phillips, B. M. (2021). Differential Patterns of Growth in Reading and Math Skills during Elementary School. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(3), 462-476. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000635

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same K-5 univariate latent-growth-curve analyses for WA-decoding and TOSREC-reading comprehension. The study reports significant negative intercept-slope correlations (r = −.21, p = .034; r = −.48, p < .001), "indicating a compensatory growth pattern" on these measures.

> "For WA-decoding and TOSREC-reading comprehension, the correlations between intercept and slope were significantly moderate and negative (r = −.21, p = .034 and −.48, p < .001, respectively), indicating a compensatory growth pattern or a negative influence from initial status to change in these skills over time"
