## Evidence

### Kong Kam Wa Terry T 2024

Kong Kam Wa Terry T, Mema B. (2024). Mental Practice to Maintain Procedural Competency of Faculty with Decreased Opportunities. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.34197/ats-scholar.2023-0150IN

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview-based thematic analysis at one tertiary PCCM center found less frequent use of MP for step-by-step task rehearsal than for strategy rehearsal; participants described visualization such as "going through it sequentially in your mind".

> "We found that fewer faculty used MP for task purposes, to rehearse the steps of a procedure, to remember its execution, or to maintain their technical aspects of the performance."
