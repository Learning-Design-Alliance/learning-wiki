## Evidence

### Çevikbaş Pınar 2026

Çevikbaş Pınar, Açıksarı Kurtuluş, Karahaliloğlu Cihad Ünsal. (2026). PHTLS training increases knowledge but paradoxically decreases self-efficacy in emergency healthcare professionals: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09149-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of 48 PHTLS-trained vs 50 non-trained emergency professionals using a 50-item PHTLS curriculum test (Cronbach's alpha = 0.82), analyzed with Mann-Whitney U. The study reports a "large effect size (Cohen’s d = 1.15)" with Hedges' g = 1.147 (95% CI: 0.722–1.573).

> "The PHTLS-trained group achieved significantly higher scores on the knowledge assessment test compared to the non-trained group (mean score 70.46 ± 14.68 vs. 55.28 ± 11.44). This difference was not only statistically significant (p < 0.001) but also represented a large effect size (Cohen’s d = 1.15)"
