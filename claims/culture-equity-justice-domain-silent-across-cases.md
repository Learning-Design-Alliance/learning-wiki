`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Within- and cross-case analysis of interview data coded against the Competencies for Occupational Therapists in Canada. The article reports "Culture, equity, and justice as a domain was not addressed in two out of the three placement cases," with only virtual care referencing access to care and resources.

> "Culture, equity, and justice as a domain was not addressed in two out of the three placement cases. The preceptors in intrapreneurship placement cases and preceptors, as well as learners, in administration placement cases did not explicitly identify this as a competency that was developed."
