## Evidence

### Michael Vaden-Kiernan 2015

Michael Vaden-Kiernan, Geoffrey Borman, Sarah Caverly, Nance Bell, Veronica Ruiz de Castilla, and Kate Sullivan. (2015). Preliminary Findings from a Multi-Year Scale-Up Effectiveness Trial of Everyday Mathematics. SREE Spring 2015 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562455

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preliminary year-one ITT analysis of a multi-site cluster-randomized trial: 4,520 K and grade 3 students in 48 schools analyzed with a three-level model including school-mean GMADE pretest as covariate. Table 2 prints the treatment coefficient 0.705 (SE 0.577, t = 1.220, p = 0.229). The article reports "positive, but it is not statistically significant"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The effect of assignment to the treatment condition is positive, but it is not statistically significant."
