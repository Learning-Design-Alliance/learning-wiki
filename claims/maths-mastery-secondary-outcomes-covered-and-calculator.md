## Evidence

### Jerrim et al. 2015

John Jerrim, Helen Austerberry, Cosette Crisan, Anne Ingold, Candia Morgan, Dave Pratt, Cathy Smith, Meg Wiggins. (2015). Mathematics Mastery: Secondary Evaluation Report. Education Endowment Foundation. https://doi.org/10.1186/ISRCTN70922140

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary-outcome analyses of the RCT comparing scores on curriculum-covered questions and calculator-section questions. Around 40% of PiM questions covered material not in the Year 7 curriculum; the report reports more progress on covered material and no negative calculator side-effects, without printing effect sizes.

> "Pupils made more progress in material that was focused on within the programme. Despite Mathematics Mastery not covering calculator use within its Year 7 syllabus, no negative side-effects were observed for this aspect of children's maths skills."
