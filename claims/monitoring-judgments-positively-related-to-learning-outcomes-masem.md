### Louise David 2024

Louise David, Felicitas Biwer, Martine Baars, Lisette Wijnia, Fred Paas, Anique de Bruin. (2024). The Relation Between Perceived Mental Effort, Monitoring Judgments, and Learning Outcomes: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09903-z

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

MASEM full mediation model on 77 correlations between monitoring and learning outcomes from the pooled corpus of 83 independent samples. The article reports "a positive parameter estimate between monitoring judgments and learning outcomes was found β = 0.29".

> "In support of Hypothesis 2, a positive parameter estimate between monitoring judgments and learning outcomes was found β = 0.29, 95% LBCI [0.22; 0.36], indicating that with each unit increase in monitoring judgments, learning outcomes increased by 0.29 units on average."
