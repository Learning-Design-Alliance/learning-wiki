`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 500 undergraduate medical students from nine Syrian medical schools (54.0% female), June 2025. Descriptive results show "the mean CPR knowledge score was 5.03 ± 2.50 out of 11" and MI knowledge 11.50 ± 2.67 of 18. Item-level analysis found only 18.6% correct on when to use an AED.

> "Overall, the mean CPR knowledge score was 5.03 ± 2.50 out of 11, and the mean myocardial infarction (MI) symptom knowledge score was 11.50 ± 2.67 out of 18"
