## Evidence

### Justyna P. Zwolak 2018

Justyna P. Zwolak, Remy Dou and Eric Brewe. (2018). Student perceptions of the value of out-of-class interactions: Attitudes vs. Practice. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.115](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.115)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of MI physics students using the SEQ: Fall 2016 (N = 80) and Spring 2017 (N = 82) responses on attitudes toward learning with peers were compared. The authors report responses "do not differ signiﬁcantly" per the two-sample test of proportions; no effect size is printed.

> "We find that responses between the two semesters do not differ signiﬁcantly (as veriﬁed by the two-sample test of proportions [6])."

### Justyna P. Zwolak 2018 (2)

Justyna P. Zwolak, Remy Dou and Eric Brewe. (2018). Student perceptions of the value of out-of-class interactions: Attitudes vs. Practice. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.115](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.115)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spring 2017 respondents were split into students with one semester of MI (N = 19) and two semesters (N = 63); attitude responses showed "no signiﬁcant differences in responses between the two groups." No effect size is printed.

> "As can be seen in Fig. 3, there are no signiﬁcant differences in responses between the two groups."
