## Evidence

### Koops Willem JM 2012

Koops Willem JM, van der Vleuten Cees PM, de Leng Bas A, Snoeckx Luc HEH. (2012). Computer supported collaborative learning in a clerkship: an exploratory study on the relation of discussion activity and revision of critical appraisal papers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-79

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Content analysis using the validated Rainbow system classified postings into outside, non-task-focussed and task-focussed activity; task-focussed units totalled 760 (revised, n = 24) versus 385 (unrevised, n = 23), Mann–Whitney U P < .000, d = 1.17. Relative percentages were similar in both groups.

> "Task-focussed activity was relatively high in both revised (73.9%), and unrevised paper discussions (69.5%), but, in absolute terms, the task-focussed activity in the revised paper discussions was double as high as in the unrevised paper discussions (P < .000)."
