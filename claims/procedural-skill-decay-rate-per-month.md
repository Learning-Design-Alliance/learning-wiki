## Evidence

### Tatel 2025

Tatel, C. E., & Ackerman, P. L. (2025). Procedural Skill Retention and Decay: A Meta-Analytic Review. Psychological Bulletin. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/bul0000481

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 1,344 effect sizes from 457 reports using random-effects meta-regression with retention interval as a continuous predictor of effect size. The article reports that "mean differences in SD units grew stronger by 0.08/month" for accuracy and 0.06/month for speed and mixed measures.

> "Results indicated that mean differences in SD units grew stronger by 0.08/month for accuracy-based performance measures and 0.06/month for speed-based and mixed performance measures."
