## Evidence

### Mintesnot Helen 2024

Mintesnot Helen, Tadele Henok, Ahmed Hayat, Moges Tamirat, Gedlu Etsegenet. (2024). Electrocardiography interpretation competency among pediatric and child health residents at Addis Ababa University, Ethiopia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06614-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 95 pediatric and child health residents at Addis Ababa University using 10 ECG Wave-Maven strips. The study reports that "Common emergent ECG conditions were rarely diagnosed correctly", with the printed percentages for each condition. No effect size is printed.

> "Common emergent ECG conditions were rarely diagnosed correctly: long QTC syndrome, 42.1%; Complete heart block (CHB), 10.5%; supraventricular tachycardia (SVT), 9.5%; ventricular tachycardia, 8.4% and hyperkalemia, 4.2%."
