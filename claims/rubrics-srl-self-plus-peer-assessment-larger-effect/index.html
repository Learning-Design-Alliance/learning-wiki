Evidence¶

Panadero 2023¶

Panadero, Ernesto; Jonsson, Anders; Pinedo, Leire; Fernández-Castilla, Belén. (2023). Effects of Rubrics on Academic Performance, Self-Regulated Learning, and self-Efficacy: a Meta-analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09823-4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis of 17 SRL effect sizes from five studies (Table 2): the both-assessments category pooled g = 0.532 versus g = 0.114 for self-assessment only, with a printed significant difference (difference = 0.418, SE = 0.086, t = 4.88, p = 0.030).

"Only one moderator variable explained part of the observed variability: when self-assessment and peer-assessment were used, the overall effect was significantly larger than when only self-assessment was used"
