## Evidence

### Dabaliz Al-Awwab 2017

Dabaliz Al-Awwab, Kaadan Samy, Dabbagh M. Marwan, Barakat Abdulaziz, Shareef Mohammad Abrar, Al-Tannir Mohamad, Obeidat Akef, Mohamed Ayman. (2017). Predictive validity of pre-admission assessments on medical student performance. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5a10.04e1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A multiple linear regression predicted preclinical PT performance from the five pre-admission variables in the retrospective dataset. It "revealed no significant predictors of PT performance among any of the five pre-admission variables"; the clinical-years PT model could not be run due to insufficient data.

> "The preclinical regression model analysis revealed no significant predictors of PT performance among any of the five pre-admission variables included in the analysis; with an overall regression equation (F(5,4)=0.692, p=0.657), and with an R2 of 0.464"
