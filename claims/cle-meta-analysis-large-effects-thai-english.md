## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Across 76 graduate studies (1989-2004), CLE students showed much higher achievement in language performance than traditional-instruction students, with effect sizes including d = 2.12 (kindergarten), d = 1.95 (Thai model 1), d = 1.51 (Thai model 2), d = 0.60 (Thai model 3), and d = 8.55 (English model 1). ## Evidence

### Rattanavich 2015

Rattanavich, S. (2015). Concentrated Language Encounter Approach in Practice for Global Teaching of Literacy: Lighthouse Strategy Implementation. International Education Studies, 8(7), 21. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v8n7p21

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of 76 graduate students' research studies on CLE instruction in Thai and English at kindergarten, primary, and secondary levels during 1989 to 2004, with dependent variables on language performance, attitudes, and personality development. The Thai-language results print "98.30% (d = 2.12)" for kindergarten down to "72.57% (d = 0.60)" for grades five to six; the English program printed percentages and d values range up to d = 8.55.

> "98.30% (d = 2.12) for the kindergarten level, 97.44% (d = 1.95) for grades one to two (model 1), 93.45% (d = 1.51) for grades three to four (Model 2), and 72.57% (d = 0.60) for grades five to six (Model 3) in the Thai language program"
