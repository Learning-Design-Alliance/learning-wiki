## Evidence

### Cortés-Rodríguez 2024

Cortés-Rodríguez, López-Rodríguez, Roman, Granero-Molina, Fernández-Sola and Hernández-Padilla. (2024). Effect of two teaching methods on nursing students' acquisition of patient-centered communication competence in older people care: a cluster randomized trial. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1510620

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

McNemar tests on pre-test versus 6-week retention-test benchmark attainment showed significant gains "regardless of the intervention"; overall competence rose from 2% to 42% (simulation) and 3% to 52% (role-play).

> "Statistically significant differences between pre-test and retention-test measures were found in the three components of the competence as well as in overall patient-centered communication in older people care competence, regardless of the intervention (p < 0.05)."
