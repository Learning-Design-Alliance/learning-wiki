### Weber Joanne 2026

Weber Joanne, Enwedo Jah-Amaka, Benkohila Amel, Kurylowich Nyla, Hillcox Brenda, Olson Kirsten. (2026). Story dramas: an exploratory study toward a deaf-led arts-integration model. Journal of Deaf Studies and Deaf Education. https://doi.org/10.1093/jdsade/enag015

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Instructor interview data: a deaf instructor reported that story dramas let her see students' visual language in natural interaction and document it on a visual sign language checklist. The article notes formal assessments are "usually context-independent and less meaningful to the students being assessed".

> "Through the drama, in the classroom, I see them interacting in a natural way so that helps me see more of what their visual language is, and I can document that on their 'visual sign language checklist.'"
