Evidence¶

Wu SS 2012¶

Wu SS, Barth M, Amin H, Malcarne V and Menon V (2012) Math anxiety in second and third graders and its relation to mathematics achievement. Front. Psychology 3:162. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00162

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-sectional correlational study of 162 second and third graders using hierarchical regression controlling for CBCL trait anxiety and WASI FSIQ. The article reports "β =− 0.26, t (126) =− 2.62, p = 0.01, R2 = 0.43" for the Math Composite, shown in Figure 1. No standardized effect size beyond the regression statistics is printed.

"When controlling for these two variables, math anxiety was still significantly and negatively correlated with math achievement [Math Composite score; β =− 0.26, t (126) =− 2.62, p = 0.01, R2 = 0.43; Figure 1]."
