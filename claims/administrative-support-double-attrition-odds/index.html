Sutcher 2016¶

Sutcher, L., Darling-Hammond, L., & Carver-Thomas, D. (2016). A coming crisis in teaching? Teacher supply, demand, and shortages in the U.S. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/coming-crisis-teaching

The report's analysis of Schools and Staffing Survey and Teacher Follow-Up Survey data (Figure 28 relates turnover probability to administrative support) found "teachers who find their administrators to be unsupportive are more than twice as likely to leave as those who feel well-supported."
