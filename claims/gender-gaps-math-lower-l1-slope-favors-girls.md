## Evidence

### Anna Widlund 2023

Anna Widlund, Markku Niemivirta, Heta Tuominen, Johan Korhonen. (2023). Growth Trajectories of Self-Concept and Interest in Mathematics and Language – Individual Differences and Cross-Domain Relations. Preprint version 1. https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5563-5044

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple-group growth model (N = 612) compared boys and girls; Wald tests showed "girls' initial levels of mathematics self-concept (𝑥2 = 44.518, p < 0.01) and interest (𝑥2 = 15.345, p < 0.01) were significantly lower than for boys"; L1 self-concept slope (𝑥2 = 6.376) and L1 interest intercept (𝑥2 = 9.399) favored girls. No effect sizes printed.

> "The Wald test revealed that girls' initial levels of mathematics self-concept (𝑥2 = 44.518, p < 0.01) and interest (𝑥2 = 15.345, p < 0.01) were significantly lower than for boys, but there were no significant differences in the slopes of either self-concept or interest in math."
