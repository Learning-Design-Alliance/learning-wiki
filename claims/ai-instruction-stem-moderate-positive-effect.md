Random effects model meta-analysis of 35 experimental and quasi-experimental studies of AI-supported instruction in STEM, with Hedges' g calculated from post-test means, standard deviations, and sample sizes. The article reports "g = 0.670 (95% CI [0.491, 0.848])" with Z = 7.35, p < 0.001.

> "Under this model, the effect size was found to be g = 0.670 (95% CI [0.491, 0.848]), with Z = 7.35 and p < 0.001, while the between-study variance was accounted for more appropriately under the REM (I2 = 15.75%)."

### Doğan Yunus 2026 (2)

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Fixed-effect analysis of the same 35 studies quantified between-study inconsistency with the Q test and I2 statistic, showing "a significant and high level of heterogeneity", which the authors say means effect sizes do not display a homogeneous distribution.
