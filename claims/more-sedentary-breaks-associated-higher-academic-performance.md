## Evidence

### Bueno Maria Raquel de Oliveira 2022

Bueno Maria Raquel de Oliveira, Werneck André de Oliveira, da Silva Danilo Rodrigues Pereira, Oyeyemi Adewale Luqman, Zambrin Lidyane Ferreira, Fernandes Romulo Araújo, Serassuelo Helio Junior, Romanzini Marcelo, Ronque Enio Ricardo Vaz. (2022). Association between patterns of sedentary time and academic performance in adolescents: the mediating role of self-concept. Revista Paulista de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1590/1984-0462/2022/40/2021106IN

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the adjusted regression models of the cross-sectional accelerometry study, "a greater number of breaks on ST were associated with higher academic performance", with breaks expressed as mean frequency per hour of sedentary time and models run separately by sex.

> "Similarly, a greater number of breaks on ST were associated with higher academic performance, while longer bouts (≥30 minutes) of ST were associated with a lower academic performance in both sexes"
