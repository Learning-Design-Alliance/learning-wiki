Analysis of the fraction of grades below C- (Table IX) finds "students from underrepresented groups are 83% ± 24% more likely to be pushed from a passing grade under CLASP4 to a failing grade under CLASP10 than are their peers", with 11.6% of URM students shifted versus 6.33% of non-URM students.
