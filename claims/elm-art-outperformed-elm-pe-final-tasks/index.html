Evidence¶

Gerhard Weber & Peter Brusilovsky 2016¶

Gerhard Weber & Peter Brusilovsky. (2016). ELM-ART – An Interactive and Intelligent Web-Based Electronic Textbook. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0066-8

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Investigation of ELM-ART's problem solving support comparing performance in final programming tasks, attempted without cognitive diagnosis help, against ELM-PE. The article reports students "performed better in the final programming tasks" and highlights lower prior-knowledge students as profiting; no statistics or effect sizes are printed.

"An investigation of the problem solving support showed that students performed better in the final programming tasks (without help from the cognitive diagnosis) compared to ELM-PE. Especially, students with lower levels of previous programming knowledge profited from learning with ELM-ART."
