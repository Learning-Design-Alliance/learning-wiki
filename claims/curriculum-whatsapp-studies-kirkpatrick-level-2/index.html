Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • WhatsApp's most cited benefit is enabling learning anytime and anywhere, but intrusiveness, message volume and cognitive load impede learning
  • Educational interventions for FM residents targeting the informal curriculum rarely produced behavior change: most were evaluated only at Kirkpatrick level 2 and study quality was low
  • Seven studies totalling 647 learners reported improved knowledge after WhatsApp-based learning, though methodological weaknesses were apparent
  • Evidence on transfer to clinical behavior and patient outcomes is scarce: only 2 studies reached Kirkpatrick level 3 and 1 reached level 4 — a broader claim this one bears on
