## Evidence

### Puolakanaho 2018

Puolakanaho, A., Lappalainen, R., Lappalainen, P., Muotka, J. S., Hirvonen, R., Eklund, K. M., Ahonen, T. P. S., & Kiuru, N. (2018). Reducing Stress and Enhancing Academic Buoyancy among Adolescents Using a Brief Web-based Program Based on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-018-0973-8

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Per-protocol analysis of the combined intervention group (n = 123) versus control (n = 82). Table 5 reports corrected between-group Cohen's d of "0.27 for buoyancy", marked significant; the within-intervention-group buoyancy effect was d = 0.28 while the control group's was 0.01.

> "The between-group effect sizes were small (Cohen’s d= 0.22 for stress, d= 0.18 for school stress, and d= 0.27 for buoyancy)"
