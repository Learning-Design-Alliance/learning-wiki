### Su Weilin 2026

Su Weilin, Chen Chen. (2026). The mediating role of academic engagement and the moderating role of academic self-efficacy in the relationship between critical thinking and academic competence: evidence from Chinese college students. BMC Psychology, 14, 700. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04446-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression (Model 6) in a three-wave study of 363 Chinese college students controlling for gender and grade. The article reports the direct effect with β = 0.287, p < 0.001, "confirming that Chinese college students’ critical thinking can directly improve their academic competence"; no standardised effect size is printed for this path.

> "The results of Model 6 demonstrate that critical thinking exerts a statistically significant positive effect on academic competence (β = 0.287, p < 0.001), confirming that Chinese college students’ critical thinking can directly improve their academic competence."
