Analytical limiting cases of the receding-image solution for a monopole moving parallel to the sheet (Section III); in the high-speed case the eddy current "is thus circular", analogous to a stationary monopole above a superconductor.

> "At low speed, that single image charge has charge , and the field is solely due to the source charge.  That is, the field of the moving monopole penetrates through the conducting plane."
