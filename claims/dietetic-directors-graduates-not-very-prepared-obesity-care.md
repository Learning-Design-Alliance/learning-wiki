Watowicz Rosanna P., Krishnan Varsha, Butsch W. Scott. (2024). A cross-sectional survey benchmarking the coverage and importance of obesity-related competencies in U.S. dietetic supervised practice programs. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11622600/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of supervised practice directors asked how prepared students are to provide nutrition care for patients with obesity upon program completion. Descriptive frequencies: "'fairly prepared'" 53% (n=19), "'very prepared'" 33% (n=12); none reported 'not at all prepared' and 14% 'somewhat prepared'.

> "more than half (53%, n = 19) of the supervised practice directors reported their students are ‘fairly prepared’ and one-third of directors (33%, n = 12) reported that their students are ‘very prepared’"
