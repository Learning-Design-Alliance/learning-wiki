## Subclaims
`q3 i0` For pupils with low prior attainment, event incentives raised GCSE Maths points scores by an estimated 0.13 standard deviations, significant at the 5% level. [→ Sibieta et al. 2014](#sibieta-et-al-2014)

## Evidence

### Sibieta et al. 2014

Luke Sibieta, Ellen Greaves, Barbara Sianesi. (2014). Increasing Pupil Motivation: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581249

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Sub-group analysis of the cluster RCT using fully-interacted linear matching for 2,619 pupils with low prior attainment (below Level 4 at Key Stage 2) in the event-incentive arm. Table 2 reports an effect size of 0.13 (0.02, 0.24), the only statistically significant attainment result in the trial. The report notes evidence ratings are not provided for sub-group analyses.

> "The estimated impact for this incentive treatment is 0.13, significant at the 5% level, which is the equivalent of approximately two months’ progress, or one quarter of a grade in GCSE Maths."
