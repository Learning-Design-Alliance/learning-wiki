# The Wilson Reading System shows potentially positive effects on alphabetics for beginning readers, based on a single randomized controlled trial

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` A randomized controlled trial of 71 third-grade students found statistically significant effects of the Wilson Reading System on WRMT-R word identification and word attack subtests, with a domain average effect size of 0.33. [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2007](#what-works-clearinghouse-2007)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2007

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Wilson Reading System®. What Works Clearinghouse, Beginning Reading. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/546

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

WWC review of the Torgesen et al. (2006) randomized controlled trial of 71 third-grade students in eight Pennsylvania school units. Findings table shows TOWRE phonetic decoding efficiency effect size 0.38 (statistically significant), WRMT-R word attack 0.58 (statistically significant), and a "domain average" effect size of 0.33, with improvement index +13 (range +6 to +22).

> "The authors reported statistically significant effects of the Wilson Reading System® on two of these outcomes (WRMT–R word identification and word attack sub- tests). The statistical significance of these findings was confirmed by the WWC."

### What Works Clearinghouse 2007 (2)

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Wilson Reading System®. What Works Clearinghouse, Beginning Reading. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/546

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The alphabetics findings table reports the WRMT-R word attack subtest with Wilson group mean 103.10 versus comparison 94.30, mean difference 8.80, effect size 0.58, statistically significant, improvement index +22. The TOWRE-PDE subtest showed effect size 0.38, also statistically significant.

> "WRMT–R: Word Attack subtest Grade 3 8/71 103.10 (15.00) 94.30 (15.00) 8.80 0.58 Statistically significant +22"
