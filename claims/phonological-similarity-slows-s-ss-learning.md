# Phonologically similar pairs are learned with lower accuracy and slower reaction-time learning than dissimilar pairs, in both groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Overall accuracy was lower for phonologically similar-matching (SM) than dissimilar-matching (DM) pairs. [→ Cao 2026](#cao-2026)
`q3 i3` The RT learning rate was significantly slower for SM than DM pairs. [→ Cao 2026 (2)](#cao-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Cao 2026

Cao, Y., Zhang, M., Gentile, F. & Bonte, M. (2026). Dynamic behavioral and neural correlates of letter-speech sound learning in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00410-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on accuracy in the S-SS learning task with 62 adults showed "a significant main effect of Condition" with η²P = .218, indicating lower accuracy for phonologically similar-matching pairs; the effect held for both groups.

> "a significant main effect of Condition (F(1, 60) = 16.647, p < .001, η²P = .218) indicated an overall lower accuracy for phonologically SM pairs."

### Cao 2026 (2)

Cao, Y., Zhang, M., Gentile, F. & Bonte, M. (2026). Dynamic behavioral and neural correlates of letter-speech sound learning in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00410-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Power-curve fitting of RT trajectories showed "the learning rate (α) was significantly slower for SM pairs compared to DM pairs", Δ = .03, p = .001, η²P = .167; accuracy learning rate α showed no significant effects.

> "the learning rate (α) was significantly slower for SM pairs compared to DM pairs, with a mean difference (Δ) of .03 (p = .001) and a significant main effect of Condition (F(1, 60) = 12.006, p = .001, η²P = .167)"
