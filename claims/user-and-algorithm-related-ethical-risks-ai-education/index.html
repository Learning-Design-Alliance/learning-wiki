Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever 2026¶

Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever (2026). Understanding ethical dimensions of AI in higher education: insights from faculty members and students. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00601-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative theme from faculty interviews. Under user-related risks participants cited academic integrity violations and lack of ethical oversight; under "Algorithm-Related Ethical Risks", they highlighted algorithmic manipulation, information reliability, privacy and data security, and algorithmic bias.

Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever 2026 (2)¶

Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever (2026). Understanding ethical dimensions of AI in higher education: insights from faculty members and students. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00601-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative theme from student interviews mirroring the faculty structure. Students cited academic integrity violations and lack of oversight as user-related risks, and "the production of false information", algorithmic manipulation, privacy and data security, algorithmic bias, and intellectual property violations as algorithm-related risks.

"Students also expressed ethical risks in a similar manner, based on users and algorithms"
