## Evidence

### van Det M. J. 2011

van Det M. J., Meijerink W. J. H. J., Hoff C., Middel L. J., Koopal S. A., Pierie J. P. E. N. (2011). The learning effect of intraoperative video-enhanced surgical procedure training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-010-1545-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled trial in which ten surgical residents each performed six laparoscopic cholecystectomies, randomized to INVEST or MAM after equal SIMENDO basic-skills preparation. The Results section reports that "absolute and relative skill improvements on the complete OSATS global rating scale were also significantly higher in the INVEST group".

> "The absolute and relative skill improvements on the complete OSATS global rating scale were also significantly higher in the INVEST group (Table 1)."
