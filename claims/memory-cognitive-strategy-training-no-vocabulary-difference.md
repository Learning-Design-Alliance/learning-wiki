## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Banisaeid 2013

Maryam Banisaeid. (2013). Comparative Effect of Memory and Cognitive Strategies Training on EFL Intermediate Learners' Vocabulary Learning. English Language Teaching, 6(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n8p108

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-groups t-test comparing post-test scores of two randomly assigned experimental groups of 30 learners each after 11 sessions of training; the memory group mean was 48.16 (SD 7.16) versus 45.10 (SD 7.94) for the cognitive group, with "t=1/570, df=58, p= 0.122". Equivalence was not tested.

> "by the achieved data ( t=1/570, df=58, p= 0.122), there is not any significan ce difference between the effect of cognitive strategies training and memory strategies training on intermediate EFL learners' vocabulary learning"
