### Yuan-Hsuan Lee and Jiun-Yu Wu 2026

Yuan-Hsuan Lee and Jiun-Yu Wu. (2026). Differential engagement with generative artificial intelligence in higher education: Gender, motivation, and achievement trajectories. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00615-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis tests across the four achievement profiles found significant differences in total StatBot use, H(3) = 11.49, p = .009, ε2 = .091; Bonferroni-adjusted post hoc comparisons showed Growing Achievers (Median = 27.5) used the chatbot significantly more than Declining Performers (Median = 4).

> "The Kruskal–Wallis test for Total Inquiry Count was significant, H(3) = 11.49, p = .009. The resulting effect size, ε2 = .091, indicates a medium-sized effect, suggesting that cluster membership explained approximately 9% of the variance in total usage ranks."
