## Evidence

### Rogers and Feller 2016

Todd Rogers, Avi Feller. (2016). Parent Beliefs and Student Absences: Large Absence-Reduction Field Experiment. SREE Spring 2016 Conference Abstract. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2016/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Assignment to the Encourage + Self condition assessed the impact of being sent at least one Encourage + Self mailing; households received an average of 3.9 mailings with Self information. The article reports an additional 0.5-day reduction versus Encourage, p<0.01.

> "The Self component reduced absences by an additional 0.5 days as compared to Encourage (p<0.01). In other words, the Self component reduced absences by a total of 6.2% as compared to receiving no mail at all (i.e. Control)."
