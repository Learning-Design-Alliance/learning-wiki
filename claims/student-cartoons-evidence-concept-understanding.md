## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Cartoons completed by students on partly-finished templates expressed perfectionism concepts such as orderliness, self-criticism, and avoidance of situations where success is not guaranteed. [→ Zousel 2013](#zousel-2013)

## Evidence

### Zousel 2013

Zousel, M. L., Rule, A. C., & Logan, S. R. (2013). Teaching Primary Grade Students Perfectionism through Cartoons Compared to Bibliotherapy. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(2), 199-218. www.iejee.com. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070469

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of student-completed cartoons from the experimental condition, made with templates from MakeBeliefsComix. The authors state "The cartoons made by students were evidence that they understood some of the perfectionism concepts being taught in the experimental condition".

> "Two other students, responding to another cartoon template, expressed additional behaviors of perfectionists – being self -critical (the upper cartoon) and having the tendency to avoid situations in which success is not guaranteed (the lower cartoon)."
