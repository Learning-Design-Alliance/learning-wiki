Effects of Argument-based Science Inquiry (ABSI) Approach Used in the Unit of "Reproduction Growth Development in Living Beings" on the Success Levels of Students. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2379-2383. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061037

Content analysis of tape-recorded lessons in the experimental group, transcribed and converted to tables, showed "the experimental group students' turn- taking frequency was 71%" against 29% for teachers; the article reports the teacher acted as a guide.
