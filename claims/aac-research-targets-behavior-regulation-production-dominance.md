## Evidence

### Ganz 2022

Ganz, J. B., Pustejovsky, J. E., Reichle, J., Vannest, K. J., Foster, M., Fuller, M. C., Pierson, L. M., Wattanawongwan, S., Bernal, A. J., Chen, M., Haas, A. N., Skov, R., Smith, S. D., & Yllades, V. (2022). Augmentative and Alternative Communication Intervention Targets for School-Aged Participants with ASD and ID: a Single-Case Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Review Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders.

`q3 · i?`

Descriptive synthesis of coded communicative functions across the 114 included studies. The article reports "the majority of the participants’ interventions involved communication functions of behavioral regulation (51%)" and calls this pattern somewhat problematic because behavior regulation is an easier target.

> "the majority of the participants’ interventions involved communication functions of behavioral regulation (51%) rather than joint attention (19%), social interaction (16%), or multiple communication functions (10%)"

### Ganz 2022 (2)

Ganz, J. B., Pustejovsky, J. E., Reichle, J., Vannest, K. J., Foster, M., Fuller, M. C., Pierson, L. M., Wattanawongwan, S., Bernal, A. J., Chen, M., Haas, A. N., Skov, R., Smith, S. D., & Yllades, V. (2022). Augmentative and Alternative Communication Intervention Targets for School-Aged Participants with ASD and ID: a Single-Case Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Review Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders.

`q3 · i?`

Coding of comprehension versus production outcomes across included studies. The article calls the near-exclusive production focus problematic, citing evidence that comprehension gains occur even when production is the intervention objective.

> "Most studies focused only on communication production (k = 107), rather than comprehension (k = 5), or both production and comprehension (k = 6)."
