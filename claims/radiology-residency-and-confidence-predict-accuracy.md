Multivariable logistic regression in the 205-physician survey predicting first-quartile diagnostic accuracy; unadjusted factors included interest in and elective rotation in diagnostic radiology and adequate training, but only the two named predictors remained independent.

> "Multivariable logistic regression analysis revealed that being a diagnostic radiology resident (OR: 13.0; 95% CI: 2.5–67.7) and having higher diagnostic confidence (OR: 2.2; 95% CI: 1.3–3.8) were the only independent predictors of achieving high diagnostic accuracy."
