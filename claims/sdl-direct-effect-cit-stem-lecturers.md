PLS-SEM analysis of survey data from 408 STEM lecturers at seven public universities in Yunnan, China. The structural model shows "a significant relationship between SDL and CIT (β = 0.210, p < 0.001)" with the 95% CI excluding zero; f² = 0.054, a small effect.
