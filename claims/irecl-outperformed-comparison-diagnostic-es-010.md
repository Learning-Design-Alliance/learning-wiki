### i-Ready Classroom Mathematics 2024

i-Ready Classroom Mathematics. (2024). Curriculum Associates. https://www.curriculumassociates.com/professional-development

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A multi-state quasi-experimental study with matched comparison groups across four states, covering Grades 3–5 and 31,192 students. After controlling for baseline differences, iRCL students "outperformed similar peers on the spring i-Ready Diagnostic for Mathematics, with an average effect size of +0.10" — a printed effect size of +0.10.

> "A multi-state quasi-experimental study evaluated the impact of i-Ready Classroom Mathematics (iRCL) on students in Grades 3–5 using matched comparison groups across four states. After controlling for baseline differences, results showed that students in schools implementing iRCL outperformed similar peers on the spring i-Ready Diagnostic for Mathematics, with an average effect size of +0.10."
