## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ishikawa 2015

Ishikawa, Y., Akahane-Yamada, R., Smith, C., Kondo, M., Tsubota, Y., & Dantsuji, M. (2015). An EFL flipped learning course design: utilizing students’ mobile online devices. Critical CALL – Proceedings of the 2015 EUROCALL Conference, Padova, Italy. http://dx.doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2015.000343

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 551 first-year students (348 experimental, 203 control) in a two-semester Japanese university EFL course; the control used paper-based materials. The comparison used the mid and low experimental groups; the high group was excluded so pre-test averages would be comparable, with a printed effect of "d=.56".

> "The increase of the scores in the experimental group was 151.38 and that in the control group was 54.04. The scores of the post-test in the two groups was compared and there was a significant difference (p<.001; d=.56)"
