## Evidence

### Zhang J 2026

Zhang J, Sachdeva M, McCaig C, Gordon E, Shapiro L, Keenan ID. (2026). Exploring 3D anatomy: A free online course supporting novice learner inquiry and spatial conceptualization. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70288

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Participant comment from Focus Group 1 within the Conceptual sub-theme, describing how the course's drawing and haptic visual observation improved detail recognition and gave "a better sense of proportion and scale" when touch was added. Other conceptual comments noted drawing "does kind of help you visualize it a bit better because it's a bit more of an active method of learning."

> "Some of the drawing and haptic visual observation [in Exploring 3D Anatomy] did help my observation, being able to pick out details I wouldn't have noticed just by looking at something and applying that to how I learned anatomy."
