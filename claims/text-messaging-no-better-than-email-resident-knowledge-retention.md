## Evidence

### Hoonpongsimanont W 2016

Hoonpongsimanont W, Kulkarni M, Tomas-Domingo P, Anderson C, McCormack D, Tu K, Chakravarthy B, Lotfipour S. (2016). Text messaging versus email for emergency medicine residents' knowledge retention: a pilot comparison in the United States. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2016.13.36

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 58 EM residents across three US residency programs, randomized within PGY to text or email delivery of identical content; paired t-test comparing mean exam score differences gave "P=0.51". Means were descriptive only, with no effect size printed.

> "Although we observed a better knowledge retention in the text group, we did not find any significant difference in knowledge retention between text and email groups (P=0.51)."
