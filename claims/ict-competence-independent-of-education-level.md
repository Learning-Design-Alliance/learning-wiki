# Teachers' ICT-use competence is high and statistically independent of educational level

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 73.9% of teachers reported applying ICT efficiently, and a chi-square independence test found no dependence between educational level and ICT-use competence (Chi-square = 2.67, p = .61). [→ García-López 2017](#garcia-lopez-2017)

## Evidence

### García-López 2017

García-López, R.-I., Cuevas Salazar, O., Ramírez-Montoya, M.-S., & Tenorio-Sepúlveda, G.-C. (2017). Competencies for Production, Search, Diffusion and Mobilization of Open Educational Resources. International Education Studies, 10(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n4p78

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square independence test on the contingency table crossing educational level (bachelor's, master's, PhD) with ICT-use competence level for 134 teachers; the accompanying text reports "73.9% of the teachers dominate this competence efficiently" and only 2.2% require assistance.

> "For the contingency Table 2 the independence test was also made between the variables level of education and the competence of use of technology, obtaining a Chi-square of 2.67 with a value of p = .61, which is not significant, concluding with this that there is no dependence between the two variables."
