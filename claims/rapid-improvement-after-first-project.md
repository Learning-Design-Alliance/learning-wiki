## Evidence

### Christopher Burke and Timothy J. Atherton 2016

Christopher Burke and Timothy J. Atherton. (2016). Developing a project-based computational physics course grounded in expert practice. [doi:10.1119/1.4975381](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4975381)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course grading study: 24 submissions from a possible total of 49 were regraded by the expert interviewees on a Good—Fair—Poor scale, with submissions stripped of ordering information and randomly assigned to single graders; mean grades per project are shown in a figure. The authors describe the trend in the plotted means.

> "Some trends are immediately clear: happily, it appears that after the initial project, the quality of work rapidly improved, and continued to improve up to the last class project (Laplace's equation)."
