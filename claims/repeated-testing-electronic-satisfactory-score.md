## Evidence

### Magdin and Turcáni 2015

Martin MAGDIN, Milan TURČÁNI. (2015). A Few Observations and Remarks on Time Effectiveness of Interactive Electronic Testing. Informatics in Education. http://dx.doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2015.06

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated testing of the experimental group after about one month, shown in Fig. 7: on paper or blackboard students did not reach the same score and showed problems in critical thinking and formal transcription, while "in repeated testing in electrical form, the students had no problems to gain satisfactory score".

> "As the figure Fig. 7 shows, the students did not get to the same score after taking the same didactic test after some time (1 month) either in paper form or on the blackboard. At the same time, the results of the repeated tests show that during their taking, many problems in critical thinking and in the creation of the formal transcription arose."
