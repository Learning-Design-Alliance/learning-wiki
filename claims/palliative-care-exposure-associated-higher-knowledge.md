## Evidence

### Silva João Paulo Aureliano 2025

Silva João Paulo Aureliano, Soares Aleida Nazareth, Pagano Adriana Silvina, Savoi Cristiana Guimarães Paes, Silva Alexandre Ernesto, Moura Alexandre Sampaio. (2025). Translation, cross-cultural adaptation, and validation of the Palliative Care Knowledge Questionnaire for PEACE (PEACE-Q) in Brazilian Portuguese. Einstein. https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2025AO1243

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-reported prior-exposure comparison within the 63-resident sample. The article reports medians of 27 versus 26 with "p=0.013"; the association is observational and no effect size is printed.

> "14 (22.2%) residents who reported caring for ≥10 patients in palliative care in the last 6 months performed better overall than the 30 residents who cared for ≤10 patients (median [IQR]: 27 [26-29] versus 26 [23-27]; p=0.013)."

### Silva João Paulo Aureliano 2025 (2)

Silva João Paulo Aureliano, Soares Aleida Nazareth, Pagano Adriana Silvina, Savoi Cristiana Guimarães Paes, Silva Alexandre Ernesto, Moura Alexandre Sampaio. (2025). Translation, cross-cultural adaptation, and validation of the Palliative Care Knowledge Questionnaire for PEACE (PEACE-Q) in Brazilian Portuguese. Einstein. https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2025AO1243

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported confidence comparison in the same sample. The article reports "p=0.044" for the Opioid Side Effects domain and, separately, "No difference was observed between these two groups in the "Oncological Pain" domain (p=0.825)"; no effect size is printed.

> "43 (68.3%) participants who reported being confident in prescribing morphine performed better in the "Side Effects of Opioids" domain compared to those who felt insecure (median [IQR]: 2.0 [2.0-3.0] versus 2.0 [1.0-2.0]; p=0.044)."
