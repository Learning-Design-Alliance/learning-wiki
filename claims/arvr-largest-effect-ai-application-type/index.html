Li 2025¶

q4 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup analysis of AI application types (AR/VR k = 8, ITS k = 61, games k = 23, others k = 7) in the meta-analysis, with AR/VR at "g = 1.765". The "other technologies" category was not statistically significant (g = 0.893, p = 0.078).

"Among all the studies, AR/VR technology had a large effect size (g = 1.765, p < 0.001). ITS (g = 0.347, p < 0.001) and personalized educational games (g = 0.292, p < 0.001) applied in STEM education for K-12 both had small effect sizes."
