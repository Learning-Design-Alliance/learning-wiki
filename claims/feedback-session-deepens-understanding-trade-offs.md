### Muhammad Shahzad Javed 2025

Muhammad Shahzad Javed, Karin Fossheim, Maximilian Roithner, Matylda N. Guzik, James Price, Beate Seibt, Marianne Zeyringer. (2025). Engaging young people for a more inclusive national energy transition: A participatory modelling framework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.12352](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.12352)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative feedback session at one participating school where modelling results were presented. Students were surprised by the prevalence of onshore wind in certain scenarios; high regional-preference costs appeared to influence students to reconsider these aspects.

> "Students largely reaffirmed their support for offshore wind and solar power, while deepening their understanding of the trade-offs and the necessity of compromises. Students viewed a high reliance on offshore wind as a pathway to sustainability, environmental preservation, and the avoidance of localised socio-political tensions"

### Muhammad Shahzad Javed 2025 (2)

Muhammad Shahzad Javed, Karin Fossheim, Maximilian Roithner, Matylda N. Guzik, James Price, Beate Seibt, Marianne Zeyringer. (2025). Engaging young people for a more inclusive national energy transition: A participatory modelling framework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.12352](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.12352)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The feedback session's reported benefits include giving students opportunities to reassess their choices, deepen understanding of renewable electricity systems and trade-offs, and "address misconceptions commonly spread through social media"; evidence is the authors' qualitative observation, no effect size printed.

> "address misconceptions commonly spread through social media—for instance, that the cost of excluding wind energy development in the energy transition is not substantial, electricity trading among countries is not indispensable to achieving net-zero, and wind turbines consume more electricity than they produce"
