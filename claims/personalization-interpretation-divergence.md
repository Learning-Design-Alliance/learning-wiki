Qualitative analysis of questionnaire responses in the Results section of the acceptability study; the authors explain the divergence by physicians seeing homogenous patient cohorts while patients see only their own condition. No quantitative measure is printed for this finding.

> "While clinical specialists perceived "personalization" to mean creating unique materials for a specific group of patients, some of the patient representatives interpreted "personalization" to mean receiving materials customized just to an individual patient."
