## Evidence

### Alex Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Alex Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Should Students be Provided Diagrams or Asked to Draw Them While Solving Introductory Physics Problems?. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.04357](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.04357)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors presented the three interventions for the two problems to seven physics faculty members and asked which group would perform best; all seven predicted Group 2, contradicting the observed result. Some faculty assumed the diagram would help despite a neutral question, and their responses about diagram confusion were negative.

> "similar to our original hypothesis, all seven faculty members incorrectly predicted that students in Group 2 would perform the best because they were given explicit diagrams clarifying the situation."
