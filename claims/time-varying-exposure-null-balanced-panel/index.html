Nick Huntington-Klein. (2026). Little Impact of ChatGPT on High School Test Scores. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08812

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Event-study specification measuring the summer dip separately by metropolitan area and year, normalized to zero in 2022, on the balanced panel. No specification in the pooled table is significant at the 5% level.

"The coefficients are 0.0100 (0.0066) in 2023, -0.0017 (0.0046) in 2024 and 0.0172 (0.0160) in 2025. None of these estimated effects are statistically distinguishable from zero."
