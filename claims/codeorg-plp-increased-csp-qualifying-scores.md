### Richard S. Brown & Emily Anne Brown 2019

Richard S. Brown & Emily Anne Brown. (2019). Estimating the Effect of a Teacher Training Program on Advanced Placement® Outcomes. International Journal of Computer Science Education in Schools, 2(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1207479

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ATT analysis of qualifying scores (3 or better) on AP Computer Science tests in the matched school sample. The article reports 11.77 more qualifying scores in all Computer Science courses and 10.41 more in Computer Science Principles, both highly statistically significant; no effect size appears in this quote.

> "program schools reported an average of 11.77 more qualifying scores in all Computer Science courses (t(332) = 5.92, p < .001) and an average of 10.41 more qualifying scores of Computer Science Principles for all students (t(332) = 6.73, p < .001), both of which were highly statistically significant."
