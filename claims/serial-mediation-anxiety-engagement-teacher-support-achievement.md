## Evidence

### Zhang Zhengdong 2026

Zhang Zhengdong, Gao Chenghua, Wu Hongwei, Ren Dong. (2026). The impact of teacher support on high school students’ mathematics achievement: the mediating roles of mathematics anxiety and learning engagement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04776-z

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap SEM serial mediation analysis (10,000 resamples, 95% CI) in Mplus 8.3 with controls. The study reports a "standardized serial indirect association of 0.018", accounting for 16.07% of the total association.

> "mathematics anxiety and learning engagement together served as a significant serial mediator in the association between teacher support and mathematics achievement, with a standardized serial indirect association of 0.018, p < 0.01, 95% CI [0.006, 0.032]"
