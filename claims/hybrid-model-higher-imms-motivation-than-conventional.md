# The hybrid group reported higher motivation toward instructional materials than the conventional group, while the digital-only group did not

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Post-training IMMS motivation scores were significantly higher in the hybrid (CaDEG) group than in the conventional (CEG) group. [→ Koçyiğit A 2025](#kocyigit-a-2025)
`q3 i?` IMMS scores of the digital-only group were similar to both the hybrid and conventional groups. [→ Koçyiğit A 2025 (2)](#kocyigit-a-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Koçyiğit A 2025

Koçyiğit A, Cavlak U. (2025). Digitalization through the use of Ren’Py-Based interactive learning experiences in physiotherapy and rehabilitation education: a randomised, controlled, single-blind study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08474-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on post-training Instructional Materials Motivation Survey scores in the three-arm trial was significant (F = 3.870, p = 0.025); Bonferroni post-hoc showed CaDEG higher than CEG (p = 0.022).

> "The IMMS scores of the CaDEG group were significantly higher than those of the CEG group (CI(95%) = 1.77 to 30.32, p = 0.022)."

### Koçyiğit A 2025 (2)

Koçyiğit A, Cavlak U. (2025). Digitalization through the use of Ren’Py-Based interactive learning experiences in physiotherapy and rehabilitation education: a randomised, controlled, single-blind study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08474-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc comparisons in the same trial found no significant IMMS difference between the digital-only group and either the hybrid (p = 0.294) or conventional (p = 0.719) groups; equivalence was not tested.

> "The IMMS scores of the DEG group were similar to those of both the CaDEG and CEG groups (CI(95%)=−4.24 to 22.62, p = 0.294; CI(95%)=−21.01 to 7.30, p = 0.719)."
