Gutiérrez-Colon Plana, M., Gallardo Torrano, P. and Grova, M. E. (2012). SMS as a learning tool: an experimental study. The EUROCALL Review, 20(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1064974

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Timing question in the year-one survey of the 13 participants. The article reports messages should be sent "between one and eight p.m." and that all preferred receiving them in the afternoon or evening.

> "The timing preferences coincided, and data suggests that future messages should be sent between one and eight p.m. They all agreed that two or three messages a week was a good quantity, and they all preferred receiving them in the afternoon or in the evening."
