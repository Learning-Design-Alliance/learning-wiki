## Evidence

### Nachiket Kotalwar 2026

Nachiket Kotalwar, Conrad Borchers, Shubham Gandhi, Partha Sarathi Purkayastha, David Yaron, Carolyn P. Rosé. (2026). Exploring Visual Access and Proactive AI Tutoring for Handwritten Mathematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Content analysis comparing tutor-move shares across conditions with Holm-corrected tests; directive hints similarly appeared in 54% versus 39% and 35%. Both differences were significant (Table 12).

> "In Visual-Proactive, error identification, naming a specific mistake, appeared in 41% of a participant's tutor messages on average, versus 20% in Text-Reactive and 22% in Visual-Reactive."

### Nachiket Kotalwar 2026 (2)

Nachiket Kotalwar, Conrad Borchers, Shubham Gandhi, Partha Sarathi Purkayastha, David Yaron, Carolyn P. Rosé. (2026). Exploring Visual Access and Proactive AI Tutoring for Handwritten Mathematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Supplementary analysis separating Visual-Proactive's requested replies from its proactive messages shows the elevated error identification and directive hints originate in tutor-initiated messages, not changes in requested replies.

> "The two significant differences came from the proactive messages: when Visual-Proactive is represented by its requested replies alone, the differences were not detected (Table 13 in Appendix E.2)."
