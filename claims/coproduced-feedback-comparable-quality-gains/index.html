Mutlu Cukurova 2025¶

Mutlu Cukurova, Wannapon Suraworachet, Qi Zhou, and Sahan Bulathwela. (2025). Towards Synergistic Teacher-AI Interactions with Generative Artificial Intelligence. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19580 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2511.19580

The review reports this secondhand, citing Reza et al.'s study of AI praxically supporting teachers in producing feedback for students. The chapter also notes participants complained about AI randomness and reengineering prompts to shape pedagogically aligned results.

"The co-produced feedback reveals comparable quality to manual feedback and statistically significant learning gains, yet with a significant reduction of feedback production time and perceived workload."
