## Evidence

### Davies DJ 2022

Davies DJ, Sam AH, Murphy KG, Khan SA, Choe R, Cleland J. (2022). BMAT's predictive validity for medical school performance: A retrospective cohort study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14819

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Univariable robust linear regressions in the ICSM cohort reported in Results Section 3.1.2, with "very small associations" for Years 1 and 5 (coefficients below 0.1) and a Year 6 standardised coefficient β = 0.14.

> "At ICSM, BMAT Section 1 scores showed very small associations with written assessment performance at ICSM in Years 1 and 5 (coefficients < 0.1) and a small association with written assessment performance in year 6 (coefficient = 0.14, 95% CI 0.08–0.21)."

### Davies DJ 2022 (2)

Davies DJ, Sam AH, Murphy KG, Khan SA, Choe R, Cleland J. (2022). BMAT's predictive validity for medical school performance: A retrospective cohort study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14819

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariable robust linear regressions in the LKCMedicine cohort (222 students) reported in Results Section 3.1.2 found "no associations with any assessment outcomes" for BMAT Section 1; no effect size was printed for this null finding, and equivalence was not tested.

> "At LKCMedicine, BMAT Section 1 scores showed no associations with any assessment outcomes."
