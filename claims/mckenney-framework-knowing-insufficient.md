# McKenney et al.'s ecological framework emphasizes knowing but not applying design knowledge

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The framework's five knowledge types (why, how, when, whom, where) are necessary but nowhere near sufficient; teachers must also understand them conceptually and be able to apply them. [→ Kirschner 2015](#kirschner-2015)

## Evidence

### Kirschner 2015

Kirschner, P. A. (2015). Do we need teachers as designers of technology enhanced learning? Instructional Science, 43, 309–322. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-015-9346-9

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Critical commentary on the McKenney et al. (2015) ecological framework for teacher design knowledge, illustrated by the difference between knowing how to pole vault and being able to clear the bar at 6 meters. The author also questions whether the average teacher is a reflective practitioner with the skills reflection requires.

> "The ﬁrst is that the framework only emphasizes knowing (i.e., what, why, how, when and who). While knowing with respect to design is necessary, it is nowhere near sufﬁcient."
