## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2014

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2014). Linked Learning Communities. WWC Intervention Report. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/630

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Synthesis of six randomized controlled trials covering about 7,400 undergraduate students across six community colleges, all meeting WWC group design standards without reservations. The WWC concluded linked learning communities have "no discernible effects on these outcomes for community college students in developmental education."

> "Overall, the effects of linked learning communities on academic achievement, degree attainment, postsecondary enrollment, credit accumulation, and progress in developmental education for postsecondary students were neither statistically significant nor large enough to be considered to be substantively important."
