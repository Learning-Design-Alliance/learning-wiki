## Evidence

### Şükrü Gönen E 2025

Şükrü Gönen E, Taşkın Egici M, Gönen E. (2025). Knowledge and Awareness of Family Physicians as Key Stakeholders Regarding Developmental Dysplasia of the Hip and the Nationwide Screening Program. Acta Orthop Traumatol Turc. https://doi.org/10.5152/j.aott.2025.24034

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 401 Istanbul family physicians reporting correct-answer rates for DDH risk-factor knowledge items; the listed factors were each known by "rates below 50%".

> "However, other risk factors such as first-born baby, high birth weight, foot deformity, prematurity, scoliosis, plagiocephaly, torticollis, ligamentous laxity, pelvic obliquity were known by physicians at rates below 50%."
