## Evidence

### Steenstra I 2026

Steenstra I, Tseng L-W, Miller KH, Diallo M, Moulis L, Badreddine M, Carbonnel F, Tostain J-B. (2026). Efficacy of an e-Learning Module on Endocrine Disruptors for Family Medicine Residents: Matched Before-And-After Cohort Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/89880

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Matched before-and-after cohort study of 78 first-year family medicine residents at University of Montpellier–Nîmes using McNemar tests on binary yes/no identification items. The study reports "correct identification ranged from 12% (9/78) to 36% (28/78)" before and 69%–97% after training, with a mean 55.56 (SD 13.54) percentage-point improvement.

> "Identification rates for all 16 EDCs improved significantly (P<.001; Figure 5). Before the training, correct identification ranged from 12% (9/78) to 36% (28/78) for 14 of the 16 substances. After the training, rates increased to 69% (54/78) to 97% (76/78)."
