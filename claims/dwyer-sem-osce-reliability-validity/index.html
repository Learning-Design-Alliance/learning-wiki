El Sherif Reem 2024¶

El Sherif Reem, Shrier Ian, Paul-Tellier Pierre, Rodriguez Charo. (2024). What do we know about Objective Structured Clinical Examination in Sport and Exercise Medicine? A scoping review. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.77841

q3 · i? · review · r2

Quantitative descriptive study of orthopedic residents after a sports medicine rotation, reported via the review. It printed "an overall Cronbach alpha of 0.91"; no standardised effect size is printed.

"the authors developed and assessed the performance of a SEM OSCE, and reported an overall Cronbach alpha of 0.91 and the ability of the OSCE to discriminate among different years of training"

El Sherif Reem 2024 (2)¶

El Sherif Reem, Shrier Ian, Paul-Tellier Pierre, Rodriguez Charo. (2024). What do we know about Objective Structured Clinical Examination in Sport and Exercise Medicine? A scoping review. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.77841

q3 · i? · review · r2

Follow-up study by the same author using the same OSCE with orthopedic residents. The review reports senior residents "consistently higher than junior residents"; the underlying study noted it was underpowered with possible examiner bias.

"One study reported that the OSCE scores of senior residents were consistently higher than junior residents: further confirming the construct validity of the OSCE in this setting."

Related Claims¶

  • Cronbach's alpha values for OSCE scores vary widely across studies, ranging from 0.43 to 0.93, with most studies not reaching good internal consistency — a broader claim this one bears on
  • OSCE designs with 5-10 stations achieve good internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha = 0.83), outperforming designs with fewer than 5 or more than 10 stations — a broader claim this one bears on
  • When OSCEs have 5-10 stations, duration per station under 10 minutes yields significantly better internal consistency (alpha = 0.88) than 10 or more minutes (alpha = 0.64) — related
  • When station number varies widely, duration time per station is not a major factor affecting OSCE internal consistency — related
  • Only a small fraction of published OSCE research in musculoskeletal and sport medicine was actually conducted in a Sport and Exercise Medicine setting — related
