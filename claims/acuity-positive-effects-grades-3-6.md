## Evidence

### Konstantopoulos 2015

Konstantopoulos, S., Li, W., Miller, S., & van der Ploeg, A. (2015). Effects of Interim Assessments on the Achievement Gap: Evidence from an Experiment. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562166

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Grade 3-6 quantile regression analyses from the cluster-randomized trial showed significant positive treatment effects in mathematics across quantiles (e.g., 0.214* at the 10th quantile) and at the 10th quantile in reading. The authors attribute these effects to Acuity.

> "It seems that Acuity affected mathematics and reading achievement positively and in some instances considerably in grades 3-6."
