## Evidence

### Uusküla A 2014

Uusküla A, Ricketts EJ, Rugman C, Kalda R, Fredlund H, Hedlund J, Dunais B, Touboul P, McNulty C. (2014). Provision of chlamydia testing, and training of primary health care staff about chlamydia testing, across four European countries. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-14-1147

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional review of national-level data from four European countries using a structured questionnaire and secondary sources. The Results report that in England and Sweden "a national strategy or plan on STI control that specifically mentions chlamydia was in force" with GP guidelines and firmer residency training. No effect size is printed.

> "In countries where chlamydia testing is provided by GPs (England, Sweden) a national strategy or plan on STI control that specifically mentions chlamydia was in force, chlamydia care guidelines for GPs were in place and STI management was more firmly established in the GP residency training curriculum"
