q2 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Post hoc subgroup analysis of the 12 competency-versus-noncompetency studies, grouping by the ratio of competency-arm to noncompetency-arm training time. Equal-time studies (2 studies) gave SMD = 0.67 (P = 0.07); longer-time studies (4 studies) gave SMD = 0.18 (P = 0.68).

"For the 6 studies with a time ratio <1 (shorter time for competency-based simulation), the pooled SMD was 1.00 (95% CI = 0.53 to 1.7, P < 0.001) indicating a large effect favoring the competency-based group"
