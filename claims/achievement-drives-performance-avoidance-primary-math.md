## Evidence

### Mägi 2010

Mägi, K., Lerkkanen, M.-K., Poikkeus, A.-M., Rasku-Puttonen, H., & Kikas, E. (2010). Relations Between Achievement Goal Orientations and Math Achievement in Primary Grades: A Follow-Up Study. Scandinavian Journal of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.1080/00313831003764545

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal structural equation model of 179 Estonian primary-grade children across two measurement waves. The model showed "Significant paths were found from children's self-reported performance-avoidance orientation and teacher-rated effort at Time 1 to math grade at Time 1" and reciprocal paths to Time 2.
