`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Item-level comparison of the lowest 10 mean VAS scores from managers in the cross-sectional study (n = 162). The article states "The lowest mean VAS score from the nurse managers was on the updating of written guidelines for care items" (61.26 manager vs 68.05 nurse).
