Tedeschi MN, Hose TM, Mehlman EK, Franklin S, Wong TE. (2023). Improving Models for Student Retention and Graduation using Markov Chains. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Markov-model comparison of LA-supported versus non-LA-supported science majors in high-attrition College of Science courses using all data Fall 2013 to Summer 2021. "LA-supported students have a median SYGR of 77% (95% CI of 73-81%)" versus 68% (66-70%) for the non-LA group.

> "We find that exposure to LAs in high-attrition science courses is associated with a 9 percentage point improvement in SYGR, relative to students who were enrolled in sections of these courses that did not have LA support"
