Evidence¶

Gross 2015¶

Gross, B., Hoffman, M., Marinari, M., DeSimone, K., & Burke, P. (2015). Flipped @ SBU: Student satisfaction and the college classroom. Educational Research Quarterly, 39(2), 37-52. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1166718

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Six flipped-only evaluation items (N = 64, Cronbach's alpha = .845) showed "at least a 3:1 ratio of student approval" on four items, with the technology-ease item reaching a 16:1 ratio.

"there were four items which had at least a 3:1 ratio of student approval: the perception that the use of technology made learning easier (16:1 ratio), that in-class activities were more effective for learning the material than listening to lectures (5.33:1 ratio), perception that the course was more engaging (3.25:1 ratio), and student willingness to recommend a flipped class to a friend (3:1 ratio)."
