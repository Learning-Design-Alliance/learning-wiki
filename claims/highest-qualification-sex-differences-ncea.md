`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-squared comparison of highest secondary qualification by sex among 649 cohort members (Table 2): University Entrance 27.4% female vs 18.1% male, p = 0.005; NCEA level 1 or 2 31.2% female vs 40.4% male, p = 0.015; no qualification recorded did not differ (p = 0.329).
