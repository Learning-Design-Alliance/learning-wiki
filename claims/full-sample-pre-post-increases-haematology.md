## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among the full sample of 86 students, knowledge increased by an average of 1.05 points out of 30 (t85 = 2.95, p <.05) and self-efficacy increased by an average of 2.10 points (t85 = 2.79, p <.05). [→ Algarni Abdulrahman Abdulrahman 2024](#algarni-abdulrahman-abdulrahman-2024)

## Evidence

### Algarni Abdulrahman Abdulrahman 2024

Algarni Abdulrahman Abdulrahman. (2024). Biomedical students’ self-efficacy and academic performance by gender in a flipped learning haematology course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05421-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the quasi-experiment's full-sample analysis, pre-to-post change scores were tested with one-sample t tests equivalent to paired t tests. The knowledge increase was significant; the self-efficacy increase of 2.10 points was also significant (t85 = 2.79, p <.05). No effect size was printed for either.

> "Among the full sample (intervention and control groups combined), knowledge increased by an average of 1.05 points out of a maximum possible score of 30 (t85 = 2.95, p <.05)."
