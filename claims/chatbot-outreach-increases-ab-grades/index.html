Meyer 2026¶

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Intent-to-treat estimates from randomized controlled trials pooling Government and Microeconomics samples (1,568 and 915 randomized students). Treatment increased A-or-B earning "relative to 61% of control-group students (about a 7% increase)"; Westfall-Young adjusted p-values show significant increase in earning a B or higher but not an A.

"Treatment students are four percentage points more likely to earn an A relative to 36% of control-group students (about an 11% increase) and arere four percentage points more likely to earn an A or B relative to 61% of control-group students (about a 7% increase)."
