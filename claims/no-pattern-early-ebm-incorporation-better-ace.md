## Evidence

### Mahmoud M.A. 2020

Mahmoud M.A., Laws S., Kamal A., Al Mohanadi D., Al Mohammed A., Mahfoud Z.R. (2020). Examining aptitude and barriers to evidence-based medicine among trainees at an ACGME-I accredited program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02341-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 80 trainees comparing ACE scores across timing-of-incorporation categories (Table 2). The study reports a 1.1-point descriptive difference with no inferential test.

> "There was no clear pattern that early incorporation of EBM into practice will result in better ACE score. For example, those who reported not incorporating EBM into their practice had a higher average ACE score; by 1.1 points, compared with those who incorporated it after residency."
