Evidence¶

Hana S. S. Al Rasheed 2014¶

Hana S. S. Al Rasheed. (2014). Examining the Effectiveness of Pre-reading Strategies on Saudi EFL College Students' Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 7(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n11p79

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact randomly assigned groups of 23 female Saudi EFL students each, over four reading passages with eight multiple-choice questions per passage. An independent-samples t-test found no significant difference: "the t value is 1.67, which is significant at .102" (df = 44).

"Table 4 shows that the t value is 1.67, which is significant at .102. Based on this result, the null hypothesis is accepted"

Hana S. S. Al Rasheed. (2014). Examining the Effectiveness of Pre-reading Strategies on Saudi EFL College Students' Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 7(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n11p79

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive means from the same quasi-experiment (Table 3): vocabulary pre-teaching mean 6.33 (SD .89), pre-questioning mean 5.8 (SD .91), out of 8. The article states "The means of the two groups are close"; no effect size is printed.

"The mean of vocabulary pre-teaching group is 6.33 out of 8, with a stan dard deviation of .89, whereas the mean of the pre-questioning group is 5.8, with a standard deviation of .91. The means of the two groups are close."
