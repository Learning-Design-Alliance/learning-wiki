`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative validity evidence: iterative expert review of item alignment (table 1) plus cognitive interviews with two teachers and meetings with six math instructional coaches after round two. Practitioners confirmed relevance and feasibility, informing item revisions.

> "all eight practitioners interviewed agreed that the math content and scenarios in the vignettes were representative of their middle school math classrooms, were at the correct level of difficulty for a middle school math teacher"
