## Evidence

### Hu 2024

Hu, Y., Elliot, A. J., Wouters, P., van der Schaaf, M., Kester, L., & Pekrun, R. (2024). Effects of peers' emotions on students' emotions, achievement goals, mental effort, and performance. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(7), 1283–1299. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000895

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Game performance (correct separator choices in video questions, maximum 20 points) in the randomized experiment was evaluated by GORICA in both analyses; the supported pattern was "ae > 0 = af", consistent with the authors' Hcog2 hypothesis. No effect sizes were printed.

> "students exposed to peers’ enjoyment and those exposed to peers’ neutral state reported lower mental effort (ae = 0 < af) and higher game performance (ae > 0 = af) than those exposed to peers’ frustration"
