Evidence¶

Bot & Eze 2016¶

Bot, T. D., & Eze, J. E. (2016). Comparative effects of concept mapping and cooperative learning strategies on senior secondary school students' achievement in mathematics-trigonometry in Kano State, Nigeria. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, 4(1), 56-66. https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9453

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA interaction analysis for the concept mapping condition (Table 8): gender × method p = 0.099, reported as not significant despite the article's confusing phrasing; method effect "F (1,65) = 38.052", p = 0.000.

"gender does not significantly interact with method to influence the students’ mean achievement scores in trigonometry when taught using concept mapping. The statistics however indicated that method of teaching has a significant effect on the groups since F (1,65) = 38.052"

Bot & Eze 2016 (2)¶

Bot, T. D., & Eze, J. E. (2016). Comparative effects of concept mapping and cooperative learning strategies on senior secondary school students' achievement in mathematics-trigonometry in Kano State, Nigeria. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, 4(1), 56-66. https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9453

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA interaction analysis for the cooperative learning condition (Table 9): gender × method F(1,65) = 6.588, p = 0.011, reported significant; method effect F(1,65) = 79.072, p = 0.000.

"there is a significant interaction effect of group and method of teaching (cooperative learning strategy) regarding the achievement of students in trigonometry since F(1,65) = 6.588 and p‐value of 0.011 is strictly less than α=0.05"
