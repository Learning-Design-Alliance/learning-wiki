## Evidence

### Makkink Andrew William & Dreyer Devin John 2021

Makkink Andrew William & Dreyer Devin John. (2021). Simulation debriefing: a perspective from emergency medical care students at three South African Higher Education Institutions. The Pan African Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.11604/pamj.2021.38.97.23009

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire survey of 153 students; frequency analysis. Table 2 shows 81 (52.9%) agreed and 43 (28.1%) strongly agreed on questions encouraging reflection; for yes/no questions, 49 (32.0%) disagreed and 53 (34.6%) were neutral versus 39 agreeing.

> "Generally, participants indicated that questions were asked in a manner that encouraged self-reflection and the provision of meaningful answers to questions related to why they did specific things."
