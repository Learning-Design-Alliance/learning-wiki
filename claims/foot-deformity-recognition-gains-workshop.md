## Evidence

### Schoen Deborah E. 2016

Schoen Deborah E., Gausia Kaniz, Glance David G., Thompson Sandra C. (2016). Improving rural and remote practitioners’ knowledge of the diabetic foot: findings from an educational intervention. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13047-016-0157-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Unpaired pre/post knowledge questions on foot deformities answered by all participants: "recognition of small muscle wasting increased from 34 %" to 85 %, and limited joint motion testing rose from 19 % to 70 %; hammertoe and claw toes improved more modestly.

> "Improvements in recognition of small muscle wasting increased from 34 % of participants in the pre-test to 85 % in the post-test and knowing how to test for limited joint motion increased from 19 % in the pre-test to 70 % of participants in the post-test."
