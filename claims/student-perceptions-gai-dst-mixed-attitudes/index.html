Evidence¶

Wei 2025¶

Wei, X., Wang, L., Lee, L.-K., & Liu, R. (2025). The effects of generative AI on collaborative problem-solving and team creativity performance in digital story creation: an experimental study. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 22:23. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-025-00526-0

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive survey results from an 18-item modified scale (Cronbach's α = 0.86) administered to the experimental group post-intervention. The article reports "moderate worry about over-reliance (M = 3.38, SD = 0.98)" alongside strong willingness scores (e.g., unique insights M = 4.48, SD = 0.60).

"moderate worry about over-reliance (M = 3.38, SD = 0.98) was observed, with students acknowledging the risk of diminished problem-solving autonomy when GAI tools are heavily integrated into the DST process"
