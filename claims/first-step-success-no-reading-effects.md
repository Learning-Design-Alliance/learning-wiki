### First Step to Success: WWC Intervention Report 2012

First Step to Success: WWC Intervention Report. (2012). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/179

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

RCT in Albuquerque Public Schools comparing 101 intervention with 97 control students on oral reading fluency and WJ-III DRB Letter-Word Identification. Domain-average effect size was –0.04; oral reading fluency showed p = 0.79, so neither outcome was statistically significant or substantively important.

> "Walker et al. (2009) found, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant difference between treatment and comparison groups on oral reading fluency."
