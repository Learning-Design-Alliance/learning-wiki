## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the single-group pretest-posttest analysis of PGY4s, RRC completion time fell from 5.0 min at pretest to 4.3 min at posttest (p = 0.756), while performance on the remaining RRC metrics and all metrics for the other two tasks was higher at posttest. [→ Radi Imad 2022](#radi-imad-2022)

## Evidence

### Radi Imad 2022

Radi Imad, Tellez Juan C., Alterio Rodrigo E., Scott Daniel J., Sankaranarayanan Ganesh, Nagaraj Madhuri B., Hogg Melissa E., Zeh Herbert J., Polanco Patricio M. (2022). Feasibility, effectiveness and transferability of a novel mastery-based virtual reality robotic training platform for general surgery residents. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

PGY4-specific inanimate analysis; the article reports improvement on all metrics "except time on RRC" with pretest 5.0 min vs posttest 4.3 min (p = 0.756). PGY2s improved significantly on all metrics of all three tasks.

> "When considering only the PGY4s, improved performance was observed on each inanimate task according to all metrics except time on RRC (pretest: 5.0 min [IQR: 4.0–5.7 min]; posttest: 4.3 min [IQR: 3.3–5.2 min]; p = 0.756)"
