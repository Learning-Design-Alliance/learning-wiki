## Evidence

### Leila Ashegh Navaie 2018

Leila Ashegh Navaie. (2018). The Effects of Reciprocal Teaching on Reading Comprehension of Iranian EFL Learners. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 9(4). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.9n.4p.26

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with two intact groups of 38 intermediate EFL learners at language institutes in Amol, Iran; the experimental group received reciprocal teaching and the control group conventional instruction for 15 sessions. The posttest t-test was significant: "t(74)=2.04, Sig=.001". No effect size is printed.

> "The results of independent samples t-test (t(74)=2.04, Sig=.001) shows that the difference between the groups is significant; thus, it can be concluded that reciprocal teaching affects reading comprehension of Iranian EFL learners."
