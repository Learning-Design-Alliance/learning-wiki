`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports the speciAL RCT (Schaefert et al., 2013) secondary outcome SF-36 Mental Composite Score at 12 months: significant improvements in both groups, larger in the intervention group (55% versus 34%), with a 4-point overall increase treated as clinically significant.

> "Schaefert and colleagues [33] measured the mental component of quality of life and the results showed significant improvements in both groups, with slightly larger improvements in the intervention group (55% versus 34%)."

### Thursby Stacie 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports the speciAL RCT primary outcome, SF-36 Physical Composite Score at 12 months: non-significant improvements in both groups, with the physical functioning domain significant in the intervention group at 12 months; somatic symptom severity was significant at 6 months only.

> "One study measured the physical component section of quality of life at 12 months post-intervention and found non-significant improvements in both the intervention and control groups [33]; at 12 months, however, the physical functioning domain was significant in the intervention group."
