### William R. Evans and Mats A. Selen 2016

William R. Evans and Mats A. Selen. (2016). Investigating the Use of Mastery-Style Online Homework Exercises in Introductory Algebra-based Mechanics in a Controlled Clinical Study. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Third clinical trial: 49 algebra-based mechanics students randomized to mastery (30) or traditional (19) homework on Newton's laws of motion shortly before the final exam, by which point students had used Newton's laws for most of the semester. The article reports "No diﬀerence was seen" between conditions; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "No diﬀerence was seen when comparing students in the mastery-homework condition with the students in the traditional-homework condition in this trial."
