## Evidence

### Kenyon Kristy L. 2016

Kenyon Kristy L., Onorato Morgan E., Gottesman Alan J., Hoque Jamila, Hoskins Sally G. (2016). Testing CREATE at Community Colleges: An Examination of Faculty Perspectives and Diverse Student Gains. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-07-0146

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Outside-evaluator observation of three class sessions per implementation at seven community colleges, using a modified Weiss protocol with four focus areas rated on a five-point scale. The evaluator found means "greater than 3.0 on a 5-point scale" across focus areas.

> "Overall, the means of the means for the 2-yr faculty cohort for each focus area were high (greater than 3.0 on a 5-point scale), indicating that the majority (60% or higher) of the observed class sessions reflected CREATE pedagogy."
