# Educators perceived FH2T positioned students as doers of mathematics with agency and mathematical decision making in a safe practice environment

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Educators reported that FH2T's safe environment, where no moves are illegal, supported students to practice, try out moves, and take ownership of their problem-solving decisions. [→ Tarik Buli 2025](#tarik-buli-2025)

## Evidence

### Tarik Buli 2025

Tarik Buli, Stefani Pautz Stephenson, Rebecca Banks, and Amanda Wortman. (2025). From Here to There (FH2T): Educator Perceptions of Game-Based Algebraic Learning. Digital Promise white paper. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7dcf9d16-b5ad-4067-be9f-be30319b1185

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Thematic analysis of confirmatory study interviews with 11 educators. Educator Reese Thompson described students as "Responsible for making decisions, instead of executing them," and other educators described FH2T as a safe place to practice and as providing a way to be an agentic learner.

> "Responsible for making decisions, instead of executing them. So it's a way to check understanding more than a way to check numeracy."
