Evidence¶

Pasqualotto Eric 2026¶

Pasqualotto Eric, Pasqualotto Tales, Lopes Victor Hugo Pinheiro, Lunelli Laura, Santos Lucas Loiola. (2026). Virtual reality surgical simulator training of residents and early-career surgeons for pedicle screw placement: a systematic review and meta-analysis. European Journal of Orthopaedic Surgery & Traumatology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00590-026-05008-1

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Pooled continuous outcomes from six RCTs: screw penetration distance "MD − 1.07 mm" (I2 = 11%) and displaced screws per resident "MD − 2.31 screws" (I2 = 26%), both favouring VR training with p < 0.01. Both outcomes were rated low-certainty by GRADE.

"there were a significant reduction in screw penetration distances (MD − 1.07 mm; 95% CI − 1.18 to − 0.96; p < 0.01; I2 = 11%; Fig. 3B) and fewer displaced screws per resident (MD − 2.31 screws; 95% CI − 3.15 to − 1.47; p < 0.01; I2 = 26%)"
