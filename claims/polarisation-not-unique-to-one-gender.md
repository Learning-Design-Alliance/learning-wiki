Gender-segregated analysis of FCI answer distributions for 66 students (56% female, 44% male) over four cohorts. Figure 4 shows the same polarisation characteristic for each gender: "for each question, there is a non-vanishing gap in the pair of graphs for pretest and posttest."

> "Coupled with the argument elaborated in the ﬁrst paragraph above, we then reach one of the main ﬁndings of this work— polarisation is NOT unique to one gender."
