# Teachers prefer GPT-4 feedback over GOAT and SBERT-Canberra feedback by a wide margin

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` GPT-4 feedback was chosen 77.5% of the time on average, versus 12% each for GOAT and SBERT. [→ Sami Baral 2024](#sami-baral-2024)

## Evidence

### Sami Baral 2024

Sami Baral, Eamon Worden, Wen-Chiang Lim, Zhuang Luo, Christopher Santorelli, Ashish Gurung, Neil Heffernan. (2024). Automated Feedback in Math Education: A Comparative Analysis of LLMs for Open-Ended Responses. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.08910](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.08910)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Preference item in the two-teacher evaluation of 100 sampled student answers; raters picked which of the three feedback messages they would give their students, and "the GPT-4 generated feedback was chosen 77.5% of the time".

> "On average, the GPT-4 generated feedback was chosen 77.5% of the time, while both GOAT and SBERT were selected only 12% of the time each."
