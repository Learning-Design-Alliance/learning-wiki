In the fifth semi-structured clinical interview (March 17, 2010) in this case study of one student, Michael opens unprompted by warning the interviewer he is "probably like, a fringe as far as students go," fearing he is "hurting your thesis." Earlier he said some people call his sense-making stance "an illness, but I have a lot of pride."

> "I'm probably like, a fringe as far as students go. As far as my views, you know, my ideals. And so, I'm just curious if you're trying to make like a statistical argument."
