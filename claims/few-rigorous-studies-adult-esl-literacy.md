# Rigorous effectiveness research on literacy instruction for adult ESL learners was nearly absent prior to this study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 17 rigorous (quasi-experimental or randomized) adult education studies reviewed, only 3 included adult ESL learners, and only 1 of those presented outcomes for ESL learners, with substantial methodological problems including 40 percent overall attrition. [→ Condelli 2010](#condelli-2010)

## Evidence

### Condelli 2010

Condelli, L., Cronen, S., Bos, J., Tseng, F., and Altuna, J. (2010). The Impact of a Reading Intervention for Low-Literate Adult ESL Learners—Executive Summary (NCEE 2011-4004). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Narrative literature review (Condelli & Wrigley, 2004) of literacy interventions for ABE and adult ESL learners, cited as the study's motivation. The one ESL study reporting outcomes had "a 40 percent overall attrition rate" and differential attrition across groups, limiting validity.

> "out of 17 adult education studies that used a rigorous methodology (i.e., quasi-experimental or randomized trials), only 3 included adult ESL learners"
