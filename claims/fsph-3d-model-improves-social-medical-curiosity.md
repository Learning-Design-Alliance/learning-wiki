## Evidence

### Zhou Fen 2026

Zhou Fen, Yu Ping, Yu Xiaofen, Han Xuyan, Wang Zheng. (2026). Family-school-hospital collaboration model for urinary system tumor early screening education. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1837667

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomized controlled pre-post study of 120 clinical medical interns (60 per group). Social medical curiosity measured with the Chinese version of the Medical Curiosity Scale (Cronbach's α = 0.866). In the results section, the mean score change was 0.63 ± 4.98 in the intervention group against 0.28 ± 4.21 in the control group (t = 2.018, df = 118, p = 0.0458, Cohen's d = 0.37), and the baseline-adjusted ANCOVA gave adjusted post-test means of 26.02 against 24.89 (difference 1.13, 95% CI 0.21–2.05, F = 5.827, p = 0.017, partial η² = 0.047). The abstract prints different change scores (1.55 ± 5.26 vs. 1.10 ± 5.02) and attributes d = 0.37 to the adjusted analysis; the paper does not explain the difference. Groups were formed by boarding status, not at random.

> "The mean score change was 0.63 ± 4.98 in the observation group versus 0.28 ± 4.21 in the control group (t = 2.018, df = 118, p = 0.0458, Cohen's d = 0.37). After baseline adjustment via ANCOVA, the adjusted post-test mean was 26.02 ± 0.37 for the observation group and 24.89 ± 0.37 for controls (mean difference = 1.13, 95% CI: 0.21–2.05; F = 5.827, df = 1,117, p = 0.017, partial η2 = 0.047)" (results)
