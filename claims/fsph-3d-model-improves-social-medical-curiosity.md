### Zhou Fen 2026

Zhou Fen, Yu Ping, Yu Xiaofen, Han Xuyan, Wang Zheng. (2026). Family-school-hospital collaboration model for urinary system tumor early screening education. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1837667

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomized controlled pre-post study of 120 clinical medical interns (60 per group). Social medical curiosity measured with the Chinese version of the Medical Curiosity Scale (Cronbach's α = 0.866). "social medical curiosity" improved significantly with Cohen's d = 0.37.

> "social medical curiosity (mean change: 1.55 ± 5.26 vs. 1.10 ± 5.02; p = 0.0458, Cohen's d = 0.37, 95% CI: 0.037 to 3.896)"
