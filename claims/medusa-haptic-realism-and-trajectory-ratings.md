### Gmeiner M 2025

Gmeiner M, Schrempf A, Thurner T, Fenz W, Sabrowsky-Hirsch B, Giretzlehner M, Prückl R, Schaffelhofer S, Major Z, Lämmermann S, Baumgartner M, Drabauer L, Nagy J, Esposito G, Colombo E, Stroh-Holly N, Gruber A. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive questionnaire results from the 40-neurosurgeon external validation: haptic realism ratings ranged from 3.13 to 3.93. A separate sentence reports the surgical trajectory to the aneurysm, defined by brain lobes and the skull, was rated important with a mean score of 3.95.

> "The study participants perceive the haptic interaction between the instrument and artificial anatomy as realistic (mean scores between 3.13 and 3.93)."
