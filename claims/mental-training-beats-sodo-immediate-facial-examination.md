# Mental Training produced significantly better structured facial examination performance than See One, Do One immediately after training

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Immediately after training (T1), the Mental Training group scored significantly higher on the OSCE checklist than the See One, Do One group, with Cohen's d of 0.86 (Rater 1) and 0.66 (Rater 2). [→ Nelskamp Arne 2021](#nelskamp-arne-2021)

## Evidence

### Nelskamp Arne 2021

Nelskamp Arne, Schnurr Benedikt, Germanyuk Alexandra, Sterz Jasmina, Lorenz Jonas, Sader Robert, Rüsseler Miriam, Seifert Lukas B. (2021). Comparison of ‘Mental training’ and physical practice in the mediation of a structured facial examination: a quasi randomized, blinded and controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02603-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-randomized controlled study of 67 4th-year dentistry students assigned to Mental Training (n = 38) or See One, Do One (n = 29), assessed by two blinded raters on a 24-item OSCE checklist. The Mental Training group scored higher at T1, "Rater 1 p < 0.001; Cohen’s d = 0.86" and d = 0.66 for Rater 2.

> "At T1, students who had been trained with the ‘Mental Training’ approach showed highly significant better results in the performance of a structured facial examination (Rater 1 p < 0.001; Cohen’s d = 0.86; Rater 2 p = 0.005; Cohen’s d = 0.66, r = 0.74) than students who had been trained with the ‘See One, Do One’ approach"
