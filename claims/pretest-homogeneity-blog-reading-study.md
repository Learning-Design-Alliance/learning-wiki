## Evidence

### Behjat 2013

Behjat, F. (2013). Joint Book Reading: How Do Blogs Foster Collaborative Comprehension? i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 3 No. 2, April-June. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068891

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test homogeneity check on the Longman TOEFL reading comprehension test (30 questions, five passages, r = 0.8 test-retest reliability) administered before instruction. The article reports the four subgroups performed the same; no effect size is printed.

> "According to Table 2, the significance level (sig. = .483) is not much lower than the value of F (F = .829). Therefore, it can be implied that regarding reading comprehension, the performances of all male and female students in the control and experimental groups were the same, and they were homogeneous."
