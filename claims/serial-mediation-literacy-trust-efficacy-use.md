# Trust in AI and academic self-efficacy serially mediate the literacy-to-continued-use association (literacy → trust → self-efficacy → use)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The serial indirect path from AI literacy through trust and then self-efficacy to continued use is significant (indirect effect = 0.07, 95% CI 0.04–0.10). [→ Peichen Hu 2026](#peichen-hu-2026)
`q3 i?` Total indirect effect through both mediators is 0.28 (95% CI 0.21–0.36), with the direct effect remaining significant, indicating partial complementary mediation. [→ Peichen Hu 2026 (2)](#peichen-hu-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Peichen Hu 2026

Peichen Hu. (2026). Psychological predictors of continued ChatGPT use among university students: the roles of AI literacy, trust, and academic self-efficacy. Front. Psychol. 17:1900190. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1900190

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Monte Carlo confidence intervals (20,000 draws) in the latent-variable SEM of 450 existing ChatGPT users tested the serial pathway; the printed estimate was "indirect eﬀect = 0.07, 95% CI (0.04, 0.10)". No standardised effect size is printed for this indirect path.

> "The serial indirect path from AI literacy through trust and then self-eﬃcacy to continued use was also signiﬁcant [indirect eﬀect = 0.07, 95% CI (0.04, 0.10); H7c], consistent with a speciﬁcation in which trust and self-eﬃcacy function as ordered rather than parallel links"

### Peichen Hu 2026 (2)
