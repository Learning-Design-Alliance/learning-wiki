## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Male university students with high physical activity have significantly higher GPA than those with moderate and low activity, and the moderate group outperforms the low group (ηp2 = 0.57). [→ Alghadier Mshari 2026](#alghadier-mshari-2026)

## Evidence

### Alghadier Mshari 2026

Alghadier Mshari, Badcock Nicholas. (2026). Sleep, movement, and marks: exploring the relationship between sleep quality, physical activity, and academic performance in male university students. PeerJ 14:e21154. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21154

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 130 male undergraduates; one-way ANOVA of GPA across IPAQ low/moderate/high categories. Means were low 2.57 ± 0.32, moderate 3.00 ± 0.31, high 3.45 ± 0.33, with Bonferroni-adjusted post hoc comparisons all p < 0.001.

> "One-way ANOVA revealed a significant main effect of IPAQ category on GPA (F(2,127) = 83.55, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.57."
