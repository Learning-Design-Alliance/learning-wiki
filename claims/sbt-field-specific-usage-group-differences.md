## Evidence

### Pillong Lukas 2025

Pillong Lukas, Sprengart Franziska Marietta, Recker Florian, et al. (2025). Reflecting realities: gauging the pulse of simulator-based training in medical minds—resonance of simulator-based ultrasound training in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07198-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Dichotomous questionnaire items on prior simulator use per clinical field in the same 343-participant survey. "56% of the practical year (PJ) students used simulators for airway management, compared to just 18% in the third-year students and 16% of physicians"; gynecology use was 19% (PJ) vs 1% (third-year) and 2% (physicians), and ultrasound diagnostics 35% vs 12% and 8% (all p < 0.001).

> "56% of the practical year (PJ) students used simulators for airway management, compared to just 18% in the third-year students and 16% of physicians (p < 0.001). Similar trends were seen in emergency medicine (p < 0.001)."
