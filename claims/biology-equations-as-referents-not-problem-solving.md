## Evidence

### Watkins 2010

Watkins, J., Hall, K., Redish, E. F., & Cooke, T. J. (2010). Understanding How Students Use Physical Ideas in Introductory Biology Courses. University of Maryland, College Park. [doi:10.1063/1.3515237](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515237)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative field notes from classroom observations of more than half the semester's lectures in the reformed Org Bio course, focusing on how instructors presented physics and equations. The notes show the instructor asking students to "think about what happens when you change a given variable" and to set the equation aside and use intuition.

> "In this reformed biology course, the instructor often returned to the equations during a discussion of form and function of organisms or when talking about the affordances and constraints of physical laws in the evolutionary story."
