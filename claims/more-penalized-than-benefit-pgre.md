## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero 2020

Nicholas T. Young and Marcos D. Caballero. (2020). The Physics GRE does not help applicants “stand out”. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2008.10712](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2008.10712)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bin-count analysis of the same 2537-applicant dataset, reported with the Fig. 3 condensed GPA/GRE bins. The article reports that "16% of all applicants score in the high GPA but low physics GRE score bin", exceeding the share who could benefit from a high score with a low GPA.

> "Second, we notice that 16% of all applicants score in the high GPA but low physics GRE score bin. That is, more applicants could be penalized for having a low physics GRE score despite a high GPA than could benefit from a high physics GRE score despite a low GPA."
