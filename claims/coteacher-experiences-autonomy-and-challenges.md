### Felix Gorny 2026

Felix Gorny, Anika Grohmann, Mareike Müller-Cleve, Daniela Bavaresco, Saskia Wirtz, Christan Brandt, Tanja Sappok. (2026). Fostering inclusion in healthcare: a systematic review of collaborative teaching with individuals with disorders of intellectual development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09930-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Semi-structured interview study (thematic analysis via systematic text condensation) with six formally employed lecturers with intellectual disabilities at a Norwegian university. The review reports "Mainly positive experiences regarding their feelings of having social relations, learning new things and experience self-determination," alongside initial discomfort reported by Mevold et al.

> "Mainly positive experiences regarding their feelings of having social relations, learning new things and experience self-determination."

### Felix Gorny 2026 (2)

Felix Gorny, Anika Grohmann, Mareike Müller-Cleve, Daniela Bavaresco, Saskia Wirtz, Christan Brandt, Tanja Sappok. (2026). Fostering inclusion in healthcare: a systematic review of collaborative teaching with individuals with disorders of intellectual development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09930-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative and phenomenological study (interviews and focus groups, MAXQDA content analysis) with co-teachers with and without intellectual disabilities, a teacher, and students. The review reports co-teachers "reported to have no formal training in teaching" and identified further training needs in assessment, classroom management, and public speaking.

> "In Rodríguez Herrero et al. [28], co-teachers reported to have no formal training in teaching."
