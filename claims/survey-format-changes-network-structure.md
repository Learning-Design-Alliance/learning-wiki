## Evidence

### Meagan Sundstrom 2025

Meagan Sundstrom, Justin Gambrell, Adrienne L. Traxler, and Eric Brewe. (2025). Evaluating recognition and recall formats of social network surveys in physics education research. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sundstrom](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sundstrom)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of two undirected networks built from the 65 matched respondents' answers, with all 201 enrolled students as nodes. Table I reports edges 160 vs 89, density 0.009 vs 0.005, transitivity 0.19 vs 0.12, components 5 vs 17, giant component 111 vs 55; no statistical tests were conducted.

> "The recognition net- work contains many more edges (i.e., higher density) and has a higher tendency for small-group clustering (i.e., higher tran- sitivity) than the recall network."
