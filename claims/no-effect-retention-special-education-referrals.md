# School closings had no effect on grade retention rates or special education referrals for displaced students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Displaced students were no more or less likely than comparison students to be retained in grade or referred to special education before or after closings. [→ Marisa de la Torre 2009](#marisa-de-la-torre-2009)

## Evidence

### Marisa de la Torre 2009

Marisa de la Torre, Julia Gwynne. (2009). When Schools Close: Effects on Displaced Students in Chicago Public Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago Urban Education Institute. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/when-schools-close-effects-displaced-students-chicago-public-schools

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of retention and special-education referral rates between displaced and matched comparison students before and after closings (Figures 6 and 7): retention 4.2% vs 4.7% prior and 4.5% vs 3.9% after; referrals 0.81% vs 0.81% prior and 0.95% vs 0.64% after; no significant differences reported.

> "The school closing policy did not have an effect either on retention rates or on special education referrals for displaced students (Figures 6 and 7)."
