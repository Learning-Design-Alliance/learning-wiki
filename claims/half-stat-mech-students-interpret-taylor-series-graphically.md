## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2011

Trevor I. Smith, John R. Thompson, Donald B. Mountcastle. (2011). Student Understanding of Taylor Series Expansions in Statistical Mechanics. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1112.4231](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1112.4231)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Written Taylor series pretest administered before the Boltzmann Factor tutorial each year for three years in an upper-division statistical mechanics course; students interpreted coefficients a1, b1, c1 from a graph. The article reports "14 out of 26 students" answered fully correctly with reasons.

> "In the three years that this question was given before tutorial instruction was implemented, 14 out of 26 students correctly determined all the signs of the various quantities and gave appropriate reasons."
