### Vural Mutlu 2017

Vural Mutlu, Koşar Mustafa Feridun, Kerimoğlu Orhan, Kızkapan Fatih, Kahyaoğlu Serdar, Tuğrul Sevil, İşleyen Hasan Burak. (2017). Cardiopulmonary resuscitation knowledge among nursing students: a questionnaire study. Anatolian Journal of Cardiology. https://doi.org/10.14744/AnatolJCardiol.2016.7156

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results section percentages for the compression-parameter items: "only 69.6% of students partially answered in a correct manner" on the ratio, and "only 33.2% of them answered all the options as correct" on depth (Figs. 2–3).

> "When we asked about the “universal compression-to-ventilation ratio” in different age groups, only 69.6% of students partially answered in a correct manner (Fig. 2). Regarding the depth of chest compression only 33.2% of them answered all the options as correct (Fig. 3)."
