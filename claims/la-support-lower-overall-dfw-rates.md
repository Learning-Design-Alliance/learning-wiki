## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen 2020

Ben Van Dusen and Jayson Nissen. (2020). Associations between learning assistants, passing introductory physics, and equity: A quantitative critical race theory investigation. Physical Review Physics Education Research 16, 010117. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.16.010117

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical generalized linear modeling of DFW outcomes for 2312 students in 41 sections at CSU Chico, restricted to instructors who had taught with LAs to control for self-selection. The overall model shows "predicted DFW rates of 31.1% without LAs and 17.1% with LAs". No effect size was printed.

> "When taught by the same instructor, courses in the introductory physics sequence have predicted DFW rates of 31.1% without LAs and 17.1% with LAs (Fig. 2). This difference was much larger than the uncertainty in the measurements."
