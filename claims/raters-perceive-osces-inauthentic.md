## Evidence

### Hyde Sarah 2022

Hyde Sarah, Fessey Christine, Boursicot Katharine, MacKenzie Rhoda, McGrath Deirdre. (2022). OSCE rater cognition – an international multi-centre qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-03077-w

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 31 think-aloud interviews with consultant-level examiners in England, Scotland and Ireland, watching trigger OSCE videos. The theme 'OSCEs are inauthentic' captured raters' views that scenarios are unrealistic and marking flawed; many participants used the word 'fake'.

> "Raters believe flaws are present in many aspects of the exam itself, the scenarios, the marking, and of most concern is the perceived negative influences these flaws have on student learning and behaviour."
