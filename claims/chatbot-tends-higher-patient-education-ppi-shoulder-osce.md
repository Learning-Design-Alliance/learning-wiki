## Evidence

### Lee Hye-Yoon 2025

Lee Hye-Yoon, Kim Jundong, Choi Hyojae, Bae Hyojin, Jeong Aram, Choi Sungyoul, Kim Ji-Hwan, Kim Chang-Eop. (2025). Comparing AI chatbot simulation and peer role-play for OSCE preparation: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08308-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot RCT OSCE results for the shoulder pain scenario: between-group Mann–Whitney comparisons of checklist percentage scores with Hedges' g and Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment. The chatbot group "recorded higher scores" in patient education and PPI, neither reaching adjusted significance.

> "Particularly in patient education (mean 85.0 vs. 66.7, Hedges’ g = 0.99, adjusted p = 0.1535) and PPI (84.7 vs. 78.4, Hedges’ g = 0.69, adjusted p = 0.1535), the AI chatbot group recorded higher scores, corresponding to large and moderate effect sizes, respectively."
