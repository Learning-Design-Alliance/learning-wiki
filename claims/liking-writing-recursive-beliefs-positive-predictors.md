## Evidence

### Wilson 2024

Wilson, J., Zhang, F., Palermo, C., Cordero, T. C., Myers, M. C., Eacker, H., Potter, A., & Coles, J. (2024). Predictors of middle school students’ perceptions of automated writing evaluation. Computers & Education, 211, 104985. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104985

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression results showing that writing-related beliefs and affect explained an additional 11.7% of variance in usability and 15.1% in usefulness in spring models. Liking writing and recursive process beliefs were consistently significant positive predictors.

> "Liking writing and recursive process beliefs increased in relative importance, with the latter exhibiting a doubling of effect size from fall to spring."
