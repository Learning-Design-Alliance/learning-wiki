Analytical critique in Sec. III.B of the assumption that fractional uncertainty in the primary eigenvalue equals the fractional uncertainty of each coordinate. The article notes this is a less restrictive alternative to computing coordinates for each adjusted density matrix. No effect size is printed.

> "However, this assumption causes the error bars to be proportional to the value of the coordinate, which may not accurately reﬂect the uncertainty in the class’s model state"
