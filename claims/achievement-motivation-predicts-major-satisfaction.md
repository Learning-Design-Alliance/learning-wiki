Alismail Abdulaziz Mohammed, Almulla Mazen Omar. (2025). Exploring the interplay between achievement motivation, students’ satisfaction with their major, and successful intelligence in supporting the sustainability of university education in SAUDI ARABIA. `q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 529 undergraduates from multiple Saudi faculties, analysed with structural equation modelling. The standardized path from achievement motivation to major satisfaction was significant, "β = 0.42, p <.001".

> "Achievement motivation exerts a substantial and statistically significant effect on major satisfaction (β = 0.42, p <.001)."
