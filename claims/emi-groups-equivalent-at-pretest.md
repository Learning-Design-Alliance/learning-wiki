## Evidence

### Mohamed Farrag Ahmed Badawi 2019

Mohamed Farrag Ahmed Badawi. (2019). The Effect of Explicit English Morphology Instruction on EFL Secondary School Students’ Morphological Awareness and Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 12(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v12n4p166

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test homogeneity checks on two intact classes (n=49 each): pre-RCT means 7.63 vs 8.06 (t = -1.029, p = 0.3062) and pre-MAT means 14.55 vs 15.08 (t = -0.35, p = 0.724), both statistically insignificant. Neither group passed the 10-point cut-off on the pre-RCT.

> "the calculated t is (-1.029) and the p value is (0.3062). By conventional criteria, this difference (P=0.3062) is considered to be statistically insignificant at 95% confidence interval"
