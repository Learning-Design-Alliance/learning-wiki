# Micro productive failure showed no effect on conceptual explanation, but a condition × time interaction emerged as the I-PS mean advantage at one day reversed at one week

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no main effect of condition on the conceptual explanation test. [→ Esther Ziegler 2021](#esther-ziegler-2021)
`q3 i2` A condition × time interaction was significant (η2p = 0.06): I-PS scored higher but nonsignificantly at T1 and lower but nonsignificantly at T2. [→ Esther Ziegler 2021 (2)](#esther-ziegler-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Esther Ziegler 2021

Esther Ziegler, Dragan Trninic, Manu Kapur. (2021). Micro productive failure and the acquisition of algebraic procedural knowledge. Instructional Science 49, 313–336. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09544-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on the conceptual explanation test, rated with a coding scheme (inter-rater κ = 88.2% and 87.0%); the article reports no condition main effect after covariates: "F(1,72) = 0.35, p = 0.558, η2p = 0.01".

> "There was no main effect of condition on conceptual explanation after controlling for mathematics grade and reasoning ability, F(1,72) = 0.35, p = 0.558, η2p = 0.01, and no main effect of time, F(1,72) = 0.14, p = 0.713, η2p = 0.00"

### Esther Ziegler 2021 (2)

Esther Ziegler, Dragan Trninic, Manu Kapur. (2021). Micro productive failure and the acquisition of algebraic procedural knowledge. Instructional Science 49, 313–336. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09544-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the same ANCOVA, the article reports a significant condition × time interaction with η2p = 0.06, and post hoc tests with d = 0.22 at T1 and d = 0.23 at T2, both nonsignificant, with the I-PS advantage reversing over the week.

> "However, there was an interaction of condition x time, F(1,72) = 4.46, p = 0.038, η2p = 0.06. Post hoc analyses showed a higher but nonsignificant mean score of the I-PS condition compared to the PS-I condition at T1, t(79) = − 0.91, p = 0.365, d = 0.22."
