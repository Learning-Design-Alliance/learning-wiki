## Evidence

### Roger E. Beaty & Paul J. Silvia 2013

Roger E. Beaty & Paul J. Silvia. (2013). Metaphorically speaking: cognitive abilities and the production of figurative language. Memory & Cognition. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-012-0258-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling of data from 191 undergraduates who completed three Gf tasks and two creative metaphor prompts rated by four raters. The model found fluid intelligence strongly predicted creative metaphors, with "β 0 .45, p 0 .017", described by the authors as large by Cohen's benchmarks.

> "As was e xpected, fluid intelligence strongly predicted creative metaphors ( β 0 .45, p 0 .017). This effect size can be interpreted as “large” using the benchmarks of .10 for small, .30 for medium, and .50 for large (Cohen, 1988)."
