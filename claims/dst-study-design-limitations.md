## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The study was retrospective, single-center, small-cohort, and DST was introduced midway through training, creating potential bias. [→ Kakiuchi Yoshihiko 2024](#kakiuchi-yoshihiko-2024)

## Evidence

### Kakiuchi Yoshihiko 2024

Kakiuchi Yoshihiko, Kuroda Shinji, Yoshida Yusuke, Kanaya Nobuhiko, Kashima Hajime, Kikuchi Satoru, Kagawa Shunsuke, Fujiwara Toshiyoshi. (2024). An effective surgical educational system in the era of robotic surgery: “Double-Surgeon Technique” in robotic gastrectomy for minimally invasive surgery. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03593-5

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' own stated limitations in the Discussion: the design was a retrospective single-center small cohort, and the mid-way introduction of DST means "there is a potential risk of bias regarding whether the observed effects are truly due to the educational impact of DST".

> "First, this study was not a randomized controlled trial, and instead retrospectively investigated a small cohort from a single institution. Second, the change to the DST was made midway after the start of regular LDG training."
