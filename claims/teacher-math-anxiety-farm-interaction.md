# Teachers' mathematics anxiety is positively associated with the mathematics anxiety of their low-SES students via a teacher-anxiety-by-FARM interaction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A significant teacher mathematics-anxiety-by-student FARM interaction (B = 0.39, p < .01) indicated that in classrooms led by teachers with high (+1 SD) mathematics anxiety, low-SES students' mathematics anxiety was higher than their high-SES peers'. [→ McLean 2023](#mclean-2023)

## Evidence

### McLean 2023

McLean, L., Janssen, J., Espinoza, P., Lindstrom Johnson, S., & Jimenez, M. (2023). Associations Between Teacher and Student Mathematics, Science, and Literacy Anxiety in Fourth Grade. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000790

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interactions model from the multiple regression analysis of 463 students in 33 classrooms, with cluster-robust standard errors, controlling for students' Time 1 mathematics anxiety. The printed interaction coefficient is B = 0.39, p < .01; no standardized effect size is reported, so impact is null.

> "The interactions model revealed a significant teacher mathematics-anxiety-by-student FARM interaction effect (B = 0.39, p < .01) on students' Time 2 mathematics anxiety."
