Evidence¶

de Koning 2025¶

de Koning, B., Zhang, S., & Sepp, S. (2025). Integrating Human Movement in Learning: Advancements in Language Instruction, Multimedia, and Theory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10027-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports the experiment by Sepp et al. (2024) on mimicking tracing and pointing gestures during timed multimedia worked examples on an iPad in geometry learning. The review states that, "Contrary to prior findings, adolescent participants who performed these gestures scored lower than those who did not." No effect size is printed.
