q2 · i? · review · r3

Qualitative synthesis of AI usage across the reviewed ITS. Camera/sensor systems retrieve skeleton representations for gesture recognition (dance, yoga, badminton), rule-based systems discern correct from incorrect poses via angles or distance-based metrics, and neural networks classify behavior such as CPR compression.

"However, the number of AI use cases for recommendations is low compared to those for feedback construction."
