## Evidence

### Cheng Luo Chih MD; Mao Wu Sheng MD; Kuei Chien Wen PhD; Sheng Huang Chen MD; Cheng Lin Wei MD; Chun Chang Yin MD 2016

Cheng Luo Chih MD; Mao Wu Sheng MD; Kuei Chien Wen PhD; Sheng Huang Chen MD; Cheng Lin Wei MD; Chun Chang Yin MD. (2016). Students' Interest in Surgery Affects Laparoscopic Practicing Performance. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2016.00039

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Linear regression analysis reported in Results and Figure 3, which prints a correlation coefficient of r = 0.89 with P < .001 for self-guided practice sessions against total average performance score. The article states the number of practice sessions "correlated significantly more with improvement of total performance scores".

> "We used linear regression analysis (Figure 3), demonstrating that the number of practice sessions correlated significantly more with improvement of total performance scores (P < .001)."
