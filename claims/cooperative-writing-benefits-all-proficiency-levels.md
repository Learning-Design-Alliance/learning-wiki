### Jiang 2016

Jiang, D. (2016). An Empirical Study on Alleviating Career English Writing Anxiety through Cooperative Learning in a Chinese Polytechnic Institute. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(1), 173. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n1p173

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interview and class-observation data. High-proficiency students reported being valued for sharp perspectives, countering the Confucian norm against individual showmanship; low-proficiency students contributed ideas despite accuracy limits and felt needed.

> "CL’s benefit to low proficient students has been revealed in previous research (for example Andrade & Williams, 2009; Wang, 2013), but in the Chinese context, high proficient ones also found CL helpful."
