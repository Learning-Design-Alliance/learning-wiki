Evidence¶

Wesley Pitts and Alison Lehner-Quam 2019¶

Wesley Pitts and Alison Lehner-Quam. (2019). Engaging the Framework for Information Literacy for Higher Education as a Lens for Assessment in an ePortfolio Social Pedagogy Ecosystem for Science Teacher Education. International Journal of ePortfolio, 9(1), 29-44. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1214596

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Case study coding of three in-service science teachers' information literacy ePortfolio entries across three modes (written ePortfolio, webinar, in-class presentation), coded for evidence of the research as inquiry frame's eight knowledge practices and nine dispositions. The study found "evidence was not consistent across all three forms of presentation."

"All three students demonstrated greater evidence of the knowledge practices and dispositions in their final in-class presentations and, for all three students, evidence was not consistent across all three forms of presentation."
