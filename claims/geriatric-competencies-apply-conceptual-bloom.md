`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Classification of all 257 competencies from the ten included studies using Bloom's revised taxonomy, conducted by four researchers with consensus resolution. The review reports "59.14% (152/257) of the geriatric core competencies were classified as Apply" and "64.2% (165/257) were classified as Conceptual".

> "In terms of Bloom’s taxonomy, 59.14% (152/257) of the geriatric core competencies were classified as Apply in the cognitive process dimension, and 64.2% (165/257) were classified as Conceptual in the knowledge dimension."
