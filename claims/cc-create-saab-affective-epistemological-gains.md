# Community college CREATE students showed significant self-rated gains in reading primary literature, understanding research, and maturation of epistemological beliefs, with no change in the knowledge-is-certain category

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Students reported significant gains in ability to read/understand primary literature (ES = 0.72), understanding of the research process (ES = 1.0), and influence of literature on that understanding (ES = 0.59), all p < 0.0001. [→ Kenyon Kristy L. 2016](#kenyon-kristy-l-2016)

## Evidence

### Kenyon Kristy L. 2016

Kenyon Kristy L., Onorato Morgan E., Gottesman Alan J., Hoque Jamila, Hoskins Sally G. (2016). Testing CREATE at Community Colleges: An Examination of Faculty Perspectives and Diverse Student Gains. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-07-0146

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Pre/post SAAB summary statements from five 2-yr campuses (n = 73), analyzed by Wilcoxon signed-rank test. All three summary gains were significant at p < 0.0001 with the printed ESs, e.g. "ES = 0.72" for reading primary literature.

> "they perceived significant gains in the ability to read and understand primary literature (p < 0.0001, ES = 0.72) and their understanding of the research process (p < 0.0001, ES = 1.0) and that reading scientific literature had informed their understanding of the research process (p < 0.0001, ES = 0.59)"
