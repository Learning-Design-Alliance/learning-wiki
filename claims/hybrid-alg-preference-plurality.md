`q2 i?` A plurality of students preferred a hybrid ALG format, and there was no significant difference in delivery-format preference across student groups (p = 0.2). [→ Malta Kiana 2025](#malta-kiana-2025)

## Evidence

### Malta Kiana 2025

Malta Kiana, Glickman Cynthia, Hunter Krystal, McBride Amanda. (2025). Comparing the impact of online and in-person active learning in preclinical medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06846-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey preference question analyzed by chi-square across Classes 2023–2025 with alpha 0.05. Among hybrid-preferring students in Classes 2023–2024 (n = 55), "47.3% chose a 25% virtual hybrid ALG" and 40.0% chose 50% virtual (Fig. 2).

> "while there was no significant difference across student groups regarding preference for a delivery method, 50.4% of students preferred a hybrid model versus 40.4% who wanted completely in person format, and a 9.2% minority of students were in favor of fully virtual ALG (p = 0.2)."
