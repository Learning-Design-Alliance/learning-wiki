# Feedback engagement indirectly associates GAA-LS scores with academic integrity intention in a cross-sectional structural model

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the structural model, GAA-LS scores were positively associated with feedback engagement (β = .56), which was positively associated with academic integrity intention (β = .37), with a significant indirect association (β = .21, 95% CI [.15, .28]). [→ Nie J 2026](#nie-j-2026)

## Evidence

### Nie J 2026

Nie J, Zhang Z, Lu X, Zhang Y and Zhang M (2026) Development and validation of the generative AI assessment literacy scale for higher education students: psychometric evidence and associations with feedback engagement and academic integrity. Front. Educ. 11:1934632. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1934632

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Study 2 structural equation model with 798 students, fit χ2(218) = 498.35, CFI = .955, RMSEA = .040; indirect effects estimated with 5,000 bootstrap samples. The model accounted for 31% of variance in feedback engagement and 36% in academic integrity intention.

> "Bootstrapping results further indicated a significant indirect association of GAA-LS scores with academic integrity intention through feedback engagement (β = .21, 95% CI [.15, .28]). The total effect was also significant (β = .45, 95% CI [.37, .53])."
