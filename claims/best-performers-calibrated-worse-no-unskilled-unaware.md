## Evidence

### Marloes L. Nederhand 2021

Marloes L. Nederhand, Huib K. Tabbers, Anique B.H. De Bruin, Remy M. J. P. Rikers. (2021). Metacognitive awareness as measured by second-order judgements among university and secondary school students. Metacognition and Learning, 16, 1-14. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09228-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive and pairwise comparison of calibration accuracy across performance quartiles among the 251 university students on the educational psychology exam. The top quartile was significantly less well calibrated than the bottom quartile; the article reports means and a p-value, no effect size.

> "pairwise comparisons even show that the 25% best performing students calibrate slightly worse ( M = 1.70, SD = 0.80) than the 25% lowest performing students ( M = 0.79, SD = 0.54 ), p < .001."
