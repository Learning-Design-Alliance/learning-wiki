## Evidence

### England Benjamin J. 2017

England Benjamin J., Brigati Jennifer R., Schussler Elisabeth E. (2017). Student anxiety in introductory biology classrooms: Perceptions about active learning and persistence in the major. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182506

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Survey comparisons of practice anxiety among the three courses; clicker anxiety differed among all three with printed effect size Cohen's f = 0.27, worksheet anxiety was higher in CMB 1 (3.51 ± 0.20) than CMB 2 or OEB (f = 0.25), and volunteering anxiety differed between OEB and CMB 2 (p = 0.015, f = 0.17).

> "Students in OEB had a mean of 2.26 ± 0.13, those in CMB 1 had a mean of 3.39 ± 0.20, and those in CMB 2 had a mean of 2.76 ± 0.12, with Cohen's f calculated as 0.27 (a medium effect)."
