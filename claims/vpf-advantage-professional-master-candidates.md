## Evidence

### Lu Chen 2025

Lu Chen, Gong Bin, Zhang Xiaochen, Wu Chengcheng, Yang Jin. (2025). Effectiveness of video-based peer feedback in self-training for skill acquisition among surgical residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08029-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis in the RCT (Table 3): PMM candidates' mean improvements favored VPF over VEF — wound care 29.3 (12.7) vs 13.1 (12.3) and catheterization 9.8 (6.2) vs 0.7 (3.2), both "P < 0.05"; suturing P = 0.28. Other residents: P = 0.62, 0.67, 0.84, no significant differences.

> "For these participants, Video Peer Feedback (VPF) proved superior to Video Expert Feedback (VEF) in both wound dressing change with suture removal, as well as indwelling urinary catheterization."
