## Evidence

### Mulvihill 2025

Mulvihill, A., Matthews, N., Chidley, K., Macmahon, S., Armitage, K. L., Sherlock, D., Dux, P. E., & Carroll, A. (2025). The meta-attention knowledge questionnaire (MAKQ): a new instrument for investigating meta-attention in a young adolescent sample. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09433-1

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Convergent validity analysis in Study Two (N = 83) correlating MAKQ dimensions with parent-rated Conners-3 and BRIEF-2; e.g., Conners DSM Inattentive Scale with meta-attention control r = −0.65, BRIEF Global Executive Composite r = −0.60, all p < .001 — correlations exceeding the |r| ≥ .37 threshold.

> "The MAKQ demonstrated convergent validity with parent report measures of attention and executive functioning, with all correlations statistically significant (see Table 6). Correlation co-efficients between parent report measures of attention and executive functioning and each of the three dimensions of the MAKQ ranged from moderate to strong negative associations."
