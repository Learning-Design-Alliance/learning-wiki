At the end of the first class period students wrote down and submitted one thing they liked and one thing they would change. The article reports descriptively that "Students generally liked that they were working together in groups." No statistics are printed.

> "Students generally liked that they were working together in groups. They were less happy that ideas were introduced before we explained the concepts to them."
