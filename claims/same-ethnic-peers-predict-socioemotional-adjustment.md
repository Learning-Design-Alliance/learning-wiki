# Having more same-racial/ethnic school peers predicts fewer externalizing behaviors and stronger interpersonal skills in kindergarten, independent of school diversity

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the joint model, racial/ethnic match was significantly associated with fewer teacher-rated externalizing symptoms (ß = −.03) and marginally associated with stronger interpersonal skills (ß = .03). [→ Benner Aprile D.; Crosnoe Robert 2011](#benner-aprile-d-crosnoe-robert-2011)
`q2 i?` No significant diversity × matching interactions emerged for either socioemotional outcome, and diversity itself was unrelated to socioemotional adjustment. [→ Benner Aprile D.; Crosnoe Robert 2011 (2)](#benner-aprile-d-crosnoe-robert-2011-2)
