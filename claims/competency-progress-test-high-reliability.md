### Wagener S 2015

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric analysis of the pilot administration of the formative competency-based progress test at eight medical schools. Internal consistency was computed for the whole test and, per the two blueprint axes, for subject groups and competency areas; the whole-test value was "α=0.954 (Cronbach's α)".

> "The reliability of the entire progress test (n=140) was α=0.954 (Cronbach's α)."
