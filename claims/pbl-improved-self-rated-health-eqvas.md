## Evidence

### Andreae Christina 2023

Andreae Christina, Tingström Pia, Nilsson Staffan, Jaarsma Tiny, Karlsson Nadine, Kärner Köhler Anita. (2023). Does problem-based learning improve patient empowerment and cardiac risk factors in patients with coronary heart disease in a Swedish primary care setting? A long-term prospective, randomised, parallel single randomised trial (COR-PRIM). BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2022-065230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stratified mixed-model analysis in the COR-PRIM randomised trial. PBL-group EQ-VAS rose from baseline M 68.13 to 77.33 at 5 years; the article reports "No significant changes were observed in the control group" at any follow-up.

> "EQ-VAS increased significantly at time 1 (M=74.64±18.05, p=0.026), time 3 (M=78.27±14.84, p=0.007) and time 5 (M=77.33±15.52, p=0.031) in the PBL group compared with baseline"

### Andreae Christina 2023 (2)

Andreae Christina, Tingström Pia, Nilsson Staffan, Jaarsma Tiny, Karlsson Nadine, Kärner Köhler Anita. (2023). Does problem-based learning improve patient empowerment and cardiac risk factors in patients with coronary heart disease in a Swedish primary care setting? A long-term prospective, randomised, parallel single randomised trial (COR-PRIM). BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2022-065230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus time × group interaction test for EQ-VAS in the trial's mixed-model analysis of the primary and secondary outcomes; significance at 5% triggered the stratified by-group analyses reported for this outcome.

> "The interaction between time and group was significant at the level of 5% (p=0.022). Therefore, the analysis was stratified by group."
