`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The WWC's effectiveness rating synthesizes eight studies (about 2,000 students) meeting WWC evidence standards with or without reservations. The report states Fast ForWord "was found to have no discernible effects" in two domains and "potentially positive effects" in the other two.

> "Fast ForWord® was found to have no discernible effects on the alphabetics and general literacy achievement domains, and potentially positive effects on the reading fluency and comprehension domains for adolescent learners."
