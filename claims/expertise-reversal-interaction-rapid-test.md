## Evidence

### Kalyuga 2004

Slava Kalyuga and John Sweller. (2004). Measuring Knowledge to Optimize Cognitive Load Factors During Instruction. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.96.3.558

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 3 with 42 Year 9 students used the rapid test to split learners into high- and low-knowledge groups randomly assigned to worked-examples or problem-solving instruction. The article reports the significant disordinal interaction above, indicating "the most efficient mode of instruction depends on the level of learners’ knowledge".

> "There was a significant knowledge–instructional format disordinal inter- action for the performance indicator measured by the rapid testing method, F(1, 38) ⫽ 9.04, MSE ⫽ 15.17, p ⬍ .01, Cohen's f effect-size index ⫽ 0.96, suggesting that the most efficient mode of instruction depends on the level of learners’ knowledge."
