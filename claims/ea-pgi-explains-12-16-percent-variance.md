Prediction analysis in three European-ancestry holdout samples (Add Health, HRS, WLS), with PGIs from a meta-analysis excluding those samples. The article reports the LDpred PGI "explains 15.8% of the variance in EduY ears in Add Health and 12.0% in HRS".

> "This PGI explains 15.8% of the variance in EduY ears in Add Health and 12.0% in HRS (Extended Data Fig. 6). The sample-size-weighted mean is 13.3%."
