## Evidence

### Boris Ruf 2024

Boris Ruf, Marcin Detyniecki. (2024). The «Huh?» Button: Improving Understanding in Educational Videos with Large Language Models. [doi:10.1109/ism63611.2024.00064](https://doi.org/10.1109/ism63611.2024.00064)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Emission measurement using the Python library "API Emission Tracker" during development for the English computer science lecture, explanations generated by GPT-4 for a duration of 13 minutes and 35 seconds of video; footprint "estimated at 150.7 kg CO2e".

> "In total, 390,962 prompt tokens and 37,435 completion tokens were used and the carbon footprint was estimated at 150.7 kg CO2e."

### Boris Ruf 2024 (2)

Boris Ruf, Marcin Detyniecki. (2024). The «Huh?» Button: Improving Understanding in Educational Videos with Large Language Models. [doi:10.1109/ism63611.2024.00064](https://doi.org/10.1109/ism63611.2024.00064)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same measurement for the German philosophy lecture: explanations generated for a duration of 15 minutes and 35 seconds (timestamps 00:30 to 16:05), with the same GPT-4 model; footprint "estimated carbon footprint amounted to 209.4 kg CO2e".

> "In total, 531,619 prompt tokens and 63,727 completion tokens were used and the estimated carbon footprint amounted to 209.4 kg CO2e."
