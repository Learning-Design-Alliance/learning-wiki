### C. Srinivas 2026

C. Srinivas, E.E. Firat, R. S. Laramee, A. P. Joshi. (2026). Mastery Learning Improves Performance on Complex Tasks on PCP Literacy Test. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1109/mcg.2026.3671005](https://doi.org/10.1109/mcg.2026.3671005)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results Summary of a between-group intervention in which undergraduate students in a Data Visualization course were assigned to an ML or a Control group and completed summative assessments per module. The article reports that for the Analyze module "the students in the ML group performed better than the students in the Control group".

> "For the Analyze module, which is a more difficult module, we see that the students in the ML group performed better than the students in the Control group."

### C. Srinivas 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results Summary of the same intervention's Remember module summative assessment. ML students' accuracy was "slightly higher with a median of 100" while Control students had "a slightly lower median score", indicating near-ceiling performance in both groups.

> "For the Remember module, the accuracy of the students in the ML (purple) group was slightly higher with a median of 100, whereas the students in the Control (green) group had a slightly lower median score."
