# Frequency-domain HRV metrics are associated with expert status but not with age in multivariate regression

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2022 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In multivariate regression, several frequency-domain HRV metrics (HF%, LF/HF, LF n.u., HF n.u.) were significantly influenced by expert status but not by participants' age. [→ Hinzmann Dominik 2022](#hinzmann-dominik-2022)

## Evidence

### Hinzmann Dominik 2022

Hinzmann Dominik, Singer Maximilian, Schmelter Valerie, Kreiser Kornelia, Gehling Kim, Ströber Lea, Kirschke Jan S, Schulz Christian M and Schneider Frederick. (2022). Differences in beginner and expert neurointerventionalists” heart rate variability during simulated neuroangiographies. Interventional Neuroradiology. https://doi.org/10.1177/15910199221128439

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate regression analysis of HRV metrics on expert status and age (Table 4); HF% p < 0.001 for expert status, HF% age p = 0.944; LF/HF expert p < 0.001, age p = 0.978. From the time domain, mean RR, median RR and mean HR were influenced by both expert status and age.

> "Likewise, five frequency domain measures were influenced by expert status but not by the age of the participants."
