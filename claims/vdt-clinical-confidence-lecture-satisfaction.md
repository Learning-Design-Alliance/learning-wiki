Gerardi Davide, Torge Diana, Bernardi Sara, Bianchi Serena, Macchiarelli Guido. (2025). Use of two virtual dissection tables in a postgraduate oral anatomy course: feedback from the students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07840-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Anonymous perception survey of the 24-resident postgraduate dental cohort, delivered two weeks after the end of the anatomy course. The article reports that "A majority of students (67%) reported improved clinical confidence and 96% were satisfied with the lecture", significant by Fisher's exact test (p < 0.05 for both items in Table 3).
