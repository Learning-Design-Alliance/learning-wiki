### James Schombert 2010

James Schombert. (2010). Introductory Astronomy as a Measure of Grade Inﬂation. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1008.4410](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1008.4410)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Score-distribution analysis (Table 2) of 794 students across four identical ASTR122 terms, graded on an objective pass/fail line at 45% with C at 45–60%, B at 60–73% and A above 73%. The article reports "About 41% of the students achieved a level of ‘satisfactory’ performance" and 22% good, 8% superior marks.
