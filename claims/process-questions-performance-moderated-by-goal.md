# Process-related questioning predicts better problem-solving performance only for students given learning goals

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The GOAL X PRO interaction significantly contributed to predicting Day 3 performance over and above math achievement; PRO correlated with performance under learning goals (r = .30 at Grade 6) but not performance goals. [→ Newman 1995](#newman-1995)

## Evidence

### Newman 1995

Richard S. Newman and Mahna T. Schwager. (1995). Students' Help Seeking During Problem Solving: Effects of Grade, Goal, and Prior Achievement. American Educational Research Journal, 32(2), 352-376. https://www.jstor.org/stable/1163435

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise regression of Day 3 performance on goal, math achievement, PRO frequency, and interactions was significant at both grades; the GOAL X PRO term contributed over and above MATH. Printed correlations include r = .30, p < .05, at Grade 6.

> "Zero-order correlations showed that a greater frequency of PRO requests was related to better performance for students given learning goals, rs = .28, p < .10, and .30, p < .05, at Grades 3 and 6, respectively, but not for students given performance goals, rs = .17 and -.12, ns."
