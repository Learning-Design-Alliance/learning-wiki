Evidence¶

Heyne 2012¶

Heyne, T., & Bogner, F. X. (2012). Guided Learning at Workstations about Drug Prevention with Low Achievers in Science Education. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v2n6p1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Mann-Whitney-U comparison of R subgroups (Table 4: G2R vs G1R, e.g., Z = -4.000, p < .001 actual; Z = -3.425, p = .001 persistent); the article reports "significant higher scores in the actual and persistent learning success". No effect size printed.

"G2R students also achieved significant higher scores in the actual and persistent learning success compared to the G1R students."

Heyne 2012 (2)¶

Heyne, T., & Bogner, F. X. (2012). Guided Learning at Workstations about Drug Prevention with Low Achievers in Science Education. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v2n6p1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Mann-Whitney-U comparison of R subgroups in guided versus teacher-centered instruction (Table 4: e.g., Z = -3.405, p = .001 actual; Z = -3.666, p < .001 persistent); the article notes differences appeared "in the comparison between G2 R (student-centered guided) and G3 R (teacher-centered)". No effect size printed.

"G2R students achieved significant higher scores in the actual and persistent learning success compared to the G3R students."
