# Years of musculoskeletal ultrasound experience correlate positively with pre- and post-course self-reported confidence but negatively with self-reported confidence improvement

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Years of MSK USG experience show a moderate positive correlation with pre-course confidence across hip (r = 0.529), knee (r = 0.500), and ankle (r = 0.542) joints. [→ Zure Mert 2025](#zure-mert-2025)
`q3 i2` A weaker positive correlation holds for post-course confidence (hip r = 0.223, knee r = 0.330, ankle r = 0.229). [→ Zure Mert 2025 (2)](#zure-mert-2025-2)
`q3 i3` Self-reported confidence improvement scores correlate negatively with experience (hip r=-0.422, knee r=-0.373, ankle r=-0.424). [→ Zure Mert 2025 (3)](#zure-mert-2025-3)

## Evidence

### Zure Mert 2025

Zure Mert, Topaloğlu Mahir, İş Enes Efe, Sezikli Selim, Sarıkaya Deniz, Dıraçoğlu Demirhan. (2025). Advancing skills and confidence in ultrasound-guided injection techniques: the impact of a cadaveric workshop. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07270-z

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis among the 109 workshop participants between years of MSK USG experience and pre-course confidence; all three correlations are moderate, positive, and significant at p < 0.01, with r = 0.529 for hip.

> "Our findings further show a moderate positive correlation between years of musculoskeletal USG experience and pre-course confidence in joint injections across the hip (r = 0.529, p < 0.01), knee (r = 0.500, p < 0.01), and ankle (r = 0.542, p < 0.01) joints."

### Zure Mert 2025 (2)

Zure Mert, Topaloğlu Mahir, İş Enes Efe, Sezikli Selim, Sarıkaya Deniz, Dıraçoğlu Demirhan. (2025). Advancing skills and confidence in ultrasound-guided injection techniques: the impact of a cadaveric workshop. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07270-z

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Same Pearson correlation analysis for post-course confidence; correlations are weaker (e.g., hip r = 0.223) but remain statistically significant across all three joints.

> "A weaker yet similar correlation was also observed in post-course confidence scores across all hip (r = 0.223, p = 0.02), knee (r = 0.330, p < 0.01), and ankle (r = 0.229, p = 0.017) joints."

### Zure Mert 2025 (3)

Zure Mert, Topaloğlu Mahir, İş Enes Efe, Sezikli Selim, Sarıkaya Deniz, Dıraçoğlu Demirhan. (2025). Advancing skills and confidence in ultrasound-guided injection techniques: the impact of a cadaveric workshop. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07270-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Same correlation analysis for improvement scores: more experienced participants showed smaller confidence gains, with negative correlations (hip r=-0.422) significant at p < 0.01 for all three joints.

> "Similarly, a meaningful yet negative correlation was observed in terms of improvement scores across hip (r=-0.422, p < 0.01), knee (r=-0.373, p < 0.01), and ankle (r=-0.424, p < 0.01) joints."
