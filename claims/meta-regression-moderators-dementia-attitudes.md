`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-regression across the 13 included studies. The review also reports interdisciplinary interventions showed a significant positive association (β = 6.96, p = 0.039), while artistic format and face-to-face interaction did not reach significance when adjusted for covariates.

> "Meta-regression showed that several moderators jointly explained a large proportion of the between-study variance (Adj R2 = 87.96%), with a statistically significant model fit [F (5,7) = 4.68, p = 0.034). Specifically, a higher percentage of female participants was significantly associated with greater improvements in attitudes (β = 0.116, p = 0.014), and studies with larger total sample sizes tended to report smaller effect sizes (β = –0.003, p = 0.011)."
