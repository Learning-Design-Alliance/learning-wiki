## Evidence

### Samina S. Masood 2014

Samina S. Masood. (2014). How to Control a Decrease in Physics Enrollment?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1405.6442](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1405.6442)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r1`

From the article's conclusions: the author's qualitative argument that evaluation procedures are "designed for popularity of courses" and ignore instructors' role in raising standards or recruiting students. No data or test is reported.

> "Instructor evaluation procedure includes certa in points, which does not evaluate a quality teacher, they evaluate a dutiful teacher. It is a discouragement for those teachers who want to work for the development of the program but at the same time, want to keep their jobs."

### Samina S. Masood 2014 (2)

Samina S. Masood. (2014). How to Control a Decrease in Physics Enrollment?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1405.6442](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1405.6442)
