Evidence¶

Randler et al. 2016¶

Christoph Randler, Peter Wüst-Ackermann & Eda Demirhan. (2016). Humor Reduces Anxiety and Disgust in Anticipation of an Educational Dissection in Teacher Students. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(4), 421-432. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1094636

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

General linear model with post-treatment STAI-S anxiety as dependent variable, treatment as fixed factor and pretest anxiety as covariate. The humor group scored lower (1.84 ± 0.03 vs 1.97 ± 0.03), with "about 8% of the variance" explained (partial eta-squared 0.081, Table 3).

"Anxiety was lower in the humor film group (1.84 ± 0.03) compared to the life history film group (1.97 ± 0.03). The treatment explained about 8% of the variance."
