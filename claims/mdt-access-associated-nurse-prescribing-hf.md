## Evidence

### Hill L 2024

Hill L, Girerd N, Castiello T, Jaarsma T, Metra M, Rosano G, Savage P, Schuuring MJ, Simpson M, Uchmanowicz I, Volterrani M, Williams R, Lambrinou E, Hage C. (2024). Examining the clinical role and educational preparation of heart failure nurses across Europe. A survey of the Heart Failure Association (HFA) of the European Society of Cardiology (ESC) and the Association of Cardiovascular Nursing and Allied Professions (ACNAP) of the ESC. European Journal of Heart Failure. https://doi.org/10.1002/ejhf.3519

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey analysis of 837 HF nurses from 15 ESC countries, with cross tabulation and chi-square testing of the relationship between access to a multidisciplinary HF team and prescribing. The article reports "42% vs. 31.7%, p = 0.04" for this association; no effect size is printed.

> "Respondents that could prescribe HF medication were noted to be more likely to have access to a specialist HF MDT (42% vs. 31.7%, p = 0.04)."
