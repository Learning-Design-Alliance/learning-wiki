q2 · i? · associational · r1

Transition analysis of fixations parsed into bigrams and trigrams across the three AOIs in the within-subjects gaze study. The article reports the printed transition counts and the 12/19 starting pattern; the authors interpret this as learners coordinating rather than isolating views.

"Memory↔Code transitions exceeded 11k each, with Metaphor selectively consulted in Memory→Metaphor→Memory cycles (Figure 6). Most participants (12/19) began with Memory, then cycled between Code and Memory while drawing in Metaphor as needed."
