Correlational analysis by ACCESS score level in Chicago K-2 data. For students scoring above 270 on ACCESS, the relationship with other assessments was very strong, allowing good cross-test prediction.

> "The assessments not designed for MLLs do not differentiate ELA skills for students with very low ACCESS scores (i.e., low English proficiency), but the same tests are strongly aligned with ACCESS scores among students with moderate-to-high ACCESS scores."
