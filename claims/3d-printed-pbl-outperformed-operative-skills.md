## Evidence

### Wang Xiao-Le 2025

Wang Xiao-Le, Xie Ying, Yue Ye-Xin, Yang Xue-Wen, Li Jian. (2025). A novel paradigm in cleft lip education: integration of 3D-printed simulator and problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07719-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Operative assessment scored by two professional doctors with five years of clinical experience, compared by independent samples t-test (n = 20 per group). Incision design: 7.7 ± 1.129 vs 6.25 ± 1.118 (t = 4.082, P < 0.001); upper lip symmetry: 7.7 ± 1.342 vs 6.15 ± 1.226 (t = 3.814, P < 0.001).

> "Additionally, the experimental group students demonstrated significantly higher scores compared to the traditional group in improvement of columella deformity, symmetry of nostril size, and upper lip symmetry (Table 3) [See Supplementary Table 5]."
