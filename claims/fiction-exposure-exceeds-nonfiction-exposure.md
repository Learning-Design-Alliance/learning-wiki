### Song 2025

Song, M., Li, L., He, D. & Cai, Q. (2025). Reading experience reveals shared and idiosyncratic neural patterns during text comprehension. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00390-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Dependent t-tests comparing exposure averaged across fiction topics (literary fiction, mystery and suspense, young adult, drama, poetry) versus non-fiction topics (prose, history, philosophy, self-help) in the 39-participant sample. Only p-values are printed; no effect size is reported.

> "dependent t-tests showed that participants had overall higher exposure in fiction topics than non-fiction topics, for the entire group (N = 39, p < .001), the high- (N = 20, p < .01) and low- (N = 19, p < .05) exposure group"
