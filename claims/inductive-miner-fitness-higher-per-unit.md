Process discovery analysis of Moodle event logs from 101 students, applying Inductive Miner to whole-course and per-unit sub-files and evaluating fitness. The article reports that "IM algorithm fitness scored higher by units than when looking at the whole course"; Table 3 prints fitness values per unit and grouping.
