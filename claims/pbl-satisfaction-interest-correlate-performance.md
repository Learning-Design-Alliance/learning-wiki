# PBL raises student satisfaction and learning interest, and both correlate positively with final overall academic performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` PBL students reported significantly higher satisfaction scores (p < 0.001) and learning interest scores (p < 0.001) than LBL students. [→ Zeng Meichun 2026](#zeng-meichun-2026)
`q2 i?` Correlation analysis demonstrated a significant positive relationship between final overall scores and both satisfaction and learning interest (Pearson's correlation). [→ Zeng Meichun 2026](#zeng-meichun-2026)

## Evidence

### Zeng Meichun 2026

Zeng Meichun, Jia Qingjun. (2026). Application of problem-based learning in EBERs-ISH theoretical and practical teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08864-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mechanism analysis in the RCT measured satisfaction via a 10-item university evaluation form and interest via the 9-item SIQ-SF, comparing groups with a two-sided Mann-Whitney U test and correlating scores with final performance using Pearson's correlation coefficient.

> "Students in the PBL group reported substantially higher satisfaction scores (p < 0.001, Fig. 5A) and learning interest scores (p < 0.001, Fig. 5B) compared to their counterparts in the LBL group."
