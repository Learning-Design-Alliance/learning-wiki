The authors characterize the monotonic pattern as "descriptive associations" that may reflect differences in study purpose, venue, task, and other characteristics rather than a causal effect of complexity.

"Reporting coverage increases across the observed LLM workflow categories, from prompt-only to engineered and retrieval/adapted/agentic methods. These are descriptive associations and may also reflect differences in study purpose, venue, task, and other characteristics."
