Ciğercci FM and Uygun N (2026). Evaluation of primary school teachers' use and perceptions of artificial intelligence in primary school mathematics instruction: a mixed-methods study. Front. Psychol. 17:1741116. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1741116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent-samples t-tests by prior AI training status in the quantitative phase (N = 302; 58 trained, 244 not). The article reports the differences as statistically significant with no effect size printed for this finding.

> "Teachers who had received prior AI-related training reported signiﬁcantly higher scores than those without training in the overall score and in the willingness, attitudes, and personal- experiences dimensions."
