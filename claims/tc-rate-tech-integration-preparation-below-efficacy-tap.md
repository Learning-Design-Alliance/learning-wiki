## Evidence

### Buss 2017

Buss, R. R., Lindsey, L., Foulger, T. S., Wetzel, K., & Pasquel, S. (2017). Assessing a technology infusion approach in a teacher preparation program. International Journal of Technology in Teaching and Learning, 13(1), 33-44. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Assessing+a+technology+infusion+approach+in+a+teacher+preparation+program

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Survey of 237 finishing teacher candidates using a repeated measures ANOVA across five Likert constructs. The article reports "F(4, 748) = 31.57, p < .001, η 2 = .144, which was a large effect size"; post hoc tests showed Technology Integration (M = 3.84) did not differ from Program Preparation Factor 1 (M = 3.90) but was lower than Teaching Efficacy (M = 4.06) and TAP Indicators (M = 4.01).
