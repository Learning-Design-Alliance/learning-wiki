Analysis of teacher-level average student gain on interim math assessments (grades 2-6) after controlling for prior achievement, comparing Exemplar, Partner-school, non-partner PLI, and non-PLI teachers (Figure 14). The report offers explanatory factors: only a minority of Partner school teachers were PLI teachers, and Partner teachers received fewer PD opportunities.

> "The box plots in Figure 14 suggest that achievement gains were highest for PLI Exemplar teachers, followed by PLI teachers in non-partner schools. Gains for students of teachers in PLI Partner schools were not higher than those of non PLI teachers districtwide."
