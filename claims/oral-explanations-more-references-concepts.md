## Evidence

### Jacob 2019

Jacob, L., Lachner, A., & Scheiter, K. (2019). Learning by Explaining Orally or In Written Form? Text Difficulty Matters. https://aspredicted.org/sv3zv.pdf

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory independent t-tests comparing features of explanations generated in the oral versus written conditions. The article reports higher social presence via personal references (d = 0.50), more concepts (d = 0.83), but no significant difference in elaborations (d = 0.33, p = .155).

> "Oral explanations contained more personal references (e.g., “I”, “you”, etc.) than written explanations, t(77) = 2.20, p = .033, d = 0.50, indicating that the social presence was higher in the oral condition than in the written condition (see Table 1)."
