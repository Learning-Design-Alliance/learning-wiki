## Evidence

### Eleanor C. Sayre 2016

Eleanor C. Sayre, Claudia Fracchiolla, Ben Van Dusen. (2016). iPad Screencasts as Evidence of Accountable Disciplinary Knowledge. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1605.08105](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1605.08105)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The article's introduction states this as a finding from prior research, cited to support the argument that end-of-chapter exercises teach a narrow kind of problem solving. The asymmetry runs from conceptual problem solving to exercise solving, not the reverse.

> "Research has shown that proﬁciency at solving end-of-chapter exercises does not promote pro- ﬁciency at solving conceptual problems, although pro- ﬁciency at conceptual problem solving promotes pro- ﬁciency at exercise solving 4,5."
