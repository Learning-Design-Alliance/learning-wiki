## Evidence

### Angel Blanch 2015

Angel Blanch. (2015). Evaluating fluid and crystallized abilities in the performance of an educational process. Springer Verlag. http://hdl.handle.net/10459.1/48328

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Conditional latent curve model on the BURN11 dataset (101 high-school students, four achievement measures across a one-week instructional project). The article reports "a significant negative effect of Gf on the slope factor (–.23)" and "a positive significant effect on the intercept factor (.49)" for Gc; no standardized effect sizes were printed.

> "Model A showed a significant negative effect of Gf on the slope factor (–.23) and a null effect on the intercept factor (.07), whereas Gc had a positive significant effect on the intercept factor (.49) although a null effect on the slope factor (–.06)."
