## Evidence

### Deutsch G.B. 2015

Deutsch G.B., Sathyanarayana S.A., Giangola M., Akerman M., DeNoto G., Klein J.D.S., Zemon H., Rubach E. (2015). Competence Acquisition for Single-Incision Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2014.00116

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Observational correlation across the 290-case retrospective cohort. The article reports a "small positive correlation between BMI and total procedure time (ρ = 0.16, P < .0063)"; ρ = 0.16 is a small effect.

> "Irrespective of surgeon, there was a small positive correlation between BMI and total procedure time (ρ = 0.16, P < .0063)."

### Deutsch G.B. 2015 (2)

Deutsch G.B., Sathyanarayana S.A., Giangola M., Akerman M., DeNoto G., Klein J.D.S., Zemon H., Rubach E. (2015). Competence Acquisition for Single-Incision Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2014.00116

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

BMI-stratified analysis (normal, overweight, obese groups) per surgeon. Surgeons 3 and 4 showed significant positive correlations; no significant difference for surgeons 1 and 2. Effect sizes are small to moderate correlations.

> "However, surgeon 3 (ρ = 0.24, P < .0169) and surgeon 4 (ρ = 0.34, P < .0031) had a significantly positive correlation between their case times and increasing BMI."
