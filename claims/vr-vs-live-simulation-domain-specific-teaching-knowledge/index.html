Evidence¶

Ke 2021¶

Ke, F., Dai, Z., Pachman, M. et al. (2021). Exploring multiuser virtual teaching simulation as an alternative learning environment for student instructors. Instr Sci, 49, 831–854. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09555-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized comparison in which 40 university teaching assistants in a 4-h training session were assigned to "a VR simulation or a live classroom simulation condition." The study found a crossover pattern: VR favored lab-teaching knowledge, live simulation favored class-teaching knowledge. No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"The study indicated that the VR simulation better promoted the lab-teaching knowledge development than the live simulation, whereas the latter better fostered class-teaching knowledge development."
