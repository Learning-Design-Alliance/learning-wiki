Qualitative thematic analysis of participant reflections, instructor observations, and debrief transcripts from Activity 1 of the capstone course, reported in Results section 4.1. Participants "frequently identified conceptual inaccuracies, over-simplifications, or flawed analogies" in chatbot output. No effect sizes are reported.
