Yunita Arian Sani Anwar 2018¶

Yunita Arian Sani Anwar, Senam, & Endang W Laksono. (2018). Meaningful Biochemistry Learning Using the Orientation-Decision-Do-Discuss-Reflect (OD3R) Method. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 17-30. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.1132a

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Supporting free-response data collected by asking students' opinions about the OD3R implementation. Comments include that the method "makes the biochemistry course more fun" and one student reported previously lacking confidence in writing laboratory reports. This is descriptive opinion data, not a tested outcome.
