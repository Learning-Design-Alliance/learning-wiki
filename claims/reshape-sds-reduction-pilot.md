`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group changes at the 16-month primary end point in a pilot cluster randomized trial (33 PCPs per group at end line), estimated using random-effects models; decreases in SDS scores indicate lower stigma. The study design precluded between-group hypothesis testing.

> "For the primary stigma outcome, SDS, there was a mean change from baseline to end line of −10.6 points (95% CI, −14.5 to −6.74 points) for PCPs in the RESHAPE group compared with −2.8 points (95% CI, −8.29 to 2.70 points) in the control group"
