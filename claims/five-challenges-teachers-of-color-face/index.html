Evidence¶

Davis Dixon 2019¶

Davis Dixon, Ashley Griffin, and Mark Teoh. (2019). If You Listen, We Will Stay: Why Teachers of Color Leave and How to Disrupt Teacher Turnover. The Education Trust & Teach Plus. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED603193 doi:10.3102/1445541

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative focus groups led by Teach Plus Policy Fellows with 88 teachers who identify as Black or Latino across five states; the report states "five challenges emerged that teachers of color face in the workforce," beginning with an antagonistic work culture leaving them feeling "unwelcome and/or invisible."

"For the focus groups, five challenges emerged that teachers of color face in the workforce: 1) They experience an antagonistic work culture that leaves them feeling unwelcome and/or invisible."
