## Evidence

### Zhang J 2026

Zhang J, Sachdeva M, McCaig C, Gordon E, Shapiro L, Keenan ID. (2026). Exploring 3D anatomy: A free online course supporting novice learner inquiry and spatial conceptualization. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70288

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The article's narrative review of prior literature reports that the lack of direct influence on anatomy knowledge test scores is likely because three-dimensional spatial anatomy is not typically or explicitly tested in assessments. The article also notes a reciprocal relationship, with anatomical dissection shown to improve spatial ability and spatial awareness and ability enhancing surgical skills.

> "This idea is supported by studies investigating spatial anatomy performance following three-dimensional learning activities and spatial training, with statistically significant improvements reported."
