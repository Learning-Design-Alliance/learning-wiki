## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Self-efficacy beliefs were positively associated with student-oriented mastery goals, whereas the linkage from personal learning goals was not statistically significant once self-efficacy was included. [→ Daumiller 2025](#daumiller-2025)

## Evidence

### Daumiller 2025

Daumiller, M., Gaspard, H., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2025). Bridging teacher motivation and instruction: Relevance of student‐oriented goals for teaching alongside personal achievement goals and self‐efficacy. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S98–S114. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12776

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-level path modelling of baseline motivation measures predicting student-oriented goals at the teacher level. The article reports self-efficacy beliefs were "positively associated with the latter" (student-oriented mastery goals), with no significant learning-goals linkage.

> "Unlike Daumiller, Fasching, et al. (2022) we did not observe a statistically significant linkage between personal learning goals and student‐oriented mastery goals; however, self‐efficacy beliefs were positively associated with the latter."
