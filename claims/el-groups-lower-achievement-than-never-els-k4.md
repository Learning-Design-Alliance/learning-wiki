## Evidence

### Johnson 2022

Johnson, A. (2022). Achievement and growth for English Learners. (NWEA Center for School and Student Progress Working Paper). https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/08959048211049419

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of MAP Growth scores (fall, winter, spring) for a kindergarten cohort of more than 56,000 students in 915 schools, plotted by group in Figure 2. The study found "never-ELs consistently had the highest test scores" across subjects and time, with the group ordering ever-ELs, always-ELs, dually-identified below them.

> "In both math and English reading and across time, never-ELs consistently had the highest test scores which were above the national average, followed by ever-ELs, always-ELs, then dually-identified students."
