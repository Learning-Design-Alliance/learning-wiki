### Manuaba Ida Bagus Amertha Putra 2022

Manuaba Ida Bagus Amertha Putra, No Yi, Wu Chien-Chih. (2022). The effectiveness of problem based learning in improving critical thinking, problem-solving and self-directed learning in first-year medical students: A meta-analysis. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0277339

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of three articles evaluating self-directed learning, using a fixed-effect model with no heterogeneity (I2 = 0%). The overall effect was "more inclined toward the conventional method" but "there was no statistical difference (p = 0.34)".

> "Self-directed learning was evaluated by using a fixed-effect model. The heterogeneity by using the I2 parameter has shown no heterogeneity (0% = might be unimportant)—the overall effect was more inclined toward the conventional method for enhancing self-directed learning. However, there was no statistical difference (p = 0.34) (Fig 5)."
