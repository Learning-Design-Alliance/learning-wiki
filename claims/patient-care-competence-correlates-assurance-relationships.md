## Evidence

### Ulupınar Fadime 2025

Ulupınar Fadime, Karasu Şeyda. (2025). Perceived vs. experienced care: a comparison between nursing students’ self-assessed competence and patients’ reported care. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12880-w

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (SPSS 27.0) between NSCS and PPNSCS subscales for 167 matched dyads. The entry reports the printed correlations "with Assurance (r = 0.646, p ≤ 0.05)" and r = 0.639 for Interpersonal Relationships; correlations are cross-sectional associations, not causal effects.

> "Similarly, the Patient Care subscale exhibited particularly robust associations with Assurance (r = 0.646, p ≤ 0.05) and Interpersonal Relationships (r = 0.639, p ≤ 0.05), indicating that students’ confidence in caregiving could be reflected in patients’ emotional and interactive experiences."
