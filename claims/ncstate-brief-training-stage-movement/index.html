Observational case study of NC State AI literacy initiatives (Fall 2024–Spring 2026) engaging more than 330 participants via post-workshop feedback surveys, instructor observation, and student-produced artifacts. The authors state "No formal pre/post validated assessment instrument was administered, and no controlled comparison group was employed."
