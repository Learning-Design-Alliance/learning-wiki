## Evidence

### Noroozi 2012

Noroozi, O., Busstra, M. C., Mulder, M., Biemans, H. J. A., Tobi, H., Geelen, A., van't Veer, P., Chizari, M. (2012). Online discussion compensates for suboptimal timing of supportive information presentation in a digitally supported learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev (2012) 60:193-221. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-011-9217-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null contrast reported for the ID timing condition in the sequential experiment: after optimal (during-task) presentation of supportive information in IDLM, the type of collaboration in phase 2 made no detectable difference to test 2 knowledge construction quality. Equivalence was not tested; the detailed post-hoc statistics lie beyond the supplied text.

> "When supportive information had been presented during the learning task clusters, however, the online and personal discussion conditions had no differential effect on knowledge construction."
