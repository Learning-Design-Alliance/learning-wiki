### Chen Daoming 2026

Chen Daoming, Li Chenxi, Su Yubin, Wu Fang, Fang Xiaoru, Chu Hongwei, Bai Shuang. (2026). Patient Safety Competency Among Operating Room Nurses: Associations With Safety Attitudes, Systems Thinking and Burnout in a Multicentre Cross‐Sectional Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70831

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis among 443 operating room nurses. Both effect sizes are below r = .10. The authors note both dimensions became significant after adjustment, with emotional exhaustion showing an unexpected positive coefficient possibly reflecting a suppression effect.

> "Neither emotional exhaustion (r = −0.049, p = 0.304) nor depersonalization (r = −0.092, p = 0.052) was significantly correlated with the total PSC score"
