Kim Yeonwoo, Calzada Esther J., Barajas-Gonzalez R. Gabriela, Huang Keng-Yen, Brotman Laurie M., Castro Ashley, Pichardo Catherine. (2018). The Role of Authoritative and Authoritarian Parenting in the Early Academic Achievement of Latino Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000192

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group SEM results for MA students; the DA model showed authoritarian parenting associated with more problem behaviors (standardized coefficient = .17). The article reports "more problem behaviors" and the listed negative associations as significant standardized paths.

> "Authoritarian parenting practices, in contrast, were associated with more problem behaviors (standardized coefficient = .21), fewer adaptive behaviors (standardized coefficient = −.22), and lower academic readiness (standardized coefficient = −.14) in students."
