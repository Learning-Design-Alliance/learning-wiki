### Nunez-Rocha RE 2026

Nunez-Rocha RE, Castillo-Flores S, Abreu AA, Ramirez R, Pettigrew M, Garces-Palacios S, Al Abbas A, Guzzetta AA, Scott DJ, Zeh HJ 3rd, Sankaranarayanan G, Polanco PM. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective single-institution study of 30 residents in a skill decay group returning after a median 91-day gap; paired comparisons showed OSATS decline, with the article reporting "declined to 28 (IQR: 26.2–29) on their return attempt (p = 0.001)".

> "the median OSATS score at the time of proficiency was 30 (IQR: 29–31.8), which declined to 28 (IQR: 26.2–29) on their return attempt (p = 0.001)"
