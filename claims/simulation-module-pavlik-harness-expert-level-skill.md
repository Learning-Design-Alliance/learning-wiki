## Evidence

### Moktar Joel MD 2016

Moktar Joel MD, Bradley Catharine S. BScPT MSc, Maxwell Alexandra BSc, Wedge John H. MD FRCS(C), Kelley Simon P. MBChB FRCS(Tr&Orth), Murnaghan M. Lucas MD MEd FRCSC. (2016). Skill Acquisition and Retention Following Simulation-Based Training in Pavlik Harness Application. The Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery. American volume. https://doi.org/10.2106/JBJS.15.00905

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled comparison of four groups (experts, ten residents, twelve allied health professionals, eleven controls) using one-way ANOVA on OSATS and GRS scores. Post-intervention, novices matched experts; the abstract reports improvement at p < 0.05 to "the level of the expert clinicians".

> "The scores of the resident and allied health groups improved post-intervention, and there were no significant differences found at time point 2 among the expert, resident, and allied health groups for any of the three measures"
