## Evidence

### Medenitzki R 2026

Medenitzki R, Dubovi I. (2026). Bodily Engagement in Virtual Reality Nursing Simulation: Implications for Students' Declarative and Procedural Learning. Nurse Educator. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000002271

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANCOVA showed "a significant main effect of time" on cybersickness (ηp2 = .148), but the time × group interaction was not significant.

> "Repeated-measures ANCOVA controlling for age revealed a significant main effect of time (F1,68 = 11.82, P = .001, ηp2 = .148), indicating that cybersickness increased after the VR experience."
