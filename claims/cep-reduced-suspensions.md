Curnutte Mary, Chapman Leah, Kesack Ashely, Afsharian Parisa, Francis Lucine, Genova Gina, Hager Erin, Cohen Juliana. (2026). School Nutrition Policies and Associations With Academic Outcomes: A Comprehensive Summary of Current Evidence. Journal of School Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/josh.70244

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review of two difference-in-differences studies (Kho and Hunter; Domina et al.) on CEP and discipline; a 2024 study found high school students' suspension probability fell by nearly 2.5%pt and middle school students by nearly 2%pt. No standardized effect size printed.

> "CEP was associated with a reduction in the likelihood of students being suspended or expelled at least once by 1%pt and strengthened with sustained implementation, with a reduction of 1.6%pt by the third year post-implementation [36]."
