Evidence¶

Terhi Vessonen 2026¶

Terhi Vessonen, Heidi Hellstrand, Pirjo Aunio, Anu Laine. (2026). Pictures in Mathematical Word Problems: A Systematic Review on the Role of Task and Student Characteristics. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10112-5

q2 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review synthesis of 14 studies of representational pictures in elementary word problems. The review reports that positive effects ranged from g = 0.09 to 2.70 and that "the largest effect sizes for pictures were in a study that utilized word problems that only included the first position of the unknown".

"the largest effect sizes for pictures were in a study that utilized word problems that only included the first position of the unknown and no other task characteristics that would increase tasks' demandingness (Van Lieshout & Xenidou-Dervou, 2018)."
