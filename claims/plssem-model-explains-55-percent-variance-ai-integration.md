# The four-predictor model explains approximately 55.4% of variance in teachers' AI integration ability

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The combination of AI Knowledge, Attitude Toward AI in ESD, ESD Teaching Material, and Use of AI explained R² = 0.554 of the variance in AI Integration Ability. [→ Riandi Riandi 2026](#riandi-riandi-2026)

## Evidence

### Riandi Riandi 2026

Riandi Riandi, Ismail Ismail, Ida Kaniawati, Wahyu Sopandi, Diana Ayu Rostikawati, Defrizal Hamka & Suhendar Suhendar. (2026). Teacher involvement in developing sustainable education materials for AI integration in green energy education. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34422-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Structural model evaluation in the PLS-SEM survey of 122 teachers reported R² = 0.554 (adjusted 0.539), which the authors characterise as moderate predictive power. R² is not a standardised effect size, so no impact magnitude is coded.

> "An R2 value of 0.554 indicates that approximately 55.4% of the variance in AI Integration Ability is explained by the combination of four exogenous constructs: (1) AI Knowledge; (2) Attitude Toward AI in ESD; (3) ESD Teaching Material; and (4) Use of AI in Science Green Energy."
