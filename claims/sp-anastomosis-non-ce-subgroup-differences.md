# Among participants who completed the task without critical errors, novices committed significantly more errors (12.3 vs 8.1) and took more than twice as long (25.3 vs 11.2 min) as experts

## Evidence

### Beatrici Edoardo 2026

Beatrici Edoardo, Santarelli Valerio, Guidotti Alessio, Pepillo Francesco, de Angelis Mario, Barretta Attilio, Cei Francesco, Frego Nicola, Paciotti Marco, Vanderstraeten Eva, Van Herzeele Isabelle, Puliatti Stefano, De Naeyer Geert, Farinha Rui, De Groote Ruben, Hemal Sij, Wiklund Peter, Crivellaro Simone, Challacombe Ben, Rashidian Niki, Mottrie Alexandre. (2026). Development and validation of objective performance metrics for robot-assisted basic surgical skills in a simulated anastomosis task using a single-port robotic system. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03996-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory subanalysis of the construct validity study restricted to participants who completed the task without critical errors (n = 6 novices, n = 9 experts). Mean errors were "12.3 [8.6–16.1]" for novices versus 8.1 for experts, p = 0.033.

> "When taking into account only those participants who completed the task without committing CEs (n = 6 for novices and n = 9 for experts), the difference in mean number of errors increased, reaching the level of significance (12.3 [8.6–16.1] vs. 8.1 [6.6–9.6] respectively for novices and experts, p = 0.033)."

### Beatrici Edoardo 2026 (2)

Beatrici Edoardo, Santarelli Valerio, Guidotti Alessio, Pepillo Francesco, de Angelis Mario, Barretta Attilio, Cei Francesco, Frego Nicola, Paciotti Marco, Vanderstraeten Eva, Van Herzeele Isabelle, Puliatti Stefano, De Naeyer Geert, Farinha Rui, De Groote Ruben, Hemal Sij, Wiklund Peter, Crivellaro Simone, Challacombe Ben, Rashidian Niki, Mottrie Alexandre. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03996-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same non-CE subgroup of the construct validity study: novices' mean completion time was "25.3 [18.0–32.7] min" versus 11.2 min for experts, p = 0.003.

> "Similarly, among those who did not incur any CEs, novices required more than twice the time of experts to complete the exercise (25.3 [18.0–32.7] min for novices and 11.2 [8.5–13.9] min for experts, p = 0.003)."
