Student survey ratings of classroom behavior in language arts/English, mathematics, social studies, and science classes. In disruptive classes students report "little mutual help, students tend to make fun of one another, and they often disrupt class."

> "Almost half the eighth graders and more than one-third of the tenth grad- ers rate their classes as disruptive or very disruptive."

### Sebring 1996 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Parallel teacher survey corroboration of student reports of disruption. The authors also note more than 40 percent of teachers judged student behavior had gotten worse during the past three years.

> "41 percent of elementary school teachers and 23 percent of high school teachers indicated that, on a typical day, student misbehavior dis- rupts their classes five times or more"
