## Evidence

### Roell M. 2022

Roell M., Bellon E., Polspoel B., Declercq M. & De Smedt B. (2022). Investigating the association between variability in sulcal pattern and academic achievement. Scientific Reports 12, 12323. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-15335-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA controlling for sex, dataset and IQ in the symbolic number comparison reaction-time measure (accuracy was at ceiling, mean 97.16 ± 2.40%). Participants with a "sectioned Right IPS had significantly longer reaction times" than those with a not sectioned IPS; no effect size was printed.

> "Right IPS was significantly associated with symbolic number ability, F(1,74) = 4.70, p = .03, and the evidence for this association was substantial BF10 = 4.13. Participants with a sectioned Right IPS had significantly longer reaction times in the symbolic number comparison task than participants with a not sectioned IPS"
