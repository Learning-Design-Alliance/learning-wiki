## Evidence

### Donnelly 2011

Donnelly, D., McGarr, O. and O'Reilly, J. (2011). A Framework for Teachers' Integration of ICT Into Their Classroom Practice. Computers and Education;57(2), 1469-1483. https://hdl.handle.net/10344/2340

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Classroom observations from the follow-on study. The article contrasts Teacher D and Teacher G, who let students play with the VCL beforehand, with Teacher I and Teacher J, who "spent a lesson showing the students the features of the VCL in a didactic and teacher-centred manner" and reduced the problem to a cookbook approach.

> "Teacher I and Teacher J did not provide their students with the opportunity to find out the features of the VCL for themselves but instead spent a lesson showing the students the features of the VCL in a didactic and teacher-centred manner."
