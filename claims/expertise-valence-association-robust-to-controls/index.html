Evidence¶

Keller 2022¶

Keller, L., Cortina, K. S., Müller, K., & Miller, K. F. (2022). Noticing and weighing alternatives in the reflection of regular classroom teaching: Evidence of expertise using mobile eye-tracking. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09570-5

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Loglinear model comparison (Table 3) on the 37-teacher sample showed Model II fit well and removing the Expertise interaction sharply worsened fit, while removing other interactions did not. "even after controlling for these variables, Expertise was associated with a difference in the distribution across the three levels" of Noticing and Alternatives.

"none of the two-way interaction effects between Noticing or Alternatives and Subject, Grade Level, Minority Status, and Gender were essential except for Expertise. This means that even after controlling for these variables, Expertise was associated with a difference in the distribution across the three levels"
