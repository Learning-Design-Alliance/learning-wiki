`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Numerical DFT results (vdW-DF1 in GPAW with ASE) reported in Table I and the results section: the student data (60×60×96 grid points, 1×1×1 k-points) give Ea = 0.48 eV versus 0.50 and 0.49 eV for medium- and high-quality settings, a deviation the authors call "less than 4%".

> "the Ea of the student calculations de- viates from the more accurate, but also more costly cal- culations of the same conﬁguration, by 0.01–0.02 eV (1–2 kJ/mol), or less than 4%."
