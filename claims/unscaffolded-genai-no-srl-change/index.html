Evidence¶

Zhang Jun 2026¶

Zhang Jun, Peng Yuting, Deng Xinyue, Zeng Qin, Wang Kai. (2026). The Impact of Unscaffolded GenAI Use on Pre-Service Teachers’ AI Readiness, Self-Regulated Learning, Critical Thinking, and Instructional Design Performance: A Quasi-Experimental Study. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071114

q3 · i0 · causal · r1

One-way ANCOVA on SRL (seven-item scale) with pretest as covariate in the quasi-experiment of 52 pre-service chemistry teachers; adjusted means were 4.15 in both groups. The article reports "F = 0.001, p = 0.977, η2 = 0.000, with a negligible effect size".

"The ANCOVA analysis showed no significant difference between the experimental and control groups, F = 0.001, p = 0.977, η2 = 0.000, with a negligible effect size."
