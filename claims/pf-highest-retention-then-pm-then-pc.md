### Jacobson Barineka Nbina & Obomanu 2011

Jacobson Barineka Nbina & Obomanu, B. Joseph. (2011). Assessment of the Effects of Problem Solving Instructional Strategies On Students' Achievement and Retention in Chemistry With Respect to Location in Rivers State. World Journal of Education, 1(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v1n2p74

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of Chemistry Retention Test scores (Table 3) for the 428-student quasi-experiment; retention was tested two weeks after the experiment. Table 3 shows urban means of 65.35 (PF), 54.72 (PM) and 48.19 (PC), with similar rural means. The strategy main effect in Table 4 is significant (F = 66.246).

> "The data reveal that the mean retention score was highest for the PF strategy, followed by the PM and least with the PC for both urban and rural locating."
