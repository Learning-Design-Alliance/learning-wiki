## Evidence

### Umoren RA 2025

Umoren RA, Ezeaka C, Berkelhamer SK, et al. (2025). Essential Newborn Care Virtual Simulations for Skills Retention in Newborn Care. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.60565

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Six-month follow-up of the 62 of 70 participants who completed the cohort study; BVM ventilation checklist scores improved from the immediate postcourse assessment instead of the expected decline. No effect size printed.

> "There were further significant gains in performance at the 6-month follow-up assessment on the BVM ventilation (median [IQR], 100% [86%-100%]; P = .04)"

### Umoren RA 2025 (2)

Umoren RA, Ezeaka C, Berkelhamer SK, et al. (2025). Essential Newborn Care Virtual Simulations for Skills Retention in Newborn Care. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.60565

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Six-month case scenario A checklist results for the same cohort, reported in Results with item-level improvements such as hand washing and skin-to-skin care reaching 100%. No effect size printed.

> "the median (IQR) case scenario A scores further improved from the immediate postcourse assessment to the 6-month assessment for ENC 1 (72% [61%-78%] vs 78% [72%-83%]; P = .001) and ENC 2 (80% [73%-87%] vs 87% [80%-93%]; P = .008)"

### Umoren RA 2025 (3)

Umoren RA, Ezeaka C, Berkelhamer SK, et al. (2025). Essential Newborn Care Virtual Simulations for Skills Retention in Newborn Care. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.60565

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Six-month case scenario B results (resuscitation and preterm referral scenarios administered only postcourse for novel exposure); the article reports several items improved significantly at 6 months. No effect size printed.

> "Median (IQR) scores improved overall on case scenario B assessments from immediately after the course to 6 months after the course for ENC 1 (76% [68%-88%] vs 93% [80%-100%]; P = .007)"
