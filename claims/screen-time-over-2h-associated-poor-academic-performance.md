## Evidence

### Adam Sagal Mohamed 2024

Adam Sagal Mohamed, Teshoma Melese Sinaga, Ahmed Awale Sh. Dahir, Tamiru Dessalegn. (2024). Household food insecurity and its association with academic performance among primary school adolescents in Hargeisa City, Somaliland. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0303034

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the cross-sectional study (n = 630) identified screen time as significantly associated with academic performance; screen time was dichotomized into ≤2 h/day and >2 h/day, excluding homework time. The article reports the association as "significantly associated with academic performance among school adolescents".

> "spending >2h/day watching TV or screen media use (AOR = 9.08, 95%CI = 4.81–17.13)"
