# Perceived learning effect, perceived expedience of cognitive training, and concentration are positively correlated

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Perceived learning effect correlated with perceived expedience of cognitive training (R coefficient = 0.87, p < 0.001). [→ Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich 2023](#kowalewski-karl-friedrich-2023)
`q2 i1` Expedience correlated with ability to concentrate (R coefficient = 0.36, p = 0.008), and concentration correlated with learning effect (R coefficient = 0.28, p = 0.042). [→ Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich 2023 (2)](#kowalewski-karl-friedrich-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich 2023

Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Seifert Laura, Kohlhas Laura, Schmidt Mona Wanda, Ali Seher, Fan Carolyn, Köppinger Karl Felix, Müller-Stich Beat Peter, Nickel Felix. (2023). Video-based training of situation awareness enhances minimally invasive surgical performance: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-023-10006-z

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of end-of-course evaluation ratings on a digital graphic scale from 1 to 100, plotted in Fig. 4a; the printed effect size is R coefficient = 0.87 (p value < 0.001). Correlations are exploratory and observational.

> "Significant Pearson correlations appeared between the perceived learning effect and the perception of the perceived expedience of cognitive training (R coefficient = 0.87; p value < 0.001, see Fig. 4a)"

### Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich 2023 (2)

Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Seifert Laura, Kohlhas Laura, Schmidt Mona Wanda, Ali Seher, Fan Carolyn, Köppinger Karl Felix, Müller-Stich Beat Peter, Nickel Felix. (2023). Video-based training of situation awareness enhances minimally invasive surgical performance: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-023-10006-z

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Further Pearson correlations from the same evaluation questionnaire, plotted in Fig. 4b and 4c; printed effect sizes are R coefficient = 0.36 (p value = 0.008) and R coefficient = 0.28 (p value = 0.042). The authors conclude only those able to concentrate stated a learning effect.

> "The stated expedience of the cognitive training and the stated ability to concentrate correlated significantly (R coefficient = 0.36; p value = 0.008, see Fig. 4b). The ability to concentrate and the stated learning effect also correlated significantly (R coefficient = 0.28; p value = 0.042, see Fig. 4c)"
