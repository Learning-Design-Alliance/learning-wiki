### Öğülmüş 2016

Öğülmüş, K., & Vuran, S. (2016). Schoolwide positive behavioral interventions and support practices: Review of studies in the Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 16, 1693–1710. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2016.5.0264

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Bradshaw, Waasdorp, and Leaf (2012), direct effects of SWPBIS on ODRs, focusing challenges, aggressive or disruptive behavior, and enhancements in prosocial behaviors and feeling management. It also cites Waasdorp et al. (2012) reporting less bullying and peer rejection in SWPBIS schools per teacher reports.

> "In another randomized controlled trials study conducted by Bradshaw, Waasdorp, and Leaf (2012) it was suggested that there are direct effects of SWPBIS on a variety of behavior problems, such as ODRs (Office Discipline Referrals), focusing challenges, aggressive or disruptive behavior, and enhancements in prosocial behaviors and feeling management."
