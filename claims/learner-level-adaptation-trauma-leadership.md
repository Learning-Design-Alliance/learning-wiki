## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants distinguished ideal methods for novice versus senior learners, with novices preferring supervision and guidance and senior learners preferring autonomy. [→ Quon S 2022](#quon-s-2022)

## Evidence

### Quon S 2022

Quon S, Riddell J, Bench KF, Roepke C, Burner E. (2022). Training Leaders in Trauma Resuscitation: Teacher and Learner Perspectives on Ideal Methods. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2021.5.51428

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Across the 25 interviews, participants perceived tape review, simulation, role modeling, and didactics as best for novices, and hands-on practice and feedback as best for senior learners; "The ideal timing of feedback changed based on the training level of the learner."

> "Learners who were earlier in their training preferred more supervision and guidance, while learners who were more advanced in their training preferred a greater degree of autonomy."
