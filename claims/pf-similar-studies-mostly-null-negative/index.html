Evidence¶

Valentina Nachtigall · Katja Serova · Nikol Rummel 2020¶

Valentina Nachtigall · Katja Serova · Nikol Rummel. (2020). When failure fails to be productive: probing the effectiveness of productive failure for learning beyond STEM domains. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09525-2

q2 · i? · causal · r?

This is the article's narrative review Tab. 2 summary of PF-similar studies in psychology, educational psychology, dental hygiene/surgery, and the Control of Variables Strategy. The review reports that "only the two by Schwartz and Bransford (1998) found a positive effect", while others showed equality with or inferiority to control conditions.

"Of all eight studies, only the two by Schwartz and Bransford (1998) found a positive effect of problem solving prior to instruction on student learning (see Table 2)."
