## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Suppressor effects were present in the standard regression analyses; the corrected predictive value of the student perspective on teacher agency for teacher positive emotions was much larger than the standard estimate. [→ Donker 2021](#donker-2021)

## Evidence

### Donker 2021

Donker, M. H., van Vemde, L., Hessen, D. J., van Gog, T., & Mainhard, T. (2021). Observational, student, and teacher perspectives on interpersonal teacher behavior: Shared and unique associations with teacher and student emotions. Learning and Instruction, vol. 73, 101414. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101414

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Contrasting several multiple regression analyses (SST vs SSC columns in Table 2) revealed suppressor effects: the student perspective on agency rose from 0.05 in standard regression to 2.70 with the correction method, "probably suppressed due to the large overlap with the observer and teacher perspective".

> "the predictive value of the students’ perspective on teacher agency had almost no predictive value for teacher positive emotions in the standard regression method (i.e., 0.05), but when using our correction method, a much larger predictive value (i.e., 2.70) was found for the student perspective, which was probably suppressed due to the large overlap with the observer and teacher perspective."
