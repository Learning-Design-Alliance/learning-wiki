## Evidence

### Yanghee Kim · Michael Tscholl 2021

Yanghee Kim · Michael Tscholl. (2021). Young children's embodied interactions with a social robot. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09978-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative theme from Study Two: in response to the robot's question "what are animals?", one child stepped and pointed left for herbivores and right for carnivores, then moved both arms to encompass omnivores; another placed a hand on the floor toward the robot to separate robots from animals. Drawn spaces were typically larger than the children.

> "Specifically, children used bodily actions and positions when they defined categories and the boundaries between the categories."
