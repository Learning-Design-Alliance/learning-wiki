Pardej S. K., Russo O., Green T. (2026). Effects of Neighborhood Level Health and Environment Quality on Academic and Executive Abilities in Youth with Noonan Syndrome Spectrum Disorder. Journal of the International Neuropsychological Society. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1355617726101969

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of Wechsler WMI scores in the NSSD and unaffected samples found "a significant diagnosis x H/E Z-score interaction effect (p=.042, partial η2=.04)", with the interaction depicted in Figure 3.

> "For the WMI, there was a significant diagnosis x H/E Z-score interaction effect (p=.042, partial η2=.04): higher H/E Z-scores were associated with stronger working memory in the NSSD group, while for the unaffected group, the slope was nearly flat."
