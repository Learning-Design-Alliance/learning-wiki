`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling of survey data from 357 faculty at eight public universities in eastern China. The revised SEM model showed "Autonomous motivations were found to be a significant positive predictor of self-efficacy (β = 0.42, p < 0.001)"; no standardized effect size beyond β is printed.

> "Autonomous motivations were found to be a significant positive predictor of self-efficacy (β = 0.42, p < 0.001), while controlled motivations did not show a statistically significant relationship with self-efficacy (β = 0.08, p = 0.173)."
