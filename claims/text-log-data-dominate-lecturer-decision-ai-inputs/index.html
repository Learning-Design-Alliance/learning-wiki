Köroğlu 2026¶

Köroğlu, M. N., Mayrhofer, J., Steinmaurer, A., Wintersberger, P., Dennerlein, S. M., & Weinhandl, R. (2026). AI-Supported lecturer decision-making in higher education: a socio-technical perspective. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00623-8

q2 · i? · review · r2

Frequency analysis of seven inductively identified input data types across the 27 included studies. The review reports "text-based data were the most frequently reported input type" and notes that biological data and image data were each reported in a single study, with most data collected in online and blended environments.

"text-based data were the most frequently reported input type (16 studies, 59.3%), followed by log data (11, 40.7%) and multimodal data (7, 25.9%)."
