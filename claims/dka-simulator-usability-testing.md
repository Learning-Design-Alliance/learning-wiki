`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Heuristic evaluation of the simulator prototype by a human factors engineer using Nielsen's validated usability heuristics. The article reports that "No critical issues were identified", while issues rated major or moderate were addressed through iterative refinement (Table 2).

> "No critical issues were identified. However, some usability issues were rated as ‘major’ or ‘moderate.’ For example, the purpose of “Notes to self” was not clear. Most of these were thought to be adequately addressed through training or by additional explanatory text"
