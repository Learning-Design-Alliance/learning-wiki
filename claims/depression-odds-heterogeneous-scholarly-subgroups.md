## Evidence

### Someko Hidehiro 2026

Someko Hidehiro, Kataoka Yuki, Mizuno Atsushi, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Tokuda Yasuharu, Shiojiri Toshiaki. (2026). Impact of scholarly activities on early postgraduate medical trainees: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09272-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of PHQ-2 depression status by clinical research experience, adjusted for confounders including age. The article reports "1.64 (95% CI: 1.13 to 2.38)" for scholarly activity without clinical research. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "the odds ratios for depression symptoms were 1.64 (95% CI: 1.13 to 2.38) for residents who engaged in scholarly activities without clinical research and 1.85 (95% CI: 0.86 to 3.99) for those who conducted clinical research"

### Someko Hidehiro 2026 (2)

Someko Hidehiro, Kataoka Yuki, Mizuno Atsushi, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Tokuda Yasuharu, Shiojiri Toshiaki. (2026). Impact of scholarly activities on early postgraduate medical trainees: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09272-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression analysis of PHQ-2 depression status by program requirement, adjusted for confounders including age. The article reports "1.68 (95% CI: 1.05 to 2.70)" for scholarly activity without requirements. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "the odds ratios for depression symptoms were 1.68 (95% CI: 1.05 to 2.70) for residents who engaged in scholarly activities without requirements and 1.60 (95% CI: 0.91 to 2.80) for those with requirements"
