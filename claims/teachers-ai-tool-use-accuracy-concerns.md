Survey findings from the pilot's four teachers. P1 noted occasional hallucinations in AI-generated explanations and worried about student misuse; P4 highlighted the ongoing need for clearer AI usage guidelines and policies.

> "Survey data revealed teachers’ active use of AI tools, such as ChatGPT and Turnitin, for lesson planning, content creation, and student support. However, challenges remain in verifying AI-generated content accuracy, managing subscriptions, and addressing varied student comfort levels with technology."
