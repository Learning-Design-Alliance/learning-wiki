## Evidence

### Quan Zhou 2026

Quan Zhou, Liao Wangjingyi, Allegrini Andrea G., Rimfeld Kaili, Wertz Jasmin, Morris Tim T., Raffington Laurel, Plomin Robert, Malanchini Margherita. (2026). Non-cognitive skills mediate education-related polygenic score associations with academic achievement across development. Nature Communications. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72838-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sensitivity analyses in TEDS repeated mediation models including general cognitive ability as a second mediator and separately adjusting for family socioeconomic status. Non-cognitive skills "remained significant, although slightly attenuated" mediators, with similar results across polygenic scores and developmental stages.

> "Results showed that non-cognitive skills remained significant, although slightly attenuated, mediators of the polygenic scores-achievement associations (Supplementary Fig. 3 and Supplementary Data 22)."
