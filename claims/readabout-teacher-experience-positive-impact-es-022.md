# ReadAbout showed a positive, statistically significant impact (effect size 0.22) on social studies reading comprehension for second-cohort students taught by teachers with one prior year of experience with the curriculum.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` There was a positive, statistically significant impact of ReadAbout on the social studies reading comprehension assessment for second-cohort students taught by teachers who were in the study both years (effect size: 0.22), and this impact was statistically significantly larger than the impact for first-cohort students taught by the same teachers. [→ James-Burdumy 2010](#james-burdumy-2010)

### James-Burdumy 2010

James-Burdumy, S., Deke, J., Lugo-Gil, J., Carey, N., Hershey, A., Gersten, R., Newman-Gonchar, R., Dimino, J., Haymond, K., and Faddis, B. (2010). Effectiveness of Selected Supplemental Reading Comprehension Interventions: Findings From Two Student Cohorts (NCEE 2010-4015). `q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison for second-cohort fifth graders taught by teachers in the study both years found a positive, statistically significant ReadAbout impact on the social studies reading comprehension assessment with "effect size: 0.22", statistically significantly larger than the first-cohort impact for the same teachers.
