## Evidence

### Xuanru Cheng 2025

Xuanru Cheng, Xian Wang, Chi-lok Tai and Lik-Hang Lee. (2025). MetaRoundWorm: A Virtual Reality Escape Room Game for Learning the Lifecycle and Immune Response to Parasitic Infections. [doi:10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752](https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

NASA-TLX questionnaire administered after the learning session in the between-subjects study; "Mental Demand was significantly lower in MetaRoundWorm than the Slide condition with a large effect size (t(46) =−3.04, p < .01, d = −.88".

> "The result showed that Mental Demand was significantly lower in MetaRoundWorm than the Slide condition with a large effect size (t(46) =−3.04, p < .01, d = −.88 [large effect size])."

### Xuanru Cheng 2025 (2)

Xuanru Cheng, Xian Wang, Chi-lok Tai and Lik-Hang Lee. (2025). MetaRoundWorm: A Virtual Reality Escape Room Game for Learning the Lifecycle and Immune Response to Parasitic Infections. [doi:10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752](https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

NASA-TLX subscales compared via Mann-Whitney U tests; "significantly reduced Temporal Demand" (r = .34) and "significantly lower" Frustration (r = .43) in the VR condition.

> "Similarly, MetaRoundWorm demonstrated significantly reduced Temporal Demand than the traditional teaching using slides ( U = 172.50, p < .05, r = .34 [medium effect]). The levels of Frustration were also significantly lower in MetaRoundWorm compared to the slide condition (U = 143.00, p < .01, r = .43 [medium effect])."
