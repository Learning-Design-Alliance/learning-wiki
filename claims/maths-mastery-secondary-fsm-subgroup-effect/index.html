Jerrim et al. 2015¶

John Jerrim, Helen Austerberry, Cosette Crisan, Anne Ingold, Candia Morgan, Dave Pratt, Cathy Smith, Meg Wiggins. (2015). Mathematics Mastery: Secondary Evaluation Report. Education Endowment Foundation. https://doi.org/10.1186/ISRCTN70922140

Subgroup analysis of the clustered RCT restricted to FSM-eligible pupils (1,610 pupils across 44 schools). The printed effect size is "+0.07" with confidence interval −0.04 to +0.17, again about one month's progress and not statistically significant.
