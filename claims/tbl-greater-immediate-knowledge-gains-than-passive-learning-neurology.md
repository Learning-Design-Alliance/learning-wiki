## Evidence

### Tan Nigel CK 2011

Tan Nigel CK, Kandiah Nagaendran, Chan Yiong Huak, Umapathi Thirugnanam, Lee Sze Haur, Tan Kevin. (2011). A controlled study of team-based learning for undergraduate clinical neurology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-91

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled modified crossover study of 49 third-year medical undergraduates during a nine-week internal medicine posting, using a mixed model ANCOVA adjusting for gender and second-year examination grades. The adjusted mean percentage change "was significantly greater than in the PL group" for TBL (10.3%) versus PL (5.8%).

> "for post-test 1, the mean percentage change in the TBL group test scores was significantly greater than in the PL group (10.3% vs 5.8%, mean difference 4.5%, 95%CI 0.7--8.3%, p = 0.021)"
