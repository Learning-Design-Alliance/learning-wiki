## Evidence

### Tekin and Parmaksız 2016

İnan Tekin, Ramazan Şükrü Parmaksız. (2016). Impact of Video Clips on the Development of the Listening Skills in English Classes: A Case Study of Turkish Students. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i9.1698

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA comparing listening scores across three film categories with 126 students; the article reports F(2,375)=2.47 with p>0.05, concluding "The success of students did not change according to film categories."

> "As it can be seen from one-way ANOVA results, there is no significant difference between the video categories students watched within the scope of implementation and their level of success F (2, 375)= 2.47; p > 0.05."
