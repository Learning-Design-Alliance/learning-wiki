Avivit Arvatz 2025¶

Avivit Arvatz, Boaz Hadas, Rotem Waitzman, Yehudit Judy Dori (2025). Putting self-regulated learning and teaching into practice: insights from two science teachers and their students. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09719-6

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis of all reflective prompts and student responses across both teachers' modules: 19 Adam prompts eliciting 430 responses and 25 Ben prompts eliciting 108 responses. "Using Spearman's correlation, we found a significant correlation between the prompts and the responses" with the printed effect size r_s = 0.31, a small-to-moderate association.

"We identified 19 prompts for Adam and 25 for Ben. Adam's 19 prompts yielded 430 responses from his 49 students. Ben's 25 prompts yielded 108 responses from his 13 students. Using Spearman's correlation, we found a significant correlation between the prompts and the responses in this sample (N = 538, rs = 0.31, p < 0.001)."
