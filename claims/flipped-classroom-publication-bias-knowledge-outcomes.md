`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Funnel plot analysis of the 127-study knowledge meta-analysis showed high asymmetry with significant small study effects (P<.001). Trim-and-fill imputation reduced the pooled effect from SMD 0.90 to SMD 0.30 (95% CI 0.002-0.59, P=.049), though it remained statistically significant.

> "the funnel plot showed high asymmetry (Figure S1 in Multimedia Appendix 1) with significant small study effects (P<.001). After applying the trim-and-fill method, the adjusted ES was attenuated (SMD 0.30, 95% CI 0.002-0.59; P=.049"
