Evidence¶

Feng 2024¶

Feng, M., Weiser, G., & Collins, K. (2024). Cost and cost effectiveness of ASSISTments online math support: Analysis from a randomized controlled study in middle school. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655721

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Cost analysis using the ingredients method, with costs applied to all students in intervention schools (n = 4,495 in 63 schools). The report states the estimate "corresponds to about $46.23 for each student" in 2021 dollars.

"In total, we estimate that it costs about $207,794.34 above business-as-usual expense to implement ASSISTments in all intervention schools for the efficacy study."
