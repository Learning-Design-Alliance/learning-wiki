## Evidence

### Rahav Koren Roni 2017

Rahav Koren Roni, Suriu Celia, Yakir Orly, Akria Luiza, Barhoum Masad, Braester Andrei. (2017). Physicians' lack of knowledge - a possible reason for red blood cell transfusion overuse? Israel Journal of Health Policy Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13584-017-0173-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive result from the professional section of the questionnaire, where questions 7 and 8 were weighted as particularly basic knowledge all physicians should answer correctly. Only 9% answered both correctly.

> "Knowledge regarding physiologic reasons for transfusion was examined in questions 7 and 8, professional section. Both questions were answered correctly by 9%."

### Rahav Koren Roni 2017 (2)

Rahav Koren Roni, Suriu Celia, Yakir Orly, Akria Luiza, Barhoum Masad, Braester Andrei. (2017). Physicians' lack of knowledge - a possible reason for red blood cell transfusion overuse? Israel Journal of Health Policy Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13584-017-0173-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive responses to question 7 in the same survey. The authors interpret the volume-related answers as suggesting "a lack of basic knowledge of the physiology of RBC transfusion".

> "Physicians who answered FALSE were also asked to mention other reasons, if any, for RBCs transfusion other than to improve oxygen delivery; 53% answered FALSE and 30% mentioned volume related reasons."
