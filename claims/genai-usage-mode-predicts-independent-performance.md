## Evidence

### Kuhn 2026

Kuhn, J., Gerjets, P., Trautwein, U., Greene, J. A., Malone, S., Vogt, P., & Fütterer, T. (2026). AI-Augmented Inquiry and Regulation in Hybrid Systems: A Control Allocation Architecture for Preserving Epistemic Agency in Hybrid Human-AI Cognition. https://osf.io/preprints

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article reports, citing Liu et al. (2026), a randomized controlled trial in which usage patterns diverged: "participants with AI access who used it primarily to obtain direct solutions (61%) showed declining independent performance relative to their own pretest." The groups did not differ at pretest. No effect size is printed in this article.

> "participants with AI access who used it primarily to obtain direct solutions (61%) showed declining independent performance relative to their own pretest, whereas those who used it for hints and clarification (27%) showed improved performance."
