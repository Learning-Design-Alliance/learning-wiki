## Evidence

### Zhao Xin 2019

Zhao Xin, Yu Xiaofeng, Zhang Xiaomei. (2019). The Role of Peer Support Education Model in Management of Glucose and Lipid Levels in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in Chinese Adults. Journal of Diabetes Research. https://doi.org/10.1155/2019/5634030

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures analysis in the randomized study: the article reports TC, TG, and LDL-C "decreased significantly after 3 months of intervention" with F = 5.727, 6.47, and 6.57, all p < 0.05. No effect size is printed.

> "Compared with the baseline, the levels of TC, TG, and LDL-C in the two groups decreased significantly after 3 months of intervention (F = 5.727, p < 0.05; F = 6.47, p < 0.05; F = 6.57, p < 0.05);"

### Zhao Xin 2019 (2)

Zhao Xin, Yu Xiaofeng, Zhang Xiaomei. (2019). The Role of Peer Support Education Model in Management of Glucose and Lipid Levels in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in Chinese Adults. Journal of Diabetes Research. https://doi.org/10.1155/2019/5634030

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same repeated-measures analysis at the 6-month interval: the article reports "no significant change in these levels for the two groups" (p > 0.05). No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "but with the prolongation of time, after 6 months, there was no significant change in these levels for the two groups (p > 0.05)."
