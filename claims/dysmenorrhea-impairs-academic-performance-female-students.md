## Evidence

### Kassaw Chalachew 2023

Kassaw Chalachew, Demareva Valeriia. (2023). Determinants of academic achievement among higher education student found in low resource setting, A systematic review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294585

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 356 female students at Haramaya University (mean age 22) reviewed in the synthesis: "moderate/severe dysmenorrhea pain intensity" (AOR = 8.53, 95% CI 4.45–16.39), premenstrual syndrome, early menarche, and low pocket money were significantly associated with dysmenorrhea's impact on performance.

> "Premenstrual syndrome [AOR = 4.86:95%CI (2.13, 11.06)], early menarche [AOR = 4.89:95%CI (2.03, 11.77)], moderate/severe dysmenorrhea pain intensity [AOR = 8.53:95%CI (4.45, 16.39)], and students monthly pocket money <150ETB [AOR = 3.91:95%CI (1.48, 10.29)] were significantly associated with the occurrence of the impact of dysmenorrhea on academic performance."
