## Evidence

### Vuorre 2021

Vuorre, M., & Metcalfe, J. (2021). Measures of relative metacognitive accuracy are confounded with task performance in tasks that permit guessing. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09257-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Monte Carlo simulation (20,000 items per hypothetical participant, 200 replications per parameter combination) varying knowledge K, guessing probability G, and resolution R. Results shown in Fig. 3 indicate that with guessing, resolution metrics are downward biased and the bias grows as knowledge decreases; other metrics (d', phi, Pearson's r, AUCs) were similarly impacted.

> "In the presence of guessing, even when the underlying resolution parameter is constant, the estimated values change dramatically depending on the level of knowledge (and hence task performance)."
