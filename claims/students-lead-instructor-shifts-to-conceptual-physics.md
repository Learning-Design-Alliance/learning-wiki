## Evidence

### Deepa N. Chari 2019

Deepa N. Chari, Hai D. Nguyen, Dean A. Zollman, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2019). Student and instructor framing in upper-division physics. [doi:10.1119/1.5120392](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5120392)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of the students-instructor shift matrix (Figure 9) from the 50 episodes, tabulating how student framing leads to instructor framing when she joins a group, typically by first listening to the ongoing discussion or answering posed questions.

> "In this matrix, follow-the-leader behavior is also common, though no longer dominant: the diagonal terms are large and cover 41% of the transitions in the matrix. The instructor's preference for conceptual physics (CP) is also apparent in the CP column: no matter what the students start with, if she shifts, it is most likely to be into the CP frame."
