Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness, 19(1), 1-26. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/combining-early-grade-assessments-to-study-literacy-skills

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Correlational analysis of K-2 assessment records from Chicago Public Schools (2013-14 to 2021-22) using descriptive statistics and correlations. The authors report that "All of the assessments are positively correlated with each other, surpassing the 0.50 threshold."

"All of the assessments are positively correlated with each other, surpassing the 0.50 threshold of a moderate relationship, and many are highly correlated, surpassing a 0.70 threshold of a strong relationship."

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same correlational analysis of Chicago K-2 records. MAP showed the strongest correlations, "ranging from 0.78 to 0.83" with ACCESS, DIBELS, and TRC-English, and 0.65 with TRC-Spanish.

"The MAP shows the strongest correlations with the other assessments, ranging from 0.78 to 0.83 with each of the other assessments (ACCESS, DIBELS, and TRC-English), except TRC-Spanish, where the correlation is 0.65."
