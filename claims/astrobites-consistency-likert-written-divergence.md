`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consistency analysis assigning numerical scores to qualitative responses for three questions (Papers, Capable, Belonging) and correlating them with averaged related Likert items for the same 52 students. The article argues the greater nuance available in written responses meant students expressed a mix of positive and negative feelings.

> "In the pre-survey, the coefficients are 0 .63, 0 .55, and 0 .49 for questions 39, 41, and 42, respectively; in the post-survey, these are 0 .28, 0 .39, and 0 .40."
