## Evidence

### Jared P. Canright and Suzanne White Brahmia 2024

Jared P. Canright and Suzanne White Brahmia (2024). Modeling novel physics in virtual reality labs: An affective analysis of student learning. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010146](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010146)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Weekly Flow Survey analysis using the eight-channel flow model, plotting perceived skill against challenge for 100-level students who completed all eight weekly surveys. In the model-generating VR labs "zero respondents reported Boredom, Worry, or Apathy," unlike any other 100-level lab.

> "In both 100-level model-generating VR labs (VR2 and VR3), zero respondents reported Boredom, Worry, or Apathy. This is not the case in any other 100-level labs."

### Jared P. Canright and Suzanne White Brahmia 2024 (2)

Jared P. Canright and Suzanne White Brahmia (2024). Modeling novel physics in virtual reality labs: An affective analysis of student learning. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010146](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010146)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of 2D interpolated-median engagement states between the NOMR VR labs and the ISLE-based hands-on circuit labs completed later in the same course. Lab VR3 and labs B1 and B2 "achieved similar states of productive engagement, landing near the diagonal in the Flow channel."

> "We note that lab VR3 and the first two weeks of circuit labs (B1 and B2) all achieved similar states of productive engagement, landing near the diagonal in the Flow channel."
