### Yangqiuting Li and Chandralekha Singh 2023

Yangqiuting Li and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). The impact of perceived recognition by physics instructors on women's self-efficacy and interest. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020125)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured empathetic interviews with 38 female undergraduate and first-year graduate students in physics courses at a large US research university; hybrid deductive and inductive coding in Nvivo. The results section reports "only 4 students (11%) reported positive perceived recognition" versus 26 (68%) reporting negative or absent recognition.

> "In our interviews, 26 (68%) of the interviewed female students reported negative perceived recognition or lack of positive recognition from their physics instructors or TAs, while only 4 students (11%) reported positive perceived recognition."
