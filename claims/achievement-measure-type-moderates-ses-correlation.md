## Evidence

### Sirin 2005

Sirin, S. R. (2005). Socioeconomic Status and Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analytic Review of Research. Review of Educational Research, 75(3), 417-453. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543075003417

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Separate moderator database of 167 independent correlations; Qb(4, 167) = 884.21, p < .001. The printed effect sizes were mean correlations: r = .35 for math, r = .32 for verbal, r = .27 for science, and r = .22 for general achievement; all Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparisons significant at p < .001.

> "the average ES was .27 for science achievement outcomes, .32 for verbal achievement outcomes, and .35 for math achievement outcomes."
