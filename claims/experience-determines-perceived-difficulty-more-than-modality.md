# Evaluator experience, not imaging modality, is the stronger determinant of perceived difficulty in endodontic diagnosis and treatment planning

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Professionals had significantly lower perceived difficulty than students regardless of modality (p = 0.000; OR = 0.44 for diagnosis, OR = 0.53 for treatment plan). [→ Fonseca 2025](#fonseca-2025)
`q2 i?` Imaging modality (PR vs CBCT) itself was not significantly associated with difficulty (p = 0.374 diagnosis, p = 0.764 treatment plan). [→ Fonseca 2025 (2)](#fonseca-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Fonseca 2025

Fonseca, Braz, Vieira, de Souza Ferreira, Damian. (2025). Do CBCT and Clinical Experience Impact Decision‐Making in Endodontic Diagnosis and Treatment Planning? A Before‐After Study. Australian Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.12966

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Ordinal logistic regression on self-reported difficulty across both evaluator groups (ten evaluators). The article reports difficulty "significantly lower among professionals (p = 0.000)" with a 56% and 47% reduction in high-difficulty likelihood; Table 4 gives OR = 0.44 and OR = 0.53.

> "The perception of difficulty, in both diagnostic decision‐making and treatment planning, was significantly lower among professionals (p = 0.000), who exhibited a 56% and 47% reduction in the likelihood of reporting high levels of difficulty for diagnostic and treatment decisions, respectively, compared to undergraduate students, regardless of the imaging modality employed."

### Fonseca 2025 (2)

Fonseca, Braz, Vieira, de Souza Ferreira, Damian. (2025). Do CBCT and Clinical Experience Impact Decision‐Making in Endodontic Diagnosis and Treatment Planning? A Before‐After Study. Australian Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.12966

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same ordinal regression, the exam (periapical vs CBCT) term was non-significant for both outcomes; Table 4 shows OR = 1.15 (p = 0.374) for diagnosis and OR = 0.95 (p = 0.764) for treatment planning. No standardized effect size is printed for these null terms.

> "These findings indicate that the evaluators' clinical experience was a more substantial determinant of the perceived complexity of endodontics than the imaging technology utilised in the evaluation"
