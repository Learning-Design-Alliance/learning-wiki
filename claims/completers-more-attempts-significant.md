## Evidence

### Herbert C 2017

Herbert C, Velan GM, Pryor WM, Kumar RK. (2017). A model for the use of blended learning in large group teaching sessions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1057-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational comparison of SCORM attempt data among 264 students. High completers made "101.3 ± 2.8 total attempts compared to 55.9 ± 5.5" (P < 0.001). Overall, students attempted each module an average of 3.3 ± 0.1 times. No effect size is printed.

> "Students who completed at least 90% of the modules also made significantly more attempts than students who completed fewer than 90% of the modules (101.3 ± 2.8 total attempts compared to 55.9 ± 5.5 total attempts; P < 0.001)."
