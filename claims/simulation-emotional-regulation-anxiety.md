DHC class 1 on emotional regulation drew on students' self-administered questionnaire responses. Participants described feeling "nervous, anxious, fearful, and tense when interacting with patients" and linked staying calm to reducing errors and patient confidence.

> "many students reported feeling nervous, anxious, fearful, and tense when interacting with patients, which often affected their performance during the simulation scenario"
