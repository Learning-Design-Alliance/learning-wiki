### M. Tuveri 2025

M. Tuveri, V. Fanti (2025). The role of natural language in understanding the universe: a teaching-learning sequence for high school students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.19812](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.19812)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of students' definitions from the crossroads formative activity, reorganized into four conceptual categories (n=41 definitions normalized in Fig. 3). The authors report that "in 29.3% of cases, students who proposed a scientific definition grounded their responses in knowledge acquired either during the activity" or prior schooling. Percentages are descriptive; no inferential test is reported.

> "Specifically, in 17.1% of cases, students who provided a definition in the form of a static statement based their response on a free interpretation of the term. In 29.3% of cases, students who proposed a scientific definition grounded their responses in knowledge acquired either during the activity"
