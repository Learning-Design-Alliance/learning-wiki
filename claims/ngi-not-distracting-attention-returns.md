## Evidence

### Jiqing Sun 2026

Jiqing Sun, Andrew Skourdoumbis. (2026). Is it distracting? A further exploration of non-gameplay social interaction during collaborative digital game-based learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-026-09476-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' summary finding from the qualitative case study: although attention momentarily shifted away from the task during NGI, it quickly returned to gameplay, and ongoing social and group flow indicated continued cognitive investment in learning goals.

> "In summary, in the context of this study, NGI did not appear to divert students from learning objectives. This finding concurred with Cheng et al. (2019), who statistically showed off-topic chat was not distracting learning."
