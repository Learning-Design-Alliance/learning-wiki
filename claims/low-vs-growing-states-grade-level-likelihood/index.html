q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of i-Ready math outcomes comparing Low and Growing state categories. The report notes averages were "nearly identical for Low and Growing states" while the grade-level improvement likelihood differed (64% vs. 60%). No significance test is reported.

"Although average scale scores were nearly identical for Low and Growing states, within two points in the Fall and one point in the Spring ( Figure 2 ), this difference is more pronounced when considering a student’s likelihood of moving up or sustaining on grade level performance – 64% in Low states versus 60% in Growing states."
