## Evidence

### Erica L. Snow 2016

Erica L. Snow, Aaron D. Likens, Laura K. Allen, & Danielle S. McNamara. (2016). Taking Control: Stealth Assessment of Deterministic Behaviors Within a Game-Based System. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0085-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Surrogate analysis in which each of the 40 students' time series was shuffled 40 times and DFA recomputed; a paired samples t-test compared surrogate to observed exponents. The article reports surrogates "produced smaller Hurst exponents than did the intact series" (t(39) = 156.90, p < 0.001).

> "shuffled surrogates produced smaller Hurst exponents than did the intact series, t(39) = 156.90, p < 0.001, supporting the conclusion that the observed Hurst exponents accurately represent the observed patterns of persistence"
