Reduced symptoms of anxiety among professional caregivers facing cardiac arrest after simulation based training. Scientific Reports 15, 9406. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-93932-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pilot case-control study at Nancy-Brabois University Hospital; within-group Wilcoxon comparison in the 26 trained participants. The article reports "trait anxiety was significantly decreased" at 3 months, though the printed post value (4.1) appears inconsistent with Panel A (34.1).

> "However, 3 months after the session, trait anxiety was significantly decreased (40.9 ± 8.9 points vs. 4.1 ± 8.6 points; p = 0.0002)."
