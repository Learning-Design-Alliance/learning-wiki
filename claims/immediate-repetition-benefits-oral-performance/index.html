Evidence¶

Rafael Zaccaron 2019¶

Rafael Zaccaron. (2019). Ideas for integrating meaningful immediate repetition tasks in the English as an additional language classroom: promoting fluency. BELT, 10(2). http://dx.doi.org/10.15448/2178-3640.2019.2.32988

q2 · i? · design · r1

The article reviews prior task-repetition research (Bygate 2001; Ahmadian and Tavakoli 2011; Lynch and Maclean 2000; Côelho 2017), reporting that "show better oral performance during a repeated task" and that repetition tends to reduce anxiety; no effect sizes are printed.

"Students, in general, show better oral performance during a repeated task, especially the less confident ones, as repetition tends to reduce anxiety (Côelho, 2017)."
