Thematic analysis of interviews with 14 consultants and two managers (general needs assessment, NVivo 12). The analysis "highlighted" the support gap "between LEDs and HEE trainees", a qualitative finding with no effect size.

> "Despite these measures, a gap in educational support for career progression between LEDs and HEE trainees was highlighted, underscoring the need for further investment in resources tailored to LEDs' needs."
