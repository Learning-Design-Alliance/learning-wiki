## Evidence

### Lotzin Annett 2019

Lotzin Annett, Buth Sven, Sehner Susanne, Hiller Philipp, Pawils Silke, Metzner Franka, Read John, Härter Martin, Schäfer Ingo. (2019). Reducing barriers to trauma inquiry in substance use disorder treatment – a cluster-randomized controlled trial. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13011-019-0211-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Covariate-adjusted linear mixed model from the cluster-randomized trial of SUD professionals. The article prints the coefficient "b = − 0.33" with CI and p-value; no standardized effect size is reported. Prior trauma training also predicted greater reduction (b = 0.26, p = .037).

> "The intervention group showed a significantly greater decrease in ‘Fear of offending the client’ than the control group (b = − 0.33, 95% CI [− 0.56, − 0.09], p = .006)."

### Lotzin Annett 2019 (2)

Lotzin Annett, Buth Sven, Sehner Susanne, Hiller Philipp, Pawils Silke, Metzner Franka, Read John, Härter Martin, Schäfer Ingo. (2019). Reducing barriers to trauma inquiry in substance use disorder treatment – a cluster-randomized controlled trial. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13011-019-0211-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Separate linear mixed model on the same trial's barrier ratings. The article prints "b = − 0.45" with CI and p < .001; no standardized effect size is reported. The authors attribute this reduction to the training directly discussing this fear and presenting research that asking does not harm clients.

> "Compared with the control group, the intervention group showed a significantly greater decrease in the ‘fear of retraumatizing the client’ (b = − 0.45, 95% CI [− 0.69, − 0.22], p < .001)."
