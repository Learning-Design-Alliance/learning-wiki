# Lecture plus 3D courseware produced significantly better learning results than lecture alone, lecture plus video, and lecture plus image instruction

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Method B (lecture + 3D courseware) learning results were superior to Method A (lecture), Method C (lecture + video), and Method D (lecture + image). [→ Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai 2013](#liang-yuan-hsiung-mu-hui-lai-2013)

## Evidence

### Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai 2013

Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai. (2013). Improving Learning Results and Reducing Cognitive Load through 3D Courseware on Color Management and Inspection Instruction. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1016926

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posteriori comparison from the MANCOVA of the 10-week quasi-experiment: Method B (M=93.642) significantly outscored Method A (M=67.005) on the hands-on post-test; null hypothesis (1) was rejected.

> "First, the learning results difference of Method B (lecture + 3D courseware-assisted instruction) (M=93.642) and Method A (lecture) (M=67.005) is reached significant."

### Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai 2013 (2)

Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai. (2013). Improving Learning Results and Reducing Cognitive Load through 3D Courseware on Color Management and Inspection Instruction. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1016926

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Posteriori comparison in the same MANCOVA: Method B significantly outscored Method C (M=78.338) in learning results; null hypothesis (3) was rejected.

> "Third, since the difference between Method B (lecture + 3D courseware-assisted instruction) and Method C (lecture + video-aided instruction) (M=78.338) is reached significant."

### Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai 2013 (3)

Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai. (2013). Improving Learning Results and Reducing Cognitive Load through 3D Courseware on Color Management and Inspection Instruction. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1016926

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`
