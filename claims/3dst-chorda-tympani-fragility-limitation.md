## Evidence

### Molinari Giulia 2025

Molinari Giulia, Emiliani Nicolas, Cercenelli Laura, Bortolani Barbara, D'Azzeo Rossana, Burato Arianna, Presutti Livio, Molteni Gabriele, Marcelli Emanuela (2025). A Novel 3D Printed Multi‐Material Simulator for Endoscopic Stapes Surgery: The “3D Stapes Trainer”. The Laryngoscope 135(9): 3356–3363. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.32168

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Intraoperative assessment of the eight recorded simulations: "In all training sessions, the ChT got interrupted", with specialists preserving integrity until atticotomy and residents damaging it earlier; two residents transected it unnoticed.

> "In all training sessions, the ChT got interrupted. The ENT specialists managed to keep its integrity until the atticotomy, whereas the residents damaged it since the preliminary dissection steps, with 2 of them transecting the nerve without notice."

### Molinari Giulia 2025 (2)

Molinari Giulia, Emiliani Nicolas, Cercenelli Laura, Bortolani Barbara, D'Azzeo Rossana, Burato Arianna, Presutti Livio, Molteni Gabriele, Marcelli Emanuela (2025). A Novel 3D Printed Multi‐Material Simulator for Endoscopic Stapes Surgery: The “3D Stapes Trainer”. The Laryngoscope 135(9): 3356–3363. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.32168

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative questionnaire administered after the training sessions: the burr-on-bone tactile feedback scored highest while "the less appreciated part was the ChT", with free comments calling the replicated nerve too thin or too fragile. The authors attribute this to the fragility of the synthetic material.

> "The item receiving the highest score was the tactile feedback of the burr on the bone, whereas the less appreciated part was the ChT, as confirmed by the free comments reporting the replicated nerve as too thin or too fragile."
