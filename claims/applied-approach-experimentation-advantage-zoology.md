Individual practical test: students had to design an experiment on food supply and mosquito-larvae development and write a detailed account for approval, with defects checked against them before a second report. This cultural and disciplinary standard gave the advantage on first reports to the economic section.

> "Of students in Section A, 35% reported correctly on the first trial, and 47% more were correct on the second,report. Of students in Section B, 60% reported correctly on the first trial, and 33% more were correct on the second trial."
