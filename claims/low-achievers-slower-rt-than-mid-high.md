`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA over low (N = 101), mid (N = 415) and high achievers (N = 102) found low achievers' VRT mean of 285.75 msec versus 241.10 msec for high achievers, with Tukey post hoc tests locating the differences between low and the other two groups.

> "There was a significant effect of VRT [F (2, 615) = 6.40, p = 0.001] and ART [F (2, 615) = 24.47, p = 0.001] on exam performance. Tukey's post hoc assessments showed that the RT of low achievers was significantly (p < 0.01) higher than the mid and high achievers. No such difference was found between the mid and high achievers (p > 0.05)"
