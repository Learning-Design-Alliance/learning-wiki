## Evidence

### Grissom TE 2020

Grissom TE, Samet RE. (2020). The Anesthesiologist's Role in Teaching Airway Management to Nonanesthesiologists: Who, Where, and How. Advances in Anesthesia 38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aan.2020.08.002

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports Clark and colleagues' single-institution trial of a 4-week anesthesiology airway rotation for EM residents. No statistically significant difference was detected; equivalence was not tested.

> "In a single-institution study, they found that intubation success rates were no different during the first 6-months of the PGY 2 after the addition of an anesthesiology rotation during the PGY 1"

### Grissom TE 2020 (2)

Grissom TE, Samet RE. (2020). The Anesthesiologist's Role in Teaching Airway Management to Nonanesthesiologists: Who, Where, and How. Advances in Anesthesia 38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aan.2020.08.002

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The trial compared rotation completers, who averaged 53 intubation attempts, with non-completers averaging 21 attempts. The printed success rates are descriptive; no effect size is reported.

> "The first-pass and overall success rates were not statistically different between the groups (78.4% and 95.7%, respectively, for the rotation group; 83.4% and 94.5%, respectively, for the no-rotation group)"
