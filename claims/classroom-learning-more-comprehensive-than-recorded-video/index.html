Rim Mekonnen Hadgu 2016¶

Rim Mekonnen Hadgu, Sophia Hoang-Vy Huynh, & Chaya Gopalan. (2016). The Use of Pre-recorded Lectures on Student Performance in Physiology. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v5n1p105

The authors' concluding interpretation of their own quasi-experimental comparison, offered as a general conclusion rather than a tested effect; the article prints no effect size for this statement. The conclusion adds that "automatizing learning has its limitations".
