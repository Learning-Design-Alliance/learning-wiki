## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Doyle Patricia 2015

Doyle Patricia, VanDenKerkhof Elizabeth G, Edge Dana S, Ginsburg Liane, Goldstein David H. (2015). Self-reported patient safety competence among Canadian medical students and postgraduate trainees: a cross-sectional survey. BMJ Quality & Safety. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjqs-2014-003142

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bonferroni-corrected pairwise ANOVA comparisons by programme year among medical students on H-PEPSS classroom dimension scores. The authors interpret the decline as possibly reflecting the culture of clinical settings or growing awareness of what students do not know.

> "first-year students reported more confidence about working in teams in the classroom setting compared with years 2–4, and fourth-year students reported significantly less confidence in what they were learning about the culture of safety in the classroom setting compared with first-year students."
