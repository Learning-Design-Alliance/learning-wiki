q3 · i? · associational · r2

Mediation analysis among male students tested eight models with pre- or post-instruction science, spatial, math, or clinical anxiety as mediators of the DMN–SN connectivity to course grade relation. Only post-instruction math anxiety produced a significant indirect effect (ab = −0.544, 95% bootstrap CI [−1.161, −0.128], 10,000 samples).

"We observed at post-instruction that including math anxiety as a variable reduced the total effect of DMN –SN and course grade, which was no longer signi ﬁcant (indirect effect = −0.544, SE = 0.267, P = 0.042; 95% bootstrap con ﬁdence intervals (CIs) = −1.161, −0.128)"
