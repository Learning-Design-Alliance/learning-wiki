# Skilled EFL writers are aware of their own writing strengths and weaknesses and exploit strengths while compensating for weaknesses such as limited vocabulary.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All nine skilled writers reported awareness of personal strengths and weaknesses and used compensatory strategies, with limited vocabulary the weakness all nine reported. [→ Yu-Ling You; Shih-Guey Joe 2001](#yu-ling-you-shih-guey-joe-2001)

## Evidence

### Yu-Ling You; Shih-Guey Joe 2001

Yu-Ling You; Shih-Guey Joe. (2001). Investigating the Metacognitive Awareness and Strategies of English-Majored University Student Writers. ERIC Document ED465281. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED465281

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview analysis of the nine skilled EFL writers on self-awareness of strength or weakness. One subject reported strength in discourse structure and limited vocabulary, and focused on well-organized structure in writing; the subjects shared the view that extensive reading could enlarge vocabulary size.

> "In fact, all of the nine subjects in this study reported that limited amount of vocabulary was one of their many weak points that needed improvement."
