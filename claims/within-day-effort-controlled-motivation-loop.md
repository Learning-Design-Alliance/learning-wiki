## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Malmberg and & Martin 2019

Lars-Erik Malmberg, Andrew J. Martin. (2019). Processes of Students' Effort Exertion, Competence Beliefs and Motivation: Cyclic and Dynamic Effects of Learning Experiences within School Days and School Subjects. Contemporary Educational Psychology (manuscript CEDPSYCH 2018-216-R2). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.03.013

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Within-day lagged MSEM (Model 2, Fig 2 left). Cross-lagged effect of prior controlled motivation on subsequent effort exertion was positive and significant.

> "Higher controlled motivation the previous learning situation predicted more enhanced effort the following situation (B = .09, β = .10; p = .003; ES = 0.18)."

### Malmberg and & Martin 2019 (2)

Lars-Erik Malmberg, Andrew J. Martin. (2019). Processes of Students' Effort Exertion, Competence Beliefs and Motivation: Cyclic and Dynamic Effects of Learning Experiences within School Days and School Subjects. Contemporary Educational Psychology (manuscript CEDPSYCH 2018-216-R2). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.03.013

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Same within-day model. Prior effort negatively predicted subsequent controlled motivation, forming a within-day loop with the positive reverse path.

> "Higher previous effort exertion predicted diminished controlled motivation from the teacher (B = -.07, β = -.10; p = .001; ES = -0.15)."
