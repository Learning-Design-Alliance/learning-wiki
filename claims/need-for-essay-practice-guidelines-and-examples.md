This is the authors' forward-looking assessment rather than a tested result: they argue "such guidelines do not yet exist for computational essays" and report having begun assembling example essays at the University of Oslo based on researcher and student work.

> "For example, to successfully write computational essays most students will need explicit guidance on explicit guidance on effective practices. However, although there are commonly-taught guidelines for ordinary essays such guidelines do not yet exist for computational essays."
