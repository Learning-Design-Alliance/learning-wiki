# Repeated iVR practice improved virtual performance metrics: Precision Score rose from 70% to 93%, case time fell from 8:44 to 3:46, and virtual fluoroscopic images fell from 42 to 5

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Across four iVR sessions on a severe SCFE model, the resident's Precision Score improved from 70% to 93%, performance time decreased from 8 minutes 44 seconds to 3 minutes 46 seconds, and virtual fluoroscopic images decreased from 42 to 5. [→ Lohre Ryan 2021](#lohre-ryan-2021)

## Evidence

### Lohre Ryan 2021

Lohre Ryan, Leveille Lise, Goel Danny P. (2021). Novel Application of Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation Training: A Case Report. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-21-00114

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive simulator metrics from the case's iVR sessions, not a controlled comparison. The score "accounts for a unique combination of guidewire attempts, screw placement, erroneous placement including starting position and screw depth, fluoroscopy use, and overall procedural time."

> "The PGY-5, when using the PrecisionOS software before surgery, sequentially improved their Precision Score (virtual performance metric) when practicing on a severe SCFE model from 70% to 93% (maximum score 100%)."
