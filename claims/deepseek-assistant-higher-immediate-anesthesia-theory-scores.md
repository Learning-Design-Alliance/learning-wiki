## Evidence

### Fan Hao 2025

Fan Hao, Du Wenqin, Yan Jia, Jiang Hong. (2025). Application of DeepSeek-based AI teaching assistant in teaching anesthesiology theories. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08494-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stratified randomized controlled trial with 24 participants per group (12 undergraduates and 12 residents each); the experimental group received traditional teaching plus AI support. Independent-samples t-test comparison found "The mean theoretical test score of the experimental group was significantly higher than that of the control group"; Table 2 reports means of 83.21 ± 12.88 vs 73.08 ± 11.77 points. No effect size is printed.
