# Expert interviews revealed two competencies absent from an initial course-syllabus-derived model: Running and Testing

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In interviews with five faculty and postdocs who use computation in research, coding of transcripts identified competencies not in the initial model — Running (scripts and file structures to execute programs) and Testing (comparison against known solutions and profiling) — and led to renaming Programming as Implementation. [→ Christopher Burke and Timothy J. Atherton 2016](#christopher-burke-and-timothy-j-atherton-2016)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study: five faculty and postdocs in Physics, Computer Science and Mathematics were interviewed by one author; two authors coded transcripts independently and retained only common items. The coding "identiﬁed additional competencies that we had not proposed in our initial model", including Running and Testing.

> "In performing this exercise, we identiﬁed additional competencies that we had not proposed in our initial model: Running, incorporating the ability to create scripts and ﬁle structures to execute the program and collect the data"
