## Evidence

### Stoltzfus JR 2016

Stoltzfus JR, Libarkin J. (2016). Does the Room Matter? Active Learning in Traditional and Enhanced Lecture Spaces. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-03-0126

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-semester technology and infrastructure survey (SCALE-UP n = 33; Traditional n = 42) with multiple-choice and open-ended items. "Students in the SCALE-UP section also responded favorably to questions regarding the classroom layout"; Table 6 shows 78% of SCALE-UP students said performance would suffer without the room type.

> "Students in the SCALE-UP section also responded favorably to questions regarding the classroom layout, while responses from students in the Traditional section to questions about the impact of the classroom were less favorable (Tables 5 and 6)."
