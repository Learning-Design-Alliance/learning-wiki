Nina Franiatte, Esther Boissin, Alexandra Delmas, Wim De Neys. (2024). Boosting Debiasing: Impact of Repeated Training on Reasoning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101845

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory order analysis with randomized task order: final responses increased by 51.1 points for the first task and 37.3 for the last; the article attributes the smaller late-battery gain to possible fatigue near the end of the one-hour session.

> "In the training group, from pre - to post-intervention, initial responses  rose by 50.2 points for the first task and 34.6 points for the last task."
