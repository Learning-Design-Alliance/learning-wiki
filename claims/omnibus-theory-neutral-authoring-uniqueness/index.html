Evidence¶

Mizoguchi 2015¶

Mizoguchi, R., & Bourdeau, J. (2015). Using Ontological Engineering to Overcome AI-ED Problems: Contribution, Impact and Perspectives. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0077-5

q2 · i? · design · r2

The authors' own characterization of OMNIBUS/SMARTIES in their retrospective, reporting that "OMNIBUS has 99 ways of goal decomposition extracted from 11 theories/models". The statement is the authors' assertion, not an external evaluation; no comparative test of other authoring systems is reported.

"In this sense, it is the first and, to our knowledge, the only authoring system that does not constrain or favor one theory over another."
