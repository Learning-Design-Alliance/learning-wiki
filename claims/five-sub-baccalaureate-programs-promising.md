# Five sub-baccalaureate programs of study met the promising-pathway threshold: construction trades (certificate) and agriculture, engineering, health, and mechanic/repair technologies (associate degrees)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Programs of study at the certificate and associate level led low-income students to good jobs at rates of 75-84%, exceeding the 69% threshold. [→ Bates 2025](#bates-2025)
