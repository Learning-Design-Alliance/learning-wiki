Evidence¶

Barzanji and Loitsch 2025¶

Barzanji and Loitsch. (2025). Exploring conversational agents for novice programmers: a scoping review. Discover Artificial Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s44163-025-00521-4

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's discussion of globality and inclusivity reports that "Of the 23 studies reviewed, only seven originated from English-speaking countries, yet 17 prototypes relied exclusively on English, even in non-English-speaking contexts". It argues this linguistic dominance creates barriers for non-native English speakers learning programming concepts and English-based syntax simultaneously.
