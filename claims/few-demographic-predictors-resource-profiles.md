## Evidence

### Weatherton M. and Aikens M 2026

Weatherton M. and Aikens M. (2026). Resource Use in Introductory Biology: Evaluating Latent Patterns, Demographic Covariates, and Effects on Final Course Grade. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-09-0212

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Manual three-step BCH multinomial logistic regression of latent class membership on gender identity, race, AP Biology enrollment, and institution. Demographic characteristics were relatively evenly distributed among the latent profiles; AP biology enrollment was not a significant predictor for any comparison.

> "Multinomial regression models showed that very few demographic covariates were significantly associated with latent profile membership (Supplemental Tables S7−S10). This is consistent with the finding that demographic characteristics were relatively evenly distributed among the latent profiles."
