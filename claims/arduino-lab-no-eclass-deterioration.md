## Evidence

### Eugenio Tufino and Giovanni Organtini 2023

Eugenio Tufino and Giovanni Organtini. (2023). Evaluation of the effectiveness of an introductory mechanics Lab with Arduino and smartphone. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.00606](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.00606)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post administration of the Italian-translated E-CLASS survey to 2021/22 course students (88 participants using nicknames), scored with a two-point expert-matching scheme. The article reports "only a slight increase in the overall score, not statistically significant" and a student viewpoint towards EXPERT increase from 0.82 to 0.85.

> "only a slight increase in the overall score, not statistically significant, was observed between the pre- and post-questionnaires. As you can see, the score for the student viewpoint towards EXPERT increased from 0.82 to 0.85."
