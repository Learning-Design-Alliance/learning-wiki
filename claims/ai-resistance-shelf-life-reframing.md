`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Open-Ended & Authentic Task Design room, second hour. One participant argued AI-resistant assignments are no longer possible; the facilitator amended that they are possible but temporary, lasting perhaps a semester. This is the authors' interpretation of the discussion, not a tested result.

> "If resistance has a shelf life, the design goal is not a task AI cannot do but a task whose difficulty survives AI doing part of it."
