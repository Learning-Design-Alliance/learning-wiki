The authors report, citing a study of unannounced obstetric in situ simulation, that "approximately one-third of all staff members thought that unannounced ISS was stressful and unpleasant" even though staff had been told in advance that unannounced simulations would take place. No effect size is printed.

> "approximately one-third of all staff members thought that unannounced ISS was stressful and unpleasant, despite the fact that all staff members beforehand had been told that a number of unannounced ISS would take place within a specific period"
