Evidence¶

Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai 2013¶

Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai. (2013). Improving Learning Results and Reducing Cognitive Load through 3D Courseware on Color Management and Inspection Instruction. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1016926

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment with 80 students in four groups analyzed by MANCOVA using the pre-test as covariate; the omnibus test was significant for learning results (F (3, 72) =29.641) and for cognitive load (F(3, 72)=3.016). No effect size is printed.

"The ANCOVA results indicated a significant difference among instructional methods with regard to the students’ learning results (F (3, 72) =29.641, p=.000<.001) and cognitive load as well (F(3, 72)=3.016, p=.016<.05)."
