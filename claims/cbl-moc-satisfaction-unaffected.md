## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The difference in 5-point satisfaction ratings between the CBL+MOC and traditional CBL groups was not statistically significant (p = 0.17), despite a higher proportion of very satisfied students in the CBL+MOC group. [→ Zhi Fei-Hang 2025](#zhi-fei-hang-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhi Fei-Hang 2025

Zhi Fei-Hang, Liu Wei, Zhang Xinyao, Zhang Tao, Su Yuhan, Huang Ruoxuan, Feng Yan-Fen, Lei Yi-Yan. (2025). Exploration of CBL combined with “Metronomic” online classroom in thoracic trauma and closed chest drainage teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08427-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subjective satisfaction rated on a 5-point scale by all students in both randomized groups. Eleven CBL+MOC students were very satisfied versus nine in CBL, but the between-group difference was not significant at p = 0.17; no effect size printed.

> "Although a higher proportion of students in the CBL + MOC group reported being “very satisfied,” the difference in satisfaction ratings between groups was not statistically significant (p = 0.17) (Fig. 6D)."
