### Favero 2025

Applications of Generative AI to support teaching and learning in Higher Education, co-located with AIED 2025, Palermo, Italy. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06878

The paper reports, citing Gerlich, a user study across age and education levels in which "a higher reliance on AI was correlated with lower performance on the HCTA critical thinking assessment". The correlation is reported as mediated by cognitive offloading; no effect size is printed.

> "A user study by Gerlich [9] across age and education levels found that a higher reliance on AI was correlated with lower performance on the HCTA critical thinking assessment by Halpern [18] mediated by cognitive offloading."
