## Evidence

### Montroy Janelle J. 2016

Montroy Janelle J., Bowles Ryan P., Skibbe Lori E., McClelland Megan M., Morrison Frederick J. (2016). The Development of Self-Regulation across Early Childhood. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0000159

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive trajectory results across the three samples showed later developers "responding at less than 35% accuracy" throughout preschool; the pattern persisted into kindergarten, with Oregon later developers' gains not picking up until 70–72 months. The article states these children were six months to a year behind intermediate peers and at least a year and a half behind early developers.

> "Yet, about 20% of children (later developers) consistently demonstrated relatively few early gains, responding at less than 35% accuracy on the behavioral self-regulation task throughout preschool."
