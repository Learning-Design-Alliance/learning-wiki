Case-study narrative of the Santa Clara–UCSF partnership reporting participants' own accounts: staff and researcher say they "have benefitted immensely from their work together," attributing success to a relationship built on trust, reciprocity, and mutual respect. No effect sizes are reported.

"In contrast to traditional notions of distant relationships between schools and “ivory tower” researchers, Uncapher and Santa Clara staff say they have benefitted immensely from their work together. The key: a collaborative, ongoing relationship built on trust, reciprocity, and mutual respect."
