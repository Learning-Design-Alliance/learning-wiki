## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among treatment-group students, more collaboration was associated with higher germane load, while the collaboration–extraneous load relationship was not significant. [→ Jamie Costley 2021](#jamie-costley-2021)

## Evidence

### Jamie Costley 2021

Jamie Costley, Mik Fanguy. (2021). Collaborative note-taking affects cognitive load: the interplay of completeness and interaction. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09979-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis within the treatment group (n = 74) using a collaboration index (turn-taking, edits, volume, sessions). The collaboration–germane correlation was "statistically significant"; the collaboration–extraneous correlation was not. No effect size was printed in the text.

> "However, only the correlation between amount of collaboration and germane load was statistically significant (hypothesis 3 is held), while the relationship between amount of collaboration and extraneous load was not (hypothesis 4 is rejected)."
