## Evidence

### Jean-François Parmentier and Brahim Lamine 2015

Jean-François Parmentier and Brahim Lamine. (2015). Interpreting gains and losses in conceptual test using Item Response Theory. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1509.03878](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1509.03878)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical IRT analysis of the proportion of answer change ICi, plotted for the FCI in Fig. 4; the contribution of the variance term δPi² is described as "not negligible, is rather small", so the change stems mainly from the test-independent parabolic term.

> "Consequently, for a group of students with a true score of 50 %, nearly 18 % of answers change from correct (resp. incorrect) to incorrect (resp. correct) in a test-retest situation."
