Ulusoy, K. (2010). Open Education Students' Perspectives on Using Virtual Museums Application in Teaching History Subjects. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 11(4). https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojde/issue/16922/176560

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Open-ended question responses from 20 students showed the most frequent opinion was that the application was "it was very enjoyable", with 17 students agreeing, followed by losing track of time and wanting virtual versions of all museums.

> "most frequently expressed opinion is "it was very enjoyable". Then students stated mostly that they couldn't realize how time passed, would like to see virtual version of all of the museums, found it a really useful experience and found rich visual images in virtual museums which are not accessible in most of the text books."
