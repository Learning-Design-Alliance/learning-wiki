## Evidence

### Choi 2026

Choi, B., Lee, H., Kuhn, L., Kim, J., Hong, S.-J., Di Martino, A., Gozzi, A., Lombardo, M. V., Morrison, F. J., Lord, C., & Kim, S. H. (2026). Executive function predicts academic and social skills in autistic kindergartners based on a multimodal approach. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, and Allied Disciplines. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70038

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analyses at kindergarten entry in a longitudinal sample of 67 autistic children aged 4–6 years. The article reports "significantly positively related to current reading" and math, with unstandardized coefficients and FDR-corrected significance; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "EF Touch was significantly positively related to current reading (B = 16.35, SE = 6.51, p = .016) and math (B = 8.23, SE = 3.05, p = .010), when controlling for age, sex, and NVIQ."
