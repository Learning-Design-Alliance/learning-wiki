## Evidence

### Li 2026

Li, Y., & Leibnitz, N. (2026). Gender expression appraisals in introductory physics courses: A cross-institutional replication. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests of androgyny discrepancy scores (Table V) in the 1845-student survey. Patterns were more consistently significant in calculus-based courses; in algebra-based courses men's positive androgyny discrepancy was not significant (p = 0.536).

> "This pattern was strongest among students from gender identity groups with smaller sample sizes (effect size = 0.34), followed by women (effect size = 0.20), and was smaller among men (effect size = 0.09)."
