## Subclaims
`q4 i?` The financial incentive treatment produced a statistically significant improvement in classwork effort in English, Maths and Science, but no significant effects on the other three effort measures. [→ Sibieta et al. 2014](#sibieta-et-al-2014)

## Evidence

### Sibieta et al. 2014

Luke Sibieta, Ellen Greaves, Barbara Sianesi. (2014). Increasing Pupil Motivation: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581249

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary-outcome analysis of the cluster RCT measuring whether pupils met thresholds on four effort domains (attendance, behaviour, classwork, homework), monitored by schools and teachers. The report states that "Across the other measures of effort, there was no secure evidence of a positive impact."

> "For the financial incentive, there was a positive and statistically significant improvement in classwork in English , Maths and Science (at the 5% level) . For the event incentive, there was also a positive impact although this was not statistically significant."
