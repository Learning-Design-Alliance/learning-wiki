Stone L, Puri MC, Guo M, Shah IH. (2021). Assessing knowledge, attitudes, and practice of health providers towards the provision of postpartum intrauterine devices in Nepal: a two-year follow-up. Reproductive Health 18, 43. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12978-021-01099-7
