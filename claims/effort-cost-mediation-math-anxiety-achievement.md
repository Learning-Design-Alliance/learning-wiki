### Kilp-Kabel 2025

Triinu Kilp-Kabel, Kaja Mädamürk. (2025). Expectancies, values, and task persistence can alleviate the negative effects of math anxiety on math performance. European Journal of Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10212-024-00928-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analyses (Table 4) showed a significant indirect path through effort cost in both grades (grade five B = −0.19, grade eight B = −0.20, both p < 0.05).

> "there was a statistically significant indirect path from math anxiety through effort cost to math achievement; therefore, students with higher math anxiety tended to have higher effort cost which in turn predicted lower math achievement"
