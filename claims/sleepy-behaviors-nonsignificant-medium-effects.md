## Evidence

### Beebe 2010

Beebe, D. W., Rose, D., & Amin, R. (2010). Attention, Learning, and Arousal of Experimentally Sleep-Restricted Adolescents in a Simulated Classroom. The Journal of Adolescent Health. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jadohealth.2010.03.005

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Secondary coded behaviors in the video-monitored subset (n=8): yawning, eye rubbing, eyes closed, head on table for ≥3 consecutive seconds. The authors report "the medium-sized effects for “Eyes Closed” and “Head down” (d=.49–.50; Figure 1)" were not statistically significant.

> "Although not statistically significant, the medium-sized effects for “Eyes Closed” and “Head down” (d=.49–.50; Figure 1) may have reached significance in a larger sample."
