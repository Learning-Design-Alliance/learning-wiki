## Evidence

### Bouchefra Said 2023

Bouchefra Said, El Ghouddany Safouane, Ouali Karim, Bour Abdellatif. (2023). Is good dietary diversity a predictor of academic success? Acta Bio Medica : Atenei Parmensis. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10210566/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear regression in the same cross-sectional study of 528 students; breakfast skipping had negative coefficients (−1.062 GPAf, −1.149 GPAm, p < 0.01). The article reports the association "was not significant for other meals".

> "Missing a meal during the day was statistically significant for breakfast (P value < 0.01) with a negative coefficient, which means that the student who skips breakfast is one time more susceptible to getting bad grades, the association was not significant for other meals."
