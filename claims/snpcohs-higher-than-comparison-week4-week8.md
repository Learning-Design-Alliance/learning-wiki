### Napamon P 2025

Napamon P, Jirapongsuwan A, Kalampakorn S, Siri S. (2025). The effect of strengthening nurse practitioners’ competency in occupational health services for agricultural workers exposed to pesticides in primary care units, Thailand: a before-and-after study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.14

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-tests comparing 30 experimental and 30 comparison NPs at each time point. Baseline differences were not significant (P≥0.05); at week 4 and week 8 the experimental group was "significantly higher mean scores across all variables than the comparison group" (e.g., knowledge week 4: 12.47±1.99 vs 10.13±1.48, P<0.001).

> "However, at both post-intervention and follow-up, the experimental group demonstrated significantly higher mean scores across all variables than the comparison group (P<0.05) (Table 4)."
