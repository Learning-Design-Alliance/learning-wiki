`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of response characteristics (Table 4) across gender and home-language groups: mean types, tokens, TTR, OOV tokens, and blank responses for correct (1) and incorrect (0) responses. The pre-trained German word embedding contained 830,875 tokens.

> "Girls produced more tokens than boys, in both correct and incorrect responses, while boys and non-native speakers’ responses contained a higher lexical diversity due to fewer tokens resulting in a higher ratio of unique tokens (i.e., types) to the total number of tokens (type–token ratio, TTR)."
