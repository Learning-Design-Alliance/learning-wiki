### Ng Curtise Kin Cheung 2020

Ng Curtise Kin Cheung. (2020). Evaluation of academic integrity of online open book assessments implemented in an undergraduate medical radiation science course during COVID-19 pandemic. Journal of Medical Imaging and Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmir.2020.09.009

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

T-test comparison of mid-semester mean scores between the 2020 online open book cohort (N = 48) and the 2019 invigilated cohort (N = 47). The article reports the 2020 mean was "statistically significantly lower" at 62.8% vs 71.8%, p < 0.0001; no effect size is printed.

> "However, the mean score of the online open book mid-semester assessment in 2020 (62.8%) was statistically significantly lower than that of the traditional invigilated mid-semester assessment in 2019 (71.8%) with p < 0.0001."
