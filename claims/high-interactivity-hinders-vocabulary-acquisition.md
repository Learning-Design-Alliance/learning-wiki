## Evidence

### Blanka KLIMOVA 2017

Blanka KLIMOVA, Jaroslav KACETL. (2017). Efficacy of Computer Games on Language Learning. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 16(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1160637

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review's Discussion account of deHaan et al. (2011): 80 randomly-selected Japanese university undergraduates paired on English and game proficiency; one played the game for 20 minutes while the paired subject watched. The review attributes the result to "the extraneous cognitive load induced by the interactivity of the game".

> "The follow-up tests revealed that both the players and the watchers of the video game recalled vocabulary from the game, but the players recalled significantly less vocabulary than the watchers."
