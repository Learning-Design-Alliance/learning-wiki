## Evidence

### Bertini-Pérez D. 2024

Bertini-Pérez D., Martin-Ibañez L., Gómez Chica P., Dobarrio-Sanz I., Rodriguez-Arrastia M., Roman P., Rueda-Ruzafa L. (2024). Effectiveness of a Haemorrhage-Control Task Simulator for Training Nursing Students: A Quasi-Experimental before-after Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/9730765

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of the preintervention assessment phase of the one-group study, citing consistency with prior studies using certified manikins that found success rates below 66% and below 24%. No effect size is printed.

> "final-year nursing students had a very poor success rate in haemorrhage management. At baseline, only half of the study participants demonstrated effective control prior to the intervention using a tourniquet. Likewise, the time required and the number of millilitres of blood lost during the preintervention phase were inadequate"
