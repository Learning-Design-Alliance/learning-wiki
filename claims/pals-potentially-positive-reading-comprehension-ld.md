# PALS shows potentially positive effects on reading comprehension for students with learning disabilities (average effect size 0.75, not statistically significant)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r1` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` WWC calculations found no statistically significant effect on the CRAB Questions Correct subscale in either study, but effect sizes of 0.60 (Fuchs et al., 1997) and 0.91 (Saenz et al., 2005) were substantively important, yielding a potentially positive effects rating with a domain average of 0.75 (improvement index +26). ## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2012

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2012). Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. WWC Intervention Report, Students with Learning Disabilities. http://kc.vanderbilt.edu/pals/

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

WWC synthesis of two RCTs measuring reading comprehension with the CRAB Questions Correct subscale in subsets of students with learning disabilities (40 and 20 students). The Fuchs et al. (1997) effect size was 0.60; the Saenz et al. (2005) effect size was 0.91; the domain average across studies was 0.75, improvement index +26, not statistically significant.

> "However, the WWC-calculated effect size for this measure was 0.60—large enough to be considered substantively important according to WWC criteria. The WWC characterizes these study findings as a substantively important positive effect."
