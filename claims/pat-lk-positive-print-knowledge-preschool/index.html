Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Phonological Awareness Training plus Letter Knowledge Training. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/InterventionReport/376

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r1

WWC synthesis of three studies (two RCTs, one quasi-experimental) of PAT+LK in center-based preschool settings. Appendix A3.2 reports the "Domain average9 for print knowledge across all studies 0.74 na +27", i.e. an effect size of 0.74 and an average improvement index of +27 percentile points, with two studies statistically significant and one indeterminate.

"Domain average9 for print knowledge across all studies 0.74 na +27"

What Works Clearinghouse 2006 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Phonological Awareness Training plus Letter Knowledge Training. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/InterventionReport/376

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial of 72 four- and five-year-olds in two Midwestern communities; children were blocked on pretest scores, gender, and age and randomly assigned. Appendix A3.2 reports consonant names effect size 1.27, improvement index +40, statistically significant.

"Gettinger (1986) found a statistically significant difference favoring the intervention group on a measure assessing children's knowledge of the names of consonants, and the WWC confirmed the statistical significance of this effect."
