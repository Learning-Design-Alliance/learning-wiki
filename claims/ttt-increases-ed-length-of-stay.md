### Haută Alexandra 2024

Haută Alexandra, Iacobescu Radu-Alexandru, Corlade-Andrei Mihaela, Nedelea Paul Lucian, Cimpoeșu Carmen Diana. (2024). Translating training to medical practice in trauma care, a literature review. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-024-02548-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Large retrospective pre/post-test study of a four-year TTT program reported by the review; it found 'an increase in ED LOS (Pre-Test 4.88 h vs. Post-Test 7.17 h, P < 0.001)'.

> "despite improvements in trained team outcomes, there was no improvement in patient outcomes such as mortality, but rather an increase in ED LOS (Pre-Test 4.88 h vs. Post-Test 7.17 h, P < 0.001)"
