## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On the four-item Likert general assessment of the course, the traditional group averaged 3.58 versus 3.30 in the blended group (p = 0.012; Mann–Whitney U significance 0.008). [→ Chaves Juan 2020](#chaves-juan-2020)

## Evidence

### Chaves Juan 2020

Chaves Juan, Lorca-Marín Antonio A., Delgado-Algarra Emilio José. (2020). Methodology of Specialist Physicians Training: From Traditional to e-Learning. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17207681

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-course satisfaction survey on a four-item Likert scale (1 to 4). The traditional group's mean was "3.58 (SD = 0.572)" versus 3.30 in the blended group; the Mann–Whitney U test corroborated the significant difference (significance 0.008).

> "Thus, in the traditional group (T) a mean of 3.58 (SD = 0.572) was obtained, compared to a mean of 3.30 (SD = 0.566) in the mixed methodology group (EL), with a p = 0.012."

### Chaves Juan 2020 (2)

Chaves Juan, Lorca-Marín Antonio A., Delgado-Algarra Emilio José. (2020). Methodology of Specialist Physicians Training: From Traditional to e-Learning. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17207681

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Recommendation item scored 0 to 10 in the deferred-phase questionnaire. Means were "8.48 in the T group vs. 8.42 in the EL group", with no significant difference between groups.

> "Regarding the item “I would recommend the course”, no significant differences were observed between the group means: 8.48 in the T group vs. 8.42 in the EL group (score from 0 to 10 points)."
