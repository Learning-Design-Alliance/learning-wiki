Qualitative synthesis of the characteristics of ten included RCTs (Table 2). Only 20% of studies used frameworks such as the ecological model, social cognitive theory or the COM-B/behaviour change wheel; five studies reported fidelity mechanisms such as facilitator daily logs, and no study addressed student compliance.
