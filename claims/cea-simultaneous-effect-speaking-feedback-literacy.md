## Evidence

### I Putu Yoga Laksana 2026

I Putu Yoga Laksana, Ni Made Ratminingsih, Made Hery Santosa & I Putu Indra Kusuma. (2026). Aligning ChatGPT with E-Portfolio Assessment as EFL Learning Model: Its Effect on Student's Speaking Performance and Feedback Literacy. Technology in Language Teaching & Learning, 8, 103616. https://doi.org/10.29140/tltl.2026.103616

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with pretest-posttest control group design; 60 first-semester vocational-college EFL students in experimental and control groups. One-way MANOVA with Wilks' lambda showed "a statistically significant difference" with "partial η2 = 0.524" on both dependent variables.

> "The result of analysis in Table 3 shows a statistically significant difference (F (2, 57) = 31.313,  p < 0.001, Wilks' λ = 0.476, partial η2 = 0.524) between the scores showed on speaking performance and feedback literacy of the participants in the control and experimental groups."
