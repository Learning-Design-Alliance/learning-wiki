## Evidence

### Scerif 2025

Scerif, G., Sučević, J., Andrews, H., Blakey, E., Gattas, S. U., Godfrey, A., et al. (2025). Enhancing children's numeracy and executive functions via their explicit integration: the ONE programme cluster randomised controlled trial. npj Science of Learning 10, 8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00302-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Intervention * EYPP interaction from the cluster RCT's intention-to-treat analysis; the table reports Hedge's g = 0.64 for this interaction, and the text notes the disadvantaged-versus-non-disadvantaged gap was reduced in the intervention group.

> "For children with EYPP eligibility, changes in EYTN scores were larger in the Intervention group (T1 = 25.04; T2 = 32.07) than in the Control group (T1 = 14.86, T2 = 18.48, p = 0.001)."

### Scerif 2025 (2)

Scerif, G., Sučević, J., Andrews, H., Blakey, E., Gattas, S. U., Godfrey, A., et al. (2025). Enhancing children's numeracy and executive functions via their explicit integration: the ONE programme cluster randomised controlled trial. npj Science of Learning 10, 8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00302-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

BAS-PC spatial skills interaction in the same trial; the table reports Hedge's g = 0.65 for the Intervention * EYPP interaction. Disadvantaged children improved more in the intervention group (T1 = 48.49; T2 = 53.98) than controls (T1 = 42.31; T2 = 44.30).

> "Children with EYPP eligibility had poorer spatial skills than children without EYPP eligibility in the Control group,p < 0.001, but not in the intervention group,p = 0.366."
