## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Two rounds of revision reduced the SD of estimates by 43% in decentralized networks and 42% in centralized networks, versus 3% in control groups. [→ Becker et al. 2017](#becker-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Becker et al. 2017

Joshua Becker, Devon Brackbill, and Damon Centola. (2017). Network dynamics of social influence in the wisdom of crowds. PNAS. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1615978114

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In decentralized networks the SD of responses narrowed significantly across 13 trials; the article reports the control condition's 3% SD decrease was significantly smaller than the 43% (decentralized) and 42% (centralized) reductions (n = 21 trials, P <0.001, Wilcoxon rank sum test).

> "two rounds of revision significantly narrowed the SD of responses (n= 13 trials, P <0.001, Wilcoxon signed rank test), producing a 43% reduction in the average SD between rounds 1 and 3"
