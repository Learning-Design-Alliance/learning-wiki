## Evidence

### Abasi-amefon O. Affia 2022

Abasi-amefon O. Affia, Alexander Nolte, and Raimundas Matulevičius. (2022). Integrating Hackathons into an Online Cybersecurity Course. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3510456.3514151](https://doi.org/10.1145/3510456.3514151)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire analysis of six selected teams grouped by course grade; team familiarity was collected once at the first hackathon because teams stayed together. High grade teams' median was 3.25 (IQR 0.13) on a 5-point scale, while most teams reported lower familiarity values. No effect size was printed.

> "However, high grade teams (𝑀 = 3.25,𝐼𝑄𝑅 = 0.13, see Team Familiarity in Fig 3) possessed an above-average and relatively higher perception of team familiarity than teams in other grade groups"
