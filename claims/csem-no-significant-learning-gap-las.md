## Evidence

### Van Dusen 2016

Van Dusen, B., White, J.-S. S., & Roualdes, E. A. (2016). The impact of learning assistants on inequities in physics student outcomes. 2016 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.085

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired t-test analysis of the CSEM portion of the LASSO data (754 students in 21 courses, all LA-supported per Table II). No significant learning gap was found; the article prints no test statistic or effect size for this contrast, and no no-LA comparison was possible.

> "We had no CSEM data for courses without LAs, but the courses with LAs did not find a  significant learning gap (Fig. 2d)."
