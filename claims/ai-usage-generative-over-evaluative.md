## Evidence

### Gibson 2025

Gibson, D., Azukas, M. E., & Knezek, G. (2025). Practitioner Beliefs and Behaviors in AI-Enhanced Education: DOT Framework Survey Evidence.

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey respondents (n = 72) rated current AI use across eleven task categories on a five-point ordinal scale (1 = Do not use, 5 = Use frequently). Highest: instructional materials (M = 3.43), research support (M = 3.21); lowest: automating grading (M = 1.60).

> "Means ranged from 1.60 (automating grading) to 3.43 (generating instructional materials), indicating that generative and content-oriented uses of AI are currently more common than automated or evaluative ones."
