## Evidence

### Lautrette Alexandre et al 2020

Lautrette Alexandre et al. (2020). Impact of take-home messages written into slide presentations delivered during lectures on the retention of messages and the residents' knowledge: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02092-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-study comparison pooling all 54 THMs from the 12 lectures (38 written, 70%), regardless of randomization group. "The THMs retention was higher for written THMs compared to THMs delivered only orally", 18% vs 8%. Multivariable analysis gave aOR = 2.94 (95% CI 2.20–3.93); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The THMs retention was higher for written THMs compared to THMs delivered only orally (18% (n = 473/2630), 95% CI 17–20 vs 8% (n = 91/1108), 95% CI 7–10, P < 0.001)"
