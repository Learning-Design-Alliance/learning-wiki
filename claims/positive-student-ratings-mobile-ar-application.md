## Evidence

### Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan 2015

Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan. (2015). Content Delivery Using Augmented Reality to Enhance Students' Performance in a Building Design and Assembly Project. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076141

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-experiment assessment in session 2: 92% of students called the mobile AR application "very helpful" or "somewhat useful" in their learning process and only 4% reported being distracted by it. Descriptive survey percentages from teacher-designed feedback questions.

> "92% of students stated that the designed mobile AR application has been "very helpful" or "somewhat useful" in their learning process and only 4% of respondents stated that they were "distracted" by this application during their learning experiment."

### Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan 2015 (2)

Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan. (2015). Content Delivery Using Augmented Reality to Enhance Students' Performance in a Building Design and Assembly Project. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076141

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-experiment assessment in session 2: "a solid majority of 86%" viewed positively the possibility of using mobile AR applications in other courses for learning abstract and difficult-to-understand topics. Students also rated willingness to recommend the tool at a mean of 4 on a 1-5 scale (standard deviation of 1.3).

> "a solid majority of 86% had a positive view of the possibility of using mobile AR applications in other courses for the purpose of learning abstract and difficult-to-understand topics."
