Available web-based teaching resources for health care professionals on screening for oral cancer. Medicina Oral, Patología Oral y Cirugía Bucal. https://doi.org/10.4317/medoral.20163

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quality assessment of 22 websites (selected from the first 100 Google and 100 HON results for "screening for oral cancer") using the 16-item DISCERN questionnaire scored 1-5, rated independently by two observers. The mean overall rating was "4.18±1.05".

> "The mean score for the overall rating of the publications using the DISCERN scale was 4.18±1.05, so minimal shortcomings in the quality of the information in the web sites considered in the study can be assumed"
