### Mawyin-Muñoz CE 2025

Mawyin-Muñoz CE, Salmerón-Escobar FJ, Hidalgo-Acosta JA, Calderon-León MF. (2025). Medical simulation: an essential tool for training, diagnosis, and treatment in the 21st century. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07610-z

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Trial by Dekhtyar et al. (2021) in which 285 medical students were randomly assigned to training in abdominal pain or loss of consciousness and assessed with pre- and post-simulation tests. The review reports "The efficiency of differential diagnoses increased by 16% AP (β = 0.37, p < 0.001) and 17% LOC (β = 0.45, p < 0.001)."
