## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gonçalves Mário Rui 2025

Gonçalves Mário Rui, Marinho Ricardo, Reis Sofia Gaspar, Viveiros Ricardo, Teixeira Manuel Moutinho, Andrade Ana Kam, do Carmo Girão Maria, Rodrigues Pedro Pina-Vaz, Castelo-Branco Sousa Miguel. (2025). LapAppendectomy4all: validation of a new methodology for laparoscopic appendectomy simulation and training. Updates in Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13304-025-02127-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective cohort study of 35 first-year general surgery residents divided into group A (video, n = 18) and group B (no video, n = 17), assessed on the silicone model with a specific scale. The "global performance scores were higher in the group that had seen the pre-simulation video" (t(33) = 2,598, p = 0.007).

> "global performance scores were higher in the group that had seen the pre-simulation video—group A (M = 12,82, SD = 2,21 vs M = 11,01, SD = 1,88, t(33) = 2,598, p = 0.007)"

### Gonçalves Mário Rui 2025 (2)

Gonçalves Mário Rui, Marinho Ricardo, Reis Sofia Gaspar, Viveiros Ricardo, Teixeira Manuel Moutinho, Andrade Ana Kam, do Carmo Girão Maria, Rodrigues Pedro Pina-Vaz, Castelo-Branco Sousa Miguel. (2025). LapAppendectomy4all: validation of a new methodology for laparoscopic appendectomy simulation and training. Updates in Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13304-025-02127-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same cohort comparison on the model's subscales: execution (M = 7,00 vs 6,05, p = 0.018) and quality (M = 5,82 vs 4,95, p = 0.046) "were also higher in this group", i.e. the video group. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Individual metrics for execution (M = 7,00, SD = 1,18 vs M = 6,05, SD = 1,34, t(33) = 2,197, p = 0.018) and quality (M = 5,82, SD = 1,41 vs M = 4,95, SD = 1,34, t(33) = 1,73, p = 0.046) were also higher in this group"
