## Evidence

### Changuiti O 2026

Changuiti O, Mahou FZ, Decormeille G, Koraich N, El Hsaini H. (2026). Hybrid simulation approach enhances technical and non-technical skills in therapeutic pharmacology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09983-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-post comparison of 78 first-year nursing students across two hybrid simulation sessions, reported in Results (Table 4). The study reports "hand hygiene compliance rose from 67.9% to 93.6%" and PPE use "increased from 78.2% to 96.2%", both p < 0.001 by McNemar test.

> "For example, hand hygiene compliance rose from 67.9% to 93.6%, and the use of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) increased from 78.2% to 96.2% (both p < 0.001)."
