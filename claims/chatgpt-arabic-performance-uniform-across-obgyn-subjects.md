## Evidence

### Cohen Adiel 2026

Cohen Adiel, Eliasi Elior, Meyer Raanan, Brezinov Yoav, Levin Gabriel. (2026). Performance of ChatGPT in Israeli Arabic-language OBGYN national medical licensure exam. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08753-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive comparison of ChatGPT-3.5 accuracy across the four manually categorized subjects of the Arabic OBGYN exam (Table 1), tested with descriptive statistics; "no difference in ChatGPT performance in the four different subjects of the exam", p = 0.579.

> "There was no difference in ChatGPT performance in the four different subjects of the exam: Gynecologic Oncology (61.5%, 95% CI: 35.1% – 87.9%), Gynecology (44.0%, 95% CI: 24.5% – 63.5%), Obstetrics (42.3%, 95% CI: 28.9% – 55.7%), Reproductive medicine and Infertility (39.4%, 95% CI: 22.7% – 56.1%), p = 0.579."
