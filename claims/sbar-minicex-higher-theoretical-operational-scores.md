# SBAR combined with mini-CEX assessment yields significantly higher theoretical and operational assessment scores than traditional clinical nursing teaching in nursing interns

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After a 3-week intervention, the observation group scored significantly higher than the control group on both theoretical assessment (89.51 ± 7.15 vs. 87.62 ± 4.45, p = 0.002) and operational assessment (94.72 ± 1.94 vs. 91.55 ± 2.13, p < 0.001). [→ Xu Bu 2025](#xu-bu-2025)

## Evidence

### Xu Bu 2025

Xu Bu, Xing Xiaoli, Zhang Guangyun, Zhang Wenting, Zhang Min, Gan Yan, Wei Tao, Xia Xiaodan. (2025). Effect of the situation-background-assessment-recommendation model combined with the mini-CEX assessment on the clinical communication, clinical thinking and comprehensive clinical abilities of nursing interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08022-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial of 405 nursing interns (control n = 201, observation n = 204) at one tertiary teaching hospital; independent sample t-tests after a 3-week intervention. The observation group scored higher on "theoretical (89.51 ± 7.15 vs. 87.62 ± 4.45, p < 0.05)" and operational assessments; Table 1 prints t = −3.185 (p = 0.002) and t = −15.681 (p = 0.000). No effect sizes are reported.

> "Table 1 shows that the observation group had significantly higher theoretical (89.51 ± 7.15 vs. 87.62 ± 4.45, p < 0.05) and operational (94.72 ± 1.94 vs. 91.55 ± 2.13, p < 0.001) assessment scores than the control group."
