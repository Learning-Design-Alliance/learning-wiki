### Lemaire 1995

Lemaire, P., & Siegler, R. S. (1995). Four Aspects of Strategic Change: Contributions to Children's Learning of Multiplication. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 124(1), 83-97. https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.124.1.83

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A 3 (session) by 5 (error type) within-subject ANOVA on percentages of retrieval error types found "the significant session by error type interaction, F(8,152) = 18.54, p< .001", with operand errors increasing greatly over sessions.

> "Of greater interest, however, was the significant session by error type interaction, F(8,152) = 18.54, p< .001. As shown in Table 2, the percentage of retrieval errors that were operand errors increased greatly over the three sessions"

### Lemaire 1995 (2)

Lemaire, P., & Siegler, R. S. (1995). Four Aspects of Strategic Change: Contributions to Children's Learning of Multiplication. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 124(1), 83-97. https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.124.1.83

> "Over the same period, percentage of errors consistent with the oddeven pattern characteristic of multiplication increased from 39% to 48% to 66%."
