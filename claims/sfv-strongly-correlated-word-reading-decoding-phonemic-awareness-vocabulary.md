### Edwards 2022

Edwards, A. A., Steacy, L. M., Seigelman, N., Rigobon, V. M., Kearns, D. M., Rueckl, J. G., & Compton, D. L. (2022). Unpacking the Unique Relationship Between Set for Variability and Word Reading Development: Examining Word- and Child-Level Predictors of Performance. Journal of Educational Psychology, 114(6). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Zero-order and age-corrected correlations among child-level predictors and SfV in a sample of 489 children in grades 2–5. The study reports "large associations with word identification (.79)" and word attack (.76).

> "All child-level predictors were significantly correlated with SfV, with large associations with word identification (.79), word attack (.76), phonological awareness (.66), and vocabulary (.50), and moderate associations with rapid letter naming (−.35) and inattention (.33)."
