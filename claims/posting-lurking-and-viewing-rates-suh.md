## Evidence

### Resnick PJ 2010

Resnick PJ, Janney AW, Buis LR, Richardson CR. (2010). Adding an Online Community to an Internet-Mediated Walking Program. Part 2: Strategies for Encouraging Community Participation. J Med Internet Res 12(4):e72. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1339

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Log analysis of participation in the single community arm (n=254): 45% posted at least once, 9% posted more than 5 times, and 20% were lurkers viewing at least one forum page per week. The printed correlation r = 0.65 between viewing and posting is large and is the basis for the impact rating.

> "Those who viewed more pages posted more messages (Poisson regression, r = 0.65, P < .001). Of all members, 5% (12/254) never viewed a forum page."
