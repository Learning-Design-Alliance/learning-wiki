## Evidence

### Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2018

Emily Alicea-Muñoz, Joan Espar Masip, Carol Subiño Sullivan, and Michael F. Schatz. (2018). Assessing a GTA professional development program. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of ten common five-point Likert statements from Orientation Surveys 2014–2016; an analysis of variance revealed no statistical differences in statement means across the three years. Means included "M = 4.54, SD = 0.70" for helpfulness and M = 2.13, SD = 0.95 for preferring more lecturing.

> "Each statement mean across three years demonstrates that GTAs in general ﬁnd the Orientation to be useful ("Going through [Orientation] before the TA job begins is helpful to me," M = 4.54, SD = 0.70) and valuable ("Microteaching was a valuable practical experience," M = 4 .54, SD = 0 .72)"
