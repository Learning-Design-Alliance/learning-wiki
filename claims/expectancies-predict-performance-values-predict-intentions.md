The review reports Meece et al. (1990), a one-year longitudinal analysis of students' math expectancies and subjective task values predicting performance and intentions, with both direct and indirect effects on the outcome variables.

> "They found that students' expectancies for success were the strongest direct predictor of their subsequent math performance, and the importance they attached to math was the strongest direct predictor of their intentions to continue taking it in high school."
