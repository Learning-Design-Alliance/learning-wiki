`q1 · i?` · `design · r?`

Retrospective instructor observation from a single-year case report, interpreted through Bandura's self-efficacy theory; no pre/post conceptual inventories were administered and no learning gains are claimed.

> "The welding phases placed the students in the expert role; this inversion normalised the subsequent physics-heavy phases. The mech-anism is consistent with Bandura's model [15]: mastery experiences in welding raised confidence to engage with unfamiliar physics."
