Evidence¶

Geers Ann E.; Hayes Heather 2011¶

Geers Ann E.; Hayes Heather. (2011). Reading, Writing, and Phonological Processing Skills of Adolescents with 10 or More Years of Cochlear Implant Experience. Ear and Hearing. https://doi.org/10.1097/AUD.0b013e3181fa41fa

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Group comparison on the Picture Spelling Test between 112 CI-HS students and 46 hearing control high school students. CI students spelled "67% of items correctly compared to 80%" with a significant Mann-Whitney test (U = 3480, z = 3.46, p = .001); 55% of CI students were within 1 SD of controls while 45% scored two or more SDs below.

"On average, the CI-HS participants were significantly poorer spellers than the hearing control group, spelling 67% of items correctly compared to 80% (Mann-Whitney U = 3480, z = 3.46, p = .001). However, more than half of the CI-HS students (55%) spelled within one standard deviation of the NHC-HS group"
