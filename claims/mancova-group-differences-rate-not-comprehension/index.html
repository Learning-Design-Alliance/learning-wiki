Evidence¶

Shimono 2018¶

Shimono, T. R. (2018). L2 reading fluency progression using timed reading and repeated oral reading. Reading in a Foreign Language, 30(1). http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

q2 · i? · causal · r1

One-way MANCOVA comparing TROR, TR, and comparison groups on posttest rate and comprehension with pretest scores as covariates; follow-up Bonferroni ANOVAs found a significant rate difference but no significant comprehension difference among groups.

"For posttest reading rates, a significant difference was found, F(2, 50) = 12.13, p < .001, partial η2 = .33. However, for posttest comprehension scores, the difference was not significant, F(2, 50) = 0.67, p = .514, partial η2 = .03."
