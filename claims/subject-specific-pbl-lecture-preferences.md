# Students prefer PBL for physiology, pathology and pharmacology concepts but prefer lectures for anatomy, biochemistry and microbiology topics

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a perception survey of 107 students, PBL was preferred for physiology (59.81%), pathology (51.40%) and pharmacology (53.27%), while lectures were preferred for anatomy (78.50%), biochemistry (45.79%) and microbiology (42.99%). [→ Adhikari Yadav Saroj 2022](#adhikari-yadav-saroj-2022)

## Evidence

### Adhikari Yadav Saroj 2022

Adhikari Yadav Saroj, Poudel Sangeeta, Pandey Oshna, Jaiswal Dhiraj Prasad, Malla Bhupendra Prakash, Thakur Brajesh Kumar, Gautam Swotantra, Pandey Sirish Raj. (2022). Performance and preference of problem-based learning (PBL) and lecture-based classes among medical students of Nepal. F1000Research 11:183. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.107103.1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Perception questionnaire (30 Likert items, alpha 0.893) administered to all 107 students; Table 2 reports preference percentages per subject, e.g. physiology PBL 59.81% and anatomy lectures 78.50%.

> "Students mostly preferred physiology, pathology, and pharmacology-related concepts through PBL whereas they preferred anatomy, biochemistry, and microbiology-related topics through lectures."
