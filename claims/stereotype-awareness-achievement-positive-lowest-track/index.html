Evidence¶

Lisa Bardach 2023¶

Lisa Bardach, Claudia Neuendorf, Kou Murayama, Thorsten Fahrbach, Michel Knigge, Benjamin Nagengast, and Ulrich Trautwein. (2023). Does students' awareness of school-track-related stereotypes exacerbate inequalities in education? npj Science of Learning 8:59. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00203-9

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Cross-sectional (RQ3) achievement paths from the multigroup growth curve models; the lowest-track effect differed significantly from the higher and combined tracks (both ps < 0.001). The authors interpret it as support for the stereotype-awareness-as-an-indicator-of-missed-opportunities hypothesis.

"Initial levels of school-track-related stereotype awareness in Grade 5 were significantly and positively related to initial levels of achievement in the lowest track (b = 0.647, p < 0.001), whereas the relationships were not statistically significant in the two other tracks"
