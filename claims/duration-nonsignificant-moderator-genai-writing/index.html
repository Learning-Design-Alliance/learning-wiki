Evidence¶

Mark Feng Teng 2026¶

Mark Feng Teng. (2026). Effectiveness of GenAI in enhancing writing performance: a meta-analysis. Asian-Pacific Journal of Second and Foreign Language Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40862-026-00444-2

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Robust meta-regression with HC3 standard errors (k = 9 non-missing for duration). The article reports duration was not significant and "explained virtually none of the between-study variance", noting most studies clustered in short to mid-term windows, restricting the range needed to test temporal moderation.

"Intervention duration did not significantly predict effect size in the robust meta-regression model (β = 0.05, p = 0.430), and duration explained virtually none of the between-study variance (Pseudo R 2 = 0.00)."
