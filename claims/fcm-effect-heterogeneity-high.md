## Evidence

### Talan 2020

Talan, T. & Batdi, V. (2020). Evaluating the Flipped Classroom Model through the Multi-Complementary Approach. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 21(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1269613

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Heterogeneity analysis within the same meta-analysis of 71 effect size comparisons. The printed Q value of 354.326 exceeded the critical value of 90.531, so the authors interpreted the distribution as heterogeneous and conducted calculations under the random effects model. No effect size for the heterogeneity itself is printed.

> "It can be said that the distribution is heterogeneous, seeing that the Q-statistical value (354.326) is greater than the critical value (90.531)."
