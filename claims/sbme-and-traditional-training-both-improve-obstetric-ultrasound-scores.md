### Zhou Jinguang 2026

Zhou Jinguang, Zhao Yongfeng, Zhou Ping, Tang Xiaohong, Liu Wengang, Guo Shiyan, Wang Jie, Li Linlu. (2026). Evaluating the effectiveness of high-fidelity simulation-based training on clinical skill transfer in obstetric ultrasound residents: a prospective randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08835-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized controlled trial of 22 PGY-2/PGY-3 residents randomized to simulator (n=11) or traditional (n=11) training; paired pre/post comparisons showed significant within-group gains (e.g., traditional group on volunteers p = 0.017), with "both training methods improved the participants’ performance".

> "The results indicate that both training methods improved the participants’ performance in the routine mid-trimester fetal ultrasound scan assessment."
