# Students without prior programming experience anchor more heavily in code and engage minimally with metaphor views compared to students with prior experience

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 1 associational `r1`, 1 qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students with prior experience showed more balanced dwell distributions (Code 39.6%, Memory 43.5%, Metaphor 13.2%) while students without prior experience anchored in code (48.1%) with minimal metaphor use (3.3%). [→ pe-dwell](#pe-dwell)
`q2 i?` Group differences in dwell distributions had moderate effect sizes (Cliff's delta -0.28 to 0.33) but the comparison was underpowered given group sizes. [→ cliffs-delta](#cliffs-delta)
