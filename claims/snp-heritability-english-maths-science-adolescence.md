## Evidence

### Donati 2021

Donati, G., Dumontheil, I., Pain, O., Asbury, K., & Meaburn, E. L. (2021). Evidence for specificity of polygenic contributions to attainment in English, maths and science during adolescence. Scientific Reports, 11, 3851. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-82877-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SNP heritability estimated by LD score regression and GREML on the three subject GWAS in ALSPAC. The article reports constrained "h2SNP = 0.36–0.54), with the highest estimate obtained for science" and GREML estimates from 0.41 (English) to 0.53 (science). No standardised effect size is printed for this finding.

> "LDSC regression h2SNP estimates using GWAS summary statistics were moderate and significant for all three academic subjects (LDSC unconstrained h2SNP = 0.29–0.34, constrained h2SNP = 0.36–0.54), with the highest estimate obtained for science"
