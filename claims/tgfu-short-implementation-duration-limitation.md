The review's synthesis of the 12 studies' durations groups them into blocks below 9 lessons, between 12 and 16 lessons, and above 18 lessons of 40–60 min, with most research in the first two blocks and all but one study based on a single unit. The authors state "The implementation of the model is carried out in too short a time to achieve significant outcomes".
