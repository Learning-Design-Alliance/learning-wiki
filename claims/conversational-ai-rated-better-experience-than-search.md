### Taneja 2026

Taneja, K., Singh, A., & Goel, A.K. (2026). Impact of Multimodal and Conversational AI on Learning Outcomes and Experience. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_60](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_60)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Survey data from the same RCT analyzed with Kruskal-Wallis and Dunn's tests with Holm correction. Effect sizes ranged "from moderate (.051) to large (.123)"; participants found conversational systems "significantly easier to navigate (p=.002, η 2 =.09) and more engaging to use (p < .001, η2 =.12)", and rated their responses as more detailed, helpful, and clear than DocSearch.
