## Evidence

### Ocal 2017

Mehmet Fatih Ocal. (2017). The Effect of Geogebra on Students’ Conceptual and Procedural Knowledge: The Case of Applications of Derivative. Higher Education Studies, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v7n2p67

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test on overall ADT post-test scores in the true experimental design (n=55) showed a 2.85-point mean difference favoring the Geogebra group; the author notes this difference came mainly from conceptual knowledge scores.

> "there was a statistically significant mean difference for students’ overall scores in Geogebra group (M=14.73, SD=4.897) and that in control group (M=11.88, SD=4.658); t(53)=2.187, p=.033"
