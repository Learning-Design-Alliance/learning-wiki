## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Students’ Understanding of Stern Gerlach Experiment. arXiv:1602.06374 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the individual interviews, students were asked whether they could think of a strategy to distinguish the superposition (|↑⟩z + |↓⟩z)/√2 from a mixture of half |↑⟩z and half |↓⟩z particles. The article reports "This task was difﬁcult for most students." One viable strategy the article describes is passing each through an SGX−, where the superposition exits one channel and the mixture hits detectors in both channels.
