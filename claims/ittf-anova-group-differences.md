### Doxey 2026

Doxey, J., Baty, B. J., Bisordi, K., & Dixon, S. (2026). Assessing genetic counselors' teaching experience and approaches to teaching in the classroom. Journal of Genetic Counseling. https://doi.org/10.1002/jgc4.70207

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ANOVA among 89 ATI-R respondents comparing ITTF scores of GCs who only teach GC students, only teach non-GC students, and teach both. The article reports "F(2, 86) = 4.08, p = 0.02" with Tukey HSD post hoc comparisons; no effect size is printed.

> "However, there was a significant difference between the average ITTF scores between the three groups (F(2, 86) = 4.08, p = 0.02)."
