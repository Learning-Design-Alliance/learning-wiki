Null result from the n=131 study testing whether prompt frequency explained uptake differences; "no significant difference between Cluster 3 and Cluster 2". Equivalence was not tested. Mean prompts: reproducers 7.63, reconstructors 9.11, integrators 7.61.

"An independent samples t-test revealed no significant difference between Cluster 3 and Cluster 2 (t(119) = − 1.84, p = 0.069, 95% CI [− 3.13, 0.12])."
