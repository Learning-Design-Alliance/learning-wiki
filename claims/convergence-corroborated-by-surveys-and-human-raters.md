## Evidence

### Littenberg-Tobias 2021

Littenberg-Tobias, J., Borneman, E., & Reich, J. (2021). Measuring Equity-Promoting Behaviors in Digital Teaching Simulations: A Topic Modeling Approach. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584211045685

`q2 · i?`

Survey corroboration of the simulation-response convergence among LE participants, using paired comparisons of composite equity mindset and equity-promoting practice scores at immediate postcourse and follow-up; the abstract prints effect sizes of 0.88 SD and 0.32 SD respectively.

> "This shift was corroborated by LE participants also expressing more equity-oriented mindsets and described engaging in more equity-promoting practices on surveys (both immediate postcourse and follow-up)."

### Littenberg-Tobias 2021 (2)

Littenberg-Tobias, J., Borneman, E., & Reich, J. (2021). Measuring Equity-Promoting Behaviors in Digital Teaching Simulations: A Topic Modeling Approach. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584211045685

`q2 · i?`

Human-rater validation for Roster Justice only: research assistants rated responses on a 5-point Avoidant-Aware rubric (weighted Krippendorff's alpha 0.76, 25% double-rated), blind to the distance measure. Topical similarity to HE participants was associated with more equitable ratings.

> "Additionally, for one of the simulations, Roster Justice, we found that LE participants whose responses were most similar topically with HE participants were also more likely to be rated as equitable by human raters."
