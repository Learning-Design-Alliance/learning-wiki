## Evidence

### cost-report

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2019, November). Supporting Postsecondary Success intervention report: InsideTrack© Coaching. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/696

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Cost figures reported in the report's cost section, drawn from the Bettinger & Baker (2014) study and a more recent program. Pricing included a fixed customization charge and a variable charge depending on the number of students coached.

> "in 2004 and 2007 InsideTrack© charged about $500 per student per semester for its most comprehensive program. This included a fixed charge for the cost of customizing its program to a particular university and a variable charge that depended on the number of students coached. A more recent InsideTrack© Coaching program launched in 2013 was reported to cost $390 per student per semester."
