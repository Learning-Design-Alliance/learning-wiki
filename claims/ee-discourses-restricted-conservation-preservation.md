## Evidence

### Elizabeth Bozoti Pasin and Reinaldo Luiz Bozelli 2016

Elizabeth Bozoti Pasin and Reinaldo Luiz Bozelli. (2016). An Analysis of Curriculum and Undergraduate Students' Discourses about Environmental Education: A Debate Concerning Teacher Training. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(18), 12197-12234. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1122668

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Discourse analysis of questionnaire responses from 40 voluntary undergraduate biology teacher-training students at a Brazilian federal public university, analyzed with French Discourse Analysis. The study reports that "The majority of the discourses indicated that EE meanings are restricted to conservation or preservation."

> "The majority of the discourses indicated that EE meanings are restricted to conservation or preservation, and some indicated confusion regarding the purposes of both EE and ecology disciplines."
