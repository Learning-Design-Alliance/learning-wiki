## Evidence

### Tachibana Tetsuya 2025

Tachibana Tetsuya, Katagiri Hiroki, Ogawa Takahisa, Koyano Gaku, Jinno Tetsuya. (2025). Enhancing the accuracy of cup placement in total hip arthroplasty using CT-based navigation by a trainee surgeon: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-025-08493-3

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on 112 trainee cases (20 alignment outliers, 17.9%; 21 position outliers, 18.8%); the first 20 cases predicted 3D position outliers with "odds ratio of 3.46". Table 5 reports OR 10.41 (3.1−34.5, p < 0.01) for alignment outliers.

> "we identified the first 20 cases as a predictive risk factor responsible for outliers with an odds ratio of 3.46 (95% CI, 1.13−10.62, p = 0.02) in the 3D position"

### Tachibana Tetsuya 2025 (2)

Tachibana Tetsuya, Katagiri Hiroki, Ogawa Takahisa, Koyano Gaku, Jinno Tetsuya. (2025). Enhancing the accuracy of cup placement in total hip arthroplasty using CT-based navigation by a trainee surgeon: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-025-08493-3

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

In the final multivariable model (age, BMI, case number, preoperative pelvic sagittal inclination), male sex predicted alignment outliers with "3.89 (95% confidence interval [CI], 1.02−14.73, p = 0.046)". The authors offer possible explanations (muscle volume, pelvic morphology) and call for further studies.

> "We identified the first 20 cases and male patients as predictive risk factors responsible for outliers with an odds ratio of 10.41 (95% confidence interval [CI], 3.1−34.5, p < 0.01) and 3.89 (95% confidence interval [CI], 1.02−14.73, p = 0.046) for cup alignment"
