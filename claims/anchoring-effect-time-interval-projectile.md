## Evidence

### Dimitrios Gousopoulos 2023

Dimitrios Gousopoulos. (2023). Investigating students' scientific reasoning through heuristic and analytical thought processes. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.07158](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.07158)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Written and audio-recorded responses to question B4 in the 45-student interview study. The article reports 36/45 used the correct formula and that "16/36 - 44.4%" were hooked on the t = 2 s anchor, preventing activation of the requested 1 s interval.

> "A large percentage of students, while using the correct mathematical formula to find the rate of change of kinetic energy (36/45), replaced the time t in the mathematical formula with t = 2sec and not with t = 1sec."
