### Chong Shiau Yun 2019

Chong Shiau Yun, Chittleborough Catherine Ruth, Gregory Tess, Lynch John, Mittinty Murthy, Smithers Lisa Gaye. (2019). The controlled direct effect of temperament at 2-3 years on cognitive and academic outcomes at 6-7 years. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0204189

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Marginal structural model analysis of the imputed LSAC sample (n = 5107) estimating controlled direct effects of temperament on outcomes at 6-7 years. The article prints the standardized effect size "0.11 SD" higher verbal (PPVT) ability per 1-unit higher persistence, and 0.06 SD higher non-verbal ability.

> "In the MSM for instance, 1-unit higher persistence (range 1–5) was associated with 0.58-unit (0.11 SD) higher verbal (PPVT) and 0.19-unit (0.06 SD) higher non-verbal ability (Matrix Reasoning)."
