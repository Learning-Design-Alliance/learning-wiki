### Lerch S.P. 2025

Lerch S.P., Huwendiek S., Nendaz M., Klöppel S., Pinilla S. (2025). Perceptions of Ad Hoc Entrustment, the Need for Supervision, and Coping Strategies in Clinical Residents: A Qualitative Study. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006019

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the interview thematic analysis, this subtheme describes how residents "observed the standards and then adapted their degree of autonomy"; self-entrustment also depended on a case's complexity and the resident's confidence, and supervisors co-constructed decisions through unstructured entrustment-based discussions.

> "The level of supervision was influenced by several factors, including social norms for self-entrustment, context-related factors, and entrustment-based discussions."
