## Evidence

### David J. Webb 2025

David J. Webb, Cassandra A. Paul. (2025). High stakes exams inflate a gender gap and contribute to systematic grading errors in one introductory physics series. [doi:10.1103/ngrs-dxtf](https://doi.org/10.1103/ngrs-dxtf)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of first-attempt and retake exam records from three retake classes in the introductory physics series. A chi-square test comparing retake uptake found "no measurable gender difference" (χ2(1, N= 399) = 0.89, p= 0.35); a t-test on average retakes (1.41 vs 1.33) also gave p= 0.49.

> "We find that 78%±4% of male students retook at least one exam and 74%±3% of female students retook at least one exam (aχ 2 test showed χ2(1, N= 399) = 0.89 withp= 0.35 so no measurable gender difference)."
