### Dreyfus 2014

Dreyfus, B. W., Gupta, A., and Redish, E. F. (2014). Applying Conceptual Blending to Model Coordinated Use of Multiple Ontological Metaphors. Department of Physics, University of Maryland. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2015.1025306](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2015.1025306)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interpretive case analysis of classroom video from an introductory physics for life sciences course, using predicate analysis and gesture analysis of one lecture episode about chemical bond energy. The authors conclude the professor's speech and gestures show "ideas from the two different mental spaces are blended into a single mental space."

> "This indicates that Prof. Farnsworth is not just switching back and forth between the two metaphors, but that ideas from the two different mental spaces are blended into a single mental space."
