## Evidence

### Cavanagh A. J. 2018

Cavanagh A. J., Chen X., Bathgate M., Frederick J., Hanauer D. I., Graham M. J. (2018). Trust, Growth Mindset, and Student Commitment to Active Learning in a College Science Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-06-0107

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate regressions with trust and mindset together: mindset was "a nonsignificant predictor of each outcome". The article also reports growth mindset was not significantly associated with course grade (zero-order r = 0.09).

> "Growth mindset is a nonsignificant predictor of each outcome, indicating that trust is accounting for a significant amount of the variance in student commitment to active learning, engagement, and final course grade over and above the influence of growth mindset"
