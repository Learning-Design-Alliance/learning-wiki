From classroom training to e-learning: a journey through the quality of learning life of nurse students in post-graduate education - A longitudinal qualitative study. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Fifth theme of the longitudinal qualitative study, "Adaptation strategies to the new educational life". Students reported becoming more expert with devices, valued reviewing video recordings of lectures as "a great added value" for those who work, and perceived strength in being a group.

> "the participants were stimulated to recognize and identify adaptation mechanisms to improve the quality of their educational life in e-learning. From this initial phase, the participants then moved on to consider e-learning as a new training opportunity for the future."
