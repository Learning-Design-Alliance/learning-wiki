### Mandic Ruzica Nikolic 2024

Mandic Ruzica Nikolic, Bjegovic-Mikanovic Vesna, Bukumiric Zoran, Terzic-Supic Zorica, Todorovic Jovana, Grujicic Sandra, Nesic Dejan, Laaser Ulrich. (2024). Exploring factors of physicians' preferences for continuing medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06374-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robust regression in the cross-sectional survey of 2,625 physicians. The article reports "Physicians who work in primary healthcare institutions appreciate more the public health domain (B = 2.327, p = 0.002)" plus essentials and clinical emergency associations.

> "Physicians who work in primary healthcare institutions appreciate more the public health domain (B = 2.327, p = 0.002), essential group of competencies (2.155, p = 00.5) and clinical emergency (0.748, p = 0.002)."

### Mandic Ruzica Nikolic 2024 (2)

Mandic Ruzica Nikolic, Bjegovic-Mikanovic Vesna, Bukumiric Zoran, Terzic-Supic Zorica, Todorovic Jovana, Grujicic Sandra, Nesic Dejan, Laaser Ulrich. (2024). Exploring factors of physicians' preferences for continuing medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06374-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robust regression in the same survey. The article reports specialists judged these domains less important, and separately that "physicians having subspecialisation less appreciate the clinical emergency domain (-2.023, p = 0,037)."

> "those physicians with specialisation judged as less important competencies in public health (-1.795, p = 0.046), group of essentials competencies (-2.901 p = 0.002|) and clinical emergency domain (-1.592, p < 0.01)."
