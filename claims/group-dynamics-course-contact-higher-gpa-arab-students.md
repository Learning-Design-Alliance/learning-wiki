## Evidence

### Kahalon 2023

Kahalon, R., Shnabel, N., Sharvit, K., Halabi, S., & Wright, S. C. (2023). High-Quality Contact With Fellow Majority Group Students Is Associated With Better Academic Performance of Minority Group Students. Personality & Social Psychology Bulletin, 49(12), 1723–1736. https://doi.org/10.1177/01461672221115943

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA of archival records for all Arab psychology students (n = 125) at a large Israeli university (2005–2015), predicting bachelor's degree GPA from group dynamics course participation with Sechem score as covariate. The article reports "participation in the group dynamics course was associated with higher GPA, F(1, 122) = 9.71, p = .002, ηp2 = .07".

> "As expected, participation in the group dynamics course was associated with higher GPA, F(1, 122) = 9.71, p = .002, ηp2 = .07, 90% CI[.02, .15]."
