Evidence¶

Magid 2014¶

Michael Magid. (2014). An Application of the L2 Motivational Self System to Motivate Elementary School English Learners in Singapore. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v2i1.232

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Focus group interview with all experimental-group participants in the final workshop; participants described positive emotions from imagining their Ideal L2 self and affirmed these feelings motivated them.

"They told me that they felt “proud”, “amazed”, and “great”. Then, I asked them if feeling proud, amazed, and great motivated them to learn English. All of them said that those feelings did motivate them to learn English, which illustrates the motivational impact of the positive emotions brought about by imagining one’s Ideal L2 self."
