## Evidence

### Polat 2017

Polat, O., Yavuz, E., A. & Tunc, A., B., O. (2017). The effect of using mind maps on the development of mathematics and science skills. Cypriot Journal of Educational Science. 12(5), 32-45. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1140786

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann Whitney-U comparison of post-test Science Skills sub-scale scores between the 15-child mind map group and the 15-child control group in the same quasi-experimental design; the printed statistic is "U= 9.000 p<.01". No effect size is reported.

> "There was a meaningful difference between the experiment group and the control group in terms of the MRPS Science Skills sub-scale (U= 9.000 p<.01). When mean rank was taken into consideration, it was understood that children who worked with mind maps had better Science Skills compared to the children who did not."
