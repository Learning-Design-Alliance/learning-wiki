## Evidence

### Kopatz 2025

Kopatz, M., Wagner, W., Neuendorf, C., Trautwein, U., Nagengast, B., and Bardach, L. (2025). Class Composition Effects in Achievement and Interest and the Role of the Learning Environment Revisited: A Conceptual Replication and Extension of Lavrijsen et al. (2022). Preprint. https://osf.io/n5xur/?view_only=48daea427ab44c9abd13b21b1e2b42f6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The abstract reports the null direct effects of within-class heterogeneity and the heterogeneity-by-differentiated-instruction interaction: "heterogeneity in achievement or interest did not predict outcomes directly." No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Although heterogeneity in achievement or interest did not predict outcomes directly, differentiated instruction was particularly advantageous in more heterogeneous German classes for both achievement and interest."

### Kopatz 2025 (2)

Kopatz, M., Wagner, W., Neuendorf, C., Trautwein, U., Nagengast, B., and Bardach, L. (2025). Class Composition Effects in Achievement and Interest and the Role of the Learning Environment Revisited: A Conceptual Replication and Extension of Lavrijsen et al. (2022). Preprint. https://osf.io/n5xur/?view_only=48daea427ab44c9abd13b21b1e2b42f6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The Educational Impact and Implications Statement elaborates the interaction: in heterogeneous German classes "where teachers adapt their instruction to individual needs, students show higher achievement and motivation in German." No comparable mathematics effects are reported in the available text.

> "Simply having classmates who vary widely in achievement or motivation does not seem to affect students’ later outcomes; however, in such heterogeneous classes where teachers adapt their instruction to individual needs, students show higher achievement and motivation in German."
