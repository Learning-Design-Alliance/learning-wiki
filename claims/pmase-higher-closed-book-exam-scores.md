## Evidence

### Zhang Yongshuo 2026

Zhang Yongshuo, Li Wenbo, Zhang Chuchen, Wang Yawei, Feng Shuo. (2026). Reforming hospital management education with the P-MASE pedagogy: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08948-8

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Prospective parallel-group RCT; 120 undergraduates stratified by major and randomized 1:1 to P-MASE (n = 60) or traditional instruction (n = 60) for 16 weeks. The "P-MASE group had higher closed-book examination scores" (88.3 ± 3.2 vs. 73.6 ± 5.8, P < 0.001); Table 4 also reports case analysis scores of 90.1 ± 4.5 vs. 65.4 ± 7.2 (P < 0.001).

> "In this educational RCT, the P-MASE group had higher closed-book examination scores than the control group (88.3 ± 3.2 vs. 73.6 ± 5.8, P < 0.001)."
