`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square test comparing racial/ethnic distributions of the two matriculating cohorts (n = 54 each). Descriptively, REM representation was 40.74% in the holistic-rubric cohort versus 37.04% in the vGRE cohort; the difference was not statistically tested as significant.

> "The multinomial distributions of race and ethnicity (White, Asian, Hispanic, and Other Underrepresented Minorities) were not significantly different χ2 (3) = .530, P = .912"
