Evidence¶

Çelik Korkmaz 2018¶

Çelik Korkmaz, Ş. & Karatepe, Ç. (2018). The Impact of Multi-sensory Language Teaching on Young English Learners’ Achievement in Reading Skills. Novitas-ROYAL (Research on Youth and Language), 12(2), 80-95. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1195281

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental nonequivalent comparison groups study of 45 fourth-grade pupils in Bursa, Turkey. Mann-Whitney U test of post-test scores showed a statistically significant difference in reading comprehension favouring the experimental group (Mdn = 71) over the control group (Mdn = 42), with a large effect size (r = -0.629).

"The Mann-Whitney U test results revealed that there was a statistically significant difference between the treatment groups’ reading comprehension (U = 66.50, p = .000, r = -0.629)."
