The playbook summarizes prior research (citing Attendance Works' compilation of key studies) on attendance-outcome links, stating low versus high attendance are linked with lower versus higher skills, grades, and graduation. Effect sizes are not printed.

"Low attendance is linked with lower academic skills, grades, and high school graduation. High attendance is linked with higher academic skills, grades, and high school graduation."

Related Claims¶

  • Attendance Support students maintained a 3.0 GPA despite low attendance but graduated high school at 71 percent, below the district average, and fewer than half attended college — related
  • Most CPS charter high schools enrolled students with eighth-grade test scores similar to or lower than their neighborhood feeder pools but with higher eighth-grade attendance — related
  • Ninth-grade GPA strongly predicts on-time high school graduation, with an especially large gap between F and D students — related
  • Risk thresholds on GPA and attendance differ for active English Learners: above 95% attendance or above 2.0 GPA predicted ~90% graduation for active ELs, while former and never ELs reached similar likelihood at 90% attendance — related
