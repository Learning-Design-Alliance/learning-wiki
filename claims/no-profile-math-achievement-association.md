## Evidence

### Hornstra 2017

Hornstra, Lisette; Majoor, Marieke; Peetsma, Thea. (2017). Achievement goal profiles and developments in effort and achievement in upper elementary school. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 87(4), 606-629. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12167

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel analysis of CITO mathematics achievement scores across grades 4-6 for 722 students found no significant profile effects. The article suggests favourable profiles in mathematics enhanced effort but not achievement gains, possibly because students worked harder without using the most effective strategies.

> "even though the observed patterns of differences in math achievement appear to be in line with our hypotheses (Figure 5), we did not find any significant associations between profile membership and (developments in) mathematics achievement"
