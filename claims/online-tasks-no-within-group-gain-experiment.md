## Evidence

### Altay 2017

Altay, İ. F., & Altay, A. (2017). The impact of online reading tasks and reading strategies on EFL learners' reading test scores. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 13(2), 136-152. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1159168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-test on the experiment group's pre- and post-test scores in the quasi-experiment. The article reports no significant difference and concludes the online reading tasks "did not affect students' reading achievement".

> "The results revealed that there was not a statistically significant difference between pre -test (M=19.52, SD=3.09) and post -test (M=20.72, SD=3.82) scores of experiment group; t(24)= -1.27, p=0.215."
