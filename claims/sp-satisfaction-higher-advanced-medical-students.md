## Evidence

### Zerbini Giulia 2025

Zerbini Giulia, Schneider Pia, Reicherts Miriam, Roob Nina, Jung-Can Kathrin, Kunz Miriam, Reicherts Philipp. (2025). A novel multi-measure approach to study medical students’ communication performance and predictors of their communication quality - a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07018-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

In the same cross-sectional simulated-consultation study (N = 43), the standardized patient rated satisfaction using a 7-item shortened MISS-21 covering rapport and communication comfort. The SP "gave significantly higher satisfaction / comfort ratings" for advanced students (Cohen's d = 0.74).

> "the SP (who was also blinded with regard to the semester of the medical students) gave significantly higher satisfaction / comfort ratings for consultations with 5th /7th semester students compared to 1st semester students (t [41] = 2.42, p =.020, Cohen’s d = 0.74)"
