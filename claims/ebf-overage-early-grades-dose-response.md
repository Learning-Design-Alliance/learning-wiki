## Evidence

### Mohammed Shamsudeen 2023

Mohammed Shamsudeen, Webb Emily L., Calvert Clara, Glynn Judith R., Sunny Bindu S., Crampin Amelia C., McLean Estelle, Munthali-Mkandawire Shekinah, Lazarous Nkhata Dube Albert, Kalobekamo Fredrick, Marston Milly, Oakley Laura L. (2023). Effects of exclusive breastfeeding on educational attainment and longitudinal trajectories of grade progression among children in a 13-year follow-up study in Malawi. Scientific Reports 13, 11413. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-38455-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Age-stratified GEE analysis (Model 2, ages 6–9, n = 972) from the same Karonga cohort, adjusted for baseline confounders. The article describes this as evidence of a dose–response effect of exclusive breastfeeding duration on age-for-grade in the 6–9 age band.

> "Children exclusively breastfed for 6 months had 36% lower odds of being over-age for grades between ages six and nine than those exclusively breastfed for less than 3 months (aOR = 0.64; 95%CI = 0.43–0.94)."

### Mohammed Shamsudeen 2023 (2)

Mohammed Shamsudeen, Webb Emily L., Calvert Clara, Glynn Judith R., Sunny Bindu S., Crampin Amelia C., McLean Estelle, Munthali-Mkandawire Shekinah, Lazarous Nkhata Dube Albert, Kalobekamo Fredrick, Marston Milly, Oakley Laura L. (2023). Effects of exclusive breastfeeding on educational attainment and longitudinal trajectories of grade progression among children in a 13-year follow-up study in Malawi. Scientific Reports 13, 11413. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-38455-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Age-stratified GEE analysis (Model 3, ages 10–13, n = 891) found no evidence of an association; the adjusted odds ratio for 6 months EBF was 0.93 (95%CI 0.64–1.35). Equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no evidence of an association between exclusive breastfeeding duration and age-for-grade between ages 10 and 13."
