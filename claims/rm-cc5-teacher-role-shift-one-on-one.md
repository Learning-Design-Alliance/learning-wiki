### Nicole Shechtman 2019

Nicole Shechtman, Jeremy Roschelle, Mingyu Feng, Corinne Singleton. (2019). An Efficacy Study of a Digital Core Curriculum for Grade 5 Mathematics. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419850482

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory RQ3 analysis of daily teacher implementation logs (average 13.4 vs. 13.3 logs per teacher; 100% vs. 87.9% completion) comparing interaction structures across groups. The article also reports control teachers led more whole-group instruction (39.0% vs. 11.7%, t = 17.7, p < .0001) and small-group work (29.5% vs. 15.5%, t = 8.8, p < .0001).

> "In the treatment group, teachers reported greater frequency of one-on-one instruction with individual students (mean of 49.9% of total log days vs. 27.0% in the control group, t = 12.3, p < .0001)."
