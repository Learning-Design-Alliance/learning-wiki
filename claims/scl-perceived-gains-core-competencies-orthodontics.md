`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-discussion questionnaire in the pilot study of 64 valid respondents. The article reports majorities believed the session "improved" or "greatly improved" competencies across the four treatment domains with the printed percentages.

> "Post-discussion surveys indicated that the majority of participants believed the session “improved” or “greatly improved” their competencies in examination and diagnosis (76.56%), treatment planning (78.13%), treatment execution (75.00%), and monitoring and management of common issues (75.00%)."
