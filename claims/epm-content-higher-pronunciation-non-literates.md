## Evidence

### Iqbal Tassawar 2018

Iqbal Tassawar, Iqbal Saqib, Hussain Syed Sajid, Khan Iftikhar Ahmed, Khan Hikmat Ullah, Rehman Attiqa. (2018). Fighting adult illiteracy with the help of the environmental print material. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0201902

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Manual pronunciation assessment after the four-week CAL learning sessions: participants in both groups were shown alphabet letters and asked to pronounce them; responses were recorded manually against unique participant ids.

> "The results showed that the mean score of the EG was significantly better than the mean score of the CG (t (54, 2) = 3.86, p < 0.008). The mean pronunciation score of the EG was (μ = 18.03, SD = 7.95) greater than the mean score of the CG (μ = 12.85, SD = 5.81)"

### Iqbal Tassawar 2018 (2)

Iqbal Tassawar, Iqbal Saqib, Hussain Syed Sajid, Khan Iftikhar Ahmed, Khan Hikmat Ullah, Rehman Attiqa. (2018). Fighting adult illiteracy with the help of the environmental print material. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0201902

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup pronunciation analyses by age and gender via independent t-tests; the EG outperformed the CG in each subgroup, with subgroup means printed in Table 5.

> "The male EG achieved a significantly better pronunciation score than the male CG and the female EG achieved a significantly better pronunciation score as compared to the female CG with (t (60, 2) = 3.27, p = 0.002) and (t (47, 2) = 2.43, p = 0.02) respectively."
