Evidence¶

Howie Erin K. 2023¶

Howie Erin K., Perryman Kristi L., Moretta Joseph, Cameron Laura. (2023). Educational outcomes of recess in elementary school children: A mixed-methods systematic review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294340

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Acute pre-post experimental study (Stapp, 2018) with 12 fifth graders observed before and after a 25-minute recess; a paired test showed time on-task rose from 36.6% to 70.3%. The review notes the study "did not have a control comparison". No effect size was printed.

"Using a paired-test for the 12 included participants, time on-task increased from 36.6% to 70.3%."
