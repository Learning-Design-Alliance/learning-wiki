Esse Ciego 2026¶

Esse Ciego, Shubbhi Taneja, Wilson Wong, and Amanpreet Kapoor. (2026). Student Perceptions and Preferences Regarding AI-Generated Instructional Videos in Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28203

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Inductive thematic analysis of open-ended responses. Theme 1 (low content quality) included inaccurate information (n=94, 58%), lack of depth (n=22, 13%), and lack of engagement (n=21, 13%); Theme 2 concerned disconnect between students and educators; Theme 3 concerned eroding educational and societal value. Nine students (6%) reported no concerns.

"We coded 163 non-blank responses to an open-ended question – “What concerns, if any, do you have about using AI-generated videos in education?”using 10 unique codes which were abstracted into 3 themes."
