Evidence¶

Trehan Abhishek 2015¶

Trehan Abhishek, Barnett-Vanes Ashton, Carty Matthew J, McCulloch Peter, Maruthappu Mahiben. (2015). The impact of feedback of intraoperative technical performance in surgery: a systematic review. BMJ Open. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4480038/

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic review conducted per PRISMA guidelines searching MEDLINE, Embase, PsycINFO, AMED and the Cochrane Database; 1531 citations screened, three eligible RCTs comprising "280 procedures by 62 surgeons". All three studies identified improvements in one or more outcomes assessed.

"Overall, feedback based on intraoperative surgical performance was found to be a powerful method for improving performance."
