Related Claims¶

  • All AITAQS items load above 0.50 on their target factor with cross-loadings below 0.30, in the hypothesized assignment (X1–X3 Feedback Quality through X10–X12 User Trust). — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The AITAQS item pool shows sampling adequacy for factor analysis, with KMO = 0.758 and a significant Bartlett test (χ² = 438.75, df = 66, p < 0.001). — related
  • Bifactor-ESEM shows the highest fit among CFA, ESEM, bifactor-CFA and bifactor-ESEM models, supporting AITAQS as one general factor plus four specific dimensions. — related
  • Exploratory factor analysis in the pilot stage supported a four-factor solution, with three items removed (a weak loading, a cross-loading, and a low item–total correlation with duplicated content) — related
