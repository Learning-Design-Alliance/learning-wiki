EL-Andari R 2023¶

EL-Andari R, Bozso SJ, Kang JJH, Fialka NM, Adams C, Nagendran J. (2023). Teaching heart valve surgery techniques using simulators: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.017921

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review (PRISMA) of PubMed and Embase to May 2022; 1322 articles screened, 67 full texts reviewed, 10 included. The review reports that "Significant improvement in surgical trainees' time to completion of tasks, number of mistakes, skills scores and theoretical knowledge" was found across the included studies.
