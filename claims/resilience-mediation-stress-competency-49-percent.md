## Evidence

### Shen Lijun 2024

Shen Lijun, Lu Yuanmei, He Yanrong, Wang Peicheng, Chen Yanhua, Li Hange, Yang Zhenghan, Zhang Jingfeng, Wang Zhenchang, Jiang Maoqing, Zheng Jianjun, Zhu Jiming. (2024). Work stress and competency among radiology residents: the mediating effect of resilience. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1415351

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three-step mediation analysis (Baron and Kenny) with hierarchical regressions on the national resident survey. The total mediating effect "was −0.494 (β = −0.57 * 0.86), accounting for 49.06% of the total effect".

> "The total mediating effect between work stress and competency was −0.494 (β = −0.57 * 0.86), accounting for 49.06% of the total effect."

### Shen Lijun 2024 (2)

Shen Lijun, Lu Yuanmei, He Yanrong, Wang Peicheng, Chen Yanhua, Li Hange, Yang Zhenghan, Zhang Jingfeng, Wang Zhenchang, Jiang Maoqing, Zheng Jianjun, Zhu Jiming. (2024). Work stress and competency among radiology residents: the mediating effect of resilience. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1415351

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap estimation of direct and indirect effects with demographic covariates. The direct effect "indicated a negative influence of work stress on competency scores [coefficient = −0.48, 95% CI = (−0.79, −0.16)]".

> "The direct effect illustrated by Table 4 indicated a negative influence of work stress on competency scores [coefficient = −0.48, 95% CI = (−0.79, −0.16)]."

### Shen Lijun 2024 (3)

Shen Lijun, Lu Yuanmei, He Yanrong, Wang Peicheng, Chen Yanhua, Li Hange, Yang Zhenghan, Zhang Jingfeng, Wang Zhenchang, Jiang Maoqing, Zheng Jianjun, Zhu Jiming. (2024). Work stress and competency among radiology residents: the mediating effect of resilience. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1415351

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robustness analysis across the six competency dimensions (Supplementary Tables S1–S6). The effect of work stress on patient care "was fully mediated by resilience"; for the other five dimensions, mediation proportions "ranged from 40 to 55%".

> "In particular, we showed that the effect of work stress on patient care was fully mediated by resilience. For the other five dimensions, the proportion of mediating effects ranged from 40 to 55%."
