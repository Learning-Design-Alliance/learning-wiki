## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On average, students could recall between 87% (midterm mean) and 88% (final mean) of the information reviewed using the TtR strategy, based on assessment scores across 59 students. [→ Divoll 2010](#divoll-2010)

## Evidence

### Divoll 2010

Divoll, Kent and Browning, Sandra (2010) "The "Classroom Ticket" to Concept Retention," International Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning: Vol. 4: No. 2, Article 10. https://doi.org/10.20429/ijsotl.2010.040210

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mixed methods study of two professors' 59 undergraduate students in classroom management courses; unannounced midterm (short answer) and final (multiple choice) exams assessed only TtR-reinforced concepts. The article reports "the mean score on the midterm across all sections was 87 while the mean for the final was 88."

> "On average, based on the assessment scores, the students could recall between 87% (midterm mean) and 88% (final mean) of the information that was reviewed using our strategy."
