## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental group taught with educational technology innovations scored significantly higher on the total achievement test than the control group taught by traditional methods (T = 6.3, significant at 0.05). [→ Ibrahim 2017](#ibrahim-2017)

## Evidence

### Ibrahim 2017

Ibrahim, H. G. (2017). Utilization of Innovations and Techniques of Educational Technology in Delivering of Educational Practicum and its Impact on Increasing Academic Achievement among Pre-Service Teachers. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(8), 185-194. https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1142431.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental post-application comparison of two groups of student teachers (n = 22 each): experimental taught with educational technology innovations, control taught by traditional methods. The article reports a statistically significant difference in favor of the experimental group, with mean scores of 42.11 versus 38.91.

> "Table (5) shows that the calculated (T) Value (6.3) is statistically significant at the level of 0.5 which emphasizes that there is a statistically significant difference between the mean scores of students of both the experimental and control groups, in favor of the former group which adopted the innovations and techniques of educational technology"
