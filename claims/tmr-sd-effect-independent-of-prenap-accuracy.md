### Nathan W. Whitmore 2024

Nathan W. Whitmore, Erika M. Yamazaki & Ken A. Paller. (2024). Targeted memory reactivation with sleep disruption does not weaken week-old memories. npj Science of Learning 9, 64. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00276-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon rank-sum test on the TMR-SD effect for low versus high pre-nap error objects per participant. The authors report that low-error trials in the present study were significantly more accurate than overall pre-nap error in the prior 1-hour-delay study, so weaker memories cannot explain the null effect.

> "there was no signi ﬁcant difference in the TMR-SD effect as a function of low versus high pre-nap error [ V =6 5 , p = 0.611], indicating a similar lack of memory decline."
