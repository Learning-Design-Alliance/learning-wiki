# Children with LA show a less severe impairment pattern than children with MLD, differing only on symbolic approximate addition

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children with LA showed group differences only on the symbolic approximate addition task, whereas children with MLD showed consistent differences across symbolic tasks. [→ Bert De Smedt 2011](#bert-de-smedt-2011)

## Evidence

### Bert De Smedt 2011

Bert De Smedt, Camilla K. Gilmore. (2011). Defective number module or impaired access? Numerical magnitude processing in first graders with mathematical difficulties. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology, 108, 278–292. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2010.09.003

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the post-hoc comparisons across the comparison and approximate addition tasks in the three achievement groups: LA children showed a "similar but less severe performance pattern" than MLD children, with deficits confined to symbolic approximate addition.

> "Children with mild forms of mathematical difficulties, i.e. children with LA, showed a similar but less severe performance pattern, with only group differences on the symbolic approximate addition task."
