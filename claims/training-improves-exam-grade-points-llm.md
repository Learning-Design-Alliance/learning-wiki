## Evidence

### Benjamin M. Chen and Hong Bao 2026

Benjamin M. Chen and Hong Bao. (2026). Training for Technology: Adoption and Productive Use of Generative AI in Legal Analysis. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.6217459](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6217459)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized mock examination, mean grade points were 2.251 (Group 2) versus 2.521 (Group 3); the trained group's advantage corresponds to "roughly one fine grade". The printed effect size is d = 0.270.

> "Participants in Group 3 did better than participants in Group 2 on the same measure, indicating that the treatment intervention increased the quality of answers (d = 0.270; p = 0.027*, one-tailed t-test)."
