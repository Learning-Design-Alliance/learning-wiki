## Evidence

### Abegglen S 2020

Abegglen S, Krieg A, Eigenmann H, Greif R. (2020). Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing (OSAD) in simulation-based medical education: Translation and validation of the German version. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244816

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interrater reliability analysis of the same 57 rated debriefings comparing single, two-rater and three-rater ICCs. The article reports "0.650 for the total score" for single raters and concludes average ratings of two raters are highly reliable for the total score, with three raters needed for category-level analysis. No effect size printed.

> "The single ratings yielded lower ICCs with 0.650 for the total score and a range of 0.274 to 0.678 for the categories (Table 2)."
