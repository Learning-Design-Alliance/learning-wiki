## Evidence

### Lei 2018

Lei, H., Cui, Y., & Zhou, W. (2018). Relationships between student engagement and academic achievement: A meta-analysis. Social Behavior and Personality, 46(3), 517–528. https://doi.org/10.2224/sbp.7054

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis via homogeneity testing (QB,BE = 429.996, df = 1, p < .001). The article reports r = .428 for other-reported versus r = .303 for self-reported behavioral engagement; emotional engagement showed the same pattern (r = .431 vs .211).

> "the effect size (r = .428, p \< .001) between other-reported behavioral engagement and academic achievement was significantly higher than that for self-reported behavioral engagement (r = .303, p \< .001)"
