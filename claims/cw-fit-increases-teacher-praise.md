# CW-FIT significantly increases teacher praise and attention to appropriate behaviors

## Subclaims
`q4 i?` CW-FIT classroom teachers increased praise/points from 4 to 40 per 20-minute observation, a significant change much greater than in comparison classrooms. [→ Debra Kamps 2015](#debra-kamps-2015)

## Evidence

### Debra Kamps 2015

Debra Kamps, Howard Wills, Harriett Dawson-Bannister, Linda Heitzman-Powell, Esther Kottwitz, Blake Hansen, and Kandace Fleming (2015). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams "CW-FIT" Efficacy Trial Outcomes. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300714565244

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GLMM analysis of teacher praise/points frequencies in the randomized trial. CW-FIT teachers went "from 4 to 40 praises/points" while comparison teachers changed only from 4.46 to 4.62.

> "For praise, there is a phase by condition interaction, F(1, 1817) = 534.87, p < .001. Within the treatment condition the change from baseline to intervention is greater as can be seen by the LSMEANS which indicated that CW-FIT classroom teachers went from 4 to 40 praises/points"
