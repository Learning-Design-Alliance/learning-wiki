The Center for the Future of Teaching and Learning 2010¶

The Center for the Future of Teaching and Learning. (2010). Ready to Succeed in the Classroom: Findings from Teacher Discussion Groups on their Experiences and Aspirations Teaching Students in the Foster Care System. Prepared for the Stuart Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED511840

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Qualitative finding from the Teachers' Wish Lists section, organized at three levels (schools, districts, community). Teachers also wanted streamlined procedures such as one counselor for all students in foster care, professional development, planning time, on-site counseling, and district help keeping children in the same school or feeder system when placements change.

"At the top of the "within schools" list was a better, faster system for communicating to teachers that they had a child or youth in foster care in the classroom. Late, incomplete, or non-existent paperwork was a common refrain."
