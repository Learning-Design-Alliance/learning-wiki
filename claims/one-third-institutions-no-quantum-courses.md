## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 484 institutions (33%) have no course whose title or description mentions quantum; the difference across Carnegie classifications is statistically significant, with a printed Cramér's V of 0.413 described as a medium effect size. [→ Piña 2025](#pina-2025)
