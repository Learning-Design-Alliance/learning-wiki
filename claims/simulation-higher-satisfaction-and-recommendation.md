### Alghzawi A 2026

Alghzawi A, Alhalaseh L, Khalifa S, Qudah R, Saleh A-A, Okour M, Arabas E, Samhouri S. (2026). Enhancing compassionate communication: comparing lecture and simulation training in breaking bad news training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09034-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training feedback questionnaire in the same cohort study: 70.9% of sixth-year vs 29.1% of fifth-year students rated the training very helpful; 64.2% vs 35.8% would recommend it to peers (p < 0.001). Percentages as printed; no effect size reported.

> "Students’ perceptions of the training experience differed between cohorts. Sixth-year students were more likely to rate the training as “very helpful” (70.9% vs. 29.1%) and to recommend it to peers (64.2% vs. 35.8%; p < 0.001) (see Supplementary Figures S1 and S2)."
