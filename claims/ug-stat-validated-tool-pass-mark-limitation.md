## Evidence

### McCracken DJ 2019

McCracken DJ, Laursen CB, Barker G, Gleeson FV, Cullen KM, Rahman NM. (2019). Thoracic ultrasound competence for ultrasound-guided pleural procedures. European Respiratory Review. https://doi.org/10.1183/16000617.0090-2019

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review description of the UG-STAT tool used in the Australasian pathway: it "assesses 100 points over 11 domains" and includes mannequin-based scanning and site marking; the review states it is validated to discriminate novice, intermediate and advanced users.

> "This tool assesses 100 points over 11 domains related to the British Thoracic Society guidelines, including familiarity with basic ultrasound parameters, recognition of ultrasound images illustrative of common pleural effusion appearances"

### McCracken DJ 2019 (2)

McCracken DJ, Laursen CB, Barker G, Gleeson FV, Cullen KM, Rahman NM. (2019). Thoracic ultrasound competence for ultrasound-guided pleural procedures. European Respiratory Review. https://doi.org/10.1183/16000617.0090-2019

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the UG-STAT validation study's printed group means (49.3 novice, 73.0 intermediate, 91.5 advanced) and argues the required 90% pass mark "may not be achievable for some users" and ignores that a more basic standard may suffice for straightforward scenarios.

> "The original validation study demonstrated mean scores of 49.3 in the novice group, 73.0 in the intermediate group, and 91.5 for advanced practitioners."
