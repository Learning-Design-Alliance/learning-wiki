# Middle school CSR schools adopting models with limited scientific research bases realized mathematics and reading gains after three years

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All CSR middle schools adopting models with limited scientific research bases realized gains of about 6 percentage points in mathematics and 3 in reading, though the reading relationship was only weakly significant. [→ Orland 2010](#orland-2010)

## Evidence

Orland, M., Hoffman, A., & Vaughn, III, E. S. (2010). Evaluation of the Comprehensive School Reform Program Implementation and Outcomes: Fifth-Year Report. U.S. Department of Education. https://www.ed.gov/about/offices/list/opepd/ppss/reports.html#title

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of middle school achievement gains from 2002 to 2005 by model research base strength. The report finds that "all CSR schools (including low-performing schools) that adopted models with limited scientific research bases realized gains after three years" of about 6 points in mathematics and 3 in reading.
