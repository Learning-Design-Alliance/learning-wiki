## Evidence

### Arciniegas Calle Maria C. 2016

Arciniegas Calle Maria C., Lobelo Felipe, Jiménez Mario A., Páez Diana C., Cortés Sebastian, de Lima Andrés, Duperly John. (2016). One-day workshop-based training improves physical activity prescription knowledge in Latin American physicians: a pre-test post-test study. BMC Public Health 16:1224. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-016-3883-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Question-by-question analysis grouped by the workshop's three topics, from the pre/post-test data of 1044 MDs. The abstract states gain was "higher for the workshop modules on screening/risk stratification and prescription" (43% and 38%) than for benefits and risks (26%); the discussion attributes this to higher baseline awareness of benefits and risks.

> "The knowledge gain was higher for the workshop modules on screening/risk stratification and prescription (43% [CI: 39–48%] and 38% [CI: 34–42%], than for the module on PA benefits and risks (26% [CI: 23–28%])."
