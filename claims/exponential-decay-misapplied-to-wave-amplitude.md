## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2002

Michael C. Wittmann. (2002). The object coordination class applied to wavepulses: Analysing student reasoning in wave physics. International Journal of Science Education 24:1, 97-118. [doi:10.1080/09500690110066944](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500690110066944)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview and written responses to the wave-math problem (figure 4) on interpreting a propagating pulse's equation. One interviewed student first gave a correct physical explanation, then reinterpreted the mathematics and concluded 'the actual function's going to be a lot smaller', changing a previously correct response.

> "Here, students apply the form e-(exponential decay) to the amplitude of the wave function, as a decay in its peak value over time, rather than to the shape of the function and its decay in value at one time. Their focus, it seems, is on the peak of the wavepulse, rather than on the whole of the wave."
