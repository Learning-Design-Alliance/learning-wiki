## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Difficult tests produced a significantly stronger threat effect than easy tests, with a gradient of stronger effects on harder tests. [→ Shewach 2019](#shewach-2019)

## Evidence

### Shewach 2019

Shewach, O. R., Sackett, P. R., & Quint, S. (2019). Stereotype Threat Effects in Settings With Features Likely Versus Unlikely in Operational Test Settings: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Applied Psychology, 104(12), 1514–1534. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/apl0000420

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analytic moderator test of test difficulty across three difficulty bands. The article reports "Difficult tests produced a significantly stronger effect than easy tests, t(54) ⫽ ⫺1.82, p ⫽ .04", while easy–moderate and moderate–difficult comparisons were nonsignificant.

> "Easy tests (50–100% correct) pro- duced an effect size of d ⫽ ⫺.24 (k ⫽ 39, N ⫽ 3,323, SD␦ ⫽ .34), moderate tests (25–50% correct) produced an effect size of d ⫽ ⫺.30 (k ⫽ 78, N ⫽ 3,958, SD␦ ⫽ .40), and difficult tests (⬍25% correct) produced an effect size of d ⫽ ⫺.40 (k ⫽ 17, N ⫽ 999, SD␦ ⫽ .21)."
