Post hoc independent samples t-test with BCa 95% confidence intervals using 5,000 bootstrap samples, comparing diagnostic performance of certified/experienced versus non-certified/untrained physicians. The article reports "no significant differences in diagnostic performance"; no effect size was printed.

> "Our post hoc analysis, which found no significant differences in diagnostic performance between those with POCUS certification or trainer experience and those without formal training or with a non-certified course, aligns with findings in the literature."
