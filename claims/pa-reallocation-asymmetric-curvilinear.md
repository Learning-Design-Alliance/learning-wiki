Numerical modelled reallocation curves in −20 to +20 minute 5-minute increments shown in Fig 1B (weekday analysis, n=1644). The article describes this as "evidence of a curvilinear (non-symmetrical) association" and states benefits appear to plateau for reallocations of five minutes or greater.

> "In the weekday analysis, there was evidence of a curvilinear (non-symmetrical) association with Attainment 8 for reallocations of time spent in physical activity, wherein modelled reductions in physical activity produced larger predicted differences in Attainment 8 than a modelled increase of the same duration (Fig 1B)."
