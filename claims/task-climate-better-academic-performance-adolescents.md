# Task-involving motivational climate is positively associated with adolescent academic performance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium
> **Currency** · newest study 2019 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Among male adolescents, task climate shows a positive direct association with academic performance (r = 0.294, p < 0.001). [→ Castro-Sánchez M. 2019](#castro-sanchez-m-2019)
`q2 i2` Among female adolescents, task climate shows a positive direct association with academic performance (r = 0.272, p < 0.01). [→ Castro-Sánchez M. 2019 (2)](#castro-sanchez-m-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Castro-Sánchez M. 2019

Castro-Sánchez M., Zurita-Ortega F., García-Marmol E., Chacón-Cuberos R. (2019). Motivational Climate in Sport Is Associated with Life Stress Levels, Academic Performance and Physical Activity Engagement of Adolescents. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16071198

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group structural equation analysis of 2452 adolescents; the male model shows a positive direct task climate to academic performance path, "positive and direct (r = 0.294)", significant at p < 0.001.

> "A significant association (p < 0.001) was also found between motivational climate and academic performance. In the case of task climate, the association found is positive and direct (r = 0.294)"

### Castro-Sánchez M. 2019 (2)

Castro-Sánchez M., Zurita-Ortega F., García-Marmol E., Chacón-Cuberos R. (2019). Motivational Climate in Sport Is Associated with Life Stress Levels, Academic Performance and Physical Activity Engagement of Adolescents. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16071198

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

The female structural model shows a positive direct task climate to academic performance association, "positive and direct (r = 0.272; p < 0.01)", in the same cross-sectional SEM.

> "In the case of task climate, the examined association is positive and direct (r = 0.272; p < 0.01)"
