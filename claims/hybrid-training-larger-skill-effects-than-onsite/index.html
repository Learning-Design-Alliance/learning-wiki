Evidence¶

Pumsopa 2025¶

Pumsopa, N., Jirapongsuwan, A., Kalampakorn, S., Siri, S., and Klainin‐Yobas, P. (2025). The Effectiveness of Community Nurse Training Programs to Improve Nurses' Competency in Community Settings: a Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis. International Journal of Nursing Practice. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijn.70090

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup analysis of nine studies (15 comparisons; 1249 participants) examining nurses' skills found "the hybrid subgroup having a larger effect size (SMD = 0.90, 95% CI = 0.67, 1.14) than the onsite subgroup (SMD = 0.44, 95% CI = 0.29, 0.58)"; heterogeneity was not observed within either subgroup.

"The effect sizes varied between the two subgroups (Chi2 = 11.32, p < 0.001; Figure 7), with the hybrid subgroup having a larger effect size (SMD = 0.90, 95% CI = 0.67, 1.14) than the onsite subgroup (SMD = 0.44, 95% CI = 0.29, 0.58)."
