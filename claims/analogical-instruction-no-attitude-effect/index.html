Evidence¶

Ugur et al. 2012¶

Gokhan Ugur, Refik Dilber, Yasemin Senpolat, Bahattin Duzgun. (2012). The Effects of Analogy on Students' Understanding of Direct Current Circuits and Attitudes towards Physics Lessons. European Journal of Educational Research, 1(3), 211-223. http://www.akademikplus.com/eujer/index.html

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Attitude to Physics Scale (15 Likert-type items, reliability 0.83) given as pre-test to both groups and post-test to the experimental group only; pre-post means were 52.22 versus 55.18, t = -1.93, p = 0.06, a marginal non-significant result, with the instruction showing "no effects on the experimental group’s attitudes".
