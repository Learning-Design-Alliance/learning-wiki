## Evidence

### Carter 2016

Carter, B. E., and Wiles, J. R. (2016). Comparing effects of comedic and authoritative video presentations on student knowledge and attitudes about climate change. Bioscene, 42(1), 16-24. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1103782

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment in two sections of a large mixed-majors introductory biology class; one section viewed IPCC films, the other comedy clips, with a no-video Control group compared on pre/post surveys. The article reports "little difference was observed" and no significant group differences in opinion change.

> "Despite our expectations about differential effects between IPCC and Comedy videos, little difference was observed. No group was significantly more or less likely to change their opinion about climate change."
