Evidence¶

WestEd 2019¶

WestEd, Learning Policy Institute, & Friday Institute for Educational Innovation at North Carolina State University. (2019). Sound Basic Education for All: An Action Plan for North Carolina. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED606141

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of NAEP trend data reported in the background section: the eighth grade Black-White math gap grew "from 29 to 37 points" between 2015 and 2017; footnotes report White scores rising from 292 to 295 while Black scores fell from 263 to 258. No effect size is printed.

"on the NAEP , between 2015 and 2017 the gap between Black and White students in both eighth grade mathematics and reading grew. In math, the gap increased substantially, from 29 to 37 points."

WestEd 2019 (2)¶

WestEd, Learning Policy Institute, & Friday Institute for Educational Innovation at North Carolina State University. (2019). Sound Basic Education for All: An Action Plan for North Carolina. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED606141

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The report's background section, summarizing NAEP trend data (Exhibit 4), states that achievement declines on NAEP began after 2013 in mathematics and reading and that achievement gaps widened. No test statistics or effect sizes are printed.

"Declines in achievement have occurred since 2013 in mathematics and reading on the National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP), and achievement gaps have widened."
