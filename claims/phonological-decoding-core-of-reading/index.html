Nation 2017¶

Nation, K. (2017). Nurturing a lexical legacy: reading experience is critical for the development of word reading skill. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0004-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review explicitly limits the lexical legacy account, stating "It is not the case that young children learn to read words by contextual guessing, as is clear from a large evidence base" (refs 49-52). The foundation is the alphabetic principle and phonological decoding skill.
