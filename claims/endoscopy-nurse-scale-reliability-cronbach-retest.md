## Evidence

### Xie G 2025

Xie G, Zeng Z, He Y, Liao X, You C. (2025). Development and psychometric test of core competency assessment scale for gastrointestinal endoscopy nurses. Medicine 2025;104:40(e44969). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000044969

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability testing within the second survey of 512 digestive endoscopy nurses, with a 2-week retest of 50 of those nurses. The scale's "overall Cronbach α coefficient for the scale was 0.841" and test–retest reliability was 0.801 (P < .001).

> "The overall Cronbach α coefficient for the scale was 0.841, with individual dimensions ranging from 0.798 to 0.959. A follow-up survey conducted 2 weeks after the initial survey with 50 nurses who had completed the questionnaire showed Pearson correlation coefficients ranging from 0.718 to 0.835 across dimensions."
