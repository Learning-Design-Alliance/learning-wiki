## Evidence

### Jessica Watkins 2010

Jessica Watkins, Janet E. Coffey, Edward F. Redish, & Todd J. Cooke. (2010). Disciplinary authenticity: Enriching the reforms of introductory physics courses for life-science students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1109.4983](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1109.4983)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of course materials from two semesters of the reformed Organismal Biology course at University of Maryland, covering worksheets, homework, and exams — "for a total of seventy-five questions analyzed." Categories were not mutually exclusive and were refined iteratively from observed patterns.

> "We developed four categories to organize the var- ious exam and homework questions that incorpo- rated physics and/or mathematics."
