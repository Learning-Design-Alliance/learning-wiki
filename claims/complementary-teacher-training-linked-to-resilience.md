García-Crespo, F. J., Fernández-Alonso, R., & Muñiz, J. (2021). Academic resilience in European countries: The role of teachers, families, and student profiles. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253409

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Teaching-variable models from PIRLS 2016 teacher questionnaire data. The article reports students of teachers with complementary training in "the theory of reading, reading evaluation, and corrective reading procedures" were "40 percentage points more likely to be resilient" in Malta; significant in 34.8% of countries (Table 8).
