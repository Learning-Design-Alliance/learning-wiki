Evidence¶

Montag Jessica L. 2015¶

Montag Jessica L., Jones Michael N., Smith Linda B. (2015). The words children hear: Picture books and the statistics for language learning. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797615594361

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Corpus comparison of a 100-picture-book corpus against length-matched contiguous CHILDES segments, using random sampling of token sizes to count unique word types. The article reports that "picture books contained 1.72 times more unique words than did child-directed speech." No effect size or significance test is printed.

"In the total sample, picture books contained 1.72 times more unique words than did child-directed speech."
