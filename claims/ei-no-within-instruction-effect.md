# Explicit information showed no within-instruction effect: PI+EI equalled PI-EI and PBI+EI equalled PBI-EI on both tasks over time

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Comparisons of PI+EI to PI-EI and of PBI+EI to PBI-EI revealed no significant differences on interpretation or production tasks, indicating EI was not a necessary component within either instruction type. [→ Adem Soruç 2017](#adem-soruc-2017)

## Evidence

### Adem Soruç 2017

Adem Soruç, Jingjing Qin, YouJin Kim. (2017). Comparing the Effectiveness of Processing Instruction and Production-Based Instruction on L2 Grammar Learning: The Role of Explicit Information. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(2), 49–70. http://dx.doi.org/1018806/tesl.v34i2.1262

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Summary of within-instruction pairwise comparisons in the Results/Discussion of the five-group study. The article reports EI "was not found to be influential when PI or PBI groups were compared to one another within the groups", with one exception: PBI-EI beat PBI+EI on immediate interpretation only.

> "EI was not found to be influential when PI or PBI groups were compared to one another within the groups (i.e., the comparison of PI+EI to PI–EI, or the comparison of PBI+EI to PBI–EI)."
