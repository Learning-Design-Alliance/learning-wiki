Evidence¶

A. Ginton 1975¶

A. Ginton, G. Urca, and R. E. Lubow. (1975). The effects of preexposure to a nonattended stimulus on subsequent learning: Latent inhibition in adults. Bulletin of the Psychonomic Society, 1975, Vol. 5 (1), 5-8. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03336682

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Controlled experiment with 40 Tel-Aviv University undergraduates randomly divided into four groups. "A comparison of the number of trials to reach the learning criterion" showed differences "in favor of the nonpreexposed groups" (U = 73.5, p < .001, one-tailed).

"A comparison of the number of trials to reach the learning criterion for the subjects preexposed to the critical stimulus (Groups PE/M and PE/NM) as compared to groups not preexposed to the critical stimulus (Groups NPE/M and NPE/NM) yielded differences in favor of the nonpreexposed groups. This latent inhibition effect was highly reliable (U = 73.5 , p < .00 1, one-tailed)."
