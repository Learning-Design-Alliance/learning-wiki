## Evidence

### Belén C. Guerra-Carrillo and Silvia A. Bunge 2018

Belén C. Guerra-Carrillo and Silvia A. Bunge. (2018). Eye gaze patterns reveal how reasoning skills improve with experience. npj Science of Learning 3:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0035-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian models on the visual search metric (number of fixations before homing in on relevant scales); the Reasoning group showed moderate evidence of improvement (BF10 ≈ 6.51) but the Comprehension group did not, and the interaction model was not supported.

> "there was moderate support for the main effect of Time (BF 10 ≈ 4.01 ± 1.14%). These results suggest that changes in visual search were likely due to individual differences and test –retest effects present across both groups, rather than to an effect of reasoning instruction/practice"
