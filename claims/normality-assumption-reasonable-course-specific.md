## Evidence

### Wei He 2022

Wei He. (2022). Achievement and Growth Norms for Course-Specific MAP® Growth™ Tests. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/achievement-and-growth-norms-for-course-specific-map-growth-tests/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Graphical diagnostic analysis (Figures 3.2–3.5) of Integrated Math 2 RIT scores and true score model residuals across fall, winter, and spring terms. The report concludes "these graphs support the assumption of marginal normality for the Integrated Math 2 test" and that normality also seemed reasonable from the scatterplot matrix.

> "The Q-Q plots indicate that most of the data fall close to the 45-degree reference line except at the very low and high ends, suggesting that normality was a reasonably good approximation"
