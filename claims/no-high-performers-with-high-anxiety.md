## Evidence

### Hart 2015

Hart, Sara A.; Logan, Jessica A.R.; Thompson, Lee; Kovas, Yulia; McLoughlin, Gráinne; & Petrill, Stephen A. (2015). A latent profile analysis of math achievement, numerosity, and math anxiety in twins. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000045

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparing the largest class (high performers, lowest math anxiety) with the other four classes, the authors observed that above-average performers on one measure generally did well on the others, and no high-performing, high-anxiety profile emerged.

> "This indicates that in this sample there was no group of children who performed considerably above average on the math achievement and/or numerosity measures and also had high math anxiety."
