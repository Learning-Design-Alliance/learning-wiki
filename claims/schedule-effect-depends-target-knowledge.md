## Evidence

### Rau 2014

Rau, M. A., Aleven, V., Rummel, N., & Pardos, Z. (2014). How Should Intelligent Tutoring Systems Sequence Multiple Graphical Representations of Fractions? A Multi-Methods Study. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0011-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The classroom experiment found the increasingly interleaved condition was "most effective on reproduction with area models but not on reproduction with number lines," which the authors attributed to the relative difficulty of number lines compared to area models.

> "The finding that the increasingly interleaved condition (which gradually moves from a blocked sequence to a more and more interleaved sequence) is most effective on reproduction with area models but not on reproduction with number lines might be attributed to the relative difficulty of number lines, compared to area models."
