## Evidence

### Chen 2025

Chen, Y., Li, J., Chui, H., & King, R. B. (2025). Peer cooperation and competition are both positively linked with mastery‐approach goals: An achievement goal perspective. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 941–961. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12784

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Step 3 full model of the doubly latent ML-SEM adding fear of failure, work mastery, demographic and school covariates. Student-level cooperation was "positively associated with mastery‐approach goals" at β = .13 and competition at β = .07; school-level effects were β = .29 (cooperation) and β = .14 (competition), all p < .001.

> "at the student level, both individual‐level peer cooperation (β = .13, p < .001, medium effect) and peer competition (β = .07, p < .001, small effect) were positively associated with mastery‐approach goals."
