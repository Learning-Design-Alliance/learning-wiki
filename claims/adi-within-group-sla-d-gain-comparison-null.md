# The ADI group showed significant within-group gains on demonstrated scientific literacy from pre- to post-test, while the structured-inquiry group did not

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Experimental-group students improved on the SLA-D (t(33) = 5.51, p < .05, d = 3.62) whereas comparison-group students did not improve significantly (t(32) = 1.25, p > .05). [→ Gülüzar Eymur · Pınar Seda Çetin 2024](#gülüzar-eymur-pınar-seda-çetin-2024)

## Evidence

### Gülüzar Eymur · Pınar Seda Çetin 2024

Gülüzar Eymur · Pınar Seda Çetin (2024). Investigating the role of an inquiry-based science lab on students' scientific literacy. Instructional Science (2024) 52:743–760. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09672-w

`q3 · i3`

Paired-samples t-tests on pre- and post-SLA-D scores reported in the Results. The article states "the overall gain for experimental group was large" (Table 4: means 7.61 to 11.4 for experimental; 7.57 to 8.60 for comparison).

> "the comparison group students, t(32) = 1.25, p > .05 did not develop a better understanding in understanding of the role of science, scientific thinking and doing, science and society, science media literacy, and mathematics in science, the experimental group students did at the end of the study, t(33) = 5.51, p < .05, d = 3.62"
