# GP registrars' self-rated confidence in clinical abilities rose significantly from pre-test to post-test after a 3-hour dementia education workshop, with a strong pre-post effect size, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` GP registrars showed a significant pre-to-post increase in self-rated Confidence in Clinical Abilities (Cohen's d = 1.710 overall; d = 1.525 for the diagnose item) after the 3-hour dementia workshop. [→ Mason Ron 2020](#mason-ron-2020)

## Evidence

### Mason Ron 2020

Mason Ron, Doherty Kathleen, Eccleston Claire, Winbolt Margaret, Long Marita, Robinson Andrew. (2020). Effect of a dementia education intervention on the confidence and attitudes of general practitioners in Australia: a pretest post-test study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033218

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test results for the 332 GP registrars in the pretest post-test study, from the Confidence in Clinical Abilities table. The diagnose item rose from 2.65 to 3.82 with t=23.85, p<0.001, mean difference 1.19 and Cohen's d = 1.525. The overall subscale Cohen's d = 1.710.

> "Confidence in ability to diagnose	GPR (n=332)	2.65	2.56 to 2.73	3.82	3.74 to 3.90	23.85	<0.001	1.19	1.525"
