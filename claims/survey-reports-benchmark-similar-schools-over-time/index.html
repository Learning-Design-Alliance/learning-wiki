Improving Chicago's Schools: Sample High School Summary 2005 2005¶

Improving Chicago's Schools: Sample High School Summary 2005. (2005). UChicago Consortium on School Data Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/improving-chicagos-schools-sample-high-school-summary-2005

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive statement from the report's overview about the comparison structure of the school-specific survey reports. The article explains the reports "show how these things have changed over time" and benchmark schools against demographically similar schools and the system. No effect sizes are reported.

"Because Chicago public schools have participated in the UChicago Consortium's surveys for the past 10 years, these reports also show how these things have changed over time, and how schools compare to a set of demographically similar schools and schools system wide."
