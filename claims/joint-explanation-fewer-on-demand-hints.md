## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Individuals in the self-explanation condition requested nearly twice as many on-demand hints (M = 94.09, SD = 51.92) as dyads in the joint-explanation condition (M = 48.57, SD = 39.59), d = 1.05. [→ Robert G.M. Hausmann 2008](#robert-gm-hausmann-2008)

## Evidence

### Robert G.M. Hausmann 2008

Robert G.M. Hausmann, Brett van de Sande, Carla van de Sande, and Kurt VanLehn. (2008). Productive Dialog During Collaborative Problem Solving. https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.0599 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0806.0599](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0806.0599)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Same 39-participant experiment; total on-demand hints collapsed across all four problems and averaged over students per condition; one-way ANOVA gave "F(1, 23) = 6.20, p = .020, d = 1.05", described as statistically and practically significant.

> "The individuals in the self -explanation condition (M = 94.09, SD = 51.92) requested nearly twice as many hints as the dyads in the joint -explanation condition (M = 48.57, SD = 39.59)."
