Zou J 2026¶

Zou J, Li N, Wang X and Du J (2026) From AI literacy to AI-integrated inquiry-based science teaching: the serial mediating roles of AI-TPACK and science teaching self-efficacy among Chinese pre-service science teachers. Front. Psychol. 17:1911909. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1911909

Same PLS-SEM survey study (n = 548); the structural path from AI-TPACK to science teaching self-efficacy was significant with "β = 0.454, 95% CI [0.374, 0.533]". The article reports f² = 0.221, a medium effect.
