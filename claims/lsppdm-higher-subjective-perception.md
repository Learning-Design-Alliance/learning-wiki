## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Pan Ruijun 2024

Pan Ruijun, Zhou Xueliang, Wu Chao, Zhang Luyang, Wang Jiayu, Zheng Minhua, Shi Ting, Cai Wei, Sun Jing. (2024). Impact of laparoscopic training course for surgical trainees based on an evidence-based pedagogical framework: randomized trial. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrae109

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-training self-administered questionnaire with six sections, completed by all 60 trainees and quality-controlled on the spot. Four of six dimensions favoured the pedagogical group; classroom environment (P = 0.104) and training supplies (P = 0.808) did not differ; no effect sizes printed.

> "there were statistically significant differences between the subjective perception scores and feedback of the two groups of trainees in terms of 'Teaching methods and time schedule' (t = 2.44, P = 0.018), 'Adequate practice opportunities' (t = 2.04, P = 0.047), 'The course contributes to clinical work' (t = 3.39, P = 0.002), and 'Post-course evaluation meets pre-course expectations' (t = 2.93, P = 0.006)"
