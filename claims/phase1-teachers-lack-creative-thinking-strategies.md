## Evidence

### Sriwongchai 2015

Sriwongchai, A., Jantharajit, N., & Chookhampaeng, S. (2015). Developing the Mathematics Learning Management Model for Improving Creative Thinking in Thailand. International Education Studies, 8(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v8n11p77

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative Phase 1 needs assessment using interviewing and observing forms with 6 mathematics teachers and 102 9th-grade students from four schools in Khonsawan District, Chaiyaphum Province, selected by cluster random sampling. The article reports that learning activities "rarely improve the students’ creative thinking" and that teachers lacked fostering ability using open-ended problems. No effect size applies.

> "Most teachers present concepts for the students by explaining details for problem solving methods through rule or algorithm approaches, and not interested in encouraging the students to investigate and think about solutions diversely."
