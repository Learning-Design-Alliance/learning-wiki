## Evidence

### Olive Ross 2026

Olive Ross, Meagan Sundstrom and N.G. Holmes. (2026). Predictive Modeling for High Impact Active Learning Classrooms. arXiv. [doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-9955835/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9955835/v1)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Predictions from the model trained on all 69 classes, applied to simulated COPUS data (Fig. 2). The abstract states these classes are "predicted to have exceptional student learning outcomes (effect sizes greater than 2)". Predictions align with measured classes in this range.

> "Classes that spend 20-40% of class time on group clicker questions, 10-20% of class time on group worksheets, and 10% or more of class time on student questions are consistently predicted to have high learning effect sizes"
