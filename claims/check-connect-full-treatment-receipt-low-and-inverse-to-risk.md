## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Heppen 2017

Heppen, J. B., Zeiser, K., Holtzman, D. J., O'Cummings, M., Christenson, S., & Pohl, A. (2017). Efficacy of the Check & Connect Mentoring Program for At-Risk General Education High School Students. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2017.1318990

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive uptake analysis defining treatment receipt as meeting a mentor at least 20 times during the year plus check data on all monitoring forms. The article reports the three printed receipt percentages.

> "Approximately 59% of students in our treatment group sample received the treatment for at least one year, 34% received the treatment for at least two years, and 14% received the treatment for all three years of implementation."

### Heppen 2017 (2)

Heppen, J. B., Zeiser, K., Holtzman, D. J., O'Cummings, M., Christenson, S., & Pohl, A. (2017). Efficacy of the Check & Connect Mentoring Program for At-Risk General Education High School Students. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2017.1318990

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of predicted graduation probabilities among treatment students by duration of receipt. The article calls this paradoxical and says it speaks to the difficulty of fully implementing the program where high risk and high mobility are intertwined.

> "the 14% of students who received Check & Connect for all three years may have been the students least in need of it; their average predicted probability of on-time graduation was 0.66, compared with 0.53 for the rest of the treatment group students."
