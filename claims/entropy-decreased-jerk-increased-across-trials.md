### Harris David J. 2017

Harris David J., Vine Samuel J., Wilson Mark R., McGrath John S., LeBel Marie-Eve, Buckingham Gavin. (2017). The effect of observing novice and expert performance on acquisition of surgical skills on a robotic platform. PLoS ONE 12(11): e0188233. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0188233

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

3 (trial) x 4 (group) mixed ANOVA on acceleration entropy. The article reports a significant main effect of trial with printed effect size ω² = .084 (quoted as "ꙍ2 = .084") and "entropy decreasing over trials", with no group main effect or interaction.

> "indicating a significant main effect of trial, F(2,232) = 11.82, p < .001, ꙍ2 = .084, BF10 = 1443.24, with entropy decreasing over trials."

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`
