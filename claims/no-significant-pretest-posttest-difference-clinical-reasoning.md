`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired-sample t-test on knowledge tests (three multiple choice, ten extended matching, four essay questions) scored blind by one instructor. The study found "no significant difference between the pretest and posttest scores"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The paired-sample t-test indicated no significant difference between the pretest and posttest scores, albeit a slight increase in the mean score (pretest mean=0.24; posttest mean=0.29, p=0.136)."
