### Schmidt 2017

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Combined CS18 and CS19 data on question order (Figure 6), pooled to reduce small-number uncertainty. The article reports the increase "is not signiﬁcant compared to the uncertainties" and that further data is needed to determine if the trend is specific to conference size, type, or subfield.

> "The cumulative frac- tion of the fourth and ﬁfth to eighth questions asked by women was 23%. This increase in the fraction of questions asked by women mirrors the trend found by Pritchard et al. (2014), but is not signiﬁcant compared to the uncertainties."
