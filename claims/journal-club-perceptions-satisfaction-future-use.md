Almuzaini Ohood K., Wali Saad M., Harbi Maan H., Azhari Hala F. (2025). Evaluating the impact of a journal club activity on pharmacy students’ learning outcomes and professional skills development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07691-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the perception/attitude and feedback domains of the post-activity survey (Table 5); proportions are as printed. Males and hospital pharmacy students gave significantly higher ratings on most of these items.

> "Most participants (81.1%) agreed or strongly agreed that the skills learned would be useful in future courses or careers, and 82.1% noted that the activity somewhat or significantly changed their perception of staying updated with current research. A large proportion (74.0%) indicated they were likely or very likely to recommend the activity to others, and 80.7% rated their overall satisfaction as satisfied or very satisfied."
