## Evidence

### Yue Xin 2025

Yue Xin, Nie Shuai, Dai Yanmei, Sun Jianping, Xu Li, Shen Jing. (2025). Evaluation of a virtual simulation system for root canal irrigation training in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08387-x

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Single-group pre-post study; blinded endodontist scored students' irrigation procedures on 3D-printed tooth models at baseline (T1) and one week after simulation training (T2). Paired t-test gave t(33) = 7.14, p < 0.001, "Cohen's d = 1.22".

> "Practical performance also improved significantly, with a mean increase of 54.4% from T1 to T2 (t(33) = 7.14, p < 0.001; Cohen's d = 1.22), reflecting substantial gains in procedural competence following simulation-based training."
