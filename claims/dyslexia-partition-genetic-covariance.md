## Evidence

### Graell M. et al 2026

Graell M. et al. (2026). Towards disentangling the polygenic contribution of dyslexia to school performance. Translational Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41398-026-04118-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

GNOVA annotation-stratified genetic covariance analysis using GWAS summary statistics for 24 traits examined how dyslexia partitions contribute to genetic overlap. ADHD, major depression, anxiety disorder, addiction factor, insomnia and loneliness showed positive covariance with the discordant and negative with the concordant partition.

> "all traits related to cognition and language ability (except for ambidexterity), as well as anorexia nervosa, autism spectrum disorder (ASD), substance use-related traits (i.e., lifetime cannabis use and alcohol use), subjective well-being, and household income, displayed negative covariance with the discordant but positive covariance with the concordant genetic load for dyslexia"
