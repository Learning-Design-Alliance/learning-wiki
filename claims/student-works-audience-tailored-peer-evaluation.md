Liu Minling, Mo Xiaocong, Li Tingwei, Dai Huiru, Fang Shuo. (2026). Bridging medical education and public health: a pedagogical model to develop future physicians' capacity in audience-tailored health communication. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1792396

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Peer evaluation (range 1-10) of three student-created mask works from the Realizing phase, analyzed with repeated-measures ANOVA across evaluation dimensions. Ratings were favorable across dimensions; no effect size is printed.

> "Overall, all three groups achieved favorable ratings across the dimensions of entertainment, relevance, and effectiveness."

### Liu Minling 2026 (2)

Liu Minling, Mo Xiaocong, Li Tingwei, Dai Huiru, Fang Shuo. (2026). Bridging medical education and public health: a pedagogical model to develop future physicians' capacity in audience-tailored health communication. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1792396

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Peer-evaluation means from Table 2 of the same work-analysis study: Group B scored entertainment 9.00 ± 1.30, relevance 9.08 ± 1.11, effectiveness 9.15 ± 1.00; the article reports P > 0.05 across groups, so the difference is not statistically significant.

> "Notably, Group B, which employed a multiple interdisciplinary composition, attained the highest mean scores in all three criteria: entertainment (9.00 ± 1.30), relevance (9.08 ± 1.11), and effectiveness (9.15 ± 1.00)."
