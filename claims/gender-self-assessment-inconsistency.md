### Ott Thomas 2024

Ott Thomas, Demare Tim, Möhrke Julia, Silber Saskia, Schwab Johannes, Reuter Lukas, Westhphal Ruben, Schmidtmann Irene, Dietz Sven-Oliver, Pirlich Nina, Ziebart Alexander, Engelhard Kristin. (2024). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05714-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory comparison of global self-assessment ratings (1–6 scale) between sexes at three time points in Study 1; females "generally tended towards a worse self-assessment than male" participants.

> "Concerning self-assessment female participants generally tended towards a worse self-assessment than male (T0: p < 0.001, T1: p = 0.027, T2, p = 0.001, additional file 3)"

### Ott Thomas 2024 (2)

Ott Thomas, Demare Tim, Möhrke Julia, Silber Saskia, Schwab Johannes, Reuter Lukas, Westhphal Ruben, Schmidtmann Irene, Dietz Sven-Oliver, Pirlich Nina, Ziebart Alexander, Engelhard Kristin. (2024). Does an instructional video as a stand-alone tool promote the acquisition of practical clinical skills? A randomised simulation research trial of skills acquisition and short-term retention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05714-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Standardised difference between self-assessment and score compared between sexes in Study 1; the difference "was observed only at T2 in the Self-Vid group (p = 0.049)", prompting sex-stratified randomisation in Study 2.

> "a significant difference of differences between females and males was observed only at T2 in the Self-Vid group (p = 0.049); moreover, there was no difference in the other points over time"
