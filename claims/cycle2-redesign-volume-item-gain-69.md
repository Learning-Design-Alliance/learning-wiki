# Redesigning the second research lesson cycle so students compared results across groups raised correct answers on the volume item from 11% to 80%

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In class 4B, after the lesson was changed so students actively sought and compared other groups' data, correct answers on the Part C volume items rose from 11% (pre-test) to 80% (post-test), an increase of 69%. [→ Ming Fai Pang 2011](#ming-fai-pang-2011)

## Evidence

### Ming Fai Pang 2011

Ming Fai Pang, Lo Mun Ling. (2011). Learning study: helping teachers to use theory, develop professionally, and produce new knowledge to be shared. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-011-9191-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Second research lesson cycle of the chemistry learning study, taught to class 4B with one change: students were told the purpose of group work and had to find groups with appropriate data to compare. "In comparison with class 4A, there was a marked improvement in the answers given for these items."

> "We found that while only 11% of the students in class 4B answered the Part C items correctly in the pre-test, 80% of the students gave the correct answers in the post-test, representing an increase of 69%."
