## Evidence

### Lee Jea Woog 2022

Lee Jea Woog, Park Sung Je, Kim Soyeon, Chung Un Sun, Han Doug Hyun. (2022). The Association Between Temperament and Characteristics, Smartphone App Use Patterns and Academic Performance of University Students. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2022.37.e143

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical logistic regression with elite group as dependent variable; Models 2 and 3 were significant (step χ2 42.9/0.001 and 11.3/< 0.042). "Model 3 was adequate for predicting the elite student group" with Nagelkerke's R2 of 0.455; the printed odds ratios are unstandardised coefficients, so no standardised effect size is reported.

> "In Model 3 (demographic + TCI + smartphone app use pattern), model χ2 (55.1/< 0.001), and Nagelkerke's R2 (0.455) (45.5% of the variance in the dependent variable of the elite students) indicated that Model 3 was adequate for predicting the elite student group."
