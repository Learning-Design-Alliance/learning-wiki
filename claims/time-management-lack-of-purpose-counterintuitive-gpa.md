## Evidence

### Bonsaksen T 2017

Bonsaksen T, Brown T, Lim HB, Fong K. (2017). Approaches to studying predict academic performance in undergraduate occupational therapy students: a cross-cultural study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0914-3

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical regression blocks 3 and 4 in the n = 712 cross-cultural sample; Table 3 reports 'time management' std. β = −0.12 (p = 0.04) and 'lack of purpose' std. β = 0.14 (p < 0.01).

> "Somewhat surprisingly, we found that lower scores on 'time management' (strategic approach) was associated with higher GPA among the students."
