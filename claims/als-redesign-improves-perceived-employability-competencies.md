## Evidence

### María-Jesús Martínez-Argüelles 2023

María-Jesús Martínez-Argüelles, Dolors Plana-Erta, Àngels Fitó-Bertran. (2023). Impact of using authentic online learning environments on students' perceived employability. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10171-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same quasi-experiment, competency questionnaire (Table 7): Mann–Whitney U tests showed significant differences for 14 of 15 competencies; results orientation was non-significant (p = .216), with the original course already highly results-oriented (M = 4.08). No effect sizes are printed.

> "students in the experimental group perceived an improvement in almost all the general employability competencies identified by Ornellas et al. (2019), with means of over 3 (on a scale 5) for 80% of the competencies, compared to only 53% over 3 in the control group."
