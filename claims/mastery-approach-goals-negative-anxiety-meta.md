### Diaconu-Gherasim 2024

Random-effects meta-analysis of 34 samples relating mastery-approach goals to anxiety, reported in Table 2 with pooled r = −.10 ("–.10***") and 95% CI −.14 to −.06; significant within-group heterogeneity (I² = 64.32%).

### Diaconu-Gherasim 2024 (2)

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The Results section states the direction of the pooled anxiety effects (pooled r = −.10 for mastery-approach): "mastery-approach goals were negatively related to anxiety" while performance-approach goals showed the opposite sign.
