Evidence¶

Kidd 2013¶

Kidd, J. K., Gadzichowski, K. M., Gallington, D. A., Lopez, C., & Pasnak, R. (2013). A Teacher-Friendly Method of Improving Reading and Mathematics. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Teacher-Friendly+Method+of+Improving+Reading+and+Mathematics%22+SREE

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Screening-test ANOVA across the four randomly assigned conditions (n = 120 analyzed) was nonsignificant, F(3,116) = 0.24, p > .05; equivalence, not interventions, was tested.

"There were no significant differences among the four groups on the number of items correct on the screening test -F(3,116) = 0.24, p >.05 – indicating that random assignment created an equivalent groups design."

Kidd 2013 (2)¶

Kidd, J. K., Gadzichowski, K. M., Gallington, D. A., Lopez, C., & Pasnak, R. (2013). A Teacher-Friendly Method of Improving Reading and Mathematics. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Teacher-Friendly+Method+of+Improving+Reading+and+Mathematics%22+SREE

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA on pattern type showed trivial dimension differences, F(3,256) = .02, p > .05; orientation, F(1,118) = .04, and missing-item position, F(2,237) = 1.69, were also nonsignificant, so variables were collapsed.

"Repeated measures ANOVA showed that differences between the dimensions were trivial, F(3,256) = .02, p >.05."
