Evidence¶

Neher Andrea N. 2025¶

Neher Andrea N., Bühlmann Florian, Müller Martin, Berendonk Christoph, Sauter Thomas C., Birrenbach Tanja. (2025). Virtual reality for assessment in undergraduate nursing and medical education – a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06867-8

q2 · i? · review · r3

Authors' discussion-level argument, not a tested result. The review proposes that tasks "could be automatically evaluated and marked off by the VR software based on a predefined OSCE checklist", freeing examiner cognitive resources for higher-order skills.

"The implementation of automated VR assessment could also facilitate the attainment of objectivity [55, 56]. Tasks, such as accurately auscultating the lungs or appropriately selecting laboratory results, could be automatically evaluated and marked off by the VR software based on a predefined OSCE checklist."
