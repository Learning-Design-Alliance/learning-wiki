Evidence¶

Knight 2016¶

Knight, S., Buckingham Shum, S., Ryan, P., Sándor, Á., & Wang, X. (2016). Designing Academic Writing Analytics for Civil Law Student Self-Assessment. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 28(1), 1–28. doi:10.1007/s40593-016-0121-0

q2 · i? · design · r2

Theme from qualitative coding of student survey comments, written reflections and lecturer notes in NVivo. One student noted the tool detected phrases like 'in conclusion' but that "the use of adverbs such as 'thus,' and 'evidently,' in conclusive statements failed to be recognized". Another worried that relying on signposts constricts writing style.

"Another limitation is that certain sentences, which were recounts or mere descriptions were deemed important, whilst more substantive parts of the essay containing arguments and original voice failed to be detected."
