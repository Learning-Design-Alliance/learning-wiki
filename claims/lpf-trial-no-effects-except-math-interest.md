### Nicolas Hübner 2026

Nicolas Hübner, Juliane Rutsch, Ann-Kathrin Schnelle, Mia Herkommer, Evelin Ruth-Herbein, Benjamin Fauth. (2026). To Grade or Not To Grade? Demystifying a Centuries-Old Debate. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10156-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal quasi-experimental field trial in Baden-Württemberg with 2,342 students (1,218 in 35 trial schools, 1,124 in 40 control schools) across three assessments from beginning of Grade 2 to end of Grade 3. The trial abolished grades and mandated competency grids, learning-development meetings, and formative feedback. "Aside from math interest, we found no statistically significant differences" across ten outcomes. No effect sizes printed for the null findings.

> "Aside from math interest, we found no statistically significant differences between students in trial schools and those in control schools by the end of Grade 3, after accounting for baseline differences."
