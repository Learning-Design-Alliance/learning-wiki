Systematic review content analysis of 69 validated digital literacy frameworks, coded inductively in two stages with dual coding (inter-rater Cohen's κ = .89 to .92). The review reports that "eleven shared final categories remained" after iterative refinement, reported in Table 2.

> "In Stage 2, the preliminary categories were merged and iteratively refined against the extracted elements until eleven shared final categories remained."
