## Evidence

### Luo Mingwei 2024

Luo Mingwei, Pang Jie, Xie Shiwei, Xu Huamin, Yan Jing. (2024). A study of the correlation between residents’ humanistic care skills and their level of emotional intelligence—A cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06097-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the 236-resident survey data, using socio-demographic characteristics and the four WLEIS dimensions as independent variables. Table 4 prints coefficients including self-assessment of emotions B = 1.817 (P = 0.001) and Emotion Control B = -1.814 (P = 0.024).

> "Multiple linear regression analysis showed that self-emotional appraisal, emotional appraisal of others, emotional control, emotional use and experience of working in the school community were independent predictors of humanistic care competencies."
