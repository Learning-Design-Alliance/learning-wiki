## Evidence

### Anna H. Miller 2025

Anna H. Miller, Daniel R. Espinas, Daniel McNeish, & Marcia A. Barnes. (2025). Dosage Response in Intensive Mathematics Interventions for Early Elementary Students With or At-Risk for Mathematics Learning Disability. Educational Psychology Review, 37, 91. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10070-y

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis (Model 3) across 163 effect sizes contributing to the group-size term (one whole-class study was masked out). The authors report "the average effect size for 1:1 interventions was 0.5 points greater than the average effect size for small-group interventions" at 20 h.

> "The main effect of group size (small-group as the reference group) on effect sizes was significant (β = 0.50, 95% CI [0.06, 0.94]). At 20 h of intervention, holding the effects of other variables constant, the average effect size for 1:1 interventions was 0.5 points greater than the average effect size for small-group interventions."
