Evidence¶

Keles 2025¶

Keles, S., ten Braak, D., Hagen, Å. M., & Melby-Lervåg, M. (2025). Validity of Early-Grade Screening Tools in the Three Tier (RTI/MTSS) Model: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Literacy, Language, and Mathematics. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10077-5

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Single moderator analyses (not meta-regression, due to insufficient eligible studies) with Wald tests. Table 2 reports literacy-literacy r = .55*** [.39,.67], language-literacy r = .38* [.14,.59], mathematics/literacy-achievement r = .27* [.21,.32]; assessment type, validity data type, publication type, and screening grade were non-significant.

"Most of the moderators (i.e., assessment type, publication type. validity data type, and grade at screening) did not show a statistically significant moderating effect. However, the domain of screening tools was identified as a statistically significant moderator."
