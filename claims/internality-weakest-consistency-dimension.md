## Evidence

### Cutrona 1985

Cutrona, C. E., Russell, D., & Jones, R. D. (1985). Cross-Situational Consistency in Causal Attributions: Does Attributional Style Exist? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 47(5), 1043-1058. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.47.5.1043

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Squared factor loadings from the eight-factor LISREL model in Study 1 estimated variance in each ASQ item explained by the three attributional style dimensions. Internality items showed 3.8 to 13.2% (M=8.5%) explained variance, indicating the least cross-situational consistency, consistent with that subscale's low internal consistency.

> "The factor corresponding to intemality appeared to be the weakest, with only 3.8 to 13.2% (M=8.5%) of the variance in the intemality items being accounted for by this factor."
