Nelson 1955¶

Nelson, H. E. and Vandermeer, A. W. (1955). The Relative Effectiveness of Differing Commentaries in an Animated Film on Elementary Meteorology. Technical Report SDC 269-7-43. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED016911

q2 · i? · causal · r?

A four-version between-group experiment in which 291 air ROTC students viewed one of four film versions with identical visuals and different commentaries and were tested immediately. The review reports "All of the modified commentaries were consistently superior to the original commentary although these differences were not statistically significant at a high level of confidence."
