## Evidence

### de Zeeuw Eveline L. 2017

de Zeeuw Eveline L., van Beijsterveldt Catharina E. M., Ehli Erik A., de Geus Eco J. C., Boomsma Dorret I. (2017). Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder Symptoms and Low Educational Achievement: Evidence Supporting A Causal Hypothesis. Behavior Genetics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10519-017-9836-4

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Between-groups differences model in the cross-sectional 12-year-old sample comparing a no-ADHD group (N = 10,019), an ADHD group with medication (N = 349) and an ADHD group without medication (N = 534) using generalized linear models. The article reports "Cohen's D = 0.56, p < 0.001" for the medicated versus unmedicated ADHD contrast on total score.

> "The ADHD group with medication outperformed the ADHD group without medication (β = 0.57, Cohen's D = 0.56, p < 0.001) on the educational achievement test at age 12 and scored almost the same as the no-ADHD group (β = −0.23, Cohen's D = −0.26, p = 0.008)."
