Evidence¶

Gonzalez Quiroz 2018¶

Gonzalez Quiroz, A., & Garza, N. R. (2018). Focus on Student Success: Components for Effective Summer Bridge Programs. Journal of Hispanic Higher Education, 17(2), 101-111. https://doi.org/10.1177/1538192717753988

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Second-stage analysis comparing intervention and control groups using t tests and propensity-matched models with α < .05. Table 3 prints, for example, term GPA of 2.67 versus 1.68 for cohort 2014 and credit hours earned of 11.2 versus 8.07 for cohort 2015. The article reports "significantly higher pass rates" and GPA differences.

"Cohort 2014 and 2015 had significantly higher pass rates for English 1301, Math 1314, and History 1301, higher semester GPA, and greater earned credit hours."
