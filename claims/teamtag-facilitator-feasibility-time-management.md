## Evidence

### Freytag J 2021

Freytag J, Stroben F, Hautz WE, Penders D, Kämmer JE. (2021). Effects of using a cognitive aid on content and feasibility of debriefings of simulated emergencies. GMS J Med Educ 38(5):Doc95. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001491.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Facilitator ratings section (3.4) of the trial: the peer-tutor facilitators rated TeamTAG's handling, structure and helpfulness on Likert-type scales; descriptive means only, no inferential test printed for these ratings.

> "In the intervention condition, the facilitators described the TeamTAG as easy to handle (M=2.00, SD=1.16) and clearly structured (M=2.25, SD=0.96) and stated that it had helped them to conduct the debriefing (M=2.50, SD=0.58)."

### Freytag J 2021 (2)

Freytag J, Stroben F, Hautz WE, Penders D, Kämmer JE. (2021). Effects of using a cognitive aid on content and feasibility of debriefings of simulated emergencies. GMS J Med Educ 38(5):Doc95. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001491.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Facilitator time ratings in section 3.4: intervention facilitators reported enough time (M=2.50) versus undecided control facilitators (M=0.33), t(2.2)=-1.75, p=.21 — not significant; no effect size printed.

> "In the intervention group, the facilitators also reported that they had enough time to debrief (MIntervention=2.50 SDIntervention=0. 587), whereas in the control group they were undecided (Mcontrol=0.33, SDcontrol=2.08), t(2.2)=-1.75, p=.21"
