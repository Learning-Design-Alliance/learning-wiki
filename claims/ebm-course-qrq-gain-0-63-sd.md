## Evidence

### Masel J. 2015

Masel J., Humphrey P. T., Blackburn B., Levine J. A. (2015). Evidence-Based Medicine as a Tool for Undergraduate Probability and Statistics Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-04-0079

`q3 · i2 · medium effect (0.63 pretest SDs)` · `design · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA comparing precourse versus postcourse QRQ results for each student in the Spring 2014 course of 40 students. The article reports "QRQ increased by 0.63 pretest SDs (p < 0.001)"; overall pretest mean was 58.95 and posttest 65.15. The gain of 0.63 pretest SDs is a standardised change printed by the article, a medium effect. The design is a single-group pre-post comparison without a control group.

> "We were delighted that, from pretest to posttest, QRQ increased by 0.63 pretest SDs (p < 0.001; Table 4), and the ATS increased by 0.32 pretest SDs (p = 0.002; Table 5)."
