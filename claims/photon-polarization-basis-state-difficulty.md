`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Semi-structured think-aloud interviews with fifteen undergraduates and graduate students during QuILT development. Students fixated on rotatable everyday polarizers and resisted treating photon polarization states as vectors in a two-dimensional space.

> "During the interviews throughout the development of the QuILT, some students claimed that the polarization states of a photon cannot be used as basis vectors for a two state system due to the fact that a photon can have any polarization state."
