## Evidence

### Koedel 2015

Koedel, C., Mihaly, K., & Rockoff, J. E. (2015). Value-Added Modeling: A Review. Preprint, forthcoming at Economics of Education Review. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Value-Added+Modeling+A+Review+Koedel

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of specification studies comparing gain-score (coefficient on lagged score fixed at one) versus lagged-score VAMs. The review reports the gain-score restriction "appears to do more harm than good", noting lagged-score models dominate despite most studies not directly correcting for measurement error in the lagged score.

> "available evidence suggests that the gain-score specification does not perform as well as the lagged-score specification under a broad range of estimation conditions (Andrabi et al., 2011; Guarino, Reckase and Wooldridge, 2015; Kane and Staiger, 2008; McCaffrey et al., 2009)"
