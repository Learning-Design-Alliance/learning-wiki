Li Yidan, Lei Li, Jiang Ling, Mao Ping, Fu Chuhan, Chen Jing, Zeng Qinghai. (2025). The dynamic case-based learning model: conducive to enhancing the practical learning effectiveness of dermatology for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07474-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Perceptual comparison item in the teacher questionnaire (n=53 dermatologists with teaching experience); teachers rated anticipated DCBL effectiveness against LBL and PBL. No implemented comparison was performed.

> "a higher proportion of teachers believed that the DCBL model would be more effective in increasing the interest of students (88.68%), the depth of understanding (90.57%), and the level of diagnosis and treatment (92.45%) in skin diseases."
