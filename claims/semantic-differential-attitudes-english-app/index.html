Evidence¶

Whitaker 2016¶

Whitaker, J. T. (2016). Evaluating the Effectiveness of Computer Applications in Developing English Learning. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(18), 12947-12958. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Dimension-level grouping of the same semantic differential survey using Osgood's (1952) evaluative, strength, and activity factors; the activity dimension measured lowest, which the author reads as students being challenged by finding time and by exercise difficulty.

"Grouping the survey questions in terms of dimension produced evaluative (M = 2.03, SD = 1.16), strength (M = 2.04, SD = 0.97), and activity (M = 1.14, SD = 1.52) results. Activity measured the lowest mean result indicating the students were challenged by finding time to use the software and by the difficulty of the exercises."
