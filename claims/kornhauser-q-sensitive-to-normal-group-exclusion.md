# Kornhauser's reported association of Q = +.61 between parental economic standing and children's intelligence depends on excluding the normal group; including normals lowers it to about +.30

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Recomputation of Kornhauser's four classes including the normal cases with the advanced cases yields Q = +.30, versus his reported +.61 when normal cases were disregarded. [→ Toops 1918](#toops-1918)

## Evidence

### Toops 1918

Toops, H. A., and Pintker, R. (1918). Mentality and School Progress. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Mentality+and+School+Progress+Toops+Pintner

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Re-analysis of a published coefficient from Kornhauser's study of parental economic standing (telephone possession) and children's intelligence, cited as background. The article states "The size of the coefficient is materially affected by leaving out the normal group".

> "Had he included all normal cases with the advanced cases, he would haye secured a much lower coefficient, Q = + .30 for the four (now different) classes"
