## Evidence

### Chen Yingshi 2025

Chen Yingshi, Feng Rixuan, Liu Mina, Yang Yang, Liu Huan, Zheng Wei, Zhao Yang, Luo Chenling. (2025). Effect of nursing simulation teaching information system based on HIS in comprehensive training course for senior nursing undergraduates: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07015-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial during a COPD case simulation in a comprehensive nursing training course (April–June 2023); 53 experimental and 58 control questionnaires analyzed with the Mann–Whitney U test. The total score was 107.94 ± 11.05 versus 103.78 ± 9.67, "p < 0.05". Satisfaction total scores did not differ significantly (p = 0.569).

> "Table 2 shows that the experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group on the self-designed classroom teaching questionnaire (p < 0.05)."
