# The constructivist model's effects on achievement and scientific thinking did not differ by gender

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` No statistically significant differences were found on achievement or scientific thinking attributed to gender, nor for the teaching-method-by-gender interaction. [→ Qarareh 2016](#qarareh-2016)

## Evidence

### Qarareh 2016

Qarareh, A. O. (2016). The Effect of Using the Constructivist Learning Model in Teaching Science on the Achievement and Scientific Thinking of 8th Grade Students. International Education Studies, 9(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n7p178

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 68 boys and 68 girls; ANCOVA showed no significant gender effect on post achievement (p=0.314) and no significant method-by-gender interaction (p=0.502).

> "there is no statistically significant difference at (α= 0.05) for the constructivist Learning model on the achievement and scientific thinking attributed to gender, and there is no statistically significant difference at (α= 0.05) for the dual interaction between teaching method and gender"
