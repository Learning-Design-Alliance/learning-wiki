## Evidence

### James and Folorunso 2012

Ajogbeje Oke James & Alonge Micheal Folorunso. (2012). Effect of Feedback and Remediation on Students’ Achievement in Junior Secondary School Mathematics. International Education Studies, 5(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n5p153

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 240 junior secondary two students in three Nigerian schools. The feedback-only group significantly outperformed the test-only control group on the mathematics posttest, indicating that even without structured remediation, providing students with feedback on formative tests improves achievement relative to testing alone.

> "Similarly, feedback without remediation group achieved significantly better than the control group."
