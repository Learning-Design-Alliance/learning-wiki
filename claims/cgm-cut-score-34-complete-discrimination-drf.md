### Jacobsen Mads Emil 2026

Assessment of surgical skills on a novel expert-informed immersive virtual reality simulator for distal radius fracture fixation: a cross-sectional validation study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08983-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Contrasting Groups' Method applied to the plotted total-score distributions of 12 novices and 11 experienced surgeons; the intersection defined the cut-score. The study reports "no false positives (novices scoring above the standard) or false negatives".

> "Using the CGM, a discriminatory standard for the total simulator score of 34.0 points (CI 23.9 to 44.1) was identified (Fig. 2), resulting in all novices scoring below, and all experienced surgeons scoring above this threshold (Fig. 3)."
