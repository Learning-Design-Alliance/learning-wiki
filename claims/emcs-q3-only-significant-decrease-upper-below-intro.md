## Evidence

### Brundage 2023

Brundage, M. J., Maries, A., & Singh, C. (2023). Using the Energy and Momentum Conceptual Survey to investigate progression in student understanding from introductory to advanced levels. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020132)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Question-level pre/post analysis in the Results section for the upper-level cohort; the article reports Q3 as "the only question on the EMCS with a significant decrease in performance from 65% on the pre-test to 48% on the post-test" and the only question where upper-level students (48%) scored lower than introductory students (61%).
