# Learners using hyperlinks produce hypervideo products of higher quality than learners using annotations

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2023 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In a controlled lab experiment, the hyperlink condition produced hypervideo products of higher expert-rated quality than the annotation condition (d = 1.01). [→ Ruf 2023](#ruf-2023)

## Evidence

### Ruf 2023

Ruf, A., Zahn, C., Roos, A.-L., & Opwis, K. (2023). How do enhanced videos support generative learning and conceptual understanding in individuals and groups? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10275-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on expert-rated hypervideo product quality in the 3x2 lab experiment (control excluded). Hyperlinks M = 4.96 vs annotations M = 4.47, "partial η 2 = 0.216, d = 1.01". An analysis of information structuring showed a similar tool effect (d = 1.47).

> "results revealed a significant main effect for Tool-use (as expected in H2b), F(1,88) = 24.414, p < 0.01, partial η 2 = 0.216, d = 1.01, indicating that learners in the hyperlink condition produced hypervideo products of higher quality (M = 4.96, SD = 0.4) than learners in the annotation condition (M = 4.47, SD = 0.5)."
