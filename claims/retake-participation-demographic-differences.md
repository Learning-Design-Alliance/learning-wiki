## Evidence

### Supriya K. 2024

Supriya K., Bang Christofer, Ebie Jessica, Pagliarulo Christopher, Tucker Derek, Villegas Kaela, Wright Christian, Brownell Sara. (2024). Optional Exam Retakes Reduce Anxiety but may Exacerbate Score Disparities Between Students with Different Social Identities. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11440740/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of gradebook and demographic data from three introductory biology courses (749, 614, and 429 analyzed students) assessed predictors of retaking. "students who scored higher on first attempts were less likely to retake exams compared with reference groups", and participation was higher when retakes were offered in class or online.

> "In all three courses, students who scored higher on first attempts were less likely to retake exams compared with reference groups. Generally, student participation in retakes was higher in Course 2 where retakes were offered in class or online compared with Course 1 where retakes were offered outside of class time."
