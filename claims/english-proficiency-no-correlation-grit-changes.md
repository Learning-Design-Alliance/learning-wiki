Secondary correlational analysis in the 31-participant pilot study relating self-reported English proficiency (average IELTS 6.7, TOEFL 110.6) to total L2 grit change. The article reports that "English proficiency does not signifi- cantly correlate with a total of L2 grit changes"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.
