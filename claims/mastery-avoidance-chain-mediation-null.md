## Evidence

### Liu Zihan 2026

Liu Zihan, Tang Wenguang, Tang Liping, Ma Yankun. (2026). The relationship between perceived teacher support and learning engagement of Chinese adolescents: a moderated chain mediation model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03990-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test (5,000 samples, 95% CI) on the 1,193-student survey: the indirect effect through mastery avoidance goal was 0.01, 95% CI[-0.002, 0.02], a confidence interval that includes zero.

> "class environment is significantly positively related to mastery avoidance goal (β = 0.30, p < 0.001), and mastery avoidance goal is significantly positively related to learning engagement (β = 0.05, p > 0.05). The indirect effect was 0.01, 95% CI[-0.002, 0.02]."
