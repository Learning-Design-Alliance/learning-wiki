### Unwin Emily 2018

Unwin Emily, Potts Henry W. W., Dacre Jane, Elder Andrew, Woolf Katherine. (2018). Passing MRCP (UK) PACES: a cross-sectional study examining the performance of doctors by sex and country. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1178-2

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup regression of 1067 non-UK graduates registered with the GMC, mostly BME (81%), adjusted for ethnicity after centre location was dropped as non-associated. Printed result: "OR = 1.47; 95% CI = 1.11-1.94".

> "Female non-UK graduates registered with the GMC had nearly one and a half times the odds of passing PACES compared to their male counterparts (OR = 1.47; 95% CI = 1.11-1.94; P < 0.01); see Table 2."
