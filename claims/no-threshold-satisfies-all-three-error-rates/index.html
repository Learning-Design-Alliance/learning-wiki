Related Claims¶

  • After humanization with Undetectable AI, fewer than 4% of AI-labeled rewrites remain detectable (FNR > 96%) for Pangram and GPTZero — related
  • Light AI-assisted edits to human-written abstracts are flagged by commercial detectors at 64–80% (Pangram) and 38–49% (GPTZero) rates — related
  • Detector flag rates on 2023–2025 original abstracts vary sharply by domain, with non-STEM rates far above STEM — related
  • Applied to 1,163 real master's theses, Pangram flagged 529 (45.5%) as containing AI-generated content, mostly at low-to-moderate levels — related
  • On fully AI-generated papers produced with ChatGPT Deep Research, Pangram achieved 65% strict accuracy while Turnitin, GPTZero and Copyleaks classified essentially all papers as false negatives — related
