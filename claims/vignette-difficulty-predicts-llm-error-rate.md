`q2 i?` Binary logistic regression showed GPT-3.5 (Exp(B)=1.033), GPT-4 (Exp(B)=1.039), and GPT-4o (Exp(B)=1.043) were each more likely to respond incorrectly on vignettes more challenging for medical students (P<.001 for all). [→ Brenton T Bicknell 2024](#brenton-t-bicknell-2024)

## Evidence

### Brenton T Bicknell 2024

Brenton T Bicknell, Danner Butler, Sydney Whalen, James Ricks, Cory J Dixon, Abigail B Clark, Olivia Spaedy, Adam Skelton, Neel Edupuganti, Lance Dzubinski, Hudson Tate, Garrett Dyess, Brenessa Lindeman, Lisa Soleymani Lehmann. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression of question difficulty tier (1 most difficult to 5 easiest, assigned from student percent-correct) on model correctness, analyzed in SPSS 29. The article reports "a higher likelihood of responding incorrectly to vignettes that were more challenging for medical student respondents" with Exp(B) between 1.033 and 1.043, all P<.001.

> "GPT-3.5 (Exp(B)=1.033, SE=0.005, P<.001), GPT-4 (Exp(B)=1.039, SE=0.006, P<.001), and GPT-4o (Exp(B)=1.043, SE=0.008, P<.001) demonstrated a higher likelihood of responding incorrectly to vignettes that were more challenging for medical student respondents (Figure 2)."
