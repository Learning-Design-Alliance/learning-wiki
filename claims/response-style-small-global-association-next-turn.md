## Evidence

### Mohammad Fahim Abrar 2026

Mohammad Fahim Abrar, Shyala Sharmin, and Roghayeh Leila Barmaki. (2026). When LLM Tutoring Responses Work: Evidence from Student Programming Conversations. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.09919](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.09919)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational chi-square analysis of the full StudyChat interaction dataset (16,851 assistant-response interactions from 203 students) found significant associations with small Cramer's V values; the authors note the data are observational, so "we report associations ... rather than causal effects".

> "Response style was significantly associated with productive continuation, 𝜒 2(7)= 100.39,𝑝<. 001,𝑉=. 078, and with unresolved continuation, 𝜒 2(7)= 125.77,𝑝<. 001,𝑉=. 087. The Cramer's 𝑉 values indicate small effect sizes."
