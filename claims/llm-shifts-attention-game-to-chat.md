## Evidence

### Elahe Oveisi and Hemanth Manjunatha 2026

Elahe Oveisi and Hemanth Manjunatha. (2026). LLM-Mediated Human–AI Interaction in Search and Rescue: Impact of Expertise on Attentional Allocation. Accepted for publication at IEEE SMC 2026. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.19514](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.19514)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Run-level eye-tracking mixed-effects analysis of fixation time in areas of interest showed participants "spent less time on the game environment" (β=−0.18, q <0.001) "and more time on the chat interface" (β= 0.09, q <0.001) under LLM support.

> "Participants spent less time on the game environment (β=−0.18 , SE = 0.023, q <0.001 ) and more time on the chat interface ( β= 0.09 , SE = 0.016, q <0.001 ), indicating that the LLM served as a prominent supplementary information source."
