Bonferroni post hoc analysis of learning scores in the three-group quasi-experiment gave a mean difference of 0.63 between flipped classroom and lecture groups, and "no significant difference was detected between the FC and control groups".

> "However, no significant difference was detected between the FC and control groups (p > 0.05)."
