## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pregnancy-measured symptom associations attenuate to ~0 after family controls, whereas age-8 symptoms remain suggestively associated (βs ~ 0.08) with maths and English scores. [→ Demange Perline A. 2026](#demange-perline-a-2026)

## Evidence

### Demange Perline A. 2026

Demange Perline A., Nivard Michel G., Torvik Fartein A., Eilertsen Espen M., Cheesman Rosa, Nordmo Magnus, Lyngstad Torkild, Ystrom Eivind, van Bergen Elsje. (2026). No evidence of parental mental health influencing children’s academic achievement: a within-family study. European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00787-026-03100-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Children-of-siblings analysis of maternal eating disorder symptoms at two time points. Pregnancy associations "completely disappeared (β ~ 0)" after family controls, while age-8 symptoms remained suggestively associated. These age-8 estimates did not survive FDR correction.

> "In contrast, this attenuation was not observed for maternal eating disorder symptoms measured when children were 8: After controlling for shared familial factors, parental symptoms remain suggestively associated with lower maths and English test scores two years later (βs ~ 0.08)."
