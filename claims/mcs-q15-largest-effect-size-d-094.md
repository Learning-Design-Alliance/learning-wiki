## Evidence

### Ghimire 2025

Ghimire, A. and Singh, C. (2025). Using Unguided Peer Collaboration to Facilitate Early Educators' Pedagogical Development: An Example from Physics TA Training. [doi:10.3390/educsci15081038](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci15081038)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Item-level analysis of TA individual vs group performance on MCS Q15, which assesses the net force direction on a positively charged particle in crossed uniform fields. The article reports d = 0.94 (large by its d > 0.6 criterion), with incorrect answers distributed across options, which the authors suggest prompted rich discussion.

> "Initially, the individual score was the second lowest at 53%, but it rose significantly to a group score of 95%, as shown in Table 1. The effect size for this question was an impressive 0.94."
