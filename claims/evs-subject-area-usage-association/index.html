Evidence¶

Marija Cubric 2015¶

Marija Cubric, and Amanda Jefferies. (2015). The benefits and challenges of large-scale deployment of electronic voting systems: University student views from across different subject groups. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.04.004

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Chi-square analysis of survey responses across three subject groups; Cramer's V for level of summative use (0.35) and students' experience (0.33) were both above the boundary for medium effects.

"Chi-square test confirmed the existence of significant associations (all at p<0.001 level) between the subject area and the corresponding variables: (a) number of modules: χ2(6)= 77.17 (b) students’ experience: χ2(4)= 131.16, and (c) level of summative use: χ2(4)= 152.58 respectively."
