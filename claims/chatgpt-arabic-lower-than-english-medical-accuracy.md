`q2 i?` In a descriptive comparison against 16 published studies totaling 9,091 English-language questions, Arabic accuracy (43.9%) was lower than pooled English accuracy (60.7%, 95% CI: 59.7%–61.7%), p < 0.001. [→ Cohen Adiel 2026](#cohen-adiel-2026)

## Evidence

### Cohen Adiel 2026

Cohen Adiel, Eliasi Elior, Meyer Raanan, Brezinov Yoav, Levin Gabriel. (2026). Performance of ChatGPT in Israeli Arabic-language OBGYN national medical licensure exam. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08753-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square comparison of the study's Arabic dataset against a question-weighted pooled accuracy from 16 PubMed-identified English-language studies; the authors state this "does not constitute a meta-analysis" and the pooled proportion is "a broad descriptive benchmark rather than an inferential comparison".

> "in 16 published studies (total 9,091 questions) pooled descriptive accuracy of ChatGPT in English was higher 60.7% (95% CI: 59.7% – 61.7%) when compared to our Arabic-language dataset test 43.9% (95% CI: 35.1% – 52.7%) p < 0.001"
