### Wyse 2018

Wyse, S. A., & Soneral, P. A. G. (2018). “Is This Class Hard?” Defining and Analyzing Academic Rigor from a Learner’s Perspective. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0278

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Survey of students enrolled in active-learning biology courses taught under a scientific teaching paradigm asked whether this course was easy or hard and why; 47% said both, 29% hard, 24% easy. Students attributed ease to course design and difficulty to cognitive demand.

> "Just shy of half of the students (47%) recognized their active-learning course to be both easy and hard, while 29% found the course hard and 24% found the course easy."
