### Kvintova Jana 2024

Kvintova Jana, Novotny Jan Sebastian, Liu Hongyang, Vachova Lucie, Kantor Jiri. (2024). Path analysis reveals cross-country differences between Czech and Chinese university students in effect of internet and smartphone addiction, mental health, and personality traits on academic achievement in the post-pandemic era. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02069-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Czech path model: PSS negatively predicted coping with study demands (B = -0.26 [-0.32, -0.2], P < 0.001) and positively predicted internet addiction (B = 0.13). Chinese model: AASD was "independently negatively affected by stress" (B = -0.18 [-0.28, -0.08], P < 0.001). No standardised effect size is printed for this finding.

> "Perceived stress had a negative impact on coping with academic demands and a positive association with internet addiction, indicating that higher stress levels can both directly and indirectly (via addiction) impair academic performance."
