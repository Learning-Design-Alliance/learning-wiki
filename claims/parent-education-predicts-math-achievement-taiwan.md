### Cai 2018

Cai, J. (2018). Effects of Home Resources and School Environment on Eighth-Grade Mathematics Achievement in Taiwan. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1801.06049](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1801.06049)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Level-1 analysis (Model 1) of the TIMSS 2011 Taiwan sample with grand-mean centered parent education variables. Mother's education (γ = 6.86) and father's education (γ = 8.19) "are signiﬁcant predictors to students’ mathematics performance" (both p < 0.001).
