## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz 2015

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz. (2015). Attending to lifelong learning skills through guided reflection in a physics class. [doi:10.1119/1.4930083](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4930083)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Thematic coding of narrative statements across the 97 reflections, summarized in Table II. The article reports that "About half of the 77 narrative statements referred to out-of-class activities", with about a quarter referring to in-class activities.

> "About half of the 77 narrative statements referred to out-of-class activities, including weekly homework assignments, studying, and long-term projects."

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz 2015 (2)

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz. (2015). Attending to lifelong learning skills through guided reflection in a physics class. [doi:10.1119/1.4930083](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4930083)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Skill-type coding of the 50 growth and 38 action statements, broken down by coded skill in Fig. 3. The article reports that "About half of growth statements and over two thirds of action statements focused on organizational skills, most often time management."

> "About half of growth statements and over two thirds of action statements focused on organizational skills, most often time management."

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz 2015 (3)

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz. (2015). Attending to lifelong learning skills through guided reflection in a physics class. [doi:10.1119/1.4930083](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4930083)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Skill-type coding of growth and action statements; the article notes that "Resilience skills were relatively uncommon among reﬂections" and that most resilience-oriented reflections focused on self-compassion rather than perseverance or intellectual courage.

> "Resilience skills were relatively uncommon among reﬂections. No action statements involved resilience skills and only 10% of growth statements did so."
