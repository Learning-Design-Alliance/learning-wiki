## Subclaims

## Evidence

### de Vreugd 2024

de Vreugd, L., van Leeuwen, A., Jansen, R., & van der Schaaf, M. (2024). Learning Analytics Dashboard Design and Evaluation to Support Student Self-Regulation of Study Behaviour. Journal of Learning Analytics, 11(3), 249–262. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8529

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Semi-structured interviews (n=16) analyzed with reflexive thematic analysis against Kirkpatrick levels found "Reflection on study behaviour was impacted (10), mainly because new insights or awareness was acquired (8)"; e.g., one participant said it provided a better picture so she would not underestimate herself.

> "Reflection on study behaviour was impacted (10), mainly because new insights or awareness was acquired (8)."

### de Vreugd 2024 (2)

de Vreugd, L., van Leeuwen, A., Jansen, R., & van der Schaaf, M. (2024). Learning Analytics Dashboard Design and Evaluation to Support Student Self-Regulation of Study Behaviour. Journal of Learning Analytics, 11(3), 249–262. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8529

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

In the same interview study, a minority reported no impact on reflection; the article states "Some participants indicated that their reflection was not impacted (5), as they already actively reflected (5)", and some forgot the LAD or put no effort into it.

> "Some participants indicated that their reflection was not impacted (5), as they already actively reflected (5)"
