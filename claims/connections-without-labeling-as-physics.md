### Abhilash Nair 2018

Abhilash Nair, Paul Irving, and Vashti Sawtelle. (2018). Using disciplinary perspectives to refine conceptions of the "real world". arXiv:1808.10429. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.062](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.062)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

This is the authors' concluding interpretation from their case study of three interviewed students. It echoes Beverly's account of using physics reasoning in her cousin's car crash: "I just didn't deﬁne them or label them as physics." The article's goal is to complicate interpretation of unfavorable shifts in attitudinal measures.

> "Our cases provide evidence that thinkin g of physics in everyday life manifests differently in studen t experiences and that students often make these connections without valuing them, which adds a complexity to interpret-ing results from these measures."
