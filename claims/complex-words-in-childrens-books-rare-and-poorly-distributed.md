## Evidence

### Korochkina 2025

Korochkina, M. & Rastle, K. (2025). Morphology in children's books, and what it means for learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00313-6

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Frequency analysis of multimorphemic versus monomorphemic words in CYP-LEX (Table 3). In the 7–9 band, half of distinct complex words appear no more than 5 times and "only 8% of complex words are repeated at least 100 times", versus 23% of monomorphemic words at 100 or more occurrences.

> "in the 7–9 age band, half of the distinct complex words are used no more than 5 times across the 400 books included in this age band, and only 8% of complex words are repeated at least 100 times."

### Korochkina 2025 (2)

Korochkina, M. & Rastle, K. (2025). Morphology in children's books, and what it means for learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00313-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Distribution analysis across the 400 books per age band. "only 1% of multimorphemic words are encountered in at least half (≥ 200) of the books" in the 7–9 band; distribution improves with age (5% and 7% in 100+ books for 10–12 and 13+) but stays below monomorphemic words (13%, 17%, 21%).

> "In the 7–9 age band, only 1% of multimorphemic words are encountered in at least half (≥ 200) of the books, and only 3% are used in at least one quarter (≥ 100) of the books."
