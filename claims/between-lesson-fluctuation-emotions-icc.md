Becker, E. S., Hascher, T., Goetz, T., & Staub, F. C. (2025). Exploring antecedents of student teachers' emotions during instructional experiences: A situation‐specific analysis. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S133–S152. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12738

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Intraclass coefficients from intercept-only multilevel models on the 511 lesson-level diary reports. The article states "the majority of variance in all three emotion experiences was between lessons", with the highest fluctuation for enjoyment (ICCbetween = .29) and anger (ICCbetween = .30), followed by anxiety (ICCbetween = .44).
