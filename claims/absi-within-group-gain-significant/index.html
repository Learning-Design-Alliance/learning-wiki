Evidence¶

Eda Pınar Tüccaroğlu 2018¶

Eda Pınar Tüccaroğlu, Yeter Şimşekli. (2018). Effects of Argument-based Science Inquiry (ABSI) Approach Used in the Unit of "Reproduction Growth Development in Living Beings" on the Success Levels of Students. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2379-2383. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061037

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Dependent samples t-test on the 55 experimental students' pre- and post-test scores on the 20-question achievement test; the mean rose from 40.76 to 45.07 and the article reports "t (54)=0.005 p< 0.05" as significant.

"while the mean of the scores which the students in the experimental group took from the multiple-choice questions of the pre -test was found as (X experimental = 40.76), the one of the scores which they took from the multiple-choice questions of the post -test was found as (X experimental = 45.07) (t (54)=0.005 p< 0.05)."

Eda Pınar Tüccaroğlu 2018 (2)¶

Eda Pınar Tüccaroğlu, Yeter Şimşekli. (2018). Effects of Argument-based Science Inquiry (ABSI) Approach Used in the Unit of "Reproduction Growth Development in Living Beings" on the Success Levels of Students. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2379-2383. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061037

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Dependent samples t-test on the 29 control students' scores; means were 43.75 (pre) and 44.55 (post) with t(28)=0.680, p>0.05, so no significant change was detected.

"A significant difference was not found between the means of the scores obtained by the students in the control group from the multiple -choice questions of the pre -test and the post-test."
