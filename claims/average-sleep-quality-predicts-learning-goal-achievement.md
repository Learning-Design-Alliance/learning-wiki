### Flueckiger Lavinia 2014

Flueckiger Lavinia, Lieb Roselind, Meyer Andrea H., Mata Jutta. (2014). How Health Behaviors Relate to Academic Performance via Affect: An Intensive Longitudinal Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0111080

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-person multilevel structural equation model of daily data from 72 first-year psychology students over 32 days. The total effect of sleep quality on learning goal achievement was significant: "c = 11.03, SE = 1.35, p<.001". No standardized effect size was printed.

> "students with higher average sleep quality reported better learning goal achievement (c = 11.03, SE = 1.35, p<.001; see Table 2 for all results)"
