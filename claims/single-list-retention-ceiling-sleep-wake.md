## Evidence

### Drosopoulos 2007

Drosopoulos, S., Schulze, C., Fischer, S., & Born, J. (2007). Sleep's Function in the Spontaneous Recovery and Consolidation of Memories. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.136.2.169

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 1 supplementary control: sleep–single list and wake–single list groups (10 participants each) learned only the A-B list. Both showed "high retention performance at retrieval testing that did not differ between the sleep and wake conditions".

> "As for the two groups learning only one list, which was introduced to assess forgetting independent of retroactive interference, both groups showed high retention performance at retrieval testing that did not differ between the sleep and wake conditions (Figure 1B), F(1, 18) ⬍ 1"
