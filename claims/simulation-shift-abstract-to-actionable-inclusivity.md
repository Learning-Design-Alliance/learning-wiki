Thematic analysis of pre/post open-ended survey responses from 39 teacher candidates in literacy methods courses. The article reports candidates "moved from broad commitments to inclusivity in the pre-surveys to more specific instructional and relational strategies in the post-surveys."

> "Theme 1 (Evolving perspectives of inclusivity) showed that candidates moved from broad commitments to inclusivity in the pre-surveys to more specific instructional and relational strategies in the post-surveys, such as integrating inclusive literature into read aloud/SEL and proactively engaging families."
