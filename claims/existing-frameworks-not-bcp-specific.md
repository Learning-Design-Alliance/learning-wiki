Weiher Gerald Marc, Woide Mirjam Naomi, Zirenko Maria, Tremmel Michael, Stangier Ulrich, Horz Holger. (2026). Psychotherapeutic Competencies in Blended Care Psychotherapy: Development of a Competence Framework. JMIR Mental Health. https://doi.org/10.2196/100125

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The Introduction section identifies gaps in existing frameworks and reviews for digital competence in psychotherapy. The authors argue these models show difficulties including lack of theoretical grounding and lack of BCP specificity.

> "On a theoretical level, they do not discuss and are not based on already existing theoretical contributions in the field of digital competence, such as the Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge (TPACK) framework [48] or the unified theory of acceptance and use of technology (UTAUT) model [49]. This hinders integration with existing research and the insights derived from it."
