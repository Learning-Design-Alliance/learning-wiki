`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation drawn from the multinomial regression findings of the 2009-nurse survey: different predictors distinguished poor-to-general from good proficiency, motivating tiered training. This is the authors' recommendation, not a tested comparison of training designs; no effect size is printed for the pathway itself.

> "These findings support a staged GI‐POCUS training pathway: foundational training emphasizing probe manipulation and high‐quality imaging, intermediate training reinforcing the quantitative analysis and standardized interpretation, and advanced training focusing on complex image recognition and integrative judgment."
