## Evidence

### Yang K 2026

Yang K, Kanwen W, Jing C, Zidan G, Chenggong J, Xue L. (2026). A MOOC-based and high-fidelity surgical simulation-integrated clinical internship mode in optometry teaching for ophthalmology graduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09610-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Five-item teacher experience questionnaire (Cronbach's α = 0.91) covering satisfaction with resources, pedagogical autonomy, burnout, workload, and student interaction, completed by 16 trained teachers. The largest effect was for "The lecture greatly enhanced students' understanding of this topic" (d = 2.99).

> "Teachers reported significantly higher satisfaction in the blended learning group across all items (all P < 0.001), with effect sizes (Cohen's d) ranging from 1.98 to 2.99."
