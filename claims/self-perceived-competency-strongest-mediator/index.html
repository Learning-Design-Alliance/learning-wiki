Evidence¶

Hofer 2024¶

Hofer, S. I., Heine, J.-H., Besharati, S., Yip, J. C., Reinhold, F., & Brummelman, E. (2024). Self-perceptions as mechanisms of achievement inequality: evidence across 70 countries. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00211-9

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Single-mediator indirect effects from the meta-analytic multi-mediator model across 70 countries. Fear of failure showed the weakest and "self-perceived competency the strongest association"; all individual indirect effects were significant at p < 0.001.

"The standardized indirect effects of the single self-perception variables in the multi-mediator model were βa×b, SMD = 0.003 for growth mindset, βa×b, SMD = 0.001 for fear of failure, βa×b, SMD = 0.003 for sense of belonging, βa×b, SMD = 0.020 for self-perceived competency, and βa×b, SMD = −0.002 for self-eﬃcacy (all ps < 0.001)."
