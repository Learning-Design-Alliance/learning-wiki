## Evidence

### Kim Seonghwan 2026

Kim Seonghwan, Ji Man, Kim Chung Young, Yun Young In, Lee Gahye, Yoon Chang Ho, Kim Mee Kum. (2026). Comparative performance of large language models in answering cornea and cataract surgery questions for resident training. BMC Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12886-026-04767-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of four LLMs on 81 board-style multiple-choice questions (27 recall-, 27 interpretation-, 27 problem-solving-type), each question asked once per model in a new chat session with a standardized prompt. The article reports the ranking above but states "no statistically significant differences were observed among the LLMs in overall performance" (Fisher's exact test).

> "Among the four LLMs, ChatGPT-5 demonstrated the highest overall accuracy (75/81; 92.59%), followed by Gemini 3.0 Pro (73/81; 90.12%) and Claude Sonnet 4.5 (73/81; 90.12%), and ChatGPT-4o (70/81; 86.42%) (Fig. 2). However, no statistically significant differences were observed among the LLMs in overall performance."
