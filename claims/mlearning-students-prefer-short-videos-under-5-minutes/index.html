Wang 2012¶

Wang, M. & Shen, R. (2012). Message design for mobile learning: Learning theories, human cognition and design principles. British Journal of Educational Technology, 43(4), 561-575. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8535.2011.01214.x

The article reports, citing Shen et al. (2009), a survey of 3000 participants from the Online College of Shanghai Jiaotong University. Students "prefer video-based mLearning materials" with each video under 5 minutes, and also anticipated learning guides, exam help and technical support services.

"A survey on 3000 participants from the Online College of Shanghai Jiaotong University shows that students prefer video-based mLearning materials, and each video should be less than 5 minutes (Shen, Wang, Gao, Novak & Tang, 2009)."
