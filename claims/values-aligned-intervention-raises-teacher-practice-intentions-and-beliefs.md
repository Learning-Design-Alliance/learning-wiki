Hecht Cameron A., Bryan Christopher J., Yeager David S. (2023). A values-aligned intervention fosters growth mindset–supportive teaching and reduces inequality in educational outcomes. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2210704120

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Teacher-level RCT during the 2020-21 school year in dual-enrollment courses; BCF analysis of postintervention practice intentions. The printed effect size was 0.19 SD (SMD = 0.19, a small effect), with posterior probability 0.96; conventional OLS estimates were 0.26 SD for intended practices and 0.37 SD for mindset beliefs.

> "the intervention increased teachers’ intentions to use growth mindset–supportive practices in their teaching by 0.19 SD [0.04, 0.33], pr(ATE > 0) = 0.96, relative to control"
