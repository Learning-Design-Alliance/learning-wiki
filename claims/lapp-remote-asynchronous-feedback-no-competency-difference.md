# Remote asynchronous feedback through Lapp produces no significant differences in competency levels compared with in-person synchronous feedback

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants trained with remote asynchronous feedback via Lapp reached approval criteria with no significant differences in competency levels relative to participants receiving in-person synchronous feedback. [→ Ulloa G 2022](#ulloa-g-2022)

## Evidence

### Ulloa G 2022

Ulloa G, Neyem A, Escalona G, Ortiz C, Varas J. (2022). Remote asynchronous feedback for unsupervised laparoscopic training: the "Lapp" platform. ABCD Arq Bras Cir Dig. https://doi.org/10.1590/0102-672020220002e1712

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of two training groups: 130 completers with in-person feedback (group A) and 39 completers with remote asynchronous feedback via Lapp (group B), analyzed with nonparametric tests. The article reports "there were no significant differences in most modules" on approval scores.

> "When comparing the approval scores obtained by both groups, there were no significant differences in most modules."
