## Evidence

### Essakow J 2024

Essakow J, Edwell A, Smith E, Daya S. (2024). Acknowledging and Addressing Microaggressions: A Virtual Experiential Learning Approach for Faculty. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11436

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Postsurvey results from the apologizing workshop, which the article calls "The most noticeable change in pre- and postsurvey scores"; all 10 respondents also agreed they felt comfortable apologizing electronically. Percentages are as printed; no effect size is reported.

> "All 10 of the postsurvey respondents selected agree or strongly agree when asked if they felt comfortable apologizing to a trainee who had been hurt by a microaggression in person (one-on-one), nine of 10 (90%) respondents selected agree or strongly agree when asked if they felt comfortable apologizing to a trainee in public"
