## Evidence

### Duvivier RJ 2011

Duvivier RJ, van Dalen J, Muijtjens AM, Moulaert VRMP, van der Vleuten CPM, Scherpbier AJJA. (2011). The role of deliberate practice in the acquisition of clinical skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-101

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 875 students in years 1-3; ANOVA with Bonferroni correction compared subscale scores between years. The authors report planning rose over the years with "small to medium practical importance (Cohen's d)"; Table 2 prints Cohen's d = 0.33 for Year 3 vs Year 1 (difference +0.24, p < 0.05).

> "The data analysis revealed statistically significant differences between the years for two subscales: planning and repetition/revision (table 2). Scores on the planning scale show an increase in planning behaviour and organisation of work over the years. The differences were of small to medium practical importance (Cohen's d)."
