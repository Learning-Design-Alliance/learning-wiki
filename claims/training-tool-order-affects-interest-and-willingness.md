## Evidence

### Liu Canying 2024

Liu Canying, Lin Jicheng, Wu Siting, Zhu Yingting, Zou Yuxian, Zhang Qi, Li Zhidong, Zhuo Yehong, Li Yiqing. (2024). Eyesi direct ophthalmoscope simulator: an effective training tool for medical undergraduates. BMC Medical Education 24:783. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05780-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kirkpatrick level 1 questionnaire comparison of interest in further learning across pre-training, Group A after Eyesi training, and Group B after TDO training; the article prints means with SEM and P values but no effect size.

> "With regard to interest for further learning, scores of Group A (8.77 ± 0.15) were significantly higher than scores obtained before training (8.08 ± 0.14, P < 0.01) and more than scores of Group B (7.49 ± 0.24, P < 0.001) (Fig. 6A)."

### Liu Canying 2024 (2)

Liu Canying, Lin Jicheng, Wu Siting, Zhu Yingting, Zou Yuxian, Zhang Qi, Li Zhidong, Zhuo Yehong, Li Yiqing. (2024). Eyesi direct ophthalmoscope simulator: an effective training tool for medical undergraduates. BMC Medical Education 24:783. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05780-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same trial's comparison of willingness to use the direct ophthalmoscope in future clinical practice: Group B after TDO training scored significantly lower than pre-training and than Group A after Eyesi training; no effect size printed.

> "Scores of Group B about their willingness to use a direct ophthalmoscope in future clinical practice (7.00 ± 0.28) were significantly lower than that of pre-training scores (8.06 ± 0.15, P < 0.01) and that of Group A (8.23 ± 0.18, P < 0.01) (Fig. 6B)."
