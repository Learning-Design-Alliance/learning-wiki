## Evidence

### Leopold Simma 2025

Leopold Simma, Schneeberger Maurice Henri, von Felten Stefanie, Seiler Michelle, Ramantani Georgia, Bölsterli Bigna Katrin. (2025). E-Learning for Pediatric Emergency Department Staff in Point-of-Care Electroencephalogram Interpretation: Prospective Cohort Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/69395

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective cohort study at a tertiary pediatric ED; mixed-effects enhanced proportional odds regression on 87 observations from 29 finishers showed "an increase in the odds of achieving higher EEG total scores at the posttest compared with the pretest." Median total score rose from 7 (IQR 5-8) to 10 (IQR 7-11) of 12.

> "Results from the ePOLR model provide evidence for an increase in the odds of achieving higher EEG total scores at the posttest compared with the pretest (OR 24.18, 95% Wald CI 7.398-79.043; P<.001)."
