## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mathematics teachers in a collaborative VR simulation coordinated speech, gestures, and movements to plan actions with virtual objects collaboratively before interacting with the objects, a theme named discussion before embodied action. [→ Walkington 2024](#walkington-2024)

## Evidence

### Walkington 2024

Walkington, C., Nathan, M. J., Huang, W., Hunnicutt, J., & Washington, J. (2024). Multimodal analysis of interaction data from embodied education technologies. Education Tech Research Dev, 72, 2565–2584. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10254-9

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Qualitative MAET of remote teacher-learners wearing Oculus Quest goggles in a virtual classroom manipulating 3D shapes. In the cylinder volume episode, Amy gestured an increasing/decreasing action, Lily responded with a beat gesture, moved toward the cylinder, then resized it. The authors state such sequences can allow for better structuring of VR activities for collaboration.

> "We named this theme “discussion before embodied action,” and examined the conditions of its presence across the corpus (see Huang et al., in press)."
