## Evidence

### ParentCorps program description 2024

ParentCorps program description, New York University Langone Health. (2024). ParentCorps. https://parentcorps.org

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

First of two randomized controlled trials in New York City, across 10 public elementary schools and 26 pre-K classes in two high-poverty districts with 1,050 Black and Latino, low-income children. ParentCorps students "performed better on the Kaufman Test of Educational Achievement" (effect size = +0.18); reading yielded the greatest impact (effect size = +0.25).

> "At the end of kindergarten, students in ParentCorps schools performed better on the Kaufman Test of Educational Achievement (KTEA), a standardized measure covering reading, writing, and math, than students in control schools (effect size = +0.18), with the reading results yielding the greatest impact (effect size = +0.25)."
