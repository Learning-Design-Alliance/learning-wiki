`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Classification of the extracted ITS corpus through the skill continua framework of Davis et al. (2000) and Galligan et al. (2000), labeling conducted by one of the authors. The review reports "The Openness Continuum has a ratio of 90:10", 68:32 for pace, 57:23:20 for continuity, 91:5:4 for interactivity, and 72:28 for complexity.

> "There is a tendency to develop ITS for skills that are fine, closed, internally paced, discrete, individual, and simple. The Granularity Continuum has a ratio of 61:39. The Openness Continuum has a ratio of 90:10."
