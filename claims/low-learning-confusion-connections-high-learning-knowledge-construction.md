The Results section (RQ2) reports that "low-learning groups tended to make stronger connections from features associated with confusion and uncertainty (e.g., verbal confusion) to individual exploration and inactivity."

> "In general, low-learning groups tended to make stronger connections from features associated with confusion and uncertainty (e.g., verbal confusion) to individual exploration and inactivity. In contrast, high-learning groups exhibited stronger connections between features related to knowledge construction, such as verbal exchanges introducing new knowledge, modifying existing knowledge, and confirming shared understandings."
