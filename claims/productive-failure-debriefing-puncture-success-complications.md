## Evidence

### Yue Zhiying 2025

Yue Zhiying, Wu Haokun, Zhong Linjun, Li Mingxia, Zeng Mingli, Zeng Yujiao, Feng Hexiang, Zheng Rujun, Sun Yu. (2025). Application of productive failure theory combined with debriefing teaching model in peripherally inserted central catheter skill training for oncology specialist nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07863-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U / chi-square comparison of recorded clinical complications during the intervention period (n = 73 per group). Table 13 shows one-time puncture success 72 (98.63%) vs 64 (87.67%), χ2 = 6.87, P = 0.009; 7-day complications 0 (0%) vs 5 (6.85%), P = 0.058.

> "the one-time puncture success rate of oncology nurses in the intervention group during PICC operation was significantly higher than that of the control group (p < 0.05), and the incidence of complications within 24 h after catheter insertion and the incidence of complications within 7 days after maintenance were significantly lower than those of the control group (p < 0.01)"
