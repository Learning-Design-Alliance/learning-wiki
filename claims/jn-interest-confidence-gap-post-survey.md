## Evidence

### Eugenio Tufino 2024

Eugenio Tufino, Stefano Oss, and Micol Alemani. (2024). Integrating Python data analysis in an existing introductory laboratory course. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad4fcc](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad4fcc)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-survey responses (figure 5, five key questions). The authors note a discrepancy: individual survey responses "partially contrast with our analysis of students' group work" showing all groups capable, possibly reflecting underestimated self-capability or group dynamics.

> "A significant majority of students (84%) found the activities with JNs interesting, with 78% of students indicating plans to use JNs in the future and 86% plans to further deepen their skills in Python. However, we found that only 50% of the respondents felt capable of successfully using JNs for data analysis, while 29% were undecided or did not feel capable (21%)."
