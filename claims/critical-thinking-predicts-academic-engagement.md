### Su Weilin 2026

Su Weilin, Chen Chen. (2026). The mediating role of academic engagement and the moderating role of academic self-efficacy in the relationship between critical thinking and academic competence: evidence from Chinese college students. BMC Psychology, 14, 700. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04446-0

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression (Model 2, Table 3) in the three-wave study of 363 students shows the critical thinking–engagement path with β = 0.558, p < 0.001. The article grades it: "The pathway from critical thinking to academic engagement (β = 0.558, p < 0.001) exhibits a large effect".

> "the results of Model 2 show that critical thinking has a statistically significant positive impact on academic engagement (β = 0.558, p < 0.001), indicating that critical thinking of Chinese college students also has a positive direct effect on their academic engagement."
