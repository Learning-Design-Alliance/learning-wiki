Evidence¶

Randi A. Engle 2011¶

Randi A. Engle, Phi D. Nguyen, Adam Mendelson. (2011). The influence of framing on transfer: initial evidence from a tutoring experiment. Instr Sci 39:603–628. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-010-9145-2

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANOVA results across transfer-of-knowing, transfer-of-learning, and transfer-after-exposure measures in the tutoring experiment. Descriptive means favored expansive (M = 71%) over bounded (M = 59%) on transfer-of-knowing, but "there was too much variability for any effects to be detected".

"For the surface area principle there were no main or interaction effects on immediate transfer when measured in each of the three ways, with p-values ranging from .12 to 1.0."
