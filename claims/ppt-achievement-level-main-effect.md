## Evidence

### Gambari 2015

Gambari, A. I., Yusuf, H. T., & Balogun, S. A. (2015). Effectiveness of PowerPoint Presentation on Students' Cognitive Achievement in Technical Drawing. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 3(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085923

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on the 45-student PPT group using pretest scores of the three achievement levels as covariates. The article reports "F (2, 41) = 9.456, p < .05" for the ability main effect; hypothesis three was rejected. No effect size is printed.

> "The result shows that F-value of 9.456 for the main effect was significant at .05 alpha level [ F (2, 41) = 9.456, p  < .05]. This means that there is statistically significant difference in the posttest mean scores of the high, medium and low achievement levels students."
