### Fritúz Gábor 2026

Fritúz Gábor, Kovács Gergely, Kállai András, Nagy Tamás, Maár Csaba, Szvath Petra, Berczi Máté, Fábry Szabolcs, Skultéti Dalma, Iványi Zsolt, Gál János, Kovács Enikő. (2026). The efficacy of BLS training among fifth-year medical students—a randomized, assessor-blinded, parallel group trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08606-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis comparison across the three skill retention assessments in Table 5 shows safety control at 60%, 44% and 22% at two weeks, two months and one year (p = 0.037).

> "At the beginning of the BLS assessment, only 60% of participants accomplished the safety control in the first skill retention assessment, and this performance declined significantly over time."

### Fritúz Gábor 2026 (2)

Fritúz Gábor, Kovács Gergely, Kállai András, Nagy Tamás, Maár Csaba, Szvath Petra, Berczi Máté, Fábry Szabolcs, Skultéti Dalma, Iványi Zsolt, Gál János, Kovács Enikő. (2026). The efficacy of BLS training among fifth-year medical students—a randomized, assessor-blinded, parallel group trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08606-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 5 reports correct depth at 55%, 81% and 17% (p = 0.0003) and median depth 6.05 cm, 5.65 cm, 7.05 cm (p = 0.004) across the three assessments; only 17% performed acceptable depth at one year, being too deep.

> "The depth of chest compressions was also poor during the first assessment and even poorer during the third skill retention assessment; however, compressions were deeper than the expected depth, which was not shallow."
