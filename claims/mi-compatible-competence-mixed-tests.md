## Evidence

### Martín-Coca A 2025

Martín-Coca A, Martín Herrero JA. (2025). Training program on motivational interviewing for prison professionals. Revista Española de Sanidad Penitenciaria. https://doi.org/10.18176/resp.00108

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Self-assessed compatible competence in the 25-participant quasi-experimental study increased across the three moments; the printed effect size was η² = 0.079 (rendered "h2 = 0.079" in the English text), a small-to-medium effect, and the article reports "a pattern of improvement in perceptions of use of MI strategies". The result depends on which statistical test is used.

> "Although this change did not reach a level of statistical significance in the ANOVA of repeated measures (F = 2.068, p = 0.138, h2 = 0.079, observed power = 0.405), it was significant in the Friedman test (c2 = 9.621, p = 0.008)"
