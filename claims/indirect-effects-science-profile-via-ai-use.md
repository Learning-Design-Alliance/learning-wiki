## Evidence

### Gyeonggeon “Boaz” Lee 2025

Gyeonggeon “Boaz” Lee (2025). High School Science Profile Predicts Adults’ Views on the Future of AI and STS. [Version 1 – Last modified: Aug 16, 2025]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.12083](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.12083)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapping (n = 2,000) with bias-corrected .95 confidence intervals in the SEM of the KEEP II panel; Table 5 prints standardized indirect effects (e.g., science interest to STS monopoly .006*). The article concludes achievement may have affected perceptions mediated by AI use, and interest via achievement and AI use. No effect-size index beyond standardized coefficients is printed.

> "science interest and science achievement showed significantly positive indirect path coefficients on both positive and negative AI perceptions, and the three STS variables (p < .05; exceptionally, the indirect effect of science interest on STS life quality is marginally significant with p = .05)."
