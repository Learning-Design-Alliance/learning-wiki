# Students participating in community mathematics practices regarded their everyday home math as inferior to higher-status school math

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who participated in the mathematics practices of their communities while attending school felt their everyday or home math practices were inferior and that school math was of higher status. [→ Na'ilah Suad Nasir 2008](#nailah-suad-nasir-2008)
