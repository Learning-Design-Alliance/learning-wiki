### Yu Huan 2026

Yu Huan, Li Jing, Jia Yan, Zhang Jiayang, Wang Zhitao, Zou Huiru. (2026). Enhancing online teaching of “subgingival scaling and root planing” through periodontal endoscopy assistance: a study on its application in a teaching-practicing module. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' discussion of the skill assessment in the three-group quasi-experiment: the PE online group, whose theoretical lecture and teacher’s demonstration were delivered online, scored comparably to the traditional group on all criteria except calculus removal rate.

> "Since all students received in-person instruction on operative skills even though students from PE online group learn the theoretical lecture and teacher’s demonstration online, they achieved comparable scores on most assessment criteria (instrument selection, positioning, instrument grasp and finger rest, angulation, and stroke), except for calculus removal rate."
