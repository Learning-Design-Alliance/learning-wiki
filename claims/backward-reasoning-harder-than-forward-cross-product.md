### Mary Bridget Kustusch 2015

Mary Bridget Kustusch. (2015). Assessing the impact of representational and contextual problem features on student use of right-hand rules. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression analysis of 2119 coded problems from think-aloud interviews found the type of reasoning required had one of the most significant impacts on both response time (p < 0.0001) and correctness (p = 0.0047), with "problems that require reasoning backward are signiﬁcantly more diﬃcult than those that require forward reasoning".

> "As expected, problems that require reasoning backward are signiﬁcantly more diﬃcult than those that require forward reasoning."
