## Evidence

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2009

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2009). Development of quantum perspectives in modern physics. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 5, 010106 (2009). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.5.010106

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey study of three introductory physics courses (PHYS1, PHYS2, PHYS3) using CLASS statement No. 41, in which agreement "decreases significantly following instruction in classical physics (to 30%, p < 0.001)" while disagreement rose to 39% from 26%.

> "Among students starting off in PHYS1, many more will agree (40%) with this statement than disagree (26%); yet the number in agreement decreases significantly following instruction in classical physics (to 30%, p < 0.001), while an increasing number of students disagree (39%)."
