## Evidence

### Andy Schang 2023

Andy Schang, Matthew Dew, Emily M. Stump, N. G. Holmes, and Gina Passante. (2023). More or better data: A new perspective on student reasoning about measurement uncertainty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020105](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020105)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 150 advanced physics students in quantum mechanics courses at five institutions, using multiple-choice more/better data questions with open explanations across four scenarios. The discussion reports the single-value frequencies quoted here; students were overall more likely to predict a single value from better data than from more data.

> "very few students indicated the distribution would result in a single value: around 10% in the 'better data' question for the Projectile Motion experiment, fewer than 5% in the 'better data' question for the other experiments"
