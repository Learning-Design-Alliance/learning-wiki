Findings from the authors' reading of participants' critical evaluations in the Results section. "The main concern raised in all the critical evaluations" was reference veracity, and closer examination often revealed citations that "do not exist".

> "The main concern raised in all the critical evaluations pertained to the veracity of the references provided by the AI software. ChatGPT can generate citations that appear accurate, attributing to authors actively publishing in relevant areas, however, closer examination often reveals that the suggested publications and citations do not exist."
