## Evidence

### Junaid Qadir and Muhammad Salman Khan 2026

Junaid Qadir and Muhammad Salman Khan. (2026). From Individual Prompts to Collective Intelligence: Mainstreaming Generative AI in the Classroom. [doi:10.1109/educon67543.2026.11574210](https://doi.org/10.1109/educon67543.2026.11574210)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey of 140 undergraduate engineering students (68 DCCN-I, 72 S&S) reporting preferred learning modality; one 16-item closed-ended questionnaire item. "Group work + AI (GCI)" was most popular overall (50%), with "Using AI alone" at 29%.

> "“Group work + AI (GCI)” was most popular overall (50%), followed by “Using AI alone” (29%), “Studying alone” (16%), with “Group work without AI” least preferred (4%)."

### Junaid Qadir and Muhammad Salman Khan 2026 (2)

Junaid Qadir and Muhammad Salman Khan. (2026). From Individual Prompts to Collective Intelligence: Mainstreaming Generative AI in the Classroom. [doi:10.1109/educon67543.2026.11574210](https://doi.org/10.1109/educon67543.2026.11574210)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same questionnaire, course-level comparison between DCCN-I (N=68) and S&S (N=72). DCCN-I students "showed higher preference for individual AI use (44%)" than S&S students (15%), which the authors attribute to DCCN-I students' prior custom chatbot access.

> "DCCN-I stu- dents showed higher preference for individual AI use (44%) compared to S&S students (15%), while S&S students strongly favored GCI (65%) versus DCCN-I (34%)."
