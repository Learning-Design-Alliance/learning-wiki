# Simulated Angoff panels produce unbiased cut-scores: the 95% CI of panel mean cut-scores included the true score in all judge-item combinations

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across 4,900 simulated panels, the 95% CI of mean cut-scores included the true cut-score (zero) for every combination of judge and item numbers, in both rounds. [→ Shulruf Boaz 2016](#shulruf-boaz-2016)

## Evidence

### Shulruf Boaz 2016

Shulruf Boaz, Wilkinson Tim, Weller Jennifer, Jones Philip, Poole Phillippa. (2016). Insights into the Angoff method: results from a simulation study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0656-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simulation study of 4,900 Angoff panels (49 judge-item combinations × 100 simulations each) comparing panel cut-scores with the true cut-score set to zero. The authors report "included the true score (zero)" in all combinations, with no relationship between mean cut-score and panel or test size.

> "The results (Fig. 1) show that the 95 % CI of the mean cut-scores for each of the 100 panels in all judge-item combinations included the true score (zero). There was no relationship observed between the mean cut-score and the number of judges or items."
