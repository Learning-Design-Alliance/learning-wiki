The review reports, citing Tene et al., an umbrella review analyzing 28 studies of VR and AR in medical education in which the majority reported positive or increased effects, yet no statistically significant association with improvement over traditional methods emerged.

> "Furthermore, in the study by Tene et al., which analyzed 28 studies, the majority reported positive or increased effects from the use of immersive technologies (25). However, statistical analysis did not reveal a significant association with improvements in medical education and training compared to traditional educational methods"
