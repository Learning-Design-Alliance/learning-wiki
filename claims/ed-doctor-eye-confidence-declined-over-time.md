`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of three UK national telephone/online surveys (1993, 2003, 2018) using a similar questionnaire; the review reports confidence "dropped to 2.3% (8/346) of FY2s feeling “confident” in 2018".

> "In 1993 and 2003, 31.3% (60/192) and 36.1% (48/133) of SHOs respectively felt “confident” in managing ophthalmic emergencies; this dropped to 2.3% (8/346) of FY2s feeling “confident” in 2018."
