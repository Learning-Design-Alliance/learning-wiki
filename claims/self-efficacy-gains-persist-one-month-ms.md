Randomised Controlled Trial. Investigacion y Educacion en Enfermeria. https://doi.org/10.17533/udea.iee.v42n2e13

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-month follow-up measurement in the RCT, during which the researcher answered patients' questions by phone and in person at the MS Society. One-month means in Table 3 remain above pre-intervention levels in both intervention groups (e.g. overall score 45.87 TMTD vs 37.06 before).

> "it shows that the effects of training have not disappeared with the passage of time, but with the follow-up and implementation of training in the activities and daily life of the patients, self-efficacy has been improved within a month after the completion of the training sessions"
