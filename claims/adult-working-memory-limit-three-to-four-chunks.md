### What is Working Memory? An Introduction and Review 2012

What is Working Memory? An Introduction and Review. (2012). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_memory

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

Narrative review of multiple studies (Chen & Cowan; Luck & Vogel; Rouder et al., as cited) concluding that Miller's seven-chunk estimate "is a practical result that emerges on the basis of strategies that participants use"; without such strategies the retained total is "only 3 or 4 pre-existing chunks".

> "when it is not possible to use chunking or covert verbal rehearsal to help performance, adults typically can retain only 3 or 4 pre-existing chunks"
