A short survey of nine ninth-grade teachers implementing TDHS Strategic Reading in 2002-2003, reported by the review citing Balfanz et al., 2004. Teachers reported the program supported more varied activities and cooperative learning strategies, and students related academic work to the real world.

> "Nine 9th grade teachers implementing TDHS Strategic Reading in 2002-2003 responded to a short survey (Balfanz et al., 2004). Overall, teachers found the program to be positive and to have a greater impact on student reading than if they had used more traditional curricula."
