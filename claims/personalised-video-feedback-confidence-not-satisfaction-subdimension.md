## Evidence

### Gökduman Keleş Mine; Göçmez Sema 2026

Gökduman Keleş Mine; Göçmez Sema. (2026). The Effect of Personalised Feedback in Simulation on Knowledge and Skills in the Management of Bleeding During Pregnancy: A Parallel Randomized Controlled Trial. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70603

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subscale analysis of the Student Satisfaction and Self-Confidence in Learning Scale in the RCT. Confidence favoured the feedback group; satisfaction did not differ. Baseline scores were homogeneous between groups.

> "the study group demonstrated significantly higher ‘Confidence in Learning’ scores (26.6 ± 1.2) compared to the control group (21.6 ± 6.7; U = 216.0, p = 0.01). However, no statistically significant difference was observed between the groups in terms of post‐test ‘Satisfaction with Current Learning’ scores (U = 383.5, p = 0.54)."
