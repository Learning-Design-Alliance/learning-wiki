### Palmer Edward 2014

Palmer Edward, Devitt Peter. (2014). The assessment of a structured online formative assessment program: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Negative binomial GEE model of Year 2 case completion with type of case, Group and their interaction as predictors. The article reports the significant interaction (p = 0.035) and rate ratios from 0.554 (Group D) to 0.859 (Group B); no standardised effect size is printed.

> "The interaction term showed that the difference between the proportion of support and incident cases completed varied across the four groups (p = 0.035). The proportion of completed cases was higher for support cases than incidental cases in all groups, particularly in Group D."
