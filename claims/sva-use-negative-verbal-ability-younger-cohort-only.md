## Evidence

### Ziyan Xu 2022

Ziyan Xu, Xinqiao Gao, Jun Wei, Huiqin Liu, & Yu Zhang. (2022). Adolescent user behaviors on short video application, cognitive functioning and academic performance. https://www.statista.com/statistics/1095186/tiktok-us-users-age/

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Two-group SEM on the same adolescent sample, verbal ability measured by an adapted Wechsler vocabulary subtest. The article reports both negative paths are significant "of the younger cohort only".

> "Similarly, verbal ability is also negatively associated with the recommender reliance (β=-0.12, p<0.01) and daily usage (β=-0.25, p<0.001) of the younger cohort only, with significant between-group difference identified regarding daily usage, ∆χ2(∆df =1) = 3.96, p<0.05."
