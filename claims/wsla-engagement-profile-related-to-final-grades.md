## Evidence

### Sánchez Judit 2024

Sánchez Judit, Lesmes Marta, Rubio Margarita, Gal Beatriz, Tutor Antonio S. (2024). Enhancing academic performance and student engagement in health education: insights from Work Station Learning Activities (WSLA). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05478-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-squared test relating observed ICAP engagement profiles (156 classifiable students from 261 observed behaviors in the 2020–2021 cohort) to final grade categories, showing "significant differences (p = 0.012)". No effect size is printed.

> "The findings revealed significant differences (p = 0.012), suggesting a significant relationship between students' engagement and their final grades."

### Sánchez Judit 2024 (2)

Sánchez Judit, Lesmes Marta, Rubio Margarita, Gal Beatriz, Tutor Antonio S. (2024). Enhancing academic performance and student engagement in health education: insights from Work Station Learning Activities (WSLA). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05478-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive distribution of grades by engagement profile from the same observational analysis: "Among those with a passive profile, 40% achieved a “fail”"; constructive profiles were the sole recipients of 'excellent' ratings (5%).

> "Among those with a passive profile, 40% achieved a “fail”, while 50% achieved a “pass.”"
