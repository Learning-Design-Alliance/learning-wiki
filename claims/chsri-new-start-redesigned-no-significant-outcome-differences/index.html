q2 · i? · associational · r3

Comparative analysis adjusting for special education status, age relative to grade, prior mobility, race/ethnicity, and prior achievement. Raw outcomes differed substantially (e.g., freshman absences of 6.0-7.4 days at new-starts versus 21.7-22.6 at redesigned schools in 2004-06), but adjusted differences were not significant.

"while students in the new-start schools did slightly better than similar students in the redesigned schools, these outcome differences were not large enough to be statistically significant"
