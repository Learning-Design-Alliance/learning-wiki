# Some students do not realize degenerate perturbation theory should be used, or focus only on the Zeeman term and neglect the fine-structure term

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Some students asked to determine a good basis did not realize DPT should be used, and others considered only the Zeeman term while ignoring the fine-structure term. [→ Emily Marshman 2019](#emily-marshman-2019)

## Evidence

### Emily Marshman 2019

Emily Marshman, Christof Keebaugh, and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Student difficulties with the corrections to the energy spectrum of the hydrogen atom for the intermediate field Zeeman effect. arXiv:2006.05000. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.marshman](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.marshman)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

General DPT difficulties observed across five years of written responses from 64 undergraduates and 42 graduate students after traditional instruction. Others incorrectly claimed DPT was unneeded because no terms in the expectation value "blow up".

> "For example, when students were asked to determine a good basis for ﬁnding the corrections to the energies of the hydrogen atom, some students did not even realize that DPT should be used."
