Systematic content analysis of open-ended questionnaire responses from 26 of 31 participants found an initial adjustment period including awkwardness and limited facial expression, alongside perceived value as a "“no risk” forum for practicing sensitive topics" and support for reflection.

> "Content analysis of the 84% (26/31) of participants who responded yielded two overarching themes: (1) “Getting used to this type of learning” and (2) “What this type of learning can do for people.”"
