Joseph Fredrick J., Vanluchene Hanne E. R., Bervini David. (2023). Simulation training approaches in intracranial aneurysm surgery—a systematic review. Neurosurgical Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10143-023-01995-5

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's overall conclusion from synthesizing 26 studies across four simulation categories. Table 4 shows no single approach included all workflow steps such as craniotomy, subarachnoid dissection, vascular control, aneurysm exposure, clipping, and post-clipping investigation.

> "The existing training methods are heterogenous and do not realistically simulate the complete microsurgical workflow. The current simulations lack certain anatomical features and crucial surgical steps."
