# Peer feedback quality and product quality increased over time for all conditions, with no significant differences between conditions at time 2 and time 3

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` PFB and product scores increased significantly from time 1 to time 3 for all four conditions, but no significant differences were found between conditions at time 2 and time 3. [→ Gielen 2015](#gielen-2015)
`q3 i?` Product scores increased significantly from time 1 to time 3 for control (χ2=18.484, p<.001), request (χ2=7.868, p=.005), content (χ2=11.667, p<.001), and combination (χ2=6.037, p=.014) conditions. [→ Gielen 2015](#gielen-2015)

## Evidence

### Gielen 2015

Gielen, M. & De Wever, B. (2015). Scripting the role of assessor and assessee in peer assessment in a wiki environment: Impact on peer feedback quality and product improvement. Computers & Education, 88, 370-386. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.07.012

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental 2x2 factorial study of 125 first-year students in 27 wiki groups using multilevel modelling for repeated measures. The abstract reports that "the quality of both peer feedback and the final product increased for all conditions over time" with no significant between-condition differences at times 2 and 3.

> "the results revealed that the quality of both peer feedback and the final product increased for all conditions over time, but no significant differences were found between the conditions at time 2 and time 3"
