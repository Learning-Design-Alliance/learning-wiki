# MDTP scores on five of seven topics significantly predict grade 8 algebra I proficiency after controlling for grade 6 CST scale score

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Scores on five of the seven MDTP topics are significant predictors of students’ odds of achieving proficiency on the algebra I CST in grade 8, even after controlling for grade 6 CST scale score; a one percentage point increase on the integers topic is associated with a 26 percent increase in the odds. [→ Chun-Wei Huang 2016](#chun-wei-huang-2016)

## Evidence

### Chun-Wei Huang 2016

Chun-Wei Huang, Jason Snipes, Neal Finkelstein, WestEd. (2016). Using assessment data to guide math course placement of California middle school students. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression with MDTP topic mastery indicators and grade 6 CST scale score as predictors for the analytic sample; the study reports "a one percent-age point increase on MDTP topic area 6, integers, is associated with a 26 percent increase in the odds of achieving proficiency."
