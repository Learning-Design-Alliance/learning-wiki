Mariann Lemke 2024¶

Mariann Lemke, Dan Murphy, Aaron Soo Ping Chow, and Angela Acuña. (2024). Early Literacy Performance in Massachusetts: Results of Ongoing Analysis of Literacy Screening Assessments. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED663754

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of screening benchmark data by grade in 2022/23. By EOY a smaller percentage of kindergarten students were significantly below benchmark than at any other grade level, and kindergartners were least often below benchmark more than once.

"The percentage of kindergarten students identified as significantly below benchmark decreased by 21 percentage points between BOY and EOY, compared to 3 percentage points for grades 1 and 2 and no change for grade 3 students."
