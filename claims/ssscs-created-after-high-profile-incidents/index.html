McKenna 2023¶

McKenna, J. M., Russo, S., Sutherland, H., Muñoz, E., Boal, A., & Petrosino, A. (2023). Research and evaluation on school safety: The structure, services, and products of state school safety centers. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED628907

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic content analysis of director interviews representing SSSCs in 29 states. Directors mainly described shootings at Columbine and Sandy Hook and less publicized local shootings as catalysts; the report states "most SSSCs were created in response to a high-profile incident such as a school shooting or natural disaster."

"Across the 29 states, most SSSCs were created in response to a high-profile incident such as a school shooting or natural disaster."
