## Evidence

### Al-Harby 2016

Al-Harby, J. S. S. (2016). The Effect of Reciprocal-Teaching Strategy on Learning Outcomes and Attitudes of Qassim-University Students in "Islamic Culture". Journal of Education and Practice, 7(6). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1092493

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental post-test comparison of two groups of 40 students each after three weeks of teaching; the table reports a total mean of 33.72 for the experimental group versus 25.90 for the control, t-test significant with magnitude effect η² = 0.36. The article states "The magnitude effect was big which proves the effectiveness of the reciprocal-teaching strategy."
