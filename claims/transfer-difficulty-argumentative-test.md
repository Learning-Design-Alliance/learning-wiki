### O. I. González-Peña 2021

O. I. González-Peña, G. Morán-Soto, R. Rodríguez-Masegosa, and B. M. Rodríguez-Lara. (2021). Effects of a thermal inversion experiment on STEM students learning and application of damped harmonic motion. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.3390/su13020919](https://doi.org/10.3390/su13020919)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Questionnaire data showed half of students (13) stated the experiment might yield better understanding if the professor spent extra time explaining theory; "This issue was more evident for seven students that mentioned having struggles to answer the argumentative test". One student noted the exam "had nothing to do with thermal inversion".
