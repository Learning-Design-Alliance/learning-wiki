### Sazilah Salam 2014

Sazilah Salam, Siti Nurul Mahfuzah Mohamad, Norasiken Bakar, Linda Khoo Mei Sui. (2014). The Designing of Online Multiple Intelligence Tools for Lecturers at Polytechnic. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1402.0273](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1402.0273)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of UMI self-perceived MI scores and CGPA entered into SPSS-19, using Pearson's product moment correlation. Table 1 reports r = 0.678, p = 0.000 for interpersonal intelligence, described as a "significant positive correlation" of moderate strength.

> "0.678 0.000 There is a significant positive correlation between perceived interpersonal intelligence and academic achievement of the students. It can also be concluded that this correlation is moderate."
