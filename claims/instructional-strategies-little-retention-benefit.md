### Semb 1994 (2)

Semb, G. B., & Ellis, J. A. (1994). Knowledge Taught in School: What Is Remembered? Review of Educational Research, 64(2), 253-286. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543064002253

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review cites the Kulik, Kulik, and Bangert-Drowns (1990) meta-analysis of learning-for-mastery and PSI studies, which found mastery-condition performance still superior at the end of the retention interval.

> "Kulik, Kulik, and Bangert-Drowns (1990), in a meta-analysis of 11 studies that included a retention interval (including 8 of the studies in Table 1), found that academic performance was still superior at the end of the interval for students in the mastery condition."
