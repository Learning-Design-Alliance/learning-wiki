## Evidence

### Angela D. Bender 2017

Angela D. Bender, Hannah L. Filmer, Claire K. Naughtin and Paul E. Dux. (2017). Dynamic, continuous multitasking training leads to task-speciﬁc improvements but does not transfer across action selection tasks. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0015-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post battery of six action-control tasks in the randomized experiment found no group-by-session effect (all p > .15); Bayesian ANOVAs (BF12 ranging 13.76–31.83) "were preferred to session × group models", supporting the null for each task.

> "mixed-measure ANOVAs examining session (pre-training, post-training) × group (single vs. multitasking training) showed no signiﬁcant training-related differences between groups on all outcome measures ( p > .15)"
