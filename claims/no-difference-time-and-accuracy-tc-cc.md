## Evidence

### Czeskleba A. 2020

Czeskleba A., Holzhausen Y., Peters H. (2020). Clinical reasoning for acute dyspnoea: comparison between final-year medical students from discipline- and competency-based undergraduate programmes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02055-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ASCLIRE time-to-diagnosis measure (seconds from end of video presentation to diagnostic decision) in the same 60-student comparison; the difference between the two programme groups was not significant for any case.

> "While TC students needed an average of 128.7 s across all cases to decide on a diagnosis, CC students needed 136.4 s. The difference was not significant for any of the six cases (numeric details in appendix 1)."

### Czeskleba A. 2020 (2)

Czeskleba A., Holzhausen Y., Peters H. (2020). Clinical reasoning for acute dyspnoea: comparison between final-year medical students from discipline- and competency-based undergraduate programmes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02055-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ASCLIRE diagnostic accuracy (dichotomous correct/incorrect) in the same 60-student comparison; TC 82.2% vs CC 77.0%, not significant for any of the six cases.

> "Overall, the accuracy in choosing the correct diagnosis was an average of 82.2% for TC students and 77.0%. for CC students. The difference was not significant for any of the six cases (numeric details in appendix 1)."
