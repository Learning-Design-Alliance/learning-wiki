## Evidence

### Kyle M. Whitcomb 2020

Kyle M. Whitcomb, Matthew W. Guthrie, Chandralekha Singh, and Zhongzhou Chen. (2020). Toward more accurate measurement of the impact of online instructional design on students' ability to transfer physics problem-solving skills. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.15051](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.15051)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cohort analysis of 2018 students with 0-1 brief attempts, split into Pass On-Ramp Pre (RK n = 32, AM n = 32) and Pass On-Ramp Post (RK n = 57, AM n = 47) cohorts, compared to propensity-score-matched 2017 cohorts using adjusted Fisher's exact tests. The Pass On-Ramp Pre cohort was "responsible for the majority of the diﬀerences on Pre-study attempts between the 2017 and 2018 samples for the RK sequence", with significant differences on RK Tutorial Pre and Example 1 Pre.

> "the Pass On-Ramp Pre cohort is responsible for the majority of the diﬀerences on Pre-study attempts between the 2017 and 2018 samples for the RK sequence"

### Kyle M. Whitcomb 2020 (2)

Kyle M. Whitcomb, Matthew W. Guthrie, Chandralekha Singh, and Zhongzhou Chen. (2020). Toward more accurate measurement of the impact of online instructional design on students' ability to transfer physics problem-solving skills. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.15051](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.15051)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same matched-cohort analysis applied to the angular momentum sequence found that "neither co- horts showed any statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerences after p-value adjustment"; equivalence was not tested, and the Fail cohort was too small for reliable propensity score matching.

> "For the AM sequence, neither co- horts showed any statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerences after p-value adjustment."
