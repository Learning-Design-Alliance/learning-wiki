## Evidence

### Dundar Sari Makbule Buse 2026

Dundar Sari Makbule Buse, Sezer Berkant. (2026). Comparative performance evaluation of ChatGPT-4 Omni and Gemini Advanced in the Turkish Dentistry Specialization Exam. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08621-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative cross-sectional analysis of 1,504 text-based multiple-choice questions from 2012–2021 DUS exams, each submitted three times per model with a 2/3 consistency rule, scored against official ÖSYM answer keys and compared with Chi-square tests. Both models exceeded 80% accuracy; the difference was not statistically significant and equivalence was not tested.

> "No statistically significant difference was found between the accuracy performance of the two chatbots (p = 0.110), with ChatGPT-4o achieving 84% (95% CI: 82–85.7) and Gemini Advanced achieving 81.8% (95% CI: 79.8–83.7)"
