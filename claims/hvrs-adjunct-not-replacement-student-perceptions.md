Nebu P., Kamran A., Monty D., Daas H., Nazzal H. (2023). Effectiveness and Student Perceptions of Haptic Virtual Reality Simulation Training as an Instructional Tool in Pre-Clinical Paediatric Dentistry: A Pilot Pedagogical Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20054226

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Perception questionnaire completed by all 14 participants after both groups experienced HVRS; ten Likert items and four open-ended questions. Table 2 shows 6 (42.9%) disagreed and 6 (42.9%) strongly disagreed with replacement, while 78.6% agreed or strongly agreed HVRS can supplement conventional training.

> "an overwhelming majority of students (86%) disagreed or strongly disagreed with the statement that HVRS training can replace conventional pre-clinical training on typodont teeth for the pulpotomy procedure, with most suggesting that HVRS may be used as an adjunct to the conventional simulation training"
