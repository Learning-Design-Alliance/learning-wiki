`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Validation analysis of selection outcomes for programs admitting by application score (test, audition, or points). The paper reports admission followed score order as expected within priority groups, with no admitted student scoring below a non-admitted peer.

> "Among students who apply to points-based selection programs, no admitted student has a lower application score than a non- admitted student within their priority group."
