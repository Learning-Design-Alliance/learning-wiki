# Predicted arithmetic scores correlate with visuospatial working memory but show poor correlation with verbal working memory

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Predicted subtraction and multiplication scores were significantly correlated with visuospatial WM subtest scores (e.g., Spatial Recall r = 0.3100 and r = 0.3664). [→ Zhang 2024](#zhang-2024)
`q2 i?` No significant correlation was found between the subtraction model's predicted scores and verbal WM scores; the multiplication model predicted digit recall (r = 0.1932) but not listening recall. [→ Zhang 2024 (2)](#zhang-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Zhang 2024

Zhang, D., Xie, Y., Wang, L. & Zhou, K. (2024). Structural and transcriptional signatures of arithmetic abilities in children. npj Science of Learning 9, 58. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00270-6

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis of predictive-model output against AWMA-S WM subtests in the same 130 children; subtraction predictions correlated with Dot Matrix (r = 0.2178) and Spatial Recall (r = 0.3100), and multiplication predictions showed similar visuospatial correlations.

> "The results of this analysis revealed that the predicted scores of subtraction predictive models were signiﬁcantly correlated with the subtests scores in visuospatial WM (Dot Matrix: r = 0.2178, P = 0.0155, Spatial Recall: r = 0.3100, P = 0.0005)."

### Zhang 2024 (2)

Zhang, D., Xie, Y., Wang, L. & Zhou, K. (2024). Structural and transcriptional signatures of arithmetic abilities in children. npj Science of Learning 9, 58. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00270-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Verbal WM correlations were largely null: no significant correlation for the subtraction model with verbal WM, while the multiplication model correlated with digit recall (r = 0.1932, P = 0.0323) but not listening recall. No effect size was printed for the null results.

> "However, there was no signiﬁcant correlation between the predicted scores of the subtraction predictive model and the scores of verbal WM (Table 2)."
