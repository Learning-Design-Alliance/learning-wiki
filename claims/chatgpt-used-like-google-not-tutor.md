### Taylor A. W. R. 2026

Taylor A. W. R., Patel F. Y., Smyth E. K., Gay S., and Bird T. (2026). AI in the Classroom: Observing Preclinical Students' Use of ChatGPT During Case‐Based Learning at a UK Medical School. The Clinical Teacher 23, no. 2: e70360. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70360

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative ethnographic finding from field notes and focus groups in the same study; a researcher-designed tutor-style scaffolding prompt was rarely used, likely because it conflicted with students' view of ChatGPT's function.

> "Many students perceived ChatGPT as like Google, resulting in short, one‐way ‘question‐answer’ interactions. It was used to find answers to workbook questions rather than dialogic exploratory questioning."
