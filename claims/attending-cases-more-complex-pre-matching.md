In the unmatched cohort of 207 patients, the study reports "Complex AO 32-C fractures were more common among attendings (28/106, 26.4%) than residents (11/101, 10.9%)" and "Severe injury (ISS > 25) occurred in 10/106 (9.4%) attendings versus 2/101 (2.0%) residents", both statistically significant.

> "Complex AO 32-C fractures were more common among attendings (28/106, 26.4%) than residents (11/101, 10.9%) (p = 0.015). Severe injury (ISS > 25) occurred in 10/106 (9.4%) attendings versus 2/101 (2.0%) residents (p = 0.027)."
