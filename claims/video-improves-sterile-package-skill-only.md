## Evidence

### Ozsaker Esma 2025

Ozsaker Esma, Koze Burcak Sahin, Donmez Yelda Candan, Dolgun Eda, Ozbayir Turkan, Giersbergen Meryem Yavuz Van, Korkmaz Fatma Demir. (2025). An investigation of the effectiveness of two different methods in teaching surgical aseptic skills: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-02870-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Laboratory skill assessment with checklists (Table 5): opening a sterile package scored 1.88 ± 0.22 in the video group versus 1.49 ± 0.38 in the control group (p = 0.001); "the mean skill score for opening a sterile package was higher in the video-supported education group".

> "It was found that the mean skill score for opening a sterile package was higher in the video-supported education group (p < 0.05)."

### Ozsaker Esma 2025 (2)

Ozsaker Esma, Koze Burcak Sahin, Donmez Yelda Candan, Dolgun Eda, Ozbayir Turkan, Giersbergen Meryem Yavuz Van, Korkmaz Fatma Demir. (2025). An investigation of the effectiveness of two different methods in teaching surgical aseptic skills: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-02870-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same laboratory assessment (Table 5): for the other three skills the means were statistically indistinguishable between groups (e.g., surgical hand-washing 1.76 ± 0.20 vs 1.76 ± 0.19, p = 0.950); "no significant difference was found regarding the mean scores".

> "no significant difference was found regarding the mean scores of surgical hand-washing, putting on and taking off the bonnet and mask, and putting on and taking off the sterile gown and gloves (p > 0.05)."
