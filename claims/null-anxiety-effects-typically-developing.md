## Evidence

### Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa 2025

Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa. (2025). Relationships of trait anxiety, test anxiety, and academic performance of Chinese undergraduates with typical developments and high- and typical-functioning specific learning disabilities. Annals of Dyslexia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-025-00328-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis in the typically developing group (n = 134). The article reports "nonsignificant effects for both direct effect of trait anxiety on academic performance (β = − 0.05, t = − 1.14, p > 0.05)" and an indirect effect whose bootstrap interval includes zero; no effect size is printed, and equivalence was not tested.

> "The mediation analysis showed, as Fig. 1a, nonsignificant effects for both direct effect of trait anxiety on academic performance (β = − 0.05, t = − 1.14, p > 0.05) and indirect effect through test anxiety (β = − 0.02, BootLLCI = − 0.0875, BootULCI = 0.0331)."
