360 Filmmakers Challenge: Key Findings from the Fall 2016 Program 2017¶

360 Filmmakers Challenge: Key Findings from the Fall 2016 Program. (2017). Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/bf98d40e-980c-4f54-9985-46bf5675d2fa

q2 · i? · design · r2

Educator survey documentation, though educator outcomes were not a primary focus, found 82% of educators "reported increased comfort in leading projects in which students have a high degree of autonomy" and 71% reported increased ability to lead students through the video production process, including educators with little prior media production experience.
