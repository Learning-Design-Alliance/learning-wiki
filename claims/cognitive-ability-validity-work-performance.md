Narrative review summarizing the Hunter-Schmidt meta-analytic tradition in industrial-organizational psychology. The review reports that meta-analyses adjusting for measurement unreliability "resulted invalidities for general cognitive ability in the 0.4 to 0.6 range across many types of jobs", replicated in other datasets and countries.
