### Yong Oh Lee 2026

Preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1109/access.2026.3657726](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3657726)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of teacher agreement by system judgment (Table 4) in the same 79-student field study: 426 correct judgments (406 endorsed) versus 452 incorrect judgments (349 endorsed). The contrast shows the system "is highly reliable in recognizing correct solutions" but weaker on incorrect ones.

> "teachers almost always endorsed system judgments when the system labeled answers as correct (95.3% agreement). However, when the system labeled answers as incorrect, agreement dropped to 77.2%."
