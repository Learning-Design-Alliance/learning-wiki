## Evidence

### Schmidt Michelle 2024

Schmidt Michelle, Schauwinhold Michael Tobias, Loeffler Leonie Anne Kathrin, Klasen Martin, Lambert Sophie Isabelle, Sopka Saša, Vogt Lina. (2024). Safety management within the scope of teaching practical clinical skills: framing errors for cardiopulmonary resuscitation training – a multi-arm randomized controlled equivalence trial. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2024.2408458

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of the three-arm randomized equivalence trial (n = 427). The equivalence results on CD, CR, and self-confidence support the conclusion that EM instructions were not detrimental to short-term CPR performance.

> "This refutes the assumption that promoting errors as an essential part of the learning process can result in learners making more errors. Instead, our results show that in the context of medical education, performance is comparable when explicitly encouraged to try out and make errors compared to avoiding errors or receiving no specific error-related instructions."
