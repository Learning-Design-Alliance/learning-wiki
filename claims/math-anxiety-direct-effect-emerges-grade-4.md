# Math anxiety's direct effect on math performance emerges in grade 4 but not grade 3, suggesting a developmental trajectory

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Self-rated math anxiety (AMAS) did not significantly predict concurrent grade-3 math performance but directly and significantly predicted grade-4 math performance (β = −0.28, P < 0.001). [→ Pellizzoni 2021](#pellizzoni-2021)

## Evidence

### Pellizzoni 2021

Pellizzoni, S., Cargnelutti, E., Cuder, A., & Passolunghi, M.C. (2021). The interplay between math anxiety and working memory on math performance: a longitudinal study. Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nyas.14722

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal path analysis (Model 1a) in 146 children tested at start of grades 3 and 4; Table 3 reports AMAS direct effect on MAT‐4 of −0.28*** and total effect −0.30**. The authors attribute the grade-3 null to a developmental stage where "the boundaries between general and specific anxiety are still vague".

> "the effect of MA on concurrent math performance in grade 3 did not reach the threshold we set for statistical significance, but MA affected directly and significantly subsequent math performance in early grade 4."
