## Evidence

### Joshua S. Cetron 2020

Joshua S. Cetron, Andrew C. Connolly, Solomon G. Diamond, Vicki V. May, James V. Haxby and David J. M. Kraemer. (2020). Using the force: STEM knowledge and experience construct shared neural representations of engineering concepts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0065-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Informational network analysis (a variant of RSA) at fMRI run 1 in N = 31 participants correlated each group's network dissimilarity matrices with a mechanical category model (cantilevers, trusses, vertical loads). "Engineering students as a group showed a signiﬁcant peak correlation with the mechanical category representation model in a bilateral anterior vOT informational network," t(15) = 2.27, p = 0.038.

> "Engineering students as a group showed a signiﬁcant peak correlation with the mechanical category representation model in a bilateral anterior vOT informational network ( t(15) = 2.27, p = 0.038), with a nonsigni ﬁcant secondary peak in an M1 informational network ( t(15) = 1.93, p = 0.073)."
