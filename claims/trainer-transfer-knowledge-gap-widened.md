## Evidence

### Caleb D. Dodd 2013

Caleb D. Dodd, Scott Burris, Steve Fraze, David Doerfert, Abigail McCulloch. (2013). Evaluating the Effectiveness of Traditional Training Methods in Non-Traditional Training Programs for Adult Learners through a Pre-test/Post-test Comparison of Food Safety Knowledge. Journal of Agricultural Education. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2013.01018

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of managerial versus non-managerial percentage scores (Table 9) across chains and groups; the overall increase in the gap was 5.4% (control) and 2.8% (treatment). No inferential test is reported.

> "The difference of food safety knowledge scores between the managerial and non-managerial employees grew larger in every group except Chain I's control group from the pre-training to the post-training."

### Caleb D. Dodd 2013 (2)

Caleb D. Dodd, Scott Burris, Steve Fraze, David Doerfert, Abigail McCulloch. (2013). Evaluating the Effectiveness of Traditional Training Methods in Non-Traditional Training Programs for Adult Learners through a Pre-test/Post-test Comparison of Food Safety Knowledge. Journal of Agricultural Education. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2013.01018

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Conclusions section, based on the widening descriptive gap and the design in which only managers received the interventional training with expectations of passing information down.

> "Information from the intervention-al training did not appear to have been distributed from the managerial employees to the non-managerial employees in an effective manner."
