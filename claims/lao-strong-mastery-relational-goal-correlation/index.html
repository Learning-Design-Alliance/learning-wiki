Evidence¶

Anongsack Mahavong 2026¶

Anongsack Mahavong, József Balázs Fejes. (2026). How are Lao teachers' goals for teaching associated with their instructional practices? Instructional Science, 54, 6. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09756-1

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Correlational analysis of the Lao teacher sample found "a significantly stronger correlation (r =.64, p <.01)" between mastery and relational goals than Butler and Shibaz (2014, r = .38) and Wang et al. (2017, r = .17), tested via Z tests. The authors interpret that in some contexts instructional improvement may align with fostering teacher–student relationships.

"our study identified a significantly stronger correlation (r =.64, p <.01), than in both of these studies (Z = 2.23, p <.05; Z = 7.04, p <.01, respectively)."
