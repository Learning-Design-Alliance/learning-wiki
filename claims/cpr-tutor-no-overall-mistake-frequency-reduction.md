# Sessions with CPR Tutor feedback showed no significant difference in overall mistake frequency compared with control sessions without feedback

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A two-sided Kolmogorov-Smirnov test comparing mistake frequency per chest compression between the feedback and control groups found no significant difference (KS statistic 0.034, p-value 0.54). [→ Di Mitri 2022](#di-mitri-2022)

## Evidence

### Di Mitri 2022

Di Mitri, D., Schneider, J., & Drachsler, H. (2022). KeepMeInTheLoop: Real-Time Feedback with Multimodal Data. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education (2022) 32:1093–1118. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00281-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of 20 sessions from 10 participants (feedback vs control) on mistake frequency per chest compression; the KS test yielded "a KS statistic of 0.034 and a p-value of 0.54", so the null hypothesis of identical distributions could not be rejected.

> "We obtained a KS statistic of 0.034 and a p-value of 0.54, meaning we could not reject the null hypothesis."
