## Evidence

### Troost 2023

Troost, A. A., van Ham, M., & Manley, D. J. (2023). Neighbourhood effects on educational attainment. What matters more: Exposure to poverty or exposure to affluence? PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0281928

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Interaction analysis from the same cohort models, presented visually in Fig 1. The article reports that "all interaction slopes are positive" and that children with a higher educated parent are less susceptible to neighbours' influence, though the difference is less pronounced than for poverty. No effect size is printed.

> "In the model with the interactions with neighbourhood affluence, all interaction slopes are positive, although the slope of the interaction between higher parental education and neighbourhood affluence is slightly flatter."
