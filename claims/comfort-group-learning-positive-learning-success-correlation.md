## Evidence

### Lenes A 2026

Lenes A, Klasen M, Bohorquez-Mendoza G, Gecht J, Sopka S, Vogt L. (2026). Psychological determinants of successful practical teaching: personality traits, self-efficacy, and subjective perception in a hands-on clinical skills course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09788-2

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis (H3); self-assessed learning success was defined as the individual change in confidence across eight physical examination skills between pre- and post-course surveys. The study reports "a substantial positive correlation (r = .355; p < .001; n = 142)".

> "These two variables showed a substantial positive correlation (r = .355; p < .001; n = 142), indicating that feeling comfortable in group learning situations goes along with higher self-assessed learning success."
