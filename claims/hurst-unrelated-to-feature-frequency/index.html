Erica L. Snow 2016¶

Erica L. Snow, Aaron D. Likens, Laura K. Allen, & Danielle S. McNamara. (2016). Taking Control: Stealth Assessment of Deterministic Behaviors Within a Game-Based System. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0085-5

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Pearson correlations between Hurst exponents and frequency of interaction with each of the four iSTART-ME feature categories. All four correlations were non-significant; the article concludes Hurst exponents capture "how they interact with these features," not which features students choose.

"students’ Hurst exponents were not significantly related to students ’ frequency of interactions with generative practice games ( r =. 2 5 , p = .11), identification mini-games (r = −.12, p = .45), personalizable features ( r = −.06, p = .70), or achievement screen views ( r = −.24, p = .13)"
