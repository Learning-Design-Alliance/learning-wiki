## Evidence

### Blackwood Lachlan 2025

Blackwood Lachlan, Hollo Zachary, Amaratunge Lahiru. (2025). Electronic learning methods compared to traditional learning methods in anaesthesia education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07502-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

A systematic review of 13 randomised controlled trials and prospective cohort studies including 572 health practitioners. The review reports that four studies favoured e-learning, one found e-learning non-inferior, three favoured traditional learning, and five found no significant difference.

> "Four eligible studies showed a statistically significant difference in their primary outcomes favouring e-learning, while one study found e-learning non-inferior to traditional learning. Three studies found a statistically significant change in favour of traditional learning and the remaining five studies did not find a significant difference when comparing e-learning and traditional learning."
