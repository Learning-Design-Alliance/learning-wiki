## Evidence

### Bjerrum Anne Sofie 2016

Bjerrum Anne Sofie, Eika Berit, Charles Peder, Hilberg Ole. (2016). Distributed practice. The more the merrier? A randomised bronchoscopy simulation study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.30517

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the discussion, drawing on the Donovan et al. meta-analytic review and Cepeda et al., arguing the strong distributed practice effect is limited to tasks low in overall complexity but high in physical requirements. This is an interpretive claim, not a tested result of the present study.

> "Consequently, the results of our study support a previously proposed association: that the size of the distributed practice effect appears to depend on task type and task complexity as well as the length of time in between practice sessions (2, 11)."
