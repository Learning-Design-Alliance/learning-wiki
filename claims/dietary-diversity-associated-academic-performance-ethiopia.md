## Evidence

### Melaku Ermiyas Endewunet 2025

Melaku Ermiyas Endewunet, Tefera Aklile Semu. (2025). Impact of Nutrition on Academic Performance of First-cycle Primary School Children in Amhara Region, Ethiopia: A Multicenter Cross-Sectional Study. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13077153/

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study using a 24-hour FAO-based dietary diversity recall; multivariable logistic regression. Minimum dietary diversity was associated with "approximately three times better" academic performance, adjusted odds ratio AOR = 3.332915 (95% CI: 1.994–5.568, p=0.0001).

> "Students who had access to minimum dietary diversity demonstrated academic performance approximately three times better than students who did not have access for minimum dietary diversity (AOR=3.332915, 95 % CI: 1.994-5.568)."
