### Paton Lewis W. 2018

Paton Lewis W., Tiffin Paul A., Smith Daniel, Dowell Jon S., Mwandigha Lazaro M. (2018). Predictors of fitness to practise declarations in UK medical undergraduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1167-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Variance components model assessing between-university variation in declaration rates in the UKMED cohort. The article notes variation might have been introduced by differences in "culture and reporting practice between medical schools", yet observed effects were trivial.

> "The intraclass correlation for medical schools was very small (< 0.03 for conduct-related declarations and < 0.02 for health declarations) suggesting only trivial overall effects of university on declarations."
