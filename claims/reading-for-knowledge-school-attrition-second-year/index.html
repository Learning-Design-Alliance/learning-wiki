Descriptive implementation finding from the second-year design: of the 28 schools leaving the study, 18 were assigned to Reading for Knowledge, and "9 of the 18 schools" assigned to it elected not to continue, removing it from the second-cohort comparison.

"Reading for Knowledge was not included in this component because 9 of the 18 schools that had been assigned to implement Reading for Knowledge elected not to continue implementing the intervention in the second year."

Related Claims¶

  • Impacts of the curricula were not statistically significantly larger after schools had one year of experience using them (Cohort 2 fifth graders). — related
  • ReadAbout showed a positive, statistically significant impact (effect size 0.22) on social studies reading comprehension for second-cohort students taught by teachers with one prior year of experience with the curriculum. — related
  • Student subgroup impact differences were rarely statistically significant: six of 288 differences in the second cohort and eight of 360 subgroup analyses in the follow-up year. — related
  • After one school year of implementation (first cohort), the curricula produced no statistically significant positive impacts, and four statistically significant negative impacts were observed. — related
  • The four reading interventions did not improve state assessment (PSSA) scores; for fifth graders they lowered reading and mathematics scores — related
