Morvannou Adèle 2024¶

RCT with 227 nurses, nurse practitioners and doctors using an online simulation with emotionally responsive virtual patients to learn motivational interviewing strategies; the table reports a "Significant increase between pre- and post-test***" for knowledge and skills and likelihood to screen, and significant treatment-control differences (p < .001).
