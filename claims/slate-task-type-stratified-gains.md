## Evidence

### Jingzhuo Wu 2026

Jingzhuo Wu, Jiajun Zhang, Yi Liu, Leqi Zheng, Yuheng Jing, Xinyuan Zhou, Quan Yang. (2026). SLATE: Are AI-Generated Slides Educationally Effective? A Benchmark for Language Teaching Quality and Learner Knowledge Acquisition. arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.06212. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.06212](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.06212)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Task-type stratified analysis of D4 gain across the 90 units (Table 5): paradigm completion (n=37) shows the highest mean gain and "47.2% near-transfer".

> "Paradigm completion is the most teachable category ( ↑ 3.5% mean gain; 47.2% near-transfer), likely because paradigm tasks present explicit input-output mappings that natu- rally lend themselves to tabular visualization and pattern-based instruction."

### Jingzhuo Wu 2026 (2)

Jingzhuo Wu, Jiajun Zhang, Yi Liu, Leqi Zheng, Yuheng Jing, Xinyuan Zhou, Quan Yang. (2026). SLATE: Are AI-Generated Slides Educationally Effective? A Benchmark for Language Teaching Quality and Learner Knowledge Acquisition. arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.06212. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.06212](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.06212)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same stratified analysis (Table 5): translation (n=31) is the only macro category with a negative mean gain, "↓ 1.1%", attributed to contextual inference demands.

> "Translation tasks show the weakest gains ( ↓ 1.1%), consistent with their reliance on contextual inference and compositional semantics that are harder to scaffold within static visual materials."
