`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Point-biserial correlations from the same 80-student exam showed both question groups within the good discriminative range, with no statistically significant difference (p = 0.578).

> "The discriminative index of the educator‐generated MCQs, as expressed by the point‐biserial correlation, averaged 0.30 ± 0.16, while the mean value for ChatGPT‐generated questions was calculated as 0.32 ± 0.16."
