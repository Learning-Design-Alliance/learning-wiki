## Evidence

### Rami Ghannam 2021

Rami Ghannam, Cecilia Chan. (2021). Teaching Undergraduate Students to Think Like Real-World Systems Engineers: A Technology-Based Hybrid Learning Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2111.13559](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2111.13559)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Online survey administered in week 16 of the TDPS module; 68 of 320 students responded (21.25% response rate) on a 6-point Likert scale. The article reports "67% of students believed" the module was valuable, with 37% strongly agreeing; no effect size is printed.

> "Furthermore, 67% of students believed that this module was a “valuable component in the degree programme”. In fact, 37% strongly agreed that this was the case."
