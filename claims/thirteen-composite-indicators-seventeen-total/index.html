Descriptive methodological statement from the appendix introduction about the factor analyses of the 1994 teacher and student surveys. The authors report the process "yielded thirteen composite indicators," to which four stand-alone measures were added for seventeen total.

"To achieve some parsimony in representing the empirical evidence, we carried out a number of factor analyses that allowed us to combine some individual measures into factors. This process yielded thirteen composite indicators."
