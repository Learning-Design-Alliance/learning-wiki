## Evidence

### Asher Laura 2021

Asher Laura, Birhane Rahel, Teferra Solomon, Milkias Barkot, Worku Benyam, Habtamu Alehegn, Kohrt Brandon A., Hanlon Charlotte. (2021). “Like a doctor, like a brother”: Achieving competence amongst lay health workers delivering community-based rehabilitation for people with schizophrenia in Ethiopia. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246158

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of FGDs and supervisor interviews. A worker praised group discussion for generating "better ideas"; workers described job satisfaction despite low pay, stress about participants' health, and wellbeing strategies including prayer and family time.

> "The group work should continue with great momentum, because the eleven workers have different views on solving problems. We can get better ideas from our discussions"
