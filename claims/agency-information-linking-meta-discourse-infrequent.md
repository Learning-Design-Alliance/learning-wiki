## Evidence

### Fu 2016

Fu, E. L. F., van Aalst, J., & Chan, C. K. K. (2016). Toward a classification of discourse patterns in asynchronous online discussions. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 11, 441-478. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-016-9245-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding of 764 notes across three discussion views using seven discourse dimensions found that agency (7.59%), information (4.95%), linking (3.08%), and meta-discourse (1.10%) together comprised less than 10% of total discourse. The authors note these are Btheoretically important dimensions of discourse.

> "BAgency,^ Binformation,^ Blinking,^ and Bmeta-discourse^ were the least frequently occurring main codes, and together comprised less than 10 % of total discourse. This result was similar to those of van Aalst (2009)."
