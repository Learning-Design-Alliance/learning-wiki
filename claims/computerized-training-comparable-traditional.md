### Kueider Alexandra M. 2012

Kueider Alexandra M., Parisi Jeanine M., Gross Alden L., Rebok George W. (2012). Computerized Cognitive Training with Older Adults: A Systematic Review. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0040588

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review-level comparison against prior meta-analyses of traditional training. The review states its effect sizes are "comparable to or better than those reported in non-computerized cognitive training interventions," citing a meta-analysis reporting an average gain of 0.73 standard deviations for classic memory training.

> "Effect sizes reported in this systematic review are comparable to or better than those reported in non-computerized cognitive training interventions. A meta-analysis of classic memory training interventions reported an average standardized pre-post training gain of 0.73 standard deviations [63]."
