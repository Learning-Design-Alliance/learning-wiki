`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive analysis of the National Adult Cardiac Surgery Audit cohort of all adults undergoing elective or urgent isolated AVR or MV surgery between 1996 and April 2019, reporting the "primary operator" proportions by procedure. No effect size is printed for these descriptive proportions.

> "Trainees performed 13 432 isolated AVR operations (18.7%) and 2472 all MV operations (9.8%). Among MV surgery, there were 9893 MVR, including 1226 trainees being the primary operator (12.4%), and 15 292 MVr, including 1246 cases performed by trainees (8.1%)."
