## Evidence

### McEwan D. 2017

McEwan D., Ruissen G.R., Eys M.A., Zumbo B.D., Beauchamp M.R. (2017). The Effectiveness of Teamwork Training on Teamwork Behaviors and Team Performance: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Controlled Interventions. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0169604

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis (Table 3) of training method in the teamwork pool: workshop d = 0.50, simulation d = 0.78, team reviews d = 0.64 all significant; didactic education d = 0.19 (k = 4, 95% CI −0.20 to 0.57, p = .341) was not significant, which does not show it has no effect. All four methods were effective for team performance (ds = 0.41–0.69).

> "with regard to the intervention method utilized, significant effects on teamwork were found for workshop training (d = 0.50), simulation-based teamwork training (d = 0.78), and team reviews (d = 0.64) but not for didactic education (d = 0.19)."
