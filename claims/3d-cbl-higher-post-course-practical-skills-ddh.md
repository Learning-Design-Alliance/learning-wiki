## Evidence

### Feng Shuo 2024

Feng Shuo, Sun Ying-Jin, Zhu Qi-Rui, Shi Si-Feng, Zhang Yong-Shuo, Yuan Feng. (2024). Integration of 3D printing and case-based learning in clinical practice for the treatment of developmental dysplasia of the hip. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05934-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized two-group study, the practical assessment covered history taking, medical record writing, imaging reading and choosing the surgical plan, totaling 100 points. The printed result was "significantly different (t = 3.283, P = 0.001)"; no effect size is reported.

> "the practical skills scores of the CBL group (57.40 ± 8.80) and 3D + CBL group (63.42 ± 11.14) were significantly different (t = 3.283, P = 0.001)"
