Olga Viberg, Chantal Mutimukwe, Åke Grönlund. (2022). Privacy in LA Research: Understanding the Field to Improve the Practice. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3), 169–182. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7751

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A qualitative synthesis finding of the 59-article scoping review: "a conceptual unclarity in how privacy and its related concepts" are used. The review reports privacy and ethics used interchangeably in several articles and privacy conflated with anonymity and security in tool-proposing papers. No effect size is printed.

> "Overall, our findings show a conceptual unclarity in how privacy and its related concepts (e.g., ethics, anonymization, informed consent, and security) are used in the LA setting."
