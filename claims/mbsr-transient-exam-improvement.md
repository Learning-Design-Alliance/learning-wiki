Non-randomized controlled trial comparing exam scores of the voluntary MBSR intervention cohort (n = 41) with controls via Mann-Whitney U and t-tests (Fig. 3). Only a p-value (0.0014) is printed for the anatomy 2 difference; no effect size is reported.

> "shortly after completion of the intervention at T2, those who participated obtained significantly better results in the anatomy 2 exam than the control cohort as indicated by a p-value of 0.0014. However, these differences were of short term and no longer detectable at T3"
