## Evidence

### Bosma et al. 2017

Tirza Bosma, Claire E. Stevenson, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2017). Differences in Need for Instruction: Dynamic Testing in Children with Arithmetic Difficulties. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2326

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Two-step cluster analysis (Ward's linkage then k-means) on standardized prompts per scenario-protocol for 59 trained children, followed by a MANOVA confirming profile differences with printed effect size partial η² = .061. "Hence, we categorized the clusters as (1) General prompts, (2) Measuring prompts, (3) Accuracy prompts and (4) Organizing prompts."

> "The results revealed, as expected (3), that children in the four clusters significantly differed in prompts provided per scenario-protocol: Wilks'Λ = .057, F(18, 141) = 13.76, p < .001, partial η² =. 061."
