## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In meta-analysis of trials assessing time to completion, trainees who used simulation completed anastomoses a mean of 205.9 seconds faster than before simulation. [→ O'Dwyer M 2022](#odwyer-m-2022)

## Evidence

### O'Dwyer M 2022

O'Dwyer M, Fleming CA, Ahern S, Barrett S, Raftery NB, Ní Dhonnchú T, Doddakula K. (2022). Objective improvement with coronary anastomosis simulation training: meta-analysis. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrab147

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of six studies (eleven groups) comparing time to completion before versus after simulation, performed in RevMan 5.4. The pooled mean difference was "205.9 s" with P < 0.001; individual effect sizes ranged from 64 to 443 s and heterogeneity was I2 = 77 per cent.

> "A mean difference of 205.9 s was observed (95 per cent c.i. 133.52 to 278.18; P < 0.001) in trainees who used simulation (Fig. 3)."
