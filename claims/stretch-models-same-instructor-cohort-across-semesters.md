# Faculty report stretch models are widely seen as effective for developmental writing and function best when the same instructor and student cohort persist across semesters

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bracco 2019

Bracco, K. R., Schrager, C., Calisi, G., Gutierrez, P., Salciccioli, M., & Finkelstein, N. (2019). College-ready in the California State University system: Campus experiences implementing EO 1110. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.calstate.edu/eo/EO-1110.html

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview and focus-group findings from nine CSU campuses on stretch course implementation. Faculty perception, not a measured comparison: the report says "most faculty reported that these models function best" with a stable instructor-cohort pairing.

> "most faculty reported that these models function best when they maintain the same instructor and student cohort across semesters"

### Bracco 2019 (2)

Bracco, K. R., Schrager, C., Calisi, G., Gutierrez, P., Salciccioli, M., & Finkelstein, N. (2019). College-ready in the California State University system: Campus experiences implementing EO 1110. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.calstate.edu/eo/EO-1110.html

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative interview finding on WC stretch courses, seven of nine campuses having implemented them before EO 1110. This is faculty-reported perception of effectiveness; the report presents no student outcome data for stretch models.

> "Faculty reported that stretch is widely seen as an effective model for developmental writing, allowing students to practice skills iteratively through writing and revision over an extended time period"
