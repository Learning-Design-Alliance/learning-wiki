# School type is significantly correlated with the learning methods used: active methods cluster in the private school and the dogmatic method in the three public schools

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Spearman correlations between school variation and method use were significant for active dialogue (.187), discovery method (.350), student presentations (.154), demonstrative method (.247), demonstrative by ICTE (.477), lab experiments (.245) and school outings (.402); the dogmatic method correlated negatively (-.122) and the interrogative method showed no significant difference. ## Evidence

### Bouchta El Batri 2019

Bouchta El Batri, Anouar Alami, Moncef Zaki, Youssef Nafidi, Driss Chenfour. (2019). Promotion of the Environmental Knowledge and Behavior through the Moroccan Syllabus of Sciences in the Middle School. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 11(4), 371-381. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2019450795

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Spearman correlations across the 400-student four-school comparative sample (Table 6, N=400); printed coefficients include r = .477 for demonstrative by ICTE and r = .402 for school outings, both large effect sizes significant at the .01 level, ranging down to -.122 (dogmatic). The interrogative method was "the only one that has not shown any significant difference in its use among the schools studied."

> "the methods that are used with very significant differences between the schools studied are active dialogue, discovery method, student presentations, demonstrative methods, laboratory experiments and School outings. These are all active methods associated primarily with private school and to a lesser extent with the school located in downtown."
