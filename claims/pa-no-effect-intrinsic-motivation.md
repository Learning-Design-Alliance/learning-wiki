# Pedagogical agents show essentially no effect on learners' intrinsic motivation (g = 0.00)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gladstone 2025

Gladstone, J. R., Schroeder, N. L., Heidig, S., Zhang, S., Palaguachi, C., & Pitera, M. (2025). Do Pedagogical Agents Enhance Student Motivation? Unraveling the Evidence Through Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10050-2

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Three-level meta-analysis with CHERVE of PA-versus-non-PA comparisons measuring intrinsic motivation. The pooled effect was effectively zero, with significant heterogeneity (Q(22) = 56.97, p < 0.001).

> "The random effects 3LMA with CHERVE examining the effects of PAs on learners' intrinsic motivation showed that PAs have essentially no influence (g = 0.00, p = 0.99, kcomparisons = 23, kstudies = 3)."
