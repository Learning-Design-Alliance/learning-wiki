## Evidence

### Abdellah Ibrahim Mohammed Elfeky & Thouqan Saleem Yakoub Masadeh 2016

Abdellah Ibrahim Mohammed Elfeky & Thouqan Saleem Yakoub Masadeh. (2016). The Effect of Mobile Learning on Students' Achievement and Conversational Skills. International Journal of Higher Education, Vol. 5, No. 3. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n3p20

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same 50-student quasi-experiment; conversational skills were rated with Spitzberg's CSRS (25 communicative behaviors plus overall performance ratings, total converted to 20). The article reports a statistically significant difference (α=0.05) in favor of the mobile-learning group, with means of "(M=12.92)" versus "(M=16.16)".

> "the T. value for the difference between participants' modified gain ratio in the control group (M=12.92) and the experimental one (M=16.16) regarding the Conversational Rating Scale was (5.71)."
