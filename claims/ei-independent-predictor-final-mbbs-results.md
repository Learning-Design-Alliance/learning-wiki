### Wijekoon Chandrani Nirmala 2017

Wijekoon Chandrani Nirmala, Amaratunge Heshan, de Silva Yashica, Senanayake Solith, Jayawardane Pradeepa, Senarath Upul. (2017). Emotional intelligence and academic performance of medical undergraduates: a cross-sectional study in a selected university in Sri Lanka. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1018-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 130 final-year medical undergraduates who sat the final MBBS examination in 2016; EI measured with the self-administered Genos EI inventory. Ordinal regression over four results categories showed "total EI was an independent predictor of academic performance at final MBBS examination".

> "Ordinal regression analysis indicated that, after adjusting for gender, total EI was an independent predictor of academic performance at final MBBS examination (β - 0.018 (95% CI 0.005-0.031); p = 0.006)"
