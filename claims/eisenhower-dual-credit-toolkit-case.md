# At Eisenhower High School, a data-informed partnership with Washington STEM coincided with CTE DC enrollment rising from 15% to 49% and advanced coursework completion rising from 28% to 63%

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` After implementing the Equitable Dual Credit Toolkit changes in 2021, Eisenhower's CTE DC enrollment jumped from 15% in 2019 to 49% in 2022, with the same percentage for Latino students, and completion of any advanced coursework rose from 28% to 63%. ## Evidence

### Kristen Hengtgen 2023

Kristen Hengtgen, Hector Biaggi. (2023). Increasing Access to Advanced Coursework. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641932

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-school case study at Eisenhower High School in Yakima County, serving a largely Latino, low-income student body. Washington STEM gathered data, administered student and staff surveys, created an Equitable Dual Credit Toolkit, and the school added student panels and teacher professional development; the brief calls the 2021-22 data "promising."

> "Data from the 2021-22 school year is promising: Enrollment in CTE DC courses jumped for all students, from 15% in 2019 to 49% in 2022, with the same percentages for Latino students. Rates for students completing any advanced coursework class also increased from 28% in 2019 to 63% in 2022."
