## Evidence

### Marshman 2015

Marshman, E. and Singh, C. (2015). A Framework for Understanding the Patterns of Student Difficulties in Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Investigation by Lin and Singh in which professors and students from two junior/senior level quantum mechanics courses categorized twenty problems by similarity of solutions after instruction. The article reports that 'Many students categorized quantum mechanics problems based on the surface features of the problems' and that student scores were 'more or less evenly distributed.'

> "Professors' categorizations were overall rated higher than those of students by three faculty members who evaluated all of the categorizations blindly (without the knowledge of whether the categories were created by the professors or students)."
