A Case for Increasing Empirical Attention to Head Start's Home-Based Program: An Exploration of Routine Collaborative Goal Setting. q2 · i? · associational · r1

Bifurcated-sample comparisons (n = 42 with goals, n = 31 without) using chi-square and discriminant analysis found no significant differences on caregiver relationship, home language, ethnicity, education, employment, family type, siblings, or child age; only child gender differed, χ2(1) = 9.95, p = 0.002.

"Gender differences emerged between the two groups; a higher proportion of girls were in the subsample of families who had identified goals (χ2(1) = 9.95, p = 0.002)."
