## Evidence

### Lock Nicole E. 2024

Lock Nicole E., DeBoer Mark D., Scharf Rebecca J., Miller Sarah E. (2024). Academic performance in moderately and late preterm children in the United States: are they catching up? Journal of Perinatology 44(6). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-024-01938-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariable linear regression on direct cognitive assessments in the nationally representative, weighted ECLS-K:2011 cohort (422 children born 32-34 6/7 weeks; 14350 term children), showing "significantly lower scores compared to term children" in the named subjects and grades; no effect size printed.

> "Children born 32–34 6/7 weeks’ had significantly lower scores compared to term children for reading in kindergarten, for science in fifth grade, and for mathematics in kindergarten, second grade, and fourth grade."
