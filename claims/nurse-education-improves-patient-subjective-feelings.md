### Lee Yoo Jin MD; Kim Eun Soo MD 2015

Lee Yoo Jin MD; Kim Eun Soo MD, PhD; Park Kyung Sik MD; Cho Kwang Bum MD, PhD; Jang Byoung Kuk MD, PhD; Chung Woo Jin MD, PhD; Hwang Jae Seok MD, PhD; Somchai Amornyotin. (2015). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Patient interviews before colonoscopy in the controlled trial: "sleep quality" worse in 20.4% educated vs 44.1% control (P < 0.001); anxiety lower (P < 0.001) and satisfaction higher (P = 0.001) in the educated ward.

> "Only 20.4% of patients in the educated ward responded that their sleep quality was worse than usual, whereas 44.1% of patients in the control ward did (P < 0.001). The reported anxiety was lower in patients in the educated ward than in control (P < 0.001)."
