What Works Clearinghouse 2016¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse intervention report: University of Chicago School Mathematics Project (UCSMP). What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/641

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Hirschhorn (1993), a 4-year quasi-experiment matching UCSMP students to comparison students on sixth-grade scores in two sites; the WWC pooled both sites and, after adjusting for multiple comparisons, characterized the finding as a statistically significant positive effect with improvement index "+23".

"In addition, Hirschhorn (1993) found statistically significant positive effects in each site on the UCSMP-developed Applications Test, and the WWC confirmed this finding, pooling the results from both sites."
