## Evidence

### Lecce 2026

Lecce, S., Stagnitto, S. M., Mascheretti, S., Lampis, V., & Devine, R. T. (2026). Children's Theory of Mind and Academic Achievement: a Meta-analysis on Reading Comprehension and Math Skills. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10185-w

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Weighted linear regression moderator analysis across cross-sectional effect sizes found region was the only significant moderator, F(3,29) = 5.42, p = .004; European samples showed weaker associations than Asian and North American samples. The printed β values are unstandardised regression estimates, so no standardised effect size is reported here.

> "The association between ToM and academic achievement was significantly lower for studies based in Europe compared with Asia and North America, tEurope-Asia = 2.48, β = .13, p = 0.02; tEurope-NorthAmerica = 3.64, β = 0.19, p < 0.001, and tending to significance compared to Oceania, ZEurope-Oceania = -0.16, p = 0.07."
