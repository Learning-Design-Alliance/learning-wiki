Publication-bias assessment for the meta-analysis of household economic and educational disadvantage on selection, using a funnel plot and Egger's regression test (performed for meta-analyses with k≥10). The test was not feasible for other outcomes with fewer effect sizes.

> "Egger’s test confirmed no significant asymmetry (z=0.075, P=0.940), indicating no statistical evidence of publication bias."
