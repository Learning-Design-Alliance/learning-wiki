## Evidence

### Freytag Julia 2019

Freytag Julia, Stroben Fabian, Hautz Wolf E., Schauber Stefan K., Kämmer Juliane E. (2019). Rating the quality of teamwork—a comparison of novice and expert ratings using the Team Emergency Assessment Measure (TEAM) in simulated emergencies. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-019-0591-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-level Mann–Whitney U tests comparing 6 novice and 6 expert raters across 84 observations of 7 teams in 6 simulated emergency cases. The article reports no significant difference on 7 items (p = .06–.86) and significantly higher novice ratings on 4 items (p = .04–.004).

> "On 7 of 11 items, no statistically significant difference was found (items 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11; p = .06–.86). However, on 4 of 11 items novices rated teamwork behaviour higher than experts on average (items 2, 3, 8, 10; p = .04–.004)."

### Freytag Julia 2019 (2)

Freytag Julia, Stroben Fabian, Hautz Wolf E., Schauber Stefan K., Kämmer Juliane E. (2019). Rating the quality of teamwork—a comparison of novice and expert ratings using the Team Emergency Assessment Measure (TEAM) in simulated emergencies. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-019-0591-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

t tests on sum score and GRS across 84 observations: sum scores did not differ significantly (t(82) = 1.8, p = .08), but novices rated the GRS about 1 point higher, a significant difference (t(82) = 2.5, p = .02).

> "across cases, we found no statistically significant difference between the TEAM sum scores for experts and novices (Mnovice = 30.4, SDnovice = 8.6, Mexpert = 27.0, SDexpert = 8.4; t(82) = 1.8, p = .08). Finally, for the GRS, we found that novices (Mnovice = 7.1, SDnovice = 1.6) gave generally higher ratings as compared to experts (Mexpert = 6.1, SDexpert = 1.9). This difference was statistically significant with t(82) = 2.5 and p = .02."
