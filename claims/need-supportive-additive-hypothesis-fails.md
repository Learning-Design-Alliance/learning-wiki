# Jointly considered, all three need-supportive practices are not each associated with classroom-level engagement (additive hypothesis not supported)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` When autonomy support, structure, and involvement are jointly considered, not all three practices are associated with classroom-level engagement. [→ Olivier 2021](#olivier-2021)

## Evidence

### Olivier 2021

Olivier, E., Galand, B., Morin, A., & Hospel, V. (2021). Need-supportive teaching and student engagement in the classroom: Comparing the additive, synergistic, and global contributions. Learning and Instruction, 71, 101389. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101389

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Multilevel analyses among 1,193 8th grade Belgian students nested in 57 math classrooms tested the additive hypothesis; the study reports that results "failed to support the additive hypothesis (H1)". No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "Results failed to support the additive hypothesis (H1), which anticipated that all three practices would be associated with classroom-levels of engagement when jointly considered."
