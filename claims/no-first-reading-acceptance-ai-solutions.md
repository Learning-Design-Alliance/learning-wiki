### Álvaro Suárez 2026

Álvaro Suárez, Richard González. (2026). Students’ Validation Criteria for AI-Generated Physics Solutions. The Physics Teacher (accepted for publication). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.16830](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.16830)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Classroom activity with 20 pre-service physics teachers in a calculus-based electromagnetism course. After one reading of an AI solution, "no student accept ed it as valid after that first reading"; 16 leaned toward valid with checks needed and 4 did not accept (Table 1).

> "After reading the AI -generated solution, no student accept ed it as valid after that first reading, as shown in Table 1. Sixteen students leaned toward considering it valid but still needed to check one or two specific aspects, while four did not accept it as valid."
