Hypothesis test in the same 468-student survey mediation analysis. The article reports PAD "was not directly related to use frequency (β = 0.04, p =.22) or intention to use ChatGTP (β = − 0.05, p =.33)"; no effect size is printed, so the null is not evidence of equivalence.

> "Second, PAD was not directly related to use frequency (β = 0.04, p =.22) or intention to use ChatGTP (β = − 0.05, p =.33), thus rejecting H2a and H2b."
