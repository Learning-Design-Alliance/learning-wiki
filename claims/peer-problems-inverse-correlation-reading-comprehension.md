Correlational analysis in the same after-school sample relating SDQ peer problems to GORT-4 comprehension; the printed correlation is "r = -.304, p<.01", a small-to-moderate inverse association.

> "Pearson‟s product correlation identified a significant inverse relationship between peer problems and reading comprehension as scored by the GORT -4 (r = -.304, p<.01). Cumulative results indicate that as peer problems decrease reading comprehension increases."
