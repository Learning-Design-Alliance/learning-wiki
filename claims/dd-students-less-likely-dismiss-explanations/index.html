q2 · i? · causal · r2

Thematic analysis of responses to the question asking students to reflect on whether and how they used the explanations, coded into Strategic Use, Conceptual Support, and Dismissed categories; Strategic Use was mentioned by over 75% of students in each group, and no differences reached statistical significance.

"DD students were less likely to dismiss explanations (resid = –1.21), while GG students were more likely to do so (resid = +0.72) and less likely to mention conceptual support (resid = –0.80)."
