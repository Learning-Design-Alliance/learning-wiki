### Drost RMWA 2016

Drost RMWA, Paulus ATG, Jander AF, Mercken L, de Vries H, Ruwaard D, Evers SMAA. (2016). A Web-Based Computer-Tailored Alcohol Prevention Program for Adolescents: Cost-Effectiveness and Intersectoral Costs and Benefits. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.5223

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup cost-effectiveness bootstrap analyses of the Alcoholic Alert cluster RCT based on background variables measured at T0. For older adolescents (≥17 years) and low educational level subgroups, ICERs were labeled "Dominant" in several analyses, indicating the intervention was both more effective and less costly than CAU.

> "Subgroup analyses showed, from both the health care and the societal perspective, and for both outcome measures, that the intervention was cost-effective for the older adolescents and those at a lower educational level (Tables 4and 5)."
