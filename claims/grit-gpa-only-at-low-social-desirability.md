## Evidence

### Kilgore Jenna 2023

Kilgore Jenna, Collins Amanda C., Miller Julie Anne M., Winer E. Samuel. (2023). Grit, depression, social desirability, and GPA. PLoS One 18(7): e0288270. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0288270

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Supplemental moderation analysis reported in the Discussion of the same cross-sectional study. The article reports grit was "only significantly related to GPA at low levels of social desirability". No effect size or test statistic was printed for this probe.

> "However, we probed the moderation and found that grit was only significantly related to GPA at low levels of social desirability (and not high levels of social desirability), thus supporting prior research indicating that social desirability may moderate the validity of grit when assessed via self-report measures"
