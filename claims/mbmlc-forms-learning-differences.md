### Inge Molenaar 2021

Inge Molenaar, Anne Horvers, Ryan S. Baker. (2021). What can moment-by-moment learning curves tell about students' self-regulated learning? Learning and Instruction, 72, 101206. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.05.003

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Paired samples t-test comparing pre-test (M = 8.53) and post-test (M = 16.66) in the pre-test/post-test design with 98 participating students. The article reports "students learned t(85) = 12.54, p < 0.001, r = 0.35, which is a medium ef- fect" during the four lessons.

### Inge Molenaar 2021 (2)

Inge Molenaar, Anne Horvers, Ryan S. Baker. (2021). What can moment-by-moment learning curves tell about students' self-regulated learning? Learning and Instruction, 72, 101206. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.05.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed models on pre-test, post-test and gain by curve form (Tables 11-13, 265 graphs, 92 students). Immediate drop had higher pre-test scores than all other curves (e.g. versus immediate peak t(236.29) = 13.51) and, because of its high pre-test, significantly lower gain than all other curves.

> "Immediate peak showed significantly higher gain compared to all other curves (close multiple spikes t(204.41) = -5.16, p < 0.001, separate multiple spikes t(224.45) = -4.34, p < 0.001)."
