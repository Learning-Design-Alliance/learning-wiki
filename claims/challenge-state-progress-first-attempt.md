## Evidence

### Cassidy 2026

Cassidy, S. (2026). The Academic Threat Appraisal Ratio Scale (ATARS): Insights into attainment, academic progression, and retention in higher education. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 90–107. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12780

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Questionnaire survey of 186 first-year undergraduates with attainment data retrieved from student records. Challenge-state students progressed at first attempt at 76.5% versus 60.8% for threat-state students; the "χ 2 (1) = 4.445, p = .035" association was significant.

> "A challenge state was associated with a greater likelihood of students progressing at first attempt (76.5%), whilst a threat state was associated with a greater likelihood of students failing to progress at first attempt (70.4%) (χ 2 (1) = 4.445, p = .035)."
