HRI '23: ACM/IEEE International Conference on Human-Robot Interaction. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2303.03956](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2303.03956)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Statistical analysis of pretest-posttest Engineering Attitudes Survey answers from 9 participants using the nonparametric Wilcoxon signed-rank test, chosen because of the small sample and no assumptions about data distributions. The article reports "no statistically significant in the increase of attitudes towards engineering (t=53.5, p= 0.45)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The average survey before the test was lower (𝜇 = 2.194500±𝜎 0.558367 ) compared to the posttest results (𝜇 = 2.239500±𝜎= 0.396796). There was no statistically significant in the increase of attitudes towards engineering (t=53.5, p= 0.45)."
