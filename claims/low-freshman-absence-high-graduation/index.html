Freshman Year: The Make-it or Break-it Year 2007¶

Freshman Year: The Make-it or Break-it Year. (2007). Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. http://ccsr.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

An observational analysis of Chicago Public Schools attendance and graduation records for the 2001 entering cohort reports that "Nearly 90 percent of freshmen who miss less than a week of school per semester graduate, regardless of their 8th grade test scores." A companion figure plots four-year graduation rates by days absent per semester, declining from 87 percent to near zero at 40-plus days.
