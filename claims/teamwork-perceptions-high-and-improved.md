## Evidence

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same one-group pretest-posttest student survey. Table 3 shows e.g. "Most students were attentive during TBL sessions" rising from 2.87 (1.20) to 3.81 (0.95), p < 0.0001, and "There was mutual respect for other teammates' viewpoints during TBL" from 4.04 (0.82) to 4.53 (0.58), p < 0.001.

> "In the Teamwork category, all relevant items were significantly increased in the post TBL interventions."
