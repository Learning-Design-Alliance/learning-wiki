### Maike Schindler 2017 (2)

Maike Schindler, Stephan Hußmann, Per Nilsson, Arthur Bakker. (2017). Sixth-grade students' reasoning on the order relation of integers as influenced by prior experience: an inferentialist analysis. Math Ed Res J 29:471–492. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13394-017-0202-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The authors' discussion of Nicole's case states the subtraction strategy is novel to this research area, while connecting it to Bofferding's (2014) finding that students apply whole-number subtraction strategies to negative numbers.

> "Such a strategy has — to our knowledge— not yet been reported in empirical research on the order relation for negative integers."
