## Evidence

### Zarshenas Ladan 2019

Zarshenas Ladan, Jahromi Leila Ashrafean, Jahromi Mohsen Faseleh, Manshadi Marieh Dehghan. (2019). Self–handicapping among nursing students: an interventional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1441-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-group pretest–posttest interventional study of 90 nursing students randomly assigned to intervention (n=45) or control (n=45), analyzed with repeated measures ANOVA. The article reports "a significant decrease in the rate of self-handicapping" in the intervention group only; no effect size is printed.

> "The results of repeated measures ANOVA showed that teaching problem solving skills in the intervention group led to a significant decrease in the rate of self-handicapping after the intervention (p < 0.001). However, no significant change was observed in the mean score of self-handicapping in the control group (p = 0.575)."
