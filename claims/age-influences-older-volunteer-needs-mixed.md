## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Age significantly influenced overall needs (F = 4.61, p = .010) and service awareness needs (F = 5.65, p = .004), with the youngest and oldest groups reporting more awareness needs than the middle group. [→ Yang 2025](#yang-2025)
`q3 i?` For service skills and interpersonal interaction, initial age differences (F = 3.56, p = .029; F = 3.53, p = .030) were not confirmed in post hoc comparison between the three age groups. [→ Yang 2025 (2)](#yang-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Yang 2025

Yang, L.-C., LAI, C.-Y., Chen, K.-M., Chen, M.-C., Chang, C.-C., Lin, T.-Y., & Kuo, C.-F. (2025). Competencies and needs of older volunteers: A comparative survey. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000712

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA and Scheffe results from the 1,000-participant survey (Table 3). The article reports "F = 5.65, p = .004" for awareness and post hoc contrasts where both the youngest and oldest groups "had more needs than those aged 75–84 years."

> "Age and educational level were shown to significantly influence volunteer service awareness needs (F = 5.65, p = .004 and F = 14.06, p < .001, respectively). Participants aged 65–74 years and ≥85 years had more needs than those aged 75–84 years (p = .030 and .031, respectively)."

### Yang 2025 (2)

Yang, L.-C., LAI, C.-Y., Chen, K.-M., Chen, M.-C., Chang, C.-C., Lin, T.-Y., & Kuo, C.-F. (2025). Competencies and needs of older volunteers: A comparative survey. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000712

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post hoc analysis in the same survey: the initial omnibus age effect on skill needs was not confirmed between the three age groups; the same pattern is reported for interpersonal interaction (F = 3.53, p = .030).

> "With regard to volunteer service skill needs, although a significant difference was initially found for age (F = 3.56, p = .029), further comparison revealed no significant difference between the three age groups."
