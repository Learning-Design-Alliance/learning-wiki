Wojcikiewicz 2025¶

Wojcikiewicz, S. K., Ralph, K., Snyder, J., Bland, J. A., & Wechsler, M. E. (2025). Teacher residencies in Texas: Supporting successful implementation. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/630.675

Learning Policy Institute analysis of federal Title II data on Texas teacher preparation enrollment and completion by route, 2012–13 through 2022–23 (Figure 2). The report notes "nearly 85,000 candidates were enrolled" but "fewer than 8,500 completed them"; no effect size is printed.

"In 2022–23, for example, nearly 85,000 candidates were enrolled in such programs but fewer than 8,500 completed them. Consequently, overall EPP completion fell by 19% during this period"
