Evidence¶

Diego Dermeval 2018¶

Diego Dermeval, Ranilson Paiva, Ig Ibert Bittencourt, Julita Vassileva, Daniel Borges. (2018). Authoring Tools for Designing Intelligent Tutoring Systems: a Systematic Review of the Literature. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0157-9

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of the 33 included studies, which examined evidence of benefits including empirical and non-empirical evaluation. It reports "empirical evidence of the benefits" of authoring tools, especially for authoring learning content and supporting model-tracing/cognitive and example-tracing tutors.

"there is empirical evidence of the benefits (i.e., mainly in terms of effectiveness, efficiency, quality of authored artifacts, and usability) of using ITS authoring tools for non-programmer authors"
