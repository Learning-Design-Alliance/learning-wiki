Yoke et al. 2013¶

Soo Kum Yoke, Cecilia Bai Rajendran, Noridah Sain, Puteri Nur Hidayah Kamaludin, Sofwah Md Nawi & Suhaili Mohd Yusof. (2013). The Use of Online Corrective Feedback in Academic Writing by L1 Malay Learners. English Language Teaching, 6(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n12p175

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired sample t-test on draft scores from the experimental study of 44 L1 Malay students in two academic writing classes, 22 per group. The article reports the Draft 2 comparison as significant, "p value is .000 (p<.05)"; no effect size is printed.

"For pair 2 (Draft 2 between the control and experimental groups), the p value is .000 (p<.05). This means that there is a significant difference after the different treatments were applied to the groups."
