## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On post-test application of instructional methods, strategies, and supports to hypothetical cases, there was a significant difference between the two groups' means, with the Blended group's post-test means higher than the Behavioral group's. [→ Dille 2013](#dille-2013)
`q2 i?` The between-subjects group effect on post-test application was statistically significant (F (2, 43) = 35.740), with the Blended group's mean increase (18.250 points on severe cases) roughly double the Behavioral group's (9 points). [→ Dille 2013](#dille-2013)

## Evidence

### Dille 2013

Dille, L. (2013). A Comparison of Two Curricular Models of Professional Development to Increase Teacher Repertoires for Instructing Students with Autism. Journal of the American Academy of Special Education Professionals (JAASEP), Fall 2013. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135486

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

General Linear Model 3(group) x 2(severity) factorial ANOVA with repeated measures on severity, on post-test application scores of the 48-teacher study. The Blended group increased from M = 7.250 to M = 25.5 on severe cases (an 18.250-point increase) versus the Behavioral group's increase from M = 6.5 to M = 15.5 (9 points); the Control group had no mean increase.

> "The between-subjects effect for groups (F (2, 43) = 35.740, p>.05) was statistically significant."
