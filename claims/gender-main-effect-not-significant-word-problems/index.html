Evidence¶

Josiah Owolabi & Tobiloba Faith Adaramati 2015¶

Josiah Owolabi & Tobiloba Faith Adaramati. (2015). Effects of Graphic Organiser on Students' Achievement in Algebraic Word Problems. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.5. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083649

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

ANCOVA of post-test achievement scores by gender in the same non-equivalent group design (27 male, 13 female). The article reports the gender main effect as not significant, with partial Eta squared of 0.044, implying gender accounts for 4.4% of variance.
