Qualitative spring 2023 interviews (14 interviews, approximately 30-60 minutes, conducted via Zoom and transcribed) with salon and showcase participants. The report states "Many of the solution providers spoke of general prosociality—a desire to be helpful towards some positive end in education", and notes responses often fell into both prosocial and equity categories.
