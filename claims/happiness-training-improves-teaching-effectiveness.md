### Liu Yongmei 2025

Liu Yongmei, Xie Jian. (2025). Happiness training effect on primary school teachers’ academic burnout and teaching effectiveness: narratives from teachers. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-02985-6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA on the Teacher Effectiveness Scale (α = 0.89) for 200 teachers; Table 3 reports overall teaching effectiveness 3.4 (0.5), 4.3 (0.4), 4.1 (0.4), F = 43.95, p < 0.001, η² = 0.31, with dimension effects η² = 0.30, 0.32, 0.28.
