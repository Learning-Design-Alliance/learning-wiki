## Evidence

### Rao Lijia 2026

Rao Lijia, Wu Jiahua, Deng Bixia, Hong Yubing, Wu Juan, Chen Yiming, Chen Mu. (2026). Efficacy of a hybrid case-based learning and simulated clinical encounter model versus lecture-based learning in dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08937-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same randomized pilot (n = 20), communication was assessed with the SEGUE Framework by blinded assessors one week after the course. The hybrid group "significantly outperformed Group B" on total score and on the two named SEGUE domains. No effect size is printed.

> "Using the SEGUE Framework, Group A significantly outperformed Group B (Total Score: 17.80 ± 1.89 vs. 14.20 ± 2.18, P < 0.05). Specifically, Group A showed superior performance in “Eliciting information” and “Understanding the patient’s perspective” (P < 0.05)"
