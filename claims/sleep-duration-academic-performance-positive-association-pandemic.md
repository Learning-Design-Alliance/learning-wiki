# Sleep duration was positively associated with academic performance before the Covid-19 pandemic, and this association did not significantly change 1-year into it

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Sleep duration was positively and significantly associated with academic performance before the Covid-19 pandemic (β = 0.23; p < 0.001), and the time*sleep duration interaction was not significant (β = − 0.18; p > 0.05). [→ Tapia-Serrano Miguel Angel 2022](#tapia-serrano-miguel-angel-2022)

## Evidence

### Tapia-Serrano Miguel Angel 2022

Tapia-Serrano Miguel Angel, Sánchez-Oliva David, Sevil-Serrano Javier, Marques Adilson, Sánchez-Miguel Pedro Antonio. (2022). 24-h movement behaviours in Spanish youth before and after 1-year into the covid-19 pandemic and its relationship to academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-21096-5

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

In the mixed-model analysis adjusting for age, sex, and BMI, the article reports the sleep duration slope before the pandemic was β = 0.23 (p < 0.001) and that "The interaction effect was not significant (p > 0.05)" 1-year into the pandemic.

> "The interaction effect was not significant (p > 0.05), that is, this association remained significant for adolescents evaluated 1-year after Covid-19 pandemic (β = 0.23 + ( − 0.18) = 0.05; p > 0.05)."
