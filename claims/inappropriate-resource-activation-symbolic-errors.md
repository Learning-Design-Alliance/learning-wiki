## Evidence

### Eugene T. Torigoe 2015

Eugene T. Torigoe. (2015). Unpacking Symbolic Equations in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1508.00535](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1508.00535)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument under the resources framework (Hammer, Elby, Scherr, Redish) applied to symbolic difficulty: the context and perceived cues activate a web of resources, and symbolic problems' surface similarity to numeric problems activates an inappropriate web. The article offers no direct test of this mechanism.

> "The following rules work for numeric problem solving but do not work for symbolic problem solving. • Symbolic equations are a general mathematical model • The remaining non-numeric symbol is the target unknown to be isolated"
