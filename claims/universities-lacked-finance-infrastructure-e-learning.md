### Zarei Soraya 2022

Challenges of higher education related to e-learning in developing countries during COVID-19 spread: a review of the perspectives of students, instructors, policymakers, and ICT experts. Environmental Science and Pollution Research International. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11356-021-14647-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Adnan and Anwar, that Pakistani universities' financial resources were insufficient for distance-learning development and that no additional government funds were allocated. This is the review's second-hand report.

> "the financial resources of the universities in Pakistan are not enough, and universities do not receive any additional fund for these expenses"

### Zarei Soraya 2022 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Hoq, that in Saudi Arabia universities hired new ICT experts and purchased new platforms requiring expensive infrastructure. Reported as the review attributes it; no effect sizes.

> "universities had to hire new ICT experts to provide support for teachers and students. In addition, they had to purchase new platforms that required expensive infrastructures"
