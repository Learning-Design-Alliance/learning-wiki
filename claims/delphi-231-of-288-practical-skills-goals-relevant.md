## Evidence

### Dannenberg Katja Anne 2016

Dannenberg Katja Anne, Stroben Fabian, Schröder Therese, Thomas Anke, Hautz Wolf E. (2016). The future of practical skills in undergraduate medical education – an explorative Delphi-Study. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001061](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001061)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-round explorative Delphi survey of physician experts assessing the 288 Consensus Statement learning goals on 4-level Likert scales; round 2 re-evaluated 103 goals selected after round 1. The article reports "A total of 231 learning goals were considered relevant and 57 learning goals were considered irrelevant."

> "In round 2, experts assessed 62 learning goals as relevant and 41 as irrelevant. In comparing the two rounds, 13 learning goals (12.6%) were rated less relevant and 4 (3.9%) were rated as more relevant in round 2. A total of 231 learning goals were considered relevant and 57 learning goals were considered irrelevant."
