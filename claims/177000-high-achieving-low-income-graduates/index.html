Estimate from the most recent NPSAS reported in the section on high-achieving students; the report notes "this number likely underestimates the actual number" of high-achieving, low-income graduates because it only includes some students.

"Using estimates from the most recent National Postsecondary Student Aid Study (NPSAS), it seems that at least 177,00028 low-income high school graduates scored high enough on the SAT/ACT29 to earn entry into a public research university."
