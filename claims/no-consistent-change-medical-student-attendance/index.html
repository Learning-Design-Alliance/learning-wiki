Narrative synthesis of 12 included studies in a systematic review. The review reports "five studies showed a decrease and six studies reported no statistically significant differences", with one showing a significant increase; no meta-analysis was possible due to heterogeneity.

"One study showed a statistically significant increase in class attendance over the study period, while five studies showed a decrease and six studies reported no statistically significant differences."
