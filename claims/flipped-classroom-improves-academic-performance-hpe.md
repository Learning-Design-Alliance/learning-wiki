## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Naing 2023

Naing, C., Whittaker, M. A., Aung, H. H., Chellappan, D. K., & Riegelman, A. (2023). The effects of flipped classrooms to improve learning outcomes in undergraduate health professional education: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews, 19, e1339. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1339

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects inverse-variance meta-analysis of 44 studies (n = 7813) comparing flipped classroom with traditional lecture-based classes on examination scores/grades. The review reports "better academic performance in the flipped class method of learning" with SMD = 0.57 (95% CI 0.25 to 0.90), I² = 98%.

> "Based on overall average effect sizes, there was better academic performance in the flipped class method of learning compared to traditional class learning (standardised mean difference [SMD] = 0.57, 95% confidence interval [CI] = 0.25 to 0.90, τ 2: 1.16; I 2: 98%; p < 0.00001, 44 studies, n = 7813)."
