Evidence¶

Hammer 2014¶

Hammer, C. S., Hoff, E., Uchikoshi, Y., Gillanders, C., Castro, D., & Sandilos, L. E. (2014). The Language and Literacy Development of Young Dual Language Learners: A Critical Review. Early childhood research quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2014.05.008

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Hammer et al.'s (2008a) longitudinal Head Start comparison: "simultaneous learners entered and exited Head Start programs with higher English vocabulary scores than sequential learners," with the opposite pattern for Spanish vocabularies; sequential learners' English vocabulary grew at a faster rate.

"Hammer and her colleagues (2008a) observed that simultaneous learners entered and exited Head Start programs with higher English vocabulary scores than sequential learners. The opposite pattern was found for children’s Spanish vocabularies."
