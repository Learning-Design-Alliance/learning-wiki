`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the practice items (P1–P5); P2 and P3 were analyzed only among the 296 students reporting prior participation. The article reports these percentages descriptively with no inferential test or effect size.

> "More than half (56.40%) of respondents reported prior participation in a virtual simulation training course (P1: "Have you ever participated in a virtual simulation experimental training course?"). Among the 296 students who reported prior participation, 66.9% agreed or strongly agreed that their experimental or clinical skills had improved through participation (P2)."
