## Evidence

### S. Y. Math 2026

S. Y. Math, O. S. Sultan, M. F. B. Z. Anuar, and C. Pacheco‐Pereira, "Interactive E‐Learning Module: Enhancing Panoramic Radiograph Interpretation Skills of Dental Students." Journal of Dental Education 90, no. 1 (2026): 136–146. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13923

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of OSRI scores for 45 final-year dental students before and after four weeks of e-learning modules. Overall DPR mean scores rose, e.g., DPR 1 from 4.8 (1.2) to 7.1 (0.9); the article reports "all p < 0.001" with no per-comparison effect size.

> "For the OSRI, the mean overall scores improved across all four DPRs after the intervention (all p < 0.001) (Table 1)."

### S. Y. Math 2026 (2)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`
