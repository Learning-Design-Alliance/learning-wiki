## Evidence

### Erdem 2026

Erdem, G., Kafescioğlu, N., & Başer, G. (2026). Are You Qualified, ChatGPT? Examining Clinical Skills and Competencies of ChatGPT in Delivering Systemic Interventions. Journal of Marital and Family Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1111/jmft.70168

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of standardized scale ratings from 12 CFT supervisors who each read the same blinded mock-session transcript. The article reports the means as descriptive: "cultural competence (M = 3.85, SD = 0.81, range 1–6)" and systemic skills M = 3.91, systemic orientation M = 3.11; no inferential tests were run.

> "Supervisors rated the GPT model's performance on cultural competence (M = 3.85, SD = 0.81, range 1–6), systemic family therapy skills (M = 3.91, SD = 0.76, range 0–6), and the integration of a systemic orientation into individual therapy (M = 3.11, SD = 0.37, range 1–5) at a moderate level."
