Evidence¶

Jeanne Wanzek 2019¶

Jeanne Wanzek, Greg Roberts, Sharon Vaughn, Elizabeth Swanson, and Katherine Sargent. (2019). Examining the Role of Pre-Instruction Academic Performance Within A Text-based Approach to Improving Student Content Knowledge and Understanding. Exceptional Children, 85(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918783187

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Model 3 of the latent moderated SEM analysis tested whether class-level Gates-MacGinitie reading comprehension moderated PACT's effect on ASK content knowledge, controlling for pretest content knowledge. The interaction was ".072; p = .17"; the article reports the standardized difference in conditional posttest means was .002 and the effect was constant across moderator values.

"Accordingly, the interaction of treatment and the pretest Gates-MacGinitie (γ04z.jxj = .072; p = .17) rep- resents the moderating effect of general read- ing comprehension on posttest content knowledge when controlling for pretreatment levels of content knowledge."
