## Evidence

### Songlin Xu 2023

Songlin Xu, Dongyin Hu, Ru Wang, and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Peer attention enhances student learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same N=311 between-subjects experiment; NASA TLX ratings showed no significant group difference (Feedback 3.355 ± 0.995 vs Control 3.171 ± 0.958; F1,301 = 1.884, P = 0.17). Equivalence was not tested; no effect size printed.

> "we did not find significant difference on cognitive workload (measured by NASA TLX24) between groups (Feedback: mean ± SD: 3.355 ± 0.995; Control: 3.171 ± 0.958; F1,301 = 1.884,P = 0.17 > 0.05)"

### Songlin Xu 2023 (2)

Songlin Xu, Dongyin Hu, Ru Wang, and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Peer attention enhances student learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis across metrics (Fig. 3) in the same experiment found workload positively correlated with group differences and test accuracy; the article prints no correlation coefficient for this statement in the text.

> "Interestingly, cognitive workload also positively correlated with group differences and test accuracy. Despite potentially increasing workload, peer attention still improved learning."
