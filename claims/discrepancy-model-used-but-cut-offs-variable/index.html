White 2018¶

White, Sonia, Graham, Linda, & Blaas, Sabrina (2018). Why do we know so little about the factors associated with gifted underachievement? A systematic literature review. Educational Research Review, 24, pp. 55-66. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2018.03.001

q3 · i? · review · r3

Review of underachievement identification across the nine articles (Tables 3–4). The review reports all nine "conceptualized gifted underachievement using a discrepancy model", but achievement measures (GPA, tests, rankings) and discrepancy metrics varied; only three used individually referenced benchmarks.

"All nine articles meeting our inclusion criteria conceptualized gifted underachievement using a discrepancy model, where students who are identified as gifted demonstrate lower than expected achievement relative to cognitive ability."
