Narrative synthesis of 29 cross-sectional studies found knowledge frequently suboptimal, with scores ranging from "11.1–11.8 out of 25–26" to over 80% in a minority of studies.

> "Across all regions, nurses' knowledge of PU/PI prevention was frequently reported as low or suboptimal. Knowledge scores ranged from very low raw scores (e.g., 11.1–11.8 out of 25–26) to higher percentage‐based scores exceeding 80% in a limited number of studies"
