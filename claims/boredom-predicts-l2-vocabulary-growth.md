The final multilevel model on repeated vocabulary measures included enjoyment, boredom and anxiety as covariates. Only boredom was a significant predictor (estimate 0.36); the null results for enjoyment and anxiety were reported with test statistics but no effect sizes.

> "Among achievement emotions, boredom was also found to predict English vocabulary ( t(415) = 2.69, p =.007). No significant fixed effect included enjoyment (t(415) = 0.504, p =.614) or anxiety (t(415) = − 1.37, p =.170)."
