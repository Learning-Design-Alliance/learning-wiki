## Evidence

### Coogan P 2020

Coogan P, Schon K, Li S, Cozier Y, Bethea T, Rosenberg L. (2020). Experiences of racism and subjective cognitive function in African American women. Alzheimer's & Dementia: Diagnosis, Assessment & Disease Monitoring. https://doi.org/10.1002/dad2.12067

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Secondary analysis restricted to women in the same quartile of daily racism in 1997 and 2009, based on the 2009 distribution. The ORs for poor SCF "were stronger among women who reported consistent levels of daily racism in 1997 and 2009", reaching "OR 4.18 (95% CI 3.24 to 5.39) in the highest quartile".
