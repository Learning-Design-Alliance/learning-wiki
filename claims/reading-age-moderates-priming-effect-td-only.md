## Evidence

### Fiveash 2023

Fiveash, A., Ladányi, E., Camici, J., Chidiac, K., Bush, C. T., Canette, L.-H., Bedoin, N., Gordon, R. L. & Tillmann, B. (2023). Regular rhythmic primes improve sentence repetition in children with developmental language disorder. npj Science of Learning 8:23. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00170-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the reading-age covariate analysis (reduced base model, three children missing reading age data), "a signi ﬁcant prime x group x reading age interaction" emerged, χ2 (1) = 5.71, p = 0.017; no effect size was printed for the interaction.

> "(2) For reading age, adding the ﬁxed effect and interactions showed a signi ﬁcant prime x group x reading age interaction, χ2 (1) = 5.71, p = 0.017, AIC = 1042.1."

### Fiveash 2023 (2)

Fiveash, A., Ladányi, E., Camici, J., Chidiac, K., Bush, C. T., Canette, L.-H., Bedoin, N., Gordon, R. L. & Tillmann, B. (2023). Regular rhythmic primes improve sentence repetition in children with developmental language disorder. npj Science of Learning 8:23. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00170-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up trend analysis of the three-way interaction: "a signi ﬁcant trend for increased performance with reading age after regular primes in children with TD", r = 0.48, p = 0.002; trends were not significant after irregular primes in TD (p = 0.28) or in DLD children (regular p = 0.35, irregular p = 0.10).

> "This interaction revealed a signi ﬁcant trend for increased performance with reading age after regular primes in children with TD, trend = 1.26, SE = 0.40, p = 0.002, z-ratio = 3.13, r = 0.48, 95% CI around effect size [0.20, 0.66]"
