## Evidence

### Anne C. Campbell and Emelye Neff 2020

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Argument from the review's framing section, supported by cited observations (Campbell & Mawer, 2019) that competing goals may even produce perverse effects, such as a winner deciding that living abroad and sending remittances best promotes development. The review states this conflation can lead to ambiguous models and inadequate evaluations.

> "students can be caught between competing and unrealistic expectations, leading to challenging decisions about remaining abroad at the completion of their programs or leading to skepticism about program aims and their roles within these schemes"
