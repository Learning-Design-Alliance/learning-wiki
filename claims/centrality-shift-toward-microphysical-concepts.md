## Evidence

### Julien-Pooya Weihs 2024

Julien-Pooya Weihs, Helge Drange, Vegard Gjerde. (2024). From Novice to Expert in Cloud Physics: a Network-Based Analysis of Learner Understanding. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.06479](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.06479)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Node-level centrality comparison between Adept and Advanced group graphs, with permutation-based z-scores and p-values. Droplet Growth gained degree centrality (+0.31|3.31|0.001) and betweenness centrality (+0.10|2.02|0.038), which the authors read as confirming "the core role that Droplet Growth gains in the Advanced graph".

> "The evolutions of degree and betweenness centralities (Table I; Supplementary File 4) confirm the core role that Droplet Growth gains in the Advanced graph, both as a highly connected concept (+0.31|3.31|0.001) and as an intermediary bridging other concepts (+0.10|2.02|0.038)."

### Julien-Pooya Weihs 2024 (2)

Julien-Pooya Weihs, Helge Drange, Vegard Gjerde. (2024). From Novice to Expert in Cloud Physics: a Network-Based Analysis of Learner Understanding. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.06479](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.06479)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same node-level comparison of Adept and Advanced graphs. The general water-cycle concept Water Mass shows a "notable centrality decrease" based on its predecessors (−0.21|3.82|0.001) and successors (−0.19|3.31|0.001).

> "Water Mass shows a notable centrality decrease based on its predecessors (−0.21|3.82|0.001) and successors (−0.19|3.31|0.001), reducing its overall influence in the life-cycle of a cloud."
