### Langdalen H 2018

Langdalen H, Abrahamsen EB, Sollid SJM, Sørskår LIK, Abrahamsen HB. (2018). A comparative study on the frequency of simulation-based training and assessment of non-technical skills in the Norwegian ground ambulance services and helicopter emergency medical services. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-018-3325-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of flight operator employees (n = 56) with health trust employees (n = 1051) from the same 2016 questionnaire on 2015 practice. Table 2 prints Fisher's exact test p-values, all < 0.001 to 0.003, for every NTS in both training and assessment; e.g. coping with fatigue training 57.1% vs 23.0%.

> "The frequency of training in and assessment of the seven NTSs among employees of the flight operator (pilots and HCMs) is statistically significantly greater than for employees of the health trust (Fig. 3 and Table 2)."
