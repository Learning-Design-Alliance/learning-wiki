Response-pattern analysis of Item 16 (Loops-FKSA1) in the main study, requiring identification of the loop structure counting valid images from a list. The correct answer was a while loop controlled by a Boolean condition; 34.9% selected the for-loop option.

"Nevertheless, 34.9% of students selected Option B, suggesting that many students misinterpreted the syntax and were unfamiliar with how while loops are defined and applied"
