### Back David A 2014

Back David A, Haberstroh Nicole, Antolic Andrea, Sostmann Kai, Schmidmaier Gerhard, Hoff Eike. (2014). Blended learning approach improves teaching in a problem-based learning environment in orthopedics - a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-17

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

All students (35 users, 18 non-users) took the mandatory OSCE with history-taking, physical examination and diagnosis of actor-patients; scores were compared between groups. No statistically significant difference was found; equivalence was not tested.

> "The results of the OSCE revealed no differences between the groups."
