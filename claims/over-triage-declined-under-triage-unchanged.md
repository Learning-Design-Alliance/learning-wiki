### Nilsson Abraham 2015

Nilsson Abraham, Åslund Kristian, Lampi Maria, Nilsson Helene, Jonson Carl-Oscar. (2015). Improved and sustained triage skills in firemen after a short training intervention. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. [doi:10.1186/s13049-015-0162-7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-015-0162-7)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive error rates from the pretest and post-test of 86 firemen. Over triage fell from 23 % to 14 %; under triage was 18 % versus 17 %. The discussion attributes under triage partly to the subjective "minor injuries only" decision point.

> "Over and under triage for the test at baseline was 23 and 18 %, respectively. Over and under triage for the test after the training was 14 and 17 %, respectively (Fig. 3)."
