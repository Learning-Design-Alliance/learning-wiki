`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

### Abraham Reina and Singaram Veena S 2024 (3)

Abraham Reina and Singaram Veena S. (2024). Self and peer feedback engagement and receptivity among medical students with varied academic performance in the clinical skills laboratory. BMC Medical Education 24, Article 1065. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06084-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`
