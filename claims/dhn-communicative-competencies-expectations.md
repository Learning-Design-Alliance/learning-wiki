`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of interviews and focus groups (35 participants). In the communicative competencies category, "11/35, 31.43%" emphasized professional, sensitive communication; participants also wanted lay-friendly explanations and adaptation of communication mode (phone, video call, or in-person) to patient needs.

> "Participants (11/35, 31.43%) emphasized that DHNs should communicate with patients in a professional yet sensitive manner, showing appreciation, empathy, and mutual respect."
