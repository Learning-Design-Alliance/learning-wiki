Clinical competencies in the management of older adults with aspiration pneumonia: a scoping review. European Geriatric Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41999-023-00898-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Phase 1 definition search: a PubMed search for 2020–2021 papers with 'aspiration pneumo*' in the title yielded 114 studies, narrowed by full-text screening to 31 studies from which defining factors were extracted (Table 1). The authors state AP "was commonly defined as pneumonia occurring in the context of aspiration or dysphagia".

> "From these studies, we found that AP was commonly defined as pneumonia occurring in the context of aspiration or dysphagia. In other words, we defined AP as “pneumonia in a patient with witnessed aspiration, frequent signs of aspiration, or risk factors for aspiration/dysphagia”."
