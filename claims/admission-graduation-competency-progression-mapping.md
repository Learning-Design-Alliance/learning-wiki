The discussion's comparative analysis (Table 6) maps admission competencies A1–A10 onto graduation competencies G1–G6. Some attributes (A2, A6, A8, A10) are described as "essential for the rigorous demands of their training", while others (A1, A3, A7) are "often inherent traits that are challenging to cultivate solely through education".

> "Admission competencies A4 (professionalism), A5 (social skills), and A9 (analytical & creative thinking) correspond to graduation competencies G1 (professionalism as a health provider), G2 (communication skills), and G3 (knowledge-based critical thinking)"
