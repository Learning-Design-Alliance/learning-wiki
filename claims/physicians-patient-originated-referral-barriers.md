Senf B , Fettel J , Demmerle C , Maiwurm P . (2019). Physicians' attitudes towards psycho‐oncology, perceived barriers, and psychosocial competencies: Indicators of successful implementation of adjunctive psycho‐oncological care? Psycho‐Oncology. https://doi.org/10.1002/pon.4962

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the survey of 102 physicians at a comprehensive cancer center, using the 23-item perceived barriers scale. The article reports barriers "were not believed to be substantial hindrances to accessing psycho‐oncological care (M = 2.38, SD = 0.62)". No effect sizes are printed.
