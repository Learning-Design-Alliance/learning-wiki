## Evidence

### Abelsson Anna 2014

Abelsson Anna, Rystedt Ingrid, Suserud Björn-Ove, Lindwall Lillemor. (2014). Mapping the use of simulation in prehospital care – a literature review. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-22-22

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's triage synthesis (10 articles since 2001) reports that triage training improved knowledge, that table-top exercises with 59 EMTs were not evaluated as ideal, and that live victims and written scenarios "may result in similar learning outcomes" despite differences in cost, time and space consumption.

> "If live victims scenarios were used instead, the participants scored this higher than written scenarios without significant differences and were, by 61 prehospital providers in an exploratory study, compared favourably, to manikins."
