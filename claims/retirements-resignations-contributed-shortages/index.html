Evidence¶

Carver-Thomas 2022¶

Carver-Thomas, D., Burns, D., Leung, M., & Ondrasek, N. (2022). Teacher shortages during the pandemic: How California districts are responding. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/899.809

q2 · i? · associational · r1

District survey responses collected August–September 2021. The report prints descriptive changes — retirements up "nearly a quarter," resignations "about two thirds," leaves "about 50%" in one large district — but no standardized effect size. A district administrator attributed increased retirements partly to burnout from distance learning.

"Five out of eight large districts reported that increased retirements and resignations contributed to having more vacancies than usual. In one large district, retirements in the 2020–21 school year increased by nearly a quarter compared to the 2018–19 school year, resignations increased by about two thirds, and leaves of absence increased by about 50%."
