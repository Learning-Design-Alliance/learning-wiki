### Maloney Ciaran M. C. 2026

Maloney Ciaran M. C., Atkin Andrew J., Beaumont Lee C., Dainty Jack R., Warburton Victoria E. (2026). Adolescents’ time-use and academic attainment: A longitudinal, compositional analysis in the Millennium Cohort Study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0346302

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal compositional analysis linking Millennium Cohort Study time-use diaries at age 14 to Attainment 8 at age 16 (weekday n=1644, weekend n=1642). The article reports the ILR coordinates were "significantly associated with Attainment 8" with p < .001 in both samples; no effect size is printed for this omnibus result.

> "After adjustment for covariates, the ILR coordinates for time-use composition were significantly associated with Attainment 8 in both the weekday (p < .001) and weekend (p < .001) samples."
