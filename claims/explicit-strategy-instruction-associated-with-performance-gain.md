## Evidence

### Charlotte Dignath & Marcel V. J. Veenman 2021

Charlotte Dignath & Marcel V. J. Veenman. (2021). The Role of Direct Strategy Instruction and Indirect Activation of Self-Regulated Learning—Evidence from Classroom Observation Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09534-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Observation study using the ATES instrument to code teachers' direct and indirect promotion of SRL and its relation to the development of students' performance. The review reports that "explicit strategy instruction was associated with a gain in performance", while a great amount of strategy teaching took place in an implicit way and supportive learning environments were rare.

> "In contrast to implicit strategy instruction, explicit strategy instruction was associated with a gain in performance."
