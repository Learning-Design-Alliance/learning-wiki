Moderator analysis of outcome construct (Table 3): retention k = 243 (g = 0.63), transfer k = 11 (g = 0.53), mixed retention and transfer k = 15 (g = 0.37). "there was no statistically significant difference between retention (g = 0.63) and transfer (g = 0.53) outcomes."

"In addition, there was no statistically significant difference between retention (g = 0.63) and transfer (g = 0.53) outcomes. However, the retention-based outcome produced a much higher effect size, and was significantly larger than the mixed-retention and transfer outcome (g = 0.37)."
