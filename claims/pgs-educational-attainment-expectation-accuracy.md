## Evidence

### Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon 2022

Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon, Davies Neil M., Morris Tim T. (2022). Investigating how the accuracy of teacher expectations of pupil performance relate to socioeconomic and genetic factors. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-11347-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression of teacher expectation accuracy on a PGS built from the largest GWAS of educational attainment, in the imputed ALSPAC sample (n = 7465). The article reports the associations "persisted after adjustment for demographic and socioeconomic factors, and the first 20 principal components of ancestry" (KS2: 0.08; KS3: 0.07).

> "Pupil’s educational attainment polygenic score associated with teacher expectation accuracy at both Key Stage 2 (0.13; 95% CI 0.11, 0.16) and Key Stage 3 (0.10; 95% CI 0.08, 0.13)"
