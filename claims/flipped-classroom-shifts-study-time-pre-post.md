## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-class study time was about 10 minutes longer and post-class review time about 30 minutes shorter in the flipped group, both significant. [→ Wei Yaru 2025](#wei-yaru-2025)

## Evidence

### Wei Yaru 2025

Wei Yaru, Peng Zhengjun. (2025). Application of the flipped classroom model based on Bloom’s Taxonomy of Educational Objectives in endodontics education for undergraduate dental students. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.18843

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Time-use comparison from the questionnaire of the two groups of 16 students; Table 2 shows pre-course preparation 32.5 ± 9.30 vs 42.8 ± 9.12 min and after-school revision 84.4 ± 31.1 vs 50.0 ± 13.8 min. No effect size printed.

> "Pre-class study time was approximately 10 min longer for the flipped classroom group compared to the lecture group (p = 0.003). However, post-class study time in the flipped classroom group was reduced by approximately 30 min compared to the lecture group (p = 0.001), a statistically significant difference."
