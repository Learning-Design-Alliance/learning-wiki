### Michael A. Gottfried 2010

Michael A. Gottfried. (2010). Evaluating the Relationship Between Student Attendance and Achievement in Urban Elementary and Middle Schools: An Instrumental Variables Approach. American Educational Research Journal, Vol. 47, No. 2, pp. 434-465. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831209350494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of baseline regression coefficients across the elementary and middle school subsamples of the Philadelphia panel (Table 3). The article prints unstandardized coefficients on days present of 0.020 for middle school versus 0.016 for elementary and full samples; no standardized effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "In fact, a slightly larger coefficient in the middle school regression12 demonstrates that attendance is more strongly correlated with a higher GPA as students advance through years of schooling (Roby, 2004)."
