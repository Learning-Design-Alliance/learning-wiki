The author analyzed student reflections in several of his own physics courses using a five-category schema from the educational literature, ranging from superficial reflections about class time to critical reflection. He reports that "students were in fact capable of serious and sophisticated reﬂection" and gained insights into attitudes and epistemological states. No effect sizes or sample sizes are printed.
