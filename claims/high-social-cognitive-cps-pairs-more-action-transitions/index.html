Evidence¶

Sanna Järvelä 2022¶

Sanna Järvelä, Carolyn P. Rosé. (2022). Relations Matter – CSCL Research Informing and Developing CL Competencies. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2022) 17:185–189. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09373-x

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of log file data from 166 sixth-grade student pairs working in an online CPS assessment environment, described in the editorial's summary of Li, Pöysä-Tarhonen and Häkkinen's study. Social network analysis produced weighted directed transition networks; pairs with "more attempts given to different actions" were interpreted as attempting more solution paths.

"The results showed that pairs with one member with high social and high cognitive CPS skills conducted more actions and demonstrated a higher average frequency of action transitions with more attempts given to different actions"
