## Evidence

### Diwan Faizan 2014

Diwan Faizan, Makana Grace, McKenzie David, Paruzzolo Silvia. (2014). Invitation Choice Structure Has No Impact on Attendance in a Female Business Training Program in Kenya. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0109873

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Probit regression of attending at least one day on individual and business characteristics (N = 1101), marginal effects reported in Table 4. The article reports that "women aged above 35 are 35 percentage points more likely to attend training than those below 35" and married women 24 points less likely; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "all else equal, women aged above 35 are 35 percentage points more likely to attend training than those below 35, while married women are 24 percentage points less likely to attend than unmarried women."
