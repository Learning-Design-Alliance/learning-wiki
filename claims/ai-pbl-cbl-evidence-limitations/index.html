Evidence¶

Wei H 2025¶

Wei H, Dai Y, Yuan K, Li KY, Hung KF, Hu EM, Lee AHC, Chang JWW, Zhang C, Li X. (2025). AI-Powered Problem- and Case-based Learning in Medical and Dental Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.100858

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review's own quality appraisal of the six included RCTs using RoB 2; all studies were judged 'some concerns' overall, and the review states there is "insufficient evidence supporting the long-term effectiveness" of AI-powered learning. No effect size applies to this methodological finding.

"It should also be noted that the overall risk of bias for all included studies was judged to have ‘some concerns’. Furthermore, there is insufficient evidence supporting the long-term effectiveness of AI-powered learning."
