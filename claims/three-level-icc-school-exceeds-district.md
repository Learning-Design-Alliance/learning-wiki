### Jessaca Spybrook 2016

Jessaca Spybrook, Carl D. Westine, Joseph A. Taylor. (2016). Design Parameters for Impact Research in Science Education: A Multistate Analysis. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858415625975

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Three-level hierarchical model analysis (students nested in schools nested in districts) of state science assessment data. In Texas Grade 5, approximately 8% of variance is between districts, 12% between schools within districts, and 80% between students within schools. Michigan's Grade 5 district-level ICC is nearly twice the school-level ICC.

> "In both states, the school-level variance is always larger than the district-level variance, ranging from almost twice as large to nearly 3 times as large."
