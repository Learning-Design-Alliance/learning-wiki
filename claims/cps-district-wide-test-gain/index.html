Docktor 2015¶

Docktor, J. L., Strand, N. E., Mestre, J. P., & Ross, B. H. (2015). Conceptual problem solving in high school physics. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 11, 020106. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.11.020106

Secondary analysis using school C's district-wide tests developed independently of the study, comparing the CPS year to the previous year's class; the 9% advantage was only marginally significant (p<0.10).

"For the 12 conceptual questions asked in the test, his class for the year using the CPS performed 9% better across the problems [marginally significant, tð11Þ¼ 1.84, p< 0.10; the CPS class also did better on 9 of the 12 problems, also p< 0.10 by a sign test]."
