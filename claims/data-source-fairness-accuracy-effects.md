As reported in the review, S7 (Yu et al., 2020) evaluated the fairness impact of features from institutional, LMS, and survey data sources in predicting student success, finding all data sources exhibit bias and institutional features produced the most unfair outcome.

> "According to the study, combining institutional and LMS data led to the most accurate prediction college success. On the other hand, features from the institutional data led to the most discriminatory behavior of the model. Meanwhile, features from survey dataset recorded the lowest accuracy while also showing high bias."
