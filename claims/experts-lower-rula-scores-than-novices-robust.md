## Evidence

### Elkhouly AG 2025

Elkhouly AG, Santhirakumaran G, Shanahan B, Batchelor T, Wilson H, Waller D, Stamenkovic S, Lau K. (2025). Objective Assessment of Ergonomics and Performance of Thoracic Surgeons During Real-Life Anatomical Robotic Pulmonary Resections. Interdisciplinary Cardiovascular and Thoracic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1093/icvts/ivaf180

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Intergroup comparison in a single-centre observational cross-sectional study of vascular dissection/division during 67 robotic anatomical resections (95 expert, 85 novice vessel events). Table 3 reports mean RULA 4.3 ± 0.4 for experts versus 4.7 ± 0.7 for novices, with the chi-square comparison across RULA scores significant: "Group 1 also had significantly lower RULA scores (4.3 vs 4.7, P < .001) (Table 3, Figure 2)."
