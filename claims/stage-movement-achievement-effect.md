## Evidence

### Antoniou 2011

Antoniou, P., Kyriakides, L., & Creemers, B. (2011). Investigating the Effectiveness of a Dynamic Integrated Approach to Teacher Professional Development. ceps Journal, 1(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130737

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel model 6 added a movement dummy (0 = no movement, 1 = moved to next stage) for the 21 DIA teachers who progressed. The printed coefficient is 0.09 (.03); the article reports the effect as statistically significant without a standardised effect size.

> "According to the results in the last column of the above table, moving to the next developmental stage was found to have a statistically significant effect on student achievement."
