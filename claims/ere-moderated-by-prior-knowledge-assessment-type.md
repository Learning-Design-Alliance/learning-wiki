## Evidence

### Tetzlaff and Peters 2025

Leonard Tetzlaff, Bianca Simonsmeier, Tabea Peters, Garvin Brod. (2025). A cornerstone of adaptivity – A meta-analysis of the expertise reversal effect. Learning and Instruction 98 (2025) 102142. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102142

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis by assessment type (grade level, profession/field, measurement, intervention, self-evaluation) across the included studies. The article also states the type of assessment "did not significantly moderate the effect for low prior knowledge learners" but was descriptively pronounced for profession/field distinctions.

> "For high prior knowledge learners, the moderation was significant (R2 = 50.00 %), with especially pronounced effect sizes when prior knowledge was defined by differences in grade level and weakest when defined by measurement or intervention."
