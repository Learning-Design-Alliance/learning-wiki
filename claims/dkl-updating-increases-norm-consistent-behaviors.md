### Duan 2026

Duan, J., Guo, X., Zheng, L. & Yin, J. (2026). A Bayesian perspective on observers' inference of group norms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00405-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures analysis of DKL (belief-updating degrees) in the same Experiment 1 sample (N = 32) showed the reported significant main effect "ηp2 = 0.62", with adjacent-condition differences and DKL values above 0 in all conditions (ds > 0.74), indicating participants updated norm-related beliefs from observation.

> "The ANOVA results revealed that the main effect of the number of norm-consistent behaviors was significant, F (1.21, 37.39) = 49.76, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.62."
