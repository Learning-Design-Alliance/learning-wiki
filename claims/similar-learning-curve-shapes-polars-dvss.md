### Hardon Sem F. 2022

Hardon Sem F., Kooijmans Anton, Horeman Roel, van der Elst Maarten, Bloemendaal Alexander L. A., Horeman Tim. (2022). Validation of the portable virtual reality training system for robotic surgery (PoLaRS): a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-021-08906-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learning-curve analysis across 528 trials executed by 38 participants in the randomized trial. The article reports "Learning curves showed similar shapes between both systems, and between both groups", and in the discussion states this indicates construct validity as both systems appear intuitive enough to be used as an effective extension of the human arm.
