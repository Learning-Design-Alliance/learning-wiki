The report cites the late-2020 Common Sense Media and Boston Consulting Group documentation of home connectivity divides during school closures: "Approximately 30 percent—15 to 16 million students—lacked access to adequate internet or devices." Rural students (37%) exceeded urban students (21%) in lacking adequate access; Native American (35%), Black (30%), and Latino (26%) students exceeded white students (18%).

> "Approximately 30 percent—15 to 16 million students—lacked access to adequate internet or devices, while nine million lacked access to both. The percentage of rural students without adequate access (37%) was higher than the still-high rate for urban students (21%)."
