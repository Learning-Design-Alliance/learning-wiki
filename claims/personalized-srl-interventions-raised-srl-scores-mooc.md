# Personalized SRL email interventions increased post-course self-regulation scores relative to control in a MOOC, while the general intervention did not

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After adjusting for pre-course SRL, post-course SRL levels were statistically significantly greater in the personalized intervention group than the control group, with no significant difference between control and general intervention groups. [→ Psathas 2023](#psathas-2023)

## Evidence

### Psathas 2023

Psathas, G., Tegos, S., Demetriadis, S. N., & Tsiatsos, T. (2023). Exploring the impact of chat-based collaborative activities and SRL-focused interventions on students’ self-regulation profiles, participation in collaborative activities, retention, and learning in MOOCs. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn 18:329–351. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09394-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on post-course SRL scores controlling for pre-course SRL, N = 277 exit-questionnaire respondents, comparing control, general, and personalized intervention groups. The omnibus test was F(2, 273) = 5.082, p < 0.01, "partial η 2 = 0.036"; no effect size was printed for the pairwise difference.

> "The students’ SRL level was statistically significantly greater in the personalized intervention group (M = 4.683, SE = 0.068) as compared to the control group (M = 4.355, SE = 0.077)"
