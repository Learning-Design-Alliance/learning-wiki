## Evidence

### Krijnen 2024

Krijnen, M. A., Wansink, B. G. J., van den Berg, Y. H. M., van Tartwijk, J., & Mainhard, T. (2024). Citizenship in the Elementary Classroom Through the Lens of Peer Relations. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09870-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Jackson et al. (2015), that in an average classroom of 20 children relational aggression nominations had "M = 3 peers, SD = 2.4 peers" and physical aggression M = 4 peers, SD = 3.8 peers, indicating some children are noticed by many classmates as less peaceful.

> "relational aggression nominations ranged from no received nominations to 14 nominations (M = 3 peers, SD = 2.4 peers). Physical aggression even ranged from no nominations to nominations by all classmates (M = 4 peers, SD = 3.8 peers"
