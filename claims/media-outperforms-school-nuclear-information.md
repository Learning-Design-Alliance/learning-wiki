### Mirofora Pilakouta 2011

Mirofora Pilakouta. (2011). TEI Piraeus students' knowledge on the beneficial applications of nuclear physics: Nuclear energy, radioactivity - consequences. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1111.4058](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1111.4058)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on main information source about radioactivity among the 131 respondents: 73% mass media (internet 56%, radio-TV 17%), 13% other, 14% school. The author concludes "our school fails to offer the appropriate knowledge about these serious issues".

> "Overall, an average of three out of four students (73%) has information from mass media (internet (56%) and radio -TV (17%)). A small number of students (13%) have other source of information and only 14% indicate school as the source of their information about radioactivity topics."
