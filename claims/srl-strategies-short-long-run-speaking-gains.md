## Evidence

### Mohammadzadeh 2018

Mohammadzadeh, A., & Sarkhosh, M. (2018). The Effects of Self-Regulatory Learning through Computer-Assisted Intelligent Tutoring System on the Improvement of EFL Learners' Speaking Ability. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 167-184. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11212a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated Measures ANOVA within each of the two SRL groups (n=15 each) across pre-test, immediate and delayed post-tests; both within-subjects time effects were significant, P < 0.05.

> "RM ANOVA with a Greenhouse-Geisser correction revealed that the mean performance in speaking differed statistically significantly between time points for the seeking -help group (F (1.786, 25.005) =35.737,  P < 0.05), for the self -monitoring group ( F (1.548, 21.666) = 87.233,  P < 0.05)"

### Mohammadzadeh 2018 (2)

Mohammadzadeh, A., & Sarkhosh, M. (2018). The Effects of Self-Regulatory Learning through Computer-Assisted Intelligent Tutoring System on the Improvement of EFL Learners' Speaking Ability. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 167-184. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11212a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bonferroni-corrected pairwise comparisons within the seeking-help group: speaking improved "from pre -test to the immediate post -test" and from pre-test to the delayed post-test (sig<0.05); the same pattern is reported for self-monitoring, so gains persisted after one month despite some score decline.

> "Post-hoc tests using the Bonferroni correction (Table 8) showed that seeking -help strategy improved speaking from pre -test to the immediate post -test and also from pre -test to the delayed post -test (sig<0.05)."
