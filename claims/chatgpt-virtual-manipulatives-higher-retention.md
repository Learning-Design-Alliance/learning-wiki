### Bolatli 2026

Bolatli, G., S. Birisci, and Z. Bolatli. 2026. An Alternative Approach in Anatomy Education: Design of a Learning Environment Based on Artificial Intelligence‐Supported Virtual Manipulatives and Investigation of Its Effectiveness. Clinical Anatomy 39, no. 1: 30–44. https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.70008

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on AAT readministered 5 months later to all three groups; descriptive retention means were SML+AI 49.37, C 32.00, SML 33.91.

> "As a result of the ANOVA test scores, there is a significant difference in retention between at least two groups (F (2, 72) = 12.02; p < 0.05)."

### Bolatli 2026 (2)

Bolatli, G., S. Birisci, and Z. Bolatli. 2026. An Alternative Approach in Anatomy Education: Design of a Learning Environment Based on Artificial Intelligence‐Supported Virtual Manipulatives and Investigation of Its Effectiveness. Clinical Anatomy 39, no. 1: 30–44. https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.70008

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LCD post hoc results (Table 6): SML+AI scored 20.25 points above C (p = 0.000) and 19.51 above SML (p = 0.001); C vs SML not significant (p = 0.988).

> "Based on the LCD post hoc test results, the source of this significance lay between SML + AI and the other groups (p < 0.05)."
