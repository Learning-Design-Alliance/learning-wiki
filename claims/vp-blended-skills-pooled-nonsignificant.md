# Virtual patient blended learning shows a moderate but non-significant pooled skills effect versus traditional education alone (SMD=0.60, CI crossing zero)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The pooled skills effect of virtual patient blended learning versus traditional education alone is moderate in magnitude (SMD=0.60) but its confidence interval crosses zero. [→ Kononowicz A A 2019](#kononowicz-a-a-2019)

## Evidence

### Kononowicz A A 2019

Kononowicz A A, Woodham L A, Edelbring S, Stathakarou N, Davies D, Saxena N, Tudor Car L, Carlstedt-Duke J, Car J, Zary N. (2019). Virtual Patient Simulations in Health Professions Education: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis by the Digital Health Education Collaboration. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/preprints.14676

`q3 · i2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of blended-learning skills outcomes. The pooled "SMD=0.60, 95% CI −0.07 to 1.27" is described by the authors as moderate positive effects, though the interval includes zero; heterogeneity was high (I2=83%).

> "The pooled effect for skills outcomes (SMD=0.60, 95% CI −0.07 to 1.27, I2=83%, n=247; Figure 5) suggests that virtual patients blended with traditional education have moderate positive effects in comparison with traditional education alone."
