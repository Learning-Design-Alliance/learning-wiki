Evidence¶

Tan Jing 2021¶

Tan Jing, Mao Jie, Jiang Yizhang, Gao Ming. (2021). The Influence of Academic Emotions on Learning Effects: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18189678

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review attributes this to two included studies: an intervention study with graduate students (Marchand and Gutierrez, n = 185) and an observational study with high school students (Saito et al., n = 108). It reports the negative-emotion effect as "positive and significant".

"Marchand and Gutierrez [59] and Saito et al. [35] found that negatively activating academic emotions (e.g., confusion, anxiety, and anger) had a positive and significant effect on learning effects, but no significant effect on positively activating academic emotions (e.g., happiness) and passively passivating academic emotions (e.g., sadness and depression)."
