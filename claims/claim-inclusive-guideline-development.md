Pather MK, Mash R. (2019). Family physicians' experience and understanding of evidence-based practice and guideline implementation in primary care practice, Cape Town, South Africa. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. 11(1), a1592. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v11i1.1592

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Framework-method analysis of 27 semi-structured interviews with family physicians. Respondents wanted teams to include "representatives of district health services, primary care and patients, and not only so-called experts", and said primary care providers should be more involved in providing relevant research despite heavy workloads.
