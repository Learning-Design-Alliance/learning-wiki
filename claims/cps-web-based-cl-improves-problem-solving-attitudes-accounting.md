# Integrating the CPS strategy into a web-based cooperative learning environment produced significantly better problem-solving attitudes than web-based cooperative learning alone or traditional lecturing in an accounting course

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2011 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After 8 weeks of experimental teaching, students taught with CPS on web-based CL (EG1) scored significantly higher on overall problem-solving attitudes than students taught with web-based CL alone (EG2) or traditional lecturing (CG), as shown by a significant MANCOVA (Wilk's Λ=.83, p<.001) and post-hoc comparisons. [→ CHENG Kai Wen 2011](#cheng-kai-wen-2011)
`q2 i?` No significant difference was observed between EG2 (web-based CL alone) and CG (traditional lecturing) on post-test problem-solving attitudes, suggesting web-based CL without the CPS strategy did not improve problem-solving attitudes over lecturing. [→ CHENG Kai Wen 2011 (2)](#cheng-kai-wen-2011-2)

## Evidence

### CHENG Kai Wen 2011

CHENG Kai Wen. (2011). When Creative Problem Solving Strategy Meets Web-Based Cooperative Learning Environment in Accounting Education. New Horizons in Education, 59(1), 106-118. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ955493

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 162 first-year university students in three intact classes in a 4-year hotel management program. MANCOVA on post-test problem-solving attitudes with pre-test as covariate showed a significant difference (Wilk's Λ=.83, p<.001), and ANCOVA showed a significant difference in overall score (F=14.94, p<.001).

> "Using the pre-test results as the covariance, the result of MANCOV A on the post-test was shown that a significant difference could be discovered (Wilk's Λ=.83,  p < . 0 0 1 )."

### CHENG Kai Wen 2011 (2)

CHENG Kai Wen. (2011). When Creative Problem Solving Strategy Meets Web-Based Cooperative Learning Environment in Accounting Education. New Horizons in Education, 59(1), 106-118. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ955493

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same quasi-experimental study, post-hoc comparisons of adjusted post-test means showed no significant difference between EG2 (web-based CL) and CG (traditional lecturing) on problem-solving attitudes, while EG1 significantly outperformed both.

> "However, no significant difference was observed between EG2 and CG."
