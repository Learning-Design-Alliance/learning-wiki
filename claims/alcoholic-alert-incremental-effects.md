## Evidence

### Drost RMWA 2016

Drost RMWA, Paulus ATG, Jander AF, Mercken L, de Vries H, Ruwaard D, Evers SMAA. (2016). A Web-Based Computer-Tailored Alcohol Prevention Program for Adolescents: Cost-Effectiveness and Intersectoral Costs and Benefits. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.5223

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Incremental effects from the Alcoholic Alert cluster RCT at T1 (n=690). Control increased 1.51 glasses/week; intervention increased 0.78, yielding a mean incremental effect of 0.73 glasses per week. For binge drinking, intervention decreased 0.16 versus an increase of 0.33 in control, a mean incremental effect of 0.49 per 30 days.

> "In the intervention condition, there was an increase of 0.78 glasses, resulting in a mean incremental effect of 0.73 glasses per week."
