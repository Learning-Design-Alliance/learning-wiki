## Evidence

### Tiraboschi Gabriel Arantes 2025

Tiraboschi Gabriel Arantes, Garon-Carrier Gabrielle, Madigan Sheri, Smith Jonathan, Surprenant Rachel, Fitzpatrick Caroline. (2025). Longitudinal associations between gaming and academic motivation during middle childhood. Psychological Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0033291725101153

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-person covariance of the RI-CLPM random intercepts (boys β = .074, girls β = .056; both p > .05 per Table 2). The design separates stable between-person differences from within-person fluctuations, with each child serving as their own baseline.

> "For both boys and girls, we found no significant between-person associations between gaming and academic motivation."

### Tiraboschi Gabriel Arantes 2025 (2)

Tiraboschi Gabriel Arantes, Garon-Carrier Gabrielle, Madigan Sheri, Smith Jonathan, Surprenant Rachel, Fitzpatrick Caroline. (2025). Longitudinal associations between gaming and academic motivation during middle childhood. Psychological Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0033291725101153

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Intraclass correlation analysis in the sensitivity analyses section quantified the within-person share of variance for both measures, supporting the choice of a within-person change model.

> "Gaming ICC3,1 was 0.307 (95% CI = .276 to .338) and motivation ICC3,1 was 0.333 (95% CI = .302 to .364), both indicating high longitudinal within-person variability, which accounts for more than 60% of the variance"
