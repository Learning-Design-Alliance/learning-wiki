### Kim Erin 2025

Kim Erin, Tchinde Marius J., Rooney Deborah M., Ngam Blessing N., Rettig R. Luke, Gross Christopher L., Snell Mark J., Barnard Melanie L., El-Hayek Kevin, Jeffcoach David R., Kim Grace J. (2025). A novel tool for assessing psychomotor proficiency in laparoscopic cholecystectomy using simulation-based training. Surgical Endoscopy 39(5):3386–3395. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11708-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis comparison between the LMIC cohort (n = 27) and HIC cohort (n = 25) on summed CHOLE-VOP checklist scores. The article prints "P = 0.008" and characterizes "ɳ2 = 0.03" as little practical effect.

> "Across LMIC and HIC users, summed checklist scores were higher among LMIC users (M = 32.41) than HIC users (M = 29.35), P = 0.008, although little practical effect, ɳ2 = 0.03 (Table 3)."

### Kim Erin 2025 (2)

Kim Erin, Tchinde Marius J., Rooney Deborah M., Ngam Blessing N., Rettig R. Luke, Gross Christopher L., Snell Mark J., Barnard Melanie L., El-Hayek Kevin, Jeffcoach David R., Kim Grace J. (2025). A novel tool for assessing psychomotor proficiency in laparoscopic cholecystectomy using simulation-based training. Surgical Endoscopy 39(5):3386–3395. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11708-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item-level contrasts from the same LMIC vs HIC comparison (Table 3), where items such as cystic duct ligation reached P < 0.001 and P = 0.001. GRS summed scores also favored LMIC users (17.54 vs 15.14, P = 0.002).

> "The LMIC users outperformed on intracorporeal suturing and knot tying (Items 5, 6, 8, 9), transection of cystic duct (Item 10), dissection and handling of gallbladder (Items 11, 13), handling the common bile duct (Item 14), and safe removal of the gallbladder (Items 15, 16) (Fig. 3)."
