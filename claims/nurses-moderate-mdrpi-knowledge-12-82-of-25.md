Descriptive cross‐sectional survey of 217 nurses at a city hospital using the Turkish‐adapted MDRPI Knowledge Questionnaire (Cronbach alpha 0.706 in this study). The article reports the mean total score of "12.82 ± 2.99" and concludes nurses' knowledge level about MDRPI was moderate; no effect size is printed.
