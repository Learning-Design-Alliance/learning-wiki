### Silies K 2025

Silies K, Huckle T, Pohontsch N, Jarchow A-M, Schütz K, Müller M, Lühmann D, Balzer K. (2025). A new role profile for nurses with expanded competencies promoting person-centered care in long-term care: a mixed-methods intervention development study. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-025-06086-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Intervention development study synthesizing literature reviews, a multiple case study of five resident cases, two stakeholder workshops and a mini-survey with 13 raters. The authors report that "The new role profile comprises four competence areas" grouping 24 fields of action and 55 nursing tasks.

> "The new role profile comprises four competence areas: 1. Managing chronic diseases; 2. Empowerment and communication; 3. Person-centered care network; and 4. Organization."
