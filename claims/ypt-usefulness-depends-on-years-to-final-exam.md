## Evidence

### Boyka Aneva 2022

Boyka Aneva, Sergej Faletič, Mihály Hömöstrei, Péter Jenei, Isza Éva, František Kundracik, Assen Kyuldjiev, Thomas Lindner, Hynek Němec, Harald Puhr, and Martin Plesch. (2022). Development of Scientific Skills via IYPT: How does YPT participation lead to hard-skill development? ELTE University Budapest. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2212.09076](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2212.09076)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regressions on the student survey with final-year students as baseline; e.g. for high school physics the 3+ years coefficient is -0,690 (p = 0,001) and for numerical simulations -0,790 (p = 0,001).

> "With few exceptions, students that were three or more years away from their final exam considered YPT-related activities as less useful to develop their hard s kills than students who were closer to their final exams."
