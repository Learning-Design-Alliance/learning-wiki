The scoping review identified two modalities of educational interventions. One refers to the use of Information and Communication Technologies through the institution's website, where educational material was uploaded, accessible 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for four consecutive months. The other three articles applied in-person presentations delivered between 30–90 min.

> "Two educational modalities were noted: face‐to‐face classes utilising PowerPoint presentations and online training accessible 24/7 via institutional websites."
