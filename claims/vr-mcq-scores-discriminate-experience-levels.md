## Evidence

### Marie Høxbro Knudsen 2023

Marie Høxbro Knudsen, Niklas Breindahl, Tor-Salve Dalsgaard, Dan Isbye, Anne Grethe Mølbak, Gerhard Tiwald, Morten Bo Søndergaard Svendsen, Lars Konge, Joanna Bergström, Tobias Todsen. (2023). Using Virtual Reality Head-Mounted Displays to Assess Skills in Emergency Medicine: Validity Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/45210

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Experimental contrasting-groups study of 59 medical students in novice, intermediate, and experienced groups who completed five 360-degree VR emergency scenarios with integrated MCQs (maximum score 28). Mean scores were 14.24 (SD 4.48), 19.95 (SD 3.66), and 22.58 (SD 3.35); "ANOVA demonstrated a significant difference between all 3 groups (P<.001)".

> "ANOVA demonstrated a significant difference between all 3 groups (P<.001). The score of the experienced group was significantly higher than that of the intermediate group (P=.04), and the intermediate group scored significantly higher than the novice group (P<.001; Table 1 and Figure 2)."
