# Overconfidence (bias) decreased from pretest to posttest across all groups including control, with no group differences

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children reduced their bias scores, showing less overconfidence at posttest across the three groups, without significant variation between groups. [→ Kristin Kolloff 2025](#kristin-kolloff-2025)

## Evidence

### Kristin Kolloff 2025

Kristin Kolloff, Ebru Ger, Claudia M. Roebers. (2025). The role of metacognitive experience: Can feedback training affect kindergarteners' monitoring accuracy? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09422-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Conditional linear mixed model on absolute monitoring accuracy showed measurement point was significant (γ = −0.06, p = 0.03) but neither feedback condition was a significant main effect, so the decline was not attributable to feedback training.

> "measurement point was a significant predictor (γ = −0.06, SE = 0.03, t(214) = −2.18, p = 0.03). We found no main effects for MC Monitoring Feedback (γ = −0.01, SE = 0.04, t(214) = −0.28, p = 0.78)"
