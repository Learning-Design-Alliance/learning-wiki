### Ha Monica MD 2023

Ha Monica MD, Rowe Allyson MD, Hendrix Katlynn, Anwar Summer BS, Mang Khup BS, Wen Frances PhD, Coon Kim EdD, Bragg Jedediah MSW, PhD, Foulks Kristin MPH, Miller-Cribbs Julie MSW, PhD, Jelley Martina MD, MSPH. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative content analysis of transcribed encounter segments from the simulation videos, tabulated as frequencies. The article reports metaphor use as a continuance of the initial ACEs discussion in 89% of cases and facilitated smoother transitions.

> "The majority (81%) of the metaphor uses that began with general ACEs discussion were perceived as smooth, in comparison with 37.5% of conversations that were prompted by patient concern."
