Cabi 2018¶

Emine Cabi. (2018). The Impact of the Flipped Classroom Model on Students' Academic Achievement. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 19(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1185114

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Focus-group interview responses on out-of-class study behavior. The article reports the time allocations above and, in Table 7, 14 students who watched the suggested videos and 10 who revised and summarized topics; the authors interpret these as successful use of rehearsal and elaboration learning strategies.

"Out of 18 students, 16 stated that they spent one-two hours studying outside the classroom, 1 student reported spending three-four hours, the other student four-five hours."
