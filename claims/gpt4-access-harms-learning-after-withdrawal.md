## Evidence

### Mutlu Cukurova 2026

Mutlu Cukurova. (2026). What do you mean by 'human–AI collaboration'? Prerequisite functions and the affordances needed to achieve it. Submitted to the Handbook of AI and the Future of Education. https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5843-4854

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The chapter reports, citing Bastani et al., a randomised controlled trial with nearly a thousand high-school mathematics students, where "unrestricted access to a GPT-4 interface raised assisted practice performance by 48 per cent" while withdrawal left students 17 per cent worse off than never-users.

> "unrestricted access to a GPT-4 interface raised assisted practice performance by 48 per cent; yet when access was subsequently withdrawn, the same students performed 17 per cent worse than controls who had never used the tool"
