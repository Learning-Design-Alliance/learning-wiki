Oomen M. W. N., Hoekstra L. T., Bakx R., Heij H. A. (2010). Learning curves for pediatric laparoscopy: how many operations are enough? The Amsterdam experience with laparoscopic pyloromyotomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-010-0880-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective comparison of the first 35 versus the next 35 laparoscopic pyloromyotomy patients at two Amsterdam centers. The complication rate "decreased to 11.4% in the second 35 LP procedures (p = 0.041)", from 31.5% in the first group; no effect size is printed.

> "In the first 35 LP-procedures, complications were experienced by 31.5% of the patients, whereas this rate decreased to 11.4% in the second 35 LP procedures (p = 0.041)."
