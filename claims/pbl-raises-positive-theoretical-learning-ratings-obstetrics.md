## Evidence

### Chao Xiaopei 2025

Chao Xiaopei, Li Xiaoyan. (2025). The application of the conceive-design-implement-operate combined with the problem-based-learning teaching method in an 8-year rotation system for obstetrics students in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07646-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pilot study at PUMCH, September 2021 to April 2022; 91 students (49 intervention, 42 control) rated theoretical teaching on a 20-item 5-point Likert self-assessment questionnaire. The article reports "the proportion of students evaluating agree and totally agree from 68.5 to 92.2%" with P < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "In general, the PBL teaching method significantly increased the proportion of students evaluating agree and totally agree from 68.5 to 92.2% (P < 0.001) (Fig. 3)."
