## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Girls scored higher than boys on SAQ academic intentions and on ASQ positive regard and similarity components, but not on anxiety/avoidance. [→ Eleni Fleva 2014](#eleni-fleva-2014)

## Evidence

### Eleni Fleva 2014

Eleni Fleva. (2014). Imagined Contact Improves Intentions towards a Hypothetical Peer with Asperger Syndrome but not Attitudes towards Peers with Asperger Syndrome in General. World Journal of Education, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v5n1p1

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In the 416-participant ANOVA, girls scored higher than boys on academic intentions (η2= .02); for ASQ component 1 "girls scor[ed] significantly higher than boys [ M= 10.8 vs. M= 10.2, F (1, 400) = 12.6, p< .001, η2= .03]".

> "A main effect of gender was also found with girls scoring higher than boys [ M= 19.4 vs. M= 18.2, F (1, 400) = 11.1, p= .001, η2= .02]"
