İlknur Özpinar 2017¶

İlknur Özpinar, Semirhan Gö kç e, Arzu Aydoğan Yenmez. (2017). Effects of Digital Storytelling in Mathematics Instruction on Academic Achievement and Examination of Teacher-Student Opinions on the Process. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 10. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i10.2595

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Written opinion forms from the 30 experimental-group students after the application; 25 reported liking the course. Separately, majority (20 students) stated they want this application to continue in future mathematics courses, while 5 did not and 9 wanted occasional use.

"Whether the students liked the course with digital storytelling was first asked to the students. 25 of them told they liked the course while 5 of them did not like it."
