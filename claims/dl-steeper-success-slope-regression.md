## Evidence

### Öcal Mustafa 2025

Öcal Mustafa. (2025). The role of video and direct laryngoscopy in medical student intubation training: a comparative study on success rates and learning curves. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07239-y

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis of success rates across the three attempts in the mannequin study, shown in Fig. 3. The authors state these findings "suggest that VL has an initial advantage; however, DL closes the gap as the number of attempts increases".

> "DL Trend: The slope is steeper (slope = 0.215), indicating that the success rate increases more rapidly with the number of attempts. VL Trend: The initial success rate is higher (intercept = 0.531), but the rate of increase is slower (slope = 0.131)."
