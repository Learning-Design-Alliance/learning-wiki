## Evidence

### Zheng 2024

Zheng, Y., Shi, A. & Liu, X. L. (2024). A working memory dependent dual process model of the testing effect. npj Science of Learning 9:56. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00268-0

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Behavioral evidence from Zheng et al. examining the interplay between WMC and WM demands in the testing effect, using image-word pairs without feedback. The article reports that "low-frequency stimuli actually yielded a negative testing effect" for low-WMC individuals.

> "individuals with high WMC exhibited the testing effect irrespective of sti- mulus frequency. Conversely, for individuals with low WMC, testing enhanced retention only for high-frequency stimuli; low-frequency stimuli actually yielded a negative testing effect, where restudy proved more effec- tive than testing."
