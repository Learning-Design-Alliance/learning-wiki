### Markus Sebastian Feser and Ingrid Krumphals 2023

Markus Sebastian Feser and Ingrid Krumphals. (2023). Viscous behavior of fluids in the eyes of adults: A global survey. Preprint of a paper accepted for publication in The Physics Educator. [doi:10.1142/s2661339523500178](https://doi.org/10.1142/s2661339523500178)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive and paired comparison from the 406-participant survey's demographic/self-evaluation instrument (10-point bipolar rating scale). The printed effect size is d = 0.32 (with t(405) = 6.62; p < 0.001). Both self-evaluations were rated moderate: science in general M = 5.28; SD = 1.93; viscous behavior M = 4.61; SD = 2.32.
