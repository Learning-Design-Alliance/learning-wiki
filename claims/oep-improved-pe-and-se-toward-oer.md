# Engaging K-12 teachers in open educational practices significantly improved their perceived ease of use and self-efficacy toward OER, with small-to-medium effect sizes

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hengtao Tang 2021

Hengtao Tang, Yu-Ju Lin, & Yingxiao Qian. (2021). Improving K-12 Teachers' Acceptance of Open Educational Resources by Open Educational Practices: A Mixed Methods Inquiry. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10046-z

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest quantitative phase with 54 teachers completing both tests in a 16-week online course. Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed PE post-test median significantly higher, with the article noting effect sizes "range between small and medium" (r = 0.26).

> "Specifically, post-test scores for PE (Mdn = 12.00) were significantly higher than that of pre-tests (Mdn = 11.00), Z = 2.68, p = .01, r = 0.26."

### Hengtao Tang 2021 (2)

Hengtao Tang, Yu-Ju Lin, & Yingxiao Qian. (2021). Improving K-12 Teachers' Acceptance of Open Educational Resources by Open Educational Practices: A Mixed Methods Inquiry. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10046-z

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Same one-group pretest-posttest quantitative phase (N = 54 analyzed). Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed SE significantly improved, r = 0.25, an effect the article describes as between small and medium.

> "For SE, post-test scores (Mdn = 8.00) also significantly improved from pre-tests (Mdn = 8.00), Z = 2.54, p = .01, r = 0.25."
