### D. K. Keblbeck 2024

D. K. Keblbeck, K. Piatek-Jimenez, and C. Medina Medina. (2024). Undergraduate physics students' experiences: Exploring the impact of underrepresented identities and intersectionality. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020120)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding on the culture of exams in the Physics Department: "the average on most tests was a failing grade and then the professors would curve the tests accordingly", which students reported as discouraging even when doing okay. No effect size is printed.

> "For the vast majority of their courses, the average on most tests was a failing grade and then the professors would curve the tests accordingly. This set a really negative tone for the students, however, always making them feel discouraged and not smart enough"
