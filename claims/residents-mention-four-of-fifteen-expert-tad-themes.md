## Evidence

### Duan D  2025

Duan D , Gan MJS , Lim LZ , Foong KWC . (2025). Simulator practice of temporary anchorage device (TAD) insertion on inter‐radicular sites improves learning and confidence in dental residency. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13724

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Think-aloud protocol study of 18 residents randomly allocated to intervention and control groups, deductively coded against a 15-theme expert coding scheme with 98.5% inter-rater agreement. The article reports that "only four themes were mentioned by more than 50% of residents" in round 1.

> "During the first round of placement, only four themes were mentioned by more than 50% of residents (both intervention and control groups; Figure 3). In three themes, consideration was made about angulation, vertical and horizontal insertion points, and one postoperative evaluation was conducted on the depth of insertion."
