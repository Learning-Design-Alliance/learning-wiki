q2 · i? · design · r?

Pre-audit versus validated/repaired evaluation of GPT-5.6-Sol on expert-authored benchmarks, with corrected scores computed on retained subsets after expert review. The article reports the rises verbatim: "mean@4 rises from 47.3% to 78.7%" on HLE-Physics and "from 61.0% to 87.2%" on CMT-Benchmark; no effect size is printed.

"On HLE-Physics, GPT-5.6-Sol's mean@4 rises from 47.3% to 78.7%. On CMT-Benchmark (Pan et al., 2026), it rises from 61.0% to 87.2%. On CritPt, the mean@5 over the 70 evaluated challenges is 32.3% before the audit, and the mean@4 over the 54 retained challenges is 87.5% after it, with a pass@4 of 94.4%."

q2 · i? · design · r?

CritPt corrected evaluation using reference solutions independently derived by auditors because official solutions were unavailable. The article reports "corrected mean@4 reaches 87.50% for GPT-5.6-Sol Max and 78.24% for Fable 5 High"; pre-audit pass@4 was unavailable.

"On the 54 challenges retained from the 56 audited, corrected mean@4 reaches 87.50% for GPT-5.6-Sol Max and 78.24% for Fable 5 High, while corrected pass@4 reaches 94.44% and 90.74%, respectively."
