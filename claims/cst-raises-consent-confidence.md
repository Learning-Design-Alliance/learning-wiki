## Evidence

### McCarrick CA 2025

McCarrick CA, Moynihan A, McEntee PD, Boland PA, Donnelly S, Heneghan H, Cahill RA. (2025). Impact of simulation training on communication skills and informed consent practices in medical students- a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07671-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous pre/post questionnaire of intervention-group students; 49 of 61 (80%) completed it before and 54 (88%) after training. A Wilcoxon Signed-Rank test compared confidence levels before and after the intervention; Fig. 1 displays the confidence results.

> "significant improvement in students’ mean self-perceived confidence post intervention compared to that at baseline (p < 0.001)."
