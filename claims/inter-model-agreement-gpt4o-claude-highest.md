## Evidence

### Kim Hyun Jin 2025

Kim Hyun Jin, Jung Kiwook, Shin Sunghwan, Lee Wookeun, Lee Jun Hyung, Park Hee Sue, Choi Qute. (2025). Performance evaluation of large language models on Korean medical licensing examination: a three-year comparative analysis. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-20066-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-model agreement analysis on A-type KMLE questions. Complete three-model agreement was 64.0% (203/317) in 2022, 75.5% (237/314) in 2023, and 73.0% (227/311) in 2024; pairs involving Gemini 1.5 Pro agreed at 68.8–83.4%.

> "Pairwise comparisons revealed that GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet had the highest agreement rates, maintained between 74.1% and 85.2% across all years."

### Kim Hyun Jin 2025 (2)

Kim Hyun Jin, Jung Kiwook, Shin Sunghwan, Lee Wookeun, Lee Jun Hyung, Park Hee Sue, Choi Qute. (2025). Performance evaluation of large language models on Korean medical licensing examination: a three-year comparative analysis. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-20066-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of cases where all three models reached the same (wrong) answer on A-type KMLE questions. The printed incorrect-consensus rates declined from 10.8% to 6.3% to 4.8% across 2022–2024, which the authors read as improving reliability of agreement.

> "The rates of incorrect consensus showed a decreasing trend: 10.8% in 2022 (22/203), 6.3% in 2023 (15/237), and 4.8% in 2024 (11/227), indicating an improvement in the reliability of model agreement over the years."
