## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Kruskal-Wallis tests with Bonferroni correction showed constructively engaged students received higher post-test and final grades than those with active, passive, or interactive engagement (adjusted p ≤ 0.01), while pre-test and teamwork grades showed no significant differences (adjusted p > 0.05). ## Evidence

### Sánchez Judit 2024

Sánchez Judit, Lesmes Marta, Rubio Margarita, Gal Beatriz, Tutor Antonio S. (2024). Enhancing academic performance and student engagement in health education: insights from Work Station Learning Activities (WSLA). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05478-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis test with Bonferroni correction comparing assessment grades across ICAP engagement profiles in the 2020–2021 cohort: "students who demonstrated constructivist engagement received higher grades than those who demonstrated active, passive, or interactive engagement (adjusted p-value ≤ 0.01)". No effect size printed.

> "both the pretest (A) and the teamwork assessment (B) showed no significant differences in grades based on student engagement (adjusted p-value > 0.05). However, when the results of the post-test (C) and the final grade (D) are compared with the students’ engagement, the graph shows that students who demonstrated constructivist engagement received higher grades than those who demonstrated active, passive, or interactive engagement (adjusted p-value ≤ 0.01)."
