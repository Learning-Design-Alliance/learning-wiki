## Evidence

### Jae-Eun Russell 2026

Jae-Eun Russell, Anna-Marie Smith, Salim George, Adam Brummett. (2026). Impact of learning analytics-based personalized feedback: an experimental study on student behavior in a large introductory STEM course. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10728-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-semester survey ratings compared via ordinal regression; helpfulness OR = 0.92, p = .50 and motivation OR = 1.15, p = 0.31, both non-significant; the authors note self-report measures may lack sensitivity.

> "students rated the feedback messages as between moderately helpful and very helpful, with no significant differences between groups (control: M = 3.31, SD = 0.94; experimental: M = 3.26, SD = 1.01)"
