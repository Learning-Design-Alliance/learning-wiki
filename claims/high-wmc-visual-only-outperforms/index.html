Evidence¶

Swanson et al. 2013¶

H. Lee Swanson, Michael J. Orosco, Cathy Lussier. (2013). Does Cognitive Strategy Training on Word Problems Compensate for Working Memory Capacity in Children with Math Difficulties? SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563039

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Post-hoc comparison of adjusted posttest means at the high pretest WMC level (z = +.50), collapsed across ability group, in the randomized treatment study. Adjusted means were .68, .39, 1.12, and .26 for verbal-only, verbal+visual, visual-only, and control; the article reports "significant differences (ps < .05) in adjusted mean scores in favor of the visual-only condition".

"A Tukey test comparing the means yielded significant differences (ps < .05) in adjusted mean scores in favor of the visual-only condition (visual-only > verbal-only = verbal+visual=control)."
