## Evidence

### Ayal 2016

Ayal, C. S., Kusuma, Y. S., Sabandar, J., & Dahlan, J. A. (2016). The Enhancement of Mathematical Reasoning Ability of Junior High School Students by Applying Mind Mapping Strategy. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(25). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1115860
