### Aleknavičiūtė 2023

Aleknavičiūtė, V., Lehtinen, E., & Södervik, I. (2023). Thirty years of conceptual change research in biology – A review and meta-analysis of intervention studies. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100556

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 33 biology conceptual change intervention studies against traditional-teaching controls. The review reports that interventions show "large effects on conceptual understanding of biological topics" and that "refutational text being the most effective single type of intervention". No pooled Hedges' g value is printed in the supplied text.

> "the results of the meta-analysis indicate that conceptual change interventions result in large effects on conceptual understanding of biological topics when compared with traditional teaching, with refutational text being the most effective single type of intervention"

### Aleknavičiūtė 2023 (2)

Aleknavičiūtė, V., Lehtinen, E., & Södervik, I. (2023). Thirty years of conceptual change research in biology – A review and meta-analysis of intervention studies. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100556

> "Altogether, 33 studies with 3841 participants were included in the meta-analysis."
