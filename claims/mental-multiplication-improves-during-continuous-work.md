## Evidence

### Edward L. Thorndike 1911

Edward L. Thorndike. (1911). Repetition versus recall in memorizing vocabularies; The effect of continuous exercise and of rest upon difficult mental multiplication. Journal of Educational Psychology, Communications and Discussions. https://archive.org/search?query=Journal+of+Educational+Psychology+Thorndike

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Unsupervised home experiment with 72 undergraduate students doing continuous mental multiplication of three-place numbers for 100 to 140 minutes, grouped by initial ability. The first group rose from .61 to .68 examples per minute and right figures from .86 to .87; the second rose from .36 to .38 examples with 80% to 83% right figures.

> "The first group averaging .61 of an example per minute in the first row did .68 examples per minute in the last row of the work period. The percentage of right figures in the answers rose from .86 to .87."
