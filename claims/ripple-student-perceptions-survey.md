## Evidence

### Hassan Khosravi 2019

Hassan Khosravi, Kirsty Kitto, Joseph Jay Williams. (2019). RiPPLE: A Crowdsourced Adaptive Platform for Recommendation of Learning Activities. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.18608/jla.2019.63.12](https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2019.63.12)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-week-7 survey advertised on Blackboard, 55 respondents, five-point Likert items on creation and evaluation of learning activities. Agreement exceeded disagreement on all four creation/evaluation activities reported.

> "Students reported that there were positive contributions to their learning from creating questions (63% agreement vs. 13% disagreement), writing solutions (63% agreement vs. 13% disagreement), answering questions (67% agreement vs. 12% disagreement), and rating questions (56% agreement vs. 16% disagreement)."

### Hassan Khosravi 2019 (2)

Hassan Khosravi, Kirsty Kitto, Joseph Jay Williams. (2019). RiPPLE: A Crowdsourced Adaptive Platform for Recommendation of Learning Activities. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.18608/jla.2019.63.12](https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2019.63.12)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey items on learner modelling and recommendation from the same 55-respondent survey; the majority also indicated the "Recommend Question" feature helped them find effective questions (62% agreement vs. 11% disagreement).

> "Students reported that the open learner model increased their motivation to study or further use the platform (64% agreement vs. 15% disagreement) and increased their trust in the system (62% agreement vs. 14% disagreement)."

### Hassan Khosravi 2019 (3)

Hassan Khosravi, Kirsty Kitto, Joseph Jay Williams. (2019). RiPPLE: A Crowdsourced Adaptive Platform for Recommendation of Learning Activities. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.18608/jla.2019.63.12](https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2019.63.12)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey items on platform effectiveness from the same survey; most students also reported RiPPLE stimulated more effective study (62% vs. 15%) and they would like to use it in other courses (52% vs. 17%).

> "Most students reported that they found the platform easy to use and navigate (73% agreement vs. 8% disagreement) and that it made them more aware of their current knowledge state (69% agreement vs. 13% disagreement)."
