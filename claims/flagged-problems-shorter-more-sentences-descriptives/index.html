Evidence¶

Kole Norberg 2025¶

Kole Norberg, Husni Almoubayyed, and Stephen Fancsali. (2025). Linguistic Features Predicting Math Word Problem Readability Among Less-Skilled Readers. Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Educational Data Mining, Palermo, Italy. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15870274

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive statistics (Table A.1) comparing flagged and non-flagged problems on all metrics. The article notes these are mean differences only: "mean differences may mask more complex patterns in the data." No significance test or effect size is reported for these contrasts.

"others demonstrated larger differences, particularly in structural elements like word count ( M =82 for non-flagged problems vs. M =70 for flagged) and sentence count ( M =5.5 for flagged problems vs. M =4.8 for non-flagged problems."
