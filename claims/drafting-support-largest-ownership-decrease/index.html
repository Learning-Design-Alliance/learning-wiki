q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-planned contrast C2 from the between-subjects experiment (n=253) on prompt-adjusted ownership means: "assistance during drafting lowers ownership substantially more than assistance during planning or revision." Only the test statistic and p-value were printed; no standardized effect size.

"C2(ai–draftvs. the average ofai–planandai–revision) was −1.65points (𝑝<. 001), showing that assistance during drafting lowers ownership substantially more than assistance during planning or revision."

Pre-planned contrast C3 directly comparing ai plan to ai revision on the ownership outcome in the same n=253 experiment, indicating "planning preserves more ownership than revision." No standardized effect size was printed.

Related Claims¶

  • Essay quality was highest for AI-generated drafts and lowest with no AI, negatively correlated with ownership, suggesting an ownership-quality tradeoff. — related
  • Any AI writing support decreases feelings of ownership compared to no AI support, with ownership following a gradient by stage (highest with no AI, then planning, revision, and AI-generated draft lowest). — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • AI support shifts perceived attribution of ideas and text toward the AI, most strongly for AI-generated drafts, and an AI draft based on the writer's own outline contributed more ideas than expected (27%). — related
  • In this experiment, drafts—human-written or AI-generated—anchored the final essay, with AI-generated drafts revised somewhat more but underlying phrases largely retained across conditions. — related
  • Planning-stage AI ideas were often redundant with or rejected in favor of writers' own ideas: idea attribution was 17.8% among users versus 2.9% among non-users, but ownership did not differ between users and non-users within ai plan. — related
