"educators shared how the automatic speech recognition feature can benefit students by supporting their thought processes or providing them a safe space to practice speaking and then selecting a different voice output, which takes the weight off their accent."

  • Four themes of difficulty in textbook listening activities emerge for both groups: speech rate, speaker's accent, word meaning, and time pressure — related
  • Respondents anticipated using LLMs most often (23%), followed by NLP (17%), predictive AI (14%), chatbots (13%), and ASR (10%) — related

  • Automated speech recognition tutoring supports early reading in three areas: word identification, attention to meaning, and motivation — related
  • Measurable vocal outputs do not by themselves carry fixed pedagogical meaning; their significance depends on interpretation in context — related
  • Students recommended ChatGPT to peers and suggested improvements to accent recognition, visual aids, and response time — related
