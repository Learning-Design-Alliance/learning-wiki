## Evidence

### Peterson David A. M. 2019

Peterson David A. M., Biederman Lori A., Andersen David, Ditonto Tessa M., Roe Kevin. (2019). Mitigating gender bias in student evaluations of teaching. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0216241

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Balance tests on survey items (S1 Table) preceding hypothesis tests in the randomized experiment. The article reports "no evidence of imbalance," with proportions and means the same across conditions, and results unchanged when separated by instructor gender.

> "we evaluated the balance of the conditions for the other items included in the survey and there was no evidence of imbalance (see S1 Table). The proportion of male students and the mean levels of the students' class in school, the students' expected grade in the class, and their GPA were the same between the treatment and the control conditions."
