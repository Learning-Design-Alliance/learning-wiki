# No modality-congruence advantage: congruent DT and intelligence task modalities did not yield stronger correlations

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The intelligence–DT correlation was not stronger for congruent than non-congruent DT–intelligence task modalities, and the DT modality × intelligence modality interaction did not improve model fit. [→ Gerwig Anne 2021](#gerwig-anne-2021)

## Evidence

### Gerwig Anne 2021

Gerwig Anne, Miroshnik Kirill, Forthmann Boris, Benedek Mathias, Karwowski Maciej, Holling Heinz. (2021). The Relationship between Intelligence and Divergent Thinking—A Meta-Analytic Update. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence9020023

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression contrasting congruent-modality coefficients (k = 342, m = 67, N = 17,610) with non-congruent ones (k = 507, m = 83, N = 25,131); the article prints only β = 0.03, z = 1.58, p = .115, with no standardized effect size. The interaction model also failed to improve fit (χ2(6) = 1.68, p = .947), refuting the authors' congruence expectation.

> "The intelligence–DT correlation was not found to be stronger for congruent modalities of measures (β = 0.03, z = 1.58, p = .115)."
