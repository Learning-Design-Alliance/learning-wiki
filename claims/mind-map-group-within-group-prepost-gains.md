## Evidence

### Tanriseven 2014

Tanriseven, I. (2014). A Tool That Can Be Effective in the Self-regulated Learning of Pre-service Teachers: The Mind Map. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 39(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.14221/ajte.2014v39n1.1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired sample t-test analysis within the experimental group (n=30, df=29) in the same quasi-experiment; the printed table shows test anxiety decreased (t=3.104, p=.004) while strategy and motivation scores increased.

> "Table 3 shows there is a meaningful difference between the experimental group’s pre- and post-test scores at a p<.01 level for elaboration, organisation, metacognitive self-regulation, time and work environment, intrinsic goal orientation and test anxiety."
