Evidence¶

Vladimir Zotov & Eric Kramkowski 2023¶

Vladimir Zotov & Eric Kramkowski. (2023). Moving-Target Intelligent Tutoring System for Marksmanship Training. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00308-z

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Mixed-factor ANOVA of pre/post sessions in the virtual range experiment (n = 28, fourteen per condition). The three-way interaction was significant; the text adds that "the average gain in hit rates was around 15% (unlike 3–4% for easy-to-hit 100m targets)" in both conditions.

"The significant interaction of Condition x Distance x Training shows that MT-ITS was especially effective for distant targets (250m)."
