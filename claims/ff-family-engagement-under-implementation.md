Implementation analysis of family engagement in the 2019-20 scale-up found a mean of "1.6 times each month", with only 30% of families contacted at the intended frequency and no site meeting the 80% benchmark.

> "The typical student’s family was contacted 1.6 times each month (Table 6). Only 30% of students’ families were successfully contacted by FF staff at least two times per month (Table 6; Figure 3). None of the 14 sites met the 80% threshold requirement for full implementation (Figure 4)."
