Stoet G 2013¶

Cross-national correlational analysis of sex differences in all four PISA assessments, shown in Figure 2. The printed effect sizes are Pearson correlations r = −0.60 to −0.78 (printed as 20.60 to 20.78), all significant at p < .001; |r| ≥ .37 codes impact 3.
