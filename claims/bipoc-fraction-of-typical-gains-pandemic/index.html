All BIPOC groups made learning gains from fall 2019 to fall 2020, but Black, AIAN, and Latinx students made only 55-66% of their pre-pandemic math gains and at least 70% of their historical reading gains, whereas Asian American students made 88% of pre-pandemic math gains and gained equivalently in reading. → Megan Kuhfeld 2021
