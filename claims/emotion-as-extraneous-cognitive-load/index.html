Evidence¶

Plass 2019¶

Plass, J. L., & Kalyuga, S. (2019). Four Ways of Considering Emotion in Cognitive Load Theory. Educational Psychology Review, 31, 339–359. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09473-5

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument presented in the article's abstract as the first of four perspectives on emotion in cognitive load theory. The article offers this as a conceptual position, describing emotions as "extraneous cognitive load, competing for the limited resources of working memory."

"One perspective describes emotions as extraneous cognitive load, competing for the limited resources of working memory by requiring the processing of task-extra or task-irrelevant information."
