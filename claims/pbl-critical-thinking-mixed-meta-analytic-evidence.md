### Lin Chun-Chih 2024

Lin Chun-Chih, Han Chin-Yen, Huang Ya-Ling, Ku Han-Chang, Chen Li-Chin. (2024). Exploring a learning model for knowledge integration and the development of critical thinking among nursing students with previous learning: a qualitative study protocol. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06118-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The article's literature review attributes this finding to Gao et al.'s meta-analysis of PBL combined with mind mapping in nursing teaching. The review reports improvement in "theoretical knowledge, practical abilities, and self-learning skills"; no effect size is printed.

> "Gao et al. [16] reported that an approach that combined mind mapping with PBL improved undergraduate nursing students' theoretical knowledge, practical abilities, and self-learning skills."

### Lin Chun-Chih 2024 (2)

Lin Chun-Chih, Han Chin-Yen, Huang Ya-Ling, Ku Han-Chang, Chen Li-Chin. (2024). Exploring a learning model for knowledge integration and the development of critical thinking among nursing students with previous learning: a qualitative study protocol. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06118-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The review attributes this null conclusion to Manuaba et al.'s meta-analysis in first-year medical students, comparing PBL with "the conventional teaching method". No effect size is printed; the review reports the authors' own conclusion.

> "Manuaba et al. [17] concluded from their study that the PBL approach did not appear to improve critical thinking and problem-solving skills compared to the conventional teaching method."
