## Evidence

### Losen 2020

Losen, D. J., & Martinez, P. (2020). Lost opportunities: How disparate school discipline continues to drive differences in the opportunity to learn. Learning Policy Institute; Center for Civil Rights Remedies at the Civil Rights Project, UCLA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED608537

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of 2015-16 CRDC school-policing reporting, finding that "over 60% of the largest school districts" reported zero arrests, which the authors interpret as evidence of underreporting. Descriptive compliance review.

> "over 60% of the largest school districts (including New York City and Los Angeles) reported zero school-related arrests. The prevalence of zeros suggests that much of the school-policing data from 2015–16 required by the federal Office for Civil Rights (OCR) were incomplete or missing."

### Losen 2020 (2)

Losen, D. J., & Martinez, P. (2020). Lost opportunities: How disparate school discipline continues to drive differences in the opportunity to learn. Learning Policy Institute; Center for Civil Rights Remedies at the Civil Rights Project, UCLA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED608537

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Policy-compliance review of state report cards against ESSA's requirement to publish collected school-policing data, finding universal noncompliance as of July 2020. The report frames this as a major oversight failure.

> "As of July 2020, not one state had fully met ESSA' s state and district report card obligation regarding their 2017–18 school-policing data."
