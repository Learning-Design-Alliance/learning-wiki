# Charter math disadvantage varies across growth periods and narrows over time, with no significant math difference in 2017-18

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Charter students' math gaps versus TPS peers were 53 fewer days of learning in the 2014-15 and 2016-17 periods, 77 fewer days in 2015-16, and not statistically different in 2017-18. [→ CREDO 2019](#credo-2019)

## Evidence

### CREDO 2019

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in South Carolina. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-south-carolina-2019-2/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Period-by-period analysis of charter versus VCR learning gains across four growth periods, 2014-15 to 2017-18; the 2017-2018 math gains did not differ statistically from TPS peers, and the report calls the year-to-year trend positive.

> "During the 2014-15 and the 2016-17 growth periods, charter students had 53 fewer days of learning progress in math than their TPS peers in the respective periods, while the gap was wider in 2015-16, reaching 77 fewer days of learning in math."
