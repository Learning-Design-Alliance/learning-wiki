## Evidence

### Corazzini Luca 2021

Corazzini Luca, D'Arrigo Silvia, Millemaci Emanuele, Navarra Pietro. (2021). The influence of personality traits on university performance: Evidence from Italian freshmen students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0258586

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS results in Table 4 show a Female coefficient of 0.148 (standard error 0.043, p < .01) in the fullest specification with parental and residence controls. The article states "women obtain higher GPA scores than men."

> "According to a literature highlighting the existence of gender differences in academic performance, the results suggest that women obtain higher GPA scores than men."

### Corazzini Luca 2021 (2)

Corazzini Luca, D'Arrigo Silvia, Millemaci Emanuele, Navarra Pietro. (2021). The influence of personality traits on university performance: Evidence from Italian freshmen students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0258586

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analyses adding Big Five × gender interaction terms (reported in S2 Appendix) find no heterogeneous trait effects by gender, despite significant gender differences in trait distributions except openness (p = .689). The article reports effects "are not heterogeneous between men and women."

> "the results from regression analyses including interaction terms between the Big Five traits and the gender dummy in the specifications suggest that the estimated effects are not heterogeneous between men and women"
