## Evidence

### Jionghao Lin 2024

Jionghao Lin, Zifei Han, Danielle R. Thomas, Ashish Gurung, Shivang Gupta, Vincent Aleven, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2024). How Can I Get It Right? Using GPT to Rephrase Incorrect Trainee Responses. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00970

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U tests on expert Likert ratings of rephrased responses for the Giving Effective Praise lesson; GPT-4 median accuracy was 1 versus human median -1 (Table 8 mean accuracy 0.92 few-shot vs -0.36 human).

> "the accuracy scores of the rephrased responses generated using both GPT models (zero-shot and few-shot) were significantly higher than the responses rephrased by the humans ( p < 0.001)"

### Jionghao Lin 2024 (2)

Jionghao Lin, Zifei Han, Danielle R. Thomas, Ashish Gurung, Shivang Gupta, Vincent Aleven, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2024). How Can I Get It Right? Using GPT to Rephrase Incorrect Trainee Responses. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00970

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Responsiveness ratings for the same lesson showed no significant GPT-4 versus human difference; human rephrasings had higher variance (Table 8 mean responsiveness 0.44 for both few-shot and human).

> "we did not observe a significant difference between the responsiveness score of the GPT-4 rephrased responses and human rephrased responses; however, the human rephrased responses had a higher variance"
