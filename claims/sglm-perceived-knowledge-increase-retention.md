### Serhatlıoğlu Seda 2026

Serhatlıoğlu Seda. (2026). Student-generated learning materials as active learning: a qualitative study of midwifery students’ experiences. BMC Medical Education 26:1504. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09749-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of anonymous written reflection forms from 26 third-year midwifery students after a 10-week SGLM activity; the knowledge-increase sub-theme appeared in 23 of 26 reflections. One student reported: "learning by creating is much more lasting than just reading."

> "I learned details that I had previously overlooked because I had to research the subject in depth. I realized that learning by creating is much more lasting than just reading."
