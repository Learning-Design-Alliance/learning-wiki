## Evidence

### de la Fuente Jesús 2020

de la Fuente Jesús, Paoloni Paola, Kauffman Douglas, Yilmaz Soylu Meryem, Sander Paul, Zapata Lucía. (2020). Big Five, Self-Regulation, and Coping Strategies as Predictors of Achievement Emotions in Undergraduate Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17103602

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of Big Five factors and the Coping Strategies Scale (Table 2, n = 642). The article reports this opposite-pattern contrast between neuroticism and the other factors, with conscientiousness showing the weakest association with total strategy use.

> "While the neuroticism factor was associated positively with total emotion-focused strategies and not associated with total problem-focused strategies, the opposite was true of all remaining factors."
