Evidence¶

López-Faican 2020¶

López-Faican, L.; Jaén Martínez, FJ. (2020). EmoFindAR: Evaluation of a mobile multiplayer augmented reality game for primary school children. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103814

q2 · i? · design · r2

One-sample t-tests on the Meier, Spada and Rummel collaboration rubric applied to the collaborative mode of the paired-children study. Table 7 reports means such as technical coordination 0.88 and individual task orientation 1.30, all significant vs neutral except time management (mean 0.13, p = 0.272), because the design included no time limit.

"The results show that all the parameters, except for time management, present a significant statistical difference with respect to the neutral value (0), which leads to rejecting the null hypotheses H0h, H0i, H0j, H0k, H0l, H0n, H0o, H0p and accepting H0m."

López-Faican 2020 (2)¶

López-Faican, L.; Jaén Martínez, FJ. (2020). EmoFindAR: Evaluation of a mobile multiplayer augmented reality game for primary school children. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103814

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Observational rubric scoring of the 19 pairs during the collaborative mode. Other printed levels include mutual understanding good for 58% and very good for 11%, orderly and fluid communication for 75%, equitable task division for 59%, and successful synchronization of features for 78% of children.

"the parameter with the highest results is the Orientation of individual tasks (P2Obs9), since 95% of the children participated active ly in the search for the solution to end the game."
