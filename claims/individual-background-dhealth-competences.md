## Evidence

### Gerit Wagner 2025

Gerit Wagner, Mickaël Ringeval, Louis Raymond, Guy Paré. (2025). Digital health competences and AI beliefs as conditions for the practice of evidence-based medicine: a study of prospective physicians in Canada. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2459910

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM results for the 177-student survey show "individual background, characterized by gender and academic level, has a significantly positive effect on both components of their dHealth competences".

> "medical students' individual background, characterized by gender and academic level, has a significantly positive effect on both components of their dHealth competences (knowledge competences: β = 0.48, p < 0.01; experiential competences: β = 0.58, p < 0.001)."
