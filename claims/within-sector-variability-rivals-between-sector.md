# Variation among Engineering programs is as large as variation between Engineering and Technology programs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Content coverage in calculus and physics final exams varied distinctly among institutions within sectors, contradicting the notion that Engineering programs vary less among themselves than Technology and Engineering programs differ across sectors. [→ Zakani 2019](#zakani-2019)

## Evidence

### Zakani 2019

Zakani, S., Kaupp, J., Turner, R. D., & Frank, B. (2019). Analyzing implicit science and math outcomes in engineering and technology programs. The Canadian Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 10(1). https://doi.org/10.5206/cjsotl-rcacea.2019.1.7994

`q2 · i?`

Content analysis of final-exam question percentages per BCCAT content area per institution, summarized in Figures 1 and 2 for calculus and physics. Some institutions covered a wide range of content areas while others focused on fewer topics, showing "distinct variability amongst institutions even within sectors".

> "These findings contradict the notion of smaller variation amongst Engineering programs compared to the differences between Technology programs and Engineering programs."
