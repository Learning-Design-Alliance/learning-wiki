## Evidence

### Sattler David N. 2015

Sattler David N., McKnight Patrick E., Naney Linda, Mathis Randy. (2015). Grant Peer Review: Improving Inter-Rater Reliability with Training. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0130450

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial with 75 Public Health professors assigned to a training video or no-video condition; accuracy was the percentage of scores matching true NIH rating scale values on four proposal summary items. The trained group scored "74% vs. 35%" correct, linear regression p < .05.

> "Participants who received training were more accurate about the rating scale values than those in the no training program condition (74% vs. 35%, respectively, linear regression, p < .05; see Fig 1)."
