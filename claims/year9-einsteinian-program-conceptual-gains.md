## Evidence

### Kaur 2017

Kaur, T., Blair, D., Moschilla, J., Stannard, W. & Zadnik, M. (2017). Teaching Einsteinian Physics at Schools: Part 3, Review of Research Outcomes. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aa83dd](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aa83dd)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

Pre/post questionnaire study of 57 academically talented Year 9 students following a twenty-lesson Einsteinian physics program in 2014. The article reports that "53 out of 57 students scored 80% or above" after the program, and the conclusion states average class scores increased from 23% to 91%. No effect size is printed.

> "Student pre-test scores were very low, with less than 10% of students scoring more than 50%. It is interesting that some of the students who had the lowest initial scores achieved results as high as the students with the highest pre-test scores. After the program, 53 out of 57 students scored 80% or above."
