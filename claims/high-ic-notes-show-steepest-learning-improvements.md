Marc Deosdad-Díez & Josep Marco-Pallarés. (2025). Note-by-note predictability modulates rhythm learning and its neural components. npj Science of Learning 10:59. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00353-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LMM on synchronization-trial asynchrony across ten repetitions of each of nine rhythms with 70 participants. Asynchrony "decreased across repetitions (F(1,68) = 131.65"; the article also reports a repetition × IC interaction (F(1,56585) = 136.76, p < 0.001), with higher-IC notes showing steeper learning curves (Fig. 1a).

> "As expected, asynchrony decreased across repetitions (F(1,68) = 131.65,p < 0.001), indicating that participants learnt to perform more accurately through the task."
