Evidence¶

Rudd 2017¶

Rudd, P., Villaneuva Aguilera, A. B., Elliott, L., & Chambers, B. (2017). MathsFlip: Flipped Learning — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.isrctn.com/search?q=&filters=intervention%3AMathsFlip%2CfunderName%3AEducation+Endowment+Foundation&searchType=advanced-search

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Intention-to-treat multilevel modelling of the primary outcome, KS2 maths fine grade scores, with pupils nested in schools nested in matched pairs, controlling for KS1 maths scores. Table 4 reports Hedges g = 0.09 (-0.28 to 0.46), p = 0.62, n = 1,129 pupils (542 intervention; 587 control). The executive summary translates this as "one additional months' progress in maths, on average".

"The MathsFlip approach had little impact on the mathematics achievement of pupils at the end of KS2. It had more impact on the secondary outcomes, controlling for their KS1 scores (see Table 4), particularly on reading and writing."
