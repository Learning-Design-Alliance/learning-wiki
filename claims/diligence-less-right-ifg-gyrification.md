## Evidence

### Fuhrmann Delia 2019

Fuhrmann Delia, Schweizer Susanne, Leung Jovita, Griffin Cait, Blakemore Sarah-Jayne. (2019). The neurocognitive correlates of academic diligence in adolescent girls. Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1080/17588928.2018.1504762

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory surface-based morphometry of the right IFG (pars triangularis) in the same 40-participant structural MRI sample, using CAT12 and linear mixed models. The article reports "higher diligence was associated with less gyrification of the right IFG"; thickness and fractal dimension nulls are printed with their χ2 and p values.

> "We found that higher diligence was associated with less gyrification of the right IFG (χ2(1) = 10.50, p = .001, β = −0.62). There was no association between diligence and cortical thickness (χ2(1) = 0.49, p = .485) or fractal dimension (χ2(1) = 0.40, p = .530)."
