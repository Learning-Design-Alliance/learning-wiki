### David Cesarini and Peter M. Visscher 2017 (2)

David Cesarini and Peter M. Visscher. (2017). Genetics and educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 2:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0005-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review of the follow-up GWAS: in a combined analysis of discovery and replication samples (N = 405,072), "the number of independent loci further increases to 162", which the review calls qualitatively similar to medical and anthropometric traits.

> "In a combined analysis of the discovery and replication samples ( N = 405,072), the number of independent loci further increases to 162. Thus, educational attainment appears to follow a pattern that is qualitatively similar to medical and anthropometric traits."
