Evidence¶

Mohd Amiruddin Mohd Kassim 2025¶

Mohd Amiruddin Mohd Kassim, Sidi Muhammad Yusoff Azli Shah, Jane Tze Yn Lim, Tuti Iryani Mohd Daud. (2025). Online-Based and Technology-Assisted Psychiatric Education for Trainees: Scoping Review. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/64773

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review describes a study by Puspitasari et al comparing trainer-led and self-paced online behavioral activation training over 4 weeks: "There was a significant increase in behavioral activation skill assessment total scores in both groups, and there were significant between-group differences favoring the trainer-led online group at both the posttraining assessment and 3-month follow-up." The authors attribute the difference to the interactive component of trainer-led sessions.
