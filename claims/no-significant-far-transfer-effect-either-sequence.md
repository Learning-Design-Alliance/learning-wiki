# Neither instructional sequence produced significant far-transfer effects in either experiment

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Far-transfer performance did not differ significantly between I-PS and PS-I groups in either experiment, though trends mirrored the near-transfer results. [→ Cheng-Wen He 2025](#cheng-wen-he-2025)

## Evidence

### Cheng-Wen He 2025

Cheng-Wen He, Logan Fiorella, Paula P. Lemons. (2025). Does Instruction-First or Problem-Solving-First Depend on Learners' Prior Knowledge? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09993-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

General discussion of the two randomized classroom experiments: far-transfer Quade ANCOVAs controlling for prior knowledge showed no significant group differences in either experiment (Experiment 1: F(1,365) = 2.59, p = .108; Experiment 2: F(1,136) = 4.14, p = .44). The authors state "the difference was not statistically significant between the I-PS and PS-I groups".

> "the results had the same trend as near-transfer performance across the two experiments, but the difference was not statistically significant between the I-PS and PS-I groups"
