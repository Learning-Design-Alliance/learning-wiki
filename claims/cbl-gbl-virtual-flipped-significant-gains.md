# Both CBL and GBL in a virtual flipped classroom produce significant within-group knowledge gains over time

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Amini Mahtab 2026

Amini Mahtab, Amini Shahideh, Alizadeh Maryam. (2026). Flipped classroom showdown: game-based vs. case-based learning in pharmacy education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09754-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA with LSD post hoc tests on 56 fourth-year PharmD students randomized to CBL (n=29) or GBL (n=27); the GBL group's Test 1–Test 3 post hoc comparison reached p = 0.001 and Test 2–Test 3 p = 0.002, while the CBL group's Test 1–Test 3 comparison reached p = 0.006.

> "the CBL group demonstrated significant improvement between Test 1 and Test 3, whereas the GBL group showed significant improvements from Test 1 to Test 3 and from Test 2 to Test 3."
