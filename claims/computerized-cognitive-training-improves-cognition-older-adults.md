## Evidence

### Kueider Alexandra M. 2012

Kueider Alexandra M., Parisi Jeanine M., Gross Alden L., Rebok George W. (2012). Computerized Cognitive Training with Older Adults: A Systematic Review. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0040588

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of 38 studies of computerized cognitive training in cognitively healthy older adults, classified into three program types. The review concludes that "all three approaches to computerized training" appear to hold promise for improving cognitive abilities in this population.

> "Based on this review, all three approaches to computerized training – classic cognitive training tasks, neuropsychological software, and video games – appear to hold promise for improving cognitive abilities in cognitively normal, community-dwelling older adults who have a higher risk of cognitive decline as they age."
