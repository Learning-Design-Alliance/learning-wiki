## Evidence

### Scior Katrina 2014

Scior Katrina, Bradley Caroline E, Potts Henry W W, Woolf Katherine, de C Williams Amanda C. (2014). What predicts performance during clinical psychology training? The British Journal of Clinical Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjc.12035

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Across the seven cohorts, 12 trainees failed a placement or provoked serious concerns, 11 failed at least one case report, and 21 received thesis corrections. In the exam 4 regression, clinical experience negatively predicted statistics marks (B = −0.21, p = .034); the authors attribute this to trainees with more experience having taken longer to gain a training place.
