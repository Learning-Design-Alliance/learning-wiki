Evidence¶

Gross Megan 2014¶

Gross Megan, Buac Milijana, Kaushanskaya Margarita. (2014). Conceptual scoring of receptive and expressive vocabulary measures in simultaneous and sequential bilingual children. American Journal of Speech-Language Pathology. https://doi.org/10.1044/2014_AJSLP-13-0026

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

ANCOVA of English receptive conceptual scores (missed PPVT items re-administered in Spanish) with SES covariate; significant group effect (ηp2 = .16), the monolingual-simultaneous contrast not significant (p = .375) while sequential bilinguals remained lower, as quoted.

"A significant main effect of group was obtained using conceptual scores, F (2, 93) = 8.6, p < .001, ηp2 = .16. Follow-up comparisons revealed that the simultaneous bilinguals’ conceptual scores did not differ from the monolinguals’ scores, p = .375. Sequential bilinguals’ conceptual scores were significantly lower than monolinguals’ scores, p < .001, and simultaneous bilinguals’ conceptual scores, p = .008."

Gross Megan 2014 (2)¶

Gross Megan, Buac Milijana, Kaushanskaya Margarita. (2014). Conceptual scoring of receptive and expressive vocabulary measures in simultaneous and sequential bilingual children. American Journal of Speech-Language Pathology. https://doi.org/10.1044/2014_AJSLP-13-0026

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

ANCOVA of English expressive conceptual scores with SES covariate; significant group effect (ηp2 = .23), monolinguals outperformed both bilingual groups (both p < .001), so conceptual scoring did not close the expressive gap for either group.

"A significant main effect of group was obtained using conceptual scores, F (2, 93) = 13.7, p < .001, ηp2 = .23. Monolinguals outperformed both the simultaneous bilinguals, p < .001, and the sequential bilinguals, p < .001, but the difference between the two bilingual groups was not significant, p = .529."
