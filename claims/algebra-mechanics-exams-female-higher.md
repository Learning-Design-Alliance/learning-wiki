## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Female students outperformed male students on average on Exams 3 and 4 of algebra-based mechanics, with weak effect sizes (d = −0.127 and d = −0.159). [→ Matthew Dew 2021](#matthew-dew-2021)

## Evidence

### Matthew Dew 2021

Matthew Dew, Jonathan Perry, Lewis Ford, William Bassichis, and Tatiana Erukhimova. (2021). Gendered Performance Diﬀerences in Introductory Physics: A Study from a Large Land-Grant University. arXiv:2009.09536. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010106](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010106)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

T-tests on z-score-transformed pooled exam data from 4 instructors and 13 lecture sections of algebra-based mechanics; the article reports female students outperforming male students with weak effect sizes on the two later-semester exams.

> "Signiﬁcant diﬀerences were observed for the third and fourth exams with female students outperforming male students on average exam scores. The eﬀect sizes for both the third ( d =−0.127) and fourth (d =−0.159) exams were weak."
