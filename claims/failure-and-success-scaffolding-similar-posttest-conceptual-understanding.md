# Failure-driven and success-driven scaffolding before instruction produce similar posttest conceptual understanding

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Failure-driven scaffolding (nudging suboptimal solutions) and success-driven scaffolding (nudging optimal solutions via low-specificity heuristics) had similar outcomes on posttest assessments of conceptual understanding. [→ Sinha 2021](#sinha-2021)

## Evidence

### Sinha 2021

Sinha, T., Kapur, M., West, R., Catasta, M., Hauswirth, M., & Trninic, D. (2021). Differential Benefits of Explicit Failure-Driven and Success-Driven Scaffolding in Problem-Solving Prior to Instruction. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(3), 530–555. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000483

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Classroom intervention study (N = 221) comparing explicit failure-driven versus success-driven scaffolding during problem-solving prior to instruction, measured by posttest assessments of conceptual understanding. The article reports the two scaffolds "had similar outcomes on posttest assessments of conceptual understanding."

> "Results suggested that failure-driven scaffolding (nudging students to generate suboptimal solutions) and success-driven scaffolding (nudging students to generate optimal solutions by giving them heuristics with low specificity) had similar outcomes on posttest assessments of conceptual understanding."
