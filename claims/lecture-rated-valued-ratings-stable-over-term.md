## Evidence

### Hood 2021

Hood, S., Barrickman, N., Djerdjian, N., Farr, M., Magner, S., Roychowdhury, H., Gerrits, R., Lawford, H., Ott, B., Ross, K., Paige, O., Stowe, S., Jensen, M., & Hull, K. (2021). “I Like and Prefer to Work Alone”: Social Anxiety, Academic Self-Efficacy, and Students’ Perceptions of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-12-0271

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey ratings of contribution to learning (1–5 scale) from the N = 227 sample. The authors report ratings changed very little over the term despite instructors integrating more than one new EBIP, and suggest student beliefs may contribute to inertia against adopting EBIPs.

> "At the start of term, lecturing (mean 4.07 ± 1.07, 95% CI [3.88, 4.25]) was considered to be one of the practices that contributed the most to learning relative to the majority of other practices."
