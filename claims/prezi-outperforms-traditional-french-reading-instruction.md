## Evidence

### Al-Hammouri 2019

Samer Al-Hammouri. (2019). The Effect of Using Prezi on Al Zaytoonah University Students' Performance in French Language Reading Skills. International Education Studies, 12(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v12n1p128

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study of 128 French-specialization students purposefully assigned to one experimental section (64 students) taught with Prezi and one control section (64 students) taught the traditional way; a t-test on post-test scores found the difference "in favor of experimental group" with a reported probability value of 0.000.

> "indicating statistically significant differences between the arithmetic averages of the scores of the control groups and experimental group in the post-test in favor of experimental group."
