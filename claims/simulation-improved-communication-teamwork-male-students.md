Theme 1 subtheme 'Improve communication skills' from interview and focus group data. A focus group member reported that simulation improved clinical communication and ability to interact with colleagues and ask for help during neonatal emergencies.

> "My ability to communicate effectively in the clinical setting has improved through simulation. I am sufficiently knowledgeable to interact with other colleagues while performing my tasks once the situation occurs in the clinical setting."
