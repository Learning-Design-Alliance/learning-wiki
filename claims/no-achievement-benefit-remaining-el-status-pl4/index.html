Analysis of subsequent SBAC math and ELA scores for students in grades 3-7 in spring 2021 or 2022 who scored at ELPAC overall PL 4, comparing those reclassified (ELP4 RFEP) with those not reclassified (ELP4 EL) by census day the following year, with standardized scores and confidence intervals.
