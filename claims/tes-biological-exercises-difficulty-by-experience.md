Completion (not time) was the outcome for the biological-material exercises on swine stomach, scored complete or not against quality criteria such as submucosal-plane dissection without perforation. The quoted completion rates are printed in the Results; no effect size is reported.

> "the completion rate of round 2 of the dissection exercise and the suturing exercise was low for group A (16.7% and 0%), higher for group B (75% and 56.3%), while for group C, all participants have completed the exercises (100% and 100%)."
