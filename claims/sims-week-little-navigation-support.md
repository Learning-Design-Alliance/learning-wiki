### Allan 2026

Allan, H. T., and M. O'Driscoll. (2026). Using Recontextualisation Theory to Understand Learning Across Multiple Sites in Simulation‐Based Nurse Education. Journal of Advanced Nursing 82, no. 5: 5513–5520. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70163

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Mixed methods evaluation study (O'Driscoll et al. 2024) of a SIMS week design mixing online and face-to-face learning, with constructivist pedagogy, debriefing and reflection on emotions. Students' engagement was observed in expressed emotions of "shock, empathy and laughter" as they reworked knowledge to link theory with practice. No effect size is printed.

> "A significant finding was that while SBE is a learning environment, distinct from both university and clinical placements, there was little or no discussion or support for students in navigating between these environments."
