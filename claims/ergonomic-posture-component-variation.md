Leinonen J, Laitala M‐L, Pirttilahti J, Niskanen L, Pesonen P, Anttonen V. (2020). Live lectures and videos do not differ in relation to learning outcomes of dental ergonomics. Clin Exp Dent Res. 2020;6:489–494. https://doi.org/10.1002/cre2.300

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Component-level analysis of the photographic posture assessment of 17 students, illustrated in Figure 3, showed best performance on clock position and shoulder/upper-arm rest and worst on neck inclination, lumbar lordosis, upper body inclination and hip–thigh angle.

> "The students succeeded best in maintaining their working position “at 9 to 12 o'clock” in relation to the patient and resting position of shoulders and upper arms. The students succeeded least in the inclination of the neck, lumbar lordosis, inclination of the upper body and angle between hip and thigh (Figure 3)."
