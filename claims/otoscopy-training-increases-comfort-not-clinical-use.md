## Evidence

### Higgins Joyce Alanna 2019

Higgins Joyce Alanna, Raman Maya, Beaumont Jennifer L., Heiman Heather, Adler Mark, Schmidt Suzanne M. (2019). A survey comparison of educational interventions for teaching pneumatic otoscopy to medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1507-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-clerkship survey of 115 students across the three randomized interventions. Between 68% and 72% in every group reported being more likely to perform otoscopy after training, and the authors conclude training "can increase student comfort, but barriers exist to using the skill in clinical practice."

> "The majority of students in all groups (68–72%) indicated they were more likely to perform otoscopy after their training (Fig. 2)."
