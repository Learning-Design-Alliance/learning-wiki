## Evidence

### Yangqiuting Li 2020

Yangqiuting Li, Kyle Whitcomb, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). How learning environment predicts male and female students' physics motivational beliefs in introductory physics courses. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.li](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.li)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Structural equation modeling (CFA plus path analysis) of combined two-semester survey data; the final model showed good fit (CFI = 0.941, TLI = 0.932, RMSEA = 0.049, SRMR = 0.042). The article reports that "all three learning environment constructs predict students' self-efficacy and interest at the end of the course" as a predictive, not causal, result.

> "We find that all three learning environment constructs predict students' self-efficacy and interest at the end of the course even after controlling for students' pre-self-efficacy and pre-interest"
