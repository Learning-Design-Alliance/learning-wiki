## Evidence

### Li S 2023

Li S, Tang Y and Zheng Y (2023) How the home learning environment contributes to children's social–emotional competence: A moderated mediation model. Front. Psychol. 14:1065978. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1065978

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent variable SEM of parent-reported Home Learning Environment Questionnaire and teacher-reported CICESC data from 443 children in 14 kindergartens in western China. The direct-effects model showed "a significant positive predictive effect on children's social–emotional competence" for both predictors, with good model fit (RMSEA = 0.055, CFI = 0.911).

> "both structural family characteristics (β = 0.64, SE = 0.07, t = 7.41, p < 0.001) and parental beliefs and interests (β = 0.72, SE = 0.10, t = 8.53, p < 0.001) had a significant positive predictive effect on children's social–emotional competence, and Hypothesis 1 was supported."
