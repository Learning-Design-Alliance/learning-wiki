## Evidence

### Yang Gao and Barbara E. Hanna 2016

Yang Gao and Barbara E. Hanna. (2016). Exploring optimal pronunciation teaching: Integrating instructional software into intermediate-level EFL classes in China. CALICO Journal. https://doi.org/10.1558/cj.v33i2.26054

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t test on pretest/posttest scores for the 20 students in Group 2 (software-only instruction): posttest M = 20.60, SD = 1.78 vs pretest M = 18.83, SD = 1.16; "t (19) = –8.33, p = 0.000, r = 0.89". No standardised effect size is printed for the gain itself.

> "With alpha set at 0.01, the paired samples t test was significantly different, t (19) = –8.33, p = 0.000, r = 0.89. After two weeks’ instruction using only the software, students had made significant improvement, evidencing the benefits of instructional software for general pronunciation quality."
