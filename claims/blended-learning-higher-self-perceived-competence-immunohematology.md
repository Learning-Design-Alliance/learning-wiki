## Evidence

### Saniee Nadia 2026

Saniee Nadia, Seidi Masoumeh, Amiri Fatemeh. (2026). Blended learning in laboratory science education: comparison of flipped classroom and traditional lectures in immunohematology training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09985-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Parallel-group randomized controlled study with 196 students. Self-perceived laboratory competence assessed two months after course completion using an 11-item checklist (Cronbach's alpha = 0.75) found significantly higher scores in the intervention group, with effect size r = 0.870.

> "For self-perceived laboratory competence, the mean ± SD scores were 31.130 ± 1.894 in the control group and 39.823 ± 1.522 in the intervention group. This difference was statistically significant, with the intervention group demonstrating a higher mean self-perceived laboratory competence score (P-value < 0.001)."
