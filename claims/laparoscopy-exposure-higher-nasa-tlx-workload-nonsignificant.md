## Evidence

### Nasit V 2021

Nasit V, Vongsurbchart T, Myszka A, Richter P, Rogula T. (2021). Medical student experience with robot-assisted surgery after limited laparoscopy exposure. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-020-01129-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

NASA-TLX survey administered immediately after robotic tasks in the same 23-student quasi-experiment. Descriptive means show a rising trend across training groups, but the article states "increased workload with training was not statistically significant (p > 0.05)"; no effect size printed.

> "Subjects in the 0 min, 20 min, and 40 min groups reported workloads of 10.03 ± 2.21, 10.68 ± 1.32, and 11.73 ± 1.75 out of 15, respectively. However, increased workload with training was not statistically significant (p > 0.05)."
