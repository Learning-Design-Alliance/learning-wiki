### Watowicz Rosanna P. 2024

Watowicz Rosanna P., Krishnan Varsha, Butsch W. Scott. (2024). A cross-sectional survey benchmarking the coverage and importance of obesity-related competencies in U.S. dietetic supervised practice programs. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11622600/

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Thematic content analysis of an open-ended survey question, performed post hoc without an a priori framework. Several programs expressed a desire to teach "weight-neutral/non-stigmatizing approaches, including trauma-informed approach and intuitive eating"; one respondent stated the competencies "run counter-intuitive to the principles of Intuitive Eating".

> "However, we identified two overarching themes in our qualitative data: approach to obesity education and treatment, and barriers to obesity education."
