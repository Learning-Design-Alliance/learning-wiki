## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Assessing Student Expertise in Introductory Physics with Isomorphic Problems, Part II: Effect of Some Potential Factors on Problem Solving and Transfer. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07678](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07678)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 3 follow-up administration added questions (17) and (19) asking students to identify correct free-body diagrams for questions (18) and (20). The strong friction misconception still prevented transfer; about 40% believed friction had magnitude μsN and about 30% μkN on question (19).

> "Although the performance improved somewhat when stu- dents were also asked about the free body diagrams (Ta- ble 1 presents data for the case when students were given questions (17)-(20)), it is not signiﬁcantly different from when they were only asked question (20)."
