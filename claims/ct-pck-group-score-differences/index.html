One-way ANOVA and descriptive comparison of survey scores across teacher characteristics (figure 2; group n's 14–88). Differences significant at p < .05; emergency-certified teachers scored "an average of 14 percentage points lower than teachers with traditional teaching certification."

"teachers who identified as White, teachers who identified as female, and teachers with five or more years of teaching experience scored an average of 14 percentage points higher on the survey than teachers not from those groups"
