Per-protocol analysis of the randomized controlled trial's primary outcome: LP success rates in the simulation group (13/28, 46.4%) versus the control group (10/25, 40.0%), compared with Fisher exact test, "p = 0.85". No effect size was printed.

> "The primary outcome, the success rates of LPs, were comparable between the two groups, with 13/28 (46.4%) in the simulation group and 10/25 (40.0%) in the control group (p = 0.85)."
