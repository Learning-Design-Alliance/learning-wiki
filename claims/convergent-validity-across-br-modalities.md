## Evidence

### Zhang Y 2026

Zhang Y, Sun J, Marshall K, Mitchell JP, Laprade J, Nederveen JP, Helli PB, Rebalka IA. (2026). A preliminary comparison of bellringer performance across three visual modalities for the assessment of anatomy knowledge. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70145

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pairwise Pearson's correlations of student sub-scores on physical, 2D, and VR bellringer items in the mock exam. All three pairwise correlations were moderate and significant (e.g., r = 0.487, p < 0.001), which the authors read as "evidence of convergent validity among the tested modalities."

> "Pearson's correlation coefficient (r) revealed moderate significant relationships for all pairwise performance comparisons (VR to 2D r = 0.487, R 2 = 0.237, p < 0.001; VR to physical r = 0.522, R 2 = 0.273, p < 0.001; physical to 2D r = 0.5219, R 2 = 0.272, p < 0.001), providing evidence of convergent validity among the tested modalities."
