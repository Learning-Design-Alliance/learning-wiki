Cancela J, Burgo H, Sande E. (2019). Physical fitness and executive functions in adolescents: cross-sectional associations with academic achievement. J. Phys. Ther. Sci. 31: 556–562. https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jpts/31/7/31_17-478/_article

Multivariate linear regression in the 713-adolescent sample identifying which physical fitness components define each executive function domain. "Inhibition/Interference has shown an association with resistance strength (30-second chair stand test, β=0.22, p=0.039) and balance (BOT-2 balance Score, β= −0.87, p=0.035)."
