`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Lag2 robustness check extending the French RI-CCLPM with second-order paths capturing delayed carryover effects spanning two semesters. The lagged self-concept effect "disappeared when including the second-order path", though the lag2 path itself was nonsignificant, so the authors do not treat it as a genuine carryover effect.

> "In French, the lagged effect of self-concept on skills disappeared when including the second-order path (β2 =[ .028,. 033], p = .435), but the added CL2 path was also nonsignificant (p = .587)—even when estimated in isolation—suggesting it does not constitute a genuine carryover effect."
