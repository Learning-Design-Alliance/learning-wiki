Sarah Mehrotra 2021¶

Sarah Mehrotra, Ivy S. Morgan, Allison Socol. (2021). Getting Black Students Better Access to Non-Novice Teachers. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED617544

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Qualitative stakeholder-engagement case study: The Ed Trust–Louisiana team shared state-level data with nearly 40 teachers of color in New Orleans Parish and gathered their perspectives. Teachers reported that "working conditions were among the primary reasons" for leaving, and participants endorsed leadership pipelines and Grow Your Own investments.

"The teachers shared that working conditions were among the primary reasons why teachers of color decide to leave their schools."
