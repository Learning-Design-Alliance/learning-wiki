# Home literacy environment predicts early literacy outcomes, with parent print exposure predicting vocabulary and book-sharing frequency predicting letter knowledge

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The parent print exposure checklist predicted vocabulary (β = .18, 3.24% of variance) while book-sharing frequency was not significant. [→ Davidse 2010](#davidse-2010)
`q3 i?` Both book-sharing frequency (β = .15) and parent print exposure (β = .17) significantly predicted letter knowledge. [→ Davidse 2010 (2)](#davidse-2010-2)

## Evidence

### Davidse 2010

Davidse, N. J., de Jong, M. T., Bus, A. G., Huijbregts, S. C. J., & Swaab, H. (2010). Cognitive and environmental predictors of early literacy skills. Read Writ 24, 395–412. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-010-9233-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression Model 1 on vocabulary (N = 174) in a sample of Dutch junior kindergartners: "the parent print exposure checklist was a moderately strong predictor ( b = .18)"; book-sharing frequency was not significant.

> "the parent print exposure checklist was a moderately strong predictor ( b = .18) and accounted for 3.24% of the variance. The frequency of book sharing was not signiﬁcant."

### Davidse 2010 (2)

Davidse, N. J., de Jong, M. T., Bus, A. G., Huijbregts, S. C. J., & Swaab, H. (2010). Cognitive and environmental predictors of early literacy skills. Read Writ 24, 395–412. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-010-9233-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression Model 1 on letter knowledge (N = 174): "frequency of book sharing was a signiﬁcant predictor (b = .15)" and parent print exposure β = .17, both significant at p < .05.

> "frequency of book sharing was a signiﬁcant predictor (b = .15) as well as the parent print exposure checklist ( b = .17)."
