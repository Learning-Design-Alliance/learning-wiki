## Evidence

### Xie Wenjia 2025

Xie Wenjia, Yuan Zhouhang, Si Yuxuan, Huang Zhengxing, Li Yingming, Wu Fei, Yao Yu-Feng. (2025). Enhancing medical students’ diagnostic accuracy of infectious keratitis with AI-generated images. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07592-y

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Between-group ANOVA with post hoc tests across the three teaching groups after the interventions found no statistical differences in accuracy, printing Cohen's d = 0.3481 with CI -0.0853 to 0.7814. A second between-group comparison of accuracy improvement printed Cohen's d = 0.1578, CI -0.2733 to 0.589. Equivalence was not tested.

> "After teaching, there were also no statistical differences in accuracy among the three groups (ANOVA and post hoc tests, Cohen’s d = 0.3481, 95% CI -0.0853 to 0.7814)."
