### Salimi and Larsari 2015

Asghar Salimi, Vahid Nowrozi Larsari. (2015). On the Comparative Impact of Self-assessment and Teacher-assessment on Iranian EFL Learners' Academic Motivation. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 6(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.6p.74

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-test for the 30-learner teacher-assessment control group (Table 4.3 prints t = -.774, df 29, Sig. .445); the article reports "not statistically significant differences" between pre- and post-motivation scores. No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "A close look at the table indicated that the significance level is .44 >0.05, indicating that there are not statistically significant differences between the scores of the pre- and post-motivation tests."
