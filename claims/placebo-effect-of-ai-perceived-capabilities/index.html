The article cites HCI research on the "placebo effect" of AI as the rationale for keeping system behavior identical across conditions; the review-level assertion is attributed to that cited work rather than tested here, so it is reported as the article attributes it.

"This phenomenon demonstrates that users alter their expectations and behaviors based on perceived capabilities of AI, even when the underlying AI performance remains the same [6]."
