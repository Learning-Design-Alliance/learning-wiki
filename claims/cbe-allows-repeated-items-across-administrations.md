# Across administrations, the CBE may re-administer an item to the same student before the longitudinal exposure window elapses when the item bank cannot fulfill test requirements, which COLO does not allow

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` An item can be re-administered to the same student within the 14-month timespan on the CBE if and only if the item bank cannot fulfill the test requirements; this is not allowed on COLO. [→ Hu 2021](#hu-2021)

## Evidence

### Hu 2021

Hu, A., Chien, M., & Meyer, P. (2021). Comparability of MAP Growth tests administered through different technology and psychometric infrastructure: A simulation study. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/comparability-of-map-growth-tests-administered-through-different-technology-and-psychometric-infrastructure-a-simulation-study/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Longitudinal item exposure counts across administrations (Tables 4.16–4.19) were compared between engines. On the CBE the longitudinal exposure control was changed "from a constraint to a guideline" to prevent test crashes from item starvation.

> "an item can be administered again to the same student within the 14-month timespan if and only if the item bank cannot fulfill the test requirements. This is not allowed on COLO but is on the CBE due to the longitudinal item exposure control being changed from a constraint to a guideline to prevent the test crash as a result of item starvation."
