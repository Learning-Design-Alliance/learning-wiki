# Teachers valued interactive discussion-based training but identified unfamiliarity with online resources as a challenge when transitioning to distance learning.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers enjoyed interactive and discussion-based in-person training and appreciated opportunities to brainstorm and walk through activities. [→ Li 2020](#li-2020)
`q2 i?` Teachers identified their unfamiliarity with navigating the online teacher resources as a notable challenge during the transition to distance learning and requested more time to explore the websites during training. [→ Li 2020 (2)](#li-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Li 2020

Li, L., Luttgen, K., Huang, K., Weiser, G., Cornfield, O., Hess, H., Chow, E., & Schneider, M. (2020). Hero Elementary Playlist RCT Study in Formal School and Distance Learning Contexts. WestEd. https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/dd/index.asp

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Educator interview findings on the TPT Hero Elementary training. Teachers valued the "interactive and discussion-based facilitation" and walking through activities individually and as a group. No effect size is reported; qualitative perception data.

> "Many teachers enjoyed the interactive and discussion-based facilitation of the in-person training. Teachers particularly appreciated the opportunity to brainstorm with other teachers and ask the facilitator questions throughout the training."

### Li 2020 (2)

Li, L., Luttgen, K., Huang, K., Weiser, G., Cornfield, O., Hess, H., Chow, E., & Schneider, M. (2020). Hero Elementary Playlist RCT Study in Formal School and Distance Learning Contexts. WestEd. https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/dd/index.asp

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Teacher interview response on how training could be improved for distance learning. Teachers reported "getting to play around with the websites more" would have helped, reflecting unfamiliarity with online teacher resources. No effect size is reported.

> "I think maybe getting to play around with the websites more while we were there would have been helpful."
