### Talafian 2025

Talafian, H., Lundsgaard, M., Mahmood, M., Shafer, D., Stelzer, T., & Kuo, E. (2025). Responsive professional development: A facilitation approach for teachers' development in a physics teaching community of practice. Teaching and Teacher Education, 153, 104812. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tate.2024.104812

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The article describes how flexibility in uptake was a responsive feature added by attending to teachers' diverse backgrounds. Teachers commonly try a few lab activities in their first year, gradually increasing iOLab activities over time. A mid-career teacher described "the freedom and pace. You're just free to pick and choose."

> "In the IPaSS, unlike many others in which our teachers were involved, we did not require them to immediately implement all university materials into their classroom practices."
