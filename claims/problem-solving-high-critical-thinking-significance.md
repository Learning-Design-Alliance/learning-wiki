Descriptive cross-tabulation of central teaching component against significance of critical thinking (Table 7) across the 115 included studies. The review reports the contrast between problem-solving-centered and practice-centered interventions, with practice-centered ones "characterized mainly by low significance of methods addressing critical thinking".

> "High and very high significance was demonstrated especially for interventions that incorporated problem-solving as opposed to those with practice as the central component. The latter interventions were characterized mainly by low significance of methods addressing critical thinking"
