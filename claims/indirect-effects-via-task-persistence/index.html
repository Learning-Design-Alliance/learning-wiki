Viljaranta 2017¶

Viljaranta, J., Silinskas, G., Lerkkanen, M.-K., Hirvonen, R., Pakarinen, E., Poikkeus, A.-M., & Nurmi, J.-E. (2017). Maternal Homework Assistance and Children’s Task-Persistent Behavior in Elementary School. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Maternal+Homework+Assistance+and+Children%E2%80%99s+Task-Persistent+Behavior+in+Elementary+School

q3 · i0 · associational · r3

The model also showed "mothers’ help in grade 2 had a marginally significant indirect effect" on Grade 4 performance via task persistence, β = –.01, p = .08 — negligible in magnitude and only marginal in significance.

"also mothers’ help in grade 2 had a marginally significant indirect effect on children’s performance in grade 4 via children’s task persistence in grade 3 (standardized indirect estimate β = –.01, S.E. = .01, p = .08)"

Viljaranta 2017 (2)¶

Viljaranta, J., Silinskas, G., Lerkkanen, M.-K., Hirvonen, R., Pakarinen, E., Poikkeus, A.-M., & Nurmi, J.-E. (2017). Maternal Homework Assistance and Children’s Task-Persistent Behavior in Elementary School. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Maternal+Homework+Assistance+and+Children%E2%80%99s+Task-Persistent+Behavior+in+Elementary+School

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Null mediation result: "the indirect effect of mothers’ monitoring in grade 2 ... was not significant" (β = .01, p = .18). The reverse indirect effects from Grade 2 performance to Grade 4 assistance were also non-significant (p = .71–.72).

"the indirect effect of mothers’ monitoring in grade 2 on children’s performance in grade 4 through children’s task persistence in grade 3 was not significant (standardized indirect estimate β = .01, S.E. = .01, p = .18)"
