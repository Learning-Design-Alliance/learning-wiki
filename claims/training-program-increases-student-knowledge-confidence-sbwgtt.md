## Evidence

### Gillett Katrine E. 2026

Gillett Katrine E., Boysen Søren R., Harding Cole N., Finch Ashley J., Hillstead Madison E., Menard Julie. (2026). Teaching Clinical Competency: Evaluating POCUS‐Guided Versus Landmark Techniques for Thoracostomy Tube Placement in Canine Cadavers by Veterinary Students. Journal of Veterinary Emergency and Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/vec.70118

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre- and post-training paired ordinal surveys from 30 veterinary students in a crossover randomized study. The results section reports that "Confidence in the procedural skill placement of an SBWGTT also improved after training, from somewhat confident (median: 2, IQR: 1–2) to very confident (median: 4, IQR: 3–4; p < 0.0001)."

> "Students had a significant increase in perceived knowledge of the modified Seldinger technique, self‐reported as minimal knowledge pre‐training (median: 2, interquartile range [IQR]: 1–2) to knowledgeable post‐training (median: 3, IQR: 3–4; p ≤ 0.0001). Confidence in the procedural skill placement of an SBWGTT also improved after training, from somewhat confident (median: 2, IQR: 1–2) to very confident (median: 4, IQR: 3–4; p < 0.0001)."
