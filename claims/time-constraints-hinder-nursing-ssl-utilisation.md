## Evidence

### Phehla 2024

Phehla, L.B., Makhene, A. & Matshaka, L., 2024, 'Student nurses' utilisation of self-directed simulation learning at a University in Gauteng,' Curationis 47(1), a2490. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v47i1.2490

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative focus group study of 19 purposively sampled third-year student nurses in four virtual focus groups, analysed with Tesch's method. The analysis revealed one main theme that "student nurses experience time constraints, which hinder their utilisation of SSL".

> "The process of data analysis revealed one main theme: student nurses experience time constraints, which hinder their utilisation of SSL."
