Evidence¶

Tampke 2013¶

Tampke, D. R., & Durodoye, R. (2013). Improving Academic Success for Undecided Students: A First-Year Seminar/Learning Community Approach. Learning Communities Research and Practice, 1(2), Article 3. http://washingtoncenter.evergreen.edu/lcrpjournal/vol1/iss2/3

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Two-sample t-tests of fall-to-spring good academic standing (GPA at or above 2.0) for FYS (n=69), FYS/LC (n=165), and control (n=109) undecided FTIC students. Good standing: FYS 85.5% vs. 68.8% (t=2.7056, p<0.0075); FYS/LC 82.4% vs. 68.8% (t=2.5415, p<0.0118).

"The outcomes associated with both the FYS and the FYS/LC intervention show significant positive differences in GPA and percent in good standing in comparison with the control group."
