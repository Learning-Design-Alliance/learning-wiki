Yeung Jerf W. K.; Xia Lily L. L. (2023). Family and Individual Contexts of Middle-School Years and Educational Achievement of Youths in Middle-Aged Adulthood. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20043279

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Indirect-effect tests following the first structural equation model (model fit CFI = 1.00, RMSEA = 0.00, SRMR = 0.00) of LSAY cohort-2 data. The article reports "βind = 0.015, 0.012, 0.014, and 0.016, p < 0.01" for parental support, and significant indirect effects of educational expectations (βind = 0.045 to 0.048, p < 0.001).

> "Table 2 shows that grade-8 academic commitment of youths significantly mediated the effects of grade-7 parental support for college education on youths' grade-9 English, mathematics, science, and social studies grade scores concurrently, βind = 0.015, 0.012, 0.014, and 0.016, p < 0.01."
