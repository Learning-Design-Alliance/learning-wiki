## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wiggins et al. 2017

Meg Wiggins, Constanza Gonzalez Parrao, Helen Austerberry and Anne Ingold. (2017). Foreign Language Learning in Primary School: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581156

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Background evidence review in the report. The best related evidence came from two US retrospective cohort studies offering 30 minutes per school day (2.5 hours per week) of foreign language instruction; the trial was explicitly not designed to assess whether teaching an additional language affects English literacy, since some control classes also taught a foreign language.

> "No Randomised Controlled Trials (RCTs) have been conducted on this topic and no large -scale studies from the UK address this issue."
