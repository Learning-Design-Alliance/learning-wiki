### Nelson 2023

Nelson, C.R.; Gabbard, J.L. (2023). Pedagogical Design Considerations for Mobile Augmented Reality Serious Games (MARSGs): A Literature Review. Electronics. [doi:10.3390/electronics12214524](https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics12214524)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Incidence counts across the review's 23 included MARSG papers (Figure 2 and Supplementary Table S2) show search-based treasure-hunt mechanics dominate; the review reports "higher incident rates include search (17), points (13), goals/quests (10), teamwork (10)."

> "Among these elements, those with higher incident rates include search (17), points (13), goals/quests (10), teamwork (10), narratives (8), clues (8), collection (7), leaderboards (5), and creation (5)."

### Nelson 2023 (2)

Nelson, C.R.; Gabbard, J.L. (2023). Pedagogical Design Considerations for Mobile Augmented Reality Serious Games (MARSGs): A Literature Review. Electronics. [doi:10.3390/electronics12214524](https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics12214524)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-referencing Figure 2 incidence rates with the framework's theory assignments, the review observes that "only two of these elements are anchored under behaviorism" among the high-incidence elements, helping explain why only one work used behaviorist elements alone.

> "Notably, only two of these elements are anchored under behaviorism, while the remaining behaviorist elements are situated in the bottom half of the usage spectrum."
