Evidence¶

Lopata 2020¶

Lopata, C., Donnelly, J. P., Thomeer, M. L., Rodgers, J. D., Lodi-Smith, J., Booth, A. J., & Volker, M. A. (2020). Moderators of School Intervention Outcomes for Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Journal of Abnormal Child Psychology, 48, 1105-1114. https://link.springer.com/journal/10802

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Prior cluster RCT (103 children, 17 intervention and 18 SAU schools) with linear mixed effects model analyses; the article reports d=1.41 (p<.001) for CAM-C, d=-1.15 (p<.001) for SRS-2, and d=1.29 (p=.001) for ASC, favoring schoolMAX.

"Results of the linear mixed effects model analyses found the schoolMAX intervention was superior to SAU on the primary measures of social-cognition (emotion recognition testing by masked evaluators; Cambridge Mindreading Face-Voice Battery for Children, d=1.41; p<.001)"
