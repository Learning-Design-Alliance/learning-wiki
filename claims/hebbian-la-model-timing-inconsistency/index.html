Evidence¶

Paré 2017¶

Paré, D. & Quirk, G. J. (2017). When scientific paradigms lead to tunnel vision: lessons from the study of fear. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0007-4

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article's analytical argument against the 1990s Hebbian model: conditioning is most effective with the US offset 10–30 s after CS onset, yet LA CS responses are transient bursts returning near baseline, and prolonged stimuli evoke synaptic potentials that rapidly become sub-threshold. The article states "it seems unlikely that NMDA receptors are engaged when the US is presented".

"given the time course of LA activity and afferent inputs during the CS, it seems unlikely that NMDA receptors are engaged when the US is presented 20 –30 s after CS onset"
