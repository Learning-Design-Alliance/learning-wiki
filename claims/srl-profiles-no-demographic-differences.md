## Evidence

### Charlotte Dignath 2021

Charlotte Dignath. (2021). For unto every one that hath shall be given: teachers' competence profiles regarding the promotion of self-regulated learning moderate the effectiveness of short-term teacher training. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09271-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-squared tests on profile membership of the 191-teacher sample showed "the profiles did not differ for teachers' gender", age, or teaching experience, consistent with hypothesis 1.2 that profiles would not vary with background characteristics.

> "the profiles did not differ for teachers' gender, χ 2(1, N = 191) = 0.006, p = 0.94, age, χ2(1, N = 191) = 3.85, p = 0.43, or teaching experience, χ2(1, N = 191) = 53.33, p = 0.19"
