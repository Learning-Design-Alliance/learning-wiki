### Zambak 2017

Zambak, V. S., Alston, D. M., Marshall, J. C., & Tyminski, A. M. (2017). Convincing Science Teachers for Inquiry-Based Instruction: Guskey's Staff Development Model Revisited. Science Educator, 25(2), 108-116. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1132092

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple linear regression analysis testing the teacher change process among the 40 participating teachers. The article reports that change in context belief was predicted by change in instructional practice with a low to medium effect size, and that "β for the predicting variable was negative, which pointed out the inverse relationship between the dependent and predicting variables."

> "However, we discovered that change in participants' context belief was predicted by the change in teach- ers' instructional practice with a low to medium Cohen (1988) effect size. R 2 for this ﬁ nding is not very strong, which suggests that other signiﬁ cant variables are missing from the equation."
