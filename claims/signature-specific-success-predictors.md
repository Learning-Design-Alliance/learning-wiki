## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Some features correlate with success across all signatures while others show signature-specific correlations, such as BCPM uGPA predicting success for three signatures but not risers. [→ Baron Tal 2020](#baron-tal-2020)

## Evidence

### Baron Tal 2020

Baron Tal, Grossman Robert I., Abramson Steven B., Pusic Martin V., Rivera Rafael, Triola Marc M., Yanai Itai. (2020). Signatures of medical student applicants and academic success. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0227108

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis across signatures (Fig 3b, 3c) using Pearson correlations with the success index; features with absolute correlation coefficient of at least 0.1 shown. Examples include parents education score correlating for solids but not improvers, and MCAT attempts negatively correlated for statics but not risers.

> "for the risers, the uGPA in biology / chemistry / physics / and math (‘BCPM uGPA’) is not predictive of success, however for the other three signatures it is. Conversely, the improvement of the risers in these courses (‘BCPM uGPA improved—Junior year’) is correlated with their success, though it is not correlated with the success of the statics (Fig 3c)."
