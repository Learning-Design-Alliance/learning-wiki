## Evidence

### Freundt Paula 2023

Freundt Paula, Nourkami-Tutdibi Nasenien, Tutdibi Erol, Janzing Paul, von Ostrowski Thomas, Langer Martin, Zemlin Michael, Steinhard Johannes. (2023). Controlled Prospective Study on the Use of Systematic Simulator-Based Training with a Virtual, Moving Fetus for Learning Second-Trimester Scan: FESIM III. Ultraschall in der Medizin. https://doi.org/10.1055/a-1984-8320

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective controlled trial comparing 11 medical student novices with a physician reference group (n=10) on a standardized simulator test of 23 second-trimester planes; the trial group "reach the physicians’ RAI after 4 weeks, i.e., 8 hours of self-training".

> "They were able to reach the physicians’ RAI after 4 weeks, i.e., 8 hours of self-training (98.0±3.6% vs. 96.5±4.5%)."
