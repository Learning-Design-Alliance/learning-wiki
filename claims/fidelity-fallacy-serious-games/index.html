Evidence¶

Tripathi 2026¶

Tripathi, P., & Kapralos, B. (2026). AI-Enabled Serious Games: Integrating Intelligence and Adaptivity in Training Systems. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21962. doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.21962

q2 · i? · review · r2

The chapter reports, citing prior systematic reviews, that the assumption that more realistic environments would improve training effectiveness was contradicted by empirical evidence. In some cases higher fidelity "increase[s] extraneous cognitive load... without proportional learning benefit while escalating development costs."

"However, empirical evidence contradicted this assumption and has shown that increased fidelity (sometimes called the fidelity fallacy) fails to reliably improve learning outcomes [42]."
