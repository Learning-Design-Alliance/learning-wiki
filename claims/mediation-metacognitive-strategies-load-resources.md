# For metacognitive strategy use, resources competitively mediate the linear effect and cognitive load partially mediates the linear and mediates the quadratic relationship

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Resources mediated a negative indirect linear effect of difficulty on metacognitive strategy use (competitive mediation); no indirect quadratic effect was supported. [→ Seufert 2024](#seufert-2024)
`q3 i?` Cognitive load partially mediated the linear relationship and mediated an indirect quadratic relationship between difficulty and metacognitive strategy use. [→ Seufert 2024 (2)](#seufert-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Seufert 2024

Seufert, T., Hamm, V., Vogt, A., & Riemer, V. (2024). The Interplay of Cognitive Load, Learners' Resources and Self-Regulation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09890-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sobel-tested mediation in the hierarchical models: increasing difficulty directly related to increased metacognitive strategy use while exerting a negative indirect effect through decreased available resources; the indirect quadratic effect was not supported.

> "we found an indirect linear effect of perceived task difficulty on metacognitive strategy use mediated by resources (a × b = -0.12, z = -3.61, SE = 0.03, p < 0.001). Similar to cognitive strategy use, the pattern of direct and indirect effects revealed a competitive mediation effect."

### Seufert 2024 (2)

Seufert, T., Hamm, V., Vogt, A., & Riemer, V. (2024). The Interplay of Cognitive Load, Learners' Resources and Self-Regulation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09890-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sobel-tested mediation in the same models: cognitive load carried an indirect quadratic relationship and partially mediated the linear relationship between perceived task difficulty and metacognitive strategy use.

> "an indirect quadratic relationship was revealed for perceived task difficulty on meta-cognitive strategy use (a × b = -0.02, z = -2.32, SE = 0.01, p = 0.020). In addition, a partial mediation of the linear relationship by cognitive load was found (a × b = 0.10, z = 3.41, SE = 0.03, p < 0.001)."
