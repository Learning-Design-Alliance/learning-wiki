## Evidence

### Steele Justin 2026

Steele Justin, Haus Jeff, Renner Ann, Schmidt Cory, Synhorst David, Mariscal Joseph, Whyte Janae, Foxworthy Christian, Sisson-Smith Ryan, Priddy Alexis, Costello Jillian, Mischler Matthew. (2026). Using virtual reality or slideshow to teach ultrasound-guided lumbar puncture: immersion increases fun. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09611-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dual-site randomized controlled trial of 55 medical students (27 slideshow, 28 VR) comparing pre-recorded lectures; level of fun was the primary outcome. The article reports "Level of fun was rated higher in the VR group (4.5 [4.0, 5.0] vs. 4.0 [3.0, 4.0], p = 0.0002)"; no effect size printed.
