## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Using a 35-second cutoff, around 50% of students were in the 0-1 brief-attempt group and 10-15% made four brief attempts across the first four modules, a pattern the authors attribute at least partly to performance-avoidance guessing. [→ Kyle M. Whitcomb 2020](#kyle-m-whitcomb-2020)

## Evidence

### Kyle M. Whitcomb 2020

Kyle M. Whitcomb, Matthew W. Guthrie, Chandralekha Singh, and Zhongzhou Chen. (2020). Toward more accurate measurement of the impact of online instructional design on students' ability to transfer physics problem-solving skills. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.15051](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.15051)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Log analysis of clickstream data from two OLM sequences (rotational kinematics and angular momentum) in introductory physics at UCF, categorizing first attempts under 35 seconds as Brief Attempts. The article reports "around 50% of the students belong to the 0-1 BA group" and 10-15% in the 4 BA group; no effect size is printed.

> "we still identified 10-15% of the students who made four brief attempts the four modules (4 BA group). On the other hand, around 50% of the students belong to the 0-1 BA group."
