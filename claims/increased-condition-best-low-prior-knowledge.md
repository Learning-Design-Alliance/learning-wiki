## Evidence

### Rau 2010

Rau, M. A., Aleven, V., & Rummel, N. (2010). Blocked versus Interleaved Practice with Multiple Representations in an Intelligent Tutoring System for Fractions. In Proceedings of the 10th International Conference on Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS 2010). https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-13388-6_45

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the in vivo experiment, low prior knowledge students in the increased condition (blocks gradually reduced from twelve problems to one) showed significant representational knowledge gains persisting to delayed post-test, supporting the hypothesis that this condition yields best learning results.

> "For the low prior knowledge group, the results showed a significant improvement from pre-test to immediate post-test for representational knowledge in the blocked and increased conditions, which for the increased condition was also significant at the delayed post-test (ps < .05)."
