Multiple-choice question Q1 administered to 184 upper-level undergraduate students at four U.S. universities after traditional instruction; the correct response was selected by only 23% of students, with the most popular incorrect option C (statements 1 and 2 only) chosen by 30% (Table III).

> "Table III shows that, in response to question Q1, only 23% of the students provided the correct response option A. This low percentage is due to the fact that many students did not realize that all three statements listed in question Q1 are true."
