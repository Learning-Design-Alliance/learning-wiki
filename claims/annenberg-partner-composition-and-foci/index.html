Karin Sconzert 2004¶

Karin Sconzert, Mark A. Smylie, & Stacy A. Wenzel. (2004). Working for School Improvement: Reflections of Chicago Annenberg External Partners. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/working-school-improvement-reflections-chicago-annenberg-external-partners

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Descriptive profile of the 45 external partners' primary foci, presented with Table 4 of the report. Other partners focused on student learning climate and social services, parent and community support, or comprehensive agendas.

"About 55 percent organized their activities around curricular and instructional improvement."
