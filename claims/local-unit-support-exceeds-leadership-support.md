### Briseño-Garzón 2016

Briseño-Garzón, A., Han, A., Birol, G., Bates, S., & Whitehead, L. (2016). Faculty perceptions of challenges and enablers of effective teaching in a large research-intensive university: Preliminary findings. Collected Essays on Learning and Teaching, 9, 133-144. https://celt.uwindsor.ca/ceelt/vol9/brisenogarzon

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey participants rated support for improving teaching practices higher at the department/local unit level (27% strongly agree, 35% agree) than at the UBC leadership level (14% strongly agree, 45% agree), suggesting perceived support diminishes at higher institutional levels.

> "27% of the participants strongly agreed and 35% agreed with the statement "my local unit is supportive of faculty improving and changing teaching practices." When local unit is replaced by UBC leadership in the question, 14% of participants strongly agreed and 45% agreed with the statement (Figure 7)."
