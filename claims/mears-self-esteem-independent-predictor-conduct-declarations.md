### Paton Lewis W. 2018

Paton Lewis W., Tiffin Paul A., Smith Daniel, Dowell Jon S., Mwandigha Lazaro M. (2018). Predictors of fitness to practise declarations in UK medical undergraduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1167-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the MEARS subpopulation of entrants; self-esteem retained at p = 0.039 with 95% CI (1.02, 2.06). Univariable analysis showed for "every standard deviation above the mean scored on the self-esteem scale, an entrant had 40% higher odds".
