Komasawa Nobuyasu, Terasaki Fumio, Nakano Takashi, Kawata Ryo. (2020). Relationships between objective structured clinical examination, computer-based testing, and clinical clerkship performance in Japanese medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0230792

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Component-level Pearson correlation analysis in the same retrospective study of 94 students' OSCE, CBT and clerkship scores. The article reports "medical interview and chest examination scores were significantly correlated with CC scores" while the other five components were not; no effect size is printed for this component contrast.
