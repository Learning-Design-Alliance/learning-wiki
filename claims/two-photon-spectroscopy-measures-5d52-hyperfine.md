# Doppler-free two-photon absorption spectroscopy on the 5S1/2→5D5/2 transition yields accurate hyperfine splittings of the 5D5/2 level, e.g. δ[(1↔2)]87Rb = 15±1 MHz, using a simple laser diode

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Doppler-free two-photon absorption resolves all hyperfine levels of the 5D5/2 excited state (except Fe=0 for 85Rb), with fitted splittings including δ[(1↔2)]87Rb = 15±1 MHz and δ[(4↔5)]85Rb = 9±1 MHz. [→ V Jacques 2009](#v-jacques-2009)
`q2 i?` The ground-state hyperfine splitting measured via the two-photon transition is 3.03±0.03 GHz for 85Rb and 6.83±0.06 GHz for 87Rb, in excellent agreement with published values. [→ V Jacques 2009 (2)](#v-jacques-2009-2)

## Evidence

### V Jacques 2009

V Jacques, B Hingant, A Allafort, M Pigeard and J F Roch. (2009). Non-linear spectroscopy of rubidium: An undergraduate experiment. https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3115 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0902.3115](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0902.3115)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Undergraduate two-photon experiment at 778.1 nm detecting 420 nm cascade fluorescence (figures 6(c)-(f)); Lorentzian fits give the 5D5/2 hyperfine splittings. The article notes the precision is poor due to laser diode instabilities but agrees with precise published measurements.

> "Data ﬁtting with Lorentzian functions leads to δ [(1↔ 2)]87Rb = 15± 1MHz, δ [(2↔ 3)]87Rb = 22±1MHz,δ [(3↔ 4)]87Rb = 28±1MHz,δ [(1↔ 2)]85Rb = 5±1MHz, δ [(2↔ 3)]85Rb = 8 ± 1MHz, δ [(3↔ 4)]85Rb = 10 ± 1MHz, and δ [(4↔ 5)]85Rb = 9± 1MHz"

### V Jacques 2009 (2)

V Jacques, B Hingant, A Allafort, M Pigeard and J F Roch. (2009). Non-linear spectroscopy of rubidium: An undergraduate experiment. https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3115 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0902.3115](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0902.3115)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Ground-state splitting extracted from the Doppler-free two-photon spectrum (figure 6(b)), with laser-frequency differences related to energy splittings by a factor one half. The article reports "excellent agreement with other published values".

> "The ground state hyperﬁne splitting is then measured to be 3 .03± 0.03 GHz for 85Rb and 6.83± 0.06 GHz for 87Rb, in excellent agreement with other published values [29]."
