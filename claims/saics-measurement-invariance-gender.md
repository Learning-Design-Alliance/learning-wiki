## Evidence

### Thomas K. F. Chiu 2025

Thomas K. F. Chiu, Murat Çoban, Ismaila Temitayo Sanusi, Musa Adekunle Ayanwale. (2025). Validating student AI competency self-efficacy (SAICS) scale and its framework. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10512-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A multiple-group CFA across gender (male: 222, female: 226) tested measurement invariance of the SAICS scale. The baseline model fit well (RMSEA = 0.057, PNFI = 0.761, CFI = 0.916), and the article states the results "demonstrate that the measuring model is consistent across male and female students."

> "The model comparison analysis revealed that both models of invariant measurement weight (change in CFI = 0.000) and invariant structural covariances (change in CFI = 0.005) differed by less than 0.01 from the baseline model. These results demonstrate that the measuring model is consistent across male and female students."
