## Evidence

### Klassen 2025

Klassen, Robert Mark, Wang, Hui, Cutting, Joe, Thompson-Lee, Sophie, Snell, Rebecca J.S., See, Beng Huat, Saiger, Michael. (2025). Digital innovations in teacher recruitment: An experimental study. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105450

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Latent change SEM in Phase 1 of a randomized two-phase experiment (N = 957 UK undergraduates assigned to TeachQuest, RJP, or control game) found "greater increases in interest in teaching and perceived person-vocation fit" for both interventions, with self-efficacy gains only for RJP.

> "Results from the latent change model suggested that compared to the control group, both the TeachQuest and RJP groups reported greater increases in interest in teaching and perceived person-vocation fit. Additionally, participants in the RJP group reported a more significant increase in self-efficacy in teaching."
