### L. M. Rebull 2018

L. M. Rebull, T. Roberts, W. Laurence, M. T. Fitzgerald, D. A. French, V. Gorjian, and G. K. Squires. (2018). Motivations of Educators for Participating in NITARP, an Authentic Astronomy Research Experience Professional Development Program. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010148)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The article reports, citing prior work [17], that "teachers reported needing more step-by-step guid-ance, context, and support" after astronomy research PD, and that without such support teachers may become frustrated and temporarily disengage. No effect size is printed.

> "After completing an astronomy research PD, teachers reported needing more step-by-step guid-ance, context, and support; without such support, teach-ers may become frustrated and (temporarily) disengage from the process [17]."
