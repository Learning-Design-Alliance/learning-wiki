## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries 2018

Alexandru Maries, Nafis I. Karim, and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Is agreeing with a gender stereotype correlated with the performance of female students in introductory physics? Physical Review Physics Education Research 14, 020119. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.14.020119

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of 917 UC calculus-based students who answered the stereotype question before taking the FCI with the Pitt calculus-based cohort who answered after; the authors describe the trends as qualitatively similar across the two question timings.

> "The results are shown in Fig. 6 and are qualitatively similar to the FCI data shown in Fig. 4.O n e hypothesis for this similarity is that female students who believe in the stereotype that men generally perform better in physics than women may experience similar stereotype threat regardless of whether they are asked the gender stereotype question before or after taking the standardized test."
