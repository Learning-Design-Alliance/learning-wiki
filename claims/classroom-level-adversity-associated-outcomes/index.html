The essay attributes this to Abry et al. (2018), a study of 1073 elementary students and teachers: higher-adversity classrooms had teachers less effective at preventing disruption, more controlling instruction, and less time on academic instruction. Reported second-hand; design details beyond the sample size are not given here.

"In a sample of 1073 elementary school students and their teachers, Abry et al. (2018) demonstrated that teacher reported first-grade classroom adversity was negatively associated with aspects of high-quality classrooms, including observations of teachers' classroom management quality"
