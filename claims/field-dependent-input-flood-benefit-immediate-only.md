## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Field-dependent learners in the experimental group significantly outperformed field-dependent control learners on the immediate post-test but not on the delayed post-test. [→ Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan 2012](#elaheh-hamed-mahvelati-jayakaran-mukundan-2012)

## Evidence

### Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan 2012

Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan. (2012). The Role of Cognitive Style in the Collocational Knowledge Development of Iranian EFL Learners through Input Flood Treatment. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p105

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests comparing field-dependent experimental (n=17) and field-dependent control (n=18) learners, reported in Table 5. Immediate post-test difference significant at "p= .017< .05"; delayed post-test non-significant at "p=.949 >.05". No effect size is printed.

> "the field-dependent learners in the experimental group meaningfully outperformed the ones in the control group only in the immediate post-test (p= .017< .05), and the difference between their means did not remain significant in the delayed post-test (p=.949 >.05)."
