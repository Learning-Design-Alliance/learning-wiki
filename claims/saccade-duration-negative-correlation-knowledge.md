## Evidence

### Süleyman Özdel 2025

Süleyman Özdel, Can Sarpkaya, Efe Bozkir, Hong Gao, Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Examining the Role of LLM-Driven Interactions on Attention and Cognitive Engagement in Virtual Classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Regression analysis relating visual scanpath metrics to knowledge scores for the Double-Slit topic; considering only mean saccade duration on primary instructional content gave the printed effect size r(18) = −.61, p = .006, with R2 = .37, adjusted R2 = .34, F(1, 18) = 10.12, p = .005.

> "Mean saccade duration explained 33.6% of the variance in knowledge questionnaire scores, with R2 = .37, adjusted R2 = .34"
