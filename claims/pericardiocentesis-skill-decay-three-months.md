## Evidence

### Huang Sih-Shiang 2024

Huang Sih-Shiang, Lin Chih-Hsien, Lin Shao-Yung, Huang Chien-Tai, Lien Wan-Ching. (2024). How to enhance the novices' learning in ultrasound-guided procedures utilizing handmade phantoms? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06458-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same prospective cohort: novices observed no pericardiocentesis clinically during the 3 months after training. Global rating declined significantly (Table 3, p = 0.015); the article attributes the decay to the procedure's rarity and higher-stakes nature.

> "Without clinical observation, novices exhibited a decline in skill proficiency in pericardiocentesis at the 3-month assessment [3 (3–4) vs. 4 (4–4.5), p = 0.015]"
