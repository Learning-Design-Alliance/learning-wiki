## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2015

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Erin Ronayne Sohr, Ayush Gupta, and Andrew Elby. (2015). "Classical-ish": Negotiating the boundary between classical and quantum particles. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.023](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.023)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative video analysis of Episode 1 of a five-student focus group completing a particle-in-a-box tutorial. The authors code Chad's language and container gesture on 'bushel' as a classical particle ontology in which 'You can't have a particle with no energy.'

> "You can't have a particle with no energy. That's like saying I have a whole bushel of no apples."

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2015 (2)

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Erin Ronayne Sohr, Ayush Gupta, and Andrew Elby. (2015). "Classical-ish": Negotiating the boundary between classical and quantum particles. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.023](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.023)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Same Episode 1 video. Al counters Chad by invoking classical mechanics: a fist-gestured 'ball' in a well 'could just be sitting there' with no kinetic energy, gestures the authors read as consistent with a classical particle ontology.

> "No. What I think they're saying is the difference, like, you could imagine, like in a classical sense, like a ball [holds out fist to represent a ball], in a well. It could just be sitting there"
