## Evidence

### Shigen Shimojo 2025

Shigen Shimojo, Yugo Hayashi. (2025). Age-related differences in explanatory activities during collaborative learning with concept maps: experimental investigation using epistemic network analysis. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09456-5

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

rENA analysis (window size 7) of ICAP-coded discourse from the 52-participant experiment: "A significant difference was found along the X-axis" (Cohen's d = 0.75); the Y-axis difference was not significant (d = 0.00). Figure 7 shows stronger active–interactive connection for older adults and constructive–interactive for younger adults.

> "A significant difference was found along the X-axis between the older adults (mean: 0.07) and young adults (mean: −0.07) (t(43.18) = 2.69, p = 0.020, Cohen’s d = 0.75), but no significant difference was found along the Y-axis between the older adults (mean: 0.00) and young adults (mean: 0.00)"

### Shigen Shimojo 2025 (2)

Shigen Shimojo, Yugo Hayashi. (2025). Age-related differences in explanatory activities during collaborative learning with concept maps: experimental investigation using epistemic network analysis. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09456-5

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Follow-up connection-strength test in the Findings section: older adults' active–interactive connection (mean = 0.44) exceeded younger adults' (mean = 0.33), "Cohen’s d = −0.53", p = 0.042.

> "When the strength of connections between active and interactive in the older and student groups was examined using a t-test, a difference was found between the older adults (mean = 0.44) and young adults (mean = 0.33) (t(48.27) = −1.97, p = 0.042, Cohen’s d = −0.53)."
