# Both flipped and nonflipped ESL/EFL students showed statistically significant increases in self-reported grammar confidence over the semester

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The control group's grammar-survey confidence changed significantly over the semester (Z = 2.60, p = 0.009), with the median falling from 3.00 to 2.67 (lower scores mean more confidence). [→ Webb 2016](#webb-2016)
`q2 i?` The experimental group's grammar-survey confidence also changed significantly (Z = 2.92, p = 0.004), with the median unchanged at 2.67 and the mean decreasing from 2.85 to 2.54. [→ Webb 2016 (2)](#webb-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Webb 2016

Webb, M., & Doman, E. (2016). Does the Flipped Classroom Lead to Increased Gains on Learning Outcomes in ESL/EFL Contexts? The CATESOL Journal, 28(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1111606

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Nonparametric Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test on the four-item grammar survey (three retained items, reliability .75) administered in weeks 2 and 14 to both groups. The control median "reduced from 3.00 to 2.67." No effect size is printed.

> "A Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test showed that the in-class gram -mar lessons in the control group (in both US and Macau) did elicit a statistically significant change in students’ confidence throughout the semester (Z = 2.60, P = 0.009)."

### Webb 2016 (2)

Webb, M., & Doman, E. (2016). Does the Flipped Classroom Lead to Increased Gains on Learning Outcomes in ESL/EFL Contexts? The CATESOL Journal, 28(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1111606

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test for the experimental group on the same survey; the median had no change at 2.67 while the mean decreased from 2.85 to 2.54. No effect size is printed for this test.

> "Similarly, the flipped grammar les -sons in the experimental group also showed a statistically significant change in students’ confidence at posttest time ( Z = 2.92, P = 0.004)."
