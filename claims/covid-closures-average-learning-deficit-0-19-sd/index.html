Di Pietro Giorgio 2023¶

Di Pietro Giorgio. (2023). The impact of Covid-19 on student achievement: Evidence from a recent meta-analysis. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100530

q3 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects robust-variance-estimation meta-analysis of 239 effect sizes from 39 studies; the precision-weighted mean effect size is Cohen's d = −0.18613 with 95% CI [-0.316, −0.055], significant at p = 0.0065.
