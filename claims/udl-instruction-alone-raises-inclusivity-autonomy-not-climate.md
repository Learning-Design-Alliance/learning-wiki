## Evidence

### Katz 2013

Jennifer Katz. (2013). The Three Block Model of Universal Design for Learning (UDL): Engaging students in inclusive education. Canadian Journal of Education, 36(1). https://journals.sfu.ca/cje/index.php/cje

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Survey MANCOVA (Table 11) with 373 treatment and 257 control students: overall F(4,620) = 21.947, partial η = .124; inclusion/exclusion η = .085; student autonomy η = .049, both p < .001. Imputed means were used because almost 25% of data were lost with complete cases only.

> "Students in treatment classes reported significantly higher levels of social and academic inclusiveness and autonomy (see table 5).  Sense of belonging and classroom climate were not significantly different, however, this is an indicator of growth for treatment classes as there were pretest differences favoring control classes."
