`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire item on course difficulty in the three-edition evaluation, rated on the 1–6 scale; agreement was "acceptable (between 4 and 5)" and improved with adjustments between editions.

> "In response to the item "the level of the course was achievable for me", the level of agreement was acceptable (between 4 and 5; it improved by certain adjustments made between the 2016/17 and 2017/18 editions of the programme)."
