Zhang Guoyong 2026¶

Zhang Guoyong, Sun Xianghong, Yang Dan, Shao Qinwen. (2026). Nursing students’ experiences in virtual simulation teaching: a meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1863553

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic search of nine databases (inception to February 7, 2025) with JBI critical appraisal; 598 records screened to 17 included studies, all rated grade B. The review reports that "Sixty-five main themes and 156 sub-themes were extracted" and integrated into nine categories and four themes.

"A total of 17 research studies from eight countries were included in the meta-synthesis. Sixty-five main themes and 156 sub-themes were extracted, which were eventually integrated into nine categories and four themes"
