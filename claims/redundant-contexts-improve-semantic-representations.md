## Evidence

### Johns 2016

Johns, B. T., Dye, M., & Jones, M. N. (2016). The influence of contextual diversity on word learning. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-015-0980-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`
