### Pasqualotto 2025

Pasqualotto, A., Cochrane, A., Venuti, P. et al. (2025). Impaired audio-visual associations in dyslexia: evidence beyond linguistic processing. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00382-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Trial-by-trial accuracy from 157 children (52 DD, 54 age-matched, 51 reading-matched) fit with nonlinear generalized Bayesian mixed-effects models. Age-matched vs. DD mean = 0.505, CI95 = [0.37,0.64]; reading-matched vs. DD mean = 0.283 on log-odds scales, showing "higher asymptotic accuracy in the learning task than children with DD".

> "Across both waves, the combined results revealed that both the age-matched and reading-matched groups demonstrated higher asymptotic accuracy in the learning task than children with DD (age-matched vs. DD mean = 0.505, CI95 = [0.37,0.64]; reading-matched vs. DD mean = 0.283, CI95 = [0.146,0.43]"
