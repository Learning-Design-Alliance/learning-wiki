## Evidence

### Tomlinson M 2025

Tomlinson M, Rotheram-Borus MJ, Stansert Katzen L, Gertsch W, le Roux I, Dippenaar E, le Roux K. (2025). Relationship of perinatal outcomes to the competence and quantity of contact with community health workers. Journal of Global Health. https://doi.org/10.7189/jogh.15.04094

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression analysis of supervisors' summed competence scores (12 domains rated by at least two supervisors observing visits) on the same 13 outcomes; all printed P-values in Table 2 range from 0.105 to 0.998, none significant. "CHW competence did not affect outcomes" per the authors.

> "There was also no significant effect on maternal outcomes based on supervisors’ ratings of CHW competence (Table 2). Similar to the number of visits, CHW competence did not affect outcomes."
