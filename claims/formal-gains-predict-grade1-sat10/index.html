Evidence¶

Shanley et al. 2017¶

Lina Shanley, Ben Clarke, Christian T. Doabler, Evangeline Kurtz-Nelson, and Hank Fien. (2017). Early Number Skills Gains and Mathematics Achievement: Intervening to Establish Successful Early Mathematics Trajectories. The Journal of Special Education, 51(3), 177–188. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022466917720455

q3 · i?

Regression of SAT-10 total raw scores on the latent gain factors and TEMA-3 pretest, collected for 457 students midway through first grade. The model explained approximately 32% of variance, and "only formal early number skills gains were associated with SAT-10 scores, β = 17, SE = 0.07, p < .05."

"This model also demonstrated adequate fit—χ 2(1592) = 4,253.64, p < .001; RMSEA = .05; CFI = .51—and explained approximately 32% of the variance in SAT-10 scores. Again, only formal early number skills gains were associated with SAT-10 scores, β = 17, SE = 0.07, p < .05."
