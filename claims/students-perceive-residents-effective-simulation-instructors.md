### Niyongombwa Irenee 2025

Niyongombwa Irenee, Sibomana Isaie, Wallace Lorraine S., Stein David. (2025). Exploring medical students’ simulation learning experience in Rwanda: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07981-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured interview study of 15 final-year Rwandan medical students; in the residents-as-instructors theme every participant reported satisfaction with resident-led simulation, "noting competence, familiarity with learning objectives, and a relaxed learning environment."

> "All students reported satisfaction with resident-led simulation, noting competence, familiarity with learning objectives, and a relaxed learning environment."

### Niyongombwa Irenee 2025 (2)

Niyongombwa Irenee, Sibomana Isaie, Wallace Lorraine S., Stein David. (2025). Exploring medical students’ simulation learning experience in Rwanda: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07981-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same qualitative interview study, a student participant explained that residents have much knowledge but "expert opinion is always needed", so having residents together with faculty was seen as most beneficial.

> "“Residents have a lot of knowledge and skills ahead of us…However, expert opinion is always needed, so ideally, having both is more beneficial…” (Student 15)."
