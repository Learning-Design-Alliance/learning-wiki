### Kidayi Paulo L. 2025

Kidayi Paulo L., Dausen Eyeshope J., Ndile Menti, Sixsmith Jane, Mawona Zuhura M., Berntsen Karin, Manangwa Saturini E., Smit Joya M., Rogathi Jane, de Zeeuw Janine. (2025). Development of a training programme to improve health literacy and respectful compassionate care competencies among undergraduate student nurses: a quantitative study. BMC Medical Education 25, 348. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06894-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Baseline survey (n = 151) using an adaptation of the Clinical Learning Environment Inventory with 6 subcategories. Students rated subcategories in a range from 56.4% agreement (Personalization) down to 39.7% (Individualization), which had the highest disagree and neutral ratings.

> "When “strongly agree” and “agree” are added, the Subcategory “Satisfaction” measuring the students’ overall satisfaction with their clinical studies was rated 41.6% and 36.9% rated “disagree” and “strongly disagree”."
