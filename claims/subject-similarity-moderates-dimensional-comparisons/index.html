Evidence¶

Jens Möller 2024¶

Jens Möller. (2024). Ten Years of Dimensional Comparison Theory: On the Development of a Theory from Educational Psychology. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09918-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review of GI/E model studies comparing subjects along the math-verbal continuum. Contrast effects appeared for math-science comparisons, small assimilation effects for math, physics, and chemistry, and heterogeneous often nonsignificant effects when biology was included.

"DCT assumes that far comparisons, such as those between math and verbal achievement in the classic I/E model studies, lead to dimensional contrast effects."

Jens Möller 2024 (2)¶

Jens Möller. (2024). Ten Years of Dimensional Comparison Theory: On the Development of a Theory from Educational Psychology. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09918-6

q3 · i? · review · r2

Meta-analysis by Möller et al. (2020) of which subject combinations produce contrast versus assimilation effects. Between dissimilar subjects several combinations showed reciprocal contrast effects (math/L2, physics/L1, physics/L2); within the verbal domain (L2/L1) smaller but significant contrast effects emerged.

"Between similar subjects, they found lower contrastive or nonsignificant dimensional comparison effects, but no significant assimilation effects."
