## Evidence

### Fei Qin 2025

Fei Qin, Zhanxin Hao, Jifan Yu, Zhiyuan Liu, Yu Zhang. (2025). AI instructional agent improves student's perceived learner control and learning outcome: Empirical evidence from a randomized controlled trial. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Behavioral log data from the RCT: interaction frequency (student-initiated questions/feedback) and learning duration were compared across the three conditions. The duration ANOVA printed F(2, 122) = 265.168, p < .001, with AI and MOOC groups about 30 minutes shorter than the human teacher group.

> "the AI instructional agent group exhibited significantly
higher interaction frequency than both the human teacher and MOOC groups, F(2, 122) = 21.755, p < .001, with a
mean difference of 5.410 and 7.015 respectively."
