## Evidence

### Onódi Zsófia 2025

Onódi Zsófia, Riba Pál, Ferdinandy Péter, Görbe Anikó, Varga Zoltán V. (2025). Implementing the flipped classroom model to enhance knowledge retention in pharmacology: a local case study at Semmelweis university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06913-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Teacher Likert questionnaire (n = 19 respondents, 10 with flipped-classroom experience); the study reports 14 of 19 teachers agreed the flipped classroom is "a significant amount of extra work", with workload fear correlated with lack of student preparation.

> "Large number of teachers irrespective of their preferred method supposed that flipped classroom is a significant amount of extra work (Q7; answer: 4–5 / agree-strongly agree; n = 14); correlation was found between the fear of extra workload and lack of student preparation (Q8)."

### Onódi Zsófia 2025 (2)

Onódi Zsófia, Riba Pál, Ferdinandy Péter, Görbe Anikó, Varga Zoltán V. (2025). Implementing the flipped classroom model to enhance knowledge retention in pharmacology: a local case study at Semmelweis university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06913-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire and Spearman correlation analysis of teacher attitudes; the article observes a two-sided response in which teachers "with prior experience in flipped classrooms were generally more receptive", while concerned teachers preferred conventional teaching (Q16) and felt less motivated by student work (Q17).

> "Those with prior experience in flipped classrooms were generally more receptive to implementing this approach, while others favored more traditional, conservative teaching methods."
