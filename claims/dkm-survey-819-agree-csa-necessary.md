### Jeong A 2025

Nationwide cross-sectional survey on the necessity of including a clinical skills assessment in the national licensure examination for Doctors of Korean Medicine. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000045366

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 2221 licensed DKMs (8.7% of approximately 25,500 registered), conducted August 2022. The article reports "81.9% of participants agreed with the need to introduce CSA for NLE"; no effect size is printed for this proportion.

> "A total of 81.9% of participants agreed with the need to introduce CSA for NLE, 10.8% remained neutral, and 7.3% disagreed (Table 2)."
