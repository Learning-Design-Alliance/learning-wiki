# Agentic AI invalidates the three assumptions (evaluation, reversibility, control) on which existing AI literacy frameworks rest

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Existing AI literacy assumes evaluation is possible, that bad decisions are reversible, and that the user remains the agent of action; agentic systems break all three at once. [→ Nama 2026](#nama-2026)

## Evidence

### Nama 2026

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in a conceptual paper: the author analyzes how agentic systems invert the evaluative relationship assumed by frameworks built on Long and Magerko's definition, noting "Agentic systems produce action chains most of which the user never observes." No empirical data are reported.
