## Evidence

### Schütte C 2025

Schütte C, Teichert S, Schultz J-H, Wittenberg T, Herpertz SC. (2025). Interactional competencies in medical student admission at the Medical Faculty Heidelberg. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001768](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001768)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal re-assessment of the IC-MD pilot cohort: "123 participants from the IC-MD pilot study (2019-2021) were re-evaluated in their third year of study" in four communicative OSCE stations rated with the same manual. Overall correlation "overall (r=.40**)" with subscale correlations as printed; no effect-size beyond r reported.

> "Results showed a significant correlation between the IC-MD results before the start of the study and the interactional skills measured in the OSCE examinations, both overall (r=.40**) and for each of the subscales sensitivity (r=.41**), structuring (r=.42**), non-intrusiveness (r=.28**) and non-hostility (r=.26**)."
