Esse Ciego 2026¶

Esse Ciego, Shubbhi Taneja, Wilson Wong, and Amanpreet Kapoor. (2026). Student Perceptions and Preferences Regarding AI-Generated Instructional Videos in Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28203 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.28203

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Post-survey knowledge test with five multiple-choice questions on video content, from the descriptive study of 170 students. The authors state this "suggests that students learned fairly well about Markdown from the AI-generated videos."

"The average aggregate test score across the five Markdown quiz questions (possible range: 0-5) at institution 1 was 𝜇𝑖1 = 4.3(min: 1; max: 5; median: 5; 𝜎= 0.88) and at institution 2 was 𝜇𝑖2 = 4.3(min: 3; max: 5; median: 4; 𝜎= 0.62)."
