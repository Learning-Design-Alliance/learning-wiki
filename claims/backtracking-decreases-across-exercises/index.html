Fine-grained analysis of replayed usage logs (Table 1(a)) from 18 analyzed first-deployment submissions and 14 second-deployment submissions, splitting proofs into milestones. The printed percentages are descriptive; no effect size is reported.

"In the first deployment these went from 94% of students backtracking the first exercise to 50% of students backtracking in the last exercise. In the second deployment, it went from 33% to 0%."
