## Subclaims

## Evidence

### George Miley 2020

George Miley, Carolina Ödman and Pedro Russo. (2020). The Inspiring Universe. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.00456](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.00456)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Pilot case study in The Netherlands (2009-2010 and 2014) in which nineteen duos of student teachers and astronomy students developed and implemented astronomy lessons at primary schools in five cities; the success judgement is the article's printed assessment.

> "The combination of an astronomy student and a student teacher ensured that lessons were both scientifically correct and pedagogically sound. Moreover, the students learned from each other."
