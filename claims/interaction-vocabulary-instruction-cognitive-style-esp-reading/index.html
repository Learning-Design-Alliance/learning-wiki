Kusumawati 2017¶

Kusumawati, E., & Widiati, U. (2017). The Effects of Vocabulary Instructions on Students’ Reading Comprehension across Cognitive Styles in ESP. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(2). https://journals.iiste.org/index.php/JEP/article/view/2894

q2 · i? · causal · r1

In the 2x2 factorial analysis (Table 3.4), the EVI/FI cell had the highest mean (81.2500), followed by IVI/FI (71.7857), EVI/FD (69.7222), and IVI/FD (53.3333). The authors interpret this pattern as an “interaction among those variables.”

"Based on the descriptive of the data above, it can be concluded that the mean score for FI students generally is the highest. It means that there are interactions among those variables."
