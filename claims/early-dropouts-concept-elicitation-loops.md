First-order transition matrices over six core practices comparing N=252 early dropouts (20.0%) with N=635 completers (50.4%); 374 ambiguous sessions were excluded. The article attributes the loops to joint student-tutor behavior, since the tutor sets each exchange's practice through its scripted sequence.

> "From problem definition, 71% of their transitions led to relevant concepts (vs. 42% for completers). Once there, they stayed there: the concepts self-transition was P=.74 ."
