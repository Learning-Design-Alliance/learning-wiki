## Evidence

### Hew Khe Foon 2016

Hew Khe Foon, Tan Cheng Yong. (2016). Predictors of Information Technology Integration in Secondary Schools: Evidence from a Large Scale Study of More than 30,000 Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0168547

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical linear modeling of PISA 2012 data from 32,256 students in 2,519 schools across 16 OECD economies found that school IT resource availability variables (computers per student and IT resource access) were significant positive predictors of IT integration in mathematics lessons in the final model, with IT resources accounting for 5.91% of between-school variance.

> "students in schools with more computers per student (CompPerStu, γ = 0.01, p < .05), with more access to IT resources (ITMain, β = 0.07, p < .01), with higher levels of IT curricular expectations (Curriculum, γ = 0.03, p < .01), and with an explicit policy on the use of IT in mathematics (Policy, γ = 0.04, p < .01) reported higher levels of IT integration"
