The article reports a comparative study of public medical schools in ban states versus control states, finding "a nearly 5% decline" versus "an 0.7% increase" in underrepresented minoritized enrollment. These are printed percentage changes, not standardized effect sizes.

> "a recent study found a nearly 5% decline in underrepresented racial and ethnically minoritized students in public medical schools in states that had implemented bans on race‐conscious admissions, whereas control states without such bans recorded an 0.7% increase over the same time period."
