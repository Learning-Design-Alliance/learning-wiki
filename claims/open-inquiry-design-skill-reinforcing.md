# Open-inquiry laboratory experience was more design-oriented and aligned with AAPT's designing-experiments outcome than guided-inquiry

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Theme frequencies show open-inquiry students' experiences most often involved experimental process and design, leading the authors to advise the open level of inquiry better reinforces design skills. [→ Bei Cai 2021](#bei-cai-2021)

## Evidence

### Bei Cai 2021

Bei Cai, Lindsay A. Mainhood, Ryan Groome, Corinne Laverty, and Alastair McLean. (2021). Designing Experiments: Student Learning Experience and Behaviour in Undergraduate Physics Laboratories. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020109)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Frequency analysis of coded themes (Fig. 1: open-inquiry themes 56-88 average references; guided-inquiry themes 26-178) from the audio-transcript coding of 13 student groups. The authors report the themes "aligning more closely with the AAPT's guidelines for designing experiments are the open-inquiry experiment themes."

> "It suggests that the open level of inquiry provides students with a design skill-reinforcing experience more so than the guided level of inquiry."
