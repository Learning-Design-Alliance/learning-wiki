Md. Abdul Qader 2024¶

Md. Abdul Qader, Tanzin Ara Ashraf, Sirajum Monira, Muhammad Asadul Islam & Mir Rumi Mustafizur Rahman. (2024). Challenges of Pronunciation Practices in the ESL Curriculum within the CLT Framework in Bangladesh: A Systematic Review. World Journal of Education, 14(3). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v14n3p137

q2 · i? · review · r2

Thematic synthesis of reviewed studies on Bangladesh's CLT-based ESL curriculum. The review reports that the curriculum prioritizes reading, writing, vocabulary and grammar while "CLT has often overlooked the explicit pronunciation instruction" and speaking and listening skills are ignored. No effect sizes are printed.

"It mostly emphasizes communicative competence by including reading and writing skills that have only been prioritized in vocabulary and grammar practice. CLT has often overlooked the explicit pronunciation instruction. This curriculum has extremely ignored speaking and listening skills that require pronunciation practice"
