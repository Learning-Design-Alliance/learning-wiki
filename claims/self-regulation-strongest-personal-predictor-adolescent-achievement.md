## Evidence

### de la Fuente Jesús 2021

de la Fuente Jesús, Malpica-Chavarria Erika Andrea, Garzón-Umerenkova Angélica, Pachón-Basallo Mónica. (2021). Effect of Personal and Contextual Factors of Regulation on Academic Achievement during Adolescence: The Role of Gender and Age. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178944

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling (Model 3, nine factors) in an ex-post-facto study of 430 students found "the most predictive variables of achievement are self-regulation (SR), and self-efficacy (SEF)". Table 4 reports a total effect of SR on achievement of 0.32 [0.30, 0.34] with partial mediation.

> "The results showed that the most predictive variables of achievement are self-regulation (SR), and self-efficacy (SEF). Likewise, there was an immediate negative effect of self-regulation on procrastination, but not of self-efficacy."
