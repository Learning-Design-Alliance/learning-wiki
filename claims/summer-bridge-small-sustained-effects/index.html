Evidence¶

Melissa Roderick 2003¶

Melissa Roderick, Mimi Engel, Jenny Nagaoka, with Brian A. Jacob, Sophie Degener, Alex Orfei, Susan Stone, Jen Bacon. (2003). Ending Social Promotion: Results From Summer Bridge. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/ending-social-promotion-results-summer-bridge

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The report's sustainability analysis compared two-year learning gains of students slightly below the test-score cutoffs (who attended) to students slightly above the cutoffs, using the discontinuity in program participation as a comparison.

"Summer Bridge students had slightly larger learning gains over two years than comparable students who did not attend the program. Although these results are encouraging, the effects were small."
