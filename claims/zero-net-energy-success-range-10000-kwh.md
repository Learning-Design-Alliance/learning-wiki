### Jasmine Singh 2022

Jasmine Singh, Viranga Perera, Alejandra J. Magana, Brittany Newell, Jin Wei-Kocsis, Ying Ying Seah, Greg J. Strimel, and Charles Xie. (2022). Using Machine Learning to Predict Engineering Technology Students' Success with Computer Aided Design. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1002/cae.22489](https://doi.org/10.1002/cae.22489)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Range-sensitivity analysis on the 128-student cohort: logistic regression accuracies were compared across candidate net energy ranges centered on 0 kWh. Based on these results the authors "decided that being within the range of -10,000 to +10,000 kWh meant achieving a zero net energy design."

> "To test what range of values near zero net energy to consider as meeting the design requirement, we ran logistic regression models for a series of final net energy ranges centered on 0 kWh. Figure 4 shows logistic regression model accuracies as a function of choosing various ranges of final net energy values to be considered as being within the design range."
