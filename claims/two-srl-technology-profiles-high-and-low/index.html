Evidence¶

Carmen Yot-Domínguez and Carlos Marcelo 2017¶

Carmen Yot-Domínguez and Carlos Marcelo. (2017). University students' self-regulated learning using digital technologies. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education 14:38. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-017-0076-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

K-means cluster analysis (after hierarchical Ward-method clustering; three outliers removed) on factor use frequencies identified four conglomerates; the two most different are reported. High-profile students use instant messaging (M = 4.78), translators (M = 4.12) and multimedia search (M = 4.10) heavily.

"They represent 84 students, which is 11.86% of the respondents. Of these, 65.5% are female, and 59.5% are between 21 and 25 years of age while 17.9% are under 20. They stand out because they have the greatest mean frequency of implementation in all the factors"

Carmen Yot-Domínguez and Carlos Marcelo 2017 (2)¶

Carmen Yot-Domínguez and Carlos Marcelo. (2017). University students' self-regulated learning using digital technologies. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education 14:38. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-017-0076-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The contrasting low-use cluster still uses instant messaging actively (M = 4.27) and Wikipedia frequently (M = 3.41), showing that even the lesser group uses some technology-based SRL strategies regularly.

"This group includes 238 students, which corresponds to 33.62% of the respondents. Of these, 62.2% are between 21 and 25 years of age. This group of students shows an intermediate level regarding the use of strategies for the superficial use of information and collaborative learning factors."
