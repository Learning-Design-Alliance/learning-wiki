## Evidence

### Chen Xiuwen 2024

Chen Xiuwen, Xiao Yao, You Yunhui, Zhu Jianxi, Liu Shiqing, Chen Huiqiong, Zhu Hong. (2024). The effectiveness of the combined PBL and Tencent Conference online teaching mode in the clinical internship teaching of cardiac surgery. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0315455

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Historical controlled trial with 68 undergraduate clinical students (PBL-online N = 30, traditional N = 38) using a 24-item researcher-designed questionnaire. The comparison "was no statistically significant difference" with p = 0.065; the printed group means were 191.17±37.61 and 175.11±33.00 and were untested as described.

> "Although there was no statistically significant difference between the two groups in the total score of cardiac surgery teaching (p = 0.065), the total score in the PBL group was 191.17±37.61, significantly higher than in the traditional group (175.11±33.00)."
