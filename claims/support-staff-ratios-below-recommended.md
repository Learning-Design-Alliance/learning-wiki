# National average school support-staff ratios fall far below recommended levels for psychologists, counselors, and social workers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Actual national average staffing ratios (school psychologist 1:1,526; school counselor 1:444; social worker 1:2,106) exceed recommended ratios (1:500, 1:250, and 1:250 respectively), based on 2015–16 federal Civil Rights Data Collection data.
