## Evidence

### Walck-Shannon 2025

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Barton, H. D., Rowell, S. F., Chalker, D. L., & Fink, A. (2025). Students Don't Learn the Way They Think They Do in a Large, Active-Learning Genetics Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-10-0251

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Ordinal logistic mixed models on postsurvey effort ratings (sample size 455), where effort was defined to students as focused mental exertion. "including activity type as a predictor did not improve fit compared with a null model", so there was no evidence students perceived a difference in effort among the activity types.

> "Model comparison showed that including activity type as a predictor did not improve fit compared with a null model (χ2 (2) = 4.30, p = 0.116, ΔAIC = −0.3). Estimates of the odds ratios had CIs that included 1 (tell-then-practice vs. prediction: OR = 1.11, 95% CI [0.85–1.46]; prediction vs. worked examples: OR = 1.20, 95% CI [0.91–1.58])"
