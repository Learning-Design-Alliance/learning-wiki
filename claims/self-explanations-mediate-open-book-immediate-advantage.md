# Generated self-explanations mediate the immediate posttest advantage of open-book over closed-book prompts

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In groups without active processing prompts, the number of generated self-explanations mediated the advantage of open-book prompts on immediate posttest performance. [→ Sara Hiller · Stefan Rumann · Kirsten Berthold · Julian Roelle 2020](#sara-hiller-stefan-rumann-kirsten-berthold-julian-roelle-2020)

## Evidence

### Sara Hiller · Stefan Rumann · Kirsten Berthold · Julian Roelle 2020

Sara Hiller · Stefan Rumann · Kirsten Berthold · Julian Roelle. (2020). Example-based learning: should learners receive closed-book or open-book self-explanation prompts? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09523-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory mediation analysis with Hayes' PROCESS macro (10,000 bootstrap samples) in Experiment 1's no-active-processing groups; the indirect effect "a × b = 3.46 [0.09, 8.31]" gave open-book learners a 3.46-unit advantage via self-explanations.

> "We found a statistically significant indirect effect via the generated self-explanations, a × b = 3.46 [0.09, 8.31]."
