### Abdulla Alabbasi Ahmed M. 2026

Abdulla Alabbasi Ahmed M., Alansari Ahmed Mohamed, Deifalla Abdelhalim, Runco Mark A., Sequeira Reginald P., Alotaibi Ghazi A., AlSaleh Aseel. (2026). Convergent thinking, divergent thinking, and openness to experience as predictors of academic success among high-achieving and typical-achieving medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08850-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same hierarchical regression of 274 MD students; divergent thinking was a composite of the Alternative Uses Test and the figural Runco Creativity Assessment Battery. The printed coefficient was β = 0.25, t = 4.30, p < .001, described as "positively and significantly related to GPA".

> "both convergent (β = 0.20, t = 3.40, p < .001) and divergent thinking (β = 0.25, t = 4.30, p < .001) were positively and significantly related to GPA"
