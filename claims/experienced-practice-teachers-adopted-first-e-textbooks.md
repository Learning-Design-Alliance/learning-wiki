Blitz survey of elementary school teachers in the EBSE e-textbook choice experiment; Question 1 concerned work experience, reported in fig. 7 with experience categories from up to 5 years to over 26 years. The article calls the result unexpected: adopters were "practice teachers with more than 16 years of work experience".
