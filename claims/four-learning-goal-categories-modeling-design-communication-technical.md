## Evidence

### Benjamin M. Zwickl 2012

Benjamin M. Zwickl, Noah Finkelstein, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2012). The Process of Transforming an Advanced Lab Course: Goals, Curriculum, and Assessments. [doi:10.1119/1.4768890](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4768890)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Faculty interview study: fifteen faculty were interviewed for about one hour and 21 faculty (66 total in physics) participated in interviews and meetings across sub-disciplines including AMO physics (8). The four categories emerged from this consensus process, as the article reports: "four categories of learning goals emerged."

> "After much discussion, comparison with labs at other institutions, reference to the literature on physics education research, and a semester of classroom observations, four categories of learning goals emerged: modeling, design, communication, and technical lab skills."
