# Students retain the Edukoi colour-to-sound mapping after two months without re-training

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` When the experiment was repeated after about two months with no re-explanation of the mapping, colour recognition remained highly above chance (93% on sketches, d = 4.12). [→ Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino 2024](#lucrezia-guiotto-nai-fovino-2024)

## Evidence

### Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino 2024

Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino, Anita Zanella, Luca Di Mascolo, Michele Ginolfi, Nicolò Carpita, Francesco Trovato Manuncola, Massimo Grassi. (2024). Evaluating the effectiveness of sonification in science education using Edukoi. Personal and Ubiquitous Computing. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00779-024-01809-5

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Design feature of T2: the second behavioural session occurred about two months after T1 with no refresher on the tool or mapping, and 148 students still achieved 93% average colour recognition on sketches (d = 4.12), indicating retention.

> "In order to assess the students’ learning from T1, in T2, no explanation on how to use Edukoi and on the adopted mapping was given prior to the test."
