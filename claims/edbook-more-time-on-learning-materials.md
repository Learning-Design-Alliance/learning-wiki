## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a within-subjects study, participants spent significantly longer time with EDBook learning materials than with the webpage-plus-ChatGPT condition (p < 0.01, ANOVA). [→ Steve Oney 2024](#steve-oney-2024)

## Evidence

### Steve Oney 2024

Steve Oney, Yue Shen, Fei Wu, Young Suh Hong, Ziang Wang, Yamini Khajekar, Jiacheng Zhang, and April Yi Wang. (2024). EDBooks: AI-Enhanced Interactive Narratives for Programming Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.10687](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.10687)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-subjects user study with 20 programming learners comparing EDBook to equivalent webpage content with a context-informed ChatGPT, in a latin square design across two tasks. The article reports the time difference as significant (p < 0.01, ANOVA), with means of 1817.85 versus 1389.90 seconds.

> "participants in theCEDBook spent significantly longer time with the learning materials (𝑥 = 1817.85, 𝜎=265.22) than in theCWeb+GPT (𝑥 = 1389.90, 𝜎=461.18)"
