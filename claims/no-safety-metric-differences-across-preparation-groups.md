## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yiasemidou M 2017

Yiasemidou M, Glassman D, Mushtaq F, Athanasiou C, Williams M-M, Jayne D, Miskovic D. (2017). Mental practice with interactive 3D visual aids enhances surgical performance. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-017-5459-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal–Wallis tests on non-normally distributed safety metrics in the same 20-trainee study; bleeding and perforations "showed marginal trends but did not reach the statistical significance threshold". Bayes factors (0.45–0.63) favoured the null for all three safety metrics.

> "no statistically significant difference was found in the frequency of the damage to vital structures (H(2) = 0.63, p = .68, ω2 = 0.03). The comparisons for non-cauterised bleeding (H(2) = 4.71, p = .13, ω2 = 0.24) and number of perforations (H(2) = 4.8, p = .082, ω2 = 0.24) showed marginal trends but did not reach the statistical significance threshold"
