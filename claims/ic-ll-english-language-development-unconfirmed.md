## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2006

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). Instructional Conversations and Literature Logs — WWC Intervention Report. http://crede.berkeley.edu/research/crede/research/llaa/1.5pubs.html

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 84 fifth-grade English language learners. Appendix A3.2 reports CTBS Language effect size 0.56 (ns, improvement index +21) and Writing effect size 0.66 (ns, +24), domain average 0.61. The report states the effect "was substantively important" despite unconfirmed significance.

> "Saunders (1999) reported a statistically significant difference favoring the fifth grade intervention group on English language development. The WWC, however, was not able to confirm the statistical significance of this finding."
