Study 1 applied the Robust Z method (Huynh & Rawls, 2011) to detect item parameter drift among on-grade recalibrated items (Table 3.3). The report states "13%–15% of items in each subject were found by the Robust Z statistics to be significantly drifted away from their underlying scales." Flagged items' average difficulty differences resembled unflagged items'.
