## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Brokk Toggerson 2020

Brokk Toggerson, Samyukta Krishnamurthy, Emily E. Hansen, and Chasya Church. (2020). Positive Impacts on Student Self-Efficacy from an Introductory Physics for Life Science Course Using the Team-Based Learning Pedagogy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired t-tests on SOSESC-P attribute-level subscales for she-identifying students (n = 91) in the TBL IPLS course. The article reports "signiﬁcant gains for she-identifying students in all attributes of the course except ‘attention’" and identifies test-taking as showing "a particularly large shift." He-identifying students showed a significant shift only in understanding (p < 0.05).

> "we see signiﬁcant gains for she-identifying students in all attributes of the course except ‘attention.’ A particularly large shift is visible in the area of ‘test taking,’ with a t = 4.392, p = 2 × 10−5."
