## Evidence

### Neitzel 2026

Neitzel, A.J., Storey, N., & Wang, X. (2026). Air Reading: A randomized evaluation of a virtual tutoring model in Louisiana and Texas schools. Center for Research and Reform in Education, Johns Hopkins University. https://education.jhu.edu/crre

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Dosage analysis replaced the treatment indicator with attendance measures; on average treatment students attended 55 sessions, with 56% reaching the high-dosage threshold of 56 or more sessions. The article reports the high-dosage effect size "(ES = +0.32, p < .05)" and the low-dosage effect size "(ES = +0.26, p < .05)"; one student was missing dosage data and removed.

> "Students who received high levels of tutoring exposure (56+ sessions) demonstrated significantly higher achievement than their control group peers (ES = +0.32, p < .05). In contrast, those in the low-dosage group showed significant but smaller impacts relative to controls (ES = +0.26, p < .05)."
