Zimiles 2025¶

Zimiles, J., Nakamoto, J., & Boal, A. (2025). Compass Care at Valor Collegiate Academies: Attendance program spotlight. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677597

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative self-report from students, caregivers, and school staff interviewed during spring 2025 WestEd site visits. One student said without the program "I would probably have a lot of bad grades and be scared of teachers. I wouldn't be able to advocate for myself." Perceived, not measured, outcomes.

"Students expressed that Compass Care staff empowered them to reach out to teachers, for example, when they needed help with understanding the curriculum or asking for makeup assignments."
