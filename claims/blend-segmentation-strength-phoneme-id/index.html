McNeill 2018¶

Qualitative item-by-item analysis (Figure 2) comparing percent correct per phoneme identification item across the research cohort, preservice comparison group and inservice-without-PD group. The graph showed the research group's strength "in segmenting words with blends (e.g., trim, brush, string)".

"Further analysis revealed 17% of the research cohort scored 100% of items correctly (compared to 0% in the preservice comparison group and 5% in the 'in-service without PD' group."
