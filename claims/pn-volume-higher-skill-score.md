# Higher annual partial nephrectomy volume is associated with higher peer-assessed technical skill scores

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Surgeons with 1-point higher overall skill scores performed a mean of 11 more partial nephrectomies per year (P < .001); volume was associated with skill in all key steps except identification of the ureter and gonadal vessels. [→ Wang Yuzhi 2024](#wang-yuzhi-2024)

## Evidence

### Wang Yuzhi 2024

Wang Yuzhi, Wilder Samantha, Hijazi Mahmoud, Myles Marquisha D., Mirza Mahin, Van Til Monica, Maatman Thomas, Ghani Khurshid R., Lane Brian R., Rogers Craig G. (2024). Surgeon Skill and Perioperative Outcomes in Robot-Assisted Partial Nephrectomy. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.21696

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression of overall skill score on annual partial nephrectomy volume for 10 surgeons (median annual volume 9, IQR 4-17). The article reports "a mean of 11 (95% CI, 10 to 13) additional partial nephrectomies per year" per 1-point higher score.

> "Higher annual partial nephrectomy volume was significantly associated with a better overall score: 1 point higher in overall skill score was associated with a mean of 11 (95% CI, 10 to 13) additional partial nephrectomies per year (P < .001)"
