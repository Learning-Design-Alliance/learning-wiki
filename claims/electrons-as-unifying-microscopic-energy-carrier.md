## Subclaims
`q1 i?` When asked about forms of energy, a student concludes on the spot that electrons are where energy is stored, unifying redox reactions, photosynthesis, and solar power. [→ Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2011](#benjamin-w-dreyfus-2011)

## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2011

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Edward F. Redish, and Jessica Watkins. (2011). Students' Views of Macroscopic and Microscopic Energy in Physics and Biology. PERC 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1106.5801](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1106.5801)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview data: asked about the different forms energy can take, the student said "I guess it would be electrons, is where energy is stored," citing redox reactions, photosynthesis, and solar power as examples. This bridging occurred among microscopic, non-biological and biological phenomena. No effect size is reported.

> ""I guess it would be electrons, is where energy is stored, I guess would be the moral of the story."
