# In a change detection task, adults outperform 4-to-5-year-olds on cued shapes while children outperform adults on uncued shapes

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Adults detect changes in cued (task-relevant) shapes better than children, but children detect changes in uncued (task-irrelevant) shapes better than adults. [→ Plebanek 2017](#plebanek-2017)
`q3 i3` The Age Group x Shape Type interaction on change detection accuracy is statistically significant. [→ Plebanek 2017 (2)](#plebanek-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Plebanek 2017

Plebanek, D. J., & Sloutsky, V. M. (2017). Costs of Selective Attention: When Children Notice what Adults Miss. Psychological Science, 28(6), 723-732. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797617693005

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Controlled experiment (Experiment 1) with 34 four- to five-year-olds and 35 adults recruited, of whom 7 children and 4 adults were excluded for poor performance, leaving 27 children and 31 adults in the analyses, performing a change detection task with overlaid red and green shapes, attention cued to one color. A-prime accuracies show adults ahead on cued shapes (d = 1.00) but children ahead on uncued shapes (d = −.59), "whereas they lagged behind children in detecting changes in irrelevant information".

> "adults outperformed children in change detection for relevant information (.943 vs. .865), t(56) = 3.79, p < .001, d = 1.00, 95%, CI [.44, 1.54], whereas they lagged behind children in detecting changes in irrelevant information (.634 vs. .771), t(56) = −2.22, p = .030, d = .−59, 95% CI [−1.11, −.05]"

### Plebanek 2017 (2)

Plebanek, D. J., & Sloutsky, V. M. (2017). Costs of Selective Attention: When Children Notice what Adults Miss. Psychological Science, 28(6), 723-732. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797617693005

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The same mixed ANOVA on A-prime change detection accuracies (2 Age x 2 Shape Type, Shape Type within-subjects) revealed the omnibus interaction: "There was a significant interaction, F(1,56) = 13.04, p < .001, η2 = .200", a large effect by the printed η².
