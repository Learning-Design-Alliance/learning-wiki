## Evidence

### Justyna P. Zwolak 2018

Justyna P. Zwolak, Michael Zwolak, and Eric Brewe. (2018). Educational commitment and social networking: The power of informal networks. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010131)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Likelihood ratio tests on logistic regression models of persistence in the Modeling Instruction physics sequence, run on the intermediate grade cohort (N = 90). The LRT showed "only the out-of- class centrality carries statistically signiﬁcant informa- tion about persistence"; no effect size is printed.

> "We ﬁnd that, for the intermediate grade cohort, only the out-of- class centrality carries statistically signiﬁcant informa- tion about persistence."
