## Evidence

### Sophie Wacker 2024

Sophie Wacker, Claudia M. Roebers. (2024). Motivating children to (pre)monitor: positive effects on monitoring accuracy? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09351-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc descriptive analysis of judgment shifts: "22.6% of judgments were found to mirror lower confidence compared to the previous pre-monitoring judgment"; 9.3% mirrored higher confidence. A chi-squared test showed a significant relationship (X²(30) = 550.20, p < .001, CC = .412).

> "But, 22.6% of judgments were found to mirror lower confidence compared to the previous pre-monitoring judgment, indicating a decrease in overconfidence as we had initially expected."
