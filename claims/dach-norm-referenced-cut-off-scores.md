### Härtl Anja 2015

Härtl Anja, Bachmann Cadja, Blum Katharina, Höfer Stefan, Peters Tim, Preusche Ingrid, Raski Bianca, Rüttermann Stefan, Wagner-Menghin Michaela, Wünsch Alexander, Kiessling Claudia, GMA-Ausschuss Kommunikative und Soziale Kompetenzen. [doi:10.3205/zma000998](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000998)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey result on standard setting in the 32 programmes with pass-relevant communication assessments. The authors state that international recommendations on criterion-oriented standard setting have apparently yet to reach German-speaking faculties to an adequate extent.

> "In the 32 programmes with communicative competencies assessments relevant to passing, norm-referenced methods [36] were predominantly used to establish a cut-off score (e.g., a specified percentage of 60%). Criterion-referenced methods [36] were only used with OSCEs."
