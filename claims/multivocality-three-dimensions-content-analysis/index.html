Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse 2010¶

Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse. (2010). The CSCL field matures. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning 5:1–3. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-009-9077-5

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

Editorial preview (type e) of the Clarà and Mauri paper: the editors report the authors' suggestion about multi-vocality, set against the field's tensions between quantitative cognitive-science approaches and qualitative situated-interaction approaches.

"Marc Clarà and Teresa Mauri of Spain close this issue of ijCSCL with the suggestion that multi-vocality in our field can be a healthy characteristic as long as we can find ways to bring the various findings into communication with each other."

"Focusing on content-analysis research, the authors identify three dimensions along which studies in this subfield of CSCL can be brought into dialectic relations with one another."
