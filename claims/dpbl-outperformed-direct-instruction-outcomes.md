## Evidence

### Winarno et al. 2017

Sri Winarno, Kalaiarasi Sonai Muthu & Lew Sook Ling. (2017). Direct Problem-Based Learning (DPBL): A Framework for Integrating Direct Instruction and Problem-Based Learning Approach. International Education Studies, 11(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p119

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental group pre-test and post-test comparison of 276 third-year Computer Networks students (140 direct instruction, 136 DPBL) at Dian Nuswantoro University. The article reports "Creative thinking skills score increased 8.4%" and knowledge increased 25.9%, with Sig. p=.00 reported in the abstract; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "Creative thinking skills score increased 8.4%, Teamwork skills  score increased 11.5%, and knowle dge score increased 25.9% of DPBL approach."
