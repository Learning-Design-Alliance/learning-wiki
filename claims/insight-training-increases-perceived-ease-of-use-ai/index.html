Evidence¶

Mesenhöller J and Böhme K 2026¶

Mesenhöller J and Böhme K (2026). Empowering teachers to use AI for differentiation: changes in teachers’ acceptance of AI-based technologies following participation in a short professional development training session. Front. Educ. 11:1875856. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1875856

q2 · i1 · design · r2

Paired-samples t-test on the SAELKIS PEOU subscale in the same pilot pre-post evaluation (N = 100). The article reports PEOU "increased significantly" with the printed statistics; pre-test PEOU was M = 2.85 (SD = .72), post-test M = 2.99 (SD = .58).

"PEOU also increased significantly, t(99) = −2.47, p = .015, mean difference = −0.13, 95% CI [−0.24, −0.03], d = −0.25, 95% CI [−0.45, −0.05]."
