In 2016 teachers, rather than administrators, were asked how often they met in grade-level teams and observed one another's classes. "Around 50 percent of elementary and 40 percent of high school teachers" reported weekly team meetings, and around half of elementary and high school teachers reported observing colleagues' classrooms at least quarterly.

"Around 50 percent of elementary and 40 percent of high school teachers in both the ELA and math response groups reported meeting at least weekly in grade-level team meetings"
