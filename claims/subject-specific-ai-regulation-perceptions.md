Cross-sectional survey analysis of Question C1 across four subjects in the n = 416 sample, visualized in Fig. 3. > "Across all years, 65.9% of mathematics students and 55.3% of science students report prohibition. A Kruskal-Wallis H test confirmed that these differences in regulatory configurations are statistically significant across the four subjects (H = 360.15, p < .001)"
