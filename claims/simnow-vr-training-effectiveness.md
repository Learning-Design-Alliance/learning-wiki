## Evidence

### Radi Imad 2022

Radi Imad, Tellez Juan C., Alterio Rodrigo E., Scott Daniel J., Sankaranarayanan Ganesh, Nagaraj Madhuri B., Hogg Melissa E., Zeh Herbert J., Polanco Patricio M. (2022). Feasibility, effectiveness and transferability of a novel mastery-based virtual reality robotic training platform for general surgery residents. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-022-09106-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pretest–posttest analysis of the whole cohort using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests; the article reports "median total score on all 4 VR tasks increased from 82.5" to 353 (p < 0.001), with time and economy of motion also significantly improved (all p < 0.001).

> "median total score on all 4 VR tasks increased from 82.5 (IQR: 35.8–141.5) to 353 (IQR: 318–366.25) out of a maximum of 400 (p < 0.001), median total time decreased from 26 min (IQR: 22.9–33.1 min) to 12.5 min"
