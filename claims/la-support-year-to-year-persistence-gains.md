## Evidence

### Tedeschi MN 2023

Tedeschi MN, Hose TM, Mehlman EK, Franklin S, Wong TE. (2023). Improving Models for Student Retention and Graduation using Markov Chains. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Supplementary Tables S3-S5 report Markov transition probabilities (Fall 2013-Summer 2021): for science majors Y1→Y2 persistence is 95% (LA) versus 87% (no-LA), with smaller differences of +2 and +3 points for Y2→Y3 and Y3→Y4; similar patterns hold for AALANA and first-generation majors.

> "In addition to the notable improvements to SYGR, LA support is associated with improvements in year-to-year persistence throughout a student's time at RIT (see Supplementary Material)"
