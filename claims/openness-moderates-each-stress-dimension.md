## Evidence

### Li Haiyang 2026

Li Haiyang, Gao Ruoxi, Xu Haibo. (2026). Impact of academic stress on achievement among medical postgraduates: the moderating role of the Big Five personality traits. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04294-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Dimension-level moderation analyses (Table 3, Models 6–9). All four Openness × stress-dimension interaction terms were significant with printed confidence intervals, showing positive moderation for professional study stress and negative moderation for the other three.

> "the Openness trait was associated with professional study stress (β = 0.065, p = 0.013, 95%CI [0.01, 0.12]), scientific research stress (β = −0.062, p = 0.030, 95%CI [−0.12, −0.01]), academic ecological stress (β = −0.107, p = 0.002, 95%CI [−0.16, −0.05]), and self-development and employment stress (β = −0.091, p = 0.002, 95%CI [−0.14, −0.04]), indicating significant interaction terms."
