## Evidence

### Lee Haesu 2026

Lee Haesu, Woo Young Keun, Park Kyue-nam, Kim Sujin. (2026). Online versus offline Fugl-Meyer lower extremity training for physical therapy students: a randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09634-5

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' stated limitations in the Limitations and clinical application section, offered as interpretation rather than tested results. They also note unequal access to lecture review, available only to the online group, may have confounded the results, and that findings may not generalize to other instruments or full clinical administration.

> "First, participant engagement in the online group could not be verified because camera use was not required, which may have affected educational fidelity. Second, all scoring was based on pre-recorded videos rather than direct patient assessment, limiting generalizability to real-world clinical practice."
