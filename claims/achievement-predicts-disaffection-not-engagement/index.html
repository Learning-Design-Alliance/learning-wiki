Evidence¶

Pöysä 2018¶

Pöysä, S., Vasalampi, K., Muotka, J., Lerkkanen, M.-K., Poikkeus, A.-M., & Nurmi, J.-E. (2018). Variation in situation-specific engagement among lower secondary school students. Learning and Instruction, 53, 64-73. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.07.007

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Between-students level of the two-level multivariate model (N = 57; GPA from Grade 7 school registers, gender controlled) found achievement significantly related to disaffection, "students with higher academic achievement reported disaffection to a lesser extent." Explained variance at this level was 26.7% for disaffection.

"However, students' academic achievement was statistically significantly related to disaffection (β = - .304, p = .000): students with higher academic achievement reported disaffection to a lesser extent than did students with lower academic achievement."

Pöysä 2018 (2)¶

Pöysä, S., Vasalampi, K., Muotka, J., Lerkkanen, M.-K., Poikkeus, A.-M., & Nurmi, J.-E. (2018). Variation in situation-specific engagement among lower secondary school students. Learning and Instruction, 53, 64-73. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.07.007

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same two-level multivariate model: no statistically significant prediction of behavioral/cognitive or emotional engagement by achievement at the between-students level; equivalence was not tested. Explained variances were 3.4% and 1.0% respectively.

"The results at the between-students level showed that students' academic achievement did not predict their ratings of behavioral/cognitive engagement or emotional engagement (after student gender was controlled for)."
