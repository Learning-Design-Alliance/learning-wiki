Descriptive analysis of beginning- and end-of-year DIBELS composite scores for 622 students in 54 sites who started the year in the at risk category. About half of movers went to some risk (22 percent), 16 percent to minimal risk, and 4 percent to negligible risk.

"Of students who started the year scoring in the at risk category on the DIBELS, 42 percent moved to a lower risk category by the end of the year."
