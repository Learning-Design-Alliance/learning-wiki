## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Tracking accuracy increased from 85% (Session 1) to 87% (Session 6) with no group difference. [→ Angela D. Bender 2017](#angela-d-bender-2017)

## Evidence

### Angela D. Bender 2017

Angela D. Bender, Hannah L. Filmer, Claire K. Naughtin and Paul E. Dux. (2017). Dynamic, continuous multitasking training leads to task-speciﬁc improvements but does not transfer across action selection tasks. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0015-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Six-session mixed-design ANOVA of training data showed a significant session main effect with printed effect size ηp² = .065 and no group difference; both groups "showed signiﬁcant pre-to-post-training improvements in their respective training-related tasks".

> "Analyses of tracking accuracy revealed a main effect of session (F(5,185) = 2.59, p = .027, η2 P = .065), such that overall tracking accuracy significantly increased from Session 1 ( M = 85%) to Session 6 ( M = 87%), and did not differ between groups"
