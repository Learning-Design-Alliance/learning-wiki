Evidence¶

Sten CW 2024¶

Sten CW, Söderström K, Stensrud B, Nylund IB, Siqveland J. (2024). The effectiveness of virtual reality training on knowledge, skills and attitudes of health care professionals and students in assessing and treating mental health disorders: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05423-0

q3 · i? · review · r3

RCT of 227 primary health care professionals comparing a VP game intervention to a no-intervention waitlist control, using a self-report composite of six Gatekeeper Behavior Scale items. The review notes changes were "reported only for the within treatment group (n = 117)" with a large effect size.

"Changes between presimulation and postsimulation scores were reported only for the within treatment group (n = 117), where the composite postsimulation score was significantly higher than the presimulation score, with a large effect size observed."
