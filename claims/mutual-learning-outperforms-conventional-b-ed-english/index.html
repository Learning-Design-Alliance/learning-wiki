Evidence¶

Arulselvi 2013¶

Arulselvi, E. (2013). Effectiveness of Mutual Learning Approach in the Academic Achievement of B.Ed Students in Learning Optional II English. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 3(3), 25-31. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068866

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two groups of 25 B.Ed students each in a College of Education in Namakkal District; the experimental group used mutual learning teams while the control received traditional instruction. The article infers that "the experimental group excels the control group in academic performance."

"Achievement scores of the Post test I of the control and experimental groups were compared and there exists significant difference between these two scores by the 't' value 13.52 which is significant at 0.01 level."

Arulselvi 2013 (2)¶

Arulselvi, E. (2013). Effectiveness of Mutual Learning Approach in the Academic Achievement of B.Ed Students in Learning Optional II English. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 3(3), 25-31. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068866

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Second post-test comparison on a different unit (Teaching Vocabulary, Reference and Study skills) between the same two groups of 25 learners each. The article again infers that "the experimental group excels the control group in academic performance."

"Achievement scores of the Post test II of the control and experimental groups were compared and there exists significant difference between these two scores by the 't' value 12.5 which is significant at 0.01 level."
