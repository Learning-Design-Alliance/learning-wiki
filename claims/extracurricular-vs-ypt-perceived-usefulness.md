# Students rated other extracurricular activities as more useful than YPT-related activities for all soft skills except presentation and debating skills

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' self-evaluations showed greater perceived usefulness for other extracurricular activities than for YPT-related activities for all soft-skill types except presentation skills and debating skills. [→ Aneva 2022](#aneva-2022)

## Evidence

### Aneva 2022

Aneva, B., Faletič, S., Hömöstrei, M., Jenei, P., Kundracik, F., Kyuldjiev, A., Lindner, T., Němec, H., Puhr, H., & Plesch, M. (2022). Development of Soft Skills via IYPT. Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Student survey t-tests comparing YPT vs. other extracurricular activities (e.g., Physics Olympiad, IJSO, EUSO); the printed table shows significant differences favouring other activities for Teamwork (t = -3.946, p = 0.000) and English skills (t = -4.661, p = 0.000).

> "In comparison to YPT -related activities, we observe greater perceived usefulness for extracurricular activities for all types of soft skills, with the notable exceptions of “Presentation skills” and “Debating skills”."
