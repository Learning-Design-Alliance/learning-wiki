## Evidence

### Xiao 2026

Xiao, R., Ye, R., Hou, X., Wen, J., Kumar, H., Liut, M., & Stamper, J. (2026). Bridging GenAI policy and practice: Brief instruction improves learning-oriented prompting in an introductory programming course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Between-condition comparison of immediate learning gains in the RCT (n=431). Conditions 4 and 3 did not differ significantly (p=.08). The ordering predicted by ICAP held at immediate post-test.

> "One-way analysis of variance confirmed significant between-condition differences (F(3,427) =62.4 ,p< .001). Tukey HSD post-hoc tests indicated that Conditions 2–4 each produced significantly greater gains than Condition 1 (all p< .001), and that Condition 4 exceeded Condition 2 (p< .01)"

### Xiao 2026 (2)

Xiao, R., Ye, R., Hou, X., Wen, J., Kumar, H., Liut, M., & Stamper, J. (2026). Bridging GenAI policy and practice: Brief instruction improves learning-oriented prompting in an introductory programming course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Delayed post-test gains in the same RCT maintained the monotonic ordering across the four conditions at the end of the semester.

> "The same graded pattern persisted at the six-week delayed post-test (mean delayed gains 6, 15, 17, and 24 percentage points in Conditions 1–4; Table 1)."
