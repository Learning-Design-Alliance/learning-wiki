Alexander Skulmowski 2023¶

Alexander Skulmowski. (2023). The Cognitive Architecture of Digital Externalization. Educational Psychology Review, 35:101. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09818-1

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports experimental findings on Internet search and metacognition (Fisher et al., 2022): "Searching the Internet leads to less memori- zation and gives learners the impression of having learned that information." Related work (Stone & Storm, 2021) found search fluency is wrongfully taken as a cue of future recall; Flanagin and Lew (2023) found people overestimate task performance with web access.
