# Nonmajor women’s attitudes improve in single-gender CL groups but decline in mixed-gender CL groups relative to TL

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Nonmajor women developed a higher positive attitude in single-gender CL (4W) than TL (d = 0.6240) but a more negative attitude in mixed-gender CL (2M+2W) than TL (d = 0.7091). [→ Almasri Firas 2021](#almasri-firas-2021)
`q3 i2` ANOVA confirms women in single-gender classes had higher positive attitudes in CL than TL (η2 = 0.05), while in mixed-gender classes women had higher positive attitudes in TL than CL (η2 = 0.071). [→ Almasri Firas 2021 (2)](#almasri-firas-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Almasri Firas 2021

Almasri Firas, Hewapathirana Gertrude I., Ghaddar Fatme, Lee Nick, Ibrahim Bashar. (2021). Measuring attitudes towards biology major and non-major: Effect of students’ gender, group composition, and learning environment. PLoS ONE 16(5): e0251453. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251453

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Results-section t-test comparisons for nonmajor women: single-gender CL favored over TL (d = 0.6240), mixed-gender CL lower than TL (d = 0.7091), both p<0.01, with printed means and SDs.

> "On the other hand, women developed higher level of positive attitude toward biology in single-gender classes CL (4W) compared to TL (W_CL μ = 4.04 SD = 0.65 versus W_TL μ = 3.59 SD = 0.66) p<0.01, d = 0.6240, and a negative attitude toward biology in CL (2M+2W) compared to TL mix-gender classes (W_CL μ = 2.59 SD = 0.65 versus W_TL μ = 3.06 SD = 0.55) p<0.01 Cohen’s d = 0.7091."

### Almasri Firas 2021 (2)

Almasri Firas, Hewapathirana Gertrude I., Ghaddar Fatme, Lee Nick, Ibrahim Bashar. (2021). Measuring attitudes towards biology major and non-major: Effect of students’ gender, group composition, and learning environment. PLoS ONE 16(5): e0251453. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251453

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Results-section ANOVAs for nonmajor women by class composition: CL advantage in single-gender classes (η2 = 0.05) reverses to a TL advantage in mixed-gender classes (η2 = 0.071), both p<0.001.

> "Women in single-gender have significantly higher positive attitude toward biology in CL (4W) group compared to TL group (F (1,1102) = 57.975, p<0.001., η2 = 0.05); however, in mix-gender class, women students developed a higher positive attitude in TL compared to CL (F (1,265) = 97.27, p<.001, η2 = 0.071)."
