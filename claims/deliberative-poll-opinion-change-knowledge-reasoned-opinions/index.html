Evidence¶

Vibeke Normann Andersen and Kasper M. Hansen 2007¶

Vibeke Normann Andersen and Kasper M. Hansen. (2007). How deliberation makes better citizens: The Danish Deliberative Poll on the euro. European Journal of Political Research, Volume 46, Issue 4, pp. 531-556. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1475-6765.2007.00699.x

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental Deliberative Poll with a representative sample of 364 Danish citizens deliberating on Denmark's participation in the single currency. The article reports "considerable changes in political opinions as the Poll proceeds, increase in level of knowledge and an improved ability to form reasoned opinions"; no effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"The participants' responses reflect a deliberative process characterized by considerable changes in political opinions as the Poll proceeds, increase in level of knowledge and an improved ability to form reasoned opinions."
