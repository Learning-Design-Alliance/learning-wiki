## Evidence

### Zhang Baoping 2020

Zhang Baoping, Li Sihong, Gao Shuting, Hou Mingfang, Chen Hong, He Lulu, Li Yiting, Guo Yumeng, Wang Errui, Cao Rui, Cheng Jingyang, Li Ruiping, Zhang Kailiang. (2020). Virtual versus jaw simulation in Oral implant education: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02152-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Objective CBCT analysis of implants placed in pig mandibles (Table 3): implant tip deviation 0.86 ± 0.34 mm (V-J) vs 2.00 ± 0.26 mm (V), angular deviation 1.77 ± 0.77° vs 5.85 ± 1.25°, P < 0.001. Order of combined training did not matter.

> "The main overall effect in the V-J and J-V groups shows improvement and higher accuracy. That is, the combination of the two training methods helped students improve their implant clinical skills, but the order (whether V-J or J-V) is independent."
