## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The treatment group scored 12.51 points (44.67%) higher than the control group on the immediate posttest, a statistically significant difference with a large effect size. [→ Kruk 2012](#kruk-2012)

## Evidence

### Kruk 2012

Mariusz Kruk. (2012). The Use of Internet Resources and Browser-Based Virtual Worlds in Teaching Grammar. Teaching English with Technology, 14(2), 52-67. http://www.tewtjournal.org

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with one intact third-grade senior high school class randomly divided into treatment (N = 13) and control (N = 14) groups; the treatment received four 45-minute lessons using online activities and Yoowalk. The article reports "12.51 or 44.67%, t(25) = -4.622, p < .001, η2 = 0.460".

> "a huge and statistically significant difference betwe en the treatment and the control group on the immediate posttest (IPostT) was observed, which amounted to 12.51 or 44.67%, t(25) = -4.622, p < .001, η2 = 0.460"
