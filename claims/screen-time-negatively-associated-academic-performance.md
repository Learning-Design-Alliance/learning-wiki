## Evidence

### Sidiq Mohammad et al 2025

Sidiq Mohammad et al. (2025). Screen time exposure and academic performance, anxiety, and behavioral problems among school children. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19409

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression (Table 3) on the 537-child survey: academic performance had unstandardized β −0.120 (SE 0.034), standardized β −0.134, t = −2.96, p = 0.003. The correlation matrix reports screen time–academic performance at −0.37. The printed regression effect size is "β – 0.12, t = −2.96, p < 0.001".

> "The linear regression model indicated that screen time was associated with children’s BMI (β − 0.98, t = 5.8, p < 0.001), academic performance (β – 0.12, t = −2.96, p < 0.001), and pediatric symptoms scale score (β – 0.178, t = 4.33, p < 0.001)."
