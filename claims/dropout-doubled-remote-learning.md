## Evidence

### Teemu Hynninen 2023

Teemu Hynninen, Henna Pesonen, Olli Lintu, and Petriina Paturi. (2023). Ongoing eﬀects of pandemic-imposed learning disruption on student attitudes. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010101)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of pass counts and dropout rates across the four cohorts (Table II): dropout was slightly less than 10 % under full contact teaching, "doubled to 17 %" during no contact teaching, and "a low dropout rate of 6 % was recovered" under limited contact teaching; no effect size is printed.

> "In 2020–2021 and no contact teaching, the dropout rate doubled to 17 %. This is in accordance with negative changes in expert-like thinking during this year. Lastly in 2021–2022 and limited contact teaching, a low dropout rate of 6 % was recovered."
