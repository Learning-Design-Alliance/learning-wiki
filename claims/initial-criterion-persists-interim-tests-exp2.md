# Effects of initial learning criterion persisted across relearning sessions on Experiment 2 interim tests but weakened with successive relearning

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In Experiment 2, fewer RMF trials were needed to reach criterion in relearning Sessions 2–4 for items initially recalled three times versus once, with a significant criterion × session interaction (ηp2 = .11). [→ Rawson 2011](#rawson-2011)
`q3 i2` In Experiment 2, recall on the first trial of Session 2 was significantly greater for items recalled three times versus once during initial learning (d = 0.63), with the effect tending to persist in Sessions 3 and 4. [→ Rawson 2011 (2)](#rawson-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Rawson 2011

Rawson, K. A., & Dunlosky, J. (2011). Optimizing Schedules of Retrieval Practice for Durable and Efficient Learning: How Much Is Enough? Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 140(3), 283–302. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0023956

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 mixed-factor ANOVA on mean RMF trials per item needed to reach one correct recall in Sessions 2–4. The article reports a main effect of Session 1 criterion (ηp2 = .11) and a significant criterion × session interaction (ηp2 = .11).

> "a main effect of Session 1 criterion, F(1, 50) ⫽ 6.34, MSE ⫽ 3.38, p ⫽ .015, ␩p2 ⫽ .11; and a significant interaction, F(2, 100) ⫽ 5.96, p ⫽ .004, ␩p2 ⫽ .11."

### Rawson 2011 (2)

Rawson, K. A., & Dunlosky, J. (2011). Optimizing Schedules of Retrieval Practice for Durable and Efficient Learning: How Much Is Enough? Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 140(3), 283–302. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0023956

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 interim retention tests (first RMF trial of each relearning session, with no restudy beforehand). Recall favored three versus one initial recalls in Session 2 with d = 0.63, and the effect tended to persist in Sessions 3 and 4.

> "As in Experiment 1, correctly recalling items three times versus one time during Session 1 led to significantly greater recall performance on the first trial in Session 2, t(56) ⫽ 2.34, p ⫽ .011, d ⫽ 0.63."
