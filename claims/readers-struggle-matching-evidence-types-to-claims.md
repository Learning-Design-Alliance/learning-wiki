The review attributes to several cited studies that "adult readers may have difficulties distinguishing between correlational and causal evidence" and evaluating claims by evidence appropriateness. It adds that younger readers may struggle to question anecdotal evidence for causal claims. No effect sizes are printed.

> "several studies have shown that adult readers may have difficulties distinguishing between correlational and causal evidence (Bleske-Rechek et al., 2015; List, 2024; List et al., 2022) and further evaluating correlational and causal claims based on the appropriateness of the evidence (Steffens et al., 2014)."
