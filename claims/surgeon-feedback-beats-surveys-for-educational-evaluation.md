`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within the same single-center pre/post study, blinded surgeon grading of assessment cases detected both significant gains (Le Fort, nasoseptal, NOE, overall) and non-improvement (upper face, ZMC, internal orbit, mandible), whereas surveys showed uniform confidence gains; the article reports this contrast directly.

> "This outcome metric better identified areas of improvement and non-improvement following our educational intervention."
