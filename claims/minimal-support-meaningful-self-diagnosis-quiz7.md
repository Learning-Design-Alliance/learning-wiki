## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For quiz 7, group D showed a positive correlation between self-diagnosis score and post (midterm) score (correlation=0.53, p value<0.05) with no pre–post correlation, matching the predicted 'meaningful' intervention pattern; groups B and C showed no significant SD–post correlations. ## Evidence

### Andrew Mason 2009

Andrew Mason, Elisheva Cohen, Chandralekha Singh, and Edit Yerushalmi. (2009). Self-Diagnosis, Scaffolding and Transfer: A Tale of Two Problems. [doi:10.1063/1.3266736](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266736)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intra-group correlation analysis (Table 3) for the easier quiz 7: group D's SD–post correlation of 0.53 (p value<0.05) with absent pre–post and pre–SD correlations fits the authors' 'meaningful' pattern, while groups B and C showed no significant correlations between any two variables.

> "there is a positive correlation between SD vs. post (but there is no correlation between pre vs. post and pre vs. SD) for group D  (correlation=0.53, p value<0 .05)."
