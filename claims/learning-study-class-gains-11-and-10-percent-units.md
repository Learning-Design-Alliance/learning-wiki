### Nilsson 2008

Nilsson, Ingrid. (2008). Alignment in teacher education and distribution of leadership: An example concerning learning study. Forum on Public Policy. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099542

Group-level analysis of pre- and post-tests in three grade-6 classes (23, 22, and 19 pupils) after three video-recorded lessons. Class 6A rose from 67% to 78% (+11), class 6C from 64% to 74% (+10), and class 6B stayed at 60% (0), as shown in Table 1.

> "The learning study showed apparently contradictory results because in the first lesson the test scores increased most , 11 percent units, and in the last lesson the test scores increa sed 10 percent units"
