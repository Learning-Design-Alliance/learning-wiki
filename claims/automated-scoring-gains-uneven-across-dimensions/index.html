Evidence¶

Chen C and Liu T 2026¶

Chen C and Liu T (2026) Impact of automated scoring on interpreting performance and self-regulated learning: evidence from a pedagogical experiment. Front. Psychol. 17:1867463. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1867463

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Four dimension-level ANOVAs on pre/post sub-scores found significant interactions with printed effect sizes "η2ₚ = 0.231" (linguistic accuracy) and "η2ₚ = 0.109" (logical coherence); the experimental group gained 3.21 points on accuracy and 2.08 on coherence, while fidelity and fluency "barely moved in the experimental group (+0.04 and +0.18)".

"The time × group interaction was significant for linguistic accuracy [F(1, 44) = 13.20, p < 0.001, η2ₚ = 0.231] and logical coherence [F(1, 44) = 5.40, p = 0.025, η2ₚ = 0.109] but not for information fidelity [F(1, 44) = 1.42, p = 0.240, η2ₚ = 0.031] or delivery fluency [ F(1, 44) = 0.099, p = 0.755, η2ₚ = 0.002]"
