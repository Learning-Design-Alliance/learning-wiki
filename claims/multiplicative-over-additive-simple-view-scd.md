## Evidence

### Spencer 2019

Spencer, M., Wagner, R. K., & Petscher, Y. (2019). The Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary Knowledge of Children with Poor Reading Comprehension despite Adequate Decoding: Evidence from a Regression-Based Matching Approach. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000274

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Discussion-section synthesis of the effect size comparisons across both SCD subgroups. The authors interpret the larger comprehension weakness as favoring a multiplicative over an additive simple view model for these children.

> "children with SCD had significant reading comprehension difficulties (d = −2.49 and −3.43) and vocabulary (d = −1.56 and −2.03); however, the observed reading comprehension difficulties were much more pronounced than children’s vocabulary weakness (d difference = 0.93 and 1.40)."
