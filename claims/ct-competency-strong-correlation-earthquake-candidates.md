## Evidence

### Görgülü Deniz 2026

Görgülü Deniz, Cömert Melike, Demir Mustafa, Şahin Çiğdem. (2026). The role of the 2023 earthquakes in the competencies and critical thinking skills of teacher candidates in Türkiye. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05006-2

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlational analysis of 304 teacher candidates at İnönü University using the PPCPS and CTSS self-report scales. The article reports r = 0.657, p < 0.01, noting coefficients of 0.60 and above indicate a strong relationship. The authors state "this association does not allow for the establishment of a causal relationship between the variables."

> "Pearson correlation analysis results show a positive, significant, and strong correlation between teacher candidates' critical thinking skills scores and their personal and professional competency scores (r = 0.657, p < 0.01)."
