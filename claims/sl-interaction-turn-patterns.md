## Evidence

### Mercedes Rico García and Paula Ferreira da Silva 2017

Mercedes Rico García and Paula Ferreira da Silva. (2017). Acquisition of Language and Intercultural Competences in Tourism and Hospitality Studies through Active Experimentation in Second Life. Teaching English with Technology, 18(2), 69-92. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1177703

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Transcript analysis of recorded SL and face-to-face role-plays counting concepts generated, turn-taking and language; the article also notes SL tasks "lasted longer than in FtF (9 versus 7 m inutes respectively".

> "the participants from the experimental group took more conversational turns ( engaged in more dynamic interaction) than those in the FtF class, but produced fewer num bers of words per turn than in the FtF interaction, although there were no significant dif ferences in the total number of words produced in the two types of conversations."
