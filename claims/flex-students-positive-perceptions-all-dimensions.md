`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

End-of-term online survey of the FLEX pilot group (n = 117) using items on interest/enjoyment, structure, coherence, usability, guidance, motivation and learning outcome. The article reports "positive perceptions of FLEX in all dimensions" with agreement from 73.0% to 84.6%; usability was rated especially positively.
