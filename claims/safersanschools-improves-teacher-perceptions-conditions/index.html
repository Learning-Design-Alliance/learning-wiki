SaferSanerSchools™ Whole-School Change program 2018¶

SaferSanerSchools™ Whole-School Change program. (2018). Evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/safersanerschools-whole-school-change/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Surveys and interviews with teachers and staff from the Pittsburgh-based study (Augustine et al., 2018) indicated general satisfaction with most program components. Ratings were "significantly more positive in participating schools than in comparison schools" on the four named domains.

"Survey ratings were significantly more positive in participating schools than in comparison schools for conduct management, teacher leadership, school leadership, and overall teaching and learning conditions."
