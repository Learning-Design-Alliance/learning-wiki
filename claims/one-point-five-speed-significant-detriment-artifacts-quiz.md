# Watching a novel lecture at 1.5× speed significantly reduced immediate quiz performance compared with 1.0× speed for the artifacts video

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Medical students randomized to watch the artifacts video at 1.5× speed scored significantly lower on a written assessment than the group watching at 1.0× speed. [→ Song K 2018](#song-k-2018)

## Evidence

### Song K 2018

Song K, Chakraborty A, Dawson M, Dugan A, Adkins B, Doty C. (2018). Does the Podcast Video Playback Speed Affect Comprehension for Novel Curriculum Delivery? A Randomized Trial. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2017.10.36027

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled pilot trial with 54 medical students randomized to two counterbalanced groups; t-test comparison of artifacts quiz scores at 1.5× vs 1.0× speed showed "a significant (p=0.0188) detriment in performance" with means 61.4 vs 72.7 and Cohen's d = 0.654.

> "There was a significant (p=0.0188) detriment in performance on the artifacts quiz at 1.5× speed (mean 61.4; 95% confidence interval [CI]-53.9, 68.9) compared to the control group at normal speed (mean 72.7; 95% CI−66.8, 78.6)."
