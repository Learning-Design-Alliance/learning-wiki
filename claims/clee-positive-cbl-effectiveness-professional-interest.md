## Evidence

### Liu Zhipeng 2024

Liu Zhipeng, Na Guanqiong, Liu Lihua, Tian Sicong, Shan Yujuan. (2024). Investigation and implementation of case-based learning in the sino-foreign joint program of preventive medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06372-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

CLEE analysis reported in the Results from 408 valid forms collected across the five cases. The CLEE measured professional interest, independent learning, critical thinking, information acquisition, and communication ability, each rated 0–4 and scored out of 100. No effect size is printed for the overall score.

> "the average score for students' course learning effectiveness evaluation exceeded 80 points (Fig. 3A), with over half scoring above 85 points (Fig. 3C)"

### Liu Zhipeng 2024 (2)

Liu Zhipeng, Na Guanqiong, Liu Lihua, Tian Sicong, Shan Yujuan. (2024). Investigation and implementation of case-based learning in the sino-foreign joint program of preventive medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06372-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dimension-level CLEE analysis reported in the Results with Fig. 3B. Professional interest rose significantly across the case sequence versus Case 1 (P < 0.05); only the significance threshold is printed, no effect size. The authors interpret this as students being "generally satisfied with the overall evaluation of the CBL course."

> "students' PI significantly increased as the case teaching was implemented (P < 0.05, Fig. 3B)"
