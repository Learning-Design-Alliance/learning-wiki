## Evidence

### Rohwer Anke 2013

Rohwer Anke, Young Taryn, van Schalkwyk Susan. (2013). Effective or just practical? An evaluation of an online postgraduate module on evidence-based medicine (EBM). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-77

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Tutor interview theme, not a tested result. Tutors argued that purely theoretical discussion misses the point and that students see relevance more clearly when learning is linked to a case from their own practice.

> "Tutors also felt strongly about the importance of making EBM applicable and relevant to students' clinical practice."
