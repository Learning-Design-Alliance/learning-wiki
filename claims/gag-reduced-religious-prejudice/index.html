Evidence¶

Obiekezie and Timothy 2015¶

Eucharia Obiageli Obiekezie & Alexander Essien Timothy. (2015). Fostering Ethnic and Religious Harmony through Classroom Language Experiences. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.25. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1078506

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent t-test comparison of post-test religious prejudice between control (mean 23.9) and experimental (mean 27.8) groups, 38 students each, in the University of Calabar International Secondary School study. The article reports "a calculated t of -2.715 and a p – value of 0.0082". No effect size printed.

"The result as shown in table 3 indicated a calculated t of -2.715 and a p – value of 0.0082. Therefore, there was evidence that students exposed to the Generalis ation Assessment Game had significantly less religious prejudice than those not exposed."
