## Evidence

### Sanyaolu Olutosin 2026

Sanyaolu Olutosin, Reeves-Doyle Brandy, Olaniyan Afolakemi C., Max Tarenina, Asala Adetoun, Osime Esther. (2026). Relationships Between Physical Activity, Sleep, Psychological Well-Being, and Academic Performance Among Native American College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph23040491

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Univariate general linear model on the secondary analysis of the Spring 2023 ACHA-NCHA sample of 1914 Native American college students, with sleep and PA as fixed factors adjusting for sex, age, and academic year. The "adjusted mean difference of 2.97" favored good sleep (good sleep M = 45.19; poor sleep M = 41.33).

> "Students with good sleep reported higher PWB than those with poor sleep, F(1, 1817) = 62.08, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.033, with an adjusted mean difference of 2.97, 95% CI: 1.69−4.24"
