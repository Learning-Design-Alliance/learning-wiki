Jones et al. 2015¶

Presented at the Annual Meeting of the Society for Research in Educational Effectiveness, Washington, DC. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED558151

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Interim PALS analysis for Cohort 2 kindergarten and first grade students. Table 5 reports standardized effect sizes of 0.438 (kindergarten) and 0.372 (first grade), each p < .0001, with a pooled standardized effect size of 0.400 (robust SE 0.0443, p < .0001) — large, at or above d = 0.8 territory relative to the printed scale.

"Second cohort MCLP participants also scored higher on the PALS (Table 5); scores were .438 and .372 standardized units higher than control students (p < .0001). Pooled together, these results indicate that the MCLP had a large impact (.40 standardized effect) on spring PALS scores (p < .0001)."
