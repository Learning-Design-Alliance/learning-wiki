## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the Expressive One Word Picture Vocabulary Test, non-native signers scored below native signers, who scored below hearing children. [→ Marshall C 2015](#marshall-c-2015)

## Evidence

### Marshall C 2015

Marshall C, Jones A, Denmark T, Mason K, Atkinson J, Botting N and Morgan G (2015) Deaf children's non-verbal working memory is impacted by their language experience. Front. Psychol. 6:527. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00527

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA controlling for age and WASI score on the Expressive One Word Picture Vocabulary Test showed a highly significant effect of group, F(2, 54) = 34.829, p < 0.001, with all three pairwise comparisons significant.

> "this was driven by all three groups being signiﬁcantly diﬀerent from one another: non-native signer < native signer, p = 0.029, non-native signer < hearing, p = 0.001, and native signer < hearing, p = 0.009."
