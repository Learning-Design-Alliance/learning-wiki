`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative inference from content analysis (propositional analysis of discourse) of student reports, posters and records in the teaching experiment. The authors attribute the disparity to the imagination borders offered by GT, where any subject was allowed, whereas the schoolyard offered little to trigger a plurality of themes.

> "While the control class, restricted to the schoolyard, opted by bryophytes (moss, slimes, and trees), the experimental class chose a different theme for each group."
