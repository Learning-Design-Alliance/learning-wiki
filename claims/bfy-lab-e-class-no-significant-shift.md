### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2017

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2017). Students’ views about the nature of experimental physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020110](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020110)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Matched pre/post analysis of BFY students (N = 1558) reports pre 0.727 and post 0.723 favorable fractions; the article states the fraction "did not shift signiﬁcantly" (p = 0.6). No effect size is printed, so none is reported here.

> "Alternatively, Table II shows that students in the BFY courses responded favorably to on average just un- der three-quarters of the E-CLASS items, and this frac- tion did not shift signiﬁcantly over one semester (or quar- ter) of instruction ( p = 0.6)."
