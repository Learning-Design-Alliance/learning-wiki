## Evidence

### Fong Raymond 2026

Fong Raymond, Kwan Connie C. Y. (2026). Accuracy of Visual Inspection of Thickened Liquids: Implications for Multidisciplinary Dysphagia Management and Clinical Nutrition. Journal of Human Nutrition and Dietetics. https://doi.org/10.1111/jhn.70294

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of 57 practising SLPs and 50 postgraduate SLP students who viewed four 12-s videos of IDDSI-standardised thickened liquids and estimated scoops of thickener. The study reports "49.5% (99/200)" student accuracy versus "47.4% (108/228)" for practitioners, with no significant difference (U = 1502.5, p = 0.60). No effect size is printed.

> "Students correctly identified the thickness in 49.5% (99/200) of trials, while practitioners were correct in 47.4% (108/228) of trials. A Mann–Whitney U test confirmed that there was no statistically significant difference in the overall accuracy between students and practising clinicians (U = 1502.5, p = 0.60)."
