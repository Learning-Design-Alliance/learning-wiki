Evidence¶

Jan van der Meij 2017¶

Jan van der Meij, Marije van Amelsvoort, Anjo Anjewierden. (2017). How design guides learning from matrix diagrams. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-017-9425-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Two-question interview after the study session, coded into dominant reading direction. Self-reports converged with the eye-tracking finding that content, not arrows, drove reading order.

"overall 63% of the participants self-reported having studied the diagram primarily based on the personality disorder types, 19.8% based on categories, 13.6% based on both types and categories, and 3.7% in random order."
