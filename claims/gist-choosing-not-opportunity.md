The review reports, citing Whyte (1986), that the GIST project followed a cohort of 2000 girls and boys through secondary school in Great Britain. Suggested causes included "the perceived difficulty of physics" and paucity of role models.

> "The primary conclusion from this analytical part of the study was that the problem was not one of girls lacking the opportunity to study science, but one of girls choosing not to study science."
