## Evidence

### Hornstra 2017

Hornstra, Lisette; Majoor, Marieke; Peetsma, Thea. (2017). Achievement goal profiles and developments in effort and achievement in upper elementary school. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 87(4), 606-629. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12167

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel analysis of CITO language achievement scores from school records across grades 4-6 for 722 students, controlling for background and cognitive ability. The article reports the gain differences as medium effect sizes: "d = .56 and d = .46 for the approach‐oriented and moderate profiles versus the multiple goals profile, respectively."

> "we did not find an association between profile membership and average achievement scores (b = 2.29, p = .539; b = 2.06, p = .573, for the approach‐oriented and moderate/indifferent profile, respectively). However, students with an approach‐oriented or moderate profile showed stronger increases in language achievement over time"
