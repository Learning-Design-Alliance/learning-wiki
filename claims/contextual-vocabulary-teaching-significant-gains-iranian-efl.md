## Evidence

### Rezaei and Dezhara 2011

Omid Rezaei, Salman Dezhara. (2011). An Investigation of the Possible Effects of Favored Contexts in Second Language Vocabulary Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 4(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v4n4p97

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest control group study (pre-experimental design) with two intact classes of Iranian institute learners; the experimental group read eight treatment passages over four sessions. The article reports a t-value of -24.3 with 48 d.f. significant at the 0.05 level, and concludes the contextual treatment was "verified to be viable both in sport and geography texts".

> "Based on the data obtained, a t-value of -24.3 with 48 d.f. (degree of freedom) is significant at the 0.05 level."
