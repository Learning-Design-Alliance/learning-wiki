## Evidence

### Næss S. Ø. 2026

Næss S. Ø., Sivertsen B., Hysing M., Dovran A., Tobiassen S., Hafstad G. S., Kjørstad K. (2026). School Functioning Among Norwegian Children With a History of Maltreatment. Journal of Interpersonal Violence. https://doi.org/10.1177/08862605251351669

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square and logistic regression in the 192-child sample: 37.0% of the multivictimization group versus 23.2% of the lower-exposure group had special education decisions (OR = 1.95 [0.99, 3.85], p = .070). No standardized effect size printed; the authors attribute the null partly to limited statistical power.

> "While no significant association was found between multivictimization and special education, the result approached significance (p = .070), suggesting that the severity of maltreatment may contribute to educational support needs."
