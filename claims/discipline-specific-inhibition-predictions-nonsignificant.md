## Evidence

### Brookman-Byrne A 2018

Brookman-Byrne A, Mareschal D, Tolmie AK, Dumontheil I (2018) Inhibitory control and counterintuitive science and maths reasoning in adolescence. PLoS ONE 13(6): e0198973. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198973

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory enter-method regressions on science and maths separately (N = 81): Stroop RT cost β = -.18, p = .080 (science accuracy) and β = -.18, p = .083 (maths accuracy); "the p-values were at trend".

> "Stroop RT cost was not a significant predictor of science (model 3) or maths (model 4) misconception accuracy, although the p-values were at trend and the coefficients were in the same direction as the combined analyses."
