## Evidence

### Isaacs 2025

Isaacs, J., Kuhfeld, M., Postell, S., & Hashim, A. (2025). Post-Pandemic School Closure Trends: Schools Serving Black Students Disproportionately Closed. NWEA White Paper. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/post-pandemic-school-closure-trends-schools-serving-black-students-disproportionately-closed/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison of lagged CCD demographics of closed versus open schools. Table 1 shows 22.3% of closed schools in 2024-25 were majority Black versus 9% of open schools. The overrepresentation "dipped slightly during the COVID-19 pandemic but has returned in the last few years."

> "schools serving a majority of Black students represented approximately one quarter of the schools that closed in 2024-25 despite representing less than 10% of all open schools."
