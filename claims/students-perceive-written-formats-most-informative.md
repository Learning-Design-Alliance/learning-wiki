## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Beth Thacker 2012

Beth Thacker, Ganesh Chapagain, David Pattillo, Keith West. (2012). The Effect of Problem Format on Students' Responses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1312.6004](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1312.6004)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Problem Format Survey administered to 91 students in the calculus-based class in Spring 2012 (Figure 8). The article prints the 95% figure for written-explanation formats and the 74% figure for MC and T/F as easiest. No inferential test is reported.

> "Show your work/Explain your reasoning combined with Short Answer and Other, all of which require written explanations, constitute (95%) the type of questions that students perceive give instructors the most information on their understanding. It is clear that the students perceive these question formats as harder, but that they feel they much better reflect their understanding of the content and skills being assessed."
