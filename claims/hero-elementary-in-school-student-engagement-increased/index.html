Evidence¶

Li 2020¶

Li, L., Luttgen, K., Huang, K., Weiser, G., Cornfield, O., Hess, H., Chow, E., & Schneider, M. (2020). Hero Elementary Playlist RCT Study in Formal School and Distance Learning Contexts. WestEd. https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/dd/index.asp

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Teacher report on engagement in the in-school implementation findings section. Teachers said students "highly engaged" and especially enjoyed viewing episodes and playing games. No quantitative engagement measure or effect size is reported; this is qualitative teacher perception.

"Teachers reported that students were highly engaged in the in-school environment, and student engagement increased over the duration of in-school implementation."
