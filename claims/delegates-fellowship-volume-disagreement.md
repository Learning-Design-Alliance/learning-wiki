### Gimm Oliver 2019

Gimm Oliver, Barczyński Marcin, Mihai Radu, Raffaelli Marco. (2019). Training in endocrine surgery. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-019-01828-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of national delegates on fellowship training; 16 of 28 (57%) replied. The article reports "Only three respondents considered that 200 or more endocrine surgical procedures should be performed during fellowship training"; no effect size is printed.

> "Sixteen national delegates (57% of 28) replied to the questionnaire on the training of fellows in endocrine surgery. Only three respondents considered that 200 or more endocrine surgical procedures should be performed during fellowship training in endocrine surgery, whereas 3 respondents considered 100–199 performed procedures to be sufficient."
