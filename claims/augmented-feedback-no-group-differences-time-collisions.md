## Evidence

### Bigras Catherine 2019

Bigras Catherine, Kairy Dahlia, Archambault Philippe S. (2019). Augmented feedback for powered wheelchair training in a virtual environment. Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12984-019-0482-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of a feedback group (terminal feedback on time, collisions and trajectory on a faded schedule) against a no-feedback control group across 18 training trials; mixed-effects models found no significant group effects. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Contrary to our hypothesis, no significant group differences were found for time to complete the VE or RE test tasks and the number of collisions."
