# The KITS intervention increased children's neural reactivity to negative performance feedback (FRN) from pre- to post-intervention, whereas services as usual did not

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children in the KITS group showed a significantly larger FRN to negative feedback at post-intervention than at baseline (t(19) = 3.20, p = .005), while the services as usual group showed no significant change. [→ McDermott 2017](#mcdermott-2017)
`q3 i?` The magnitude of FRN change from T1 to T2 differed significantly between groups, with the KITS group increasing (more negative, M = -5.21 µV) and the SAU group decreasing (M = 2.12 µV). [→ McDermott 2017 (2)](#mcdermott-2017-2)

## Evidence

### McDermott 2017

McDermott, J. M., Pears, K. C., Bruce, J., Kim, H. K., Roos, L. E., Yoerger, K., & Fisher, P. A. (2017). Improving Kindergarten Readiness in Children with Developmental Disabilities: Changes in Neural Correlates of Response Monitoring. Applied Neuropsychology: Child. https://doi.org/10.1080/21622965.2017.1286239

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA results from the randomized trial (n = 41 ERP subsample; 20 KITS, 21 SAU) on the flanker task. The KITS group's FRN to negative feedback became more negative from baseline to post-intervention, "compared to T1 (M = -15.57 µV, SD = 6.51 µV)" as printed in the results.

> "Post hoc paired samples t-tests revealed a significant difference between T1 and T2 for the KITS group (t(19) = 3.20, p = .005), such that this group exhibited a larger FRN in response to negative feedback at T2 (M = -20.78 µV, SD = 8.94 µV) compared to T1"

### McDermott 2017 (2)

McDermott, J. M., Pears, K. C., Bruce, J., Kim, H. K., Roos, L. E., Yoerger, K., & Fisher, P. A. (2017). Improving Kindergarten Readiness in Children with Developmental Disabilities: Changes in Neural Correlates of Response Monitoring. Applied Neuropsychology: Child. https://doi.org/10.1080/21622965.2017.1286239

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group follow-up t-test on FRN change scores in the same RCT subsample. The SAU group "showed a reduced (i.e., less negative) FRN to over time (M = 2.12 µV, SD = 10.22 µV)" per the printed results.

> "an independent samples t-test (t(39) = 2.63, p = .012) confirmed that the magnitude of change in the FRN between T1 and T2 was significantly different between groups (see Figure 3) with the KITS group exhibiting an increased FRN (i.e., more negative) over time (M = -5.21 µV, SD = 7.30 µV) compared to the SAU group"
