## Evidence

### Iqbal Tassawar 2018

Iqbal Tassawar, Iqbal Saqib, Hussain Syed Sajid, Khan Iftikhar Ahmed, Khan Hikmat Ullah, Rehman Attiqa. (2018). Fighting adult illiteracy with the help of the environmental print material. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0201902

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Content-complexity comparison: the number of letters and syllables of each alphabet content item in ELC and TLC were extracted and compared with paired-sample t-tests to check whether content complexity could explain the outcome differences.

> "The analysis regarding a number of letters in the ELC vs TLC found that there are significantly more letters (t = 2.828, p < 0.01) in ELC as compared to the TLC (ELCμ = 4.16, TLCμ = 3.76)."

### Iqbal Tassawar 2018 (2)

Iqbal Tassawar, Iqbal Saqib, Hussain Syed Sajid, Khan Iftikhar Ahmed, Khan Hikmat Ullah, Rehman Attiqa. (2018). Fighting adult illiteracy with the help of the environmental print material. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0201902

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-test on syllable counts of ELC versus TLC content found no significant difference; the article reports no effect size for this null result.

> "However, there were no significant differences found between syllables of the ELC and TLC."
