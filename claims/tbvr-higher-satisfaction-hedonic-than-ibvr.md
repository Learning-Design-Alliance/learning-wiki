## Evidence

### Lin Wan-Ni 2025

Lin Wan-Ni, Chuang Hai-Hua, Chao Yi-Ping, Hsin Li-Jen, Kang Chung-Jan, Fang Tuan-Jen, Li Hsueh-Yu, Lee Li-Ang. (2025). Image-based and textbook-based virtual reality training on operational skills among junior residents: a proof of concept study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07245-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

AttrakDiff2 questionnaire administered post-intervention in the cross-over pilot; IBVR "demonstrated significantly lower scores in hedonic stimulation" and hedonic identification than TBVR. GSS was also lower for IBVR (p = 0.03); pragmatic quality and attractiveness did not differ.

> "the IBVR module demonstrated significantly lower scores in hedonic stimulation (1.5 [IQR, 0.9–1.8] vs. 1.9 [IQR, 1.5–2.4]; p = 0.01) and hedonic identification (1.6 [IQR, 1.2–1.8] vs. 2.1 [IQR, 1.7–2.7]; p = 0.03) compared to the TBVR module"
