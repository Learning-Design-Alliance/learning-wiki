Azkiyah et al. 2014¶

S.N. Azkiyah, Simone Doolaard, Bert P. M. Creemers, M.P.C. (Greetje) Van Der Werf. (2014). The Effects of Two Intervention Programs on Teaching Quality and Student Achievement. Journal of Classroom Interaction, 49(1), 4-11. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1100411

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multilevel covariance analysis of post-test scores; student-level variance (67%) was twice the school-level variance (33). Pretest coefficient .146 (SE .028), gender (female) .397 (SE .155), province (Banten) -1.955 (SE .484), school national exam (high) 1.877 (SE .862) in model 4.

"Student prior achievement and gender had significant effects: students who achieved a high score in the pre-test also achieved a high score in the post-test and female students achieved higher than the male students. Surprisingly, SES as represented by father’s education, was not significant at p < .01."
