de la Torre 2013¶

University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/turning-around-low-performing-schools-chicago-full-report

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Difference-in-difference comparison of reformed elementary schools against matched non-reformed schools with similar prior performance and student types, using standardized ITBS/ISAT scores in grades 3-8. The report finds "significant improvements in test scores compared with similar schools" but notes gains were not immediate.

"Elementary schools that went through reform made significant improvements in test scores compared with similar schools that did not; however, large improvements in achievement did not occur immediately."

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Trend analysis of standardized reading and math scores four years after intervention, adjusted for changes in entering student characteristics. The gap to the system average "decreased by nearly half in reading and by almost two-thirds in math." No effect size is printed.

"The gap in test scores between reformed elementary schools and the system average decreased by nearly half in reading and by almost two-thirds in math four years after the intervention took place."
