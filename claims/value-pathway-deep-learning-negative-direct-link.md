## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kickert R 2018

Kickert R, Stegers‐Jager KM, Meeuwisse M, Prinzie P, Arends LR. (2018). The role of the assessment policy in the relation between learning and performance. Medical Education 52(3):324–335. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13487

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural paths from the final invariant multi-group SEM (Fig. 2) fitted to both policy cohorts. The article reports "a negative direct relationship between deep learning and average grade" and a positive direct self-efficacy relation.

> "Consequently, there was a positive path from value through deep learning, resource management and participation to Year‐1 performance (see Fig. 2). There also was a negative direct relationship between deep learning and average grade, whereas self‐efficacy showed a positive direct relation with average grade."

### Kickert R 2018 (2)

Kickert R, Stegers‐Jager KM, Meeuwisse M, Prinzie P, Arends LR. (2018). The role of the assessment policy in the relation between learning and performance. Medical Education 52(3):324–335. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13487

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reported R² from the final multi-group SEM for the two policy groups. The article states the model "explained 34% of the variance in average grades" for the old group and 32% for the new group; no standardised effect size is printed for this.

> "The model explained 34% of the variance in average grades for students under the old assessment policy, and 32% of the variance for students under the new assessment policy."
