## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The group of operators systematically educated in endoscopy since the beginning of specialization had a significantly higher overall stone-removal success rate than the self-taught or loosely supervised group. [→ Librenjak Davor 2011](#librenjak-davor-2011)

## Evidence

### Librenjak Davor 2011

Librenjak Davor, Šitum Marijan, Gugić Dijana, Milostić Kazimir, Duvnjak Mario. (2011). Ureterorenoscopic treatment of ureteral stones – influence of operator's experience and skill on the procedure outcome. Croatian Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.3325/cmj.2011.52.55

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective comparison of 422 ureterorenoscopy procedures at one Croatian department (2001-2009), split between 4 early-trained and 4 later-trained urologists. The results section reports "a significantly higher overall success rate (χ2 = 24.22, P < 0.001)" for the first group; no effect size is printed.

> "The first group of operators had a significantly higher overall success rate (χ2 = 24.22, P < 0.001)."
