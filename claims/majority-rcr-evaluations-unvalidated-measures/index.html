M. van Loon 2026¶

A Review of Measures Used and a Content Analysis of Outcome Measures with Reported Validation Evidence. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10187-8

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic scoping review following PRISMA, searching three databases to March 2023. The review reports "162 studies met our inclusion criteria" and that "46 (28%) articles used measures described as validated"; of 31 unique measures, 20 lacked specific RCR items or had inaccessible evidence.

"The systematic scoping review retrieved 1917 results, of which 162 studies met our inclusion criteria and reported the impact of RCR courses on participants"

Related Claims¶
