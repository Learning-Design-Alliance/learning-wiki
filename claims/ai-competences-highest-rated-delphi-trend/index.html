q2 · i? · associational · r1

Delphi survey phase in which 70 international experts rated 15 AI-in-education themes on importance, impact, and challenges (1–10 scale). The article reports the competence theme as highest-rated: "AI-related competences for learners and educators (M = 8.57)" for Impact and M = 8.64 for Importance.

"The highest-rated theme in terms of Impact was AI-related competences for learners and educators (M = 8.57), which also emerged as the most Important (M = 8.64). Although it was perceived as relatively challenging (M = 7.49), it still held a positive priority indicator"
