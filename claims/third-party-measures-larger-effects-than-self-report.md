## Evidence

### McEwan D. 2017

McEwan D., Ruissen G.R., Eys M.A., Zumbo B.D., Beauchamp M.R. (2017). The Effectiveness of Teamwork Training on Teamwork Behaviors and Team Performance: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Controlled Interventions. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0169604

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Measurement moderator analysis (Table 3) in the teamwork pool comparing third-party versus self-report criterion measures. Both were significant, but third-party d = 0.80 exceeded self-report d = 0.38 (Q = 6.02, p = 0.014). For team performance, objective (d = 0.61) and third-party (d = 0.56) measures were both significant.

> "significant improvements on teamwork emerged when either third-party (d = 0.80) or self-report (d = 0.38) measures of teamwork were utilized; the effect size for third-party measures was significantly larger (Q = 6.02, p = 0.014) than the effect size for self-report measures."
