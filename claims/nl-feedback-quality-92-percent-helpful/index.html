Evidence¶

Hasnain Heickal and Andrew Lan 2026¶

Hasnain Heickal and Andrew Lan. (2026). A Classroom Study of LLM-Generated Feedback Intervention in Introductory Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08807 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.08807

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quality evaluation of 442 natural language feedback instances using GPT-4.1 as a judge, labeling each as helpful, vague, incorrect, or harmful. The authors note "no feedback instance explicitly revealed a solution or bypassed student reasoning".

"We find that 92.5% of instances are labeled helpful (409/442), 5.4% vague (24/442), 2.0% incorrect (9/442), and none harmful."
