## Evidence

### Diao Y 2025

Diao Y, Wu M, Xu J, Pan Y. (2025). Multiple Large Language Models’ Performance on the Chinese Medical Licensing Examination: Quantitative Comparative Study. JMIR Human Factors. https://doi.org/10.2196/77978

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Seven-year comparison of the four Chinese models' scores against official answer keys, with DeepSeek strongest in unit 1 (73.2%) and unit 3 (71.5%) accuracy. "DeepSeek achieved the highest overall average score across the NMLE" with mean 454.8 versus 442.3, 426.5, and 413.7.

> "Among the 4 Chinese LLMs, DeepSeek achieved the highest overall average score across the NMLE in 2018 to 2024, with a mean score of 454.8, which was notably higher than those of ERNIE Bot (442.3), Tongyi Qianwen (426.5), and Doubao (413.7)"

### Diao Y 2025 (2)

Diao Y, Wu M, Xu J, Pan Y. (2025). Multiple Large Language Models’ Performance on the Chinese Medical Licensing Examination: Quantitative Comparative Study. JMIR Human Factors. https://doi.org/10.2196/77978

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square tests of DeepSeek's overall accuracy against each other Chinese model; "DeepSeek’s overall average accuracy (75.8%, SD 2.73%) was significantly higher than those of the other Chinese models" on all three pairwise comparisons.

> "DeepSeek’s overall average accuracy (75.8%, SD 2.73%) was significantly higher than those of the other Chinese models (χ²₁=11.4, P=.001 vs ERNIE Bot; χ²₁=28.7, P<.001 vs Tongyi Qianwen; χ²₁=45.3, P<.001 vs Doubao)"
