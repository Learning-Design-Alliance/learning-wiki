# Mastery-approach goal scales containing goal-relevant language are not significantly related to performance outcomes, while scales lacking goal-relevant language correlate positively with performance

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hulleman 2010

Hulleman, C. S., Schrager, S. M., Bodmann, S. M., & Harackiewicz, J. M. (2010). A Meta-Analytic Review of Achievement Goal Measures: Different Labels for the Same Constructs or Different Constructs With Similar Labels? Psychological Bulletin. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0018947

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis of mastery-approach scale item content across the 243-study corpus (91,087 participants). Scales with goal-relevant language showed r = .05 (not significant); scales lacking such language showed r = .14, "a positive relationship with performance outcomes".

> "Mastery-approach goal scales that contained goal-relevant language were not significantly related to performance outcomes (r ⫽ .05), whereas those that did not contain goal-relevant language had a positive relationship with performance outcomes (r ⫽ .14)"
