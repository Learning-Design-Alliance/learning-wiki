## Evidence

### Yifan Lu 2025

Yifan Lu, K. Supriya, Shanna Shaked, Elizabeth H. Simmons, and Alexander Kusenko. (2025). Incentivizing supplemental math assignments and using AI-generated hints is associated with improved exam performance. arXiv. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/v93w-ytyc](https://doi.org/10.1103/v93w-ytyc)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of hint usage rates across PEER/WA and URG/Man groups with 95% confidence intervals (Fig. 4), based on hint-use logs from the Kudu homework platform; the article reports no difference across gender and race/ethnicity groups.

> "We observed no difference in the use of AI-generated hints across gender and race/ethnicity groups Fig. 4."

### Yifan Lu 2025 (2)

Yifan Lu, K. Supriya, Shanna Shaked, Elizabeth H. Simmons, and Alexander Kusenko. (2025). Incentivizing supplemental math assignments and using AI-generated hints is associated with improved exam performance. arXiv. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/v93w-ytyc](https://doi.org/10.1103/v93w-ytyc)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive uptake analysis of the Kudu hint feature on question pools of roughly 40 problems per exam; "88% of the students used AI-generated hints on at least 1 homework question" and 57% on at least 5 questions.

> "when available, 88% of the students used AI-generated hints on at least 1 homework question (among the problems that we found to match the exam questions), and 57% students used AI-generated hints on at least 5 questions."
