## Evidence

### K. Cecire 2011

K. Cecire (2011). Particle Physics Masterclasses. Proceedings of the DPF-2011 Conference, Providence, RI. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1109.2559](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1109.2559)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Observations from mentors and teachers in the 2011 U.S. program found that "putting ATLAS and CMS masterclass i nstitutes together in the same video conference caused problems"; students did not understand the other experiment's terminology and could lose interest. No quantified measure is reported.

> "putting ATLAS and CMS masterclass i nstitutes together in the same video conference caused problems. The greatest of these was that students did not understand terminology and the exercise from the "other" experiment and were therefore confused"
