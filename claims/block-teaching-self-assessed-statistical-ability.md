## Evidence

### Li Chen 2018

Li Chen, Wang Ling, Zhang Yuhai, Li Chanjuan, Xu Yongyong, Shang Lei, Xia Jielai. (2018). Assessment of a block curriculum design on medical postgraduates’ perception towards biostatistics: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1232-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square-tested self-assessment percentages (Fig. 1) for nine statistical topics in the two cohorts; block students were more acquainted with descriptive statistics, interval estimation, t-test, chi-square test, and linear regression and correlation.

> "Compared to the traditional teaching group, almost 25% more students in the block group could apply the knowledge with rationale and software practice."

### Li Chen 2018 (2)

Li Chen, Wang Ling, Zhang Yuhai, Li Chanjuan, Xu Yongyong, Shang Lei, Xia Jielai. (2018). Assessment of a block curriculum design on medical postgraduates’ perception towards biostatistics: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1232-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square comparison of self-assessment ratings between cohorts found no significant difference for analysis of variance and nonparametric tests; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, no statistically significant difference was observed in the self-assessment ability regarding the knowledge of analysis of variance and nonparametric test between the two groups."
