## Evidence

### Si Qingzong 2026

Si Qingzong, Li Siqi, Cai Lulu, Huang Qian, Huang Chupeng, Zhang Xinyue, An Xiaoli. (2026). Experiential teaching centered on science popularisation videos: a randomised controlled trial on the impact on learning outcomes and core competencies of dental preventive medicine students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09910-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Assessor-blinded parallel RCT of 72 dental preventive medicine students; ANCOVA with baseline T0 as covariate. The between-group difference was significant (F = 17.612, P<0.001, partial η² = 0.203), described as a "large effect size".

> "After adjusting for baseline levels, the adjusted mean score for the intervention group at T1 was 22.606 (95% CI: 22.031–23.182), which was significantly higher than that of the control group (20.894; 95% CI: 20.318–21.469)."

### Si Qingzong 2026 (2)

Si Qingzong, Li Siqi, Cai Lulu, Huang Qian, Huang Chupeng, Zhang Xinyue, An Xiaoli. (2026). Experiential teaching centered on science popularisation videos: a randomised controlled trial on the impact on learning outcomes and core competencies of dental preventive medicine students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09910-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Independent samples t-test on score improvement (T1 − T0) between the intervention (n=36) and control (n=36) groups; the printed Cohen's d of 0.657 falls in the medium-to-large range.

> "An independent samples t-test revealed that the score improvement in the intervention group (3.33 ± 2.18) was significantly higher than that in the control group (1.69 ± 2.29) (t = 2.787, p = 0.007, Cohen’s d = 0.657)"
