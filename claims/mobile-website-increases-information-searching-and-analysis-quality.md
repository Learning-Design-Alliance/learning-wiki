## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Isham Shah bin Hassan 2011

Isham Shah bin Hassan, Ramlee Mustafa & Mohd Arif Ismail. (2011). Mobile Technology and CAD Technology Integration in Teaching Architectural Design Process for Producing Creative Product. i-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, 8(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102114

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of search frequencies logged during the six-week design project across the treatment and control polytechnics; the article reports the search totals and means as printed, with no inferential test on this measure.

> "Research outcomes shows that the total number of student searches in treatment group is 392 (M=6.53) and total searches in control group is 178 (M=2.97). This outcome clearly shows that student searching activities increased with web site integration related to real case based on mobile technology."

### Isham Shah bin Hassan 2011 (2)

Isham Shah bin Hassan, Ramlee Mustafa & Mohd Arif Ismail. (2011). Mobile Technology and CAD Technology Integration in Teaching Architectural Design Process for Producing Creative Product. i-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, 8(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102114

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test comparing quality of design information produced in the analysis activity, evaluated with a 20-item instrument (Cronbach's alpha 0.9242). The article reports "t (118) = 18.369, significant".

> "In analysis stage, inferential data shows that there is a significant difference between the mobile technology integrated data (M=81.2) and conventional method (M=65.5), t (118) = 18.369, significant."
