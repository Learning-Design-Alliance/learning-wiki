### The appraisal of fear appeals as a challenge or a threat influences a subsequent decline or increase in attainment value and academic self-efficacy 2014

The appraisal of fear appeals as a challenge or a threat influences a subsequent decline or increase in attainment value and academic self-efficacy. (2014). https://eric.ed.gov/?q=appraisal+of+fear+appeals+challenge+threat+attainment+value+academic+self-efficacy

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Paths from the same three-wave SEM (H1 and H2), estimated in Mplus with robust maximum likelihood and cluster-adjusted standard errors. The attainment value path to challenge appraisal was β = .53, p < .001. The pattern supports EVT/CVT accounts in which attainment value establishes personal relevance and academic self-efficacy establishes response options.

> "A stronger challenge appraisal followed from higher attainment value (β = .53, p < .001) and higher academic self-efficacy (β = .25, p < .001) whereas a stronger threat appraisal resulted from higher attainment value (β = .31, p < .001) and lower academic self-efficacy (β = -.38, p < .001)."
