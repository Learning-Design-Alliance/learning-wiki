## Evidence

### Malone 2025

Malone, L. A., Hill, N. M., Tripp, H., Zipunnikov, V., Wolpert, D. M. & Bastian, A. J. (2025). The control of movement gradually transitions from feedback control to feedforward adaptation throughout childhood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00304-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA comparing epochs of Push ball (participants aged 5+) and Launch ball (162 participants aged 5+) with task and age-group as main effects. The article concludes younger children "were just less successful at the task (i.e., they miss the target more)" when only adaptation was possible.

> "There was no effect of task (F1571 = 0.007, p = 0.94) nor a task x age-group interaction (F6571 = 0.95,p = 0.46). As expected, there was a signiﬁcant effect of age-group (F6571 =2 . 8 ,p = 0.01)."
