## Evidence

### Assumpção 2003

Assumpção, R. (2003). Proposal of an experimental strategy to introduce Relativity in elementary physics courses. Originally published 2000. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0301010](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0301010)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classroom activity in which about 20 students in groups of 4 measured height and fall time of three blocks using conventional clocks and visual detection. The author reports g-values "may typically range from 5 m/s2 to 15 m/s2", with data rejection by most students.

> "The relatively low frequency of good results ( g-values may typically range from 5 m/s2 to 15 m/s2 ! ) causes embarrassment. Most students reject t he e xperimental data and some even refuse to present a report ."
