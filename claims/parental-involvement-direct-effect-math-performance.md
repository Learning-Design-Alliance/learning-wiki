## Evidence

### İlter İlhan 2025

İlter İlhan, Aksoy Nuri Can, Ceylan Mehmet. (2025). The relationships between parental involvement, teacher support, and mathematics performance: mediating roles of academic self-efficacy and academic buoyancy. BMC Psychology, 13, 1222. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03547-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same PLS-SEM structural model (n = 363 sixth-graders); the direct path from parental involvement to mathematics performance was significant (β = 0.093, p < 0.04), supporting H2. Only the path coefficient is printed, no standardized effect size.

> "After controlling for gender and age, the analysis revealed the results showed that both perceived TS (β = 0.151, p < 0.001) and PI (β = 0.093, p < 0.04) had positive and significant direct effects on MP, thereby supporting hypotheses H1 and H2."
