## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After controlling for prior mental health and demographics, neither incomplete compulsory-school grades nor non-eligibility to higher education nor academic performance at age 12 predicted internalizing or externalizing problems at age 20. [→ Agnafors S 2021](#agnafors-s-2021)

## Evidence

### Agnafors S 2021

Agnafors S, Barmark M, Sydsjö G. (2021). Mental health and academic performance: a study on selection and causation effects from childhood to early adulthood. Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00127-020-01934-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear regressions (Models 4–5) in the SESBiC cohort predicting Adult Self Report scores at age 20; the study reports "no association was found between incomplete grades from compulsory school and externalizing problems at age 20", and likewise no associations for non-eligibility or age-12 performance. Equivalence was not tested.

> "In multivariate analysis, no association was found between incomplete grades from compulsory school and externalizing problems at age 20 after controlling for gender, maternal symptoms of postpartum depression, conduct problems at age 12 and emotional problems at age 12 (Table 3)."
