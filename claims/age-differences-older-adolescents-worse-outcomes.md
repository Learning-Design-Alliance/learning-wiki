Age main effect in the descriptive analysis of the 1100-adolescent cross-sectional study (600 young 12–14 vs 500 older 15–16 per Table 1). The article reports "The older adolescents show more boredom, less fun, less PA practice, lower academic performance and higher alcohol consumption."

> "Age was significantly correlated to the variable of boredom (p = 0.003), fun (p < 0.001), PA (p < 0.001), academic performance (p < 0.001) and alcohol consumption (p < 0.001). The older adolescents show more boredom, less fun, less PA practice, lower academic performance and higher alcohol consumption."
