## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Richardson 2013

Richardson, Ronald; Brouillette, Liane. (2013). Historic and Ethnic Music Found to Increase Geographic Understanding: A Quasi-Experimental Study. Journal for Learning through the Arts, 9(1). http://escholarship.org/uc/item/71g4x3cr

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Change-score analysis of the 29-question ToGRA attitude survey for the same 215 students: control attitudes declined (mean change -0.11) while treatment attitudes rose (0.45); the article reports the difference statistically significant, p=<0.0001, and an overall effect size of 0.569 (medium).

> "The mean change in control group scores, -0.11 (with a standard deviation of 0.52) is subtracted from the mean change in treatment group scores, 0.45 (with a standard deviation of 0.60) resulting in a Difference of Means score of 0.4"
