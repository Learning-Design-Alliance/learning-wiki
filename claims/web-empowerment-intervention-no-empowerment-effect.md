## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a pragmatic RCT, the web-based intervention had no significant effects on empowerment, general self-efficacy, mastery, or coping compared with a sham-website control. [→ Samoocha D 2011](#samoocha-d-2011)

## Evidence

### Samoocha D 2011

Samoocha D, Snels IAK, Bruinvels DJ, Anema JR, van der Beek AJ. (2011). Effectiveness of an Interactive Website Aimed at Empowerment of Disability Benefit Claimants: Results of a Pragmatic Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Occupational Rehabilitation. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10926-010-9283-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-armed pragmatic RCT with 123 intervention and 119 control claimants; regression analyses adjusted for baseline value and gender. The article reports "no significant effects on empowerment, general self-efficacy, mastery, and coping"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The intervention www.wiagesprek.nl had no significant effects on empowerment, general self-efficacy, mastery, and coping."

### Samoocha D 2011 (2)

Samoocha D, Snels IAK, Bruinvels DJ, Anema JR, van der Beek AJ. (2011). Effectiveness of an Interactive Website Aimed at Empowerment of Disability Benefit Claimants: Results of a Pragmatic Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Occupational Rehabilitation. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10926-010-9283-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal GEE analysis of outcomes measured at three time points (Competence and Impact subscales, general self-efficacy, mastery), correcting for regression to the mean; "no statistically significant differences in the development of empowerment over time".

> "Additional longitudinal GEE analysis furthermore pointed out no statistically significant differences in the development of empowerment over time, between the two study groups"
