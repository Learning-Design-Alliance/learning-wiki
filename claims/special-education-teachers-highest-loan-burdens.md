`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

NTPS 2020–21 analysis by field of main teaching assignment (Table A2, Figure 4) reports "the most likely to have ever taken out student loans (65.2%)" for special education teachers, with 44.4% owing some or all and 15.4% owing their entire balance.

> "Compared to teachers in other subject areas, teachers working on behalf of students with disabilities are the most likely to have ever taken out student loans (65.2%)."
