### Montero-Marin et al 2021

Montero-Marin et al. (2021). Teachers “Finding Peace in a Frantic World”: An Experimental Study of Self-Taught and Instructor-Led Mindfulness Program Formats on Acceptability, Effectiveness, and Mechanisms. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000542

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple mediation path-analytic analysis comparing treatment conditions through pre-post gains in mindfulness and self-compassion, using a weighted log-likelihood function with cluster-robust standard errors and Monte Carlo confidence intervals. The article reports the instructor-led arm indirectly improved outcomes via these mediators relative to the self-taught arm.
