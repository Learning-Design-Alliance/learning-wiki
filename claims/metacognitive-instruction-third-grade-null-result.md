> "The comparison of the results of the control group and the experimental group in the pre -test with third graders has shown that they are statistically different (the difference is statistically valid, p=0.000), whereas the results of the post -test indicate that the difference between the two groups is not significant ( t(128)=2.83, p=0.778."
