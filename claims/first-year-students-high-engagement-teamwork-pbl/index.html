q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports that the single first-year-focused study (Yadav et al., Nepal) used a descriptive self-report questionnaire; students reported "high engagement (97%) and improved teamwork (87%)". These are perception percentages, not effect sizes.

"The results of this cross-sectional study (with a descriptive self-report questionnaire) found that first-year students have a positive attitude toward PBL since they reported the following: high engagement (97%) and improved teamwork (87%)."
