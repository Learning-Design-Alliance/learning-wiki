# Procedure times for ultrasound-guided CVC placement steps were shorter with AR-US than S-US but did not reach statistical significance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mean times T1, T2 and TU were shorter with AR-US than with S-US, but the differences were not statistically significant (e.g., TU mean difference -17.03 s, 95% CI -38.92 to 4.88, p = 0.13). [→ Liao Shu-Chen 2024](#liao-shu-chen-2024)

## Evidence

### Liao Shu-Chen 2024

Liao Shu-Chen, Shao Shih-Chieh, Gao Shi-Ying, Lai Edward Chia-Cheng. (2024). Augmented reality visualization for ultrasound-guided interventions: a pilot randomized crossover trial to assess trainee performance and cognitive load. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05998-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Main crossover comparison of procedure step times on a CVC task trainer. The printed estimates show a T2 mean difference of -14.13 s (p = 0.14) and a TU mean difference of -17.03 s (p = 0.13), both non-significant, favouring AR-US descriptively.

> "T2 (seconds): 38.19 (25.54) vs. 52.32 (59.60), mean difference: -14.13 (95% CI: -33.03 to 4.78), p = 0.14; TU(seconds): 60.40 (32.82) vs. 77.43 (67.67), mean difference: -17.03 (95% CI: -38.92 to 4.88), p = 0.13 (Table 2)."

### Liao Shu-Chen 2024 (2)

Liao Shu-Chen, Shao Shih-Chieh, Gao Shi-Ying, Lai Edward Chia-Cheng. (2024). Augmented reality visualization for ultrasound-guided interventions: a pilot randomized crossover trial to assess trainee performance and cognitive load. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05998-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The trial's authors interpret the consistently shorter but non-significant AR-US times, which they say "may be attributed to the limited number of participants and the varying levels of experience with both ultrasound and AR".

> "We found the times T1, T2 and TU to be shorter for AR-US than for S-US, although without reaching statistical significance (Fig. 3), which may be attributed to the limited number of participants and the varying levels of experience with both ultrasound and AR."
