## Evidence

### Lutz G 2017

Lutz G, Pankoke N, Goldblatt H, Hofmann M, Zupanic M. (2017). Enhancing medical students’ reflectivity in mentoring groups for professional development – a qualitative analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0951-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative focus group interviews with 14 first-year mentees (5 focus groups) plus individual interviews with 8 mentors and 1 co-mentor, analyzed by thematic content analysis. The article reports that attitudes "were diverse" and that some groups split early while remaining members "wanted to do that and they could use it."

> "Students’ attitudes regarding the usefulness of reflecting real, study-related intra- and, interpersonal topics at medical school were diverse. For instance, a student described how the group split early on."
