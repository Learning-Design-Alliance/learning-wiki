`q2 i?` Reliability analysis with SPSS 18.0 showed the VKS used in the pilot reached α = .96, at the upper end of the accepted reliability range for language tests. [→ Unterrainer 2012](#unterrainer-2012)

## Evidence

### Unterrainer 2012

Unterrainer, Eva Maria. (2012). Mobile Learning in Foreign Language Learning: Podcasts and Lexicon Acquisition in the Elementary Instruction of Italian. In L. Bradley & S. Thouësny (Eds.), CALL: Using, Learning, Knowing, EUROCALL Conference, Gothenburg, Sweden, 22-25 August 2012, Proceedings. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED574913

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Reliability analysis of the 30-word VKS (scored 30–150 points) administered in the pilot study, computed with SPSS 18.0. The article reports the printed α value and cites McNamara's accepted range; no effect size is involved.

> "According to the reliability analysis with SPSS 18.0, the used VKS shows α = .96 and is therefore located at the upper end of the scale, since in language tests values ranging from .70 to .90 are accepted as reliable"
