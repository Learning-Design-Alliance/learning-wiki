## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Failure-rates for the items "check responsiveness", "correct compression point" and "no-flow-time" were significantly higher when rated by peers than by professionals (McNemar p = 0.004, 0.001 and < 0.001). [→ Beck Stefanie 2016](#beck-stefanie-2016)

## Evidence

### Beck Stefanie 2016

Beck Stefanie, Ruhnke Bjarne, Issleib Malte, Daubmann Anne, Harendza Sigrid, Zöllner Christian. (2016). Analyses of inter-rater reliability between professionals, medical students and trained school children as assessors of basic life support skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0788-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

McNemar's test comparing item failure-rates of the same 339 school children rated by peer- and professional-assessors, with Bonferroni-adjusted significance level of 0.6 %. The article reports "p-value of McNemar’s test for "check responsiveness" = 0.004".

> "Peers and professionals rated the items "check responsiveness", "correct compression point" and "no-flow-time" significantly different (p-value of McNemar’s test for "check responsiveness" = 0.004, "correct compression point" = 0.001 and "no-flow-time" < 0.001)."
