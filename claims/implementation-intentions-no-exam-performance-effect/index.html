David, L., Biwer, F., Crutzen, R., & de Bruin, A. (2026). Understanding and supporting university students' use of distributed practice via implementation intentions. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00448-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Linear model predicting final exam grade from group and prior performance in the ESM study: "the main effect of group was not statistically signiﬁcant" for intervention, while "prior performance was a signiﬁcant positive predictor of ﬁnal exam grade". II students achieved comparable outcomes with less time invested.

"the main effect of group was not statistically signiﬁcant (β = −2.51, SE = 2.25, p = 0.271, 95% CI [ −7.03; 2.01]), whereas prior performance was a signiﬁcant positive predictor of ﬁnal exam grade (β = 0.57, SE = 0.12,p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.32; 0.83])"
