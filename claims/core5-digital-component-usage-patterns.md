Descriptive analysis of Core5 program usage logs (date, login and logout time per session) for treatment students during 2016–2017. The article reports the per-week and per-grade minute figures printed above; these are descriptive statistics with no inferential test.

> "Students used the digital component for an average of 78.4 min per week. A look at mean number of minutes over the school year showed discrepancies across grades: use was strongest in second grade (2651.4) and third grade (2396.2), lower in kindergarten (2099.3) and first grade (2146.6), and weakest in fourth grade (1569.8) and fifth grade (1209.9)."
