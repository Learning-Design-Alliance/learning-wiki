## Evidence

### Zajic et al 2026

Zajic et al. (2026). Could Instructor Talk Drive CURE Effectiveness? A Comparative Study of Instructor Talk in Introductory Lab Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-09-0241

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison of audio recordings from 24 CURE and 24 non-CURE introductory biology lab instructors. The Mann-Whitney U test showed "a medium effect ... of course type on immediacy talk (U = 493, r = 0.283, CI [0.03, 0.53], p = 0.051)"; CURE instructors showed a median of 2.4 occurrences per hour versus 2.0 for non-CURE instructors.

> "A Mann-Whitney U test comparing CUREs (n = 24) and non-CUREs (n = 24) showed a medium effect (Hemphill, 2003; Gignac and Szodorai, 2016) of course type on immediacy talk (U = 493, r = 0.283, CI [0.03, 0.53], p = 0.051)."
