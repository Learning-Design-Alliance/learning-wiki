Evidence¶

Jafarpour 2014¶

Mohsen Jafarpour. (2014). The Effect of Incorporating the Extensive Reading Approach into Vocabulary Instruction on Learners' Lexical Depth in an EFL Context. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.3p.162

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Independent t test comparing Read's WAT scores of 20 treatment and 20 control students after 4 months; the article reports "t (38) = -3.234" at p < .05 with a mean difference of -14.100 at 95% confidence. No effect size is printed.

"It shows that the difference is statistically significant, t (38) = -3.234, at p < .05, 2-tailed. Therefore, the null hypothesis of no difference between group means was rejected."
