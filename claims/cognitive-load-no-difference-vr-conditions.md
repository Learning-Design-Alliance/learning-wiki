## Evidence

### Medenitzki R 2026

Medenitzki R, Dubovi I. (2026). Bodily Engagement in Virtual Reality Nursing Simulation: Implications for Students' Declarative and Procedural Learning. Nurse Educator. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000002271

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANCOVA found "no significant effect of time" and "no significant time × group interaction" for cognitive load, indicating free movement did not add mental effort.

> "Repeated-measures ANCOVA controlling for age showed no significant effect of time (Wilks' lambda = .940, F2,64 = 2.04, P = .138) and no significant time × group interaction (Wilks' lambda = .944, F2,64 = 1.91, P = .157)."
