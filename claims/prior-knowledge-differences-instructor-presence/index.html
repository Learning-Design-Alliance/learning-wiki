Evidence¶

Tim Kühl 2025¶

Tim Kühl, Felicia Teske, Martin Merkt, Christina Sondermann. (2025). Temporary instructor presence in lecture slides does not enhance online learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10435-0

q3 · i2 · causal · r3

Experiment 1 assessed prior knowledge in hindsight on a 6-point scale; the ANOVA showed "significant differences between conditions", F(2, 123) = 4.16, p = 0.018, η2p = 0.063, with the temporary condition rating prior knowledge significantly higher than the never-present condition (p = 0.015).

"for the item that asked about judging prior knowledge in the hindsight, a one-factorial ANOVA revealed significant differences between conditions, F(2, 123) = 4.16, p = 0.018, η2p = 0.063."

Tim Kühl 2025 (2)¶

Tim Kühl, Felicia Teske, Martin Merkt, Christina Sondermann. (2025). Temporary instructor presence in lecture slides does not enhance online learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10435-0

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

Experiment 2 measured prior knowledge before learning; a "significant main effect for self-rated prior knowledge", F(2, 216) = 3.30, p = 0.039, η2p = 0.030, emerged, with the never-present condition rating higher than the permanent condition (p = 0.041).

"However, there was a significant main effect for self-rated prior knowledge, F(2, 216) = 3.30, p = 0.039, η2p = 0.030."
