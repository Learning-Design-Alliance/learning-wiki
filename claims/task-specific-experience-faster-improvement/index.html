Evidence¶

Podolsky 2025¶

Podolsky, Anne, and Linda Darling-Hammond. (2025). Think Again: Do the Returns to Teacher Experience Fizzle Out? Washington, D.C.: Thomas B. Fordham Institute. https://fordhaminstitute.org/national/research/think-again-do-returns-teacher-experience-fizzle-out

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief reports, citing Cook and Mansfield, that task-specific experience accounts for a substantial share of experience returns; the fraction is quoted, no effect size printed.

"a study of North Carolina high school students found that “about a quarter to a third of the returns to years of experience are actually specific to the subject that the teacher taught.”"

Podolsky 2025 (2)¶

Podolsky, Anne, and Linda Darling-Hammond. (2025). Think Again: Do the Returns to Teacher Experience Fizzle Out? Washington, D.C.: Thomas B. Fordham Institute. https://fordhaminstitute.org/national/research/think-again-do-returns-teacher-experience-fizzle-out

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief reports, citing Blazar's California district study, that grade switching correlates with smaller experience returns and higher turnover; an observational association, no effect size printed.

"a study of a large urban school district in California found that elementary teachers were frequently required to switch grades, particularly in low-achieving schools with high proportions of students of color, and that this grade switching was associated with smaller returns to experience and higher rates of turnover among teachers."
