# A binding state SBP mandate increases 4th-grade math achievement by 2.2 points (7.7% of a standard deviation) and reading by 2.0 points (5.4% of a standard deviation) in NAEP difference-in-differences estimates

## Evidence

### Frisvold David E 2015

Frisvold David E. (2015). Nutrition and Cognitive Achievement: An Evaluation of the School Breakfast Program. Journal of Public Economics. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpubeco.2014.12.003

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-differences analysis of the 2003 4th-grade NAEP math and reading samples (53,430 math; 51,640 reading), comparing schools above and below state FRP thresholds across states with differing thresholds. The study reports "a binding state mandate increases math achievement by 2.2 points" and reading by 2.0 points; no standardised effect size (d, g, r) is printed, so no magnitude beyond the printed points and percent-of-SD figures is claimed.

> "a binding state mandate increases math achievement by 2.2 points (with a standard error of 0.902), which is 7.7 percent of a standard deviation and 0.9 percent of the mean, and reading achievement by 2.0 points (with a standard error of 1.124), which is 5.4 percent of a standard deviation"
