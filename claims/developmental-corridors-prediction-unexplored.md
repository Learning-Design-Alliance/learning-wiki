Richters C, Schmidmaier R, Popov V, Schredelseker J, Fischer F, Fischer MR. (2024). Intervention skills – a neglected field of research in medical education and beyond. GMS Journal for Medical Education 41(4):Doc48. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001703.shtml

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Authors' interpretive statement in the section on implementing interventions: the "developmental corridor" describes the typical range of variation in a patient's health status influenced by social, individual, genetic, and interventional factors. The authors report no empirical study of this prediction process.

> "To the best of our knowledge, the cognitive process of predicting intervention-based developmental corridors is largely unexplored. As a result, there is a lack of knowledge regarding the optimal teaching and support strategies for such prediction processes."
