Feasibility was assessed by GP-reported outcomes in the prospective cohort study: "FLUS was completed in all patients, except two", with no unexpected events reported and median time consumption of 15 min (IQR 10-15), varying from 3 to 30 min.

> "FLUS was completed in all patients, except two, where FLUS was only partly performed due to logistical reasons. Time consumption for performing FLUS (including labelling and recording of videos) was a median of 15 min (IQR 10-15) but varied from 3 to 30 min."
