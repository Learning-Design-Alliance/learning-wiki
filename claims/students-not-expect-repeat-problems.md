## Evidence

### Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2008

Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2008). Reflection and Self-Monitoring in Quantum Mechanics. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1063/1.3266713](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266713)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Think-aloud interviews lasting 1-1.5 hours with four paid student volunteers from the 14-student group, conducted the following semester, plus two shorter interviews. The article reports students "did not expect a problem they had encountered on a previous exam to occur on the final exam" and quoted student comments about studying homework solutions more carefully.

> "In interviews, students frequently noted that they did not expect a problem they had encountered on a previous exam to occur on the final exam. This expectation often resulted in a more careful review of homework assignments than previous exams."
