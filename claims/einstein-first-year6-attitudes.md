### Kaur 2017 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Responses to the "too young" question from Year 6 students in the Pitts et al. (2014) data. The article reports "the majority of the class (14) thought that they were not too young to understand Einsteinian physics."

> "Five members of the class thought that they were too young to understand Einsteinian physics; the majority of the class (14) thought that they were not too young to understand Einsteinian physics and others were undecided."

### Kaur 2017

Kaur, T., Blair, D., Moschilla, J., Stannard, W. & Zadnik, M. (2017). Teaching Einsteinian Physics at Schools: Part 3, Review of Research Outcomes. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aa83dd](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aa83dd)

`q2 · i?`

Attitudinal results for a Year 6 class (n=26 per figure captions) following a six-lesson program, taken from data given in Pitts et al. (2014). The article reports "70% of students responded that the ideas of space, time and gravity were very interesting"; no effect size is printed.

> "Figure 1(a) shows that 70% of students responded that the ideas of space, time and gravity were very interesting. Only one student identified the program as boring."
