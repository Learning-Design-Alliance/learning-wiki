## Evidence

### Qu Bo 2012

Qu Bo, Zhao Yu-hong, Sun Bao-zhi. (2012). Assessment of Resident Physicians in Professionalism, Interpersonal and Communication Skills: a Multisource Feedback. International Journal of Medical Sciences. https://doi.org/10.7150/ijms.3353

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis of the six evaluator questionnaires from the 258-resident multisource feedback study. The five non-patient surveys each yielded "communication skills and professionalism" factors; the patient questionnaire yielded four factors accounting for 72.67% of total variance.

> "The factor analysis identified 2 factors on the attending doctor, resident self, resident peer, nurse, and office staff surveys respectively: communication skills and professionalism, which accounted for 70.87% (Table 2), 71.01% (Table 3), 70.67% (Table 4), 75.54% (Table 5) and 74.62% (Table 6) of the variance respectively."
