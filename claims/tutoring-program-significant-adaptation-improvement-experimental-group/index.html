Zeleeva et al. 2016¶

Vera P. Zeleeva, Svetlana S. Bykova, Silvia Varbanova. (2016). Psychological and Pedagogical Support for Students' Adaptation to Learning Activity in High Science School. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(3), 151-161. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1092226

Pearson χ² comparison of adaptation-level distributions before and after the experimental work (2013/2014) in a pedagogical experiment at Vyatka State University of Humanities. The experimental group's χ² = 6.173 exceeded the critical score 5.991, so "variance in distribution are statistically valid (hypothesis Н1)"; the control group's χ² = 0.028 was not.
