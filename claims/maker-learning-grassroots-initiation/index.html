Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year One 2017¶

Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year One. (2017). Digital Promise and Maker Education Initiative. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/dfc366e2-83b1-4b7b-8899-c7519dc364d8

q2 · i? · associational · r1

October 2016 Maker Promise Champions Survey completed by 201 maker champions, the educators most responsible for implementing maker learning. The demographic analysis reports that "Teachers, librarians, and coaches account for 57 percent of maker champions, while administrators account for only 35 percent."

"Making in schools is typically initiated and developed at the grassroots level. Teachers, librarians, and coaches account for 57 percent of maker champions, while administrators account for only 35 percent ."
