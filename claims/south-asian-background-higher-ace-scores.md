Cross-sectional survey of 80 trainees; Table 1 reports mean ACE scores by region of medical education (South Asia 9.9 vs North Africa 8.2). The study reports a "difference of 1.7 points out of 15 questions".

> "Those with a medical educational background from South Asia scored the higher on the ACE tool than those graduating from other regions. The biggest difference was between South Asian graduates and those graduating from North Africa, with a difference of 1.7 points out of 15 questions (see Table 1)."
