## Evidence

### Hao 2025

Hao, Z., Jiang, J., Yu, J., Liu, Z., & Zhang, Y. (2025). Student-AI Interaction in an LLM-Empowered Learning Environment: A Cluster Analysis of Engagement Profiles. arXiv:2503.01694v3. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis tests comparing the four engagement clusters derived from hierarchical clustering (NbClust, Ward's method) of system-profile variables for 305 students. "significant group differences" appeared on all four dimensions. Cluster sizes were 97, 60, 75 and 73.

> "The results suggested there were significant group differences on the total prompt number that students sent ( χ2 = 243.80, p < .001), average message length (χ2 = 213.18, p < .001), completed module number (χ2 = 233.50, p <.001), and total time spent (χ2 = 240.61, p < .001)."
