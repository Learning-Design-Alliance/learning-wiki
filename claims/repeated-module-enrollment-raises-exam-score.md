Longitudinal analysis of students who completed the course multiple times across successive years, modeled with "linear mixed model with random intercepts". The printed coefficient is 0.88 points per enrollment; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "A total of 1,231 assessments for 571 students were incorporated into the linear mixed model. With each course enrollment, the final exam score increased on average by 0.88 points (95% CI: 0.76–0.99), p < 0.001."
