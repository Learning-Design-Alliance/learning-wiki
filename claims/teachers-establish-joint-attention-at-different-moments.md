Jarodzka, H., Skuballa, I., & Gruber, H. (2020). Eye-Tracking in Educational Practice: Investigating Visual Perception Underlying Teaching and Learning in the Classroom. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09565-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Pouta et al.'s mobile eye-tracking study of experienced and student teachers during frontal teaching and pupil-teacher interaction episodes, capturing what teachers looked at and how they interpreted it via retrospective verbal protocols. The review attributes the finding to the authors.

> "the authors showed that apart from other differences in their professional vision, both groups used different moments during their instruction of fractions in mathematics to establish joint attention with their pupils."
