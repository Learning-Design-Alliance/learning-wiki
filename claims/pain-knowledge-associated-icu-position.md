ANOVA and independent t-test analyses of total KASRP scores against demographic variables in the 112-nurse cross-sectional survey. The article reports "no association between nurses’ knowledge and attitude with demographic variables, except for the current position in the ICU (p=0.043)." No effect size is printed.
