Tim Coughlan. (2020). The use of open data as a material for learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09706-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Finding from the thematic analysis of ten educator interviews in the Teaching Approach category. The article reports that "Eight of ten interviewees employed forms of project or inquiry-based learning" to harness relevance, broad scope and authenticity, and one educator noted the scaffolding was the same as for collected data.
