Evidence¶

Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan 2015¶

Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan. (2015). Content Delivery Using Augmented Reality to Enhance Students' Performance in a Building Design and Assembly Project. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076141

q2 · i? · causal · r1

An academic survey of 241 junior-level civil, environmental, and construction engineering students at UCF in 2012-13 (15% female, 85% male): 92% of respondents "identified themselves as visual learners", agreeing that 2D/3D visualization or multimedia helps them learn abstract topics. 54% agreed they learn better in collaborative settings.

"Results indicated that 92% of respondents identified themselves as visual learners. In particular, this group agreed to the statement that "they learn better when the instructor uses 2D/3D visualization or multimedia to teach abstract engineering and scientific topics""

Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan 2015 (2)¶

Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan. (2015). Content Delivery Using Augmented Reality to Enhance Students' Performance in a Building Design and Assembly Project. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076141

q2 · i? · causal · r1

The same survey asked respondents whether they owned a technology-enabled device usable in the classroom: 93% declared they own "either a smartphone or a tablet device or both". This device ownership motivated the choice of a mobile AR platform.

"93% declared that they own either a smartphone or a tablet device or both (Figure 2), and can easily use it in their daily activities."
