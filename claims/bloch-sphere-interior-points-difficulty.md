# The most persistent difficulty on questions 5a-c was students indicating states in the interior of the Bloch sphere cross-section rather than on the edge of the circle, which persisted on the post-test at lower levels.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students tended to indicate mixed states (interior points) instead of pure states on the edge of the circle, a difficulty reduced but not eliminated by the tutorial. [→ Peter Hu 2023](#peter-hu-2023)

## Evidence

### Peter Hu 2023

Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, Roger S. K. Mong, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Student Understanding of the Bloch Sphere. https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad2393

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre/post study (N = 28) on questions 5a-c, which asked students to indicate states more likely to yield one measurement outcome than another on a cross-section of the Bloch sphere; interior points represent mixed states, which were beyond the scope of the tutorial and course, so closer responses received partial credit.

> "On the pre-test and the post-test, the most common difficulty for questions 5a-c was for students to indicate states on the interior of the cross-section of the Bloch sphere, rather than the edge of the circle."
