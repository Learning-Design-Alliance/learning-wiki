## Evidence

### Noguchi 2024

Noguchi, Y. (2024). Harmonic memory signals in the human cerebral cortex induced by semantic relatedness of words. npj Science of Learning, 9:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00221-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 false-alarm comparison between lure and unseen-new probes in the SemL condition (n = 34). Lure FA rates were "signiﬁcantly higher (t(33) = 4.04,p = 0.0003,d = 0.70)" than to new words (4.56 ± 0.93% vs 1.18 ± 0.70%).

> "In SemL, the FA rates to lures (4.56 ± 0.93%) were signi ﬁcantly higher (t(33) = 4.04,p = 0.0003,d = 0.70) than those to new words (1.18 ± 0.70%), showing a false memory arising from a semantic integration."

### Noguchi 2024 (2)

Noguchi, Y. (2024). Harmonic memory signals in the human cerebral cortex induced by semantic relatedness of words. npj Science of Learning, 9:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00221-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 memory task (final dataset of 27 participants, no attentional cue) replicated the false-memory effect: lure FA rate "5.56 ± 1.72%" significantly exceeded new-probe FA rate (1.85 ± 0.66%), t(26) = 2.51, p = 0.019, d = 0.55.

> "The FA rate to lure probes (5.56 ± 1.72%) was signiﬁcantly higher ( t(26) = 2.51, p = 0.019, d =0 . 5 5 ) than that to new probes (1.85 ± 0.66%)."
