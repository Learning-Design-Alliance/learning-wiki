Delphi round 3 case formulation ratings. Holistic mental state examination reached 90.7% agreement, followed by personalised support (88.4%) and knowledge of youth mental health conditions (86%). Access to means for self-harm or suicide (69.8%) and carer awareness (69.8%) fell just below threshold.
