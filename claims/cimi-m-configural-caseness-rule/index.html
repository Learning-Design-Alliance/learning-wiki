de Goede, M. L., Boelen, P. A., Litz, B. T., & ter Heide, F. J. J. (2026). Mapping moral injury: Development and feasibility of the Clinical Interview for Moral Injury—military version (CIMI-M). PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0359553

q2 · i? · design · r3

Definitional statement from the instrument-aim section describing the configural decision rule included in the interview manual. The article presents it as a formalization of treatment-warrant judgment, not a diagnosis, and reports no validity or reliability data for the rule.

"We experimentally defined clinical caseness as: (1) endorsement of at least one high-stakes and currently distressing PMIE; (2) reports of functionally interfering problems in at least two domains of moral injury; (3) that are new onset; and (4) for which recovery has been impeded or is unlikely without clinical intervention."
