Evidence¶

Gibson 2025¶

Gibson, D., Azukas, M. E., & Knezek, G. (2025). Practitioner Beliefs and Behaviors in AI-Enhanced Education: DOT Framework Survey Evidence.

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive item statistics from the cross-sectional survey of 72 higher education practitioners. The Oversight and Governance factor items showed ceiling effects (means over 4.60), which the authors note limits discriminating power.

"Ninety-seven percent of respondents agreed or strongly agreed that AI-generated materials need to be critically evaluated by instructors (M = 4.79, SD = 0.60)"

Gibson 2025 (2)¶

Gibson, D., Azukas, M. E., & Knezek, G. (2025). Practitioner Beliefs and Behaviors in AI-Enhanced Education: DOT Framework Survey Evidence.

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Multi-select prompting-technique item from the same survey; respondents selected all techniques they employ from a list, and only 2 of 72 accepted AI responses unverified.

"Notably, only two respondents (2.8%) reported accepting AI responses without further verification, indicating that critical evaluation of AI-generated outputs is a near-universal norm within this cohort."
