## Evidence

### Wijnen-Meijer M 2015

Wijnen-Meijer M, ten Cate O, van der Schaaf M, Burgers C, Borleffs J, Harendza S. (2015). Vertically integrated medical education and the readiness for practice of graduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0514-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis in the UHTRUST study between number of completed PPEFs and FOC/EPA scores; the article prints no correlation coefficients, so no effect size is reported. The authors concluded PPEF responders in the German group form a positively biased subgroup.

> "Significant correlations for five FOCs and two EPAs were identified, which led us to believe that the number of completed PPEFs is an indicator of proficiency."

### Wijnen-Meijer M 2015 (2)

Wijnen-Meijer M, ten Cate O, van der Schaaf M, Burgers C, Borleffs J, Harendza S. (2015). Vertically integrated medical education and the readiness for practice of graduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0514-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Completion rates as printed in the PPEF results section of the UHTRUST assessment; because of the correlation findings the authors decided not to compare PPEF scores between the two groups.

> "Within the Dutch group 95 % of the PPEFs were completed, the German group only completed 67 %."
