Fronius 2019¶

Fronius, Darling-Hammond, Persson, Guckenburg, Hurley, & Petrosino. (2019). Restorative Justice in U.S. Schools: An Updated Research Review. WestEd Justice & Prevention Research Center. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED595733

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Vincent et al.'s (2016) study of student opinion shifts after full-staff SWPRD trainings at "a large, majority-minority school." Before implementation Black and LGBTQ students reported more bullying; after implementation "Black and White students had similar perceptions." No statistics are printed.

"Prior to SWPRD implementation, Black students were more likely than their White peers to indicate that there was bullying in the school, and LGBTQ students were more likely than straight students to indicate that there was bullying or harassment. After implementation, Black and White students had similar perceptions, as did LGBTQ and straight students"
