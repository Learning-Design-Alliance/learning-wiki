## Evidence

### Kudish Philip 2016

Kudish Philip, Shores Robin, McClung Alex, Smulyan Lisa, Vallen Elizabeth A., Siwicki Kathleen K. (2016). Active Learning Outside the Classroom: Implementation and Outcomes of Peer-Led Team-Learning Workshops in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-01-0051

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

End-of-semester survey analysis comparing URM and non-URM and AP and non-AP responses on seven benefit items; URM and non-URM responded similarly. The study reports "significantly more non-AP students than AP students" rated four benefits positively.

> "significantly more non-AP students than AP students said that the SGMs either somewhat helped (3) or helped a lot (4) to enhance their interest in biology, their understanding of biology, their enjoyment of the course, and their comfort in taking biology courses (chi-square tests, p < 0.05)."

### Kudish Philip 2016 (2)

Kudish Philip, Shores Robin, McClung Alex, Smulyan Lisa, Vallen Elizabeth A., Siwicki Kathleen K. (2016). Active Learning Outside the Classroom: Implementation and Outcomes of Peer-Led Team-Learning Workshops in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-01-0051

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman rank-order correlations between SGM attendance and ordinal benefit ratings, pooled across both courses. The study reports correlations of r = 0.22 to 0.40 (p < 0.001), a small-to-moderate association, for "all seven putative benefits in both courses."

> "student attendance was positively correlated with their ratings for all seven putative benefits in both courses (Spearman rho tests; r ranged from 0.22 to 0.40, p < 0.001)."
