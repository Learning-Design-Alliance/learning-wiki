# No CCTST sub-scale (inductive, deductive, analysis and interpretation, inference, evaluation and explanation) shows a significant pre-to-post change for peer leaders, and no sub-scale differs significantly between groups

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Paired t-tests on the five CCTST sub-scales within the PLTL group revealed no significant pre-to-post differences for any sub-scale. [→ Snyder Julia J. 2015](#snyder-julia-j-2015)
`q2 i?` Friedman's chi-square tests revealed no significant differences across PLTL and control groups on any of the five sub-scales. [→ Snyder Julia J. 2015 (2)](#snyder-julia-j-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Snyder Julia J. 2015

Snyder Julia J., Wiles Jason R. (2015). Peer Led Team Learning in Introductory Biology: Effects on Peer Leader Critical Thinking Skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115084

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group analysis of the 37 PLTL leaders' CCTST sub-scale scores. "no significant differences were revealed for any of the five critical thinking sub-scales"; the printed t values ranged from t(36) = −1.061 to .780, with p values .296 to .724, and no effect sizes were reported.

> "Paired t-tests were conducted to compare the pre-test and post-test scores of the five critical thinking sub-scales, and no significant differences were revealed for any of the five critical thinking sub-scales"

### Snyder Julia J. 2015 (2)

Snyder Julia J., Wiles Jason R. (2015). Peer Led Team Learning in Introductory Biology: Effects on Peer Leader Critical Thinking Skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115084

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Nonparametric between-group tests (used because sub-scale distributions were non-normal) comparing PLTL and control groups on all five CCTST sub-scales; all Friedman chi-square tests were non-significant (p = 0.105 to 0.879).

> "Friedman’s chi-square test revealed no significant differences across groups for deductive reasoning, x2 (1) = 0.556, p = 0.456; inductive reasoning, x2(1) = 0.023, p = 0.879; analysis and interpretation, x2 (1) = 2.632, p = 0.105; inference, x2 (1) = 0.220, p = 0.639; and evaluation and explanation, x2 (1) = 0.818, p = 0.366 (Tables 5 and 6)."
