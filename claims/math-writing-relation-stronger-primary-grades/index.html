Evidence¶

Young-Suk Grace Kim 2024¶

Young-Suk Grace Kim, Dandan Yang, Jinkyung Hwang. (2024). Are Mathematics and Writing Skills Related? Evidence from Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09960-4

q4 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analytic moderation analysis by grade level (pre-K–2, 3–5, 6–12, college and beyond) across the 211 included studies. The adult correlation of r = 0.30 was significantly weaker than primary grades (r = 0.52) and upper elementary grades (r = 0.42).

"the correlation was 0.30 (SE = 0.05, p < 0.001) for adult learners in college and beyond, which was statistically significantly weaker than the correlation for children in primary grades (r = 0.52, SE = 0.03, p < 0.001)"
