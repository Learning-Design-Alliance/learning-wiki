Cross-project qualitative finding summarized in Table 5, which pairs each barrier with a corresponding support, e.g. time constraints with recurring structures such as regular data debriefs, and communication gaps with actionable data shared through accessible formats.

"Time constraints, limited capacity, funder expectations, and communication gaps emerged as the most consistent barriers, while recurring structures, relational trust, actionable data, and networked channels functioned as the supports that made participation more feasible when present."
