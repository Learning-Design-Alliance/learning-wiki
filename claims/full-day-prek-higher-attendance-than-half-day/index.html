Evidence¶

Ehrlich 2021¶

Ehrlich, S.B., Allensworth, E.M., & Tansey, J. (2021). Meeting families' needs: Attendance rates in full-day vs. half-day pre-k. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/meeting-families-needs

q2 · i? · causal · r2

District-wide observational analysis of CPS administrative attendance data, 2012-13 through 2015-16, roughly 25,000 pre-k students per year. The article reports "significantly higher attendance rates" for full-day students, with differences significant in models controlling for gender, special education, EL status, and neighborhood measures.

"Across all years of the study, students in CPS full-day programs had significantly higher attendance rates than students in half-day programs"
