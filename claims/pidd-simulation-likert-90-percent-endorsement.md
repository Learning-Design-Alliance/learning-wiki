## Evidence

### Cavuoto Petrizzo Marie 2019

Cavuoto Petrizzo Marie, Barilla-LaBarca Maria-Louise, Lim Youn Seon, Jongco Artemio M., Cassara Michael, Anglim James, Stern Joel N.H. (2019). Utilization of high-fidelity simulation to address challenges with the basic science immunology education of preclinical medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1786-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous four-item 5-point Likert evaluation administered one week after the required simulation, completed by 96% of the class (N = 98). Positive responses "ranged from 90% (n = 88) to 92% (n = 90)" across items on integration, PIDD knowledge, clinical reasoning, and debrief-enhanced learning. Descriptive percentages only; no inferential test.

> "The percentage of students responding positively to the individual queried items ranged from 90% (n = 88) to 92% (n = 90)"
