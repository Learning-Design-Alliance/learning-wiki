## Evidence

### Lock Nicole E. 2024

Lock Nicole E., DeBoer Mark D., Scharf Rebecca J., Miller Sarah E. (2024). Academic performance in moderately and late preterm children in the United States: are they catching up? Journal of Perinatology 44(6). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-024-01938-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariable logistic regression of teacher performance ratings in the weighted ECLS-K:2011 cohort compared 32-34 6/7 weeks children with term children, printing adjusted odds ratios of "1.40", "1.49", and "1.89" for below-grade performance in kindergarten.

> "32–34 6/7 weeks’ children were more likely to perform significantly below grade average in reading, mathematics, and science in kindergarten with adjusted odds ratios of 1.40 (95th percent confidence interval CI 1.03, 1.89), 1.49 (CI 1.02, 2.17), and 1.89 (CI 1.20, 2.99), respectively"
