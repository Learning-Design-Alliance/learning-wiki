Fares Fawzi, Seyed Parsa Neshaei, Marta Knezevic, Tanya Nazaretsky, and Tanja Käser. (2026). REFINE: Real-world Exploration of Interactive Feedback and Student Behaviour. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Classroom study analysis relating question categories to task scores graded by a teaching-team member on the Basis, IH, and Step rubric. The article reports descriptive tendencies only; a student-level permutation test with overlapping category membership found "no significant differences in practice scores by question category".

> "Students who askedTaskquestions tended to have lower scores, consistent with difficulty interpreting problem requirements. In contrast,Solutionquestions were associated with higher scores, reflecting engagement focused on verification rather than initial understanding."
