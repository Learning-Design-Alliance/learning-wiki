q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multinomial logistic regression on 2021/22 school characteristics and survey-derived implementation levels for 271 schools found a significant foster-care association; "The only characteristic that showed consistent differences across implementation levels was the percentage of students in a school who were in foster care."

"Regression models that controlled for relevant school characteristics, such as student enrollment and per student spending, revealed a statistically significant association between having a higher percentage of students in a school in foster care and the likelihood of being in a higher implementation group"
