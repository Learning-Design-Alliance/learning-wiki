### Barnawi Ahlam 2024

Barnawi Ahlam, Sonbol Ahmed M., Al-Shawwa Lana, Abulaban Alwalla, Asiri Khalil, Bagasi Abdulaziz, Alafari Reem, Alamoudi Aliaa Amr. (2024). Employing students' evaluations and tutors' perceptions to evaluate a faculty development program on problem-based learning at the Faculty of Medicine, King Abdulaziz University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05662-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of category and item means from the 284-student questionnaire. The article reports "the intra-personal behavior category had the lowest mean score" at 2.98 ± 1.0, with the strengths-and-weaknesses item lowest at 2.78 ± 1.2.

> "the intra-personal behavior category had the lowest mean score (mean ± SD = 2.98 ± 1.0, scale = 1–5). At the item level, the "searching for various resources on one's own" item had the highest mean score (mean ± SD = 3.58 ± 1.1, scale = 1–5), while the "providing a clear picture of their strengths and weaknesses as a tutor" item had lowest mean score (mean ± SD = 2.78 ± 1.2, scale = 1–5)."
