`q1 i?` Prior studies cited in the article found vocabulary pre-teaching to be less effective than other forms of listening support such as question preview or topical knowledge. [→ Mihara 2015](#mihara-2015)

## Evidence

### Mihara 2015

Mihara, K. (2015). Effects of Phonological Input as a Pre-Listening Activity on Vocabulary Learning and L2 Listening Comprehension Test Performance. TESL-EJ 19(2). https://tesl-ej.org/pdf/ej74/a1.pdf

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The article reports, citing Berne (1995) and Chang and Read (2006, 2008), that vocabulary pre-teaching was less effective than other forms of listening support. Berne found question preview produced significantly higher scores than control while vocabulary preview did not.

> "Berne (1995) and Chang and Read (2006, 2008) were in agreement that vocabulary pre-teaching was less effective than other forms of listening support."
