# Inhibitory control is moderately positively correlated with mathematical ability in elementary school children (r = 0.19)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Across 241 effect sizes from 86 independent samples of primary school children, inhibitory control shows a moderate positive correlation with mathematical ability. [→ Zhu 2025](#zhu-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhu 2025

Zhu, X., Tang, Y., Lu, J., Song, M., Yang, C., & Zhao, X. (2025). Inhibitory Control and Mathematical Ability in Elementary School Children: A Preregistered Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 37(1), 1. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09976-w

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Preregistered three-level random-effects meta-analysis of 241 effect sizes from 86 samples (14,223 children, mean age 8.67 years). The pooled correlation was r = 0.19, 95% CI [0.14, 0.23], p < 0.001, described as "a moderate positive correlation". Effect sizes were heterogeneous within and between studies.

> "The analysis yielded a moderate positive correlation between inhibitory control and mathematical ability (Lipsey & Wilson, 2001), r = 0.19, 95% CI [0.14, 0.23], p < 0.001 (see Table 1), indicating that better inhibitory control is correlated with superior mathematical ability."
