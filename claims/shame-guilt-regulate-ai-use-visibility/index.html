q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interview study of 19 computing students: in the results on hiding behaviors and selective disclosure, the article reports that "a subset of participants (8 of 19) described selective disclosure that was limited to specific social contexts," with concealment extending beyond trusted contexts.

"When asked about when they felt comfortable sharing their use of AI, a subset of participants (8 of 19) described selective disclosure that was limited to specific social contexts."
