## Evidence

### Stirparo Giuseppe 2024

Stirparo Giuseppe, Bellini Lorenzo, Solla Daniele, Stirparo Pierfrancesco, Fagoni Nazzareno, Gambolò Luca. (2024). Should basic life support–defibrillator training be compulsory for newly licensed Italian physicians? An observational study. Journal of Cardiovascular Medicine. https://doi.org/10.2459/JCM.0000000000001645

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison in the same observational study of 120 newly licensed physicians, assessed after an 8-hour AHA-standardized BLSD course with mannequin CPR and smartphone-app feedback visible only to the instructor. The McNemar test showed "a statistically significant difference in the percentage of performance" with no printed effect size.

> "A standard McNemar test determined a statistically significant difference in the percentage of performance in young physicians pre and post-BLSD course intervention (92.5 vs. 15.8%, P < 0.05)."
