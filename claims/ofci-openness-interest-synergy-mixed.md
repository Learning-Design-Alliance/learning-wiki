The review reports, citing the named studies, the state of evidence for the Openness-Fluid-Crystallized-Intelligence (OFCI) model's assumed synergy between openness/intellectual curiosity and domain-specific interest. The review attributes supporting, compensating, and zero findings to the cited works; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Prior research has supported the assumed synergistic effects, both on the state and trait level (Ziegler et al., 2012, 2018); yet, studies also found compensating effect (Zhang & Ziegler, 2022), and zero effects (Schumacher et al., 2025)."
