## Evidence

### de Sena David P. 2019

de Sena David P., Fabrício Daniela D., da Silva Vinícius D., Bodanese Luiz Carlos, Franco Alexandre R. (2019). Comparative evaluation of video-based on-line course versus serious game for training medical students in cardiopulmonary resuscitation: A randomised trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0214722

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Time spent in self-directed learning was compared between groups with Student's t test in the randomised trial. The game group remained interested "18.57±0.66 min for the game group vs 7.41±0.43 for the video group; p < 0.001".

> "students showed a preference for using games, as suggested by the longer time they remained interested in the method (18.57±0.66 min for the game group vs 7.41±0.43 for the video group; p < 0.001)"
