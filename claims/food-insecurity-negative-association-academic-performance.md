`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of 47 studies using a vote-counting synthesis after JBI quality appraisal; the review reports that "Forty-three (91.5%)" of included studies found an inverse association, 38 of them with GPA, and no study reported a positive association.

> "Forty-three (91.5%), comprising 42 cross-sectional and one longitudinal study (N = 176,947), reported an inverse association between food insecurity and academic performance/outcomes. Specifically, 38 studies reported association with GPA"
