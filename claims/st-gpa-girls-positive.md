## Evidence

### Byman 2026

Byman, A., Ulmanen, S., Pyhältö, K., Syväoja, H., Kulmala, J., & Tammelin, T. H. (2026). Gendered patterns in the associations between in-school physical activity, classroom peer ecology, and academic achievement among primary school students. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0351971

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multigroup SEM with accelerometer-measured in-school ST among 517 students; the girls' ST-to-GPA path was significant (p = .020) and the gender equality constraint significantly worsened fit. The article reports "in-school ST was positively associated with GPA among girls (0.15)".

> "Standardized regression estimates showed that in-school ST was positively associated with GPA among girls (0.15), whereas the association was negative and non-significant among boys (−0.07). A model comparison showed that constraining the significant path to be equal across genders worsened model fit, Δχ² (1) = 6.31, p = .012."
