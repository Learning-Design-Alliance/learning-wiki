## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ivar Bråten & Helge I. Strømsø 2011

Ivar Bråten & Helge I. Strømsø. (2011). Measuring strategic processing when students read multiple texts. Metacognition Learning 6:111–130. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9075-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression with intratextual comprehension (IntraVT) as the dependent variable in the same 71-student subsample. Prior knowledge entered at Step 1 was significant (F(1, 69)=13.32, p=.001, R²=.16); the strategy step was not, and "only prior knowledge uniquely predicted scores on the IntraVT, β=.36, p=.002".

> "The addition of accumulation and cross-text elaboration scores in Step 2 did not result in a statistically significant increment in the variance accounted for, Fchange(2, 67)=.97, p=.385, R²=.18."
