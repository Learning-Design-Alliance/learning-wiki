## Evidence

### Arcand KK 2024

Arcand KK, Schonhut-Stasik JS, Kane SG, Sturdevant G, Russo M, Watzke M, Hsu B and Smith LF (2024). A Universe of Sound: processing NASA data into sonifications to explore participant response. Front. Commun. 9:1288896. https://doi.org/10.3389/fcomm.2024.1288896

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey enjoyment ratings (Fig. 3) for the Galactic Center, Cassiopeia A, and Deep Field, compared between BLV and sighted groups via K-S tests. The majority selected "4 (Agree) and 5 (Agree Strongly)"; the Cassiopeia A p-value of 0.620 did not suggest a significant difference, and no effect size is printed.

> "Generally, all participants reported enjoying the soniﬁcations, with the majority selecting 4 (Agree) and 5 (Agree Strongly)."
