## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Paul Justice 2019

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Student understanding of Fermi energy, the Fermi-Dirac distribution and total electronic energy of a free electron gas. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Written question 2b responses and interviews. Students gave responses such as "number of particles in each single-particle state with energy 𝜖"; some derived the density of states from the Fermi energy expression via dN/dE, which coincidentally gives the correct expression but can mislead students into thinking density of states is the number of particles per unit energy.

> "When asked to explain what the density of states means, a common difficulty was relating it to particle number density or probability of occupying a particular single -particle state (confusion between the density of states and the distribution function)"
