## Evidence

### Perruchet 1998

Perruchet, P., & Vinter, A. (1998). PARSER: A Model for Word Segmentation. Journal of Memory and Language, 39, 246-263. https://doi.org/10.1006/jmla.1998.2576

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical simulation result (Study 1, 100 runs) modeling the Saffran et al. (1996b) experiment with six trisyllabic words and 4536 syllables. The article reports "a mean of 2064 syllables" for the loose criterion and a mean of 3837 for the strict criterion, compared with the 4536 syllables participants actually heard.

> "The loose criterion was reached after processing a mean of 2064 syllables, with a range of 735 to 4479 syllables."
