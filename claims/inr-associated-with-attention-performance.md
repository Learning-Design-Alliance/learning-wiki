## Evidence

### Rosen Maya L. 2021

Rosen Maya L., Lurie Lucy A., Sambrook Kelly A., Meltzoff Andrew N., McLaughlin Katie A. (2021). Neural mechanisms underlying the income-achievement gap: The role of the ventral visual stream. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.101025

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regressions in the 62-child sample, controlling for age and sex, tested log income-to-needs against sensitivity (d′) on both scanner attention tasks. The article reports INR "was positively associated with d′ on both the cued (β = 0.411, p < .001) memory-guided attention (β = 0.423, p = .002) tasks".

> "INR was positively associated with performance on both attention tasks. Specifically, INR was positively associated with d′ on both the cued (β = 0.411, p < .001) memory-guided attention (β = 0.423, p = .002) tasks."
