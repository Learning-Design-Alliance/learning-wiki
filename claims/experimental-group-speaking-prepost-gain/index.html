Evidence¶

Touran Ahour & Saeideh Entezari Maleki 2014¶

Touran Ahour & Saeideh Entezari Maleki. (2014). The Effect of Metadiscourse Instruction on Iranian EFL Learners' Speaking Ability. English Language Teaching, 7(10). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p69

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Paired samples t-test on the experimental group's (n = 17) PET speaking pretest and posttest scores, reported in Results section 3.4.3. Table 8 prints a paired-differences mean of -4.059 and Sig. (2-tailed) of .000; no effect size is printed.

"As indicated in Tabl e 8, the sig. (2-tailed) was lower than 0.05 level of significance. So, a significant difference was proved between the pretest and posttest in the experimental group."
