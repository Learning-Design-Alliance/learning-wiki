## Evidence

### Mavragani Amaryllis 2025

Mavragani Amaryllis, Coco Laura, Mungoli Neelesh, Gerdes John, Schooley Benjamin, Sharp Dakota, Miller Juliana. (2025). The Design and Evaluation of a Simulation Tool for Audiology Screening Education: Design Science Approach. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/47150

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized pilot in an Introduction to Audiology class: control (n=16) received in-person instruction only; test group (n=17) also used the HSS. Mann-Whitney U tests on pretest-posttest ordinal survey responses showed a significant difference for explaining screening to a child, with "P=.02, 2-tailed t test".

> ""I am confident in my ability to explain hearing screening procedures to a child" (Mann-Whitney U=71.0, n1=n2=16; P=.02, 2-tailed t test)"

### Mavragani Amaryllis 2025 (2)

Mavragani Amaryllis, Coco Laura, Mungoli Neelesh, Gerdes John, Schooley Benjamin, Sharp Dakota, Miller Juliana. (2025). The Design and Evaluation of a Simulation Tool for Audiology Screening Education: Design Science Approach. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/47150

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomized pilot survey analysis (Mann-Whitney U on ordinal Likert responses, 16 surveys per group). The item on determining if otoscopy is abnormal reached "P=.02, 2-tailed t test"; the normal-otoscopy item reached P=.02 as reported in the same Results passage.

> ""I am confident in my ability to determine if otoscopy is abnormal" (Mann-Whitney U=74.5, n1=n2=16 P=.02, 2-tailed t test)"
