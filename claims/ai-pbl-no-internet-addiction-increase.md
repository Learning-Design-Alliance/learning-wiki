## Evidence

### Ergezen Sahin Gizem 2025

Ergezen Sahin Gizem, Aras Bayram Gulay, Sanchez Sierra Alberto, Akdemir Simay, Kurc Dogukan, Tarakci Devrim, Tunali Ayse Nur. (2025). Effects of artificial intelligence based physiotherapy educational approach in developing clinical reasoning skills: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07926-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary-outcome analysis of the randomized trial using the 20-item Internet Addiction Test at two time points; the AI-PBLG's within-group change was non-significant (p = 0.323, d = 0.23), and the between-group effect was "negligible" and non-significant.

> "For IAT, no significant group differences were detected between the AI-PBLG and PBLG participants. The mean change in the AI-PBLG was ΔX̄=4.7 (SD = 20.3), while in the PBLG it was ΔX̄=6.5 (SD = 14.9), yielding a negligible between-group effect (Cohen’s d=–0.10, p = 0.771)."
