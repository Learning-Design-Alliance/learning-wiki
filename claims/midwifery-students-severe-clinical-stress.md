## Evidence

### Rezaei Behrooz 2020

Rezaei Behrooz, Falahati Juliana, Beheshtizadeh Raziyeh. (2020). Stress, stressors and related factors in clinical learning of midwifery students in Iran: a cross sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1970-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 70 undergraduate midwifery students at one Iranian university using the Persian PSS. The study reports "a severe stress level" with 55.7% high, 41.4% moderate and 2.9% low; no effect size is printed.

> "The mean score of PSS was (29.81 ± 7.68), indicating a severe stress level. In detail, 55.7% of the students reported their stress at a high level, 41.4% at a moderate level and 2.9% at a low level."
