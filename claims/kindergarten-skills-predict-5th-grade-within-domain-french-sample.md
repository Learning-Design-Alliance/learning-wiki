## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Kindergarten reading scores correlate medium-sized with 5th-grade reading scores (r(92)=.31). [→ Le Diagon Sarah 2025](#le-diagon-sarah-2025)
`q3 i3` Kindergarten math scores correlate large-sized with 5th-grade math scores (r(92)=.52). [→ Le Diagon Sarah 2025 (2)](#le-diagon-sarah-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Le Diagon Sarah 2025

Le Diagon Sarah, Jacquel Marie, Van der Henst Jean-Baptiste, Prado Jérôme. (2025). Individual differences in executive functions and theory of mind mediate the relation between academic skills from kindergarten to 5th grade. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0324547

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal correlational analysis of 95 French children followed from kindergarten to 5th grade, reported as zero-order correlations in Results. The within-domain reading correlation was significant with a printed effect size of "r(92)=.31, p = .002", described by the article as of medium size.

> "The correlation was of medium size for reading skills (r(92)=.31, p = .002) and of large size for math skills (r(92)=.52, p < .001)."

### Le Diagon Sarah 2025 (2)

Le Diagon Sarah, Jacquel Marie, Van der Henst Jean-Baptiste, Prado Jérôme. (2025). Individual differences in executive functions and theory of mind mediate the relation between academic skills from kindergarten to 5th grade. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0324547

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same zero-order correlational analysis in the same longitudinal sample. The within-domain math correlation was significant with a printed effect size of "r(92)=.52, p < .001", described by the article as of large size; regression confirmed it (β = .53).

> "The correlation was of medium size for reading skills (r(92)=.31, p = .002) and of large size for math skills (r(92)=.52, p < .001)."
