# Arizona students in foster care had the lowest statewide testing participation rate, declining sharply in later grades

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At every tested grade level students in foster care were less likely than other subgroups to participate in statewide testing, falling from 94 percent in grades 3–4 to 77 percent in grade 10. [→ Barrat 2015](#barrat-2015)

## Evidence

### Barrat 2015

Barrat, V. X., Berliner, B., & Felida, N. J. (2015). Arizona's Invisible Achievement Gap: Education Outcomes of Students in Foster Care in the State's Public Schools. San Francisco: WestEd. http://www.azed.gov/research-evaluation/files/2013/11/2013-a-f-technical-manual.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of participation in AIMS/AIMS A or AZELLA testing in grades 3–8 and 10 in 2012/13 (figure 8). Participation for the statewide population, low-SES students, students with disabilities, and English language learners was around 96 percent in early grades, declining to about 93 percent in grade 10.

> "For students in foster care, 94 percent took a statewide assessment in grades 3 and 4, on par with the other subgroups. However, the rate of test taking started to decrease sharply in grade 7, dropping to 81 percent in grade 8, and 77 percent in grade 10."
