## Evidence

### Xie Zhi-Bo 2025

Xie Zhi-Bo, Cheng Xin-Yu, Li Xiao-Yan, Zhang Yi-Fan. (2025). Team based learning pedagogy enhances the education quality: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07175-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Pooled analysis of four studies measuring knowledge retention, defined as the process by which information is stored and later retrieved from memory. The analysis found retention scores "significantly higher in the TBL group than in the LBL group".

> "Four studies [13, 25-27] indicated that the mean test scores of knowledge retention (the process by which information is stored, and later retrieved from memory) were significantly higher in the TBL group than in the LBL group (SMD = 1.03, 95%CI 0.38 to 1.69, I2 = 86.7%, random-effects model)"
