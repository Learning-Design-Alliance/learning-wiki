## Evidence

### Tithi 2026

Tithi, S.D., Alam, N., Yasir, T., Shi, Y., Tian, X., Chi, M., Barnes, T. (2026). Adaptive Scaffolding for Cognitive Engagement in an Intelligent Tutoring System. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07308

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive gap analysis in the N=113 study defining the test gap as the difference in mean scores between High and Low prior knowledge groups; BKT narrowed the gap from 23.8 to 5.4, Control from 24.5 to 10.8, and DRL from 27 to 12.8 (all p<0.001 for the gaps).

> "All training policies narrowed the achievement gap from pretest to posttest based on prior knowledge, with reductions of 55.9%Control, 52.6%DRL, and 77.1% forBKT."
