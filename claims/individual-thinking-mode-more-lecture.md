# Individual Thinking clicker class sessions devote significantly more time to instructor lecturing and student listening than the other two clicker-use modes.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Individual Thinking sessions had a significantly greater mean percentage of 2-min time intervals with lecturing and listening compared with Peer Discussion and Alternative Collaboration sessions. [→ Lewin JD 2016](#lewin-jd-2016)

## Evidence

### Lewin JD 2016

Lewin JD, Vinson EL, Stetzer MR, Smith MK. (2016). A Campus-Wide Investigation of Clicker Implementation: The Status of Peer Discussion in STEM Classes. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-10-0224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA with Tukey's post hoc test comparing the three clicker-use modes on percentage of 2-min time intervals with lecturing and listening; the article reports "p < 0.05, in both cases" for Individual Thinking versus the other two modes.

> "In addition, Individual Thinking class sessions had a significantly greater mean percentage of 2-min time intervals with instructor lecturing (Lec) and student listening (L) compared with the other two types of class sessions that use clickers (Figure 8B, one-way analysis of variance [ANOVA], Tukey's post hoc test, p < 0.05, in both cases)."
