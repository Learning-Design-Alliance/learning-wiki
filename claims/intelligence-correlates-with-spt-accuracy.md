## Evidence

### Gehlbach 2004

Gehlbach, Hunter. (2004). A New Perspective on Perspective Taking: A Multidimensional Approach to Conceptualizing an Aptitude. Educational Psychology Review 16 (3). https://doi.org/10.1023/B:EDPR.0000034021.12899.11

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

The review reports a meta-analysis finding intellectual functioning, "as assessed by a variety of intelligence measures", positively associated with SPT accuracy at r = .23. The review attributes the modest size to the review authors' synthesis of prior studies.

> "Davis and Kraus's (1997) meta-analysis showed that intellectual functioning (as assessed by a variety of intelligence measures) is positively associated with social perspective taking accuracy at a modest level (r = .23)."

### Gehlbach 2004 (2)

Gehlbach, Hunter. (2004). A New Perspective on Perspective Taking: A Multidimensional Approach to Conceptualizing an Aptitude. Educational Psychology Review 16 (3). https://doi.org/10.1023/B:EDPR.0000034021.12899.11

`q2 · i3` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports Selman's (1980) finding that IQ correlated with his five developmental stages of perspective taking at r = .77, described as much stronger than the meta-analytic estimate. No sample details are printed in the review.

> "Selman (1980) found that IQ correlated much more strongly with his five stages of perspective taking (r = .77)."
