## Evidence

### Tania S. Moraga-Calderón 2020

Tania S. Moraga-Calderón, Henk Busiman and Julia Cramer. (2020). The relevance of learning quantum physics from the perspective of the secondary school student: A case study. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.30935/scimath/9545](https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9545)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of section C answers and paired interviews with four students. The article reports that acknowledging technological applications was not enough for students to see the societal relevance of learning quantum physics.

> "none of these students expressed how quantum -scientific literacy would allow them to develop their own voice as citizens and judge or participate in future debates."
