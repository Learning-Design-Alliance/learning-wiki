Yang AY, Patel NA, Khan M, Cherry-Bukowiec JR, Brown LR, Machado-Aranda DA, Mazza MB, Chong S. (2022). In-person radiologist to review the trauma panscan: a high-fidelity simulation training program for radiology trainees at an academic level 1 trauma center. Emergency Radiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10140-022-02109-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Timestamp analysis of the 62-study group panscan checklists and RIS preliminary report stamps, compared with pre-implementation control times of 50.7 ± 15.6 min and 45.9 ± 15.8 min. The article prints descriptive means and SDs only; no significance test is reported for the scanner-versus-preliminary-report contrast.

> "The mean time to provide results at the scanner using the panscan checklist was 11.1 min (range 3–56 min). The mean time from completion of the panscan checklist to issuance of the preliminary report for the CT head and cervical spine studies was reduced to 24.4 ± 9.8 min and for the CT chest, abdomen, and pelvis decreased to 16.3 ± 6.9 min."
