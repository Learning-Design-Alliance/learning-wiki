## Evidence

### Taylor Margaret C. 2026

Taylor Margaret C., Diaz Hannah L. (2026). Guiding Midwives to Precept Students: Two Evidence‐Based Models. Journal of Midwifery & Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/jmwh.70075

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing Gatewood et al, a multidisciplinary study of community clinicians precepting nurse practitioner students: a two-hour workshop with pre- and postsurveys found participants "reported that the preceptor training influenced them to make changes in their teaching". No effect sizes printed.

> "The authors offered a two‐hour workshop to 36 community clinicians across disciplines. Using pre‐ and postsurveys, the authors found that the participants who used the OMP reported that the preceptor training influenced them to make changes in their teaching, promoting positive feedback and correction of mistakes for their students."

### Taylor Margaret C. 2026 (2)

Taylor Margaret C., Diaz Hannah L. (2026). Guiding Midwives to Precept Students: Two Evidence‐Based Models. Journal of Midwifery & Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/jmwh.70075

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article reports, citing Miura et al, a pilot training program for NPs using validated questionnaires; NPs using OMP training reported improvements in self-confidence and willingness to precept. The article notes it was a small study of 9 NPs. No effect sizes printed.

> "The NPs who used the OMP training in their clinical teaching reported improvements in self‐confidence and in their experiences as educators with an improved willingness to serve as preceptors."
