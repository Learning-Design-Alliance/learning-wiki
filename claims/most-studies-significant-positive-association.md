The qualitative synthesis of the 31 included studies found "Nineteen of the 31 papers reported a statistically significant positive association(s)" (ps < .05), while eight reported non-significant positive associations and four reported mixed findings across multiple measures. Individual study correlations ranged widely (e.g. r = .00 to r = .84 in Table 2).
