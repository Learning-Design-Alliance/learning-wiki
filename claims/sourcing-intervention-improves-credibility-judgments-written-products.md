# The intervention improved students' sourcing in credibility judgments and in written products relative to controls

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The intervention fostered students' attention to source features in their credibility judgments (d = 0.39) and their use of sources in written products (d = 0.37). [→ Hämäläinen 2023](#hamalainen-2023)

## Evidence

### Hämäläinen 2023

Hämäläinen, E. K., Kiili, C., Räikkönen, E., Lakkala, M., Ilomäki, L., Toom, A., & Marttunen, M. (2023). Teaching sourcing during online inquiry – adolescents with the weakest skills benefited the most. Instructional Science, 51, 135–163. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09597-2

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Linear regression analyses of post-test Sourcing in credibility judgments and Sourcing in written product, controlling for corresponding pre-test scores, reading fluency, prior topic knowledge, and topic order; Table 4 reports group effects of d = 0.39 [0.17; 0.61] and d = 0.37 [0.15; 0.58].

> "the regression analyses (see Table 4) showed that the intervention fostered students’ attention to source features in their credibility judgments as well as their use of sources in their written products."

### Hämäläinen 2023 (2)

Hämäläinen, E. K., Kiili, C., Räikkönen, E., Lakkala, M., Ilomäki, L., Toom, A., & Marttunen, M. (2023). Teaching sourcing during online inquiry – adolescents with the weakest skills benefited the most. Instructional Science, 51, 135–163. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09597-2

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Baseline descriptive comparison of the intervention (N = 175–191) and control (N = 143–162) groups showed the groups performed equally on all variables except a small pre-test advantage in credibility judgments with printed effect size d = 0.22, which the regressions controlled for.

> "In the pretest, the intervention group outperformed the control group only in Sourcing in credibility judgments ( t(342.03) = -2.05, p = .041, d = 0.22)."
