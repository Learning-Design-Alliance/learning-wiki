Evidence¶

Koposov 2014¶

Koposov, R., Gundersen, K. K. & Svartdal, F. (2014). Efficacy of Aggression Replacement Training among Children from North-West Russia. International Journal of Emotional Education, 6(1), 14-24. https://www.um.edu.mt/cres/ijee

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA of parent and teacher SSRS social competence ratings in the quasi-experimental evaluation (29-41 parents, 41-48 teachers per group). A significant time effect occurred in both conditions; no effect size is printed.

"The ANOVA indicated a significant effe ct of time, F (1, 64) = 58.16, p < .001, reflecting that SSRS scores for both informants and both conditions changed markedly between pre and post (Figure 4). Obviously, there were no differences between the ART and control groups."

Related Claims¶

  • ART effects on self-reported social competence are moderated by age, with reliable effects in the 6-9 and 10-14 groups but not among children aged 15 and older — reports the opposite
  • The 30-hour ART programme is associated with a significant increase in children's self-reported overall social competence, with no change in the control group — related
  • Parent and teacher ratings of externalizing problem behaviour decreased from pre to post in both intervention and control groups, with no other significant effects — related
  • Younger ART participants showed increased self-reported scores on all four SSRS subscales (cooperation, assertion, empathy, self-control) from pre to post, whereas no changes were observed in the control group — related
