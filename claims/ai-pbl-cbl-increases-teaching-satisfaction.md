## Evidence

### Yang 2026

Yang, F., Wang, J., & He, L. (2026). Application of AI-assisted PBL-CBL combined teaching in the diagnosis and treatment of post-herpetic neuralgia: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 26(1), 1149. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09489-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Author-created satisfaction questionnaire (Cronbach's α = 0.83) distributed to all 120 interns with a 100% valid response rate. The observation group's "total satisfaction rate" was 95% versus 85% in the control group; overall satisfaction score also significantly higher (t = 1.98, P < 0.05).

> "The total satisfaction rate of the observation group was 95% (57/60) which is higher than the control group 85% (51/60)."

### Yang 2026 (2)

Yang, F., Wang, J., & He, L. (2026). Application of AI-assisted PBL-CBL combined teaching in the diagnosis and treatment of post-herpetic neuralgia: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 26(1), 1149. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09489-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same satisfaction questionnaire. "No statistically significant difference" in teamwork satisfaction between groups; authors suggest teamwork satisfaction may depend more on team dynamics than on AI tools.

> "No statistically significant difference was observed between the two groups in teamwork satisfaction (t = 1.89, P > 0.05)"
