## Evidence

### Chance-Revels 2026

Chance-Revels, R., Phan, Q., Crawford, K., Steiger, L., & Swan, B. A. (2026). Enhancing Community Health Worker Communication: Evaluating Simulation-Based Training in Motivational Interviewing. Journal of Community Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10900-025-01543-w

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

A paired-samples t-test on pre- and post-training confidence surveys of 277 CHW trainees showed a statistically significant modest improvement in confidence applying MI skills (d = 0.33, 95% CI [0.21, 0.45]). This was the second-strongest effect among the three domains.

> "Confidence in the ability to apply motivational interviewing (MI) skills also increased significantly, t(276) = 5.52, p <.001, d = 0.33, 95% CI [0.21, 0.45], suggesting a modest positive change in trainees' perceived competence to address health behavior change."
