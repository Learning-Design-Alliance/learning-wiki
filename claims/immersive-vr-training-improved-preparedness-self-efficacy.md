## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre–post one-group study (Alsaqer and Alhmoud 2025, Jordan) of an immersive VR disaster training program including theoretical instruction, VR simulation scenarios such as mass casualty incidents and triage, and structured debriefing, delivered over three sessions in 90 registered emergency department nurses. The review reports "p < 0.001"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Significant improvement in disaster preparedness (NPDCC) and disaster response self‐efficacy (DRSES) from pretest to posttest (p < 0.001); follow‐up scores showed slight decline but remained significantly higher than baseline (p < 0.001), indicating short‐term retention of skills."
