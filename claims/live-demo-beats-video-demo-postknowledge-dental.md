## Evidence

### Alkattan R 2025

Alkattan R, Alreshaid L, Sarvestan J. (2025). The Effectiveness of Live and Prerecorded Video Demonstrations in Teaching Restorative Dentistry to Undergraduate Students: Cohort Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/74383

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Cohort study of 51 first-year dental students quasi-randomized by serial number to LD (26) or VD (25) in a preclinical restorative dentistry course. "knowledge was significantly higher with LD" on the 10-item postprocedural questionnaire, with a medium-large effect size (Cohen d=0.65).

> "After the demonstration, knowledge was significantly higher with LD (mean 71.22, SD 17.3) than VD (mean 58.4, SD 21.7; P=.02; Cohen d=0.65)."
