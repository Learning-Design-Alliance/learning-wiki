## Evidence

### Zhao Yu 2021

Zhao Yu, Pinto Llorente Ana María, Sánchez Gómez María Cruz. (2021). Digital competence in higher education research: A systematic literature review. Computers & Education, 168, 104212. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104212

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis of the four articles exploring digital competence's impact on students' achievement; the review attributes this association to He and Li (2019), He and Zhu (2017), and He et al. (2018), and notes the article reports no pooled effect size.

> "students with higher level of digital competence tended to more get involved in DIL"

### Zhao Yu 2021 (2)

Zhao Yu, Pinto Llorente Ana María, Sánchez Gómez María Cruz. (2021). Digital competence in higher education research: A systematic literature review. Computers & Education, 168, 104212. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104212

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review reports, attributing to Kim et al. (2019), that within a university e-learning context digital competence and digital readiness showed positive and significant effects on academic engagement; no effect size is printed in the review.

> "Kim et al. (2019) indicated that digital competence and digital readiness had positive and significant effects on academic engagement."
