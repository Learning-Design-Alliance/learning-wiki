## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The learning-phase MANOVA showed a large multivariate main effect of group experience and a medium-sized main effect of information distribution, with a nonsignificant interaction. [→ Jimmy Zambrano 2019](#jimmy-zambrano-2019)

## Evidence

### Jimmy Zambrano 2019

Jimmy Zambrano, Femke Kirschner, John Sweller, Paul A. Kirschner. (2019). Effects of group experience and information distribution on collaborative learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-019-09495-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus MANOVA over learning-phase performance, mental effort, and efficiency: group experience η²p = .26 (large) and information distribution η²p = .10; the interaction was nonsignificant (F(2, 60) = .67, p = .52).

> "MANOVA revealed significant main effects for group experience, F (2, 60) = 10.40, Wilks' Λ = .74, p < .001, /u1D7022 p = .26, and information distribution, F (2, 60) = 3.33, Wilks' Λ = .90, p = .04, /u1D7022 p = .10, which indicate that these variables affect a combination of performance, mental effort, and efficiency scores."
