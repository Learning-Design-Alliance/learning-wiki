# JOL reactivity depended on pair relatedness: negative reactivity for unrelated word pairs but no reactivity for related word pairs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i0`–`i2`

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` When participants made JOLs, there was negative reactivity for unrelated word pairs (d = 0.37) but no reactivity for related word pairs (d = −0.03). [→ Murphy 2024](#murphy-2024)

## Evidence

### Murphy 2024

Murphy, D. H., Rhodes, M. G., & Castel, A. D. (2024). Age-related differences in metacognitive reactivity in younger and older adults. Metacognition and Learning, 19, 863–877. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09391-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The JOL × relatedness interaction was significant, F(1, 477) = 26.64, p < .001, ηp2 = 0.05, BF10 > 100. JOLs impaired recall of unrelated pairs, "t(479) = 4.08, p < .001, d = 0.37".

> "Next, whether or not participants made JOLs interacted with relatedness [ F(1, 477) = 26.64, p < .001, ηp2 = 0.05, BF10 > 100] such that when participants made JOLs, there was negative reactivity for unrelated word pairs [ pholm < 0.001, d = 0.46]; a separate t-test corroborated this effect [ t(479) = 4.08, p < .001, d = 0.37"

### Murphy 2024 (2)

Murphy, D. H., Rhodes, M. G., & Castel, A. D. (2024). Age-related differences in metacognitive reactivity in younger and older adults. Metacognition and Learning, 19, 863–877. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09391-0
