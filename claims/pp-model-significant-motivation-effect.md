## Evidence

### Christidamayani 2020

Christidamayani, A. P., & Kristanto, Y. D. (2020). The Effects of Problem Posing Learning Model on Students' Learning Achievement and Motivation. Indonesian Journal on Learning and Advanced Education, 2(2), 100-108. https://doi.org/10.23917/ijolae.v2i2.9981

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-tests on the learning motivation questionnaire before and after implementation in the quasi-experimental pre-test post-test control group study. After implementation, experimental group motivation "was significantly higher than the students in control group", supporting the motivation hypothesis.

> "Furthermore, it can be concluded from Table 4 that students' learning motivation in experimental group was significantly higher than the students in control group after the implementation."
