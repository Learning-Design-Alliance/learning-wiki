### SIPIYARUK Kawin 2023

SIPIYARUK Kawin, KAEWSIRIRAT Prachworrakit, SANTIWONG Peerapong. (2023). Technology-enhanced simulation-based learning in orthodontic education: A scoping review. Dental Press Journal of Orthodontics. https://doi.org/10.1590/2177-6709.28.3.e2321354.oar

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, from Lo et al.'s clinical trial of an assisted bracket navigation system, that accuracy of bracket placement was compared between conventional and AR methods for novice and expert orthodontists, with enhancement shown for novices.

> "The clinical trial reported that the intervention could enhance the accuracy of bracket placement for novice orthodontists, by comparing the accuracy of bracket placement between the conventional and AR methods by measuring the deviations of bracket positions"
