Discussion of the mixed ANOVA results: the authors had intended cues to modulate anticipation and automatization, but found no cue effect on RTs or dual-task costs; some results indicated cues were actually detrimental for developing an explicit visual representation of the sequence.

> "However, this wa s not the case for anticipatory cues provided during practice— response times did not differ signiﬁcantly when sequences were learnt with or without anticipatory cues."
