Evidence¶

Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir 2025¶

Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir. (2025). ChatGPT-Assisted Lesson Planning for Children's STEAM Arts Education: An Experimental Study on Benefits, Challenges, Methods, and a Prompt Framework. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00513-6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-study questionnaire with 48 valid responses plus three in-depth teacher interviews on lesson-planning practices. The article reports "70.8% teachers reported difficulties in integrating resources from multiple platforms" and 60.4% reported difficulty assessing quality and relevance.

"70.8% teachers reported difficulties in integrating resources from multiple platforms, highlighting the fragmentation of teaching and learning materials."

Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir 2025 (2)¶

Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir. (2025). ChatGPT-Assisted Lesson Planning for Children's STEAM Arts Education: An Experimental Study on Benefits, Challenges, Methods, and a Prompt Framework. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00513-6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-study questionnaire on AI perceptions among the 48 surveyed teachers. The article reports "Approximately 30% of teachers have already integrated AI tools into their teaching practices" and over 50% expressed a positive outlook, with interest in AI-generated outlines rated 3.7 out of 5 on average.

"Approximately 30% of teachers have already integrated AI tools into their teaching practices, reflecting the gradual expansion of AI adoption in education."
