## Evidence

### Singh 2010

Singh, C. and Zhu, G. (2010). Surveying Students' Understanding of Quantum Mechanics. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The QMS was given twice to one full-year junior-senior class that used research-based learning tools: at the end of the first semester (11 students) and of the second (9 students). "The average student performance did not deteriorate after a whole semester." Descriptive means only.

> "The course did not directly involve the contents in the QMS. It is encouraging that the average student performance did not deteriorate after a whole semester."
