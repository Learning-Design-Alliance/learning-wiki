## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students spent a mean of 1.6 additional seconds per question on questions that included ethnicity, a statistically significant difference. [→ Mohan Jenanan 2025](#mohan-jenanan-2025)

## Evidence

### Mohan Jenanan 2025

Mohan Jenanan, Pishia Michaela, Alim Duaa, McGown Patrick J., Amin Anjali, Pinder Richard J., Halse Omid, Brown Celia, Sam Amir H. (2025). The impact of ethnicity information in single best answer questions on student performance, item response time and psychometric properties. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10489-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Response-time analysis from the same randomised parallel-forms study (n = 267 students), using per-question timing from the risr/assess platform; paired t-test. The article reports "1.6 additional seconds ... statistically significant (t = 3.95, p < 0.01)".

> "Students spent a mean of 1.6 additional seconds (standard deviation = 5.6 seconds) on questions that included ethnicity compared to those that did not. This difference was statistically significant (t = 3.95, p < 0.01)."
