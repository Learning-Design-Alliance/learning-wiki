`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post within-group comparison in the pilot RCT found the management-comfort increase significant only in the simulation group, with printed effect sizes "d=0.63" (traditional) and "d=1.3" (simulation). The discussion calls this a large effect size restricted to comfort in management.

> "However, only the simulation group reported a significant increase in self-reported comfort in managing shortness of breath in a pregnant patient (d=0.63; p=0.051 for the traditional group and d=1.3; p=0.0003 for the simulation group, Table 2)."
