Lina Wirth 2026¶

Lina Wirth, Timo Ehmke, Jan Retelsdorf, Poldi Kuhl. (2026). Linguistic demands of instructions: Effects on students’ expectancy-value beliefs. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09799-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the N = 123 path model, the difficult-video × language-abilities interaction term was significant for expectancies of success: "a significant negative effect of the interaction … (β = −0.29, SE = 0.03, z = −2.66, p =.008)". The other interaction terms were non-significant.
