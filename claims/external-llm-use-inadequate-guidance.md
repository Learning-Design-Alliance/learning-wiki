### Madeleine Eastwood 2026

Madeleine Eastwood, Harshith Narne, Joseph Hilby, Paul Denny, Ashish Aggarwal, and Amanpreet Kapoor. (2026). Guardrails or Roadblocks? Effects of Pedagogical Style and Context Awareness in AI Teaching Assistants for Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29995

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported external LLM use from the post-survey in the randomized experiment; a chi-square test found no significant condition differences. The Socratic + Full Context condition reported the highest rate (23%, 7 of 30 students) versus 9–15% in the other conditions.

> "Overall, 15% of students (20 of 132) re- ported using another LLM or chatbot during the experiment. Although usage rates differed descriptively across conditions, the differences were not statistically significant (𝜒 2(3)= 2.47, 𝑝=. 48)."
