In the L3 stage of the RCT, the stepwise CBME group had significantly shorter operative time (120 ± 15 min vs. 180 ± 20 min, P < 0.001), lower blood loss, and better cosmetic outcomes than the conventional training group.

> "Under supervised instruction, the experimental group outperformed the control group in breast-conserving surgery, with significantly shorter operative time (120 ± 15 min vs. 180 ± 20 min, P < 0.001), lower intraoperative blood loss (35.2 ± 8.7 mL vs. 58.4 ± 12.3 mL, P < 0.001), and better cosmetic outcomes (92% vs. 78%, P = 0.043)."
