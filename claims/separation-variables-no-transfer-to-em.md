## Evidence

### Justyna P. Zwolak and Corinne A. Manogue 2014

Justyna P. Zwolak and Corinne A. Manogue. (2014). Revealing Differences Between Curricula Using the Colorado Upper-Division Electrostatics Diagnostic. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.3400](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.3400)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation of their CUE findings: OSU students meet separation of variables mainly in the Schrödinger-equation context (Waves in One Dimension, Central Forces, Mathematical Methods), with only one day on Laplace's equation plus 2 or 3 homework problems in the E&M Capstone. This is an interpretive claim, not a tested comparison.

> "As the CUE has re- vealed, however, this is not happening and the separation of variables procedure does not become a natural E&M problem-solving technique for students once they depart from the quantum world."
