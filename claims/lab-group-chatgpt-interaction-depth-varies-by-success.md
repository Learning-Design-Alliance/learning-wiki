## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sebastian Kilde-Westberg 2025

Sebastian Kilde-Westberg, Andreas Johansson, and Jonas Enger. (2025). Generative AI as a lab partner: a case study. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/ggy1-3kjk](https://doi.org/10.1103/ggy1-3kjk)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video and chat-log analysis of seven lab groups found struggling groups "did little to no follow-up" while Groups 4 and 6 probed answers, re-asked questions in fresh chats, and discussed replies among themselves.

> "For groups 1, 2, 3, and 5, interactions with ChatGPT focused on asking simple questions and they did little to no follow-up."
