Johnson 2023¶

Johnson, R. C. (2023). School funding effectiveness: Evidence from California's Local Control Funding Formula. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/529.194

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of which uses of LCFF funding are most strongly associated with improved student outcomes, comparing spending categories across districts. The article frames the three inputs as each related to sustaining a strong, stable teacher workforce.

"The results reveal that roughly 84% of the variation in school spending effectiveness can be explained and is predominantly driven by the trio of combined funding impacts of class size reductions, teacher salary increases, and reductions in teacher turnover."
