## Evidence

### Alba López-Moraga 2024

Alba López-Moraga, Laura Luyten & Tom Beckers. (2024). A history of avoidance does not impact extinction learning in male rats. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00223-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered classification of persistent Avoiders in Experiment 2, where the platform was present during extinction. Using the criterion of more than 50% of CS time on the platform on the third extinction day, "We did not identify any rats meeting that criterion"; reanalysis with prior criteria also found none.

> "We did not identify any rats meeting that criterion, so all rats in Experiment 2 were considered non-persistent Avoiders according to our preregistered deﬁnition."

### Alba López-Moraga 2024 (2)

Alba López-Moraga, Laura Luyten & Tom Beckers. (2024). A history of avoidance does not impact extinction learning in male rats. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00223-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered classification of resistant Avoiders in Experiment 3, with shortened two-day extinction. Only "two rats that qualiﬁed as resistant Avoiders (2/11: 18%)" under both the preregistered and prior criteria; preregistered comparisons were not performed due to the low number.

> "We identiﬁed two rats that qualiﬁed as resistant Avoiders (2/11: 18%). When applying the criteria used in a previous study 15, we found only 18% of resistant Avoiders as well."
