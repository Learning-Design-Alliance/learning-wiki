## Evidence

### Wang Dong 2025

Wang Dong. (2025). Integration of Three-dimensional Visualization Reconstruction Technology with Problem-based Learning in the Clinical Training of Resident Physicians Specialized in Pheochromocytoma. Current Medical Imaging. https://doi.org/10.2174/0115734056327236250101052226

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 50 urology residents (25 per group) at Peking Union Medical College Hospital, January 2022 to January 2024. The experimental group received integrated 3D visualization and PBL instruction; the control received traditional teaching. Theoretical examination scores favored the experimental group, "92.15±3.22 and 81.09±4.46, respectively (< 0.0001)".

> "The results of theoretical examinations for the experimental group and the control group were 92.15±3.22 and 81.09±4.46, respectively (< 0.0001)."
