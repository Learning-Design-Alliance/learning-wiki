## Evidence

### Yakmaci-Guzel 2013

Yakmaci-Guzel, B., & Adadan, E. (2013). Use of multiple representations in developing preservice chemistry teachers' understanding of the structure of matter. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 8(1), 109-130. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008597

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group quasi-experimental study of 19 preservice chemistry teachers using Wilcoxon signed-ranks tests; the article reports "a statistically significant difference between the participants' total pre and total posttest scores (z=3.34, p=0.001)". Part-level tests were also significant (part a z=2.88, p<0.005; part b z=2.77, p<0.01; part c z=2.18, p<0.05).
