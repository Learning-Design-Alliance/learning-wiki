## Evidence

### Khrisat and Mahmoud 2013

Abdulhafeth A. Khrisat & Salameh Saleem Mahmoud. (2013). Integrating Mobile Phones into the EFL Foundation Year Classroom in King Abdulaziz University/KSA: Effects on Achievement in General English and Students' Attitudes. English Language Teaching, 6(8). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n8p162

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire study of the forty foundation-year participants using a twenty-item five-point Likert instrument with reported reliability of 0.84. The article's summary of the willingness subgroup shows "60% responded positively". Percentages are descriptive; no inferential test is reported.

> "A summary of the percentages of the six items shows that 25% of the sample responded negatively to the six items of the first subgroup that is using the mobile in the EFL classroom, whereas 15% were neutral and 60% responded positively"
