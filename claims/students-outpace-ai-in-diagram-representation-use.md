### Amogh Sirnoorkar 2024

Amogh Sirnoorkar, Dean Zollman, James T Laverty, Alejandra J Magana, Sanjay Rebello, Lynn A Bryan. (2024). Student and AI responses to physics problems examined through the lenses of sensemaking and mechanistic reasoning. [doi:10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100318](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100318)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Distribution of representation use in mechanistic reasoning (Table 4) across student and ChatGPT responses to both Gravitron formats. The article reports that every student solution to the open-ended problem accompanied force diagrams, and that "only three of the AI solutions reflected the same"; students also used gestures (4 of 8 each format) which AI did not.
