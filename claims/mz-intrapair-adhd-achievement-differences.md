## Evidence

### de Zeeuw Eveline L. 2017

de Zeeuw Eveline L., van Beijsterveldt Catharina E. M., Ehli Erik A., de Geus Eco J. C., Boomsma Dorret I. (2017). Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder Symptoms and Low Educational Achievement: Evidence Supporting A Causal Hypothesis. Behavior Genetics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10519-017-9836-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

MZ twin intra-pair differences model correlating within-pair differences in mother-rated ADHD symptoms with differences in Cito educational achievement. Correlations were significant for boys and girls; the article concludes "the twin with more ADHD symptoms had a lower educational achievement score than his or her co-twin". No standardized effect size is printed for this finding.

> "Thus, in genetically identical twin pairs, the twin with more ADHD symptoms had a lower educational achievement score than his or her co-twin."
