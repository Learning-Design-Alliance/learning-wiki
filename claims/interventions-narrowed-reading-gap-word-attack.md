`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Table 4 and Figures 1-12 report relative gap reduction (RGR), defined as the impact divided by the difference between the population average and the control group average at follow-up; the third-grade Word Attack RGR was 0.68. The report states the gap was "reduced... by about two-thirds for students in the third-grade cohort."

> "Being in one of the interventions reduced the reading gap in Word Attack skills by about two-thirds for students in the third-grade cohort.  On other word-level tests and a measure of reading comprehension, the interventions reduced the gap for students in the third-grade cohort by about one-sixth to one-third."
