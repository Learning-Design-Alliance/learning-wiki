Evidence¶

Lenat 2014¶

Lenat, D. B., & Durlach, P. J. (2014). Reinforcing Math Knowledge by Immersing Students in a Simulated Learning-By-Teaching Experience. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0016-x

q2 · i? · design · r1

Observational sessions with sixth graders in Austin: naturalistic peer tutoring and a paper-and-pencil game (Clued In) with a solver and clue-giver. The authors report tutors "had a tendency to just take over the problem" and often provided inaccurate feedback.

"Both situations suggested that sixth grade math students were not very good math tutors, even if they themselves were good at math. Rather than help the tutee, peer tutors had a tendency to just take over the problem and do it themselves."
