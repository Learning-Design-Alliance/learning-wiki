# Combined pragmalinguistics- and sociopragmatics-focused input-based instruction (CI) produced significantly better written production of English request hedges than sociopragmatics-focused instruction alone (SI) and a control group

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Takimoto 2014

Takimoto, M. (2014). Evaluating the Effects of Input-based Approaches to the Teaching of Pragmalinguistics and Sociopragmatics in Second Language Pragmatics: A Case of English Request Hedges. The IAFOR Journal of Language Learning, I(I). https://iafor.org/journal-of-language-learning

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Two-way repeated-measures ANOVA of discourse completion test scores for 45 Japanese university learners assigned to CI (n=15), SI (n=15), and control (n=15) intact classes. The article reports a significant main effect for instruction, F (2, 42) = 18.46, p = .000 < .05, with the printed effect size Eta2 = .976, and the quoted post-hoc contrast.

> "The post-hoc Scheffé tests for the main effect of treatment indicate the following contrasts: (a) the pragmalinguistics- and sociopragmatics-focused instruction (CI) group performed significantly better than the sociopragmatics-focused instruction (SI) group and the control group"
