## Evidence

### Wang Xinyu 2026

Wang Xinyu, Li Xinquan, Zhang Jiahui, Bao Sihan, Bian MaoWang, Sun Jiabin, Cai Weiqin, Xiao Yang, An Hongqing. (2026). Cultivating competent public health professionals: the role of research participation and academic motivation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09145-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 906 public health students using propensity score matching (nearest neighbour, radius, kernel, Mahalanobis, caliper) to compare research participants with non-participants on an entropy-weighted job competency score. The article reports "ATT values estimated by the five matching methods range from 0.0372 to 0.0445" with all significant at the 1% level.
