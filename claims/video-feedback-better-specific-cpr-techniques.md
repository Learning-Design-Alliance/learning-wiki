## Evidence

### Miri Kheizaran 2024

Miri Kheizaran, Yaghoubi Ali, Kholousi Sadaf, Yousofzadeh Mahdi, Zanganeh Alireza, Gharayi Mehdi, Namazinia Mohammad. (2024). Comparative study on the impact of ‘Infographic versus video feedback’ on enhancing students’ clinical skills in basic life support. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05763-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-intervention comparison of mannequin-based technique performance in 38 students per group, reported in Table 3. The airway-opening contrast is quoted; the same section reports depth (81.6% vs. 57.9%, P = 0.045), rate (84.2% vs. 55.3%, P = 0.006), and hand position (73.7% vs. 47.4%, P = 0.019) favoring video feedback.

> "The Video Feedback group also showed a significantly higher percentage of participants demonstrating the appropriate technique to open the airway (84.2%) compared to the Infographic group (36.8%) with a P value of less than 0.001."
