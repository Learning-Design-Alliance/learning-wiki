## Evidence

### Ana Cebollero-Salinas 2022

Ana Cebollero-Salinas, Jacobo Cano-Escoriaza, Santos Orejudo. (2022). Are socio-emotional e-competencies protective factors against habitual digital behaviors (media multitasking, phubbing, cybergossip) in Spanish secondary students?. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104464

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group SEM on n = 776 students. Unlike the regulatory competencies, the social e-competency path is positive in both gender and age models. The authors attribute this to virtual environments where distant, anonymous relations favor prosocial online behaviors that reinforce commenting and sharing conducts.

> "Social e-competency serves as a relevant, invariant predictor among genders (β = 0.18) and age groups (β = 0.22)."
