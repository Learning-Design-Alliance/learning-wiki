# In a pilot at a brachytherapy workshop, the patient preparation VR module was associated with statistically significant increases in self-reported confidence for recalling steps, explaining steps, identifying equipment, and explaining equipment function (N=11)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the patient preparation VR module, paired Wilcoxon tests showed increased confidence for recalling steps (W = 65, p = 0.002) and explaining steps (W = 51, p = 0.023). [→ Varlukhin 2024](#varlukhin-2024)
`q2 i?` Confidence for identifying equipment (W = 65, p = 0.002) and explaining equipment function (W = 60, p = 0.0078) also increased after the patient preparation module. [→ Varlukhin 2024 (2)](#varlukhin-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Varlukhin 2024

Varlukhin, A., Smith, M., Alam, F., Tagger, A., Morton, G., Paudel, M., Loblaw, A., Mendez, L., Hoover, D., Karshafian, R., Nusrat, H. (2024). Development and pilot evaluation of a virtual reality simulator for HDR prostate brachytherapy. [doi:10.1016/j.jmir.2024.03.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmir.2024.03.029)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-group pre/post pilot of the patient preparation module at the Hands On Brachytherapy Workshop; eleven participants completed paired surveys. The article reports "(W = 65, p = 0.002)" for recalling steps and "(W = 51, p = 0.023)" for explaining steps; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Recalling steps had increased after the training session (W = 65, p = 0.002). The explanation of steps increased after the training session (W = 51, p = 0.023)."

### Varlukhin 2024 (2)

Varlukhin, A., Smith, M., Alam, F., Tagger, A., Morton, G., Paudel, M., Loblaw, A., Mendez, L., Hoover, D., Karshafian, R., Nusrat, H. (2024). Development and pilot evaluation of a virtual reality simulator for HDR prostate brachytherapy. [doi:10.1016/j.jmir.2024.03.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmir.2024.03.029)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same paired-survey pilot cohort (N=11) at the workshop; the article reports "(W = 65, p = 0.002)" for identifying equipment and "(W = 60, p = 0.0078)" for explaining equipment function, with no effect sizes printed.

> "Identification of equipment increased after the training session (W = 65, p = 0.002).  Explanation of equipment function increased after the training session (W = 60, p = 0.0078)."
