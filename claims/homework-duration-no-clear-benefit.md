### Nathan McJames 2023

Nathan McJames, Andrew Parnell, and Ann O'Shea. (2023). Little and Often: Causal Inference Machine Learning Demonstrates the Benefits of Homework for Improving Achievement in Mathematics and Science. https://timssandpirls.bc.edu/timss2019/international-results

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Duration results from the multivariate Bayesian Causal Forests analysis of the TIMSS 2019 Irish eighth-grade sample, relative to a baseline of up to fifteen minutes per assignment. Table 2 prints mathematics effects of -0.42 (-3.22, 2.48) for 16-30 minutes and 0.23 (-3.11, 3.53) for more than 30 minutes; science intervals are mostly positive but small.

> "Notice that all credible intervals in the duration section of Table 2 include zero within their range, meaning we can not be confident that increasing homework duration will increase achievement."
