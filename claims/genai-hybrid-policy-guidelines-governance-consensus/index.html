q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Delphi consensus finding from Phase 1 open-ended responses of a 35-member international expert panel. The article reports that "The majority of the panelists (88%)" favored combining formal policies with flexible guidelines; only four panelists favored guidelines alone and none advocated policies alone.

"The majority of the panelists (88%) indicated that effective GenAI implementation in HE requires a combination of formal policies and flexible guidelines, rather than prioritizing one form of governance."
