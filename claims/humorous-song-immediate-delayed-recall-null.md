## Evidence

### Rafiee et al. 2010

Marzieh Rafiee, Zohre Kassaian, Hossein Vahid Dastjerdi. (2010). The Application of Humorous Song in EFL Classrooms and Its Effects on Listening Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 3(4). http://www.ccsenet.org/elt

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intra-group Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test comparing the experimental group's immediate recall (M=54.00) and delayed recall after a 3-week interval (M=57.33). The article reports the sig. level is not equal to or less than 0.05, so "the two sets of scores are not significantly different"; no effect size is printed.

> "Because this experiment had small samples, and the data did not meet the stringent assumptions of the parametric techniques, a Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test was conducted to measure the effect of song on participants' immediate and delayed recall."
