### Mathieu Bouville 2008

Mathieu Bouville. (2008). Exam fairness. https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.4235 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0803.4235](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0803.4235)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Conceptual analysis of exam length in the worked example section: fairness of treatment cannot decide the question since all students face the same time limit, and the situation is symmetric between slow-but-careful and fast-but-careless students. Cited recommendations for unlimited time are noted.

> "On the other hand, a short test (timewise) tends to penalize more conscientious students while a longer exam may penalize faster students. Whatever we do, we are bound to give an advantage to someone: all we can do is choose to whom."
