### Silventoinen Karri 2023

Silventoinen Karri, Luukkonen Juha, Myrskylä Mikko, Martikainen Pekka. (2023). Birth size, school performance and family social position: a study of 650,000 children. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-023-02757-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sequentially adjusted population-averaged models in the Finnish register cohort (Model 4 adds maternal and paternal education and household income to Model 3). "(32% in boys and 22% in girls)" of the BL association was explained relative to the demographically adjusted model.

> "For BL, adjusting the results for gestational risk factors and family SEP (Model 4) explained a part of the association (32% in boys and 22% in girls) when compared to the results adjusted only for demographic factors (Model 2)."
