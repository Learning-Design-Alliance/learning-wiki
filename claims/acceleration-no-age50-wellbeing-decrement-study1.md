## Evidence

### Bernstein Brian O. 2021

Bernstein Brian O., Lubinski David, Benbow Camilla P. (2021). Academic Acceleration in Gifted Youth and Fruitless Concerns Regarding Psychological Well-Being: A 35-Year Longitudinal Study. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000500

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal survey of 1,636 participants from three SMPY cohorts identified before age 13 (1972–1983) and tracked over 35 years. The first principal component of five well-being measures showed a printed correlation of r = −.06 (p = .02) with the acceleration composite and r = .03 with age of high school graduation; Table 1 correlations all fall below Cohen's small threshold.

> "Overall, the correlation between psychological well-being and the acceleration composite was r(1,495) = −.06, p = .02; the correlation between psychological well-being and age of high school graduation was r(1,495) = .03, p = .207."
