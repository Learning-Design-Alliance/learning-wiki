## Evidence

### Mahdaviazad H 2021

Mahdaviazad H, Keshtkar V, Emami MJ, Kargarshouroki Z, Vosoughi AR. (2021). Osteoporosis guideline awareness among Iranian nurses: results of a knowledge and attitudes survey. Journal of Preventive Medicine and Hygiene. [doi:10.15167/2421-4248/jpmh2021.62.2.1738](https://doi.org/10.15167/2421-4248/jpmh2021.62.2.1738)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of all orthopedic-ward nurses in hospitals affiliated to Shiraz University of Medical Sciences, August–December 2016, 143 respondents (89.3% response rate). The article reports "11.60 ± 3.10" knowledge and "3.47 ± 0.92" attitude, rated moderate on Bloom's scale; the authors conclude scores were "not satisfactory".

> "The overall mean (± SD) score of knowledge and attitude for all participants was 11.60 ± 3.10 (out of maximum possible score of 18) and 3.47 ± 0.92 (out of maximum possible score of 5), respectively."
