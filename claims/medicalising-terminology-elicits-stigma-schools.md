`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 'Perceptions and understanding of terminology' (codes 'Medicalising condition' and 'Stigma'). The article reports such terms might enforce an unhelpful sense of 'normal' and 'abnormal', and learners proposed reducing stigma by specifying each child's strengths and difficulties.

> "Learners from all three stakeholder groups highlighted that certain terminology such as the use of the word 'disorder' can medicalise the condition, and using such terms can be detrimental by eliciting stigma in certain occupational contexts such as in schools"
