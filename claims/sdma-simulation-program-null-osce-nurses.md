Lin Mei-Hsiang, Lin Shu-Chuan, Lee Yu-Hsia, Wang Pao-Yu, Wu Hon-Yen, Hsu Hsiu-Chin. (2021). The effectiveness of simulation education program on shared decision-making attitudes among nurses in Taiwan. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0257902

Quasi-experimental study of 100 nurses at a Taiwanese medical center, assigned 1:1 to the SDMA cultivation program or no intervention, assessed by OSCE two months later. The experimental group's "average SDMA score for the experimental group was 17.78 (SD = 3.58)" versus 17.62 in controls; p = .82, no effect size printed.

> "The average SDMA score for the experimental group was 17.78 (SD = 3.58), which was slightly higher than that of the control group (17.62 ± 3.51), although this difference was not statistically significant (p = .82; Table 2)."
