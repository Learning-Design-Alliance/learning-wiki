## Evidence

### Pla‐Canalda Elsa 2025

Pla‐Canalda Elsa, Jiménez‐Herrera María F., Fernández‐Sáez José, Concha‐Martínez Pablo, and Martínez‐Segura Estrella. (2025). Impact of Educational Intervention on Noninvasive Ventilation (NIV) in Nurses and Students. The Clinical Teacher 22, no. 5. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon comparison of COM-VA scores between the posttheoretical-practical and postsimulation stages in the same 66-participant study (Table 5). The postclass-to-postsimulation gain was "1.17%" (0.08 median points, p = 0.009), smaller than the 8.21% pre-to-post improvement.

> "The percentage of improvement between the postclass and postsimulation COM‐VA was 1.17%, while the percentage of improvement between the preintervention and postintervention COM‐VA was 8.21%"
