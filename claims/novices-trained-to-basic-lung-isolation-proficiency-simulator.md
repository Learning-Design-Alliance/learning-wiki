## Evidence

### Latif Rana K. 2016

Latif Rana K., VanHorne Edgar M., Kandadai Sunitha Kanchi, Bautista Alexander F., Neamtu Aurel, Wadhwa Anupama, Carter Mary B., Ziegler Craig H., Memon Mohammed Faisal, Akça Ozan. (2016). Teaching basic lung isolation skills on human anatomy simulator: attainment and retention of lung isolation skills. BMC Anesthesiology 16:7. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-015-0169-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled simulation study: 30 senior medical students trained to a benchmark proficiency level and tested against 5 experts on the HAAS. Post-training passing rates reached 28/30 (93%) for both devices, matching the abstract's report that "passing rates showed similar success rates after training (P >0.99)".

> "Experts’ and novices’ double lumen endobronchial tube and bronchial blocker passing rates showed similar success rates after training (P >0.99)."
