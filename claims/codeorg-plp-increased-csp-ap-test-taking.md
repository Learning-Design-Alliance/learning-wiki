## Evidence

### Richard S. Brown & Emily Anne Brown 2019

Richard S. Brown & Emily Anne Brown. (2019). Estimating the Effect of a Teacher Training Program on Advanced Placement® Outcomes. International Journal of Computer Science Education in Schools, 2(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1207479

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental ATT analysis using propensity score weighting (twang GBM) of 167 treatment schools matched to 167 comparison schools on state, enrollment, minority percentage, and free/reduced lunch. The article reports the increase was "almost 18 additional AP Computer Science tests taken" with t(332) = 6.72, p < .001; no standardized effect size appears in this quote.

> "On average, participation in the Code.org program generated an average increase of almost 18 additional AP Computer Science tests taken in the 2016-2017 school year; t(332) = 6.72, p < .001."
