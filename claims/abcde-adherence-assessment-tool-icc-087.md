## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The newly developed assessment instrument, incorporating all 24 ABCDE items from the Dutch APLS manual, achieved an intraclass correlation coefficient of 0.87 (95% CI 0.74–0.94) for intra-observer reliability. [→ Linders M 2021](#linders-m-2021)

## Evidence

### Linders M 2021

Linders M, Binkhorst M, Draaisma JMT, van Heijst AFJ, Hogeveen M. (2021). Adherence to the ABCDE approach in relation to the method of instruction: a randomized controlled simulation study. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-021-00509-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Psychometric testing of the study's assessment tool: ten previously videotaped scenarios were scored twice by the same observer two weeks apart. Face validity came from consensus among 6 experts; content validity from derivation of all items from the Dutch APLS manual.

> "The intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC) was 0.87 (95% CI 0.74–0.94), with an overall difference of 1.4% (p = 0.39), which signifies an almost perfect agreement."
