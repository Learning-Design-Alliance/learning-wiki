Ana Paula Melo, Anita Sundrani, Julia Turner & Ofer Malamud. (2024). How Digital Platforms Provide Individualized Learning Experiences in Mathematics Across School Settings, Grades 1-5. E4 Center. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2024/06/27/how-digital-platforms-provide-individualized-learning-experiences-in-mathematics/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Figure 3-based analysis of daily completed-lesson composition for students in grades 1-5, shown separately for Number and Operations and Algebra and Algebraic Thinking. The upward trend remained after controlling for students' fall scores and school score differences.

> "Students who began the fall semester below grade level initially completed little to zero grade-level content, a pattern reflected in N&O and A&AT domains. Over time, students increasingly completed more on-grade-level lessons as they neared the winter diagnostic exam, reaching about 20 to 30 percent of “on or above” grade-level lessons."
