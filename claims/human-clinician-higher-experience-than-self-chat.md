## Evidence

### Coristine A 2025

Coristine A, Li D, Foroutan DB, Ojo TF, Menon NP, Aditya I, Cook DA, Overgaard J, Pankratz VS, Del Fiol G, Aakre CA. (2025). Virtual Patients Using Large Language Models: Scalable, Contextualized Simulation of Clinician-Patient Dialogue With Feedback. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/68486

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Final-rating subgroup comparison (mixed models ANOVA, n=180 ratings) between 48 human-clinician conversations and 12 GPT-GPT self-chat conversations, matched on design features.

> "Conversations involving human clinicians had higher experience ratings than those with GPT as clinician (ie, differences ≥0.57) but similar authenticity (ie, differences ≤0.31) and—as would be expected—similar feedback ratings (ie, difference −0.05)."
