## Evidence

### Wu Zhiwu 2021

Wu Zhiwu, Li Mingming, Zhu Fangzhi, Lei Lei, Cheng Ran, Hu Tao. (2021). The effects of oral health education regarding periodontal health on non-dental undergraduates in southwestern China—exploring the feasibility of an e-learning course for oral health promotion. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-021-01476-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of 68 e-learning non-dental students with 134 traditional-course non-dental students after the courses; baselines were consistent (P > 0.05). The article reports higher e-learning scores on items 2, 3 and 4 (P < 0.05) — aetiology/risk factors, periodontal-systemic health, and outcomes of tooth in elderly individuals — and "no significant differences in other items".

> "After the course, the score of students taking the e-learning course was higher than that of students taking the traditional course for items 2, 3 and 4 (P < 0.05). There were no significant differences in other items."
