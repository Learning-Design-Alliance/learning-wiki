## Evidence

### Janaka 2025

Janaka, N., Zhao, S., Ram, A., Sun, R., Tan, S. T. J. W., Li, D., & Hsu, D. (2025). Progressive Sentences: Combining the Benefits of Word and Sentence Learning. MobileHCI '25 Adjunct. [doi:10.1145/3737821.3749564](https://doi.org/10.1145/3737821.3749564)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot 1 (N=5) compared five modality combinations during Walking. No statistically significant differences, but text+image+audio yielded the highest recall and text-only the lowest. All participants preferred the multimodal combination.

> "While there were no statistically significant differences, the text-only condition (i.e., NoImageNoAudio) had the lowest recall scores, whereas the text+image+audio combination (i.e.,ImageWithTextAudio) yielded the highest, aligning with theMultimedia Principle."
