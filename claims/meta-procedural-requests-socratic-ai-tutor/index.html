Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi and N. Sanjay Rebello 2026¶

Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). A bottom-up taxonomy of student discourse with a Socratic AI physics tutor. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07373 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.07373

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Observational frequency analysis of the 2,874-turn corpus from 221 sessions. The article reports "Next Step Guidance(4.4%, the second-largest category overall)" within a band of requests for the tutor to supply the next strategic move. No effect size is printed.

"A second large band consists of turns in which the student does not advance the problem themselves but instead asks the tutor what to do:Next Step Guidance(4.4%, the second-largest category overall)"
