## Evidence

### Horii Satoko 2021

Horii Satoko, Nguyen Chinh Thi Minh, Pham Huong Thi Thu, Amaike Naomi, Ho Hien Thi, Aiga Hirotsugu. (2021). Effectiveness of a standard clinical training program in new graduate nurses’ competencies in Vietnam: A quasi-experimental longitudinal study with a difference-in-differences design. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254238

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental longitudinal difference-in-differences study of 280 new graduate nurses (206 intervention, 74 control) across nine intervention and seven control hospitals in Vietnam, using a web-based self-administered competency questionnaire at baseline and follow-up. The intervention group's mean increase was "greater than that of the control group" with d = 0.53 (95% CI 0.26 to 0.80).

> "Mean increase in total competency scores of the intervention group (1.61, SD 1.42) was greater than that of the control group (0.88, SD 1.17). Cohen’s d was 0.53 (95% CI 0.26 to 0.80), indicating an intermediate effect (Table 4)."
