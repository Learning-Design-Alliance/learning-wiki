`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics (Table 3, n=510) from the online survey on 5-point Likert scales; means are descriptive with no inferential test reported. Students "the least used the interest enhancement strategy of MRSs".

> "students showed the highest score in the goal orientation strategy (mean±SD, 3.59±0.65), followed by the behavioral reinforcement strategy (3.55±0.68) and environmental control (3.40±0.73). Students the least used the interest enhancement strategy of MRSs to regulate their learning motivation (3.23±0.80)."
