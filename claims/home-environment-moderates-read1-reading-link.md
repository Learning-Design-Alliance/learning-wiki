`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation regression analyses (Table 3) in the GRaD sample; the article also reports significant SES × RU2-Short terms for word reading accuracy (b = −3.52, p = 0.02) and reading comprehension (b = −1.05, p = 0.01).

> "Cross-product terms between parental education and word reading accuracy (b = 0.92, p < 0.01), parental education and word reading ﬂuency (b = 1.00, p < 0.01), and between parental education and reading comprehension (b = 0.22, p = 0.01) were all signiﬁcant."
