# The formative MEQ* score is a significant predictor of summative MEQ-1 performance, explaining 40.5% of variance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Scores on the formative MEQ* correlate with summative MEQ-1 scores in both cohorts and alone explain 40.5% of MEQ-1 variance in regression. [→ Büssing Oliver 2021](#bussing-oliver-2021)
`q3 i?` Demographic and admission variables (age, high school graduation grade, vocational training, pre-study, self-efficacy) were not significant predictors of MEQ-1 total score. [→ Büssing Oliver 2021](#bussing-oliver-2021)

## Evidence

### Büssing Oliver 2021

Büssing Oliver, Ehlers Jan P., Zupanic Michaela. (2021). The prognostic validity of the formative for the summative MEQ (Modified Essay Questions). GMS Journal for Medical Education 38(6), Doc99. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001495.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis on 88 medical students from two cohorts at UW/H. The formative MEQ* score alone resolved "40.5% of the variance (β=1.58, T=7.61, p=.000)"; the other admission variables were not significant predictors of the MEQ-1 total score.

> "In contrast, the score from the formative MEQ* alone was able to resolve 40.5% of the variance (β=1.58, T=7.61, p=.000), and 44.4% in the joint model with the aforementioned variables"
