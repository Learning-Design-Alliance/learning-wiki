### Hayo Reinders and Sorada Wattana 2015

Hayo Reinders and Sorada Wattana. (2015). Affect and willingness to communicate in digital game-based learning. ReCALL, Volume 27, Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0958344014000226

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of five students in a fifteen-week game-based learning program at a university in Thailand, based on six interviews per student. The authors report gameplay benefits "in terms of lowering their affective barriers to learning". No effect size is reported.

> "The results showed that gameplay had a number of benefits for the participants in this study, in particular in terms of lowering their affective barriers to learning and increasing their willingness to communicate."
