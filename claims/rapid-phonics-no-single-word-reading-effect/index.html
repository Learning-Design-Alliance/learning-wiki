Evidence¶

King 2015¶

King, B., & Kasim, A. (2015). Rapid Phonics Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581293

q3 · i? · causal · r2

SWRT was administered four times (April 2013, July 2013, September 2013, December 2013) to monitor summer fall-back. Intervention means rose from 82.51 in July to 85.48 in September and control from 81.67 to 86.22; these did not constitute significant differences.

"The SWRT did not detect any impact of the intervention, either in intention to treat , or in those who actually received the intervention compared to those who did not, neither did it find any decline in word reading scores after the summer break."
