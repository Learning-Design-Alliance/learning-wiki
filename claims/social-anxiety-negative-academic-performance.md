## Evidence

### Mou Qiaoxing 2024

Mou Qiaoxing, Zhuang Jie, Wu Qunhong, Zhong Yaqin, Dai Qianqian, Cao Xin, Gao Yuexia, Lu Qingyun, Zhao Miaomiao. (2024). Social media addiction and academic engagement as serial mediators between social anxiety and academic performance among college students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01635-7

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Regression-based analysis of the cross-sectional survey of 2,661 college students at Nantong University, controlling for sex, age, and academic major, found "social anxiety was significantly negatively associated with academic performance (β = -0.097, p < 0.001)". The serial mediation model explained 8.9% of variance in GPA (F = 43.240, p < 0.001).

> "Results revealed that social anxiety was significantly negatively associated with academic performance (β = -0.097, p < 0.001), indicating that for per one standard deviation (SD) increase in social anxiety, academic performance would decrease by 0.097 SD."
