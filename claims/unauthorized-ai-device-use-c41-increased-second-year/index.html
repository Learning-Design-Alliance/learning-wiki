Ruishi Chen 2026¶

Ruishi Chen, Victor R. Lee, Annie Camey Kuo, Denise Clark Pope, Sarah Miles. (2026). Cheating in the second year of generative AI chatbots: a follow-up study on high school student cheating behaviors. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10587-1

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive survey analysis of item C41 in the 2023-24 six-school survey. The article reports the 24.27% figure and, comparing with Lee et al. (2024) rates of 6.44% and 15.20%, states "it is worth noting that this number for the new year is much higher." No inferential test of change was applied.

"For item C41, 24.27% of students reported using artificial intelligence or digital devices (e.g., ChatGPT, smartphones) as unauthorized aids during assessments, assignments, or homework."
