Overcoming the Research-to-Practice Gap: A Randomized Trial With Two Brief Homework and Organization Interventions for Students With ADHD as Implemented by School Mental Health Providers. Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology. Advance online publication. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/ccp0000265

The article's summary of the planned post hoc contrasts between the two active interventions in the randomized three-group trial; Table 2 reports HOPS-CHIEF Cohen's d of -0.68 for parent organized actions and -0.43 for teacher organized actions, significant after FDR correction.
