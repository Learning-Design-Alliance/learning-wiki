## Evidence

### Egenberg Signe 2017

Egenberg Signe, Karlsen Bjørg, Massay Deodatus, Kimaro Happiness, Bru Lars Edvin. (2017). “No patient should die of PPH just for the lack of training!” Experiences from multi-professional simulation training on postpartum hemorrhage in northern Tanzania: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0957-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group finding from the regional referral hospital: staff adopted their own ward morning report, saying "We did pick this up from the training." The authors state debriefing "seems to have enhanced change of mind set, enabling the informants to transfer learning to clinical practice."

> "After the training where staff got accustomed to debriefing sessions, the management of the maternity ward at the regional referral hospital prioritized the departmental morning reports to enhance reflective practice related to clinical management."
