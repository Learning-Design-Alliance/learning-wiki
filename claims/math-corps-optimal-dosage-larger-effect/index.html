Math Corps Program Description 2024¶

Math Corps Program Description. (2024). https://www.evidenceforessa.org/program/math-corps

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Subgroup analysis within the first randomized study (489 students): students receiving the optimal tutoring dosage showed "effect size = +0.24" on STAR Math, described as more robust than the overall effect.

"Among those who received the optimal dosage, results were even more robust (effect size = +0.24)."
