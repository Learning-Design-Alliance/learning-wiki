## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A significant video type × experience interaction emerged (F(4200) = 2.43, p = 0.049); novices rated the mixed video higher than experienced raters (p = 0.018) but did not differ significantly from intermediate raters (p = 0.089), and intermediate and experienced raters did not differ (p = 0.805).
