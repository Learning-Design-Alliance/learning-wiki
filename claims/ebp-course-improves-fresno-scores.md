`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear Mixed Model for the intervention group (n = 18) on the Fresno Test, a performance-based assessment of broader theoretical EBP knowledge; Table 3 reports a 24.2% difference (F = 35.7, p < 0.001), with the intervention accounting for "48.1% of the total variance".

> "The Fresno Test model exhibited excellent fit (conditional R 2 = 0.631), with the intervention accounting for 48.1% of the total variance (marginal R 2 = 0.481) and the random effects contributing an additional 15%, totaling 63.1% of the explained variance."
