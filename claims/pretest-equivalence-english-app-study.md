`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest comparison in the same pretest-posttest experiment, tested with a t test two-sample assuming unequal variances; the article reports "no significant difference in the mean scores" between groups, supporting baseline equivalence. Equivalence was not formally tested beyond this t test.

> "There was no significant difference in the mean scores between the control group (M = 24.19, SD = 5.07, N = 21) and the experimental group (M = 26.21, SD = 4.38, N = 24), t(40) = -1.42, p = 0.164."
