## Evidence

### Lee R. 2026

Lee R., Dhami N., Gibson W., Hamza D. M., Oswald A. E., Moffat M. (2026). Assessing the Quality of Narrative Feedback in Entrustable Professional Activities Using the EFeCT Feedback Scoring Tool. The Clinical Teacher 23(1): e70295. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70295

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis in the Methods: three evaluators independently scored an initial 22-EPA sample (ICC 0.67, below the 0.70 cut-off), then after consensus calibration a 24-EPA sample showed ICC 0.96. The final average ICC across all 748 EPAs was 0.66, indicating moderate inter-rater reliability.

> "This demonstrated an average measure of 0.96 with a lower bound confidence interval of 0.93 and an upper bound confidence interval of 0.98. These results indicated excellent inter‐rater reliability [23]."
