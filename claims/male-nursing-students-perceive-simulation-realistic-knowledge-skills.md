### Maria Dias Jacqueline 2025

Maria Dias Jacqueline, Sara Abraham Mini, Arsyad Subu Muhammad, Al Yateem Nabeel, Reefat Ahmed Fatma, Abdourahman Abdillahi Raliya, Jamal Saleh Noof, Mohamed Ali Samira, Abshir Hashi Kowsar, Mottershead Richard. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive qualitative study using semi-structured individual interviews and two online focus groups with male baccalaureate nursing students who completed the maternity practicum at the University of Sharjah. In thematic analysis of Theme 1, students reported practicing "in the simulation much like in actual clinical settings" and that simulation "improved my knowledge and skills" for equipment and neonatal care procedures.

> "Before the simulation, I had no idea how to use resuscitation equipment or score the baby prior to the physical activity. After arriving here for the simulation, I learned how to operate this equipment. It improved my knowledge and skills"
