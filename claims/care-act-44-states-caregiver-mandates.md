> "Currently, 44 U.S. states have enacted the Caregiver Advise Record and Enable (CARE) Act, which mandates hospitals: (1) record the designated family caregiver's name in the patient's medical record during a hospital stay; (2) notify the caregiver of the patient's discharge; and (3) provide education and instruction on necessary post-discharge care."
