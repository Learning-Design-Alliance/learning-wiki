Cross-sectional survey of 976 healthcare professionals in eight Amhara referral hospitals; intention measured by three five-point Likert items with a median-split classification. The study found "506 (51.8%) of health professionals" intended to use eLearning-based CPD; no effect size is printed for this prevalence.
