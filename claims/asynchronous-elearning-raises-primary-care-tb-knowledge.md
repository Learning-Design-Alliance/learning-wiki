# Asynchronous E-learning improved TB knowledge among primary care workers but not clinicians or public health physicians

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Primary care workers who participated in asynchronous E-learning scored 10.9 points higher than non-participants, gaining 1.8 points per module. [→ Wang Zi-Yue 2021](#wang-zi-yue-2021)
`q2 i?` Obtaining the asynchronous training certificate (all modules finished) was associated with an 11.2-point higher average score among primary care workers. [→ Wang Zi-Yue 2021 (2)](#wang-zi-yue-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Wang Zi-Yue 2021

Wang Zi-Yue, Zhang Li-Jie, Liu Yu-Hong, Jiang Wei-Xi, Jia Jing-Yun, Tang Sheng-Lan, Liu Xiao-Yun. (2021). The effectiveness of E-learning in continuing medical education for tuberculosis health workers: a quasi-experiment from China. Infectious Diseases of Poverty. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40249-021-00855-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple linear regression in the China-Gates TB quasi-experiment on primary care worker survey data, controlling for fixed effects and individual characteristics; the trained group had "a higher average score of 10.9 points (P < 0.001)". Asynchronous learning had no significant impact on clinical or public health physicians.

> "Compared with those who did not participate, trained primary care workers had a higher average score of 10.9 points (P < 0.001). For each module they attended, their knowledge score could increase 1.8 points on average (P < 0.001)."

### Wang Zi-Yue 2021 (2)

Wang Zi-Yue, Zhang Li-Jie, Liu Yu-Hong, Jiang Wei-Xi, Jia Jing-Yun, Tang Sheng-Lan, Liu Xiao-Yun. (2021). The effectiveness of E-learning in continuing medical education for tuberculosis health workers: a quasi-experiment from China. Infectious Diseases of Poverty. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40249-021-00855-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same regression model in the quasi-experiment, dose-response specification: the printed coefficient for certificate completion among primary care workers was 11.24***, described as participants' average score increasing "by 11.2 points (P < 0.001)".

> "after they obtain a certificate (indicates that all modules are finished), the average score of the participants could increase by 11.2 points (P < 0.001)."
