q2 · i? · design · r3

Observational field records from later-cycle group discussions in the eight-week intervention. The record (OBS-G7) shows that "participation became more evenly distributed across group members" as students rotated roles, with justification drawing on both AI outputs and psychological theories.

"As students rotated roles, participation became more evenly distributed across group members. Participants increasingly asked clarifying questions, challenged one another's interpretations, and justified their recommendations using both AI-generated information and psychological theories"

q2 · i? · design · r2

Reflective journal excerpt (RJ-P23) from the intervention, illustrating participant difficulty with the reflective and evaluative roles. The article adds that the Reflective Researcher role was most frequently described as challenging, while the Case Constructor role was generally perceived as more accessible.

"I felt comfortable analyzing information, but it was much harder for me to evaluate other people's decisions critically during the reflective tasks"
