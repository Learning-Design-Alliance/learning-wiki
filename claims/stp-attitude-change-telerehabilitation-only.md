## Evidence

### Temehy Basema 2025

Temehy Basema, Soundy Andrew, Rosewilliam Sheeba. (2025). Evaluating an online stroke training programme for Saudi Arabian physiotherapists for improving their knowledge and confidence in long-term stroke care: a pilot mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06837-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-Square comparison of pre-post attitude ratings in the 26-participant quantitative phase. "attitudes towards telerehabilitation showed a significant change" (Chi-Square 15.02, p = 0.02), while education, outcome measures and goal-setting ratings did not reach significance.

> "In contrast, attitudes towards telerehabilitation showed a significant change, with those considering it 'extremely important' increasing from 15 participants (57.7%) pre-training to 18 (69.2%) post-training (p = 0.02)."

### Temehy Basema 2025 (2)

Temehy Basema, Soundy Andrew, Rosewilliam Sheeba. (2025). Evaluating an online stroke training programme for Saudi Arabian physiotherapists for improving their knowledge and confidence in long-term stroke care: a pilot mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06837-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same quantitative phase. Ratings for importance of education, outcome measures and goal-setting all increased descriptively (e.g. goal-setting 'extremely important' from 53.8% to 84.6%) but the article reports these changes were not statistically significant.

> "Following STP training participants showed improvement in attitudes, however, these changes in attitude scores for education, outcome measures, and goal-setting were not statistically significant."
