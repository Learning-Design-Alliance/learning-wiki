Evidence¶

Karaduman 2019¶

Batdal Karaduman, G. (2019). The Effects of Conceptual Change Texts Enriched with Metaconceptual Activities on the symmetry of Classroom Teacher Candidates. Acta Didactica Napocensia, 12(1), 107-116. https://doi.org/10.24193/adn.12.1.7

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Pretest-posttest experiment with 60 third-grade teacher candidates at Istanbul University split into two groups of 30; the post-test comparison used an independent groups t-test (t = -2.649, df = 58, p = .010), with the experimental group mean 22.1000 versus 19.7333 for the control group. The finding shows "the difference between the arithmetic averages of the groups was statistically significant".

"When Table 2 was analyzed, the difference between the arithmetic averages of the groups was statistically significant. The experimental group, which reads the texts of conceptual change enriched with metaconceptual activities, is more effective in conceptual understanding when compared to the control group who reads plain texts."
