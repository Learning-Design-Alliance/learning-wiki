## Evidence

### Schewior 2024

Schewior, L., & Lindner, M. A. (2024). Revisiting Picture Functions in Multimedia Testing: A Systematic Narrative Review and Taxonomy Extension. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09883-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of twenty-one articles with text-only controls examining representational pictures. Fourteen reported higher accuracy, six found no effects, and one reported moderator-dependent effects, providing "clear evidence for a multimedia effect in testing."
