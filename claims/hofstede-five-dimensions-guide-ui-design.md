Narrative review section on HCI. The review reports that designers use 'models of culture' from intercultural communication as guidelines, on the argument that communicative interactions between groups mimic those between technology and user.

> "Hofstede’s (1980, 1991) cultural model of ﬁve dimensions (power distance, uncertainty avoidance, masculinity–femininity , individualism–collectivism and time orientation) has frequently been mapped as a guide to user interface design."
