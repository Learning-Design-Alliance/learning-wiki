## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Gemini's odds of a correct answer were significantly higher in English than Polish (OR = 1.85, small effect size), while ChatGPT-4 and Claude showed no significant language differences. [→ Wójcik 2025](#wojcik-2025)

## Evidence

### Wójcik 2025

Wójcik, D., Adamiak, O., Czerepak, G., Tokarczuk, O. & Szalewski, L. (2025). Comparing the performance of ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude in English and Polish on medical examinations. Scientific Reports 15, 33083. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17030-0

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Within-chatbot language contrasts (Table 9): Gemini English/Polish OR = 1.85, p < 0.001, which the article describes as a small effect size; ChatGPT-4 OR = 0.95 (p = 0.746) and Claude OR = 1.20 (p = 0.278) showed no significant differences between languages.

> "The odds ratio was 1.85, indicating that the odds of Gemini providing a correct answer were 84.5% higher in English than in Polish. This difference was statistically significant, and corresponded to a small effect size."
