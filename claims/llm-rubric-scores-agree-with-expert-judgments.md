## Evidence

### Yong Oh Lee 2026

Yong Oh Lee, Byeonghun Bang, Sejun Oh. (2026). LLM-Driven Rubric-Based Assessment of Algebraic Competence in Multi-Stage Block Coding Tasks with Design and Field Evaluation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.3390/app162110595](https://doi.org/10.3390/app162110595)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Field study with 42 middle school students comparing LLM-derived rubric scores with expert-assigned ratings across five rubric dimensions. The study reports "r=0.79, ρ=0.76, both p<0.001", average bias M=5.1 points, MAE = 12.4, RMSE = 15.8, and Bland–Altman limits of agreement without extreme outliers.

> "Both Pearson and Spearman correlations confirmed strong positive associations between the two evaluation methods (r=0.79, ρ=0.76, both p<0.001), underscoring convergent validity."
