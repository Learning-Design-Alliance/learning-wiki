## Evidence

### Färber Christoph Maria 2022

Färber Christoph Maria, Lemos Martin, Said Yekta-Michael Sareh. (2022). Effect of an endodontic e-learning application on students’ performance during their first root canal treatment on real patients: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03463-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the two test groups of the same quasi-experimental cohort: test group 2 attended lectures and seminars online via Zoom during COVID-19, with only patient treatments in person. The article reports "no significant difference in treatment success in real patients" between the groups.

> "There was no significant difference in treatment success in real patients between test group 1 (who experienced face-to-face teaching with access to the e-learning application) and test group 2 (who experienced exclusively digital teaching; Fig. 3)."
