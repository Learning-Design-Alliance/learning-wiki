Chapman 1921¶

Chapman, J. Crosby and Dale, A. Barbara. (1921). A Further Criterion for the Selection of Mental Test Elements. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org/search?query=A+Further+Criterion+for+the+Selection+of+Mental+Test+Elements

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Matched-groups comparison of 50 pairs of children with identical National Intelligence Test totals (Old Dull average age 14 years 7 months; Young Bright 9 years 3 months). The article reports "a very large ratio in favor of the Young Bright group" on Test 4; Table I prints a Y.B./O.D. ratio of 1.51 for Test IV.

"Table I shows that in Test 4 (opposites), there is a very large ratio in favor of the Young Bright group."
