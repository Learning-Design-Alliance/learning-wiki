# A gender gap of ~9 points appears even on a low-stakes diagnostic assessment, contradicting prior findings that the gap disappears in low-stakes assessments

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The gender gap was present on a no-consequence diagnostic assessment (~9 points; 0.45 SD), partially refuting the hypothesis that gaps vanish in low-stakes assessments. [→ Ilana Ram 2025](#ilana-ram-2025)

## Evidence

### Ilana Ram 2025

Ilana Ram, Adam Shwartz, Ido Roll. (2025). Minding the gender gap in advanced STEM courses: effects of student preparedness and activity level. Instructional Science (2025) 53:1457–1478. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09748-1

`q3 · i?`

A 15-item multiple-choice diagnostic assessment (Cronbach's alpha = 0.724) with no impact on grades was administered at the start of Course IV to all 315 participants. The study reports "A gap was also present on the low-stakes diagnostic assessment" of about 9 points; the article prints no standardized effect size statistic for this contrast, so impact is null.
