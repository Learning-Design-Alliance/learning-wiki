## Evidence

### Andersen Mikkel Porsborg 2017

Andersen Mikkel Porsborg, Starkof Liis, Sessa Maurizio, Mortensen Rikke Nørmark, Vardinghus-Nielsen Henrik, Bøggild Henrik, Lange Theis, Torp-Pedersen Christian. (2017). The indirect and direct pathways between physical fitness and academic achievement on commencement in post-compulsory education in a historical cohort of Danish school youth. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-017-4712-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate analyses of the 1084-pupil cohort comparing girls (n = 530) and boys (n = 554) on fitness and achievement. The article reports the opposite directions of the two sex differences, consistent with prior findings it cites.

> "Girls physical fitness was on average 6.56 mL·kg−1·min−1 (95% CI: -7.39; −5.74) lower than for boys. The opposite relation was observed for academic achievement where girls scored on average 0.79 (95% CI: 0.53; 1.05) grades higher than boys."
