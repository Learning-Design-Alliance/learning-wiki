## Evidence

### Solís 2017

Solís, M., Scammacca, N., Barth, A. E., & Roberts, G. J. (2017). Text-based Vocabulary Intervention Training Study: Supporting Fourth Graders with Low Reading Comprehension and Learning Disabilities. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 15(1), 103-115. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1141988

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 44 fourth graders (treatment n=25, comparison n=19) in two rural schools; RM-ANOVA on the curriculum-based measure showed the treatment group outperforming the comparison group, F(1,42)=9.27, p=.004, "partial η2 = 0.18".

> "RM-ANOV A results calculated on total scores for the curriculum-based measure with items 15 and 16 dropped indicated that the improvement in scores from pre-test to post-test differed significantly between groups, with the treatment group outperforming the comparison group. F(1,42)=9.27, p=.004, partial η2 = 0.18."

### Solís 2017 (2)

Solís, M., Scammacca, N., Barth, A. E., & Roberts, G. J. (2017). Text-based Vocabulary Intervention Training Study: Supporting Fourth Graders with Low Reading Comprehension and Learning Disabilities. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 15(1), 103-115. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1141988

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Re-analysis of the same randomized experiment after regression imputation for 17 students missing items 15 and 16 due to a photocopying error; the difference favored the treatment group, F(1,42)=13.50, p=.001, "partial η2 = 0.24".

> "The results based on total scores with missing data imputed also resulted in a statistically significant difference between groups in improvement in scores from pre-test to post-test that favored the treatment group, F(1, 42)=13.50, p=.001, partial η2 = 0.24, with a slightly larger effect size."
