## Evidence

### Estrella Michelle M 2012

Estrella Michelle M, Sisson Stephen D, Roth Jennifer, Choi Michael J. (2012). Efficacy of an internet-based tool for improving physician knowledge of chronic kidney disease: an observational study. BMC Nephrology. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2369-13-126

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective analysis of CKD training module data from U.S. internal medicine residents (4,702 residents, 2005–2009). The article reports "the mean pre-test scores ranging from 45.1%" to 57.0%, with SDs of 18.2% and 20.2%, characterizing baseline performance as poor.

> "Overall, the annual baseline performance on the CKD module was poor, with the mean pre-test scores ranging from 45.1% (standard deviation [SD]: 18.2%) to 57.0% (SD: 20.2%) from 2005 to 2009."
