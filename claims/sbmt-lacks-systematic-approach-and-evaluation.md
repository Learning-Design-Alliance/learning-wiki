## Evidence

### Stocker M 2017

Stocker M, Laine K, Ulmer F. (2017). Use of simulation-based medical training in Swiss pediatric hospitals: a national survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0940-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results across 24 units assessing systematic approach per the Kern framework. Research activities accompanied SBMT in only 4 of 24 units (16.6%); 8 of 24 enabled structured participant evaluation.

> "The vast majority conducted training that was neither embedded within a larger educational curriculum (19 out of 24: 79.2%) nor evaluated (16 out of 24: 66.6%) by its participants."
