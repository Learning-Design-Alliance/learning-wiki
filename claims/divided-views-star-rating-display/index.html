Evidence¶

Raigul Zheldibayeva 2025¶

Raigul Zheldibayeva, Ana Karina de Oliveira Nascimento, Vania Castro, Bill Cope and Mary Kalantzis. (2025). Generative AI Feedback, English Writing and Teacher Rubrics: A Multiple-Case Study of CyberScholar. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17055

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Teacher 03 (School C) post-survey in the case study; students viewed stars "as feedback" rather than grades, which the teacher reported encouraged revision without grade anxiety.

"The students were encouraged when they made revisions and saw an improvement in their score. They trusted the results and didn't see it as a grade but rather feedback."

Raigul Zheldibayeva 2025 (2)¶

Raigul Zheldibayeva, Ana Karina de Oliveira Nascimento, Vania Castro, Bill Cope and Mary Kalantzis. (2025). Generative AI Feedback, English Writing and Teacher Rubrics: A Multiple-Case Study of CyberScholar. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17055

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Teacher 02 (School B) post-survey; he called the 4-star rating "basic," suggested replacing stars with a 0-10 point or percentage scale, and worried high performers might skip reading criterion feedback.

"My students are already too distracted by the stars and not paying close enough attention to the feedback that the assistant gives."
