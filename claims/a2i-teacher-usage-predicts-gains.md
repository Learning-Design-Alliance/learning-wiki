## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among immediate-treatment teachers, more A2i use over the school year was associated with greater student word reading skill gains, about a one-month increase per 100 extra minutes. [→ Connor 2022](#connor-2022)

## Evidence

### Connor 2022

Connor, Carol McDonald; May, Henry; Sparapani, Nicole; Hwang, Jin Kyoung; Adams, Ashley; Wood, Taffeta S.; Siegal, Sarah; Wolfe, Cassidy; Day, Stephanie. (2022). Bringing Assessment-to-Instruction (A2i) Technology to Scale: Exploring the Process from Development to Implementation. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000758

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of A2i-generated teacher user-logs for immediate-treatment teachers only, predicting spring L2M scores controlling for fall L2M scores; the association was descriptive of logged usage and no standardized effect size was printed.

> "For every 100 extra minutes teachers spent using A2i, their students’ scores generally increased by 0.1 GEs or about a one-month increase."
