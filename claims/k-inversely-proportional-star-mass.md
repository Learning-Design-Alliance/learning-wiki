### R. Montecinos 2025

R. Montecinos, C. Hernández, I. Fuentes-Morales, F. Alarcón, I. Benito, L. Laroze & S. Pérez. (2025). Analyzing new planetary systems at school: Applications of Newton's Universal Gravitation and Kepler's Third Law. The Physics Teacher (AAPT). https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0192450

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Numerical result of the teaching-sequence activity in which eight extrasolar systems' stellar masses were derived from planet b data using Eqs. (1) and (4). TRAPPIST-1 and GAT 1370 stars have "masses around 0.09 solar masses", resolving the earlier open question about their high k values.

> "Table III shows that these stars have masses around 0.09 solar masses. Therefore, students can conclude that k is inversely proportional to the star's mass in the system. Higher k values are expected in systems with lower stellar masses, k values around 1 are expected for Sun-like stars, and lower k values are expected for systems with higher stellar masses."
