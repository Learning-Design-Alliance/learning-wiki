## Evidence

### Lei Bao 2013

Lei Bao, Yeounsoo Kim, Amy Raplinger, Jing Han, Kathleen Koenig. (2013). Affective Factors in STEM Learning and Scientific Inquiry: Assessment of Cognitive Conflict and Anxiety. Special Issue of Research on Education Assessment and Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1403.0599](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1403.0599)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Timing measured during the 13 response-validity interviews, which were students' first exposure to iCARE. The article reports that with about 120 students in three classes, time "dropped significantly to the level of 3 to 5 minutes for subsequent uses."

> "We found that the average time was 8 minutes and 53 seconds with a standard deviation of 2 minutes and 46 seconds."
