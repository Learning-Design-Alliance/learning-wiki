## Evidence

### Makkink Andrew William & Dreyer Devin John 2021

Makkink Andrew William & Dreyer Devin John. (2021). Simulation debriefing: a perspective from emergency medical care students at three South African Higher Education Institutions. The Pan African Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.11604/pamj.2021.38.97.23009

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire survey of 153 students at three South African HEIs; frequency analysis. Table 2 shows 86 (56.2%) agreed and 26 (17.0%) strongly agreed on comparing performance with predetermined objectives; 90 (58.8%) agreed the facilitator explains the simulation purpose.

> "Participants indicated that debriefing generally addressed learning objectives in a manner that allowed them to compare their performance with the required objectives or outcomes."
