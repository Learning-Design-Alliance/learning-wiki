Evidence¶

Denervaud 2020¶

Denervaud, S., Fornari, E., Yang, X.-F., Hagmann, P., Immordino-Yang, M. H., & Sander, D. (2020). An fMRI study of error monitoring in Montessori and traditionally-schooled children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0069-6

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Main effect of pedagogy in the second-level ANOVA of fMRI activation: Montessori students showed "increased activation of the left occipital cortex and right cuneus", plus right superior parietal lobule and medial prefrontal cortex, some extending into left ACC.

"regardless of whether the response was correct or incorrect, relative to the traditionally-schooled students, the Montessori students showed increased activation of the left occipital cortex and right cuneus (Fig. 4 top panel, and Table 2B), and contralateral regions at trend-level; the right superior parietal lobule; and the medial prefrontal cortex"
