### Hardon S. F. 2019

Hardon S. F., van Kasteren R. J., Dankelman J., Bonjer H. J., Tuynman J. B., Horeman T. (2019). The value of force and torque measurements in transanal total mesorectal excision (TaTME). Techniques in Coloproctology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10151-019-02057-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation from the pre- and post-course trials of the seven-novice construct-validity study on the instrumented TaTME box trainer. The article reports that "all participants exceeded the 3 N safety threshold for tissue manipulation force" at both assessments. No effect size or test statistic is printed for this observation.

> "It was observed that all participants exceeded the 3 N safety threshold for tissue manipulation force during placement of the transanal purse-string suture at the pre- and post-course assessment."
