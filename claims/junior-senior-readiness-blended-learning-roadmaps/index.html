Andreeva et al. 2018¶

Nadezhda M. Andreeva, Ivan P. Artyukhov, Elena G. Myagkova, Nikolay I. Pak, Zhamilya K. Akkasynova. (2018). Organizing Blended Learning for Students on the Basis of Learning Roadmaps. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 9(2), 47-64. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181973

q1 · i? · design · r2

This is the authors' concluding generalisation from their experience of applying learning roadmaps with students of different universities (SFU, KrasSMU, KSPU, KazNPU); it is stated as an experience-based conclusion, not a tested comparison, and no effect size or test statistic is printed for it.

"Junior students have almost no readiness for self-educational academic activity in conditions of blended learning;"

Andreeva et al. 2018 (2)¶

Nadezhda M. Andreeva, Ivan P. Artyukhov, Elena G. Myagkova, Nikolay I. Pak, Zhamilya K. Akkasynova. (2018). Organizing Blended Learning for Students on the Basis of Learning Roadmaps. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 9(2), 47-64. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181973

q1 · i? · causal · r1

A second experience-based conclusion from the same multi-university application: senior students in the history of computer science course were allowed to select the form of classes convenient for them, and the authors conclude they embraced blended learning principles while preferring traditional forms. No measure or statistic is printed.

"Senior students are eager to embrace the principles of blended learning, yet they nevertheless prefer the traditional forms and methods of learning;"
