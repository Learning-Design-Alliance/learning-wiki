### Gehlbach 2018

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article describes, citing Rosenthal and Jacobson (1968), teachers told that a predictive test identified students expected to make large leaps (in reality randomly assigned); teachers then behaved in ways that confirmed the planted pet theories. The article presents this as prior work illustrating confirmation bias.

> "Consequently, these students ended up performing significantly better than their peers for whom no suggestion of substantial academic growth had been seeded by the experimenters."
