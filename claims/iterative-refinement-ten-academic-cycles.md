Curricular integration of a 4-year simulation-based education program on clinical reasoning for physiotherapy students: a descriptive design and implementation report. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09289-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive report of iterative refinement using SQUIRE-EDU guidelines. The article documents adaptations across ten academic cycles including simplified case scripts, standardized debriefing using structured models (PEARL and PLUS/DELTA), telehealth integration, and scenario replacement with collaborative MOSAICO model formats.

> "Over the cycles, the SBEP was progressively refined based on faculty, simulated participants' and students feedback, focusing on scenario fidelity, feasibility, and alignment with intended CR sub-processes (subjective interview, examination, and treatment)"
