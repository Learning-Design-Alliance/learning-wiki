## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Baker et al. 2004

Ryan Shaun Baker, Albert T. Corbett, Kenneth R. Koedinger, Angela Z. Wagner. (2004). Off-Task Behavior in the Cognitive Tutor Classroom: When Students "Game the System". CHI 2004, Vienna, Austria. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00190176v1

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

In the observational study, combined off-task frequency correlated with post-test at r = -0.33, yet the authors report "substantial difference between different types of off-task behavior", with gaming the system the only significant individual predictor.

> "How frequently a student was off-task (with all types of off-task behavior combined) was a reasonably good predictor of their performance on the post-test, F(1,68)=8.52, p<0.01, r= -0.33."
