## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors' intended learning lessons include that error probabilities (type I, type II, and power) can be easily evaluated by replicating the study many times under similar conditions using a simulation, and that students should appreciate the distinction between analysis of a single study and of a collection of estimates from its replication.
