### Patricia A. Young 2021

Patricia A. Young. (2021). The ever evolving MOOC. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09959-6

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editorial attributes this enumerated list of six learner motivations to Loizzo and Ertmer's (2016) study of MOOC learning culture, reporting it as the article's account of why adult learners enroll. It is a second-hand summary, not a new analysis.

> "Adult learners take these courses because they are (1) interested in the topic; (2) seek a specific type of knowledge attainment; (3) find it a place to critique the quality of learning as they are consumers of information; (4) find lurking to be learning; (5) revel in their ability as self-sufficient learners or (6) understand peer review as an undesired thorn of MOOCs."
