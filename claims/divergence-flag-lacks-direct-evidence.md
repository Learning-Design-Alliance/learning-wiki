van der Maas, H. L. J., & Molenaar, P. C. M. (1992). Stagewise Cognitive Development: An Application of Catastrophe Theory. Psychological Review, 99(3), 395-417. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.99.3.395

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The authors' own assessment of the state of evidence for the divergence flag: unlike bimodality and spurts, divergence has not been tested as a transition criterion, though they link it to K. W. Fischer et al.'s (1984) optimal test conditions via the splitting variable.

> "It appears that divergence has not been used as a criterion for stage transitions in cognitive devel- opmental psychology, hence no direct experimental evidence can be given."
