# Reaction times are faster during threat than safe conditions only on the high-load (3-back) task, in both adolescents and adults

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` RT was faster in the threat than safe condition for the 3-back task, with no condition differences at 1-back or 2-back. [→ Patel Nilam 2016](#patel-nilam-2016)

## Evidence

### Patel Nilam 2016

Patel Nilam, Vytal Katherine, Pavletic Nevia, Stoodley Catherine, Pine Daniel S., Grillon Christian, Ernst Monique. (2016). Interaction of Threat and Verbal Working Memory in Adolescents. Psychophysiology. https://doi.org/10.1111/psyp.12582

`q3 · i?`

Follow-up tests of the significant Condition by Load interaction on RT (F(2,96) = 13.88, p < .001, ηp2 = .22) in the rANOVA on correct-response RT. The study found "RT was faster in the threat vs. safe condition for the 3-back task (p < .001)" with no differences at lower loads.

> "Findings showed that RT was faster in the threat vs. safe condition for the 3-back task (p < .001), but not different between these conditions in the 1-back (p > .1) or 2-back task (p > .05)"
