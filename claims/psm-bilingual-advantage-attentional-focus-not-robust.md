## Evidence

### Goodrich 2019

Goodrich, J. M., Koziol, N. A., Yoon, H., & Leiva, S. (2019). Do Spanish-English Bilingual Children Outperform Monolingual English-Speaking Children on Executive Function Tasks in Early Childhood? A Propensity Score Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/edu

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same propensity score matching analysis of ECLS-K:2011 data; attentional focus was a teacher-report CBQ short form measure. The article reports the attentional focus effect was not among those "robust to unobserved confounds." No effect size is printed.

> "After matching groups (n = 252 matched pairs of monolingual and bilingual children), bilinguals had greater teacher-rated inhibitory control and attentional focus than did monolinguals; however, only the effect for inhibitory control was robust to unobserved confounds."
