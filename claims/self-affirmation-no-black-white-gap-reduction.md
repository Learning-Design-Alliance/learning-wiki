## Evidence

### Borman 2013

Borman, G. D., & Grigg, J. (2013). An Effort to Close Achievement Gaps at Scale through Self-Affirmation. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563077

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

District-wide replication of Cohen et al.'s self-affirmation intervention using state test scores; Table 2 reports the African-American x Self-Affirmation interaction as 0.549 (reading) and 1.811 (mathematics), neither significant. The article states it finds "no evidence that the self-affirmations closed the Black-White achievement gap."

> "We find no evidence that the self-affirmations closed the Black-White achievement gap."
