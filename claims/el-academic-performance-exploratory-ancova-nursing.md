## Evidence

### Tutal Özgür 2026

Tutal Özgür. (2026). Concretizing the abstract: embodied learning as a pedagogical strategy for teaching measurement and evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10321-8

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Prespecified baseline-adjusted one-way ANCOVA with Bonferroni correction (α=0.017) on a researcher-selected 16-item multiple-choice test (KR-20=0.55), yielding ηp² = 0.133. The unadjusted post-test comparison favored the embodied learning group (69.58 vs 58.20, p=0.041, g=0.748), but this more conservative test is exploratory.

> "For academic performance, the ANCOVA revealed a non-significant effect of group after controlling for pre-test scores, F (1, 28) = 4.299, p = 0.047, ηp2 = 0.133. Although the adjusted mean difference favored the embodied-learning group (adjusted difference = 11.07 points, 95% CI [0.13, 22.00]), the comparison did not meet the prespecified multiplicity-corrected threshold (p = 0.047 > 0.017)."
