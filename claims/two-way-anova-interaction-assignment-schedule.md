## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The interaction between group (EoL vs weekly) and evaluation type (assignment, midterm, final, overall) was statistically significant, as were both main effects. [→ Janelle Resch 2015](#janelle-resch-2015)

## Evidence

### Janelle Resch 2015

Janelle Resch. (2015). Part I - A case study in post-secondary mathematics: the importance of mental health awareness. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1511.06699](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1511.06699)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA on the randomized MATH 235 data testing group, evaluation type, and their interaction; the interaction null hypothesis was rejected. The author states the test "cannot say what the diﬀerence is"; no effect size is printed.

> "The p-value of the interactions is p = 7.21e − 29< 0.05 and so, the null hypothesis H03 is rejected. We conclude there are statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerences in the interaction between the diﬀerent types of course evaluation (i.e., assignment, midterm, ﬁnal and overall marks) and the groups of students."
