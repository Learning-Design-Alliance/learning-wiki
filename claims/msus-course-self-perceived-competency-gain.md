## Evidence

### Weimer Andreas 2024

Weimer Andreas, Recker Florian, Vieth Thomas, Buggenhagen Holger, Schamberger Christian, Berthold Rainer, Berthold Svenja, Stein Stephan, Schmidmaier Gerhard, Kloeckner Roman, Neubauer Ricarda, Müller Lukas, Weinmann-Menke Julia, Weimer Johannes. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subjective evaluation of the study group via digital questionnaires at T1 and T2 using a seven-point Likert format: "A significant increase in self-perceived competence was measured both in the overall score (Δ2.6 ± 1.4)", also in general and MSUS-specific skills (p < 0.001).

> "A significant increase in self-perceived competence was measured both in the overall score (Δ2.6 ± 1.4) and in the categories "general ultrasound skills" (Δ2.1 ± 0.9) and "MSUS-specific skills" (Δ3.1 ± 1.8) (p < 0.001)."
