## Evidence

### Moyer-Packenham 2014

Moyer-Packenham, P.S., Baker, J., Westenskow, A., Anderson-Pence, K.L., Shumway, J.F., and Jordan, K.E. (2014). Predictors of Achievement When Virtual Manipulatives are Used for Mathematics Instruction. REDIMAT, Vol 3(2), 121-150. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2014.46

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple linear regression analysis of third-grade Post-test 1 scores found four significant demographic predictors for the CI group (R2 = .332) but only subjective ability for the VM group (R2 = .080), indicating fewer demographic factors influenced VM performance.

> "For the CI group subjective ability, gender, SES, and ELL status, were significant predictors of Post -test 1 scores, F(4, 82)= 9.703, MSE= 3124.657, p< .000, R2= .332, while subjective ability was the only significant predictor of Post -test 1 scores for the VM group, F(1,72)= 6.186, MSE= 1768.818, p= .015, R2= .080."

### Moyer-Packenham 2014 (2)

Moyer-Packenham, P.S., Baker, J., Westenskow, A., Anderson-Pence, K.L., Shumway, J.F., and Jordan, K.E. (2014). Predictors of Achievement When Virtual Manipulatives are Used for Mathematics Instruction. REDIMAT, Vol 3(2), 121-150. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2014.46

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis of third-grade Post-test 2 (retention) scores found subjective ability and ELL status predicted CI scores (R2 = .43) while only subjective ability predicted VM scores (R2 = .304).

> "The best -fitting model for the third-grade CI group on Post -test 2 included subjective ability and ELL status, F(2, 82)= 30.143, MSE= 7574.587, p< .000, R2= .43, while the model for the VM group consisted only of subjective ability, F(1, 72)= 30.972, MSE= 8683.746, p< .000, R2= .304."

### Moyer-Packenham 2014 (3)

Moyer-Packenham, P.S., Baker, J., Westenskow, A., Anderson-Pence, K.L., Shumway, J.F., and Jordan, K.E. (2014). Predictors of Achievement When Virtual Manipulatives are Used for Mathematics Instruction. REDIMAT, Vol 3(2), 121-150. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2014.46

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple linear regression analysis of fourth-grade Post-test 1 scores found subjective ability and SES predicted CI scores (R2 = .153) while only race predicted VM scores (R2 = .081).

> "For the CI group the best fitting model for Post -test 1 consisted of subjective ability and SES, F(2, 102)= 9.027, MSE= 3210.881, p< .000, R2= .153), while the model for the VM group included only race, F(1, 90)= 7.865, MSE= 3934.904, p= .006, R2= .081."

### Moyer-Packenham 2014 (4)

Moyer-Packenham, P.S., Baker, J., Westenskow, A., Anderson-Pence, K.L., Shumway, J.F., and Jordan, K.E. (2014). Predictors of Achievement When Virtual Manipulatives are Used for Mathematics Instruction. REDIMAT, Vol 3(2), 121-150. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2014.46

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis of fourth-grade Post-test 2 scores found subjective ability and SES predicted CI scores (R2 = .22) while no significant predictors were identified for the VM group.

> "The preferred model for the CI group alone for predicting Post -test 2 scores included subjective ability and SES, F(2, 102)= 14.124, MSE= 3444.874, p< .000, R2= .22; there were no significant predictors of Post-test 2 for the VM group."
