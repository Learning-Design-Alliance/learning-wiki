Theoretical argument in the opening section of the paper. Stern asserts that pedagogy historically targeted the average child, that subnormal-child pedagogy arose a few decades earlier, and that "What we still lack, however, is a pedagogy of supernormal children." The article offers no data for this historical claim.

> "For centuries we have had a pedagogy of normal children; indeed, until quite recently, pedagogy as a whole was well-nigh exclusively directed, both in theory and in practice, upon the average child."
