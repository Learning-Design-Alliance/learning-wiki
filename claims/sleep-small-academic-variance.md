## Evidence

### Grasby KL 2026

Grasby KL, Larsen SA, Gregory AM, Blunden S, Lewis KS, Madrid-Valero JJ, Coventry WL, Byrne B, Olson RK. (2026). Associations between sleep habits, performance in reading and mathematics, and inattention and hyperactivity. PLoS One 21(5): e0347892. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0347892

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Between-within (BW) mixed-model analyses of NAPLAN reading and mathematics scores in Australian twins across Grades 3–9 report that sleep fixed effects explain "a very small amount of variation in reading (ranging from 0.5–2.3%) and mathematics scores (ranging from 0.8–1.9%)."

> "Taken together, the fixed effects for sleep explained a very small amount of variation in reading (ranging from 0.5–2.3%) and mathematics scores (ranging from 0.8–1.9%)."
