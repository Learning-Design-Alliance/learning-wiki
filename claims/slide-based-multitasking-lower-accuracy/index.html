Evidence¶

Wammes J.D. 2019¶

Wammes J.D., Ralph B.C.W., Mills C., Bosch N., Duncan T.L. & Smilek D. (2019). Disengagement during lectures: Media multitasking and mind wandering in university classrooms. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.12.007

q2 · i? · associational · r1

A multilevel regression on 2343 probe-quiz data points from Study 2, with participant as a random variable, found "only media multitasking emerged as a negative predictor of quiz performance" (-0.10, CI 95 = -0.16, -0.04); mind wandering and elaborative estimates were not significant.

"In this model, the intercept (or the accuracy for on-task responses) was 0.65 (CI 95 = 0.61, 0.68), and only media multitasking emerged as a negative predictor of quiz performance, -0.10 (CI 95 = -0.16, -0.04)."
