### Nourian 2026

Nourian, L.; Callis, A.; Patterson, S.; Miao, J.; Heard, J.; Tigwell, G. W. (2026). Understanding How International Students in the U.S. Are Using Conversational AI to Support Cross-Cultural Adaptation. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15127

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparative analysis of survey challenge-prevalence and AI-usage items (Figure 2). The survey of 60 international students shows the mismatch: "although only 29% of students reported experiencing logistical difficulties, 86% reported using AI for logistical tasks." The authors attribute this to the high-stakes, on-demand nature of logistical tasks.
