### Fares Fawzi 2026

Fares Fawzi, Seyed Parsa Neshaei, Marta Knezevic, Tanya Nazaretsky, and Tanja Käser. (2026). REFINE: Real-world Exploration of Interactive Feedback and Student Behaviour. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Offline evaluation on an existing dataset of 177 student solutions to a discrete mathematics proof task, comparing three models before and after one judge-guided refinement step. The article reports "significant gains" for all three models with no effect size printed.

> "Across models, self-regulated next steps improved most. Paired McNemar exact tests (t=0 vs. t=1, Benjamini–Hochberg corrected) confirmed significant gains for GPT-5, Qwen3-30B-Thinking, and Qwen3-8B-REFINE (p < .001)."
