## Subclaims
`q3 i3` ERD peaked at 400-800 ms and ERS at 1600-2000 ms after word presentation, with area differences, both with large printed effect sizes. [→ Jäncke L 2010](#jancke-l-2010)

## Evidence

### Jäncke L 2010

Jäncke L, Sandmann P. (2010). Music listening while you learn: No influence of background music on verbal learning. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-6-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The four-way ANOVA of EEG alpha-band data in the main experiment showed a typical time course: strong event-related desynchronization peaking at the second time segment (400-800 ms) followed by the strongest event-related synchronization at the fifth (1600-2000 ms). The printed effect size for the time course is η² = 0.86; brain-area differences were also significant (η² = 0.81).

> "This time course is highly significant (F(4,67) = 106.2, p < 0.001, eta2 = 0.86)."
