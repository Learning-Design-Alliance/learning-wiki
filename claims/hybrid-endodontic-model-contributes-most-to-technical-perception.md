## Evidence

### Özdemir O 2026

Özdemir O, Matara BM, Gündoğar M. (2026). Preclinical endodontic training using natural/simulative models: a cross-sectional study involved trainee and educator perspectives on clinical competence. BMC Medical Education, 26:378. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08715-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online questionnaire study of 238 participants in Türkiye (students 46.8%, dentists 29.1%, residents/PhD 9.7%, endodontists 7.6%, educators 8.9%). Comparative analysis between educational models and technical perceptions (Table 5) showed "contributes more to technical clinical perception (p < 0,001)". Data are self-assessed perceptions, not tested performance outcomes.

> "when the contribution of educational models to perception is evaluated, the hybrid model, which extracted teeth, artificial teeth, and phantom models in combination, contributes more to technical clinical perception (p < 0,001)."
