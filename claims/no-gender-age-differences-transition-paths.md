## Evidence

### Schneider 2013

Schneider, M., & Hardy, I. (2013). Profiles of Inconsistent Knowledge in Children's Pathways of Conceptual Change. Developmental Psychology, 49(9). https://doi.org/10.1037/a0030976

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests comparing eight groups (seven transition paths plus rest category) on demographic variables found no significant differences for gender (p = .257, N = 160) or age (p = .174, N = 156); equivalence was not tested beyond these non-significant tests.

> "None of these groups differed in the proportions of boys and girls, ␹2(7) ⫽ 8.940, p ⫽ .257, N ⫽ 160, or in their age distribution, ␹2(21) ⫽ 26.908, p ⫽ .174, N ⫽ 156."
