## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who consumed breakfast frequently (5–7 days) had good academic performance compared with those who consumed it rarely, with an adjusted odds ratio of 0.08 for poor performance. [→ Adam Sagal Mohamed 2024](#adam-sagal-mohamed-2024)

## Evidence

### Adam Sagal Mohamed 2024

Adam Sagal Mohamed, Teshoma Melese Sinaga, Ahmed Awale Sh. Dahir, Tamiru Dessalegn. (2024). Household food insecurity and its association with academic performance among primary school adolescents in Hargeisa City, Somaliland. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0303034

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the cross-sectional study (n = 630) found habitual breakfast intake significantly associated with academic performance; the frequent (5–7 days) category had "AOR = 0.08, 95%CI = 0.03–0.20" for poor performance versus rare intake.

> "Habitual consumption of breakfast has a significant association with academic performance. Students who consumed their breakfast frequently had good academic performance as compared to their counterparts."
