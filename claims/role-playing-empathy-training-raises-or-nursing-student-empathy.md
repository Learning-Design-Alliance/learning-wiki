## Evidence

### Larti N 2018

Larti N, Ashouri E, Aarabi A. (2018). The effects of an empathy role-playing program for operating room nursing students in Iran. J Educ Eval Health Prof. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2018.15.29

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparative pretest-posttest study with 1-month follow-up among 77 operating room nursing students (36 experimental, 41 control) at Isfahan University of Medical Sciences; the "total mean empathy scores in the experimental group immediately after" were higher, P< 0.001 (Table 3: 120.58 vs 103.98). No effect size printed.

> "However, the total mean empathy scores in the experimental group immediately after and 1 month after the intervention were higher than those in the control group (P< 0.001)."
