Evidence¶

Sunny Yu 2026¶

Sunny Yu, Myra Cheng, Ahmad Jabbar, Ilia Sucholutsky, Katherine M. Collins, Dan Jurafsky, Robert D. Hawkins. (2026). Cognitive offloading and the speedup illusion in human-AI interaction. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23177 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.23177

q3 · i? · causal · r3

In the prediction sample, a linear mixed-effects model with prediction target and assistance source as fixed effects showed people "expect AI assistance to reduce task completion times by 68.5 seconds". The expected speedup was significantly greater for AI than for another highly intelligent participant (β=−51.4, p<0.001), and participants preferred AI assistance (β=1.37, p<0.001).
