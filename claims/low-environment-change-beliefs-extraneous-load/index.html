q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical prediction in the possibility mindset and learning processes section, drawing on cognitive load theory (Sweller, 1988). The article argues that under low environment-change beliefs the environment itself functions as an ongoing source of extraneous load; no empirical test is reported.

"the environment becomes an ongoing source of extraneous load, making it harder to sustain attention, encode material, and coordinate strategies even when motivation is intact"
