Surgeon-feedback section reporting residents' qualitative assessments of the two 3D-printed models after dissection. The article states the transparent model "was considered ideal for inexperienced surgeons" and that its visualization helped understanding of hidden structures in preparation for the opaque and fresh-frozen models.

> "The transparent model was considered ideal for inexperienced surgeons, as it provided a clear visualization of the marked anatomical structures. This feature facilitated an accurate understanding of the spatial arrangement, enabling more precise dissection and greater familiarity with the anatomy."
