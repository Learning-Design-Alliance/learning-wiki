## Evidence

### Giacomelli G 2019

Giacomelli G, Ferré F, Furlan M, Nuti S. (2019). Involving hybrid professionals in top management decision-making: How managerial training can make the difference. Health Services Management Research, 32(4), 168–179. https://doi.org/10.1177/0951484819844778

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Propensity score matching analysis (Table 7) adjusted for age, gender and organizational affiliation. Matched average-treatment-effect-on-the-treated differences were 11.67 for PIK, 11.92 for UPI and 14.51 for CDI, and the article concludes "This seems to mitigate the selection bias".

> "When computing the propensity score, all variables considered at some stage in our model as dependent (including the mediators) showed a significantly different mean value between the two groups defined by the participation in management training, after matching the samples over individual characteristics"
