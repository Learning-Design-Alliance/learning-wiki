## Evidence

### Goedegebuure E.P. 2026

Goedegebuure E.P., Boellaard T.N., van Dijk-de Haan M.C., et al. (2026). Improving membranous urethral length measurements on prostate MRI: a comparison of online training methods. European Radiology 36(8):6419-6427. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00330-026-12468-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects linear regression with experience (<10 vs ≥10 years) and training group as covariates, plus an interaction term between course effect and reader experience. A third model included resident vs. radiologist as a covariate instead of experience years.

> "A non-significant difference was observed between experienced and less-experienced radiologists, with a slightly higher diagnostic gain for less-experienced radiologists (p = 0.13). When comparing residents with radiologists, no statistically significant differences in training effects were observed (p = 0.77)."
