`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Gender comparison across Likert items in the 91-student survey (45 male, 46 female respondents), analyzed with Chi Square/Fisher's Exact tests on grouped responses. The article reports only one item differed: "I prioritized the concepts I needed to review was ranked more positively by females (97.8 %) than by males (84.4 %), p = 0.030."
