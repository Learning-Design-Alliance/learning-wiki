## Evidence

### Minkang Kim 2021

Minkang Kim, Jean Decety, Ling Wu, Soohyun Baek and Derek Sankey. (2021). Neural computations in children's third-party interventions are modulated by their parents' moral values. npj Science of Learning 6:38. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00116-5

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the central LPP (400–600 ms) window in the same ERP analysis of 42 children. In the Non-Protester group the mean LPP to harming scenes (M = 1.894) was smaller than to helping scenes (M = 2.753), with "medium effect size (Cohen 's d = 0.518)".

> "However between Protesters and Non-Protesters, there was signi ﬁcant Group × Condition interaction effect, F(1, 40) = 4.286; p = 0.045, power = 0.545, f = 0.328"
