## Evidence

### Mashmool Hossein 2026

Mashmool Hossein, Mahmodi Mohammad Azim, Amouzeshi Zahra, Bakhshi Toktam. (2026). Effect of hybrid simulation-based training on improving nursing students' patient interaction and suturing skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08841-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of post-intervention suturing scores in the two-group quasi-experiment; the article reports an effect size of 2.75 and, in the Discussion, "Cohen's d > 0.80 [large effect]" for the between-group suturing difference.

> "Regarding suturing skills, the median post-intervention score was 18.33 (IQR: 17.55–19.16) in the intervention group and 15.00 (IQR: 11.66–19.16) in the control group, indicating a statistically significant difference between the two groups (p = 0.013)."
