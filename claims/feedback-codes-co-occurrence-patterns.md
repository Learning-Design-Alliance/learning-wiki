Spearman's rank correlation analysis across all six coders combined on the coded sample of 100 teacher feedback messages. The article reports these positive co-occurrence patterns as indicating that affirmation is perceived as emotionally supportive and mathematical advice often acts as scaffolding; no correlation coefficients are printed in the text.

> "For instance, correlations between "Confirmation" and "Positive Affect," suggest that when teachers affirm the correctness of a student's response, such feedback is often perceived as emotionally supportive. Similarly, "Mathematical Suggestions" and "Hints" were positively correlated"
