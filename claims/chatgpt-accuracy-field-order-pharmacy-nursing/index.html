Evidence¶

Jin Hye Kyung 2024¶

Jin Hye Kyung, Lee Ha Eun, Kim EunYoung. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT-3.5 and GPT-4 in national licensing examinations for medicine, pharmacy, dentistry, and nursing: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05944-8

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects subgroup analysis by field of health licensing examination. Pooled accuracies were "71.5% (95% CI, 66.3-76.2%) in pharmacy" down to 61.8% in nursing; the difference between fields was significant (Q = 15.334, p = 0.002).

"The effect size for percentage of performance was at an average rate of 71.5% (95% CI, 66.3-76.2%) in pharmacy, 69.7% (95% CI, 65.9-73.2%) in medicine, 63.2% (95% CI, 54.6-71.1%) in dentistry, and 61.8% (95% CI, 58.7-64.9%) in nursing."
