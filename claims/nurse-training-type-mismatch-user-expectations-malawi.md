Qualitative descriptive study; theme 1 (curriculum reforms) from panel discussion and focus groups with nurse educators, practitioners and preceptors. Participants reported "a mismatch between the type of nurses and the expectations of the service users" and urged curricula formulated per users' actual health needs, including more primary-health-care-oriented registered nurses.
