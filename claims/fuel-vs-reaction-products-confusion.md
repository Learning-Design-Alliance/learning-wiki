### Mirofora Pilakouta 2011

Mirofora Pilakouta. (2011). TEI Piraeus students' knowledge on the beneficial applications of nuclear physics: Nuclear energy, radioactivity - consequences. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1111.4058](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1111.4058)

Survey item Q6 on radionuclides released in a nuclear accident: 58% chose plutonium and uranium, 18% correctly cesium and iodine, 12% iodine and plutonium, 11% 'don't know'. The author concludes students "confuses the nuclear fuel with the reaction products".

> "A big number of students (58% ) have the wrong perception that Plutonium and Uranium are the radioactive elements that are mainly released in the e nvironment. Only 18% answered correctly (cesium and iodine)."
