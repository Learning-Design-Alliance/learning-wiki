## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Bootstrapped mediation analysis showed a significant indirect effect of technology access on satisfaction through motivation (B = 0.111), with motivation mediating 31.2% of the total effect; the direct effect remained significant (B = 0.245). [→ Innab 2023](#innab-2023)

## Evidence

### Innab 2023

Innab, A., & Alqahtani, N. (2023). The mediating role of E‐learning motivation on the relationship between technology access and satisfaction with E‐learning. Nursing Open, 10, 2552–2559. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1513

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mediation analysis using Hayes' process macro model 4 with 5000 bootstrapping re-extractions on N = 139. The indirect effect through motivation was 0.111 and "the ratio of the indirect effect to the total effect, was 0.312"; the direct effect (B = 0.245, p < .05) remained significant, indicating partial mediation.

> "it was found that technology access had a significant effect on satisfaction with e‐learning of 0.111, within 95% CI, through motivation. The proportion of mediation (PM), the ratio of the indirect effect to the total effect, was 0.312"
