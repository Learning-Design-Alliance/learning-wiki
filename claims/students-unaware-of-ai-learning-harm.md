# Students do not perceive the learning harm from copying GPT solutions and overestimate their exam performance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` GPT Base students who performed worse on the exam did not perceive worse performance or learning, and GPT Tutor students perceived improvements that did not occur. [→ Bastani Hamsa 2025](#bastani-hamsa-2025)

## Evidence

### Bastani Hamsa 2025

Bastani Hamsa, Bastani Osbert, Sungu Alp, Ge Haosen, Kabakcı Özge, Mariman Rei. (2025). Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning: Evidence from high school mathematics. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`
