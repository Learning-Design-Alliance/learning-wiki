## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Of the 10 target techniques, only the five drilled techniques occurred at quantifiable frequencies; the others occurred rarely. [→ Becker Erin A. 2017](#becker-erin-a-2017)

## Evidence

### Becker Erin A. 2017

Becker Erin A., Easlon Erin J., Potter Sarah C., Guzman-Alvarez Alberto, Spear Jensen M., Facciotti Marc T., Igo Michele M., Singer Mitchell, Pagliarulo Christopher. (2017). The Effects of Practice-Based Training on Graduate Teaching Assistants' Classroom Practices. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar58. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-05-0162

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Frequency analysis across 158 coded classroom sessions found drilled techniques (circulate, cold call, right is right, stretch it variants) occurred often while "techniques that were not drilled occurred rarely"; Table 4 shows counts as low as 4–7 for no apology, normalize error, and praise improvement.

> "Significantly, techniques that were not drilled occurred rarely, at frequencies too low to pass our filter for IRR."
