### Bassah Nahyeni 2016

Bassah Nahyeni, Cox Karen, Seymour Jane. (2016). A qualitative evaluation of the impact of a palliative care course on preregistration nursing students’ practice in Cameroon. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-016-0106-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of focus group and critical incident interview data identified four barrier themes. Students reported nurses telling them "there are more important things to do" with recovering patients, that nurses "know little or nothing about palliative care", and that the absence of designated places, personnel, policy and resources for palliative care in general wards hindered implementation.

> "nursing students reported struggling with the lack of support from qualified nurses in the practice setting and lack of resources and policy for palliative care, while implementing their learning. They equally said it was challenging implement learning when the family was death denying or the patients were difficult."
