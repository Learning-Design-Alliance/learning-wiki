# Language aptitude correlates with vocabulary retention after audiovisual learning but not after grasping-based learning

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` LLAMA scores correlated positively with vocabulary scores in the AV condition (r = 0.42) and AVO condition (r = 0.35), but not significantly in the AVOG condition (r = 0.26, p = .076). [→ Manuela Macedonia 2023](#manuela-macedonia-2023)

## Evidence

### Manuela Macedonia 2023

Manuela Macedonia, Brian Mathias, Astrid E. Lehner, Susanne M. Reiterer, Claudia Repetto. (2023). Grasping Virtual Objects Benefits Lower Aptitude Learners' Acquisition of Foreign Language Vocabulary. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09835-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of composite LLAMA scores with average vocabulary test scores per condition in the same sample of 46 adults. Printed effect sizes: correlation r = 0.42 (AV, p = .003), r = 0.35 (AVO, p = .017), and r = 0.26 (AVOG, p = .076, not significant).

> "LLAMA scores showed a significant positive correlation with vocabulary test scores in the A V learning condition (r (46) = 0.42, p = .003) and A VO learning condition (r (46) = 0.35, p = .017), but not in the A VOG learning condition (r (46) = 0.26, p = .076)"
