## Evidence

Czeskleba A., Holzhausen Y., Peters H. (2020). Clinical reasoning for acute dyspnoea: comparison between final-year medical students from discipline- and competency-based undergraduate programmes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02055-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' comparison of their participants' ASCLIRE results with previously reported expertise-level results from Kunina-Habenicht et al.; the authors state this adds validity evidence for the test and for this study's results.

> "Across all six cases, the participants’ results for choosing the correct diagnosis (TC students 82%, CC students 77%) ranked between those of medical experts (94%) and those of medical students before entering the final-year clerkship (56%, study years 1–5)."
