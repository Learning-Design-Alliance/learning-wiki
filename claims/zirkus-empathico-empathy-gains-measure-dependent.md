### Naumann 2023

Naumann, S., Bayer, M., Kirst, S., van der Meer, E. & Dziobek, I. (2023). A randomized controlled trial on the digital socio-emotional competence training Zirkus Empathico for preschoolers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00169-8

`q4 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Pre-registered RCT with 74 preschoolers randomized to Zirkus Empathico or active control; ITT ANCOVAs on change scores covarying pre-training scores. EMK parent empathy "indicated larger increases in empathy for the Zirkus Empathico group" (d = 0.28, p = 0.045), but GEM parent rating (d = 0.23, p = 0.32) and EMK child assessment (p = 0.07) showed no group differences.
