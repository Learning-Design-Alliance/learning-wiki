## Evidence

### Burr Steven A 2013

Burr Steven A, Brodier Elizabeth, Wilkinson Simon. (2013). Delivery and use of individualised feedback in large class medical teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-63

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two surveys of medical students: an online behaviour-based survey of years 1 and 2, and an interviewer-administered attitude-based survey of years 1 to 3; the questions on revisiting objectives were answered by 44 students in years 2 and 3. Only 39% of responders used the report to direct further learning; the article reports that overall 61% of learners did not use the report for its intended purpose and that usefulness ratings declined across successive semesters (Figure 4).
