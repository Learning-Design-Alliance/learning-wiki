Evidence¶

Shakir T 2026¶

Shakir T, Lingam G, Boal M, Francis N, Chand M. (2026). Technology-enhanced training in basic robotic surgical skills: a systematic review. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03633-w

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports a single prospective cohort study of 30 medical students receiving real-time AR overlays of speed, grip force or relative inter-instrument movement during three simulator tasks. The AR speed subgroup showed the quoted velocity gain; the review rates AR evidence base as low.

"Greatest improvements were noted in the AR speed group, whereby mean instrument velocity almost doubled in a bimanual carrying task from 21 to 38 mm/sec (p < 0.05)."
