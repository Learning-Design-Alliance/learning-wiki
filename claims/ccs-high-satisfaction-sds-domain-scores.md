`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative component of a descriptive mixed-methods study: 103 second-year nursing students completed the 20-item Simulation Design Scale after four community care scenarios. The article reports "mean domain scores of approximately 4.5" with support lowest at 4.49 ± 0.65; overall α = 0.977.

> "Students reported high levels of agreement with the SDS items, with mean domain scores of approximately 4.5. Support received the lowest score (4.49 ± 0.65), whereas Objectives and Information received the highest (4.54 ± 0.61)"
