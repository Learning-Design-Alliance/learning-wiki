Peña 2024¶

Peña, M., Vásquez-Venegas, C., Cortés, P., Pittaluga, E., Herrera, M., Pino, E. J., Escobar, R. G., Dehaene-Lambertz, G., & Guevara, P. (2024). A brief tablet-based intervention benefits linguistic and communicative abilities in toddlers and preschoolers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00249-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-post equivalent-group experiment with 152 preschoolers (~42 months); Study group (n=68) showed greater TECAL vocabulary gain than Control (n=76), Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney Z = -2.34, P = 0.019, r = -0.12; total score Z = -2.15, P = 0.031, r = -0.18. "the Study group had signi ﬁcantly greater standardized gain than the Control group in the TECAL vocabulary subscale and total scores"
