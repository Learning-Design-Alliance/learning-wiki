## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Overall self-directed learning ability increased significantly for both BA and CA students after the 12-week treatment. [→ Orawiwatnakul 2011](#orawiwatnakul-2011)

## Evidence

### Orawiwatnakul 2011

Orawiwatnakul, W., & Wichadee, S. (2011). A Comparison Of Students' Outcomes In Two Classes: Business Administration Students VS Communication Arts Students Based On Self-Directed Learning Activities. Contemporary Issues In Education Research, 4(5). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1072831

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-samples t-tests of overall Guglielmino SDL questionnaire scores (BA t=5.70, CA t=6.34, both p=.00). The article reports "BA and CA students gained higher self-directed learning ability after the intervention at a significance level of .05"; no effect size is printed.

> "From Table 8, it was found that BA and CA students gained higher self-directed learning ability after the intervention at a significance level of .05. That is, self-directed learning activities could be used to enhance self-directed learning ability of the two groups."
