Video-review analysis in the before-and-after quality improvement study found the combined teamwork event count rose from 15.0 to 18.0, "without reaching statistical significance (p = 0.056)"; no effect size was printed.

> "The total number of all five teamwork events increased from 15.0 (10.0–24.3) to 18.0 (13.5–30.5) after the delivery of in situ SBT, but without reaching statistical significance (p = 0.056; Fig. 2)."
