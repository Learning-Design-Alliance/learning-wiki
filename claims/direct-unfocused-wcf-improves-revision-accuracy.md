# Direct unfocused WCF leads elementary EFL students to significantly outperform a no-feedback control group when revising previously written drafts

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Elementary EFL students who received direct unfocused WCF scored significantly higher on grammatical accuracy in the revisions of three writing tasks than students who received no error correction. [→ Khanlarzadeh and Nemati 2016](#khanlarzadeh-and-nemati-2016)

## Evidence

### Khanlarzadeh and Nemati 2016

Mobin Khanlarzadeh, Majid Nemati. (2016). The effect of written corrective feedback on grammatical accuracy of EFL students: An improvement over previous unfocused designs. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research 4(2). https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact classes (experimental n = 16, control n = 17) using one-way between-subjects ANOVA on revision scores. The study reports "a significant difference was found between the grammatical accuracy of the two groups" on all three revisions; no effect size was printed.

> "a significant difference was found between the grammatical accuracy of the two groups on revision 1 (F = 8.39, p = .007), revision 2 (F = 6.70, p = .015), and revision 3 (F = 17.35, p = .00)."
