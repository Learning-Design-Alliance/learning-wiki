Windrim Catherine, Charleson Colin, Gualdron David Rojas, Quevedo Alvaro, Gotha Lara, Van Mieghem Tim, Ryan Greg, Windrim Rory. (2025). Virtual Reality Simulation in Teaching Fetoscopic Laser Placental Photocoagulation in Twin‐To‐Twin Transfusion Syndrome. Prenatal Diagnosis. https://doi.org/10.1002/pd.6807

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective validation study using a standardized five-point Likert questionnaire with 31 participants stratified into three experience groups; positive validation was the percentage of agree responses. The study reports internal consistency of Cronbach's α = 0.92 and reports this as a pilot with an unvalidated questionnaire.

> "The simulator received strong positive validation (defined as “somewhat agree” or “strongly agree” responses) across all measured aspects, with training effectiveness receiving the highest endorsement (87%, 95% CI: 83%–91%), followed by user engagement (85%, 95% CI: 81%–89%) and TTTS environment reproduction (84%, 95% CI: 80%–88%)."
