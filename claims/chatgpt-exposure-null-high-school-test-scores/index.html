Nick Huntington-Klein 2026¶

Nick Huntington-Klein. (2026). Little Impact of ChatGPT on High School Test Scores. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08812

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Observational panel analysis of school-test-subject-grade-year test scores on a balanced 2022-2025 panel with unit and state-by-year fixed effects. The baseline estimate is "-0.0096 (0.0098)" and the higher-precision-metro estimate "-0.0098 (0.0115)", both small and statistically insignificant.

"The baseline high school estimate is -0.0096 (0.0098). Restricting to the higher-precisionareas, meaningthosewhose2023-24activityoccupiesmoreoftheirown index scale, gives -0.0098 (0.0115)."

Nick Huntington-Klein 2026 (2)¶

Nick Huntington-Klein. (2026). Little Impact of ChatGPT on High School Test Scores. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08812

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Measurement-error bounds analysis dividing confidence-interval ends by the classical attenuation factor from the exposure measure's reliability. The article concludes the effect is "less than about 0.04 standard deviations in absolute terms".

"a one standard deviation increase in educational ChatGPT usage across metropolitan areas changed high school test scores by less than about 0.04 standard deviations in absolute terms, based on Table5."
