### Murad Amal A. 2025

Murad Amal A., Sawalha Murad A., Maabreh Roqia Saleem, Elsabagh Eman E., Roque Mark Y., Alharbi Muna, Abdelkader Raghad, Saleh Zyad T. (2025). Exploring male nursing students’ experiences and perspectives in a maternal-newborn clinical course using high-fidelity simulation: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07436-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 4 sub-theme from the phenomenological interviews. One student emphasized the simulation’s realism: "The scenario and the mannequin looked real, approximately 85% similar to an actual patient".

> "Students with limited clinical placement opportunities found HFS particularly beneficial, as it provided a realistic and risk-free setting to practice essential maternal-newborn care skills."

### Murad Amal A. 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 4 preparation sub-theme. A student recounted, "It was very helpful during my internship period", which the authors read as simulations translating into improved confidence during actual clinical placements.

> "Participants appreciated the opportunity to apply their knowledge and skills in simulated scenarios before entering the clinical environment."
