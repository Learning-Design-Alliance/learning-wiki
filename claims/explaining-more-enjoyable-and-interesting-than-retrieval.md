## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jacob Leonie 2021

Jacob Leonie, Lachner Andreas, Scheiter Katharina. (2021). Does increasing social presence enhance the effectiveness of writing explanations? PLoS ONE 16(4): e0250406. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250406

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

ANOVA on enjoyment (two items, 4-point scale) in the four-condition experiment; the article reports ηp2=.15 with all three explaining conditions exceeding retrieval.

> "Results revealed significant differences among conditions, F(3, 133) = 7.91, p < .001, ηp2=.15. Post-hoc comparisons showed that students in all explaining conditions (i.e., standard written condition: p = .012; chat condition: p < .001; oral condition: p = .012) enjoyed the learning activity more than students in the retrieval condition."

### Jacob Leonie 2021 (2)

Jacob Leonie, Lachner Andreas, Scheiter Katharina. (2021). Does increasing social presence enhance the effectiveness of writing explanations? PLoS ONE 16(4): e0250406. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250406

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

ANOVA on interest in the same experiment; the article reports ηp2=.23 and post-hoc comparisons showing all three explaining conditions rated the activity as more interesting than retrieval.

> "Results of an ANOVA with experimental condition as independent variable showed a significant effect of condition, F(3, 133) = 13.11, p < .001, ηp2=.23."
