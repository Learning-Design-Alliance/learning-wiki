# Performance on questions from the non-vodcast lecturer's lectures did not differ significantly between the two cohorts.

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On 21 identical CA questions covering material delivered by the other lecturer (no vodcasts), both cohorts scored 61.3% with no significant difference (P=0.5). [→ Rae 2017](#rae-2017)

## Evidence

### Rae 2017

Rae, M. G., & McCarthy, M. (2017). The impact of vodcast utilisation upon student learning of Physiology by first year graduate to entry medicine students. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 17(2), 1-23. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v17i2.21125

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Internal control analysis: performance on questions from the non-vodcast lecturer's three lectures showed no difference between cohorts, supporting that the CA improvement was specific to vodcast-covered material.

> "this analysis revealed that there was no statistically significant difference in performance between the two year groups (61.3±6.3% in 12-13 vs 61.3±5.6% in 13-14, n=21, P=0.5, Student's paired t-test; data not shown)."
