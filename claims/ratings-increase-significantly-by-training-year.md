### Tekian A 2019

Tekian A, Park YS, Tilton S, Prunty PF, Abasolo E, Zar F, Cook DA. (2019). Competencies and Feedback on Internal Medicine Residents' End-of-Rotation Assessments Over Time: Qualitative and Quantitative Analyses. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000002821

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects regression accounting for clustering of learners on 1,869 assessments from one cohort; the article reports ratings "increased slightly but significantly with each training year (PGY1: mean = 5.31 [SD = 0.59], PGY2: mean = 5.58 [SD = 0.47], PGY3: mean = 5.86 [SD = 0.43]; P < .001 for each pairwise comparison)". No effect size is printed.
