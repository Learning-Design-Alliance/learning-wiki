## Evidence

### Barhoumi 2015

Chokri Barhoumi. (2015). The Effectiveness of WhatsApp Mobile Learning Activities Guided by Activity Theory on Students' Knowledge Management. Contemporary Educational Technology, 6(3), 221-238. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105764

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on post-achievement test scores of 34 experimental and 34 control students in the course Scientific Research Methods in Information Science. Means were "2.79" (control) and "3.41" (experimental); the difference favored the experimental group at the 0.05 alpha level. No effect size was printed.

> "Table 3 shows that the value of the t -test calculated for the equality of the means of the control and experimental groups is 2.234, which is greater than the value of t -test of the table."
