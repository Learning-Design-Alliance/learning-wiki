`q2 · i1`

Pearson correlation in the 147-student wartime sample. The authors attribute the effect to possible psychological overload among highly agreeable individuals, especially women, facing social expectations and caregiving demands.

> "agreeableness showed a weak but positive association with indicators of a decline in well-being (r = .16, p < .05), contrary to our expectation regarding its protective function"
