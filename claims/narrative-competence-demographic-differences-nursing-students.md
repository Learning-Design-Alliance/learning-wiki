`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Group comparisons in the 1,025-student survey (Table 1): student leaders scored 149.16 ± 17.47 vs 145.59 ± 18.06; highly democratic family atmosphere was associated with the highest scores (151.84 ± 18.39).

> "Students with leadership experience (t = 2.963, P = 0.003) and who experienced democratic family dynamics (F = 16.958, P < 0.001) had significantly greater narrative competence scores."
