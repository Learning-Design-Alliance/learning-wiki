## Evidence

### Samson 2022

Samson, K. L. I., Fischer, J. A. J., & Roche, M. L. (2022). Iron Status, Anemia, and Iron Interventions and Their Associations with Cognitive and Academic Performance in Adolescents: A Systematic Review. Nutrients, 14(1), 224. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu14010224

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review synthesis of 16 cross-sectional and intervention studies reporting school performance measures; the review reports 11 of 16 studies found evidence of influence, with heterogeneous measures and "no relationship in other settings".

> "There may be evidence to suggest the iron status and anemia influence school performance as reported by 11 of the 16 studies. Additionally, all four of the studies that examined iron supplementation and school performance found positive effects."
