# Adolescents and parents perceive obesity affects achievement indirectly through attitude to school rather than directly

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adolescent girls with obesity and their parents perceived that academic achievement is not directly related to body weight but depends on pupils' attitude towards learning and effort. [→ Martin A 2017](#martin-a-2017)

## Evidence

### Martin A 2017

Martin A, Booth JN, McGeown S, Niven A, Sproule J, Saunders DH, Reilly JJ. (2017). Longitudinal Associations Between Childhood Obesity and Academic Achievement: Systematic Review with Focus Group Data. Current Obesity Reports, 6(3), 297–313. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13679-017-0272-9

`q2 · i?`

Focus group findings from four adolescent girls with obesity and their parents/guardians. "Parents/guardians and adolescents perceived that academic achievement is not necessarily related to body weight per se; academic achievement depends on pupils' attitude towards learning and efforts put into school work."
