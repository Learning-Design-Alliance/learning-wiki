# The complexity of one-step equations increases with the number of operational and relational lines

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The greater the number of operational and relational lines in a one-step equation, the greater its complexity, because learners must manipulate interacting elements across those lines. [→ Ngu 2014](#ngu-2014)

Analytical argument from a cognitive load theory analysis of one-step equations, not an empirical test. The paper states "The higher the number of operational and relational lines, the greater the complexity of the equations", grounding complexity in element interactivity across lines.
