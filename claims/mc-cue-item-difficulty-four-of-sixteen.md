## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Average scores differ significantly between versions on 4 of 16 items (Mann-Whitney U-test, p < 0.05); the MC version is easier on only Q9 and Q11, where answers are more easily recognized than generated. ## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2013

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2013). Multiple-choice Assessment for Upper-division Electricity and Magnetism. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.079](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.079)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Per-item analysis shown in Fig. 3 comparing average scores on each of the 16 items across the two versions in the E&M 1 course. For Q1 students more often selected an inappropriate method on MC; for Q16 the difference may reflect loss of credit for working through the math.

> "differences between the scores are significant for 4 of 16 items (Mann-Whitney U-test, p < 0.05). It is tempting to assume that the MC version would be easier; however, this is only the case for 2 of the 4 (Q9 & 11)"
