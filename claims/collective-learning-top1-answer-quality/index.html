q3 · i? · causal · r1

Analysis of Top 1 answer quality and frequency for each question in the Collective Learning experiment with 180 participants; the Question 5 Top 1 answer comprised "1 positive strategy and 4 very positive ones", while the Question 6 Top 1 scored 6.

"On Question 5, the Top 1 answer obtained a score of 9. This is relevant because participants were presented with 15 possible ways of dealing with the case: 5 negative ones, 5 positive ones, and 5 very positive ones."
