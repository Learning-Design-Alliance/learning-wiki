Evidence¶

Jie Cao 2026¶

Jie Cao, Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Christian Schunn, Elizabeth A. McLaughlin, Jionghao Lin, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2026). Assessing the Impact and Underlying Pathways of Sequenced AI Feedback on Student Learning. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07469 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.07469

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized experiment (layered n=100, non-layered n=99) in an online higher-education lesson on the multimedia principle; ANCOVA on post-test with pre-test as covariate. The contrast was significant, t(196) = 2.32, p = .02, and the model accounted for 34% of variance (R2 = .34).

"After controlling for initial performance, students in the non-layered feed- backgroupachievedsignificantlyhigherpost-testscores(M ad j = 11.24,SE= 0.25) than those in the layered feedback group (Mad j = 10.40,SE= 0.25)."
