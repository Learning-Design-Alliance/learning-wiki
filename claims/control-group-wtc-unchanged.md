### Mesgarshahr and Abdollahzadeh 2014

Abulfazl Mesgarshahr, Esmaeel Abdollahzadeh. (2014). The impact of teaching communication strategies on EFL learners’ Willingness to Communicate. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2014.4.1.4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t test within the control group, which followed the regular EFL curriculum with no CS treatment, showed "no statistically significant difference in WTC s c o r e s f o r t h e c o n t r o l g r o u p" from pre-test to post-test (t (52) = 1.27, p > .05). No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.
