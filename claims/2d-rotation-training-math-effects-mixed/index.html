Evidence¶

Jeffrey Buckley 2018¶

Jeffrey Buckley, Niall Seery, Donal Canty. (2018). A Heuristic Framework of Spatial Ability: a Review and Synthesis of Spatial Factor Literature to Support its Translation into STEM Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9432-z

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports that in 6- to 8-year-olds, Cheng and Mix found "statistically signif- icant gains in performance relative to a pre-test" with an immediate mathematics post-test, whereas Hawes et al.'s week-delayed post-test after a different training intervention showed no improvement.

"Cheng and Mix ( 2014) implemented a mathematics post-test directly after the training and found statistically signif- icant gains in performance relative to a pre-test."

Jeffrey Buckley 2018 (2)¶

Jeffrey Buckley, Niall Seery, Donal Canty. (2018). A Heuristic Framework of Spatial Ability: a Review and Synthesis of Spatial Factor Literature to Support its Translation into STEM Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9432-z

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Hawes et al. found "statistically significant correlations between pre-test measures of two-dimensional mental rotations and mathematics problems ranging fromr = 0.40 to 0.63", and posited Cheng and Mix's gains were a priming effect from similar problems.

"They did find statistically significant correlations between pre-test measures of two-dimensional mental rotations and mathematics problems ranging fromr = 0.40 to 0.63."
