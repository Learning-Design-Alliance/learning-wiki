Evidence¶

Feng 2024¶

Feng, M., Weiser, G., & Collins, K. (2024). Cost and cost effectiveness of ASSISTments online math support: Analysis from a randomized controlled study in middle school. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655721

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

Intent-to-treat impact analysis from the ASSISTments North Carolina replication RCT, following a preregistered analysis plan. The analytic sample was 5,991 students (2,961 intervention, 3,030 comparison); the reported difference corresponds to "an effect size of 0.10 (Hedges's g)".

"The analysis revealed that intervention group students scored higher (0.8 points) than comparison students on the EOG-MA08 test, with this difference being statistically significant (estimated mean score for intervention = 535.13, comparison = 534.33; p = 0.011)."
