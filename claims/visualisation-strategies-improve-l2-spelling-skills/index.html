Evidence¶

Nahari 2016¶

Nahari, A. A., & Alfadda, H. A. (2016). From Memorising to Visualising: The Effect of Using Visualisation Strategies to Improve Students' Spelling Skills. English Language Teaching, 9(6), 1. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n6p1

q3 · i? · causal · r1

A quasi-experimental study of 42 sixth-grade Saudi students compared an experimental group trained in visualisation strategies to a control group using traditional methods. The experimental group's post-test mean (21.52) exceeded the control group's (15.85), a statistically significant difference favouring visualisation training.

"There were statistically significant differences in the total average score of the post test between the experimental group and the control group favouring the experimental group."
