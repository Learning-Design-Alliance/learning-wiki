### Avcu Cagla and Yildiz Tülin 2026

Avcu Cagla and Yildiz Tülin. (2026). The Effect of 3D Printer and Moulage Use on Improving Stoma Care Knowledge and Skills in Nursing Students: A Randomized Controlled Trial. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.71068

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison using Mann–Whitney U test on the 23-item Stoma Care Skills Graded Scoring Key (Cronbach's alpha 0.817). Experimental mean 45.42 ± 1.97 vs control 43.57 ± 2.93; "The experimental group's stoma care skill scores were significantly higher than the control group" with r = 0.472.

> "A statistically significant difference was found between the participants' stoma care skill adequacy scores according to the groups. (p < 0.001) The experimental group's stoma care skill scores were significantly higher than the control group."
