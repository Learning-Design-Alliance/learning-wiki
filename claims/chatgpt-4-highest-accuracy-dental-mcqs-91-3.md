# ChatGPT-4 achieved the highest accuracy of the five tested AI models on standardised dental MCQs, at 91.3% in both testing sessions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ChatGPT-4 correctly answered 137 of 150 dental MCQs (91.3%) in the first round and maintained 137 correct answers (91.3%) in the second session, the highest accuracy among the five models tested. [→ Alshammari Abdullah F. 2025](#alshammari-abdullah-f-2025)

## Evidence

### Alshammari Abdullah F. 2025

Alshammari Abdullah F., Madfa Ahmed A., Anazi Bassam Ali, Alenezi Yousef E., Alkurdi Khlood A. (2025). Comparison of accuracy and consistency of AI Language models when answering standardised dental MCQs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07624-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative cross-sectional evaluation of five AI models on the same 150 textbook MCQs. In the first round "ChatGPT-4 achieved the highest accuracy" with 137 correct (91.3%), ahead of Grok XI (90.7%) and Qwen 2.5 (89.3%); accuracy percentages are descriptive, no effect size printed.

> "In the first round of testing (Table 1), ChatGPT-4 achieved the highest accuracy, correctly answering 137 questions (91.3%). Grok XI followed closely with 136 correct answers (90.7%), and Qwen 2.5 provided 134 correct responses (89.3%)."
