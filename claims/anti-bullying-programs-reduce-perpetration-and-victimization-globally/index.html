Evidence¶

Hannah Gaffney 2019¶

Hannah Gaffney, David P. Farrington, & Maria M. Ttofi. (2019). Examining the Effectiveness of School-Bullying Intervention Programs Globally: a Meta-analysis. International Journal of Bullying Prevention. https://doi.org/10.1007/s42380-019-0007-4

q4 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 100 independent evaluations using the multiplicative variance adjustment model, with effect sizes mostly corrected for clustering. The pooled odds ratios correspond to "an approximate reduction of 19–20% and 15–16% for bullying perpetration and victimization respectively."

"Overall, our meta-analysis found that anti-bullying programs were effective in reducing both school-bullying perpetration (OR = 1.324; 95% CI 1.27 –1.38; p < 0.001) and school-bullying victimization (OR = 1.248; 95% CI 1.27 –1.38; p < 0.001) outcomes."
