## Evidence

### Olson Jonathan R. 2020

Olson Jonathan R., Coldiron Jennifer Schurer, Parigoris Ryan M., Zabel Michelle D., Matarese Marlene, Bruns Eric J. (2020). Developing an Evidence-Based Technical Assistance Model: a Process Evaluation of the National Training and Technical Assistance Center for Child, Youth, and Family Mental Health. The Journal of Behavioral Health Services & Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11414-020-09686-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVAs compared mean SAIS subscale scores across low, medium, and high TA-usage categories among 21 grantees, with Tukey's HSD showing significant differences between high- and low-level users. The comparison contrasts "high users of TA" with "low users of TA"; only F statistics are printed.

> "Additionally, high users of TA reported significantly less progress than low users of TA in the areas of governance (F = 4.22, p = 0.032), management policies and procedures (F = 5.11, p = 0.018), and geographical area covered (F = 5.77, p = 0.012)."

### Olson Jonathan R. 2020 (2)

Olson Jonathan R., Coldiron Jennifer Schurer, Parigoris Ryan M., Zabel Michelle D., Matarese Marlene, Bruns Eric J. (2020). Developing an Evidence-Based Technical Assistance Model: a Process Evaluation of the National Training and Technical Assistance Center for Child, Youth, and Family Mental Health. The Journal of Behavioral Health Services & Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11414-020-09686-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The same ANOVA analyses found no significant differences across usage levels for two of the five SAIS domains. A non-significant test with no printed effect size is reported as no significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "There also were no significant differences across TA levels for progress on supporting local service delivery (F = 0.74, p = 0.492) or continuous quality improvement (F = 1.90, p = 0.183)."
