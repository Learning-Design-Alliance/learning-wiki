## Evidence

### Feedback processing and emotion regulation in nursing students during internship 2022

Feedback processing and emotion regulation in nursing students during internship. (2022). https://eric.ed.gov/?q=feedback+processing+and+emotion+regulation+in+nursing+students+during+internship

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same interview study, thematic analysis of feedback interpretation showed emotion type varied with feedback focus and valence. The article reports negative person-focused feedback "caused unpleasant and activating emotions such as anger, shame, anxiety, and surprise with a high intensity."

> "Negative feedback that focused on the students’ person caused unpleasant and activating emotions such as anger, shame, anxiety, and surprise with a high intensity; it also generated unpleasant and deactivating emotions like sadness, disappointment, and hopelessness"

### Feedback processing and emotion regulation in nursing students during internship 2022 (2)

Feedback processing and emotion regulation in nursing students during internship. (2022). https://eric.ed.gov/?q=feedback+processing+and+emotion+regulation+in+nursing+students+during+internship

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the same interviews found task-focused negative feedback could yield hope when improvement clues were present. The article states such feedback generated "pleasant and activating emotions like hope if the students were given or identified clues for improvement."

> "Negative feedback that focused on the task generated unpleasant and activating emotions like shame and anxiety, or pleasant and activating emotions like hope if the students were given or identified clues for improvement."
