Evidence¶

Zhao Qianhui and Li Shuhui 2026¶

Zhao Qianhui and Li Shuhui. (2026). Exploring Middle School Students' Interactions and Perceptions of Using Generative AI in STEAM Learning Environments. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091626

q2 · i? · design · r2

Retrospective single-item 5-point satisfaction ratings from 12 eighth-grade students, supplemented by thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews using emotion graphs as recall aids. The study reports "high satisfaction with GenAI use in STEAM learning environments (M = 4.75, SD = 0.40)".

"the 12 students demonstrated high satisfaction with GenAI use in STEAM learning environments (M = 4.75, SD = 0.40). Interview data further revealed that students' satisfaction changed throughout the learning process, mainly following two patterns: gradual improvement and fluctuation."
