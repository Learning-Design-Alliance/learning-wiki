# Post-test mental effort correlates negatively with global post-test performance in all three integrative-activity conditions

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Mental effort invested in the post-test correlated negatively with global performance (conceptual knowledge and problem-solving) in the self-explanation, concept map completion and model concept map study conditions. [→ Dyer 2015](#dyer-2015)

## Evidence

### Dyer 2015

Dyer, J.-O., Hudon, A., Montpetit-Tourangeau, K., Charlin, B., Mamede, S., & van Gog, T. (2015). Example-based learning: comparing the effects of additionally providing three different integrative learning activities on physiotherapy intervention knowledge. BMC Medical Education, 15, 37. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0308-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Spearman rank correlations between post-test mental effort and global performance, reported in the Results of the randomized experiment. Correlations were negative in all conditions: "r = −.527", "r = −.482", "r = −.665".

> "negatively correlated with global performance (conceptual knowledge and problem-solving) on the post-test in the self-explanation (r = −.527, P = .003), concept map completion (r = −.482, P = .007) and model concept map study (r = −.665, P < .001) conditions"
