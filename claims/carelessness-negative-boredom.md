## Evidence

### Maria Ofelia Z. San Pedro 2014

Maria Ofelia Z. San Pedro, Ryan S. J. d. Baker, & Ma. Mercedes T. Rodrigo. (2014). Carelessness and Affect in an Intelligent Tutoring System for Mathematics. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0015-y

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of tutor logs and field-observed affect from the Philippine scatterplot tutor study. The article reports carelessness "negatively correlated with boredom in both interfaces", which the authors found somewhat surprising.

> "Care-lessness was negatively correlated with boredom in both interfaces, r=−0.36, p=0. 004 for NoScooter group; r=−0.35, p=0.004 for Scooter group."
