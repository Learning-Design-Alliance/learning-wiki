## Evidence

### Inka Sara Hähnlein 2024

Inka Sara Hähnlein, Pablo Pirnay-Dummer. (2024). Promoting pre-service teachers' knowledge integration from multiple text sources across domains with instructional prompts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10363-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA (Type III, Greenhouse-Geisser correction) on pre-post perceived relevance of knowledge integration in the three-group experiment (N = 109). The article reports "a significant interaction effect of time (within) and group (between) on perceived relevance was found"; no main effects of time or group were significant.

> "However, as Figure 6 illustrates, a significant interaction effect of time (within) and group (between) on perceived relevance was found (F[2,105] = 5.68, p =.005"

### Inka Sara Hähnlein 2024 (2)

Inka Sara Hähnlein, Pablo Pirnay-Dummer. (2024). Promoting pre-service teachers' knowledge integration from multiple text sources across domains with instructional prompts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10363-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Tukey-HSD-adjusted mixed post-hoc analysis following the mixed ANOVA. The increase was "for only EG1, the group receiving combined prompts"; the printed MDiff = − 0.31 is an unstandardised mean difference, and no standardised effect size is reported.

> "Tukey-HSD-adjusted mixed post-hoc analysis revealed a significant (p = .02) increase in perceived relevance of knowledge integration for only EG1, the group receiving combined prompts (MDiff = − 0.31, 95% − CI[− .58, − .033])."
