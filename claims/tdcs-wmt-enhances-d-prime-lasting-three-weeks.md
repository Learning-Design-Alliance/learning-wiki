## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Active tDCS plus working memory training produced higher post-training d-primes than sham, with no significant group-by-time interaction. [→ Yufeng Ke 2023](#yufeng-ke-2023)

## Evidence

### Yufeng Ke 2023

Yufeng Ke, Shuang Liu, Long Chen, Xiashuang Wang and Dong Ming. (2023). Lasting enhancements in neural efficiency by multi-session transcranial direct current stimulation during working memory training. npj Science of Learning 8:48. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00200-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANCOVAs on 4- and 6-back d-primes at Post1 and Post21 in a randomized active-versus-sham HD-tDCS design (N = 34) showed significant group main effects (e.g., 4-back p = 0.002, η2p = 0.261) while "the main effects of time and the group-by-time interaction effects in both tasks were non-significant".

> "these group differences were significant after training with higher levels of d-primes in the active group, compared to the sham group. Given that the two groups displayed the same level of performance in d-prime prior to training, it is less likely that higher levels of d-prime at Post-tests in the active group are associated with "more room to improve.""
