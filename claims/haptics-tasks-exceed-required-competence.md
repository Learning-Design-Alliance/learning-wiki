# Clinical teachers judged the haptics software's available learning activities excessive for the required competence and curriculum time

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants reported that the number of haptics learning activities exceeded what students could complete in the required time or should be expected to do. [→ Foxton Richard 2025](#foxton-richard-2025)

## Evidence

### Foxton Richard 2025

Foxton Richard, San Diego Jonathan P., Lu Emily, Rajadurai Susha, Bartlett Anitha, McIlwane Clare, Austin Rupert, Srisontisuk Pinsuda, Sagoo Anika K., Piper Kim. (2025). Scaffolding competence‐based learning design in the dental simulation curriculum. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.12985

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Workshop 2 breakout feedback, recorded in facilitator field notes, stated "There were too many learning activities provided by the haptics software" for students to complete in the available time. The discussion attributes this to prior use of 'off the shelf' software activities not informed by curricular discussion.

> "There were too many learning activities provided by the haptics software and dental students would be unable to complete all the tasks in the required time nor should they be expected to do all of them."
