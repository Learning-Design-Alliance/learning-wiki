## Evidence

### Adam Raabe 2024

Adam Raabe, Jessica Reynolds, Akshitha Kukudala, and Huthaifa Ashqar. (2024). The Effect of Funding on Student Achievement: Evidence from District of Columbia, Virginia, and Maryland. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.03147](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.03147)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regression of graduation rates on the estimated percent of the community with three or more risk factors, using 147 counties in 2019; the article reports no assumption violations but a weaker fit due to data spread.

> "there is a statistically significant coefficient where for each percentage point increase in the population with three or more risk factors, there is a predicted drop in graduation rate of 0.25 percentage points (R2 = 0.0875, F(1, 146)=14.01, p<0.001)"
