Evidence¶

Pyc 2007¶

Pyc, M. A., & Rawson, K. A. (2007). Examining the efficiency of schedules of distributed retrieval practice. Memory & Cognition, 35(8), 1917-1927. https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/BF03192925

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 1 mixed-factor ANOVA showed a main effect of activity, F(1,156) = 767.03, p < .001; test–restudy items had significantly higher final test performance than study-only items, replicating prior research with study-only controls.

"The finding that test–restudy items had significantly higher performance than did study-only items is not surprising and replicates previous research that has used study-only control conditions"

Pyc 2007 (2)¶

Pyc, M. A., & Rawson, K. A. (2007). Examining the efficiency of schedules of distributed retrieval practice. Memory & Cognition, 35(8), 1917-1927. https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/BF03192925

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 2 mixed-factor ANOVA, F(1,63) = 96.2, p < .001; schedule main effect and interaction were not significant, F(2,63) = 2.69, p = .076 and F(2,63) = 2.06, p = .137; an ANCOVA using single-condition performance as covariate also showed no group difference, F(2,62) = 2.03, p = .14.

"indicating that repeated test-restudy trials resulted in significantly higher final test performance than did a single test-restudy trial"
