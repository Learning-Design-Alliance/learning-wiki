Evidence¶

Michaela Minárechová 2016¶

Michaela Minárechová. (2016). Using a concept cartoon© method to address elementary school students' ideas about natural phenomena. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, Vol. 4, No. 2, 214-228. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1107824

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Analysis of worksheets from three students selected for full attendance, coding four worksheet items with the five analytical categories. "the transformation of students´ initially imperfect ideas towards its improved versions" was found "at all three students", and statements drew on realized practical verifications.

"When we compared the worksheets to posttest we found out the transformation of students´ initially imperfect ideas towards its improved versions in some of their answers (at all three students). It was especially in topics of thermal conductivity and density of matters."
