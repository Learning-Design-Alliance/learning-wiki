## Evidence

### Abdullah 2014

Abdullah, G., Rossy, D., Ploeg, J., Davies, B., Higuchi, K., Sikora, L., & Stacey, D. (2014). Measuring the Effectiveness of Mentoring as a Knowledge Translation Intervention for Implementing Empirical Evidence: A Systematic Review. Worldviews on Evidence-Based Nursing, 11(5), 284-300. https://doi.org/10.1111/wvn.12060

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports contrasts against no intervention from a controlled before-and-after study (Wallen et al., 2010, 159 nurse leaders) and a pre/post study (Mariano et al., 2009, 20 nurses); beliefs in EBP increased in one and showed no difference in the other.

> "Compared to no intervention, nurses exposed to mentoring as part of a multifaceted intervention had increased beliefs in EBP (M = 57.2%–62.6% vs. 58.0%–58.2%, p = .025; Wallen et al., 2010) or no difference (Mariano et al., 2009)."

### Abdullah 2014 (2)

Abdullah, G., Rossy, D., Ploeg, J., Davies, B., Higuchi, K., Sikora, L., & Stacey, D. (2014). Measuring the Effectiveness of Mentoring as a Knowledge Translation Intervention for Implementing Empirical Evidence: A Systematic Review. Worldviews on Evidence-Based Nursing, 11(5), 284-300. https://doi.org/10.1111/wvn.12060

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports null contrasts against no intervention for nurses' uptake of nonguideline-based research evidence in two studies (Mariano et al., 2009; Wallen et al., 2010), and no difference in retention in Wallen et al. (2010); equivalence was not tested.

> "Two studies reported no difference between groups of nurses’ uptake of nonguideline-based research evidence into clinical practice (Mariano et al., 2009; Wallen et al., 2010)."

### Abdullah 2014 (3)

Abdullah, G., Rossy, D., Ploeg, J., Davies, B., Higuchi, K., Sikora, L., & Stacey, D. (2014). Measuring the Effectiveness of Mentoring as a Knowledge Translation Intervention for Implementing Empirical Evidence: A Systematic Review. Worldviews on Evidence-Based Nursing, 11(5), 284-300. https://doi.org/10.1111/wvn.12060

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Pre/post test study (Morgan, 2012) in a community hospital with 15 nurses implementing a pressure ulcer guideline; the review reports a 5% reduction in the prevalence of hospital acquired pressure ulcers, a descriptive pre/post change with no printed effect size.

> "Nurses’ participation in mentoring as part of a multifaceted intervention also led to a 5% reduction in the prevalence of hospital acquired pressure ulcers (Morgan, 2012)."
