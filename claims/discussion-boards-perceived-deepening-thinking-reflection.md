### Mącznik Aleksandra K. 2015

Mącznik Aleksandra K., Ribeiro Daniel Cury, Baxter G. David. (2015). Online technology use in physiotherapy teaching and learning: a systematic review of effectiveness and users’ perceptions. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4587774/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review synthesis of three studies combining discussion boards with other technologies such as chat, website, or e-diary. Boards were perceived as "deepening the thinking and facilitating reflection, allowing for learning from multiple perspectives."

> "Discussion boards were perceived as deepening the thinking and facilitating reflection, allowing for learning from multiple perspectives, and facilitating communication and support. Also, discussion boards allowed students to ‘collect their thoughts before responding’ while ‘not put on the spot’ [41]."
