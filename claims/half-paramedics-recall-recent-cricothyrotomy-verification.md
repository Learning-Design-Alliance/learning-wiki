Furin Molly MD, MS, Kohn Melissa MD, MS, Overberger Ryan DO, MS, Jaslow David MD, MPH. (2016). Out-of-Hospital Surgical Airway Management: Does Scope of Practice Equal Actual Practice? Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2016.3.28729

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on skills verification administered to 86 paramedics during annual medical-command reauthorization skills reviews; only the 80 respondents able to recall were characterized. The article reports "Half of the subjects who were able to recall (40/80, 50% 95% CI [39–61%]) reported having proficiency verification for cricothyrotomy within the past five years." Annual medical-director skills testing is required, but the article notes there is no uniform verification process.
