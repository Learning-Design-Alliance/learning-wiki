## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2018

Michael C. Wittmann. (2018). Research in the Resources Framework: Changing environments, consistent exploration. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/revperv2.5.5](https://doi.org/10.1119/revperv2.5.5)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video and transcript analysis of a single conversation between two students in a sophomore mechanics class solving an integral with under-specified initial conditions; one student used undetermined constants, the other limits of integration, and both methods were correct.

> "we regularly observed that students using either integration method used “compute anti-derivative” and “extract boundary conditions,” but we observed different resources in what they did with the boundary conditions, either “add constant” and “find value” or “choose limits” and “apply limits.”"
