## Evidence

### Siegler 1998

Siegler, R. S., & Stern, E. (1998). Conscious and unconscious strategy discoveries: A microgenetic analysis. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 127(4), 377-397. https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.127.4.377

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison in the eight-session experiment (31 children randomly assigned after pretest). The Results section reports the shortcut-use difference with "t(29) = 4.42, p < .001"; the unconscious shortcut difference was also significant (t(29) = 3.97, p < .001). No effect size printed.

> "children in the blocked condition used the most advanced strategy, the shortcut strategy, more often than did children in the mixed condition, t(29) = 4.42, p < .001."
