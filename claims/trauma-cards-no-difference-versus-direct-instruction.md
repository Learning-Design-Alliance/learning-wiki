## Evidence

### Nilsson Abraham 2015

Nilsson Abraham, Åslund Kristian, Lampi Maria, Nilsson Helene, Jonson Carl-Oscar. (2015). Improved and sustained triage skills in firemen after a short training intervention. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. [doi:10.1186/s13049-015-0162-7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-015-0162-7)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of improvement scores between 40 trauma card participants and 46 direct instruction participants. Median improvement was 1.5 [0–3] versus 2 [0–3.5]; "This difference was not significant (P > 0.05)".

> "There were 40 participants in the trauma card session and 46 participants in the direct instruction session. Triage improvement score (post-test score—pretest score) for the trauma card group was 1.5 [0–3] compared with 2 [0–3.5] for the direct instruction group. This difference was not significant (P > 0.05)."
