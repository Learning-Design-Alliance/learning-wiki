## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Marija Cubric 2015

Marija Cubric, and Amanda Jefferies. (2015). The benefits and challenges of large-scale deployment of electronic voting systems: University student views from across different subject groups. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.04.004

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Non-parametric comparison across three types of EVS use (non-summative, combination, summative-only) among 590 students. Mann-Whitney follow-up tests with Bonferroni correction confirmed summative-only use was associated with lower learning benefits and higher challenges.

> "The Kruskal-Wallis test revealed that “learning benefits” and “challenges” (1st and 2nd component in Table 5) were both significantly affected by the type of use (Table 8) where H(2) =27.23, p< 0.001 and H(2)= 10.09, p<0.01 respectively."
