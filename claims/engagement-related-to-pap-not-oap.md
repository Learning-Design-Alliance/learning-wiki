How Digital Hoarding Is Related to the Academic Performance of University Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22081186

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Structural model hypothesis assessment in the survey of 327 university students found engagement related only to the PAP measure, with the OAP path significant only at the 10% level. No standardized effect size for these paths is printed in the provided text.

> "Academic engagement is related only to auto-perceived academic performance (H1a), but not to auto-perceived score (H1b). However, if we adjust the significance level to 10%, academic engagement is positively associated with academic performance."
