## Evidence

### Rivière E 2025

Rivière E, Der Sahakian G, Tremblay M-L, Chiniara G. (2025). Development and validation of the QASSH scale: a tool for assessing the quality of simulation scenarios in healthcare education. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2486971

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Within-variant comparisons of mean scores between the 12 experienced raters involved in QASSH development and the 23 naive raters, using Student's t-test. Differences appeared only for "the threshold scenario in SP" and "the high-quality scenario in immersive simulation"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Student’s t-test for the differences between experienced and naive raters within each scenario in each variant of QASSH showed no statistical differences, except for the threshold scenario in SP (95% CI 0.431–0.125) and the high-quality scenario in immersive simulation (95% CI 0.132–0.006)."
