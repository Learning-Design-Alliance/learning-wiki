## Evidence

### Li Ting 2024

Li Ting, Song Ruohong, Zhong Wenjie, Liao Wenao, Hu Jiang, Liu Xilin, Wang Fei. (2024). Use of problem-based learning in orthopaedics education: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05244-1

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 10 RCTs (n=711) reporting dichotomous interest outcomes, fixed effects model, no heterogeneity (I2 =0%). Pooled odds ratio OR=4.70 (95% CI: 3.20~6.93, P<0.00001), a large effect favouring PBL.

> "A total of 10 [14, 22, 25, 37, 45, 48, 55–57, 60] studies (n=711) reported interest in teaching. There was no heterogeneity (P=0.82, I2 =0%); therefore, a fixed effects model was used. This meta-analysis examined dichotomous outcomes and revealed that the PBL teaching group reported higher interest in teaching than the traditional teaching group (OR=4.70, 95% CI: 3.20~6.93, P<0.00001; Fig. 7)."

### Li Ting 2024 (2)

Li Ting, Song Ruohong, Zhong Wenjie, Liao Wenao, Hu Jiang, Liu Xilin, Wang Fei. (2024). Use of problem-based learning in orthopaedics education: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05244-1

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 16 RCTs (N=1380), fixed effects model (I2 =33%). Pooled odds ratio OR=5.43 (95% CI: 3.83~7.69, P<0.00001), a large effect favouring PBL.

> "A total of 16 [13–15, 17, 18, 21–24, 28, 37, 38, 42, 43, 45, 49] studies (N=1380) reported teaching satisfaction. There was no significant heterogeneity (P=0.10, I2 =33%); therefore, a fixed effects model was used. Our study demonstrated that PBL teaching yielded higher levels of teaching satisfaction than traditional teaching (OR=5.43, 95% CI: 3.83~7.69, P<0.00001; Fig. 8)."
