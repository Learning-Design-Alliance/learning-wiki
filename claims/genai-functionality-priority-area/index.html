Thematic analysis of qualitative responses from 166 capstone business students identified three priority areas, the first being "understanding and optimising GenAI functionality". This is a qualitative descriptive finding with no effect size reported.

"students identifying three priority areas for development: (1) understanding and optimising GenAI functionality; (2) exploring GenAI applications across contexts; (3) navigating ethical and legal dimensions of GenAI"
