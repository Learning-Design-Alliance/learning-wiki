## Evidence

### Becker Erin A. 2017

Becker Erin A., Easlon Erin J., Potter Sarah C., Guzman-Alvarez Alberto, Spear Jensen M., Facciotti Marc T., Igo Michele M., Singer Mitchell, Pagliarulo Christopher. (2017). The Effects of Practice-Based Training on Graduate Teaching Assistants' Classroom Practices. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar58. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-05-0162

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post-feedback comparison of technique-frequency change among GTAs receiving coaching, appreciation, or no feedback, using Wilcoxon rank-sum tests on four quantifiable techniques; only p-values are printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "a significant positive relationship between coaching feedback and technique frequency was found for right is right (p = 0.04) and circulate (active; i.e., GTA-initiated small-group contact; p = 0.03; Figure 3)"

### Becker Erin A. 2017 (2)

Becker Erin A., Easlon Erin J., Potter Sarah C., Guzman-Alvarez Alberto, Spear Jensen M., Facciotti Marc T., Igo Michele M., Singer Mitchell, Pagliarulo Christopher. (2017). The Effects of Practice-Based Training on Graduate Teaching Assistants' Classroom Practices. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar58. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-05-0162

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same feedback analysis: cold-call frequency showed "no significant change in use of cold call ... regardless of the type of feedback provided"; the article also reports a negative relationship between appreciation feedback and stretch it: explain logic (p = 0.009).

> "However, no significant relationship was found between appreciation feedback and implementation frequency for other techniques. In contrast to the other three techniques investigated, no significant change in use of cold call was observed regardless of the type of feedback provided."
