## Evidence

### Edwards 2025

Edwards, R. J., Yeates, P., Lefroy, J., & McKinley, R. (2025). Understanding contexts and mechanisms through which video based benchmarking promotes alignment of examiners' scoring in objective structured clinical exams. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10454-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Realist interview study of 16 OSCE examiners (6 experienced, 5 novice, 5 intermediate) at one medical school; participants prepared with written station information then were interviewed before benchmarking. The article reports that "baseline expectations remained uncertain" despite preparation.

> "Despite their preparation, participants' baseline expectations remained uncertain because they were derived from an abstract description of performance which requires experience of judging student performances on the station to clarify its interpretation"
