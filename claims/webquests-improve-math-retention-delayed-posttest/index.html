Yenmez 2017¶

Yenmez, A. A., Özpinar, İ., & Gökçe, S. (2017). Use of WebQuests in Mathematics Instruction: Academic Achievement, Teacher and Student Opinions. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(9). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050913

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experiment with 39 experimental and 39 control ninth-graders; the same teacher taught both groups and only the experimental group solved four WebQuests. The delayed posttest difference favored the experimental group, described as "in favor of the experimental group" (t(76)=2.322, p=.023).
