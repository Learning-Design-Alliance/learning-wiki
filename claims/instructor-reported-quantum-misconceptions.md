### Shayan Majidy 2024

Shayan Majidy. (2024). Addressing misconceptions in university physics: A review and experiences from quantum physics educators. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.20923](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.20923)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interviews with 12 quantum physics instructors who collectively taught over 100 quantum courses; instructors reported that some students interpret entanglement as faster-than-light communication, with the belief that "a controllable local action on system A could instantly send a message to its entangled partner." Accounts describe perceived difficulties, not measured prevalence.
