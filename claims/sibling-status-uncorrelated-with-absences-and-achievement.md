`q2 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Partial correlation analysis of the full district sample versus the sibling sample. The article reports correlations between being in a family with siblings and reading and math SAT9 test performance are ⫺0.01 and ⫺0.01, respectively, and that across all variable categories the correlation values are extremely small.

> "Note that the correlation between having a sibling and total days absent is 0.04. Hence, there is no meaningful systematic relationship between being in a household of siblings and being absent."
