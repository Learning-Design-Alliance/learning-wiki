### Rodriguez 2025

Rodriguez, J. M. P., Austria, G. S., & Millar, G. B. (2025). The Role of AI, Blockchain, Cloud, and Data (ABCD) in Enhancing Learning Assessments of College Students. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.5321233](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5321233)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis (Table 5) in a survey of 383 university students in the Philippine NCR using stratified sampling. All three Pearson correlations were negligible: "Pearson's r values are extremely close to zero ( -0.006, 0.035, and 0.022)" with p-values above 0.05, so the null hypothesis was accepted.

> "Pearson's r values are extremely close to zero ( -0.006, 0.035, and 0.022), while the p -values (0.911, 0.495, and 0.667) are significantly higher than the universal signi ficance level of 0.05, and hence the null hypothesis (Ho) is accepted."
