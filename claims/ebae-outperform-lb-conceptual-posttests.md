## Evidence

### Nafis I. Karim 2018

Nafis I. Karim, Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Impact of evidence-based flipped or active-engagement non-flipped courses on student performance in introductory physics. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2017-0171](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2017-0171)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Inter-group comparison of pooled algebra-based physics I courses at the University of Pittsburgh using t-tests and Cohen's d on FCI data. The article reports effect sizes of "0.314 (same instructor teaching both courses in the same semester) and 0.233" with no statistically significant pretest difference (p = 0.831 and 0.901).

> "the effect sizes for comparing FCI posttest  performance of students in EBAE courses with students in LB courses are 0.314 (same instructor teaching both courses in the same semester) and 0.233 when different courses using similar methods are combined"

### Nafis I. Karim 2018 (2)

Nafis I. Karim, Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Impact of evidence-based flipped or active-engagement non-flipped courses on student performance in introductory physics. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2017-0171](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2017-0171)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Inter-group comparison of pooled calculus-based physics II courses using t-tests and Cohen's d on CSEM data. The article reports effect sizes of "0.357 (same instructor teaching both courses) and 0.494" with no statistically significant pretest difference (p = 0.895 and 0.886).

> "Table IV also shows that the effect sizes for comparing CSEM posttest  performance of students in EBAE courses with students in LB courses are 0.357 (same instructor teaching both courses) and 0.494 when different courses using similar methods are combined (which are considered medium effect sizes)."
