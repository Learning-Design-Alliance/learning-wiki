Related Claims¶

  • The Terman (Stanford Revision) vocabulary test words show an order of difficulty for kindergarten and Grade I children that is noticeably different from the order indicated on the test blank. — related
  • The first four Terman vocabulary words appear in the same order for the kindergarten and IB subgroup as for the IA children, and the first 13 words for the two groups are the same words though in different order. — related
  • For very young children, more abstract vocabulary terms such as afloat, haste, mellow, noticeable, outward and skill are relatively more difficult, while more concrete terms such as dungeon, envelope, eyelash, nerve, plumbing, pork and snip are learned earlier. — related
