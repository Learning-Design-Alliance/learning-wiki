## Evidence

### Stochlinski Caroline 2026

Stochlinski Caroline, Maleczek Mathias, Korn Lukas, Gleiss Andreas, Breckwoldt Jan, Roessler Bernhard. (2026). Skill retention of advanced airway techniques after simulation training – a randomized prospective study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08646-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized prospective trial: 44 anaesthesiology residents, group A (self-made model) vs group B (SimMan 3G), final evaluation on the SimMan 3G for both. The mean difference was "− 0.56 points", CI lower limit "− 1.31 points", above the −2-point margin. A worst-case analysis imputing missing values also confirmed non-inferiority (mean difference −0.64, CI −1.39).

> "The mean difference in scores between the self-made model and the conventional training model was − 0.56 points, with the self-made model achieving fewer points. The lower limit of the one-sided 97.5% confidence interval for the mean difference was − 1.31 points."
