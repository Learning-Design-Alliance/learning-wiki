### Mainwaring Elizabeth M. A. 2026

Mainwaring Elizabeth M. A., Guidozzi Nadia, James Jonnie P., Pantling Jai, Menon Nainika, Salem Riadh, Markar Sheraz R. (2026). Objective technical skills assessment in laparoscopic cholecystectomy: a systematic review of manual, kinematic, and AI-based tools. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-13309-z

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's own synthesis of 60 included studies (41 manual, 6 kinematic, 13 AI), grouped by modality and rated across Messick's six validity domains, concluded that "no true gold standard currently exists" for LC skill assessment. The synthesis was narrative, as heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis.

> "No single tool demonstrated comprehensive validity evidence across all Messick domains and therefore, no true gold standard currently exists."
