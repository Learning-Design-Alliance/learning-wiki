# Students given more control spent significantly more weekly time on listening practice, indicating greater effort and autonomy

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Experimental-group students' average weekly out-of-class listening practice rose from 1.83 hours before the experiment to 2.79 hours during it, a significant paired difference (Sig. 2-tailed 0.000). [→ Wang 2010](#wang-2010)

## Evidence

### Wang 2010

Yushan Wang. (2010). To Give Control to Learners or Not? A Comparative Study of Two Ways of Teaching Listening. English Language Teaching, Vol. 3, No. 2. http://www.ccsenet.org/elt

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples T-test on self-reported weekly listening practice time of the experimental group (N=51): Time 1 mean 1.8382, Time 2 mean 2.7941, t = -3.942, df = 50, Sig. (2-tailed) .000. Time 1 came from a questionnaire, Time 2 from learner diaries.

> "From Table 4.3.1 we can see that before the experiment, the average time of the subjects of the experimental group spent on the listening practice is 1.83 hours per week and during the experiment 2.79 hours per week."
