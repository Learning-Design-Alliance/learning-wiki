# Correlation between student and instructor overall poster ratings improved after the intervention (peer r = 0.51 to 0.68; self r = 0.18 to 0.37)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The peer–instructor correlation for overall poster quality rose from r = 0.51 to r = 0.68 after the intervention. [→ Saterbak Ann 2018](#saterbak-ann-2018)
`q3 i3` The self–instructor correlation rose from r = 0.18 (no correlation) to r = 0.37 (weak correlation). [→ Saterbak Ann 2018 (2)](#saterbak-ann-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Saterbak Ann 2018

Saterbak Ann, Moturu Anoosha, Volz Tracy. (2018). Using a Teaching Intervention and Calibrated Peer Review™ Diagnostics to Improve Visual Communication Skills. Annals of Biomedical Engineering. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10439-017-1946-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Linear regression of averaged peer scores against instructor scores on Statement 14 (1–10 scale) for standard and intervention conditions; "the correlation improved to r = 0.68 for the intervention condition". Printed correlation r = 0.68 (i = 3).

> "For the standard condition, there was a weak correlation (r = 0.51) between the average peer score and the instructor score. As shown in Fig. 2b, the correlation improved to r = 0.68 for the intervention condition."

### Saterbak Ann 2018 (2)

Saterbak Ann, Moturu Anoosha, Volz Tracy. (2018). Using a Teaching Intervention and Calibrated Peer Review™ Diagnostics to Improve Visual Communication Skills. Annals of Biomedical Engineering. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10439-017-1946-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Regression of single self-evaluation scores against instructor scores on Statement 14 for both conditions; "the r value rose to 0.37, suggesting a weak correlation". Printed correlation r = 0.37 (i = 3).

> "For the standard condition, there was no correlation between self- and instructor evaluation (r = 0.18). After the intervention, the r value rose to 0.37, suggesting a weak correlation."
