## Evidence

### Okulu 2018

Okulu, H. Z., & Oğuz Ünver, A. (2018). The Process of Facilitating Knowledge Acquisition and Retention: An Inquiry into Magnetic Poles with Challenging Questions. International Education Studies, 11(5), 25-37. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n5p25

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Concept-frequency analysis of pretest, posttest, and retention test responses of 65 third-year pre-service teachers, comparing concepts used to define magnetic pole before and after the sequential inquiry implementation. The study reports that the misconception of positive (+) and negative (-) was used much less after implementation, and concepts such as end, charge, and polarization were no longer included in participant expressions.

> "This is an indication that the frequency of use of misconceptions associated with the magnetic pole was reduced and the used concepts were oriented towards the basic concepts of magnetism."
