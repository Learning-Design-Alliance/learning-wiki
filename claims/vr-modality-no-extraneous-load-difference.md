## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Patrick Albus 2023

Patrick Albus, Tina Seufert. (2023). The modality effect reverses in a virtual reality learning environment and influences cognitive load. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09611-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on extraneous cognitive load in the VR modality experiment, controlling for immersion, which had a significant impact on ECL (ηp² = 0.18). No significant condition difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "we found no significant difference between the conditions for ECL (H5: F(1,58) = 0.73, p = .39, ηp2 = 0.012)"
