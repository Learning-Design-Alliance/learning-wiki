## Evidence

### Woolf K 2016

Woolf K, Rich A, Viney R, Needleman S, Griffin A. (2016). Perceived causes of differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical training: a national qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013429

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the 137-participant qualitative dataset found IMG trainees felt cultural difficulties affected relationships "with colleagues more than with patients".

> "Cultural differences could impede good educational relationships for IMGs. It was generally agreed that IMGs who found it difficult to adapt to UK patient-centred care and who—even if they spoke English as a first language—struggled with colloquialisms, would struggle with colleagues."

### Woolf K 2016 (2)

Woolf K, Rich A, Viney R, Needleman S, Griffin A. (2016). Perceived causes of differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical training: a national qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013429

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The peer-support subtheme reported that IMGs sought trusted support from other IMGs, while UKGs organised peer networks "something they felt IMGs missed".

> "many IMGs said they particularly valued the opportunity to meet other IMGs who could be trusted to understand and not to judge, and described supporting junior IMG colleagues."
