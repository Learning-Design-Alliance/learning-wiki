Exploratory comparison of NASA TLX global change scores in the randomized trial (0–100 visual scale): control "a reduction of -3.0 (95% CI: -8.2 to 2.3)", intervention an increase of 1.3; "not statistically significant (p = 0.254)". No effect size printed.

> "measured using the NASA TLX, showed a reduction of -3.0 (95% CI: -8.2 to 2.3) in the control group, compared to an increase of 1.3 (95% CI: -4.0 to 6.5) in the intervention group. The between-group difference in change was not statistically significant (p = 0.254)."
