### Wilcox 2019

Wilcox, B. R., & Corsiglia, G. (2019). A cross-context look at upper-division student difficulties with integration. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

On a scalar potential exam follow-up asking about large-r behavior, 72% of students correctly stated the 1/r fall-off. The most common alternative answer (17%) was that the potential "goes to zero" without specifying how.
