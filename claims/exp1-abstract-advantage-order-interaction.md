## Evidence

### Amy Bennett 2018

Amy Bennett, Matthew Inglis and Camilla Gilmore. (2018). The Cost of Multiple Representations: Learning Number Symbols with Abstract and Concrete Representations. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000318

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

A 2x2 ANCOVA on Experiment 1 accuracy (condition within-subjects, order between-subjects, age covariate) found "a significant condition by order interaction effect" with partial η² = .095. For the concrete-first group, abstract scores were significantly higher, t(35) = 3.361, p = .002.

> "This revealed a significant condition by order interaction effect, F(1, 71) = 7.43, p = .008, p2 = .095"
