Evidence¶

Xenia Vamvakoussi 2004¶

Xenia Vamvakoussi, Stella Vosniadou. (2004). Understanding the structure of the set of rational numbers: a conceptual change approach. Learning and Instruction. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00190233

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The abstract states this point based on the reported empirical study of ninth graders, which distinguished aspects supported by prior natural-number knowledge (algebraic properties) from aspects constrained by discreteness presuppositions (density).

"We make the point that understanding rational numbers is not indiscriminately difficult"
