Burns 2021¶

Burns, D., Espinoza, D., Ondrasek, N., & Yang, M. (2021). Students experiencing homelessness: The conditions and outcomes of homelessness among California students. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/students-experiencing-homelessness

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Regression analysis of 2015–16 California student-level and school-level data (Tables 2 and 3) models predictors of CAASPP ELA and mathematics achievement. The report reports this "negative association holds both for all students and those experiencing homelessness". No effect size or coefficient value is printed in the available text.

"Schools with high teacher turnover and large proportions of underprepared teachers are associated with lower student achievement, even after accounting for other student- and school-level factors. This negative association holds both for all students and those experiencing homelessness."
