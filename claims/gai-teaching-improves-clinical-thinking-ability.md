## Evidence

### Ma Xingming 2026

Ma Xingming, Liu Xianting, Sun Haojie. (2026). The effectiveness of GAI-assisted teaching methods in medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1813108

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 14 continuous-variable studies (I2 = 85%) and fixed-effects meta-analysis of four dichotomous-variable studies (I2 = 18%) reported in the clinical thinking ability section; the latter gave "OR = 10.97, 95% CI: 4.93–24.40, p < 0.00001".

> "The results of the meta-analysis showed a significant improvement in clinical thinking ability in the experimental group compared with the control group (SMD = 1.18, 95% CI: 0.86–1.50, p < 0.00001)."
