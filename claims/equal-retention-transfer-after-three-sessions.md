### Van Brakel-Van Lobenstein 2025

Van Brakel-Van Lobenstein, N., Van Leeuwen-Prins, S. T., Verdoes, L., De Waal, G. M., Kamsteeg, M. C. J., Oudejans, R. R. D., Devereaux, J., van Zaalen, Y., Dikken, J., & Renden, P. G. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study 2 assessed 52 first-year nursing students (12 male, 40 female) after three training sessions, using a retention test and a dual-task transfer test with Kikuhime pressure measurement. ANOVAs, t-tests and chi-square tests found no significant group differences; equivalence was not tested.

> "The ANOVAs on the variables Pressure of the bandage, Deviation from the range 40–60 mmHg and Performance time revealed no significant differences between groups."
