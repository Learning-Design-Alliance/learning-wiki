Mahoney B, Luebbert E. (2021). Updates on Simulation in Obstetrical Anesthesiology Through the COVID-19 Pandemic. Anesthesiology Clinics. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anclin.2021.08.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Kiwalabye and colleagues assessed preparedness of anesthesia interns in managing a failed obstetric intubation after their anesthesiology rotation using simulated scenarios. The review reports "a pass rate of only 40%"; no effect size is printed.

> "They observed a pass rate of only 40% despite prior exposure to an Essential Steps in Managing Obstetric Emergencies training module, leading them to propose that this gap in skill acquisition discovered by simulated scenarios can be remedied through the use of simulation-based education during their training."
