## Evidence

### Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah 2013

Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah. (2013). The Impact of Using SMS as Learning Support Tool on Students' Learning. International Education Studies, 6(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n10p112

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-samples t-test for the 23-student control section receiving only face-to-face instruction; means rose from 15.21 to 69.34 with "t (22) = -27.35, p = .00".

> "the mean of the pretest scores (M = 15.21, SD = 6.12) was significantly different than the mean of the posttest scores (M = 69.34, SD = 13.50), t (22) = -27.35, p = .00."

### Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah 2013 (2)

Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah. (2013). The Impact of Using SMS as Learning Support Tool on Students' Learning. International Education Studies, 6(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n10p112

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-samples t-test for the 29-student SMS-supported experimental section; means rose from 15.69 to 77.93 with "t (28) = -46.91, p = .00".

> "for the experimental group, the mean of the pretest scores (M = 15.69, SD = 6.23) for the participants was significantly  different than the mean of the posttest scores (M = 77.93, SD = 9.86), t (28) = -46.91, p = .00."
