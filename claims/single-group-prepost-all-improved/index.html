Review synthesis of 39 single-group pre/post studies of distance simulation; the review reports all showed improvement in self-assessment (n = 13), knowledge (n = 3), or skills acquisition (n = 23).

"Thirty-nine studies used a single-group pre/postdesign, and all showed an improvement after distance simulation training either in self-assessment (n = 13), knowledge (n = 3), or skills acquisition (n = 23)."
