## Evidence

### Farnaz S 2020

Farnaz S, Zoghoul S, Alahmad M, Al Qudah H. (2020). The influence of gender on clinical examination skills of medical students in Jordan: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02002-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey analysis of opposite-sex examination frequency in 188 Jordanian final-year medical students. The abstract results state "females were significantly more likely to examine male patients than vice versa" with the printed odds ratios; the Discussion attributes this to male patients being more comfortable with chest or abdominal exposure.

> "females were significantly more likely to examine male patients than vice versa (0.49 (0.27–0.88) for cardiovascular and 0.39 (0.21–0.71) for respiratory examinations)"
