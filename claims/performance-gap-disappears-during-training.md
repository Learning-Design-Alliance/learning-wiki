Creutzfeldt Johan, Hedman Leif, Felländer-Tsai Li. (2012). Effects of pre-training using serious game technology on CPR performance – an exploratory quasi-experimental transfer study. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-20-79

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Discussion of the quasi-experimental transfer study, in which each team trained in three scenarios between two assessment scenarios. The authors report that "the performance gap in the last scenario had disappeared" after five scenarios.

> "After having trained in 5 scenarios, the knowledge gap in CPR related knowledge was not detectable, and the performance gap in the last scenario had disappeared."
