## Evidence

### Lassen 2021

Lassen, I. C., Arielle-Evans, A. A., Ríos, L., Lewandowski, H. J., & Dounas-Frazer, D. R. (2021). Student ownership and understanding of multi-week final projects. [doi:10.1119/perc.2021.pr.lassen](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2021.pr.lassen)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Emergent thematic analysis of 15 life-grid interviews with students from upper-division physics lab courses, coded with an a priori ownership-as-relationship scheme. In the choice phase, four students described gaining "DOMAIN knowledge" and five described gaining "MANAGERIAL knowledge"; no effect size is reported for this qualitative finding.

> "In total, nine participants each discussed new understand- ing in the CHOICE phase once or twice during their inter- views. Of these, four described gaining DOMAIN knowledge, and ﬁve students described gaining MANAGERIAL knowl- edge."
