## Evidence

### Kuang H 2026

Kuang H, Li J and Weng Y (2026) Student-AI interaction in computer-assisted consecutive interpreting: patterns and performance. Front. Psychol. 17:1890808. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1890808

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison of cross-stage pattern consistency between 11 trained and 11 untrained trainees, recruited from a 16-week compulsory CACI course versus three institutions without such training. Descriptive percentages only; no effect size printed.

> "Group A (trained students) demonstrated a higher consistency rate (66.7%) than Group B (untrained students, 50.0%)."

### Kuang H 2026 (2)

Kuang H, Li J and Weng Y (2026) Student-AI interaction in computer-assisted consecutive interpreting: patterns and performance. Front. Psychol. 17:1890808. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1890808

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive distribution of cluster membership by group and stage, reported in the results section with Figure 4. The article notes task mode and interpreting direction had little impact on clustering results.

> "Group A dominated Cluster 1 (Intensive Engagers), with 20% later transitioning to Cluster 2 (Fast Scanners) during output. Group B initially distributed across Cluster 2 (Fast Scanners) and Cluster 3 (Traditionalists), with Fast Scanners remaining stable but Traditionalists transitioning widely."
