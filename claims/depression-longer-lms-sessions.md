## Evidence

### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026

Arya VarastehNezhad, Fattaneh Taghiyareh. (2026). Behavioral and Performance Indicators of Depression and Anxiety in Electronic Learning Systems. Department of Computer Engineering, University of Tehran. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254)

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Session-based analysis of Group 2 (37 students), sessions defined with an 8-hour inactivity threshold: "longer maximum sessions" among depressed students (67,099 vs 40,979, d=1.072), with Spearman correlations of 0.498 (BDI-II score) and 0.495 (depression status).

> "Depressed students in this group had substantially longer maximum sessions than non-depressed students, with mean values of 67,099 versus 40,979, corresponding to a mean difference of 26,1 20 and a percentage difference of 63.74% (p=0.0029, d=1.072)."

### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026 (2)

Arya VarastehNezhad, Fattaneh Taghiyareh. (2026). Behavioral and Performance Indicators of Depression and Anxiety in Electronic Learning Systems. Department of Computer Engineering, University of Tehran. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254)

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Same Group 2 session analysis: greater total session duration (227,377 vs 137,559, d=0.938) and higher average session duration (7,461.87 vs 5,027.64, d=0.914) among depressed students; depression status correlated with average session duration (ρ=0.392, p=0.0165).

> "Depressed students in Group 2 also had greater total session duration than non-depressed students, with mean values of 227,377 versus 137,559, a mean difference of 89,817.51, and a percentage difference of 65.29% (p=0.0082, d=0.938)."
