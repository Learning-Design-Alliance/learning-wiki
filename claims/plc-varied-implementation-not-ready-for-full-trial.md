## Evidence

### Cinzia Rienzo 2016

Cinzia Rienzo, Heather Rolfe and David Wilkinson. (2016). Powerful Learning Conversations: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581274

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative process evaluation using a framework approach: NIESR interviews with eight staff in four intervention schools, attendance at training events, YST training surveys, and Exeter University visit reports to all nine intervention schools.

> "Interviews and observations with teachers  found the programme was implemented in different ways and with different levels of understanding in different schools ."

### Cinzia Rienzo 2016 (2)

Cinzia Rienzo, Heather Rolfe and David Wilkinson. (2016). Powerful Learning Conversations: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581274

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Process-evaluation findings on delivery barriers: absence of a resource manual, insufficient definition of the distinctive features of sports feedback, lack of time as an additional activity, limited lead-teacher authority, and reported class-control concerns.

> "Teachers were not provided with a pack of resources or manual to follow , and for some teachers this appears to have been a barr ier to delivery."
