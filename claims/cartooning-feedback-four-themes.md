## Evidence

### Bahsoun Soukaina 2026

Bahsoun Soukaina, Morris Mhairi, Akam Elizabeth. (2026). Cartooning in Biological Learning. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70040

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inductive thematic analysis (following Maguire & Delahunt and Braun & Clarke) of free-form answers to three qualitative questions. Improved learning experience accounted for 36% of responses and engagement 32%; "67% of responses to this question (equivalent to 30% of the overall free‐form responses)" cited socialization, communication and group work, and 24% of responses to the benefits question described skill development.

> "A total of 120 comments were analyzed, with Figure 9 illustrating the frequency of responses associated with each of the four themes that were identified: Improved learning experience, engagement, collaborative learning, and skill development."
