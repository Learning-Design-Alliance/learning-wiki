### Athanasopoulos 2023

Athanasopoulos, P. (2023). Cognitive dissonance or p-prims? Towards identifying the best way to overcome misconceptions in physics. Prestatyn High School, UK. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2308.15601](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2308.15601)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The article reports, citing Sadler et al. (2013), a large-scale study of 9,556 students of 181 middle school physical science teachers in the USA. The article itself cautions the correlation is not proof of causation and an intermediate variable such as general teaching skill could contribute.

> "For items that had a very popular wrong answer, the teachers who could identify this misconception had larger classroom gains, much larger than if the teachers knew only the correct answer."
