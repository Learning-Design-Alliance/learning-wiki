Jionghao Lin, Zifei Han, Danielle R. Thomas, Ashish Gurung, Shivang Gupta, Vincent Aleven, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2024). How Can I Get It Right? Using GPT to Rephrase Incorrect Trainee Responses. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00970

Mann-Whitney U tests on expert ratings for the Determining What Students Know lesson (Table 10 mean accuracy 0.70 few-shot vs 0.85 human); equivalence was not established, only no detected difference. Few-shot responsiveness was marginally higher than human (p = 0.08).
