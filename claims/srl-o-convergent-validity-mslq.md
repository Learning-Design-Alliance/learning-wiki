# SRL-O subscales show convergent validity with corresponding MSLQ scales

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` SRL-O self-efficacy correlated strongly and positively with MSLQ self-efficacy (r = .658), and SRL-O negative achievement emotion correlated positively with MSLQ test anxiety (r = .463) and negatively with most other MSLQ scales. [→ Broadbent 2023](#broadbent-2023)

## Evidence

### Broadbent 2023

Broadbent, J., Panadero, E., Lodge, J. M., & Fuller-Tyszkiewicz, M. (2023). The self-regulation for learning online (SRL-O) questionnaire. Metacognition and Learning, 18, 135–163. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09319-6

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Convergent validity analysis correlating SRL-O subscales with MSLQ subscales in the validation sample (n = 321). Table 7 reports SRL-O self-efficacy with MSLQ self-efficacy at .658 and SRL-O negative achievement emotion with MSLQ test anxiety at .463; the article states the scales showed good correspondence as expected.

> "the SRL-O self-efficacy scale had a significantly strong positive correlation with the MSLQ self-efficacy scale."
