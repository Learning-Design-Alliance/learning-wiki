## Subclaims
`q3 i?` All seven SRL sub-constructs were significantly correlated with teaching, social, and cognitive presence (0.48 < r < 0.77). [→ Chungsoo Na 2024](#chungsoo-na-2024)
`q3 i?` Splitting self-evaluation into self-evaluation with strategies and self-evaluation against peers yielded a seven-factor model with better fit than the six-factor OSLQ model. [→ Chungsoo Na 2024 (2)](#chungsoo-na-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Chungsoo Na 2024

Chungsoo Na, Soojeong Jeong, Jody Clarke-Midura, Youngin Shin. (2024). Linking self-regulated learning to community of inquiry in online undergraduate courses: A person-centered approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10380-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive correlation analysis (Table 3) of SRL and CoI sub-constructs among the 750 participants. Correlations ranged from 0.48 to 0.77, all significant at p < 0.01.

> "The sub constructs of SRL were significantly correlated to three sub-constructs of the perceptions of the CoI (0.48 < r < 0.77)."

### Chungsoo Na 2024 (2)

Chungsoo Na, Soojeong Jeong, Jody Clarke-Midura, Youngin Shin. (2024). Linking self-regulated learning to community of inquiry in online undergraduate courses: A person-centered approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10380-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of the OSLQ. Items 21–24 in the self-evaluation scale loaded differently, motivating the split into two sub-facets; the seven-factor model improved all fit indices over the six-factor model.

> "we found that the seven-factor model showed a better fit than the original six-factor model, χ2(231) = 904.82, CFI = 0.94, TLI = 0.93, RMSEA = 0.06, SRMR = 0.04."
