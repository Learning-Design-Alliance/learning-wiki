Lehto Tuomas MSc 2011¶

Lehto Tuomas MSc, Oinas-Kukkonen Harri PhD. (2011). Persuasive Features in Web-Based Alcohol and Smoking Interventions: A Systematic Review of the Literature. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3222186/

Systematic review coding of social support features across 23 RCTs using the PSD model's social support category. The review reports that "The most common means to providing social support were asynchronous peer discussion forums and synchronous chat rooms."
