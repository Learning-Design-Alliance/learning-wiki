## Evidence

### Ziadat 2016

Ziadat, A. H., & Al Ziyadat, M. T. (2016). The Effectiveness of Training Program Based on the Six Hats Model in Developing Creative Thinking Skills and Academic Achievements in the Arabic Language Course for Gifted and Talented Jordanian Students. International Education Studies, 9(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n6p150

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment with random assignment: 59 gifted 7th graders at one school were "divided randomly" into an experimental group (n = 27) and a control group (n = 32); the article does not describe how the randomisation was done. ANCOVA on Torrance B posttest controlling for pretest favored the experimental group, whose mean rose from 126.89 (pretest) to 160.56 (posttest) while the control went from 127.66 to 128.03. The article reports "the calculated (F) value reached (124.593)" with statistical significance at α ≤ 0.05.

> "the calculated (F) value reached (124.593) which is higher than the critical value of (F = 4.00). It can be concluded that there are differences among the groups in favor of the experimental group, indicating the positive impact of the designed Six Hats Training Model"
