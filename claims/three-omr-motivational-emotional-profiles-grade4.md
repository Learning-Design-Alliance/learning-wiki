# Hong Kong fourth graders' OMR self-efficacy and emotions form three distinct motivational-emotional profiles: positive, moderate, and ambivalent

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Latent profile analysis of self-efficacy and emotion indicators yielded a three-group solution (positive, moderate, ambivalent profiles). [→ Zhu 2026](#zhu-2026)
`q2 i?` The ambivalent profile combined high self-efficacy and high enjoyment with high boredom and anxiety. [→ Zhu 2026 (2)](#zhu-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Zhu 2026

Zhu, S., Yao, Y., Cheong, C. M., & Zhu, X. (2026). Latent motivational-emotional profiles and their effects on strategy use and performance in online multimodal reading tasks: A mixed-methods investigation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102386

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis of questionnaire data from 280 fourth-grade students, using three self-efficacy and three emotion indicators; the three-class solution showed entropy of .836 and was chosen over a four-class solution whose first class held too few individuals.

> "Given that the third group's entropy exceeded .80 (.836) and other fit indices were satisfactory, a three-group solution was deemed most appropriate for the analysis."

### Zhu 2026 (2)

Zhu, S., Yao, Y., Cheong, C. M., & Zhu, X. (2026). Latent motivational-emotional profiles and their effects on strategy use and performance in online multimodal reading tasks: A mixed-methods investigation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102386

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Profile characterization from the same LPA: the Positive Group (G1, N = 96, 34.29%) showed high self-efficacy with high enjoyment and low boredom and anxiety; the Moderate Group (G2, N = 111, 39.64%) showed moderate levels across all six indicators; the "Ambivalent Group (G3, N = 73, 26.07%)" showed the paradoxical pattern.

> "The Ambivalent Group (G3, N = 73, 26.07%) exhibited a paradoxical pattern: high self-efficacy and high enjoyment, but also high boredom and anxiety."
