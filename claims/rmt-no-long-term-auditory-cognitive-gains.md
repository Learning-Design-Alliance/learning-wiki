## Evidence

### Shiels 2026

Shiels, L., Tomlin, D., Payne, J.M. et al. (2026). Benefits of classroom remote microphone technology for inattentive children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00427-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Phase two behavioral assessments in a soundproof booth at baseline and end-of-trial (Table 3), covering auditory processing (LiSN-S, DDdT, FPT), attention (IVA-QS) and auditory memory (TAPS-4). No between-group coefficient reached significance; e.g., speech recognition in noise p = 0.239, full-scale attention p = 0.286, auditory STM p = 0.832.

> "Although the devices benefited listening and attention when activated, long-term classroom use did not lead to improvements in skills associated with LiD, including auditory processing, memory and attention."
