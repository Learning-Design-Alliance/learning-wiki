### Briseño-Garzón 2016

Briseño-Garzón, A., Han, A., Birol, G., Bates, S., & Whitehead, L. (2016). Faculty perceptions of challenges and enablers of effective teaching in a large research-intensive university: Preliminary findings. Collected Essays on Learning and Teaching, 9, 133-144. https://celt.uwindsor.ca/ceelt/vol9/brisenogarzon

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Open-ended survey responses from 1177 faculty across 11 faculties were coded with codes emerging from the data. Workload was the most frequently cited challenge, with 341 comments, and faculty reported spending an average of 65 hours per term preparing for class and 34 hours marking in their highest-enrolment course.

> "Workload was the number one challenge for teaching identified by participants (341 comments). Faculty indicated in their comments that as a result of competing demands, they were left with little or no time to update teaching materials, properly prepare for class, or try new teaching techniques"
