## Evidence

### Imundo 2024

Imundo, M. N., Zung, I., Whatley, M. C., & Pan, S. C. (2024). When Two Learners Are Better Than One: Using Flashcards with a Partner Improves Metacognitive Accuracy. Metacognition and Learning. https://link.springer.com/journal/11409/articles

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 allowed dropping; self-reported dropping was coded by two blind raters (κ = .99). Most Individual learners who dropped did so believing they had learned the pair, whereas all four Paired droppers cited difficulty. The authors suggest better calibration may have reduced premature dropping.

> "A Chi-square test revealed that significantly more Individual learners (53%) dropped flashcards from study than Paired learners (5%), χ2 (2) = 44.43, p < .001."
