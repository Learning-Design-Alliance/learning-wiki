`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Step 2 of the logistic regression in the 203-student survey; Table 5 prints O.R. 3.918 (p = 0.032) for changes in study context and habits. The effect did not hold once cognitive and psychopathological predictors were added.

> "In step 2, variables related to the DE experience, i.e., changes in study habits and the study environment following the pandemic, were entered; compared to students who were used to studying alone, those who were used to studying with other or in social environments had an approximately 4 times increased the likelihood of worse performance."
