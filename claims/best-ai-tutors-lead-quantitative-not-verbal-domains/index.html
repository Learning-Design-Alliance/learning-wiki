Descriptive pattern across the seven GRE domains shown in Figure 1A, with 95% confidence intervals per condition; the text states "the best AI tutors have higher mean gains than humans in three of the four domains". Descriptive means, no test printed.

"In Quantitative, pooled AI and human mean learning gains are generally close, while the best AI tutors have higher mean gains than humans in three of the four domains. In Verbal, human mean gains are close to those of the best AI tutors, but remain above the pooled AI mean in all three domains."
