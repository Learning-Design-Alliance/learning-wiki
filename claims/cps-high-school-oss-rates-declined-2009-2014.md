# Out-of-school suspension rates in CPS high schools declined each year from 24 percent in 2009-10 to 16 percent in 2013-14

Descriptive analysis of CPS administrative suspension data from 2008-09 to 2013-14, excluding charter, alternative, and special education schools. The report states "about 1-in-7 high school students (16 percent) received an out-of-school suspension" in 2013-14, down from 24 percent in 2009-10.

> "In 2013-14, about 1-in-7 high school students (16 percent) received an out-of-school suspension (OSS). This number is down from the highest point in the 2009-10 school year when about 1-in-4 high school students (24 percent) received an OSS."
