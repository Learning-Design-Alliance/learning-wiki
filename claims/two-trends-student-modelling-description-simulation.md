## Evidence

### Valdés Aguirre 2016

Valdés Aguirre, B., Ramírez Uresti, J. A., & du Boulay, B. (2016). An Analysis of Student Model Portability. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0113-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article's introduction reviews student modelling trends, contrasting one trend that stores attributes with another that builds representations that "reason about and simulate student behaviour", estimating the likelihood that the next answer will be correct from the last three answers. This is an authorial characterisation of the field, not a tested result.

> "The other focuses in building representations that can reason about and simulate student behaviour, e.g., if the student has answered the last 3 questions right, the model will estimate the likelihood of the next answer being correct."
