Dirk 2016¶

Dirk, Judith; Schmiedek, Florian. (2016). Fluctuations in elementary school children's working memory performance in the school context. The journal of educational psychology 108 (2016) 5, S. 722-739. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000076

Variance decomposition from the ambulatory assessment study of 110 Grade 3 and 4 students; the article reports the three timescales "accounted for roughly the same extent of observed day-to-day variability" with large individual differences.

"Across task conditions, day-to-day, occasion-to-occasion, and moment-to-moment variability accounted for roughly the same extent of observed day-to-day variability with large individual differences in the amount of reliable fluctuations at the different timescales."
