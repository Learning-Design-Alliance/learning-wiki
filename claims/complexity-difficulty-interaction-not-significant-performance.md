### Ines Zeitlhofer 2026

Ines Zeitlhofer, Joerg Zumbach. (2026). Germane processes moderate the effectiveness of complex desirable difficulty tasks. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09807-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the factorial MANOVA, the hypothesized amplifying interaction (Hypothesis 1.3) was "not significant on either a multivariate level" or univariate level for time and trials; no effect size beyond the printed η² values was reported.

> "However, the interaction of complexity and desirable difficulty was not significant on either a multivariate level ( F (2, 127) = 2.32, p = .10; η² = 0.04; Hypothesis 1.3) or a univariate level (time: F (1, 128) = 0.67, p = .42, η² = 0.01; trials: F (1, 128) = 1.23, p = .27, η² = 0.01)."
