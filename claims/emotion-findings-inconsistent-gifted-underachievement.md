`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of emotion findings (§4.3.2). Underachievers scored significantly higher on academic anxiety items (Obergriesser & Stoeger, 2015) and were less emotionally engaged (Ritchotte et al., 2014), while Schick and Phillipson (2009) reported no significant difference in emotional control between groups.

> "The three articles that investigated emotional factors associated with gifted underachievement were less consistent in their findings, which appear dependent on the specific aspect of emotion that is being investigated."
