## Evidence

### Abdelhai R 2012

Abdelhai R, Yassin S, Ahmad MF, Fors UGH. (2012). An e-learning reproductive health module to support improved student learning and interaction: a prospective interventional study at a medical school in Egypt. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-11

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective interventional study: paired t-tests comparing pre- and post-quiz scores of 171 e-learning participants in all four modules (e.g. Measurements of Health post 86.93 ± 16.69 vs pre 58.02 ± 20.86, paired difference 28.91 ± 25.43) were "highly significant with P < 0.001" for each module.

> "The learning outcomes for group 1, as measured by improved course scores between the pre- and post-quizzes were highly significant with P < 0.001 [Table 1]."
