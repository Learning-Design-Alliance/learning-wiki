## Evidence

### Funa 2021

Funa, A. A., & Prudente, M. S. (2021). Effectiveness of problem-based learning on secondary students’ achievement in science: A meta-analysis. International Journal of Instruction, 14(4), 69-84. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2021.1445a

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis in Comprehensive Meta-Analysis version 3 across the 11 pooled studies. JHS (k = 6, n = 1047) obtained ES = .745 and SHS (k = 5, n = 375) ES = 1.015; the between-group heterogeneity test yielded p = .342, so "the JHS and the SHS share common effect sizes".

> "In addition, the heterogeneity results (Q < df; and p > .05) showed no significant difference which demonstrate that the JHS and the SHS share common effect sizes."
