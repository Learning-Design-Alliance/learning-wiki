## Evidence

### Staacks 2025

Staacks, S., Dorsel, D., Krampe, A., Hagedorn, M., Leier, E., Heinke, H., & Stampfer, C. (2025). Bluetooth sensors in phyphox with Arduino and MicroPython – Paving the way from an idea to an experiment for teachers and learners. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.09373](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.09373)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

An accompanying questionnaire in the same 36-participant lab course collected self-assessed prior experience and post-course confidence. Students rated prior experience at a mean of 1.6 and post-course confidence at a mean of 3.1 on the printed scales. These are descriptive self-report means; no effect size or significance test is printed.

> "the students assessed their previous experience with microcontrollers to be low (mean of 1.6 on a scale from 1 (for “no experience”) to 4 (for “much experience”)), but confirmed afterwards that they now felt confident to use the microcontroller from the experiment in other projects (mean of 3.1 on a scale from 1 (no) to 4 (yes)"
