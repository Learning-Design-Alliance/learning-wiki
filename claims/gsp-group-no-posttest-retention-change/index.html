Evidence¶

Kesan 2013¶

Kesan, C. & Çalıskan, S. (2013). The effect of learning geometry topics of 7th grade in primary education with dynamic Geometer's Sketchpad geometry software to success and retention. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008875

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks test comparing the experimental group's (n=21) post-test and retention test scores; the article reports no significant difference. Note the article's own sentence mislabels the group as the control group.

"there was not a significant difference between the means of post-test and retention test of the students in experimental group (z=1,900; p>0,05). This shows that the control group's academic achievement gained at the end of computer-aided geometry teaching has not changed within a month after the training."
