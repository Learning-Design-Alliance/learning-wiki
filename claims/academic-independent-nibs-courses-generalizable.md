### Fried P.J. 2021

Fried P.J., Santarnecchi E., Antal A., et al. (2021). Training in the practice of noninvasive brain stimulation: Recommendations from an IFCN committee. Clinical Neurophysiology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinph.2020.11.018

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Expert consensus recommendation from the IFCN committee's guidelines section on training courses and trainers; the article offers this as an assertion with no empirical test. The committee distinguishes industry/company-dependent workshops from academic courses and states training in a specific device can be added after academic training.

> "Academic courses provide a richer transfer of knowledge, that ideally should include hands-on training participants on various different systems to provide generalizable expertise."
