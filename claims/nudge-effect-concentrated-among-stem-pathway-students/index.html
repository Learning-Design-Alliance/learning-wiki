Evidence¶

Ross E. O'Hara 2019¶

Ross E. O'Hara, Betsy Sparrow. (2019). A Summer Nudge Campaign to Motivate Community College STEM Students to Reenroll. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419875715

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis of the randomized controlled trial (STEM pathway N = 1,971): linear probability treatment effect 0.106** (OR 1.59), control mean 0.585. The authors note STEM nudged students reenrolled "at nearly the same rate as non-STEM students, closing the observed retention gap based on program of study."

"we found significant effects of the intervention on retention among students participating in STEM, with those who received nudges reenrolling at a rate 10 percentage points higher than the control group rate of 58%."

Ross E. O'Hara 2019 (2)¶

Ross E. O'Hara, Betsy Sparrow. (2019). A Summer Nudge Campaign to Motivate Community College STEM Students to Reenroll. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419875715

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis of the same RCT (non-STEM N = 820): treatment effect −0.017 (OR 0.923), control mean 0.712, not statistically significant; equivalence was not tested.

"In fact, STEM students who received nudges reenrolled at nearly the same rate as non-STEM students, closing the observed retention gap based on program of study. No treatment impact was found for stu- dents who were not enrolled in STEM pathways."
