### Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang 2017

Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang. (2017). Comparing the efficacy of digital flashcards versus paper flashcards to improve receptive and productive L2 vocabulary. The EUROCALL Review, Volume 25, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.4995/eurocall.2017.6964

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test comparing PV gains between the PF and DF groups. The article reports no significant difference, t(50) = .24, p = .81; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, there was not a significant difference between the PV increase between the PF group (M = 3.12, SD = 3.27) and the DF group (M= 3.31, SD = 2.28), t(50), = .24, p = .81."
