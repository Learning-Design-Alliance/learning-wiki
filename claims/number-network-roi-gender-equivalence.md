# Within the number processing network, girls and boys show statistical equivalence in neural maturity and no variance differences

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the independently-defined number processing network (bilateral IPS, bilateral IFG, ACC), ROI analyses showed statistical equivalence between genders, no mean differences, and no variance differences. [→ Kersey 2019](#kersey-2019)

## Evidence

### Kersey 2019

Kersey, A. J., Csumitta, K. D., & Cantlon, J. F. (2019). Gender similarities in the brain during mathematics development. npj Science of Learning 4:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0057-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Region-of-interest analyses on the number processing network defined by an independent localizer scan (number matching > face, shape, and word matching). The article reports the regions "showed statistical equivalence, not statistical differences, and no differences in variance" with full statistics in Supplementary Table 1.

> "In accord with the whole-brain analyses, these regions showed statistical equivalence, not statistical differences, and no differences in variance (Fig. 4b; t tests: max t(102) = 1.08, p = 0.28; equivalence-tests: min t1(102) = 3.82, p = 0.0001"
