## Evidence

### Liuyufeng Li 2024

Liuyufeng Li, Khe Foon Hew, Jiahui Du. (2024). Gamification enhances student intrinsic motivation, perceptions of autonomy and relatedness, but minimal impact on competency: a meta-analysis and systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10337-7

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review of measurement instruments across the 31 included studies (RQ1). Self-report questionnaires such as the IMI interest/enjoyment scale dominated (29 studies); five studies used semi-structured interviews; behavioral free-choice measures were absent.

> "However, to the best of our knowledge, none of the studies included in this review assessed intrinsic motivation by monitoring the students' free-choice behavior"
