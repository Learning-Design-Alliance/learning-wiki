q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the guide's introduction, citing prior scholarship (Margolis and colleagues; Code.org et al., 2020) rather than presenting new data. The guide defines students experiencing marginalization in computing as "Black, Native American, and Latinx students; students with disabilities; girls and non-binary students."

"our education system has historically prepared only some students with these skills (Margolis, Goode, & Ryoo, 2015) and has systematically excluded certain student populations (Margolis et al, 2008). Large inequalities continue to exist in access to equipment and learning opportunities needed to build computational skills for students that experience marginalization"
