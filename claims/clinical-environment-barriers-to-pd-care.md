Riddell Lisa Marie, Scott Sophie, Hasson Felicity. (2026). A Qualitative Exploration of Trainee Nurse Practitioners' Experiences and Readiness to Provide Care for Borderline Personality Disorder in Primary Care. International Journal of Mental Health Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/inm.70299

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the interview study, thematic analysis found the emergency department and primary care environments described as overwhelmingly negative spaces for patients with personality disorder. Appointments were very short, which could hinder providing holistic care, and mental health did not fit into a 10-minute appointment.

> "The fast‐paced, high‐stimulus settings, combined with the lack of privacy, space, time and available mental health specialists, were consistently identified as major barriers to providing appropriate care."
