## Evidence

### Fredericks Salim 2021

Fredericks Salim, ElSayed Mostafa, Hammad Mustafa, Abumiddain Omneya, Istwani Leila, Rabeea Abdulla, Rashid-Doubell Fiza, Bella Abdelhaleem M.E. (2021). Anxiety is associated with extraneous cognitive load during teaching using high-fidelity clinical simulation. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.1994691

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study of final-year medical students; STAI-S measured at three time points across 59 session attendances. A Friedman test found "a significant difference between the scores χ2 (2) = 45.313, p < 0.001", with Dunn-Bonferroni post hoc differences between both pre- and post-scenario versus post-debriefing (p < 0.001).

> "State-anxiety scores were high for both the pre- scenario and post-scenario time points with a significant fall following debriefing. The median STAI-S scores were 41.0 (IQR: 33.0–50.0), 46.0 (IQR: 33.0–52.0) and 31.0 (IQR: 23.0–39.0)"
