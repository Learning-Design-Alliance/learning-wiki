### Bobos Pavlos 2021

Bobos Pavlos, Pouliopoulou Dimitra V., Harriss Alexandra, Sadi Jackie, Rushton Alison, MacDermid Joy C. (2021). A systematic review and meta-analysis of measurement properties of objective structured clinical examinations used in physical therapy licensure and a structured review of licensure practices in countries with well-developed regulation systems. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255696

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Structured review reporting published PCE pass rates for Canada, 2013–2017. Home-educated candidates passed at "94%" (written) and "90%" (clinical), versus 78% and 72% for first-time international candidates. Descriptive percentages; no effect size printed.

> "The pass rate for the written examination was 94%, and the pass rate for the clinical examination was 90%. The mean pass rate for first-time international candidates was 78% for the written and 72% for the clinical examination."

### Bobos Pavlos 2021 (2)

A systematic review and meta-analysis of measurement properties of objective structured clinical examinations used in physical therapy licensure and a structured review of licensure practices in countries with well-developed regulation systems. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255696

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Structured review reporting published NPTE pass rates for the USA, 2016–2020: "92% for USA-educated candidates and 37% for foreign-educated candidates". Descriptive percentages; no effect size printed.

> "The pass rate of the USA’s National Physical Therapy examination (NPTE) for years 2016–2020 was 92% for USA-educated candidates and 37% for foreign-educated candidates."
