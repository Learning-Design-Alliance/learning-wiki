Vocabulary Improvement Program for English Language Learners and Their Classmates 2006¶

Vocabulary Improvement Program for English Language Learners and Their Classmates. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.brookespublishing.com/store/books/lively-6342/index.htm

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

In the same WWC subsample analysis of the randomized controlled trial, the PPVT-R showed effect size –0.11 (mean difference –2.35, favoring comparison) and Morphology showed 0.18; both were ns. Fewer than a third of Morphology words were included in the intervention, and PPVT-R is a standardized test of untaught vocabulary.

"The WWC analysis of the English language learner subsample found no statistically significant and no substantively important impacts on the Peabody Picture Vocabulary Test-Revised (PPVT-R) and the Morphology measure."
