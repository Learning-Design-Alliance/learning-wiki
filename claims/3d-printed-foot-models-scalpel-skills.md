## Evidence

### Anning Naomi 2024

Anning Naomi, Tehan Peta. (2024). Simulation in podiatry teaching and learning: A scoping review. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1002/jfa2.70020

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Mixed methods study (Banwell et al., as charted in Table 1) combining an RCT, a repeat-measure study, and focus groups with undergraduate podiatry students practicing scalpel skills on 3D printed foot models and foot ulcer models. The review reports similar anxiety and confidence changes versus standard teaching; no effect size is printed.

> "In comparison to standard teaching methods, the use of 3D printed foot models had similar decreases in anxiety and increases in confidence measures"
