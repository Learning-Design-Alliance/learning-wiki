Parallel student and faculty survey items on the same physics problem (Fig. 4). The article reports "Less than one-third of faculty respondents would recommend or highly recommend students using tools like ChatGPT for explaining concepts (n= 8, 29%) or gathering missing information (n= 9, 32%)", with only 7% recommending genAI for calculations and 11% for verifying correctness.
