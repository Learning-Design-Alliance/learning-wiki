## Evidence

### Coertjens Liesje 2017

Coertjens Liesje, Donche Vincent, De Maeyer Sven, Vanthournout Gert, Van Petegem Peter. (2017). To what degree does the missing-data technique influence the estimated growth in learning strategies over time? A tutorial example of sensitivity analysis for longitudinal data. PLoS ONE 12(9): e0182615. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182615

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sensitivity analysis of latent growth models on one cohort's memorizing subscale scores across three waves, comparing eight missing-data techniques. The article reports "a significant decrease over time" under MCAR/MAR models but that MNAR models "did not confirm a declining trend over time"; the H&G model's overall mean slope was not significant (b = −.002, table), because students who dropped out stayed constant on this subscale (b = .043, se = .054, p = .43 for that group).

> "For the memorizing subscale, the models, assuming either MCAR or MAR, indicated a significant decrease over time, combined with variance in intercepts but not in slopes. However, the models assuming MNAR did not confirm a declining trend over time."
