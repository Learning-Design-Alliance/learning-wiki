# Training for speed in handwriting may be secured without considerable sacrifice of quality

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a 6A grade, slow writing averaged 46.8 letters per minute at quality 14.95, natural writing 73.1 at 14.60, and rapid writing 82.3 at 14.12, supporting the conclusion that speed may be gained with little quality loss. [→ Thompson 1911](#thompson-1911)
