# Students in the collaborative online comic group spent more time on learning-related behaviors and less time on learning-unrelated behaviors than students in the other two groups

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In-class observation showed the collaborative learning group had the highest frequency of creating comics with dialogue in Chinese writing (29.41%) and the lowest frequencies of learning-unrelated behaviors (playing with others 0.94%, moving around 0.47%, playing alone 0.63%).
