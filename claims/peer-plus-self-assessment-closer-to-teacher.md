# Combining peer assessment with self-assessment brings marks closer to the teacher's marks

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Peer marks incorporating self-assessment (SPM) were the closest to teacher marks in the CA-2006 exam, and the authors conclude the combination gives better validity. [→ Bouzidi 2009](#bouzidi-2009)

## Evidence

### Bouzidi 2009

Bouzidi, L., & Jaillet, A. (2009). Can Online Peer Assessment be Trusted?. Educational Technology & Society, 12(4), 257-268. https://www.ifets.info/

`q2 · i?`

Item analysis of the CA-2006 exam (17 items, 69 exam sheets): the TM-SPM mean difference (0.12) was smaller than the TM-PM difference (0.39), and the article states "the peer assessment incorporating the self-assessment that is the closest to the assessment by the teacher".

> "We also note that it is the peer assessment incorporating the self-assessment that is the closest to the assessment by the teacher."
