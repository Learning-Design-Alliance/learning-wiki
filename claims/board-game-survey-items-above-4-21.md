Anonymous voluntary survey distributed at the conclusion of each of the three editions, with eleven Likert-scale items (1 = lowest to 5 = highest). The article reports "All items scored above 4.21 (5.00 is the maximum)", with items on integration with prior courses (4.21) and adequacy of time (4.33) rated comparatively lower but still strong.

> "All items scored above 4.21 (5.00 is the maximum), with particularly high agreement on the activity's ability to increase enjoyment of the related courses (items 1 and 9, average = 4.96 and 4.92, respectively) and its overall quality (item 11, average = 4.88)."
