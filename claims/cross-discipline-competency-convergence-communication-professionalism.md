Verboven Kenneth, Wikström-Grotell Camilla, Lehtinen-Jacks Susanna, Davis Mary E., O'Donoghue Gráinne, Tuominen Pipsa P. A., Agten Anouk, Curran Fiona, Cunningham Caitriona. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-study comparison of competencies reported in the 22 included papers found "significant agreement in the competencies identified by differing HCP disciplines", with divergence concentrated in clinical domains. Competencies in overarching domains such as communication and professionalism were generally more consistent across disciplines.

> "There was significant agreement in the competencies identified by differing HCP disciplines. As expected, greater divergence in competencies existed in the clinical assessment and management domains, which likely incorporate the more unique elements of each discipline's scope of practice."
