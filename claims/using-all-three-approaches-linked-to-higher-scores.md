## Evidence

### Çakiroğlu 2013

Çakiroğlu, Ü. (2013). Using a hybrid approach to facilitate learning introductory programming. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1), 161-177. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008878

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experts' review of experimental-group posttest responses coded which approaches (CFC, analogy, template) each student used per question. The article states "These statistics present some evidence that students who used all of the approaches performed better than others"; this is a correlational observation, not a tested comparison.
