Perception item from the 144-student pilot survey. The article reports "93.1% reported that the difficulty level of the pre-class tests was appropriate" as a descriptive finding with no inferential analysis.

> "The overwhelming majority of students (n = 134, 93.1%) reported that the difficulty level of the pre-class tests was appropriate, rating them as "just right" in terms of challenge. Only nine students (6.2%) found the tests too difficult, and one student (0.7%) considered them too easy."
