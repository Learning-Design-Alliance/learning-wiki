## Evidence

### Eugenio Tufino 2025

Eugenio Tufino, Bor Gregorcic. (2025). Creating a customisable Socratic AI physics tutor. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae0d23](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae0d23)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' qualitative comparison of dialogues generated with Gemini 2.5 Pro: the custom Socratic tutor Gem versus the standard model on the same physics problems. The authors state the standard model "provides the complete solution directly rather than engaging in a pedagogical dialogue". Dialogues were author-generated illustrative examples, repeated multiple times to check consistency.

> "A standard, non-customized Gemini model, when presented with the same problem, provides the complete solution directly rather than engaging in a pedagogical dialogue. This highlights the value of role engineering in transforming a general-purpose solution provider into a pedagogical guide."
