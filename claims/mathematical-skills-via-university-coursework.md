For five theoretical mathematical skills (Figures 20–21), "The vast majority of respondents gained these skills through university coursework". Phenomenology, gravity/cosmology, and theory-oriented particle physics were obtained by a majority; formal mathematics and BSM physics by only a sizable minority.
