`q2 i?` Of 15 eligible direct-service sites, eight adopted CP and seven did not; adopters and nonadopters differed significantly in age (χ2[3] = 20.1; P = 0.000), race (χ2[3] = 27.3; P = 0.000), providing care to pregnant women (χ2[1] = 13.8; P = 0.000), and having heard of CP (χ2[1] = 24.188, P = 0.000). ## Evidence

### Xaverius Pamela K. 2014

Xaverius Pamela K., Grady Mary Alice. (2014). Centering Pregnancy in Missouri: A System Level Analysis. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/285386

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square comparison of survey respondents from the eight adopting versus seven nonadopting direct-service sites (110 vs. 79 respondents). The article reports the four significant χ2 tests quoted; no significant difference was found in weekly patient volume or time per patient, and no effect sizes are printed.

> "Characteristics of adopters were significantly different than nonadopters, in terms of age (χ 2[3] = 20.1; P = 0.000), race (χ 2[3] = 27.3; P = 0.000), providing care to pregnant women (χ 2[1] = 13.8; P = 0.000), and having heard of CP (χ 2[1] = 24.188, P = 0.000)."
