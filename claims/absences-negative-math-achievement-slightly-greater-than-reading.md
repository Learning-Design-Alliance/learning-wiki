## Evidence

### Michael A. Gottfried 2011

Michael A. Gottfried. (2011). The Detrimental Effects of Missing School: Evidence from Urban Siblings. American Journal of Education 117(2). https://doi.org/10.1086/657886

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Baseline value-added regressions for SAT9 math on the sibling sample (n = 6,818 in math models). The article reports that "The effect sizes for absences in the SAT9 math models hover around a value of ⫺0.08j" and that a one standard deviation increase in absences relates to a statistically significant decrease in math performance.

> "It is apparent from the results in table 4 and the corresponding effect sizes that the negative relationship between missing school and standardized testing achievement is slightly greater for math than for reading."
