# In-person but not online self-regulated multiple-choice students completed fewer restudy trials before their first practice test than cued-recall students.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Delivery format moderated early regulation: in-person SRL-MC participants restudied less before their first test than SRL-CR participants, while online participants in the two groups restudied similar amounts. [→ Badali 2023](#badali-2023)

## Evidence

### Badali 2023

Badali, S., Rawson, K.A. & Dunlosky, J. (2023). How do Students Regulate Their Use of Multiple Choice Practice Tests?. Educational Psychology Review 35, 43. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09761-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Group-by-format analysis of restudy behavior before the first test trial. In-person means were M = .5 (SRL-MC) versus M = 1.4 (SRL-CR), while online means were both .6; the reported test was "F = 7.38 , p < .001." Only the test statistic is printed, so no effect size is coded.

> "In-person participants completed fewer restudy trials before their first test trial in the SRL-MC group ( M = .5) compared to the SRL-CR group ( M = 1.4) but online participants completed similar amounts of restudy trials before first test trial in both groups (SRL-MC M = .6, SRL-CR M = .6), F = 7.38 , p < .001."
