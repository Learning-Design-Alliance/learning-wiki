## Evidence

### Tia Achil 2025

Tia Achil, Konan Amoin G., Hauser Jonas, Ndri Kouassi Y., Ciclet Olivier, Esso Lasme E., Nindjin Charlemagne. (2025). A Cross-Sectional Study of the Relationship Between Dietary Micronutrient Intake, Cognition and Academic Performance Among School-Aged Children in Taabo, Côte d’Ivoire. Nutrients 17(22):3602. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu17223602

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariable chi-squared analysis in the same cross-sectional study of 252 children, comparing cognitive performance categories across nutrient intake tertiles; the article reports significant associations with "cognitive test scores". Only test statistics and p-values are printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "chi-squared tests (χ2) revealed significant associations between thiamin (χ2 = 20.8; p < 0.001), riboflavin (χ2 = 12.6; p = 0.002), folate (χ2 = 10.4; p = 0.006), iron (χ2 = 7.1; p = 0.029), zinc (χ2 = 6.8; p = 0.033), nutrient blend (χ2 = 7.5; p = 0.024) intakes and cognitive test scores"
