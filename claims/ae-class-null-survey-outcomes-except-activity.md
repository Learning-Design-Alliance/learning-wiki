## Evidence

### Dysterheft 2016

Dysterheft, J., Chaparro, G., Rice, L., & Rice, I. (2016). Investigating the Outcomes and Perceptions of an Inclusive Aquatic Exercise Class for University Students with Physical Disabilities. Journal of Postsecondary Education and Disability, 31(1), 41-56. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Investigating+the+Outcomes+and+Perceptions+of+an+Inclusive+Aquatic+Exercise+Class

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-methods intervention study with six SWD in an adapted aquatic exercise class and six inactive controls, surveyed pre and post a five-week period. The authors report that "no significant differences occurred between the intervention and control group" on survey measures. Equivalence was not tested; the authors note possible insufficient power or insensitive measures.

> "Our first hypothesis was not completely support - ed, as no significant differences occurred between the intervention and control group for pre- and post-mea- sures, except recreational physical activity (Table 2)."

### Dysterheft 2016 (2)

Dysterheft, J., Chaparro, G., Rice, L., & Rice, I. (2016). Investigating the Outcomes and Perceptions of an Inclusive Aquatic Exercise Class for University Students with Physical Disabilities. Journal of Postsecondary Education and Disability, 31(1), 41-56. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Investigating+the+Outcomes+and+Perceptions+of+an+Inclusive+Aquatic+Exercise+Class

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of LTPAQ-SCI change scores between the six-participant intervention and six-participant control groups found a significant difference (U = 2.00, z = -2.58, p = 0.01). The authors attribute this partly to the AE class participation itself; no effect size is printed.

> "Results of the Mann-Whitney U test indicated significant differences between groups for LTPAQ-SCI pre- and post-intervention changes (U = 2.00, z = -2.58, p = 0.01)."
