## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lichand 2024

Lichand, G., Christen, J., & Van Egeraat, E. (2024). Neglecting students’ socio-emotional skills magniﬁed learning losses during the pandemic. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00235-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

CATE analysis by whether the targeted phone number belonged to the student, on the summary test-score measure with controls and IPW (960 observations); targeting was not randomly assigned but phone ownership was balanced across treatment and control.

> "targeting students mattered for the effects of nudges on standardized test scores: its effect size was signiﬁcantly larger when students were targeted directly (0.31 s.d. vs. 0.08 s.d., a difference signiﬁcant at the 10% level)"
