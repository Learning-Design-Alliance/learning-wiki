### Vinesh Rajpaul 2014

Vinesh Rajpaul, Saalih Allie, Sarah-Louise Blyth. (2014). The introductory astronomy course at the University of Cape Town: probing student perspectives. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020126](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020126)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre-course free-response question answered by 78 students and post-course four-option question answered by 89 students. The article reports the notable change was a decrease in students dismissing the Big Bang as unsubstantiated speculation; post-course 42 (47%) held there is strong evidence for it.

> "The notable change from pre-course to post-course is the decrease in the proportion of students who dismissed the Big Bang theory as being akin to unsubstantiated speculation."
