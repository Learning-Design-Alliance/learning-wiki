### Gao Wei 2019

Gao Wei, Ou Tongwen, Jia Jianguo, Fan Jie, Xu Jianjun, Li Jin, Cui Xin, He Xinzhou, Li Xueli. (2019). Development and evaluation of a training model for paracentetic suprapubic cystostomy and catheterization. Clinics. https://doi.org/10.6061/clinics/2019/e435

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 40 fourth-year medical students (20 per group) in a prospective observational study; performance was video-recorded and scored by urologists (60% subjective, 40% objective time-based). The study reports "91.0 vs. 86.8, p=0.002" by Mann-Whitney U test.

> "The median final score was significantly higher in the experimental group than in the control group (91.0 vs. 86.8, p=0.002)."
