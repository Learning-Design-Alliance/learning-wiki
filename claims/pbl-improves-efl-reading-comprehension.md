## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At α = .05, intermediate EFL learners taught through project-based activities (magazines or wall newspapers) scored significantly higher on a reading comprehension post-test than learners taught via CLT. ## Evidence

### Mona Poorverdi Shiraz and Ebrahim Ezati Larsari 2014

Mona Poorverdi Shiraz and Ebrahim Ezati Larsari. (2014). The Effect of Project-Based Activities on Intermediate EFL Students' Reading Comprehension Ability. The Journal of Effective Teaching, Vol. 14, No.3, 38-54. https://uncw.edu/cte/et/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on post-test scores of 120 intermediate female EFL students (40 per group) at Kish English Institute of Rasht, comparing two project-based groups with a CLT control group. The article reports F = 26.266, Sig. .000, and states there is "a significant positive relationship between using Project-based tasks and improving reading comprehension."

> "The relatively high value of F = 26.266 showed that there is, indeed, a significant positive relationship between using Project-based tasks and improving reading comprehension."
