## Evidence

### Beyazhancer Rumeysa 2026

Beyazhancer Rumeysa, Kahraman Nurcan, Demir Barış. (2026). Resilience and academic performance in mathematics and science among middle school students: The mediating role of computational thinking. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0360966

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Parallel mediation analysis in Mplus 8.1 with maximum likelihood estimation and bias-corrected bootstrap confidence intervals based on 5000 resamples, treating mathematics and science achievement as separate outcomes and controlling for gender. The study reports the indirect effect on mathematics achievement was "β = 0.070, p = .001" and that the direct association "was not statistically significant."

> "This mediation effect was statistically significant for both science (β = 0.064, p = .001) and mathematics (β = 0.070, p = .001). Since the direct association between psychological resilience on mathematics achievement was not statistically significant, the findings were statistically consistent with CT mediating this relationship."
