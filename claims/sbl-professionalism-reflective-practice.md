Dairo Yetunde M., Hunter Kirsty, Ishaku Timothy. (2024). The impact of simulation-based learning on the knowledge, attitude and performance of physiotherapy students on practice placement. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05718-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Findings from the 'Professionalism' theme: the nine-to-five routine, uniform and travelling to a non-usual venue added realness; students with no clinical experience commented most readily on using station time and prioritising assessment and intervention elements.

> "In this theme, participants highlighted the professional skills gained from their simulation placement, including understanding time management, the physiotherapist’s role in practice, adherence to the code of conduct, and personal development."
