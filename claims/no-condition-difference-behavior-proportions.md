## Evidence

### Rachel Lam 2019

Rachel Lam. (2019). What students do when encountering failure in collaborative tasks. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0045-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann–Whitney U-tests comparing behavior proportions across the two conditions (10 dyads each); no significant difference, equivalence not tested. The authors note Select used more Functional and fewer Dysfunctional behaviors on average.

> "There were no significant differences for Functional behaviors, z = −0.81, p = 0.46, or Dysfunctional behaviors, z = −0.81, p = 0.46. However, we note that the sample means aligned with the posttest results."
