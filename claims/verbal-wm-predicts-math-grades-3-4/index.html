Evidence¶

Pellizzoni 2021¶

Pellizzoni, S., Cargnelutti, E., Cuder, A., & Passolunghi, M.C. (2021). The interplay between math anxiety and working memory on math performance: a longitudinal study. Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nyas.14722

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Path analysis (Model 1a) in 146 children; LS also negatively associated with DAYS_T (β = −0.37, P < 0.001) and AMAS (β = −0.15, P = 0.04). The authors say this "confirms the fundamental role of verbal WM as a math precursor".

"A single measure of verbal WM capacity (LS) was found positively and directly related to math learning at the beginning of grades 3 and 4."
