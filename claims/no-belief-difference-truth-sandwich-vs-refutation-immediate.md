## Evidence

### Swire-Thompson 2025

Swire-Thompson, Briony, Butler, Lucy, & Rapp, David N. (2025). The truth sandwich format does not enhance the correction of misinformation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 117(6), 851–862. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000961

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Frequentist follow-up contrast in the Experiment 1 within-subjects ANOVA: post-correction belief "did not differ as a function of whether participants received bottom–loaded refutations or truth sandwiches" (p = .546).

> "However, as predicted, belief did not differ as a function of whether participants received bottom–loaded refutations or truth sandwiches (p = .546; see Figure 1, panel A)."

### Swire-Thompson 2025 (2)

Swire-Thompson, Briony, Butler, Lucy, & Rapp, David N. (2025). The truth sandwich format does not enhance the correction of misinformation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 117(6), 851–862. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000961

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

A 2 × 2 within-subjects Bayesian ANOVA excluding the control "returned strong evidence for a null effect of correction condition (BF01 = 19.68)" and moderate evidence for the null interaction (BF01 = 6.76).

> "This analysis returned strong evidence for a null effect of correction condition (BF01 = 19.68), and moderate evidence for the null for the pre/post × correction condition interaction (BF01 = 6.76)."
