## Evidence

### Nirupama Devaraj and Jaishankar Raman 2014

Nirupama Devaraj and Jaishankar Raman. (2014). The Effect of Student Learning Styles, Race and Gender on Learning Outcomes: The Case of Public Goods. Journal of Learning in Higher Education, Fall 2014 (Volume 10 Issue 2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1143339

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment comparing 84 students in three experiment-treatment macro sections with 28 students in a lecture-based micro control section, assessed on six public-goods multiple-choice questions. The article reports "no significant impact on the learning outcomes" in total points, and a negative experiment effect significant at the 10% level in Model 2.

> "We find that experiment based learning has no significant impact on the learning outcomes as measured by the total points scored by a student in the post experiment questionnaire."
