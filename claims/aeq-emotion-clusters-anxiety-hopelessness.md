## Evidence

### Reinhard Pekrun 2002

Reinhard Pekrun, Thomas Goetz, Wolfram Titz, & Raymond P. Perry. (2002). Academic Emotions in Students' Self-Regulated Learning and Achievement: A Program of Qualitative and Quantitative Research. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326985ep3702_4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analyses of AEQ trait scales in university samples (N = 230 for learning- and class-related scales; N = 222 for test emotion scales). The authors note "the high correlations between anxiety and hopelessness (r > .70)" but kept the scales separate because they may relate differentially to outcomes.

> "Concerning interrelations of different emotions within these groups, four clusters of emotions emerged: (a) enjoyment, hope,andpride;(b)relief;(c)anxiety,shame,andhopelessness; and (d) anger and boredom."
