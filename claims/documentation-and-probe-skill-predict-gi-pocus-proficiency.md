## Evidence

### Wang Gang 2026

Wang Gang, Liu Yingying, Wang Shengyun, Gao Silong, Lin Hui, Wu Qian, Huang Debin, Pan Yueshuai, Zhao Yuxiao, Wei Huimin, Wei Lili. (2026). Factors Influencing ICU Nurses’ Proficiency in Point‐of‐Care Gastrointestinal Ultrasound: A National Cross‐Sectional Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/3178958

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression in the same 2009-nurse survey. Documentation of findings in nursing charts independently predicted proficiency, with "OR for nondocumenters vs. documenters = 0.347" (OR = 0.347).

> "nurses who documented GI‐POCUS findings in nursing charts were more likely to report “good” rather than “poor” proficiency (OR for nondocumenters vs. documenters = 0.347, 95% CI: 0.231–0.521, p < 0.001)"

### Wang Gang 2026 (2)

Wang Gang, Liu Yingying, Wang Shengyun, Gao Silong, Lin Hui, Wu Qian, Huang Debin, Pan Yueshuai, Zhao Yuxiao, Wei Huimin, Wei Lili. (2026). Factors Influencing ICU Nurses’ Proficiency in Point‐of‐Care Gastrointestinal Ultrasound: A National Cross‐Sectional Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/3178958

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same regression model on the cross-sectional survey. Probe-operation skill independently separated good from poor proficiency, with "an OR of 0.332" for nonproficient versus proficient nurses (OR = 0.332).

> "Proficiency in probe operation also independently distinguished “good” from “poor” proficiency, with an OR of 0.332 (95% CI: 0.195–0.564, p < 0.001) for nonproficient versus proficient nurses."
