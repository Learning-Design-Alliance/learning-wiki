Evidence¶

Jonathan Firth 2021¶

Jonathan Firth, Ian Rivers and James Boyle. (2021). A systematic review of interleaving as a concept learning strategy. Review of Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/rev3.3266

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analytic subset of the systematic review comparing interleaved versus blocked schedules on transfer tasks presenting novel items from learned categories. The review reports "a benefit with effect sizes of up to 0.66" for transfer.

"transfer (as tested by presenting novel items from a learned category) a benefit with effect sizes of up to 0.66"
