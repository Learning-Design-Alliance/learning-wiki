## Evidence

### Liu Bingyan 2026

Liu Bingyan, Xiang Shufang, Xiao Baolai, Xiang Jinjian, Sun Yimin, Ren Yuan. (2026). Application of a modified Peyton’s four-step approach in clinical skills training for residents: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09499-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-month 5-point Likert satisfaction questionnaire in the RCT; Table 6 reports overall satisfaction 4.50 ± 0.50 vs 3.38 ± 0.80 (t = 9.110, P < 0.001) and improve clinical skills 4.63 ± 0.52 vs 3.23 ± 0.93 (t = 10.202).

> "the overall satisfaction was significantly higher in the experimental group than in the control group, particularly in areas of stimulating learning interest, improving clinical skills, stimulating active learning, and enhancing teamwork (P < 0.05; Table 6)."
