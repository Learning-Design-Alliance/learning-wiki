`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Case-study episode 2: contrary to instructors' advice, the dyad created the goal of synchronizing two models into one unitary model; to the students "the final project was therefore a work of art, despite being a composite model of motion".

> "Their final project was therefore a coordinated performance: Matt ran the visual simulation designed in ViMAP while Ariana played the audio tune designed in TuneBlocks in a synchronized fashion."

### Amy Voss Farris and Pratim Sengupta 2014 (2)

Amy Voss Farris and Pratim Sengupta. (2014). Democratizing Children's Computation: Learning Computational Science as Aesthetic Experience. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.08619](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.08619)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of the dyad's TuneBlocks model, in which pitch and duration served as variables representing change in speed, widening the "representational palette" with musical attributes such as tempo and pitch.

> "They used a gradually decreasing pitch to indicate the decreasing altitude of a roller coaster moving down a steep incline, and programmed the duration of each note to represent speed, where increasingly shorter, closer together tones indicated increasing speed."
