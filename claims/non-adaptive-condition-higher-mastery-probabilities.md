## Evidence

### Sibley 2026

Sibley, L., Berner, T., & Schmalfeldt, T. (2026). Effectiveness of adaptive versus non-adaptive intelligent tutoring systems in early primary mathematics. Computers and Education Open 11. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100420

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory ANOVA of ITS log data on the level of skills students reached during the intervention in the N = 132 experiment. The article reports η² = .06 with means of 0.67 (SD = 0.18) versus 0.58 (SD = 0.19), attributing the difference to the adaptive condition's mastery-gated progression logic.

> "Results showed that students in the non-adaptive condition reached significantly higher average mastery probabilities than students in the adaptive condition, F(1, 128) = 7.85, p = .006, η² = .06 (M = 0.67, SD = 0.18 vs. M = 0.58, SD = 0.19)."
