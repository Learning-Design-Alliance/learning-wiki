`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative theme “Anticipation of the unknown”: interview participants described uncertainty about the new simulation environment as emotionally difficult and “potentially increasing cognitive load”; some also reported anxiety impairing memory recall during scenarios.

> "As well as it being emotionally difficult to tolerate, uncertainty was seen by many participants as potentially increasing cognitive load."
