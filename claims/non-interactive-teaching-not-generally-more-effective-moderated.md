# Non-interactive teaching was not generally more effective than restudy, but was more effective in humanities, upper secondary education, and when graded

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · theoretical `r2`–`r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across 20 teaching units (N = 191 students, within-participants design), non-interactive teaching was not generally more effective than restudy. [→ Lachner 2024](#lachner-2024)
`q2 i?` Moderation analyses revealed non-interactive teaching was more effective in the humanities, in upper secondary education, and when it was graded as an external incentive. [→ Lachner 2024 (2)](#lachner-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Lachner 2024

Lachner, A., Sibley, L., & Wagner, S. (2024). Practice Recommendations or Not? The LoGeT Model as Empirical Approach to Generate Localized, Generalized, and Transferable Evidence. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09876-z

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

In the article's third example (Sibley, Russ et al., 2023b), ten design teams of in-service teachers and master's students developed 20 teaching units; students (N = 191) received different sequences of non-interactive teaching and restudy across two comparably difficult lessons in a within-participants design with five open-ended questions per unit.

> "Overall, it could be demonstrated that non-interactive teaching was not generally more effective than restudy"

### Lachner 2024 (2)

Lachner, A., Sibley, L., & Wagner, S. (2024). Practice Recommendations or Not? The LoGeT Model as Empirical Approach to Generate Localized, Generalized, and Transferable Evidence. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09876-z

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Moderation analyses in the same within-participants schooling study (N = 191) identified boundary conditions: the advantage of non-interactive teaching over restudy emerged "in the humanities as well as upper secondary education and when it was graded, as external incentive."

> "However, additional moderation analyses revealed that non-interactive teaching was more effective in the humanities as well as upper secondary education and when it was graded, as external incentive."
