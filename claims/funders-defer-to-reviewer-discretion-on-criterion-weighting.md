The report's review of seven funding agencies' instructions finds that, except for NWO, funders do not specify criterion weighting; "the criteria are not defined precisely and the relative emphasis placed on them is left to the reviewer's discretion." No effect size is printed.

> "Except for the Netherlands Organisation for Scientific Research (NWO), the funders we reviewed do not specify how much weight reviewers should assign to the intellectual merit and to the broader impacts of research."
