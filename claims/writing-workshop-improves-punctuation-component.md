## Evidence

### Salem 2013

Ashraf Atta M. S. Salem. (2013). The Effect of Using Writer's Workshop Approach on Developing Basic Writing Skills (Mechanics of Writing) of Prospective Teachers of English in Egypt. English Language Teaching, 6(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n7p33

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Component analysis of the same one-group pre-post study (n = 40). Punctuation pretest mean was 4.075 (SD 2.66) and posttest mean 21.3875 (SD 3.41), with t-value 27.599 and effect size 0.975, a "statistically significant difference".

> "It is clear from the data represented in table (4) that there is a statistically significant difference between mean scores of the subjects of the study in the pretest and posttest on the second component "punctuation" of the writing test."
