Benjamin M. Zwickl, Noah Finkelstein, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2013). Incorporating learning goals about modeling into an upper-division physics laboratory experiment. arXiv:1301.4414. [doi:10.1119/1.4875924](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4875924)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Theoretical argument from the paper's section on the modeling framework: systematic error arises when assumptions or approximations are invalid, and "through the explicit use of models, students are able to connect these suggestions to the assumptions and idealizations in t he model, evaluate the appropriateness of any assumptions or idealizations, and reﬁne th e model or apparatus to reduce the systematic error."

> "An added beneﬁt of the emphasis on modeling is that it provides a natu ral way to discuss systematic error in the laboratory."
