## Evidence

### Alshurman 2017

Wael Mohammad Alshurman. (2017). The effects of the first part of the CoRT program for teaching thinking (BREADTH) on the development of communication skills among a sample of students from Al al-Bayt University in Jordan. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.3069

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest quasi-experimental study of all 36 students (20 female, 16 male) enrolled in a CoRT BREADTH training session at Al al-Bayt University. The T test showed "statistically significant differences between the pre and post scales in favor of the post scale"; Table 1 prints pre mean 70.53 (SD 4.878), post mean 94.17, T = 86.742, significance 0.000. No effect size is printed.

> "Table 1 shows there are statistically significant differences between the pre and post scales in favor of the post scale, indicating that the first part (BREADTH) is efficient in developing the communication skills of Al al -Bayt University students."
