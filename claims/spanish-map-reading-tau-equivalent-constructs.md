Internal-structure validity analysis (Chapter 8) of goodness-of-fit indices for the Spanish MAP Growth Reading tests. The report concludes the constructs are "at least tau-equivalent and most of them are parallel equivalent" across language background groups; no fit index values are quoted in the text provided.
