## Evidence

### Stochlinski Caroline 2026

Stochlinski Caroline, Maleczek Mathias, Korn Lukas, Gleiss Andreas, Breckwoldt Jan, Roessler Bernhard. (2026). Skill retention of advanced airway techniques after simulation training – a randomized prospective study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08646-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory correlation analysis in the same trial, using Kendall's Tau-b with a threshold of <0.2 classified as no association. The first-evaluation association was "Kendall’s Tau-b 0.492, p < 0.001"; the overall median number of previous simulated FONA procedures was 1.

> "The number of previously simulated FONA procedures and the achieved score at the first evaluation show a moderate association (Kendall’s Tau-b 0.492, p < 0.001)."

### Stochlinski Caroline 2026 (2)

Stochlinski Caroline, Maleczek Mathias, Korn Lukas, Gleiss Andreas, Breckwoldt Jan, Roessler Bernhard. (2026). Skill retention of advanced airway techniques after simulation training – a randomized prospective study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08646-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same exploratory analysis, final (three-month) evaluation: "practically no correlation" with Kendall's Tau-b 0.12, p = 0.364, below the study's 0.2 association threshold.

> "Regarding the achieved FONA score at the final evaluation, there was practically no correlation between these two variables (Kendall’s Tau-b 0.12, p = 0.364)"
