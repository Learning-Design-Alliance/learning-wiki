## Evidence

### Akkuzu 2016

Akkuzu, N. & Uyulgan, M.A. (2016). How to Improve Students' Comprehension concerning the Major Terms of Functional Groups?--In the Experiment of OrCheTaboo Game. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n2p196

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttest mean-score comparisons per experiment (alcohols-ethers, aldehydes-ketones, carboxylic acids-esters, maleic-fumaric synthesis, iodoform synthesis) between control (N=30) and experimental (N=32) groups. Mean increase differences ranged from 15.51 to 24.33 in favor of the experimental group; no effect sizes are printed for these tests.

> "As a result of the independent t-tests applied to find out if these increases are statistically significant or not, they have been found out to be significant (respectively, t= -3.41, p(.01)<.05; t= -4.29, p(.00)<.05; t= -4.32, p(.00)<.05; t= -4.54, p(.00)<.05; t=-3.99, p(.00)<.05)."
