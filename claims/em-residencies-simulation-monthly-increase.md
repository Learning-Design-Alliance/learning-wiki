Cross-sectional national survey of 100 EM program directors (36% response rate), analyzed with frequency tabulations and chi-square comparison to the 2008 Okuda survey. The discussion reports "75% of programs now reporting simulation sessions at least once per month compared to just 42% of programs in 2008."
