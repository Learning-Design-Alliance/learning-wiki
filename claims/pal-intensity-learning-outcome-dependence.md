### Daly-Smith A. 2019

Daly-Smith A., Quarmby T., Archbold V.S.J., Routen A.C., Morris J.L., Gammon C., Bartholomew J.B., Resaland G.K., Llewellyn B., Allman R., Dorling H. (2019). `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Novel theme from workshop discussions on PAL intensity; a participant noted that "the classroom constraint is it's not a physical environment and if most activities are moderate to vigorously active you're not going to be able to learn." The authors state this finding has received little or no attention in previous literature.
