# Full-scale IQ predicts reading comprehension more strongly than word reading, and working memory and processing speed remain significant predictors after IQ is controlled

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Full-scale IQ significantly predicted both reading outcomes over and above the four cognitive factors, with a much larger standardized loading on comprehension (.78) than word reading (.27). [→ Christopher 2012](#christopher-2012)
`q3 i?` Working memory and processing speed continued to predict reading after Full-scale IQ was controlled, showing their contributions are not redundant with IQ. [→ Christopher 2012](#christopher-2012)
