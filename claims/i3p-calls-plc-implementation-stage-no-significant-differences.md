# Teams from all three I3P calls functioned as PLCs at the implementation stage, with no statistically significant differences between calls across the five PLC dimensions

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All PLC dimensions had means above 3 for teams from all three I3P calls, indicating the implementation stage, though values were higher for I3P-3. [→ Layton-Jaramillo 2025](#layton-jaramillo-2025)

## Evidence

### Layton-Jaramillo 2025

Layton-Jaramillo, S.E., Duarte-Velasco, O.G., Aguaded-Ramírez, E.M., & Carrillo-Rosúa, F.J. (2025). Innovation in university science teaching through professional learning communities. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 15(1), 142-161. https://doi.org/10.3926/jotse.3115

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire survey of I3P participants (n=14, 21, and 32 for I3P-1, I3P-2, I3P-3) rated five PLC dimensions on a 1-4 scale. "All dimensions had a mean greater than 3" across calls, with I3P-3 highest.

> "All dimensions had a mean greater than 3, indicating that teams from all three I3P calls are functioning as PLCs at the implementation stage; however, the values were higher for I3P-3."

### Layton-Jaramillo 2025 (2)

Layton-Jaramillo, S.E., Duarte-Velasco, O.G., Aguaded-Ramírez, E.M., & Carrillo-Rosúa, F.J. (2025). Innovation in university science teaching through professional learning communities. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 15(1), 142-161. https://doi.org/10.3926/jotse.3115

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U tests (p<0.05) and Cohen's d compared PLC dimension scores between the three calls. "The differences between the calls are not statistically significant for any of the dimensions"; effect sizes were low in most pairwise comparisons.

> "The differences between the calls are not statistically significant for any of the dimensions. However, the effect size (ES) is low across all dimensions when comparing I3P-1 with I3P-3."
