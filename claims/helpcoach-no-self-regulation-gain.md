## Evidence

### Hyoungwook Jin 2026

Hyoungwook Jin, Weirui Peng, Jieun Han, Q. Vera Liao, and Xu Wang. (2026). HelpCoach: Scaffolding Targeted AI Help-Seeking During Problem-Solving. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28918 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.28918](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.28918)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result from the between-subjects study (n=40) using three MSLQ subscales (metacognitive self-regulation, effort regulation, help seeking) on 7-point Likert scales at three stages. The article reports no significant improvement or retention; no effect size is printed.

> "However,HelpCoachdid not significantly outperformBaseline in improving or retaining self-reported self-regulation, suggesting potential limits in transferring these skills to more general and independent settings, perhaps due to the short intervention period."
