Promising Practices: Smart State Strategies for Building Intensive Tutoring Systems 2022¶

Promising Practices: Smart State Strategies for Building Intensive Tutoring Systems. (2022). Education Trust, Education Reform Now, and FutureEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED622839

q2 · i? · design · r1

In its profile of Tennessee's TN ALL Corps, the brief reports the department's own guidance statement that in-school-day tutoring "is more effective than before- or after-school programming." No study or effect size is printed; the comparison is attributed to TDOE guidance.

"Notably, TDOE doesn't require tutoring to occur during the school day. However, it does note that this is more effective than before- or after-school programming."
