# Tutor ratings do not entirely tap one underlying trait; one- and two-factor models fit unsatisfactorily

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Confirmatory factor analysis showed neither a one-factor nor a two-factor (process/content) hypothesis fitted the tutor-rating data entirely satisfactorily. [→ Schmidt 1995](#schmidt-1995)

## Evidence

### Schmidt 1995

Schmidt, H., van der Arend, A., Kokx, I. & Boon, L. (1995). Peer versus staff tutoring in problem-based learning. Instructional Science 22: 279-285. https://doi.org/10.1007/bf00891781

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of the four tutor-rating items. The one-factor model gave Chi-square 91.018, df = 2, CFI 0.94; the two-factor process/content solution gave Chi-square 42.996, df = 1, CFI 0.97 — neither fitted entirely satisfactorily, suggesting ratings do not entirely tap the same underlying trait.

> "The one-factor hypothesis produced a Chi-square of 91.018, df = 2, p < :0.001. Bentler-Bonett's Comparative Fit Index for this case equaled 0.94."
