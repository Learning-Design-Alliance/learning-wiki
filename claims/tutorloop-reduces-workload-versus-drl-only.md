Workload was measured as gaze entropy per second in the same N=187 between-subjects study and analyzed with linear mixed-effects models (Table 3/4). The authors conclude "LLM-based feedback refinement plays a key role in mitigating workload" while purely DRL-driven strategies may add cognitive burden.
