## Evidence

### Reinholz 2014

Reinholz, D. L., Corbo, J. C., Dancy, M. H., Finkelstein, N., & Deetz, S. (2014). Towards a Model of Systemic Change in University STEM Education. [doi:10.2307/j.ctv2x00vcx.13](https://doi.org/10.2307/j.ctv2x00vcx.13)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' faculty interviews in departments that had received SEI funding found evidence of "slippage" in implemented reforms, attributed to the end of funding and to new faculty not involved with the SEI teaching transformed courses. No sample size or effect size is printed for this finding.

> "our faculty interviews have provided evidence of “slippage” in departments where reforms were made, due to the end of funding and new faculty who were not involved with the SEI teaching the transformed courses."
