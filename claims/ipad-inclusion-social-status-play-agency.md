## Evidence

### Van Dusen 2014

Van Dusen, B. (2014). The Roots of Physics Students' Motivations: Fear and Integrity (Doctoral dissertation, School of Education). University of Colorado Boulder. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1502.04256](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1502.04256)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Exploratory pilot study (Van Dusen & Otero, 2012) in an urban high school physics class using "Ethnographic field notes and student survey responses"; the authors report the three observed trends and hypothesize these experiences could lead to learning. No effect sizes are reported.

> "Ethnographic field notes and student survey responses led to the observation of three general trends in students interactions with iPads, screencasting, and physics: (1) students demonstrated increased social status, (2) students demonstrated opportunities to engage in play, and (3) students demonstrated a sense of agency in their work."
