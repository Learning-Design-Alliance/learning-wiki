Bahramnezhad Fatemeh, Keshmiri Fatemeh. (2025). Clinical reasoning education: evaluating the effect of script strategy and reflective practice on nursing students’ reasoning development. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-025-07338-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quantitative results of a quasi-experimental study of 58 nursing clerkship students, assessed with a seven-question hypothesis formation test scored 0–20 by two independent raters. The mean was "18.54 ± 11.33 (out of 20)" and scores were "higher than the cut-off score (p-value = 0.001)" against the Cohen-method cutoff of 10.8.

> "The mean score of the hypothesis formation test was reported as 18.54 ± 11.33 (out of 20) the minimum score was 16.50 and the maximum was 20. The results showed the students’ score in reasoning skills was higher than the cut-off score (p-value = 0.001)."
