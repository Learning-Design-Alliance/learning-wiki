## Evidence

### Fuoad Sura Ali 2022

Fuoad Sura Ali, El-Sayed Walid, Marei Hesham. (2022). Effect of different teaching/learning approaches using virtual patients on student’s situational interest and cognitive load: a comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03831-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intragroup Friedman repeated-measures comparison of median situational interest in Group 1 (n=69, lecture then independent VPs); Table 1 prints p < 0.001 for the independent VPs session. Wilcoxon pairwise tests showed the decline significant "between 30 minutes and 40 minutes (p-value: < 0.001)".

> "However, there was a significant difference between the median score at repeated time points during conducting the independent VPs session (Table 1)."
