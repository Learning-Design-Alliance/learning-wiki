## Evidence

### Ximenes Barrat 2025

Ximenes Barrat, V., Diercks, C., Berliner, B., Derby, K., Chen Gaddini, M., & Pivnick, L. (2025). Revisiting California's invisible achievement gap: Trends in education outcomes of students in foster care in the context of the local control funding formula. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resource/revisiting-californias-invisible-achievement-gap/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of CDE CALPADS data, 2016–17 through 2022–23, defining chronic absence as missing 10 percent or more of expected attendance days. When absences peaked in 2021–22, students in foster care with any absences averaged 24 days absent versus 17 for all students.

> "students in foster care, who were struggling with attendance even prior to the pandemic, remained much more likely to be chronically absent compared to students in other high-need groups. In 2021–22, nearly half of students in foster care were chronically absent"
