## Evidence

### Kiyoshi Shikino 2025

Kiyoshi Shikino, Kazuyo Yamauchi, Nobuyuki Araki, Ikuo Shimizu, Hajime Kasai, Tomoko Tsukamoto, Hiroshi Tajima, Yu Li, Misaki Onodera, Shoichi Ito. (2025). Understanding Community Health Care Through Problem-Based Learning With Real-Patient Videos: Single-Arm Pre-Post Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/68743

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Tutor-assessed 5-dimension rubric (each dimension on a 10-point scale) applied during the two PBL core time sessions. Wilcoxon signed rank comparison showed total scores rising from "median 38, IQR 33-43" to "median 40, IQR 35-44".

> "The students' total scores significantly improved from core time 1 (median 38, IQR 33-43) to core time 2 (median 40, IQR 35-44), with P<.001 and an effect size (r) of 0.516."
