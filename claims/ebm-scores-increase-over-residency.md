## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Mean EBM test scores rose significantly from PGY-1 to PGY-2 (Cohen's d = 0.49) and PGY-1 to PGY-3 (Cohen's d = 0.91) for all residents. [→ Feldstein David A 2010](#feldstein-david-a-2010)

## Evidence

### Feldstein David A 2010

Feldstein David A, Maenner Matthew J, Srisurichan Rachaya, Roach Mary A, Vogelman Bennett S. (2010). Evidence-based medicine training during residency: a randomized controlled trial of efficacy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-59

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal within-cohort analysis of all 48 residents' repeated test scores found the PGY-2 mean "1.35 points" higher than PGY-1 (d = 0.49) and the PGY-3 mean 2.79 points higher (d = 0.91), both significant.

> "The mean score at PGY-2 was 1.35 points (95% CI: 0.55, 2.16) higher than at PGY-1 (p = 0.001), Cohen's d = 0.49. The mean score at PGY-3 was 2.79 points (95% CI: 1.86, 3.73) higher than at PGY-1 (p < 0.001), Cohen's d = 0.91."
