## Evidence

### Hodgkiss 2018

Hodgkiss, A., Gilligan, K. A., Tolmie, A. K., Thomas, M. S. C., & Farran, E. K. (2018). Spatial cognition and science achievement: The contribution of intrinsic and extrinsic spatial skills from 7 to 11 years. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 88(4). https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12211

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical/stepwise multiple regression on N = 123 children aged 7–11; covariates age and BPVS-III entered first. In the final model, which accounted for 65% of the variance in total science scores, mental folding was a stronger predictor (β = .211) than scaling (β = .162).

> "Mental folding accounted for an additional 6% of the variance in total science score, ∆F(1,119) = 20.62, p = <.001"
