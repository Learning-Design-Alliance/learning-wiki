## Evidence

### Amaresh Das 2010

Amaresh Das. (2010). Econometric Assessment of 'One Minute' Paper as a Pedagogic Tool. International Education Studies, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v3n1p17

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

SUR estimation of two separate post-test equations for the two Fall 2005 sections (30 and 29 students), both taught with the one-minute paper, using the student version of Eviews. The article reports equal effectiveness; no effect size is printed.

> "It is found to be equally effective when I have  tested it by using a seemingly unrelated regression that allows the error terms to be correlated across separate but related regressions."
