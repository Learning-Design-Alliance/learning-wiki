q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review synthesis of eight surveys on GP attitudes to EBM. The review reports "a minority of GPs (range 11% to 42%) perceived EBM to be of limited value in primary care" while a clear majority felt positive or welcoming. In one Australian survey, 27% agreed EBM could be described as 'cookbook medicine.'

"Four surveys indicated that a minority of GPs (range 11% to 42%) perceived EBM to be of limited value in primary care, [9, 13, 14, 43] although these and four additional surveys found a clear majority of respondents felt positive or welcoming towards EBM"
