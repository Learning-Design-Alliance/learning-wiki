## Evidence

### Sakamoto Kayo 2026

Sakamoto Kayo, Mitani Sohei, Nishio Naoki, Kitani Takashi, Sato Eriko, Tanaka Keiko, Ugumori Toru, Wakisaka Hiroyuki, Hato Naohito. (2026). Development of an electrosurgery-compatible simulation task for quantitatively assessing oral cancer resection skills: initial validity evidence. BMC Medical Education, 26, 408. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08743-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-task questionnaire study: 10 male fellowship-trained head and neck surgeons (median age 42 years) completed a 5-point Likert questionnaire after performing the task. Replication scored 4.40 and quantitative measures 4.80; the surgeons "highly valued the convenience of the simulation task (4.90)". No effect size is printed.

> "The replication of oral cancer resection was rated highly (4.40). Furthermore, the surgeons agreed with the four quantitative measures used to assess surgeons' skills (4.80)."
