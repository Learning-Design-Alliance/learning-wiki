## Evidence

### Juliette C. Désiron 2026

Juliette C. Désiron, Julien Venni, Mireille Bétrancourt. (2026). Mapping the Heterogeneity of Emotional Design Features in Multimedia Research: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10138-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic content review of 35 studies on emotional design in pictorial representations, coded within and between studies. The review reports "a substantial heterogeneity in emotional design implementations, especially in terms of visual sophistication and anthropomorphic elements."

> "Content analysis within and between studies, revealed a substantial heterogeneity in emotional design implementations, especially in terms of visual sophistication and anthropomorphic elements."

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Fine-grained coding of the 10 unique human-cell materials (Fig. 3; Table 5) found inconsistencies even within single studies: "a cell might have a mouth and eyes when another has eyes only" in the same condition.

> "The analysis revealed various inconsistencies in terms of experimental manipulation, even within the same study (Fig. 3; Table 5). For anthropomorphism, within the same condition, a cell might have a mouth and eyes when another has eyes only"
