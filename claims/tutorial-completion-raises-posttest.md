## Evidence

### Pusic Martin V 2012

Pusic Martin V, MacDonald Wendy A, Eisman Harley O, Black John B. (2012). Reinforcing outpatient medical student learning using brief computer tutorials: the Patient-Teacher-Tutorial sequence. BMC Medical Education, 12:70. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-70

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison in the randomized trial between the 92 protocol completers who did a tutorial and the 52 who completed the post-test but no tutorial: post-test means of 54.0% (SD 13%) versus 37.0% (SD 12.5%), 95% CI for difference +12.5%, +21.5%. No standardized effect size printed.

> "Students who were able to complete a tutorial scored higher on the post-test (54.0% (SD 13%) vs. 37.0% (12.5%) than did the 52 who did the post-test but not a tutorial (95% CI for difference +12.5%, +21.5%)."
