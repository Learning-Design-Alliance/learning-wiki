### Wang 2026

Wang, V. (2026). California's universal prekindergarten implementation, 2024–25. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/540.887

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results on SEL strategies (Table 6, 2022–23 to 2024–25). Play-based learning rose from 69% to 82%; developmental observations rose from 32% to 61%; open-ended, self-directed learning opportunities rose from 19% to 48%.

> "In 2024–25, the most common strategies included play-based learning (82%), designing developmentally appropriate learning environments (73%), and engaging in developmental observations (61%)."
