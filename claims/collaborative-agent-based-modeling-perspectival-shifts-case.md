## Evidence

### Farris 2014

Farris, A. V., & Sengupta, P. (2014). Perspectival Computational Thinking for Learning Physics: A Case Study of Collaborative Agent-based Modeling. Proceedings of the 11th International Conference of the Learning Sciences (ICLS 2014). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1403.3790](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1403.3790)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interaction-analysis case study of 23 minutes of collaboration between a dyad of 10-year-old males (Arnav and Liam) using ViMAP to model two graphs of motion; multimodal transcripts were coded line-by-line for the speaker's POV. The study "examine[s] the process through which computational thinking develops in a perspectival fashion."

> "We examine the process through which computational thinking develops in a perspectival fashion as two middle school students collaborate with each other in order to develop computational models of two graphs of motion."
