# Network television news exposure does not increase with age and shows little intergenerational similarity

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Network television news exposure shows no evidence of age-related differences at either measurement point, and parent-child similarity is not particularly large. [→ Tims 1983](#tims-1983)

## Evidence

### Tims 1983

Tims, Albert R., Jr. (1983). Development of Public Affairs Media Use. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED238044

Age-group ANOVAs of days watching national network news in the two-wave survey were nonsignificant at both waves; mean viewing fell from 3.25 days (winter) to 2.69 days (fall), a significant decline (t = 3.61, p < .001).
