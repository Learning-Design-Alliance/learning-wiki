### Badrinarayan 2025

Badrinarayan, A. (2025). Instructionally relevant assessment systems: What is the role of performance assessments? Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/411.469

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

A multi-state listening tour asked teachers and district instructional leaders what instructional changes they make and why; educators unprompted reported altering instruction to match statewide summative and interim assessments, including cutting projects and adding test review.

> "teachers frequently share that they change high-quality instructional materials and approaches to better match the tests. These changes typically serve to limit opportunities for deep, engaging, and meaningful learning."
