## Evidence

### Bing 2007

Bing, T. J., & Redish, E. F. (2007). Analyzing Problem Solving Using Math in Physics: Epistemological Framing via Warrants. https://www.compadre.org/PER/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Ethnographic observation from videotaped study sessions across a range of physics courses led the authors to this conjecture about frame stickiness; the article offers it as an observational conjecture, not a tested comparison, and prints no effect size.

> "especially for students who are closer to the novice level, epistemological frames can be "sticky"; that is, stu- dents can get trapped in them for relatively long peri- ods of time (many minutes)."
