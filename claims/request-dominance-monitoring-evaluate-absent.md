Rania Abdelghani, Peter Kaiser, and Kou Murayama. (2026). Regulating the AI Tutor: Intentions, Help-Seeking, and Self-Regulated Learning in Adolescent GenAI Use. Accepted at the NextGen Learning Interfaces Workshop, AIED 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08568 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.08568](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.08568)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

AI-coded deductive SRL scheme applied to all 808 student turns using Gemini 2.5 Pro with a frozen prompt, fixed seed. The article reports descriptive distributions (Figure 3) and states most students rarely made comprehension needs explicit or evaluated AI responses; no inferential test is printed.

> "Interactions were dominated by REQUEST turns (M= 72.9%of task-relevant turns;M dn= 75.0%, IQR63.6–87.5%). PLAN was the only other frequent function (M= 18.1%,M dn= 15.1%). MON- ITOR and EVALUATE were nearly absent, with medians of0%and means of5.7%and3.4%respectively"
