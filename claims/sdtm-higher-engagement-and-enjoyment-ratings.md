## Subclaims
`q3 i3` SDTM trainees rated lessons more enjoyable (4.28 vs 3.64, d = 0.96) and reported more initiative (4.41 vs 3.26, d = 1.82) on 5-point Likert feedback. [→ Yang Liu 2022](#yang-liu-2022)

## Evidence

### Yang Liu 2022

Yang Liu, Li Wen, Zou Jian, An Junnan, Zeng Bin, Zheng Yitao, Yang Jiming, Ren Jia. (2022). The application of the spot the difference teaching method in clinical skills training for residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03612-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous 5-point Likert questionnaire after rotation: "I would act as a teacher" 4.41 vs 3.26, T = 8.171, P = 0.000, d = 1.82; enjoyability 4.28 vs 3.64, d = 0.96; learn-a-lot 4.28 vs 3.69, d = 0.85.

> "This novel teaching method enabled the residents to show more initiative in class (4.41 versus 3.26; P < 0.001) with a large effect size (d = 1.82)"
