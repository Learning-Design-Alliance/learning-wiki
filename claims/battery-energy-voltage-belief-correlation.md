## Subclaims
`q2 i3` On DIRECT 1.1 questions about two batteries in series, believing they provide more energy (46%) was associated with believing they provide more voltage (51%), Pearson r = 0.45; parallel-battery beliefs correlated at r = 0.37 and same-energy beliefs at r = 0.41.
