## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yang 2025

Yang, L.-C., LAI, C.-Y., Chen, K.-M., Chen, M.-C., Chang, C.-C., Lin, T.-Y., & Kuo, C.-F. (2025). Competencies and needs of older volunteers: A comparative survey. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000712

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA and Scheffe results from the 1,000-participant survey. The article reports "F = 4.23, p = .015" for the skills dimension and post hoc contrasts in which the least-experienced group "had more needs" than the 1–5-year and 5+-year groups.

> "Volunteering experience and educational level were shown to significantly influence volunteer service skill needs (F = 4.23, p = .015 and F = 19.24, p < .001, respectively). Participants with <1 year of volunteering experience had more needs than those who had served for either 1–5 years or 5 years or more (p = .025 and .016, respectively)"
