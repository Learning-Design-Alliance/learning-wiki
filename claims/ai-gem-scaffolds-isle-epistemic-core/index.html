Eugenio Tufino 2026¶

Eugenio Tufino, Paola Damiani. (2026). Embodied inquiry with AI as facilitator: an exploratory case study. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21349

q2 · i? · design · r2

Exploratory reading of chat transcripts from three student pairs interacting with a custom Gem named “Physics ISLE Lab process” built on Google Gemini 3 Pro. The Gem “named the epistemic stage” and requested multiple explanations before tests, with Pair B’s inverted order flagged and restored.

"Across the three pairs, the Gem’s first moves followed the ISLE script closely. After the groups had floated the cans and shared photographs, it named the epistemic stage and asked for at least two or three distinct explanations."
