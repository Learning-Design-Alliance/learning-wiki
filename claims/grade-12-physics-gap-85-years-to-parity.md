### Eamonn Corrigan 2023

Eamonn Corrigan, Martin Williams, and Mary A. Wells. (2023). High School Enrolment Choices — Understanding the STEM Gender Gap. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/s42330-023-00285-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42330-023-00285-y)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weighted least squares regression of median female proportion over time for Ontario public schools (Table I: 12U Physics estimate +0.157, p = .017). The article calls the increase statistically significant but minimal, and reports the 85-year extrapolation under an assumed constant annual change of 0.16 percentage points.

> "Extrapolating from the 2018 level of 36 .4 ± 0.5% and assuming a constant annual change of 0.16 percentage points, it would take 85 years – until 2103 – for grade 12U Physics to reach a median female proportion of 50%."
