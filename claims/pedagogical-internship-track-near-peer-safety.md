Interview quote from Respondent 4 in the 'Developing a culture of peer teaching and learning' subtheme, describing perceived psychological safety and effectiveness of learning in the near-peer group.

> "There is a higher level of openness when doing peer-assisted learning; people are more humble about what they know and don’t know, and being in a safe group makes learning much more effective."
