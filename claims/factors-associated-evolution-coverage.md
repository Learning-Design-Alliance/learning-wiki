### Hidaka BH 2015

Hidaka BH, Asghar A, Aktipis CA, Nesse RM, Wolpaw TM, Skursky NK, Bennett KJ, Beyrouty MW, Schwartz MD. (2015). The status of evolutionary medicine education in North American medical schools. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0322-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlations between school-level coverage and curriculum-time variables in the 2013 dean survey, reported in Results. The article prints "rs = 0.59, P < 0.01" and "rs = 0.46, P < 0.01"; correlations are treated as the printed effect sizes.

> "A school’s global rating of evolution coverage was correlated with the number of hours devoted to these topics (rs = 0.59, P < 0.01), hours spent teaching how to apply evolutionary thinking to specific medical problems (rs = 0.46, P < 0.01),"

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`
