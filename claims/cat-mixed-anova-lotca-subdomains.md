## Evidence

### Belhan Çelik 2025

Belhan Çelik, Sümeyye; Bumin, Gonca. (2025). Improving Refugee Children: A Randomized Controlled Trial on the Impact of Cognitive Activity Training. BMC Psychology, 13, 1186. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03586-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA in the RCT (study n=17, control n=17). Simple effects with Bonferroni correction showed no pre-intervention group differences; "post-intervention scores significantly improved in the study group (p <.001), with no significant changes observed in the control group".

> "Mixed-design ANOVA results revealed statistically significant interaction effects in the LOTCA subdimensions, specifically in SP, MP, VMO, TP, and Attention scores (respectively; F(1.32) = 32.099, p <.001; F(1.32) = 13.74, p =.001, F(1.32) = 63.566, p <.001; F(1.32) = 58.863, p <.001; F(1.32) = 24.123, p <.001) (Table 4)."
