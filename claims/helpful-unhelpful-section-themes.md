## Evidence

### Mueller 2026

Mueller, L. O., Bamrah, M., Petrie, K. L., Tour, E., McDonnell, L., Lo, S. M., & Meaders, C. L. (2026). The Fourth Credit Hour: How do we Teach in Biology Discussion Sections? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0185

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive thematic analysis of 4053 optional open responses by two coders to 97.4% final agreement (average Cohen's Kappa 0.79). Seven helpful and five unhelpful aspects were identified; the most common unhelpful codes were problems with content/IA, environments not conducive to learning, and modality.

> "the majority (3385, 84%) included statements that were coded as helpful aspects of discussion sections, while a smaller number (753, 18%) included statements that were coded as unhelpful aspects of discussion sections"
