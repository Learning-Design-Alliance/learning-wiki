## Evidence

### Grainger R 2018

Grainger R, Dai W, Osborne E, Kenwright D. (2018). Medical students create multiple-choice questions for learning in pathology education: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1312-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Technology Acceptance Model survey subscale (Cronbach's α = 0.896) administered to 62 students on a seven-point Likert scale. The authors report "73% (45/62) of students agreed that PeerWise is easy to use" while only 29% (18/62) agreed it is a good learning tool.

> "Although 73% (45/62) of students agreed that PeerWise is easy to use, 61% (38/62) of students did not perceive PeerWise as useful in enhancing their learning."
