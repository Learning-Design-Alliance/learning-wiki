## Evidence

### Taylor A. W. R. 2026

Taylor A. W. R., Patel F. Y., Smyth E. K., Gay S., and Bird T. (2026). AI in the Classroom: Observing Preclinical Students' Use of ChatGPT During Case‐Based Learning at a UK Medical School. The Clinical Teacher 23, no. 2: e70360. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70360

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Observational contrast across CBL groups in the focused ethnography; field notes describe one weakly collaborative group that "utilised ChatGPT more than any other group as they were not interacting".

> "Less interactive groups were more likely to use ChatGPT for passive question‐answering and to use it before or instead of group discussion. Outputs were often accepted without group analysis or cross‐checking."
