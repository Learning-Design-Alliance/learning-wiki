## Evidence

### Zipeng Ji 2025

Zipeng Ji, Pengcheng An, and Jian Zhao. (2025). ClassComet: Exploring and Designing AI-generated Danmaku in Educational Videos to Enhance Online Learning. [doi:10.1145/3715336.3735762](https://doi.org/10.1145/3715336.3735762)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Formative study with seven university students combining contextual inquiry while watching Bilibili educational videos and semi-structured interviews; the team coded transcripts into an affinity diagram deriving five insights, finding danmaku should be "knowledgeable, carry positive emotions, show encouragement, and be expressed in effective ways".
