## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students given their midterm rank scored 3.517 points higher on the final examination on average than control students, a statistically significant difference. [→ Kajitani Shinya 2020](#kajitani-shinya-2020)

## Evidence

### Kajitani Shinya 2020

Kajitani Shinya, Morimoto Keiichi, Suzuki Shiba. (2020). Information feedback in relative grading: Evidence from a field experiment. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231548

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Field RCT in a compulsory economics course at a Japanese university; OLS with classroom fixed effects and midterm score covariate on 254 students. The treatment coefficient was "3.517 and statistically significant". No standardized effect size printed.

> "The magnitude of the coefficient for D is 3.517 and statistically significant. This indicates that the scores in the final examination for students who received information on their relative rank in the midterm examination are 3.517 points higher on average than the scores for students who did not get this information."
