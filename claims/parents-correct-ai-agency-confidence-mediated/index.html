In the parent Correct-AI mediation model, agency negatively impacted confidence (β = -0.4668, p = 0.0236), confidence significantly positively impacted intention to use (β = 0.5200, p < 0.001), and the indirect effect of agency on ITU through confidence was statistically significant (Effect = -0.2428, BootLLCI = -0.4956, BootULCI = -0.0347). → Alexander John Karran 2025
