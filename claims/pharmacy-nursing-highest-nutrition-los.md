### Kupis Robert 2026

Kupis Robert, Rudzińska Anna, Piotrowicz Karolina. (2026). Nutrition-related competencies in undergraduate training of health professionals in Poland: a descriptive analysis of educational standards. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09284-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive comparative analysis of Polish educational standards. Pharmacy contained 16 nutrition-related learning objectives out of 320 total, nursing bachelor 10 out of 257, and nursing master 4 out of 183.

> "Pharmacy and combined nursing programmes (bachelor and master) consisted of the biggest number of nutrition-related LOs, while none were identified for paramedics, laboratory diagnostics and physiotherapy."
