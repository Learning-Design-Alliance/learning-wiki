`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the 931-nurse cross-sectional questionnaire survey. The authors note scores exceeding 70% of the maximum indicate sufficient knowledge, positive attitude, and proactive practice; the practice median at the scale maximum is descriptive, with no inferential test reported for this claim.

> "The median scores for knowledge, attitude, and practice were 7 (IQR 6–8), 46 (IQR 40–50), and 50 (IQR 42–50), respectively. The practice score showed a ceiling effect, with the median reaching the maximum possible value (50), suggesting limited discrimination at the upper end."
