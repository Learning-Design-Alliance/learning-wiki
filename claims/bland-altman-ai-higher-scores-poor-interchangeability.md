### Alpay Suha 2025

Alpay Suha, Darafarin Yasemen, Dagdelen Burcu, Al-Shammari Sana Mahroos Mkhailef, Buyukbayram Isil Kaya. (2025). Comparison of ChatGPT-4o and expert evaluation in endodontic education: a cross-sectional pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08358-2

Agreement analysis of the same AI–expert score pairs. The article reports wide limits of agreement (e.g., overextension/perforation mean difference 1.58, LoA −1.6 to 4.76) and that "AI tended to assign slightly higher scores than the expert evaluator across most criteria".
