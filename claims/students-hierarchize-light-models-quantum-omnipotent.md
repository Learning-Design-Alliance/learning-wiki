# Students arrange light models in a strict hierarchy in which the quantum model, introduced last, is seen as encompassing the others and closer to reality

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · qualitative `r?` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2004 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Many students' model conceptions are associated with a strict hierarchy: the ray model is perceived as contained in the wave model, and wave and ray models as contained in the quantum model, giving the last-introduced quantum model an almost omnipotent position. [→ Martin Erik Horn 2004](#martin-erik-horn-2004)
`q2 i?` Students legitimate the quantum model's outstanding position by claiming it is closer to reality, assuming reality has been recognized by the teacher or experts. [→ Martin Erik Horn 2004 (2)](#martin-erik-horn-2004-2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Analysis of interview excerpt III from the holography study; the student treats earlier-taught models as having lower explanatory capacity and new model structures as mere expansions of known explanation patterns rather than independent constructs.

> "As the following interview excerpt (figure 7) shows, the student perceives the model of rays as contained in the wave model and the wave and ray model contained in the quantum model."

### Martin Erik Horn 2004 (2)

Martin Erik Horn, Antje Leisner, Helmut F. Mikelskis. (2004). Difficulties of Model Construction in Optics. German Physical Society Spring Conference Leipzig 2002. Originally published 2002. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0402080](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0402080)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Authors' interpretation following interview excerpt III, in which the student answers "Yes, it is therefore better, it is closer to reality" when asked whether the quantum model encompasses the other two models.

> "The outstanding position of the quantum model in the eyes of the students is legitimated in that the quantum model is closer to reality. This claim is based on the assumption that reality can indeed be recognized and has in fact been recognized by the teacher or other experts."
