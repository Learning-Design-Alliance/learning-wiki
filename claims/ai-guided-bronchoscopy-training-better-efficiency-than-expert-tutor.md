## Evidence

### Agbontaen Kaladerhan O. 2025

Agbontaen Kaladerhan O., Cold Kristoffer M., Woods David, Grover Vimal, Aboumarie Hatem Soliman, Kaul Sundeep, Konge Lars, Singh Suveer. (2025). Artificial Intelligence-Guided Bronchoscopy is Superior to Human Expert Instruction for the Performance of Critical-Care Physicians: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Critical Care Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/CCM.0000000000006629

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Parallel-group RCT of 40 critical-care physicians (19 AIG, 21 ETG) randomized 1:1 by stratified block after a pretraining baseline test. The results section reports the AI group more efficient with "MIT (–7.9 s, 0.027)", a moderate effect size of 0.35 per Cohen's classification.

> "At final test bronchoscopy, the AIG were (median difference, p): more efficient, MIT (–7.9 s, 0.027) and SR (–7 revisits, 0.019), and significantly faster than ETG (–77 s, 0.022)"
