Evidence¶

Abderrahim Agnaou; Hayat El Asri 2025¶

Abderrahim Agnaou; Hayat El Asri. (2025). Artificial Intelligence and Collaborative Learning: Impacts on Creativity, Critical Thinking, and Problem-Solving. JERAP Vol. 15 (2025) Iss. 1. https://doi.org/10.5590/JERAP.2025.15.2120

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Mixed-methods study of LLM use in team-based undergraduate projects; the reported null finding is that there were "no significant differences in student perceptions" across digital familiarity, AI comfort, and prior AI use. No effect size is printed, so no magnitude is reported.

"Findings revealed no significant differences in student perceptions based on digital familiarity, AI comfort, or prior AI use, suggesting that digital competency does not strongly shape how students perceive the influence of LLMs on collaboration and creativity."
