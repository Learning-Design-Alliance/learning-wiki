# Content Acquisition Podcasts produced significantly higher posttest scores than textbook-based instruction for preservice teachers

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Preservice teachers taught with Content Acquisition Podcasts achieved significantly higher posttest scores than controls using textbooks, with d = 1.09 and d = 1.21 across two content areas. [→ Adrie A. Koehler 2023](#adrie-a-koehler-2023)

## Evidence

### Adrie A. Koehler 2023

Adrie A. Koehler, Daniela Rezende Vilarinho-Pereira. (2023). Using social media affordances to support Ill-structured problem-solving skills: considering possibilities and challenges. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10060-1

`q3 · i3` · `review · r2`

Quantitative experiment with 164 preservice teachers in an introductory special education course; the experimental group received instruction using CAPs while controls had textbooks, a graphic organizer, and an outline. The review reports the experimental group "achieved significantly higher scores than the participants from the control group" (Course Content Area 1: F(1,156) = 43.10, d = 1.09; Area 2: F(1,150) = 57.61, d = 1.21).

> "the participants in the experimental group (Course Content Area 1: M = 12.4, SD = 1.89; Course Content Area 2: M = 13.9 SD = .99) achieved significantly higher scores than the participants from the control group"
