# RAN predicts irregular word spelling in Yrs 5–6, regular word spelling in Yrs 2 and 6, and pseudoword spelling in Yr 2, varying with spelling experience

## Evidence

### Niolaki 2022

Niolaki, G., Vousden, J., Terzopoulos, A., Shepherd, D.-L., Debney, L., & Masterson, J. (2022). Spelling predictors; investigating the role of phonological ability and rapid naming in a large cross-sectional British study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101635

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-model RAN-by-year interactions for 641 children controlling for reading and sublexical knowledge; significant RAN effects appeared only in specific year groups per word type (e.g., Yr 2*RAN for regular words, t = -4.1).

> "RAN was a significant predictor of irregular word spelling only in Yrs 5 and 6. RAN was also predicting regular word spelling in Yr 2 and 6 and pseudoword spelling in Yr 2."
