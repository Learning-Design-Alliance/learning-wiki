## Evidence

### Zhang 2023

Zhang, D., Ke, S. (E.), & Mo, Y. (2023). Morphology in Reading Comprehension Among School-Aged Readers of English: A Synthesis and Meta-Analytic Structural Equation Modeling Study. Journal of Educational Psychology, 115(5), 683–699. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000797

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

TSSEM Stage 2 analysis of the pooled correlation matrix (k = 107, N = 21,818) estimated indirect effects as the product of component paths. Effects were "β = .140 [.105, .177]" via word reading and "β = .190 [.160, .221]" via vocabulary knowledge, indicating partial mediation.

> "The indirect effects of MA on reading comprehension were also significant: β = .140 [.105, .177] and β = .190 [.160, .221] for the routes via word reading and vocabulary knowledge, respectively."
