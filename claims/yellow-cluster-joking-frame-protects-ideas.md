Qualitative episode analysis of group B on the electrostatics tutorial, where explanation-like ideas first appeared alongside joking behavior. The authors interpret that "such ambiguity can be protective for a speaker who ventures an idea the group might judge unfavorably."

> "Such ambiguity can be protective for a speaker who ventures an idea the group might judge unfavorably; Chris may pose the idea as a joke in order to incur less risk in sharing it."
