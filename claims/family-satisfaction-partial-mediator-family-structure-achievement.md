## Evidence

### Gordeeva Tamara O. 2026

Gordeeva Tamara O., Dvorskaya Elena V., Sychev Oleg A. (2026). Family structure and family satisfaction as factors of adolescents' autonomous motivation, cheating, and academic achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0357562

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling (Mplus 8, MLR, 5,000 bootstrap resamples) on the same sample of 2,402 adolescents tested indirect effects via family satisfaction; the model "indicated an excellent fit" with CFI = 0.998, RMSEA = 0.048.

> "in particular the effects of family structure on academic autonomous motivation (0.08; p ≤ 0.001; 95% CI [0.05, 0.11]) and autonomous homework motivation (0.09; p ≤ 0.001; 95% CI [0.05, 0.12]). We also found the overall statistically significant indirect effect of family structure (via family satisfaction and then via the other variables) on GPA (0.04; p ≤ 0.001; 95% CI [0.03, 0.06]) and homework cheating (–0.06; p ≤ 0.001; 95% CI [–0.08, –0.04])."
