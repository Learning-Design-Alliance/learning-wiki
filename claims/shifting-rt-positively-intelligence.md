## Evidence

### Ger Ebru 2023

Ger Ebru, Roebers Claudia M. (2023). The Relationship between Executive Functions, Working Memory, and Intelligence in Kindergarten Children. Journal of Intelligence. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10143737/

`q3 · i2`

Exploratory correlational analysis in the N = 147 sample: mean RT in the mixed HF block correlated positively with RIAS intelligence, "r = 0.23, p < .01", holding after controlling for accuracy. Authors call this counterintuitive and suggest more intelligent children may inhibit themselves to respond more slowly.
