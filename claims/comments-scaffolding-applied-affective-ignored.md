## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Sequential analysis showed participants preferred to apply feedback containing comments or scaffolding and to ignore positive, negative, or assessing feedback. [→ Junting Yin 2025](#junting-yin-2025)

## Evidence

### Junting Yin 2025

Junting Yin, Zheyu Jin, Yuxuan Zhang, Xuening Li, Yangzhuo Li, Guoping Zhang & Junlong Luo. (2025). Applying, not ignoring: how feedback uptake and neural synchrony drive creativity. npj Science of Learning 10:61. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00351-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Behavioral sequence analysis using GSEQ 5.1 with adjusted residuals (z > 1.96 threshold) for IF and OF groups. The article concludes that "why" (comment) or "how" (scaffolding) feedback was more likely applied, and more application was associated with better acquisition.

> "when participants received feedback containing comments or scaff olding, individuals preferred to apply the feedback. In contrast, when participants received positive or negative feedback or assessed feedback, they were more likely to ignore it."
