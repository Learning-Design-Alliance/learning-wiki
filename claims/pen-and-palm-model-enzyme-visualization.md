The review's Table 2 characterizes the Pen and Palm work as a conceptual model and pedagogical proposal, not an empirical trial: "complex biochemical concepts, such as enzyme function, can be effectively visualized" using a pen and palm. The review states further validation was recommended; no outcome data are printed.
