## Evidence

### Rienzo et al. 2015

Cinzia Rienzo, Heather Rolfe and David Wilkinson (NIESR). (2015). Changing Mindsets: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581132

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial of the pupil workshops intervention involving six schools and 286 pupils, with pupils randomly allocated to mindset workshops or a time-matched study-skills active control. The evaluation reports "two additional months' progress in both English and maths" that were not statistically significant, with the English finding close to significance.

> "made an average of two additional months' progress in both English and maths compared to those in the control group. These findings were not statistically significant"
