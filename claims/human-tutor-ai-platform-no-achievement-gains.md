## Evidence

### Robinson 2026

Robinson, Carly D., David Gormley, Ana Trindade Ribeiro, and Susanna Loeb. (2026). Access is Not Enough: Human Support Improves Engagement with AI Tutoring. EdWorkingPaper No. 26-1451, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai26-1451

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ITT analysis of end-of-year ELA scores (i-Ready in District A, aimsWeb in District B) from two RCTs (166 and 161 observations). Table 1 shows point estimates of -0.052 and -0.129; no statistically significant effect was detected, and equivalence was not tested.

> "We did not find evidence that working with a human tutor improved reading achievement in either district (Table 1, Column 4). In both districts, the estimated effects on students' spring ELA scores were not statistically significant and negative in magnitude."
