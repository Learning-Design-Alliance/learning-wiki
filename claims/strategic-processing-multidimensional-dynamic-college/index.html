Patricia A. Alexander 2017¶

Patricia A. Alexander. (2017). Issues of Constructs, Contexts, and Continuity: Commentary on Learning in Higher Education. Educational Psychology Review, 29, 345–351. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9409-3

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary reports that Dinsmore's (2017) systematic review examined 134 studies of strategic processing, considering levels-of-processing, trait, and state models, and that this examination "reinforces his core argument" for multidimensional, dynamic models. As reported second-hand in the commentary.

"His detailed examination of 134 studies reinforces his core argument that any theoretical model of strategic processing among college students must embrace its multidimensional and dynamic nature."
