## Evidence

### Kramer 2024

Kramer, A.-W., Huizenga, H. M., Van Duijvenvoorde, A. C. K., & Krabbendam, L. (2024). Do I want to learn today? Day-to-day variations in adolescents' academic motivation and effort. Learning and Motivation, 85. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lmot.2023.101957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the self-report model, physically attending school (contrast coded) positively predicted academic motivation, "β= 0.27, 95% CI = [0.14, 0.40]"; data were collected during COVID-19 partial school closings.

> "Finally, we found a positive between-person effect of physical school attendance (β= 0.27, 95% CI = [0.14, 0.40])."

### Kramer 2024 (2)

Kramer, A.-W., Huizenga, H. M., Van Duijvenvoorde, A. C. K., & Krabbendam, L. (2024). Do I want to learn today? Day-to-day variations in adolescents' academic motivation and effort. Learning and Motivation, 85. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lmot.2023.101957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Average stress (β= 0.20), average homework hours (β= 0.18) and time (β= 0.08) were trending positive between-person predictors, each with "90% CI" excluding zero but not 95% CIs.

> "We also found trending between-person effects of stress, (β= 0.20, 90% CI = [0.01, 0.38]), homework hours, (β= 0.18, 90% CI = [0.01, 0.37]), and time (day) (β= 0.08, 90% CI = [0.01, 0.15])."
