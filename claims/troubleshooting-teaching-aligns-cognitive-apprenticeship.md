## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Electronics lab instructors' approaches to troubleshooting instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.113.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.113.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Self-reported teaching practices from 20 interviews. Instructors described questioning to elicit expectations (Elm), coaching on what to check (Sycamore), modeling systematic approaches aloud (Alder, Walnut), and scaling support over the semester (Filbert).

> "almost all instructors said they employed one or more of the following practices: asking questions, coaching, verbalizing reasoning, modeling how to troubleshoot, and fading support"
