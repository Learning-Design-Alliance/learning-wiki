Im Ji-Eun, Gu Ja-Young, Bae Jung-Hee, Lee Jae-Gi. (2025). Development and user experience of a three-dimensional object-based virtual reality-simulation tool for dental radiography training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06623-4

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' stated limitation and recommendation in the Discussion: the RCT collected no performance or clinical-competence outcome. "surveys alone are inadequate for objectively verifying educational effectiveness"; they also note the study did not investigate individual user characteristics such as visual fatigue, dizziness, or cyber sickness.

> "Since surveys alone are inadequate for objectively verifying educational effectiveness, future research should incorporate quantitative methods, such as scored practical assessments to provide objective data."
