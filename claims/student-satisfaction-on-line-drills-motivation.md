## Evidence

### Jamie Lentin 2014

Jamie Lentin, Anna H. Jonsdottir, David Stern, Victoria Mokua, Eva Dogg Steingrimsdottir, Magnea Run Vignisdottir and Gunnar Stefansson. (2014). A MOBILE WEB FOR ENHANCING STATISTICS AND MATHEMATICS EDUCATION. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1406.5004](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1406.5004)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

In-class surveys from earlier tutor-web experiments, with similar comments from Kenya, e.g. "Doing maths online was the best experience I ever had with maths", contrasted with almost universal discontent in a previous survey of the same service course.

> "Several in-class surveys reflect a general student satisfaction with the methodology while also demonstrating that an on-line system alone is not enough: Students want a combination of the drilling system which gives immediate feedback as well as regular, marked, homework."
