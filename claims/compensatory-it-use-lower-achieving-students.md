## Evidence

### Hew Khe Foon 2016

Hew Khe Foon, Tan Cheng Yong. (2016). Predictors of Information Technology Integration in Secondary Schools: Evidence from a Large Scale Study of More than 30,000 Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0168547

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

HLM results across models showed consistent negative relationships between mothers' education and prior academic achievement with IT integration. The authors interpret this as suggesting teachers may have used IT-integrated lessons as a compensatory strategy, assigning individual computer use to lower achieving students for immediate learning feedback.

> "boys (Male, β = 0.08, p < .01), students with less educated mothers (MoEdu, β = -0.01, p < .05), and students with lower levels of prior academic achievement (Repeat, β = 0.04, p < .01) reported higher levels of IT integration"
