# After 8 weeks, the AWT experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group on all five writing criteria, with lower error count

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mann Whitney U tests after the application showed meaningful differences favouring the experimental group on adequacy of the word count (p=0.005), form of constructed sentences (p=0.015), punctuation (p=0.005), relevancy in writing (p=0.009) and error count (p=0.003). ## Evidence

### Nazife Burcu TAKIL 2016

Nazife Burcu TAKIL. (2016). The use of “ask, write, throw” technique as a writing skill practice when teaching Turkish as a foreign language. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.2682

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test Mann Whitney U comparison of the 18-learner AWT group and 17-learner control group after 8 weeks. Table 6 reports z values of 2.762, 2.414, 2.788, 2.581 and 2.903; the experimental group "scored higher than the control group; also the error count went down."

> "a meaningful distinction between the writing skills of the control group and the experimental group has occurred over the chosen criteria after the application of the technique (p<0.05)."
