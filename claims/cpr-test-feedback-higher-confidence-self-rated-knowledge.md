## Evidence

### Nord Anette 2017

Nord Anette, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Herlitz Johan, Svensson Leif, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). Effect of two additional interventions, test and reflection, added to standard cardiopulmonary resuscitation training on seventh grade students' practical skills and willingness to act: a cluster randomised trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fixed-response questionnaire in the cluster randomised trial compared self-reported confidence between the CPR-only and test-and-feedback groups, finding "73% vs 88%; p=0.002" directly after training and "73% vs 82%; p=0.025" at 6 months.

> "A lower proportion of the students in group O versus group T stated they felt more confident to act after participating in the training session, when asked directly after training (73% vs 88%; p=0.002) and at 6 months follow-up (73% vs 82%; p=0.025)."

### Nord Anette 2017 (2)

Nord Anette, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Herlitz Johan, Svensson Leif, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). Effect of two additional interventions, test and reflection, added to standard cardiopulmonary resuscitation training on seventh grade students' practical skills and willingness to act: a cluster randomised trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same questionnaire directly after training: self-rated sufficiency of knowledge for chest compressions was "60% (O) versus 81% (T, p<0.001)" and for rescue breathing "57% vs 75% (p<0.001)"; no T-vs-RT differences were found.

> "There were also differences in how the students considered that they had enough knowledge to do chest compressions, 60% (O) versus 81% (T, p<0.001), and to do rescue breathing, 57% vs 75% (p<0.001)."
