M. Said Doğru & Emily K. Faulconer 2026¶

M. Said Doğru & Emily K. Faulconer. (2026). ChatGPT as a Virtual Laboratory Teaching Assistant in Undergraduate Biology. Research in Science Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11165-025-10271-z

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Per-question t-tests following a single-factor ANOVA comparing SME and learner ratings of the 'voice' component for AI- versus human-authored responses. Most questions differed significantly, "with human TAs demonstrating stronger instructor voice for these questions".

"Most questions showed significant differences in instructor voice rating by both instructors and students, with human TAs demonstrating stronger instructor voice for these questions."

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Per-question t-tests on the 'understanding' component of teaching effectiveness, rated by SMEs and students for AI- versus human-authored responses. Most questions differed significantly, "with human TAs demonstrating a more substantial understanding".
