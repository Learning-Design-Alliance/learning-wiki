dos Santos Kawata KH, Ueno Y, Hashimoto R, Yoshino S, Ohta K, Nishida A, Ando S, Nakatani H, Kasai K and Koike S (2021) Development of Metacognition in Adolescence: The Congruency-Based Metacognition Scale. Front. Psychol. 11:565231. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.565231

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations between CMS scores and AQ-J-50 scores were computed in the survey 1 sample (n = 148). The CMS metacognition score correlated with the social skills subscale (r = 0.22, p < 0.01) and total score (r = 0.20, p < 0.05), while no correlations were observed for self-judgment accuracy.

> "The CMS metacognition score was also associated with the AQ-J-50 social skills subscale (weak, r= 0.22, p < 0.01; see Table 5 ) and total score (weak, r= 0.20, p < 0.05)."
