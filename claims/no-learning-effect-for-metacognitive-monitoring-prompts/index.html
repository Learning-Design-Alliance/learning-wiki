Evidence¶

Lallé 2017¶

Lallé, S., Mudrick, N., Conati, C., Taub, M., Azevedo, R. (2017). On the Influence on Learning of Student Compliance with Prompts Fostering Self-Regulated Learning. Proceedings of the 10th International Conference on Educational Data Mining. https://educationaldatamining.org/EDM2017/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Regressions over the three behavioral measures for the 16 students with valid gaze data found no effects for these metacognitive monitoring prompts; Appendix B reports effect sizes such as ηp2 = .00 to .03. The authors caution the dataset is not large.

"We found no effects and small effect sizes (see Appendix B) for the other inferred compliance pro mpts, namely stay on page and move to next page , two prompts related to metacognitive monitor ing processes."
