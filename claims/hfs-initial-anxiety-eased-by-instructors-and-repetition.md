## Evidence

### Murad Amal A. 2025

Murad Amal A., Sawalha Murad A., Maabreh Roqia Saleem, Elsabagh Eman E., Roque Mark Y., Alharbi Muna, Abdelkader Raghad, Saleh Zyad T. (2025). Exploring male nursing students’ experiences and perspectives in a maternal-newborn clinical course using high-fidelity simulation: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07436-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 3 sub-theme from interviews with male nursing students in the maternal-newborn course. One participant said, "At first, I felt nervous and anxious because it was my first time seeing and touching a mannequin with exposed reproductive features".

> "Many students expressed discomfort and unease when they first interacted with the mannequins, particularly during procedures involving reproductive health."

### Murad Amal A. 2025 (2)

Murad Amal A., Sawalha Murad A., Maabreh Roqia Saleem, Elsabagh Eman E., Roque Mark Y., Alharbi Muna, Abdelkader Raghad, Saleh Zyad T. (2025). Exploring male nursing students’ experiences and perspectives in a maternal-newborn clinical course using high-fidelity simulation: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07436-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same qualitative theme, instructor sub-theme. Students valued structured pre-simulation guidance; one noted that as simulations progressed, "I was not worried anymore once I started performing the procedure".

> "Participants emphasized that faculty members played a key role in alleviating their anxiety through clear instruction and supportive presence."
