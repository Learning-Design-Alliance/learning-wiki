## Subclaims
`q3 i?` With higher prior mastery-approach goals, students receiving performance-approach goal instructions reported lower mental effort than those not receiving them (t = -2.26, p = 0.024), a vibration effect consistent with the mismatch hypothesis. [→ Hu 2024](#hu-2024)

## Evidence

### Hu 2024

Hu, Y., Wouters, P., van der Schaaf, M., & Kester, L. (2024). The effects of achievement goal instructions in game-based learning on students' achievement goals, performance, and achievement emotions. Learning and Instruction, 93, 101943. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101943

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized experiment (N = 450), robust regression showed a significant prior mastery-approach goals × performance-approach instructions interaction on mental effort, "t = 2.26, p = 0.024"; no significant moderating effects on achievement goals, posttest performance, or achievement emotions.

> "Robust regression revealed that there was a statistically significant interaction effect between prior mastery-approach goals and the performance-approach goal instructions (t =  2.26, p = 0.024) on mental effort"
