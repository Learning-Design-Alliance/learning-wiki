## Evidence

### Fola-Adebayo 2014

Fola-Adebayo, T.J., 2014, 'Influence of Pre-question and genre-based instructional strategies on reading', Reading & Writing 5(1), Art. #51, 9 pages. https://doi.org/10.4102/rw.v5i1.51

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on pre-post data from a randomised block experiment with 285 first-year undergraduates at two Nigerian specialised universities, three experimental groups and one control over four weeks. The article reports F(3,260) = 2.5; p > 0.05, so Ho1 was accepted.

> "the main effect of instructional strategy is not significant on the achievement of the undergraduates in reading comprehension ( F(3,260) = 2.5; p > 0.05)"
