# The TA-gaze result is interpreted as preschoolers understanding the teachable agent as an entity with agency that acts on its own knowledge

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The pattern of looking more at the TA after it had been in charge can be interpreted as children ascribing agency to the TA, i.e., goal-directed action based on its own knowledge. [→ Gulz 2020](#gulz-2020)

## Evidence

### Gulz 2020

Gulz, Agneta; Londos, Ludvig & Haake, Magnus. (2020). Preschoolers' Understanding of a Teachable Agent-Based Game in Early Mathematics as Reflected in their Gaze Behaviors – an Experimental Study. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education (2020) 30:38–73. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00193-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the gaze result from the experimental session; the article frames it as an inference, not a tested measure of understanding. The TA and clouds differ categorically only for children with sufficient understanding of agency.

> "This can be interpreted as an indication that the children had an understand- ing of their teachable agent as an entity that, like themselves and unlike other dynamic visual elements in the game, made decisions based on own 'knowledge'."
