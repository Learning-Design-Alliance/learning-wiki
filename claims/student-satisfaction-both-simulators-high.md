### Scherer-Quenzer AC 2026

Scherer-Quenzer AC, Reinhart K, Diessner J, Altides A, Buechel J, Blau-Schneider B, Sauer ST, Deschler-Baier B, Woeckel A, Kiesel M. (2026). Comparative validation of a cost-effective in-house 3D-printed LLETZ simulator versus a commercial training model. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-026-08413-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire of 60 medical students answering 21 identical questions on a 10-point Likert scale (1 = strongly agree/very good). Both groups rated enjoyment at 1.1 and willingness to do further simulation training at 1.2–1.3.

> "The only statistically significant difference was found in the question: "Training in electrosurgery improved my medical education" (p < 0.05), with Group A rating it 1.2 (0.38) and Group B 1.6 (0.93)."
