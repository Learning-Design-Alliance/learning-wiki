## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Denervaud Solange 2019

Denervaud Solange, Knebel Jean-François, Hagmann Patric, Gentaz Edouard. (2019). Beyond executive functions, creativity skills benefit academic outcomes: Insights from Montessori education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225319

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Kindergarten creativity measured with abstract drawing items from a standardized test, scored blindly by three judges; ANCOVA controlling for age gave convergent creativity d = 0.82 and divergent creativity d = 0.90 favoring Montessori.

> "Convergent creativity (score)	3.88 (1.49)	2.74 (1.27)	3.54, p = 0.013	11.8, p = 0.001	0.82"

### Denervaud Solange 2019 (2)

Denervaud Solange, Knebel Jean-François, Hagmann Patric, Gentaz Edouard. (2019). Beyond executive functions, creativity skills benefit academic outcomes: Insights from Montessori education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225319

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Elementary subgroup comparison on the same standardized creativity drawing test; ANCOVA controlling for age gave convergent creativity d = 1.07 and divergent creativity d = 0.76 favoring Montessori schoolchildren.

> "Convergent creativity (score)	5.13 (1.46)	3.53 (1.51)	6.07, p = 0.003	43.93, p < 0.001	1.07"
