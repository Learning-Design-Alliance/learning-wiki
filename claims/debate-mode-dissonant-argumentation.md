## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the Debate Mode, two students explicitly disagree and use evidence, claims, and warrants, while remaining group members do not speak; it ends when one student capitulates or stops arguing. [→ Alanna Pawlak 2018](#alanna-pawlak-2018)

## Evidence

### Alanna Pawlak 2018

Alanna Pawlak, Paul W. Irving, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2018). Development of a Modes of Collaboration framework. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010101)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative characterization from video episodes (A1 and A2) in which Lindsay and Michael dispute whether electric field vectors cancel; the authors code explicit disagreement, critique, and use of claims, evidence, and warrants. Debate is the only Mode with a fixed participant count: "The Debate Mode is the only Mode which has a specific number of participants – two."

> "The Debate Mode is characterized by dissonant interactions in the social dimension, argumentation in the discursive dimension, and can be characterized by specific or abstract content in the disciplinary content dimension."
