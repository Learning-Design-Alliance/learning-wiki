## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Girard 2021

Girard, C., Bastelica, T., Léone, J., Epinat-Duclos, J., Longo, L. & Prado, J. (2021). Nurturing the reading brain: home literacy practices are associated with children's neural response to printed words through vocabulary skills. npj Science of Learning 6:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00112-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (10,000 iterations) in the fMRI sample controlled for parental income and education. Home literacy practices predicted vocabulary (path a, coefficient = 0.427, p = 0.024); "path b, coefﬁcient = 0.036, STE = 0.026, 95% CI Lower = −0.003, p = 0.062" was marginal.

> "Critically, the relation between frequency of home literacy practices and word adaptation was signiﬁcantly mediated by vocabulary (path a*b, coefﬁcient = 0.015, STE = 0.014, 95% CI Lower = 0.001, p = 0.046)."
