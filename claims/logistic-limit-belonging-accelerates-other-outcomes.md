### Mohammed A. Alrizqi 2025

Mohammed A. Alrizqi. (2025). From Interviews to Equations: A Multi-Phase System Dynamics Model of Engineering Student Engagement. 2025 International System Dynamics Conference (WIP, withdrawn before publication). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.00382](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.00382)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Second Vensim experiment adding a logistic-style limit on sense of belonging growth. The article reports need for validation "reaches a ﬂoor" while confidence, personal growth, motivation, and learning "increase faster than in the baseline simulation". Simulation-only; no effect sizes printed.

> "the need for validation decreases, but it reaches a ﬂoor, eﬀectively mirroring the behavior in the sense of belonging. Interestingly, we can see that other variable, including conﬁdence, personal growth, motivation, and learning, actually increase faster than in the baseline simulation."
