## Evidence

### Smart JR 2015

Smart JR, Kranz K, Carmona F, Lindner TW, Newton A. (2015). Does real-time objective feedback and competition improve performance and quality in manikin CPR training – a prospective observational study from several European EMS. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-015-0160-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective two-stage observational study of 10 European EMS sites (n = 200 adult assessments per stage) using standardised scenarios; Table 3 reports all adult CPR metrics improved significantly (p = 0.00), with overall CPR score rising from 81.0 to 95.1. "The overall mean depth of compressions increased by 7.4 mm in Stage 2".

> "The overall mean depth of compressions increased by 7.4 mm in Stage 2 and the rate reduced to 112 per minute. Mean overall leaning was reduced (−1.0 mm) in Stage 2 and the mean overall flow fraction improved to 84.7 %."
