Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang 2017¶

Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang. (2017). Comparing the efficacy of digital flashcards versus paper flashcards to improve receptive and productive L2 vocabulary. The EUROCALL Review, Volume 25, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.4995/eurocall.2017.6964

Self-reported estimated out-of-class study time collected via the post-treatment L1 survey, broken down in Figures 1 and 2. The article reports "the PF group (62%)" studied outside class versus 31% of the DF group; no statistical test is reported.

"A much larger percentage of the students in the PF group (62%) took advantage of the opportunity to study the words outside of class compared with the DF group (31%)."
