### Siedahmed 2025

Siedahmed, A., Pei, Y., Sales, A., Heffernan, N. T., Gagnon-Bartsch, J. A., Zhang, D., Schuetze, B. A., & Zengilowski, A. (2025). E-TRIALS: Empowering Data-Driven Decisions to Enhance Computer-Based Learning Platforms. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.10545](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.10545)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Support-comparison RCT in ASSISTments: students randomly assigned to Similar (157 students after filtering) or Different (134 students) example feedback. The article reports "no significant difference between the similar and different feedback groups" on proximal and distal scores; e.g., proximal t-test estimate 0.0254, SE 0.0333, p 0.4461.

> "From Table 5a, we observe no significant difference between the similar and different feedback groups. However, after covariate adjustment, the standard errors, as indicated by the Std.Error column, are reduced."
