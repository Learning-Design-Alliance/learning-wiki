## Evidence

### Josephine C. Meyer 2026

Josephine C. Meyer, Steven J. Pollock, Bethany R. Wilcox, Gina Passante. (2026). Assessing student learning in quantum computing: Lessons from developing a test item on phase kickback. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00564-1](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00564-1)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of v2.2 response patterns (N=777) interpreted distractor combinations against the authors' expectations: 'ca' "peaks in popularity among above-average students", while 'cc' is most pronounced among below-median students and may stem from a misleading classical XOR analogy.

> "The most common incorrect answer, “ca,” is consistent with the incorrect notion that the state of a single qubit can always be written as a ket."
