### Geary David C. 2023

Geary David C., Hoard Mary K., Nugent Lara, Ünal Zehra E. (2023). Sex Differences in Developmental Pathways to Mathematical Competence. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000763

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling (Model 3) in the 342-adolescent longitudinal sample, estimating direct paths from Spatial and In-Class Attention latent variables to a second-order Mathematics latent variable. The article prints "β = 0.36" and "β = 0.42"; no standardized effect-size magnitude beyond these path coefficients is given.

> "The path from In-Class Attention to Mathematics was significant, β = 0.36, t = 7.34, p < .001, as was the path from Spatial to Mathematics, β = 0.42, t = 3.33, p < .001."
