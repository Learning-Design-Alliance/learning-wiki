# Witnessing mentor teachers model advocacy actions shaped novice ESL teachers' advocacy beliefs and practices

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Two participants (Lucy and Nora) cited seeing experienced teachers enact equitable practices and advocacy as influential in their own development as advocates. [→ Garrone-Shufran 2022](#garrone-shufran-2022)

## Evidence

### Garrone-Shufran 2022

Garrone-Shufran, S. (2022). "I Think You Have to Be a Fighter": Novice ESL Teachers' Descriptions of Advocacy for Emergent Bilinguals. Teacher Education Quarterly, Summer 2022. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1361502

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview findings from the case study: Lucy described two mentor teachers from her paraprofessional years, and Nora described a supervising practitioner who immediately modified assessments for an EL, as models that impacted their beliefs and practices.

> "Both Lucy's and Nora's experiences seeing advocacy actions in practice reinforce the importance of finding advocacy role models to mentor aspiring teachers (Athanases & Martin, 2006; Whipp, 2013)."
