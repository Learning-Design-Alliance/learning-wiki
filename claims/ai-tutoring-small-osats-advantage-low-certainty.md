`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of four studies (3 RCTs and 1 pilot prospective study, 268 total participants) comparing AI tutoring with expert instruction, reported in the article's Results. The pooled mean difference in "expert-rated OSATS scores (MD 0.20; 95% CI, 0.01 to 0.39)" was significant with I2 = 0%, rated low certainty via GRADE.
