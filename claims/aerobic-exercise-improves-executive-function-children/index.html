Berwid Olga G. 2012¶

Berwid Olga G., Halperin Jeffrey M. (2012). Emerging Support for a Role of Exercise in Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder Intervention Planning. Current Psychiatry Reports. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11920-012-0297-4

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative review of experimental child studies. The review reports that longer-term interventions improved singular executive functions while short-term exercise showed "clear evidence that short-term exercise improved inhibitory control," and that six recent studies each found at least nominal executive improvement.

"Several recent reviews have concluded that both chronic and acute aerobic exercise improves executive abilities, most notably inhibitory control, with more limited effects on non-executive cognitive skills such as attention, perception, and visuomotor coordination"

Berwid Olga G. 2012 (2)¶

Berwid Olga G., Halperin Jeffrey M. (2012). Emerging Support for a Role of Exercise in Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder Intervention Planning. Current Psychiatry Reports. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11920-012-0297-4

q3 · i? · review · r2

Cross-over trial in which 552 children aged 8–12 were randomized by school into two counterbalanced groups receiving 15 minutes of daily moderately intense aerobic exercise for one week versus control activities. No significant interactions with BMI or ADHD symptoms emerged.

"Results indicated no main effect of exercise, but a counterbalance group x exercise interaction emerged such that only those who received the exercise intervention during the second week benefited."
