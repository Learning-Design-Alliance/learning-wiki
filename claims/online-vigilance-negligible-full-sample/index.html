Evidence¶

le Roux 2021¶

le Roux, D.B., Parry, D.A., Totolo, A., Iyawa, G., Holloway, J., Prenter, A., Botha, L. (2021). Media Multitasking, Online Vigilance and Academic Performance among Students in three Southern African Countries. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.104056

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-sectional survey regression (N = 1445) comparing RQ1 and RQ3 models. The article reports the ΔR² = .02 was statistically significant (p < .001) yet represents "negligible explanatory power"; the authors interpret the association as partially mediated by online vigilance.

"While statistically signiﬁcant (∆ R2 = .02,p < .001), the small change in variance explained indicates that, over and above the media use variables used as predictors for RQ1, online vigilance provides negligible explanatory power over academic performance."
