## Evidence

### Kardaş 2016

Kardaş, D. (2016). The use of personalized texts for teaching Turkish as a second language. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2538

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

True experimental pretest-posttest control group design with 24 students (12 per group) at Gazi University TÖMER over six weeks. The experimental group received personalized examples and exams; the control group received textbook examples and unreal-information questions. The article reports a statistically meaningful difference [t (24)= -4.465, p<0.01], with "average academic success level was 74.5 while it was 89.3 in experimental group".

> "In control group average academic success level was 74.5 while it was 89.3 in experimental group for which the same grammar topics were taught."
