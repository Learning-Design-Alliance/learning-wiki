# No group difference in the quality of symbolic number representations between adults with dyscalculia and controls

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` ROI decoding and searchlight analyses found no significant group differences for symbolic number representations, though decoding was near chance levels. [→ Bulthé Jessica 2019](#bulthe-jessica-2019)

## Evidence

### Bulthé Jessica 2019

Bulthé Jessica, Prinsen Jellina, Vanderauwera Jolijn, Duyck Stefanie, Daniels Nicky, Gillebert Céline R., Mantini Dante, Op de Beeck Hans P., De Smedt Bert. (2019). Multi-method brain imaging reveals impaired representations of number as well as altered connectivity in adults with dyscalculia. Neuroimage, 190, 289-302. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2018.06.012

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ROI-based decoding in the All Regions ROI; overall symbolic decoding performance was markedly lower than for non-symbolic numbers (63% to 47%), and the authors note this low sensitivity may have masked a possible group difference.

> "For symbolic numbers there was no significant difference in the quality of the neural representations between controls and dyscalculia (t46 = −0.56, p = 0.58)."
