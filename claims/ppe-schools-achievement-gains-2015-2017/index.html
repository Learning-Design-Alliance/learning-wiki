Aggregated comparison of statewide assessment proficiency in schools with leaders enrolled in PPE versus non-PPE schools, 2015–2017, reported alongside Figure 1. The ELA proficiency percentage rose "39 percent during this period, compared to 4 percent" at non-PPE schools. No test statistics or effect sizes are printed.

"The percentage of students at PPE schools who tested proficient on the statewide English language arts assessment rose 39 percent during this period, compared to 4 percent for students at non-PPE schools."

Same aggregated 2015–2017 comparison for mathematics proficiency, reported in the text accompanying Figure 1 using New Mexico Department of Education data. PPE schools rose 28 percent versus 11 percent at non-PPE schools. No test statistics or effect sizes are printed.

"On the statewide mathematics assessment, the percentage of students at PPE schools who tested proficient rose 28 percent during this period, compared to 11 percent at non-PPE schools."
