Post-intervention comparison of both groups (N=22 each) on three self-report secondary outcomes. Simulation design evaluation was 4.42 ± 0.56 vs. 3.55 ± 0.40 (t = 5.88, p < .001) and practice immersion 4.54 ± 0.50 vs. 4.33 ± 0.35 (t = 3.612, p = .001), all favoring the VR group.
