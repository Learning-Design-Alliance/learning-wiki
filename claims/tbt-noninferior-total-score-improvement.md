## Evidence

### Montanari Eliana MD 2016

Montanari Eliana MD, Schwameis Richard MD, Louridas Marisa MD, Göbl Christian MD, PhD, MSc, Kuessel Lorenz MD, Polterauer Stephan MD, Husslein Heinrich MD, PLLM, Torquati. Alfonso. (2016). Training on an inexpensive tablet-based device is equally effective as on a standard laparoscopic box trainer: A randomized controlled trial. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000004826

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 32 medical students randomized 1:1 to TBT or SBT, training 4 FLS tasks twice weekly for 4 weeks, tested on a neutral trainer. The article reports "Total score improvement was found to be greater in the TBT group than in the SBT group" with noninferiority shown.

> "Total score improvement was found to be greater in the TBT group than in the SBT group, although this difference did not reach statistical significance (Table 3). Noninferiority regarding the total score improvement in the TBT group could be shown (Table 3)."
