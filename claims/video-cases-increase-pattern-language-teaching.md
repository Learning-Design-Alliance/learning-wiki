# Video cases increase the number of design patterns from the pattern language 'Teaching' that pre-service teachers mention in written advice.

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` The experimental group using video cases showed a significant pre-post increase in the number of design patterns mentioned in the written advice, while the control group showed no progress. [→ Geerts 2018](#geerts-2018)

## Evidence

### Geerts 2018

Geerts, W. M., Steenbeek, H. W., & van Geert, P. L. C. (2018). Effect of Video-Cases on the Acquisition of Situated Knowledge of Teachers. International Education Studies, 11(1), 64. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p64

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

In the written advice task, the experimental group's mean number of design patterns mentioned increased from pre-test (M = 1.49, SD = 0.39) to post-test (M = 1.78, SD = 0.51), while the control group showed no such progress between the pre- (M = 2.1, SD = 0.37) and post-test (M = 2.1, SD = 0.74).

> "The observed difference between the pre- and post-test is significant for the experimental group (d = 0.698, p<.001), which is interpreted as a moderately meaningful difference."
