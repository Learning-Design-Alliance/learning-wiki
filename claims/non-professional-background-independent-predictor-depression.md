Paton Lewis W., Tiffin Paul A., Smith Daniel, Dowell Jon S., Mwandigha Lazaro M. (2018). Predictors of fitness to practise declarations in UK medical undergraduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1167-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two separate backwards stepwise multivariable models for the coded depression outcome (IVQ33/ITQ50 model N = 1459; ITQ100 model N = 1619). Printed ORs were 4.39 (p = 0.010) and 4.78 (p = 0.001); confidence also remained significant in the IVQ33/ITQ50 model (OR 0.52).

> "Table 4 shows that both of the multivariable models for depression report that a non-professional background is a significant independent predictor of declaring depression, and in both models the odds ratio is greater than four."
