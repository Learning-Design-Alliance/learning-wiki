## Evidence

### Kelley Commeford 2022

Kelley Commeford, Eric Brewe, and Adrienne Traxler. (2022). Characterizing active learning environments in physics using latent profile analysis. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010113)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-profile LPA (model 1) on COPUS profiles from observed class periods of six physics active learning pedagogies. The article reports "successful sorting of observations" into the two categories; no effect size is printed.

> "The 2 proﬁle solution is included due to the successful sorting of observations into ‘interactive lecture-like’ and ‘other’, as seen in Table V."

### Kelley Commeford 2022 (2)

Kelley Commeford, Eric Brewe, and Adrienne Traxler. (2022). Characterizing active learning environments in physics using latent profile analysis. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010113)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Discussion of the two-profile box plots (top panel of figure 2): the article identifies I.Lec, S.L, and I.1o1 as the codes with "the largest separation between the two proﬁles". Descriptive, no effect size printed.

> "The largest separation between the two proﬁles occurs with the instructor lecturing (I.Lec), students lis- tening (S.L), and the instructor having one on one con- versations with students or small groups (I.1o1)."
