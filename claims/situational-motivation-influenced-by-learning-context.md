### Törmänen 2025

Törmänen, T., Ketonen, E., Lehtoaho, E., Turunen, M., Zabolotna, K., Shubina, T., & Järvenoja, H. (2025). Situational Motivation in Academic Learning: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10036-0

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative synthesis of the 80 included studies reports "65% (n = 40) provided empirical evidence for this claim" in the situation-specific wave versus 28% (n = 5) in the momentary wave, covering educational context, subject, learning environment, task, and social interaction.

> "Among the studies in the situation-specific wave, 65% (n = 40) provided empirical evidence for this claim. In contrast, only 28% (n = 5) of the studies in the momentary wave offered evidence on the contextual variation of situational motivation."
