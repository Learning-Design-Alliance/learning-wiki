### Aura‐Tormos Juan Ignacio 2025

Aura‐Tormos Juan Ignacio, Vello‐Ribes M. Angeles, Frechina‐Borras Neus, Casaña‐Ruiz M. Dolores, Catalá‐pizarro Montserrat. (2025). Evaluating Haptic Virtual Reality Simulator for Pulpotomy Training in Primary Teeth: A Pilot Study on Dental Student Pespectives. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70013

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Spearman correlational analysis in the pilot survey; the article reports "a perfect correlation (ρ = 1.00)" between SP12 satisfaction and SP10 desire for further virtual simulation use, with similar high correlations (ρ = 0.76) between SP11 and SP10/SP12.

> "a perfect correlation (ρ = 1.00) was observed between satisfaction with the virtual simulator (SP12) and the desire to use the virtual simulator for additional paediatric dentistry procedures (SP10)."

### Aura‐Tormos Juan Ignacio 2025 (2)

European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70013

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reliability analysis of the 12-item Likert questionnaire in the pilot study; "a Cronbach's alpha coefficient of 0.91" was found, with no item reducing alpha when removed.

> "The analysis yielded a Cronbach's alpha coefficient of 0.91, indicating a high level of internal consistency among the items."
