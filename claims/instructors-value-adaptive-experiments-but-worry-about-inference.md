### Williams 2021

Williams, J. J., Nogas, J., Deliu, N., Shaikh, H., Villar, S., Durand, A., & Rafferty, A. (2021). Challenges in Statistical Analysis of Data Collected by a Bandit Algorithm: An Empirical Exploration in Applications to Adaptively Randomized Experiments. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.12198](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.12198)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interviews with all three instructors who deployed TS in their university courses. Instructors appreciated the dashboard showing assignment probability and wanted to encode prior knowledge in future versions.

> "Instructors found it valuable to have data be more rapidly used to help future students, and were more willing to conduct adaptive experiments in the future due to the potential for current students to benefit"
