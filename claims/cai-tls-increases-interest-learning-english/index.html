Jinajai and Rattanavich 2015¶

Nattapong Jinajai & Saowalak Rattanavich. (2015). The Effects of Computer-Assisted Instruction Based on Top-Level Structure Method in English Reading and Writing Abilities of Thai EFL Students. English Language Teaching, 8(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n11p231

A t-test on the interest questionnaire of 32 ninth grade students found significantly higher interest after CAI-TLS instruction (t = 2.890, p = .008), with a mean of 3.66 on a five-point Likert scale.
