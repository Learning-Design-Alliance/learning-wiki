### Hill 2015

Hill, K. (2015). Integrating Instruction, Curricula, and Assessments in the L2 Classroom. The Electronic Journal for English as a Second Language. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1074705

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental study with 30 first-year Japanese university students in the intervention group and 22 in a historical control group. The intervention group received integrated dynamic speaking assessments; the control received non-dynamic curriculum-based speaking assessments. The study group scored "16.1% higher than the control group on the post-CRA".

> "The study group scored 16.1% higher than the control group on the post-CRA and a one-way ANOVA analysis was extremely statistically significant (see Table 3)."
