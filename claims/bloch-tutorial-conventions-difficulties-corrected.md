# Common pre-test difficulties with Bloch sphere conventions — the factor of θ/2, angle starting points, and state locations — were largely corrected on the post-test.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Difficulties such as drawing a polar angle half the required size and starting angles from the wrong axes were largely corrected after the tutorial. [→ Peter Hu 2023](#peter-hu-2023)

## Evidence

### Peter Hu 2023

Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, Roger S. K. Mong, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Student Understanding of the Bloch Sphere. https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad2393

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre/post study (N = 28): on pre-test questions 3a-d most students neglected to multiply θ/2 by 2 or started angles from unconventional axes; the tutorial's explicit attention to the θ/2 factor and its Cartesian-axes exercise addressed these, and the authors report the tutorial greatly increased facility with spherical coordinate notations.

> "The tutorial calls explicit attention to the 𝜃 2 present in the form cos 𝜃 2 |0⟩ + sin 𝜃2 𝑒𝑖𝜙|1⟩, and included an exercise directing students to find the values of the angles for states that corresponded to the Cartesian axes. Both issues discussed above were largely corrected on the post-test"
