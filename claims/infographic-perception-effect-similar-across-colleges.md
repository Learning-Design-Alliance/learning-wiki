### Alqudah 2019

Alqudah, D., Bidin, A., & Md Hussin, M. A. H. (2019). The Impact of Educational Infographic on Students’ Interaction and Perception in Jordanian Higher Education: Experimental Study. International Journal of Instruction, 12(4), 669-688. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12443a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis test on the perception variable comparing the two colleges; the calculated H was 0.01, below the approximated chi-square value of 3.841 at 0.05 significance and 1 degree of freedom, so the null hypothesis was accepted. Table 8 reports chi-square .001, df 1, Asymp. Sig. .980. This is a non-detection, not a tested equivalence.

> "The null hypothesis was accepted referring to the statistical results of the Kruskal –Wallis test, which means that no  difference occurred between the scientific college sample (IT students) and humanistic college sample (ECO students) as regards the impact of instructional infographics on their information perception"
