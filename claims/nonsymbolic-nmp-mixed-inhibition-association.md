# Non-symbolic magnitude processing shows mixed associations with inhibition measures

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The association between inhibition and non-symbolic numerical magnitude processing was not univocal: an association appeared with the non-numerical stroop task but not with the numerical stroop task. [→ Bellon E 2016](#bellon-e-2016)

## Evidence

### Bellon E 2016

Bellon E, Fias W and De Smedt B (2016) Are Individual Differences in Arithmetic Fact Retrieval in Children Related to Inhibition? Front. Psychol. 7:825. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00825

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory correlational analysis in 86 third graders testing whether domain-general inhibition explains non-symbolic task performance; the non-numerical stroop also associated with symbolic processing, but these associations did not explain the magnitude-processing–fact-retrieval link.

> "However, the results on the association between inhibition and the non-symbolic numerical magnitude processing task were not so univocal. We observed an association with the non-numerical stroop task, but found no association with the numerical stroop task."
