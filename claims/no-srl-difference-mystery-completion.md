### Sabourin 2013

Sabourin, J. L., Shores, L. R., Mott, B. W., & Lester, J. C. (2013). Understanding and Predicting Student Self-Regulated Learning Strategies in Game-Based Learning Environments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 23, 94–114. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0004-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-squared analysis of mystery completion rates across SRL groups. "the percentage of students who solved the mystery did not differ significantly based on SRL group"; descriptive rates were 38% High, 38% Medium, 25% Low.

> "A chi-squared analysis indicated that the percentage of students who solved the mystery did not differ significantly based on SRL group (χ2 (2, N=260) = 4.72, p=0.094) though on average, 38 % of High, 38 % of Medium and 25 % of Low SRL students completed the mystery."
