In the single-center parallel-group randomized controlled trial (N = 51 in three groups of 17), mixed repeated-measures ANOVA on Sexual Attitudes and Beliefs Scale scores showed "the effect of time was statistically significant" with no printed effect size; only the F statistic and p-value are reported.

> "It was determined that the participants’ SABS scores decreased significantly after the intervention. According to the results of the mixed repeated -measures ANOVA, the effect of time was statistically significant (F = 113.31, p < 0.001)."
