## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a quasi-experiment, participants exposed to and learning about rivalry through comic strips or cartoons reported significantly different perceptions of their rival teams than controls, with SSIS scores as covariate. ## Evidence

### Cody T. Havard & Skylar S. Workman 2018

Cody T. Havard & Skylar S. Workman. (2018). Adventures With Sport Rivalry Man: Initial Testing of a Classroom Method Using Comics and Cartoons to Teach about Rivalry and Fan Behavior. The University of Memphis. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1200586

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 31 students in sport management and fan behavior classes; treatment group completed an extra-credit exposure and learning program on www.SportRivalry.com. The MANCOVA using SSIS scores as covariate was significant ("Wilks' Lambda .593(4, 25) = 4.293, p = .009"). No effect size was printed.

> "Results of the MANCOVA were significant (Wilks' Lambda .593(4, 25) = 4.293, p = .009), indicating that exposure to and learning about rivalry using the comic strips or cartoons influenced fan rival perceptions."
