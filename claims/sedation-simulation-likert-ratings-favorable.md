## Evidence

### Dupont A 2024

Dupont A, Ciener D, Monteilh C, Bharath A, Thomas A, Wolpert K, Pearce J. (2024). Pediatric Emergency Medicine Didactics and Simulation (PEMDAS): Pediatric Sedation Complications. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11384

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Voluntary post-session evaluation by 53 of 58 participants at three institutions, five questions on a 5-point Likert scale (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree) plus three open-ended questions. The article reports the Likert means "(M = 4.37)", "(M = 4.36)", "(M = 4.40)", "(M = 4.50)" with no variability or effect size.

> "Learners rated the scenario as clinically relevant (M = 4.37) and effective at improving their comfort level in caring for critically ill patients (M = 4.36). Learners felt the debrief provided valuable learning (M = 4.40) and was a safe learning environment (M = 4.50)."
