## Evidence

### Balakrishnan Athira 2021

Balakrishnan Athira, Nair Sreedharan, Kunhikatta Vijayanarayana, Rashid Muhammed, Unnikrishnan M. K., Jagannatha P. S., Chandran Viji P., Khera Kanav, Thunga Girish. (2021). Effectiveness of blended learning in pharmacy education: An experimental study using clinical research modules. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256814

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective cluster randomized trial with three arms (DT control, WEL and BL interventions); 241 students from 12 colleges completed pre- and post-tests. BL total score "64.26±18.19" exceeded DT "56.65±8.73" and WEL "52.11±22.06", p<0.001; Dunn's post hoc confirmed BL highest (p<0.001 for both pairwise comparisons).

> "Mean knowledge score of students in blended learning group was higher than those in the didactic teaching and web- based e- learning program (64.26±18.19 Vs 56.65±8.73 Vs 52.11±22.06,p<0.001)."
