Moderator analysis of delivery mode (teacher, researcher, task materials, or teacher plus task materials) across the 67-study dataset. The article reports teachers or task materials "were more effective than interventions that were delivered by researchers" for two SRL outcomes; no effect size is printed in the available text.

"One notable finding was that interventions that were delivered by teachers or task materials were more effective than interventions that were delivered by researchers for two outcomes related to self-regulated learning."
