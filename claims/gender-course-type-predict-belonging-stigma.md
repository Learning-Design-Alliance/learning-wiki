## Evidence

### Li 2026

Li, Y., & Leibnitz, N. (2026). Gender expression appraisals in introductory physics courses: A cross-institutional replication. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two multiple linear regression models predicting gender stigma consciousness and sense of belonging from gender, race/ethnicity, and course type (Table VII) in the 1845-student sample. Course type predicted stigma consciousness (b = 0.50, p < 0.001) and belonging (b = 0.14, p < 0.05); race/ethnicity predicted belonging (b = −0.22, p < 0.001).

> "women and students in gender identity groups with smaller sample sizes reported higher gender stigma consciousness (b = 1.41, SE = 0.08, p < 0.001) and lower sense of belonging (b = −0.62, SE = 0.06, p < 0.001) than men."
