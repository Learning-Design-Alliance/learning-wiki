## Evidence

### Mary A. Pyc 2009

Mary A. Pyc, Katherine A. Rawson. (2009). Testing the retrieval effort hypothesis: Does greater difficulty correctly recalling information lead to higher levels of memory? Journal of Memory and Language 60 (2009) 437–447. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2009.01.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 compared fixed ISI schedules (functional ISI held constant for higher-criterion items) against contracting schedules. The fixed versus contracting main effect on final test performance was not significant, and power functions fit best in both schedule types, arguing against the contracting-ISI alternative interpretation.

> "Inconsistent with this alternative, however, the main effect of fixed versus contracting ISI was not significant (Table 4)."
