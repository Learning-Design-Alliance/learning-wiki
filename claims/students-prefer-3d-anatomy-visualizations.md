Triepels CPR, Smeets CFA, Notten KJB, Kruitwagen RFPM, Futterer JJ, Vergeldt TFM, Van Kuijk SMJ. (2019). Does three‐dimensional anatomy improve student understanding? Three‐Dimensional Methods for Learning Anatomy. Clinical Anatomy 32:25–33. https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.23405

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Subjective-evaluation synthesis across 17 of the 21 included articles that used a questionnaire; the review reports most participants found the 3D methods easier and more enjoyable, with some finding them disorienting and frustrating.

> "Most of the participants in the studies reported that the 3D methods were easier and more enjoyable to use. Sometimes, however, due to the complicated anatomical configuration, participants found the 3D methods disorienting and frustrating"
