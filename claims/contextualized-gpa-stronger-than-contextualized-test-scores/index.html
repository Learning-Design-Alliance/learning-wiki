q2 · i? · review · r?

The article reports, citing Bastedo, Umbricht, Bauch, Byun, and Bai (2023), that "contextualized high school GPA had a stronger relationship with success than contextualized standardized test scores"; contextualized measures consider applicant GPA and test scores relative to others at their high school. No effect sizes are printed in this article.

"The study found that contextualized high school GPA had a stronger relationship with success than contextualized standardized test scores."
