## Evidence

### Moll Lisa 2026

Moll Lisa, Riessen Reimer, Dahlmann Philipp, Häske David. (2026). Effect of low-dose, high-frequency advanced life support training versus annual full-day training on simulation-based resuscitation performance: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09717-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled simulation-based trial with 35 EMS professionals (intervention n = 18, control n = 17) assessed with a validated 29-item rating instrument at baseline and 12 months. The trial reports "a significantly higher overall mean score than the control group at 12 months (4.7 ± 0.2 vs. 4.2 ± 0.3; p < 0.001)"; Table 2 prints Hedges' g = -2.13 (-2.95 to -1.28).

> "For the primary outcome, the intervention group achieved a significantly higher overall mean score than the control group at 12 months (4.7 ± 0.2 vs. 4.2 ± 0.3; p < 0.001), corresponding to a 11.9% relative between-group difference."
