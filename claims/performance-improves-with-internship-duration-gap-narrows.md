An assessment of clinical reception training using standard patient and dental simulator in prosthodontic dentistry for dental undergraduates: a historical control trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06563-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures analysis across 2-week and 8-week real-patient assessments showed improvement over time in both groups; the article prints no effect size for this change.

> "In both the SP and control groups, the performance of students improved with the duration of the internship (Fig. 4)."

### Yao Yitong 2024 (2)

Yao Yitong, Yang Guihong, Li Xueling, Li Yanshan, Zhou Jing, Hong Yun, Yang Ling, Zhao Yu. (2024). An assessment of clinical reception training using standard patient and dental simulator in prosthodontic dentistry for dental undergraduates: a historical control trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06563-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors' discussion of the time trend reports that the between-group gap narrows as clinical exposure accumulates, while the SP group retains an advantage; no effect size is printed.

> "However, as students spend more time in the clinic, the gap between the experimental and control groups narrows and its impact diminishes; nevertheless, the SP group still outperforms the control group."
