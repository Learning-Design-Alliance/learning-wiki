Evidence¶

Holly M. Hart 2002¶

Holly M. Hart, Elaine Allensworth, Douglas L. Lauen, Robert M. Gladden. (2002). Educational Technology: Availability and Use in Chicago's Public Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/educational-technology-its-availability-and-use-chicagos-public-schools

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey analysis of nearly 100,000 student and teacher responses in 434 Chicago public schools reports use levels: 19 percent intensive, 38 percent moderate, 25 percent limited, and "Another 17 percent of students say they never use technology at school" for the surveyed activities.

"Only 19 percent of students in grades 6 through 10 use technology intensively at school. These students analyze/ graph data or create presentations about once a week, as well as perform common tasks such as word processing or Internet research almost every day."
