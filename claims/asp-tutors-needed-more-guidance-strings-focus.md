## Evidence

### Sarah Haywood 2015

Sarah Haywood, Julia Griggs, Cheryl Lloyd, Stephen Morris, Zsolt Kiss, Amy Skipp. (2015). Creative Futures: Act, Sing, Play Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581247

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Qualitative case-study process evaluation in five schools, using observations of strings sessions and in-depth interviews with tutors and class teachers analysed with Framework. The report attributes the guidance need to tutors' limited experience "teaching groups of primary school children".

> "However, the process evaluation suggested that some tutors —particularly those with less experience of teaching groups of primary school children— needed more guidance on how to run their sessions."

### Sarah Haywood 2015 (2)

Sarah Haywood, Julia Griggs, Cheryl Lloyd, Stephen Morris, Zsolt Kiss, Amy Skipp. (2015). Creative Futures: Act, Sing, Play Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581247

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

From the same process evaluation, the report links the guidance need to engagement problems: "in some of the strings workshops, it was a struggle to keep students on task". This concern was noted as not necessarily typical.

> "This led to related concerns that in some of the strings workshops, it was a struggle to keep students on task."
