## Evidence

### Selbing 2025

Selbing, I., Becker, N., Pan, Y., Lindström, B., & Olsson, A. (2025). Effects of described demonstrator ability on brain and behavior when learning from others. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00292-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model comparison by mean AIC over 38 participants identified a mixed-strategy model with description-sensitive copying as best, but the authors note it "has substantial support... only when compared to the 26th best model or worse and thus does not stand out as a clear winner".

> "This was a model which included both copying and observational outcome learning and where the copying rate was sensitive to the demonstrators' description."
