### Beck Christopher 2014

Beck Christopher, Butler Amy, Burke da Silva Karen. (2014). Promoting Inquiry-Based Teaching in Laboratory Courses: Are We Meeting the Grade? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.13-12-0245

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

The review narrates the Basey and Francis (2011) exception to the positive-effect trend: students significantly preferred scripted versions over inquiry-based versions "for one of three exercises used in a first-semester introductory biology laboratory course".

> "They found that students had a significantly greater preference for scripted versions of laboratory exercises as compared with inquiry-based versions of the same exercises for one of three exercises used in a first-semester introductory biology laboratory course."
