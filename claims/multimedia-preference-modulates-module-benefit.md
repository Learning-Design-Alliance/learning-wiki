# Module benefit held for students who strongly preferred multimedia learning (d = 0.39) but showed no significant difference for students strongly opposed to multimedia learning; no treatment × preference interaction

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Strongly agree multimedia learners showed significantly higher normalized gain in the module treatment (d = 0.39, p = 0.01), while strongly disagree learners showed no significant difference between treatments (p = 0.91). [→ Goff Eric E. 2017](#goff-eric-e-2017)
`q2 i?` Two-way ANOVA showed no significant interaction between treatment condition and multimedia learning preference (F(1, 527) = 0.45, p = 0.50). [→ Goff Eric E. 2017 (2)](#goff-eric-e-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Goff Eric E. 2017

Goff Eric E., Reindl Katie M., Johnson Christina, McClean Phillip, Offerdahl Erika G., Schroeder Noah L., White Alan R. (2017). Efficacy of a Meiosis Learning Module Developed for the Virtual Cell Animation Collection. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(1):ar9. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-03-0141

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Stratified comparison of students self-identifying as multimedia learners on a five-point Likert scale; "strongly agree" selectors in the module treatment scored significantly higher (d = 0.39, p = 0.01).

> "Students in the learning module treatment that self-identified as multimedia learners (“strongly agree” selectors) show significantly higher normalized gain score (t(127.18) = 2.63, p = 0.01, d = 0.39) when compared with the traditional lecture treatment"

### Goff Eric E. 2017 (2)

Goff Eric E., Reindl Katie M., Johnson Christina, McClean Phillip, Offerdahl Erika G., Schroeder Noah L., White Alan R. (2017). Efficacy of a Meiosis Learning Module Developed for the Virtual Cell Animation Collection. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(1):ar9. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-03-0141

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA found "no significant interaction between treatment condition and multimedia learning preference" (F(1, 527) = 0.45, p = 0.50); the overall regression also showed no effect of this factor (t(406) = −1.41, p = 0.12).

> "Two-way ANOVA also shows no significant interaction between treatment condition and multimedia learning preference (F(1, 527) = 0.45, p = 0.50)."
