## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the distractor-mapping analysis of NMQ 03, free-text responses included incorrect ideas (Category X, over 10% of responses, mentioning time instead of distance) not covered by the MCQ distractor options, and none of the free-text responses mapped to distractors B or C. ## Evidence

### Ashutosh Kumar Pathak 2026

Ashutosh Kumar Pathak, Mark A. J. Parker, Andrew J. A. James, Sally E. Jordan, and Jonathan Nylk. (2026). Student responses to a modified Force Concept Inventory: The Newtonian Mechanics Quiz. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.02432](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.02432)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Distractor-mapping analysis of pre/post responses at University A: 93 students who received NMQ 03 in the same format in both tests (50 MCQ, 43 FTR). Incorrect free-text answers were mapped to MCQ distractors, with unmatched answers placed in "Category X". The article reports "none of the free-text responses mapped to the distractor options B or C".

> "Category X, which contained more than 10% of all free-text responses, contained incorrect answers that mentioned time instead of distance. NMQ 03 does not ask about time, but some students provide answers as if the question did ask about time."
