# Learning gains showed significant main effects of hint condition and subject, with no significant condition-by-subject interaction

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A Two-Way ANOVA on ranked data found significant main effects of condition (F(2, 262) = 5.037, p = 0.0071) and subject (F(3, 262) = 6.737, p = 0.0002) on learning gains. [→ Pardos ZA 2024](#pardos-za-2024)

## Evidence

### Pardos ZA 2024

Pardos ZA, Bhandari S (2024) ChatGPT-generated help produces learning gains equivalent to human tutor-authored help on mathematics skills. PLoS ONE 19(5): e0304013. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0304013

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus Two-Way ANOVA on ranked data from the randomized experiment (N = 274), conducted after Shapiro-Wilk rejected normality. The article also reports "no statistically significant interaction between condition and subject (F(6, 262) = 0.901, p = 0.495)"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "we found significant main effects of the condition (F(2, 262) = 5.037, p = 0.0071) and the subject (F(3, 262) = 6.737, p = 0.0002), indicating that the type of hints provided had statistically significant impacts on the learning gains"
