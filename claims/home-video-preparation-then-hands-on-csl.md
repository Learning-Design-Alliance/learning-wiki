## Evidence

### Müller Lina R. 2019

Müller Lina R., Tipold Andrea, Ehlers Jan P., Schaper Elisabeth. (2019). TiHoVideos: veterinary students' utilization of instructional videos on clinical skills. BMC Veterinary Research 15, 326. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-019-2079-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Structured, hidden observational study of 159 students actively using CSL learning stations, combined with a second questionnaire; an instructional video existed for 124 of the 159 observed stations. The article reports "109 students responded in the affirmative (68.6%)" to having watched the station's video. Separately, "105 (66%) students indicated 'at home'", and "70 (44%) students used a tablet or laptop".
