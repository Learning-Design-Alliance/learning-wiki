## Evidence

### Brian Mathias 2022

Brian Mathias, Christian Andrä, Anika Schwager, Manuela Macedonia, Katharina von Kriegstein. (2022). Twelve- and Fourteen-Year-Old School Children Differentially Benefit from Sensorimotor- and Multisensory-Enriched Vocabulary Training. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09648-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed effects modeling of composite scores across the three testing time points in the 75-child intervention. Both gesture- and picture-enriched learning benefited outcomes "irrespective of testing time point and children's grade level", despite teachers omitting the L2 stimuli from lessons for 6 months.

> "there was no significant interaction between learning condition and time point (χ 2 (4, N  =  75)  =  4.29, p  =  0.37, v = 0.12)"
