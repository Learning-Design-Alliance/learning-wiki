## Evidence

### Burgess A 2020

Burgess A, van Diggele C, Roberts C, Mellis C. (2020). Feedback in the clinical setting. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02280-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The review reports, citing prior literature, that self-assessment is often wrong and that "High performers tend to underestimate their own performance, and lower performers tend to overestimate". No effect size is printed; the review attributes this to cited studies including a systematic review of physician self-assessment accuracy.

> "Lack of external feedback. Without external feedback, students may generate their own feedback - but, self-assessment is often wrong [4]. High performers tend to underestimate their own performance, and lower performers tend to overestimate [9]."
