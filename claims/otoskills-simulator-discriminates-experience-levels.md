## Evidence

### Fieux Maxime 2021

Fieux Maxime, Gavoille Antoine, Subtil Fabien, Bartier Sophie, Tringali Stéphane. (2021). Otoskills training during covid-19 pandemic: a before-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02706-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot validation analysis comparing baseline (T1) results of 3 experts, 7 intermediate and 9 novice surgeons using the Kruskal-Wallis nonparametric test; median total PS followed the level of experience (p = 0.004), supporting construct validity of the simulator.

> "There were statistically significant differences in the median total PS, which differed by more than 10 % between novice, intermediate, and expert surgeons (16.8 [16.2–19.2], 21.3 [19.5–21.8], and 25.8 [25.1–27.9], respectively, p-value = 0.004)"

### Fieux Maxime 2021 (2)

Fieux Maxime, Gavoille Antoine, Subtil Fabien, Bartier Sophie, Tringali Stéphane. (2021). Otoskills training during covid-19 pandemic: a before-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02706-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same baseline discriminative-power analysis, GRS scores (maximum 30) increased with experience level and differed significantly among the three groups (p = 0.003), which the authors cite as evidence of the GRS's discriminating power.

> "The GRS scores followed the same trend for novices, intermediate surgeons, and expert surgeons (13.0 [8.0–17.0], 23.0 [17.5–23.5], and 30.0 [30.0–30.0], respectively, p-value = 0.003; Table 1)."
