# Grounded theory analysis of 666 quotes identified five categories of Instructor Talk, with Building the Instructor/Student Relationship most prevalent at 35.4% of quotes

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Coding of 666 quotes from 29 class sessions produced five emergent categories of Instructor Talk: Building the Instructor/Student Relationship, Establishing Classroom Culture, Explaining Pedagogical Choices, Sharing Personal Experiences, and Unmasking Science. [→ Seidel 2015](#seidel-2015)
`q2 i?` Building the Instructor/Student Relationship was the most prevalent category, accounting for 236 quotes or 35.4% of the semester's total quotes. [→ Seidel 2015 (2)](#seidel-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Seidel 2015

Seidel, S. B., Reggi, A. L., Schinske, J. N., Burrus, L. W., & Tanner, K. D. (2015). Beyond the Biology: A Systematic Investigation of Noncontent Instructor Talk in an Introductory Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Grounded theory analysis of transcripts from 29 recorded class sessions of one cotaught introductory biology course. The article reports "In total, 666 quotes were identified over the course of 29 recorded class sessions," from which the five categories emerged.

> "In total, 666 quotes were identified over the course of 29 recorded class sessions."

### Seidel 2015 (2)

Seidel, S. B., Reggi, A. L., Schinske, J. N., Burrus, L. W., & Tanner, K. D. (2015). Beyond the Biology: A Systematic Investigation of Noncontent Instructor Talk in an Introductory Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Quantitative prevalence analysis of the same 666-quote dataset. Building the Instructor/Student Relationship received "236 quotes... representing 35.4% of the total quotes for the semester," making it the most prevalent of the five categories; percentages are descriptive with no inferential test.

> "In total, 236 quotes were classified as Building the Instructor/Student Relationship, representing 35.4% of the total quotes for the semester."

### Seidel 2015 (3)

Seidel, S. B., Reggi, A. L., Schinske, J. N., Burrus, L. W., & Tanner, K. D. (2015). Beyond the Biology: A Systematic Investigation of Noncontent Instructor Talk in an Introductory Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interrater reliability check in which an additional coder classified a randomly chosen 10% subset of 66 quotes. Category-level agreement was 85% and subcategory-level 75%, with "Cohen’s kappa at the category level (0.80) and the subcategory level (0.74)"; kappa values are reliability statistics, not effect sizes.

> "To determine the statistical reliability of agreement, we measured Cohen’s kappa at the category level (0.80) and the subcategory level (0.74), with results that indicated a high level of agreement between coders."
