## Subclaims
`q3 i?` LASSO-based and in-class assessments provided equivalent participation rates and equivalent student performance scores when instructors used in-class reminders, multiple email reminders, and credit for pretest and posttest participation. [→ Van Dusen 2018](#van-dusen-2018)

## Evidence

### Van Dusen 2018

Van Dusen, B. (2018). LASSO: A New Tool to Support Instructors and Researchers. APS Forum on Education Newsletter, Fall 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1812.02299](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1812.02299)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

A randomized between groups experimental design by Nissen et al. compared computer-based LASSO administration with paper-and-pencil tests. "Models of student performance indicated that tests administered with LASSO had equivalent scores to those administered in class." No effect size is printed; equivalence of participation and scores is reported for the 1,310 students in 3 college physics courses.

> "Analysis of 1,310 students in 3 college physics courses indicated that LASSO-based and in-class assessments provide equivalent participation rates when instructors used four recommended practices"
