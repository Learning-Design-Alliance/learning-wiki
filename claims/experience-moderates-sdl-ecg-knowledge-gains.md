`q2 i?` In one pre-test/post-test study without a comparison group, one-way ANOVA showed a statistically significant association between years of experience and the change in knowledge scores (p = 0.020); nurses with 1–5 years had the largest pre-test-to-post-test rise, while those with 11 or more years had a non-significant pre-test-to-post-test rise (p = 0.257).
