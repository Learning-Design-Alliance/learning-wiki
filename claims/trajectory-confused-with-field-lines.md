### Cristian Raduta 2005

Cristian Raduta. (2005). General Students' Misconceptions Related to Electricity and Magnetism. The Ohio State University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0503132](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0503132)

`q1 · i?` · `review · r1`

Interview study by Tornkvist et al. (Ref. 18) asking students to draw a likely trajectory for a particle with zero initial velocity at a given point in a given field (Fig. 10). The same misconception of confusing trajectory with field lines was documented here as in the mechanics misconception. No effect size is printed.

> "76% of the students made the trajectory follow the field line. 7% drew it toward the supposed pole (assumed, implied one), and 6% were completely confused. Only 11% of the students offered reasonable trajectories"
