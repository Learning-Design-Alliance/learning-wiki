Occelli Valeria 2017¶

Occelli Valeria, Lacey Simon, Stephens Careese, Merabet Lotfi B., Sathian K. (2017). Enhanced Verbal Abilities in The Congenitally Blind. Experimental Brain Research. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5436932/

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Group-specific correlational analyses. The congenitally blind showed a spatial memory–spatial imagery correlation of "r = .77, p = .005"; the sighted group showed no significant correlation between these measures.

"Better spatial memory was associated with better spatial imagery performance in the congenitally blind group (r = .77, p = .005) but not the sighted group."
