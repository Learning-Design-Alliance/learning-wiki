## Evidence

### Weimer Johannes Matthias 2025

Weimer Johannes Matthias, Eigenseher Michael, Stiehl Simon Alexander, Nürnberg Dieter, Ludwig Michael, Stäuber Marie, Weimer Andreas, Kloeckner Roman, Dirks Klaus, Lorenz Liv, Buggenhangen Holger, Julia-Weinmann-Menke, Merkel Daniel. (2025). Prospective comparison of static versus dynamic images in abdominal ultrasound education - a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07711-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial within certified DEGUM basic ultrasound courses; groups received identical video training differing only in image format. The dynamic group scored "34.1 ± 6.3 vs. control: 30.7 ± 6.1, p = 0.001" on the 54-question theory test (145 datasets analysed).

> "The study group achieved significantly higher scores in the overall theory test (study: 34.1 ± 6.3 vs. control: 30.7 ± 6.1, p = 0.001)"
