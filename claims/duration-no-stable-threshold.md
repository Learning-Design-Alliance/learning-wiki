### Yidi Zhang 2026

Yidi Zhang, Jindi Wang, Pedro Bem-Haja, Zhipeng Li, Lei Shi, Thomas K. F. Chiu. (2026). Educational Short Videos: Bibliometric Trends, Thematic Structure, and Operationalisation. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.06932](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.06932)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Duration extraction from accessible bibliographic information and open-access full texts for 511 records (23.6% of the corpus). The article reports "Durations were right-skewed, with a median of 5.00 minutes (IQR 2.32–10.00), a mean of 6.21 minutes, and an observed range of 0.08–30.00 minutes", and states this broad distribution did not reveal a consistently applied cut-off.

> "Durations were right-skewed, with a median of 5.00 minutes (IQR 2.32–10.00), a mean of 6.21 minutes, and an observed range of 0.08–30.00 minutes."

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Rule-based extraction of eight LRMI-derived dimensions from titles, abstracts and keywords of all 2,169 records, validated on 200 records (κ = .904, 86.4% accuracy). The article reports "The educationalRole dimension was identified most frequently, appearing in 1,528 records (70.5%)", followed by educationalUse, teaches and interactivityType.

> "The educationalRole dimension was identified most frequently, appearing in 1,528 records (70.5%)."
