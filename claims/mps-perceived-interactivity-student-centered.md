## Evidence

### Kuo 2013

Kuo, F.-O., Yu, P.-T., & Hsiao, W.-H. (2013). Develop and evaluate the effects of multimodal presentation system on elementary ESL students. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018039

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative analysis of semi-structured interviews with 11 randomly selected experimental-group students, with opinions classified into user's state and previous experience, system properties, and usage context. Students reported, for example, that "Operating materials in front of classmates made me feel participating more in class".

> "The analysis of students' questionnaires and semi-structured individual interviews showed that the IWB technology enhance students mostly associated with interactivity and active participation (Schmid, 2008, 2010)."
