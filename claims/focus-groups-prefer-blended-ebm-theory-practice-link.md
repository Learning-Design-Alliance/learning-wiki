## Evidence

### Ilic Dragan 2013

Ilic Dragan, Hart William, Fiddes Patrick, Misso Marie, Villanueva Elmer. (2013). Adopting a blended learning approach to teaching evidence based medicine: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-169

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenological focus-group study with 37 students across four clinical sites, transcripts analysed independently by two investigators using thematic analysis in NVivo. Students also noted limitations: perceived knowledge gaps and a wish for facilitators to teach on the run.

> "Students preferred using a blended-learning approach as it primarily allowed them to make the link between the theoretical aspects of EBM and the practical aspect of application at the bedside with patients"
