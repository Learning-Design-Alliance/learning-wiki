### Conway 1991

Conway, M. A., Cohen, G., & Stanhope, N. (1991). On the Very Long-Term Retention of Knowledge Acquired Through Formal Education: Twelve Years of Cognitive Psychology. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 120(4), 395-409. https://psycnet.apa.org/record/xge120040395

Principal-components orthotran-varimax factor analysis of all retention tests, CRs, RI, and age produced a four-factor solution in which "none of the retention variables or CRs loaded on the Tem- poral Factor"; the factors separated recall, confidence, temporal, and recognition variance.
