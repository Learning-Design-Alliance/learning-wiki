Evidence¶

Ament 2015¶

Ament, J. R., & Pérez Vidal, C. (2015). Linguistic outcomes of English medium instruction programmes in higher education: A study on economics undergraduates at a Catalan university. Higher Learning Research Communications, 5(1), 47-68. http://dx.doi.org/10.18870/hlrc.v5i1.239

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon signed-ranks tests on the immersion group (N=7) across one academic year found all p-values above .05 for listening, grammar, cloze, and all CAF writing measures; the article states "the IM group shows no significant gains in any of the language domains measured."

"The results for the first three tasks, listening grammar, and cloze, all reflect values where p=>.05 (Table 6). The CAF measures also reflect values where p= >.05, for all tasks."
