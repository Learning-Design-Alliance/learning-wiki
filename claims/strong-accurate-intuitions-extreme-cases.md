## Evidence

### Mark Eichenlaub 2019

Mark Eichenlaub, Edward F. Redish. (2019). Blending physical knowledge with mathematical form in physics problem solving. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-04627-9_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-04627-9_6)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Across problems including the half-Atwood machine, electric field on a ring of charge, springs in series/parallel, and the ellipse problem, the authors found that "students have strong and accurate physical intuitions for the extreme or special cases."

> "In every case, we found that students have strong and accurate physical intuitions for the extreme or special cases. In some circumstances, students consistently spontaneously play the sanity check game using special case reasoning."
