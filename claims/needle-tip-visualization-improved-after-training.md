## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The proportion with identified needle tip visualization, the secondary outcome, rose from 60% at pre-test to 83% at posttest after training, in one uncontrolled pre-post study (P = .03). [→ Kikuchi M 2021](#kikuchi-m-2021)

## Evidence

### Kikuchi M 2021

Kikuchi M, Asao T, Tokumine J, Lefor AK, Matsushima H, Andoh H, Tanaka K, Kanamoto M, Ideno Y. (2021). A novel system for teaching the in-plane vascular access technique: a simulation study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000027201

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two ultrasonography experts who did not attend the seminars rated recorded ultrasound views just before puncture as clearly visible, visible, or unidentified. Identified ratings rose from 25 (60%) to 35 (83%), with unidentified falling from 40% to 17% ("P = .03", McNemar test).

> "Needle tip visualization significantly improved after training (Table 3)."
