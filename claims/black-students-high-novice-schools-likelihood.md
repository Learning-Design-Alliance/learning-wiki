### Sarah Mehrotra 2021

Sarah Mehrotra, Ivy S. Morgan, Allison Socol. (2021). Getting Black Students Better Access to Non-Novice Teachers. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED617544

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

State-level descriptive analysis of CRDC 2017-18 data defining high-novice schools as those with more than 20% novice teachers. The report contrasts Black and non-Black student exposure, finding "about 25% of Black students" in high-novice Mississippi schools versus 7% of non-Black students; Maryland shows nearly 40% versus about 20%.
