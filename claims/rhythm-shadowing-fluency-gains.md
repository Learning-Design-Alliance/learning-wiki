The review reports two quasi-experimental studies of rhythm-shadowing (speaking in synchrony with a metronome or tapping beat); improvements in "mean length of run and pause ratio" are described as measurable but no effect sizes are printed.

> "two quasi-experimental studies ( Cancer et al., 2022; Trofimovich & Gatbonton, 2006) reported that learners who engaged in rhythm-shadowing—speaking in synchrony with a metronome or tapping beat—demonstrated measurable improvements in mean length of run and pause ratio, both indicators of fluency."
