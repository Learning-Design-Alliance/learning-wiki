Jonathan C. Roberts (2024). Creating Data Art: Authentic Learning and Visualisation Exhibition. EG UK Computer Graphics & Visual Computing (2024). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2408.07590](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2408.07590)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

This is the author's design rationale, from the paper's background section, arguing that the exhibition focus constrains attention and provides clear instruction while remaining open-ended to allow creativity. The author states students can discuss clear answers to the 5 W's questions (who, what, how, why, when).

> "Specifying 'for exhibition' provides a well-defined set of constraints. We know who the 'user' is, when the task is required, what is required (something suitable for display), who will need to understand it (the public), where it will be displayed"
