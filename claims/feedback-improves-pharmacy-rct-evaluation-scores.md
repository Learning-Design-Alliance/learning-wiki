## Evidence

### Nawanopparatsakul 2024

Nawanopparatsakul, S., Watana, S., Taesotikul, T., Phuagphong, P., & Chinpaisal, C. (2024). Development of Skills for Pharmacy Students in Evaluating Randomized Controlled Trials Using CASP Checklist. Proceedings of ICRES 2024 -- International Conference on Research in Education and Science. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673088

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-group pre-post assessment of 18 fifth-year pharmacy students in the 565 313 New Drugs course; rubric-scored individual assignments before and after instructor feedback. Post-feedback average was "96.11 ± 8.32%", with Table 2 reporting p-value < 0.0001 versus the pre-feedback 71.11 ± 5.57%.

> "The average score surged to 96.11 ± 8.32%, a statistically significant improvement. Notably, students effectively achieved the learn ing objectives in evaluating medications using the CASP tool."
