## Evidence

### Fei Gao 2023

Fei Gao, Lin Hua, Paulo Armada-da-Silva, Juan Zhang, Defeng Li, Zhiyi Chen, Chengwen Wang, Meng Du, Zhen Yuan. (2023). Shared and distinct neural correlates of first and second language morphological processing in bilingual brain. npj Science of Learning 8:33. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00184-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difference-score analysis of the behavioral data from the 30 bilingual participants: the Chinese facilitation effect was 25.8 ± 33 ms versus −0.7 ± 72 ms for English, a difference the article reports as marginal (p = 0.06); accuracy did not differ (p = 0.91).

> "Chinese morpholo- gical facilitation effect (semantic minus morphological: 25.8 ± 33 ms) seems to be greater than English ( −0.7 ± 72 ms) on RT (p = 0.06), while L1 Chinese and L2 English showed comparable ACC patterns ( p = 0.91)."
