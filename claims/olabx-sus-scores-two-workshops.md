### Dafli E 2015

Dafli E, Antoniou P, Ioannidis L, Dombros N, Topps D, Bamidis PD. (2015). Virtual Patients on the Semantic Web: A Proof-of-Application Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3933

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Formative usability survey at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki workshop using the 10-item System Usability Scale. The article reports the raw SUS score was "a 65.83, placing the OLabX application higher than 40% of all products tested"; no effect size is printed.

> "The raw SUS score from all 33 questionnaires was a 65.83, placing the OLabX application higher than 40% of all products tested."

### Dafli E 2015 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Formative usability survey at the Vilnius (ULAC) workshop with approximately 12 health professionals using the SUS questionnaire. The article reports the raw score was "a 60.8 placing the OLabX application higher than approximately 30% of all products tested"; no effect size is printed.

> "The raw SUS score from all 12 questionnaires was a 60.8 placing the OLabX application higher than approximately 30% of all products tested."
