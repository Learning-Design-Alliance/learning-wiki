Evidence¶

Mitchell P. Herz 2023¶

Mitchell P. Herz, Lamar L. Johnson, and Eric W. Crane. (2023). Accountability for Postsecondary Readiness: A 50-State Analysis. Region 15 Comprehensive Center at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED633301

q2 · i? · design · r3

A 50-state document analysis of all ESSA plans on the U.S. Department of Education website as of May 1, 2023, with two raters coding measures and state agency staff verifying the data. The review found "41 out of 53 ESSA plans (77%) included a postsecondary readiness indicator."

"In total, 41 out of 53 ESSA plans (77%) included a postsecondary readiness indicator as a scoring element that contributed to identifying schools for additional support."

Related Claims¶

  • Postsecondary readiness indicators emphasize academic measures (98% of plans) and career measures (83%) far more than military measures (27%) — related
  • Postsecondary readiness indicators carry an average weight of 16 percent of high school summative accountability scores, ranging from 5 to 33 percent — related
  • Indicator complexity varies from 1 to 23 measures (averaging just under seven), and states with more measures do not necessarily weight the indicator more — related
  • Maryland lacked a measure of schools' support of academic growth from kindergarten to grade 3, creating a critical accountability need under ESSA — related
