## Evidence

### Doors to Discovery™ WWC Intervention Report 2013

Doors to Discovery™ WWC Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. (2013). Doors to Discovery™ Intervention Report, Early Childhood Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/153

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

PCER Consortium (2008, Chapter 6) randomized study with high child-level attrition, 29 classrooms/182 children, using the Pre-CTOPPP Elision subtest with ANCOVA estimation. The reported effect size was 0.14 (improvement index +6), p > 0.05, rated an indeterminate effect and no discernible effects overall.

> "The authors reported that differences between the Doors to Discovery™ group and the comparison group were not statistically significant and, according to WWC criteria, were not large enough to be considered substantively important."
