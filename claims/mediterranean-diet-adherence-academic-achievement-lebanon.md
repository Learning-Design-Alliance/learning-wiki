## Evidence

### Hayek J. 2020

Hayek J., Schneider F., Tueni M., de Vries H. (2020). Is Academic Achievement Related to Mediterranean Diet, Substance Use and Social-Cognitive Factors: Findings from Lebanese Adolescents. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12051535

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 563 Lebanese adolescents analyzed by multivariate logistic regression. The final model reports "aOR:1.34; 95% CI 1.15–1.56" for KIDMED adherence; bivariate association was also significant (p < 0.001).

> "In the third and final model, in which socio-cognitive factors were also added, the odds of having high academic achievement remained significantly greater for students with high adherence to the MeD (aOR:1.34; 95% CI 1.15–1.56)."
