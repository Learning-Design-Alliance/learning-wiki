Ponniah 2011¶

Ponniah, R. J. (2011). The Effectiveness of the Comprehension Hypothesis: A Review on the Current Research on Incidental Vocabulary Acquisition. i-manager's Journal on English Language Teaching, 1(2), April-June 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071042

q2 · i? · review · r1

Review reports Sánchez and Schmitt (2010): 20 Spanish EFL students read the authentic novel 'Things Fall Apart'; vocabulary of Ibo words measured across spelling recognition, word class recall, meaning recall and meaning recognition. Mean gain 9.39 words (28% of 34 targets).

"The subjects acquired an average of 9.39 (28%) words out of the 34 target words, with more gains on the words that occurred more than five times in the novel."
