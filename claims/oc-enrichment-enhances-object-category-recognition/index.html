Evidence¶

Gattas 2022¶

Gattas, S., Collett, H. A., Huff, A. E., Creighton, S. D., Weber, S. E., Buckhalter, S. S., Manning, S. A., Ryait, H. S., McNaughton, B. L., & Winters, B. D. (2022). A rodent obstacle course procedure controls delivery of enrichment and enhances complex cognition. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00134-x

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Object category recognition task (sample 10 min; delays 30 min and 1 h) in the four-group design. Paired-samples t-tests showed the OC, t(9) = −5.895, p < 0.001, and EH, t(9) = −2.937, p = 0.017, groups discriminated the novel category at 1 h; no effect size is printed for the OC vs SH contrast.

"planned comparisons using independent samples t-tests revealed a signiﬁcant difference in group performance on the task between the OC and the SH group, t(18) = 3.884, p = 0.001, when the delay was 1 h."
