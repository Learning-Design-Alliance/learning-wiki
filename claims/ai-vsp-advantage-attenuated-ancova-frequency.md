## Evidence

### Sun Ning 2026

Sun Ning, Zhou Xintong, Yang Zhongqian, Zhou Yu, Ma Ruihang, Wang Zhong. (2026). Application of AI-based virtual standardized patients in physician-patient communication training: a study based on the SEGUE framework. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1768518

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary ANCOVA analysis in the RCT. The authors interpret that the AI-VSP advantage "may be largely attributable to their facilitation of more frequent and flexible practice opportunities rather than to the modality itself"; no effect size is printed.

> "when post-intervention SEGUE scores were entered as the dependent variable in an ANCOVA model, with group as the fixed factor and pre-intervention score and training frequency as covariates, the between-group difference was no longer statistically significant (t = −1.41, df = 80, P =.163)."
