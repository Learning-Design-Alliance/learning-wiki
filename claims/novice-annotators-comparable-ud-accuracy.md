## Evidence

### Lauren Levine 2025

Lauren Levine, Junghyun Min, and Amir Zeldes. (2025). Building UD Cairo for Old English in the Classroom. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.18718](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.18718)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classroom annotation activity in a mid-level mixed over/undergraduate Historical Linguistics course; Figure 1 plots UAS and LAS by annotator group. Novices had only minimal UD instruction but could use Grew-match corpus searches and UDPipe/Stanza parsers. No effect size is printed.

> "while the mean scores (indicated with red dots) are somewhat higher for the experienced group, novice students were able to achieve rather comparable scores by using corpus searches and automatic modern English parsers as references, and the median novice LAS score is even higher than the experienced one"
