Evidence¶

Swanson et al. 2013¶

H. Lee Swanson, Michael J. Orosco, Cathy Lussier. (2013). Does Cognitive Strategy Training on Word Problems Compensate for Working Memory Capacity in Children with Math Difficulties? SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563039

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed model ANCOVA of the randomized 2 (MD vs. NMD) x 4 (treatment) design on posttest CMAT solution accuracy, with pretest accuracy and WMC as covariates. The article reports "A significant effect emerged for the treatment x WMC interaction"; the risk x treatment x WMC interaction only approached significance, p < .10.

"A significant effect emerged for the treatment x WMC interaction, F (3,140) =4.25, p < .01 and the pretest CMAT score, F (1,140) =76.70, p < .001."
