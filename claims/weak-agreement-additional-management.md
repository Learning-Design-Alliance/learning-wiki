`q3 i?` Agreement on additional imaging was moderate (κ = 0.45) and on conservative treatment modalities weak (κ = 0.39), with the student recommending less imaging than surgeons. [→ Yin David 2019](#yin-david-2019)

## Evidence

### Yin David 2019

Yin David, Cabana François, Tousignant-Laflamme Yannick, Bédard Sonia, Tousignant Michel. (2019). Can a physiotherapy student assume the role of an advanced practice physiotherapist in Orthopaedic surgery triage? A prospective observational study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-019-2864-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the 86-patient study, additional management recommendations were tallied from standardized forms. The student recommended imaging for "15.1% vs. OS: 30.2%" of patients; conservative-treatment agreement was κ = 0.39.

> "Generally, the PS suggested less additional imaging tests compared to the OSs (PS: 15.1% vs. OS: 30.2%) and agreement for these was moderate (κ = 0.45)."
