## Evidence

### Kim S 2020

Kim S, Yang Eunbae B. (2020). Does group cohesion foster self-directed learning for medical students? A longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1962-7

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlation and regression analysis of three-year accumulated mean scores in the longitudinal cohort. The article reports "a significant positive relationship (r = .57, p < .001)" and a regression model that "predicted 32% variance" (F = 131.363, p < .001).

> "The correlation of overall mean scores between SDLRS and GCS scores accumulated for the three years showed a significant positive relationship (r = .57, p < .001). The correlation between the seven subdomains of SDLRS and GCS scores showed that all subdomains of SDLRS scores had significant positive relationships with GCS mean scores (p < .01)."
