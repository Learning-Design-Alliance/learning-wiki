Zeitschrift Fur Gerontologie Und Geriatrie. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00391-026-02646-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-country comparison of DACH responses (n = 291) against other European region countries (n = 5134), reported in the Results comparison subsection and Supplementary File 5. Percentages are descriptive deviations; no effect size is printed.

> "Areas of noteworthy difference (more than 20% deviation from other European region countries) were found for the topics healthy ageing (26.5%), falls and mobility (22.1%), delirium (24.5%), dementia (26.2%), assessment of cognitive status (25.0%), functional capacity (26.3%) and malnutrition (26.9%), advance healthcare directives (25.5%), palliative care (22.3%), chronic pain (24.1%), geriatric rehabilitation (27.8%), orthogeriatrics (21.3%) and dysphagia (22.3%)."
