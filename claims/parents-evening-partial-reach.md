## Evidence

### Vanderhoven 2015

Vanderhoven, E., Schellens, T., Vanderlinde, R., & Valcke, M. (2015). Developing educational materials about risks on social network sites: A design-based research approach. Ghent University. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9415-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Study 3 combined the course with a parents' evening (146 pupils, average age 12.92; 50 parents). Quantitative impact was hard to interpret because only 19 children of attending parents completed both pretest and posttest.

> "53% of the parents who attended the information evening gave their children some information about the risks on SNSs and how to behave more safely after the information session"

### Vanderhoven 2015 (2)

Vanderhoven, E., Schellens, T., Vanderlinde, R., & Valcke, M. (2015). Developing educational materials about risks on social network sites: A design-based research approach. Ghent University. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9415-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The authors note the self-selection problem: attending parents were already involved, and characteristics of non-attending parents could not be measured.

> "since only 15% of the invited parents attended the information evening, simply organizing information evenings may not be the best way to involve all parents"
