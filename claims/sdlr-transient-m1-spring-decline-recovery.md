## Evidence

### Boadum O 2026

Boadum O, Wilson TD, Thompson K, Robertson EM, Notebaert A. (2026). Self-directed learning readiness in preclinical medical education: Patterns, variation, and academic outcomes from a mixed longitudinal and cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09867-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal mixed-effects analysis of repeated participants (267 students with two or more semesters) from the 2022–2024 survey study. The article reports the pairwise semester changes with p-values and a net rise of "approximately 3.6% over two years"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Pairwise analyses of repeated participants revealed a modest decline from M1 Fall to M1 Spring (Δ ≈ −0.9%, p = .018), followed by increases through M2 Fall (+ 2.4%, p = .025) and M2 Spring (+ 1.2%, p = .012), yielding a net rise of approximately 3.6% over two years."

### Boadum O 2026 (2)

Boadum O, Wilson TD, Thompson K, Robertson EM, Notebaert A. (2026). Self-directed learning readiness in preclinical medical education: Patterns, variation, and academic outcomes from a mixed longitudinal and cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09867-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary linear mixed-effects model treating semester as a continuous variable (coded 1–4) in the same longitudinal sample. The slope βstd = +0.03 was nonsignificant (p = .30); no effect size is printed.

> "The secondary continuous-time model showed a small, nonsignificant overall linear slope (βstd = + 0.03, 95% CI − 0.03 to + 0.10, p = .30), indicating that SDL-R gains occurred most prominently at curricular transition points rather than as a uniform linear trend."
