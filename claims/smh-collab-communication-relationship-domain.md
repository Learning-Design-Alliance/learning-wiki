First theme reported in the Results section of the Delphi study. Panelists identified critical skills including active listening and constructive feedback, dispositions including 'empathy, psychological safety, humility, and openness to feedback', and knowledge of confidentiality laws (FERPA/HIPAA).

> "Panelists emphasized that collaboration depends on clear, respectful, and transparent communication across multiple modes, including in-person meetings, emails, phone calls, texts, and virtual platforms, ensuring all team members, families, and community partners are informed."
