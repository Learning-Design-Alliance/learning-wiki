### L. B. Okun 2008

L. B. Okun. (2008). The theory of relativity and the Pythagorean theorem. https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.2379 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0809.2379](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0809.2379)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical argument in section 8.10 for two or more free particles: because E and P are conserved, the system mass "M 2 = E 2 − P 2" is conserved, yet "mass is no t additive". The author criticises conflating non-additivity with non-conservation, citing Landau and Lifshitz.

> "With E and P conserved, the mass M of a system (a set) of particles, deﬁned by the formula M 2 = E 2 − P 2, must be conserved as well. In contrast to energy and momentum, however, mass is no t additive: M ⁄= ∑ mi."
