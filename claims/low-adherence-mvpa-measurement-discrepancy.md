Watson Amanda, Dumuid Dorothea, Maher Carol, Olds Tim. (2022). Associations between meeting 24-hour movement guidelines and academic achievement in Australian primary school-aged children. Journal of Sport and Health Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2020.12.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive proportions from Fig. 1 in the CheckPoint sample (self-report n = 1270; accelerometry n = 927). Sleep guideline compliance was similar across methods (74.0% self-report vs. 72.4% accelerometry), while MVPA compliance varied substantially; these are descriptive percentages with no test statistics.

> "Results for meeting the MVPA guideline varied between self-report (77.4%) and accelerometry measures (48.2%). The screen time guideline (self-report only) was met by approximately one-third (32.9%) of children."
