# US expectancy ratings show a conceptual generalization gradient: stimuli more similar to the CS+ elicit higher ratings (GS1 > GS2 > GS3)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` During generalization, US expectancy ratings decreased as conceptual similarity to the CS+ decreased (main effect ηp2 = 0.520; GS1 > GS2 > GS3, all p < 0.001). [→ Xin Liu 2025](#xin-liu-2025)

## Evidence

### Xin Liu 2025

Xin Liu, Benjamin Becker, Ya Jie Wang, Ying Mei, Haoran Dou & Yi Lei. (2025). A visual generalization gradient of conceptual stimuli based on fear acquisition in visual and auditory modalities. npj Science of Learning 10:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00318-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on US expectancy ratings in the generalization phase of the 40-participant experiment, where GSs varied in conceptual similarity to the CS+ (high GS1, moderate GS2, low GS3). The article reports F2, 158 = 85.506 with effect size ηp2 = 0.520, and pairwise differences all p < 0.001.

> "the main effect of GS type on US expectancy ratings was signiﬁcant (F2, 158 = 85.506, p <0 . 0 0 1 ,ηp2 =0 . 5 2 0 ) . Ratings for GS1 were signi ﬁcantly higher than for GS2 and GS3 (both p < 0.001), with ratings for GS2 also being signiﬁcantly higher than for GS3 (p < 0.001)."
