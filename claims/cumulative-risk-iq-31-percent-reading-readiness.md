# A cumulative risk index and nonverbal IQ together account for 31% of variance in reading readiness at school entry

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Nonverbal IQ (R² = .18) and cumulative risk (R² = .03) each uniquely predicted reading readiness, jointly explaining 31% of its variance; IQ did not predict attention and behaviour. [→ Dilnot 2017](#dilnot-2017)

## Evidence

### Dilnot 2017

Dilnot, J., Hamilton, L., Maughan, B. and Snowling, M. (2017). Child and Environmental Risk Factors Predicting Readiness for Learning in Children at High-Risk of Dyslexia. Development & Psychopathology, 29(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.1017/S0954579416000134

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Parallel regression analyses entering the cumulative risk index with nonverbal IQ (performance IQ at T1) as predictors of school-entry outcomes. The article reports the two factors "predicted 31% of the variance in reading readiness and 10% of variance in attention and behaviour".

> "Together these two factors assessed in preschool predicted 31% of the variance in reading readiness and 10% of variance in attention and behaviour at school entry."
