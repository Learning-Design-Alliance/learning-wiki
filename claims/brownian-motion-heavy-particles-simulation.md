Simulation demonstration of Brownian motion (Fig. 5): two heavy particles are immersed in gas at 250 K and move due to collisions; the trajectory is displayed and a dedicated window monitors their distance from the initial position as a function of time. No effect size is printed.

> "Two larger circles with masses hundred times larger than the atoms are immersed into the g as which is initiated at the temperature of 250 K. As a result of the collisions they begin t o move."
