Evidence¶

Anna Keune 2022¶

Anna Keune (2022). Material syntonicity: Examining computational performance and its materiality through weaving and sewing crafts. Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2022.2100704

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Video assemblage analysis of student-loom intra-actions across the weaving unit. Four intra-actions started regular and remained regular, while most started with irregular patterns that "could not be translated into functions"; over time projects increased in regularity, as in Devanie's hourglass skip pattern whose pseudocode showed increased regularity.

"Analyzing student-loom intra-actions during computationally relevant moments and changes of patterns over time showed that computation in weaving was characterized by the performance of automation. Four student-loom intra-actions produced pattern variations that started regular and remained regular. However, most student-loom intra-actions started with irregular patterns that could not be translated into functions. Over time, projects increased in regularity."
