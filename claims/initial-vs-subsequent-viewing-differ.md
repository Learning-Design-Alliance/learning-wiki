## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' first views often terminated at the second interaction point (of five), where the starter Python script download link appeared, and later views differed from initial ones. [→ John M. Aiken 2013](#john-m-aiken-2013)

## Evidence

### John M. Aiken 2013

John M. Aiken, Shih-Yin Lin, Scott S. Douglas, Edwin F. Greco, Brian D. Thoms, Marcos D. Caballero and Michael F. Schatz. (2013). Student Use of a Single Lecture Video in a Flipped Introductory Mechanics Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.2620](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.2620)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of clickstream logs for the Fall 2013 semester: 622 total views, "4.2±0.2 average views per student", and 148 students (91.9% of the class) viewed at least once. The article attributes the differing initial behavior to the download link at the second interaction point.

> "Students viewed “Creating a Computer Model of Constant Velocity Motion” 622 times during the Fall 2013 semester (4.2±0.2 average views per student). 148 students (91.9% of the class) viewed the video at least once."
