## Evidence

### Scherer-Quenzer AC 2026

Scherer-Quenzer AC, Reinhart K, Diessner J, Altides A, Buechel J, Blau-Schneider B, Sauer ST, Deschler-Baier B, Woeckel A, Kiesel M. (2026). Comparative validation of a cost-effective in-house 3D-printed LLETZ simulator versus a commercial training model. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-026-08413-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Resection status was assessed after the first excision attempt in the randomized student cohort (n = 60). The article reports no statistically significant difference between the two simulators; in attempt three 100% vs 83.3% and in attempt four 96.7% vs 73.3% achieved R0.

> "Regarding R0 resections, there was no statistically significant difference between the two simulators. During the first attempt, 17 students achieved complete (R0) resection using the conventional simulator (56.7%), and 19 participants (63.3%) using the in-house model."
