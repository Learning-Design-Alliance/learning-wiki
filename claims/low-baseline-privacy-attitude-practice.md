## Evidence

### Adib-Hajbaghery Mohsen 2016

Adib-Hajbaghery Mohsen, Faraji Mona. (2016). Comparison of effect between group discussion and educational booklet on Iranian nursing students’ attitude and practice toward patient privacy. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2016.13.29

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive pretest finding from the two-group study: baseline attitude means were 12.73±7.08 (discussion) and 14.55±10.31 (booklet), practice means 14.34±1.54 and 14.79±2.95, with no between-group differences (P=0.454 and P=0.493). The authors interpret that "patient privacy is not adequately noticed in the nursing curriculum".

> "Before the intervention, all students in the present study had lower attitude and practice scores on respecting patient privacy."
