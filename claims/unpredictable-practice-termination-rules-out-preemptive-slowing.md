## Evidence

### Marlene Bönstrup 2020

Marlene Bönstrup, Iñaki Iturrate, Martin N. Hebart, Nitzan Censor and Leonardo G. Cohen. (2020). Mechanisms of offline motor learning at a microscale of seconds in large-scale crowdsourced data. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0066-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 4: 71 participants trained 41 trials with randomized 5–10 s practice periods and 10 s rest, preventing preemptive slowing toward a predictable practice end. Micro-offline gains remained substantial (1.52 ± 0.27 keypresses/s, P < 0.001) and micro-online changes were nil.

> "No signi ﬁcant differ-ences in total early learning or microscale learning were evident (P > 0.05) and microscale learning between groups was statistically equivalent (TOST for equivalence)."
