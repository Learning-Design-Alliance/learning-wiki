### Ruiz-Martín Héctor 2024

Ruiz-Martín Héctor, Blanco Fernando, Ferrero Marta. (2024). Which learning techniques supported by cognitive research do students use at secondary school? Prevalence and associations with students’ beliefs and achievement. Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-024-00567-5

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

In the same correlational analysis of the techniques survey against average grades (n = 2,414), the article reports that the composite of supported techniques excluding distributed practice reached “the highest correlation with grades (r = 0.300)”.

> "The highest correlation with grades (r = 0.300) was achieved when combining all research-supported techniques except for distributed practice."
