## Evidence

### Nieminen 2007

Nieminen, T. A. (2007). Project-based assessment for graduate coursework in physics. Australian Institute of Physics (AIP) 17th National Congress 2006: Refereed Papers. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0702046](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0702046)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Author's observational results from the 2005-2006 trials: completion within the allowed time was almost universal, and "every question was attempted by at least one student" on the five-choice second exams.

> "The time spent by the students on the questions was greater than expected, with three days being typical. The variety of methods employed by the students was surprising; for the ﬁrst exam in 2005, where all students attempted the same question, there were ﬁve completely distinct strategies used by the 13 students"
