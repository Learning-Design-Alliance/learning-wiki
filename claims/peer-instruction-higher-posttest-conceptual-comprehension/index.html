Evidence¶

Al-Hebaishi 2017¶

Safaa Mohammad Al-Hebaishi. (2017). The Effect of Peer Instruction Method on Pre-Service Teachers' Conceptual Comprehension of Methodology Course. Journal of Education and Learning, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v6n3p70

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre/post study of 78 female pre-service teachers; treatment group (N = 41) received peer instruction, comparison group (N = 37) traditional lecture for 16 weeks. The article reports the post-test means were "statistically different (t = 7.901; sig = 000**)"; no effect size is printed in this sentence.

"The peer instruction method group's mean post-test score was M = 37.219; whereas, the regular instruction group's mean score was M = 31.216, and thus, statistically different (t = 7.901; sig = 000**)."

Al-Hebaishi 2017 (2)¶

Safaa Mohammad Al-Hebaishi. (2017). The Effect of Peer Instruction Method on Pre-Service Teachers' Conceptual Comprehension of Methodology Course. Journal of Education and Learning, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v6n3p70

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Effect-size computation reported in the results section, calculated from the between-subjects t-test value and the degrees of freedom. The article reports "d = 1.81" and calls it "a very large effect size among students in the treatment group".

"The findings showed that d = 1.81. Hence, peer instruction method exhibited a very large effect size among students in the treatment group."
