Smylie 2006¶

Smylie, Mark A., and Stacy A. Wenzel. (2006). Promoting Instructional Improvement: A Strategic Human Resource Management Perspective. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/promoting-instructional-improvement-strategic-human-resource-management-perspective

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

The report reviews two types of policy instruments used to promote intellectually ambitious instruction. It argues "guidance instruments can direct, motivate, and perhaps induce compliance" but cannot build capacity alone, and professional development needs complementary incentives and conditions.

"Although guidance instruments can direct, motivate, and perhaps induce compliance with particular teaching practices, they do not help teachers develop the knowledge and skills required to change their practice in meaningful and sustainable ways."
