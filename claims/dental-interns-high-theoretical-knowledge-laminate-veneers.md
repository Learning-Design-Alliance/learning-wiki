### Muhammed Ubeydullah 2026

Muhammed Ubeydullah, Yanıkoğlu Nuran, Ökten Tuba. (2026). Knowledge, attitudes, practices and clinical preparedness regarding porcelain laminate veneers among dental interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09863-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 134 fifth-year dental interns at Atatürk University using a 21-item KAP instrument (14 objective, 7 subjective). The analysis of objective items in Table 2 found "correct response rates exceeding 85% for most items"; no effect size was reported for this descriptive finding.

> "participants generally demonstrated a high level of theoretical knowledge regarding porcelain laminate veneers, with correct response rates exceeding 85% for most items, including indications, contraindications, advantages, disadvantages, ceramic material selection, and common complications and failure modes"
