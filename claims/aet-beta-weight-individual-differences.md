## Evidence

### Stanovich 1997

Keith E. Stanovich and Richard F. West. (1997). Reasoning Independently of Prior Belief and Individual Differences in Actively Open-Minded Thinking. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.89.2.342

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results of 349 separate multiple regressions, one per participant, in the Argument Evaluation Test study; each participant's 23 evaluation scores were regressed on argument quality and prior belief scores. The article reports the mean beta weight for argument quality was ".330" with wide variation, and that "Only 30 of 349 participants had beta weights less than zero."

> "Across the 349 regressions, the mean standardized beta weight for argument quality was .330 {SD = .222). These latter values varied widely—from a low of —.489 to a high of .751."
