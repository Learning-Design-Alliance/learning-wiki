## Evidence

### Dudley 2015

Dudley, D. A., Cotton, W. G., & Peralta, L. R. (2015). Teaching approaches and strategies that promote healthy eating in primary school children: a systematic review and meta-analysis. International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity, 12, 28. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-015-0182-8

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of contingent reinforcement approaches: four studies, all reporting significance of at least p < .05; six effect sizes from only two studies, four large and two minimal. The review reports an average Cohen's d of Md = 1.34.

> "Based on these two studies, the average effect size for contingent reinforcement in promoting FV consumption or preference is Md = 1.34. More studies are needed in order to ascertain an average effect size with less variance"
