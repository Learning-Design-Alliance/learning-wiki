## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Only teaching shift (p = .019) and academic year (p = .032) showed statistically significant differences in total SCLS score; morning-shift students (mean 55.0) scored higher than afternoon (53.7) and second-year students (55.3) higher than third (53.6) and fourth (54.5).
