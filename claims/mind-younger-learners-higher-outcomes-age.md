## Evidence

### Monib Wali Khan 2025

Monib Wali Khan, Qazi Atika, Apong Rosyzie Anna, Santos Jose Hernandez, Mahmud Malissa Maria. (2025). The MIND model a microlearning AI-integrated instructional design for enhanced learning outcomes. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-24910-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Because normality was not met, age-group differences in the experimental group were tested with Kruskal–Wallis (χ2(2) = 8.495, p < .05); Mann–Whitney post hoc tests showed 18–24 versus 35–44 and 18–24 versus 25–34 significant at p < .05, while 25–34 versus 35–44 was not.
