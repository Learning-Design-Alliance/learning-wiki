Read Well® 2010¶

Read Well®. (2010). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. http://store.cambiumlearning.com/resource.aspx?page=ProgramOverview&site=sw&parentId=019005451

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

The WWC review of the Frasco (2008) RCT reports a positive vocabulary finding on the PPVT-III. Appendix A3.2 prints a mean difference of 7.71, an effect size of 0.56, and an improvement index of 21.06; the effect size is "large enough to be considered substantively important according to WWC criteria".

"Frasco (2008) reported a positive and statistically significant difference in vocabulary gains as measured by the Peabody Picture Vocabulary Test–Third Edition (PPVT-III)."
