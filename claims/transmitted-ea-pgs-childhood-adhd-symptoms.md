# The transmitted EA polygenic score is associated with children's ADHD symptoms both at home (β = − 0.13) and at school (β = − 0.13), a cross-domain genetic transmission effect

`q2 i1` The transmitted EA PGS significantly predicted children's ADHD symptoms in both the home and school contexts, with equal negative coefficients. [→ de Zeeuw Eveline L. 2020](#de-zeeuw-eveline-l-2020)

## Evidence

### de Zeeuw Eveline L. 2020

de Zeeuw Eveline L., Hottenga Jouke-Jan, Ouwens Klaasjan G., Dolan Conor V., Ehli Erik A., Davies Gareth E., Boomsma Dorret I., van Bergen Elsje. (2020). Intergenerational Transmission of Education and ADHD: Effects of Parental Genotypes. Behavior Genetics, Volume 50, Issue 4. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10519-020-09992-w

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Within the childhood model 1 regressions on transmitted and non-transmitted EA PGSs, the transmitted EA PGS significantly predicted "ADHD symptoms at home (β = − 0.13, p < 0.001) and ADHD symptoms at school (β = − 0.13, p < 0.001)"; the non-transmitted EA PGS was not significant.
