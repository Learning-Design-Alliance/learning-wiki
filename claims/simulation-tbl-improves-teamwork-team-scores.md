## Evidence

### Zhang Li-dan 2026

Zhang Li-dan, Wang Li, Li Ya-ting, Guo Yao-yu, Lin Hong-xin, Ye Xiao-ting, Chen Hua-bao, Tang Yan-lai, Chen Chun, Xu Ling-ling. (2026). Effectiveness of integrated simulation and team-based learning in paediatric postgraduates for cardiopulmonary resuscitation training: a multiple methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08694-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison within the same 24-participant cohort, teamwork assessed with the French TEAM scoring system; Table 3 reports t = 6.07, P < 0.05. Only a test statistic is printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "Quantitative data showed a significant improvement in team collaboration scores (28.33 ± 2.46 vs. 32.99 ± 2.85, P < 0.05). Qualitative feedback highlighted "clear division of labor" and "complementary collaboration" as key strengths, echoing Johnson's meta-analytic findings."
