### Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck 2023

Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck. (2023). Effects of positive and negative social feedback on motivation, evaluative learning, and socio-emotional processing. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00178-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

SC-IAT administered at the end of the SID-Video study to assess implicit cue valence; a 2 × 2 mixed permutation F-test showed a significant main effect of condition (p = 0.0042) with no significant main effect of age or interaction. Mean D-scores: positive cue 0.14 (young) and 0.16 (older); negative cue 0.04 and −0.06.

> "The one-sample permutationt-tests on the D-scores for the positive and negative cue across both age groups showed that the D-score for the positive cue was signiﬁcantly different from zero (p = 0.0003) while the D-score for the negative cue was not"
