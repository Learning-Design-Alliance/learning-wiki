`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reported from the original randomized study (intervention n = 57; control n = 55); despite randomization the intervention group scored generally lower on the pretests. Post-test adjusted means favour the intervention group on spelling, comprehension, speed and decoding (Table 2).

> "A structural equation model showed that there was a signiﬁcant effect of the intervention at the post-test in favour of the intervention group on spelling, reading comprehension, reading speed (Wolff, 2010, 2011) and word decoding (Wolff, 2010)."
