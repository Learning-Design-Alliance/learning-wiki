### Harris Rebecca B. 2019

Harris Rebecca B., Grunspan Daniel Z., Pelch Michael A., Fernandes Giselle, Ramirez Gerardo, Freeman Scott. (2019). Can Test Anxiety Interventions Alleviate a Gender Gap in an Undergraduate STEM Course? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-05-0083

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression of post-course trait anxiety on treatment controlling for pre-course anxiety in the quasi-randomized sections. The article reports the intervention "did not change self-reported test anxiety" (β = −0.98, p = 0.31); the gender-by-treatment interaction was also non-significant (β = 0.58, p = 0.63).

> "Controlling for test anxiety at the start of the course, we found that the intervention did not change self-reported test anxiety (main effect of treatment: β = −0.98, p = 0.31)."
