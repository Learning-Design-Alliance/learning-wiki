## Evidence

### Kelley 1916

Kelley, Truman Lee. (1916). A constructive ability test. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. VII, No. 1. https://www.google.com/search?q=Kelley+1916+A+Constructive+Ability+Test+Journal+of+Educational+Psychology

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Psychometric evaluation of the merit scale built by the Thorndike handwriting-scale method from rankings by ten judges of structures built by normal and subnormal boys. The printed reliability correlations are r = .98 (quote) and r = .92, "the correlation between an individual's placement of a large number of samples and the true placement in the scale is .92"; practice with 40-50 samples halves the probable error of judgment.

> "The grades given to the samples would correlate with other scale values, derived in a similar manner from the judgments of other equally competent judges to the extent of .98."
