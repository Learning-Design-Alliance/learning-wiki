## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taught with a blended critical-thinking program scored higher on academic achievement than students taught traditionally (Mean=15.67 vs 13.88). [→ Mosalanejad 2010](#mosalanejad-2010)

## Evidence

### Mosalanejad 2010

Mosalanejad, L., Alipor, A., & Zandi, B. (2010). A Blended Education Program Based on Critical Thinking and Its Effect on Personality Type and Attribution Style of the Students. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 11(2). http://tojde.anadolu.edu.tr/tojde39/articles/article_12.htm

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 41 students randomly divided into blended and traditional groups; independent t-test showed the blended group's final scores were higher, with the article reporting T=3.06 and p=0.004.

> "The mean score of academic achievement in the blended education group (Mean=15.67, SD=1.65, T=3.06, p=0,004), was higher than that of the traditional education (Mean=13.88, SD=2.06)."
