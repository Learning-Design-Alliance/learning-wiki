Xiaoyu Bai · Manfred Stede 2023¶

Xiaoyu Bai · Manfred Stede. (2023). A Survey of Current Machine Learning Approaches to Student Free-Text Evaluation for Intelligent Tutoring. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education (2023) 33:994–1032. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00323-0

q2 · i? · review · r2

The survey reports Nadeem et al. (2019)'s finding in the context of hybrid neural and feature-based approaches to essay scoring; Nadeem et al. also used contextualised word embeddings from a static BERT model to initialise their LSTM-based architecture. No effect size is printed.
