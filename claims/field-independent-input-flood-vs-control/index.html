Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan 2012¶

Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan. (2012). The Role of Cognitive Style in the Collocational Knowledge Development of Iranian EFL Learners through Input Flood Treatment. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p105

Independent-samples t-tests reported in Table 4 comparing field-independent experimental (n=17) and field-independent control (n=18) learners on both post-tests; the article reports significance at p=.000 for both. No effect size is printed.

"in the case of field independent learners, the subjects in the input flood group achieved significantly higher scores than the ones in the control group in both immediate and delayed post-tests."
