Evidence¶

Puckett 2021¶

Puckett, H. M., Bossaller, J. S., & Sheets, L. R. (2021). The impact of project ECHO on physician preparedness to treat opioid use disorder: a systematic review. Addiction Science & Clinical Practice, 16, 6. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13722-021-00215-z

q2 · i? · review · r2

Post-session survey data from 104 physician participants across ECHO sessions (41 participants submitted 299 surveys). Reported learning covered diagnosis, behavioral/medical interventions, outside resources, and stigma reduction; self-assessment only.

"254/295 (85%) of respondents reported that they had learned something new from others’ cases, and 231/249 (93%) responded that the information they learned would be useful."

Puckett 2021 (2)¶

Puckett, H. M., Bossaller, J. S., & Sheets, L. R. (2021). The impact of project ECHO on physician preparedness to treat opioid use disorder: a systematic review. Addiction Science & Clinical Practice, 16, 6. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13722-021-00215-z

q2 · i? · review · r2

Same post-session survey study of ECHO case presentations. The review also notes 70/81 (86%) rated the quality of input 5/5; all data are self-report.

"65/84 (77%) of those who presented a case changed their patient care plans as a result of the ECHO."
