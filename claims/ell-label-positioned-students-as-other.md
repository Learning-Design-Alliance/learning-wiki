## Evidence

### Takeuchi 2020

Takeuchi, M.A. (2020). Geopolitical configuration of identities and learning: Othering through the institutionalized categorization of “English language learners. Cognition and Instruction. Advance online publication https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2020.1825438

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interactional analysis of video excerpts involving Daniel, a recently arrived immigrant student classified as an ELL. Iris gave directives, slowed her talk, offered amplified cues, and corrected his French, in a structure similar to “foreigner talk”; Daniel returned to playing with paper instead of the suggested task.

> "Such discursive structures simultaneously perpetuate “ratiolinguistic ideologies that conflate certain racialized bodies with linguistic deficiency” (Flores & Rosa, 2015, p. 150). This chain of interaction positioned Iris as a more competent multilingual and Daniel as a learner of languages; in other words, this chain of interactions positioned Daniel as the Other who was deprived of speaking and expressing."
