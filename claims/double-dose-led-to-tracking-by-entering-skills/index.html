Double-dose algebra policy evaluation in the Chicago Public Schools 2003¶

Double-dose algebra policy evaluation in the Chicago Public Schools. (2003). Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/ujree20

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Evaluation of the Chicago double-dose algebra policy reports an organizational side effect of the requirement that students below the national median take a support algebra course: schools began tracking algebra classes by students' entering math skills.

"The policy also led schools to track algebra classes by students' entering math skills."
