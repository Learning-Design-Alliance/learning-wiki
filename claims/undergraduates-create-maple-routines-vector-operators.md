### Danilo T. Alves 2011

Danilo T. Alves, Silvio C. F. Pereira Filho. (2011). Computer algebra systems as tools for learning Physics and Mathematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1104.4461](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1104.4461)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

A pedagogical experience at the Federal University of Pará in which undergraduate students programmed simple Maple routines for the operators grad, div and rot as tools for learning electromagnetism. The authors state students applied their own routines "exploring themselves the meaning of these operators" and call it a successful experience, referring to Ref. [4] for details.

> "The students could apply their own routines (operators) in scalar a nd vector ﬁelds, exploring themselves the meaning of these operators. It was a successful experience, as described in Ref. [4]."
