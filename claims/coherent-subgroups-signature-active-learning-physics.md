### Adrienne L. Traxler 2020

Adrienne L. Traxler, Tyme Suda, Eric Brewe, and Kelley Commeford. (2020). Network positions in active learning environments in physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

CONCOR positional analysis of week-1 and week-10 network surveys in four introductory physics curricula (Peer Instruction, SCALE-UP, ISLE, context-rich problems) from the CALEP project. The abstract reports a "late-term tendency to form coherent subgroups that communicate actively among themselves but have few int er-position links", which the authors propose may be a network signature of active learning classes pending wider data collection.

> "At the level of detail s hown in the blockmodels, most of the curricula are more alike than diﬀerent, showing a late-term tendency to form coherent subgroups that communicate actively among themselves but have few int er-position links."
