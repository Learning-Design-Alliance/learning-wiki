Huiyoung Shin. (2022). Social contagion of academic behavior: Comparing social networks of close friends and admired peers. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0265385

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SAOM analysis of admire peer networks found a non-significant similar engagement selection effect (B = 0.15, SE = 0.15, OR = 1.16); equivalence was not tested, only non-significance reported.

> "However, similar engagement selection effect was not significant for the admire networks, indicating that similarity in academic engagement did not affect formation of the admire relations."
