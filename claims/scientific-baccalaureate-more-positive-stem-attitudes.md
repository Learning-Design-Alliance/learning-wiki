# Pre-service teachers with a scientific baccalaureate hold significantly more positive attitudes toward integrated STEM education than those with a literary baccalaureate

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Students with a scientific baccalaureate scored significantly higher than students with a literary baccalaureate on overall attitudes toward integrated STEM education (r=0.39, medium effect). [→ Aziz Amaaz 2024](#aziz-amaaz-2024)
`q2 i3` The scientific-literary difference was significant on all three dimensions: importance (r=0.37) and self-confidence (r=0.47) medium, enjoyment (r=0.14) small. [→ Aziz Amaaz 2024 (2)](#aziz-amaaz-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Aziz Amaaz 2024

Aziz Amaaz, Abderrahman Mouradi, Moahamed Erradi, Ali Allouch. (2024). Moroccan pre-service elementary teachers: attitudes toward STEM education and mobile devices. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE). https://doi.org/10.11591/ijere.v13i5.28205

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U test comparing 174 scientific-baccalaureate and 52 literary-baccalaureate students; Table 6 reports overall attitude U=2009.5, z=-6.091, p=0.000, r=0.39 (medium effect), with scientific students scoring higher (M=3.01 vs M=2.62).

> "the Mann -Whitney U test used to compare the attitudes of BEES students with a scientific baccalaureate degree and those with a literary baccalaureate degree toward integrated STEM education revealed statistically significant differences between the means of the responses of these two groups of students"

### Aziz Amaaz 2024 (2)

Aziz Amaaz, Abderrahman Mouradi, Moahamed Erradi, Ali Allouch. (2024). Moroccan pre-service elementary teachers: attitudes toward STEM education and mobile devices. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE). https://doi.org/10.11591/ijere.v13i5.28205

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Three further Mann-Whitney tests in Table 6: importance U=2214.5, p=0.000, r=0.37; self-confidence U=1501.5, p=0.000, r=0.47; enjoyment U=3649.5, p=0.033, r=0.14; scientific-baccalaureate means higher on all.

> "The differences between the mean scores of the responses of the two groups of students to the items measuring "importance of integrated STEM education" and "self -confidence in implementing integrated STEM education" are moderate, while the differences between the mean scores of the responses of the two groups of students to the items measuring "enjoyment of implementing integrated STEM education" are small"
