## Evidence

### Benjamin P. Koester 2016

Benjamin P. Koester, Galina Grom, and Timothy A. McKay. (2016). Patterns of Gendered Performance Difference in Introductory STEM Courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1608.07565](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1608.07565)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

LASSO predictive analysis of grade prediction across all 116 large courses, supplemented by linear regression and matching on covariates including college of enrollment, ACT Math, ACT English, and high school GPA. The article reports these adjustments only "narrow gender performance diﬀerences somewhat"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "High school GPA and standardized tests scores add little comparative predictive power, and while they narrow gender performance diﬀerences somewhat, they call far short of eliminating those seen in our large intro STEM courses."
