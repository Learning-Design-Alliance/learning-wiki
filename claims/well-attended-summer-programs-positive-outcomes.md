The report cites an evidence review of summer learning programs as its basis, attributing positive outcomes across five domains to well-implemented, well-attended programs. The report itself did not evaluate outcomes; it states the review "concluded that summer programs can be an effective way to address students' academic and developmental needs."

> "when well implemented and well attended, summer enrichment programs, academic programs, and employment programs have demonstrated positive outcomes for youth in areas related to program content, including academic achievement in reading and math, social development, mental health, school engagement, and reduction of risk-taking behaviors."
