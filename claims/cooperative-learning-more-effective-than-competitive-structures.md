The review's synthesis of motivational studies of cooperative classrooms, citing Ames and Archer (1988), Gallimore (1981), and Johnson and Johnson (1985). It concludes cultural similarity per se may matter less than the culture-general adaptive value of cooperative learning.

### Phalet 2004 (2)

> "the introduction of small group learning improved the school achievement of Hawaiian children (Jordan, 1984)."
