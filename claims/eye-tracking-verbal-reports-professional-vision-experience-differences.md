## Evidence

### Jarodzka 2020

Jarodzka, H., Skuballa, I., & Gruber, H. (2020). Eye-Tracking in Educational Practice: Investigating Visual Perception Underlying Teaching and Learning in the Classroom. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09565-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Wyss et al.'s video-based study in which teacher educators' detection (eye-tracking) and interpretation (verbal reports) of critical incidents were compared with student teachers'. The combination of methods, the review notes, allows understanding not only what and where a teacher looked but also why.

> "they found evidence that the combination of eye-tracking and verbal reports has the potential to capture differences between more- and less-experienced teachers, in particular for those who have detected the critical incident."
