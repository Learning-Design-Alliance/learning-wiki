## Evidence

### Elham Dehghanpour & Mahmood Hashemian 2015

Elham Dehghanpour & Mahmood Hashemian. (2015). Efficiency of Using a Web-Based Approach to Teach Reading Strategies to Iranian EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 8(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n10p30

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t test on a 10-item, 5-point Likert questionnaire (adopted from Anderson, 2002) measuring frequency of reading strategy use in hypertext comprehension, administered before and after the 20-hr program to the same 30 participants. No effect size is printed.

> "the participants' familiarity and usage of reading strategies in the pretraining questionnaire ( M = 21.20, SD = 5.96) were significantly different from their familiarity and usage in the posttraining questionnaire (M = 31.76, SD = 4.43), t(29) = -9.18, p = .000."
