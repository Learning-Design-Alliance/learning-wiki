### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Categorization of Quantum Mechanics Problems by Professors and Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02947](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02947)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Scoring study in which three faculty members evaluated all categorizations of 20 quantum mechanics problems by 22 students and 6 faculty, assigning scores of 0, 1, or 2 per category. The article reports faculty categorizations were "rated higher overall than those by students"; no effect size is printed.

> "Figure 1 shows that the categorizations by faculty members were rated higher overall than those by students, despite the diversity in faculty responses."
