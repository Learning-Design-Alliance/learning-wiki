Narrative review attributes this account of deliberate practice to Ericsson & Charness (2004), citing it as the mechanism by which experts attain and maintain high skill levels. No effect size is printed.

> "According to Ericsson & Charness (2004), those that attain high skill levels do so because they continually reflect on their own performance [15]. They tend to focus on the areas in which they are not doing well and practice that competence."
