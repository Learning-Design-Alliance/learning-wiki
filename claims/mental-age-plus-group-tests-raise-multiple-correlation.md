### Arthur I. Gates 1923

Arthur I. Gates. (1923). The correlations of achievement in school subjects with intelligence tests and other variables (Continued). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/record/1923-02675-001

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple, partial and multiple correlation analysis (Table VII) averaging grades IV, V and VI. The author reports the combined multiple correlation reaches 0.605 versus 0.54 (Mental Age) and 0.52 (Group Tests) alone, and notes "The addition of 0.07 or 0.09 to correlations of these magnitudes is important."

> "when the Stanford Mental Age and Group Tests are perfectly combined by use of weights obtained by the regression equation, the multiple correlation is 0.605 as compared to 0.54 or 0.52, which Mental Age or the Group Test, respectively, alone gives."
