# Engage-to-Unlock redistributed effort across tasks: participants spent more time writing and less time evaluating, without increasing overall task duration

## Evidence

### Xiaotian Su 2026

Xiaotian Su, Laura Rimell, Jiazheng Li, Amal Rannen-Triki, Ulrich Paquet, Lisa Anne Hendricks, Rida Qadri, Daphne Ippolito and Piotr Mirowski. (2026). Who Thinks First? Designing Productive Friction with Engage-to-Unlock GenAI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01518

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the controlled four-condition Prolific experiment (N=398), planned interaction contrasts from a repeated-measures mixed-effects model compared the change in duration from writing to evaluation. The article reports the Condition× Task interaction was significant, F(3, 394)= 7.85, p < .001, and total duration did not differ significantly across conditions (F(3, 216.86)= 1.02, p=. 383).

> "Adaptive participants spent 4.77 min longer on Task 1 but 4.80 min less on Task 2, yielding a significant cross-task difference-in-differences of−9.57min ( 𝑝Holm < .001)."
