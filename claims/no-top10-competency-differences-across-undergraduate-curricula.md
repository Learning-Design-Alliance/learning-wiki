## Evidence

### Fürstenberg Sophie 2017

Fürstenberg Sophie, Schick Kristina, Deppermann Jana, Prediger Sarah, Berberat Pascal O., Kadmon Martina, Harendza Sigrid. (2017). Competencies for first year residents – physicians’ views from medical schools with different undergraduate curricula. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0998-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online ranking survey of surgeons and internists at three German medical schools with different undergraduate curricula (vertically integrated at Hamburg and Oldenburg, traditional at Munich). The article reports that "No significant differences were found regarding the top 10 competencies" across the three universities; no effect size was printed.

> "No significant differences were found regarding the top 10 competencies between the participating physicians from Hamburg, Oldenburg, and Munich."
