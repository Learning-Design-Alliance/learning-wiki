## Evidence

### Sibatmika Babu 2026

Sibatmika Babu, Ravishankar Chatta Subramaniam, Carina M. Rebello, Jason W. Morphew, N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Integrative Thinking in Computational Physics Design Essays. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.22154](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.22154)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Rubric developed through iterative analysis of CPDEs from 14 teams in one laboratory section of a first-semester calculus-based physics course; three raters independently applied an initial rubric to a subset of three CPDEs and reached consensus. The analysis "distinguished three levels of integrative thinking: superficial (Level 1), intermediate (Level 2), and substantial (Level 3)."

> "The qualitative coding of the 14 team CPDE reports, guided by the WoT4EDP framework and the finalized analytic rubric, resulted in an operational framework for characterizing the depth of integrative thinking in students' written work. The analysis distinguished three levels of integrative thinking: superficial (Level 1), intermediate (Level 2), and substantial (Level 3)."
