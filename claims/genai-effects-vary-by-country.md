Narrative review synthesis attributing regional variation to resource equity and cultural factors; the review reports, citing So et al. and Yilmaz et al., that GenAI's interactive modes "could weaken teachers' authority" in Korean universities and materials deviated from national curricula in Turkey.

> "the effect of GenAI technologies on educational outcomes proved negative in Korea and Turkey, possibly due to their cultural preferences for traditional teacher-student interaction (So et al., 2024), curricular misalignment (Yilmaz et al., 2024), and technical limitations, such as algorithm bias and low-quality training data"
