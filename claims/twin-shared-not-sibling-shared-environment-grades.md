## Evidence

### Eifler Eike Friederike 2019

Eifler Eike Friederike, Starr Alexandra, Riemann Rainer. (2019). The genetic and environmental effects on school grades in late childhood and adolescence. PLoS ONE 14(12): e0225946. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225946

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model selection via χ2-difference tests on the twin-sibling SEM across mathematics, German, and GPA in both cohorts. The discussion states this implies grades depend on children's partially heritable abilities "instead of on the family status, income or other factors shared by all siblings".

> "For all cases a model including additive genetic effects (a), twin-shared environmental (ct) as well as non-shared environmental effects (e) was found to be the best compromise between parsimony and model fit, confirming an ACtE model (d = cs = 0; see S1 and S2 Tables)."
