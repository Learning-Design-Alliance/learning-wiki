Phase 1 survey of occupational therapists with entry-to-practice master's degrees (n = 317 respondents). The article prints the frequencies and percentages for the four reflective-practice strategies.

> "The strategies reported in phase 1 as being most used to promote reflective practice included informally talking to colleagues about difficulties encountered in practice (273/303; 90.1%), analyzing one's own critical incidents encountered in practice (145/303; 47.9%) followed by discussion groups about practice (136/303; 44.9%) and mentor support (122/303; 40.3%)."
