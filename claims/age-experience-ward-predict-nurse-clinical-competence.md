## Evidence

### Babaei Keivan 2023

Babaei Keivan, Sadeghian Efat, Khodaveisi Masoud. (2023). The Predictors of Clinical Competence among Hospital Nurses: A Cross-Sectional Study in Hamadan, West Iran. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v33i1.5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 270 nurses in four Hamadan university hospitals using the Nurse Competence Scale; linear regression in SPSS identified the three predictors. The article reports "adjusted R=0.778", i.e. "significantly predicted 77% of the variance of CC".

> "Variables with significant relationship with the total mean score of CC at a level of less than 0.1 were entered into the linear regression model. Results revealed that age, affiliated ward, and work experience significantly predicted 77% of the variance of CC (adjusted R=0.778)."
