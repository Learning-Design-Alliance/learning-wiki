## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Task engagement at T1 significantly predicted enjoyment at T6 (β = .29), supporting reciprocal enjoyment-engagement relations. [→ Chevalère 2023](#chevalere-2023)

## Evidence

### Chevalère 2023

Chevalère, J., Yun, H. S., Henke, A., Pinkwart, N., Hafner, V., & Lazarides, R. (2023). A sequence of learning processes in an intelligent tutoring system from topic-related appraisals to learning gains. Learning and Instruction, 87, 101799. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101799

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Reverse path model inverting temporal precedence (learning-related measures at T1 predicting emotions at T6, N = 140); enjoyment at T6 then predicted final performance (β = .26, p < .001), while boredom at T6 did not (p = .53).

> "Students’ initial task engagement (T1) significantly predicted their later enjoyment after sixty minutes (T6), β = .29, SE = .09, t = 3.28, p = .001 [.12; .46]."
