Notarnicola Angela, Maccagnano Giuseppe, Pesce Vito, Pierro Silvia Di, Tafuri Silvio, Moretti Biagio. (2014). Effect of teaching with or without mirror on balance in young female ballet students. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/1756-0500-7-426

Repeated measures ANOVA in the randomized two-group study of 64 dancers found a significant time effect on BESS total score between T0 and T1; only the F statistic was printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "The repeated measures ANOVA analysis shows that for BESS total score there is a difference due to the time (F = 3.86; p < 0.05)."
