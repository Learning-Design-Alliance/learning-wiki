## Evidence

### Jitendra 2016

Jitendra, A. K., Harwell, M. R., Karl, S. R., Dupuis, D. N., Simonson, G., Slater, S. C., & Lein, A. E. (2016). Schema-based instruction: The effects of experienced and novice teacher implementers on seventh-grade students' proportional problem solving. Learning and Instruction, 44, 53-64. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2016.03.001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental cluster design comparing 31 experienced (Cohort 1) and 42 novice (Cohort 2) SBI teachers. Fidelity was coded from classroom videos using a 0-3 rubric across seven SBI features, with inter-rater reliability averaging 0.84. The article reports "no statistically significant differences between cohorts on the total score and all individual items."

> "The mean total score across the seven items on the fidelity checklist was 14.06 (SD = 3.05) for Cohort 1 and 14.45 (SD = 3.26) for Cohort 2 out of a possible 21 points (higher scores are consistent with greater fidelity). Results indicated no statistically significant differences between cohorts on the total score and all individual items"
