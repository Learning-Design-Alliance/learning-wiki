# Extraneous Load from verbose, displaced instructions shows mixed effects: it reliably lengthens training time, raises workload only under fixed training, and slows the retention trial in Experiment 1 without hurting retention accuracy

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In Experiment 1, higher Extraneous Load significantly increased training time (η2 = .05) and retention test time (62 to 84 sec, η2 = .10) but not workload or retention error rate. [→ Pharmer 2025](#pharmer-2025)
`q3 i1` In Experiment 2, higher Extraneous Load significantly increased training time (η2 = .05) and workload (η2 = .01) but not retention measures. [→ Pharmer 2025 (2)](#pharmer-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Pharmer 2025

Pharmer, R. L., Wickens, C. D., Plabst, L., Clegg, B. A., Hirshfield, L. M., Lewis, J. E., Nicoly, J. B., Spencer, C. A., & Ortega, F. R. (2025). The Impact of Simple, Brief, and Adaptive Instructions within Virtual Reality Training: Components of Cognitive Load Theory in an Assembly Task. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 repeated-measures ANOVA on retention test time in the 60-participant VR assembly study. The article reports the Extraneous Load effect "increasing the time of the retention trial from 62 to 84 sec"; workload showed no Extraneous Load effect (F(1,58) = 0.46, p = .49).

> "did reveal a significant main effect of Extraneous Load  (F(1,58) = 42.17, p < .001, η2 = .10), increasing the time of the retention trial from 62 to 84 sec, an increase of 22 seconds.."

### Pharmer 2025 (2)

Pharmer, R. L., Wickens, C. D., Plabst, L., Clegg, B. A., Hirshfield, L. M., Lewis, J. E., Nicoly, J. B., Spencer, C. A., & Ortega, F. R. (2025). The Impact of Simple, Brief, and Adaptive Instructions within Virtual Reality Training: Components of Cognitive Load Theory in an Assembly Task. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 NASA-TLX ANOVA in the fixed-training condition with 58 participants. The article reports "a significant main effect of Extraneous Load (F(1,57) = 7.89, p = < .01, η2 = .01)", absent in Experiment 1; retention test time showed no Extraneous Load effect (F(1,57) = 0.04, p = .84).

> "revealed a significant main effect of Intrinsic Load (F(1,57) = 16.82, p < .001, η2 = .03) and a significant main effect of Extraneous Load (F(1,57) = 7.89, p = < .01, η2 = .01)."
