### Yimwilai 2015

Supaporn Yimwilai. (2015). An Integrated Approach to Teaching Literature in an EFL Classroom. English Language Teaching, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n2p14

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Three-way ANOVA with one repeated measure on critical thinking pre/post scores. The article reports the three-way interaction "F = 6.82 p < .011" with η2 = .085, so the researcher analyzed each university individually.

> "the effect of integrated approach on critical thinking skills of students at UP significantly differed from that of students at UB, at the level of .05 (F = 6.82 p < .011). The effect size was .085."
