`q2 i?` Some students supported mandatory peer teaching because teaching is part of the medical profession, while others worried that unmotivated mandatory peer teachers would reduce educational quality for peer learners. [→ George SS 2025](#george-ss-2025)

## Evidence

### George SS 2025

George SS, Bonnevier A, Stenfors T, Kenne E. (2025). Learning to teach by teaching your peers: exploring students’ needs for training in the undergraduate medical education curriculum. BMC Medical Education 25(1):414. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07022-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Results section on desired structure: opinions were mixed among both PTs and PLs about mandatory PAL; some saw value for students who would not otherwise take the role, others feared "decreased educational quality for the PLs".

> "Students were worried that including mandatory teaching for all medical students would lead to decreased educational quality for the PLs. Apprehensions were that PTs would be demotivated, unengaged or that they would teach something that was incorrect."
