## Evidence

### Rahimi Hamed 2025

Rahimi Hamed, Etedali Hooman, Latifhadad Mohammad. (2025). The impact of social well-being on students’ academic motivation and academic achievement: a case study from Iran. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08109-3

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlational study of 261 students at Dezful University of Medical Sciences using Keyes' SWBS and Vallerand's AMS; Spearman's analysis found a "weak, positive, and significant relationship" with social well-being (r = 0.234, per Table 2).

> "The Spearman's correlation coefficient showed that students' academic motivation had a weak, positive, and significant relationship with social well-being and its two components (social coherence and social acceptance)."
