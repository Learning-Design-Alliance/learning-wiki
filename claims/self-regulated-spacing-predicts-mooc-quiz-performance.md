## Evidence

### Paulo F. Carvalho 2020

Paulo F. Carvalho, Faria Sana and Veronica X. Yan. (2020). Self-regulated spacing in a massive open online course is related to better learning. npj Science of Learning 5:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0061-1

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of MOOC log data from 747 course completers, with student and unit as random factors and pretest grade and time spent in unit as covariates. Spacing was defined as the number of sessions per unit; the model showed "spacing was a significant predictor of unit quiz grades, β = 0.10".

> "Results (see Supplementary Table 1) revealed that spacing was a significant predictor of unit quiz grades, β = 0.10, SE = 0.02, t(6593.84) = 6.41, p < 0.001."
