Evidence¶

Mery Luz Vega; Christian Hederich M 2015¶

Mery Luz Vega; Christian Hederich M. (2015). The Impact of a Cooperative Learning Program on the Academic Achievement in Mathematics and Language in Fourth Grade Students and its Relation to Cognitive Style. New Approaches in Educational Research 4(2), 84-90. https://doi.org/10.7821/naer.2015.7.124

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pre/post study of 76 fourth-grade students (33 experimental, 43 control); univariate ANCOVA on math post-test with pretest as covariate found a significant GROUP effect (F=24.4, p<0.001); the model explained "28.3% of post-test variance".

"Significant differences in favor of the experimental group were observed (F= 24,4; p<0,001); this effect might be exclusively attributed to the cooperative learning program."
