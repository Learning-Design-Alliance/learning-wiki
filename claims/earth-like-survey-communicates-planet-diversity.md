Elizabeth J. Tasker, Kana Ishimaru, Nicholas Guttenberg and Julien Foriel. (2020). Earth-Like: An education & outreach tool for exploring the diversity of planets like our own. [doi:10.1017/s1473550419000326](https://doi.org/10.1017/s1473550419000326)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A self-selected questionnaire attached to the Earth-Like website received 72 responses, including a US high school class and an in-service physics teacher class. "The majority of participants selected within the ﬁrst three statements" aligned with project goals; only three people found the site confusing. No effect sizes are reported.
