## Evidence

### Dean William H. FRCOphth 2019

Dean William H. FRCOphth, Murray Neil L. MD, Buchan John C. FRCOphth, Golnik Karl MD, Kim Min J. MSc, Burton Matthew J. PhD. (2019). Ophthalmic Simulated Surgical Competency Assessment Rubric for manual small-incision cataract surgery. Journal of Cataract and Refractive Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcrs.2019.04.010

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Masked reliability study: 8 recorded simulated SICS procedures (4 novice, 4 experienced surgeons) independently graded by 4 expert SICS surgeons using the Sim-OSSCAR. Krippendorff α was calculated separately for each of the 20 steps.

> "Table 2 shows the results for all 20 items in the Sim-OSSCAR, of which 17 exhibited an inter-rater agreement coefficient of Krippendorff α greater than 0.60."
