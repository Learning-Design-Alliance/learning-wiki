The editorial reports an ethnographic study by Esra Alagoz of 22 teenage boys identified as disengaged at school, finding they "engaged in quality argumentation in 81 % of their argumentative exchanges" in WoW chat, in contrast to their school performance.

> "The investigation concludes that in contrast to their performance in school and contrary to expectations based on earlier publications on argumentation, when the subjects chatted in the fantasy multiuser videogame of World of Warfare (WoW) they “engaged in quality argumentation in 81 % of their argumentative exchanges. ”"
