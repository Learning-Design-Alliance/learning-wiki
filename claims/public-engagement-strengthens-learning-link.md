## Evidence

### Karen Masters 2016

Karen Masters, Eun Young Oh, Joe Cox, Brooke Simmons, Chris Lintott, Gary Graham, Anita Greenhill, Kate Holmes. (2016). Science Learning via Participation in Online Citizen Science. Journal of Science Communication (JCOM), Special Issue: Citizen Science, Part II. [doi:10.22323/2.15030207](https://doi.org/10.22323/2.15030207)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regressions run separately for each of the five project sub-samples (e.g. Galaxy Zoo N=475, Snapshot Serengeti N=165), compared against Cox et al. (2015) public engagement rankings. The article reports significant positive correlations in each project and suggests project-level heterogeneity is partially explained by public engagement success, tied to communication via blogs, Twitter and Talk.

> "This reveals that the higher ranked projects in public engagement success are found to have a stronger association between active engagement and project specific science knowledge."
