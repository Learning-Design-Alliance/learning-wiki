## Evidence

### Akçay 2016

Nilüfer Okur Akçay. (2016). Implementation of Cooperative Learning Model in Preschool. Journal of Education and Learning, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v5n3p83

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semi-experimental pretest-posttest study with experimental and control groups: 38 nursery children randomly assigned to a Jigsaw Group (N=20) and Control Group (N=18). The independent t-test showed "significant difference between them (t(36)=6.15; p<0.05)" favoring the jigsaw group. No effect size is printed.

> "It was determined that the arithmetic mean of the posttest results of the experimental group was 7.70 and the standard deviation was 1.62; the arithmetic mean of the control group was 4.43 and the standard deviation was 1.74, and there was significant difference between them (t(36)=6.15; p<0.05)."
