## Evidence

### Lisa A DelSignore 2016

Lisa A DelSignore, Traci A Wolbrink, David Zurakowski, Jeffrey P Burns. (2016). Test-Enhanced E-Learning Strategies in Postgraduate Medical Education: A Randomized Cohort Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6199

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group repeated measures mixed-model ANOVA in the randomized cohort of 49 residents showed significant pre-to-post improvement in all groups (full video F1,46=80.52; logic puzzle F1,46=67.36; short answer F1,46=87.98; all P<.001). Mean posttest scores were 63%, 63%, and 67% respectively.

> "Mixed-model ANOVA showed significant improvement in knowledge gain between pre- and post-test scores in each of the 3 groups during the initial testing session (full video: F1,46=80.52, P<.001; logic puzzle: F1,46=67.36, P<.001; short answer questions: F1,46=87.98, P<.001)."
