q2 · i? · associational · r?

Bias-assessment items administered after the scenario prompts showed very similar distributions between groups; no statistically significant country difference on either overall-opinion prompt (effect sizes 0.0085 and 0.0301, p = 0.88 and 0.59612). Equivalence was not tested.

"As shown in Table4, Mann–Whitney U tests showed no statistically significant difference in responses between the groups for either prompt."

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Two-way ANOVA with factors Country and Year of Study across the full dataset ruled out academic progress as an explanation for the cross-country differences; Korean students were mostly in third and fourth year while Canadian students were mostly in their first two years.

"AsshowninTable3,theacademicprogresswasnotasignificantfactorforeitherjudgmentoutcome (“Acted unethically” p = 0.4891 or “Against the rules” p = 0.4406), with very little interaction effect (p > 0.99 in both categories)."
