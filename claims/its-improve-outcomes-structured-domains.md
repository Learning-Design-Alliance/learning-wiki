The review reports, citing Holmes et al. and Woolf, that ITS can improve learning outcomes. The paper describes ITS as simulating human tutoring via automated feedback, guidance, and assessment, noting particular relevance for adult self-directed and distance learners. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Research suggests that ITS can improve learning outcomes, especially in domains that require structured knowledge acquisition, such as language learning or technical skills (Holmes et al., 2022; Woolf, 2021)."
