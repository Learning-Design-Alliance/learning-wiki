### Rossettini G 2024

Rossettini G, Rodeghiero L, Corradi F, Cook C, Pillastrini P, Turolla A, Castellini G, Chiappinotto S, Gianola S, Palese A. (2024). Comparative accuracy of ChatGPT-4, Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini in the Italian entrance test for healthcare sciences degrees: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05630-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational cross-sectional study (September 2023) in which all "820 multiple-choice questions" from past CINECA tests were entered into three chatbots and scored correct or incorrect; a chi-square test found "a statistically significant difference in accuracy" (p < 0.001). No effect size was reported.

> "Overall, we found a statistically significant difference in accuracy between the answers of the three chatbots (p < 0.001)."
