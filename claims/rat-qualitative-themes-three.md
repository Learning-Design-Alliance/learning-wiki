Punja R, Punja D, Prasad Kamachi Christdoss Ruben. (2026). Large-group virtual anatomy via a smart digital podium: a preliminary cross-sectional study of student perspectives toward remote anatomage teaching in a medical lecture hall. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10019-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Reflexive thematic analysis following Braun and Clarke's six-phase framework on 30 substantive responses from 87 non-blank entries. The second theme reflected "pragmatic advocacy" for combining modalities, and the third was "projection visibility" raised by 2/94 students (2.1%).

> "Three recurring themes emerged with full consensus between the two investigators. The first theme—visual engagement and retention—was most prominent: students described RAT as ‘more interactive,’ superior for ‘visual retention,’ and explicitly helpful in making structures ‘visible’ and ‘easier to understand.’"
