## Evidence

### Brogaard L 2022

Brogaard L, Glerup Lauridsen K, Løfgren B, et al. (2022). The effects of obstetric emergency team training on patient outcome: A systematic review and meta‐analysis. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14263

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of five studies reporting Apgar scores less than 7 at 5 minutes. The observational trials involved "considerable heterogeneity (I 2 = 83%)"; GRADE certainty was moderate, downgraded one level for inconsistency.

> "Apgar scores less than 7 at 5 min were reported in two RCTs 15 , 16 with a combined OR of 0.87 (95% CI 0.72–1.05) (I 2 = 13%) and in three observational trials 19 , 20 , 21 with a combined OR of 0.77 (95% CI 0.51–1.19)."
