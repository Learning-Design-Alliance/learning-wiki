## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q2 · i3` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study (Mtiraoui et al. 2025) of a 6-hour immersive RAPID-PFA workshop based on international PFA guidelines, with lectures, small-group practice, role-play, and vignette debriefing, in 108 third-year nursing students (54 per group). The review reports "d ≈ 0.97" for perceived competencies and disaster preparedness and "d ≈ 0.86" for self-efficacy.

> "Intervention group improved significantly vs control in: perceived competencies (overall score ↑ from ∼64.5 to ∼118–123 across follow‐ups; p < 10− 3; d ≈ 0.97), disaster preparedness (overall ↑ from ∼2.4 to ∼4.2–4.3; p < 10− 3; d ≈ 0.97), helping capacity/confidence (more “very confident/confident”; p < 10− 3), and self‐efficacy (↑ from ∼21.9 to ∼29.8–30.9; p < 10− 3; d ≈ 0.86). Effects maintained at 1 and 3 months; no major drop between T2 and T3 for several outcomes."
