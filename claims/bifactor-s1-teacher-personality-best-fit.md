# Bifactor-(S-1) models of teacher Big Five personality fit student and teacher data better than single-domain or three-facets models

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across all five Big Five domains, bifactor-(S-1) models showed better fit (lower AIC) than three-facets models, which in turn fit better than single-domain models. [→ Burić 2023](#buric-2023)

## Evidence

### Burić 2023

Burić, Irena, Butković, Ana and Kim, Lisa (2023) Teacher personality domains and facets: Their unique associations with student-ratings of teaching quality and teacher enthusiasm. Learning and Instruction. 101827. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101827

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model comparison of 15 predictive models (single-domain, three-facets, bifactor-(S-1)) fitted with doubly latent MSEM in Mplus 8.8; the article reports ΔAIC values substantially greater than 2 favoring bifactor-(S-1) models, concluding the "bifactor-(S-1) representation of teacher personality is supported by the data to the greatest extent".

> "These results suggest that the bifactor-(S-1) representation of teacher personality is supported by the data to the greatest extent."
