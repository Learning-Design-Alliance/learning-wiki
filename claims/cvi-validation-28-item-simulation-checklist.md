`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validity assessment by five experts using a 1-5 Likert scale on appearance, clarity and relevance, with 0.80 as the acceptable CVI minimum. The article reports that "two showed an CVI of 60% (0.6)" and were reformulated, so the domain "ended up with 14 items".

> "In the scenario in action domain, of the 16 items analyzed, two showed an CVI of 60% (0.6) in the appearance, clarity and relevance criteria, while the others showed an CVI of 80% (0.8)."

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Checklist finalization after expert review: the overall assessment domain was removed and "The instrument ended up with 28 items", comprising briefing, scenario in action, and debriefing stages with actions marked positive, negative, or partially.

> "The fourth domain, referring to overall assessment (4 items), was removed from this version. The instrument ended up with 28 items. This version was used by the experts during the simulation validity."
