# Individual differences are a necessary condition for the power law artifact: with a single parameter value the artifact fails to emerge

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Myung et al. 2000

In Jae Myung, Cheongtag Kim, and Mark A. Pitt. (2000). Toward an explanation of the power law artifact: Insights from response surface analysis. Memory & Cognition, 28(5), 832-840. https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03198418

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Simulation paralleling the Table 1 procedure but generating the 10 forgetting curves from a single parameter value per model (0 = 0.24 power; 0 = 0.11 exponential), testing whether individual differences are required for the artifact. The authors report that "An artifact failed to emerge".

> "An artifact failed to emerge: The power and exponential models fitted their own data best 95% and 99% of the time, respectively."
