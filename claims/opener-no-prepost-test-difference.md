# The Opener produced no difference on pre/post test scores across the whole project

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no statistically significant difference on pre/post tests between students who had an Opener and those who did not. [→ Zertuche 2012](#zertuche-2012)

## Evidence

### Zertuche 2012

Zertuche, A., Gerard, L., & Linn, M. C. (2012). How do Openers Contribute to Student Learning? International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(1), 79-92. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070484

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post tests administered before and 1-14 days after the week-long WISE project, scored with KI rubrics. The article reports no significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "there was no difference on the pre/post tests between students who had an Opener ( M = 1.74, SD = 2.50) and those who did not ( M = 1.55, SD = 2.16), t(255) = -.511, p = .61."
