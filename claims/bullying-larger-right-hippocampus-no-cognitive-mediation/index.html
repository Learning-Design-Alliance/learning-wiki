Evidence¶

Menken Miriam S. 2023¶

Menken Miriam S., Rodriguez Rivera Pedro J., Isaiah Amal, Ernst Thomas, Cloak Christine C., Chang Linda. (2023). Longitudinal alterations in brain morphometry mediated the effects of bullying victimization on cognitive development in preadolescents. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2023.101247

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

LMM analysis of FreeSurfer-segmented subcortical volumes in the matched ABCD sample over two visits. The article reports "larger right hippocampal volumes" in bullied children with d = 0.52, and no other subcortical group differences.

"Bullied children had larger right hippocampal volumes than non-bullied children (d=0.52, [95% CI, 0.03–1.01]; P = 0.036) (Fig. 3A, Table S7), but not in any other subcortical brain volumes (P = 0.09–0.96; Table S7)."

Menken Miriam S. 2023 (2)¶

Menken Miriam S., Rodriguez Rivera Pedro J., Isaiah Amal, Ernst Thomas, Cloak Christine C., Chang Linda. (2023). Longitudinal alterations in brain morphometry mediated the effects of bullying victimization on cognitive development in preadolescents. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2023.101247

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Mediation and association analyses testing whether hippocampal volume related to cognition in the same sample. The article reports no impact on Oral Reading Recognition, Processing Speed, or Flanker scores, and the authors state it did not act as a mediator.

"Despite relatively larger right hippocampal volumes in the bullied children, it had no impact on any of the three cognition scores (Oral Reading Recognition: P = 0.43; Pattern Comparison Processing Speed: P = 0.71; Flanker Inhibitory Control and Attention: P = 0.09)"
