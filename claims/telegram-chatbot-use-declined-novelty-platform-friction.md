## Evidence

### Hilliger 2026

Hilliger, I., Pérez-Sanagustín, M., González, C., Villalobos, E. and Celis, S. (2026). A GAI-Based Chatbot for Scaffolding Self-Regulated Learning: Insights from a Design-Based Research Approach. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1), 74–88. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9143

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Cognitive walkthroughs with 12 undergraduates in Education and Society at U1, analyzed thematically. One student reported using it only "the first two weeks that it was presented to us" before stopping. Engaged users found it helpful for administrative tasks like checking dates and readings.

> "its use declined over time. This drop was attributed to several factors, including the perception that Telegram, the platform hosting the chatbot, was not widely used among students"
