## Evidence

### Goodridge 2017

Goodridge, W. H., Lawanto, O., & Santoso, H. B. (2017). A Learning Style Comparison between Synchronous Online and Face-to-Face Engineering Graphics Instruction. International Education Studies, 10(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n2p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A quasi-experimental comparison of six sections of an engineering graphics course (30 synchronous online, 78 face-to-face) taught by the same instructor with identical curricula. Mann-Whitney tests showed "significantly higher score on their final grades" for synchronous online Convergers, with Z = -2.324; p < .05. No effect size was printed.

> "Converger students in the synchronous online group showed a significantly higher score on their final grades than the Converger students in the face-to-face group (Z = -2.324; p < .05)."
