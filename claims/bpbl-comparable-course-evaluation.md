## Evidence

### Servos Ulrike 2022

Servos Ulrike, Reiß Birger, Stosch Christoph, Karay Yassin, Matthes Jan. (2022). A simple approach of applying blended learning to problem-based learning is feasible, accepted and does not affect evaluation and exam results—a just pre-pandemic randomised controlled mixed-method study. Naunyn-Schmiedeberg's Archives of Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00210-022-02306-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire data collected at the final exam from the randomised main study, with three scales derived by varimax factor analysis; Fig. 2 reports n = 179–188 (cPbL) and n = 75–78 (bPbL). Likert scales ran from 1 (= true) to 5 (= not true), and "we found no difference between students having attended bPbL or cPbL groups".

> "In the randomised main study, however, we found no difference between students having attended bPbL or cPbL groups with respect to the evaluation of CbL scepticism, familiarity with the Internet platform ILIAS, and satisfaction with the course"
