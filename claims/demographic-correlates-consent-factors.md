## Evidence

### Warren Li 2022

Warren Li, Kaiwen Sun, Florian Schaub, Christopher Brooks. (2022). Disparities in Students' Propensity to Consent to Learning Analytics. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 32:564–608. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00254-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three OLS models on 119 survey completers regressed each key factor on demographics; "Black students are less trusting of the institution when compared to White students" and female students show more collection concern and greater instructor-data-use comfort (instructor comfort: p = 0.034).

> "Black students are less trusting of the institution when compared to White students. Female students also correlate with having more data collection concerns, as measured by the IUIPC collection subscale, while having greater comfort with instructor use of data for course purposes."
