The Illusion of Competence: Self-Perceived Digital Literacy and AI Readiness Among European Secondary Students. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26010 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.26010](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.26010)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Intra-pathway analysis using independent t-tests with Welch's correction compared male-female self-efficacy within each elective field; only the Technology pathway (n=111) showed a significant gap, the authors call "statistically significant".

> "However, within the Technology-oriented pathway (𝑛= 111), a statistically significant gender gap emerged (𝑝= 0.046), as illustrated in Figure 3, with male students reporting disproportionately higher self-efficacy than their female classmates."
