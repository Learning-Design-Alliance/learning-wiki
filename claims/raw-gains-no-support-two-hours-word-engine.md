## Evidence

### McLean 2013

McLean, S., Hogg, N., & Rush, T. W. (2013). Vocabulary learning through an online computerized flashcard site. The JALT CALL Journal, 9(1), 79-98. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1108008

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Discussion of unadjusted pre-post gains from the year-long quasi-experiment (Table 3: vocabulary+ER gain 1147.54, vocabulary gain 1107.81). The article states the raw gains "failed to provide support" for the two-hour advantage; no effect size is printed.

> "the raw mean VST gain scores failed to provide support for the claim that two hours of Word Engine led to greater VST score increases than one hour, since the vocabulary and ER group actually achieved slightly higher VST score gains, as shown in Table 3."
