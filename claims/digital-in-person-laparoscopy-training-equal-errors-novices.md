## Evidence

### Geissler Mark Enrik 2024

Geissler Mark Enrik, Bereuter Jean-Paul, Geissler Rona Berit, Bökkerink Guus Mattheus Johannes, Egen Luisa, Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Haney Caelan. (2024). Comparison of distance versus in-person laparoscopy training using a low-cost laparoscopy simulator—a randomized controlled multi-center trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11069-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 40 laparoscopic novices randomized to digital (n=19) or in-person (n=21) training on a standardized six-session curriculum, assessed on the ForceSense system. The study reports no significant difference in mean overall errors, with "control: 3.48 vs. intervention: 3.37, p value = 0.86".

> "Overall, there were no significant differences for the mean occurrence of errors between both groups (control: 3.48 vs. intervention: 3.37, p value = 0.86) (Fig. 3A)."
