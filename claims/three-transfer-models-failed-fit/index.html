Evidence¶

Donath 2025¶

Donath, J. L., Lüke, T., Tran, U. S., Botes, E., & Goetz, T. (2025). The Transfer of Teacher Training to Inclusive Classroom Practice: A Meta-Analytic SEM Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10081-9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Model-comparison results from the MASEM analyses (main, same-database k = 163, and RCT/quasi-experimental k = 58 subsets). The article states the three models "appeared to be not well-suited" and that Knowledge-Skills-Beliefs fit reasonably only in the RCT subset.

"the Knowledge-to-Practice model, the Reaction-to-Training model, and the Practice-to-Belief model all appeared to be not well-suited to explain the available empirical data on the transfer process."
