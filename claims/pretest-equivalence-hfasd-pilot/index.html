Roux et al. 2015¶

Catherine Roux, Eric Dion, Anne Barrette. (2015). Enhancing Reading Comprehension Among Students With High-Functioning Autism Spectrum Disorder: A Randomized Pilot Study. Canadian Journal of Education 38:1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057963

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive report of pre-test scores in the randomized pilot (N = 13); the article notes "interven- tion students had slightly higher main idea identification and anaphoric relations scores", an apparent initial advantage addressed by computing difference scores.

"Students from both conditions had similar pre-test knowledge of definitions and compre- hension scores. However, in comparison to their peers in the control condition, interven- tion students had slightly higher main idea identification and anaphoric relations scores."
