## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When a resident takes the floor and the PD repeatedly mitigates the raised issue, the conversation becomes a pseudo-dialogue in which the PDs take over the agenda. [→ Duitsman 2019](#duitsman-2019)

## Evidence

### Duitsman 2019

Duitsman, M. E., van Braak, M., Stommel, W., ten Kate-Booij, M., de Graaf, J., Fluit, C. R. M. G., & Jaarsma, D. A. D. C. (2019). Using conversation analysis to explore feedback on resident performance. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-019-09887-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Conversation-analytic case analysis of Extract 4, in which both PDs challenged and invalidated a resident's raised insecurity. The article concludes that "the result is a pseudo-dialogue" when repeated mitigations follow the resident taking the floor, with the questions having merely made the conversation appear dialogic.

> "If taking the floor is followed by repeated mitigations by the PD(s), then the result is a pseudo-dialogue (Skovholt 2018)."
