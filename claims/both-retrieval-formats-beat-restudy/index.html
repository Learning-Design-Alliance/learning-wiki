Evidence¶

Michelle L. Rivers 2025¶

Michelle L. Rivers, Paige E. Northern, Sarah K. Tauber. (2025). Does Retrieval Demand Moderate the Effectiveness of Covert Retrieval Practice? Comparing Covert and Overt Retrieval Practice for Learning Key Terms and Definitions. Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:61. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10040-4

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Experiment 1 showed "a standard testing effect" for definitions: overt retrieval (M = 0.50) exceeded restudy (M = 0.35), printed effect size gav = 0.81. The general discussion reports testing-effect sizes of g = 0.63–0.91 for overt versus restudy across experiments.

"For those who practiced definitions, we observed a standard testing effect, with recall being higher following overt retrieval (M = 0.50, SE = 0.03) compared to restudy (M = 0.35, SE = 0.02), t(51) = 6.38, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.10, 0.19], gav = 0.81."

Michelle L. Rivers 2025 (2)¶

Michelle L. Rivers, Paige E. Northern, Sarah K. Tauber. (2025). Does Retrieval Demand Moderate the Effectiveness of Covert Retrieval Practice? Comparing Covert and Overt Retrieval Practice for Learning Key Terms and Definitions. Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:61. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10040-4

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

In Experiment 1, covert retrieval of definitions also beat restudy with printed effect size gav = 0.31; across experiments the covert-versus-restudy testing effect ranged from g = 0.16–0.54 when practice and test matched.

"Further, recall was higher following covert retrieval compared to restudy, t(51) = 2.52, p = 0.015, 95% CI [0.01, 0.10], gav = 0.31."
