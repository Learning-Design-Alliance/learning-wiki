## Evidence

### Lundegren N 2021

Lundegren N, Jönsson A, Lindberg P. (2021). An upgrade of the Malmö model by implementing case‐based teaching and learning, in an undergraduate dental education. European Journal of Dental Education, 25(4), 649-656. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.12642

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational report from the dental programme's own course evaluations after the 2018 transition from PBL to case-based teaching with flipped classroom seminars; the authors call these tentative findings, with no effect sizes or test statistics reported.

> "After we introduced the flipped classroom approach into seminars, we noticed higher levels of activity, engagement and attendance amongst the students, and for the first time in many years according to the course evaluations, the students considered the seminars to be very good learning activities."
