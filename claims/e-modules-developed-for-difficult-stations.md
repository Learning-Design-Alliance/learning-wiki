### Kwant Kelly J. 2015

Kwant Kelly J., Custers Eugene J. F. M., Jongen-Hermus Femke J., Kluijtmans Manon. (2015). Preparation by mandatory E-modules improves learning of practical skills: a quasi-experimental comparison of skill examination results. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0376-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison reported from Table 3 data: 2011-2012 scores were lower on stations later given e-modules than on stations without them. The article calls this "an accidental finding" with no faculty policy behind it, and no test statistics are printed.

> "In 2011–2012 students obtained lower scores on the OSCE stations for which e-module preparation was developed in 2012–2013 than on the stations for which no e-modules were yet developed in 2012–2013 (Table 3)."
