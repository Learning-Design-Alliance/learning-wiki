## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Instructor and student interviews identified recurring student difficulties in advanced electrodynamics, including remembering and translating the four Maxwell equations, interpreting conservation laws, deriving boundary conditions, and unpacking vectors in plane waves. [→ Baily 2013](#baily-2013)

## Evidence

### Baily 2013

Baily, C., Dubson, M., & Pollock, S. (2013). Developing Tutorials for Advanced Physics Students: Processes and Lessons Learned. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.003](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.003)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative theme synthesis from one-hour interviews with six instructors who had recently taught E&M 2 at CU, supplemented by discussions with nine outside faculty. The authors report that "Students had difficulty remembering the four Maxwell equations" and interpreting conservation laws; no prevalence data were collected.

> "Students had difficulty remembering the four Maxwell equations, in either differential or integral form (and with translating between the two representations). They had trouble physically interpreting mathematical expressions of conservation laws, such as the continuity equation."
