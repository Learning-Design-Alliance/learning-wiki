## Evidence

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single self-assessment item (insufficient to high) on the week 1 and week 12 surveys in the flipped classroom cohort, where "82.4% of students assessed their SL skill competency as moderate or lower"; by semester end 47.4% rated good or high and none rated insufficient.

> "Initially, 82.4% of students assessed their SL skill competency as moderate or lower and only 17.6% of students assessed their skills as good or high."

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025 (2)

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post survey item on confidence interpreting graphs (P = 0.108 in Table 1); the discussion notes this perception result was not in alignment with practical graphical data interpretation skill, which increased by 17.7%.

> "The one skill that students did not report increased confidence in their capabilities during the semester was the interpretation of results presented in graphical form (P > 0.05)."
