Evidence¶

Christina Clark Tuttle 2013¶

Christina Clark Tuttle, Brian Gill, Philip Gleason, Virginia Knechtel, Ira Nichols-Barrer, Alexandra Resch. (2013). KIPP Middle Schools: Impacts on Achievement and Other Outcomes. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/kipp-middle-schools-impacts-on-achievement-and-other-outcomes-full-report

q4 · i? · causal · r?

Randomized experimental analysis relying on KIPP admissions lotteries to identify comparison students; the lottery design "account[s] for students’ prior achievement, as well as factors such as student and parent motivation." No effect size is printed on this page.

"One of the report’s analyses confirms the positive impacts using a rigorous randomized experimental analysis that relies on the schools’ admissions lotteries to identify comparison students, thereby accounting for students’ prior achievement, as well as factors such as student and parent motivation."
