## Evidence

### van de Steeg L 2015

van de Steeg L, IJkema R, Wagner C, Langelaan M. (2015). The effect of an e-learning course on nursing staff's knowledge of delirium: a before-and-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0289-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Before-and-after study without a control group in 17 Dutch hospitals; multivariable multilevel linear regression adjusted for repeated measures and demographics. Final mean was 87.4 (95% CI 86.7 to 88.2, n = 618) versus baseline 79.3; the article reports "an effect size of 0.8" (Cohen's d).

> "On average, final test scores were 8.1 points higher than baseline test scores (95% CI 7.4 to 8.8, p <0.01), resulting in an effect size of 0.8."
