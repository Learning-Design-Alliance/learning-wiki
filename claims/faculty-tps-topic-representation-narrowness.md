### Rica Sirbaugh French and Edward E. Prather 2020

Rica Sirbaugh French and Edward E. Prather. (2020). From A Systematic Investigation of Faculty-Produced Think-Pair-Share Questions to Frameworks for Characterizing and Developing Fluency-Inspiring Activities. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020138](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020138)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Coding of the 'stars' topical-area questions within the 353-question corpus found "a considerable over-reliance on the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram"; the same questions show an overwhelming preference for the overarching task compare.

> "the 67 questions in the “stars” topical area show a considerable over-reliance on the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram. This diagram, and the words that frame the question and context of the problem, are frequently the only representations used."
