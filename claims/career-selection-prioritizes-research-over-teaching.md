A theoretical-argument element of the model (Selection: The Tenure-Track Job Market). The authors reason that indirect selection may occur if research productivity correlates with lecturing, and that for tenure "there is no positive selection to promote instructors who use student-centered teaching practices".

> "Tenure-track hiring processes at research-focused institutes place a greater emphasis on research products than teaching experience"
