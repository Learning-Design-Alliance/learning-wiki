### Zheng Hui 2026

Zheng Hui, Ouyang Yun, Zeng Yifei, He Xinyu, Song Liping, Yu Xiangyuan. (2026). Systematic evaluation of the teaching effectiveness of flipped classroom mode in undergraduate preventive medicine courses in China. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1896815

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 8 studies comparing collaborative communication skills; substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 70.5%, p = 0.001) led to a random-effects model. Begg’s test found no significant publication bias (P = 0.063). The article prints no standardized effect size for this finding.

> "the cooperative communication ability of students in the FC group was significantly higher than that in the LBL group, with a pooled RR of 1.60 (95% CI: 1.28–2.01)"
