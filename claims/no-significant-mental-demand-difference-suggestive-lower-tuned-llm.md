### Kumar 2025

Kumar, H., Reza, M., Mitchell, J., Musabirov, I., Zhang, L., & Liut, M. (2025). Understanding Help-Seeking Behavior of Students Using LLMs vs. Web Search for Writing SQL Queries. Preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3735091.3737569](https://doi.org/10.1145/3735091.3737569)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mental demand was self-reported using the NASA-TLX Mental Demand subscale (1-10) in the randomized interview study. The test was not significant, but "there is suggestive evidence that students in the Instructor-tuned LLM conditions reported that the task was less mentally demanding compared to the other conditions."

> "The Kruskal-Wallis H test for mental demands was not significant (𝜒 2(2) = 2.05, 𝑑 𝑓 = 2, 𝑝 = 0.36). However, there is suggestive evidence that students in the Instructor-tuned LLM conditions reported that the task was less mentally demanding compared to the other conditions"
