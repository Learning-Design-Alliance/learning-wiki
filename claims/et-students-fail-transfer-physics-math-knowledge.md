### Djafar K. Mynbaev 2008

Djafar K. Mynbaev, Candido Cabo, Roman Ya. Kezerashvili, Janet Liou-Mark. (2008). Support of Study on Engineering Technology from Physics and Mathematics. https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.1950 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0807.1950](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0807.1950)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Case-study description by faculty at City Tech, an urban minority-serving career-oriented college, identifying the knowledge-transfer problem their project addresses. The authors state students "do not always transfer the knowledge they acquired in physics and mathematics to their engineering-technology classes." No outcome data are reported for this observation.

> "One of the problems in engineering technology education is that the students do not correlate closely engineering and science subjects and do not always transfer the knowledge they acquired in physics and mathematics to their engineering-technology classes."
