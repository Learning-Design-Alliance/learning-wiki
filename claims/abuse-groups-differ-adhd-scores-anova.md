# Abuse typology groups differed significantly on overall ADHD symptom scores, with only the non-abused group differing significantly from the three abuse groups in post hoc analysis

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A one-way ANOVA found significant differences between abuse groups on ADHD scores, F(2, 2,939)=40.07, p<0.001; post hoc tests showed only the non-abused group differed significantly from the three abuse groups. [→ Sanderud K. 2016](#sanderud-k-2016)

## Evidence

### Sanderud K. 2016

Sanderud K., Murphy S., Elklit A. (2016). Child maltreatment and ADHD symptoms in a sample of young adults. European Journal of Psychotraumatology. https://doi.org/10.3402/ejpt.v7.32061

`q3 · i?`

One-way ANOVA on abuse typologies and overall ADHD score in the national survey. The overall abuse group scored M=16.87 (SD=4.30), emotional abuse M=16.01, sexual abuse M=15.30, and the non-abused group "much lower levels of ADHD (M=13.81, SD=3.72)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Post hoc analysis indicated that only the non-abused group differed significantly from the three abuse groups with much lower levels of ADHD (M=13.81, SD=3.72)."
