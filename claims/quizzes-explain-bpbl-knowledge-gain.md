`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation offered in the discussion for the bPBL knowledge-gain result, citing prior evidence on quiz efficacy and on examination-oriented learning cultures in East Asian societies. This is an explanation, not a tested result of this study; the article adds that further research is required on how examination-centred educators perceive online quizzes.
