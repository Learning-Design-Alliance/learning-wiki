# Nurses who rate their genomics understanding as excellent/good score significantly higher on the Knowledge Score than those rating it poor

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Self-reported understanding of general genomics/genetics was significantly associated with Knowledge Score (p = .005, mean difference 0.68), with the excellent/good group scoring higher. [→ Laaksonen M 2025](#laaksonen-m-2025)
`q2 i?` Understanding of the genetics of common health challenges showed the same pattern (p = .001, mean difference 0.63). [→ Laaksonen M 2025 (2)](#laaksonen-m-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Laaksonen M 2025

Laaksonen M, Paavilainen E, Koivisto A-M, Halkoaho A. (2025). Genomic competence among nurses: A spotlight on ethics. Nursing Ethics, 32(8). https://doi.org/10.1177/09697330251366594

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

T-test comparing self-rated understanding groups on Knowledge Score in the survey of 234 nurses; the poor group (n = 171) averaged 8.98 versus 9.66 for the excellent/good group (n = 44), a "mean difference 0.68" at p = .005.

> "When a two-level classification (excellent/good – poor) of self-reported understanding of general genomics/genetics is compared to the KS, there is a statistically significant difference (p = .005) in means between the groups, according to the T-test (mean difference 0.68)."

### Laaksonen M 2025 (2)

Laaksonen M, Paavilainen E, Koivisto A-M, Halkoaho A. (2025). Genomic competence among nurses: A spotlight on ethics. Nursing Ethics, 32(8). https://doi.org/10.1177/09697330251366594

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Second T-test in the same survey: nurses rating their understanding of the genetics of common health challenges as poor scored 8.83 versus 9.46 for the good group, a "mean difference 0.63" at p = .001.

> "In addition, understanding of the genetics of common health challenges revealed a similar pattern (p = .001, mean difference 0.63). Those who perceived their understanding as poor had a lower overall KS on average (n = 120, mean score 8.83) compared to those who perceived their understanding as good (n = 95, mean score 9.46)."
