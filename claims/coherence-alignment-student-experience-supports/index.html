Evidence¶

Faye Kroshinsky 2026¶

Faye Kroshinsky, Mary Ann Pitcher, Jackson Overton-Clark, Shanette Porter. (2026). Supports for Centering Student Experience. UChicago Consortium Field Scan. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/supports-for-centering-student-experience

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Field scan interviews with 27 stakeholders; all participants spoke about the need for coherence and alignment from their vantage points supporting schools, with external partners focusing on school-level priorities and district staff on cross-office alignment.

"Across all participants, a desire for better coordination, communication, and coherence across professional learning and supports were identified as needed to create a more coherent, viable, and effective experience for educators leading the work in schools."

Faye Kroshinsky 2026 (2)¶

Faye Kroshinsky, Mary Ann Pitcher, Jackson Overton-Clark, Shanette Porter. (2026). Supports for Centering Student Experience. UChicago Consortium Field Scan. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/supports-for-centering-student-experience

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

District staff participants reported that principals "experience a disconnect between our various departments and initiatives in the district," making efforts like Cultivate feel like 'another thing' on school leaders' plates.

"District staff participants overwhelmingly identified a need to build greater coherence and partnership between central offices and departments."
