Evidence¶

Hardy 2025¶

Hardy, J. E., & Rodrigo, V. (2025). Incorporating Extensive Reading into the Foreign Language Curriculum. Dimensions, 60. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1466517

q2 · i? · review · r1

The review reports, citing Elley (1991), Nuttall (2005), Suk (2017), and Yamashita (2008), that ER improves comprehension, facilitating skill development and deeper understanding and interpretation of texts. No effect sizes are printed.

"Extensive reading has been shown to significantly improve reading comprehension by affording learners regular exposure to reading in a low-stress environment"

Hardy 2025 (2)¶

Hardy, J. E., & Rodrigo, V. (2025). Incorporating Extensive Reading into the Foreign Language Curriculum. Dimensions, 60. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1466517

q2 · i? · review · r1

The review attributes fluency gains to cited work by Grabe (2009) and Taguchi et al. (2004), and notes Grabe and Stoller (2019) analyzed how ER contributes to decoding skills and overall comprehension in L1 and L2 contexts. No effect sizes are printed.

"In terms of fluency, extensive reading significantly improves reading fluency by increasing reading speed and accuracy, by promoting automaticity in word recognition and processing (Grabe, 2009; Taguchi et al., 2004)."
