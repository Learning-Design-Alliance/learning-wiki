## Evidence

### Keller 2020

Keller, L., Preckel, F., & Brunner, M. (2020). Nonlinear Relations Between Achievement and Academic Self-Concepts in Elementary and Secondary School: An Integrative Data Analysis Across 13 Countries. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000533

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interrupted regressions with the two-lines test on representative TIMSS Grade 8 mathematics data from eight countries across two cycles. The printed coefficient for higher achievers, "mean β2 = 0.58", exceeded that for lower achievers; in about half of countries both segments were positive, in the other half β1 was close to zero while β2 was positive.

> "For students in Grade 8, the relation between achievement and self-concept in mathematics was stronger for higher achieving students than for lower achieving students (mean β1 = 0.25, 95% CI [0.08, 0.42]; mean β2 = 0.58, 95% CI [0.50, 0.66]; Figure 1b)."
