Evidence¶

Victoria Abramenka-Lachheb 2025¶

Victoria Abramenka-Lachheb, Gamze Ozogul. (2025). Interaction analysis of learning objects in online courses: What are their interactive characteristics and design intent behind them? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10556-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic analysis of interview data (RQ3): interactions served to let learners "build prerequisite knowledge prior to engaging in such authentic learning tasks," with one participant noting interactive areas made learners feel more encouraged to continue reading and researching.

"Eleven participants used interactive elements (e.g., quizzes, pop-up windows) to highlight key content and key concepts required for learners to know prior to engaging in more complex tasks."

Victoria Abramenka-Lachheb 2025 (2)¶

Victoria Abramenka-Lachheb, Gamze Ozogul. (2025). Interaction analysis of learning objects in online courses: What are their interactive characteristics and design intent behind them? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10556-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Second RQ3 theme from the same interview corpus: participants fostered engagement through (1) choice to navigate content, (2) use of visuals, and (3) tools enhancing learning such as virtual reality; branching scenarios required decisions with consequences.

"Ten participants specifically discussed engagement as the purpose of making learning content and activities dynamic and interactive."
