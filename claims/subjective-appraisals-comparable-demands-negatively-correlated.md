## Evidence

### Flegr 2023

Flegr, S., Kuhn, J., & Scheiter, K. (2023). How to foster STEM learning during Covid-19 remote schooling: Combining virtual and video experiments. Learning and Instruction, 86, 101778. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101778

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory ANOVAs (RQ3, Table 3) on six subjective-appraisal measures showed no condition differences. Perceived cognitive demands correlated with post-test scores at rs(139) = −.53, p < .0001 and with follow-up at rs(66) = −.35; most other correlations were not significant at follow-up.

> "We furthermore conducted exploratory ANOVAs with students' subjective appraisals as dependent variables for RQ3; they showed no difference between conditions (see Table 3). Accordingly, students from the three experimental conditions perceived their learning in similar ways."
