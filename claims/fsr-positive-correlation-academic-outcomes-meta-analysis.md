## Evidence

### Fathi 2026

Fathi, Z., Zarei, P., Fong, C. J., Saha, A. K., & Sharmeen, S. (2026). The Caring Professor: A Meta-Analysis of Associations between Faculty-Student Relationships and Postsecondary Student Success. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10100-9

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 128 effect sizes from 36 studies (39 samples) using inverse-variance weighted random-effects models with robust variance estimation. The pooled correlation was "r =.18, 95% CI [0.14, 0.23]", significant at p < .001.

> "The pooled correlation between FSR and the combined academic outcome was r =.18, 95% CI [0.14, 0.23], t(127) = 8.26, p <.001, indicating a small but statistically significant positive association."
