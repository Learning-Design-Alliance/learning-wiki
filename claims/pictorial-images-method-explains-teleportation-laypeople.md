`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical/argumentative claim from the introduction: the paper's stated objective is to let physicists explain teleportation to the general public. The author states the approach "relies almost entirely on “pictures”" rather than the usual equations, since mathematics scares non-scientists away.

> "it uses only the simplest mathematics (one only needs to accept that certain objects are either 0 or 1) and relies almost entirely on “pictures”, allowing the layperson to visualize – and thus, understand – what is happening."
