### Hyoungwook Jin 2024

Hyoungwook Jin, Seonghee Lee, Hyungyu Shin, and Juho Kim. 2024. Teach AI How to Code: Using Large Language Models as Teachable Agents for Programming Education. Proceedings of the CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3642349

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects user study in which 40 algorithm novices taught binary search with either full TeachYou or a baseline without Mode-shifting and Teaching Helper. The article reports Mode-shifting 'improved the density of knowledge-building messages in the conversations' significantly, p = 0.03, Cohen's d = 0.71.
