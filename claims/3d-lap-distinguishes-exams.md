## Evidence

### Laverty JT 2016

Laverty JT, Underwood SM, Matz RL, Posey LA, Carmel JH, Caballero MD, Fata-Hartley CL, Ebert-May D, Jardeleza SE, Cooper MM. (2016). Characterizing College Science Assessments: The Three-Dimensional Learning Assessment Protocol. PLoS ONE 11(9): e0162333. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0162333

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Coding analysis of two exams per discipline (A exams from canonical tasks, B exams from research-based curricula) using the 3D-LAP, with points weighted by task value. The B exams were "dominated by tasks that might elicit evidence of two or three dimensions, with at least 40% of the points being awarded for three-dimensional tasks."

> "Fig 4 shows that in each of the A exams, most of the points on the exam are given to tasks with little potential to elicit more than one dimension. In contrast, each of the B exams is dominated by tasks that might elicit evidence of two or three dimensions, with at least 40% of the points being awarded for three-dimensional tasks."
