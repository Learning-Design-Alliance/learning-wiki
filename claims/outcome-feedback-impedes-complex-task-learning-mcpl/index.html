Kluger 1996¶

Kluger, A. N., & DeNisi, A. (1996). The effects of feedback interventions on performance: A historical review, a meta-analysis, and a preliminary feedback intervention theory. Psychological Bulletin, 119(2), 254-284. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.119.2.254

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Narrative review attribution: the authors report, citing multiple MCPL-paradigm studies and a recent review by Balzer et al. (1989), that outcome feedback impedes complex-task learning, leading some researchers to question whether people can learn complex rules from feedback at all. No effect size is printed; the reviewed studies' designs are not described here.
