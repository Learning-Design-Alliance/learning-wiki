Quesen 2026¶

Quesen, S. (2026). What educational research looked like when scores were rising: A comparative analysis of AERA conference programs, 2005–2014 and 2022–2025. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681368

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Cross-era string-match analysis of AERA programs finds the social/identity terms moved in different directions rather than uniformly: equity up 408 percent, identity up 26 percent (from 294.4 to 370.0), social justice down 23 percent, and critical race theory up 58 percent but "remained low in absolute terms".

"The data show a more nuanced pattern than a wholesale shift toward identity topics: Equity research increased dramatically (+408%), from 140.0 to 711.2 mentions per year. This is the largest shift in the data."
