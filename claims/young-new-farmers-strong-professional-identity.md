Luo Tao, Huang Qingpeng, Mai Jingyan. (2026). Development status of young new farmers from the perspective of vocational ability: A research analysis based on 21 cities in Guangdong Province. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0349224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 302 young new farmers in Guangdong Province using a 50-item questionnaire with 5-point Likert scale. The study reports that 94% recognized young farming as a profession and 84.67% chose it as a long-term career.

> "Table 1 shows that 94% of the respondents regarded "young farmers" as a profession rather than merely an entrepreneurial form. Most respondents held a "very optimistic" or "relatively optimistic" attitude towards the prospects of this profession, and 84.67% of the respondents expressed their willingness to choose it as a long-term career."
