## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Experimental-group students reported significantly greater gains in developing understanding (d = 0.89), attitudes changing (d = 0.55) and integration of learning (d = 0.46) than controls. [→ Liu Weichu 2021](#liu-weichu-2021)

## Evidence

### Liu Weichu 2021

Liu Weichu, Wang Jun, Zhang Han, Yu Changfeng, Liu Shuai, Zhang Cen, Yu Jingya, Liu Qiao, Yang Bing. (2021). Determining the effects of blended learning using the community of inquiry on nursing students' learning gains in sudden patient deterioration module. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.914

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t tests on three SALG learning-gain sections in the two-group quasi-experimental trial. The article reports Cohen d = 0.89 for developing understanding, d = 0.55 for attitudes changing and d = 0.46 for integration of learning, all favouring the CoI-based blended learning group.

> "Specifically, there was significant difference for learning gains in two groups, in terms of developing understanding (t = 6.62, p < .001, Cohen d = 0.89), attitudes changing (t = 4.15, p < .001, Cohen d = 0.55) and integration of learning (t = 3.47, p = .001, Cohen d = 0.46)."
