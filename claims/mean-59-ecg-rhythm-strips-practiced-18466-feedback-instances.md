## Evidence

### Waechter Jason 2019

Waechter Jason, Reading David, Lee Chel Hee, Walker Mathieu. (2019). Quantifying the medical student learning curve for ECG rhythm strip interpretation using deliberate practice. GMS Journal for Medical Education 36(4):Doc40. [doi:10.3205/zma001248](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001248)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational cohort study of 314 consenting first-year students at two Canadian medical schools using online practice modules; descriptive practice analytics show "59 rhythm strips practiced per student" (mean 4.1 practice repetitions per diagnosis, SD=2.7). The article separately reports 18,466 total practice instances.

> "The mean number of times each of the 14 diagnoses were practiced per student was 4.1 (SD=2.7), representing 59 rhythm strips practiced per student"
