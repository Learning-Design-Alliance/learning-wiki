This is the study's Method section describing the trial design as a protocol, not a result. Departments are matched on prevalent diagnostic category and average treatment duration, then randomized within pairs. The article states 936 professionals from 43 departments are registered, with the experimental condition training all staff (n = 532, 21 departments) and the control condition (22 departments, 404 professionals) receiving guideline dissemination as usual.

> "In a multicenter cluster randomized controlled trial, 43 psychiatric departments spread over 10 regional mental health institutions throughout The Netherlands will be clustered in pairs with respect to the most prevalent diagnostic category of patients and average duration of treatment."
