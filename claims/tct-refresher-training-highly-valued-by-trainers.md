## Evidence

### Mousset RA 2026

Mousset RA, de Vos tot Nederveen Cappel WH, Boonstra JJ, Langers AMJ, Brand PLP. (2026). Time to Refresh: Design and Evaluation of Refresher Training to Sustain Procedural Teaching Skills. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2443

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Kirkpatrick level 1 evaluation using an online post-training questionnaire completed by 12 of 16 participants (75% response). The article reports that "All respondents perceived participation in the refresher training following completion of the initial TCT course as valuable".
