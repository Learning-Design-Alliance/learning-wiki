## Evidence

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2009

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2009). Development of quantum perspectives in modern physics. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 5, 010106 (2009). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.5.010106

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-course consistency analysis combining responses from both PHYS3 courses across three questions (QWI essay, Plinko essay, QA No. 16); "the majority of students end up with a mixed perspective (p < 0.01 by pairwise proportion test to each of quantum and realist groups)."

> "Table IX shows that the majority of students end up with a mixed perspective (p < 0.01 by pairwise proportion test to each of quantum and realist groups), sometimes applying a quantum perspective and sometimes a realist perspective."

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2009 (2)

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2009). Development of quantum perspectives in modern physics. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 5, 010106 (2009). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.5.010106

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-tabulation of post-instruction responses to QA No. 16 grouped by QWI essay perspective; "18% of those who disagreed with QA No. 16, and 33% of those who agreed, were offering a response that was inconsistent with their response to the QWI essay question."

> "18% of those who disagreed with QA No. 16, and 33% of those who agreed, were offering a response that was inconsistent with their response to the QWI essay question."

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2009 (3)

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2009). Development of quantum perspectives in modern physics. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 5, 010106 (2009). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.5.010106

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pairwise proportion tests on combined PHYS3 consistency data; "more students (p = 0.05, two tailed, and pairwise) end with a consistently realist perspective than a quantum perspective." The mixed state dominated each course independently (71% and 50%).

> "Intriguingly, more students (p = 0.05, two tailed, and pairwise) end with a consistently realist perspective than a quantum perspective."
