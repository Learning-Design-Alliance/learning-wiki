  • Immersive reality training produced higher knowledge and skill scores than conventional training in dental studies — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • AccuTouch VR simulator training improved live fibreoptic intubation performance in inexperienced paediatric residents, reducing time to intubation from 5.15 to 0.88 minutes — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Most digital teaching methods demonstrated positive effects on practical skills acquisition, particularly simulation-based, VR-supported or interactive formats, but effect magnitude and consistency varied substantially — a broader claim this one bears on
