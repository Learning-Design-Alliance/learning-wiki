## Evidence

### Guilding C 2021

Guilding C, Pye RE, Butler S, Atkinson M, Field E. (2021). Answering questions in a co-created formative exam question bank improves summative exam performance, while students perceive benefits from answering, authoring, and peer discussion: A mixed methods analysis of PeerWise. Pharmacol Res Perspect. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.833

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression on the 2015 cohort testing authoring as a proposed learning mechanism; the model was significant (F(4, 439) = 136.548) explaining 55% of variance, but "mean authoring (t = −0.77, p = .441) nonsignificant". No effect size printed.

> "Only mean answering was a significant predictor (t = 3.641, p < .001), with mean rating (t = −1.226, p = .221) and mean authoring (t = −0.77, p = .441) nonsignificant predictors."
