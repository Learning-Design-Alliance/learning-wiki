## Evidence

### Lina Shanley 2017

Lina Shanley, Mari Strand Cary, Ben Clarke, Meg Guerreiro, Michael Thier. (2017). Instructors’ technology experience and iPad delivered intervention implementation: A mixed methods replication study. Educational Technology Research and Development 65, 815–830. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/instructors-technology-experience-and-ipad-delivered-intervention-implementation-a-mixed-methods-replication-study/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Two studies during development of the KinderTEK iPad kindergarten mathematics intervention examined the relationship between instructor-reported technology experience and implementation as measured by student use; the authors report the experience range "corresponded with variable student use."

> "Study results show participating instructors had a range of pre-intervention technological experience with iPads and this range corresponded with variable student use in terms of both within class variability of student use and percentage of students meeting the duration of use target."
