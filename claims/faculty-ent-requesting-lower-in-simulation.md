### Rochlen LR 2017

Rochlen LR, Housey M, Gannon I, Mitchell S, Rooney DM, Tait AR, Engoren M. (2017). Assessing anesthesiology residents’ out-of-the-operating-room (OOOR) emergent airway management. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-017-0387-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observed requesting behavior during the simulation for 63 residents, compared with pre-survey self-reports (32% requesting faculty sometimes/very often; 11% ENT). Percentages as printed; no effect size reported.
