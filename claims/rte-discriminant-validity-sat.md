## Evidence

### Wise 2005

Wise, S. L., & Kong, X. (2005). Response Time Effort: A New Measure of Examinee Motivation in Computer-Based Tests. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, Montreal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED490203. Published version, Applied Measurement in Education: [doi:10.1207/s15324818ame1802_2](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15324818ame1802_2)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Discriminant validity analysis in the 472-examinee sample, correlating RTE scores with SAT-Verbal and SAT-Quantitative scores obtained from student records. The correlations were "near zero", which the authors take as evidence the RTE scores showed discriminant validity.

> "Table 1 shows that the correlations between RTE scores and the SAT subscale scores were near zero, providing support for Hypothesis 3."
