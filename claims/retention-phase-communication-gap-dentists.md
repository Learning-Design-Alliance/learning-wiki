## Evidence

### Schreuder MM 2025

Schreuder MM, Renkema A-M, Kuijpers-Jagtman AM, Padmos JAD. (2025). Dutch dentists’ involvement in orthodontic retention: monitoring, opinions, competence and communication gaps. The European Journal of Orthodontics. https://doi.org/10.1093/ejo/cjaf020

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey section on responsibility and communication (Table 4) among 212 GDPs. Wishes versus received frequencies were compared descriptively: 83.5% wanted information on retention duration while 59.2% reported never receiving it, and 61.6% never received information on retainer type.

> "GDPs appreciated to receive information from the orthodontic practitioner regarding the start and duration of the retention phase, retainer type, and by which practitioner the retainer is preferably monitored. The frequency with which dentists were informed about these topics does not correspond to their wishes"
