## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Models excluding Ln and Ln-1 achieved lower cross-validated correlations (0.438 vs 0.446 for Cognitive Tutor; 0.301 vs 0.397 for ASSISTments), and the differences were statistically significant. [→ Baker 2011](#baker-2011)

## Evidence

### Baker 2011

Baker, Ryan S.J.d., Goldstein, Adam B., Heffernan, Neil T. (2011). Detecting Learning Moment-by-Moment. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. In Press. https://doi.org/10.3233/JAI-2011-015

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Re-fit comparison of full versus restricted P(J) models on both tutor log datasets, using a test of the difference between two correlation coefficients for correlated samples aggregated with Stouffer's Z; the difference was "highly statistically significant" in both systems.

> "According to this test, the difference between the two models is highly statistically significant, both for the Cognitive Tutor data, Z=116.51, p<0.0001, and for the ASSISTments data, Z = 66.34, p<0.001."
