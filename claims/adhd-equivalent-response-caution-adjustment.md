## Evidence

### Fosco 2019

Fosco, W. D., Kofler, M. J., Alderson, R. M., Tarle, S. J., Raiker, J. S., & Sarver, D. E. (2019). Inhibitory Control and Information Processing in ADHD: Comparing the Dual Task and Performance Adjustment Hypotheses. Journal of Abnormal Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-018-0504-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian mixed ANOVA comparing 81 children with ADHD and 63 without. The analysis found "introducing intermittent stop trials affected the ADHD and Non-ADHD groups equivalently" (BF01 = 5.56), supporting intact self-regulation of speed-accuracy tradeoffs in ADHD.

> "There was moderate evidence that groups were equivalent in boundary separation (BF01 = 3.26; d = 0.22), and that introducing intermittent stop trials affected the ADHD and Non-ADHD groups equivalently (group x task interaction: BF01 = 5.56)."
