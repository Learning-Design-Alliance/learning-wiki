### Sedlacek 2025

Sedlacek, Q., Villa, A. M., III, Friend, M., Pérez, G., Dozier, S., Haeger, H., Lomelí, K., & Mejia, J. A. (2025). How Representation Matters: Conceptualizing Systemic Racism to Develop Diversity Research Agendas for Undergraduate STEM Education. `q2 · i1` · `review · r2`

The review reports that the 2017 FSSE update (42 colleges) replicated the pattern with small standardized effects: "significant standardized effect sizes ranging between 0.03 and 0.08." The review characterizes the association as small and highly variable, and stresses it requires non-essentialist explanation.

> "Nelson Laird et al. (2018) found significant standardized effect sizes ranging between 0.03 and 0.08, depending on the specific marginalized racial/ethnic identities and pedagogical practices being examined."
