## Evidence

### Maloney Ciaran M. C. 2026

Maloney Ciaran M. C., Atkin Andrew J., Beaumont Lee C., Dainty Jack R., Warburton Victoria E. (2026). Adolescents’ time-use and academic attainment: A longitudinal, compositional analysis in the Millennium Cohort Study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0346302

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weekday isotemporal substitution analysis (n=1644) across 20-minute media reallocations from each of the five other behaviour sets. The article reports this "mixed pattern of associations with Attainment 8" and no effect sizes beyond the tabulated βs.

> "For instance, in the weekday data, reallocations to media activities from physical activity, school-related activities, or hobbies were associated with a reduction in academic attainment, whereas substituting time from sleep and domestic tasks was associated with enhanced academic attainment."
