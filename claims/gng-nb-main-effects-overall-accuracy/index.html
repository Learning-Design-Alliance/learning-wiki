Evidence¶

Chevalier N. 2012¶

Chevalier N., Sheffield T. D., Nelson J. M., Clark C. A. C., Wiebe S. A., Espy K. A. (2012). Underpinnings of the Costs of Flexibility in Preschool Children: The Roles of Inhibition and Working Memory. Developmental Neuropsychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3308575/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

In local-cost models, GNG main effects were significant at 3;9 (γ = .038, p = .008) and 4;6 (γ = .059, p = .043); children one SD above the GNG mean were "3.8% more accurate overall... and 5.9% more accurate at age 4;6."

"GNG performance had a significant main effect at ages 3;9 (γ = .038, p = .008) and 4;6 (γ = .059, p = .043). Children who scored one SD higher than average were 3.8% more accurate overall than children who scored at the GNG mean at age 3;9, and 5.9% more accurate at age 4;6."

Chevalier N. 2012 (2)¶

Chevalier N., Sheffield T. D., Nelson J. M., Clark C. A. C., Wiebe S. A., Espy K. A. (2012). Underpinnings of the Costs of Flexibility in Preschool Children: The Roles of Inhibition and Working Memory. Developmental Neuropsychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3308575/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Nebraska Barnyard main effects were significant at 3;9 (γ = .056, p = .036) and 4;6 (γ = .040, p = .001) but not at 5;3 (γ = .016, p = .36), indicating overall accuracy benefits of working memory at the younger ages only.

"Similarly, NB performance had a significant main effect at ages 3;9 (γ = .056, p = .036), 4;6 (γ = .040, p = .001) and 5;3 (γ = .016, p = .36)."
