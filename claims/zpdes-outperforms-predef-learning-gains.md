## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In an RCT with 265 children, test-score gains over time were larger under ZPDES than under the Predefined sequence algorithm (algo x pre/post effect η2 = 0.331). [→ Clément 2024](#clement-2024)

## Evidence

### Clément 2024

Clément, B., Sauzéon, H., Roy, D., & Oudeyer, P.-Y. (2024). Improved Performances and Motivation in Intelligent Tutoring Systems: Combining Machine Learning and Learner Choice. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.01669](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.01669)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Three-way mixed ANOVA (algo x choice x pre/post) on pre/post math test scores in the 265-child RCT; the article reports marginal means (Predef: 6.83/8.36; ZPDES: 6.74/9.38) and states that "children working with ZPDES algorithm learned more than the ones working with the Predefined sequence algorithm".

> "The main significant effect revealed an increase of the test score across time (pre/post factor effect, [F (1, 261) = 129.25, p − value = 0.000, η2 = 0.331]) which is boosted under the ZPDES condition compared to Predefined condition"
