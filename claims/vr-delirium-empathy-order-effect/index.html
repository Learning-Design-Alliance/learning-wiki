Evidence¶

EL Hussein Mohamed Toufic 2026¶

EL Hussein Mohamed Toufic, Sheehan Dawson S., Hirst Sandra. (2026). Simulation‐Based Education to Support Nurses' Critical Thinking in Delirium Recognition: A Scoping Review. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70640

q2 · i? · review · r2

This was a prospective, single-centre, quasi-experimental crossover study with 18 clinically active registered nurses comparing VR delirium training (DelTrain via head-mounted display) with a traditional presentation-based training across three measurement points. The review reports "significant increases in empathy and knowledge... following virtual‐reality training, with the largest gains when virtual reality preceded traditional didactic instruction"; no effect size is printed.

"Significant increases in empathy and knowledge were documented following virtual‐reality training, with the largest gains when virtual reality preceded traditional didactic instruction (Günther et al. 2025)."
