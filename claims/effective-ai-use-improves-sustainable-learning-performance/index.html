In the PLS-SEM structural model of the 623-student three-wave sample, the study reports that "Eﬀective AI use positively inﬂuences sustainable learning performance". Sustainable learning performance is measured as perceived long-term retention, independent application, and transfer.

"Eﬀective AI use positively inﬂuences sustainable learning performance but also increases AI over-reliance, which negatively aﬀects learning outcomes."
