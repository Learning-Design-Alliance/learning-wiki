This is the article's reflective argument (Lesson One) about the affective dimension of problem solving, offered as authors' interpretation with no empirical data. The essay claims that repeated encounters with "countless problems that initially seemed overwhelming" let learners "begin to develop confidence"; it reports no data on whether they do.

> "Yet after working through countless problems that initially seemed overwhelming, we begin to develop confidence in our ability to find a way forward—both within and beyond the physics classroom."
