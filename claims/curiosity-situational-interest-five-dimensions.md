## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The review synthesizes five dimensions distinguishing curiosity from situational interest, treating curiosity as an aversive dopaminergic wanting state and situational interest as positive opioid liking affect. [→ Shin 2019](#shin-2019)

## Evidence

### Shin 2019

Shin, D. D., & Kim, S. (2019). Homo Curious: Curious or Interested? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09497-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review synthesis. The authors propose that situational interest follows the hedonic principle and the opioid liking system, whereas curiosity follows drive theory and the dopaminergic wanting system, with curiosity triggered specifically by "a recognizable information gap."

> "we summarize five dimensions that set curiosity apart from situational interest: (1) theoretical account, (2) biological underpinnings, (3) triggering factors, (4) related affect, and (5) information search."
