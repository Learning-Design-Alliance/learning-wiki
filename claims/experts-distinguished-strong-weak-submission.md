## Evidence

### Christopher Burke and Timothy J. Atherton 2016

Christopher Burke and Timothy J. Atherton. (2016). Developing a project-based computational physics course grounded in expert practice. [doi:10.1119/1.4975381](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4975381)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-grader consistency exercise: two project-5 submissions perceived to differ greatly in quality were graded per competency by the interviewed experts; a panel with four experts discussed discrepancies, attributing most differences to whether graders looked at the code or just the report. The experts "all agreed that submission B represented work of a high quality".

> "Submissions A and B were clearly distinguished from one another in quality by the graders, but the degree of consistency is diﬀerent between the two."
