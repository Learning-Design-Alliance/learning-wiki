## Evidence

### BADRU 2016

BADRU, Ademola K. (2016). Problem – Based Instructional Strategy and Numerical Ability as Determinants of Senior Secondary Achievement in Mathematics. Journal of Education and Practice. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102799

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA main effect of gender in the same quasi-experimental study. The article reports F(1,196) = 0.5151, p < 0.05, with female students' adjusted mean of 19.743 versus 13.372 for male students.

> "Concerning the main effects of Gender on students’ achievement in mathematics, the result revealed that the outcome was significant (F (1, 196)  = 0.5151; P < 0.05)."
