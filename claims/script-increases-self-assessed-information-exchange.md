## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Learners in the scripted group felt they had been more active in exchanging information related to heuristic strategies than control learners (d=0.82). [→ Zottmann Jan M. 2018](#zottmann-jan-m-2018)

## Evidence

### Zottmann Jan M. 2018

Zottmann Jan M., Dieckmann Peter, Taraszow Tatjana, Rall Marcus, Fischer Frank. (2018). Just watching is not enough: Fostering simulation-based learning with collaboration scripts. GMS Journal for Medical Education 35(3), Doc35. [doi:10.3205/zma001181](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001181)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Subjective measure from a 14-item collaboration questionnaire (e.g. "I discussed CRM principles with others during the observational phases") on a four-point Likert scale, Cronbach's α=.67, completed at the end of training. The printed effect size was d=0.82.

> "learners in the scripted group also felt they had been more active in terms of exchanging information related to heuristic strategies than learners in the control group. This difference in self-assessed activity was significant, t(31)=-2.31, p=.028, d=0.82."
