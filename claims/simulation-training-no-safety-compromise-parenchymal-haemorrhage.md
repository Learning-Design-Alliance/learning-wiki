## Evidence

### Svobodová Veronika 2023

Svobodová Veronika, Maršálková Hana, Volevach Ekaterina, Mikulík Robert. (2023). Simulation-based team training improves door-to-needle time for intravenous thrombolysis. BMJ Open Quality 12(1):e002107. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2022-002107

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kirkpatrick level 4 patient-safety outcome from the same nationwide registry cohort. Parenchymal haemorrhage was numerically less frequent in trained centres (3.5% vs 5.4%) but the difference did not reach significance (p=0.054); any ICH differed non-significantly (8.5% vs 9.7%, p=0.35).

> "Also, any PH occurred in 5.4% of patients treated in centres without simulation training as compared with 3.5% in centres with simulation training (p=0.054)."
