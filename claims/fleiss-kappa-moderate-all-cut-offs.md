`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-reader reliability analysis of competence-test classifications by the 69 trained pathologists, calculated in R version 3.5.1. The article reports scoring "was moderately reliable (Κ 0.581, 0.556 and 0.535, respectively)" at the three cut-offs, interpreted against the article's stated moderate band of 0.41–0.60.

> "Fleiss' Κappa analysis of inter-reader reliability indicated that, at all three TC cut-offs (≥1%, ≥25% and ≥ 50%), the scoring was moderately reliable (Κ 0.581, 0.556 and 0.535, respectively) for classifying cases as PD-L1 high or low/negative."
