`q2 i?` During the enacted choreography, learners immediately connected which planet can overtake another with the planets' different speeds, concluding that Mars never passes by and Mercury is the fastest inner planet. [→ Rollinde 2019](#rollinde-2019)

## Evidence

### Rollinde 2019

Rollinde, E. (2019). Enacting Planets to Understand Occultation Phenomena. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1910.09403](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1910.09403)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative observation of the gamified overtaking activity with French 12-year-old pupils in September 2019. When the teacher asked observers to raise hands only if Mars was involved in an overtaking, "Mars is never the one that pass by", and most learners immediately connected this with Mars's slow speed; the same held for Mercury when it was the target planet.

> "It then appears that Mars is never the one that pass by . The connection with the different speed was immediately perceived by most learners who almost shouted "but of course, Mars is travelling so slowly!"."
