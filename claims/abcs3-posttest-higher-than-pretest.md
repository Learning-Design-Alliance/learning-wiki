## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the two-day simulation seminar, full-category participants' scores on a 22-question case-based multiple-choice test rose from pre-test to post-test in one group with no comparison group. [→ Pantelidis P 2016](#pantelidis-p-2016)

## Evidence

### Pantelidis P 2016

Pantelidis P, Staikoglou N, Paparoidamis G, Drosos C, Karamaroudis S, Samara A, Keskinis C, Sideris M, Giannakoulas G, Tsoulfas G, Karagiannis A. (2016). Medical students’ satisfaction with the Applied Basic Clinical Seminar with Scenarios for Students, a novel simulation-based learning method in Greece. J Educ Eval Health Prof. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2016.13.13

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre/post comparison: all 60 full-category participants took the same 22-question norm-referenced test before and after the seminar. "The pre-test score was 70.08 (±16.23), while the post-test score was 85.53 (±13.40)" with significant improvement (P<0.001).

> "The pre-test score was 70.08 (±16.23), while the post-test score was 85.53 (±13.40). The independent-samples Wilcoxon signed rank test showed that significant improvement took place (P<0.001)"
