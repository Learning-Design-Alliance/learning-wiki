Principal Leadership Practices 2021¶

Chapter 13 of Exploring Principal Development and Teacher Outcomes, edited by Peter Youngs, Jihyun Kim, and Madeline Mavrogordato. The chapter's overview states, based on a longitudinal multi-method study of hundreds of schools, that "the effects of leadership are largely indirect--mediated through school organizational processes." No effect size is printed on the page.
