### Celebioglu Morkoc and Aktan Acar 2014

Ozlem CELEBIOGLU MORKOC & Ebru AKTAN ACAR. (2014). Effectiveness of Multipurpose Unit Early Classroom Intervention Program for 4–5-Year-Old Children. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2014.5.2065

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group comparison of final test scores of the 14-child experimental and 14-child control groups using Mann Whitney U tests; the article states "All these differences were in favor of the experimental group", with p-values as printed and a .05 chi-square result for general developmental level.

> "Statistically significant differences were obtained for the Denver II Fine Motor (p < .05) and Gross Motor (p < .05), the Developmental Assessment Observation Form Social Development ( p < .05 ), and the Language Development ( p < .05 ), Cognitive ( p < .001) Development and Self-Care Skills ( p < .001 ) sub-dimensions."
