Keller, M. M., Yanagida, T., Lüdtke, O., & Goetz, T. (2025). How Similar Are Students' Aggregated State Emotions to Their Self-Reported Trait Emotions? Results from a Measurement Burst Design Across Three School Years. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09995-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Semi-partial correlation analysis of incremental predictive validity in the three-wave study (N = 161), with outcomes of grades, intrinsic value, self-concept, and approach/avoidance career orientation. The article reports traits were "only systematically correlated to external criteria for students' enjoyment".

> "Overall, self-reported traits (corrected for aggregated emotional states) were only systematically correlated to external criteria for students' enjoyment and somewhat for pride."
