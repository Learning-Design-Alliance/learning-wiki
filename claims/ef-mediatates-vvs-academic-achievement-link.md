## Evidence

### Rosen Maya L. 2021

Rosen Maya L., Lurie Lucy A., Sambrook Kelly A., Meltzoff Andrew N., McLaughlin Katie A. (2021). Neural mechanisms underlying the income-achievement gap: The role of the ventral visual stream. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.101025

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

ROI regression in the 62-child sample tested associations between VVS activation and an EF composite (backwards digit span, Simon Says, DCCS). Cued-attention associations were "significantly associated with EF (β = 0.315, p = .015; β = 0.302, p = .015"; memory-guided attention showed only trend-level associations after correction.

> "Neural activation in left and right VVS during cued attention was significantly associated with EF (β = 0.315, p = .015; β = 0.302, p = .015, respectively; Fig. 7A). After multiple comparison correction, there was a trend toward a positive association between activation during memory-guided attention and EF in both left and right VVS (β = 0.255, p = .068; β = 0.237, p = .068, respectively; Fig. 7B)."

### Rosen Maya L. 2021 (2)

Rosen Maya L., Lurie Lucy A., Sambrook Kelly A., Meltzoff Andrew N., McLaughlin Katie A. (2021). Neural mechanisms underlying the income-achievement gap: The role of the ventral visual stream. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.101025

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (Hayes, SPSS) in the same sample tested EF as mediator of the VVS–achievement link. The article reports "a significant indirect effect of left and right VVS activation during cued attention (95% CI: [.189, 4.159] and 95% CI: [.132, 3.600]" plus a trend for right VVS during memory-guided attention; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "We found a significant indirect effect of left and right VVS activation during cued attention (95% CI: [.189, 4.159] and 95% CI: [.132, 3.600], respectively) and of left VVS during memory-guided attention (95% CI: [.178 4.255] and a trend through activation in the right VVS during memory-guided attention (90% CI:.152 to 4.061) on academic achievement through EF."
