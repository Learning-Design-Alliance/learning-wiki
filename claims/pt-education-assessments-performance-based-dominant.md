Systematic charting of the 92 assessments across 139 included studies in this scoping review (PubMed, CINAHL, ERIC, Scopus through April 2025). The review reports "Performance-based assessments were the most common format" with the printed counts and percentages for simulation and workplace formats.

> "Ninety-two distinct assessments were included in the articles, with multiple versions of an assessment (e.g., updated versions, translations) counted as a single assessment. Performance-based assessments were the most common format with 40 (43.5%) simulation-based assessments, 28 (30.4%) workplace-based assessments"
