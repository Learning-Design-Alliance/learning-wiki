### Charles Baily 2017

Charles Baily, Qing X. Ryan, Cecilia Astolfi and Steven J. Pollock. (2017). Conceptual Assessment Tool for Advanced Undergraduate Electrodynamics. PHYS. REV. – PHYS. ED. RES. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020113)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison of aggregate post-instruction scores from 16 course offerings taught by 12 instructors at 9 institutions, contrasting standard lecture courses with transformed courses. The article reports "significant differences between traditional versus transformed courses"; at the student level, 41.5%±2.0% versus 59.3%±0.9%.

> "The four standard lecture-based courses have an average of 42.0%±4.8% (N=72); and courses taught with varying degrees of interactivity had an average of 56.8%±2.9% (N=423)."
