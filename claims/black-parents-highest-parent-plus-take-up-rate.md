`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of federal NPSAS:UG data in the Black Parent PLUS loan borrowers section, which reports that "5.4% of Black parents took out a Parent PLUS loan — a higher percentage than any other race and ethnicity." The brief also cites an external analysis finding the share of Black PLUS borrowers with incomes below $30,000 nearly tripled from 2008 to 2018, reaching 44% versus 10% of White PLUS borrowers in 2018.

> "In the 2015-16 academic year, 5.4% of Black parents took out a Parent PLUS loan — a higher percentage than any other race and ethnicity."
