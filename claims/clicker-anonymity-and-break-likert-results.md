## Evidence

### M. Alamgir Hossain 2021

M. Alamgir Hossain, Petra M. Menz, and John M. Stockie. (2021). An Open-Access Clicker Question Bank for Numerical Analysis. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1080/10511970.2021.1954113](https://doi.org/10.1080/10511970.2021.1954113)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Likert-scale question Q8 of the mid-semester survey (137 responses) rated ten statements about the clicker experience. The authors interpret the result as showing "a strong preference for the anonymity of a clicker poll" and that "Clickers provide a positive break from the rest of lecture."

> "The first three statements (A,B,C) all received more than 80% positive responses (either “agree” or “strongly agree”) on the Likert scale"
