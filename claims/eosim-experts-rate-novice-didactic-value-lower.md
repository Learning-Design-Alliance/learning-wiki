`q3 i?` On Task 2, post hoc analysis showed experts rated the module's didactic value for training novice laparoscopic surgeons significantly lower than novices (2.0 versus 3.8, p < 0.001) and lower than the target group (p = 0.033). [→ Leijte Erik 2019](#leijte-erik-2019)

## Evidence

### Leijte Erik 2019

Leijte Erik, Arts Elke, Witteman Bart, Jakimowicz Jack, De Blaauw Ivo, Botden Sanne. (2019). Construct, content and face validity of the eoSim laparoscopic simulator on advanced suturing tasks. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-06652-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Individual-question analysis of questionnaire ratings on the upside-down needle transfer task. Experts gave the lowest mean rating (2.0) for didactic value for novice training; post hoc tests confirmed expert-versus-novice (p < 0.001) and expert-versus-target (p = 0.033) differences. A similar expert-versus-novice difference appeared on Task 3 (p = 0.004).

> "Analysis of the difference in opinion scores between the groups for the individual questions, resulted a p-value of < 0.001 regarding the didactic value of the module to train novice laparoscopic surgeons (Novice 3.8 Target 3.3 Expert 2.0). Post hoc analysis shows a statistical difference between Experts versus Novices (p < 0.001) and Experts versus Target group (p = 0.033)."
