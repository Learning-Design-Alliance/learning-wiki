## Evidence

### Ellington Laura E 2020

Ellington Laura E, Becerra Rosario, Mallma Gabriela, Tantaleán da Fieno José, Nair Deepthi, Onchiri Frankline, Nielsen Katie R. (2020). Knowledge acquisition and retention after a high flow training programme in Peru: a quasi-experimental single group pre–post design. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-035125

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retention analysis excluding paediatric residents compared post-baseline (n=67) with pre-3-month (n=19) and pre-12-month (n=33) scores using Wilcoxon rank-sum tests. The article reports significant differences for overall short-term retention (p=0.017) and long-term retention (p=0.001), indicating knowledge decline at both intervals.

> "Overall short-term and long-term retention suffered throughout the study period, with participants failing to retain minimum competency at 3-month and 12-month follow-up assessments (74% (61–78) and 70% (63–76), respectively)"
