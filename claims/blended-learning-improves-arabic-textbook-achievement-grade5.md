## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Fifth graders taught with blended learning scored significantly higher on the post achievement test than students taught traditionally, after ANCOVA adjustment for pretest scores. [→ Al-Madani 2015](#al-madani-2015)

## Evidence

### Al-Madani 2015

Feras Mohammed Al-Madani. (2015). The Effect Of Blended Learning Approach On Fifth Grade Students' Academic Achievement In My Beautiful Language Textbook And The Development Of Their Verbal Creative Thinking In Saudi Arabia. Journal of International Education Research, 11(4). https://clutejournals.com/index.php/JIER

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 49 fifth graders: Experimental Group (25) taught via blended learning, Control Group (24) taught traditionally, on unit six of My Beautiful Language Textbook. ANCOVA on post achievement test gave F = 27.558, Sig. 0.000, "in favor of the students in Experimental Group". Means: experimental 28.2 vs control 24.83.

> "Table 2 shows value of F statistics (27.55) and significance level (0.000) which indicates statistical significant difference at (α ≤ 0.05) between the students' mean scores in the post achievement test in favor of the students in Experimental Group who studied via blended learning."
