## Evidence

### Jesper Bruun 2019

Jesper Bruun, Pia J. Ray, Linda Udby. (2019). Network analyses of student engagement with online textbook problems. Preprint submitted to Computers & Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1903.11390](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1903.11390)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Similarity network built from 231 problem session networks (of 2184 total) using Euclidean distances in rotated PC space, sparsified with LANS. Fuzzy Infomap found 12 clusters, "two of these clusters consisted of one session network each", leaving ten clusters over 229 sessions.

> "Fuzzy Infomap found 12 clusters in total. However, two of these clusters consisted of one session network each and where discarded from further analysis."
