`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Perceptions were gathered through interviews with 15 learners and 5 educators, who rated usefulness on a 7-point Likert scale and explained their ratings. The article reports that the identified comprehensibility and perceived usefulness suggest the potential feasibility of adapting the networks into pedagogical instruments.

> "Lastly, we further interviewed these learners and the 5 educators who led the reflection sessions to explore the perceived usefulness of these networks in facilitating reflection on communication (RQ3)."
