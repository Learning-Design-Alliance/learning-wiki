Evidence¶

Vaughn 2013¶

Vaughn, S., Roberts, G., & Reutebuch, C. (2013). Collaborative Strategic Reading: Replications with Consideration of the Role of Fidelity. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563062

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Year 1 cluster-randomized trial (61 classes, 782 students) in Texas and Colorado middle schools, analyzed with multilevel models in Mplus. The quote reports the Gates-MacGinitie posttest means and "effect size of g = 0.12"; AIMSweb and TOSREC differences were not significant.

"The model-estimated (Level-2 latent) posttest average standard score on the Gates-MacGinitie was 95.87 for comparison classes and 97.04 for the treatment conditions (ǻȤ2 = 9.91, p < .01). This is equivalent to an (bias corrected Hedges) effect size of g = 0.12."
