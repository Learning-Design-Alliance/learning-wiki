Phenomenological analysis of debriefing data, performed by a senior surgical attending and a fourth-year surgical resident using the data frame theory of sense-making. The analysis of "492 individual quotes" yielded five themes: teamwork and communication, psychological safety, leadership and accountability, stability, and outcome focus with attention to detail.

> "Our qualitative analysis revealed 492 individual quotes. The results suggest that the observed behavioral changes are a result of a sense-making emerging from 5 specific themes as discussed below."
