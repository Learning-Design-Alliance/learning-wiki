## Evidence

### Muca Edlira 2023

Muca Edlira, Molino Monica, Ghislieri Chiara, Baratta Mario, Odore Rosangela, Bergero Domenico, Valle Emanuela. (2023). Relationships between psychological characteristics, academic fit and engagement with academic performance in veterinary medical students. BMC Veterinary Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-023-03695-0

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations from Table 1 of the 231-student survey: GPA–engagement r = 0.25, GPA–academic fit r = 0.19, GPA–self-efficacy r = 0.15 (all p < 0.05 as printed); GPA–internal locus of control r = -0.05 was not significant. Descriptive correlations, printed in the table.

> "The results show that the GPA positively correlated with work engagement, academic fit, and self-efficacy; work engagement showed a negative correlation with exhaustion and a positive correlation with academic fit, internal locus of control, and self-efficacy;"
