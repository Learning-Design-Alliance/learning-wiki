# The intervention effect increases with the number of Tekster lessons students complete

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was a statistically significant positive relationship between the number of student-completed lessons and the intervention effect; students completing all 16 lessons had an ES of 0.40 versus 0.32 overall. [→ Bouwer 2018](#bouwer-2018)

## Evidence

### Bouwer 2018

Bouwer, R., Koster, M., & van den Bergh, H. (2018). Effects of a Strategy-Focused Instructional Program on the Writing Quality of Upper Elementary Students in the Netherlands. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(1), 58–71. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000206

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis including lessons completed as a fixed factor; students completed on average 10 lessons (SD = 4). The regression coefficient was β = 0.21 (SE = 0.09, p < .01); completers of all 16 lessons showed a gain of 5.99 points, ES 0.40.

> "The results indicated a statistically significant, positive relationship between the number of student-completed lessons and the intervention effect, /H9252/H110050.21 ( SE /H110050.09, p /H11021.01). On average, students who completed all 16 Tekster lessons had a gain score of 5.99, which translates to an ES of 0.40."
