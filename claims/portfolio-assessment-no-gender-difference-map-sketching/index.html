Ugodulunwa 2015¶

Ugodulunwa, C., & Wakjissa, S. (2015). Use of Portfolio Assessment Technique in Teaching Map Sketching and Location in Secondary School Geography in Jos, Nigeria. Journal of Education and Practice, 6(17). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079911

t-test for independent samples on post-test Geography Achievement Test scores of 19 male and 30 female students in the portfolio-assessment group. The article reports a non-significant difference at the .05 level; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.
