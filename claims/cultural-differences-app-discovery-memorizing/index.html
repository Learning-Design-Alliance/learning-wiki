Evidence¶

Joanna Kic-Drgas 2024¶

Joanna Kic-Drgas, Ferit Kılıçkaya. (2024). Exploring novel approaches to digital self-regulated learning: a study on the use of mobile applications among Polish and Turkish EFL pre-service teachers. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10374-w

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Chi-Square Test of Independence on application-discovery sources in the two-country survey. The article prints the effect size phi = .376 for this association, a moderate-level association; the printed test is χ 2 (1, n = 206) = 29.180, p = .001.

"Fewer participants in both groups indicated teacher's recommendation (χ 2 (1, n = 206) = 29.180, p = .001, phi = .376)."

Joanna Kic-Drgas 2024 (2)¶

Joanna Kic-Drgas, Ferit Kılıçkaya. (2024). Exploring novel approaches to digital self-regulated learning: a study on the use of mobile applications among Polish and Turkish EFL pre-service teachers. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10374-w

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Chi-Square comparison of post-pandemic habit items between national groups. The article prints the effect size phi = .230 for the "Memorising comes easier for me" item, which the majority of Polish students disagreed with; the printed test is χ 2 (2, n = 206) = 10.889, p = .004.

"Regarding cultural differences, the only significant difference was found on the item "Memorising comes easier for me" ((χ 2 (2, n = 206) = 10.889, p = .004, phi = .230), which the majority of the Polish students disagreed with."
