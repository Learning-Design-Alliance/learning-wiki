## Evidence

### Hu Yanli 2019

Hu Yanli, Leeuwen René Van, Li Fan. (2019). Psychometric properties of the Chinese version of the spiritual care competency scale in nursing practice: a methodological study. BMJ Open, 9(10), e030497. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-030497

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the C-SCCS in the EFA sample (n=355). The article reports the alpha values of .93, .92 and .89 for the three subscales and a Guttman split-half coefficient of .84; item analysis found that "the deletion of any items in the scale would not have improved the Cronbach’s alpha".

> "The Cronbach’s alpha coefficients of the three subscales were .93, .92, and .89, and the Guttman split-half coefficient for the C-SCCS was .84"
