Daniel L. Reinholz, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer. (2017). Personalized instructor responses to guided student reflections: Analysis of two instructors' perspectives and practices. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/1.5002683](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5002683)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Self-reported instructor perspectives from hour-long post-semester semi-structured interviews. Taylor asked the authors to emphasize that short reflections were "one of the worst obstacles" to productive use of the GRF; one student never wrote longer reflections despite encouragement.
