## Evidence

### Scherer-Quenzer AC 2026

Scherer-Quenzer AC, Reinhart K, Diessner J, Altides A, Buechel J, Blau-Schneider B, Sauer ST, Deschler-Baier B, Woeckel A, Kiesel M. (2026). Comparative validation of a cost-effective in-house 3D-printed LLETZ simulator versus a commercial training model. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-026-08413-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 60 medical students without prior LLETZ experience assigned to a commercial or in-house simulator, five excisions each, evaluated with Mann–Whitney U test and Cohen's d. The article reports "adjusted p = 0.002" and "a Cohen’s d of 1.01" favoring the in-house simulator in attempt five.

> "A statistically significant difference between the two simulators was found in attempt five. The unadjusted p value was < 0.001 and the result remained significant after Holm adjustment (adjusted p = 0.002). The effect size was large, with a Cohen’s d of 1.01."
