Azimi 2026¶

Azimi, S. (2026). AI Literacy over Tool Design: A Mixed-Methods Study of Scaffolded versus Unrestricted Generative AI in Programming Education. Delft University of Technology. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16784

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Observational finding across the 33-student cohort: model understanding "varied widely across the cohort, and in every case it was self-taught, since the course did not teach it". The association with the highest scores is descriptive; no effect size is printed.

"The students with the best understanding of the models wrote more effective prompts and used the tools more deliberately, and they also achieved the highest assignment scores."
