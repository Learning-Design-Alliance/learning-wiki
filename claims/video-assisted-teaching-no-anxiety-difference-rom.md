## Evidence

### Melikoğlu Seçil Erden 2025

Melikoğlu Seçil Erden, Demircan Burcu, Yorulmaz Yasemin Kıyak. (2025). The effect of video-assisted teaching on nursing students'practice skills and anxiety levels in Range of Motion (ROM) exercises: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07714-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized controlled trial, the State Anxiety Inventory was administered to both groups (n = 36 each) in the laboratory before the ROM practice performance. The video group's mean was "42.36 ± 8.96 versus 45.00 ± 10.45 in the control group", with no statistical difference (p = 0.433). No effect size was printed for this comparison; the post hoc power for state anxiety was 21%.

> "The state anxiety scores of the video group students were 42.36 ± 8.96 versus 45.00 ± 10.45 in the control group. There was no statistical difference between the state anxiety scores of the groups (p = 0.433)"
