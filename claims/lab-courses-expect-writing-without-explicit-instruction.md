## Evidence

### Briley L. Lewis 2025

Briley L. Lewis. (2025). Surveying the State of Writing Education in Physics and Astronomy. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/453f-l8hy](https://doi.org/10.1103/453f-l8hy)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey question answered by 433/515 participants about whether laboratory courses included a writing component; descriptive percentages only. 64% (260/409) reported receiving feedback from a mentor, most often a faculty member (70%).

> "73% of respondents say they were expected to write in these courses, but never explicitly taught how to do so. 23% report both an expectation for writing and writing instruction in these courses, while 3% report lab courses without any writing and 1.4% did not take any lab courses."
