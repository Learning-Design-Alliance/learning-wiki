## Evidence

### Xie Dili 2026

Xie Dili, Sun Xuechun, Guo Jianshu, Liu Peng, Yang Junling, Zhong Ping. (2026). Integrating iHEC into standardized residency training for chronic disease management: a structural equation analysis of competency outcomes via digital health platforms. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1926948

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (5,000 bias-corrected resamples) within a structural equation model fitted with MLR estimation, showing a significant "indirect effect of the teaching model on educational performance through SDL" (β = 0.38); the direct effect remained significant, identifying SDL as a partial mediator.

> "The indirect effect of the teaching model on educational performance through SDL was statistically significant (β = 0.38; unstandardized B = 4.82; SE = 0.09; 95% bootstrap CI, 0.21–0.55; p < 0.001)."
