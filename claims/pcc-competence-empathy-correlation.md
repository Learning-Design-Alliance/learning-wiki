## Evidence

### Shen L 2024

Pearson correlation analysis in the same cross-sectional survey (n = 437). The article reports "a strong positive correlation (R = 0.700, P < .001) between PCC competence and empathy"; Table 3 shows all subdimension correlations significant at the 0.01 level.
