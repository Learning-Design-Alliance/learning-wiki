## Evidence

### Bielik Viktor 2025

Bielik Viktor, Nosáľ Vladimír, Nechalová Libuša, Špánik Milan, Žilková Katarína, Grendar Marian. (2025). The prediction model of academic achievement based on cardiorespiratory fitness and BMI status for ninth-grade students. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-024-05353-2

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression with fit-normal-weight as reference, mathematics outcome. Unfit boys with obesity showed significantly lower odds of the greatest achievement level, "OR = 0.38; 95% CI, 0.20–0.71; p = 0.003".

> "Simiriarly, in mathematics unFit + BwO compared to Fit + normal weight boys had significantly lower odds for having a greater level of academic achievement (OR = 0.38; 95% CI, 0.20–0.71; p = 0.003)."

### Bielik Viktor 2025 (2)

Bielik Viktor, Nosáľ Vladimír, Nechalová Libuša, Špánik Milan, Žilková Katarína, Grendar Marian. (2025). The prediction model of academic achievement based on cardiorespiratory fitness and BMI status for ninth-grade students. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-024-05353-2

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same regression, Slovak language outcome. Unfit overweight girls had significantly lower odds at levels 3 and 4 versus fit normal-weight girls, including "level 4 (OR = 0.17; 95% CI, 0.08–0.38; p < 0.001)"; mathematics level 4 was OR = 0.32.

> "Unfit + Overweight girls compared to Fit + normal weight girls had significantly lower odds for having a greater level of academic achievement in Slovak language level 3 (OR = 0.33; 95% CI, 0.15–0.72; p = 0.005); and level 4 (OR = 0.17; 95% CI, 0.08–0.38; p < 0.001)."
