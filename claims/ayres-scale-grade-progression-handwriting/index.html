Evidence¶

King 1912¶

King, I., & Johnson, H. (1912). The writing abilities of the elementary and grammar school pupils of a city school system measured by the Ayres scale. Journal of Educational Psychology, 3(9), 514–520. doi:10.1037/h0071006

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Grading of 966 specimens from grades 1–8 of one city system by a single judge who did not attend to age or grade. The article reports "the steady improvement manifest in the various grades from the 1st to 8B," with 5B the only marked exception.

"it is evident that a single judge can ap7)ly the scale with a fair degree of consistency, as is shown by the steady improvement manifest in the various grades from the 1st to 8B."
