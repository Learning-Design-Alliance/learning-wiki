## Evidence

### Obafemi 2015

Obafemi, D. T. A. (2015). Bridging Gender Gap in the Physics Classroom: The Instructional Method Perspective. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.20. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079043

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A 4x2 ANCOVA of students' understanding of Light waves classified by instructional methods and gender, using pretest scores as a covariate, in a quasi-experimental study of 55 SS2 physics students. The article reports "Gender is not significant" with F = 0.894 against a critical value of 4.00.

> "Gender is not significant since its calculated F 1,46 value is 0.894 at degree of freedom of 1,46 and probability level of 0.05 against the F 1,46 critical value of 4.00."
