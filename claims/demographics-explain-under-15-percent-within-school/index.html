Evidence¶

Jessaca Spybrook 2016¶

Jessaca Spybrook, Carl D. Westine, Joseph A. Taylor. (2016). Design Parameters for Impact Research in Science Education: A Multistate Analysis. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858415625975

q2 · i? · associational · r3

R2 estimation from conditional two- and three-level hierarchical models with student demographic covariates (gender, ethnicity, SES proxy, LEP status) across three state databases. In the two-level model, explanatory power is much larger at the school level, generally larger in Michigan and Wisconsin than in Texas.

"Across all grades, databases, and both models, less than 15% of the variance in students’ science test scores within schools is explained by the demographics."
