## Evidence

### Sun Yanxia 2017

Sun Yanxia, Pan Chuxiong, Li Tianzuo, Gan Tong J. (2017). Airway management education: simulation based training versus non-simulation based training-A systematic review and meta-analyses. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-017-0313-7

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis pooling six comparative trials of SBT versus NSBT for airway management. The review reports SBT had a favorable effect on behavior performance, with pooled fixed-effects SMD = 0.30 (95% CI 0.06 to 0.54); the article states "the effect size was small."

> "Six studies [9, 10, 12, 16, 17, 19] reported suitable data on behavior performance. SBT had favorable effect on behavior performance in comparison with NSBT controls. Pooled fixed-effects SMD was 0.30 (95% CI 0.06 to 0.54)"
