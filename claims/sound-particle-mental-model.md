# A particle mental model of sound propagation is stable, coherent and widely applicable at all educational levels

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students perceive sound propagation as travel of a multitude of tiny material entities with momentum, volume and shape, sent off spherically from the source; this model was confirmed at elementary, high school and college level. [→ Zdeslav Hrepic 2011](#zdeslav-hrepic-2011)

## Evidence

### Zdeslav Hrepic 2011

Zdeslav Hrepic. (2011). Students' Concepts in Understanding of Sound. Originally published 1998. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1105.3995](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1105.3995)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of open-ended written answers in the 1997 survey; no single question targeted this model, so no statistical weight is given. The author found the model "stable, coherent, and a widely applicable" across levels, consistent with prior reports by Linder, Maurines and others.

> "The idea about sound propagating as a particle was found to be stable, coherent, and a widely applicable alternative mental model of sound propagation at all educational levels."
