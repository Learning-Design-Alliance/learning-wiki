# Isolated EF interventions show robust near-term EF gains but poor transfer, including to early mathematics

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Isolated EF training improves the trained EF but shows poor near transfer and limited evidence of transfer to untrained functions such as mathematics, especially when computerised. [→ Scerif 2023](#scerif-2023)

## Evidence

### Scerif 2023

Scerif, G., Blakey, E., Gattas, S., Hawes, Z., Howard, S., Merkley, R., O'Connor, R., & Simms, V. (2023). Making the Executive 'Function' for the Foundations of Mathematics: the Need for Explicit Theories of Change for Early Interventions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09824-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of EF intervention studies and meta-analyses (e.g. Melby-Lervåg & Hulme 2013; Kassai et al. 2019), reporting "poor evidence of even near transfer" between EF components and limited transfer to mathematics.

> "while evidence for the immediate efficacy of isolated EF training on the trained EF seems robust even in pre-schoolers, there is poor evidence of even near transfer (from one EF to another, for example, from inhibitory training to working memory outcomes; e.g. Thorell et al., 2009)."
