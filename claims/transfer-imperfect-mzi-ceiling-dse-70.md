## Evidence

### Maries 2015

Maries, A., Sayer, R., and Singh, C. (2015). Investigating transfer of learning in advanced quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.047](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.047)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors' quantitative comparison of MZI post-test averages and DSE transfer question averages, given as part of their hypotheses about the observed transfer; it implies that transfer from the MZI to the DSE context is not perfect.

> "in the MZI post -test, student average scores were near the ceiling (~ 90%), while the averages on the DSE transfer questions were around 70% for both undergraduates and graduate students , implying that the transfer from the MZI to the DSE context is not perfect ."
