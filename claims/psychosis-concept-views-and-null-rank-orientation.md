### Urkin B 2025

Urkin B, Parnas J, Raballo A, Koren D. (2025). Clinical Activity and Psychopathological Knowledge Are Related to Real-World Performance of Leading International Psychiatrists in Diagnosing Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders. Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1159/000549313

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fisher's exact test on the dichotomized psychosis-definition variable in the 30-psychiatrist sample. The article reports the association was "close to but not statistically significant (p = 0.07)" with better performance for the mixed descriptive/etiological view.

> "The association with participants' concept of psychosis was close to but not statistically significant (p = 0.07). Participants who entertained a mixed descriptive/etiological view showed better diagnostic performance."

### Urkin B 2025 (2)

Urkin B, Parnas J, Raballo A, Koren D. (2025). Clinical Activity and Psychopathological Knowledge Are Related to Real-World Performance of Leading International Psychiatrists in Diagnosing Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders. Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1159/000549313

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fisher's exact tests on academic rank (professor vs non-professor) and main theoretical orientation in the same sample. The article reports both associations were "weak and nonsignificant (p = 0.34 and p = 0.41, respectively)."

> "The associations between accuracy and academic rank and orientation were weak and nonsignificant (p = 0.34 and p = 0.41, respectively)."
