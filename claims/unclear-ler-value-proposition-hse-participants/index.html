value-prop-1¶

Hodge, M., Shah, Z., Patterson, M., Luke Luna, C. (2023, September). Onboarding and Education Design Principles for Learning and Employment Records. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/194

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative key-finding analysis of workshop transcripts and storyboards from the two co-design sessions. The report lists "Unclear Value Proposition" among common challenges, noting participants with non-linear journeys "were unable to include their experiences during onboarding."

"Participants expressed a concern of how this tool was different from existing platforms like Indeed and LinkedIn. In addition, participants with non-linear journeys were unable to include their experiences during onboarding, giving the impression that it is not meant for individuals with non-linear journeys."
