## Evidence

### Sabourin 2013

Sabourin, J. L., Shores, L. R., Mott, B. W., & Lester, J. C. (2013). Understanding and Predicting Student Self-Regulated Learning Strategies in Game-Based Learning Environments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 23, 94–114. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0004-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA on Intrinsic Motivation Inventory subscales across SRL groups. "High SRL students reported experiencing significantly more interest and enjoyment, and attributed greater value and importance to the task".

> "ANOV A analyses indicated significant differences in the subscales of interest/enjoyment (F(2, 257) =9 . 4 4 ,p<0.01), effort/importance (F(2, 257) = 8.86, p<0.01), and value/usefulness ( F(2, 257) =1 2 . 2 3 ,p<0.01). Tukey post-hoc com- parisons indicated that High SRL students reported experiencing significantly more interest and enjoyment, and attributed greater value and importance to the task than either Medium or Low SRL students."
