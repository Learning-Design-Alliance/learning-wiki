## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Haută Alexandra 2024

Haută Alexandra, Iacobescu Radu-Alexandru, Corlade-Andrei Mihaela, Nedelea Paul Lucian, Cimpoeșu Carmen Diana. (2024). Translating training to medical practice in trauma care, a literature review. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-024-02548-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Prospective pre/post-test study in a level-1 trauma center reported by the review; ATLS training 'decreased time to secondary survey (14 to 6 min P = 0.004) and time to CT (23 to 16 min P = 0.01)'.

> "Long et al. show that ATLS trauma training programs decreased time to secondary survey (14 to 6 min P = 0.004) and time to CT (23 to 16 min P = 0.01) in a cohort of 67 trauma patients from a level-1 trauma center [12]."

### Haută Alexandra 2024 (2)

Haută Alexandra, Iacobescu Radu-Alexandru, Corlade-Andrei Mihaela, Nedelea Paul Lucian, Cimpoeșu Carmen Diana. (2024). Translating training to medical practice in trauma care, a literature review. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-024-02548-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Retrospective pre/post-test study cited by the review; the program showed 'a significant reduction from 22.3 to 18.6 min (P = 0.001) from emergency department reception to CT'.

> "A similar program using video-assisted debriefing and in situ simulations has shown a comparable effect with a significant reduction from 22.3 to 18.6 min (P = 0.001) from emergency department reception to CT [26]."

### Haută Alexandra 2024 (3)

Haută Alexandra, Iacobescu Radu-Alexandru, Corlade-Andrei Mihaela, Nedelea Paul Lucian, Cimpoeșu Carmen Diana. (2024). Translating training to medical practice in trauma care, a literature review. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-024-02548-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Prospective interventional study at a low-incidence level-2 regional hospital; the review reports training 'did not improve trauma overall processing time and time to CT'.

> "Kristiansen and Col. performed a prospective interventional study on the effects of ATLS training on team performance and patient-related outcomes in a low-incidence level-2 regional hospital and found training did not improve trauma overall processing time and time to CT [22]."
