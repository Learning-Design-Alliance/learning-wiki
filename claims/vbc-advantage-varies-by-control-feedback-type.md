## Evidence

### Anderson Steven Mark 2025

Anderson Steven Mark, Gillanders Sarahlouise, Hickey Anne, Offiah Gozie, Davis Niall. (2025). Video Killed the Radio Star—A Meta‐Analysis on Video‐Based Coaching to Improve Surgical Skill. World Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1002/wjs.12494

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis dividing control arms into three categories: no specific feedback; individualized feedback without video review; and supplementary material such as training videos or lecture slides. The largest advantage was against "controls who received no specific feedback", with effect sizes SMD 1.58 and SMD 1.26.

> "Significantly higher mean performance scores (SMD 1.58, 95% CI 0.45, 2.70, and p = 0.006) and mean change scores (SMD 1.26, 95% CI 0.40, 2.11, and p = 0.004) were seen in the VBC compared to controls who received no specific feedback."
