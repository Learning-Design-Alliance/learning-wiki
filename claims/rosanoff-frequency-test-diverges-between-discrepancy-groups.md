# The Rosanoff High Standard Frequency Test, but not writing-speed measures, diverges sharply between high-discrepancy and control students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Correlations between the Rosanoff High Standard Frequency score and scholarship differed markedly between groups: about 0.63 in the control group versus about 0.00 in the discrepancy group. [→ Stone 1924](#stone-1924)
`q2 i?` Correlations between normal speed of writing and scholarship and intelligence were low in both groups (about 0.10 or less). [→ Stone 1924 (2)](#stone-1924-2)

## Evidence

### Stone 1924

Stone, Charles Leonard. (1924). Disparity Between Intelligence and Scholarship. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational comparison of 45 control men (discrepancy not over 2 per cent) with the discrepancy group on five measurements. The printed table shows r Rosanoff High Speed Frequency with scholarship of 0.632±0.063 (control) versus 0.002±0.103 (discrepancy), and with intelligence 0.643±0.062 versus 0.036±0.101.

> "In the correlations there were marked divergences between the control group and the discrepancy group in only two of the five measurements"

### Stone 1924 (2)

Stone, Charles Leonard. (1924). Disparity Between Intelligence and Scholarship. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org
