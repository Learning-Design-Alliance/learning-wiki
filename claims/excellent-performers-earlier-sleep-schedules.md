## Evidence

### Manjareeka Magna 2025

Manjareeka Magna, Dasgupta Subhronil, Kanungo Pratyush, Das Rama Chandra. (2025). Perceived stress and academic achievement among medical students with different chronotypes: a cross sectional study on first year medical students from India. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07281-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent t-test of MCTQ sleep-wake variables between 54 Excellent and 48 Average performers. The article reports weekday wake-up means of "6:40 AM ± 2.7" versus "7:37 AM ± 1.9, p =.04".

> "Excellent performers had earlier weekday wake-up times (6:40 AM ± 2.7) than Average performers (7:37 AM ± 1.9, p =.04)."

### Manjareeka Magna 2025 (2)

Manjareeka Magna, Dasgupta Subhronil, Kanungo Pratyush, Das Rama Chandra. (2025). Perceived stress and academic achievement among medical students with different chronotypes: a cross sectional study on first year medical students from India. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07281-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same t-test comparison of weekend wake-up times between performance groups. The article reports "8:47 AM ± 2.12" versus "11:33 AM ± 1.41, p <.001"; weekend bedtimes and weekend sleep duration also differed significantly.

> "A similar pattern emerged for weekend wake-up times, with Excellent performers waking up significantly earlier (8:47 AM ± 2.12) than Average performers (11:33 AM ± 1.41, p <.001)."
