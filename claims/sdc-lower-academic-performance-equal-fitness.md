## Evidence

### de Greeff 2014

de Greeff, J. W.; Hartman, E.; Mullender-Wijnsma, M. J.; Bosker, R. J.; Doolaard, S.; & Visscher, C. (2014). Physical fitness and academic performance in primary school children with and without a social disadvantage. Health Education Research, 29(5), 853-860. https://doi.org/10.1093/her/cyu043

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of 544 primary school children (130 SDC, 414 non-SDC, mean age = 8.0 +/- 0.7) using fitness tests and scores on mathematics, spelling and reading. The study reports "SDC did not differ on physical fitness, compared with non-SDC, but scored significantly lower on academic performance." No effect size is printed for this contrast.
