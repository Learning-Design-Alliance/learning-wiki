## Evidence

### Kulnig Johannes 2018

Kulnig Johannes, Füreder Lisa, Harrison Nicole, Frass Michael, Robak Oliver. (2018). Performance and skill retention of five supraglottic airway devices for the pediatric difficult airway in a manikin. European Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-018-3134-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Manikin study of 41 pediatricians with randomized device and condition order, single insertion attempt per device, placement judged by two experienced anesthesiologists. The Results text states "only the AirQ, AuraG, and LT were inserted within 30 s correctly by all participants under physiologic conditions" and again under CS.

> "Of note, only the AirQ, AuraG, and LT were inserted within 30 s correctly by all participants under physiologic conditions. In TE, AirQ and LT had the highest success rate. In CS, AirQ, AuraG, and LT again all were inserted within 30 s."
