Reflexive thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke, 2006) of four open-ended survey questions, coded manually by the author. Student quotes illustrate bounded use, e.g. using AI like a tutor "As long as you're confirming accuracy" while synthesis remains "solely my own".

> "The qualitative analysis suggests not blanket distrust, nor straightforward rejection, but a more complex effort to keep AI useful while preventing it from becoming epistemically dominant. Four themes were especially prominent: conditional usefulness, verification as part of the work, fear of cognitive substitution, and successful-student identity under pressure."
