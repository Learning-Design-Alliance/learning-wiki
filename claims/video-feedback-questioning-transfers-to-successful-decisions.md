### García-González Luis 2013

García-González Luis, Moreno M. Perla, Moreno Alberto, Gil Alexander, del Villar Fernando. (2013). Effectiveness of a Video-Feedback and Questioning Programme to Develop Cognitive Expertise in Sport. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0082270

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

2x2 Mixed ANOVA on decision-making percentages measured by systematic observation of 4 pre-test and 4 post-test matches per player. The "interaction of Test-time and Group" was significant with printed effect size ηp 2=.682 (large, ≥.14); groups were equivalent at pre-test (p=.167).

> "Multivariate analysis shows a significant effect in the interaction of Test-time and Group (Wilks Lambda =.318; F(1,9) =19.318; p =.002; ηp 2=.682). Univariate analysis showed no significant differences between experimental and control groups in pre-test"
