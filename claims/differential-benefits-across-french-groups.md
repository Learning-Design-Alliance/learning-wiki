`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive and inferential pattern across the three language groups in the interrupted time-series: before the interventions both French groups differed from the German group but not from each other; the interventions produced the reported convergence pattern. No effect size is printed for this group-level pattern.

> "The first intervention led to an improvement in both French-speaking groups, with candidates from schools in the canton of Fribourg benefitting more than other French speakers. There were no longer reliable differences between the French-speaking group from Fribourg and the German-speaking group, but there still was an observable difference between the two French-speaking groups."
