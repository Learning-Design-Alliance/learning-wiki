## Evidence

### Sartania Nana 2014

Sartania Nana, McClure John D, Sweeting Helen, Browitt Allison. (2014). Predictive power of UKCAT and other pre-admission measures for performance in a medical school in Glasgow: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective observational study of one 2007 Glasgow cohort (n = 187 with complete confounder data) using SPSS GLM multiple regression on z-standardised measures. The mutually adjusted model shows UKCAT total beta 0.209 (p = 0.005) for EPM and beta 0.218 (p = 0.004) for H&C, with "the only pre-admissions measure to show an independent association with H&C". No standardised effect size was printed for this finding.

> "Thus, UKCAT total was independently associated with both composite measures in analyses before and after adjustment for gender, age, ethnicity and deprivation. It was the only pre-admissions measure to show an independent association with H&C."
