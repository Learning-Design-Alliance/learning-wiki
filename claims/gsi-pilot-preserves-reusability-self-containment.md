## Evidence

### Luis de-la-Fuente-Valentín 2011

Luis de-la-Fuente-Valentín, Abelardo Pardo, Carlos Delgado Kloos. (2011). Generic service integration in adaptive learning experiences using IMS learning design. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2010.12.007

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative technical evaluation from a 4-hour workshop case study with 22 teachers using the GRAIL engine in .LRN, each enacting an individual course instance with a Google Forms questionnaire. The authors report "no reported problems specifically related to the interaction among IMS LD and Google Forms" and that an automation script kept administrative costs reasonable.

> "Since there were no reported problems specifically related to the interaction among IMS LD and Google Forms, it can be said that GSI-shaped UoLs are as reusable as IMS LD courses are (RQ1)."
