### Rossettini G 2024

Rossettini G, Rodeghiero L, Corradi F, Cook C, Pillastrini P, Turolla A, Castellini G, Chiappinotto S, Gianola S, Palese A. (2024). Comparative accuracy of ChatGPT-4, Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini in the Italian entrance test for healthcare sciences degrees: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05630-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive classification of chatbot errors using the standardized categorization (logical, information, statistical error), rated by the same independent rater pair. Logical errors were defined as correctly identifying relevant information but failing to convert it into an appropriate answer.

> "With respect to category of errors (Table 3), in ChatGPT-4 (tot = 45), the main frequent reason was “Logical error” (n = 40, 88.9%), followed by a few cases of “Information error” (n = 4, 8.9%) and statistic (n = 1, 2.2%) errors."
