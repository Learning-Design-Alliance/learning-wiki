Cauet, E., & Kauertz, A. (2025). A Stimulus-Response Model for Explaining When Students Decide to Engage in a Science Task: Developing the TSMS Model Through Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.00490](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.00490)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument grounded in Thompson's (2009) metacognitive framework of reasoning. The article further specifies that 'Higher FOR should increase the influence of task-type-specific self-efficacy beliefs, value attributions, and cost attributions on task-related motivational evaluations'. No effect size is printed; this is a model assumption.

> "A metacognitive feeling of rightness (FOR) and feeling of difficulty (FOD) accompany the heuristic construction of the FIMM and specify task-related self-efficacy, value, and perceived costs."
