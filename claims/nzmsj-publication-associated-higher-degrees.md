## Evidence

### Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S. 2019

Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S., Wells Cameron I., Wilkinson Tim J. (2019). Publication in a medical student journal predicts short- and long-term academic success: a matched-cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1704-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Matched-cohort analysis of post-graduation higher-degree attainment among 49 NZMSJ authors and 98 controls using graduate qualification databases. Printed effect sizes: OR = 2.63 for any higher degree and OR = 9.21 for PhD completion; the PhD odds ratio is the larger of the two.

> "Compared with controls, NZMSJ authors were significantly more likely to obtain any higher degree (55.1% (n = 27) vs. 31.6% (n = 31), Univariate OR 2.63, 95% CI 1.30–5.32, p = 0.007), including PhD (16.3% vs. 2.0%, Univariate OR 9.21, 95% 1.88–45.05, p = 0.004) following graduation."
