## Evidence

### Wang Yizhen 2026

Wang Yizhen, Zhang Xiaomeng, Wang Chao, Xing Xiaoming, Nayak Vrinda. (2026). Online learning resource preferences across learning stages: a cognitive load theory perspective in UK medical students in early years. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09694-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA comparing mean usefulness ratings (out of 5) across three learning stages in the same survey sample. Mean scores were 3.17 ± 1.05 pre-class, 3.55 ± 1.09 post-class, and 3.65 ± 1.23 pre-exam; the text reports "pre-class vs. post-class, p = 0.0012" and "pre-class vs. pre-exam, p = 0.0138".

> "participants found the OLRs more useful after class and before exams than before class (pre-class vs. post-class, p = 0.0012; pre-class vs. pre-exam, p = 0.0138)"
