## Evidence

### Tran 2012

Tran, V. D., & Lewis, R. (2012). The Effects of Jigsaw Learning on Students' Attitudes in a Vietnamese Higher Education Classroom. International Journal of Higher Education, 1(2), 9. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v1n2p9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A pre-test/post-test non-equivalent comparison-group study with 80 final-year Vietnamese students matched into two groups of 40 by age, gender and GPA. The experimental group received six weeks of jigsaw instruction while the control group received lecture-based teaching. The article reports that the experimental group 'perceived their instruction as more cooperative and more student-centered' and showed significantly greater improvement on achievement and retention measures.

> "students in the experimental group (N = 40), who perceived their instruction as more cooperative and more student-centered, had significantly greater improvement on both achievement and retention measures than did the students in the control group (N = 40)"
