## Evidence

### Başaranoğlu Mert 2025

Başaranoğlu Mert, Akbay Erdem, Erdem Erim. (2025). AI-generated questions for urological competency assessment: a prospective educational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07202-x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis within the prospective rotation study of 42 interns, relating subspecialty clinical exposure hours to performance improvement. The article reports "r = 0.78, p < 0.001", a large correlation; the design is observational, not causal.

> "A strong positive correlation was observed between clinical exposure time and performance improvement (r = 0.78, p < 0.001)."

### Başaranoğlu Mert 2025 (2)

Başaranoğlu Mert, Akbay Erdem, Erdem Erim. (2025). AI-generated questions for urological competency assessment: a prospective educational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07202-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Secondary subgroup analysis of the 42-intern cohort comparing students with (n = 15) and without (n = 27) prior clinical exposure. The article reports p = 0.002 at baseline and p = 0.24 at final assessment; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Participants with prior clinical exposure (n = 15) showed higher baseline scores (mean: 52.3%, 95% CI: 48.7–55.9%) compared to those without prior exposure (n = 27, mean: 41.4%, 95% CI: 38.0-44.8%, p = 0.002), though this advantage diminished by the final assessment"
