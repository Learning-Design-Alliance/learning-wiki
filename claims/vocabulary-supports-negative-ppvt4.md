### McLeod et al. 2019

Ragan H. McLeod, Ann P. Kaiser, and Jessica K. Hardy. (2019). The Relation Between Teacher Vocabulary Use in Play and Child Vocabulary Outcomes. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121418812675

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed model analysis relating teacher vocabulary supports to standardized posttest raw scores for the 53 children, controlling for age and pretest. The article reports the PPVT-4 raw score "decreased by 0.41 (SE = .16; p < .05)" per additional support; no standardized effect size was printed.
