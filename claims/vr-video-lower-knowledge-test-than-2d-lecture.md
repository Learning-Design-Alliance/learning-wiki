## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Professional therapists randomly assigned to the 2D control group averaged 92.00% on the knowledge test while the 3D VR group averaged 77.80%, with the 3D group's scores more dispersed (10-25 points vs 20-25 points). [→ Killough 2024](#killough-2024)

## Evidence

### Killough 2024

Killough, D. E. (2024). The Ability of Virtual Reality Technologies to Improve Comprehension of Speech Therapy Device Training. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2404.15534](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2404.15534)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-video knowledge test in a randomized study of TalkTools clients: "19 professional therapists from the experimental "3D" group and 5 professional therapists from the control "2D" group participated in the survey." Scores were computed out of 25 points after one question was omitted; the quoted group averages are descriptive.

> "with 3D receiving anywhere from 10-25 points (with an average score of 77.80%), while 2D's was much more condensed from only 20-25 points (with an average score of 92.00%)."
