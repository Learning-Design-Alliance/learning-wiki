`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of test length and duration (in minutes) from the Nebraska Fall 2019/Winter 2020 data. The article reports similar magnitude and direction of duration differences in both terms, suggesting factors other than the Altair effect; small duration effect sizes (0.2–0.3) appeared in a few grades.

> "CBE and COLO have comparable test lengths averaging around 40–41 items per Reading test and 50–51 items per Mathematics test. Notable differences are observed for test duration as the CBE group has higher test durations than the COLO group in both terms and content areas, with the differences being more prominent in Reading."
