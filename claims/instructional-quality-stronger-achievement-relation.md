## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Math achievement relates more strongly to perceived instructional quality in math classes (r = .416) than to relations with math teachers (r = .132); language achievement shows the same asymmetry (r = .275 vs. r = .090). [→ Arens 2016](#arens-2016)

## Evidence

### Arens 2016

Arens, A.K., & Möller, J. (2016). Dimensional comparisons in students' perceptions of the learning environment. Learning and Instruction, 42, 22–30. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.11.001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Factor-correlation results (Model 2) in the N = 4926 sample show instructional quality more highly related to achievement than student-teacher relations. The article reports "higher relations to students’ perceived instructional quality in math classes (r = .416; p < .001)" versus r = .132 for math-teacher relations.

> "Math achievement demonstrated relatively higher relations to students’ perceived instructional quality in math classes (r = .416; p < .001) and relations to their math teachers (r = .132; p < .001) compared to instructional quality in language classes (r = .105; p < .001) and students’ relations to their language teacher (r = -.014; ns)."
