`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the SEM results by informant in the longitudinal sample. The article reports the asymmetry that "gains in caregiver-rated child well-being were linked with children's social competence but not their academic skills," and the reverse for child self-reports.

> "Specifically, gains in caregiver-rated child well-being were linked with children's social competence but not their academic skills. In contrast, gains in children's self-reported well-being were linked with their academic skills but not with their social competence."
