Evidence¶

Susana Sánchez Castro 2026¶

Susana Sánchez Castro, Mª Ángeles Pascual Sevillano, Rogelio Buceta Otero, Javier Fombona Cadavieco. (2026). Developing computational thinking in teacher education: a brief experience with coding, robotics, and machine learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10744-6

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Posttest comparison (Mann–Whitney U) of the experimental group (n = 105) against the non-equivalent control group (n = 39) on the knowledge dimension. The article reports "a small effect size (r =.29)" favoring the experimental group; no pretest was administered.

"The results reveal statistically significant differences between the experimental and control groups (U = 2821.5, p =.00046), with a small effect size (r =.29)."
