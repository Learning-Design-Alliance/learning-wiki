## Evidence

### Naghshi Maryam 2024

Naghshi Maryam, Janati Ali, Raoofi Samira, Khodayari-Zarnaq Rahim. (2024). Exploring the Challenges of Implementing Managers' Competency Assessment Center in the Health System: A Phenomenological Study. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v34i6.2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The training course challenges sub-theme came from content analysis of 33 interviews in the phenomenological study; participants called for courses to be made more operational and content updated. The article reports no measured effectiveness of the courses.

> "Lack of competency development programs for managers who need development, theory-oriented development courses, unit management for organizing AC, and conducting competence development courses, problems related to conducting face-to-face development classes, and inadequate content of development courses are the most important challenges related to the training and development of managers."
