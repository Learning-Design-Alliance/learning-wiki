### Shikino Kiyoshi 2024

Shikino Kiyoshi, Nishizaki Yuji, Kataoka Koshi, Fukui Sho, Yokokawa Daiki, Shimizu Taro, Yamamoto Yu, Nagasaki Kazuya, Kobayashi Hiroyuki, Tokuda Yasuharu. (2024). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06395-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional analysis of 4,677 resident physicians from 563 teaching hospitals who took GM-ITE 2022 and answered the CSV-IE free-text diagnosis question. The article reports "A total of 470 (10.0%) participants provided correct diagnosis" and the score contrast above; interrater reliability of CSV-IE scoring was κ = 0.92.

> "A total of 470 (10.0%) participants provided correct diagnosis. The mean (± SD) GMITE score of all 4,677 participants was 45.77 ± 8.13. GM-ITE scores in the correct CSV-IE group were significantly higher than in the incorrect CSV-IE group (50.03 ± 8.54 vs. 45.29 ± 7.94, p < .0001)."
