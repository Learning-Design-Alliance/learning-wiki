## Evidence

### Mabuza LH 2020

Mabuza LH, Mntla PS. (2020). Generalist practitioners' self-rating and competence in electrocardiogram interpretation in South Africa. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. 12(1), a2421. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v12i1.2421

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 93 generalist practitioners at a 2019 refresher course in Pretoria, using 20 ECG tracings reviewed by a specialist cardiologist. The article reports "274/1860 (14.7%)" correct answers combined across the three tracing categories.

> "The total number of correct answers (combining the three categories) achieved by all the 93 participating medical practitioners was 274/1860 (14.7%)."
