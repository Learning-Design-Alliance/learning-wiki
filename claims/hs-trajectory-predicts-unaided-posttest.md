## Evidence

### Abdelghani 2026

Abdelghani, R., Kaiser, P., Murayama, K. (2026). Changes in Help-Seeking Strategies Predict unaided Performance during AI-based Mathematical Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28472

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Hierarchical regression with N = 89 adding the two within-session shift indices (SRL and HS change) after pre-test and static features. The temporal block significantly increased explained variance: "∆R 2 =.096,F(2,81) = 5.43,p=.006".

> "after the static block had already been included, the temporal features increased explained variance fromR2 =.185toR 2 =.281,∆R 2 =.096,F(2,81) = 5.43,p=.006."

### Abdelghani 2026 (2)

Abdelghani, R., Kaiser, P., Murayama, K. (2026). Changes in Help-Seeking Strategies Predict unaided Performance during AI-based Mathematical Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28472

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Coefficient for the HS change score in the full model with N = 89. Students whose requests became relatively more understanding-oriented subsequently performed better without AI; the association was significant after adjustment (p = .007).

> "In the full model, the HS change score was positively associated with unaided post-test performance,β=.29, 95% CI[.10,.49],p=.003"
