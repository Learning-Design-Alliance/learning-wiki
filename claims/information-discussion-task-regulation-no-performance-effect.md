# Information discussion and regulation of task-related activities did not significantly predict group performance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Neither information discussion (β=.123, p=.56) nor regulation of task-related activities (β=−.036, p=.87) significantly predicted group performance. [→ Janssen 2010](#janssen-2010)

## Evidence

### Janssen 2010

Janssen, J., Erkens, G., Kirschner, P. A., & Kanselaar, G. (2010). Task-related and social regulation during online collaborative learning. Metacognition and Learning.

`q2 · i?`

Multiple regression on 101 groups' factor scores; both factors were non-significant predictors of group performance. The authors note this contradicts literature suggesting critical discussion and task regulation support collaboration; equivalence was not tested.

> "Furthermore, information discussion and regulation of task-related activities did not significantly predict group performance ( β=.123, p=.56, and β=−.036, p=.87 respectively)."
