## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental group's post-test scores improved significantly over pre-test in both vocabulary reading and sentence reading after instruction plus monitor training. [→ Qian Liu & Zunwei Fu 2011](#qian-liu-zunwei-fu-2011)
`q2 i?` The difference between the experimental and control groups was significant in both post-tests though not at pre-test. [→ Qian Liu & Zunwei Fu 2011 (2)](#qian-liu-zunwei-fu-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Qian Liu & Zunwei Fu 2011

Qian Liu & Zunwei Fu. (2011). The Combined Effect of Instruction and Monitor in Improving Pronunciation of Potential English Teachers. English Language Teaching, 4(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v4n3p164

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 30 juniors in the experimental group; paired samples tests on vocabulary reading (T2) and sentence reading (T3) against pre-test (T1), with the article reporting "considerable improvement in both T2 and T3". Table 4 prints t = 14.429 and t = 12.882, both sig. .000; no effect size is printed.

> "The results showed that its mean of T2 was 8.1677 and that of T3 was 7.533. As can be seen from table 4, the learners in this group had achieved considerable improvement in both T2 and T3, with that of T2 the most significant."

### Qian Liu & Zunwei Fu 2011 (2)

Qian Liu & Zunwei Fu. (2011). The Combined Effect of Instruction and Monitor in Improving Pronunciation of Potential English Teachers. English Language Teaching, 4(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v4n3p164

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples tests comparing the experimental and control groups on T2 and T3; Table 5 prints t = 13.309 (T2) and t = 10.747 (T3), df = 58, sig. .000. No effect size is printed, so impact is null.

> "Though the performances of these two groups were judged to be similar in T1, the differences between them proved to be significant in both T2 and T3."
