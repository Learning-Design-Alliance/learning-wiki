## Evidence

### Wilding 2024

Wilding, K., Wright, M., & von Stumm, S. (2024). Using DNA to Predict Education: a Meta-analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09928-4

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Multilevel meta-analysis of polygenic score predictions of educational achievement (school grades and test scores); the pooled estimate was ρ = 0.24, p < 0.001, "reflecting a positive relation that approximates a large effect size". All estimates but one (Rabinowitz et al., 2019) were positive and significant.

> "The meta-analytic association between polygenic scores and educational attainment was ρ = 0.24, p < 0.001 (95% CI from 0.18 to 0.30), reflecting a positive relation that approximates a large effect size"
