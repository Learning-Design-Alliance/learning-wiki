# Pre/post survey shows significant knowledge gain after the bilayer lab session

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2014 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Student knowledge scores of membrane biophysics improved significantly from pre-test to post-test after the hands-on bilayer session. [→ Vadim Shlyonsky 2014](#vadim-shlyonsky-2014)

## Evidence

### Vadim Shlyonsky 2014

Vadim Shlyonsky, Freddy Dupuis & David Gall. (2014). The OpenPicoAmp: an open-source planar lipid bilayer amplifier for hands-on learning of neuroscience. arXiv. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0108097](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0108097)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired pre/post True/False survey administered at the beginning and end of the laboratory session, scored 0 or 1 per answer with a knowledge score as the sum of correct answers. A paired samples t-test showed a significant difference (p = 0.0015, n=121), with means rising from "M = 7.12, SD = 1.79" to "M = 7.85, SD = 1.77". No effect size is printed.

> "There was a significant diﬀerence (p = 0.0015, n=121) in the pre-test (M = 7.12, SD = 1.79) and the post-test knowledge (M = 7.85, SD = 1.77), suggesting that the proposed material improved their knowledge of core concepts of membrane biophysics."
