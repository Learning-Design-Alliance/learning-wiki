## Evidence

### Xu Liyuan 2025

Xu Liyuan, Xu Qinrong, Liu Chunya, Chen Baozhen, Wang Chunxia. (2025). Virtual Standardized Patients for Improving Clinical Thinking Ability Training in Residents: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/73196

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Patient-examination assessment from the randomized controlled trial after 4 weeks of training, reported in Figure 3D-F. The VSP group showed "significantly greater improvement in physical examination ability" and in auxiliary examination ability; total objective examination improvement also favored the VSP group (Cohen d=1.16, P<.001).

> "the medical students in the experimental group showed significantly greater improvement in physical examination ability (mean 5.86, SD 1.44 vs mean 4, SD 1.34; F1, 59=26.41; Cohen d=1.11; 95% CI 4.50-5.36; P<.001) and in auxiliary examination ability (mean 6.11, SD 1.74 vs mean 4.15, SD 1.63; F1, 59=20.36; Cohen d=1.01; 95% CI 4.63-5.63; P<.001) compared to the control group."
