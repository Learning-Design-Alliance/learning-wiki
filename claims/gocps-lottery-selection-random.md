## Evidence

### Lisa Barrow 2018

Lisa Barrow, Lauren Sartain, & Marisa de la Torre. (2018). GoCPS: A First Look at Applications and Offers. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/gocps-first-look-applications-and-offers

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Validation analysis of GoCPS selection data checking whether lottery number assignment correlates with applicant demographics, prior achievement, or listed rank. The paper reports these are "unrelated to how high or low their lottery number is."

> "Student characteristics, inclu ding demographics and prior academic achievement, and the order in which students rank a program on their application are unrelated to how high or low their lottery number is."
