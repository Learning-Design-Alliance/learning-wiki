Drexel Carole, Jacobson Anne, Hanania Nicola A, Whitfield Ben, Katz Jay, Sullivan Thomas. (2011). Measuring the impact of a live, case-based, multiformat, interactive continuing medical education program on improving clinician knowledge and competency in evidence-based COPD care. International Journal of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease. https://doi.org/10.2147/COPD.S18257

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Matched-group comparison of 50 randomly selected CME participants with 50 demographically matched nonparticipants surveyed via case vignettes immediately after the programs. The article prints the effect size as a "50% nonoverlapping difference in the evidence-based clinical choices" (Cohen's d expressed as percent nonoverlap); no standardized d value is printed.
