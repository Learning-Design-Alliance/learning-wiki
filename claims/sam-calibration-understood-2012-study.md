### Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario 2014

Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario. (2014). Involving Users to Improve the Collaborative Logical Framework. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/893525

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory observational study at the 2012 Madrid Science Week with 17 participants (outcomes from 11 analyzed after excluding two pilot CLFs). SAM calibration used 8 emotionally standardized IAPS images; the article reports scores followed "the expected trend: decreasing levels of valence and increasing levels of arousal".

> "all the 11 participants that have been analyzed (except one of them in the valence and two in the arousal) seem to have understood the scale and properly assigned values to it as the graphics show that scores follow the expected trend: decreasing levels of valence and increasing levels of arousal."
