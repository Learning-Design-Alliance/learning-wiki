Knowledge acquisition and retention after a high flow training programme in Peru: a quasi-experimental single group pre–post design. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-035125

Across all six assessment points in the pre–post study, clinical application of knowledge median scores remained at or near 50% (IQR 0–50 at most time points); only the 12-month post-refresher comparison reached significance (p=0.010), while baseline (p=0.504) and 3-month (p=0.272) comparisons did not.
