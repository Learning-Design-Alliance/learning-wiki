### Bozavlı 2017

Ebubekir Bozavlı. (2017). Effect of Phonetic Association on Learning Vocabulary in Foreign Language. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i1.1968

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact groups of 33 Turkish 6th graders each after a three-hour vocabulary lesson. The experimental group used a phonetic association test with homophonous Turkish words; the control received conventional teaching. The article reports the first-week success-rate ranges: "the experimental group’s success rate ranged from 48% to 80%" versus 16% to 60% for controls.

> "In the study conducted during the first week, aiming to determine the rate of learning, the rate of the control group success ranged from 16% to 60% while the experimental group’s success rate ranged from 48% to 80%."
