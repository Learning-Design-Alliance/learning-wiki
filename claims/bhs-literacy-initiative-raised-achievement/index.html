Evidence¶

Noguera 2017¶

Noguera, P. (2017). Taking deeper learning to scale. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/deeper-learning-to-scale

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Case-study evidence from BHS, which had a 44% MCAS ELA failure rate and 75% mathematics failure rate when testing began. The report states that in the Literacy Initiative's first year "the failure rate on the MCAS was reduced by half, and the proficiency rate doubled."

"In the first year of the Literacy Initiative the failure rate on the MCAS was reduced by half, and the proficiency rate doubled."

Noguera 2017 (2)¶

Noguera, P. (2017). Taking deeper learning to scale. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/deeper-learning-to-scale

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Longitudinal case-study outcome data reported for BHS. By 2009, "78% of Brockton's 10th-grade students achieved either advanced or proficient levels in ELA," 98% of graduates passed the mathematics and English exam, and 97% of 2008 graduates went on to higher education.

"In 2009, 78% of Brockton's 10th-grade students achieved either advanced or proficient levels in ELA (matching the state percentage), and 60% achieved similar levels in mathematics."
