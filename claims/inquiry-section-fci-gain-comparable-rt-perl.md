## Evidence

### Beth Thacker 2013

Beth Thacker, Hani Dulli, Dave Pattillo, and Keith West. (2013). Lessons from a Large-Scale Assessment: Results from Conceptual Inventories. Physics Department, Texas Tech University. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1312.6000](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1312.6000)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post FCI assessment comparing the small inquiry-based, laboratory-based algebra-based section with large lecture sections using RT labs and PERL instruction; the article reports "0.34 +/- 0.02" and no significant difference. Equivalence was not formally tested beyond the stated comparison.

> "The mean FCI normalized gain for the Inquiry-based (INQ) class is 0.34 +/- 0.02 and not significantly different from that for the large lecture sections when both RT labs and PERL instruction are implemented."
