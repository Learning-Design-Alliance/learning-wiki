Secondary outcome analysis in the 192-video LAELAPS-3 cohort testing OSATS prediction of complications other than DGE. OSATS "showed a predictive value for grade B/C bile leakage (AUC: 0.676, P = 0.024)", but "showed no predictive value for other PD complications (POPF, postoperative pancreatic hemorrhage, nor complications Clavien-Dindo grade ≥3)".

> "Furthermore, OSATS showed a predictive value for grade B/C bile leakage (AUC: 0.676, P = 0.024), However, the RGE OSATS scores showed no predictive value for other PD complications (POPF, postoperative pancreatic hemorrhage, nor complications Clavien-Dindo grade ≥3)."
