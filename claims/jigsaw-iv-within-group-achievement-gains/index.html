Esin Özdemir 2016¶

Esin Özdemir, Ali Arslan. (2016). The Effect of Self-regulated Jigsaw IV on University Students' Academic Achievements and Attitudes towards English Course. Journal of Education and Training Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i5.1453

Paired samples t-tests within the quasi-experiment: experimental group t = -30.96, p = 0.000; control group t = -8.994, p = 0.018. The article states "the achievement post-test of the experimental group is significantly higher than their pre-test" and likewise for the control group.
