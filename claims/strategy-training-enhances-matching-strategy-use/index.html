Evidence¶

Banisaeid 2013¶

Maryam Banisaeid. (2013). Comparative Effect of Memory and Cognitive Strategies Training on EFL Intermediate Learners' Vocabulary Learning. English Language Teaching, 6(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n8p108

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of the post-questionnaire (27 statements, three-point Likert scale) comparing the two training groups on four strategy-use categories; the article reports significant levels of 0.000 for memory and cognitive strategy uses, with the memory-trained group higher on memory use (2.14 vs 1.82) and the cognitive-trained group higher on cognitive use (2.45 vs 1.43).

"The result shows that the uses of two strategies of me mory and cognitive are affected by two different cognitive and memory strategy training. By the overall mean, it can be deduced that memory strategies training and cognitive strategy training respectively enhance memory and cognitive strategy uses."
