> "Noah's Ark (Australia) conducted semi-structured telephone interviews with 14 families participating in the RBI bootcamp training. The families said it was cathartic, beneficial, and relaxing. They highlighted the positive impact of having a professional who was warm, caring, and personable really listen to them."
