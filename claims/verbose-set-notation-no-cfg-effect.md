## Evidence

### Schmalstieg 2025

Schmalstieg, F., Schmellenkamp, M., Schwerter, J., & Zeume, T. (2025). Difficulty Generating Factors for Context-free Language Construction Assignments. arXiv. [doi:10.1145/3702652.3744201](https://doi.org/10.1145/3702652.3744201)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fixed-effects panel regression across three task groups in the CFG panel. The aggregate verbose set notation coefficients were small and non-significant across all metrics, but Task group 3 showed significant task-level effects, stressing task-specific sensitivity to notation complexity.

> "Across Task groups 1–3, verbose instead of compact set notation (baseline) did not significantly impact aggregate performance or perception (Table 4). However, in Task group 3, students made more attempts, took longer, and were less likely to succeed on the first try"
