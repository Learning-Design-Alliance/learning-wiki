## Evidence

### Fortunka Kamila et al 2024

Fortunka Kamila et al. (2024). Knowledge and Training Needs in Nosocomial Infection among Hospital Staff in the City of Kielce, Poland: A Cross-Sectional Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/9243232

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Spearman's rho) in the 635-nurse survey relating rank-coded recency of last training to the raw knowledge index and its areas. All three correlations were statistically significant but very small in magnitude.

> "A statistically significant relationship was found: the higher the index of general knowledge (r = −0.080; p=0.044) and knowledge for area I (r = −0.081; p=0.041), the shorter the time since the last training. The subjective assessment of the respondents' own knowledge also increases (r = −0.183; p=0.001)"
