## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In Experiments 1a and 1b, search RTs were significantly slower in the presence of a high-reward distractor than low-reward and no-reward distractors, replicating value-driven attention to trained feature values. [→ Oudeng Jia 2025](#oudeng-jia-2025)
`q3 i?` A mixed ANOVA found no reward × experiment interaction between Experiments 1a and 1b in the feature-match condition. [→ Oudeng Jia 2025 (2)](#oudeng-jia-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Oudeng Jia 2025

Oudeng Jia, Qingsong Tan, Sihan Zhang, Ke Jia & Mengyuan Gong. (2025). The precision of attention selection during reward learning influences the mechanisms of value-driven attention. npj Science of Learning 10:49. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00342-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Controlled lab experiment (N = 40 per experiment) with a two-day training/test design. In the feature-match test condition, one-way repeated-measures ANOVAs on RTs showed a significant reward main effect, "F(2, 78) = 10.54,p < 0.001", with printed effect sizes ηp2 = 0.213 in Experiment 1a and ηp2 = 0.093 in Experiment 1b.

> "on RTs revealed a signi ﬁcant main effect of reward (Experiment 1a: F(2, 78) = 10.54,p < 0.001,ηp 2 = 0.213; Experiment 1b: F(2, 78) = 3.98,p = 0.023, ηp 2 = 0.093)."

### Oudeng Jia 2025 (2)

Oudeng Jia, Qingsong Tan, Sihan Zhang, Ke Jia & Mengyuan Gong. (2025). The precision of attention selection during reward learning influences the mechanisms of value-driven attention. npj Science of Learning 10:49. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00342-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-experiment comparison of the feature-value capture effect between Experiments 1a and 1b found "no signiﬁcant interaction effect (p = 0.968)" with Bayesian support (BF01 = 4.410), indicating the feature-value effect held at both similarity levels; no effect size was printed for this null.

> "A mixed two-way ANOVA (reward history: high- vs. low-reward × experiment: 1a vs. 1b) revealed no signiﬁcant interaction effect (p = 0.968), with moderate evidenc e supporting the null effect (BF01 = 4.410)."
