Grasby KL, Larsen SA, Gregory AM, Blunden S, Lewis KS, Madrid-Valero JJ, Coventry WL, Byrne B, Olson RK. (2026). Associations between sleep habits, performance in reading and mathematics, and inattention and hyperactivity. PLoS One 21(5): e0347892. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0347892

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

BW model results for parent-rated SWAN inattention and hyperactivity show sleep variables explain more variance in behavioral outcomes than academic ones, with hyperactivity variance "ranging from 3.4–7.4%" and inattention "ranging from 2.8–6.2%."

> "Taken together, the fixed effects for sleep explained a small amount of variation in inattention (ranging from 2.8–6.2%) and hyperactivity (ranging from 3.4–7.4%)."
