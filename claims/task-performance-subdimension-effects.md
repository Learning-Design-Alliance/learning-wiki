## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Groups in the match condition scored significantly higher on suitability, justification and correctness than groups in the conceptual condition, with trends versus the simulation condition. [→ B. Slof 2010](#b-slof-2010)

## Evidence

### B. Slof 2010

B. Slof, G. Erkens, P. A. Kirschner, J. G. M. Jaspers. (2010). Design and effects of representational scripting on group performance. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-010-9148-3

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVAs with Bonferroni post hoc analyses on the separate performance topics for 32 groups; match groups scored higher on suitability (d = 1.45), justification (d = 1.53; d = 1.29) and correctness (d = 1.83) than conceptual groups, with trends versus simulation groups.

> "condition effects were found for suitability (F(3, 28) = 2.99, p= .03), justiﬁcation ( F(3, 28) = 4.23, p = .01) and correctness (F(3, 28) = 2.99, p = .03). The mean scores indicated that there were several signiﬁcant differences between conditions."
