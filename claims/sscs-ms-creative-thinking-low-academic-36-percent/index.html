Yusnaeni 2017¶

Yusnaeni, Corebima, A. D., Susilo, H. & Zubaidah, S. (2017). Creative Thinking of Low Academic Student Undergoing Search Solve Create and Share Learning Integrated with Metacognitive Strategy. International Journal of Instruction, 10(2), 245-262. http://www.e-iji.net/dosyalar/iji_2017_2_16.pdf

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest nonequivalent control group study in three Kupang senior high schools analyzed by two-way ANCOVA and LSD post hoc test; the abstract reports the "36.18% higher" improvement for low academic students under SSCS+MS.
