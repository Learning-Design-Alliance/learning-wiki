## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The symmetric time-dilation question pair 5 and 6 was anti-correlated pre-test (r56,pre = −0.25, p = 0.02) but became the most highly correlated pair post-test (r56,post = 0.56, p ≤ 5 × 10−5), indicating the asymmetry misconception was reduced. `q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-test correlation analysis of the ANU Physics 2 class. The article reports "r56,post = 0 .56, signiﬁcant at the p ≤ 5 × 10−5 level", with post-test item difficulties P5,post = 0.83 and P6,post = 0.78 and normalized gains g5 = 0.54 and g6 = 0.67, against a pre-test anti-correlation of −0.25.
