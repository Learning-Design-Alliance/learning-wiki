Thomas 2026¶

Thomas, D.R., Borchers, C., Vanacore, K.P., Koedinger, K.R., & Kizilcec, R.F. (2026). Modernizing Ground Truth: Four Shifts Toward Improving Reliability and Validity in AI in Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29141 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2603.29141

A position paper on reliability and validity in AI in education. It reports, as a finding of prior work, that LLM pre-labels "can actually decrease human accuracy when the model is incorrect" because annotators uncritically adopt the AI's suggestion, and presents no study of its own. The study it cites is Wang, X., Kim, H., Rahman, S., Mitra, K., & Miao, Z. (2024). Human-LLM collaborative annotation through effective verification of LLM labels.
