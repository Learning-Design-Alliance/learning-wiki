Evidence¶

Termos 2013¶

Termos, M. H. (2013). The Effects of the Classroom Performance System on Student Participation, Attendance, and Achievement. International Journal of Teaching and Learning in Higher Education, 25(1), 66-78. http://www.isetl.org/ijtlhe/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre/posttest analysis using 10 copyrighted questions developed by biology faculty, administered at the beginning and end of the semester (115 experimental and 24 control students took the pretest). The control group started with the second-highest pretest average, yet every CPS section showed a larger percentage increase on the posttest.

"However, all experimental sections’ averages on the posttest were higher than that of the control group that had 74.74% increase even though the score average on the pretest was the second highest!"
