## Evidence

### Schripsema NR 2016

Schripsema NR, van Trigt AM, van der Wal MA, Cohen-Schotanus J. (2016). How Different Medical School Selection Processes Call upon Different Personality Characteristics. PLoS ONE 11(3): e0150645. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0150645

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA post hoc comparison within the same 1019-student cohort: non-participating lottery students scored lower on conscientiousness than both the top-GPA group and rejected-then-lottery students (MD -2.81 to -1.30, p<0.05).

> "The lottery group that had not participated in the multifaceted selection process had a lower mean conscientiousness score than the top pre-university group and the selection-rejected lottery group (MD: -2.81– -1.30; SE:0.47–0.60; p<0.05)."
