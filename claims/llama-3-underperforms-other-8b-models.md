Children's English Reading Story Generation via Supervised Fine-Tuning of Compact LLMs with Controllable Difficulty and Safety. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13709 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.13709](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.13709)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-model comparison in the Results section: Llama 3 stories "always underperform those from the other two LLMs" on most metrics outside the baseline, while Granite 3.3 and Apertus show no marked differences between experimental designs.

> "Across the three 8B LLMs we examined (except for our baseline experiment), stories generated by Llama 3 always underperform those from the other two LLMs on most evaluation metrics. In contrast, the evaluation results of stories produced by Granite 3.3 and Apertus do not show marked differences between the various experimental designs."
