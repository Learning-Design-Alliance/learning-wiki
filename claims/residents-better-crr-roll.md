Secondary-outcome analysis of CRR by surgeon level within the same 3,195-patient retrospective multicenter cohort. Residents' median CRR in ROLL was 2.0 versus attendings' 2.8, a statistically significant difference (p = 0.006); the article reports "residents achieved better results compared to attending surgeons in the ROLL group".
