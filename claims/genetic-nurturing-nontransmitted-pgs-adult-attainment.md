`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article reports this published genetic-nurturing design study, in which parental genotypes were split into transmitted and non-transmitted halves. "both sets of parent PGS predicted adult offspring 's educational attainment"; Kong et al. coined the non-transmitted effect "genetic nurturing". No effect size is printed.

> "what the researchers found was both sets of parent PGS predicted adult offspring 's educational attainment. The predictive value of the transmitted PGS was unsurprising, as this captures directly transmitted genetic effects."
