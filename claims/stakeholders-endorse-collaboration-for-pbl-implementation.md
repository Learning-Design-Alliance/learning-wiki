`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative component of a sequential exploratory mixed-method study: semi-structured individual interviews (n = 11) and focus group interviews (n = 33) with purposively sampled nurse educators from three universities and nurse managers from three hospitals. The article reports that "The results indicated the importance of intra-professional, inter-professional and inter-institutional collaboration." No effect size is printed.
