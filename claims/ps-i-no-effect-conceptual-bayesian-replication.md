# In a Bayesian-reasoning replication, problem-solving prior to instruction (PS-I) showed no significant advantage over instruction-first (I-PS) on conceptual knowledge

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Loibl 2024

Loibl, K., & Leuders, T. (2024). Can failure be made productive also in Bayesian reasoning? A conceptual replication study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09670-y

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Controlled experiment with 75 undergraduate teacher students in a 2×2 design (order of phases × type of instruction). The ANOVA on conceptual knowledge (max 4 points) found "no significant differences regarding the factor order of phases" (F(1,71) = 0.12, p = .73, ηp² = 0.002); equivalence was not tested.

> "For conceptual knowledge, the ANOVA revealed no significant differences regarding the factor order of phases (hypothesis 1a: F (1,71) = 0.12, p = .73, ηp 2 = 0.002)"
