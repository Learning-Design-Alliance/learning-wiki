EdTrust's analysis of Colorado public high school FAFSA rates under House Bill 21-1330's grant program, which applied only to grant-receiving high schools: "a small uptick in FAFSA submissions (0.8%) and completions (0.7%)." Descriptive percentages only.

"After the implementation of the Fund My Future grant for the class of 2022, Colorado saw a small uptick in FAFSA submissions (0.8%) and completions (0.7%)."
