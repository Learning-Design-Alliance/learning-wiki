## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Inhibition's relationship with mathematics achievement was fully mediated by number fact knowledge and calculation skills; indirect paths through algebra and geometry were not significant and the direct effect was no longer significant. [→ Spiller J 2023](#spiller-j-2023)

## Evidence

### Spiller J 2023

Spiller J, Clayton S, Cragg L, Johnson S, Simms V, Gilmore C. (2023). Higher level domain specific skills in mathematics; The relationship between algebra, geometry, executive function skills and mathematics achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0291796

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Parallel mediation model (n = 95) with inhibition as predictor. Total effect was .35; direct effect dropped to .09 (p = .173) after mediators were included, indicating full mediation via the two arithmetic-based skills.

> "For inhibition, there were significant indirect effects on mathematics achievement through number fact knowledge and calculation skills. The indirect paths through algebra and geometry were not significant. After controlling for the mediators, there was no longer a direct effect of inhibition on mathematics achievement"
