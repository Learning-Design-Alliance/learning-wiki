Evidence¶

Henry Cook 2023¶

Henry Cook, Tiffani Apps, Karley Beckman, Sue Bennett. (2023). Digital competence for emergency remote teaching in higher education: understanding the present and anticipating the future. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10194-4

q2 · i? · review · r2

Phase 1 thematic review of 15 empirical ERT studies (nine surveys and six case studies) found technology selection was shaped by these four factors. The review reports educators "refrained from adopting new technologies" and relied on existing day-to-day digital practices such as uploading recordings and synchronous messaging.

"Teachers selected technologies based on four factors: familiarity, availability, functionality, and technical competence. Teachers' selection of technologies for use during ERT was typically driven by familiarity with available technologies as several studies reported educators refrained from adopting new technologies to support learning and teaching"
