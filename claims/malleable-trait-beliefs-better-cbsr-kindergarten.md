## Evidence

### Compagnoni 2019

Compagnoni, M., Karlen, Y., & Maag Merki, K. (2019). Play it safe or play to learn: mindsets and behavioral self-regulation in kindergarten. Metacognition and Learning, 14, 291–314. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09190-y

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA with 147 kindergarteners controlling EF, gender, and age; CBSR was higher for children believing traits are malleable (M = 3.91) than stable (M = 3.65). The article prints the effect size partial η² = .034 (small), with no significant main effect of goal orientation (F(1,132) = 0.72, p = .399).

> "The analysis of variance for CBSR showed a significant main effect of trait beliefs on CBSR with a small to medium effect size (Cohen 1992) (F(1,132) = 4.67, p = .033, partial η 2= . 0 3 4 , f = .19)"
