A descriptive policy scan of eight states' community school investments based on ARP plans, legislative records, and state education agency websites. The report notes Illinois and Vermont exclusively draw on ESSER funds, New Mexico blends state and federal Title I funds, and Florida and New York's investments date back approximately a decade.
