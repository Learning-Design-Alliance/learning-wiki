Evidence¶

Maier 2017¶

Maier, A., Daniel, J., Oakes, J., & Lam, L. (2017). Community schools as an effective school improvement strategy: A review of the evidence. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/community-schools-effective-school-improvement-report

q2 · i? · review · r3

The report's synthesis finding for Pillar 2, drawing on 24 studies including 14 research syntheses of expanded learning time and opportunities. The review reports an association with positive academic and nonacademic outcomes; no pooled effect size is printed.

"Thoughtfully designed expanded learning time and opportunities provided by community schools—such as longer school days and academically rich and engaging after-school, weekend, and summer programs—are associated with positive academic and nonacademic outcomes, including improvements in student attendance, behavior, and academic achievement."

q2 · i? · review · r3

A moderator statement from the same synthesis finding: the review reports that study design quality relates to effect magnitude for expanded learning time, with the best-designed studies showing the strongest positive effects. No effect size is printed.

"Notably, the best-designed studies show the strongest positive effects."
