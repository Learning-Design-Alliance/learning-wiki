## Evidence

### Hecht 2023

Hecht, C. A., Murphy, M. C., Dweck, C. S., Bryan, C. J., Trzesniewski, K. H., Medrano, F. N., Giani, M., Mhatre, P., & Yeager, D. S. (2023). Shifting the mindset culture to address global educational disparities. npj Science of Learning 8:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00181-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Correlational field study at a large four-year university measured the mindset beliefs of 150 STEM professors and linked them to the outcomes of over 15,000 students. The article reports the gap was "0.19 GPA points lower" in fixed-mindset courses versus "0.10 GPA points" in growth-mindset courses.

> "In courses taught by instructors with more of a fixed mindset, underrepresented racial/ethnic minority students received grades that were 0.19 GPA points lower than their racial/ethnic majority peers, on average, whereas this gap was roughly half of that in courses taught by instructors with more of a growth mindset (0.10 GPA points)."
