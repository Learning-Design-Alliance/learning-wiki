### Menken 2022

Peer victimization (bullying) on mental health, behavioral problems, cognition, and academic performance in preadolescent children in the ABCD Study. Frontiers in Psychology, 13, 925727. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.925727

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional ABCD analysis of bullying-by-sex interactions on CBCL scores. The study reports "interactions were found between bullying status and sex" with ΔR² = 0.001, p < 0.01, and "bullied boys having greater problems than the bullied girls" in four syndrome scales.

> "interactions were found between bullying status and sex in CBCL scales for withdrawn/depressed, social, thought, and aggression (Figure 3C; ΔR2 = 0.001, p < 0.01), with the bullied boys having greater problems than the bullied girls in each of these syndrome scales"
