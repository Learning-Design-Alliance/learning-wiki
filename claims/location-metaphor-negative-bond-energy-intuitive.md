### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2013

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Benjamin D. Geller, Julia Gouvea, Vashti Sawtelle, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2013). Negative Energy: Why Interdisciplinary Physics Requires Multiple Ontologies. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.020](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.020)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interview data: Betsy compared two choices of zero on a potential energy diagram similar to Figure 1, saying that taking the well bottom as ground makes falling into a well easier to grasp than taking the well top as ground. The authors note this contrasts with the expectation that students generally have more difficulty with negative energy.

> "Because of the potential well metaphor, Betsy ac- tually finds it more intuitive to think of the bond po- tential energy as negative than as positive."
