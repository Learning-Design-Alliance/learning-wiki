# SDL-R shows a consistent performance-linked gradient across perceived class rank and self-reported GPA terciles, most pronounced in planning and implementation

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Boadum O 2026

Boadum O, Wilson TD, Thompson K, Robertson EM, Notebaert A. (2026). Self-directed learning readiness in preclinical medical education: Patterns, variation, and academic outcomes from a mixed longitudinal and cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09867-4

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional one-way ANOVA across perceived rank categories in the full sample (lower 25% n = 114; middle 50% n = 456; top 25% n = 237). The overall effect was large (η² = 0.102); domain analyses confirmed significant gradients for LM, PI, and SM but not IC.

> "perceived rank categories (top 25%, middle 50%, lower 25%) showed a significant SDL-R gradient (lower 25%: M = 75.72; middle 50%: M = 81.50; top 25%: M = 86.12; F(2, 804) = 45.80, p < .001, η² = 0.102; Fig. 4)"

### Boadum O 2026 (2)

Boadum O, Wilson TD, Thompson K, Robertson EM, Notebaert A. (2026). Self-directed learning readiness in preclinical medical education: Patterns, variation, and academic outcomes from a mixed longitudinal and cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09867-4

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across self-reported GPA terciles (n = 546). Domain analyses confirmed significant effects for LM, PI, and SM; the PI gradient (lower M = 21.58; top M = 25.20) was described as particularly pronounced. IC showed no significant gradient.

> "Total SDL-R differed significantly across GPA terciles (lower: M = 79.07; middle: M = 82.26; top: M = 85.71; F(2, 543) = 20.26, p < .001, η² = 0.069)."
