# Learning motivation, confidence in practice, and class satisfaction are strongly positively intercorrelated after mobile video learning

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In the intervention group, learning motivation correlated with class satisfaction (r = 0.725) and confidence in practice (r = 0.717), and confidence correlated with satisfaction (r = 0.772). [→ Lee Nam-Ju 2016](#lee-nam-ju-2016)

## Evidence

### Lee Nam-Ju 2016

Lee Nam-Ju, Chae Sun-Mi, Kim Haejin, Lee Ji-Hye, Min Hyojin Jennifer, Park Da-Eun. (2016). Mobile-Based Video Learning Outcomes in Clinical Nursing Skill Education: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Computers, Informatics, Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1097/CIN.0000000000000183

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations computed among posttest variables in the intervention group (n = 36). Motivation-satisfaction "r = 0.725, P < .001", motivation-confidence r = 0.717, confidence-satisfaction r = 0.772, all statistically significant.

> "There were significant positive correlations for learning motivation with class satisfaction (r = 0.725, P < .001) and confidence in practice (r = 0.717, P < .001). Lastly, there was a significant positive correlation between class satisfaction and confidence (r = 0.772, P < .001)."
