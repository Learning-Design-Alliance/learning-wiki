## Evidence

### Chen Lulin 2025

Chen Lulin, Li Yizhao, Guo Xiaoyan, Liu Wei. (2025). Comparison of case-based learning and traditional lecture in teaching residents on research misconduct: a controlled before-and-after study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0322336

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null within-group findings from the matched before-and-after study (Table 4): control-group agreement rates for these three severe behaviors showed no statistically significant change, while CBL-group rates fell significantly. No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "traditional lecture has not shown statistically significant improvements in participants' attitudes towards these three behaviors, indicating that solely relying on traditional lecture for moral education or behavioral norm promotion may not effectively change participants' understanding and attitude towards these unethical practices."
