Confirmatory factor analysis in Amos with 100 elementary children (51 male, 49 female, ages 8 to 13) from Ciudad Juárez. The article reports that "Six of the eight scales of the PALS used in this study were validated" under the Hooper et al. criteria, with fit indices printed per scale in Figs. 2–9.
