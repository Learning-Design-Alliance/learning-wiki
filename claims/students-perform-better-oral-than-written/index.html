Fenton 2025¶

Fenton, A. (2025). Reconsidering the Use of Oral Exams and Assessments: An Old Way to Move Into a New Future. Educational Researcher, 54(7), 430-436. https://doi.org/10.3102/0013189X251333638

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of comparison studies, citing Burke-Smalley (2014) and Roecker (2007), reports students performed better on oral measures than written measures. No effect sizes are printed; the review attributes the finding to the cited studies.

"Many studies have compared written assessments to oral ones and found students performed better on oral measures than written measures (e.g., Burke-Smalley 2014; Roecker, 2007)."
