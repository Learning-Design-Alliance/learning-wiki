## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Day Alison J. 2021

Day Alison J., Fenn Kimberly M., Ravizza Susan M. (2021). Is it worth it? The costs and benefits of bringing a laptop to a university class. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251792

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational correlation between tracked class-related and off-task laptop durations across the semester; the measures were independent since laptop use did not fill class time. The article reports the variables "were negatively correlated, r(101) = -0.44, p < 0.001".

> "Contrary to our prediction, these variables were negatively correlated, r(101) = -0.44, p < 0.001. Thus, participants who spent more time using their laptops for class-related activities tended to spend less time doing off-task activities."
