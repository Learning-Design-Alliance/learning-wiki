# Embodied learning instruction produced large, baseline-adjusted improvements in nursing students' attitudes toward educational measurement compared to traditional lectures.

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In a pretest-posttest randomized controlled design with 31 senior nursing students, the embodied learning group showed a statistically significant group effect on attitudes toward educational measurement after ANCOVA adjustment and Bonferroni correction (F(1,28)=36.311, p<0.001, ηp²=0.565). ## Evidence

### Tutal Özgür 2026

Tutal Özgür. (2026). Concretizing the abstract: embodied learning as a pedagogical strategy for teaching measurement and evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10321-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Prespecified baseline-adjusted one-way ANCOVA with Bonferroni correction (α=0.017) on the attitudes toward educational measurement inventory, with group as fixed factor and pre-test score as covariate, yielding ηp² = 0.565. The embodied learning group's post-survey mean was 144.87 (SD=7.88) versus 127.50 (SD=9.40) for the comparison group.

> "For attitudes toward educational measurement, a significant group effect emerged, F (1, 28) = 36.311, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.565, indicating that the treatment group scored substantially higher than the comparison group at post-test after controlling for pre-test attitudes."
