Rodríguez Rincón, Y., Munárriz, A., Campión Arrastia, M. J., & Goicoechea López-Vailo, M. I. (2025). Instructional design for tutoring on interactive platforms: creating educational interventions overcoming the digital gap. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10516-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The article reports Attali's (2015) approach: feedback on each possible error in multiple-choice questions improves performance on same-topic problems because feedback prompts reflection on mistakes and greater effort on subsequent questions.

> "According to this idea, direct feedback on each possible error in this type of question not only promotes an improvement in the performance of problems on the same topic but also favors more effective learning than when no such feedback is received."
