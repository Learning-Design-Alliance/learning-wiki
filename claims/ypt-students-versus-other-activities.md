`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same 62-student subset survey. The article interprets the neutral result as emphasizing YPT's impact, since its contribution is practically comparable to a broad spectrum of other activities possibly directly targeting some of the selected skills.

> "When comparing the contribution of YPT to that of other activities, there is a weak but clear influence of YPT in the case of high-level hard skills. The perception of YPT for the development of other curriculum-related hard skills as well as soft skills is within statistical errors comparable to that of other activities."
