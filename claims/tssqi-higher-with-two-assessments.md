## Evidence

### Mueller Sophia 2011

Mueller Sophia, Weichert Nina, Stoecklein Veit, Hammitzsch Ariane, Pascuito Giulia, Krug Christian, Holzer Matthias, Pfeiffer Mona, Siebeck Matthias, Schmidmaier Ralf. (2011). Evaluation of effectiveness of instruction and study habits in two consecutive clinical semesters of the medical curriculum munich (MeCuM) reveals the need for more time for self study and higher frequency of assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-62

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Wilcoxon rank sum test with continuity correction comparing TSS/QI between the surgical semester (one assessment) and internal medicine semester (two assessments), with Bonferroni correction. Groups were "significantly different when compared globally and at week 8"; no effect size was printed.

> "TSS/QI differed between the group with one assessment and the group with two assessments per semester. Groups were significantly different when compared globally and at week 8, and not different when compared at week 1 and week 15. Adjustment for multiple testing was applied."
