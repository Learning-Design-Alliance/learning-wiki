# Procedural video and live demonstration produce equivalent blind-assessed Adam's Clasp fabrication scores in fourth-year dental students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Fourth-year dental students randomly assigned to watch a procedural video scored similarly on blind-assessed Adam's Clasp fabrication to students attending a live demonstration (means 6.69 vs 6.78; P = 0.864). [→ Alqahtani ND 2015](#alqahtani-nd-2015)

## Evidence

### Alqahtani ND 2015

Alqahtani ND, Al-Jewair T, AL-Moammar K, Albarakati SF, ALkofide EA. (2015). Live demonstration versus procedural video: a comparison of two methods for teaching an orthodontic laboratory procedure. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0479-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 49 fourth-year male dental students assigned to live demonstration (n = 26) or procedural video (n = 23); blind assessment by one faculty member. "No significant difference was detected between the two groups"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The mean students’ scores in the fabrication of the Adam’s clasp were 6.69 and 6.78 for the live demonstration (group A) and the procedural video (group B), respectively. No significant difference was detected between the two groups (P = 0.864)."
