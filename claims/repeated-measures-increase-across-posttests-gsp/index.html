Poh Geik Tieng & Leong Kwan Eu 2013¶

Poh Geik Tieng & Leong Kwan Eu. (2013). Improving Students' Van Hiele Level Of Geometric Thinking Using Geometer's Sketchpad. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 2(3). www.mojet.net. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086363

q2 · i? · causal · r1

One-way repeated measures ANOVA on the experimental group's three posttests (n = 16). The article states "the interventio n using Geometer's Sketchpad helped the students improve their van Hiele level of geometric thinking" across successive lessons.

"The results indicated that there weresignificant differences in the students' van Hiele's level of geometric thinking during posttest 1 (M = 1.31, SD = 0.79), posttest 2 (M = 1.81, SD = 0.54), and posttest 3 (M = 2.00, SD = 0.00), F (1.29, 19.29) = 5.71, p = .02"
