# Lexia Reading has potentially positive effects on alphabetics for beginning readers

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2009

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2009). WWC Intervention Report: Lexia Reading, Beginning Reading. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/274

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

WWC synthesis of two studies meeting evidence standards and one meeting standards with reservations in the alphabetics domain. The WWC-computed domain average effect size was 0.27, not statistically significant, and the rating was potentially positive; the report states the "average improvement index for alphabetics is +11 percentile points."

> "The average improvement index for alphabetics is +11 percentile points across the two studies, with a range of –31 t o +50 percentile points across findings."
