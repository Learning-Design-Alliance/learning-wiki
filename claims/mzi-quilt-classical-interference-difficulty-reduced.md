## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2014

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2014). Developing an Interactive Tutorial on a Mach-Zehnder Interferometer with Single Photons. 2014 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.056

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Question 1 of the pre-/posttest assessed understanding of classical interference of light in the MZI; Table 1 reports the difficulty at 78% on the pretest and 33% on the posttest. The article notes the average posttest percentage for Q1 was 58 (Table 2), and that "many students still had difficulty with Questions 1 and 4 on the posttest."

> "78% of the students incorrectly agreed with this statement on the pretest (see Table 1), indicating that they did not take into account the interference phenomenon taking place at the detectors. After working on the QuILT, this difficulty was reduced."
