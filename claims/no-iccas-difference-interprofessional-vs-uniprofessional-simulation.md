## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Final-year medical and nursing students assigned to interprofessional simulation showed no greater ICCAS gain than those in uniprofessional training (p = 0.181 medical, p = 0.441 nursing). [→ Krielen P 2023](#krielen-p-2023)

## Evidence

### Krielen P 2023

Krielen P, Meeuwsen M, Tan ECTH, Schieving JH, Ruijs AJEM, Scherpbier ND. (2023). Interprofessional simulation of acute care for nursing and medical students: interprofessional competencies and transfer to the workplace. BMC Medical Education 23, 105. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04053-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 205 students completing ICCAS before and after training, assigned pragmatically to interprofessional (intervention) or uniprofessional (control) simulation. The study reports "no differences in total ICCAS scores" between groups; Table 2 prints difference p-values of 0.181 (medical) and 0.441 (nursing).

> "Comparison of the control and intervention groups for medical and nursing students showed no differences in total ICCAS scores."
