## Evidence

### Onishi Shun 2026

Onishi Shun, Sugita Koshiro, Murakami Masakazu, Yano Keisuke, Harumatsu Toshio, Tabata Yumiko, Tsuruno Yudai, Kedoin Chihiro, Nagano Ayaka, Kawano Takafumi, Ieiri Satoshi. (2026). Objective suturing skill assessment in a hands-on course increases medical students’ aspiration to become surgeons. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39306-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures observational study of 185 fifth-year medical students during pediatric surgery clerkship; VAS aspiration surveyed before and after a two-hour suturing session. The article reports the median increased from 60 to 65, "significantly increased to 65 (range:45-85) after training (p<0.001)"; no effect size printed.

> "The median of VAS score for aspiration to become a surgeon before training was 60 (range: 35-79) and significantly increased to 65 (range:45-85) after training (p<0.001)"
