`q3 i2` Explicit aim was significantly larger in the Japanese group than in the Norwegian group in both early and late adaptation periods (ηp² = 0.11). [→ Yamada 2025](#yamada-2025)

## Evidence

### Yamada 2025

Yamada, C., Itaguchi, Y., & Rodríguez-Aranda, C. (2025). Unconscious cultural cognitive biases in explicit processes of visuomotor adaptation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00335-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

A two-way mixed ANOVA on explicit aim (verbal reports converted to degrees) in a visuomotor adaptation experiment with 24 Norwegian and 24 Japanese students found a significant cultural group effect, "ηp 2 =0 . 1 1" (ηp² = 0.11), with larger Japanese means (early M = −13.93°) than Norwegian (early M = −6.42°).
