## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Participants fixated local landmarks 144% more often without a VGL, indicating a shift in the spatial information processed under the two conditions. [→ Jia Liu 2022](#jia-liu-2022)

## Evidence

### Jia Liu 2022

Jia Liu, Avinash Kumar Singh, Anna Wunderlich, Klaus Gramann and Chin-Teng Lin. (2022). Redesigning navigational aids using virtual global landmarks to improve spatial knowledge retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00132-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Eye-tracker fixation heatmap analysis from 156 trials per landmark condition across participants, analysed with a 2 × 11 factorial repeated-measures ANOVA. Main effect of trial condition: F1,25 = 68.721, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.733; landmark identity: F10,250 = 16.649, partial η² = 0.400.

> "participants more often ﬁxated on the local landmarks when they did not have a VGL to orient to, leading to a 144% higher number of ﬁxations on local landmarks"
