## Evidence

### Devine A 2012

Devine A, Fawcett K, Szűcs D, Dowker A. (2012). Gender differences in mathematics anxiety and the relation to mathematics performance while controlling for test anxiety. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-8-33

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analyses in the girls' subsample found TA negatively correlated with mathematics performance, Pearson r = −.207, p = 0.008; Spearman R = −0.195.

> "TA was also negatively correlated with maths performance (Pearson r = −.207; p = 0.008. Spearman R = −0.195; p <0.05)."

### Devine A 2012 (2)

Devine A, Fawcett K, Szűcs D, Dowker A. (2012). Gender differences in mathematics anxiety and the relation to mathematics performance while controlling for test anxiety. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-8-33

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analyses in the boys' subsample found the TA-performance correlation only marginally significant, Pearson r = −0.110, p = 0.07. In opposing partial correlations, performance was not correlated with TA in either gender.

> "In contrast to girls, the correlation of TA and performance was only marginally significant in boys (Pearson r = −0.110; p = 0.07; Spearman R = −0.106; p <0.07)."
