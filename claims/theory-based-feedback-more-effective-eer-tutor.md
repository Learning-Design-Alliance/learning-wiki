# Theory-based feedback in EER-Tutor was more effective in supporting learning than the original designer-intuition feedback

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who received theory-based feedback (blame assignment plus remediation of the violated domain principle, per learning from performance errors theory) learned constraints faster than students who received the original EER-Tutor feedback. ## Evidence

### Antonija Mitrovic & Pramuditha Suraweera 2016

Antonija Mitrovic & Pramuditha Suraweera. (2016). Teaching Database Design with Constraint-Based Tutors. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:448–456. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0084-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A study comparing EER-Tutor to a version providing theory-based feedback re-engineered according to Ohlsson's theory of learning from performance errors. The article reports that "theory-based feedback is more effective in supporting learning"; no effect size is printed.

> "The results of evaluation showed that theory-based feedback is more effective in supporting learning: students who received such feedback learned constraints faster than the students who received the original EER-Tutor feedback (Zakharov et al. 2005)."
