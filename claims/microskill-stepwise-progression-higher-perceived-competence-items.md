## Evidence

### Wu Zongxi 2026

Wu Zongxi, Wang Mingyue, Luo Xueting, Liu Xiangqi, Yang Le, Gao Siyong, Zhang Sien, Zheng Guangsen. (2026). Structured Microskill Stepwise Progression for Undergraduate Tooth Extraction: A Randomized Mixed-Methods Study. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.111097

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory item-level analysis of a 15-item questionnaire using Mann–Whitney U tests with Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction. Four items remained significant after correction. The authors state these findings should be regarded as

> "Before correction, six questionnaire items showed nominally significant between-group differences; after Benjamini–Hochberg false discovery rate correction, four items remained statistically significant: independence in basic procedures, competence in simple extractions, initiative in clinical learning, and objectivity of assessment criteria."
