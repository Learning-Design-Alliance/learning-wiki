## Evidence

### Hou Jie 2026

Hou Jie, Huang Yanhong, Wang Qing, Yao Xiaoxue, Zhang Peizheng, Zhao Fengqing. (2026). AI Dependence and Self-Reported Creativity Among Chinese College Students: The Roles of Motivation for AI Use and Perceived AI Literacy. Behavioral Sciences, 16(9), 1712. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091712

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 5764 Chinese university students; observed-variable parallel mediation in lavaan with 5000 bootstrap resamples. The article reports the total effect "was significant and negative (β = −0.165" with the direct effect β = −0.122 remaining after mediators were controlled.

> "The total effect of AI dependence on self-reported creativity was significant and negative (β = −0.165, Boot SE = 0.013, 95% Boot CI [−0.195, −0.143], p < 0.001)."
