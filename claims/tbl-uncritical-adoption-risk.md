The review's concluding argument, an interpretive claim (type e) drawn from its finding that research is framed by an assumption of TBL's value. It warns the absence of deeper understanding could continue a pattern of uncritical, potentially sub-optimal adoption by medical schools.

> "it may be encouraging uncritical adoption of TBL which may not be fit‐for‐purpose in some settings but nonetheless is transported, rather than translated into local educational practice, and (2) it has hindered the critical examination and therefore greater understanding of the nature and effects of this pedagogical intervention on teaching and learning processes."
