## Evidence

### Kail 1991

Robert Kail. (1991). Developmental Change in Speed of Processing During Childhood and Adolescence. Psychological Bulletin, 109(3), 490-501. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.109.3.490

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Secondary analysis of the Kail (1986a) practice study, in which 9-year-olds, 13-year-olds, and adults received 240 mental-rotation trials daily for 16 days; RTs from first and last days (24 means per age) were compared with Equation 6 predictions (m9.5 = 1.72, m13.3 = 1.33). For 9-year-olds expected RTs accounted for 92.66% of variance, slightly underestimating unpracticed performance.

> "For 13-year-olds, the expected RTs account for 94.54% of the variance in youths' actual RTs, with no evidence of systematic mispredictions of those RTs."
