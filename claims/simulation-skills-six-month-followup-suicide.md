## Evidence

### Richard Océane 2023

Richard Océane, Jollant Fabrice, Billon Grégoire, Attoe Chris, Vodovar Dominique, Piot Marie-Aude. (2023). Simulation training in suicide risk assessment and intervention: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2023.2199469

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs (n = 1,097) assessing skills. The six-month pooled effect versus active comparators was SMD = 0.40 (95% CI 0.04–0.76), a printed standardized mean difference; one study measured intent to intervene and the other the Suicide Intervention Response Inventory version 2.

> "At six-month follow-up, a significant large effect size was found when comparing simulation training to an active comparator (two studies [51,52] SMD = 0.40, 95% CI 0.04–0.76) with low heterogeneity (I [2] = 0%)."
