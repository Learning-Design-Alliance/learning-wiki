# A 16-week personalized teaching intervention was associated with significantly higher posttest metacognitive strategy use across planning, monitoring, and evaluation among vocational college students.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental group scored significantly higher than controls on all three metacognitive strategy dimensions at posttest. [→ Zhao Xiaojuan 2026](#zhao-xiaojuan-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhao Xiaojuan 2026

Zhao Xiaojuan. (2026). Psychological characteristics of metacognitive strategy use and vocabulary development paths among vocational university students in a personalized english teaching environment. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04595-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 120 vocational college students in two intact classes randomly assigned at class level to a 16-week personalized intervention or conventional instruction. Posttest between-group t-tests reached significance for each dimension (planning: t = 3.42, p = .001; monitoring: t = 4.05, p < .001; evaluation: t = 2.47, p = .015).

> "the experimental group's posttest scores on planning (M = 3.74, SD = 0.68), monitoring (M = 3.89, SD = 0.61), and evaluation (M = 3.52, SD = 0.72) all exceeded those of the control group"
