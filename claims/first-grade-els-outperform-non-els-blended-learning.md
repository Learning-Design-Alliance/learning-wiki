## Evidence

### Elizabeth R. Kazakoff 2018

Elizabeth R. Kazakoff, Paul Macaruso, Pam Hook. (2018). Efficacy of a blended learning approach to elementary school reading instruction for students who are English Learners. Education Tech Research Dev 66:429–449. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9565-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RMANOVA on 244 first graders (122 EL, 122 non-EL) on the NWF subtest. The interaction between EL status and time was significant (Greenhouse–Geisser adjusted F(1,241) = 5.743, p < 0.05); post-hoc tests showed "ELs performed significantly better than non-ELs at the end"; no standardized effect size for the post-hoc contrast is printed.

> "Post-hoc testing with Bonferonni corrections indicated that ELs were not significantly different from non-ELs in the beginning of the Year One study ( p = 0.195), but ELs performed significantly better than non-ELs at the end of the Year One study ( p \ 0.01)."
