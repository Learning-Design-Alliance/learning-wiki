Swain Cara, Cohen Hugo, Routh Jennifer, Helgesson Gert, Rickard Rory, Karlgren Klas. (2025). Optimising the educational utility of live tissue training in trauma surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07846-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Framework Method thematic analysis of fifteen semi-structured interviews with physicians experienced in LTT as learners (n=9) or educators (n=6). The analysis found "Optimisation of LTT was discussed by the participants across three themes: Planning, Delivering, and Experiencing LTT", structured to reflect stages of participation in a simulated learning event.
