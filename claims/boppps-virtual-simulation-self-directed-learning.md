## Evidence

### Shen Qi-ling 2026

Shen Qi-ling, Gu Yi-mei, Zhang Biao-xin, Zhou Li-hua, Wu Kai-le, Tong Bu-sheng, Yang Ju-fang, Li Jia-xin, Hu Shao-Hua. (2026). BOPPPS model with virtual simulation system for otorhinolaryngology head and neck surgery nursing interns: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09648-z

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment measuring self-directed learning with the SLAS in 120 nursing interns. The "total score of the intervention group was 106.35 ± 16.08" versus 99.57 ± 19.93 in controls, with d = 0.37.

> "The total score of the intervention group was 106.35 ± 16.08, which was higher than that of the control group (99.57 ± 19.93), and the difference between the groups was statistically significant (t = 2.047, P = 0.043). The effect size was small to medium (d = 0.37, 95% CI: 0.01 to 0.73)."
