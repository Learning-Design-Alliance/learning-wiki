## Evidence

### Kadmon Guni 2016

Kadmon Guni, Kadmon Martina. (2016). Academic Performance of Students with the Highest and Mediocre School-leaving Grades: Does the Aptitude Test for Medical Studies (TMS) Balance Their Prognoses? GMS Journal for Medical Education 33(1):Doc7. [doi:10.3205/zma001006](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001006)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 3 subgroup analysis by TMS categories (Figure 2 boxplots with post-hoc Mann-Whitney tests) found this band performed significantly below most TMS-taking best leavers but "clearly better than the minimal required standard of 60%"; subgroups below TMS 117 were too rare for clear conclusions.

> "The mediocre school leavers with TMS scores between 117 and 124 performed, on average, significantly less well than the majority of the best school leavers who had taken the TMS (see Figure 2 (Fig. 2), Points A and B). Yet, they performed clearly better than the minimal required standard of 60% ("pass")."
