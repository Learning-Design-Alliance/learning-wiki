Indicative process-evaluation survey evidence from six teacher trials. Two teachers set Medly as directed homework and four used it during lesson time; business-as-usual revision included "Tassomai, Sparx Science, BBC Bitesize and printed revision materials", so the contrast was sometimes Medly versus an already technology-rich environment.
