`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-arm prospective pilot study of 16 medical students; paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests compared pre/post quiz scores. The "median change 10, 95% CI 6.0 to 11.5" was reported; no standardized effect size printed.

> "The median pre-workshop quiz score was 7/21, and this increased significantly to 17/21 (p = 0.0005; median change 10, 95% CI 6.0 to 11.5) after the workshop (Figure 3)."
