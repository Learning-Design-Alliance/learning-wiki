## Evidence

### Alexandra Werth 2022

Alexandra Werth, Jessica R. Hoehn, Kristin Oliver, Michael F. J. Fox, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2022). Rapid Transition to Remote Instruction of Physics Labs During Spring 2020: Instructor Perspectives. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Closed-response survey analysis of 129 courses before and after the spring 2020 transition, visualized in a Sankey diagram (Fig. 3) showing instructors "reported switching more towards emphasizing physics concepts rather than lab skills."

> "When asked about the broad learning goals of the class (developing skills, reinforcing concepts, or a mixture of both) for both before and after the transition, instructors reported switching more towards emphasizing physics concepts rather than lab skills."

### Alexandra Werth 2022 (2)

Alexandra Werth, Jessica R. Hoehn, Kristin Oliver, Michael F. J. Fox, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2022). Rapid Transition to Remote Instruction of Physics Labs During Spring 2020: Instructor Perspectives. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Table II reports learning-goal percentages before and after transition for first-year (n = 73) and beyond-first-year (n = 50) courses; the article notes "a 6.5 times increase" for beyond-first-year courses versus 1.9 times for first-year courses.

> "there was a 6.5 times increase in the number of beyond-ﬁrst-year courses primarily emphasizing physics concepts after the transition to remote instruction compared to only a 1.9 times increase in ﬁrst-year-courses having this learning goal."
