### Gao Ning 2024

Gao Ning, Fu Kun, Li Na, He Wei. (2024). Research on the application of CBL combined with BOPPPS teaching mode in oral and maxillofacial surgery teaching: a randomized controlled study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-77432-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study; students receiving emergency patients were observed by two trained attending physicians blinded to grouping, and the average of five assessments was used. Table 2 reports clinical decision-making "17.68 ± 1.00" versus "12.74 ± 2.10" and total score "87.05 ± 3.34" versus "65.11 ± 8.50"; for medical history writing ("17.37 ± 1.16" versus "15.89 ± 2.49") the text reports P > 0.05, but the table prints t = 2.3335, p = 0.002.

> "Except for the medical history writing level without statistically significant difference (P > 0.05), the other four items and total scores of the experimental group were better than those of the control group, with statistically significant difference (P < 0.05), as shown in Table 2."
