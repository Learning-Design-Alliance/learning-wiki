### What Works Clearinghouse 2007

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: SpellRead™. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Randomized trial of 47 first- and second-grade students in Newfoundland; the review reports that WWC analysis "confirmed statistically significant differences for only four of the outcomes", though study authors had reported six significant effects. No standardized effect size is printed for the individual outcomes.

> "However, the WWC analysis confirmed statistically significant differences for only four of the outcomes (WRMT–R word attack subtest, the TOWRE phonetic decoding efficiency subtest, and the CTOPP blending words and segmenting words subtests)."
