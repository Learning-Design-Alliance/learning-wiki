### Stephenson E 2026

Stephenson E, Morrigan K, Irfan A, Bascombe K, Okorie M. (2026). Artificial intelligence-assisted feedback in pharmacology education: a pilot evaluation of a custom generative model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09300-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A 5-point Likert-scale survey of the moderated AI-generated feedback completed by 11 of 20 participants in the pilot; medians with interquartile ranges were calculated for each item.

> "Clarity received a median score of 4. Actionability, impact on confidence and overall usefulness yielded similar medians of 4, with most students rating the feedback 4 or 5. Distributions were skewed toward positive responses; no student rated any item below 3."
