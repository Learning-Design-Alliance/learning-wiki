## Evidence

### Eric Richter 2022

Eric Richter, Jeffrey P. Carpenter, André Meyer, Dirk Richter. (2022). Instagram as a platform for teacher collaboration and digital social support. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104624

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Descriptive analysis of manifest scales in the survey of 249 teachers. The article reports "a rather low level of collaboration among participants on Instagram" overall, with information seeking highest and co-creating lowest, alongside great diversity within the sample.

> "The different means of the four scales indicate that most teachers use Instagram for information seeking (M = 3.78, SD = 1.03). In contrast, teachers participate in co-creating only to a very limited extent (M = 1.34, SD = 0.61)."
