Evidence¶

Schleepen TMJ 2016¶

Schleepen TMJ, Van Mier HI, De Smedt B (2016) The Contribution of Numerical Magnitude Comparison and Phonological Processing to Individual Differences in Fourth Graders' Multiplication Fact Ability. PLoS ONE 11(6): e0158335. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0158335

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression (N = 63) controlling Raven's intellectual ability, motor reaction time, and nonword repetition in earlier steps; the magnitude comparison step was significant, F(2,57) = 13.3, p < .001, with ΔR² = 24.4%.

"This analysis revealed that introducing numerical magnitude comparison in step 4 explained an additional 24.4% of the variance in multiplication performance, and this change in R2 was significant, F(2,57) = 13.3, p < .001."
