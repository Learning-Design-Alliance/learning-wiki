Related Claims¶

  • TAIA produced higher adjusted post-test scientific achievement than worksheet-supported inquiry, controlling for pre-test and ICT competency — related

  • TAIA students perceived more teacher support for hypothesis generation and argumentation and enjoyed inquiry more; perceived experiment-related support did not differ — related
