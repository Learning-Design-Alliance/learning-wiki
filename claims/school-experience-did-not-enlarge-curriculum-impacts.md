# Impacts of the curricula were not statistically significantly larger after schools had one year of experience using them (Cohort 2 fifth graders).

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Second-cohort impacts were not statistically significantly different from zero or from the first-cohort impacts, so one year of school experience with the curricula did not enlarge impacts. [→ James-Burdumy 2010](#james-burdumy-2010)

## Evidence

### James-Burdumy 2010

James-Burdumy, S., Deke, J., Lugo-Gil, J., Carey, N., Hershey, A., Gersten, R., Newman-Gonchar, R., Dimino, J., Haymond, K., and Faddis, B. (2010). Effectiveness of Selected Supplemental Reading Comprehension Interventions: Findings From Two Student Cohorts (NCEE 2010-4015). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the fifth-grade component of the second year, a new cohort of 4,142 fifth-grade students in schools with one prior year of experience using the curricula was pre- and post-tested; impacts were "not statistically significantly different from zero or from the impacts for the first cohort of students." No effect sizes are printed.

> "Impacts were not statistically significantly larger after schools had one year of experience using the curricula. Impacts for the second cohort of students were not statistically significantly different from zero or from the impacts for the first cohort of students."
