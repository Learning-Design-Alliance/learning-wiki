Terhi Vessonen 2026¶

Terhi Vessonen, Heidi Hellstrand, Pirjo Aunio, Anu Laine. (2026). Pictures in Mathematical Word Problems: A Systematic Review on the Role of Task and Student Characteristics. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10112-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review synthesis of student-characteristic findings across eight studies; the review also reports that "gender was not related to association between pictures and students' performance". No effect sizes are printed for these moderator findings.

"Affective variables were not shown to relate to the effects of pictures on students' mathematical word problem-solving performance (i.e., Lindner, 2020)."
