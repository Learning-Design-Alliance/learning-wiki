# Collaborative speech strongly co-occurs with actions on virtual objects and virtual hand gestures in shared VR geometry tasks

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across 108 episodes of collaborative VR geometry work, actions on virtual objects and collaborative talk moves were intertwined, occurring simultaneously or sequentially. [→ Wen Huang 2023](#wen-huang-2023)
`q2 i?` Gesture codes representational/iconic and beat had high concurrent times with speech codes representing and negotiating; pointing also co-occurred with establishing shared understanding and planning. [→ Wen Huang 2023](#wen-huang-2023)

## Evidence

### Wen Huang 2023

Wen Huang, Candace Walkington, Mitchell J. Nathan. (2023). Coordinating modalities of mathematical collaboration in shared VR environments. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09397-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Multimodal analysis of 108 episodes of video from three groups of in-service teachers in a shared VR environment produced 1818 simultaneous relationships and 1723 sequential relationships between labeled action instances. The matrices showed the "intertwined relationship between perform- ing actions on virtual objects and engaging in collaborative talk moves."
