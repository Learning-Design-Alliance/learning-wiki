## Evidence

### Zsifkovits Monika 2025

Zsifkovits Monika, Amplatz Lukas, Triebner Nina, Utz Janine, Kornhuber Johannes, Spitzer Philipp. (2025). Randomized controlled trial of asynchronous vs. synchronous online teaching formats: equal knowledge after training, greater acceptance and lower intrinsic motivation through asynchronous online learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07481-4

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

IMI perceived choice subscale comparison between randomized groups; the webinar group scored higher, "Cohen's d = − 0.354, 95% CI [-0.476, − 0.231]". The authors call this result unexpected given asynchronous formats' usual time flexibility.

> "In addition, the webinar led to a significantly greater level of perceived freedom of choice (IMI perceived choice) than the learning platform did (MLP = 3.29 ± 1.30; MW = 3.73 ± 1.12; t (1042) = -5.71; p <.001, p_adj < 0.013; Cohen's d = − 0.354, 95% CI [-0.476, − 0.231]; Fig. 4B)."
