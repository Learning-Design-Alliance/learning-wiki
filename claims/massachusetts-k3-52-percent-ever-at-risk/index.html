Descriptive analysis of literacy screening benchmark scores from state grantees for 2020/21 and 2021/22, representing about 10 percent of the state's K–3 population; the report states "52 percent of students were ever classified as below benchmark or showing any level of risk of reading difficulty."

"Across all time periods combined (beginning, middle, and end of year), 52 percent of students were ever classified as below benchmark or showing any level of risk of reading difficulty. About 27 percent of students were at the highest levels of risk as defined by each assessment."
