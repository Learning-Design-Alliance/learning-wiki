Yao Qianyin, Zhu Peiyi, Zou Zixi, Cheng Yatao, Liang Yaojian, Huang Wenyu. (2025). Synergising flipped classroom and case-based learning in resource-intensive anatomy education in China: a quasi-experimental study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-102001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-report survey data discussed in the Discussion section, comparing FCCL and TC students' reported metacognitive strategy use; percentages are self-reported proportions, no effect size printed. The authors tie this to "mastery experiences" building self-efficacy.

> "83.1% of FCCL students frequently highlighted key sections for study, nearly double the rate of TC students (46.9%). Similarly, substantial gaps were observed in students’ self-reported ability to connect new information to prior knowledge (73.3% vs 56.3%) and to synthesise knowledge during review (70.5% vs 51.6%)."
