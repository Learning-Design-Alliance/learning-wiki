q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Weighted pooled success rates by tooth type from the three‐study meta‐analysis (351 molars, 216 pre‐molars, 202 anterior teeth). The review attributes the gradient to "the complexity of molar anatomy"; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Anterior and pre‐molar teeth showed greater success rates than molars (82.2% and 79.4% vs. 68.7%), which may reflect the complexity of molar anatomy."
