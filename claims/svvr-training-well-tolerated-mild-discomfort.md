Tolerability assessment using the Virtual Reality Sickness Questionnaire (oculomotor and disorientation subscales) administered immediately after training to the 54 experimental-group participants. The article also reports no serious adverse events and no discontinuation due to physical discomfort.

> "Among the 54 participants in the experimental group, 18 (33.3%) reported no discomfort after the SVVR training. Among those who reported discomfort, total Virtual Reality Sickness Questionnaire scores ranged from 3.33 to 47.50, with a mean score of 15.85."
