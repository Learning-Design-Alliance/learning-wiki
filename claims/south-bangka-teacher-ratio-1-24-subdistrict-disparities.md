`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Documentary analysis of the Educational Profile 2020 of South Bangka reported in Table 1 of this qualitative case study. The article states the average ratio is "generally still reasonable, namely 1:24" while sub-district ratios range "between 1:20 to 1:29".

> "Table 1 shows that the average teacher -student ratio in South Bangka Regency is generally still reasonable, namely 1:24, meaning that a teacher supports 24 students. However, if we look at the sub-district data, we will find quite large discrepancies between sub-districts, namely between 1:20 to 1:29."
