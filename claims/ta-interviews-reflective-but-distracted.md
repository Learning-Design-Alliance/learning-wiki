Think-aloud interviews with 11 TAs (four of whom had done the quantitative task at least a year earlier), focused on questions where TA predictions diverged from student data. The article reports the TAs "were reflective and often had reasonable thoughts regarding how students may be reasoning."

> "Think-aloud interviews with TAs engaged in the task of determining students’ MCIs suggested that the TAs were reflective and often had reasonable thoughts regarding how students may be reasoning about the questions."
