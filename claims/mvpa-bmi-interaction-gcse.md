`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction analysis in the prospective observational study of 845 adolescents, testing moderation of each exposure by sex, IMD and BMI z-score. The article reports the MVPA × BMI z-score interaction "3·7(0·1, 7·3) p = 0.046" with non-significant strata.

> "There was an interaction between MVPA and BMI z-score (β(95 % CI)) 3·7(0·1, 7·3) p = 0.046), but in stratified analyses neither approached significance but the association was negative for those with lower BMI (−3.9 (−10.7,2.9) p = 0.246) and positive for those with higher BMI (1.2 (−3.9,6.4) p = 0.616)."
