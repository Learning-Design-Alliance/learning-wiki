## Evidence

### Fies 2021

Fies, C., & Packham, C. (2021). Interdisciplinary Teams for Teacher Professional Development. ASP Conference Series. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.04878](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.04878)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Oral and written feedback from the pilot run, collected through daily reflections and focus group interviews. Participants recommended "generally revising the session format as too much of the on-campus content meetings took the shape of lectures" and revisiting content to remove redundancies across sessions.

> "The specific recommendations for improvement consisted of (1) generally revising the session format as too much of the on-campus content meetings took the shape of lectures; (2) revisiting content across the two weeks as there were some redundancies across sessions"
