Evidence¶

de Oliveira Carlota Rocha 2026¶

de Oliveira Carlota Rocha, da Silva Barboza Andressa, de Andrade Juliana Silva Ribeiro, Lund Rafael Guerra. (2026). Scoping Review on Active Teaching and Learning Methodologies in Dentistry. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13109

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis of included studies. The review reports that "PBL [13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18] and flipped classrooms [2, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23] frequently demonstrated better post‐test performance and knowledge retention" while gamification's clinical improvements were inconsistent.

"gamification often increased engagement but did not consistently lead to significant clinical improvements [8, 9, 10, 11, 12], while PBL [13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18] and flipped classrooms [2, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23] frequently demonstrated better post‐test performance and knowledge retention"
