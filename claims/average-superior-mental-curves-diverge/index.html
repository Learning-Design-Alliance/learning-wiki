Related Claims¶

  • In a prospective longitudinal sample (ECLS-K:2011), numbers reversed performance follows a logarithmic growth pattern, decelerating over ages 6–11 — related
  • Math achievement growth from preschool through second grade is best characterized by a latent basis curve rather than linear, exponential, or quadratic functions — related
  • Task-based inhibitory control (Stop Signal Task) improves curvilinearly from ages 11 to 15, with growth slowing around middle adolescence — related
  • Performance on inhibitory control measures improves with age, with some tasks reaching performance plateaus beginning around 4 years of age — related
  • The relationship between socioeconomic resources and children's cognitive development strengthens from infancy to a peak around age 4–4½, then weakens slightly by kindergarten entry. — related
