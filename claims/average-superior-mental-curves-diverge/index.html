Bird T. Baldwin 1922¶

Bird T. Baldwin. (1922). The Relation Between Mental and Physical Growth. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. XIII, No. 1. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/13/1/193

Mean mental growth curves from repeated Stanford-Binet examinations of average and superior boys and girls, plotted to age 14. The author states "These superior and average children develop at different levels and grow increasingly dissimilar with age."

"These superior and average children develop at different levels and grow increasingly dissimilar with age. This divergence in growth curves of average and superior children has been assumed as probable, but has not here-to-fore been empirically demonstrated."
