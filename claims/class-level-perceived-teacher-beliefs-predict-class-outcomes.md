## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lee 2025

Lee, H. J., Kim, S., & Bong, M. (2025). Social antecedents of children's mindsets, motivation and achievement in math: Investigating parental beliefs and perceived teacher beliefs. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 849–870. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12764

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Between-class paths from the multilevel path analysis of 507 students in 28 classrooms. The fixed-mindset-to-mindset path was not significant at class level, but "related positively to their test anxiety" (β = .34), and expectations predicted self-efficacy and persistence.

> "Aggregated at the class‐level, student perceptions of their teacher's fixed mindset did not significantly connect to their own fixed mindset (β = .29, p = .067) but related positively to their test anxiety (β = .34, p = .031; see Figure 1). Teachers' expectations collectively perceived by the students in the same classroom positively predicted the class‐level self‐efficacy (β = .45, p = .004) and persistence in math (β = .39, p = .026)."
