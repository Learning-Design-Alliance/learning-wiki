## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a hierarchical regression with 39 students with ASD, adding the BRIEF-A Plan/Organize subscale to SRS-A symptom severity significantly improved the model and tripled explained variance in academic progress to 36%. [→ Dijkhuis Renée 2020](#dijkhuis-renee-2020)

## Evidence

### Dijkhuis Renée 2020

Dijkhuis Renée, de Sonneville Leo, Ziermans Tim, Staal Wouter, Swaab Hanna. (2020). Autism Symptoms, Executive Functioning and Academic Progress in Higher Education Students. Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-019-04267-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Hierarchical multiple regression in a study of 54 young adults with ASD (analysis n = 39 with academic progress data), SRS-A entered first and BRIEF-A Plan/Organize second. The model "tripled the explained variance in academic progress to 36%"; only the F statistic and p value are printed, no effect size.

> "the second model adding the subscale plan/organize of the BRIEF-A showed a statistically significant improvement [F(2, 34) = 9.38, p = .001], and tripled the explained variance in academic progress to 36%"
