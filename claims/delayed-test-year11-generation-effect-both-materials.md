### Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests 2016

Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests. (2016). Submitted 29/01/2016. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2016.06.007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the delayed-test ANOVA, Year 11 students showed no guidance x element interactivity interaction (F(1, 44) = .012, p = .915); instead "the main effect of guidance was significant", with low guidance superior for both material types.

> "the main effect of guidance was significant, F(1, 44) = 4.40, MSe = 298.65, p = .042, ηр² = .091 with both high and low element interactivity information resulting in higher test scores after low guidance."
