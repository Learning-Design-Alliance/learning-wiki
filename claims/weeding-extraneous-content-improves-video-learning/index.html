Evidence¶

Brame Cynthia J 2016¶

Brame Cynthia J. (2016). Effective Educational Videos: Principles and Guidelines for Maximizing Student Learning from Video Content. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-03-0125

q2 · i? · review · r2

The essay reports, citing Ibrahim et al. (2012), that weeding "can improve retention and transfer of new information from video". Extraneous features force learners to judge whether to attend to them, increasing extraneous load.

"Ibrahim et al. (2012) has shown that this treatment can improve retention and transfer of new information from video."
