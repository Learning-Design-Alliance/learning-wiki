## Evidence

### Martinez 2017

Liza E. Martinez. (2017). Adult Hispanic ESL Students and Graded Readers. The CATESOL Journal, 29(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1164286

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact upper-intermediate adult ESL classes, reported in the Findings section. The treatment class read graded readers via scaffolded silent reading; the control class did not. The article reports that "just 48% of the participants in the control group scored higher" while 82% of the treatment group did; no effect size or significance test is printed.

> "Eighty-two percent of the participants in the treatment group scored higher on the posttest than the pretest. In contrast, just 48% of the participants in the control group scored higher on the posttest compared to the pretest."
