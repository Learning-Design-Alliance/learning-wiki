## Evidence

### Russell AE 2015

Russell AE, Ford T, Russell G (2015) Socioeconomic Associations with ADHD: Findings from a Mediation Analysis. PLoS ONE 10(6): e0128248. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0128248

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics (Table 1) showed proportionately more ADHD-group parents in lower education and employment bands, but unadjusted logistic regressions for mother education (p 0.406), partner education (p 0.067) and employment (p 0.847; p 0.610) were not significant. This contradicted the authors' genetic-confounding hypothesis.

> "Although ADHD was more prevalent in families whose parents had lower occupational status, and lower educational levels, these indicators did not show significant association with ADHD in children in the current sample."
