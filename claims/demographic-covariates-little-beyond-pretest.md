## Evidence

### Hedges 2007

Hedges, L. V., & Hedberg, E. C. (2007). Intraclass Correlation Values for Planning Group-Randomized Trials in Education. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 29(1), 60-87. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373707299706

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the demographic covariates models (gender, race/ethnicity, parent education as proxy for SES) fitted to the national samples, "Demographic characteristics are less effective covariates", explaining "up to one half of the between-school variance in all and low-SES schools."

> "Demographic characteristics are less effective covariates, but they can explain up to one half of the between-school variance in all and low-SES schools."

### Hedges 2007 (2)

Hedges, L. V., & Hedberg, E. C. (2007). Intraclass Correlation Values for Planning Group-Randomized Trials in Education. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 29(1), 60-87. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373707299706

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the mathematics full-population results, demographic characteristics explained "little additional variance (at either the student or the school level) beyond what is explained by the pretest", and their inclusion "does not appear to be useful if pretest scores are available."

> "In general, demographic characteristics explain little additional variance (at either the student or the school level) beyond what is explained by the pretest, and thus their inclusion in analysis models does not appear to be useful if pretest scores are available."
