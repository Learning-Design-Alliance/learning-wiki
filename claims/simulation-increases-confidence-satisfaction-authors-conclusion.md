`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretive conclusion from this cross-sectional descriptive study, not a tested experimental comparison; the study measured satisfaction and self-confidence after simulation but did not manipulate simulation exposure. The article offers no effect size for this assertion.

> "Clinical simulations help students to increase their levels of confidence and satisfaction, enabling them to face real scenarios in clinical practice better prepared and with more confidence."
