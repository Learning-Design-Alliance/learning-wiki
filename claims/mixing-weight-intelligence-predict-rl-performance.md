## Evidence

### Chengyan Yang 2025

Chengyan Yang, Tongran Liu, Mengxin Wen & Xun Liu. (2025). The distinct functions of working memory and intelligence in model-based and model-free reinforcement learning. npj Science of Learning 10:72. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00363-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise linear regression on data from 50 participants with corrected reward rate as outcome and WM load, mixing weight, and intelligence as predictors. The optimal model had R² = 0.60 (AIC = −357, BIC = −344); mixing weight also positively predicted performance (β = 0.08, p < 0.001).

Intelligence scores positively predicted task performance (β = 0.003, SE = 0.001, t = 2.62, p = 0.01)"
