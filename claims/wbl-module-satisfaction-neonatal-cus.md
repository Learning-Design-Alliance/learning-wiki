## Evidence

### Ben Fadel Nadya; McAleer Sean 2020

Ben Fadel Nadya; McAleer Sean. (2020). Impact of a web-based module on trainees’ ability to interpret neonatal cranial ultrasound. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02400-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Satisfaction survey (modified Larsen et al. 1979 questionnaire) completed anonymously by all 23 trainees who studied the module, using 5-point Likert items. Self-reported perception data only; no effect size is printed.

> "Ninety-one percent of respondents strongly agreed that the module contents were relevant to their practice. Ninety-six percent strongly agreed that the module provided appropriate technology use with a reasonably fast downloading speed and display of images"
