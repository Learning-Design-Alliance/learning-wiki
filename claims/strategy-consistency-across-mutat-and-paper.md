## Evidence

### Adesina 2014

Adesina, A., Stone, R., Batmaz, F., & Jones, I. (2014). Touch Arithmetic: A process‐based Computer‐Aided Assessment approach for capture of problem solving steps in the context of elementary mathematics. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2014.06.015

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative case analysis of three students (Lucas, Julia, Mark) comparing paper workings with MuTAT trace-derived strategies; Lucas used as-presented throughout, Julia place-value in four of five MuTAT questions, Mark a mixture. The authors explicitly limit generalisability.

> "This result suggests that the strategies used on paper may not be different from the one used on the application. However this finding cannot be extrapolated to all students as most of them did not completely present their workings on paper."
