## Evidence

### Bjørn Fjukstad 2019

Bjørn Fjukstad, Nina Angelvik, Morten Grønnesby, Maria Wulff Hauglann, Hedinn Gunhildrud, Fredrik Høisæther Rasch, Julianne Iversen, Margaret Dalseng, Lars Ailo Bongo. (2019). Teaching Electronics and Programming in Norwegian Schools Using the air:bit Sensor Kit. arXiv. [doi:10.1145/3304221.3325527](https://doi.org/10.1145/3304221.3325527)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student survey (90 responses). 94% used the online materials while building and programming; 83% of those found them helpful to a large or very large degree, 14% to some extent, 4% not at all or to a small degree. Separately, 69% reported being satisfied or very satisfied with the project and 8% dissatisfied or very dissatisfied.

> "94% of the students reported that they used the online materials while they where building and programming the air:bit . From these, 83% reported that these where either to a large degree or to a very large degree helpful."
