## Evidence

### Julian Rudisch 2024

Julian Rudisch, Luis K. H. Holzhauer, Karmen Kravanja, Fred H. Hamker & Claudia Voelcker-Rehage. (2024). A systematic review of observational practice for adaptation of reaching movements. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00271-5

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review of six studies assessing aftereffects after observational practice; standardized mean differences calculable for four studies, "with effect sizes ranging between d =−2.07 to d = −5.44 and an overall effect size of d =−3.05."

> "All studies demonstrated strong aftereffects (i.e., deviations from a straight line in a non-rotated environment) following physical but small or absent aftereffects following observational practice."
