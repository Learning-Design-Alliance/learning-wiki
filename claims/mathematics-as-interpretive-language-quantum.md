Carlini, A., Lanza, A., Monti, D., Soso, A., Losero, E., Mari, M., Oñate Orozco, M., Bondani, M. (2026). Teaching quantum mechanics in secondary schools: A qubit-first learning path to Bell's inequalities. arXiv. [doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-11352498/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11352498/v1)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative focus-group interviews with 17 self-selected FSL students in Genoa, analyzed on five dimensions with a rubric. Students reported that "mathematical concepts initially appeared abstract but acquired meaning once they were applied experimentally".

> "The overwhelming majority of students did not perceive the mathematical formalism as an obstacle. Instead, most responses were classified at Level 3 (Support), indicating that mathematics was progressively understood as an interpretative language connecting theoretical models with physical phenomena."
