## Evidence

### Inusah Salifu · Usman Kojo Abonyi 2023

Inusah Salifu · Usman Kojo Abonyi. (2023). Managing large classes in virtual teaching: experiences of university teachers in Ghana during COVID-19. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10151-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on frustrations of managing large virtual classes. Case 1 participants cited power cuts, poor connectivity and data costs; other cases cited student shyness in discussions, inability to observe attention on video, and no teaching assistants assigned.

> "the participants in #C1 complained of frequent electricity power cuts, unstable internet connectivity, and the inability of students to access data for recurring virtual lectures."

### Inusah Salifu · Usman Kojo Abonyi 2023 (2)

Inusah Salifu · Usman Kojo Abonyi. (2023). Managing large classes in virtual teaching: experiences of university teachers in Ghana during COVID-19. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10151-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Participant P5 in Case 2 described using a Moodle platform to assess analytical and writing skills but reported delayed feedback due to class size; the class met via Zoom afterwards to discuss poorly explained concepts.

> "However, it's impossible to give immediate virtual feedback to students because of the class size, and it takes me about three weeks to complete marking their assignments individually."
