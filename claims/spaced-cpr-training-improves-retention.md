## Evidence

### Lee Hyeon-Ji 2026

Lee Hyeon-Ji. (2026). Training interval, feedback, and retention in cardiopulmonary resuscitation education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09538-4

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of 20 interventional studies (RCTs and quasi-experimental designs) published 2015–2024; the review reports that "spaced training was associated with improved CPR skill retention compared with single-session, massed instruction." Table 3 records a randomized trial showing better compression-depth retention with spaced training at 3 months. No meta-analysis was feasible due to heterogeneity.

> "A consistent pattern across included studies was that spaced training was associated with improved CPR skill retention compared with single-session, massed instruction."
