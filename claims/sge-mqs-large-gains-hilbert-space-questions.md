# After the SGE MQS, upper-level QM students scored over 90% on the two Hilbert-space/physical-space and basis-choice questions, up from 61% and 40% on the pretest

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` In one group with no comparison group, upper-level QM students' scores on the first two test questions rose from pretest to posttest after the SGE MQS, exceeding 90% on both posttest questions. [→ Paul Justice 2019](#paul-justice-2019)
`q2 i2` On question 1 the mean rose from 61% to 96% (normalized gain 0.88, Cohen's d = 0.50) and on question 2 from 40% to 92% (gain 0.87, d = 0.71). [→ Paul Justice 2019](#paul-justice-2019)

## Evidence

### Paul Justice 2019

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Improving student understanding of quantum mechanics underlying the Stern-Gerlach experiment using a research-validated multiple-choice question sequence. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab2135](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab2135)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

In-class implementation study (N=48 averaged over two years, two instructors) with pretest after lecture and posttest after the clicker-based SGE MQS; Table 1 prints effect sizes d = 0.50 for question 1 (61% to 96%) and d = 0.71 for question 2 (40% to 92%), with normalized gains 0.88 and 0.87. Students scored "greater than 90% on both questions" posttest.

> "Table 1 shows that there is significant  improvement on the first two questions from the pretest (after traditional lecture-based instruction in relevant concepts) to posttest (after the SGE MQS) with s tudents using the SGE MQS scoring greater than 90% on both questions."
