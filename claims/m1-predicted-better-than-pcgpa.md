## Evidence

### Jaehn M 2025

Jaehn M, Hissbach J, Frickhoeffer M, Weppert D, Zimmerhofer A, Hampe W, Kadmon M, Becker N. (2025). Predictive validity of admission tests and educational attainment on preclinical academic performance – a multisite study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07974-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression results across all German medical schools, corrected for range restriction. The article prints R2 values of 0.08 versus 0.13 (TMS sample) and 0.14 versus 0.29 (HAM-Nat sample) for PCGPA versus M1; these are variance-explained values, not standardized effect sizes.

> "Overall, independent variables predicted M1 better than PCGPA in both the TMS sample (R2PCGPA = 0.08 and R2M1 = 0.13) and the HAM-Nat sample (R2PCGPA = 0.14 and R2M1 = 0.29)."
