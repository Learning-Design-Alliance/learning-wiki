## Evidence

### Mittal Monica 2019

Mittal Monica, Supramaniam Prasanna Raj, Lim Lee Nai, Hamoda Haitham, Savvas Mike, Narvekar Nitish. (2019). Is the clinician an independent variable in embryo transfer outcomes under standardized direct and indirect supervision? A 5-year observational cohort study. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001215](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001215)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort analysis stratified for confounding variables known to impact cycle outcomes (age ≤37 years, soft catheter, expanded blastocyst grade ≥2) for first and last 50 transfers of each clinician. The article reports "No significant difference is noted in the learning curves for the two broad groups of practicing clinicians" (p=0.95 and p=0.37).

> "CPRs for experienced clinicians was 65.7% (first 50 transfers) and 40.9% (last 50 transfers); CPR for trainees was 66.7% (first 50 transfers) and 53.6% (last 50 transfers); p=0.95 and p=0.37, respectively."
