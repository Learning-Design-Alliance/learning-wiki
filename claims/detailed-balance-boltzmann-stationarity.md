# Analytically, an ensemble distributed according to the Boltzmann distribution remains in that distribution under any transition probability satisfying detailed balance, though detailed balance is sufficient but not necessary

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` If an ensemble starts distributed according to the Boltzmann distribution and the transition probability satisfies detailed balance, the resulting ensemble remains in the Boltzmann distribution. [→ Jan Tobochnik and Harvey Gould 2007](#jan-tobochnik-and-harvey-gould-2007)

## Evidence

### Jan Tobochnik and Harvey Gould 2007

Jan Tobochnik and Harvey Gould. (2007). Teaching statistical physics by thinking about models and algorithms. arXiv:0712.3488. https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.3488 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0712.3488](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0712.3488)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Analytical derivation in Section V: substituting the Boltzmann distribution into the detailed balance condition and the Markov update equation shows the stationary state is preserved; the partition function Z does not appear, which matters because Z is usually not known. No effect size applies.

> "Thus, we have shown that if we start with an ensemble distributed ac cording to the Boltz- mann distribution and use a transition probability that satisﬁes deta iled balance, then the resulting ensemble remains in the Boltzmann distribution."

### Jan Tobochnik and Harvey Gould 2007 (2)

Jan Tobochnik and Harvey Gould. (2007). Teaching statistical physics by thinking about models and algorithms. arXiv:0712.3488. https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.3488 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0712.3488](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0712.3488)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The derivation's scope statement: detailed balance constrains only the ratio of transition probabilities and is not a necessary condition for stationarity, as the article states explicitly.

> "Note that deta iled balance is suﬃcient, but we have not shown that detailed balance is necessary fo r an ensemble to remain in a stationary state, and it turns out that it is not."
