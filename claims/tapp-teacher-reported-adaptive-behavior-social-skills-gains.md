### Teachers and Parents as Partners (TAPP) Family Engagement evidence summary 2012

Teachers and Parents as Partners (TAPP) Family Engagement evidence summary. (2012). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/teachers-and-parents-as-partners-tapp/

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

First randomized controlled trial (2012, 21 Midwestern elementary schools, 207 K–3 students with disruptive behaviors, 82 teachers) comparing TAPP to control. Teacher ratings showed "significantly greater improvements in student adaptive behavior" (ES +0.39) and social skills gains (ES +0.47).

> "Teachers in the TAPP group reported significantly greater improvements in student adaptive behavior on the BASC-2 compared to the control group (effect size = +0.39), as well as significantly higher gains in social skills on the SSRS (effect size = +0.47)."
