## Evidence

### Ayush Gupta 2007

Ayush Gupta, Edward F. Redish, and David Hammer. (2007). Coordination of Mathematics and Physical Resources by Physics Graduate Students. 2007 PERC, AIP Conference Proceedings, Volume 951. https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.0012 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0803.0012](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0803.0012)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding of the same 16 homework solutions for explicit treatment of ω as real or complex (Table 2): 5 of 7 expanded-form-only students showed awareness of ω as complex, with 3 justifying errors by calling the imaginary part very small and 2 explicitly computing it for damping. The article reports that "71% (5 of 7)" showed such awareness.

> "The 10 students who showed awareness of ω as complex were evenly divided in solving for the roots correctly and incorrectly – the division being determined by whether they had access to the compact form of the dielectric function."
