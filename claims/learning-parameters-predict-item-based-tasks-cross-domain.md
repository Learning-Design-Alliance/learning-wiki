# Individual power-fit learning parameters predict performance in item-based reading, spelling, and maths tasks across domains

## Evidence

### Marinelli CV 2021

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Separate multiple regression analyses (Enter method) using individual power-fit parameters as predictors in 125 children: a and b, "but not the exponent c, significantly predicted" orthographic decision and spelling of words with unpredictable transcription (both p < 0.01).

> "the scaling parameters a (asymptotic performance) and b (difference between initial and asymptotic performance), but not the exponent c, significantly predicted the reading performance in the Orthographic Decision test (p < 0.01) and the spelling performance on words with unpredictable transcription (p < 0.01)"

### Marinelli CV 2021 (2)

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Across the predicted item-based regressions (orthographic decision, ambiguous-word spelling, arithmetic facts, times table in series, multiplications, additions and subtractions within 10), "all regression analyses for which we expected a significant relationship were significant", with overall R2 from 0.071 to 0.131.

> "Inspection of the table indicates that all regression analyses for which we  expected a significant relationship were significant (with overall R 2 ranging from 0.071 to 0.131)"

### Marinelli CV 2021 (3)

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

For the maths tasks (Arithmetic Facts, Times Table in Series, Multiplications, Additions and Subtractions within 10), "The scaling parameter b significantly entered in all analyses"; a entered only for Arithmetic facts and Multiplications; c never entered significantly.

> "The scaling parameter b significantly entered in all analyses, while the scaling parameter a entered in the analyses on Arithmetic facts and Multiplications but not the other two; the exponent c did not enter significantly in any of these regression analyses"
