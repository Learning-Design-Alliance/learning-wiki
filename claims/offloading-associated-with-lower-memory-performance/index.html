Evidence¶

Alexander Skulmowski 2023¶

Alexander Skulmowski. (2023). The Cognitive Architecture of Digital Externalization. Educational Psychology Review, 35:101. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09818-1

q2 · i? · causal · r?

A series of computer-based visual pattern copying experiments (Grinschgl, Papenmeier, & Meyerhoff, 2021a, as reviewed here) varied offloading cost and test awareness. In the first study, "Those participants who did not experience a temporal cost of offloading used this strategy more often but were less successful in a memory test." Later experiments showed test awareness reduced offloading and its negative impact.
