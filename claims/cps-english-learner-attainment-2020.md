# Students who began as English Learners graduated from high school at a slightly higher rate than never-classified EL students and enrolled in college at about the same rate, with more two-year enrollment

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who began as ELs graduated within four years at 84.1 percent, slightly above the district average of 83.2 percent and above never-classified ELs at 82.8 percent (2016–17 cohort). [→ Malone 2021](#malone-2021)
`q2 i?` Graduates who began as ELs immediately enrolled in college at about the same rate as never-classified ELs (61.0 vs 60.8 percent), but were more likely to enroll in two-year colleges (46.6 vs 38.5 percent of enrollees). [→ Malone 2021 (2)](#malone-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Malone 2021

Malone, M., Mahaffie, S., Hernandez, G., Usher, A., & Nagaoka, J. (2021). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2020. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/hs

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison of four-year graduation rates by EL status for the 2016–17 ninth-grade cohort (Figure 14: all students N=25,696; began as ELs N=8,534 at 84.1 percent; never classified N=17,162 at 82.8 percent). "Began as ELs" includes students who took the ACCESS test at any point, including former ELs.

> "Students who began as ELs graduated from high school at a rate slightly higher than the district average."

### Malone 2021 (2)

Malone, M., Mahaffie, S., Hernandez, G., Usher, A., & Nagaoka, J. (2021). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2020. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/hs

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison of immediate college enrollment by EL status for spring 2020 graduates (Figure 15: began as ELs N=7,094; never classified N=13,896); EL graduates were "more likely to immediately enroll in two-year colleges" (46.6 vs 38.5 percent of enrollees), though four-year colleges were more common overall in both groups.

> "High school graduates who began as ELs immediately enrolled in college at about the same rate as students who were never classified as ELs."
