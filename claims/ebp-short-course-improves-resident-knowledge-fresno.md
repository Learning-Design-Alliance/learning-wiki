# Family medicine residents' EBP knowledge and skills measured by the Fresno test rose from 63.9 to 111.6 after a four half-day EBP short course, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Evidence

### Argimon-Pallàs Josep M 2011

Argimon-Pallàs Josep M, Flores-Mateo Gemma, Jiménez-Villa Josep, Pujol-Ribera Enriqueta. (2011). Effectiveness of a short-course in improving knowledge and skills on evidence-based practice. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-12-64

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-after study of 152 second-year family medicine residents in Catalonia Primary Care Teaching Units; paired t-test of pre/post Spanish Fresno test scores (pre 63.9, SD 24.3; post 111.6, SD 30.4). Residents "gained on average 22.5% of score of the total possible score"; 28.8% adjusted for potential for improvement.

> "the mean difference between pre-test and post-test was 47.7, a statistically significant result with 95% CI of 42.8-52.5 (p < 0.0001)"
