## Evidence

### Vietz E 2019

Vietz E, März E, Lottspeich C, Wölfel T, Fischer MR, Schmidmaier R. (2019). Ward round competences in surgery and psychiatry - a comparative multidisciplinary interview study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1554-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative content and frequency analysis of 30 surgical and 30 psychiatric interviews (N = 60 total), using a coding scheme from a prior internal medicine ward round study. The article reports these ten competences were "described by over 70% of the interviewees in both specialities" in the entire interview.

> "In the entire interview the following competences were described by over 70% of the interviewees in both specialities: (1) collaborative clinical reasoning, (2) clinician - patient communication, (3) clinician - team communication, (4) organization, (5) teamwork, (6) management of difficult situations and error-management, (7) self-management, (8) teaching, (9) empathy and (10) nonverbal communication"
