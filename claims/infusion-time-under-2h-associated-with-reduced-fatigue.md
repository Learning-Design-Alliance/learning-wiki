## Evidence

### Mallick R. 2020

Mallick R., Henderson T., Lahue B. J., Kafal A., Bassett P., Scalchunes C. (2020). Subcutaneous immunoglobulin in primary immunodeficiency – impact of training and infusion characteristics on patient-reported outcomes. BMC Immunology. [doi:10.1186/s12865-020-00371-y](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12865-020-00371-y)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression on the IDF survey cohort tested infusion characteristics against PROMIS Fatigue tertile membership. "having an actual infusion time ≤ 2 h was also associated with reduced fatigue" (p = 0.01); Table 4 shows OR = 1.80 for infusion <2 h.

> "Finally, having an actual infusion time ≤ 2 h was also associated with reduced fatigue (p = 0.01) (Table 4)."
