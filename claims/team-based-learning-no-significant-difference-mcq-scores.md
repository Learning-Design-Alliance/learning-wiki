## Evidence

### Zhang Z 2024

Zhang Z, Zhang Z, Yu Y, Jiang M, Deng L, Guo L. (2024). Impact of different teaching modes on medical students' performance under the scoring criteria for multiple-choice questions: A meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000041008

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials and crossover trials comparing team-based learning with traditional teaching. The pooled analysis found "no significant difference in the scores between the 2 groups, with a SMD of 0.48".

> "The analysis results indicate that there is no significant difference in the scores between the 2 groups, with a SMD of 0.48, 95% CI (–0.36, 1.31)"
