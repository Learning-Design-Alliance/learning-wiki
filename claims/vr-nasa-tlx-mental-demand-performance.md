## Evidence

### Marie Høxbro Knudsen 2023

Marie Høxbro Knudsen, Niklas Breindahl, Tor-Salve Dalsgaard, Dan Isbye, Anne Grethe Mølbak, Gerhard Tiwald, Morten Bo Søndergaard Svendsen, Lars Konge, Joanna Bergström, Tobias Todsen. (2023). Using Virtual Reality Head-Mounted Displays to Assess Skills in Emergency Medicine: Validity Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/45210

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

NASA-TLX workload ratings collected after the scenarios from all 59 participants and compared with nonparametric tests. "The average mental demand across groups was a NASA-TLX score of 13.3 (on a scale from 1-21)".

> "The average mental demand across groups was a NASA-TLX score of 13.3 (on a scale from 1-21), and the average experienced effort was rated as 12.5 (on a scale from 1-21). There were no significant differences between groups when evaluating physical demand (P=.90), temporal demand (P=.21), and frustration (P=.33) during the scenarios (Table 2)."

### Marie Høxbro Knudsen 2023 (2)

Marie Høxbro Knudsen, Niklas Breindahl, Tor-Salve Dalsgaard, Dan Isbye, Anne Grethe Mølbak, Gerhard Tiwald, Morten Bo Søndergaard Svendsen, Lars Konge, Joanna Bergström, Tobias Todsen. (2023). Using Virtual Reality Head-Mounted Displays to Assess Skills in Emergency Medicine: Validity Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/45210

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

NASA-TLX experienced-performance subscale comparison between groups using the Wilcoxon rank-sum test. "The experienced group evaluated their performance significantly higher than the novice group (P=.007)"; the authors interpret this as participants generally understanding their performance well.

> "The experienced group evaluated their performance significantly higher than the novice group (P=.007; Figure 4)."
