Evidence¶

Paul Macaruso 2020¶

Paul Macaruso, Shani Wilkes, Jen Elise Prescott. (2020). An investigation of blended learning to support reading instruction in elementary schools. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09785-2

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent sample t-test comparing Fall 2016 pretest RIT means of the treatment and control groups, establishing pretest scores as a covariate. The article reports the significant difference favoring control with the means and test statistic printed above.

"The t-test showed a significant difference (t(3720) = 3.821, p < .001) favoring the control group (177.68) over the treatment group (174.24)."
