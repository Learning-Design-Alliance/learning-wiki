## Evidence

### Chanal J 2020

Chanal J, Paumier D. (2020). The school-subject-specificity hypothesis: Implication in the relationship with grades. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0230103

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-level Pearson correlations between questionnaire items and grades (Table 4); the article reports that the two grade-referencing items are "each positively related to students’ grades in three different school subjects". Printed item correlations include .14, .12 and .08 for "To get good grades".

> "Notably, two of these items, explicitly referring to grades (“To get good grades” and “To avoid bad grades”) are each positively related to students’ grades in three different school subjects (French, English and physical education)."
