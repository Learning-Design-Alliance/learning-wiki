## Subclaims
`q2 i0` A Mann-Whitney test showed a significant difference between the median ranks of treatment and control rubric score distributions, with a small effect size (r = 0.07). [→ Aaron Reinhard 2022](#aaron-reinhard-2022)

## Evidence

### Aaron Reinhard 2022

Aaron Reinhard, Alex Felleson, Paula C. Turner, and Maxwell Green. (2022). Assessing the Impact of Metacognitive Post-Reﬂection Exercises on Problem-Solving Skillfulness. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010109)

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of final-exam rubric scores from three cohorts at Kenyon College (control cohort 1, treatment cohorts 2 and 3), scored by three independent raters. The article reports "a small eﬀect size (r = 0.07, 95% CI [0.01, 0.14])" and notes per-characteristic distributions did not differ.

> "A Mann-Whitney test, assuming the two distributions have the same general shape, showed a signiﬁcant diﬀerence between their median ranks ( W = 90948, p-value = 0.037), although with a small eﬀect size (r = 0.07, 95% CI [0.01, 0.14])."
