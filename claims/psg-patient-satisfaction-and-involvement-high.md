## Evidence

### Stevens A 2017

Stevens A, Köke A, van der Weijden T, Beurskens A. (2017). Ready for goal setting? Process evaluation of a patient-specific goal-setting method in physiotherapy. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-017-2557-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Patient questionnaire (n = 219) using a single satisfaction question on an 11-point scale (0 = not satisfied, 10 = very satisfied), collected during the six-week follow-up period. The mean was 8.8 (SD 1.0).

> "Patients (n = 219) rated their satisfaction about the conversation about their problems and treatment at a mean of 8.8 (SD 1.0, range 5 to 10), on a 0–10 scale."

### Stevens A 2017 (2)

Stevens A, Köke A, van der Weijden T, Beurskens A. (2017). Ready for goal setting? Process evaluation of a patient-specific goal-setting method in physiotherapy. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-017-2557-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Four-item patient questionnaire on involvement (N = 219); 90% answered 'Yes' to awareness of treatment goals and 82.2% reported feeling they had contributed to their treatment plan.

> "The majority of the patients (90%) reported that they had felt involved in the PSG in terms of awareness of their treatment goals, and that their activity problems were reflected in these goals."
