Çakiroğlu 2013¶

Qualitative classroom observations of the experimental group during the 10-week intervention, interpreted alongside the quantitative results. The article also notes "some analogies in programming concepts (memory, arrays, variables, stacks, etc.) were taught to the EG" and that analogies took the instructor extra time.

"During the intervention the researcher generally observed that students easily grasped the use of three new approaches. At the beginning they used CFC in informal ways within their own sentences, but they learnt to develop CFC sentences in time."
