## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Changes in cardiorespiratory fitness were not statistically associated with changes in the highest GP (β = 0.06, p = 0.25; Cohen's f2 = 0.003, a negligible effect). [→ Toru Ishihara 2021](#toru-ishihara-2021)

## Evidence

### Toru Ishihara 2021

Toru Ishihara, Noriteru Morita, Toshihiro Nakajima, Koji Yamatsu, Koichi Okita, Masato Sagawa and Keita Kamijo. (2021). Differential effects of changes in cardiorespiratory fitness on worst- and best- school subjects. npj Science of Learning 6:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00086-8

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis predicting change in highest GP in the 469-student longitudinal sample gave β = 0.06, 95% CI −0.04 to 0.16, p = 0.25, adjusted ΔR2 = 0.003; the printed effect size Cohen's f2 = 0.003 is negligible. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Multiple regression analyses indicated that these associations between changes in cardiorespiratory ﬁtness with changes in the lowest and highest GPs remained unchanged (Cohen 's f2 = 0.02, 1 −β = 0.87 and Cohen's f2 = 0.003, 1 −β = 0.22, respectively) after controlling for the household income, maternal education, sex, change in BMI, and change in learning time"
