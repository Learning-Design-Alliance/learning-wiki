## Evidence

### Koch 2008

Koch, S. C., Müller, S. M., & Sieverding, M. (2008). Women and computers. Effects of stereotype threat on attribution of failure. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2008.05.007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental 2×3 design (sex × threat condition) with 86 German college-age students; a binary logistic regression tested attribution of failure after an induced technical failure. The study found that "in the negative threat condition, women attributed the failure more internally (to their own inability), and men more externally (to the faulty technical equipment)."

> "Results suggest a stereotype threat effect on women’s attribution of failure: in the negative threat condition, women attributed the failure more internally (to their own inability), and men more externally (to the faulty technical equipment)."
