# Deaf Community Capital scores differed by mentee parents' education level, an unexplained difference

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Kruskal-Wallis tests showed a significant difference in Deaf Community Capital scores by parents' education (H(2) = 9.63, p = 0.008), with a significant pairwise difference between both-parents-college and one-parent-college (p = 0.023). [→ Braun 2017](#braun-2017)

## Evidence

### Braun 2017

Braun, D. C., Gormally, C., & Clark, M. D. (2017). The Deaf Mentoring Survey: A Community Cultural Wealth Framework for Measuring Mentoring Effectiveness with Underrepresented Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-07-0155

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Kruskal-Wallis test across three parental-education categories. The authors analyzed mentor-mix proportions (65.9%, 83.3%, 86.7% Deaf/Deaf-know by category) but state their data show no trend between increasing parental education and decreasing Deaf Community Capital and they cannot explain the difference.

> "We found a significant difference in scores reported of mentoring relationships in Deaf Community Capital (H(2) = 9.63, p = 0.008) but not in the other factors (p = 0.261, 0.546, 0.357)."
