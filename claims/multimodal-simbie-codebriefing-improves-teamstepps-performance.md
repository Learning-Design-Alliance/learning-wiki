## Evidence

### Khuansiri Narajeenron et al 2025

Khuansiri Narajeenron et al. (2025). Enhancing Team Strategies and Tools to Enhance Performance and Patient Safety Performance Through Medical Movies, Massive Open Online Courses, and 3D Virtual Simulation–Based Interprofessional Education: Mixed Methods Double-Blind Quasi-Experimental Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/67001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Double-blinded quasi-experimental study of 87 clinical undergraduates in 3 arms (n=29 each), analyzed with generalized estimating equations adjusting for health care disciplines. The "mean improvement in scores for arm B was 0.63 (95% CI 0.45-0.81; P<.001)" versus control.

> "the mean improvement in scores for arm B was 0.63 (95% CI 0.45-0.81; P<.001), which was significantly higher than that of the control group."

### Khuansiri Narajeenron et al 2025 (2)

Khuansiri Narajeenron et al. (2025). Enhancing Team Strategies and Tools to Enhance Performance and Patient Safety Performance Through Medical Movies, Massive Open Online Courses, and 3D Virtual Simulation–Based Interprofessional Education: Mixed Methods Double-Blind Quasi-Experimental Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/67001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same quasi-experimental study; VR-based arm C's overall mTPOT improvement of 0.54 (95% CI 0.36-0.72) was "significantly higher than that in the control group" per generalized estimating equation analysis.

> "Similarly, the mean improvement in scores for arm C was 0.54 (95% CI 0.36-0.72; P<.001), which was also significantly higher than that in the control group."

### Khuansiri Narajeenron et al 2025 (3)

Khuansiri Narajeenron et al. (2025). Enhancing Team Strategies and Tools to Enhance Performance and Patient Safety Performance Through Medical Movies, Massive Open Online Courses, and 3D Virtual Simulation–Based Interprofessional Education: Mixed Methods Double-Blind Quasi-Experimental Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/67001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Domain-level results from the same study: all five TeamSTEPPS domain scores improved significantly in both experimental arms (P<.001), with largest gains in team structure (arm B: 1.04) and smallest in mutual support (0.36-0.49); control arm changes were not significant.

> "Additionally, all TeamSTEPPS domain scores showed significant improvement in both arm B and arm C (P<.001). In contrast, no significant changes were observed in the control group (arm A) for either the overall mTPOT scores or within the 5 TeamSTEPPS domains"
