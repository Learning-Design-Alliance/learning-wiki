### Christenson 2026

Christenson, J., de Langis, K., Hayati, S. A., & Kang, D. (2026). Effects of Varying LLM Access on Essay Writing Behavior. [doi:10.18653/v1/2026.bea-1.48](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.bea-1.48)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-interaction survey responses from the randomized three-group pilot. The limited group reported "more ownership towards their essays," with 62.5% willing to submit as independent work versus 25% in the unlimited group.

> "First, participants in the limited group expressed more ownership towards their essays. When asked if they would submit it as their own work, 62.5% responded yes, 25% were unsure, and 12.5% declined."
