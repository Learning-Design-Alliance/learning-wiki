## Evidence

### A. Mason 2007

A. Mason, E. Cohen, E. Yerushalmi, and C. Singh. (2007). Identifying Differences In Diagnostic Skills Between Physics Students: Developing A Rubric. [doi:10.1063/1.3021239](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3021239)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability check in which two researchers independently graded approximately 10 sample students for the rollercoaster problem, then discussed how criteria should be applied objectively. The article reports graders "could agree to within at least 80% of each other" and that reliability was consistent across about 200 students; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "We found that the graders could agree to within at least 80% of each other in grading the rubric. This established a reasonable inter-rater reliability that was consistent in a more thorough analysis of about 200 students."
