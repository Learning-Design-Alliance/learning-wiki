## Evidence

### Zafra-Tanaka JH 2018

Zafra-Tanaka JH, Hurtado-Villanueva ME, Saenz-Naranjo MdP, Taype-Rondan A. (2018). Self-perceived competence in early diagnosis of cervical cancer among recently graduated physicians from Lima, Peru. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203778

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretive limitation statement in the Discussion, citing prior studies that physicians fail to rate themselves accurately; no new data are offered for this argument in the present study.

> "the level of competence was self-assessed, which is less reliable than direct observation of competence, performed by a group of experts because: 1) acquiescence bias might be present, meaning that the physicians included in the study tried to answer all the questions in a positive way, and 2) perceived competencies do not always match objectively measured competencies"
