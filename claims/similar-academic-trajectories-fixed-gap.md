### Rees Philippa 2025

Rees Philippa, Dronavalli Mithilesh, Carter Ben, Dickson Michelle, Green Charles, Lawler Kate, Lee Evelyn, Uebel Hannah, Gale Chris, Oei Ju Lee. (2025). School Performance of Preterm-Born Children After Intraventricular Hemorrhage. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.47584

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical growth curve modeling of NAPLAN trajectories in the four groups, estimating intercepts and slopes from all available data points, found "all groups had similar trajectories of academic progression with increasing age, overall and for each domain".

> "Notably, all groups had similar trajectories of academic progression with increasing age, overall and for each domain"

### Rees Philippa 2025 (2)

Rees Philippa, Dronavalli Mithilesh, Carter Ben, Dickson Michelle, Green Charles, Lawler Kate, Lee Evelyn, Uebel Hannah, Gale Chris, Oei Ju Lee. (2025). School Performance of Preterm-Born Children After Intraventricular Hemorrhage. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.47584

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

The growth models, including a group × time interaction term while controlling for specified confounders, showed between-group differences "remained fixed, with no significant catch-up, plateau, or decline in total or domain-specific performance with increasing age".

> "The difference in trajectories between groups over time, however, remained fixed, with no significant catch-up, plateau, or decline in total or domain-specific performance with increasing age"
