## Evidence

### Jie Liu 2025

Jie Liu, Zhifeng Li, Jiawang Yang, Hao He & Fang Cui. (2025). The neurocognitive mechanism underlying math avoidance among math anxious people. npj Science of Learning 10:50. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00343-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chain mediation (PROCESS model 6, 5000 bootstrap samples) tested whether DDM difficulty sensitivity (M1) and ROI BOLD activity (M2) mediate math anxiety scores to avoidance (difference in accept choices between difficulties). Path 1 effects were significant, e.g. 0.15 via the HPC model (95% CI excluding zero).

> "signiﬁcant chain mediation effects were observed in three of the above-mentioned ROIs: the HPC, the PI and the MCC. For the HPC, the indirect effect for all the three pathways were signiﬁcant. The effect value is 0.15 for indirect path1 (95%CI: (0.00–0.29]),"
