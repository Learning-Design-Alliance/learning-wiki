`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis in the 179-student pilot study relating Motivational Persistence Scale factors to conscientiousness and exam score. The article reports "a statistically significant positive correlation between persistence and conscientiousness dimension (r = 0.518, p < 0.0001)", plus significant score correlations for ambition (r = 0.173), implementation (r = 0.255), recurrence (r = −0.269), and self-discipline (r = 0.227).
