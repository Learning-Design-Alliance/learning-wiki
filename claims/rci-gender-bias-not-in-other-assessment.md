## Evidence

### J. S. Aslanides and C. M. Savage 2013

J. S. Aslanides and C. M. Savage. (2013). The Relativity Concept Inventory: development, analysis and results. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.010118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.010118)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Gender comparison in the ANU Physics 2 class (18 females, 45 males on the post-test). The article reports the differences "signiﬁcant at the p ≤ 0.05 level according to the Kolmogorov-Smirnov test", e.g. mean item difficulty 0.63 vs 0.72 post-test (p = 0.003), while exam (p = 0.95), homework (p = 1) and ATAR (p = 0.96) showed no difference.

> "males scored higher than females in: the pre-test, post-test, normalised gain, and in conﬁdence. All these diﬀerences are signiﬁcant at the p ≤ 0.05 level according to the Kolmogorov-Smirnov test."
