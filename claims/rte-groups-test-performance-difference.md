## Evidence

### Wise 2005

Wise, S. L., & Kong, X. (2005). Response Time Effort: A New Measure of Examinee Motivation in Computer-Based Tests. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, Montreal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED490203. Published version, Applied Measurement in Education: [doi:10.1207/s15324818ame1802_2](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15324818ame1802_2)

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA (Table 2) comparing ILT test performance across three RTE groups in the 472-examinee sample. The row for ILT Test Performance reports means of 37.87, 47.33, and 63.26 with F(2,469) = 87.22, p < .001, and effect size ω² = .26; the authors state the lowest and highest RTE groups "showing performance differences that exceeded two standard deviations."
