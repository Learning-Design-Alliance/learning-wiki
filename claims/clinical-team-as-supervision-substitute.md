## Evidence

### Lerch S.P. 2025

Lerch S.P., Huwendiek S., Nendaz M., Klöppel S., Pinilla S. (2025). Perceptions of Ad Hoc Entrustment, the Need for Supervision, and Coping Strategies in Clinical Residents: A Qualitative Study. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006019

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis subtheme from the 12 interviews. Residents reported that interdisciplinary workspace structures allowed team members to provide supervision "not only when a resident was distressed and felt overwhelmed but sometimes as an implicit standard," e.g., consulting experienced nursing staff.

> "In addition to the official supervisor, usually senior physicians or leading physicians, the residents had other, unofficial sources of clinical supervision, including other attending physicians, peer residents, psychologists, and nurses."
