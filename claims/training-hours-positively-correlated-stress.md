Jiang Juan, Deng Pengbo, Li Ruijia, Yang Qian, Hu Chengping, Li Yuanyuan. (2025). The effect of research training on research skills, academic performance, and stress level among medical undergraduates in China: a cross-sectional survey. `q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis among the 95 trained undergraduates: "weekly training hours were significantly positively correlated with stress scores" (r = 0.248), measured by the seven stress items of the DASS-21 (score range 0–21).

> "Notably, weekly training hours were significantly positively correlated with stress scores (r = 0.248, P = 0.015)."
