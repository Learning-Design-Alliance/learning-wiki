## Evidence

### Andrew Ferstl and Nathan Moore 2007

Andrew Ferstl and Nathan Moore. (2007). Measuring the 2D Vector Aspect of Momentum Using Only One Dimension. http://groups.physics.umn.edu/physed/Research/PSL/pslintro.html [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0803.4142](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0803.4142)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors report this from their own teaching experience, not from a controlled study; students are encouraged to work on the prediction in cooperative groups before coming to lab. The article states students "seem to learn comparatively more physics" under this pre-lab prediction structure.

> "this pre-lab work, or “prediction” significantly improves the lab experience for students in that they seem to learn comparatively more physics and do not seem to be so quickly frustrated when they encounter problems in the lab."
