### Şenel Elaldı 2016

Şenel Elaldı, Veli Batdı. (2016). Comparing Effects of Different Applications on Pre-Service Teachers: A Meta–Analysis. Journal of Education and Training Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i7.1456

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of studies on retention of pre-service teachers, computed with Hedges' d in MetaWin and classified by Thalheimer and Cook (2002). The review reports "Learning Together Technique had the highest effect size ( d =2,0606" and Jigsaw the lowest (d=0,5461).

> "Table 1 shows that, in terms of retention scores, while Learning Together Technique had the highest effect size ( d =2,0606 -a huge level -) in primary school pre -service teachers, the Jigsaw Technique had the lowest one ( d= 0,5461-a medium level -) in pre -service te achers studying in the department of Turkish Education."
