## Evidence

### Mohamad Nawal 2025

Mohamad Nawal, Rousseau Kim-Louise, Dowlut Fatimah, Gering Milton, Thomas Kevin G. F. (2025). Symptoms of ADHD and Other Common Mental Disorders Influence Academic Success in South African Undergraduates. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547241310659

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression (Table 10) on three-wave completers; significant model fit F(7, 172) = 3.39, p < .001, R2 = .12, "accounted for almost entirely by the contribution of BDI-II and BAI scores"; ASRS and AUDIT scores were non-significant predictors. No effect size estimate was printed for the individual predictors.

> "the combination of ADHD symptoms with symptoms of depression, anxiety, and risky alcohol use was a stronger predictor of academic adjustment at the end of the semester than ADHD symptoms alone (specifically, higher combined symptom counts predicted worse adjustment; see Table 10)"
