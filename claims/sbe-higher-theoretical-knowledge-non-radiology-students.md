## Evidence

### Yu Huajun 2025

Yu Huajun, Zhao Jin, Su Lina, Zhang Jianjun. (2025). Application of simulation-based education in the instruction of medical students from non-radiology specialties. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07572-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative study of 317 second-year non-radiology medical students assigned to SBE (n=82) or TBE (n=235) by rotation batch, not randomized. The SBE group's median theoretical score was "93.25 vs. 71.00" for TBE, Z = -13.48, P < 0.001; no standardized effect size is printed for this contrast in the quoted text.

> "The post-test results at the end of the teaching activity revealed that students in the SBE group achieved significantly higher scores on theoretical knowledge (93.25 vs. 71.00, Z = -13.48, P < 0.001)"
