`q1 · i?`

The authors' stated educational importance of the study: they argue increased cognitive flexibility, defined as the ability to retrieve and use information from varied knowledge bases, "may strengthen the effectiveness of phonemic awareness, reading and spelling instruction" for low-flexibility students. This is an interpretive claim, hedged with may.

> "Increased cognitive flexibility (i.e. the ability to retreive and use information from a variety of knowledge bases to construct situational meaning in a complex knowledge domain) may strengthen the effectiveness of phonemic awareness, reading and spelling instruction for kindergarten and first-grade students with low cognitive flexibility."
