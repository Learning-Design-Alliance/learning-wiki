Moffitt 2020¶

Moffitt, RL; Padgett, C; Grieve, R. (2020). Accessibility and emotionality of online assessment feedback: Using emoticons to enhance student perceptions of marker competence and warmth. Computers and Education, 143. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103654

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Same between-groups emoticon experiment (N = 241 undergraduates, faux essay with written feedback); the competence contrast is reported at the 3-emoticon frequency, comparing happy against sad and confused faces, with no effect size printed.

"Furthermore, marker competence was significantly higher when 3 happy face emoticons were presented in the feedback than when 3 sad or confused faces were included."
