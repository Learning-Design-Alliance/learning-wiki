Evidence¶

Wanzek 2016¶

Wanzek, J., Vaughn, S., Scammacca, N., Gatlin, B., Walker, M. A., & Capin, P. (2016). Meta-analyses of the effects of tier 2 type reading interventions in grades K-3. Educational Psychology Review, 28, 551-576. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED579894

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Trim-and-fill (Card, 2012) funnel-plot asymmetry analysis applied to each meta-analysis. For standardized foundational skills, "16 studies may be missing" and the adjusted mean effect size was 0.32; standardized language/comprehension adjusted to 0.22 and not-standardized to 0.75.

"the trim-and-fill analyses found evidence of publication bias that suggested that 16 studies may be missing. The mean effect size calculated using imputed values for the missing studies was 0.32 (95% CI = 0.21, 0.43)."
