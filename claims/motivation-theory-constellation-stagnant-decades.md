The article cites Wigfield and Koenka's (2020) editorial observation that "the dominant theories in the academic motivation field" were unchanged between the 2000 and 2020 Contemporary Educational Psychology special issues. This is an attributed observational claim, not a systematic analysis.

> "When we look back to the 2000 CEP [i.e., Contemporary Educational Psychology] special issue, it is interesting to us that the dominant theories in the academic motivation field included in this special issue [i.e., the 2020 special issue] remain the same theories as were represented in that issue"
