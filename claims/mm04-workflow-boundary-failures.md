# In the MM-04 workflow case, similar-looking model activity has different evidential validity: GPT-5.5 closes the evidence-to-action loop while GLM-5.1 leaves the assigned deck unchanged and Gemini-3.1-Pro misses required numeric or visual evidence

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Three representative trajectories fail at different links of one teaching contract: GPT-5.5 closes the loop, GLM-5.1 produces plausible material without changing the assigned deck, and Gemini-3.1-Pro reaches the designated artifact but misses required evidence. ### Zixin Chen 2026

Zixin Chen, Peng Liu, Rui Sheng, Haobo Li, Jianhong Tu, Xiaodong Deng, Kashun Shum, Dayiheng Liu, Huamin Qu. (2026). Are Agents Ready to Teach? A Multi-Stage Benchmark for Real-World Teaching Workflows. Preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14322

`q1 · i?` · `design · r?`

Case-study audit of the Stage 3 workflow MM-04, in which an ECON101 instructor requests supply–demand remediation from the Fall 2023 midterm and the existing Week 5 deck; four weak KCs must be recovered from 120 historical attempts. The article shows verifier-level checkpoints localize distinct breaks in the evidence–decision–action chain.

> "GPT-5.5 closes the loop: diagnosed weaknesses become visual slide remediation, the quiz targets the same concepts, and the messages refer to that completed intervention. GLM-5.1 crosses the content boundary but not the object-identity boundary: it produces plausible microeconomics material while leaving the assigned deck unchanged"
