## Evidence

### Luckie Douglas B. 2017

Luckie Douglas B., Hoskinson Anne-Marie, Griffin Caleigh E., Hess Andrea L., Price Katrina J., Tawa Alex, Thacker Samantha M. (2017). Integrating Concepts in Biology Textbook Increases Learning: Assessment Triangulation Using Concept Inventory, Card Sorting, and MCAT Instruments, Followed by Longitudinal Tracking. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-06-0204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of ICB students (n = 76) and controls (n = 98) in equal-sized sections of the same course with reformed pedagogies, ACT-normalized. ICB students showed "significantly higher gains at the end of the academic year" (+43.19 ± 7.02%, p < 0.01).

> "Overall, students who participated in the ICB curriculum had significantly higher gains at the end of the academic year compared with students in the traditional biology course (+43.19 ± 7.02%, p < 0.01; Figure 1A)."
