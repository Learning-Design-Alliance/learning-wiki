## Evidence

### Steegen 2018

Steegen, A.; Hasendonckx, F.; Cock, M. (2018). Can an Interactive Learning Path on A Tablet PC Counter Misconceptions on the Formation of Clouds and Wind? Review of International Geographical Education Online (RIGEO), 8 (1), 53-73. http://www.rigeo.org/vol8no1/Number1Spring/RIGEO-V8-N1-3.pdf

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA on posttest gain scores controlling for pretest results (F(2,306)=5.599, p=0.004) with post-hoc comparisons. Tablet condition significantly underperformed both other formats; traditional and experiment conditions did not differ significantly (p=0.280).
