## Evidence

### Gicquelais Rachel E. 2019

Gicquelais Rachel E., Mezuk Briana, Foxman Betsy, Thomas Laura, Bohnert Amy S. B. (2019). Justice involvement patterns, overdose experiences, and naloxone knowledge among men and women in criminal justice diversion addiction treatment. Harm Reduction Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12954-019-0317-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted quasi-Poisson regressions among the 151 women in the same cross-sectional sample. Both associations were described as "marginally more likely" with confidence intervals crossing 1 and p-values of 0.08 and 0.07; no effect size was printed.

> "Women who experienced an overdose (PR [95% CI] 1.5 [0.95–2.4], p = 0.08) or witnessed an overdose (PR [95% CI] 1.4 [0.97–2.1], p = 0.07) in their lifetime were marginally more likely to have naloxone knowledge."
