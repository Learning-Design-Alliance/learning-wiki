## Evidence

### Warren 2010

Warren, S. J., Dondlinger, M. J., Jones, G., & Whitworth, C. (2010). Leveraging PBL and Game to Redesign an Introductory Course. i-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, 7(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1098365

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental, posttest comparison of 89 undergraduates in a large southwestern university; treatment n=32, comparison n=57. The article reports a two-sample t-test assuming unequal variances with t=3.90, crit=1.67, and posttest means of M=85.96 (treatment) vs M=78.83 (comparison).

> "student achievement, as measured by post-test in both groups, and compared using a two-sample t-test assuming unequal variances showed statistically significant improvement in the treatment group the comparison group"
