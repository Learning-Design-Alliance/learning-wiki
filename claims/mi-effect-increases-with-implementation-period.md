## Evidence

### Eyüp Yurt & Seyat Polat 2015

Eyüp Yurt & Seyat Polat. (2015). The Effectiveness of Multiple Intelligence Applications on Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Social Studies Education Research 6(1), 84-122. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105368

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Sub-group moderator analysis of the 81 comparisons classified by implementation period into three groups. The homogeneity test showed a significant difference; the article concludes the effect increases as the implementation period becomes longer.

> "According to results of the analysis, average effect size of these studies was calculated as 0,868; 1, 274 and 1,606 respectively. The result of the homogeneity test showed that there was a significant difference between these effect size values according to their implementation process (Q B=8,303; p=0,016)."
