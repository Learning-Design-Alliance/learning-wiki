## Evidence

### Jiayu Huang 2025

Jiayu Huang, Ruoxin Ritter Wang, Jen-Hao Liu, Boming Xia, Yue Huang, Ruoxi Sun, Jason (Minhui) Xue, Jinan Zou. (2025). A Meta-Analysis of LLM Effects on Students Across Qualification, Socialisation, and Subjectification. A PREPRINT. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22725

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis within the qualification domain (k = 123 effect sizes) found LLM role (QB = 13.341, p = .0013) and intervention duration (QB = 18.091, p < .001) highly significant; >8-week interventions yielded g = 1.024 versus g = 0.389 for 1–4 weeks.

> "LLMs framed as interactive tutors (g= 0.902 ) or collaborative partners (g= 0.842 ) generated substantially larger improvements in performance than when they functioned as passive tools (g= 0.436 )."
