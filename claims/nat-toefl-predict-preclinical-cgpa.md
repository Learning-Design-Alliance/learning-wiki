## Evidence

### Dabaliz Al-Awwab 2017

Dabaliz Al-Awwab, Kaadan Samy, Dabbagh M. Marwan, Barakat Abdulaziz, Shareef Mohammad Abrar, Al-Tannir Mohamad, Obeidat Akef, Mohamed Ayman. (2017). Predictive validity of pre-admission assessments on medical student performance. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5a10.04e1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on retrospective admission and performance data from a Saudi medical college, predicting preclinical cGPA from five pre-admission variables. The regression found "the NAT being a stronger predictor" of the two significant positive predictors; the overall equation was significant (F(5,5)=10.729, p=0.01, R2 of 0.951).

> "the NAT (p=0.04, B=0.08, t=2.752) and TOEFL (p=0.017, B=0.01, t=3.527) scores were both significant positive predictors of students' cGPA in preclinical years of medical school, with the NAT being a stronger predictor"
