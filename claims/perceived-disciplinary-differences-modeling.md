Analytical argument (Section III) identifying four perceived differences between the disciplines that arise from the nature of the systems each studies; the article argues these are reconciled by the shared modeling process.

> "Biology and physics typically diﬀer in the types of systems that each studies, the behavior of those systems, the kinds of measurements that each makes, and the role of mathematics in each ﬁeld."
