### Lukics Krisztina Sára 2022

Lukics Krisztina Sára, Lukács Ágnes. (2022). Modality, presentation, domain and training effects in statistical learning. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-24951-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Third three-way ANOVA (Modality, Domain, Training Type) on the modality-optimized conditions of the same 360-participant artificial grammar experiment. The Modality effect was "not significant"; only ηp2 < 0.01 is printed.

> "With presentation type fitted to modality, the effect of Modality was not significant, F(1,228) = 0.34, p = 0.560, ηp2 < 0.01, BF10 = 1335.81."
