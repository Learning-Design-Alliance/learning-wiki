# Improvements in executive function mediate the intervention's effects on learning-related behaviors and problem behaviors

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The indirect effect of treatment assignment on learning-related behaviors through EF improvement was significant (b = 0.13, CI [0.05, 0.26]). [→ Brock et al. 2018](#brock-et-al-2018)
`q3 i?` The indirect effect of treatment assignment on problem behaviors through EF improvement was significant (b = –0.07, CI [–0.17, –0.01]). [→ Brock et al. 2018 (2)](#brock-et-al-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Brock et al. 2018

Laura L. Brock, William M. Murrah, Elizabeth A. Cottone, Andrew J. Mashburn, and David W. Grissmer. (2018). An after-school intervention targeting executive function and visuospatial skills also improves classroom behavior. International Journal of Behavioral Development, 42(5), 474–484. https://doi.org/10.1177/0165025417738057

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple mediation analysis with bias-corrected bootstrapping (10,000 iterations, 95% CIs; N = 87) tested indirect effects through EF. The learning-related behavior indirect effect CI excluded zero; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The CIs around the indirect effects of EF on learning-related ( b ¼ 0.13, CI [0.05, 0.26]) and problem behaviors ( b ¼ –0.07, CI [–0.17, –0.01]) were found to exclude zero"

### Brock et al. 2018 (2)

Laura L. Brock, William M. Murrah, Elizabeth A. Cottone, Andrew J. Mashburn, and David W. Grissmer. (2018). An after-school intervention targeting executive function and visuospatial skills also improves classroom behavior. International Journal of Behavioral Development, 42(5), 474–484. https://doi.org/10.1177/0165025417738057

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same bootstrap mediation analysis found the indirect effect through EF on problem behaviors had a CI excluding zero (b = –0.07, CI [–0.17, –0.01]); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The CIs around the indirect effects of EF on learning-related ( b ¼ 0.13, CI [0.05, 0.26]) and problem behaviors ( b ¼ –0.07, CI [–0.17, –0.01]) were found to exclude zero"
