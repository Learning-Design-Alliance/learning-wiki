Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience 2019¶

Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience. (2019). Introduction to the Learning Sciences: 10 Key Insights & Practices for Educators. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/d5550311-8f60-4d0d-9d4d-14821054e0e8

The synthesis reports that autonomy and meaningful goals improve intrinsic motivation, leading students to persist longer and process information more deeply; student interest is also called "a powerful inﬂuence on learning".

"when students have a sense of control over their own learning, and the opportunity to set goals that are not only personally meaningful but also have the potential to beneﬁt the world, their intrinsic motivation improves"
