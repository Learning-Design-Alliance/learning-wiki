Koch, M., & Peters Hinton, V. (2025). Voices of Undergraduate Students Taking Introductory Statistics Courses [Project Report]. Digital Promise.

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theme from student interviews: 28 students cited wanting "detailed feedback with explanations that link to course content." A student example quote states the feedback "doesn't tell you why it's wrong, so I just move on."

> "The feedback doesn't tell you why it's wrong, so I just move on. It would be more helpful if it said, 'This is the correct answer, and you can find it in module X.'"
