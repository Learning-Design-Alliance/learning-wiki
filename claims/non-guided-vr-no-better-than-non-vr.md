# VR (non-guided exploration) did not differ significantly from Non VR for either assimilator or accommodator learners

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For assimilator learners the non-guided VR vs Non VR comparison was not significant (p of 0.503); for accommodator learners it was also not significant (p of 0.542). [→ Chwen Jen Chen 2005](#chwen-jen-chen-2005)

## Evidence

### Chwen Jen Chen 2005

Chwen Jen Chen, Seong Chong Toh, & Wan Mohd Fauzy Wan Ismail. (2005). Are Learning Styles Relevant To Virtual Reality? Journal of Research on Technology in Education, 38(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ728898

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc pairwise comparisons with Holm's sequential Bonferroni following the significant one-way ANCOVAs. The corresponding accommodator comparison "was not signiﬁcant (p of 0.542 is not less than 0.05)"; adjusted mean differences were 2.352 (assimilators) and 2.197 (accommodators). No effect sizes are printed.

> "The comparison between the assimilator learners of the Non VR mode and the assimilator learners of the VR (non-guided exploration) mode was not signiﬁcant (p of 0.503 is not less than 0.05)."
