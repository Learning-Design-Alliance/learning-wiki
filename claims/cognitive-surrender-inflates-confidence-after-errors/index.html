Dongming Mei 2026¶

Dongming Mei, Katherine Moore, and Ben Sayler. (2026). Preparing Students for AI-Powered Materials Discovery: A Workflow-Aligned Framework for AI Literacy, Equity, and Scientific Judgment. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09624 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.09624

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The paper reports, citing Shaw and Nave's experimental work, that 'AI assistance can increase accuracy when AI is correct, reduce accuracy when AI is faulty, and inflate confidence even after errors.' No effect sizes are printed in the paper's account.
