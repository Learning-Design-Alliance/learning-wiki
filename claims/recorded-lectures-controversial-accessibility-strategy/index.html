Gurher Sidhu 2025¶

Gurher Sidhu, Fatima Atif, Franz Newland. (2025). "AI is reducing options I had used": exploring faculty perceptions of accessible course design in a ChatGPT world. York University. https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/e4hmv_v1

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Theme from thematic analysis of survey free-text and six faculty interviews on recorded lectures and hybrid classrooms. Faculty reported that "attendance is “decimated”", students deferred viewing until just before tests, discussion quality eroded, and privacy worries included deepfakes.

"Many professors indicated that 246 when recordings of live lectures are made available, attendance is “decimated”, leading to observably 247 poorer grades and a lower quality learning experience."
