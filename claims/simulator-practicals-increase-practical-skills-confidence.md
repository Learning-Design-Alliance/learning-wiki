Mazgutova N, Mokra D, Calkovska A, Plevkova J, Biringerova Z, Mokry J. (2026). Teaching Physiology-First Experience With Using a Simulator in Teaching Physiology of the Cardiovascular System. Physiological Research. https://doi.org/10.33549/physiolres.935831

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported questionnaire and open-ended comment data from the end-of-session survey; students felt the simulation resembled professional practice and built confidence in auscultation and blood pressure measurement skills.
