## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 81% of students agreed the poster presentation was an efficient form of assessment and 75% agreed it was a fair way to assess students. [→ Ross Andrew 2019](#ross-andrew-2019)

## Evidence

### Ross Andrew 2019

Ross Andrew, Dlungwane Thembelihle, Van Wyk Jacqueline. (2019). Using poster presentation to assess large classes: a case study of a first-year undergraduate module at a South African university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1863-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student Likert questionnaire (n = 230 returned) in the same cross-sectional case study; 184 of 228 respondents (81%) agreed the poster presentation was an efficient form of assessment. A separate item showed 169 of 225 (75%) agreeing posters are a fair way to assess students.
