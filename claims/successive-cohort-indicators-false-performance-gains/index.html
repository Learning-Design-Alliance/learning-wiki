Glazerman 2011¶

Glazerman, Steven M., and Liz Potamites. (2011). False Performance Gains: A Critique of Successive Cohort Indicators. Working Paper 2. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/false-performance-gains-a-critique-of-successive-cohort-indicators

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

The paper's stated purpose, per its abstract, is to assess which test-score-based school evaluation methods "provide useful information for school accountability and why." The title identifies the paper's central critique: successive cohort indicators can produce false performance gains. No empirical statistics are printed in the available text.
