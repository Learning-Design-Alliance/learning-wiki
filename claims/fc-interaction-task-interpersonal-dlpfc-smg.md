## Evidence

### Zijun Yin 2025

Zijun Yin, Bin Xuan, Chengchi Liu, Jingchao Yi, Xiaoyan Zheng & Mingming Zhang. (2025). The influence of task and interpersonal interdependence on cooperative behavior and its neural mechanisms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00303-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures 2×2 ANOVA across 21 fNIRS channels found a significant interaction in BA9 (DLPFC.R) and BA40 (SMG.R), "F (1, 66) = 4.234*, p = 0.044, ηp2 = 0.060"; post hoc tests showed friend > stranger FC under LICT (p = 0.041) and HICT > LICT for strangers (p = 0.026).

> "the intra-brain FC values under the friend dyad were signiﬁcantly higher than those under the stranger dyad in the BA9 (CH04: DLPFC.R) and BA40 (CH18: SMG.R),p = 0.041."
