## Evidence

### Jung Sua 2025

Jung Sua, Park Jinkyung. (2025). Educational needs for medication safety competence among nurses by clinical ladder stage. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0319483

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group finding: beyond on-the-job training, participants reported minimal medication safety training, and experienced nurses requested detailed analyses of medication error cases while Group 2 requested examples.

> "All groups acknowledged that medication safety education was insufficient and raised concerns about the limited opportunities for face-to-face education owing to varying work shifts and inconsistent end times."

### Jung Sua 2025 (2)

Jung Sua, Park Jinkyung. (2025). Educational needs for medication safety competence among nurses by clinical ladder stage. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0319483

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Participant quotation from Group 2 characterizing onboarding medication safety education as an extension of basic content that did not suit practicing nurses' needs.

> "I did receive medication safety education during the new employee training, but I found the level and content to be quite basic, almost at an elementary school level. It was rather ordinary and unremarkable."
