## Evidence

### Jiang Su 2026

Jiang Su, Woodcock Anna, Haddad Kamel, Hernandez Paul R. (2026). Engaging Latinx or Hispanic-Identifying Families in Science Education: A Longitudinal Intervention Study. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001047

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Diagnostic placebo analyses compared Control Group 1 and Control Group 2 on all outcome variables at T2 and T3 using propensity score-weighted post hoc tests with a Scheffé adjustment. Only descriptive statistics suggested a group difference in family obligations at the six-month follow-up before latent modeling.

> "no significant differences emerged between the control groups on any outcome after weighting, suggesting that the propensity score adjustment successfully balanced the groups on observed characteristics and that subsequent treatment effects are unlikely to reflect preexisting differences between control groups"
