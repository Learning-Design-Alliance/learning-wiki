### Keith R Bujak 2010

Comparing the Matter and Interactions Curriculum with a Traditional Physics Curriculum: A Think Aloud Study. Originally published 2008. Transcript coding of the falling balls problem in the think-aloud study; the formula F = ma led M&I students astray, whereas most remaining M&I students and 14 of 18 correct traditional students relied on the fact that mass does not matter.
