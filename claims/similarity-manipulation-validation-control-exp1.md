# Target-distractor similarity manipulation validated: low-similarity search is faster than high-similarity search

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Oudeng Jia 2025

Oudeng Jia, Qingsong Tan, Sihan Zhang, Ke Jia & Mengyuan Gong. (2025). The precision of attention selection during reward learning influences the mechanisms of value-driven attention. npj Science of Learning 10:49. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00342-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Control Experiment 1 (N = 16) had participants perform singleton search under both similarity conditions with no reward feedback. Normalized RTs were "signiﬁcantly faster responses in low than high target- distractor similarity condition", with printed Cohen's d = −1.200, confirming the similarity manipulation was valid.

> "Normalized RTs were signiﬁcantly faster responses in low than high target- distractor similarity condition (two-sample t-test: t(78) = -5.37,p < 0.001, Cohen’sd= −1.200, 95% CI = [−1.673,−0.720])."
