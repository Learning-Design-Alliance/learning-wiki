## Evidence

### Nabeela C 2025

Nabeela C, Shykhon M, Milward M, Yonel Z. (2025). The use of 360-videos to bridge the gap between pre-clinical and clinical dental teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06946-w

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of three online focus groups (5–8 students each) with 20 undergraduate dental students about to start periodontology clinical training; transcripts were coded inductively in NVivo. The study reports "the importance of familiarity with the clinical environment, preparation prior to attending clinical sessions and the benefit of practical experience."

> "The 3 key themes identified were: the importance of familiarity with the clinical environment, preparation prior to attending clinical sessions and the benefit of practical experience."
