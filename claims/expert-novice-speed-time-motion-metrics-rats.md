### Elkhouly AG 2025

Elkhouly AG, Santhirakumaran G, Shanahan B, Batchelor T, Wilson H, Waller D, Stamenkovic S, Lau K. (2025). Objective Assessment of Ergonomics and Performance of Thoracic Surgeons During Real-Life Anatomical Robotic Pulmonary Resections. Interdisciplinary Cardiovascular and Thoracic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1093/icvts/ivaf180

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kinovea-based video motion tracking of 2 working instruments during 180 pulmonary vessel isolations/divisions (95 expert, 85 novice) in 67 robotic resections. The results section reports: "Group 1 expressed a higher speed (2.8 ± 0.8 vs 2.1 ± 0.2 cm/s, P < 0.001) with a shorter surgical time in group 1 (387 ± 277 vs 520 ± 322 s, P = .001)."

> "Group 1 expressed a higher speed (2.8 ± 0.8 vs 2.1 ± 0.2 cm/s, P < 0.001) with a shorter surgical time in group 1 (387 ± 277 vs 520 ± 322 s, P = .001)."
