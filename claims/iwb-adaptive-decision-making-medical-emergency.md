### Jun J 2014

Jun J, Bridges SM. (2014). Educational Technologies in Problem-Based Learning in Health Sciences Education: A Systematic Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3240

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Study comparing two groups of seven third-year medical students solving a simulated medical emergency, one using a traditional whiteboard and one using an IWB. The review reports that "IWB group participants engaged in more adaptive decision-making behavior earlier than the traditional whiteboard group."

> "Group differences were found in that IWB group participants engaged in more adaptive decision-making behavior earlier than the traditional whiteboard group, which led to shared understandings and subsequently to more effective patient management."
