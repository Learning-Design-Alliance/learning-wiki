## Evidence

### Wang Ji-nuo 2026

Wang Ji-nuo, Lv Hanbing, Sun Yi, Cao Wen, Jiang Luhua. (2026). A workplace-based learning program on clerkship of internal medicine improves medical student's clinical competence: a retrospective comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08719-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

In the same retrospective cohort, the 6-station formative OSCE total was compared between WBLP (n = 39) and control (n = 38) groups; the quote reports "Cohen's d = 0.97, 95% CI 0.50–1.45". All 77 students completed the OSCE.

> "The total OSCE score was higher among WBLP participants (86.4 ± 5.4 vs. 80.9 ± 5.9, p < 0.001; Cohen's d = 0.97, 95% CI 0.50–1.45), reflecting overall enhanced clinical proficiency (Fig. 2B)."
