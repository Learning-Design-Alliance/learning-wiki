## Evidence

### Hao 2025

Hao, Z., Jiang, J., Yu, J., Liu, Z., & Zhang, Y. (2025). Student-AI Interaction in an LLM-Empowered Learning Environment: A Cluster Analysis of Engagement Profiles. arXiv:2503.01694v3. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney U tests on coded message proportions between subclusters of active completers (n=45 and n=28) showed significant differences across behavioral, cognitive, and emotional dimensions.

> "the results showed that Cluster 4–I had a significantly higher proportion of asking question ( M= 0.66 , SD= 0.14 ) and negotiating and confirming ideas (M= 0.08 , SD= 0.07 ) compared to Cluster 4–II (M= 0.32 , SD= 0.14 ; M= 0.05 , SD= 0.04 , respectively), z= 6.56 ,p < .001; and z= 2.39 , p=.02 , respectively."
