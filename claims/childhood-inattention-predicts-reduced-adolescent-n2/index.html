Evidence¶

Einziger Tzlil 2021¶

Einziger Tzlil, Ben-Shachar Mattan S., Devor Tali, Shmueli Michael, Auerbach Judith G., Berger Andrea. (2021). "My Brain Can Stop": An ERP Study of Longitudinal Prediction of Inhibitory Control in Adolescence. Brain Sciences, 11(1), 100. https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci11010100

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression models in this prospective longitudinal study of 63 boys predicted N2 amplitude at 17 years from symptom domains averaged across 54 months and 7 years. The article reports "inattention symptoms throughout childhood uniquely predicted the N2 amplitude"; the model was significant with adjusted R2 of 11%.

"inattention symptoms throughout childhood uniquely predicted the N2 amplitude, above and beyond hyperactivity-impulsivity symptoms, and mothers' education."
