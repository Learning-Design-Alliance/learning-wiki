## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hafen 2015

Hafen, C. A., Ruzek, E. A., Gregory, A., Allen, J. P., & Mikami, A. Y. (2015). Focusing on teacher–student interactions eliminates the negative impact of students’ disruptive behavior on teacher perceptions. International Journal of Behavioral Development. https://doi.org/10.1177/0165025415579455

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-level MPlus analysis of the randomized MTP-S trial (86 teachers, 1195 students) predicting Spring teacher projections from Fall student-reported disruptive behavior, controlling for Fall projections, grade level and gender. The article reports b = –.21, 95% CI [–.33, –.09].

> "students who reported greater disruptive behavior in the Fall were significantly more likely to have worse educational attainment projections from their teacher in the Spring, b ¼ –.21, 95% CI [–.33, –.09]."
