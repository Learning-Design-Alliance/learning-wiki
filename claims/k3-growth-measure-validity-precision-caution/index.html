Lisa Dragoset 2019¶

Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education, Evaluation, and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/measuring-school-performance-for-early-elementary-grades-in-maryland

The report's Key Findings state that a K-3 school-level growth measure was estimated and examined, and that "concerns about its validity and precision" suggest cautious use for accountability.

"A K–3 school-level growth measure was estimated and examined. The study identified some concerns about its validity and precision that suggest it should be used for accountability with caution."
