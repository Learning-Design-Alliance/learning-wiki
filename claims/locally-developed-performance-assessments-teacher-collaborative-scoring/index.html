Jenna Howard Terrell 2023¶

Jenna Howard Terrell, Sarah Barzee. (2023). Graduation Requirements and Measures: A Review of Performance Assessment Implementation in Select States for the New York State Education Department. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED633310

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Cross-case finding from state interviews: "every state relies on locally-developed performance assessments that are developed and piloted by teachers." Interviewees stressed local context, and states like Oregon and Virginia provide resources such as student samples.

"Each state has its own procedures for developing and scoring performance assessments, though every state relies on locally-developed performance assessments that are developed and piloted by teachers."

Jenna Howard Terrell 2023 (2)¶

Jenna Howard Terrell, Sarah Barzee. (2023). Graduation Requirements and Measures: A Review of Performance Assessment Implementation in Select States for the New York State Education Department. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED633310

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview finding on scoring practice: teachers "develop and calibrate the scoring rubrics together and then pilot the rubrics" before sharing them, per the report's cross-case synthesis.

"Almost all case study states mentioned that they approached scoring as a collaborative process, where teachers develop and calibrate the scoring rubrics together and then pilot the rubrics before making them available to others."
