### Rauner 2018

Rauner, M., Perna, L. W., & Kanter, M. J. (2018). California College Promise: Program Characteristics and Perceptions from the Field. WestEd. https://californiacollegepromise.WestEd.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/College-Promise-Landscape-Scan-2018.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview findings on perceived student outcomes; five interviewees emphasized financial support enabling low-income students to study more and work less, and two expected enrollment increases from targeted outreach.

> "All 13 interviewees who responded to the question about student outcomes predicted that College Promise programs will improve college enrollment, persistence, and completion."

### Rauner 2018 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`
