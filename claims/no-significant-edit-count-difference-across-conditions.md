### Kumar 2025

Kumar, H., Reza, M., Mitchell, J., Musabirov, I., Zhang, L., & Liut, M. (2025). Understanding Help-Seeking Behavior of Students Using LLMs vs. Web Search for Writing SQL Queries. Preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3735091.3737569](https://doi.org/10.1145/3735091.3737569)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Number of edits to the final SQL query was manually extracted from video recordings in the randomized interview study. The test was not significant, and "students needed just under 2 changes to arrive at their final query in the assigned time."

> "The Kruskal-Wallis H test for changes between conditions was not significant (𝜒 2(2) = 1.55, 𝑑 𝑓 = 2, 𝑝 = 0.46). In all conditions, students needed just under 2 changes to arrive at their final query in the assigned time."
