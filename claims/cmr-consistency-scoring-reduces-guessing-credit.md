### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2014

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2014). Coupled Multiple-response vs. Free-response Conceptual Assessment: An Example from Upper-division Physics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.6310](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.6310)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Simulation check of the CMR scoring rubric: 100 computer-generated random responses were scored, averaging 13%, which the authors attribute to the rubric's consistency checks subtracting points for reasoning elements inconsistent with the chosen method.

> "Using this scoring rubric on 100 computer generated re- sponses simulating random guessing, we found an average score of 13%."
