`q1 · i?` · `review · r3`

The SREA reports, citing Sevdalis et al., that "individual differences across the population in visual representation strength determines how MP impacts performance", with groups needing separation on imagery strength via validated tools such as the Mental Imagery Questionnaire. This is the review's attribution, not a new test.

> "Sevdalis et al. [41] argue that individual differences across the population in visual representation strength determines how MP impacts performance."

### Snelgrove Huon; Gabbott Ben 2020 (2)

Snelgrove Huon; Gabbott Ben. (2020). Critical analysis of evidence about the impacts on surgical teams of ‘mental practice’ in systematic reviews: a systematic rapid evidence assessment (SREA). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02131-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesis of MP content and process across the six reviews: delivery modes ranged from group and one-to-one delivery, video and written scripts, to self-directed home practice and embedding in relaxation or warm-up routines. The authors conclude "it was difficult to discern within and across the reviews the exact nature of the impacts of MP".

> "As a result, it was difficult to discern within and across the reviews the exact nature of the impacts of MP. Or rather it was difficult to see the boundaries of MP with other kinds of stimuli to shape performance."
