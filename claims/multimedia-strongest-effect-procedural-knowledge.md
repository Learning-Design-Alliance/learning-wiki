### Tani 2022

Tani, M., Manuguerra, M., & Khan, S. (2022). Can videos affect learning outcomes? Evidence from an actual learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10147-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model 3 contrast analysis stratified by knowledge type and number of correct questions. Contrasts for viewers versus identical non-viewers were "0.201 (.007)" for technical and "0.246 (.026)" for contextual knowledge.

> "Watching the multimedia presentation raised the probability of correctly answering these test questions by about 20 points (from about 65/100 to about 84/100)—a large effect."
