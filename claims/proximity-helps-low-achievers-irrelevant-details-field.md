## Evidence

### Britt Hadar 2023

Britt Hadar, Maayan Katzir, Sephi Pumpian, Tzur Karelitz and Nira Liberman. (2023). Psychological proximity improves reasoning in academic aptitude tests. npj Science of Learning 8:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00158-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Field experiment embedded in a consequential national AAT (N = 1744, two waves, 2018 and 2019), distance between participants, relevance within participants. The distance × AAT × relevance interaction emerged (p = 0.015) while distance × AAT alone was not significant.

### Britt Hadar 2023 (2)

Britt Hadar, Maayan Katzir, Sephi Pumpian, Tzur Karelitz and Nira Liberman. (2023). Psychological proximity improves reasoning in academic aptitude tests. npj Science of Learning 8:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00158-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up of the Study 3 three-way interaction: high achievers were unaffected by relevance, distance or their interaction (p = 0.503, 0.890, 0.880), whereas "the perfor-mance of low-achieving examinees was impaired by adding irrelevant information"; distance also interacted with relevance (p = 0.031), with accuracy lower on distal-irrelevant questions (p = 0.030).

> "the perfor-mance of low-achieving examinees was impaired by adding irrelevant information B = −0.146, SE = 0.050, z = −2.91, p = 0.003."

### Britt Hadar 2023 (3)

Britt Hadar, Maayan Katzir, Sephi Pumpian, Tzur Karelitz and Nira Liberman. (2023). Psychological proximity improves reasoning in academic aptitude tests. npj Science of Learning 8:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00158-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive means from the Study 3 field data for below-average-AAT examinees on irrelevant-added questions: "performance on proximal questions ( M = 0.52) was higher compared to distal questions ( M = 0.48) by 7%"; no effect size was printed.

> "For participants with below-average AAT score, performance on proximal questions ( M = 0.52) was higher compared to distal questions ( M = 0.48) by 7%, but only when the questions were made more dif ﬁcult by including irrelevant details."
