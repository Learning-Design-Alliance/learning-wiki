## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Electronics lab instructors' approaches to troubleshooting instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.113.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.113.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Self-reported interviews with 20 electronics lab instructors revealed a gap between valuing troubleshooting and directly assessing it: "only half of instructors said they directly assessed students' ability to troubleshoot."

> "while almost all instructors in our study said that troubleshooting is an important learning outcome for students in electronics lab courses, only half of instructors said they directly assessed students’ ability to troubleshoot"
