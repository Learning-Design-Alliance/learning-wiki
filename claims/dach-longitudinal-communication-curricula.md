`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey result from the 40 participating degree programmes. The article reports ten programmes with fully longitudinal communication curricula, 25 with partial or newly implemented ones, and five without, with over 80% of programmes emphasising instruction in the second and third years.

> "Ten of the 40 degree programmes indicated that all of their courses in communicative competencies were coordinated in accordance with a longitudinal curriculum."
