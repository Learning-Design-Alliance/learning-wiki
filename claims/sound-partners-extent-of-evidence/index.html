Sound Partners 2010¶

Sound Partners. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Sound Partners. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.wri-edu.org/partners/sound-partners.htm

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The WWC review of 18 studies found that "the seven studies included 442 students from kindergarten and first grade in urban schools in the Pacific Northwest and the Midwest," with four meeting standards and three meeting standards with reservations.

"Based on these seven studies, the WWC considers the extent of evidence for Sound Partners on beginning readers to be medium to large for alphabetics, fluency, and comprehension and small for general reading achievement."
