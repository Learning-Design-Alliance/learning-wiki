## Evidence

### DiPietro Joan 2019

DiPietro Joan, Kelemen Arpad, Liang Yulan, Sik-Lanyi Cecilia. (2019). Computer- and Robot-Assisted Therapies to Aid Social and Intellectual Functioning of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina55080440

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized trial (22 treatment, 21 waitlist control) of the Mind Reading software plus in vivo rehearsal at a US college campus. "ER in faces demonstrated significance at p < 0.001 in post-test and follow-up phases," with effect sizes described as large but no numeric value printed.

> "ER in faces demonstrated significance at p < 0.001 in post-test and follow-up phases, indicating improvement in the clinical group over the control group. Effect sizes were large."

### DiPietro Joan 2019 (2)

DiPietro Joan, Kelemen Arpad, Liang Yulan, Sik-Lanyi Cecilia. (2019). Computer- and Robot-Assisted Therapies to Aid Social and Intellectual Functioning of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina55080440

`q3 · i2` · `review · r2`

Same randomized trial's emotion encoding outcome. Post-test "d = 0.61" and follow-up "d = 0.85" favored the treatment group; emotion decoding achieved significance at follow-up only.

> "Differences between groups at post-test were t [40] = 2.33, p = 0.0125 (one-tail), d = 0.61 and at follow-up t [40] = 2.93, p = 0.003 (one-tail), d = 0.85, favoring the treatment group."
