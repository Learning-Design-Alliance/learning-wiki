## Evidence

### Shakir T 2026

Shakir T, Lingam G, Boal M, Francis N, Chand M. (2026). Technology-enhanced training in basic robotic surgical skills: a systematic review. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03633-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The review reports a randomised study of 60 robotic novices allocated to CESIR multimodal feedback (cameras plus force/pressure sensors enabling retrospective visualisation of hand-foot kinematics) or conventional feedback after two simulator tasks. The quoted score improvements are from that study.

> "Simulator scores showed a greater improvement in the CESIR group from 64 to 91, whereas the conventional group improved from 63 to 79 (p < 0.05)."
