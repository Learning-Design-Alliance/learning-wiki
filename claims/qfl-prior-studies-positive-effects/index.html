Evidence¶

Wiggins et al. 2017¶

Meg Wiggins, Mary Sawtell and Dr John Jerrim. (2017). Learner Response System: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581136

q2 · i2 · causal · r3

The report attributes this to a 2011 randomised evaluation by the Institute for Effective Education involving seven UK primary schools, reporting a printed effect size of "effect: +0.39, p > 0.001" on maths achievement. It also reports the 2012 grammar trial (42 schools) found effects of 0.16, enhanced to 0.26 for those using handsets at least three times a week. These are second-hand findings reported by this evaluation, not its own data.

"The intervention produced positive effects on maths achievement (effect: +0.39, p > 0.001)."
