`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey data across 1991, 1994, and 1997 (Figure 4) show rising test-preparation time; the authors add that direct observations suggest surveys "may not adequately capture its true growth."

> "In 1991, 44 percent of CPS elementary teachers indicated that they spent 12 or more hours of instructional time preparing students to take tests. In 1994, that figure rose to 47 percent, and in 1997 it rose again to 64 percent. In fact, by 1997 almost half of CPS elementary teachers reported spending more than 20 hours on this activity."
