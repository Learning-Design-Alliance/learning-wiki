## Evidence

### E. Tufino 2025

E. Tufino, P. Onorato and S. Oss. (2025). Exploring active learning in physics with ISLE-based modules in high school. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1088/1742-6596/2950/1/012021](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/2950/1/012021)

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Pre/post self-assessment study in two 12th-grade classrooms (N=44) after ~10-hour ISLE-iOLab modules. The article reports "significant improvements in students' self-assessment of all 8 scientific (sub-) abilities", with Cohen's d "ranged from 0.59 to 1.24"; the largest (1.24) was for describing observed phenomena.

> "The statistical analysis using the Wilcoxon test showed significant improvements in students' self-assessment of all 8 scientific (sub-) abilities assessed from before to after the teaching intervention."
