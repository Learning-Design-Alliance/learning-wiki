Counihan H, Harvey SA, Sekeseke-Chinyama M, Hamainza B, Banda R, Malambo T, Masaninga F, Bell D. (2012). Community Health Workers Use Malaria Rapid Diagnostic Tests (RDTs) Safely and Accurately: Results of a Longitudinal Study in Zambia. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observer checklist assessment of blood-safety steps at 3, 6, and 12 months. All CHWs wore gloves; isolated errors included two CHWs reusing a glove pair and one lancet disposed of with regular waste due to no sharps box.

> "The CHWs were instructed during training to report needle-stick injuries immediately to receive post-exposure prophylaxis for HIV, but no such injuries were observed or reported during the 12-month period."
