# VR-based training received significantly higher ratings for medical safety awareness and radiation protection than conventional training (r=0.856)

## Evidence

### Kakimoto Akihiro 2026

Development and Educational Evaluation of a Radiation Visualization System in a Virtual Reality Training Module for Radiopharmaceutical Administration: Mixed Methods Study of Students’ Learning Experiences. `q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Primary outcome domain D in the within-participants comparison of 20 students; Wilcoxon signed-rank test significant at the Bonferroni-adjusted threshold (α=.0071) with effect size r=0.856. Secondary item D3 (distance, shielding, time principles) rose from 2.95 to 4.55 (r=0.800).

> "The (D) medical safety awareness and radiation protection domain yielded mean scores of 3.07 (SD 0.57) for conventional training and 4.35 (SD 0.46) for VR-based training."
