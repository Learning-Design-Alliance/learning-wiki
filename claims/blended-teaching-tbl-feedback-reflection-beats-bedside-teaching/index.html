Evidence¶

Delavari S 2024¶

Delavari S, Barzkar F, Rikers MJP, Pourahmadi M, Soltani Arabshahi SK, Keshtkar A, Dargahi H, Yaghmaei M, Monajemi A. (2024). Teaching and learning clinical reasoning skill in undergraduate medical students: A scoping review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309606

q3 · i3 · review · r2

The review reports this from Gong et al.'s study (reference [70]): a blended intervention combining team-based learning, feedback, reflection and teacher summarizing outperformed traditional bedside teaching, with printed effect size SMD = 1.241 and wide confidence interval 0.136–2.346.

"Blended teaching (TBL + feedback + reflection + summarizing key points and commented on students' performance by teacher) compared with traditional bedside teaching had a statistically significant and very large effect on clinical reasoning performance (SMD, 1.241 [0.136–2.346]; P = 0.002)"
