### Nielsen Anne Møller 2012

Nielsen Anne Møller, Isbye Dan Lou, Lippert Freddy Knudsen, Rasmussen Lars Simon. (2012). Basic life support and automated external defibrillator skills among ambulance personnel: a manikin study performed in a rural low-volume ambulance setting. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-20-34

Manikin assessment with Laerdal PC SkillReporting System; one-sample t tests against pre-set criteria found "not significantly different from the minimum value of 40 mm (p = 0.26)" and likewise for the rate criterion. No effect sizes printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "The mean compression depth was 42 (±7) mm (Figure 2) and not significantly different from the minimum value of 40 mm (p = 0.26). Likewise, the mean compression rate of 123 (±15)/min (figure 2) was not significantly different from our defined maximum compression rate of 120 (p = 0.37), but 10 of 20 participants had rates higher than the maximally allowed."
