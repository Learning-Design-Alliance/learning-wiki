# Higher child-reported CU traits predict lower English, Math and Science grades in secondary students, controlling for externalizing problems and sociodemographics

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` CU traits were significantly related to lower grades in each of English, Math and Science after adjustment for covariates. [→ Bird E. 2019](#bird-e-2019)

## Evidence

### Bird E. 2019

Bird E., Chhoa C. Y., Midouhas E., Allen J. L. (2019). Callous-Unemotional Traits and Academic Performance in Secondary School Students: Examining the Moderating Effect of Gender. Journal of Abnormal Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-019-00545-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression (Model 1) on English grades from school records for 437 students nested in classes; the ICU total score was a significant predictor while externalizing problems was not. The article reports "The ICU total score but not externalizing problems was a significant predictor in Model 1."

> "The ICU total score but not externalizing problems was a significant predictor in Model 1. Older students and male (compared to female) students had lower English grades."

### Bird E. 2019 (2)

Bird E., Chhoa C. Y., Midouhas E., Allen J. L. (2019). Callous-Unemotional Traits and Academic Performance in Secondary School Students: Examining the Moderating Effect of Gender. Journal of Abnormal Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-019-00545-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression on Math grades from school records; both the ICU total score and externalizing problems significantly predicted lower Math grades, as the article states: "both externalizing problems and the ICU total score significantly predicted lower Math grades."

> "In Model 1, both externalizing problems and the ICU total score significantly predicted lower Math grades. Age and living in a single-parent family were also related to lower Math grades."

### Bird E. 2019 (3)

Bird E., Chhoa C. Y., Midouhas E., Allen J. L. (2019). Callous-Unemotional Traits and Academic Performance in Secondary School Students: Examining the Moderating Effect of Gender. Journal of Abnormal Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-019-00545-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression on Science grades from school records; the ICU total score and externalizing problems both significantly predicted lower Science grades, per "the ICU total score and externalizing problems significantly predicted lower Science grades."

> "In Model 1, the ICU total score and externalizing problems significantly predicted lower Science grades. Living in a single-parent family, free school meal eligibility and English as an additional language were related to lower Science grades."
