## Evidence

### Orsi Talles Dias 2020

Orsi Talles Dias, Ana Lucia Ribeiro Valadares, Paula Miranda Esteves Orsi, Isabella Miranda Esteves Orsi, Alexandre Sampaio Moura. (2020). Simulation-based Training for Pelvic and Breast Physical Examination: Effect on the Anxiety and Self-confidence of Medical Students. RBGO Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0040-1718433

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training assessment of the 80-student cohort using the Student Satisfaction and Self-Confidence in Learning Scale validated for Brazilian Portuguese. The study reports satisfaction was "very high (mean 6.85 ± 0.39)" and self-confidence "also high (5.97 ± 0.88)", both without significant gender differences.

> "Student satisfaction was very high (mean 6.85 ± 0.39) and there was no statistically significant difference between the female and male students. Self-confidence was also high (5.97 ± 0.88) following SBT, with no difference between genders."
