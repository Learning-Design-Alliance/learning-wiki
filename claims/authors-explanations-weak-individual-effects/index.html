Evidence¶

Pınar Bulut & Yaşar Barut 2016¶

Pınar Bulut & Yaşar Barut. (2016). The Whole is Greater than the Sum of the Parts: The Effects of an Antenatal Orientation Interviews Training for Prospective Parents Postnatal Depression Levels. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.23. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1112927

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, not a tested result: they contrast their informative orientation format with prior skills-based group counseling programs and note more than 80% of participants were college graduates, so the information provided may have been already known.

"Therefore, this research, carried out merely for in formation and orientation purposes, may be less eff icient than different approaches that include skills education."
