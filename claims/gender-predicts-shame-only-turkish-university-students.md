# Gender predicts shame, but not guilt or loneliness, among Turkish university students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Gender alone accounted for approximately 4% of the total variance in shame scores, but had no predictive power on guilt and was not a significant predictor of loneliness. [→ Akbağ 2010](#akbag-2010)

## Evidence

### Akbağ 2010

Akbağ, M. & Erden İmamoğlu, S. (2010). The Prediction of Gender and Attachment Styles on Shame, Guilt, and Loneliness. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 10(2), 669-682. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ889191

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression analysis of survey data from 360 Marmara University students (183 female, 177 male) found "gender alone accounted for approximately 4% of the total variance in shame scores (F=15.57, p<.001)". No effect size was printed.

> "Th e results showed that gender alone accounted for approximately 4% of the total variance in shame scores (F=15.57, p<.001)."

### Akbağ 2010 (2)

Akbağ, M. & Erden İmamoğlu, S. (2010). The Prediction of Gender and Attachment Styles on Shame, Guilt, and Loneliness. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 10(2), 669-682. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ889191

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same hierarchical regression analysis, the guilt model showed "gender did not have any predictive power on guilt (F=.47, p>.05)"; no effect size was printed for this null finding.

> "It was seen that gender did not have any predictive power on guilt (F=.47, p>.05)."

### Akbağ 2010 (3)

Akbağ, M. & Erden İmamoğlu, S. (2010). The Prediction of Gender and Attachment Styles on Shame, Guilt, and Loneliness. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 10(2), 669-682. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ889191

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the loneliness regression, "gender was not a signifi cant predictive factor for loneliness (F=2.02, p<.05)" while all attachment styles were significant; no effect size was printed for the gender term.

> "Finally, gender was not a signifi cant predictive factor for loneliness (F=2.02, p<.05), whereas all attachment styles were signifi cant."
