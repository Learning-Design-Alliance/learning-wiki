## Evidence

### Gogate Parikshit 2019

Gogate Parikshit, Biswas Partha, Das Taraprasad, Nirmalan Praveen, Natarajan Sundaram. (2019). Ophthalmology residency training in India: Comparing feedback about how the training equips ophthalmologists to combat retinal diseases. READS report #6. Indian Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4103/ijo.IJO_1960_18

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey results on surgeries performed during residency: post-2002 trainees reported more vitrectomies (mean 5.7, SD 20 vs 3.1, SD 17) and pre-2002 trainees more retinal detachment surgeries (5.9 vs 1.5), yet "the median value was 0" for both groups and both surgery types.

> "For both groups and both types of surgeries, however, the median value was 0, and the range was 0 to > 100 surgeries. The wide SDs suggested extreme variability in various residency training programs. Most programs did not offer vitreous or retina surgical training to their residents."
