CORDTRA diagram analysis of recorded workshop sessions (six of eight sessions coded) traced the teachers' design process minute by minute; the authors report that in the later sessions "teachers tested their prototypes and received mentor feedback" and revisited earlier phases iteratively.

"In Figure 3-(d), teachers tested their prototypes and received mentor feedback. They briefly revisited Define Educational Problem and Empathize & Analyze to refine their ideas, then continued with Interactive Design, incorporating Behavioral Activities (BA), Learner Differentiation (LD), and Thinking Process (TP)."
