`q3 i?` Subjective stress is higher for sonography tutors than AaLplus tutors and higher for novice than senior tutors, with significant interactions showing steeper declines for less experienced tutors. [→ Hundertmark Jan 2019](#hundertmark-jan-2019)

## Evidence

### Hundertmark Jan 2019

Hundertmark Jan, Alvarez Simone, Loukanova Svetla, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik. (2019). Stress and stressors of medical student near-peer tutors during courses: a psychophysiological mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education 19:95. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1521-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RM-ANOVA comparison of VAS-Stress across tutor type (AaLplus, n = 36 vs. sonography, n = 24) and experience (senior, n = 32 vs. novice, n = 28). The article reports significant interaction effects: "On earlier days, with low experience, and in sonography tutors, stress values start higher but decline more steeply".

> "They are higher for sonography tutors than for AaLplus tutors, overall M = 3.23 (SD = 2.33) vs. overall M = 2.21 (SD = 2.19), and higher for novice than for senior tutors, MΔ = 0.70. The general stress pattern is similar but not equal over conditions, as indicated by significant interaction effects"
