## Evidence

### Ning 2025

Ning, Zhuang, Wen, Xu, Wang, Peng, Huang and Tian. (2025). Evaluating ECAMM-based training efficacy for malaria microscopists in Hunan Province, China. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1598917

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Error-pattern analysis across the four training rounds in which 24 trainees evaluated 18 non-malarial-parasite smears, generating 432 diagnostic entries, of which "348 (80.6%) entries were correctly identified as nmps". Percentages of each misidentification type are printed.

> "Among these, 348 (80.6%) entries were correctly identified as nmps. Misidentifications included 26 (6.0%) entries as P. falciparum (pf), 29 (6.7%) as P. vivax (pv), 18 (4.2%) as P. ovale (po), and 11 (2.5%) as Plasmodium malariae (pm)."

### Ning 2025 (2)

Ning, Zhuang, Wen, Xu, Wang, Peng, Huang and Tian. (2025). Evaluating ECAMM-based training efficacy for malaria microscopists in Hunan Province, China. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1598917

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Error analysis of the po-positive slides in the same four-round assessment; accuracy on po-positive smears (43.8%) was the lowest of the species, with the largest misclassification share (20.8%) as pm. Descriptive proportions as printed.

> "Evaluation of 6 po-positive blood smears generated 144 responses, with 63 (43.8%) accurate diagnoses. Misclassifications were distributed as 28 (19.4%) as pv, 30 (20.8%) as pm, 9 (6.3%) as pf, and 14 (9.7%) as nmps."
