### Song Hongfeng 2026 (2)

Song Hongfeng, Hu Anqi, Li Xueyan. (2026). Beyond Positive or Negative: Latent Profiles of University Students’ AI Attitude and Their Career-Development Correlates. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091460

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

BCH comparisons across profiles show that the low-threat and high-threat engaged profiles were statistically indistinguishable on both career outcomes, so threat level alone did not predict career preparation.

> "Notably, Positive Empowerment and High-Perception High-Vigilance did not differ significantly on either outcome, despite their contrasting threat levels."
