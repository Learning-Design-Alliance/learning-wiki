Evidence¶

Ulrike Padó 2024¶

Ulrike Padó, Yunus Eryilmaz, Larissa Kirschner. (2024). Short-Answer Grading for German: Addressing the Challenges. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00383-w

q2 · i? · design · r2

Small usability study with six teachers experienced in short-answer grading (four German-as-second-language, two computer science) using ASYST on sample student answers; "100% effectiveness of the tool", median completion time 2:15 min, and an average SUS score of 88.8 (minimum 67.5, maximum 100), above the GUI mean of 76 reported via Bangor et al.

"All testers were able to complete the task successfully, leading to 100% effectiveness of the tool. The median time taken on the task (from setting the input language to completed saving of the results, and counting the run time of the tool) was 2:15 min."
