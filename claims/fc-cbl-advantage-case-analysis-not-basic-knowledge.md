## Evidence

### Cai L 2022

Cai L, Li Yl, Hu Xy, Li R, Khoshnood A. (2022). Implementation of flipped classroom combined with case-based learning: a promising and effective teaching modality in undergraduate pathology education. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000028782

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-class quiz analysis in the same quasi-experiment; quiz items were classified by Bloom's taxonomy, with case-analysis items covering higher cognitive categories. The FC group scored higher on "questions of clinical case analysis" (P < .001). No effect size printed.

> "higher scores regarding the questions of clinical case analysis were observed in the FC group than in the LBC group (36.27 ± 1.22 vs 25.01 ± 1.26, P < .001)"

### Cai L 2022 (2)

Cai L, Li Yl, Hu Xy, Li R, Khoshnood A. (2022). Implementation of flipped classroom combined with case-based learning: a promising and effective teaching modality in undergraduate pathology education. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000028782

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same quiz analysis: basic theoretical knowledge (remember/understand items) showed "no statistically significant difference" between groups (P = .089); equivalence was not tested. No effect size printed.

> "the scores related to basic theoretical knowledge in the FC and LBC groups were 42.46 ± 0.66 and 40.52 ± 0.88, respectively, with no statistically significant difference (P = .089)"
