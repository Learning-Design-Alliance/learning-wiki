Gao Xiaoxing, Huang Xiaoming, Hu Rongrong, Zhang Li, Liu Huiting, Zhang Bingqing, Wei Chong, Qiu Wei, Zhang Mengyu, Sun Xuefeng. (2026). Evaluating Large Language Model–Based Automated Scoring in a Voice-Based Virtual Standardized Patient Platform for Medical Students: Cross-Sectional Agreement Study. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subscale analyses in the same 92-student cross-sectional VSP study repeated the primary mixed-effects model for each 50-point domain. The article reports "stronger alignment for information gathering than for communication," with communication showing "greater rater dependence and lower reproducibility."

> "For information gathering, β=0.46 (P<.001); ρ=0.49; ICC=0.54 (95% CI 0.38‐0.67); VPC=0.23. For communication, β=0.27 (P<.001); ρ=0.28; ICC=0.29 (95% CI 0.08‐0.47); VPC=0.52, indicating greater rater dependence and lower reproducibility."
