## Evidence

### Arthur 1998

Arthur, W., Jr., Bennett, W., Jr., Stanush, P. L., & McNelly, T. L. (1998). Factors That Influence Skill Decay and Retention: A Quantitative Review and Analysis. Human Performance, 11(1), 57-101. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327043hup1101_3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis of degree of overlearning (DOV, per Driskell et al.'s operationalization), restricted to data points reporting overlearning information because 83% (N = 148) had no overlearning manipulation or information.

> "The results presented in Table 4 indicate that there was a fairly limited range in the degree of overlearning used in the studies in the data set. Consistent with this, the differences in the amount of skill decay as a function of degree of overlearning were also limited."
