## Evidence

### Worth 2015

Worth, J., Sizmur, J., Ager, R. & Styles, B. (2015). Improving Numeracy and Literacy. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN20621819

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Pre-specified secondary sub-group analysis within the cluster-randomised trial. The report states the interventions did not significantly impact FSM pupils and that "the number of pupils eligible for FSM in each group was small"; no effect size is printed for this sub-group analysis.

> "The interventions did not have a significant impact on the sub-group of FSM pupils. The number of pupils eligible for FSM in each group was small, which limited the precision of this analysis."

### Worth 2015 (2)

Worth, J., Sizmur, J., Ager, R. & Styles, B. (2015). Improving Numeracy and Literacy. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN20621819

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Interaction analysis of group allocation and FSM status in the same trial, illustrated in Figure 2 from multilevel model predictions. FSM pupils achieved a higher than expected score in the literacy group versus control, while non-FSM pupils achieved a lower than expected score; no effect size is printed.

> "The interaction between the literacy group and FSM status was positive and significant, which shows that while the literacy intervention showed a negative but insignificant effect for all pupils, the literacy intervention had a differential impact on FSM pupils."
