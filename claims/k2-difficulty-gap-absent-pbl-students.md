## Evidence

### Zahid Muhammad A. 2016

Zahid Muhammad A., Varghese Ramani, Mohammed Ahmed M., Ayed Adel K. (2016). Comparison of the problem based learning-driven with the traditional didactic-lecture-based curricula. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5749.80f5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test item analysis comparing measured item difficulty of K1 versus K2 MCQs between the old (220 items) and new (225 items) curriculum examinations, using Student's t-test for independent samples. No effect size is printed.

> "The post-test item analysis showed that the old curriculum students found K2-questions to be significantly (p = 0.001) more difficult than K1-questions while no such difference was found by the new curriculum students (Table 3)."

### Zahid Muhammad A. 2016 (2)

Zahid Muhammad A., Varghese Ramani, Mohammed Ahmed M., Ayed Adel K. (2016). Comparison of the problem based learning-driven with the traditional didactic-lecture-based curricula. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5749.80f5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Reported proportions of students answering K2-type questions correctly in the two curricula, with a printed p = 0.036 for the K2 contrast; K1 performance was similar (68% vs 67%). No effect size is reported.

> "56% of the old and 64% of the new curricula students answered the K2 type questions correctly (p = 0.036) while 68% of the old and 67% of the new curricula students answered the KI type questions correctly."
