## Evidence

### Pavlidis Nicholas 2021

Pavlidis Nicholas, Peccatori Fedro A., Aapro Matti, Eniu Alex, Rassy Elie, Cavalli Franco, Rolfo Christian, Costa Alberto. (2021). Eight-Year Experience of the Certificate of Competence and Advanced Studies Program Organized by the European School of Oncology. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-021-02105-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of exam records from three breast cancer cohorts (CCB1–CCB3) drawn from ESO archives. Scoring categories were Very Good, Good, and Satisfactory in 41%, 39%, and 12% respectively, and the article reports that "7% of participants failed the exam." Two participants initially failed or were disqualified but passed on retake.

> "The mean percentage pass of the five-module tests was 79.3% (range 79–80%), and that of the certification exam was 83% (range 75–87%)"
