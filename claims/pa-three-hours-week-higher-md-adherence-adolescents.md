## Evidence

### Chacón-Cuberos 2018

Chacón-Cuberos, R.; Zurita-Ortega, F.; Martínez-Martínez, A.; Olmedo-Moreno, E.M.; Castro-Sánchez, M. (2018). Adherence to the Mediterranean Diet Is Related to Healthy Habits, Learning Processes, and Academic Achievement in Adolescents: A Cross-Sectional Study. Nutrients 10(11):1566. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu10111566

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional t-test comparison (Table 2) among 1059 Spanish adolescents contrasting those active more than three hours weekly with less active peers on the KIDMED global score; "the overall score for adherence to a MD was higher" in the active group (9.34 ± 1.90 vs. 8.67 ± 2.04, p < 0.001). No standardised effect size is printed.

> "Finally, the overall score for adherence to a MD was higher for adolescents who were physically active (9.34 ± 1.90 vs. 8.67 ± 2.04)."
