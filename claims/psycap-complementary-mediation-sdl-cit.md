## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The indirect effect of SDL on CIT via PsyCap is significant (β = 0.329, p < 0.001), and because both direct and indirect effects are positive, the mediation is complementary (partial). [→ Su Wenhao 2025](#su-wenhao-2025)

## Evidence

### Su Wenhao 2025

Su Wenhao, Fadzil Hidayah Mohd, Rauf Rose Amnah Abd. (2025). Bridging self-directed learning and competency in interdisciplinary teaching among STEM lecturers: How psychological capital connects. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0319305

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (5,000 resamples) in PLS-SEM on the 408-lecturer sample. The article reports "the indirect effect of SDL on CIT via PsyCap is 0.329 (P < 0.001)" alongside the significant direct effect, classifying the mediation as complementary.

> "the indirect effect of SDL on CIT via PsyCap is 0.329 (P < 0.001), reaching a significant level, which indicates the presence of a mediation effect. Additionally, the direct effect of SDL on CIT is 0.210 (P < 0.001), also significant, suggesting that PsyCap serves as a partial mediator between SDL and CIT."
