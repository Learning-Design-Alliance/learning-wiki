# Peer-student ultrasound tutors in three training groups had higher practical scanning-skill scores after training than before, in pre-post measurements without a comparison group

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` OSCE scanning scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in all three training groups, in pre-post measurements without a comparison group (relative gains 155.0%, 112.0%, 123.5%). [→ Celebi Nora 2019](#celebi-nora-2019)
`q1 i?` The one-week course-only group's scanning-skill scores were higher at post-test than at pre-test, in a pre-post measurement without a comparison group. [→ Celebi Nora 2019 (2)](#celebi-nora-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Celebi Nora 2019

Celebi Nora, Griewatz Jan, Malek Nisar Peter, Hoffmann Tatjana, Walter Carina, Muller Reinhold, Riessen Reimer, Pauluschke-Fröhlich Jan, Debove Ines, Zipfel Stephan, Fröhlich Eckhart. (2019). Outcomes of three different ways to train medical students as ultrasound tutors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1556-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post OSCE (depict and label 15 anatomical structures in maximum three images within eight minutes) in the same 44-tutor quasi-randomized study; Table 2 prints relative OSCE gains of 155.0% (I), 112.0% (C) and 123.5% (CI), "all p < 0.001, paired t-tests".

> "All teaching groups improved substantially, significantly, and comparably in both theoretical knowledge and practical skills (all p < 0.001, paired t-tests)."

### Celebi Nora 2019 (2)

Celebi Nora, Griewatz Jan, Malek Nisar Peter, Hoffmann Tatjana, Walter Carina, Muller Reinhold, Riessen Reimer, Pauluschke-Fröhlich Jan, Debove Ines, Zipfel Stephan, Fröhlich Eckhart. (2019). Outcomes of three different ways to train medical students as ultrasound tutors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1556-4

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of their own pre-post results: the one-week course-only group's scanning improvement was "comparable to the I- and CI-group" despite the latter having several weeks of practice.

> "However, most notably, the improvement in scanning skill in the C-group who was trained for one week only was comparable to the I- and CI-group who had several weeks to practice the scanning."
