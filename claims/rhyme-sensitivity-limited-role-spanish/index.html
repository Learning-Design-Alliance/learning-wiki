Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Technical Report 2026¶

Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Technical Report. (2026). NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/spanish-map-reading-fluency-technical-report/

q1 · i? · design · r2

The report states these abilities "tend to show reduced differentiation among readers by grade 1 and beyond (Carrillo, 1994)" and that in bilingual populations rhyme performance may reflect cross-linguistic influence from English. Syllable and phoneme awareness are described as more central and durable drivers of decoding development in Spanish.

"However, in Spanish, rhyme sensitivity is not a sole predictor of later reading success and does not reliably distinguish between stronger and weaker readers beyond the earliest stages of development."
