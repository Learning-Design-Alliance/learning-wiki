Cohort comparison within the user study across two deployment modalities. "The remote cohort achieved an overall meancompetencescore of 3.36 (SD= 0.64, MD= 3.5), closely mirroring the in-person mean score of 3.42 (SD= 0.61, MD= 3.5)"; median usability was 4.4 (remote) vs 4.0 (in-person).
