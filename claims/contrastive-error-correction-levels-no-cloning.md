## Evidence

### Glen Evenbly 2024

Glen Evenbly. (2024). Exploring Gamification in Quantum Computing: The Qubit Factory. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.11995](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.11995)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Design rationale from the article's level-design section (Sec. VII), describing levels CII.E and H.C which showcase classical and quantum error correction. The authors argue the contrast makes users confront that "qubits cannot cloned" and adopt entanglement-with-ancillas strategies; no user data are reported.

> "However, if they attempt as such they will be hit with an immediate roadblock: that, unlike classical bits, qubits cannot cloned48 so using a repetition strategy is not feasible. Thus users are confronted with a key difference between regular bits and qubits, and must rethink their error correction strategy accordingly."
