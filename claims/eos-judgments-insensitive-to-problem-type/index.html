Evidence¶

Marie Geurten & Patrick Lemaire 2017¶

Marie Geurten & Patrick Lemaire. (2017). Metacognition for Strategy Selection during Arithmetic Problem Solving in Young and Older Adults. https://www.google.com/search?q=Metacognition+for+Strategy+Selection+during+Arithmetic+Problem+Solving+in+Young+and+Older+Adults

q2 · i0 · causal · r2

Additional analyses of absolute EoS judgments across age groups and tasks found "did not give higher EoS scores on homogenous (M = 2.72) than on heterogeneous (M = 2.64) problems, F<1". The authors take this as suggesting participants did not use unit-digit proximity to 0 as a prospective cue.

"Results indicated that participants – whatever their age or the task – did not give higher EoS scores on homogenous (M = 2.72) than on heterogeneous (M = 2.64) problems, F<1"
