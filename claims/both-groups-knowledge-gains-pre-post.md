## Evidence

### Karimian Z 2025

Karimian Z, Momeni M, Zarifsanaiey N. (2025). Enhancing radiographic interpretation: effects of gamification on medical students' knowledge, skills, and satisfaction — a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07523-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests within each of the two groups of 41 students showed significant pre-to-post gains; routine group rose from 12.56 ± 1.51 to 16.67 ± 1.45 and gamification group from 13.08 ± 0.94 to 18.52 ± 1.14 on the 0–20 MCQ test.

> "The comparison of post-test and pre-test scores in the routine training group (P < 0.001) and in the gamification group (P < 0.001) showed a significant difference in post-test scores compared to pre-test scores."
