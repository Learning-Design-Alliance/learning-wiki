## Evidence

### Kesan 2013

Kesan, C. & Çalıskan, S. (2013). The effect of learning geometry topics of 7th grade in primary education with dynamic Geometer's Sketchpad geometry software to success and retention. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008875

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks test comparing the control group's (n=21) post-test and retention test scores after traditional geometry teaching; the article reports no significant change within one month.

> "there was not a significant difference between the groups' scores of post-test and retention test of the students in control group (z=1,402; p>0,05). This shows that the control group's academic achievement gained at the end of traditional geometry teaching has not changed within a month after the training."
