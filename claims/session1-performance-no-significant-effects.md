## Evidence

### Wang 2025

Wang, H., Thompson‐Lee, S., & Klassen, R. M. (2025). Performance on classroom simulations enhances preservice teachers' motivation in teaching: A latent change perspective. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S239–S255. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12761

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent change SEM of the 1411‐participant sample found the Session 1 performance path non‐significant for all three outcomes. Zero‐order correlations also showed participants performing better in Session 1 "tended to have lower levels of self‐efficacy in the pre‐test but higher levels of self‐efficacy after Session 1".

> "the results showed that participants' simulation performance in Session 1 did not significantly influence any changes in the outcome variables."
