### Jiang Juming 2026

Jiang Juming, Ye Megan Zichen, Kwok Tyrone Tai-On, Wong Janet Yuen Ha. (2026). GenAI-Supported Virtual Patients in Health Care Education: Systematic Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/82756

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

RCT reported in the review's Table 3 (Luo et al, n=13 per group, 1-hour session): the LLM-based digital patient group outperformed controls on history-taking, with the review noting "The LLMDP group showed significantly higher MHTAn scores". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The LLMDP group showed significantly higher MHTAn scores (mean 64.62, SD 9.52) vs control (54.12, SD 8.80), mean difference 10.50 points (95% CI 4.66‐16.33, P<.001)."
