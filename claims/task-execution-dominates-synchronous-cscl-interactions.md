Duong Ngo, Andy Nguyen, Belle Dang, Ha Ngo. (2024). Facial Expression Recognition for Examining Emotional Regulation in Synchronous Online Collaborative Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 34:650–669. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00378-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video qualitative analysis of 14 group recordings from 36 students produced 379 coded 30-second segments; task execution was the modal category at f = 62.3%, "most social interactions in collaborative learning are related to task execution (f = 62.3%)."

> "In line with previous studies (e.g., Järvelä et al., 2016; Malmberg et al., 2017), most social interactions in collaborative learning are related to task execution (f = 62.3%)."
