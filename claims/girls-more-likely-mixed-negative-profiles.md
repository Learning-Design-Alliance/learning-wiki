## Evidence

### Symes 2025

Symes, W., Lichtenfeld, S., Wood, P., & Putwain, D. W. (2025). Profiles of control, value and achievement emotions in primary school mathematics lessons. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 888–902. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12768

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

R3STEP multinomial logistic analysis with the Positive profile as reference in the 883-student sample (443 girls, 440 boys). The quote reports the printed β values, odds ratios and confidence intervals for girls versus boys.

> "Gender was significantly related to the likelihood of belonging to the Negative profile (β = .50, p < .05) and the Mixed profile (β = .51, p < .01), relative to the Positive profile. More specifically, girls were more likely than boys to belong to the Negative profile (OR = 1.65, 95% CI [1.05 2.58]) or the Mixed profile (OR = 1.66, 95% CI [1.24 2.23]), relative to the Positive profile."
