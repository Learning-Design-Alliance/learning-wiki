### Kaur 2024

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire analysis of 22 one-day workshop participants using 12 Likert-scale items across three dimensions. The authors report "a very high positive reaction, high levels of self-assessed learning and confidence"; item 10 showed the support-request process was unclear. No effect sizes printed.

> "Overall, the workshop questionnaire  results suggest a very high positive reaction, high levels of self-assessed learning and confidence in teaching modern physics concepts."
