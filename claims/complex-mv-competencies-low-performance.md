Rocha de Macedo Bruno, Lima Carolina Saldanha, Haydar Ahmed, Holanda Marcelo Alcantara, Hayashi Fatima Kiyoko, Ferreira Juliana Carvalho. (2025). Impact of a Competency-based Mechanical Ventilation Course Using Virtual Simulation. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.34197/ats-scholar.2024-0083OC

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Competency-level analysis of virtual SBA item performance (Table 3) across five scenarios for the 81 completing residents. Percentages correct are printed per item, ranging from 92.6% for factual recognition tasks down to 25.9% and 27.2% for complex ventilator-adjustment tasks; these are descriptive proportions with no effect size.

> "The rate of correct answers varied widely. Recognition of pressure-controlled mode by observing ventilator waveforms and diagnosing ARDS had 92.6% correct answers, whereas setting initial parameters in obstructive lung disease had 25.9% correct answers and adjusting parameters to correct patient–ventilator asynchrony had 27.2% correct answers."
