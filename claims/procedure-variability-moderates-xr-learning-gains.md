# UP improvement over repeated XR sessions is greater and faster for ventriculostomy than for tumor resection

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A single XR training session significantly improved UP for ventriculostomy immediately and at a 47-day average follow-up, while tumor resection improvements were non-significant or subtle and required four to five sessions to detect. [→ Iop A. 2022](#iop-a-2022)

## Evidence

### Iop A. 2022

Iop A., El-Hajj V.G., Gharios M., de Giorgio A., Monetti F.M., Edström E., Elmi-Terander A., Romero M. (2022). Extended Reality in Neurosurgical Education: A Systematic Review. Sensors. https://doi.org/10.3390/s22166067

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis comparing longitudinal UP gains across procedure types: ventriculostomy, a low-variability procedure relying on anatomical knowledge, showed significant improvement after one session; tumor resection gains were subtle and needed four or five sessions.

> "in both tumor resection studies [58,59], more training sessions (four and five, respectively) were required to detect even the small improvements in UP, as compared to studies reporting on ventriculostomy procedures, where a single training session was sufficient to significantly improve UP both immediately [19,65] and at a 47-day average follow-up [19]."
