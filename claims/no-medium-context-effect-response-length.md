# No significant medium, context, or medium-by-context effects on written response length as a measure of behavioral engagement

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The ANCOVA on response length showed no statistically significant main effect of medium or context and no significant interaction; equivalence was not tested. [→ Lucia Mason 2022](#lucia-mason-2022)

## Evidence

### Lucia Mason 2022

Lucia Mason, Christian Tarchi, Angelica Ronconi, Lucia Manzione, Natalia Latini, Ivar Bråten. (2022). Do medium and Context Matter when learning from multiple complementary Digital texts and videos? Instructional Science (2022) 50:653–679. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09591-8

`q3 · i?`

Null result from the same 3×2 ANCOVA design, using total word count of written responses to four essay questions as an index of effort. All effects were negligible and non-significant: "no statistically significant main effect of medium" or context, and no interaction.

> "This analysis showed no statistically significant main effect of medium, F(2, 245) = 0.01, p = .988, η2 p = 0.001, or context, F(1, 245) = 0.05, p = .821, η2 p = 0.001; nor was the effect of their interaction statistically significant, F(2, 245) = 0.43, p = .650, η2 p = 0.004."
