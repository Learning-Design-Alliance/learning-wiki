### Cukurova 2017

Cukurova, M., Luckin, R., Millán, E., & Mavrikis, M. (2017). The NISPI framework: Analysing collaborative problem-solving from students' physical interactions. Preprint submitted to Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.08.007

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Answer to the study's second research question, based on comparing NISPI index values across groups ranked high, medium and low by trained human observers using the OECD-based CPS analysis framework. No effect size or statistical test is printed.

> "The results show that the concepts of synchrony, equality and intra-individual variability are good predictors of CPS competence of students. However, individual accountability, when measured with the coding scheme on its own in practice-based learning environments, appears to be less valuable to predict the quality of CPS."
