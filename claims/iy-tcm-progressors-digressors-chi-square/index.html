Herman, K. C., & Reinke, W. M. (2017). Improving Teacher Perceptions of Parent Involvement Patterns: Findings From a Group Randomized Trial. School Psychology Quarterly, 32(1), 89-104. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/spq0000169

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Transition analysis comparing intervention and control conditions in the group randomized trial. The article also reports "only 2% of control parents in the Low/Low class at baseline transitioned to the most adaptive classes at follow-up, compared to 25% for the intervention group" and 80% of High/High transitions came from intervention.

"Only 7% of parents for control teachers in the lower two adaptive classes at baseline transitioned to a more adaptive class at follow-up, compared to 39% of parents for intervention teachers who transitioned to a more adaptive class."
