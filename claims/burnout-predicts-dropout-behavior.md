`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Narrative synthesis of three longitudinal studies measuring actual dropout behavior (two via self-report, one via enrollment status the next academic year). The review reports that "high levels of cynicism and inadequacy predicted higher dropout rates"; no pooled effect size was computed for behavior.

> "In these studies, high levels of cynicism and inadequacy predicted higher dropout rates as did profiles reflecting high levels of overall (or total) burnout."
