## Evidence

### Savelsbergh 2016

Savelsbergh, E. R., Prins, G. T., Rietbergen, C., Fechner, S., Vaessen, B. E., Draijer, J. M., & Bakker, A. (2016). Effects of innovative science and mathematics teaching on student attitudes and achievement: A meta-analytic study. Educational Research Review, 19, 158-172. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2016.07.003

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-regression of overall attitude effects (Akçay et al. excluded) found a significant negative coefficient for grade level (B = -0.10, SE = 0.04, p = 0.01). The authors state "the effectiveness of interventions decreases for older students."

> "The negative effect of grade level is signi ﬁcant however, indicating that the effectiveness of interventions decreases for older students."
