# TAIA students perceived more teacher support for hypothesis generation and argumentation and enjoyed inquiry more; perceived experiment-related support did not differ

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` EG students reported significantly higher perceived hypothesis-related support, argumentation-related support, and inquiry enjoyment than CG students. [→ Fan Chen 2026](#fan-chen-2026)
`q3 i?` No significant group difference was found for perceived experiment-related support. [→ Fan Chen 2026 (2)](#fan-chen-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Fan Chen 2026

Fan Chen, Gaowei Chen. (2026). How a technology-augmented inquiry approach affects teacher practice and student learning in disadvantaged science classrooms. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09788-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-intervention Mann-Whitney U tests on a single-item-per-construct survey (pilot-tested with 20 rural eighth-grade students; see Table 2 means, e.g. argumentation support 3.15 vs 1.83). Only test statistics are printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "EG students reported significantly higher perceived usefulness of teacher support during hypothesis generation [ Z = −4.42, p < 0.001] and scientific argumentation [Z = −5.66, p < 0.001], as well as greater enjoyment of inquiry activities [ Z = −3.56, p < 0.001]."

### Fan Chen 2026 (2)

Fan Chen, Gaowei Chen. (2026). How a technology-augmented inquiry approach affects teacher practice and student learning in disadvantaged science classrooms. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09788-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of experiment-related support perceptions found no significant difference (p = 0.17); equivalence was not tested. The authors attribute this to both groups using identical physical equipment during experimentation.

> "However, no significant difference was found in perceived experiment- related support [ Z = −1.37, p = 0.17]."
