Assessment of 62 real patient DPRs (N = 30 before, N = 32 after) against a 10-point compliance checklist, double-checked by two independent reviewers. Key components other than findings critical to patient management also improved (all p < 0.05); dentition rose from 53.3% to 100%.

> "Overall compliance with DPR reporting in clinical practice increased from 63.3% before to 81.3% after the module (p = 0.03) (Table 3)."
