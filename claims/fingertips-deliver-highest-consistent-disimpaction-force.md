## Evidence

### Parris Dawn 2025

Parris Dawn, Xue Yang, Navaseelan Thurga, Salvadores Fernandez Carmen, Javidan Maryam, Gupta Rohit, Salaris Nikolaos, Chen Jeremy, Tiwari Manish K., Siassakos Dimitrios. (2025). Characterising Forces Applied During the Simulated Management of Impacted Fetal Head: Pre‐Clinical Methods Study. BJOG: An International Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.70107

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feasibility study with 13 obstetricians of varying seniority performing simulated disimpaction wearing a sensorised glove with 12 sensors; force traces and boxplots (Figures 4–5) show fingertip sensors "recorded some of the highest maximum force values" with low variability. Median maximum forces included 21.70 N at sensor 5 and 17.56 N at sensor 11 on the dominant hand.

> "Sensors 3, 5, 9 and 11 (located on the fingertips—Figure 3) recorded some of the highest maximum force values, suggesting that fingertips play a central role in applying strong, localised force during fetal disimpaction."
