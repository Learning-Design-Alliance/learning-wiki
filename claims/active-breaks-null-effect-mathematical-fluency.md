## Evidence

### Melguizo-Ibáñez 2026

Melguizo-Ibáñez, E., Zurita-Ortega, F., Tadeu, P., Ubago-Jiménez, J. L., & Alonso Vargas, J. M. (2026). Classroom Active Breaks as a Physical-Educational Intervention for Executive Functions and Mathematical Outcomes: a Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10146-3

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 6 mathematical-fluency studies (n = 1369): pooled g = 0.03 under RVE, p = 0.87; the article states "The convergence of null findings indicates that no reliable effect was detected."

> "For mathematical fluency both the averaged (g = 0.02, 95% CI [− 0.36, 0.42], Z = 0.13, p = 0.89) and RVE models results (g = 0.03, 95% CI [− 0.38, 0.39], t(5) = 0.16, p = 0.87) yielded trivial and non-significant effects."
