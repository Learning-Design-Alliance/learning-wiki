# Full word-pair cues can promote rather than disrupt reactivation when delivered with sufficient spacing and during stable NREM sleep

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Contrary to prior reports that full cue-feedback pairs can block reactivation, this study found enhanced neural responses to the second word, particularly for difficult items. [→ Shin 2025](#shin-2025)

## Evidence

### Shin 2025

Shin, G.-H., Kweon, Y.-S., Oh, S., & Lee, S.-W. (2025). Personalized targeted memory reactivation enhances consolidation of challenging memories via slow wave and spindle dynamics. npj Science of Learning 10:47. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00340-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation from ERP and TFR analyses showing enhanced responses following the second word of each cue pair, suggesting the second word functioned as a reinstatement signal; each pair was delivered with approximately 5 s spacing during stable NREM sleep.

> "the effectiveness of full-pair cues may depend on temporal precision, memory strength, and sleep-stage alignment, and that under optimal con ditions, full cues can successfully promote memory consolidation."
