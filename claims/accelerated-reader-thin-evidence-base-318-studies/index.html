Accelerated Reader™ 2010¶

Accelerated Reader™. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Accelerated Reader™, Adolescent Literacy. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.renlearn.com/ar/

q3 · i? · review · r3

The report's literature search covering documents publicly available by July 2009. The WWC characterizes the extent of evidence as small for reading fluency and medium to large for comprehension; no qualifying studies examined alphabetics or general literacy achievement.

"A total of 318 studies reviewed by the WWC investigated the effects of Accelerated Reader ™ on adolescent learners. One study (Bullock, 2005) is a randomized controlled trial that meets WWC evidence standards."
