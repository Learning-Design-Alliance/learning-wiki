## Evidence

### Mileder Lukas P. 2024

Mileder Lukas P., Baik-Schneditz Nariae, Pansy Jasmin, Schwaberger Bernhard, Raith Wolfgang, Avian Alexander, Schmölzer Georg M., Wöckinger Peter, Pichler Gerhard, Urlesberger Berndt (2024). Impact of in situ simulation training on quality of postnatal stabilization and resuscitation—a before-and-after, non-controlled quality improvement study. European Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-024-05781-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post knowledge test with the European Resuscitation Council Newborn Life Support instrument, analysed with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test in 29 of 48 participants, showed a significant increase (15 versus 18 correct, p < 0.001); no effect size was printed.

> "Twenty-nine of the 48 training participants (60.4%) answered the standardized cognitive knowledge test before and after in situ SBT, showing a significant increase in correctly answered questions between the pre- and post-training periods (15 [13–15.5] versus 18 [17–19], p < 0.001)."
