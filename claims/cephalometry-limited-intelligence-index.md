### De Sanctis 1911

De Sanctis, S. (1911). Mental development and the measurement of the level of intelligence. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 2, 498-507. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0069835

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument reviewing the cephalometric tradition (with cited figures such as a minimum cephalic circumference of about 51 centimeters). The author concludes these considerations "have a very limited application in practice" and prefers direct psychological measurement.

> "If correlations actually exist, we do not know enough about them to determine their exact nature. On this account the direct method of measurement, or the psychological method, is preferable."
