`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup AUROC analysis of the MRMC reader study (Table 2). The results state AUROCs were lower "when interpreting mammograms of women with high breast density compared to those with non-dense breasts," and the with-AI increase (0.82 to 0.84, P=.13) was not significant.

> "The AUROCs were lower for all reader groups when interpreting mammograms of women with high breast density compared to those with non-dense breasts, at 0.82 (95% CI 0.81‐0.83) and 0.93 (95% CI 0.92‐0.93), respectively."
