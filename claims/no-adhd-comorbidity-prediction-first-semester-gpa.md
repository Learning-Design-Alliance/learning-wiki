## Evidence

### Mohamad Nawal 2025

Mohamad Nawal, Rousseau Kim-Louise, Dowlut Fatimah, Gering Milton, Thomas Kevin G. F. (2025). Symptoms of ADHD and Other Common Mental Disorders Influence Academic Success in South African Undergraduates. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547241310659

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression (Table 8) on the Time 1 sample predicted weighted first-semester GPA; the significant fit was driven by Grade 12 score and age controls, with BDI-II the only significant symptom predictor (p = .02). No effect size was printed for the symptom predictors.

> "Although the model fit was statistically significant (F[7, 498] = 28.82, p < .001, R2 = .29), this observation is accounted for almost entirely by the influence of two control variables (Grade 12 score, age); the other predictor variables (with the exception of BDI-II score, which was significant on its own) accounted for no significant portion of the variance in the outcome, all ps > .10."
