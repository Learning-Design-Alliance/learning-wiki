### Detlev Leutner · Johanna Biele 2025

Detlev Leutner · Johanna Biele. (2025). Without Integration, Everything Is Nothing: A Meta-Analysis of the Effectiveness of Instructional Support for Drawing-to-Learn. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10067-7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis of comprehension outcomes in the random-effects meta-analysis; Hypothesis 3 was rejected. Only the homogeneity test statistic is printed, no effect size for the moderator contrast.

> "the increase of germane cognitive load (support including/not including the increase of GCL as a potential impact on cognitive processing) turned out to be a non-significant moderator of the effect of drawing support on students' comprehension as well, Q(1) = 0.92, p = 0.339"
