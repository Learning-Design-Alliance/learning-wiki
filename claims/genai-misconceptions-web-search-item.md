## Evidence

### Markus H. Hefter 2026

Markus H. Hefter, Benjamin Paaßen, Kirsten Berthold. (2026). GenAI Knowledge, Epistemic Orientation, and Intellectual Values Predict Undergraduate Students’ Critical GenAI Use. AI Educ. 2026, 2, 27. https://doi.org/10.3390/aieduc2030027

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory item-level analysis of the 12-item objective knowledge test with certainty ratings in the same sample. The authors report that “97.0% of the participants believed that ‘AI chatbots perform web searches’” while only 9.0% had misconception scores below zero.

> "the correctness rates for our 12 items ranged from 3.0% (Item 4) to 91.0% (Item 2). In other words, 97.0% of the participants believed that “AI chatbots perform web searches.” (Item 4). However, when looking at the misconception score, which combines correctness and certainty ratings, only 9.0% of participants had a misconception score below zero."
