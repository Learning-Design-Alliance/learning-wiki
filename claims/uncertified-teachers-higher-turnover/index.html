Evidence¶

Tan 2026¶

Tan, T., Wei, W., Carver-Thomas, D., & García, E. (2026). Teacher turnover in the United States: Who moves, who leaves, and why. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/248.479

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of NTPS/TFS turnover by certification status. The report compares "teachers with temporary, waiver or emergency, or no certification (14.7% vs. 20.1%)" against fully certified teachers; these descriptive differences were not tested for significance.

"Smaller shares of teachers with regular or probationary teaching certificates moved schools or left teaching between 2020–21 and 2021–22 compared to teachers with temporary, waiver or emergency, or no certification (14.7% vs. 20.1%)."

Tan 2026 (2)¶

Tan, T., Wei, W., Carver-Thomas, D., & García, E. (2026). Teacher turnover in the United States: Who moves, who leaves, and why. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/248.479

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Descriptive analysis by certification and experience (Figure 5). The report states the 1–3 years turnover share was "about 40% higher among teachers not fully certified," with the executive summary printing 24% vs. 17%.

"The share of teachers with 1–3 years of experience who moved schools or left teaching was about 40% higher among teachers not fully certified than among fully certified teachers."
