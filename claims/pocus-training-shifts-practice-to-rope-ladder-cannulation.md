## Evidence

### Hill K 2023

Hill K, Jaensch A, Childs J, McDonald S. (2023). Evaluation of point of care ultrasound (POCUS) training on arteriovenous access assessment and cannula placement for haemodialysis. The Journal of Vascular Access. https://doi.org/10.1177/11297298231194100

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observed practice change reported in the discussion of the three-site implementation: trained nurses scanned prevalent patients and in all cases found new cannulation sites, which the authors describe as an important shift from the primary area cannulation technique and consistent with best-practice guidelines.

> "All nurses participating in the training used POCUS on prevalent patients in their unit and in all cases identified new areas for cannulation that had not previously been used creating a change to rope ladder technique."
