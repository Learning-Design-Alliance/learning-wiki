`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive sex comparison of average scattering and of proportions passing over 10 advance tests across the three scales and diagnostic classifications. The author offers this as slight confirmation of the view that the male sex is more variable, citing Yeung's finding that girls were only 0.63 as variable as boys.

> "Thus the girls consistently vary more in the 1908 scale, and the boys in the other two, which are usually recognized as being more accurate than the 1908."
