## Evidence

### Gundo Rodwell 2021

Gundo Rodwell, Gundo Beatrice, Chirwa Ellen, Dickinson Annette, Mearns Gael Janine. (2021). Effect of an educational programme on critical care nurses' competence at two tertiary hospitals in Malawi. Malawi Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.4314/mmj.v33i4.3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre- and post-test study of a three-day training delivered to 41 ICU/HDU nurses at two Malawian tertiary hospitals; competence measured by paper-based self-assessment. The article reports "M = 608.2, SD = 59.6" pre and "M = 684.7, SD = 29.7" post, with a mean increase of 76.9, 95% CI [59.3, 94.5].

> "The participants' scores on the Intensive and Critical Care Nursing Competence Scale before the training, M = 608.2, SD = 59.6 increased significantly after the training, M = 684.7, SD = 29.7, p <.0001 (two-tailed)."
