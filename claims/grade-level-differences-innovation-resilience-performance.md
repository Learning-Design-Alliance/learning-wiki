## Evidence

### Qi Chunlin 2025

Qi Chunlin. (2025). The impact of adolescent innovation on academic resilience, distance learning self-efficacy, and academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-91542-7

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Independent samples t-tests comparing 452 middle school and 454 high school students. The article reports η2 = 0.35 for academic resilience and η2 = 0.32 for academic performance, both favoring high school students (Table 5).

> "High school students scored significantly higher in online learning innovativeness (M = 3.92, SD = 1.26) compared to middle school students (M = 2.52, SD = 0.94), with an MD of 1.40 (t = -18.89, η2 = 0.36), reflecting a strong explanatory power of grade level for this variable."

### Qi Chunlin 2025 (2)

Qi Chunlin. (2025). The impact of adolescent innovation on academic resilience, distance learning self-efficacy, and academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-91542-7

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Independent samples t-test for digital learning self-efficacy by grade level, printing η2 = 0.06. The article states the small effect size indicates grade level has "a limited impact on this variable".

> "High school students achieved slightly higher scores (M = 3.73, SD = 0.86) compared to middle school students (M = 3.25, SD = 1.03), with an MD of 0.48 (t = -7.51, η2 = 0.06)."
