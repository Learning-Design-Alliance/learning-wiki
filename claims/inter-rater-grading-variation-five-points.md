Connor Gorman, David Webb, Kevin Gee. (2018). Hierarchical Linear Modeling Approach to Measuring the Effects of Class Size and Other Classroom Characteristics on Student Learning in an Active-Learning Based Introductory Physics Course. University of California, Davis. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1809.00218](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1809.00218)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article reports, citing early inter-rater reliability studies of K-12 teachers, that variation between teachers grading the same assignment was about 5 points on a 100 point scale; a few studies disagreed. Primary sources of variation were inability to distinguish similar merit, differing grading standards, and differing weights.

> "These studies largely showed a significant degree of variation between teachers (about 5 points on a 100 point scale), though a few studies disagreed with this conclusion."
