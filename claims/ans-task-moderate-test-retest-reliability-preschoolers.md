### Shusterman Anna 2016

Shusterman Anna, Slusser Emily, Halberda Justin, Odic Darko. (2016). Acquisition of the Cardinal Principle Coincides with Improvement in Approximate Number System Acuity in Preschoolers. PLoS ONE 11(4): e0153072. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0153072

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Test-retest reliability of the Panamath-based Who Has More task across roughly three months in the longitudinal sample (r = .46, a moderate correlation). The authors note measurement noise would limit detection of acuity effects and call for implicit measures such as ERP, NIRS, or eye-tracking for this age group.
