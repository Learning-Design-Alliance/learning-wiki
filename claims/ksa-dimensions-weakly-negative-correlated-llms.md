# Knowledge, Skills, and Attitude scores are largely independent across frontier LLMs, with Knowledge–Skills correlation r=−0.51 and Knowledge–Attitude r=−0.63

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across seven evaluated models, Knowledge correlates negatively with Skills (r=−0.51) and with Attitude (r=−0.63), while Skills and Attitude correlate weakly positively (r=0.22). [→ Unggi Lee 2026](#unggi-lee-2026)

## Evidence

### Unggi Lee 2026

OpenLearnLM Benchmark: A Unified Framework for Evaluating Knowledge, Skill, and Attitude in Educational Large Language Models. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13882 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2601.13882](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2601.13882)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis across ten evaluation dimensions over seven frontier models (Pearson r across 7 models, Figure 6c). The article reports "r=−0.51" between Knowledge and Skills, r=−0.63 between Knowledge and Attitude, and r=0.22 between Skills and Attitude; no significance tests are printed.

> "The cor- relation between Knowledge (average of Content and Peda- gogy) and Skills (average of six centers) isr=−0.51, indi- cating that models strong in factual knowledge do not neces- sarily excel in practical educational tasks."
