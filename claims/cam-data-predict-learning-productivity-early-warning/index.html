Evidence¶

Kubsch 2025¶

Kubsch, M., Strauß, S., Grimm, A., Gombert, S., Drachsler, H., Neumann, K., & Rummel, N. (2025). Self-regulated Learning in the Digitally Enhanced Science Classroom: Toward an Early Warning System. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10011-9

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Hyperparameter search across 220 ML models (four model types, eleven data-source combinations, five timepoints) with tenfold cross-validation on data from 214 students. Integrated CAM combinations outperformed unimodal ones; "the models that used the aggregated cognitive data (Meta & Emo & agg. knowledge) yielded higher accuracies".

"Overall, combining cognitive, affective, and metacognitive data yielded the highest accuracies (Meta & Emo & all knowledge / Meta & Emo & agg. knowledge), with the models that used the aggregated cognitive data (Meta & Emo & agg. knowledge) yielded higher accuracies than those that used all individual cognitive scores"
