## Evidence

### Atlanoğlu Ş 2026

Atlanoğlu Ş, Gedik MA. (2026). Large language models in radiology exams: A cross-sectional comparative analysis of performance in Turkish and English. Medicine 2026;105:26(e49517). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000049517

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Language-based performance analysis in which the same 100 questions were administered separately in Turkish and English to ChatGPT-5 and Gemini 2.5 Pro. McNemar tests found no significant difference (P = 1.000 for both models); Cohen kappa indicated "good" linguistic consistency for both models.

> "The analysis revealed no statistically significant difference in success rates between the 2 languages in either model (McNemar, P = 1.000). Assessment of the agreement between responses in different languages showed that both ChatGPT-5 (kappa = 0.733) and Gemini 2.5 Pro (kappa = 0.734) demonstrated a "good" level of linguistic consistency"
