Evidence¶

Magnetic Reading Foundations 2022¶

Magnetic Reading Foundations. (2022). https://www.curriculumassociates.com/professional-development

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

A quasi-experimental study in school year 2022-23 compared 822 second grade students using the program with 793 students in comparable schools using different literacy curricula, controlling for baseline demographics and achievement. Students using the program scored higher ("effect size =+0.15").

"The use of Magnetic Reading Foundations had a positive and statistically significant effect on spring i-Ready Diagnostic scores after controlling for baseline demographics and achievement."

Magnetic Reading Foundations 2022 (2)¶

Magnetic Reading Foundations. (2022). https://www.curriculumassociates.com/professional-development

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The study spanned 11 schools in two Northeast states, 6 schools in one Midwest state, and 24 schools in one Western state. After matching and attrition, 822 program students were compared to 793 comparison students in comparable schools; no effect size is printed for the sample composition.

"Across the 41 schools, after matching and attrition, a total of 822 second grade students using Magnetic Reading Foundations were compared to 793 students, enrolled in comparable schools, using different literacy curricula."
