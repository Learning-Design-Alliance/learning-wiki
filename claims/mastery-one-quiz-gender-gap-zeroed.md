## Evidence

### David J. Webb and Wendell H. Potter 2021

David J. Webb and Wendell H. Potter. (2021). Gender-grade-gap zeroed out under a specific intro-physics assessment regime. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2102.10451](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.10451)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

HLM analysis of 67 CLASP classes (Winter 2013–Winter 2016, 17,205 grades), comparing usual GRC-graded classes (N = 16,246, gap -0.223, P < 10^-3) with one-quiz-per-lecture mastery classes (N = 636, gap 0.025, P = 0.766). The article reports "female students had slightly higher grades than male students but not significantly higher" in mastery classes.

> "The usual gender gap shows that the grades of female students were almost a quarter of a standard deviation below those of male students for canonical grading in CLASP courses. On the other hand, for the mastery classes female students had slightly higher grades than male students but not significantly higher."
