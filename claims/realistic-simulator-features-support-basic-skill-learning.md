# Simulator features that realistically simulate basic PPH management steps may have supported learning of those skills

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors interpret that the MamaNatalie mannequin's features — a removable placenta, a contracting/relaxing uterus, and ability to bleed up to 1500 ml — may have helped learning of basic PPH management skills. [→ Nelissen Ellen 2017](#nelissen-ellen-2017)

## Evidence

### Nelissen Ellen 2017

Nelissen Ellen, Ersdal Hege, Mduma Estomih, Evjen-Olsen Bjørg, Twisk Jos, Broerse Jacqueline, van Roosmalen Jos, Stekelenburg Jelle. (2017). Clinical performance and patient outcome after simulation-based training in prevention and management of postpartum haemorrhage: an educational intervention study in a low-resource setting. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1481-7

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, not a tested result: they suggest these simulator features "may have helped the learning of these skills" of giving oxytocin, removing the placenta by controlled cord traction, and performing uterine massage. No experiment isolated simulator features.

> "The mannequin that was used in the HMS BAB training programme has features that very realistically simulate these basic steps: a placenta that can be removed by controlled cord traction, a uterus that can contract and relax, and the ability of the mannequin to bleed up to 1500 ml."
