## Evidence

### Stingone JA 2026

Stingone JA, McVeigh KH, Lednyak L. (2026). Early Intervention Developmental Programming and Childhood Academic Outcomes. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.55890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Retrospective cohort analysis of the NYC Longitudinal Study of Early Development (214,370 children; 13,022 EI recipients), using random-forest propensity score matching. The matched comparison showed "Higher absolute test scores were observed in ELA (estimate, 0.045; 95% CI, 0.021-0.069)"; no effect size beyond the printed estimate is reported.

> "Higher absolute test scores were observed in ELA (estimate, 0.045; 95% CI, 0.021-0.069) and greater incidence of meeting test-based standards in both math (incidence ratio, 1.08; 95% CI, 1.06-1.10) and ELA (incidence ratio, 1.09; 95% CI, 1.07-1.12) among children with a greater probability of receiving EI services when comparing propensity score–matched samples."
