`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation combining the Public Outreach Database statistics and preliminary survey results; the survey subset showed 11.3% of participants with a PhD, 34.9% a Master's and 28.1% a Bachelor's degree, so 74.3% hold an academic degree.

> "Theactualaudience still seems to largely consist of highly-educated science enthusiasts, who are more likely to spend a family trip in a science museum with their children."
