# Global field power is positively associated with associative learning performance (d') at 322–634 ms, with the 322–507 ms period sourced to the middle occipital gyrus (BA 19)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Stronger activation of active neural sources (GFP) was significantly associated with higher d' at 322–634 ms; the sub-period 322–507 ms was localized to the middle occipital gyrus (BA 19), suggesting better learners engage more visual-discrimination resources. [→ Raynal 2024](#raynal-2024)
`q3 i?` The two early GFP sub-periods (322–392 ms and 437–507 ms) shared one topography (r = 0.92, p < 0.001), distinct from the 530–600 ms period, supporting a split into two effects. [→ Raynal 2024 (3)](#raynal-2024-3)

## Evidence

### Raynal 2024

Raynal, E., Schipper, K., Brandner, C., Ruggeri, P. & Barral, J. (2024). Electrocortical correlates of attention differentiate individual capacity in associative learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00236-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

GFP analysis of ERPs from the same 38-participant EEG associative learning task; the analysis "revealed a signiﬁcant positive association of the GFP with d’ at 322–634 ms".

> "The GFP analysis revealed a signiﬁcant positive association of the GFP with d’ at 322–634 ms (Fig.2a), indicating that stronger activation of active neural sources was associated with higherd’."

### Raynal 2024 (2)

Raynal, E., Schipper, K., Brandner, C., Ruggeri, P. & Barral, J. (2024). Electrocortical correlates of attention differentiate individual capacity in associative learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00236-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

sLORETA source estimation for the 322–507 ms GFP covariance map in the same study; sources "included the middle occipital gyrus (BA 19)", which the authors link to discriminating shapes and colors.

> "The underlying sources for this period (Fig. 2d) included the middle occipital gyrus (BA 19), with a maximum value at MNI coordinates X= −40, Y = −85, Z = −10."

### Raynal 2024 (3)

Raynal, E., Schipper, K., Brandner, C., Ruggeri, P. & Barral, J. (2024). Electrocortical correlates of attention differentiate individual capacity in associative learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00236-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spatial-correlation check on the divided GFP interval in the same EEG study: the first two ~70 ms windows merged (r = 0.92, p < 0.001) while the 530–600 ms window formed a distinct topography.

> "The results indicated that the two ﬁrst periods were very similar and could be merged into one (r = 0.92, p < 0.001), while the third period was not highly correlated with the other two (r = 0.59,p < 0.001 with the ﬁrst one andr = 0.69,p < 0.001 with the second one) and thus reﬂected a distinct topography."
