## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Gain differences favoring TPS were significant on analysis (t=3.32, p=0.001), argument (t=16.09, p<0.001), prioritization (t=3.45, p=0.001), problem solving (t=9.39, p<0.001), and resolution (t=5.04, p<0.001). [→ Mahmoud Kaddoura 2013](#mahmoud-kaddoura-2013)

## Evidence

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of pretest-to-posttest gain differences on the five HESI CT subscales (analysis, argument, prioritization, problem solving, resolution) between the TPS and Non-TPS groups, reported in Table 2 with t tests and Mann-Whitney tests; the article notes the observations held "with a very minor exception".
