Descriptive statistics from Table 1 of the pretest-posttest-delayed posttest study with 61 Grade 8 students. The article reports a trend favoring the Clicker Group and "high individual differences among the participants in the Clicker Group"; these are descriptive patterns, not tested effects.

> "a trend showing its advantage on the two posttests (e.g. M=6.61 vs. M=4.97; see Figure 3 ); and (2) a higher SD on both posttests (e.g. SD=3.81 vs. SD=2.31; see Table 1 ), indicating high individual differences among the participants in the Clicker Group"
