## Evidence

### Wahba R 2020

Wahba R, Datta R, Bußhoff J, Bruns T, Hedergott A, Gietzelt C, Dieplinger G, Fuchs H, Morgenstern B, Möller D, Hellmich M, Bruns CJ, Stippel DL. (2020). 3D Versus 4K Display System – Influence of “State-of-the-art”-Display Technique on Surgical Performance (IDOSP-study) in Minimally Invasive Surgery: A Randomized Cross-over Trial. Annals of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLA.0000000000004328

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Figure 2 course analysis of individual performance trajectories for the task "rope pass" across the six repetitions. The article describes this as a training effect in the course of the repetitions; a comparable trend was seen for mistakes.

> "The performance time continuously dropped to its minimum at the end, for participants that started with the 4K system and finished the Parkour with the 3D systems. The subjects that started with the 3D system reached the shortest time with the 3D system after 3 repetitions, followed by an increase of the performance time with the 4K system, not reaching the 3D-minimum again."
