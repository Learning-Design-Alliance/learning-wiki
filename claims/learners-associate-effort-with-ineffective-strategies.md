Scheiter, K., Ackerman, R., & Hoogerheide, V. (2020). Looking at Mental Effort Appraisals through a Metacognitive Lens: Are they Biased? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09555-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Kirk-Johnson et al. (2019), that learners who gave higher effort appraisals to interleaved practice (known to be more effective than blocked practice) nevertheless "evaluated interleaved practice as being less effective, and chose it less often as the preferred strategy for future st udy," suggesting negative connotations of effort.

> "Although learners gave higher effort appraisals to interleaved practice, as expected, they evaluated interleaved practice as being less effective, and chose it less often as the preferred strategy for future st udy"
