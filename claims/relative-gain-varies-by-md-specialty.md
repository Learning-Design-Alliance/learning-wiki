One-day workshop-based training improves physical activity prescription knowledge in Latin American physicians: a pre-test post-test study. BMC Public Health 16:1224. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-016-3883-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Specialty sub-group comparison of individual relative gain among 1044 MDs, analyzed with t-tests. The Results text reports the "other specialties" sub-group "had the greatest relative knowledge gain (44% CI: 34–55%)" at p < 0.05; sports medicine MDs had the lowest gain (14% per the discussion).

> "In addition, the “other specialties” sub-group had the greatest relative knowledge gain (44% CI: 34–55%) and this gain was significantly greater than all the other specialty groups evaluated (p <0.05) (Fig. 3)."
