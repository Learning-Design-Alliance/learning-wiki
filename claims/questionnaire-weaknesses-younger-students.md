> "Results also indicate weaknesses in using questionnaire methods when observing younger students."

### Tejinder Kaur 2022 (2)

Tejinder Kaur, Aishwarya Banavathu, David Blair, Rahul Choudhary, and Alex Foppolli. (2022). Can 8-Year-Olds Understand Concepts of Einsteinian Physics?. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.00842](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.00842)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The Class 1 teacher trialled a multiple-choice questionnaire, repeating it after one week; students chose different answers a week later, which the authors say validated that even multiple-choice answers were not good enough.

> "Class 1 teacher said the students knew the answers, but it was difficult for them to articulate their answers and put them into writing."
