# When students generated a correct entry after a bottom-out hint, they were successful in about 90% of instances

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After seeing a bottom-out hint, students who generated a correct entry were successful in 90% of instances on average, possibly after several tries. [→ Kasia Muldner · Winslow Burleson · Brett Van de Sande · Kurt VanLehn 2010](#kasia-muldner-winslow-burleson-brett-van-de-sande-kurt-vanlehn-2010)

## Evidence

### Kasia Muldner · Winslow Burleson · Brett Van de Sande · Kurt VanLehn 2010

Kasia Muldner · Winslow Burleson · Brett Van de Sande · Kurt VanLehn (2010). An analysis of students’ gaming behaviors in an intelligent tutoring system: predictors and impacts. User Model User-Adap Inter. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11257-010-9086-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hint-utility analysis of Andes logs measuring the percentage of time each student was eventually successful at generating the correct entry after each hint type. The article notes students may require several attempts and that hint A is not counted as successful if hint B precedes the correct entry.

> "If students did generate a correct entry (or entries) after seeing a bottom-out hint, on average, they were successful in 90% of instances"
