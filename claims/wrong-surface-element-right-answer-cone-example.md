### Sergio Rojas 2009

Sergio Rojas. (2009). On the teaching and learning of physics: A Criticism and a Systemic Approach. https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.1151 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0902.1151](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0902.1151)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is the article's worked illustrative example (Section 4), based on the author's teaching experience: using dS = 2πrdz gives S = πRH (wrong) yet still yields I = MR²/2, the right answer. The author reports students perform these "wrong compu tations" without hesitation.

> "Steeping on our teaching experience, it is too easy for students to perform without hesitation the just aforementioned wrong compu tations, as presented in the previous step. And it is not easy to get students to realize their mistake."
