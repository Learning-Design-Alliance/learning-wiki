Arán Filippetti 2020¶

Study 1 used a latent-variable SEM approach to test the flexibility components' contribution to academic skills. The authors report that "only SF proved to be a significant predictor of writing and reading comprehension."

"Besides, only SF proved to be a significant predictor of writing and reading comprehension by using a latent-variable structural equation approach (SEM)."
