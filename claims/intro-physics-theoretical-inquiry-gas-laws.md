### M.E. McCracken 2015

M.E. McCracken. (2015). The Two Subcultures: The teaching of theory and Physics' place in the college curriculum. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1508.00273](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1508.00273)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The author describes, from his own teaching practice, a classroom exercise in which students propose an invisible-particle model of gases and, after applying algebra, "have derived the empirical gas laws". He calls this "a powerful way of gaining knowledge" and theoretical inquiry; it is an instructional example, not an empirical study.

> "In their second semester of physics, we propose to students an insight: What if gases are made up of tiny particles, too small to see, and they just bounce around inside of a volume and don't interact with one another? The students, willing to humor their instructor, find that forty minutes later, after applying a little algebra"
