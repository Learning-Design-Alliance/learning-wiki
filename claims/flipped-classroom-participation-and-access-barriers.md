## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors identify reliance on students watching lectures at home and unequal home technology access as barriers to the flipped model. [→ Karimi and Hamzavi 2017](#karimi-and-hamzavi-2017)

## Evidence

### Karimi and Hamzavi 2017

Mehrnoosh Karimi, Raouf Hamzavi. (2017). The Effect of Flipped Model of Instruction on EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension: Learners' Attitudes in Focus. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 8(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.1p.95

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' stated limitation in the conclusion section, not an empirical result. They also note that many students lack access to the technology needed for viewing lecture material, and that the study was confined to a limited number of EFL students (N=50).

> "Unfortunately, there is no way to guarantee students will oblige or cooperate with the flipped model and students who do not complete the videos or study at home may come to class unprepared."
