## Evidence

### Netzer R. 2025

Netzer R., Levi N., Ganchrow K., Naan Y., Elboim-Gabyzon M. (2025). Clinical Competency in Managing Malnutrition–Sarcopenia Syndrome Among Physical Therapists: A Cross-Sectional Survey. Nutrients 17(2), 281. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu17020281

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Six-statement Likert-scale attitudes and beliefs measure (Cronbach's alpha 0.7) in the survey of 337 PTs. The article reports importance ratings of "4.5 ± 0.6 out of 5" and "4.2 ± 0.7 out of 5" against self-belief ratings of "2.4 ± 1.0 and 2.1 ± 1.0 out of 5". No effect size printed.

> "Participants highly agreed on the importance of diagnosing and treating sarcopenia (4.5 ± 0.6 out of 5) and malnutrition (4.2 ± 0.7 out of 5) within the scope of physical therapy practice. However, the self- belief in their ability to diagnose and treat these conditions was noticeably low (2.4 ± 1.0 and 2.1 ± 1.0 out of 5, respectively)"
