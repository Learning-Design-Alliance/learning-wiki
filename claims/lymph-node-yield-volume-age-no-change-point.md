`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Secondary outcome in the volume comparison: a persistent group-level difference in lymph node yield without a within-surgeon learning-curve change point.

> "The number of lymph nodes removed and examined was higher for ‘higher-volume surgeons’ (median 11 [IQR 5–20]) compared with ‘lower-volume surgeons’ (median 5 [IQR 3–11]) (p < 0.001), but no change points were present in the graphs (Fig. 1e)."
