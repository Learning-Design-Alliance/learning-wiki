Shiza Khaqan 2024¶

Shiza Khaqan, Gisela Redondo-Sama. (2024). A systematic review of the role of dialogic leadership: Characterization and impacts. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100618

q2 · i? · review · r2

Thematic synthesis of the review's institutional-level outcomes; the review reports that increased dialogue "brought about social unity" and "resulted in creating long-lasting friendships", citing multiple case studies of learning communities and activists. No effect size is printed.

"As dialogue is the cornerstone of dialogic leadership (Redondo-Sama, 2016), an increase in dialogue not only brought about social unity but also resulted in creating long-lasting friendships (Campos et al., 2009; Le´on--Jim´enez, 2020; Morl`a-Folch et al., 2022; Serradell, 2015; Valls et al., 2017)."
