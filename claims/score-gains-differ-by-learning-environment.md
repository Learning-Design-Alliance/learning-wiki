## Evidence

### Figueroa 2025

Figueroa, R. B., Jr., & Jung, I. (2025). Integrating VR tours in online language learning: A design-based research study. Australasian Journal of Educational Technology, 41(1). [doi:10.14742/ajet.9498](https://doi.org/10.14742/ajet.9498)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Post-hoc tests following a significant Friedman test of score gain by physical learning environment (home, workplace, café) in the DBR study. The article also reports the omnibus result as "F(2) = 10.89, p < 0.01"; no effect size printed.

> "The Nemenyi post-hoc test revealed significant differences in score gains between home-based and workplace-based learners (p < 0.05), as well as between home-based and café-based learners (p < 0.05)."
