measgeom¶

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2013, December). Elementary School Mathematics intervention report: DreamBox Learning. Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/627

q3 · i0 · causal · r3

Supplemental subscale findings from the same RCT (n = 550): intervention mean 155.50 (SD 18.30) versus comparison 151.80 (SD 18.10), effect size 0.15, improvement index +6, p = 0.03. The report states the WWC-computed critical p-value was 0.02 for this subtest, so it is not statistically significant.
