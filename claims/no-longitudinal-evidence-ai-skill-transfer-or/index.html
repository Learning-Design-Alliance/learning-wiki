Evidence¶

Banskota Bibek 2025¶

Banskota Bibek, Bhusal Rajan, Yadav Prkash Kumar, Banskota Ashok Kumar. (2025). Artificial intelligence in orthopaedic education, training and research: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08162-y

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review identifies a critical evidence gap: almost none of the included studies followed trainees into real surgery, and robust comparisons between AI-based curricula and standard teaching are scarce.

"a paucity of literature exists to evaluate the effect of a longitudinal VR curriculum on direct patient care performance"
