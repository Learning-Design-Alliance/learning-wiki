## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On 14 Likert items, participants agreed or strongly agreed that virtual content is correctly aligned to real objects (median 5), spatial relationships are easy to perceive (median 5), and gesture interaction is easy and intuitive (median 5), with voice interaction at median 4.5 and spatial sounds rated as making the experience more immersive (median 4.5). ## Evidence

### Condino Sara 2018

Condino Sara, Turini Giuseppe, Parchi Paolo D., Viglialoro Rosanna M., Piolanti Nicola, Gesi Marco, Ferrari Mauro, Ferrari Vincenzo. (2018). How to Build a Patient-Specific Hybrid Simulator for Orthopaedic Open Surgery: Benefits and Limits of Mixed-Reality Using the Microsoft HoloLens. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Likert questionnaire (14 items, 5-point scale) administered after the HipSim task session in the twenty-participant qualitative study; medians with interquartile ranges summarize visual and audio perception and interaction items. The overall median opinion on visual fatigue was neutral (median 3.5).

> "Overall, participants agreed/strongly agreed that the virtual content is correctly aligned to the real objects (median 5), it is easy to perceive the spatial relationships between real and virtual objects (median 5), they did not notice motion of virtual content (median 4), they did not notice latency (median 4)"
