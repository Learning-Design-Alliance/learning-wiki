# Offering experimental Pell Grants for short occupational programs to low-income students who already hold a bachelor's degree increased program enrollment and completion by about 20 percentage points

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among Pell-eligible, un- or underemployed students with a bachelor's degree, being offered experimental Pell Grant funds increased enrollment in and completion of short occupational programs by about 20 percentage points relative to not being offered the funds. ### Jaime Thomas 2020

Jaime Thomas, Naihobe Gonzalez, Nora Paxton, Andrew Wiegand, Leela Hebbar. (2020). The Effects of Expanding Pell Grant Eligibility for Short Occupational Training Programs: Results from the Experimental Sites Initiative. NCEE 2021–001. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs/2021001/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`
