Adrie A. Koehler, Daniela Rezende Vilarinho-Pereira. (2023). Using social media affordances to support Ill-structured problem-solving skills: considering possibilities and challenges. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10060-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Mixed-methods study of 62 undergraduates at a South Korean university examining Kakao-Talk use outside class to extend in-class writing activities. The review reports "significantly higher posttest scores (M = 32.79, SD = 9.82) in comparison with pretest scores (M = 18.24, SD = 11.11)" for students with low English proficiency, plus a sense of presence, challenges, and efficacy.

> "The results showed significantly higher posttest scores (M = 32.79, SD = 9.82) in comparison with pretest scores (M = 18.24, SD = 11.11). Participating in the activities via Kakao-Talk also allowed students to create a sense of presence"
