The review reports, in its Stroke Units section, a meta-analytic comparison the authors attribute to a recent landmark meta-analysis, stating stroke unit care "reduces mortality by saving two additional lives per 100 patients treated" versus general medical wards. No effect size or confidence interval is printed.
