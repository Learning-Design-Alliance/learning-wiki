Evidence¶

Shadaan 2013¶

Shadaan, P., & Leong, K. E. (2013). Effectiveness of Using Geogebra on Students' Understanding in Learning Circles. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 1(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086434

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment with two intact Year 9 classes (experimental n = 28, control n = 25) in an international school in Selangor; independent samples t-test on post test scores showed the experimental mean of 16.46 exceeded the control mean of 12.24, with a "mean score difference between the groups was 4.22 with a t-value of 3.989" and p < .05.

"Table 3 shows that the control group obtained a mean score of 12.24 while the experimental group obtained a mean score of 16.46. The mean score difference between the groups was 4.22 with a t-value of 3.989."
