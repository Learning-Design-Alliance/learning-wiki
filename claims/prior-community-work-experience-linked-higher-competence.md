## Evidence

### Asher Laura 2021

Asher Laura, Birhane Rahel, Teferra Solomon, Milkias Barkot, Worku Benyam, Habtamu Alehegn, Kohrt Brandon A., Hanlon Charlotte. (2021). “Like a doctor, like a brother”: Achieving competence amongst lay health workers delivering community-based rehabilitation for people with schizophrenia in Ethiopia. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246158

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of per-worker mean ENACT-E scores (Table 1) showed consistent rankings across training and pilot, and the article states that "those with previous relevant experience tended to score higher at all time points". Small sample; no inferential tests.

> "Across the role-play training assessments, mean ENACT scores ranged from 2.06 (CBR worker 6) to 2.55 (CBR workers 3 and 4) (Table 1). The gap had narrowed during months 5–11 of the pilot, with mean scores ranging from 2.80 (CBR worker 6) to 2.97 (CBR worker 4)."
