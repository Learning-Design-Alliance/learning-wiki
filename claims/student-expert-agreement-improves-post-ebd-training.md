`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective cohort study measuring inter-operator agreement between students and expert reference answers using Fleiss multirater kappa. Agreement improved from "poor" at T0 to "fair" at T1 (kappa = 0.274).

> "At T0, student‐expert agreement was poor (−0.057, 95% CI [−0.094; −0.019], p = 0.003) (Table 2), but improved significantly at T1 (kappa = 0.274, 95% CI [0.226; 0.321], p = 0.001) (Table 3)."
