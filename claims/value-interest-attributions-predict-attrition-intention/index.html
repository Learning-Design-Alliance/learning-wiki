Narrative review reporting Norman et al. (2024): college students rated possible causes of a motivation problem in a course; attributions to lack of value and interest were the strongest negative predictors of intention to take additional classes. Similar patterns appeared in recommendations about a hypothetical student.

"attributions to lack of value and interest were the strongest (negative) predictors of the students' reported intention to take additional classes in the field, over and above the (positive) effect of course grade (Norman et al., 2024)"
