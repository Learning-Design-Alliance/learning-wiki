## Evidence

### Farnaz S 2020

Farnaz S, Zoghoul S, Alahmad M, Al Qudah H. (2020). The influence of gender on clinical examination skills of medical students in Jordan: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02002-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 188 Jordanian final-year medical students comparing intimate examination frequency by sex via chi-square tests. The results state "the gender division was more prominent for intimate examinations" with printed odds ratios from Table 4 for breast, vaginal and prostate examinations.

> "the gender division was more prominent for intimate examinations, with a lower odds of males conducting breast 0.11 (0.04–0.28) and vaginal examinations 0.22 (0.02–1.98) and more male students conducting prostate examinations OR 11.00 (1.39–87.03)"
