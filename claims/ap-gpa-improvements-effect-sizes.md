## Evidence

### Pablo Egana-delSol 2023

Pablo Egana-delSol. (2023). The impacts of a high-school art-based program on academic achievements, creativity, and creative behaviors. npj Science of Learning 8:39. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00187-6

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Doubly robust reweighted regression and PSM analyses of students participating in at least two AP workshops versus matched controls found significant GPA improvements with printed effect sizes of 0.55 sd overall, 0.61 sd language, 0.36 sd math, and 0.33 sd art, including "0.55 sd on overall GPA."

> "The evidence supporting Hypothesis 3 came in the form of a signiﬁcant impact of 0.55 sd on overall GPA, 0.61 sd on language GPA, 0.36 on math GPA, and 0.33 sd on art GPA"
