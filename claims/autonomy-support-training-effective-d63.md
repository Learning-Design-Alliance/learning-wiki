`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The article's meta-analysis synthesized 19 independent studies with 20 effect sizes using Hunter–Schmidt methods, with effect sizes weighted by sample size and corrected for artifacts. It found the training programs were "overall, effective with a weighted effect size of 0.63."

> "A meta-analysis of the findings from 19 studies with 20 effect sizes showed that the training programs were, overall, effective with a weighted effect size of 0.63."
