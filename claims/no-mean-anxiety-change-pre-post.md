## Evidence

### Remy Dou and Justyna P. Zwolak 2019

Remy Dou and Justyna P. Zwolak. (2019). Practitioner’s guide to social network analysis: Examining physics anxiety in an active-learning setting. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020105](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020105)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired samples t-tests on imputed PARS scores from two Modeling Instruction sections (N=126 combined). The article reports "no signiﬁcant diﬀerence between the mean pre and post physics anxiety total scores"; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "Paired samples t-tests showed no signiﬁcant diﬀerence between the mean pre and post physics anxiety total scores, regardless of instructor ( t = 0.74, p = 0.46 for Section A, t =−1.74, p = 0.09 for Section B) , nor when combining instructor data ( t = −0.65, p = 0.52)."
