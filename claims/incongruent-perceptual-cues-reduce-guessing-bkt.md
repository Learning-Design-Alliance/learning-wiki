# In a perceptual-cue intervention reanalysis, incongruent spacing and incongruent color conditions were associated with reduced guessing relative to neutral conditions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Posterior contrasts showed credible reductions in the guess parameter under incongruent spacing (-0.058, 95% CrI [-0.093, -0.024]) and incongruent color (-0.082, 95% CrI [-0.121, -0.040]) relative to neutral conditions. [→ Siddhartha Pradhan 2026](#siddhartha-pradhan-2026)

## Evidence

### Siddhartha Pradhan 2026

Siddhartha Pradhan, Yanping Pei, Morgan Lee, Puyuan Zhang, Erin Ottmar, and Adam C. Sales. (2026). StanBKT: Rethinking Parameter Estimation in Bayesian Knowledge Tracing. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23048 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.23048](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.23048)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Multi BKT reanalysis with MCMC of an open-source perceptual cue intervention study (688 sixth-grade students who completed all tasks, 19,952 observations, nine conditions in a 3x3 spacing-by-color design). "the clearest and most consistent structure emerges inP(G)" with credible reductions under incongruent conditions; credible intervals excluding zero were interpreted as evidence of systematic change.

> "In contrast, the clearest and most consistent structure emerges inP(G), where both spacing and color produce sizable and credible deviation from the neutral condition, particularly under incongruent conditions (spacing:-0.058[-0.093,-0.024]; color:-0.082[-0.121,-0.040])."
