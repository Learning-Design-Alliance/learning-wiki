## Evidence

### Shaeema Zaman Ahmed 2022

Shaeema Zaman Ahmed, Carrie A. Weidner, Jesper H. M. Jensen, Jacob F. Sherson, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2022). Student use of a quantum simulation and visualization tool. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac93c7](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac93c7)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coded analysis of the five think-aloud interviews (Fig. 3 categorization). The authors report "roughly equal number of items" in the correct-reasoning-after-Composer category and the no-reasoning-upon-revision category. This is a qualitative categorization with no effect size.

> "Holistically, we see that while Composer can facilitate the development of correct reasoning, this is not universal, as indicated by the roughly equal number of items in Fig. 3 corresponding to cases III (correct reasoning provided after using Composer) and IV (no reasoning provided upon revision or no answer provided upon revision)."
