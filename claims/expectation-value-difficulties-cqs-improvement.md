Challenges in addressing student difficulties with quantum measurement of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. Qualitative analysis of responses to question 5 in the second in-person class found pretest errors such as listing eigenvalues with probabilities or writing phrases like "expected value" instead of calculating the expectation value; these were much reduced on the post-test.

> "All these difficulties were observed in much smaller numbers on the post-test."
