## Evidence

### Jacob T. Stanley 2015

Jacob T. Stanley, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Laura Kiepura, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2015). Investigating student ownership of projects in an upper-division physics lab course. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.079](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.079)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' interpretation of coded reflection excerpts, including one student describing feeling "frustrating and draining" during a stalled search for a mode lock and an "ecstatic occasion" upon finding one. The Affect coding category reached 91% agreement and kappa 0.80.

> "This suggests that the emotional evolution of students’ experience in the lab setting is complex and does not ﬁt neatly into one particular aﬀective category."
