The synthesis reports that deep thinking and connection-making let students build strong memories and make "meaningful connections between the material, their own lives, and the world around them".

> "When students see how material relates to their lives and interests as well as other concepts they already know, they have frameworks for understanding the material more easily and can learn it more deeply."
