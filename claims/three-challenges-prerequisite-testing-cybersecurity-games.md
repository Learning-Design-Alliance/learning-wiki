Valdemar Švábenský and Jan Vykopal. (2018). Challenges Arising from Prerequisite Testing in Cybersecurity Games. Proceedings of SIGCSE '18: The 49th ACM Technical Symposium on Computing Science Education. https://doi.org/10.1145/3159450.3159454

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' reflective account of the experiment design (Section 6.3). The three named challenges are "calibrating the test to predict the possession of skills most relevant to the game", the limited time frame (assessment capped at 8 minutes, still seen as an inconvenience), and embedding the pretest in the game.

> "Despite using a well-established framework for question-writing [2] and following best practices of assessment design, we were confronted by three main challenges of prerequisite testing."
