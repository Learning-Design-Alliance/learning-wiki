Roschelle 2024¶

The reviewers' own interpretive statement in the Risks section, not a tested result. They state that "the behavior of generative AI tools are difficult to explain, inspect, or predict" and attribute this complexity to the statistical nature of the mechanisms.

"Digital Promise notes that underlying all risks is the fact that the behavior of generative AI tools are difficult to explain, inspect, or predict; this underlying complexity is inherent in the statistical nature of their mechanisms."
