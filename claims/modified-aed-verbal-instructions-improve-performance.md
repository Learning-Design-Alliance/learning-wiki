## Evidence

### Tayebi-Hillali Hoda 2026

Tayebi-Hillali Hoda, Aballe Marta, Santana Denisse, Varela-Aneiros Iván, Diniz-Freitas Márcio, Diz-Dios Pedro. (2026). Competence in the use of a semi-automated external defibrillator among undergraduate dental students: evaluation and proposal for improvement. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10025-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second-phase quasi-experimental comparison with 39 final-year dental students using an 80-second modified instruction set. The article reports "significant improvements were observed compared to the first phase" across device activation, adherence to instructions, electrode connection, and safety check, with median time to shock delivery decreasing to 116 s.

> "In the second phase, significant improvements were observed compared to the first phase in the following items: device activation (p < 0.001), adherence to instructions (p < 0.001), electrode connection (p = 0.006), and safety check prior to shock delivery (p < 0.001). The median time to shock delivery decreased to 116 s (95% CI 107–124 s) (p < 0.001)."
