### Datu 2025

Datu, J. A. D., Sun, X. H., Liu, S., Pang, F., & Watt, H. (2025). Are gritty children more engaged in math? Triarchic model of grit, math self-efficacy beliefs, and math engagement in Chinese children in Macau. School Psychology International, 46(6), 582–602. https://doi.org/10.1177/01430343251386902

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of the cross-sectional survey of 794 Macau primary students positioned math self-efficacy as an intermediate variable and the four engagement dimensions as outcomes. The article reports that "math self-efficacy positively predicted all four math engagement dimensions"; individual path coefficients are not printed in the text provided.
