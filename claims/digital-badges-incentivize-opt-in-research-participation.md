Design-evolution report from the Expected Requirements section (user experience work). The platform, whose studies are opt-in, replaced gift cards with shareable digital badges after iterative user research on adult-learner motivation; no comparative effectiveness data are reported.

> "Transitioning from a previous strategy to offer a small number of gift cards, OpenStax Kinetic has moved toward a scalable strategy to incentivize students to participate in studies by offering digital badges that can be shared with their professional networks, providing learners with tangible evidence of their accomplishments."
