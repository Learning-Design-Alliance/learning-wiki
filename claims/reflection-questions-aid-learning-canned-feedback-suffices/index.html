Evidence¶

Sandra Katz 2003¶

Sandra Katz, David Allbritton, John Connelly. (2003). Going Beyond the Problem Given: How Human Tutors Use Post-Solution Discussions to Support Transfer. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 13, pp.79-116. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00197313

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Study 2 assigned forty-six students to three conditions in Andes: no reflection questions, reflection questions with human tutors, or the same questions with canned feedback. Learning was measured by overall and conceptual/quantitative gain scores. "Students learned more with reflection questions and feedback than without."

"Students learned more with reflection questions and feedback than without, but the canned feedback and human tutored conditions did not differ significantly."
