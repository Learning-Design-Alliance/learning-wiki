## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A mathematics education expert rated system feedback for correctly judged open-ended responses on a four-dimension rubric, yielding a pooled mean of 3.98 (n = 754, SD = 1.78), with item means from 4.55 (Q9) down to 3.33 (Q15). [→ Yong Oh Lee 2026](#yong-oh-lee-2026)

## Evidence

### Yong Oh Lee 2026

Yong Oh Lee, Byeonghun Bang, Joohyun Lee, Sejun Oh. (2026). Personalized Auto-Grading and Feedback System for Constructive Geometry Tasks Using Large Language Models on an Online Math Platform. Preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1109/access.2026.3657726](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3657726)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative expert evaluation using a five-point Likert rubric on accuracy, clarity, instructional guidance, and linguistic tone, applied to feedback on responses where teachers agreed with the system's grading. The pooled mean was "3.98 (n = 754)"; Q15 scored lowest (mean = 3.33, SD = 2.03).

> "Aggregating across all seven explanatory questions yields a pooled mean of 3.98 (n = 754) with a pooled standard deviation of 1.78, suggesting that the feedback was generally viewed as educationally serviceable but with substantial variability across items."
