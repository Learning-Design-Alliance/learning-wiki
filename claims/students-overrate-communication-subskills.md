# Students rate themselves significantly more positively than trainers on several communication sub-skills, with effect sizes from small to very high

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Students rated themselves higher than trainers on taking the patient's history (d = 0.316), empathy (d = 0.212), and the silence method (d = 0.403), among others (p < 0.001). [→ Akan Hülya 2025](#akan-hulya-2025)

## Evidence

### Akan Hülya 2025

Akan Hülya, Delibaş Hamza Yasin, Koran Serhat, Yiğit Pakize, Tanriöver Özlem. (2025). Enhancing self-regulated learning: a comparison of medical students’ self-assessment and faculty evaluations on communication skills in simulated patient interviews. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07794-4

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Dependent-sample t-tests on ten five-point Likert items from 193 students and their trainers. Table 3 prints effect sizes: Q16 d = 0.316, Q20 d = 0.212, Q21 d = 0.403 (t = 11.387, p < 0.001), Q17 d = 0.122, Q18 d = 0.126, Q23 d = 0.191; students scored higher on all but time use.

> "a significant difference was found between assessors and students in the questions of taking the patient’s history, questioning the patient’s perspective, anxiety and expectation, using time effectively, using empathy with the patient, using the silence method and using the reflection technique while taking the patient’s history (p < 0.001)"
