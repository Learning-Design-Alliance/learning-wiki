### Zhou Fen 2026

Zhou Fen, Yu Ping, Yu Xiaofen, Han Xuyan, Wang Zheng. (2026). Family-school-hospital collaboration model for urinary system tumor early screening education. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1837667

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Non-randomized controlled pre-post study of 120 clinical medical interns (60 per group) in a provincial hospital in China. The intervention group received a 6-month family-school-hospital collaborative program via family WeChat groups with 3D printing-assisted teaching; the control group received traditional clinical teaching only. "significantly greater improvements in knowledge score" with Cohen's d = 0.56.

> "After adjusting for baseline, the intervention group showed significantly greater improvements in knowledge score (mean change: 12.48 ± 9.98 vs. 6.28 ± 11.55; p = 0.0046, Cohen's d = 0.56, 95% CI: 1.97 to 10.43)"
