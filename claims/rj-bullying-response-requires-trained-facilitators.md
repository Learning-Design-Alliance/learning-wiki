Narrative review, attributed to Molnar-Main (2014) drawing on limited evidence: RJ conferences between bully and victim are potentially useful, but "given the power dynamics involved in bullying," trained adult facilitators are needed and victims may still be uncomfortable.

> "However, given the power dynamics involved in bullying, it is important to have trained adult facilitators involved, and even then the victim may not be comfortable facing the bully due to potential consequences (Amstutz & Mullet, 2005)."
