Moderator analyses from the multivariate meta-regression comparing outcome types and grade levels. The article reports "none of those comparisons yielding significant differences" for outcome type and no significant differences for grade level; no effect sizes printed for these tests.

"The comparison by grade level after dividing the studies' outcomes into those relating to grades K to 2 and those relating to grades 3 to 6 also yielded no significant differences, so we found no evidence that grade level was a significant moderator of effects."
