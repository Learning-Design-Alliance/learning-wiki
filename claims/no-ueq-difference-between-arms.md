Between-group comparison of UEQ scale scores in the quasi-randomized study, using two-tailed t tests. The article reports "no statistically significant difference between the HoloLens and control groups" across all six scales with the printed P values.

> "Statistical analysis using a simple 2-tailed t test showed no statistically significant difference between the HoloLens and control groups for attractiveness, perspicuity, efficiency, dependability, stimulation, and novelty (P=.37, P=.26, P=.57, P=.38, P=.79, and P=.12, respectively)."
