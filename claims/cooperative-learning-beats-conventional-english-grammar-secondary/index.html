Evidence¶

Singaravelu 2010¶

G. Singaravelu. (2010). Impact of Co-operative Learning Strategies in English. i-manager's Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. 4, No. 2, August-October 2010. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102270

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Rotational group experiment: the control group of 40 standard-X students was tested after conventional teaching and again after co-operative learning treatment. The article reports "null hypothesis is rejected at 0.05 levels" for this contrast (Table 4).

"The calculated 't' value is (32.62) greater than table value (1.96). Hence null hypothesis is rejected at 0.05 levels."

Singaravelu 2010 (2)¶

G. Singaravelu. (2010). Impact of Co-operative Learning Strategies in English. i-manager's Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. 4, No. 2, August-October 2010. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102270

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive means from the same rotational group design: the control group's post-test mean rose from 8.68 (conventional) to 19.12 (co-operative learning), reported as descriptive support without an effect size.

"Post-test of control group scored through conventional method is 8.68 and the same group scores more mean 19.12 in treatment of cooperative learning."
