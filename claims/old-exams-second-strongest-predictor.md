## Evidence

### James K. Hirons 2025

James K. Hirons, Jonathan D. Perry, Dawson T. Nodurft, Scott Crawford, William Bassichis, and Tatiana L. Erukhimova. (2025). Click, Watch, Learn: The Impact of Student Self-Study Materials on Physics E&M Course Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.12143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.12143)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of click-tracking usage data matched to course records for 1368 students; old-exam usage had β = 0.221, p < 0.001 in the interaction model, β = 0.262 in the log-transform model, and β = 0.236 in the hybrid model, second only to Calculus I grade in each.
