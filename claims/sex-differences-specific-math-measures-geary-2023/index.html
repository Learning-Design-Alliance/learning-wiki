Evidence¶

Geary David C. 2023¶

Geary David C., Hoard Mary K., Nugent Lara, Ünal Zehra E. (2023). Sex Differences in Developmental Pathways to Mathematical Competence. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000763

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Mean sex differences in the longitudinal study of 342 adolescents (169 boys) assessed from 6th to 9th grade, reported in the Results sex-differences section. The article prints the effect size d = .51 for the Algebra Word problem test, the largest of the four advantages.

"Boys performed better than girls on the Algebra Word problem test (d = .51), Function Mapping measure (d = .28), Equation Memory (d = .23), and Arithmetic Fluency (d = .26), but there were no other sex differences on the mathematics measures (ps > .05)."
