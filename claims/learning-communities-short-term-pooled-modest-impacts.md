Mixed Results from Six Large Randomized Controlled Trials of Learning Communities in Community Colleges. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563290

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Pooled short-term findings from five randomized trials at six colleges operating learning communities with developmental English or math, tracking students for the program semester and two follow-up semesters (Figures 1-2 show cumulative credits for the pooled developmental education sample).

> "On average, these programs produced no impact on persistence, a half-credit impact on credits earned in the targeted subject (English or mathematics), no impact on credits outside that subject, and a half-credit effect on total credits earned."
