## Evidence

### Falcone Veronica 2024

Falcone Veronica, Catic Anja, Heinzl Florian, Steinbauer Philipp, Wagner Michael, Mikula Fanny, Dorittke Tim, Roessler Bernhard, Farr Alex. (2024). Impact of a virtual reality-based simulation training for shoulder dystocia on human and technical skills among caregivers: a randomized-controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-57785-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Nested multivariable linear regression on 61 participants with HELP-RER(2) as dependent variable; the abstract reports VR association at "p = 0.003" and Table 4 lists estimates of −0.506 (study group) and −0.823 (>10 years). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Interestingly, 360°-VR (p = 0.004) and >10 years of expertise (p = 0.03) were independently associated with higher HELP-RER(2) scores (Table 4)."
