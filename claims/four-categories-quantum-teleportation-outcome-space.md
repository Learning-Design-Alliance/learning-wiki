## Evidence

### Sebastian Kilde-Westberg 2026

Sebastian Kilde-Westberg, Andreas Johansson, Anna Pearson, and Jonas Enger. (2026). Making sense of quantum teleportation: An intervention study on students' conceptions using a diagrammatic approach. arXiv:2511.21443 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00594-9](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00594-9)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Phenomenographic analysis of video-recorded group work, written exercise and exam responses, and a group interview identified 'four categories of qualitatively different ways of understanding' quantum teleportation, hierarchically ordered from physical transportation to entanglement-based teleportation (Table I).

> "In particular, we identified four categories of qualitatively different ways of understanding how a complete quantum state can be transferred across space and time using entanglement during the intervention (Table I), i.e., a model of the principle of quantum teleportation"

### Sebastian Kilde-Westberg 2026 (2)

Sebastian Kilde-Westberg, Andreas Johansson, Anna Pearson, and Jonas Enger. (2026). Making sense of quantum teleportation: An intervention study on students' conceptions using a diagrammatic approach. arXiv:2511.21443 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00594-9](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00594-9)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

In lesson-two group work on E2, the highest category 'emerged most clearly when the pre-service teachers were working with E2', while being rarely seen among the upper-secondary school students.

> "Category 4 was rarely seen in the group work session for the upper-secondary school students. Rather, it emerged most clearly when the pre-service teachers were working with E2"
