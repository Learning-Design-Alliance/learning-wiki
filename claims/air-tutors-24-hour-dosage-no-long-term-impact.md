# Among students receiving at least 24 hours of Air Tutors tutoring, no statistically significant long-term effect on statewide math assessments was detected

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Fong 2026

Fong, A., Chen-Gaddini, M., & Huang, M. (2026). An Evaluation of the Air Tutors Program: A 1-Year Follow-Up on Math Achievement. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681370

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

24-hour dosage analysis comparing 404 treatment students who met the 24-hour threshold with 1,070 comparison students on spring 2025 statewide assessments. The article reports the combined effect size was "–0.012, and this difference was not statistically significant (p = .804)"; district-specific estimates were near zero.

> "The combined effect size across District A and District B was –0.012, and this difference was not statistically significant (p = .804; see Table 4 for details)."
