Descriptive survey results on teaching practice numbers. The article reports per-site counts from 60 to 538 with the printed distribution, and separately states "a sufficient number of teaching practices is present at each of n=31 sites". Recruitment occurs via informative meetings (n=22), homepages (n=18), and written letters (n=8).
