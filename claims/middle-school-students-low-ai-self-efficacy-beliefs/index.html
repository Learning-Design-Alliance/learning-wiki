Self-reported belief data from the Williams et al. middle school workshop curriculum emphasizing AI for creativity and dance, delivered online during the pandemic. The commentary reports these percentages as descriptive self-reported beliefs; no inferential statistics are printed.

"A finding that stands out for the authors (and me) is that only 17% of the participants felt they were “smarter” than AI and only a third thought they could “control” AI."
