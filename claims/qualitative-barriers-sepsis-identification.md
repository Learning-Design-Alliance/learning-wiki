`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Directed content analysis and grounded theory of 13 semi-structured interviews with GLOSS regional and country coordinators, conducted July–August 2017. Twenty-four subtopics emerged and were grouped into four framework themes; this barrier theme is one of them.

> "We also inquired about the challenges that healthcare providers faced in their facilities in correctly identifying and managing maternal sepsis. Among the emerging subtopics were: limited availability of resources and poor quality of services, difficulties with health system management, insufficient training, and lack of protocols."
