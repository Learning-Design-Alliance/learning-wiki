Theoretical analysis in the discussion section, derived from the structure of the projectile motion causal network rather than from new data. The article adds that "the same equation can represent different causal situations", citing x = x0 + v0t + ½at2 with differing independent variables across contexts.

> "Horizontally, time and speed have causal influence on distance, but vertically the initial speed is the common cause of distance and time. Therefore, learning to differentiate causal relations for horizontal and vertical motion may be a key step in learning about projectile motion."
