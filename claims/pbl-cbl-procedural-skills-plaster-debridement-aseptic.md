## Evidence

### Xiong Xianghua 2025

Xiong Xianghua, Xu Jiongnan, Luo Mingyan, Niu Dongsheng, Bi Qing, Wang Zhaofu, Zhang Jun. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning combined with case-based learning versus lecture-based learning in orthopedic education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07741-3

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 2 studies (100 students) on plaster immobilization skills. The pooled estimate favored PBL-CBL: "SMD = 2.42, 95% CI: 1.89–2.95, P < 0.0001" with moderate heterogeneity (I2 = 40%).

> "The PBL-CBL group demonstrated significantly better mastery of plaster immobilization skills than the LBL group (SMD = 2.42, 95% CI: 1.89–2.95, P < 0.0001, I2 = 40%)"

### Xiong Xianghua 2025 (2)

Efficacy of problem-based learning combined with case-based learning versus lecture-based learning in orthopedic education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07741-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 2 studies (100 students) on debridement technique proficiency, a dichotomous outcome. Pooled risk ratio favored PBL-CBL: "RR = 1.44, 95% CI: 1.15–1.80, P = 0.001" with no heterogeneity (I2 = 0%); no standardized effect size was printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "The PBL-CBL group demonstrated better debridement technique proficiency than the LBL group (RR = 1.44, 95% CI: 1.15–1.80, P = 0.001, I2 = 0%)"
