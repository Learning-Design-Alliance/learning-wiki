Goodman, B., Linton, F., & Gaimari, R. (2016). Encouraging Student Reflection and Articulation Using a Learning Companion: A Commentary. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 26, 474–488. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0041-4

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The authors' definitional and hypothesis-setting account of deliberative discussion in their collaborative learning studies: statements proposing, continuing, or countering solution steps and explicit agreement or disagreement "are taken as evidence of deliberative discussion."

> "Any statement during student dialogue that proposed a potential solution step, that continued another ’s proposal, or posed an alternative to a postulated solution step, and statements that explicitly agreed or disagreed with someone, are taken as evidence of deliberative discussion."
