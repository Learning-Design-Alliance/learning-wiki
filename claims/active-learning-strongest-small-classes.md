## Evidence

### Freeman S 2014

Freeman S, Eddy SL, McDonough M, Smith MK, Okoroafor N, Jordt H, Wenderoth MP. (2014). Active learning increases student performance in science, engineering, and mathematics. PNAS 111(23):8410-8415. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1319030111

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis of examination-score effect sizes by binned class size (small, medium, large). Variation was significant (Q = 6.726, df = 2, P = 0.035), and the article reports effects were statistically significant in all three size categories.

> "Heterogeneity analyses indicated significant variation in terms of course size, with active learning having the highest impact on courses with 50 or fewer students (Fig. 3 B and Table S1 C; Q = 6.726, df = 2, P = 0.035; Fig. S4 )."
