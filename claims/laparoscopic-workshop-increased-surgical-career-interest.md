## Evidence

### Chen Pin-Chun 2024

Chen Pin-Chun, Yang Po-Wen, Kao Yi-Kai, Chen Chia-Hung, Tsai Chih-Jong, Chen Yi-Chieh, Song Ling-Chiao, Tsai Kai Lung, Wu Richard C., Chen Chih-I. (2024). Laparoscopic training workshop to assess medical students' skill acquisition and interest in surgical careers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05708-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Primary outcome of the workshop study: a 12-item questionnaire with a 0-10 self-rated interest scale completed pre- and post-training by 43 of 44 participants (97% response rate). The article reports the post-workshop "mean score escalated to 9.3". No effect size or test statistic for this change is printed.

> "The post-workshop mean score escalated to 9.3, and the median increased to 10, the maximum value of the scale. The narrowed range of scores, from 7 to 10, underscores a heightened and more uniform interest in surgery as a career choice among the participants"
