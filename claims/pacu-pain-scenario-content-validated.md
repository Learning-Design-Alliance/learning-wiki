`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content-validity stage with eight nurse experts rating 20 items on a 1-4 Likert scale; the second-stage scale analysis yielded "S-CVI/UA; 85 and S-CVI/Ave; 0.98", and the article concludes the scenario content was validated. No effect size is printed.

> "Furthermore, the indices obtained from scale analysis were also adequate (S-CVI/UA; 85 and S-CVI/Ave; 0.98). Accordingly, the content proposed by the scenario was considered validated."
