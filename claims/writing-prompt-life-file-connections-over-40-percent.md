## Evidence

### Christine O'Donnell 2020

Christine O'Donnell, Edward Prather, & Peter Behroozi. (2020). Making Science Personal: Inclusivity-Driven Design for General-Education Courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.10218](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.10218)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the Spring 2019 course, almost all class sessions included a 5-minute index-card writing prompt reflecting on that day's lecture; the dark-matter prompt intentionally asked what students believe in but cannot see. Responses were categorized as scientific or life-file connections.

> "Some responses were scientiﬁc, such as gravity or oxygen, but over 40% of responses connected to "life ﬁles", such as God, souls, or love."
