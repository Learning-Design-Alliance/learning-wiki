## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Seventh-grade students taught with drama for 8 weeks scored significantly higher on Turkish rhetorical skills at posttest than control-group students taught with conventional curriculum activities (p = .001, t = 3.427). [→ Mehmet Nuri Kardaş & Raşit Koç 2017](#mehmet-nuri-kardas-rasit-koc-2017)

## Evidence

### Mehmet Nuri Kardaş & Raşit Koç 2017

Mehmet Nuri Kardaş & Raşit Koç. (2017). Effect of Drama Instruction Method on Students' Turkish Verbal Skills and Speech Anxiety. International Journal of Progressive Education, 13(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131916

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest-posttest experimental model with control group; 18 test and 19 control seventh-grade students, 8 weeks of drama vs. Turkish curriculum activities. Independent samples t-test on TRSS posttest scores: "This numerical difference favoring the test group was found to be statistically significant (p: .001 ˂ 0.05; t: 3.427)."

> "Test group mean score was 89, while control group posttest mean score was 78. This numerical difference favoring the test group was found to be statistically significant (p: .001 ˂ 0.05; t: 3.427)."
