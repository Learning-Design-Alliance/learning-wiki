Authors' report of the school observation project in which twelve students observed the Moon in October and November with a Bresser Messier reflector telescope. The authors state "the overall observational skills of the members increased"; no assessment data or effect size is printed.

> "By conducting the observations the overall observational skills of the members increased as they got acquainted with the various ”observable” geological features of the Moon like mare, mountain ranges, craters, lunar rays, mountainous land forms etc."
