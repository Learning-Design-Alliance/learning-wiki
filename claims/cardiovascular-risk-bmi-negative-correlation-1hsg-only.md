A multifactorial approach of nutritional, intellectual, brain development, cardiovascular risk, socio-economic, demographic and educational variables affecting the scholastic achievement in Chilean students: An eight- year follow-up study. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis (Table 1); the cardiovascular risk factors index correlated r = -0.199 with language and r = -0.182 with mathematics in 1HSG, but these results did not contribute to explain the regression results.

> "With regard to cardiovascular risk factors, negative and significant correlations were found only in the 2010 1HSG for both tests, with the exception of tobacco use for LSA in school-age children from the 2010 1HSG"

### Ivanovic Daniza M. et al 2019 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

> "A negative correlation between the 2009 SIMCE and current nutritional status expressed as Z-BMI was found only for LSA in school-age children from the 2010 1HSG (p = .0019)"
