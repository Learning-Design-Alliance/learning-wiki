## Evidence

### He Qian 2026

He Qian, Chang Guanglei, Su Ning, Yang Yingying, Ma Jun. (2026). Generative Artificial Intelligence Use and Learning Engagement Among Medical Undergraduates: Statistical Indirect and Configurational Associations Involving Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091694

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Necessity analysis in the fsQCA of 498 cases with high learning engagement as the outcome set. GAI use consistency (0.645) was far below the 0.90 threshold, indicating high GAI use was not a necessary condition.

> "The consistency values for competence need satisfaction and GAI use were 0.873 and 0.645, respectively, both below the necessity threshold."

### He Qian 2026 (2)

He Qian, Chang Guanglei, Su Ning, Yang Yingying, Ma Jun. (2026). Generative Artificial Intelligence Use and Learning Engagement Among Medical Undergraduates: Statistical Indirect and Configurational Associations Involving Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091694

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same necessity analysis. Relatedness need satisfaction passed the consistency threshold (0.919) but its RoN of 0.498 indicated limited relevance, so the authors concluded "no single condition was interpreted as a strictly necessary condition for high learning engagement."

> "Relatedness need satisfaction yielded a necessity consistency of 0.919, exceeding the commonly used threshold of 0.90. However, its RoN value was only 0.498, indicating relatively limited relevance as a necessary condition."
