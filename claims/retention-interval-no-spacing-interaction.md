## Subclaims
`q2 i0` There is a significant negative main effect of retention interval on quiz grades, and no significant spacing by retention interval interaction. [→ Paulo F. Carvalho 2020](#paulo-f-carvalho-2020)

## Evidence

### Paulo F. Carvalho 2020

Paulo F. Carvalho, Faria Sana and Veronica X. Yan. (2020). Self-regulated spacing in a massive open online course is related to better learning. npj Science of Learning 5:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0061-1

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

From the same mixed regression on MOOC log data: "shorter retention intervals being associated with higher quiz grades, β = −0.07", while the spacing-by-retention-interval interaction was not significant (β = 0.02, p > 0.20).

> "although there was also a significant main effect of retention interval with shorter retention intervals being associated with higher quiz grades, β = −0.07, SE = 0.01, t(6664.91) = −5.19, p < 0.001, there was not a significant spacing by retention interval effect, β = 0.02, SE = 0.01, t(6512) = 1.28, p > 0.20."
