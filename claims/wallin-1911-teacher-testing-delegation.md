The author's workload argument in the laboratory-functions section: per-child testing time is printed as "from forty-five minutes to an hour", motivating delegation of tests to trained special-room teachers and reservation of the laboratory for the remainder.

> "To apply the Binet-Simon tests thoroughly requires from forty-five minutes to an hour. Hence it would seem best to limit the work in the laboratory to the tests wlu'ch cannot be satisfactorily carried out by the trained special-room teachers."
