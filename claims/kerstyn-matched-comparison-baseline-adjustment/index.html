kerstyn-baseline-match¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2017, July). Primary Mathematics intervention report: I CAN Learn®. Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/676

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental matching procedure in Kerstyn (2002), conducted across 36 middle schools in one Florida district over two school years, with 143 intervention classrooms and 381 comparison classrooms. Despite matching, the analytic sample for the 2001 MJ-3 finding had baseline differences of 0.17 standard deviations; the author's baseline-adjusted analysis was positive and not statistically significant.

"district staff selected comparison classrooms that used traditional instruction and matched them on several factors including: students’ prior achievement, time of day, instructional time, class size, and proportion of minority students."
