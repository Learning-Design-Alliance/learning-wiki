Evidence¶

Jessica Joiner 2017¶

Jessica Joiner, Matthew Piva, Courtney Turrin and Steve W. C. Chang. (2017). Social learning through prediction error in the brain. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0009-2

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review of human neuroimaging findings on advice-taking decisions. The review notes TPJ and STS activations are not specific to ToM tasks but occur "in situations when considering other's information to guide one's actions".

"When participants take into account the advice of another person to make a decision about obtaining potential rewards, dmPFC, middle temporal gyrus (MTG), STS, and TPJ activations signal social prediction error."

Related Claims¶
