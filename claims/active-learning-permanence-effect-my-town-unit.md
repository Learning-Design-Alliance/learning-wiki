## Evidence

### Yenen 2019

Yenen, E. T., & Dursun, F. (2019). The Effect of Active Learning Approach on Student Achievement in Secondary School 5th Grade English Course. Novitas-ROYAL (Research on Youth and Language), 13(2), 175-186. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/novitas

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Permanence test: the same achievement test was re-administered four weeks after the post-test to both groups. Experimental mean was "X =82.81" and control "X =69.90", t(63) = -4.26, p < .05; declines were 2.70% versus 4.91%. No effect size was printed.

> "However, this decrease was 2.70% in the experimental group and 4.91% in the control group. In other words, the difference between the achievement test scores between the experimental ( X =82.81) and control ( X =69.90) group was further increased. This difference was also significant with respect to t -test results [t (63) = - 4.26, p <.05]."
