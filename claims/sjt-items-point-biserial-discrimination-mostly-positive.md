`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item-discrimination analysis of the pilot SJT in the same 303-respondent sample, using corrected point-biserial correlations that remove the index item from the total score. "most of the item (90%)" were positive and 72% reached at least 0.10. A few items showed negative correlations.

> "the corrected correlation between performance on each specific item and overall test performance show that most of the item (90%) have a positive correlation, while 72% of the items have a point-biserial correlation of at least 0.10."
