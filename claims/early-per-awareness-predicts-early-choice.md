## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Whether students knew of PER before graduate school significantly predicts when they chose to pursue it, with a medium effect size of 0.678. [→ Ben Van Dusen 2014](#ben-van-dusen-2014)

## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen 2014

Ben Van Dusen, Ramón S. Barthelemy, and Charles Henderson. (2014). Educational trajectories of graduate students in physics education research. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 10, 020106. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.10.020106

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Chi-squared analysis of survey trajectory paths (Table I): 81% of those who knew of PER before graduate school chose it when applying, versus 72% of those who learned of it in graduate school choosing during graduate school. The printed effect size is 0.678.

> "Differences in when people who knew of PER prior to graduate school, or not, chose to engage in PER (represented by the second and third rows of Fig. 1) were statistically significant ( p< 0.001) and have a medium effect size of 0.678."
