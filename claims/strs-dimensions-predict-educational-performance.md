### Austerberry 2026

Austerberry, C., Corfield, E. C., Havdahl, A., Smajlagic, D., Brandlistuen, R. E., Vaage Wang, M., Fish, L., Bernardi, M., van de Grint‐Stoop, J., Bekkhus, M., & Fearon, P. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path b coefficients from the three SEMs in the MoBa cohort. All three relationship dimensions were significantly associated with mother-rated reading and math performance in the expected direction.

> "higher ratings of student–teacher closeness at age 5 were significantly directly associated with higher educational performance at 6–8 years (b: β = 0.07, 95% CI [0.03, 0.11], p = .001), as were lower levels of student–teacher conflict (b: β = −0.14, 95% CI [−0.18, −0.10], p < .001), and more positive teacher responses to students (b: β = 0.18, 95% CI [0.13, 0.22], p < .001)"
