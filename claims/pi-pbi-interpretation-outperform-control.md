# All four instructional groups (PI and PBI, with and without EI) outperformed the control group on the aural interpretation task at immediate and delayed posttests

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` On the aural interpretation task, a repeated measures ANOVA showed a significant Group × Test interaction (η2=0.15), with all instructional groups outperforming the control group at both posttests. [→ Adem Soruç 2017](#adem-soruc-2017)

## Evidence

### Adem Soruç 2017

Adem Soruç, Jingjing Qin, YouJin Kim. (2017). Comparing the Effectiveness of Processing Instruction and Production-Based Instruction on L2 Grammar Learning: The Role of Explicit Information. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(2), 49–70. http://dx.doi.org/1018806/tesl.v34i2.1262

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

A 5 × 3 factorial repeated measures ANOVA on aural interpretation scores of 144 Turkish EFL elementary learners across five groups and three test times. The article reports "a significant Group × Test interaction, F (8,144) =6.35, p <0.001, η2=0.15" and main effects for Test (η2=0.40) and Group (η2=0.27).

> "It showed a main effect for the within-subjects variable Test, F (2,144) =91.07, p <0.001, η2=0.40; a main effect for the between-subjects variable Group, F (4,144) =12.98, p <0.001, η2=0.27; and a significant Group × Test interaction, F (8,144) =6.35, p <0.001, η2=0.15."
