Cross-sectional survey across all Jordanian medical schools during March–April 2020; 530 sixth-year students who had completed a radiology rotation answered seven X-rays via an electronic questionnaire. The article reports "We included a total of 530 participants in this study" with balanced gender distribution.

> "We included a total of 530 participants in this study, 265 (50%) were men and 265 (50%) women, with a mean age of 23.23 (± 1.41) years."
