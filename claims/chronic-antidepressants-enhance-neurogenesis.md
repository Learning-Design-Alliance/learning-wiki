Katherine L. Jones, Mei Zhou and Dhanisha J. Jhaveri. (2022). Dissecting the role of adult hippocampal neurogenesis towards resilience versus susceptibility to stress-related mood disorders. npj Science of Learning 7:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00133-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review citing rodent studies of tranylcypromine, fluoxetine, reboxetine and electroconvulsive therapy, which the review reports increased proliferation and enhanced neurogenesis. The replicated finding is the "requirement of chronic but not acute administration".

> "These findings were replicated in several subsequent studies which highlighted the requirement of chronic but not acute administration of these clinical antidepressants to produce the neurogenic effect 56–59."
