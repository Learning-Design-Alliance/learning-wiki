### Flashlight360 program description 2024

Retrospective quasi-experimental study with grade 1-12 ELL students in a suburban district, using propensity-score matching with over 4,000 one-year treatment students. The program page reports "one-year treatment students outgained matched comparison students by more than 4 points" on WIDA ACCESS composite scores, effect size = +0.10.

> "Over 4,000 students using Flashlight360 for one year and approximately 300 students using Flashlight360 for two years were propensity-score matched with similar students not exposed to the program."
