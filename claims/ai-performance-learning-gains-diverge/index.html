Lixiang Yan and Dragan Gašević. (2026). Agentivism: a learning theory for the age of artificial intelligence. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07813 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.07813

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article's synthesis of emerging empirical research on generative AI in writing, reported in its introduction. It states that "learners can produce stronger essays while revising less independently and gaining no corresponding advantage in underlying knowledge, a pattern described as metacognitive laziness". No effect sizes are printed in the quoted passage.

Lixiang Yan and Dragan Gašević 2026 (2)¶

Lixiang Yan and Dragan Gašević. (2026). Agentivism: a learning theory for the age of artificial intelligence. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07813

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article reports, citing prior work on scientific inquiry with ChatGPT, that "Students using ChatGPT in inquiry tasks may report lower cognitive load while producing less sophisticated reasoning". The quoted passage prints no test statistics or effect sizes.

"Students using ChatGPT in inquiry tasks may report lower cognitive load while producing less sophisticated reasoning"
