## Evidence

### Jaebong Yoo & Jihie Kim 2014

Jaebong Yoo & Jihie Kim. (2014). Can Online Discussion Participation Predict Group Project Performance? Investigating the Roles of Linguistic Features and Participation Patterns. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0010-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Regression analysis with grade as dependent variable over N = 173 groups; Table 7 reports APTTD correlating r = .21 with grade. A two-group illustration found Group A (final APTTD 0.86) received a higher grade than Group B (0.37), 0.93 vs. 0.72.

> "APTTD has the second largest regression coefficient, B = .20 ( p < .001). This is consistent with recent findings from other researchers (Michinov et al. 2011) that high procrastinators tend to get lower grades."
