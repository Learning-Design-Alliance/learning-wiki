## Evidence

### Khumsikiew et al. 2015

Jeerisuda Khumsikiew, Sisira Donsamak, and Manit Saeteaw. (2015). A Model of Small-Group Problem-Based Learning In Pharmacy Education: Teaching in the Clinical Environment. The IAFOR Journal of Education, Volume III - Issue II - Summer 2015. https://iafor.org/journals

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with 36 fifth-year pharmacy students; paired t-tests compared before/after self-assessment ratings on a 17-item questionnaire (Cronbach's Alpha 0.96). Mean differences per item ranged from 1.06 to 1.41, all with "P value" < 0.05; no effect size was printed.
