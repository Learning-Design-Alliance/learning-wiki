Chow Tak Sang 2026¶

Chow Tak Sang, To Ken, Lam Bess Yin Hung, Lau Kung Wong. (2026). A Multidimensional Analysis of AI Literacy Determinants: External Resources, Digital Skills, and Psychological Profile Among University Students. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16081448

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Subdimensional regression models (all five significant, explained variance 0.26–0.32) on the 303-student sample. The article states these findings "challenge any straightforward reading of digital competence as a general technical foundation", suggesting its contribution operates through judgement and application rather than operational facility.

"Digital competence predicted critical evaluation, creative application, and ethical competence, but not technical proficiency or communication proficiency. Perceived resources predicted only communication proficiency (β = 0.22, p < 0.001) and creative application (β = 0.17, p < 0.05)."
