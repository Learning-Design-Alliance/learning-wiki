## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Song Xiuli 2024

Song Xiuli, Hu Qian. (2024). The relationship between Freshman students' mental health and academic achievement: chain mediating effect of learning adaptation and academic self-efficacy. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-20738-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test with 5000 resamples and 95% confidence intervals on the same 3871-freshman dataset. The chain path is significant "with a magnitude of -0.016, accounting for 40.57% of the total effect"; total effect was -0.039.

> "The confidence interval of “mental health → learning adaptation → academic self-efficacy → academic achievement” does not include 0, and the mediating path is significant, with a magnitude of -0.016, accounting for 40.57% of the total effect."
