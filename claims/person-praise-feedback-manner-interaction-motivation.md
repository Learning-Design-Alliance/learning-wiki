# Person praise and feedback manner significantly interact on task motivation, such that praise raises trials under ego-involving feedback and lowers them under task-involving feedback

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The person praise × feedback manner interaction on number of trials attempted is significant, b = 2.59, p <.001, ΔR² = 0.11. [→ Cheng-Hong Liu 2026](#cheng-hong-liu-2026)
`q3 i?` Under task-involving feedback, students receiving person praise attempted fewer items than those not receiving praise (b = −1.8, p <.001); under ego-involving feedback, praise led to more items (b = 0.78, p =.051, marginal). [→ Cheng-Hong Liu 2026 (2)](#cheng-hong-liu-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Cheng-Hong Liu 2026

Cheng-Hong Liu, Xian-Rui Yin. (2026). Effects of person praise on students' motivation and performance under ego-involving and task-involving feedback conditions. Instructional Science (2026) 54:34. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09789-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OLS regression within a PROCESS Model 7 moderated mediation analysis of the 2×2 experiment (N = 146) found the interaction on trials significant: "b = 2.59, t = 4.57, p <.001, ∆R2 = 0.11", with the interaction term adding 11% to the model's variance explained; the article prints no standardized effect size for the interaction.

> "More importantly, the PP × FM interaction was significant, b = 2.59, t = 4.57, p <.001, ∆R2 = 0.11."

### Cheng-Hong Liu 2026 (2)

Cheng-Hong Liu, Xian-Rui Yin. (2026). Effects of person praise on students' motivation and performance under ego-involving and task-involving feedback conditions. Instructional Science (2026) 54:34. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09789-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple slopes from the same regression-based analysis of 146 randomly assigned junior high students. Ego-involving praise slope: "b = 0.78, t = 1.97, p =.051" (marginal); task-involving praise slope: "b = −1.8, t = −4.47, p <.001".

> "Simple slopes analysis showed that for participants under the ego-involving feedback condition, receiving person praise led them to answer more items relative to not receiving praise, b = 0.78, t = 1.97, p =.051."
