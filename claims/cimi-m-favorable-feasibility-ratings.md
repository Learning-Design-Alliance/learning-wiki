### de Goede 2026

de Goede, M. L., Boelen, P. A., Litz, B. T., & ter Heide, F. J. J. (2026). Mapping moral injury: Development and feasibility of the Clinical Interview for Moral Injury—military version (CIMI-M). PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0359553

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Descriptive statistics from a feasibility study at two Dutch tertiary trauma centers with eight veterans who completed the Debriefing Instrument for Patients and a 10-point overall rating. The study reports "an average rating of M = 7.63 (SD = 1.16, range 5–9)"; no effect sizes or inferential tests are printed.

> "Veterans rated the CIMI-M favorably, with an average rating of M = 7.63 (SD = 1.16, range 5–9)."

### de Goede 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Descriptive statistics from the same feasibility study, from the Debriefing Instrument for Clinicians completed by seven clinicians, two of whom conducted the interview twice. The article prints "an average rating of M = 8.41 (SD = 0.38, range 7.50–8.50)" with no inferential statistics.

> "Clinicians rated the CIMI-M very positively, with an average rating of M = 8.41 (SD = 0.38, range 7.50–8.50)."
