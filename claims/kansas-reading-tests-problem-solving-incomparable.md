`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Editorial critique of the Kansas Reading Tests, which the author says went to the extreme of introducing arithmetical, physical and practical problems as comprehension indicators; no empirical data are reported.

> "The solution of these problems undoubtedly depends on the comprehension of what is read, but the mental processes in- volved are so much more elaborate than those brought into play in the grasping of ordinary reading material that the two activities are scarcely comparable."
