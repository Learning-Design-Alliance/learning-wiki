## Evidence

### Li You 2023

Li You, Cao Liang, Zhang Huixia, Pang Weiyi, Sun Yan, Zhang Zhiyong. (2023). Application of flipped classroom combined with case-based learning in Introduction to Environmental Health Science. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2023.1264843

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-course anonymous questionnaire of the 22 experimental-group students (22 of 22 returned, 100% recovery). Across nine satisfaction items, "dissatisfied or lower" responses were nearly absent; only one student reported dissatisfaction with classroom interaction.

> "The total satisfaction of students with the teaching form, teaching content, and class hours, classroom atmosphere, academic burden, evaluation system, and learning guidance method, autonomous learning ability, learning enthusiasm, and participation in interaction adopted by the Introduction to Environmental Health Science exceeded 85%"
