`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review describes the CONIC model as a construct-specific integrative theory, citing empirical studies supporting it. Interest functions as a strong "pull" factor while conscientiousness serves as an internal "push" factor driving persistence "even in the absence of intrinsic value". No effect sizes are printed in the quoted text.

> "the Conscientiousness × Interest Compensation (CONIC) model proposes that conscientiousness and interest can, to some extent, compensate for one another in explaining academic effort and achievement (Trautwein et al., 2019; for empirical studies supporting the model, see e.g., Bareis et al., 2024; Song et al., 2020)."
