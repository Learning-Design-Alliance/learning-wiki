### Al Shammari Malak 2021

Al Shammari Malak, Hassan Ali, AlShamlan Nouf, Alotaibi Sarah, Bamashmoos Manar, Hakami Amani, Althunyan Abdullatif, Basager Shymaa, Motabgani Sameerah, Aljubran Sawsan, Alsaif Hind S. (2021). `q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation computed in the 205-physician survey study between self-rated confidence (5-point scale) and overall accuracy score; the article reports "ρ = 0.39; P < 0.001".

> "There was a significant positive correlation (ρ = 0.39; P < 0.001) between the diagnostic confidence in the interpretation and the overall score attained by the physicians."
