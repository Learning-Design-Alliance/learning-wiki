## Evidence

### Jorge-Soto Cristina 2019

Jorge-Soto Cristina, Abilleira-González Maite, Otero-Agra Martin, Barcala-Furelos Roberto, Abelairas-Gómez Cristian, Szarpak Łukasz, Rodríguez-Núñez Antonio. (2019). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Student t-test comparison of CPR quality metrics by sex in the 47-participant practical test (29 men, 18 women per Table 2), using Laerdal PC Skill Reporting Software against 2015 ERC quality standards. Both depth means fell within the 50–60 mm goal; no effect size is printed.

> "Mean compression depth goal (50–60 mm) was achieved by both groups with deeper CC performed by men (56.1 ± 4.03 mm vs. 52.17 ± 5.51 mm, p = 0.007). Full chest recoil was better in women (72.2 ± 32.8%) than in men (45.4 ± 32.9%, p = 0.009)."
