`q3 i?` Indirect paths of TSRQ on anxiety via self-concept (β = −0.129) and value appraisals (β = −0.127) were significant while the direct effect was not, consistent with full mediation. [→ Wu Qiong 2026](#wu-qiong-2026)

## Evidence

### Wu Qiong 2026

Wu Qiong. (2026). The impact of teacher-student relationship quality on achievement emotions in English as a foreign language within a Confucian heritage cultural context: an examination of the mediating roles of cognitive appraisals. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04561-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (5000 resamples, bias-corrected 95% CIs) within the LSEM of 1103 Chinese college EFL learners. The indirect paths were significant while "the direct effect of TSRQ on anxiety was not significant (β = − 0.056, p = .166)".

> "For anxiety, both the indirect paths via EFL-related self-concept (β = − 0.129, p < .001, 95% C.I. [-0.170, − 0.095]) and via value appraisals (β = − 0.127, p < .001, 95% C.I. [-0.175, − 0.085]) were significant. In contrast, the direct effect of TSRQ on anxiety was not significant (β = − 0.056, p = .166)."
