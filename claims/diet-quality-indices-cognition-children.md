## Evidence

### Naveed Sehrish 2020

Naveed Sehrish, Lakka Timo, Haapala Eero A. (2020). An Overview on the Associations between Health Behaviors and Brain Health in Children and Adolescents with Special Reference to Diet Quality. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17030953

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reporting observational studies: the review states a "higher BSDS" and better DASH adherence "have been linked to better cognition in children and particularly in boys aged 6–8". No effect sizes printed.

> "A higher BSDS reflecting a better adherence to healthy diet in the Nordic countries and the better adherence to the DASH diet have been linked to better cognition in children and particularly in boys aged 6–8 [143]."

### Naveed Sehrish 2020 (2)

Naveed Sehrish, Lakka Timo, Haapala Eero A. (2020). An Overview on the Associations between Health Behaviors and Brain Health in Children and Adolescents with Special Reference to Diet Quality. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17030953

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review reports a Finnish observational study in which BSDS and FCHEI in Grade 1 related to better reading skills in Grades 1–3, while "a Mediterranean Diet Score" was "not associated with academic performance". No effect sizes printed.

> "Recently, a healthier diet assessed by the BSDS and the FCHEI in Grade 1 was related to better reading skills in Grades 1–3 in Finnish children [148]. However, a Mediterranean Diet Score reflecting Mediterranean style diet was not associated with academic performance [148]."
