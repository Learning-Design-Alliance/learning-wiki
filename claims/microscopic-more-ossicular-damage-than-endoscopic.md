## Evidence

### Anschuetz Lukas 2019

Anschuetz Lukas, Stricker Daniel, Yacoub Abraam, Wimmer Wilhelm, Caversaccio Marco, Huwendiek Sören. (2019). Acquisition of basic ear surgery skills: a randomized comparison between endoscopic and microscopic techniques. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1803-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA in a randomized crossover laboratory study of 33 participants performing standardized grasping and dissection tasks in a temporal bone model, comparing techniques within subjects. The article reports "a significant higher number of damages to the ossicular chain" with the microscopic technique, ηp2 = 0.350.

> "However, a significant higher number of damages to the ossicular chain was observed with the microscopic as compared to the endoscopic technique (F1, 27 = 14.560, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.350)."
