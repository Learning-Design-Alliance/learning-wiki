## Evidence

### Raafat Gabriel 2009

Raafat Gabriel. (2009). The Efficacy of Explicit Grammar Instruction and its Impact on L2 Rule-Learning: A literature review. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED509345

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Radwan (2005), a study of 42 randomly selected EFL participants in two Washington universities assigned to four conditions (textual enhancement, rule-oriented, content-oriented, control) with pre-tests, two post-tests, and think-aloud verbal protocols. The "rule-oriented group whose degree of explicitness is high outperformed other groups".

> "rule-oriented group whose degree of explicitness is high outperformed other groups of implicit instruction or no instruction at all"
