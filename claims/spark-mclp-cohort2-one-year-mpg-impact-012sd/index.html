Evidence¶

Jones et al. 2015¶

Curtis J. Jones, Rachel Lander, Rachel Westrum. (2015). The Interim Results of a Randomized Control Trial of the SPARK Early Literacy Program/Milwaukee Community Literacy Project. Presented at the Annual Meeting of the Society for Research in Educational Effectiveness, Washington, DC. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED558151

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Interim analysis of Cohort 2 (576 consented students; 286 MCLP, 290 control) using fall 2013 baseline and spring 2014 MPG results. Table 4 reports a pooled standardized effect size of 0.12 standard deviations, robust SE 0.0482, p < .01 — small, below d = 0.2; the first-grade effect (0.177, p = .006) drove the pooled result while second grade was non-significant (-0.047, p = .664).

"However, after only one year of participation, the MCLP was found to have a similar impact on the MAP/MPG scores of the second cohort (0.12 standard deviations) (Table 4)."
