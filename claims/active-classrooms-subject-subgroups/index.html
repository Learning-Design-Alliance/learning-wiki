Bedard Chloe 2019¶

Bedard Chloe, St John Laura, Bremer Emily, Graham Jeffrey D., Cairney John. (2019). A systematic review and meta-analysis on the effects of physically active classrooms on educational and enjoyment outcomes in school age children. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0218633

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Subject-level subgroup analyses (Table 2): math 11 studies, n = 526, SMD = 0.08 (-0.09 to 0.26); reading 6 studies, n = 401, SMD = 0.04 (-0.16 to 0.24); language 2 studies, n = 43, SMD = 1.07 (0.42 to 1.72). Statistical tests of subgroup differences by subject could not be performed.

"Pooling nine studies shows a small increase in math scores of active classrooms (SMD = 0.08) however the 95% CI includes small possible harm and no effect. Pooled effects of six studies show similarly small and imprecise improvement in reading performance."

Related Claims¶
