## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In introductory physics labs students are almost never engaged in experiment design and rarely consider how uncertainty varies during an experiment and affects measurement quality. [→ Pilakouta 2011](#pilakouta-2011)

## Evidence

### Pilakouta 2011

Pilakouta, M. (2011). Statistical uncertainty in educational experiment on the attenuation of gamma radiation. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1111.4056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1111.4056)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The author's contextual observation motivating the study: students "rarely care about how uncertainty may vary during the experiment and how this affects the quality of their measurements". In most preparatory experiments the range and step of measurements is set by the teacher, or many measurements are available so quality considerations never arise.

> "In an introductory physics lab, s tudents are almost never engaged to the design of the experiment. So in practice they have only a theoretical view of the limitations of the experimental setup and the way these limitations affect the measurement s uncertainty."
