Nikendei Christoph, Diefenbacher Katja, Köhl-Hackert Nadja, Lauber Heike, Huber Julia, Herrmann-Werner Anne, Herzog Wolfgang, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Jünger Jana, Krautter Markus. (2015). `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme J of the thematic analysis of interviews with 4 SPs before their first assignment. The majority answered they had no concerns and felt well prepared; one SP was slightly afraid of forgetting something, and "in isolated cases there was a fear that the students would not be able to respect the SPs’ privacy".
