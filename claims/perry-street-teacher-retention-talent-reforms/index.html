Evan 2019¶

Evan, A. (2019). From the brink of closure: Key factors in one charter school's successful turnaround. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://dcpcsb.egnyte.com/dl/hdsUO9pDbh/

Case-study evidence from the talent section: pre-turnaround over 50 percent of staff left in 2013–14; after reforms including a DC-market-aligned salary scale and December-start recruitment, the school "retained over 90 percent of its effective teachers."

"Between 2017–18 to 2018–19, for example, Perry Street retained over 90 percent of its effective teachers."
