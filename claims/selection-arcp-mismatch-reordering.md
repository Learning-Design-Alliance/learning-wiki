## Evidence

### Tiffin Paul A 2018

Tiffin Paul A, Orr James, Paton Lewis W, Smith Daniel T, Norcini John J. (2018). UK nationals who received their medical degrees abroad: selection into, and subsequent performance in postgraduate training: a national data linkage study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023060

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Authors' interpretation (type e) comparing the recruitment modelling (table 5) with the ARCP modelling (tables 6–7) in the same national dataset. UK OGs received higher mean interview z-scores than non-UK IMGs, and controlling for interview restacked groups into an order more consistent with ARCP performance.

> "The patterns observed in the selection data were not precisely replicated, with a ‘re-ordering’ of UK and non-UK overseas medical graduates. In the present case, the dissonance between the selection and ARCP results could be largely, if not wholly, explained by the differential interview performances between UK and non-UK IMGs."
