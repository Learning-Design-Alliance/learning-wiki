## Evidence

### Fan Jun-Yu 2020

Fan Jun-Yu, Tseng Ying-Jung, Chao Li-Fen, Chen Shiah-Lian, Jane Sui-Whi. (2020). Learning outcomes of a flipped classroom teaching approach in an adult-health nursing course: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02240-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

GEE analysis of MINS self-awareness pre/post scores in the quasi-experiment; the group-by-time interaction was B = -0.18, p < 0.001, favoring the control group. The authors suggest control students "recognized their limitations" and adjusted their learning strategies.

> "However, the results regarding self-awareness were the opposite; the CG showed greater improvement in self-awareness from the pre-test to the post-test (B = - 0.18, p < 0.001)."
