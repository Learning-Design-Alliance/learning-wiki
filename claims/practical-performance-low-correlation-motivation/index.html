Marshall A. Baker & J. Shane Robinson 2017¶

Marshall A. Baker & J. Shane Robinson. (2017). The Effects of an Experiential Approach to Learning on Student Motivation. Journal of Agricultural Education, 58(3), 150-167. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2017.03150

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Within the same MCA analysis of the 87-student experiment, the practical assessment (designing, building, and testing a wind blade for voltage output) correlated significantly but low with motivation: "r = .22, p = .02". The semipartial contribution was .18 (p = .11) controlling for creative and analytical measures.
