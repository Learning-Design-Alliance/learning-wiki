## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Cisgender men had greater explicit warmth bias than cisgender women (median 90 vs 100; p = 0.001, r = 0.14) and stronger implicit anti-Indigenous associations (median -0.31 ms vs -0.19 ms; p < 0.001, r = 0.13). [→ Roach Pamela 2022](#roach-pamela-2022)
