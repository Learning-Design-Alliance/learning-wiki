The editorial reports, citing prior studies by the author's group, that examination of educational journals found intervention and experimental research "becoming increasingly scarce" while prescriptive recommendations from nonexperimental research increase. No effect sizes are printed.

> "My colleagues and I have examined volume after volume of educational journals and concluded that intervention and experimental research are becoming increasingly scarce (Hsieh et al . 2005), and the rate at which authors are making prescriptive recommendations based on observational, nonexperimental research is increasing (Robinson et al . 2007)."
