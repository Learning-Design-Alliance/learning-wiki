## Evidence

### Creutzfeldt Johan 2012

Creutzfeldt Johan, Hedman Leif, Felländer-Tsai Li. (2012). Effects of pre-training using serious game technology on CPR performance – an exploratory quasi-experimental transfer study. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-20-79

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory quasi-experimental controlled study with three groups of pre-clinical medical students (n = 8, 12, 10). The 6 months MVW pre-trained group scored "93 (±11)% compared to 65 (±28)%" on CPR-specific knowledge versus controls (p < 0.05); the 18 months group scored in between.

> "The 6 months group displayed greater CPR-related knowledge than the control group, 93 (±11)% compared to 65 (±28)% (p < 0.05), the 18 months group scored in between (73 (±23)%)."
