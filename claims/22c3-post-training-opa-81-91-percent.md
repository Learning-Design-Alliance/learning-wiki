## Evidence

### Jasani Bharat 2020

Jasani Bharat, Bänfer Gudrun, Fish Rebecca, Waelput Wim, Sucaet Yves, Barker Craig, Whiteley Jessica L., Walker Jill, Hovelinck Rudy, Diezko Rolf. (2020). Evaluation of an online training tool for scoring programmed cell death ligand-1 (PD-L1) diagnostic tests for lung cancer. Diagnostic Pathology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13000-020-00953-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Competence-test scoring by 15 pathologists on digital images of 22C3-stained NSCLC samples, compared with expert consensus reference scores. The article reports mean OPA "was 81% (95% CI: 76–85%)" at ≥50% and 91% (95% CI: 87–95%) at ≥1%, "with best agreement at a ≥ 1% TC cut-off".

> "After training using the Dako PD-L1 IHC PharmDx 22C3 assay stained slides, mean OPA was 81% (95% CI: 76–85%) at the ≥50% TC cut-off and 91% (95% CI: 87–95%) at the ≥1% TC cut-off, with best agreement at a ≥ 1% TC cut-off (n = 15; Fig. 1b)."
