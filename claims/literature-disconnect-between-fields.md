The article reports a citation search showing the disconnect between fields: of over a thousand citations to the seminal generation-effect paper, only one examines guided notes, and additional guided-notes papers mentioning the effect do not cite the foundational paper. A Google Scholar search reportedly found no response-card papers mentioning retrieval practice or vice versa.
