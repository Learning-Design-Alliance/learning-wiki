## Evidence

### Hofer 2024

Hofer, S. I., Heine, J.-H., Besharati, S., Yip, J. C., Reinhold, F., & Brummelman, E. (2024). Self-perceptions as mechanisms of achievement inequality: evidence across 70 countries. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00211-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Meta-analytic metaregressions across 57 countries (country-level indices available for 57 of 70). Social mobility predicted the competency a-path (b = 0.045), b-path (b = 5.647), and a×b-path (b = 1.952); Fig. 4 plots the a×b prediction.

> "the social mobility index signiﬁcantly predicted the indirect effects of SES on reading achievement via self-perceived competency ( b = 1.952; Fig. 4) and sense of belonging ( b = −0.227)."
