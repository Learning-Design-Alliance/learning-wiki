## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across all four path models, whether the robotic tutor was physically present (condition B) or only virtual (condition A) showed no significant relation to students' task performance. [→ Ackermann 2025](#ackermann-2025)

## Evidence

### Ackermann 2025

Ackermann, H., Henke, A., Chevalère, J., Yun, H. S., Hafner, V. V., Pinkwart, N., & Lazarides, R. (2025). Physical embodiment and anthropomorphism of AI tutors and their role in student enjoyment and performance. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00293-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Path-model analysis of data from 56 students randomly assigned to condition A (virtual avatar only) or condition B (additional physical Pepper robot), with task performance measured as concept-map progress in Betty's Brain. The article reports no significant relation, and no effect size for this contrast is printed.

> "Also, all path models consistently showed no signiﬁcant relation between the tutor's physical presence and students' task performance (H1c)."
