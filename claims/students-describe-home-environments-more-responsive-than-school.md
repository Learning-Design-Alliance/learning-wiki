# Students in the focus group and interviews described home learning environments during the pandemic as, for some, more responsive to their needs than school environments

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mosley 2022

Mosley, J., & Valdez, A. (2022). Learning from young people about how safe and supportive conditions at home can inform school climate. Center to Improve Social and Emotional Learning and School Safety at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED621228

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

A qualitative virtual focus group with five students spanning elementary, middle, and high school, plus informal interviews with nine students, elicited reflections in which "their home environments have been more responsive than their school environments to their needs as learners." No effect sizes are reported; findings are student testimony.

> "CISELSS staff invited students to share their honest reflections about how they experienced learning from home during the pandemic, including how their home environments have been more responsive than their school environments to their needs as learners."
