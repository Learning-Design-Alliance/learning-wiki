## Evidence

### Lim Jaeseo 2022

Lim Jaeseo, Ko Hyunwoong, Park Jooyong, Ihm Jungjoon. (2022). Effect of active learning and online discussions on the academic performances of dental students. BMC Medical Education, 22, 312. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03377-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A six-item 7-point Likert prior-knowledge survey administered before the online experiment. Relevant topic items scored low on a 7-point scale (criminal procedure code M = 1.98, SD = 1.06; accusation and charge M = 2.45), and the article reports no between-group differences, supporting baseline comparability.

> "However, no background knowledge differences were found between the four groups. Thus, the students had little background knowledge related to the learning topic, and little difference was noted in their prior knowledge level."
