λ-sweep ablation across six real-world domains (30 trials per condition, Bonferroni correction), reported in Section 5.3 and Table 3. The article reports the λ = 0 difference from random as statistically significant (p < 0.001) with "mean SI = 0.329"; no standardized effect size is printed for this contrast.
