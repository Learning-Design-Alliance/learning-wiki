Nick Huntington-Klein 2026¶

Nick Huntington-Klein. (2026). Little Impact of ChatGPT on High School Test Scores. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08812

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Interaction of the in-session indicator with area college student share, scaled from the 10th to 90th percentile. The fall interaction is "0.122 (0.040)***"; the spring interaction is not distinguishable from zero.

"The interaction coefficient is then 0.122 (0.040)* in the fall window against a main effect of 0.104 (0.047)."

Nick Huntington-Klein 2026 (2)¶

Nick Huntington-Klein. (2026). Little Impact of ChatGPT on High School Test Scores. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08812

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Descriptive decomposition of the summer-dip measure by college student share across metropolitan areas; the article reports "10.0 percent of the cross-area variance" and adds a residualized-treatment robustness check.

"Linearly predicting summer-dip measures with college student share shows that college share accounts for 10.0 percent of the cross-area variance in the dip."
