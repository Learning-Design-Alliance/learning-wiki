# School-level ICT policy factors (ICT plan, support, training) are significantly associated with teachers' classroom use of ICT in Flemish primary schools

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Variables at both the school and teacher level significantly influence teachers' use of ICT in their class, including perceptions of internal ICT support, perceptions of the ICT plan content, and the number of in-service trainings. [→ Tondeur 2008](#tondeur-2008)

## Evidence

### Tondeur 2008

Tondeur, J., van Keer, H., van Braak, J., & Valcke, M. (2008). ICT integration in the classroom: Challenging the potential of a school policy. Computers & Education, 51, 212-223. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2007.05.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel analysis (IGLS in MlwiN) of 574 teachers nested in 53 Flemish primary schools; the final model retained significant predictors including perception of internal ICT support, perception of ICT plan content, and number of in-service trainings. The article reports these as "significant positive effect" predictors; no standardised effect size appears in the quoted passage, so none is coded.

> "As the ﬁxed parameters in the ﬁnal model in Table 4 reveal, variables at both the school and teacher level signiﬁcantly inﬂuence teachers’ use of ICT in their class."
