Evidence¶

Huib K. Tabbers 2010¶

Huib K. Tabbers, Bastiaan de Koeijer. (2010). Learner control in animated multimedia instructions. Instructional Science, 38, 441–453. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-009-9119-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Log-file analysis of the learner control group (n = 26) showed every participant exceeded the 210.6 s system-paced duration; mean time-on-task was 359.7 s (SD 122.3), with large variance up to 717.2 s.

"When looking at time-on-task, the results show that all participants in the learner control condition had spent more time on the multimedia lesson than the 210.6 s in the condition without learner control (t(25) = 6.22, p< .001)."
