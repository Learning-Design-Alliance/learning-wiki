Rotated component matrix (Figure 7b) from PCA of AMI galaxy responses shows sA218 loads 0.431 and sA225 0.368 on the centralization factor, so students endorsing centralization misconceptions also say there is only one or a few galaxies. Loadings are descriptive, not effect sizes.

> "We note that from Figure 7(b), sA218, the misconception that there is only one galaxy in the universe, has a loading of 0.431 on the second factor of galaxy statements, pertaining to centralization."
