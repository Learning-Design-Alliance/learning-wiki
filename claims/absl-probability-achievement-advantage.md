## Evidence

### Ömer Sinan Can 2016

Ömer Sinan Can, Tevfik İşleyen. (2016). Teaching Probability to Pre-Service Teachers with Argumentation Based Science Learning Approach. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.33. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1122873

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 41 third-year pre-service teachers (experimental n=20, control n=21) in a public Turkish university; Independent Samples t-Test on post-test scores after five weeks of instruction, with Levene's test showing variances were not statistically different (p>.050). No effect size printed.
