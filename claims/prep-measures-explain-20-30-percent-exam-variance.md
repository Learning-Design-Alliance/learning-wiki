`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression models predicting final exam scores using only the two incoming preparation measures (Table III). The article reports "almost one third of the overall variance"; Table III prints adjusted R-squared values of 0.18, 0.34, and 0.27 with significant CI and math coefficients. R-squared is variance explained, not a standardized effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).
