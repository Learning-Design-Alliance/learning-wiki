Evidence¶

Jason Willis 2020¶

Jason Willis, Sara Menlove Doutre, Kelsey Krausen, Tyson Barrett, Tye Ripma, and Ruthie Caparas. (2020). Study of California's Special Education Funding System: Phase I Report — A Descriptive Analysis of Special Education Funding in California. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616102

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of the AB 602 formula in the executive summary reports that the formula "allocates varying amounts of per-student funding for each special education local plan area (SELPA), based on varying historical SELPA costs for services provided under J-50."

"In 2019/20, per-student funding ranged from $557 to more than $900 per student, due to rates determined using the J-50 formula (CDE, 2020d)."
