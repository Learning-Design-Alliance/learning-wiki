## Subclaims
`q2 i?` An explicit work-based calculation for the charge-sphere system yields exactly half the Coulombic energy between the real charge and its image, despite the less symmetric geometry. [→ M.M. Taddei 2009](#mm-taddei-2009)

## Evidence

### M.M. Taddei 2009

M.M. Taddei, T.N.C. Mendes, C. Farina. (2009). Subtleties on energy calculations in the image method. https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4497 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0903.4497](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0903.4497)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivation in Section 3 integrating the image-method force along the axis OX for a charge q at distance a from a grounded sphere of radius R (a > R). The result is "nothing but 1/2 the Coulombic energy" with image q′ = −Rq/a; the authors note the surprise that the same factor 1/2 appears.

> "which is nothing but 1/2 the Coulombic energy between charge q and its image q′ = −Rq/a. At ﬁrst sight, it seems amazing that the same factor 1 /2 appears."
