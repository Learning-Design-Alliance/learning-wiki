# Four visual patterns of moment-by-moment learning curves occur in grade-five arithmetic practice with an ALT, with immediate peaks most frequent and single spikes absent.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Moment-by-moment learning curves coded from 265 graphs showed four forms: immediate peak (45%), immediate drop (25%), separated multiple spikes (17%), and close multiple spikes (13%). [→ Inge Molenaar 2021](#inge-molenaar-2021)

## Evidence

### Inge Molenaar 2021

Inge Molenaar, Anne Horvers, Ryan S. Baker. (2021). What can moment-by-moment learning curves tell about students' self-regulated learning? Learning and Instruction, 72, 101206. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.05.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Coding study of 265 moment-by-moment learning curve graphs from 92 students' ALT log data, coded by two trained coders with kappa 0.74. The article reports "Immediate peak were most frequent with 118 curves (45%)"; separated multiple spikes occurred 46 times (17%) and close multiple spikes 35 times (13%), while single spike curves did not occur.

> "We found four visual patterns of learning curves, shown in Fig. 4. Immediate peak were most frequent with 118 curves (45%), followed by immediate drop (occurred 66 times, 25%)."
