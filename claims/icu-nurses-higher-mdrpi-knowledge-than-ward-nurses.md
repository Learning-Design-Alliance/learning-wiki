Aydinli Ayşe; Deniz Doğan Sevgi. (2025). Investigation of Nurses' Level of Knowledge on Medical Device‐Related Pressure Injuries: A Descriptive and Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70776

One‐way ANOVA comparing worked‐unit groups in the 217‐nurse cross‐sectional survey; Table 1 prints F = 6.947, p = 0.001 with the ordering 1,2 < 3. No effect size is printed. The authors attribute the difference to intensive care nurses' greater exposure to MDRPIs.
