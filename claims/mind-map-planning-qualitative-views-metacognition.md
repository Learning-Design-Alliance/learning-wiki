## Evidence

### Tanriseven 2014

Tanriseven, I. (2014). A Tool That Can Be Effective in the Self-regulated Learning of Pre-service Teachers: The Mind Map. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 39(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.14221/ajte.2014v39n1.1

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Content analysis of open-ended questionnaire answers from the experimental group (n=30), coded against MSLQ dimensions with pre-formed themes; frequencies are descriptive, no effect size printed.

> "Most of the pre-service teachers’ expressions are brought together under the theme of metacognitive self-regulation: learning awareness (f=10), noticing the deficiencies (f=11), and remedying deficiencies (f=21)."

### Tanriseven 2014 (2)

Tanriseven, I. (2014). A Tool That Can Be Effective in the Self-regulated Learning of Pre-service Teachers: The Mind Map. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 39(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.14221/ajte.2014v39n1.1

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same content analysis of the open-ended questionnaire, coded under effort management and time and study environment themes; self-reported perceptions only.

> "nearly all the pre-service teachers mentioned the positive effects of the mind map on an increase in concentration (f=24), while more than half mentioned its positive effects on planned study (f=21)"
