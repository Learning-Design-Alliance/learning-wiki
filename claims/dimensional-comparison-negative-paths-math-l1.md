## Evidence

### Möller 2020

Möller, J., Zitzmann, S., Helm, F., Machts, N., & Wolff, F. (2020). A Meta-Analysis of Relations Between Achievement and Self-Concept. Review of Educational Research, 90(3), 376–419. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654320919354

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Multivariate random-effects meta-analysis of the classic I/E model (k = 118 data sets, n = 213,121). The null model showed significant heterogeneity (I2 > .94 for all six path coefficients), justifying moderator analyses. The authors state the paths "were significantly negative" with β = −.17 and β = −.20, replicating the 2009 meta-analysis.

> "both path coefficients of subject achievement on noncorresponding self-concept (indicating dimensional comparison effects) were significantly negative (β = −.17 from mathematics achievement to L1 self-concept, β = −.20 from L1 achievement to mathematics self-concept)"
