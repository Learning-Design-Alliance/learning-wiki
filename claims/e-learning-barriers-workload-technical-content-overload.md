### Gruner D 2015

Gruner D, Pottie K, Archibald D, Allison J, Sabourin V, Belcaid I, McCarthy A, Brindamour M, Augustincic Polec L, Duke P. (2015). Introducing global health into the undergraduate medical school curriculum using an e-learning program: a mixed method pilot study. BMC Medical Education 15:142. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0421-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic analysis of the four focus groups grouped barriers into student workload issues (including "work overload" and time constraints) and module issues, with technical problems predominant; students also complained modules were "a bit wordy" and lacked opportunities to ask for clarification.

> "Barriers associated with the e-learning modules themselves included predominantly “technical problems,”"
