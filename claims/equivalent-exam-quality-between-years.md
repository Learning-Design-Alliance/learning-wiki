## Evidence

### Varachotisate P et al 2022

Varachotisate P et al. (2022). Student academic performance in non-lecture physiology topics following the abrupt change from traditional on-site teaching to online teaching during COVID-19 pandemic. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2022.2149292

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item analysis of 126 pre-COVID and 131 COVID-year summative items covering percent-correct value, discrimination index, and modified Ebel minimal passing level. The article reports "65.37 ± 9.21% ... vs. 66.05 ± 10.10% ... P = 0.20"; equivalence testing was not performed.

> "Also, the minimal passing level calculated by the modified Ebel method did not significantly differ between the two academic years (65.37 ± 9.21% in the pre-COVID-19 year vs. 66.05 ± 10.10% in the COVID-19 year, P = 0.20, Figure 2(c)."
