## Evidence

### Prinz 2026

Prinz, M., Schäfer, E., Bürklein, S. & Donnermeyer, D. (2026) Endodontic diagnostics training in undergraduate dental education: An observational pilot study on AI‐driven virtual patient e‐learning. International Endodontic Journal, 59, 1145–1159. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.14277

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive questionnaire study of 72 dental students completing both time points. Likert responses on duplicated items moved toward agreement at T2 compared with T1 (Table 6), with the article stating the improvement occurred "in each cohort (4th‐ and 5th‐year students) and overall". Analysis was descriptive only; no inferential test or effect size was reported.

> "The confidence to perform endodontic diagnostics independently improved both in each cohort (4th‐ and 5th‐year students) and overall after the training on the e‐learning tool."
