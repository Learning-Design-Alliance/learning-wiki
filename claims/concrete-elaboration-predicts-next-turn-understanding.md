## Evidence

### Shayla Sharmin 2026

Shayla Sharmin, Mohammad Fahim Abrar, Roghayeh Leila Barmaki. (2026). Micro-level AI Feedback Features and Student Responses in Consecutive LLM Tutoring Interactions. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.08952](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.08952)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

GEE logistic regression clustered by user on 1,702 confusion windows from the StudyChat corpus, an observational analysis of naturalistic LLM tutoring logs. The article reports "OR = 1.82, 95% CI [1.07, 3.11], p = .027" for elaboration predicting next-turn understanding.

> "GEE results confirmed that concrete elaboratio n was a significant positive predictor of understanding (OR = 1.82, 95% CI [1.07, 3.11], p = .027), indicating 1.82 times higher odds of understanding after controlling for affective language and response length"
