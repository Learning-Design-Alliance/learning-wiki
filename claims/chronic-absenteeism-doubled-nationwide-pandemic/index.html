EdTrust 2023¶

EdTrust. (2023). 5 Things for Advocates to Know About Chronic Absenteeism. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED627994

q2 · i? · review · r1

The brief reports that 8 million students were chronically absent before pandemic school closures and that in 2020-21 at least 10 million students missed 10% or more of school days, with state data showing "chronic absenteeism doubled in 2021-22 in these states".

"Recent data from Connecticut, Michigan, California, and Ohio reveal that chronic absenteeism doubled in 2021-22 in these states, and it has likely doubled nationwide to 16 million students."
