Evidence¶

Kit S. Double 2025¶

Kit S. Double, Micah B. Goldwater, Damian P. Birney. (2025). Reactivity to confidence ratings: evidence of impaired rule-learning. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09413-5

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Mixed ANOVA on training response times. The JOL condition also responded significantly slower than the confidence rating group in Block 1, t(706) = 2.74, p =.015, and Block 2, t(706) = 2.51, p =.037. No significant main effect of condition was found.

"The interaction between block number and condition was significant, F(10, 3530) = 2.54, p =.005, ηp2 =. 007. Post-hoc tests with Holm corrections suggested that in the JOL condition responded significantly slower than the no-rating group in Block 1, t(706) = 2.83, p =.015, and Block 2, t(706) = 2.35, p =.038."
