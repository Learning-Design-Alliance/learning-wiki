## Evidence

### Yusuf S 2026

Yusuf S, Eyup DI, Emre E, Necati E, Dursun B, Arda T. (2026). Operative time-based learning curve and surgical outcomes of laparoscopic radical prostatectomy in a surgeon with limited open surgery experience. BMC Surgery 26:184. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03577-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Segmented logarithmic regression on 90 chronologically ordered cases validated the CUSUM-suggested breakpoint: the post-61 slope (b = 89.79, p = 0.233) was "not statistically different from zero", whereas the learning-phase slope was significantly negative (b = − 20.06; p = 0.004).

> "In contrast, in the subsequent phase (Cases 62–90), the regression equation was OT = -166.22 + 89.79 × ln(Case Number). A weak positive correlation was observed (r = 0.229; 95% CI, -0.150 to 0.549) and the slope was not statistically different from zero (b = 89.79; 95% CI, − 61.09 to 240.66; R² = 0.052; p = 0.233). This loss of slope significance supports Case 61 as the proficiency threshold in our series."

### Yusuf S 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CUSUM analysis of operative time showed a visual peak at Case 40 (maximum 576.7 min), but the authors state this inflection "did not represent an objective statistical breakpoint" and was superseded by segmented regression.

> "CUSUM analysis showed an early upward trend, with a visual turning point at Case 40, where the maximum CUSUM value (576.7 min) exceeded the mean operative time. However, this inflection did not represent an objective statistical breakpoint."
