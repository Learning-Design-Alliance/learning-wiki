### Iqbal 2018

Iqbal, M., & Rafi, M. S. (2018). A Comparative Study of English Language Teaching Practices at the Access Program and Public Schools. Bulletin of Education and Research, 40(3), 231-249. https://pu.edu.pk/journals/index.php/ber/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent sample t test on post-test scores of 200 Access and 200 non-Access students, reported in Table 7. Access students came from eight randomly selected Access classes; the public school students were a purposive sample of students who had taken the Access entry test and been offered admission but did not join or quit within three months, chosen for similarity rather than individually matched. Pre-test means were almost equal (18.19 vs 18.02, sig. .652). The article reports "the mean score of Access students is 42.6165" versus 26.1100 for non-Access, t = 40.359, sig. (2-tailed) .000; no effect size is printed.
