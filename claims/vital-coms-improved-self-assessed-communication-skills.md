## Evidence

### Fiona Quigley 2025

Fiona Quigley, Leona Ryan, Raymond Bond, Toni McAloon, Huiru Zheng, Anne Moorhead. (2025). A Virtual Simulator to Improve Weight-Related Communication Skills for Health Care Professionals: Mixed Methods Pre-Post Pilot Feasibility Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65949

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-methods pre-post feasibility study: 31 participants completed the knowledge self-assessment before and after training; exploratory paired-samples t test found mean scores increased from 28.36 to 32.35 with "a mean difference of 3.9" and Cohen d=1.03 (t30=−5.76, P=.001).

> "mean scores increased from 28.36 (SD 7.6) pretraining to 32.35 (SD 5.7) post-training, with a mean difference of 3.9 (SD 3.7; 95% CI 2.54‐5.6). The effect size, as measured by Cohen d, was 1.03"
