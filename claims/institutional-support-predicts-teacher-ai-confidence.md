Cross-sectional survey of 260 teachers in Pampanga, Philippines (52 elementary, 161 secondary, 47 college), analyzed with moderated multiple regression. The first model found support predicted confidence; the model accounted for 47.6% of variance (R2 = 0.476) with strong fit (F = 87.35, p < 0.001).
