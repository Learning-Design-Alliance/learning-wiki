## Evidence

### Mohar Sebastian 2026

Mohar Sebastian, Crawford Ana María, Vásquez Ulises Sánchez, Serna-Muñoz Ricardo, Martínez Rodrigo Rubio. (2026). The educational impact of a serious game for airway management skills of medical trainees. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09552-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory item-level analysis across the six clinical vignettes in the pilot trial found no significant between-group difference in change scores for any case; the article states these findings "support robust learning in both groups" while the game's incremental effect "remains hypothesis-generating".

> "When comparing change scores (post − pre) between groups, no statistically significant differences were observed for any individual case or for the total score (all p > 0.05)."
