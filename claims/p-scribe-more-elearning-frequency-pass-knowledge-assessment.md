### Kalfsvel L 2022

Kalfsvel L, Versmissen J, van Doorn A, van den Broek W, van der Kuy H, van Rosse F. (2022). Better performance of medical students on pharmacotherapy knowledge and skills tests is associated with practising with e‐learning program P‐scribe. Br J Clin Pharmacol. https://doi.org/10.1111/bcp.15077

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective log analysis of P-scribe use frequency for the same 270-student cohort. The article reports "six e‐learning modules more" for first-time passers, with the difference statistically significant; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "Students passing the Dutch National Pharmacotherapy Assessment first time, practised on average six e‐learning modules more (P < .05; 95% CI of difference [4.1–7.0]) than students who failed their first attempt."
