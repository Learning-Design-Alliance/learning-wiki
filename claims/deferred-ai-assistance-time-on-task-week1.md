`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on time spent on hint writing per condition per week in the randomized experiment; in Week 1 the Deferred condition's average time was more than double that of the other groups. The article reports significance "in Week 1 (p = 0.02), but not in Week 2 (p = 0.35)".

> "An ANOVA confirmed that this difference was statistically significant in Week 1 (p = 0.02), but not in Week 2 (p = 0.35)."
