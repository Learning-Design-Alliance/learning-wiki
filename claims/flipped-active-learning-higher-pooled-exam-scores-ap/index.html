Evidence¶

Maria Entezari & Mohammad Javdan 2016¶

Maria Entezari & Mohammad Javdan. (2016). Active Learning and Flipped Classroom, Hand in Hand Approach to Improve Students Learning in Human Anatomy and Physiology. International Journal of Higher Education, Vol. 5, No. 4. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n4p222

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of pooled grades on three multiple-choice exams between the active-learning treatment (n=32) and control (n=34) groups, both taught by the same instructor. The treatment group averaged "76.8 ± 1.89" vs "67.8 ± 1.88", p<0.001; no effect size was printed.

"Students’ performance was significantly better in the treatment group for all combining data of the three exams [Mean ± SE; 76.8 ± 1.89 in the treatment group vs 67.8 ± 1.88 in the control group, p<0.001]."
