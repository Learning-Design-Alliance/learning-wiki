## Evidence

### İlhan 2018

İlhan, B. (2018). Length in listening texts as a single determiner of difficulty for comprehension. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(3), 336-346. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1193259

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second quasi-experimental comparison on a 03.42-minute listening text of the same genre and topic, with 13 students per group as printed in Table 4. The article reports "no significant difference" with t (24) = 1,024, p = 0.316; no effect size is printed.

> "T-test analysis of the comprehension scores for both groups indicated that there was no significant difference in scores for experimental group (M = 44,61, SD = 18,53) and control group (M = 36,92, SD = 19,74; t (24) = 1,024, p = 0.316, two-tailed)."
