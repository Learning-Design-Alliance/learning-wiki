## Evidence

### Broks VMA 2023

Broks VMA, Stegers-Jager KM, Fikrat-Wevers S, Van den Broek WW, Woltman AM. (2023). The association between how medical students were selected and their perceived stress levels in Year-1 of medical school. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04411-0

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel linear regression (Model 2) on PSS-14 scores of 650 Year-1 students from cohorts 2013, 2014, 2018, controlling for gender and cohort, showed "higher stress perception levels compared to students selected by high grades" for assessment-selected students, with a small effect size of 0.3.

> "Students selected by assessment (B = 2.25 [0.84–3.66], ES = 0.3 (small), p < .01) or weighted lottery (B = 3.95 [1.49–6.42], ES = 0.5 (medium), p < .01) had higher stress perception levels compared to students selected by high grades"
