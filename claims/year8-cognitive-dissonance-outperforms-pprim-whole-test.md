## Evidence

### Athanasopoulos 2023

Athanasopoulos, P. (2023). Cognitive dissonance or p-prims? Towards identifying the best way to overcome misconceptions in physics. Prestatyn High School, UK. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2308.15601](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2308.15601)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental classroom comparison of two intact top-set year 8 classes (8p1 cognitive dissonance, 8p2 p-prim) taught the same two-lesson sequence on Newton's first law, post-test only. Mean post-test scores were 65% versus 48%; the effect size of 0.92 (95% CI 0.23-1.61) was calculated with the pooled standard deviation following Coe (2002).

> "The 8p1 group receiving the cognitive dissonance instruction has outperformed the 8p2 group. Treating 8p2 as the control group, we can calculate the effect size of the cognitive dissonance instruction to be 0.92 with the 95% confidence interval being (0.23-1.61)."
