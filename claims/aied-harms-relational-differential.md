## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Harms from educational AI often arise not from deliberate design but from the inherent losses of representing complex social phenomena in data, and are experienced differently according to people's backgrounds. [→ Neil Selwyn 2023](#neil-selwyn-2023)

## Evidence

### Neil Selwyn 2023

Neil Selwyn. (2023). Constructive Criticism? Working with (Rather than Against) the AIED Back-Lash. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00344-3

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument in the commentary, citing examples such as exam proctoring systems failing to detect Black students' faces and non-binary students forced to mis-identify in system profiles. The article states harms are "relational in nature" and more substantial for some groups than others.

> "Such harms might not be designed deliberately into technologies, but arise from the inevitable reductions and losses inherent in representing complex social phe-nomenon in datafied forms, and then processing these data through complex com-putational procedures."
