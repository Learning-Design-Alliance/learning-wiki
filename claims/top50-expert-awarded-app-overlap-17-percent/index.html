Evidence¶

Vaala 2015¶

Vaala, S., Ly, A., & Levine, M.H. (2015). Getting a read on the app stores: A market scan and analysis of children's literacy apps. New York, NY: The Joan Ganz Cooney Center at Sesame Workshop. https://joanganzcooneycenter.org/publication/getting-a-read-on-the-app-stores-a-market-scan-and-analysis-of-childrens-literacy-apps/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Market scan of 183 literacy apps for ages 0-8 sampled from three stores' Top 50 lists and three expert review sites over eight weeks in 2014. The report states "only 17% of the apps that had won awards in our sample were also found among the lists of Top 50 Paid or Top 50 Free apps". No effect size is printed.

"In fact, only 17% of the apps that had won awards in our sample were also found among the lists of Top 50 Paid or Top 50 Free apps across the three app stores during the same period."
