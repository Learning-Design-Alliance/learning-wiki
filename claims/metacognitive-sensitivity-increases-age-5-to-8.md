## Evidence

### Vo 2014

Vo, V. Y., Li, R., Kornell, N., Pouget, A., & Cantlon, J. F. (2014). Young Children Bet On Their Numerical Skills: Metacognition in the Numerical Domain. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614538458

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A repeated measures two-way ANOVA of age group by condition on phi showed a main effect of age group (F(2, 36) = 9.73, p < .001), no condition effect, and no interaction; sensitivity generally increases with age among 5- to 8-year-olds.

> "We found a main effect of age group, F(2, 36) = 9.73, p < .001, but no effect of condition, F(1, 36) = 0.02, p = .90, and no interaction, F(2, 36) = 0.32, p = .73."
