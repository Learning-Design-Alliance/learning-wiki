## Evidence

### Binnie Rebecca S. L. 2021

Binnie Rebecca S. L., Bonsor Stephen J. (2021). The implementation of a flipped classroom approach at a UK dental school. British Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41415-021-3125-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of formal and informal SCEF feedback volumes before versus after the format change in the ADM&B course. The article reports the difference as significant without printing a test statistic or effect size.

> "There were significantly more comments from the students via the SCEF forms following the change to a FC blended learning approach than before the changes were made."

### Binnie Rebecca S. L. 2021 (2)

Binnie Rebecca S. L., Bonsor Stephen J. (2021). The implementation of a flipped classroom approach at a UK dental school. British Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41415-021-3125-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

SCEF numerical ratings on a four-point scale (SA, GA, GD, SD) introduced in academic year 2019/20, reported as percentages in Figures 2 and 3 for course enjoyment and teaching format effectiveness.

> "As can be seen in Figures 2 and 3, all students agreed that the course was enjoyable and effective, with only one BDS3 student who generally disagreed that the teaching format was effective."
