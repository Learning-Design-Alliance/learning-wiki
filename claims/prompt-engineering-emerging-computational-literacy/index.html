Synthesis of prompt-engineering findings across the 58 included studies (Section 5.2.1). The review reports that "The effectiveness of AI is highly dependent on the learner's metacognitive skills", requiring learners to formulate effective prompts and critically evaluate AI output, and concludes prompt engineering represents a new computational literacy rather than an optional skill enhancement.

"The effectiveness of AI is highly dependent on the learner's metacognitive skills, as they must know how to formulate effective prompts and critically evaluate the AI's output (Hartley et al., 2024; Amoozadeh et al., 2024)."
