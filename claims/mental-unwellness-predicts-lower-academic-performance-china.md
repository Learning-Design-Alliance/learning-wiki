## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Higher Grade 7 mental unwellness is associated with worse Grade 8 performance in Chinese (β = -0.953), mathematics (β = -1.411), and English (β = -1.471), all p < 0.05. [→ Liu Keqiao 2023](#liu-keqiao-2023)

## Evidence

### Liu Keqiao 2023

Liu Keqiao. (2023). Middle school students’ mental unwellness and academic performance in China: The effects of parental involvement. PLoS One 18(11): e0294172. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294172

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Two-level HLM on weighted CEPS panel data (level-1 n = 7,924 students in 112 schools) predicting Grade 8 Chinese scores from Grade 7 mental unwellness, controlling for prior performance and demographics. The article reports "β = -0.953, p < 0.05"; parallel math and English models print β = -1.411 and β = -1.471.

> "After holding constant the control variables, middle schoolers’ higher levels of mental unwellness in Grade 7 were still related to their worse performance in Chinese in Grade 8, β = -0.953, p < 0.05 (Table 3, Model 1)."
