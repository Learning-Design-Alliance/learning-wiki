## Evidence

### Antoniou Mark 2016

Antoniou Mark, Ettlinger Marc, Wong Patrick C. M. (2016). Complexity, Training Paradigm Design, and the Contribution of Memory Subsystems to Grammar Learning. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0158812

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 of the PLoS ONE artificial-grammar study: a 2 × 2 ANOVA compared a baseline group trained passively on picture-word pairings with a test-only control group, finding that "the baseline group (61.2%) outperformed the test-only group (43.4%)". The same ANOVA showed the simple grammar (64.3%) was easier than the complex (42.5%), with no significant interaction, p = .314.

> "A main effect of group, F(1, 59) = 15.3, p < .001, ηp2 = .206, revealed that overall the baseline group (61.2%) outperformed the test-only group (43.4%)."
