## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Simulation-group students scored higher on expected comfort for their next LP (8.3 vs 7.5, p = 0.03), comfort with the puncture itself (8.3 vs 7.1, p = 0.002), and expected success likelihood (7.9 vs 6.8, p = 0.008). [→ Felten Renaud 2025](#felten-renaud-2025)

## Evidence

### Felten Renaud 2025

Felten Renaud, Bigaut Kevin, Wirth Thomas, Kremer Laurent, Gauer Lucas, Arnold Cécile, Ollivier Irène, Godet Julien, Scherlinger Marc, Dubois Maxime, Sebbag Eden, De Sèze Jerome, Gottenberg Jacques-Eric. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory questionnaire (Table 2, n = 122) after simulator training in both groups: expected success likelihood significantly higher in the simulation group (p = 0.008); overall next-LP comfort p = 0.03 and puncture comfort p = 0.002 also favored the simulation group. No effect sizes printed.

> "Students in the simulation group felt they had a better chance of successfully performing their subsequent LP. They scored this likelihood at 7.9 (SD 1.8) compared to 6.8 (SD 2.4) in the control group (p = 0.008)."
