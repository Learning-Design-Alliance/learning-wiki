Patricia D. Simon 2026¶

Patricia D. Simon, Lily Min Zeng, Luke K. Fryer. (2026). A systematic review of student engagement research in adaptive learning platforms. Computers and Education Open. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100360

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing an included study, that "girls exhibited higher behavioral engagement" than boys on time spent, help-seeking, and confidence; the discussion contrasts this with a K12 study where males showed more positive motivation and engagement.

"In another study, girls exhibited higher behavioral engagement (time spent on the program), agentic engagement (help-seeking), and affective engagement (higher confidence and lower frustration) than boys [ 63]."
