## Evidence

### Chevalère Johann 2021

Chevalère Johann, Cazenave Loreleï, Berthon Mickaël, Martinez Ruben, Mazenod Vincent, Borion Marie-Claude, Pailler Delphine, Rocher Nicolas, Cadet Rémi, Lenne Catherine, Maïonchi-Pino Norbert, Huguet Pascal. (2021). Computer-assisted instruction versus inquiry-based learning: The importance of working memory capacity. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0259664

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test imbalance analysis (Fig 3) comparing IBL and CAI students on prior knowledge, SES, WM and ASC. Non-significant differences: prior knowledge F(1, 507) = 1.31, p = .25; SES χ2(1) = 3.02, p = .08; WM F < 1. Covariates were fixed at their grand mean in subsequent models.

> "Only ASC showed an imbalance at pre-test, F(1, 507) = 17.09, p < .001; however, as the effect size was very small (η2 = .03), it was easily dealt with in the subsequent statistical procedure by fixing all covariates at their grand mean"
