### Deutsch G.B. 2015

Deutsch G.B., Sathyanarayana S.A., Giangola M., Akerman M., DeNoto G., Klein J.D.S., Zemon H., Rubach E. (2015). Competence Acquisition for Single-Incision Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2014.00116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis comparison of cohort operative times across the 4 surgeons in the retrospective review. Median procedure times were 70.5, 113, 79, and 113 minutes for surgeons 1–4 respectively.

> "However, when we compared times between surgeons, there were statistically significant differences in all 3 cohorts evaluated (first, middle, and last)."
