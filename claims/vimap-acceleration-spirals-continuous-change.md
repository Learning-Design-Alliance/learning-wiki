## Evidence

### Pratim Sengupta 2012

Pratim Sengupta, Amy Voss Farris, Mason Wright. (2012). From Agents to Continuous Change via Aesthetics: Learning Mechanics with Visual Agent-based Computational Modeling. Technology, Knowledge & Learning (accepted for publication). [doi:10.1007/s10758-012-9190-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10758-012-9190-9)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative observation across the two studies of Activity 1.2, in which students used speed-up and slow-down commands to create growing and shrinking spirals, articulating change as "repeated, discrete increments or decrements of step  sizes." The authors illustrate with the case of Nathan, who debugged his program using the delay and code step-through scaffolds.

> "Our studies show that this activity can enable learners to formally articulate their intuitive understanding of a continuous process of change in terms of repeated, discrete increments or decrements of step  sizes."
