## Evidence

### Kainulainen et al. 2017

Mikko Kainulainen, Jake McMullen, and Erno Lehtinen (2017). Early Developmental Trajectories Toward Concepts of Rational Numbers. Cognition and Instruction, 35:1, 4-19. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1251287

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Latent transition analysis of the 5-class model across three measurement points printed transition probabilities between classes. The article reports the NnB-to-High probabilities of "3% and 7%" at Times 2 and 3.

> "Those students in the NnB class only show 3% and 7% probabilities of transitioning directly into the High class at Times 2 and 3, respectively."

### Kainulainen et al. 2017 (2)

Mikko Kainulainen, Jake McMullen, and Erno Lehtinen (2017). Early Developmental Trajectories Toward Concepts of Rational Numbers. Cognition and Instruction, 35:1, 4-19. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1251287

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The discussion summarizes the longitudinal pathways: of students showing a strong NNB at Time 1, "roughly 70%" still showed it at the final measurement despite a year of fraction and decimal instruction.

> "In total, roughly 70% of the students with NNB at the first time point appeared to have it also in the final measurement."
