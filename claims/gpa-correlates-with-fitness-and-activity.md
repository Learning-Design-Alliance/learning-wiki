## Evidence

### Ðurić Saša 2021

Ðurić Saša, Bogataj Špela, Zovko Vinko, Sember Vedrana. (2021). Associations Between Physical Fitness, Objectively Measured Physical Activity and Academic Performance. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.778837

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis in 20 Slovenian girls found GPA–fitness correlations "mostly moderate (r = 0.43–0.64)" and GPA–PA correlations moderate to high (r = 0.59–0.87); the conclusions state the highest correlation was between GPA and combined daily PA. A similar trend held for mathematics and language grades.

> "the correlations with PF were mostly moderate (r = 0.43–0.64; with the exception of Taping, where r = 0.26), while they were moderate to high for PA (r = 0.59–0.87)."
