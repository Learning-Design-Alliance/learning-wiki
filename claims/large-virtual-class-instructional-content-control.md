### Inusah Salifu · Usman Kojo Abonyi 2023

Inusah Salifu · Usman Kojo Abonyi. (2023). Managing large classes in virtual teaching: experiences of university teachers in Ghana during COVID-19. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10151-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on managing instructional delivery, illustrated by individual participants' practices. Teachers asked questions during lectures (P9, P1); one (P1) required students to log in with their names so he could call on them; one (P10) held WhatsApp video discussions outside lecture times; one (P8) used Zoom breakout rooms whose student representatives then led discussion in the main session; and some used flipped classroom (P1) and case study (P11) techniques. These are the practices individual teachers reported, not practices shown to be used by all or most participants.

> "They had the experiences through the attempt to create interactive virtual environments and to utilise the learner-centred approach."
