Interview theme on managing instructional delivery. Teachers asked questions throughout lectures, required students to log in with their names, held WhatsApp video discussions outside lecture times, and used Zoom breakout rooms where student representatives led discussions, plus flipped classroom and case study techniques.

> "They had the experiences through the attempt to create interactive virtual environments and to utilise the learner-centred approach."
