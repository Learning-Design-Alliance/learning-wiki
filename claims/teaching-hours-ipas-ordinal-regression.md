## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In ordinal logistic regression, total weekly time teaching any learners was significantly associated with total IPAS score (likelihood ratio p = 0.005); teaching 5–10 hours weekly vs <1 hour gave OR = 6.29 (95% CI: 1.03–38.37; p = 0.046). [→ Neal Ashley E. 2026](#neal-ashley-e-2026)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Ordinal logistic regression on survey data from the four-center cross-sectional study. The article reports a significant overall association (likelihood ratio p = 0.005) and "OR = 6.29, 95% CI: 1.03–38.37"; the wide confidence interval reflects small cell sizes.

> "sonographer respondents who taught for 5–10 hours weekly vs < 1 hour weekly had increased odds of higher total IPAS scores (OR = 6.29, 95% CI: 1.03–38.37; p = 0.046)."
