# Exam environment critically moderates GenAI learning effects: significant large gains when GenAI is permitted during assessment (g = 0.76) versus non-significant slightly negative effects when it is not (g = -0.06).

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Maier 2026

Maier, S., Gunzenhäuser, M., Schweisthal, J., Schneider, M., & Feuerriegel, S. (2026). A meta-analysis of the effect of generative AI on productivity and learning in programming. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04779

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Univariate mixed-effects meta-regression across 11 learning effect sizes testing exam environment as moderator. The article reports the omnibus test significant (Q_M(1) = 7.23, p = 0.007, 45.49% of heterogeneity). "when AI tools were permitted during the assessment, the estimated effect was large and statistically significant (g=0.76,95%CI: [0.24, 1.28],p= 0.004)"; without GenAI access, g = -0.06 (95% CI [-0.36, 0.24], p = 0.674).

> "In contrast, when AI tools were permitted during the assessment, the estimated effect was large and statistically significant (g=0.76,95%CI: [0.24, 1.28],p= 0.004)."
