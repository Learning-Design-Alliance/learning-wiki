## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the MANCOVA, the main effect of condition on per-item recall post-test scores was significant. [→ Lia 2024](#lia-2024)

## Evidence

### Lia 2024

Lia, Z. R.-Y., Barry, C., & Cukurova, M. (2024). Adult learners' recall and recognition performance and affective feedback when learning from an AI-generated synthetic video. Technical Report. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.10384](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.10384)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A MANCOVA over the ten fill-in-the-blank recall items in the 400-participant experiment; multivariate normality failed but large-sample MANCOVA was used and PERMANOVA (F(3,374) = 3.34, p < .01) verified that "the main effect of the conditions remained significant (Pillai's Trace = 0.4...)".

> "Even though accounting for the latent influences of the covariates in the MANCOVA model, the main effect of the conditions remained significant (Pillai's Trace = 0.4, F(30,1053) = 5.4, p < .001, η2 = .13)."
