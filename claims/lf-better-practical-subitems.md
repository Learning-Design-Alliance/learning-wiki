## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The LF group performed significantly better on breathing control, continuous chest compression while charging the defibrillator, electrocardiogram analysis, and time interval between electric shocks. [→ Massoth C 2019](#massoth-c-2019)

## Evidence

### Massoth C 2019

Massoth C, Röder H, Ohlenburg H, Hessler M, Zarbock A, Pöpping DM, Wenk M. (2019). High-fidelity is not superior to low-fidelity simulation but leads to overconfidence in medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1464-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Video analysis of assessment scenarios (ventricular fibrillation case) rated by two independent investigators using a predefined score sheet. On four sub-items "students in the LF group performed significantly better"; performance was comparable on most other criteria (Table 2).

> "With regard to "breathing control" (p = 0.012), "continuous chest compression while charging the defibrillator" (p = 0.017), "electrocardiogram analysis" (p = 0.021) and "time interval between electric shocks" (p = 0.016), students in the LF group performed significantly better"
