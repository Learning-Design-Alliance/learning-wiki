### Ahana Ghosh 2026

Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-41153-2_7](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-41153-2_7)

Learning-phase comparison across four randomly allocated groups (97 None, 105 Code-Rec, 97 Code-Quiz, 99 Plan-Quiz) on 12 Hour of Code tasks, analyzed with Kruskal-Wallis and Mann-Whitney U tests with Bonferroni correction. The article reports "All three feedback groups significantly outperformedNoneonHoCsuccess rates" and Cliff's δ w.r.t. None of 0.66 (Code-Rec), 0.37 (Code-Quiz), 0.37 (Plan-Quiz).
