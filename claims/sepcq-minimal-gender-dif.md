## Evidence

### Zachariae R 2015

Zachariae R, O'Connor M, Lassesen B, Olesen M, Kjær LB, Thygesen M, Mørcke AM. (2015). The self-efficacy in patient-centeredness questionnaire – a new measure of medical student and physician confidence in exhibiting patient-centered behaviors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0427-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

DIF analysis with women (N = 358) as reference group and men (N = 90) as focal group, using Mantel-Haenszel Chi-Square and an IRT-based K-index approach. The article reports "almost all items had negligible gender-related DIF (category A)", with item 25 moderate DIF favoring men and item 26 slightly exceeding the K-index criterion favoring women (K = 0.34 and 0.32).

> "almost all items had negligible gender-related DIF (category A). The exception was item 25 ("I am confident that I am able to stay focused on what is best for the patient if there is a professional disagreement about the diagnosis and treatment") which showed moderate item bias (B) in favor in men"
