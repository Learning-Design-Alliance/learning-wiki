# Access to GenAI story ideas causally increases evaluators' assessments of story novelty and usefulness relative to a human-only baseline

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Writers randomly given the option to obtain GenAI ideas produced stories evaluators rated as more novel and more useful than human-only writers. [→ Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser 2023](#anil-r-doshi-and-oliver-p-hauser-2023)

## Evidence

### Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser 2023

Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser. (2023). Generative artificial intelligence enhances creativity but reduces the diversity of novel content. https://aspredicted.org/136723

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Pre-registered online experiment: 293 writers randomized to Human only or GenAI-idea conditions; 600 evaluators rated stories. The results section reports "the provision of AI ideas improves the story’s novelty by 6.7%" and usefulness by 6.4%, with unstandardized OLS coefficients as printed.

> "We find that the provision of AI ideas improves the story’s novelty by 6.7% (b=0.259, p=0.001, see Table S3; compared to the Human only mean of 3.85) and its usefulness by 6.4% (b=0.319, p<0.001; compared to the Human only mean of 5.02)."
