## Evidence

### Laukkanen L 2022

Laukkanen L, Suhonen R, Löyttyniemi E, Leino-Kilpi H. (2022). The usability, feasibility and fidelity of the Ethics Quarter e-learning intervention for nurse managers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03241-w

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Structured 5-point Likert feasibility questions created for this study (face-validity assessed by an expert panel) administered to 95 nurse managers. All mean values were above 4 (range 1–5); duration adequacy scored 4.62 (SD 0.51).

> "The NMs considered that the EQ offered a good way to learn nursing management ethics, the mean score being 4.65 (SD 0.50, range 3–5)."
