## Evidence

### de la Fuente Jesús 2021

de la Fuente Jesús, Malpica-Chavarria Erika Andrea, Garzón-Umerenkova Angélica, Pachón-Basallo Mónica. (2021). Effect of Personal and Contextual Factors of Regulation on Academic Achievement during Adolescence: The Role of Gender and Age. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178944

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA/MANOVA 3 × 3 (SR level × socio-educational context level, n = 430) found "the greater the SR level had a higher self-efficacy level, a lower procrastination level, and a higher achievement level"; for achievement SR F(2,430) = 42,325, r² = 0.167 and FE F(2,430) = 4322, r² = 0.020, post-hoc SR,3>2>1 and FE,3,2>1.

> "the greater the SR level had a higher self-efficacy level, a lower procrastination level, and a higher achievement level. Complementary, a higher parent's education had a higher achievement level."
