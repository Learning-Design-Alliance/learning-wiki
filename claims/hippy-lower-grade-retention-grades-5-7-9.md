## Evidence

### Brown 2014

Brown, A., & Lee, J. (2014). School Performance in Elementary, Middle, and High School: A Comparison of Children Based on HIPPY Participation During the Preschool Years. School Community Journal, 24(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1048627

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analysis compared retention rates since kindergarten for 516 HIPPY and 516 matched comparison children. In 5th grade, 14.6% of HIPPY children had one-year retentions versus 59.2% of comparison children; in 9th grade, 23.5% versus 46.6%. No significant difference was found in 3rd grade.

> "Statistical significances on grade retentions between the two groups were found in the 5th, 7th, and 9th grades."
