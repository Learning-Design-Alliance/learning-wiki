# TBL effects on theoretical knowledge and practical skills are larger in interventions lasting more than 4 weeks

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Subgroup analyses by intervention duration showed significantly larger effect sizes for studies lasting more than 4 weeks than for those lasting 4 weeks or less, for both outcomes. [→ Fu Jie 2026](#fu-jie-2026)

## Evidence

### Fu Jie 2026

Fu Jie, Chen Xiu, Li Jinglun, Peng Lilei. (2026). TBL alone or combined with other teaching methods for neurology education in China: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09468-1

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of theoretical knowledge scores by intervention duration; the subgroup difference was significant (p = 0.01). For practical skills the same ordering held (SMD = 2.12, SMD = 1.36, SMD = 0.60) with p < 0.0001, and heterogeneity was fully resolved in the duration-defined subgroups (both I2 = 0%).

> "In the subgroup analysis according to intervention duration (≤ 4 weeks vs. > 4 weeks vs. Not reported), ranked by effect size from largest to smallest, the results were: Not reported (SMD = 1.40, 95% CI: 0.94 to 1.85), > 4 weeks (SMD = 0.55, 95% CI: 0.19 to 0.91) and ≤ 4 weeks (SMD = 0.53, 95% CI: -0.08 to 1.15), and the subgroup difference was statistically significant (p = 0.01)."
