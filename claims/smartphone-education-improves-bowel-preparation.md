## Evidence

### Wen MC 2020

Wen MC, Kau K, Huang SS, Huang WH, Tsai LY, Tsai TY, Tsay SL. (2020). Smartphone education improves embarrassment, bowel preparation, and satisfaction with care in patients receiving colonoscopy: a randomized controlled trail. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000023102

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Multiple logistic regression with backward selection in the RCT (70 per group), with bowel preparation rated by gastroenterologists on the Aronchik Scale; the model explained 9.7% of variation (Nagelkerke R2 = 0.097, P < .05). The printed odds ratio = 2.46 falls in the medium range.
