`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Systematic mapping review of 96 primary-secondary transitions articles published 2008-2018, reported in this paper's background synthesis. The review found that "most of the authors of the 96 included articles had neither clearly defined nor operationalised transitions".

> "They found that most of the authors of the 96 included articles had neither clearly defined nor operationalised transitions"

### Bagnall 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Discourse analysis of the same 96 articles, focusing on words used, research design, and use of theory, identified nine conceptualisations ranging from change (54 articles) to turning points (3 articles), as printed in Table 1.

> "After a rigorous cross-check by the research team, they identified nine conceptualisation/s of primary-secondary transitions (see Table 1)."
