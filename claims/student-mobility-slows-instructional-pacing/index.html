Sebring 2003¶

Sebring, Penny Bender. (2003). Research Agenda 2004-2008. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org/publications

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The agenda reports prior research (citing Kerbow) showing mobility harms schools. The source states "the pacing of instruction slow down as teachers work to accommodate new students" in high-mobility schools; no effect size is printed.

"Previous research has shown that school mobility, especially if it occurs often and within school years, can be harmful. Schools with high percentages of mobile students tend to see the pacing of instruction slow down as teachers work to accommodate new students."
