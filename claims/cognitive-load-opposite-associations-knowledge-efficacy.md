## Evidence

### Chang Chia-Chen 2026

Chang Chia-Chen, Wu Wen-Chi, Chen Li-Chu, Tsai Ping-Hsiu, Tung Chen-Yin. (2026). Effects of online collaborative learning and simulation-based learning on pedagogical knowledge and teaching efficacy in digital education. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-48620-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Covariate estimates from the multivariable linear mixed-effects models in the quasi-experimental teacher study. The quote reports cognitive load β = 0.004 for knowledge and β = −0.11 for efficacy with CIs and p-values; these are unstandardised coefficients, not effect sizes.

> "pedagogical knowledge had a significant positive association with cognitive load (β = 0.004, 95% CI = 0.003–0.01, p < 0.001). By contrast, teaching efficacy was positively associated with a postgraduate qualification (β = 2.79, 95% CI = 1.30–4.28, p < 0.001) but negatively associated with health education specialisation (β = −2.47, 95% CI = −4.20 to −0.75, p = 0.005) and cognitive load (β = −0.11, 95% CI = −0.20 to −0.01, p = 0.030)."
