Conclusion of the systematic review comparing the IHC Framework to 22 included frameworks. The uniqueness judgement follows the review's comparisons of purposes, elements, development methods, evaluation, and use, and is paired with the authors' observation that the review shows how frameworks can be improved.

> "The IHC Key Concepts Framework appears to be unique.  Our review has shown how it and other frameworks can be improved by taking account of the ways in which other related frameworks have been developed, evaluated, and made useful."
