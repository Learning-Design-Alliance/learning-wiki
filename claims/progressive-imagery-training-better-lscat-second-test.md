## Evidence

### Lin Wenxue 2025

Lin Wenxue, Yu Jian, Peng Xiaoping, Xia Jianfu, Huang Bingchen, Li Rizeng. (2025). Study on the application of progressive training method combined with imagery training method in laparoscopic suturing skills training for resident physicians. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06928-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled trial of 16 residents (9 experimental, 7 analyzed control) trained in intracorporeal suturing; Mann-Whitney U comparison at 150 min. Table 3 prints second-test medians: LS-CAT total 13.0 vs 18.0 (P = 0.005), error count 1.0 vs 4.0 (P = 0.001), with "significantly lower than those of the control group". No effect size printed.
