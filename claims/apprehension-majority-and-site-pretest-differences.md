## Evidence

### Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield 2017

Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield. (2017). A Quasi-Experimental Examination: Cognitive Sequencing of Instruction Using Experiential Learning Theory for STEM Concepts in Agricultural Education. Journal of Agricultural Education, 58(4), 175-191. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2017.04175

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive result from the KLSI v3.1 classifications in the quasi-experiment. The authors note that if this proportion holds in the wider population, many agricultural education students could benefit from instruction beginning with concrete experiences.

> "The majority of students in this study ( n = 86) had a preference for grasping experience through apprehension."

### Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield 2017 (2)

Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield. (2017). A Quasi-Experimental Examination: Cognitive Sequencing of Instruction Using Experiential Learning Theory for STEM Concepts in Agricultural Education. Journal of Agricultural Education, 58(4), 175-191. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2017.04175

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Pretest equivalence ANOVAs across the four test sites in the quasi-experiment. The water science pretest showed no significant site differences with a small ηp2 = 0.03, while the soil science pretest differed significantly between sites (ηp2 = 0.15), with post hoc differences only between sites three and four.

> "No significant differences (F(3,117) = 1.22, p = 0.30, ηp2 = 0.03) were found in the pretest water science assessment scores between students at the sites.  The ANOVA examination of the raw scores on the soil science unit exams revealed statistically significant differences (F(3,117) = 5.10, p = 0.02, ηp2 = 0.15) in the means between sites on the soil science pretest assessment."
