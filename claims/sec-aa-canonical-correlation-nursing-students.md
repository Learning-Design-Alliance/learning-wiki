## Evidence

### Kim Sun-Hee 2021

Kim Sun-Hee, Shin Sujin. (2021). Social–Emotional Competence and Academic Achievement of Nursing Students: A Canonical Correlation Analysis. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. [doi:10.3390/ijerph18041752](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18041752)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional descriptive survey of 195 nursing students at four South Korean universities; canonical correlation analysis after standardizing six SEC and two AA variables. The article reports "the canonical correlation coefficient was 0.762" with Wilks coefficient 0.394, F = 15.332, p < 0.001. The X and Y sets represented SEC and AA with 58.5% and 61.1% of variance.

> "The Wilks coefficient for the canonical correlation between the two variable groups proved meaningful at 0.394 (F = 15.332, p < 0.001) and the canonical correlation coefficient was 0.762 (Figure 1), which is large effect size."
