## Evidence

### Mahomed S 2021

Mahomed S, Ross A, Van Wyk J. (2021). Training and assessing undergraduate medical students’ research: Learning, engagement and experiences of students and staff. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v13i1.2559

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two items in the same student questionnaire (n = 213 for the report-preference item; 134 respondents for the alternative-method item) produced divergent results: 39.9% preferred an individual report, while of those asked about alternatives "only 15 (11.2%) indicated in the affirmative". No effect size reported.
