Stevens 2014¶

Stevens, W. D., Allensworth, E. M., Pareja, A. S., de la Torre, M., Gwynne, J. A., Rosenkranz, T., & Sebastian, J. (2014). Free to Fail or On-Track to College: An Introduction to the UChicago CCSR Research Series. UChicago CCSR Research Brief. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/free-fail-or-track-college

q3 · i? · associational · r2

A 2005 UChicago CCSR analysis of 10 years of longitudinal district records across Chicago high schools examined whether ninth-grade course performance predicted graduation. It found on-track students were "almost four times more likely to graduate" than students not meeting the two criteria, more predictive than background or test scores.

"Students who earned at least five academic credits and had no more than one semester F in a core course in ninth grade—called "on-track to graduate" —were almost four times more likely to graduate than students who did not meet these two criteria."
