## Evidence

### Jean-François Parmentier and Brahim Lamine 2015

Jean-François Parmentier and Brahim Lamine. (2015). Interpreting gains and losses in conceptual test using Item Response Theory. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1509.03878](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1509.03878)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

IRT calculation assuming θpost = θpre, plotted in Fig. 3; the article adds that "For a pre-test score value of 50% both gains and losses have the same value around 35%". This makes the usual interpretation of gains as learning unsecured.

> "Results are plotted in Fig. 3, which clearly show that even when no learning occurs gain is an increasing function of the pre-test score and raise up to one."
