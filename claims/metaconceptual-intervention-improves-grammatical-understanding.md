# A metaconceptual grammar intervention improved 14-year-old Dutch pre-university students' grammatical understanding, with effects most probably between medium and large and replicated across groups by the switching replications design

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who received the metaconceptual intervention scored higher on grammatical understanding than control students, with the effect replicated across the two groups by design. [→ van Rijt 2022](#van-rijt-2022)

## Evidence

### van Rijt 2022

van Rijt, Jimmy; Myhill, Debra; De Maeyer, Sven; Coppen, Peter-Arno. (2022). Linguistic metaconcepts can improve grammatical understanding in L1 education: evidence from a Dutch quasi-experimental study. PLoS ONE 17(2): e0263123. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0263123

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Switching replications quasi-experiment with 196 Dutch ninth-grade pre-university students, analyzed with Bayesian multivariate models on a Test for Grammatical Understanding. The Group 1 intervention effect (Occ2 * Group1) parameter's 95% HDI is "situated between 1.42 and 3.06"; Group 2's effect HDI ranged 1.37–2.73.

> "The 95% of most credible estimates for this parameter concerning the understanding score are situated between 1.42 and 3.06 indicating that students in this group score higher than students in Group 2 who did not receive the intervention at that measurement occasion."
