# Before VR training, pedicle screw placement accuracy increases with surgeon seniority, from 48.06% in PGY1-5 to 77.19% in PGY > 10

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pre-training freehand accuracy rose with seniority: Group A (PGY1-5) was lowest at 48.06%±14.87%, Group B (PGY6-10) moderate at 66.83%±13.42%, and Group C (PGY > 10) highest at 77.19%±9.99%, with significant between-group differences. [→ Lei Y 2025](#lei-y-2025)

## Evidence

### Lei Y 2025

Lei Y, Zhang Z, Zhou F, Xia T, Xu N, Sun Z. (2025). Virtual reality force feedback spine surgery simulator training for pedicle screw placement: assessing the impact of seniority and spinal region. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07695-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline freehand sawbone assessment of the 64 fellows, scored by four blinded observers. The article reports "significant differences between these groups (p < 0.001 for A vs. B and A vs. C; p = 0.013 for B vs. C)".

> "Group A (PGY1-5) had the lowest accuracy at 48.06%±14.87%, while Group B (PGY6-10) had a moderate accuracy of 66.83%±13.42%, and Group C (PGY > 10) had the highest accuracy at 77.19%±9.99%."
