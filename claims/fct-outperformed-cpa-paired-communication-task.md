# On a paired focused communication task measuring implicit knowledge, the FCT group significantly outperformed both the control group and the CPA group at delayed posttest

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At delayed posttest the FCT group (84.78%) scored significantly higher than the CPA group (61.76%) and the control group (53.45%) on the focused communication task. [→ Broszkiewicz 2011](#broszkiewicz-2011)
`q3 i?` At immediate posttest the difference between the two experimental groups approached but did not reach significance (F = 3.16, p = .08), with FCT at 89.06% and CPA at 78%. [→ Broszkiewicz 2011](#broszkiewicz-2011)

## Evidence

### Broszkiewicz 2011

Broszkiewicz, A. (2011). The effect of focused communication tasks on instructed acquisition of English past counterfactual conditionals. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 1(3), 335-363. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136449

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of pair recordings of a focused communication task, scoring obligatory contexts for the third conditional. The article concludes the FCT group "outperformed not only the control but also the CPA group during the test comprising a communication task"; the CPA group dropped almost 17% at delayed posttest, non-significantly.

> "T h e d e l a y e d p o s t t e s t r e s u l t s c o n f i r m e d t h e v a r i a b l e e f f e c t s o f t h e t r e a t m e n t between the CPA and the FCT groups as the mean percentage scores differed significantly (FCT = 84.78%, CPA = 61.76%, F = 5.70, p = .02)"
