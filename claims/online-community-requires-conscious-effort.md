## Evidence

### Murphy 2009

Murphy, E., & Rodriguez-Manzanares, M. A. (2009). Sage without a Stage: Expanding the Object of Teaching in a Web-Based, High-School Classroom. International Review of Research in Open and Distance Learning, 10(3). https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl [doi:10.19173/irrodl.v10i3.579](https://doi.org/10.19173/irrodl.v10i3.579)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the Findings (Community section) of the interview study, e-teachers reported that with students dispersed across up to 100 communities and two time zones, "Creating community online “requires a conscious ef fort,”" whereas F2F community arose naturally from physical co-presence. The Discussion adds that all interactions "required more deliberate, intentional, and planned approaches."

> "Creating community online “requires a conscious ef fort,” unlike in the F2F context, where it can occur naturally because of physical co-presence."
