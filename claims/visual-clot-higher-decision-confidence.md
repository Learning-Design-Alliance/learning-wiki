## Evidence

### Castellucci C 2023

Castellucci C, Malorgio A, Budowski AD, Akbas S, Kolbe M, Grande B, Braun J, Noethiger CB, Spahn DR, Tscholl DW, Roche TR. (2023). Coagulation Management of Critically Bleeding Patients With Viscoelastic Testing Presented as a 3D-Animated Blood Clot (The Visual Clot): Randomized Controlled High-Fidelity Simulation Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/43895

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed logistic regression with a random intercept per participant on binary decision confidence (confident or unconfident) measured by questionnaire after the simulated case in the 59-team simulation study. The model revealed "more than three and a half times higher odds" of confidence with Visual Clot; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "The mixed logistic regression model revealed more than three and a half times higher odds (odds ratio 3.60, 95% CI 1.49-8.71; P=.005) of being confident when Visual Clot was used instead of ROTEM temograms (Figure 3C)."
