Damacharla 2018¶

Damacharla, P., Dhakal, P., Stumbo, S., Javaid, A. Y., Ganapathy, S., Malek, D. A., Hodge, D. C., & Devabhaktuni, V. (2018). Effects of Voice-Based Synthetic Assistant on Performance of Emergency Care Provider in Training. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0166-3

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on testing-phase times across iterations for both groups. An "increase in training iterations reduced the time taken in the testing section by both groups" (experiment z = −2.6694; control z = −2.8682); no effect size is printed.

"two-tailed Wilcoxon sign ranked test (experimental = (z = −2.6694, p = 0.00758 for p < 0.05), control = (z = −2.8682, p = 0.0041 for p < 0.05)) validates our hypothesis that an increase in training iterations reduced the time taken in the testing section by both groups"
