## Evidence

### Korzeniowski Przemyslaw 2016

Korzeniowski Przemyslaw, Brown Daniel C., Sodergren Mikael H., Barrow Alastair, Bello Fernando. (2016). Validation of NOViSE: A Novel Natural Orifice Virtual Surgery Simulator. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/1553350616669896

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validity questionnaire with six statements on task adequacy and training utility, administered to the same 14 participants. Agreement was higher among experts than nonexperts; free-text comments noted refinement needs in clipping and cutting.

> "All Experts and 8 out of 10 Nonexperts agreed or strongly agreed that NOViSE can be a useful training tool for NOTES. All Experts and 6 out of 10 Nonexperts stated that they would recommend the simulator to others."
