## Evidence

### Angela Johnson 2026

Angela Johnson. (2026). How to design research-aligned diversity, equity, and inclusion interventions in physics. Physical Review Physics Education Research 22, 010601. https://doi.org/10.1103/q643-8928

`q3 · i1` · `review · r2`

Preregistered experiment by Carnes and colleagues: faculty from randomly selected departments attended a workshop designed to increase awareness of gender bias and promote equity-consistent habits. Experimental-department faculty reported significantly better fit, valuing of research, and comfort raising conflicts, with effect sizes 0.11 to 0.32.

> "Effect sizes for these differences ranged from 0.11 to 0.32 [31]."

### Angela Johnson 2026 (2)

Angela Johnson. (2026). How to design research-aligned diversity, equity, and inclusion interventions in physics. Physical Review Physics Education Research 22, 010601. https://doi.org/10.1103/q643-8928

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Hiring outcome from the same Carnes and colleagues intervention: experimental departments hired 47% women among new faculty versus about 32% in control departments over two years post-intervention. The article prints no effect size for this hiring difference. A scaled-up replication across 19 departments of medicine in 16 states found similar small but significant improvements.

> "In the two years following the intervention, about 32% of the new faculty hired by the control departments were women vs 47% of the new faculty hired in the experimental departments [62]."
