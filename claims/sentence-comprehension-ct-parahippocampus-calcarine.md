`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain vertex-wise regression (n = 21) reported in the Results section. The authors state that "children aged 10-12 years who performed better in reading comprehension had a thicker cortex in these two regions." No effect size was printed.

> "Two significant clusters correlated with sentence reading comprehension independent of age, gender and performance IQ (Table 3, Figure 1B). These clusters were in the left parahippocampus (LPHP: x, y, z = -27, -1, -27) and right calcarine fissure (RV1: x, y, z = 9, -104, 5)."
