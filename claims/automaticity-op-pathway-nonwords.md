### Roembke Tanja C. 2019

Roembke Tanja C., Hazeltine Eliot, Reed Deborah K., McMurray Bob. (2019). Automaticity of Word Recognition is a Unique Predictor of Reading Fluency in Middle-School Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000279

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Nonword-only hierarchical regression on oral fluency in the same sample. The article reports masked Find the Rhyme "significantly associated with oral fluency (ΔR2 = 0.151)" while "a parallel words-only analysis" did not reach significance, supporting OP-pathway automaticity.

> "As before, masked performance on Find the Rhyme was significantly associated with oral fluency (ΔR2 = 0.151). This analysis did not reach significance in a parallel words-only analysis (see Supplement S2), suggesting that masked Find the Rhyme may tap automaticity in the OP pathway."
