## Evidence

### Stergiakouli Evie 2017

Stergiakouli Evie, Martin Joanna, Hamshere Marian L., Heron Jon, St Pourcain Beate, Timpson Nicholas J., Thapar Anita, Davey Smith George. (2017). Association between polygenic risk scores for attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder and educational and cognitive outcomes in the general population. International Journal of Epidemiology. [doi:10.1093/ije/dyw216](https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyw216)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Secondary sex-stratified regression analysis in ALSPAC (5748 children with DAWBA data: 51.1% male, n = 2942; 48.9% female, n = 2806), performed because of higher numbers of boys with ADHD. The article reports no difference between boys and girls and prints no effect size for this contrast.

> "Sex-stratified analysis did not point to different effects between boys and girls (Supplementary Table 1, available as Supplementary data at IJE online)."
