## Evidence

### Torres-Lopez Lucia V. 2022

Torres-Lopez Lucia V., Cadenas-Sanchez Cristina, Migueles Jairo H., Esteban-Cornejo Irene, Molina-Garcia Pablo, H. Hillman Charles, Catena Andres, Ortega Francisco B. (2022). Does sleep-disordered breathing add to impairments in academic performance and brain structure usually observed in children with overweight/obesity? European Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-022-04403-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional hierarchical linear regression in the ActiveBrains baseline sample of 109 children with overweight/obesity (school grades analyzed in n = 85), with confounders in step 1 and the SRBD scale entered in step 2. The study reports "β = -0.269, P = 0.007" for grade point average; only these two school-grade associations survived the Benjamini–Hochberg correction.

> "Regarding school grades, SDB severity was significantly associated with natural and social sciences grades and grade point average (β = -0.226, P = 0.024; β = -0.269, P = 0.007, respectively)."
