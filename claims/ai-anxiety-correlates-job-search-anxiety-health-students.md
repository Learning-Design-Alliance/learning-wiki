## Evidence

### Dağ 2026

Dağ, E., Nal, M., Topuz, İ., Kılınç, H., Demir, Y. & Bektaş, G. (2026). Artificial intelligence perceptions and career anxiety among health sciences students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-42030-z

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 821 health sciences students at Turkish universities using Pearson correlation in SPSS 26.0. The study found "a positive and significant correlation between AI anxiety and job search anxiety (r = 0.233, p < 0.001)"; income status correlated negatively (r = −0.098).

> "The analysis revealed a positive and significant correlation between AI anxiety and job search anxiety (r = 0.233, p < 0.001), as well as a negative and significant correlation between income status (r = − 0.098, p < 0.05)."
