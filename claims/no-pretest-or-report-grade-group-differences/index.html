Mehmet Alper Ardıç; Tevfik İşleyen 2018¶

Mehmet Alper Ardıç; Tevfik İşleyen (2018). The Effect of Mathematics Instruction through Computer Algebra Systems on the Academic Achievements of Secondary Education Students: Turkey Example. Journal of Education and e-Learning Research, 5(3): 165-173. https://doi.org/10.20448/journal.509.2018.53.165.173

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mann-Whitney U comparisons of report grades and PAT pretest scores between groups before implementation; neither comparison was significant (pretest U=2.478, p>.05, r=-.049). The article treats this as evidence of equal readiness; equivalence was not formally tested.

"it is understood that there is no significant difference between the mathematics report grades of the students from the experimental group and the control group (U=2599, p>.05, r=-.096)."
