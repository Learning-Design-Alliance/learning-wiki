## Evidence

### Puolakanaho 2018

Puolakanaho, A., Lappalainen, R., Lappalainen, P., Muotka, J. S., Hirvonen, R., Eklund, K. M., Ahonen, T. P. S., & Kiuru, N. (2018). Reducing Stress and Enhancing Academic Buoyancy among Adolescents Using a Brief Web-based Program Based on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-018-0973-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat hierarchical linear modelling with the full randomized sample (n = 243 across iACTface, iACT, and control groups), gender as covariate. The article reports "no statistically significant changes (p > 0.05) in any of the outcome measures"; no effect sizes were printed for this analysis.

> "The Mplus analyses and Wald test showed no statistically significant changes (p > 0.05) in any of the outcome measures: The two different intervention groups did not differ from the control group in their changes during the intervention."
