Evidence¶

Baş 2016¶

Baş, G. (2016). The effect of multiple intelligences theory-based education on academic achievement: A meta-analytic review. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 16, 1833–1864. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2016.6.0015

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis across 64 master's theses and 11 doctoral dissertations; the fixed effects model was adopted since the heterogeneity test showed a homogeneous distribution. The article calls the finding generalizable to both thesis types.

"the effect in this research of MIT-based instruction on students’ academic achievement levels is not significant in terms of being a Master’s thesis or doctoral dissertation"
