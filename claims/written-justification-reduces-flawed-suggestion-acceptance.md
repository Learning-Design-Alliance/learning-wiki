## Evidence

### Yan Tao 2026

Yan Tao, Jennifer Meyer & René F. Kizilcec. (2026). Think Before You Accept: Can Written Justification Reduce Uncritical Uptake of AI Writing Suggestions? Preprint. Under review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.23936](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.23936)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment embedded in an in-class writing activity in an upper-division data science course; primary comparison justification (n=64) vs. selection-only (n=65). Follow-up chi-square tests per suggestion showed lower acceptance for all four flawed suggestions, e.g. "Suggestion C (χ2 = 9.54,p=.002)".

> "The average acceptance rate of flawed suggestions decreased from 65% to 41% with required decision justification, with no corresponding reduction in the average acceptance of sound suggestions (81% vs. 86%)."
