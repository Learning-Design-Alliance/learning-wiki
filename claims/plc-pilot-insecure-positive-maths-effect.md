## Subclaims
`q2 i2` The secondary outcome (Maths attainment, Access Maths Test) showed a positive effect of the intervention (ES=0.49, 95% CI 0.06 to 0.91), but the result is not secure due to school dropout and technical test failures. [→ Cinzia Rienzo 2016](#cinzia-rienzo-2016)

## Evidence

### Cinzia Rienzo 2016

Cinzia Rienzo, Heather Rolfe and David Wilkinson. (2016). Powerful Learning Conversations: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581274

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat two-level analysis of the pilot cluster-RCT for Maths (final sample 937 pupils; 387 intervention, 550 control). The printed effect size ES=0.49 is medium, but the report calls it a very imprecise estimate with the lower confidence limit very close to zero.

> "With respect to the secondary outcome (attainment in Maths) the effect size is larger and results show a positive eff ect of the intervention on Maths (ES=0.49, [95% CI, 0.06 , 0.91)"
