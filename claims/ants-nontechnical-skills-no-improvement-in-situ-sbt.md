`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blinded external-video-rating analysis in a before-and-after quality improvement study: two external neonatologists independently rated delivery room recordings with the ANTS score, finding "no differences in either the four main ANTS categories" or the 15 elements. No effect sizes were printed.

> "According to both video evaluators, there were no differences in either the four main ANTS categories (Fig. 1) or in any of the 15 ANTS elements (Table 2)."
