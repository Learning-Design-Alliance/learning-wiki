## Evidence

### Matos Jason 2017

Matos Jason, Petri Camille R., Mukamal Kenneth J., Vanka Anita. (2017). Spaced education to improve competency in medical knowledge. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0181418

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stratified analysis from the same self-matched randomized trial, adjusted for clustering and PGY. Interns (PGY-1) showed "a significantly lower risk of incorrect responses to reinforced material" (RR = 0.83, p = 0.04); residents did not (RR = 1.01, p = 0.95).

> "Although there was no evidence of benefit among junior and senior residents (RR = 1.01; 95% CI, 0.83–1.22; p = 0.95), we observed a significantly lower risk of incorrect responses to reinforced material among interns (RR = 0.83, 95% CI, 0.70–0.99, p = 0.04)"
