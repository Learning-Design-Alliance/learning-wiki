### Paul Justice 2019

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Student understanding of Fermi energy, the Fermi-Dirac distribution and total electronic energy of a free electron gas. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Written question 5 and interviews. One interviewed student drew an exponentially increasing nFD(ϵ) at T > 0 K; another incorrectly claimed a peak appears and shifts to higher energies as temperature increases, possibly confused with the Maxwell speed distribution; two students drew the FD distribution as a delta function at ϵ = 0 at T = 0 K, confusing fermionic and bosonic cases.

> "On the pretest, students struggled with the graphical representation of the Fermi -Dirac distribution function both at zero and non -zero temperatures."
