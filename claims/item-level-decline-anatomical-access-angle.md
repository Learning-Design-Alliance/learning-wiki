> "In the Vid-Self group in Study 1, the decrease in score from T1 to T2 was based mainly on the following items: anatomical access (weighted: 3), angle of insertion (weighted: 2), injection of local anaesthetic, fixation of the cannula, and marking of the patient (weighted: 1 each)"
