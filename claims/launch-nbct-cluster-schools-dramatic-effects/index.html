Hart 2006¶

Hart, H. M., Ponisciak, S. M., Sporte, S. E., & Stevens, W. D. (2006). Principal and Teacher Leadership in Chicago: Continuing Analysis of Three Initiatives. Evaluation Report prepared for The Chicago Public Education Fund, Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/principal-and-teacher-leadership-chicago-continuing-analysis-three-initiatives

q1 · i? · associational · r1

Analysis of teacher survey ratings in only two elementary schools that combined a LAUNCH principal with an NBCT cluster, testing The Fund's theory of action that concentrating talented teachers and leadership moves schools toward improvement. Measures included instructional leadership, teacher influence, school commitment, collective responsibility, and innovation.

"Results of this analysis showed a significant positive effect on 8 of 16 measures for the two LAUNCH schools with NBCT clusters compared to other schools with comparably experienced principals."
