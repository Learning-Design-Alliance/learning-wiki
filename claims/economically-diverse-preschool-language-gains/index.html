Plasencia 2024¶

Plasencia, S., & Melnick, H. (2024). Strategies to foster integration in early childhood education. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/639.507

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report describes a small study comparing language growth of 3- to 6-year-olds from low-income families in economically integrated versus primarily low-income preschools. Children started with similar scores, but "children in the economically diverse programs had significantly greater gains in their language scores."

"While the children from low-income families entered preschool with similar language scores, by the spring, children in the economically diverse programs had significantly greater gains in their language scores than peers in less economically diverse programs."
