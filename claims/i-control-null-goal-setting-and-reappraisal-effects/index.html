In the Mplus pre–post pilot analysis, the I Control Goal Setting Questionnaire and the ERQ-CA Cognitive Reappraisal and Expressive Suppression scales showed no significant treatment effects; equivalence was not tested. The article suggests insufficient time for knowledge gains to transfer to goal-setting skill.

"For the ICGS and ERQ-CA, all C × T interactions and main effects were non-significant."
