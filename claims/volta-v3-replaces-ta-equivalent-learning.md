## Subclaims
`q2 i0` In Fall 2015, when the experimental group relied on VOLTA v3 with decreased TA involvement, ANOVA showed no difference in learning versus the control group, with a small effect size (d = 0.15). [→ Saleheen 2018](#saleheen-2018)

## Evidence

### Saleheen 2018

Saleheen, F., Wang, Z., Picone, J., Butz, B. P., and Won, C.-H. (2018). Efficacy of a Virtual Teaching Assistant in an Open Laboratory Environment for Electric Circuits. Advances in Engineering Education. http://www.temple.edu/csnap/volta.html

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA on gain scores of 17 students in 11 lab assignments in the Fall 2015 TA vs. VOLTA LAB 3 experiment, where the experimental group received assistance via VOLTA v3 and the TA did not answer questions directly. The article reports p = .768 and "d was found to be 0.15", a small effect.

> "For Cohort 3, d was found to be 0.15 for F(1,16) = 0.087 . The effect size (d = 0.15) indicates that this is a small effect."
