## Evidence

### Linton Debra L. 2014

Linton Debra L., Farmer Jan Keith, Peterson Ernie. (2014). Is Peer Interaction Necessary for Optimal Active Learning? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.13-10-0201

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment: two sections of the same nonmajors biology course taught by the same instructor in one semester, differing only in group versus individual processing of in-class activities. "included significantly more correct concepts in their answers to the essay questions on all four exams" (exam p values: 0.043, 0.023, 0.00018, 0.00078), analyzed by ANCOVA with pretest covariate, n = 102 cooperative, 98 individual.

> "However, students in the cooperative group section included significantly more correct concepts in their answers to the essay questions on all four exams (Figure 5)."
