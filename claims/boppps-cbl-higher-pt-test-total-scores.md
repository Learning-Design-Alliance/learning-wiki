# BOPPPS-CBL instruction produces significantly higher post-training total test scores than traditional lecture-based teaching in pediatric dentistry clerkship

## Evidence

### Lu Jiaxuan 2025

Lu Jiaxuan, Wu Ruixue, Zhu Ling, Zhao Linlin, Zhao Wei, Ma Lan. (2025). Application of the BOPPPS combined with CBL method in clinical clerkship of pediatric dentistry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07967-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 74 fourth-year students (37 per group) during a one-week pediatric dentistry clerkship. Mann-Whitney U testing of Pt-test totals showed "significantly higher total scores in the Pt-test compared to the control group (P < 0.01, r = 0.32)". Pre-test totals did not differ between groups.

> "Following the execution of the BOPPPS-CBL teaching model, the experimental group showed significantly higher total scores in the Pt-test compared to the control group (P < 0.01, r = 0.32)"
