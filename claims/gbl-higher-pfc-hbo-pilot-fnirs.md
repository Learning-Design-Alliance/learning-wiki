Descriptive fNIRS analysis of overall prefrontal cortex activation in the pilot between-subjects study (six participants per condition). The article reports the game condition's higher ∆HbO and a "357.14% greater increase in oxygen utilization", while stating there is no significant difference between conditions.

> "The game condition has higher ∆HbO (M=0.21) values than the video condition (M=-0.54), indicating more neural activities. Participants who played the game exhibited a 357.14% greater increase in oxygen utilization than those who watched the video."
