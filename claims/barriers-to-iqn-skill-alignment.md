### Kurup Chanchal 2025

Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2025). Exploring the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Analysis of Recruiting Managers' View Points. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70184

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Sub-theme 'skills are not always the priority' from the manager interviews. One manager noted vacancies were filled "even if it means individuals cannot use their skills in their area", and another said employment opportunities favour "employing someone with fewer complexities".

> "Immediate staffing needs often force managers to prioritise filling vacancies over ensuring the best skills match"

### Kurup Chanchal 2025 (2)

Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2025). Exploring the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Analysis of Recruiting Managers' View Points. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70184

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Sub-theme 'communication is a challenge'. Managers reported English proficiency influenced candidates' experience and that assertive escalation was "more problematic, particularly for nurses from developing countries, although it wasn't always the case".

> "Differences in language proficiency and cultural communication styles complicate demonstrating specialty skills."

### Kurup Chanchal 2025 (3)

Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2025). Exploring the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Analysis of Recruiting Managers' View Points. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70184

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Sub-theme 'competing demands limit time'. Managers said more time would have allowed a more strategic vision, that they could not invest beyond day-to-day business on international staff, and that in small sites a single clinical nurse manager handles everything including clinical governance.

> "Heavy workloads restrict managers' capacity to assess and support IQNs thoroughly"
