## Evidence

### Dou 2018

Dou, R., Brewe, E., Potvin, G., Zwolak, J. P., & Hazari, Z. (2018). Understanding the development of interest and self-efficacy in active-learning undergraduate physics courses. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2018.1488088](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2018.1488088)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model comparison tests in the SEM analysis of three MI courses (N = 221). The article reports all three models "explained significantly more variance than similar models without the PageRank centrality variable", with χ2(3) values of 2084.4, 2086.7, and 2081.8, all p < 0.001.

> "All three models (i.e. A, B, and C, respectively) explained significantly more variance than similar models without the PageRank centrality variable [χ2(3) = 2084.4, p < 0.001; χ2(3) = 2086.7, p < 0.001; χ2(3) = 2081.8, p < 0.001]."
