## Evidence

### Faulkner Nicholas 2020

Faulkner Nicholas, Wright Breanna, Lennox Alyse, Bismark Marie, Boag Jane, Boffa Sophie, Waxman Bruce, Watson-Kruse Janet, Paine Geoff, Bragge Peter. (2020). Simulation-based training for increasing health service board members' effectiveness: a cluster randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-034994

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Secondary-outcome survey of the intervention arm only, analysed with thematic analysis. Participants "reported feeling more comfortable and confident to speak up and ask questions in the board meetings", though some wanted the training pitched differently or lengthened.

> "The median score on a series of statements about the utility of the training was 5.14 (on a scale with a possible range of 1–6), suggesting a generally positive response. Overall, qualitative responses to the training session were also positive."
