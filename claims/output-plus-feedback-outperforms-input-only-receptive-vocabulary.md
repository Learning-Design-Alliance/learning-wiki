# Beginning EFL learners who produced written output and received corrective feedback outperformed input-only learners on a delayed receptive vocabulary post-test

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2015 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group (pushed output plus corrective feedback) scored significantly higher than the comprehensible-input-only control group on the post-test of receptive vocabulary acquisition. [→ Nowbakht and Shahnazari 2015](#nowbakht-and-shahnazari-2015)

## Evidence

### Nowbakht and Shahnazari 2015

Mohammad Nowbakht, Mohammadtaghi Shahnazari. (2015). The Comparative Effects of Comprehensible Input, Output and Corrective Feedback on the Receptive Acquisition of L2 Vocabulary Items. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.4p.103

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized two-group experiment with 30 beginning L1-Persian EFL learners (15 per group); independent-samples t-test on the one-week delayed post-test showed a significant difference, with the experimental group mean of 18.4 versus "13.2" for the control group (t = 2.92, p = .007).

> "The results indicate that the experimental and control groups are statistically different in the post-test ( p< .05). Also, the mean score of the experimental group is 18.4 while for the control group this score is equal to 13.2."
