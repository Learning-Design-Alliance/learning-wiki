### Romero 2019

Romero, M. (2019). Computers in Secondary Schools: Educational Games. In Encyclopedia of Education and Information Technologies. Springer. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-60013-0_31-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-60013-0_31-1)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The chapter reports, citing Giles & Price (2008), that escape and social pressure relate to higher levels of addictive game use, while "the parental control" is among factors not linked with the use of games. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Using games as a way to escape or due to social pressure is also leading to higher levels of addictive uses of games; however, among the factors which do not seem to be linked with the use of games is the parental control (Giles & Price, 2008)."
