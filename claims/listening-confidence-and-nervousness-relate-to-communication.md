# Year 2 observed communication was related to confidence in knowing how to listen to patients and to lower nervousness, but not to general communication confidence

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In year 2, Interview Rating Scale score was significantly correlated with confidence in knowing how to listen to patients (partial r = 0.23) and with nervousness in talking to patients (partial r = -0.26). [→ Joekes K 2011](#joekes-k-2011)
`q2 i?` No relationship was found between observed communication and general self-reported confidence or nervousness in year 1. [→ Joekes K 2011 (3)](#joekes-k-2011-3)
