## Subclaims
`q3 i?` High-order rule learning decreased during practice and increased during rest periods in Experiment 3 (long ASRT). [→ Romain Quentin 2021](#romain-quentin-2021)
`q3 i?` High-order rule learning was present only in Experiment 3, not in Experiment 2. [→ Romain Quentin 2021 (2)](#romain-quentin-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Romain Quentin 2021

Romain Quentin, Lison Fanuel, Mariann Kiss, Marine Vernet, Teodóra Vékony, Karolina Janacsek, Leonardo G. Cohen and Dezso Nemeth. (2021). Statistical learning occurs during practice while high-order rule learning during rest period. npj Science of Learning 6:14. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00093-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results of Experiment 3 (long ASRT, 25 participants, 8 weekly sessions). The article reports high-order rule learning "increased during rest periods" and decreased during practice, with printed t, p and d values.

> "high-order rule learning decreased during practice (Experiment 3: Monline = −3.03 ± 6.15 s, t(24) = −2.42, p = 0.02, d = 0.50) and increased during rest periods (Experiment 3: Mofﬂine = 2.76 ± 6.01 s, t(24) = 2.25, p = 0.03, d = 0.46)"

### Romain Quentin 2021 (2)

Romain Quentin, Lison Fanuel, Mariann Kiss, Marine Vernet, Teodóra Vékony, Karolina Janacsek, Leonardo G. Cohen and Dezso Nemeth. (2021). Statistical learning occurs during practice while high-order rule learning during rest period. npj Science of Learning 6:14. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00093-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Across-experiment comparison in Results: the block × triplet interaction for pattern vs random-high trials reached significance only in the long ASRT experiment; in Experiment 2 the ANOVA gave F(44, 7876) = 1.42, p = 0.055.

> "High-order rule learning, deﬁned as the increase of the difference in RT between pattern and random-high trials, was present only in Experiment 3 (long ASRT)"
