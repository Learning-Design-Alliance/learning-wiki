### Zhou Quan 2024

Zhou Quan, Gidziela Agnieszka, Allegrini Andrea G., Cheesman Rosa, Wertz Jasmin, Maxwell Jessye, Plomin Robert, Rimfeld Kaili, Malanchini Margherita. (2024). Gene-environment correlation: the role of family environment in academic development. Molecular Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-024-02716-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Two-mediator SEM models in the TEDS sample tested each family environment jointly with family SES (age-7 SES for outcomes at ages 7, 9 and 12; age-16 SES for age-16 outcomes). The study reports other environmental indirect effects "remained significant, albeit attenuated", across all PGSs and developmental stages.

> "Although we found that family SES played a significant role in mediating the PGS predictions of academic achievement, the indirect effects of other environmental measures (e.g. harsh parenting and CHAOS and supportive home environment) remained significant, albeit attenuated (Supplementary Fig. 30 and Supplementary Table 14)."
