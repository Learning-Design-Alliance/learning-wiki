## Evidence

### Stenseth Hege Vistven et al 2025

Stenseth Hege Vistven et al. (2025). Simulation-Based Learning Supported by Technology to Enhance Critical Thinking in Nursing Students: Scoping Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/58744

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Thematic synthesis (RQ 3) of included studies' reports of enablers and barriers, deductively sorted by the D&M IS Success Model; the review reports that realism features were "reported as increasing perceived realism; theory-to-practice transition; and, hence, nursing students' development of critical thinking skills."

> "Realistic replication of the human physiological features of the patient [75,81,107,131,138], realistic changes in patients' conditions, and being able to visually inspect the patient's status and see the effect of measures [75,81,107,108,131] were reported as increasing perceived realism; theory-to-practice transition; and, hence, nursing students' development of critical thinking skills."
