### Andrew J. Haugen and Nathan T. Moore 2014

Andrew J. Haugen and Nathan T. Moore. (2014). A model for including Arduino microcontroller programming in the introductory physics lab. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.7613](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.7613)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anecdotal report from the two pilot semesters. In 2011 one student built a simulation of the Ranger 7 mission on his Arduino board, and some 2011 students later used Arduino boards in senior engineering design projects. Some 2013 students took National Instrument's initial Labview Certification Exam, and at least one student landed a summer internship partly because of his Labview exposure. The authors label this evidence anecdotal; no systematic measures are reported.

> "Anecdotally, in each iteration there were a handful of students who latched onto the idea and produced remarkable results."
