## Evidence

### Punch 2011

Punch, R., & Hyde, M. B. (2011). Communication, Psychosocial, and Educational Outcomes of Children with Cochlear Implants and Challenges Remaining for Professionals and Parents. International Journal of Otolaryngology. https://doi.org/10.1155/2011/573280

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Parent survey subscale analysis (N = 247) comparing expected and experienced rehabilitation stress. The printed statistic is "t = −2.41 (df = 246), P < 0.05"; no effect size is reported. Qualitative data attributed stress to appointments, travel, equipment problems, and distance from services.

> "A statistically significant difference was found between parents' expectations and experiences on the rehabilitation stress subscale of the survey (t = −2.41 (df = 246), P < 0.05), with parents experiencing more stress than they had expected before their children's CI."
