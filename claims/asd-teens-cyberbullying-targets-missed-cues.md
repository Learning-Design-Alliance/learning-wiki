The brief asserts, without cited evidence, that individuals with autism are "often easy targets" of cyberbullying due to missed social cues, with possible anxiety, depression, and low self-esteem as outcomes.

> "Individuals with autism are often easy targets because they can miss social cues and not realize they are being bullied or if they do, they may lack the skills to stand up for themselves or to communicate with others about what is going on. This can lead to anxiety, depression, and low self-esteem."
