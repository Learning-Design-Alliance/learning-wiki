## Evidence

### González-Abós C 2026

González-Abós C, Reyes LG, Lorenzo C, Ausania F. (2026). MyIntuitive telemetry identifies proficiency thresholds in robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12591-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Per-protocol Full-Case telemetry comparison across the 60-case phase split in the single-surgeon series. Console time showed "a clear decreasing trend across the curve"; active time also differed (428 vs. 365 min; P < 0.001).

> "the median console time was 434 min (IQR 400–479) overall and differed between phases (470 in Group A vs. 395 in Group B; P < 0.001), with a clear decreasing trend across the curve"
