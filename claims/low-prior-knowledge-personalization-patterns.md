Chernikova, O., Sommerhoff, D., Stadler, M., Holzberger, D., Nickl, M., Seidel, T., Kasneci, E., Küchemann, S., Kuhn, J., Fischer, F., & Heitzmann, N. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis by prior knowledge (Table 3) in the meta-analysis of N = 143 studies; for low prior knowledge learners, non-personalized scaffolding with adaptable task progression yielded g = 1.37 (SE = 0.26; N = 9) versus g = 0.68 for fully non-personalized simulations, while adaptable scaffolding conditions were non-significant and smallest.
