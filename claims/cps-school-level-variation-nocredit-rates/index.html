Evidence¶

Gwynne 2022¶

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., & Liang, Y. (2022). Student engagement in learning during COVID-19: Students' course grades in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/departments/office-of-equity/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

School-level analysis of 417 district-run CPS elementary schools, classifying schools by no-credit grade rates pre-pandemic, spring 2020, and spring 2021. The report finds "120 schools ( just under one-third of elementary schools)" with rates of 20 percent or more in spring 2020.

"When the pandemic hit in spring 2020, there were 120 schools ( just under one-third of elementary schools) in which 20 percent or more of all course grades given were no-credit grades, and 164 schools in which 5-19 percent or more of the grades given out were no-credit grades"
