## Evidence

### King 2025

King, R. B., & Wang, F. (2025). The rich get richer: Socioeconomic advantage amplifies the role of growth mindsets in learning. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 792–810. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12755

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 2 of the three-level analysis of PISA 2018 data tested Growth mindset × Family SES interactions. The article reports no significant moderation for achievement but significant positive moderation for "intrinsic motivation (b = .01, SE = .00, p < .001, effect size = .02)" and engagement.

> "family SES did not moderate the relationship between growth mindsets and academic achievement (b = −.15, SE = .24, p > .05, effect size = .00), supporting the universalist hypothesis. However, the interaction between growth mindsets and family SES was significant for intrinsic motivation (b = .01, SE = .00, p < .001, effect size = .02) and academic engagement (b = .01, SE = .00, p < .05, effect size = .01), supporting the rich get richer hypothesis."
