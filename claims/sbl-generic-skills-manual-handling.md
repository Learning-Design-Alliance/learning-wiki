## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students described manual handling training as highly positive and reported that practice with hospital equipment such as slide sheets, banana boards and the hoist helped them on placements. [→ Dairo Yetunde M. 2024](#dairo-yetunde-m-2024)

## Evidence

### Dairo Yetunde M. 2024

Dairo Yetunde M., Hunter Kirsty, Ishaku Timothy. (2024). The impact of simulation-based learning on the knowledge, attitude and performance of physiotherapy students on practice placement. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05718-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the 'Generic practical skills' theme: one participant said that when her neurological-placement supervisor asked her to show skills, "we could do it, thanks to simulation placement"; students requested more frequent equipment use across the four weeks.

> "Manual handling training was received with astounding positivity being described as “fantastic”, a “strong component” and a key take away for use in practice placement especially within the field of neurology."
