# Model reframings consistently receive better ratings than expert-teacher reframings, including 23% higher challenge-seeking behavior

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` GPT-4 reframings were rated better than GMSL-trained teachers' reframings on perceived growth mindset, shame, respect, and challenge-seeking. [→ Kunal Handa 2023](#kunal-handa-2023)

## Evidence

### Kunal Handa 2023

Kunal Handa, Margaret Clapper, Jessica Boyle, Rose E Wang, Diyi Yang, David S Yeager, Dorottya Demszky. (2023). "Mistakes Help Us Grow": Facilitating and Evaluating Growth Mindset Supportive Language in Classrooms. [doi:10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.549](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.549)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of model versus EXPERT response types in the same teacher (n=174) and student (n=1,006) survey evaluation, with conditional means controlling for demographics and baseline motivational framework. "23% increased challenge-seeking behavior" for models over experts.

> "However, unexpectedly, the models also consistently receive better ratings than the EXPERT teacher response, showing 8-10% increased perceived growth mindset, 9-15% decreased shame, 9-10% increased respect, and importantly, 23% increased challenge-seeking behavior."
