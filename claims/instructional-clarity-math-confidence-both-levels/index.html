Lena Asp 2026¶

Lena Asp, Alli Klapp, & Victoria Rolfe. (2026). Associations between student-perceived teaching quality and students’ mathematics confidence and mathematics achievement: A study of Swedish grade 4 TIMSS 2019. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09767-y

q3 · i? · associational · r3

MSEM Models D1–D5 with mathematics confidence as outcome in the same Swedish grade 4 TIMSS 2019 sample. The study reports instructional clarity "significant and substantially related to mathematics confidence at both levels (0.41w; 0.83b)", stable in Model D5 (0.40w; 0.80b); only standardised path coefficients are printed, no effect size.

"The results of Model D1 show that instructional clarity is significant and substantially related to mathematics confidence at both levels (0.41w; 0.83b), as in the reference model. Classroom management is not significantly related to mathematics confidence as in the reference model (0.09w; 0.45b)."
