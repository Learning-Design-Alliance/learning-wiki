`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Robustness test redefining village-head education as three binary dummies (university, high school, junior high and below) in Models 8-10 of Table 4 on the same 1,720-sample CFPS data. The printed coefficients are 0.131, 0.073, and -0.129; no standardized effect size is reported.

> "The coefficient for university education is 0.131, which is notably higher than the coefficient for high school education at 0.073. This indicates that higher educational attainment among village heads corresponds to a greater positive impact on adolescents' academic performance."
