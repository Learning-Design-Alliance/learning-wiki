### Borakati Aditya 2021

Borakati Aditya. (2021). Evaluation of an international medical E-learning course with natural language processing and machine learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02609-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Lexicon-based sentiment analysis of merged free-text feedback from the course questionnaire, with valence shifters accounted for via the sentimentr package. The article reports the AFINN score of "+ 1.54/5 (SD 1.19)" and syuzhet "+ 0.287/1 (SD 0.390)"; no effect size or test is printed.

> "The mean sentiment score using the AFINN lexicon was + 1.54/5 (SD 1.19) and + 0.287/1 (SD 0.390) using the syuzhet lexicon for all free text feedback. These both indicated overall positive feedback"
