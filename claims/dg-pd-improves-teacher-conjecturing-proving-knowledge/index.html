Jiang et al. 2015¶

Zhonghong Jiang, Edwin Dickey, Alexander White, Ewelina McBroom, M. Alejandra Sorto, & Alana Rosenwasser. (2015). A Dynamic Geometry-Centered Teacher Professional Development Program and Its Impact. Proceedings of the 37th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education, East Lansing, MI: Michigan State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED584322

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pre/post administration of a project-developed 26-item conjecturing-proving test at the summer institute for the 64 randomly assigned teachers. The article reports "an average gain of 1.37" with a significant paired-sample t-test; the DG-control gain difference (1.56 vs 1.18) was not significant (p = .670).

"Average scores were 20.49 on the pre -test and 21.86 on the post -test with an average gain of 1.37. A paired -sample t-test showed that this gain was statistically significant (p = .00 3)."
