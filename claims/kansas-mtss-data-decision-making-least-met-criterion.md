# Data-based decision making is the least-met full-implementation criterion while universal screening is the most-met

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Only 43.9% of schools met the data-based decision making criterion in 2014, whereas 87.5% reported conducting universal screening three times per year (once a year for high school). [→ Kristin Reedy 2015](#kristin-reedy-2015)

## Evidence

### Kristin Reedy 2015

Kristin Reedy, Ed.D., and Natalie Lacireno-Paquet, Ph.D. (2015). Implementation and Outcomes of Kansas Multi-Tier System of Supports: Final Evaluation Report—2014. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559729

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Further analysis of 2014 self-reported survey data on the nine full-implementation criteria. The report separately states the universal screening criterion was most likely met, with 87.5% of schools reportedly screening three times per year.

> "the criterion related to data-based decision making is the one that is met the least consistently, with only 43.9% of schools meeting the criterion in 2014"
