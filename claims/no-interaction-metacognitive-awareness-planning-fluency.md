# Metacognitive awareness and planning level show no significant interactive effects on fluency

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2016 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Neither metacognitive awareness, planning level, nor their interaction significantly affected fluency change from pre-test to post-test in the two-way repeated measures ANOVA. [→ Seifoori 2016](#seifoori-2016)

## Evidence

### Seifoori 2016

Seifoori, Z. (2016). Metacognitive awareness and the fluency of task-based oral output across planning conditions: The case of Iranian TEFL students. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 4(1), 11-26. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way repeated measures ANOVA on pre-test and post-test fluency scores of all 114 participants. The time effect was significant (F=5.95, p=.01), but "none of the interactive effects" reached significance; no effect sizes were printed.

> "none of the interactive effects of the metacognitive awareness (F=.93, p=.33 >.05), planning levels (F=2.6, p=.07 >.05), and the metacognitive awareness and levels of planning (F=.93, p=.39 <.05) had significant effects"
