`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Differential analysis of grade increases by sex (male vs female) across the four assessment styles, using t-tests and Cohen's d (Table VI). "the largest difference being about half a percentage point"; male students earned statistically higher midterm exam grades than female students under both default and alternative aggregation schemes.

> "We find all of the effect sizes to be less than 0.2, resulting in a negligible effect size."
