## Evidence

### González-Amarante P 2025

González-Amarante P, Romero-Padrón MA. (2025). Motivations for a career in teaching: medical students’ projections towards their future role. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06536-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Open question asking for 3 main traits of a good teacher plus forced choice across four Kiani et al. domains in the same survey; Table 3 shows patience top for women (54%) and empathy for men (52%).

> "With the open question, 57 different attributes emerged. The top 10 qualities represent 137 mentions (67%) and are listed in Table 3."

### González-Amarante P 2025 (2)

González-Amarante P, Romero-Padrón MA. (2025). Motivations for a career in teaching: medical students’ projections towards their future role. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06536-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Students were asked to describe attributes of teachers who fell short in performance; the article contrasts this asymmetry with their demand for respect from competent educators.

> "However, when exposing characteristics of a bad teacher they tended to use traits under the teaching domain (uninterested, lacking teaching skills and intolerant to questions) followed by professional aspects (irresponsible)."
