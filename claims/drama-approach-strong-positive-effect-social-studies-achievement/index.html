Evidence¶

Yildirim 2022¶

Yildirim, E., Tikman, F., & Senturk, M. (2022). Meta-analysis on the effectiveness of using the drama approach in social studies course. International Journal on Social and Education Sciences (IJonSES), 4(1), 17-33. https://doi.org/10.46328/ijonses.301

q3 · i3

Meta-analysis of 38 effect sizes from 31 experimental or quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control-group studies (863 experimental, 867 control students), analyzed in CMA under the random effects model. The overall effect size was d = 1.115 with a 95% CI of 0.915–1.315 and Z = 10.945, "a significant and positive value at the „strong‟ level".

"the overall effect size of the drama approach applied in the social studies course on the academic achievement of the students was measured as 1.115, which represented a significant and positive value at the „strong‟ level according to the classification of Cohen et al. (2007)"
