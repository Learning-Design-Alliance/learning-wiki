Biagioni Gabriela, Comodo Fernanda, Coelho Marcelo Santos. (2026). Technical Quality and Students' Perception of Endodontic Preclinical Training Using Natural or LikeReal Artificial Teeth. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of errors across 408 teeth assessed visually and radiographically. Damage to marginal ridge occurred in 11.76% of natural teeth versus 1.96% of artificial teeth; fillings > 2 mm short occurred in 31.86% versus 11.27%, both significant at p < 0.05.

> "In the NTG, the occurrence of damage to the marginal ridge and filling > 2 mm short to the apex was more frequent than in the ATG; ledges and root fractures were more frequent in the ATG (p < 0.05)."
