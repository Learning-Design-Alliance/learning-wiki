## Evidence

### Pizzie 2025

Pizzie, R. G., Sortino, R. M., Kim, C. E.-Y., & Inghram, R. (2025). Math anxiety and science anxiety are associated with spatial cognition and STEM interest in deaf, hard of hearing, and hearing people. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00336-z

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Zero-order correlations in the full sample (Table 1): math anxiety r = 0.39 (p < 0.001), science anxiety r = 0.34 (p < 0.001), spatial anxiety r = 0.22 (p < 0.05) with MR commission error rate. The article reports "math, science, and spatial anxiety are related to MR commission error rate" with "more anxiety ... related to poorer performance".

> "Our data conﬁrmed our predictions that math, science, and spatial anxiety are related to MR commission error rate (Table1), and that more anxiety was related to poorer performance on the MR task."

### Pizzie 2025 (2)

Pizzie, R. G., Sortino, R. M., Kim, C. E.-Y., & Inghram, R. (2025). Math anxiety and science anxiety are associated with spatial cognition and STEM interest in deaf, hard of hearing, and hearing people. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00336-z

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Zero-order correlations in the full sample (Table 1) showed math anxiety r = -0.61, science anxiety r = -0.37, and spatial anxiety r = -0.33 with STEM interest, all p < 0.01. The article reports "All three types of anxiety are negatively related to interest in studying STEM".

> "We hypothesized that increased anxiety would be related to decreased interest in STEM. All three types of anxiety are negatively related to interest in studying STEM (Table 1)."
