Post-survey perceptions in the same study: usefulness for summarization, web research, policy development, and analysis did not differ by background (all ps > 0.05). NNES perceived competency marginally increased, Z = −1.89, p =.06, rrb = − 0.35; NES did not change, p =.48.

> "There was a statistically significant difference in the per-ceived usefulness for the writing process with non-native English speaking students reporting the tool to be statistically significantly more useful (mean rank = 15.87) than did native English speaking students (mean rank = 10.27), U = 47.00, p =.03, rrb = − 0.42."
