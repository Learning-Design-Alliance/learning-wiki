`q2 i3` Self-reported diagnostic reasoning skills improved significantly between Inventory 1 and 2 for total score (r = 0.45), Structure in Memory (r = 0.45) and Flexibility in Thinking (r = 0.37). [→ Ainge Lucinda E. 2025](#ainge-lucinda-e-2025)

## Evidence

### Ainge Lucinda E. 2025

Ainge Lucinda E., Edgar Amanda K., Kirkman Jacqueline M., Armitage James A. (2025). Developing clinical reasoning along the cognitive continuum: a mixed methods evaluation of a novel Clinical Diagnosis Assessment. BMC Medical Education, Volume 25, Article 31. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06613-6

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test comparing validated Diagnostic Thinking Inventory for Optometry responses before and after the third CDA in a voluntary cohort (55 completed Inventory 1, 42 completed Inventory 2). The article reports a "moderate level of effect" with printed r = 0.45 for total score and Structure in Memory, r = 0.37 for Flexibility in Thinking.

> "A Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed significant improvement in self-reported diagnostic reasoning skills between Inventory 1 and 2 with a moderate level of effect, for total score (Z = 2.930, p = 0.003, r = 0.45) and the two subdomains Structure in Memory (Z = 2.925, p = 0.003, r = 0.45) and Flexibility in Thinking (Z = 2.381, p = 0.003, r = 0.37)."
