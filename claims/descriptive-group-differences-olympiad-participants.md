### Tschisgale 2026

Tschisgale, P., & Neumann, K. (2026). “What If I Had Participated?”—A Machine Learning-Based Counterfactual Analysis of Non-Participants’ Success Potential in STEM Competitions: The Case of the German Physics Olympiad. Leibniz Institute for Science and Mathematics Education (IPN), Kiel, Germany. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.35151](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.35151)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparisons of 282 participants and 1,103 non-participants across 31 indicators (Table 1) show the largest effects "with |d| ≥ 1.0" favoring participants, e.g., physics self-concept d = +1.25, subject interest d = +1.28, physics/engineering skills d = +1.15, mathematics skills d = +1.03.

> "The largest effect sizes, with |d| ≥ 1.0, favored Physics Olympiad participants and were observed for STEM-related skills, physics-specific motivational-affective variables (subject interest in physics, topic interest in physics, and physics career motivation), as well as physics and mathematics self-concept."
