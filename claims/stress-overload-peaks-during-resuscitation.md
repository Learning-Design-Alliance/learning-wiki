## Evidence

### Hunziker S 2013

Hunziker S, Pagani S, Fasler K, Tschan F, Semmer NK, Marsch S. (2013). Impact of a stress coping strategy on perceived stress levels and performance during a simulated cardiopulmonary resuscitation: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-227X-13-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measurement of self-reported stress/overload at four time points (baseline, resuscitation, patient awakening, debriefing) in the simulation trial; overall mean was 10.9 (SD 1.8) on the 1–20 scale, peaking during resuscitation (about 15.9/15.6 in Table 2).

> "Stress/overload levels significantly increased during the resuscitation period as compared to the two periods before and after resuscitation (Figure 2)."
