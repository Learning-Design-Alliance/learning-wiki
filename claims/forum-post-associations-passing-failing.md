### Pardos 2019

Pardos, Z. A., & Horodyskyj, L. (2019). Analysis of Student Behaviour in Habitable Worlds Using Continuous Representation Visualization. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.18608/jla.2019.61.1](https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2019.61.1)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis adding Piazza forum posts (but not views) to the skip-gram input sequences, with separate projections per pass/no-pass group (Figure 7). The article reports that "Failing students' forum postings tend to have a slightly stronger association with applications," suggesting help-seeking during rather than before applications. No statistics are printed.
