## Evidence

### Schroder Hans S. 2017

Schroder Hans S., Fisher Megan E., Lin Yanli, Lo Sharon L., Danovitch Judith H., Moser Jason S. (2017). Neural evidence for enhanced attention to mistakes among school-aged children with a growth mindset. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2017.01.004

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational ERP analysis of 123 children performing a child-friendly go/no-go task; mindset entered as covariate in the Pe ANOVA. The Accuracy x Mindset interaction emerged (F(1, 122) = 4.80, p = 0.03, η2p = 0.04), and "higher growth mindset endorsement was associated with larger Pe difference" (r = 0.20, p = 0.03).
