## Evidence

### Hu 2017

Hu, Jingjing; Gao, Xuesong (Andy). (2017). Using think-aloud protocol in self-regulated reading research. Educational Research Review, v.22, pp. 181-193. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.09.004

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Denton et al. (2015), that "under-performing students reportedly used significantly less of monitoring strategies" than high performers, who also increased monitoring over time while low performers decreased it.

> "Denton et al. (2015) found that under-performing students reportedly used significantly less of monitoring strategies in reading than high performing ones."

### Hu 2017 (2)

Hu, Jingjing; Gao, Xuesong (Andy). (2017). Using think-aloud protocol in self-regulated reading research. Educational Research Review, v.22, pp. 181-193. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.09.004

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Linderholm and van den Broek (2002), a working-memory-capacity difference in metacognitive comments that appeared only for study reading, which the authors say confirms the process is context-dependent.

> "They found that low-WMC readers made fewer metacognitive comments while reading to study than high-WMC readers did, but the two groups of readers shared similar processes while reading for entertainment."
