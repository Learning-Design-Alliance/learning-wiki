Evidence¶

Behroozizad and Majidi 2015¶

Sorayya Behroozizad, Sudabeh Majidi. (2015). The Effect of Different Modes of English Captioning on EFL learners' General Listening Comprehension: Full text Vs. Keyword Captions. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.4p.115

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Paired sample t-test on the full-captions group's PET listening pre- and post-test scores (n=15). The pre-test mean was 20.80 and the post-test mean 45.86, an increase the authors report as "statistically significant increase in the full captions group".

"As Table 2 illustrated, there was a statistically significant increase in the full captions group after the treatment. The results of paired samples t-test indicates that the significant value (.000) was less than p<0.05 which revealed that the difference is significant."
