## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Contrary to the hypothesis that accompanying reflection would be more beneficial, the two reflection timings did not differ from each other on posttest diagnostic accuracy. [→ Fink Maximilian C. 2021](#fink-maximilian-c-2021)

## Evidence

### Fink Maximilian C. 2021

Fink Maximilian C., Heitzmann Nicole, Siebeck Matthias, Fischer Frank, Fischer Martin R. (2021). Learning to diagnose accurately through virtual patients: do reflection phases have an added benefit? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02937-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise comparison within the same ANCOVA on posttest diagnostic accuracy in the three-group experiment (N = 121): "accompanying reflection and concluding reflection did not differ from each other". No effect size printed for this contrast.

> "A pairwise comparison showed that, in contrast to H1.2, accompanying reflection and concluding reflection did not differ from each other, t(114) = 0.93, p = .356."
