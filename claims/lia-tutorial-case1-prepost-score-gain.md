### DeVore 2013

Pre/post evaluation in which six graduate students worked through the tutorial, scored with a rubric deliberated by three researchers. The Case 1 pretest average was "37.5% while the post1test average score is 91.7%".

> "Table 2 shows that the average score obtained on the pre1test for Case 1 (using the rubric in Table 1) is 37.5% while the post1test average score is 91.7%."
