## Evidence

### Koğar 2025

Koğar, H., Sayın, A., Şekercioğlu, G., Yılmaz Koğar, E., and Kafes, H. (2025). The Mediating Role of Academic Resilience and Cognitive Test Anxiety in the Association Between Smartphone Addiction and Academic Achievement. Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.70800

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped indirect-effect analysis in the 828-student chain mediation model. The article reports the anxiety-only path at "B = ‐0.096" and the chain resilience→anxiety path at "B = ‐0.009", both with confidence intervals excluding zero. Table 3 also prints the standardized indirect effects: β = −0.066 for the anxiety path, −0.038 for the resilience path and −0.007 for the resilience→anxiety chain.

> "(ii) the mediating role of cognitive test anxiety between smartphone addiction and academic achievement (B = ‐0.096, 95% CI [‐0.139, ‐0.054]), and (iii) the mediating role of academic resilience and cognitive test anxiety between smartphone addiction and academic achievement (B = ‐0.009, 95% CI [‐0.017, ‐0.004])."
