## Evidence

### Liu Min 2025

Liu Min, Dong Biao, Pang Xiaonan, Liu Bailong. (2025). Impact of Competency-Based Education and Multidisciplinary Team Teaching for Standardized Training Resident on Radiation Oncology. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-025-02686-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cohort comparison of 128 trainees (64 per group) assessed via rotation completion exams on a 100-point scale; between-group difference tested with independent t tests. The experimental group scored "98.3 ± 1.9 vs. 97.7 ± 1.3, P < 0.05".

> "The experimental group showed significant improvements in final exam scores (98.3 ± 1.9 vs. 97.7 ± 1.3, P < 0.05) and higher satisfaction scores (95.92 ± 2.80 vs. 87.73 ± 3.35, P < 0.001)."
