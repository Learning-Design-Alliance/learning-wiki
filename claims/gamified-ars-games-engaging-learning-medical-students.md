## Evidence

### Pettit RK 2015

Pettit RK, McCoy L, Kinney M, Schwartz FN. (2015). Student perceptions of gamified audience response system interactions in large group lectures and via lecture capture technology. BMC Medical Education 15:92. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0373-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

End-of-year 32-item questionnaire of 91 of 106 first-year osteopathic medical students (86% response) after 22 gamified TP microbiology lectures; Likert responses grouped positive vs neutral/negative and analyzed with Chi Square/Fisher's Exact tests. The article reports "a significant majority of the respondents agreed or strongly agreed that the games were engaging"; 82% agreed they gained an interest in microbiology.

> "A significant majority of the respondents agreed or strongly agreed that the games were engaging, and an effective learning tool."
