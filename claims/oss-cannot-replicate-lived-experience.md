The review reports that three studies [16, 25, 28] acknowledged this limitation; "psychological experiences such as stigma and long-term self-perception cannot be fully reproduced in short-term simulations," and one study suggested including individuals with lived experience in future activities.

> "Ruegsegger noted that chronic joint stress, altered gait, metabolic factors, and especially psychological experiences such as stigma and long-term self-perception cannot be fully reproduced in short-term simulations [28]."
