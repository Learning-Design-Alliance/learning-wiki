Related Claims¶

  • PBL in family medicine residency programs is associated with high learner satisfaction, though not always rated above lectures — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Of 4310 screened abstracts, 21 studies of PBL in PGME were included, spanning eight specialties and four Kirkpatrick outcome levels — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Student preference for CBL, PBL or TBL over traditional teaching was reported in 15 of 22 studies (68.1%), but only two studies used a controlled comparison with lecture — related
  • TBL is associated with higher satisfaction, positive attitudes and motivation versus lecture, but preferences are mixed — related
  • Most reviewed studies found active learning improved satisfaction and knowledge acquisition and was superior to traditional lectures — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Student satisfaction favours traditional teaching over PBL methods (pooled MD -0.14), conflicting with the skills and knowledge findings — reports the opposite
  • Trainees and observers reported generally positive experiences with seven tools in 10 studies (Kirkpatrick level 1) — related
