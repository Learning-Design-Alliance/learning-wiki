### Van Iddekinge 2024

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Psychometric meta-analysis of predictive-design studies of academic performance as a predictor of job performance. The article reports the "mean corrected r was .21 (CI [.18, .24])" across 114 samples (N = 18,676), with statistical artifacts accounting for only 35.2% of the variance in corrected rs.

> "Across 114 independent samples, the mean observed r was .16, and the mean corrected r was .21 (CI [.18, .24])."
