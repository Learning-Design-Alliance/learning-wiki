# At the school level, domain-identical pretests show exceptional prognostic power from grade 5 onward, but are weaker predictors at the student and classroom levels across the school career

## Evidence

### Stallasch 2024

Stallasch, S. E., Lüdtke, O., Artelt, C., Hedges, L. V., & Brunner, M. (2024). Single- and Multilevel Perspectives on Covariate Selection in Randomized Intervention Studies on Student Achievement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09898-7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Multilevel (three-level in grades 1-10, two-level in grades 11-12) design parameters meta-analyzed across the six German samples for the total student population, showing level-specific prognostic strength of IP.

> "From grade 5 on, IP was the strongest of all covariate types and showed exceptional prognostic properties at L3, where 98/78% of variance was explained in lower/upper secondary school. Across the entire school career, however, IP turned out to be a weaker predictor at both L1 and L2."
