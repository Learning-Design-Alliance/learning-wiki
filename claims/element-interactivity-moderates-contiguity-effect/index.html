Evidence¶

Ginns 2006¶

Ginns, P. (2006). Integrating information: A meta-analysis of the spatial contiguity and temporal contiguity effects. Learning and Instruction, 16(6), 511-525. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2006.10.001

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis within the meta-analysis of 50 effects, using the article's dichotomous coding of element interactivity (43 high vs 7 low element interactivity studies). The article reports "zcontrast = 3.09, p = 0.002" for the high-versus-low contrast.

"The mean weighted effect size was significantly larger for effects coded as using high element interactivity materials ( d = 0.78, 95% confidence interval 0.69 – 0.87) than for low element interactivity materials (d = 0.28, 95% confidence interval –0.03 – 0.58), zcontrast = 3.09, p = 0.002."
