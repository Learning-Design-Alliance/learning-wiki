## Subclaims
`q2 i?` NC and selection students have a significantly higher chance (non-overlapping confidence intervals) of a very good or good OSCE grade compared with waiting-list and special-quota students. [→ Tsikas 2023](#tsikas-2023)

## Evidence

### Tsikas 2023

Tsikas, Stefanos A.; Afshar, Kambiz. (2023). Clinical experience can compensate for inferior academic achievements in an undergraduate objective structured clinical examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04082-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ordered logistic regression (Table A, Online Appendix; Fig. 2 marginal effects at mean control values) predicting OSCE grade probabilities per admission quota. NC and selection students show "a significantly higher chance (the confidence intervals do not overlap)" of the top grades.

> "NC and selection students have a significantly higher chance (the confidence intervals do not overlap) to achieve a 'very good' (1) or 'good' (2) grade in the OSCE, compared to the waiting list group and special quotas."
