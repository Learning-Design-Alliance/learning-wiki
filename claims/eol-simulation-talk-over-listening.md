### Brezis Mayer 2017

Brezis Mayer, Lahat Yael, Frankel Meir, Rubinov Alan, Bohm Davina, Cohen Matan J, Koslowsky Meni, Shalomson Orit, Sprung Charles L, Perry-Mezare Henia, Yahalom Rina, Ziv Amitai. (2017). `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

RIAS-based analysis of the recorded simulation sessions from the first 10 workshops found providers' talk exceeded actors' talk "by a 2:1 ratio", and silence episodes over 5 seconds appeared in only 35% of clips, usually once. These are descriptive frequencies; no inferential test or effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Providers talked far more often than actors did by a 2:1 ratio, and episodes of silence lasting more than 5 s occurred in only 35% of video-clips (usually only once)."
