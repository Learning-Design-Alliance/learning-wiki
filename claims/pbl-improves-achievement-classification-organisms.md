`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test post-test study of 360 Form Three Biology students in eight classes from four Kenyan secondary schools. ANOVA on post-test Classification Achievement Test scores showed F-values above the critical value of 3.52, "indicating that there was a statistically significant difference between the two groups".
