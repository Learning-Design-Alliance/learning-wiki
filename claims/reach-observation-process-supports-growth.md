## Evidence

### Susan E. Sporte 2013

Susan E. Sporte, W. David Stevens, Kaleen Healey, Jennie Jiang, and Holly Hart. (2013). Teacher Evaluation in Practice: Implementing Chicago's REACH Students. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-evaluation-practice-implementing-chicagos-reach-students

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

District-wide surveys of Chicago principals and teachers during the first year of REACH implementation. The survey found "76 percent of teachers said the evaluation process at their school encourages their professional growth," with an identical percentage of administrators expecting instructional improvement.

> "On the survey, 76 percent of teachers said the evaluation process at their school encourages their professional growth. Similarly, 76 percent of administrators reported believing that the observation process would result in instructional improvement at their school"
