## Evidence

### Sigayret 2022

Sigayret, K., Tricot, A., & Blanc, N. (2022). Unplugged or plugged-in programming learning: A comparative experimental study. Computers and Education, 184, 104505. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104505

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post comparison of the Motivation and self-belief Test found "a significant drop in motivation in this group (F(1, 215) = 6.15; p = .014; d = 0.34)" in the unplugged group only.

> "On the other hand, the results showed a significant drop in motivation in this group (F(1, 215) = 6.15; p = .014; d = 0.34). In the plugged-in group, the pre-test/post-test evolution was never significant, neither for the total scores nor for the results in the three different item categories."
