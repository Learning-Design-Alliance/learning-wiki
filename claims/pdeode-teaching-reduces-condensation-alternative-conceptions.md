## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bayram Costu 2011

Bayram Costu, Alipasa Ayas, Mansoor Niaz. (2011). Investigating the effectiveness of a POE-based teaching activity on students' understanding of condensation. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-011-9169-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-, post- and delayed post-test analysis of 52 first-year primary science education students' responses to a five-item Condensation Conceptual Test. The article reports that "most students experienced conceptual change"; for the 2nd SAC the proportion decreased from 31 to 2% for the pre- and post-tests.

> "students' alternative conceptions changed from the pre-test, with improvement noted in the post-, and delayed post-test scores. That is, most students experienced conceptual change."
