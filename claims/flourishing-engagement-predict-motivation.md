## Evidence

### Chen Xiang 2025

Chen Xiang, Samad Sarminah, Kim Wansoo. (2025). Teacher competence and students’ motivation for learning in Chinese higher education: mediating roles of psychological flourishing and student engagement, and the moderating role of AI integration. BMC Psychology, 13, 1378. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03674-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

In the PLS-SEM structural model of the 467-student survey, flourishing showed the stronger path to motivation, "β = 0.417, t = 8.745, p < 0.001", with f² = 0.296 (medium-to-large).

> "ELF (H3: β = 0.417, t = 8.745, p < 0.001) and ELE (H6: β = 0.225, t = 5.331, p < 0.001) each positively influenced SELM"
