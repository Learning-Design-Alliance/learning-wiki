## Evidence

### Shaw Tarren J. 2019

Shaw Tarren J., Yang Suann, Nash Troy R., Pigg Rachel M., Grim Jeffrey M. (2019). Knowing is half the battle: Assessments of both student perception and performance are necessary to successfully evaluate curricular transformation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210030

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Sentiment analysis of trigrams extracted from free-response SALG questions, with positive vs. negative frequency compared across years using chi-square tests with Bonferroni corrections. The article reports "positive sentiments were more abundant than negative sentiments in all years" (Fig 4).

> "Regarding pedagogy, positive sentiments were more abundant than negative sentiments in all years, and this proportion was higher in the latter two years (p<0.0001)."
