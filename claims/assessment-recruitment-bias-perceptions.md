## Evidence

### Woolf K 2016

Woolf K, Rich A, Viney R, Needleman S, Griffin A. (2016). Perceived causes of differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical training: a national qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013429

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the qualitative dataset, IMG and BME UKG trainees described subtle recruitment bias, perceiving rejection attributed to factors like experience when "your CV actually might match your colleague or even be better than your colleague's".

> "Participants from all groups believed recruitment processes were vulnerable to bias and some UKG trainers had concerns about employing IMGs."

### Woolf K 2016 (2)

Woolf K, Rich A, Viney R, Needleman S, Griffin A. (2016). Perceived causes of differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical training: a national qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013429

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

UKGs were more critical of ARCPs, which they felt could depend on colleagues signing them off; IMGs were more critical of Royal College examinations and worried accents would disadvantage them.

> "Royal College examinations were generally perceived as more rigorous and fair than Annual Review of Competence Progression (ARCP) assessments and recruitment."
