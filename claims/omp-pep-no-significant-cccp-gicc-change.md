## Evidence

### Jeong Hye Won 2021

Jeong Hye Won, Ju Deok, Choi Myoung Lee, Kim Suhyun. (2021). Development and Evaluation of a Preceptor Education Program Based on the One-Minute Preceptor Model: Participatory Action Research. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182111376

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

One-group pre-post study of 30 preceptor nurses at Korean tertiary hospitals completing the eight-week OMP-based PEP; repeated measures analysis and t-tests. The CCCP rose from "3.74 (±0.42) to 3.76 (±0.32)" with no statistically significant change (t = 0.29, p = 0.777 in Table 2).

> "The CCCP increased from 3.74 (±0.42) to 3.76 (±0.32), and among the subdomains, the facilitator and educator scores also increased after application of the program, but this was not statistically significant."

### Jeong Hye Won 2021 (2)

Jeong Hye Won, Ju Deok, Choi Myoung Lee, Kim Suhyun. (2021). Development and Evaluation of a Preceptor Education Program Based on the One-Minute Preceptor Model: Participatory Action Research. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182111376

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Same one-group pre-post quantitative evaluation of the OMP-based PEP with 30 preceptor nurses. Communication competence on the GICC-15 "decreased from 51.67 (±4.77) points to 51.41 (±4.73) points", not statistically significant (t = 0.44, p = 0.666 in Table 2).

> "The GICC-15 decreased from 51.67 (±4.77) points to 51.41 (±4.73) points, but this also was not statistically significant."
