# Perceived usefulness of AI and intent to continue using it differ significantly across challenge domains, with academic tasks rated most useful

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Kruskal-Wallis tests showed significant differences across domains for usefulness (χ2 = 15.14, p=0.002) and intent to continue (χ2 = 11.27, p=0.010), but not satisfaction (χ2 = 6.74, p=0.08). [→ Nourian 2026](#nourian-2026)
`q2 i?` Post hoc tests showed students find AI significantly more useful for academic than socio-cultural tasks (p=0.0048) and are more likely to continue using it for academic tasks (p=0.0054). [→ Nourian 2026 (2)](#nourian-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Nourian 2026

Understanding How International Students in the U.S. Are Using Conversational AI to Support Cross-Cultural Adaptation. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15127 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.15127](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.15127)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis tests on Likert ratings of usefulness, satisfaction, and intent to continue across the four challenge domains in the survey of 60 international students. The test shows "a significant difference between usefulness" and intent of continuation across domains but not satisfaction.

> "there is a significant difference between usefulness (𝜒 2 = 15.14, p-value=0.002) and intent of continuation (𝜒 2 = 11.27, p-value=0.010), but not for the satisfaction variable (𝜒 2 = 6.74, p-value=0.08)."

### Nourian 2026 (2)

Nourian, L.; Callis, A.; Patterson, S.; Miao, J.; Heard, J.; Tigwell, G. W. (2026). Understanding How International Students in the U.S. Are Using Conversational AI to Support Cross-Cultural Adaptation. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15127

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post hoc pairwise tests following the Kruskal-Wallis analysis in the survey study. The article reports "Students are significantly more likely to continue using AI for Academic tasks (p=0.0054)" because usefulness is higher for academic than socio-cultural tasks; logistical vs academic (p=0.28) and socio-cultural vs psychological (p=1) were not significant.

> "Students are significantly more likely to continue using AI for Academic tasks (p=0.0054) because it is significantly more useful for those tasks (p=0.0048)."
