## Evidence

### Otiato Maxwell X. 2024

Otiato Maxwell X., Ma Runzhuo, Chu Timothy N., Wong Elyssa Y., Wagner Christian, Hung Andrew J. (2024). Surgical gestures to evaluate apical dissection of robot-assisted radical prostatectomy. Journal of Robotic Surgery 18(1):245. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-01902-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis of gesture usage against five DART domains across the 78 annotated AD videos; correlations were both positive and negative, "ranged from -0.397 to 0.316" (p < 0.05).

> "Correlations between the surgical gestures and technical skills assessments ranged from -0.397 to 0.316 using Spearman’s correlation coefficient (p < 0.05, Fig. 1b)."

### Otiato Maxwell X. 2024 (2)

Otiato Maxwell X., Ma Runzhuo, Chu Timothy N., Wong Elyssa Y., Wagner Christian, Hung Andrew J. (2024). Surgical gestures to evaluate apical dissection of robot-assisted radical prostatectomy. Journal of Robotic Surgery 18(1):245. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-01902-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary association analyses on the same 78-video cohort: greater retraction proportion linked to lower total DART (p < 0.01), and higher gesture count and time per gesture linked to lower Efficiency scores (p < 0.01).

> "The total count of gestures and time spent per gesture was significantly associated with the Efficiency domain (p < 0.01). Total DART scores were significantly associated with the proportion of retraction gestures (p < 0.01)."
