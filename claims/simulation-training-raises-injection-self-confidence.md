## Evidence

### Chatterji R 2024

Chatterji R, Foote J, Fry M, Erwin A, Crutcher J, Kesto W. (2024). Simulated subacromial injection instruction improves accuracy and skill level: a model for musculoskeletal procedural training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05456-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-testing survey in the randomized cadaveric study of 24 primary care physicians. Self-confidence was "significantly higher in the intervention group (3.67) compared to the control group (3.08)"; overall, participants rating themselves 4 or higher rose from 29.2% before to 58.3% after the study.

> "Post-testing evaluation of self-confidence level on a scale of 1–5 was found to be significantly higher in the intervention group (3.67) compared to the control group (3.08) with (p = 0.04)."
