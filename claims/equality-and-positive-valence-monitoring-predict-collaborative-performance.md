## Evidence

### Haataja 2021

Haataja, E., Malmberg, J., Dindar, M., & Järvelä, S. (2021). The pivotal role of monitoring for collaborative problem solving seen in interaction, performance, and interpersonal physiology. Metacognition and Learning, 17, 241–268. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09279-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

GEE analysis of video-coded monitoring interactions from 19 groups of three (N = 57) during a business simulation's exploration phase. The best-fitting model showed "higher equality of participation in monitoring interactions and more monitoring with positive valence predicted better task performance following the transition episodes." Both were significant positive predictors.
