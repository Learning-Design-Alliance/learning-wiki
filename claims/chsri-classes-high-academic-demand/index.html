David Stevens 2008¶

David Stevens, Sue Sporte, Sara Ray Stoelinga, Alissa Bolz. (2008). Lessons from High Performing Small High Schools in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/lessons-high-performing-small-high-schools-chicago

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative classroom observation study of 23 English/language arts classes across three CHSRI schools, spring 2008. Researchers categorized activities by skill type and found "Students spent at least part of the period in activities aimed at building high-level skills in 21 of the 23 observed classes."
