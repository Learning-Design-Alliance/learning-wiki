## Evidence

### Ana Katalinic 2026

Ana Katalinic, Vanja Slavuj, Danijela Jaksic. (2026). Artificial Intelligence in Online Education: A Systematic Review of Its Impact on Learner Engagement and Satisfaction. Educ. Sci. 2026, 16, 389. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16030389

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review reports, citing Roca et al. (2024), that chatbots integrated into LMS offering personalized assistance and timely feedback were associated with higher satisfaction. The review attributes the finding to the included study.

> "Learners generally report higher satisfaction and engagement when the chatbot is seen as a useful tool that supports their academic progress"
