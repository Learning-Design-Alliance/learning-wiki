Evidence¶

Debra Kamps 2015¶

Debra Kamps, Howard Wills, Harriett Dawson-Bannister, Linda Heitzman-Powell, Esther Kottwitz, Blake Hansen, and Kandace Fleming (2015). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams "CW-FIT" Efficacy Trial Outcomes. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300714565244

q4 · i? · causal · r2

GLMM analysis of reprimand frequencies in the randomized trial. Treatment teachers "decreased reprimands from 7.48 in baseline to 4.45" while comparison teachers increased from 8.42 to 9.49 (p = .01).

"For reprimands, there is also a significant interaction, F(1, 1796) = 53.48, p < .001. The treatment classroom teachers decreased reprimands from 7.48 in baseline to 4.45 during CW-FIT"
