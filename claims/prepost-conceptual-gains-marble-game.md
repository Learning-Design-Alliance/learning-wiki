## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' scores on open-ended conceptual questions about equilibrium, diffusion, the Nernst equation, Fick's law and the Boltzmann factor improved substantially from pretest to posttest. [→ Peter Hugo Nelson 2012](#peter-hugo-nelson-2012)

## Evidence

### Peter Hugo Nelson 2012

Peter Hugo Nelson. (2012). Teaching introductory STEM with the Marble Game. [doi:10.1016/j.bpj.2012.11.2943](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpj.2012.11.2943)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Short-answer pre-post test in Biophysics (Spring 2012, N = 13), with the pretest on the first day of class and the posttest in the final exam, scored for volunteered correct concepts. No student identified Brownian motion on the pretest while "4/13 students included this in the post-test answers", and the article reports substantial improvement on all five questions.

> "In the pre-test no students identified “Brownian motion” whereas 4/13 students included this in the post-test answers."
