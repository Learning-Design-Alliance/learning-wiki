### Gintaras Dūda 2025

Gintaras Dūda (2025). Teaching STEM Courses using Ignatian Pedagogy. Creighton University. [doi:10.15365/2164-7666.1525](https://doi.org/10.15365/2164-7666.1525)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the author's sophomore classical mechanics course, after finding students scored dismally on the CCMI and weekly quizzes, he administered mini competency exams spread through the semester with unlimited re-takes. "All students in the course successfully demonstrated mastery of all eight competencies." No sample size or effect size is printed.

> "All students in the course successfully demonstrated mastery of all eight competencies, although several students took four or ﬁve attempts on the more diﬃcult ones."
