Winarno 2018¶

Learning-impact questionnaire of the mDPBL group (n=136, Table 5). All DPBL items showed higher mean scores, e.g. liking DPBL teaching at 4.12 and always discussing with the group at 4.12; the boredom item scored 2.09.
