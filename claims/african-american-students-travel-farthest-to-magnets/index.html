Elaine M. Allensworth 2000¶

Elaine M. Allensworth, Todd Rosenkranz. (2000). Access to Magnet Schools in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/access-magnet-schools-chicago

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of CPS student record files, fall semester 1999 (Figure 15), computing average distance traveled to each magnet elementary school by student race/ethnicity. The report states "While the average distance traveled for whites, Latinos, and Asians is around 2.6 to 2.7 miles, the average distance traveled by African-Americans is 3.27 miles."

"While the average distance traveled for whites, Latinos, and Asians is around 2.6 to 2.7 miles, the average distance traveled by African-Americans is 3.27 miles (20 percent higher than the next highest distance traveled, which is 2.69 miles by Latinos)."
