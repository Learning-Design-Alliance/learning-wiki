Evidence¶

Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day 2003¶

Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day, with the assistance of David Wakelyn. (2003). External Support to Schools on Probation: Getting a Leg Up? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED474395

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

From the study's implementation analysis of partner and probation manager assistance; the authors judge that the "intensity of assistance delivered to probation schools is, we believe, simply inadequate". Train-the-trainer extensions were observed to be much too weak, while on-site literacy coordinators held more promise.

"While varying among providers, the intensity of assistance delivered to probation schools is, we believe, simply inadequate to address the substantial needs of the individuals and organizations concerned"

Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day 2003 (2)¶

Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day, with the assistance of David Wakelyn. (2003). External Support to Schools on Probation: Getting a Leg Up? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED474395

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Based on respondent interviews and case study school observations; the authors report that "lines of communication generally flowed from central office personnel to providers" with little feedback, and that similar communication problems were mirrored in case study schools.

"the lines of communication generally flowed from central office personnel to providers with little opportunity for feedback or for open communication and collective problem-solving among external partners, among probation managers, or between partners and probation managers"

Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day 2003 (3)¶

Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day, with the assistance of David Wakelyn. (2003). External Support to Schools on Probation: Getting a Leg Up? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED474395

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

From the study's provider-strategy analysis; despite the goal of developing capacity throughout the school unit, the study found providers "generally lacked a comprehensive intervention or dissemination strategy" grounded in organizational change understanding.

"support providers generally lacked a comprehensive intervention or dissemination strategy based on an articulated theory of action or understanding of organizational change"
