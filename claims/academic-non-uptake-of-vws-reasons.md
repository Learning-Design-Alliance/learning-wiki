Authors' informal poll of their own professional members in the Concluding Comments section, explaining MICA's failure to engage a broader segment of the astrophysics community. The printed poll size is "about 50"; the majority answer was lack of time.

> "We have polled our professional members (about 50) who have not continued to use VWs beyond their initial visit as to why. The majority answer was that they do fid the technology interesting, but simply do not have the time to invest in exploring it now."
