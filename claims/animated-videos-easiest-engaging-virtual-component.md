`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Teacher interview and survey data from Study 1's Results section: teachers "consistently found that the videos were the easiest activity to implement during distance learning," reported students connecting with characters across genders and ethnicities, and called the episodes "one of the strongest components of the program."

> "Another teacher agreed, characterizing the episodes as “a great equalizer” that “created the most equal platform for special education, English learners, and other abilities to all come together.”"
