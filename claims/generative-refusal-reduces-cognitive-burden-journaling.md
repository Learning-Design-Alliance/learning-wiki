# AI-generated questions instead of draft text significantly reduce actors' cognitive burden during character journaling

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In a 14-day randomized crossover field study, AI-assisted journaling with question-only prompts produced a significant decrease in cognitive burden compared with unassisted freewriting. [→ Sora Kang 2026](#sora-kang-2026)

## Evidence

### Sora Kang 2026

Sora Kang. (2026). Stop Writing for Me: Generative Refusal in AI Tools for Thought. In Proceedings of CHI 2026 Workshop on Tools for Thought. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24751

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Field study of Actor's Note with 29 professional and student actors in a randomized crossover design comparing an AI-assisted phase to an unassisted freewriting phase, with daily surveys. The AI-assisted phase showed "a significant decrease in Cognitive Burden", with the article printing an effect size of eta-squared = .381 (p < .001), a large effect.

> "Despite maintaining the friction of writing, results showed a sig- nificant decrease in Cognitive Burden (𝜂2 𝑝 =.381, 𝑝<.001)"
