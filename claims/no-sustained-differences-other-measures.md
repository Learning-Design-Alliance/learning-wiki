## Subclaims
`q3 i?` ANCOVAs found no significant intervention-control differences on vocabulary, working memory, spelling, reading comprehension or reading speed, though small non-significant effect sizes favoured the intervention group on reading comprehension (d = 0.18) and reading speed (d = 0.26). ## Evidence

### Wolff 2016

Ulrika Wolff. (2016). Effects of a Randomized Reading Intervention Study Aimed at 9-Year-Olds: A 5-Year Follow-up. Dyslexia, 22, 85–100. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1529

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Grade-8 ANCOVAs (Table 3) controlling for grade-3 scores: reading speed d = 0.26 (p = .20), reading comprehension d = 0.18 (p = .40), spelling d = 0.02, vocabulary d = 0.14, working memory d = 0.26. None of these reached significance; only word decoding did.

> "There were no signiﬁcant differences between the intervention and the control group on vocabulary, working memory, spelling, reading comprehension and reading speed. However, there were small effect sizes in favour of the intervention group on reading comprehension and reading speed."
