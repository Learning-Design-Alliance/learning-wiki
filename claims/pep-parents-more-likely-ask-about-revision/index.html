Miller 2016¶

Miller, S., Davison, J., Yohanis, J., Sloan, S., Gildea, A. and Thurston, A. (2016). Texting Parents: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581121

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Secondary parent-outcome analysis from telephone interviews collected by the project delivery team and linked to trial data by the evaluation team. The report identifies asking about revision as the sole parent outcome with a positive effect unlikely to have occurred by chance; no effect size is printed.

"the only positive effect that was unlikely to have occurred by chance was in relation to the likelihood of parents asking their child about revising for upcoming tests, where intervention parents were more likely to do so than control parents."
