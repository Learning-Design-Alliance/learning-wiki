### Fosli CA 2025

Fosli CA, Sperling S, Jensen RD, Skaarup SH. (2025). In-situ simulation-based team training reduces incidence of negative events during bronchoscopy. A prospective educational intervention cohort study. Respiratory Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12931-025-03205-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kirkpatrick level 2 questionnaire rating stress on a ten-point scale before and after training across scenarios from small nose bleeding to respiratory and cardiac arrest; a null result with no effect size printed. The authors note life-threatening events "remained profoundly stressful".
