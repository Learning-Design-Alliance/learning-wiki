### Starch and & Elliott 1911

Daniel Starch and Edward C. Elliott. (1911). Reliability of the Grading of High-School Work in English. The School Review. http://www.journals.uchicago.edu/t-and-c

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fig. 11 plots each teacher's mark on paper A against the same teacher's mark on paper B. If teachers marked both papers correspondingly high or low the curves would be parallel; instead the variations are "by no means uniform".

> "Thus paper B is marked on the average 8 points lower than paper A, yet nineteen of the one hundred and forty-two teachers marked it higher than paper A, and twenty-three marked it 15 or more points lower than paper A."
