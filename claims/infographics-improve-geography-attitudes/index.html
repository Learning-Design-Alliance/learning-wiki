Çifçi 2016¶

Taner Çifçi. (2016). Effects of Infographics on Students Achievement and Attitude towards Geography Lessons. Journal of Education and Learning, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v5n1p154

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent samples t-test of post-treatment ASGL scores for the pooled experimental groups (X = 2.96) versus control groups (X = 2.25) in the Solomon Four Group study, a difference "in favor of the experimental group".

"When the analysis results given in Table 13 are examined , it is seen that there is a statistically significant difference between the experimental and the control group [t (111) = 8.202; p < .05]. This difference is in favor of the experimental group."

Çifçi 2016 (2)¶

Taner Çifçi. (2016). Effects of Infographics on Students Achievement and Attitude towards Geography Lessons. Journal of Education and Learning, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v5n1p154

q2 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA of post-treatment attitude scores across the four groups (between-groups sum of squares 14.2; within-groups 23.4), reporting a "significant difference between the post-treatment attitude scores of the students in the experimental and the control groups".

"According to the data analysis seen in Table 14, there is a significant difference between the post-treatment attitude scores of the students in the experimental and the control groups [f (1-111) = 67,27; p < .05]."
