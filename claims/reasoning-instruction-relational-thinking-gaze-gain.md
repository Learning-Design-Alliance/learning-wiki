## Evidence

### Belén C. Guerra-Carrillo and Silvia A. Bunge 2018

Belén C. Guerra-Carrillo and Silvia A. Bunge. (2018). Eye gaze patterns reveal how reasoning skills improve with experience. npj Science of Learning 3:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0035-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian mixed regressions on the relational thinking gaze metric (total fixation duration on relevant relations); the article reports the data are 3.66 times more likely under the Group×Time model than the main-effects model, with extreme evidence for improvement in the Reasoning group.

> "the interaction model was best supported by the relational thinking data (BF 10 ≈ 7.27 ± 1.51%)"

### Belén C. Guerra-Carrillo and Silvia A. Bunge 2018 (2)

Belén C. Guerra-Carrillo and Silvia A. Bunge. (2018). Eye gaze patterns reveal how reasoning skills improve with experience. npj Science of Learning 3:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0035-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian mixed regression on the relational integration metric (saccades between the two relevant scales); the interaction was not supported, though the Reasoning group alone showed moderate evidence of improved integration.

> "there was moderate evidence against including the interaction term to model the relational integration data (BF 10 ≈ 0.17 ± 2.32%)"
