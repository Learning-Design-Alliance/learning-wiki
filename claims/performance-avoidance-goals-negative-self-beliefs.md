## Evidence

### Mason 2012

Mason, L., Boscolo, P., Tornatora, M. C., & Ronconi, L. (2012). Besides knowledge: a cross-sectional study on the relations between epistemic beliefs, achievement goals, self-beliefs, and achievement in science. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-012-9210-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM path analysis of fifth-grade data (n = 213) showed performance-avoidance goals strongly negatively predicted self-concept (β = -0.65) and self-efficacy (β = -0.46). Similar negative effects on both self-beliefs emerged at eighth and eleventh grades.

> "Mastery goals (b = 0.11, t = 2.14) also had a positive effect on self-concept, whereas performance-avoidance goals (b = -0.65, t = -13.12) had a negative effect."
