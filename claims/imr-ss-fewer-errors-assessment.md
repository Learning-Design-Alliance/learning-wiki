## Evidence

### Qian He 2025

Qian He, Jiahao Li, Ye Xiao, Jingjing Tian, Ningbo Mao, Sheng Lu, Yu Zhang, Yu Zhao. (2025). An immersive mixed reality surgical self-training system for precise pedicle screw placement: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07978-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Safety errors (pedicle breaches, collisions) were auto-flagged by the IMR system during the exercise session: 0/16 IMR vs 4/16 control, p = 0.051, not significant. In the assessment session, two blinded spine surgeons reviewed video and recorded omitted or misused steps as procedural errors; the IMR group's median of 0 errors versus the control group's 2 was significant (p < 0.01), and "the IMR group committed significantly fewer errors in all categories" (instrument-usage, node-task, benchmark).
