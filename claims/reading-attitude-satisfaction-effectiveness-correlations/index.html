Evidence¶

Shih 2013¶

Shih, R.-C., Cho, C.-L., Tsai, C.-C., & Lou, S.-J. (2013). Employing Microsoft Live@Edu Cloud Platform to Assist in Teaching Chinese Reading for Junior High School Students. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018038

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Pearson product-moment correlations among the four factors showed reading attitude–effectiveness r=.473** (p<.01), satisfaction–effectiveness r=.435* (p<.05), and attitude–satisfaction r=.649*** (p<.001), indicating significantly positive associations.

"The results show that there are significantly positive correlations among reading attitude, learning satisfaction, and learning effectiveness of reading, suggesting that the higher the students' reading attitude, learning, the higher the learning effectiveness."

Shih 2013 (2)¶

Shih, R.-C., Cho, C.-L., Tsai, C.-C., & Lou, S.-J. (2013). Employing Microsoft Live@Edu Cloud Platform to Assist in Teaching Chinese Reading for Junior High School Students. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018038

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Multiple regression analysis showed reading attitude obtained a positive β value, indicating learning effectiveness of reading can be positively predicted by reading attitude; no specific coefficient value is printed.

"Also, the results of multiple regression analysis show that reading attitude obtained positive value of β, indicating learning effectiveness of reading can be positively predicted by the reading attitude."
