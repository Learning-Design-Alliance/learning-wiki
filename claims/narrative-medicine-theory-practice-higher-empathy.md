# Nursing students who received narrative medicine education combining theory with clinical practice showed higher empathic ability than students receiving regular courses

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yang Ningxi 2018

Yang Ningxi, Xiao Han, Cao Yingnan, Li Shiyue, Yan Hong, Wang Yifang. (2018). Does narrative medicine education improve nursing students’ empathic abilities and academic achievement? A randomised controlled trial. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060518781476

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomised controlled trial of 180 nursing students in six class clusters; post-hoc LSD comparisons after one-way ANOVA showed Group 3 (theory plus practice) scored higher than Group 1 on empathic ability, with "significant differences between Group 1 and Group 3" (P<0.05). Mean T3 empathy: 110 (13.51) vs 104.79 (11.82).

> "However, the LSD test revealed significant differences between Group 1 and Group 3 in internship scores, graduation thesis achievement and empathic ability (P<0.05)."
