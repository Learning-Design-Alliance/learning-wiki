### J. E. Wallace Wallin 1905

J. E. Wallace Wallin. (1905). The Doctrine of Formal Discipline: Two Neglected Instances of Transfer of Training. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A secondary observation from experiments in which figures were reversed in the direct and indirect visual fields; the author calls it less significant but a pertinent suggestion, an acquired foveal disposition spreading to adjacent retinal elements.

> "But after practice with certain figures it sometimes happened that the figures (the cube, pyramid and book) reversed most readily when a point outside the figures was fixated. This furnishes an instance of the transference of fixation motives attaching to the fovea to the peripheral retina."
