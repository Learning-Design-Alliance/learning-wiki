### Eckardt F 2026

Eckardt F, Gerhardt M, Priglinger S. (2026). Teaching ophthalmic microsurgical skills: conventional microscopy versus heads-up visualization. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10269-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Task-level paired analysis within the same crossover study; only the navigation task differed significantly between systems in both modules. Table 2 reports Cohen's d of -0.43 (cataract navigation) and -0.24 (vitrectomy navigation).

> "If the individual tasks within the cataracts and vitrectomy modules are considered separately, a significant difference was observed only in the navigation task, both in the cataract and in the vitrectomy module (p = 0.023, p = 0.025)."
