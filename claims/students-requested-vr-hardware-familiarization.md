Savino Sandro, Mormando Giulia, Saia Giorgia, Da Dalt Liviana, Chang Todd P., Bressan Silvia. (2023). SIMPEDVR: using VR in teaching pediatric emergencies to undergraduate students—a pilot study. European Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-023-05254-z

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative triangulation of student comments with questionnaire findings in the pilot cohort. The authors conclude that an assumption that millennials and younger students can navigate VR hardware confidently in a healthcare training setting should not be made; no quantitative effect size is printed.
