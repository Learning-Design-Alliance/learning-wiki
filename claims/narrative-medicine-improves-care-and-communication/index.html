Evidence¶

Dai Dongjun 2026¶

Dai Dongjun, Fan Yuzhi, Zhang Wenbao, Wei Qichun. (2026). The effect of narrative medicine-based education on academic performance and humanistic communication in residency training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53552-x

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of 3 randomized controlled studies (128 vs 128 residents), humanistic care ability mainly measured with the Caring Ability Inventory or Mini-CEX. The pooled effect size SMD = 2.62 (95% CI 0.26 to 4.99) was significant with very high heterogeneity (I2 = 96.6%).

"narrative medicine education was associated with improved humanistic care ability (3 studies with 128 residents in narrative medicine group and 128 in LBL group, SMD = 2.62, 95% CI 0.26 to 4.99, P = 0.03; I2 = 96.6%)"

Dai Dongjun 2026 (2)¶

Dai Dongjun, Fan Yuzhi, Zhang Wenbao, Wei Qichun. (2026). The effect of narrative medicine-based education on academic performance and humanistic communication in residency training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53552-x

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of 7 randomized controlled studies (197 vs 196 residents), communication predominantly measured by the SEGUE framework. The pooled effect size SMD = 2.19 (95% CI 0.21 to 4.17) was significant with very high heterogeneity (I2 = 94.3%).

"and doctor-patient communication (7 studies with 197 residents in narrative medicine group and 196 in LBL group, SMD = 2.19, 95% CI 0.21 to 4.17, P = 0.03; I2 = 94.3%)"
