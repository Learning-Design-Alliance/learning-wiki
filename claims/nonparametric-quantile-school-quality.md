## Evidence

### Kim Stienstra 2024

Kim Stienstra, Antonie Knigge & Ineke Maas. (2024). Gene-environment interaction analysis of school quality and educational inequality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00225-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multigroup (non-parametric) gene-environment interaction across quantiles of school quality (Supplementary Figs. 1–4), testing for threshold effects. The article reports "There is more genetic vari ance in the lowest-quality schools and less in the other four quantiles", with only the first-vs-fourth quantile Wald test significant.
