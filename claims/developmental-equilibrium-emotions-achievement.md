### Lichtenfeld 2022

Lichtenfeld, S., Pekrun, R., Marsh, H. W., Nett, U., & Reiss, K. (2022). Achievement Emotions and Elementary School Children’s Academic Performance: Longitudinal Models of Developmental Ordering. Journal of Educational Psychology, in press. https://osf.io/ywt8q/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Comparing freely estimated versus constrained reciprocal effects models for the three-wave learning-related emotions, fit reductions were small (ΔCFI < .007, ΔRMSEA < .002, ΔSRMR < .009), supporting equivalence of effects across one-year intervals. Constrained models were used for interpretation as more parsimonious and precise.

> "these results showed that both autoregressive paths and linkages between emotions and achievement remained stable across the elementary school years, providing evidence for developmental equilibrium."
