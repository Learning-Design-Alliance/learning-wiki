q2 · i? · design · r3

Blind reliability audit in which an independent human second coder, using no AI assistance, coded a stratified sample of 75 rows (25% of the dataset) covering all 20 universities and all 15 cases. The study reports "Unweighted Cohen’s kappa reached 0.395".

"The independent human second coder agreed with 43 of 75 primary overall classifications (57.3%). Unweighted Cohen’s kappa reached 0.395 (bootstrap 95% CI [0.244, 0.539]), which indicates moderate agreement on the demanding five-category nominal scale."
