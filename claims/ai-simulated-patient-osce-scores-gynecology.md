### Gao Lingling 2026

Gao Lingling, Zhang Li, Zhang Yanxin, Wu Min, Xu Jianbo, Lu Dan. (2026). Utilizing generative artificial intelligence to create simulated patient for history taking in gynecology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09219-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the same RCT (n = 40), two faculty examiners graded a station-specific OSCE checklist covering interview process domains. The trial reports "significantly higher total OSCE scores" with Cohen's d = 1.10 favoring the AI group.

> "The AI group achieved significantly higher total OSCE scores compared to the control group (89.43 ± 3.76 vs. 85.38 ± 3.62, p = 0.0010, Cohen’s d = 1.10)."
