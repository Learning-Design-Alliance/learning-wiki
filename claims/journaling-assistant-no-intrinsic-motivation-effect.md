# The LLM-based journaling assistant shows no significant effect on students' intrinsic motivation for creating a learning journal

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No significant effect of the journaling assistant on intrinsic motivation was found, and no significant interaction with the course. [→ Sven Scheu 2026](#sven-scheu-2026)

## Evidence

### Sven Scheu 2026

Sven Scheu, Simone N. Loeffler and Alexander Maedche. (2026). Designing a mobile chatbot-based learning journaling system for intrinsic motivation and engagement. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00589-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Type-II ANOVA on IMI enjoyment among the 157 active participants in the randomized field experiment found no significant assistant effect and no significant course × assistant interaction. Results over all participants were similar (F(1, 175) = 0.48, p = 0.49; interaction F(1, 175) = 0.40, p = 0.53).

> "Regarding H2, no significant effects could be found for the assistant ( F (1, 153) = 0 .18,p =0 .67). There were also no significant interaction effects between the features ( F (1, 153) = 0 .09,p =0 .77)."
