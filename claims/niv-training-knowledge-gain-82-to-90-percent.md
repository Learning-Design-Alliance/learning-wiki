# Nursing students' and professionals' NIV knowledge rose from 82.2% correct preintervention to 90.2% postsimulation after a combined theoretical-practical and simulation-based intervention, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Evidence

### Pla‐Canalda Elsa 2025

Pla‐Canalda Elsa, Jiménez‐Herrera María F., Fernández‐Sáez José, Concha‐Martínez Pablo, and Martínez‐Segura Estrella. (2025). Impact of Educational Intervention on Noninvasive Ventilation (NIV) in Nurses and Students. The Clinical Teacher 22, no. 5. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental longitudinal study of 66 participants (30 students, 36 professionals) at one Spanish hospital, April–June 2021, using a 12-item multiple-choice test at three time points. Correct answers rose from "82.2% of the test questions" to 89.8% and then 90.2%, a total improvement of 9.15%; incorrect answers fell from 17.8% to 9.8%.

> "Before the intervention, participants answered 82.2% of the test questions correctly. After the completion of the first phase of the intervention (theoretical‐practical formation), this percentage significantly increased to 89.8% and continued increasing up to 90.2%"
