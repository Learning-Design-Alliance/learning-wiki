# The appearance of architectural elements marked as certain is remembered better than those marked as uncertain in multimedia learning with uncertainty expressions

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` A significant main effect of uncertainty value on memory for the appearance of architectural elements favored certain over uncertain elements. [→ Glaser 2022](#glaser-2022)

## Evidence

### Glaser 2022

Glaser, M., Lengyel, D., Toulouse, C., & Schwan, S. (2022). How Do We Deal with Uncertain Information? Effects of Verbal and Visual Expressions of Uncertainty on Learning. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:1097–1131. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09659-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Mixed ANOVA in the Results section of the experiment with 124 participants, condition as between-subjects and uncertainty value as within-subject factor, showed a main effect of uncertainty value on memory for element appearance with "ηp2 = 0.678". Table 2 reports the post-hoc ordering "Certain > uncertain".

> "The results showed a significant main effect of uncertainty value, F (2, 240) = 252.625, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.678."
