## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Meg Foster 2023

Meg Foster, Philip von Doetinchem, and Sandra von Doetinchem. (2023). Virtual physics laboratory courses: An evaluation of students' self-efficacy and intelligence mindset. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2301.02699](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2301.02699)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of mindset scores and Kahoot! quiz averages (Figure 2, Participants n = 26) in a remote physics laboratory. The article reports mindsets and quiz scores "became more strongly related at the end of the semester"; no correlation coefficient is printed.

> "Compared to the beginning of the semester, intelligence mindsets and average Kahoot! scores became more strongly related at the end of the semester, suggesting that students with high quiz averages developed stronger growth mindsets than students with low quiz averages."
