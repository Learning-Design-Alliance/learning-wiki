## Evidence

### G.L. Lippi 2018

G.L. Lippi. (2018). Multiple choice homework as a cost-effective and efficient tool for student self-training. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1809.04968](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1809.04968)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Level-test comparison from the 30-minute multiple-choice mathematical competence test administered in 2017-18, reported in the Analysis section. The author offers the difference as a possible indication of willingness to engage, not as a tested effect; no statistical test is printed.

> "Students who later chose to do the homework scored sensibly higher (Fig. 3 – values from Table 2) in this initial test ( Glt = 0.39) than their remaining colleagues (Glt = 0.31): this may be an indication of the willingness of this group to engage in the additional training."
