## Evidence

### Sohlin AM 2025

Sohlin AM, Kjærgaard J, Hoffman IM, et al. (2025). Immersive virtual reality training: Addressing challenges and unlocking potentials. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.15748

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study: 10 semi-structured interviews with simulation centre directors, course leaders and researchers after observing and debriefing an IVR paediatric emergency scenario. Participants "perceived IVR as a supplement rather than a replacement for other simulation modalities" and named the learner groups and goals above. No effect size reported.

> "They perceived that, with current technology, IVR is better suited for familiarising undergraduate learners and junior health professionals with emergency management algorithms and practising the interpretation of clinical findings and verbal communication, rather than for highly experienced teams exploring complex teamwork dynamics."
