## Evidence

### Joss Ives 2016

Joss Ives, Matias de Jong Van Lier, Nutifafa Kwaku Sumah, and Jared B. Stang. (2016). Examining Student Participation in Two-Phase Collaborative Exams through Video Analysis. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.038](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.038)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Video analysis of groups in the group phase of two-phase exams in two introductory physics courses; Figure 1 (left panel) breaks down interaction categories by fraction of students correct, showing "an extremely strong dependence on percentage of students correct".

> "this breakdown shows an extremely strong dependence on percentage of students correct. Groups with at least one correct student, but no more than half appear to be the most productive from the perspective of maximizing the group's likelihood of engaging in an MA interaction."
