## Evidence

### Meaders Clara L. 2025

Meaders Clara L., Mendez Lilyan, Aguilar Alfonso Godínez, Rivera Angelita T., Vasquez Izabella, Mueller Liam O., Owens Melinda T. (2025). An Asynchronous Chemistry-in-biology Intervention Improves Student Content Knowledge and Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education 24(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-05-0151

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Contingency-table analysis of survey-reported prior chemistry coursework for 1780 introductory biology students across five quarters found "a small but significant relationship between chemistry coursework and student identities from historically underserved communities" (Cramer's V = 0.091). A greater percentage of non-PEER continuing-generation students had more than 1 year of high school chemistry.

> "there was a small but significant relationship between chemistry coursework and student identities from historically underserved communities (X2 (9,1780) = 43.9, p < 0.001, Cramer's V = 0.091)"
