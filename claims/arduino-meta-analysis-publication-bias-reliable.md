# Publication bias tests indicate the meta-analysis results are reliable, with fail-safe N of 1930

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The fail-safe N (1930) exceeded 5k + 10, leading the authors to conclude publication bias is unlikely among the included studies. [→ Lee 2020](#lee-2020)

## Evidence

### Lee 2020

Lee, E. (2020). A meta-analysis of the effects of Arduino-based education in Korean primary and secondary schools in engineering education. European Journal of Educational Research, 9(4), 1503-1512. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.9.4.1503

`q3 · i?`

Publication bias inspection of the meta-analysis. A funnel (tunnel) plot was not completely symmetrical, but the fail-safe N of 1930 exceeded the 5k + 10 criterion, so the authors concluded the results can be interpreted as reliable.

> "In this case, the coefficient ( N) is 1930, which is larger than 5k + 10 (k is the number of studies), and thus the results of this study can be interpreted as reliable."
