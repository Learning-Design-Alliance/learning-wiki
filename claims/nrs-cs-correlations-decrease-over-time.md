### Verkerk 2026

Verkerk, J. C., van de Rijt, B., & van Viersen, S. (2026). Development of numerical relational and counting skills as components of early numeracy. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102313

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations between NRS and CS regression factor scores across six time points in the population sample showed all correlations positive and significant, decreasing "from .64 at T1 to .44 at T6"; within-component correlations also decreased slightly over time.

> "Correlations between NRS and CS also decrease slightly over time, ranging from .64 at T1 to .44 at T6, suggesting that the relationship between NRS and CS is stronger in kindergarten than in G1."
