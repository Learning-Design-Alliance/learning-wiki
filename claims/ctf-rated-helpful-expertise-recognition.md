## Evidence

### Sang-Yoon Chang 2022

Sang-Yoon Chang, Kay Yoon, Simeon Wuthier, and Kelei Zhang. (2022). Capture the Flag for Team Construction in Cybersecurity. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.08971](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.08971)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-course ratings on the two CTF-specific questionnaire items, asked only of the ten students who experienced the 2021 CTF exercise. The article reports "Q5 mean=4.2; Q6 mean=3.8" on a 1-5 agreement scale; no effect size or significance test is printed.

> "Students’ average ratings for the CTF exercise (Q5 mean=4.2; Q6 mean=3.8) also indicate a positive impact of the CTF exercise on expertise recognition and role assignment."
