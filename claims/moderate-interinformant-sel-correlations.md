Bivariate correlations among the three informants' SEL composite ratings in the same sample. All three informant pairs correlated significantly and moderately, with the parent–teacher correlation highest (r = .54, a large correlation by the r code table).

> "there were significant moderate correlations between children and both parents (r = .43, p < .01) and teachers (r = .44, p < .01), as well as between parents and teachers (r = .54, p < .01), regardless of grade level."
