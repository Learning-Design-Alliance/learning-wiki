Dejonckheere 2016¶

Dejonckheere, P. J. N., De Wit, N., Van de Keere, K., & Vervaet, S. (2016). Exploring the classroom: Teaching science in early childhood. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 8(4), 537-558. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1109852.

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

The first MANOVA on total explorations (informative plus uninformative) found "An effect of group on the number of explorations was found in the post-test ( Mcontr = 4.30; SD= 3.13; Mexp= 6.63; SD= 2.54), F(1,56)= 9.74, p<0.003, partial η²= 0.20".

"An effect of group on the number of explorations was found in the post-test ( Mcontr = 4.30; SD= 3.13; Mexp= 6.63; SD= 2.54), F(1,56)= 9.74, p<0.003, partial η²= 0.20, but not in the pre-test, F<4."
