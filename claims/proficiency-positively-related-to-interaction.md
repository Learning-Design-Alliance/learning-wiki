The review reports, citing Chiang (2005), a study that paired students by English proficiency to examine the impact of proficiency on interaction behaviors. High-proficiency groups "showed more conﬁ dence" and more successful clarification attempts; low-proficiency groups relied heavily on Chinese (L1). No effect size is printed.
