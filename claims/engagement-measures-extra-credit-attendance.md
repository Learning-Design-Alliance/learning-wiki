# Hybrid-section students earned significantly fewer extra-credit points, and attendance correlated with total points across the cohort

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students in the hybrid section earned significantly fewer extra-credit points than students in the traditional section (11.7 ± 4.6 vs. 13.8 ± 3.3; p < 0.001). [→ Adams A. E. M. 2015](#adams-a-e-m-2015)
`q3 i2` Attendance (participation points) did not differ significantly between sections, but correlated with total points (whole cohort r = 0.36, p < 0.001). [→ Adams A. E. M. 2015 (2)](#adams-a-e-m-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Adams A. E. M. 2015

Adams A. E. M., Randall S., Traustadóttir T. (2015). A Tale of Two Sections: An Experiment to Compare the Effectiveness of a Hybrid versus a Traditional Lecture Format in Introductory Microbiology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0118

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of extra-credit points (maximum 16, for practice exams and a practice quiz) between the two randomized sections. The article reports the hybrid section mean of 11.7 ± 4.6 (73%) vs. 13.8 ± 3.3 (86%) in the traditional section, p < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "students in the hybrid section earned on average 11.7 ± 4.6 extra credit points (or 73%), while students in the traditional section earned on average 13.8 ±3.3 points (or 86%; p < 0.001)."

### Adams A. E. M. 2015 (2)

Adams A. E. M., Randall S., Traustadóttir T. (2015). A Tale of Two Sections: An Experiment to Compare the Effectiveness of a Hybrid versus a Traditional Lecture Format in Introductory Microbiology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0118

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation between participation points (which closely reflected attendance) and total points in the two-section study. Average participation points did not differ significantly between sections (23.7 ± 3.0 vs. 23.7 ± 4.0); the article reports "r = 0.36, p < 0.001" for the whole cohort.

> "There was a significant correlation between attendance and total points in the whole cohort (r = 0.36, p < 0.001) and within each section (hybrid: r = 0.37, p < 0.001; traditional: r = 0.41, p < 0.001)."
