# Pooled satisfaction and self-confidence effects are sensitive to individual studies, while knowledge, performance and self-efficacy effects are robust in leave-one-out analysis

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Removing single studies made the satisfaction pooled effect significant (0.51, p=0.002; 0.48, p=0.018; 0.42, p=0.047), while objective outcomes remained robust (knowledge 0.38–0.58; performance 0.43–0.57). [→ La Cerra Carmen 2019](#la-cerra-carmen-2019)
`q3 i?` Self-efficacy showed no change in leave-one-out analysis, remaining non-significant in all cases (ranging from −0.13 to 0.26). [→ La Cerra Carmen 2019 (2)](#la-cerra-carmen-2019-2)
