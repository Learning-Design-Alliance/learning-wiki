### Tan Jing 2021

Tan Jing, Mao Jie, Jiang Yizhang, Gao Ming. (2021). The Influence of Academic Emotions on Learning Effects: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18189678

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review reports two studies [38,54] with null effects on learning performance. In Beege et al. (n = 162 high school students, educational videos), "Academic emotions do not affect the learning effect, but promote the transfer, memory, and mental load". Equivalence was not tested.

> "an observational study [54] suggested that, after academic emotional guidance occurs, it can improve memory and mental load without affecting learning effects."
