## Evidence

### Duitsman 2019

Duitsman, M. E., van Braak, M., Stommel, W., ten Kate-Booij, M., de Graaf, J., Fluit, C. R. M. G., & Jaarsma, D. A. D. C. (2019). Using conversation analysis to explore feedback on resident performance. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-019-09887-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Conversation-analytic finding across the eight recorded meetings: "PDs and residents frequently downplay negative as well as positive feedback". The authors relate this to hedging, a linguistic strategy of mitigating claims by introducing elements of doubt or signaling lack of commitment, also reported prevalent in written narrative feedback.

> "Another important finding of our study is the pervasiveness of mitigation in feedback conversations. PDs and residents frequently downplay negative as well as positive feedback."
