## Evidence

### Pattinson 2025

Pattinson, S. R., Savelberg, H., & Atherley, A. (2025). Readiness for the Realities of Internship: An Ethnographic Study Using Legitimation Code Theory. Perspectives on Medical Education, 14(1), 628–640. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1802

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From participant observation (approximately 180 hours over 28 visits) and interviews. The authors observed that some interns were "coping better in the face of these challenges than others", with fatigue affecting work and personal lives.

> "Some interns appeared to be coping better in the face of these challenges than others. For some internship was 'exciting' and 'not so bad' while others were 'anxious' and 'overwhelmed,' with constant tiredness and fatigue affecting their work and personal lives."
