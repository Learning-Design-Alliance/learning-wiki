Evidence¶

Angeline S. Lillard 2019¶

Angeline S. Lillard. (2019). Shunned and Admired: Montessori, Self-Determination, and a Case for Radical School Reform. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09483-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Deci et al. (1999), that "using extrinsic rewards depletes motivation once the rewards are removed" for already-motivated activities, and that intrinsic motivation is positively related to school performance whereas extrinsic motivation is negatively related.

"if people are already motivated to engage in an activity (as perhaps young children are in school learning), using extrinsic rewards depletes motivation once the rewards are removed"
