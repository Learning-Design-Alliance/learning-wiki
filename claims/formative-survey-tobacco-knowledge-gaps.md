`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive formative needs assessment of 106 clinicians using a 13-item instrument (one 1-100 confidence visual analog scale plus 12 knowledge items). The article states these findings "were used solely to inform topic selection and sequencing" and are not analyzed as outcomes.

> "Notably, only 32% (n=34) correctly identified the first-line pharmacologic treatment, and additional gaps were observed across counseling approaches, cognitive behavioral strategies, and management in pregnancy."
