Evidence¶

Muskita 2020¶

Muskita, M., Subali, B., & Djukri. (2020). Effects of Worksheets Base the Levels of Inquiry in Improving Critical and Creative Thinking. International Journal of Instruction, 13(2), 519-532. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13236a

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experiment comparing guided inquiry and conventional classes; post-test Mann-Whitney Asymp. Sig. was .000 for critical and creative aspects (pre-test .072 and .889). Experimental post-test means were 79 (critical) and 80 (creative) versus 55 and 51 in the control class.

"Thus it shows that the average critical and creative thinking ability of students from the post-test results by using guided inquiry and conventional learning based worksheet is different and higher than those using conventional learning."
