Xie Guanli, Liao Jianglong, Tang Xiaoxia, Fu Han, Liu Duo, Deng Li, Wang Tao, Hong Xin. (2026). The impact of generative artificial intelligence on clinical skills and knowledge acquisition in medical undergraduates: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09458-3

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Leave-one-out sensitivity analysis conducted in Stata 15.0 across the knowledge-outcome studies, identifying Bhatia 2024 as the sole influential study driving the null pooled effect.

> "for knowledge acquisition, the pooled effect remained non-significant regardless of which study was removed, with the sole exception of Bhatia 2024, upon its removal, the pooled effect became statistically significant (SMD = 0.32; 95% CI: 0.05 to 0.59, P = 0.02)"

### Xie Guanli 2026 (2)

The impact of generative artificial intelligence on clinical skills and knowledge acquisition in medical undergraduates: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09458-3

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Leave-one-out sensitivity analysis across the clinical-skills studies, identifying Briones-Zamora 2026 as the sole influential study; heterogeneity decreased only marginally (I² from 88% to 86%).

> "for clinical skills among clinical medicine students, the overall pooled effect became statistically significant when Briones-Zamora 2026 was omitted (SMD = 0.40; 95% CI: 0.04 to 0.76, P = 0.03), while removal of any other individual study did not substantially alter the non-significant result"
