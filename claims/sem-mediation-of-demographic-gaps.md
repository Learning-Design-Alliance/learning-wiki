## Subclaims
`q2 i?` SEM mediation models show demographic effects on final exam scores are mediated through math SAT/ACT and CI pre-scores, with no direct demographic effect remaining, except a significant but smaller FG gap at PM. [→ Shima Salehi 2019](#shima-salehi-2019)

## Evidence

### Shima Salehi 2019

Shima Salehi, Eric Burkholder, Peter Lepage, Steven Pollock, and Carl Wieman. (2019). The impact of incoming preparation and demographics on performance in Physics I: a multi-institution comparison. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1905.00389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1905.00389)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling using the Lavaan package in R (Appendix B), testing whether incoming preparation mediates demographic effects on exam scores. The article reports good fit at all institutions (RMSEA 0-0.07, CFI above 0.95, SRMR 0-0.1 acceptable ranges, insignificant chi-square) and full mediation for URM and gender, with partial mediation for FG at PM.

> "Based on the results of these mediation models, for all three institutions, the differences in incoming preparation of under-represented demographic groups (URM, FG, and female students) mediated the difference in their final exam scores."
