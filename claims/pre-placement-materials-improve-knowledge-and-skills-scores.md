## Evidence

### Hogg H. D. Jeffry 2020

Hogg H. D. Jeffry, Pereira Michael, Purdy Julian, Frearson Richard J. R., Lau Gordon B. (2020). A non-randomised trial of video and written educational adjuncts in undergraduate ophthalmology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1923-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intention-to-treat comparison across three consecutive cohorts (N = 33 written, N = 32 video, N = 33 none) in a non-randomised trial. The article reports the printed means and p values; no standardised effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Students receiving learning materials scored higher in the post-placement tests of knowledge (p < 0.001), 74.8% (72.4–77.2%) vs 63.6% (95%CI 59.3–67.9%) and skills (p = 0.04), 86.9% (83.9–89.9%) vs 81.3% (77.2–85.4%)."
