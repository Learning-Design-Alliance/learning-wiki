Systematic review (PRISMA 2020) of 20 studies from 12 countries, synthesized narratively after JBI appraisal (14 high, 6 moderate quality). The review reports that "almost all studies link training through simulation scenarios with an improvement in safety-related skills and knowledge." No pooled effect size was computed.
