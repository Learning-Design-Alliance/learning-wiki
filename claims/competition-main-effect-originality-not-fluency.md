## Evidence

### Boot Nathalie 2017

Boot Nathalie, Nevicka Barbara, Baas Matthijs. (2017). Creativity in ADHD: Goal-Directed Motivation and Domain Specificity. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/1087054717727352

`q3 · i2`

Study 2 repeated-measures ANOVAs (competition vs. no competition, ADHD vs. controls, age covariate) on AUT scores. The main effect of competition on originality was significant, while fluency and flexibility showed no main or interaction effects (all ps > .124).

> "However, we found a significant main effect of competition, F(1, 87) = 12.89, p = .001, ηp2 = .13, for the originality of ideas. Participants generated more original ideas under competition (M = 1.86, SD = 0.56) than in the absence of competition (M = 1.72, SD = 0.33)."
