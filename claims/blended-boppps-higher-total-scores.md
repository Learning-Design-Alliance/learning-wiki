## Evidence

### Li Shuze 2024

Li Shuze, Wei Wei, Li Xiaofeng, Ma Li, Li Qiujuan, Sun Xiance, Chen Xin. (2024). Impacts of blended learning with BOPPPS model on Chinese medical undergraduate students: a comprehensive systematic review and meta-analysis of 44 studies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05917-x

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 13 randomized controlled trials with 2030 students pooling total scores (theoretical knowledge, practical skills, daily performance) using Stata version 14.0. The pooled estimate was "SMD = 1.193" with high heterogeneity (I2 = 93.5%).

> "The hybrid BOPPPS teaching mode was related to higher total scores (SMD = 1.193; 95% CI = 0.813–1.572; I2 = 93.5%; P < 0.001;)"
