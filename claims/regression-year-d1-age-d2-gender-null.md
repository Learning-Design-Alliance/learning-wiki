## Evidence

### Gonzalez-Sanz Juan D. 2026

Gonzalez-Sanz Juan D., Teresa-Morales Cristina, Rodríguez-Pérez Margarita, Torres-Enamorado Dolores, Feria-Ramírez Carmen, Urcola-Pardo Fernando. (2026). Nursing students’ evaluations of the flipped classroom using the FLAS scale: a quantitative analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08625-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression with academic year, age, gender and university as predictors of FLAS dimension scores in 432 nursing students. The article reports "F = 13.151, p = .001" for year on D1 and "F = 4.779, p = .029" for age on D2; no effect sizes are printed.

> "academic year was the most consistent predictor, although it only reached statistical significance for D1 (F = 13.151, p = .001) and was not significant for the other dimensions. Age was a significant predictor only for D2 (F = 4.779, p = .029), but not for the other dimensions. Gender was not a statistically significant predictor for any of the dimensions"
