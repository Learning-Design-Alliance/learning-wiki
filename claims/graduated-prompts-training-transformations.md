## Evidence

### Kirsten W. J. Touw 2019

Kirsten W. J. Touw, Bart Vogelaar, Merel Bakker, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2019). Using electronic technology in the dynamic testing of young primary school children: predicting school achievement. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09655-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Second RM-ANOVA in the same 164-child control-group experiment, with total correctly applied transformations as dependent variable; trained children's mean rose from 81.34 to 86.96 versus 80.29 to 80.54 in controls, and the "Session × Condition" interaction was significant.

> "a Session × Condition (Wilks' λ = 0.86, F(1, 162) = 26.06, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.14) effect was found"
