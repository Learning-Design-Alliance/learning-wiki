## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The class effect on both tests was significant in both conditions but much more pronounced in the unplugged group (η² = 0.228 and 0.351) than the plugged-in group (η² = 0.139 and 0.133). [→ Sigayret 2022](#sigayret-2022)

## Evidence

### Sigayret 2022

Sigayret, K., Tricot, A., & Blanc, N. (2022). Unplugged or plugged-in programming learning: A comparative experimental study. Computers and Education, 184, 104505. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104505

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Class-by-class comparison within each condition found "a significant class effect on performance" in both conditions, with larger η² values in the unplugged group on both tests.

> "there was a significant class effect on performance in the Mastery of computational concepts Test and the Algorithmic Test in both conditions, but this impact was much more pronounced in the unplugged group (F(5, 118) = 9.07; p < .001; η² = 0.228 and F (5,118) = 12.8; p < .001; η² = 0.351, respectively) compared to the plugged-in group (F (3,76) = 3.83; p < .01; η² = 0.139 and F (3,76) = 3.89; p < .001; η² = 0.133, respectively)."
