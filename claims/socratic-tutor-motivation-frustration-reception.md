## Evidence

### Eugenio Tufino 2025

Eugenio Tufino. (2025). NotebookLM as a Socratic physics tutor: Design and preliminary observations of a RAG-based tool. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1142/s2661339525500143](https://doi.org/10.1142/s2661339525500143)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two sequential demonstration settings: an in-depth activity with pre-service teachers (aged 23-42) where frustration at withheld solutions was observed, then a brief webinar with approximately 95 in-service teachers on electromagnetic induction problems using the updated manual.

> "The reception from this second group, which interacted with the updated tutor, was overwhelmingly positive, with many expressing surprise and enthusiasm for the Socratic interaction. This session also served as a successful technical test of the system’s scalability."
