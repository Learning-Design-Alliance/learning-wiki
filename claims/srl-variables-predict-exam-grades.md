## Evidence

### Wong 2026

Wong, J., Khalil, M., Suschevskiy, V., Baars, M., de Koning, B., & Paas, F. (2026). Student engagement profiles in a mobile app: Links to self-regulated learning and performance. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10586-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on 266 observations after listwise deletion, with profile membership and three CFA latent variables (self-efficacy, time-management, self-evaluation) predicting final grade; the final CFA model showed only marginal fit (CFI = 0.747).

> "The active and utilitarian profiles have a significant positive correlation with final grades. Additionally, self-efficacy and time management are significantly and positively associated with the final grade. While self-evaluation is also a significant predictor of the final grade, the relationship is inversed."
