### Pless Anina 2021

Pless Anina, Hari Roman, Brem Beate, Woermamm Ulrich, Schnabel Kai P. (2021). Using self and peer video annotations of simulated patient encounters in communication training to facilitate the reflection of communication skills: an implementation study. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001451

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mandatory online course evaluation of the expanded fourth-year communication skills course at the University of Bern, completed by all 247 students. In the evaluation, "More students considered watching peers' videos to be more valuable (66%) than watching their own videos (53%)."
