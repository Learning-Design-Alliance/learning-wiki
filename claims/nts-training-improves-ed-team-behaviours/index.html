The review reports behavioural improvement "across study designs", with ratings on validated instruments "such as the Team Emergency Assessment Measure (TEAM) and the Trauma Non-Technical Skills scale (T-NOTECHS)". GRADE certainty was moderate.

"Behavioural performance improved across study designs, including more frequent closed-loop communication, clearer role allocation and prioritisation, improved situational awareness, and higher teamwork ratings measured using validated instruments such as the Team Emergency Assessment Measure (TEAM) and the Trauma Non-Technical Skills scale (T-NOTECHS) (moderate certainty)."
