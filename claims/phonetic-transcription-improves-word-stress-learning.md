## Evidence

### Ghorbani 2019

Ghorbani, M. R. (2019). The effect of phonetic transcription on Iranian EFL students' word stress learning. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 15(2), 400-410. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1220802

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental post-test comparison of two intact first-semester EFL classes (EG n = 16, CG n = 18) analyzed with an independent samples t-test in SPSS; the treatment ran for 10 sessions, 45 minutes of each 90-minute class, with a 45-day interval between pre-test and post-test. The EG mean of "48.62" exceeded the CG mean of "36.77" with a significant t-test; no effect size was printed.

> "As shown in Table 2, there was a significant difference between the EG (M = 48.62, SD = 6.46) and the CG [M = 36.77, SD = 4.10; t (6.28) = 0.000, p < .05]."
