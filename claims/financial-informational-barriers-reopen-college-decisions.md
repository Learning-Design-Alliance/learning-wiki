### Arnold 2015

Arnold, Karen D.; Chewning, Alexandra; Castleman, Benjamin; Page, Lindsay C. (2015). Advisor and Student Experiences of Summer Support for College-Intending, Low-Income High School Graduates. Journal of College Access. http://scholarworks.wmich.edu/jca/vol1/iss1/3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative findings from advisor interviews and the intervention model show students facing first-year funding shortfalls from $500 to $18,000 struggled to make sense of their gap; the article states students "are forced to reopen the previously-completed process of deciding whether and where to go to college."

> "Unable to find an affordable way to matriculate at their desired college, these students are forced to reopen the previously-completed process of deciding whether and where to go to college."
