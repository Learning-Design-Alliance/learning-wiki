## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2020

Martin O. Archer. (2020). Schools of all backgrounds can do physics research: On the accessibility and equity of the PRiSE approach to independent research projects. arXiv. [doi:10.5194/gc-2020-37](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-2020-37)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survival-analysis-based equity test within PRiSE across 2017/18 and 2018/19: no bias in completing a year by school category, FSM, higher education participation rate, deprivation, or ethnic diversity; the quoted tests all show non-significant differences (e.g. "p = 0.524", p = 0.865).

> "We find no biases in schools' ability to successfully complete a year by school category (χ2 (4) = 3.21,p = 0.524), free schools meals percentage ( +4% absolute and 1.33× relative differences in medians of schools which do and do not complete a year,p = 0.865)"

### Martin O. Archer 2020 (2)

Martin O. Archer. (2020). Schools of all backgrounds can do physics research: On the accessibility and equity of the PRiSE approach to independent research projects. arXiv. [doi:10.5194/gc-2020-37](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-2020-37)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-years retention analysis comparing backgrounds of schools returning for multiple years versus those that do not: no significant differences in school category, FSM, higher education participation rate, deprivation, or ethnic diversity (printed χ2 and p values all non-significant).

> "Again we find no signiﬁcant differences in schools which participate in PRiSE for multiple years compared to those which don’t in terms of school category ( χ2 (4) = 2.22, p = 0.695)"
