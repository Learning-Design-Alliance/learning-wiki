# In a fully guided static navigation implant protocol in vitro, operators with no dental experience achieve angular deviation comparable to experienced surgeons

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mean angular deviation did not differ statistically significantly between experienced surgeons (DOC), dental technicians (TEC), and non-clinician office workers (OFC) using a fully guided drilling protocol. [→ Reiff FS 2024](#reiff-fs-2024)

## Evidence

### Reiff FS 2024

Reiff FS, Bischoff C, Woelfler H, Roehling S. (2024). Influence of clinical expertise and practical experience on transfer accuracy in guided dental implant placement - an in vitro study. Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10006-024-01269-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In vitro experiment with 36 operators in three expertise groups placing two implants each into identical maxillary models (N = 72 implants); angular deviation means were 1.9° (DOC), 2.0° (TEC), 2.3° (OFC), compared with Mann-Whitney U tests. "no statistically significant differences were found" between groups.

> "In the group comparison, no statistically significant differences were found between the control group DOC and the test groups TEC (p = 0.642) and OFC (p = 0.348). The comparison between the two test groups also showed no statistically significant difference (p = 0.257)."
