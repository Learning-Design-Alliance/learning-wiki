## Evidence

### Zhao 2025

Zhao, C., Silva, M., & Poulsen, S. (2025). Autograding Mathematical Induction Proofs with Natural Language Processing. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 35, 3202–3232. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00498-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-activity Likert survey of students in the First and Random groups (S01-S03 vs. S08-S10), with pairwise t-tests comparing perceived accuracy, helpfulness, and satisfaction. The article reports p < 0.05 for accuracy and helpfulness but no standardized effect size; satisfaction differences were not statistically significant for either group.

> "On average, human graders are perceived to be more accurate and helpful than the AI autograder, with statistically signiﬁcant differences ( p < 0.05)."
