## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Centripetal Acceleration: Often Forgotten or Misinterpreted. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06361](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06361)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument illustrated by a traffic-intersection scenario: passengers in decelerating Car B sense the deceleration inertially, while passengers in Car A must use higher-level cognitive tools to infer Car B's deceleration visually. The article also notes further exploration of motion perception is required.

> "while humans can visually obtain a reasonably good sense of the s peed of an object quickly, the magnitude of acceleration of an object is diﬃcult to gauge visually ."
