## Evidence

### Youngwoo Bryan Yoon & Wi Hoon Jung 2025

Youngwoo Bryan Yoon & Wi Hoon Jung. (2025). Impact of impulsivity on the relationship of the brain structures with school performance. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00344-z

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analyses between residualized GMVs of the six GPA-associated clusters and BIS-11 total score (p < 0.05), with age, sex, handedness, education duration, and TIV regressed out. Two of six clusters were significant: the study reports "the GMV of the left cerebellum... was negatively correlated with impulsivity (total score of BIS-11; r = −0.170, p value = 0.036)" and the right caudate nucleus was positively correlated (r = 0.164, p = 0.042) (Fig. 2).

> "the GMV of the left cerebellum (MNI x, y, z coordi- nates =−22, −88, −24) was negatively correlated with impulsivity (total score of BIS-11;r = −0.170,p value = 0.036)"
