## Evidence

### Talsma 2018

Talsma, K., Schüz, B., Schwarzer, R., & Norris, K. (2018). I believe, therefore I achieve (and vice versa): A meta-analytic cross-lagged panel analysis of self-efficacy and academic performance. Learning and Individual Differences, 61, 136-150. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2017.11.015

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis comparing matched (task-based self-efficacy with task-based performance) versus non-matched (domain-based self-efficacy) subgroups of the k=11 studies. Effects were "stronger" for matched studies but "did not reach statistical significance".

> "Reciprocal effects were stronger for studies in which the specificity of the self-efficacy measure matched the performance measure, though the differences did not reach statistical significance."
