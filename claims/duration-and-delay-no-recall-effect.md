## Subclaims
`q2 i?` GAM smooth terms for learning duration were not significant for either condition on immediate recall (Learn Your Way: edf = 1, F = 0.48, p = .49; Textbook: edf = 1.82, F = 1.43, p = .283) or long-term recall. [→ Courtney Heldreth 2026](#courtney-heldreth-2026)

## Evidence

### Courtney Heldreth 2026

Courtney Heldreth, Diana Akrong, Laura M. Vardoulakis, Nicole E. Miller, Yael Haramaty, Lidan Hackmon, Lior Belinsky, Abraham Ortiz Tapia, Lucy Tootill, and Scott Siebert. (2026). An Experimental Evaluation of an AI-Powered Interactive Learning Platform. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.3389/frai.2026.1783117](https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2026.1783117)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Semi-parametric generalized additive models with smoothing terms nested by condition modeled learning duration against recall scores; the long-term model explained 9.1% of deviance and "learning duration did not meaningfully influence performance outcomes" in either condition.

> "the smooth terms for learning duration were not significant for Learn Your Way (ed f= 1, F = 1.53, p = .222) or the Textbook condition (ed f= 1, F = 0 .15, p = .703). Collectively, these findings suggest that learning duration did not meaningfully influence performance outcomes on either the immediate or long-term recall assessment"
