## Evidence

### Niessen A. Susan M. 2016

Niessen A. Susan M., Meijer Rob R., Tendeiro Jorge N. (2016). Predicting Performance in Higher Education Using Proximal Predictors. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0153663

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression with HSMG and trial-studying score predicting first-year dropout in the Dutch-secondary-education subsample; the model was significant (χ2(2) = 17.02, p < .01, Nagelkerke's pseudo R2 = .13). The article cautions these coefficients cannot be directly compared to least-squares results.

> "Pseudo-partial correlations equaled pr = .09 for HSMG and pr = .13 for the trial-studying score. Thus, the unique contribution of the trial-studying test when taking HSMG into account was slightly larger than vice versa."
