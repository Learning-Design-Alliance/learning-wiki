## Evidence

### Bjork 2020

Bjork, R. A., & Bjork, E. L. (2020). Desirable difficulties in theory and practice. Journal of Applied Research in Memory and Cognition, 9(4), 475-479. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-applied-research-in-memory-and-cognition. [doi:10.1016/j.jarmac.2020.09.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jarmac.2020.09.003)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument in the metamemory considerations section. The article argues that because difficulty-inducing conditions slow apparent learning, reliance on current performance causes both mis-judgment of learning and preference for poorer conditions. No new empirical data are reported in this section.

> "Thus, to the extent that we interpret current performance as a valid measure of learning we become susceptible not only to mis-judging whether learning has or has not occurred, but also to preferring poorer conditions of learning over better conditions of learning."
