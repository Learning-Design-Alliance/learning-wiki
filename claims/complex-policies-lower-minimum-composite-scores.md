Camila Morales and Alonzo Lepper. (2024). English Learner Reclassification Policy Structures and Student Characteristics. E4 Center, Northwestern University. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2024/06/25/english-learner-reclassification-policy-structures-and-student-characteristics/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of minimum ACCESS 2.0 composite scores required for reclassification, limited to the 36 WIDA consortium states, shown in Figure 4. The brief reports small yet significant differences and finds the same pattern when states are grouped by the number of criteria.

> "In particular, there is a negative relationship between policy complexity and minimum composite scores. For instance, states that rely on a single ELP requirement set their reclassification policy's minimum composite score, on average, 0.6 proficiency points higher than the composite score for states with multiple mandated requirements, including non-ELP assessments."
