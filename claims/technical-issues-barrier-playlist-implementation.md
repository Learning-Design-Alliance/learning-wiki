## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Technical issues frustrated students to the point of disengagement and impeded implementation, engagement, and playlist completion. [→ Li 2019](#li-2019)

## Evidence

### Li 2019

Li, L., Maher, B., Weiser, G., Orenstein, A., Chow, E., & Atienza, S. (2019). Hero Elementary playlist pilot study in after-school programs. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED608115

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational and interview study of four after-school sites; educators reported unreliable tablet internet connections, scanning login failures, projector setup difficulty, and app glitches (e.g., games freezing), with the authors expecting fixes through further development of the digital assets.

> "Technical issues created a significant barrier to implementation and student engagement. The biggest barriers were: (1) the log-in process; (2) the logistics of using devices such as the mi-fi and the projector; and (3) various glitches in digital activities."
