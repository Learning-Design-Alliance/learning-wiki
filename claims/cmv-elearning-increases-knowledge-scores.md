## Evidence

### Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al 2022

Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al. (2022). eLearning significantly improves maternity professionals' knowledge of the congenital cytomegalovirus prevention guidelines. Australian & New Zealand Journal of Obstetrics & Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13500

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post evaluation of the eLearning course using a generalised linear mixed-effects model with random intercept, controlling for prior CMV education and length of practice. Scores rose "from 17.2 to 22.8, P < 0.001" (T1 n = 363; T2 n = 238).

> "Adjusted total mean scores between T1 and T2 significantly improved with an increase of +5.56 (from 17.2 to 22.8, P < 0.001)."
