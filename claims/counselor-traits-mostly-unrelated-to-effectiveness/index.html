Rowe 1975¶

The review's summary of the studies using scales and rating devices, stating that "Most variables investigated were not significantly related to the criterion employed" and that contrary results often appeared on re-investigation. No pooled effect sizes are printed.

"1. Most variables investigated were not significantly related to the criterion employed. 2. Where a variable was found to be significant once but was investigated more than once, contrary results were often shown."
