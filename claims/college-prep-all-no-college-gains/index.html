Allensworth 2008¶

Allensworth, E., Nomi, T., Montgomery, N., & Lee, V. E. (2008). College Preparatory Curriculum for All: Consequences of Ninth-Grade Course Taking in Algebra and English on Academic Outcomes in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/college-preparatory-curriculum-all-consequences-ninth-grade-coursetaking-algebra-and

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Longer-term enrollment-effect results (Table 2): students in the two lowest-ability groups were slightly more likely to earn credits beyond geometry (a three percentage point increase for lowest-ability students, p<.01), but not beyond Algebra II; final GPAs declined slightly (p<.05) and four-year college probability decreased slightly for all but the lowest-ability group.

"Thus, a policy that replaced remedial with college-prep coursework in the ninth grade for all students did not prepare more students for college—overall, students were no more likely to obtain credits in upper-level math courses, or attend a four-year college."
