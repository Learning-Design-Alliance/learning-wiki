## Evidence

### Nergiz Ercil Cagiltay 2015

Nergiz Ercil Cagiltay, Erol Ozcelik, Nese Sahin Ozcelik. (2015). The effect of competition on learning in games. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.04.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing post-test scores of 142 undergraduates randomly assigned to competition or control versions of an ER-diagram serious game; the competition group "performed better in the post-test than the control group" (M = 24.47 vs 20.92, t(140) = 2.51, p = .01).

> "The results indicated that the competition group ( M ¼ 24.47,SD ¼ 8.93) performed better in the post-test than the control group ( M ¼ 20.92, SD ¼ 7.81), t(140) ¼/C0 2.51, p ¼ .01."
