## Evidence

### Hassan KE 2025

Hassan KE, Sethi A, Junaid SM, Khattak T, Khattak M. (2025). Readiness for self-directed learning among undergraduate medical students in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa during COVID-19, Pakistan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06745-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent-sample t-test comparing SDL mean scores of private (N = 432, mean 232.00) versus public (N = 392, mean 221.30) college students in the cross-sectional survey; the article reports "a statistical significance (p-value = < 0.001)".

> "The mean score of SDL among students enrolled in private medical colleges was significantly higher compared to those enrolled in public medical colleges, with a statistical significance (p-value = < 0.001)."

### Hassan KE 2025 (2)

Hassan KE, Sethi A, Junaid SM, Khattak T, Khattak M. (2025). Readiness for self-directed learning among undergraduate medical students in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa during COVID-19, Pakistan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06745-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square crosstabulation of SDL readiness level by medical college setting in the same survey; the article reports private students "were more likely to report good readiness for SDL (58.4%)" with p < 0.001.

> "However, students enrolled in private medical colleges were more likely to report good readiness for SDL (58.4%) compared to those enrolled in public sector medical colleges (41.6%) with a statistical significance (p-value = < 0.001)."
