Evidence¶

Franzen 1921¶

Franzen, R., & Knight, F. B. (1921). Criteria to Employ in Choice of Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075312

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Analytical argument that the reliability of averages and spread measures are functions of test reliability, so group comparisons are 'not permissible unless we know the reliability of the tests'. Support is the authors' reasoning, illustrated by the telephone-diagnosis analogy; no data reported.

"the use for experimentation of a test with a low reliability leads to faulty conclusions and classification; diagnoses and definition of goals made on a basis of unreliable material is tantamount to prescriptions of a doctor who has made a diagnosis over a telephone."
