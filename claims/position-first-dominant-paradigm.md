### Alexis Buzzell 2025

Alexis Buzzell, Ramón Barthelemy, and Tim Atherton. (2025). Quantum mechanics curriculum in the US: Quantifying the instructional time, content taught, and paradigms used. arXiv. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Syllabus coding of 112 modern physics courses and 99 quantum mechanics courses using a paradigm coding scheme developed from the literature (SGE before Schrödinger equation, matrix vs differential equations, textbook). The article reports all modern physics courses position-first and QM courses 26.3% spin-first vs 73.7% position-first.

> "Of the 112 modern physics or equivalent courses, all used a position-first approach. For the quantum mechan- ics courses, it was found that spin-first approaches were used by some instructors (26.3%), but position-first ap- proaches were still the more commonly used approach (73.7%)."
