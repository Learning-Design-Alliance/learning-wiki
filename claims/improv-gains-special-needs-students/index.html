DeMichele 2015¶

DeMichele, M. (2015). Improv and ink: Increasing individual writing fluency with collaborative improv. International Journal of Education & the Arts, 16(10). http://www.ijea.org/v16n10/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Subgroup analysis within the Study 1 quasi-experimental comparison at the inner-city summer program. The special needs experimental subgroup's word usage rose 227% and sentence usage 310%, versus 50% and 49% in the comparison subgroup, yielding a "177% increase in word usage and a 261% increase in sentence usage over the comparison group".
