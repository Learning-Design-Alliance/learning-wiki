Test-retest analysis two weeks after baseline with 185 respondents. The article reports "Thirty items had good and fair correlations (r = 0.418–0.620)" while 12 items had low correlations, indicating mixed stability across items.

> "At retest, 185 participants completed the EBMQ (response rate = 57.85%), as n = 23 (42%) were uncontactable. Thirty items had good and fair correlations (r = 0.418–0.620) while 12 items had low correlations (r = < 0.4)."
