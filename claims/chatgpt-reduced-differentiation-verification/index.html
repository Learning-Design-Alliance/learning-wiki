Evidence¶

Shravika Mittal 2026¶

Shravika Mittal, Su Lin Blodgett, Q. Vera Liao. (2026). Learning by Chatting? Investigating the Impact of Generative AI on Information Seeking and Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11669 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.11669

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Thematic analysis of diary entries using the Table 1 codebook: credibility-based differentiation appeared in 0% of ChatGPT entries versus 30.71% of Google entries; type-of-need differentiation 0% vs 10.00%. The article attributes this to offloading information selection to ChatGPT.

"Participants in the ChatGPT condition reported fewer instances of differentiating information, i.e., assessing (and filtering) information based on specific criteria (e.g., personal relevance, credibility, type of information need) and their indicators"

Shravika Mittal 2026 (2)¶

Shravika Mittal, Su Lin Blodgett, Q. Vera Liao. (2026). Learning by Chatting? Investigating the Impact of Generative AI on Information Seeking and Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11669

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Diary-entry analysis of the verifying stage: three ChatGPT instances via another source, four via prior knowledge, plus three expressions of intent to verify without action; "participants in the Google condition verified information more frequently (31instances)."

"in the verifying stage (after information extraction), there were only seven instances where ChatGPT participants checked the accuracy of the information."
