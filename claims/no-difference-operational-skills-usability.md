### Kakimoto Akihiro 2026

Kakimoto Akihiro, Fujise Daisuke, Watanabe Shota, Otaka Yutaka, Minemoto Sakura, Yasumatsu Ayaka, Hasegawa Shin. (2026). Development and Educational Evaluation of a Radiation Visualization System in a Virtual Reality Training Module for Radiopharmaceutical Administration: Mixed Methods Study of Students’ Learning Experiences. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/104397

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Primary outcome domains B and E in the within-participants comparison of 20 students; Wilcoxon signed-rank tests nonsignificant after Bonferroni correction (P=.83, r=0.049 for B; P=.29, r=0.239 for E). Equivalence was not tested.

> "The (B) operational understanding and technical skills (vial and shielding manipulation) domain showed comparable results across both training modalities, with mean scores of 3.60 (SD 0.52) for conventional training and 3.55 (SD 0.66) for VR-based training."
