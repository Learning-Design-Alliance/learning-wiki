`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative content analysis of an 8-question open-ended opinion form completed by all 5 participating mathematics teachers after the implementations. Teachers' coded responses (Table 9) show 100% agreement on successful, useful, suitable, and intelligence-domain codes and 80% on creative. No effect size applies.

> "In Table 9, we observe that the teachers who participated in this study thought that the study activities were creative, successful, useful, and suitable to the students' different levels and that they addressed the students' different intelligence domains."
