## Evidence

### Maas MJM 2015

Maas MJM, van Dulmen SA, Sagasser MH, Heerkens YF, van der Vleuten CPM, Nijhuis-van der Sanden MWG, van der Wees PJ. (2015). Critical features of peer assessment of clinical performance to enhance adherence to a low back pain guideline for physical therapists: a mixed methods design. BMC Medical Education 15:203. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0484-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported outcomes from questionnaires and interviews in the mixed-methods study. Table 5 lists change in attitudes, awareness of professional limitations, and improved self-efficacy beliefs as learning outcomes. Participants did not explicitly report changes in management of patient problems; no effect size is printed.

> "The PA program resulted in distinct levels of self-reported behavioral change. Although participants studied the updated guidelines prior to the program and were tested on their knowledge with clinical vignettes, they reported that applying knowledge in the context of PA increased their understanding of the guidelines, and facilitated their use in clinical practice."
