## Evidence

### Sofia Magalhães 2020

Sofia Magalhães, Luísa Carneiro, Teresa Limpo & Marisa Filipe (2020). Executive functions predict literacy and mathematics achievements: The unique contribution of cognitive flexibility in grades 2, 4, and 6. Child Neuropsychology, 26(7), 934-952. https://doi.org/10.1080/09297049.2020.1740188

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-step hierarchical regression with 243 second graders predicting literacy marks; Step 1 (R² = .24) showed fluid intelligence and inhibitory control as significant predictors; Step 2 adding flexibility yielded "Fchange <1", a null result with no printed effect size.

> "The inclusion of cognitive ﬂexibility on Step 2 did not increase the amount of explained variance in literacy achievement, Fchange <1 ."

### Sofia Magalhães 2020 (2)

Sofia Magalhães, Luísa Carneiro, Teresa Limpo & Marisa Filipe (2020). Executive functions predict literacy and mathematics achievements: The unique contribution of cognitive flexibility in grades 2, 4, and 6. Child Neuropsychology, 26(7), 934-952. https://doi.org/10.1080/09297049.2020.1740188

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-step hierarchical regression with 243 second graders predicting mathematics marks; Step 1 (R² = .34) showed fluid intelligence, attention, and inhibitory control as significant predictors; adding flexibility at Step 2 gave "Fchange <1". Equivalence was not tested.

> "When cognitive ﬂexibility was entered on Step 2, there was no signi ﬁcant increase in the amount of explained variance in mathematics achievement, Fchange <1 ."
