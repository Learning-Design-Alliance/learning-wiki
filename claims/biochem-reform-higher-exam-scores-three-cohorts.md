## Evidence

### Xu Shuzi 2025

Xu Shuzi, Chen Yiyang, and Wu Juanjuan. (2025). Practical Exploration of Medical Students' Curriculum Education Model in the Context of New Medical Science—Taking Biochemistry and Molecular Biology as an Example. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education 53, no. 6: 647–655. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70019

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on 2020 final-exam grades: clinical mean 72.82 (SD 6.10, n=60), stomatology mean 75.58 (SD 8.16, n=60); "the stomatology program receiving significantly higher grades" at p = 0.037. Quasi-experimental cohort comparison; no effect size printed.

> "Based on the obtained t‐statistic value of 2.105 and the corresponding significance level of 0.037 (less than 0.05), it can be concluded that a notable disparity existed between the grades of the clinical and stomatology programs, with the stomatology program receiving significantly higher grades."

### Xu Shuzi 2025 (2)

Xu Shuzi, Chen Yiyang, and Wu Juanjuan. (2025). Practical Exploration of Medical Students' Curriculum Education Model in the Context of New Medical Science—Taking Biochemistry and Molecular Biology as an Example. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education 53, no. 6: 647–655. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70019

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

2021 cohort t-test: clinical mean 72.91 (SD 6.86), stomatology mean 76.71 (SD 9.90); stomatology "significantly higher" at p = 0.021. No effect size printed.

> "Based on the t‐statistic value of 2.347 and the associated significance level of 0.021 (less than 0.05), it can be concluded that the grades for the clinical and stomatology programs differ significantly, with the stomatology program's grades being significantly higher than those for the clinical program."
