## Evidence

### Yli-Piipari 2016

Yli-Piipari, S., Layne, T., McCollins, T., & Knox, T. (2016). The Impact of Classroom Physical Activity Breaks on Middle School Students’ Health-Related Fitness: An Xbox One Kinetic Delivered 4-Week Randomized Controlled Trial. JTRM in Kinesiology, 12:1-18. http://www.health.gov/paguidelines/pdf/paguide.pdf

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The same analysis of covariance for the high-adherence subgroup versus control in the 4-week randomized trial also showed a significant effect on abdominal strength and endurance, "η2 = .08", a small-to-moderate effect by the article's printed guidelines. No significant effect was found for upper body strength or flexibility in this analysis.

> "intervention had a significant effect on students’ cardiovascular endurance (F[2,52] = 23.95, p < .001, η2 = .30) and abdominal strength and endurance (F[2,52] = 3.24, p = .049, η2 = .08)"
