## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zhang Jie 2021

Zhang Jie, Xing Jiawei, Zheng Min, Sheng Jie, Zhang Kailiang, Zhang Baoping. (2021). Effectiveness of virtual simulation and jaw model for undergraduate periodontal teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-03064-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized four-group experiment (60 students, n=15 per group) comparing VR, jaw-model, and combined training sequences after uniform theoretical teaching. Combined groups scored "V-J: 60.00 ± 4.47; J-V: 58.33 ± 4.35" versus V 50.67 ± 3.72 and J 49.00 ± 4.31.

> "The scores of the second theoretical examination were significantly higher compared with the first theoretical examination scores in each group (V: 50.67 ± 3.72; J: 49.00 ± 4.31; V-J: 60.00 ± 4.47; J-V: 58.33 ± 4.35)"
