## Evidence

### Mavragani Amaryllis 2025

Mavragani Amaryllis, Coco Laura, Mungoli Neelesh, Gerdes John, Schooley Benjamin, Sharp Dakota, Miller Juliana. (2025). The Design and Evaluation of a Simulation Tool for Audiology Screening Education: Design Science Approach. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/47150

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Thematic analysis of 4 free-response questions administered to the test group (n=17) in the posttest survey; responses were grouped by similarity and counted. The most-mentioned liked aspect was hands-on experience and increased confidence (5 of 17 responses).

> "the students found that the hands-on experience gained by using the simulator provided an easy-to-use and useful learning experience with the audiometer that increased their confidence in their ability to perform hearing screening."
