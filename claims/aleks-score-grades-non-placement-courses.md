## Evidence

### Marc Harper 2013

Marc Harper, Alison Ahlgren Reddy. (2013). Detecting Concepts Crucial for Success in Mathematics Courses from Knowledge State-based Placement Data. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1312.1127](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1312.1127)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of Fall 2010 ALEKS score bands versus grade proportions in Calculus II (231 students) and Calculus III (241 students), neither governed by placement requirements. Figure 1 shows grade distributions rising with score; the authors note the pattern is "essentially always present" across courses and years.

> "despite the fact that there is no explicit calculus content in the asse ssment, there is a strong and obvious relationship between the initial ALEKS score and the distribution of g rades of students in the course."
