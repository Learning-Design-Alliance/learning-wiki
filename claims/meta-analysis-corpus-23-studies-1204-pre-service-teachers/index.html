Evidence¶

Şenel Elaldı 2016¶

Şenel Elaldı, Veli Batdı. (2016). Comparing Effects of Different Applications on Pre-Service Teachers: A Meta–Analysis. Journal of Education and Training Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i7.1456

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Literature search of 2005–2015 national and international databases screened 324 potential studies; 23 met inclusion criteria and were analyzed in MetaWin. The review reports "A total sample size of 1204 pre -service teachers were retained for the analysis."

"Of the 23 studies, 9 were related to retention (4 articles, 2 PhD thes es, and 3 master’s theses) and 14 were related to attitude (2 articles, 6PhD theses, and 6 master’s theses). A total sample size of 1204 pre -service teachers were retained for the analysis."

Related Claims¶

  • Learning Cycle Model, Use of Animation, Reflective Diaries and Multiple Intelligence Theory Based Teaching each had very large retention effect sizes for pre-service teachers — related
  • Instructional design competence of pre-service teachers comprises knowledge, ability, and personal attributes dimensions — related
  • The review's search identified 539 studies, of which 39 met inclusion criteria, spanning 9927 participants with samples from 7 to 3268 — related
  • The meta-analysis corpus comprised 40 studies yielding 45 data comparisons from 2013-2022 — related
  • Among applications for retention of pre-service teachers, the Learning Together Technique had the highest effect size (d=2,0606, huge) and the Jigsaw Technique the lowest (d=0,5461, medium) — related
  • For attitude of pre-service teachers, Transformative Learning Theory had the highest effect size (d=1,4760, huge) and Problem Based Learning the lowest (d=-0,3949, classified as negligible) — related
