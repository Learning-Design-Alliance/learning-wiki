# Work experience, very good clinical competence, field satisfaction, and academic success predict self-directed learning scores in multiple linear regression

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i1` small
> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In a backward-method multiple linear regression on 450 nursing students, having healthcare work experience showed a significant direct relationship with SDL scores (β = 0.14, p = 0.004), very good clinical competence a direct relationship (β = 0.22, p < 0.001), dissatisfaction with nursing an inverse relationship (β = −0.10, p = 0.035), and weak or very weak academic success inverse relationships (β = −0.14 and β = −0.23). [→ Reza Badakhshan 2025](#reza-badakhshan-2025)

Reza Badakhshan, Saeed Badakhshan, Farzaneh Bagheriyeh, Fariba Hosseinzadegan. (2025). The relationship between self-directed learning ability and clinical competence among nursing students: a cross-sectional descriptive study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08108-4

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression (backward method) in the Findings section of the cross-sectional study, after checking Durbin-Watson (= 1.85), homoscedasticity, and normality. The model also printed inverse relationships for dissatisfaction (β = −0.10, p = 0.035) and for weak (β = −0.14) and very weak (β = −0.23) academic success.

> "having work experience has a significant direct relationship with the average score of the self-directed learning scale (β = 0.14, p = 0.004). Very good clinical competence also has a significant direct relationship with the average score of the self-directed learning scale (β = 0.22, p < 0.001)."
