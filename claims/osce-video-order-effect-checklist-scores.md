## Evidence

### Kozato An 2020

Kozato An, Patel Nimesh, Shikino Kiyoshi. (2020). A randomised controlled pilot trial of the influence of non-native English accents on examiners’ scores in OSCEs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02198-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of score-change patterns between the two randomised groups: in group 1 (n = 16) over half rated the first video higher; in group 2 (n = 15) only 33% did. The authors call the viewing order "a confounding variable".

> "More than 50% of the examiners in group 1 scored the first video A higher than the second video B. Only 33% of the examiners in group 2 scored the first video C higher than the second video D."
