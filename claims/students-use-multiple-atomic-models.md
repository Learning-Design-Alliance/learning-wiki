### S. B. McKagan 2007

S. B. McKagan, K. K. Perkins, and C. E. Wieman. (2007). Why we should teach the Bohr model and how to teach it effectively. https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.1541 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0707.1541](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0707.1541)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Coding of final-exam responses across four semesters found student model use could not be treated as mutually exclusive, unlike previous studies. The authors report "a large fraction of students used multiple models" and note the percentage explicitly discussing models increased after curriculum changes (p < 0.05).

> "a large fraction of students used multiple models, either by implicitly blending ideas from diﬀerent models or by explicitly comparing and contrasting models."
