Evidence¶

Warren Li 2022¶

Warren Li, Kaiwen Sun, Florian Schaub, Christopher Brooks. (2022). Disparities in Students' Propensity to Consent to Learning Analytics. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 32:564–608. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00254-2

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Open-coded survey responses (Cohen's κ = .77) from 116 respondents: 92 consented, citing improved education (30%), research insights (20.65%), and benefits to other students (20.65%); "Of the 24 students who denied consent... 63% expressed privacy concerns" and 20% cited lack of transparency.

"Of the 24 students who denied consent (see Table 7), 63% expressed privacy concerns (e.g., data breach, uncomfortable sharing student data), and 20% expressed concern over lack of transparency on how data is collected, used, and by whom."
