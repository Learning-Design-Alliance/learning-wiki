## Evidence

### Kim Min-Kyeong 2024

Kim Min-Kyeong, Kim Hae Won. (2024). Impact of a change from A–F grading to honors/pass/fail grading on academic performance at Yonsei University College of Medicine in Korea: a cross-sectional serial mediation analysis. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2024.21.20

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of two cohorts at one Korean medical school: 127 students under A–F grading (2014) and 111 under honors/pass/fail grading (2015). Table 1 reports Basic Medical Science Examination scores of 48.76±9.51 versus 52.00±8.54, t=-2.749, P=0.006; the text states "the honors/pass/fail grading class demonstrating higher performance".

> "The academic performance of the groups was different, with the honors/pass/fail grading class demonstrating higher performance on the Basic Medical Science Examination."
