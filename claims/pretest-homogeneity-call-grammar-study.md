# The experimental and control groups were homogeneous in grammar knowledge before the intervention

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` An independent samples t-test on pre-test scores showed no significant difference between the control and experimental groups (t = .177, p = .860). [→ Alian 2016](#alian-2016)

## Evidence

### Alian 2016

Alian, J., Khodabandeh, F., & Soleimani, H. (2016). The effect of CALL-based tasks on EFL learners’ grammar learning. Teaching English with Technology, 18(3), 54-68. http://www.tewtjournal.org

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test analysis of the 60 selected learners (30 per group, selected from 140 as within one SD of the mean of 32.18, SD 2.12): group means were 8.17 and 8.20, and the t-test showed "no significant difference in grammar knowledge".

> "the p-value was more than .05(.860), and the t-observed .177 was less than the t-critical, 2.04. Therefore, the participants were homogeneous and there was no significant difference in grammar knowledge between the control and the experimental groups on the pretest"
