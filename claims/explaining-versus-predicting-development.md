## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Standard analyses like regression optimize model fit for explanation, whereas machine-learning approaches choose coefficients to minimize prediction error on new out-of-sample data; both serve complementary goals. [→ van Atteveldt 2021](#van-atteveldt-2021)

Theoretical/methodological argument from the article's fourth identified gap. Predicting outcomes can provide insights on the need for additional support, e.g., early intervention or policy, while explanatory methods highlight underlying mechanisms that can be targeted by intervention.
