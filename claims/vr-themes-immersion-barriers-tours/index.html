Jacqueline Żammit 2024¶

Jacqueline Żammit. (2024). Could virtual reality be the next approach for international students learning Maltese? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10375-9

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis using NVivo of the recorded online Zoom focus group with 25 ML2 teachers. The article lists theme code counts, with immersion at 25 codes, physical barriers at 22, and tours at 20.

"VR is a completely immersive learning experience (25 codes): This theme highlights the immersive nature of VR, which can provide a fully engaging learning experience for ML2 students."
