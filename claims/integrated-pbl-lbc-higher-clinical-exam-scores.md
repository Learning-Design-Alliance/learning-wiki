# Undergraduate medical students receiving integrated PBL combined with LBC scored significantly higher than LBC-only students in most clinical examinations (all P < .01)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` An experimental group taught with integrated PBL plus LBC scored significantly higher than an LBC control group in Clinical Skills, Internal Medicine I and II, Introduction to Surgery, General Surgery, Specialty Surgery, and Clinical Medical Level Test (all P < .01). [→ Zhan H-Q 2023](#zhan-h-q-2023)

## Evidence

### Zhan H-Q 2023

Zhan H-Q, Zhang X-X, Qin R, Fei J, Dong G-Y, Hao J-H. (2023). Application of integrated problem-based learning combined with lecture-based classroom teaching in undergraduate medical education: An effective teaching model in a Medical School in China. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000034792

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 118 undergraduates (experimental n = 60, control n = 58) at Anhui Medical University; means with 95% CIs printed for each subject, all P < .01. "the experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group in Clinical Skills" with CI 4.19–5.89. No effect sizes printed.

> "the experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group in Clinical Skills (94.20 ± 0.10 vs 89.16 ± 0.41, 95% confidence interval [CI] 4.19–5.89), Internal Medicine I (83.38 ± 1.25 vs 77.49 ± 1.62, 95% CI: 1.85–9.93)"
