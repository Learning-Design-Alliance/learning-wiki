### Quinn 2020

Quinn, Jamie M.; Wagner, Richard K.; Petscher, Yaacov; Roberts, Greg; Menzel, Andrew J.; Schatschneider, Christopher. (2020). Differential Co-Development of Vocabulary Knowledge and Reading Comprehension for Students with and without Learning Disabilities. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000382

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Numerical result derived from the fitted multiple-group LCS model and displayed as a vector plot (Figure 3) of expected growth by starting value, grouped by LD status, with 90% confidence ellipses and a 200-student subset of actual estimates. This is a model-derived expectation, not a measured experiment.

> "for children who start with high vocabulary knowledge but low reading comprehension skills, students who do not have an LD are expected to grow nearly twice as much in reading comprehension as compared to students who have an LD (see the bottom right portion of Figure 3)"
