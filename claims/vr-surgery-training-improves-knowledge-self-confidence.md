# A randomized controlled trial found immersive virtual reality surgery training improved the knowledge and self-confidence of surgical residents

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a multisite, single-blind, parallel randomized controlled trial with 95 residents from 7 dental schools, the group using a virtual reality surgery application (LMC and Oculus Rift) showed improved knowledge and self-confidence compared with a control group using a standard presentation. [→ Alvarez-Lopez F 2019](#alvarez-lopez-f-2019)
