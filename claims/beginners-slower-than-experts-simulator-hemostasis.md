## Evidence

### Lee Dong Seok 2019

Lee Dong Seok, Ahn Ji Yong, Lee Gin Hyug. (2019). A Newly Designed 3-Dimensional Printer-Based Gastric Hemostasis Simulator with Two Modules for Endoscopic Trainees (with Video). Gut and Liver. https://doi.org/10.5009/gnl18389

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Procedure-time comparison across experience levels in the simulator test with 11 beginners and 10 experts, each measured five times. Beginners averaged "72.7±7.1 seconds" for hemoclipping versus 19.7±1.2 for experts; injection times were 92.1±9.8 versus 36.3±2 seconds.

> "The mean procedure time per bleeding hole with hemoclipping module was 72.7±7.1 seconds for beginner group and 19.7±1.2 seconds for expert group."
