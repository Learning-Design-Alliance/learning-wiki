# Score-access barriers push musicians with BLV toward memorization and listening-based learning, and accessible notation tools miss performance-critical details

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 qualitative `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Because braille must be read by touch and cannot be consulted while playing, memorization becomes the default pathway for learners with BLV. [→ score-1](#score-1)
`q2 i?` Screen-reader-based notation tools deliver information slowly and omit performance-critical details such as bowings, fingerings, and special techniques. [→ score-2](#score-2)
