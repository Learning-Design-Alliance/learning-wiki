## Evidence

### Pan Pan 2026

Pan Pan, Zhou YaXiong, Jiang YaoWen, Xia YiQin, Han TianYong, Pan E., Chen Yao. (2026). Knowledge-based Basic Life Support competency and associated factors among healthcare professionals across different hospital levels: a multicenter cross-sectional study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1881120

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression with primary hospitals as reference. The article reports 'OR = 1.48, 95% CI: 1.09–2.00, p = 0.011' for tertiary hospitals and notes the secondary hospital association was not statistically significant.

> "HCPs working in tertiary hospitals also had higher odds of achieving high knowledge-based BLS competency than those working in primary hospitals (OR = 1.48, 95% CI: 1.09–2.00, p = 0.011). The association for secondary hospitals was not statistically significant (OR = 1.27, 95% CI: 0.89–1.81, p = 0.179)."

### Pan Pan 2026 (2)

Pan Pan, Zhou YaXiong, Jiang YaoWen, Xia YiQin, Han TianYong, Pan E., Chen Yao. (2026). Knowledge-based Basic Life Support competency and associated factors among healthcare professionals across different hospital levels: a multicenter cross-sectional study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1881120

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear regression for total BLS knowledge score. The article reports 'B = 3.75, 95% CI: 1.76–5.75, p < 0.001' for tertiary and 'B = 3.25, 95% CI: 0.92–5.58, p = 0.006' for secondary hospitals.

> "After adjustment, working in a tertiary hospital (B = 3.75, 95% CI: 1.76–5.75, p < 0.001) or secondary hospital (B = 3.25, 95% CI: 0.92–5.58, p = 0.006) remained positively associated with total BLS knowledge score."
