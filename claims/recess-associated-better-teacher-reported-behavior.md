## Evidence

### Howie Erin K. 2023

Howie Erin K., Perryman Kristi L., Moretta Joseph, Cameron Laura. (2023). Educational outcomes of recess in elementary school children: A mixed-methods systematic review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294340

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional analysis within the ECLS-K longitudinal cohort (Barros et al., 2009) of 10,301–11,624 third graders, 70% of whom had 15 minutes or more of recess per day, found "better teacher reported behavior" among students with some recess. No effect size was printed.

> "Students with some recess were in classrooms with better teacher reported behavior compared to those with no or minimal recess."
