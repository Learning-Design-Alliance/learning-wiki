## Evidence

### Li Ting 2025

Li Ting, Hu Yidan, Song Ruohong, Liu Hangyu, Gao Xin, Wang Fei, Xiao Chengwei, Liu Xilin. (2025). Unveiling the ultimate advantage: a meta-analysis of 3D visualization and problem-based learning in orthopedic education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06654-5

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of four RCTs (N = 230) with a fixed-effect model found "students had higher overall satisfaction with 3D + PBL teaching compared to LBL teaching". GRADE certainty was moderate; no significant heterogeneity was observed (I2 = 14%).

> "The meta-analysis indicated that students had higher overall satisfaction with 3D + PBL teaching compared to LBL teaching (OR = 3.32, 95% CI: 1.84–5.99, P < 0.0001; see Fig. 11)."
