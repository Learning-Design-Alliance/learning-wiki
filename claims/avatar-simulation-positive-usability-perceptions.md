## Evidence

### Jessica Thompson 2020

Jessica Thompson, Simon White, Stephen Chapman. (2020). Interactive Clinical Avatar Use in Pharmacist Preregistration Training: Design and Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/17146

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire review of the EHC interactive clinical avatar by a purposive sample of 10 preregistration trainees and newly qualified pharmacists, analyzed descriptively. "The fidelity of the simulation was reported as realistic by 80% (8/10) of participants"; all Likert medians were in the agree or strongly agree categories.

> "The fidelity of the simulation was reported as realistic by 80% (8/10) of participants, with it making them feel as if they were the pharmacist and were making the same decisions as in real practice (70% [7/10] and 90% [9/10] agreement, respectively)."
