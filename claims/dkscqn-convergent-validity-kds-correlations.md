## Evidence

### Mubeen Raffa 2026

Mubeen Raffa, Khan Asghar, Kiyani Humaira Shamim, Butt Ayesha Kamal, Bogaardt Hans, Noveen Sadaf. (2026). Development and validation of dysphagia knowledge and self-reported competence questionnaire for nurses. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0358250

`q3 · i3` · `design · r3`

A total of 418 participants completed both the DKSCQ-N and the KDS developed by Rhoda et al. Table 4 reports correlations of r = 0.799 (signs and symptoms), r = 0.896 (complications) and r = 0.715 (management), all significant at p < 0.001, providing external convergent evidence mainly for the knowledge domain.

> "The results presented in Table 4 indicate a strong and statistically significant correlation across the corresponding subdomains. These findings provide evidence for knowledge domain, as KDS is primarily a knowledge-based scale."
