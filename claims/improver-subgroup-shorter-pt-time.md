# Post hoc subgroup analysis: trainees who improved their OSATS score between sessions had shorter PT times and faster knot completion at session 2

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the post hoc analysis, group 2 (OSATS score increased between sessions, n = 21) had significantly shorter PT time than group 1 (n = 22, p = 0.024), significantly higher OSATS at session 2 (p = 0.0005), and significantly shorter knot completion time (130 s vs 211 s, p = 0.014). `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post hoc subgroup analysis of individual 5-mm results, splitting the 43 trainees by OSATS score evolution (group 1 n = 22, group 2 n = 21). The article reports knot time "significantly shorter in group 2" and that only group 2 improved time between sessions (p < 10−5); PT time was also shorter in group 2 (p = 0.024).

> "The knot completion time was also significantly shorter in group 2 (130s [120;156]) than in group 1 (211s [169;241], p = 0.014) at the end of session #2, and only group 2 trainees significantly improved their time between both sessions"
