## Evidence

### Herbstreit Frank 2017

Herbstreit Frank, Merse Stefanie, Schnell Rainer, Noack Marcel, Dirkmann Daniel, Besuch Anna, Peters Jürgen. (2017). Impact of standardized patients on the training of medical students to manage emergencies. Medicine 96(5):e5933. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000005933

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome in the same randomized trial: all students received identical ACLS training and were assessed on a high-fidelity simulator. Scores did not differ ("P = 0.253"), indicating no transfer beyond the taught scenarios.

> "the students' performance in the ACLS scenario, that is, a scenario unrelated to the test scenarios, did not differ between cohorts (standardized patient cohort: points 15 ± 1.3 vs traditional cohort: 15.2 points ± 1.1; P = 0.253)"
