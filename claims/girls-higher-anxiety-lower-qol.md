## Evidence

### Zile Inta 2021

Zile Inta, Bite Ieva, Krumina Indra, Folkmanis Valdis, Tzivian Lilian. (2021). Association between Anxiety, Quality of Life and Academic Performance of the Final-Year-Students in Latvia. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18115784

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Gender comparison in the 287-student cohort using Mann–Whitney tests; the article reports "Girls had significantly higher level of anxiety than boys (p < 0.01)", with 31.9% of girls versus 14.5% of boys highly anxious at baseline. No effect size printed.

> "Girls had significantly higher level of anxiety than boys (p < 0.01)."

### Zile Inta 2021 (2)

Zile Inta, Bite Ieva, Krumina Indra, Folkmanis Valdis, Tzivian Lilian. (2021). Association between Anxiety, Quality of Life and Academic Performance of the Final-Year-Students in Latvia. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18115784

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Gender comparison of SF-36 domain scores; the article states "Girls had significantly lower QOL for all domains"; e.g., baseline energy/fatigue median difference was −14.5 [−19.3; −9.80] favouring boys. No standardised effect size printed.

> "Girls had significantly lower QOL for all domains, both at baseline and follow-up (p < 0.01 for all domains both at baseline and for follow-up)"
