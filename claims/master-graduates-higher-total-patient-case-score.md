## Evidence

### Peikert S. A. 2026

Peikert S. A., Wiegel S. V., Blank J., Woelber J. P., Ratka‐Krüger P. (2026). Assessment of Knowledge Levels in a Blended‐Learning CPD Programme in Periodontology: A Cross‐Sectional Comparative Study. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70099

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory cross-sectional comparison of 32 Master graduates and 26 Curriculum graduates completing a standardised fictitious patient case under supervised examination conditions. Group comparison via one-way ANOVA and Scheffé method showed the total-score difference was significant (p < 0.05), with the contrast reported as −11.30 (t = −7.99).

> "For the total score, group 1 had a mean of 33.94 (SD 4.46) and group 2 had 22.63 (SD 6.53)."
