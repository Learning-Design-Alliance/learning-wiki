Smart, R., Adams, D., & Simpson, K. (2026). The who, how and what of educational outcome research for autistic students published in the last decade: A systematic quantitative literature review. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 286–305. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70015

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Quality assessment of all 112 items using the STROBE checklist (maximum 22). The review reports a "mean global reporting quality score was 16.9 (76.8%)", with academic items highest and disciplinary items lowest.

> "The mean global reporting quality score was 16.9 (76.8%), with academic achievement items reporting on the highest average number of items (mean 17.2, 78.2%) and disciplinary outcome items on the lowest average number of items (mean 15.2, 69.1%)."
