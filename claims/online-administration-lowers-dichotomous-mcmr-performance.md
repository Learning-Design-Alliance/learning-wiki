## Evidence

### Alexis Olsho 2021

Alexis Olsho, Trevor I. Smith, Philip Eaton, Charlotte Zimmerman, Andrew Boudreaux, and Suzanne White Brahmia. (2021). Online test administration results in students selecting more responses to multiple-choice-multiple-response items. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2106.02137](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2106.02137)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Classical test theory item difficulty comparison (percentage answering completely correctly) for the four single-correct MCMR items, paper vs. online at Institution 1, shown in Fig. 6. The article reports "binomial test p < . 001" for items 1, 5, and 6 with "Cohen's h, 0.38 < h < 0.46".

> "For three of the four items (1, 5, and 6), the difference in difficulty is statistically signiﬁcant (binomial test p < . 001), though the effect size is small (Cohen's h, 0.38 < h < 0.46)."
