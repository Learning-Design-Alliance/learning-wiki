Evidence¶

Knellesen 2024¶

Knellesen, J., Händel, M., & Golke, S. (2024). Effects of reading instructions on pre-service teachers' judgment bias when learning from texts. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09371-w

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Testing phase of Experiment 1, CLT text: contrast analysis between instruction groups on application performance bias. The article reports that "participants in the application instruction group did not differ in their overestimation from students in the other two conditions"; all three groups showed significant overestimation.

"However, contrary to H2.4, participants in the application instruction group did not differ in their overestimation from students in the other two conditions, t(63) < 1, p = .521."
