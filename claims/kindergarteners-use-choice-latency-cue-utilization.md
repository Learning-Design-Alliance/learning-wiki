## Evidence

### Kristin Kolloff 2025

Kristin Kolloff, Ebru Ger, Claudia M. Roebers. (2025). The role of metacognitive experience: Can feedback training affect kindergarteners' monitoring accuracy? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09422-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed model with confidence judgments as dependent variable, choice latency as Level 1 predictor, and condition as Level 2 predictor in 214 children. The latency effect was highly significant (p < 0.001); no main effects for condition or measurement point emerged.

> "As expected, shorter choice latencies in the recognition test were significantly asso- ciated with higher CJs (γ = −0.06, SE = 0.01, t (6,543) = −7.93, p < 0.001), indicat- ing substantial cue utilization."
