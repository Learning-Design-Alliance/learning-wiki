### Lura Forcum and William Harms 2005

Lura Forcum and William Harms. (2005). New report shows that high-quality induction can help keep teachers on the job. University of Chicago press release announcing Keeping New Teachers: A First Look at the Influence of Induction in the Chicago Public Schools. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Survey analysis of novice CPS teachers' backgrounds. The report also states that among novice high school teachers, "having a master's degree or higher and being a graduate of CPS are associated with being less likely to remain in the profession." Background data show 27 percent of new elementary and 21 percent of new high school teachers were CPS graduates.
