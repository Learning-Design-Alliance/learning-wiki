Team-based learning (TBL) curriculum combined with video vignettes improves performance of undergraduate medical students on OSCE compared with TBL alone. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary simple linear regression analysis relating overall OSCE score to written knowledge-test success in each study group separately, reported as R-squared percentages. The article prints R-squared values only; no standardized effect size or regression coefficients are given.

> "In the comparator group, given the R2, 11% of the variability of the overall OSCE score was explained by the written test score. In the intervention group, given the R2, 14% of the variability of the overall OSCE score was explained by the written test score."
