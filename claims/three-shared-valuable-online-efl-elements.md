## Evidence

### Yang Chunye 2023

Yang Chunye, Xu Defeng. (2023). Predicting student and instructor e-readiness and promoting e-learning success in online EFL class during the COVID-19 pandemic: A case from China. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0284334

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multiple-choice question in which respondents chose 6 of 15 items as the most valuable learning elements (n = 5914 students; n = 1752 instructors). Teacher-student interaction had a universalization rate of 59.5% among students and 49.83% among instructors, per Tables 7 and 8.

> "Students and instructors had the same attitude towards three elements: "teacher-student interaction", "practicing problem-solving ability" and "teacher presence" (see Fig 5), which showed that students and instructors want to involve in the class through interaction and improve the problem-solving ability after the EFL class."
