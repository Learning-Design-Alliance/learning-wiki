Blackwood Lachlan 2025¶

Blackwood Lachlan, Hollo Zachary, Amaratunge Lahiru. (2025). Electronic learning methods compared to traditional learning methods in anaesthesia education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07502-2

q1 · i? · review · r3

The review suggests that blended learning or flipped classroom models may enable learners to process theoretical and knowledge-based constructs using e-learning resources at their own pace prior to traditional practical skills learning.

"Utilising both approaches – both traditional and e-learning modalities, blended learning or ‘flipped classroom’ models help may also enable learners to process theoretical and knowledge-based constructs using e-learning resources at their own pace, prior to engaging in more traditional methods of learning practical skills in the simulated or clinical environment."
