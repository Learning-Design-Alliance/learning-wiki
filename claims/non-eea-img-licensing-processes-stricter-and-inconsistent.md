## Evidence

### Price T 2018

Price T, Lynn N, Coombes L, Roberts M, Gale T, de Bere SR, Archer J. (2018). The International Landscape of Medical Licensing Examinations: A Typology Derived From a Systematic Review. Int J Health Policy Manag. https://doi.org/10.15171/ijhpm.2018.32

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Musoke's dissertation and other work, that in Sweden non-EU/EEA IMGs described the licensing process as "disorganized, bureaucratic, and stricter than the process undertaken by EU/EEA IMGs". The review reports this second-hand and notes a lack of readily available research on this category of NLE.

> "In Sweden for example, non-EU/EEA IMGs have described the national licensing process in that country as being disorganized, bureaucratic, and stricter than the process undertaken by EU/EEA IMGs."
