# Student trust in the instructor positively predicts overall course engagement in an active-learning course

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Trust significantly predicts student engagement in an active-learning course, over and above growth mindset. [→ Cavanagh A. J. 2018](#cavanagh-a-j-2018)

## Evidence

### Cavanagh A. J. 2018

Cavanagh A. J., Chen X., Bathgate M., Frederick J., Hanauer D. I., Graham M. J. (2018). Trust, Growth Mindset, and Student Commitment to Active Learning in a College Science Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-06-0107

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate regression from the same N = 245 survey: trust significantly predicted engagement (β = 0.35, t(239) = 5.66, p < 0.001), with growth mindset nonsignificant in the same model. Engagement was measured with five subscales of the Motivated Strategies for Learning Questionnaire.

> "trust is a strong and positive predictor of each of the three outcomes, including commitment to active learning, β = 0.38, t(238) = 6.29, p < 0.001; engagement, β = 0.35, t(239) = 5.66, p < 0.001"
