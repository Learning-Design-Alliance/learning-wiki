## Evidence

### Sarah Garner 2012

Sarah Garner, Michael J. Tremmel, Sarah J. Schmidt, John P. Wisniewski, Eric Agol. (2012). Evaluation of a College Freshman Diversity Research Program. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1311.5486](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1311.5486)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis linking the student course database with the graduate database by system key (Figure 3), plotting STEM graduation fraction versus grade in first math class for each initial math course. Pre-MAP students generally do very well in their first math classes. A notable exception: Pre-MAP students placing into 300-level math (all transfer students) are less likely than average to pursue STEM majors.

> "Figure 3 shows that a student’s likelihood to major in STEM is greatly affected by their math placement as well as their performance in that math class."
