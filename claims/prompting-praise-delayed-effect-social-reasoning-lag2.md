# High-level prompting and specific praise continue to predict students' social reasoning one turn later (Lag-2), suggesting a vicarious reinforcing experience

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Nagpal 2025

Nagpal, M., Lin, T.-J., Kraatz, E., Kim, S., Ha, S. Y., & Glassman, M. (2025). Reciprocal interactions between teachers' instructional moves and students' social reasoning during collaborative small group discussions. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09718-7

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same SDA of the 24 Week-4 CSR discussions. The article reports the Lag-2 effects "remained significant" with odds ratios of 1.86 for high-level prompting and 6.30 for specific praise, and offers the vicarious reinforcement interpretation as a suggestion.

> "At Lag-2 but not Lag-3, the effects of high-level prompting (B = 0.62, SE = 0.22, p = 0.00, 95% CI [0.18, 1.05]) and specific praise (B = 1.84, SE = 0.71, p = 0.01, 95% CI [0.44, 3.25]) remained significant, suggesting that high-level prompting and specific praise may have created a vicarious reinforcing experience for students to exercise their social reasoning"
