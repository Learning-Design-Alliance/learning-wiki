The IES-sponsored evaluation of Enhanced Academic Instruction in After-School Programs delivered school-day math curricula adapted to after-school settings; students received "an average of 57 hours of enhanced math instruction." The guide prints no effect size, only significance.

"had modest but statistically significant improvements in math achievement after one year compared with students in a regular after-school program."
