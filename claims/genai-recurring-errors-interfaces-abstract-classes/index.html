Marina Lepp 2026¶

Marina Lepp, Joosep Kaimre. (2026). Revisiting the Performance of Generative Artificial Intelligence on Introductory Object-Oriented Programming Assessments: Insights from 2026. https://orcid.org/0000-0003-3303-5245

q2 · i? · design · r2

Error analysis across the graded test and exam solutions identified recurring misconceptions clustered around conceptually demanding OOP topics. The article states these concepts "continue to represent a persistent weakness of current GenAI systems", consistent with prior studies.

"Several evaluated GenAI systems incorrectly assumed that abstract classes implementing interfaces must provide implementations for all interface methods, confused the use of the extends and implements keywords, misunderstood the role of the @Override annotation, or incorrectly interpreted the use of the abstract keyword within interfaces."
