Supplementary physiological tests accompanying the acuity-sustain program. The retina was found to lose little, if any, more efficiency under one lighting system than another, tested after as many as ten hours of work under the system in question.

> "The loss of efficiency sustained by the eye in an unfavor- able lighting situation seems to be muscular, not retinal. The retina has been found to lose little, if any, more in efficiency under one than another of the lighting systems employed"
