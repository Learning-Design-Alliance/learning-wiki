# GeoGebra instruction with guided discovery worksheets produced significantly higher quadratic functions achievement than textbook-based instruction in a randomized pretest-posttest experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Tenth-grade students taught quadratic functions with GeoGebra software and guided discovery worksheets achieved significantly higher achievement scores than control students taught with textbook and paper-pencil methods. [→ Övez 2018](#ovez-2018)

## Evidence

### Övez 2018

Filiz Tuba Dikkartin Övez. (2018). The Impact of Instructing Quadratic Functions with the Use of Geogebra Software on Students’ Achievement and Level of Reaching Acquisitions. International Education Studies, 11(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n7p1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest-posttest control group experiment with 62 tenth-grade students randomly assigned to groups (31 each). Independent samples t-test on achievement scores: test group gain 2.65 (pretest 0.41 to posttest 3.06) versus control gain 1.1 (0.38 to 1.48), "a significant difference between achievement score averages".

> "According to “t” test results, it was observed that there was a significant difference between achievement score averages [t = -5.61; p=.001<.05]. This finding indicates that for students’ achievement level computer –aided education applied in test group was more effective than the technique of text book, paper, and pencil used in the control group."
