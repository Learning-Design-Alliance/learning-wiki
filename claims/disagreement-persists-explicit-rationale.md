Ginsburg S. (2014). Duty hours as viewed through a professionalism lens. BMC Medical Education 14(Suppl 1):S15. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-S1-S15

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a study of faculty evaluations of student professionalism in which the students' rationale was made explicit; disagreement persisted, and attendings expressed tension over "the behaviour or the reasoning behind it". The review offers no effect size for this finding.

> "In one study, disagreement among faculty persisted even when the rationale behind students’ behaviour was made explicit [13]."
