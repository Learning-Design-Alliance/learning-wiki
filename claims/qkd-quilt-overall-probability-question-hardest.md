# Computing the overall probability of generating a shared key bit is the hardest QKD QuILT skill, improving from roughly 10% to roughly 60% correct

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` On the question requiring students to combine four probabilities to determine the overall probability of generating a shared bit, only slightly over 10% answered correctly on the pretest versus roughly 60% on the posttest (Cohen's d = 1.16). [→ DeVore 2016](#devore-2016)

## Evidence

### DeVore 2016

DeVore, S. and Singh, C. (2016). An Interactive Learning Tutorial on Quantum Key Distribution. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.01301](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.01301)

`q3 · i3` · `design · r3`

Results for Q8, which requires properly accounting for each of four probabilities in a table to determine an overall probability of roughly 44.2%. The table row prints 13.5% pretest and 62.2% posttest correct, t = 8.32, Cohen's d = 1.16, p < 0.001.

> "Table 2 shows that slightly over 10% of students answered this question correctly on the pretest while roughly 60% of students determined this probabi lity correctly on the posttest"
