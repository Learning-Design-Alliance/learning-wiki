### Reading Horizons Discovery 2022

Reading Horizons Discovery. (2022). Evidence rating summary, promising. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/reading-horizons-discovery-2/

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A retrospective matched (quasi-experimental) study of K-3 students in Los Angeles Unified School District compared fall 2021 to spring 2022 DIBELS gains. After propensity score matching, "4,706 treatment students nested in 323 schools and 8,805 comparison students nested in 510 schools" were analyzed; the effect size was "+0.05".

> "Results showed a statistically significant positive impact on DIBELS 8 composite score for students in the treatment group compared to matched comparison students (effect size = +0.05), earning the Reading Horizons Discovery a Promising rating."
