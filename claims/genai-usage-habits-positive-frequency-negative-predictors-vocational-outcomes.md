## Evidence

### Song Yafeng 2026

Song Yafeng, Zhao Kangjian, Li Li, Dong Wei. (2026). Research on the Impact of Generative Artificial Intelligence Usage Behavior on the Learning Outcomes of Higher Vocational Students. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071166

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on 1110 valid questionnaires from Chinese higher vocational students, with gender and grade as controls. The printed equation gives a negative coefficient for usage frequency (−0.122X1) and positive coefficients for usage habits (0.258X2) and usage contexts (0.196X3); per the article's variable definitions, X1 is frequency, X2 habits, X3 contexts.

> "Substituting the data in Table 6 yields the regression equation for the relationship between higher vocational students’ GenAI usage behavior and their perceived learning outcomes. Y = 2.72 − 0.122X1 + 0.258X2 + 0.196X3 − 0.059Z1 − 0.028Z2 − 0.023Z3"

### Song Yafeng 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

> "The F-test (F = 51.347, p < 0.001) confirms the overall validity of the regression model."
