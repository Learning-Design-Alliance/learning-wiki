## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving student understanding of addition of angular momentum in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06165)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Question given to 26 students asking them to choose a basis for two spin-1/2 particles and write the matrix of S1·S2 in that basis. About "33% of the students did not realize that the basis vectors in the coupled representation are eigenstates of the operator ˆS2"; 15% could not find a complete basis set, and about 8% listed subspace basis vectors as product-space basis vectors.

> "About 33% of the students did not realize that the basis vectors in the coupled representation are eigenstates of the operator ˆS2, so that the matrix elements of ˆS1 · ˆS2 can be calculated more easily in the coupled representation than in the uncoupled representation."
