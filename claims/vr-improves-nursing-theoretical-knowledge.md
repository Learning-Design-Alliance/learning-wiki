# VR instruction improves nursing students' theoretical knowledge more than traditional teaching methods (SMD = 0.97)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Meta-analysis of eleven RCTs found VR significantly improved nursing students' theoretical knowledge compared to traditional teaching methods (SMD = 0.97, 95% CI [0.48, 1.46], P < 0.001). [→ Liu Kai 2023](#liu-kai-2023)

## Evidence

### Liu Kai 2023

Liu Kai, Zhang Weiwei, Li Wei, Wang Ting, Zheng Yanxue. (2023). Effectiveness of virtual reality in nursing education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04662-x

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis in Review Manager 5.3 of eleven randomized controlled trials comparing VR instruction with traditional nursing teaching. High heterogeneity (p < 0.001, I2 = 92%) required a random effects model; the pooled "SMD = 0.97" favoured VR for theoretical knowledge.

> "The combined results showed that the use of VR technology was effective in improving students’ theoretical knowledge compared to other traditional nursing teaching methods (SMD = 0.97, 95% CI [0.48, 1.46], P < 0.001, Fig. 5) ."
