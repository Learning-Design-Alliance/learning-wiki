Evidence¶

Traulsen 2024¶

Traulsen, S. J., & Zander, L. (2024). Straighten your back, open your arms! Effects of instructor's body postures in educational videos on students' interest and motivation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101959

q3 · i2 · causal · r3

Pilot experiment, 2×2 ANOVA on perceived agency (α = .81). The vertical dimension was significant (partial η2 = .066) whereas the horizontal dimension "had no effect" (partial η2 = .009, p = .197); the interaction was also non-significant.

"Perceived agency was significantly influenced by the variations on the vertical dimension, F (1, 190) = 13.415, p < .001, partial η2 = .066, while variations of the horizontal dimension, F (1, 190) = 1.675, p = .197, partial η2 = .009, had no effect."

Traulsen 2024 (2)¶

Traulsen, S. J., & Zander, L. (2024). Straighten your back, open your arms! Effects of instructor's body postures in educational videos on students' interest and motivation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101959

q3 · i2 · causal · r3

Preregistered experiment with 434 students; 2×2 ANOVA showed vertical (partial η2 = .041) and, "now also", horizontal effects (partial η2 = .017) plus a significant interaction (partial η2 = .011), with the lowest agency in the slumped/closed condition.

"perceived agency was, significantly affected by variations of the vertical dimension, F (1, 430) = 18.385, p < .001, partial η2 = .041, but now also by the horizontal dimension, F (1, 430) = 7.289, p = .007, partial η2 = .017."
