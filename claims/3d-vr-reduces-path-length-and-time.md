### Shakir T. 2025

Shakir T., Lingam G., Francis N., Chand M. (2025). Acclimatising with 3D virtual reality video improves simulator performance: initial findings of a randomised controlled feasibility trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12153-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same feasibility RCT, automated performance metrics aggregated across the four tasks. The article reports "The mean instrument path length for the 3D VR video group was 305.09 cm" versus 413.72 cm (p < 0.01), and 183.59 s versus 294.72 s completion time (p < 0.01).

> "The mean instrument path length for the 3D VR video group was 305.09 cm, compared to 413.72 cm for the 2D video group (p < 0.01). The mean completion time for the 3D VR video group was 183.59 s, while the 2D video group took an average of 294.72 s (p < 0.01)"
