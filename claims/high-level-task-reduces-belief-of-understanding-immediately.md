## Evidence

### Madeleine Hörnlein and Christoph Kulgemeyer 2025

Madeleine Hörnlein and Christoph Kulgemeyer. (2025). Learning Science and the Illusion of Understanding: Exploring the Effects of Integrating Learning Tasks after Explainer Videos. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.02824](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.02824)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Study 1 (N = 244 physics students) randomized experiment: paired-samples t-test within the high-level task group comparing belief of understanding after the video versus after the task. The article reports a statistically significant decrease with a medium effect size, "𝑑=0.69".

> "In the task group, scores on the belief of understanding scale before and after the task showed a statistically significant decrease with a medium effect size in a paired-samples t-test (𝑡(88)=6.50, 𝑝<0.001, 𝑑=0.69)."
