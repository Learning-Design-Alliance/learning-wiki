Descriptive statistics and one-way ANOVA on the full sample (n = 12,960; group ns 4329, 4510, 4121 as printed in Table 1). Mean total academic score was 216.560 in the middle group versus 210.796 and 211.336; mean weekly activity was 2.920 h overall. These are unadjusted group means, not regression estimates.

> "Both are the highest in the group of teenagers whose weekly physical activity duration is greater than 0 and less than 3.5 h. The physical fitness level was the highest among the adolescents who exercised more than 3.5 h per week."
