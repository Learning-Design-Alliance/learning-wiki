## Evidence

### Fulya Turk 2017

Fulya Turk. (2017). Evaluation of the Effects of Conflict Resolution, Peace Education and Peer Mediation: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Education Studies; Vol. 11, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p25

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis across five duration bands (from 1-10 hours to 31 hours and above, plus unspecified). All effect sizes were positive, with the shortest and a mid-range band largest, but the difference was not statistically significant.

> "the effect sizes of studies with application durations of 0-10 hours (g= 1,837) and 21-30 hours (1,894) are greater. However, the difference between the effect sizes is not statistically significant since the Q=7,502 value is lower than the critical value of 11,070"
