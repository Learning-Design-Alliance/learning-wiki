## Evidence

### Eckenrode et al. 2016

Eckenrode, J. W., Prather, E. E., & Wallace, C. S. (2016). Correlations between students' written responses to lecture-tutorial questions and their understandings of key astrophysics concepts. *Journal of College Science Teaching, 45*(3), 65–73. [doi:10.2505/4/jcst16_045_03_65](https://doi.org/10.2505/4/jcst16_045_03_65)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of written responses from 454 students in spring 2010–2012 Astro 101 mega-courses, using Pearson's R²; Table 2 prints R² values of 0.012, 0.017, 0.043 (gains) and 0.037, 0.047, 0.000 (exam items). The article reports "no significant correlation" for both outcome measures.

> "We observed no significant correlation between students’ correctness and coherency scores and their normalized gain scores or their scores to related exam items."
