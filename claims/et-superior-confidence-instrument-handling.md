## Evidence

### Mooney SS 2022

Mooney SS, Hiscock RJ, Hicks L, Narula S, Maher PJ, Readman E, Pendlebury A, Ellett L. (2022). We live in a virtual world: Training the trainee using an integrated visual reality simulator curriculum. Aust N Z J Obstet Gynaecol. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13521

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Six-month live operating assessment in the prospective cohort: "ET demonstrating superior general laparoscopy skills scores (adjusted P = 0.001 for OSA‐LS ‘confidence of instrument handling’ domains)"; only this comparison survived Holm-Sidak adjustment.

> "A significant difference was seen between NT and ET, with ET demonstrating superior general laparoscopy skills scores (adjusted P = 0.001 for OSA‐LS ‘confidence of instrument handling’ domains)."

### Mooney SS 2022 (2)

Mooney SS, Hiscock RJ, Hicks L, Narula S, Maher PJ, Readman E, Pendlebury A, Ellett L. (2022). We live in a virtual world: Training the trainee using an integrated visual reality simulator curriculum. Aust N Z J Obstet Gynaecol. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13521

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The overall OSA-LS total score contrast between experienced and novice groups was significant only unadjusted (P = 0.009); the article reports scores for both trained groups remained well below expected, with ET median 29.3 (26.3–32.0) of 45.

> "There was a trend toward higher overall OSA‐LS scores (unadjusted P = 0.009) but this did not remain significant after adjustment. Scores for both trained groups remained well below expected"
