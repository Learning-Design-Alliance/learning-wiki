## Evidence

### Abdelhalim 2015

Safaa M. Abdelhalim. (2015). Children Literature Based Program for Developing EFL Primary Pupils’ Life Skills and Language Learning Strategies. English Language Teaching, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n2p178

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA on the language learning strategies questionnaire post-test yielded F = 256.586, p = .0001, η² = .85, favoring experimental groups over control. The article characterizes this as a large effect.

> "Table 1 reveals that there is a statistically significant difference between the two Experimental groups, and the control group students’ mean scores on the post-administration of the three life skills measures and the language learning strategies questionnaire at the level of (0.01), in favor of the experimental groups"
