### Deliang Wang and Cunling Bian 2026

Deliang Wang and Cunling Bian. (2026). Scaffolding Critical Engagement with GenAI: Transforming Ethnic Minority Preparatory Students’ Collaborative Discourse in Prompt Engineering Tasks. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28630

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of reflective essays found students experienced the scaffolding not merely as technical training but as a challenge to blind trust in algorithmic outputs; many reported a shift toward seeing themselves as active evaluators or gatekeepers of AI-generated content rather than passive recipients.

> "the teacher intervention—particularly the modeling with contrasting cases—functioned as an important turning point that reconfigured their collaborative norms with GenAI."
