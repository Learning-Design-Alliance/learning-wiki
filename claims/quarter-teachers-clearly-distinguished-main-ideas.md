### Mirzagitova 2016

Mirzagitova, A. L. & Akhmetov, L. G. (2016). Formation of the Professional and Didactic Culture of the Future Teacher. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(14), 6675-6689. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1115889

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of 34 subject teachers (average age 37-40 years, predominantly female, 28 of 34) conducted by students during teaching practice in the 2012-13 and 2013-14 academic years. The article reports "only ¼ of the teachers (9)" clearly distinguish the main ideas and the basic concepts.

> "The observation and analysis of the teachers’ pedagogical activity has showed that during classes only ¼ of the teachers (9) clearly distinguish the main ideas and the basic concepts. The rest of them do it in part, or do not do it at all."
