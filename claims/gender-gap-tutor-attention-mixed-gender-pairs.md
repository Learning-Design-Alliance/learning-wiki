### Qingyang Zhang 2025

Qingyang Zhang, Rose E. Wang, Ana T. Ribeiro, Dora Demszky, Susanna Loeb. (2025). Educator Attention: How computational tools can systematically identify the distribution of a key resource for students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.20135](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.20135)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 2 regresses individual attention on pair composition, own and partner baseline achievement, and relative achievement, clustering standard errors at the pair level. Female students in mixed-gender pairings receive "5.2 p.p. less attention than their male partners" (β = −0.052, p < 0.001); the gap is concentrated in content and management attention.

> "Female students receive significantly less individual attention than their male counterparts, with the largest disparity emerging in mixed-gender pairings, where female students receive 5.2 p.p. less attention than their male partners [ β = −0.052(−0.081, −0.023), p < 0.001], even when controlling for achievement levels."
