## Evidence

### Bialystok Ellen 2011

Bialystok Ellen. (2011). Coordination of Executive Functions in Monolingual and Bilingual Children. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3139691/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

3-way ANOVA on reaction times for language group, condition, and modality. "There was a main effect of condition in which the single task was performed more rapidly than the dual task, F (1, 60) = 152.48, p < .0001", plus a condition-by-modality interaction.

> "There was a main effect of condition in which the single task was performed more rapidly than the dual task, F (1, 60) = 152.48, p < .0001, a main effect of modality, F (1, 60) = 11.56, p < .001, and an interaction of condition and modality, F (1, 60) = 145.01, p < .0001"

### Bialystok Ellen 2011 (2)

Bialystok Ellen. (2011). Coordination of Executive Functions in Monolingual and Bilingual Children. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3139691/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Omnibus 3-way ANOVA on accuracy scores. "A 3-way ANOVA for language group, condition, and modality on accuracy scores revealed an effect of language group, F (1, 61) = 4.26, p < .04, condition, F (1, 61) = 157.96, p < .0001", with modality-by-language and condition-by-modality interactions.

> "A 3-way ANOVA for language group, condition, and modality on accuracy scores revealed an effect of language group, F (1, 61) = 4.26, p < .04, condition, F (1, 61) = 157.96, p < .0001, and interactions of modality and language, F (1, 61) = 5.07, p < .02, and condition and modality, F (1, 61) = 9.18, p < .004"
