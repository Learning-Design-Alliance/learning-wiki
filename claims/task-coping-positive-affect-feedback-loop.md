## Evidence

### Stockinger 2026

Stockinger, K., Daumiller, M., & Dresel, M. (2026). Relations between university teachers' teaching‐related coping strategies and well‐being over time: A cross‐lagged panel analysis. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 54–72. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12777

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent cross-lagged panel model of 489 German university teachers surveyed at T1 (November 2020) and T2 (February 2021), controlling for gender, rank, experience, COVID-19 impact and home-office. The article reports "positive feedback loops" among the two constructs; no standardized effect size is printed for these paths in the quoted text.

> "T1 task‐oriented coping was positively related to the slope of changes in positive affect, and T1 positive affect was positively related to the slope of changes in task‐oriented coping, implying positive feedback loops among these constructs over time, as expected."
