## Evidence

### Saxena Sparsha 2024

Saxena Sparsha, Wright William S, Khalil Mohammed K. (2024). Gender differences in learning and study strategies impact medical students’ preclinical and USMLE step 1 examination performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05494-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent t-tests on LASSI percentile scores from 618 medical students (350 females, 262 males) across six classes, administered after the first semester. The article reports "significant differences in mean scores between males and females" on seven scales; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Anxiety, Attitude, Information Processing, Selecting Main Ideas, Self-Testing, Test Strategies, and Using Academic Resources had significant differences in mean scores between males and females (Table 3)."
