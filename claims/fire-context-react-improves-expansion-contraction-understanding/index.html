Er Nas et al. 2016¶

Sibel Er Nas, Tülay Şenel Çoruhlu and Arzu Kirman Bilgin. (2016). The effect of fire context on the conceptual understanding of students: “expansion-contraction”. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.2958

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of 56 5th graders (experiment 27, control 29) using Mann-Whitney U-test; the article reports "a significant difference in favour of the experiment group" on post-test scores.

"There is a significant difference in favour of the experiment group between the post test scores implemented on experiment and control groups (U= 238, p<0.05)."
