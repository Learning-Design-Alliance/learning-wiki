## Evidence

### Mermelstein 2015

Mermelstein, A. D. (2015). Improving EFL learners’ writing through enhanced extensive reading. Reading in a Foreign Language, 27(2), 182–198. http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttest comparison of the treatment group (TG) and control group (CG) using multivariate analysis of posttest scores in a one-year university EFL study. The article reports the "TG significantly outperformed the CG on five of the six subscales"; no effect size is printed in this comparison.

> "The results indicate that the TG significantly outperformed the CG on five of the six subscales. The one subscale where the TG did not achieve significant gains over the CG was organization."
