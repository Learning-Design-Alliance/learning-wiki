## Evidence

### de Zeeuw Eveline L. 2017

de Zeeuw Eveline L., van Beijsterveldt Catharina E. M., Ehli Erik A., de Geus Eco J. C., Boomsma Dorret I. (2017). Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder Symptoms and Low Educational Achievement: Evidence Supporting A Causal Hypothesis. Behavior Genetics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10519-017-9836-4

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional bivariate twin and within-person regression model in the Netherlands Twin Register sample of 12-year-old twins, using mother-rated Conners ADHD symptoms and the Cito educational achievement test. The article reports the direct path "was significant (β = −0.50, χ2 (1) = 32.99, p < 0.001)" while correcting for genetic and environmental effects.

> "The direct path between ADHD symptoms and educational achievement was significant (β = −0.50, χ2 (1) = 32.99, p < 0.001)."
