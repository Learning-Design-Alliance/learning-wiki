## Subclaims
`q3 i2` L-PMd and IP-sham groups showed significantly greater sleep-related offline gain than R-PMd and RP-sham groups, with the effect emerging after the first night of sleep and persisting across subsequent nights. [→ Taewon Kim 2021](#taewon-kim-2021)

## Evidence

### Taewon Kim 2021

Taewon Kim, John J. Buchanan, Jessica A. Bernard and David L. Wright. (2021). Improving online and offline gain from repetitive practice using anodal tDCS at dorsal premotor cortex. npj Science of Learning 6:31. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00109-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

A 4 (Condition) × 2 (sleep1, sleep2) mixed ANOVA on sleep-related difference scores gave a significant condition effect with printed effect size ηp² = 0.12; post hoc tests showed the L-PMd and IP-S conditions had significantly greater sleep-related offline gain than the R-PMd and RP-S conditions.

> "manner similar to that observed for IP-S, F(3,60) = 2.85, p < .05, ηp 2 = 0.12 (Fig. 1F)."
