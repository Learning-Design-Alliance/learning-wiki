### Yueqiao Jin 2024

The article reports that GLAT scores predicted performance on a GenAI chatbot-supported visual-analytics comprehension task better than the self-reported ChatGPT Literacy Scale, in an OLS regression controlling for baseline score and visualisation literacy (mini-VLAT) with 83 students.
