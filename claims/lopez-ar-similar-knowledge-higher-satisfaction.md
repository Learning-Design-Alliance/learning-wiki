## Subclaims

## Evidence

### K Anniesmitha 2026

K Anniesmitha, Rao K G Mohandas, Aithal P Ashwini, Bandekar Nikita N., Mandal Tatiyana, George Bincy M. (2026). Effectiveness of technology-enhanced learning in neuroanatomy education among health professionals - a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09764-w

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Study of 67 neuropsychology students comparing a mobile AR application for brain visualisation with a 2D textbook approach on knowledge, satisfaction and preference. The review's Table 5 additionally reports a strong AR preference effect (odds = 12.3, χ2 = 12.3, P < 0.001).

> "The two methods boosted knowledge similarly, but the 3D method had greater satisfaction ratings and was more popular among students."
