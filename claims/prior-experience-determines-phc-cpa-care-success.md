Qualitative thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 35 nursing professionals from two Family Health Strategy units; this finding comes from the knowledge-and-experience thematic category (182 RUs, 34.8% of the corpus). The article states "having previous hospital experience or CPR experience is a determining factor".

> "Professionals showed that having previous hospital experience or CPR experience is a determining factor for the success of CPA care in PHC, even though the period of experience was reported as between one and five years for the majority of participants (57.9%)."
