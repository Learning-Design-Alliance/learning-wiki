Coombs 1987¶

Coombs, Lucinda H. (1987). The GED Writing Skills Test Essay Teacher Training Manual. Maine Dept. of Educational and Cultural Services / University of Maine. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED324536

q2 · i? · design · r1

The manual describes the scoring procedure for the 1988 GED essay as printed in its introduction: holistic scoring on a six-point scale, two independent readers, and a third reader when scores differ by more than one point. No reliability statistics are reported.

"The essay will be scored by highly trained readers who will use a six-point holistic scoring scale. The essaywill be read independently by two readers, and if there is a difference oZ more than one pant, a third reader will readthat piece."
