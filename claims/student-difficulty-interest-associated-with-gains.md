### Andrews T. M. 2011

Andrews T. M., Leonard M. J., Colgrove C. A., Kalinowski S. T. (2011). Active Learning Not Associated with Student Learning in a Random Sample of College Biology Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.11-07-0061

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results of the CINS-abbr linear regression model using student posttest survey responses coded on a five-point Likert scale and averaged per course. The article reports both perception variables were "significantly positively associated with student learning on the CINS-abbr."

> "how difficult students found a course relative to past science courses and how interesting students found a course were also significantly positively associated with student learning on the CINS-abbr (p = 0.040 and p = 0.021, respectively)."
