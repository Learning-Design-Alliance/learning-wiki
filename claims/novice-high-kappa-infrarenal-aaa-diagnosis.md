## Evidence

### Nguyen Anh TV 2013

Nguyen Anh TV, Hill Geraldine B, Versteeg Matthew PT, Thomson Ian A, van Rij Andre M. (2013). Novices may be trained to screen for abdominal aortic aneurysms using ultrasound. Cardiovascular Ultrasound. https://doi.org/10.1186/1476-7120-11-42

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kappa-statistic analysis of interobserver agreement on aortic dilatation (≥ 3 cm) between each novice-assessor pair in the 215-participant screening study. The article reports "a Kappa coefficient greater than 0.8" for infrarenal AAA and no agreement for suprarenal dilatation; kappa is a test statistic, not a standardised effect size.

> "Novices had very high levels of agreement with assessors in the diagnosis of infrarenal AAA, with all novices having a Kappa coefficient greater than 0.8. Novices had no agreement with the assessors in the identification of localised suprarenal aortic dilatation."
