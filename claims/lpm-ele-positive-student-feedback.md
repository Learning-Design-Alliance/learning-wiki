Feedback was collected via a questionnaire and free-text comments from three cohorts: a nine-student focus group, 28 first-year students, and over seventy students from all years (Table 3 shows mean Likert scores around 3.6–4.5). Students "found navigation intuitive" and praised clinical cases, though some flagged abbreviations and dense pages.

> "The students reported that they found navigation intuitive, that it successfully communicated the importance of the physical principles of the cardiovascular system and was useful for demonstrating the effects of common cardiovascular pathologies"
