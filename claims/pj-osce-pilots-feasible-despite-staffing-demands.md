## Evidence

### Noll C 2026

Noll C, Mikuteit M, Golon S, Louzek C, Afshar K, Just I, Steffens S. (2026). Feasibility and implementation of a final year Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) in a medical model curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10350-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Project team's feasibility rating following two pilot OSCEs in 2022 with volunteer fifth- and sixth-year students. The team "rated the PJ-OSCE as feasible" despite staffing demands.

> "Despite the considerable staffing requirements, the project team rated the PJ-OSCE as feasible."

### Noll C 2026 (2)

Noll C, Mikuteit M, Golon S, Louzek C, Afshar K, Just I, Steffens S. (2026). Feasibility and implementation of a final year Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) in a medical model curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10350-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Four focus groups (total n = 22) with participating students conducted in October 2022. Participants "emphasized the overall relevance of the examination" while "time constraints during the OSCE and the associated stress were consistently identified as negative aspects".

> "In all four student focus groups, participants emphasized the overall relevance of the examination and its close alignment with clinical practice. However, time constraints during the OSCE and the associated stress were consistently identified as negative aspects."
