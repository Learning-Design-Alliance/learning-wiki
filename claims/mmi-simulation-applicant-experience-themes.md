Wildermuth Anne, Battista Alexis, Anderson LaKesha N. (2024). Simulation in admissions interviews: applicant experiences and programmatic performance prediction. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.78961

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of the post-interview survey's free-response prompt. Of 192 interviewed applicants, 120 (63.1%) completed the survey, 65 responded to the free-response prompt, and 50 (77%) comments were included; authors coded responses inductively for valence (positive, negative, contemplative) and perceived frame (gains or losses).

> "There were several themes among the applicants’ perceived gains and losses. These include Showcasing Strengths and Passion, Feelings of Fairness, Accessing Program Faculty, and Impacts on Certainty"
