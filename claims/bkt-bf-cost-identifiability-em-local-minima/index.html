Related Claims¶

  • A linear regression on skill variables (n, dim, pc) predicts the minimum RSS value for BKT-BF training with high predictive ability — related
  • OptimNN achieves lower test RMSE than EM, CGD, and SGD for fitting BKT and its variants across four tutoring datasets — related
  • The EM solver consistently outperformed stochastic gradient descent for fitting the models, though by a small margin — related
  • In a preliminary PCA, RMSE is highly correlated with the slip parameter S, while T and G appear orthogonal to RMSE — related
  • The very high adjusted R² of the RSS-estimation model may indicate BKT works better when percent correct is very high, leaving little room for T and G — related
  • The identifiability problem of the BKT HMM arises because combinations of P(G) and P(L0) with the same product A give identical functional forms — related
  • The survey reports, citing Desmarais and Baker, that students using the BKT-sequence recommendation algorithm solved more difficult exercises, obtained higher performance and spent more time in the system than students using the traditional approach. — related
  • The Knowledge Tracing Algorithm does not suffer the identifiability problem: all four parameters affect its behavior separately — related
  • BKT implemented as an RNN layer in PyTorch recovers generating parameters comparably to brute-force grid-search BKT while scaling to large datasets — related
  • Algorithmically inferred measurement functions may intensify, rather than resolve, the problem of nomic measurement in analyzing student learning — related
