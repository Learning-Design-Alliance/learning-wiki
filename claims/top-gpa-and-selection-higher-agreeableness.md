# Top pre-university GPA and multifaceted selection groups score higher on agreeableness than lottery-admitted students who did not participate in selection

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Schripsema NR 2016

Schripsema NR, van Trigt AM, van der Wal MA, Cohen-Schotanus J. (2016). How Different Medical School Selection Processes Call upon Different Personality Characteristics. PLoS ONE 11(3): e0150645. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0150645

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA of NEO-FFI agreeableness across four admission groups (F3, 1014 = 3.70; p<0.05); post hoc tests showed top-GPA and selection groups "had higher mean agreeableness scores than the lottery group" (MD 1.38–1.60, p<0.01).

> "The top pre-university group and the group that was accepted in the multifaceted selection process had higher mean agreeableness scores than the lottery group that had not participated in the multifaceted process (MD: 1.38–1.60; SE:0.37–0.46; p<0.01)."
