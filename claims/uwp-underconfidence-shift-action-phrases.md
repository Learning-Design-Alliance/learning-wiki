# Calibration of JOLs shifts toward underconfidence across learning cycles for action phrases under both verbal and enactive encoding, without first-cycle overconfidence

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Calibration shifted significantly toward underconfidence across cycles, with underconfidence in Cycles 2 and 3 but only nonsignificant overestimation in Cycle 1, and this pattern did not vary by encoding type. [→ Kubik 2022](#kubik-2022)

## Evidence

### Kubik 2022

Kubik, V., Jemstedt, A., Mahjub Eshratabadi, H., Schwartz, B. L., & Jönsson, F. U. (2022). The underconfidence-with-practice effect in action memory: The contribution of retrieval practice to metacognitive monitoring. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09288-2

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Controlled lab experiment with 59 undergraduates learning 36 action phrases over three cycles (enactive vs. verbal encoding, between subjects). Calibration shifted toward underconfidence, "F(1.65, 94.29)  = 39.28, p < 0.001, ω2 = 0.178"; Cycle 1 showed only nonsignificant overestimation (d = 0.19), Cycle 2 pronounced underconfidence (d = 0.78).

> "Thus, overall, calibration shifted significantly toward underconfidence, F(1.65, 94.29)  = 39.28, p < 0.001, ω2 = 0.178, both from Cycle 1 to Cycle 2, V = 167.00, p < 0.001, rrb = 0.81, and from Cycle 1 to Cycle 3"
