### Sarı et al. 2017

İrfan Sarı, Nadir Çeliköz, Süleyman Ünal. (2017). The Effect of Peer Support on University Level Students’ English Language Achievements. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131776

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent T-test on posttest scores of the unsuccessful experimental group (Supported, posttest M = 48.38) versus the unsuccessful control group (Not Supported, M = 43.02), each n = 200, t = 3.198, df = 398, p = .001. The article reports "meaningful difference between the postt est scores of supported and those who are not supported"; no effect size is printed for this comparison.

> "The p was realized as 0.001 at the p<0.05 which mea ns there is meaningful difference between the postt est scores of supported and those who are not supported."

### Sarı et al. 2017 (2)

İrfan Sarı, Nadir Çeliköz, Süleyman Ünal. (2017). The Effect of Peer Support on University Level Students’ English Language Achievements. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131776

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

> "Looking a t the effect size (d) to clearly identify the effect of the trea tment, the d of the supported group is 0,59 which i s at the acceptable level but 0,19 (slight increase) for the not supported group"
