## Evidence

### Linda Castañeda 2024

Linda Castañeda, Victoria I. Marín, Daniel Villar-Onrubia. (2024). Relational topologies in the learning activity spaces: operationalising a sociomaterial approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10296-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Sunburst-chart topology of tool introducer versus tool use, classified with passive, interactive and creative uses building on the PICRAT model, from RICT student self-reports. The article reports prescribed tools "are the most dominant for reading and interacting" while creative uses are dominated by personal tools; discovered tools are always a minority for every use.

> "The tools that are used as prescribed by the teacher are the most dominant for reading and interacting. Nevertheless, the more creative the use is, the more dominant the personal tools are, that is, those that students bring from previous experiences and established personal practices."
