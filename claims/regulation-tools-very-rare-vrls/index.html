Related Claims¶

  • Group awareness tools showing peer presence, peer activities, or multiple artifacts are integrated in many educational VRLs, but performance and teacher-presence indicators are never standalone
  • Only a minority of VRL studies assess collaboration quality or learning, but most studies that measure learning report positive impact
  • Guidance tools supporting collaboration are relatively uncommon in educational VRLs, integrated in only 13 of 62 reviewed systems
  • With a scripted collaboration designed to elicit metacognition, deep-level metacognitive regulation stays rare and students mostly accept rather than co-construct or share regulation
  • A systematic review found literature reviews on collaborative learning in VR use very broad VR definitions, with few included papers using HMDs — related
  • Comparison and integration of different types of group awareness information is a research gap despite firmly established positive tool effects — related
  • Collaborative VRLs are predominantly STEM-focused, virtual, non co-located, synchronous, and 2D — related
  • Collaboration is rarely explicitly designed or measured in virtual-laboratory physics studies despite group work being the norm — related
