## Evidence

### Tavşan Oğuz 2026

Tavşan Oğuz, Kara Ongun Seden, Aktaş İlkim, Çelikkol Berk. (2026). Dental students’ self-confidence toward endodontics according to educational stages: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09186-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same longitudinal cohort (n = 98), Friedman test results. The article reports "Self-confidence in rubber dam placement decreased from 3.41 ± 1.10 in the preclinical stage to 2.46 ± 1.21 in the clinical internship"; bleaching also declined. Flare-up management rose at observation then fell at internship.

> "Self-confidence in rubber dam placement decreased from 3.41 ± 1.10 in the preclinical stage to 2.46 ± 1.21 in the clinical internship. Similarly, confidence in performing non-vital bleaching dropped from 3.46 ± 0.98 to 2.70 ± 1.19."
