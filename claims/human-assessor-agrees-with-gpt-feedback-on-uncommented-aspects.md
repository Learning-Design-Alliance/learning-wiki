## Evidence

### Dai 2024

Wei Dai, Yi-Shan Tsai, Jionghao Lin, Ahmad Aldino, Hua Jin, Tongguang Li, Dragan Gašević, Guanliang Chen. (2024). Assessing the Proficiency of Large Language Models in Automatic Feedback Generation: An Evaluation Study. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, 7, 100299. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100299

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Supplementary validation in which a human assessor rated GPT comments on aspects the original instructor had not addressed (Table 9), choosing agree, partially agree, or disagree. "90.10% of these pieces of feedback obtained agreement from the human assessor" for GPT-4; 73.33% for GPT-3.5.

> "more feedback pieces generated by GPT-4 (101 out of 103 pieces of feedback) contained comments that were not commented on by the original human instructors, and 90.10% of these pieces of feedback obtained agreement from the human assessor."
