`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Accuracy verification experiment in wet-lab training comparing the trinocular system with the six-camera system; Table 1 reports per-instrument mean position errors (e.g., grasping forceps 3.28 mm) and attitude angle errors (e.g., 0.86°). The article concludes the system "can measure the movement of instruments with practical accuracy."

> "The missing value of the motion data of the instruments was linearly interpolated, the median of the positional error of the task was approximately 2 mm to 4 mm, and that of the attitude angles was approximately 0.8 to 1.4°."
