## Evidence

### Fabriz 2014

Fabriz, S., Dignath-van Ewijk, C., Poarch, G., Büttner, G. (2014). Fostering self-monitoring of university students by means of a standardized learning journal—a longitudinal study with process analyses. Eur J Psychol Educ, 29, 239–255. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10212-013-0196-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the four-course pre–post comparison, the combined condition (structured learning diary plus weekly course intervention on self-regulated learning) showed gains in both strategy use and self-efficacy; the article reports "increases in their strategy use and their self-efficacy." No effect sizes are printed.
