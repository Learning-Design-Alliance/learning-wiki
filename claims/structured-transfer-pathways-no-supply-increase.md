`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

DDD analysis of CCCCO Datamart data from all departments in all 112 colleges from 2009 to 2014, examining the effect of ADT programs on the number of sections offered. The coefficient on ln(number of sections) was 0.013 (SE = 0.013), not significant.
