Serial mediation analysis (5000 bootstrap samples) on the 110-student sample; the three-mediator serial path H11 was significant with "(95% CI = 0.004 to 0.116)" (Table 5). Underlying path coefficients: emotional experience predicted social belonging (B = 0.748, p < 0.001) and social belonging predicted higher-order thinking (B = 0.269, p < 0.01).

> "technology acceptance had a significant impact on learning satisfaction, through the serial mediating paths of “emotional experience–social belonging–higher-order thinking” (95% CI = 0.004 to 0.116)"
