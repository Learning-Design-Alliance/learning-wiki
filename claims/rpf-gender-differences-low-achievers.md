## Evidence

### Hermes et al. 2021

Henning Hermes, Martin Huschens, Franz Rothlauf, Daniel Schunk. (2021). Motivating low-achievers—Relative performance feedback in primary schools. Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jebo.2021.04.004

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory gender analysis within the low-achieving subgroup (n = 60 boys, 39 girls), with reduced statistical power. The printed effect sizes for girls were an effect size of 0.48 SD (p = .037) on the motivation test and an effect size of 0.69 SD (p = .002) on teacher-rated motivation; boys' differences were smaller and insignificant.

> "For both the motivation test as well as teacher-rated motivation, the difference between low-achieving children in the treatment group and low-achieving children in the control group is signiﬁcant for girls (0.48 SD, p = .037; 0.69 SD, p = .002) and smaller and insigniﬁcant for boys."
