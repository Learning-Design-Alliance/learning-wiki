# Pretesting before accessing ChatGPT significantly improves final test performance compared with immediate AI access in a structured statistics task

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students who attempted the problem before consulting ChatGPT scored significantly higher on a transfer multiple-choice test than students who accessed ChatGPT immediately (d = 1.543). [→ Mahir Akgun 2025](#mahir-akgun-2025)

## Evidence

### Mahir Akgun 2025

Mahir Akgun, Sacip Toker. (2025). Struggle First, Prompt Later: How Task Complexity Shapes Learning with GenAI-Assisted Pretesting. A PREPRINT - APRIL 15, 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.10249](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.10249)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

True-experimental randomized posttest study with 61 undergraduates (31 no-pretest, 30 pretest; 60 analyzed after outlier removal) in an intermediate statistics course. The pretest group attempted a two-way chi-square problem before ChatGPT access; the no-pretest group accessed it immediately. "Cohen's d = 1.543" with means 91.85 vs 78.59.

> "The independent sample t-test results revealed a significant difference between the pretest and no-pretest groups in the final test scores, t(58) = 5.979, p < .001, Cohen's d = 1.543, indicating a large effect size"
