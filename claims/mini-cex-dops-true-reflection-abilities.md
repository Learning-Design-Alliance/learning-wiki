Nie Shengxiao, Ma Shengmiao, Wang Lei. (2026). Perspectives and challenges in implementing nursing-specific mini-CEX and DOPS: a descriptive qualitative study of clinical nursing educators and novice nurses in a tertiary hospital in China. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-113912

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semistructured interviews with 13 educators and 11 novice nurses; this subtheme quote reports the educator view that written examination scores fail to capture actual clinical competence, with direct observation showing "real-time performance in clinical settings".

> "All clinical educators believed that mini-CEX and DOPS provide a more accurate representation of novice nurses’ clinical nursing capabilities. These tools enable direct observation of novice nurses’ real-time performance in clinical settings, not just exam performance."
