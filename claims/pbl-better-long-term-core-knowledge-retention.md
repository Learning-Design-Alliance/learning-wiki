## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Heijne-Penninga M. 2012

Heijne-Penninga M., Kuks J. B. M., Hofman W. H. A., Muijtjens A. M. M., Cohen-Schotanus J. (2012). Influence of PBL with open-book tests on knowledge retention measured with progress tests. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-012-9386-8

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of progress test scores of fifth- and sixth-year students (n = 1,648) at three Dutch medical schools, analyzed with Bonferroni-corrected t tests. PBL students "significantly outperformed TC students on core knowledge", with av ES = 0.58–0.74 (PBLob vs TC) and 0.37–0.43 (PBLcb vs TC).

> "PBLob and PBLcb students significantly outperformed TC students on core knowledge (Table 2). Differences between TC and PBLob students were medium to high for seven of the eight test moments (av ES = 0.58–0.74). Differences between TC and PBLcb were small to medium for all eight test moments (av ES = 0.37–0.43)."
