### Drost RMWA 2016

Drost RMWA, Paulus ATG, Jander AF, Mercken L, de Vries H, Ruwaard D, Evers SMAA. (2016). A Web-Based Computer-Tailored Alcohol Prevention Program for Adolescents: Cost-Effectiveness and Intersectoral Costs and Benefits. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.5223

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Costing analysis for running the Alcoholic Alert intervention. Tailored feedback software costs were €7 per participant per week over a mean intervention duration of 4 weeks (€28). The five sessions took 2.5 hours total (1.5 hours at school at €8.30 per hour and 1 hour of free time at €12.50), valued at €25 per participant. Hosting costs (€300 per year) and development costs were excluded as sunk costs.

> "In sum, the total intervention costs per participant were an estimated €53."
