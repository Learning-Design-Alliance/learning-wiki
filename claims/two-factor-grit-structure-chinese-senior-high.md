## Evidence

### Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang 2026

Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang. (2026). Unpacking grit in exam-oriented education: divergent roles of perseverance and consistency in foreign language achievement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04312-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CFA comparing a one-factor and a two-factor grit model in the study's sample of 310 students. The one-factor model failed fit thresholds (χ2/df = 7.697, RMSEA = 0.147), while the two-factor model showed "good fit to the data", supporting the "two-factor structure of grit".

> "By contrast, Model 2 demonstrated good fit to the data (χ2/df = 2.070, RMSEA = 0.059, CFI = 0.964, TLI = 0.953). These results provide strong support for the two-factor structure of grit."
