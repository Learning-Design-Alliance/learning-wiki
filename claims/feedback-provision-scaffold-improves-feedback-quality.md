# A feedback provision scaffold of prompts plus a sample solution significantly improves the quality of feedback messages pre-service teachers provide on peers' case analyses

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pre-service teachers who received a feedback provision scaffold formulated higher-quality feedback messages than those without it. [→ Julia Hornstein 2025](#julia-hornstein-2025)

## Evidence

### Julia Hornstein 2025

Julia Hornstein, Martin Greisel, Ingo Kollar. (2025). Promoting Pre-Service Teachers' Evidence-Informed Reasoning Through Peer-Feedback: Effects of a Feedback Provision Scaffold and a Feedback Integration Scaffold. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09745-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Linear mixed model in the randomized 2×2 experiment (N = 254) predicting feedback quality provided, with own and peers' initial case analysis quality as covariates; the article reports a "statistically significant and positive" scaffold effect, with descriptively higher means with the scaffold (M = 0.44) than without (M = 0.24).

> "The effect of the feedback provision scaffold was statistically significant and positive. Thus, Hypothesis 1 was confirmed."
