## Evidence

### Winston Kalman A 2013

Winston Kalman A, Van Der Vleuten Cees PM, Scherpbier Albert JJA. (2013). Remediation of at-risk medical students: theory in action. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-132

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Discourse analysis of Talk Sample 9 (T5, asking questions, 3rd group meeting). After a groupthink misunderstanding about Parkinson's disease features, the teacher pressed a simple question; the article reports this "elicits the admission that they don't know the meaning of this term" and produced a drawing-based eureka moment taking less than a minute.

> "Notice how this elicits the admission that they don't know the meaning of this term (U17, U21): everyday English can be as problematic as technical jargon."
