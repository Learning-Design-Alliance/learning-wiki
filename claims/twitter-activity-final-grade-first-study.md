### Alexia Katrimpouza 2017

Alexia Katrimpouza, Nikolaos Tselios and Maria-Christina Kasimati. (2017). Twitter adoption, students' perceptions, Big Five personality traits and learning outcome: Lessons learned from 3 case studies. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1703.04047](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1703.04047)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis in the first study (N=19) linked total Twitter activity to the course's final grade, with the article printing r=.49 and p=.03. The article reports the finding from one case study; it is correlational, so causality is not established.

> "A relationship emerged between Twitter activity and the course's final grade (r= .49, p= .03)."

### Alexia Katrimpouza 2017 (2)

Alexia Katrimpouza, Nikolaos Tselios and Maria-Christina Kasimati. (2017). Twitter adoption, students' perceptions, Big Five personality traits and learning outcome: Lessons learned from 3 case studies. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1703.04047](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1703.04047)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the third study (N=46), correlational analysis of Twitter activity measures (total, questions, activities, comments) against lab and final scores printed no significant relationships (Table 3 r values -.123 to .229); the second study likewise showed no significant relationship. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The data analysis did not unveil any significant relationships between Twitter activity and academic performance (Table 3)."
