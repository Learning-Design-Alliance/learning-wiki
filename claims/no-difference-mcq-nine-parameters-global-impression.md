## Evidence

### Liu B. 2019

Liu B., Hunt L. M., Lonsdale R. J., Narula H. S., Mansaray A. F., Bundu I. and Bolkan H. A. (2019). Comparison of surgical skill acquisition by UK surgical trainees and Sierra Leonean associate clinicians in a task‐sharing programme. BJS Open 3(2): 218–223. https://doi.org/10.1002/bjs5.50122

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same blinded simulated-surgery comparison, the article reports "No difference in performance was seen between the two groups" for the MCQ test (mean 8·0 versus 8·9, P = 0·295), time to complete the simulation (456 versus 522 s, P = 0·149), and global impression (P = 0·183); equivalence was not formally tested.

> "No difference in performance was seen between the two groups for the MCQ test, nine of the 12 performance parameters and the assessors' global impression."
