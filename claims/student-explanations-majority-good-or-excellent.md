# Over 95% of student-authored questions included an explanation, mostly of good or excellent quality

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In Physics 1A repositories, over 95% of questions had an explanation and approximately two-thirds were rated good or excellent in each year. [→ Bates 2013](#bates-2013)

## Evidence

### Bates 2013

Bates, S. P., Galloway, R. K., Riise, J., and Homer, D. (2013). Assessing the quality of a student-generated question repository. https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.2202

`q3 · i2`

Rating of explanations accompanying sampled Physics 1A questions (N=150 and N=200) on a five-point rubric (Table II), shown in Figure 3. The article reports "over 95% of cases students did construct an explanation" and roughly two-thirds good or excellent. No effect size is printed.

> "The ﬁgure shows that in over 95% of cases students did construct an explanation of some kind, and in the vast majority of cases these were of good or excellent quality (approximately two-thirds of the questions sampled were in the ‘good’ or ‘excellent’ categories for each of the two years)."
