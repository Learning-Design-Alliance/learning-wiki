## Evidence

### Seabi 2012

Seabi, J. (2012). Feuerstein's mediated learning experience as a vehicle for enhancing cognitive functioning of remedial school learners in South Africa. Australian Journal of Educational & Developmental Psychology, 12, 35-45. http://www.newcastle.edu.au/journal/ajedp/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests on pre-post RCPM scores (out of 24) in a pre-post quasi-experimental design with 28 experimental and 39 control learners. Experimental means rose from 16.75 (SD 4.9) to 19.67 (SD 3.5); the control group moved from 20.05 to 20.23 with no significant change. The article reports "improvement of scores from the pre- to the post-test phase was only found within the experimental group."

> "a statistically significant (t [1, 27] = -6.14, p<.01) improvement of scores from the pre- to the post-test phase was only found within the experimental group."
