## Evidence

### David W. Putwain 2026

David W. Putwain, Andrea Mallaburn, Hanna Gaspard. (2026). Student perceptions of teacher messages about the value of science in the classroom. Learning and Instruction 104 (2026) 102365. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102365

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ML-SEM analyses at the student level (L1) with autoregressive controls on prior achievement and aspirations; three models (expectancy, utility value, effort cost) each predicted T4 achievement and aspirations from T3 beliefs. The article characterizes effects as mainly large, with a moderate negative path from effort cost to achievement, but prints no standardized coefficient in the quoted text.

> "Students who reported higher expectancy, utility value, and lower effort cost than their classmates (i.e., the L1 portion of the model) showed higher achievement and aspirations, beyond prior levels, with mainly large effect sizes (an exception was the moderate negative relation from effort cost to achievement)."
