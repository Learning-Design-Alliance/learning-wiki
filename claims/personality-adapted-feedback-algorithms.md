The review reports, citing Dennis et al. (2016), user studies investigating whether a conversational agent should employ a slant in feedback and which emotional support messages to use given learner personality and performance; "two algorithms were created, then evaluated and refined after a qualitative study with teachers". No effect sizes are printed.

> "User studies were run to understand the relationship between progress feedback and emotional support for students with different personalities and test scores. As a result, two algorithms were created, then evaluated and refined after a qualitative study with teachers."
