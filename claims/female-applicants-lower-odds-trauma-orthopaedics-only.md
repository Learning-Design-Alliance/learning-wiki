## Evidence

### Seehra Jaspreet Kaur 2025

Seehra Jaspreet Kaur, Ellis Ricky, Doleman Brett, McLarty Esther, Lund Jonathan. (2025). Fair access to higher surgical training in the UK: an equity, diversity and inclusion analysis of national selection in 2024. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-106487

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Specialty-stratified multivariable analysis of 522 trauma and orthopaedics applicants in the 2024 ST3 national selection, adjusted for age, ethnicity and country of qualification. The article reports a "highly significant reduction in the odds" for female applicants, with an adjusted odds ratio of aOR=0.44 (95% CI 0.25 to 0.79).

> "Applicants identifying as female had a highly significant reduction in the odds of receiving an offer of a national training number compared with male counterparts (aOR=0.44, 95% CI 0.25 to 0.79, p=0.006) (table 2)."
