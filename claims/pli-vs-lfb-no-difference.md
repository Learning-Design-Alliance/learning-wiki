## Evidence

### van Cappellen van Walsum AM 2022

van Cappellen van Walsum AM, Henssen DJHA. (2022). E-Learning Three-Dimensional Anatomy of the Brainstem: Impact of Different Microscopy Techniques and Spatial Ability. Anatomical Sciences Education 15(2):317-329. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.2056

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Two-sample t tests comparing 19 PLI-group and 19 LFB-group students after block randomization: the PLI group mean of "78.16 ± 10.70%" did not differ significantly from the LFB group's 77.63 ± 17.82% (P = 0.913; Cohen's d = 0.04).

> "Post-intervention test scores in the PLI group (78.16 ± 10.70%) were not significantly different from those in the LFB group (77.63 ± 17.82%) (P = 0.913; Cohen's d = 0.04)."

### van Cappellen van Walsum AM 2022 (2)

van Cappellen van Walsum AM, Henssen DJHA. (2022). E-Learning Three-Dimensional Anatomy of the Brainstem: Impact of Different Microscopy Techniques and Spatial Ability. Anatomical Sciences Education 15(2):317-329. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.2056

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Two-sample t tests on the one-week follow-up test between the randomized groups: PLI "68.68 ± 17.78%" versus LFB 64.21 ± 14.65% showed no significant difference (P = 0.403; Cohen's d = 0.30).

> "Long-term test scores in the PLI group (68.68 ± 17.78%) were not significantly different from those in the LFB group (64.21 ± 14.65%) (P = 0.403; Cohen's d = 0.30)"
