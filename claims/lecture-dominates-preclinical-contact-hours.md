Contact hours were calculated from curriculum mapping software for 2015-16 and 2016-17, excluding clinical skills courses, Endocrine and Reproduction, introductions, assessments, and review sessions. TBL, patient presentation, peer teaching, and simulation each represented 1-3% of contact time.

> "Of the 801 total hours of preclinical instructional contact hours that were included, lecture was utilized most often (65%, 524 of 801), followed by laboratory (10%), small group CBL (8%), and large group CBL (7%)."
