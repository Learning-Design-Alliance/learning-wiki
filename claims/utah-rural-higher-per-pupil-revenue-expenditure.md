Descriptive analysis of general fund revenue and expenditure from USBE data, adjusted to 2016 values using the consumer price index. In 2017 rural districts had CPI-adjusted per-pupil revenue of $9,216 versus $6,673 in non-rural districts.

> "In 2017, CPI-adjusted per-pupil revenue was $9,216 and $6,673 in rural and non-rural districts, respectively . In addition, the average general fund CPI-adjusted per-pupil expenditure was $8,915 in rural districts and $6,489 in  non- rural districts."
