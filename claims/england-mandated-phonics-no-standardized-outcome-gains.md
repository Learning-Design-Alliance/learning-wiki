## Evidence

### Jeffrey S. Bowers 2021

Jeffrey S. Bowers. (2021). Yes Children Need to Learn Their GPCs but There Really Is Little or No Evidence that Systematic or Explicit Phonics Is Effective: a response to Fletcher, Savage, and Sharon (2020). Educational Psychology Review (2021) 33:1965–1979. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09602-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

Natural-experiment evidence from England, where every state school has been legally required to teach synthetic phonics since 2007 and all children take the Year 1 Phonics Screening Check since 2012. No improvement on PIRLS, PISA, or SATs is reported.

> "However, as I detail in my review, there is little or no evidence that the introduction of phonics has improved performance on any of the standardized reading outcomes, namely, the international standardized tests of PIRLS, PISA, nor the national reading tests in England called SATs."

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The author computed means from the GOV.UK 'phonics tables' for 2012–2019: mean PSC scores rose from 29.5/40 to 33.8/40, a modest increase despite the pass rate rising from 58% to 82%, possibly due to training to the test such as practicing nonsense words.

> "The mean scores improved from 29.5/40 to 33.8/40."
