## Evidence

### Bjork 2020

Bjork, R. A., & Bjork, E. L. (2020). Desirable difficulties in theory and practice. Journal of Applied Research in Memory and Cognition, 9(4), 475-479. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-applied-research-in-memory-and-cognition. [doi:10.1016/j.jarmac.2020.09.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jarmac.2020.09.003)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Definitional and theoretical statement in the desirable versus undesirable difficulties section. The article defines desirable difficulties by the processes they trigger and states the boundary condition that without background knowledge or skills they become undesirable. No new data reported.

> "Desirable difficulties, versus the array of undesirable difficulties, are desirable because they trigger encoding and retrieval processes that support learning, comprehension, and remembering. If, however, the learner does not have the background knowledge or skills to respond to them successfully, they become undesirable difficulties."

### Bjork 2020 (2)

Bjork, R. A., & Bjork, E. L. (2020). Desirable difficulties in theory and practice. Journal of Applied Research in Memory and Cognition, 9(4), 475-479. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-applied-research-in-memory-and-cognition. [doi:10.1016/j.jarmac.2020.09.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jarmac.2020.09.003)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical statement with an example about generation versus presentation. The article states optimal difficulty varies with prior learning and that generation is desirable only when the learner can succeed or at least activate relevant aspects, citing Little & Bjork and Richland et al. as prior work.

> "The level of difficulty that is optimal, therefore, will vary with the degree of a learner's prior learning. In general, for example, it is desirable to have learners generate a skill or some knowledge from memory, rather than simply showing them that skill or presenting that knowledge, but a given learner needs to be equipped by virtue of prior learning to succeed at that generation"
