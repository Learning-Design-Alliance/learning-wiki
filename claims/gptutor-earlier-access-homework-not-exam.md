# Earlier access to GPTutor was associated with higher homework scores but this benefit did not transfer to exam scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Earlier access to GPTutor was associated with higher homework scores (small-to-medium standardized effect), but this benefit did not transfer to independent exam assessments. [→ Chen 2026](#chen-2026)
`q3 i?` No significant group differences were found on Midterm 2 or Midterm 3 exam scores by access condition. [→ Chen 2026 (2)](#chen-2026-2)

[doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_41](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_41)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects regression on homework scores in a staggered-access study with 148 students. The article reports "a small-to-medium standardized effect (β = 0.23)" for earlier access on HW5–HW6 scores.

> "After adjustment, earlier access was associated with higher HW5–HW6 scores (B = 2.71, SE = 1.22, 95% CI[0.32, 5.09], p = .026), corresponding to a small-to-medium standardized effect (β = 0.23)."

> "In contrast, OLS regressions predictingMidterm 2andMidterm 3from condition (controlling forMidterm 1) showed no significant group differences (Midterm 2: B = −2.32, p = .134;Midterm 3: B = 2.46, p = .283)."
