## Evidence

### Rahimi A. Masie 2023

Rahimi A. Masie, Hardon Sem F., Willuth E., Lang F., Haney Caelan M., Felinska Eleni A., Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Müller-Stich Beat P., Horeman Tim, Nickel F., Daams Freek. (2023). Force-based assessment of tissue handling skills in simulation training for robot-assisted surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-023-09905-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multicenter prospective study in which 60 participants across three experience groups performed six trials of a standardized robotic suturing task; Kruskal-Wallis and Mann-Whitney U tests showed "Significant differences between the novices, intermediates and experts" on all four force/time parameters, with printed p-values and no effect sizes reported.

> "Significant differences between the novices, intermediates and experts were observed regarding the total completion time (41 s vs 29 s vs 22 s p = 0.003), mean non zero force (29 N vs 33 N vs 19 N p = 0.032), maximum impulse (40 Ns vs 31 Ns vs 20 Ns p = 0.001) and force volume (38 N3 vs 32 N3 vs 22 N3 p = 0.018)"
