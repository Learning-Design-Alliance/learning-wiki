Záhorec 2010¶

Záhorec, J., Hašková, A., & Munk, M. (2010). Impact of Electronic Teaching Materials on Process of Education – Results of an Experiment. Informatics in Education, 9(2), 261–281. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1064255

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of closed questionnaire item 6 (reasons for learning physics); answers did not differ among groups. Students also preferred teacher-led explanation using teaching aids and did not want to be involved in using new knowledge.

"The results showed that currently the learners have a very low internal motivation to learning physics knowledge what, according to us, is connected with the fact that they can not see any possibilities how to apply and use this knowledge in their everyday life"
