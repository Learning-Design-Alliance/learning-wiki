## Evidence

### Michelle S. Peter 2019

Michelle S. Peter, Samantha Durrant, Andrew Jessop, Amy Bidgood, Julian M. Pine, Caroline F. Rowland. (2019). Does speed of processing or vocabulary size predict later language growth in toddlers? Cognitive Psychology 115 (2019) 101238. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2019.101238

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of mean RT scores per child across the three measurement points. The authors conclude that "individual differences in speed of language processing remained broadly stable between 19 and 31months", though the 25-to-31-month correlation was not significant.

> "There was a significant correlation in mean RTs between 19 and 25months (r(68)=0.23,p=.029), and between 19 and 31months (r(69)=0.28, p=.009), indicating that fast processors at 19months continued to be fast processors six months later at 25months, and 12months later at 31months."
