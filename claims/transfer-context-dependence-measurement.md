### Noguchi 2026

Noguchi, M. (2026). Exploring Applications of Transfer-State Large Language Models: Cognitive Profiling and Socratic AI Tutoring. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.27454](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.27454)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Preliminary investigation 1: four IPII-based self-description questions rated blind by four LLM coders with median consensus across 11 conditions. The article reports the limited difference and, in Table 6, a small effect (d = 0.40) for the corresponding five-model comparison; Opus 4.6 non-transfer outperformed many transfer conditions, indicating confounding with baseline reasoning ability.

> "The mean MAS-A total score was 18.5 (18.4 for the corresponding five conditions) for the non-transfer group and 20.0 for the transfer group, indicating a limited group difference."
