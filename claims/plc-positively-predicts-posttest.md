## Evidence

### Fei Qin 2025

Fei Qin, Zhanxin Hao, Jifan Yu, Zhiyuan Liu, Yu Zhang. (2025). AI instructional agent improves student's perceived learner control and learning outcome: Empirical evidence from a randomized controlled trial. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the RCT sample with post-test score as dependent variable and PLC, gender, age, SRL, and pre-test as predictors; the model was estimated with statistical power of 0.948. The β values are unstandardized-scale coefficients as printed; no standardized effect size printed.

> "The results showed that PLC significantly predicted post-test performance (β = 0.055, p
< .05), indicating that students who perceived greater control over their learning tended to perform better, even after
accounting for prior achievement and learner background. Pre-test scores were also significant (β = 0.223, p < .05); all
other covariates were non-significant."
