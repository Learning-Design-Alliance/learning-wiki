## Evidence

### Aparicio M. 2026

Aparicio M., López-Herce J., Montalvo N., Solana M. J., Vázquez P., Mencía S., López-Herce Jesus. (2026). Simulation based learning as a strategy for patient education training in medical students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-57950-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective nonrandomized comparative study of 67 medical students across three pediatric rotations; tutor-rated practical assessment after three weeks of rotation. The simulation group scored highest, with "the PICU students (group 2) scored significantly higher (8.17 [0.14])" than the other two groups.

> "For the tutor rating, the PICU students (group 2) scored significantly higher (8.17 [0.14]) than did the PCP (group 1) (7.44 [0.18]; p = 0.004) and PER (group 3) (7.18 [0.14]); p < 0.001) students."
