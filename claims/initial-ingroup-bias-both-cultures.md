## Evidence

### Yuqing Zhou 2025

Yuqing Zhou, Björn Lindström, Alexander Soutschek, Pyungwon Kang, Shihui Han, Philippe N. Tobler & Grit Hein. (2025). Learning reduces ingroup bias more with perceived losses than gains across cultures. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00362-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed models on pre-learning ratings from 112 Swiss (Study 1) and 100 Chinese (Study 2) male participants validated the group manipulation. The article reports "initial impressions were more positive" for ingroup, with printed β and χ2 values for all three measures (β = 0.389, 0.129, 0.234).

> "initial impressions were more positive (χ2 = 176.28, p <0 . 0 0 1 , β = 0.389, 95% CI = [0.33 0.45]) and initial expectations (χ2 = 9.20,p =0 . 0 0 2 , β = 0.129, 95% CI = [0.045 0.212]), as well as initial closeness ( χ2=4 5 . 1 1 , p < 0.001, β = 0.234, 95% CI = [0.165 0.303]) were higher for the ingroup"
