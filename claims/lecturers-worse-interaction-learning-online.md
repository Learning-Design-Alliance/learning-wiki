## Evidence

### Förster C 2020

Förster C, Eismann-Schweimler J, Stengel S, Bischoff M, Fuchs M, Graf von Luckner A, Ledig T, Barzel A, Maun A, Joos S, Szecsenyi J, Schwill S. (2020). Opportunities and challenges of e-learning in vocational training in General Practice – a project report about implementing digital formats in the KWBW-Verbundweiterbildungplus. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001390](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001390)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Lecturer evaluation (n=11, all non-KWBW lecturers) via the same online questionnaire; "All lecturers felt that the interaction with the GP-trainees was worse (n=7) or significantly worse (n=4)".

> "55% (n=6) assessed the learning success for GP-trainees to be worse, 45% (n=5) considered it comparable to classical teaching formats. All lecturers felt that the interaction with the GP-trainees was worse (n=7) or significantly worse (n=4)."
