Bickford Michael 2025¶

Bickford Michael, Alruwaili Fayez, Ragab Sara, Rothenberg Hanna, Abedin-Nasab Mohammad (2025). Impact of extended reality on robot-assisted surgery training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-025-02559-z

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis comparing XR training to dry lab training on GEARS scores. The result was "not statistically significant" with Cohen's d = -1.09 and a 95% CI of [-3.17, 0.98].

"A random-effects analysis of the GEARS scores showed that participants with extended reality had lower GEARS scores than those that were trained in extended reality. (Cohen’s d = −1.09, 95% CI [−3.17, 0.98]). However, this result is not statistically significant (P = 0.30)"
