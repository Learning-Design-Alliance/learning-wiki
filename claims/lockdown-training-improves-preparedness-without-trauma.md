## Evidence

### Schildkraut 2020

Schildkraut, J., Grogan, K., & Nabors, A. (2020). Should schools be conducting lockdown drills? WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/schools-lockdown-drills/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The brief reports, citing Zhe and Nickerson (2007), that students "retained knowledge about how to respond without inducing anxiety or significantly changing their perceptions of school safety". This is the brief's secondhand report of that study, not a new analysis.

> "Zhe and Nickerson (2007) found that students who went through lockdown training and then participated in a lockdown drill retained knowledge about how to respond without inducing anxiety or significantly changing their perceptions of school safety."

### Schildkraut 2020 (2)

Schildkraut, J., Grogan, K., & Nabors, A. (2020). Should schools be conducting lockdown drills? WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/schools-lockdown-drills/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The brief reports, citing Schildkraut et al. (2020), that drills and training "improved feelings of preparedness". Student perceptions of safety decreased over time, but the brief notes the decline was small relative to preparedness gains and may reflect community violence exposure.

> "Schildkraut and colleagues (2020) found that participating in lockdown drills and training improved feelings of preparedness, with students reporting a greater familiarity with how to respond when lockdowns and other emergency calls are made."
