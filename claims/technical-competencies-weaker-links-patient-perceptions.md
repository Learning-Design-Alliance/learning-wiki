## Evidence

### Ulupınar Fadime 2025

Ulupınar Fadime, Karasu Şeyda. (2025). Perceived vs. experienced care: a comparison between nursing students’ self-assessed competence and patients’ reported care. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12880-w

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis across the 167 matched dyads, reported in the Results section. The printed coefficients r = 0.221 to 0.353 (p ≤ 0.05), quoted as "coefficients ranging from 0.221 to 0.353 (p ≤ 0.05)", are moderate correlations (i = 2); no effect-size summary beyond r is given.

> "In contrast, correlations involving Clinical Biomedical Knowledge and Critical Thinking and Reasoning were weaker, with coefficients ranging from 0.221 to 0.353 (p ≤ 0.05), hinting that these cognitive and theoretical dimensions may have a more limited influence on patients’ care perceptions."

### Ulupınar Fadime 2025 (2)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`
