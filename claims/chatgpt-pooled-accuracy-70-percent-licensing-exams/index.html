Evidence¶

Jin Hye Kyung 2024¶

Jin Hye Kyung, Lee Ha Eun, Kim EunYoung. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT-3.5 and GPT-4 in national licensing examinations for medicine, pharmacy, dentistry, and nursing: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05944-8

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of 23 studies (144 effect sizes) of ChatGPT-3.5 and GPT-4 accuracy on national licensing examinations in medicine, pharmacy, dentistry, and nursing. The pooled estimate was "70.1% (95% CI, 65.0-74.8%)"; heterogeneity was high (Q = 1201.303, I2 = 98.2%).

"The overall effect size for the percentage of performance of ChatGPT models was 70.1% (95% CI, 65.0-74.8%)."
