Moderator analyses across the meta-analytic datasets examined structure components, setting, sample, and paired supports, and "underscored the importance of emphasizing anticipatory strategies, minimizing the controlling aspects of structure, and considering the broader context". No single moderator effect size is printed here.

> "Results related to variation suggested some universality, particularly across grade levels, and underscored the importance of emphasizing anticipatory strategies, minimizing the controlling aspects of structure, and considering the broader context, including the country context, income background of students, or whether structure is paired with other psychological supports."
