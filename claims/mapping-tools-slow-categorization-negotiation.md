## Evidence

### Solli 2018

Solli, A., Mäkitalo, Å., & Hillman, T. (2018). Rendering controversial socioscientific issues legible through digital mapping tools. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9286-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interaction analysis of 90 minutes of synced video and screen-capture recordings of one upper secondary group mapping the HPV vaccine controversy, showing how tool features and student logics interacted. The analysis shows "alignments and misalignments between the digital tool" and student logics prompted negotiation of how to make sense of a controversy.

> "alignments and misalignments between the digital tool used and students’own logics prompted students to engage in productive collaborative negotiation of how to make sense of a controversy."
