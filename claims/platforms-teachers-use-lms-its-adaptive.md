Qualitative synthesis of the n = 11 included studies' platform descriptions. The review reports that n = 3 studies did not specify their platform, while the rest used named LMSs, Snappet, and tutoring systems; it concludes "the specific type of digital learning platform appears to be not that relevant".

> "In the studies of Knoop-van Campen and Molenaar (2020) as well as Molenaar and Knoop-van Campen (2018), Snappet as an adaptive learning platform was used by teachers and students."
