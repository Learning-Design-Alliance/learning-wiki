q2 · i? · review · r2

Qualitative synthesis of active verbal cueing excerpts in the review's results. Cues such as hinting, redirecting, fill-in-the-blank requests and highlighting critical features restrict the information novices attend to; a physics tutor's closed yes/no question limited the novice to two options to consider in depth.

"The most frequently occurring verbal cues serve to direct the attention of the learner to a specific visual area without completely giving away the solution; they narrow down the set of possible correct next options"
