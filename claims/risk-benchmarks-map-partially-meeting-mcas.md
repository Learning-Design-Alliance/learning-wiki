# Grade 3 screener benchmark scores marking any level of risk map to MCAS ELA scores from mid Partially Meeting Expectations to the beginning of Meeting Expectations

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Equipercentile linking places most any-risk benchmark scores within the MCAS Partially Meeting Expectations performance level, with some variability across assessments. [→ Lemke 2023](#lemke-2023)

## Evidence

### Lemke 2023

Lemke, M., Murphy, D., Soo Ping Chow, A., Spencer, H., & Zhang, A. (2023). Comparing early literacy screening assessment benchmarks in Massachusetts. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638508

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Equipercentile linking analysis of grade 3 screener benchmark scores onto the MCAS ELA scale, using data from more than 4,000 students across 28 districts and 63 schools in 2021/22. The analysis shows risk benchmarks 'fall in the MCAS Partially Meeting Expectations performance level, though there is some variability' (Figure 1).

> "Results of an equipercentile linking analysis show that most benchmark scores that identify students at any level of risk (for example, the score that divides students who are "At Benchmark" or "Above Benchmark" from students who are "Below Benchmark" or "Well Below Benchmark" in DIBELS 8th Edition terms) fall in the MCAS Partially Meeting Expectations performance level, though there is some variability (see Figure 1)."
