Evidence¶

Barrow 2019¶

Barrow, L., & Sartain, L. (2019). GoCPS: A first look at ninth-grade applications, offers, and enrollment. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of CPS Masterfile enrollment data from fall 2017 and fall 2018. The report states neighborhood schools enrolled a similar number of ninth-graders, the share at highest-rated schools was unchanged, and most students who accepted an offer (80 percent) enrolled at the school where they accepted it.

"Overall first-time ninth-grade enrollment stayed relatively stable between fall 2017 (before GoCPS) and fall 2018 (after GoCPS)."
