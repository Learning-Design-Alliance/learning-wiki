Deutsch G.B., Sathyanarayana S.A., Giangola M., Akerman M., DeNoto G., Klein J.D.S., Zemon H., Rubach E. (2015). Competence Acquisition for Single-Incision Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2014.00116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Complication analysis of the retrospective 290-case cohort (Table 3). Conversion to multiple port or open/repositioning totaled 9 events (3.1%); most conversions were for suboptimal retraction to achieve better visualization.

> "The most common complication involved conversion to a multiport laparoscopic cholecystectomy, occurring in 2.8% patients (n = 8). Only 1 patient required a transition to open cholecystectomy for severe adhesions and inadequate visualization."
