## Evidence

### Pather MK 2019

Pather MK, Mash R. (2019). Family physicians' experience and understanding of evidence-based practice and guideline implementation in primary care practice, Cape Town, South Africa. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. 11(1), a1592. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v11i1.1592

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding from the 27-physician study: contextualisation could be to the user's scope of practice and to the health-system level. One FP said practitioners may "adapt it to their environment" but "must do it as a team", making the modified guideline accepted practice for that facility.

> "the practitioners in that practice should be allowed to look at the guideline and so to speak, adapt it to their environment. But when they do that, they must do it as a team"
