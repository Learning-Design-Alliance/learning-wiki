## Evidence

### Sloth Sigurd Beier 2021

Sloth Sigurd Beier, Jensen Rune Dall, Seyer-Hansen Mikkel, Christensen Mette Krogh, De Win Gunter. (2021). Remote training in laparoscopy: a randomized trial comparing home-based self-regulated training to centralized instructor-regulated training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-021-08429-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized comparative trial with two parallel arms (46 randomized; 45 posttest; 39 retention) analyzed with a mixed model for repeated measurements of LASTT and SUTT1 performances. The article reports "no significant inter-group differences" and "no evidence of group-test interactions"; no effect sizes were printed.

> "Our analyses revealed no significant inter-group differences in any of the performance measures at pretest, posttest, or retention test, respectively (p values > 0.05). In addition, we found no evidence of group-test interactions"
