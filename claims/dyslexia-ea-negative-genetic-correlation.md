### Graell M. et al 2026

Graell M. et al. (2026). Towards disentangling the polygenic contribution of dyslexia to school performance. Translational Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41398-026-04118-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

LDSC analysis of GWAS summary statistics for dyslexia (N = 51,800 cases, 1,087,070 controls) and EA (N = 1,131,881) estimated SNP heritabilities and a negative genetic correlation, "rg = −0.186, SE = 0.019, P = 1.75E-22". MiXeR showed 99.5% of dyslexia trait-influencing variants shared with EA.

> "We estimated a SNP-based heritability (h2SNP) of 0.179 for dyslexia (SE = 0.0071) and 0.102 for EA (SE = 0.0023) and found a negative genetic correlation between them (rg = −0.186, SE = 0.019, P = 1.75E-22)"
