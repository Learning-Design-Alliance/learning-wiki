# The GN mode provides equivalent benefits to both surface-approach and deep-approach students, with no significant difference in their posttest scores.

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no significant difference in mean posttest scores between surface-approach and deep-approach students exposed to the GN mode. [→ Hii 2013](#hii-2013)

## Evidence

### Hii 2013

Hii, S. C., & Fong, S. F. (2013). Effects of Multimedia-Based Graphic Novel Presentation on Critical Thinking among Students of Different Learning Approaches. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018032

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA with pretest as covariate compared surface-approach (n = 65) and deep-approach (n = 35) students in the GN mode. The main effect of learning approach was not significant, F(1, 97) = 1.844, p = 0.178, showing "no significant difference in the mean posttest scores" between the two groups.

> "The results in Table 5 show that there was no significant difference in the mean posttest scores for the surface and deep-approach students exposed to the GN mode, F(1, 97) = 1.844, p = 0.178."
