Hyoungwook Jin 2026¶

Hyoungwook Jin, Weirui Peng, Jieun Han, Q. Vera Liao, and Xu Wang. (2026). HelpCoach: Scaffolding Targeted AI Help-Seeking During Problem-Solving. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28918 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.28918

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Survey-based correlational analysis in the Study 1 classroom deployment (44 undergraduates): students rated their question specificity on a 7-point Likert scale. Spearman correlations with observed specificity were non-significant in all conditions, e.g., r_Baseline = .080, p = .778.
