Means 2020¶

Means, B., Peters, V., Neisler, J., & Griffiths, R. (2020). STEM Courses During the COVID Pandemic: Lessons from Spring 2020. Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7900cb79-6ea5-44d0-aad6-34aa81e9bf4b

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Case study interviews at nine institutions using adaptive courseware (e.g., ALEKS, MyMathLab); one math instructor estimated a few days of preparation, and students in those sections described consistent homework and smoother transitions with higher satisfaction.

"Instructors who used adaptive courseware before COVID described spending less time than their colleagues did preparing their courses for the transition to remote instruction."
