Survey administered at baseline and 5 weeks to both study groups; confidence improved significantly in both groups (Fig. 4), with the emergency-situation gain greater in the workshop group, "p = 0.012".

> "While the improvement in routine medical practice did not differ significantly between groups, it was greater in the workshop group compared to the control group in emergency situation (median improvement of 1, IQR 1–2 vs. 0, IQR 0–1, p = 0.012)."
