## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The three allomorphs behaved significantly differently, with -id unproblematic, -d easier to produce than -t, and a significant Time × allomorphy interaction showing improvement for -d but not -t. [→ Cardoso 2018](#cardoso-2018)
