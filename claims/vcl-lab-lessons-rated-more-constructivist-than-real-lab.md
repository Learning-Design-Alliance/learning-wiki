## Evidence

### Zeynep Tatli & Alipasa Ayas 2012

Zeynep Tatli & Alipasa Ayas. (2012). Virtual Chemistry Laboratory: Effect of Constructivist Learning Environment. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 13(1). https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojde

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational analysis during the chemical changes unit: lessons of both control groups and the experimental group were observed for 11 lesson-hours each and scored with a semi-structured teaching-philosophy form (traditional 1 to constructivist 4). Table 4 shows Teacher D's laboratory mean was 93.6 (C) with EG versus 46.0 (CT) with CG-II; classroom means were 69.3 and 76.0 (both CC).

> "It is seen that teacher D’s “close to traditional” with CG -II group in laboratory lessons, and “constructivist” with EG students in laboratory lessons."
