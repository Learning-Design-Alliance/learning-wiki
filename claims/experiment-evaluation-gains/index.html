Evidence¶

Eva Erdosne Toth 2000¶

Eva Erdosne Toth, David Klahr, and Zhe Chen. (2000). Bridging Research and Practice: A Cognitively Based Classroom Intervention for Teaching Experimentation Skills to Elementary School Children. Cognition and Instruction, 18(4), 423–459. https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci1804_1

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Pre/post paper-and-pencil experiment evaluation test (10 airplane-comparison items) administered to all students in the classroom study; means rose from 61% to 87%, t(70) = 8.72, p < .0001.

"Mean experiment evaluation scores increased from 61% correct prior to instruction to 87% correct after instruction,t(70) = 8.72,p < .0001."

Eva Erdosne Toth 2000 (2)¶

Eva Erdosne Toth, David Klahr, and Zhe Chen. (2000). Bridging Research and Practice: A Cognitively Based Classroom Intervention for Teaching Experimentation Skills to Elementary School Children. Cognition and Instruction, 18(4), 423–459. https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci1804_1

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Same evaluation test, consistency analysis (N = 74): students correct on at least 9 of 10 comparisons rose from 28% to 76%, χ2(1, N = 74) = 31.2, p = .001.

"The percentage of students who were consistently good evaluators of experiments designed by others, that is, those who could evaluate correctly at least 9 of the 10 comparisons,increased from 28% prior to instruction to 76% after instruction, χ2(1, N = 74) = 31.2, p = .001."
