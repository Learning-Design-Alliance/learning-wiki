## Evidence

### Yang 2026

Yang, F., Wang, J., & He, L. (2026). Application of AI-assisted PBL-CBL combined teaching in the diagnosis and treatment of post-herpetic neuralgia: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 26(1), 1149. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09489-w

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Single-center RCT with 120 interns randomized 1:1 (n = 60 each) at the Affiliated Hospital of Yunnan University; four-week intervention. Between-group comparison used independent samples t-tests with Cohen's d. The observation group scored "significantly higher than the control group" on the theoretical examination (d = 0.54).

> "the observation group scored significantly higher than the control group in both the theoretical examination (t = 2.98, P < 0.01, 95% CI: 0.87–4.49, Cohen's d = 0.54)"
