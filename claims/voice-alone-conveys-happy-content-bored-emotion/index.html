Evidence¶

Lawson 2022¶

Lawson, A. P., & Mayer, R. E. (2022). The Power of Voice to Convey Emotion in Multimedia Instructional Messages. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00282-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA on ratings of the happy instructor in the Mturk within-subjects experiment (agent present vs. agent absent × four emotion scales). The article reports "not a significant main effect of agent presence" and no interaction, consistent with the voice hypothesis.

"There was not a significant main effect of agent presence, F(1, 99) = 0.59, p = 0.443, nor an interaction, F(3, 297) = 0.39, p = 0.759."

Lawson 2022 (2)¶

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA on ratings of the content instructor in the same within-subjects experiment. The article reports "no main effect of agent presence" and no interaction, consistent with the voice hypothesis.

"There was no main effect of agent presence, F(1, 99) = 1.70, p = 0.195, nor was there an interaction, F(3, 297) = 0.86, p = 0.464."
