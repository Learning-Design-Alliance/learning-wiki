# Moving data visualization to the first project in the DataTrail curriculum boosted student motivation, per the authors' program experience

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In the DataTrail program, having students create visuals in the very first project, with pre-written code, helped motivate students and gave instructors insight into their code. [→ Savonen 2024](#savonen-2024)

## Evidence

### Savonen 2024

Savonen, Candace; Wright, Carrie; Hoffman, Ava M.; Humphries, Elizabeth, M.; Cox, Katherine E. L.; Tan, Frederick J.; Leek, Jeffrey T. (2024). Motivation, Inclusivity, and Realism Should Drive Data Science Education. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.12688/f1000research.134655.2](https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.134655.2)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' experiential report from the DataTrail program: after reorganizing the curriculum so visualization comes in the first project rather than the last quarter, they report students were motivated and instructors gained insight into student code. No systematic evaluation is reported.

> "So now, we have students create visuals in the very ﬁrst project (with some help of code we have pre-written for them). Not only has this helped motivate our students, but it has the added beneﬁt of giving us more insight into what students are writing and trying with their code."
