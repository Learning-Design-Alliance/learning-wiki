## Evidence

### Haavisto 2025

Haavisto, M., Lepola, J., & Jaakkola, T. (2025). The “simple” view of learning from illustrated texts and videos. Learning and Instruction, 100, 102200. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102200

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-study comparison with the highly similar Haavisto et al. (2023) setting, not an experimental manipulation; the article reports "26.3% higher post-test scores with the material available" and "8.3% higher in the delayed retention test", while transfer was 26.4% worse.

> "Unsurprisingly, the present study observed 26.3% higher post-test scores with the material available. More compellingly, participants scored 8.3% higher in the delayed retention test (without material in both studies)."
