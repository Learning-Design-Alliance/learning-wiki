## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Favarato Maria Helena 2026

Favarato Maria Helena, Peres Isabela Chagas, de Fátima Lopes Calvo Tibério Iolanda. (2026). Is simulation always a safe learning environment? Anxiety and social anxiety symptoms in first-year medical students during simulated patient encounters. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09827-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal comparison of first versus last responses among 21 students who submitted more than one response; STAI-state means of "48.10 ± 10.55" and "49.05 ± 12.67" differed non-significantly (p = 0.66), as did LSAS (p = 0.09).

> "Twenty-one students submitted more than one response over time. When comparing their first with their last response, STAI-state mean was 48.10 ± 10.55 in their first evaluation and 49.05 ± 12.67 in their last, showing no significant difference over time (p = 0.66). LSAS score behavior over the course of the research was 41.19 ± 27.22 on their first response and 46.24 ± 27.81 in their last, not statistically significant (p = 0.09)."
