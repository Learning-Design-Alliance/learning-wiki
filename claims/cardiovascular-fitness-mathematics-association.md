## Evidence

### de Greeff 2014

de Greeff, J. W.; Hartman, E.; Mullender-Wijnsma, M. J.; Bosker, R. J.; Doolaard, S.; & Visscher, C. (2014). Physical fitness and academic performance in primary school children with and without a social disadvantage. Health Education Research, 29(5), 853-860. https://doi.org/10.1093/her/cyu043

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel analysis of data from 544 children (130 SDC, 414 non-SDC) relating cardiovascular fitness test results to mathematics, spelling and reading scores. The analysis showed a positive association with mathematics, "beta = 0.23". The article prints this regression coefficient but no standardised effect size, so no impact magnitude is coded. The authors conclude this link holds "regardless of socioeconomic status".

> "In the total group, multilevel analysis showed positive associations between cardiovascular fitness and mathematics (beta = 0.23), and between cardiovascular fitness and spelling (beta = 0.16), but not with reading."
