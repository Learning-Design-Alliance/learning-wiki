### Charlotte Zimmerman 2020 (2)

Charlotte Zimmerman, Alexis Olsho, Suzanne White Brahmia, Andrew Boudreaux, Trevor Smith, and Philip Eaton. (2020). Exploring student facility with “goes like” reasoning in introductory physics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.zimmerman](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.zimmerman)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interview analysis of correct answerers on Ferris Wheel: students who plugged in points "choos[e] physically relevant points to better understand the behavior of the system," which the article says "has been identiﬁed as an expert-like behavior in previous studies." Students typically approached this strategy as a last effort.

> "This kind of problem solving—speciﬁcally choosing physically relevant points to better understand the behavior of the system—has been identiﬁed as an expert-like behavior in previous studies [20]."
