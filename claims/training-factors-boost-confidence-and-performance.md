### Hautz Wolf E. 2026

Hautz Wolf E., Marcin Thimo, Schauber Stefan K., Walter Robin, Hautz Stefanie C., Birrenbach Tanja, Lehmann Beat, Sauter Thomas C., Hari Roman, Kämmer Juliane E. (2026). Self-Monitoring Accuracy over Time for a Complex Diagnostic Skill. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1767

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed-effects models for confidence as the dependent variable found that training-related factors — previous ultrasound training, peer teaching, and additional training after T1 — were positively associated with higher confidence. The performance model similarly found higher performance for peer teaching group and additional practice hours.

> "Training-related factors (previous ultrasound training, peer teaching, additional training after T1) were positively associated with higher confidence."
