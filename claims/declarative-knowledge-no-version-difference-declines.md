# Both intervention versions raise declarative metacognitive knowledge about retrieval practice equally, and this knowledge declines over three weeks regardless of version

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Declarative metacognitive knowledge did not differ between versions at T1 (d = 0.16) or T2; the only detectable effect was a decline over time (η 2 p = .045). [→ Lisi Wang 2023](#lisi-wang-2023)

## Evidence

### Lisi Wang 2023

Lisi Wang, Katherine Muenks, Veronica X. Yan. (2023). Interventions to Promote Retrieval Practice: Strategy Knowledge Predicts Intent, but Perceived Cost Predicts Usage. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000813

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Mixed ANOVA on declarative metacognitive knowledge (belief that retrieval practice beats rereading) across both sessions. Both versions taught this content, and knowledge 'decreased over time, regardless of the intervention version'; at T1 the version difference was d = 0.16, not significant.

> "The only statistically detectable effect was the main effect of time point, F (1, 278) = 13.14, p < .001, η 2 p = .045. Participants' declarative metacognitive knowledge decreased over time, regardless of the intervention version they received"
