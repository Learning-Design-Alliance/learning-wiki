# GAI-assisted teaching significantly increases student satisfaction with teaching compared with traditional teaching (SMD = 1.52; OR = 5.58)

## Subclaims

`q3 i3` Pooled across 15 continuous-variable studies, satisfaction was significantly higher in the GAI group (SMD = 1.52, 95% CI: 1.01–2.02); dichotomous studies gave OR = 5.58. [→ Ma Xingming 2026](#ma-xingming-2026)

`q3 i2` Across three studies, the GAI group reported higher teaching satisfaction than the traditional group (SMD 1.18, 95% CI 0.50–1.87; p < 0.01). [→ Li Juan 2025](#li-juan-2025)

## Evidence

### Ma Xingming 2026

Ma Xingming, Liu Xianting, Sun Haojie. (2026). The effectiveness of GAI-assisted teaching methods in medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1813108

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 15 continuous-variable studies (random effects, I2 = 96%) and 23 dichotomous-variable studies (fixed effects, I2 = 0%) reported in the student satisfaction section; dichotomous studies gave "OR = 5.58, 95% CI: 4.27–7.28, p < 0.0001".

> "The meta-analysis results showed a significant improvement in teaching satisfaction in the experimental group compared with the control group (SMD = 1.52, 95% CI: 1.01–2.02, p < 0.00001)."

### Li Juan 2025

Li Juan, Yin Kaiyu, Wang Yida, Jiang Xiaoqin, Chen Dongxu. (2025). Effectiveness of generative artificial intelligence-based teaching versus traditional teaching methods in medical education: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07750-2

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random-effects meta-analysis of teaching-satisfaction surveys from three RCTs. The GAI group reported higher satisfaction with a pooled effect size of SMD = 1.18 (95% CI 0.50–1.87, p < 0.01, I² = 79%); the difference was statistically significant.

> "There were three studies conducted on the survey on teaching satisfaction in both student groups, and the GAI group reported higher satisfaction than the traditional group (SMD 1.18, 95% CI 0.50 to 1.87; p < 0.01; I² = 79%)"
