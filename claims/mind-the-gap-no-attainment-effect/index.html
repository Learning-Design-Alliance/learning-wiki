Evidence¶

Dorsett 2014¶

Dorsett, R., Rienzo, C., Rolfe, H., Burns, H., Robertson, B.-A., Thorpe, B., & Wall, K. (2014). Mind the Gap: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581160

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Randomised controlled trial comparing Mind the Gap schools to business-as-usual control schools, with InCAS tests administered six months after intervention. The report states there was "no statistically significant effect on the primary outcome". The summary table prints an effect size of -0.141 (-0.697, 0.414) for 492 pupils, rated not statistically significant.

"The intervention had no statistically significant effect on the primary outcome, a combined reading and numeracy score , so we do not have sufficient evidence to conclude that any observed effect was caused by the programme rather than occurring by chance."
