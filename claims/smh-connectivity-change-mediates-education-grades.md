### Rakesh 2025

Rakesh, D., Sadikova, E., & McLaughlin, K. A. (2025). Associations among socioeconomic disadvantage, longitudinal changes in within-network connectivity, and academic outcomes in the ABCD study. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2025.101587

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Structural equation modeling in the ABCD sample (N = 4745) tested whether residualized connectivity change mediated SES-to-grades associations, covarying motion, age change, age, and sex, corrected at pFDR < 0.05; the printed indirect effect was B = 0.003, an unstandardized coefficient, so no standardized effect size is available.

> "When all three SES indicators were included in the model simultaneously, greater increases in in within-SMN (H) connectivity mediated the association between lower parental educational attainment and lower school grades (B = 0.003, CI 0.001–0.007, p = 0.043; see Fig. 2)."
