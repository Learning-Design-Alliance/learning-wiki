Related Claims¶

  • Of 22 included studies on active learning in public health education, 11 used PBL only, 6 used TBL, 3 integrated PBL and CBL, and 2 used CBL only — related
  • Student satisfaction with CBL, PBL and TBL in public health education was high, reported in 21 of 22 studies (95.4%) — related
  • In a randomised trial of 80 first-year postgraduate oncology trainees, CBL produced significantly higher examination scores and satisfaction than traditional lecture-based learning, though confidence intervals crossed the null for examination results — related
  • Findings from other postgraduate specialties suggest PBL and CBL improve performance, knowledge and confidence, but overall RCT quality in this field is deemed unsatisfactory — related
  • Two controlled studies found significantly higher examination scores and satisfaction in TBL groups compared with traditional lecture-based instruction — related
