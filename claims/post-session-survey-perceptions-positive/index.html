Goetz 2015¶

Goetz, J. E., & Barber, C. R. (2015). Evaluating a pre-session homework exercise in a standalone information literacy class. Communications in Information Literacy, 9(2). https://pdxscholar.library.pdx.edu/comminfolit/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Summary of a seven-item post-session survey with three open-ended items administered to all participants after the library session; database search skills and citation management software usage were frequently mentioned as valuable elements.

"The results of the post -session survey indicated that participants generally perceived the session to be very relevant, that they felt moderately prepared to perform research, and that attending the session was moderately to very worthwhile."
