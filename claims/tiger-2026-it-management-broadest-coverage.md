To identify the depth of health informatics needs per role, the authors counted core competency areas with an average relevance rating of at least 80%. The finding that "IT Management in Nursing comprised the largest number (26)" supports the recommendation of comprehensive education for this role.

> "The analysis revealed that IT Management in Nursing comprised the largest number (26). Nursing Management followed with 23 core competency areas, whereas Quality Management (11), Coordination of Interprofessional Care (9), and Clinical Nursing (9) exhibited only half or less of this number."
