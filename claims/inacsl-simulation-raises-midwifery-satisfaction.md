## Evidence

### Tu Lewei 2026

Tu Lewei, Wu Huijie, Xia Xinyi, Zhu Xuehua, Mei Binbin, Zhang Yan, Xu Mengyan, Yang Yeqin. (2026). Effects of standards of best practice in simulation-based training among midwifery students in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08081-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study with a historical control design at one Chinese university; 129 students (intervention n = 62, control n = 67) rated satisfaction on the NLN Student Satisfaction and Self-Confidence in Learning Scale. The intervention group showed "significantly higher scores than the control group" (median 4.30 vs 4.00); the printed median difference was -0.200 (95% CI: -0.400 to 0.0), P = 0.040. No standardized effect size is printed in the results text for this comparison.

> "For student satisfaction, the intervention group demonstrated significantly higher scores than the control group, with a median of 4.30 (P25 = 4.00, P75 = 4.60) versus 4.00 (P25 = 3.80, P75 = 4.40)."
