Evidence¶

Grobler 2012¶

Grobler, B., Bisschoff, T., & Beeka, A. (2012). Changing perceptions of teachers regarding the importance and competence of their principals as leaders. South African Journal of Education, 32(1), 40-55. https://journals.co.za/handle/10520/EJC132899

q3 · i? · causal · r1

A scatter-plot analysis of pre- and post-test importance means yielded an R² of 0.994, indicating that "99.4% of the variance in the pre-test importance means can be explained by the post-test importance means", confirming stability of importance perceptions.
