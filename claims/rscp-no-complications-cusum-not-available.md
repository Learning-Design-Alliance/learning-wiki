## Evidence

### Park Kena 2024

Park Kena, Kwon Ji Young, Yoo Eun-Hee, Lee Seon Hwa, Song Jeong Min, Pyeon Seung Yeon. (2024). Application of CUSUM analysis in assessing learning curves in robot-assisted sacrocolpopexy performed by experienced gynecologist. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-024-02691-x

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

In the retrospective 50-case single-surgeon series, the article reports "There were no intraoperative and short-term post-operative complications during the span of this study" and no conversions; the complication/conversion-based CUSUM analysis was therefore not available. The authors attribute the absence of complications partly to the surgeon's plentiful prior experience.

> "There were no intraoperative and short-term post-operative complications during the span of this study. Furthermore, there were no conversions because of robotic surgery failure. CUSUM analysis based on complication and conversion rate, therefore, was not available."
