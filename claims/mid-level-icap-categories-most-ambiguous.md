`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Disagreement analysis of human confusion matrices plus agent refinement counts (Figure 3): bidirectional confusion between adjacent mid-levels (e.g., 11–23% of Level 4 items coded Level 5 by the other annotator); agents likewise modified Levels 3–5 most, mirroring human refinement.

> "Human disagreements occurred most frequently between Levels 4 and 5 (24% of all disagreement pairs), followed by Levels 5 and 6 (17%) and Levels 3 and 4 (16%), although this pattern may partly reflect the higher frequency of Level 5 behaviors."
