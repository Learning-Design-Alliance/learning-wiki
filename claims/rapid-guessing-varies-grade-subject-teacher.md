Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 40(2), 267-284. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=The+influence+of+rapidly+guessed+item+responses+on+teacher+value-added+estimates

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The article reports an examination of rapid guessing prevalence on achievement tests, finding "differences in rapid guessing across grades, subjects, and teachers." No effect sizes for these differences are printed in the available text.

> "We observed differences in rapid guessing across grades, subjects, and teachers; however, this behavior did not appear to have a substantive effect on teacher value-added estimates."
