Nanteni Ganesan & Leong Kwan Eu 2018¶

Nanteni Ganesan & Leong Kwan Eu. (2018). Effectiveness of Fathom on Statistical Reasoning among Form Four Students. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences, 6(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1192963

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pilot study conducted at secondary schools in Klang under conditions similar to the actual study, using internal-consistency reliability because the test was administered only once. The instrument has thirteen questions gauging statistical reasoning.

"There were 30 subjects in the sample ( N=30) participated and the Statistical Reasoning Assessment instrument obtained Cronbach Alpha of .82 hence the coefficient indicated that the instrument were reliable."
