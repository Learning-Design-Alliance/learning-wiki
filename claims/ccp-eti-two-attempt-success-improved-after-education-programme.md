### Appelboam Thomas 2025

Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-025-01444-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective single-service evaluation of 390 cardiac-arrest patients intubated by CCPs from April 2019 to December 2024, comparing 2019 and 2024 rates with chi-squared tests. The evaluation reports "Overall ETI success rates improved by 7.9% points to 97.7% in 2024 (χ2 = 4.205, p = 0.040)".

> "Over the course of six years (comparing 2019 to 2024, Fig. 1), first-pass success rates improved by 18.4% points to 86.2% in 2024 (χ2 = 7.125, p = 0.0076). Overall ETI success rates improved by 7.9% points to 97.7% in 2024 (χ2 = 4.205, p = 0.040)."
