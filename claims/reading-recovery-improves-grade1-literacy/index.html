WWC synthesis of May et al. (2015), a low-attrition randomized controlled trial with 6,888 grade 1 students in 1,254 U.S. schools. The report states there is "moderate evidence that Reading Recovery® positively impacted student achievement in literacy" at the end of the implementation year.

"Based on one study that meets WWC standards, there is moderate evidence that Reading Recovery® positively impacted student achievement in literacy immediately after the intervention."
