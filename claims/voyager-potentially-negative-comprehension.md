### Voyager Universal Literacy System® — WWC Intervention Report 2007

Voyager Universal Literacy System® — WWC Intervention Report. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/538

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

One quasi-experimental study (Hecht, 2003; 213 kindergarten students in four low-income Orange County, Florida schools) contributed the only comprehension finding. The WWC-printed effect size was –0.67 (medium-to-large negative), with an improvement index of –25 percentile points.

> "Hecht (2003) reported a negative and statistically significant effect on the Stanford-Binet Intelligence Expressive Vocabulary subtest. This outcome was not statistically significant according to the WWC analysis but the effect size was large enough to be substantively important (that is, smaller than –0.25)."
