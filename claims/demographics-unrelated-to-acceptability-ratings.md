### Lundervold 2013

Lundervold, D. A., Ament, P. A., Holt, P. S., & Hunt, L. S. (2013). Comparison of Younger and Older Adults' Acceptability of Treatment for Generalized Anxiety Disorder Co-Occurring with Parkinson's Disease. International Journal of Behavioral Consultation and Therapy, 8(2). https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1555-7855

`q3 · i0`

Point biserial correlations (Table 2) related demographic and anxiety/PD history variables to AARP-Med and AARP-BRT ratings. The text states "Only one demographic variable (history of counseling) was significantly relat ed to acceptability of treatment"; Table 2 shows currently receiving counseling correlated with AARP-BRT (r = .25, p = .01), and immediate family PD (r = -.19, p = .03) and anxiety (r = -.21, p = .03) with AARP-Med.
