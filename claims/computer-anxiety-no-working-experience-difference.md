# Computer anxiety scores among teachers do not significantly differ by working experience at posttest or follow-up

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Working experience showed no significant difference in mean computer anxiety score at posttest (ηp2 = 0.034) or follow-up (ηp2 = 0.026), though the pretest difference was significant (P = .031, ηp2 = 0.096). [→ Onah BI 2023](#onah-bi-2023)

## Evidence

### Onah BI 2023

Onah BI, Ede MO, Eze BN, Uzoegwu CR, Mgboji C, Agbo GC, Uzuagu AU, Chiamagu C, Agbo PA, Oneli JO, Ogbueghu SN. (2023). Effectiveness of rational emotive behavior education (REBE) on computer anxiety among school teachers enrolled in e-learning. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000034303

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA from the same trial across the three measurement points; the pretest comparison was significant (P = .031) while posttest and follow-up were not, so the effect does not replicate across all periods.

> "there was no significant difference among teachers with various working experiences in the mean computer anxiety score in Nigeria, F (1101) = 3.084, P = .031, ηp2 = 0.096; F (1101) = 1.008, P = .393, ηp2 = 0.034; and F (1101) = .764, P = .518, ηp2 = 0.026, respectively."
