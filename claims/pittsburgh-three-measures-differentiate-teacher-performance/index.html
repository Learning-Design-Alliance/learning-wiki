Evidence¶

Duncan Chaplin 2014¶

Duncan Chaplin, Brian Gill, Allison Thompkins, Hannah Miller. (2014). Professional Practice, Student Surveys, and Value-Added: Multiple Measures of Teacher Effectiveness in the Pittsburgh Public Schools. Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic, Institute of Education Sciences. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The report's Key Findings state, based on analysis of district data on observations of professional practices, student surveys, and value-added contributions to achievement growth, that "All three measures of teacher effectiveness being developed by the Pittsburgh Public Schools" can differentiate performance.

"All three measures of teacher effectiveness being developed by the Pittsburgh Public Schools—professional practice, student surveys, and value- added measures—have the potential to differentiate teacher performance."
