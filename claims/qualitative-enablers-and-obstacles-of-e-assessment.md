## Evidence

### Nilsson Annika 2015

Nilsson Annika, Engström Maria. (2015). E-assessment and an e-training program among elderly care staff lacking formal competence: results of a mixed-methods intervention study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-0843-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of semi-structured interviews with 15 staff who completed the e-training program, purposively sampled for variation in age, gender and e-assessment results; the Enablers category captured learning that was "easy, flexible and accessible" and growing self-confidence. Obstacles included nervousness about being filmed and lack of confidence in their own abilities.

> "The staff described this category in terms of being able to test their skills and knowledge in an easy, flexible and accessible way as well as feeling motivated to receive grades, seeing possibilities and experiencing that their self-confidence increased during the process."
