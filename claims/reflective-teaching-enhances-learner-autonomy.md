## Evidence

### Abdolrezapour 2015

Abdolrezapour, P., & Fallah, E. (2015). An investigation into the impact of reflective teaching on EFL learners’ autonomy and intrinsic motivation. Cypriot Journal of Educational Science. 10(4), 305-315. https://doi.org/10.18844/cjes.v10i4.229

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test comparing post-treatment learner autonomy of the experimental group (mean 32.66, SD 1.88) and control group (mean 27.80, SD 2.04), each of 30 learners. The article reports "p-value equals 0.002" and concludes the experimental group outperformed the control group.

> "Table 4 shows that p-value equals 0.002 which is lower than the confidence level of 0.05. As a result, it can be concluded that the post -treatment means of two groups in terms of Learner Autonomy are significantly different."
