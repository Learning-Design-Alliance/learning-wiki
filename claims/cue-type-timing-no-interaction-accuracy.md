## Evidence

### Waldeyer 2024

Waldeyer, J., Endres, T., Roelle, J., Baars, M., & Renkl, A. (2024). How to Optimize Self-Assessment Accuracy in Cognitive Skill Acquisition When Learning from Worked Examples. Educational Psychology Review, 36, 103. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09944-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 exploratory ANOVA analysis (N = 129). The interaction of prompted cue type and timing of cue prompt on absolute accuracy "did not reveal a statistically significant interaction effect", F(1, 125) = 0.10, p = 0.758. No effect size beyond the printed ηp2 < 0.01 was reported.
