Evidence¶

Roessingh 2006¶

Roessingh, H. (2006). BICS-CALP: An Introduction for Some, a Review for Others. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 23(2), Spring 2006. https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v23i2.57

q1 · i? · review · r1

The author states as expert interpretation that L1 transfer is "an efficient process" for older arrivals, but that the learning curve "starts to look sluggish" once learners outgrow their L1. No empirical data support this sentence in the article.

"Older-arriving students can transfer what they already know from L1 into English, an efficient process. But whenever the ESL learner outgrows his or her first language and must then acquire both English and the associated new conceptual information together, the learning curve starts to look sluggish."
