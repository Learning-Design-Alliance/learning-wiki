### Coleman E. 2019

Coleman E., O'Connor E. (2019). The role of WhatsApp® in medical education; a scoping review and instructional design model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1706-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Scoping review synthesis of 23 studies of WhatsApp in medical education. The review reports that seven studies, in a total of 647 learners, showed "an improvement in learner knowledge" and one showed a behaviour change, while noting methodological weaknesses in each knowledge study.

> "a total of sixteen studies described its use primarily for educational purposes, of which seven reported, in a total of 647 learners, an improvement in learner knowledge, and one reported a change in learner behaviour"
