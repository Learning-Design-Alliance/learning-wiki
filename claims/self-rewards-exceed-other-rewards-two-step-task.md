## Evidence

### Yangchu Huang 2026

Yangchu Huang, Xinyi Du & Shanshan Zhen. (2026). Deciding for others diminishes model-based decision-making but depends on individual prosociality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00397-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-tests on task performance of 92 adults in a gamified two-step task with self- and other-related blocks. The study reports "significantly higher reward scores than deciding for others" with printed effect size d = 0.75; rewards for self were M ± SD = 662.05 ± 33.09 versus 624.15 ± 62.83 for others.

> "deciding for oneself was associated with significantly higher reward scores than deciding for others, t(91) = 6.68, p < 0.001, 95% CI [26.64, 49.16], d = 0.75"
