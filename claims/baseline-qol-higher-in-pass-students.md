### Hettiarachchi Manjula 2014

Hettiarachchi Manjula, Lakmal Fonseka Chathuranga, Gunasekara Priyanka, Jayasinghe Prasanjanie, Maduranga Dasun. (2014). How does the quality of life and the underlying biochemical indicators correlate with the performance in academic examinations in a group of medical students of Sri Lanka? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v19.22772

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-group comparison of pre-exam WHOQOL-BREF domain scores among 78 students: 54 passed at first attempt and n=24 were referred. Table 3 marks baseline differences in physical health, psychological and environment domains (p=0.05); the referred group's total score improved significantly post-exam (repeated measures, p=0.05).

> "The 'pass' students had significantly higher scores in each domain when compared with their counterparts, who were 'referred' at the examination (n=24; Table 3)."
