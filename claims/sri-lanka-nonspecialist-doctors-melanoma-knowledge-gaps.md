### Herath H. M. M. T. B. 2018

Herath H. M. M. T. B., Keragala B. S. D. P., Udeshika W. A. E., Samarawickrama S. S. M., Pahalagamage S. P., Kulatunga Aruna, Rodrigo Chaturaka. (2018). Knowledge, attitudes and skills in melanoma diagnosis among doctors: a cross sectional study from Sri Lanka. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3499-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive cross-sectional questionnaire study of 123 non-specialist doctors at the National Hospital of Sri Lanka (response rate 61.5%). The knowledge component was assessed against British Association of Dermatologists guidelines; "Only 4 out of 123 (3.3%) correctly stated all four types of melanoma". Risk-factor awareness was better (82.9%).

> "Only 4 out of 123 (3.3%) correctly stated all four types of melanoma and 99 (80.5%) could not name any. However, 102 (82.9%) doctors were aware of at least two risk factors for melanoma."
