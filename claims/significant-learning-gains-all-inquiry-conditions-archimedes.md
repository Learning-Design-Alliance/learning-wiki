# Inquiry learning in a virtual laboratory on Archimedes' principle produces significant conceptual knowledge gains in all three guidance conditions

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Learners' scores increased significantly from pre- to post-test in all conditions, with large effects (EDT dCohen = 1.33; CS dCohen = 0.83; CM dCohen = 0.92). [→ Siswa van Riesen 2018](#siswa-van-riesen-2018)

## Evidence

### Siswa van Riesen 2018

Siswa van Riesen, Hannie Gijlers, Anjo Anjewierden, Ton de Jong. (2018). Supporting learners' experiment design. Education Tech Research Dev (2018) 66:475–491. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9568-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Controlled experiment with 120 third-year pre-university students (86 analyzed) randomly assigned within class to three conditions. Per-condition Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed large printed effect sizes: EDT dCohen = 1.33, CS dCohen = 0.83, CM dCohen = 0.92; the overall pre-to-post effect was dCohen = 0.99.

> "Learners' scores significantly increased from pre- to post-test in all conditions (EDT condition: Z = 4.133, p \ .001, dCohen = 1.33; CS condition: Z = 3.576, p \ .001, dCohen = 0.83; CM condition: Z = 3.044, p = .002, dCohen = 0.92), showing a large learning effect in all conditions."
