Allensworth 2008¶

Allensworth, E., Nomi, T., Montgomery, N., & Lee, V. E. (2008). College Preparatory Curriculum for All: Consequences of Ninth-Grade Course Taking in Algebra and English on Academic Outcomes in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/college-preparatory-curriculum-all-consequences-ninth-grade-coursetaking-algebra-and

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Difference-in-difference interrupted time-series (Table 3) comparing affected schools to unaffected control schools. Failures for lowest-ability students increased 7.4 percentage points (p<.05), average-ability students were absent more often (3.14 days, p<.05), lowest-ability math GPAs declined .15 points (p<.01), and math achievement scores were unaffected.

"Compared to the pre-policy years, post-policy students in the two lower-ability groups were more likely to earn credits for Algebra I or higher in ninth grade—increases of 8.8 and 7.4 percentage points for the lowest- and low-ability groups (p<.01)."
