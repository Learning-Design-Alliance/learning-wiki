Linear mixed model on targeted math fluency (iPad task similar to Woodcock-Johnson III Math Fluency) in 725 students, controlling for BRT and demographics with school as random effect. The quote reports the test statistic and variance explained; "attention showed positive main effects on math fluency scores". No standardised effect size printed.

> "attention showed positive main effects on math fluency scores (F(1, 571) = 65.60, p < 0.001, R2m (model) = 0.27, R2c (model) = 0.27)"
