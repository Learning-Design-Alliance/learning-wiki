`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of mean trust scores across all robot–task combinations in the N = 81 experiment. The article reports "r = .56–.89 and p < .001", and interprets that participants accepting a robot in one context tended to accept the same and other robots across contexts.

> "A correlation matrix of mean trust scores (Fig. 5) revealed strong positive associations across all robot–task combinations, with r = .56–.89 and p < .001."
