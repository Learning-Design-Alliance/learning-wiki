## Evidence

### Tsigarides Jordan 2017

Tsigarides Jordan, Wingfield Laura R., Kulendran Myutan. (2017). Does a PBL-based medical curriculum predispose training in specific career paths? A systematic review of the literature. BMC Research Notes. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5219658/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Study of final-year medical students (n = 511) at the University of New Mexico School of Medicine examining self-reported student interest in specialties and how this changes throughout medical school with a PBL vs non-PBL curriculum. The review reports both retention and switching findings at P = 0.05.

> "They found that significantly more PBL students retained an interest in a career in primary care (P = 0.05) and significantly more PBL students switched to an interest in primary care during medical school (P = 0.05)."
