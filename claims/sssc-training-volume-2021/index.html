Retrospective Qualtrics survey of SSSC activity for calendar year 2021. The report states "15 (88.2%) conducted trainings between January 1 and December 31, 2021," with event counts ranging from 2 to 250 and averaging 90 trainings per SSSC.

q2 · i? · design · r2

Smartsheet activity records submitted by 9 SSSCs from nine different states documenting 2021 training events. The report prints "a total of 1,098 training events in 2021"; participants most often were campus/district administrators, local law enforcement, and non-law-enforcement school safety professionals.

"The SSSCs reported a total of 1,098 training events in 2021, ranging from 2 to 303 training events, with an average of 122 per SSSC."
