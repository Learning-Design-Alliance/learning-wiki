## Evidence

### Kelly-Ann Allen 2025

Kelly-Ann Allen. (2025). School Belonging: Evidence, Experts, and Everyday Gaps. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10055-x

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Expert interview with Christine Logel, drawing on her own research on belonging uncertainty (Logel et al., 2021; Walton & Cohen, 2007). She reports that everyday events such as a bad grade can temporarily lower belonging. No effect size is printed.

> "Students who have a higher average level of belonging, but also have higher belonging uncertainty, will be at risk of negative outcomes if they hit enough bumps in the road of their academic journey."
