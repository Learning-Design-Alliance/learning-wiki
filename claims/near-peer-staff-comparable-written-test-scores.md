# Near-peer tutored and staff-tutored medical students achieved statistically comparable written critical appraisal knowledge and skills test scores at mid- and end-of-module

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At the end of the module, mean written test scores did not differ significantly between near-peer tutored and staff-tutored students (t(241) = 1.553, p = 0.122). [→ Widyahening Indah S. 2019](#widyahening-indah-s-2019)

## Evidence

### Widyahening Indah S. 2019

Widyahening Indah S., Findyartini Ardi, Ranakusuma Respati W., Dewiasty Esthika, Harimurti Kuntjoro. (2019). Evaluation of the role of near-peer teaching in critical appraisal skills learning: a randomized crossover trial. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5c39.b55b

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized crossover trial of 241 fourth-year medical students in the CE-EBM module, with the full cohort crossing over between tutors after two sessions. "the difference in mean + SD of written test scores ... was again statistically non-significant (t(241) = 1.553, p = 0.122)"; means were 68.20 (near-peer) vs 70.61 (staff).

> "At the end of the module, the difference in mean + SD of written test scores for the near-peer tutored group (68.20 + 12.50) and the staff-tutored group (70.61+10.99) was again statistically non-significant (t(241) = 1.553, p = 0.122)."

### Widyahening Indah S. 2019 (2)

Widyahening Indah S., Findyartini Ardi, Ranakusuma Respati W., Dewiasty Esthika, Harimurti Kuntjoro. (2019). Evaluation of the role of near-peer teaching in critical appraisal skills learning: a randomized crossover trial. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5c39.b55b

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mid-test after the first two discussion sessions in the same crossover trial, covering clinical question formulation, search, diagnosis and prognosis. "no statistically significant difference (t(241) = -1.520, p = 0.130)" between near-peer (65.89) and staff-tutored (63.18) groups.

> "A mid-evaluation following the first two discussion sessions found no statistically significant difference (t(241) = -1.520, p = 0.130) between mean + SD of written test scores for the near-peer tutored group (65.89 + 12.86) and the staff-tutored group (63.18 + 13.69)."
