## Evidence

### Shilling V 2025

Shilling V, Starkings R, Fallowfield L. (2025). Feasibility and acceptability of a virtual learning module for navigating angry conversations in clinical encounters. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06726-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Conceptual content analysis of free-text responses in the 20-student pilot. Pre-intervention, 3/15 realism sections were positive and 10/15 negative; post-intervention "18 sections of text were categorised as relating to realism; 9 were positive and 9 were negative." Participants reported the experience was more realistic than expected but characters did not fully express emotion.
