## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For 17 of 19 belonging items there were no significant modality differences; in-person students more often agreed they made new friends (48% vs 39%) and remote students more often agreed instructors were interested in them (43% vs 35%). [→ Mueller 2026](#mueller-2026)

## Evidence

### Mueller 2026

Mueller, L. O., Bamrah, M., Petrie, K. L., Tour, E., McDonnell, L., Lo, S. M., & Meaders, C. L. (2026). The Fourth Credit Hour: How do we Teach in Biology Discussion Sections? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0185

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests of independence with Holm correction across 19 binary belonging items. In-person students reported making new friends at 48% versus 39% remote (Cramer's V = 0.07); remote students agreed instructors were interested at 43% versus 35% (V = 0.067).

> "First, students from in-person discussion sections were significantly more likely to agree with the statement “I made new friends in this course” (χ² (1, N = 4989) = 25.809, p < 0.001, Cramer's V = 0.07)."
