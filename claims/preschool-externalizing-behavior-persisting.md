## Evidence

### Ansari 2018

Ansari, A. (2018). The persistence of preschool effects from early childhood through adolescence. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000255

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same matched-sample path models of the ECLS-K 1998 cohort, teacher-reported externalizing behavior (adapted Social Rating Scale) was worse for preschool attendees at kindergarten entry with a printed effect size of E.S. = 0.18 SDs, persisting at reduced levels through fifth grade (E.S. = 0.05–0.16 SDs).

> "preschool attendees were found to demonstrate higher levels of externalizing behavior upon kindergarten entry (E.S. = 0.18 SDs), which persisted at reduced levels through the end of fifth grade (E.S. = 0.05–0.16 SDs)."
