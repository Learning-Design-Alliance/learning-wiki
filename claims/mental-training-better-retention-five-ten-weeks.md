## Evidence

### Nelskamp Arne 2021

Nelskamp Arne, Schnurr Benedikt, Germanyuk Alexandra, Sterz Jasmina, Lorenz Jonas, Sader Robert, Rüsseler Miriam, Seifert Lukas B. (2021). Comparison of ‘Mental training’ and physical practice in the mediation of a structured facial examination: a quasi randomized, blinded and controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02603-0

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Curricular formative OSCE (8 stations) taken 5–10 weeks after training by all 67 students, video-recorded and rated by two blinded examiners. The Mental Training group retained significantly better performance, with "Cohen’s d = 0.38" (Rater 1) and d = 0.64 (Rater 2).

> "In the long-term retention test 5–10 weeks after the intervention (T2), students who had been trained with the ‘Mental Training’ approach again showed significantly better results than students who had been trained with the ‘See One, Do One’ approach (Rater 1 p < 0.05; Cohen’s d = 0.38; Rater 2 p = 0.008; Cohen’s d = 0.64, r = 0.64)"
