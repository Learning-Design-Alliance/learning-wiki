## Evidence

### Peng Wang 2026

Peng Wang, Lesya Ganushchak, Camille Welie, Roel van Steensel. (2026). Emotions Are Not Random: Machine Learning Reveals Predictable Patterns in a 21-Day Second Language Learning Trajectory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10141-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Variance decomposition and lag-1 autocorrelations computed within individuals across the 6,918 EMA episodes (Table 1). The article describes a "moderate inertia" regime where recent history matters but context retains leverage.

> "ICCs indicate that within-person variation dominates, accounting for 62–68% across outcomes. Meanwhile, the mean lag-1 autocorrelations (r ≈.17–0.19) show modest but reliable inertia."

### Peng Wang 2026 (2)

Peng Wang, Lesya Ganushchak, Camille Welie, Roel van Steensel. (2026). Emotions Are Not Random: Machine Learning Reveals Predictable Patterns in a 21-Day Second Language Learning Trajectory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10141-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

GAM trend and STL seasonal-trend decomposition of each learner's 21-day series; cyclical components explained 10.4–17.6% of variance and varied idiosyncratically across learners.

> "These linear trends accounted for only a small proportion of total variance (5–20%), indicating that short-term fluctuations dominated emotional and cognitive trajectories."
