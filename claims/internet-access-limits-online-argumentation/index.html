Fettahlıoğlu 2018¶

Fettahlıoğlu, P., & Kaleci, D. (2018). Online Argumentation Implementation in the Development of Critical Thinking Disposition. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i3.2904

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of interview responses; Table 6 lists the limitations theme at 30.77% of statements, split between computer problems and internet problems. The article also reports candidates "strongly supported the elimination of internet connection problems to improve the course".

"In the limitations part of the application, it is identified that teacher candidates have computer and Internet access problems especially out of the university and therefore not all the teacher candidates can always follow the Moodle application, as they want."
