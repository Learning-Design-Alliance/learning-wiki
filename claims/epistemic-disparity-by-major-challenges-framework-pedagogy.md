Authors' interpretation (type e) of the Table 1 major-by-orientation distribution and Table 2 gain comparisons for the 39-student cohort. The article concludes that "diversity of majors appears to be linked to an epistemic disparity that presents a challenge to problem -solving framework pedagogies."

> "While biology majors were more likely to prefer and benefit from  a framework-oriented view, this is not true for chemistry and computer science majors, who prefer performance, or for HBS majors, who either prefer performance or have a v aguely-defined view of problem -solving."
