Will Yeadon, Tom Hardy. (2024). The Impact of AI in Physics Education: A Comprehensive Review from GCSE to University Levels. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad1fa2](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad1fa2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Length comparison of LLM responses to an example beta-minus decay question under the three prompting styles, reported in the example-answer section. The article prints averages of "427 characters", "405 characters", and "just 228 characters".

> "The Zero-shot approach, while thorough, often yielded verbose answers, averaging 427 characters in length. In contrast, the In-context Learning method trimmed responses to an average of 405 characters. The Confirmatory Check approach stood out as the most concise, with answers averaging just 228 characters."
