## Evidence

### de Beer Marianne 2015

de Beer Marianne, Mårtensson Lena. (2015). Feedback on students' clinical reasoning skills during fieldwork education. Australian Occupational Therapy Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/1440-1630.12208

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative archive analysis correlating FPE grades with AAP grades. The group-level correlation was r = 0.486; the per-hospital correlation was r = 0.181, which the authors use to argue academic prowess does not explain FPE performance.

> "The students as a group, without taking hospital placement into account, obtained an average AAP of 64.3% for all occupational therapy modules with a correlation between the FPE and AAP of r = 0.486."
