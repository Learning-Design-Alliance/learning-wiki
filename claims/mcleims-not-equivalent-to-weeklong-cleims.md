> "This condensed format should be interpreted as a pragmatic adaptation rather than a proven equivalent to the original week-long CLEIMS model. Although the shorter format may reduce opportunities for repeated practice, progressive complexity, and long-term consolidation, it may be more feasible for integration into a crowded undergraduate medical curriculum."
