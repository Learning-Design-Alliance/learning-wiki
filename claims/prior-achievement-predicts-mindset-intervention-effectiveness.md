## Evidence

### Bosch 2021

Bosch, Nigel. (2021). Identifying Supportive Student Factors for Mindset Interventions: A Two-model Machine Learning Approach. Computers & Education, 167, 104190. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104190

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A secondary analysis of the National Study of Learning Mindsets (over 10,000 students) used XGBoost and Shapley values to model intervention effects on GPA. The analysis found "pre-experiment GPA was the most important predictor of the intervention effect" after controlling for baseline GPA via a first model.

> "Feature importance results in Table 2 indicate that pre-experiment GPA was the most important predictor of the intervention effect (M = 0.037, SD = 0.028), despite controlling for overall relationships between past GPA and future GPA effects by applying model 1."
