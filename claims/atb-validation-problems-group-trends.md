Glen Smith, Adit Gupta, and Chris MacLellan. (2024). Apprentice Tutor Builder: A Platform For Users to Create and Personalize Intelligent Tutors. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2404.07883](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2404.07883)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

User log analysis of validation problems, defined as any additional problem used to train the agent after it has demonstrated the ability to fully solve a problem. The article reports the opposing trends for "the Low group" and "the High group".

> "For the Low group, the average number of validation problems increased from 0.33 to 1.0 when going from the FR tutor to the S25 tutor. In contrast, this number decreased from 2.25 to 0.15 for the High group."
