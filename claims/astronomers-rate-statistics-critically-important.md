### Eadie 2019

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Preliminary, exploratory online survey of current and former CASCA members (123 responses, roughly 25% response rate) conducted in July 2019. On Question 3's 1–5 importance scale, "approximately 78% of responses being either 4 or 5" and 40.7% chose 5; no effect size is reported.

> "Respondents overwhelming indicated that statistics was towards the “critically important” level, with approximately 78% of responses being either 4 or 5 (5 was chosen by 40.7% of all respondents)."
