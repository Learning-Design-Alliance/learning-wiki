Ninth Grade Course Performance Found to Be the Strongest Predictor of High School Graduation for Chicago English Language Learners 2012¶

Ninth Grade Course Performance Found to Be the Strongest Predictor of High School Graduation for Chicago English Language Learners. (2012). University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research / National High School Center. http://www.betterhighschools.org

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Cohort analysis of the 2004-05 CPS ninth-grade ELL cohort. The report states newcomers after age 12 were "less likely to graduate than other students with the same grades and attendance"; school quality was the most important associated factor. No effect size printed.

"ELL students who were newcomers to CPS after age 12 were less likely to graduate than other students with the same grades and attendance. The most important factor associated with these lower graduation rates was the quality of schools these students attended"
