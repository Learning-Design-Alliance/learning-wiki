## Evidence

### Fraser S 2019

Fraser S, Wright AD, van Donkelaar P, Smirl JD. (2019). Cross-sectional comparison of spiral versus block integrated curriculums in preparing medical students to diagnose and manage concussions. BMC Medical Education 19(17). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1439-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Definition-of-concussion results: both curricula showed gaps on diagnostic criteria; the one-symptom difference between groups was not statistically significant (p = 0.062). Headache, dizziness, and confusion were selected by 99, 94, and 97% with no significant differences between curricula.

> "across all medical students only 59% of participants knew that "less than 1/3 of all concussions involve a loss of consciousness"; and just 32% (26% spiral; 40% block, X21, 148 = 3.47, p = 0.062) were aware that only one symptom is required to make the clinical diagnosis of concussion."
