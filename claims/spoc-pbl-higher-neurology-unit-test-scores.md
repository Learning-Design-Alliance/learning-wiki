## Evidence

### Li Xiaoling 2024

Li Xiaoling, Li Fanju, Liu Wei, Xie Qinfang, Yuan Boyao, Wang Lijuan, Wang Manxia. (2024). Effectiveness of the application of small private online course combined with PBL model based on massive open online course in the teaching of neurology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06460-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 48 fourth-year clinical medicine students (24 per group) taught the same neurology chapter by the same teachers. The article reports the SPOC+PBL mean "84.29 ± 1.65" versus LBL "77.0 ± 1.92", with P < 0.01, effect size = 4.07, power = 1 (Fig. 4).

> "The average unit test score of the SPOC + PBL group was 84.29 ± 1.65, while in the LBL group, the average score was 77.0 ± 1.92"
