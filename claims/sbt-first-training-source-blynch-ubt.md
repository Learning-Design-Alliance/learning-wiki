`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 39 providers (Table 2), Fisher's exact test. Trained providers were more likely to have first learned the techniques from the GO MOMS course or a colleague (p = 0.0086 and 0.0068); no effect size printed.

> "Participants without GO MOMS training were significantly less likely to have ever been taught UBT and B-Lynch (p = 0.0353 and p = 0.0153, respectively)."
