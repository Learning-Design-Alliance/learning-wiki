## Evidence

### Armstrong G 2010

Armstrong G, Blashki G, Joubert L, Bland R, Moulding R, Gunn J, Naccarella L. (2010). An evaluation of the effect of an educational intervention for Australian social workers on competence in delivering brief cognitive behavioural strategies: A randomised controlled trial. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-10-304

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-sample t-tests at follow-up in the RCT against the printed base competency threshold of 39 on the 66-point CTS; the intervention group mean was 45.78 (t(15) = 2.20, p = 0.044) and the control group mean 30.68 was significantly smaller (t(13) = 6.06, p < 0.001). No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The mean competence of 45.78 within the intervention group at follow-up was statistically significantly larger than the CTS base competency threshold of 39; one sample t(15) = 2.20, p = 0.044."
