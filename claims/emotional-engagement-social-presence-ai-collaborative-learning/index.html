Evidence¶

Kovari 2025¶

Kovari, A. (2025). A systematic review of AI-powered collaborative learning in higher education: Trends and outcomes from the last decade. Social Sciences & Humanities Open, 11, 101335. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssaho.2025.101335

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of affective-factor studies (narrative review attributing this to Molina-Barron et al., 2023, and Avsec et al., 2022) reports that AI-driven systems tracking emotional cues can intervene to re-engage students emotionally. No effect sizes are printed.

"AI tools that increase social presence and emotional engagement can make learners feel closer and more connected to the learning process, thus improving AI-based collaborative learning outcomes"
