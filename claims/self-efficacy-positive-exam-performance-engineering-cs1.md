# Self-efficacy is positively associated with exam performance among engineering students in a flipped CS1 course

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Self-efficacy factor scores correlate positively with exam averages (r = 0.081, p = .044), a small but statistically significant association. [→ Griffin Pitts 2026](#griffin-pitts-2026)
`q2 i?` Self-efficacy scores increase across exam-performance quartiles, with the highest performers reporting the strongest self-efficacy (Kruskal-Wallis χ²(3) = 10.662, p = .014). [→ Griffin Pitts 2026 (2)](#griffin-pitts-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Griffin Pitts 2026

Griffin Pitts, Ashish Aggarwal. (2026). Engineering Students' Self-Efficacy, Perceptions, and Performance in a Flipped CS1 Course. Preprint, to appear in Proceedings of the 2026 IEEE Frontiers in Education Conference (FIE). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of survey and exam data from 602 engineering students in a flipped CS1 course found "self- efficacy was positively related to exam performance (r = 0.081, p = .044)"; the correlation is small and no effect size beyond r is printed.

> "Pearson's correlations showed that self- efficacy was positively related to exam performance (r = 0.081, p = .044)"

### Griffin Pitts 2026 (2)

Griffin Pitts, Ashish Aggarwal. (2026). Engineering Students' Self-Efficacy, Perceptions, and Performance in a Flipped CS1 Course. Preprint, to appear in Proceedings of the 2026 IEEE Frontiers in Education Conference (FIE). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quartile analysis dividing 602 students by exam score (Q1 lowest, Q4 highest) showed "significant differences across quartiles for self- efficacy", with Q4 students reporting the strongest self-efficacy (M = 4.150, SD = 0.702) versus Q1 (M = 3.908, SD = 0.768).

> "Kruskal–Wallis tests indicated significant differences across quartiles for self- efficacy (χ 2(3) = 10.662, p = .014)"
