The prediction models were derived from Random Forest coefficient estimates on the 37-country PISA 2018 dataset, where "a country with a score of greater than 3,219 from the model obtained from the Reading section can be successful" and analogous maths and science thresholds were proposed. No accuracy metrics or validation statistics are printed for these models.

> "it is predicted that a country with a score of greater than 3,219 from the model obtained from the Reading section can be successful. It is predicted that a country with a score of greater than 2.396 in Maths and greater than 2.732 in Science will be successful."
