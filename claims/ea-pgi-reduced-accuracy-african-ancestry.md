Cross-ancestry portability analysis predicting EduYears in African-genetic-ancestry subsamples of HRS and Add Health. The article notes the UKB-based shortfall exceeds what population differences in allele frequencies and LD alone predict, and is greater for EA than prior work found for height, BMI and six other phenotypes.

> "the incremental R 2 was 1.3% (95% confidence interval (CI), 0.6% to 2.2%) and 2.3% (95% CI, 1.1% to 3.7%), implying that the relative accuracies for EA in the HRS and Add Health are only 11% and 15%, respectively."
