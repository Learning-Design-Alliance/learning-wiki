## Evidence

### Nyman A 2019

Nyman A, Korhonen T, Lehtonen L, Haataja L, on behalf of the PIPARI Study Group. (2019). School performance is age appropriate with support services in very preterm children at 11 years of age. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.14763

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Gender comparisons within each group using logistic regression (Table 4); e.g. concentration in preterm boys OR 4.6 (2.0–10.5), p <0.001, and in control boys OR 3.0 (1.3–7.0), p 0.011.

> "Classroom functioning, on the other hand, was weaker in boys than girls in both groups in all other aspects apart from social skills."

### Nyman A 2019 (2)

Nyman A, Korhonen T, Lehtonen L, Haataja L, on behalf of the PIPARI Study Group. (2019). School performance is age appropriate with support services in very preterm children at 11 years of age. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.14763

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 4 logistic regressions within the preterm group found no significant academic gender differences; in controls, boys only had weaker text production (OR 5.4, 2.0–14.7, p <0.001).

> "There were no gender differences in any aspects of the academic functioning of the very preterm group."
