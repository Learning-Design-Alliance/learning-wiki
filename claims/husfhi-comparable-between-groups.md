Impact of a virtual reality-based simulation training for shoulder dystocia on human and technical skills among caregivers: a randomized-controlled trial. In the randomized crossover trial, the 14-item HuFSHI questionnaire showed no statistically significant group difference (Table 2 reports p = 0.34 and p = 0.60 for the two sessions). Equivalence was not tested; no effect size is printed.
