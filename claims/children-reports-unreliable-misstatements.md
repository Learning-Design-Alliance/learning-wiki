Lipmann, O. (1911). Pedagogical Psychology of Report. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070700

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The author's synthesis of the experimental report-research literature, stating that "reports are in general extremely unreliable" and that misstatements increase for children even when observers had "absolutely no reason" to lie. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The first and most obvious result of the report-research is to show that reports are in general extremely unreliable. While this is true to a high degree even of the reports of adults, the number of misstatements shows a marked increase when the reports of children are investigated."
