### Jung 2013

Jung, M., Hartman, P., Smith, T., & Wallace, S. (2013). The Effectiveness of Teaching Number Relationships in Preschool. International Journal of Instruction, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085361

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Supplementary analysis of posttest scores comparing the six children who received additional math instruction with other intervention and control children. Their higher mean of 109.00 was descriptive and not statistically significant.

> "Although, at the sample level, their mean score was higher (M = 109.00), their scores did not differ significantly from other children in the intervention or control group, F(2,70) = 1.46, p = .24."
