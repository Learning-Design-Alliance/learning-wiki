# Hong Kong and Singaporean students are more formal in tone than L1 and Thai students when using adversative and resultive connectives

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When using adversative, causal or resultive DCs, L1 and Thai students are more informal than Hong Kong and Singaporean students, who favour formal forms such as however, nevertheless and hence. [→ Hu 2015](#hu-2015)

## Evidence

### Hu 2015

Hu, C., & Li, Y. (2015). Discourse Connectives in L1 and L2 Argumentative Writing. Higher Education Studies. https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v5n4p30

`q2 · i2`

Frequency comparison of formal versus informal adversative and resultive DCs across sub-corpora (Section 4.2.3). The article reports the same trend for so (149 times in L1) versus therefore (40 times), and notes Singaporean students use the formal hence frequently whereas it does not occur in L1 and Thai essays.

> "the more formal adversative connective however occurs only 48 times whereas its informal equivalent but occurs 178 times in L1 writers’ essays. In sharp contrast, but occurs only 49 times whereas however occurs 129 times in essays written by Singaporean students."
