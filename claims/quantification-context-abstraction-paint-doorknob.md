## Evidence

### Suzanne White Brahmia 2016

Suzanne White Brahmia, Andrew Boudreaux, Stephen E. Kanim. (2016). Obstacles to mathematization in introductory physics. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.01235](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.01235)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Isomorphic-pair comparison in the large-N multiple-choice assessment of freshman engineering students: item V involves paint covering a wall, item VI involves electric charge spreading on a doorknob. The authors state that abstract quantities such as electric charge seem to inhibit the reasoning students are successful with in less abstract contexts, quoting "performance on item V was significantly stronger than on item VI."

> "As shown in Table VI, performance on item V was significantly stronger than on item VI."
