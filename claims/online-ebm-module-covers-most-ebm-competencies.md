## Evidence

### Rohwer Anke 2013

Rohwer Anke, Young Taryn, van Schalkwyk Susan. (2013). Effective or just practical? An evaluation of an online postgraduate module on evidence-based medicine (EBM). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-77

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Document review comparing module learning outcomes, material, methods and assessments against five pre-specified EBM key competencies drawn from the CanMeds scholar domain. The review found coverage of "phrasing answerable questions; accessing the literature; critical appraisal" and application, though the audit competency was not addressed.

> "The document review indicated that the EBM module covered most EBM competencies: phrasing answerable questions; accessing the literature; critical appraisal of the literature and the application of research results in the clinical practice."
