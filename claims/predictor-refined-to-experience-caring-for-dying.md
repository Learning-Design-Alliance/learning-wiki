## Evidence

### Mascio R 2026

Mascio R, Lynch S, Phillips J, Best M. (2026). Predictors of nurses’ spiritual care competence: A replication study with Australian palliative care nurses. Palliative & Supportive Care. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1478951526102119

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' synthesis across the original general-nurse study and this palliative-nurse replication: training adequacy separated models only in the general sample, whereas palliative care experience was significantly associated with more competent models within the palliative sample. This is the authors' interpretive conclusion (type e), not a single new test.

> "While the replication results are largely concordant with those of the original study, the current study refines “training adequacy” as a predictor to “experience (whether on-the-job or training) in caring for the dying.”"
