### Michael C. Wittmann 2002

Michael C. Wittmann. (2002). Limitations in predicting student performance on standardized tests. Originally published 1998. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0209006](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0209006)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of Student B's written comments on the MPEX question sheet. Her comment "Work all of the problems (and do all of that and more)" was coded favorable but indicated unfavorable attitudes, matching her behavior of cranking through math without interpreting or relating it to the physics.

> "She often gave favorable responses for unfavorable reasons. For example, she (favorably) disagreed with the question, "All I need to do to understand most of the basic ideas in this course is just read the text, work most of the problems, and/or pay close attention in class,""
