Monte Carlo simulation of the two-level random model (Fig. 3), run with the printed parameters (Ne = 10000, Ninf = 0.53, Nsup = 0.47, pinf = 0.2, psup = 0.25). The article reports "buen acuerdo" with field data and a maximum discrepancy of approximately 2% at x = 7. No effect size is printed.
