### Mayer-Hirshfeld Ilana 2019

Mayer-Hirshfeld Ilana, Agarwal Gauri G., Jackson Jennifer S. (2019). SVI Self-Study Guide: A Study Tool for Independent Preparation for the AAMC Standardized Video Interview. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10805

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation (type e) in the Discussion, not a separately tested result: the larger improvement in feelings of preparedness than in knowledge performance is attributed to practice with the novel testing format. The article offers this as an inference from the two quiz outcomes.

> "This suggests that students benefited the most from the SVI Self-Study Guide's opportunity to practice answering questions in the SVI test format. This is consistent with the fact that the SVI has a novel testing format."
