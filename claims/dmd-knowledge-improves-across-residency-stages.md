## Evidence

### Al Jabri Zamzam 2026

Al Jabri Zamzam, Alsawaie Maryam, Saki Malehi Amal, Al Futaisi Amna, Al-Amrani Fatema. (2026). An Assessment of Paediatricians' Knowledge and Perspectives of Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy in Oman. Sultan Qaboos University Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.18295/2075-0528.2973

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 151 Omani paediatric residents and paediatricians (October 2024–February 2025) comparing knowledge scores by residency year via Kruskal-Wallis testing. "The total score significantly differed among different groups (P <0.001), with R1 residents scoring the lowest." Mean total scores rose from 52.4 (R1) to 66.9 (non-residents).

> "The total score significantly differed among different groups (P <0.001), with R1 residents scoring the lowest. The differences were specifically found between R1 and non-residents (P <0.001), R1 and R4 residents (P = 0.004) and R2 and non-residents (P = 0.01)."
