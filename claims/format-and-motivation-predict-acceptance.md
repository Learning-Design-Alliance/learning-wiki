## Evidence

### Zsifkovits Monika 2025

Zsifkovits Monika, Amplatz Lukas, Triebner Nina, Utz Janine, Kornhuber Johannes, Spitzer Philipp. (2025). Randomized controlled trial of asynchronous vs. synchronous online teaching formats: equal knowledge after training, greater acceptance and lower intrinsic motivation through asynchronous online learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07481-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise multiple linear regression across all participants, with age and educational level as covariates, identified the delivered "learning format (β = − 0.238, p <.001)" as a significant predictor of acceptance of the online teaching format.

> "Multiple linear regression analysis across all participants demonstrated that the following factors predicted the acceptance of the online teaching format: learning format (β = − 0.238, p <.001), perceived competence (β = 0.053, p =.012)"

### Zsifkovits Monika 2025 (2)

Zsifkovits Monika, Amplatz Lukas, Triebner Nina, Utz Janine, Kornhuber Johannes, Spitzer Philipp. (2025). Randomized controlled trial of asynchronous vs. synchronous online teaching formats: equal knowledge after training, greater acceptance and lower intrinsic motivation through asynchronous online learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07481-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The same regression model predicting acceptance of the online teaching format showed "intrinsic motivation (β = 0.274, p <.001)" was a significant predictor; previous experience with online learning was also significant.

> "and intrinsic motivation (β = 0.274, p <.001), (F (15, 1028) = 153.626; p <.001, p_adj < 0.013)"
