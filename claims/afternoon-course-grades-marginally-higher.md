## Evidence

### Yeo Sing Chen 2023

Yeo Sing Chen, Lai Clin K. Y., Tan Jacinda, Lim Samantha, Chandramoghan Yuvan, Tan Teck Kiang, Gooley Joshua J. (2023). Early morning university classes are associated with impaired sleep and academic performance. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01531-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Analysis of course grades in 33,818 students with an equivalent course credit workload, categorising courses as morning-only, afternoon-only or mixed morning/afternoon. The printed difference for afternoon-only courses was "0.016, 95% CI = 0.006 to 0.026" grade points; no standardised effect size was reported.

> "Course grades were marginally higher for afternoon-only courses, but not mixed morning/afternoon courses, compared with morning-only courses (difference in grade point relative to morning-only courses, Tukey's test: afternoon-only courses, 0.016, 95% CI = 0.006 to 0.026, P = 0.0042"
