### Francesco Bartolucci 2009

Francesco Bartolucci, Fulvia Pennoni and Giorgio Vittadini. (2009). Assessment of school performance through a multilevel latent Markov Rasch model. https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.4961 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0909.4961](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0909.4961)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model-selection analysis over fitted models with k1 from 1 to 5 and k2 from 1 to 7 (Table 4) found the minimum BIC at k1 = 4 and k2 = 6, so "four diﬀerent types of school classes" and six student ability subgroups were identified.

> "We observe that the lowest value of the BIC index corresponds to four typologies of clus ters ( k1 = 4) and six 15 math ability levels ( k1 = 6). We then identify six subgroups of students with diﬀerent levels of ability and four diﬀerent types of school classes."
