# Adults rated the LLM-powered iCub storytelling system as usable, with a total SUS score of 76.70 indicating good product usability

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a usability study with 25 adult participants, the iCub narrative system reached a total SUS score of 76.70, which the authors interpret as overall good product usability. [→ Maria Lombardi 2025](#maria-lombardi-2025)

## Evidence

### Maria Lombardi 2025

Maria Lombardi, Carmela Calabrese, Davide Ghiglino, Caterina Foglino, Davide De Tommaso, Giulia Da Lisca, Lorenzo Natale, Agnieszka Wykowska. (2025). Would you let a humanoid play storytelling with your child? A usability study on LLM-powered narrative Human-Robot Interaction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02505

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-interaction usability assessment in the validation experiment: 26 adults were recruited (one excluded), each inventing three stories with iCub, then completing the SUS. The article reports the total score of 76.70 and cites an empirical evaluation of the questionnaire for the interpretation.

> "The total score reached 76.70, which indicates an overall good product usability according to an empirical evaluation of the questionnaire [34]."
