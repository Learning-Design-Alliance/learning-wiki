## Evidence

### Yanagita Yasutaka 2025

Yanagita Yasutaka, Shikino Kiyoshi, Yokokawa Daiki, Ishizuka Kosuke, Tsukamoto Tomoko, Li Yu, Uehara Takanori, Ikusaka Masatomi. (2025). Appropriate semantic qualifiers increase diagnostic accuracy when using a clinical decision support system: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07294-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial primary outcome: 220 diagnoses from 22 CDSS-group students across ten case vignettes were classified by SQ appropriateness; 93 of 133 appropriate-SQ diagnoses (69.9%) were correct versus 48 of 87 inappropriate-SQ diagnoses (55.2%), a "significantly higher" difference.

> "Among CDSS users, diagnostic accuracy was significantly higher for appropriate SQs compared to that for inappropriate SQs (χ2(1) = 4.97, p = 0.026; Table 3)."
