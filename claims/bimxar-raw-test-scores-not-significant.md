## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ziad Ashour 2022

Ziad Ashour, Zohreh Shaghaghian, Wei Yan. (2022). BIMxAR: BIM-Empowered Augmented Reality for Learning Architectural Representations. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.03207](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.03207)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pretest–posttest score analysis of the pilot study at the 0.05 significance level: the Sign test "revealed no significant differences between the pretest and posttest sessions" for both SBST and ART scores.

> "The results from the Sign test for the SBST and the ART scores with a significance level of 0.05 revealed no significant differences between the pretest and posttest sessions (p = 0.3281 and p = 0.3750 for SBST and ART respectively)."

### Ziad Ashour 2022 (2)

Ziad Ashour, Zohreh Shaghaghian, Wei Yan. (2022). BIMxAR: BIM-Empowered Augmented Reality for Learning Architectural Representations. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.03207](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.03207)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive score results from the pilot study: "The mean scores in the SBST (mean Pretest = 82.92, mean Posttest = 86.67) improved by 4.52%" and the ART mean scores improved by 6.32%.

> "The mean scores in the SBST (mean Pretest = 82.92, mean Posttest = 86.67) improved by 4.52%. The mean scores in the ART (mean Pretest = 84.82, mean Posttest = 90.18) improved by 6.32%."
