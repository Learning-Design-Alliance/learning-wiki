## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zeynep Isvan and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Zeynep Isvan and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Student Understanding of Coulomb's Law and Gauss's Law. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06369](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06369)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Cumulative multiple-choice test administered to different populations: tutorial group N=278 averaged 59%; without-tutorial classes N=135 averaged 38%; Honors N=182 averaged 42%; upper-level pre-test 44%, post-test 49%; first-year grads N=33 averaged 75%. T-tests compared the tutorial group with each group.

> "Table 3 shows that students who worked through the tutorials signiﬁcantly outperformed both Honors students and those in upper-level undergraduate courses, but not physics graduate students."
