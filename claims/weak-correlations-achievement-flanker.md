## Evidence

### Beckmann 2021

Beckmann, J.; Lang, C.; du Randt, R.; Gresse, A.; Long, K.Z.; Ludyga, S.; Müller, I.; Nqweniso, S.; Pühse, U.; Utzinger, J.; Walter, C.; Gerber, M. (2021). Prevalence of Stunting and Relationship between Stunting and Associated Risk Factors with Academic Achievement and Cognitive Function: A Cross-Sectional Study with South African Primary School Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 18(8), 4218. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18084218

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis (Table 2) of school grades and Flanker performance in the cross-sectional sample (boys n = 664, girls n = 613). The article reports "slightly stronger in girls (r = 0.19 to 0.30) than in boys (r = 0.12 to 0.19)"; among boys, correlations with incongruent stimuli were not significant.

> "With regard to accuracy, the bivariate correlations between academic achievement and the Flanker task performance indicators were slightly stronger in girls (r = 0.19 to 0.30) than in boys (r = 0.12 to 0.19)."
