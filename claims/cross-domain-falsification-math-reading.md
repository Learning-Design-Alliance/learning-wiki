## Evidence

### Bailey 2018

Bailey, D. H., Duncan, G. J., Watts, T., Clements, D. H., & Sarama, J. (2018). Risky Business: Correlation and Causation in Longitudinal Studies of Skill Development. American Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1037/amp0000146

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of the 1998–99 ECLS-K cohort (Figure 1), comparing within-domain and cross-domain longitudinal correlations from fall of kindergarten. The article reports math-to-reading correlations "virtually indistinguishable from math-to-math correlations beyond first grade", and notes Duncan et al. (2007) meta-analytic estimates of .26 (math-to-reading) versus .10 (reading-to-math).

> "Figure 1 shows this is the case for math and anti-social behavior but decidedly not for math-to-reading correlations, which are virtually indistinguishable from math-to-math correlations beyond first grade."
