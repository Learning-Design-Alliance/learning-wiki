Evidence¶

Popenici 2017¶

Popenici, S. A. D., & Kerr, S. (2017). Exploring the impact of artificial intelligence on teaching and learning in higher education. Research and Practice in Technology Enhanced Learning, 12:22. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41039-017-0062-8

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

A documented deployment cited in the paper's introduction: IBM's supercomputer Watson "provides student advice for Deakin University in Australia at any time of day throughout 365 days of the year". The authors argue such bespoke around-the-clock feedback reduces the need to employ the same number of administrative staff.
