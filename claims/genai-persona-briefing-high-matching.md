## Evidence

### Felix Buendia-Garcia 2025

Felix Buendia-Garcia. (2025). Using Generative AI Tools in Collaborative UX design Courses. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00518-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Course-based evaluation in Creative Technology courses (2023): student teams proposed personas, then a GenAI assistant assessed matching between persona attributes and briefing items. The article reports "average global values of 85% in the mobile phone campaign" and 80% for the drink campaign.

> "the matching degree could be considered as high as they displayed average global values of 85% in the mobile phone campaign (85–90% P1, 85% P2, 80–85% P3) and 80% referred to the drink campaign (90% P1, 75% P2, 95% P3)"
