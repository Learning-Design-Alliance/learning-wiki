## Evidence

### Junga A 2026

Junga A, Kockwelp P, Dabel J, Scherzer SH, Risse B, Holling M, Marschall B, Friederichs H. (2026). Olfactory Cues and Patient Safety Behavior in Virtual Reality Medical Simulation: Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/93148

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary outcome analysis in the same 95-participant controlled trial. The intervention effect on glove use was "not statistically significant (β=0.37, SE 0.44; P=.40), corresponding to an OR of 1.44 (95% CI 0.62-3.45)"; the 8.2 percentage-point difference could not be distinguished from chance.

> "Gloves were used by 13 of 43 participants (30.2%) in the control group, compared with 20 of 52 participants (38.5%) in the intervention group. The logistic regression model predicting glove use (formula: glove use ~ intervention) showed weak explanatory power (Tjur R2=0.01)."
