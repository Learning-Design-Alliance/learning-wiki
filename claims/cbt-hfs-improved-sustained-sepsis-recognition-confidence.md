## Evidence

### Marsack 2023

Marsack, W. J., De Gagne, J. C., Reid, J., Pakieser‐Reed, K., Francisco, M. A., & Kang, H. S. (2023). Evaluation of computer‐based training and high‐fidelity simulation to improve early recognition of sepsis on the adult general ward. Nursing Open, 10, 4880–4887. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1718

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA in the same one-group QI study (n = 36/29/21 across timepoints) on confidence measured with a five-item 4-point scale. The article reports a large effect size, ηp² = 0.25, with post hoc differences at both posttest and 90-day follow-up.

> "There was a statistically significant difference in the mean scores of confidence in early recognition of sepsis between the three timepoints, F (2,83) = 13.67, p < 0.001, η p 2 = 0.25. Post hoc testing using Tukey's test revealed statistically significant differences between Time 1 and Time 2 (p < 0.001) and Time 1 and Time 3 (p = 0.005)"
