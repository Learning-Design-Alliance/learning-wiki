## Evidence

### Dimitrios Gousopoulos 2023

Dimitrios Gousopoulos. (2023). Investigating students' scientific reasoning through heuristic and analytical thought processes. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.07158](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.07158)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interview responses to the velocity-at-impact question in the 45-student study. The article reports "10/45 - 22.2%" replaced the question-goal with the heuristic question, since speed was more activated in their minds.

> "many students (10/45 - 22.2%) replaced the question with the question: “ What is the body speed?” without including the direction of the velocity in their answer."
