Attitude survey using the concept and reality link clusters of the MPEX administered to students in the 1998 algebra-based course before and after the semester. Favorable responses "remained exactly the same" at 68% and 67%, with unfavorable responses increasing slightly; the authors conclude there was very little change in class attitudes.
