Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2026.23.6

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 6 effect sizes for progression outcomes, using parental education (first-generation status) and parental income; effect sizes for parental occupation were not available. The pooled effect size was OR = 0.56 (95% CI 0.53–0.59).

> "The pooled composite effect (k=6) revealed that students from economically and educationally disadvantaged households had a 44% lower chance of progressing through medical school (OR, 0.56; 95% CI, 0.53–0.59)."

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Authors' supplementary analysis converting ORs to Cohen's d and calculating progression-to-selection effect ratios for parental income, first-generation status, and the composite household disadvantage measure; no effect size value is printed in the text.

> "The calculated ratios indicated that the effects of these predictors were meaningfully larger for progression than for selection (Supplement 8)."
