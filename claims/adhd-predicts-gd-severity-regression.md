## Evidence

### Hawi Nazir 2024

Hawi Nazir, Samaha Maya. (2024). Relationships of gaming disorder, ADHD, and academic performance in university students: A mediation analysis. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0300680

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regression in the results section of the cross-sectional survey. The quote reports the overall prediction; the same section reports gender-specific coefficients (females β = .49, 23.7% variance; males β = .21, 4.2% variance), both significant. The article notes for every unit increase in ADHD score, 8.9 units increase in IGD score is predicted.

> "The ADHD scale score was a significant predictor of IGD, β = .27, t(346) = 4.93, p < .001, accounting for 38% of the variance in IGD scores, R2 = .38, F(1,346) = 24.25, p < .001."
