## Evidence

### Khodadadeh Alireza 2025

Khodadadeh Alireza, Rivaz Mozhgan, Torabizadeh Camellia. (2025). The effects of flipped classroom and jigsaw teaching strategies on learning, retention of course content, and satisfaction among nursing students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07662-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same quasi-experimental study re-administered the 20-item MCQ unexpectedly six weeks after the final session. Retention means were 11.03 (FC), 10.76 (jigsaw), 10.03 (LBT), with "no significant difference between the groups regarding retention of course content" (eta squared 0.02, observed power 0.2).

> "Table 2 shows that although the mean scores of FC group were higher than Jigsaw and LBT, there was no significant difference between the groups regarding retention of course content. (p = 0.33)."
