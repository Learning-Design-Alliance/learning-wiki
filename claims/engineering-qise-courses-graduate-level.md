Analysis of course levels (Fig. 1) found engineering quantum courses mostly at the graduate level, in contrast to physics where most quantum courses are undergraduate. The sample includes "38 undergraduate engineering QISE courses" representing "a fraction (27%) of QISE courses in engineering."

> "Although there are 38 undergraduate engineering QISE courses in our sample with titles such as “Quantum Computing,” “Introduction to Quantum Technologies,” and “Quantum Cryptography,” they represent a fraction (27%) of QISE courses in engineering."
