Raghuveer Parthasarathy. (2014). “The Physics of Life,” an undergraduate general education biophysics course. [doi:10.1088/0031-9120/50/3/358](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9120/50/3/358)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

First-week diagnostic math quiz and class discussion reported in the Challenges and Outcomes section. The article attributes the gap to memorization of symbol-manipulation rules versus understanding what exponents mean, noting the latter "involves an understanding of what exponents mean."

> "However, a much smaller fraction ( ∼ 25%), when asked a question like “if y is proportional to x3, andx doubles, what happens to y?,” will answer correctly."
