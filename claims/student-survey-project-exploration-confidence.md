## Evidence

### Marcos D. Caballero and Steven J. Pollock 2013

Marcos D. Caballero and Steven J. Pollock. (2013). A Model for Incorporating Computation Without Changing the Course: An example from middle-division classical mechanics. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/1.4837437](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4837437)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-semester survey of the spring 2012 CM 1 offering, completed by 94% of students, reported in the students' reflections section. The authors report "most students ( > 80%)" said the project let them explore new physics; percentages are descriptive with no test statistic.

> "Of those who completed the survey, most students ( > 80%) said the computational project gave them the op- portunity to explore new physics and to use new com- putational techniques."

### Marcos D. Caballero and Steven J. Pollock 2013 (2)

Marcos D. Caballero and Steven J. Pollock. (2013). A Model for Incorporating Computation Without Changing the Course: An example from middle-division classical mechanics. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/1.4837437](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4837437)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same end-of-semester survey reported in the students' reflections section. The authors report "∼90% of our students gained conﬁdence with Math- ematica" and link confidence to performance, citing computational-anxiety literature; no effect size is printed.

> "More- over, ∼90% of our students gained conﬁdence with Math- ematica, which is important because a lack of conﬁdence hinders student performance on computational activi- ties."
