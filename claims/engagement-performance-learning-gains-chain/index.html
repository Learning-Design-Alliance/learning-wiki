Evidence¶

Chevalère 2023¶

Chevalère, J., Yun, H. S., Henke, A., Pinkwart, N., Hafner, V., & Lazarides, R. (2023). A sequence of learning processes in an intelligent tutoring system from topic-related appraisals to learning gains. Learning and Instruction, 87, 101799. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101799

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Average path model (N = 140): engagement predicted final concept-map performance, β = .26, which predicted learning gains (residualized posttest net of pretest); a paired t test showed "learning gains were significant, t(139) = 6.29, p < .001".

"more engagement in Betty’s Brain was associated with a significantly better final performance score on the concept map, β = .26, SE = .08, t = 3.43, p = .001 [.11; .40], which in turn yielded higher learning gains, β = .18, SE = .06, t = 3.17, p = .002 [.070; .29]"
