# Schooling during the COVID-19 pandemic produced no significant negative effects on first-grade students' reading or math achievement compared with regular schooling

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` First-grade students schooled during the pandemic showed no significant negative effects on reading achievement (b = 0.03, p = 0.718) or math achievement (b = −0.01, p = 0.878) at end of Grade 1 compared with pre-pandemic peers, controlling for baseline differences. ### Golle 2026

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`
