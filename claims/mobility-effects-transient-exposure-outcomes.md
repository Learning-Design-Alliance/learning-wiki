`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Theme three of the review groups children into trajectories of exposure; "transient exposure was revealed to have implications for educational and cognitive outcomes" in 22 studies (29%), compared against stable reference trajectories.

### Molly Grant 2023 (2)

Molly Grant, Kane Meissel, Daniel Exeter. (2023). Promoting Temporal Investigations of Development in Context: a Systematic Review of Longitudinal Research Linking Childhood Circumstances and Learning-related Outcomes. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09734-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

In Letourneau et al.'s study as reviewed, children of mothers depressed before age one did not differ in language from never-depressed mothers, while persistent or later-onset depression raised chances of lower language; "early improvements to maternal health were beneficial to children."

> "early improvements to maternal health were beneficial to children, whereas later deterioration posed a threat to children’s cognitive development"
