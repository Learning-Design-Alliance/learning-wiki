### E. Ergonul and S. Buyukcoban 2026

E. Ergonul and S. Buyukcoban. (2026). Fostering interactive medical education: integrating Web 2.0 tools through a universal design for learning framework. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2639193

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive thematic analysis with an interpretive phenomenological lens of 15 individual interviews (approximately 30 minutes each) and two focus groups of five participants, purposively sampled from the trained faculty. The analysis found the experience "intertwined with power dynamics, emotional labour, and elements of institutional resistance".

> "Thematic analysis revealed that the use of Web 2.0 tools was not merely a technical adaptation process, but rather a complex experience intertwined with power dynamics, emotional labour, and elements of institutional resistance."
