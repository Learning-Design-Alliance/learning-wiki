## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The number of times students self-declared they had practiced each skill correlated with their retest performance, with skill-level Spearman's rho ranging from 0.09 (blood glucose monitoring) to 0.83 (arterial blood gas). [→ Offiah G 2019](#offiah-g-2019)

## Evidence

### Offiah G 2019

Offiah G, Ekpotu LP, Murphy S, Kane D, Gordon A, O’Sullivan M, Sharifuddin SF, Hill ADK, Condron CM. (2019). Evaluation of medical student retention of clinical skills following simulation training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1663-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire-based self-report of practice frequency (n = 51) correlated with retest performance using Spearman's correlation; Table 3 prints rho values including "0.83" for arterial blood gas, 0.73 for venepuncture, and 0.09 for blood glucose monitoring. The correlation is self-report based and no effect size summary is given for the overall relationship.

> "The results demonstrate a correlation between the numbers of times that students self-declared that they had practiced the skills and a high competency rate as depicted in Table 3."
