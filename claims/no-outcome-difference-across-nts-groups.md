Comparison of clinical outcomes between low NTS and high NTS groups in the 85-case single-center cohort; the study was designed and powered for process metrics, not survival end points. Equivalence was not tested.

> "there were no statistically significant differences in return of spontaneous circulation (67.7% versus 57.4%, P=0.54) or survival to discharge (12.9% versus 18.5%, P=0.69) between the 2 groups"
