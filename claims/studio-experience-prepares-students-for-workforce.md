# Students believed interdisciplinary studio experiences would ease their transition into the workforce

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students reported that skills gained in the studio — working with different kinds of people, working autonomously, and learning from other disciplines — would help them "break the learning curve" entering the workforce. [→ Warr 2020](#warr-2020)

## Evidence

### Warr 2020

Warr, M. & West, R. E. (2020). Bridging Academic Disciplines with Interdisciplinary Project-based Learning: Challenges and Opportunities. The Interdisciplinary Journal of Problem-based Learning. https://doi.org/10.14434/ijpbl.v14i1.28590

`q2 · i? · the article prints no effect size for this finding` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview findings: students believed the practical, problem-based nature of CID projects prepared them for careers, with one student explaining "You are making a product that is more like what you're going to be doing in the career you're in" and another predicting the experience would "break the learning curve."

> "Several students said that their experiences in the CID studio will help them transition into the workforce."
