# Human-shadow prompting generates broad psychometric variance while AI-assistant responses are uniform

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Esposito 2026

Esposito, N., Tricarico, A., Porzio, L., Aghazadeh Ardebili, A., & Stella, M. (2026). Math Education Digital Shadows for Investigating Learning with GenAI: Mathematics Performance, Anxiety, and Confidence in LLMs. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27618 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.27618](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2604.27618)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Task 2 analysis comparing summed psychometric score distributions between human-shadow and AI-assistant runs across 14 LLMs. The article reports that "the variance among AI-assistant responses is negligible" and that several models, including Anita 24B (Uncensored) and Phi-4 (Reasoning+), provided identical responses and scores under AI-assistant instructions.

> "The human-shadow distributions display considerable variance across all three scales, indicating that human-shadow conditioning successfully generated a broad spectrum of simulated educational cognition and varied psychological attitudes towards mathematics."
