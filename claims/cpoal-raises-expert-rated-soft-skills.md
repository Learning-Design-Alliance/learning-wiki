### Wang Tianlin 2025

Enhancing professional adaptability and clinical soft skills of new clinical pharmacists through a pre-training model in China: a randomized controlled trial. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Expert-panel evaluation after one year of standardized training: three general medicine and three clinical pharmacy experts observed pharmacists completing pharmacy rounds, patient medication education, and case presentations. Table 4 reports adaptability difference 3.89 (p = 0.03), comprehensive analytical skills 3.44 (p = 0.04), clinical reasoning 3.22 (p = 0.02). Rater agreement was high: "the Kendall Concordance Coefficient, which yielded a value of 0.887".

> "The CPOAL group exhibited higher total soft skills scores compared to the control group (187.78 ± 8.57 vs. 171.33 ± 14.10, p = 0.009)"
