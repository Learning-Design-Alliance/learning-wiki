### Agbontaen Kaladerhan O. 2025

Agbontaen Kaladerhan O., Cold Kristoffer M., Woods David, Grover Vimal, Aboumarie Hatem Soliman, Kaul Sundeep, Konge Lars, Singh Suveer. (2025). Artificial Intelligence-Guided Bronchoscopy is Superior to Human Expert Instruction for the Performance of Critical-Care Physicians: A Randomized Controlled Trial. `q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In the RCT's primary between-group comparison, segment revisits were "–7 revisits, 0.019" favoring the AI group, with a moderate effect size of 0.37 per Cohen's classification. However, the outlier-removed secondary analysis found no significant difference in SR (–4.5, p = 0.245).

> "At final test bronchoscopy, the AIG were (median difference, p): more efficient, MIT (–7.9 s, 0.027) and SR (–7 revisits, 0.019), and significantly faster than ETG (–77 s, 0.022)"
