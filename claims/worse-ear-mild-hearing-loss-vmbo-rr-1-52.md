## Evidence

### Reijers Stefanie N.H. 2025

Reijers Stefanie N.H., Vroegop Jantien L., Jansen Pauline W., Kremer Bernd, van der Schroeff Marc P. (2025). The Impact of Slight to Mild Hearing Loss on Academic Performance and Behavior of 9–15‐Year‐Olds. The Laryngoscope. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.31908

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional analysis of 4688 children aged 13–15 in the Generation R cohort compared hearing-level prevalence across three school levels. The worse-hearing-ear relative risk for VMBO versus VWO was "RR = 1.52 (95% CI [1.14, 2.02])"; the corresponding better-hearing-ear risk (RR = 1.64, CI [0.95, 2.81]) was not significant.

> "For the worse‐hearing ear, the relative risk (RR) was VMBO: RR = 1.52 (95% CI [1.14, 2.02]) and HAVO: RR = 0.93 (95% CI [0.65, 1.32])."
