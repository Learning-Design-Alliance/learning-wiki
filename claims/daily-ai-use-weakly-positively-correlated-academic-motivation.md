### Zhang Y 2026

Zhang Y, Shi Y and Lu J (2026). The relationship between AI anxiety and academic motivation among university students: the mediating role of emotion regulation and the moderating role of gender. Front. Psychol. 17:1918525. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1918525

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate Pearson correlations (Table 1) in the cross-sectional survey of 1,484 students. AI contact duration showed a 'weak positive correlation with academic motivation (r = 0.053, p < 0.05)'; daily AI use was included as a control variable in the regression analyses.

> "AI contact duration showed a weak positive correlation with academic motivation (r = 0.053, p < 0.05), while gender was negatively correlated with academic motivation (r = −0.094, p < 0.001)."
