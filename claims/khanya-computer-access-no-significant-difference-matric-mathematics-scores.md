# Schools with long-term Khanya computer access showed no significant difference in 2007 Matric Mathematics mean student scores compared to schools without long-term access

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A Mann-Whitney U test comparing 14 experimental schools (computers since 2001) with 17 control schools (computers from 2006) found no significant difference in mean student scores (U = 91, z = -1.11, p = 0.266). [→ Smith and Hardman 2014](#smith-and-hardman-2014)
`q2 i?` After excluding four outlier schools, an independent samples t-test on redefined groups also showed no significant difference (t(25) = 1.938, p = 0.064), with eta squared = 0.131 indicating a small effect size. [→ Smith and Hardman 2014 (2)](#smith-and-hardman-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Smith and Hardman 2014

Garth Spencer Smith and Joanne Hardman. (2014). The impact of computer and mathematics software usage on performance of school leavers in the Western Cape Province of South Africa: A comparative analysis. International Journal of Education and Development using Information and Communication Technology, Vol. 10, Issue 1, pp. 22-40. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu/viewarticle.php?id=1678

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A non-parametric Mann-Whitney U test was performed because Kolmogorov-Smirnov indicated the data was not normally distributed. The test "revealed no significant difference in the mean rank" between groups. The unit of analysis was individual schools, with 31 high schools in the sample.

> "The test revealed no significant difference in the mean rank of the experimental group (mean rank = 18; n = 14) and the control group (mean rank = 14.35; n = 17), U = 91; z = -1.11; p = 0.266"

### Smith and Hardman 2014 (2)

Garth Spencer Smith and Joanne Hardman. (2014). The impact of computer and mathematics software usage on performance of school leavers in the Western Cape Province of South Africa: A comparative analysis. International Journal of Education and Development using Information and Communication Technology, Vol. 10, Issue 1, pp. 22-40. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu/viewarticle.php?id=1678

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

After excluding four outlier schools (one selective intake, three ex-Model C), the redefined groups showed normally distributed data with equal variances (Levene's p = 0.4129). The t-test found "no significant difference" between groups. Eta squared was calculated as 0.131, a small effect size.

> "This revealed that there is no significant difference between the results for the control schools (mean = 0.6106, std deviation = 0.365) and the experimental schools (mean = 0.9286; std deviation = 0.484); t(25) = 1.938, p = 0.064 (2-tailed)"
