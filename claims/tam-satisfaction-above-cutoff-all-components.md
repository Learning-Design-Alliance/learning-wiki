## Evidence

### Karimian Z 2025

Karimian Z, Momeni M, Zarifsanaiey N. (2025). Enhancing radiographic interpretation: effects of gamification on medical students' knowledge, skills, and satisfaction — a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07523-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported 21-item TAM questionnaire (5-point Likert scale) administered only to the gamification group after the course; a minimum score of 3 out of 5 was the preset acceptability cutoff, and all components exceeded it.

> "The results showed that the average scores for all components surpassed 3, with the highest averages found in enjoyment, desirability (willingness to repeat), ease of use, and positive attitude."
