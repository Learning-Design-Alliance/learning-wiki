## Evidence

### Paul A. Kirschner 2004

Paul A. Kirschner, Jan Willem Strijbos, Karel Kreijns, Pieter Beers. (2004). Designing electronic collaborative learning environments. Educational Technology Research and Development, 52(3), pp.47-66. https://hal.science/hal-00703892

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The article's descriptive account of how electronic collaborative learning environments are designed and used, stating that designers follow "their own constructivist interpretations" and that student use is often perfunctory. No empirical data or effect sizes are printed for this account.

> "Designers design them according to their own constructivist interpretations of what collaborative learning is and what it should achieve. Educators employ them with different educational approaches and in diverse situations to achieve different ends. Students use them, sometimes very enthusiastically, but often in a perfunctory way."
