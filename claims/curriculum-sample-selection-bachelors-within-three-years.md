## Evidence

### de Visser M 2016

de Visser M, Fluit C, Fransen J, Latijnhouwers M, Cohen-Schotanus J, Laan R. (2016). The effect of curriculum sample selection for medical school. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-016-9681-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis restricted to students with ≥42 first-year credits (n = 570) in the 2010–2011 cohorts; the article reports the unadjusted contrast as significant and the adjusted contrast as non-significant.

> "Compared to selected students, a lower percentage of lottery-admitted students obtained their Bachelor's degree within 3 years of study (79 vs 66, p = .01). Adjusted for pu-GPA, no difference remained (p = .22)."
