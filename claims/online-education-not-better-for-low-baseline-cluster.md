## Evidence

### Goudarzi Houman 2026

Goudarzi Houman, Onozawa Masahiro, Ito Yoichi M., Takahashi Makoto. (2026). Clustering of students in pre-clinical medical education and identification of academic performance profiles: does online education work for all medical students? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08737-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive cluster-profile analysis (Table 3, Cluster 2, n = 54); approximately 80% had online/combined education, with the highest online percentage across clusters (52%). The authors interpret this as a subgroup "for whom online or combined education may not result in better learning outcomes." No effect size printed.

> "Students in this cluster had the lowest university entrance exam English scores, baseline general English skills (writing, reading, speaking, and listening), and medical terminology, and showed the second lowest academic performance after cluster 1 (final exam score; mean ± SE; 76.8 ± 1.2)."
