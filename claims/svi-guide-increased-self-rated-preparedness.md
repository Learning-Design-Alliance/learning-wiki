# Using the SVI Self-Study Guide significantly increased students' self-rated preparedness to take the SVI, especially for delivering a complete answer within 3 minutes, providing an organized response, and overall confidence

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Self-rated perceived preparedness to take the SVI increased significantly on the overall average (p = .014) and on three of five components, with the largest gains in ability to deliver a complete answer within 3 minutes (p < .001), organized response (p = .002), and overall confidence (p = .004). [→ Mayer-Hirshfeld Ilana 2019](#mayer-hirshfeld-ilana-2019)

## Evidence

### Mayer-Hirshfeld Ilana 2019

Mayer-Hirshfeld Ilana, Agarwal Gauri G., Jackson Jennifer S. (2019). SVI Self-Study Guide: A Study Tool for Independent Preparation for the AAMC Standardized Video Interview. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10805

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Small pre/postquiz evaluation: 10 learners took the prequiz and 8 the postquiz; self-rated preparedness (1-10 scale) was compared with two-tailed t tests. The overall average rose from 5.380 to 7.300 (p = .014), and "Ability to deliver a complete answer within 3 minutes" rose from 4.500 to 7.250 (p < .001).

> "There was a significant difference (p < .05) in learners' perceived preparedness to take the SVI in three out of five questions assessing perceived preparedness, as well as on the overall average (Table 1)."
