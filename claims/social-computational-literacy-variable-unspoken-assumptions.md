## Evidence

### Luke Nearhood and Patti Hamerski 2025

Luke Nearhood and Patti Hamerski. (2025). Student experiences in a computational physics lab through the lens of Physics Computational Literacy. 2025 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.Nearhood

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the five-student interview study, Alice describes how her anxiety disorder shapes her social engagement: she "sit[s] at a table alone" and talks with TAs rather than collaborating with peers, while believing collaboration works for most people. Carl, by contrast, found pairing with someone of similar coding knowledge helpful. The authors read this as social computational literacy development being underwritten by unspoken assumptions.

> "I'm good at coding. I know what I'm doing. I know how to ask for help. So I sit at a table alone. I talk to the TAs, we chit chat, I ask them questions. But I don't necessarily collaborate with my peers."
