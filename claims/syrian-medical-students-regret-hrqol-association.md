### Kheder Maria 2026

Kheder Maria, Nasra Ahmad Bishr, Alghoutani Sarah, Al-Hammash Hiba, Saleh Abdullah, Assaad Alaa, Almansour Shahd, Kreimesh Nussaiba, Kanaa Leen, Latifeh Youssef, Alsaid Bayan. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted multiple linear regression (n=1702) with R5M as outcome and demographic, academic, lifestyle variables plus SF-12 scores as predictors, using HC3 robust inference. The model found "higher MCS-12 scores were associated with lower R5M" and likewise higher PCS-12; only unstandardized coefficients were printed.

> "higher MCS-12 scores were associated with lower R5M (B=−0.023 per point, 95% CI −0.028 to −0.017, p<0.001), as were higher PCS-12 scores (B=−0.012, 95% CI −0.018 to −0.006, p<0.001)"
