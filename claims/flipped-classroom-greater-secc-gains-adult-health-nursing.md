## Evidence

### Fan Jun-Yu 2020

Fan Jun-Yu, Tseng Ying-Jung, Chao Li-Fen, Chen Shiah-Lian, Jane Sui-Whi. (2020). Learning outcomes of a flipped classroom teaching approach in an adult-health nursing course: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02240-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GEE analysis of pre- and post-intervention SECC scores in a quasi-experimental study of 485 RN-to-BSN students (EG n=287, CG n=198), with group, time, and group-by-time interaction terms. The "interaction effect of group by time" for overall SECC was B = 0.23, p < .001, but no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The results showed that, in most subscales, participants in the EG demonstrated greater improvements than those in the CG (p < 0.01; Fig. 2); however, group differences regarding changes in basic biomedical science, life-long learning, and estimated competence after graduation were not significant."
