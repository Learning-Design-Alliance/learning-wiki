Feng 2025¶

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., Huang, C.-W., Luttgen, K., Grady, J., Yang, S., Lavine, A., Collins, K., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments: Evaluation final report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677852

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Descriptive participant reporting in the Settings and Participants section of the QED study. The final treatment sample included "middle school math teachers from 36 public schools across 24 U.S. states", with 18 rural schools and 23 Title I-eligible schools; 55% of students were eligible for free or reduced-price lunch.

"The treatment group involved 59 teachers and their 2,855 students in 6th through 8th grades"

Related Claims¶

  • vPLC-augmented ASSISTments did not significantly improve overall middle school math achievement relative to a virtual comparison group — related
  • A virtual comparison group matched on pretest, locale, FRL, and test dates achieved baseline equivalence with the treatment group (effect size = -0.03) — related
  • Low-performing students (below the 50th pretest percentile) showed significant improvement with vPLC-augmented ASSISTments — related
  • Rural students overall did not show a significant effect, but low-performing rural students did — related
  • Across the full sample, students receiving the ASSISTments intervention performed comparably to their virtual comparison peers on end-of-year MAP Growth — related
  • Implementation data show teachers shifted independent practice toward in-class ASSISTments assignments, a change the study links to more equitable access for rural students with limited home connectivity — related
