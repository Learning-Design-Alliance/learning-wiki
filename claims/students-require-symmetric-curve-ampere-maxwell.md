### Álvaro Suárez 2025

Álvaro Suárez, Arturo C Marti, Kristina Zuza, Jenaro Guisasola. (2025). Understanding student challenges with circulation and Ampère-Maxwell law. arXiv. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ada1b1](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ada1b1)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenographic analysis of written responses from 65 students to a metacognitive question, plus interviews with 12 students; Table 1 assigns 20.0% to Category C, whose referential aspect is that "the selected curve should be symmetrical in order to be able to calculate the field."

> "We found that one in five students disagree with the choice of curve for calculating the circulation. Many of them believe that Amp` ere-Maxwell's law can only be used to calculate magnetic fields if the curve used is symmetrical"
