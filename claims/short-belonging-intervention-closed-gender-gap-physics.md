## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2021

Chandralekha Singh. (2021). Using social psychological interventions to make physics classes equitable and inclusive. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2112.10732](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2112.10732)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The author reports on a field intervention in a required introductory calculus-based physics course for first-semester physical science and engineering majors, in which randomly selected recitations received the activity. The article reports that it "closed the gender gap in performance" relative to comparison recitation sections; no effect size is printed.

> "What is heartening is that this short intervention closed the gender gap in performance compared to the comparison group involving the recitation sections in which this short intervention did not take place [28]."
