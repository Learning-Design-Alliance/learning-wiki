`q2 i?` Raters report that students tailor learning towards passing the OSCE rather than real-life practice, performing by rote and engaging superficially with patients. [→ Hyde Sarah 2022](#hyde-sarah-2022)

## Evidence

### Hyde Sarah 2022

Hyde Sarah, Fessey Christine, Boursicot Katharine, MacKenzie Rhoda, McGrath Deirdre. (2022). OSCE rater cognition – an international multi-centre qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-03077-w

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Within the 'OSCEs are inauthentic' theme of the 31-interview qualitative study, raters described students learning how the OSCE worked and how marks were gained, ticking boxes rather than responding to the patient, an unforeseen and unwanted consequence of the format.

> "Students are learning to pass examinations, rather than learning how to practice medicine."
