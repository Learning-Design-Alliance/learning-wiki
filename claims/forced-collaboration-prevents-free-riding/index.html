Evidence¶

Elina Riivari 2021¶

Elina Riivari, Marke Kivijärvi, Anna-Maija Lämsä. (2021). Learning teamwork through a computer game: for the sake of performance or collaborative learning? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10009-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Theory-driven qualitative content analysis of student feedback and team reports in the HRM course case; students positively appraised "forced collaboration", reporting that the plot could only be discovered through active communication and that free riders were not allowed.

"The game thus prevented social loafing because everyone could, and in fact had to, take part in the game."

Elina Riivari 2021 (2)¶

Elina Riivari, Marke Kivijärvi, Anna-Maija Lämsä. (2021). Learning teamwork through a computer game: for the sake of performance or collaborative learning? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10009-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Same qualitative analysis of the four cohorts' feedback forms and team reports; the theme of communication was "identified as the most important learning outcome", with students reflecting they would plan more, listen to others, and communicate more specifically.

"As one student put it, “You needed the others all the time in order to succeed.” In analyzing students’ learning experi- ences, the theme of communication was identified as the most important learning outcome."
