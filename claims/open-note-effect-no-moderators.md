## Evidence

### Sato 2015

Sato, B. K., He, W., Warschauer, M., & Kadandale, P. (2015). The Grass Isn't Always Greener: Perceptions of and Performance on Open-Note Exams. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0121

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple regression (571 observations, class fixed effects) comparing open- versus closed-note scores across research experience, returner status, GPA, course grade, and gender found "none of the examined factors correlated with a difference." Reported as null findings; no effect sizes printed.

> "Despite these observations, none of the examined factors correlated with a difference in open- versus closed-note testing (Figure 3, A–E, and Table 2)."
