### Foorman 2018

Foorman, B. R., Herrera, S., & Dombek, J. (2018). The relative impact of aligning Tier 2 intervention materials with classroom core reading materials in grades K–2. The Elementary School Journal, 118(3). https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/journals/esj

Three-level HLM subgroup analysis probing the three-way interaction at ±1 SD of the sentence-comprehension baseline grand mean; no comparison was significant, but the article prints effect sizes of g = .37 (Cohort 1 high baseline) and g = .28 (Cohort 2 low baseline) favoring stand-alone, which it calls substantively important.

### Foorman 2018 (2)

Foorman, B. R., Herrera, S., & Dombek, J. (2018). The relative impact of aligning Tier 2 intervention materials with classroom core reading materials in grades K–2. The Elementary School Journal, 118(3). https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/journals/esj

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

HLM subgroup analysis of the grade 1 word reading outcome following a significant Cohort × Treatment interaction; probing within cohorts showed no significant comparison but a printed effect size of g = .29, a "22-point difference on the FRA word reading outcome in favor of the embedded intervention in Cohort 1".

> "Results from this investigation did not show any signiﬁcant comparisons; however, a substantively important 22-point difference on the FRA word reading outcome in favor of the embedded intervention in Cohort 1 (g p .29) was observed."
