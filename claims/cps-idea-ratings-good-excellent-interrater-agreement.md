## Evidence

### Van Hooijdonk 2023

Van Hooijdonk, M., Mainhard, T., Kroesbergen, E. H., & Van Tartwijk, J. (2023). Creative problem solving in primary school students. Learning and Instruction, 88, 101823. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101823

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analysis in Study 2, in which two postgraduate raters who received 16 hours of training rated students' self-selected top-three ideas on 5-point scales using a modified Consensual Assessment Technique. The ICCs for originality "ranged from 0.75 to 0.87, indicating excellent agreement", with comparable values for completeness and practicality. No effect size is printed.

> "The ICCs for originality ranged from 0.75 to 0.87, indicating excellent agreement. The ICCs for completeness ranged from 0.68 to 0.80, indicating good to excellent agreement. The ICCs for practicality ranged from 0.79 to 0.89, again indicating excellent agreement."
