## Evidence

### Mohamed Rasha A. 2024

Mohamed Rasha A., Alhujaily Muhanad, Ahmed Faransa A., Nouh Wael G., Almowafy Abeer A. (2024). Exploring the potential impact of applying web-based training program on nurses’ knowledge, skills, and attitudes regarding evidence-based practice: A quasi-experimental study. PLOS ONE 19(2): e0297071. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0297071

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 3 of the 64-nurse study compares demographic and occupational variables against total knowledge score via repeated-measures ANCOVA and t-tests: workplace t = 7.55, P = 0.008; years of experience F = 0.64, P = 0.049, both significant at p≤0.05.

> "A significant relationship was found in the mean score of knowledge of the nurses and their workplace, and years of experience"

### Mohamed Rasha A. 2024 (2)

Mohamed Rasha A., Alhujaily Muhanad, Ahmed Faransa A., Nouh Wael G., Almowafy Abeer A. (2024). Exploring the potential impact of applying web-based training program on nurses’ knowledge, skills, and attitudes regarding evidence-based practice: A quasi-experimental study. PLOS ONE 19(2): e0297071. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0297071

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 3 reports non-significant ANCOVA/t-test results for age (F = 2.31, P = 0.085), sex (t = 0.55, P = 0.460), and academic qualification (F = 1.51, P = 0.227); equivalence was not tested.

> "However, no relationship was found between the mean score of the nurses’ knowledge and their age, sex, or academic qualification"
