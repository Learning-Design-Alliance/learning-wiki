### Guo Eddie 2024

Guo Eddie, Ramchandani Rashi, Park Ye-Jean, Gupta Mehul. (2024). OSCEai: personalized interactive learning for undergraduate medical education. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.79220

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 37 first-year medical students in the University of Calgary Class of 2026 RIME curriculum who used OSCEai in class and answered a voluntary anonymized survey on valued features. The article reports "33/37 participants highlighted the ability to request data" and 30/37 self-paced learning.

> "33/37 participants highlighted the ability to request data, such as physical exam findings and lab results; 30/37 respondents selected self-paced learning (i.e., able to complete the cases whenever and wherever they desire); 29/37 participants selected realistic patient interactions"
