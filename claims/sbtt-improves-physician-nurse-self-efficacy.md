## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Meurling Lisbet 2013

Meurling Lisbet, Hedman Leif, Sandahl Christer, Felländer-Tsai Li, Wallin Carl-Johan. (2013). Systematic simulation-based team training in a Swedish intensive care unit: a diverse response among critical care professions. BMJ quality & safety 22(6):485-494. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjqs-2012-000994

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post matched-questionnaire analysis (n=102) using a four-item seven-point self-efficacy questionnaire in a 2-year ICU SBTT project. The overall mean rose from "5.6 (0.9)" to "5.9 (0.7)", main effect p<0.0001, mean difference 0.28 (95% CI 0.12 to 0.44); per-profession simple effects were significant for physicians (p=0.001) and nurses (p=0.006) but not nurse assistants (p=0.538).
