Evidence¶

Haifang Liu 2026¶

Haifang Liu. (2026). AI-enhanced legal pedagogy: a case study of generative AI integration in property law courses. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10698-9

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Group-by-time interaction on legal reasoning scores across the 16-week semester, shown in Fig. 3 as a sustained upward trajectory for the experimental group; the article prints partial η2 = .26 for the pre-test-adjusted analysis of the same outcome, a large effect by the η² thresholds.

"the experimental group generated faster growth compared to the control group, as demonstrated by the statistically significant group-by-time interaction effect, F(1,178) = 52.73, p < .001"

Haifang Liu 2026 (2)¶

Haifang Liu. (2026). AI-enhanced legal pedagogy: a case study of generative AI integration in property law courses. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10698-9

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

ANCOVA on post-test legal reasoning with pre-test as covariate in the same quasi-experiment; the article prints partial η2 = .26, which exceeds the .14 threshold for a large effect, while noting ANCOVA "controls for initial ability levels only".

"ANCOVA controlling for pre-test scores confirmed this pattern, F(1,177) = 61.24, p < .001, partial η 2 = .26, indicating that the association between GenAI-enhanced instruction and learning gains remained substantial after accounting for pre-test differences"
