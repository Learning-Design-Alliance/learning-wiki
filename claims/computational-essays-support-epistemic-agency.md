### Odden 2022

Odden, T. O. B., Silvia, D., & Malthe-Sørenssen, A. (2022). Using Computational Essays to Redistribute Epistemic Agency in Undergraduate Science. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1002/tea.21821](https://doi.org/10.1002/tea.21821)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Aggregate trend analysis of 103 coded computational essays (45 from MSU, 58 from UiO) using a 0–2 scale codebook across four categories. The analysis found that 'the majority of students demonstrated a level of performance that would be considered sufficient for a passing grade (1 or 2)' across most dimensions.
