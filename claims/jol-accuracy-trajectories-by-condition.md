## Evidence

### Graf 2026

Graf, L., Bassner, P., Anzinger, M., Dietrich, F., Krusche, S., & Poquet, O. (2026). Interactive learning dashboards: rethinking learning visualisations as engagement tools. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-14082-1

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Figure 6 analysis of JoL–mastery correlations over three even time bins: elicit rose from r = 0.332 (n = 32) through r = 0.378 (n = 19) to "the correlation strengthened further (r = 0.482, p < 0.001)" (n = 53).

> "We observe a positive change over time of JoL accuracy under the elicit condition. In the elicit condition, the correlations between JoL and sys-tem's assessment show a consistent increase across the three time periods."

### Graf 2026 (2)

Graf, L., Bassner, P., Anzinger, M., Dietrich, F., Krusche, S., & Poquet, O. (2026). Interactive learning dashboards: rethinking learning visualisations as engagement tools. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-14082-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Figure 6 analysis: the none condition fell from r = 0.428 (p = 0.026, n = 27) to r = 0.176 (p = 0.221, n = 50) and further to r = 0.110 (p = 0.537, n = 34).

> "The none condition presented a decline in correlation over time suggesting poor support for the accuracy of the student's judgement of learning."

### Graf 2026 (3)

Graf, L., Bassner, P., Anzinger, M., Dietrich, F., Krusche, S., & Poquet, O. (2026). Interactive learning dashboards: rethinking learning visualisations as engagement tools. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-14082-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Figure 6 analysis: tell-condition correlations were negative in periods one and two (r = −0.419, p = 0.084; r = −0.076, p = 0.654) before "the correlation turned significantly positive (r = 0.571, p = 0.003)" in period three.

> "However, in the third time period ( n = 25), the cor- relation turned significantly positive (r = 0.571, p = 0.003)."
