## Evidence

### Okabayashi 2022

Okabayashi, S., Kitazawa, K., Kawamura, T., & Nakayama, T. (2022). E-Learning Material of Evidence-Based Medicine for Laypersons. HLRP: Health Literacy Research and Practice, 6(4), e290-e299. https://doi.org/10.3928/24748307-20221113-01

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Focus group interview category on efficacy: although "the figures and illustrations appeared friendly, the accompanying sentences were too long," and some participants desired more detailed descriptions despite satisfaction with the quizzes.

> "Regarding efficacy (difficulties and readability), the participants found the true/false quizzes to be relatively easy; however, the detailed descriptions were difficult to follow, as they contained many technical words."
