## Evidence

### Tiffin Paul A 2018

Tiffin Paul A, Orr James, Paton Lewis W, Smith Daniel T, Norcini John J. (2018). UK nationals who received their medical degrees abroad: selection into, and subsequent performance in postgraduate training: a national data linkage study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023060

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Subgroup analysis within the UK OG group comparing PLAB-route and EEA-qualified (PLAB-exempt) doctors on ARCP outcomes. The article reports "OR 1.16, 95% CI 0.91 to 1.47, p=0.24" — no statistically significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "We also noted no significant difference in the odds of a less satisfactory ARCP outcome between UK OGs who are generally expected to sit the PLAB test (n=812) and those who had qualified from a medical school within the EEA (n=793, OR 1.16, 95% CI 0.91 to 1.47, p=0.24)"
