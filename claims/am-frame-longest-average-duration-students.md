## Evidence

### Deepa N. Chari 2019

Deepa N. Chari, Hai D. Nguyen, Dean A. Zollman, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2019). Student and instructor framing in upper-division physics. [doi:10.1119/1.5120392](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5120392)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Statistical analysis of frame durations coded from the 50 video episodes. Students entered the AM frame 78 times for an average of 90 seconds each, and the article reports the AM average was significantly greater than all other frames. No effect size is printed; only a p-value is reported.

> "Results from a one-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post hoc test show that the average time per count in the algorithmic math (AM) frame is signiﬁcantly greater than that in all other frames ( p < 0.05) for students."
