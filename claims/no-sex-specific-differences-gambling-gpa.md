The review reports on two included studies that tested whether the gambling-academic performance association varied by gender: "neither found sex-specific differences." No effect sizes or test statistics are printed.

> "Only two studies included in this review examined differences in the association between gambling and academic performance by gender (Avenyo et al., 2024; LaBrie et al., 2003), and neither found sex-specific differences."
