### Alelaimat and Ghoneem 2012

Abeer Rashed Alelaimat, Khowla Abd Al_Raheem Ghoneem. (2012). The Effect of Educational Modules Strategy on the Direct and Postponed Study's Achievement of Seventh Primary Grade Students in Science, in Comparison with the Conventional Approach. Higher Education Studies, Vol. 2, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v2n2p40

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of the direct post-test by teaching method and gender in the two-group quasi-experiment; the article reports no significant difference due to student gender or the gender-by-approach interaction. Equivalence was not tested; the article attributes the null result to identical abilities, preparations and social conditions for both sexes.

> "the same table shows that there are no difference with statistic significance (á ≤0.05) about the direct achievement test due to student gender, interaction between the gender and the approach."
