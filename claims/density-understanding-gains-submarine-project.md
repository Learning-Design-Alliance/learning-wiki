## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mouhamadou Thiam 2017

Mouhamadou Thiam. (2017). Exploring the Archimedes Principle Through Engineering. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1612.08317](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1612.08317)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Teacher-reported classroom evidence: the author compared students' answers to the same four probes before and after the project, reporting that responses "attested that they had gained a better understanding of density after completing the project". No effect size or test statistic is printed.

> "My students' responses to these probes attested that they had gained a better understanding of density after completing the project compared to the beginning of the unit."

### Mouhamadou Thiam 2017 (2)

Mouhamadou Thiam. (2017). Exploring the Archimedes Principle Through Engineering. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1612.08317](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1612.08317)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r?`

Author-reported outcome from student reports: after the project, students "used the terms \"more dense\" and \"less dense\" instead of \"heavy\" and \"light\"" when explaining submarine behavior, which the author treats as evidence of conceptual change. No effect size printed.

> "Additionally, they used the terms "more dense" and "less dense" instead of "heavy" and "light" in their reports when explaining the behavior of their submarines."
