## Evidence

### Bueckle 2022

Bueckle, Andreas; Buehling, Kilian; Shih, Patrick C.; Börner, Katy. (2022). Optimizing Performance and Satisfaction in Matching and Movement Tasks in Virtual Reality with Interventions Using the Data Visualization Literacy Framework. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.3389/frvir.2021.727344](https://doi.org/10.3389/frvir.2021.727344)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

RUI VR study, post-questionnaire satisfaction on a 5-point Likert scale: further analyses after the performance results showed "a significantly higher feeling of satisfaction in the experiment group of the 2D Desktop setup" (p = 0.004) and in VR Standup (p = 0.006). No effect size is printed.

> "found a significantly higher feeling of satisfaction in the experiment group of the 2D Desktop setup (median experiment group: 4, IQR: [4 - 5], median control group: 3, IQR: [2 - 4], Mann-Whitney-U-Test, p = 0.004) and in VR Standup"
