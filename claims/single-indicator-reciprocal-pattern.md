## Evidence

### Helmke 1995

Helmke, A., & van Aken, M. A. G. (1995). The causal ordering of academic achievement and self-concept of ability during elementary school: A longitudinal study. Journal of Educational Psychology, 87(4), 624-637. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.87.4.624

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the article's own LISREL comparisons (Models 1, 2a, 2b on the 697-student sample): models with one achievement indicator yielded reciprocal cross-lagged effects, and "there is an increasing dominance of paths leading from achievement to self-concept."

> "The former models, which use either test performance or marks (such as the majority of relevant studies, see Table 1), yield a reciprocal model, implying that self-concept in elementary school serves both as cause and as effect."
