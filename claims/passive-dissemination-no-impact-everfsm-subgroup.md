### Lord 2017

Lord, P., Rabiasz, A., and Styles, B. (2017). 'Literacy Octopus' Dissemination Trial: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN84508118

`q4 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Pre-specified subgroup analysis on the primary outcome using a model with everFSM and everFSM-by-intervention interaction covariates, plus a model on everFSM children only. Effect sizes versus control were 0.00 (CEM), -0.01 (IEE), 0.00 (ResearchEd), 0.00 (How2s), each with 95% CIs spanning zero (e.g. -0.03 to 0.03); "There was no evidence that any of the Literacy Octopus passive communication approaches impacted on pupil attainment in literacy."

> "There was no evidence that any of the Literacy Octopus passive communication approaches impacted on pupil attainment in literacy. This was the case for all pupils and for the sub-group of pupils who have ever been eligible for free school meals."
