### Haroon M. Al Tawarah 2017

Haroon M. Al Tawarah, Omar M. Mahasneh & Nourah A. Al-Hawartheh. (2017). The Effect of Practical Presentations' Strategy on the Achievement of the Ninth Graders Students of the Pre-Vocational Education Subject in Shobak District Schools. International Education Studies, 10(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n5p109

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 92 ninth graders in four Shobak district schools (46 experimental, 46 control) studying sanitation and environment waste recycling units; ANCOVA on post-test scores with pre-test as covariate. Raw post-test means were 83.0 (SD 7.3) for the experimental group and 60.6 (SD 7.4) for the control group (Table 4). Table 5 prints the group term as F = 4.226, p = 0.053, which is not significant at the article's stated α = 0.05, yet the abstract and text describe the difference as statistically significant in favor of the experimental group. The reported figures do not agree with one another (a 22-point raw gap with SDs near 7 and similar pre-test means would not give F ≈ 4), so the adjusted result cannot be read with confidence either way; no effect size is printed.
