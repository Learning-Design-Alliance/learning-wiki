The author's experiential claim from the Chief Advantages section: the form's "novelty" means reluctant judges "will do the ranking when presented with this form," attributed to the elimination of labor. No frequency count or systematic evidence is printed.

"One of the advantages of such a form lies in its novelty, for it is frequently found that a judge who refuses or is disinclined to write names down in order of merit, will do the ranking when presented with this form. It appeals to him as a sort of game."
