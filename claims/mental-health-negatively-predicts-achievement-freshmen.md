## Evidence

### Song Xiuli 2024

Song Xiuli, Hu Qian. (2024). The relationship between Freshman students' mental health and academic achievement: chain mediating effect of learning adaptation and academic self-efficacy. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-20738-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 3871 freshmen (2830 male, 1041 female; mean age 18.17 ± 0.85) at a Hebei vocational college; Pearson correlation and regression with mental health (SCL-90) predicting achievement. The study reports "significantly negatively predict academic achievement (β=-0.012, t=-2.298, p < 0.05)".

> "After conducting regression analysis with mental health as the independent variable and academic achievement as the dependent variable, it was found that mental health issues can significantly negatively predict academic achievement (β=-0.012, t=-2.298, p < 0.05)."
