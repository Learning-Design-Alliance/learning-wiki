Ye, P.-G., Mo, K., Long, Y., Liu, M., Sang, H., & Zheng, J. (2026). ARPG+: a simulation-based study of real-time coaching for educational LLM prompting. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00606-9

### Ye 2026 (2)

Ye, P.-G., Mo, K., Long, Y., Liu, M., Sang, H., & Zheng, J. (2026). ARPG+: a simulation-based study of real-time coaching for educational LLM prompting. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00606-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Overload distribution analysis (Fig. 8) categorizing 5000 interactions per method (250 learners × 20 turns) as severe, mild, or safe. ARPG+ achieves "97.2% safe operation"; Baseline suffers "18.3% overload" with 523 severe incidents, a 35-fold increase over ARPG+.

> "ARPG+ achieves 97.2% safe operation with only 15 severe events (0.3%). Base -line suffers 18.3% overload, including 523 severe incidents (10.5%)—a 35-fold increase."
