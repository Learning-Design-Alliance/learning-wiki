## Evidence

### Meer 2009

Meer, J.P. van & Theunissen, N.C.M. (2009). Prospective educational applications of mental simulation: A meta-review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-009-9097-8

`q3 · i2` · `review · r3`

Synthesis of three consulted meta-analyses (Driskell et al. 1994; Feltz & Landers 1983; Hinshaw 1991) in this meta-review of 10 reviews covering about 630 studies. The review reports "Effect sizes of 0.48, 0.53 and 0.68" favoring MS over no practice, i.e. roughly half a standard deviation or more.

> "Effect sizes of 0.48, 0.53 and 0.68 are reported, indicating that employment of MS influences performance positively when compared to no practice at all"
