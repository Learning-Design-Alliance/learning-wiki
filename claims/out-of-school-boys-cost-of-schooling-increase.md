## Evidence

### Nandi 2023

Nandi, A., Haberland, N., Kozak, M., & Ngô, T. D. (2023). The gendered effects of the COVID-19 pandemic on adolescent literacy and schooling outcomes in India. npj Science of Learning, 8, 42. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00193-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

PSM and IPW analysis of reasons for non-enrollment among out-of-school boys (n=2,964) from NFHS-5. "Out-of-school post-COVID boys reported the cost of schooling as the reason at 5 –6% higher rates" than matched pre-COVID boys (all three methods p<0.01).

> "Similarly, out-of-school post-COVID boys reported the cost of schooling as the reason at 5 –6% higher rates as compared to boys in the matched pre-COVID group."
