# JACK provides both evaluative and descriptive feedback, with descriptive feedback referring to the student's input and reusing previous values

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Splitting exercises into steps lets students receive detailed individual feedback per step, combining an evaluative score (0–100) with descriptive feedback messages that can refer to the student's input and involve calculations. [→ Nils Schwinning 2018](#nils-schwinning-2018)

### Nils Schwinning 2018

Nils Schwinning, Michael Striewe, Till Massing, Christoph Hanck, Michael Goedicke. (2018). Towards digitalisation of summative and formative assessments in academic teaching of statistics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1811.02391](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1811.02391)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Definitional/design description of JACK's feedback options, mapped onto the Tunstall and Gsipps typology. The score is "an integer number in the range of 0 to 100"; feedback messages may contain dynamically created graphics, refer to the student's input, and reuse values from previous steps.

> "According to the typology of Tunstall and Gsipps [14], JACK thus provides both evaluative and descriptive feedback: The score is an integer number in the range of 0 to 100 based on a grading scheme provided by the author."
