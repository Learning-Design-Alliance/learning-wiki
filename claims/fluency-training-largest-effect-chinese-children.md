`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-regression comparing intervention methods across the 28 included RCTs. The article reports fluency training had the largest effect (g = 1.78), though it also cautions elsewhere that there are a limited number of Chinese fluency studies and efficacy "should be concluded with caution".

> "Specifically, fluency training (g = 1.78) appeared as the most effective intervention method with a large effect."
