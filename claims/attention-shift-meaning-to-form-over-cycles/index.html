Evidence¶

Rafael Zaccaron 2019¶

Rafael Zaccaron. (2019). Ideas for integrating meaningful immediate repetition tasks in the English as an additional language classroom: promoting fluency. BELT, 10(2). http://dx.doi.org/10.15448/2178-3640.2019.2.32988

q2 · i? · design · r1

The article draws on Schmidt's (2001) claim that attention is a limited resource and on Bygate and Samuda (2005) to explain why repetition cycles let learners "shift to linguistic aspects of the message"; the author reports noticing students correcting their own speech during repetition.

"once students have focused on the message they wanted to convey in the first cycle, the repetition of the task allows for their attention to shift to linguistic aspects of the message (Bygate and Samuda, 2005) over the cycles."
