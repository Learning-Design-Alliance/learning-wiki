# The jigsaw method produced significantly higher learning scores than both flipped classroom and lecture-based teaching in nursing students

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Nursing students taught with the jigsaw method scored significantly higher on a 20-item MCQ learning posttest than students taught with flipped classroom or lecture-based teaching (p = 0.004, eta squared = 0.09). [→ Khodadadeh Alireza 2025](#khodadadeh-alireza-2025)

## Evidence

### Khodadadeh Alireza 2025

Khodadadeh Alireza, Rivaz Mozhgan, Torabizadeh Camellia. (2025). The effects of flipped classroom and jigsaw teaching strategies on learning, retention of course content, and satisfaction among nursing students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07662-1

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental posttest study of 111 nursing students allocated to flipped classroom (n = 35), jigsaw (n = 43), or lecture (n = 34) over four sessions. ANOVA gave learning means of 12.23, 14.00, and 11.73 with "significantly higher learning scores (p < 0.05)" and eta squared 0.09.

> "Compared with the other two methods, the jigsaw method resulted in significantly higher learning scores (p < 0.05)."
