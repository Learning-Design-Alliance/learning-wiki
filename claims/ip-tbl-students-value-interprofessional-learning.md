## Evidence

### Burgess A 2020

Burgess A, Kalman E, Haq I, Leaver A, Roberts C, Bleasel J. (2020). Interprofessional team-based learning (TBL): how do students engage? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02024-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire completed by 117/311 (38%) of participants immediately after the session, using RIPLS and team-process items on a five-point Likert scale. The article reports 88% agreement that viewpoints were respected and 83% that opinions were encouraged; 84% would welcome learning with other healthcare students again and 85% agreed shared learning helps teamwork.

> "Notably, 88% of respondents agreed that “Different points of view from all health professions were respected by team members”, and 83% agreed that “Team members encouraged one another from all health professions to express their opinions”."
