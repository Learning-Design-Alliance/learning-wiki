`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended questions from the 26 autistic students on support received; accommodations mentioned included exam allowances, note-takers, alternative assessment methods, and specialised rooms. Descriptive counts; no effect size.

> "Autistic students (n = 16; 62%) reported that they received support from their institution and the types of support that were explicitly mentioned included mentoring (n = 6; 23%) disability services support (n = 4; 15%) and study tutors (n = 2; 8%)."
