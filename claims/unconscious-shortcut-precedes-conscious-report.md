## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Siegler 1998

Siegler, R. S., & Stern, E. (1998). Conscious and unconscious strategy discoveries: A microgenetic analysis. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 127(4), 377-397. https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.127.4.377

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Microgenetic experiment with 31 German second graders assessed trial-by-trial via solution times and retrospective verbal reports. The article reports that "27 of the 31 children (88%)" showed implicit shortcut use before explicit report; no effect size is printed.

> "combining the blocked and mixed conditions, 27 of the 31 children (88%) generated the short solution times that denned implicit use of the shortcut strategy before they generated verbal explanations explicitly reporting use of the strategy."
