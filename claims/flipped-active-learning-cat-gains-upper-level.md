# Critical thinking CAT scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in flipped intermediate and upper-level life science courses using active learning, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Students in BI 225 Evolutionary Ecology and BI/CH 308 Biochemistry showed significant pre-to-post CAT gains with effect sizes of 0.52 and 0.46, while the introductory BI 125 gain of 1.51 points was marginal (p = 0.06). [→ Styers M. L. 2018](#styers-m-l-2018)

## Evidence

### Styers M. L. 2018

Styers M. L., Van Zandt P. A., Hayden K. L. (2018). Active Learning in Flipped Life Science Courses Promotes Development of Critical Thinking Skills. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-11-0332

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Matched pretest/posttest CAT analysis of three Fall 2015 flipped courses (BI 125 n=29, BI 225 n=19, BI/CH 308 n=18 paired pairs). "There was a consistent pattern of higher posttest mean scores across all three courses"; BI 225 and BI/CH 308 gains were significant with effect sizes 0.52 and 0.46.

> "There was a consistent pattern of higher posttest mean scores across all three courses, although the gain for BI 125 (1.51 points) was not significant (p = 0.06; Table 4)."
