Fettahlıoğlu 2018¶

Fettahlıoğlu, P., & Kaleci, D. (2018). Online Argumentation Implementation in the Development of Critical Thinking Disposition. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i3.2904

Paired samples t-test on the control group (n=21); means were 4.56 pre-test and 4.42 post-test, and "no statistically significant difference has been found between them" [t(20)=1.02, p>.05]. Equivalence was not tested.

"It is observed in Table 4that teacher candidates’ point average in the pre -test is 4.56 and post-test point average is 4.42. According to the paired samples t -test applied to understand if there is a statistically significant difference between these points, no statistically significant difference has been found between them [t(20) =1.02, p> .05]."
