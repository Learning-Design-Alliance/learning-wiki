## Subclaims
`q2 i?` From baseline to 2-year follow-up, within-network connectivity increased significantly in most networks and decreased in the retrosplenial temporal, salience, and ventral attention networks, with no significant change in the sensorimotor mouth network. [→ Rakesh 2025](#rakesh-2025)

## Evidence

### Rakesh 2025

Rakesh, D., Sadikova, E., & McLaughlin, K. A. (2025). Associations among socioeconomic disadvantage, longitudinal changes in within-network connectivity, and academic outcomes in the ABCD study. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2025.101587

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal analysis of resting-state fMRI in the ABCD study (N = 4745) characterized time-related change in twelve networks between baseline and 2-year follow-up using residualized change models; the article reports "Connectivity within most networks" increased while retrosplenial temporal, salience, and ventral attention decreased.

> "Connectivity within most networks, including the auditory, cingulo-opercular, default mode, cingulo-parietal, dorsal attention, fronto-parietal, sensorimotor (hand), and visual networks increased from T1 to T2. On the other hand, connectivity within the retrosplenial temporal, salience, and ventral attention decreased from T1 to T2."
