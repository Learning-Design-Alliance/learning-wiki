## Evidence

### Olsen 2017

Olsen, J. K., Rummel, N., & Aleven, V. (2017). Learning Alone or Together? A Combination Can Be Best! Proceedings of the 12th International Conference on Computer Supported Collaborative Learning. https://learning-analytics.info/journals/index.php/ijcscl/article/view/9151

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

HLM analysis from the same 307-student study. The article reports "a significant main effect of grade" favoring 5th graders and "a significant interaction between grade and pretest and posttest" indicating "the 4th graders had higher learning gains."

> "there was a significant main effect of grade, t(297) = 2.93, p < .05, r = .17, with the 5th graders having higher test scores than the 4th grade students. Surprisingly, there was a significant interaction between grade and pretest and posttest, t(301) = -5.53, p < .05, r = .3, indicating that the 4th graders had higher learning gains than the 5th graders"
