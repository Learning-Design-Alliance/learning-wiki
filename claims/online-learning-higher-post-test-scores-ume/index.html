Evidence¶

Pei L 2019¶

Pei L, Wu H. (2019). Does online learning work better than offline learning in undergraduate medical education? A systematic review and meta-analysis. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2019.1666538

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis of 15 post-test score comparisons extracted from 16 included studies of undergraduate medical students, pooled in RevMan 5.3. The pooled effect favored online learning: "the online learning group having higher post-test scores (SMD = 0.81; 95% CI: 0.43, 1.20)".

"Figure 4 shows the two groups were significantly different (Z = 4.17; p < 0.0001), with the online learning group having higher post-test scores (SMD = 0.81; 95% CI: 0.43, 1.20)."
