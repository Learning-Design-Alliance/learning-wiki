## Evidence

### Lee Hye-Yoon 2025

Lee Hye-Yoon, Kim Jundong, Choi Hyojae, Bae Hyojin, Jeong Aram, Choi Sungyoul, Kim Ji-Hwan, Kim Chang-Eop. (2025). Comparing AI chatbot simulation and peer role-play for OSCE preparation: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08308-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot RCT OSCE results: 19 Korean medicine students randomized after stratification by baseline knowledge test; dizziness-scenario checklist scores compared with Mann–Whitney U tests and Hedges' g. The PRP group's advantage showed "moderate-to-large effect sizes" that did not reach adjusted significance (p = 0.2698, 0.1792).

> "Particularly in history taking (mean 74.4 vs. 66.2) and physical examination (31.5 vs. 18.8), the PRP group’s advantage corresponded to moderate-to-large effect sizes (Hedges’ g = −0.68, −1.11, adjusted p-value = 0.2698, 0.1792)."
