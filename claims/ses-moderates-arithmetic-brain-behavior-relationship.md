`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The Perspective reports, citing Demir, Prado and Booth's behavioral and imaging investigation of children's mathematical cognition, that "SES moderated the relationship between behavior and brain activation", indicating qualitative rather than purely quantitative neural differences. No effect size is printed.

> "SES moderated the relationship between behavior and brain activation: in children from higher SES homes, the activity of regions associated with verbal performance (including the left middle temporal gyrus) tracked mathematical ability"
