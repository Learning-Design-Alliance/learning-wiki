Lachner 2025¶

Lachner, A., Russ, H., Hübner, N., Sibley, L., & Scheiter, K. (2025). When Does Learning by Non-interactive Teaching Work? A Large-Scale Analysis of Learner Characteristics in a Classroom Setting. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10060-0

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Background meta-analytic evidence reported in the article's introduction: the article attributes to Ribosa and Duran (2022) an analysis of 23 studies comprising 62 comparisons, yielding "a small but significant advantage (SMD = 0.17)". The article is not itself this meta-analysis.

"Their results revealed a small but significant advantage (SMD = 0.17) for non-interactive teaching over the control condition. At the same time, the variance among studies was considerably large, as some studies produced non-significant or even negative effects"
