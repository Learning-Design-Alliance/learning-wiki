## Evidence

### Emre Gönülateş and Gerd Kortemeyer 2015

Emre Gönülateş and Gerd Kortemeyer. (2015). A New Item Response Theory Model for Open-Ended Online Homework with Multiple Allowed Attempts. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.010118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.010118)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Modeling study of first-attempt homework data from a 256-student physics course, comparing a reduced 2P3TL model (γj ≡ 0) against exam-based 2PL ability. The copying-only fit shows R² = 0.53253 versus R² = 0.37713 for the final-attempt 2PL model, so "the predictive power of student ability improved by absorbing copying into another trait."

> "the predictive power of student ability improved by absorbing copying into another trait, since at least in theory, students are not able to copy on exams. In fact, remarkably, the predictive power is now better than the estimates based on the 2PL analysis of the final homework attempt"
