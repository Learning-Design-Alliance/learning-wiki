Castellucci C, Malorgio A, Budowski AD, Akbas S, Kolbe M, Grande B, Braun J, Noethiger CB, Spahn DR, Tscholl DW, Roche TR. (2023). Coagulation Management of Critically Bleeding Patients With Viscoelastic Testing Presented as a 3D-Animated Blood Clot (The Visual Clot): Randomized Controlled High-Fidelity Simulation Study. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative survey on user acceptance: participants who used Visual Clot rated 4 statements on a 4-point Likert scale after the simulation. "Most participants (64/85, 75% agreed or strongly agreed)" felt better prepared; 79/85 (93%) said interpretation was easy to learn and 73/85 (86%) would use it in practice.
