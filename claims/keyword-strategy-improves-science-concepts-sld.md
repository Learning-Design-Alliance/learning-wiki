## Evidence

### Aracı 2023

Aracı, N., Melekoğlu, M. A., & Çetin, M. E. (2023). Impact of a Mnemonic Strategy on Learning Science Concepts for Middle School Students With Specific Learning Disabilities. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 21(1), 69-85. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1386167

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control group study with 27 middle school students with SLD in Türkiye. Mixed-model ANOVA on science concept measurement tool scores showed a significant between-group difference with a large effect size, "η2 = .517".

> "a significant difference was found between the mean scores of the experimental and control groups, regardless of the test (F(1, 25) = 26.751; p < .05; η2 = .517). Accordingly, the experimental group (x̄ = 5.00) was more successful, with an average greater than that of the control group (x̄ = 1.92)."
