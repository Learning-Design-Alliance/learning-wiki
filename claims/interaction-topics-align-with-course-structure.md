## Evidence

### L. N. Simpfendoerfer 2024

L. N. Simpfendoerfer, Meagan Sundstrom, Matthew Dew, and N. G. Holmes. (2024). What topics of peer interactions correlate with student performance in physics courses?. arXiv. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad358b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad358b)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Thematic coding of 1,982 reported interactions (1,177 lab, 805 lecture) with Fuzzy Kappa interrater reliability of 0.93, exceeding the 0.80 threshold. The article reports small-group work in about 60% of lab explanations; homework occurred most often (about 40%) in lecture.

> "In the lab courses (top panel of Fig. 3), small-group work is the most common interaction topic, with students mentioning this topic in about 60% of the explanations."
