## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study (Wong et al. 2022, Hawaii) converting the Disaster Aftermath Interprofessional Simulation to a fully online format via Zoom breakout rooms and shared documents, with 63 interprofessional students compared with pooled in-person cohorts of 150. The review reports "p = 0.0001" and "p = 0.0020"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Interprofessional collaborative skills increased significantly in the online cohort based on Interprofessional Collaborative Competency Attainment Survey total mean scores (retrospective pre–post). Online students’ mean overall total score was higher than in‐person both pre (online 4.12 vs. in‐person 3.73; p = 0.0001) and post (online 4.50 vs. in‐person 4.25; p = 0.0020)."
