Podiya Jayalaxmi Kaniyagundi, Navaneetham Janardhana, Bhola Poornima. (2025). Influences of school climate on emotional health and academic achievement of school-going adolescents in India: a systematic review. BMC Public Health 25, 54. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-21268-0

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

Synthesis of the included studies, principally Jethwani-Keyser (2008, N = 300, Bengaluru) using the Perceived School Related Stress Scale, Psychological Sense of School Membership scale, and Hare Self Esteem scale; the review reports "stress related to school was negatively associated with both school connectedness/belongingness (r=-.20, p < .05)" and a suggested mediating role of self-esteem (r = -.25, p < .01).

> "stress related to school was negatively associated with both school connectedness/belongingness (r=-.20, p < .05) and academic achievements. Additionally, the research suggested a mediating role of self-esteem (r =-.25, p < .01) in the relationship between school belongingness and academic success"
