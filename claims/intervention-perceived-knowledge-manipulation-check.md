## Evidence

### Puppart 2025

Puppart, B., & Aru, J. (2025). A Brief AI Literacy Intervention Does Not Significantly Reduce Over-Reliance and Increases Under-Reliance on ChatGPT: A Randomized Study. University of Tartu. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.10556](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.10556)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Manipulation-check ratings on a four-point Likert scale after reading. Usage knowledge showed "t(34) = 2.26, p = .030, d = 0.76"; working-mechanism knowledge showed t(34) = 2.09, p = .044, d = 0.70; the Limitations difference was not significant (p = .577, d = 0.19).

> "participants in the intervention group reported higher perceived knowledge in the “Usage” category (M = 3.42, SD = 0.77) than in the control group (M = 2.77, SD = 0.97), t(34) = 2.26, p = .030, d = 0.76"

### Puppart 2025 (2)

Puppart, B., & Aru, J. (2025). A Brief AI Literacy Intervention Does Not Significantly Reduce Over-Reliance and Increases Under-Reliance on ChatGPT: A Randomized Study. University of Tartu. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.10556](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.10556)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null results from the randomized comparison: post-puzzle confidence ratings (five-point Likert scale) and reliance decision times showed no significant group differences, and no effect sizes were printed for these tests.

> "No significant group differences were observed in confidence ratings or decision time."
