## Evidence

### Okada 2024

Okada, A., Sherborne, T., Panselinas, G., & Kolionis, G. (2024). AI in Education: A Cross-National Study of Open Schooling Using the CARE-KNOW-DO Framework for Sustainable Development Goals. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3960164/v1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-national survey of 330 students analysed with SPSS 27; the article reports the percentage of students scoring above 3 by age group, stating "the highest percentage (91%) observed in the 17–18 year age group".

> "Age-related differences revealed variation in perceptions of transversal skills, with the highest percentage (91%) observed in the 17–18 year age group, 86% in the 15–16 year age group, 76% in the 13–14 year age group and 69% in the 12 year age group."
