`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary's reading of Trninic's (2018) case study, which skillfully compares martial arts training with mathematics education. The author reports that "It is by repetition and continued guidance that the student discovers" the intended lesson, supporting integration of guidance and discovery. This is a qualitative case study, so no effect size is printed.

> "It is by repetition and continued guidance that the student discovers what the martial arts teacher wants him to discover. The story resonates well with my own experiences in sports, music, and mathematics, that teachers can prepare and guide some lessons, but that learners can only discover these lessons in their full embodied sense after much deliberate practice."
