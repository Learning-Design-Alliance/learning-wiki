## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Devine A 2012

Devine A, Fawcett K, Szűcs D, Dowker A. (2012). Gender differences in mathematics anxiety and the relation to mathematics performance while controlling for test anxiety. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-8-33

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Partial correlation analyses controlling for TA in the girls' subsample found MA remained strongly negatively correlated with performance, r = −.301, p <0.001; a difference test found the MA-performance correlation significantly stronger in girls than boys (p = 0.0233).

> "The effect of TA was controlled for in partial correlation analyses. In girls MA remained strongly negatively correlated with performance (r = −.301; p <0.001)."

### Devine A 2012 (2)

Devine A, Fawcett K, Szűcs D, Dowker A. (2012). Gender differences in mathematics anxiety and the relation to mathematics performance while controlling for test anxiety. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-8-33

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Partial correlation analyses controlling for TA in the boys' subsample found the MA-performance correlation only marginally significant, r = −0.108, p = 0.07. The authors interpret that boys' anxiety may simply reflect general test anxiety.

> "In contrast, the correlation of MA and performance was only marginally significant in boys (r = −0.108; p = 0.07)."
