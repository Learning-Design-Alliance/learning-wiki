The review cites a UNICEF multi-country descriptive study in which adolescents were "digitally savvy and heavily online, yet they largely used platforms for entertainment and leisure"; the report concluded these patterns were not necessarily translating into stronger digital literacy or critical thinking.

> "In a UNICEF multi-country study of adolescents' online engagement in East Asia and the Pacific, young people were described as digitally savvy and heavily online, yet they largely used platforms for entertainment and leisure rather than for knowledge acquisition or skill development"
