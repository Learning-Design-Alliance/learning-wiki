Evidence¶

Zennure Elgün Gündüz 2016¶

Zennure Elgün Gündüz, İsmail Doğan Ünal. (2016). Effects of Multiple Intelligences Activities on Writing Skill Development in an EFL Context. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(7): 1687-1697. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040722

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative interviews with ten randomly selected students after post-testing, plus researcher observations; students reported enjoying games and repeated use of vocabulary. The article offers these reflections as interpretive support, with no quantitative attitude measure.

"The students said they the lessons instructed through MI -based activities were more interesting and enjoyable for them. Therefore, they could pay attention to the lessons and learn more easily."
