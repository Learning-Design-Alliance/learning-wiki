## Evidence

### Gürsoy 2016

Gürsoy, E., Kesner, J. E., & Salihoglu, U. M. (2016). Clinical Supervision Model in Teaching Practice: Does it Make a Difference in Supervisors' Performance?. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 41(11). https://doi.org/10.14221/ajte.2016v41n11.5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Nonparametric comparison of CT supervisor-satisfaction surveys (12 Likert-type items, Cronbach's alpha .98) between experimental (n=108 CTs) and control (n=32 CTs) groups. The article reports a statistically significant difference favoring the experimental group; no effect size is printed.

> "Similarly, the Mann-Whitney U test for the CTs evaluations of the US revealed statistically significant differences in the overall rating of the US, U=774.500, p< .001. Cooperating teachers who were a part of the experimental group had on average, a higher evaluation score of the US."
