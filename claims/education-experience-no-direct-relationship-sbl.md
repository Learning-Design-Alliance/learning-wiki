## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Levin 2025

Levin, O., Frei-Landau, R., & Goldberg, C. (2025). "No pain, no gain": Simulation-based learning in teacher education: The mediating role of simulation hindrances. PLoS ONE, 20(1), e0317255. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0317255

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null result from the SEM analysis of the N = 370 sample (education ranged 12–25 years, M = 17.4; teaching experience 0–38 years, M = 14.4). The article reports "no direct significant relationship was found"; no effect size was printed. Equivalence was not tested.

> "It is worth noting that no direct significant relationship was found involving either participants' level of education or the accumulation of professional experience; hence, these variables were excluded from the analysis."
