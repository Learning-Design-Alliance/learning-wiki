## Evidence

### Wang Huang 2023

Wang Huang, Xu Tianyuan, Zhang Mengxue. (2023). A mixed method study on Chinese primary school EFL teachers’ preparation, affecting factors and support needed to implement intercultural foreign language teaching. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0284146

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire study of 93 Chinese primary school EFL teachers recruited via snowball sampling through the Primary Teacher Education Alliance. Teachers rated preparedness on a 4-point scale; "up to 67.74% of teachers choose either inadequately prepared or not well prepared for the first question, and 64.52%" for the second.

> "The mean scores for the two questions were 2.215 and 2.290, respectively, which shows that teachers are not well prepared for both cultural teaching goals."
