## Evidence

### Xingliang Chen & Antonija Mitrovic & Moffat Mathews 2019

Xingliang Chen & Antonija Mitrovic & Moffat Mathews. (2019). Investigating the Effect of Agency on Learning from Worked Examples, Erroneous Examples and Problem Solving. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education (2019) 29:396–424. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00179-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures mixed effects model on pre/post-test scores of 40 participants (20 Low-Agency, 19 High-Agency after outlier removal) in a 100-min SQL-Tutor session. The article reports "no significant interaction", F(1,37) = .002, p = .963, but a significant pre-to-post improvement with the printed effect size partial ƞ2 = .661.

> "There was no significant interaction between the test scores and the group, F(1,37) = .002, p = .963. There was a significant difference in test scores between the pre- and post-test, F(1,37) = 55.56, p < .001, partial ƞ2 = .661"
