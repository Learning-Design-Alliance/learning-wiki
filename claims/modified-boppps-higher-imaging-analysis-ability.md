## Evidence

### Wei Mingchao 2026

Wei Mingchao, Tang Jian, Wang Jun, Song Zhengkai, Ji Piyou, Wang Yumin. (2026). Effectiveness of a modified BOPPPS teaching model in theoretical education for thoracic surgery: an empirical study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08909-1

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Non-randomized quasi-experiment with two intact classes (n = 48 each) taught the same lung tumour content within 300 minutes; post-course imaging analysis scores were 22.8 ± 2.7 vs. 20.5 ± 3.0, "significantly higher in clinical imaging analysis ability (P = 0.021, Cohen's d = 0.79)".

> "the experimental group scored significantly higher in clinical imaging analysis ability (P = 0.021, Cohen's d = 0.79) and comprehensive clinical application ability (P = 0.032, Cohen's d = 0.89)"
