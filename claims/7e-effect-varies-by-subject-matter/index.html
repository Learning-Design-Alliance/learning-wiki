Balta 2016¶

Balta, N., & Sarac, H. (2016). The Effect of 7E Learning Cycle on Learning in Science Teaching: A meta-Analysis Study. European Journal of Educational Research, 5(2), 61-72. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.5.2.61

q3 · i2

Sub-group analysis of the meta-analysis grouping effect sizes by subject: biology (4), chemistry (2), physics (16), and science (13). The printed group-average effect sizes were chemistry ES = 2.066 and science ES = 1.002, with a significant between-group QB(3) = 11.885, p < .05.

"The between -group comparison was significant for this grouping, ( QB (3) = 11.885, p < .05) indicating t hat the effect sizes differed significantly among the subjects. Where chemistry subject had the maximum (2.066) and science subject had the minimum (1.002) group averages."
