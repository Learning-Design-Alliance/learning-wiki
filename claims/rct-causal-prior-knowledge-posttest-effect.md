# Nine randomized controlled trials support a causal effect of prior knowledge on posttest knowledge (rP+ = .394), not attributable to intelligence confounding

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In the nine included RCTs, the manipulated prior knowledge had a significant positive causal effect on posttest knowledge (rP+ = .394). [→ Simonsmeier 2022](#simonsmeier-2022)
`q2 i?` The RCT effect size did not differ significantly from that of non-RCT designs (R² = .001, p = .269), and controlling for intelligence did not significantly reduce the correlation (z = 0.219, p = .413). [→ Simonsmeier 2022 (2)](#simonsmeier-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Simonsmeier 2022

Simonsmeier, B. A., Flaig, M., Deiglmayr, A., Schalk, L., & Schneider, M. (2022). Domain-specific prior knowledge and learning: A meta-analysis. Educational Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2021.1939700

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Nine RCTs (45 effect sizes) in which participants were randomized into at least two groups and prior-knowledge levels were manipulated before learning via pre-teaching, pretraining, or prerequisite instruction; the printed effect size rP+ = .394 was significant and positive.

> "These studies found evidence for a causal effect of prior knowledge on posttest knowledge, as evidenced by the significant positive effect size of rPþ ¼ .394."

### Simonsmeier 2022 (2)

Simonsmeier, B. A., Flaig, M., Deiglmayr, A., Schalk, L., & Schneider, M. (2022). Domain-specific prior knowledge and learning: A meta-analysis. Educational Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2021.1939700

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator and partial-correlation checks: the RCT vs. non-RCT comparison gave R² = .001, p = .269, and partial correlations controlling intelligence in 45 studies were not significantly reduced, so the association was not attributed to intelligence confounding.

> "Controlling the correlation for intelligence across the 45 studies for which this was possible did not lead to a statistically significant decrease (z ¼ 0.219, p ¼ .413)."
