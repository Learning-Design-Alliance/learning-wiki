### Jin et al. 2026

Yimeng Jin, Chunyu Ma, Danyang Liu, Junpeng You, Johan Lauwereyns. (2026). Effort enhances understanding, while appreciation drives engagement in Cross-sentence semantic processing. Acta Psychologica. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2026.107927

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory experiment in which 24 university students read randomly paired, unconnected sentences from a novel and chose per trial whether to think aloud; trial-level LMMs showed the significant Trial Type effects, and "Chosen-effort Trials consistently received higher ratings than Low-effort Trials".

> "LMMs revealed significant main effects of Trial Type for Sense Score, Interest Score, and Like Score (Table 2). Across all three evaluations, Chosen-effort Trials consistently received higher ratings than Low-effort Trials for Sense Score, Interest Score, and Like Score."
