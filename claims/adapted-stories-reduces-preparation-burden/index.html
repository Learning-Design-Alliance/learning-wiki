Buyankhishig Enkhjargal 2026¶

Buyankhishig Enkhjargal, Himanshi Lalwani, Hanan Salam. (2026). AI-Assisted Social Story Intervention for Special Education: The Design of AdaptED Stories. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24245

q2 · i? · design · r3

Qualitative findings from the Phase 3 think-aloud study with seven practitioners. P1 highlighted "how easy it was to make a story tailored to my student's level for story narration, with no need to look for appropriate images to use." Perceived burden reduction, not an objectively measured time saving.

"These accounts suggest that practitioners valued the system not only for its ease of use, but also for its potential to streamline parts of the preparation process that would otherwise require additional manual effort."
