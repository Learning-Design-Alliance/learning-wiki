## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among fifth-grade public-school students in Shanghai, frequency of minimal-intensity physical activity (walking) was significantly and positively associated with teacher-reported academic performance scores after adjustment for demographic, socioeconomic, health and time-use confounders. [→ Zhang Yunting 2015](#zhang-yunting-2015)

## Evidence

### Zhang Yunting 2015

Zhang Yunting, Zhang Donglan, Jiang Yanrui, Sun Wanqi, Wang Yan, Chen Wenjuan, Li Shenghui, Shi Lu, Shen Xiaoming, Zhang Jun, Jiang Fan. (2015). Association between Physical Activity and Teacher-Reported Academic Performance among Fifth-Graders in Shanghai: A Quantile Regression. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115483

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional regression analysis of 1470 Shanghai fifth-graders drawn by stratified random sampling of 10 public schools in 2009, using teacher-reported APS as the outcome. The article reports MiIPA "had a significant positive association with APS (β = 0.12, SE = 0.04)".

> "MiIPA had a significant positive association with APS (β = 0.12, SE = 0.04), when adjusted for demographic, family socioeconomic status, health status, and time spent on other activities."
