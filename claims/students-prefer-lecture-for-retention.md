## Evidence

### Schreiber Benjamin E 2010

Schreiber Benjamin E, Fukuta Junaid, Gordon Fabiana. (2010). Live lecture versus video podcast in undergraduate medical education: A randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-68

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported preference question answered by the 66 questionnaire respondents in the crossover trial. Percentages are descriptive only, as printed: "54% found the lecture format much better". Similar preferences were reported for comfort and pleasantness (48% much better lecture).

> "In terms of learning and retaining new information, 54% found the lecture format much better, 40% found the lecture format a little better and only 6% found the podcast format a little better. No students found the podcast format much better."
