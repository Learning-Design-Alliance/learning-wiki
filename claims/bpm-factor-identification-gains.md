## Evidence

### Patel Mandeep K. 2026

Patel Mandeep K., Timko‐Swaim Lynn, Conklin Perrilynn, Lu Wei‐Hsin. (2026). Curriculum Integration of an Interactive Blood Pressure Measurement Module to Improve Healthcare Students' Knowledge, Confidence, and Skills. The Journal of Clinical Hypertension. https://doi.org/10.1111/jch.70234

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-squared tests comparing pre/post survey response distributions in a mixed-method pre–post study. Medical students' all-four-correct responses rose from 58.4% to 83.5% (χ² = 15.26, p = 0.002); PA students' from 76.7% to 84.5% (χ² = 8.62, p = 0.035).

> "significantly more medical students (pre: 58.4% vs. post: 83.5%) were able to correctly identify all four factors (Factors A, B, C, D in Table 3) that can affect BP results [χ [2] = 15.26, p = 0.002]. Similarly, significantly more PA students (pre: 76.7% vs. post: 84.5%) selected all four correct factors [χ [2] = 8.62, p = 0.035]"
