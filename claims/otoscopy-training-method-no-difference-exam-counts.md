## Evidence

### Higgins Joyce Alanna 2019

Higgins Joyce Alanna, Raman Maya, Beaumont Jennifer L., Heiman Heather, Adler Mark, Schmidt Suzanne M. (2019). A survey comparison of educational interventions for teaching pneumatic otoscopy to medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1507-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey of 115 pediatric clerkship students randomized to task trainer (n=39), video (n=37), or peer practice (n=38), recording otoscopic exams in logbooks over six weeks. Median pneumatic exam counts were 4, 4, and 2; the Kruskal-Wallis difference was "not significant for either outcome (p = 0.455, p = 0.253)".

> "The median number of pneumatic otoscopic exams performed was 4 for the task trainer group (range 0–80), 4 for the peer-peer practice group (range 0–70), and 2 for the video instruction group (range 0–50) (Table 2). Using an Independent Samples Kruskal-Wallis, the difference among the groups was not significant for either outcome (p = 0.455, p = 0.253)."
