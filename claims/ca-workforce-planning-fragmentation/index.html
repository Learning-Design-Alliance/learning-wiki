The Collaborative, with WestEd, conducted an extensive review of documents, statute and incentive structures, and interviews with districts, county offices, educator preparation programs, and workforce experts. Findings "consistently pointed to fragmentation" in the supporting infrastructure.

"The statute encourages— but does not usually mandate— interagency collaboration to support teacher workforce planning, preparation, recruitment, and retention."
