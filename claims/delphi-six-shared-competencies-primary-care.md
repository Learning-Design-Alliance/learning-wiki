## Evidence

### Lee Jin Young 2025

Lee Jin Young, Min Yul Ha, Yim Jun, Park Kwi Hwa, Yune So Jung. (2025). Core shared competencies between physicians and nurses in primary care: a Delphi study in Korea. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.359

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Three-round online Delphi survey (November 2024–February 2025) with 30 experts (12 physicians, 18 nurses); 29 participated in Rounds 2 and 3. Round 3 means ranged from 4.45 to 4.90 with CVRs of 0.79–1.00; the article reports "All items met consensus criteria (CVR ≥0.34)". No effect size is printed.

> "Through iterative consensus, six shared competencies were derived: (1) patient-centered integrated care, (2) treatment plan development and implementation, (3) communication and collaboration, (4) professional development as a team member, (5) Evaluation and feedback on service outcomes, and (6) disease prevention and health promotion. All items met consensus criteria (CVR ≥0.34)."
