`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Eylon and Reif's (1984) study: students learned identical content "presented either hierarchically or sequentially," and hierarchical presentation produced significantly better subsequent-task performance; the review quotes the researchers' conclusion that hierarchical organization should facilitate complex-task retrieval.

> "In the study, students learned the same content with knowledge presented either hierarchically or sequentially. They found that students performed significantly better in subsequent tasks when taught the content with knowledge presented hierarchically."
