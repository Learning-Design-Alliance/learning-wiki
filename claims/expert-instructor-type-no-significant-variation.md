### Hughes Thomas C 2010

Hughes Thomas C, Jiwaji Zoeb, Lally Kamaldeep, Lloyd-Lavery Antonia, Lota Amrit, Dale Andrea, Janas Robert, Bulstrode Christopher JK. (2010). Advanced Cardiac Resuscitation Evaluation (ACRE): A randomised single-blind controlled trial of peer-led vs. expert-led advanced resuscitation training. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc subgroup analysis of the expert-led arm comparing senior doctor (6/12 High Passes), middle-grade doctor (11/22) and nurse (16/24). "Did not indicate significant inter-group variation" on either outcome; the authors warn these small-numbers post-hoc data "should be treated with caution".

> "Analysis of these results did not indicate significant inter-group variation in the proportions awarded Pass (Chi Squared p = 0.472) or High Pass grade (Chi Squared p = 0.213)."
