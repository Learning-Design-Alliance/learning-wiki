# Expert and beginner neurointerventionalists differ in HRV metrics during simulated cerebral angiographies, with experts showing decreased short-term HRV

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Experts and beginners significantly differed in three time-domain HRV metrics (RMSSD, NN50, pNN50), with lower values in experts; NN50 and pNN50 remained significant after Benjamini-Hochberg correction. [→ Hinzmann Dominik 2022](#hinzmann-dominik-2022)

## Evidence

### Hinzmann Dominik 2022

Hinzmann Dominik, Singer Maximilian, Schmelter Valerie, Kreiser Kornelia, Gehling Kim, Ströber Lea, Kirschke Jan S, Schulz Christian M and Schneider Frederick. (2022). Differences in beginner and expert neurointerventionalists” heart rate variability during simulated neuroangiographies. Interventional Neuroradiology. https://doi.org/10.1177/15910199221128439

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simulator study in which six beginners and five experts each performed diagnostic angiographies on a VIST simulator; 105 simulations analyzed (beginners N = 60; experts N = 45). Group-wise comparison showed "decreased RMSSD, NN50, pNN50 in experts; all p < 0.05". No effect sizes reported.

> "The heart rate variability of experts and beginners significantly differed in three time domain HRV metrics (decreased RMSSD, NN50, pNN50 in experts; all p < 0.05)"
