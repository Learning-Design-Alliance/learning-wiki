# Elementary ESL students taught vocabulary with the MPS on an interactive whiteboard achieved significantly higher post-test scores than students taught with a blackboard

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Fifth-graders using the MPS scored significantly higher on an English vocabulary post-test than blackboard-taught peers (79.78 vs 76.20, p < .05). [→ Kuo 2013](#kuo-2013)

## Evidence

### Kuo 2013

Kuo, F.-O., Yu, P.-T., & Hsiao, W.-H. (2013). Develop and evaluate the effects of multimodal presentation system on elementary ESL students. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018039

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 134 fifth-graders in six intact classes randomly assigned to instructional approach; independent-samples t-test on post-test vocabulary scores. The experimental group mean was "higher than the 76.20 (SD = 8.61) for the control group", t(125.794) = 2.087, p < .05.

> "The mean of scores was 79.78 (SD = 11.13) for the experimental group, higher than the 76.20 (SD = 8.61) for the control group. There is a significant difference between these two groups, t(125.794) = 2.087, p < .05."
