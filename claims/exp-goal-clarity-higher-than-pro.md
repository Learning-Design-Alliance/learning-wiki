# Experimental tasks were rated higher than programming tasks on goal clarity, with no significant difference in feasibility at home

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Goal clarity was rated significantly higher for the nine experimental tasks than the three programming tasks (r = 0.61). [→ Lahme 2025](#lahme-2025)
`q2 i?` Feasibility at home did not differ significantly between experimental and programming tasks. [→ Lahme 2025 (2)](#lahme-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Lahme 2025

Lahme, S. Z., Dorsel, D., Heinke, H., Klein, P., Müller, A., Stampfer, C., and Staacks, S. (2025). Recitation tasks revamped? Students’ perceptions of smartphone-based experimental and programming tasks in introductory mechanics. [doi:10.1103/bwpv-z3xh](https://doi.org/10.1103/bwpv-z3xh)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Weekly-survey comparison of task-quality subscales (goal clarity, feasibility at home) between nine Exp- and three Pro-tasks; goal clarity differed significantly with p= 0.04 and r= 0.61, an effect size printed in the article. All scores were above 3.5 on a 5-point agreement scale.

> "Mann-Whitney-U test revealed no significant difference between the average scores of the nine Exp- and three Pro-tasks for feasibility at home (U = 6.00, Z = 1.39, p= 0.21, r = 0 .40) but for goal clarity (U = 2 .00, Z =
2.13, p= 0.04, r= 0.61)."
