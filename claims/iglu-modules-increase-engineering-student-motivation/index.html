Evidence¶

Bai 2016¶

Bai, H., Aman, A., Xu, Y., Orlovskaya, N., & Zhou, M. (2016). Effects Of Web-Based Interactive Modules On Engineering Students' Learning Motivations. American Journal of Engineering Education, 7(2). https://www.clutejournals.com/index.php/AJEE

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pre/posttest study of senior engineering students (Data 1, N = 122 after listwise deletion) taught with IGLU modules for 25 minutes. The pairwise comparison showed the posttest was "at 2.09 [.32, 3.87] points significantly higher" than the pretest (p = .021).

"the mean of motivation posttest (M = 69.10; SD = 14.13) (see Table 4) was at 2.09 [.32, 3.87] points significantly higher (See Table 7) than the mean of pre-test score (M = 67.01, SD = 13.01)"
