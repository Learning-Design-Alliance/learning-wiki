## Evidence

### Gong C 2025

Gong C, Shen Y, Wang J, Zhang P, Li Z. (2025). Application of ultrasound simulation training in intensive care nursing teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06968-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study of 40 ICU nurses (n = 20 per group) at China-Japan Friendship Hospital; two-tailed independent samples t-test comparing post-training theoretical scores. The simulation group scored "M = 42.2; SD = 2.49" versus "M = 37.8; SD = 4.53" in the control group, p < 0.001.

> "The mean of the theoretical score after the experimental group (M = 42.2; SD = 2.49) is significant compared to that of the control group (M = 37.8; SD = 4.53), t (1,39) = -3.85; p < 0.001."

### Gong C 2025 (2)

Gong C, Shen Y, Wang J, Zhang P, Li Z. (2025). Application of ultrasound simulation training in intensive care nursing teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06968-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group pre-post comparison in the experimental group (n = 20) reported in the Results section: theoretical scores rose from "M = 35.45; SD = 3.46" before to "M = 42.25; SD = 2.49" after training, t (1,19) = -7.140.

> "The mean of the theoretical score after of the experimental group (M = 42.25; SD = 2.49) is significant compared to that of the score before the test, (M = 35.45; SD = 3.46), t (1,19) = -7.140; p < 0, 0001."
