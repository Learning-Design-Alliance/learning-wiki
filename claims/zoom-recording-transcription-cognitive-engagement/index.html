Duncan Culbreth · Florence Martin 2025¶

Duncan Culbreth · Florence Martin. (2025). Exploring the role of synchrony in asynchronous, synchronous, and quasi-synchronous online learner engagement. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10504-y

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Content analysis of essays and modality reflections in the RQ3 findings; the article calls this unexpected use "ultimately bichronous, as students blended different modalities" within one platform.

"several participants cognitively engaged by using Zoom's recording and transcription features; two students had to since they were absent from the synchronous session, but several others (n = 4) attended and still followed up with these asynchronous tools toward better comprehension and completion of their assigned essay."
