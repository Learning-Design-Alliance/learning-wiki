## Evidence

### Horii Satoko 2021

Horii Satoko, Nguyen Chinh Thi Minh, Pham Huong Thi Thu, Amaike Naomi, Ho Hien Thi, Aiga Hirotsugu. (2021). Effectiveness of a standard clinical training program in new graduate nurses’ competencies in Vietnam: A quasi-experimental longitudinal study with a difference-in-differences design. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254238

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Per-competency difference-in-differences analysis across all 25 competencies in Table 4, same 280-nurse sample. Printed effect sizes include d = 0.66 for professional development, d = 0.58 for legal compliance, and d = 0.54 for rapport with patients (intermediate), while the three technical competencies were "indicating no effect" (d = 0.12, 0.17, 0.18).

> "Cohen’s d for “professional development,” “legal compliance,” and “rapport with patients” were 0.66 (95% CI 0.39 to 0.94), 0.58 (95% CI 0.31 to 0.85), and 0.54 (95% CI 0.27 to 0.81), respectively. Cohen’s d for “medical equipment management,” “data collection,” and “proper care delivery” were 0.12 (95% CI -0.15 to 0.39), 0.17 (95% CI -0.09 to 0.44), and 0.18 (95% CI -0.09 to 0.44), respectively, indicating no effect."
