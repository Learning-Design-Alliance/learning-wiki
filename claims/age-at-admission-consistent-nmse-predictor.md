## Evidence

### Shioiri Toshiki 2024

Shioiri Toshiki, Nakashima Michiyo, Tsunekawa Koji. (2024). When can we identify the students at risk of failure in the national medical licensure examination in Japan using the predictive pass rate? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05948-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression across the five time points in the 637-student cohort. Table 3 prints age-at-admission ORs of 0.834 (admission) through 0.863 (6th grade), each with p < 0.05; the text states it showed "a consistently strong influence".

> "Especially, the age at admission showed a consistently strong influence with the likelihood of passing the NMLE."
