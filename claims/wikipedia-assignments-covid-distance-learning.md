### Shani Evenstein Sigalov 2025

Shani Evenstein Sigalov, Piotr Konieczny. (2025). Writing for the greater good: what do educators think about using Wikipedia as a teaching tool? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10505-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Follow-up questionnaire sent in May 2022 to 169 original participants; 52 (31%) responded. Respondents attributed the minimal disruption to assignments being "already predominantly online", requiring the least adjustment compared with other course parts.

> "63.4% of respondents reported either no change (34.6%) or only slight changes (28.8%) in how they taught with Wikipedia during the COVID-19 pandemic. Only 19.3% noted that the pandemic significantly altered their approach"
