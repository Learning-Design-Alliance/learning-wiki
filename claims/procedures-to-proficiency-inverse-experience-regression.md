### Chaiperm Tanchanok 2025

Assessment of Learning Curve for Radiofrequency Ablation in Twin Reversed Arterial Perfusion Sequence: A Simulation Model Study. Prenatal Diagnosis. https://doi.org/10.1002/pd.6801

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Regression analysis across the 12 participants related procedures needed for proficiency to years of experience in invasive prenatal procedures, fitting "the operative point of proficiency = 23.499 + (18.977/years of experience), R 2 = 0.544."

> "The operative point of proficiency was negatively correlated with the participants' years of experience in invasive prenatal testing, best‐fitted to the inverse regression curve: the operative point of proficiency = 23.499 + (18.977/years of experience), R 2 = 0.544 (Figure 4)."
