## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Questions in danmaku regularly received many brief peer replies forming collective answers, and repeated similar needs could create a co-confusion atmosphere; on the discussion board many questions received no or few responses, though answered ones could sustain follow-up rounds. ## Evidence

### Bo Yang 2024

Bo Yang. (2024). Combining Danmaku and Discussion Boards: Toward A Scalable and Sociable Environment for Mass Collaboration in MOOCs. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09426-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis (RQ3) of outstanding interactional behaviors examined excerpts of question-related exchanges. It found danmaku questions "regularly received a number of replies from peers" producing co-answering, while "many questions received no or few responses on the discussion board," creating a feeling of lonely confusion.

> "Danmaku formulated as questions regularly received a number of replies from peers, achieving a “co-answering” phenomenon"
