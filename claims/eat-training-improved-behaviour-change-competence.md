## Evidence

### Beck A.K. 2020

Beck A.K., Baker A.L., Carter G., Wratten C., Bauer J., Wolfenden L., McCarter K., Britton B. (2020). Assessing Adherence, Competence and Differentiation in a Stepped-Wedge Randomised Clinical Trial of a Complex Behaviour Change Intervention. Nutrients 12(8), 2332. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12082332

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Competence rated on the modified CTS-R behaviour change counselling item by blind independent coders of the recorded consultation sample in the stepped-wedge trial. Table 2 reports OR = 4.176 (95% CI 1.905–9.153, p < 0.001) for meeting the Dreyfus competence threshold and β = 0.386 (p < 0.001) for the mean score, described as "significantly greater for sessions conducted after training".

> "Regression analyses confirmed that both the number of sessions attaining competence (35.2 vs. 56.2%) and the mean level of competence (2.21 vs. 2.72) achieved was significantly greater for sessions conducted after training in the EAT intervention"
