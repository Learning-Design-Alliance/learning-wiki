## Evidence

### Johnsson V 2026

Johnsson V, Petersen OB, Svendsen MBS, et al. (2026). To guide or not to guide: A randomized study on the use of needle guide for chorionic villus sampling training. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.70108

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests compared item-level performance between needle-guided and freehand transfer tests. All four items favored the needle guide, e.g. "pre‐puncture ultrasound assessment (mean difference [95% CI] = 0.4 [0.2–0.6]; t[370] = 4.1, p < 0.001)"; sampling technique and overall performance showed mean differences of 0.8 and 0.7 (both t[370] = 5.8, p < 0.001).

> "Sub‐analysis of individual assessment items showed significantly higher scores when using a needle guide across all four items: pre‐puncture ultrasound assessment (mean difference [95% CI] = 0.4 [0.2–0.6]; t[370] = 4.1, p < 0.001)"
