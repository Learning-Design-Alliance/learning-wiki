# An EBP educational program produces a statistically significant intervention-by-time interaction on undergraduate nursing students' EBP knowledge and skills

## Evidence

### Cardoso 2021

Cardoso, D., Couto, F., Cardoso, A.F., Bobrowicz-Campos, E., Santos, L., Rodrigues, R., Coutinho, V., Pinto, D., Ramis, M.-A., Rodrigues, M.A., Apóstolo, J. (2021). The Effectiveness of an Evidence-Based Practice (EBP) Educational Program on Undergraduate Nursing Students’ EBP Knowledge and Skills: A Cluster Randomized Control Trial. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 18(1), 293. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18010293

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized controlled trial of 148 undergraduate nursing students in six optional courses, analyzed by intention-to-treat with two-way mixed ANOVA. The omnibus interaction was significant: "F (1, 146) = 9.550, p = 0.002, partial η2 = 0.061".

> "The two-way mixed ANOVA showed a statistically significant interaction between the intervention and time on EBP knowledge and skills, F (1, 146) = 9.550, p = 0.002, partial η2 = 0.061"
