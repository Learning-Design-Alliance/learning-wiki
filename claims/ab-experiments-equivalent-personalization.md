Conceptual/theoretical statement in the paper's discussion of aligning experimentation and personalization. Versions can be personalized based on any variables in the User Variable Store coming from other MOOClets. The paper states this "aligns a key behavioral science methodology of experimentation with work on intelligent tutoring systems and machine learning." This is an authors' theoretical claim, not an empirical test.
