`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sex-stratified fully adjusted logistic regression for high language grades in the I.Family cohort; the quote names all three girls-specific indicators with their printed ORs and p-values. No such associations were significant for boys.

> "three health indicators are significantly associated with high language achievement in girls but not in boys: lower BMI z-scores (0.78 [0.67, 0.90], p = 0.001), higher healthy diet scores (1.03 [1.01, 1.05], p = 0.004) and better sleep quality (0.57, [0.34, 0.96], p = 0.034"
