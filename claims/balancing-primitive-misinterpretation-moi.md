Case A interview episode with graduate student G1 on unscaffolded pretest Q1; the analysis reports the student misinterpreted local charge density as total charge and used a 'balancing' primitive to place image charges. The authors identify this reasoning as incorrect.

> "G1 was interpreting ‘balancing’ as the process of making the net charge zero by adding the image charges."
