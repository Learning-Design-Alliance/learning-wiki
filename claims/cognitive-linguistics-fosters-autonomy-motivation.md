## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The author proposes that the cognitive-linguistic treatment's key pedagogical implication is learner autonomy: once students understand the nature of particles and their relation to verbs, they can work out phrasal-verb meanings themselves, which the author links to motivation via Dornyei (2014).
