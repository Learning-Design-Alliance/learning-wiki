## Evidence

### Giesbrecht Ed M. 2022

Giesbrecht Ed M., Rushton Paula W., Dubé Evemie. (2022). Wheelchair service provision education in Canadian occupational therapy programs. PLoS ONE 17(2): e0262165. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0262165

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey results on delivery approaches (Table 3) show "Lectures and laboratories with instructors were the most frequent pedagogic approaches" and that content was mostly integrated into mandatory courses rather than offered as full courses.

> "For most programs, wheelchair related content was distributed throughout the curricula as part of mandatory courses. Only a limited number of programs offered wheelchair related content in full mandatory courses or full optional courses. Lectures and laboratories with instructors were the most frequent pedagogic approaches used to deliver wheelchair content."
