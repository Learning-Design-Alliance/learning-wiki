### L. Petitdemange and S. Nashed 2025

L. Petitdemange and S. Nashed. (2025). ENABLING BLIND AND VISUALLY IMPAIRED INDIVIDUALS TO PURSUE CAREERS IN SCIENCE. SF2A 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.17620](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.17620)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' August 2024 pilot summer school for BVI high school students considering higher education in science, with three participants, followed for three months. "All three students successfully integrated into their university courses" and continued using the tools; one participant "now rarely relies on transcription centers" for LaTeX-based work.

> "The program had three participants and focused on teaching command-line usage, LaTeX, and Python, as well as advising on assistive technologies like using a Braille display in a terminal. Over the following three months, we monitored their progress. All three students successfully integrated into their university courses"
