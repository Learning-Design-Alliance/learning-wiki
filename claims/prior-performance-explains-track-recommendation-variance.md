# Students' prior performance explains the large majority of variance in track recommendations, the school leavers' test explains more than reading and mathematics scores for test‐based recommendations, and SES adds only about 0.5%

> **Currency** · newest study 2021 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Prior performance explains a substantial part of variance in both recommendation types (84.38% test‐based; 75.85% judgement‐based). [→ van Leest 2021](#van-leest-2021)
`q2 i?` For test‐based recommendations, the school leavers' test explains a larger part of track recommendations than reading comprehension and mathematics performance. [→ van Leest 2021 (2)](#van-leest-2021-2)

## Evidence

### van Leest 2021

van Leest, A., Hornstra, L., van Tartwijk, J., & van de Pol, J. (2021). Test‐ or judgement‐based school track recommendations: Equal opportunities for students with different socio‐economic backgrounds? The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 91(1), 193–216. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12356

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression models (Model 2) showed 'A substantial part of the differences in track recommendations was explained by students' prior performance', with 84.38% explained for test‐based and 75.85% for judgement‐based recommendations.

> "A substantial part of the differences in track recommendations was explained by students' prior performance (test‐based recommendations: RModel22  = 84.38%; judgement‐based recommendations: RModel22  = 75.85%)."

### van Leest 2021 (2)

van Leest, A., Hornstra, L., van Tartwijk, J., & van de Pol, J. (2021). Test‐ or judgement‐based school track recommendations: Equal opportunities for students with different socio‐economic backgrounds? The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 91(1), 193–216. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12356

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

For the test‐based models, the school leavers' test explained a larger part of track recommendations (82.37%) than reading comprehension and mathematics performance (70.79%).

> "For test‐based recommendations, results indicated that the school leavers' test explained a larger part of track recommendations than students' reading comprehension and mathematics performance (school leavers' test: RModel2a2  = 82.37%; reading comprehension and mathematics performance: RModel2b2  = 70.79%)."

### van Leest 2021 (3)

van Leest, A., Hornstra, L., van Tartwijk, J., & van de Pol, J. (2021). Test‐ or judgement‐based school track recommendations: Equal opportunities for students with different socio‐economic backgrounds? The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 91(1), 193–216. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12356

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In Model 3, 'After accounting for prior performance, SES explained approximately 0.5% of the variance in track recommendations' across both recommendation types.

> "After accounting for prior performance, SES explained approximately 0.5% of the variance in track recommendations (test‐based recommendations: RModel32  = 84.80%; judgement‐based recommendations: RModel32  = 76.41%)."
