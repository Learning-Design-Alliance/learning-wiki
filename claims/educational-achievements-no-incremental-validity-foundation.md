# In multivariable multilevel models, EPM decile and SJT independently predict foundation completion, but educational achievements do not add incremental value

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the cross-classified multilevel multivariable model, EPM decile (OR 1.140) and SJT z-score (OR 1.183) were significant predictors, while educational achievements was not independently significant (OR 0.998, p=0.944). [→ Smith Daniel T 2018](#smith-daniel-t-2018)
`q3 i?` Converting SJT to deciles, one decile increase independently raised odds of outcome 6 by 5% (p=0.02) versus 14% for EPM; educational achievements remained non-significant (OR 0.995, p=0.867). [→ Smith Daniel T 2018 (2)](#smith-daniel-t-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Smith Daniel T 2018

Smith Daniel T, Tiffin Paul A. (2018). Evaluating the validity of the selection measures used for the UK’s foundation medical training programme: a national cohort study. BMJ Open 2018;8:e021918. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-021918

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel multivariable logistic regression (model 1) in the UKMED cohort, with trainees cross-classified within medical schools and foundation schools. The article reports that "The education achievement measure is not independently and statistically significantly associated with successful completion when all three measures are included in a model." Table 3 prints OR 1.140 for EPM decile and 1.183 for SJT z-score.

> "In terms of interpreting the results, for an increase of one EPM the odds of successful completion of the foundation programme increased by approximately 14%. Similarly, for an increase of 1 SD on the SJT z-score the odds of an outcome 6 increased by approximately 18%."

### Smith Daniel T 2018 (2)

Smith Daniel T, Tiffin Paul A. (2018). Evaluating the validity of the selection measures used for the UK’s foundation medical training programme: a national cohort study. BMJ Open 2018;8:e021918. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-021918

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model 2 of the same multilevel analysis, with SJT z-score converted to within-year decile ranks to allow direct comparison of adjusted ORs. Table 3 prints OR 1.141 for EPM decile, 1.049 for SJT z-score decile, and 0.995 (p=0.867) for educational achievements.

> "As before, one decile increase in the EPM increases the odds of an outcome 6 by 14%; one decile increase in the rank of the SJT z-score independently increases the chance of an outcome 6 by 5% (p=0.02)."
