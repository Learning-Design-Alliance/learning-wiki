Julia Lademann, Jannik Henze, and Sebastian Becker-Genschow. (2024). Building Bridges – AI Custom Chatbots as Mediators between Mathematics and Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.15747](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.15747)

In the randomized controlled study, situational interest was measured with four items after engagement with the materials. The EG scored higher than the CG, with a printed effect size of d = 0.45 ("small effect size").

> "The measured situational interest was significantly higher in the EG ( 𝑝𝑝 =  0. 00223, 𝑑𝑑 =  0. 45, small effect size) than in the CG."
