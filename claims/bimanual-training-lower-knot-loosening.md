`q3 i?` Knots tied by the bimanual group loosened less on average than knots tied by the dominant-hand group (p = 0.011). [→ Asencio FA 2018](#asencio-fa-2018)

## Evidence

### Asencio FA 2018

Asencio FA, Ribeiro HASA, Romeo A, Wattiez A, Ribeiro PAGA. (2018). The Effect on Performance Time and Quality of the Knots after Mono or Bimanual Training of Laparoscopic Intracorporeal Knot Tying according to the Gladiator Rule Technique. Rev Bras Ginecol Obstet. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0038-1649494

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Knot-quality evaluation at an engineering laboratory using a pachymeter and dynamometer traction up to 17 N on knots from the 41-resident training study; the comparison of loosening averages between groups A and B was statistically significant (p = 0.011).

> "A statistically significant difference was also identified when comparing the knot loosening average in mm; group A had an average of 3.27 mm of loosening, and group B, 2.36 mm."
