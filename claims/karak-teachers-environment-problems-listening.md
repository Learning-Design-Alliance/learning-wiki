## Evidence

### Alrawashdeh 2017

Alrawashdeh, A. I. & Al-zayed, N. N. (2017). Difficulties That English Teachers Encounter while Teaching Listening Comprehension and Their Attitudes towards Them. English Language Teaching, 10(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n5p167

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Questionnaire domain analysis of 55 randomly chosen Karak English teachers. The teaching environment domain had an overall mean of 3.32 (medium); the highest-ranked item was "Electricity supplies are not available" and the lowest "The number of students in classes is suitable".

> "It can be noticed from Table 3, which is related to “Teaching learning environment”.The highest rank was for “Electricity supplies are not available”; the results indicate the lowest level was for “The number of students in classes is suitable”, with a mean of (1.91) and standard deviation of (1.01)."
