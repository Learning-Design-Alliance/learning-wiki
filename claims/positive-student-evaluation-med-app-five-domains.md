## Evidence

### niromand Elham 2024

niromand Elham, Mansoory Meysam Siyah, Ramezani Ghobad, Khazaei Mohammad Rasool. (2024). Design, implementation and evaluation of e-learning program for common diseases to smartphone-based medical students: at a developing university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-05023-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A 25-question questionnaire across five domains (satisfaction, efficiency, learning, usability, scientific-educational) on a 1–5 Likert scale was used; Table 2 prints domain means such as satisfaction 4.48 ± 0.32 (training) and an overall score of 4.1 ± 0.32 (training) and 4.13 ± 0.28 (internship).

> "The questionnaires were disseminated among a total of 100 interns and trainees for evaluating the application, and 92 students diligently completed and returned them. Within the trainee student group, the highest scores were achieved in the following order: satisfaction, usability, learning, efficiency, and educational quality, with an overall score surpassing 4"
