# The hybrid viva-voce format demonstrated higher inter-examiner consistency, correlation, and reliability (Cronbach's α = 0.663) than the structured and traditional formats

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment
> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` Among 151 first-year medical students assessed by two examiners across three viva formats, the hybrid format showed higher correlation coefficients and reliability than structured and traditional formats, with only its Cronbach's α exceeding the 0.7 threshold. [→ Rasalkar Kavita 2025](#rasalkar-kavita-2025)

`q2 i?` The Wilcoxon signed rank test revealed significant inter-examiner variability in structured viva-voce and Set 2 of traditional viva-voce (p < 0.05), while the hybrid method showed better consistency between examiners. [→ Rasalkar Kavita 2025](#rasalkar-kavita-2025)

Reliability analysis (Cronbach's α and ICC) of paired scores from two examiners for 151 phase 1 MBBS students in a comparative observational cross-sectional study. The article reports "low reliability of 0.595 for structured viva-voce and moderate reliability for traditional (0.626) and hybrid viva-voce method (0.663)". No standardized effect size is printed for the reliability contrast, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Only hybrid viva-voce Cronbach α reached the threshold value of > 0.7. In comparison, the hybrid viva-voce consistently demonstrated higher correlation coefficients and reliability than the structured and traditional formats"
