## Evidence

### Festl-Wietek T 2026

Festl-Wietek T, Schröpel C, Holderried F, Herrmann B, Griewatz J, Ehehalt S, Junne F, Zipfel S, Herrmann-Werner A, Erschens R. (2026). Communication-Based Teaching on Childhood Obesity and the Planetary Health Diet in Medical Education: Proof-of-Concept Study Comparing 4 Information Sources. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/92644

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive scoring of group-task responses across 13 courses; additional correct aspects beyond the sample solution were awarded 0.5 points each. The article reports the ChatGPT and Google groups "provided 4.03 units of additional correct information per question and course".

> "On average, the ChatGPT and Google groups provided 4.03 units of additional correct information per question and course, whereas the paper group (mean 1.88, SD 1.0) and the video group (mean 1.42) provided fewer additional correct information units."
