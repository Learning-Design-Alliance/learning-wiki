David Racovan 2026¶

David Racovan, Ajay Rawat, Christopher K. May, Jeffrey A. Turkstra. (2026). Argus: Academic Integrity in the Era of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36073

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal observational analysis of six offerings comparing averaged homework H-scores against closed-note, proctored exam percentages per student; the text reports a negative relationship emerging in Spring 2024 and strengthening through "Spring 2026 (𝑟=− 0.537)". Spring 2020 was limited because a project replaced exams beyond Midterm 1.

"Beginning in Spring 2024, a negative relationship begins to emerge (𝑟=− 0.244), and strengthens in Spring 2025 (𝑟=− 0.450) and Spring 2026 (𝑟=− 0.537)."
