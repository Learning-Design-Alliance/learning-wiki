## Evidence

### Maizel Julien 2014

Maizel Julien, Guyomarc'h Laurianne, Henon Pierre, Modeliar Santhi Samy, de Cagny Bertrand, Choukroun Gabriel, Slama Michel. (2014). Residents learning ultrasound-guided catheterization are not sufficiently skilled to use landmarks. Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/cc13741

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective observational study of 172 procedures (84 UG, 88 LM) performed by eight inexperienced residents, analyzed with generalized estimating equations. The comparison showed "a lower success rate (72% versus 84%; P = 0.01)" and higher complications for LM; no effect size was printed.

> "LM procedures were associated with a lower success rate (72% versus 84%; P = 0.01), a higher complication rate (22% versus 10%; P = 0.01) and a higher mean number of attempts (1.8 ± 0.9 versus 1.6 ± 0.8; P = 0.001) compared to the UG technique"
