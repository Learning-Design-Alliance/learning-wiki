## Evidence

### Baric 2019

Baric, V., Andreassen, M., Öhman, A., & Hemmingsson, H. (2019). Using an interactive digital calendar with mobile phone reminders by senior people - a focus group study. BMC Geriatrics, 19, 116. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-019-1128-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group study with 20 community-dwelling seniors (11 men, 9 women; mean age 73) who used RemindMe for six weeks, analysed by content analysis. Participants "described RemindMe as easy to use" while integration into routines was difficult, partly because new mobile/smartphone routines were required.

> "Although the participants described RemindMe as easy to use, they had a difficult time incorporating RemindMe into their daily routines. In part, these difficulties were the result of the participants needing to change their mobile/smartphone routines."
