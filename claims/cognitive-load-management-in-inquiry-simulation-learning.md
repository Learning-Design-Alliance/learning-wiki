The article cites Perez et al. (2017), a sequence-mining study of inquiry-based learning with simulations of electrical circuits. Successful low-prior-knowledge students "chose to explore simple circuits first" and "introduced pauses in their learning," patterns absent in unsuccessful learners. No effect size is printed.

> "In this study, low-prior-knowledge students who were more successful in learning chose to explore simple circuits first, whereas this pattern was not observed in similar students who did not learn successfully. Also, the successful students introduced pauses in their learning, which was not observed in the less successful learners."
