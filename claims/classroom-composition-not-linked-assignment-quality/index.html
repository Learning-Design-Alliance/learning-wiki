Fred M. Newmann 2001¶

Fred M. Newmann, Anthony S. Bryk, Jenny K. Nagaoka. (2001). Authentic Intellectual Work and Standardized Tests: Conflict or Coexistence? Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/authentic-intellectual-work-and-standardized-tests-conflict-or-coexistence

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Classroom-level correlational analysis across the fieldwork sample of 437 classes in 19 schools (Table 1). The authors report "weak, statistically insignificant correlations" between assignment quality and composition variables; the printed coefficients (e.g., 0.06, 0.09) are small and no significance tests are quoted here.

"Across the fieldwork sample of 437 classes in 19 schools, we found only weak, statistically insignificant correlations between the quality of teach- ers' assignments and the racial or socioeconomic composition of their class- rooms and the level of students' prior achievement."
