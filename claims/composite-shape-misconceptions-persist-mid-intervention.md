## Evidence

### Eunhye Flavin 2025

Flavin, E., Chung, M., Hwang, S., & Flavin, M. T. (2025). Augmented reality for area measurement reasoning of elementary students. *Educational Technology Research and Development, 73*(4), 2663–2697. [doi:10.1007/s11423-025-10502-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10502-0)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of written Test 2 responses in the multiple-case study: although Myrlène and Daisy correctly solved basic shape problems, both answered the composite L-shape problem incorrectly; the authors say students "exhibited misconceptions when faced with composite shape problems".

> "Myrlène calculated the perimeter of an L-shape (rather than an area) by summing all the side lengths (6 + 2 + 1 + 1). Daisy multiplied all the side lengths (6 × 2 × 9 × 7)."
