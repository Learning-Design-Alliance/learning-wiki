### Mileder LP 2021

Mileder LP, Bereiter M, Wegscheider T. (2021). Telesimulation as a modality for neonatal resuscitation training. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.1892017

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Between-group comparisons in the same pilot study using Student's t-test or Mann-Whitney-U-test found "no differences in pre- and post-test knowledge scores between medical students and neonatal nurses" (p = 0.427 and p = 0.418).
