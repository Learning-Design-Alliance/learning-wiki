### Baldwin 1911

Baldwin, B. T. (1911). Educational Psychology at the Cleveland Meeting of the American Psychological Association. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Summary of Baldwin's comparative learning study of adolescent delinquent white and negro girls using a wigwag code for "sixteen consecutive days"; the "76 practice curves show a wide range of individual differences and significant periods of acceleration and retrogression," and the negro girls as a group accomplished less work and were less accurate. No effect size is printed.

> "B. T. Baldwin's paper is a comparative study of The progress of learning of adolescent delinquent white and negro girls as shown by a wigwag code adapted for this purpose and applied for sixteen consecutive days. He finds this type of material furnishes a very reliable means for evaluating learning capacity."
