Paired t-test reported in Table (4) for the 19-student control group, which received textbook instruction only. No effect size is printed for this null finding; equivalence was not tested.

> "a paired t-test performed on the mean scores of the pre-test versus the post-test for the control group indicates no significant differences regarding the five aspects of essay writing: point of view (0.701), unity and coherence (0.832), development (0.747), organization (0.193), and thinking (0.652). The overall p-value was 0.565."
