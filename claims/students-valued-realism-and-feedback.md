## Evidence

### Shenai Neeta MD 2024

Shenai Neeta MD, Fulmer Valerie, Gowl Catherine MFA, See Jordan MD, Peterson Ryan MD, Van Deusen Reed MD. (2024). A Verbal De-escalation Standardized Patient Workshop for Third- and Fourth-Year Medical Students. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11417

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Open-ended evaluation-form responses from clerkship students. Students cited a "realistic situation" that "forced you to react fast," valued "Real time feedback," and appreciated serving as observers, which felt like "having a supporter in your corner while in the room."

> "In their narrative feedback regarding the best aspects of the workshop, students highlighted the relevance to real life de-escalation situations"
