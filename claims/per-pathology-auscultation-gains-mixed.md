## Evidence

### Tokuda Yasuharu 2020

Tokuda Yasuharu, Matayoshi Tetsutaro, Nakama Yasunori, Kurihara Masaru, Suzuki Tomoharu, Kitahara Yusuke, Kitai Yuya, Nakamura Takashi, Itokazu David, Miyazato Tatsuya. (2020). Per-pathology McNemar comparisons in the same before-and-after study of 50 junior doctors (Table 3). The quote names the three significant pathologies with printed percentages; Table 3 shows non-significant p-values for S3, AS, AR and MR, so strength is mixed.

> "The improvement was statistically significant for double gallop (5/50 [10%] vs. 15/50 [30%]), mitral stenosis (0/50 [0%] vs. 6/50 [12%]), and pericardial friction rub (1/50 [2%] vs. 40/50 [80%])."
