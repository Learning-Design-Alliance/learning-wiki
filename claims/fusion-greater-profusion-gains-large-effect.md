## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ben Clarke 2014

Ben Clarke, Christian T. Doabler, Mari Strand Cary, Derek Kosty, Scott Baker, Hank Fien, and Keith Smolkowski. (2014). Preliminary Evaluation of a Tier 2 Mathematics Intervention for First-Grade Students: Using a Theory of Change to Guide Formative Evaluation Activities. School Psychology Review, 43(2), 160-177. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567775

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pilot randomized block trial in nine schools: 89 at-risk first graders randomly assigned to Fusion (n = 44) or standard district practice (n = 45); groups did not significantly differ on any demographic characteristics or pretest outcome measures. The Condition-by-Time interaction showed "p = .015, Hedges's g = 0.82".

> "The treatment group had statistically significantly greater gains on our proximal measure of conceptual understanding, ProFu- sion, compared with control participants (esti- mate = 12.9, p = .015, Hedges's g = 0.82), corresponding to a large effect."

### Clarke 2014

Clarke, B., Doabler, C. T., Strand Cary, M., Kosty, D., Baker, S., Fien, H., & Smolkowski, K. (2014). Preliminary Evaluation of a Tier 2 Mathematics Intervention for First-Grade Students. School Psychology Review, 43(2), 160-177. https://www.nasponline.org/journals/spr

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized block pilot trial in nine schools: 89 at-risk first graders randomly assigned to Fusion (n = 44) or standard district practice (n = 45); random-effects models compared Condition-by-Time gains. The article reports a large effect on ProFusion with Hedges's g = 0.82 (p = .015).

> "The treatment group had statistically signiﬁcantly greater gains on our proximal measure of conceptual understanding, ProFu- sion, compared with control participants (esti- mate /H1100512.9, p /H11005.015, Hedges's g /H110050.82), corresponding to a large effect."

### Strand Cary 2017

Strand Cary, M. G., Clarke, B., Doabler, C. T., Smolkowski, K., Fien, H., & Baker, S. K. (2017). A practitioner implementation of a Tier 2 first grade mathematics intervention. Learning Disability Quarterly, 40(4), 211–224. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714715

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

The article reports the concurrent formal pilot study, in which 89 at-risk first graders were randomly assigned to Fusion (n = 44) or control (n = 45), with lessons delivered by research team-trained district interventionists. The review attributes the "estimate = 12.9, p = .015, Hedges's g = 0.82" result to Clarke et al. (2014).

> "gain scores of intervention students were significantly greater than their control peers on a proximal measure of conceptual understanding (estimate = 12.9, p = .015, Hedges's g = 0.82, corresponding to a large effect)"
