Evidence¶

Bayat et al. 2017¶

Abbas Bayat, Ahmadreza Jamshidipour, Masoud Hashemi. (2017). THE BENEFICIAL IMPACTS OF APPLYING FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT ON IRANIAN UNIVERSITY STUDENTS' ANXIETY REDUCTION AND LISTENING EFFICACY. International Journal of Languages' Education and Teaching, 5(2). https://doi.org/10.18298/ijlet.1740

q1 · i? · causal · r1

This is a conceptual statement in the article's significance-of-the-study section, citing Bailey (1996) and Alderson and Wall (1993): positive wash back occurs when "teachers use tests to get their students to pay more attention to lessons", while fear of poor results may lead to teaching to the test. The article offers this as background argument, not tested data.

"The influence of testing on teaching and learning is called wash back effect (Bailey, 1996)."
