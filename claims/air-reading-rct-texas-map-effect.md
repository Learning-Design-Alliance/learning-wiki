### Air Reading 2025

Air Reading. (2025). Air Reading program description and outcomes review. https://www.evidenceforlearning.org.au

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

A randomized study of nearly 400 grade 1-6 students in six rural Texas elementary schools during spring 2023-2024; students needing reading intervention were stratified by scheduling slots and randomly assigned to Air Reading or business-as-usual instruction, yielding an "effect size = +0.12" on NWEA MAP reading.

> "students who participated in Air Reading scored significantly higher on the NWEA MAP reading assessment than their peers in the control group (effect size = +0.12)"
