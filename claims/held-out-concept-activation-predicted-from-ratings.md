## Evidence

### Robert A. Mason 2021

Robert A. Mason, Reinhard A. Schumacher and Marcel Adam Just. (2021). The neuroscience of advanced scientific concepts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00107-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Predictive-modeling analysis using fMRI data from 7 of 10 participants, with factor locations established from the other 3. The regression mapping from mean expert ratings of 44 concepts to activation in 30 cluster locations predicted the held-out concept; mean normalized rank accuracy across the seven participants was 0.70 (p < 0.001 by permutation test), and 0.69 with all ten participants.

> "the model had a good fit to the data as indicated by a mean R2 of 0.82 (averaged over the 45 predictions and seven test participants, with a standard deviation of 0.11"
