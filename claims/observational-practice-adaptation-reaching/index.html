Evidence¶

Julian Rudisch 2024¶

Julian Rudisch, Luis K. H. Holzhauer, Karmen Kravanja, Fred H. Hamker & Claudia Voelcker-Rehage. (2024). A systematic review of observational practice for adaptation of reaching movements. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00271-5

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r?

Systematic review pooling 18 studies of reaching adaptation (1029 young adults; RCT or quasi-experimental designs). Pooled effect "d = −2.16" versus no practice, "indicating an error reduction as compared to a control group." Certainty of evidence rated moderate for young adult populations.

"Results of the studies showed that observational practice led to adaptation in both, visuomotor rotation and force- field paradigms ( d = −2.16 as compared to no practice)."
