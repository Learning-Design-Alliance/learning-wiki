## Evidence

### Wayne Harrison 2026

Wayne Harrison, Rahil Khowaja, Emma Dobson, Germaine Uwimpuhwe, and Steve Higgins. (2026). Evaluating AI Tutoring at the Speed of Innovation: Practitioner-Led Micro-Randomised Trials of an AI Tutoring Platform in GCSE Science. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14789

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Threshold-based estimates from the original evaluation, re-examined in this paper. The authors state the pattern "is consistent with the hypothesis that effective use matters" but that conditioning on post-randomisation engagement does not identify a causal complier effect.

> "The original evaluation additionally reported progressively larger treatment estimates after restricting the intervention group to students crossing thresholds of 10, 20 and 30 questions."
