## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On a 0–10 VAS survey, students rated RP as a useful tool (mean 9.0, SD 0.1), critical-thinking enhancement (mean 8.4, SD 0.2), preparation (mean 8.2, SD 0.2), and enjoyment (mean 8.5, SD 0.2). [→ Tawanwongsri W 2019](#tawanwongsri-w-2019)

## Evidence

### Tawanwongsri W 2019

Tawanwongsri W, Phenwan T. (2019). Reflective and feedback performances on Thai medical students’ patient history-taking skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1585-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymised post-course survey of all 48 students using a 0–10 visual analogue scale with an extra comment section. The article prints the means and SDs above plus enjoyment at 8.5 ± 0.2; these are descriptive self-report ratings with no inferential test.

> "The students agreed with a usefulness of reflective practice with the mean score of 9.0 ± 0.1. They rated their level of agreement on the topics ‘reflective practice helps me prepare my studies in advance effectively’ and ‘reflective practice enhances my critical thinking skills based on the clinical reasoning’ with a mean score of 8.2 ± 0.2 and 8.4 ± 0.2, respectively."
