## Evidence

### E. Yerushalmi 2008

E. Yerushalmi, A. Mason, E. Cohen, C. Singh. (2008). Effect of Self Diagnosis on Subsequent Problem Solving Performance. AIP Conf. Proc. 1064. [doi:10.1063/1.3021272](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3021272)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

An unpublished independent categorization study in which introductory-level students grouped 25 problems by similarity of material; the quiz and midterm problems were among them, e.g. the tire-swing problem placed in a pendulum or tension category.

> "Our research shows that students did not understand the similarity of the two problems and often placed them in different, mutually exclusive categories."
