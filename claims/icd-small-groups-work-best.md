## Evidence

### M. Hütten 2017

M. Hütten, T. Karg, C. Schwerdt, C. Steppa and M. Walter. (2017). The International Cosmic Day – An Outreach Event for Astroparticle Physics. 35th International Cosmic Ray Conference — ICRC2017. [doi:10.22323/1.301.0405](https://doi.org/10.22323/1.301.0405)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Organizers' experiential report in the Facts and Figures section, not a controlled comparison; it states the event "works best in smaller groups with 20 or less students". No effect sizes or tests are printed.

> "We experienced that the event works best in smaller groups with 20 or less students."

### M. Hütten 2017 (2)

M. Hütten, T. Karg, C. Schwerdt, C. Steppa and M. Walter. (2017). The International Cosmic Day – An Outreach Event for Astroparticle Physics. 35th International Cosmic Ray Conference — ICRC2017. [doi:10.22323/1.301.0405](https://doi.org/10.22323/1.301.0405)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The same experiential section reports a detector-sharing limit: "not more than six students should share a detector for autonomous experimenting", with teacher-demonstrated experiments recommended for larger groups.

> "Larger group sizes per detector are still feasible, however, experience showed that not more than six students should share a detector for autonomous experimenting, and experiments should be demonstrated by the teacher in larger groups."
