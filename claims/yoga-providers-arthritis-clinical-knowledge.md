### Biswas I 2025

Biswas I, Adebusoye B, Lewis S, Chattopadhyay K (2025). Clinical knowledge, experiences, and perceptions of yoga providers in arthritis treatment: a UK-based qualitative study. Rheumatology International 45(4):88. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00296-025-05843-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 20 UK yoga providers found they were "fairly knowledgeable about arthritis including its types, signs and symptoms, causes". Providers gathered health information via questionnaires and discussion at first attendance.

> "Yoga providers were fairly knowledgeable about arthritis including its types, signs and symptoms, causes, and mechanisms contributing to joint damage in major types"
