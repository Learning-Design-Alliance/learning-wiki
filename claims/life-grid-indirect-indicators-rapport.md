`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

The authors' qualitative observation across both implementations: "the use of indirect indicators facilitated rapport building exchanges for a majority of students," with respondents expressing happy surprise at the completed grid and interviewers finding commonalities through indirect-indicator discussion. No effect size is reported.

> "Overall, despite slightly different approaches, the use of indirect indicators facilitated rapport building exchanges for a majority of students in both the physics and biology study."
