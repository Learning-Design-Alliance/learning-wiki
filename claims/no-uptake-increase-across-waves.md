`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Quantitative uptake assessment across three sequential waves of two trusts each, with the implementation plan revised between waves (booklets, email nudges, auditing dashboard, self-referral button, REACT Champion role). The article reports no evidence of increase and hedges that the 18-month timeframe may have been too limited.

> "There was no evidence of an increase in implementation outcomes across the 3 waves, or as a result of the evolving implementation plan, though the timeframe of this analysis may have been too limited to adequately assess this."
