## Evidence

### Ishihara 2020

Ishihara, T., Nakajima, T., Yamatsu, K., Okita, K., Sagawa, M. & Morita, N. (2020). Longitudinal relationship of favorable weight change to academic performance in children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0063-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Per-subject simple-slope results from the main analysis in 197 children. The article describes the pattern as "robustly found across academic subjects", but the printed values show Social studies did not reach significance (p = 0.11), so the claim is coded mixed. Impact coded from printed β = −0.28 for Japanese.
