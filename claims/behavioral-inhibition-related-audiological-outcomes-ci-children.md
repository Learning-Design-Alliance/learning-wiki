Horn, D. L., Davis, R. A. O., Pisoni, D. B., & Miyamoto, R. T. (2004). Visual attention, behavioral inhibition and speech/language outcomes in deaf children with cochlear implants. Int Congr Ser. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ics.2004.07.048

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' overall conclusion from the retrospective correlational analysis of 47 children tested on the dCPT delay task against audiological outcomes collapsed into intervals of 1, 2, and 3 years of CI use; the support is the pattern of significant bivariate and partial correlations reported in Results.
