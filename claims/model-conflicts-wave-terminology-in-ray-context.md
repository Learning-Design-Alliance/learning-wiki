### Martin Erik Horn 2004

Martin Erik Horn, Helmut F. Mikelskis. (2004). Concept Mapping as an Instrument for Evaluating an Instruction Unit on Holography. ESERA Conference, Noordwijkerhout. Originally published 2003. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0411081](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0411081)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative finding from the student interviews conducted alongside the pre-post evaluation. The article reports "cognitive conflicts exist between the models" because students mixed wave-model terminology into ray-model contexts; most students correctly pinpointed experimental features.

> "During student interviews it became evident that cognitive conflicts exist between the models, since many participating students used the terminology of the wave model within the context of the ray model."
