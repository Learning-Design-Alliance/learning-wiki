## Evidence

### Marinelli CV 2021

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Accuracy ANOVA comparing A1, A20, and B1 in the same sample: "errors decreased from 2.52% in A1 to 1.61% in A20" (p = 0.09), rising to 3.14% in B1 (p < 0.001 vs A20); a separate learning effect across 20 presentations was also significant.

> "errors decreased from 2.52% in A1 to 1.61% in A20 ( p = 0.09) for increasing again to 3.14% in the B1 matrix (with respect to the A20 matrix, p < 0.001); accuracy in performing the B1 matrix was not significantly different from the A1 matrix"
