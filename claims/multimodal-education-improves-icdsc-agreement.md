## Evidence

### R.‐L. Chang 2025

R.‐L. Chang, S.‐F. Siao, S.‐C. Ku, Y.‐C. Yeh, Y.‐C. Chang, and C. C.‐H. Chen (2025). Improving Intensive Care Unit Nurses' Delirium Assessment Performance Through a Multimodal Educational Intervention. Nursing in Critical Care 30(5): e70168. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70168

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre–post intervention study in three medical ICUs (847 paired assessments: 420 pre, 427 post) comparing bedside nurses' ICDSC ratings with a blinded expert nurse. The reported improvement in "the overall percentage agreement rose from 76% to 82%" was tested with z-tests (p = 0.04).

> "Cohen's kappa value increased from 0.63 (95% CI: 0.57–0.70) to 0.71 (95% CI: 0.66–0.77; p = 0.04), whereas the overall percentage agreement rose from 76% to 82%"

### R.‐L. Chang 2025 (2)

R.‐L. Chang, S.‐F. Siao, S.‐C. Ku, Y.‐C. Yeh, Y.‐C. Chang, and C. C.‐H. Chen (2025). Improving Intensive Care Unit Nurses' Delirium Assessment Performance Through a Multimodal Educational Intervention. Nursing in Critical Care 30(5): e70168. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70168

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline phase of the same paired-assessment audit: 420 pre-intervention pairs showed "a Cohen's kappa value of 0.63 (95% CI: 0.57–0.70)" and 76% overall agreement, establishing the pre-intervention performance level.

> "the ICU nurse performance of delirium assessment before the intervention showed only moderate consistency, with a Cohen's kappa value of 0.63 (95% CI: 0.57–0.70), and overall agreement of 76%"
