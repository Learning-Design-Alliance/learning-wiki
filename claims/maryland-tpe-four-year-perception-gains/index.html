Evidence¶

Slotnik 2016¶

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2016). Stay the Course: Teacher and Principal Evaluation in Maryland. WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED589981

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Cross-sectional survey analysis of four annual administrations (2013-2016, nearly 60,000 responses total), with Mann-Whitney U tests of year-to-year differences. The report finds "a year-by-year increase" in agreement on all tracked items, marked significant at the 95% confidence level.

"For every survey item, there has been a year-by-year increase in the percent of principals and teachers who agree with the statements on "Systemic Context," the "Quality of TPE Frameworks and Processes," and "Student Learning Objectives.""
