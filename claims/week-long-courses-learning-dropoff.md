The author's lesson from the original 1998 week-long LANL course, which ran nine times over four years. This is the author's experiential interpretation rather than a measured result; no effect size is printed.

> "Intensive week-long courses are easy to schedule (particularly if instructors are travelling) but by the last two days, attendees' brains are full and learning drops off significantly."
