Tesfai FM 2024¶

Tesfai FM, Nagi J, Morrison I, Boal M, Olaitan A, Chandrasekaran D, Stoyanov D, Lanceley A, Francis N. (2024). Objective assessment tools in laparoscopic or robotic‐assisted gynecological surgery: A systematic review. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14840

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review synthesis of validity evidence by tool category. For APMs the review reports "Nine out of 11 (81.8%) APM showed construct or criterion validity"; it similarly reports 10/13 global tools and 9/13 procedure-specific tool studies showing construct or concurrent validity, while predictive validity was absent.
