Vy Le, Jayson M. Nissen, Jason W. Morphew, Hua Hua Chang, and Ben Van Dusen. (2026). Mechanics Cognitive Diagnostic: Testing Fine-Grained Learning Objectives in Introductory Physics. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09584 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.09584

q2 · i? · design · r2

Comparison with MCD-v1, which applied DINA to the same three RBAs with four broad skills. "The 14-LO structure improved fit for all three RBAs," with FMCE remaining the only marginal-fit assessment under both structures.

"The present 14-LO structure improved fit for all three RBAs: FCI RMSEA2 fell from 0.048 to 0.033, EMCS RMSEA2 fell from 0.028 to 0.022, and FMCE RMSEA2 fell from 0.090 to 0.065."
