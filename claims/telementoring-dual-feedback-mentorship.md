`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative finding from the same thematic analysis, drawing on interviews, observations and field notes across both DBR cycles. Early feedback focused on guiding probe handling, with confusion when terms were interpreted differently; over time participants learned that deliberately "using the same words – tilting, rocking, sliding, rotating" with precise indications and manipulations were important investments in learning, contributing to mentorship that helped build a relationship of trust.

> "engaging with psychomotor feedback via telementoring was hampered by communicative challenges and required a dynamic relationship between teacher and trainee with dual feedback roles: developing a shared understanding of psychomotor instruction and supportive mentorship."
