## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Boonyapison 2025

Boonyapison, K., Sittironnarit, G., & Rattanaumpawan, P. (2025). Association between the big five personalities and academic performance among grade 12 students at international high school in Thailand. Scientific Reports. [doi:10.1038/s41598-025-01038-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-01038-7)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable analysis in the questionnaire study identified "a higher BMI (0.90 [0.81–0.99; p-value = .03])" and extensive physical activity as negative factors. The authors note self-study and physical activity may be negatively correlated, so only physical activity entered the model.

> "Furthermore, a higher BMI (0.90 [0.81–0.99; p-value = .03]) and having extensive physical activities (0.30 [0.09–0.99; p-value = .05]) were identified as the negatively associated factors for academic achievement."
