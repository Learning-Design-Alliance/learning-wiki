### J.R. Persson 2015

J.R. Persson. (2015). Evaluating the Force Concept Inventory for diﬀerent student groups at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1504.06099](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1504.06099)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-test reliability analysis of FCI pre- and post-test data from three NTNU course groups (TFY4104, TFY4115, TFY4145) in Fall 2012. The article reports that the obtained Kuder-Richardson indices are "all over 0.8 (Table 4)", a level above the 0.7 threshold for group measurements and over 0.8 for assessing individuals.

> "In this study the obta ined Kuder-Richardson reliability indices are all over 0.8 (Table 4).Somethin g that also open up for individual assessment."
