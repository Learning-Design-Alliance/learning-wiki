## Evidence

### Kramer A 2025

Kramer A, Foley J, Hansen C, Teramoto M. (2025). Parent‐reported academic outcomes after a mild traumatic brain injury in the pediatric population. J Sch Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/josh.13502

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the cross-sectional survey of 285 parents (16.0% response rate out of 1777); before injury 43.2% had A grades versus 34.4% after injury. Overall, "significantly more negative grade changes than positive grade changes (p < .001)". No effect size printed.

> "The majority of children (55.4%; n = 158) were reported to have had no grade change after injury, while 28.1% (n = 80) reported negative grade changes by at least 1 letter grade"
