# ISHS attendance increases the odds of economically disadvantaged students entering a Texas four-year college by 75 to 80 percent, with no significant college-entry effect for students overall

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Economically disadvantaged ISHS students had 75% and 80% higher odds of entering a Texas four-year college in fall 2014 or ever by spring 2016 (p < .05). [→ Means 2018](#means-2018)
`q2 i?` After model adjustment, there was no significant difference in four-year college entrance between ISHS and comparison students overall, nor for Hispanic and female students. [→ Means 2018 (2)](#means-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Means 2018

Means, Wang, Wei, Iwatani, & Peters. (2018). Broadening Participation in STEM College Majors: Effects of Attending a STEM-Focused High School. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858418806305

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Propensity score-weighted HLM on Texas higher education enrollment records (Table 3; economically disadvantaged n = 548 ISHS / 1,294 comparison). The printed odds increases of 75% and 80% are percentages, not standardized effect sizes, so impact is null.

> "The odds of their entering a Texas four-year college the fall after high school or any time from 2014 to spring 2016 are increased by 75% and 80%, respectively, for economically disadvantaged students who went to an ISHS (p < .05)."

### Means 2018 (2)

Means, Wang, Wei, Iwatani, & Peters. (2018). Broadening Participation in STEM College Majors: Effects of Attending a STEM-Focused High School. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858418806305

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same HLM analysis of college enrollment for all students (n = 867 ISHS / 1,997 comparison). The raw mean advantage (.41 vs .31) was "no significant difference" after adjustment; no effect size is printed.

> "after adjusting for the many demographic, prior achievement, and school charac- teristics in the analytic model, there is no significant difference."
