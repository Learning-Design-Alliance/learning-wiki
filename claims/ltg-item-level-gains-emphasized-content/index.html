Evidence¶

Seago 2013¶

Seago, N. (2013). Supporting Teachers' and Students' Knowledge of Geometric Similarity. Proceedings of the 35th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED584497

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory item-level comparison of percent-correct gains for treatment versus comparison students on the 20-item student assessment; no effect size is printed for this analysis. The 11 favored items "included all 3 items targeting dilation, 3 of the 4 items targeting congruence transformations, and both items targeting properties of similar figures."

"For 11 of the 20 items, the gain in the percent of students answering the item correctly favored the treatment group by 8 percentage points or more. None of the gains favored the comparison group by more than 5 percentage points."
