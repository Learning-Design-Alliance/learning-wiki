Experiment 2: 105 undergraduates; the cued-recall group studied and was tested on 70 word pairs (14 minutes) versus 20 in Experiment 1. The key post-rest gain test showed "no significant difference between groups, t(103) = -0.55, p = .59, d = -0.075, BF01 = 4.24", with the Bayes factor again favoring the null.
