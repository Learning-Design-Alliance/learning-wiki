## Evidence

### Angélique Remacle 2023

Angélique Remacle, Stéphane Bouchard, Dominique Morsomme. (2023). Can teaching simulations in a virtual classroom help trainee teachers to develop oral communication skills and self-efficacy? A randomized controlled trial. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104808

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVAs comparing pre- and post-intervention assessments in the randomized controlled trial found significant Time effects: phonetographic surface (F = 9.240, p = .004, η²p = .192), vocal effort (F = 12.191, p = .001, η²p = .238), and self-efficacy (F = 50.74, p < .001, η²p = .565). The article reports these as "a positive effect of both indirect and mixed interventions".
