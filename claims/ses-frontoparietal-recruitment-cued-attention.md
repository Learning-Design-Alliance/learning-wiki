## Evidence

### Rosen Maya L. 2021

Rosen Maya L., Lurie Lucy A., Sambrook Kelly A., Meltzoff Andrew N., McLaughlin Katie A. (2021). Neural mechanisms underlying the income-achievement gap: The role of the ventral visual stream. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.101025

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain regression with log income-to-needs as predictor in the 62-child sample, cluster-corrected with 3dClustSim. The article reports "a significant positive association between INR and activation during the cued attention task in the left inferior frontal gyrus extending into anterior insula, left putamen, and left parahippocampal gyrus"; no whole-brain associations during memory-guided attention.

> "We found a significant positive association between INR and activation during the cued attention task in the left inferior frontal gyrus extending into anterior insula, left putamen, and left parahippocampal gyrus (Fig. 6; Table 3). We found no associations between INR and activation in the whole brain analysis during the memory-guided attention task."
