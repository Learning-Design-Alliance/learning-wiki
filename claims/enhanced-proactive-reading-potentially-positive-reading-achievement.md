### Enhanced Proactive Reading 2006

Enhanced Proactive Reading. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

WWC synthesis of two randomized controlled trials (Vaughn, Cirino, et al., 2006; Vaughn, Mathes, et al., 2006) totaling more than 130 first-grade English language learners in four Texas schools. The report states the program "was found to have potentially positive effects on reading achievement"; no effect size is printed in this summary statement.

> "Enhanced Proactive Reading  was found to have potentially positive effects on reading achievement and no discernible effects on English language development."
