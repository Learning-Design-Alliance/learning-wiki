### Si Chen 2026

Si Chen, Haocong Cheng, Suzy Su, Lu Ming, Sarah Masud, Qi Wang, and Yun Huang. (2026). Redesigning Educational Videos for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Learners. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1145/3772318.3791470](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791470)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Omnibus linear mixed model over LCD ratings in the 16-participant within-subject study; post-hoc Satterthwaite t-tests showed Focusing on Essential Information (t(798) = 5.81, p < .001) and Fostering Connection (t(798) = 4.23, p < .001) each rated higher than Reducing Irrelevant Information, with no significant difference between the first two.
