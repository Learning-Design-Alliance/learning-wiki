# AIfred is faster for early image-generation modifications but ChatGPT becomes faster at four modifications, with no overall significant time difference

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For one, two, and three image modifications AIfred shows lower completion times, but ChatGPT becomes faster at four modifications; overall times (167 s vs. 208 s) do not differ significantly (p=.32). [→ Gregorio Orlando 2026](#gregorio-orlando-2026)

## Evidence

### Gregorio Orlando 2026

Gregorio Orlando, Milan Groshev, Eduardo Castelló Ferrer. (2026). AIfred: Augmented Learning through Functional Robotic Embodiment at the Desk. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38737

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Figure-reported completion times by number of image modifications show AIfred faster at one to three modifications and ChatGPT faster at four; overall, "task completion time with AIfred is 167 seconds and with ChatGPT it is 208 seconds (p=.32)". No effect size is printed.

> "For one, two, and three modifications, AIfred shows lower completion times than ChatGPT. ChatGPT, however, becomes faster at four modifications."
