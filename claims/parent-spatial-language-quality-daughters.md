# Parents use a greater variety of spatial words (higher spatial language quality) with daughters than sons, regardless of SES or talkativeness

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In a MANOVA, parents uttered more unique spatial words with girls than boys (quality), with no quantity difference. [→ Farran 2025](#farran-2025)
`q3 i2` The child-sex effect on spatial language quality remained after controlling for SES and parent talkativeness. [→ Farran 2025 (2)](#farran-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Farran 2025

Farran, E. K., Fink, E., Hughes, C., Gilligan-Lee, K. A., & The NewFAMS team. (2025). Limited sex differences in spatial language in parent-child dyads. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102006. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102006

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA (N = 104) with parent spatial language quantity and quality as dependent variables, parent sex and timepoint within-subject, child sex between-subject. Quality showed a child-sex effect, "F (1, 102) = 4.68, p = .033, ηp2 = .044"; quantity did not (F < 1).

> "Follow up ANOVAs demonstrated that the main effect of child sex was not significant for spatial language quantity
(F < 1), but analysis of spatial language quality demonstrated that
parents uttered more unique spatial words during block play with girls
than with boys, F (1, 102) = 4.68, p = .033, ηp
2 = .044."

### Farran 2025 (2)

Farran, E. K., Fink, E., Hughes, C., Gilligan-Lee, K. A., & The NewFAMS team. (2025). Limited sex differences in spatial language in parent-child dyads. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102006. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102006

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

MANCOVA controlling for family SES and parent total words showed the child-sex effect persisted for spatial language quality, "F (1, 100) = 7.87, p = .006, ηp2 = .073", but not quantity (F < 1); the timepoint effect became non-significant.

> "the effect of child sex remained, Λ
= .895, F(2, 99) = 5.83, p = .004, ηp
2 = .105 (spatial language quality: F
(1, 100) = 7.87, p = .006, ηp
2 = .073; spatial language quantity: F < 1)."
