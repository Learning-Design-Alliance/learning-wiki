`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validity questions on 5-point Likert scales in the same 26-respondent survey. Lower average scores concerned whether the model exposes trainees to stress similar to clinical conditions and is useful for fetal positioning (≥ 50% and ≥ 60% of scores ≥ 3/5).

> "Respondents considered the model useful and would recommend it for training (≥ 71% of scores ≥ 3/5) and improving complex fetoscopic skills, such as instrument handling and suturing (≥ 83% and 100% of score ≥ 3/5 respectively; Table 2)."
