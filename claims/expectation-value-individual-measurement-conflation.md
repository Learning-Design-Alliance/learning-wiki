## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Transfer of Learning in Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Research with QM students on observables found "Many students conclude that for a phys- ical observable, its expectation value and individual mea- surement are the same"; many also believe individual measurements cannot be performed.

> "Many students conclude that for a phys- ical observable, its expectation value and individual mea- surement are the same or that each individual mea- surement of the observable will yield the expectation value."

### Chandralekha Singh 2016 (2)

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Transfer of Learning in Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

After relevant instruction, students asked about measuring the electron-nucleus distance in ground-state hydrogen: "Many students claimed it will always yield the same value and some explicitly cited the Bohr model."

> "Many students claimed it will always yield the same value and some explicitly cited the Bohr model."
