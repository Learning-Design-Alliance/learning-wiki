Evidence¶

Anderson 2019¶

Anderson, S. J., Jamniczky, H. A., Krigolson, O. E., Coderre, S. P. & Hecker, K. G. (2019). Quantifying two-dimensional and three-dimensional stereoscopic learning in anatomy using electroencephalography. npj Science of Learning 4:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0050-4

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Module 1 learning-curve analysis of proportion-correct across five blocks: accuracy increased over blocks with a large effect ("F(3.22, 580.24) = 315.15, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.64") but no significant group differences ("F(2, 180) = 3.04, p = 0.05, η2 = 0.03"). Module 2 likewise showed no group differences.

"There was a signi ﬁcant effect of block number on neuroanatomical structure identi ﬁcation perfor-mance, F(3.22, 580.24) = 315.15, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.64. There were no signiﬁcant differences among groups, F(2, 180) = 3.04, p = 0.05, η2 = 0.03."
