The review's Research Question 6 synthesis of nine ITS studies, which the review concludes represent "one of the most mature and technologically sophisticated implementations of AI-driven personalized learning." Outcomes are attributed to the reviewed studies.

"Most studies reported positive learning outcomes, particularly in programming, mathematics, and STEM, with recent work also addressing motivational and affective dimensions through emotion recognition, gamification, and conversational agents."
