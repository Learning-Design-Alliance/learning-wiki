Jabarethina G, John KC, Anitha R. (2025). Evolving roles of hospital administrators in India: a qualitative study on health information technology competencies for improved healthcare delivery. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-092133

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Grounded-theory telephone interviews with 27 hospital administrators (16 male, 11 female, 3–20 years' experience) across Indian healthcare domains; participants stated that "practice and experience are the foundations" of their competencies, with one saying "I learned everything through experience."

> "In addition, they stated that practice and experience are the foundations on which they build such competencies."
