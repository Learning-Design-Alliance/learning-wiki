### Topal 2018

Topal, İ.H. (2018). An investigation into the recognition and production of pitch patterns of if-clauses by turkish pre-service teachers of english. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(4), 285-300. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201884

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of the written and oral pre-tests of 61 Turkish freshman ELT participants. The author reports that "Approximately 80% of the participants failed to recognize the pitch patterns of If-clauses in general" and about half failed at production, leading to the remedial session.

> "Approximately 80% of the participants failed to recognize the pitch patterns of If-clauses in general. Regarding the production of the pitch patterns of If-clauses, approximately half of the participants failed to produce the correct pitch patterns of If-clauses"
