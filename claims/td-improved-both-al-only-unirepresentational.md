## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In both intervention groups, interpretation of the transverse density of vector arrows improved significantly from pre- to post-test. [→ Christoph Hoyer 2024](#christoph-hoyer-2024)
`q3 i?` Arrow-length interpretation improved significantly only in the unirepresentational group; the change was not significant in the multirepresentational group. [→ Christoph Hoyer 2024 (2)](#christoph-hoyer-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Christoph Hoyer 2024

Christoph Hoyer, Raimund Girwidz. (2024). Vector representations and unit vector representations of fields — problems of understanding and possible teaching strategies. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon pre-post tests within each intervention group of the 124-student study: transverse-density interpretation "improved significantly" in both the multirepresentational and unirepresentational groups.

> "However, in both groups, the interpretation of the transverse density of vector arrows (TD) improved significantly."

### Christoph Hoyer 2024 (2)

Christoph Hoyer, Raimund Girwidz. (2024). Vector representations and unit vector representations of fields — problems of understanding and possible teaching strategies. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon pre-post comparison in the unirepresentational group showed "significantly better results were achieved in the items related to the interpretation of arrow lengths (AL)"; the multirepresentational group's change was not significant.

> "In the “unirepresentational” group, significantly better results were achieved in the items related to the interpretation of arrow lengths (AL) in the post-test compared to the pretest. In contrast, these changes were not significant in the “multirepresentational” group."
