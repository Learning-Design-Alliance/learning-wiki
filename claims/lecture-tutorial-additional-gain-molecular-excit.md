## Evidence

### Wallace 2015

Wallace, C. S., Prather, E. E., Hornstein, S. D., Schlingman, W. M., Chambers, T. G., & Burns, J. O. (2015). A New Lecture-Tutorial for Teaching about Molecular Excitations and Synchrotron Radiation. [doi:10.1119/1.4937972](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4937972)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two Astro 101 sections at CU-Boulder in spring 2014; one section assessed post-lecture, the other post-Lecture-Tutorial. "post-Lecture-Tutorial was 57% (n = 50)" versus 49% post-lecture; z-test and t-test both p = 0.06, not significant at conventional thresholds.

> "The average correct score for the students post-lecture was 49% (n = 73). The average correct score for the students post-Lecture-Tutorial was 57% (n = 50)."
