## Evidence

### Scherff Aline D. 2025

Scherff Aline D., Kääb Stefan, Fischer Martin R., Berndt Markus. (2025). EYE-ECG2: Addressing medical student feedback in an RCT with eye-tracking videos featuring cued retrospective reporting and modified learning sequences for ECG interpretation skills training. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001771

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise backward multiple regression model-fitting (AIC-based, replicating EYE-ECG1's analytical strategy) showed that randomising case order attenuated the distinctive predictive role of clinical case 1, removing it from the best-fitting BS and RS models. No effect size was printed.

> "randomising the order of presentation of clinical case 1 resulted in an attenuated effect and even in the removal of case 1 from the best-fitting model for both the BS and RS model (for predictors included in the final models refer to table 1 (Tab. 1))."
