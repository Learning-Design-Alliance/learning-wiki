### Grilli 2015

Grilli, L., Pennoni, F., Rampichini, C., and Romeo, I. (2015). Exploiting TIMSS and PIRLS combined data: multivariate multilevel modelling of student achievement. [doi:10.1214/16-aoas988](https://doi.org/10.1214/16-aoas988)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null multivariate multilevel model fitted to the Italian combined TIMSS&PIRLS 2011 sample with MI-combined estimates. Between-class correlations are 0.93 (Read-Math), 0.97 (Math-Scie) and 0.98 (Read-Scie); within-class correlations are 0.71-0.81. The article reports "the three scores are highly correlated, in particular at class level".
