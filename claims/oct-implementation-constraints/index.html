Shantha and Mekala 2017¶

S. Shantha, S. Mekala. (2017). The Role of Oral Communicative Tasks (OCT) in Developing the Spoken Proficiency of Engineering Students. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 8(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.2p.161

Qualitative observation-based answer to Research Question 2, drawing on the researcher's observation sheets; the article also cites Norris (2009) on impediments to TBLT and reports that "lesser contact hours and non-responsive students" were the challenges found in this implementation.

"Majorit y of the participants were first time speakers and were affected by their fear of failure. The stage fear prevented them from speaking and comprehending the prompting cues. The students could not practise and prepare for the tasks in the home environment d ue to lack of compatible sociolinguistic atmosphere and their inability to generate sentences in English on their own."
