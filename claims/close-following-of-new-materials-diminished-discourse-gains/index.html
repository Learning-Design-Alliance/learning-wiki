Evidence¶

Perry 2019¶

Perry, R., Marple, S., & Reade, F. (2019). Roadblocks and routes: Professional development in Math in Common districts. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/permissions

q1 · i? · design · r1

District-reported observation from Oceanside Unified during the 2016–17 adoption of new elementary materials: discourse successes "diminished when teachers followed their new instructional materials too closely." The report notes this phenomenon was not uncommon across the initiative. No effect size is printed.

"successes that teachers had demonstrated in supporting students' academic discourse — based on what they had learned from studying the standards and participating in the PD — diminished when teachers followed their new instructional materials too closely."
