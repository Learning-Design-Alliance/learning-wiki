Evidence¶

Setiyo Prajoko 2017¶

Setiyo Prajoko, Mohamad Amin, Fatchur Rohman, Muhana Gipayana. (2017). The Usage of Recycle Materials for Science Practicum: Is There Any Effect on Science Process Skills? International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.6, No.1. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis of a 10-question student feedback questionnaire on recycle-materials-based practicum, stated to meet construct and content validity per Prajoko et al. (2016). The article reports "average score 2.82 out of a maximum score 3". Descriptive only; no inferential test or effect size.

"Based on the questionnaire that obtained average score 2.82 out of a maximum score 3. The results indicated that students positively respond use of recycle materials in the science practicum."
