## Subclaims
`q4 i?` At 6-months follow-up, 21.1% of residents with dementia in intervention nursing homes had an unplanned hospital transfer or death in hospital compared to 19.0% in control homes (OR 1.14), a non-significant difference. [→ Tropea Joanne 2022](#tropea-joanne-2022)

### Tropea Joanne 2022

(2022). Evaluation of IMproving Palliative care Education and Training Using Simulation in Dementia (IMPETUS-D) a staff simulation training intervention to improve palliative care of people with advanced dementia living in nursing homes: a cluster randomised controlled trial. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-022-02809-x

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomised controlled trial of 24 nursing homes with 1304 residents with dementia (608 intervention, 696 control), analysed by logistic regression with cluster-weighted standard errors on an intention-to-treat basis. The odds ratio "1.14 (95% CI, 0.82-1.59), p = 0.44" shows no significant difference.

> "Analysis of the primary outcome (proportion of hospital transfers or deaths in hospital among residents with dementia over 6 months) yielded an odds ratio of 1.14 (95% CI, 0.82-1.59), p = 0.44."
