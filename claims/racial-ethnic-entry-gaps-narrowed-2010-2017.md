# Black-White and Hispanic-White achievement gaps at kindergarten entry narrowed significantly between 2010 and 2017 in both math and reading

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The Black-White math gap narrowed from 0.64 to 0.53 SD and the reading gap from 0.51 to 0.42 SD; the Hispanic-White gap narrowed from 0.68 to 0.59 SD in math and 0.64 to 0.53 SD in reading, all significantly. [→ Kuhfeld 2019](#kuhfeld-2019)

## Evidence

### Kuhfeld 2019

Kuhfeld, Megan, Soland, James, Pitts, Christine, & Burchinal, Margaret. (2019). Trends in Children's Academic Skills at School Entry: 2010 to 2017. EdWorkingPaper No. 19-137, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. http://www.edworkingpapers.com/ai19-137

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression-estimated standardized gaps by year and subject using weighted MAP Growth fall kindergarten scores, with standard errors adjusted for nesting of students in schools; t-tests compared 2010 and 2017 gaps. The Hispanic-White gap also "significantly narrowed, dropping from 0.68 to 0.59 SD in math and from 0.64 to 0.53 SD in reading."

> "In 2010, the average math score for White kindergarteners was 0.64 SD higher than their Black peers, which is significantly larger than the 0.53 SD difference in 2017. In reading, the Black-White gap significantly narrowed from 0.51 to 0.42 SD over the eight year span."
