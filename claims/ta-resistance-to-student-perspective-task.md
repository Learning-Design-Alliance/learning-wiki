### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Categorization of problems to assess and improve proficiency as teachers and learners. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07677](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07677)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational notes taken immediately after discussion with the 21 graduate students in the TA training course, who claimed the task was "useless", "impossible", and had "no bearing" on their TA duties. The article reports that "Not a single graduate student stated that they saw merit in the task".
