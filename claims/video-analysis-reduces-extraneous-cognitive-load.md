# Tablet-PC-supported video analysis reduces intervention-induced extraneous cognitive load compared with traditional experimental tools in regular physics lessons

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students using tablet-PC video analysis reported significantly lower extraneous cognitive load than students using stopwatch, tape measure and graphing calculator, with no significant difference in intrinsic (p = .751) or germane load (p = .694). [→ Sebastian Becker 2020](#sebastian-becker-2020)

## Evidence

### Sebastian Becker 2020

Sebastian Becker, Pascal Klein, Alexander Gößling, Jochen Kuhn. (2020). Using Mobile Devices to Augment Inquiry-Based Learning Processes with Multiple Representations. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101350](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101350)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized controlled trial in 18 courses from 11 German secondary schools; after propensity score matching N=262. One-factorial ANOVA on the Leppink et al. cognitive-load sub-scales found "a significantly lower intervention-induced extraneous cognitive load for the TG" with η2=0.101.

> "a significantly lower intervention-induced extraneous cognitive load for the TG could be detected with high test power ( F(1,240)=27.01, p<10-3, η2=0.101, 1-β=1.000)"
