Smith 2022¶

Smith, T. E., Thompson, A. M., & Maynard, B. R. (2022). Self‐management interventions for reducing challenging behaviors among school‐age students: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews, 18, e1223. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1223

Meta-analysis of the group-design sample (4 studies, 422 participants, 11 behavioral effects; three experimental, one quasi-experimental) with robust variance estimation yielded the standardized mean difference effect size "g = 0.63, 95% CI [0.08, 1.17]".
