Evidence¶

Silver 2022¶

Silver, A. M., Elliott, L., & Libertus, M. E. (2022). Parental math input is not uniformly beneficial for young children: The moderating role of inhibitory control. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000679

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Robustness regression of children's math achievement on parents' overall talk and inhibitory control showed "the interaction between parents' overall talk and children's inhibitory control was not significant", indicating specificity to number talk rather than talk in general.

"Notably, the interaction between parents' overall talk and children's inhibitory control was not significant, β = .08, p = .372."

Silver 2022 (2)¶

Silver, A. M., Elliott, L., & Libertus, M. E. (2022). Parental math input is not uniformly beneficial for young children: The moderating role of inhibitory control. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000679

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Robustness regression of children's vocabulary on number talk, inhibitory control, and their interaction was not statistically significant overall, and "the interaction ... was not statistically significant", indicating specificity to math rather than cognitive abilities broadly.

"The interaction between parents' number talk and children's inhibitory control was not statistically significant, β = .05, p = .652."
