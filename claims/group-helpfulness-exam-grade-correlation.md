## Evidence

### Rahul Jilakara and David P. Waters 2020

Rahul Jilakara and David P. Waters. (2020). Determination of the helpfulness of physics exam study methods. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2012.10693](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2012.10693)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlations (rcorr in R) compared grades of students spending more study time on the four helpful study methods with those relying more on the two unhelpful methods; only p < 0.05 values were reported.

> "For the groupings, signiﬁcant correlations showed up in 11 of the exams."

### Rahul Jilakara and David P. Waters 2020 (2)

Rahul Jilakara and David P. Waters. (2020). Determination of the helpfulness of physics exam study methods. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2012.10693](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2012.10693)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Example from Exam 4 of the Figure 4 correlation table (fourth exam in the fall of 2017), described as indicative of the evidence found; conceptual grades were excluded from the table because fewer correlations appeared.

> "there is a signiﬁcant positive correlation between using study methods that are labeled on average as more helpful and the overall exam grades and the grades from problems."
