## Evidence

### R. P 1922

R. P. (1922). Reviews of Ballard, Group Tests of Intelligence and Carothers, Psychological Examinations of College Students. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 380-381. https://eric.ed.gov

`q1 · i?` · `review · r?`

The reviewer's account of a monograph on superior children, who are characterized as having a good home and superior parents. The monograph author emphasizes "the importance of environmental factors" throughout; the reviewer questions the soundness of some of these opinions.

> "According to our author the common factor could just as well be environmental as innate. "The common factor or factors may be a varying admixture of innate ability, formal training, incidental education and social conditions.""
