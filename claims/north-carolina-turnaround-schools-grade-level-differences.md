`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Thompson, Brown, Townsend, Henry, and Fortner (2011) compared North Carolina turnaround schools to slightly higher-performing schools between 2006 and 2010. "At the high school level students’ academic achievement was significantly greater in turnaround schools than in comparison schools." Graduation rate differences were not statistically significant, and middle school reading improvements were also not statistically significant.

> "At the high school level students’ academic achievement was significantly greater in turnaround schools than in comparison schools. Graduation rates increased more in turnaround schools than in comparison schools, though the difference was not statistically significant."
