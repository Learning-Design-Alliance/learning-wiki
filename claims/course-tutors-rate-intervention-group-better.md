## Evidence

### Zöll Lisa 2025

Zöll Lisa, Deschner James, Hollinderbäumer Anke, Nowak Thomas, Zajontz Tamara, Behling Lina, Fischbeck Sabine, Schmidt Uwe, Pfeiffer-Grötz Thekla J. (2025). The effects of communication training on communication competence – a 360° evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07676-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-semester external assessment by course tutors, who supervise 6–7 students each with only selective insights, showed the intervention group rated significantly better on questions 3.3, 3.5, 3.7 and 3.8 (p < 0.001 to p = 0.007), covering conversation structure, summarizing, behaviour and questioning techniques.

> "The intervention group was rated significantly better by the course assistants in the following parameters compared to the control group: 3.3 (p < 0.001), 3.5 (p = 0.007), 3.7 (p = 0.001) and 3.8. (p = 0.006)"
