## Evidence

### Qian Ma 2026

Qian Ma, Yingfan Zhou, Shubhang Kaushik, Aamod Joshi, Aditya Majumdar, Noah Apthorpe, Yan Shvartzshnaider, Sarah Rajtmajer, Brett Frischmann. (2026). Pedagogical Friction in Password Creation. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.06650](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.06650)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Behavior-knowledge alignment analysis in the randomized experiment, defined as the proportion of triggered tips/associated rules both resolved during post-test password creation and answered correctly on the exit quiz. The article reports alignment "high and similar across groups"; no effect size is printed.

> "behavior-knowledge alignment is high and similar across groups: 88.6% for T1, 84.2% for T2, and 85.7% for T3. This illustrates that when participants resolved a rule in their post-test password, they usually answered at least one quiz question about that rule correctly."
