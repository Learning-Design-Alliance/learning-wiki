# E&M students show significant pre- to post-instruction gains on most divergence and curl survey statements

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The percentage of E&M students selecting correct statements C7 (71% to 94%), D4 (53% to 85%), D9 (29% to 71%) and D10 (63% to 81%) improved significantly from pre- to post-instruction (p≤0.005 for all four statements). [→ Charles Baily 2014](#charles-baily-2014)

## Evidence

### Charles Baily 2014

Charles Baily, Laurens Bollen, Andrew Pattie, Paul van Kampen and Mieke De Cock. (2014). Student thinking about the divergence and curl in mathematics and physics contexts. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.008](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.008)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post comparison of the same E&M students at St Andrews (N=71) and KU Leuven (N=15), combined (post N=86), after instruction through the full Maxwell equations in differential form; "Significant improvements" were seen on four correct statements.

> "Significant improvements can also be seen in the percentage of students who selected correct statements C7 (from 71% to 94%), D4 (from 53% to 85%), D9 (from 29% to 71%) and D10 (from 63% to 81%); 𝑝≤0.005 for all four statements."
