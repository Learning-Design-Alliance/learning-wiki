## Evidence

### Kelli Shar 2020

Kelli Shar, Rosemary S. Russ, James T. Laverty. (2020). Student Epistemological Framing on Paper-Based Assessments. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020101)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case analysis of three frame shifts among 67 coded problem-solving attempts. The authors report "All were caused by a change in a single epistemological resource," attributed to two influences: interviewer intervention and assessment feature. Only 20 instances were transcribed and coded for resources.

> "While students seldom shifted frames, we observed three instances of a frame shift. All were caused by a change in a single epistemological resource. This was the result of two main inﬂuences: interviewer intervention and assessment feature."
