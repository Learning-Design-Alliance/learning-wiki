Students chose their learning method at the beginning of the course; baseline comparison in Table 2 shows GPA 8.60±0.98 (blended) vs. 8.11±0.98 (on-site), p < 0.001. This is an observational self-selection finding, not an experimental effect.

> "An important finding was that students with higher GPA scores more often chose an online classroom learning environment, indicating a preference for learning formats that are more information and communication technology oriented."
