`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Meta‐aggregation of 89 extracted findings from 17 qualitative studies, categorised against the JBI EBHC model framework. The review reports that "none of the findings were associated with the sub‐dimension of evidence synthesis", which the authors call a new finding of the review.

> "The synthesised findings were categorised into the four sub‐dimensions instead of five sub‐dimensions according to the JBI's EBHC model, as none of the findings were associated with the sub‐dimension of evidence synthesis"
