## Evidence

### Neslihan Yondemir Çalişkan 2025

Neslihan Yondemir Çalişkan, Emine Şendurur. (2025). The effect of cumulative eye movements' guidance of experts on transition from novice to expert. Education Tech Research Dev, 73:59–89. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10414-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test post-test control group experiment with 30 matched novice Photoshop students watching eight weekly videos; performance was scored weekly on rubrics out of 100 points. The t-test showed "significant mean differences between design performance scores of groups with medium effect size, t(28) = 3.073, p < 0.05, r = 0.558."

> "Descriptive statistics show that the design performance scores of the experimental group (M = 79.56, SD = 6.76) are higher than the control group (M = 67.79, SD = 13.22)."
