Qualitative Likert ratings (7-point scale) collected after each of the 20 interviews in the pilot study; the article reports all profile-consistency ratings positive, and 19 of 20 positive for cohesion and realism, with two isolated "somewhat disagree" ratings.

> "When asked whether the patient's behavior was consistent with its configured profile, the ratings for all interviews were positive, including "strongly agree" (n=5), "agree" (n=14), and "somewhat agree" (n=1; Figure 5B)."
