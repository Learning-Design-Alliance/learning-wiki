## Evidence

### Iñarrairaegui M 2022

Iñarrairaegui M, Fernández-Ros N, Lucena F, Landecho MF, García N, Quiroga J, Herrero JI. (2022). Evaluation of the quality of multiple-choice questions according to the students’ academic level. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03844-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Item analysis of 257 MCQ (198 factual, 59 case-based) comparing discrimination between academic-level groups. Table 2 shows factual questions discriminated significantly differently between groups (0.20 vs 0.28, P < 0.001) while case-based questions did not (0.24 vs 0.30, P = 0.296); the text states "These differences were not found when the discriminative indices of case-based questions were compared between groups."
