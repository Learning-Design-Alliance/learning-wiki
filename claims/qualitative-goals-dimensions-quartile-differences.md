`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Blinded video-based qualitative assessment by two experienced laparoscopic surgeons using the GOALS scale (five facets, 5-25 points). All qualitative variables differed across quartiles; e.g. overall score rose from 10.8 (quartile 1) to 20 (quartile 4).

> "In the qualitative analysis, all variables showed a statistically significant difference (p < 0.001) (Table 3)."
