# A theory-driven SRL learning analytics dashboard improves academic writing knowledge gains in human-AI collaborative writing

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group improved writing knowledge significantly from pre- to post-test while the control group did not, with a significant time × group interaction. [→ Chen 2026](#chen-2026)

## Evidence

### Chen 2026

Chen, A., Lian, J., Kuang, X., & Jia, J. (2026). Can theory-driven learning analytics dashboard enhance human-AI collaboration in writing learning? Insights from an empirical experiment. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1016/j.iheduc.2025.101054](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.iheduc.2025.101054)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 52 postgraduate students in an academic abstract writing task with GenAI; a 2×2 mixed-design ANOVA found a significant time × group interaction (F(1, 50) = 5.616, p = .023, η² = .078), and post-hoc tests showed the EG improved while "the CG showed no significant change".

> "Results showed that the experimental group (EG) demonstrated a significant improvement in academic writing knowledge from pre -test (M = 81.36, SD = 11.72) to post-test (M = 90.00, SD = 6.90), t = -3.47, adjust p = 0.002."
