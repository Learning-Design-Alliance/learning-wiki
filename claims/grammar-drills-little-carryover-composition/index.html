Coombs 1987¶

Coombs, Lucinda H. (1987). The GED Writing Skills Test Essay Teacher Training Manual. Maine Dept. of Educational and Cultural Services / University of Maine. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED324536

q2 · i? · design · r1

The manual's introduction cites accumulated educational research as the rationale for the 1988 GED Language Arts panel's recommendation that writing be measured directly in an essay. The claim is asserted as a research summary without cited studies or effect sizes.

"Over 75 years of educational research demonstrates thatthere is -ery little carryover from grammar instruction and skill drills to successin composition."
