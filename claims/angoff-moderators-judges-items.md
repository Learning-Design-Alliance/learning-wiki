### Kannan 2025

Kannan, S., & Gowri, S. (2025). Angoff methods in standard setting in health professional education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education, 25, 1727.

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-regression analyses explored heterogeneity by number of judges and test length. The analysis found "each additional judge was associated with a 0.19-percentage point increase in cut scores (p = 0.003)" and "each additional test item was associated with a 0.05-percentage point increase in the pass rate (p = 0.001)".

> "Meta-regression indicated that each additional judge was associated with a 0.19-percentage point increase in cut scores (p = 0.003) while each additional test item was associated with a 0.05-percentage point increase in the pass rate (p = 0.001)."
