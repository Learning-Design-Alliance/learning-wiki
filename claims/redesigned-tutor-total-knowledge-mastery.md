## Evidence

### Qianru Lyu 2026

Qianru Lyu, Conrad Borchers, Meng Xia, Karen Xiao, Paulo F. Carvalho, Kenneth R. Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Evaluating a Data-Driven Redesign Process for Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Within-tutor learning analysis of the crossover study using an Additive Factors Model fit to log data, estimating end-of-practice knowledge per KC. The article reports a printed effect size of dp = 0.40 for total mastery and notes the increase reflected broader coverage of skills, since the Redesigned condition included five more skills. A BKT-based replication gave dp = 0.34.

> "Based on iAFM estimates, students mastered more knowledge in the Redesigned condition (M = 7.61, SD = 2.88) than in the Original condition (M = 6.03, SD = 2.26),t(115) = 4.33,p < .001,dp = 0.40."

### Qianru Lyu 2026 (2)

Qianru Lyu, Conrad Borchers, Meng Xia, Karen Xiao, Paulo F. Carvalho, Kenneth R. Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Evaluating a Data-Driven Redesign Process for Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Same within-tutor learning analysis, comparing average mastery per KC between conditions with both AFM and BKT estimates; no significant difference on either measure, so the total-mastery advantage reflects practicing more skills rather than deeper mastery of each.

> "Average KC mastery did not differ significantly between conditions using either AFM (0.683 vs. 0.658,t(115) = 1.11,p=.270) or BKT estimates (0.687 vs. 0.672,t(115) = 0.48,p=.630)."
