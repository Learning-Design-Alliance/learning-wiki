## Evidence

### Olaniyan 2015

Olaniyan, A. O., & Omosewo, E. O. (2015). Effects of a Target-Task Problem-Solving Model on Senior Secondary School Students' Performance in Physics. Science Education International, 25(4), 522-538. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086539

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of post-test PTCE scores for the 60 experimental-group students by gender (29 male, 31 female). Gender F = 4.819, Sig. .032. Table 5 printed mean gains of 18.15 for males and 12.84 for females; the text states "The male students performed better than female students".

> "The P value (.032) is less than the P alpha level of 0.050. (P<0.050), and shows that the null hypothesis needs to be rejected."
