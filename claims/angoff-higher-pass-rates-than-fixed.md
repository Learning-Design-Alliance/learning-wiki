## Evidence

### Kannan 2025

Kannan, S., & Gowri, S. (2025). Angoff methods in standard setting in health professional education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education, 25, 1727.

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

A mixed treatment comparison meta-analysis of 21 studies (22,622 examinees) pooled pass rates from studies comparing Angoff variants against conventional fixed methods. The pooled estimate for Angoff was "OR: 7.48; 95% CI: 3.24–17.28".

> "Mixed treatment comparison pooled estimates revealed significantly higher pass rates with Angoff (OR: 7.48; 95% CI: 3.24–17.28) and norm-referenced mean–2SD methods (OR: 1.79; 95% CI: 1.10–2.91)."
