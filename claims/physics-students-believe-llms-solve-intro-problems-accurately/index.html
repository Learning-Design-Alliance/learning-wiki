Evidence¶

Jake O'Brien 2026¶

Jake O'Brien, Margaret Ellis, Alma Robinson, John Simonetti, and Naren Ramakrishnan. (2026). Skepticism vs. Convenience: Physics Students' Perceptions and Use of Large Language Models Before and After Instruction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21037 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.21037

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Pre-lesson Likert survey of 52 first-year physics majors in a required FYE course, reported as agreement/disagreement percentages. The pre-survey found "79% agreement and 13% disagreement" that LLMs solve introductory physics problems with high accuracy; no effect size applies to this descriptive result.

"The vast majority of students believe LLMs to be capable of solving introductory physics problems with high accuracy (accuracy-intro-physics), with 79% agreement and 13% disagreement."
