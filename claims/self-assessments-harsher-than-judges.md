`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of self-assessed and judge-rated speech quality on the same 0-10 scale in the Results section of the 61-participant study. Although the two ratings correlated significantly (r = .47, p < .001), "participants consistently rated themselves harshly relative to judges (M's of 3.02 versus 4.66, t(60) = 5.90, p < .001)".
