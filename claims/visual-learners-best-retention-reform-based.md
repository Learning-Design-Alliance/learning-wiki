## Evidence

### M. N. Modebelu & C. C. Ogbonna 2014

M. N. Modebelu & C. C. Ogbonna. (2014). Reform-Based-Instructional Method and Learning Styles on Students' Achievement and Retention in Mathematics: Administrative Implications. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies 2(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.2n.2p.48

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Scheffe multiple-comparison test on retention scores of the experimental group. The article concludes "the visual learners retained better the mathematics knowledge acquired more than the auditory and tactile learners"; descriptive retention means were 66.92, 52.0 and 47.33 for visual, auditory and tactile learners.

> "the visual learners retained better the mathematics knowledge acquired more than the auditory and tactile learners. This finding is in agreement with Agwaga (2010) that stressed that children learn not only in different ways, but different rates."
