## Evidence

### Schneider Simon 2026

Schneider Simon, Stricker Daniel, Berendonk Christoph, Baron-Stefaniak Joanna, Walter Robin. (2026). Abdominal ultrasound performance assessment: a comparison of generic and extended OSCE rating scales. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08994-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The split-plot ANOVA showed a scale × station interaction (η² = 0.062, small effect) but no scale × series interaction, indicating the between-scale difference varied by station yet was consistent across the eight series.

> "Though, a significant interaction between type of rating scale and station was found (F(11, 461) = 2.782, p = 0.002, η2 = 0.062, small effect size), the interaction between type of rating scales and series was not significant (p = 0.108), suggesting the difference between scales was balanced across the 12 series."

### Schneider Simon 2026 (2)

Schneider Simon, Stricker Daniel, Berendonk Christoph, Baron-Stefaniak Joanna, Walter Robin. (2026). Abdominal ultrasound performance assessment: a comparison of generic and extended OSCE rating scales. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08994-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc paired-samples t-tests with Bonferroni correction identified significant between-scale score differences only at the retroperitoneum and hepatic veins stations, the anatomically complex stations; difficulty differed significantly for only two stations.

> "Analyses yielded statistically significant differences between participants' performance P only for the stations "retroperitoneum" and "hepatic veins" (p < 0.001, Appendix 3)."
