engage2learn (e2L): Evidence Rating 2022¶

engage2learn (e2L): Evidence Rating. (2022). https://www.evidenceforlearning.org

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Retrospective matched study during 2021-2022 with 169 6th-8th grade teachers in 10 schools in a large urban Texas district, using outcome data on over 2000 students. Students of e2L teachers "scored significantly higher on the NWEA MAP" with the printed effect sizes +0.33 and +0.19.

"Students of engage2learn teachers scored significantly higher on the NWEA MAP than students of non-e2L teachers in the same schools (effect size = +0.33), as well as in comparison schools (effect size = +0.19)."
