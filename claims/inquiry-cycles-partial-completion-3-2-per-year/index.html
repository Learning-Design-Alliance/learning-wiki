Evidence¶

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report 2024¶

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report. (2024). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA242-1.html

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Artifact-based implementation analysis of 25 NSI in Research Question 2. The report also states that of initiated cycles with artifacts, on average 50 percent were complete, with documented evidence of plan, do, study, and act phases.

"Based on artifact data, 20 of the 25 NSI (80 percent) implemented inquiry cycles in at least some of their schools. Across NSI, about 61 percent of schools engaged in cycles."

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report 2024 (2)¶

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report. (2024). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA242-1.html

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Implementation analysis in Research Question 2 based on CI artifacts. A separate passage reports schools in instructional and early warning and response NSI initiated more cycles (3.8 and 2.7) than schools in well-matched postsecondary NSI (2.2).

"The average number of initiated cycles per year was 3.2 for those CI teams that engaged in cycle activities, which is consistent with prior research about inquiry cycles."
