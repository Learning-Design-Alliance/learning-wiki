## Evidence

### Iordanou 2010

Kalypso Iordanou. (2010). Developing Argument Skills Across Scientific and Social Domains. Journal of Cognition and Development. https://doi.org/10.1080/15248372.2010.485335

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of coded electronic dialogues from the intervention study (56 dialogues per topic after exclusions). The article prints the effect size for this finding as "partial g2 = .30" (partial η² = .30), a large effect: "a three-way interaction, F(2, 53) = 11.08, p \< .001".

> "An analysis of overall counterargument usage revealed a three-way interaction, F(2, 53) = 11.08, p \< .001, partial g2 = .30, for Condition x Time x Topic."

### Iordanou 2010 (2)

Kalypso Iordanou. (2010). Developing Argument Skills Across Scientific and Social Domains. Journal of Cognition and Development. https://doi.org/10.1080/15248372.2010.485335

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Two-way Condition x Time repeated-measures ANOVA on the social topic dialogues. The printed effect size is "partial g2 = .63" (partial η² = .63): "a significant Time x Condition interaction, F(2, 53) = 44.58, p \< .001", with both experimental conditions equally effective and the control condition ineffective.

> "A two-way analysis on the social topic showed that there is a significant Time x Condition interaction, F(2, 53) = 44.58, p \< .001, partial g2 = .63."
