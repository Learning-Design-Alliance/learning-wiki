`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article argues from the author's experience that agency-mediated programs (like NIUS and SRFP) face selection challenges because "the probability of selecting students with inadequate backgro und training or motivation is quite high" when selection rests on university grades. This is an anecdotal observation.

> "With a completely uneven grading system of our universities, the probability of selecting students with inadequate backgro und training or motivation is quite high when the selec- tion depends mainly on their university grades."
