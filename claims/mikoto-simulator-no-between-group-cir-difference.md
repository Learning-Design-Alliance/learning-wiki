`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary endpoint analysis of a multicenter cluster-randomized trial (12 trainees, 11 institutions, Japan and Taiwan), using mixed-effects logistic regression with trainee as random intercept and pre-training CIR as covariate. The article reports "no statistically significant between‐group difference in post‐training CIR"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Post‐training CIR was 80.0% (72/90) in the mikoto group versus 77.8% (70/90) in controls (adjusted OR, 1.34; 95% confidence interval, 0.37–4.84; p = 0.606)."
