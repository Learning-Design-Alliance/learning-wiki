## Evidence

### Abbiati Milena 2016

Abbiati Milena, Baroffio Anne, Gerbase Margaret W. (2016). Personal profile of medical students selected through a knowledge-based exam only: are we missing suitable students? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.29705

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression on the 311-student cohort with selection as the binary outcome; Table 5 prints OR 1.4 [1.1; 1.9], p=0.016 for the diligent facet. The odds ratio is unstandardised, so no standardised effect size is reported.

> "Logistic regression analysis (Table 5) confirmed that the profile's facet of 'diligence' increased the odds of being selected."
