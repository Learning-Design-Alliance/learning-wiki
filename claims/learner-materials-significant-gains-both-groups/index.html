Evidence¶

Brahm Fleisch 2011¶

Brahm Fleisch, Nick Taylor, Roelien Herholdt and Ingrid Sapire. (2011). Evaluation of Back to Basics mathematics workbooks: a randomised control trial of the Primary Mathematics Research Project. South African Journal of Education, Vol 31:488-504. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136612

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pre/post comparison within each group of the 44-school RCT: Table 5 reports mean gains of 8.62 (CM) and 8.13 (PM) percentage points, each "statistically significant... (p value of 0 .00)". No effect size is printed.

"We found that not only did both the Primary Mathematics and Classroom Mathematics groups make statistically significant gains between the pre-test and the post-test, but the magnitude of gains was substantially higher than most interventions in South Africa typically achieve"
