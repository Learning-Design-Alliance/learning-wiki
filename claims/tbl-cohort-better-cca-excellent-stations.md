### Drovandi Aaron 2026

Drovandi Aaron, Sheader Elizabeth, Kingston Margaret. (2026). Integrated Team-Based Learning in a UK Undergraduate Medical Programme. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70470

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of clinical competency assessment (CCA) outcomes between the TBL cohort (n = 433) and two previous PBL cohorts (n = 507 and n = 416). Findings show the three cohorts performed similarly in passing their CCA stations, though the TBL cohort performed significantly better in having stations marked as 'excellent' compared with PBL cohorts (both p < 0.05).
