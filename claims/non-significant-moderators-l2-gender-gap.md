### Chen 2026

Chen, X., Zhou, Z., & Padilla, A. M. (2026). Do Females Outperform Males on Second/Foreign Language (L2) Achievement? A Meta-Analytical Review across Three Decades. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10092-6

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Omnibus moderator tests in the three-level mixed-effects models found no significant effects for these eight study features; equivalence was not tested. Subgroup point estimates still varied (e.g., speaking g = 0.49).

> "all other remaining study moderators including — publication year ( F(1, 135) = 0.007, p > 0.05), mean age ( F(1, 17) = 0.91, p > 0.05), sampling method ( F(1, 42) = 1.71, p > 0.05), target language (F(2, 134) = 1.39, p > 0.05), L2 as a major (F(1, 89) = 2.88, p > 0.05), L2 proficiency level (F(3, 60) = 1.41, p > 0.05), L2 achievement measure ( F(1, 135) = 0.10, p > 0.05), and L2 achievement mode ( F(5, 131) = 0.77, p > 0.05)), all showed non-significant effects."
