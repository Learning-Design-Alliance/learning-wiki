## Evidence

### Cheng Wei-Qin 2025

Cheng Wei-Qin, He Ling, Cai Jin-Hua, Fan Xiao. (2025). Hybrid BOPPPS model in radiology education: a case study of pediatric undergraduates. BMC Medical Education, 25. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07279-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire comparison of the 205 BOPPPS and 213 LBL students. Self-learning ability means were 4.10 ± 0.55 (BOPPPS) vs 3.97 ± 0.58 (LBL), t = 2.270; study pressure 3.70 ± 0.56 vs 3.63 ± 0.54, t = 1.277. No effect sizes were printed.

> "However, the difference in self-learning ability improvement became non-significant post-correction (p = 0.024 > 0.00625). There was no significant difference in study pressure between the two groups (p = 0.202)."
