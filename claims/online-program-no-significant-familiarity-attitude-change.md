## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental one-group pretest–posttest study (AlOtaibi et al. 2024, Saudi Arabia) of a structured 10-hour online educational program with six modules delivered over two consecutive days, in 88 registered nurses. The review reports "p > 0.05" for the pre–post contrasts; no effect size is printed.

> "Slight improvements observed in familiarity and attitudes toward disaster preparedness; overall familiarity increased from 3.16 ± 1.39 (pre) to 3.26 ± 1.18 (post), and attitudes increased from 2.26 ± 0.34 to 2.29 ± 0.31. However, no statistically significant differences were found between pre‐ and post‐intervention assessments (p > 0.05)."
