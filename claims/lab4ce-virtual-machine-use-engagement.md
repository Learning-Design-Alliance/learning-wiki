# Using Lab4CE is associated with higher engagement in remote Linux practice than installing a local virtual machine (more than 50% vs 25% of students)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Julien Broisin 2017

Julien Broisin, Rémi Venant & Philippe Vidal. (2017). Lab4CE: a Remote Laboratory for Computer Education. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 27:154–180. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0079-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory usage analysis of 139 first-year undergraduates during four Linux practical sessions; use of Lab4CE was voluntary. The authors interpret the usage differential as "a positive effect of Lab4CE on learners' engagement"; no effect size is printed.

> "more than 50 % of the students used a virtual machine made available through the Lab4CE environment, whereas only 25 % of them (i.e., 31 students) installed a virtual Linux-based machine on their own computer."
