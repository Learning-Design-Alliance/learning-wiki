## Evidence

### Jiang 2016

Jiang, D. (2016). An Empirical Study on Alleviating Career English Writing Anxiety through Cooperative Learning in a Chinese Polytechnic Institute. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(1), 173. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n1p173

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semi-structured interviews with nine students selected by anxiety level, conducted during the experiment. Students attributed relief to the shared-risk principle: "Since the credit is given to the whole group, no humiliation is personal."

> "One of the most frequently mentioned advantages of cooperative writing was the humiliation -free environment."
