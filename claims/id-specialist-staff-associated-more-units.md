Trollor JN, Eagleson C, Turner B, Tracy J, Torr JJ, Durvasula S, Iacono T, Cvejic RC, Lennox N. (2018). Intellectual disability content within tertiary medical curriculum: how is it taught and by whom? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1286-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of Phase 2 survey data from 12 schools comparing unit counts by staff expertise; no statistical test is reported. Schools without specialised staff offered "an average of 2.67 intellectual disability units (range = 2-3 units)".

> "Nine schools had staff members who specialised or had a particular interest in intellectual disability and these schools offered an average of 4.67 intellectual disability units (compulsory and elective; range = 1-12 units)."
