## Evidence

### Lee Grace Y 2013

Lee Grace Y, Yip Calvin CK, Yu Edwin CS, Man David WK. (2013). Evaluation of a computer-assisted errorless learning-based memory training program for patients with early Alzheimer's disease in Hong Kong: a pilot study. Clinical Interventions in Aging. https://doi.org/10.2147/CIA.S45726

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Carry-forward analysis (Wilcoxon signed rank within groups, Kruskal-Wallis between groups) of the pilot trial's three-month follow-up. The article notes "Deterioration in many outcome score items was observed after three months." No effect sizes are printed.

> "The carry-forward effect was defined as the change in the three-month follow-up score from the post-test score, and no significant differences were detected between the three groups in any of the outcome variables."
