## Evidence

### Bruijns BA 2022

Bruijns BA, Vanderloo LM, Johnson AM, Adamo KB, Burke SM, Carson V, Heydon R, Irwin JD, Naylor P-J, Timmons BW, Tucker P. (2022). Change in pre- and in-service early childhood educators’ knowledge, self-efficacy, and intentions following an e-learning course in physical activity and sedentary behaviour: a pilot study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-022-12591-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-specific McNemar Chi-Square analysis of ECEs' guideline knowledge in the pre-post pilot. The article reports the printed percentages and test statistic; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "when asked to select the appropriate screen time guideline for a 3-year-old, only 11.6% of ECEs indicated the correct time limit pre-course, whereas 61.9% of ECEs selected the correct answer after completing the course (X2 [117] = 50.21, p = .000)."
