q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the integration section. The article argues that without integration such overlap leads to measurement redundancy and ambiguous interpretation, and gives compensatory examples such as a highly motivated student offsetting limited executive functioning with increased time and effort.

"Redundancy arises when similar outcomes, such as persistence, are explained by different constructs (e.g., conscientiousness versus personal interest)."
