Umbrella review synthesis of moderator findings: the article notes all students, regardless of initial academic standing, benefitted from TBL.

### Alizadeh Maryam 2024 (2)

Alizadeh Maryam, Masoomi Rasoul, Mafinejad Mahboobeh Khabaz, Parmelee Dean, Khalaf Rusul Jasim, Norouzi Ali. (2024). Team-based learning in health professions education: an umbrella review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06147-x

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Umbrella review synthesis: one meta-analysis reported freshmen derived greater benefits in theoretical scores with no statistically significant difference in skill scores; nursing students showed higher grades, satisfaction, engagement and attitude in two reviews.

> "According to a single metanalysis, female students in a TBL course experienced a greater increase in examination scores compared to male students in China [25]."
