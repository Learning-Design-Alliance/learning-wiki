## Evidence

### Chuey 2021

Chuey, A., McCarthy, A., Lockhart, K., Trouche, E., Sheskin, M. & Keil, F. (2021). No guts, no glory: underestimating the benefits of providing children with mechanistic details. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00108-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear regression on part-name task with 180 6–9-year-olds in immediate-test, delayed-test, and control conditions; Bonferroni-adjusted post hoc comparisons. Immediate-test (M = 4.37) was marginally better than delayed-test (M = 4.02), "t(174) = 2.08, p = 0.079"; delayed beat control (M = 1.92, p < 0.001). Memory improved with age, p = 0.002.

> "Children in the immediate test condition (M = 4.37, SD = .82) remembered engine-part names marginally better than those in the delayed test condition (M = 4.02, SD = 1.00), t(174) = 2.08, p = 0.079"
