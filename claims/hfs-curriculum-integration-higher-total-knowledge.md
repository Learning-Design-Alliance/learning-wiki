## Evidence

### Ismail Ahmad 2026

Ismail Ahmad, Al-Modallal Hanan, Aldardeir Njoud, Gomma Nada, Eltohamy Nadia Abd ElHamed. (2026). The Impact of High‐Fidelity Simulation and Curriculum Integration on Nursing Students’ Competence and Satisfaction: A Quasiexperimental Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13140285/

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasiexperimental comparison of two groups of third-year nursing students (intervention n = 30, control n = 30) assessed two weeks after the intervention. The intervention group scored "83% vs. 50%" on total knowledge; a post hoc power analysis reported an effect size of 1.33, classified as large.

> "The total knowledge score of the students exposed to the HFS/curriculum‐integrated session was significantly higher than that of the control group (83% vs. 50%, p ≤ 0.05)."
