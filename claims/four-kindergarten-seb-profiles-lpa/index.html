Evidence¶

Collie 2018¶

Collie, Rebecca; Martin, Andrew; Nassar, N; Roberts, CL. (2018). Social and Emotional Behavioral Profiles in Kindergarten: A Population-Based Latent Profile Analysis of Links to Socio-Educational Characteristics and Later Achievement. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000262

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Study 1 applied latent profile analysis to the population of NSW kindergarten students in 2012 (N = 100,776) based on five SEBs; the analysis "revealed four different SEB profiles" as named in the abstract's results statement.

"Results revealed four different SEB profiles: social-emotional prosocial (SE-Prosocial), SE-Anxious, SE-Aggressive, and SE-Vulnerable groups."

Related Claims¶

  • Kindergarten SEB profiles are associated with significantly different achievement in Grades 3 and 5, highest for SE-Prosocial and lowest for SE-Vulnerable — related
  • Kindergarten SEB profile membership is differentially associated with gender, age, language background, SES, and learning disability status — related
  • The four SEB profiles replicate in an independent 2009 kindergarten cohort (n = 52,661) — related
  • Latent profile analysis of kindergarten pre-reading measures yields six distinct performance profiles, including PA risk, RAN risk, and double-deficit risk groups — related
