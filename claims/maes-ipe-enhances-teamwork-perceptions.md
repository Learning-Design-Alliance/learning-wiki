## Evidence

### Fenzi Giulio 2024

Fenzi Giulio, Alemán-Jiménez Carolina, López-Ferrándiz Lucia, Leal-Costa Cesar, Díaz-Agea José Luis. (2024). Enhancing Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Training: An Interprofessional Approach With Undergraduate Medicine and Nursing Students Using Self-Learning Methodology in Simulated Environments (MAES)—A Qualitative Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/9470402

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of focus groups and structured debriefings with 23 final-year nursing and medical students at two Spanish universities. Students reported that "The dynamics help us to get to know each other better and to work as a team" (S3), and that interprofessional presence improved responsibility and specific skills (S11).

> "The MAES methodology significantly enhances teamwork in interprofessional settings, as students noted its positive impact on scenario design, simulation, and debriefing, fostering better collaboration and nontechnical skill development."
