## Evidence

### Ngoc Lan Duong Thi 2025

Ngoc Lan Duong Thi, Quynh Anh Nguyen Ngoc, Hoang Thi Phuong Thao. (2025). A pre – post quasi-experimental study of team-based learning effectiveness for Vietnamese nursing students. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323656

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post administration of the four-item Perceived Collective Efficacy scale (Cronbach's alpha 0.88) to the same 186 students; paired t-test in the results section gives t = -8.667, p < 0.001 for PCE. No effect size is printed.

> "CES and PCE scores also increased significantly post-TBL (31.37 ± 2.002 vs. 29.54 ± 2.186; t = -8.981, p < 0.001; 4.03 ± 0.488 vs. 3.64 ± 0.461, t = -8.667, p < 0.001)."
