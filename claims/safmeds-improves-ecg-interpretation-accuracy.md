## Evidence

### Rabbitt Louise 2020

Rabbitt Louise, Byrne Dara, O'Connor Paul, Gorecka Miroslawa, Jacobsen Alan, Lydon Sinéad. (2020). A pragmatic randomised controlled trial of SAFMEDS to produce fluency in interpretation of electrocardiograms. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02021-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pragmatic RCT with 13 intervention and 14 control final-year medical students; ANCOVA compared post-test percentage accuracy controlling for baseline. The article reports a "significant effect of group assignment" with "partial η2 = .67 (large effect)"; intervention M = 61.5% (SD = 12.10) vs control M = 31.63% (SD = 12.5).

> "There was also a significant effect of group assignment identified on post-test performance, F(1,24) = 48.8, p < .001, partial η2 = .67 (large effect)."
