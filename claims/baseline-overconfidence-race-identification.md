`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Week-two opening exercise in both courses (N = 97): students completed the race and ethnicity section of the 2010 U.S. Census, then were asked hypothetically whether they could identify others' race from pictures or country of origin; most (94%) said they would have no difficulty.

> "In both classes, 97% of the students reported ha v- ing no issue filling out the form and were confident in their understandings of their own race and ethnicity."
