The editorial cites survey research by Spilovoy et al. (2020) on faculty selection of classroom materials, reporting that "only 14% actually use them in any way" despite favorable beliefs. No effect size is printed.

> "even though most higher education faculty in my country (the U.S.) believe that open educational resources (OER) are a good idea, only 14% actually use them in any way, and only 8% of those aware of focused initiatives at their institutions will commit to using OER in the next three years (Spilovoy et al. 2020)."
