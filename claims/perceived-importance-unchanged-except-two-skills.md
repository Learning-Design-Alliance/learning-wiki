### Hvidt EA 2018

Hvidt EA, Ammentorp J, Søndergaard J, Timmermann C, Hansen DG, Hvidt NC. (2018). Developing and evaluating a course programme to enhance existential communication with cancer patients in general practice. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2018.1459235

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative evaluation of perceived importance ratings on a 1–5 Likert scale at T1 and T2 for the nineteen course participants; Table 2(b) reports the distribution of score changes. The exception was "Communication about R/S concerns" and "Active listening and being present".

> "The GPs’ perceived importance of the eight communication skills did not change significantly from T1 to T2 except for their experiences of the questions: “Communication about R/S concerns” and “Active listening and being present”."
