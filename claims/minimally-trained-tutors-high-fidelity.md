## Evidence

### Lindo 2018

Lindo, E. J., Weiser, B., Cheatham, J. P., & Allor, J. H. (2018). Benefits of structured after-school literacy tutoring by university students for struggling elementary readers. Reading & Writing Quarterly, 34(2), 117–131. https://doi.org/10.1080/10573569.2017.1357156

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fidelity observations of 13.5% of tutoring sessions, twice each semester, by research assistants using observation forms covering corrective feedback, organization, script adherence, and praise. The article notes interrater reliability observations were not conducted because of limited resources.

> "Fidelity of implementation over both semesters ranged from 84% to 100%, with the average being 96.3%."
