### Stirparo Giuseppe 2024

Stirparo Giuseppe, Bellini Lorenzo, Solla Daniele, Stirparo Pierfrancesco, Fagoni Nazzareno, Gambolò Luca. (2024). Should basic life support–defibrillator training be compulsory for newly licensed Italian physicians? An observational study. Journal of Cardiovascular Medicine. https://doi.org/10.2459/JCM.0000000000001645

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline assessment in a retrospective observational study of 120 physicians no more than 6 months after graduation, using an AHA BLS course questionnaire and 2 minutes of mannequin CPR without feedback. The study found "only 19 (15.8%) were able to perform effective CPR with adequate depth and frequency of compressions."

> "Fifty-nine physicians (49.2%) showed good knowledge of the theoretical basis of CPR, 37 (30.8%) were able to perform CPR on a mannequin, but only 19 (15.8%) were able to perform effective CPR with adequate depth and frequency of compressions."
