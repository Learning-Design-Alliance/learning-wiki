## Evidence

### Filippini 2012

Filippini, A. L., Gerber, M. M., & Leafstedt, J. M. (2012). A Vocabulary-Added Reading Intervention for English Learners At-Risk of Reading Difficulties. International Journal of Special Education, 27(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1001055

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on NWF gain scores (no missing pretest or posttest data) comparing PAD-only, vocab+ SR, and vocab+ MA conditions. The article reports "the average student in the SR group performed at the 64 th percentile of the PAD group ( d = .37)" and d = .44 for MA; within-group pre-post effect sizes were d = .49 (SR), d = .33 (MA), d = .17 (PAD).

> "The results of the ANOVA did not show the anticipated main effect, F(2, 68) = 1.02, p = .367."
