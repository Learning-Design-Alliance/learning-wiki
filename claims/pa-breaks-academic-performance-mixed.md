`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of six academic-performance studies with mixed findings; GRADE certainty for academic performance was rated very low due to low study quality, heterogeneity, and mixed results.

> "Six studies assessed academic performance, with four studies indicating improvements [23, 28, 30, 32] and two showing no effects [35, 38], also yielding unclear evidence (66.7%)."

### Deng Qianting; Wu Chongyun; Yang Luodan 2026 (2)

Deng Qianting; Wu Chongyun; Yang Luodan. (2026). Effect of classroom physical activity breaks on learning behaviors and academic achievements in university students: a systematic review. Int J Behav Nutr Phys Act. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-026-01936-7

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Null comparisons of active workstations versus traditional desks on exam scores; the review reads these as evidence workstations do not impair cognition, not as tested equivalence.

> "studies evaluating active workstations found no significant differences in academic exam scores between users of these workstations and those using traditional desks, suggesting that active workstations do not impair cognitive performance [30, 35]."
