## Evidence

### Bayram Hilal Çelik 2025

Bayram Hilal Çelik, Ergün Sibel. (2025). Effect of screen-based simulation on pediatric drug administration among nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2025-0249en

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 198 nursing students randomized (n = 99 per group) after a four-hour verbal lecture, with the intervention group additionally completing screen-based simulation scenarios. Independent-samples t-test showed the intervention group's post-test self-efficacy was "statistically significantly higher." No effect size was printed.

> "The mean post-test score of the intervention group was found to be 68.08 ± 10.77. The mean posttest score for the same scale was 50.81 ± 13.91 in the control group, and the mean score of the intervention group was found to be statistically significantly higher."
