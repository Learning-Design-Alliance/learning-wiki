# Need satisfaction and autonomy support did not predict external regulation, and introjected associations did not hold in the full model

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Variance in external regulation explained by need satisfaction was negligible (R2 = .004) in relative weight analysis. [→ Bureau 2022](#bureau-2022)
`q3 i?` Small meta-analytic associations between both autonomy support sources and introjected regulation did not hold when needs and autonomy support predicted introjection within a single model. [→ Bureau 2022 (2)](#bureau-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Bureau 2022

Bureau, J. S., Howard, J. L., Chong, J. X. Y., & Guay, F. (2022). Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research, 92(1), 46–72. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211042426

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Relative weight analysis of the three psychological needs predicting motivation types from pooled meta-analytic correlations. For external regulation, "variance explained by needs was negligible (R2 = .004), making the relative weight analysis meaningless"; the MASEM models likewise gave external regulation a mean R2 of .01.

> "For external regulation, variance explained by needs was negligible (R2 = .004), making the relative weight analysis meaningless."

### Bureau 2022 (2)

Bureau, J. S., Howard, J. L., Chong, J. X. Y., & Guay, F. (2022). Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research, 92(1), 46–72. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211042426

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

The discussion of the MASEM results reports that small bivariate autonomy-support associations with introjected regulation "did not hold when needs and autonomy support predicted introjection within a single model", with only small consistent positive indirect effects on introjected regulation.

> "despite meta-analytic correlations showing a small association between both autonomy support sources and introjected regulation, these associations did not hold when needs and autonomy support predicted introjection within a single model."
