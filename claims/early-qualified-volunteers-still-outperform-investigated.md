## Evidence

### Mehdizadeh Leila 2015

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Dacre Jane. (2015). Are the General Medical Council’s Tests of Competence fair to long standing doctors? A retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0362-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Limitations section of the retrospective cohort study comparing both groups. The authors conclude there is "no clear evidence that long standing doctors under investigation are disadvantaged by the test of competence format", since a format-familiarity disadvantage would show a similar pattern among volunteers.

> "It should however be noted that volunteer doctors who graduated between 1970 and 1992 still performed better on all outcomes than doctors in the fitness to practise group."
