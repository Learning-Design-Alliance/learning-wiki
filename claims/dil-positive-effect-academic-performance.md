## Evidence

### Mehrvarz 2021

Mehrvarz, Mahboobe; Heidari, Elham; Farrokhnia, Mohammadreza; Noroozi, Omid. (2021). The mediating role of digital informal learning in the relationship between students' digital competence and their academic performance. Computers & Education 167 (2021) 104184. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104184

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey SEM analysis (AMOS) of 319 students at Shiraz University, Iran, testing H1. The article prints the DIL-to-performance path as "(β = 24, p = 0.0001)", an unstandardised coefficient with no standardised effect size, so no effect magnitude can be coded.

> "The findings indicated that increasing DIL had a positive effect on improving students' academic performance (β = 24, p = 0.0001), which confirmed our first research hypothesis (i.e., H1)."
