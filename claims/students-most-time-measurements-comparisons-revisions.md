### Laura Ríos 2019

Laura Ríos, Benjamin Pollard, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2019). Using think-aloud interviews to characterize model-based reasoning in electronics for a laboratory course assessment. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010140)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Aggregated coded data from 10 TAPS interviews with upper-division physics students, counting 30-second intervals per Modeling Framework subtask. The authors report students "spent the most amount of time in the Make Measurements, Make Compar- isons, and Enact Revisions subtasks," with much less time on Propose Causes and Construct Models.

> "We found that students spent the most amount of time in the Make Measurements, Make Compar- isons, and Enact Revisions subtasks."
