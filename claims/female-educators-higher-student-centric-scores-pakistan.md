### Sukkurwalla 2024

Sukkurwalla, A., Zaidi, S.J.A., Taqi, M., Waqar, Z., & Qureshi, A. (2024). Exploring medical educators’ perspectives on teaching effectiveness and student learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06465-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey using the Approaches to Teaching Inventory with 93 medical and dental educators in Karachi, analysed with Mann-Whitney U tests. The authors report "The p-value obtained from the test was 0.001"; the quote names the winning group: "female teachers scoring higher than their male counterparts did".

> "The analysis indicated that gender significantly influenced student-centric scores, with female teachers scoring higher than their male counterparts did."
