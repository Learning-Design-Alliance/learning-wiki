Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day 2003¶

Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day, with the assistance of David Wakelyn. (2003). External Support to Schools on Probation: Getting a Leg Up? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED474395 doi:10.1037/e565292006-001

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview and observation study of 11 of the 18 external partners serving probation schools; the authors report that "Only 4 of the 11 partners included in this study" had a comprehensive, research-based literacy approach. No effect size reported.

"One rather surprising finding was that most support providers implement strategies that only weakly attempt to improve literacy. Only 4 of the 11 partners included in this study had developed a comprehensive, research-based approach to literacy instruction."
