Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting 2024¶

Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting. (2024). Linking Disparate Strands: A Critical Review of the Relationship Between Creativity and Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09973-z

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The review reports a study of Dutch 9–13-year-olds in which "children who showed stronger verbal convergent thinking performed better on single- and multi-solution mathematical problems", attributed to logical thinking and implementing set techniques.

"In Dutch 9–13-year-olds, children who showed stronger verbal convergent thinking performed better on single- and multi-solution mathematical problems than those with weaker CT performance (de Vink et al., 2022)."
