## Evidence

### Phehla 2024

Phehla, L.B., Makhene, A. & Matshaka, L., 2024, 'Student nurses' utilisation of self-directed simulation learning at a University in Gauteng,' Curationis 47(1), a2490. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v47i1.2490

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group finding from the subtheme on a compacted academic timetable. A 31-year-old female participant described that "our schedules [timetable] are very congested from first year already", leaving class, preceptored simulation and work-integrated learning hours with little free time.

> "Okay, so our schedules [timetable] are very congested from first year already. There is time wherein we are expected to be in class for theory and then we've got allocated time for simulation laboratory with preceptors"
