### Adriana Postiglione 2021

Adriana Postiglione, Ilaria De Angelis. (2021). Experience gravity in the classroom using the rubber sheet: an educational proposal from the collaboration between University and School. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abcab4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abcab4)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational report following the annual Modern Physics teacher course in which the authors "presented the activity and trained the 25 participants to the use the exhibit". Two teachers followed the first educational card's model and one built an improved closable version; the authors cite this as a first confirmation of the approach's feasibility.

> "as soon as the course finished in February 2020 (before Italian schools were closed due to Covid-19 lockdown), three of the teachers who attended it immediately built the DIY structure, thus long before the publication of the book."
