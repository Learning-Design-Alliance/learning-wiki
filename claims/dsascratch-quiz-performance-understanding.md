`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two multiple-choice quizzes administered during the three-day workshop: Quiz-1 (5 questions) on basic Scratch block categories and Quiz-2 (11 questions) on data-structure and algorithm fundamentals taught with DSAScratch. For Quiz-2 the article prints only that "most students understood the basic concepts" (Fig. 8 shows the 10 participants' scores). No effect sizes or test statistics are printed.

> "On Quiz-1 8 (80%) of the participants got all the questions correct, whereas 2 (20%) participants got 4 on 5."
