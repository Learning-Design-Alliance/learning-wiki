## Evidence

### Rashid Mustafa S 2011

Rashid Mustafa S, Sobowale O, Gore D. (2011). A near-peer teaching program designed, developed and delivered exclusively by recent medical graduates for final year medical students sitting the final objective structured clinical examination (OSCE). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-11

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feedback on seven lectures from 118 students (812 responses); descriptive Likert percentages and means only, no inferential statistics.

> "Students agreed or strongly agreed the content delivered in the lectures was relevant to the final OSCE in 92.4% (n = 750) of responses (mean Likert score 4.42/5.00; SD ± 1.16)."

### Rashid Mustafa S 2011 (2)

Rashid Mustafa S, Sobowale O, Gore D. (2011). A near-peer teaching program designed, developed and delivered exclusively by recent medical graduates for final year medical students sitting the final objective structured clinical examination (OSCE). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-11

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feedback on the small-group seminar rotation from the same 118-student cohort; descriptive Likert data only.

> "Of the 1153 responses regarding small group seminars 91.4% (n = 1054) agreed or strongly agreed the aims and objectives of the sessions were clear whilst 96.4% (n = 1112) found the content covered was directly relevant to their OSCE preparation (mean Likert score 4.52/5.00; SD ± 1.26)."
