### Hawley Chelsea E. 2019

Hawley Chelsea E., Triantafylidis Laura K., Phillips Sarah C., Schwartz Andrea Wershof. (2019). Brown Bag Simulation to Improve Medication Management in Older Adults. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10857

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre- and postsimulation self-efficacy assessment plus a postsimulation knowledge assessment with 32 learners from six professions (pharmacy 17, medicine 9, rehabilitation therapy 2, psychology 2, social work 1, advanced practical nursing 1) on a geriatrics rotation. The article reports significant improvement (p < .05) with no effect size printed.
