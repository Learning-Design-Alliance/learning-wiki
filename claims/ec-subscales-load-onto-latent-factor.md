`q3 · i?`

The SEM model confirmed "all subscales were part of an effortful control latent factor" with standardized loadings from .52 to .73 (all p < .001), consistent with previous research.

> "As expected, attentional focusing (β = .73, p < .001), inhibitory control (β = .70, p < .001), perceptual sensitivity (β = .52, p < .001), and low intensity pleasure (β = .55, p < .001), all loaded onto our effortful control latent variable (Fig. 3)."
