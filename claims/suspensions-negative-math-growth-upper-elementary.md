# Suspensions showed significant negative associations with math growth in grades 3-5, with increasingly negative effects

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In GLM models predicting math growth, suspensions were significantly negatively associated with growth in grades 3-5 (B = -0.79*, -0.98**, -0.92**), with increasingly negative effects. [→ Bahar 2025](#bahar-2025)

## Evidence

### Bahar 2025

Bahar, K., & Auletto, A. (2025). Exploring the Impact of School Discipline and Attendance on Academic Achievement. E4 Center, Northwestern University. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2025/05/05/exploring-the-impact-of-school-discipline-and-attendance-on-academic-achievement/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

GLM regression models predicting i-Ready math scale-score growth from Fall 2021 to Spring 2022 for K-5 students (Table 1). Suspension coefficients for grades 3-5 were -0.79 (p<.05), -0.98 (p<.01), and -0.92 (p<.01); coefficients are unstandardized, so no effect size is printed.

> "For math, suspensions significantly impacted grades 3-5, with increasingly negative effects."
