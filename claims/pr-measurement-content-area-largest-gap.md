# Puerto Rico–nation score gaps in NAEP mathematics were largest in the measurement content area (80 points at grade 8 in 2003; 76 points in 2005), with smaller gaps in geometry.

Content-area analysis of the 2003 grade 8 NAEP mathematics assessment comparing Puerto Rico and national public school students across the five NAEP content areas. The report prints gaps of "between 51 and 67 points" and "80 points" for measurement; the 2005 grade 8 measurement gap was 76 points.

> "Students from Puerto Rico scored lower than students from the nation in all five NAEP mathematics content areas. The difference between students in Puerto Rico and the nation was between 51 and 67 points for all content areas except measurement, where the difference was 80 points (figure 8)."
