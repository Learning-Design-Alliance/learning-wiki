## Evidence

### Melesse Girma Tufa 2024

Melesse Girma Tufa, Amde Tewodros, Tezera Robel. (2024). Competency in evidence‐based medicine and associated factors among medical radiology technologists in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.777

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable binary logistic regression on the cross-sectional survey data; the study reports AOR = 1.892 (95% CI 1.140–3.141) for having time versus not, with lack of time to search and read research papers reported by 51.5%.

> "MRTs who had enough time to search and apply research evidence were 1.89 times (AOR = 1.892, 95% CI = 1.140, 3.141) higher odds to have a good EBM practice compared to those who do not."

### Melesse Girma Tufa 2024 (2)

Melesse Girma Tufa, Amde Tewodros, Tezera Robel. (2024). Competency in evidence‐based medicine and associated factors among medical radiology technologists in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.777

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same multivariable logistic regression; the study reports AOR = 2.093 (95% CI 1.271–3.440) for institutional support and AOR = 1.653 (95% CI 1.028–2.656) for EBM resource access, with lack of free internet access the most reported barrier (64.8%).

> "The odds of having a good EBM practice were 2.09 times (AOR = 2.093, 95% CI = 1.271, 3.440) higher for MRTs who have institutional support than those who do not have institutional support. MRTs who had access to EBM resources were 1.65 times (1.028, 2.656) more likely to practice EBM than others who did not have access to EBM resources"
