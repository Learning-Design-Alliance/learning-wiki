## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Cyber leaders reported that teenagers would rather hear cyber safety messages from someone their own age than from adults, and that younger students trusted them and sought their advice. [→ Cross et al. 2015](#cross-et-al-2015)

## Evidence

### Cross et al. 2015

Donna Cross, Leanne Lester, Amy Barnes, Patricia Cardoso and Kate Hadwen. (2015). If it’s about me, why do it without me? Genuine student engagement in school cyberbullying education. International Journal of Emotional Education, 7(1), 35-51. www.um.edu.mt/cres/ijee. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085606

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of in-depth interviews with cyber leaders (12 interviews in 2010; 30 in 2011), coded with open, axial and selective processes with 80% inter-coder agreement. Leaders also described students approaching them for advice about cyber issues.

> "The cyber leaders also reported they believed the information they were providing would be perceived by other students as more credible or relevant, than from adults."
