de Jong, T., Gillet, D., Rodríguez-Triana, M. J., Hovardas, T., Dikke, D., Doran, R., Dziabenko, O., Koslowsky, J., Korventausta, M., Law, E., Pedaste, M., Tasiopoulou, E., Vidal, G., & Zacharia, Z. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of 2414 implemented ILSs created during the reference period July 2015 to December 2019. The study traced design starting points and found "the majority (75.56%) of the implemented ILSs originated in Graasp, starting from an empty ILS that the teacher populated with materials" rather than from published ILSs or labs on the sharing platform.
