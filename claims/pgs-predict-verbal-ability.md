## Evidence

### Guang Guo 2022

Guang Guo, Meng-Jung Lin and Kathleen Mullan Harris. (2022). Socioeconomic and genomic roots of verbal ability from current evidence. npj Science of Learning 7:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00137-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel models of verbal ability on the two PGSs without SES (Table 3, combined sample N = 7194; OLS R2 0.126). The article reports the education PGS coefficient of 2.27 and ability PGS coefficient of 0.44, both statistically significant.

> "one standard deviation of the education PGS is associated with 2.27 points of verbal ability, and one standard deviation of the ability PGS is associated with 0.44 points of verbal ability"
