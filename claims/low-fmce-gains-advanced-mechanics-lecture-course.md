Michael C. Wittmann. (2002). Limitations in predicting student performance on standardized tests. Originally published 1998. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0209006](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0209006)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre/post FMCE administration to 10 matched students in a University of Maine sophomore mechanics course on the first and last day of class. The article reports the class overall FMCE gain as 0.14 in Figure 1, described as "normalized gains were low".

> "Student performance on the FMCE before instruction was weak, and their normalized gains were low (see Figure 1). This is consistent with other courses in which little active learning is used."
