# Four-year college graduation rates rose from 56.0 to 59.4 percent between 2003 and 2013, with larger gains at public institutions (4.9 points) than private nonprofits (2.3 points)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kimberlee Eberle-Sudré 2015

Kimberlee Eberle-Sudré, Meredith Welch, and Andrew Howard Nichols. (2015). Rising Tide: Do College Grad Rate Gains Benefit All Students? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566656

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of IPEDS six-year graduation rates for 1,309 four-year institutions, using three-year weighted averages. The report states rates "increased from 56.0 percent to 59.4 percent," and that public institutions gained 4.9 percentage points versus 2.3 at private nonprofits (Figure 1).

> "Between 2003 and 2013, graduation rates among students at public and private nonprofit four-year colleges increased from 56.0 percent to 59.4 percent."
