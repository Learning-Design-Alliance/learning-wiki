Evidence¶

Alsamadani 2011¶

Hashem Ahmed Alsamadani. (2011). The Effects of the 3-2-1 Reading Strategy on EFL Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 4(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v4n3p184

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Item-level ANCOVA on the 85-student quasi-experiment at alpha = .01, with η² = .52 (partial eta squared = .52), a large printed effect size. The author's stated explanation is that "students used the discussion time to determine their understanding (comprehension) and utilized questions to strengthen this understanding".

"Theses results show a strong effect of strategy use on the students' ability to answer questions about text details, F (1, 82) = 88.23, P = .000, partial eta squared = .52."
