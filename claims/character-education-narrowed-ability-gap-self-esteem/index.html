Evidence¶

Tannir 2013¶

Tannir, A., & Al-Hroub, A. (2013). Effects of character education on the self-esteem of intellectually able and less able elementary students in Kuwait. International Journal of Special Education, 28(1), 47-59. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1023237

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis from Figure 2 data. The pre-treatment gap of 4.71 points narrowed to 1.67 points after the character education program, as the less able group improved while the able group declined slightly.

"The intellectually able self-esteem level started at 23.21 and intellectually less able group started at 18.50 making the difference between the groups 4.71. After the treatment, the intellectually able mean self-esteem slightly decreased to 22.50, while the intellectually less able group increased to 20.83."
