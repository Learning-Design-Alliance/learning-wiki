## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Pelaez 2019

Pelaez, K., Levine, R. A., Guarcello, M., Laumakis, M., & Fan, J. (2019). Using a Latent Class Forest to Identify At-Risk Students in Higher Education. Journal of Educational Data Mining, 11(1). https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/283

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational application of the LCF algorithm to 2188 deidentified PSY 101 students from three semesters, with chi-squared tests comparing each group's DFW proportion to the rest of the class (High group p-value 0.004). The article reports "a High category with the highest DFW percentage of 28%".

> "We identified a High category with the highest DFW percentage of 28%, a Mid category with a DFW percentage of 12%, and a Low category with a DFW percentage of 6%."
