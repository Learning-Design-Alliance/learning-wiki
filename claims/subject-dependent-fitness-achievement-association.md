Subject-wise comparison pooling the four highly-fit groups against the 0F group in the nationwide BCTJH dataset. "average improvement in test scores of 2.2 points (4.3%) in language" up to 3.4 points (6.7%) in math, which "suggest a subject-dependent association."

> "students who were highly fit for at least one year showed an average improvement in test scores of 2.2 points (4.3%) in language, 2.8 points (5.5%) in foreign language, 3.1 points (6.1%) in social science, 3.3 points (6.5%) in science, and 3.4 points (6.7%) in math"
