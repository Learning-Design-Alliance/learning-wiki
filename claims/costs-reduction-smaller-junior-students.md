## Evidence

### Leiva Hernández Carolina 2026

Leiva Hernández Carolina, Aguilera Felipe Rodrigo, Aravena-Castillo Verónica, Hidalgo V. Viviana, Farías Orellana Javier, Stuardo-Parada Adriana. (2026). Dental students’ attitudes toward community service: a pre–post survey study of a service-learning experience in Chile. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10109-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis of the costs subscale by academic level (Junior n = 25, Intermediate n = 19, Senior n = 33) found juniors changed −0.07 (p = 0.716) while intermediates changed −1.03 and seniors −1.17; "the reduction was significantly smaller among junior students than among intermediate (p = 0.003) and senior students (p < 0.001)".

> "Tukey-adjusted comparisons confirmed that the reduction was significantly smaller among junior students than among intermediate (p = 0.003) and senior students (p < 0.001), with no significant difference between the latter two groups (p = 0.846)"
