### Pimbblet 2024

Pimbblet, K.A., & Morrell, L.J. (2024). Can ChatGPT pass a physics degree? Making a case for reformation of assessment of undergraduate degrees. European Journal of Physics. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad9874](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad9874)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors assessed GPT-4 across all modules of a physics degree and categorised performance by task type. They report that "coding tasks are performed exceptionally well" while "Multiple step problems and longer prose are generally poorer along with interdisciplinary problems."

> "In general, coding tasks are performed exceptionally well, along with simple single-step solution problems. Multiple step problems and longer prose are generally poorer along with interdisciplinary problems."
