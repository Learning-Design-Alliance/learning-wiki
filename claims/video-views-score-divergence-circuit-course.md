# Weekly video views and scores diverged: a week of low video views preceded a fourfold surge in views with underperforming scores, then higher scores with less video attention

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Analytics dividing weekly scores by weekly video views showed that in week 3 views were lower than expected (students already excelled at Mesh analysis but had never used the Nodal method), the number of views soared by 4.3 times of average scores the next week while scores underperformed, and later students made higher scores with less video attention. Results section analytics of weekly scores divided by the number of video views, shown in Figure 5, for the 2021 cohort. The article reports the 4.3-fold surge in views with underperforming scores, and separately that students could make higher scores with less video attention than the prior week. Interpretations draw on student interviews about Mesh versus Nodal method experience.
