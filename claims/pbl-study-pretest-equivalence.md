## Evidence

### Temel 2014

Senar Temel. (2014). The effects of problem-based learning on pre-service teachers’ critical thinking dispositions and perceptions of problem-solving ability. South African Journal of Education, 34(1), Art. #769. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136398

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t tests on pre-test scores established group equivalence before the intervention: CCTDI t = 1.887, p = 0.066, and PSI t = 0.243, p = 0.809 (Table 2), so "the experimental group and control group are equivalent" at the start.

> "it can be observed that there is no significant difference between the critical thinking disposition scores of pre-service teachers in both the experimental group and control group (t = 1.887, p = 0.066)."
