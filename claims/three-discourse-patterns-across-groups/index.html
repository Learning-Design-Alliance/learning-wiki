Comparative analysis of discourse patterns across the three groups, visualized in Fig. 7. Group 2 also displayed the highest rate of off-task talk, while group 3 showed the most structured and interactive collaboration with all members contributing to discussion and artifact.

Xinran Zhu · Bodong Chen · David DeLiema 2026 (2)¶

Xinran Zhu · Bodong Chen · David DeLiema. (2026). Advancing collaborative discourse through knowledge synthesis. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09463-6

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Group-level coding analysis of group 3's discussion: "40%" of actions during the synthesis phase were regulative, intertwined with epistemic moves such as negotiating meaning and elaborating peer ideas. Aria initiated discussion by pointing out consistent structures across individual syntheses.

"Their discussion featured the highest percentage of regulative actions (40% during the synthesis phase), which supported the coordination of their work and the development of ideas."
