Qualitative thematic analysis by English subject experts of the 50 and 47 indirectly learned words reported by the two experimental groups. The second group's 47 words grouped under religion (12), death (5) and adjectives for people (7), with 23 words ungrouped.

"The 50 words were grouped under three themes such as politics (5), jobs (7), adjectives for people (11), and the other 27 words cannot be grouped under any theme in the first experimental group."
