Igor Korsun. (2017). The Formation of Learnersí Motivation to Study Physics in Terms of Sustainable Development of Education in Ukraine. Journal of Teacher Education for Sustainability, vol. 19, no. 1, pp. 117ñ128. https://doi.org/10.1515/jtes-2017-0008

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental pedagogical study with 122 learners in the control group and 121 in the experimental group, aged 15ñ17, during the 2015ñ2016 academic year. The post-test difference between groups was tested with Pearson's criterion c2, giving "c2 empirical = 6.10" against a critical value of 5.99 at the 0.05 level, supporting the alternative hypothesis.

> "Empirical value c2 empirical of measure levels of cognitive activity of CG and EG for the post-test is: c2 empirical = 6.10. Because c2 empirical > c2 0.05, then the accuracy of statistical significance of characteristics of EG and CG after the post-test is 95%."
