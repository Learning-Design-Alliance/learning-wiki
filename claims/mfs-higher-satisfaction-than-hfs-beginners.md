## Evidence

### Alconero-Camarero 2021

Alconero-Camarero, A.R., Sarabia-Cobo, C.M., Catalán-Piris, M.J., González-Gómez, S., & González-López, J.R. (2021). Nursing Students’ Satisfaction: A Comparison between Medium- and High-Fidelity Simulation Training. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18020804

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 393 second-year nursing students (207 Cantabria HFS, 186 Seville MFS) using Student's t and ANOVA on SSHF domains; Table 1 shows e.g. simulation utility 4.63 (MFS) vs 4.30 (HFS), t = 7.321, p < 0.005. The study reports "the differences are statistically significant".

> "The medium-fidelity group had higher scores in all the domains (except “Relationship between theory and practice”), and the differences are statistically significant."
