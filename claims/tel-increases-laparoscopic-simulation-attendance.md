## Evidence

### Lowe B 2022

Lowe B, Ng J, Jordan S, Waynforth D, Angstetra D, Brazil V. (2022). Test‐enhanced learning improves learner attendance during a laparoscopic box trainer simulation program. Australian and New Zealand Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13528

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective cohort study of 40 randomly allocated medical students over a ten-week box trainer program; linear regression showed higher TEL attendance. The article reports "71 vs 51.5%, P = 0.03" with the TEL group attending 143 of 200 possible attendances versus 103 in the control group.

> "Attendance in the TEL group was significantly higher than in the standard group (71 vs 51.5%, P = 0.03)."
