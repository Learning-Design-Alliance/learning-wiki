## Evidence

### Bulut 2017

Aydın Bulut. (2017). Improving 4th Grade Primary School Students' Reading Comprehension Skills. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(1): 23-30. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050103

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation-form data (coded by SQ3R stages) collected during the ten-week intervention. The authors observed students preparing with prior knowledge, visuals and keywords; two students relied on only one image instead of combining visual elements.

> "With the exception of two of them, Umut and Güler, the students were able to implement the Survey stage in their reading process. Umut and Güler also had some problems in reading the v isual cues."

### Bulut 2017 (2)

Aydın Bulut. (2017). Improving 4th Grade Primary School Students' Reading Comprehension Skills. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(1): 23-30. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050103

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation data on the Question stage of SQ3R during the intervention. Students' predictions and question formulation from visuals were weaker than the authors anticipated.

> "The success of this stage was found to not be as high as expected. At this stage, some students made predictions and created questions from only one image. Some students described the images as they appeared instead of formulating and answering questions."
