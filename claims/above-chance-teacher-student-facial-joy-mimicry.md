## Evidence

### Frenzel 2024

Frenzel, A. C., Dindar, M., Pekrun, R., Reck, C., & Marx, A. K. G. (2024). Joy is reciprocally transmitted between teachers and students: Evidence on facial mimicry in the classroom. Learning and Instruction, 91, 101896. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101896

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational study of 13 university instructors and 69 students videotaped during 45 minutes of real-life instruction, with automated facial emotion coding (Emotient FACET) and cross-recurrence quantification analysis against surrogate data. The article reports "cross-recurred substantially above chance level (p < 0.003)" within the critical lag window.

> "Our first key substantial finding was that students' facial expressions of joy cross-recurred substantially above chance level (p < 0.003) during a time window of about -2s and +3s seconds relative to the instructors' expressions."
