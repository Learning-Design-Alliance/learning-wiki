Evidence¶

McKeown 2018¶

McKeown, M. G., Crosson, A. C., Moore, D. W., & Beck, I. L. (2018). Word Knowledge and Comprehension Effects of an Academic Vocabulary Intervention for Middle School Students. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831217744181

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Mixed analysis of oral recall scores for two expository texts, one containing RAVE words and one untaught AWL words, controlling GMRT pretest. Recalls averaged 8 text units, about 21% of the text, which the authors note was limited compared with prior narrative-text studies.

"The analysis indicated no significant interaction between group and text and no significant main effect for text type or for treatment"
