Spearman rank correlation analysis of IMSD graduates who received undergraduate training from PWIs (Table 5), relating GRE scores, GPAs, and publication outcomes. The article reports coefficients -0.663, 0.696, and 0.762 as significant and notes the findings substantiate first-semester Ph.D. GPA as an indicator for PWI-trained graduates.
