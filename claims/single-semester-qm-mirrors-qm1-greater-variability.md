## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jesse Kruse 2025

Jesse Kruse, Molly Griston, and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2025). Characterization of Upper-Level Undergraduate Quantum Mechanics Courses in the U.S. [doi:10.1103/63s3-7tb8](https://doi.org/10.1103/63s3-7tb8)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of histograms of topic-importance fractions for QM1 (N=14, Fig. 5) and single-semester (N=40, Fig. 6) instructors, counting topics where at least 90% or fewer than 10% consider the topic important. The article reports "showing less agreement among instructors" in single-semester courses.

> "In the sample of QM1 instructors, 42 of the 84 topics were in this range. In the sample of single-semester instructors, 29 of the topics were in this range, showing less agreement among instructors about which topics are important or unimportant."
