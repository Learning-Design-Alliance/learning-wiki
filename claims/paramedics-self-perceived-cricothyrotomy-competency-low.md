## Evidence

### Furin Molly MD 2016

Furin Molly MD, MS, Kohn Melissa MD, MS, Overberger Ryan DO, MS, Jaslow David MD, MPH. (2016). Out-of-Hospital Surgical Airway Management: Does Scope of Practice Equal Actual Practice? Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2016.3.28729

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-report survey of the same 86-paramedic cohort using Likert-scale items on comfort and confidence. Responses showed "Seventy-three percent of subjects indicated that they do not believe that they are trained well enough to perform out-of-hospital surgical airway management (63/86; 95% CI [64–82%])" and only 40% confident in first-attempt success. The study assessed perceived competency, not actual procedural ability.

> "Seventy-three percent of subjects indicated that they do not believe that they are trained well enough to perform out-of-hospital surgical airway management (63/86; 95% CI [64–82%]). Similarly, only 40% (34/86, 95% CI [30–50%]) were confident in their ability to perform cricothyrotomy correctly on the first attempt."
