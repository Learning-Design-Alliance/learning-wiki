Evidence¶

Topacio 2018¶

Topacio, K. N. M. (2018). Exploring the use of online educational platform in teaching writing among ESL students. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(1), 86-101. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1175799

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study with 22 control and 23 experimental first-year Electrical Engineering students. Post-test z = 1.12, within the tabular range of ±1.96 at 0.05 level, indicating "no significant difference was found to exist between the two groups." Both groups improved significantly from pre-test.

"no significant difference was found to exist between the two groups namely the control and experimental groups in their scores in the post -test"
