Kohs 1917¶

Kohs, S. C. (1917). An annotated bibliography of recent literature on the Binet-Simon scale (1913-1917). Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0073125

q2 · i? · review · r2

Annotation of Kuhlmann's symposium contribution (1916, Jour. of Educ. Psychol. 7: 280-282), which also calls part-credit Point Scale scoring "wrong in principle when applied to all tests indiscriminately" and notes test procedure must vary by age-period.

"The present danger to the future of psychology in this field lies much more in the psychologist's hostile attitude and destructive criticism of the Binet-Simon tests, which are admittedly imperfect, than it does in the errors in mental diagnosis resulting from their use, even when employed by relatively untrained examiners."
