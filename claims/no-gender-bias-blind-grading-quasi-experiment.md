## Evidence

### Bischoff Carina Saxlund 2021

Bischoff Carina Saxlund, Ejrnæs Anders, Rubin Olivier. (2021). A quasi-experimental study of ethnic and gender bias in university grading. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254422

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OLS regression on 896 written exams from a Danish quantitative methods course, exploiting the 2018 open to 2019 blind grading shift, with a female*open interaction term (β5 = -0.08, p = 0.88). The article reports "The coefficient is small and highly insignificant, however."

> "The coefficient is small and highly insignificant, however. The analysis does not support the hypothesis that examiners are influenced by knowledge of gender identity when grading exams."
