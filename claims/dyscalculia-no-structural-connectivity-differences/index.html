Evidence¶

Bulthé Jessica 2019¶

Bulthé Jessica, Prinsen Jellina, Vanderauwera Jolijn, Duyck Stefanie, Daniels Nicky, Gillebert Céline R., Mantini Dante, Op de Beeck Hans P., De Smedt Bert. (2019). Multi-method brain imaging reveals impaired representations of number as well as altered connectivity in adults with dyscalculia. Neuroimage, 190, 289-302. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2018.06.012

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Deterministic tractography (ExploreDTI/TrackVis) in 23 controls and 21 participants with dyscalculia; FA, MD, AD and RD were extracted for corpus callosum, IFOF, ILF and arcuate tracts with FDR correction across tracts; e.g. FA t-values ranged from −1.85 to 1.41.

"None of the tracts under investigation showed a significant group difference (even at uncorrected level for multiple comparisons) for fractional anisotropy (FA), mean diffusivity (MD), axial diffusivity (AD), and radial diffusivity (RD) values."
