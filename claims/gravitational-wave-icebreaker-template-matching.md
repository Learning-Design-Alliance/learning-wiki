Michelle B. Larson, Louis J. Rubbo, Kristina D. Zaleski and Shane L. Larson. (2006). Science Icebreaker Activities: An Example from Gravitational Wave Astronomy. Originally published 2005. [doi:10.1119/1.2353577](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.2353577)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The article describes how the icebreaker models the gravitational wave detection process: groups match one of six templates to a noisy data stream, then "compare their solution with other groups", mirroring coincidence detection across observatories. No effect size is reported; this is a design description of the implemented activity.

> "Participants identify a mode l signal within a noisy data stream, and compare their solution with other groups to provide conﬁden ce that the correct signal has been found."
