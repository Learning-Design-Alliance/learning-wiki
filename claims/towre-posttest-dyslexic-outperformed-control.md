## Evidence

### Pfenninger 2015

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttest Mann-Whitney comparisons on the TOWRE show the trained dyslexic group remained significantly below both nondyslexic groups; the article notes dyslexic learners also skipped a high number of words, though skipped words decreased significantly over time in the Test Dyslexic group (z = 2.78, p = .005).

> "Despite gains, however, the Test Dyslexic group did not catch up with the Test Nondyslexic group or even the Control Nondyslexic group (in both cases U = 100, p < .001)"
