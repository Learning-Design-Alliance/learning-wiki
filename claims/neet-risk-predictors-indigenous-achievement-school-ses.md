## Evidence

### Philip Parker 2021

Philip Parker, Kelly-Ann Allen, Rhiannon Parker, Jiesi Guo, Herbert W Marsh, Geetanjali Basarkod, and Theresa Dicke. (2021). School Belonging Predicts whether an Emerging Adult will be Not in Education, Employment, or Training (NEET) after School. https://www.pwc.com.au/government/government-matters/youth-unemployment.html

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel logistic model (Table 3): Indigenous OR = 1.80 [1.55, 2.08], Achievement OR = 0.67, SES OR = 0.83, School Average SES OR = 0.64, School Average Achievement OR = 1.19. School-average achievement's positive coefficient was attributed to multicollinearity with school-average SES (r = 0.718).

> "High-levels of achievement and SES, and being female were all associated with a lower likelihood of being NEET, while Indigenous participants were almost two times more likely to be NEET than non-Indigenous participants."
