Nils J. Mikkelsen, Nicholas T. Young, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2021). Investigating institutional influence on graduate program admissions by modelling physics GRE cut-off scores. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010109)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Significance analysis of 19 logistic regression models (cut-offs 620-800) summarized in Figure 3 and Table II with Bonferroni-corrected p-values. The article reports these three institutional features significant to a majority of cut-offs; no effect size is printed.

> "When it comes to the institutional features, those sta- tistically signiﬁcant to a majority of the P-GRE cut-oﬀs include attending a most competitive institution, an in- stitution practicing some of the highest amounts of re- search activity, and an institution with a large physics bachelor program."
