`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up multilevel models controlling pretest score and gender in the intention-to-treat sample showed the SRL group still scored higher than controls on the three outcomes, with printed d values of .252, .833 and .706.

> "the SRL intervention group reported statistically significantly higher scores at follow‐up compared to the control group on math perseverance (B = .794, p = .041, d = .252), theory of intelligence (B = 1.726, p < .001, d = .833) and math performance (B = 4.046, p < .001, d = .706)"
