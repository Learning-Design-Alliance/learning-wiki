Evidence¶

Naing 2023¶

Naing, C., Whittaker, M. A., Aung, H. H., Chellappan, D. K., & Riegelman, A. (2023). The effects of flipped classrooms to improve learning outcomes in undergraduate health professional education: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews, 19, e1339. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1339

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects inverse-variance meta-analysis of 44 studies (n = 7813) comparing flipped classroom with traditional lecture-based classes on examination scores/grades. The review reports "better academic performance in the flipped class method of learning" with SMD = 0.57 (95% CI 0.25 to 0.90), I² = 98%.

"Based on overall average effect sizes, there was better academic performance in the flipped class method of learning compared to traditional class learning (standardised mean difference [SMD] = 0.57, 95% confidence interval [CI] = 0.25 to 0.90, τ 2: 1.16; I 2: 98%; p < 0.00001, 44 studies, n = 7813)."

Related Claims¶

  • The academic performance advantage of flipped classrooms persists when studies with imputed data are excluded (SMD = 0.54), at low certainty — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Undergraduate health professional students report more positive satisfaction with flipped class learning than with traditional class learning (SMD = 0.48), at low certainty — related
  • Only a minority of flipped-classroom studies in health professional education are RCTs, and risk of bias in non-randomised studies is high — related
  • The AI-enhanced flipped classroom raises post-class assignment accuracy over the traditional classroom, an advantage the standard flipped classroom did not show — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The AI-enhanced flipped classroom improves comprehensive assessment and pre-class preparation/PPT quality over the flipped classroom, but not in-class presentation performance or professional knowledge mastery — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Both flipped and AI-enhanced flipped classrooms achieve higher in-class question accuracy than the traditional classroom — related
  • Time-on-task is frequently unequal across compared conditions, with active learning conditions typically requiring more home time — related
  • Blended Learning Improves Outcomes — related
  • A blended/hybrid flipped calculus course with an FC Orientation module improved student learning performance and perceptions significantly over the traditional lecture format — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Flipped classroom shows significant positive effects across class sizes, with the largest reported effect for medium-sized classes — related
  • Flipped classroom has a moderately positive effect on college students' academic performance (SMD = 0.66) — related
  • The social nature of learning activities shows no detectable moderating effect on flipped classroom learning gains — related
  • Flipped classroom significantly improves theoretical exam scores over lecture-based learning in Chinese undergraduate preventive medicine courses (pooled SMD 0.99) — related
  • Flipped classrooms in higher education show a medium overall positive effect on student outcomes compared with traditional lecture-based classrooms — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Flipped classrooms are associated with higher theoretical knowledge scores than lecture-based teaching in medical education (pooled SMD = 1.26), with very high heterogeneity — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
