## Evidence

### Auerbach Anna Jo and Schussler Elisabeth 2017

Auerbach Anna Jo and Schussler Elisabeth. (2017). A Vision and Change Reform of Introductory Biology Shifts Faculty Perceptions and Use of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0258

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

A posteriori category from qualitative interview analysis: assessment mentions grew "from one in year 1" to three in year 2 and six in year 3 (Table 4). Assessment had no subthemes; instructors reflected on what to test, how to test, and assessment as instruction.

> "During the first year of reform, only one instructor reflected on assessment when thinking about his or her teaching. During the second year, three instructors were thinking about assessment in their classrooms. And by the third year, six instructors were considering assessment when describing their AL practices."
