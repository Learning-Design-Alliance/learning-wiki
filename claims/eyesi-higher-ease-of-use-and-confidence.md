## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Liu Canying 2024

Liu Canying, Lin Jicheng, Wu Siting, Zhu Yingting, Zou Yuxian, Zhang Qi, Li Zhidong, Zhuo Yehong, Li Yiqing. (2024). Eyesi direct ophthalmoscope simulator: an effective training tool for medical undergraduates. BMC Medical Education 24:783. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05780-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kirkpatrick level 1 evaluation in the randomized trial: post-training questionnaire comparisons between Group A (after Eyesi training) and Group B (after TDO training) on ease of use and two confidence items, all P < 0.001; the article prints no effect size.

> "Compared with Group B (4.71 ± 0.33), the students in Group A achieved higher scores (6.71 ± 0.21) in "the ease of use of training tool." Furthermore, scores of "self-confidence to examine for healthy volunteers or patients with retinal diseases in future clinical practice" in Group A (7.47 ± 0.21; 6.93 ± 0.22) were higher relative to Group B (5.94 ± 0.35; 5.17 ± 0.34), P < 0.001 (Table 3)."
