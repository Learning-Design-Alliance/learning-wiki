## Evidence

### Yue Yang 2025

Yue Yang, Mengyao Guo, Yuxuan Wu, Wally Niu, Emmanuel Corona, Bruce Daniel, Christoph Leuze, Fred Baik. (2025). VR MRI Training for Adolescents: A Comparative Study of Gamified VR, Passive VR, 360◦ Video, and Traditional Educational Video. [doi:10.1109/ismar-adjunct68609.2025.00081](https://doi.org/10.1109/ismar-adjunct68609.2025.00081)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects study of 11 adolescents (ages 10–16) comparing four preparation modalities; head motion recorded via Quest 3 sensors. The Friedman test showed a significant condition effect, and "the Game-VR condition yielded lower motion than Passive-VR (p < 0.01) and 360 ◦ Video (p < 0.01)".

> "A Friedman test found a significant effect of condition on head motion ( X2 = 26.0, p < 0.001). Post-hoc comparisons confirmed that the Game-VR condition yielded lower motion than Passive-VR (p < 0.01) and 360 ◦ Video (p < 0.01)."
