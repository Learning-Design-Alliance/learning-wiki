LLMs' generation probabilities for multiple-choice distractors correlate moderately with how often students choose them

Across 3,202 four-option MCQs with real student response distributions, LLaMA and Qwen models (0.5B–72B) assigned distractor probabilities that correlated with student selection at Pearson r = 0.28–0.37, and when wrong chose students' most common wrong answer about 51–59% of the time.
