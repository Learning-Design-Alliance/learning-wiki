## Evidence

### Grasby KL 2026

Grasby KL, Larsen SA, Gregory AM, Blunden S, Lewis KS, Madrid-Valero JJ, Coventry WL, Byrne B, Olson RK. (2026). Associations between sleep habits, performance in reading and mathematics, and inattention and hyperactivity. PLoS One 21(5): e0347892. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0347892

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlations showed better sleep quality associated with less inattention and hyperactivity in each grade, and less daytime sleepiness and snoring associated with less inattention in Grades 7 and 9, with correlations "varying from .10 to .17."

> "Where significant, correlations between the sleep variables and attention and hyperactivity were slightly stronger than those for the reading and maths results but were still small in size (varying from .10 to .17)."
