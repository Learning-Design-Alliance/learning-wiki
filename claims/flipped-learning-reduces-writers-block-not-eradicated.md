## Evidence

### Salem 2018

Salem, A. A. M. S. (2018). Engaging ESP University Students in Flipped Classrooms for Developing Functional Writing Skills, HOTs, and Eliminating Writer's Block. English Language Teaching, 11(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n12p177

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

T-test comparison on Rose's QIWB (24 items, five subscales) in the quasi-experiment. Table 11 reports total means of 42.23 (experimental) vs 59.76 (control), t = 12.82, p = .00; the article states "the levels of writers' block have been alleviated but it was not eradicated".
