Country-wise forest plots (Figures 1 and 2) from two-level models estimated independently per country; the article reports some non-significant estimates (e.g., thirteen countries in science) but "largely consistently positive across countries".

"Thus, these forest plots show that the effect of student achievement on enjoyment was largely consistently positive across countries, for all three domains (even though the results from the three-level models hinted at variation in the size of effects)."
