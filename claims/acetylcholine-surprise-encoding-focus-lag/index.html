q2 · i? · review · r2

Review narrative citing Decker and Duncan (2020) on acetylcholine as a gate for learning and pattern differentiation; no effect size printed. The review also cites pharmacological blockade causing memory deficits in young people and cholinergic stimulation improving memory in older people.

"When outcomes or information are surprising, acetylcholine release is high, causing the hippocam- pus to encode new memories based on how they are distinct from prior memories (Decker & Duncan, 2020)."

Lauren Margulieux 2025 (2)¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review narrative on the sequencing of (nor)epinephrine-driven alertness before acetylcholine-driven focus, citing Rajkowski et al. (1994) and Usher et al. (1999) on alertness making undifferentiated awareness and movement more likely. No effect size printed.

"Because learners have to generate alertness before they can focus, there can be a 5- to 10-min period at the start of a task in which focusing attention is difficult."
