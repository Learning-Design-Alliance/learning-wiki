### Alaimo Giuseppe 2026

Alaimo Giuseppe, Mancin Stefano, Ferrante Sergio, Morenghi Emanuela, Palomares Sara Morales, Cattani Daniela, Cosmai Simone, Lopane Diego, Petrelli Fabio, Cangelosi Giovanni, Mazzoleni Beatrice. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross‐sectional survey of 91 oncology nurses (59.49% response rate) at a single Northern Italian tertiary hospital using the validated Italian nutrition knowledge questionnaire. The mean score was "20.0 ± 1.9, with a range of 16.3–24.6"; on this instrument the lowest possible score (15) indicates the best performance.

> "Ninety‐one out of the 159 eligible nurses completed the questionnaire, resulting in a response rate of 59.49%. The mean score achieved was 20.0 ± 1.9, with a range of 16.3–24.6."
