# Maternal ADHD polygenic risk scores are associated with lower maternal educational achievement and lower maternal IQ

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mothers with higher ADHD polygenic risk scores reported lower educational achievement on a 5-point qualification scale (β = −0.09, 95% CI −0.10 to −0.06, P = 0.005). [→ Stergiakouli Evie 2017](#stergiakouli-evie-2017)
`q2 i?` Mothers with higher ADHD polygenic risk scores had lower total IQ (β = −0.6, 95% CI −1.2 to −0.1, P = 0.03). [→ Stergiakouli Evie 2017](#stergiakouli-evie-2017)

Regression analysis in ALSPAC mothers (Table 2: 7280 mothers for highest qualification; 2313 for maternal total IQ; mean age 45 years 4 months at IQ testing). The quote reports "β = −0.09 points in the education scale (−0.10 to −0.06), P = 0.005" and "β = −0.6 (−1.2 to −0.1), P = 0.03".

> "maternal high genetic loading as indicated by ADHD polygenic risk scores was associated with lower maternal educational achievement [β = −0.09 points in the education scale (−0.10 to −0.06), P = 0.005], as measured by the mothers’ self-reported highest qualification obtained and lower maternal IQ [β = −0.6 (−1.2 to −0.1), P = 0.03; and Table 2]."
