# Split-attention worked examples lose their advantage over conventional practice, while integrated formats restore it

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Geometry worked examples requiring learners to mentally integrate separate diagram and text sources showed no advantage over conventional problem solving, and conventional practice was faster on posttest time-to-solution. [→ Atkinson 2000](#atkinson-2000)
`q2 i?` Restructuring worked examples by integrating verbal explanations into the diagram enhanced learning relative to both conventional problem solving and split-source worked examples. [→ Atkinson 2000 (2)](#atkinson-2000-2)

## Evidence

### Atkinson 2000

Atkinson, R. K., Derry, S. J., Renkl, A., & Wortham, D. (2000). Learning from Examples: Instructional Principles from the Worked Examples Research. Review of Educational Research, 70(2), 181-214. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543070002181

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Tarmizi and Sweller, an experiment assigning participants to conventional practice or worked-example conditions using circle-geometry materials requiring integration of problem statement, theorems, and diagram. The review reports 'no significant differences in favor of worked examples on any of the measures'.

> "they found in this case no significant differences in favor of worked examples on any of the measures. Instead, they found a difference in favor of conventional problem solving on time-to-solution on the posttest."

### Atkinson 2000 (2)

Atkinson, R. K., Derry, S. J., Renkl, A., & Wortham, D. (2000). Learning from Examples: Instructional Principles from the Worked Examples Research. Review of Educational Research, 70(2), 181-214. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543070002181

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Tarmizi and Sweller, that Experiments 4 and 5 with an essentially similar design showed that 'integrating verbal explanations into a diagram enhanced learning' over both conventional practice and split-source examples.

> "the authors found that simply restructuring the worked example by integrating verbal explanations into a diagram enhanced learning in comparison to conventional problem solving and split-source worked examples."
