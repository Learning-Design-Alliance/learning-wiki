### Wang Tongyao 2026

Evaluation of a train-the-trainer implementation strategy on providers' competency, opportunity and motivation for advance care planning in residential care homes: a mixed-methods study. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-025-06898-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-and-post workshop survey within an explanatory sequential mixed-methods study of the train-the-trainer ACP program; paired analyses included participants completing both assessments. The article reports "significant increases in their ACP competency scores from 80.44 ± 15.12 to 96.12 ± 10.79 (t = 5.953, p < 0.001)". No effect size was printed.

> "Upon completion of the ACP workshop, participants had significant increases in their ACP competency scores from 80.44 ± 15.12 to 96.12 ± 10.79 (t = 5.953, p < 0.001), but no change in their readiness for engaging in ACP (Z = − 0.988, p = 0.329)."
