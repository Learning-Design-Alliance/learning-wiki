## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Nakayama N 2021

Nakayama N, Ejiri H, Arakawa N, Makino T. (2021). Stress and anxiety in nursing students between individual and peer simulations. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.680

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational heart rate variability comparison during the first patient assessment phase of the same simulation study (individual n = 50; peer n = 59). The LF/HF ratio, used as an indicator of sympathetic activity, was "significantly increased under peer simulation relative to individual simulation (p = .020)"; no effect size was printed.

> "In addition, the LF/HF component was significantly increased under peer simulation relative to individual simulation (p = .020) (Figure 2)."
