### Bloom and Weiland 2014

Howard S. Bloom & Christina Weiland. (2014). To What Extent Do Head Start's Effects on Children's Language, Literacy, Mathematics, and Socio-Emotional Skills Vary Across Individuals, Subgroups, and Centers? SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562943

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

F tests compared residual outcome variances for treatment versus control children in the Head Start Impact Study first follow-up year. Table 2 prints treatment/control variance ratios of 0.811*** for receptive vocabulary and 0.825*** for early numeracy, and 1.01 for early reading.

> "We also found that the individual residual variance was statistically significant for treatment versus control group children for three outcomes – receptive vocabulary, early numeracy, and oral comprehension, with differences especially pronounced for receptive vocabulary and early numeracy (see Table 2)."
