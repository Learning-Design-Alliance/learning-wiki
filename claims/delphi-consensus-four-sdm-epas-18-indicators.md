## Evidence

### Baghus A 2020

Baghus A, Giroldi E, Muris J, Stiggelbout A, van de Pol M, Timmerman A, van der Weijden T. (2020). Identifying Entrustable Professional Activities for Shared Decision Making in Postgraduate Medical Education: A National Delphi Study. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000003618

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

A 3-round modified online Delphi study among a purposively sampled multidisciplinary Dutch expert panel (N = 32 completing all rounds) rated EPAs and behavioral indicators on 7-point Likert statements; consensus was defined as a median score of >= 6 with >= 75% scoring >= 6, and the article reports "30 (94%) agreed" on the final list.

> "After 3 Delphi rounds, consensus was reached on 4 SDM EPAs and 18 behavioral indicators for postgraduate medical education (see Table 2). Of the 32 experts, 30 (94%) agreed on this list of SDM EPAs and behavioral indicators."
