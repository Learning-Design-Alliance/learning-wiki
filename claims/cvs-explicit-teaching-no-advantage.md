de Jong, T., Lazonder, A. W., Chinn, C. A., Fischer, F., Gobert, J., Hmelo-Silver, C. H., Koedinger, K. R., Krajcik, J. S., Kyza, E. A., Linn, M. C., Pedaste, M., Scheiter, K., & Zacharia, Z. C. `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review quotes the Schwichow et al. (2016) meta-analysis of CVS instruction, which found explicit rule teaching "no different from studies in which CVS rules were not explicitly taught," countering claims that direct instruction is superior for CVS learning.

> "we found studies that explicitly taught a CVS rule to have effect sizes (g = 0.58, 95% CI = 0.46–0.70) no different from studies in which CVS rules were not explicitly taught (g = 0.65, 95% CI = 0.51–0.79)."
