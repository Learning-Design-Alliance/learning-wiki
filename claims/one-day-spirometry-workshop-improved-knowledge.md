## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ayuk Adaeze 2020

Ayuk Adaeze, Ndukwu Chizalu, Uwaezuoke Samuel, Ekop Eno. (2020). Spirometry practice and the impact of a phase 1 training workshop among health workers in southern Nigeria: a cross-sectional study. BMC Pulmonary Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12890-020-01291-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Knowledge test analysis among the 50 participants with complete paired scores in this descriptive cross-sectional study of a one-day workshop; the article reports the pre/post difference as significant (p < 0.001) with means "52.95 (24.14) %" and "64.61 (16.84) %". No effect size is printed.

> "The mean pre-lecture percentage test (SD) score was 52.95 (24.14) %, with scores ranging from 11.86 to 91.0%, while the mean post-test score was 64.61 (16.84) %"
