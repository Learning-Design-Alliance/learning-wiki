Evidence¶

Yildirim 2022¶

Yildirim, E., Tikman, F., & Senturk, M. (2022). Meta-analysis on the effectiveness of using the drama approach in social studies course. International Journal on Social and Education Sciences (IJonSES), 4(1), 17-33. https://doi.org/10.46328/ijonses.301

q3 · i?

Moderator analysis comparing 13 studies of 1–4 weeks (d = 0.906, moderate) with 25 studies of 5–10 weeks (d = 1.222, strong). The between-group test found "no statistically significant difference between the groups", so achievement did not change by duration.

"Since the homogeneity value of QB=2.429, calculated in this study, was smaller than the accepted critical value of 3.841 (QB=2.429.05), it could be assumed that there was no statistically significant difference between the groups."
