### Lee 2024

Lee, J., Hicke, Y., Yu, R., Brooks, C., & Kizilcec, R. F. (2024). The Life Cycle of Large Language Models: A Review of Biases in Education. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.11203](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.11203)

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's own conceptual argument, stated in its abstract and discussion, that established bias metrics do not adequately address educational LLM outputs. The authors give three reasons: text is "high-dimensional", "there can be multiple correct responses", and tailoring "may be pedagogically desirable rather than unfair."

> "We discuss why current measures of bias from traditional machine learning fail to transfer to LLM-generated content in education, such as tutoring conversations because the text is high-dimensional, there can be multiple correct responses, and tailoring responses may be pedagogically desirable rather than unfair."
