## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The experimental group increased its external regulation after the gamified intervention (ES = 0.34), while no other motivational variable changed significantly in either group. ## Evidence

### Ferriz-Valero Alberto; Østerlie Ove; García Martínez Salvador; García-Jaén Miguel 2020

Ferriz-Valero Alberto; Østerlie Ove; García Martínez Salvador; García-Jaén Miguel. (2020). Gamification in Physical Education: Evaluation of Impact on Motivation and Academic Performance within Higher Education. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17124465

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 127 university PETE students (GAM n = 62, CON n = 65) using the CMEF questionnaire pre and post. Wilcoxon tests showed only GAM external regulation rose (Z = -2.667; p = 0.008; ES = 0.34). The article calls this "the experimental group increased its ER".

> "no significant differences were observed, as shown in Table 5, regarding pre-test and post-test comparisons (p < 0.05) in either of the two educational groups, GAM vs. CON, except for the variable of external regulation (ER) in the GAM group, whose post-test value was higher"
