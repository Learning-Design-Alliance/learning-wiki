`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Media-use analysis of the 98 archive projects. The authors report only "a minority of the projects (2%) use haptic elements and these all have edu- cation as their primary goal", and that "two thirds of the projects are inter- active".

> "Another finding from our surveys is that most of the projects use a multi-sensory approach (64%) rather than sound only (Figure 3; Source Data Table 2). Most common is to combine soniﬁcation with visuals, with 62% of projects combin- ing sound with graphical visualisations, videos or a graphical user interface (GUI)."
