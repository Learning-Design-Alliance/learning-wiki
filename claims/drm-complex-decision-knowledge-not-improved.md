# Knowledge scores rose from pre-test to post-test after the videos for principles, decontamination and triage, but did not show a statistically significant change for treatment (p = 0.029) or transport (p = 0.180), in one uncontrolled pre-post study

`q2 i?` Knowledge scores for DRM principles and medical management rose from pre-test to post-test in one uncontrolled pre-post study (p < 0.001), including triage and decontamination. [→ Saiboon Ismail M. 2021](#saiboon-ismail-m-2021)

## Evidence

### Saiboon Ismail M. 2021

Saiboon Ismail M., Zahari Fareena, Isa Hisham M., Sabardin Dazlin M., Robertson Colin E. (2021). E-Learning in Teaching Emergency Disaster Response Among Undergraduate Medical Students in Malaysia. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.628178

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Sub-analysis of the same 168-participant pre/post study (Table 3). Principles score rose from 2.91 ± 1.43 to 6.24 ± 2.53 and medical management from 4.08 ± 1.97 to 7.06 ± 3.05; "significant improvement in knowledge on the principles and medical management of DRM (p < 0.001)".

> "On sub-analysis there was significant improvement in knowledge on the principles and medical management of DRM (p < 0.001)."

### Saiboon Ismail M. 2021 (2)

Saiboon Ismail M., Zahari Fareena, Isa Hisham M., Sabardin Dazlin M., Robertson Colin E. (2021). E-Learning in Teaching Emergency Disaster Response Among Undergraduate Medical Students in Malaysia. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.628178

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same pre/post study, Table 3 sub-components. The article reports triage and decontamination improved significantly while "treatment and transport with p = 0.029 and p = 0.180, respectively" did not reach the p < 0.01 threshold. No effect sizes printed.

> "The medical management components like triage and decontamination also significantly improved between pre- and post-test (all p < 0.001) but not in the areas of treatment and transport with p = 0.029 and p = 0.180, respectively."
