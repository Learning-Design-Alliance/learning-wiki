## Evidence

### Quan Zhou 2026

Quan Zhou, Liao Wangjingyi, Allegrini Andrea G., Rimfeld Kaili, Wertz Jasmin, Morris Tim T., Raffington Laurel, Plomin Robert, Malanchini Margherita. (2026). Non-cognitive skills mediate education-related polygenic score associations with academic achievement across development. Nature Communications. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72838-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Time-lagged mediation analyses in TEDS tested whether non-cognitive skills at earlier ages mediated polygenic score associations with later academic achievement. Parent-rated education-specific non-cognitive skills at age 9 mediated associations with achievement at age 12 (β = 0.08) and age 16 (β = 0.08), with "effect sizes comparable to those observed cross-sectionally".

> "parent-rated education-specific non-cognitive skills measured at age 9 mediated the educational atainment polygenic score associations with academic achievement measured at both age 12 (β = 0.08 [95% CI, 0.03, 0.12], p = 0.004) and age 16 (β = 0.08 [95% CI, 0.05, 0.11], p < 0.001)."
