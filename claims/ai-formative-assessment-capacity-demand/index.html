Evidence¶

anchor-formative¶

Griffiths, R., Peters Hinton, V., Daal, S., Pattenhouse, M. (2026, July). K‑12 AI Infrastructure: Findings from educator and developer outreach. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/309

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

The report's formative assessment section, drawing on educator interviews. One teacher said "Feedback is the most time consuming thing for teachers, so anything that can help lessen that load would be great," and an administrator wanted AI to assess each student fairly, consistently, and quickly.

"A large body of research shows that high-quality formative assessment is one of the most powerful levers for improving student performance (Black & Wiliam, 1998; Klute et al., 2017), but providing immediate, detailed feedback consistently for all students is a significant operational challenge."
