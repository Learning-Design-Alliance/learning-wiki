## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Neither physical activity nor fast-food consumption was significantly associated with English or Math grades directly or via prefrontal function mediators. [→ Papasideris M. 2021](#papasideris-m-2021)

## Evidence

### Papasideris M. 2021

Papasideris M., Ayaz H., Safati A. B., Morita P. P., Hall P. A. (2021). Examining the relationships among adolescent health behaviours, prefrontal function, and academic achievement using fNIRS. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience, 50, 100983. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.100983

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null findings from the multiple mediation models (Sections 3.2.1-3.2.2) in the adolescent study; the indirect effect of active minutes on Math grades through R-dlPFC OxyHb had a 95% confidence interval including zero. Equivalence was not tested.

> "There were, however, no significant links between physical activity or fast-food consumption and academic achievement, either directly or mediated by brain health parameters."
