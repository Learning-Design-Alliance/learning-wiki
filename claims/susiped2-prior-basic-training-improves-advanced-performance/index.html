q2 · i? · review · r3

Study of an advanced pediatric MIS curriculum (SuSiPed 2.0) consisting of six training modules, with all participants repeating the curriculum three times and evaluating closeness to reality and relevance on Likert scales. The review reports "prior basic training significantly increases the performance in a more advanced pediatric MIS simulator".

"This study shows that prior basic training significantly increases the performance in a more advanced pediatric MIS simulator. The authors suggested that both basic and advanced SuSiPed modules can be combined into a comprehensive pediatric MIS training program."
