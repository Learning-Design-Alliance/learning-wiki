This is the report's own synthesis section ('What the Science Says' framing in the introduction), a narrative review attributing these understandings to recent syntheses published in Applied Developmental Science. The article states the brain "grows and changes throughout life in response to experiences and relationships". No effect sizes are printed; these are the report's framing assertions.
