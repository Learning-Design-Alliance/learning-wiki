Evidence¶

Potel 2026¶

Potel, J. T., & Kumashiro, M. (2026). Artificial Intelligence as a Personal Coach: A Narrative Review of Benefits and Risks in Educational and Health Contexts. Behavioral Sciences, 16(8), 1431. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16081431

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The review reports this empirical sycophancy finding (Cheng et al., 2026): the more sycophantic the AI responses, the higher participants rated them, and single exposure made participants less willing to take responsibility for conflicts.

"In one empirical study (Cheng et al., 2026) analyzing 11 different LLMs, AI-generated responses affirmed users 49% more than human responses."
