# TBL enhances students' communication skills more than LBL according to student questionnaires

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A larger proportion of TBL students reported the pedagogy greatly enhanced their communication skills (93.18% TBL vs 76.15% LBL; RR = 1.22, 95% CI [1.10, 1.36]). [→ Lang Bingchen 2019](#lang-bingchen-2019)

## Evidence

### Lang Bingchen 2019

Lang Bingchen, Zhang Lingli, Lin Yunzhu, Han Lu, Zhang Chuan, Liu Yantao. (2019). Team-based learning pedagogy enhances the quality of Chinese pharmacy education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1724-6

`q3 · i?`

Meta-analysis of two studies involving 262 students (132 receiving TBL) pooling questionnaire responses on communication skills; I2 of 0% indicated no substantial heterogeneity, so a fixed-effect model was chosen. The article prints no standardized effect size, only "RR = 1.22 with 95% CI [1.10,1.36]".

> "The results from the questionnaires demonstrated that TBL method enhanced students' communication skills obviously (the ratio of students thinking the pedagogy method had great influence on enhancing communication skills: 93.18% in TBL group vs 76.15% in LBL group, RR = 1.22 with 95% CI [1.10,1.36], p < 0.0001)"
