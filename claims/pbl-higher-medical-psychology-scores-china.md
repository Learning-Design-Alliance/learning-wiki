# PBL is associated with significantly higher total medical psychology examination scores than traditional lecture-based teaching in China (Hedges' g = 1.510)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across nine included studies, students taught with PBL scored significantly higher on total medical psychology examinations than students taught with traditional lecture-based teaching (Hedges' g = 1.510, 95%CI 0.792–2.227, p<0.001, random effect model). [→ Gao Junwei 2020](#gao-junwei-2020)

## Evidence

### Gao Junwei 2020

Gao Junwei, Yang Ling, Zhao Jinghui, Wang Lian, Zou Jiao, Wang Chunxiang, Fan Xiaotang. (2020). Comparison of problem-based learning and traditional teaching methods in medical psychology education in China: A systematic review and meta-analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0243897

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review and meta-analysis of nine studies (551 cases, 496 controls) comparing PBL with traditional lecture-based teaching in Chinese medical psychology courses, pooling Hedges' g under a random effect model. The pooled estimate was "Hedges’ g = 1.510" with a significant difference favoring PBL.

> "the combined results of the overall comparison revealed a significant difference in the total examination scores of students in favor of the PBL approach, compared with traditional teaching under the random effect model (Hedges’ g = 1.510, 95%CI 0.792–2.227, p<0.001)"
