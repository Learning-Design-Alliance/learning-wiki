## Subclaims
`q1 i?` During an oral exam, the instructor tacitly noticed that the student was in the Algorithmic Physics frame, inferred from the instructor's own stimulated recall account. [→ Hass 2020](#hass-2020)

## Evidence

### Hass 2020

Hass, C. A. F., Ryan, Q. X., & Sayre, E. C. (2020). Teacher Noticing and Shifting of Student Epistemic Framing. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2002.12879](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2002.12879)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case study of one 4-minute episode within a 37-minute oral exam in upper-division E&M, analyzed with video and a stimulated recall interview. The authors write that "the instructor told us that Abbey was just following a set of steps", which they interpret as tacit noticing of the Algorithmic Physics frame.

> "In the stimulated recall interview, the instructor told us that Abbey was just following a set of steps. The instructor told us they wanted Abbey to think about how the electric field direction should influence the orientation of the cylinder."
