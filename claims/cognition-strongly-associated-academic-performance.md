## Evidence

### McPherson A 2018

McPherson A, Mackay L, Kunkel J, Duncan S. (2018). Physical activity, cognition and academic performance: an analysis of mediating and confounding relationships in primary school children. BMC Public Health, 18:936. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-018-5863-1

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis (Model 2) in the 601-child sample, with cognition as a latent variable of four CNSVS indicators and academic performance as a latent variable of e-asTTle reading and maths scores. The printed effect size is r = 0.750, described by the article as strong.

> "While cognition was strongly associated with academic performance (r = 0.750), physical activity was also associated with cognition (r = 0.138)."
