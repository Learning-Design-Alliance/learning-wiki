`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Downstream pilot of the full workshop (didactic, simulation with competency rubric, and questionnaire) with 6 obstetrics and gynecology residents and 17 family and community medicine residents. The article reports "All participants completed the simulation without experiencing any unanticipated technical problems."

> "All participants completed the simulation without experiencing any unanticipated technical problems. Faculty were comfortable using the supplied evaluation rubric and had no difficulty providing individualized learner feedback after the simulation. Resident feedback on the simulation was generally favorable; most indicated the simulation provided a clear, safe, and constructive learning experience."
