Evidence¶

Esin Özdemir 2016¶

Esin Özdemir, Ali Arslan. (2016). The Effect of Self-regulated Jigsaw IV on University Students' Academic Achievements and Attitudes towards English Course. Journal of Education and Training Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i5.1453

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative descriptive analysis of semi-structured interviews with 6 randomly selected experimental-group students, coded using Zimmerman's self-regulated learning phases. Student statements illustrated goal setting, metacognitive monitoring, help seeking and self-satisfaction.

"after the use of these "forethought, performance and self-reflection" phases, it becomes clear that they can use self-regulated learning in their independent studies, evaluate their own learning and feel satisfied with their learning."
