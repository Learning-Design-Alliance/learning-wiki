Sukitkanaporn 2014¶

Sukitkanaporn, T., & Phoocharoensil, S. (2014). English Presentation Skills of Thai Graduate Students. English Language Teaching, 7(3). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n3p91

Cross-sectional survey of 26 conference attendees rating presenters on a five-point Likert scale. The article reports significant differences favoring CR 752 students on visual clarity, understandability, interest, text placement, and color use, with p-values as printed.

"Their visual aids used in the presentation were clear ( p = 0.0498), easy to understand ( p = 0.0051), and was interesting ( p = 0.0338). The presenters used visual ai ds that showed the relevant text on the same slide (p = 0.0118), and used color to emphasize a point (p = 0.0485)."
