### SaferSanerSchools™ Whole-School Change program 2018

SaferSanerSchools™ Whole-School Change program. (2018). Evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/safersanerschools-whole-school-change/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized study by Augustine et al. (2018) in which 44 Pittsburgh schools were assigned to the program or control, with implementation over 2 years. Average daily attendance showed a significant student-level effect: "effect size = +0.07". School-level results were not significant.

> "Results were not significant at the school level but were significant for both average daily attendance (effect size = +0.07) and chronic absence (effect size = +0.05) at the student level"
