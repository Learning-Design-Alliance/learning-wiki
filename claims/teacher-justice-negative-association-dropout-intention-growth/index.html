Evidence¶

Grazia 2024¶

Grazia, V., Molinari, L., Mameli, C. (2024). Contrasting school dropout: The protective role of perceived teacher justice. LEARNING AND INSTRUCTION, 89, 1-9. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101826

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal questionnaire study of 547 9th-grade students surveyed at three time points (T1, T2, T3) across the school year. The latent growth model showed "intentions to dropout increased over time" and teacher justice was negatively associated with the increase at T2 and T3.

"A latent growth model indicated that intentions to dropout increased over time and perceptions of teacher justice had a negative association with the increase both at T2 and T3, even after controlling for gender, nationality, motivation, and perceived learning difficulty."
