Evidence¶

Maadi Mahdi Alajmi & Hanan Abdullah Al-Hadiah 2017¶

Maadi Mahdi Alajmi & Hanan Abdullah Al-Hadiah. (2017). Effectiveness of using the iPad in Learning to Acquire the Mental and Performance Skills in Teaching Social Studies Curriculum. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v6n1p1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-test T-test on the 48-student sample: experimental mean 8.05 (SD 0.152) versus control 8.33 (SD 0.155), T-test value 0.624, significance 0.535. Performance skills are described as map determination, reading and drawing, and drawing charts and graphs.

"there are no statistically significant differences between the averages of the two experimental and the control groups in the performance skills in the pre-test, which means the equality of the two groups in the level of performance skills before applying the using of iPad Device in the Education."
