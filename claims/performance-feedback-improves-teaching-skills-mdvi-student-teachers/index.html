Evidence¶

Safak 2016¶

Safak, P., Yilmaz, H. C., Demiryurek, P., & Dogus, M. (2016). The effect of performance feedback provided to student-teachers working with multiple disabilities. European Journal of Educational Research, 5(3), 109-123. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.5.3.109

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Single-group quasi-experiment with 11 final-year student teachers; Wilcoxon signed-rank test on TSOF scores from video-recorded pretest and posttest lessons. The article reports "the increase was large (r = -.53)" with significant rank difference favoring posttest.

"The results of the analysis indicated that the median post - test ranks (median=82) were statistically significantly higher than the median pre-test ranks (median= 68.4), Z= -2.49, p<. 05 and the increase was large (r = -.53)."
