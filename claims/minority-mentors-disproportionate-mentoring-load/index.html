Kucirkova 2023¶

Kucirkova, N. (2023). Commentary on Highly Successful Female Educational Psychologists: Equity and Intersectionality in Success Definitions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09727-3

The commentary argues, from its intersectionality analysis and interviewee accounts, that "being the only mentor from a disadvantaged group means being frequently asked to perform disproportionally more mentoring than scholars from privileged backgrounds". It cites Dr Raychelle Burks on the burden of representing all Black people.
