## Evidence

### Hans van der Meij 2018

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij, Tessa Voerman, Evert Duipmans. (2018). Supporting motivation, task performance and retention in video tutorials for software training. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 597–614. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9560-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA on initial and final self-efficacy for the control, review, and 2-demo conditions found no condition effect; the expected strongest gain for the review condition was not observed. No effect size was printed for the condition contrast.

> "There was no main effect of condition and no interaction between time and condition, F \ 1."

### Hans van der Meij 2018 (2)

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij, Tessa Voerman, Evert Duipmans. (2018). Supporting motivation, task performance and retention in video tutorials for software training. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 597–614. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9560-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA over the four regular task tests found no condition effect on learning; an ANCOVA on the transfer test with pre-test as covariate likewise "revealed no difference between conditions". No effect size was printed for these contrasts.

> "Also, there was no main effect of condition and no interaction between time and condition, F \ 1."
