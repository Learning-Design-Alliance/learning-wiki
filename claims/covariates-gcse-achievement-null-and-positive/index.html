Linear and logistic regression covariate estimates in the imputed ALSPAC sample. The quote reports the home learning environment and intelligence-confidence coefficients; the article reports no strong evidence for teacher opinion, temperament, work confidence or friendship quality.

"Both linear and logistic models provided strong evidence for associations between the home learning environment at age 3 and achievement (β: 3.34; 95% CI: 2.06, 4.61; OR: 1.07; 95% CI: 1.03, 1.12), and between a child ’s con ﬁdence in their intelligence and achievement at age 8 ( β: 3.62; 95% CI: 2.65, 4.6; OR: 1.09; 95% CI: 1.05, 1.13)."
