### Yachina 2016 (2)

Yachina, N. P., Valeeva, L. A., & Sirazeeva, A. F. (2016). E-Teaching Materials as the Means to Improve Humanities Teaching Proficiency in the Context of Education Informatization. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(4), 433-442. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1094652

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

> "73% of students confirmed that experience with e -teaching materials, obtained in higher educational institution, was used in teaching practice, and 17% are focused on participation of e -teaching materials development for disciplines of the humanities."
