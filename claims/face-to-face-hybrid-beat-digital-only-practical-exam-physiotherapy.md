# Conventional and hybrid instruction produced higher practical exam scores than digital-only Ren'Py instruction in physiotherapy students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Practical exam scores differed significantly between the three instructional groups, with both the conventional (CEG) and hybrid (CaDEG) groups scoring significantly higher than the digital-only (DEG) group. [→ Koçyiğit A 2025](#kocyigit-a-2025)

## Evidence

### Koçyiğit A 2025

Koçyiğit A, Cavlak U. (2025). Digitalization through the use of Ren’Py-Based interactive learning experiences in physiotherapy and rehabilitation education: a randomised, controlled, single-blind study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08474-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three-arm randomized controlled trial of 80 second-year physiotherapy students; ANOVA on practical exam scores after a one-week retention interval showed "significant differences were observed in the practical exam scores between the groups" (F = 7.823, p < 0.001).

> "However, significant differences were observed in the practical exam scores between the groups (df = 2; MS = 1045.020; F = 7.823; CI(95%) = 0.03 to 0.30; p < 0.001)."

### Koçyiğit A 2025 (2)

Koçyiğit A, Cavlak U. (2025). Digitalization through the use of Ren’Py-Based interactive learning experiences in physiotherapy and rehabilitation education: a randomised, controlled, single-blind study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08474-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni post-hoc pairwise comparisons in the same trial: CEG beat DEG (p = 0.007) and CaDEG beat DEG (p = 0.002), while "The practical exam scores of the CaDEG and CEG groups were similar (CI (95%)=−7.06 to 8.86, p = 1.000)."

> "Both the CEG and CaDEG groups had significantly higher practical exam scores compared to the DEG group (CI (95%) = 2.22 to 18.01, p = 0.007 and CI (95%)=−18.51 to −3.52, p = 0.002, respectively)."
