Robert P. Dalka and Justyna P. Zwolak. (2024). Network analysis of graduate program support structures through experiences of various demographic groups. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020106](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020106)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural comparison of ten ASES backbone networks (full plus demographic splits) using NDC, EEJ, and purity measures. The article concludes each demographic network "contains a unique set of edges", so support-structure experiences are connected differently.

> "This means that each network within the demographic-based splits contains a unique set of edges, indicating that the experiences of support structures are connected in different ways."
