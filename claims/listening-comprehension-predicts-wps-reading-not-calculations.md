## Evidence

### Spencer Mercedes 2022

Spencer Mercedes, Fuchs Lynn S., Geary David C., Fuchs Douglas. (2022). Connections between Mathematics and Reading Development: Numerical Cognition Mediates Relations between Foundational Competencies and Later Academic Outcomes. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000670

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

From the final pruned path analysis mediation model (N = 370 first graders followed to the end of second grade). Table 1 gives the direct path from early listening comprehension as 0.167 (95% CI 0.099 to 0.237) to later WPS and 0.103 (0.026 to 0.185) to later reading, with no path to calculations and no indirect path via NC; the Discussion agrees ("Early language was a stronger predictor of later WPS (0.167) compared to later reading (0.103)"). The Results sentence quoted below swaps the two outcomes, apparently a typo. The data are observational, so these are associations, not evidence that listening comprehension causes the later outcomes.

> "Early listening comprehension was associated with later reading (0.167) and WPS only (0.103); intermediary NC did not mediate this relation."
