`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of interrelations among the six strategies in Study 1 (350 German university students). The article attributes the positive expression–support link to "the social-communicative consequences expression may share withseeking supportfrom others", and reports expression and suppression were consistently negatively related.
