## Evidence

### Meg Foster 2023

Meg Foster, Philip von Doetinchem, and Sandra von Doetinchem. (2023). Virtual physics laboratory courses: An evaluation of students' self-efficacy and intelligence mindset. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2301.02699](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2301.02699)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of attitudes toward online learning in a remote physics laboratory course. On Question 3, "Female students reported a mean response of 3.9 ± 0.1, whereas males reported a mean of 3.0 ± 0.2 (p = 0.005)"; 70% of females versus 35% of males agreed or strongly agreed. No effect size is printed.

> "Female students reported a mean response of 3.9 ± 0.1, whereas males reported a mean of 3.0 ± 0.2 (p = 0.005). Further analysis revealed that female students were twice more likely to report satisfaction with the course's online materials than male students."
