## Evidence

### Irving King and Hugo Gold 1911

Irving King and Hugo Gold. (1911). A Tentative Standardization of Certain "Opposites Tests". University of Iowa. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=A+Tentative+Standardization+of+Certain+Opposites+Tests

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Grouping the 100 subjects by penalties for errors and computing average times (Tables I and II): best 50 on easy opposites averaged 123 seconds versus 135 for the poorest 50; on hard lists the most accurate averaged 59 (penalty units) versus 41 for the least accurate, with no time difference.

> "It will be seen from these tables that there is a slight correlation between speed and accuracy in the case of the easy lists but that in the case of the hard lists the time taken by the more accurate is practically identical with the time taken by the less accurate."
