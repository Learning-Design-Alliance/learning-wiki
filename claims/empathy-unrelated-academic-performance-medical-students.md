## Evidence

### Chen Hao 2024

Chen Hao, Xuan Hanwen, Cai Jinquan, Liu Meichen, Shi Lei. (2024). The impact of empathy on medical students: an integrative review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05448-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Austin et al.'s cross-sectional and longitudinal study of 273 UK medical students used JSPE across years and end-of-year overall academic performance; the review reports no statistical evidence of a relationship at any grade, consistent with studies from Iran and South Korea.

> "At any grade, there was no statistical evidence indicated that empathy scores were related to the academic performance of medical students."
