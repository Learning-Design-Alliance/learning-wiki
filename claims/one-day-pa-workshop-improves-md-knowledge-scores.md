## Evidence

### Arciniegas Calle Maria C. 2016

Arciniegas Calle Maria C., Lobelo Felipe, Jiménez Mario A., Páez Diana C., Cortés Sebastian, de Lima Andrés, Duperly John. (2016). One-day workshop-based training improves physical activity prescription knowledge in Latin American physicians: a pre-test post-test study. BMC Public Health 16:1224. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-016-3883-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study without a control group of 1044 MDs across 41 workshops in 12 Latin American countries, using a 20-question multiple-choice test. Scores "improved significantly from 67% (pre-test) to 82% (post-test)" with p < 0.001; no effect size was printed.

> "Mean test scores for all participating MDs improved significantly from 67% (pre-test) to 82% (post-test) after the workshop (p <0.001)."
