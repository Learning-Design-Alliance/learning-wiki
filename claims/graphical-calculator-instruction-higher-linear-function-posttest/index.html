Evidence¶

Kissi et al. 2016¶

Philip Siaw Kissi, Opoku Gyabaah and Sampson Kwadwo Boateng. (2016). The Effects of the use of Microsoft Math Tool (Graphical Calculator) instruction on students' performance in linear functions. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.21. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1109378

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pre-test post-test two-group study of 98 SHS students in Accra; experimental group (n=50) taught with the graphical calculator, control (n=48) by lecture, same instructor. The article reports "a significant difference, t (96) = -6.984, p = 0.00"; post-test means were 12.88 (control) and 17.74 (experimental).

"Independent T-test with equal variance assumed analysis showed that there was a significant difference, t (96) = -6.984, p = 0.00, p < 0.05 in linear function performance of students between the control and the experimental groups."
