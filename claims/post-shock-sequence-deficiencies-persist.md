`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second-phase results for 39 students using modified instructions. The article reports the "items with the lowest compliance" were safety contact check (46.2%), immediate CPR resumption (7.7%), and rhythm reassessment (0%), with the latter two showing no statistically significant improvement over the first phase.

> "The items with the lowest compliance were ensuring that no one was in contact with the patient before shock delivery (46.2%; p < 0.001), immediate resumption of cardiopulmonary resuscitation after shock delivery (7.7%; p = 1.000), and rhythm reassessment after 2 min (0%; p = 0.117)."
