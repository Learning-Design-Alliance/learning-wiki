Evidence¶

Jonna Malmberg 2019¶

Jonna Malmberg, Eetu Haataja, Tapio Seppänen, Sanna Järvelä. (2019). Are we together or not? The temporal interplay of monitoring, physiological arousal and physiological synchrony during a collaborative exam. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning 14:467–490. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-019-09311-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative content analysis of 1 h 51 min of video from four groups' collaborative exams identified individual monitoring utterances, coded by an independent coder with 84.95% agreement and κ = .74. The article reports "cognition most frequently (f = 138)".

"Altogether, the four groups monitored their progress 243 times. The students monitored cognition most frequently (f = 138), followed by behaviour ( f = 99). They monitored motivation and emotions six times collectively."

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Group-level case analysis showed per-member monitoring frequencies varied widely, e.g. in group 1 the left student contributed 25 events (44.64%) and the right student 11 (19.64%), yet "each group member contributed to monitoring the group's progress".
