Exploratory random-effects meta-regressions on study-level moderators in the primary objective AP analysis. No moderator was significant; the article notes fewer than 10 studies contributed to some models, so these analyses should be interpreted with caution. No effect sizes were printed.

> "Meta-regression models showed that none of the participant characteristics considered (proportion of women, mean age, or mean BMI) significantly influenced the association between MedDiet adherence and objective AP (p = 0.794, 0.691, and 0.545, respectively"
