### Syväoja 2019

Syväoja, H. J., Kankaanpää, A., Joensuu, L., Kallio, J., Hakonen, H., Hillman, C. H., & Tammelin, T. H. (2019). The Longitudinal Associations of Fitness and Motor Skills with Academic Achievement. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise. https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000002031

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the final cross-lagged path models for the 954-student cohort, which included GPA, motor skills, and aerobic or muscular fitness simultaneously, neither fitness component significantly predicted GPA.

> "Aerobic fitness or muscular fitness did not predict GPA."
