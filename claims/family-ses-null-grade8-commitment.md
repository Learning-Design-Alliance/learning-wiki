## Evidence

### Yeung Jerf W. K.; Xia Lily L. L 2023

Yeung Jerf W. K.; Xia Lily L. L. (2023). Family and Individual Contexts of Middle-School Years and Educational Achievement of Youths in Middle-Aged Adulthood. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20043279

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second structural equation model of LSAY cohort-2 data. The article reports a non-significant path from grade-7 family SES to grade-8 academic commitment ("β = −0.001, p > 0.05"); equivalence was not tested.

> "Nevertheless, grade-7 family SES only significantly predicted higher educational performance of youths in grade 9 and educational achievement in adulthood, β = 0.108 and 0.015, p < 0.001, but not their grade-8 academic commitment, β = −0.001, p > 0.05."
