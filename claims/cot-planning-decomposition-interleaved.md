### Kamalov 2025

Kamalov, F., Santandreu Calonge, D., Smail, L., Azizov, D., Thadani, D. R., Kwong, T., Atif, A. (2025). Evolution of AI in Education: Agentic Workflows. https://github.com/AzizovDilshod/Multi-agent-System-for-Essay-Assessment/tree/main

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Abuelsaad et al. (2024), that "chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting significantly improved LLMs' multistep reasoning abilities". Section 4.2 describes two approaches per Singh et al. (2024) and Xu et al. (2023): decomposition, where sub-tasks are fully planned then executed sequentially, and the interleaved approach, where decomposition, planning, and execution are intricately intertwined.
