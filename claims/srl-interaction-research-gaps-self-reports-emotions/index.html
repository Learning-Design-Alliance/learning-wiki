Niels J. de Ruig 2023¶

Niels J. de Ruig, Peter F. de Jong, Marjolein Zee. (2023). Stimulating Elementary School Students' Self-Regulated Learning Through High-Quality Interactions and Relationships: A Narrative Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09795-5

Descriptive instrument analysis across the 30 included studies. The review adds that self-report measures often focus on a narrow subset of strategies, so the multiple component structure of SRL is not always captured; only two studies used multidimensional instruments (CP-SRLI, MSLQ).

"In total, 21 out of the 30 included studies (70%) relied exclusively on student self-reports measures for SRL."
