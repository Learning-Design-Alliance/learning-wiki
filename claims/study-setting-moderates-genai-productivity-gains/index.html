Evidence¶

Maier 2026¶

Maier, S., Gunzenhäuser, M., Schweisthal, J., Schneider, M., & Feuerriegel, S. (2026). A meta-analysis of the effect of generative AI on productivity and learning in programming. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04779

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Univariate mixed-effects meta-regression across 16 productivity effect sizes comparing laboratory, enterprise, and open-source settings. The article reports the omnibus test significant (Q_M(2) = 10.51, p = 0.005, ~36% of heterogeneity) and subgroup estimates: "Laboratory experiments yield a large and significant effect (g= 0.73,p <0.001)" versus non-significant enterprise and open-source effects.

"Laboratory experiments yield a large and significant effect (g= 0.73,p <0.001), whereas effects in enterprise (g= 0.19,p= 0.448) and open-source settings (g= 0.01,p= 0.975) are substantially smaller and do not differ signif- icantly from zero."
