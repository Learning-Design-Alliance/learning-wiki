# Native and non-native speaking fourth graders do not differ in monitoring resolution of their open-ended text comprehension answers (Study 2)

## Evidence

### Buehler 2021

Buehler, F. J., van Loon, M. H., Bayard, N. S., Steiner, M., & Roebers, C. M. (2021). Comparing metacognitive monitoring between native and non-native speaking primary school students. Metacognition and Learning, 16, 749–768. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09261-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up univariate tests in Study 2's text comprehension MANOVA found "neither group differences on the monitoring discrimination score" nor on Gamma correlations (both η2p = 0.01), even though both groups monitored above chance (Gammas significantly different from zero).

> "Interest- ing ly, univariate tests revealed neither group differences on the monitoring discrimination score, F(1, 70) = 0.57; p = 0.45; η2 p = 0.01, nor on Gamma cor relations, F (1, 70) = 0.45; p = 51.; η2 p = 0.01."
