### Gutiérrez-Páez 2023

Gutiérrez-Páez, N. F., Santos, P., Hernández-Leo, D., & Carrió, M. (2023). A study of motivations, behavior, and contributions quality in online communities of teachers: A data analytics approach. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104829

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-case analysis of two iterations of log data, questionnaires, and expert assessment in an online pre-service teacher community; the authors report behavior and quality "are affected by the incentives and the features implemented in the supporting platform".

> "In contrast, the results show that behavior and the contributions' quality are affected by the incentives and the features implemented in the supporting platform of the online community."
