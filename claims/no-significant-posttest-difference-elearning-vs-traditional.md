## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no statistically significant difference in post-knowledge test scores between the control (traditional teaching) and intervention (E-learning) groups; equivalence was not tested. [→ Verheijden Michelle J. H. 2021](#verheijden-michelle-j-h-2021)

## Evidence

### Verheijden Michelle J. H. 2021

Verheijden Michelle J. H., Martens Herm, Heeneman Sylvia. (2021). A dermatology E-learning programme is perceived as a valuable learning tool in postgraduate general practice training. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.612f.3d6c

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test comparing post-test scores of 9 control and 12 intervention GP residents in the randomized quantitative arm. The article reports "no significant difference", with means of 66.38% versus 64.92% and "t(18)=0.351, p=0.730, Cohen’s d=0.10".

> "There was no significant difference in post-knowledge test scores of the control (M=66.38%, SD=15.78%) and intervention group (M=64.92%, SD=13.65%, t(18)=0.351, p=0.730, Cohen’s d=0.10)."
