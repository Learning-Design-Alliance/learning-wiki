What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2016). Functional Behavioral Assessment-based Interventions: Children Identified With or At Risk for an Emotional Disturbance. WWC Intervention Report. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/667
