## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Multiple regressions show BME effects at each stage of both examinations after adjusting for earlier performance, e.g. beta = -.265 for Part 1 and beta = -.208 for CSA after taking MRCP(UK) and AKT into account. [→ Wakeford Richard 2015](#wakeford-richard-2015)

## Evidence

### Wakeford Richard 2015

Wakeford Richard, Denney MeiLing, Ludka-Stempien Katarzyna, Dacre Jane, McManus I C. (2015). Cross-comparison of MRCGP & MRCP(UK) in a database linkage study of 2,284 candidates taking both examinations: assessment of validity and differential performance by ethnicity. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0281-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Series of multiple regression analyses (Table 3) assessing the BME effect after earlier performance was taken into account; printed coefficients include beta = -.265 (Part 1) and beta = -.208 (CSA), all p < .001 except AKT Evidence Interpretation (p = .393, NS) after other sub-scores were included.

> "Table 3 also shows that there is an ethnicity effect in both MRCGP and MRCP(UK) at each stage of each examination, BME candidates performing less well even after taking performance at previous stages into account."
