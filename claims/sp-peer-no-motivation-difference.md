### Hecimovich Mark 2014

Hecimovich Mark, Volet Simone. (2014). Simulated learning in musculoskeletal assessment and rehabilitation education: comparing the effect of a simulation-based learning activity with a peer-based learning activity. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0253-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures MANOVA on the 3-item engagement in lifelong learning scale (t1 α = .72; t2 α = .82) in the two-condition study. The article reports "no interaction effect" and a significant overall increase; no effect size is printed.

> "A MANOVA for group by time for the motivation scale revealed no interaction effect. Overall both groups displayed greater motivation for lifelong professional development overtime (F = 10.05 (1, 40), p = .003)."
