Evidence¶

Bickford Michael 2025¶

Bickford Michael, Alruwaili Fayez, Ragab Sara, Rothenberg Hanna, Abedin-Nasab Mohammad (2025). Impact of extended reality on robot-assisted surgery training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-025-02559-z

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis comparing XR training to dry lab training on time to complete. The result was "not statistically significant" with Cohen's d = 0.65 and a 95% CI of [-0.22, 1.52].

"A random-effects analysis of time to complete showed a moderate, not statistically significant increase in time to complete for participants trained in virtual reality compared to participants trained in a dry lab (Cohen’s d = 0.65, 95% CI [−0.22,1.52], p = 0.14)"
