## Evidence

### Shen Yi 2026

Shen Yi, Wang Ye, Wu Shiyu, Yang Rumei, He Ming, Ma Qi. (2026). A cross-sectional comparison of nursing knowledge attitudes and practices in adverse event management based on simulation training experience. Scientific Reports 16, 8401. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39781-0

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis of KAP domain scores in the 931-nurse cross-sectional survey. All three pairwise correlations were positive and statistically significant (P < 0.001), with the attitude–practice coefficient (r = 0.876) exceeding the knowledge–attitude (r = 0.628) and knowledge–practice (r = 0.671) coefficients.

> "Correlation analysis demonstrated significant positive relationships between knowledge and attitude (r = 0.6280, P < 0.001), as well as knowledge and practice (r = 0.6707, P < 0.001). A strong correlation was also found between attitude and practice (r = 0.8756, P < 0.001; Table 5)."
