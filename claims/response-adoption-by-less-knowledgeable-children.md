## Evidence

### Diego Pablo de la Hera 2019

Diego Pablo de la Hera, Mariano Sigman and Cecilia Ines Calero. (2019). Social interaction and conceptual change pave the way away from children 's misconceptions about the Earth. npj Science of Learning 4:12. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0051-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Trait-matching analysis over 15 dyads comparing matching of 44 coded traits before (L1M1) and after (L2M2) interaction. Matching "significantly increased after the collaborative task", t(14) = 3.41, p = 0.004, d = 0.99.

> "A paired-samples t test showed that the mean number of traits matching between peers signiﬁcantly increased after the colla- borative task, t(14) = 3.41, p = 0.004, d = 0.99, 95% CI (0.23, 1.75)"

### Diego Pablo de la Hera 2019 (2)

Diego Pablo de la Hera, Mariano Sigman and Cecilia Ines Calero. (2019). Social interaction and conceptual change pave the way away from children 's misconceptions about the Earth. npj Science of Learning 4:12. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0051-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t tests of response adoption (change in traits matching the partner's Interview 1 responses). Adoption was "significant only for Children L", t(14) = 4.91, p = 2.32E−04, d = 1.51; for Children M it was not significant, d = .11.

> "Paired-samples t tests showed that response adoption was found signiﬁcant only for Children L, whereas not for Children M, t(14) = 4.91, p = 2.32E−04, d = 1.51, 95% CI (0.70, 2.32)"
