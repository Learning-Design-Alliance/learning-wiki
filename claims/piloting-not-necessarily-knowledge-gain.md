## Evidence

### Nilsson Maria Skyvell 2010

Nilsson Maria Skyvell, Pennbrant Sandra, Pilhammar Ewa, Wenestam Claes-Göran. (2010). Pedagogical strategies used in clinical medical education: an observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the observational analysis of the piloting strategy: teachers used guiding questions, statements or signals to keep students on track, but "Students acted according to the teacher's directives without discussing the meaning or intended goal", so understanding was rarely verifiable.

> "Easing the student's actions by piloting does not necessarily lead to the intended perception or increase of knowledge. Students acted according to the teacher's directives without discussing the meaning or intended goal."
