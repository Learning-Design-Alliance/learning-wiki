# The LCAS iteration scale differentiates CURE students from traditional lab students with a medium effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` CURE students rated their courses higher on the iteration scale than traditional lab students (p < 0.05, d = 0.39). [→ Corwin Lisa A. 2015](#corwin-lisa-a-2015)

## Evidence

### Corwin Lisa A. 2015

Corwin Lisa A., Runyon Christopher, Robinson Aspen, Dolan Erin L. (2015). The Laboratory Course Assessment Survey: A Tool to Measure Three Dimensions of Research-Course Design. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0073

`q3 · i1` · `design · r3`

Same Welch-corrected known-groups comparison (iteration scale n = 134: 72 CURE, 62 traditional). The iteration contrast reached p < 0.05 with "d = 0.39". Table 4 reports iteration means of 28.71 (CURE) versus 26.53 (traditional).

> "CURE students also rated their courses higher on the discovery and relevance (p < 0.001, d = 0.71) and iteration (p < 0.05, d = 0.39) scales than students in traditional lab courses."
