## Evidence

### Kumar Manish 2025

Kumar Manish, Mittal Mahima, Chavan Seema Shankarsingh, Krishnan S Vimal, Gilbert John H.V., Sanatombi Devi Elsa. (2025). Interprofessional simulation training in acute pediatrics using rapid cycle deliberate practice (ip-star). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08302-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pre-post study of 72 third-year MBBS and BSc Nursing students; knowledge measured with a 10-item MCQ test before and after the simulation intervention. The article reports "pre-test median 6 (2) vs. post-test median 8 (2), p < 0.01"; no effect size is printed.

> "Knowledge acquisition showed a marked increase, with post-test scores demonstrating significantly higher median (IQR) values compared to pre-test scores - pre-test median 6 (2) vs. post-test median 8 (2), p < 0.01."
