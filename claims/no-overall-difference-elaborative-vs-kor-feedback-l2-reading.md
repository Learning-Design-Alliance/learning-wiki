## Subclaims
`q3 i1` After controlling for pre-test reading comprehension, post-test scores did not differ significantly between the elaborative feedback group and the knowledge of response feedback group. [→ Andy Bown 2017](#andy-bown-2017)

## Evidence

### Andy Bown 2017

Andy Bown. (2017). Elaborative Feedback to Enhance Online Second Language Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 10(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n12p164

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 92 participants (47 Group 1, 45 Group 2) completing both tests; ANCOVA controlling for pre-test. "no significant difference in mean scores on the post-test". The article reports a small effect size, partial eta squared = .015, with 1.5% of post-test variance accounted for by treatment condition.

> "The results of the ANCOV A indicated that, after contro lling for performance on the pre-test, there was no significant difference in mean scores on the post-test between Group 1 (M=7.09, SD=2.17) and Group 2 (M=6.44, SD=2.38), F(1, 89)=1.39, p= .241."
