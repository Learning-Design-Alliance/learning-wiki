Evidence¶

Cookson 2018¶

Cookson, P. W., Jr., Darling-Hammond, L., Rothman, R., & Shields, P. M. (2018). The tapestry of American public education: How we can create a system of schools worth choosing for all? Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/tapestry-american-public-education

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's review of charter school research, covering more than 6,700 charter schools enrolling 2.7 million students in 44 states and DC. It specifically flags virtual and distance-learning charters as significantly worse performing.

"Studies of charter school quality have consistently found great variation in student achievement, with some performing better than district schools and others performing similarly or worse."

Cookson 2018 (2)¶

Cookson, P. W., Jr., Darling-Hammond, L., Rothman, R., & Shields, P. M. (2018). The tapestry of American public education: How we can create a system of schools worth choosing for all? Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/tapestry-american-public-education

q2 · i? · review · r3

The report's review of access and integration studies, including a 2010 Civil Rights Project finding that Black charter students were twice as likely to be in intensely segregated settings, and California research finding 20% of charters illegally restricted access for high-need students.

"Studies have also shown that students with disabilities are underrepresented in charter schools."
