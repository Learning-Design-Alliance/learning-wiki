## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The post-class theoretical score in the 3D printing + PBL group was significantly higher than in the traditional group (8.33 ± 1.33 vs 7.06 ± 1.64, t = −6.983, P < .001). [→ Jiang W 2022](#jiang-w-2022)

## Evidence

### Jiang W 2022

Jiang W, Jiang W, Jin P, Zhang J, Xia J, Wei W, Qing B. (2022). Application of 3D printing technology combined with PBL teaching method in clinical teaching of cerebrovascular disease: An observational study. Medicine 2022;101:47(e31970). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000031970

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of examination scores between 269 randomly divided interns (traditional n = 133, 3D printing + PBL n = 136) reported in Results §3.2, Table 2: post-class theoretical score "8.33 ± 1.33" vs 7.06 ± 1.64, t = −6.983, P < .001.

> "The post-class theoretical and clinical practice scores in the 3D printing + PBL group were significantly higher than that in the traditional group (P < .001, Table 2, Fig. 2)."
