## Evidence

### Kaluschke Maximilian 2023

Kaluschke Maximilian, Yin Myat Su, Haddawy Peter, Suebnukarn Siriwan, Zachmann Gabriel. (2023). The effect of 3D stereopsis and hand-tool alignment on learning effectiveness and skill transfer of a VR-based simulator for dental training. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0291389

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learning-transfer analysis correlating real learning gain (expert-rated pre/post plastic teeth) with virtual learning gain (trial #1 to trial #6 simulator scores) across the four condition groups of the 40-participant study.

> "Group 2 had the highest correlation (R = 0.49, p = 0.15), this indicates that in our setup, the conditions monoscopic 3D & hand-tool alignment create a learning environment that best translates the acquired skill to the real world."
