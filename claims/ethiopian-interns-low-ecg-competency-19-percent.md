### Godana Honey 2026

Godana Honey, Kedir Seman, Zewdie Ayalew, Arero Godana. (2026). Assessment of electrocardiography interpretation competency of Ethiopian medical interns: a multi-site study. Scientific Reports 16, 3939. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-33969-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 220 interns at four randomly selected Ethiopian medical colleges using a 14-item ECG test; competency defined as scoring ≥80%. The study reports "The overall competency rate was 19.1%" with an average score of 65.93%.

> "The overall competency rate was 19.1%, with significant institutional variation (p < 0.001), ranging from 7.1% to 32.7%."
