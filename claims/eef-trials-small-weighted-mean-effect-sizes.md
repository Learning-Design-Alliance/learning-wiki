## Evidence

### Demack 2021

Demack, S., Maxwell, B., Coldwell, M., Stevens, A., Wolstenholme, C., Reaney-Wood, S., Stiell, B., & Lortie-Forgues, H. (2021). Review of EEF projects: Evaluation report. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED625480

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of effect sizes from the first 82 EEF RCT evaluations published up to January 2019. The review reports "an overall meta-analysis weighted mean effect size of +0.04 standard deviations (SD)" for primary ITT attainment outcomes.

> "A total of 133 primary ITT effect sizes were reported by the 82 evaluations in the review and an overall meta-analysis weighted mean effect size of +0.04 standard deviations (SD) was observed."

### Demack 2021 (2)

Demack, S., Maxwell, B., Coldwell, M., Stevens, A., Wolstenholme, C., Reaney-Wood, S., Stiell, B., & Lortie-Forgues, H. (2021). Review of EEF projects: Evaluation report. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED625480

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of secondary ITT attainment effect sizes from 35 of the 82 EEF evaluations. The weighted mean was "+0.01 SD", smaller than the primary outcome mean.

> "A total of 78 secondary ITT attainment effect sizes were reported by 35 of the 82 evaluations in the review and an overall meta-analysis weighted mean effect size of +0.01 SD was observed."

### Demack 2021 (3)

Demack, S., Maxwell, B., Coldwell, M., Stevens, A., Wolstenholme, C., Reaney-Wood, S., Stiell, B., & Lortie-Forgues, H. (2021). Review of EEF projects: Evaluation report. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED625480

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of FSM (free school meals) subsample attainment effect sizes from 73 of the 82 EEF evaluations. The weighted mean was "+0.03 SD".

> "A total of 149 FSM subsample attainment effect sizes were reported by 73 of the 82 evaluations in the review and an overall meta-analysis weighted mean effect size of +0.03 SD was observed."
