### Wu Zongxi 2026

Wu Zongxi, Wang Mingyue, Luo Xueting, Liu Xiangqi, Yang Le, Gao Siyong, Zhang Sien, Zheng Guangsen. (2026). Structured Microskill Stepwise Progression for Undergraduate Tooth Extraction: A Randomized Mixed-Methods Study. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.111097

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Score dispersion in end-of-rotation modified DOPS performance was compared between the two groups using the Brown–Forsythe test. The experimental group showed lower dispersion, with scores ranging from 87 to 100 versus 75 to 96 in the control group. The authors note this finding

> "The Brown–Forsythe test indicated a statistically significant between-group difference in score dispersion, F(1, 38) = 4.75, P = .036. This finding should be interpreted cautiously given the modest sample size and the bounded nature of DOPS scores."
