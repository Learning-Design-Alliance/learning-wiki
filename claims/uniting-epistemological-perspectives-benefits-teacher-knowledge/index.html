Theoretical argument in the introduction and theoretical framework: the two perspectives are presented as coexisting types of knowledge rather than mutually exclusive stances, and attending to both is said to maximize affordances of a deliberate, often deductive mindset as well as an aware, often inductive mindset. The article offers no empirical test of this claim.
