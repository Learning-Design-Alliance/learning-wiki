## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers describe dispersed energy as increasingly negligible and unable to be harnessed, gesturing outward as they speak of energy spreading or dissipating. [→ Daane 2014](#daane-2014)

## Evidence

### Daane 2014

Daane, Vokos, and Scherr. (2014). Goals for teacher learning about energy degradation and usefulness. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 10, 020111. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.10.020111

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Episode analysis: Owen describes thermal energy from a hot plate traveling until its impact is 'more negligible,' with an outward spreading gesture; Vicki describes energy dispersing ('It juuuust keeeps goooing out') and shakes her head when asked if it could be harnessed; Rosie equates dissipation with 'gone away from us.'

> "He then states that the impact of the energy becomes more negligible as it spreads through a larger volume, similar to the idea that energy becomes less useful as it disperses."
