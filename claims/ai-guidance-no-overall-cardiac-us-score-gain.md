## Evidence

### Karni Ofri 2025

Karni Ofri, Shitrit Itamar Ben, Perlin Amit, Jedwab Roni, Wacht Oren, Fuchs Lior. (2025). AI-enhanced guidance demonstrated improvement in novices' Apical-4-chamber and Apical-5-chamber views. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06905-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized controlled trial of 44 novices (21 AI-enhanced, 23 non-AI) after an identical six-hour cardiac US course; blinded expert scoring of exam clips. The article reports "no statistically significant difference" in the overall Cardiac US Score; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no statistically significant difference in the Cardiac US Score between the two study groups [mean 67% (SD ± 15%) vs. mean 64% (SD ± 13%), p = 0.17; Table 2; Fig. 3]."
