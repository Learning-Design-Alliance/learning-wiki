# Studies of simulated patient-based CST report positive effects on trainee confidence and communication performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Empirical studies of SP-based CST across student and practising HCP settings report increased confidence, improved communication performance judged by SPs, and improved standardised assessment performance. [→ Pilnick Alison 2018](#pilnick-alison-2018)

The review's synthesis of empirical SP studies across settings (anaesthesia, obstetrics, emergency medicine, psychiatry, speech and language) reports positive effects including "increased confidence of the students or HCPs". No effect sizes are printed.
