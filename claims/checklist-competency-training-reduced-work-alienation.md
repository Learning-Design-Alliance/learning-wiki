## Evidence

### Wang Liuliu 2026

Wang Liuliu, Liu Zeyu, Tong Minghui, Guo Junyan, Zhu Hongmei. (2026). Effect of a checklist-based core competency training and evaluation system on work-related alienation among critical care nurses. Frontiers in Public Health, 14. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1708734

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired pre-post measurement of 150 critical care nurses using the 20-item Work Alienation Questionnaire (Cronbach's α 0.953 as reported). The article states work alienation scores "declined from 42.14 ± 2.23 to 35.49 ± 4.59" with p < 0.001.

> "Specifically, work alienation scores declined from 42.14 ± 2.23 to 35.49 ± 4.59, perceived organizational support increased from 15.24 ± 2.16 to 21.19 ± 1.08"
