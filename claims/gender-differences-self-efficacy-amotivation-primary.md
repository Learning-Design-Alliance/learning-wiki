## Evidence

### Usán Pablo 2022

Usán Pablo, Salavera Carlos, Quílez-Robres Alberto, Lozano-Blasco Raquel. (2022). Behaviour Patterns between Academic Motivation, Burnout and Academic Performance in Primary School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191912663

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the 398-student sample (224 boys, 174 girls; Table 2) comparing boys and girls on burnout and motivation dimensions. The study reports "Cohen's d = −0.220" for self-efficacy favouring girls and "Cohen's d = −0.280" for amotivation favouring boys.

> "self-efficacy (the most adaptive dimension of burnout) yielded a (small) significant size effect, especially in girls (Cohen's d = −0.220), whereas amotivation was found to be higher among boys (Cohen's d = −0.280)."
