## Evidence

### Chen Hui-Wen 2025

Chen Hui-Wen, Tsai Hung-Wei, Chen Yi-Chun, Chiu Yu-Jui, Kang Enoch Yi-No, Hsu Yi-Ting, Issenberg S. Barry, Wu Jen-Chieh. (2025). Exploring effects of a booster workshop on progression and retention of resuscitation skills of residents when added to regular low-dose simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06705-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group comparison (Mann-Whitney U test) in a quasi-experimental comparative study of 89 residents at a single Taiwanese teaching hospital, reported in Results Table 3. The article reports "significantly higher scores" for the SET-CPR group with p < 0.05 and no effect size.

> "At the mid-test, the SET-CPR training group had significantly higher scores than the regular low-dose training group for medical task performance, teamwork behavior performance, patient safety attitude, and time to initiation of chest compressions (p < 0.05)."
