# Verbal memory test scores correlate positively with intrinsic academic motivation scores in junior high but not elementary students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Digit span forward score correlated positively with intrinsic academic motivation score in junior high students (r = .262), and digit span forward and kana comprehension scores intercorrelated (r = .182). [→ Mizuno K 2011](#mizuno-k-2011)

## Evidence

### Mizuno K 2011

Mizuno K, Tanaka M, Fukuda S, Imai-Matsumura K, Watanabe Y. (2011). Relationship between cognitive function and prevalence of decrease in intrinsic academic motivation in adolescents. Behavioral and Brain Functions 7:4. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-7-4

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in junior high students (Table 2). Digit span forward r = .262, list learning r = .209, list recall r = .186, story comprehension r = .264 (all p < .05); the discussion reports the digit span-kana comprehension correlation as "r = .182, p = .038".

> "In the junior high school students, the scores for list learning and list recall tests, digit span forward test, and comprehension of the story in the kana pick-out test were positively correla-ted with intrinsic academic motivation score."
