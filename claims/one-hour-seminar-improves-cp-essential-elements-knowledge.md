## Evidence

### Xaverius Pamela K. 2014

Xaverius Pamela K., Grady Mary Alice. (2014). Centering Pregnancy in Missouri: A System Level Analysis. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/285386

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Matched pretest-posttest observational study of seminar attendees (n = 223 surveys; this paired analysis restricted to the n = 70 who had heard of CP before). The article reports the paired t-test: "pretest average = 63.5% and posttest average = 88.4%" with t = − 7.052, P = 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "a paired samples t-test revealed a statistically significant posttest improvement in the overall understanding of the 14 essential elements of CP (pretest average = 63.5% and posttest average = 88.4%) (t = − 7.052, P = 0.001)"
