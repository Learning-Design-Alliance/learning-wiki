### Gutiérrez-González R 2025

Gutiérrez-González R, Royuela A, Zamarron A. (2025). Student engagement in a flipped undergraduate medical classroom to measure optimal video-based lecture length. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2479752

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression of the viewing index (average minutes per video divided by total length, capturing cumulative visits) on video length, seminar status, weeks to exam and embedded questions; "videos under 5 minutes (p = 0.049)" was independently significant. No effect size printed.
