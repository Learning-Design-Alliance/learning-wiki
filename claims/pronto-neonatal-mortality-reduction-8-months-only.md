### Walker Dilys M. MD 2016

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Noncumulative difference-in-differences analysis of hospital-based neonatal mortality from 50,589 live births across 24 hospitals, adjusted for NICU presence. The 8-month estimate was "IRRDID = 0.60; 95% CI, 0.37–0.95"; the effect was not significant at 4 or 12 months; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "Incidence of hospital-based neonatal mortality tended to be lower in the intervention group relative to controls, and at the 8-month interval posttraining, it was significantly lower by 40% after adjustment for baseline differences (IRRDID = 0.60; 95% CI, 0.37–0.95)"
