Spain 2018¶

Spain, A.K., Ehrlich, S.B., Cowhy, J.R., Dasgupta, D.K., & Lockaby, T. (2018). A community effort to support the transition from pre-K to kindergarten. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research and Chapin Hall at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/community-effort-support-transition-pre-k-kindergarten

q2 · i? · design · r2

Findings from the developmental evaluation's triangulated survey, interview, focus group, and observation data on relationships among teachers and family members. Participants reported that community events created space for informal conversations, with some describing these conversations as changing how teachers and parents communicated about children's academic progress and behavior.

"Teachers and parents repor ted a greater sense of community as a result of their PKTP participation. Pre-K and kindergarten teachers reported feeling more connected to each other across grade levels and schools because of their participation in PKTP."
