## Evidence

### Koc Akran 2018

Koc Akran, S., & Uzum, B. (2018). The effect of the layered curriculum on the 6th grade students' learning styles in Science lesson. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 4(3), 141-152. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.4.3.141

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square analysis of Kolb Learning Styles Inventory pre-test data from 22 experimental-group students (12 female, 10 male) in a quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test design; the article reports a significant gender difference (p=.00; p<.05), described as "on behalf of female students" with converging (66.7%) and assimilating (16.7%) styles and males accommodating (60%) and diverging (40%).

> "In Table 1, a significant difference by gender in the pre -test styles of students in the experimental group who were exposed to the layered curriculum was observed (p=.00; p<.05)."
