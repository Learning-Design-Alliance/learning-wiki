# Mann-Whitney tests found no significant difference in the number of answers between clicker and non-clicker groups in either experimental period

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2018 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the first period, the difference in numbers of answers between Group A (clickers) and Group B (non-clickers) was not statistically significant. [→ Unal Cakiroglu 2018](#unal-cakiroglu-2018)
`q2 i?` In the second period, the difference in numbers of answers between the groups, with roles swapped, was also not statistically significant. [→ Unal Cakiroglu 2018 (2)](#unal-cakiroglu-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Unal Cakiroglu 2018

Unal Cakiroglu, Fatih Erdogdu, Seyfullah Gokoglu. (2018). Clickers in EFL Classrooms: Evidence from Two Different Uses. Contemporary Educational Technology, 9(2), 171-185. https://doi.org/10.30935/cet.414820

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-parametric Mann-Whitney test comparing the two groups (n = 10 each) in the first period, reported in Table 1. The article reports "U=28.00, p>0.05" but notes Group A (using clickers) seemed to respond more by sum of ranks; equivalence was not tested.

> "there is no significant differences between the numbers of answers given by the Group A and B during first period ( U=28.00, p>0.05)."

### Unal Cakiroglu 2018 (2)

Unal Cakiroglu, Fatih Erdogdu, Seyfullah Gokoglu. (2018). Clickers in EFL Classrooms: Evidence from Two Different Uses. Contemporary Educational Technology, 9(2), 171-185. https://doi.org/10.30935/cet.414820

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney test for the second period, reported in Table 2 with "U=35.00, p>0.05"; by sum of ranks Group B (using clickers) seemed to respond more than Group A. No effect size is printed.

> "there is no significant differences between the numbers of answers given by the Group A and B during second period ( U=35.00, p>0.05)."
