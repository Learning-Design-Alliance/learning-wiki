### Briley L. Lewis 2023

Briley L. Lewis, K. Supriya, Graham H. Read, Kaitlin L. Ingraham Dixie, Rachel Kennison, and Anthony R. Friscia. (2023). Eﬀects of Popular Science Writing Instruction on General Education Student Attitudes Towards Science: A Case Study in Astronomy. Originally published 2022. `q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interviews with 5 students from the 2021-2022 course offerings; students reported that writing explanations increased comprehension and that writing "married" science with humanities critical thinking, providing an entry point based on prior experiences.

> "I think by having to relay the information in an accessible, clear manner, you have to really understand it. . . Like you have to understand it well, in order to explain it to diﬀerent groups of people."
