## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The pre-presentation flooding group produced significantly fewer grammatical errors in dictogloss than the post-presentation flooding group. [→ Rikhtegar 2015](#rikhtegar-2015)

## Evidence

### Rikhtegar 2015

Rikhtegar, O., & Gholami, J. (2015). The Effects of Pre- versus Post-Presentation Input Flooding via Reading on the Young Iranian EFL Learners' Acquisition of Simple Past Tense. English Language Teaching, 8(3), 80-88. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n3p80

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on dictogloss grammatical error counts (n = 57), F = 79.376, p = 0.001; LSD post hoc showed the pre-post difference of "0.61" significant at 0.006, favouring fewer errors in the pre-presentation group.

> "the estimated mean difference of pre and post-presentation groups (0.61) is significant at 0.006 alpha level. The results of table 5 show that the mean of grammatical errors of post-presentation group is higher than pre-presentation group."
