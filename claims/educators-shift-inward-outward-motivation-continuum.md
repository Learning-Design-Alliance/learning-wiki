### L. M. Rebull 2018

L. M. Rebull, T. Roberts, W. Laurence, M. T. Fitzgerald, D. A. French, V. Gorjian, and G. K. Squires. (2018). Motivations of Educators for Participating in NITARP, an Authentic Astronomy Research Experience Professional Development Program. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010148)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative narrative analysis of open-ended feedback surveys from NITARP educator participants at up to four waypoints; the authors report that "participants fall over the whole range" of the inward/outward focus continuum and move along it during the year. No effect size is printed.

> "In practice, participants fall over the whole range, and moreover, they move along the continuum during the year."
