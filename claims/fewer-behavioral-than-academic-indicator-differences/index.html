Evidence¶

Hester 2022¶

Hester, C., Lazarev, V., Zacamy, J., Nardi, C., & Feygin, A. (2022). Student group differences in Arkansas' indicators of postsecondary readiness and success. Regional Educational Laboratory Southwest. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs/regions/southwest/rel_2023-145.asp

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of the same statewide cohort data comparing behavioral indicator attainment (suspension, expulsion, attendance) across student groups. The authors report "substantial differences occurred in four behavioral indicators" for the groups with the most differences, fewer than the six or seven for academic indicators.

"However, for all of these groups, substantial differences occurred in four behavioral indicators (table 2, last column) compared with substantial differences in six or seven academic indicators (see table 1, last column)."
