Evidence¶

Angela Johnson 2020¶

Angela Johnson, Megan Kuhfeld. (2020). Fall 2019 to fall 2020 MAP Growth attrition analysis. NWEA Research. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/fall-2019-to-fall-2020-map-growth-attrition-analysis/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Attrition analysis comparing 5.2 million students tested in fall 2019, contrasting "stayers" and "attriters" on demographics and prior achievement. The study found "systematic demographic differences across subjects and grades" favoring stayers on race/ethnicity and prior achievement.

"The results of this study showed systematic demographic differences across subjects and grades: a larger fraction of attriters were minoritized students, students with lower achievement in fall 2019, and students in schools with higher concentrations of racial/ethnic minorities and socioeconomically-disadvantaged students."
