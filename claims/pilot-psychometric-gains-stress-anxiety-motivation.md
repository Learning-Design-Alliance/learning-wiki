## Evidence

### Ben Chaabene 2025

Ben Chaabene, N.E.H., Hammami, H., Kahloul, L. (2025). Decoding Student Minds: Leveraging Conversational Agents for Psychological and Learning Analysis. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.10441](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.10441)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Pre-post psychometric assessment at T0, T1, T2 during the 8-week pilot (N=45) using PSS, STAI, and AMS; Table 7 reports changes of -19.2% (stress, d = 0.87), -16.4% (anxiety, d = 1.20), and +26.2% (motivation, d = 1.07), with "Holm-Bonferroni-adjusted p <0.01 for all measures".

> "Paired-sample t-tests confirmed the statistical significance of these improvements after correction for multiple comparisons across the three psychometric measures (Holm-Bonferroni-adjusted p <0.01 for all measures"
