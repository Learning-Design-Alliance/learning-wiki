### Naeimi and Foo 2015

Maki Naeimi & Thomas Chow Voon Foo. (2015). Vocabulary Acquisition through Direct and Indirect Learning Strategies. English Language Teaching, 8(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n10p142

Effect size analysis of the pre-post improvement for the 30-student direct strategies group. The article reports η2 = 0.11 and classifies it as a large effect size against Cohen (1988) criteria, though the printed value sits between the moderate (0.06) and large (0.14) thresholds.

> "The magnitude of the differences in the means was large (η2 = 0.11). Compared to this eta-squared value obtained (η2 = 0.021) to Cohen (1988) criteria (0.01 = small effect, 0.06 = moderate ef fect, and 0.14 = large effect ), this is clear that the effect size found is a large effect size."
