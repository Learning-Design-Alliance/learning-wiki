Evidence¶

Gambari 2016¶

Gambari, A. I., Shittu, A. T., Daramola, F. O., & James, M. (2016). Effects Of Video-Based Cooperative, Competitive And Individualized Instructional Strategies On The Performance Of Senior Secondary Schools Students In Geometry. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences, 4(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1116321

q3 · i? · causal · r1

One-way ANOVA on post-test Geometry Achievement Test scores of 120 students in four groups (30 each) after six weeks of video-based treatment. The article reports "significant difference was established among the experimental gro ups and control group" (F = 266.164, p = 0.000); no effect size is printed.

"From the table, the calculated F -value (266.164, p = 0.000) was significant at 0.05 alpha level. This indicates that statistically, significant difference was established among the experimental gro ups and control group."
