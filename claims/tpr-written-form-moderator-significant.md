# Teaching the written form of words significantly moderates TPR effects: studies teaching written forms averaged ES=1.777 versus 0.770 for those that did not

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The written-form analytical theme significantly explained variance (Qb=5.875, p=0.015), with implemented studies showing ES=1.777 (1.044, 2.509) versus 0.770 (0.415, 1.125) for non-implementers. [→ Tugba Inciman Celik 2021](#tugba-inciman-celik-2021)

## Evidence

### Tugba Inciman Celik 2021

Tugba Inciman Celik, Tolga Cay & Sedat Kanadli. (2021). The Effect of Total Physical Response Method on Vocabulary Learning/Teaching: A Mixed Research Synthesis. English Language Teaching, 14(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v14n12p154

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Categorical moderator analysis (Table 6) of the written-form theme across the 13 studies; five studies (Nguyen et al., 2020; Harrath, 2016; Kassem, 2010; Zhen, 2011; Khusniyati et al., 2020) implemented it. The difference was significant at p=0.015.

> "the average effect size was calculated as Theme 6 (Implemented), 1.777 (1.044, 2.509) in the studies in which the TPR method was implemented, in which the practitioners also taught the written forms of the words, while the effect size was calculated as Theme 6 (Non-implemented), 0.770 (0.415, 1.125)"
