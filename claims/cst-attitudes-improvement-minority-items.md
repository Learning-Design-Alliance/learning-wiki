## Evidence

### Jin Hye Kyung 2019

Jin Hye Kyung, Park So Hyun, Kang Ji Eun, Choi Kyung Suk, Kim Hong Ah, Jeon Min Seon, Rhie Sandy Jeong. (2019). The influence of a patient counseling training session on pharmacy students’ self-perceived communication skills, confidence levels, and attitudes about communication skills training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1607-x

`q2 · i?`

Paired t-test with Bonferroni correction on the modified CSAS attitude scale (19 items, 5-point Likert) in the same sixty-student pre-post survey; the "pre, 4.00 (0.45) vs. post, 4.22 (0.45) p < 0.001" total score improved significantly.

> "pre, 4.00 (0.45) vs. post, 4.22 (0.45) p < 0.001 (Table 3)"

### Jin Hye Kyung 2019 (2)

Jin Hye Kyung, Park So Hyun, Kang Ji Eun, Choi Kyung Suk, Kim Hong Ah, Jeon Min Seon, Rhie Sandy Jeong. (2019). The influence of a patient counseling training session on pharmacy students’ self-perceived communication skills, confidence levels, and attitudes about communication skills training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1607-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' discussion of item-level results: "only 3 of the survey questions about self-assessed attitudes showed significant changes", unlike the communication skills and confidence results.

> "Although the total mean score of the self-assessed attitude items improved significantly, only 3 of the survey questions about self-assessed attitudes showed significant changes, which was different from the results of the self-assessed communication skills and confidence level questions."
