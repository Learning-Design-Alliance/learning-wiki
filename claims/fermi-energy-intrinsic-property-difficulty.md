### Paul Justice 2019

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Student understanding of Fermi energy, the Fermi-Dirac distribution and total electronic energy of a free electron gas. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Written pretest question 1a and interviews in the upper-level QM course. Students gave responses such as "Cube B has higher Fermi energy because higher states must be filled"; the difficulty is with level structure and its dependence on sample size, and one student wrote correct equations but "did not take into account the volume" and the number density of free electrons.
