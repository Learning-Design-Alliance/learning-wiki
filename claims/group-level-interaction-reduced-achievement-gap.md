## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The interaction between group and level of pre-achievement was significant in favor of the high achievement group in the experimental condition, and low achievers in the experimental group approached the control group’s high achievers. ## Evidence

### Ziad Mohammed Mahmoud Qabaja 2016

The Effect of Using the “SQP2RS via WTL” Strategy through Science Context to 10th Graders’ Reading Comprehension in English in Palestine.
