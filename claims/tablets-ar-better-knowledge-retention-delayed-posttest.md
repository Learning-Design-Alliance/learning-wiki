## Evidence

### Fokides and Mastrokoukou 2018

Emmanuel Fokides and Aikaterini Mastrokoukou. (2018). Results from a Study for Teaching Human Body Systems to Primary School Students Using Tablets. Contemporary Educational Technology, 9(2), 154-170. https://doi.org/10.30935/cet.414808

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Bonferroni-corrected post-hoc comparison on the delayed post-test (max = 35) in the quasi-experiment. "Group3 (mean rank score = 31.76) fared better than Group2" and also better than Group1 (r = -0.59); Group2 also beat Group1 (r = -0.34).

> "Delayed post-test. Group3 (mean rank score = 31.76) fared better than Group2 (mean rank score = 19.24), (U = 156.00, Z = -3.05, p = 0.002) and the effect size was medium (r = -0.43)."
