## Evidence

### Daly R 2026

Daly R, Tunney E, Spooner M, Offiah G, Flood K, Kent F. (2026). Case‐based learning (CBL) in undergraduate health professions education: A realist review. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.70179

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's assessment programme theory, supported by Carrasco (2018, 2021) and Park (2020), reports that subjective behavioural scores from CBL did not correlate with knowledge assessment performance; Park found students considered it 'better to passively allow the dysfunction of others to continue than to raise alarms'.

> "Scores on subjective assessment of student behavioural outcomes from CBL such as preparation and participation were not found to correlate to student performance on assessment of knowledge."
