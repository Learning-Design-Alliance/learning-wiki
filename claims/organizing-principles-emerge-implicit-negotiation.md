# Organizing principles for a shared workspace emerge from implicit negotiation of conventions among group members

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Groups of students arrive at shared principles for organizing contributions in a shared drawing space through implicit negotiation, without oral communication. [→ Overdijk 2008](#overdijk-2008)

## Evidence

### Overdijk 2008

Overdijk, M. & van Diggelen, W. (2008). Appropriation of a shared workspace: Organizing principles and their application. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-008-9038-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case study of seven groups of three fourth-grade secondary students using a graphical shared workspace for a role-play argumentative discussion over two lessons, analyzed through episodes of critical events reconstructed from the tool's replay function. The study found that "certain principles for organization emerge from an implicit negotiation of conventions."

> "The results of our study illustrate how certain principles for organization emerge from an implicit negotiation of conventions, with a mutual influence between the students and the tool."
