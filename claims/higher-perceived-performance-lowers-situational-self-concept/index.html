Evidence¶

Lisa Hasenbein 2024¶

Lisa Hasenbein, Ulrich Trautwein, Jens-Uwe Hahn, Stephan Soller, Richard Göllner. (2024). An experimental test of the Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effect using an immersive virtual reality classroom. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09646-4

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Regression analyses in the IVR experiment related perceptions to self-concepts; the printed standardized coefficients are β = −0.19 (situational, significant) and β = −0.03 (dispositional, p = 0.426); the registered-count variable was also nonsignificant (situational β = −0.05, p = 0.122).

"The perceived performance level was significantly negatively related to situational self-concept (β = −0.19, SE = 0.05, p < 0.001), showing that students who perceived the class' performance level as higher reported lower levels of situational self-concept"
