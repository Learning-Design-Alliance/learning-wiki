Case example of Fresno Unified's 2017 placement of an entire residency cohort of 25 residents at Wishon Elementary, with on-site co-taught preparation courses. The report states achievement scores "improved in English language arts (ELA) by 32 points and mathematics by 28 points."

"Within 1 year with 25 residents at the school site, school achievement scores improved in English language arts (ELA) by 32 points and mathematics by 28 points."
