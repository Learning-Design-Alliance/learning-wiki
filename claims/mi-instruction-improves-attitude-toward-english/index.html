Evidence¶

Habib Soleimani 2012¶

Habib Soleimani, Ahmad Moinnzadeh, Zohreh Kassaian & Saeed Ketabi. (2012). The Effect of Instruction Based on Multiple Intelligences Theory on the Attitude and Learning of General English. English Language Teaching, 5(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n9p45

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA on the researcher-made 15-item Likert attitude scale for the experimental (n=32) and control (n=29) groups; F = 13.41 (df 1 and 58) exceeded 8.34, indicating "Multiple Intelligence-based instruction has a meaningful effect on the attitudes of students towards learning English". Descriptive means rose from 62 to 66.43 in the experimental group versus 52.03 to 52.93 in the control.

"It is seen that the F calculated (13.41) with a df of 1 and 58 is larger than 8.34. Therefore, again at the .01 level of significance it can be claimed that Multiple Intelligence-based instruction has a meaningful effect on the attitudes of students towards learning English"
