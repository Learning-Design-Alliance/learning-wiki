### Gamble Blakey Althea 2019

Gamble Blakey Althea, Smith-Han Kelby, Anderson Lynley, Collins Emma, Berryman Elizabeth, Wilkinson Tim. (2019). “They cared about us students:” learning from exemplar clinical teaching environments. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1551-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Paper-based four-question exemplar survey administered to all 80 fifth-year medical students at one tertiary site; response rate 66% (54/80). Students voted departments best and worst for supportive learning; the top exemplar received "48% of all votes (26/54)". No effect size is printed.

> "Two clinical areas (ECA 1 & 2) emerged clearly as exemplars, one with 48% of all votes (26/54), out of 22 departments, and a corresponding near-absence (one vote) for this department in terms of unsupportive practices."
