## Evidence

### Jindal AA 2024

Jindal AA, Agnew T, Siriwardena D, Nikita E, Nolan W. (2024). An education-based intervention investigating the accuracy of community-based optometrists evaluating limbal anterior chamber depth. Eye. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41433-024-03492-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same pre/post vignette study; grades were converted from the 7-point percentages to traditional van Herick grades 1–4 and weighted kappa computed against the expert panel. The article reports the 4-point kappa was "higher at 0.64" post-intervention; no significance test for the scale contrast is printed.

> "For the 4-point grading scale, the mean weighted kappa for assessment 1 was 0.61 (Substantial), 95% CI 0.56–0.66 (moderate-substantial) and for assessment 2 it was higher at 0.64 (Substantial), 95% CI 0.58–0.69 (moderate-substantial)."
