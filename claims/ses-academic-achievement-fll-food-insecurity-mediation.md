## Evidence

### Doustmohammadian A 2022

Doustmohammadian A, Omidvar N, Keshavarz-Mohammadi N, Eini-Zinab H, Amini M, Abdollahi M. (2022). The association and mediation role of Food and Nutrition Literacy (FNLIT) with eating behaviors, academic achievement and overweight in 10–12 years old students: a structural equation modeling. Nutrition Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12937-022-00796-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM Model 2 (Fig. 5) on the 803-student Tehran sample links FNLIT subscales to determinants and outcomes; the partial mediation of SES on achievement via Food Label Literacy is reported with printed coefficients.

> "SES predicted academic achievement partially through the mediating effect of “Food Label Literacy” (of SES on “Food Label Literacy”, β = 0.19, P < 0.01; of “Food Label Literacy” on academic achievement, β = 0.11, P < 0.05)."

### Doustmohammadian A 2022 (2)

Doustmohammadian A, Omidvar N, Keshavarz-Mohammadi N, Eini-Zinab H, Amini M, Abdollahi M. (2022). The association and mediation role of Food and Nutrition Literacy (FNLIT) with eating behaviors, academic achievement and overweight in 10–12 years old students: a structural equation modeling. Nutrition Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12937-022-00796-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same SEM Model 2 results section: the indirect SES-to-achievement pathway through food insecurity is negative, alongside the direct positive SES effect, with all coefficients printed in the quote.

> "The results indicated, despite the direct relationship between SES and academic achievement (β = 0.25, P < 0.001), an indirect but negative relationship existed through food insecurity (of SES on food insecurity, β = − 0.42, P < 0.001; of food insecurity on academic achievement, β = − 0.10, P < 0.05)."
