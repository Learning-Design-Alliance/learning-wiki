Evidence¶

Bodke Parag 2026¶

Bodke Parag, Jumle Rahul. (2026). Large language models in undergraduate medical education: a systematic review of examination performance, knowledge acquisition, and learner perceptions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09999-7

q3 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis of 9 USMLE benchmark evaluation studies within the review's Results. The review reports "GPT-4 achieved a mean accuracy of 86%" versus GPT-3.5 at 56%; no pooled effect size was computed due to heterogeneity.

"Across 9 USMLE benchmark studies, GPT-4 achieved a mean accuracy of 86% (IQR: 81%–89%), substantially above the historically defined passing standard of approximately 60%. GPT-3.5 mean accuracy was 56% (IQR: 52%–62%)"
