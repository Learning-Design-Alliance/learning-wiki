### Schnaubert 2019

Schnaubert, L., & Bodemer, D. (2019). Providing different types of group awareness information to guide collaborative learning. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 14, 7–51. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9293-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Reported learning-outcome result of the experimental dyad study on blood-sugar regulation and diabetes mellitus. Despite gains, group awareness information "did not have the expected impact on learning outcomes"; no effect size is printed, so no magnitude is reported here.

> "However, while the learners gained knowledge and confidence during collaboration, providing group awareness information did not have the expected impact on learning outcomes."
