Means 2021¶

Means, B., Peters, V., Neisler, J., Wiley, K., & Grifths, R. (2021). Lessons from remote learning during COVID-19. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/116

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same national student survey (n = 1,008, weighted sample 23% Hispanic, 15% Black, 37% under $50,000 income). The survey found minoritized students more likely to experience connectivity problems "often or very often (22%)" versus 12% of non-minoritized students; hardware/software problems showed the same pattern (12% vs. 5%).
