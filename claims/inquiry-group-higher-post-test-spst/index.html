The Impact of Inquiry Based Instruction on Science Process Skills and Self-efficacy Perceptions of Pre-service Science Teachers at a University Level Biology Laboratory. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(3): 603-612. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040319

Mann-Whitney U test comparing post-test SPST scores of the experimental group (mean rank 24.96) and control group (mean rank 12.04), each n=12, after the 14-week intervention; groups were equivalent at pretest (U=60.50, p=.503).

"This result indicates that there is a significant difference between the SPST post-test scores of the experimental group and the control group in favor of the experimental group (U=66.50, p=.020< .05)."
