## Evidence

### Serrada-Tejeda S 2026

Serrada-Tejeda S, Obeso-Benítez P, Hernández-Hernández L, Martínez-Cuervo F, Pérez-de-Heredia-Torres M, Sánchez-Herrera-Baeza P, Yusta-Ceacero A, Martínez-Piédrola RM. (2026). Effects of a brief aging simulation suit intervention on clinical empathy in healthcare professionals working in long-term care: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-026-07564-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with 82 healthcare professionals from four nursing homes; two-factor repeated-measures ANOVA of JSPE-HPS total scores. The interaction was significant, F(1, 80) = 13.54; p < .001, with effect size ηp² = 0.145; the EG increased 7.49 points and the CG -0.88.

> "However, the interaction between group and time was also statistically significant, showing that the group influenced the progression of scores. Participants in the CG showed no significant change in their scores from pre-to post-intervention, whereas those in the EG demonstrated a significant increase."
