## Evidence

### Urrila 2017

Urrila, A. S. et al. (2017). Sleep habits, academic performance, and the adolescent brain structure. Scientific Reports 7, 41678. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep41678

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Causal mediation analyses (Imai et al. algorithms, 5000 bootstraps) with school grade average as dependent factor and GMV as independent factor tested weekend bedtime as mediator: "Weekend bedtime accounted for 43.8% (p < 0.001) of the total effect" in one cluster and 33.7% (p = 0.03) in the other.

> "Weekend bedtime accounted for 43.8% (p < 0.001) of the total effect between GMV in the frontal superior medial cortex and ACC, and school performance. Also, it accounted for 33.7% (p = 0.03) of the total effect between GMV in the precuneus and paracentral lobule, and school performance."
