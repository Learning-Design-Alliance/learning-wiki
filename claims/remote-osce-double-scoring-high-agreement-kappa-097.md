## Evidence

### Fu Zhihui 2025

Fu Zhihui, Wu Yuhong, Xu Lingling, Cai Fen, Liu Ren, Jiang Zhehan. (2025). Optimizing cost-effectiveness in remote objective structured clinical examinations through targeted double scoring methodologies. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2467477

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of scoring data from a pilot remote OSCE administered to 550 clinical medicine undergraduates in China, with each performance rated remotely by two assessors. The analysis reports a "Pearson correlation of 0.9" between raters' total scores and Cronbach's alpha of 0.66 across all raw scores.

> "Overall, the total scores rated by Rater 1 and Rater 2 form a Pearson correlation of 0.9 showing high total-score-level consistency, while computed via all raw scores, Cronbach's ɑ reaches 0.66, an acceptable value for performance assessment."
