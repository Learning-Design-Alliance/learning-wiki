This is an author position statement in the misconceptions brief, offered with no evidence or empirical support. The other four myths addressed are that AI thinks like humans, creates original ideas, will replace teachers, and is always accurate.

"Poorly used AI lowers rigor. Well-designed AI use can support rigor by requiring students to analyze, revise, justify, and reflect."
