`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The report's review of state waiver plans reports that "nearly half the states receiving waivers chose some version of the 'cut the gap in half' achievement goal," a framework the Education Trust developed from multi-state data analysis; the goal requires faster improvement from groups starting farthest behind.

> "Nearly half the states receiving waivers chose some version of the "cut the gap in half" achievement goal. The idea for this goal originated at The Education Trust after extensive analysis of data from multiple states to identify rates of improvement and gap closing that meet the "ambitious but achievable" test."
