## Evidence

### Kiver Benjamin 2025

Kiver Benjamin, Berberat Pascal O., Gartmeier Martin. (2025). The relation between teaching-related self-efficacy and general job-related well-being – a cross-sectional study among young physicians. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001777

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation in the cross-sectional survey of 293 physicians, with teaching experience recorded in four categories from up to one year to over 10 years; teaching experience "showed a significant correlation" with teaching-related self-efficacy (ρ=0.186; p=0.002; N=281).

> "As postulated in Hypothesis 4, teaching experience showed a significant correlation (ρ=0.186; p=0.002; N=281) with the teaching-related self-efficacy of physicians"

### Kiver Benjamin 2025 (2)

Kiver Benjamin, Berberat Pascal O., Gartmeier Martin. (2025). The relation between teaching-related self-efficacy and general job-related well-being – a cross-sectional study among young physicians. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001777

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of variance across the four teaching-experience categories in the same survey produced F(3, 277)=4.003, p=0.008; a "post-hoc Tukey HSD test" found a significant difference between the up-to-one-year group (M=2.81, SD=0.34) and the 5-to-10-year group (M=2.98, SD=0.34).

> "An analysis of variance of the individual response categories of teaching experience and teaching-related self-efficacy as the dependent variable produced a significant result (F(3, 277)=4.003, p=0.008)"
