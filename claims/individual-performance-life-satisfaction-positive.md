## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Higher individual performance (self-reported GPA in Mathematics and German) is associated with higher life satisfaction among seventh graders. [→ Rathmann Katharina 2018](#rathmann-katharina-2018)

## Evidence

### Rathmann Katharina 2018

Rathmann Katharina, Herke Max, Bilz Ludwig, Rimpelä Arja, Hurrelmann Klaus, Richter Matthias. (2018). Class-Level School Performance and Life Satisfaction: Differential Sensitivity for Low- and High-Performing School-Aged Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15122750

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel Model 2 of the NEPS SC3 sample (n = 5196 students) added z-standardized individual performance; the printed coefficient was 0.39 (SE 0.03, p < 0.001). The article reports that "lower performance was related to lower life satisfaction".

> "Adjusting for control variables, Model 2 considers students' individual performance, being positively associated with life satisfaction, meaning that lower performance was related to lower life satisfaction."
