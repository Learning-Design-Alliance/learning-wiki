## Evidence

### Gifford 2026

Gifford, K., Ehrlich, G. S., Bumbacher, E., & Kuo, E. (2026). “I forgot the formula:” How students can use coherence to reconstruct a (partially) forgotten equation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/yfxm-m4sw](https://doi.org/10.1103/yfxm-m4sw)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focal cases from the interviews: Sam, a community college student, used an "electrons as people" analogy to reason about current flow and discharge rates; Riley chained "greater resistance→decreased current→greater charge." The article notes Riley's solution contained "incorrect aspects"—the electron-jumping model led Riley to an incorrect initial conclusion about circuit D—and that Sam did not compare circuit C's slower discharge against circuit D's greater initial charge.

> "Sam’s switch to qualitative reasoning led to the correct determination of Circuit B and Circuit D having the least and most amount of charge remaining, respectively."
