## Evidence

### Dirzyte Aiste 2021

Dirzyte Aiste, Vijaikis Aivaras, Perminas Aidas, Rimasiute-Knabikiene Romualda, Tchounwou Paul B. (2021). Associations between Depression, Anxiety, Fatigue, and Learning Motivating Factors in e-Learning-Based Computer Programming Education. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18179158

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across five PHQ-9 depression categories in a sample of 444 e-learners. Challenging goals scores were "significantly higher in the minimal depression group (4.833, ±0.808) in comparison to all groups". No effect size was printed.

> "An analysis of variance revealed that the effect of depression on challenging goals was significant, F (4, 387) = 14.261, p < 0.001."
