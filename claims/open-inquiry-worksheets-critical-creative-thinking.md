## Evidence

### Muskita 2020

Muskita, M., Subali, B., & Djukri. (2020). Effects of Worksheets Base the Levels of Inquiry in Improving Critical and Creative Thinking. International Journal of Instruction, 13(2), 519-532. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13236a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment comparing open inquiry and conventional classes; post-test Mann-Whitney Asymp. Sig. was .000 for critical and creative aspects (pre-test .582 and .70). Experimental post-test means were 79 (critical) and 82 (creative) versus 56 and 54 in the control class.

> "Thus it shows that the average critical and creative thinking ability of students from the posttest results by using worksheets based on open inquiry and conventional learning there are differences and are higher than those using conventional learning."
