## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Cross-tabulation of physical activity level against high/average/low GPA categories showed no statistically significant difference (Chi-square 0.0666, p = 0.97), and stratified gender comparisons were also non-significant. [→ Raja Saud Mohammed 2025](#raja-saud-mohammed-2025)

## Evidence

### Raja Saud Mohammed 2025

Raja Saud Mohammed, Elfaki Mohammed Ahmed, Ibrahim Alaa, Maroof Hibaallah, Abdelrahim Abrar. (2025). The relationship between physical activity and academic achievement in medical students: a single-institution cross-sectional study in Sudan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07985-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analysis of GPA categories (high ≥ 3.5, average 2.75–3.49, low < 2.75) against physical activity level in the same 295-student cross-sectional study. The article prints "Chi-square 0.0666, p = 0.97" and no effect size.

> "Cross-tabulation between the levels of physical activity and categories of GPA did not show statistically significant difference in the academic achievement between physically active and inactive students (Chi-square 0.0666, p = 0.97) even when stratified by gender (p value 0.29 versus 0.31 in males and females, respectively)."
