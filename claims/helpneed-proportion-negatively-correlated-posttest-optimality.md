# A higher proportion of HelpNeed steps during training is significantly negatively correlated with posttest optimality, most strongly for the global-absolute efficiency metric

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Training HelpNeed proportion correlates negatively with posttest optimality for all four efficiency metrics (all p < 0.01), with the global-absolute metric most correlated (r = -0.36). [→ Maniktala 2020](#maniktala-2020)

## Evidence

### Maniktala 2020

Maniktala, M., Cody, C., Isvik, A., Lytle, N., Chi, M., & Barnes, T. (2020). Extending the Hint Factory for the Assistance Dilemma: A Novel, Data-driven Help-Need Predictor for Proactive Problem-solving Help. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 12, No 4. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/450

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis on datasets f18 and s19 (N = 437), chosen to control for a problem-ordering change in Fall 2018. Pearson correlations between each metric's training HelpNeed proportion and posttest optimality were all significant at p < 0.01; Table 3 prints r = -0.36 for Global-Absolute, the most correlated metric.

> "Table 3 shows that training HelpNeed is signiﬁcantly and negatively correlated to posttest optimality for all four of the eﬃciency metrics. A negative correlation suggests that the higher the proportion of HelpNeed steps in training, the worse the posttest optimality."
