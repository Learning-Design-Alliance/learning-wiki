Evidence¶

Pınar 2020¶

Pınar, E. & Yıldız, A. (2020). The Effect of Journal Writing, One of the “Writing to Learn” Activities, on Achievement and Science Attitude in the Fourth Grade Science Course. Journal of Current Researches on Social Sciences, 10(1), 179-196. https://doi.org/10.26579/jocress.354

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-test on SCAT administered as a retention test three weeks after the post-test to the same 24 experimental and 20 control students; the article reports a 10.8-point mean difference that is not statistically significant (t42=1.942; p>.05).

"it is revealed that there is difference (10.8) between the mean scores of the experimental (77.86) and control groups (67.06); however, this difference is not statistically significant (t42=1.942; p>.05)."
