## Evidence

### Liu L  2020

Liu L , Zhang J , Peng Y , et al. (2020). Learning curve for transcatheter aortic valve replacement for native aortic regurgitation: Safety and technical performance study. Clinical Cardiology. 2020;43:475–482. https://doi.org/10.1002/clc.23332

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective comparison of prospectively collected intraoperative measures between the early-experience group (first 52 cases) and late-experience group (next 82 cases); contrast dye was also lower in group 2 (84.96 ± 17.76 vs 59.95 ± 12.83 mL, P<.05). Only group means and P values are printed; no effect size is reported.

> "The mean total surgical time was 94.22 ± 30.07 in group 1 vs 70.43 ± 15.02 minutes in group 2 (P < .05), and the mean fluoroscopy time was 11.52 ± 3.81 in group 1 vs 6.47 ± 1.53 minutes in group 2 (P < .05)."
