## Evidence

### Wang Siqi 2025

Wang Siqi, Yang Di, Zhao Wenbo, Yan Xiaoyuan, Zhang Xiyue, Gao Wenqing, Guo Jiajie. (2025). The combination of a SPOC with evaluative interactive learning in dental adhesion education: a comparison with a lecture-based learning mode. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07261-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Operational assessment (6 points, six items such as selection of bonding methods and acid etching) administered two weeks after class to six randomly selected students per group. Table 4 reports EIL "5.67 ± 0.52" vs LBL 3.33 ± 2.16, t = 2.57, P = 0.045.

> "Six students in each group were randomly selected for the task of skill operation, and those in the EIL group obtained significantly higher scores than did those in the LBL group (Table 4; Fig. 7A)."
