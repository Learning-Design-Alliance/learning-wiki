Evidence¶

Lasha Labadze 2023¶

Lasha Labadze, Maya Grigolia and Lela Machaidze. (2023). Role of AI chatbots in education: systematic literature review. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00426-1

q2 · i? · review · r3

Systematic literature review of 67 studies selected from an initial 563 papers across four databases. The review reports that students primarily gain in "homework and study assistance, a personalized learning experience, and the development of various skills."

"We found that students primarily gain from AI-powered chatbots in three key areas: homework and study assistance, a personalized learning experience, and the development of various skills."

Related Claims¶

  • AI chatbots provide students detailed feedback, step-by-step homework solutions, and self-quizzing support — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • AI chatbots enable flexible personalized learning by adapting teaching strategies to individual student needs — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Purpose-built chatbot effects on educational performance are mixed, with some studies showing no benefits or worse outcomes than comparison conditions — reports the opposite
  • One RCT found AI-assisted simulation inferior to standardized patient simulation for breast self-examination skills — reports the opposite
  • Faculty testing of AI chatbots on real student homework found feedback quality uneven, prompting-dependent, and focused on surface features — reports the opposite
  • AI chatbots support skills development in writing, problem-solving, and group discussion facilitation — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Usability testing with educators and developers identified chatbot strengths for independent challenge-solving but limited feedback for low-participation learners — related
