## Evidence

### Weidinger 2019

Weidinger, A. F., Steinmayr, R., & Spinath, B. (2019). Ability Self-Concept Formation in Elementary School: No Dimensional Comparison Effects Across Time. Developmental Psychology, 55(5), 1005–1018. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0000695

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Original I/E models (not controlling for prior self-concepts) showed asymmetric dimensional effects: math grades negatively predicted German self-concepts (β = −.30 to −.15, p < .05), while German grades had no significant negative effects on math self-concepts.

> "we found a significant negative effect of students’ math grade at t3, t4 and t5, respectively, on students’ self-concept in German assessed 4 months later (i.e., at t4, t5 and t6, respectively). The effects were small to moderate (⫺.30 ⱕ ␤ ⱕ ⫺.15, .07 ⱕ SE ⱕ .08, p ⬍ .05)"
