### Bisogno Arianna; Leone Ludovica; D'Oria Veronica; Agostoni Carlo; Abodi Martina 2026

Bisogno Arianna; Leone Ludovica; D'Oria Veronica; Agostoni Carlo; Abodi Martina. (2026). Learning About Healthy Nutrition by Doing: Experiential Approaches in School-Based Nutrition Education. Nutrients 18(10):1610. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu18101610

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cluster-RCT by Viggiano et al. including more than 3000 students aged 9-19 years, per the review's Table 1/2 summary. Table 2 reports BMI z-score 0.44 vs. 0.58 at 6 months (p = 0.001) and 0.34 vs. 0.58 at 18 months (p = 0.017); knowledge 6.5 vs. 4.6 (p < 0.001). The review reports the conclusion that Kaledo "had a sustained effect on the BMI z-score". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Kaledo improved nutrition knowledge and dietary behavior over 6 months and had a sustained effect on the BMI z-score at 6 and 18 months after the game."
