### Heijne-Penninga M. 2012

Heijne-Penninga M., Kuks J. B. M., Hofman W. H. A., Muijtjens A. M. M., Cohen-Schotanus J. (2012). Influence of PBL with open-book tests on knowledge retention measured with progress tests. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-012-9386-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired comparison of core versus backup subtest percent-correct scores across four 200-item progress tests in academic year 2008–2009. The article reports "better on core than on backup knowledge (paired t test, p < 0.0001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Students’ performance on the four tests moments was better on core than on backup knowledge (paired t test, p < 0.0001), for each of the 2 year groups at all three medical schools."
