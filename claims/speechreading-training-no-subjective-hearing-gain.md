### Raffael Schmitt 2023

Raffael Schmitt, Martin Meyer and Nathalie Giroud. (2023). Improvements in naturalistic speech-in-noise comprehension in middle-aged and older adults after 3 weeks of computer-based speechreading training. npj Science of Learning 8:32. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00179-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Beta-distribution GLMMs on Listening Effort Questionnaire and SSQ scores from the same 62 participants at pre- and post-training. The session × group interaction "was not signi ﬁcant" for listening effort (ΔΧ2(1) = 2.92, p = 0.087), so no training effect was detected on subjective effort.

> "the potential training effect, depicted by the interaction between session and group, was not signi ﬁcant— neither for the perceived listening effort ( ΔΧ2(1) = 2.92, p = 0.087) (Fig. 6; Table 3) nor the speech, spatial, and hearing qualities ( ΔΧ2(1) = 3.49, p = 0.062)"
