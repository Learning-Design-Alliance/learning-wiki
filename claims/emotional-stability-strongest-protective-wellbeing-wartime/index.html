Zavhorodnia 2026¶

Zavhorodnia, O. V., & Shepelova, M. V. (2026). Big Five Traits and Tolerance for Uncertainty as Protective Factors of Subjective Well-Being of Students in Martial Law Conditions. Europe's Journal of Psychology, 22(2). https://doi.org/10.5964/ejop.17767

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Cross-sectional Pearson correlation analysis of 147 Ukrainian students surveyed in 2024 under martial law. The authors report emotional stability "demonstrated the strongest negative association with a decline in well-being (r = –.49, p < .01)".
