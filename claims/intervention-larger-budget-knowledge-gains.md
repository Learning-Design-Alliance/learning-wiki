## Evidence

### Reichert et al. 2015

Collin Reichert, Cinzia Cervato, Dale Niederhauser, and Michael D. Larsen. (2015). Understanding Atmospheric Carbon Budgets: Teaching Students Conservation of Mass. Journal of Geoscience Education 63, 222–232. https://doi.org/10.5408/14-055.1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 49 self-selected experimental students against 127 lecture-only controls in an introductory physical geology course, with gain computed as posttest minus pretest. The article reports "difference in average gain = 0.41, t(174) = 2.18, p = 0.03"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "In the experimental group, the average gain was 0.94 points (SD 1.15, t(48) = 5.74, p < 0.001). The larger gain in scores for the experimental group relative to the control group was also statistically significant (difference in average gain = 0.41, t(174) = 2.18, p = 0.03)."
