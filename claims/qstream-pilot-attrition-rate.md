### Robinson Tracy 2017

Robinson Tracy, Janssen Anna, Kirk Judy, DeFazio Anna, Goodwin Annabel, Tucker Kathy, Shaw Timothy. (2017). New Approaches to Continuing Medical Education: a QStream (spaced education) Program for Research Translation in Ovarian Cancer. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-015-0944-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Completion data from the pilot: 29 clinicians enrolled, eight did not commence, and 18 of 21 who commenced (86%) completed. Given the program targeted senior clinicians and involved no formal CME points, the completion rate was considered acceptable for this cohort.

> "Although there was a 13 % attrition rate (for those who commenced the course), it is consistent with attrition rates reported by other online education programs"
