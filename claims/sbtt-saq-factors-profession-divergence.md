## Evidence

### Meurling Lisbet 2013

Meurling Lisbet, Hedman Leif, Sandahl Christer, Felländer-Tsai Li, Wallin Carl-Johan. (2013). Systematic simulation-based team training in a Swedish intensive care unit: a diverse response among critical care professions. BMJ quality & safety 22(6):485-494. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjqs-2012-000994

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post matched SAQ (ICU version) analysis of 114 participants across six factors. Table 1 reports safety climate rising from 56.6 (15) to 63.5 (14), p<0.001, mean difference 7.46 (95% CI 4.56 to 10.36); nurse assistants improved on teamwork climate (p=0.008), safety climate (p<0.001) and working conditions (p=0.009), nurses on safety climate (p<0.001), physicians on none.

> "In contrast to the other professions, they improved in scores for teamwork climate, safety climate and working conditions, matched by a reduction in sick leave."
