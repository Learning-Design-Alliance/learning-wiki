Evidence¶

David Menendez 2023¶

David Menendez. (2023). Cues to generality: Integrating linguistic and visual information when generalizing biological information. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000812

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Internal meta-analysis of Studies 2-6 (757 recruited, 508 passing attention checks) using fixed-effects weighted Cohen's d, showing a "large difference between generic and non-generic statements, d = 0.857" with low heterogeneity (I2 = 2.1%).

"Overall, there was a large difference between generic and non-generic statements, d = 0.857 [0.712, 1.002], with low between-study heterogeneity, I2 = 2.1%."

David Menendez 2023 (2)¶

David Menendez. (2023). Cues to generality: Integrating linguistic and visual information when generalizing biological information. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000812

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

The same internal meta-analysis found a "medium-sized difference between exemplified generics and non-generic statements, d = 0.580", and a smaller generic-vs-exemplified difference of d = 0.265, both with low heterogeneity.

"There was a medium-sized difference between exemplified generics and non-generic statements, d = 0.580 [0.446, 0.715], with low between-study heterogeneity, I2 = 21.0%."
