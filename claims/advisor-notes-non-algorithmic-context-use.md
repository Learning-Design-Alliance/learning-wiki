### Schechtman 2025

Schechtman, K., Brandon, B., Stafford, J., Li, H., & Liu, L. T. (2025). Discretion in the Loop: Human Expertise in Algorithm-Assisted College Advising. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3757887.3767689](https://doi.org/10.1145/3757887.3767689)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative coding of the 1,888 free-form advisor comments in the MAAPS meeting logs, used to complement the quantitative ExpertTest* by identifying the particular non-algorithmic information guiding advisor decision making. The abstract likewise reports "a pattern of advisors incorporating diverse forms of contextual information—such as personal circumstances, financial issues, and student engagement—into their decisions."

> "Qualitative analysis of advisor comments shows that advisors incorporate non-algorithmic context, such as the student's personal circumstances, career goals, or financial troubles, into these interventions."
