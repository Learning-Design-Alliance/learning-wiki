## Evidence

### Beaumont 2022

Beaumont, J., Putwain, D. W., Gallard, D., Malone, E., Marsh, H. W., & Pekrun, R. (2022, in press). Students' emotion regulation and school-related wellbeing: Longitudinal models juxtaposing between- and within-person perspectives. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://osf.io/W5CPE

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Mediation analysis using 95% confidence intervals around indirect-effect point estimates in both three-wave models among 2,365 students; intervals excluding zero indicated significance. The authors interpret these as "positive feedback loops between reappraisal and wellbeing over time". Indirect-effect sizes are not printed in the provided text.

> "For both the CLPM and the RI-CLPM, there were indirect relations between (1) T1 reappraisal and T3 reappraisal mediated by T2 wellbeing, and (2) T1 wellbeing and T3 wellbeing mediated by T2 reappraisal."
