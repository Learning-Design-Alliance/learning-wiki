### Serhatlıoğlu Seda 2026

Serhatlıoğlu Seda. (2026). Student-generated learning materials as active learning: a qualitative study of midwifery students’ experiences. BMC Medical Education 26:1504. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09749-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Self-confidence sub-theme from written reflections of 26 midwifery students, coded in 12 of 26; a related educator-role sub-theme appeared in 10 of 26. The student attributed comfort to having "prepared it myself and knew every step."

> "I usually get very nervous when giving presentations, but during this process, I felt more comfortable explaining my material. Because I had prepared it myself and knew every step. This gave me confidence."
