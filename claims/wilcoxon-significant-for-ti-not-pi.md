## Evidence

### Boon Leong Lan 2022

Boon Leong Lan, Pooi Mee Lim and Patrick W. C. Ho. (2022). A modified peer instruction protocol: peer versus teacher's instruction. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2201.10804](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2201.10804)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired samples Wilcoxon test (used instead of the t-test because neither Q1 nor Q2 marks were normally distributed) on paired Q1/Q2 marks: TI 121 students, PI 137 students. The article reports "TI elicited a statistically significant change ... in the student's paired marks (from Q1 to Q2), but PI did not".

> "The test shows that the median of the differences between the paired marks is statistically significantly different from zero (p value = 0.0015) for TI (Semester A & B), but not statistically significantly different from zero (p value = 0.12) for PI (Semester C & D & E)."
