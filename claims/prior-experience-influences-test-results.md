BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05608-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of questionnaire baseline variables against theory test results (Supplement 4) in the prospective observational study (n = 141). Only p-values are printed; no effect sizes for these correlations.

> "At T1, previous practical ultrasound experience ("yes") had a significant correlation (p < 0.05) with the overall test result. "Dealing with other radiological topics" correlated to a significantly higher (p < 0.05) overall test result at T2."
