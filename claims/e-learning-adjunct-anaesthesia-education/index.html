Blackwood Lachlan 2025¶

Blackwood Lachlan, Hollo Zachary, Amaratunge Lahiru. (2025). Electronic learning methods compared to traditional learning methods in anaesthesia education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07502-2

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's concluding synthesis states that e-learning is an important adjunct to traditional learning methods and that, in the appropriate clinical teaching context, e-learning program outcomes can outperform those from traditional learning methodologies.

"E-learning is an important adjunct to traditional learning methods and when undertaken in the appropriate clinical teaching context, outcomes from e-learning programs can outperform those from traditional learning methodologies."
