## Evidence

### Boal Matthew W. E. 2024

Boal Matthew W. E., Afzal Asma, Gorard Jack, Shah Aishwarya, Tesfai Freweini, Ghamrawi Walaa, Tutton Matthew, Ahmad Jawad, Selvasekar Chelliah, Khan Jim, Francis Nader K. (2024). Development and evaluation of a societal core robotic surgery accreditation curriculum for the UK. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-02062-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired comparisons of simulator-recorded scores on da Vinci SimNow across three VR assessment domains. VR-automated scores improved by "a mean difference of 98.25" and VR M-GEARS by 24.10, both p < 0.001; VR OCHRA errors fell by -69.67 (p < 0.001). No effect sizes printed.

> "VR-automated scores (n = 35) demonstrated a mean difference of 98.25 (SD 68.75, 95% CI 74.64–121.87, p < 0.001). Virtual reality M-GEARS scores (n = 35) mean difference was 24.10 (SD 11.45, 95% CI 20.17–28.03, p < 0.001)"
