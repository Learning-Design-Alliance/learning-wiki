Evidence¶

Montero-Marin et al 2021¶

Montero-Marin et al. (2021). Teachers “Finding Peace in a Frantic World”: An Experimental Study of Self-Taught and Instructor-Led Mindfulness Program Formats on Acceptability, Effectiveness, and Mechanisms. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000542

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Cluster-randomized trial of 206 teachers from 43 schools randomized by school to instructor-led or self-taught mindfulness training, analyzed with linear mixed-effects models. The article reports that changes over time significantly differed between the groups in all these outcomes, "favoring the instructor-led arm." No effect size for this omnibus statement is printed in the supplied text.

Related Claims¶

  • The instructor-led mindfulness arm, compared with the self-taught arm, indirectly improved teacher outcomes by enhancing mindfulness and self-compassion as mediating factors — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The instructor-led mindfulness arm showed significant pre-post improvements in mindfulness, self-compassion, well-being, perceived stress, anxiety, depression, and burnout, while the self-taught arm showed significant pre-post improvements in self-compassion and well-being — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Self-taught mindfulness training may be a useful option for teachers who cannot access or commit to an instructor-led program, provided they engage with mindfulness practice — related
  • Both mindfulness training formats showed similar participant expectancy and engagement, but teachers perceived the instructor-led arm as more credible — related
