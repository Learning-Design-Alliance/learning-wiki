## Evidence

### Wang Cixiao 2020

Wang Cixiao, Xie A’na, Wang Weimin, Wu Hongbin. (2020). Association between medical students’ prior experiences and perceptions of formal online education developed in response to COVID-19: a cross-sectional study in China. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-041886

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction-term regression on the 99,559-student survey; the article reports that no other interactions (with gender, area or academic performance) were significantly associated with evaluation or satisfaction.

> "The association of PU with the evaluations of students in clinical medical education (β=−0.07; 95% CI −0.09 to 0.05; p<0.01) and clerkship rotation (β=−0.10; 95% CI −0.12 to 0.08; p<0.01) were significantly lower than that the effect of PU on general education."
