# Christ and Davie (2009) found positive, statistically significant effects of Read Naturally® Software Edition on three reading fluency measures

## Subclaims

### Read Naturally® Updated July 2013 — WWC Intervention Report 2013

Read Naturally® Updated July 2013 — WWC Intervention Report. (2013). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/407

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial (Christ & Davie, 2009) in which 106 third-grade students at or below the 40th percentile in oral reading fluency and comprehension were randomly assigned within classrooms. Table A2 prints a reading fluency improvement index of +14, statistically significant; the WWC confirmed the positive significant differences on the three fluency measures.
