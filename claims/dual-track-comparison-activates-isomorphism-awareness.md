# Dual-track comparison of manual estimation with AI prediction activated students' isomorphism awareness in a Grade 5 lesson

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After comparing their manual trend estimations with the AI's prediction (72.3°C predicted vs 72°C actual), students verbalized that AI reasons like them, marking a shift from 'I am estimating' to 'AI is also estimating'. [→ Li Li 2025](#li-li-2025)

### Li Li 2025

Li Li, Yu Cao. (2025). Awareness of Technological Isomorphism: Integrating AI into Elementary Mathematics Teaching on Data and Prediction, A Case Study of the Compound Line Graph. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09598

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative discourse analysis of Segment 2 (AI Prediction Comparison, 8 minutes) of the compound line graph case lesson, in which a demonstration program overlaid students' manually plotted values with the AI's curve based on the first four data points. The authors interpret verbalizations such as "So it's kind of doing what I did" as observable indicators of emerging awareness.

> "When S4 stated that "it is also looking for patterns hidden within the data, " a cognitive shift occurred from "I am estimating" to "AI is also estimating.""
