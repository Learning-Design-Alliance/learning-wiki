### What Works Clearinghouse 2015

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2015). Head Start: WWC intervention report. http://www.acf.hhs.gov/programs/ohs

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

These are supplemental findings from the DHHS ACF (2010) randomized controlled trial presented in Appendix D, including social-emotional subscale comparisons and comparisons by mothers' race/ethnicity (one composite measure and one subscale measure). They do not factor into the intervention's ratings of effectiveness.

> "the authors reported, and the WWC confirmed, a statistically significant and positive effect of Head Start on one social-emotional subscale for the 3-year-old cohort at the end of the intervention year."
