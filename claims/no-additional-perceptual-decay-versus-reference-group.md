Experiment 2B compared a Switch group (N=10) that walked overground blocks 2–4 with a reference group (N=10) taking matched sitting breaks. Between-group differences in additional perceptual treadmill decay were "not different from zero", e.g. −0.05% [−22.72, 24.09]% for treadmill 2-1.
