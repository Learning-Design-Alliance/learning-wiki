Hertzog C, Dunlosky J. (2011). Metacognition in Later Adulthood: Spared Monitoring Can Benefit Older Adults' Self-regulation. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3903298/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a study (Cavallini et al., 2010) in which older adults trained in imagery and sentence generation, some receiving transfer instructions discussing application to a grocery-list task; "The transfer instructions resulted in reliable transfer to text learning." The authors note a recent unpublished replication.

> "The transfer instructions resulted in reliable transfer to text learning – a task that they had never practiced nor had been discussed during training."
