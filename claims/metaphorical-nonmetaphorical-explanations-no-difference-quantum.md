### Meinsma 2026

Meinsma, A. L., Reijnierse, W. G., & Cramer, J. (2026). Like a coin spinning in the air: the effect of (non-)metaphorical explanations on comprehension and attitudes towards quantum technology. PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1075/msw.25018.mei](https://doi.org/10.1075/msw.25018.mei)

### Meinsma 2026 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Pairwise comparison for actual comprehension in the same experiment: the difference between explanation types "was not significant" (mean difference = 0.13, p = 0.069); the article treats this as no additional benefit of metaphors.

> "The difference between the non-metaphorical group and the metaphorical group was not significant (mean difference = 0.13, SE = 0.06, p = 0.069)."
