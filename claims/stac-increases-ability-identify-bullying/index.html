Evidence¶

Midgett 2016¶

Midgett, A., Doumas, D., Trull, R., & Johnston, A. (2016). A Randomized Controlled Study Evaluating a Brief, Bystander Bullying Intervention with Junior High School Students. Boise State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1146195

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Chi-square analysis at 30-day follow-up in the randomized controlled study, using a single Yes/No item ("Have you seen bullying at school in the past month?"). The article reports a printed effect size of φ = .32, which the authors call a medium effect size.

"results of the chi square analysis indicated a significant difference between the intervention and control group in the ability to identify bullying at the 30-day follow-up, χ2(1) = 5.05, p < .05, φ = .32. Examination of the φ coefficient indicates the effect size was medium. A significantly higher percentage of students in the intervention group indicated they had seen bullying in the past month (70%) relative to students in the control group (39%)."
