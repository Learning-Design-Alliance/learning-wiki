# Mobile application supplement improves global ultrasound OSCE scores in physiotherapy students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group with mobile app access scored significantly higher on global ultrasound OSCE than the control group (p < .001). [→ Fernández-Lao Carolina 2016](#fernandez-lao-carolina-2016)

## Evidence

### Fernández-Lao Carolina 2016

Fernández-Lao Carolina, Cantarero-Villanueva Irene, Galiano-Castillo Noelia, Caro-Morán Elena, Díaz-Rodríguez Lourdes, Arroyo-Morales Manuel. (2016). The effectiveness of a mobile application for the development of palpation and ultrasound imaging skills to supplement the traditional learning of physiotherapy students. BMC Medical Education 16:274. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0775-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Single-blinded randomized controlled study of 49 physiotherapy students; the m-learning group (N = 25) scored 12.000 ± 2.572 versus 9.000 ± 2.943 in the control group (N = 24) on global ultrasound OSCE, a "significantly higher" result.

> "The global scores for the OSCE components were significantly higher in the experimental group than in the control group for both ultrasound (p < .001) and palpation skills (p = .034)."
