## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Qualitative focus groups identified varied patterns of PACK use: openly in consultation, covertly for fear of appearing incompetent, as a peer-learning basis in shared consulting rooms, and after consultations to check decisions. [→ Mash R 2017](#mash-r-2017)

## Evidence

### Mash R 2017

Mash R, Pather M, Rhode H, Fairall L. (2017). Evaluating the effect of the Practical Approach to Care Kit on teaching medical students primary care: Quasi-experimental study. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v9i1.1602

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Framework-method analysis (Atlas-ti) of focus group interviews conducted at the end of each intervention rotation. Students reported using PACK during consultations, covertly, with peers, and as post-consultation checking; one student "used mine when I did my patient write-ups".

> "Some students found it more useful to refer to the guide after the consultation was finished to check whether they had done the right thing or had been sufficiently comprehensive."
