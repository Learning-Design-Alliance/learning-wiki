## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Cronbach's alpha for the SEPCQ-27 total and subscales ranged from 0.74 to 0.95 across student and physician samples, indicating acceptable internal consistency. [→ Zachariae R 2015](#zachariae-r-2015)

## Evidence

### Zachariae R 2015

Zachariae R, O'Connor M, Lassesen B, Olesen M, Kjær LB, Thygesen M, Mørcke AM. (2015). The self-efficacy in patient-centeredness questionnaire – a new measure of medical student and physician confidence in exhibiting patient-centered behaviors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0427-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Psychometric evaluation in Sample 2A (N = 448 students) with descriptive reliability analysis. The article reports alphas "ranged between 0.83 and 0.94" for this sample; Table 3 shows a range of 0.74–0.95 across samples including physicians (0.84–0.95).

> "Internal consistencies (Cronbach's Alpha) ranged between 0.83 and 0.94 (see Table 3), correlations between subscales ranged between 0.50 and 0.60, and subscale-to-total scale correlations ranged between 0.78 – 0.86"
