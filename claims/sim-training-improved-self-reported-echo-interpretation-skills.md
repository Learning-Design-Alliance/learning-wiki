## Evidence

### Maatouk Iyed 2024

Maatouk Iyed, Maatouk Amani, Gafsi Besma, Mili Emna, Chaouch Mohamed Ali, Toumi Samir, Ouanes Islem, Ben Salem Faouzi, Ouanes Besbes Lamia. (2024). Contribution de l'apprentissage de l'échocardiographie par simulation à la formation des résidents en médecine. La Tunisie Médicale. https://doi.org/10.62438/tunismed.v102i7.4793

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-experimental before-after study of 28 medical residents at the Simulation Center of the Faculty of Medicine of Monastir, evaluated by French-language questionnaire before and after training. Self-reported interpretation skills 'significantly increased before and after training (p< 0.05)'.

> "The percentage of participants reporting having skills required in the interpretation of echocardiography significantly increased before and after training (p< 0.05)."

### Maatouk Iyed 2024 (2)

Maatouk Iyed, Maatouk Amani, Gafsi Besma, Mili Emna, Chaouch Mohamed Ali, Toumi Samir, Ouanes Islem, Ben Salem Faouzi, Ouanes Besbes Lamia. (2024). Contribution de l'apprentissage de l'échocardiographie par simulation à la formation des résidents en médecine. La Tunisie Médicale. https://doi.org/10.62438/tunismed.v102i7.4793

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire comparison of clinical-syndrome responses before and after training (Table 2). Correct responses for tamponade or acute pulmonary heart rose from '15 (53.6)' to '27 (96.4)' with p=0.001; several other syndrome items also improved significantly.

> "Tamponade or acute pulmonary heart (massive pulmonary embolism) 13 (46.4) 15 (53.6) 1 (3.6) 27 (96.4) 0.001"
