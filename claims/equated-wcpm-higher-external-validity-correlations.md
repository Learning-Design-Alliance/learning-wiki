## Evidence

### Chen 2020

Chen, J., & Simpson, M. A. (2020). Equating words-correct-per-minute (WCPM) scores across passages of MAP Reading Fluency. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/equating-words-correct-per-minute-wcpm-scores-across-passages-of-map-reading-fluency/

Convergent validity analysis using only students who took both MAP Reading Fluency and MAP Growth Reading in the same term (Table 3.4; e.g., Fall 2018 p2, N 19,945, raw 0.556 versus equated 0.605). The report says equated scores "generally showed higher correlations."

> "Compared with the raw WCPM scores, equated WCPM scores generally showed higher correlations with students’ RIT scores from MAP Growth Reading, with only one exception (Fall 2018, p1)."
