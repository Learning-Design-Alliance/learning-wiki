Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). Instructional Conversations and Literature Logs — WWC Intervention Report. http://crede.berkeley.edu/research/crede/research/llaa/1.5pubs.html

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial in which 58 students were matched and randomly assigned within classrooms; 32 English language learners (16 intervention, 16 comparison) in grades 4–5 were analyzed. Appendix A3.1 reports factual comprehension effect size 0.82 and interpretive comprehension 0.80, both "Statistically significant", improvement index +29 each. The WWC "was only able to confirm the statistical significance of the factual comprehension measure."

"Saunders and Goldenberg (1999) found that Instructional Conversations and Literature Logs had statistically significant positive effects on both measures of factual comprehension and interpretive comprehension."
