## Evidence

### Slotnik 2016

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2016). Stay the Course: Teacher and Principal Evaluation in Maryland. WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED589981

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

District-aggregated 2016 survey analysis rank-ordered into quartiles by teacher agreement on seven SLO support items (Figure IV-1). The report calls the 20 percentage point difference evidence that some districts prepare educators better than others.

> "In the top quartile of districts with stronger SLO implementation, 62% of teachers agree that they receive at least five SLO support strategies. At the other extreme, in districts with weaker SLO implementation, only 42% of teachers agree"
