Evaluating a problem-based learning model integrated with 3D anatomy software and software-assisted annotation in undergraduate spinal surgery education: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08652-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 120 fifth-year clinical medicine undergraduates randomized to 3D+PBL+annotation teaching (n = 60) or traditional LBL (n = 60). The t-test comparison of subjective case-analysis scores favored the experimental group, "39.33 ± 5.38 vs. 32.08 ± 4.79, P < 0.001". No effect size is printed.
