Evidence¶

Vignoles et al. 2015¶

Anna Vignoles, John Jerrim, Richard Cowan. (2015). Mathematics Mastery: Primary Evaluation Report. Education Endowment Foundation. http://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/library/maths-mastery-primary

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Meta-analysis of two EEF-funded randomised controlled trials of Mathematics Mastery (this Year 1 trial and a Year 7 trial), discussed in the executive summary and conclusion; the report cautions that meta-analysis requires care when studies vary in important ways.

"Combining the findings from this study and a second randomised controlled trial of Mathematics Mastery involving Year 7 pupils shows a statistically significant average impact of one additional month's progress. This combined finding strengthens the overall evidence for the approach"

Jerrim et al. 2015¶

John Jerrim, Helen Austerberry, Cosette Crisan, Anne Ingold, Candia Morgan, Dave Pratt, Cathy Smith, Meg Wiggins. (2015). Mathematics Mastery: Secondary Evaluation Report. Education Endowment Foundation. https://doi.org/10.1186/ISRCTN70922140

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Meta-analytic combination of this Year 7 effectiveness trial with a separate Year 1 RCT, discussed in a summary report on the EEF website. The combined estimate is statistically significant at one month's progress; no effect size or confidence interval is printed in this report.

"Combining the findings from this study and a second randomised controlled trial of Mathematics Mastery involving Year 1 pupils shows a statistically significant average impact of one additional month's progress."
