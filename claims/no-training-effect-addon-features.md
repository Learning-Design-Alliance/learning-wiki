## Evidence

### Amita Goyal Chin 2016

Amita Goyal Chin, Ugochukwu Etudo, Mark A. Harris. (2016). On Mobile Device Security Practices and Training Efficacy: An Empirical Study. Informatics in Education, 15(2). https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2016.12

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Third MANOVA testing H3 in the same quasi-experiment, with add-on feature use as dependent variables. The test was non-significant ("F (2, n = 344) = .738, p = .479"), as were the encryption and anti-virus univariate tests.

> "Results of the omnibus MANOV A indicate that we were unable to detect any statistically significant difference on the propensity to use add-on features to provide protection between individuals who received general security training and individuals who did not: F (2, n = 344) = .738, p = .479; Wilk's Λ = .996, Partial η 2 = .004."
