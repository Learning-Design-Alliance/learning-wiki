## Evidence

### van der Gulden R 2020

van der Gulden R, Heeneman S, Kramer AWM, Laan RFJM, Scherpbier-de Haan ND, Thoonen BPA. (2020). How is self-regulated learning documented in e-portfolios of trainees? A content analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02114-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Content analysis of 90 e-portfolios; monitoring was rated with a single item plus memos, focusing on coherence between feedback, reflections and learning goals. "Signs of monitoring were present in three-quarters of the e-portfolios (74.4%)".

> "Signs of monitoring were present in three-quarters of the e-portfolios (74.4%). However, in one-third of these cases (24 of 67) the monitoring behaviour was limited, i.e. monitoring was mostly restricted to a feedback request, via a Mini-CEX, that related to one of the learning goals."
