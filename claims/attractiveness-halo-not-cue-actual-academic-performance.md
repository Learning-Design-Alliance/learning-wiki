## Evidence

### Talamas SN 2016

Talamas SN, Mavor KI, Perrett DI. (2016). Blinded by Beauty: Attractiveness Bias and Accurate Perceptions of Academic Performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0148284

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of ratings of 100 university-student faces against credit-weighted GPA. The study reports "no relationship between attractiveness and actual academic performance (r = 0.03)" while attractiveness correlated r = 0.81 with perceived intelligence and r = 0.74 with perceived academic performance.

> "there was no relationship between attractiveness and actual academic performance (r = 0.03), but a strong positive correlation between attractiveness and perceived intelligence (r = 0.81), attractiveness and perceived academic performance (r = 0.74) and attractiveness and perceived conscientiousness (r = 0.81)"
