# Motivational scaffolding in an e-learning environment produces a large main effect on academic self-efficacy in higher education students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students who interacted with the e-learning environment containing motivational scaffolding reported significantly higher academic self-efficacy than students using the environment without scaffolding. [→ Valencia-Vallejo et al. 2017](#valencia-vallejo-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Valencia-Vallejo et al. 2017

Nilson Valencia-Vallejo, Omar López-Vargas, Luis Sanabria-Rodríguez. (2017). Effect of Motivational Scaffolding on E-Learning Environments: Self-Efficacy, Learning Achievement, and Cognitive Style. Journal of Educators Online. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1168944

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

A 2 x 3 factorial MANOVA on 65 first-semester university students compared environments with and without motivational scaffolding. The scaffolding group reported higher self-efficacy (M = 5.86, SD = 0.50) than the control group (M = 4.36, SD = 0.59), with "η2 = .65".

> "a significant main effect of the e-learning environment exists (F (1, 59) = 111.773, p < = 0.01, η2 = .65) on self-efficacy in favor of the students that interacted with the version of the e-learning environment that included the motivational scaffolding."
