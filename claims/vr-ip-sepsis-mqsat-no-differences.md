### Medwell Lauren 2026

Medwell Lauren, Old Tim, Ahmed Awais, Holloway Victoria, McTaggart Lauren, Morgan Dafydd, O'Keeffe Callum, Olori Jemaima, Payne Ellie, Rainforth Christopher, Ramanujam Namritha, Vaidya Kriti, Jacobs Chris (2026). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Primary analysis of the four-arm randomized comparative pilot at Great Western Hospital (32 final-year medical students analyzed): one-way ANCOVA of assessment mQSAT scores, with number of prior simulations or debriefs as covariate, found "no statistically significant differences were observed between any of the study groups for any domain".

> "For mQSAT scores (n=32), no statistically significant differences were observed between any of the study groups for any domain by ANCOVA analysis."
