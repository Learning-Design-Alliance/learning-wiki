## Evidence

### Bjarne Skjødt Worm 2013

Bjarne Skjødt Worm. (2013). Learning from Simple Ebooks, Online Cases or Classroom Teaching When Acquiring Complex Knowledge. A Randomized Controlled Trial in Respiratory Physiology and Pulmonology. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0073336

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison in the randomized trial (n = 21 per group): the omnibus ANOVA for complex scenarios was significant (F = 16.77, p < 0.001); eBook improvement was 48.4% versus 59.4% (eCase) and 58.9% (classroom), pairwise p < 0.001 for eBook against each.

> "For analysis of complex scenarios (pulmonology cases), the group given textbook material (group 1a) acquired significantly less improvement than either of the other two groups (eCase or Classroom) (table 2)."

### Bjarne Skjødt Worm 2013 (2)

Bjarne Skjødt Worm. (2013). Learning from Simple Ebooks, Online Cases or Classroom Teaching When Acquiring Complex Knowledge. A Randomized Controlled Trial in Respiratory Physiology and Pulmonology. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0073336

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U-test comparison in Table 2 of the randomized trial: eCase versus classroom improvement differed by 0.1 percentage points (95% CI −0.7–1.0), p = 0.58, so the two case-based methods were statistically comparable.

> "1b (eCase) vs 2 (Classroom)	59.4% vs 58.9%	0.1 (−0.7–1.0)	0.58"
