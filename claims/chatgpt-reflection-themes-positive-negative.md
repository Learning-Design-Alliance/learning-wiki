`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Inductive, bottom-up thematic analysis of individual reflections written after ChatGPT-supported activities, coded by the first author and verified by the third author. Students valued efficiency and perspective-giving but reported inaccuracy requiring fact-checking and reduced critical thinking.

> "Qualitative analysis of students' reflections generated eight positive themes about ChatGPT use, including efficiency, addressing knowledge gaps, and generating human-like responses, and eight negative themes, including generic responses, lack of innovation, and counterproductive to self -discipline and thinking."
