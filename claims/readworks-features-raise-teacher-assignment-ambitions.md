### Tare 2019

Tare, M. & Shell, A. R. (2019). Designing for learner variability: Examining the impact of research-based edtech in the classroom. Washington, DC: Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/82839c44-adb1-400c-b422-9bfbbca65985 [doi:10.51388/20.500.12265/81](https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/81)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two overarching survey questions about the feature suite, asked of national survey respondents who had tried at least one feature. The report interprets these percentages as suggesting "the features may encourage teachers to challenge students and maintain high expectations for their performance."

> "Of the respondents who had tried at least one of the features on our survey, 89 percent responded that they are likely to assign more articles on ReadWorks and 82 percent said that they are likely to assign higher-level articles on ReadWorks as a result of the features available to aid students."
