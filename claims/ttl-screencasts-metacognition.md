## Evidence

### Susan Nicholson-Dykstra 2013

Susan Nicholson-Dykstra, Ben Van Dusen and Valerie Otero. (2013). Teaching to Learn: iPads as Tools for Transforming Physics Student Roles. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.053](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.053)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative finding from teacher-documented classroom observations and student reflections across a school year in one AP Physics B class, including observed peer feedback discussions and rubric co-construction. The article reports no quantitative measure or effect size for this metacognitive shift.

> "Yet, students exhibited greater metacognition regarding the teaching and learning process, with a focus on student (audience) engagement, assessment and growth.  Students also identified their roles in the learning process as being both learner and teacher."
