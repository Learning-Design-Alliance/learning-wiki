Evidence¶

Schroeder 2025¶

Schroeder, N. L., Jaldi, C. D., Zhang, S., & Yamoah, K. (2025). AI-Assisted Data Extraction for Systematic Reviews in Education. https://osf.io/nraxf/?view_only=879654690be249cc9237525ed7f86ec2

q2 · i? · design · r2

Authors' conclusion from the main-study confusion-matrix analyses of 112 studies, where LLMs "had often extracted inaccurate information at least 7% of the time" and up to 39% of the time, and falsely reported variables as not reported 3–8% of the time.

"Our results show that in a research synthesis context in education, LLMs should not be relied on for fully automated data extraction tasks without human oversight and validation."
