## Evidence

### Mabuza LH 2020

Mabuza LH, Mntla PS. (2020). Generalist practitioners' self-rating and competence in electrocardiogram interpretation in South Africa. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. 12(1), a2421. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v12i1.2421

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-rating analysis in the cross-sectional study (n = 93), compared with actual performance using Fisher's exact test. Table 3 shows self-rated 'Good' practitioners scored 11/40 (27.5%) correct versus 136/1140 (11.9%) for 'Poor'. The article states self-rating "tallied well" with correct answers.

> "about two-thirds of the generalist practitioners rated themselves as having a poor ECG competency, whilst less than 3% rated themselves as having good competence."
