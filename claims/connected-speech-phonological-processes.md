The article identifies four phonological processes comprising connected speech: "reduction, elision, assimilation, and contraction". It further notes that instructing connected speech can assist learners in comprehending rapid speech.

> "Connected speech is a phenomenon to be occurred in spoken language and includes phonological processes such as reduction, elision, assimilation, and contraction."
