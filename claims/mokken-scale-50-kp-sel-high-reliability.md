## Evidence

### Cao Zhen-Yu 2024

Cao Zhen-Yu, Lin Feng, Feng Chun. (2024). Interpretation of course conceptual structure and student self-efficacy: an integrated strategy of knowledge graphs with item response modeling. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05401-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Psychometric analysis of the 50-item scale in 208 rehabilitation therapy students. The article reports "All four coefficients were > 0.95, indicating good internal consistency for the 50-item scale", including LCRC = 0.9776.

> "Cronbach's α = 0.9684, Guttman's λ2 = 0.9689, Molenaar Sijtsma Statistic = 0.9708, LCRC = 0.9776. All four coefficients were > 0.95, indicating good internal consistency for the 50-item scale."
