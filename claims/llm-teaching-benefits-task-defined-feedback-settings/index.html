Evidence¶

Liu Xiaoliu 2026¶

Liu Xiaoliu, Pan Jiayue, Zhang Chengyi, Wang Xiaomei, Wan Jinghua, Wang Qiang. (2026). Effectiveness of large language model-based teaching interventions in medical education: a systematic review and stratified meta-analysis of 14 studies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09879-0

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review's narrative synthesis of included-study results, attributed to specific primary studies (e.g., Brügge 2024; Wang 2025). It reports favorable feasibility and learning signals for GPT-based history-taking training and improved clinical decision scores under simulated-patient and structured-feedback conditions. Attributed narratively, so quality is capped at 2.

"Among the included studies, the clearest signals appeared in case-based progression, history-taking practice, simulated patients, and structured feedback training [6]. These settings share features of clearly defined tasks, immediate feedback, and repeated practice, which align closely with the technical strengths of LLM-based tools [27]."
