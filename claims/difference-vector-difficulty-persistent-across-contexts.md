## Evidence

### Wilcox 2019

Wilcox, B. R., & Corsiglia, G. (2019). A cross-context look at upper-division student difficulties with integration. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exam-solution coding across all three contexts found 30-45% of students unable to correctly express the difference vector. The authors note the fraction "remains high, and perhaps even increases slightly, suggesting that this diﬃculty is persistent across multiple exposures."

> "students had somewhat less diﬃculty pro- ducing a correct expression with 30-45% of students unable to do so (30%, N = 22 of 72 in gravitation; 45%, N = 45 of 130 in electrostatics; 35%, N = 47 of 135 in magneto- statics). However, the fact that the fraction of students exhibiting diﬃculties with the diﬀerence vector remains high, and perhaps even increases slightly, suggests that this diﬃculty is persistent across multiple exposures."
