# ChatGPT-3 assistance did not improve students' essay grades: the control group scored slightly higher (2.39 vs 2.00) and the group effect was not statistically significant

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2023 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students writing with ChatGPT-3 assistance did not achieve higher essay grades than control students; the control group averaged slightly higher (2.39 ± 0.71 vs 2.00 ± 0.73), and group was not a significant predictor of essay score (P = 0.184). [→ Željana Bašić 2023](#zeljana-basic-2023)

## Evidence

### Željana Bašić 2023

Željana Bašić, Ana Banovac, Ivana Kružić, Ivan Jerković. (2023). Better by You, better than Me? ChatGPT-3 as writing assistance in students' essays. University Department of Forensic Sciences, University of Split. [doi:10.35542/osf.io/n5m7s](https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/n5m7s)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled quasi-experiment with nine students per group, balanced by module and GPA, writing an 800–1000-word Croatian essay. The article reports "a slightly higher average score in the control (2.39 ± 0.71) than in the GTP group (2.00 ± 0.73)"; no effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "GTP and control group , on average, obtained grade C, with a slightly higher average score in  the control (2.39 ± 0.71) than in the GTP group (2.00  ± 0.73)"

### Željana Bašić 2023 (2)

Željana Bašić, Ana Banovac, Ivana Kružić, Ivan Jerković. (2023). Better by You, better than Me? ChatGPT-3 as writing assistance in students' essays. University Department of Forensic Sciences, University of Split. [doi:10.35542/osf.io/n5m7s](https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/n5m7s)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear regression on the 18 students' overall essay scores tested group, writing duration, module and GPA. The article reports "none of the predictors had a significant effect on the outcome", including group (P = 0.184); only a p-value is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "However, none of the predictors had a significant effect on the outcome: group (P  = 0.184), writing duration (P  = 0.669), module (P = 0.388), and GPA (P = 0.532)."
