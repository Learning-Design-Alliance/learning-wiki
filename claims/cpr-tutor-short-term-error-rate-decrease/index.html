Evidence¶

Di Mitri 2022¶

Di Mitri, D., Schneider, J., & Drachsler, H. (2022). KeepMeInTheLoop: Real-Time Feedback with Multimodal Data. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education (2022) 32:1093–1118. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00281-z

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Analysis of the feedback intervention study with 10 participants performing two 1-minute sessions each; the authors computed first derivatives of Error Rate 10 seconds before and after feedback firing, finding that "the Error Rates of all five target classes are decreasing soon after the feedback is prompted".

"We found out that, on average, the Error Rates of all five target classes are decreasing soon after the feedback is prompted."
