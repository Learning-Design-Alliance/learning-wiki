### Zachary A. Pardos 2023

Zachary A. Pardos, Shreya Bhandari. (2023). Learning gain differences between ChatGPT and human tutor generated algebra hints. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.06871](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.06871)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of OATutor logs (time in lesson and hint request actions) across the four conditions, reported in Table 2. The article states "A similar amount of time was spent" and that "there was not a time savings"; no inferential test is reported for this measure.

> "A similar amount of time was spent between both control and experiment in the two lessons, indicating that while the number of hints requested in the control was much greater, because manual hints were unbounded in the number that could be authored, there was not a time savings by seeing fewer hints in the experiment conditions."
