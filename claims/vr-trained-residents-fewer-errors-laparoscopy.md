Anyinkeng A, Mesfin GS, Taryim M, Efuetlateh J, Tsion H, Ahmed AK. (2025). The role of remote and virtual surgical training in expanding cardiothoracic surgical capacity in low-resource regions. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-03142-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a randomized controlled trial comparing VR-trained and non-VR-trained residents; non-VR-trained residents "made three times more errors, required 58% longer surgical time" (P < 0.0037). Reported second-hand by the review; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "A randomized controlled trial compared VR-trained residents with their non-VR-trained peers and consistently demonstrated superior performance among the VR-trained residents. For instance, in laparoscopic procedures, non-VR-trained residents made three times more errors, required 58% longer surgical time, and demonstrated less consistent outcomes (P < 0.0037)"
