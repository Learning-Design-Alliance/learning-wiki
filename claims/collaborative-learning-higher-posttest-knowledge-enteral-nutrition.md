## Evidence

### Acun Aysun 2026

Acun Aysun, Aksoy Bulgurcu Rahime. (2026). The effect of collaborative learning approach on nursing students' knowledge and skill learning for enteral nutrition: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education 26:328. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08687-w

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Open-label randomized controlled trial with 90 first-year nursing students randomized 45/45; the collaborative learning (Jigsaw) group versus the individual-practice control group on a 20-question 100-point enteral nutrition knowledge test. The article reports "significantly higher post-test knowledge scores compared with the control group (p = 0.007), with a medium effect size (d = 0.587)".
