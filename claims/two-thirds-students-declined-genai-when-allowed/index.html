Mixed-methods study across three teacher education courses embedding GenAI in assessment, using a survey (N = 85), student interviews (N = 11), and declarations of GenAI use in submitted assignments (N = 158). The finding that "approximately two-thirds of the students decided not to adopt GenAI when allowed" is the headline adoption result.
