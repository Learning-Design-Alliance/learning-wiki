`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group comparison in the pilot RCT on the section-position item type. The digital group outperformed the traditional group at both post-test 1 (P = 0.051) and post-test 2 (P = 0.055), but neither reached significance.

> "In the post-test 1, the digital group performed better in terms of the position and orientation of the probe (hereinafter referred to as “section position”), but no significant difference was shown (traditional group: 31.7 ± 22.3 vs. digital group: 54.4 ± 19.3, P = 0.051)."
