Evidence¶

Quinn 2016¶

Quinn, D. M., & Kim, J. S. (2016). Predictors and Moderators of Teacher Learning and Changes in Practice: Evidence from a Randomized Trial of a Teacher-Adapted Literacy Program. SREE Spring 2016 Conference abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567622

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Cluster-randomized trial in 27 high-poverty elementary schools; OLS regression on a standardized PCA literacy learning index from spring teacher surveys (alpha above .84), controlling for randomization bloc, standard errors clustered at school. The article reports no significant condition effect and an experience difference of ES=.51 sd.

"condition did not have a significant effect on teachers' self-reported learning, but teachers across conditions who were new to READS reported learning more than teachers who had participated in READS before (ES=.51 sd)"
