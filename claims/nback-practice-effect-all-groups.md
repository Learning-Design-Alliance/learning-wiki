## Evidence

### Scheibe 2009

Scheibe, S., & Blanchard-Fields, F. (2009). Effects of Regulating Emotions on Cognitive Performance: What Is Costly for Young Adults Is not so Costly for Older Adults. Psychology and Aging, 24(1), 217-223. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0013807

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus repeated-measures ANOVA of combined N-Back performance at baseline and three post-induction time points. The article reports a main effect of time with η2 = .48 and that "all participants in all conditions increased their performance." No further practice gains appeared at the two subsequent measurements.

> "there was a strong multivariate main effect of time, F (3, 134) = 41.91, p = .001, η2 = .48. Across time, all participants in all conditions increased their performance, with the largest increase from baseline to T1."
