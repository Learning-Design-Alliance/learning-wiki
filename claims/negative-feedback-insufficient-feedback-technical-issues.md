Grounded-theory coding of open-ended questionnaire responses from junior students. The dominant negative theme was "insufficient guidance/feedback", followed by "insufficient integration of the intervention into the class".

> "Junior students’ negative comments (n = 82) indicated concerns with insufficient guidance/feedback (n = 30), insufficient integration of the intervention into the class (n = 20), lack of prior knowledge to make best use of the collaborative activities (n = 6) and issues with the technology (n = 6)"
