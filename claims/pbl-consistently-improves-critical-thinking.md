## Evidence

### Dawit T. Tiruneh 2014

Dawit T. Tiruneh, An Verburgh & Jan Elen. (2014). Effectiveness of Critical Thinking Instruction in Higher Education: A Systematic Review of Intervention Studies. Higher Education Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/hes.v4n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesis of the review of three PBL studies, in which problems were addressed through small group discussion without explicit CT instruction. In Sendag and Odabasi's online PBL study, the PBL group "significantly outperformed" the instructor-led group; the other two studies also found PBL groups significantly outperformed lecture groups.

> "They found that students in the online PBL group significantly outperformed those in the online instructor-led group."
