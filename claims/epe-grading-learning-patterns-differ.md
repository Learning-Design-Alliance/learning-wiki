`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

EPE grading learning curves (cubic spline GEE, 4 internal knots) in the 200-case study. Urology trainees had significantly higher baseline agreement (OR 1.90 [1.11, 2.93]), offset by a steeper first-50-case learning curve for radiology trainees (+17.57% vs −5.65%); an early inflection point at 11.0 cases was identified for radiology trainees.

> "Radiology trainees showed sustained improvement throughout (+25.04% [15.18, 36.12]), while urology trainees’ performance remained stable, with no significant improvement observed by study end (+5.44% [−4.48, 17.50])."
