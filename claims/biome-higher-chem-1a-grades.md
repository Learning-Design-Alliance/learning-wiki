## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the propensity score–matched sample, BIOME students' CHEM 1A final grades were 0.19 GPA points higher than matched non-BIOME peers (p < 0.001). [→ Wilton Mike 2021](#wilton-mike-2021)

## Evidence

### Wilton Mike 2021

Wilton Mike, Katz Daniel, Clairmont Anthony, Gonzalez-Nino Eduardo, Foltz Kathy R., Christoffersen Rolf E. (2021). Improving Academic Performance and Retention of First-Year Biology Students through a Scalable Peer Mentorship Program. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-02-0039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Propensity score–matched regression (genetic matching via MatchIt; BIOME n = 393, matched control n = 344; standardized mean differences < |0.10|) with CHEM 1A GPA as outcome; Table 5 reports BIOME beta 0.19, p = 0.002, 730 observations. No standardized effect size printed.

> "In this case, the increase in CHEM 1A final grade was 0.19 GPA (p < 0.001) points for a student in BIOME compared with students like them (matched on demographics and other confounding variables) in the matched sample (Table 5)."
