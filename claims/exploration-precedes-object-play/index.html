Wynberg 2021¶

Wynberg, E. R., Boland, A., Raijmakers, M. E. J., & van der Veen, C. (2021). Towards a Comprehensive View of Object-Oriented Play. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09608-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review statement within the evolutionary psychology perspective section, attributed to prior work (Belsky and Most 1981; Hutt 1966; Pellegrini 2009). The review reports that "Exploration always precedes other interactions with objects" and frames exploration as necessary but insufficient for play.

"Exploration always precedes other interactions with objects, such as object play: Exploration is a necessary, albeit insufficient, condition for play"
