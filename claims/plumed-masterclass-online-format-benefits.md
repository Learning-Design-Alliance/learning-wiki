## Evidence

### Tribello 2024

Tribello, G. A., et al. (2024). PLUMED Tutorials: a collaborative, community-driven learning ecosystem. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.03595](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.03595)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' retrospective interpretation of their experience running seven online PLUMED Masterclass sessions, each with an hour-long Zoom lecture, a week of homework with Slack support, and a solutions lecture. This is an author judgment, not a measured comparison: "possibly better than anything we have developed for previous, in-person meetings."

> "The material prepared for PLUMED Masterclass was possibly better than anything we have developed for previous, in-person meetings. This improvement was mostly due to having the time to refine old tutorials based on student feedback."
