## Evidence

### Taveira-Gomes Tiago 2014

Taveira-Gomes Tiago, Saffarzadeh Areo, Severo Milton, Guimarães M Jorge, Ferreira Maria Amélia. (2014). A novel collaborative e-learning platform for medical students - ALERT STUDENT. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-143

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Adoption survey administered after the second classroom session to the same 48 FMUP students, using a 4-point likert scale. Printed means included 3.77 for use in other basic science subjects and 3.66 for recommending the system to colleagues; students were "willing to use them as a privileged element for their medical education".

> "Both surveys have shown that students generally agreed that the tools provided were useful and simple and were willing to use them as a privileged element for their medical education."
