### Redish 2014

Redish, E. F., Sawtelle, V., & Turpen, C. (2014). The role physics can play in a multi-disciplinary curriculum for non-physics scientists and engineers. Proceedings of the Frontiers in Mathematics and Science Education Research Conference, Famagusta, North Cyprus. [doi:10.30935/scimath/9621](https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9621)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interview and classroom-discussion case evidence from the chemical-energy unit of NEXUS/Physics (Dreyfus et al. 2014). While some students showed the persistent piñata-model confusion identified in the chemistry education literature, Gregor gave a sophisticated account distinguishing the isolated ATP hydrolysis mechanism from the biological framing in which water is always available. The authors concluded the two disciplinary framings must both be respected and reconciled.

> "What we take from this example is that we were taking too narrow a view when we viewed "the energy stored in a chemical bond" as a misconception."
