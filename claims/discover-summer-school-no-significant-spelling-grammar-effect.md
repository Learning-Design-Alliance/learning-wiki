## Evidence

### Torgerson 2014

Torgerson, D., Torgerson, C., Jefferson, L., Buckley, H., Ainsworth, H., Heaps, C., & Mitchell, N. (2014). Discover Summer School Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581103

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary analysis of the randomised trial using the spelling and grammar score from the Progress in English test, conducted on 69 pupils (44 intervention, 25 control) tested in 13 secondary schools, adjusted for baseline-predicted level only. The article reports "an effect size of 0.15" with a confidence interval crossing zero.

> "There was no evidence o f a statistically significant difference in spelling and grammar score between the two randomised groups with a non -significant increase of 0.58 marks for those in the intervention group when compared with those in the control group (p = 0.53, 95% CI: -1.26 to 2.42). This relates to an effect size of 0.15 (95% CI: -0.34 to 0.63)."
