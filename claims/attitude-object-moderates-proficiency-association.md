## Evidence

### Maria Adelina Ianos 2026

Maria Adelina Ianos, Andrei Rusu, Ramona Paloş, Cristina Petreñas, Clara Sansó. (2026). Linking Language Attitudes to Proficiency Across Learning Contexts: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Correlational Evidence. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10193-w

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level moderator analysis across four attitude objects. Attitudes toward the language (k = 93, r = .36, CI [.32, .40]); language learning (k = 19, r = .27); speakers (k = 17, r = .15, CI [−.06, .34], RVE p = .099); learning situation had only three effects from two clusters, precluding reliable inference.

> "The omnibus test for attitude object was statistically significant in the primary three-level analysis, F(3, 128) = 4.18, p =.007, indicating that the four attitude objects do not share a common true effect size."
