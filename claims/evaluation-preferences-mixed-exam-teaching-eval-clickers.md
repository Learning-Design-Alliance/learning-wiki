## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 89% favour keeping a final exam, but the modalité winning most support is a mix of final examination and continuous assessment (mostly homework). [→ Cécile de Hosson 2015](#cecile-de-hosson-2015)
`q2 i?` 95% favour evaluation of their teaching, mostly by external questionnaires often associated with collective discussion with students; 75% want it at the end of the course. [→ Cécile de Hosson 2015 (2)](#cecile-de-hosson-2015-2)
