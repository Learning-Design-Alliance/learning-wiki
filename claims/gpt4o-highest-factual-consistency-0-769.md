Factual consistency was assessed by segmenting medical records into minimal fact units and measuring semantic similarity with VP responses using the bge-small-zh-v1.5 model. The GPT-4o–based approach achieved the highest mean score of "0.769 (SD 0.04)" and "significantly outperforming the single-LLM baseline (adjusted P=.03)".
