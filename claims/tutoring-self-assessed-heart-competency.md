## Evidence

### Leidi Antonio 2024

Leidi Antonio, Krauer Juliette, Soret Guillaume, Parent Thibault, Marti Christophe, Meyer Philippe, Juillerat André, Bex Stijn, Suh Noémie, Rouyer Frédéric, Siegenthaler Nils, Grosgurin Olivier. (2024). Heart and lung point-of-care ultrasonography tutoring in internal medicine: a randomized controlled trial. Journal of Ultrasound. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40477-024-00968-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-assessment survey (5-point Likert, dichotomized) administered after the course and at 6 months in the randomized trial. The heart US contrast was significant; the lung US contrast was not, so the effect holds for heart but not lung imaging.

> "At follow-up, a higher proportion of participants in the intervention arm considered themselves globally competent for heart (60% versus 0%, p = 0.003) and lung US (60% versus 25%, p = 0.19), although the difference was statistically significant only for heart US."
