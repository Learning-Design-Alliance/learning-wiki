## Evidence

### Zhao Hui; Gu Huijuan 2026

Zhao Hui; Gu Huijuan. (2026). Thoughtless use of generative artificial intelligence and college students’ self-directed learning: a multi-group SEM analysis of gender differences. Scientific Reports 16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-54337-y

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM path analysis of self-reported data from 487 undergraduates collected by snowball sampling. The direct TUGA→SDL path was significant and negative: "β = − 0.42, t = 6.76, p < .001".

> "Among the direct predictors of SDL, MOV exhibited the strongest effect (β = 0.68, t = 11.63, p < .001), followed by the TUGA (β = − 0.42, t = 6.76, p < .001) and SE (β = 0.23, t = 3.93, p < .001)."
