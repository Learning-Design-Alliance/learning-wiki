## Evidence

### Jang Yujin 2021

Jang Yujin, Suh Youngmeen. (2021). Longitudinal Effects of Parental Academic Support on Academic Achievement in Korea: Will You Be a Companion or a Manager in Your Children’s Academic Support? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182010823

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM paths in the KELS panel analysis (Table 4): PAS as manager ⇒ self-regulation, −0.02 (S.E. 0.03), standardized −0.03, not significant; PAS as manager ⇒ academic achievement, 1.54 (S.E. 1.23), standardized 0.03, not significant — i.e. "did not affect" either outcome. No d/r-family effect size is printed.

> "PAS as a manager, however, did not affect adolescents’ self-regulation and academic achievements and had a positive effect on amotivation."

### Jang Yujin 2021 (2)

Jang Yujin, Suh Youngmeen. (2021). Longitudinal Effects of Parental Academic Support on Academic Achievement in Korea: Will You Be a Companion or a Manager in Your Children’s Academic Support? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182010823

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same structural equation model: the manager ⇒ amotivation path is printed as 0.08 (S.E. 0.03), standardized 0.07, p < 0.01 — a small positive effect on amotivation. The authors call this result surprising: managerial support "surprisingly, was found to increase their amotivation". No d/r-family effect size is printed.

> "PAS as a manager, however, did not affect adolescents’ self-regulation and academic achievements and had a positive effect on amotivation."
