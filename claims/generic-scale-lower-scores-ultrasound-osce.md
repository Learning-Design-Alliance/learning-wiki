## Evidence

### Schneider Simon 2026

Schneider Simon, Stricker Daniel, Berendonk Christoph, Baron-Stefaniak Joanna, Walter Robin. (2026). Abdominal ultrasound performance assessment: a comparison of generic and extended OSCE rating scales. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08994-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate split-plot ANOVA of performance scores (percentages of a maximum of 30 points) found the scale-type × performance interaction significant with η² = 0.047, a small effect; mean performance was 75.38% on the generic versus 77.99% on the extended scale (p < 0.001).

> "we found a significant interaction between type of rating scale and performance P (F(1, 461) = 22.95, p < 0.001, η² = 0.047; small effect), in other words the two rating scales rated participants' performance significantly different with the generic rating scale attributing lower scores than the extended scale."
