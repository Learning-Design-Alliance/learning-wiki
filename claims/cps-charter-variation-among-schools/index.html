Gwynne 2017¶

Gwynne, J.A., & Moore, P.T. (2017). Chicago's charter high schools: Organizational features, enrollment, school transfers, and student performance. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://cps.edu/NewSchools/Pages/RFP2017.aspx

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Chapter 5/6 variation analysis across charter high schools on test scores, college enrollment, and college selectivity in the controlled models; at a small number of charters average test scores exceeded 0.50 standard deviations above the district average versus 0.30 at the highest-performing non-charter schools, and some charter college enrollment rates exceeded 70 percent versus around 50 percent at top non-charters.

"once we controlled for differences in incoming skills, experiences, and background characteristics, there was far more variation among charter schools on these outcomes than among non-charter schools."
