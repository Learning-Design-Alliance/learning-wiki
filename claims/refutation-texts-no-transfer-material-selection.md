### Anja Prinz 2022

Anja Prinz, Julia Kollmer, Lisa Flick, Alexander Renkl, Alexander Eitel. (2022). Refuting student teachers' misconceptions about multimedia learning. Instructional Science, 50, 89–110. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09568-z

Planned orthogonal contrast on the material-selection transfer test (four selection tasks, maximum 4 points) in the randomized experiment. The article reports "no significant difference" with "t(93) = −0.07, p = 0.943, η2 < 0.01"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The first contrast revealed no significant difference between the refutation-texts conditions and the standard-texts condition, t(93) = −0.07, p = 0.943, η2 < 0.01, which does not support Hypothesis 3a."
