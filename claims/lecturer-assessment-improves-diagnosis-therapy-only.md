## Evidence

### Fünger S. M. 2016

Fünger S. M., Lesevic H., Rosner S., Ott I., Berberat P., Nikendei C., Sonne C. (2016). Improved self- and external assessment of the clinical abilities of medical students through structured improvement measures in an internal medicine bedside course. GMS Journal for Medical Education 33(4), Doc59. [doi:10.3205/zma001058](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001058)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

External assessment by lecturers via the same anonymous online questionnaire after each course date, compared T0 versus T1 with Mann-Whitney-U tests. The article prints "MV T0=3.0±1.0, MV T1=2.7±0.7, p.=0.038" for Diagnosis and a significant Therapy improvement.

> "The difference between SS 12 and SS 13 on the one hand and between WS 13/14 and SS 14 on the other is significant for the category "Diagnosis" (MV T0=3.0±1.0, MV T1=2.7±0.7, p.=0.038) and "Therapy" (MV T0=3.8±1.1, MV T1=3.1±1.0, p<0.001)"

### Fünger S. M. 2016 (2)

Fünger S. M., Lesevic H., Rosner S., Ott I., Berberat P., Nikendei C., Sonne C. (2016). Improved self- and external assessment of the clinical abilities of medical students through structured improvement measures in an internal medicine bedside course. GMS Journal for Medical Education 33(4), Doc59. [doi:10.3205/zma001058](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001058)

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

In the same lecturer external assessment comparison, the history & examination category showed no statistically significant change; the article prints "MV T0=2.4±0.8, MV T1=2.3±0.8, p.=0.798". No effect size is reported, so the magnitude is unmeasured.

> "The difference in the "Medical history & examination“ is not significant (MV T0=2.4±0.8, MV T1=2.3±0.8, p.=0.798)"
