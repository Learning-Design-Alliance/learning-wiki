Robinson SJA, Ritchie AMA, Pacilli M, Nestel D, McLeod E, Nataraja RM. (2024). Simulation-Based Education of Health Workers in Low- and Middle-Income Countries: A Systematic Review. Glob Health Sci Pract. https://doi.org/10.9745/GHSP-D-24-00187

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of 97 studies of SBE in LMICs, synthesized with descriptive statistics and narrative synthesis due to heterogeneity. The review reports that of the subset testing significance, "42 (52%) demonstrated a partial improvement in outcomes and 19 (23%) demonstrated a complete improvement." No pooled effect size was computed.

> "Eighty-one studies tested for statistical significance (84%). Of this subset, 42 (52%) demonstrated a partial improvement in outcomes and 19 (23%) demonstrated a complete improvement."
