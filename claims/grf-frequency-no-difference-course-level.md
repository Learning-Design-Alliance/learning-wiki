Comparison of GRF coding frequencies between forty-one first-year Metacognition students and thirty-eight V&W physics/engineering majors. The t-test "revealed no statistically signiﬁcant differences"; equivalence was not tested. Figure 1 shows nearly identical prevalence of narrative statements in both courses.

> "A t-test of the frequency of coded statements by week and across the entire semester revealed no statistically signiﬁcant differences between the two sets of statements."
