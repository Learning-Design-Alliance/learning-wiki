### Lehto Tuomas MSc 2011

Lehto Tuomas MSc, Oinas-Kukkonen Harri PhD. (2011). Persuasive Features in Web-Based Alcohol and Smoking Interventions: A Systematic Review of the Literature. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3222186/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

RCT among 290 people willing to quit smoking who smoked 10 or more cigarettes daily, testing a fully automated digital smoking cessation intervention (Happy Ending) including Web pages, SMS, interactive voice response, and emails. The review reports "Participants in the treatment group reported clinically and statistically significantly higher repeated point abstinence rates than control participants."

> "Participants in the treatment group reported clinically and statistically significantly higher repeated point abstinence rates than control participants. (20% treatment vs 7% control, odds ratio [OR] = 3.43, 95% confidence interval [CI] = 1.60 - 7.34, P = .002)"
