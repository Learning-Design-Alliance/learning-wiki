Johnson 2024¶

Johnson, D.W., Hinze-Pifer, R., Orta, D., & Guz, S. (2024). Mitigating the academic impacts of proximity to homicide: The role of schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/mitigating-the-academic-impacts-of-proximity-to-homicide

q2 · i? · associational · r2

OLS regressions related 5Essentials survey measures of school climate and organization to school-specific outcome changes with proximity to homicide, adjusting for the percent of students in proximity. The report cautions the analysis is correlational and cannot rank which measures mattered most.

"Schools that mitigate the negative effects of living in close geographic proximity to homicide on students’ academic outcomes were characterized by strong, positive school climates across a range of measures, including engaging instruction and trusting, connected relationships among students and between students and adults."
