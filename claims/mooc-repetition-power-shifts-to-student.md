## Evidence

### Werner Krauth 2014

Werner Krauth. (2014). Coming home from a MOOC. [doi:10.1109/mcse.2015.30](https://doi.org/10.1109/mcse.2015.30)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The author's qualitative observation about MOOC viewing modes: students "routinely accelerate or slow down the video player", stop, quit, and replay difficult sequences, so videos are not viewed in linear time. No measurement or survey statistic is attached to this statement.

> "The power to repeat is now in the hand of the individual student and it suits individual needs."
