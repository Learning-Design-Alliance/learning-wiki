Evidence¶

Campoli 2022¶

Campoli, A. K., & Darling-Hammond, L. (with Podolsky, A., & Levin, S.). (2022). Principal learning opportunities and school outcomes: Evidence from California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/438.376

q3 · i0 · associational · r2

Linear regression on the full professional development sample of principals reporting professional development in the prior 2 years, from approximately 460 schools and 314,000 students. Table 4 prints the overall index at 0.817* for ELA and 1.281** for mathematics, with leading instruction at 0.869* and 1.176** respectively. Forecasting a 9-point mathematics difference between index scores of 2 and 9, the report states it "is equivalent to 0.17 SD."

"In mathematics, the 9-point difference in gains is equivalent to 0.17 SD, which can be interpreted as almost 3 months (55 days) of additional instruction."
