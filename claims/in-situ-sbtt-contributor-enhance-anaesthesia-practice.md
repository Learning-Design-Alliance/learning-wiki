### Finstad Anne Strand 2023

Finstad Anne Strand, Aase Ingunn, Bjørshol Conrad Arnfinn, Ballangrud Randi. (2023). In situ simulation-based team training and its significance for transfer of learning to clinical practice—A qualitative focus group interview study of anaesthesia personnel. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04201-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative inductive content analysis of follow-up focus group interviews with 14 anaesthesia personnel two weeks and six months after interprofessional in situ SBTT. The abstract reports that participants "experienced that interprofessional in situ SBTT motivated transfer of learning" and raised awareness of their own practice.

> "Anaesthesia personnel experienced that interprofessional in situ SBTT motivated transfer of learning and provided the opportunity to be aware of own practice regarding NTS and teamwork."
