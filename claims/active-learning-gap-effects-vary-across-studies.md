## Evidence

### Theobald Elli J. et al 2020

Theobald Elli J. et al. (2020). Active learning narrows achievement gaps for underrepresented students in undergraduate science, technology, engineering, and math. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1916903117

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Granular analysis of per-study gap changes (Fig. 2) shows heterogeneous effects: the printed counts are 10 of 15 examination studies favoring MGS gains, five favoring non-MGS, and 15 of 26 passing-rate studies favoring MGS failure reduction. In 8 of the 10 cases gaps shrank but MGS students still performed less well.

> "In 10 of the 15 studies with examination score data, students from MGS showed disproportionate gains under active learning relative to students from non-MGS"
