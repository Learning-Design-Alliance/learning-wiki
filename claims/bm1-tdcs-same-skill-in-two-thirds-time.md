## Evidence

### Cox Morgan L. 2020

Cox Morgan L., Deng Zhi-De, Palmer Hannah, Watts Amanda, Beynel Lysianne, Young Jonathan R., Lisanby Sarah H., Migaly John, Appelbaum Lawrence G. (2020). Utilizing transcranial direct current stimulation to enhance laparoscopic technical skills training: A randomized controlled trial. Brain Stimulation. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brs.2020.03.009

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learning-curve comparison from the randomized trial's six 20-min blocks: the bM1 group's fourth-block performance matched the sham group's sixth-block level, which the authors translate to "about 40 min less total training time". No effect size is printed for this derived comparison.

> "Subjects training with active tDCS reached approximately the same level of technical skill in roughly two thirds the time as compared with sham tDCS, based on performance levels following the fourth and sixth training blocks"
