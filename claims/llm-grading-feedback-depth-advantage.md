`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative comparison of feedback depth from the course's parallel grading experiment (Section 7). The authors attribute the contrast partly to human graders being senior undergraduates balancing grading with their own workload. LLM feedback examples appear in Figure 7.

> "human grader comments were typically two to three words (e.g., "wrong calculation" or "missing plot"), whereas the LLM grading system produced multi-sentence feedback identifying the specific error, explaining why the approach was incorrect, and suggesting how to fix it"
