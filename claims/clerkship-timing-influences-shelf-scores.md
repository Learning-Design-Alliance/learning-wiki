### Astroth C 2026

Astroth C, Lunow-Luke T, Smith M, Chang J, Grigorian A, O'Leary M P. (2026). Shelf help: what powers medical student subject exam performance in the United States. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2708427

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reports, citing Crisafio & Cho (n=518), that later clerkship timing and taking internal medicine first improved psychiatry shelf performance (p<0.0001). Similar findings for surgery shelf after IM.

> "taking the clerkship later in the year and after internal medicine was found improve performance"
