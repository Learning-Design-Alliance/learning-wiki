## Evidence

### Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt 2021

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt. (2021). Developmental brain dynamics of numerical and arithmetic abilities. npj Science of Learning (2021) 6:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00099-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of a small number of structural brain imaging studies: gray matter volume of the arithmetic network "is positively correlated with higher arithmetic skills", and white matter tracts connecting prefrontal with posterior parietal and occipitotemporal cortex show positive associations; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The evidence from this work suggests that the gray matter volume of the arithmetic network is positively correlated with higher arithmetic skills 132,133."

### Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt 2021 (2)

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt. (2021). Developmental brain dynamics of numerical and arithmetic abilities. npj Science of Learning (2021) 6:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00099-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's stated limitation: the correlations are based on cross-sectional data, so it is unknown whether structural characteristics "are the cause or consequence of arithmetic development"; the review reports this as an open question, not a finding.

> "In the absence of longitudinal data, we currently do not know whether these structural variations are the cause or consequence of arithmetic development."
