## Evidence

### Moustafa HAM 2024

Moustafa HAM, Hamid AE, Hassoub G, Kassem AB. (2024). Assessing the impact of critical care training on pharmacy students in Egypt: a pre-post study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06427-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Spearman correlations in Table 9 (n = 72): self-efficacy pre rs = 0.344 (p = 0.003); overall pre rs = 0.370 (p = 0.001). Post-training correlations were not significant (rs = 0.111 and 0.036).

> "The student’s overall knowledge before training was significantly positively correlated with both self-efficacy and overall self-efficacy and self-esteem, with a p-value of less than 0.05 (Table 9)"

### Moustafa HAM 2024 (2)

Moustafa HAM, Hamid AE, Hassoub G, Kassem AB. (2024). Assessing the impact of critical care training on pharmacy students in Egypt: a pre-post study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06427-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`
