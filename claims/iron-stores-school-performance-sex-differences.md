### Samson 2022

Samson, K. L. I., Fischer, J. A. J., & Roche, M. L. (2022). Iron Status, Anemia, and Iron Interventions and Their Associations with Cognitive and Academic Performance in Adolescents: A Systematic Review. Nutrients, 14(1), 224. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu14010224

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review reports one cluster-randomized trial's baseline ferritin analysis: girls with low iron stores had lower reading speed and correct sentences than iron-replete girls, while boys with low iron stores had higher reading speed and correct sentences.

> "low iron stores (defined here as ferritin ≤ 25 µg/L due to the low number of children with iron deficiency as ferritin < 15 µg/L) were associated with worse school performance in girls but not boys (p = 0.033)"
