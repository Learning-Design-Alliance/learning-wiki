## Evidence

### Moyer-Packenham 2014

Moyer-Packenham, P.S., Baker, J., Westenskow, A., Anderson-Pence, K.L., Shumway, J.F., and Jordan, K.E. (2014). Predictors of Achievement When Virtual Manipulatives are Used for Mathematics Instruction. REDIMAT, Vol 3(2), 121-150. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2014.46

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis of fourth-grade retention scores found SES and race predicted VM scores (R2 = .179) while no significant predictors were identified for the CI group, a reversal of the general pattern.

> "For the CI group, there were no significant predictors for retention in Grade 4, while the best fitting model for the VM group included SES and race, F(2, 72)= 9.576, MSE= 2293.532, p< .000, R2= .179."
