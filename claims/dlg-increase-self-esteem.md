# DLG participation is associated with increased self-esteem across primary, special, and adult education

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers in a special education school uniformly perceived improvement in their students' self-esteem, attributed to a climate of trust in the DLG. [→ Ruiz-Eugenio 2023](#ruiz-eugenio-2023)
`q2 i?` Biographical and case studies report increased belief in one's competencies among bullied students, inmates, and immigrant mothers participating in DLGs. [→ Ruiz-Eugenio 2023 (2)](#ruiz-eugenio-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Ruiz-Eugenio 2023

Ruiz-Eugenio, L., Soler-Gallart, M., Racionero-Plaza, S., & Padrós, M. (2023). Dialogic literary gatherings: A systematic review of evidence to overcome social and educational inequalities. Educational Research Review, 39, 100534. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100534

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In the special education school case study (n = 43), "all the teachers of primary and secondary grades and the teachers of the transition course to the world of work perceived an improvement in the self-esteem of their students", linked to a climate of trust where no one laughs at contributions.

> "all the teachers of primary and secondary grades and the teachers of the transition course to the world of work perceived an improvement in the self-esteem of their students"

### Ruiz-Eugenio 2023 (2)

Ruiz-Eugenio, L., Soler-Gallart, M., Racionero-Plaza, S., & Padrós, M. (2023). Dialogic literary gatherings: A systematic review of evidence to overcome social and educational inequalities. Educational Research Review, 39, 100534. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100534

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis for RQ4 states that "The narratives collected in different case studies on the impact of DLG have identified an improvement in believing in one's competencies and consequently an increase in self-esteem", citing studies of a bullied Romani girl, a UK boy, and a Moroccan prison inmate.

> "The narratives collected in different case studies on the impact of DLG have identified an improvement in believing in one's competencies and consequently an increase in self-esteem in students in primary schools, special education, and adult education"
