## Evidence

### Shima Salehi 2019

Shima Salehi, Eric Burkholder, Peter Lepage, Steven Pollock, and Carl Wieman. (2019). The impact of incoming preparation and demographics on performance in Physics I: a multi-institution comparison. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1905.00389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1905.00389)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis across HSEC, HSWC, and PM comparing how the demographic coefficients change as preparation predictors are added (Table II, Fig. 1). The article reports the differential pattern quoted here and concludes multiple measures are needed to characterize incoming preparation, particularly subject-specific ones.

> "Overall, the majority of URM and FG gaps could be explained by math SAT/ACT, while math SAT/ACT was negligible in explaining the gender gap. However, all of the gender gap could be explained by CI pre-score."
