## Evidence

### Mohammad Saber Khaghaninezhad & Ghasem Jafarzadeh 2014

Mohammad Saber Khaghaninezhad & Ghasem Jafarzadeh. (2014). Investigating the Effect of Reduced Forms Instruction on EFL Learners' Listening and Speaking Abilities. English Language Teaching, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n1p159

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-test on the experimental group's fill-in-the-blank dictation test of 20 taped statements; the mean rose from 3.83 to 5.56. The control group's change from 3.98 to 4.10 was not significant (p = 0.161).

> "As Table 8 indicates the experimental group made a significant improvement in the "reduced forms" recognizing post-test (p = .000 < .05)."

### Mohammad Saber Khaghaninezhad & Ghasem Jafarzadeh 2014 (2)

Mohammad Saber Khaghaninezhad & Ghasem Jafarzadeh. (2014). Investigating the Effect of Reduced Forms Instruction on EFL Learners' Listening and Speaking Abilities. English Language Teaching, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n1p159

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing both groups (25 each) on the recognition post-test; experimental mean 5.56 versus control mean 4.10. The article concludes reduced-forms practice was effective in increasing learners' ability to understand reduced forms.

> "there was a significant difference between the participants of two group's performances in the "reduced forms" recognition post-test (p = 0.000 < .05); the experimental group significantly outperformed the control group on the "reduced forms" recognizing post-test"
