Jacquelynne S. Eccles · Allan Wigfield 2024¶

Jacquelynne S. Eccles · Allan Wigfield. (2024). The Development, Testing, and Refinement of Eccles, Wigfield, and Colleagues' Situated Expectancy-Value Model of Achievement Performance and Choice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09888-9

q3 · i? · theoretical · r3

Longitudinal within-classroom study (N = 3000) with four waves across the junior high transition, designed to separate within- and across-year change. The article reports the "strongest declines" occurred across the transition; self-esteem recovered somewhat in seventh grade while ability beliefs kept declining.

"As we had predicted, the strongest declines in students' self-esteem and beliefs about ability/expectancies for success occurred between spring of the pre-transition year to fall of the post-transition year (Wigfield et al., 1991)."
