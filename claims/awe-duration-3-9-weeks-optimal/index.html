q3 · i3 · review · r2

Meta-analytic moderator analysis of intervention duration. The article reports "g = 1.13" and "g = 0.80" for the 3–9-week period and notes inconsistent effects beyond 10 weeks.

"The effectiveness of AWE significantly increases when the duration was in 3–9-week period (Ngo et al., 2022: g = 1.13; Xue, 2024, g = 0.80). When durations beyond 10 weeks, the effects of AWE were inconsistent."
