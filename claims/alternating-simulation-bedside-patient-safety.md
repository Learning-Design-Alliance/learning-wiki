## Evidence

### Yang et al. 2018

Jing Yang, Si-min Huang, Ze-jian Li, Lie Feng & Chun-ting Lu. (2018). Integrating Simulation Scenarios and Clinical Practices Guided by Concepts of Translational Medicine. International Journal of Higher Education, 7(1), 10. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v7n1p10

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive account of the observation group's teaching procedure: students take histories and perform cooperative PE items in the ward, then practice rejected or invasive items, such as bone marrow aspiration, on simulators with immediate graphic-analysis feedback.

> "for those examinations that the patients may reject the demonstration or performance of, such as palpation or percussion of the lung, the heart, or abdomen, the students were directed to the clinical skills comprehensive training center to practice those items on the medical or virtual simulation teaching system"
