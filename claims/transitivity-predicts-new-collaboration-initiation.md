# Transitivity predicts the initiation of new potential collaborations at scientific events

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Selecting someone who is a collaboration partner of one of the researcher's previous collaboration partners was more likely than selecting someone without such a previous relation. [→ Eberle 2021](#eberle-2021)
