Evidence¶

McCarthy Roisin 2026¶

McCarthy Roisin, Maher Liam, O'Dea Angela. (2026). Validation status of definitive airway management simulators: a systematic review. BJA: British Journal of Anaesthesia. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bja.2026.01.027

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review searching PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane Library to September 2025 following PRISMA guidelines; 939 records screened and 17 articles included. The review reports that "most studies assessing only one or two domains" of simulator validity.

"From 939 studies screened, 17 articles met the inclusion criteria, and 29 different simulators were included. Validation domains varied widely, with most studies assessing only one or two domains."

Related Claims¶

  • All included simulation-based research studies were judged to have high or critical risk of bias, and only 16 of 2481 screened studies met Level 4 inclusion criteria — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
