## Evidence

### Garrud Paul 2012

Garrud Paul, Yates Janet. (2012). Profiling strugglers in a graduate-entry medicine course at Nottingham: a retrospective case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-124

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Case-control style comparison of the study group (N = 53) against the comparison group of students making normal progress (N = 309) using archived admissions data. The article reports the odds ratio "OR = 2.03, 95% CI 1.29-3.57" for degree class group; there were no differences in gender (p > 0.10).

> "those with poorer initial degrees (2ii or 3rd class) were more likely to be in the study group (p = 0.017; OR = 2.03, 95% CI 1.29-3.57) than those with 1st or 2i degrees."
