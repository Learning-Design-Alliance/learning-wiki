# Fragmented California educator data systems limit leaders' ability to assess bilingual workforce investments and address shortages

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` State teacher workforce data are dispersed across agencies and reported inconsistently, with limited bilingual-specific reporting on preparation, assignment, or retention. [→ Grayson 2026](#grayson-2026)
`q2 i?` Regional pilot partners had to manually collect and reconcile data from multiple systems, leaving essential workforce insights out of reach even with extensive effort and technical support. [→ Grayson 2026 (2)](#grayson-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Grayson 2026

Grayson, D., & Jacobson, A. (2026). Advancing achievement through biliteracy: Brief #2: State policy levers to support a strong and sustainable bilingual teacher workforce in California. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681323

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Statewide analysis of BLA workforce supply, demand, and assignment trends conducted by WestEd. The analysis found data "dispersed across state agencies" with limited bilingual-specific reporting, preventing assessment of recent investments.

> "Teacher workforce data are dispersed across state agencies and are reported inconsistently, with limited reporting that is specific to bilingual teacher preparation, assignment, or retention."

### Grayson 2026 (2)

Grayson, D., & Jacobson, A. (2026). Advancing achievement through biliteracy: Brief #2: State policy levers to support a strong and sustainable bilingual teacher workforce in California. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681323

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Regional BLA workforce planning pilot in Kern County with county, district, and educator preparation program partners in 2025 workshops. Partners found basic workforce questions required "manually collecting and reconciling data from multiple state and local systems."

> "Answering basic questions about bilingual workforce supply, shortages, or retention required manually collecting and reconciling data from multiple state and local systems such that essential workforce insights remained out of reach, even with extensive effort and technical support."
