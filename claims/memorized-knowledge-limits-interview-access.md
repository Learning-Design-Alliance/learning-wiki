## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2002

Michael C. Wittmann, Rachel E. Scherr. (2002). Epistemology mediating conceptual knowledge: Constraints on data accessible from interviews. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0207077](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0207077)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of phase 1 of the interview, in which Sarah answered conductor/insulator questions with "Memorized it!" and referred questions about bound electrons to organic chemistry, saying "I have no idea!" The authors report her approach "constrains the inferences that can be made" about her knowledge of electric charge flow.

> "the interviewer's insight into Sarah's reasoning is constrained by her nearly exclusive use of memorized knowledge and the recitation of ideas from other classes with no further explanation."
