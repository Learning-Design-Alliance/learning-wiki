Deployment record of the second air:bit round: 11 schools, 164 students, 62 kits built, used in four different subjects across all three upper secondary years. During spring 2018 students uploaded more than 400,000 unique measurements to the online web application; the largest class compared air pollution levels on kindergarten playgrounds.

> "The second round was in spring 2018 with 11 participating schools, and 164 students from across Northern Norway. In the second round, the participating teachers chose to use the air:bit project in four different subjects, from all three years of upper secondary school. The 164 students built in total 62 air:bit sensor kits."
