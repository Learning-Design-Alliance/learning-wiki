# Student comments directing AI code generation are dominated by What comments, with How comments second and Why comments rare

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across four cohorts, 77.0% of analyzed comments were What comments, 21.0% How comments, and only 0.4% Why comments. [→ rq1-dist](#rq1-dist)

## Evidence

### rq1-dist

Nasser Giacaman, Valerio Terragni, Paul Denny, and Viraj Kumar. 2026. Commenting with Copilot: A Taxonomy and Multi-Year Analysis of Student Code-Generation Specifications. Proceedings of the 2nd ACM Virtual Global Computing Education Conference (SIGCSE Virtual 2026). https://doi.org/10.1145/3795867.3830978

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Aggregate analysis of LLM-classified comments from a four-year Copilot activity (1,161 students, 52,172 analyzed comment instances). The article reports "comments were dominated byWhatcomments (77.0%)" with How at 21.0% and Why at 0.4%; no effect size is printed.

> "Overall, comments were dominated byWhatcomments (77.0%), followed byHowcomments (21.0%), whileWhycomments were rare (0.4%)."
