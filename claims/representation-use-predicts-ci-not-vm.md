## Evidence

### Moyer-Packenham 2014

Moyer-Packenham, P.S., Baker, J., Westenskow, A., Anderson-Pence, K.L., Shumway, J.F., and Jordan, K.E. (2014). Predictors of Achievement When Virtual Manipulatives are Used for Mathematics Instruction. REDIMAT, Vol 3(2), 121-150. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2014.46

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Ethogram-based regression analysis of third-grade representation use found pictorial-individual and symbolic-individual predicted CI Post-test 1 scores (R2 = .911), while no significant predictors were found for the VM group.

> "A linear regression analysis identified two significant predictors of Post-test 1 scores in the CI group, PI and SI, F(2,8) = 14.58, p  = .005, R2 = .911."

### Moyer-Packenham 2014 (2)

Moyer-Packenham, P.S., Baker, J., Westenskow, A., Anderson-Pence, K.L., Shumway, J.F., and Jordan, K.E. (2014). Predictors of Achievement When Virtual Manipulatives are Used for Mathematics Instruction. REDIMAT, Vol 3(2), 121-150. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2014.46

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Ethogram-based regression analysis of fourth-grade representation use found pictorial-group use predicted CI Post-test 1 scores (R2 = .719), while no significant predictors were found for the VM group.

> "The fourth -grade analysis identified one significant predictor of Post -test 1 scores in the CI group: PG, F(1,1)= 6.414, p= .045, R2= .719."
