## Evidence

### Carlo Maria Carbonaro 2020

Carlo Maria Carbonaro, Alessia Zurru, Viviana Fanti, Matteo Tuveri, Gianluca Usai. (2020). Cooperative Problem Solving: an experience of high-school teaching updating. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison of mean vote distributions between students from scientific (84% of the 98-student sample, 8 schools) and classical (16%, 1 school) high schools. "students coming from scientific high school obtained better results with respect to their classical colleagues".

> "What emerges from our analysis is that students coming from scientific high school obtained better results with respect to their classical colleagues."

### Carlo Maria Carbonaro 2020 (2)

Carlo Maria Carbonaro, Alessia Zurru, Viviana Fanti, Matteo Tuveri, Gianluca Usai. (2020). Cooperative Problem Solving: an experience of high-school teaching updating. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same school-type comparison focused on the evaluation step of the CPS scheme. "students coming from classical high schools have obtained zero in this step"; for scientific schools the result confirmed the group-level difficulty with evaluation.

> "students coming from classical high schools have obtained zero in this step, meaning they are not able to evaluate their results at all."
