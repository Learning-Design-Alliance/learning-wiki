Cross-sectional UK survey of 302 respondents from a random sample of 500 GPs, reported in the systematic review. "90% of 302 respondents claimed to integrate evidence and clinical experience." The review notes this study was vulnerable to responder and non-responder bias. A Turkish survey found 89% disagreed that EBM devalues clinical experience.
