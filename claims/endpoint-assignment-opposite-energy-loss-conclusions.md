`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive analysis of the Group One spreadsheet approach, in which velocity references the current and immediately previous position row. The article reports the resulting chart shows "a steady decay in total energy, suggesting drag as the main source of energy loss."

> "When combined with a normalized potential energy of g*h, this will generate the chart shown in figure 1, this shows a steady decay in total energy, suggesting drag as the main source of energy loss"

### David Marasco 2020 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive analysis of the Group Two spreadsheet approach, in which the first velocity references positions from the second and third rows. The article reports the graph suggests "mechanical energy is lost on the bounces" and that energy increases in flight.

> "This approach generates a graph as in figure 2, suggesting that mechanical energy is lost on the bounces.  And that energy increases while the ball is in flight between bounces!"
