## Evidence

### Alkhammash L 2025

Alkhammash L, Saraco AN, Goes Schaurich C, Lo BSY, Krebs C. (2025). The impact of an immersive virtual reality anatomy lab for informal science education. Anat Sci Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70087

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Series of MANOVAs testing the categorical variable prior VR experience against IMMS, CLQ, IPQ, and SSQ scores in the study cohort. The article reports "no statistically significant impact on the survey scores with the exception of a sense of virtual presence"; equivalence was not tested and no effect sizes are printed.

> "prior VR experience, regardless of whether that experience was in the context of anatomy education or not, had no statistically significant impact on the survey scores with the exception of a sense of virtual presence (IPQ, p < 0.05)"
