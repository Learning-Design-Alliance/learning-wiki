# No statistically significant difference between inquiry and pre-inquiry semesters on survey constructs, with only two inquiry activities among 18 labs

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was not a statistically significant difference in means between the two semesters, and the authors state they cannot reliably deduce the effectiveness of adding inquiry because only two of 18 labs were inquiry-based. [→ McConnell 2010](#mcconnell-2010)

## Evidence

### McConnell 2010

McConnell, N. J., Medling, A. M., Strubbe, L. E., Moth, P., Montgomery, R. M., Raschke, L. M., Hunter, L., & Goza, B. (2010). A College-Level Inquiry-Based Laboratory Activity on Transiting Planets. ASP Conference Series. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1009.3940](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1009.3940)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of pre/post survey means between the Spring 2009 (pre-inquiry) and Fall 2009 (with inquiry) cohorts; no statistically significant between-semester difference. Equivalence was not tested; the dose of inquiry was two activities out of 18 total labs.

> "There is, however, not a statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence in the means between the two semesters. Given that during the period of th is assessment, only two inquiry activities were introduced out of 18 total labs, we c annot reliably deduce the level of eﬀectiveness of adding inquiry to the laboratory curriculum."
