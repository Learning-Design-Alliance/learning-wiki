Royel M. Johnson 2026¶

Royel M. Johnson. (2026). Reframing Belonging in Higher Education: an Intersectional Ecological Model for Research, Policy, and Practice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10104-5

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The essay reports, citing Brown et al. (2022), a survey study of more than 1,000 undergraduates examining how national racial violence and #BlackLivesMatter protests shaped STEM students' belonging and confidence, illustrating macrosystem-level effects reverberating into higher education.

"Using survey data from more than 1,000 undergraduates, the study found that Black and Latinx students were significantly more likely than their white peers to report declines in belonging and confidence following widely publicized incidents of police violence, while most white students reported little to no impact."
