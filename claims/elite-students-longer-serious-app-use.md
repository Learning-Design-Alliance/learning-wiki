## Evidence

### Lee Jea Woog 2022

Lee Jea Woog, Park Sung Je, Kim Soyeon, Chung Un Sun, Han Doug Hyun. (2022). The Association Between Temperament and Characteristics, Smartphone App Use Patterns and Academic Performance of University Students. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2022.37.e143

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Four-week smartphone log comparison of app use time between elite (n = 37) and general (n = 114) groups. "elite students used serious apps for longer than general students" (z = 2.79, P = 0.009), while total use time did not differ significantly; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in the total smartphone usage time between elite and general students (z = 1.42, P = 0.164). However, elite students used serious apps for longer than general students (z = 2.79, P = 0.009)"
