Case study of two UiO students (materials science and physics) who scored 22 (high agency). They researched actual railguns for physically meaningful parameters, built a model with air resistance and coriolis force, refined it iteratively, and discussed assumptions and limitations. 'The students defined a novel question, based in a real-life context, and built a sophisticated multi-step model to investigate it,' scoring 2 on all categories except code efficiency and new packages.
