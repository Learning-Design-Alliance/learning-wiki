# Introductory accounting students who completed a group-based IF-AT scratch-off card review scored significantly higher on midterm objective exams than students in lecture-review control sections

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students in treatment sections using scratch-off cards in team review outperformed control students on the objective portion of two midterm exams (mean difference over 12 points, p = 0.0013). [→ Lawrence C. Mohrweis & Kathe M. Shinham 2015](#lawrence-c-mohrweis-kathe-m-shinham-2015)

## Evidence

### Lawrence C. Mohrweis & Kathe M. Shinham 2015

Lawrence C. Mohrweis & Kathe M. Shinham. (2015). Enhancing Students' Learning: Instant Feedback Cards. American Journal Of Business Education – First Quarter 2015 Volume 8, Number 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1053380

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A section-level experiment at a southwest US public university: 100 treatment students did a 20-item team scratch-off review before two midterms; 73 control students had the same review questions with instructor-led review. Table 1 reports mean scores of 145.23 (SD 26.35) versus 133.11 (SD 23.44), with the difference "significant (p = 0.0013, Table 1)". No effect size is printed.

> "The mean difference in mid -term scores of the two groups was over 12 points and the difference was significant (p = 0.0013, Table 1)."
