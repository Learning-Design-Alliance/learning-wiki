## Evidence

### Molabe Manoko 2026

Molabe Manoko, Rasesemola Richard, Matlala Sidwell, Matshaka Lerato. (2026). Virtual simulation as a catalyst for equitable and authentic interprofessional learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09773-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the focus groups, participants described scenarios that escalated complexity after correct initial management and role assignments built to target identified weaknesses, e.g. being placed as team lead in a chaotic emergency room scenario. The authors note the study did not directly evaluate algorithm-driven adaptive systems.

> "After I correctly managed the initial airway, the patient's condition deteriorated into a complex sepsis scenario, which felt like the system was challenging me specifically based on my skill level. It pushed me right to the edge of my competence without being overwhelming"
