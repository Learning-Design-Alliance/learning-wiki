Hardon Sem F., Horeman Tim, Bonjer H. Jaap, Meijerink W. J. H. Jeroen. (2018). Force-based learning curve tracking in fundamental laparoscopic skills training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-6090-7

Pre- versus post-course paired comparison of ForceSense motion and time metrics in six novices; e.g. task 1 time fell from 204.09 ± 65.12 s to 78.08 ± 14.22 s (p = 0.004) and task 5 from 216.89 ± 54.81 s to 65.07 ± 18.30 s (p < 0.001), as the article prints "Lower instrument handling outcomes (i.e., time and motion parameters) were observed in five tasks".
