## Evidence

### Apekshya Ghimire and Chandralekha Singh 2025

Apekshya Ghimire and Chandralekha Singh. (2025). Reflections of quantum educators on strategies to diversify the second quantum revolution. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1119/5.0223983](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0223983)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interview reflection from the female educator, exemplified by her own interactions in physics with men who knew less but portrayed themselves as pinnacles of brilliance. She worried this inequitable culture "will seep into this new field of QIST".

> "She felt that the only way to diversify QIST is to change the systemic cultural issues in physics and other related disciplines that make up QIST in which brilliance is over-emphasized and many individuals from the dominant group are pretending and portraying to know more than they do"

### Apekshya Ghimire and Chandralekha Singh 2025 (2)

Apekshya Ghimire and Chandralekha Singh. (2025). Reflections of quantum educators on strategies to diversify the second quantum revolution. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1119/5.0223983](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0223983)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview reflection from a male educator who said "I don't see things as automatically getting fixed just because we rebranded" and urged pushing back against inequitable culture. These are expert opinions, not measured outcomes.

> "He did not think that QIST being a new field makes it easier to diversify than the sub-fields such as physics, “I do think that there's in some sense…we could just like start over afresh, because this [QIST] is a new field, and we don't carry over the baggage…I'm not sure that it's succeeding so far”"
