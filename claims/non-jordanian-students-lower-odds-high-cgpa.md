`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression (Table 3) in the 1,209-student survey; non-Jordanians were 16.6% of the sample. The adjusted odds ratio of 0.3 (95% CI 0.2–0.5, p < 0.001) compares non-Jordanian to Jordanian students on the outcome cGPA > 3.5.

> "Additionally, non-Jordanian students were less likely to achieve a cGPA > 3.5 compared to Jordanian students (aOR = 0.3; 95% CI: 0.2–0.5; p < 0.001)."
