## Evidence

### Zafra-Tanaka JH 2018

Zafra-Tanaka JH, Hurtado-Villanueva ME, Saenz-Naranjo MdP, Taype-Rondan A. (2018). Self-perceived competence in early diagnosis of cervical cancer among recently graduated physicians from Lima, Peru. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203778

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Poisson regression with robust variance on the same 432-physician cross-sectional survey, estimating prevalence ratios for clerkship health care system. The armed forces/police versus MINSA contrast gave "PR: 1.32, 95% CI: 1.11–1.58" for VIA and PR 1.31 (1.08–1.59) for VILI; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Those who performed their gynecology/obstetric clerkship at the armed forces/police hospitals had a higher prevalence of self-perceived adequate competencies in VIA (PR: 1.32, 95% CI: 1.11–1.58) and VILI (PR: 1.31, 95% CI: 1.08–1.59) compared to those who performed their gynecology/obstetric clerkship in MINSA hospitals"
