q1 · i? · review · r2

This is the report's own summary assertion, supported by its description of scoring approaches in programs such as AP, IB, and NAEP that train raters, use multiple raters, and audit samples; the report presents no new empirical analysis of reliability.

"When they are designed appropriately and used in conjunction with other measures, performance assessments can be reliably scored, generating trustworthy and comparable scores at the student and aggregate levels."
