# Step-level OSCE checklist performance is similar between video and simulation groups after Bonferroni correction

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No individual CPR checklist step differed significantly in correct-execution rate between groups once the Bonferroni-corrected alpha (0.0017) was applied. [→ Telli Serkan 2026](#telli-serkan-2026)

## Evidence

### Telli Serkan 2026

Telli Serkan, Camgöz Süleyman, Yaman Merve, Arslan Seyda Ferah. (2026). Facilitated interactive video versus simulation-based training after a standardized lecture for basic life support in medical students: a randomized trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09128-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Step-level chi-square/Fisher comparisons of 30 OSCE checklist items between groups, with Bonferroni correction for 30 comparisons. Only Step2 favored the video group uncorrected (p < 0.05); after correction "the difference did not remain significant".

> "Although in the initial analysis only the rate of correct execution of Step2, “Gently shake the patient’s shoulders and shout loudly — ‘Are you okay?’,” was significantly higher in the video group than in the simulation group (p < 0.05), after applying the Bonferroni correction (α = 0.0017), the difference did not remain significant."
