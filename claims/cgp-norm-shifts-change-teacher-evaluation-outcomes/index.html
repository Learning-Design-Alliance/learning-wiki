Evidence¶

Hashim 2026¶

Hashim, A. K. (2026). How post-COVID norm shifts change the meaning of MAP Growth metrics for school accountability. NWEA White Paper. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/how-post%e2%80%91covid-norm-shifts-change-the-meaning-of-map-growth-metrics-for-school-accountability/

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The paper's illustrative reading example in the teacher-evaluation section: the same sixth-grade RIT gain corresponds to a CGP of the 19th percentile under 2020 norms and the 22nd under 2025 norms. Illustrative, no effect size printed.

"a sixth-grade student with a fall RIT score of 200 who grows 3 points by spring would place at the 19th percentile for CGP under 2020 norms, but under the 2025 norms, that same RIT score gain corresponds to the 22nd percentile."

Hashim 2026 (2)¶

Hashim, A. K. (2026). How post-COVID norm shifts change the meaning of MAP Growth metrics for school accountability. NWEA White Paper. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/how-post%e2%80%91covid-norm-shifts-change-the-meaning-of-map-growth-metrics-for-school-accountability/

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The paper's illustrative math example: the same 8-point gain yields a CGP of the 50th percentile under 2020 norms versus the 34th under 2025 norms, which the paper attributes to higher national growth expectations.

"a sixth-grade student with a fall RIT score of 240 who grows 8 points by spring would place at the 50th percentile under the prior, 2020 norms. But under the 2025 norms, the same RIT score gain only corresponds to a CGP of the 34th percentile."
