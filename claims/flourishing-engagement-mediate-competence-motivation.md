## Evidence

### Chen Xiang 2025

Chen Xiang, Samad Sarminah, Kim Wansoo. (2025). Teacher competence and students’ motivation for learning in Chinese higher education: mediating roles of psychological flourishing and student engagement, and the moderating role of AI integration. BMC Psychology, 13, 1378. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03674-0

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r?`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (5,000 resamples) in the PLS-SEM model confirmed the flourishing pathway, "indirect β = 0.125, t = 4.915, p < 0.001", with 95% CI excluding zero.

> "ELF mediated the effect of ETC on SELM (H4: indirect β = 0.125, t = 4.915, p < 0.001), while ELE also mediated this link (H7: indirect β = 0.074, t = 4.222, p < 0.001)"

### Chen Xiang 2025 (2)

Chen Xiang, Samad Sarminah, Kim Wansoo. (2025). Teacher competence and students’ motivation for learning in Chinese higher education: mediating roles of psychological flourishing and student engagement, and the moderating role of AI integration. BMC Psychology, 13, 1378. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03674-0

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

The same bootstrapped analysis confirmed the engagement mediation pathway, "indirect β = 0.074, t = 4.222, p < 0.001"; both mediations were partial because the direct effect remained significant.

> "ELF mediated the effect of ETC on SELM (H4: indirect β = 0.125, t = 4.915, p < 0.001), while ELE also mediated this link (H7: indirect β = 0.074, t = 4.222, p < 0.001)"
