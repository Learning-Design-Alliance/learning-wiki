## Evidence

### Day 2017

Day, Stephanie L.; Connor, Carol M. (2017). Examining the relations between self-regulation and achievement in third grade students. Assessment for Effective Intervention, 42(2), 97-109. https://doi.org/10.1177/1534508416670367

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Across the HLM models of spring mathematics and reading outcomes in the 282-student sample, the 'wait for go' variable was tested and then dropped. The authors report it "was not a significant predictor in any of the academic models"; no effect size is printed, so this is a non-detection.

> "Children’s ability to wait for “go” was not a significant predictor in any of the academic models and was thus removed from the models."
