Evidence¶

Noël M-P and Rousselle L 2011¶

Noël M-P and Rousselle L (2011) Developmental changes in the profiles of dyscalculia: an explanation based on a double exact-and-approximate number representation model. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 5:165. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2011.00165

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Narrative review synthesis ordering non-symbolic comparison studies by participants' age. The review reports a dissociation between younger (6–9 years) and older (10+) samples, with only the latter showing significant differences; no effect sizes are printed.

"for tasks using non-symbolic numbers, a dissociation appears between the studies which tested younger (6–9 years old) versus older children (10 years old and above): only the latter showed some signiﬁcant difference"
