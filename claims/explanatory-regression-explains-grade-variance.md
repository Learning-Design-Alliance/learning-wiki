## Evidence

### Saqr M 2018

Saqr M, Fors U, Nouri J. (2018). Using social network analysis to understand online Problem-Based Learning and predict performance. PLoS ONE 13(9): e0203590. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203590

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Explanatory stepwise backward multivariable linear regression (RQ2, Table 5) controlling for previous GPA, age and gender in 215 students. The model reached "adjusted R2 of the final model (5th step) was 0.75"; tutor out-degree and group size were negative predictors.

> "The adjusted R2 of the final model (5th step) was 0.75, F (9,185) = 66.7, P<0.01). In addition to previous performance and female gender, the factors that reflected student interactivity such as density, clustering, and social capital were the most significant positive predictors of performance."
