Sunaina Sharma 2026¶

Sunaina Sharma. (2026). Educational integrity in GenAI-augmented assessment: making judgement visible. International Journal for Educational Integrity 22:6. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00216-y

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Conceptual-theoretical argument, not an empirical result. The paper distinguishes judgement from reflection (Schön) and metacognition (Zimmerman), calling it "a normative evaluative act exercised under conditions of epistemic uncertainty". No data are reported; the paper explicitly states it does not report empirical findings.

"Judgement is defined here as the learner’s capacity to weigh options, justify academic choices, and assume responsibility in relation to disciplinary standards and ethical expectations"
