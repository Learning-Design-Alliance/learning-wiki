Evidence¶

Jeroen Janssen 2008¶

Jeroen Janssen, Gijsbert Erkens, Paul A. Kirschner, Gellof Kanselaar. (2008). Effects of representational guidance during computer-supported collaborative learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9078-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multilevel analyses of students' logged online activity in the 124-student study; the article reports no significant condition effects on any of the four activity measures, so outcome differences cannot be attributed to activity volume.

"No signiﬁcant effects of condition on time online ( b =- 1.46, p = .16), adding ( b = 0.00, p = .99), editing (b = 0.17, p = .80), and deleting elements ( b =- 0.10, p = .72) in the Debate-tool were found using multilevel analyses."
