## Evidence

### Gutiérrez-González R 2023

Gutiérrez-González R, Zamarron A, Royuela A, Rodriguez-Boto G. (2023). Flipped classroom applied to Neurosurgery in undergraduate medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04158-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of four teaching units in the 2021/2022 academic year: TU 4 (69 students) used a flipped classroom, TUs 1-3 (187 students) traditional lectures, all sitting the same simultaneous multiple-choice exam. The flipped unit's mean score was "significantly higher in TU 4 (16.88/26)"; Table 1 reports Student-T t = -2.0370, p = 0.043.

> "Finally, the mean score obtained in the specific area of Neurosurgery was significantly higher in TU 4 (16.88/26) compared to the rest of the TUs (15.59/26)."
