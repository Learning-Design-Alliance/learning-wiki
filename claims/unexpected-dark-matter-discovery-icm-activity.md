### Cantalupo 2022

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The author's facilitator account of the ICM X-ray emission activity, in which students testing the Bremsstrahlung hypothesis found implied gas temperatures 'on the order of 10 million degrees Kelvin'. This is a design-based practitioner report, not a controlled study, and no effect size is printed.

> "they invariably arrive at a similar possible conclusion: much more matter than what is visible must be present in order for the gas to be so hot ! This unexpected result , which can be confirmed through several other independent experiments, could imply the presence of "dark matter""
