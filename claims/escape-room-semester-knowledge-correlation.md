## Evidence

### Baessler F 2025

Baessler F, Hornung TP, Schuster TJ, Hardt N, Felter E, Anzenberger E, Ely DR, Lichtenstein T, Steiner K, Koelkebeck K, Flasbeck V, Spitzer P, Speerforck S, Pinilla S, Khatamzas E, Mueller B, Zafar A. `q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Two-sided Pearson correlational analysis of demographic variables in the 45-student evaluation found "The students' semester level was positively correlated" with both test scores, while the semester–gain association was non-significant (equivalence not tested).

> "The students' semester level was positively correlated with higher pre-test (r =.536, p <.001, 95% CI [0.288, 0.717]) and post-test (r =.411, p =.006, 95% CI [0.134, 0.629]) scores, but not with greater knowledge gain from the intervention (r=-.153, p =.322, 95% CI [−0.427, 0.147])."
