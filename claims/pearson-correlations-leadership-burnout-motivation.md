`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis using SPSS 28.0 on the 403-respondent dataset, providing preliminary evidence for subsequent SEM. All correlations were significant at p < 0.001, with "learning burnout was significantly negatively correlated with learning motivation (r = − 0.436)".

> "benevolent leadership was significantly negatively correlated with learning burnout (r = − 0.405, p < 0.001) and significantly positively correlated with learning motivation (r = 0.303, p < 0.001). Moral leadership was significantly negatively correlated with learning burnout (r = − 0.419, p < 0.001) and significantly positively correlated with learning motivation (r = 0.395, p < 0.001). Authoritarian leadership was significantly positively correlated with learning burnout (r = 0.464, p < 0.001) and significantly negatively correlated with learning motivation (r = − 0.362, p < 0.001). Learning burnout was significantly negatively correlated with learning motivation (r = − 0.436, p < 0.001)."
