McCullough 2022¶

Observational analysis of CSSS responses on the three-item Respect for Diversity scale; the brief reports statistically significant variations by race or ethnicity, with mean agreement for all staff close to 90 percent. No effect size is printed.

"As with the Instructional Equity scale, staff identifying as African American had more negative perceptions of respect for diversity in their school settings."
