Fueling Brains 2024¶

Fueling Brains. (2024). Fueling Brains. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/fueling-brains/

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

A two-year quasi-experimental study with a matched sample of 352 pre-kindergarten students (174 Fueling Brains, 178 control) in a Southeast Texas district. Adjusting for pretests, the program group showed a rapid vocabulary advantage with "effect sizes = +0.18".

"Fueling Brains students significantly outperformed peers on the CIRCLE assessment's rapid vocabulary and phonological awareness subtests (effect sizes = +0.18 and +0.26 respectively)"
