## Evidence

### Al Rumayyan Ahmed 2018

Al Rumayyan Ahmed, Ahmed Nasr, Al Subait Reem, Al Ghamdi Ghassan, Mohammed Mahzari Moeber, Awad Mohamed Tarig, Rotgans Jerome I., Donmez Mustafa, Mamede Silvia, Schmidt Henk G. (2018). Teaching clinical reasoning through hypothetico-deduction is (slightly) better than self-explanation in tutorial groups: An experimental study. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-018-0409-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive observation from the randomized experiment: pooled test-phase accuracy was low, with the hypothetico-deduction condition at mean 0.22 (SD 0.14) and self-explanation at mean 0.17 (SD 0.12). The authors state the achievements were "well under the achievements of students in similar studies", and attribute this to participants' possibly insufficient knowledge.

> "With mean scores around 0.20 on a scale between 0 and 1, our participants’ achievements were well under the achievements of students in similar studies [20, 21]."
