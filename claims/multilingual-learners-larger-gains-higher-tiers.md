## Evidence

### Sussman 2022

Sussman, J., Melnick, H., Newton, E., Kriener-Althen, K., Draney, K., Mangione, P., & Gochyyev, P. (2022). Preschool quality and child development: How are learning gains related to program ratings? Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/california-preschool-quality-ratings [doi:10.54300/422.974](https://doi.org/10.54300/422.974)

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Regression analysis by multilingual learner status across three DRDP domains; Table 2 reports effect sizes up to 0.19 SD for multilingual learners in Tier 5 mathematics. The article notes the larger-gain pattern was "not statistically significant."

> "Multilingual learners in Tier 5 programs gained an additional 2.6–2.8 months of learning and development compared to multilingual learners in Tier 3 programs. In addition, attendance in a higher-tier program was associated with relatively larger gains for multilingual learners than non-multilingual learners in each area"
