## Evidence

### Acaray Ali 2026

Acaray Ali. (2026). How digital stress affects academic procrastination and subjective well-being: mediating effect of ego depletion. BMC Psychology, 14, 449. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04164-7

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis in the same 768-student survey. The article reports correlations only for approval anxiety, FOMO, and connection overload with well-being, with "FOMO (r=-0.31, p < 0.01)" the strongest; no correlation is printed for availability stress or online vigilance.

> "Several digital stress dimensions were negatively related to psychological well-being (approval anxiety (r=-0.22, p < 0.01), FOMO (r=-0.31, p < 0.01), connection overload (r=-0.07, p < 0.05))."
