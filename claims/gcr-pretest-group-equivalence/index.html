Evidence¶

Hamidreza Fatemipour & Shiva Hemmati 2015¶

Hamidreza Fatemipour & Shiva Hemmati. (2015). Impact of Consciousness-Raising Activities on Young English Language Learners’ Grammar Performance. English Language Teaching, 8(9). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n9p1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent samples t test on the 60-item researcher-made grammar pretest (Levene F = 2.1, p = 0.15; t = 0.34, df = 58, p = 0.73). Pretest means were 26.13 (control) and 25.63 (experimental). The article concludes the groups "belonged to the same population."

"the probability associated with the t-observed value (0.73) was higher than the significant level of (0.05). It was safely concluded that the two groups belonged to the same population in terms of their grammar performance before the treatment."
