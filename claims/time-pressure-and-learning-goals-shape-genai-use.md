## Evidence

### Karl Henrik Fredly 2026

Karl Henrik Fredly, Tor Ole B. Odden and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2026). How undergraduate physics students use generative AI for computational modeling. [doi:10.1103/vg1v-dkrh](https://doi.org/10.1103/vg1v-dkrh)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview analysis of pragmatic objectives found "Around half the students noted time-pressure as an important influence"; some felt they lost learning opportunities, indicating understanding risks alone did not stop unhelpful use.

> "Around half the students noted time-pressure as an important influence on how they used genAI. For these students genAI was seen as a helpful tool for saving time, especially when it came to writing code."

### Karl Henrik Fredly 2026 (2)

Karl Henrik Fredly, Tor Ole B. Odden and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2026). How undergraduate physics students use generative AI for computational modeling. [doi:10.1103/vg1v-dkrh](https://doi.org/10.1103/vg1v-dkrh)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of epistemic objectives found every interviewed student highlighted understanding arguments, equations and code; because of this focus on learning, "Students therefore tried moderating their genAI use", some using genAI mainly for explanations rather than solutions.

> "Students therefore tried moderating their genAI use, so that it would improve their learning, or at least not hinder it."
