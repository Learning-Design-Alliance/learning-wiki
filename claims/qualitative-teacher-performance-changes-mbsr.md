## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Thematic analysis of teacher interviews identified four themes of perceived professional performance change: wiser engagement, greater self-efficacy, better emotion regulation, and greater resilience. [→ Rupprecht 2017](#rupprecht-2017)

## Evidence

### Rupprecht 2017

Rupprecht, S., Paulus, P. & Walach, H. (2017). Mind the teachers! The impact of mindfulness training on self-regulation and classroom performance in a sample of German school teachers. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(4), 565-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.4.565

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative interviews with intervention teachers, thematically coded by the main author. Teachers described setting boundaries, pausing before reacting, using bodily awareness, and using mindfulness to switch off from stressors — e.g. "I improved in setting boundaries and delegating tasks."

> "Teachers reported engaging more wisely in work demands, terminating activities that cause stress while finding more space to teach creatively."
