## Evidence

### Yibo Meng 2026

Yibo Meng, Ruiqi Chen, Zhiming Liu, and Xiaolan Ding. (2026). TibetCPR: A Multimodal Tactile Feedback System to Enhance Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Training in High-Altitude Regions of Tibet. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07765 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.07765](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.07765)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled study with 40 participants; depth accuracy was the proportion of compressions in the 50–60 mm guideline range. Experimental accuracy rose from M=0.277 to M=0.558 while control was essentially unchanged; ANCOVA gave F(1,37)=203.17, partial η²=.846.

> "A direct between-group comparison of the change scores ( Δ=𝑇 3−𝑇 1) confirmed that the experimental gain was substantially larger than the control change,𝑡( 38)= 11.56,𝑝<. 001, Cohen's 𝑑= 3.66"
