## Evidence

### Pratim Sengupta 2014

Pratim Sengupta, Gokul Krishnan and Mason Wright. (2014). Integrated STEM in Elementary Grades Using Distributed Agent-based Computation. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1402.7252](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1402.7252)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of intermediate and final Phase 3 products before and after user testing. Final designs of "six out of seven groups allowed users to draw multiple shapes", a change the authors attribute directly to user testing.

> "We found that the final designs of six out of seven groups allowed users to draw multiple shapes, whereas their initial designs during Phase 3 were more constrained and could only generate a specific shape (typically, a circle)."

### Pratim Sengupta 2014 (2)

Pratim Sengupta, Gokul Krishnan and Mason Wright. (2014). Integrated STEM in Elementary Grades Using Distributed Agent-based Computation. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1402.7252](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1402.7252)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative findings across all groups in the design study: a UCD focus "resulted in students refining both the software and hardware components of their programs by engaging in iterative design cycles", with users testing designs at three stages (self, peers, parents).

> "a focus on User -Centered Design resulted in students refining both the software and hardware components of their programs by engaging in iterative design cycles"

### Pratim Sengupta 2014 (3)

Pratim Sengupta, Gokul Krishnan and Mason Wright. (2014). Integrated STEM in Elementary Grades Using Distributed Agent-based Computation. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1402.7252](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1402.7252)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case observations from the same study: "the reliability of the output of their ViMAP program also improved as a result of improvement of their physical structures", e.g., Chuck added a flat paper strip and Jerry a wider plate.

> "In some cases we also noticed that the reliability of the output of their ViMAP program also improved as a result of improvement of their physical structures."
