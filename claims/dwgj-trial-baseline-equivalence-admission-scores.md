## Evidence

### Shao Wenming 2025

Shao Wenming, Cheng Xin, Gu Wanjie, Yan Yu, Liu Hiu, Zhang Jialin, Zhang Li, Yin Haiyan, Zhang Mingya. (2025). A combined approach of simulation-based "debriefing with good judgment" and case-based learning to enhance clinical thinking in Chinese residents. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1718961

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison in the randomized trial found "no significant differences were observed" on admission examinations: theory 360.29 ± 13.13 vs 364.66 ± 8.67 (t = −1.64, p = 0.11); skill 80.11 ± 4.66 vs 78.89 ± 4.55 (t = 1.12, p = 0.27).

> "The theoretical and skill-based admission examination scores were compared between the two groups, and no significant differences were observed (Theory: Control 360.29 ± 13.13 vs DwGJ 364.66 ± 8.67; t = −1.64, p = 0.11; Skill: Control 80.11 ± 4.66 vs DwGJ: 78.89 ± 4.55; t = 1.12, p = 0.27)."
