Synthesis of pedagogical benefits across the 58 included studies (Section 5.3.1, Figure 5). The review reports "Personalized Feedback (44.83%, 26/58)" as the top benefit, followed by learning gains (32.76%), time efficiency (29.31%), debugging support (20.69%), and 24/7 availability (15.52%), with LLMs facilitating self-paced learning and student autonomy.
