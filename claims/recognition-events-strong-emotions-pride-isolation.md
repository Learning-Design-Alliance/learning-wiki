Concurrent phenomenological analysis of STEM career aspirations in underrepresented youth: Role of experiences and identity [Presentation]. 2025 NARST Annual International Conference, National Harbor, MD. `q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic finding from the interview analysis: Mercy described her math teacher never caring about her struggles and felt less motivated to join STEM clubs due to isolation; Sam felt less connected to mathematics when a teacher overlooked her competence.

> "misrecognition events were also associated with strong emotions, but rather than a sense of pride, students described feelings of isolation and rejection."
