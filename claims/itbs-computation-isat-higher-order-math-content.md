## Evidence

### Easton 2003

Easton, J. Q., Correa, M., Luppescu, S., Park, H.-S., Ponisciak, S., Rosenkranz, T., & Sporte, S. (2003). How Do They Compare? ITBS and ISAT Reading and Mathematics in the Chicago Public Schools, 1999 to 2002. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org/publications/briefs/onlineresources.html

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Documentary comparison of the content/process classifications of items on the two mathematics tests (Table 3), qualified by the authors' note that ISAT double-counts items across areas, making strict comparison difficult. The article reports "the ITBS is much more heavily weighted toward computation."

> "This difficulty aside, it is clear that the ITBS is much more heavily weighted toward computation, and that the ISAT contains up to three times as many items on algebra, geometry, measurement, and probability and statistics."
