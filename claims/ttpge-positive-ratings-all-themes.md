## Evidence

### Sayı 2018

Sayı, A. K. (2018). Teachers’ Views about the Teacher Training Program for Gifted Education. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n4p262

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of a researcher-developed questionnaire completed by 71 teachers after the certificate program. All five theme means exceeded 5.60 on the 6-point scale, e.g. program “M: 5.76, SD: 0.47” and pedagogical qualifications “M: 5.82, SD: 0.53”.

> "About the Teacher Training Program for Gifted Education, when the opinions of the teachers regarding the effectiveness of the program are evaluated over 6 points, it is seen that the five areas under evaluation are around and above the score of “5.60”."
