q3 · i? · review · r2

A study of Harlem Children's Zone charter schools, which provide expanded learning time, integrated student supports, and active parent engagement, compared academic outcomes of lottery winners with applicants not selected, using a post-hoc random admissions lottery analysis with statistical controls (ESSA Tier 1 or 2).

"They found HCZ students scored significantly higher on math and reading tests than students who attended other schools, in both third and eighth grades."
