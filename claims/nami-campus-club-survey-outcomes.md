## Evidence

### WestEd 2024

WestEd. (2024). Stanislaus County Office of Education: Project Cal-Well Journey. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655972

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive end-of-year survey of NAMI On Campus Club participants (Table 1, n = 33–40 per item): 96% agreed the club "can influence how teens feel about mental health," 92% gained future-useful skills, 72% more interested in health care or mental health careers. Self-report, no comparison group.

> "Through end-of-year surveys, NAMI On Campus Club participants reported that they were confident their clubs were making a difference on their school campuses and that club participation impacted them personally."
