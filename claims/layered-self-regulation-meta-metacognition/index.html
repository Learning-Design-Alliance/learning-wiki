This is a theoretical argument from the commentary, using a hypothetical reading example; regulation is exercised within layers such as annotation, concept mapping, or social discourse, whose recruitment itself requires management effort. No empirical data are offered.

"such a ‘portfolio’ of regulation layers needs itself to be regulated. So perhaps it is tempting to invoke ‘meta-metacognition’"
