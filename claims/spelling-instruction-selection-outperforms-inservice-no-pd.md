### McNeill 2018

McNeill, B. C. (2018). Improving Preservice Teachers' Phonemic Awareness, Morphological Awareness and Orthographic Knowledge. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 43(1). http://ro.ecu.edu.au/ajte/vol43/iss1/2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results-section descriptive comparison on the 12-item spelling instruction selection task (Carreker, 2007), in which teachers choose the most appropriate instructional focus for groups of children's spelling errors. Descriptive analysis showed "58% of participants from the research cohort scored more than 50%" versus 25% of the comparison preservice group.

> "The research cohort scored an average of 63.7% (23.9) in the spelling instruction selection task. This is in comparison to the 54.5% and 63.6% scores achieved by the in-service without PD' group and the 'in-service with PD' group respectively."
