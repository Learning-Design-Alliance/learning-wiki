Seeskin 2020¶

Seeskin, A., Mahaffie, S., & Usher, A. (2020). The forgotten year: Applying lessons from Freshman success to Sophomore year. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/the-forgotten-year

Disaggregated cohort analysis (Figure 16) shows sophomore gains "have not been experienced equally by all groups of students": "21 percent of Latino young men and 22 percent of Black young men" were off-track, and only 30 and 20 percent respectively earned a 3.0 or higher GPA.
