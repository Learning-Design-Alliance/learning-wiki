## Evidence

### Rees Philippa 2025

Rees Philippa, Dronavalli Mithilesh, Carter Ben, Dickson Michelle, Green Charles, Lawler Kate, Lee Evelyn, Uebel Hannah, Gale Chris, Oei Ju Lee. (2025). School Performance of Preterm-Born Children After Intraventricular Hemorrhage. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.47584

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted linear regression at age 8 to 9 years in the cohort showed the high-grade IVH group's consistently lower total and domain-specific scores "remained clinically and statistically significant after adjustment (eg, overall NAPLAN AMD, −0.50; 95% CI, −0.71 to −0.30)" versus very preterm controls. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "This difference remained clinically and statistically significant after adjustment (eg, overall NAPLAN AMD, −0.50; 95% CI, −0.71 to −0.30)"

### Rees Philippa 2025 (2)

Rees Philippa, Dronavalli Mithilesh, Carter Ben, Dickson Michelle, Green Charles, Lawler Kate, Lee Evelyn, Uebel Hannah, Gale Chris, Oei Ju Lee. (2025). School Performance of Preterm-Born Children After Intraventricular Hemorrhage. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.47584

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

The cohort's age 10 to 11 comparison showed high-grade IVH children "continued to have significantly lower total attainment scores (AMD, −0.59; 95% CI, −0.85 to −0.34)"; at age 12 to 13 years the adjusted total difference was AMD, −0.61 (95% CI, −1.05 to −0.17). No standardized effect size was printed.

> "These differences persisted at age 10 to 11 years, where children with high-grade IVH continued to have significantly lower total attainment scores (AMD, −0.59; 95% CI, −0.85 to −0.34)"
