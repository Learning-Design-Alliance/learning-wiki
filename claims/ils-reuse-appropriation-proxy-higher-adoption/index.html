Evidence¶

María Jesús Rodríguez-Triana 2020¶

María Jesús Rodríguez-Triana, Luis P. Prieto, Tobias Ley, Ton de Jong, Denis Gillet. (2020). Social practices in teacher knowledge creation and innovation adoption: a large-scale study in an online instructional design community for inquiry learning. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-020-09331-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of 40,235 ILSs where reuse (creating a copy of another user's ILS and editing it) served as a proxy for knowledge appropriation. Adoption rates were 19.77% for reused ILSs versus 4.51% for non-reused ones; the article reports the reused rate as "about four times higher."

"adoption rates for reused ILSs being about four times higher than those created from scratch (or likely to be backup/demo copies). A chi-squared test of independence also rejected the null hypothesis (i.e., independence of appropriation and adoption): χ2 (1, N = 40,235) = 1395.36, p < 0.001."
