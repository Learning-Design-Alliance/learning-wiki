## Evidence

### Slotnik 2015

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2015). Change in Practice in Maryland: Student Learning Objectives and Teacher and Principal Evaluation. Mid-Atlantic Comprehensive Center (WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED568386

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-tabulations of the statewide educator survey (N principal 2015=1,160; teacher 2015=17,344) comparing principal and teacher agreement levels across TPE items. The report notes “perception gaps are narrowing between principals and teachers.”

> "Principals tend to be more positive about TPE on survey items than do teachers, yet teachers are showing greater improvement in their perceptions. While there are differences between the two groups, perception gaps are narrowing between principals and teachers."
