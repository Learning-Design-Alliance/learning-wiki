## Evidence

### Shen Jiliang PhD 2024

Shen Jiliang PhD, Zhang Yaping PhD, Zhang Bin PhD, Lu Chen PhD, Cao Jiasheng PhD, Chen Mingyu MD, Zheng Bin MD, PhD, Yang Jin PhD. (2024). Simulation training of laparoscopic biliary-enteric anastomosis with a three-dimensional-printed model leads to better skill transfer: a randomized controlled trial. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000001079

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled trial in which 15 surgeons (5 per group) trained by video only, general suture practice, or 3D-printed model practice, then performed a laparoscopic biliary-enteric anastomosis on isolated swine organs. The "3D model group tended to be shorter" with P=0.040; means were 29.4±2.3, 42.4±9.7, and 45.2±12.3 min.

> "The average operation time of the 3D model group, simple-suture group, and video group was 29.4±2.3 min, 42.4±9.7 min, and 45.2±12.3 min, respectively. The operation time in the 3D model group tended to be shorter than that in the other two groups (P=0.040)."
