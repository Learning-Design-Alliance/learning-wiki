## Evidence

### Kremser Wolfgang 2021

Kremser Wolfgang, Kranzinger Stefan, Bernhart Severin. (2021). Design and Implementation of a Gesture-Aided E-Learning Platform. Sensors. https://doi.org/10.3390/s21238042

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Field test in which 19 participants (12 female, 7 male, mean age 36.6) independently took a predefined online GAL lesson and completed a German SUS translation. The article reports the "median and mean SUS score was 75", rated Acceptable and Good; individual scores ranged from 42.5 to 97.5, with 14 of 19 (73.7%) rating it Acceptable.

> "The median and mean SUS score was 75, with no differences between genders. Consequently, the reference implementation received a C on the grade scale, and a “Good” according to the adjective rating scale. On the acceptability rating scale, the reference implementation was rated “Acceptable”."
