`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of mindset-category transitions (Figure 4) in the N = 508 matched sample. The article reports the odds of men starting and remaining Confident are "1.95 times higher than those of women (1.95:1)", and calls the Confident-to-Hesitant transition "a very large effect, with women being more than twice as likely to show that transition." No standardized effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "First, men were 16% more likely to remain in the Confident category than were women. Second, women who were initially in the Confident category were 12% more likely to transition all the way into the Hesitant category than were men."
