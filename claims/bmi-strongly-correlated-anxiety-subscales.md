## Evidence

### Sidiq Mohammad et al 2025

Sidiq Mohammad et al. (2025). Screen time exposure and academic performance, anxiety, and behavioral problems among school children. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19409

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis in the 537-child survey; the children fell in the normal weight category on average (mean BMI 18.1 ± 3.2). The printed effect size for panic agoraphobia is "r = 0.851". The authors suggest children with excessive screen time tend to have higher BMIs and reduced social interaction.

> "There was a strong correlation between increased BMI and panic agoraphobia score (r = 0.851), generalized anxiety score (GAS) (r = 0.807), separation anxiety score (SAS) (r = 0.805), social phobia score (SPS) (r = 0.804)"
