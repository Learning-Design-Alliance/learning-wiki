### Moeller 2017

Moeller, J., Brackett, M. A., Ivcevic, Z., & White, A. E. (2017). High school students' feelings: Discoveries from a large national survey and an experience sampling study. Preprint / self-archived. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=High+school+students%E2%80%99+feelings+Moeller+Brackett+Ivcevic+White

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Four-week ESM study (472 students, five Connecticut public high schools, 18,610 responses, three signals per school day) with 13 single-item feelings rated 1-4. In-the-moment measures showed "positive feelings like calm, happy, and relaxed came in second, third, and fourth place".

> "Although 'tired' was again the most frequently experienced feeling, positive feelings like calm, happy, and relaxed came in second, third, and fourth place, followed by stressed (place 5) and boredom (place 6)."
