## Evidence

### Zhang Yanqi 2015

Zhang Yanqi, Zhou Liang, Liu Xiaoyu, Liu Ling, Wu Yazhou, Zhao Zengwei, Yi Dali, Yi Dong. (2015). The Effectiveness of the Problem-Based Learning Teaching Model for Use in Introductory Chinese Undergraduate Medical Courses: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0120884

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis of pass rates for the PBL+LBL vs LBL comparison (6 studies), using a random effects model after high heterogeneity (I2 = 94%, P<0.001). The confidence interval includes 1.0, indicating no statistically significant difference.

> "The analytical results reveal that the PBL+LBL model did not produce a significantly higher course examination passing rate than the traditional teaching model [RR: 1.09, 95% CI: (1.00, 1.20)]"
