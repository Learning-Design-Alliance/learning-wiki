## Evidence

### Argimon-Pallàs Josep M 2011

Argimon-Pallàs Josep M, Flores-Mateo Gemma, Jiménez-Villa Josep, Pujol-Ribera Enriqueta. (2011). Effectiveness of a short-course in improving knowledge and skills on evidence-based practice. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-12-64

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary analysis: 44 residents (23.2%) reported prior formal EBP training. Their pre/post difference was 45.8 (CI 35.5-56.2, p < 0.0001); ES 1.78 and SRM 1.60, similar to the whole group.

> "Residents who self-reported former training had higher scores in the pre-test than the rest of the residents [75.9 (SD = 29.2) vs 57.6 (SD = 23.1): CI 95%: 9.1-25.6; p < 0.0001]"
