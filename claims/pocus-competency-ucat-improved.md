`q2 i?` Mean UCAT total scores improved from 64.9 (SD = 13.5) in module two to 78.9 (SD = 3.6) in module six, a significant increase. [→ Buhumaid R 2025](#buhumaid-r-2025)

## Evidence

### Buhumaid R 2025

Buhumaid R, Kilian P, Zidan MA, Salman H, AlSharhan R, Khansaheb H, Zary N. (2025). From novice to proficient: a longitudinal study of POCUS skill development through a hybrid certification program in the United Arab Emirates. BMC Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07138-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA of UCAT scores from nine participants with complete data at three assessment points (modules two, four, six). The improvement was quantified with "improvement mean = 13.5" and 95% CI (6.3, 20.7).

> "The UCAT scores improved from 64.9 (SD = 13.5) in module two to 78.9 (SD = 3.6) in module six (p < 0.001)."
