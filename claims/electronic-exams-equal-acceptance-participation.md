## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When students could freely choose between electronic and paper-based exams, participation numbers in the electronic exams equalled the paper-based exams, which the authors read as acceptance of the system. [→ Nils Schwinning 2018](#nils-schwinning-2018)

## Evidence

### Nils Schwinning 2018

Nils Schwinning, Michael Striewe, Till Massing, Christoph Hanck, Michael Goedicke. (2018). Towards digitalisation of summative and formative assessments in academic teaching of statistics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1811.02391](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1811.02391)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational report from the two statistics lectures where two electronic exams complemented two paper-based exams and students chose freely. The article reports equal participation numbers and interprets this as "the acceptance of the sytem among them"; no statistical test is reported.

> "Furthermore the participation numbers in the electronic exams are equal to the paper-based exams. As students are free to choose which exam they would like to take this shows the acceptance of the sytem among them."
