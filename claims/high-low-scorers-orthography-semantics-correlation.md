## Evidence

### Otsuka 2020

Otsuka, S., & Murai, T. (2020). The multidimensionality of Japanese kanji abilities. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-59852-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Supplementary analyses splitting examinees at the median total Kanken score to test whether higher correlations in older groups merely reflect higher scores. The article frames this as "In contrast to the higher correlations in the older groups whose scores were generally higher".

> "In the 2016 data, the correlations in the examinees with high scores (r = 0.38, n = 8,077) were lower than that of people with low scores (r = 0.44, n = 7,893; z = 4.62, p < 0.001)."
