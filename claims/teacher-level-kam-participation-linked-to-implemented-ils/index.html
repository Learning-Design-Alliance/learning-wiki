Evidence¶

María Jesús Rodríguez-Triana 2020¶

María Jesús Rodríguez-Triana, Luis P. Prieto, Tobias Ley, Ton de Jong, Denis Gillet. (2020). Social practices in teacher knowledge creation and innovation adoption: a large-scale study in an online instructional design community for inquiry learning. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-020-09331-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Teacher-level descriptive analysis complementing the ILS-level results. Teachers with any KAM participation were involved in implemented ILSs at 42.37%, 53.16% and 52.53%, against "the dataset average of 16.27%." The authors read this as suggesting social practices, not ILS quality, may be behind adoption.

"at least 35.13%, 18.92% and 13.18% of 20,064 teachers in our dataset had participated in ILSs with some level of maturation, scaffolding or appropriation, respectively."
