# Elementary student mobility in CPS is predominantly a within-district phenomenon, with CPS-to-CPS transfers accounting for 50-60 percent of school-year transfers and two-thirds of summer transfers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Most elementary transfers occur from one CPS school to another CPS school, and the decline in these within-district moves largely explains the falling school-year in-mobility rate. [→ Marisa de la Torre 2009](#marisa-de-la-torre-2009)

## Evidence

### Marisa de la Torre 2009

Marisa de la Torre, Julia Gwynne. (2009). Changing Schools: A Look at Student Mobility Trends in Chicago Public Schools Since 1995. Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/changing-schools-look-student-mobility-trends-chicago-public-schools-1995

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of CPS transfer destinations and origins in Chapter 1, disaggregated in Figures 2A-2D. The report finds within-district moves are "between 50 and 60 percent of the total transfers into schools" during the school year and two-thirds during the summer.

> "During the school year, these types of moves represent between 50 and 60 percent of the total transfers into schools (Figures 2A and 2B); during the summer, they represent two-thirds of all transfers"
