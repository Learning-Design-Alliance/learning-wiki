### Pustejovsky 2018

Pustejovsky, J. E. (2018). Procedural sensitivities of effect sizes for single-case designs with directly observed behavioral outcome measures. Psychological Methods. https://osf.io/hkzsm/

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Simulation results for a 50% treatment change with continuous recording (Figure 2). At 20% prevalence and 2-per-minute incidence, PND's expected value "ranges from 42% to 95%" across procedural variations, crossing multiple interpretive benchmark categories.

> "when prevalence is 20% and incidence is twice per minute, the expected value ranges from 42% to 95%, depending on session length and the number of baseline observations. By the guidelines of Scruggs and Mastropieri (1998), the same intervention might appear to be “ineffective” or “very effective” depending solely on features of the study’s design."
