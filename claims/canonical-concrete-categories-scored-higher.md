## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Scores on context-based categories such as hydrogen atom or harmonic oscillator were higher than on other concrete categories such as infinite square well or free particle, with four of the 20 questions belonging to each system. ## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Categorization of Quantum Mechanics Problems by Professors and Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02947](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02947)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Scoring result from the three faculty evaluators: the hydrogen atom category scored at least 3 of 6 for three of its four questions, while the infinite square well category did not reach 50% for any of its four questions. Faculty explained this via a notion of canonical quantum systems.

> "on concrete or context-based categories such as “hydrogen at om” or “harmonic oscillator” were higher than other concrete categories such as “inﬁnite square well” or “free particle”"
