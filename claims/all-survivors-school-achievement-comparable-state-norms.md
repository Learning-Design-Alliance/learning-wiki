## Evidence

### Al‐Kaylani HM 2022

Al‐Kaylani HM, Reasoner EE, Loeffler BT, Mott SL, Madasu S, Liu A, Langbehn K, Conrad AL, Dickens D, Grafft A, Harshman L, Modi AJ, van der Plas E. (2022). Characterizing academic performance in pediatric acute lymphoblastic leukemia with population‐based achievement tests. Cancer Reports. https://doi.org/10.1002/cnr2.1560

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of 269 school-administered Iowa Assessment observations from 63 ALL survivors treated 2000–2019, compared with Iowa Department of Education state means. The article reports "similar to statewide norms" for reading, with an estimated non-proficient reading rate of 27.3% (95% CI 17.7:39.4).

> "Average reading scores of ALL survivors across grades 3–11 were similar to statewide norms (Figure 2A). Scores across grades within individuals are shown in Figure 2B."

### Al‐Kaylani HM 2022 (2)

Al‐Kaylani HM, Reasoner EE, Loeffler BT, Mott SL, Madasu S, Liu A, Langbehn K, Conrad AL, Dickens D, Grafft A, Harshman L, Modi AJ, van der Plas E. (2022). Characterizing academic performance in pediatric acute lymphoblastic leukemia with population‐based achievement tests. Cancer Reports. https://doi.org/10.1002/cnr2.1560

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed effects analysis of school-based math scores from the same cohort of 63 survivors (269 observations). The article reports math performance "approximated state normative data", with non-proficiency of 27.4% (95% CI 19.3:37.4), contrary to the authors' hypothesis.

> "As shown in Figure 2C, math performance of participants approximated state normative data. The sample's estimated rate of non‐proficiency in math was within the expected range: 27.4% (95% CI 19.3:37.4)."
