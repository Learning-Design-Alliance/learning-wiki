Attash Heba M., Dawood Omar T., Aladul Mohammed I., Essa Narmin S. (2025). Association between ramadan fasting, sleep disturbances, fatigue, and academic performance among Iraqi university students: A cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-24482-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the 922-student survey (Table 6). Note the article's own inconsistency: the narrative reports these aORs as above 1, while Table 6 prints 0.63 and 0.56 for the same contrasts; the discrepancy is reported as printed, not resolved.

> "Breakfast timing also showed significant associations, with students who typically ate breakfast between 9 and 11 AM (aOR = 1.58, 95% CI [1.07–2.33], p = 0.02) or after 11 AM (aOR = 1.79, 95% CI [1.08–2.96], p = 0.03) having higher odds of high academic performance compared to those who ate immediately after waking up."
