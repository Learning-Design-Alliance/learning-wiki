# A simulation-based CRM course improved teams' overall and category CRM skills from pretest to posttest, with large effect sizes (|r| = 0.581–0.601) that did not reach statistical significance after Bonferroni correction

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Teams of EM interns improved overall CRM performance and all five CRM categories from pretest to posttest, with large effect sizes ranging from |r| = 0.581 to 0.601, but improvements were not statistically significant with Bonferroni-corrected p-values. [→ Parsons Jessica R. MD 2018](#parsons-jessica-r-md-2018)

## Evidence

### Parsons Jessica R. MD 2018

Parsons Jessica R. MD, MSMS; Crichlow Amanda MD, MSMS; Ponnuru Srikala MD; Shewokis Patricia A. PhD; Goswami Varsha MD; Griswold Sharon MD, MPH. (2018). Filling the Gap: Simulation-based Crisis Resource Management Training for Emergency Medicine Residents. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2017.10.35284

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pilot pretest–posttest study of 14 EM interns in four teams during a two-week course, rated by two independent raters on the Ottawa CRM Global Rating Scale. Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni correction (corrected p = 0.0083) were not significant, but "large effect sizes ranging from |r| = 0.581 to 0.601" were reported for overall and all category scores.

> "The improvements in overall CRM and each CRM category all showed large effect sizes ranging from |r| = 0.581 to 0.601."
