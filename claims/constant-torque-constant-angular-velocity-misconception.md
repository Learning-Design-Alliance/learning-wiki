## Evidence

### Lorenzo G. Rimoldini and Chandralekha Singh 2005

Lorenzo G. Rimoldini and Chandralekha Singh. (2005). Student understanding of rotational and rolling motion concepts. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of responses to multiple-choice question (5), where options (b) and (d) were the most common distractors: the article reports the linear-motion misconception "became “constant net to rque implies constant angular velocity.”" Students choosing option (d) were confused about whether net torque was proportional to a change in velocity or change in acceleration.

> "The “constant net force implies constant velocity” (and vice versa) misconception became “constant net to rque implies constant angular velocity.”"
