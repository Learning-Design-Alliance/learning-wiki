Following the pilot, Nearpod was adopted as the primary environment for content delivery, interaction, and assessment, and presented at GIREP 2023 and MPTL 2023 workshops. The article reports teachers highlighted "its accessibility without user registration" and drawbacks including content opening in separate windows and limited numerical data processing.

> "Teachers highlighted its accessibility without user registration, its visual presentation style that reduced skipped content, and its capacity to integrate multiple learning activities into one coherent experience."
