Descriptive survey results from the Spring 2016 and Spring 2017 Engineering Applications course (Table 2). For example, formally teaching nanotechnology concepts rose from a mean of 2.26 (SD 1.15) at the beginning to 2.80 (SD 1.01) at the end; the authors describe students as "increasingly motivated to investigate the implications of nanotechnology".
