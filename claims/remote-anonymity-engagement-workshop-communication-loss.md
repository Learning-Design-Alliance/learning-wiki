`q2 i?` Remote lectures saw increased question volume attributed to anonymous written submission, whereas remote workshops showed reduced interactivity and communication difficulties. [→ Wong Wei Jin 2025](#wong-wei-jin-2025)

## Evidence

### Wong Wei Jin 2025

Wong Wei Jin, Lee Shaun Wen Huey, Lee Ronald Fook Seng. (2025). Pharmacy students’ perspective on remote flipped classrooms in Malaysia: a qualitative study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group thematic analysis of remote lecture experiences: students reported an "increase in engagement in terms of the volume of questions asked," linked to anonymous posting and ease of written question formulation.

> "there was an increase in engagement in terms of the volume of questions asked due to increased confidence in asking questions and the ease of formulating questions in written form"

### Wong Wei Jin 2025 (2)

Wong Wei Jin, Lee Shaun Wen Huey, Lee Ronald Fook Seng. (2025). Pharmacy students’ perspective on remote flipped classrooms in Malaysia: a qualitative study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same focus group study, workshop subtheme: students reported the "absence of non-verbal cues from peers complicating communication," reduced interactivity from lack of physical presence, and breakout-room slowdowns of facilitation.

> "disadvantages include the absence of non-verbal cues from peers complicating communication"
