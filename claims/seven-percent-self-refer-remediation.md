The review reports, citing Guerrasio et al., that combining ten years of data "approximately 7% of students were self-referred" to a remediation program. The authors add that self-referrers tend to be high achievers with chronic performance anxiety.

> "Combining 10 years of data, Guerrasio et al. [21] found approximately 7% of students were self-referred to a remediation program for medical students and professionals."
