Evidence¶

Brooks 2026¶

Brooks, E., Hawi, Z., Hosler, J. et al. (2026). High-intensity cardiovascular exercise facilitates online motor skill learning, with no effect of BDNF genotype. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00432-8

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Linear mixed models of four secondary skill components (trial time, force error, reaction time, movement time) across all 51 participants; only force error showed a Group × Block interaction, consistent with HIIT improving accuracy rather than speed.

"However, an interaction of Exercise Group x Block was observed for force error (F5, 254 = 2.52, p = .03), indicating that over the session participants in the HIIT group demonstrated a greater improvement in accuracy compared to the control group"
