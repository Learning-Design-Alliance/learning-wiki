## Evidence

### Han Ying 2022

Han Ying, Zheng Bin, Zhao Linyong, Hu Jiankun, Zhang Chao, Xiao Ran, Wang Chunyan, Pu Dan. (2022). Impact of background music on the performance of laparoscopy teams. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03503-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc Bonferroni pairwise comparisons from the within-subject ANOVA in 57 teams. The quote states the noisy environment "led to significantly more negative effects" than music relative to neutral; Table 3 shows noisy vs. neutral significant for total OT time (p < 0.001) and suture time (p < 0.001).

> "Compared with the neutral environment, the noisy environment led to significantly more negative effects than the music environment which led to a positive impact on performance."
