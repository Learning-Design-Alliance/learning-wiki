Descriptive statistics on school-round participation during the pandemic, reported alongside Fig. 1. The article attributes the drop to school closures and funding limitations, notes it organised the whole year online, and states "we are steadily growing back to above 7k students per year."

> "Thereafter, we can see the negative effect of the Covid-19 pandemic between the years 2020 and 2021 where the overall participation dropped to about 18 % of the previous year."
