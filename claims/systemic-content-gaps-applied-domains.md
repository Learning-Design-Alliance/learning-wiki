### Turaev 2026

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Compositional analysis of the unified labor-market corpus (5,186 unique openings) against the curricular census of 1,922 CLOs, projected onto eleven ESCO domains; the two compositions "differ substantially (Cramér's V= 0.213)". Table 4 reports representation ratios up to 4.00 for Software Engineering & Project Management.

> "The market demands competencies in Software Engineering and Project Management at four times the rate at which the curriculum supplies them, in Systems and Infrastructure at three times, and in Web and Mobile Development and in Cybersecurity at roughly two-and-a-half and one-and-a-half times."
