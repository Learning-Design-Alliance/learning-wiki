# Students rate RCDP higher than SWSP on the Simulation Design Scale overall, including the support and problem-solving factors

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The RCDP strategy was rated better than SWSP on the Simulation Design Scale as a whole (p=0.03), and specifically on support (p<0.001) and problem-solving (p=0.02). [→ da Silva Gisele Cabral 2025](#da-silva-gisele-cabral-2025)

## Evidence

### da Silva Gisele Cabral 2025

da Silva Gisele Cabral, Campanharo Cássia Regina Vancini, Lopes Maria Carolina Barbosa Teixeira, Okuno Meiry Fernanda Pinto, Miura Carla Roberta Monteiro, Batista Ruth Ester Assayag. (2025). Clinical simulation and deliberate practice in rapid-cycle deliberate practice: a randomized clinical trial. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0632

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of Simulation Design Scale responses from 134 students; RCDP means exceeded SWSP on support (4.84 vs 4.68, p<0.001) and problem-solving (4.82 vs 4.73, p=0.02), and "in the scale application result as a whole, the RCDP strategy was best assessed by students (p=0.03)".

> "In relation to the Simulation Design Scale, the RCDP strategy was best assessed in relation to support, which refers to when the participant had their needs recognized during the simulated activity (p<0.001), and problem-solving, which assesses the opportunities to solve the problem situations identified in the scenario (p=0.02)."
