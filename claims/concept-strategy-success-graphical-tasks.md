## Evidence

### Laurens Bollen 2015

Laurens Bollen, Paul van Kampen, and Mieke De Cock. (2015). Students’ difficulties with vector calculus in electrodynamics. arXiv preprint arXiv:1502.02830v2 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Pretest strategy analysis (N=30, Table II) distinguishing concept, formula, description, unclear and no-answer categories. The formula-based technique (23% of students) worked when the vector function could be found but failed on complex cases (0% on Field 7 divergence).

> "Students who used a description based strategy or give little or no explanation seem to have a low chance of being successful in determining the divergence and curl of a graphical representation of a vector ﬁeld."
