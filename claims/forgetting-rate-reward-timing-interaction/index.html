Evidence¶

Zhongyu Hu & Jiongjiong Yang 2025¶

Zhongyu Hu & Jiongjiong Yang. (2025). Timing of reward cues alters the interaction between reward and interest on memory over time. npj Science of Learning 10:82. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00374-7

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

ANOVA on the forgetting rate (accuracy decline from 10 min to 1 day relative to immediate accuracy), with experiment, reward, and interest as factors; the article describes the forgetting rate as a behavioral marker of consolidation, with a large printed effect size (ηp2 = 0.20).

"The results revealed a signiﬁcant three-way interaction on the forgetting rate (F (1, 78) = 19.13, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.20)"
