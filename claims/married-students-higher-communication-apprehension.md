## Evidence

### Al Awaji Nisreen 2022

Al Awaji Nisreen, Zaidi Uzma, Awad Salwa S., Alroqaiba Nouf, Aldhahi Monira I., Alsaleh Hadeel, Akil Shahnaz, Mortada Eman M. (2022). Moderating Effects of Self-Esteem on the Relationship between Communication Anxiety and Academic Performance among Female Health College Students during the COVID-19 Pandemic. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192113960

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney comparison of PRCA by marital status in the survey of 287 students. The article reports "the median PRCA was significantly higher in married female students (90; IQR: 36.4) than in single students (73; IQR: 20)".

> "Thus, the median PRCA was significantly higher in married female students (90; IQR: 36.4) than in single students (73; IQR: 20). Similarly, marital status was statistically significantly different among the students regarding their interpersonal communication (U = 358.000, z = −2.42, p = 0.01)."
