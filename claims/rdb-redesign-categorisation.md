`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive account of the RDB redesign developed by the IPPOG Web working group after consultations with science communication experts and target audiences; a large number of categories, item types and audience types were collapsed to fewer more targeted ones, approved by the IPPOG community and teachers.

> "Currently there are almost 400 items in RDB which  are filtered by topics, item types, target audiences and language. Users can choose among 44 physics topics (from particles and their interactions through dark matter, extra-dimensions to detectors and accelerators), 41 item types (e.g. video, poster, lesson plan, etc.)"
