### Allan Pau 2016

Allan Pau, Chen Yu Sui, Lee Verna Kar Mun, Sow Chew Fei, Alwis Ranjit De. (2016). What does the multiple mini interview have to offer over the panel interview? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.29874

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

t-test and ANOVA comparisons of mean PI and MMI scores by sex, age, race, and academic-ability band among 131 applicants. The study reports a mean PI score of "15.7 (95% CI 15.2–16.1)" and mean MMI score of "72.5 (95% CI 70.8–74.1)" with no significant associations.

> "The mean PI score was 15.7 (95% CI 15.2–16.1) and mean MMI score was 72.5 (95% CI 70.8–74.1), both of which were not statistically significantly associated with sex, age, race or academic ability"
