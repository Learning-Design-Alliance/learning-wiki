Gao X (2026) From unified to differentiated materials: generative AI–supported adaptation of EAP reading materials. Front. Psychol. 17:1887565. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1887565

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Two-way ANOVA (Table 7) on immediate unsupported application — an unseen academic passage completed with no glosses, labels, or prompts after the main reading task. Only the proficiency effect was significant, F(2, 126) = 45.28, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.418; no equivalence was tested.

"The material eﬀect was non-signiﬁcant, F(2, 126) = 0.82, p = 0.445, and η2 p = 0.013, and the Proﬁciency × Material interaction was also non-signiﬁcant, F(4, 126) = 0.31, p = 0.870, and η2 p = 0.010."
