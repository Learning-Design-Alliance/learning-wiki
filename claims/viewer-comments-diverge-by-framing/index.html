Evidence¶

Shayla Sharmin 2025¶

Shayla Sharmin, Mohammad Al-Ratrout, Mohammad Fahim Abrar, and Roghayeh Leila Barmaki. (2025). How YouTube Frames ChatGPT Use in Education: An Epistemic Network Analysis with Supporting Multimodal Metadata. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08698 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.08698

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative thematic analysis of 936 comments from 28 videos (nG1=56, nG2=306, nG3=574), treated as an independent triangulation layer. The analysis found over-reliance concern "concentrated in G3 comments" and absent from G1 and G2 comments.
