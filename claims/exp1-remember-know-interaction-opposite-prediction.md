# In Experiment 1, reminder+interference yielded greater recollection strength than interference alone on Remember/Know judgments

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` A significant reminder × interference interaction on percent Remember responses showed recollection strength higher in the reminder+interference condition than in the interference-alone condition. [→ Michael Batashvili 2022](#michael-batashvili-2022)
`q2 i3` Reminders increased Remember responses (η² = 0.22) and interference decreased them (η² = 0.02). [→ Michael Batashvili 2022 (2)](#michael-batashvili-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Michael Batashvili 2022

Michael Batashvili, Rona Sheaffer, Maya Katz, Yoav Doron, Noam Kempler and Daniel A. Levy. (2022). Behavioural reconsolidation interference not observed in a within-subjects design. npj Science of Learning 7:26. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00143-w

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 (40 participants) Remember/Know judgments analysed with a 2×2 repeated-measures ANOVA; the article reports the interaction as quoted, with recollection strength "in the reminder + interference condition was signiﬁcantly greater than in the interference-alone condition".

> "there was a signi ﬁcant interaction found between reminder and interference conditions, F(1,39) = 9.753, p = 0.003, η² = 0.03, BF10 = 0.36"

### Michael Batashvili 2022 (2)

Michael Batashvili, Rona Sheaffer, Maya Katz, Yoav Doron, Noam Kempler and Daniel A. Levy. (2022). Behavioural reconsolidation interference not observed in a within-subjects design. npj Science of Learning 7:26. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00143-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Main effects in the same ANOVA: reminder, F(1,39) = 30.09, p < 0.001, η² = 0.22; interference, F(1,39) = 35.71, p < 0.001, η² = 0.02, with fewer Remember responses under interference (62.35% vs 69.50%).

> "showing that participants gave more “Remember” responses when there was a reminder present (Mean = 74.54%, SD = 18.46%) compared to pairs that had no reminder (Mean = 57.26%, SD = 20.83%)."
