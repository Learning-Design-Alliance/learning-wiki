# An interactive-methods scheme for forming social tolerance raised the tolerance index of future teachers by 34% in the experimental group versus 5% in the control group

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the formative experiment, the experimental group's tolerance index rose from 8.7 to 11.7 points (34%) while the control group rose from 8.5 to 8.9 points (5%), with Wilcoxon T-test differences significant at p<0.01 in both groups. [→ Nagovitsyn et al. 2018](#nagovitsyn-et-al-2018)

## Evidence

### Nagovitsyn et al. 2018

Roman S. Nagovitsyn, Dana K. Bartosh, Aleksandr Y. Ratsimor, Yuri G. Maksimov. (2018). Formation of Social Tolerance Among Future Teachers. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2018.4.754

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental semester study of 72 future-teaching students (36 experimental, 36 control) using the Intol test; the article reports "increased by 34 %" in the experimental group versus 5% in the control group, with Wilcoxon T-test reliability at p<0.01.

> "The index of tolerance in the EG was 11.7 points – increased by 34 %,  and in the CG it was 8.9 points – increased by 5 % (Table 1)."
