Akbari-Chehrehbargh Z, Tavafian SS. (2022). Impact of El-Poems study: the e-learning for postural education in music students: a randomized controlled trial protocol. Trials. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-022-06335-4

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r?`

This is a stated hypothesis in a prospective RCT protocol, not a reported result. The trial will enroll 204 students per group and assess musculoskeletal pain via the Nordic Body Map questionnaire and a numerical rating scale at baseline and six-month follow-up. No outcome data exist yet; the article offers no effect size for this hypothesis.

> "The primary hypothesis is that MP rate and intensity in the intervention group will reduce, compared with the controls."
