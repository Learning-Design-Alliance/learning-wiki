Evidence¶

Lenka Schnaubert · Freydis Vogel 2022¶

Lenka Schnaubert · Freydis Vogel. (2022). Integrating collaboration scripts, group awareness, and self-regulation in computer-supported collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2022) 17:1–10. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09367-9

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

The editorial reports a four-session study of shared regulation in small groups learning task-oriented reading with a collaboration script assigning roles and supporting planning, execution, and reflection, using conversation analyses with coding schemes. It reports "deep-level metacognitive utterances are rare". No effect size printed.

"This study shows that while metacognitive regulation can be quite frequent when learning with a collaboration script designed to elicit metacognitive processes, deep-level metacognitive utterances are rare, and students mostly accept each other’s suggestions and much less frequently co-construct or share metacognitive interaction – despite the use of the script."
