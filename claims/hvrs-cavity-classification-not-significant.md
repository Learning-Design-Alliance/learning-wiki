Şimşek Emine, Satar Selimcan. (2026). The effect of haptic virtual reality simulation training before clinical endodontic practice on student performance and perception. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09577-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cavity classification (poor/moderate/good) analyzed with the Fisher–Freeman–Halton exact test showed a distribution favoring the experimental group, but the difference was not statistically significant (FFH = 3.722, p = 0.124, Cramer's V = 0.352).

> "no poor classification was observed in the experimental group (0.0%), whereas the rates of moderate and good classifications were 60.0% and 40.0%, respectively. In the control group, the rates of poor, moderate, and good classifications were 13.3%, 73.3%, and 13.3%, respectively"
