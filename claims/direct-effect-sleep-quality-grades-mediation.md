## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a multiple mediational model, the direct effect of sleep quality on mean academic score is significant (β = −0.31; p < 0.01) and the total effect is significant (β = −0.35; p < 0.001), while the indirect effects through Mediterranean diet adherence and self-efficacy have confidence intervals crossing zero. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bootstrapped multiple mediational model (10,000 bootstrap samples, PROCESS macro for SPSS) with sleep quality as X, average grade as Y, and diet adherence and self-efficacy as chained mediators. The article reports a "significant (β = −0.31; p < 0.01)" direct effect and a significant total effect (β = −0.35; p < 0.001).

> "the direct effect, including adherence to the Mediterranean diet (M1) and self-efficacy (M2), between sleep quality and mean academic score is significant (β = −0.31; p < 0.01) (Table 3). The total effect between both variables is also significant (β = −0.35; p < 0.001)."
