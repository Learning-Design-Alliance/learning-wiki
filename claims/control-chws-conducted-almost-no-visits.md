### Tomlinson M 2025

Tomlinson M, Rotheram-Borus MJ, Stansert Katzen L, Gertsch W, le Roux I, Dippenaar E, le Roux K. (2025). Relationship of perinatal outcomes to the competence and quantity of contact with community health workers. Journal of Global Health. https://doi.org/10.7189/jogh.15.04094

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Implementation observation across the 23 control-group CHWs at four clinics (392 mothers): despite perinatal home visits being part of the job description and an additional month of training, "fewer than 7% of CHWs in the control group ever implemented home visits".

> "Since fewer than 7% of CHWs in the control group ever implemented home visits and no data was available on the frequency of visits, we focussed on the CHWs in the intervention group."
