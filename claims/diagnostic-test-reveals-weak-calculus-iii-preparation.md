Radoslav M. Dimitrić. (2013). Feedback from students' tests as a tool in teaching: A case study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1305.5173](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1305.5173)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-study analysis of one Calculus III course for science majors; a diagnostic test was given at the first class meeting and made specifically for this course. The author reports the printed thresholds: "about 90%" indicates preparedness and "below 60%" requires serious discussion; the class mean was 35% (median 38%).

> "Diagnostic test score of about 90% would indicate that the student is prepared for the course and the score below 60% requires instructor's serious discussion as to what to do about the required material."
