## Evidence

### Luo 2025

Luo, T., Muljana, P. S., Ren, X., & Young, D. (2025). Exploring instructional designers' utilization and perspectives on generative AI tools: A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 741–766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10437-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey analysis of familiarity ratings for ten GenAI tools (RQ1), measured from Extremely Familiar to Not at all Familiar. ChatGPT, Midjourney and Microsoft Bing were "the top three tools that were rated most familiar" among the 70 respondents.

> "IDs showed varying levels of familiarity with different AI tools within the context of ID, with ChatGPT, Midjourney and Microsoft Bing being the top three tools that were rated most familiar."

### Luo 2025 (2)

Luo, T., Muljana, P. S., Ren, X., & Young, D. (2025). Exploring instructional designers' utilization and perspectives on generative AI tools: A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 741–766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10437-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey analysis of self-reported frequency of GenAI tool use (RQ1). ChatGPT was the most frequently utilized tool, "with over half of the respondents (51.43%) reporting daily usage," while tools such as Education Copilot, Khanmigo, and SlidesAI exhibited low levels of usage.

> "ChatGPT emerges as the most frequently utilized tool, with over half of the respondents (51.43%) reporting daily usage."
