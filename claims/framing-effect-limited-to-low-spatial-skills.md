## Evidence

### Sally P.W. Wu 2019

Sally P.W. Wu, Jainaba Corr, Martina A. Rau. (2019). How instructors frame students' interactions with educational technologies can enhance or reduce learning with multiple representations. Computers & Education 128 (2019) 199–213. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.09.012

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc analysis of the HLM on transfer posttest gains, splitting students into low (0–33rd percentile), medium, and high spatial skills groups. The article reports F(2, 544) = 2.654, p = .071 for the low spatial skills group only, described as marginally significant.

> "Post-hoc comparisons showed that the effect of condition was marginally significant only among students with low spatial skills, F(2, 544) = 2.654, p = .071, but not among students with medium or high spatial skills, ps > .10."
