Hamzah Nurul Rimadhayanti, Mohd Fadzil Abdul Hanid & Mohamad Ikram Zakaria (2026). The effect of segmented-interactive video demonstration on student performance in procedural skills among healthcare students. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10471-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group Mann-Whitney U analysis of IPPI communication components (rapport, explaining procedure, closure) reported in Table 10, comparing the segmented-interactive treatment group with the continuous-video control group; Mann-Whitney U = 1605.000, Z = −2.015, and no effect size was printed.

> "the p-value is 0.044 and less than 0.05. Therefore, the null hypothesis was rejected. There are significant differences in students’ performance in the communication component using the segmented interactive video compared to students watching a continuous video"
