## Evidence

### Delgado 2022

Delgado, P., Luna, C. A., Dissanayaka, A., Oshinowo, O., Waggoner, J. J., Schley, S., Fernandez, T., & Myers, D. R. (2022). An economical in-class sticker microfluidic activity develops student expertise in microscale physics and device manufacturing. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2208.10368](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2208.10368)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way ANOVA on post-assessment confidence-weighted scores from the same 11-student cohort found 'no statistical difference between physics and manufacturing post-assessment confidence weighted scores'; equivalence was not otherwise tested.

> "A one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) revealed that there was no statistical difference between physics and manufacturing post-assessment confidence weighted scores."

### Delgado 2022 (2)

Delgado, P., Luna, C. A., Dissanayaka, A., Oshinowo, O., Waggoner, J. J., Schley, S., Fernandez, T., & Myers, D. R. (2022). An economical in-class sticker microfluidic activity develops student expertise in microscale physics and device manufacturing. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2208.10368](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2208.10368)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-assessment data from the same test: manufacturing scores 'were lower than the pre-assessment scores for the physics questions', despite manufacturing being mostly discussed in class.

> "However, student confidence weighted pre-assessment scores related to manufacturing were lower than the pre-assessment scores for the physics questions."
