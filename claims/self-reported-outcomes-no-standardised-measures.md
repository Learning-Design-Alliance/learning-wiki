Terry Julia, Wilks Rachel, Davies Joanne. (2025). Simulated learning interventions to improve communication and practice with deaf and hard of hearing patients: a systematic review and qualitative synthesis. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10452-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of participant outcomes across the six studies, reported in the Results section. Sanders et al. measured comfort with the Disability Situations Inventory pre and post; others used self-rated confidence, interviews, rich pictures and reflective debriefing. The review reports "significant improvement in comfort levels" in Sanders et al. and increased empathy in McLaughlin et al.

> "No standardised measures were used and different studies' objectives varied with some reporting an increase in the development of their clinical judgement"
