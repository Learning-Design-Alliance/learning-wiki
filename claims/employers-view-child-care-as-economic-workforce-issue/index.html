The report cites a U.S. Chamber of Commerce Foundation study of employers: "50% of employers who lost employees amid the pandemic cited child care concerns." Chamber leaders interviewed reported demand for child care exceeding supply and shortages for nontraditional work hours. No effect size is printed.
