## Evidence

### Yoshiaki Matsuzawa 2013

Yoshiaki Matsuzawa, Sayaka Tohyama, and Sanshiro Sakai. (2013). THE COURSE DESIGN TO DEVELOP META-COGNITIVE SKILLS FOR COLLABORATIVE LEARNING THROUGH TOOL-ASSISTED DISCOURSE ANALYSIS. PROCEEDINGS, COINs13. [doi:10.1504/ijode.2014.065098](https://doi.org/10.1504/ijode.2014.065098)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two course cohorts: the 2010 control reflected in their own way, the 2012 experimental group analyzed their own discourse with KBDeX. RubKB ratings of the term-report question showed "The average score was 2.31 for the 2010 class and 3.20 for the 2012 class", a significant unpaired t-test difference. No effect size was printed.

> "The average score was 2.31 for the 2010 class and 3.20 for the 2012 class. An unpaired t-test has been conducted for comparison. The average score in the 2012 class is significantly higher than that for the 2010 class ( t(81)=4.58, p<.01)."
