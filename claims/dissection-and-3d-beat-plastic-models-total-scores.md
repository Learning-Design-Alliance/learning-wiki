## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized trial of first-year medical students, total examination scores were significantly higher for dissection and 3D software groups than for the plastic models group (P < 0.0001), while dissection and 3D did not differ significantly. [→ Sudha R 2024](#sudha-r-2024)

## Evidence

### Sudha R 2024

Sudha R, Kumar GG, Ratnajeet C, Tushar K, Kishlay KM, Das AK, Shekhar S. (2024). Comparative Analysis of Multidimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy: A Randomized Control Trial. Annals of African Medicine 23(3):459-465. https://doi.org/10.4103/aam.aam_214_23

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post hoc test on examination totals in an RCT of 270 completing first-year medical students randomized to dissection, plastic models, or 3D software; the article reports "significantly better than groups taught by plastic models (P < 0.0001)". Table 3 prints total means of 61.69 (dissection), 61.76 (3D), and 48.58 (plastic).

> "In total scores, dissection and 3D groups performed significantly better than groups taught by plastic models (P < 0.0001). However, scores between dissection and 3D groups did not vary significantly."
