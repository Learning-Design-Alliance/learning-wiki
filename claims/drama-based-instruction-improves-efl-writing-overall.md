## Evidence

### Bataineh 2017

Bataineh, R. F., & Salah, N. A. (2017). The Effectiveness of Drama-Based Instruction in Jordanian EFL Students' Writing Performance. TESOL International Journal, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1247797

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with two intact sections of 40 male eighth-graders randomly assigned by coin toss; pre-tested, treated nine weeks, post-tested. The experimental group outperformed the control group, "with means of 31.58 and 25.63, respectively" (t = 5.27, df = 78, Sig. 0.00).

> "Table 3 also shows a statistically signifcant difference (at α ≤ 0.05) in the two groups’ overall writing performance on the post-test, which can be attributed to drama-based instruction."
