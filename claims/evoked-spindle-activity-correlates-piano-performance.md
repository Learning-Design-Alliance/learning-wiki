> "For pitch accuracy, we identified a significant negative correlation between evoked spindle activity and performance accuracy in the 'SP' condition (r = -0.50, p = 0.02). Similarly, for rhythm accuracy, evoked spindle activity in the 'SPd' condition showed a significant negative correlation with rhythm performance (r = -0.57, p = 0.02)."
