# Most moderator variables are not significantly related to rubric effects on academic performance, including rubric design specifics

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2`–`r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For standardized mean differences on academic performance, none of the eleven moderator variables was significantly related to effect sizes. [→ Panadero 2023](#panadero-2023)
`q3 i?` Rubric design specifics (number of criteria and performance levels) do not significantly affect outcomes. [→ Panadero 2023 (2)](#panadero-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Panadero 2023

Panadero, Ernesto; Jonsson, Anders; Pinedo, Leire; Fernández-Castilla, Belén. (2023). Effects of Rubrics on Academic Performance, Self-Regulated Learning, and self-Efficacy: a Meta-analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09823-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator meta-regressions on 54 standardized mean differences from 21 studies, covering publication year, gender, age, educational level, sessions, criteria, performance levels, self/peer assessment, design, and quality; none reached significance.

> "No outliers were detected, and none of the potential moderator variables were significantly related to the observed effect sizes (see Table 1 )."

### Panadero 2023 (2)

Panadero, Ernesto; Jonsson, Anders; Pinedo, Leire; Fernández-Castilla, Belén. (2023). Effects of Rubrics on Academic Performance, Self-Regulated Learning, and self-Efficacy: a Meta-analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09823-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The authors' interpretation of their moderator results alongside prior reviews: differences in the number of criteria and performance levels "do not seem to have a major impact on how students perform."

> "However, these studies, including our own, found that the spe- cifics of rubric design do not significantly affect the outcomes (Jonsson & Pana- dero, 2017; Brookhart, 2018)."
