# Novice users rate WriteSim TCExam easy to use and report improved understanding of manuscript subsections and content placement

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a self-selected online survey, most of the 10 responding novice users agreed or strongly agreed that WriteSim was fast, easy to learn and use, and that brief use improved their understanding of manuscript subsection roles and content placement. [→ Shah Jatin 2010](#shah-jatin-2010)

## Evidence

### Shah Jatin 2010

Shah Jatin, Rajgor Dimple, Vaghasia Meenakshi, Phadtare Amruta, Pradhan Shreyasee, Carvalho Elias, Pietrobon Ricardo. (2010). WriteSim TCExam - An open source text simulation environment for training novice researchers in scientific writing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-39

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Online user survey completed by 10 of 14 tested novice researchers using DADOS-Survey. Most respondents were female and post-graduate trained, most had never published a peer-reviewed manuscript. They rated speed, navigation and ease of use highly, and 90% would look forward to future use.

> "A majority of them reported the application to be fast (agree = 40%, strongly agree = 60%), intuitive to navigate (agree = 90%, strongly agree = 10%), and easy to use (agree = 60%, strongly agree = 40%)."
