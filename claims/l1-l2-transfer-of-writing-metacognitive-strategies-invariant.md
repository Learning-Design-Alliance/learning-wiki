# L1 writing metacognitive strategies transfer to L2 writing, invariant across L2 proficiency and academic major groups

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wandong Xu 2024

Wandong Xu, Xinhua Zhu. (2024). Examining metacognitive strategy use in L1 and L2 task-situated writing: effects, transferability, and cross-language facilitation. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09387-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of the 502-participant dataset found a strong cross-language path: "L1 writing metacognitive strategies constituted a significant predictor of L2 writing metacognitive strategies ( β = 0.62, p < 0.001)".

### Wandong Xu 2024 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`
