## Evidence

### Bati 2015

Bati, K. & Kaptan, F. (2015). The Effect of Modeling Based Science Education on Critical Thinking. Educational Policy Analysis and Strategic Research, 10(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1127622

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-groups t-test on the 60 experimental-group students' CCT-X scores in a pre-test–post-test quasi-experimental design; the printed means are 33.38 (pre) and 35.98 (post), with t(59) = -3.738, p = .000. The article reports "a significant difference between pre and post scores".

> "Table 10 shows that there was a significant difference between pre and post scores of the experimental group (p<0.05). In other words, application of the program led to a significant difference in students' critical thinking scores."
