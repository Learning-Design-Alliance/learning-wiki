Naoko Hayashida. (2025). Beyond the Winding Path of Learning: Exploring Affective, Cognitive, and Action-Oriented Prompts for Communication Skills. GenAICHI: CHI 2025 Workshop on Generative AI and HCI. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.06646](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.06646)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Reflexive thematic analysis of 180 open-ended evaluator excerpts from a two-round evaluation of 30 LLM-generated instructional texts; this finding comes from the Expressed Preferences theme. The analysis found "information-rich content (EP_Informative Content, et103) were described as desirable." No effect size is reported; findings are qualitative.

> "Evidence-based content such as research references (EP_Supporting Evidence, et161), explanations facilitating actionable steps (EP_Actionable Instruction, et150), and information-rich content (EP_Informative Content, et103) were described as desirable."
