## Evidence

### Kvintova Jana 2024

Kvintova Jana, Novotny Jan Sebastian, Liu Hongyang, Vachova Lucie, Kantor Jiri. (2024). Path analysis reveals cross-country differences between Czech and Chinese university students in effect of internet and smartphone addiction, mental health, and personality traits on academic achievement in the post-pandemic era. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02069-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-country comparison of the two final SEM path models (Czech n = 1075, Chinese n = 710). The article reports that "the variables formed a complex network of interacting factors" for Czech students, whereas for Chinese students "the effect of each cluster of factors was separate for individual domains of academic achievement".

> "While the variables in Czech students form a relatively complex network among each other, the effect of most of the variables on academic achievement in Chinese students was compartmentalized into separate groups."
