## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Significant interaction effects were found for duration-delay (p = 0.013), delay-variance (p = 0.020), and duration-delay-variance (p = 0.036), indicating high interrelatedness of the three planning indicators. [→ Eichmann 2019](#eichmann-2019)
`q3 i?` The duration indicator is positively related to success when the delay is high but has a very small negative effect when the delay is low; variance is positively related to success when the delay is low and negatively related when the delay is high. [→ Eichmann 2019](#eichmann-2019)

## Evidence

### Eichmann 2019

Eichmann, B., Goldhammer, F., Greiff, S., Pucite, L., & Naumann, J. (2019). The role of planning in complex problem solving. Computers & Education, 128, 1-12. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.08.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A GLMM including fixed effects of all three indicators and their interactions, fitted to log data from N = 1346 German students on 21 interactive PISA 2012 CPS tasks, found "Signiﬁcant eﬀects were found for the interactions of duration-delay, delay-variance, and duration-delay-variance."
