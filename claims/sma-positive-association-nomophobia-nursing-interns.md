`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 3, N = 585) among Saudi nursing interns showed "small to moderate positive associations with the four categories of nomophobia (ranging from r = 0.23 to r = 0.42, p = 0.001)". The emerging SEM model also showed a direct positive path from SMA to nomophobia (β = 0.12, p = 0.002), with SMA contributing 7.80% of nomophobia's variance.

> "SMA had small to moderate positive associations with the four categories of nomophobia (ranging from r = 0.23 to r = 0.42, p = 0.001), but negatively associated with ASE (r = −0.18, p = 0.001)"
