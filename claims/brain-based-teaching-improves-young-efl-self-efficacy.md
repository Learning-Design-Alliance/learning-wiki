## Evidence

### Oghyanous 2017

Parastoo Alizadeh Oghyanous. (2017). The Effect of Brain-Based Teaching on Young EFL Learners' Self-Efficacy. English Language Teaching, 10(5), 158. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n5p158

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with 60 Iranian EFL learners aged 13-16; experimental group (n=30) received brain-based teaching for 12 sessions while control group received regular instruction. Independent samples t-test showed "significant differences existed between experimental and control groups in self-efficacy posttest".

> "According to the results of independent samples t-test significant differences existed between experimental and control groups in self-efficacy posttest, T (51.37)=5.34, p=0.00"

### Oghyanous 2017 (2)

Parastoo Alizadeh Oghyanous. (2017). The Effect of Brain-Based Teaching on Young EFL Learners' Self-Efficacy. English Language Teaching, 10(5), 158. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n5p158

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline comparison of the same 60 learners before intervention confirmed group equivalence: "mean scores of the two groups were not significantly different at pretest".

> "Independent samples t-test also indicated that mean scores of the two groups were not significantly different at pretest, T (58) =0.51, p=0.60"
