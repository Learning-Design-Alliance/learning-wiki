## Evidence

### Reubenson Alan A. 2025

Reubenson Alan A., Riddell Hugh, Brewer Margo L., Ng Leo, Gucciardi Daniel F. (2025). Individual differences and self-regulatory factors are credible determinants of physiotherapy student performance on clinical placement: Insights from a measurement burst design study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10453-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-placement (level 2) models using the Brief Resilience Scale and adapted MSLQ motivational subscales among 97 students. The article reports "no significant associations"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size printed.

> "There were no significant associations between level 2 variables (i.e., resilience and motivational factors) and mean levels of scores or between-placement variability in clinical or professional performance."

### Reubenson Alan A. 2025 (2)

Reubenson Alan A., Riddell Hugh, Brewer Margo L., Ng Leo, Gucciardi Daniel F. (2025). Individual differences and self-regulatory factors are credible determinants of physiotherapy student performance on clinical placement: Insights from a measurement burst design study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10453-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-placement model of a bespoke social support measure (availability and seeking) against the professional APP dimension. The article reports students "performed worse on placements where they reported seeking more support from their partner/spouse"; no standardised effect size printed.

> "Regarding the professional dimension only, students performed worse on placements where they reported seeking more support from their partner/spouse."
