# A week of reflective writing after a general chemistry lab experiment produces a statistically significant posttest increase compared to a control group

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students who completed reflective writing after experimental work scored significantly higher on a post-intervention test than control students (p = 0.000; z = -11.390), with no significant pretest difference between groups (p = 0.631). [→ Han et al. 2012](#han-et-al-2012)

## Evidence

### Han et al. 2012

Ng Sook Han, Ho Ket Li, Lee Choy Sin, Keng Pei Sin. (2012). The Evaluation of Students' Written Reflection on the Learning of General Chemistry Lab Experiment. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Science, 2(4). http://www.moj-es.net

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-/post-intervention study in which 191 treatment and 191 control students (randomized) took five short-answer questions before and after one week of reflective writing following a titration experiment; analysis used the Wilcoxon signed-rank test, showing "a statistically significant difference between control and treatment groups". No effect size is printed.

> "In the post-intervention test, there was a statistically significant difference between control and treatment groups ( p = 0.000; z = -11.390)."
