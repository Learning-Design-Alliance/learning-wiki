## Evidence

### Gu 2025

Gu, C., Nastase, S. A., Zada, Z., & Li, P. (2025). Reading comprehension in L1 and L2 readers: neurocomputational mechanisms revealed through large language models. npj Science of Learning, 10:46. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00337-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Participant-specific ridge-regression encoding models built from the eighth layer of GPT-2 with leave-one-run-out cross-validation over five fMRI runs (51 L1, 55 L2). Alignment was strongest in bilateral lower-order visual regions and higher-order regions such as precuneus/PCC and LPFC, with "no significant group difference" reported.

> "We observed signiﬁcant model-brain alignment in widespread bilateral brain areas spanning visual and language in both L1 and L2 readers"
