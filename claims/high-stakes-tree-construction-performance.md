### Hobbs F. Collin 2013

Hobbs F. Collin, Johnson Daniel J., Kearns Katherine D. (2013). A Deliberate Practice Approach to Teaching Phylogenetic Analysis. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe-13-03-0046

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive performance analysis of a new tree-construction item on the second laboratory exam (seven taxa plus an extant outgroup; most parsimonious answer contained two instances of homoplasy). "57.8% of students received full credit" and 93.3% received 75% or more of possible points. No comparison-group data exist for this new item.

> "Of the 135 intervention group students who took the second laboratory exam, 57.8% of students received full credit for constructing an error-free phylogenetic tree. Almost all students (93.3%) received 75% or more of the possible points, indicating that intervention group students were capable of constructing complex phylogenetic trees."
