The comment reports, citing prior research, that adding friction to meaningless tasks increases appraised purpose. It uses this to explain why people perceive their own prose as more meaningful than ChatGPT-assisted prose. The cited study's design is not described in this article.

"Even when individuals work on objectivel y meaningless tasks, simply adding friction to these tasks increases their appraised sense of purpose and meaning6."
