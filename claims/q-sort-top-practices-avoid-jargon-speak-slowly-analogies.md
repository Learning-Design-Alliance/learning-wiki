### Tsai Han-Yi 2024

Tsai Han-Yi, Lee Shoou-Yih D., Coleman Cliff, Sørensen Kristine, Tsai Tzu-I. (2024). Health literacy competency requirements for health professionals: a Delphi consensus study in Taiwan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05198-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In Round 2 the Q method was applied: each of the 20 experts placed all 32 practice items on an e-format Q-sorting board ordered by importance. The Q-sort grid's mean and SD scores ranged "from 7.6 (SD ± 1.5) to 2.5 (SD ± 1.2)" (Fig. 1, Table 5).

> "Of the 32 health literacy practices, “avoidance using medical jargon,” “speaking slowly and clearly with patients,” and “using analogies and examples” were deemed most important by the panelists."
