## Evidence

### Luo 2016

Luo, W., Pelletier, J., Duffin, K., Ormand, C., Hung, W., Shernoff, D., Zhai, X., Iverson, E., Whalley, K., Gallaher, C., and Furness, W. (2016). Advantages of Computer Simulation in Enhancing Students' Learning About Landform Evolution: A Case Study Using the Grand Canyon. Journal of Geoscience Education 64, 60-73. https://doi.org/10.5408/15-080.1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-laboratory attitudinal survey (Table VI, 25 items, 1-6 scale) completed by students after both activities. The article reports an average mean of "4.23" and, for the four items comparing WILSIM-GC with paper-based methods, an average of "4.39".

> "The mean scores for most questions were above 4, and average of mean scores (excluding questions 9 and 13) was 4.23, indicating that most students generally agree with the statements."
