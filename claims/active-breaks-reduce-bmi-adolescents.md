## Evidence

### Latino Francesca 2023

Latino Francesca, Tafuri Francesco, Saraiello Emma, Tafuri Domenico. (2023). Classroom-Based Physical Activity as a Means to Improve Self-Efficacy and Academic Achievement among Normal-Weight and Overweight Youth. Nutrients 15(9):2061. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15092061

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Anthropometric analysis in the 100-student controlled trial; BMI measured with standardized instruments before and after the program showed a "meaningful ‘Time×Group’ interaction" with η2p = 0.37; post hoc t = 7.97, d = 1.12 for the EG.

> "A meaningful ‘Time×Group’ interaction was also reached for BMI (F1,98 = 59.30, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.37, large effect size)."

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

> "After the intervention program, the percentage of overweight children decreased from 46% to 28%."
