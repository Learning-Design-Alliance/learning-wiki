Analysis of administrative records for first-time third, sixth, and eighth graders in spring 1997 shows "Thirty-one percent of third graders and 20 percent of sixth and eighth graders were not subject to the policy," with grade-specific exclusion reasons as printed.

> "Thirty-one percent of third graders and 20 percent of sixth and eighth graders were not subject to the policy. For third graders, participation in bilingual education was the primary reason for exclusion. For sixth and eighth graders, classification in special education programs was the primary reason for exclusion."
