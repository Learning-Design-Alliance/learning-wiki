# Rasch-based refinement reduced the Early Ed Essentials surveys to 24 teacher measures (122 items) and 9 parent measures (42 items)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Applying Rasch-based criteria (person reliability, item fit, difficulty spread, and DIF) to the original surveys produced refined measures of 24 measures and 122 items on the teacher survey and 9 measures and 42 items on the parent survey. [→ Ehrlich 2018](#ehrlich-2018)

## Evidence

### Ehrlich 2018

Ehrlich, S.B., Pacchiano, D.M., Stein, A.G., Wagner, M.R., Luppescu, S., Park, S., Frank, E., Lewandowski, H., & Young, C. (2018). Organizing early education for improvement: Testing a new survey tool. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research and the Ounce of Prevention Fund. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/organizing-early-education-improvement-testing-new-survey-tool

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Measurement development analysis using the Rasch IRT model (Winsteps, Version 3.90.2) on teacher responses from validation sites plus additional CPS respondents (n=2,748 additional teacher responses) and parent responses. Items were revised or removed based on "person reliability," item fit (infit mean square < 1.30), difficulty spread, and differential item functioning.

> "These efforts and decisions led to the refined survey measures used in the next stage of analyses — with 24 measures and 122 items from the teacher survey and 9 measures and 42 items from the parent survey."
