## Evidence

### Jonte Bernhard 2018

Jonte Bernhard. (2018). What matters for students' learning in the laboratory? Do not neglect the role of experimental equipment! Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9469-x

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Affordance analysis of the tape timer lab (dots on a paper tape at 0.01 s intervals). The article states that "it is not possible to address the direction of acceleration by using this technology to record the cart's motion after pushing it up the inclined plane, or the magnitude of the acceleration at the turning point."

> "Since the tape is pulled by the cart through the tape timer, only downward motion can be studied. Upward motion would buckle the tape."
