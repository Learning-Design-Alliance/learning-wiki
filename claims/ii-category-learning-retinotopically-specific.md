# Information-integration category learning is retinotopically specific: accuracy drops at an untrained opposite-hemifield location

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` After II category training at one peripheral location, post-test accuracy at the transfer location in the opposite hemifield is significantly lower than at the trained location (d = 1.34). [→ Zhu 2026](#zhu-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhu 2026

Zhu, J.-P., Xie, X.-Y., Yu, C. & Zhang, J.-Y. (2026). Double training promotes retinotopic transfer of category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00396-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 1 (baseline group): 20 participants completed 10 blocks of the II category task with feedback, then post-tests at trained and transfer locations. Transfer accuracy was significantly lower, "Cohen's d = 1.34"; mean transfer index was 0.77 ± 0.04, significantly below 1.

> "accuracy at the transfer location was significantly lower than at the trained location (Fig. 2c; trained: 0.78 ± 0.02; transfer: 0.66 ± 0.02; difference = 0.127 ± 0.02, t = 6.22, p < 0.0001, 95% confidence interval (CI) [0.09, 0.17], Cohen's d = 1.34)"
