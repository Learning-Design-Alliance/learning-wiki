Thematic analysis of postintervention open-ended responses (T1) and immediate narrative evaluations in the pilot RCT. Categories included connectedness (n=7), recovery contribution (n=5), lack of contribution (n=5), and mixed emotional responses (n=1), with one participant reporting narratives triggered painful memories and nightmares.

"Participants described positive experiences related to recognition, hope, and meaning-making, but some also reported a lack of contribution to recovery and occasional distress."
