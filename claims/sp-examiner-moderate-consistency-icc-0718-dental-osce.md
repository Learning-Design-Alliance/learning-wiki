## Evidence

### Zhu Feng 2023

Zhu Feng, Wu Li, Shao Xiuxiu, Huang Lijuan, Meng Xiangfeng, Nie Rongrong. (2023). Scoring consistency of standard patients and examiners in the developed dental objective structured clinical examination system. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04087-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Consistency analysis of paired SP and examiner scores for 146 residents assessed at Nanjing Stomatological Hospital between 2018 and 2021, using a 2-way random-effects ICC. Means were "90.45 ± 3.52 and 91.53 ± 4.13" and the ICC was 0.718, described as medium consistency.

> "The average score of all examinees provided by SPs and examiners was 90.45 ± 3.52 and 91.53 ± 4.13, respectively. The consistency analysis showed that the intraclass correlation coefficient was 0.718, which was indicative of medium consistency."
