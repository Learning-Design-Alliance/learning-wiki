## Evidence

### Schwill Simon 2020

Schwill Simon, Reith Dorothee, Walter Tobias, Engeser Peter, Wensing Michel, Flum Elisabeth, Szecsenyi Joachim, Krug Katja. (2020). How to ensure basic competencies in end of life care – a mixed methods study with post-graduate trainees in primary care in Germany. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-020-00540-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dichotomous questions plus free-text qualitative content analysis in the follow-up survey of 153 intervention-group trainees. Reported changes included increased understanding for dying patients and relatives, enhanced self-confidence, and growth of awareness and interest in end-of-life care.

> "In total, 66% (n = 101/153) could reflect on their attitude because of the course and n = 28 (18.3%) had changed their attitude because of the intervention."
