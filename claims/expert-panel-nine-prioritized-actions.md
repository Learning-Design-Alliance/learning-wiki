### Juliá‐Sanchis 2025

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phase 2 one-round expert panel (seven members: coordinator, five faculty with simulation expertise, one student representative) rated 23 action points from non-priority (1) to top priority (7); items with mean above six were selected. Top-scoring items included establishing a fiction contract (6.57) and good-judgement debriefing training (6.57).

> "In Phase 2, these action points were prioritized by the expert panel. Those with an average score above six were selected and grouped into three categories (competitiveness of the master's degree program, aspects of the prebriefing, and aspects of the debriefing) detailed below."
