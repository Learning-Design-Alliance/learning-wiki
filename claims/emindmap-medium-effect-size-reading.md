## Evidence

### Mohaidat 2018

Mohaidat, M. M. T. (2018). The Impact of Electronic Mind Maps on Students' Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 11(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n4p32

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Effect-size analysis accompanying the same ANCOVA of posttest scores in the quasi-experimental study. The article reports η² = 0.1267 and Δ = 0.751, which it classifies as "medium" per Kailani and Shraifeen (2011), noting the value is close to the significant-effect threshold.

> "As for the size of the effect, the value of ( η2) was calculated and it was (0.1267). This value reflected that the power of the relationship was medium based on the total variance of the independent variable of the sample when the IMindMap was used. The Delta value ( Δ) was (0.751)."
