## Evidence

### Kelley 2015

Kelley, E. S., Goldstein, H., Spencer, T. D., & Sherman, A. (2015). Effects of automated Tier 2 storybook intervention on vocabulary and comprehension of preschool children with limited oral language skills. Early Childhood Research Quarterly, 31, 47-61. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2014.12.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed ANOVA on the Assessment of Story Comprehension, a researcher-made curriculum-based measure with nine parallel unfamiliar stories, administered approximately monthly to both groups (n = 9 per group). No significant group × time interaction on total score.

> "For the ASC total score, there was no signiﬁcant effect for group, a signiﬁcant effect of time [F(3, 48) = 4.05, p = 0.01, /DC1/SUB2 = 0.20], and no significant group × time interaction."

### Kelley 2015 (2)

Kelley, E. S., Goldstein, H., Spencer, T. D., & Sherman, A. (2015). Effects of automated Tier 2 storybook intervention on vocabulary and comprehension of preschool children with limited oral language skills. Early Childhood Research Quarterly, 31, 47-61. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2014.12.004

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Mixed 2 × 4 ANOVA on the four inferential ASC questions (maximum 8 points) in the same group design. The significant group × time interaction had η2 = 0.23 as printed; the article hypothesized group differences would emerge on inferential questions because the program targeted them.

> "For inferential questions, there was no main effect of group or time, but a signiﬁcant group × time interaction [F(3, 48) = 4.86, p ≤ 0.01, /DC1/SUB2 = 0.23]."
