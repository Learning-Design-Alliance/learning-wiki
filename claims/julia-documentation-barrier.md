## Evidence

### Luc T. Paoli 2024

Luc T. Paoli, Pavan K. Inguva, Andrew J. Haslam, and Pierre J. Walker. (2024). Confronting the thermodynamics knowledge gap: A short course on computational thermodynamics in Julia. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1016/j.ece.2024.03.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ece.2024.03.002)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey item on aspects of Julia programming that present an activation barrier (figure 10), from the 16-participant pilot. The authors attribute this to Julia's comparative youth, with packages having less comprehensive documentation and support than Python/MATLAB equivalents, and expect improvement over time.

> "The lack of documentation, which was not a significant a issue with Python, has become more of a barrier."
