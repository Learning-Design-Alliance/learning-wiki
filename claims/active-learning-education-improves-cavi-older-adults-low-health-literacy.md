## Evidence

### Uemura Kazuki 2021

Uemura Kazuki, Yamada Minoru, Kuzuya Masafumi, Okamoto Hiroshi. (2021). Effects of Active Learning Education on Arterial Stiffness of Older Adults with Low Health Literacy: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Atherosclerosis and Thrombosis. https://doi.org/10.5551/jat.58354

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis of a single-blinded RCT with 60 participants aged 65 and older with low health literacy, randomized to active learning (n=30) or didactic education (n=30), analyzed by intention-to-treat with multiple imputation. The intervention group showed "significant improvement in CAVI" with "Cohen’s d =0.82".

> "The multiple imputation analysis revealed that the intervention group showed significant improvement in CAVI [between-groups difference (95% confidence interval)=-0.78 (-1.25 to -0.31), Cohen’s d =0.82]"
