## Subclaims
`q2 i0` In a pilot cluster-randomized study, MDT-CBL interns achieved a numerically higher total knowledge test score (6.2 ± 2.5) than control interns (5.8 ± 3.4), with no statistically significant difference (p = 0.52; Cohen's d = 0.14). [→ Tang Shiying 2026](#tang-shiying-2026)

## Evidence

### Tang Shiying 2026

Tang Shiying, Deng Shaohui, Tian Yu, Qiu Min, Zhang Shudong. (2026). Integrating multidisciplinary team teaching with case-based learning in urology internship education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08730-w

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Pilot cluster-randomized controlled study (3 clusters; 32 experimental, 52 control interns) with a 20-item written test administered once post-intervention. Total score Cohen's d = 0.14, p = 0.52 (95%CI -0.86, 1.66); the printed effect size is below d = 0.2, so the difference is negligible in magnitude. The description quotes "none of these differences reached statistical significance".

> "interns in the MDT-CBL group achieved numerically higher mean scores on the theoretical knowledge test compared to those in the traditional teaching group, both for upper urinary tract diseases (2.5 vs. 2.2), lower urinary tract diseases (4.0 vs. 3.7), and in the total score (6.2 vs. 5.8). However, none of these differences reached statistical significance (all p > 0.05)."
