## Evidence

### Hillemans Vera 2023

Hillemans Vera, van de Mortel Xander, Buyne Otmar, Verhoeven Bas H., Botden Sanne M.B.I. (2023). Objective assessment for open surgical suturing training by finger tracking can discriminate novices from experts. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2023.2198818

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Construct validity analysis of 76 participants (57 novices, 19 experts) performing four suturing tasks, compared with Mann Whitney U tests. The study reports "Novices and experts differed significantly for the parameters time (p < 0.001) and distance (<0.001<p < 0.041) in all four tasks"; smoothness also differed significantly (p < 0.001) for all four tasks.
