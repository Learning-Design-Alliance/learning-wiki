## Evidence

### Javier Pulgar 2019

Javier Pulgar, Carlos Rios and Cristian Candia. (2019). Physics problems and instructional strategies for developing social networks in university classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1904.02840](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1904.02840)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression on information-seeking network data from 82 first-year engineering students in three sections of a General Mechanics course, collected in week 7. The model for log betweenness showed "a positive eﬀect for Class 3 (1.294, p <0.05), predicting 23% of the variance" (Table 1, R2 = 0.234), controlling for gender, test scores and good-student nominations.

> "On model for logarithm betweenness centrality, a centrality metric recognized with network connectivity, we observed a positive eﬀect for Class 3 (1.294, p <0.05), predicting 23% of the variance."
