## Evidence

### van Eeden 2017

van Eeden, R. & van Vuuren, J., 2017, 'The cognitive processing potential of infants: Exploring the impact of an early childhood development programme', South African Journal of Childhood Education 7(1), a499. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajce.v7i1.499

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of BSID (III) composite scores for the intervention group (N = 29) across the two assessments; the article reports "numerical increases on all subscales", clearest on the Cognitive and Social-Emotional Scales. No inferential test on the Social-Emotional Scale is reported.

> "There were numerical increases on all subscales for the intervention group after the intervention with scores ranging between 99.27 and 130.63 (Table 1). This was especially clear in the case of the Cognitive Scale and the Social-Emotional Scale."
