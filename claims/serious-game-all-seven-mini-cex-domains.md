## Evidence

### Deldar Kolsoum 2026

Deldar Kolsoum, Tavousi Seyed Hasan, Ahmadabadi Ali, Rakhsha Mohsen, Khodabandeloo Farnoosh, Froutan Razieh. (2026). Enhancing clinical competence in burn management through a serious educational game: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09970-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the RCT of 70 medical students, domain-level ANCOVA comparisons of intervention versus control were "statistically significant for every skill (all corrected P-values < 0.001)" with printed effect sizes of partial η² = 0.71 to 0.90, reported as secondary outcomes.

> "An ANCOVA, adjusted for baseline scores and corrected for multiple comparisons using the Holm-Bonferroni method, confirmed that the post-intervention differences were statistically significant for every skill (all corrected P-values < 0.001). The effect sizes for these differences were consistently large, as indicated by partial eta squared values ranging from 0.71 to 0.90"
