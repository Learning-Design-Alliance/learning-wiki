## Evidence

### Bailey 2018

Bailey, D. H., Duncan, G. J., Watts, T., Clements, D. H., & Sarama, J. (2018). Risky Business: Correlation and Causation in Longitudinal Studies of Skill Development. American Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1037/amp0000146

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling applied to three correlational datasets (Table 1) compared with MS paths inferred from three experimental studies. The article reports weighted study average MS paths of .35 (state-trait) versus .44 (experimental), and that the state-trait estimate "falls within the confidence interval of every observed impact in the TRIAD study".

> "In fact, the average MS path estimated from the state-trait model falls within the confidence interval of every observed impact in the TRIAD study, while this is true of only 1 out of 5 regression-based estimates."
