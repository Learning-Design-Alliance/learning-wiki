Evidence¶

Zandieh 2012¶

Zandieh, Z., & Jafarigohar, M. (2012). The Effects of Hypertext Gloss on Comprehension and Vocabulary Retention under Incidental and Intentional Learning Conditions. English Language Teaching, 5(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n6p60

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Comprehension test results from the same 184-participant comparison-group study; both groups scored around 50% of the 15-point total. The article notes t observed of 0.69 was lower than t critical of 1.98.

"The intentional group members did not score better than the intentional group members on the comprehension test and both groups did very moderately as supported by the group mean of 13.89 for the incidental group, and the mean of 14.56 for intentional group, when t observed of 0.69 was lower than t critical of 1.98 which means that the difference was not significant."
