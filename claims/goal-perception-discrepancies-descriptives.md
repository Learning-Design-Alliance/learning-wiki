## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zhao 2025

Zhao, N., Dou, D., Chen, X., Chen, F., Luo, R., Zhu, X., & Xiang, G.-X. (2025). Exploring the congruence between perceived parent–teacher achievement goals and student academic outcomes: A study using polynomial regression with response surface analysis. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 603–626. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12742

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive preliminary analysis of the 4944-student dataset; paired t-tests on perceived teacher versus parent goal levels supported proceeding to congruence modelling.

> "both the differences of mastery goals (t = −15.453, p < .001) and performance goals (t = −9.646, p < .001) between teacher and parent were statistically significant."

### Zhao 2025 (2)

Zhao, N., Dou, D., Chen, X., Chen, F., Luo, R., Zhu, X., & Xiang, G.-X. (2025). Exploring the congruence between perceived parent–teacher achievement goals and student academic outcomes: A study using polynomial regression with response surface analysis. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 603–626. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12742

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Table 4) among perceived goals and IRT-scaled achievement; correlations between teacher and parent goal perceptions ranged from 0.053 to 0.411.

> "Parents' performance goals were significantly negatively correlated with both students' mathematics and language achievement. However, no significant correlation was found between parents' mastery goals and language achievement."
