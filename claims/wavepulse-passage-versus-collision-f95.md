## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 1999

Michael C. Wittmann, Richard N. Steinberg, Edward F. Redish. (1999). Making Sense of How Students Make Sense of Mechanical Waves. The Physics Teacher. [doi:10.1119/1.880142](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.880142)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Diagnostic test given to 182 students at the beginning of Fall-1995 before all instruction on waves. The most common incorrect answer (one fifth of students) showed permanent cancellation; one student wrote "[Part of] the greater wave is canceled by the smaller one."

> "A correct response to the question (shown in Figure 6(a)), given by 55% of the students, shows that the wavepulses pass through each other. Superposition of waves should have no permanent effect on the two waves."
