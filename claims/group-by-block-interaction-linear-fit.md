# Training group and trial block interacted in producing linear estimation patterns, indicating different rates of learning across feedback conditions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A 4x4 repeated-measures ANOVA showed effects of training group, trial block, and their interaction on whether the linear function best fit children's estimates. [→ Opfer 2007](#opfer-2007)

## Evidence

### Opfer 2007

Opfer, J. E., & Siegler, R. S. (2007). Representational change and children's numerical estimation. Cognitive Psychology 55 (2007) 169–195. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2006.09.002

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus repeated-measures ANOVA over the four conditions and four trial blocks in Experiment 2. The interaction F(9, 171) = 3.83, p < .001 reflected faster learning in the 150-feedback group, whose estimates fit the linear function on 60% of trial blocks versus 3% in the no-feedback group.

> "A 4 (training group: 5-, 150-, 725-, or no-feedback) £ 4 (trial block: pretest, 1, 2, 3) repeated-measures ANOVA indicated eVects for training group, F(3, 57) D 13.50, p < .001, for trial block, F(3, 171) D 26.36, p < .001, and for the interaction between the two variables, F(9, 171) D 3.83, p < .001"
