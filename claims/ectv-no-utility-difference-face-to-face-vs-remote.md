## Evidence

### Fielding Alison 2025

Fielding Alison, Mundy Benjamin, Tapley Amanda, Gani Sarah, Ali Rula, Bentley Michael, Boland Rachael, Zbaidi Lina, Holliday Elizabeth, Ball Jean, van Driel Mieke, Klein Linda, Magin Parker. (2025). Educational utility of observational workplace-based assessment modalities in Australian vocational general practice training: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07328-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of GP registrars (741 observations) and ECT visitors (696 observations) after ECTVs in 2020, analysed with multivariable logistic regression (GEE). The study reports no significant differences between face-to-face and remote video/phone ECTVs, with an abstract-reported multivariable "p-value range: .07-.96". No effect size printed.

> "No significant differences in educational utility ratings were identified between face-to-face and remote video/phone ECTVs (Table 4)."
