### Bhuvaneswari S 2026

Bhuvaneswari S, Bamini Devi N, Helen Shaji J C, Lakshmi L. (2026). Voices of learners: a qualitative exploration of nursing students’ satisfaction with simulation-based basic life support training. BMC Medical Education, 26(1). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09260-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic finding from five focus group discussions with 40 nursing students. Students reported that initial anxiety gave way to accomplishment and increased confidence after repeated practice, with one stating "After practicing here, I feel less scared to handle real emergencies."

> "While initial anxiety was commonly reported, students described a sense of accomplishment and increased confidence following repeated practice and successful scenario completion. Many participants emphasized that simulation provided a safe space to make mistakes and learn without fear of harming patients."
