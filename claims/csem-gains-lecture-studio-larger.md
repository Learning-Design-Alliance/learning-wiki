## Evidence

### David P. Smith 2018

David P. Smith, Laurie E. McNeil, David T. Guynn, Alice D. Churukian, Duane L. Deardorff, Colin S. Wallace. (2018). Transforming the content, pedagogy and structure of an introductory physics course for life sciences majors. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/1.5058685](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5058685)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of CSEM average normalized gains before and after the Spring 2015 transition to Lecture/Studio with new content. The article adds that gains of 0.20-0.30 in the transformed course are comparable to published CSEM gains for algebra-based (0.25) and calculus-based (0.23) physics students, and concludes the teaching format had a statistically significant impact on gains.

> "The measured learning gains for the courses taught in the Lecture/Studio format were strikingly larger than any measured gain for the course in its traditional format."
