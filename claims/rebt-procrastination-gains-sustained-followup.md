## Evidence

### Amoke CV 2025

Amoke CV, Ede MO, Mawila-Chauke D, Obeagu IE. (2025). Disputing and challenging academic procrastination behaviors in students using REBT approach. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000041725

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up repeated measures ANOVA in the same quasi-experimental study of 129 adolescents: REBT group mean 48.19 (SD 5.62) versus control 53.06 (SD 5.48), described as a "significant effect on participants’ academic procrastination" with d = 0.877.

> "The follow-up results also showed that the intervention had a significant effect on participants’ academic procrastination, F(1, 127) = 24.820, P = .001, d = 0.877."
