Descriptive subscale results across N = 22 participants playing one generated scenario each. Coherence (Cronbach's α = 0.87) had M = 2.92, SD = 1.02, and "its scale CI's upper bound only just brushes the mid -point" of the five-point scale.

"story –content coherence was clearly the weakest dimension: its scale CI's upper bound only just brushes the mid -point. Three of the four coherence items scored below the mid -point"
