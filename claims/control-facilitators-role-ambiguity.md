## Evidence

### de la Varre 2010

de la Varre, C., Keane, J., & Irvin, M. J. (2010). Enhancing online distance education in small rural US schools: A hybrid, learner-centered model. Journal of Asynchronous Learning Networks, 15(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ951828

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of end-of-year facilitator interviews. Control facilitators described uncertainty about boundaries, with one noting "I was unsure of what line I was crossing." The article states role ambiguity "did not appear to be an issue with the intervention facilitators."

> "For the facilitators in the control group, training-related feedback pointed to a lack of clarity about their role, and the standard training materials did not seem to offer enough guidance."
