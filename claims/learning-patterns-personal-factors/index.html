Evidence¶

Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche 2017¶

Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche. (2017). A Learning Patterns Perspective on Student Learning in Higher Education: State of the Art and Moving Forward. Educ Psychol Rev 29:269–299. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9414-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of studies since 2004 (e.g., Donche et al. 2013; Fryer et al. 2016; Ferla et al. 2008; Zhu et al. 2008; Vermunt 2005) concludes learning patterns are embedded in this range of personal factors, while motivations, beliefs and attributions remain malleable over time.

"the empirical studies discussed above have shown that the learning patterns that students adopt are embedded in a range of personal factors such as personality, academic motivation, goal orientation, attributions of academic success, self-efficacy, effort, epistemological and intelligence beliefs, prior education, age, and gender"

Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche 2017 (2)¶

Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche. (2017). A Learning Patterns Perspective on Student Learning in Higher Education: State of the Art and Moving Forward. Educ Psychol Rev 29:269–299. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9414-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports that personality associations (openness with self-regulated and deep processing, conscientiousness with analytic processing, neuroticism with lack of regulation) are weak to moderate, supporting the dynamic rather than trait-like nature of learning patterns.

"The strength of these associations is weak to moderate which provides further evidence that learning patterns are not stable traits"
