Conceptual distinction drawn in Section 3.2 of the position paper. The article presents this as a definitional contrast rather than an empirically tested difference; no data support it.

"Unlike prompt engineering, which focuses primarily on optimizing outputs, AI intuition emphasizes epistemic judgment, knowing when to trust, verify, constrain, or disengage from AI systems."
