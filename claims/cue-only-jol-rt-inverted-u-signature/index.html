Evidence¶

Ritter 2025¶

Ritter, C., Hausman, H., Gaschler, R., Tiffin-Richards, S. P., & Kubik, V. (2025). The Forward Effect of Judgements of Learning on Memory and Transfer in Inductive Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10094-4

q2 · i? · causal · r3

Non-preregistered item-level mixed-effects and piecewise regression analyses of JOL response times in Experiment 1. Below the 50% breakpoint, slopes differed between cue-only and cue–target JOLs, χ2(1) = 5.26, p = .022; only test statistics are printed.

"Collapsing across JOL types, there was a significant quadratic effect, χ2(1) = 34.32, p < .001, establishing nonlinearity in the overall JOL–RT relationship (Fig. 5). As predicted, only cue-only JOLs showed the inverted Upattern."
