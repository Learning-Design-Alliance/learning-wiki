## Evidence

### Yu Yang 2026

Yu Yang, Luoye Fangxin, You Danhui, Xu Zhenhong, Liu Yuping, Wu Li, Deng Qian, Ning Yanting, Liu Liqiong. (2026). Heterogeneity and associated factors of palliative care core competency among oncology nurses: a latent profile analysis. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21645

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 241 oncology nurses from five Shenzhen hospitals using cluster sampling; LPA models (1–4 classes) fitted in R with ECC, CPC, and PSA as continuous indicators. The three-class solution showed "entropy = 0.931" with significant LMR and BLRT tests.

> "LPA revealed three distinct profiles: low-competency (27%), moderate -competency (45%), and high-competency (28%). The three-profile solution was selected by considering model fit indices, entropy, class proportions, and substantive interpretability (entropy = 0.931; Lo-Mendell-Rubin and bootstrap likelihood ratio tests P < 0.001)."
