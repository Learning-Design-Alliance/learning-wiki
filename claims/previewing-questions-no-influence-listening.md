### Rouhi 2014

Rouhi, A., Nabavi, S.-M., & Mohebbi, H. (2014). The effects of previewing questions, repetition of input, and topic preparation on listening comprehension of Iranian EFL learners. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 2(2), 73-85. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Findings for the previewing questions condition (n = 25) of the four-group classroom experiment: one hearing preceded by question preview. The article reports no statistically significant difference from the control group; equivalence was not tested.

> "In the present study, the previewing questions group was not able to perform better than the topic preparation and repetition of input groups."
