Quantile regression analysis reported in Persson et al, one of the two included Swedish cohort studies, examining how the diabetes effect varies across the attainment distribution. The review reports the effect as greatest in the lowest quantile and insignificant for the highest.

> "Quantile regression—negative effect of T1DM greatest in the lowest quantile of attainment, diminishing with increasing attainment quantiles, becoming statistically insignificant for the highest attaining quantile."
