## Evidence

### Yeung C 2017

Yeung C, Friesen F, Farr S, Law M, Albert L. (2017). Development and implementation of a longitudinal students as teachers program: participant satisfaction and implications for medical student teaching and learning. BMC Medical Education 17:28. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0857-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post questionnaire comparison in the mixed methods evaluation of 18 second-year medical students, analyzed with two-way repeated measures ANOVA and Wilcoxon signed rank tests. The article reports a statistically significant increase with no effect size printed.

> "Participants’ confidence in small and large group teaching and in answering audience questions were significantly increased by the end of the program"

### Yeung C 2017 (2)

Yeung C, Friesen F, Farr S, Law M, Albert L. (2017). Development and implementation of a longitudinal students as teachers program: participant satisfaction and implications for medical student teaching and learning. BMC Medical Education 17:28. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0857-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same pre/post questionnaire analysis (Fig. 2b). The article reports statistically significant improvements in perceived teaching skill domains; only the significance of the change is stated, with no effect size printed.

> "This is in line with significant improvements in their self-perceived teaching skills, which included skills like their ability to gauge audience understanding, prepare for small and large group teaching sessions, and address different learning needs"
