## Evidence

### Tadese Mesfin 2022

Tadese Mesfin, Yeshaneh Alex, Mulu Getaneh Baye. (2022). Determinants of good academic performance among university students in Ethiopia: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03461-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariable cross-tabulation among the 615 students found smoking significantly associated with performance (p = 0.028); khat use was also significant (p = 0.044, 55.0% of users poor). Only chi-square p-values are printed, with no effect size.

> "About 58.8% of students who smoke cigarettes had PAP, whereas 66.7% of students who didn’t smoke scored GAP (chi2 p = 0.028)."
