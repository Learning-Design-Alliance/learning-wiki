## Evidence

### Zhao Jingjie 2020

Zhao Jingjie, Xu Xinliang, Jiang Hualin, Ding Yi. (2020). The effectiveness of virtual reality-based technology on anatomy teaching: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled studies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1994-z

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis by learner type across the 15 trials (12 studies of medical students, 3 of residents), reported in Table 2. The review reports the printed SMDs and p values for both subgroups.

> "the moderator analysis revealed significant benefits of VR in the subgroup of medical students (SMD = 0.51; 95% CI 0.02–1.01, p = 0.04), whereas VR have no significant influence on residents (SMD = 0.67; 95% CI -0.45–1.01, p = 0.24)"
