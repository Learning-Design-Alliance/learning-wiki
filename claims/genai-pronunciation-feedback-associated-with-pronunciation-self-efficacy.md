# Chinese EFL learners' positive perceptions of GenAI-based pronunciation feedback are positively associated with their English pronunciation self-efficacy

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Perceptions of GenAI-based pronunciation feedback are positively and significantly associated with English pronunciation self-efficacy (β = 0.607). [→ Lu Y 2026](#lu-y-2026)

## Evidence

### Lu Y 2026

Lu Y, Yang Y, Cui T, Yang Z, Cai Y and Jing B (2026) Associations between generative AI–based pronunciation feedback and willingness to communicate in English: the mediating role of English pronunciation self-efficacy. Front. Psychol. 17:1918980. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1918980

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis within the study's covariance-based SEM of 1,701 Chinese university EFL learners, using bias-corrected bootstrapping with 5,000 resamples. The direct path from GenAI feedback to EPSE was significant, "β = 0.607", with a confidence interval excluding zero.

> "Similarly, GenAI feedback was positively associated with EPSE, B = 0.655, β = 0.607, 95% CI [0.606, 0.711], p < 0.001, indi -cating a strong association between GenAI and EPSE."
