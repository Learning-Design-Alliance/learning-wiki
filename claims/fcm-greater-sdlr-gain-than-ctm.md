### Chikeme Paulina Chigwara 2024

Chikeme Paulina Chigwara, Ihudiebube-Splendor Chikaodili Ndidiamaka, Ogbonnaya Ngozi Phoebe, Mbadugha Chisom Joy, Elodi Laurentia Onyinye. (2024). Flipped classroom model versus conventional teaching method: effects on nursing students’ self-directed learning readiness in a research methodology course. Pan African Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.11604/pamj.2024.47.70.38359

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental study of 64 fourth-year nursing students at two Southeast Nigerian universities, with 32 per intact class exposed to FCM or conventional teaching for 8 weeks. Independent-sample t-test of pre-to-post SDLRS mean-difference showed FCM "improved more" with "p = 0.030"; no effect size is printed.

> "Results in Table 3 showed that the students in the FCM group (MD = 0.18) improved more in their self-directed learning readiness than the students in the CTM group (MD = 0.08) following exposure to the intervention (p = 0.030)."
