Evidence¶

Burns 2022¶

Burns, D., Espinoza, D., Adams, J., & Ondrasek, N. (2022). California students in foster care: Challenges and promising practices. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/118.471

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of publicly available 2018–19 California DataQuest data on absenteeism. Students in foster care averaged 15 days absent versus 10 for others; high school students in foster care averaged nearly 23 days, missing "1 out of every 8 school days."

"In total, nearly 28% of students in foster care were chronically absent, while for non-foster students, this was just 12%."

Burns 2022 (2)¶

Burns, D., Espinoza, D., Adams, J., & Ondrasek, N. (2022). California students in foster care: Challenges and promising practices. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/118.471

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of 2018–19 suspension data. The report states the 15% foster-care rate "was more than 4 times the rate for non-foster students (3.4%)" and was especially high among African American students in foster care (22%), more than half of whom were suspended multiple times.

"The suspension rate (in school and out of school) for students in foster care in 2018–19 was 15%, about the same rate as for the previous 2 years."
