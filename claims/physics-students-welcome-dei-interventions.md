The review summarizes published reports of DEI interventions in physics settings, noting "typically, 75% –100% of participants have welcomed the experience." One study found 75%–80% of student responses positive, 15%–20% neutral, and 0%–5% negative.

> "Daane and Rifkin reported that 96% of high school students found that a DEI unit was worthwhile, and 90% of college students said that a similar unit was a positive experience [39]."
