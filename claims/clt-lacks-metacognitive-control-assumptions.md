## Evidence

### Katharina Scheiter 2025

Katharina Scheiter. (2025). Commentary: How can We Come to Terms when Discussing the Role of Effort from the Perspective of Cognitive Load Theory and Theories of Self-regulated Learning? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10037-z

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the commentary's synthesis of the control mapping problem: CLT defines resources only at the object level (visuo-spatial sketchpad, phonological loop) and, per the article, "comparable assumptions are missing in CLT" for metacognitive regulation.

> "while SRL theories contain explicit assumptions on how cognition (as well as motivation, affect, and effort) is monitored and regulated through metacognition, comparable assumptions are missing in CLT."
