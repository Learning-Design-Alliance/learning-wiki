## Evidence

### Mascheroni J 2024

Mascheroni J, Stockburger M, Patwala A, Mont L, Rao A, Retzlaff H, Garweg C, Gallagher AG, Verbelen T. (2024). Surgical skill simulation training to proficiency reduces procedural errors among novice cardiac device implanters: a randomized study. Europace. https://doi.org/10.1093/europace/euae229

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Video-scored randomized comparison of 30 novice implanters found the PBP group made "61.2% fewer Critical Errors"; the abstract additionally reports P < 0.001 for this contrast.

> "PBP trainees consistently outperformed SIM trainees in the surgical phases of the implant procedure. In comparison to the SIM group, the PBP group completed, on average, 11.0% more procedural Steps and made 57.1% fewer Errors, 61.2% fewer Critical Errors, and 60.7% fewer All Errors Combined"
