## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Parents with higher ANS (w-score) reported children with higher skills (pathway = .16), and higher parent ANS was associated with lower spatial environment scores (pathway = -.17). [→ Hart 2016](#hart-2016)
`q2 i?` In the final model, parent ANS had a direct effect (pathway = .15, 2% of variance) and an indirect effect through the spatial factor (1% of variance), with worse-ANS parents reporting more spatial activities. [→ Hart 2016 (2)](#hart-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Hart 2016

Hart, S. A., Ganley, C. M., & Purpura, D. J. (2016). Understanding the Home Math Environment and Its Role in Predicting Parent Report of Children's Math Skills. PLoS ONE 11(12): e0168227. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0168227

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Third alternative-explanation structural model adding parent ANS (Panamath w-score, n = 315 after exclusions) as direct and indirect predictor; including ANS made the spatial factor nonsignificant and no standardized effect size was printed.

> "Results indicated that parent ANS has a positive, direct relation to parent report of child skills, in that parents with higher ANS had children with higher parent report of child skills (pathway = .16). Additionally, higher parent ANS was associated with lower scores on the spatial environment specific factor (pathway = -.17)."

### Hart 2016 (2)

Hart, S. A., Ganley, C. M., & Purpura, D. J. (2016). Understanding the Home Math Environment and Its Role in Predicting Parent Report of Children's Math Skills. PLoS ONE 11(12): e0168227. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0168227

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fourth final model with all alternative explanations included; the indirect ANS effect through the spatial factor accounted for 1% of the variance in parent report of child skills.

> "Parent ANS also had an indirect effect on parent report of child skills, through the spatial environment specific factor, in that parents with worse ANS scores reported participating in more spatially-related home activities which then predicted lower parent reported child math skills."
