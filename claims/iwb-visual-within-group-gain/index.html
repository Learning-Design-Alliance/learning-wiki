Evidence¶

Abdulkadir Kırbas 2018¶

Abdulkadir Kırbas. (2018). The Effect of Interactive Whiteboard Applications Supported by Visual Materials on Middle School Students' Listening Comprehension and Persistence of Learning. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(11): 2552-2561. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061120

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-samples t test within the experimental group (n=36) comparing pre- and post-test LAT scores: t = -27.41, p = .000, significant at the .05 level. The article attributes the difference to the increase in the experimental group's post-test scores.

"the pre-test achievement score of the students in the experimental group was 51.25 before the implementation, while their mean post-test score was 76.94. The t value, which was calculated based on the pre- and post- test scores of the experimental group, was -27.41. The paired -samples t test that was carried out to determine whether there was any difference between the pre-test and post-test yielded p=.000"
