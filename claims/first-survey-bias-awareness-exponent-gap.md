### Laszlo Frazer 2019

Laszlo Frazer, Heather F. Higginbotham, Toby D. M. Bell, and Alison M. Funston. (2019). 'It's fundamental': Quantum dot blinking experiment to teach critical thinking. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00679](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00679)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Written free-response survey of 15 volunteers immediately after data analysis, with four prompts. The article also reports 73% identified a data-exclusion decision and 53% identified a threshold criterion; every participant thought about decisions in at least one of three ways.

> "While 60% of students identified a potential eﬀect of bias, only 20% suggested that bias might change the value of the power law exponent, µ. None of those students successfully identiﬁed a relationship between a bias and the distribution of oﬀ-state durations."
