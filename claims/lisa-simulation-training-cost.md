### Breindahl Niklas 2025

Breindahl Niklas, Bay Emma Therese, Heiring Christian, MacLean-Nyegaard Ingrid Rose, Gudiksen Amalie, Frithioff Andreas, Henriksen Tine Brink, Tolsgaard Martin Grønnebæk, Aunsholt Lise. (2025). Assessing competency in less invasive surfactant administration: simulation-based validity evidence for the LISA-AT scores. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-025-03868-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Micro-costing analysis (four-step approach) based on the observed median 6 attempts of ~10 min each, simulator depreciation, and Danish salary assumptions. Reports median direct costs of "USD 68" (45–102); sensitivity analysis identified raters' salary as the key variable.

> "Based on the micro-costing procedure, the direct costs of onsite LISA simulation training with basic simulators using trained investigators as onsite raters were USD 68, ranging from USD 45–102 per novice to achieve the minimum passing score (Table 5)."
