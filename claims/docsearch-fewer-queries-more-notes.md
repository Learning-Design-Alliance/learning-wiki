### Taneja 2026

Taneja, K., Singh, A., & Goel, A.K. (2026). Impact of Multimodal and Conversational AI on Learning Outcomes and Experience. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_60](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_60)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Behavioral log analysis from the RCT measuring queries and notepad edits across conditions. The DocSearch group made "M= 5.27,SD= 2.74queries" and examined M=18.8 search results; the article summarizes this as "a more traditional approach to reading with fewer queries and more note-taking."

> "Compared to the systems with conversa- tional AI, theDocSearchgroup made significantly fewer queries (p < .001, d= −0.63), but made significantly more updates to their notes (p=.008, d= 0.50)."
