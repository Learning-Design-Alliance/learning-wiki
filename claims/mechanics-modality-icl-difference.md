`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject mechanics experiment in which 54 students completed inquiry, design, and game labs and rated load after each. The article reports "a statistically significant difference in ICL between the three modalities (p < .001)"; design produced the highest ICL and game the lowest. No effect size is printed.

> "One-way ANOV A results indicate the there is a statistically significant difference in ICL between the three modalities (p < .001) exceeding a critical value of (F crit = 3.05)."
