### Almagribi Ahmad Zaker 2024

Almagribi Ahmad Zaker, Al-Qahtani Saad Misfer, Assiri Abdullah M., Mehdar Khlood Mohammed. (2024). Perceptions of medical students at Najran University on the effectiveness of problem-based learning and team-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06148-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kirkpatrick Level 3 (behavior) survey responses from the cross-sectional study of 106 preclinical NU students. The article reports that "TBL required more individual preparation and effort, with 49.1% of participants identifying it as such" and 61.3% saw TBL as needing lecturer involvement versus 7.5% for PBL. The article does not explain the discrepancy between the 49.1% figure and its stated comparison value.

> "According to the responses, TBL required more individual preparation and effort, with 49.1% of participants identifying it as such, while 22.6% identified it as such. The majority (61.3%) believed that TBL required lecturer involvement, while only 7.5% believed the same for PBL."
