## Evidence

### Williams 2017

Williams, S. N., Thakore, B. K., & McGee, R. (2017). Providing Social Support for Underrepresented Racial and Ethnic Minority PhD Students in the Biomedical Sciences: A Career Coaching Model. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0021

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview study of 33 URM students; Table 2 codes 19 students receiving informational support from coaches. This quote shows a coach sharing knowledge of a minority-focused NIH fellowship the student's PI had never heard of.

> "My coach told me about the Institutional Research and Academic Career Development Awards at the NIH. It's like a teaching and research post doc and a grant that half the students have to be female and half the students have to be underrepresented minorities"

### Williams 2017 (2)

Williams, S. N., Thakore, B. K., & McGee, R. (2017). Providing Social Support for Underrepresented Racial and Ethnic Minority PhD Students in the Biomedical Sciences: A Career Coaching Model. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0021

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same interview study; 17 students were coded as receiving informational support from peers. This quote shows a student learning grant-framing strategies from a peer who had won an NSF fellowship.

> "I talked with another guy in my group who I know got an NSF [National Science Foundation] fellowship and I asked him how he was successful and he told me exactly what he did."
