In the qualitative interview study, Professor Duniya called hype "a double-edged sword", noting it keeps people interested in quantum mechanics and attracts industry, while creating "a real need to manage expectations" because deliverables lie far in the future. Other educators echoed both positives (funding, jobs) and harms (over-promising).

> "I think that the hype is both helping us and hurting us"
