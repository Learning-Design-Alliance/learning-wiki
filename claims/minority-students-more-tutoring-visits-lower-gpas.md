## Evidence

### Cooper 2010

Cooper, E. (2010). Tutoring Center Effectiveness: The Effect of Drop-In Tutoring. Journal of College Reading and Learning, 40(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ887303

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of visit frequency by ethnicity and first-generation status in the two fall 2007 cohorts. The article also reports, citing Kezar & Eckel, that for tutored classes both minority and first-generation students had lower average GPAs than Caucasian or non-first-generation students.

> "On average minority students visited the TC more often than did Caucasian students (F= 9.472, p=.002), but there was no significant difference between the visits of first generation and non-first generation students."
