## Evidence

### Wu Yun 2026

Wu Yun, Li Senyuan, Gao Tong, Zhou Yipeng, Zhuang Yuandong. (2026). Tracing-based drawing method enhances anatomy learning outcomes and proportional awareness: a randomized cross-over study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10024-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects model treating questionnaire ratings as repeated observations within the 60 students. The article reports "an adjusted mean difference of 0.55 points on the five-point scale (95% CI: 0.49 to 0.62; p < 0.0001)"; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "students rated TBDM higher than TMBM across all eight learner-reported dimensions, including learning interest, structural understanding, memory clarity, confidence in expression, perceived clinical utility, sense of achievement, overall satisfaction, and intention for continued use."
