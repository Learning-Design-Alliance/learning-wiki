Craigo L. 2017¶

Craigo L., Ehri, L. C., & Hart, M. (2017). Teaching community college students strategies for learning unknown words as they read expository text. Higher Learning Research Communications, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.18870/hlrc.v7i1.350

q3 · i0 · causal · r1

ANCOVA on the fourth passage, read independently without intervention, in the same randomized experiment; Table 3 prints transfer ds of .03, .03, and .00 for comprehension questions and negative ds for cloze.

"As shown in Tables 3 and 4, the treatment groups performed no better than controls on the two comprehe nsion measures involving questions and cloze. Effect sizes were close to zero."

Craigo L. 2017 (2)¶

Craigo L., Ehri, L. C., & Hart, M. (2017). Teaching community college students strategies for learning unknown words as they read expository text. Higher Learning Research Communications, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.18870/hlrc.v7i1.350

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Transfer-task definition recall ANCOVA; the article notes definitions were not provided on the transfer task, so the definition application strategy could not be used, and definition-trained groups scored slightly worse (ds -.42 and -.10).

"The ANCOVA of transfer task perf ormance revealed no significant main effect of treatment, indicating that word learning strategies were not utilized when participants read text independently."
