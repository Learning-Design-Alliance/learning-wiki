# CLIL participation shows no significant effect on primary students' Spanish-language subject marks

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a longitudinal two-group study of Galician primary schools, the CLIL factor had no significant effect on Spanish-language marks (p=0.152). [→ González Gándara 2015](#gonzalez-gandara-2015)

## Evidence

### González Gándara 2015

González Gándara, D. (2015). CLIL in Galicia: Repercussions on academic performance. Latin American Journal of Content and Language Integrated Learning, 8(1), 13-24. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1132120

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal variance analysis of school marks from 13 experimental and 44 control classrooms (747 students) in Galician public primary schools. The time*CLIL interaction for Spanish marks was non-significant, with the article reporting "no significant effect from the CLIL factor" (p=0.152).

> "Finally, there is no significant effect from the CLIL factor on any of the two subjects studied; in fact, it is very far from being significant (p=0.275 y p=0.152)."
