# Academic-media multitasking shows no direct long-term relationship with subsequent academic achievement in adolescents

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a three-wave longitudinal study, academic-media multitasking did not predict subsequent academic achievement scores. [→ W.A. van der Schuur 2020](#wa-van-der-schuur-2020)

## Evidence

### W.A. van der Schuur 2020

W.A. van der Schuur, S.E. Baumgartner, S.R. Sumter, P.M. Valkenburg. (2020). Exploring the long-term relationship between academic-media multitasking and adolescents' academic achievement. New Media and Society. https://doi.org/10.1177/1461444819861956

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three-wave longitudinal study with 3–4 month intervals among 1215 adolescents aged 11–15 (52% boys). The study found "neither support for a direct nor indirect long-term relationship" with achievement; no effect size is printed in the available text.

> "the findings showed neither support for a direct nor indirect long-term relationship between academic-media multitasking and subsequent academic achievement scores"
