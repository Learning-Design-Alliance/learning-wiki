### Stephen Kanim and Ximena C. Cid 2017

Stephen Kanim and Ximena C. Cid. (2017). The demographics of physics education research. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.02598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.02598)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey analysis of upper-level course subjects in the three-journal corpus. The authors estimate about 58% of the upper-division research sample would have SAT Math above 600 versus about 25% expected at all schools, and about 60% White versus 45% of all students.

> "Our data includes results from 6,722 students in upper-level physics courses. (Again, this is after removing students from unnamed institutions.)  Only 22 different universities are represented, and over 95% of these students attend 9 universities. Since the two universities reporting the most students account for 80% of the upper-level students included in this study, the demographics of these two universities strongly determines the demographics of physics education research conducted in upper-level courses"
