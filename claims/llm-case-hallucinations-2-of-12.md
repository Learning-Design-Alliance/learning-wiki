Expert review of the twelve ChatGPT-generated cases found hallucinations in two: Case 8 (chalazion) inappropriately included high-frequency ultrasound findings, and Case 4 (acute angle-closure glaucoma) reported corneal edema preventing fundus examination yet paradoxically described gonioscopy findings.

> "Additionally, 16.67% of the generated cases exhibited hallucinations [13–17], characterized by contradictory descriptions of ancillary tests and deviations from clinical practices."
