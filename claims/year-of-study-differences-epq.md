Best Educational Techniques in High-Fidelity Simulation According to Nursing Students—Adaptation and Validation of the Educational Practices Questionnaire (EPQ). `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Socio-demographic analysis of the Polish sample (Table 7): Collaboration EPQ-PO scores differed significantly by year of studies, with second-year students (M = 9.22) higher than third-year (M = 8.75), p = 0.049; gender, age and residence differences were not significant.

> "The results for the scale regarding collaboration in the EPQ questionnaire (Table 7) show a statistically significant differentiation in the points obtained in terms of year of studies (p = 0.049). The second year of studies (9.22) demonstrated a significantly higher result in this subscale than did the third year of studies (8.75)"
