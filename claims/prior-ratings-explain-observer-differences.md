## Evidence

### Sartain 2011

Sartain, L., Stoelinga, S. R., & Brown, E. R., with Luppescu, S., Kapadia Matsko, K., Miller, F. K., Durwood, C. E., Jiang, J. Y., & Glazer, D. (2011). Rethinking Teacher Evaluation in Chicago: Lessons Learned from Classroom Observations, Principal-Teacher Conferences, and District Implementation. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Statistical re-analysis of the matched ratings showed that after controlling for prior checklist evaluation ratings, "much of the variation between principal and observer ratings disappeared," and the high-end difference "is no longer statistically significant." The authors offer two interpretations: principals may use historical knowledge or may rate more accurately.

> "When we took into account teachers' previous evaluation ratings in the statistical models, much of the variation between principal and observer ratings disappeared."
