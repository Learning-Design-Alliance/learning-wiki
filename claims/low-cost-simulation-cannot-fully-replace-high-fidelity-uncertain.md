This is the review authors' interpretive statement in the Discussion, not a tested result. The authors argue that "their ability to fully replace high-fidelity methods remains uncertain" and that a clearer understanding of comparative educational outcomes is still needed.

> "While they offer significant advantages, particularly in resource-constrained environments, their ability to fully replace high-fidelity methods remains uncertain. Some critical psychomotor or situational skills may require more immersive training."
