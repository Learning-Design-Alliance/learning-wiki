`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors' instructional implication drawn from their finding that recognition patterns are course-specific rather than universally tied to academic year. They argue interventions "may need to be implemented consistently throughout a course sequence", revising prior work's early-only recommendation.

> "if patterns in peer recognition–such as gender biases–are unlikely to follow a consistent pattern over time, interventions aimed at equitable peer recognition may need to be implemented consistently throughout a course sequence, rather than just early on in the course sequence as prior work suggests [14]."
