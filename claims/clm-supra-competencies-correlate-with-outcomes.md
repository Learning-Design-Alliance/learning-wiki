## Evidence

### M. Young and V. Dulewicz 2025

M. Young and V. Dulewicz. (2025). A model of command, leadership and management competency as a predictor of the performance, potential and rate of advancement of Royal Navy senior officers. Military Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/08995605.2025.2493392

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Table 7, n = 450–481) of summated CLM supra-competency scale scores against Performance, Potential and ROA. The article reports "All the supra-competency scale scores are significantly correlated with the DVs", e.g. Create Success with ROA at r = .225**.

> "All the supra-competency scale scores are significantly correlated with the DVs. The Conceptualise Scale Score is correlated with previous ROA at the 5% level whilst Align, Interact and Create Success are correlated at the 1% level."
