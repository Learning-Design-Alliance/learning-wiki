## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Patients treated by the simulation-trained team had a statistically significantly shorter median time from ER arrival to initiation of hemostasis than patients treated under traditional practice. [→ Hong Yucai 2017](#hong-yucai-2017)

## Evidence

### Hong Yucai 2017

Hong Yucai, Cai Xiujun. (2017). Effect of team training on efficiency of trauma care in a Chinese hospital. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060517717401

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective before-and-after comparison at one Chinese university hospital over 15 months; 51 team-group versus 93 control-group grade I trauma patients, compared with the rank-sum test. The team's median time to hemostasis was 31.0 minutes versus 113.5 for controls, reported as "shorter times from ER arrival to initiation of hemostasis".

> "the team group showed shorter times from ER arrival to initiation of hemostasis (31.0 [13.5–58.5] vs. 113.5 [77–150.50] min, P = 0.01)"
