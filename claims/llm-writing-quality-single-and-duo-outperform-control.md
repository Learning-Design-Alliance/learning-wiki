## Evidence

### Harsh Kumar 2026

Harsh Kumar, Zi Kang (Jace) Mu, Jonathan Vincentius, Ashton Anderson. (2026). Human Thinking under Plural LLM Assistance: Mathematical Problem Solving and Open-Ended Writing. Working Paper, July 2026, Toronto, Canada. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02677 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.02677](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2604.02677)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Three-condition between-subjects Prolific experiment (247 participants with two usable essays); essay quality was rubric-based, LLM-annotated, and validated against human raters (Spearman ρ=.73–.77); the omnibus OLS was significant (F(2,244)=21.91, R²=0.152).

> "Planned pairwise comparisons with Bonferroni correction showed that both LLM conditions outperformed Control (Control vs. Single: Δ= 0.332,𝑡( 244)= 6.21,𝑝<. 0001; Control vs. Duo: Δ= 0.234,𝑡( 244)= 4.21,𝑝= 0.0001)."
