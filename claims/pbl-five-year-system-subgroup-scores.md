## Evidence

### Gao Junwei 2020

Gao Junwei, Yang Ling, Zhao Jinghui, Wang Lian, Zou Jiao, Wang Chunxiang, Fan Xiaotang. (2020). Comparison of problem-based learning and traditional teaching methods in medical psychology education in China: A systematic review and meta-analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0243897

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup meta-analysis of five studies of students in the five-year system (269 cases, 269 controls) under a random effect model. The estimate was "Hedges’ g = 1.571", significantly favoring PBL; heterogeneity was not obviously decreased.

> "the total examination scores of five-year system students were still significantly higher in the PBL group than in the control group (Hedges’ g = 1.571, 95% CI 0.751–2.391, p<0.001)"
