# Lesson plan preparation mean rose from 1.56 (very low) to 4.13 across cycles after the "Among" leadership model, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The mean score for preparing lesson plans increased from 1.56 (very low) before the action to 2.69, 3.44, and 4.13 across the three cycles. [→ Rusman 2020](#rusman-2020)

## Evidence

### Rusman 2020

Rusman, Sunarti, & Rahadian. (2020). Application of the "Among" Leadership Model to Improve Teacher Work Discipline. International Journal of Innovative Science and Research Technology. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2011.07284](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2011.07284)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results section of the action research, scored by the percentage of lesson plans ready to use, converted to a Likert scale. The lesson-plan mean rose from 1.56 to 4.13; only descriptive means are printed, with no effect size or test.

> "The score reached before the action was 1.56 (very low). After the application of the "Among" leadership model, the mean score achieved in the first cycle rose to 2.69 (medium), increased again in the second cycle to 3.44 (high), and increased again in the third cycle to 4. 13 (high)."
