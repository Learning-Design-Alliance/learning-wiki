### J Guisasola 2024

J Guisasola, J Ametller, D Baccino, M Carli, S Kapon, A C Marti, M Monteiro, O Pantano, T Peer, P Sarriugarte, M Schvartzer, A Suárez and K Zuza. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey study (Suárez et al. 2022) administering the 42-statement CLASS test online to first-year Physics/Mathematics and Biology/Biochemistry students taking General Physics I at Universidad de la República, Uruguay. The authors highlight that many life-sciences students try to solve problems by "finding the right equation and substituting", known as "plug and chug".

> "a considerable percentage of life science students (higher than that of physical science students) adopted a novice type of behavior in problem solving."
