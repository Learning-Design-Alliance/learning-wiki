# The social presence subscale of the CoI survey instrument shows inconsistent dimensionality across validation studies

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · review `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Validation studies of the Arbaugh et al. (2008) CoI survey's social presence subscale disagree on its structure: three dimensions in one study, two in another, and a later Rasch analysis suggesting unidimensionality without a multidimensionality test. [→ Karel Kreijns 2022](#karel-kreijns-2022)

## Evidence

### Karel Kreijns 2022

Karel Kreijns, Kate Xu, Joshua Weidlich. (2022). Social Presence: Conceptualization and Measurement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09623-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of CoI survey instrument validation evidence. It adds that Abbitt and Boone (2021) found the subscale fitted the Rasch model, suggesting unidimensionality, but did not report a Rasch test for multidimensionality to confirm it.

> "While the Arbaugh et al. ( 2008) validation study revealed three dimensions for the social presence subscale in the CoI survey instrument (group cohesion, open communication, and affective expression; see also Díaz et al., 2010), an empirical study performed by Carlon et al. ( 2012) showed only two dimensions (social comfort and social experience)"
