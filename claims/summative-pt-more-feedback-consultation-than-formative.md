## Evidence

### van Wijk 2024

van Wijk, E. V., van Blankenstein, F. M., Donkers, J., Janse, R. J., Bustraan, J., Adelmeijer, L. G. M., Dubois, E. A., Dekker, F. W., & Langers, A. M. J. (2024). Does 'summative' count? The influence of the awarding of study credits on feedback use and test-taking motivation in medical progress testing. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10324-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire analysis (n = 264 responders) using logistic regression comparing the formative PT-group (n = 113) and summative PT-group (n = 151). The summative group consulted ProF more often, "aOR 1.92, 95% CI 1.10–3.34", and the answer key more often (aOR 1.92, 95% CI 1.04–3.55).

> "Students who took the summative PT reported consulting ProF (aOR 1.92, 95% CI 1.10–3.34) and the answer key (aOR 1.92, 95% CI 1.04–3.55) more often than students who made the formative PT."

### van Wijk 2024 (2)

van Wijk, E. V., van Blankenstein, F. M., Donkers, J., Janse, R. J., Bustraan, J., Adelmeijer, L. G. M., Dubois, E. A., Dekker, F. W., & Langers, A. M. J. (2024). Does 'summative' count? The influence of the awarding of study credits on feedback use and test-taking motivation in medical progress testing. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10324-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of ProF logging data of all participating students, including non-responders, for the February 2022 PT. The effect was "significant (β:0.251, p:0.003)" after adjustment for logging around earlier summative PTs, shown in Fig. 3.

> "This analysis showed that there were more ProF logging sessions around the summative PT in February 2022 than around the formative PT (β:0.444, p:<0.001). After adjustment for logging behaviour in earlier summative PTs in September 2021 and December 2022, this effect remained significant (β:0.251, p:0.003)"
