### Liam Doyle 2026

Liam Doyle, Alexandru Maries, Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Exploring pedagogical content knowledge of physics teaching assistants using the Energy and Momentum Conceptual Survey. [doi:10.1103/fgqc-dfbz](https://doi.org/10.1103/fgqc-dfbz)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative analysis of PCK task scores for 70 first-year physics graduate TAs across four cohorts, shown in Figure 1. The article reports the distribution was "close to a normal distribution" with an average of 58% and 90% of TAs between 45% and 75%.

> "the performance of the TAs is close to a normal distribution, where the scores of the vast majority of the TAs are bunched around the average (58%). In particular, 90% of the TAs had average PCK scores between 45% and 75%."
