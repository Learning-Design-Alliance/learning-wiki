## Evidence

### Edward Finch 2026

Edward Finch, Jun Jie Lim, Samuel Birks, Ansaam El‐Sherif, Stephen Cross, Jamie Sterland, Deborah Clark (2026). A Structured Ultrasound‐Guided Cannulation Course to Prepare Medical Students for Foundation Training. The Clinical Teacher 23, no. 1: e70320. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70320

`q3 · i3` · `design · r3`

Pre- and post-simulation questionnaires on 5-point Likert scales compared with the Mann–Whitney U test; Table 3 prints per-outcome medians and effect sizes, e.g. confidence in performing US‐guided cannulation rose from median 1 to 4 with r = 0.81. The printed effect sizes span r = 0.63–0.87, all large.

> "Students demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in confidence across all measured learning outcomes following the training (p < 0.001). Notably, the effect sizes for these improvements were large across all outcomes (r = 0.63–0.87), indicating substantial practical significance in addition to statistical significance (see Table 3)."
