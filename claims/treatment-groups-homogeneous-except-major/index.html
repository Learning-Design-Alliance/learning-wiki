Amita Goyal Chin 2016¶

Amita Goyal Chin, Ugochukwu Etudo, Mark A. Harris. (2016). On Mobile Device Security Practices and Training Efficacy: An Empirical Study. Informatics in Education, 15(2). https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2016.12

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pearson Chi-Square tests compared treated (n=187) and untreated (n=160) groups on seven dimensions; only major differed significantly, driven by "the higher proportion of accounting majors in the untreated group (13.8% vs. 3.8%)" and more economics majors in the treated group.

"However, we did observe a statistically significant difference in the distribution of respondents around major academic areas. This difference is largely attributable to the higher proportion of accounting majors in the untreated group (13.8% vs. 3.8%)"
