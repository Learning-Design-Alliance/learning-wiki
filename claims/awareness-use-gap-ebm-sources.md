Melesse Girma Tufa, Amde Tewodros, Tezera Robel. (2024). Competency in evidence‐based medicine and associated factors among medical radiology technologists in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.777

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey section on awareness of information sources, reported with Figure 3; the study reports 93.1% awareness versus 38.8% regular use of PUBMED and 91.3% versus 33.4% for the Evidence-Based Medicine journal, with the Cochrane Database least known (46%).

> "Regarding awareness of information sources, 93.1% of MRTs knew about the PUBMED database, but only 38.8% used it regularly. The Evidence‐Based Medicine journal was known by 91.3% of MRTs, but only 33.4% had used it."
