In the longitudinal analysis of Chicago students' civic commitments, two factors showed negative relations. The study reports that "The concentration of poverty in students' neighborhoods had a negative effect on civic commitments" and, which the authors found more surprising, parental press for academic achievement also had a negative effect.

"The concentration of poverty in students' neighborhoods had a negative effect on civic commitments. More surprisingly, parental press for academic achievement also had a negative effect on students' civic commitments."
