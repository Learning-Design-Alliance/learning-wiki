AI-coded help-seeking typology over the 96 students with at least one REQUEST turn, reported descriptively in the results section (Figure 3). The article notes the low verification rate contrasts with the 69.7% who said they wanted the AI to check their understanding.

"requests were predominantly instrumental (M= 75.1%,M dn= 85.7%) rather than executive (M= 24.9%). Content was primarily procedural (M= 37.4%) and con- ceptual (M= 25.1%), followed by answer-seeking (M= 21.1%) and verification (M= 16.3%,M dn= 0%)"
