## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The paper argues that building increasingly complex agentic AIED systems damages learner agency, that outcomes in education's hypercomplex system cannot be predicted, that permanent scaffolding assumes infinite resources, that values are ignored or oversimplified, and that an individualistic stance is perpetuated. `q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is a theoretical argument in a position paper, not an empirical test. The authors enumerate five problematic assumptions of current AIED, including that agency is a zero-sum game and that tools can be designed without considering human values, and argue these lead to downstream risks to learner flourishing.

> "designing learning technology under these faulty assumptions leads to downstream risks such as those mentioned above, not only to long-term learning and agency, but also to learner flourishing"
