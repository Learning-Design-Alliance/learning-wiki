## Evidence

### Toma 2000

Toma, Tony. (2000). Real-Time Courseware Design: The LAVAC Video Sequencer. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED444508

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational report from classroom use of the video sequencer interface, in the Results section. Each segment is numbered and shown as a colored square on a line; the author notes "60 segments for a 3-minute video will be much easier to listen than 10" as the students' perceived workload cue.

> "Unexpectedly, most students stop the video after three or four sequences have been played, just to see the "menu" of their work."
