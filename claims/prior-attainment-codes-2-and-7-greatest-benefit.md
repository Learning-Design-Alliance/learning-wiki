## Evidence

### Roberts N 2015

Roberts N, Spencer-Smith G, Vänskä R, Eskelinen S. (2015). From challenging assumptions to measuring effect: Researching the Nokia Mobile Mathematics Service in South Africa. South African Journal of Education, 35(2). https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v35n2a1045

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive disaggregation of the 1,950-learner dataset by Grade 9 attainment code (Table 2); high attainers not using the service dropped 31 points versus 22 for regular users, and failing regular users maintained attainment while non-users declined a further 9.5 points.

> "The learners who benefitted most from the service (who showed the greatest shift in their attainment), were those who had narrowly failed Mathematics (Code 2: 26 -39%), or attained distinctions (Code 7: 80 -100%) in Grade N ine."
