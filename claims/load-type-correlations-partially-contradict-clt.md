## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Meta-analytic correlations among the cognitive load types (ICL, ECL, GCL) partially contradict the theory-based assumptions of cognitive load theory. [→ Felix Krieglstein 2022](#felix-krieglstein-2022)

## Evidence

### Felix Krieglstein 2022

Felix Krieglstein, Maik Beege, Günter Daniel Rey, Paul Ginns, Moritz Krell, Sascha Schneider. (2022). A Systematic Meta-analysis of the Reliability and Validity of Subjective Cognitive Load Questionnaires in Experimental Multimedia Learning Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09683-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Construct validity part of the meta-analysis: correlations between ICL, ECL, and GCL were collected and meta-analytically calculated across the included experiments. The article states the inter-type correlations "partially contradict the ory-based assumptions"; no pooled correlation coefficients are printed in the supplied text.

> "Correlations among the cognitive load types partially contradict the ory-based assumptions, whereas correlations with learning-related variables support assumptions derived from cognitive load theory."
