# CLASP students achieved an average normalized FCI gain of 0.39, above the traditional-course range and in the middle of the interactive-engagement range, despite only about a third of one quarter on mechanics

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium
> **Currency** · newest study 2012 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` FCI pre/post testing of 898 students in four groups (1999-2001) yielded an average pretest of 31% correct and an average normalized gain of 0.39 ± 0.01, which the authors place above Hake's traditional-course range (0.24 ± 0.03) and in the middle of the interactive-engagement range. [→ Potter 2012](#potter-2012)

## Evidence

### Potter 2012

Potter, W., Webb, D., West, E., Paul, C., Bowen, M., Weiss, B., Coleman, L., & De Leone, C. (2012). Sixteen years of Collaborative Learning through Active Sense-making in Physics (CLASP) at UC Davis. https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.6970 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.1205.6970](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.1205.6970)

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

FCI administered as pre-test at the start of CLASP A and post-test at the end of CLASP B to 898 students across four groups in 1999-2001. The article notes the FCI was never administered to Physics 5, so the traditional-course comparison uses Hake's published gain of 0.24 ± 0.03.

> "The Force Concept Inventory (pre-test at beginning of CLASP A and post-test at the end of CLASP B) was given to four diﬀerent groups of students (total of 898 students) in 1999-2001 resulting in an average pretest score of 31% correct and an average normalized gain of 0.39 ± 0.01."
