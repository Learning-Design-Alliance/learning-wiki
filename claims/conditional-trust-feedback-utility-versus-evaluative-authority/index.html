Rayed AlGhamdi 2026¶

Rayed AlGhamdi. (2026). Who Should Grade My Work? Student Perspectives on Transparent AI-Assisted Writing Assessment in Higher Education. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6277-2124

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Theme 3 of the thematic analysis (7 explicit plus 3 partial articulations in Table 1). Participants endorsed AI for initial feedback only, e.g. "Doctors can use AI for [reading] feedback for exams only, but if they use it for [grading] students, problems will happen."

"The most analytically distinctive pattern in the dataset was that participants could simultaneously affirm the usefulness of ChatGPT’s feedback and reject ChatGPT as an appropriate final evaluator. Participants drew a consistent distinction between “this feedback is useful” and “this system should decide my grade.”"
