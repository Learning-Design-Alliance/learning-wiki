## Evidence

### Mason 2018

Mason, A. J., & Bertram, C. A. (2018). Consideration of Learning Orientations as an Application of Achievement Goals in Evaluating Life Science Majors in Introductory Physics. University of Central Arkansas, Department of Physics and Astronomy. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010125)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA comparison of normalized gains on CLASS item clusters (PS-G, PS-C, PS-S, CU, ACU) among 218 students grouped by learning orientation, with Levene's tests for equality of variances. Framework-oriented students' gains exceed "the vague-oriented students (p < 0.02, all comparisons)" and Performance-oriented students except on PS-C. No printed effect size.

> "the framework -oriented students have statistically higher gains across all item clusters than do the vague-oriented students (p < 0.02, all comparisons); with the exception of the PS -C cluster, framework -oriented students also have stronger gains than do performance-oriented students (p > 0.3, PS-C; p < 0.01, all other comparisons )."
