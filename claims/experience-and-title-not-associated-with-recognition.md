## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In logistic regression, neither experience (≥5 years OR 1.51, 95% CI 0.43–5.24, p = 0.519) nor professional title (e.g., senior physician OR 3.51, 95% CI 0.76–16.17, p = 0.107) was associated with increased odds of detecting asynchronies correctly. [→ Liu Shengjun 2025](#liu-shengjun-2025)
