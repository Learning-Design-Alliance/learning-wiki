Device-modality subgroup analysis of the primary outcome in the randomized trial. Direct laryngoscopy comparisons (86.4% vs 80.7%, OR 1.61, P=0.49) were not significant; authors note providers strongly preferred video laryngoscopy in this population.

> "When video laryngoscopy was used, first attempt success was 91.9% (193/210) in the treatment group versus 81.9% (181/221) in the control group (odds ratio 2.58 (95% CI 1.48 to 4.5), P=0.001)."
