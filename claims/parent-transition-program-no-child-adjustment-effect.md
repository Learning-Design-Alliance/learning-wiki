## Evidence

### Giallo 2010

Giallo, R., Treyvaud, K., Matthews, J., & Kienhuis, M. (2010). Making the Transition to Primary School: An Evaluation of a Transition Program for Parents. Australian Journal of Educational & Developmental Psychology, 10, 1-17. https://www.newcastle.edu.au/journal/ajedp/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the cluster-randomized trial, child outcomes were measured at Time 3 via parent and teacher report using the Children's Adjustment to School Scale and School Readiness Scale. No significant differences were found between conditions on any child outcome measure.

> "There were no significant differences between the intervention and control conditions on parent and teacher ratings of children's happiness to go to school, academic adjustment, social adjustment and school readiness."
