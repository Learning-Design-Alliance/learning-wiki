# GenAI access disproportionately benefits less inherently creative writers, equalizing creativity scores across writers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among low-DAT (less inherently creative) writers, GenAI access produced larger improvements in novelty, usefulness and emotional ratings than among high-DAT writers, for whom access had little effect. [→ Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser 2023](#anil-r-doshi-and-oliver-p-hauser-2023)

## Evidence

### Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser 2023

Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser. (2023). Generative artificial intelligence enhances creativity but reduces the diversity of novel content. https://aspredicted.org/136723

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Heterogeneity analysis interacting the continuous DAT score with condition (Tables S10–S11) in the randomized experiment; DAT was completed by 291 of 293 writers (mean 77.24, SD 6.48). For low-DAT writers, well-written ratings rose by up to 26.6% and enjoyment by up to 22.6%.

> "Among low-DAT writers, having access to 1 GenAI idea improves a story’s novelty by 6.3% and having access to 5 GenAI ideas yields improvements of 10.7%."
