Hannah Beaux, Pegah Karimi, Otilia Pop, Rob Clark. (2024). Guiding Empowerment Model: Liberating Neurodiversity in Online Higher Education. [doi:10.1109/fie61694.2024.10893125](https://doi.org/10.1109/fie61694.2024.10893125)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

A narrative synthesis of around 100 secondary-research articles argues that UDL and trauma-informed instruction, while documented as positively affecting learner achievement, offer "limited direction" for adult online learners. No effect size is reported; this is the authors' literature-based argument.

> "these frameworks are based on a synchronous, instructor-led environment and provide limited direction for addressing a nuanced and complex adult online learner population who may face a variety of distinct socioeconomic, neurocognitive, and social factors"
