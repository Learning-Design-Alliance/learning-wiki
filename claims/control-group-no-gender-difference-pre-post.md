Koc Akran, S., & Uzum, B. (2018). The effect of the layered curriculum on the 6th grade students' learning styles in Science lesson. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 4(3), 141-152. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.4.3.141

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square analyses of Kolb inventory data from 21 control-group students (11 female, 10 male) at pre-test and post-test; the article reports no significant difference at pre-test (p=.47) and, for post-test, "there isn't any significant dif ference between their learning  styles and gender (p=.28; p>. 05)". No effect sizes are printed.
