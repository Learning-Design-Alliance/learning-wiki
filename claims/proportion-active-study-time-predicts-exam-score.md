## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Walck-Shannon 2021

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Rowell, S. F., & Frey, R. F. (2021). To What Extent Do Study Habits Relate to Performance? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-05-0091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression on exam 1 (n = 422) and exam 2 (n = 456) with the base model of preparation, absences, and total study hours. Students who spent all study time on active strategies scored "5.5% higher and 10.0% higher" on exams 1 and 2 than those who spent none.

> "the proportion of time students spent using active strategies added significant additional predictive value for exam 1, F(1, 416) = 8.770, p = 0.003, ΔR2 = 0.014; and exam 2, F(1, 450) = 14.848, p = 0.0001, ΔR2 = 0.024."
