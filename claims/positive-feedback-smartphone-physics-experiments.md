### Leong 2019

Leong, T. K., Wee, L. K., García-Clemente, F. J., & Esquembre, F. (2019). Promoting the Joy of Learning by Turning a Smartphone into Scientific Equipment. GIREP-ICPE-EPEC-MPTL conference, Budapest. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1911.06655](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1911.06655)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational feedback reported after the experiments were implemented in schools with Grades 11–12 students following teacher professional development workshops. The article reports positive feedback without systematic measures, quoting that "abstract concepts can be visualized using smartphones".

> "Both teachers and students' feedback were positive. They appreciated how the abstract concepts can be visualized using smartphones. They also appreciate that the web app doesn’t require any installation and could be implemented quickly."
