## Evidence

### Rotthoff Thomas 2014

Rotthoff Thomas, Ostapczuk Martin S., Kröncke Klaus D., Zimmerhofer Alexander, Decking Ulrich, Schneider Matthias, Ritz-Timme Stefanie. (2014). Criterion validity of a competency-based assessment center in medical education – a 4-year follow-up study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v19.25254

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of n=18 students in the voluntary fourth-year peer feedback project, relating Year 1 AC scores to the mean peer assessment of communicative and social behavior. The AC total score correlation was "moderate and marginally significant"; communicative competency reached significance (r=0.50).

> "The AC total score also showed a moderate and marginally significant correlation with the overall external peer feedback score provided in Year 4 [r=0.38, p=0.06 (one-tailed)]. In addition, communicative competency correlated strongly with the overall peer feedback [r=0.50, p=0.02 (one-tailed)]."
