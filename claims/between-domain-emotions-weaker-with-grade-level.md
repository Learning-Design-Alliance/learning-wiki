## Evidence

### Goetz 2006

Goetz, T., Frenzel, A. C., Pekrun, R., Hall, N. C., & Lüdtke, O. (2006). Between- and Within-Domain Relations of Students' Academic Emotions. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel three-level models estimating homogeneity of domain-specific emotion scales in 307 Grade 8 and 235 Grade 11 students. The homogeneity index h(d) was lower in Grade 11 (Mdn .11) than Grade 8 (Mdn .30), with differences significant for all five emotions (p < .01 for four emotions, p < .05 for boredom).

> "h(d) ranged from .22 to .38 in the Grade 8 sample (Mdn /H11005.30) and from .06 to .20 in the Grade 11 sample (Mdn /H11005.11). For each of the five emotions, differences in h(d) were statistically significant across the two samples"
