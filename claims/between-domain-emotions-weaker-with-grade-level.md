## Evidence

### Goetz et al. 2007

Thomas Goetz, Anne C. Frenzel, Reinhard Pekrun, Nathan C. Hall, and Oliver Lüdtke. (2007). Between- and Within-Domain Relations of Students' Academic Emotions. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.715

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel (Mplus 4.0) three-level analysis of between-domain homogeneity of emotion scales in 307 Grade 8 and 235 Grade 11 students. The article reports the printed h(d) ranges and medians and that every emotion's difference was statistically significant.

> "h(d) ranged from .22 to .38 in the Grade 8 sample (Mdn /H11005 .30) and from .06 to .20 in the Grade 11 sample (Mdn /H11005 .11). For each of the five emotions, differences in h(d) were statistically significant across the two samples ( p /H11021 .01 for enjoyment, pride, anxiety, and anger; p /H11021 .05 for boredom)."
