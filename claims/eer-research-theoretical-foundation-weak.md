## Evidence

### Vorderobermeier 2024

Vorderobermeier, A., Abel, J., & Sailer, M. (2024). Theoretical foundations and approaches in research on educational escape rooms: A systematic review. Educational Research Review, 44, 100625. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100625

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review using the ROSES schema across Scopus, EbscoHost, and Web of Science; 876 records retrieved, 341 retained for full-text screening. The review reports the percentage breakdown: "only 7% explicitly linked considerations of educational theories and approaches with EERs".

> "Approximately 37% of the articles lacked any educational or learning-theoretical basis. Slightly more than half (56%) merely engaged in name-dropping or had a very weak connection to EERs, and only 7% explicitly linked considerations of educational theories and approaches with EERs as teaching tools."
