Cheng-Wen He 2025¶

Cheng-Wen He, Logan Fiorella, Paula P. Lemons. (2025). Does Instruction-First or Problem-Solving-First Depend on Learners' Prior Knowledge? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09993-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Supplementary analyses reported in a footnote tested whether prior knowledge score moderated the sequence effects in each experiment. The authors caution the analysis "is limited because the students in each experiment had a restricted range of prior knowledge scores".

"The results indicated that prior knowledge was not a significant moderator in either experiment."
