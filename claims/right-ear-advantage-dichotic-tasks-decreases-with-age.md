# A right-ear advantage appears in both dichotic listening tasks and decreases with age on the Dichotic Sentences subtest

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Significantly better right-ear results (right-ear advantage) were found on the Dichotic Words and Dichotic Sentences subtests. [→ Iva I. Hrastinski 2023](#iva-i-hrastinski-2023)
`q3 i?` On the Dichotic Sentences task, the right ear advantage decreased with increasing age; a similar trend on Dichotic Words was not statistically significant. [→ Iva I. Hrastinski 2023 (2)](#iva-i-hrastinski-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Iva I. Hrastinski 2023

Iva I. Hrastinski, Ana M. Bonetti, Mladen A. Hedjever. (2023). Relationship between auditory processing skills and academic achievement of elementary school children. Specijalna edukacija i rehabilitacija, 22(2), 95-116. https://doi.org/10.5937/specedreh22-37415

`q3 · i?`

Wilcoxon signed rank tests of ear differences across the sample. The article reports "significantly better results in the right ear, or a right-ear-advantage (REA), was noted for Dichotic Words (Z = -8.60, p  = 0.00 1 ) and Dichotic Sentences (Z = -7.22, p = 0.001) subtests."

> "significantly better results in the right ear, or a right-ear-advantage (REA), was noted for Dichotic Words (Z = -8.60, p  = 0.00 1 ) and Dichotic Sentences (Z = -7.22, p = 0.001) subtests."

### Iva I. Hrastinski 2023 (2)

Iva I. Hrastinski, Ana M. Bonetti, Mladen A. Hedjever. (2023). Relationship between auditory processing skills and academic achievement of elementary school children. Specijalna edukacija i rehabilitacija, 22(2), 95-116. https://doi.org/10.5937/specedreh22-37415

`q3 · i?`

Age-specific ear analysis on the Dichotic Sentences subtest. The article reports "the right ear advantage decreased with increasing age," while for Dichotic Words a similar trend was observed but without statistically significant differences.

> "Furthermore, age-related ear differences were noted on the Dichotic Sentences task, where the right ear advantage decreased with increasing age."
