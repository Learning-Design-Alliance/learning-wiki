### Suya Liu · Yun Dai · Oi Lam Ng · Zhihui Cai 2025

Suya Liu · Yun Dai · Oi Lam Ng · Zhihui Cai. (2025). Gender Disparity in Computational Thinking Pedagogy and Assessment: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10095-3

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis of pedagogical tool across 100 effect sizes. Programming tools showed a significant male-favoring effect; robots showed the largest but non-significant effect. The article states robustness for non-digital (k = 5), mixed (k = 5), and other tools (k = 7) was weakened by small sample sizes.

> "Robots (g = 0.173, p = 0.073) demonstrated the largest effect size, followed by mixed tools (g = 0.155, p = 0.346), non-digital activities (g = 0.135, p = 0.409), and programming ( g = 0.125, p < 0.01). Other types of tools (e.g., web-based tools) showed a negative and statistically significant effect size ( g = −0.407, p < 0.01)"
