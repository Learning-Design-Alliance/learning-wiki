## Evidence

### Oliver Passon 2018

Oliver Passon, Thomas Zügge and Johannes Grebe-Ellis. (2018). Pitfalls in the teaching of elementary particle physics. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aadbc7](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aadbc7)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Critical assessment of introduction strategies in the surveyed educational literature, drawing on chemistry education research showing many students hold alternative frameworks about the classical particle model. The microscope metaphor is also technically problematic since no diffraction limit applies to particle detection.

> "It is to be expected that simply using the common term “particles” without further explanation runs the risk to invoke the misconception of (very) small but ordinary pieces of matter."
