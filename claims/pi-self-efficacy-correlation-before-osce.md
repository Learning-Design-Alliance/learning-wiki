Spearman's rank correlation analysis of baseline (pre-OSCE) questionnaire data from 48 occupational therapy students found "A significant positive correlation" between the two scale total scores (r = .354, P < 0.01).

> "A significant positive correlation was observed between the total scores for professional identity and self-efficacy before OSCE (r  =  .354, P  <  0.01). Significant correlations were found in 7 items of the subscales for professional identity and self-efficacy before OSCE"
