Evidence¶

Rickles 2016¶

Rickles, J., Heppen, J., Taylor, S., Allensworth, E., Michelman, V., Sorensen, N., Walters, K., & Clements, P. (2016). Getting Back on Track: Who Needs to Recover Algebra Credit After Ninth Grade? (Research Brief 3). American Institutes for Research & University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.air.org/CreditRecovery

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Attendance analysis of ninth-grade absence data for 36,423 CPS students. On average students who failed Algebra I were absent 41 days versus 15 days, and the brief reports suspension rates of 46% versus 16% for students who failed versus passed.

"More than half (56%) of the students who failed Algebra I missed 30 or more days of school during their ninth-grade year, whereas less than one fifth (14%) of students who passed Algebra I missed that many days of school."
