q2 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review narrative synthesis of trials comparing OCDE with face-to-face learning on knowledge gain. The review reports an "RM range 0.95-1.17" across 8 studies and 495 participants, graded very low because of high risk of bias, heterogeneity, and indirectness.

"Compared with face-to-face learning, the effect of OCDE on knowledge gain is uncertain (ratio of the means [RM] range 0.95-1.17; 8 studies, 495 participants; very low grade of evidence)."
