`q3 i?` In multilevel multivariable analysis, the Very Low-Volume group's GM-ITE scores were significantly higher than the Low-Volume group's (adjusted coefficient 2.007, p = 0.021). [→ Ishizuka K 2026](#ishizuka-k-2026)

## Evidence

### Ishizuka K 2026

Ishizuka K, Nishizaki Y, Kataoka K, Shimizu T, Nojima M, Yamamoto Y, Shikino K, Fukui S, Nagasaki K, Kobayashi H, Ohta M, Tokuda Y. (2026). Impact of inpatient volume on residents' In-training examination scores and burnout in Japanese community hospitals: a nationwide cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08664-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel multivariable linear regression with random intercept on 4,867 residents from 564 community hospitals, adjusting for hospital- and resident-level covariates. The adjusted difference was "approximately 2 points on an 80-point scale, indicating a small effect size at the level of individual residents" per the authors.

> "the GM-ITE® score was significantly higher in the Very Low-Volume group than in the Low-Volume group (coefficient: 2.007; 95% confidence interval (CI): 0.298–3.717, p = 0.021)"
