The guidance cites studies, unnamed in the text, that a finalist pool including "at least two women and two candidates of color" raises the odds of hiring a woman or a candidate of color by factors of 79 and 194. Second-hand; no effect size printed.

"Studies demonstrate that if at least two women and two candidates of color are in the finalist pool, the odds of hiring a woman or a candidate of color are 79 and 194 times greater, respectively."
