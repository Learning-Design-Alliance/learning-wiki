## Evidence

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2009

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2009). Teaching and understanding of quantum interpretations in modern physics courses. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.6.010101

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-term survey responses on preferred interpretation of the double-slit experiment from the course whose instructor took an agnostic approach. The article reports "a significant majority of students" preferred the realist interpretation in that course.

> "a significant majority of students from a second modern physics course (taught by the agnostic instructor quoted above) said they preferred a realist interpretation, agreeing that each electron is a tiny particle that travels through one slit or the other on its way to the detector."
