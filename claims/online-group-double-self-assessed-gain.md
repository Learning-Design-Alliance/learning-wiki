# Students in the online communication training reported a significantly larger pre/post gain in self-assessed communication skills than the face-to-face group

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The average self-assessed gain in communicative competence was twice as large in the online group (M = 1,54, SD = 0,94) as in the face-to-face group (M = 0,75, SD = 0,87), a significant difference (p < 0,001) on a repeated-measures ANOVA. [→ Gür Deniz 2024](#gur-deniz-2024)

## Evidence

### Gür Deniz 2024

Gür Deniz, Offergeld Christian, Fabry Götz, Wünsch Alexander. (2024). Vermittlung kommunikativer Kompetenzen in der Lehre der HNO-Heilkunde: Vergleich zwischen einer Online- und Präsenzveranstaltung. HNO. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00106-024-01475-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of 10-cm visual analog scale self-ratings (16 items) pre/post, interpreting within- and between-subject effects, in the non-randomized comparison of the online (n = 29) and face-to-face (n = 108) cohorts; the article reports "einen signifikanten Unterschied dieser Mittelwerte (p < 0,001)" and no effect size.

> "Der Durchschnitt von Zuwachs an kommunikativen Kompetenzen in der Online-Gruppe (M = 1,54 [SD = 0,94]) ist insgesamt doppelt so groß wie in der Präsenzgruppe (M = 0,75 [SD = 0,87]). Die statistische Überprüfung liefert einen signifikanten Unterschied dieser Mittelwerte (p < 0,001)."
