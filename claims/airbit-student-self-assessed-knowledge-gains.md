## Evidence

### Bjørn Fjukstad 2019

Bjørn Fjukstad, Nina Angelvik, Morten Grønnesby, Maria Wulff Hauglann, Hedinn Gunhildrud, Fredrik Høisæther Rasch, Julianne Iversen, Margaret Dalseng, Lars Ailo Bongo. (2019). Teaching Electronics and Programming in Norwegian Schools Using the air:bit Sensor Kit. arXiv. [doi:10.1145/3304221.3325527](https://doi.org/10.1145/3304221.3325527)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-project student survey (90 of 164 students, 55% answer rate) with retrospective before/after self-ratings on a five-level Likert scale, top and bottom categories combined. For example, 'How to write a computer program' moved from 39% to 18% in the little category and 23% to 50% in the much category; Arduino knowledge moved 26% to 59% in the much category.

> "Survey results from the self-assessed general knowledge before and after participating in the air:bit project. We have combined the top and bottom categories."
