## Evidence

### Lonigan 2016

Lonigan, C. J., & Phillips, B. M. (2016). Response to Instruction in Preschool: Results of Two Randomized Studies with Children At Significant Risk of Reading Difficulties. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000054

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized evaluation in Study 1: 93 children qualified at midyear in Title I preschools and were randomized to need-aligned small-group Tier II instruction or Tier I only, analyzed with multi-level models. The article reports "no other main effects of the language-focused instruction on the language outcomes (ps > .13)".
