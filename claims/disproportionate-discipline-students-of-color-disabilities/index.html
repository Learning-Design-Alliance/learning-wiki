Cardichon 2019¶

Cardichon, J., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2019). Protecting students' civil rights: The federal role in school discipline. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/student-civil-rights-school-discipline

q2 · i? · review · r1

Analysis of the U.S. Department of Education's Civil Rights Data Collection for 2015-16 shows African American male students "comprised 8% of students enrolled and 25% of students who received an out-of-school suspension." The report also cites students with disabilities as 12% of enrollment but 26% of suspensions.

"During the 2015–16 school year, African American male students comprised 8% of students enrolled and 25% of students who received an out-of-school suspension."
