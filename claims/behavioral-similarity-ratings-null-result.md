### Cetron 2026

Cetron, J.S., Hillis, M.E., Diamond, S.G., May, V.V. & Kraemer, D.J.M. (2026). Neural patterns reflect conceptual grasp of novice students following first class learning in physics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00394-3

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null result from the behavioral similarity probe, in which participants rated pairwise similarity of the 12 stimulus items at each quiz timepoint and the ratings were compared to the expert categorical model. The article reports the null without a printed statistic and offers the possible explanation that novices relied on surface-level similarities; no effect size is printed.

> "since these classifier scores neither correlate with individual quiz score nor show a clear learning trajectory over time, we have insufficient evidence that this task as administered was able to measure the relevant constructs."
