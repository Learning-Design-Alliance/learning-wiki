Evidence¶

Dikshit et al. 2013¶

Jyotsna Dikshit, Suresh Garg, Santosh Panda. (2013). Pedagogic Effectiveness of Print, Interactive Multimedia, and Online Resources: A Case Study of IGNOU. International Journal of Instruction, 6(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085398

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Observational data from the five-day online treatment at two telelearning centres with 20 students, one hour of chat per day (Table 2). The article reports hits "ranged from 200 to 500" and questions from 10 to 25, with quality low on Day 1 and high on Day 5.

"the number of hits ranged from 200 to 500 and the number of questions ranged from 10 to 25. The level and quality of the questions was low on Day 1, but improved gradually and was quite high on Day 5."
