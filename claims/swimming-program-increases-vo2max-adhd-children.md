## Evidence

### Hattabi Soukaina 2022

Hattabi Soukaina, Forte Pedro, Kukic Filip, Bouden Asma, Have Mona, Chtourou Hamdi, Sortwell Andrew. (2022). A Randomized Trial of a Swimming-Based Alternative Treatment for Children with Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192316238

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial with 40 children with ADHD (20 per group) assessed pre and post a 12-week swimming program. The repeated measures ANOVA showed "only maximum oxygen consumption (F (1, 38) = 102.001, p < 0.001, ἠ2 = 0.729) increased, with a large effect in the experimental group." The printed effect size is ἠ2 = 0.729, which the article classifies as a large effect.

> "For maximum oxygen consumption, the repeated measures ANOVA results indicated that after the intervention, only maximum oxygen consumption (F (1, 38) = 102.001, p < 0.001, ἠ2 = 0.729) increased, with a large effect in the experimental group."
