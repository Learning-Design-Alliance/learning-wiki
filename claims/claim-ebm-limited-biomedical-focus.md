## Evidence

### Pather MK 2019

Pather MK, Mash R. (2019). Family physicians' experience and understanding of evidence-based practice and guideline implementation in primary care practice, Cape Town, South Africa. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. 11(1), a1592. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v11i1.1592

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenological interview study of 27 Cape Town family physicians analysed with the framework method. A respondent explained that evidence-based medicine is "mainly looking at the biomedical side of issues" and concluded "it's limited"; no effect size is reported for this qualitative finding.

> "it's mainly looking at the biomedical side of issues. It doesn't delve much into the psychosocial, cultural, political and administrative issues involved in patient care. So, in other words it's limited."
