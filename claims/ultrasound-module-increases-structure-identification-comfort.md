## Evidence

### Keddis Mira T 2011

Keddis Mira T, Cullen Michael W, Reed Darcy A, Halvorsen Andrew J, McDonald Furman S, Takahashi Paul Y, Bhagra Anjali. (2011). Effectiveness of an Ultrasound Training Module for Internal Medicine Residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-75

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post Likert survey of interns after the orientation ultrasound module; 61 (80%) rated post-module comfort. Table 1 shows post means from "3.61 (0.84) for peritoneal cavity to 4.48 (0.62) for internal jugular vein", each with conservative p-values.

> "The conservative upper bounds for the paired t-test p-values showed a significant increase in confidence in the understanding of ultrasound principles (p < .0001) and significant increases in comfort identifying all seven structures (all p < .002)."
