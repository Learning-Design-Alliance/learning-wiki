## Evidence

### Knox Shane 2014

Knox Shane, Cullen Walter, Dunne Colum. (2014). Continuous Professional Competence (CPC) for Irish paramedics and advanced paramedics: a national study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-41

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

National anonymous online survey of all registered Irish paramedics and APs (n=1816 invited, 789 responses, 43%). Descriptive Likert-scale results: registration "personally important by 89% (697) of respondents" and 77% stated CPC was extremely important.

> "Registration with PHECC was considered personally important by 89% (697) of respondents, with only 2% disagreeing. Indeed, in the context of active professional pre-hospital practitioners, 77% (615) of the paramedics/APs stated that CPC was extremely important."
