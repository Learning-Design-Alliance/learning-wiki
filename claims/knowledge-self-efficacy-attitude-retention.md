## Evidence

### Sahin Abdul Samet 2026

Sahin Abdul Samet, Yilmaz Mutlu, Imamoglu Melih, Beser Muhammet Fatih, Karaca Yunus, Pasli Sinan, Gunduz Abdulkadir. (2026). Effectiveness and retention of extremity and junctional tourniquet training among medical interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09111-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the 32-intern study, knowledge (8.7 ± 1.4 to 8.4 ± 1.3), self-efficacy (36.4 ± 3.1 to 35.3 ± 3.3) and attitude (30.6 ± 2.5 to 29.5 ± 2.5) showed no significant post-test to follow-up decline on Bonferroni-corrected Wilcoxon tests.

> "At the two-month follow-up, the slight decrease in mean attitude scores did not reach statistical significance, indicating that the positive attitudinal gains were largely sustained."
