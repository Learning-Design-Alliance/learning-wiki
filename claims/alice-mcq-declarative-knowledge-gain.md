## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean correct answers on the same ten multiple-choice questions rose from 5 out of 10 (SD 1) before simulator use to 7 out of 10 (SD 1) after, in one uncontrolled pre-post study (P<.01). [→ Kleinert R 2015](#kleinert-r-2015)

## Evidence

### Kleinert R 2015

Kleinert R, Heiermann N, Plum PS, Wahba R, Chang D, Maus M, Chon S, Hoelscher AH, Stippel DL. (2015). Web-Based Immersive Virtual Patient Simulators: Positive Effect on Clinical Reasoning in Medical Education. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.5035

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prequestionnaire and postquestionnaire multiple-choice questions on the underlying disease, analyzed with the Student t test. Mean correct answers rose from "5 out of 10 (SD 1)" to "7 out of 10 correct answers (SD 1)", reported as "a significant impact on declarative knowledge gain (P<.01)".

> "the mean number of correct answers was 5 out of 10 (SD 1). In the postquestionnaire, the students achieved 7 out of 10 correct answers (SD 1). Hence, ALICE showed a significant impact on declarative knowledge gain (P<.01)."
