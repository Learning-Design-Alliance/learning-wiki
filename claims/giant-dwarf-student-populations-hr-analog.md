Observational reading of the aggregate academic HR diagrams (Fig. 4) for 364 Physics 1 and 261 Physics 2 students. The authors themselves hedge the evidence as marginal given the sample size, and note the dwarf group is expected to be smaller for PHYS 2 than PHYS 1 due to adaptive student behavior.

> "There is some evidence, perhaps marginal given the sample size, for populations corresponding to astronomical “giants” (i.e., students who excel on tests despite expending little eﬀort on homework)"
