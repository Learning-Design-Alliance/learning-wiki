### Lord 2017

Lord, P., Sims, D., White, R., & Roy, P. (2017). Evidence for the Frontline: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581162

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Monitoring-information and survey analysis from the E4F pilot in 32 English schools (14 primary, 16 secondary, 2 special) between September 2015 and July 2016. The report states "Demand was at the upper end of expectations" with 192 question-posting users, and 60% of survey respondents reporting use.

> "Demand was at the upper end of expectations: 192 users from the 32 schools (around 9% of teachers) posted a question over the year of the pilot. Sixty percent of teachers who responded to the survey indicated that they used the service to ask a question or to read the responses."
