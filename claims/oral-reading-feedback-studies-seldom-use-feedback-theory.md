### Grønli 2024

Grønli, K. M., Walgermo, B. R., McTigue, E. M., & Uppstad, P. H. (2024). Teachers' Feedback on Oral Reading: A Critical Review of its Effects and the use of Theory in Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09957-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's narrative synthesis of theoretical foundations across the 24 studies. Only five studies explicitly incorporated feedback theories, and no more than two used models listed by Lipnevich and Panadero (2021); the review states "studies reference empirical research more than established theories" while reading-theory engagement was more explicit.

> "Regarding feedback theory, we found that studies reference empirical research more than established theories."

### Grønli 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's coding of the 24 studies' knowledge paradigms, since "None of the 24 studies explicitly discusses its knowledge paradigm". Theory-engagement coding (adapted from Nieminen et al., 2023, Ashwin, 2012, and Hew et al., 2019) scored all studies 0–12, with Table 4 showing no theory advancement in any study.

> "Our findings indicate that 8 studies adopt a behaviorist perspective, 13 studies a cognitive one, and 3 studies a sociocultural one."
