## Evidence

### Zhou Huiqin 2025

Zhou Huiqin, Huang Liyuan, Du Yuanzhuo, Lio Jonathan, Luo Bin, Xiao Xingyuan, Yang Kun. (2025). Group competition mechanism in surgical robot training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08080-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the RCT's work-habit analysis, one-way ANOVA on arm movement distances found "Group B performing significantly better than Group A" (P < 0.05), reported for both MB and TR master workspace range values in Fig. 3.

> "A one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) test was performed to compare the arm movement distances between the two groups. There was a statistically significant difference between the groups (P < 0.05), with Group B performing significantly better than Group A, as presented in Fig. 3."

### Zhou Huiqin 2025 (2)

Zhou Huiqin, Huang Liyuan, Du Yuanzhuo, Lio Jonathan, Luo Bin, Xiao Xingyuan, Yang Kun. (2025). Group competition mechanism in surgical robot training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08080-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The workspace range parameter (clutch-pedal proficiency) showed means of 9.06 ± 2.06 (Group A) and 8.74 ± 1.96 cm (Group B) with "no statistically significant difference (P > 0.05)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The workspace range values for Groups A and B were 9.06 ± 2.06 and 8.74 ± 1.96 cm, respectively, with no statistically significant difference (P > 0.05)."
