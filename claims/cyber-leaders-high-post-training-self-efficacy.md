### Cross et al. 2015

Donna Cross, Leanne Lester, Amy Barnes, Patricia Cardoso and Kate Hadwen. (2015). If it’s about me, why do it without me? Genuine student engagement in school cyberbullying education. International Journal of Emotional Education, 7(1), 35-51. www.um.edu.mt/cres/ijee. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085606

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-training online questionnaire completed by 154 of the recruited cyber leaders (98% of post-training respondents) in the CFSP intervention schools; self-efficacy items had alpha=0.900. The article reports "discuss ways to prevent cyberbullying with students (94%)" and comparable high percentages across the ten self-efficacy items (Table II).

> "After completing the training, most students felt t hey had the skills to: discuss ways to prevent cyberbullying with students (94%), parents (89%) and teachers (86%); discuss safe and appropriate technology use with students (94%)"
