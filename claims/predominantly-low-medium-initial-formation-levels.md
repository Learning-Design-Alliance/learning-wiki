`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Verifying experiment at M. Auezov South-Kazakhstan State University and H.A. Yassawi International Kazakh-Turkish University with 586 students (312 experimental, 274 control), plus 60 language and content teachers at various stages (Tables 5-7, Figure 1). The control group's 1.8% high level comprised 5 students who graduated from special schools with advanced language learning.

> "the high level of formation of the experimental group students' professional competence on the basis of polylingual approach is absent; the majority of students have low, medium, and sufficient levels (respectively, 48.4%, 35.9% and 15.7%)"
