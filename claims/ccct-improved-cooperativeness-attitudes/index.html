Özgür Tutal 2026¶

Özgür Tutal. (2026). Artificial intelligence assisted design of a novel cooperative learning technique for higher education. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-50770-1

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Paired samples t-test on the cooperativeness scale within the experimental CCCT group (n = 31), from pre X = 68.03 (SD = 10.63) to post X = 75.19 (SD = 8.02), p = 0.007; the article reports a moderate effect size of g = 0.751.

"a notable increase between the participants' pre-survey mean scores ( X = 68.03, SD = 10.63) and post-survey mean scores ( X = 75.19, SD = 8.02). This observed increase was found to be statistically significant ( p = 0.007), indicating that the CCCT implementation had a meaningful influence on enhancing participants' attitudes towards cooperativeness"
