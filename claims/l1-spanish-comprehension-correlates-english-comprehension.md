Kittle, J. M., Amendum, S. J., & Budde, C. M. (2024). What Does Research Say About the Science of Reading for K-5 Multilingual Learners? A Systematic Review of Systematic Reviews. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09942-6

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Narrative synthesis of two systematic reviews (Dressler & Kamil, 2006; Riches & Genesee, 2006). The review reports the positive L1–L2 comprehension correlation and the English-proficiency moderator; Riches and Genesee found Spanish reading comprehension was the best predictor of English comprehension one year later. No effect size is printed.

> "Dressler and Kamil (2006) found that L1 (Spanish) reading comprehension was positively correlated with English reading comprehension. They noted that this relationship was weaker for students with lower levels of English language proficiency"
