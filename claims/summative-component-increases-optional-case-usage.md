### Palmer Edward 2014

Palmer Edward, Devitt Peter. (2014). The assessment of a structured online formative assessment program: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-8

Descriptive comparison of usage tables for the two years (Tables 5 and 6). The article reports "twice as many students attempted cases on average" in the optional module and approximately 3 times higher attempting in minitest modules; no effect size is printed.
