## Evidence

### Lithoxoidou 2017

Lithoxoidou, L. S., Georgopoulos, A. D., Dimitriou, A. Th., & Xenitidou, S. Ch. (2017). “Trees have a soul too!” Developing Empathy and Environmental Values in Early Childhood. International Journal of Early Childhood Environmental Education, 5(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1158501

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative analysis of anthropocentric subcategories (natural resource, health, recreational-aesthetic value) among 147 total initial-interview responses, plotted in Figure 2. The Health subcategory increased more than sixfold; the authors attribute this to attainment of the program's cognitive objectives supporting emotional goals.

> "In the second interview subcategory C.I.1 remains stable, C.I.2 increased more than sixfold its answers and C.I.3 increases them considerably, as shown in Figure 2."
