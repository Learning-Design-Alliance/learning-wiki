Rubric-level analysis of MER-involving connections across the three performance categories; the article reports, for example, low-performing students with 4 substantial, 2 intermediate, and 1 superficial SBT–MER connection versus high-performing students with 4 superficial, 2 intermediate, and 1 substantial.

> "These findings indicate that metacognitive reflection does not consistently increase in sophistication with overall performance, unlike the more consistent patterns observed for SBT–DBT and SBT–MBT."
