## Evidence

### Ulrik Lyngs 2020

Ulrik Lyngs, Kai Lukoff, Petr Slovak, William Seymour, Helena Webb, Marina Jirotka, Jun Zhao, Max Van Kleek, Nigel Shadbolt. (2020). 'I Just Want to Hack Myself to Not Get Distracted': Evaluating Design Interventions for Self-Control on Facebook. CHI '20, April 25–30, 2020, Honolulu, HI, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376672

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The same logging analysis (RQ1) for the goal-reminder condition: daily visits fell from a median of 29.4 to 10.6 ("r = 0.63"), and visit duration showed only a trend towards shortening (d = 0.51, p = 0.07, not significant).

> "number of daily visits declined (median number of visits in baseline = 29.4, median in intervention = 10.6, p = 0.01, r = 0.63, Wilcoxon signed rank test); and there was a trend towards shorter visits (mean tab visit duration in baseline = 1m 25s, mean in intervention = 1m 15s, t(14) = 1.96, p = 0.07, d = 0.51)"
