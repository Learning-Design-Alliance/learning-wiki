## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Effect size significantly varies by application duration (QB=13.554, p<.05); applications of 21-30 hours (ES=1.769) and 31-40 hours (ES=1.514) show large effects while 1-10 hours shows a medium effect (ES=.525). [→ Özgür Ulubey 2018](#ozgur-ulubey-2018)

## Evidence

### Özgür Ulubey 2018

Özgür Ulubey. (2018). The Effect of Creative Drama as a Method on Skills: A Meta-analysis Study. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 6(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1172791

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis across the 63 studies grouped by application duration (1-10 to 41+ hours). The article reports the significant Qbetween statistic and per-level effect sizes, with 1-10 hours medium (ES=.525) and 21-30 and 31-40 hours large.

> "it was found that the effect size significantly varies depending on the duration of experimental application (QB=13.554, p<.05). The effect size in the experimental application of 21-30 hours (ES=1.769) and 31-40 hours (ES=1.514) is large according to the Cohen’s (1988) classification."
