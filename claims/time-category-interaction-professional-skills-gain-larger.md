### Golos Anat 2019

Golos Anat, Tekuzener Esti. (2019). Perceptions, expectations and satisfaction levels of occupational therapy students prior to and after practice placement and comparison of practice placement models. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1762-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Two-way repeated measures ANOVA on all students' pre- and post-placement scores across the two skill categories. The article reports the interaction was significant with Partial η2 = .27, and that the change in professional skills (d-score = .59) was higher than in personal skills (d-score = .28).
