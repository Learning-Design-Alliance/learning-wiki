### Mochizuki 2019

Mochizuki, T., Nishimori, T., Tsubakimoto, M., Oura, H., Sato, T., Johansson, H., Nakahara, J., & Yamauchi, Y. (2019). Development of software to support argumentative reading and writing by means of creating a graphic organizer from an electronic text. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09676-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analytic coding of the 63 students' essays on six dichotomous criteria (Cohen's kappa 0.73–0.97). Fisher's exact tests found significant class differences on criteria 1–3 (e.g., identifying grounds p = .014); criteria 4–6 showed no significant associations.

> "There were significant associations in criteria 1 to 3, meaning that the students in the knowledge-mapping class were able to identify and explain the author's main statement and grounds in a well-organized manner"
