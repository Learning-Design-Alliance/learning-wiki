### Mirofora Pilakouta and John Sinatkas 2020

Mirofora Pilakouta and John Sinatkas. (2020). Surveying Undrergraduare Greek Student's Knowledge on Some Issues of Radiations and Nuclear Energy. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.03549](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.03549)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire survey of 313 undergraduate students (254 TEI Piraeus, 59 TEI West Macedonia), analyzed with SPSS frequency recording and subgroup comparisons. The article reports that "the percentage of the correct responses in all groups and subgroups were low (less than 50%) or very low (less than 20%)" except A1 and A2.

> "Overall, almost in all the questions , figure: 1, and 2, Table1 and 2, the percentage of  the correct responses in all groups and subgroups were low  (less than 50%) or very low (less than 20%). The only exception was the answers in the questions A1 and A2."
