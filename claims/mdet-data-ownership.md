`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article asserts, citing Szott (2014) and Song (2014), that student-collected authentic data with mobile devices fosters ownership; this is an attributed claim with no effect size or study design printed.

> "adding mobile devices as a tool to take data in experiments, can give students a sense of "ownership" (Szott, 2014) because it is by their own measurements (and sometimes devices) that they obtain real, authentic data for further analysis"
