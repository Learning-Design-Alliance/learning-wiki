`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 524 clinical nurses in a tertiary hospital in Hangzhou, China (March–April 2024), analyzed with descriptive statistics. The article reports that "the proportions of clinical nurses who were rated “good” were much lower for knowledge (10.3%) and practice (13.4%) than attitudes (65.3%)"; the mean total score was 96.17 ± 12.32.
