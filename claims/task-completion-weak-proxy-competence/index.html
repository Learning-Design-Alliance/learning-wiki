Qusay H. Mahmoud 2026¶

Qusay H. Mahmoud. (2026). Judgment-Centred Software Engineering Education: A Post-Hype Review and Framework for AI-Augmented Learning. Preprint, September 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29473 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.29473

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports observational novice-programming research by Prather et al. in which "some students used GenAI as an accelerator" while struggling students risked "an illusion of competence", showing completion does not reveal understanding.

"Prather et al. [2024] observed that some students used GenAI as an accelerator while struggling students could experience compounded metacognitive difficulties and an illusion of competence."

Qusay H. Mahmoud 2026 (2)¶

Qusay H. Mahmoud. (2026). Judgment-Centred Software Engineering Education: A Post-Hype Review and Framework for AI-Augmented Learning. Preprint, September 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29473

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes to Qiao et al.'s systematic review of 31 code-comprehension studies both "genuine opportunities for tailored explanation" and inaccurate or unclear explanations, with novices struggling to prompt and to evaluate output.

"Qiao et al. [2026], reviewing 31 studies of GenAI for code comprehension, found genuine opportunities for tailored explanation alongside inaccurate or unclear explanations and difficulties for novices in both prompting and evaluation."
