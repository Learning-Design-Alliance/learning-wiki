## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Impact of instructional approach on students' epistemologies about experimental physics. arXiv:1609.05758. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

3-way ANCOVAs run separately on the N = 3481 matched subsample after splitting by gender, controlling for pre-instruction score and student major. Instructional approach is significant for both groups (F-test, p ≪ 0.01); only the test statistic is printed.

> "Instructional approach was also a signiﬁcant predictor for both men and women (F-test, p ≪ 0.01), with a higher mean for students in Transformed courses in both cases."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Impact of instructional approach on students' epistemologies about experimental physics. arXiv:1609.05758. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same gender-split 3-way ANCOVAs of the matched E-CLASS data; student major significantly predicted post-instruction scores for both men and women (F-test, p = 0.01), with no effect size printed.

> "Student major was a signiﬁcant predictor of post-instruction scores for both men and women (F-test, p = 0 .01)."
