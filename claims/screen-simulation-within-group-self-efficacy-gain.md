## Evidence

### Bayram Hilal Çelik 2025

Bayram Hilal Çelik, Ergün Sibel. (2025). Effect of screen-based simulation on pediatric drug administration among nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2025-0249en

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-test within the intervention group (n = 99) in the randomized controlled trial, comparing pre-test and 20-day post-test MASSCNS scores after four screen-based simulation scenarios. Only means and p are printed; no effect size is reported.

> "A statistically significant difference was found between the pre- and post-test scores for the MASSCNS in the intervention group (p < 0.05). When the mean values were examined, it was found that the mean pre-test scores of the intervention group (43.65 ± 14.08) were lower than the mean post-test scores (68.08 ± 10.77)"
