`q2 i?` In a case-control study of 20 physicians, the expert group (more than 30 epiduroscopy experiences) achieved a higher median training score than the beginner group (75.00 vs. 67.50; P < 0.01). [→ Lee Jong Joo 2020](#lee-jong-joo-2020)

## Evidence

### Lee Jong Joo 2020

Lee Jong Joo, Ko Junho, Yun Yeomin, Jang Seong-Wook, Ha Yoon, Kim Yoon Sang, Shin Dong Ah. (2020). Feasibility of the Epiduroscopy Simulator as a Training Tool: A Pilot Study. Pain Research & Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/5428170

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case-control study of 20 physicians (7 beginners, 13 experts) each performing the simulator 20 times in one day; Mann-Whitney comparison of groups. The article reports "the training score was better in the expert group (67.50 vs. 75.00; P < 0.01)".

> "While there was no difference in the time to reach a virtual target between both groups with a median time of 83.50 s vs. 83.00 s, the training score was better in the expert group (67.50 vs. 75.00; P < 0.01)"
