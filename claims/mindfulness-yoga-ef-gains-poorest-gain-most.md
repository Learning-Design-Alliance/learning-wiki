# Mindfulness training improves EFs most in 7–9 year-olds with initially poorer EFs, and yoga improves EFs where physical training alone does not

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After mindfulness training, 7–9 year-olds with initially poorer EFs improved overall and in shifting and monitoring, bringing scores up to average, with improvements reported by both teachers and parents. [→ Diamond Adele 2011](#diamond-adele-2011)
`q2 i?` Girls assigned to yoga improved EFs, most evidently when EF demands were greatest, while physical training without mindfulness produced no EF improvement. [→ Diamond Adele 2011 (2)](#diamond-adele-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Diamond Adele 2011

Diamond Adele, Lee Kathleen. (2011). Interventions shown to Aid Executive Function Development in Children 4–12 Years Old. Science. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1204529

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a mindfulness training study with sessions of sitting meditation, sensory-awareness activities, and body scan, with demands increasing over time; improvements generalized across contexts.

> "After mindfulness training, greater EF improvements were found in 7–9 year-olds with initially-poorer EFs than those with initially-better EFs, compared with controls (who silently read) (30)."

### Diamond Adele 2011 (2)

Diamond Adele, Lee Kathleen. (2011). Interventions shown to Aid Executive Function Development in Children 4–12 Years Old. Science. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1204529

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports girls aged 10 and 13 randomly assigned to yoga or physical training; only yoga, which included mindfulness components, improved EFs.

> "Yoga training (physical training, relaxation, and sensory awareness) improved EFs, with improvements most evident when EF demands were greatest. Physical training (physical activity without mindfulness) produced no EF improvement."
