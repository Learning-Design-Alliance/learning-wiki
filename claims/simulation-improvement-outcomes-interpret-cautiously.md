The article's framing argument: healthcare is a complex adaptive system whose behavior 'can be nonlinear and therefore unpredictable', so simulations may not capture all aspects of the surrounding system that affect an innovation's execution or effects. Reasoned argument, no data reported.

> "the outcomes of simulation-based healthcare improvement exercises should be interpreted cautiously with this in mind even when every effort has been made to collect high-quality evaluation data."
