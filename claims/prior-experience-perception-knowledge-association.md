### Cook David A 2014

Cook David A, Thompson Warren G. (2014). Comfort and experience with online learning: trends over nine years and associations with knowledge. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-128

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

General linear model regression relating prior-experience ratings to mean knowledge posttest percent correct, adjusted for study year, among residents with prior online learning. The article reports "a significant association (b = 1.56; p = 0.02)"; the coefficient is unstandardized, so no effect size is coded.
