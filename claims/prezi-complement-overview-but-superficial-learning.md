## Evidence

### Hyll Mary 2019

Hyll Mary, Schvarcz Robert, Manninen Katri. (2019). Exploring how medical students learn with the help of a digital presentation: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1569-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of the interviews showed students treated the presentation as "a complement to other pedagogical activities offered in the course", using it as a base for sorting and categorising antibiotics with other sources. Some found it contained too much information with a complicated diagram and relied on a table-form handout instead; others said the context was missing and learning objectives were unclear or missing.

> "The students saw the presentation as a complement to other pedagogical activities offered in the course. Some students used the Prezi presentation as a base for guiding their studies, and built upon its structure."
