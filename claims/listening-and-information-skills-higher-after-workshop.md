### Polivka L 2024

Polivka L, Delcour C, Dufresne H, Bartoli S, Bataille P, Bekel L, Bonigen J, Deladrière E, Dimarcq S, Felix A, Havas C, Le Goff H, Levy M, Riback E, Welfringer-Morin A, Houdouin V, Hadj-Rabia S, Bodemer C, Faye A, Melki I. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

External evaluation of communication skills and attitudes on the OSCE grid: listening scored "0.67 out of 1 vs. 0.48, p = 0.04" and information provision 0.66 vs. 0.48, p = 0.026, favouring the workshop group.

> "the scores for “ability to listen” and “ability to provide the patient with information” were significantly higher in the interventional group than in the control group (respectively 0.67 out of 1 vs. 0.48, p = 0.04; 0.66 vs. 0.48, p = 0.026)"
