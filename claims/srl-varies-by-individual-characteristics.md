## Evidence

### Kizilcec 2017

Kizilcec, R. F., Pérez-Sanagustín, M., & Maldonado, J. J. (2017). Self-regulated learning strategies predict learner behavior and goal attainment in Massive Open Online Courses. Computers & Education, 104, 18–33. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2016.10.001

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Penalized (elastic net) regressions on 27 individual characteristics predicting each of six SRL strategy scores among 4,831 learners, with coefficients estimated simultaneously adjusting for all other predictors. "Older learners reported consistently higher levels of SRL, except for help seeking."

> "Older learners reported consistently higher levels of SRL, except for help seeking. Women reported lower levels of strategic planning, elaboration, and self-evaluation; however, women reported higher levels of goal setting, task strategies, and especially help seeking."
