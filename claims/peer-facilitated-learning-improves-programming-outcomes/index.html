Evidence¶

Tsai Chia-Wen 2023¶

Tsai Chia-Wen, Lin Michael Yu-Ching, Cheng Yih-Ping, Lee Lan-Yu, Chyr Wen-Li, Lin Chih-Hsien, Lin Jian-Wei, Tsai Meng-Chuan. (2023). The effects of online peer-facilitated learning and distributed pair programming on students’ learning. Computers & Education, 203, 104849. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104849

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-tests comparing pooled peer-facilitated (G1, G2; n=66) vs non-peer-facilitated (G3, G4; n=62) groups on three post-test measures. Programming skills: t(126)=3.433, p=.001; enjoyment: t(126)=2.210, p=.029; intention: t(126)=2.386, p=.018.

"the peer-facilitated learning group students' programming skills, enjoyment of learning, and intention to learn, were significantly higher than the non-peer-facilitated learning groups (p < .05)"
