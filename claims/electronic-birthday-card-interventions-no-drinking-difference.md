## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students randomly assigned to control, social norms, risk reduction, or combined e-card cohorts did not differ significantly in the number of drinks consumed on their 21st birthday. [→ Glassman T 2010](#glassman-t-2010)

## Evidence

### Glassman T 2010

Glassman T, Dodd V, Kenzik K, Miller EM, Sheu JJ. (2010). Social norms vs. risk reduction approaches to 21st birthday celebrations. Am J Health Educ. 2010;41(1):38-45. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ871144

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized four-cohort experiment (n=702 respondents of 2,302 invited) at a large public southeastern university; ANOVA compared mean birthday drinks across control, social norms, risk reduction, and combo cohorts. The article reports "no significant difference found between the four cohorts" (F = 1.001, P = 0.392); no effect size printed.

> "There was no significant difference found between the four cohorts concerning the number of drinks consumed (F = 1.001, df = 3/538, P = 0.392)."
