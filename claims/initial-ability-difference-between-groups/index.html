Wenno et al. 2016¶

Izaak Hendrik Wenno, Pieter Wattimena, Luky Maspaitela. (2016). Comparative Study between Drill Skill and Concept Attainment Model towards Physics Learning Achievement. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.5, No.3. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-test data collected before the learning process showed group 1 at 80% fail and group 2 at 66.67% fail categorization. The authors state these results "indicate that there is difference initial ability between two classes".
