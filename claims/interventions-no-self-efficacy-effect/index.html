Evidence¶

Mukherjee 2023¶

Mukherjee, S., Rohles, B., Distler, V., Lenzini, G., & Koenig, V. (2023). The effects of privacy-non-invasive interventions on cheating prevention and user experience in unproctored online assessments: An empirical study. Computers and Education, 207, 104925. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104925

q3 · i? · causal · r2

OLS regression on test-taking self-efficacy (measured with an 8-item adapted NGSE scale, Cronbach's alpha = 0.95) in the between-subjects experiment (N = 997) showed no significant overall or individual intervention effects.

"Both the overall effect (p = 0.136) and the individual effects of the interventions were found to be statistically non-significant."
