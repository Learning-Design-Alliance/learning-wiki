Related Claims¶

  • Basic prompt injection attempts failed or were detected: instruction manipulation alone failed in all 9 iterations, role-playing without concealment was detected in output, and metadata-hidden injections failed in all 9 iterations — related
  • Asking the AI tool directly about hidden instructions exposed the prompt injection in all 6 security-check iterations, but the authors caution this is not a robust safeguard — related
  • A revised injection combining instruction manipulation and role-playing with an explicit do-not-mention instruction succeeded in all 9 iterations across docx, pdf, and htm formats — related
  • Indirect prompt injections combining instruction manipulation, role-playing, and obfuscation altered AI-assigned grades with attack success rates of 94–100% while remaining undetected — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Learners rarely detect manipulation of offloaded information and may replace internal memory with altered external content — related
