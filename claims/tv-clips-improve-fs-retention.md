## Evidence

### Koç and Koç 2017

Didem Koban Koç & Serdar Engin Koç. (2017). On the Role of Media Input in the Learning of Formulaic Sequences by EFL Learners. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(3), 93–110. https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1275

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on delayed posttest scores of 20 experimental and 20 control participants. The article reports the difference was "statistically significant (t = -2.04, p = .05)" with means of 11.00 versus 8.35 and a printed effect size d = .02 described as small significance.

> "the differences in the retention of the FSs between the two groups was statistically significant (t = -2.04, p = .05). The experimental group (M = 11.00, SD = 3.72) retained the FSs at a higher rate than the control group (M = 8.35, SD = 4.41). Cohen’s effect size value (d = .02) suggested a small significance."
