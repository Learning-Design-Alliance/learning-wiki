## Evidence

### Tommy Wells & Amy Auletto 2024

Tommy Wells & Amy Auletto. (2024). Understanding the Impact of Educators' Social-emotional Learning (SEL) Skills on Student Academic Growth. E4 Center. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2024/08/21/understanding-the-impact-of-educators-social-emotional-learning-sel-skills-on-student-academic-growth/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Inferential analysis of the 269-educator survey found well-being predicted emotion regulation skills; school climate was not predictive of emotion regulation or relationship management, and well-being was not predictive of relationship management.

> "Moreover, when accounting for perceptions of school climate and NSLP percentage, well-being still predicted emotion regulation skills."
