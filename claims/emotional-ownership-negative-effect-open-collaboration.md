Educators' Open Educational Collaboration Online: The Dilemma of Emotional Ownership. (2016). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1115784

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 100 educators analyzed with structural equation modelling found "a negative influence of emotional ownership of knowledge on intention to engage in online collaboration". The path coefficient was -0.290 (p<0.05).

> "H2 was not supported while we identified a negative influence of emotional ownership of knowledge on intention to engage in online collaboration."
