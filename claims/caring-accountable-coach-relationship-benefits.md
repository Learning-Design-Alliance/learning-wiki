## Evidence

### Parker 2010

Parker, D. R., Field Hoffman, S., Sawilowsky, S., & Rolands, L. (2010). An Examination of the Effects of ADHD Coaching on University Students' Executive Functioning. Journal of Postsecondary Education and Disability, 24(2), 115-132. https://www.edgefoundation.org

`q2 · i?`

Qualitative interview responses to the second research question about benefits of coaching services. Students described coaches as empathetic and knowledgeable about ADHD, and reported that being held accountable helped them progress rather than harming the relationship.

> "Their comments overwhelmingly focused on helpful dynamics in their relationship with a caring coach. Some students spoke to qualities in the coach, such as being a skilled facilitator of the student's planning process, tailoring suggestions to that student's needs or preferences, holding students accountable in a respectful but consistent manner."
