## Evidence

### Richard Cox & Paul Brna 2015

Richard Cox & Paul Brna. (2015). Twenty Years on: Reflections on "Supporting the Use of External Representations in Problem Solving". Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:193–204. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0054-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The authors report, from their ER card-sort studies (a knowledge-elicitation method in which subjects sorted a varied range of ERs into categories that made conceptual sense to them), that "better diagrammatic reasoners tend to sort graphs, charts, notations and diagrams into fewer and more cohesive categories". No effect size is printed.

> "We found that better diagrammatic reasoners tend to sort graphs, charts, notations and diagrams into fewer and more cohesive categories and name them more accurately than poorer diagrammatic reasoners."
