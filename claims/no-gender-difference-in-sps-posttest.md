## Evidence

### Chengere Ashebir Mekonnen 2025

Chengere Ashebir Mekonnen, Bono Beyene Dobo, Zinabu Samuel Assefa, Jilo Kedir Woliy. (2025). Enhancing secondary school students’ science process skills through guided inquiry-based laboratory activities in biology. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0320692

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analysis comparing male and female posttest SPS scores in both groups (EG: 22 males, 24 females; CG: 13 males, 16 females). The article reports no significant differences and concludes "gender did not have a significant impact on SPS scores in either group".

> "In the EG, the t-value was 1.25 with a p-value of.216 (p > .05), and in the CG, the t-value was -.94 with a p-value of.353 (p > .05). These findings indicate that gender did not have a significant impact on SPS scores in either group."
