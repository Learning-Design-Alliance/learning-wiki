## Evidence

### Deng Jing 2025

Deng Jing, Tan Yifei, Chen Jing, Guo Xiujing, Wang Ling, Xie Chuan. (2025). Virtual reality simulation to teach communication for undergraduate nursing students: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08450-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's thematic synthesis of challenges across the included studies identifies realism, character responsiveness limited to predefined responses, and language barriers. The authors note virtual agents have "constrained capacity for affective communication and risks of dialogic discontinuity."

> "The prominent challenge is ensuring that conversations in the VR environment are relevant to real-life situations. This stems from virtual agents’ constrained capacity for affective communication and risks of dialogic discontinuity"
