## Evidence

### Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi 2025

Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi (2025). Consistency between self-reported and log data to understand students’ experience of learning in flipped classrooms. Scientific Reports 15, 34369. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17052-8

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVAs and chi-square tests comparing students grouped above versus below the mean of the two perception scales (n = 135 vs n = 136) showed significant differences on all variables "Except for the surface learning approach (F(1, 269) = 0.10, p = 0.75, η2 = 0.00)"; the largest printed effect was η2 = 0.21 for the deep approach.

> "Except for the surface learning approach (F(1, 269) = 0.10, p = 0.75, η2 = 0.00), the two groups of students differed significantly on all the variables: self-reported deep approach (F(1, 269) = 69.74, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.21), self-reported weekly learning time (χ2(1) = 4.20, p = 0.03, φ = 0.12), observed learning approach (χ2(1) = 13.73, p < 0.001, φ = 0.23), observed weekly learning time (F(1, 269) = 24.13, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.08), and course marks (F(1, 269) = 7.76, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.03)."
