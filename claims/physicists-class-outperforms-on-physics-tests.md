G. S. Djordjevica and D. Pavlovic-Babic. (2009). High School Class for Gifted Pupils in Physics and Sciences and Pupils' Skills Measured by Standard and Pisa Test. [doi:10.1063/1.3322362](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3322362)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent evaluation by a team nominated by the Serbian Ministry of Education, testing the first 'physicists' generation (n=9) against 'mathematicians' (n=8) and standard class (n=25), all 4th grade. Table 2 reports total scores of 85.68%, 74.26% and 34.47% respectively, and 69.66% vs 37.5% vs 0.0% at complexity level 4.
