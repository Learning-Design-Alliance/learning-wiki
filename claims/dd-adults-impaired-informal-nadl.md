# Adults with developmental dyscalculia are impaired in everyday numerical tasks, particularly time estimation, measure, and money usage

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Adults with DD scored significantly lower than controls on the NADL Informal Test total (r = -0.458, p = 0.001). [→ Vigna Giulia 2022](#vigna-giulia-2022)
`q3 i3` Adults with DD scored significantly lower than controls in money usage (r = -0.403, p = 0.003). [→ Vigna Giulia 2022 (2)](#vigna-giulia-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Vigna Giulia 2022

Vigna Giulia, Ghidoni Enrico, Burgio Francesca, Danesin Laura, Angelini Damiano, Benavides-Varela Silvia, Semenza Carlo. (2022). Dyscalculia in Early Adulthood: Implications for Numerical Activities of Daily Living. Brain Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci12030373

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon rank-sum comparison on the NADL Informal Test completed by 21 participants with DD and 26 controls. DD mean was 19.57 (SD 2.86) versus 21.62 (SD 1.10) for controls, effect size "−0.458", p = 0.001, one-sided.

> "TOTAL INFORMAL * 23 19.57 2.86 21.62 1.10 130.5 0.001 −0.458 0.047"

### Vigna Giulia 2022 (2)

Vigna Giulia, Ghidoni Enrico, Burgio Francesca, Danesin Laura, Angelini Damiano, Benavides-Varela Silvia, Semenza Carlo. (2022). Dyscalculia in Early Adulthood: Implications for Numerical Activities of Daily Living. Brain Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci12030373

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Money usage subtest of the NADL Informal Test comparing DD and control participants; DD mean 6.95 (SD 0.92) versus 7.62 (SD 0.75) for controls, effect size "−0.403", p = 0.003. Bayes factors confirmed substantial evidence of H1 for money usage.

> "Money * 8 6.95 0.92 7.62 0.75 156.5 0.003 −0.403 0.208"
