The article reports that MIT's mechanical engineering program achieved 49.5% women in 2017, in a context where women receive only "19.5% of bachelor degrees in engineering" nationally. The article attributes this to structural changes including the Women's Technology Program, hiring more women faculty, and modernizing curriculum.

> "In a climate where women receive 19.5% of bachelor degrees in engineering and only 7.9% of mechanical engineers are women, the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) succeeded to attract 49.5% women in 2017 to its mechanical engineering program."
