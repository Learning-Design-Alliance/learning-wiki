# Certified preceptors reported high satisfaction with the certification system, with every questionnaire item scoring above 4.7 on a 5-point scale (Cronbach's α = 0.936)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among 145 certified preceptors responding to a 20-item questionnaire, the mean score for each item exceeded 4.7 on a 5-point Likert scale, and the questionnaire showed Cronbach's α of 0.936, KMO of 0.861, and significant Bartlett's sphericity. [→ Tang Yinying 2026](#tang-yinying-2026)

## Evidence

### Tang Yinying 2026

Tang Yinying, Linyue Tan, Chen Xiuwen, Chen Huiqiong (2026). Construction and practice of a certification system for clinical nursing preceptors: a “license to teach” approach in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08953-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-developed questionnaire (20 closed items, 4 open) administered to certified preceptors; 145 valid responses from 148 returned. Means and SDs are reported descriptively (e.g., overall satisfaction item 4.91, SD 0.29). The article separately reports reliability as "an overall Cronbach’s α of 0.936" and "KMO = 0.861; Bartlett's test of sphericity χ2 = 2520.209, p < 0.001". No effect size is printed.

> "The results showed that the mean score for each item exceeded 4.7, indicating high overall satisfaction with the certification system among participants."
