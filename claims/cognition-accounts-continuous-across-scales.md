Conlin, L., Gupta, A., & Hammer, D. (2009). Where to find the mind: Identifying the scale of cognitive dynamics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1003.0491](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1003.0491)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the article's framing section, building on complex-systems accounts of emergent behavior (flocking, synchronization) and on the resources model. The authors state the perspectives 'represent different scales of a complex system, rather than as fundamentally different approaches.'

> "We suggest that these can be seen as similar dynamics at different scales, that a 'society of mind' model of individual cognition is theoretical continuous with a 'mind of society' model of social cognition."
