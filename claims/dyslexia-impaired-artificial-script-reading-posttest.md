### Cao 2026

Cao, Y., Zhang, M., Gentile, F. & Bonte, M. (2026). Dynamic behavioral and neural correlates of letter-speech sound learning in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00410-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-tests on posttests in 62 adults: "the DD group scored significantly lower than the TR group on each of the Syllable, Letter, and Word reading tasks" (all ts > 2.10, all ps < .05); no group difference on visual symbol recognition. No effect sizes printed for these group contrasts.

> "the DD group scored significantly lower than the TR group on each of the Syllable, Letter, and Word reading tasks (all ts > 2.10, all ps < .05). No significant differences were observed between the two groups on the more basic visual symbol recognition task (Posttest Visual)."
