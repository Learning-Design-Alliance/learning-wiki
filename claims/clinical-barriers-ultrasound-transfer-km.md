`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of student reflections on application and transfer in the portfolio study found barriers "included the inability of Korean Medicine doctors to claim insurance reimbursement", equipment cost, and portable device limits such as low resolution and limited operating time.

> "The reasons cited included the inability of Korean Medicine doctors to claim insurance reimbursement for ultrasound use, financial burden associated with purchasing ultrasound equipment, and limitations of portable ultrasound devices, such as low image resolution and limited usable operating time."
