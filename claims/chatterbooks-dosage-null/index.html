Evidence¶

Styles 2014¶

Styles, B., Clarkson, R., & Fowler, K. (2014). Chatterbooks: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581155

q3 · i0 · causal · r3

On-treatment analysis using tutor-log counts of sessions experienced per child, with the median nine sessions for both interventions. The report cautions this analysis may be biased due to self-selection to differing levels of exposure.

"As for the definitive analysis described above, a repeated-measures model was used for the dosage analysis. It revealed no significant effect : having one more session was associated with a change in post -test score of -0.02 (-0.12, 0.08)."
