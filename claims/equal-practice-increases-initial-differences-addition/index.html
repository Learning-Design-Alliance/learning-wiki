Table 7 reports average gross gains by initial-score bands within each grade; for example, grade 7 pupils in the highest band gained 19.4 on average while lower bands gained less. The article reports gains by initial status rising from 11.4 for the lowest band to 19.1 for the highest.

"The effect of equal practice upon groups of different initial ability within the same grade is to increase rather than reduce the initial differences. The facts are shown in Table 7. Those who by original capacity or circumstances of training are ahead at the start maintain and increase their lead."
