Mahsa Bakhshaei 2020¶

Mahsa Bakhshaei, Angela Hardy, Jason Ravitz, John Seylar. (2020). Instructional Coaching Holds Promise as a Method to Improve Instruction with Technology. SITE 2020 - Online, April 7-10, 2020. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/5465e54a-b290-4210-97fb-514a3aa13e21

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Correlational analyses of DLP year 2 teacher survey data linking partnership perceptions to ITU confidence ("corr between .16 and .21, p < .001") and to challenge improvement ("corr for index score = .43, p < .001"). The article prints correlations but not in a standardized metric the coding rules accept, so impact is left uncoded.

"Correlation analyses show a positive significant relationship between teachers reporting coaching as a partnership and their confidence in the six areas of their ITU ability (corr between .16 and .21, p < .001) as well as their average improvement in their selected classroom challenge(s), such as assessment or differentiation (corr for index score = .43, p < .001)."

Survey correlations from the DLP: voluntary participation with a printed "corr with improvement in classroom challenges = .36, p < .001" and non-evaluative support with "corr with improvement in classroom challenges = 0.3, p < .001 ", as printed; no further standardized effect size is given.

"The most important conditions seem to be that teacher participation is voluntary (corr with improvement in classroom challenges = .36, p < .001) and that teachers receive support from a coach in a non-evaluative manner (corr with improvement in classroom challenges = 0.3, p < .001 )."
