## Evidence

### Junko Yamashita 2013

Junko Yamashita. (2013). Effects of extensive reading on reading attitudes in a foreign language. Reading in a Foreign Language, 25(2). http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl [doi:10.64152/10125/66872](https://doi.org/10.64152/10125/66872)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Greenhouse-Geisser corrected 2 x 4 repeated-measures ANOVA on data from 61 students found the omnibus time × attitude interaction significant (F(2, 134) = 12.101, p < .001, ηp² = .168), with the main effect of attitude also significant (ηp² = .611).

> "the main effect of attitude and the interaction between time and attitude were significant, F(2, 138) = 94.053, p < .001, ηp2= .611; F(2, 134) = 12.101, p < .001, ηp2 = .168"
