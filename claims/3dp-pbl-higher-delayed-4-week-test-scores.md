## Evidence

### Jiang Dingyuan 2025

Jiang Dingyuan, Duan Kui, Li Zhigang, Chen Xueming, Li Nani. (2025). The effect of 3D printing integrated with problem-based learning on AGP training for thoracolumbar fractures: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08152-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

In the same RCT, the delayed test administered four weeks after the course to assess knowledge retention was compared between groups with Mann–Whitney U tests. The 3DP score was "significantly higher", r = 0.593 [0.429, 0.719].

> "in the delayed post-course test (4 weeks), the score of the 3DP group (median = 82.5, IQR: 80.0–90.0) was significantly higher than that of the control group (median = 70.0, IQR: 65.0–75.0) (U = 255.00, p < 0.001, r = 0.593, 95% CI [0.429, 0.719])"
