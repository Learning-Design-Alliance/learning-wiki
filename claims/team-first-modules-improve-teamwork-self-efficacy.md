Matched-pair pre/post survey analysis of five cohorts of medical students (matched pairs 215, 226, 223, 129, 184 for M1–M5) using an 8-item self-efficacy survey on a 100-point scale (Cronbach's alpha 0.883–0.965). The article reports "significant improvement in self-efficacy scores post-survey compared to pre-survey (p < 0.0001)" in all modules; no effect size is printed in this passage.

> "Composite data suggests each module builds self-efficacy, or confidence, in teamwork competencies. All modules show significant improvement in self-efficacy scores post-survey compared to pre-survey (p < 0.0001)."
