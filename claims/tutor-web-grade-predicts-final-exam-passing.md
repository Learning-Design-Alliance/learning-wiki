## Evidence

### Jamie Lentin 2014

Jamie Lentin, Anna H. Jonsdottir, David Stern, Victoria Mokua, Eva Dogg Steingrimsdottir, Magnea Run Vignisdottir and Gunnar Stefansson. (2014). A MOBILE WEB FOR ENHANCING STATISTICS AND MATHEMATICS EDUCATION. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1406.5004](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1406.5004)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of data from 200+ students in a 2013 introductory calculus class at the University of Iceland, plotted in Fig. 2, showing the probability of passing goes from "negligible to almost 100%" as tutor-web grade rises from 0 to 10.

> "Fig. 2 is based on data from 200+ students and compares the performance on a final exam to the performance in the mobile tutor -web (black curve). A strong relationship is clear in the figure, where the probability of passing goes from negligible to almost 100% as the web-based performance is taken from 0 to a perfect grade of 10."
