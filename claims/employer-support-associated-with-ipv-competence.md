## Evidence

### Anyango Cartrine 2025

Anyango Cartrine, Häggström Gunfridsson Erling, Namatovu Fredinah. (2025). Service providers’ self-perceived competence in supporting women with disabilities subjected to intimate partner violence: insights from a Swedish survey. Global Health Action. https://doi.org/10.1080/16549716.2025.2476822

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 2 of the multiple linear regression in the Swedish cross-sectional survey, adjusting for gender and occupation. The coefficients are unstandardized β values on the competence score; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Full support was associated with a 20% increase in self-perceived competence (β = 0.20 CI: 0.16, 0.25), while partial support corresponded to a 13% increase (β = 0.13 CI: 0.09, 0.17) compared to no employer support."
