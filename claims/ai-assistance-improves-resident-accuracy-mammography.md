`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same MRMC reader study (500 cases, 17 readers), the results section reports significant accuracy gains for junior (3.1%, P=.005) and senior (4.3%, P=.002) residents with AI assistance; the same section states the consultant gain was not significant (P=.24).

> "Junior resident radiologists had improved accuracy from 75.8% to 78.9% (difference 3.1%, 95% CI of difference (1.3%, 4.9%), P=.005) and senior resident radiologists had improved accuracy from 76.1% to 80.4% (difference 4.3%, 95% CI of difference (1.8%, 6.9%), P=.002)"
