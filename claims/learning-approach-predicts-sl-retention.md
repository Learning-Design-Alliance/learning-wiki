## Evidence

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlational analysis (n = 46) of learning approach scores against TOSLS reassessment after 4 months; the abstract prints r = 0.298, P = 0.044 for deep learning total scores with retention.

> "Conversely, reassessed overall TOSLS scores were positively correlated with both deep learning total scores and deep strategy scores (P < 0.05; Fig. 5)."

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025 (2)

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlational analysis (n = 46) of learning approach scores against TOSLS reassessment after 4 months; the abstract prints r = −0.314, P = 0.034 for surface learning total scores with retention.

> "Correlative analyses showed a negative relationship between students' overall TOSLS scores at the start of the Fall 2023 semester and either their surface learning total scores or surface strategy scores (P < 0.05; Fig. 5)."
