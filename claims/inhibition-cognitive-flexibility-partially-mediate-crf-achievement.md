## Evidence

### Visier-Alfonso ME 2020

Visier-Alfonso ME, Sánchez-López M, Martínez-Vizcaíno V, Jiménez-López E, Redondo-Tébar A, Nieto-López M. (2020). Executive functions mediate the relationship between cardiorespiratory fitness and academic achievement in Spanish schoolchildren aged 8 to 11 years. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231246

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (PROCESS macro) with CRF as independent variable and language and mathematics grades as dependent variables found that "inhibition and cognitive flexibility mediated the association between CRF and academic achievement"; the direct effect remained significant, indicating partial mediation.

> "Inhibition and cognitive flexibility mediated the association between CRF and academic achievement, controlling for age and mother educational level."

### Visier-Alfonso ME 2020 (2)

Visier-Alfonso ME, Sánchez-López M, Martínez-Vizcaíno V, Jiménez-López E, Redondo-Tébar A, Nieto-López M. (2020). Executive functions mediate the relationship between cardiorespiratory fitness and academic achievement in Spanish schoolchildren aged 8 to 11 years. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231246

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

The authors report in the Discussion that the mediator effect of executive functions between CRF and academic achievement was "approximately 15 to 36%" of the total effect across the mediated models.

> "Our results indicated that the mediator effect of EF between CRF and academic achievement was approximately 15 to 36%."
