# In an adjusted regression model, earlier course year and no prior emergency-course participation were associated with larger theoretical learning gains

## Subclaims

`q2 i3` Multiple linear regression found an inversely proportional association between course year and theoretical learning (coefficient −0.542, SE 0.215, p=0.015) and an association with prior emergency-course participation (coefficient −0.903, SE 0.437, p=0.044), with R²=0.253. [→ Tobase L 2017](#tobase-l-2017)

`q2 i?` In a forward stepwise linear regression, learning was inversely associated with enrolled year (coefficient 0.542, p=0.015) and with prior emergency-class participation (coefficient 0.903, p=0.044, R2=0.253). [→ Tobase Lucia 2017](#tobase-lucia-2017)

## Evidence

### Tobase L 2017

Tobase L, Peres HHC, Gianotto-Oliveira R, Smith N, Polastri TF, Timerman S. (2017). The effects of an online basic life support course on undergraduate nursing students' learning. Int J Med Educ 8:309-313. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5985.cbce

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Adjusted multiple linear regression on the 62 completers treated theoretical learning as the dependent variable and course year and prior emergency-course completion as independent variables; the article prints r = −0.542 (p = 0.015) for course year and Table 2 prints R²= 0.253.

> "A significant association between learning and course year was found when the students participated in an emergency course before the online course. Moreover, inversely proportional associations and higher gains were seen for students in their early years (r = −0.542, p = 0.015) or those who did not previously participate in an emergency course"

### Tobase Lucia 2017

Tobase Lucia, Peres Heloisa Helena Ciqueto, Tomazini Edenir Aparecida Sartorelli, Teodoro Simone Valentim, Ramos Meire Bruna, Polastri Thatiane Facholi. (2017). Basic life support: evaluation of learning using simulation and immediate feedback devices. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.1957.2942

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary multiple linear regression (forward stepwise) on theoretical learning in the same quasi-experimental study. The article prints unstandardised coefficients "coefficient 0.542, PE 0.215, p=0.015" and "coefficient 0.903; PE 0.437; p=0.044, R2=0.253"; no standardised effect size is reported.

> "The association was shown to be significant, and inversely proportional to learning, in relation to the enrolled year in the nursing school course (coefficient 0.542, PE 0.215, p=0.015), and participation of the student in an emergency class before the online course (coefficient 0.903; PE 0.437; p=0.044, R2=0.253)."
