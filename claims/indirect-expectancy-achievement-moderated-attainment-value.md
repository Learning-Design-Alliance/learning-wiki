# Expectancy is indirectly related to achievement via behavioural engagement, and this indirect relation is stronger at low attainment value

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The indirect effect of T3 expectancy on T5 achievement via T4 engagement was significant (β = .13, 95% CIs [.02, .23]), as was the indirect effect of the interaction (β = -.16, 95% CIs [-.05, -.28]). [→ Putwain 2018](#putwain-2018)
`q3 i?` Conditional indirect slopes showed the expectancy-to-achievement indirect relation was substantial and significant at low attainment value but weaker and non-significant at high attainment value. [→ Putwain 2018 (2)](#putwain-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Putwain 2018

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., Pekrun, R., Becker, S., & Symes, W. (2018). Expectancy of Success, Attainment Value, Engagement, and Achievement: A Moderated Mediation Analysis. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/key-stage-2-tests-2016-mathematics-test-materials

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Indirect effects from the moderated mediational SEM. "T3 expectancy (β = .13, SE = .06, 95%CIs [.02, .23])" was indirectly related to achievement via engagement; attainment value alone was not.

> "T3 expectancy (β = .13, SE = .06, 95%CIs [.02, .23]), and the interaction between T3 expectancy and T3 attainment value (β = -.16, SE = .07, 95% CIs [-.05, -.28]), but not T3 attainment value (β = .02, SE = .07, 95%CIs [-.09, 12]), were indirectly related to T5 achievement mediated by T4 engagement"

### Putwain 2018 (2)

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., Pekrun, R., Becker, S., & Symes, W. (2018). Expectancy of Success, Attainment Value, Engagement, and Achievement: A Moderated Mediation Analysis. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/key-stage-2-tests-2016-mathematics-test-materials

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Conditional indirect simple slopes estimated at ±.5SD of attainment value (Table 5). "At low attainment value, the indirect relationship between T3 expectancy and T5 achievement was substantial and significant"; at high value it was not significant.

> "At low attainment value, the indirect relationship between T3 expectancy and T5 achievement was substantial and significant. At high attainment value, this relationship was weaker and no longer statistically significant."
