## Evidence

### Raper Jaron D. MD 2024

Raper Jaron D. MD, Khoury Charles A. MD, Marshall Anderson MD, Smola Robert MD, Pacheco Zachary MD, Morris Jason MD, Zhai Guihua PhD, Berger Stephanie MD, Kraemer Ryan MD, Bloom Andrew D. MD. (2024). Rapid Cycle Deliberate Practice Training for Simulated Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation in Resident Education. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11000545/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled curriculum evaluation with 10 teams (28 RCDP, 27 IS participants); times extracted from video of a subsequent immersive simulation. Pause duration was the only timed metric reaching significance: "6.20 vs 14.20 seconds (P = 0.01)". No effect size printed.

> "Although there were trends toward shorter mean times to completion of critical actions for RCDP vs IS groups, we observed only one category with a statistically significant difference: CPR mean pause duration in seconds was 6.20 vs 14.20 seconds (P = 0.01) in RCDP vs IS groups (Table 2)."
