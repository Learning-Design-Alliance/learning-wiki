## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In cross-lagged panel SEM, growth mindset before training predicted post-training WJ-III Math Fluency in the training group (β = 0.216, p = 0.025); this cross-lagged effect was not significant in controls (β = −0.219, p = 0.191). [→ Lang Chen 2022](#lang-chen-2022)

## Evidence

### Lang Chen 2022

Lang Chen, Hyesang Chang, Jeremy Rudoler, Eydis Arnardottir, Yuan Zhang, Carlo de los Angeles and Vinod Menon. (2022). Cognitive training enhances growth mindset in children through plasticity of cortico-striatal circuits. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00146-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-lagged panel analysis using SEM on longitudinal pre- and post-training data. Model-fit comparison showed the constrained model "fit worse than the baseline model, χ2(1) = 4.433, p = 0.035", indicating the cross-lagged effect differed between groups.

> "growth mindset prior to training was associated with post- training math skills in the training group, β = 0.216, p = 0.025, which suggests that higher levels of growth mindset lead to better math performance with training"
