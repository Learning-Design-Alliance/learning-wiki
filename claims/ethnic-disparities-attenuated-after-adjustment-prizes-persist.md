## Evidence

### Ferreira Tomas 2025

Ferreira Tomas, Collins Alexander M, Handscomb Arthur, French Benjamin, Bolton Emily, Fortescue Amelia, Plumb Ella, Feng Oliver, The FAST Collaborative. (2025). Socioeconomic and demographic predictors of extracurricular achievements among UK medical students (FAST study). BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-103062

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fully adjusted logistic regression in the FAST survey (8,035 participants with complete demographic data). The article reports attenuated overall ethnic ORs and no model-fit improvement (χ²=5.97, df=4, p=0.2), but persistent prize disparities (white vs Asian OR 1.27; white vs black OR 1.59, both p=0.001). No standardised effect size is printed.

> "Compared with white students, ORs for overall extracurricular attainment were 1.05 (95% CI 0.94 to 1.19, p=0.34) for Asian students and 1.27 (95% CI 1.02 to 1.56, p=0.03) for black students. Inclusion of ethnicity did not significantly improve overall model fit (χ²=5.97, df=4, p=0.2)"
