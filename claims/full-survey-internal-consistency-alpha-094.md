# The full 32-item computing career intentions survey shows very good internal consistency (alpha = 0.94) in a large-scale field test

## Evidence

### Kao 2023

Kao, Y., Murphy, D., Hubbard Cheuoua, A., Kannan, P., Tsan, J., Jennings, K. E., Smith, H., Emanuel, S., & Miller, E. R. (2023). Survey development for assessing student computing career intentions: Technical report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638631

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classical item analysis of the large-scale field test (552 retained responses after data cleaning) computed coefficient alpha for the full survey. The article reports that "the survey’s alpha estimate of 0.94 is very good", and that no item significantly contributed to or detracted from internal consistency.

> "Alpha values greater than or equal to 0.6 are generally considered acceptable, and values between 0.8 and 0.95 are considered very good. Using those guidelines, the survey’s alpha estimate of 0.94 is very good."
