## Evidence

### Sutapa Dey Tithi 2025

Sutapa Dey Tithi, Xiaoyi Tian, Min Chi and Tiffany Barnes. (2025). Investigating the Impact and Student Perceptions of Guided Parsons Problems for Learning Logic with Subgoals. CSEDM'25 Workshop. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.04712](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.04712)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Median-split moderation analysis using Mann-Whitney U tests with Bonferroni correction across pretest, level-end, and posttest phases (15 Control, 23 GPP per proficiency group).

> "GPPs significantly improved rule application accuracy for low prior knowledge students at both level-end test (Control = 51.6, GPP = 68.3, p < .001) and posttest (Control = 66.7, GPP = 79.1, p < .001) problems. High prior knowledge students showed comparable final rule accuracy across groups (Control = 78.5, GPP = 80.5, 𝑝 = 0.68)"

### Sutapa Dey Tithi 2025 (2)

Sutapa Dey Tithi, Xiaoyi Tian, Min Chi and Tiffany Barnes. (2025). Investigating the Impact and Student Perceptions of Guided Parsons Problems for Learning Logic with Subgoals. CSEDM'25 Workshop. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.04712](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.04712)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Step count and time analyses by proficiency group in the same moderation analysis; low prior knowledge students showed no significant time differences.

> "GPPs reduced redundant steps for high prior knowledge students during level-end test problems (Control = 11.5, GPP = 9.66,p = 0.02) but increased steps for low prior knowledge counterparts (Control = 9.71, GPP = 11.64,p = 0.03). High prior knowledge students in the GPP condition showed significantly reduced posttest times (p=0.01)."
