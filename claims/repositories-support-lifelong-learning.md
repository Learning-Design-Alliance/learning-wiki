## Evidence

### Maloney S 2013

Maloney S, Chamberlain M, Morrison S, Kotsanas G, Keating JL, Ilic D. (2013). Health Professional Learner Attitudes and Use of Digital Learning Resources. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2094

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the 3 focus groups identified a theme that "online repositories may be an effective tool to support lifelong learning and health care delivery", with students wanting continued access after graduation to revise as new graduates.

> "It would help if we still had access to it after we finish...because as a new grad, you’re still going to be wanting to revise."
