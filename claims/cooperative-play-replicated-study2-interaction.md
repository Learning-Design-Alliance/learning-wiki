# The augmentation effect on cooperative play replicated in Study 2, with a large playset-by-behavior interaction and no overlap in confidence intervals

## Evidence

### Yuill N 2014

Yuill N, Hinske S, Williams SE and Leith G (2014) How getting noticed helps getting on: successful attention capture doubles children's cooperative play. Front. Psychol. 5:418. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00418

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Study 2: 33 children (mean age 8 years 4 months) in 11 triads played two sessions with AKC or KC; the interaction effect was large (η2 = 0.79) and the article reports "cooperative play was more frequent in the AKC condition than in the KC, [ F(1, 32) = 71.48, p < 0.001]" with non-overlapping 95% CIs for cooperative and parallel play.
