Expert evaluation of the OTIL Model using a researcher-designed form with 8 five-point-scale items plus an open-ended question. Six items received the highest mean (x=5.00, SD=0.00); Items 2 and 3 received 4.67 (SD=0.577). The overall rating was "4.92 (SD=0.282)".
