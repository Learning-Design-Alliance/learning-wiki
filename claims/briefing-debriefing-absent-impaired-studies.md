`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review's audit of briefing and debriefing practices across the impaired-performance studies, citing prior evidence that effective pre-scenario briefing and post-scenario debriefing are associated with improved instructional and learning outcomes.

> "Of the eleven studies reporting impaired performance, only two (18.1 %) mentioned structured briefing and debriefing processes. Three (27.3 %) performed a debrief with no pre-scenario brief. Three performed a brief with no post-scenario debrief, and three performed neither brief nor debrief."
