Akinwumi 2017¶

Akinwumi, J. O. (2017). Effects of Gender and School Location on the Ekiti State Secondary Schools Students' Achievement in Reading Comprehension in English Language. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(5). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1133080

A 2 x 3 ANCOVA of inferring-word-meanings scores by gender and treatment with pre-test covariate, in the same 270-student quasi-experiment. The sex-by-group interaction was non-significant ("F = 0.463, p > 0.05").

"Table 2 showed that there was no significant differ ence in the achievement of male and female students in inferring word meanings between the experimental and control groups F = 0.463, p > 0.05 level of significance. The null hypothesis was not rejected."
