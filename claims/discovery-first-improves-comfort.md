## Evidence

### Xu J 2018

Xu J, Campisi P, Forte V, Carrillo B, Vescan A, Brydges R. (2018). Effectiveness of discovery learning using a mobile otoscopy simulator on knowledge acquisition and retention in medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-018-0317-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 2x2 mixed effects ANOVA on self-reported comfort (5-point Likert scale) found a "significant group x test interaction (F1,36 = 4.61, p = 0.04)". Post-hoc analysis showed the discovery-first group improved from 1.85 to 2.68 while the instruction-first group did not improve significantly.

> "An analysis of participants' self-reported comfort showed a significant group x test interaction (F1,36 = 4.61, p = 0.04), where only the discovery then instruction group's comfort improved significantly."
