q3 · i? · associational · r3

Comparative SEM analysis in the pooled ProFAN sample testing whether reciprocal effects were artifacts. The RI-LTVC and STARTS variants "failed to challenge the RI-CCLPM results", with core parameters "consistently nonsignificant" in both domains.

"Similarly, the RI-LTVC and STARTS variants, designed to rule out spuriousness due to latent confounding or residual error, also failed to challenge the RI-CCLPM results. In both domains, their core parameters—latent confounder loadings in the RI-LTVC and residual variances in the STARTS—were consistently nonsignificant."
