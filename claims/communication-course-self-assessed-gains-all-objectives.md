## Evidence

### von Lengerke Thomas 2011

von Lengerke Thomas, Kursch Angelika, Lange Karin, APG-Lehrteam MHH. (2011). The communication skills course for second year medical students at Hannover Medical School: An evaluation study based on students' self-assessments. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung 28(4), Doc54. [doi:10.3205/zma000766](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000766)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison within the 2009/10 course evaluation: 267 students completed T0, 180 completed T1 at the last session; paired t-tests were used. Gains were largest for history taking, where "The biggest differences were found for the item on history taking", followed by breaking bad news (+1.4), SDM (+1.2), self-awareness (+1.0), and approaching new patients (+0.7).

> "The T1-T0 comparisons show that significant progress was made regarding all seven learning objectives. The biggest differences were found for the item on history taking (“to know how”: mean difference=1.7, “to show how”: 1.8, p<.0001 for all tests)"
