### Ming Fai Pang 2011

Ming Fai Pang, Lo Mun Ling. (2011). Learning study: helping teachers to use theory, develop professionally, and produce new knowledge to be shared. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-011-9191-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental learning study in one Hong Kong primary school: two teachers taught two classes with a variation-theory lesson plan; two comparison teachers taught 'in the usual way as in previous years'. On the open-ended question 4, "57.14%" of the learning study group versus "29.03%" of the comparison group gave correct, properly explained answers.

> "The percentage of students in the learning study group (57.14%) who managed to provide a correct answer and gave a proper explanation using the concept of 'equal shares' was much higher than that for the comparison group (29.03%)."
