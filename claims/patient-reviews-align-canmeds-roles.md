## Evidence

### Pulkki KH 2025

Pulkki KH, Pira S, Young M, Scott GM, Nhan C, Fung K, Le Blanc G, Nguyen LHP. (2025). Considering the potential unintended consequences of RateMDs: an exploratory study in one specialty. Canadian Medical Education Journal 16(2). https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.77821

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Deductive qualitative descriptive coding of 878 written reviews linked to low (≤2/5) RateMDs ratings for Canadian otolaryngologists. The authors report that review "content of the narrative reviews associated with low physician ratings aligned well" with the CanMEDS 2015 framework's seven roles. No effect size is printed.

> "While patients likely have little knowledge of formal medical educational frameworks, we found that content of the narrative reviews associated with low physician ratings aligned well with the CanMEDS 2015 physician competency framework."
