Key Questions: How important are course grades as predictors of students' future success? 2025¶

Key Questions: How important are course grades as predictors of students' future success? (2025). UChicago Consortium / FutureEd. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

In this research-practice interview, Allensworth states that across the overall population of college-going students, "course grades are consistently the strongest indicators of students' general academic preparation," and that grades alone give the most comprehensive readiness information. No effect sizes are printed.

"If we look at the overall population of college-going students, students' course grades are consistently the strongest indicators of students' general academic preparation."
