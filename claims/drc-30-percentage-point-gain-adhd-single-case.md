## Evidence

### Pyle 2017

Pyle, K. K., & Fabiano, G. A. (2017). Daily Report Card Intervention and Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder: A Meta-Analysis of Single-Case Studies. Manuscript submitted to Exceptional Children October 2016; accepted March 2017. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED583708

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

A three-level HLM meta-analysis of single-case graphed data (1570 data points, 40 student cases, 14 studies). The partially conditional model estimated the phase effect: "participants gaining approximately 30 percentage points from baseline to treatment, β = 30.32, t(12) = 8.82, p < .001". No effect size for this model is in this quote; see discussion for Hedges g.

> "The partially conditional model examined the effect of the Level 1 Phase predictor (0 = baseline, 1 = treatment) on the data points. This model indicated that the average treatment effect across studies was significant, with participants gaining approximately 30 percentage points from baseline to treatment, β = 30.32, t(12) = 8.82, p < .001."
