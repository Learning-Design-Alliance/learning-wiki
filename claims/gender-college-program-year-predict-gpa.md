## Evidence

### Alhurishi Sultana A. 2021

Alhurishi Sultana A., Aljuraiban Ghadeer S., Alshaikh Fahdah A., Almutairi Mona M., Almutairi Khalid M. (2021). Predictors of students' academic achievements in allied health professions at King Saud University: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02525-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear regression in the cohort of 1634 graduates found "female gender (β = 0.479, 95 % CI = 0.422 to 0.516)", CAMS college, program type and earlier graduation year predicted academic achievement, all at p < 0.05.

> "The model confirmed that female gender (β = 0.479, 95 % CI = 0.422 to 0.516), CAMS college (β = 0.174, 95 % CI = 0.095 to 0.170), the type of program (β = 0.107, 95 % CI = 0.008 to 0.021), year of graduation; earlier ones (β=-0.087, 95 % CI=-0.077 to -0.030) could predict the Academic achievement."
