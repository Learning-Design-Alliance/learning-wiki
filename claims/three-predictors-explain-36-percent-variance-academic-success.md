## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The final stepwise regression model with self-regulation, engagement and self-efficacy explained 36.0% of variance in academic success, F(3.536) = 102.45, p < .001. [→ Martincová J 2025](#martincova-j-2025)
`q2 i?` Each added predictor significantly improved the model: engagement added 4.0% variance (ΔF = 35.25, p < .001) and self-efficacy added 2.0% (ΔF = 16.10, p < .001). [→ Martincová J 2025 (2)](#martincova-j-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Martincová J 2025

Martincová J, Staňková I, Snopek P, Zemánková L. (2025). Engagement, self-efficacy and self-regulation as predictors of academic success in non-medical healthcare education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08043-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression analysis (Model 3: self-regulation, engagement, self-efficacy) predicting study success in the 540-student survey. The article reports "R² = 0.36 (adjusted R² = 0.36)" with F(3.536) = 102.45, p <.001.

> "This final model achieves the highest explanatory power, with R² = 0.36 (adjusted R² = 0.36), indicating that it explains 36.0% of the variance in academic success, F(3.536) = 102.45, p <.001."

### Martincová J 2025 (2)

Martincová J, Staňková I, Snopek P, Zemánková L. (2025). Engagement, self-efficacy and self-regulation as predictors of academic success in non-medical healthcare education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08043-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-change statistics from the stepwise regression in Table 3. Model 2 added engagement (ΔF(1.537) = 35.25, p <.001, 4.0% improvement); Model 3 added self-efficacy with the quoted 2.0% improvement.

> "The addition of self-efficacy improves the model's explanatory power by 2.0% compared to Model 2, and the statistical test confirms the significance of this improvement, ΔF(1.536) = 16.10, p <.001"
