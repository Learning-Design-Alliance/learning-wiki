Lisa Barrow 2018 (2)¶

Lisa Barrow, Lauren Sartain, & Marisa de la Torre. (2018). GoCPS: A First Look at Applications and Offers. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/gocps-first-look-applications-and-offers

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of top-ranked programs by program type across subgroups (Figure 7). The paper reports IB program popularity concentrated among high-performing, higher-SES, and white or Asian/other applicants, while CTE programs were more popular among low-performing and African American students.

"IB programs are most popular among high -performing students, students living in tier 4 (higher-SES) neighborhoods, and white or Asian/other students."
