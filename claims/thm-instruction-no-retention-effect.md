## Evidence

### Lautrette Alexandre et al 2020

Lautrette Alexandre et al. (2020). Impact of take-home messages written into slide presentations delivered during lectures on the retention of messages and the residents' knowledge: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02092-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary outcome of the randomized controlled study: accordance between lecturers' THMs and residents' recollected THMs, assessed 1 month after the final lecture by blinded reviewers. Retention was "not different between the intervention and control groups" at 13% vs 17%.

> "The rate of THMs retention was not different between the intervention and control groups (13% (n = 238/1791), 95% CI 12–15 vs 17% (n = 326/1947), 95% CI 15–18, P = 0.40)"
