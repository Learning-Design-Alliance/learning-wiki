Classification of all 22 recorded MOCA simulation scenarios by dantrolene administration time and expert video review using criteria including timely diagnosis, cognitive aid use, and communication effectiveness. "Five of the 22 (23%)" teams were high-performing; all teams eventually administered dantrolene.

> "Five of the 22 (23%) anesthesia teams were determined to be high-performing based on administering dantrolene at < 10 min and/or performing other actions consistent with appropriate treatment such as calling the MH hotline and requesting a cognitive aid"
