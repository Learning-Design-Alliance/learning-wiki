Learning of Content Knowledge and Development of Scientific Reasoning Ability: A Cross Culture Comparison. https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.2061 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0807.2061](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0807.2061)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis of all three tests taken by one C1 class (Table 6): Lawson–FCI 0.12, Lawson–BEMA 0.17, FCI–BEMA 0.70; one U1 class gives Lawson–FCI 0.20. The article prints these correlation coefficients but no standardized effect size for this finding.

> "C1 (N=80) 0.12 0.17  0.70 
U1 (N=111) 0.20"
