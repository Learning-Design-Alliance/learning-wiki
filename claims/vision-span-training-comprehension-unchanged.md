## Evidence

### Kana'an et al. 2014

Basim Hamdan Ibrahim Kana'an, Salahud Din Abdul Rab & Ahlullah Siddiqui. (2014). The Effect of Expansion of Vision Span on Reading Speed: A Case Study of EFL Major Students at King Khalid University. English Language Teaching, 7(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p57

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on comprehension scores (out of 10) for both groups in Tables 3 and 4: control Z = -.677, p = .499; test Z = -.388, p = .698. The article reports "both groups maintained the same level of comprehension in both tests"; no effect size is printed.

> "Thus, the results indicate that both groups maintained the same level of comprehension in both tests, which affirms the researchers’ hypotheses 3 and 4, which state that the reading comprehension score of both the control and test groups in post-test is very similar to that in the pre-test."
