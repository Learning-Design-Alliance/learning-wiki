## Evidence

### Awadalla Suheir 2020

Awadalla Suheir, Davies E. Bethan, Glazebrook Cris. (2020). A longitudinal cohort study to explore the relationship between depression, anxiety and academic performance among Emirati university students. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-020-02854-z

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional group comparison at baseline in the UAE cohort: Possible MDD (n = 136) versus Non-MDD students on GPA, reporting the effect size "d = 0.3". The Possible MDD group was also less satisfied with studies (p = 0.015).

> "As hypothesized, students in the Possible MDD group (n = 136) had lower GPA scores compared to students below the cut-off (t = 2.98 p = 0.003, d = 0.3)."

### Awadalla Suheir 2020 (2)

Awadalla Suheir, Davies E. Bethan, Glazebrook Cris. (2020). A longitudinal cohort study to explore the relationship between depression, anxiety and academic performance among Emirati university students. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-020-02854-z

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Six-month follow-up comparison among the 134 follow-up responders: Possible MDD (n = 58) versus Non-MDD (n = 76) GPA, means "2.40 vs 2.68", effect size "d = 0.4". Follow-up responders had higher baseline symptom rates.

> "students in the Possible MDD group (n = 58) had significantly lower GPA scores at follow-up compared to students in the Non-MDD group (n = 76) (mean 2.40 vs 2.68, t = 2.13, p = 0.035, d = 0.4)"
