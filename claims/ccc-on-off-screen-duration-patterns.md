Duration data from video recordings of CCC studying sessions. Participants spent "almost twice as long with the abstract stimuli off screen" than on screen, and off-screen durations increased with the number of relations across phases.

> "Across phases, participants spent almost twice as long with the abstract stimuli off screen (M = 67.15 s, range: 56.14–76.09 s) than with the abstract stimuli on screen (M = 34.85 s, range: 17.36–51.42 s)."
