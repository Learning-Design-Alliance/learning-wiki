# In regression models, undergraduate GPA significantly predicted MLab exam performance while prior MLec completion, gender, and ethnicity did not

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Multiple linear regression controlling for GPA, ethnicity, gender, and MLec completion found GPA a significant predictor of MLab exam performance, with MLec completion not significant for VF, F, or NF questions. [→ Sato Brian K. 2017](#sato-brian-k-2017)

## Evidence

### Sato Brian K. 2017

Sato Brian K., Lee Amanda K., Alam Usman, Dang Jennifer V., Dacanay Samantha J., Morgado Pedro, Pirino Giorgia, Brunner Jo Ellen, Castillo Leanne A., Chan Valerie W., Sandholtz Judith H. (2017). What’s in a Prerequisite? A Mixed-Methods Approach to Identifying the Impact of a Prerequisite Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0260

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression models of MLab exam performance controlling for GPA, gender, ethnicity, and MLec completion. In the printed model, GPA carries a coefficient of 0.13 with p = 5.3e–06 on VF questions, while MLec completion is p = 0.76. Coefficients are unstandardized, so no effect size is reported.

> "While GPA was a significant predictor of student performance, the other variables (including MLec completion) did not impact performance on MLab VF, F, or NF exam questions"
