### Alsadi 2025

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing enrolment types (LPN-to-RN bridging vs new admission from secondary school) in the same national survey; Table 4 reports p < 0.001. No effect size is printed.

> "LPN to RN program students have statistically higher SDS scores (M = 3.91 ± 0.71) and EPQ scores (M = 4.08 ± 0.52) compared to new admission students' scores (M = 3.14 ± 0.89 and M = 3.23 ± 0.75, respectively)."
