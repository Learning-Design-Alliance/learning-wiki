## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The augmented reminding model's simulation reproduces the qualitative pattern that the inflection point at short retention intervals occurs at shorter lags than at longer ones. [→ Benjamin 2010](#benjamin-2010)

## Evidence

### Benjamin 2010

Benjamin, Aaron S., & Tullis, Jonathan. (2010). What makes distributed practice effective? http://www.psych.uiuc.edu/~asbenjam/SuperadditivityReferences.htm. [doi:10.1016/j.cogpsych.2010.05.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2010.05.004)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical simulation of the reminding model augmented with Wickelgren-style retarded forgetting (Equation 6, Figure 6). The simulation captures the qualitative effects in the Glenberg (1976) data, with the inflection point at short retention intervals considerably shorter than at longer ones.

> "All important qualitative effects evident in the Glenberg (1976) data are captured here; most importantly, the inflection point at short RI is considerably shorter than at longer RI."
