Evidence¶

SIPIYARUK Kawin 2023¶

SIPIYARUK Kawin, KAEWSIRIRAT Prachworrakit, SANTIWONG Peerapong. (2023). Technology-enhanced simulation-based learning in orthodontic education: A scoping review. Dental Press Journal of Orthodontics. https://doi.org/10.1590/2177-6709.28.3.e2321354.oar

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, from Sakowitz et al.'s randomized controlled trial comparing a virtual reality Oculus headset approach with a conventional 2D approach for orthognathic cases, that both improved understanding with no significant between-group difference; equivalence was not tested.

"The randomized controlled trial (with pre-, post-, and follow-up tests) demonstrated the improvements of understanding in diagnosis and treatment planning in both VR and conventional approaches, without significant difference between the two groups"
