## Evidence

### Cofini Vincenza 2022

Cofini Vincenza, Perilli Enrico, Moretti Annalucia, Bianchini Valeria, Perazzini Matteo, Muselli Mario, Lanzi Sabrina, Tobia Loreta, Fabiani Leila, Necozione Stefano. (2022). E-Learning Satisfaction, Stress, Quality of Life, and Coping: A Cross-Sectional Study in Italian University Students a Year after the COVID-19 Pandemic Began. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19138214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 471 Italian university students analysed with hierarchical multiple regression. The authors report that stress was "the only factor inversely associated with e-learning satisfaction" in the final model, with beta = −0.121 and p < 0.001.

> "higher stress scores were related to lower satisfaction scores, it was, in fact, the only factor inversely associated with e-learning satisfaction (beta= −0.121; p < 0.001)."
