Somya Swarnkar, Rittick Roy, Tejinder Kaur, and David Blair. (2023). On the effectiveness of the early introduction of modern physics in school curriculum: the case of the structure of atom versus wave-particle duality. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.16525](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.16525)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus-group and questionnaire responses from the same Indian undergraduate sample; "almost 30% of the students" gave the medium-to-travel response, which the authors tie to the curriculum's first introduction of waves as sound waves requiring a medium.

> "When asked “What do we mean when we say light has a wave na- ture?”, almost 30% of the students reported that “light needs a medium to travel”, along with a mention of the interference of light."
