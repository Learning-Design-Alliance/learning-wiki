## Evidence

### Hardie J.A. 2020

Hardie J.A., Brennan P.A. (2020). Are you surgically current? Lessons from aviation for returning to non-urgent surgery following COVID-19. The British Journal of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjoms.2020.06.012

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article describes, citing Ellis et al., human-factors conditions that reduce capability during an operating list and their magnification during COVID-19. Descriptive statement; no effect size printed.

> "Capability may reduce throughout an operation or operating list as HF such as fatigue, hunger or stress and anxiety occur. These factors may be magnified during the COVID-19 period, with additional challenges of increased fatigue, reduced communication and additional physical limitations whilst wearing PPE."

### Hardie J.A. 2020 (2)

Hardie J.A., Brennan P.A. (2020). Are you surgically current? Lessons from aviation for returning to non-urgent surgery following COVID-19. The British Journal of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjoms.2020.06.012

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article describes, citing Mentis et al. and Koca et al., theatre factors raising workload including distractions, unfamiliar equipment questions, body habitus and ergonomic instruments. Descriptive; no effect size printed.

> "Workload may be affected by other factors within the operating theatre, such as distraction caused by individuals entering the environment, questions about equipment unfamiliar to the scrub team, or other interruptions such as phone calls or bleeps."
