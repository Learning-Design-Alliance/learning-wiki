## Evidence

### Daly Shaun C. MD 2013

Daly Shaun C. MD, Hooper Elizabeth A. MD, Rinewalt Daniel MD, Myers Jonathan A. MD, Millikan Keith W. MD, Luu Minh MD. (2013). Resident Training in Single-Incision Compared with Traditional Cholecystectomy. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/108680813X13693422521791

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective case-control study of 80 elective resident-performed cholecystectomy patients (20 SILS, 60 traditional LC) at Rush University Medical Center over 15 months. The article reports "similar for the SILS cohort and the traditional LC cohort" with median times of 70.0 and 66.0 minutes, P = .54.

> "Median operative times were similar for the SILS cohort and the traditional LC cohort (70.0 minutes and 66.0 minutes, respectively; P = .54)."
