Evidence¶

Marín 2023¶

Marín, V. I., Buntins, K., Bedenlier, S., & Bond, M. (2023). Invisible borders in educational technology research? A comparative analysis. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10195-3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bibliometric keyword-cluster analysis of the 200 most frequent keywords per country, weighted equally by occurrence. The analysis found that "research foci deviate considerably" across Germany, Spain and the UK, with country-specific communities indicated.

"Research topics emerged as distinct for each country, covering a range of topics and even indicating specific communities within countries. Despite commonly shared terms, research foci deviate considerably."
