## Evidence

### Klingenberg 2024

Klingenberg, S., Bosse, R., Mayer, R. E., & Makransky, G. (2024). Does Embodiment in Virtual Reality Boost Learning Transfer? Testing an Immersion-Interactivity Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09956-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the pre-registered experiment, in-lesson performance was measured as error counts in laboratory safety, dexterity, and serial dilution accuracy during the simulation's test part. The article reports that "students’ performance during the virtual lesson predicted their real-life transfer test." No effect size or test statistic is printed in the available text.

> "Additionally, students’ performance during the virtual lesson predicted their real-life transfer test."
