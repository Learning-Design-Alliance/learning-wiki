Evidence¶

Snipes 2016¶

Snipes, J., & Tran, L. (2016). Early indicators and academic mindsets in the Clark County School District. San Francisco, CA: REL West @ WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED573499

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Logistic regression on CCSD survey data (adapted from the BEL-S survey) for 13,578 students with 8th grade mindset measures, predicting 9th grade on-track status (appendix C, table C2).

"8th grade students' self-reported beliefs regarding growth mindset, as well as their self-reports of academic behavior, were positively related to the probability that they were on track at the end of the 9th grade."

Snipes 2016 (2)¶

Snipes, J., & Tran, L. (2016). Early indicators and academic mindsets in the Clark County School District. San Francisco, CA: REL West @ WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED573499

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Same logistic regression analysis of 13,578 students. The null finding for performance avoidance is conditional on the other measures in the model; equivalence was not tested.

"student reports of performance avoidance, that is, the extent to which students reported hiding academic effort in order to avoid embarrassment or failure, were not significantly related to 9th grade on-track status once measures of growth mindset and academic behavior were accounted for"
