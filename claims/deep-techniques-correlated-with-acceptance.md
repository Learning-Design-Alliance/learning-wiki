# Use of deep learning techniques was moderately and significantly correlated with acceptance of the hybrid model

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Searching for meaning (rs= 0.42, P= 0.03), active learning (rs= 0.41, P= 0.03), and combined deep techniques (rs= 0.42, P= 0.03; rs= 0.49 with global HELAM-L, P= 0.01) were moderately correlated with acceptance. [→ Marco Lionel Di 2017](#marco-lionel-di-2017)

## Evidence

### Marco Lionel Di 2017

Marco Lionel Di, Venot Alain, Gillois Pierre. (2017). Does the acceptance of hybrid learning affect learning approaches in France?. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2017.14.24

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlations in the midwifery survey between HELAM-L acceptance and Wolfs' learning-strategy scale. The article reports "a moderately significant correlation between acceptance of the model and the strategy of searching for meaning"; the printed effect sizes are rs = 0.42 (P= 0.03) for searching for meaning and rs = 0.41 (P= 0.03) for active learning, both moderate by the article's own scale.

> "We found a moderately significant correlation between acceptance of the model and the strategy of searching for meaning (rs= 0.42, P= 0.03; significant [S]). We also found a moderately significant correlation between acceptance and active learning (rs= 0.41, P= 0.03; S)"

### Marco Lionel Di 2017 (2)

Marco Lionel Di, Venot Alain, Gillois Pierre. (2017). Does the acceptance of hybrid learning affect learning approaches in France?. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2017.14.24

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Composite deep-technique score (technique 1 plus technique 5) correlated with acceptance in the same survey; the printed effect sizes are rs = 0.42 (P= 0.03) with the model and rs = 0.49 (P= 0.01) with the global HELAM-L score, both printed as significant and moderate.

> "Using deep learning technique (technique 1+technique 5) was also moderately but significantly correlated with the acceptance of the model (rs= 0.42, P= 0.03; S) and to the global HELAM-L score (rs= 0.49, P= 0.01; S)"
