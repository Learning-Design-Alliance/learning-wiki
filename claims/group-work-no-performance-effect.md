## Evidence

### Koppenborg 2026

Koppenborg, Markus, Hüffmeier, Joachim, & Klingsieck, Katrin B. (2026). Is procrastination among students lower in group work? Evidence from a registered field experiment. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70069

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered OLS regression (Model 3) on task performance, measured by error-based compensation, comparing conjunctive group work to individual work. Non-significant overall fit and coefficient. Descriptively, means pointed in the hypothesized direction (5.08 vs. 6.13 correct entries).

> "Results revealed a non‐significant overall model fit and a non‐significant coefficient for conjunctive group work (see Model 3 in Table 6). Thus, H1b was not supported."

### Koppenborg 2026 (2)

Koppenborg, Markus, Hüffmeier, Joachim, & Klingsieck, Katrin B. (2026). Is procrastination among students lower in group work? Evidence from a registered field experiment. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70069

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered OLS regression (Model 7) comparing additive group work to individual work on task performance. Both overall model fit and condition coefficient were non-significant, answering RQ1b with no difference.

> "Results revealed a non‐significant overall model fit and a non‐significant coefficient for additive group work (see Model 7). In view of RQ1b, task performance does not differ between additive group work and individual work."
