Evidence¶

Nicholas David Bowman 2012¶

Nicholas David Bowman, Daniel Schultheiss, and Christina Schumann. (2012). 'I'm Attached, and I'm a Good Guy/Gal!': How Character Attachment Influences Pro- and Anti-Social Motivations to Play Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Games. CyberPsychology, Behavior, and Social Networking. https://doi.org/10.1089/cyber.2011.0311

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression of pro-social gaming motivations on demographics, game play factors, and four CA subdimensions in a survey of 450 MMORPG players. Sense of control was significant (beta = 0.113, p < 0.05 in Table 1), described as 'a significant positive predictor of pro-social gaming.'

"Of the four CA dimensions, a greater sense of control over one's character control was a significant positive predictor of pro-social gaming when controlling for demographics and game play factors"
