### Ansquer Raihei 2019

Ansquer Raihei, Mesnier Thomas, Farampour Farnam, Oriot Denis, Ghazali Daniel Aiham. (2019). Long-term retention assessment after simulation-based-training of pediatric procedural skills among adult emergency physicians: a multicenter observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1793-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison of 0-10 perception scales (Table 4); stress during simulation differed (p = 0.02) while self-confidence (p = 0.15), pre-simulation stress (p = 0.12), dissatisfaction (p = 0.47) and realism (p = 0.14) did not. A separate correlation found no stress-performance link (p = 0.13). Only p-values printed; no effect size.
