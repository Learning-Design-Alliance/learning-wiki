Nouri Haniyeh 2025¶

Nouri Haniyeh, Mahdavi Abdollah, Abedi Ali, Mohammadnia Alireza, Hamedan Mahnaz, Amanzadeh Masoud. (2025). Performance of large language models in medical licensing examinations: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.36

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup analysis by LLM type across the 36 included studies (GPT-4 n=30, GPT-3.5 n=23, Bing n=4, Claude n=3, Gemini/Bard n=3). The review reports GPT-4 "pooled accuracy was highest" at 81% versus 60% for GPT-3.5, P=0.001.

"By model type, pooled accuracy was highest for GPT-4 (81%), followed by Bing (79%), Claude (74%), Gemini/Bard (70%), and GPT-3.5 (60%), with statistically significant differences among models (P=0.001)."
