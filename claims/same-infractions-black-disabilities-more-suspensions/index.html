The administrative-data analysis compared suspension outcomes across subgroups within the same infraction types and found Black students and students with disabilities "significantly more likely to receive out-of-school suspensions than other subgroups". No effect size or test statistic is printed.

"Even when they were involved in the same types of infractions, Black students and students with disabilities were significantly more likely to receive out-of-school suspensions than other subgroups."
