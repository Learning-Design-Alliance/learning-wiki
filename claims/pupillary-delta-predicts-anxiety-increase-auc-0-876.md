## Evidence

### Martín-Rodríguez Francisco et al 2025

Martín-Rodríguez Francisco et al. (2025). Pupilometer efficacy in monitoring anxiety in undergraduate medical students during high-fidelity clinical simulation. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-95290-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise logistic regression in the 61-student randomized trial predicting binarized STAI increase from demographics, scenario, and vital-sign and pupillary deltas. Table 2 prints "Odds ratios" for delta of pupillary diameter of 7.049 (95% CI 1.750-49.161, p = 0.049).

> "Only the difference in the diameter of the pupillary delta diameter was significant (p = 0.049) (Table 2)."

### Martín-Rodríguez Francisco et al 2025 (2)

Martín-Rodríguez Francisco et al. (2025). Pupilometer efficacy in monitoring anxiety in undergraduate medical students during high-fidelity clinical simulation. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-95290-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model evaluation in the same trial, shown as an ROC curve (Fig. 2) and internally validated by bootstrapping with 1000 iterations. The article reports "an AUC of 0.876" with the stated confidence interval.

> "The predictive validity of the model, derived from multivariate logistic regression, presented an AUC of 0.876 (95% confidence interval: 0.706-1)"
