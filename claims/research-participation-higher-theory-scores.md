## Evidence

### de Mendonça Vitor Rosa Ramos 2013

de Mendonça Vitor Rosa Ramos, Alcântara Thiago, Andrade Nilvano, Andrade Bruno Bezerril, Barral-Netto Manoel, Boaventura Viviane. (2013). Analysis of theoretical knowledge and the practice of science among brazilian otorhinolaryngologists. Brazilian Journal of Otorhinolaryngology. https://doi.org/10.5935/1808-8694.20130087

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison using the Mann-Whitney test of mean right answers per individual between respondents with and without undergraduate research participation; the article prints means of 3.1 vs. 2.16 and p = 0.0103, with no effect size.

> "The mean percentage of right answers to the six theoretical questions given by individuals who had participated in research projects was greater than the percentage seen in subjects without exposure to undergraduate research (3.1 vs. 2.16, p = 0.0103)"
