## Evidence

### Sunil Kumar D 2026

Sunil Kumar D, Sucheta Dandekar, G. Hari Prakash. (2026). Dynamic CBME in action: rule-based digital case-based learning to evaluate antibiotic-stewardship reasoning in MBBS learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09810-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

SUS usability assessment in the 271-learner study. Approximately "39.1% (n = 106) rated usability as Good", 36.9% as Average, and 24.0% as Poor.

> "The mean SUS score was 62.4 +/- 18.7, corresponding to marginal-to-average usability and falling below the conventional SUS acceptability benchmark of 68."

### Sunil Kumar D 2026 (2)

Sunil Kumar D, Sucheta Dandekar, G. Hari Prakash. (2026). Dynamic CBME in action: rule-based digital case-based learning to evaluate antibiotic-stewardship reasoning in MBBS learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09810-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-item NASA-TLX workload assessment in the same study. The authors conclude the module "imposed an acceptable cognitive load without inducing excessive frustration".

> "The median mental effort score was 4.0 (IQR: 3.0-5.0) on a 7-point scale, indicating moderate cognitive engagement. The median frustration score was 3.0 (IQR: 2.0-4.0), suggesting relatively low frustration."
