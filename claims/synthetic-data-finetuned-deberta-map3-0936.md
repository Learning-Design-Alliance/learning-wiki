## Evidence

### Zhang 2025

Zhang, L., Zhang, M., Wang, W. L., & Luo, Y. (2025). Simulation as Reality? The Effectiveness of LLM-Generated Data in Open-ended Question Assessment. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.06371](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.06371)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Phase I performance evaluation on the 200-question Kaggle LLM Science Exam test set, a synthetic dataset. The RAG-plus-fine-tuning pipeline reached a "MAP@3 local test score of 0.936" versus 0.532 for DeBERTa alone; no effect size is printed.

> "Ultimately, we analyzed the incorrect answer options generated by the model, which achieved a MAP@3 local test score of 0.936. Notably, the MAP@3 score of 0.532 was obtained when using DeBERTa alone without the enhancement from our method."
