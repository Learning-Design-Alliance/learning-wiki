Evidence¶

Yongliang Wang & Yaobo Wang 2026¶

Yongliang Wang & Yaobo Wang. (2026). Explaining learning engagement in AI-assisted learning through teacher support and achievement goals: insights from goal-setting theory. Humanities and Social Sciences Communications 13:1215. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-026-07636-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Structural equation modeling of survey data from 758 Chinese university EFL learners tested nine hypothesized paths, of which six were significant. The supported paths include teacher support to mastery-approach (β = 0.434) and to performance-approach (β = 0.402), both p < 0.001; the avoidance-goal paths were unsupported, with "teacher support to mastery-avoidance" among the three non-significant paths.

"Only three paths were not supported: teacher support to mastery-avoidance, teacher support to performance-avoidance, and mastery-avoidance to engagement."
