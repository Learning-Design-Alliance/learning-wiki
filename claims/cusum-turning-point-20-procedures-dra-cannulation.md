Cheng X, Zhang J, Huang G, Zhou J, Xiong J. (2025). Evaluation of the learning curve for ultrasound-guided distal radial artery cannulation in novices using a cumulative sum analysis: A prospective observational study. Medicine 2025;104:46(e45962). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000045962

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Prospective observational analysis of 180 procedures by 3 novice residents; pooled mean CUSUM curves plotted for three time outcomes, and the text reports that the curves "revealed that the TP were 20 procedures".

> "The pooled mean CUSUM curve for the overall procedural time, identification time, and cannulation time revealed that the TP were 20 procedures (Fig. 2a, 2b, and 2c)."
