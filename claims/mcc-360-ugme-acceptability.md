## Evidence

### Bartman I 2025

Bartman I, St-Onge C, Roy M, Gingerich A, Katsoulas E, Chahine S, Gagnon N. (2025). Multi-source feedback in undergraduate medical education: a pilot study. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.79283

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student perceived-acceptability results from the post-evaluation survey (seven respondents) in the pilot's Acceptability section. All seven answered yes that the 'Report provided information that was not available elsewhere' and that the information was of value; the facilitated session format and time spent with the facilitator were each rated very acceptable by all seven. Criticisms centred on the data collection burden for overloaded supervisors. No effect size is printed.

> "They described receiving information in the report that was not available to them elsewhere and in an acceptable format. They were unanimous in stating the facilitated session was very acceptable."
