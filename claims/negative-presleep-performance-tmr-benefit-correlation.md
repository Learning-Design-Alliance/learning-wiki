## Evidence

### Göldi 2019

Göldi, M. & Rasch, B. (2019). Effects of targeted memory reactivation during sleep at home depend on sleep disturbances and habituation. npj Science of Learning 4:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0044-2

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis within the home experiment relating baseline recall before each night to the cued-minus-uncued benefit; night 3 showed "r = −0.31, P = 0.01", while nights 1 and 2 showed no association. Correlation, not causal manipulation.

> "We observed a negative correlation between pre-sleep performance levels and TMR beneﬁts during night 3 only ( r = −0.31, P = 0.01). No association was observed during night 1 ( r = 0.12; P > 0.30) and night 2 ( r = −0.20; P = 0.11)."
