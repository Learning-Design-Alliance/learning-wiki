# The NHT-ARIAS learning strategy produces significantly higher student motivation than the direct ARIAS learning strategy in senior high school biology

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taught with NHT-ARIAS showed significantly higher motivation after learning than students taught with direct ARIAS (ANCOVA significance 0.000 < 0.05). [→ Mustami 2018](#mustami-2018)

## Evidence

### Mustami 2018

Mustami, M. K., & Safitri, D. (2018). The Effects of Numbered Heads Together -Assurance Relevance Interest Assessment Satisfaction on Students’ Motivation. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 123-134. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.1139a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest nonequivalent control group study with 78 eleventh graders in Makassar, Indonesia, 39 per treatment, analyzed with ANCOVA in SPSS 20.0 after normality and homogeneity checks. The calculated significance value (0.000) was below the 0.05 alpha, so H0 was rejected; no effect size is printed.

> "This finding shows that there is a significance different found in students’ motivation between the students who learned using the direct ARIAS learning strategy and those who learned using the NHT -ARIAS learning strategy."
