A document scan and state engagement conducted for the brief identified the named states with official pilots. The brief also reports Tennessee's pilot "has grown to almost 800 educators from more than 25 school districts," with 60 of 100 micro-credentials earned in the past year from a STEM group.

"At least 10 state education agencies—Arkansas, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Louisiana, Rhode Island, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas, and Washington—have launched official micro-credential pilots."
