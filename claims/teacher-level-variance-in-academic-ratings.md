## Evidence

### Baker Courtney N. 2015

Baker Courtney N., Tichovolsky Marianne H., Kupersmidt Janis B., Voegler-Lee Mary Ellen, Arnold David H. (2015). Teacher (Mis)Perceptions of Preschoolers' Academic Skills: Predictors and Associations With Longitudinal Outcomes. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000008

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null-model ICCs from HLM (ICClang and pre-lit = .54 and ICCmath = .88), indicating "individual teachers tend to rate preschoolers within their classrooms very similarly." Replicates Kilday et al.'s approximately 40% estimate with higher values.

> "Our findings report even higher values, with 54% of the variability in teachers' ratings of language and pre-literacy and 88% of the variability in math associated with teacher rather than child characteristics."
