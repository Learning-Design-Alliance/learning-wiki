### Afef Saihi and Vian Ahmed 2026

Afef Saihi and Vian Ahmed. (2026). Uncovering adoption personas for generative AI in higher education: a clustering-based segmentation approach. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00583-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Numerical clustering analysis of the standardized 13-variable dataset. The silhouette coefficient was computed for k from 2 to 10; the highest score occurred at k = 2, but the authors report that "k = 4 emerges as a strong contender, displaying a visible 'elbow'" and selected it for interpretability.

> "This combination of dendrogram and silhouette-based evidence supports k = 4 as a balanced and theoretically justifiable solution. Accordingly, k = 4 was selected for the final clustering analysis."
