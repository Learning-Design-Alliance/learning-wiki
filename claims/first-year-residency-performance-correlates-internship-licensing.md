Pearson correlations among eight variables for the 160 physicians completing both stages (Fig. 2B; Fig. 3 scatterplots by department). The article reports "medical internship performance (r = 0.179, P < 0.05)" and r = 0.176 for the licensing written score.

> "performance in the first year of residency was significantly associated with the scores of both medical internship performance (r = 0.179, P < 0.05) and the written part of the medical licensing examination (r = 0.176, P < 0.05) (Figs. 2B, 3B, and C) but not with the medical school GPA or residency selection procedures"
