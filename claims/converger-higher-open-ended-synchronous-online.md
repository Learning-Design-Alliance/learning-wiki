### Goodridge 2017

Goodridge, W. H., Lawanto, O., & Santoso, H. B. (2017). A Learning Style Comparison between Synchronous Online and Face-to-Face Engineering Graphics Instruction. International Education Studies, 10(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n2p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same Mann-Whitney analysis of the six-section engineering graphics quasi-experiment, synchronous online Convergers scored "significantly higher score on their final grade for the open ended activity" than face-to-face Convergers, Z = -3.778; p < .01. No effect size printed.
