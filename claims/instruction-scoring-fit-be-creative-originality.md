# Be-creative instruction combined with originality scoring yields the strongest intelligence–DT correlation

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The instruction × scoring interaction was significant, with the highest correlation for be-original/creative instructions paired with originality/creative-quality scoring. [→ Gerwig Anne 2021](#gerwig-anne-2021)

## Evidence

### Gerwig Anne 2021

Gerwig Anne, Miroshnik Kirill, Forthmann Boris, Benedek Mathias, Karwowski Maciej, Holling Heinz. (2021). The Relationship between Intelligence and Divergent Thinking—A Meta-Analytic Update. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence9020023

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Interaction model restricted to be-fluent and be-original/creative instructions crossed with fluency and originality scorings; the article prints only the fit statistic χ2(1) = 6.70, p = .010, with no effect size for the interaction. The be-creative/originality combination exceeded be-fluent/fluency (z = −3.16, p = .008) and be-creative/fluency (z = −4.88, p < .001); under be-fluent instructions, fluency and originality correlations did not differ (z = −0.94, p = .697).

> "a model including the interaction between instruction and scoring showed a significantly better fit to the data, χ2(1) = 6.70, p = .010"
