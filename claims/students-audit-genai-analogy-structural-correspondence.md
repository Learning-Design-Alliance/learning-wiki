## Evidence

### structural-audit

Seth Bernstein and Naaz Sibia. 2026. Flawed but Memorable: Student Critical Reception of Interest-Personalized GenAI Analogies in Computing Education. Koli Calling '26. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06095 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.06095](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.06095)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Reflexive thematic analysis of think-aloud and interview data from ten CS2-completing participants evaluating personalized and generic explanations. The article reports that a recurring failure was "a structural mismatch between the analogy and the real-world domain it borrowed from"; e.g., P6 corrected a One Piece linked-list analogy as implying a circular list.

> "A recurring failure participants identified was a structural mismatch between the analogy and the real-world domain it borrowed from, echoing Halasz and Moran's [25] concern that analogical models are only ever partially structure-preserving."
