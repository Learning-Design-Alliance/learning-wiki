### Bilas Paul 2024

Bilas Paul, Shantanu Chakraborty, and Ganga Sharma. (2024). Breaking Barriers: Investigating Gender Dynamics in Introductory Physics Lab Classes. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.15049](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.15049)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of time spent on lab tasks across three institutions, with instructors recording student involvement at ~20-minute intervals and independent samples t-tests comparing average time distributions by gender. The article reports "significant gender differences (t = 2.637, p= 0.007)" only at Fairmont State.

> "While SUNY Farmingdale State College (t = 0.142, p= 0.444) and Valdosta State University (t = −0.475, p= 0.319) showed no significant differences between male and female students' participation, Fairmont State University demonstrated significant gender differences (t = 2.637, p= 0.007)"
