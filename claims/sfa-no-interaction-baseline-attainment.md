## Evidence

### Miller et al. 2017

Sarah Miller, Andy Biggart, Seaneen Sloan, Liam O'Hare. (2017). Success for All: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581417

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-specified subgroup analysis of the cluster RCT comparing pupils with age-standardised BPVS scores below 85 (n=251) with those at 85 and above (n=1468), using interaction terms in the main multilevel models.

> "The resulting models did not provide any evidence of an interaction in relation to any of the three outcomes, suggesting that the SfA programme does not result in different outcomes for children with different levels of ability at baseline."
