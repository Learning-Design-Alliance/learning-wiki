## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Tetzlaff and Peters 2025

Leonard Tetzlaff, Bianca Simonsmeier, Tabea Peters, Garvin Brod. (2025). A cornerstone of adaptivity – A meta-analysis of the expertise reversal effect. Learning and Instruction 98 (2025) 102142. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102142

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of the difference of differences across 60 studies, following Kalyuga (2007) and Mayer (2001); the pooled interaction effect was "d = 0.971, 95 % CI [0.631, 1.312], k = 88, j = 60", larger than zero with a CI excluding zero.

> "By subtracting the effect size for high prior knowledge learners from the one for low prior knowledge learners at the study level we can appraise the Expertise Reversal Effect in general as a strong effect (d = 0.971, 95 % CI [0.631, 1.312], k = 88, j = 60)."
