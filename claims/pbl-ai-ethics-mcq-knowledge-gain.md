### Chartash David 2026

Evaluation of a Problem-Based Learning Program’s Effect on Artificial Intelligence Ethics Among Japanese Medical Students: Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/84535

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pretest–posttest study of 118 fourth-year medical students; a linear mixed-effects model on 24-item MCQ scores at three time points showed the stepwise increase, with the posttest score "significantly exceeded the baseline score by 1.42 points (β=1.42; P<.001)".

> "Furthermore, the end-of-program (posttest) score significantly exceeded the baseline score by 1.42 points (β=1.42; P<.001)."
