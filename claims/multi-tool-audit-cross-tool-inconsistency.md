## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The same known-provenance manuscript, submitted to five commercial detectors on the same day, received classifications ranging from Winston AI's 0% human to GPTZero's categorical label Human Generated, with three AI-leaning and two human-leaning outcomes. The author's documented exploratory audit of one manuscript (4,613–4,727 words) on 30 August 2026, with timestamped, hash-verifiable artefacts in Supplementary Data S1, found "substantial cross-tool inconsistency". The audit was not blinded and used single runs; it supports existence claims, not prevalence estimates.

> "Winston AI reported 0% human. Copyleaks and Originality.ai returned approximately 80–81% AI. GPTZero returned 42% AI while assigning the categorical label "Human Generated". Tool E reported 34% AI / 66% human."
