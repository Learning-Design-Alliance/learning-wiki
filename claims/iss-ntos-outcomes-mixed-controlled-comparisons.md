## Evidence

### Evans Kerry 2022

Evans Kerry, Woodruff Jenny, Cowley Alison, Bramley Louise, Miles Giulia, Ross Alastair, Cooper Joanne, Baxendale Bryn. (2022). GENESISS 2—Generating Standards for In-Situ Simulation project: a systematic mapping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03401-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review category 4: twenty-seven studies (samples 20 to 750 participants) with outcome measures including self-reported confidence, performance, communication, anxiety and knowledge. The review reports "Significant improvements in confidence scores were reported for single session" designs and similar improvements across other measures.

> "Significant improvements in confidence scores were reported for single session [96, 98, 111, 114], three session [112, 117] or regular departmental training [2]."

### Evans Kerry 2022 (2)

Evans Kerry, Woodruff Jenny, Cowley Alison, Bramley Louise, Miles Giulia, Ross Alastair, Cooper Joanne, Baxendale Bryn. (2022). GENESISS 2—Generating Standards for In-Situ Simulation project: a systematic mapping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03401-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports four controlled comparisons with null results: lecture-based versus ISS training for situational awareness, ISS versus simulation-centre obstetric emergency training, ISS versus no interprofessional education, and ISS versus mandatory resuscitation training. Equivalence was not tested; no effect sizes were printed.

> "Four studies which compared ISS groups with control or comparison groups reported no statistical significant difference in outcomes"
