# Observer, student, and teacher perspectives on teacher agency overlap largely, but observed and teacher-self-reported communion are not significantly associated

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was a large, statistically significant overlap between observer, student, and teacher self-reported levels of teacher agency. [→ Donker 2021](#donker-2021)
`q3 i?` There was no significant association between observed and teacher self-reported communion, against what a shared expert role would imply. [→ Donker 2021](#donker-2021)

## Evidence

### Donker 2021

Donker, M. H., van Vemde, L., Hessen, D. J., van Gog, T., & Mainhard, T. (2021). Observational, student, and teacher perspectives on interpersonal teacher behavior: Shared and unique associations with teacher and student emotions. Learning and Instruction, vol. 73, 101414. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101414

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analyses at the teacher/class level in Mplus, accounting for students nested in teachers, for 80 teachers. The study found "no significant association between the observed and teachers’ self-reported levels of communion", while agency showed large overlap across the three perspectives.

> "Interestingly, and against what a shared expert role of teacher and observer would imply, there was no significant association between the observed and teachers’ self-reported levels of communion, suggesting that teachers perceived their communion differently from what was observed."
