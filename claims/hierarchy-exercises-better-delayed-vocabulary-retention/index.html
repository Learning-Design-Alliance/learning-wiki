Evidence¶

Lin 2013¶

Lin, Ching-Ying & Hsu, Wei Shu. (2013). Effects of Hierarchy Vocabulary Exercises on English Vocabulary Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 6(9). doi:10.5539/elt.v6n9p110

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Delayed vocabulary post-test administered one week after the immediate post-test, with no advance notice and no additional practice. Independent t-test showed the hierarchy group retained significantly more words than the copying group.

"The mean of the experimental group (M = 83.04, SD = 23.71) was higher than that of the control group (M= 67.86, SD = 28.80). The results revealed that there was a significant difference between the two groups ((t (53.229) = 2.159, p = .035< .05)."

Lin 2013 (2)¶

Lin, Ching-Ying & Hsu, Wei Shu. (2013). Effects of Hierarchy Vocabulary Exercises on English Vocabulary Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 6(9). doi:10.5539/elt.v6n9p110

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive comparison of immediate and delayed means in both groups. The article reports the between-test difference "in the experimental group (5.77) was less than that of the control group (8.35)", attributing decline to the one-week time lapse without rehearsal.

"In the experimental group, the mean scores decreased from 88.81 to 83.04 and in the control group the mean scores decreased from 76.21 to 67.86."
