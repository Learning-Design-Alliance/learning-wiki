## Evidence

### Rose E 2018

Rose E, Jhun P, Baluzy M, Hauck A, Huang J, Wagner J, Kearl YL, Behar S, Claudius I. (2018). Flipping the Classroom in Medical Student Education: Does Priming Work? Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2017.8.35162

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous end-of-rotation survey collected over nine months (97 surveys); students reported median engagement of 5.3 with traditional lecture and 5.0 with online videos on a seven-point scale.

> "When asked which format they preferred, 34 students (35% who completed the survey) preferred traditional lecture and 60 students (62% surveyed) preferred the combined format of pre-class online videos with in-class case discussion."
