`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Skills assessment using five clinical case scenarios across 895 students, with 4,195 therapy choices and 5,429 prescriptions scored against European guidelines. The study reports "46.2% (range 15–76)" inappropriate therapies and "54.7% (range 34–65)" of prescriptions with errors.

> "Overall, 46.2% (range 15–76) of the therapies were inappropriate and 54.7% (range 34–65) of the prescriptions contained one or more prescribing errors. The most common errors were “less effective drug choice” (19.6%), “incomplete/incorrect drug prescription” (18.0%), and “overdosing” (17.9%)."
