# Elementary teaching practices during a standardized lesson are more often teacher-directed than child-centered, with no grade differences in frequency

## Evidence

### Mariëtte H. van Loon 2021

Mariëtte H. van Loon, et al. (2021). Connecting teachers' classroom instructions with children's metacognition and learning in elementary school. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09248-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational coding of the same 21 teachers' transcripts. A Wilcoxon signed rank test showed teaching "was most often teacher-directed" (z = −2.87, p = .004). Kolmogorov-Smirnov tests found no grade differences (e.g., cognitive strategies D = .567, p = .905).

> "Although teachers used a combination of teacher-directed and child-centered instructions, a Wilcoxon signed rank test showed that teaching was most often teacher-directed, z = −2.87, p = .004."
