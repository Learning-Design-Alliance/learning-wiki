### Zhang Guoyong 2026

Zhang Guoyong, Sun Xianghong, Yang Dan, Shao Qinwen. (2026). Nursing students’ experiences in virtual simulation teaching: a meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1863553

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Synthesized challenges finding from the meta-synthesis; students also reported complex operational procedures requiring extensive practice, English-language barriers (one scenario taking more than eight hours for a non-native speaker), cultural discrepancies in cases, and limited scenario diversity and graphics quality.

> "The most frequently mentioned drawbacks of VS systems reported by students revolve around its perceived lack of “realism.”"

### Zhang Guoyong 2026 (2)

Zhang Guoyong, Sun Xianghong, Yang Dan, Shao Qinwen. (2026). Nursing students’ experiences in virtual simulation teaching: a meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1863553

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesized student-challenges finding; one student described sweating and feeling sick during VR use. The review notes physical side effects are associated with usage duration, visual design, and operating behaviors, citing prior work.

> "Some students experienced adverse physical reactions after participating in VR teaching projects, such as nausea, dizziness, and vision problems, along with a risk of injury."
