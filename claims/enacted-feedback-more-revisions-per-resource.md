Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Jason M. Lodge, Naomi Winstone, Dragan Gašević, Hassan Khosravi. (2026). Making AI-Generated Feedback Matter: A Large-Scale Study of Feedback Workflows and Student Enactment. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11625

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Negative-binomial GLMM on revision counts capped at the empirical 99th percentile (maximum 8 revisions). The Enacted/Directed contrast showed IRR = 2.52, a difference of 0.363 revisions per resource, p < .001. Observed medians were 0 in all three conditions.

> "Mean revision counts after capping were highest inEnacted Feedback(M= 0.87 , SD= 1.82 ), followed byDirected Feedback( M= 0.46 , SD= 1.26 ), andSelf-Directed Feedback(M= 0.01 ,SD= 0.24 )."
