## Evidence

### Alvarez-Peregrina Cristina 2020

Alvarez-Peregrina Cristina, Sánchez-Tena Miguel Ángel, Andreu-Vázquez Cristina, Villa-Collar Cesar. (2020). Visual Health and Academic Performance in School-Aged Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17072346

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional optometric screening of 10,218 children aged 6–12 across Spain, classified by family-reported academic performance (1006 bad, 9212 good). The screening found "differences between both cohorts for all of the values measured (p < 0.001)" across visual acuity and near point of convergence.

> "Table 4 shows values of monocular visual acuity in far vision, binocular visual acuity in near vision and near point of convergence. It shows data from the total of the subjects, and of both cohorts. There are differences between both cohorts for all of the values measured (p < 0.001)."
