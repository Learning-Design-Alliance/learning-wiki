# Lockdown measures are positively associated with expected political participation but show null or negative associations with expected electoral participation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Greater lockdown stringency and longer school closures were associated with increases (or smaller decreases) in expected political participation. [→ Kennedy 2026](#kennedy-2026)
`q2 i?` No relationship was found between average stringency and changes in expected electoral participation, and a negative association was found with school closure duration. [→ Kennedy 2026 (2)](#kennedy-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Kennedy 2026

Kennedy, A. I., Mejía-Rodríguez, A. M., Strietholt, R. (2026). Civic Education Outcomes After Emergency Lockdowns: International Evidence from the COVID-19 Pandemic. Preprint submitted to Learning and Instruction. https://github.com/OxCGRT/covid-policy-dataset

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Fixed-effects regression of ICCS 2016/2022 changes on OxCGRT measures. The article reports significant positive relationships between both COVID-19 policy measures and changes in expected political participation.

> "Specifically, we find that greater lockdown stringency and longer school closures were associated with increases (or smaller decreases) in expected political participation."

### Kennedy 2026 (2)

Kennedy, A. I., Mejía-Rodríguez, A. M., Strietholt, R. (2026). Civic Education Outcomes After Emergency Lockdowns: International Evidence from the COVID-19 Pandemic. Preprint submitted to Learning and Instruction. https://github.com/OxCGRT/covid-policy-dataset

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same regression analysis. The article reports no significant relationship for stringency with expected electoral participation (estimated at 0.01SD, not statistically significant) and a negative association for closure duration.

> "we do not find a relationship between average stringency of lockdown measures and changes in expected electoral participation. We even find a negative association between school closure duration and changes in the electoral participation measure."
