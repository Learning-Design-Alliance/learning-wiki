## Subclaims
`q2 i3` MCQ scores correlated significantly with human-graded open responses (r(86) = 0.421) and with LLM-scored open responses (GPT-4o r(86) = 0.406; LearnLM r(86) = 0.477), indicating LLM scoring has predictive validity without human grading. [→ Danielle R. Thomas 2025](#danielle-r-thomas-2025)

## Evidence

### Danielle R. Thomas 2025

Danielle R. Thomas, Conrad Borchers, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2025). Beyond Agreement: Rethinking Ground Truth in Educational AI. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.00143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.00143)

`q2 · i3` · `theoretical · r2`

The paper reports, citing Thomas et al. (2025a), correlations of participants' MCQ scores with LLM scores (GPT-4o, Gemini 1.5 Pro, LearnLM) on open responses in scenario-based tutor training; the human-graded correlation is "r(86) = 0.421, p < .001" (r = 0.421), with LearnLM at r(86) = 0.477.

> "The analysis revealed a significant, positive correlation between MCQ scores and human-graded open-response scores, r(86) = 0.421, p < .001."
