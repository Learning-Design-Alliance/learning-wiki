## Evidence

### Lee 2024

Lee, J., Hicke, Y., Yu, R., Brooks, C., & Kizilcec, R. F. (2024). The Life Cycle of Large Language Models: A Review of Biases in Education. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.11203](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.11203)

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's discussion of harms arising from human-computer interaction at the deployment step. It states that if students over-rely on an intelligent tutoring system that appears adept at generating empathetic and expert responses, they may place undue trust in potentially unethical, unveriﬁed, or hallucinated information.

> "As a result, users might anthropomorphize these systems, viewing them as human-like, which can lead to overreliance or unsafe use."
