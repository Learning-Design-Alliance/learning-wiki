### M. Madden 2007

M. Madden, P.W.H. Chung, C.W. Dawson. (2007). The Effect of a Computer-Based Cartooning Tool On Children's Cartoons and Written Stories. Loughborough University Institutional Repository. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/2.5/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental classroom study with experimental and control groups in two primary schools; paired and unpaired Student's t-tests at the 95% level were applied to CHECK TEXT indicators and teacher codes for first, second and improvement comparisons. The article reports no consistent intervention effect, with improvement effects in one experimental group not repeating in the other.

> "It is suggested that the main conclusion to be drawn from the above results is that no one intervention produced clearly diﬀerent results from the others, in the sense of the CHECK TEXT indicators"
