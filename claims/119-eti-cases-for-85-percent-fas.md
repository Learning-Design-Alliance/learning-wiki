### Lee Gun Tak 2022

Lee Gun Tak, Park Jong Eun, Woo Sook-young, Shin Tae Gun, Jeong Daun, Kim Taerim, Lee Se Uk, Yoon Hee, Hwang Sung Yeon. (2022). Defining the learning curve for endotracheal intubation in the emergency department. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-19337-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prediction-equation estimates derived from the GLMM coefficients relating cumulative ETI cases to predicted FAS probability, plotted in Fig. 2. The model-based inversion showed "at least 119 ETI cases were needed to achieve an FAS probability of ≥ 85%". No standardized effect size is printed for these thresholds.

> "At least 119 ETI cases were needed to achieve an FAS probability of ≥ 85%. In addition, at least 66 and 189 cumulative ETI cases were required to achieve FAS probabilities of 80% and 90%, respectively."
