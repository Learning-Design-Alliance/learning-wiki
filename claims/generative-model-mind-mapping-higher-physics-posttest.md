Jurnal Pendidikan Tambusai, Volume 8 Nomor 3. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.07884](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.07884)

Quasi-experimental post-test only study with an experimental class of 30 students and a control class of 27 students at SMAN 1 Painan; the experimental mean was "79,20" versus "76,15" for the control on a 25-item objective post-test.

> "Berdasarkan analisis data  hasil belajar pengetahuan dari post -test didapatkan angka rata -rata dari grup eksperimen  79,20, dan nilai rata -rata dari grup kontrol 76,15."
