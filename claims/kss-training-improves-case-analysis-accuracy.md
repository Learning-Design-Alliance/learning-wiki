### Hu Yaojia 2022

Hu Yaojia, Zheng Bingya, Zhu Lihui, Tang Shuo, Lu Qi, Song Qingqing, Zhang Na, Zhong Yan. (2022). The effectiveness of emergency knowledge training of pediatric medical workers based on the knowledge, skills, simulation model: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03267-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results-section comparison of pre-post accuracy on the case-analysis items in the quasi-experimental study; Table 5 shows each of the eight items improving significantly, e.g. Question14-(6) from 33.10% to 85.40% correct (X2 = 950.54, P = 0.00).

> "The number of participants answering correctly the eight questions based on a case analysis of emergency treatment knowledge increased significantly after the training (P = 0.00)."

### Hu Yaojia 2022 (2)

Hu Yaojia, Zheng Bingya, Zhu Lihui, Tang Shuo, Lu Qi, Song Qingqing, Zhang Na, Zhong Yan. (2022). The effectiveness of emergency knowledge training of pediatric medical workers based on the knowledge, skills, simulation model: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03267-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Discussion-section statement of the defibrillation accuracy contrast drawn from the same study's pre-post data, reporting the rate rising from 52.6% to 95.4% after the four-step first aid training approach.

> "Of significant interest is that only 52.6% of participants knew how to properly defibrillate after a cardiac arrest before the training, and after the training, the accuracy rate increased to 95.4%."
