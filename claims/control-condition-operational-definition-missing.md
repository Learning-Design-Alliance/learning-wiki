## Evidence

### Amedee Marchand Martella 2023

Amedee Marchand Martella, Ronald C. Martella, Jane K. Yatcilla, Alexandra Newson, Eric N. Shannon, Charissa Voorhis. (2023). How Rigorous is Active Learning Research in STEM Education? An Examination of Key Internal Validity Controls in Intervention Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09826-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Frequency coding of the 176 Freeman et al. (2014) articles for operational definitions found control-condition features fully defined in 19.9%, partially in 32.4%, and "control features were not operationally defined in 47.7% of the articles." Experimental variables were fully defined in 50.0%.

> "For the control condition, all features of the control variable(s) were operationally defined in 19.9% of the articles, and these features were partially operationally defined in 32.4% of the articles. Control features were not operationally defined in 47.7% of the articles."
