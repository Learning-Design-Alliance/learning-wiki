## Evidence

### Maciej Jakubowski 2025

Maciej Jakubowski, Tomasz Gajderowicz & Harry Anthony Patrinos. (2025). COVID-19, school closures, and student learning outcomes. New global evidence from PISA. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00297-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Unconditional quantile (RIF) regressions fitted at different PISA score percentiles (Supplementary Fig. 1). For the average student the loss rises "from around 14 points in the countries with the shortest closures but increases to 24 in countries with the most prolonged closures".

> "For countries with more prolonged closures, the declines are more significant among the lowest-achieving students, while in all countries, the best-achieving students lost around 8 -9 points."
