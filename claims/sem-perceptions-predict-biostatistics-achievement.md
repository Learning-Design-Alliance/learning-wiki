## Evidence

### Li Chen 2018

Li Chen, Wang Ling, Zhang Yuhai, Li Chanjuan, Xu Yongyong, Shang Lei, Xia Jielai. (2018). Assessment of a block curriculum design on medical postgraduates’ perception towards biostatistics: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1232-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Confirmatory factor analysis within SEM (Mplus 7, maximum likelihood) on the block cohort linked five latent perception constructs to final exam scores; fit indices were CFI 0.968 and RMSEA 0.055, and "the ability resulted in only 9.4% of the variance in the exam score".

> "Then, the course value positively affected the self-assessment ability level of statistical knowledge (β = 0.404). Finally, the latent ability variable positively affected the achievement score (β = 0.094)."
