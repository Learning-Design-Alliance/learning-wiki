## Evidence

### Van Kelsey 2025

Van Kelsey, Tasawar Sana, Brendel Elaina B. K., Law Camille, Mahajan Anisha, Brownell‐Riddell Carissa, Diamond Natalia, Ritchie Kerry, Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a 'Students as Partners' model to develop an authentic assessment promoting employability skills in undergraduate life science education. FEBS Open Bio 15(3): 506–522. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13941

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-assignment online survey of 173 students in a fourth-year life science pathophysiology course asked each to select the single employability skill with greatest growth. Students reported the greatest growth in "SL (41.6% of students)" and communication/knowledge translation (34.7%). Percentages are self-reported perceptions, not tested skill measures.

> "Students reported the greatest growth in these same transferable skills, namely SL (41.6% of students) and communication/knowledge translation (34.7% of students). Additionally, 16.2% of students identified growth in their CT capabilities and 7.5% of students identified growth in their problem‐solving skills"
