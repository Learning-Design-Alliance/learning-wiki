# Nearly half of the 95 scaled measures used in character education program studies were developed for the study under review, with only 32 available off the shelf

Cross-case analysis of all scaled instruments used in studies of the 36 programs (Executive Summary and Table 3). The review found "46 were developed for the study under review," 17 adapted, and 32 off the shelf, with six off-the-shelf scales used across more than one program.
