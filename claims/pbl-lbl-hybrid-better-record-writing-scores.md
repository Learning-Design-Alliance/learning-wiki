## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students taught with PBL+LBL performed significantly better on medical record writing assessments than students taught with LBL alone (RR = 3.44, 95% CI 2.94–3.93, P < .00001). [→ Liu CX 2020](#liu-cx-2020)

## Evidence

### Liu CX 2020

Liu CX, Ouyang WW, Wang XW, Chen D, Jiang ZL. (2020). Comparing hybrid problem-based and lecture learning (PBL + LBL) with LBL pedagogy on clinical curriculum learning for medical students in China: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000019687

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of the 7 studies reporting the medical record writing assessment score, analyzed with a random effects model (I2 = 87%). The pooled relative risk favored PBL+LBL: "RR = 3.44, 95% CI: 2.94∼3.93".

> "Meta-analysis results indicated that the medical record writing assessment performance of students was significantly better in PBL + LBL group than in LBL group (RR = 3.44, 95% CI: 2.94∼3.93, P < .00001)"
