## Evidence

### Noordegraaf-Eelens 2019

Noordegraaf-Eelens, L., Kloeg, J., & Noordzij, G. (2019). PBL and sustainable education: addressing the problem of isolation. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 24(5), 971-979. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-019-09927-z

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Comparative conceptual analysis of the three PBL models: the Danish model at Alborg has students define problems before starting projects, adding contextualization through student-proposed problems and parallel lectures, though the article judges that isolation still occurs in these models.

> "As a result, in the Danish model students' degree of self-direction is higher, they learn more skills (e.g., planning, monitoring), and are able to apply their knowledge to real-life situations."
