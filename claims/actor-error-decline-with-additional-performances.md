## Evidence

### Abshire Martha A. 2020

Abshire Martha A., Li Xintong, Basyal Pragyashree Sharma, Teply Melissa L., Singh Arun L., Hayes Margaret M., Turnbull Alison E. (2020). Actor feedback and rigorous monitoring: Essential quality assurance tools for testing behavioral interventions with simulation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233538

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted Poisson regression of quality assurance error counts from the SCIP trial, with models adjusted for the SFM performing and whether the physician disclosed the patient was sick enough to die. The article reports "Incidence Rate Ratio[IRR] = 0.95, 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.93–0.97, P<0.001" for total errors per additional performance; no standardized effect size is printed for this finding.

> "In adjusted analyses, each additional performance by a SFM was associated with a statistically significant decrease in the risk of committing an error (Incidence Rate Ratio[IRR] = 0.95, 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.93–0.97, P<0.001)"
