Rashida G. Gabdrakhmanova and Aleksandr Y. Kostarev. (2016). Pedagogical Modeling of the Competitive Activity of Athletes in Russian Lapta. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(13), 6090-6101. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational analysis of competitive activity: more than 200 games involving 425 players of various skill levels and gender were verbatim recorded and analyzed, plus pedagogical observation of training of players of different qualifications and gender. The functional level includes game episodes divided into elementary, easy, medium, difficult and highly complex.

> "Using pedagogical observation method, we identified functional, dynamic and component and target-oriented levels of play activities in Russian lapta."
