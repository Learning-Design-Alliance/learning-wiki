## Evidence

### Bakri Awaji 2020

Bakri Awaji, Ellis Solaiman and Lindsay Marshall. (2020). Investigating the Requirements for Building a Blockchain-Based Achievement Record System. [doi:10.1145/3411681.3411691](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411681.3411691)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire items on technology knowledge (Study 1, n=247). The majority (55.32%) reported no understanding of blockchain and 80% reported never having transacted with blockchain technology.

> "In Figure 7, it can be seen that the majority of respondents (55.32%) did not have any understanding of blockchain technology, while 80% of participants also said that they have never undertaken a transaction using blockchain technology."

### Bakri Awaji 2020 (2)

Bakri Awaji, Ellis Solaiman and Lindsay Marshall. (2020). Investigating the Requirements for Building a Blockchain-Based Achievement Record System. [doi:10.1145/3411681.3411691](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411681.3411691)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire items on digital wallet awareness (Study 1). 44.15% had no knowledge of digital wallets and 71.70% had never used digital wallets, Ethereum wallets or Metamask; 28.30% had experience with digital wallets.

> "As shown in Figure 8, (44.15%) had no knowledge about digital wallets, and most stakeholders (71.70%) had never used digital wallets, Ethereum wallets or Metamask."
