# A simple curriculum of web-based theory plus fixed-repetition virtual simulator training did not improve OSATS or NRS scores of residents' first operative laparoscopic salpingectomy compared with standard clinical education

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized trial of 20 junior residents, there was no statistical difference in OSATS or NRS scores between the intervention and control groups on their first operative laparoscopy. [→ Jokinen E 2019](#jokinen-e-2019)

## Evidence

### Jokinen E 2019

Jokinen E, Mikkola TS, Härkki P. (2019). Effect of structural training on surgical outcomes of residents' first operative laparoscopy: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-06657-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized, blinded trial in Helsinki University Hospital and Hyvinkää Hospital with ten residents per group; first laparoscopic salpingectomy videos were rated by three blinded assessors. The article reports "no statistical difference in OSATS or NRS scores" with the printed means above; no effect size is given.

> "The mean for total OSATS points was 16.4 (SD 5.3) in the intervention group and 16.2 (SD 5.7) in the control group, and the mean NRS was 2.9 (SD 1.6) and 3.1 (SD 1.7), respectively."
