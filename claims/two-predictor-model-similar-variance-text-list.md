## Evidence

### Altani 2019

Altani, A., Protopapas, A., Katopodi, K., & Georgiou, G. K. (2019). From Individual Word Recognition to Word List and Text Reading Fluency. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000359

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analyses (Table 5) predicting serial word reading and text reading from discrete word reading and serial digit naming across grades and languages. The authors report "accounted for similar amounts of variance" in the two outcomes; direction of small differences varied across grades and languages.

> "our results showed that the two-predictor model of serial digit naming and discrete word reading accounted for similar amounts of variance in word list reading fluency and text reading fluency."
