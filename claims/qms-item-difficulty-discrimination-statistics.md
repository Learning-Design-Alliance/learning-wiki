## Evidence

### Singh 2010

Singh, C. and Zhu, G. (2010). Surveying Students' Understanding of Quantum Mechanics. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analysis of the QMS administered to 109 students; item difficulties "approximately ranged between 0.2 and 0.8" and 26 of 31 items were below 0.5. Descriptive item statistics, no effect size.

> "The item difficulty of each question (percentage of students who correctly answered each question), shown in Fig. 1, approximately ranged between 0.2 and 0.8. Most of the item difficulties (26 out of 31) were below 0.5."

### Singh 2010 (2)

Singh, C. and Zhu, G. (2010). Surveying Students' Understanding of Quantum Mechanics. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Point biserial discrimination, the correlation between the score on a particular question and the total test score minus that question's score, was computed for the 109-student administration; "The PBD approximately ranged from 0.2 to 0.6." Descriptive only.

> "The PBD approximately ranged from 0.2 to 0.6 with half of the questions with PBD higher than 0.4 and two items with PBD lower than 0.2."
