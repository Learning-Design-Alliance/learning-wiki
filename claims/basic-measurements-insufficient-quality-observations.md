## Evidence

### Karoui Haykel 2025

Karoui Haykel, Rwandarwacu Victor P., Niyonzima Jonathan, Makuza Antoinette, Nkuranga John B., D'Acremont Valérie, Kulinkina Alexandra V. (2025). Identifying clinical skill gaps of healthcare workers using a digital clinical decision support algorithm during outpatient pediatric consultations in primary health centers in Rwanda. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0318284

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Prospective observational study of 188 routine consultations in the 10 lowest-performing health centers, using a standardized digital evaluation form; the study clinician documented whether each measure was done or skipped and whether quality was sufficient or insufficient. The observations found temperature, weight and MUAC "more often assessed, but with insufficient quality". Percentages are descriptive; no effect size is printed.

> "Temperature, weight, and MUAC were more often assessed, but with insufficient quality (67%, 70%, and 69%, respectively). Height showed a mixed pattern, with 21% of assessments skipped and 54% deemed insufficient."
