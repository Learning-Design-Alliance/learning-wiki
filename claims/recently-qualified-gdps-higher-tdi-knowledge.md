`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Scoping review synthesis of cross-sectional questionnaire studies of GDPs. The review reports that "more recently qualified GDPs had higher knowledge levels" in most studies, with five studies finding GDPs qualified within 5 years highest. No pooled effect size is printed.

> "Most often, it was determined that more recently qualified GDPs had higher knowledge levels (Baginska and Wilczynska-Borawska 2013; Basir et al. 2018; Buldur and Kaptan 2018; Cvijic et al. 2024; Dhaimy et al. 2017; Duruk and Erel 2020; Re et al. 2014).
