Evaluation of the Teacher Incentive Fund: Final Report on Implementation and Impacts of Pay-for-Performance Across Four Years. q2 · i? · causal · r?

The report's implementation findings state that "Districts typically implemented most components of the performance-based compensation system throughout all four years," but fewer than half planned to continue bonuses once the grant was over. No effect size applies to this descriptive finding.

"Districts typically implemented most components of the performance-based compensation system throughout all four years of their programs. However, fewer than half the districts planned to keep offering performance bonuses once the grant was over."
