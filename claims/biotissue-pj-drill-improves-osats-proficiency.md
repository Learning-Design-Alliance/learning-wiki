## Evidence

### Yoshioka Ryuji 2021

Yoshioka Ryuji, Imamura Hiroshi, Ichida Hirofumi, Gyoda Yu, Mizuno Tomoya, Mise Yoshihiro, Saiura Akio. (2021). Simulation training in pancreatico-jejunostomy using an inanimate biotissue model improves the technical skills of hepatobiliary-pancreatic surgical fellows. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244915

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Uncontrolled observational prospective study in which eight HBP surgical fellows repeated a biotissue PJ drill five times, assessed by two blinded supervisor surgeons using OSATS. The article reports a statistically significant improvement with "a P value of 0.003 and 0.014" for the two graders; no effect size is printed.

> "The average OSATS score, as assessed by both the supervisor surgeons, improved significantly from the first to the final drill, with a P value of 0.003 and 0.014 for the assessment by the two surgeons, respectively."
