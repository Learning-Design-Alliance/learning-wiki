### Åke Ingerman 2003

Åke Ingerman, Shirley Booth. (2003). Expounding on physics. A phenomenographic study of physicists talking of their physics. Chalmers University of Technology. Originally published 2002. [doi:10.1080/0950069032000070298](https://doi.org/10.1080/0950069032000070298)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' observation from the interview discussions, illustrated by an interviewer's category C exposition linking mathematics and physics which the interviewee then engaged with. They conclude the forms, not who speaks, are significant as structures.

> "Further it can be seen that the form employed by one of them can encourage the form of the response"
