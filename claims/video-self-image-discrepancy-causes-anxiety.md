## Evidence

### Hunukumbure Agra Dilshani 2017

Hunukumbure Agra Dilshani, Smith Susan F, Das Saroj. (2017). Holistic feedback approach with video and peer discussion under teacher supervision. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1017-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview study of seven medical students; many overcame initial anxiety during the session, and a graduate-entry student with regular video feedback (M5) was less apprehensive, suggesting exposure reduces apprehension.

> "The main challenge identified on videos was the anxiety of watching own self (Fig. 2) when their expected self-image was different to that of their actual image."
