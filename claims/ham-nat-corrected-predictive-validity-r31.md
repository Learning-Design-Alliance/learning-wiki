## Evidence

### Meyer Hubertus 2019

Meyer Hubertus, Zimmermann Stefan, Hissbach Johanna, Klusmann Dietrich, Hampe Wolfgang. (2019). Selection and academic success of medical students in Hamburg, Germany. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1443-4

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correction applied to the observed first-order correlation within the selected group, where range restriction reduced the HAM-Nat standard deviation by u = 0.75. The article states the uncorrected value "only reflects outcome differentiation amongst successful applicants."

> "Correction for indirect range restriction yielded a validity coefficient of r = .313 using Thorndike's case C formula. The Bayesian method MICE yielded r = .304."
