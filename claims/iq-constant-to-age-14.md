ARTHUK S. OTIS (Stanford University). (1917). A Criticism of the Yerkes-Bridges Point Scale, with Alternative Suggestions. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. VIII, No. 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Otis+criticism+Yerkes-Bridges+point+scale

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing Terman and Stern, that IQs from successive testing of the same individuals remain "fahly constant up to the age of 14" (the article's spelling). Otis argues that, combined with his constancy-of-brightness hypothesis, this supports equal absolute increments of intelligence up to about age 14.

> "Terman and Stern have found, however, by successive testing of the same individuals that I Q's gotten by dividing mental by chronological age do remain fahly constant up to the age of 14"
