## Evidence

### Etchemendy 2011

Etchemendy, E.; Baños, RM.; Botella, C.; Castilla López, DV.; Alcañiz Raya, ML.; Rasal, P.; Farfallini, L. (2011). An e-health platform for the elderly population: The butler system. Computers and Education. 56(1):275-279. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2010.07.022

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A pre-post assessment of the Butler platform with elderly users: mood measures were "assessed through visual analogical scales before and after the platform use" and analyzed with MANOVAs. The abstract reports that after use "the participants' insensitive emotions increased and their negative ones decreased"; no effect size is printed, so impact is not coded.

> "These measures were assessed through visual analogical scales before and after the platform use, with the outcome measures MANOVAS analyses were performed."
