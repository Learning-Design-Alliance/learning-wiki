Laura Ríos, Benjamin Pollard, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2019). Pathways to proposing causes for unexpected experimental results. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.rios](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.rios)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative finding from the interview corpus: in Lorca's episode the proposed cause (characteristic impedance of BNC cables) "does not explain the noise pick-up he observed" because the circuit operated where cable capacitance is negligible; the authors state this episode is "more typical of the complete data set."

> "Often, the post hoc proposed cause is not the exact reason for the discrepancy, which highlights how difficult it is to propose causes."
