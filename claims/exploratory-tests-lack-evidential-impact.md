# Statistical tests applied to exploratory analyses lack evidential impact because the denominator of tests becomes unspecified

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` If hypotheses and analyses are not predesignated, the denominator of possible tests is undefinable, so null-hypothesis tests lose their informative value; exploratory tests carry no evidential weight. [→ Preregistration and Registered Reports 2021](#preregistration-and-registered-reports-2021)

## Evidence

### Preregistration and Registered Reports 2021

Preregistration and Registered Reports. (2021). https://osf.io/ya594

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument from de Groot (1956/2014), quoted by the article: long-term error control in frequentist statistics depends on a defined denominator of tests, which post hoc analysis destroys. The article adds Gelman and Loken's garden of forked paths as the same problem.

> "One “is allowed” to apply statistical tests in exploratory research, just as long as one realizes that they do not have evidential impact."
