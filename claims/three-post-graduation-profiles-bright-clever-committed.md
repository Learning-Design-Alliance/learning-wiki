## Evidence

### Walkiewicz M 2026

Walkiewicz M. (2026). Why academic selection fails to protect against physician burnout: a brief research report from a 25-year longitudinal programme. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1836438

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cluster analysis (binary Euclidean distance, Ward's method) on standardised outcome scores from the longitudinal cohort (Walkiewicz et al., 24). Differences were substantial: "satisfaction: F(2,47) = 20.18, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.46; life satisfaction: F(2,47) = 29.09, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.55; income: F(2,47) = 16.80, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.42".

> "The third cluster (n = 16, 32%), labelled the “Bright,” was characterised by the highest licensing-examination performance (z = +1.17) but reported below-average career satisfaction (z = −0.24), the lowest general wellbeing (z = −0.35), the lowest life satisfaction (z = −0.89), and the lowest self-reported income (M = 2.63)."
