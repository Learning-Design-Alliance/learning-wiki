## Evidence

### Fukuda 2018

Fukuda, A. (2018). The Japanese EFL Learners' Self-Regulated Language Learning and Proficiency. Journal of Pan-Pacific Association of Applied Linguistics, 22(1), 65-87. https://doi.org/10.25256/PAAL.22.1.4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent-samples t-tests on MSLQ learning-strategy factors comparing the 67-student L group and 30-student H group. The study reports "significant differences between the L and H groups" with H-group participants receiving higher scores on MCS, ER, and CP; coping with problems showed the largest difference (L-H = -0.81).

> "Independent sample t-tests also revealed significant differences between the L and H groups in MCS (t (43) = -2.53, p < .05), ER (t (95) = - 3.20, p < .01), and CP ( t (95) = 3.00, p < .01)."
