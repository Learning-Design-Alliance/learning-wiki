## Evidence

### Astroth C 2026

Astroth C, Lunow-Luke T, Smith M, Chang J, Grigorian A, O'Leary M P. (2026). Shelf help: what powers medical student subject exam performance in the United States. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2708427

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reports observational findings across multiple specialties that more prior clerkships predict higher shelf scores. No studies found showing a negative correlation between number of clerkships completed and exam scores.

> "The total number of core clerkship students completed before the neurology shelf exam was associated with higher scores [42], and similar results have been reported in psychiatry [35,43]."
