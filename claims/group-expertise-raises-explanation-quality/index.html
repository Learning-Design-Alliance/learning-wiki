Janssen 2010¶

Janssen, J., Kirschner, F., Erkens, G., Kirschner, P. A., & Paas, F. (2010). Making the Black Box of Collaborative Learning Transparent: Combining Process-Oriented and Cognitive Load Approaches. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9131-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Webb et al. (1998), that "the quality of explanations is higher in groups with above-average students than in groups without these students." No effect size or sample details are printed.

"Research has demonstrated that it is important that a certain level of expertise is available within the group, because the quality of explanations is higher in groups with above-average students than in groups without these students (Webb et al. 1998)."
