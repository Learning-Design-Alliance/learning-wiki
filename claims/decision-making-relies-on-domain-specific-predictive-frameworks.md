### Argenta M. Price 2021

Argenta M. Price, Candice J. Kim, Eric W. Burkholder, Amy V. Fritz, and Carl E. Wieman. (2021). A Detailed Characterization of the Expert Problem-Solving Process in Science and Engineering; Guidance for Teaching and Assessment. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1187/cbe.20-12-0276](https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-12-0276)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' analysis of the coded interviews: the study focused on identifying decisions, not how they were made. They report the decision process "relied on specialized disciplinary knowledge and experience" organized in a common structure they call a "predictive framework."

> "while the decisions-to-be-made were the same across disciplines, how the experts made those decisions varied greatly by discipline and individual."
