## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2020

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Laura Ríos, and H. J. Lewandowski (2020). Preliminary model for student ownership of projects. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.dounas-frazer](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.dounas-frazer)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview excerpts coded in the study's two consensus coding rounds. Student Olivia framed her group's search for a "ﬁnal project-worthy" topic as intellectual contribution supporting ownership; instructor Mac described emotional investment in topic choice as "a piece of ownership."

> "CHOICE was co-coded with CONTRIBUTIONS or EMO - TIONAL RESPONSES , as evidenced by Olivia and Mac. Olivia described how her group’s search for a “ﬁnal project-worthy” topic (i.e., intellectual CONTRIBUTIONS to the project) sup- ported them to feel ownership of the project"

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2020 (2)

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Laura Ríos, and H. J. Lewandowski (2020). Preliminary model for student ownership of projects. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.dounas-frazer](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.dounas-frazer)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Coding of the same interview corpus found no choice-phase excerpts coded with new understanding. The authors "believe that new understanding can indeed be generated during the ﬁrst phase," noting Mac requires students to write project proposals during choice.

> "There were no instances where CHOICE was co-coded with NEW UNDERSTANDING . However, we believe that new un- derstanding can indeed be generated during the ﬁrst phase of a project."
