# Lower inhibitory control in early adolescence predicts greater increases in delinquency from middle adolescence to emerging adulthood

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2`–`r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Worse task-based inhibitory control (higher SSRT) at age 11 predicted increases in delinquency from middle adolescence to emerging adulthood. [→ Fosco 2019](#fosco-2019)
`q3 i?` Lower parent-reported inhibitory control in early adolescence predicted greater increases in delinquency both from early to middle adolescence and from middle adolescence to emerging adulthood. [→ Fosco 2019 (2)](#fosco-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Fosco 2019

Fosco, W. D., Hawk, L. W. Jr., Colder, C. R., Meisel, S. N., & Lengua, L. J. (2019). The development of inhibitory control in adolescence and prospective relations with delinquency. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adolescence.2019.08.008

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Prospective growth-model prediction analysis in the 387-family longitudinal sample: Wave 7 (Mage=18.2) rule-breaking regressed on the SST intercept and slope controlling for Wave 3 scores, with IQ and sex as covariates. The intercept, but not the slope, predicted later delinquency increases.

> "worse inhibitory control in early adolescence (i.e., higher SSRTs at age 11, the intercept) predicted increases in delinquency from middle adolescence to emerging adulthood (b= 0.001, SE= 0.001, p=.05)"

### Fosco 2019 (2)

Fosco, W. D., Hawk, L. W. Jr., Colder, C. R., Meisel, S. N., & Lengua, L. J. (2019). The development of inhibitory control in adolescence and prospective relations with delinquency. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adolescence.2019.08.008

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Parallel prediction models using the parent-reported EATQ-R intercept (no growth was detected, so only the intercept was modeled). Lower early-adolescent ratings also predicted increases from early to middle adolescence (b= −0.15, SE= 0.002, p<.001).

> "lower inhibitory control ratings in early adolescence were associated with steeper increases in delinquent behavior from middle adolescence to emerging adulthood, (b= −0.16, SE=.007, p<.001)"
