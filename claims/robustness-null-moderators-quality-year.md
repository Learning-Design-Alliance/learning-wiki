Two continuous moderator meta-regressions in the three-level model: risk of bias (range 1–8, Δr = .002 across the full range) and publication year (1988–2025, Δr = −.032 over 37 years), both non-significant in three-level and RVE analyses.

> "These null results indicate that higher methodological quality was not associated with smaller (or larger) effect sizes, and that studies at different points on the quality spectrum converged on similar estimates."
