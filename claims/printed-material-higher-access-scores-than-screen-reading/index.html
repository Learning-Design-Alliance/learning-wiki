Evidence¶

Tuncer 2014¶

Tuncer, M., & Bahadır, F. (2014). Effect of screen reading and reading from printed out material on student success and permanency in Introduction to Computer Lesson. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 13(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1034226

q3 · i? · causal · r2

A pretest–posttest control group experiment with 78 freshman vocational students (38 screen reading, 40 printed material) analyzed access scores via ANCOVA. The corrected access average was 10.34 for control versus 7.85 for experiment, and the article reports "a significant difference between average Access scores."

"According to results on table 6, there is a significant difference between average Access scores [F (1,76) =6.078, p<.05]. This difference is in favour of reading from printed out material group."
