## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jeffrey Buckley 2018

Jeffrey Buckley, Niall Seery, Donal Canty. (2018). A Heuristic Framework of Spatial Ability: a Review and Synthesis of Spatial Factor Literature to Support its Translation into STEM Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9432-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that Hegarty and Waller "designed a valid measure of perspective taking ability" whose confirmatory factor analysis supports uniqueness from mental rotation factors, further supported by Wang et al.'s meta-analysis distinguishing small-scale and large-scale spatial ability.

> "Hegarty and Waller ( 2004) designed a valid measure of perspective taking ability and through confirmatory factor analysis have shown evidence to support the uniqueness from mental rotation factors."

## Discussion

Which factors the review counts as spatial, and so which can be separated at all, follows its [criteria for identifying spatial factors](../research-methods/criteria-for-identifying-spatial-factors.md): abilities concerned with visual stimuli and not influenced by semantic knowledge.
