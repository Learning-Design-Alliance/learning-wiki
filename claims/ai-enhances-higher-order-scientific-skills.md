`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Thematic finding from the RQ1 section of the teacher interview/questionnaire study. Teachers described students designing experimental plans autonomously, generating and testing hypotheses, and analyzing long-term plant growth data with AI support.

> "Teachers reported that AI tools significantly enhanced students' acquisition of higher-order scientific skills, including experimental design, inquiry, hypothesis testing, data analysis, modeling, argumentation, and creative exploration."
