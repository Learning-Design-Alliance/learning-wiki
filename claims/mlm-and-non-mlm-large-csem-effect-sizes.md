## Evidence

### Moore 2018

James Christopher Moore. (2018). Efficacy of Multimedia Learning Modules as Preparation for Lecture-Based Tutorials in Electromagnetism. Educ. Sci. 2018, 8, 23. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8010023

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cohen's d computed for the CSEM raw gains (non-MLM n = 44, MLM n = 33): d = 1.25 for non-MLM and d = 1.64 for MLM. The author notes large effect sizes are unsurprising since both groups used research-verified pedagogies.

> "Both the non-MLM and MLM groups demonstrate very large (>1.2) effect sizes, with the MLM treatment group demonstrating an effect size close to what Sawilowsky describes as “huge” [32]."
