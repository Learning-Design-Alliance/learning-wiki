## Evidence

### Jacob 2019

Jacob, L., Lachner, A., & Scheiter, K. (2019). Learning by Explaining Orally or In Written Form? Text Difficulty Matters. https://aspredicted.org/sv3zv.pdf

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

ANCOVA on meta-comprehension accuracy (bias) after the learning activity, controlling for study-phase judgments. Post-hoc tests showed "a significant difference between students who explained orally and students who wrote an explanation (p < .001)" and oral versus retrieval (p = .026); written versus retrieval showed no effect (p = .446).

> "Results demonstrated a main effect of learning activity, F(2, 111) = 9.05, p < .001, η²p  = .032, and a main effect of text difficulty, F(1, 111) = 41.11, p < .001, η²p  = .039, indicating that students rated their comprehension more precisely in the low-difficult text condition than in the high-difficult text condition (see Table 1)."
