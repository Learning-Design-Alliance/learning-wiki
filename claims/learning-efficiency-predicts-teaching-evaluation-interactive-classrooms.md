## Evidence

### Zhang Wanting 2025

Zhang Wanting, Sun Junhua, Yao Haichang. (2025). The impact of learning efficiency on teaching evaluation in urban-rural interactive classrooms: a moderated mediation model. BMC Psychology, 13. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03313-8

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis of questionnaire data from 449 students in 18 randomly selected rural Hope Primary Schools; means reported for all four variables and all correlations significant at P < 0.01, with "the association between learning efficiency and teaching evaluation was the strongest (r = 0.569)".

> "Among these, the association between learning efficiency and teaching evaluation was the strongest (r = 0.569), while the correlation of Network environment with other variables was relatively weaker (r = 0.219)"

### Zhang Wanting 2025 (2)

Zhang Wanting, Sun Junhua, Yao Haichang. (2025). The impact of learning efficiency on teaching evaluation in urban-rural interactive classrooms: a moderated mediation model. BMC Psychology, 13. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03313-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PROCESS Model 4 mediation analysis with Bootstrap (10,000 replicates, 95% CI) on the same 449-student questionnaire sample; the direct effect of learning efficiency on teaching evaluation was 0.437, and the Bootstrap confidence interval excluded zero.

> "The direct effect (0.437) and mediating effect (0.156) account for 73.65% and 26.35% of the total effect, respectively."
