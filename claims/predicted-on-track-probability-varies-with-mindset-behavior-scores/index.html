Snipes 2016¶

Predicted probabilities from separate logistic regressions (figure 6, table C2), sample size = 13,578. The report states the academic-behavior probability "varied from a low of 52 percent" to "86 percent for students with the highest score."

"The estimated probability of being on track varied from a low of 54 percent among students with the lowest score on the 5-point growth-mindset scale (1) to a high of 83 percent among students with the highest score (5) on the growth-mindset scale."
