# More frequent practice-test participation predicts higher absolute accuracy of item-specific judgments in the final exam, regardless of feedback

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In both studies, students who engaged in more practice tests provided more accurate judgments (higher absolute accuracy) in the posttest. [→ Nick Naujoks 2022](#nick-naujoks-2022)

## Evidence

### Nick Naujoks 2022

Hierarchical regression in Study 1 (N = 201, no feedback) predicting posttest absolute accuracy from pretest accuracy and practice-test participation; Table 3 shows step 2 ΔR² = .03*, β = .18*. Study 2 showed a similar pattern (β = .17*).
