# In a cluster-randomized trial among Syrian schoolchildren aged 10–11, the leaflet cluster achieved significantly better oral health knowledge than the E-learning cluster at 6 and 12 weeks, with a higher knowledge gain.

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children in the Leaflet cluster had significantly better oral health knowledge than those in the E-learning cluster at 6 weeks (P < 0.001) and at 12 weeks (P < 0.001). [→ Al Bardaweel Susan 2018](#al-bardaweel-susan-2018)
`q3 i?` The knowledge gain from baseline was significantly higher in the Leaflet cluster (34.19 ± 11.35) than in the E-learning cluster (19.60 ± 9.75), P < 0.001. [→ Al Bardaweel Susan 2018 (2)](#al-bardaweel-susan-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Al Bardaweel Susan 2018

Al Bardaweel Susan, Dashash Mayssoon. (2018). E-learning or educational leaflet: does it make a difference in oral health promotion? A clustered randomized trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-018-0540-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cluster-randomized trial with 100 children per cluster; independent samples t-test compared mean knowledge scores between clusters at 6 and 12 weeks. Leaflet cluster scored 82.87 ± 10.69 and 89.12 ± 8.16 versus E-learning 72.16 ± 10.25 and 74.66 ± 8.98; "children in Leaflet cluster had significantly better knowledge than those in E-learning cluster".

> "However, children in Leaflet cluster had significantly better knowledge than those in E-learning cluster at 6 weeks (P < 0.001) and at 12 weeks (P < 0.001) (Table 2)."

### Al Bardaweel Susan 2018 (2)

Al Bardaweel Susan, Dashash Mayssoon. (2018). E-learning or educational leaflet: does it make a difference in oral health promotion? A clustered randomized trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-018-0540-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same trial's between-cluster analysis of knowledge gain relative to baseline: "the increase was higher in Leaflet cluster than in E-learning cluster (34.19 ± 11.35 versus 19.60 ± 9.75, respectively, P < 0.001)". No effect size printed.

> "Further analysis using independent samples t-test revealed that the difference in knowledge gain was statistically significant between the two clusters, and that the increase was higher in Leaflet cluster than in E-learning cluster (34.19 ± 11.35 versus 19.60 ± 9.75, respectively, P < 0.001)."
