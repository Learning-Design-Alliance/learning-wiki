`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Design-process observation from the artifact development report: the virtual format could not support technical skills such as hand hygiene with soap and water or PVC removal and insertion, so the focus shifted to non-technical competencies, mitigated by combining the simulation with other modalities.

> "Refocusing the learning objectives became necessary since, in a virtual environment, it would not be possible to perform practical technical skills associated with best practices for BSI prevention"
