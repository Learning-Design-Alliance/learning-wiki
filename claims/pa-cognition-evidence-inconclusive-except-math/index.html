The commentary's background synthesis of existing reviews reports that "other systematic reviews and meta-reviews concluded that the evidence for a beneficial influence ... is inconclusive", with math performance the exception. The article attributes this to heterogeneity in primary-study characteristics and review procedures.

"However, other systematic reviews and meta-reviews concluded that the evidence for a beneficial influence of regular physical activity on cognitive and academic performance is inconclusive (Daly-Smith et al., 2018; Singh et al., 2018; Wassenaar et al., 2020) except for math performance"

Related Claims¶

  • Classroom physical activity increases children's on-task behaviour, with no consistent gain on attention or other cognitive tests
  • Guided play outperforms direct instruction or free play on a few early-childhood outcomes, with no reliable difference on most others, in one meta-analysis
  • School-aged children show reduced physical activity and increased sedentary behavior during the summer break across multiple countries
  • The effects of physical activity on cognition and academic outcomes depend on the subdomain examined — related
  • Longitudinal physical activity programs have a positive effect on executive functions in preadolescent children (g = 0.24) — related
  • First-graders using Kid Space showed physical activity during play-based learning, in an exploratory case study without a comparison — related
  • Effects of school-based physical activity on academic performance are not uniform and appear higher for mathematics skills — related
  • Effects of classroom physical activity breaks on academic performance in university students are mixed or inconclusive — related
  • Eight of thirteen reviews concluded physical activity benefits cognitive performance, five reported mixed or inconclusive evidence, and none suggested worse cognition
  • Mathematics is the subject where positive associations with physical activity are found most consistently in systematic reviews, though meta-analytic effects range from null to medium — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Evidence for physical activity benefits on academic achievement in youth is inconsistent: six of twelve reviews concluded benefits, six mixed or inconclusive — related
