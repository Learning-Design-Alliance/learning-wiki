## Evidence

### Jonck Christopher 2024

Jonck Christopher, Weimer Andreas Michael, Fundel Beatrice, Heinz Wolfgang, Merkel Daniel, Fiedel Hendrik, Ille Carlotta, Kloeckner Roman, Buggenhagen Holger, Piepho Tim, Weimer Johannes. (2024). Development and evaluation of a point-of-care ultrasound curriculum for paramedics in Germany – a prospective observational study and comparison. BMC Medical Education 24:811. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05816-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multivariate linear regression on the paramedic study group's test results. The article reports the quoted coefficient and p-value for TestT1 and states no significant influencing factors were observed for TestT2, TestT3 and P-SimT3; the authors interpret this as indicating the efficacy of the tailored curriculum.

> "In a multivariate linear regression analysis of the performance of the study group for the total score of TestT1, “prior attendance of at least one ultrasound course” was identified as a significant influencing factor for a better test result (β = 11.2; p = 0.034)."
