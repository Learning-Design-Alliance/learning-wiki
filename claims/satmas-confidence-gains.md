## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students completing the course reported increased confidence in A-E assessment (AD 1.38), suggesting investigations and management plans (AD 1.78), and managing a trauma patient overall (AD 1.98), all p < 0.001. [→ Kumar PR 2024](#kumar-pr-2024)

## Evidence

### Kumar PR 2024

Kumar PR, Large J, Konda N, Hashmi Y, Adebayo O, Sivaraman M, Lee JJ. (2024). Student advanced trauma management and skills (SATMAS): a validation study. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-024-02456-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post-course Likert-scale feedback from the 63 students who completed all seven sessions, analysed with the Wilcoxon matched pairs signed rank test. Absolute differences of 1.38, 1.78 and 1.98 on a 5-point scale; no standardised effect size printed.

> "students that completed the teaching programme reported improvement in their confidence with assessing a trauma patient through an A-E assessment (AD 1.38, p < 0.0001), suggesting initial investigations and an initial management plan for a trauma patient (AD 1.78, p < 0.001), and thus more confident with managing a trauma patient as a future doctor (AD 1.98, p < 0.0001)"
