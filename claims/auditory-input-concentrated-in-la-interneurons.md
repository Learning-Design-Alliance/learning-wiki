# Auditory thalamic and cortical input to BLA interneurons is concentrated in the LA: thalamic EPSCs are larger in LA than BA interneurons (191 vs 53 pA, p < 0.0001)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1`–`r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Direct thalamic (AT) input reaches ~91% of LA interneurons but 67% of BA interneurons, and AT-evoked EPSCs are significantly smaller in BA (53 ± 14 pA) than LA (191 ± 18 pA; p < 0.0001). [→ Polepalli 2020](#polepalli-2020)
`q3 i?` Cortical (AC) input innervates 85% of LA but only 38% of BA interneurons; individual AC input amplitude does not differ between nuclei (p = 0.5888). [→ Polepalli 2020 (2)](#polepalli-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Polepalli 2020

Polepalli, J. S., Gooch, H. & Sah, P. (2020). Diversity of interneurons in the lateral and basal amygdala. npj Science of Learning 5:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0071-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Optogenetic (ChR2) stimulation of AT afferents in acute slices from GAD67-GFP mice; BA AT-EPSC peak 53 ± 14 pA vs LA 191 ± 18 pA, p < 0.0001. "and it was signiﬁcantly smaller (peak amplitude: 53 ± 14 pA; p < 0.0001)". No standardised effect size printed.

> "In the BA, 67% (12/18) of interneurons received AT input (Fig. 3a) and it was signiﬁcantly smaller (peak amplitude: 53 ± 14 pA; p < 0.0001) as compared to interneurons in the LA"

### Polepalli 2020 (2)

Polepalli, J. S., Gooch, H. & Sah, P. (2020). Diversity of interneurons in the lateral and basal amygdala. npj Science of Learning 5:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0071-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ChR2 stimulation of TeA cortical afferents in the same slice preparations; only 38% of BA interneurons (6/16) received AC input, with non-significant amplitude difference to LA (p = 0.5888). "input to individual cells (127 ± 50 pA) was not signiﬁcantly different".

> "AC input to BA interneurons was also sparse with 38% of cells (6/16; Fig. 3b) being innervated, but input to individual cells (127 ± 50 pA) was not signiﬁcantly different to that in the LA ( p = 0.5888)"
