Narrative synthesis of adverse-effect reporting across 7 included RCTs. The most commonly reported effects were dizziness, nausea, eye fatigue, and blurred vision, with additional symptoms including headache, neck pain, and unsteadiness.

> "Seven studies reported adverse effects associated with IVR interventions. One study documented no adverse events [41], while the remaining six studies reported various side effects [18, 19, 30, 34, 37, 43], predominantly related to visual and vestibular symptoms"
