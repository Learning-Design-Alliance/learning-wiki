# Before the presentation, intervention and control groups did not differ on theory of intelligence, task intentions or goal orientation

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pre-test ANOVAs found no significant differences between groups on theory of intelligence, choosing easy or complex tasks, or goal orientation. [→ Skipper 2015](#skipper-2015)

## Evidence

### Skipper 2015

Skipper, Y. (2015). An evaluation of an intervention to change first-year psychology students' theory of intelligence. Psychology Teaching Review, 21(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1146563

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test one-way ANOVAs comparing the randomly assigned groups on all dependent measures. The article reports results "were not significant for theory of intelligence F(1,74)=1.132, p=.291" and the other measures listed.

> "Results from this analysis were not significant for theory of intelligence F(1,74)=1.132, p=.291, choosing an easy task F(1,79)=.181, p=.672, choosing a complex task F(1,79)=.534, p=.467 or goal orientation F(1,78)=.290, p=.592"
