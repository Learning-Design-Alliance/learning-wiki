Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2014). Performance of Graduate Students at Identifying Introductory Physics Students’ Difficulties Related to Kinematics Graphs. 2014 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.039

Results for the quantitative PCK task on the two slope-computation questions, where 46% of introductory students computed slope as y/x. Graduate students identifying the less common reading-off-the-y-value difficulty were 36% on Q6 and 44% on Q17. Only percentages are printed.

> "Table 4 shows that few GSs  (20% and 16%) identified the major difficulty of roughly half of the introductory students, but more GSs identified the less common introductory student difficulty."
