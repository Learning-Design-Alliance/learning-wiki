### Babayiğit 2026

Babayiğit, S., & Trenkic, D. (2026). Vocabulary knowledge is key to understanding and addressing disparities in higher education. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 127–151. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70000

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of weighted average module marks from university records; the three-group ANOVA did not reach significance, so EAL and EFL students were combined, yielding "a statistically significant L1E advantage", t(179) = 2.18, p = .030, d = .3.

> "A comparison of their mean marks with those of the L1E group revealed a statistically significant L1E advantage with a small to medium effect size, t(179) = 2.18, p = .030, d = .3."
