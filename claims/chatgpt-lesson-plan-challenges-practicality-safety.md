## Evidence

### Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir 2025

Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir. (2025). ChatGPT-Assisted Lesson Planning for Children's STEAM Arts Education: An Experimental Study on Benefits, Challenges, Methods, and a Prompt Framework. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00513-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured teacher interviews on challenges of AI-assisted planning. Teachers noted ChatGPT "frequently produces overly idealized and polished lesson plans" impractical for daily teaching, included unsafe tools despite age constraints, and generated repetitive content requiring filtering.

> "In practice, ChatGPT frequently produces overly idealized and polished lesson plans that may be suitable for competitions or open classes but prove impractical in daily teaching."
