### Edelbring Samuel 2020

Edelbring Samuel, Alehagen Siw, Mörelius Evalotte, Johansson AnnaKarin, Rytterström Patrik. (2020). Should the PBL tutor be present? A cross-sectional study of group effectiveness in synchronous and asynchronous settings. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02018-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann–Whitney U pairwise comparison of the tutor-in-the-room group (n = 136) and the tutor-online group (n = 17); Table 3 reports cognitive medians of 24 versus 23 with p = 0.01, while motivational, demotivational and overall ratings were n.s. Only descriptive medians and the p-value are printed.
