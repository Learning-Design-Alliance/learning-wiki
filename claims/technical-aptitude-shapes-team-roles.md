## Evidence

### Elina Riivari 2021

Elina Riivari, Marke Kivijärvi, Anna-Maija Lämsä. (2021). Learning teamwork through a computer game: for the sake of performance or collaborative learning? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10009-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative content analysis of student reflections; students without prior computer game experience mostly followed instructions, and the discussion reports that dependence on a few technologically experienced members limited others' ability to explore the game.

> "The above quotations show how the adoption of a particular team role was partly related to one’s technical aptitude (or lack thereof)."
