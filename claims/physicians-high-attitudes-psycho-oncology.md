## Evidence

### Senf B  2019

Senf B , Fettel J , Demmerle C , Maiwurm P . (2019). Physicians' attitudes towards psycho‐oncology, perceived barriers, and psychosocial competencies: Indicators of successful implementation of adjunctive psycho‐oncological care? Psycho‐Oncology. https://doi.org/10.1002/pon.4962

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-report survey of N = 102 physicians at the University Hospital Frankfurt (response rate 85%) using a newly constructed 6-point Likert questionnaire, analysed descriptively. The article reports "the value of psycho‐oncology in the hospital as high (M = 5.24, SD = 0.55)". No effect sizes are printed.

> "Physicians rated the value of psycho‐oncology in the hospital as high (M = 5.24, SD = 0.55), and they reported a high degree of commitment to psycho‐oncology (M = 4.97, SD = 0.99), as well as positive beliefs about the efficacy of psycho‐oncology (M = 4.40, SD = 0.70)."
