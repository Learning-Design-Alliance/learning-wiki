## Evidence

### Stieha Vicki 2024

Stieha Vicki, Earl Brittnee, Hagens Harrisen, Haynes Meagan, Ulappa Amy, Bond Laura, Oxford Julia Thom. (2024). An exploration of the relationship between active learning and student motivation in STEM: a mixed methods study. Advances in Physiology Education. https://doi.org/10.1152/advan.00247.2022

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Triangulation of COPUS profiles, descriptive observation notes, and SDT survey results across seven instructors showed that instructors using overlapping practices (e.g., Q&A) differed in motivation scores; "similar practices implemented by different instructors can have different outcomes".

> "Our results support the conclusion that similar practices implemented by different instructors can have different outcomes."
