## Evidence

### Michos 2025

Michos, K., Schmitz, M.-L., & Petko, D. (2025). Learning Analytics in Schools: Is Digital Data Use Influenced by Teacher-Level or School-Level Factors? Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(3), 87–101. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8567

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weighted descriptive statistics from the survey (N = 2,247); mean digital data use was 2.76 (SD 1.39), data literacy 2.58 (1.26), tool availability 3.32 (1.32), and positive beliefs 3.38 (0.94) on 5-point scales. The authors interpret this as technologies being available while teachers are hesitant about skill and actual use.

> "almost 50% of teachers agreed on having access to technologies that provide digital student data. However, 47% of teachers disagreed on knowing how to improve their teaching and student learning with digital data. Similarly, almost 50% of teachers disagreed with using digital student data from available platforms to plan and adjust their teaching"
