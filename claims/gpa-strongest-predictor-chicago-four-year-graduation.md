## Evidence

### Jenny Nagaoka 2008

Jenny Nagaoka, Melissa Roderick, and Vanessa Coca. (2008). Barriers to College Attainment: Lessons from Chicago. The Consortium on Chicago School Research at The University of Chicago / Center for American Progress. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/barriers-college-attainment-lessons-chicago

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

An analysis of Chicago graduates from the classes of 1998 and 1999 designed to disentangle grades and test scores, controlling for student characteristics, course-taking, and other high school performance measures. It found students with a "3.0 unweighted GPA were 15 percentage points more likely to graduate" than peers with 2.5.

> "Among similar Chicago students who entered a four-year college with similar achievement test scores, students who entered with a 3.0 unweighted GPA were 15 percentage points more likely to graduate from a four-year college within six years than students who entered a four-year college with a 2.5 GPA."
