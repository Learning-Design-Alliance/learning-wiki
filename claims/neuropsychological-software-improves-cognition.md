`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review synthesis of nine neuropsychological software studies in older adults aged 60 to 94. The review reports that "all reviewed studies found benefits of training on memory" except one, with effect sizes ranging from 0.20 to 2.92 and a median of 0.56.

> "With the exception of Blackford [49] all reviewed studies found benefits of training on memory. Effect sizes ranged from 0.20 [6] to 2.92 [4] with a median effect size of 0.56."
