## Evidence

### Graell M. et al 2026

Graell M. et al. (2026). Towards disentangling the polygenic contribution of dyslexia to school performance. Translational Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41398-026-04118-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Ordinal mixed-effect models in the INSchool cohort tested the dyslexia PGS excluding EA-associated variants against school grades. The dyslexia-specific partition was associated with worse language performance but not mathematics, supporting dyslexia-specific genetic effects on language-related academic skills.

> "PGSDYS_noEA, were associated with worse performance in first language (OR = 0.84, 95% CI = 0.79–0.89) and foreign language (OR = 0.85, 95% CI = 0.8–0.9), but showed no significant association with performance in mathematics"
