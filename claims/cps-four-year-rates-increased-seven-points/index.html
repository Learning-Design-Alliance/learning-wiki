Nagaoka 2023¶

Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Arneson, A. (2023). The four years fallacy: Four-year vs. six-year bachelor's degree completion rates. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/the-four-years-fallacy

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Trend analysis of CPS cohorts from 2004 to 2014 shown in Figure 1, using National Student Clearinghouse data on immediate four-year enrollees. The report states four-year rates "increased by 7 percentage points" and six-year rates rose 3 points. No effect size is printed.

"CPS graduates' four-year bachelor's degree completion rates have increased by 7 percentage points over the past 10 years, even as the number of college enrollees nearly doubled from 4,588 in 2004 to 8,893 in 2014. The six-year completion rate has shown a more modest 3 percentage point increase."
