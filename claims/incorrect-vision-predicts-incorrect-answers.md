## Evidence

### Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic 2024

Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT on the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression performed on all 780 coded responses to test how well incorrect vision and incorrect reasoning predict answer incorrectness. The article reports the printed odds ratios (OR=6.53 and OR=2.05) but no standardised effect size, so no magnitude is coded. The authors conclude faulty vision was the primary cause of most incorrect answers.

> "incorrect vision is a strong predictor of answer incorrectness (β=1.887, OR=6.53, SE=0.175, Z=10.783, p<0.001), while incorrect reasoning was a moderate predictor of answer incorrectness (β=0.718, OR=2.05, SE=0.182, Z=3.958, p<0.001)"
