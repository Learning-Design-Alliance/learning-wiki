### Harsh Kumar 2024

Harsh Kumar, Ruiwei Xiao, Benjamin Lawson, Ilya Musabirov, Jiakai Shi, Xinyuan Wang, Huayin Luo, Joseph Jay Williams, Anna Rafferty, John Stamper, Michael Liut. (2024). Supporting Self-Reflection at Scale with Large Language Models: Insights from Randomized Field Experiments in Classrooms. arXiv preprint. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-condition paired comparison in Study 1's randomized field experiment: reflection-group self-confidence rose from 4.18 to 4.56 (t = −2.03, df = 65, p = 0.046). The control group showed no apparent change (means 4.04 to 4.15, p = 0.5269).

> "for the group who were assigned to the reflection condition, we observed an apparent increase in self-confidence (from 4.18 before the assignment to 4.56 after the assignment). Paired t-test suggests this difference is significant ( 𝑡 =−2.03, 𝑑 𝑓 = 65, 𝑝 = 0.046)"
