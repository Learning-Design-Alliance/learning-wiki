## Evidence

### Guba 2026

Guba, T.-P., Barbieri, C. A., Jansen, A., & Morris, A. (2026). Supports for preservice teachers' metacognitive monitoring and explanations of fraction comparisons. Instructional Science (2026) 54:18. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09773-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Calibration analysis comparing completeness scores to confidence ratings (1: Not at all Confident to 5: Very Confident), coded as Underconfident, Accurate, or Overconfident. Mean confidence was M = 4.28 (SD = 0.56) at first draft and M = 4.51 (SD = 0.60) at revision; the article reports "Most PSTs also showed limitations in calibration, indicating over-confidence."

> "Participants were generally confident with mean confidence ratings of M = 4.28 (SD = 0.56) at first draft and M = 4.51 (SD = 0.60) at revision, collapsed across all tasks and conditions. Confidence ratings were broken into three categories: Underconfident (meaning they were less confident than their explanation was complete), Accurate (meaning their confidence matched their explanation completeness), and Overconfident (meaning they were more confident than their explanation was complete."
