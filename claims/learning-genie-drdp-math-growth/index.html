q2 · i0 · causal · r?

The same quasi-experimental study of 366 prekindergarten and kindergarten students reported greater DRDP math growth for Learning Genie users, with the printed effect size of "+0.12" for math matching that for language and literacy.

"Students who used Learning Genie showed greater growth on the Desired Results Developmental Profile (DRDP) in language and literacy (effect size = +0.12) and math (+0.12)."
