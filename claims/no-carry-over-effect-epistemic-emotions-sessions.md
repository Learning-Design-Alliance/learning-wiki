## Evidence

### Ketonen 2026

Ketonen, E. E., Kruskopf, M., Lonka, K., & Mattlin, M. (2026). Dynamics of university students' epistemic emotions during game-based learning in an international relations course. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09771-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

DSEM lagged models on the 212 within-person session observations. Model 2 with session type found only boredom's carry-over significant (0.23). The result is null: equivalence was not tested.

> "To examine the carry-over effect (i.e. intra-individual stability) in students' epistemic emotions (RQ1), lagged models with random slopes were constructed for each epistemic emotion separately (Model 1). No significant autoregressive effects were found"
