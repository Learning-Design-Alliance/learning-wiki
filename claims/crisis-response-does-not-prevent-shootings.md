## Evidence

### Langman 2018

Langman, P., Petrosino, A., & Persson, H. (2018). Five misconceptions about school shootings. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/five-misconceptions-about-school-shootings/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The brief argues that lockdown procedures and survival trainings such as "Run, Hide, Fight" are emergency responses activated after a gunman is already in the building, and therefore do not prevent attacks. It positions threat assessment as the proactive complement to reactive crisis response.

> "These trainings and procedures are important and can save lives, but they are not the only thing that schools can do, and crisis response procedures do not prevent school shootings"
