### Brener A 2020

Brener A, Lewnard I, Mackinnon J, Jones C, Lohr N, Konda S, McIntosh J, Kulinski J. (2020). Missed opportunities to prevent cardiovascular disease in women with prior preeclampsia. BMC Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12905-020-01074-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary sub-analysis of the pre-intervention chart review (n = 89), reported in Results text describing Fig. 3. The comparison of female versus male providers reached no statistical significance (p = 0.80); no effect size was printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "a sub-analysis (Fig. 3) identified no gender difference among providers who inquired about a history of preeclampsia during the visit (50% of female providers and 55% of male providers, p = 0.80)."
