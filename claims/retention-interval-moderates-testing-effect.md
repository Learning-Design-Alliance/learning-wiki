# Retention interval moderates the testing effect, with effects often smaller, null, or reversed at short intervals

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Testing effects emerge reliably at long retention intervals but are of lesser magnitude, null, or reversed at short intervals of minutes. [→ ri-1](#ri-1)

## Evidence

### ri-1

Rowland, C. A. (2014, August 25). The Effect of Testing Versus Restudy on Retention: A Meta-Analytic Review of the Testing Effect. Psychological Bulletin. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/a0037559
