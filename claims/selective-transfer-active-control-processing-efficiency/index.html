Evidence¶

Zwilling 2019¶

Zwilling, C. E., Daugherty, A. M., Hillman, C. H., Kramer, A. F., Cohen, N. J. & Barbey, A. K. (2019). Enhanced decision-making through multimodal training. npj Science of Learning 4:11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0049-x

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Summary of ANCOVA results across the processing efficiency tests (Pattern Comparison, Letter Comparison, DSST) and the switch cost measure in the RCT. Active control showed reliable gains in Letter Comparison items correct (F = 3.41, p = 0.020) and reaction-time decreases, supporting "improvements in processing ef ﬁciency, but not executive function".

"First, active control training produced improvements in processing ef ﬁciency, but not executive function. Second, the uni- and multi-modal interventions improve executive function, but not processing ef ﬁciency."
