# Teachers' references to the NSES five essential features of inquiry increased across five lesson-sharing reflections

> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` Across five lesson-sharing events, teachers increasingly characterized lessons using the five essential features of inquiry, with four of five features referenced by the fifth lesson. [→ Ben Van Dusen 2011](#ben-van-dusen-2011)

`q2 i?` Across five lesson-sharing reflections, references consistent with the NSES inquiry framework rose from only Evidence and Explain (lessons 1 and 3, none in lesson 2) to four of the five features by lesson 5, with Connect still unreferenced. [→ Mike Ross 2018](#mike-ross-2018)

## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen 2011

Ben Van Dusen, Mike Ross, and Valerie Otero. (2011). Changing Identities and Evolving Conceptions of Inquiry through Teacher-Driven Professional Development. [doi:10.1063/1.3680073](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680073)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding of lesson-sharing reflections from four teachers against the five NSES essential features showed increasing references across the five lessons; "By the fifth lesson, however, four of the five essential features were referenced." Only Connect was never referenced.

> "As shown in Figure 2, the frequency with which the teacher participants characterized the shared lessons' inquiry-orientation using the five essential features of inquiry increased over the course of the five lessons."

### Mike Ross 2018

Mike Ross, Ben Van Dusen, Samson Sherman, Valerie Otero. (2018). Teacher-Driven Professional Development and the Pursuit of a Sophisticated Understanding of Inquiry. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1812.02312](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1812.02312)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding of five lesson-sharing reflections (prompts on what made lessons inquiry-oriented and how to modify them), posted on an online message board during the first five months, against the five NRC essential features. Coded references were relatively evenly distributed across the four participants.

> "By the fifth lesson, however, four of the five essential features were referenced. Only evaluating explanations in connection with scientific knowledge (Connect) was not referenced."
