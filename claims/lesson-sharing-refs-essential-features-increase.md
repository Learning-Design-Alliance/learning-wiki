## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across five lesson-sharing events, teachers increasingly characterized lessons using the five essential features of inquiry, with four of five features referenced by the fifth lesson. [→ Ben Van Dusen 2011](#ben-van-dusen-2011)

## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen 2011

Ben Van Dusen, Mike Ross, and Valerie Otero. (2011). Changing Identities and Evolving Conceptions of Inquiry through Teacher-Driven Professional Development. [doi:10.1063/1.3680073](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680073)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding of lesson-sharing reflections from four teachers against the five NSES essential features showed increasing references across the five lessons; "By the fifth lesson, however, four of the five essential features were referenced." Only Connect was never referenced.

> "As shown in Figure 2, the frequency with which the teacher participants characterized the shared lessons' inquiry-orientation using the five essential features of inquiry increased over the course of the five lessons."
