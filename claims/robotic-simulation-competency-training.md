`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Trial by De Groote et al. (2022): 36 junior residents from 16 training centers randomized to progressive competency-based robotic training or traditional training. The review reports "the PBP group was ~ 10 times more likely to demonstrate competence [P = 0.006]."

> "In the TT group, 3/18 achieved competence, compared with 12/18 in the PBP group (i.e., the PBP group was ~ 10 times more likely to demonstrate competence [P = 0.006])."
