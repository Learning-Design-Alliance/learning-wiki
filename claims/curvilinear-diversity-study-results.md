## Evidence

### Schelfhout Stijn 2019

Schelfhout Stijn, Wille Bart, Fonteyne Lot, Roels Elisabeth, De Fruyt Filip, Duyck Wouter. (2019). The effects of vocational interest on study results: Student person – environment fit and program interest diversity. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0214618

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Program-level (curvi-)linear regressions of average GPA and PASS rate on interest diversity across the 39 open-access programs, reported in the Results for research question 2. The curvilinear terms were "significant with high levels of explained variance" (GPA R2 = .29; PASS R2 = .44) while the linear terms were not significant (GPA p = .43; PASS p = .09).

> "The curvilinear regressions of study results on interest diversity however, were significant with high levels of explained variance, F(2,36) = 7.19, p = .002, R2 = .29 and F(2,35) = 13.84, p < .001, R2 = .44 for GPA and PASS respectively."
