## Evidence

### Gerd Kortemeyer 2014

Gerd Kortemeyer. (2014). An Empirical Study of the Eﬀect of Granting Multiple Tries for Online Homework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.2276](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.2276)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Homework performance separated by students above or below the median of combined exam scores, for the 20- and 99-tries semesters (Fig. 5). The article reports "no discernible diﬀerence in the slopes of the exponential ﬁts" between groups after initial tries.

> "However, there is no discernible diﬀerence in the slopes of the exponential ﬁts; after the initial tries, both groups have similar “decay rates”λs andλa for both success and giving up, i.e., there is no diﬀerence in how they are making use of later tries."
