# Use of the DRDP assessment in TK increased notably to 34% of LEAs while LEA-developed assessment use fell from 66% to 44%

## Evidence

### Wang 2026

Wang, V. (2026). California's universal prekindergarten implementation, 2024–25. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/540.887

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results on TK assessments (Table 5). LEA-developed assessments remained most common but fell from 66% in 2022–23 to 44% in 2024–25; about 1 in 10 LEAs used no common TK assessment. The DRDP is an observational assessment across multiple domains.

> "Use of the DRDP, on the other hand, has increased notably statewide, with over one third of LEAs (34%) using it in TK in 2024–25."
