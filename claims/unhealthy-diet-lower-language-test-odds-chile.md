## Evidence

### Correa-Burrows P 2016

Correa-Burrows P, Burrows R, Blanco E, Reyes M, Gahagan S. (2016). Nutritional quality of diet and academic performance in Chilean students. Bulletin of the World Health Organization. https://doi.org/10.2471/BLT.15.161315

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of a prospective Chilean cohort (n = 395) linking diet quality at age 16 to language test performance. In the fully adjusted Model 4 the language-test odds ratio for unhealthy versus healthy diet was "0.42 (0.18–0.98)" (Table 2).

> "Compared to participants with healthy diets, those with unhealthy diets were significantly less likely to perform well in language (OR: 0.40; 95% CI: 0.18–0.89) and mathematics (OR: 0.42; 95% CI: 0.19–0.88)"
