## Evidence

### Rachel Freed 2022

Rachel Freed, David McKinnon, Michael Fitzgerald, and Christina M. Norris. (2022). Development and validation of an astronomy self-efficacy instrument for understanding and doing. Physical Review Physics Education Research 18, 010117. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.18.010117

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

One-way ANOVA on a random sample of 58 cases from cohort A (robotic-telescope lab course) matched to cohort B's 58 nonlab students, with full-Bonferroni-protected p below 0.025. The printed effect size is "Cohen’s d ¼ 2.28"; descriptive means were 43.793 (lab) versus 20.293 (nonlab) out of 50.

> "Table VIII demonstrates that there is a highly significant difference in the ISE scores of cohorts A and B [Fð1; 114Þ¼ 221.100, p ≪ 0.0001] with a very large effect size of greater than 2 σ (Cohen’s d ¼ 2.28)."
