Phonological Awareness Training 2012¶

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Systematic WWC review of four RCTs meeting standards without reservations, totaling 78 children with disabilities in preschool settings. Table 1 reports a rating of potentially positive effects, an improvement index averaging +13 (range –16 to +46), and a "Small" extent of evidence.

"Phonological awareness training was found to have potentially positive effects on communication/language competencies for children with learning disabilities in early education settings."
