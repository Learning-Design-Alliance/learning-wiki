Zachariah 2025 (2)¶

Zachariah, I., Peters Hinton, V., Ruiz, P., Dallas, D., Liberman, B., & Karim, S. (2025). AI Literacy in Postsecondary Education. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/270

q2 · i? · review · r1

Policy analysis of the top 100 universities' ChatGPT policies, described in the brief as showing "cautious openness" and delegation of course-level AI decisions to educators. Appendix A adds that none of these universities banned AI use.

"the tone that top postsecondary institutions are setting is one of cautious openness about AI implementation, according to an analysis of the top 100 universities’ policies on the use of ChatGPT, with a majority of universities leaving decision-making on AI use in their courses to educators"
