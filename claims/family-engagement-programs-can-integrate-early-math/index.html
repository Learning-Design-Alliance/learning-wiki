Harris 2017¶

Harris, B., Petersen, D., & Wulsin, C. S. (2017). Integrating Mathematical Thinking Into Family Engagement Programs. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/integrating-mathematical-thinking-into-family-engagement-programs

q2 · i? · design · r?

This is the brief's summary finding from the implemented projects of the Heising-Simons Foundation grantees, who "developed and implemented a rich array of early math projects in a variety of settings." No effect sizes or evaluation statistics are printed.

"Their efforts show that despite the challenges faced by practitioners and the families they serve, there is room to integrate mathematical thinking and activities across a range of family engagement programs."
