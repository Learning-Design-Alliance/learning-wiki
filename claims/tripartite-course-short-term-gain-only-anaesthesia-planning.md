### Qin Z 2026

Impact of a tripartite framework on perioperative clinical reasoning in junior anaesthesiology residents in China: a longitudinal quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08706-w

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Short-term follow-up of the same quasi-experimental study (n = 45 experimental, n = 37 control), reported in Results. The experimental group "significantly outperformed the control group only in anaesthesia planning" with d = 0.47; Table 1 shows MCQ d = 0.11 (p = 0.6265) and oral exam d = 0.42 (p = 0.0602), both non-significant.

> "In the short-term assessment (3–4 months post-course), the experimental group significantly outperformed the control group only in anaesthesia planning (64.2 ± 5.8 vs. 60.8 ± 8.5, p < 0.05, d = 0.47), with no statistically significant differences observed in MCQs or oral examination scores during this short-term follow-up."
