# PAL lessons elicit moderate-to-vigorous heart-rate responses (73% HRmax) versus very light-to-light intensity in NAL lessons (51% HRmax)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean lesson intensity was 73% HRmax during PAL versus 51% HRmax during NAL, a significant physiological effect. [→ Bacon Paul 2021](#bacon-paul-2021)

## Evidence

### Bacon Paul 2021

Bacon Paul, Lord Rachel N. (2021). The impact of physically active learning during the school day on children's physical activity levels, time on task and learning behaviours and academic outcomes. Health Education Research. https://doi.org/10.1093/her/cyab020

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Heart-rate monitoring (Polar RS400) in a subset of eight participants across all PAL and NAL lessons; intensity expressed as percentage of predicted HRmax (Astrand model). The PAL response was significantly higher (P < 0.001); no effect size printed.

> "There was a significant physiological effect (P < 0.001) of PAL activities inducing a higher HR response (152.12 ± 10.569 b.p.m.) than NAL activities (101.96 ± 12.994 b.p.m.)."
