## Evidence

### Joore Ivo Kim 2018

Joore Ivo Kim, van Bergen Jan E. A. M. J, ter Riet Gerben, van der Maat A., van Dijk N. (2018). Development and evaluation of a blended educational programme for general practitioners' trainers to stimulate proactive HIV testing. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-018-0723-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired pre/post questionnaires from 74 GP trainers; knowledge items analysed with McNemar tests, attitude items with Wilcoxon signed rank tests. For example, correct answers on late presentation rose from 42.9% to 72.9% (p = 0.000); attitude items showed "minimal to no significant differences".

> "The proportion of GP trainers that answered the knowledge-related questions correctly was significantly higher after the programme for every question"
