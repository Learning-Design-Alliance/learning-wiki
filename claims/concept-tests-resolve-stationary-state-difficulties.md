## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students Understanding of Quantum Measurement Part 2: Development of Research-based Learning Tools. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06926](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06926)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Percentage-correct analysis of Test A Q1 (whether a wavefunction is an energy eigenstate): 13% comparison group, 67% experimental pre-test after concept tests, 87% experimental post-test after the QuILT (15 students per column). The authors attribute the gain to the in-class concept tests.

> "From Q1 in Test A and Q2 in Test B, we observe that the concept test s in class resolved many of the students’ diﬃculties related to the diﬀerence between the stationary states and the eigenstates of the operators corresponding to any physical observables."
