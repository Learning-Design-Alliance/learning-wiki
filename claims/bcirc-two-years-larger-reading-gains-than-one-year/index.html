q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Subgroup findings from the Calderón, Hertz-Lazarowitz, and Slavin (1998) quasi-experiment: students with two years of BCIRC scored 36.83 versus 23.83 in comparison, with a printed effect size of 0.87 ("0.87 Statistically significant +31"). The WWC notes a clustering correction did not change this significance.

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Subgroup findings from the same quasi-experiment: students with one year of BCIRC scored 28.83 versus 23.83 in comparison, with a printed effect size of 0.33 ("0.33 ns +13"). The WWC reports this finding became nonsignificant after correcting for clustering.
