q1 · i? · review · r?

The review's interpretive analysis of its classification results argues the pattern "is unlikely to be attributed to technological limitations" because the same models underpin all five types; the distribution instead reflects which entanglements have become institutionally legitimate. This is the authors' theoretical interpretation, not a tested result.

"This pattern is unlikely to be attributed to technological limitations, as the same large language models (e.g., ChatGPT) that underpin Assistant and Enabler applications are also fully capable of supporting Generator, Reimaginator, or Synthesizer functions."
