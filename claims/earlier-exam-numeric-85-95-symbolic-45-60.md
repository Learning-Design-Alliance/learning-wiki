## Evidence

### Eugene T. Torigoe 2011

Eugene T. Torigoe. (2011). How numbers help students solve physics problems. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1112.3229](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1112.3229)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Reported from the author's earlier final-exam study comparing numeric and symbolic question pairs in a large introductory physics course; the article cites these as the ranges motivating the interview design. No effect size is printed.

> "In that earlier work the numeric versions had an average score between 85-95%, while the symbolic versions had an average score between 45-60%."
