# Work-related factors (organizational learning culture, job control, job demands) are significantly positively related to SRL strategies in continuing education

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` SRL strategies show moderate positive relationships with organizational learning culture (r = .26) and job control (r = .28), and a weak to moderate relationship with job demands (r = .21) in CE. [→ Yvonne M. Hemmler 2024](#yvonne-m-hemmler-2024)

## Evidence

### Yvonne M. Hemmler 2024

Yvonne M. Hemmler, Dirk Ifenthaler. (2024). Self-regulated learning strategies in continuing education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100629

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Pooled three-level random-effects meta-analyses of work-related factors across the included CE studies. The review reports "a weak to moderate relationship for job demands (r= .21) and moderate relationships for organizational learning culture (r= .26) and job control (r= .28)" with SRL strategies.

> "The average correlation coefficients indicated a weak to moderate relationship for job demands (r= .21) and moderate relationships for organizational learning culture (r= .26) and job control (r= .28)."
