## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Comparing pre- and post-course TSSI survey scores for 13 students yielded a small positive change (Δ = 0.07) that was not statistically significant (p = 0.170, Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test). [→ Christine O'Donnell 2020](#christine-odonnell-2020)

## Evidence

### Christine O'Donnell 2020

Christine O'Donnell, Edward Prather, & Peter Behroozi. (2020). Making Science Personal: Inclusivity-Driven Design for General-Education Courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.10218](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.10218)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre- and post-course survey using 25 items from the Thinking About Science Survey Instrument (TSSI), with coding adjusted for four items; 13 students responded to both surveys. The overall average moved from 0.63 to 0.70; the change was not statistically significant.

> "Comparing students' average pre-course full survey score and average post-course survey score, we see a small positive change (∆ = 0.07) with p = 0.170 from a Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test."
