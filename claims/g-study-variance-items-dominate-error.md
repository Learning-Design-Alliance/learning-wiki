## Evidence

### Talbot 2017

Talbot, R. M., III. (2017). A Multi-Faceted Approach to Scrutinizing the Reliability of a Measure of STEM Teacher Strategic Knowledge. University of Colorado Denver. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1702.06592](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1702.06592)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

G Study variance-component estimation (p x i x r design) using GENOVA on 60 respondents' FA scores, reported with Table 4 and Figure 7. The article finds "a very large portion of the variance in observed scores (almost 60%) is in the person by item (p x i) interaction."

> "While relatively little variance is attributable to the items themselves, a very large portion of the variance in observed scores (almost 60%) is in the person by item (p x i) interaction."
