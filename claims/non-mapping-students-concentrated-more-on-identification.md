## Evidence

### Mochizuki 2019

Mochizuki, T., Nishimori, T., Tsubakimoto, M., Oura, H., Sato, T., Johansson, H., Nakahara, J., & Yamauchi, Y. (2019). Development of software to support argumentative reading and writing by means of creating a graphic organizer from an electronic text. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09676-1

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Post-lesson 4-point Likert usability questionnaire administered to both classes. The t test showed the non-knowledge-mapping class concentrated more on identification, which the authors attribute to their strategy being limited to underlining plus reading.

> "The non-knowledge-mapping students concentrated more on identifying the author's argument than the knowledge-mapping students (t (61) = − 2.107, p = .039, d = 0.520)"
