## Evidence

### Klingenberg 2024

Klingenberg, S., Bosse, R., Mayer, R. E., & Makransky, G. (2024). Does Embodiment in Virtual Reality Boost Learning Transfer? Testing an Immersion-Interactivity Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09956-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the 2x2 between-subjects experiment, 177 high school students learned a pipetting lesson in immersive VR (HMD) or on a PC screen. The article reports that VR conditions showed "significantly higher levels of presence, agency, location, body ownership, and embodied learning compared to participants in the PC conditions." No effect sizes are printed for these differences.

> "Results demonstrated that students in the VR conditions experienced significantly higher levels of presence, agency, location, body ownership, and embodied learning compared to participants in the PC conditions."
