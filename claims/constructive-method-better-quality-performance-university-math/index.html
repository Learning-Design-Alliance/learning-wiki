Evidence¶

Voskoglou 2019¶

Voskoglou, M. (2019). Comparing Teaching Methods of Mathematics at University Level. Education Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci9030204

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Classroom experiment with two engineering departments at the Graduate T.E.I. of Western Greece; D1 received the explicit method, D2 the constructive method. GPA index computed from post-test linguistic grades shows "the experimental group demonstrated a better-quality performance than the control group" (2.17 vs 1.55).

"Replacing the data of Table 1 with those of Equation (1), one ﬁnds the values GPA = 1.55 for D1 and GPA = 2.17 for D2. Therefore, the experimental group demonstrated a better-quality performance than the control group."
