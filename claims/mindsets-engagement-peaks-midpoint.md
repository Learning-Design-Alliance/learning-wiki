### Gage 2025

Gage, N. (2025). The impact of the Mindsets mathematics program on student engagement, ability to reason, and repeated reasoning. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677579

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Three-time-point CLMM on the engagement dataset with ordered time (beginning, middle, end) and random intercepts for classroom and challenge. The linear trend was also significant (b = 1.24, SE = 0.17, z = 7.33, p < .001), and Figure 1 shows "the highest latent engagement at the Interim phase, followed by a moderate drop at post."

> "the significant negative quadratic effect (b = –1.46, SE = 0.17, z = –8.59, p < .001) suggests that engagement peaked at Interim and declined slightly by post"
