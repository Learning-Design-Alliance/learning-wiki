## Evidence

### Ranjbar Fataneh 2024

Ranjbar Fataneh, Sharif-Nia Hamid, Shiri Maryam, Rahmatpour Pardis. (2024). The effect of spaced E-Learning on knowledge of basic life support and satisfaction of nursing students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05533-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental two-group study of 106 first-year nursing students; intervention (n=59) received three one-hour spaced sessions, control (n=47) one three-hour massed session. Results report "the intervention group showing a higher increase" in BLS knowledge after training.

> "Post-training, knowledge scores improved in both groups, with the intervention group showing a higher increase."
