## Evidence

### Şengör K 2025

Şengör K, Alptekin HM, Kara Ö, Görücü R, Ayoğlu T, Akyüz N. (2025). Assessment tool for surgical hand washing skills: a scale development study. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-03192-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-observer agreement analysis in which two trained instructors simultaneously observed and separately scored each of 105 nursing students' handwashing performances. The article reports the total-score ICC was "calculated as 0.951 (p < 0.001)" with the lowest item ICC at 0.795, which the authors characterize as a very high and reliable level of agreement; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "the lowest ICC value for agreement between the two observers was 0.795, and the ICC for the total score was calculated as 0.951 (p < 0.001)"
