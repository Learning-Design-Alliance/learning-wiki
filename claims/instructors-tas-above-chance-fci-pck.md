## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Instructors (average 6.25) and graduate students (average 6.01) scored significantly better than random guessers (average 3.71, of a maximum of 9.21) on the FCI-related PCK task. [→ Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013](#alexandru-maries-and-chandralekha-singh-2013)

## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Physics Instructors and Teaching Assistants Using the Force Concept Inventory. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. Originally published 1995. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of 30 instructors and 25 graduate students in a TA training course against 24 simulated random guessers, scored out of a maximum of 9.21. The study reports instructors at "68% of the maximum possible" and graduate students at 65%, with both groups' p values versus random guessing less than 0.001.

> "the average of instructors (68% of the maximum possible FCI related PCK score) and the average of graduate students (65% of the maximum possible FCI related PCK score) are very close"
