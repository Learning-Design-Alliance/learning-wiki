Evidence¶

Andrea Roxana Bellot 2025¶

Andrea Roxana Bellot, Mar Gutiérrez-Colón Plana, Katarzyna Agnieszka Baran. (2025). Redefining Literature Education: The Role of ChatGPT in Undergraduate Courses. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00497-3

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Questionnaire item Q16 (Table 5) compared perceptions of whether the new ending appears sound and convincing in plot creation. The experimental group showed greater uncertainty, with "Maybe" responses at 38.10% versus 18.18% in the control group; percentages are descriptive with no test reported.

"The control group demonstrated a higher degree of confidence in the alternative ending's plausibility, with 68.18% affirming its soundness and convincing nature."
