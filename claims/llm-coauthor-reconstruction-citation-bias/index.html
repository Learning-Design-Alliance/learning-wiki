Yifan Liu 2026¶

Yifan Liu, Jaime Arguello, Orland Hoeber, Chang Liu, Soo Young Rieh, Luanne Sinnamon, et al. (2026). Report on CHIIR 2026 Workshop on Generative AI and Academic Search (GAI&AS). ACM SIGIR Forum. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08936

q2 · i? · associational · r?

A lightning-talk investigation using the Discoverable Name Extraction (DNE) set-based metric evaluated three LLMs (DeepSeek R1, Llama 4 Scout, Mixtral 8×7B) against bibliographic ground truth from OpenAlex and Google Scholar spanning 1,596 seed authors across 10 disciplines and 8 global regions. The report states "highly cited researchers are reconstructed by LLMs at approximately twice the rate of lower-cited peers". No effect size is printed.

"Their central finding is unambiguous: highly cited researchers are reconstructed by LLMs at approximately twice the rate of lower-cited peers, a disparity that grows with model size and reflects the disproportionate repetition of prominent names in citation-heavy training corpora."
