Salah 2016¶

Dr. Raed Omar Salah. (2016). Effectiveness of Using Computerized Educational Packages in Teaching Math Curriculum on the Learning of Student in Eighth Grade at Ma'an City. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.12. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099555

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental two-way ANCOVA of post-exam scores for 109 eighth-grade students in two schools (one male, one female), with experimental groups taught via computerized packages and control groups taught traditionally for four weeks.
