## Evidence

### Dignath 2022

Dignath, C., Rimm-Kaufman, S., van Ewijk, R., & Kunter, M. (2022). Teachers' Beliefs About Inclusive Education and Insights on What Contributes to Those Beliefs: a Meta-analytical Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09695-0

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 102 papers (2000–2020) yielding 191 effect sizes from 40,898 teachers in 40 countries, with standardized mean scores computed for each belief component. The synthesis reports beliefs were "in the mid-range of scales, indicating room for growth."

> "On average, teachers' cognitive appraisals, emotional appraisals, and efficacy about inclusion were found to be in the mid-range of scales, indicating room for growth."
