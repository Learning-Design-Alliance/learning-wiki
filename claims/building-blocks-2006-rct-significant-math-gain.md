## Evidence

### SRA Real Math Building Blocks PreK 2007

SRA Real Math Building Blocks PreK. (2007). WWC Intervention Report. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.gse.buffalo.edu/org/buildingblocks/

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with 28 teachers and 202 children from low- to mixed-income families in New York State; teachers randomly assigned to intervention or business-as-usual. Appendix A3 reports means of 61.69 versus 53.22, "8.47" mean difference, effect size 1.06, statistically significant, improvement index +36.

> "Clements and Sarama (2006) analyzed group differ-ences between the Building Blocks for Math intervention group and the business-as-usual comparison group on one math out-come measure (Early Mathematics Assessment). The difference between groups was statistically significant and favored children in the Building Blocks for Math group."
