## Subclaims

## Evidence

### McGlashan H.L. 2017

McGlashan H.L., Blanchard C.C.V., Sycamore N.J., Lee R., French B., Holmes N.P. (2017). Improvement in children's fine motor skills following a computerized typing intervention. Human Movement Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.humov.2017.10.013

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-intervention serial reaction time task data from all 65 children. Sequence-condition slopes were −9.59 ± 2.51 ms/repetition versus +1.14 ± 3.31 ms/repetition for random; Z-scores also differed significantly (t(64) = 3.45, p = .001), indicating implicit sequence learning.

> "There was a significant difference between sequence and random conditions on the slopes of RT across repetitions t(64) = 2.49, p = .015. Learning slopes for the sequence condition were −9.59 ± 2.51 ms/repetition compared to the random condition, which was +1.14 ± 3.31 ms/repetition."
