Qarareh 2016¶

Qarareh, A. O. (2016). The Effect of Using the Constructivist Learning Model in Teaching Science on the Achievement and Scientific Thinking of 8th Grade Students. International Education Studies, 9(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n7p178

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of 136 eighth graders in two Jordanian schools; ANCOVA on post achievement scores favored the constructivist group (M=25.79 vs 23.06), with a significant teaching-method effect.

"Table 7 shows statistically significant differences at the level of (α=0.05) between the means of students' score on the post achievement attributed to the teaching method (Constructivist Learning Model, Traditional Method)"
