## Evidence

### Lepka Piotr 2026

Lepka Piotr, Szcześniak Dorota, Sikora Zofia, Matkowski Rafał. (2026). Effect of transcranial direct current stimulation on psychomotor skill acquisition during laparoscopic training: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12992-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized sham-controlled trial of 33 medical students without prior laparoscopic experience, randomized to active tDCS (n = 16) or sham (n = 17) during LASTT simulation training; ANCOVA adjusting for baseline performance and covariates showed a significant group effect for Bi T4, "F (1,13) = 14.92, p = 0.002".

> "Active tDCS was independently associated with higher final bimanual coordination performance compared with sham stimulation (adjusted ANCOVA: F (1,13) = 14.92, p = 0.002)."
