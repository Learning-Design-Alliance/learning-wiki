### Huang C-Y 2026

Huang C-Y, Ho S-W, Chen P-M, Chen C-C. (2026). Simulation-based ultrasound-guided lumbar puncture training using a flipped classroom: a single-arm pilot programme evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10070-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests and Wilcoxon signed-rank sensitivity analyses on needle-pass counts and procedure times for the seven residents who completed both OSCEs. No effect reached significance.

> "The number of needle passes (median 7 vs. 12; mean 5.57 ± 2.13 vs. 13.29 ± 9.22, p = 0.101; Wilcoxon p = 0.062) and procedure time (195 ± 100 vs. 315 ± 131 s, p = 0.180; Wilcoxon p = 0.219) both favoured the ultrasound-guided phase but did not reach statistical significance in this small paired sample."
