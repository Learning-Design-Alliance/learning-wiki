## Evidence

### Faure Theresa 2024

Faure Theresa, Weyers Imke, Voltmer Jan-Bennet, Westermann Jürgen, Voltmer Edgar. (2024). Test-reduced teaching for stimulation of intrinsic motivation (TRUST): a randomized controlled intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05640-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported knowledge estimates at t5 and t8 in the randomized study increased in all groups with no significant group differences; the article prints F statistics only, so no effect size is reported.

> "From t5 to t8 an increase in perceived knowledge was seen in all study groups (for medical knowledge, F(1, 112) = 30.38, p < 0.001, for anatomical knowledge, F(1, 130) = 39.10, p < 0.001). Differences between the intervention groups and the CG were not significant."

### Faure Theresa 2024 (2)

Faure Theresa, Weyers Imke, Voltmer Jan-Bennet, Westermann Jürgen, Voltmer Edgar. (2024). Test-reduced teaching for stimulation of intrinsic motivation (TRUST): a randomized controlled intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05640-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Perceived preparation for the written and oral parts of M1 rose from t5 to t8 equally across the three study conditions; the null group contrast was not accompanied by an effect size, so equivalence was not established.

> "Likewise, the perceived preparation for the exam increased from t5 to t8 (for the written exam, F(1, 120) = 10.96, p = 0.001, for the oral exam, F(1, 130) = 29.22, p < 0.001), but there was no difference in change over time between the study groups (data not shown)."
