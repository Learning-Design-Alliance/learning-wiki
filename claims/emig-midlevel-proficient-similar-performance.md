`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the discussion of the cohort trial results, noting that mid-level and proficient groups had similar times for each of the two hysteroscopic exercises and similar accuracy on several laparoscopic tasks. The mid-level residents came from simulation-oriented centers, which may not be representative.

> "The similar performance of the mid-level residents compared with the proficient cohort could reflect some selection bias. The mid-level residents were all from centers where there was an interest and experience in simulation-based medical education, usually with an established fellowship in minimally invasive gynecologic surgery program, which may not be representative."
