## Evidence

### Sudha R 2024

Sudha R, Kumar GG, Ratnajeet C, Tushar K, Kishlay KM, Das AK, Shekhar S. (2024). Comparative Analysis of Multidimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy: A Randomized Control Trial. Annals of African Medicine 23(3):459-465. https://doi.org/10.4103/aam.aam_214_23

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA and Bonferroni post hoc comparisons by question type in the same randomized trial (n = 270 completers). Table 3 prints tag-question means of 23.85 (plastic), 31.11 (dissection), and 31.17 (3D), with F = 367.68, P < 0.0001; MCQ, Bloom 1, and Bloom 2 showed the same pattern.

> "Tag questions showed a significantly higher score in dissections and 3D group compared to plastic model groups (P < 0.0001)."
