The paper reports, citing the 2015 Building a Grad Nation report, a projected shortfall of "310,000 students" against the 2020 graduation target, with 80 percent from low-income families, 20 percent English language learners, and 40 percent with learning and other differences. This is a cited projection, not a study conducted in the paper.
