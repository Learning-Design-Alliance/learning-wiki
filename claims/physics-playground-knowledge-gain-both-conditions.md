## Evidence

### Battipede 2025

Battipede, E., Giangualano, A., Boffi, P., Clerici, M., Calvi, A., Cassenti, L., Cialini, R., Van Den Weghe, T. L. A., Addimando, L., Lanzi, P. L., Gallace, A. (2025). Physics Playground: Insights from a Qualitative-Quantitative Study about VR-Based Learning. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-88881-6_8](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-88881-6_8)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quantitative study with 44 participants randomly assigned to VR (N=22) or slideshow (N=22), pre- and post-tests of 19 multiple-choice physics questions. The mixed ANOVA showed a "significant main effect of learning on test performance" with no between-condition difference.

> "A mixed ANOVA 2x2 found a significant main effect of learning on test performance (F(1, 42) = 98.80, p < .001) but no significant difference between conditions (F(1, 42) = 0.0188, p = 0.892)."
