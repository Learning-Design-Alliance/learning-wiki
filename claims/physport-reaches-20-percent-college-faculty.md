Demographic comparison of Verified Educator counts against AIP Statistical Research Center population surveys. The article estimates 13,000 U.S. college faculty total, 27,000 high school physics teachers, and 7,000 AAPT members, yielding the printed percentages in Table II.

> "PhysPort has 2,600 Verified Educators who are U.S. college faculty, and 85% of those who give their department name are in a physics-related department; thus, it is estimated that about 20% of U.S. college physics faculty are Verified Educators."
