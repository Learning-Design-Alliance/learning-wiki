Silaban et al. 2018¶

Wenny Silaban, Biner Ambarita & Usman Hadi. (2018). The Development and Implementation of Learning Material on Exposition Text to Improve Students' Achievement on Bahasa Indonesia. International Education Studies, 11(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n11p53

Quasi-experiment at SMA 5 Medan with four purposively selected classes, 30 analysed students per class after removing high pre-test outliers. The article reports post-test 1 means of "M=89.63±5.32" for experimental and "M=77.25±5.06" for control classes; no test statistic or effect size is printed.
