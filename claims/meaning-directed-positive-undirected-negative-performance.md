## Evidence

### Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche 2017

Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche. (2017). A Learning Patterns Perspective on Student Learning in Higher Education: State of the Art and Moving Forward. Educ Psychol Rev 29:269–299. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9414-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of studies on learning patterns and outcomes (e.g., Vermunt 2005; Martínez-Fernández and Vermunt 2015; Donche et al. 2014; Vanthournout et al. 2012) reports that "Undirected learning is consistently and negatively related to academic performance in all disciplines".

> "Meaning-directed learning is generally positively related to various indicators of academic performance, in different academic disciplines. Reproduction-directed learning mainly shows negative relations with exam performance."
