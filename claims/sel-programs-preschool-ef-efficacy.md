Synthesis of eight teacher-led SEL studies (average sample 296.13, the largest of all approaches). The review reports "Six of the studies (75.0%)" had at least one significant outcome and 11 of 23 effects (47.8%) were significant; four interventions were classified as high efficacy, all conducted in 2020.
