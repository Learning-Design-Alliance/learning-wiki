## Evidence

### Pape-Lindstrom 2018

Pape-Lindstrom, P., Eddy, S., & Freeman, S. (2018). Reading Quizzes Improve Exam Scores for Community College Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-08-0160

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal quasi-experiment comparing three control terms (2009-2011) with three experimental terms (2012-2015) of the same instructor's introductory biology course; multiple regression with Treatment estimate 0.049, p = 0.0002. The article reports "earned 4.9% more total exam points" for treatment students.

> "On the basis of the model, students with equivalent non-biology GPAs who are in the treatment group earned 4.9% more total exam points, on average, than a student with the same college GPA in the control group."
