The effect of combining analogy-based simulation and laboratory activities on Turkish elementary school students' understanding of simple electric circuits. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(4). https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojet

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' informal classroom observation during the teaching implementations, reported without any measured motivation data or test; the article offers no systematic evidence for this observation.

"it was also observed during the teaching implementations that the students using analogy-based simulations were more motivated for the course, their attention was not distracted and even the student with low level of success were quite eager to participate into the course."
