## Evidence

### Zhou Yuanyuan 2016

Zhou Yuanyuan, Graham Lori, West Courtney. (2016). The relationship between study strategies and academic performance. Int J Med Educ 7:324-332. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.57dc.fe0f

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlations from the 168-student cohort (Table 1). First-measure correlations were weaker: only "Test Strategies" was positively correlated with Year I academic performance (r(155) = .172, p < .05). The largest printed second-measure correlation, r = .278, falls in the .20-.36 range (medium impact).

> "With the second LASSI measure, five subscales were statistically significantly correlated with Year II academic performance. They included: Concentration (r(139) = .173, p < .05), Motivation (r (140) = .218, p < .01), Selecting Main Ideas (r(139) = .278, p < .01), Time Management (r(139) = .213, p < .05), and Test Strategies (r(140) = .249, p < .01)."
