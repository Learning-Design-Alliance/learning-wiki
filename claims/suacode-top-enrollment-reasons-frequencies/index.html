q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Frequency distribution of codes from the grounded-theory analysis of 450 randomly sampled responses, shown in Figure 1 and reported in the results text. The percentages are descriptive frequencies; no inferential test or effect size is printed.

"Figure 1 shows that 20.3% of the students from the 450 analyzed enrolled because they are interested in learning to code, 19.2% applied so they could impact and contribute to building and developing society and 12.0% want to develop new skills, and experience for advanced work."
