### Kou 2025

Kou, H., Luo, W., Li, X. et al. (2025). Mindfulness training enhances face working memory: evidence from the drift-diffusion model. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00389-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

DDM nondecision-time improvements in the randomized experiment: at T2 the training group showed larger t improvements for fearful (Cohen's d=.57) and neutral (d=.68) faces; at T3 only the neutral face remained significant (t(118)=3.11, p=.012, d=.57). The authors conclude t is not central to the cognitive mechanism of mindfulness training.
