# Engaging with the QuILT substantially improves student performance on quantum computing concepts, with large effect sizes on difficult questions about N vs 2^N distinctions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r3` · `q2` · `i2`–`i3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Physics students improved substantially on questions about N vs 2^N distinctions after the tutorial, e.g., Question 4a from 30% to 81% (d = 1.32) and Question 4b from 54% to 97% (d = 1.34). [→ Hu 2024](#hu-2024)
`q2 i2` QCQI students also improved substantially, e.g., Question 6 from 39% to 83% (d = 0.78) and Question 4a from 50% to 86% (d = 0.70). [→ Hu 2024 (2)](#hu-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Hu 2024

Hu, P., Li, Y., & Singh, C. (2024). Investigating and improving student understanding of the basics of quantum computing. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020108)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r3`

Matched pre/post data for N = 35 physics majors in the QM course, graded with inter-rater reliability greater than 90%. Table 2 shows large effect sizes on Questions 4a-4c about N vs 2^N distinctions, e.g., "4a 30% 81% 0.73 1.32", with Cohen's d = 1.32, d = 1.34 and d = 1.17 and normalized gains of 0.73-0.94.

> "4a 30% 81% 0.73 1.32 4b 54% 97% 0.94 1.34 4c 53% 89% 0.76 1.17"

### Hu 2024 (2)

Hu, P., Li, Y., & Singh, C. (2024). Investigating and improving student understanding of the basics of quantum computing. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020108)

`q2 · i2` · `design · r3`

Matched pre/post data for N = 18 students in the interdisciplinary Foundations of Quantum Computing course. Table 3 shows medium-to-large effect sizes on Questions 4a-4c, e.g., "4a 50% 86% 0.72 0.70", with Cohen's d = 0.70, d = 0.55 and d = 0.76 and normalized gains of 0.71-0.81.

> "4a 50% 86% 0.72 0.70 89% 4b 61% 89% 0.71 0.55 89% 4c 56% 92% 0.81 0.76 87%"
