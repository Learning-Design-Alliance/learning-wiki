## Evidence

### Blohm M. 2015

Blohm M., Lauter J., Branchereau S., Krautter M., Köhl-Hackert N., Jünger J., Herzog W., Nikendei C. (2015). "Peer-Assisted Learning"(PAL) in the Skills-Lab – an inventory at the medical faculties of the Federal Republic of Germany. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000952](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000952)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of administrative framework with data from 33 faculties. The article reports tutors "employed up to 5 years" at an "average payment of 9.2 € per hour" (range 7–17 €); 30 faculties had physician involvement in management, 3 were purely student-run, and funding came from faculty resources (20) or tuition/quality-assurance funds (7).

> "The tutors were employed up to 5 years at the various locations with an average payment of 9.2 € per hour (range 7 - 17 € per hour; 26 contacts gave details on this aspect; 26/33; 78.7%)."
