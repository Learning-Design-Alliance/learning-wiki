# Four-year college graduation rates remained flat at about 49 percent for direct four-year enrollees, with much lower rates for two-year (8 percent) and delayed enrollees (4 percent)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The six-year bachelor's completion rate for CPS graduates who enrolled directly in a four-year college stayed at about 49 percent, while immediate two-year enrollees graduated at 8 percent and non-immediate enrollees at 4 percent. [→ Nagaoka 2018](#nagaoka-2018)

Descriptive trend analysis shown in Figure 6 for high school graduating classes of 2003–2010, using NSC data. The brief reports 8 percent completion for direct two-year enrollees and 4 percent for graduates who did not enroll directly.
