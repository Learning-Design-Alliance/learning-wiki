`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

A student in the FGD data stated "lecturers can supervise and assess our competencies concurrently as they provide feedback," contrasting this with current teaching methods that miss this opportunity.

> "With simulation, lecturers can supervise and assess our competencies concurrently as they provide feedback, while we evaluate ourselves on how we have performed to perfect our skills"

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

A clinical instructor in the interview data explained that students who grasp desired outcomes "will act in accordance with the given instructions and demonstrate their acquired knowledge or skills," allowing lecturer assessment and student self-assessment.

> "Simulation is very effective in assessing and evaluating knowledge and skill acquisition."
