## Evidence

### Park Kye-Yeung 2019

Park Kye-Yeung, Park Hoon-Ki, Hwang Hwan-Sik. (2019). Group randomized trial of teaching tobacco-cessation counseling to senior medical students: a peer role-play module versus a standardized patient module. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1668-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Group-randomized trial of 113 fourth-year medical students randomized by clerkship rotation order to an SP or RP module, each lasting three-and-a-half hours. Paired t-tests showed significant pre-to-post OSCE gains with printed effect sizes d = 0.87 (SP group) and d = 0.77 (RP group), both p < 0.001.

> "In both groups, post-intervention OSCE scores increased significantly compared to baseline (Cohen’s d 0.87, p < 0.001 in SP group; d 0.77, p < 0.001 in RP group)."
