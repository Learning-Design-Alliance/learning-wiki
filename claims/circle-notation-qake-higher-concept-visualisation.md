# Experts rate the Circle Notation and Qake higher than the Bloch sphere and Quantum Bead for visualising quantum measurement, superposition and probabilistics

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Expert ratings of how adequately representations visualise quantum measurement, superposition and probabilistics differ significantly, with the Circle Notation and Qake rated higher on average (pairwise d = 0.42–0.51). [→ Qerimi 2025](#qerimi-2025)

## Evidence

### Qerimi 2025

Qerimi, L., Malone, S., Rexigel, E., Mehlhase, S., Kuhn, J., Küchemann, S. (2025). Exploring the mechanisms of qubit representations and introducing a new category system for visual representations: Results from expert ratings. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1)

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Expert rating of concept adequacy (quantum measurement, superposition, probabilistics); Friedman test significant (χ2(3) = 37.258, p < .001). The entanglement concept was excluded from this category due to rater disagreement.

> "This difference was found to exist mainly between the Circle Notation and the Bloch sphere ( d = 0.43), the Quantum Bead and the Circle Notation ( d = 0.42), Qake and the Bloch sphere ( d = 0.48), and Qake and the Quantum Bead ( d = 0.51). Circle Notation (mean: 4 .02 ± 1.07) and Qake (mean: 3 .88 ± 1.05) were rated higher on average."
