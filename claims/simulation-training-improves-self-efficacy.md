Watters Colm, Reedy Gabriel, Ross Alastair, Morgan Nicola J, Handslip Rhodri, Jaye Peter. (2015). Does interprofessional simulation increase self-efficacy: a comparative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2014-005472

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Precourse/postcourse questionnaire study of 187 participants (115 nurses/midwives, 57 doctors) at the SaIL Centre; paired t test showed "a mean shift in confidence from 63% (SD 14.6) before training to 77% (SD 12.3) after training".
