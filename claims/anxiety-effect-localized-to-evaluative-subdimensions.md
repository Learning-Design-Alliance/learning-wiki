Parallel four-step regressions refitted for each of the five AI literacy subdimensions on the same sample. The article concludes the positive residual effect "arises entirely from the two evaluative dimensions and is absent from the three performance-oriented dimensions", suggesting anxiety operates through heightened vigilance rather than operational skill.

> "Its positive association was confined to critical evaluation (β = 0.14, p < 0.05) and ethical competence (β = 0.20, p < 0.001) and was non-significant for technical proficiency, communication proficiency, and creative application."
