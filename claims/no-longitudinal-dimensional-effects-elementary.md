# Longitudinal dimensional comparison effects on elementary school students' ability self-concepts do not occur from Grades 2 to 4

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Longitudinal dimensional effects of grades (achievement → noncorresponding self-concept) were absent across both 4-month and 1-year intervals in Grades 2–4. [→ Weidinger 2019](#weidinger-2019)
`q3 i?` Longitudinal dimensional effects of self-concepts (self-concept → noncorresponding grade) were weak and mostly nonsignificant across both intervals. [→ Weidinger 2019](#weidinger-2019)

## Evidence

### Weidinger 2019

Weidinger, A. F., Steinmayr, R., & Spinath, B. (2019). Ability Self-Concept Formation in Elementary School: No Dimensional Comparison Effects Across Time. Developmental Psychology, 55(5), 1005–1018. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0000695

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent cross-lagged panel analysis (RI/E model) of 540 German students assessed seven times over 24 months found no dimensional effects of grades on noncorresponding self-concepts: "(⫺.07 ⱕ ␥ ⱕ ⫺.01, p ⱕ .911" at 4-month intervals. The 1-year interval model likewise showed "no significant negative or positive effects".

> "There were no longitudinal dimensional effects of grades for the 4-months intervals before the middle of Grade 3, and— contrary to expectations—not afterward either (⫺.07 ⱕ ␥ ⱕ ⫺.01, p ⱕ .911"
