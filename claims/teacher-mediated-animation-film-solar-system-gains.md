# Teacher-mediated learning activity with an animation film produces greater gains in Solar System knowledge than individual viewing alone in first-graders

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental group's mean score increased significantly from 4.96 at Test 1 to 8.15 at Test 3 after reviewing the film and completing the teacher-led activity. [→ Vereș 2020](#veres-2020)
`q2 i?` The experimental group's progress after the mediated activity (2.11) exceeded its progress after viewing alone (1.08) and the control group's viewing-only progress (0.60). [→ Vereș 2020 (2)](#veres-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Vereș 2020

Vereș, S. & Magdaș, I. (2020). The Use of Animation Film in Forming Representations about the Planet Earth and the Solar System. Romanian Review of Geographical Education, 9(1), 38-59. https://doi.org/10.23741/RRGE120183

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental action-research with 27 first-graders split into an experimental group (13) and control group (14), matched on initial-test averages. The EG mean rose from 4.96 to 8.15 after the teacher-mediated activity; the article reports "a significant increase" without a test statistic.

> "There is a significant increase in the average of the scores obtained by the EG in the three tests, from 4.96 in Test 1 to 8.15 in Test 3."

### Vereș 2020 (2)

Vereș, S. & Magdaș, I. (2020). The Use of Animation Film in Forming Representations about the Planet Earth and the Solar System. Romanian Review of Geographical Education, 9(1), 38-59. https://doi.org/10.23741/RRGE120183

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of progress between Test 1 and Test 2 for both groups after individual film viewing, reported in Table 2 of the results section. The EG gained 1.08 points versus 0.60 for the CG; the article prints no effect size for this contrast.

> "Table 2 shows a greater progress in the EG (1.08) than in the CG (0.60)."
