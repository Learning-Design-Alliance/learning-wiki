## Evidence

### Alfonsi Valentina 2020

Alfonsi Valentina, Scarpelli Serena, D'Atri Aurora, Stella Giacomo, De Gennaro Luigi. (2020). Later School Start Time: The Impact of Sleep on Academic Performance and Health in the Adolescent Population. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17072574

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of delayed-start studies, outcomes mostly estimated by sleep habits survey, reports a significant delay in wake time; it adds that "later rise time varies from a minimum of 21 to a maximum of 61 min".

> "As expected, a significant delay in wake time of students with later start times was observed in an overwhelming majority of studies"

### Alfonsi Valentina 2020 (2)

Alfonsi Valentina, Scarpelli Serena, D'Atri Aurora, Stella Giacomo, De Gennaro Luigi. (2020). Later School Start Time: The Impact of Sleep on Academic Performance and Health in the Adolescent Population. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17072574

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing its reference [106], that grade level did not predict variation in sleep gains, "suggesting the maintenance of effect regardless of the specific school grade". No effect size is printed.

> "The grade level in school (elementary school, middle school, high school) was not a predictor of any variations in the magnitude of increased sleep [106], suggesting the maintenance of effect regardless of the specific school grade."
