`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Related samples t-test on the 18 test-group students' SAS scores before and after drama instruction; the article reports the posttest mean of 36.94 corresponds to very low anxiety, with "a significant difference between pretest and posttest scores favoring posttest results (p: .000 ˂ 0.05; t: 10.162)."

> "Statistical analysis showed that there was a significant difference between pretest and posttest scores favoring posttest results (p: .000 ˂ 0.05; t: 10.162). The finding demonstrates that drama instruction method had a significant effect on students' speech anxiety."
