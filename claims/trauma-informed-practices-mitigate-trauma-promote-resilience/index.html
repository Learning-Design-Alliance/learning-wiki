Pate 2020¶

Pate, C. (2020). Strategies for trauma-informed distance learning. Center to Improve Social and Emotional Learning and School Safety at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED606091

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The brief asserts, citing background literature on trauma and adverse childhood experiences, that trauma-informed practices mitigate trauma effects and promote resilience. The brief offers no empirical data or study of its own for this assertion.

"Trauma-informed practices — such as creating safe and supportive learning environments and helping students develop consistent, positive relationships with peers and adults — are powerful ways to mitigate the effects of trauma and to promote resilience in children and youth."
