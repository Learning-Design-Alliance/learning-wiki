## Evidence

### Eric Brewe 2012

Eric Brewe, Laird Kramer, and Vashti Sawtelle. (2012). Investigating student communities with network analysis of interactions in a physics learning center. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 8, 010101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.8.010101

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Hierarchical multiple regression on Bonacich centrality from a 99-student collaboration network, run in UCINET with bootstrap methods. Model 1 (days per week) gave "R2 ¼ 0:301, Fð1; 106Þ¼ 41:81, p< 0:001"; model 2 adding major gave R2 = 0.482 with a 2.11 standard deviation improvement in correlation.

> "after entering days per week in PLC in the equation, the model significantly predicted the observed data (see model 1 in Table III), R2 ¼ 0:301, Fð1; 106Þ¼ 41:81, p< 0:001"
