## Evidence

### James Pengelley · Peter R. Whipp · Nina Rovis-Hermann 2023

James Pengelley · Peter R. Whipp · Nina Rovis-Hermann. (2023). A Testing Load: Investigating Test Mode Effects on Test Score, Cognitive Load and Scratch Paper Use with Secondary School Students. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09781-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The mental-effort hierarchical linear model showed "Students invested more effort in paper questions (M = 4.08, 95% CI = 3.19, 4.96) compared to computer questions (M = 3.71, 95% = 2.83, 4.59)"; post-hoc comparisons were significant for easy (p < 0.001) and difficult (p = 0.039) questions, with a nonsignificant interaction (p = 0.08).
