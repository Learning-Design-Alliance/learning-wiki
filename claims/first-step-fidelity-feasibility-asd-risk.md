Education and Training in Autism and Developmental Disabilities, 50(4), 397-407. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581234

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Process and fidelity data collected during the trial for the ASD-risk subsample: "School implementation quality (fidelity) was excellent for coaches (.94"; range .88 to 1.00) and good for teachers (.82; range .41 to 1.00). Children received 96% of program days and families 89% of home sessions; parent compliance (.59) and implementation quality (.62) were only moderately satisfactory.
