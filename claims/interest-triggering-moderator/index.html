Evidence¶

van der Sande 2023¶

van der Sande, L., van Steensel, R., Fikrat-Wevers, S., & Arends, L. (2023). Effectiveness of Interventions that Foster Reading Motivation: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09719-3

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Categorical moderator analysis (analog-to-the-ANOVA) on immediate post-tests. Interventions triggering interest (k = 29 motivation; k = 16 comprehension) showed d = 0.51 and d = 0.41 versus d = 0.21 and d = 0.13 for those not triggering interest; Q(1) = 5.85* and 4.75*.

"Interest was a significant positive moderator of affirming motivations and reading comprehension: interventions that triggered interest had larger effects on affirming motivations and reading comprehension than those that did not."

  • Related decorative pictures do not increase triggered or maintained situational interest; in Experiment 2 they reduced maintained interest — related
  • Programs focusing solely on motivation are equally effective in stimulating affirming motivations and reading comprehension as programs combining motivational support with skills instruction — related
