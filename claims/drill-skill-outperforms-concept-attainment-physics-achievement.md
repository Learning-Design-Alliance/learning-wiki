# Drill skill model produces higher physics learning achievement than concept attainment model in Indonesian junior high school students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taught with the drill skill model achieved significantly higher physics learning achievement than students taught with the concept attainment model. [→ Wenno et al. 2016](#wenno-et-al-2016)
`q2 i?` After the drill skill treatment no students remained in the fail category, while after concept attainment instruction 46.7% of students were still categorized as fail. [→ Wenno et al. 2016 (2)](#wenno-et-al-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Wenno et al. 2016

Izaak Hendrik Wenno, Pieter Wattimena, Luky Maspaitela. (2016). Comparative Study between Drill Skill and Concept Attainment Model towards Physics Learning Achievement. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.5, No.3. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study with two intact classes of 30 students each at a State Junior High School of Ambon; one class taught with drill skill model and one with concept attainment model. The t-test analysis showed a "significant difference between the two models of learning on student learning achievement".

> "The results of t-test analysis (Table 4) showed significant difference between the two models of learning on student learning achievement."

### Wenno et al. 2016 (2)

Izaak Hendrik Wenno, Pieter Wattimena, Luky Maspaitela. (2016). Comparative Study between Drill Skill and Concept Attainment Model towards Physics Learning Achievement. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.5, No.3. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test mastery data from the quasi-experiment: the drill skill group moved from 80% fail on pretest to no students in the fail category, while the concept attainment group remained at 46.7% fail after treatment, described as "still fail categorized (46.7 %)".

> "before drill skill model was given, as much as 80 % of students were failed categorized, while after the drill skill model treatment was given, there is no student got fail category"
