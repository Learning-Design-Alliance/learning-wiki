## Evidence

### Smyth Wendy 2013

Smyth Wendy, Fielding Elaine, Beattie Elizabeth, Gardner Anne, Moyle Wendy, Franklin Sara, Hines Sonia, MacAndrew Margaret. (2013). A survey-based study of knowledge of Alzheimer's disease among health care staff. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2318-13-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weighted ANOVA of ADKS scores by professional group in the N = 360 survey (Table 1), F = 17.32, df 3,355, p <.001; medicine mean 26.08, support mean 22.14. Only the test statistic is printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "Professional Group Nursing 169 (46.2) 23.90 (2.84) 17.32 3,355 <.001 Medicine 11 (10.3) 26.0 8 (1.80) Allied Health 81 (11.8) 23.87 (3.69) Support 99 (31.7) 22.14 (3.41)"

### Smyth Wendy 2013 (2)

Smyth Wendy, Fielding Elaine, Beattie Elizabeth, Gardner Anne, Moyle Wendy, Franklin Sara, Hines Sonia, MacAndrew Margaret. (2013). A survey-based study of knowledge of Alzheimer's disease among health care staff. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2318-13-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression (Model 1, Table 3, N = 359) predicting ADKS score with demographic variables; nursing was the omitted category. The model explained about 12% of variance (adjusted R2 = 0.125). Unstandardised coefficients are printed, not standardised effect sizes.

> "Age and professional group were independent predictors, with those less than 30 scoring about 1 point higher, those in the medicine group scoring 1.8 points higher and those in the support group 1.8 point lower on the ADKS (other things equal)."
