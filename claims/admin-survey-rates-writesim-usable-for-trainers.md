## Evidence

### Shah Jatin 2010

Shah Jatin, Rajgor Dimple, Vaghasia Meenakshi, Phadtare Amruta, Pradhan Shreyasee, Carvalho Elias, Pietrobon Ricardo. (2010). WriteSim TCExam - An open source text simulation environment for training novice researchers in scientific writing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-39

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Online admin survey (DADOS-Survey) completed by 13 of 15 junior and senior researchers matched to the administrator profile after a 15-minute tutorial and exploration of the application. Ratings were on a five-point agreement scale; respondents reported basic computer literacy.

> "Majority of the participants reported the application to be fast (agree = 53.33%, strongly agree = 26.66%), easy to learn (agree = 60%, strongly agree = 13%), easy to use (agree = 60%, strongly agree = 20%)"
