## Evidence

### Nyman A 2019

Nyman A, Korhonen T, Lehtonen L, Haataja L, on behalf of the PIPARI Study Group. (2019). School performance is age appropriate with support services in very preterm children at 11 years of age. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.14763

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression on all 123 preterm vs 133 controls (Table 2): reading OR 2.1 (1.1–3.8) p 0.02, mathematics OR 1.9 (1.1–3.2) p 0.02, text production OR 1.8 (1.0–3.2) p 0.04, independent work OR 2.2 (1.2–4.2) p 0.01.

> "Problems with reading, text production and performing mathematics were more common in the very preterm children than the controls. Problems with independent work and persistency were also more common in very preterm children."

### Nyman A 2019 (2)

Nyman A, Korhonen T, Lehtonen L, Haataja L, on behalf of the PIPARI Study Group. (2019). School performance is age appropriate with support services in very preterm children at 11 years of age. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.14763

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 2: social skills OR 0.9 (0.5–1.8) p 0.85 and group work OR 1.5 (0.8–2.9) p 0.21, both non-significant. The authors note this contrasts with meta-analytic reports of social adjustment difficulties in very preterm children.

> "They did not, however, have more problems with social skills or group work than the controls."
