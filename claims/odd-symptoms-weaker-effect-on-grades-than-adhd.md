# Pure ODD symptoms have a negative effect on academic performance at 16 relative to controls, but weaker than that of pure ADHD symptoms

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The ODD group scored below controls but above the ADHD and ADHD+ODD groups in most subjects (e.g., male general subjects: ODD 7.14 vs ADHD 6.63 and controls 7.61). [→ Seppä S 2023](#seppa-s-2023)

## Evidence

### Seppä S 2023

Seppä S, Halt A-H, Nordström T, Hurtig T. (2023). Effects of Symptoms of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD) on Academic Performance and Educational Attainment. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-023-01598-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

ANOVA/Welch post hoc comparisons of male register grades in NFBC1986 (Table 2, post hoc C > O > A/AO for general subjects). The ODD group fell between controls and the ADHD groups. Only means and p-values were printed, no effect size.

> "Also, the ODD group had higher grades than ADHD and ADHD + ODD groups, so that the mean grade for the ODD group in general subjects, for example, was 7.14 (SD 1.20, p < 0.001) whereas it was 6.63 in the ADHD group (SD 1.00, p < 0.001)"
