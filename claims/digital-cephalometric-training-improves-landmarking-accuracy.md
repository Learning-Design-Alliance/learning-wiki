## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lin Jiayu 2025

Lin Jiayu, Liao Zhihao, Dai Jingtao, Wang Manyi, Yu Ruixue, Yang Hong, Liu Chufeng. (2025). Digital and artificial intelligence-assisted cephalometric training effectively enhanced students’ landmarking accuracy in preclinical orthodontic education. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-05978-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post study of 40 undergraduate students at Southern Medical University, divided into pairs, who labeled lateral radiographs A and B before and after practicing with digital cephalometric software; the article reports the score increase was "statistically significant (p < 0.001)". No effect size is printed.

> "The findings indicated that the average total score before training was 154.7, which significantly increased to 174.6 after training"
