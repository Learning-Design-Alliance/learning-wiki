## Evidence

### Vashti Sawtelle & Chandra Turpen 2014

Vashti Sawtelle & Chandra Turpen. (2014). Leveraging a Relationship with Biology to Expand a Relationship with Physics. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1409.5471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1409.5471)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview reflection (Clip 10) on a recitation task modeling DNA as a spring, in which Violet drew on her biology knowledge to explain DNA structure to the TA; the authors cite this as her identification with biology becoming consequential for her engagement in physics. No effect sizes reported.

> "And I went up to the chalkboard and like started drawing a string of DNA to show [the TA] because she was like I don't know any biology. And I was like ok I'll help you with this part, and I mean it just clicked so much more easier for me."

### Vashti Sawtelle & Chandra Turpen 2014 (2)

Vashti Sawtelle & Chandra Turpen. (2014). Leveraging a Relationship with Biology to Expand a Relationship with Physics. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1409.5471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1409.5471)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview data (Clip 6, end of first semester) in which Violet contrasts a regular physics class that "just doesn't relate" with this course, reporting that seeing connections among the three disciplines changed her affective stance. No effect sizes reported.

> "But now that I can see the relationship between all three, it's kind of made me like physics more."
