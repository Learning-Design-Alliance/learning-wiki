Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day 2003¶

Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day, with the assistance of David Wakelyn. (2003). External Support to Schools on Probation: Getting a Leg Up? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED474395

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

From the provider strategy analysis: partners' professional development consisted mostly of "traditional half-day or full-day workshops" with limited one-on-one assistance and classroom modeling. The executive summary adds little examination of student work and few ongoing collaborative opportunities.

"Most professional development takes the form of traditional half-day or full-day workshops with more limited attention to one-on-one assistance and modeling in classrooms"
