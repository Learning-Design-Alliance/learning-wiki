## Evidence

### Dong Minghua 2026

Dong Minghua, He Huitao, Wei Bowen. (2026). When digital engagement becomes dysregulated: associations with aggression, achievement motivation, and academic performance in adolescents. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1896602

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Sensitivity analysis repeating the cluster-adjusted GPA model after excluding DE17–DE19, which "explicitly referred to homework, studying, or school responsibilities." The reduced five-item score (α = 0.79) remained significant, modestly attenuated from the primary analysis.

> "the reduced regulatory-difficulty score remained negatively associated with GPA after adjustment for the demographic and design-related covariates, total aggression, and achievement motivation (B = −0.085, robust SE = 0.037, β = −0.186, 95% CI [−0.166, −0.004], p = 0.031)."
