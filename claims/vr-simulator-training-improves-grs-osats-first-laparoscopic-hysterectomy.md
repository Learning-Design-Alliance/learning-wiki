# Virtual reality simulator training before the first laparoscopic hysterectomy improves residents' Global Rating Scale OSATS scores in the live operation

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Residents randomized to ten simulator sessions scored higher on the OSATS Global Rating Scale in their first live laparoscopic hysterectomy than control residents (17.0 vs 11.2, p = 0.002). [→ Jokinen Ewa 2020](#jokinen-ewa-2020)

## Evidence

### Jokinen Ewa 2020

Jokinen Ewa, Mikkola Tomi S., Härkki Päivi. (2020). Simulator training and residents’ first laparoscopic hysterectomy: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-019-07270-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 20 residents; intervention group trained ten times with the VR hysterectomy module, controls did not. Blind video assessment showed "17.0 (SD 3.1) in the intervention group and 11.2 (SD 2.4) in the control group (p = 0.002)". No effect size printed for this outcome.

> "The mean score in the Global Rating Scale was 17.0 (SD 3.1) in the intervention group and 11.2 (SD 2.4) in the control group (p = 0.002)"
