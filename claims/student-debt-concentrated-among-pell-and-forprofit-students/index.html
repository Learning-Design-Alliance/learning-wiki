Michael Dannenberg and Mamie Voight 2013 (2)¶

Michael Dannenberg and Mamie Voight. (2013). Doing Away With Debt: Using Existing Resources to Ensure College Affordability for Low and Middle-Income Families. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED543214

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The report compares debt accumulation across sectors, reporting 53 percent of for-profit bachelor's recipients exceed $30,500 versus 24 percent at private nonprofits and 12 percent at public institutions. Descriptive comparison; no effect size printed.

"More than half (53 percent) of bachelor's recipients at for-profit colleges accumulate more than $30,500 on their way to a degree, compared with only 24 percent of students at private nonprofit colleges and 12 percent of students at public colleges and universities."
