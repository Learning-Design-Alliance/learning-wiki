Evidence¶

Yajing Zhang 2023¶

Yajing Zhang, Thi Kim Truc Huynh and Benjamin James Dyson. (2023). Deliberately making miskates: Behavioural consistency under win maximization and loss maximization conditions. npj Science of Learning 8:55. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00206-6

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Between-participant comparison across Experiments 1 (win maximization, 71 analyzed) and 2 (loss maximization, 46 analyzed) in Matching Pennies; the Results state "the deployment of either stay or shift behaviour was more consistent" under each goal in the goal-congruent direction.

"When the goal of the task was win maximization , the deployment of either stay or shift behaviour was more consistent following wins relative to losses, but when the goal of the task was loss maximization , the deployment of either stay or shift behaviour was more consistent following losses relative to wins."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Humans can readily abandon operant win-stay/lose-shift outcome-action associations when a loss-maximization goal requires the reversed strategies — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • In win maximization, performance is more consistent following wins than losses in both repetition- and alternation-exploitable opponents — related
  • In loss maximization, the pattern reverses: performance is more consistent following losses than wins — related
  • Win-stay/lose-shift associations are not privileged: consistency sometimes favours win-shift, and reaction times show no outcome-by-action interaction — related
