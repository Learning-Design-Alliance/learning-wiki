Iñarrairaegui M, Fernández-Ros N, Lucena F, Landecho MF, García N, Quiroga J, Herrero JI. (2022). Evaluation of the quality of multiple-choice questions according to the students’ academic level. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03844-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item analysis of 257 MCQ, with 52 questions classified as easy (difficulty index 0.7 or higher). Table 2 reports easy-question discrimination of 0.11 in group 1 versus 0.28 in group 2 (P < 0.001), while within group 1 easy versus non-easy differed (P < 0.001) but not within group 2 (P = 0.663); the text states "Easy questions were significantly less discriminative than non-easy questions in group 1, but not in group 2."
