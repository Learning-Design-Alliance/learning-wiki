## Evidence

### Ding Chun 2019

Ding Chun, Li Shengguo, Chen Baihua. (2019). Effectiveness of flipped classroom combined with team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning on ophthalmology teaching for eight-year program students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1861-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported time data from the comparative study of 67 students, analysed by independent t-test. The article reports preparation of 62.344 ± 9.331 min vs. 10.286 ± 5.550 min and review of 18.281 ± 4.854 min vs. 65.714 ± 14.909 min, both p < 0.001.

> "The students in the FC-TCLEBL group spent significantly more time preparing for class than those in the lecture-based classroom group (62.344 ± 9.331 min vs. 10.286 ± 5.550 min, p < 0.001). The time spent on review was significantly lower in the FC-TCLEBL group (18.281 ± 4.854 min vs. 65.714 ± 14.909 min, p < 0.001)."

### Ding Chun 2019 (2)

Ding Chun, Li Shengguo, Chen Baihua. (2019). Effectiveness of flipped classroom combined with team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning on ophthalmology teaching for eight-year program students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1861-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same self-reported time data; total time (80.625 ± 9.483 min vs. 76 ± 15.425 min) showed no statistically significant difference between groups, p = 0.141; equivalence was not tested.

> "No significant difference was found for the total time between the two groups (80.625 ± 9.483 min vs. 76 ± 15.425 min, p = 0.141)."
