## Evidence

### Chou 2013

Chou, C. (2013). A study on the effectiveness of applying "Readers' Theater" as English remedial instruction for underachievers. Taiwan Journal of TESOL, 10(1), 77-103. http://english.moe.gov.tw/ct.asp?xItem=7773&ctNode=516&mp=1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests on one-minute oral reading assessments for 25 experimental and 24 control underachievers; the article reports t-values of -15.72 and -7.69, "both of which reached the .001 significance level." Means rose from 86.40 to 126.40 (experimental) and 87.33 to 103.25 (control).

> "This indicates that both experimental and control groups displayed significant improvements in their English fluency following 15 weeks of English remedial instruction."
