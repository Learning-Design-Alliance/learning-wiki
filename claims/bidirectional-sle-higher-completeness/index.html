Pérez-Sanagustín 2015¶

Pérez-Sanagustín, M., Muñoz-Merino, P. J., Alario-Hoyos, C., Soldani, X., & Delgado Kloos, C. (2015). Lessons Learned from the Design of Situated Learning Environments to Support Collaborative Knowledge Construction. Computers & Education, 87, 70-82. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.03.019

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Second mixed ANOVA of the same experiment, on the completeness dimension of teacher rubric scores (1-5 scale, three teachers' grades averaged per team per machine). The group effect was significant, F(1, 45) = 12.635, p=0.001, with a mean of "2.81" for the CG and 3.55 for the EG; the pairwise 95% interval was [0.323, 1.168].

"The mixed ANOVA analysis revealed that, in terms of completeness, the type of group also has a statistically significant effect, F(1, 45) = 12.635, p=0.001, being the difference in favor of the EG."
