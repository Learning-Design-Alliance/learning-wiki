## Evidence

### Kruse 2013

Kruse, G. & Drews, D. (2013). Using Performance Tasks to Improve Quantitative Reasoning in an Introductory Mathematics Course. International Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 7(2), Article 19. https://doi.org/10.20429/ijsotl.2013.070219

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of final course averages against total post QR scores. In Study 1 all three sections showed no close correlation (e.g., rPT = .139), whereas the Study 2 PT section showed r = 0.455; the authors suggest grade incentive may have helped legitimize QR.

> "In section PT however, course averages did correlate with total QR scores, r = 0.455, n = 28, p = .015."
