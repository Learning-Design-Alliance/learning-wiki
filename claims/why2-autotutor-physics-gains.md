## Evidence

### Nye 2014

Nye, B. D., Graesser, A. C., & Hu, X. (2014). AutoTutor and Family: A Review of 17 Years of Natural Language Tutoring. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0029-5

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

Review reports the WHY2/AutoTutor pretest/posttest study against read-textbook and no-physics-materials controls, with normalized learning gains of 0.32 σ for AutoTutor versus 0.04 σ for read-textbook. Printed effect sizes: 0.61 σ and 1.22 σ.

> "This study found learning gains of 0.61 σ for students in the AutoTutor condition over the control posttest mean and 1.22 σ over the read-textbook posttest mean"

### Nye 2014 (2)

Nye, B. D., Graesser, A. C., & Hu, X. (2014). AutoTutor and Family: A Review of 17 Years of Natural Language Tutoring. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0029-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review reports that WHY2/AutoTutor's LSA-based statistical matching and WHY2/Atlas's finite-state, physics-specific hybrid classifier yielded comparable performance; no effect size printed for this comparability.

> "Despite using significantly different approaches to dialog management and semantic matching, both systems performed comparably"
