`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Numerical IRT analysis results across all FMCE items and data subsets (DS1full, DS2full, AllPre, AllPost, Combined). The article reports the correct response "is unambiguously determined to have the largest ak value" in every analysis, supporting parameter-based ranking.
