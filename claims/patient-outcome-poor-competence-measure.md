## Evidence

### Fairburn Christopher G. 2011

Fairburn Christopher G., Cooper Zafra. (2011). Therapist competence, therapy quality, and therapist training. Behaviour Research and Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2011.03.005

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Conceptual argument in the essay's section on methods of assessing skill at implementing a treatment. The authors reason that patients vary in responsiveness and that, citing cardiac surgery research, most variability between surgeons is attributable to differences in the proportion of high risk patients treated, so "crude outcome data can be misleading".

> "Its main shortcoming is that it is an indirect measure as patient outcome is affected by variables other than the quality of the treatment provided, a key one being the characteristics of the patients in question."
