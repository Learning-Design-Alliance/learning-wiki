## Evidence

### Silva Cristiana Silveira 2011

Silva Cristiana Silveira, Souza Murilo Barreto, Filho Roberto Silveira Silva, de Medeiros Luciana Molina, Criado Paulo Ricardo. (2011). E-learning program for medical students in dermatology. Clinics. https://doi.org/10.1590/S1807-59322011000400016

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same prospective two-group study, paired pre/post comparisons were analyzed with a paired t test or Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Table 1 shows control pretest 3.75 (SD 1.16) rising to 7.75 (SD 1.8) and online pretest 3.92 (SD 2.1) rising to 9.0 (SD 0.8), both "p <0.05" with per-group improvement "p <0.01".

> "Although the posttest exam scores significantly improved for both groups (p <0.01), the students who participated in the online discussions associated with face-to-face seminars had significantly higher posttest scores than the students who only attended face-to-face discussions"
