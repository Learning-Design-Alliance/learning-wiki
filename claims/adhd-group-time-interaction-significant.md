## Evidence

### Froelich 2012

Froelich, J., Breuer, D., Doepfner, M., & Amonn, F. (2012). Effects of a teacher training programme on symptoms of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder. International Journal of Special Education, 27(3), 76-87. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1001061

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multilevel between-group comparison for ADHD symptoms. The group effect was non-significant (f = 2.07, p = .158) but the "group x time interaction" was significant (f = 4.98, p < .031).

> "a significant group x time interaction during the treatment period (df 1,41; f = 4.98; p < .031) indicating a treatment effect in the between-group comparison"
