# MD-only third graders in fact retrieval tutoring outperform PROC/E and control students on fact retrieval

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` For MD-only students, both fact retrieval tutoring conditions outperformed procedural computation/estimation instruction and control on fact retrieval, with large effect sizes. [→ Powell 2009](#powell-2009)

## Evidence

### Powell 2009

Powell, S. R., Fuchs, L. S., Fuchs, D., Cirino, P. T., & Fletcher, J. M. (2009). Effects of Fact Retrieval Tutoring on Third-Grade Students with Math Difficulties with and without Reading Difficulties. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice, 24(1), 1-11. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-5826.2008.01272.x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up Tukey comparisons in the randomized trial (n = 139) showed MD-only students in both fact retrieval conditions beat PROC/E and control students. Discussion reports effect sizes of 1.11 and 0.96 versus PROC/E and 1.50 and 1.19 versus control.

> "for MD-only students, the two fact retrieval tutoring groups did not differ from one another, but each outperformed the PROC/E group (p < .0159 and p < .0051, respectively) as well as the control group (p < .0089 and p < .0028)"
