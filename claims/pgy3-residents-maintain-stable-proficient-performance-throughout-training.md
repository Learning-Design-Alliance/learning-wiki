## Evidence

### Li Congqing 2026

Li Congqing, Song Fei, Xu Youjiang, Zhang Liwei, Wang Wenyan. (2026). Efficacy of laparoscopic simulator training in standardized obstetrics and gynecology residents: a prospective study demonstrating skill convergence. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-46957-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA for the PGY-3 group (n = 10) showed no statistically significant differences in completion times for any module over the four weeks, consistent with a performance plateau among senior residents already proficient at baseline. The article reports their "performance remained stable and consistently efficient across all four weeks".

> "In stark contrast, PGY-3 residents entered the training with a high level of baseline proficiency. Their performance remained stable and consistently efficient across all four weeks, with no statistically significant differences in completion times for any module over time."
