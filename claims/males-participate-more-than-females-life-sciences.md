## Evidence

### Bailey E. G. 2020

Bailey E. G., Greenall R. F., Baek D. M., Morris C., Nelson N., Quirante T. M., Rice N. S., Rose S., Williams K. R. (2020). Female In-Class Participation and Performance Increase with More Female Peers and/or a Female Instructor in Life Sciences Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-12-0266

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Classroom observation study of 34 life sciences courses; paired t tests compared male and female participation fractions (Figure 1A p < 0.0001, n = 34 classes; Figure 1C p = 0.0001). The text reports "an average of 32% of males and 22% of females participating at least once" and average verbal participation rates of 0.77 (males) versus 0.43 (females).
