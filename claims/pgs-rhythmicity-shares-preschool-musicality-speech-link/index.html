q2 · i? · associational · r3

Confirmatory factor analysis of PGS-rhythmicity-associated measures (N = 5873) identified a three-factor structure — Fmusicality, Fcognition, F*speech — with "a good model ﬁt (CFI = 0.95, TLI = 0.93, RMSEA = 0.042, SRMR = 0.028"; factor loadings were virtually identical after adding PGS rhythmicity to the model.

Related Claims¶

  • Latent musicality correlates moderately with speech-related communication (r = 0.50) and modestly with cognition (r = 0.33), but not with pragmatic or social-communication factors — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Polygenic scores for rhythmicity associate with school-age speech-related communication and verbal-cognition outcomes, with two associations passing multiple-testing correction and four at the nominal level — related
  • Genetic variation shared with rhythmicity does not account for the association between preschool musicality and school-age verbal cognition — related
  • Polygenic scores for adult rhythmicity validate ALSPAC preschool and school-age musicality measures, extending genetic associations with rhythmicity to an earlier developmental window — related
  • Phenotypic and genetic associations with musicality are robust to genetic confounding via educational attainment — related
  • Preschool and school-age musicality measures predict school-age communication and social communication, explaining up to 6% of phenotypic variance, while infant nursery-rhyme measures explain less than 1%
  • In latent difference score models of ability domains, nearly all PGS-to-change pathways are small and nonsignificant, while age consistently predicts pre-test level and change — related
