Evidence¶

Jonte Bernhard 2018¶

Jonte Bernhard. (2018). What matters for students' learning in the laboratory? Do not neglect the role of experimental equipment! Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9469-x

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Analysis of about 15 h of video from three, two and eight recorded groups in the photogates, tape timer and probeware labs respectively. In the tape timer lab students used the tape only as an indicator of time for a fixed distance, determining "the average velocity and not the instantaneous velocity", and motion concepts featured very rarely.

"In the photo-gate lab, the discourse of all groups consistently centred on reading and noting the numbers (the times tA, tB and tAB) from the electronics box, as illustrated in Excerpt 1."

Jonte Bernhard 2018 (2)¶

Jonte Bernhard. (2018). What matters for students' learning in the laboratory? Do not neglect the role of experimental equipment! Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9469-x

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The author's interpretation of the photogate and tape-timer transcripts: students lacked the conceptual resources to interpret numbers and dots, resembling "conceptually blindfolded" discourse. In contrast, in the probeware lab students' discourses consistently centred on making sense of motion concepts such as position, velocity and acceleration.

"It is clear that the student did not perceive the "world" through a human–technology–world mediated experience, but numbers and dots produced by the measurement technology. This is a human–technology (–world) experience (cf. Ihde 1979, 1991)."
