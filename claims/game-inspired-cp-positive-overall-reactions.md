### Ian D. Beatty and Lauren A. Harris 2014

Ian D. Beatty and Lauren A. Harris. (2014). Pwning Level Bosses in MATLAB: Student Reactions to a Game-Inspired Computational Physics Course. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.006](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.006)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured end-of-course interviews with 11 of 15 CP1 students, analyzed by constant-comparison coding with a second analyst checking a subset of transcripts. Nine of eleven gave generally positive responses, one negative, one ambiguous; course evaluations corroborated: eight of thirteen rated the course 5/5.

> "Nine of eleven respondents answered with a generally positive statement, such as "I really love it. It's the only class that I have that like I'm sad when class is over"
