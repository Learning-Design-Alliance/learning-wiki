In the qualitative four-state case study, a Louisiana state official emphasized, "It's really hard to make it a requirement when families don't have computer access at home," which forces schools to open computer labs during the day or host evening events with staff assistance onsite.

"Universal FAFSA policies assume that students and families have consistent access to computers and high-speed internet, but this is not the reality in many communities, particularly in rural and low-income areas"
