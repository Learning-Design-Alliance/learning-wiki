# Multimedia motivational introductory messages produce better immediate knowledge-test outcomes than traditional teaching aids in elementary Nature and Society lessons

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students taught with multimedia motivational messages in the lesson introduction scored significantly better on several immediate knowledge tests than students taught the same content with traditional aids. [→ Lauc 2020](#lauc-2020)

## Evidence

### Lauc 2020

Lauc, T., Jagodić, G. K., & Bistrović, J. (2020). Effects of Multimedia Instructional Message on Motivation and Academic Performance of Elementary School Students in Croatia. International Journal of Instruction, 13(4), 491-508. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13431a

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Field quasi-experiment in four Croatian schools; experimental group (N=97) received multimedia introductory activities, control group (N=73) traditional aids. Independent t-tests showed significant differences on Test 1.2 (t = 2.821, p = .006) and Test 1.3 (t = 4.940, p = .001), with the experimental group higher on "the second and the third test of the first part of the experiment".

> "The t-test for independent groups revealed a significant difference between groups regarding the second and the third test of the first part of the experiment."
