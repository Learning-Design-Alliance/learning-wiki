Curiosity Corner 2009¶

Curiosity Corner. (2009). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.successforall.org/early/early_curiosity.htm

q3 · i? · review · r3

WWC synthesis of two studies: PCER Consortium (2008), a randomized controlled trial with 18 preschools assessing PPVT-III and TOLD-P:3, and Chambers et al. (2001), a quasi-experimental study of 316 children using MSEL language scales. The report states "no discernible effects on oral language."

"According to WWC criteria, these two studies show no discernible effects on oral language."
