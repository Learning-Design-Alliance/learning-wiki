## Evidence

### Julia Carbone & Susanne Diekelmann 2024

Julia Carbone & Susanne Diekelmann. (2024). An update on recent advances in targeted memory reactivation during sleep. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00244-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review attributes to Ngo and Staresina a closed-loop study cueing 120 verb-image-sound associations during SO up-states or down-states in NREM sleep. "reduced overnight forgetting when cueing occurred during the SO up-state compared to the down-state"; no effect size printed.

> "Using this protocol, behavioral results revealed reduced overnight forgetting when cueing occurred during the SO up-state compared to the down-state (Fig. 2a)."

### Julia Carbone & Susanne Diekelmann 2024 (2)

Julia Carbone & Susanne Diekelmann. (2024). An update on recent advances in targeted memory reactivation during sleep. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00244-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that in Wang and colleagues' word-pair closed-loop study, "No differences were found in memory performance betweenup-state and down-state cueing", and likewise Göldi et al. found no up-state superiority over down-state directly; equivalence was not established.

> "No differences were found in memory performance betweenup-state and down-state cueing."
