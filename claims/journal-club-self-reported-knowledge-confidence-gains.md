## Evidence

### Almuzaini Ohood K. 2025

Almuzaini Ohood K., Wali Saad M., Harbi Maan H., Azhari Hala F. (2025). Evaluating the impact of a journal club activity on pharmacy students’ learning outcomes and professional skills development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07691-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey administered immediately after journal club sessions to 207 pharmacy students, using a validated four-domain instrument; descriptive results show "89.4% acknowledged acquiring new knowledge or skills" and 77.7% reported confidence gains. No effect sizes were printed.

> "Most participants (89.4%) acknowledged acquiring new knowledge or skills through the activity. Key skills improved, including team collaboration (59.4%) and presentation skills (58.9%). Participants also reported increased confidence in critically evaluating research, with 77.7% feeling slightly or much more confident."
