Evidence¶

Bart-Plange 2025¶

Bart-Plange, D.-J., Henderson, K., Hoffman, K., & Trawalter, S. (2025). Critiquing and Reimagining Belonging in Public Spaces in Higher Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10061-z

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Driskell and Trawalter (2021), that antebellum architecture feels unwelcoming to Black students, and cites Cheryan and colleagues (2009) showing masculine objects in computer science spaces convey to women that they do not belong. Greek housing's design and demographics may symbolize segregation.

"Indeed, research has shown that antebellum architecture feels unwelcoming to Black students (Driskell & Trawalter, 2021; see also, Clowney, 2020; Nelson, 2023)."
