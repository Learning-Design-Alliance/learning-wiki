## Evidence

### Bartlett Richard D. 2017

Bartlett Richard D., Radenkovic Dina, Mitrasinovic Stefan, Cole Andrew, Pavkovic Iva, Denn Peyton Cheong Phey, Hussain Mahrukh, Kogler Magdalena, Koutsopodioti Natalia, Uddin Wasima, Beckley Ivan, Abubakar Hana, Gill Deborah, Smith Daron. (2017). A pilot study to assess the utility of a freely downloadable mobile application simulator for undergraduate clinical skills training: a single-blinded, randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1085-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test questionnaire in the pilot trial (n = 27) rating confidence in each core clinical skills competency on a 10-point Likert scale. Descriptive means only are printed; no inferential test of the between-procedure differences is reported.

> "Arterial blood gases (ABGs) were the procedure that students felt least confident in performing (mean Likert score 3.7 / 10), and this was closely followed by male urinary catheterisation (mean Likert score 3.88 / 10)."

### Bartlett Richard D. 2017 (2)

Bartlett Richard D., Radenkovic Dina, Mitrasinovic Stefan, Cole Andrew, Pavkovic Iva, Denn Peyton Cheong Phey, Hussain Mahrukh, Kogler Magdalena, Koutsopodioti Natalia, Uddin Wasima, Beckley Ivan, Abubakar Hana, Gill Deborah, Smith Daron. (2017). A pilot study to assess the utility of a freely downloadable mobile application simulator for undergraduate clinical skills training: a single-blinded, randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1085-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analysis of pre-test questionnaire data across year-4 and year-5 students in the pilot sample. The non-significant comparison (p = 0.614) contrasts with prior work the authors cite; no effect size is printed.

> "pre-test self-assessed male urinary catheterisation confidence was not found to vary significantly by year group (mean Likert scores: year 4 = 3.7 / 10, year 5 = 4.1 / 10, p = 0.614)."
