## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Villar 2025

Villar, E., Real‐Deus, E., Martínez‐López, Z., Mayo, M. E., & Tinajero, C. (2025). Perceived peer support, motivational self‐regulation and academic achievement in adolescents. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1325–1346. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12783

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Summary of the three PROCESS Model 4 mediation regressions (5000 bootstrap runs, 95% CIs excluding zero) in the 463-student sample; Tables 3–5 print indirect effects such as PreST a1*b1 = .10 [.03, .18] for emotional support.

> "Personally referred performance self‐talk and self‐efficacy enhancement were found to positively mediate the relationship between perceived support from peers (whether emotional support, guidance and reassurance of worth) and achievement, while enhancement of situational interest was a negative mediator of this relationship"

### Villar 2025 (2)

Villar, E., Real‐Deus, E., Martínez‐López, Z., Mayo, M. E., & Tinajero, C. (2025). Perceived peer support, motivational self‐regulation and academic achievement in adolescents. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1325–1346. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12783

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reported after the three mediation models; full mediation pattern with no significant direct paths remaining once the strategies were in the models.

> "All of the observed mediating effects are total effects, as none of the direct effects (path c′) were significant after the inclusion of the MRSs"
