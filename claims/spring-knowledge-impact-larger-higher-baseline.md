# The spring knowledge impact was larger for teachers with higher baseline knowledge: significant for teachers 1 SD above average (34 percentile points) but not 1 SD below (8 points)

## Subclaims
`q4 i?` The estimated spring impact was positive and statistically significant for teachers with baseline knowledge 1 SD above average (improvement index of 34 percentile points) but not for teachers 1 SD below average (8 percentile points). [→ Garet 2016](#garet-2016)
`q4 i?` Fall impacts did not differ by baseline knowledge (25 vs 23 percentile points, both significant), indicating the initial boost was not sustained for lower-knowledge teachers. [→ Garet 2016 (2)](#garet-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Garet 2016

Garet, M. S., Heppen, J. B., Walters, K., Parkinson, J., Smith, T. M., Song, M., Garrett, R., Yang, R., & Borman, G. D. (2016). Focusing on mathematical knowledge: The impact of content-intensive teacher professional development, executive summary (NCEE 2016-4009). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Moderator analysis within the RCT using baseline knowledge scores standardized with the control-group mean and SD. The report states the initial boost was not sustained for teachers who began with lower levels of knowledge, even with school-year supports.

> "the impact of the PD on teachers' knowledge in the spring was larger for teachers with higher baseline knowledge scores than for teachers with lower baseline scores"

### Garet 2016 (2)

Garet, M. S., Heppen, J. B., Walters, K., Parkinson, J., Smith, T. M., Song, M., Garrett, R., Yang, R., & Borman, G. D. (2016). Focusing on mathematical knowledge: The impact of content-intensive teacher professional development, executive summary (NCEE 2016-4009). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Fall moderator analysis from the same trial, showing similar significant impacts at both baseline levels before the school-year supports were delivered.

> "The estimated impact of the PD on fall teacher knowledge was positive and statistically significant for teachers with a baseline knowledge score 1 standard deviation above average (improvement index of 25 percentile points) as well as for teachers 1 standard deviation below average (improvement index of 23 percentile points)"
