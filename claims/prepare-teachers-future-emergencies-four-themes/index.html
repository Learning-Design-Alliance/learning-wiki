An 2021¶

An, Y., Kaplan-Rakowski, R., Yang, J., Conan, J., Kinard, W., & Daughrity, L. (2021). Examining K-12 teachers' feelings, experiences, and perspectives regarding online teaching during the early stage of the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10008-5

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis of open-ended survey and interview responses from the same mixed-methods study. The article reports "Four major themes were identified regarding how to better prepare teachers for future emergencies"; 66% of survey respondents also wanted to learn more about online teaching.

"Four major themes were identified regarding how to better prepare teachers for future emergencies: (1) professional development for online learning, (2) technology access, (3) technology training for both teachers and students, and (4) action plans and communication."
