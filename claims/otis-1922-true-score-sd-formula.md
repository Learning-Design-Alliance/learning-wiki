### Arthur S. Otis 1922

Arthur S. Otis. (1922). The Method for Finding the Correspondence Between Scores in Two Tests. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075112

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

A rigorous algebraic derivation (the article's second proof): since X = x + e with errors uncorrelated with true scores, squaring and summing yields σ²x = σ²X − σ²e, and with the author's reliability formula this gives σx = √rxx·σX. Analytical, not empirical.

> "This equation constitutes a formula for finding the standard deviation of true scores of a group of individuals from the standard deviation of the obtained scores of those individuals, knowing the reliability coefficient of correlation obtained from the same group of individuals."
