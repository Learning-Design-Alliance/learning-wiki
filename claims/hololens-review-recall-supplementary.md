## Evidence

### Lin Joseph 2026

Lin Joseph, Ghazi Sarmad, Kuss Ethan, Shehata Sarah. (2026). Medical students’ experiences of mixed-reality-based anatomy instruction in a non-cadaveric curriculum: a qualitative case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09881-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of 12 first-year medical students; thematic analysis found perceived memory support was tied to how HoloLens was integrated into broader study practices, with a smaller group reporting little clear advantage over conventional digital anatomy tools.

> "Being able to interact with it helps me remember stuff a little bit more than just looking at a static image."
