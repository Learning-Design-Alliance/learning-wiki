## Evidence

### Karim Sadeghi & Negar Ahmadi 2012

Karim Sadeghi & Negar Ahmadi. (2012). The Effect of Gloss Type and Mode on Iranian EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 5(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n12p100

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test comparing the audio gloss group (M = 6.73) with the no-gloss control group (M = 5.27) on the immediate post-test; the article reports "t (28) = 3.36, p = .002" with a mean difference of 1.467. No effect size is printed.
