## Evidence

### Laninga-Wijnen 2018

Laninga-Wijnen, L., Ryan, A., Harakeh, Z., Shin, H., & Vollebergh, W. A. M. (2018). The Moderating Role of Popular Peers' Achievement Goals in 5th- and 6th-Graders' Achievement-Related Friendships: A Social Network Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology, vol. 110, no. 2, pp. 289-307. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000210

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article cites Gest et al. (2008) for this validity evidence, attributing the correlations to that prior work. Peers are described as expert observers whose proximity permits observations not always captured by tests, GPAs, or teacher ratings.

> "peer-perceived achievement has been shown to be a valid indicator of adolescent academic competence that is highly correlated with grade point average (GPA; correlations varying from .60 to .70; Gest et al., 2008), but that also captures unique information"
