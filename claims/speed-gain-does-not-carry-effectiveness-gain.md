Peters, C. C. (1916). The Influence of Speed Drills Upon the Rate and the Effectiveness of Silent Reading. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0073150

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The author's own interpretation (observation 5) of the seven-month drill experiment: speed drills may bring speed up to an individual's possibilities, but the naturally slow reader may not become rapid without impairing effectiveness.

> "This experiment has not confirmed the doctrine that increase in speed will carry with it, in any given individual, also increase in effectiveness."
