Teach For America 2016¶

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

This is the systematic review's synthesis of the English language arts domain from five studies (53,595 students). The report finds: "In the five studies, the estimated impact of TFA teachers on English language arts achievement was neither statistically significant nor large enough to be substantively important", with all five study findings characterized as indeterminate.
