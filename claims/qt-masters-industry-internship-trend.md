### Simon Goorney 2025

Simon Goorney, Borja Muñoz, Jacob Sherson. (2025). Quantum Technology master’s: A shortcut to the quantum industry?. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-024-00299-x](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-024-00299-x)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of internship offerings from 51 primary master's programs, gathered from program websites and communication with faculty representatives and categorised as university, industry, or either. The article reports "an increasing trend towards internships carried out in an industry setting".

> "Prior to 2019, none of the established programs offered internships in companies, whilst as of 2020 greater than 50% of newly launched program’s offer an industry internship."
