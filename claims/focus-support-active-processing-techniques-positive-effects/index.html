Eitel 2025¶

Eitel, A., Krebs, M.-C., Schöne, C. (2025). Introducing the FoRe-Squares Model: Focus Support and Reward as Key Predictors of Effective Technology-Augmented Instruction. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10045-z

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review's synthesis of active-processing techniques states that "empirical evidence reveals the positive effects" of testing, self-explaining, concept mapping, and drawing, each mapped to SOISG processes such as strengthening and generating. No pooled effect sizes are printed in this sentence.

"Accordingly, empirical evidence reveals the positive effects of techniques such as testing (e.g., Yang et al., 2021), self-explaining (Bisra et al., 2018), concept mapping (Schroeder et al., 2018), or drawing (Cromley et al., 2020)."
