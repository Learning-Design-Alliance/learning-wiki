q2 · i? · causal · r2

Log analysis of submitted AI-agent session logs across the three conditions. The article reports no significant difference in "total exchange length per session", with no test statistic or effect size printed; equivalence was not tested.

"Analyses of submitted session logs indicated that interaction volume — measured by total exchange length per session — did not differ significantly across conditions, supporting the comparability of practice exposure independent of its structure."
