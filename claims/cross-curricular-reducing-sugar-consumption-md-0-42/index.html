Evidence¶

Dudley 2015¶

Dudley, D. A., Cotton, W. G., & Peralta, L. R. (2015). Teaching approaches and strategies that promote healthy eating in primary school children: a systematic review and meta-analysis. International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity, 12, 28. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-015-0182-8

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of two cross-curricular studies reducing SLB or fruit juice consumption; both reported statistically significant reductions at p < .05 or better. The review reports a mean Cohen's d of Md = 0.42 but cautions about the small number of studies.

"The mean effect size for cross-curricular approaches at reducing SLB or fruit juice consumption was Md = 0.42. This was within the Zone of Desired Effects [11], but more investigation may be required given the small number of studies included in the analysis."
