Evidence¶

Breideband 2025¶

Breideband, T., Bush, J. B., Reitman, J. G., Rose, S., Weatherley, J., Penuel, W. R., & D'Mello, S. (2025). A Feasibility and Implementation Integrity Study of the Community Builder (CoBi): An AI-based Collaboration Support System in K-12 Classrooms. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00509-2

q2 · i? · design · r3

Classroom pilot of CoBi v1 in three middle school classrooms with 24 fully consented students: "From a technical perspective, CoBi performed adequately in the classrooms." Visualizations reached end states approximately 15 minutes into collaborative activity.

"From a technical perspective, CoBi performed adequately in the classrooms. The browser interfaces (for both CoBi and the audio-visual recorder) loaded properly, and the visualizations updated as expected in 10-s intervals."

q2 · i? · design · r3

Field notes and classroom video from the same pilot showed that "there were situations where the state of the visualization on the teacher TV did not match the state of the visualization on students’ Chromebooks," causing students to misread the class-wide display as group-level.
