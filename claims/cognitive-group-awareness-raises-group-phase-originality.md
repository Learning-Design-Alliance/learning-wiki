## Evidence

### Farrokhnia 2024

Farrokhnia, M., Noroozi, O., Baggen, Y., Biemans, H., & Weinberger, A. (2024). Improving hybrid brainstorming outcomes with computer-supported scaffolds: Prompts and cognitive group awareness. Computers & Education 227 (2025) 105229. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105229

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Group-level ANCOVA comparing 11 triads per experimental condition in the group phase. The originality difference was significant (F(1,19) = 6.37, p = .02, ηp² = 0.25); fluency, flexibility, and persistence showed no significant differences (p = .26, .77, .13).

> "The average originality of ideas generated in experimental 2 condition (Adjusted M = 2.91, SE = 0.26) was shown to be significantly higher than those in experimental 1 condition (Adjusted M = 2.62, SE = 0.23), thus confirming H4"
