## Evidence

### Springvloet Linda 2014

Springvloet Linda, Lechner Lilian, Oenema Anke. (2014). Planned development and evaluation protocol of two versions of a web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention aimed at adults, including cognitive and environmental feedback. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-14-47

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive account of target-group involvement: a "consumer panel consisted of 55 people from the target group" contacted multiple times by e-mail or phone. They provided input on advantages and disadvantages of the target behaviors, barriers, and home storage locations, plus feedback on the intervention name and recruitment materials. The article states this helped address beliefs and barriers salient in the target group.

> "The consumer panel consisted of 55 people from the target group (mean age 51.31 years (SD = 12.43); 63.6% women; 40% low-educated) who were recruited via spreading flyers in low SES neighborhoods and recruiting people in shopping malls."
