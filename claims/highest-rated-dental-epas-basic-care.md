### Kosić Nejra 2025

Kosić Nejra, Gerhardt‐Szép Susanne. (2025). Twenty‐one Entrusted Professional Activities in German Dental Education—Day‐One Competencies. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13931

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey (n = 100) with 6‐point Likert ratings; EPA 1 had the highest mean (5.69) and EPA 7a 5.57. The article notes "Practical skill‐related EPAs were not necessarily rated higher than those related to knowledge or communication".

> "The highest‐rated EPAs were related to obtaining the patient's history (EPA 1), examining the patient (EPA 2a), obtaining informed consent (EPA 2e), knowledge about the etiology of dental diseases (EPA 3a), acquiring diagnostic and screening results (EPA 4a), and performing adhesive and non‐adhesive restorative treatment (EPAs 7a and 7b)."
