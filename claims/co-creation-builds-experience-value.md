# Co-creation has a strong positive effect on student experience value in higher education (path 0.90, significant at the 5% level).

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the full structural model, co-creation positively and significantly influences students' experience value, with the model explaining 82% of the variance. [→ Muji Gunarto 2020](#muji-gunarto-2020)

## Evidence

### Muji Gunarto 2020

Muji Gunarto, Ratih Hurriyati. (2020). CREATING EXPERIENCE VALUE TO BUILD STUDENT SATISFACTION IN HIGHER EDUCATION. Dinasti International Journal of Education Management and Social Science, 1(3). [doi:10.31933/dijemss.v1i3.166](https://doi.org/10.31933/dijemss.v1i3.166)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Explanatory survey of 357 final-year students and alumni at 32 higher education institutions in South Sumatra, analyzed with SEM in LISREL. The structural model reports "a very high positive influence (0.90)" of co-creation on experience value, with t = 11.43 and R² = 0.82 as printed in Table 3.

> "The value of co-creation also has a very high positive influence (0.90) on the value of experience for students."
