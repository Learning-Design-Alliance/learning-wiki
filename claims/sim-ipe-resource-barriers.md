## Evidence

### Najjuma Josephine N. 2024

Najjuma Josephine N., Muhumuza Albert, Santorino Data, Sekyere Samuel Owusu, Ocheke Amaka N., Yiltok Simon J., Bajunirwe Francis. (2024). Barriers and facilitators to interprofessional simulation-based learning in a Ugandan medical school: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06521-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

A medical student in focus group reported sessions limited to two hours on Thursdays: "you are limited by time. So maybe for our success, for that case, we need to increase more time." The article states few meetings limit realizing the desired benefits of interprofessional learning.

> "So you find that the group you're dividing is having many students and you are limited by time. So maybe for our success, for that case, we need to increase more time."

### Najjuma Josephine N. 2024 (2)

Najjuma Josephine N., Muhumuza Albert, Santorino Data, Sekyere Samuel Owusu, Ocheke Amaka N., Yiltok Simon J., Bajunirwe Francis. (2024). Barriers and facilitators to interprofessional simulation-based learning in a Ugandan medical school: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06521-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis found "The limited space in the simulation labs was mentioned as a barrier to accommodating a required number of students from different professions to enable IPE," producing long waits, fatigue and loss of morale; a student noted two labs for a nursing class of 33.

> "Then another thing would be space. He has talked about it. So if this one is to be a success, then they should put into account the space bit of it. We have two labs at the moment, and you realize we are 33 in our class of nurses."

### Najjuma Josephine N. 2024 (3)

Najjuma Josephine N., Muhumuza Albert, Santorino Data, Sekyere Samuel Owusu, Ocheke Amaka N., Yiltok Simon J., Bajunirwe Francis. (2024). Barriers and facilitators to interprofessional simulation-based learning in a Ugandan medical school: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06521-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

A nursing faculty key informant reported inadequate instructor numbers: "sometimes you go and you find the same facilitators and they get tired," with shortages causing oversized groups and lacking models and equipment.

> "You know sometimes you go and you find the same facilitators and they get tired. So, if we could get enough man power or personnel."
