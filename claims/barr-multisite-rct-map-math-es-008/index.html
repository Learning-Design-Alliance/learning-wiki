Building Assets Reducing Risks (BARR) — Evidence Rating 2023¶

Building Assets Reducing Risks (BARR) — Evidence Rating. (2023). https://www.barrcenter.org

q4 · i? · causal · r?

A multi-site student-level randomized trial in six high schools in Maine, California, and Minnesota found "a small but statistically significant positive impact on math outcomes measured by NWEA MAP (effect size = +0.08)". The sample was 67% African American, 10% English learners, 13% special education, 72% free or reduced-price lunch.

"A multi-site student-level randomized trial conducted in six high schools in Maine, California, and Minnesota (n = 1,631; 67% African American, 10% English learners, 13% special education, 72% free or reduced-price lunch) found a small but statistically significant positive impact on math outcomes measured by NWEA MAP (effect size = +0.08)."
