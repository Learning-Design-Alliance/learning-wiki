### Odden 2019

Odden, Lockwood, and Caballero. (2019). Physics Computational Literacy: An Exploratory Case Study Using Computational Essays. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020152](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020152)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Analysis of the collected computational essays found "All students in the present study included at least one code-produced visual in their computational essay". Students also reported making intermediary plots to check whether results looked realistic, and two lightning groups repeatedly ran simulations to understand strike locations under varying conditions.

> "All students in the present study included at least one code-produced visual in their computational essay, most frequently plots of particle trajectories and other associated quantities (like energy) as a function of time."
