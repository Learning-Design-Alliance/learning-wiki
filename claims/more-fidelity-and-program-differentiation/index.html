Kim 2023¶

Kim, J. S., Burkhauser, M. A., Relyea, J. E., Gilbert, J. B., Scherer, E., Fitzgerald, J., Mosher, D., & McIntyre, J. (2023). A Longitudinal Randomized Trial of a Sustained Content Literacy Intervention From First to Second Grade: Transfer Effects on Students' Reading Comprehension. Journal of Educational Psychology, 115(1), 73-98. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000751

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Teacher survey data on instructional time during the Grade 1 implementation year. The survey also showed treatment teachers devoted nearly 72 more minutes per week to social studies, while ELA/reading time was similar for treatment and control groups.

"Overall, treatment group teachers (M = 177.34, SD = 130.46) reported spending approximately 68 more minutes per week on science instruction than control teachers (M = 109.05, SD = 99.37)."

Kim 2023 (2)¶

Kim, J. S., Burkhauser, M. A., Relyea, J. E., Gilbert, J. B., Scherer, E., Fitzgerald, J., Mosher, D., & McIntyre, J. (2023). A Longitudinal Randomized Trial of a Sustained Content Literacy Intervention From First to Second Grade: Transfer Effects on Students' Reading Comprehension. Journal of Educational Psychology, 115(1), 73-98. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000751

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Program differentiation analysis comparing read-aloud text complexity and literacy-task openness between conditions. Audio-recorded lessons from 12 treatment and 11 control classrooms were rated, and treatment tasks were more authentic, collaborative, challenging, student-directed, and sustained.

"The average Lexile levels of the books used in the treatment group (science books M = 696.25L; social studies books M = 941.67L) was much higher than those from the control group (science books M = 522.63L; social studies books M = 439.09L)."
