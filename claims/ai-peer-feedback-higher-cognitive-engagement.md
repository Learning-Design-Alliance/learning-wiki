# AI-peer integrated feedback produces significantly higher posttest cognitive engagement than conventional peer feedback in EFL writing

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` After controlling for pretest scores, the experimental group showed significantly higher posttest cognitive engagement than the control group (partial η2 = 0.32), the largest of the three engagement effects. [→ Liu W 2026](#liu-w-2026)

## Evidence

### Liu W 2026

Liu W (2026) AI-peer integrated feedback in second language writing classes: exploring students' engagement and writing performance. Front. Psychol. 17:1871951. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1871951

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANCOVA on posttest cognitive engagement with pretest covariate in the quasi-experiment. Adjusted means were 4.07 (experimental) versus 3.02 (control), "mean difference = 1.05, 95% CI [0.77, 1.33]"; partial η2 = 0.32, the largest of the three engagement effects.

> "ANCOV A for cognitive engagement also yielded a significant main effect of group ( F(1, 119) = 57.03, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.32)."
