Evidence¶

Sudha and Amutha 2015¶

A. Sudha, S. Amutha. (2015). Higher Secondary Learners' Effectiveness towards Web Based Instruction (WBI) on Chemistry. Universal Journal of Educational Research 3(7). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2015.030706

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment comparing a WBI experimental group with a traditionally taught control group (24 students each) on a 25-question achievement test. The post-test means were M=20.25 (SD 1.073) for the experimental group and M=18.33 (SD 1.404) for the control group, reported as "differ significantly in the post–test mean scores at 0.01 level of significance".

"'T' test of table.2 indicated that higher secondary level chemistry students of control group (N=24) and experimental group (N=24) differ significantly in the post–test mean scores at 0.01 level of significance."
