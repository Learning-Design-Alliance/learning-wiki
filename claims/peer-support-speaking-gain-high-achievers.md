## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among high English achievement students, the peer-supported group scored significantly higher than the non-peer group on all six speaking aspects (p < .05 for all). [→ Muslem 2017](#muslem-2017)

## Evidence

### Muslem 2017

Muslem, A., & Abbas, M. (2017). The Effectiveness of Immersive Multimedia Learning with Peer Support on English Speaking and Reading Aloud. International Journal of Instruction, 10(1). http://www.e-iji.net/dosyalar/iji_2017_1_13.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA on the high-achievement subsample (n = 12 with peer support, 13 without) for speaking. For example, phrasing means were 6.50 versus 5.30, F(1,24) = 26.261, p = .000, with "p < .05 for all the aspects".

> "High achievement students in the peer -support group reported consistently higher mean scores for all dimensions of oral production in reading and the results of ANOVA tests reported significant differences, i.e., that p < .05 for all the aspects."
