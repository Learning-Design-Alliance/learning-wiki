## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2018

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Laura Ríos, Benjamin Pollard, Jacob T. Stanley, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2018). Characterizing lab instructors' self-reported learning goals to inform development of an experimental modeling skills assessment. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020118)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The article's literature review of existing assessments (e.g., Kuo et al., PSRUS, PLIC) concludes each provides only partial measures and all are designed for introductory courses; this is a review assertion, not a tested result.

> "While there are a few ongoing efforts to assess some aspects of students' model-based reasoning in introductory physics lab and lecture courses [24–26], no modeling assessments exist for upper-division labs."
