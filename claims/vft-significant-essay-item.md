## Evidence

### Norbaizura HARIS & Kamisah OSMAN 2015

Norbaizura HARIS & Kamisah OSMAN. (2015). The Effectiveness of a Virtual Field Trip (VFT) Module in Learning Biology. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 16(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1092841

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on the single high-level essay question in the quasi-experiment; treatment post-test mean 47.50 versus control 9.17 (Table 8). The article prints F=104.566 and sig= 0.000; no effect size is reported.

> "Table:  9 shows that there is significant difference in achievement scores for essay items for students taught using VFT and students taught using conventional methods for the topic of Colonization and Succession in Mangrove Ecosystems with the value of  F=104.566 and sig= 0.000 (p <0.05)."
