### Lilin Zhu and Gang Xiang 2021

Lilin Zhu and Gang Xiang. (2021). Investigating student understanding of heat engine: a case study of Stirling engine. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac342b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac342b)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quantitative analysis of written answers from three classes (sizes 14 to 54) at Sichuan University in 2019. The article reports science error ratios of "8.3% (Xiang), 14.3% (Zhu) and 10.2% (Wu)" against engineering error ratios of 71.8%, 52.1% and 71.4% (Table I). No effect size is printed.

> "The bad news is that the engineering error ratios were extremely high, which were above 50% for all the classes."
