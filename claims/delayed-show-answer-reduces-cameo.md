## Evidence

### Alexandron 2017

Alexandron, G., Ruipérez-Valiente, J. A., Chen, Z., Muñoz-Merino, P. J., Pritchard, D. E. (2017). Copying@Scale: Using Harvesting Accounts for Collecting Correct Answers in a MOOC. Computers & Education, 108, 96-114. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.01.015

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of harvesting on quizzes with show answer accidentally enabled (6.4% of submissions harvested) versus other chapter quizzes (3.83%), and midterm (3.43%, disabled) versus final exam (6.35%, enabled).

> "Clearly removing 'show answer' feedback substantially reduces CAMEO. That it does not reduce it further reflects that students can still harvest solutions using exhaustive search"
