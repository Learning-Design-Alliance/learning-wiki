## Evidence

### Sapey-Triomphe 2023

Sapey-Triomphe, L.-A., Sanchez, G., Hénaff, M.-A., Sonié, S., Schmitz, C., & Mattout, J. (2023). Disentangling sensory precision and prior expectation of change in autism during tactile discrimination. npj Science of Learning, 8:54. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00207-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Experiment I behavioral analysis of threshold dispersions: NT showed adaptation (d = 0.49, p < 0.01) while ASD did not (p = 0.38); the group comparison was "significantly higher in NT than ASD participants" with d = 0.70 and BF = 5.85.
