## Evidence

### Perry 2026

Perry, R.C., Filippi, R., Toledano, M.B. et al. (2026). Multilingualism educational attainment and cognitive development in UK adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00411-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal SEM of change in a latent cognitive factor (cognitive flexibility, verbal and spatial working memory, non-verbal reasoning) in 1,673 SCAMP adolescents; the null pattern "held when controlling for SES and ethnicity" (SM β = .09, p = .217; ML β = -.02, p = .758). No effect size printed.

> "there was no significant effect of SM (ϐ = .06, p = .260) or ML (ϐ = -.06, p = .282) on change in cognitive factor scores from age 12 to age 14"

### Perry 2026 (2)

Perry, R.C., Filippi, R., Toledano, M.B. et al. (2026). Multilingualism educational attainment and cognitive development in UK adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00411-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Same longitudinal change model: "SES (ϐ = .29, p = .014) and ethnicity (ϐ = .19, p = .007) were both significant predictors of cognitive development", with explained variance rising from R2 = .01 to R2 = .08.

> "SES (ϐ = .29, p = .014) and ethnicity (ϐ = .19, p = .007) were both significant predictors of cognitive development and variance explained increased by eight-fold when these factors were included in the model"
