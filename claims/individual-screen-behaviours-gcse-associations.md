International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-015-0269-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post hoc multilevel regression analyses of 845 adolescents (Table 3): TV viewing β −9.6 (p = 0.011), internet use β −8.8 (p = 0.023), computer games β −9.8 (p = 0.052), homework β 17.4 (p = 0.007), reading β 29.9 (p = 0.003). The article reports "all non-screen behaviours were positively associated with academic performance".

> "TV viewing and Internet use were separately negatively associated with academic performance whereas all non-screen behaviours were positively associated with academic performance."
