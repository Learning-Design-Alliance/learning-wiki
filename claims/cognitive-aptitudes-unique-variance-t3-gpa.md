### Vera María; Cortés José A 2021

Vera María; Cortés José A. (2021). Emotional and Cognitive Aptitudes and Successful Academic Performance: Using the ECCT. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 18(24), 13184. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413184

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-sample dominance analysis for T3 GPA in 796 students reports the three cognitive aptitudes' standardized dominance estimates (Table 4: verbal 0.004, numerical 0.008, abstract 0.003), with the printed percentages quoted above.

> "we confirmed that cognitive aptitudes explain a unique percentage of the variance in T3 academic performance (5.77%). This percentage is distributed in 3.08% for numerical aptitude (H1b confirmed); 1.54% for verbal aptitude (H1 a confirmed); and 1.15% for abstract reasoning (H1c confirmed)."
