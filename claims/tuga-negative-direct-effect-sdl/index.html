Hui Zhao & Huijuan Gu 2026¶

Hui Zhao & Huijuan Gu. (2026). Thoughtless use of generative artificial intelligence and college students’ self-directed learning: a multi-group SEM analysis of gender differences. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-54337-y

Path analysis from the PLS-SEM bootstrapping results (Table 6) in the Results section. The TUGA→SDL path was significant and negative, with "β = − 0.42, t = 6.76, p < .001", supporting H1. No standardized effect size beyond β is printed.

"Among the direct predictors of SDL, MOV exhibited the strongest effect (β = 0.68, t = 11.63, p < .001), followed by the TUGA ( β = − 0.42, t = 6.76, p < .001) and SE ( β = 0.23, t = 3.93, p < .001)."
