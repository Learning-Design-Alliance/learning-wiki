# Teachers lacking nutrition education background were less likely to teach nutrition

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In multivariate logistic regression, teachers who never received nutrition education were less likely to teach nutrition than those with formal college nutrition education (aOR 3.60, 95% CI 1.86–6.96). [→ Li Fan 2020](#li-fan-2020)

## Evidence

### Li Fan 2020

Li Fan, Yuan Yaqun, Xu Xinming, Chen Jingsi, Li Jiaxuan, He Gengsheng, Chen Bo. (2020). Nutrition Education Practices of Health Teachers from Shanghai K-12 Schools: The Current Status, Barriers and Willingness to Teach. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17010086

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression on the 904-teacher survey (Table 3). Teachers with no nutrition education had aOR 3.60 (95% CI 1.86–6.96) for not teaching versus formally educated teachers; the article reports "a poorer background of nutrition education, were less likely to teach nutrition".

> "health teachers who were male, with a higher school level, less concerned about nutrition, and had a poorer background of nutrition education, were less likely to teach nutrition (Table 3)."
