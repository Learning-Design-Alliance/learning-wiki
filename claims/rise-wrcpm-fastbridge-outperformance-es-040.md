### Schechter et al 2024

Schechter et al. (2024). Scholastic RISE and RISE Up program evaluation, Florida district, 2022-2023 school year. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/scholastic-rise-and-rise-up/

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Matched comparison during the 2022-2023 school year in a Florida district; a subgroup of eight treatment schools and all comparison schools used FastBridge between January and May 2023. Treatment students "did significantly outperform students in the control group on Words Read Correctly Per Minute" with effect size = +0.40.
