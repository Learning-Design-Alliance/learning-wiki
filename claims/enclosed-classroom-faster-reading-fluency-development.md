## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Within-child reading fluency development (change in words read per minute) was significantly greater during enclosed-classroom terms than open-plan terms. [→ Gary Rance 2023](#gary-rance-2023)

## Evidence

### Gary Rance 2023

Gary Rance, Richard C. Dowell and Dani Tomlin. (2023). The effect of classroom environment on literacy development. npj Science of Learning 8:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00157-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-child analysis of the 146 students completing the longitudinal protocol, with environment alternated term-by-term. Enclosed terms gained "14.0 (SD = 12.4) words/min" versus 7.2 for open-plan terms (t = 4.24, p < 0.001).

> "Mean change in WARP score for enclosed school terms was 14.0 (SD = 12.4) words/min and for the open-plan terms was 7.2 (SD = 12.9) words/min ( t = 4.24, p < 0.001; 95% CI for paired difference: 3.7, 10.0 words/min)."

### Gary Rance 2023 (2)

Gary Rance, Richard C. Dowell and Dani Tomlin. (2023). The effect of classroom environment on literacy development. npj Science of Learning 8:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00157-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed effect analysis with participant as random variable and condition, order and year as fixed factors; the article reports the enclosed-versus-open effect size as 0.26 (weak) based on pooled SD = 14.3. Order and year were not significant.

> "The condition factor was signiﬁcant ( F = 108.3, p < 0.001) but “order” (F = 0.28, p = 0.597) and “year” (F = 1.50, p = 0.215) were not."
