Related Claims¶

  • ReadAbout had a positive and significant impact in the study's second year among teachers with one year of experience using the intervention — related
  • ReadAbout showed a positive, statistically significant impact (effect size 0.22) on social studies reading comprehension for second-cohort students taught by teachers with one prior year of experience with the curriculum. — related
  • Four supplemental reading comprehension curricula had no positive impact on fifth-grade students' test scores, with negative impacts in some cases — a broader claim this one bears on
  • The four supplemental reading comprehension curricula had no statistically significant impacts on students one year after the end of their implementation (Cohort 1 follow-up in sixth grade). — related
  • Reading for Knowledge had a statistically significant negative impact on students' reading comprehension scores in the study's first year — related
  • A large randomized trial found no statistically significant effects of Project CRISS® on standardized grade 5 comprehension measures (James-Burdumy et al., 2009) — related
  • After one school year of implementation (first cohort), the curricula produced no statistically significant positive impacts, and four statistically significant negative impacts were observed. — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Student subgroup impact differences were rarely statistically significant: six of 288 differences in the second cohort and eight of 360 subgroup analyses in the follow-up year. — related
  • Project CRISS® produced statistically significant greater free-recall gains than regular instruction in grades 4 and 6 (Horsfall & Santa, 1994) — related
