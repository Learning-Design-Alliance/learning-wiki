`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

First-year evaluation of the Pay for Success initiative begun in February 2015 with nearly 400 children in six sites. The benchmark required performance at or above the national average on five of six subscales of the Teaching Strategies Gold Assessment System; the article reports "59% of 4-year-olds met the defined school readiness benchmark".

> "The first year evaluation findings were reported in spring 2016 for 328 of 449 4-year-olds who met the eligibility criteria. Results indicated that 59% of 4-year-olds met the defined school readiness benchmark (Gaylor et al., 2016). Among full-day preschool participants, 67% met the benchmark."
