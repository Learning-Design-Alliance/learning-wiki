## Subclaims
`q3 i?` 31% of programs reported simulation equipment sitting idle and unused, most often citing inadequate training; chi-square analyses showed better faculty education resources (χ2 (1, N = 377) = 50.112, p < 0.01) and personnel resources (χ2 (1, N = 374) = 24.388, p < 0.01) were reported more frequently in programs where equipment was not idle. ## Evidence

### McKenna K. D. 2015

McKenna K. D., Carhart E., Bercher D., Spain A., Todaro J., Freel J. (2015). Simulation Use in Paramedic Education Research (SUPER): A Descriptive Study. Prehospital Emergency Care. [doi:10.3109/10903127.2014.995845](https://doi.org/10.3109/10903127.2014.995845)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analyses from the census survey relating reported faculty education and personnel resources to whether equipment sat idle; 31% of programs reported idle equipment and inadequate training was the top cited reason (26%).

> "We found that better faculty education resources χ2 (1, N = 377) = 50.112, p < 0.01 and personnel resources χ2 (1, N = 374) = 24.388, p < 0.01 were reported more frequently in programs in which equipment was not idle."
