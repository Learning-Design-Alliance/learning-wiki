## Subclaims

## Evidence

### K. Moore 2013

K. Moore, J. Giannini & W. Losert. (2013). Toward Better Physics Labs for Future Biologists. [doi:10.1119/1.4870388](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4870388)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' summary of multiple surveys administered in two small IPLS test classes at UMD in 2012-2013 (maximum N = 31), with graduate student developers as TAs; they report a shift in perception with "A majority of our mostly pre-med students" valuing the labs for their career. No effect size is printed.

> "One significant accomplishment of these pilot labs is the shift of student perception.  A majority of our mostly pre-med students considered the physics laboratories as valuable for their future career."
