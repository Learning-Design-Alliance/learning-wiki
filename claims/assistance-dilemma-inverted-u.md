The review reports, citing work on the assistance dilemma, that quicker or easier task progress does not necessarily translate to increased learning gains, and that extensive assistance should be provided for new, difficult tasks and gradually reduced as expertise grows (Kalyuga et al. 2003).

> "Studies on the assistance dilemma (Koedinger et al. 2008) generally imply an inverted U-shaped relationship between task difficulty (after assistance) and learning progress"
