# Among four-year college enrollees, former and late-arriving English Learners persisted at or above district-average rates while long-term English Learners with IEPs persisted at substantially lower rates

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2`–`r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Late-arriving English Learners had a 78% two-year persistence rate in four-year colleges and former English Learners 74%, compared to a 70% district average. [→ de la Torre 2024](#de-la-torre-2024)
`q2 i?` Long-term English Learners with IEPs persisted in four-year colleges at 55%, well below the district average of 70%, but similar to other students with IEPs (53%). [→ de la Torre 2024 (2)](#de-la-torre-2024-2)

## Evidence

### de la Torre 2024

de la Torre, M., Blanchard, A., Franklin, K., Angeles, C., & Allensworth, E.M. (2024). English Learners in Chicago Public Schools: A Spotlight on High School Students. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/english-learners-in-chicago-public-schools-a-spotlight-on-high-school-students

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of four-year college enrollees in CPS cohorts. Despite below-average enrollment (32% vs. 46%), late-arriving English Learners persisted at higher rates than even former English Learners.

> "Late-arriving English Learners had a slightly higher two-year persistence rate in four-year colleges at 78%."

### de la Torre 2024 (2)

de la Torre, M., Blanchard, A., Franklin, K., Angeles, C., & Allensworth, E.M. (2024). English Learners in Chicago Public Schools: A Spotlight on High School Students. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/english-learners-in-chicago-public-schools-a-spotlight-on-high-school-students

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive analysis among four-year college enrollees. Other students with IEPs with similar cumulative GPAs had a similarly low persistence rate of 53%, and appendix comparisons showed the differences were not substantial versus similar students with IEPs.

> "Long-term English Learners with IEPs had a two-year persistence rate of 55%, compared to the district average of 70%."
