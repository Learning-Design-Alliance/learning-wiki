### Pierre Haritz 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Narrative review attributing this cross-sectional finding to Yao et al.'s study integrating digital, AI, and scientific literacy in medical AI trust. The article reports AI literacy specifically reduced trust in medical AI; no effect sizes are printed.

> "A further study showed that digital literacy increased trust in medical AI, but AI literacy specifically reduced it, as deeper awareness of risks generated what the authors call informed skepticism rather than blind confidence"
