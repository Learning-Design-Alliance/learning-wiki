### Digital Promise 2018

Digital Promise. (2018). Meeting Learners Where They Are: Using Microsoft Forms to Drive Improvement in Learning Outcomes. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8a12967d-b67d-404f-875f-3dc61d0cc32e [doi:10.51388/20.500.12265/52](https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/52)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview evidence from one Scottish elementary teacher whose students worked in small groups to write quiz questions in Forms; negotiating realistic answer options prompted discussion the teacher had not planned.

> "So, they talked about what other kinds of real answers there could be, and they decided to put in more sensible numbers. I didn’t plan on that part, but it ended up being a really good part of the lesson."
