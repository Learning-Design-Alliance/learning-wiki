# iSTART training showed no effect on posttest comprehension when participants were prompted to self-explain

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no main effect of iSTART treatment on posttest comprehension score, no main effect of question type, and no significant interaction. [→ McCarthy 2017](#mccarthy-2017)

## Evidence

### McCarthy 2017

McCarthy, K. S., Jacovina, M. E., Snow, E. L., Guerrero, T. A., & McNamara, D. S. (2017). iSTART therefore I understand: But metacognitive supports did not enhance comprehension gains. Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Education, Wuhan, China: Springer. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED577122

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 2 (treatment) x 2 (question type) ANCOVA controlling for pretest comprehension compared iSTART and control conditions on the posttest. The article reports "no main effect of treatment on posttest comprehension score, F < 1.00, ns".

> "This analysis indicated no main effect of treatment on posttest comprehension score, F < 1.00, ns. There was no main effect of question type, F < 1.00, nor was there a significant interaction, F < 1.00"
