## Evidence

### Guoyang Liu 2025

Guoyang Liu, Yueyuan Zheng, Michelle Hei Lam Tsang, Yazhou Zhao & Janet H. Hsiao. (2025). Understanding the role of eye movement pattern and consistency during face recognition through EEG decoding. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00316-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Individual-level correlation analysis among 84 participants performing an old-new face recognition task with simultaneous EEG and eye tracking. The article reports that "a more eye-focused pattern was associated with a higher EEG decoding accuracy" in the high-alpha band (Table 1, r = 0.242, p < 0.05).

> "decoding performance measure TOI-AUC in the high-alpha band was negatively correlated with the eye movement pattern measure A– B scale, indicating that a more eye-focused pattern was associated with a higher EEG decoding accuracy."
