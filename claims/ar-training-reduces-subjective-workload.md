## Evidence

### Xiong Jian 2024

Xiong Jian, Dai Xiaoqin, Zhang Yuyang, Liu Xingchao, Zhou Xiyuan. (2024). Augmented reality for basic skills training in laparoscopic surgery: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11387-5

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of two studies measuring subjective workload with the NASA Task Load Index during laparoscopic cholecystectomy. Only two studies "used the same assessment tool, providing consistent support for this finding"; GRADE rated the evidence moderate quality.

> "AR technology led to a mean reduction of 2.95 points in perceived workload (95% CI [−4.95, −0.95], p = 0.003)"
