### Budak Vildan 2025

Budak Vildan, Kılıç Hülya Fırat, Cevheroğlu Seda. (2025). The Impact of a Flipped Learning on Nursing Students' Patient Safety Competencies and Satisfaction With the Education Method: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 69 fourth-year nursing students randomly assigned to flipped learning (n = 34) or traditional instruction (n = 35) for 6 weeks. The intervention group's PSCSE total gain was significantly higher, "t = 2.093; p = 0.040"; posttest means were 183.56 ± 13.27 vs 180.11 ± 17.48.

> "A comparison of the pretest and posttest measurements revealed that the scores obtained by the intervention group from the PSCSE (t = 2.093; p = 0.040)"
