### Yoshimatsu Yuki 2023

Yoshimatsu Yuki, Ohtake Yoichi, Ukai Mamiko, Miyagami Taiju, Morikawa Toru, Shimamura Yoshinosuke, Kataoka Yuki, Hashimoto Tadayuki. (2023). “Diagnose, Treat, and SUPPORT”. Clinical competencies in the management of older adults with aspiration pneumonia: a scoping review. European Geriatric Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41999-023-00898-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Comparison of the original JAPEP simplified-Delphi list against competencies extracted from the 99 included studies (Table 2). The review reports that "three new competencies were identified and added to the list", and that no competencies were omitted because all were discussed in included studies.

> "In addition, three new competencies were identified and added to the list. These were prevention of recurrence, prognosis evaluation, and palliative care."
