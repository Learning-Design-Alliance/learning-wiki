Evidence¶

Crouch 2003¶

Crouch, C. H., Watkins, J., Fagen, A. P., & Mazur, E. (2003). Peer Instruction: Engaging Students One-on-One, All At Once. Research-Based Reform of University Physics. https://scholar.google.com

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Cross-year comparison on one quantitative problem included on the spring 2000 final exam of the algebra-based E&M course, which had also been given in spring 1999 under a different, traditionally teaching instructor. The article reports PI students "significantly outperformed" the traditionally taught students; no effect size is printed.

"We found that the students taught with PI (spring 2000, N = 155) significantly outperformed the students taught traditionally (spring 1999, N = 178),"
