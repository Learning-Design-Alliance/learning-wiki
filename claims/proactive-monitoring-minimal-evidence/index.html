The guide's Summary of Evidence for Recommendation 1 reports six contributing studies, five meeting WWC group design standards without reservations. The two positive studies examined the graduating school domain, but "there is no direct test of the full recommendation."

"Two studies found that the recommended practices improved student outcomes in at least one of the three outcome domains related to dropout prevention."
