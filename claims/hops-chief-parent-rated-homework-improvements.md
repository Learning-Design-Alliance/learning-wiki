## Evidence

### Langberg 2017

Langberg, J. M., Dvorsky, M. R., Molitor, S. J., Bourchtein, E., Eddy, L. D., Smith, Z. R., Oddo, L. E., & Eadeh, H.-M. (2017). Overcoming the Research-to-Practice Gap: A Randomized Trial With Two Brief Homework and Organization Interventions for Students With ADHD as Implemented by School Mental Health Providers. Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology. Advance online publication. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/ccp0000265

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVAs comparing HOPS (N = 111), CHIEF (N = 111), and waitlist (N = 52) on posttreatment outcomes controlling for pretreatment scores, with FDR-corrected post hoc contrasts. The article reports "parent HPC homework completion factor, F = 29.19, p <.001" and parent HPQ total F = 30.30; the abstract states effect sizes were large.

> "Significant effects at posttreatment, controlling for pretreatment score, were found for both organization and homework outcomes including: for parent HPC homework completion factor, F /H1100529.19, p /H11021.001 and homework materials management factor, F /H1100517.21, p /H11021.001; parent HPQ total score, F /H1100530.30, p /H11021.001;"
