### Panczyk M 2017

Panczyk M, Juszczyk G, Zarzeka A, Samoliński Ł, Belowska J, Cieślak I, Gotlib J. (2017). Evidence-based selection process to the Master of Public Health program at Medical University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1007-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis of MCQ exams across five recruitment years (2008–2012). "The level of reliability of MCQs exams as measured by Cronbach's α oscillated between 0.530 and 0.747, with the highest recorded for 2012." Standard error of measurement was approximately three points (range 3.0–3.4).
