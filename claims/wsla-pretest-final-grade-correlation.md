## Evidence

### Sánchez Judit 2024

Sánchez Judit, Lesmes Marta, Rubio Margarita, Gal Beatriz, Tutor Antonio S. (2024). Enhancing academic performance and student engagement in health education: insights from Work Station Learning Activities (WSLA). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05478-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman's rank correlation analysis of grades from the 2019–2020 (n = 268) and 2020–2021 (n = 233) cohorts. The printed effect sizes are r = 0.923 (2020–2021) and r = 0.875 (2019–2020) for pre-test versus final grades, both p < 0.01, i.e., "correlation coefficient = 0.923" and "correlation coefficient = 0.875". Correlations are observational, not causal.

> "the most robust correlation between pre-test scores and final grades is observed in both the 2020–2021 cohort (correlation coefficient = 0.923, p-value < 0.01) and the 2019–2020 cohort (correlation coefficient = 0.875, p-value < 0.01)"
