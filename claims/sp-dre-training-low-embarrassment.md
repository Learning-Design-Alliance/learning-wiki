## Evidence

### Nikendei Christoph 2015

Nikendei Christoph, Diefenbacher Katja, Köhl-Hackert Nadja, Lauber Heike, Huber Julia, Herrmann-Werner Anne, Herzog Wolfgang, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Jünger Jana, Krautter Markus. (2015). Digital rectal examination skills: first training experiences, the motives and attitudes of standardized patients. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0292-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of post-training interviews with 4 SPs. Three of the four reported no embarrassment, which they attributed to "the professional implementation and pleasant atmosphere during the training" or to personal biographical experiences; one SP found the lecturer-performed DRE embarrassing.

> "Three of the four SPs indicated that they did not have any feelings of embarrassment at any time during the training."
