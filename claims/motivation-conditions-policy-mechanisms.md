## Evidence

### Goicolea Isabel 2015

Goicolea Isabel, Hurtig Anna-Karin, San Sebastian Miguel, Vives-Cases Carmen, Marchal Bruno. (2015). Developing a programme theory to explain how primary health care teams learn to respond to intimate partner violence: a realist case-study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-0899-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Findings from the La Virgen realist case study, based on thematic analysis of interviews and observation. The article reports that Murcia's regional interventions 'have not equally reached all La Virgen´s professionals and that its adoption was mediated by the individual interest in IPV'; uncommitted providers hardly participated in voluntary training and felt no pressure from weak monitoring.

> "Since training was offered on a voluntary basis, only those professionals with interest in the topic benefited from the training."
