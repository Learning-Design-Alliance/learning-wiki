## Evidence

### Paschke 2025

Paschke, P., Steinmayr, R., Mertens, A., Neubauer, A. B., & Spinath, B. (2025). Re‐Examination of the Reciprocal Relation Between Intrinsic Motivation and Competence Beliefs in Math Using the Random‐Intercept Cross‐Lagged Panel Framework. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.70028

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Across all fitted RI-CLPMs in both datasets, "the random intercepts correlated positively and significantly", with correlations ranging from r = 0.63 to r = 0.89, indicating strong stable between-person associations between the two constructs.

> "The random intercepts correlated positively and significantly in all RI‐CLPMs (Dataset 1 cross‐lagged: r = 0.84, p < 0.001; Dataset 1 autoregressive: r = 0.89, p < 0.001; Dataset 2, seven time points cross‐lagged: r = 0.73, p < 0.001; Dataset 2, seven time points autoregressive: r = 0.70, p < 0.001; Dataset 2, three time points cross‐lagged: r = 0.63, p = 0.047; Dataset 2, three time points autoregressive: r = 0.77, p < 0.001)."
