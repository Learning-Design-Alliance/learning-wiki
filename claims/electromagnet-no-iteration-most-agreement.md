Jacob T. Stanley, Weifeng Su, and H. J. Lewandowski (2017). Using lab notebooks to examine students' engagement in modeling in an upper-division electronics lab course. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Notebook coding of the electromagnet activity, where a complete cycle required prediction, measurement, and comparison, with revision expected only if comparison indicated disagreement. "all but two felt their measurement agreed with their prediction" and "no student proceeded thusly" with iterative cycles. Descriptive result.

> "Of those, all but two felt their measurement agreed with their prediction and therefore did not proceed with a revision to their model or sys- tem. Only these two students documented revisions to their model but neither made subsequent measurements or predictions. Though this activity had a good deal of opportunity for iterative modeling cycles, no student proceeded thusly."
