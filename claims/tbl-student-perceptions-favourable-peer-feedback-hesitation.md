`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of qualitative outcomes from 19 of the 37 included studies; recurring themes included "increased confidence, motivation, and appreciation for peer learning".

> "Of 37 studies, 19 included qualitative insights on student outcomes related to TBL participation. Studies on student perceptions of TBL were generally favourable, emphasising enhanced engagement, collaboration, and critical thinking."

### Ahmad-Naik Danish 2026 (2)

Ahmad-Naik Danish, Webb Alexandra, Namboothiri Vaishnavi Krishnan, Valter Krisztina. (2026). The practices and strategies for implementing team-based learning in pre-clinical medical education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08713-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes peer-feedback hesitation to Burgess et al. and Lerchenfeldt et al., reporting students' "difficulty in offering constructive criticism" and a need for training in professional feedback.

> "Studies by Burgess et al. [15] and Lerchenfeldt et al. [20] have highlighted students’ difficulty in offering constructive criticism, indicating a need for more training in delivering professional feedback."
