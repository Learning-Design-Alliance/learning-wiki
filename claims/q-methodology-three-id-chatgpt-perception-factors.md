## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Q-factor analysis of 19 instructional designers' Q sorts yielded three distinct perception factors toward ChatGPT integration. [→ Fan Yang · Jill E. Stefaniak 2025](#fan-yang-jill-e-stefaniak-2025)
`q2 i?` Factor 1 (Pessimistic Evaluators) accounted for the highest explained variance at 37%, with 10 participants loading significantly. [→ Fan Yang · Jill E. Stefaniak 2025](#fan-yang-jill-e-stefaniak-2025)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Q-methodology study in which 19 practicing instructional designers sorted 25 statements about integrating ChatGPT into design practice; principal component analysis with varimax rotation produced a three-factor solution. The article reports that the "three-factor solution, which explained 61% of the variance in the model, was generated from the Q-factor analysis."
