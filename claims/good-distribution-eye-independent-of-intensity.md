## Evidence

### C. E. Ferree and Gertrude Rand 1917

C. E. Ferree and Gertrude Rand. (1917). The Power of the Eye to Sustain Clear Seeing Under Different Conditions of Lighting. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072369

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Laboratory testing program comparing actual lighting installations in common use, with acuity-sustain records taken before and after reading tasks. The authors report that when distribution factors are controlled, the eye is "practically independent of intensity" across the ranges encountered in lighting work.

> "our tests indicate that the eye, so far as the ranges that are apt to be encountered in the work of lighting are concerned, is practically independent of intensity"
