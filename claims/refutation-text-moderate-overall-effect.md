## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Across 44 independent comparisons, refutation text was associated with a moderate positive effect on learning outcomes (g = 0.41) relative to non-refutation conditions. [→ Schroeder 2022](#schroeder-2022)

## Evidence

### Schroeder 2022

Noah L. Schroeder & Aurelia C. Kucera. (2022). Refutation Text Facilitates Learning: a Meta-Analysis of Between-Subjects Experiments. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09656-z

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 44 independent between-subjects comparisons from 33 studies, totaling 3,869 participants, comparing refutation text to non-refutation text conditions. The pooled effect was "a moderate overall effect size (g = 0.41" with significant heterogeneity (I2 = 60.76).

> "A random-effects meta-analysis of the 44 independent comparisons found a moderate overall effect size (g = 0.41, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.30, 0.51], n = 3,869) with significant heterogeneity (Q(43) = 109.59, p < 0.001, I2 = 60.76)."
