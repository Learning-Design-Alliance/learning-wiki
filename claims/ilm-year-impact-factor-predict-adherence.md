## Evidence

### Carina Schreiber & Michael Becker 2026

Carina Schreiber & Michael Becker. (2026). Intensive Longitudinal Methods in School Research: A Systematic Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10203-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

Linear regression on the adherence score (mean of met/not-met guideline ratings) for the 257 included articles: "publication year ( R² = 0.08, F(1, 256) = 23.32, p <.001)" significantly predicted adherence, indicating improving reporting practices over time.

> "both publication year ( R² = 0.08, F(1, 256) = 23.32, p <.001) and journal impact factor (R² = 0.05, F(1, 256) = 12.0, p <.001) statistically significantly predicted articles’ adherence to the reporting guidelines."

Same regression analysis: "journal impact factor (R² = 0.05, F(1, 256) = 12.0, p <.001)" significantly predicted adherence, indicating greater transparency in higher-impact journals; the review notes adherence remained questionably low even for recent articles.

> "both publication year ( R² = 0.08, F(1, 256) = 23.32, p <.001) and journal impact factor (R² = 0.05, F(1, 256) = 12.0, p <.001) statistically significantly predicted articles’ adherence to the reporting guidelines."
