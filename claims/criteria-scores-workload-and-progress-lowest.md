Descriptive analysis of Likert-scale criterion scores (N = 56) in the cross-sectional survey. The article reports "workload cost (criterion A4, rated on average at 3.29/5)" and "the capacity of the tool to account for trainee progress (criterion B6, rated at 3.53/5)" as the lowest scores; no inferential test or effect size is printed for these descriptives.

> "Despite relatively high scores overall, certain criteria such as workload cost (criterion A4, rated on average at 3.29/5) and the capacity of the tool to account for trainee progress (criterion B6, rated at 3.53/5) obtained lower scores, which calls for reflection and adjustments to improve these aspects."
