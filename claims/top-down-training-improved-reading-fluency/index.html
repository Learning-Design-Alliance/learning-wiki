Oliver and Young 2016¶

Rhonda Oliver, Shahreen Young. (2016). Improving reading fluency and comprehension in adult ESL learners using bottom-up and top-down vocabulary training. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 6(1), 111-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2016.6.1.6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Experiment 2, the same classroom quasi-experiment with contextual (top-down) training of 32 target words highlighted in a projected training text. Transfer-text reading time was faster than control (M = 324.24 s vs M = 359.80 s; p < .0001); words per minute was also significantly higher (99.38 vs 91.61). No effect size is printed.
