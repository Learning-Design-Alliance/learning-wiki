`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with five expert academics following quantitative scoring. The review of expert narratives reports candidates "did not conduct evaluations with students on learning outcomes after the game"; interview coding reliability was κ = 0.79, p < .001. No effect size is reported for this qualitative finding.

> "Experts stated that candidates did not conduct evaluations with students on learning outcomes after the game and could not pedagogically conclude the process."
