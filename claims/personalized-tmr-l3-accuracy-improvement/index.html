Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Personalized TMR improves error correction (higher incorrect-to-correct transitions) and reduces persistence of incorrect responses, mainly for challenging items — related
  • TMR efficacy depends on memory strength and task difficulty: benefits concentrate on moderately strong and challenging items, with limited advantage for easy (L1) items — a broader claim this one bears on
  • In the personalized TMR group, memory improvement for challenging items correlates positively with SW-spindle coupling (r = 0.70), but no such correlation appears in the All condition or other groups — related
  • Auditory cueing during NREM sleep elevates slow wave and spindle power relative to no stimulation, with effects concentrated in frontal and central regions — related
  • Full word-pair cues can promote rather than disrupt reactivation when delivered with sufficient spacing and during stable NREM sleep — related
  • Multivariate classification of SW power, spindle power, and SW-spindle coupling distinguishes the personalized TMR, TMR, and control groups above chance, peaking around 3 s after cue onset — related
  • Hippocampal sharp-wave ripples during SWS and waking support memory consolidation and spatial task performance — related
