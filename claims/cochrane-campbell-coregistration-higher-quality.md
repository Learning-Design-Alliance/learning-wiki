# Campbell reviews co-registered with Cochrane have higher methodological quality than reviews registered only with Campbell

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · review `r3` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Reviews co-registered with Cochrane were rated of higher AMSTAR 2 quality than Campbell-only reviews (OR = 5.57). [→ Wang X 2021](#wang-x-2021)

## Evidence

### Wang X 2021

Wang X, Welch V, Li M, Yao L, Littell J, Li H, Yang N, Wang J, Shamseer L, Chen Y, Yang K, Grimshaw JM. (2021). The methodological and reporting characteristics of Campbell reviews: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1134

`q3 · i3` · `review · r3`

Ordered logistic regression across 96 reviews (22 co-registered with Cochrane, 74 Campbell-only) found co-registered reviews of "higher methodological quality than those only registered with Campbell" (OR = 5.57, 95% CI [2.13, 14.58], p < .001).

> "Reviews co‐registered with Cochrane were of higher methodological quality than those only registered with Campbell (OR = 5.57, 95% CI [2.13, 14.58], p < .001)"
