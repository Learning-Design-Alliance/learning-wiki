## Evidence

### Gagnon 2022

Gagnon, D. J., Baker, R. S., Gagnon, S., Swanson, L., Spevacek, N., Andres, J., Harpstead, E., Scianna, J., Slater, S., San Pedro, M. O.C.Z. (2022). Exploring players' experience of humor and snark in a grade 3-6 history practices game. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09906](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09906)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment assigning four script versions to anonymous grade 3-6 players of Jo Wilder and the Capitol Case; one-way ANOVA and Kruskall-Wallis tests on embedded questionnaires. The article reports a "significant effect of game script type on player perception of Jo's likability" and on game humor, with no significant effect for enjoyment or interest in history.

> "There was a significant effect of game script type on player perception of Jo’s likability for the four game script types, χ2 (df = 3)= 235.6, p<.0001, as well as on the player’s perception of game humor"
