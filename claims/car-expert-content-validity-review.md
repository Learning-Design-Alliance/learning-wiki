`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content and substantive validity evidence collected through expert review surveys. Post-secondary biology educators (n = 6 for CAR 2.0, including five new participants plus one returning reviewer) from a range of institution type, teaching experience, and disciplinary expertise reviewed the CAR 2.0. All reviewers agreed the revised version was clearly written with clear distinctions between performance levels.

> "we collapsed expert feedback on completeness and clarity questions, and found that 90% of respondents (9/10) agreed that the CAR's five key components sufficiently describe an excellent scientific conclusion."
