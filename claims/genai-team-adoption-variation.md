### Hauke Sandhaus 2024

Hauke Sandhaus, Maria Teresa Parreira, and Wendy Ju. (2024). Student Reflections on Self-Initiated GenAI Use in HCI Education. LLMs as Research Tools Workshop at CHI conference. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.01467](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.01467)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Group interview findings in §3.1 on how students used GenAI: heavily integrating teams anthropomorphized the AI as a collaborator — "We would kind of just say - hey let's ask Chatty" (I1) — and even allocated it a hypothetical share of a project prize.

> "one group found it to be generally unhelpful early on and discontinued its use for interactive device design. Some teams shifted to using GenAI only for isolated use cases where they felt it excelled, while a few teams integrated GenAI into the core of their project work."
