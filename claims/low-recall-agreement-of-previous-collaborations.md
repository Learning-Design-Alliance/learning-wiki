## Evidence

### Eberle 2021

Eberle, J., Stegmann, K., Barrat, A., Fischer, F., & Lund, K. (2021). Initiating scientific collaborations across career levels and disciplines – a network analysis on behavioral data. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09345-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive social network analysis of the pre-workshop collaboration networks from social network questionnaires across workshops; the article reports the average reciprocity value as "r = 0.544, SD = 0.121" for perceived previous collaborations.

> "the average reciprocity value indicates that participants disagree quite a lot in their perception of whether they had collaborated with each other before (r = 0.544, SD = 0.121); only about half of the previous collaborations that were reported by one participant were confirmed by the indicated previous collaboration partner."
