### Chen Yijian 2026

Chen Yijian, Huang Xiaojun, Qiu Haibo, Cheng Linling, Yu Yunsong, Ma Xiaochun, Feng Sizhou, Li Qi, Wu Depei, Huang Wenxiang, Chen Dechang, Lv Xiaoju, Hu Jianda, Wang Jingbo, Li Jiabin, Yang Wenjie, Zhan Qingyuan, Sun Bing, Wang Minggui. (2026). Video-Based Training on Physicians' Proficiency in Invasive Fungal Disease: A Multicentre, Cluster Randomised Controlled Trial. Mycoses. https://doi.org/10.1111/myc.70198

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomised multicentre trial (18 tertiary hospitals in China) comparing video vs. PDF training; ANCOVA on per-protocol set score changes with pre-training scores as covariate. The video group showed "more pronounced improvements post‐training than the PDF group in total" scores (p = 0.008).

> "In the PPS, the video group demonstrated more pronounced improvements post‐training than the PDF group in total (7 [−4, 16] vs. 0 [−8, 13], p = 0.008), IC (3 [0, 4] vs. 0 [−3, 3], p < 0.001), and IA (3 [−4, 10] vs. 1 [−6, 8], p = 0.044)"
