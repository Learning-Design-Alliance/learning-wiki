## Evidence

### Poletti Martina 2017

Poletti Martina, Rucci Michele, Carrasco Marisa. (2017). Selective attention within the foveola. Nature Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4622

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 4 repeated the Experiment 3 discrimination task with normal fixation in five observers. The article reports effects also present in trials without microsaccades and when the center of gaze remained far from the target (>14′; F(2,4) = 52; p<0.0001) or close to the fixation marker (<7′; F(2,4) = 19; p=0.0009).

> "Also in this case there were significant accuracy (ANOVA F(2,4) = 79; p<0.0001) and reaction time (ANOVA F(2,4) = 98; p<0.0001) effects."
