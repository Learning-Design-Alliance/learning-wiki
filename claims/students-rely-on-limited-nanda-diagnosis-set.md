### Kestel 2026

Kestel, Sıdıka, and Korkmaz, Fatoş. (2026). Nursing Students' Perceptions of Instructional Approaches in Nursing Diagnosis Education: A Qualitative Study. Nursing Open, 13(5). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70571

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of student interviews found narrow diagnostic repertoires. Students attributed this to uncertainty interpreting data and anxiety about unfamiliar diagnoses, with one noting that unfamiliar diagnoses "induces anxiety and concern about potential errors."

> "Despite the extensive array of over 200 NANDA‐I diagnoses, participants reported consistently identifying only 7 or 8 diagnoses as care problems during their training, with limited exposure to diagnoses in different domains."
