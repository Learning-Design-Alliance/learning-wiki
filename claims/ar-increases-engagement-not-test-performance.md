Pilot study of 62 second-year Italian medical students comparing AR+3D printing model (AEducaAR) versus traditional atlas learning for topographical learning; "no statistical (t test, unpaired) difference in objective test results" but increased engagement on Likert survey (43/62 response rate). No effect size printed.
