# Training participation predicts progression from poor to general GI-POCUS proficiency but not from general to good

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wang Gang 2026

Wang Gang, Liu Yingying, Wang Shengyun, Gao Silong, Lin Hui, Wu Qian, Huang Debin, Pan Yueshuai, Zhao Yuxiao, Wei Huimin, Wei Lili. (2026). Factors Influencing ICU Nurses’ Proficiency in Point‐of‐Care Gastrointestinal Ultrasound: A National Cross‐Sectional Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/3178958

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression in the 2009-nurse survey. Training predicted the poor-to-general transition with "OR for untrained vs. trained = 0.556" (OR = 0.556) but not the good level; the null for good proficiency is reported without an effect size.

> "training participation was associated with the transition from “poor” to “general” proficiency (OR for untrained vs. trained = 0.556, 95% CI: 0.419–0.737, p < 0.001) but did not significantly predict “good” proficiency"
