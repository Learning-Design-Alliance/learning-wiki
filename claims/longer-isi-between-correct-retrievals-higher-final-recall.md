## Evidence

### Mary A. Pyc 2009

Mary A. Pyc, Katherine A. Rawson. (2009). Testing the retrieval effort hypothesis: Does greater difficulty correctly recalling information lead to higher levels of memory? Journal of Memory and Language 60 (2009) 437–447. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2009.01.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2: 98 undergraduates practiced under short or long contracting or fixed ISI schedules. The article reports the replicated main effect of ISI with means .87 versus .62; no effect size printed.

> "A significant main effect of ISI indicated that final test performance was greater after longer versus shorter ISIs between correct retrievals (long ISI M = .87, SE = .03; short ISI M = .62, SE = .02)."
