## Subclaims
`q3 i?` MME student participation in the three intervention components was positively associated with perceived WMC skills, with statistically significant associations for role play (p=0.001), web-patient encounter (p<0.0001), and number of components (p=0.002), but not web course participation (p=0.21). Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-020-06571-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exposure analysis within MME schools (Table 4): students completing two components had the highest mean OSCE score at 9.02 (SE 0.39) versus 8.22 (SE 0.44) for none, p=0.28; no effect size printed.

> "At MME schools, student participation in the three intervention components was positively associated with total OSCE score, with the largest difference for the web course (0.43 items difference on average), although the associations were not statistically significant (Table 4)."
