Follow-up LMM with a gender by PC2-quartile interaction on exam scores from all courses. The article reports the significant Q2 interaction and non-significant Q3/Q4 interaction terms; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "the average increase in exam score when comparing a medium level of learner-centered activity (Q2 of PC2) to a low level of learner-centered activity (Q1 of PC2) was significantly larger in male students than in female students (3.78 versus 2.59 exam points; p < 0.001)"
