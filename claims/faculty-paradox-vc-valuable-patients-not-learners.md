## Evidence

### Lee J 2022

Lee J, Kron F. (2022). Virtual care is growing, but who will train upcoming learners to practice it? Med Educ. 2022;56(12):1155-1157. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14934

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The commentary reports, citing Shepherd and colleagues' qualitative exploration of faculty and learner experiences of VC integration, that faculty "helpful for patients but not for learners". The commentary attributes this paradox to VC's original intended beneficiaries being patients.

> "Faculty paradoxically saw VC as valuable and not valuable—helpful for patients but not for learners. This contradiction is not surprising given that the original intended beneficiaries of VC were patients, not learners."
