### Briseño-Garzón 2016

Briseño-Garzón, A., Han, A., Birol, G., Bates, S., & Whitehead, L. (2016). Faculty perceptions of challenges and enablers of effective teaching in a large research-intensive university: Preliminary findings. Collected Essays on Learning and Teaching, 9, 133-144. https://celt.uwindsor.ca/ceelt/vol9/brisenogarzon

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quantitative survey data on time allocation in faculty's largest enrolment courses showed lecture consumed significantly more time than any other classroom practice, though the average proportion was less than 50% with high variability (larger standard deviation than other variables).

> "in their largest enrolment courses, participants report spending significantly more time on lecture (described in the survey as "Instructor or TA presenting content") than any other classroom practice (Figure 3). Encouragingly, the average proportion of time spent on presentation of content is less than 50%, but it should be noted this variable has a much larger standard deviation than the others."
