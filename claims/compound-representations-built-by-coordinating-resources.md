# A student solving a complex physics problem builds compound representations by linking semiotic resources across multiple modes

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In an oral exam case, one exemplary student combined gestural, diagrammatic, and written-symbolic semiotic resources into compound representations that developed across three episodes to determine the direction and magnitude of the magnetic field of a current sheet. [→ Nandana Weliweriya 2019](#nandana-weliweriya-2019)

## Evidence

### Nandana Weliweriya 2019

Nandana Weliweriya, Eleanor C. Sayre, Dean Zollman. (2019). Case Study: Coordinating Among Multiple Semiotic Resources to Solve Complex Physics Problems. arXiv. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aaf5d5](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aaf5d5)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Detailed case study analysis of transcripts and video from Larry's individual oral exam in an upper-division Electromagnetism I course, coded by three researchers for reliability. The analysis found Larry "builds up his compound representation to include more details in it" across episodes, ending with the magnitude B = µ0α/2.

> "Larry’s case exempliﬁes coordination between multiple semiotic resources with diﬀerent disciplinary aﬀordances to build up compound representations."
