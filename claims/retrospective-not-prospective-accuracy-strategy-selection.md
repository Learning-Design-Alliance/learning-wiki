## Evidence

### Marie Geurten & Patrick Lemaire 2017

Marie Geurten & Patrick Lemaire. (2017). Metacognition for Strategy Selection during Arithmetic Problem Solving in Young and Older Adults. https://www.google.com/search?q=Metacognition+for+Strategy+Selection+during+Arithmetic+Problem+Solving+in+Young+and+Older+Adults

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment with 37 young and 29 older adults; t tests compared A'ROC indexes to chance in selection-execution and selection-only tasks. The coefficients were "significantly larger than .50 in both young and older adults for the RCJ, but not for the EoS judgments". No effect size printed.

> "results of the t tests comparing A' ROC indexes to chance showed that the metacognitive coefficients were significantly larger than .50 in both young and older adults for the RCJ, but not for the EoS judgments"
