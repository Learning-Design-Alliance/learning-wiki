Fixed-effect estimates from the fully adjusted negative-binomial mixed-effects model (eTable 4). The same model showed rural practice (IRR, 1.18) and older, more comorbid patient panels (IRR, 1.02 and 1.21) associated with increased prescribing; specialty was not associated in adjusted analyses.
