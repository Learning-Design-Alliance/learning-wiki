`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the template-based coding of 77 participants' interview statements, "Positive design features (63/295, 21.4%)" was the largest subtopic within positive statements describing Visual Clot (179/319, 56.1% of all statements). Example comments included pictorially summarized presentation and seeing relevant ROTEM information at-a-glance.
