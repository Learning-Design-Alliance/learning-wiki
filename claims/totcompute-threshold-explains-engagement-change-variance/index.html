Evidence¶

Ghergulescu 2016¶

Ghergulescu, I. & Muntean, C. H. (2016). ToTCompute: A Novel EEG-Based TimeOnTask Threshold Computation Mechanism for Engagement Modelling and Monitoring. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0111-2

q2 · i? · design · r2

Experimental case study with 50 participants playing the Fire Protocol educational game while wearing an Emotiv EPOC headset; regression analysis indicated TimeOnTask "explains up to 76.2 % of the variance in engagement change."

"The results of an experimental case study show that the proposed ToTCompute mechanism can be used to automatically compute threshold values for the TimeOnTask generic engagement metric, with regression analysis indicating that TimeOnTask explains up to 76.2 % of the variance in engagement change."

Olga C. Santos 2016¶

Olga C. Santos, Milos Kravcik, Jesus G. Boticario. (2016). Preface to Special Issue on User Modelling to Support Personalization in Enhanced Educational Settings. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:809–820. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0114-z

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Ghergulescu and Muntean (2016), an experimental case study of the ToTCompute mechanism, which computes with EEG the TimeOnTask threshold after which student engagement decreases; it "explains up to 76.2 % of the variance in engagement change". No standardized effect size is printed.

"The results of an experimental case study showed that ToTCompute could be used to automatically compute threshold values for the TimeOnTask generic engagement metric, which explains up to 76.2 % of the variance in engagement change."
