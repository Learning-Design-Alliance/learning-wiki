Karen Aldrup · Bastian Carstensen · Uta Klusmann. (2022). Is Empathy the Key to Effective Teaching? A Systematic Review of Its Association with Teacher-Student Interactions and Student Outcomes. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09649-y

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of 41 records; nine of twelve studies on bullying intervention found effects (62% of effects significant and positive). Studies used hypothetical bullying scenarios with self-reported empathic concern and a "greater likelihood of intervening in the bullying situation".

> "Seven studies, including teachers from preschool to the secondary school level, found that teachers feeling empathic concern for a hypothetical student who was a victim of bullying reported a greater likelihood of intervening in the bullying situation"
