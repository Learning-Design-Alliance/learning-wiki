# Self-other rating differences vary across competency domains in first-year nursing OSCEs, with the largest discrepancy in social/communication skills (Rating Type × Domain interaction η²ₚ = 0.416)

## Evidence

### Roszipal Benjamin 2025

Roszipal Benjamin, Szelesi Gabriella, Ernst Martin, Hoffelner Alexander, Wagner Michael. (2025). Competency development in early nursing training: a cross-sectional OSCE study of self-assessment versus examiner ratings. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08279-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of 102 first-year nursing students with complete data from a summative second-semester OSCE, comparing self- and examiner ratings across three domains. The printed interaction effect size was large: η²ₚ = 0.416 (F(2, 202) = 71.96, p <.001). Main effects of Rating Type (η²ₚ = 0.152) and Domain (η²ₚ = 0.484) were also significant.

> "These effects were qualified by a statistically significant Rating Type × Competence Domain interaction, F(2, 202) = 71.96, p <.001, η²ₚ = 0.416, demonstrating that the pattern of self-other differences varied across domains."
