## Evidence

### Høllesli 2022

Høllesli, L. J., Ajmi, S. C., Kurz, M. W., Tysland, T. B., Hagir, M., Dalen, I., Qvindesland, S. A., Ersdal, H., & Kurz, K. D. (2022). Simulation‐based team‐training in acute stroke: Is it safe to speed up? Brain and Behavior, 12, e2814. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.2814

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis of the same before-after registry cohort, categorizing stroke mimics into eight prespecified groups and comparing proportions before versus after simulation training. The vertigo contrast "8 patients/2.5% vs. 55 patients/8.6%" reached p < .001; no effect size is printed.

> "When subgrouping SMs into prespecified categories, there was a significantly higher proportion of patients with peripheral vertigo (8 patients/2.5% vs. 55 patients/8.6%, p < .001) among IVT‐treated patients (Table 4) after implementation of simulation training."
