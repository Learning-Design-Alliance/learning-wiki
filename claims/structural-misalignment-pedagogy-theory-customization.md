`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-tabulation and Sankey analysis (Figs. 8–10) of pedagogical goals, theoretical principles, and customization methods across the 23 studies. The review reports a dominant route of improving writing products through technically optimized models, with hybrid architectures embedding learning theories notably rare.

> "Figure 9 illustrates that prompt engineering and fine-tuning were predominantly aligned with performance-oriented theories. Even when learning-, discourse-, or interaction-centered frameworks were invoked, their influence was typically confined to interface design or task framing, while the underlying AI systems operated in a largely theory-agnostic manner."
