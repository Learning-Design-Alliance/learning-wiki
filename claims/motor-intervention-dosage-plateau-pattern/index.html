Evidence¶

Ketcheson et al. 2017¶

Leah Ketcheson, Janet Hauck and Dale Ulrich. (2017). The effects of an early motor skill intervention on motor skills, levels of physical activity, and socialization in young children with autism spectrum disorder: A pilot study. Autism 21(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361316650611

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pairwise t-tests of biweekly TGMD-2 observations in the experimental group only (n = 11). Locomotor gains were significant weeks 0–2 and 2–4 (t(1, 10) = 3.54 and 2.48), then "plateaued thereafter"; object control showed a significant weeks 4–8 gain (t(1, 10) = 2.42, p ⩽ 0.05).

"Locomotor raw scores plateaued thereafter (from weeks 4 to 6 and beyond)."
