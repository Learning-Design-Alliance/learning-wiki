## Evidence

### de Villiers Scheepers 2015

de Villiers Scheepers, M. J. & Maree, L. (2015). Fostering Team Creativity in Higher Education Settings. e-Journal of Business Education & Scholarship of Teaching, 9(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1167371

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA in the same quasi-experimental study. Supportive communication means rose from 3.593b before the workshop to 3.978a after, and the "perceived improvement of this skill endured to the integration stage" (3.8600a three months later).

> "Supportive communication mean scores were also significantly different over time [Wilks Lambda=0.44, F(3, 47) =20.780, p<0.0005], however the effect size was not as strong as for the other constructs (multivariate partial eta squared =.57)"
