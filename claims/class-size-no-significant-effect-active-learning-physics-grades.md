### Connor Gorman 2018

Connor Gorman, David Webb, Kevin Gee. (2018). Hierarchical Linear Modeling Approach to Measuring the Effects of Class Size and Other Classroom Characteristics on Student Learning in an Active-Learning Based Introductory Physics Course. University of California, Davis. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1809.00218](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1809.00218)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

HLM analysis of five years (2012–2016) of grade data from three active-learning introductory physics courses at a large public R1 university, with students nested in discussion-labs nested in lectures. The article reports that class size "does not have a significant effect on student grades"; no effect size is printed, so no magnitude is reported.

> "It is found that class size over the ranges studied does not have a significant effect on student grades which were used as a proxy for students’ understanding of the underlying material."
