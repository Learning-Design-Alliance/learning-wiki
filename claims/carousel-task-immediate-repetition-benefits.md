The article summarises Lynch and Maclean's (2000) ESP-course carousel study: pairs prepared posters, then presenters answered rotating visitors' questions without repeating a rehearsed presentation. The review reports benefits "in terms of pronunciation, vocabulary and grammar for most participants"; no statistics are printed.
