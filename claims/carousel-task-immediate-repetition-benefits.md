## Evidence

### Rafael Zaccaron 2019

Rafael Zaccaron. (2019). Ideas for integrating meaningful immediate repetition tasks in the English as an additional language classroom: promoting fluency. BELT, 10(2). http://dx.doi.org/10.15448/2178-3640.2019.2.32988

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The article summarises Lynch and Maclean's (2000) ESP-course carousel study: pairs prepared posters, then presenters answered rotating visitors' questions without repeating a rehearsed presentation. The review reports benefits "in terms of pronunciation, vocabulary and grammar for most participants"; no statistics are printed.

> "The results indicated that immediate repetition offered benefits in terms of pronunciation, vocabulary and grammar for most participants."
