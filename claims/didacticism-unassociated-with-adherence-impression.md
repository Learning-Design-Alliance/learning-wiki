## Evidence

### Wojewodka G 2017

Wojewodka G, Hurley S, Taylor SJC, Noble AJ, Ridsdale L, Goldstein LH. (2017). Implementation fidelity of a self-management course for epilepsy: method and assessment. BMC Medical Research Methodology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12874-017-0373-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Simple regression analysis across the 15 evaluated sessions testing didacticism as predictor of adherence and overall impression. The article reports "no associations between didacticism and adherence" and no association with overall impression; adding group size as covariate did not change results.

> "Using simple regression analysis, we found no associations between didacticism and adherence (β = 0.23 [95% CI: -0.27 – 0.87]), or between didacticism and overall impression scores (β = 0.081 [95% CI: -0.52 – 0.68])."
