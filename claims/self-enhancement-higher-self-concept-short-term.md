## Evidence

### Sticca 2017

Sticca, F., Götz, T., Nett, U. E., Hubbard, K., & Haag, L. (2017). Short- and Long-Term Effects of Over-Reporting of Grades on Academic Self-Concept and Achievement. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(6), 842-854. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000174

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional associations among intercepts from a trivariate parallel process latent growth model of three waves (916, 719, 647 students) across four subjects. The study found "higher initial scores of self-enhancement were associated with higher initial scores of self-concept"; no direct self-enhancement–achievement intercept association was reported.

> "Thus, higher initial scores of self-enhancement were associated with higher initial scores of self-concept, which were in turn associated with higher initial scores of achievement."
