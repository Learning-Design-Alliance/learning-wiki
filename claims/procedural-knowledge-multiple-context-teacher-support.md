## Evidence

### Yavuz Akpinar 2018

Yavuz Akpinar, Özlem Şengül. (2018). Two Modalities of the Contextualized Courseware in Three Modalities of Classroom Use. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165740

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc Bonferroni comparison following the procedural-knowledge ANOVA [F(3, 147)=3,934; p=0,010] in the four-group quasi-experiment. The article reports group A "is significantly higher than the group D", with the context-courseware groups performing similarly.

> "achievement of procedural knowledge of Logic unit of the group A (M=0,95; SD=1,632) is significantly higher than the group D (M=0,00; SD=1,358), whereas the groups studying with one or more context software performed similar achievement"
