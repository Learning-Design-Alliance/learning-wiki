## Evidence

### Casey W. Miller 2019

Casey W. Miller, Benjamin M. Zwickl, Julie R. Posselt, Rachel T. Silvestrini, Theodore Hodapp. (2019). Typical Physics PhD Admissions Criteria Limit Access to Underrepresented Groups but Fail to Predict Doctoral Completion. Draft preprint; journal version in Science Advances. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1126/sciadv.aat7550](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aat7550)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Result from the multivariate logistic regression across models, using NRC-based ranking terciles. The article reports Tier 1 enrollment associated with two times higher completion odds (e.g., US women p = 0.008; US men p < 0.001); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The parameter with the strongest, consistent relationship with PhD completion is program rank, with probability of completion significantly higher for more highly ranked programs."
