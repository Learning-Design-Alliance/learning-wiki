## Evidence

### Naoto Nishida 2022

Naoto Nishida, Hinako Nozaki, and Buntarou Shizuki. 2022. Laugh at Your Own Pace: Basic Performance Evaluation of Language Learning Assistance by Adjustment of Video Playback Speeds Based on Laughter Detection. In Proceedings of the Ninth ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S '22). https://doi.org/10.1145/3491140.3528299

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Experiment with 20 EFL learners in a with-system vs without-system comparison using two constructed videos from a sitcom with comprehension quizzes. "learners with low L2 proficiency" (lowest 2 per group) scored higher with the system; effect sizes 2.69 (Q1) and 1.71 (Q2).

> "learners with low L2 proficiency (the lowest 2 people from each group) could answer both V1 and V2 more correctly with our system (Q1:p<.005 and Q2:p<.05, the effect sizes are 2.69 and 1.71"
