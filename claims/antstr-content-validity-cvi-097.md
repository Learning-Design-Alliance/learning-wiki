## Evidence

### Celik N 2025

Celik N, Sarmasoglu Kilikcier S, Tasdelen Teker G, Keskin G, Akın M, Ozmert S, Elcin M, Senel E. (2025). Anaesthesiologists' Non-Technical Skills in Denmark Form: cross-cultural adaptation, reliability and validity of Turkish version in paediatric surgery team. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-003249

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Lawshe-technique content validity ratings by eight experts (three anaesthesia and resuscitation, three medical education, two surgical and nursing management) using a 3-point appropriateness scale; 'all items were retained' because CVR values exceeded 0.78.

> "All items had CVR values higher than the recommended value of 0.78 for situations involving eight experts,30 thus, all items were retained."

### Celik N 2025 (2)

Celik N, Sarmasoglu Kilikcier S, Tasdelen Teker G, Keskin G, Akın M, Ozmert S, Elcin M, Senel E. (2025). Anaesthesiologists' Non-Technical Skills in Denmark Form: cross-cultural adaptation, reliability and validity of Turkish version in paediatric surgery team. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-003249

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validity index computed by averaging item CVR values from the eight-expert Lawshe review; the CVI 'was estimated to be 0.97', which the authors interpret as strong consensus and evidence of content validity.

> "The content validity index (CVI), calculated by averaging the CVR values for all items, was estimated to be 0.97."
