## Evidence

### Gobet Fernand 2022

Gobet Fernand, Sala Giovanni. (2022). Cognitive Training: A Field in Search of a Phenomenon. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/17456916221091830

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Summary of first-order meta-analyses (Tables 1 and 2) across ten populations, comparing uncorrected naive estimates with estimates corrected for publication bias and restricted to active control groups; corrected estimates are essentially zero.

> "the estimated effect sizes of the first-order meta-analyses are small in Table 1 (range = 0.04–0.19) and essentially zero in Table 2 (range = −0.03 to 0.02). In both tables, the amount of true heterogeneity is very small."

### Gobet Fernand 2022 (2)

Gobet Fernand, Sala Giovanni. (2022). Cognitive Training: A Field in Search of a Phenomenon. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/17456916221091830

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Second-order meta-analysis integrating 14 independent first-order meta-analyses covering WM training, video-game training, music, chess, and exergames; the corrected second-order grand mean for far transfer was g = 0.00 with zero true between-meta-analyses variance.

> "The analysis included 14 statistically independent first-order meta-analyses (332 samples, 1,555 effect sizes, and 21,968 participants) of near- and far-transfer effects in different populations"
