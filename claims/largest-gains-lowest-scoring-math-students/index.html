New research: Chicago showed instructional improvements under standards reform 2022¶

New research: Chicago showed instructional improvements under standards reform, while many other places did not. (2022). UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · causal · r?

A districtwide analysis of Chicago student achievement data between 2014-15 and 2017-18 found the largest improvements "for students with the lowest tested math scores". No effect size is printed in the release.

"The largest improvements in mathematics instruction and learning gains were for students with the lowest tested math scores, who were least likely to be in classes with strong instructional practices prior to standards implementation."
