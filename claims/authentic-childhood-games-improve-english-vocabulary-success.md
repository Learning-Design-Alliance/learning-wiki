## Evidence

### Hursen 2016

Hursen, C., & Salaz, D. (2016). Investigating the effects of authentic childhood games in teaching English. Cypriot Journal of Educational Science. 11(2), 58-62. http://sproc.org/ojs/index.php/cjes/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on post-test scores of 43 kindergarten students (22 experimental, 21 control) after a 6-week intervention. Experimental group M=89.17 (sd=8,73) versus control M=62.16 (sd=14,73); "there was a significant difference (t=7,352, p<0.05)".

> "While both groups pre-test results showed that there weren’t any significant difference (t=.523, p>0.05), the post-test results showed that there was a significant difference (t=7,352, p<0.05) between both groups."
