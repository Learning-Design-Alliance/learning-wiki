`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A questionnaire administered to all students of the two General Physics 2 courses in Fall 2010 asked their opinion of the in-class group activities. The report states "43.2% said they liked it, whereas 16.8% disliked it, and the remaining 40% were indifferent", and the authors interpret the large indifferent group as doubt about the group work.
