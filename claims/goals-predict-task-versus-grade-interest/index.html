Evidence¶

Darnon 2006¶

Darnon, C., Muller, D., Schrager, S. M., Pannuzzo, N., & Butera, F. (2006). Mastery and Performance Goals Predict Epistemic and Relational Conflict Regulation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 98(4), 766–776. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.98.4.766

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Study 2 logistic regression on the binary request measure showed "Mastery goals significantly predicted the request for the text" (B = 0.55, Wald χ² = 4.88, p < .03); performance goals and the interaction were non-significant.

"Mastery goals significantly predicted the request for the text ( B /H11005 0.55), Wald /H92732(1, N /H1100563) /H110054.88, p /H11021.03. The more participants endorsed mastery goals, the more they asked for the detailed text."

Darnon 2006 (2)¶

Darnon, C., Muller, D., Schrager, S. M., Pannuzzo, N., & Butera, F. (2006). Mastery and Performance Goals Predict Epistemic and Relational Conflict Regulation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 98(4), 766–776. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.98.4.766

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Study 2 logistic regression showed "the more participants endorsed performance goals, the more they asked for their grade" (B = 0.41, Wald χ² = 4.22, p < .04); mastery goals (B = −0.21, p = .39) and the interaction were non-significant.

"As expected, the more participants endorsed performance goals, the more they asked for their grade ( B /H110050.41), Wald /H92732(1, N /H11005 63) /H110054.22, p /H11021.04."
