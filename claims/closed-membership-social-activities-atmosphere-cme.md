## Evidence

### Dionyssopoulos A 2014

Dionyssopoulos A, Karalis T, Panitsides EA. (2014). Continuing medical education revisited: theoretical assumptions and practical implications: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0278-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview data: closed, invitation-restricted participation was among the parameters interviewees cited as accounting for the EWAPS positive atmosphere.

> "annual workshops have been designed as closed events and the attendance is restricted to members. A prerequisite for a non member to participate is by invitation of the board of participants only"

### Dionyssopoulos A 2014 (2)

Dionyssopoulos A, Karalis T, Panitsides EA. (2014). Continuing medical education revisited: theoretical assumptions and practical implications: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0278-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interviewees considered the inclusive social programme central to building a long-lasting friendly collaborative atmosphere, with one participant noting members "keep in touch and collaborate all year round".

> "the scientific program is counterbalanced by a variety of social activities which include all participants and their families. This policy has been considered by interviewees to contribute to establishing over the years a friendly collaborative atmosphere amongst members, which even exceeds the remit of the annual meetings"
