Wang 2026¶

Wang, N. (2026). The impact of artificial intelligence literacy on learning engagement among university students: the mediating role of psychological capital and the moderating role of professional identity. Frontiers in Psychology, 17:1892204. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1892204

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Independent-samples t-tests and one-way ANOVA on demographic differences in the 1,198-student survey (§4.2). Males "scored significantly higher than females on artificial intelligence literacy"; student leaders also scored higher on psychological capital (t = 5.46) and professional commitment (t = 5.79), with grade differences on engagement (F = 8.19) and commitment (F = 15.82).

"males scored significantly higher than females on artificial intelligence literacy (t = 5.51, p < 0.001). Females scored significantly higher than males on learning engagement (t = 9.74, p < 0.001). Student leaders reported significantly higher levels of learning engagement (t = 5.72, p < 0.001)"
