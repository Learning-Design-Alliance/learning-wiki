Simon Baradziej 2026¶

q1 · i? · review · r3

Industry-reported evaluation of the Agent Laboratory framework (Johns Hopkins University and AMD), in which coordinated specialised agents progress a research idea through literature review, plan formulation, and experimental implementation. The review reports the co-pilot quality ratings; no effect size is printed.

"Human oversight in “co-pilot” mode improved output quality ratings from an average of 3.8/10 to 4.38/10, with particularly significant improvements in clarity (+0.23) and presentation (+0.33)."
