## Evidence

### Emily Marshman 2018

Emily Marshman, Zeynep Y. Kalender, Christian Schunn, Timothy Nokes-Malach, and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). A longitudinal analysis of students' motivational characteristics in introductory physics courses: Gender differences. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2017-0185](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2017-0185)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Change-score analysis from the beginning to the end of calculus-based Physics 2 (electricity and magnetism); the overall MANOVA on motivation change was not significant, but the fascination univariate test was. No effect size printed.

> "That is, females' average fascination decreased significantly more than males (ANOVA result gives F(1,246)=6.412, p=0.012) after taking a calculus-based Physics 2 course."

### Emily Marshman 2018 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same change-score analysis in calculus-based Physics 2; the value univariate test reached significance while the overall MANOVA did not. No effect size printed for the change.

> "Furthermore, females' value associated with physics decreased significantly more than males' (ANOVA result gives F(1,246 )=5.637, p=0.018) in calculus-based Physics 2."
