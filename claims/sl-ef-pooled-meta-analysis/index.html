Evidence¶

Pedraza 2024¶

Pedraza, F., Farkas, B. C., Vékony, T., Haesebaert, F., Phelipon, R., Mihalecz, I., Janacsek, K., Anders, R., Tillmann, B., Plancher, G. & Németh, D. (2024). Evidence for a competitive relationship between executive functions and statistical learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00243-9

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Fixed-effect meta-analytic model pooling the two studies' correlations between EF factor scores and implicit SL. The quote reports "r = −0.115, 95% CI = [-0.218, -0.008], p = 0.035"; heterogeneity variance was τ2 = 0 with I2 of 0% and non-significant Cochran's Q.

"The pooled effect size was negative and significantly different from zero, r = −0.115, 95% CI = [-0.218, -0.008], p = 0.035 (Fig. 6). The weights of Study 1 and Study 2 in the pooled effect were 54.3% and 45.7%, respectively."
