Improvement of students’ communication skills through targeted training and the use of simulated patients in dental education—a prospective cohort study. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey-based self-report in the same prospective cohort (n = 34), comparing responses before and after the targeted training with the Wilcoxon signed rank test. The article reports significance for open-ended questions only; no effect size is printed.
