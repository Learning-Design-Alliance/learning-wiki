### Lin John C 2024

Lin John C, Shin Christopher, Greenberg Paul B. (2024). The impact of the medical school admissions interview: a systematic review. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.76138

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review synthesis of self-reported personality surveys and final-year emotional intelligence and social competence measures in structured-interview studies at University Sains Malaysia. The review reports the interviewed cohort "scored higher on openness, conscientiousness, agreeableness, and extroversion"; no effect sizes were printed.

> "Students in the interviewed cohort scored higher on openness, conscientiousness, agreeableness, and extroversion and lower on neuroticism in self-reported surveys.26,28 Final-year medical students in the interviewed cohort also had higher levels of global emotional intelligence and social competence.26"
