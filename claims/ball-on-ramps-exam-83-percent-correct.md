## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2006

Michael C. Wittmann, Jeffrey T. Morgan, Roger E. Feeley. (2006). Laboratory-Tutorial activities for teaching probability. Submitted to Physical Review Special Topics Physics Education Research. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Final examination question taken from Jolly and Bao, requiring students to compare ball speeds on two ramp levels, recognize equal level lengths, and compare time on each level to total time. Table IV reports 45% correct with the explicit 2:1 ratio and 38% qualitatively correct; responses mapping the physical picture to the P(x) graph were not accepted.

> "Of the 44 students answering the question, 83% were completely or qualitatively correct with correct reasoning, while 14% showed some sort of sloped line, usually on level 2."
