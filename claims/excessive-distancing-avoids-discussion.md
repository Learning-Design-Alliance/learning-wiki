## Evidence

### Luke D. Conlin 2015

Luke D. Conlin. (2015). The use of epistemic distancing to create a safe space to sensemake in introductory physics tutorials. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1508.01574](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1508.01574)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video case study of one four-student group's first tutorial discussion (Episode 1, "Whatever. Next!"). The analyst interprets Chrissie's laughter, Daria's "we" and "pretty much," and Alan's "Whatever. Next!" as distancing moves whose combined effect was that "the group avoiding discussing their ideas." No effect size is reported.

> "Overall they epistemically distance themselves in a way that they have the effect of the group avoiding discussing their ideas."
