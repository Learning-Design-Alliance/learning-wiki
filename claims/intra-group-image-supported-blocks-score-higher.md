## Evidence

### Gimeno A. 2026

Gimeno A., Alberola‐Zorrilla P., Blasco‐Serra A., Sánchez‐Zuriaga D., Zaragozá R. (2026). Online anatomical images are an effective resource to improve knowledge acquisition and practical exam performance in gross anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70254

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intra-group comparison across all 180 intervention students using Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed rank test; the article reports "median of differences 1.000" with p < 0.0001, including students who took no quizzes.

> "in the practical exam, scores on the head, neck, and thorax questions were significantly higher than those on the abdomen and pelvis questions across the entire experimental group (median of differences 1.000), including students who did not engage with the online quizzes."
