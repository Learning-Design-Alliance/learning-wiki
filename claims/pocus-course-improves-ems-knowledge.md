# In one single-group pre-post study, EMS providers' knowledge of POCUS fundamentals and eFAST image interpretation rose from pre-course to post-course after an 8-hour simulation-based POCUS course with pre-course e-learning

## Evidence

### Weber Ariana 2025

Weber Ariana, Misra Asit, Rodriguez Richard D., Brotons Angel "Al", Mosetti Maria Antonietta, Lewiss Resa E., Tiozzo Eduard, Motola Ivette. (2025). Effectiveness of a simulation-based point-of-care ultrasound course for prehospital providers - a single group quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07675-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Single-group quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study of 128 EMS providers across 11 courses (2017–2024), using 20-item content-matched multiple-choice assessments. The article reports "a 21.4% point increase (p <.001; Cohen's d = 1.52)".

> "The pre-course assessment mean knowledge score was 67.6% (95% CI 64.7–70.5). The post-course mean knowledge score was 89.0% (95% CI 87.3–97.0), resulting in a 21.4% point increase (p <.001; Cohen's d = 1.52)."
