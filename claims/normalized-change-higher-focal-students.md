## Evidence

### Hoskins Tyler D. 2017

Hoskins Tyler D., Gantz J. D., Chaffee Blake R., Arlinghaus Kel, Wiebler James, Hughes Michael, Fernandes Joyce J. (2017). Effectiveness of a Low-Cost, Graduate Student–Led Intervention on Study Habits and Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-subjects tests from the repeated-measures ANOVA on normalized change (c) in Fall 2014 showed focal students' normalized gains exceeded controls', and normalized change differed by lecture section; the treatment × section interaction was not significant.

> "between-subjects tests revealed that, across all pairs of exams, normalized change in scores was higher for focal students than control students (F(1, 504) = 18.69, p = 0.0001, Figure 1B), normalized change differed by lecture section (F(2, 504) = 9.19, p < 0.0001)"

### Hoskins Tyler D. 2017 (2)

Hoskins Tyler D., Gantz J. D., Chaffee Blake R., Arlinghaus Kel, Wiebler James, Hughes Michael, Fernandes Joyce J. (2017). Effectiveness of a Low-Cost, Graduate Student–Led Intervention on Study Habits and Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-subjects tests on normalized change (c) in Spring 2015 replicated the Fall pattern: focal students showed higher normalized change than controls, with a lecture-section effect and no treatment × section interaction.

> "between-subjects tests revealed that, across all pairs of exams, normalized change in scores was higher for focal students than control students (F(1, 535) = 5.49, p = 0.0152, Figure 1D), normalized change differed by lecture section (F(2, 535) = 4.22, p = 0.0195)"
