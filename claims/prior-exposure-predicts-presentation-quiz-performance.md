## Evidence

### Steven W. Tarr and Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2024

Steven W. Tarr and Emily Alicea-Muñoz. (2024). Reflecting to learn in a physics multimedia communication course. [doi:10.1119/perc.2024.pr.tarr](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2024.pr.tarr)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized peer-evaluation study, a check-all-that-apply question established prior knowledge; 1033 quiz responses across 47 questions were graded. Prior-exposure students "moderately outperformed students who indicated none" (d = 0.31 ± 0.06, p <0.001).

> "students who indicated having some prior exposure to presentation content (e.g., college course, internship, self study) moderately outperformed students who indicated none, with high statistical significance (d = 0.31 ± 0.06, p <0.001)."
