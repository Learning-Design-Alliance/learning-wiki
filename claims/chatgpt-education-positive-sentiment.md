### Li 2023

Li, L., Ma, Z., Fan, L., Lee, S., Yu, H., & Hemphill, L. (2023). ChatGPT in education: A discourse analysis of worries and concerns on social media. [doi:10.1007/s10639-023-12256-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-023-12256-9)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RoBERTa-based sentiment classification of 247,484 tweets collected via the Twitter Search API from December 1, 2022 to March 31, 2023. The article reports 49,528 negative, 127,638 neutral, and 70,318 positive tweets, with the positive line "consistently positioned above the blue line in Figure 3."

> "The sentiment analysis suggests that Twitter users have a generally positive attitude towards the application of ChatGPT in education, as indicated by the orange line consistently positioned above the blue line in Figure 3."
