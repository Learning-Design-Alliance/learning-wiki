Evidence¶

Eu 2006¶

Leong Kwan Eu. (2006). Impact Of Geometer's Sketchpad On Students Achievement In Graph Functions. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology. http://www.mojet.net

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive questionnaire results on understanding of graph functions: the greater-depth item drew 61.54% combined disagreement, and on Question 5, 38.46% were unsure the software helped them understand graph functions better. Percentages only.

"From Question 6, 38.46% did not agree that they get to learn the topics on graph functions in greater depth, 23.08% strongly did not agree and another 19.23% were not convinced of the statement."

Eu 2006 (2)¶

Leong Kwan Eu. (2006). Impact Of Geometer's Sketchpad On Students Achievement In Graph Functions. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology. http://www.mojet.net

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive questionnaire results on self-confidence in problem solving (Table 6): agreement was a minority (15.38%) while combined disagreement reached 53.85% on the confidence item. Percentages only, no inferential test.

"For Question 10, 15.38% agreed on this while 38.46% were not sure whether they felt more confident at solving problems in the topic of function."
