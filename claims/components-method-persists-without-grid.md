## Evidence

### Jeffrey M. Hawkins 2009

Jeffrey M. Hawkins, John R. Thompson, and Michael C. Wittmann. (2009). Students’ Consistency of Graphical Vector Addition Method on 2-D Vector Addition Tasks. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings (venue not printed in text). [doi:10.1063/1.3266704](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266704)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview observation of Students D and E on the non-gridded questions. Student D drew components on an axis he made up; Student E sketched grid lines over each vector at the same scale and orientation. The article reports "Neither student changed from the components method when an obstruction to its use was present."

> "Neither student changed from the components method when an obstruction to its use was present in the question. Instead they persisted, one student starting with the method despite not having a grid"
