## Evidence

### Steven F. Wolf 2014

Steven F. Wolf, Leanne Doughty, Paul W. Irving, Eleanor C. Sayre and Marcos D. Caballero. (2014). Just Math: A New Epistemic Frame. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.065](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.065)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative video analysis of homework help session data: two researchers independently reviewed approximately 9 hours of video for postures, gestures, and gaze patterns, negotiating candidate episode start and stop times. The authors report the behaviors are "similar in nature" to the Worksheet e-frame.

> "The behaviors observed while students are in the Just Math e-frame are similar in nature to the behaviors described for the previously discussed Worksheet e-frame."
