## Evidence

### Çakiroğlu 2013

Çakiroğlu, Ü. (2013). Using a hybrid approach to facilitate learning introductory programming. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1), 161-177. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008878

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three open-ended pretest questions administered to both groups before the 10-week intervention; the article reports no significant difference between groups (t(74): -7.66, p: 0.446>0.05 as printed in Table 5). No effect size is printed.

> "The mean scores of the pretest were found to be simi lar between the two groups: EG  (M: 48.29; SD: 13.47) and CG (M: 50.52; SD: 11.96)."
