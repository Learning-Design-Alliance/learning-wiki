## Evidence

### Priniski 2019

Priniski, S. J., Rosenzweig, E. Q., Canning, E. A., Hecht, C. A., Tibbetts, Y., Hyde, J. S., & Harackiewicz, J. M. (2019). The Benefits of Combining Value for the Self and Others in Utility-Value Interventions. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000343

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the RCT's primary analyses, the omnibus intervention contrast against the summary-writing control condition was not significant for course performance or the motivational outcomes, as quoted.

> "Finally, there were no overall effects of the intervention contrast (comparing all UV conditions, on average, to the control condition) or interactions with the intervention contrast"

### Priniski 2019 (2)

Priniski, S. J., Rosenzweig, E. Q., Canning, E. A., Hecht, C. A., Tibbetts, Y., Hyde, J. S., & Harackiewicz, J. M. (2019). The Benefits of Combining Value for the Self and Others in Utility-Value Interventions. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000343

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the supplementary dummy-coded replication model comparing each UV condition individually against control, no condition reached significance; the authors attribute this to the smaller sample size, as quoted.

> "We did not find any statistically significant intervention effects in the dummy-coded model for the current study, likely due to the smaller sample size"
