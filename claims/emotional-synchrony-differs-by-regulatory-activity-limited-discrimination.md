## Evidence

### Duong Ngo 2024

Duong Ngo, Andy Nguyen, Belle Dang, Ha Ngo. (2024). Facial Expression Recognition for Examining Emotional Regulation in Synchronous Online Collaborative Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 34:650–669. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00378-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

FER results resampled into 379 coded 30-second segments, with synchrony counted second-by-second; the test yielded p = 0.029. Descriptively, synchrony "appear most often in SSRL segments (M = 8.67, SD = 10.69) while occurring least in socio-emotional interactions (M = 4.49, SD = 4.32)".

> "The Kruskal-Wallis H Test showed a slightly significant difference in the number of emotional synchronies among different regulatory activities with p = 0.029."

### Duong Ngo 2024 (2)

Duong Ngo, Andy Nguyen, Belle Dang, Ha Ngo. (2024). Facial Expression Recognition for Examining Emotional Regulation in Synchronous Online Collaborative Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 34:650–669. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00378-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors note large standard deviations compound the problem, "making it challenging to attribute a future mean value, such as 8.59, to any specific condition," and call for more discriminative measures.

> "Nevertheless, the close mean values for emotional synchrony frequency among interactions for SSRL (M SSRL = 8.67), Cognitive (M Cognitive = 8.31), and Task Execution (M Task Execution = 8.50) suggest that this metric is not sufficient to differentiate among these interactions."
