## Subclaims

Numerical simulation shown in Fig. 7: average marks drawn from a normal distribution with standard deviation 5, 30 students, results averaged over 1000 iterations. The article reports "The composite student achievement remains almost constant up to 70% average marks due to the passing threshold" and that achievement reaches 100% at about 80% average marks.

> "From this ﬁgure, it can be seen that CLO attainment is a linear function of questions' average marks mapped to that CLO."
