## Evidence

### Gregor Stiglic 2023

Gregor Stiglic, Leon Kopitar, Zhe He, Primoz Kocbek, Lucija Gosak, Prithwish Chakraborty, Pablo Meyer, Jiang Bian. (2023). Improving Primary Healthcare Workflow Using Extreme Summarization of Scientific Literature Based on Generative AI. DSHealth at SIGKDD '23, August 6 – 10, 2023, Long Beach, CA. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.15715](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.15715)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square comparison of accuracy for US students across the three groups. Table 1 prints UC1 accuracy 0.847 (G1), 0.922 (G2), 0.792 (G3) with "G2>G3", and UC2 0.924 (G1), 0.844 (G2), 0.775 (G3) with "G1>G3".

> "Similarly, for US Students, the results showed there were statistically significant differences in both UC1 ( χ2=6.778, p=0.034) as well as UC2 (χ2=12.905, p=0.002), where the post hoc pairwise comparisons in UC2 showed that accuracy in G1 is higher than in G3, but for UC1 we observed that G2 is higher than G3."
