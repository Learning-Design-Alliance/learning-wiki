Anonymous satisfaction survey using an autonomous evaluation scale (Cronbach's α = 0.75) administered to both groups. Table 4 reports "16(84.2)" experimental versus "10(52.6)" control for improved subjective initiative, "15(78.9)" versus "9(47.4)" for clinical speculative thinking, and "13(68.4)" versus "8(42.1)" for increased study pressure (p = 0.220).
