Evidence¶

Cho Eunsoo 2015¶

Cho Eunsoo, Roberts Garrett J., Capin Philip, Roberts Greg, Miciak Jeremy, Vaughn Sharon. (2015). Cognitive Attributes, Attention, and Self-Efficacy of Adequate and Inadequate Responders in a Fourth Grade Reading Intervention. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4793275/

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

MANOVA profile analysis across six cognitive attributes (working memory, nonverbal reasoning, verbal knowledge, listening comprehension, phonological awareness, rapid naming) for the four reader groups. The omnibus test showed "statistically significant differences in cognitive attributes across the groups, F (21, 442) = 9.89, Wilk's Λ = 0.384, partial η2 = .273". A significant shape effect (F (18, 441) = 3.984, partial η2 = .132) confirmed profiles differed in pattern, not just level.

"We found statistically significant differences in cognitive attributes across the groups, F (21, 442) = 9.89, Wilk's Λ = 0.384, partial η2 = .273. The multivariate effect size was estimated at .54, indicating 54% of the variance in the first canonical variate was accounted for by group membership."
