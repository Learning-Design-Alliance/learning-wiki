# DLP teachers reported significant increases in using technology for both content and pedagogy, but no significant difference from non-DLP teachers in basic technology skills

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` DLP teachers reported statistically significant increases in using technology to teach content and improve pedagogy. [→ Bakhshaei 2018](#bakhshaei-2018)
`q2 i?` There was no significant difference between DLP and non-DLP teachers in perceived basic technology skills. [→ Bakhshaei 2018 (2)](#bakhshaei-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Bakhshaei 2018

Bakhshaei, M., Hardy, A., Francisco, A., Noakes, S., & Fusco, J. (2018). Fostering Powerful Use of Technology Through Instructional Coaching: Results from the Pilot Year of the Dynamic Learning Project. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/540c3325-fd5f-408e-a347-2409f57848dd

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post survey of participating teachers across 50 pilot schools; the article reports "significant increases in using technology for both teaching content and pedagogy" without printing a test statistic or effect size.

> "The teachers who were coached (DLP teachers) reported significant increases in using technology for both teaching content and pedagogy—in other words, teachers are using technology both to support what they are teaching and how they are teaching it"

### Bakhshaei 2018 (2)

Bakhshaei, M., Hardy, A., Francisco, A., Noakes, S., & Fusco, J. (2018). Fostering Powerful Use of Technology Through Instructional Coaching: Results from the Pilot Year of the Dynamic Learning Project. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/540c3325-fd5f-408e-a347-2409f57848dd

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

End-of-year survey comparing coached and non-coached teachers in the pilot schools found "not a significant difference" in perceived basic technology skills; equivalence was not tested, and no effect size is printed.

> "What is especially interesting is that there was not a significant difference in how DLP and non-DLP teachers perceive their basic technology skills"
