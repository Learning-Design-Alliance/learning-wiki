Evidence¶

Sibuma 2018¶

Sibuma, B., Wunnava, S., John, M.-S., Anggoro, F., & Dubosarsky, M. (2018). The Impact of an Integrated Pre-K STEM Curriculum on Teachers' Engineering Content Knowledge, Self-Efficacy, and Teaching Practices. 2018 IEEE Integrated STEM Conference (ISEC). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED585271

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pilot RCT during the 2017-2018 academic year in 5 preschools; questionnaires were administered to 42 teachers (21 intervention, 21 control) in Fall 2017 and Spring 2018. The design is described as a "randomized control trial design" with classrooms matched on baseline measures and randomly assigned.

"Using a randomized control trial design, researchers examined the impact of the curriculum in 1 7 Pre-K c l a s s r o o m s ( 8 i n t e r v e n t i o n classrooms, 9 control classrooms) in central Massachusetts."
