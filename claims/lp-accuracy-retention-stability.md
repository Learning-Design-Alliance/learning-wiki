## Evidence

### Lydon Sinéad 2019

Lydon Sinéad, Reid McDermott Bronwyn, Ryan Ethel, O’Connor Paul, Dempsey Sharon, Walsh Chloe, Byrne Dara. (2019). Can simulation-based education and precision teaching improve paediatric trainees’ behavioural fluency in performing lumbar puncture? A pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1553-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group repeated assessment of the 10 intervention participants at baseline, final training trial, stability trial (with interrupting questions as distraction) and retention trial a minimum of one month post-intervention (M = 84.2 days). Baseline median accuracy was 33.33 versus 100, 96.96 and 100 at the later timepoints; only the test statistic is printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "A Friedman’s ANOVA indicated that there was a significant difference in performance across conditions, χ2(3) = 22.66, p < .001."
