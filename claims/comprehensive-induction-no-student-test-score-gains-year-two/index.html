q3 · i? · causal · r?

Second-year findings from Mathematica's national randomized controlled evaluation of comprehensive teacher induction, comparing outcomes against business-as-usual induction programs. The report states comprehensive induction "did not lead to measurable improvements in student test scores"; no effect size is printed on this page.

"comprehensive induction did not lead to measurable improvements in student test scores, percentage of teachers remaining in their district or in the profession, or in qualifications of the teachers who were retained"
