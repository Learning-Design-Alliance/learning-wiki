Caleb D. Dodd 2013¶

Caleb D. Dodd, Scott Burris, Steve Fraze, David Doerfert, Abigail McCulloch. (2013). Evaluating the Effectiveness of Traditional Training Methods in Non-Traditional Training Programs for Adult Learners through a Pre-test/Post-test Comparison of Food Safety Knowledge. Journal of Agricultural Education. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2013.01018

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive demographic findings (Tables 3 and 4) across 300 questionnaires from 44 stores; the authors draw the reading-level recommendation directly from this distribution.

"Over half of all the employees who participated in the study reported their highest level of education to be a high school diploma or some high school. Based on this finding, food safety training should target a junior high reading level."
