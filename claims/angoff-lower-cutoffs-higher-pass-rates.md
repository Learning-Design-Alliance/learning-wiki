The review reports, from Khalid et al.'s comparison of Angoff with relative/norm-referenced, Hofstee and Cohen methods on written assessments, that "The Angoff method resulted in lower cut-off scores, leading to higher pass rates and lower failure rates". The relative method produced results similar to Angoff.
