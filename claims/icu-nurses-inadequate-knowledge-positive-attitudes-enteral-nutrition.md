## Evidence

### Song Changli 2026

Song Changli, Zhang Wei, Zhu Lijuan, Ge Teng, Cao Min, and Xu Haihong. (2026). Knowledge, Attitudes and Practices Regarding Enteral Nutrition Management Among ICU Nurses. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70260

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 420 ICU nurses in 15 Chinese hospitals using a self-developed questionnaire dichotomised at 80% of the maximum score. Knowledge out of a 0–23 range scored "17.87 ± 2.49", far below the modified Bloom's adequacy cut-off, so the article concludes knowledge was inadequate while attitudes and practices were adequate.

> "The mean knowledge score was 17.87 ± 2.49. Significant variations in knowledge scores were observed by gender (t = 10797.5, p = 0.001), hospital level (t = 11 031, p = 0.022) and participation in enteral nutrition lectures (t = 20 714, p = 0.001) (Table 1)."
