## Evidence

### Robertson Eleanor 2015

Robertson Eleanor, Morgan Lauren, New Steve, Pickering Sharon, Hadi Mohammed, Collins Gary, Rivero Arias Oliver, Griffin Damian, McCulloch Peter. (2015). Quality Improvement in Surgery Combining Lean Improvement Methods with Teamwork Training: A Controlled Before-After Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0138490

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled before-after observational study of 96 observed operations (45 active, 51 control) in a specialist orthopaedic hospital; glitches were deviations from the recognised process noted independently by two trained observers. The active group's "mean glitch rate decreased to 4.38 (sd = 2.50) glitches per hour" while the control group's rose; the article reports the between-group difference as significant (p<0.001).

> "mean glitch rate decreased to 4.38 (sd = 2.50) glitches per hour in the active group (difference = -6.10; 95% CI -9.29 to -2.92) whilst in the control group it rose to 13.20 (sd = 5.37) glitches per hour"
