## Evidence

### Rajan Kiran 2022

Rajan Kiran, Pandit Anand S. (2022). Comparing computer-assisted learning activities for learning clinical neuroscience: a randomized control trial. BMC Medical Education 22:522. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03578-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial with 16 students per group; perceptions measured on a five-point Likert survey (Cronbach's alpha 0.81) and compared with the Mann–Whitney U test. The eModule was "significantly more enjoyable (p = 0.001), engaging (p = 0.0005)" and useful than the Wiki group.

> "The eModule group found their learning activity significantly more enjoyable (p = 0.001), engaging (p = 0.0005), recommendable (p = 0.002) and useful for their studies (p = 0.01) as compared to the Wiki group."
