`q2 i3` The correlation between number of problems with diagrams drawn and final exam score is R=0.40 for the PR group and R=0.24 for the traditional group. [→ Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2016](#andrew-mason-and-chandralekha-singh-2016)

## Evidence

### Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Helping students learn effective problem solving strategies by reflecting with peers. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05628](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05628)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of final-exam scores against counted diagrams and scratchworks (Table 3) for the traditional (N=107) and PR groups; all listed correlations significant, e.g. diagram R=0.40 (p=0.000) PR vs R=0.24 (p=0.014) traditional. The printed correlation R=0.40 corresponds to a large effect.

> "For example, the correlation coeﬃcient R for the number of problems with diagrams drawn vs. the ﬁnal exam score is higher for the PR group compared to the traditional group (R=0.40 vs. R=0.24). The PR group also showed a stronger correlation than the traditional group even when the quantitative and conceptual questions were considered separately"
