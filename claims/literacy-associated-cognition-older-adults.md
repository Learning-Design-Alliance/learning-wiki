## Evidence

### Bennett JS 2012

Bennett JS, Boyle PA, James BD, Bennett DA. (2012). Correlates of health and financial literacy in older adults without dementia. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2318-12-30

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional regression analysis of global cognition (19-test composite) and five domains in 556 older adults without dementia. The 90th- vs 10th-percentile literacy contrast yielded a global cognition difference of "about 1.51 standard deviations higher", robust to income and chronic conditions adjustment.

> "a person with the 90 th percentile total literacy score had a global cognition score about 1.51 standard deviations higher than a person with the 10 th percentile total literacy score (Table 2). Results were similar in fully adjusted model."
