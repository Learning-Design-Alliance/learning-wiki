`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Correlational analysis across the 310-participant congress sample relating module completion time to self-reported experience measures. For ESD marking, the printed effect sizes include Spearman's r = -0.42 for EGDs performed and Spearman's r = -0.40 for colonoscopies, all P < 0.001; the article concludes the more experienced a participant was, the faster the completion.

> "For the "ESD marking" module, we detected slightly stronger negative correlations of completion time with years of endoscopy experience (Spearman's r -0.26, P < 0.001), with number of EGDs performed (Spearman's r -0.42, P < 0.001), and with the number of colonoscopies performed (Spearman's r −0.40, P < 0.001"
