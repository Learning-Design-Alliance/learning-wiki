## Evidence

### Mads Kock Pedersen 2016

Mads Kock Pedersen, Birk Skyum, Robert Heck, Romain Müller, Mark Bason, Andreas Lieberoth and Jacob F. Sherson. (2016). Virtual learning environment for interactive engagement with advanced quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.013102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.013102)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Cohort comparison of the optional anonymous midterm test administered in week five of both years: 35 students answered in 2013 and 17 of 47 in 2014, with "a high-medium eﬀect size (r = 0.45)".

> "A Mann-Whitney U test revealed that stu- dents in the 2014 cohort performed signiﬁcantly better in the midterm test than their peers from 2013 (z = −3.30, p< 0.001) with a high-medium eﬀect size (r = 0.45)"
