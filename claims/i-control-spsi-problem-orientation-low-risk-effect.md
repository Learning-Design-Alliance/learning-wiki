## Evidence

### Smith et al. 2017

Stephen W. Smith, Ann P. Daunic, James Algina, Donna L. Pitts, Kristen L. Merrill, Michelle M. Cumming, and Courtney Allen. (2017). Self-Regulation for Students With Emotional and Behavioral Disorders: Preliminary Effects of the I Control Curriculum. Journal of Emotional and Behavioral Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/1063426616661702

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student self-report SPSI-A Problem Orientation outcome in the Mplus pre–post pilot analysis: the Covariate × Treatment interaction was significant, favoring I Control for students with more positive pretest scores. The article attributes the pattern to differential completion of the final SPS unit.

> "The effect of I Control was larger, however, for participants with lower (more positive) covariate scores, with the simple effect test significant at the low value (z = −2.07, p = .020, ES = −.55)."
