## Evidence

### Boyd Meredith R. 2017

Boyd Meredith R., Lewis Cara C., Scott Kelli, Krendl Anne, Lyon Aaron R. (2017). The creation and validation of the Measure of Effective Attributes of Trainers (MEAT). Implementation Science. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13012-017-0603-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Known-groups validity test in a between-subject 2 × 2 factorial experiment in which undergraduates viewed counterbalanced personable and professional trainer videos; paired-samples t tests compared MEAT subscale scores by trainer type, supporting the reported discrimination.

> "As predicted, the Charisma score was higher for the personable, but the Credible score was higher for the professional trainer: t(70) = −7.95, p < .001 and t(72) = 2.52, p = .014, respectively"
