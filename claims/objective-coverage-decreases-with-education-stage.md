### Blaum Wolf E. 2012

Blaum Wolf E., Dannenberg Katja A., Friedrich Torsten, Jarczewski Anne, Reinsch Anne-Katrin, Ahlers Olaf. (2012). The practial use of the consensus statement on practical skills in medical school – a validation study. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung 29(4), Doc58. [doi:10.3205/zma000828](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000828)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Coverage analysis by stage of medical education in the same classification study. The results report 63% (70.3% core) at the first elective clinical rotation, 48.6% (53% core) by the practical year, and 39.3% (41.8% core) by the beginning of continuing education; "there is a decrease in the coverage of objectives (to the required depth) as the student level of education increases".

> "the objectives that must be covered, to the required depth, by the time of the first elective clinical rotation amounts to 63% of all learning objectives (and 70.3% of the core objectives)."
