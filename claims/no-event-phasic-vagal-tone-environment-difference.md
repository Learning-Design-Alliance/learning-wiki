## Evidence

### Mygind Lærke 2018

Mygind Lærke, Stevenson Matt P., Liebst Lasse S., Konvalinka Ivana, Bentsen Peter. (2018). Stress Response and Cognitive Performance Modulation in Classroom versus Natural Environments: A Quasi-Experimental Pilot Study with Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15061098

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same within-subjects pilot (47 children): during the d2 test the environment factor did not predict "event (β = 1.1 ± 1.07, p = 0.161)" or "phasic (β = −0.94 ± 1.07, p = 0.366)" vagal tone. No effect sizes were printed.

> "The GEE indicated that the environments predicted tonic vagal tone (β = 1.13 ± 1.06, p = 0.031), but not event (β = 1.1 ± 1.07, p = 0.161) and phasic (β = −0.94 ± 1.07, p = 0.366) vagal tone while controlling for age, gender and condition sequence"
