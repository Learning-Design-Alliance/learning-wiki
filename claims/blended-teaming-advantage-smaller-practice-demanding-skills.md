## Evidence

### He Zhicheng 2024

He Zhicheng, Li Hua, Lu Lan, Wang Qiang, Wu Qingming, Lu Lili. (2024). The application of blended teaching in medical practical course of clinical skills training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05730-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of their own randomized comparison across OSCE items: blended teaching helped more on content-rich but simple operations, where students could "repeatedly learn" online, than on practice-demanding ones.

> "However, when faced with tasks that require more offline practice, such as CPR and the four major puncture operations, the performance improvement is not as significant as observed in the two aforementioned items."
