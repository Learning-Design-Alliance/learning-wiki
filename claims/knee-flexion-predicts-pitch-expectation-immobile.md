## Evidence

### Moura 2023

Moura, N., Vidal, M., Aguilera, A. M., Vilas-Boas, J. P., Serra, S. & Leman, M. (2023). Knee flexion of saxophone players anticipates tonal context of music. npj Science of Learning 8:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00172-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Granger causality tests in the immobile condition, with outliers removed; the article reports "P4 [ p < 0.0001, F = 4.897]" and per-passage statistics for P1–P3.

> "Evidence of this causal relationships was also found in the IMO condition as involuntary flexion was observed: P1 [ p = 0.024, F = 2.025], P2 [p = 0.009, F = 2.296], P3 [p = 0.078, F = 1.663] and P4 [ p < 0.0001, F = 4.897]"
