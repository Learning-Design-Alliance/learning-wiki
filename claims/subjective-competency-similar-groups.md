`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-assessment on 7-point Likert scales at T2 in both groups (n=104). Total subjective competency did not differ (p = 0.94), though students rated "topography" higher (p = 0.01). No effect size printed.

> "Both groups estimated their competencies at similar levels after the preparation period (study 4.3 ± 0.9 vs. reference 4.3 ± 1.0; p = 0.94). The study group reported higher scores in the subcategory “topography” (p = 0.01)"
