### Jérôme Brender 2026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster analysis of session-3 prompts from 66 students identified four prompting patterns; a chi-square test comparing cluster distribution by tutor condition found no significant difference (χ² = 1.12, p = 0.77), so "the type and quality of prompting patterns enacted by students did not differ as a function of tutor condition."

> "A χ 2 test examining the distribution of students across prompting-pattern clusters by intervention condition revealed no significant differences between the SG- and PR-tutor conditions (χ 2 = 1.12,p= 0.77)."
