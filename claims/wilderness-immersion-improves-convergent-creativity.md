`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a between-subjects experiment in which hikers completed the RAT on day four of a multi-day wilderness hike against a pre-hike control, showing a "50% increase in creative performance on the RAT". No effect size is printed.

> "Atchley et al.'s (2012) study on 56 American hikers on a multi-day hike demonstrated a 50% increase in creative performance on the RAT after 4 days immersed in nature."
