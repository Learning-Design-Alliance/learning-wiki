## Evidence

### Schreuder MM 2025

Schreuder MM, Renkema A-M, Kuijpers-Jagtman AM, Padmos JAD. (2025). Dutch dentists’ involvement in orthodontic retention: monitoring, opinions, competence and communication gaps. The European Journal of Orthodontics. https://doi.org/10.1093/ejo/cjaf020

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey analysis of competence ratings (Table 2) among 210 GDPs who repaired or placed BRs. The article reports a statistically significant positive association between weekly patient-treatment hours and felt competence in repairing BRs, printing "Kendall rank correlation coefficient, τb = 0.125, P = .025"; the abstract reports 26% insufficiently competent in repairing and 33% in placing.

> "The more hours dentists were involved in patient treatment per week, the more competent they felt in repairing BRs (Kendall rank correlation coefficient, τb = 0.125, P = .025)."
