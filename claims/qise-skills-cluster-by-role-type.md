### Shams El-Adawy 2026 (2)

Shams El-Adawy, A.R. Piña, Benjamin M. Zwickl, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2026). Experimental Skills for Undergraduate Career Preparation in Quantum Information Science and Engineering. [doi:10.1103/56hq-4pns](https://doi.org/10.1103/56hq-4pns)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The results section reports the single bridging position (system operator) draws on all four skill categories, while public-facing and business roles rely most on communication and collaboration while needing some familiarity with other categories.

> "Bridging roles exhibit the broadest range of experi- mental skills across categories. The one position classi- fied as a bridging role (e.g., system operator) draws on experimental & project design, communication & collab- oration, computation & data analysis, and instrumenta- tion."
