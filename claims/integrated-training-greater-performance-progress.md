# Students in the integrated training program showed significantly greater performance progress between course assessments than students in the original program

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` The performance progress score increase was significantly greater in the integrated condition than the original condition (r = -0.35). [→ Ludo W. van Meeuwen 2018](#ludo-w-van-meeuwen-2018)

## Evidence

### Ludo W. van Meeuwen 2018

Ludo W. van Meeuwen, Saskia Brand-Gruwel, Paul A. Kirschner, Jeano J. P. R. de Bock, Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2018). Fostering self-regulation in training complex cognitive tasks. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 53–73. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9539-9

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Performance progress was the difference between two course assessments (62-item form, 0–100%) at the start and end of the 7-week ACS training. The integrated condition showed significantly greater progress, with "r =- 0.35" printed.

> "The integrated condition ( Mdn = 20, range 2–29) showed signiﬁcantly greater progress than the original condition did ( Mdn = 11, range 1–26), U = 60.00, z =- 1.863, p = 0.032, r =- 0.35."
