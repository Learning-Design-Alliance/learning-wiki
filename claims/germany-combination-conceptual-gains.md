## Subclaims
`q2 i1` German fifth and sixth graders in the ITS & ELE condition gained conceptual fractions knowledge (d = 0.59) whereas the ITS-only condition's conceptual scores decreased descriptively (d = −0.32, CI including zero). [→ Mavrikis 2022](#mavrikis-2022)

## Evidence

### Mavrikis 2022

Mavrikis, M., Rummel, N., Wiedmann, M., Loibl, K., & Holmes, W. (2022). Combining exploratory learning with structured practice educational technologies to foster both conceptual and procedural fractions knowledge. Education Tech Research Dev, 70, 691–712. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10104-0

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest non-equivalent groups study in Germany (N ITS & ELE = 100, N ITS only = 51) using an online fractions test. Table 1 reports conceptual subscale effect sizes: "0.59 [.30, .87]" for ITS & ELE versus "− 0.32" for ITS only.

> "Conceptual Germany ITS & ELE 0.79 0.74 1.21 0.69 0.59 [.30, .87] ITS only 0.73 0.63 0.53 0.64 − 0.32 [− .71, .07]"
