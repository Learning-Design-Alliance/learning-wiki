### González-Abós C 2026

González-Abós C, Reyes LG, Lorenzo C, Ausania F. (2026). MyIntuitive telemetry identifies proficiency thresholds in robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12591-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary RA-CUSUM analysis of length of stay adjusted for patient age in the same 100-case series. The curve showed a pronounced downward trend initially, then "LOS stabilised at case 28" with mild upward drift thereafter.

> "Further analysis of the learning curve showed that LOS stabilised at case 28."

### González-Abós C 2026 (2)

González-Abós C, Reyes LG, Lorenzo C, Ausania F. (2026). MyIntuitive telemetry identifies proficiency thresholds in robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12591-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary RA-CUSUM analysis of Clavien–Dindo ≥III complications adjusted for age and BMI, plus crude CUSUM of intraoperative blood loss, which "did not demonstrate a clear temporal trend" and remained stable throughout the series.

> "RA-CUSUM analysis of major complications, adjusted for age and BMI, showed an initial phase with fewer complications than expected, followed by a transition around 30 cases and late stabilisation thereafter"
