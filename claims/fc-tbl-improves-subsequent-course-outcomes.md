## Evidence

### Qu Meng 2024

Qu Meng, Hou Qinlong, Li Xushen, Yu Chunyan, Xia Jichen, Dong Zhiheng. (2024). Application of a flipped classroom incorporating modified team-based learning in molecular biology laboratory teaching: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06450-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Tracking study comparing final exam scores of the same two cohorts (LBL n = 54, FC-TBL n = 55) in four follow-up courses, expressed as median/interquartile ranges and analysed by Mann-Whitney U-test; Table 3 reports Cohen's d from 0.504 to 0.857.

> "the FC-TBL group outperformed the LBL group in Gene Diagnosis and Gene Therapy (P = 0.003), Medical Biotechnology and Applications (P = 0.037), Introduction to Medical Science Research (P = 0.041), and Hospital Infection (P = 0.049)."
