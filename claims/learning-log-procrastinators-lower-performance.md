`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes this to Goda et al. (2015), a study of learning patterns and performance in computer-assisted language learning with respect to self-regulation strategies and procrastination. The review reports that "70% of learners where classified as 'procrastinators'" and that this group had significantly lower performance; no effect size is printed.

> "An analysis of learning logs of university students revealed seven different learning patterns. 70% of learners where classified as 'procrastinators' and only about 7% belonged to the group 'learning habit', with the first group having a significantly lower learning performance than that of the learning habit group."
