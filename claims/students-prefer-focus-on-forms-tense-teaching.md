Post-test questionnaire with 215 valid responses from both experimental and control groups; experimental and control groups showed no significant differences across the nineteen questions. Respondents also agreed most that constant practice improves grammatical correctness (mean 4.0).

> "Worth noting is that 65.1 percent (140 divided by 215) of the subjects preferred the “focus on formS” approach (the analyzing, synthesizing, and comparing method) (Question 18) and 64.7 percent (139 divided by 215) of them deemed it the most effective teaching method (Question 19)."
