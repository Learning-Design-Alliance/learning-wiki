`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theoretical-sampling email sent to all eight interviewed participants; these three replied that class size and budget did not correlate with their pedagogy choice. Sam reiterated he lectures regardless of class size; Kevin cited content prerequisites as the largest factor.

> "Kevin, Lonnie, and Sam all replied that their classes are generally small, and they have not experienced any correlation between class size (or budget) and pedagogy choice."
