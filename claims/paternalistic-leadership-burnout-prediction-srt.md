`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling of 403 valid questionnaires from Chinese medical students on the SRT, analyzed in AMOS 28.0. The SEM path analysis found that "authoritarian leadership exhibits a significant positive effect on learning burnout (β = 0.289)" while benevolent and moral leadership showed significant negative effects.

> "Benevolent leadership has a significant negative effect on learning burnout (β = − 0.227, S.E. = 0.041, C.R. = − 4.135, p < 0.001), and moral leadership also shows a significant negative effect (β = − 0.198, S.E. = 0.043, C.R. = − 3.438, p < 0.001). In contrast, authoritarian leadership exhibits a significant positive effect on learning burnout (β = 0.289, S.E. = 0.041, C.R. = 5.034, p < 0.001)."
