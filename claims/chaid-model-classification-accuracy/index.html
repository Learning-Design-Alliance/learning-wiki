Evidence¶

Muñoz-Rodríguez 2023¶

Muñoz-Rodríguez, J. M., Patino Alonso, C., Pessoa, T., & Martín-Lucas, J. (2023). Identity profile of young people experiencing a sense of risk on the internet: A data mining application of decision tree with CHAID algorithm. Computers & Education, 197, 104743. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104743

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Performance evaluation of the CHAID classification tree on the survey data, with data split into training (75%) and test (25%) sets and tenfold cross-validation; the printed accuracy and misclassification figures are reported verbatim, with no standardised effect size.

"The average percentage of overall correct classification and the risk value of misclassification in the 10-fold cross validation was 35.4% (S.D. = 0.54) and values for cross validation were 38.6% (S.D. = 0.98). The training sample correctly classified 64.6% of young people, and the test-sample revealed that it correctly classified 61.4% of young people"
