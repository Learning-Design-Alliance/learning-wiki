Dual mediation analysis with pre-test score as covariate, using nonparametric bootstrap with 1,000 iterations to estimate ACME, ADE, and total effect with percentile 95% CIs (Fig. 4). Q6 and Q7 were selected as mediators based on SHAP feature importance.

> "The indirect effect via facilitation of knowledge mastery (Q6) was estimated at 6.48 (95% CI: 5.18–7.94, P < 0.001), while the effect via stimulation of learning interest (Q7) was 3.16 (95% CI: 2.01–4.28, P < 0.001)."
