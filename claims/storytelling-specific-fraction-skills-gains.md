### Charalambos Lemonidis & Ioanna Kaiafa 2019

Charalambos Lemonidis & Ioanna Kaiafa. (2019). The Effect of Using Storytelling Strategy on Students’ Performance in Fractions. Journal of Education and Learning, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v8n2p165

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann Whitney comparisons of post-test skill categories (Table 4): comparing fractions U=465.00, p=0.007; equivalent fractions U=551.00, p=0.029; creating and manipulating representations U=528.50, p=0.040; problem-solving U=515.50, p=0.025.

> "the use of storytelling had a positive effect on specific mathematical skills, such as comparing fractions, finding equivalent fractions, creating and manipulating representations and problem solving."

### Charalambos Lemonidis & Ioanna Kaiafa 2019 (2)

Charalambos Lemonidis & Ioanna Kaiafa. (2019). The Effect of Using Storytelling Strategy on Students’ Performance in Fractions. Journal of Education and Learning, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v8n2p165

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Mann Whitney comparisons (Table 4) showed part-whole interpretation U=683.50, p=0.574 and use of number line U=676.50, p=0.607; no significant group difference on either skill category.

> "There was no statistically significant difference between the two groups in activities that are related to the part-whole interpretation of fraction and to place fractions on the number line."
