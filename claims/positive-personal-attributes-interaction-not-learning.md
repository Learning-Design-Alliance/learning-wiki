## Evidence

### Iqbal M 2016

Iqbal M, Velan GM, O’Sullivan AJ, Balasooriya C. (2016). Differential impact of student behaviours on group interaction and collaborative learning: medical students’ and tutors’ perspectives. BMC Medical Education 16(1):217. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0730-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme from focus groups and interviews on positive personal attributes (organised, caring, pleasant, helpful, accommodating, polite, appreciative); students and tutors agreed these "did not always impact on learning". Qualitative perceptions; no effect sizes.

> "The behaviours within this theme had a positive impact on group interaction, but both students and tutors agreed that they did not always impact on learning."
