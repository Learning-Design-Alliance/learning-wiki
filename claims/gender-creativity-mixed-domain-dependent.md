van der Zanden, P. J. A. C.; Meijer, P. C.; Beghetto, R. A. (2020). A review study about creativity in adolescence: Where is the social context? Thinking Skills and Creativity, 38, article 100702. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2020.100702

`q2 · i?`

Narrative synthesis of demographic findings across the sample. The review reports "mixed findings regarding the relationship between gender and creativity": girls scored higher on domain-specific fluency, flexibility, and elaboration; boys higher on biology scientific creativity; girls higher on social creativity; several studies found no significant differences.

> "With respect to gender, the studies in our sample reported mixed findings regarding the relationship between gender and creativity"
