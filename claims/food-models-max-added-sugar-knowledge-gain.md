## Evidence

### Santaló María Isabel 2019

Santaló María Isabel, Gibbons Sandra, Naylor Patti-Jean. (2019). Using Food Models to Enhance Sugar Literacy among Older Adolescents: Evaluation of a Brief Experiential Nutrition Education Intervention. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11081763

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA in the cluster-randomized trial (Table 2) for this knowledge item: F(1, 201) = 138.14, p = 0.00, printed effect size η2 = 0.41, rising from 2% to 75% correct in the intervention group versus 11% to 14% in controls.

> "Eighty percent of the intervention group correctly answered the maximum recommended amount of added sugar that can be present in a healthy diet compared to 10% of the control group."
