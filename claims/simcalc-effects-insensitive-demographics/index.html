Roschelle 2018¶

Menlo Park, CA: SRI International. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7a123d3c-22ec-46db-b7cf-709c3aa65583

In the Texas 7th-grade experiment data, the article reports that "91% of 7th grade classrooms in the treatment group" exceeded the control mean gain, supporting broad benefit across varied classrooms.

"Indeed, 91% of 7th grade classrooms in the treatment group had mean classroom gains exceeding the mean gain in the control group, suggesting that most classrooms in T exas could see a benefit"

  • SimCalc replacement units produced statistically significant pretest-to-posttest gains in conceptual understanding with student-level effect sizes of .63, .50, and .56 in three Texas experiments — related
