Zhang Song 2018¶

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup analysis of the knowledge-score meta-analysis dividing students into probationer and intern subgroups; both showed statistically significant differences favoring PBL, with the printed SMDs and confidence intervals as quoted.

"The subgroup analysis also showed that a statistically significant difference in the probationer subgroup (SMD = 1.32, 95% CI [0.67, 1.97]) and the intern subgroup (SMD = 0.82, 95% CI [0.28, 1.36])."

Zhang Song 2018 (2)¶

Zhang Song, Xu Jiancheng, Wang Hongwei, Zhang Dong, Zhang Qichuan, Zou Liguang. (2018). Effects of problem-based learning in Chinese radiology education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000010069

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup analysis of the skill-score meta-analysis by participant distribution; both probationer and intern subgroups showed statistically significant differences favoring PBL, with larger pooled effects among probationers.

"The results also showed a statistically significant difference in the probationer subgroup (SMD = 2.45, 95% CI [1.36, 3.54])[9,10,14,15,17,19–21] and intern subgroup (SMD = 1.56, 95% CI [0.86, 2.27])."
