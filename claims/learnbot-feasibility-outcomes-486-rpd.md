`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective collection in the E-MIPS registry of all consecutive RPDs performed after training at 20 high-volume centers in 12 countries. The article reports a Clavien-Dindo grade ≥III rate of 31.4% (n=153), POPF 21.2% (n=103), and in-hospital/30-day mortality of 2.7% (n=13).

> "Overall, 486 RPD procedures were performed. Median intraoperative blood loss was 200 mL (IQR: 100–400), operative time was 475 minutes (IQR: 414–553.3), and the conversion rate was 18.3% (n=89)."
