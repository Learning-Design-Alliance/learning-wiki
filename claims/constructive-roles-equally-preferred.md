### Wangda Zhu 2025

Wangda Zhu, Guang Chen, Yumeng Zhu, Lei Cai, and Xiangen Hu. (2025). Investigating Students' Preferences for AI Roles in Mathematical Modelling: Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.06617)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-hoc Wilcoxon comparisons among Tutor, TA, Peer, and Excellent Student rankings in the within-subjects experiment; all pairwise tests had p > .14. Equivalence was not tested beyond non-significance, so no effect size is reported.

> "Importantly, no significant differences were found among the four constructive AI roles (Tutor, TA, Peer, and Excellent Student; allps > .14), indicating that students valued competent and supportive AI personas similarly, regardless of the specific pedagogical approach embodied."
