## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Spearman correlations with passing the one-year hands-on test were r = 0.85 (p < 0.001) for clip uploads, r = 0.7 (p = 0.001) for hands-on teaching session attendance, and r = 0.5 (p = 0.03) for monthly conference attendance. [→ Janjigian Michael 2021](#janjigian-michael-2021)

## Evidence

### Janjigian Michael 2021

Janjigian Michael, Dembitzer Anne, Srisarajivakul-Klein Caroline, Hardower Khemraj, Cooke Deborah, Zabar Sondra, Sauthoff Harald. (2021). Design and evaluation of the I-SCAN faculty POCUS program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02453-2

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis in an observational cohort of 19 hospitalists completing one-year assessments, relating program participation metrics to the final hands-on test. The article reports "r = 0.85, p < 0.001" for clip uploads and the other correlations as printed.

> "There was a correlation between passing the one-year hands-on test with the number of clip uploads (r = 0.85, p < 0.001), attending hands-on teaching sessions (r = 0.7, p = 0.001), and with attendance at monthly conferences (r = 0.5, p = 0.03)."
