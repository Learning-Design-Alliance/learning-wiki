## Evidence

### Malone et al. 2019

Malone, A. S., Fuchs, L. S., Sterba, S. K., Fuchs, D., & Foreman-Murray, L. (2019). Does an integrated focus on fractions and decimals improve at-risk students' rational number magnitude performance? *Contemporary Educational Psychology, 59*, 101782. [doi:10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.101782](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.101782)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cross-classified partially nested multilevel model analysis of the FM+DM condition versus control. The article reports "significantly stronger performance compared to control on all four fraction outcomes" with a mean ES of 1.23 across the four fraction outcomes, and performance comparable to FM+FAPP.

> "Even so, the integrated fraction-decimal intervention also produced significantly stronger performance compared to control on all four fraction outcomes. Moreover, this condition's performance was comparable to that of students in the full fraction intervention on all four fraction outcomes."
