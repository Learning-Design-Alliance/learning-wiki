## Evidence

### Jaeseo Lim 2020

Jaeseo Lim, Hwiyeol Jo, Byoung-Tak Zhang, Jooyong Park. (2020). Human-Like Active Learning: Machines Simulating the Human Learning Process. 34th Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2020), Vancouver, Canada. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2011.03733](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2011.03733)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Same randomized human experiment (54 undergraduates, ANCOVA on three question types). The article reports "F (1, 52) = 42.62,p<. 001,η2 =.46" for transfer items, the largest conceptual measure of application to new cases.

> "For the transfer type items, the D group (M = 12.21,SD = 2.76) scored higher than the L group (M = 7.16,SD = 2.73),F (1, 52) = 42.62,p<. 001,η2 =.46"
