## Evidence

### Andreae Christina 2023

Andreae Christina, Tingström Pia, Nilsson Staffan, Jaarsma Tiny, Karlsson Nadine, Kärner Köhler Anita. (2023). Does problem-based learning improve patient empowerment and cardiac risk factors in patients with coronary heart disease in a Swedish primary care setting? A long-term prospective, randomised, parallel single randomised trial (COR-PRIM). BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2022-065230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stratified mixed-model analysis of blood-sample HDL-C in the COR-PRIM randomised trial, adjusted for age, sex, education and marital status. The control group showed "no significant change" at time 1, 3 or 5 compared with baseline.

> "HDL-C increased significantly at time 1 (M=1.37 mmol/L±0.43, p=0.003), time 3 (M=1.42 mmol/L±0.55, p<0.001) and time 5 (M=1.39 mmol/L±0.41, p<0.001) in the PBL group compared with baseline"

### Andreae Christina 2023 (2)

Andreae Christina, Tingström Pia, Nilsson Staffan, Jaarsma Tiny, Karlsson Nadine, Kärner Köhler Anita. (2023). Does problem-based learning improve patient empowerment and cardiac risk factors in patients with coronary heart disease in a Swedish primary care setting? A long-term prospective, randomised, parallel single randomised trial (COR-PRIM). BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2022-065230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus time × group interaction test for HDL-C in the trial's mixed-model analysis; its significance at the 5% level triggered the stratified by-group analyses reported for this outcome.

> "The interaction between time and group was significant at alpha level 5% (p=0.016). Therefore, the analysis was stratified by group."
