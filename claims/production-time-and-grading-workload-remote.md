## Evidence

### Schwarz G 2022

Schwarz G, Bleiner D, Günther D. (2022). On video lectures during remote teaching and beyond. Analytical and Bioanalytical Chemistry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00216-022-03983-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' own time accounting for pre-recorded spring 2020 lectures in the Element Analysis course; a first-person experiential estimate, not a measured comparison. The assistant reviewed recordings and served as a visual anchor next to the camera.

> "After the initial setup and testing of the technical equipment, the additional time spent pre-recording lectures was less than 15 min per lecture, including active work time for video editing."

### Schwarz G 2022 (2)

Schwarz G, Bleiner D, Günther D. (2022). On video lectures during remote teaching and beyond. Analytical and Bioanalytical Chemistry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00216-022-03983-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of where the increased preparation time arose after the shift to remote teaching: transferring weekly problem sets into the online form sheet and hand-grading many items. No timing data are printed.

> "Although some of the questions were automatically graded, many needed to be graded by hand, which took longer than on paper."
