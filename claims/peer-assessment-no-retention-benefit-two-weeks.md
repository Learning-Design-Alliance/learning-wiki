### Krogh Charlotte Loumann 2014

Krogh Charlotte Loumann, Ringsted Charlotte, Kromann Charles B., Rasmussen Maria Birkvad, Todsen Tobias, Jørgensen Rasmus Lundhus, Jacobsen Rikke Borre, Dahl Jørgen B., Konge Lars. (2014). Effect of Engaging Trainees by Assessing Peer Performance: A Randomised Controlled Trial Using Simulated Patient Scenarios. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/610591

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Retention test two weeks after the four-hour simulation course, with trainees acting individually as team leader and performances scored by two blinded raters. "no significant difference between the two groups" on either measure; equivalence was not tested.

> "The learning outcome, assessed two weeks after the course, showed no significant difference between the two groups, regarding either checklist scores (P = 0.923) or global ratings (P = 0.322) (Table 1)."
