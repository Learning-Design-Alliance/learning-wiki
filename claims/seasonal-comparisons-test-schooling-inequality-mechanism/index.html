Evidence¶

Dennis Condron 2021¶

Dennis Condron, Douglas Downery, Megan Kuhfeld. (2021). Schools as refractors: Change in variance in children's cognitive skills change while in school versus out. Sociology of Education, 94(4), 316-340. https://journals.sagepub.com/home/soe doi:10.1177/00380407211041542

q1 · i? · causal · r?

The abstract describes the study's design: seasonal comparisons on "recent national data" from MAP Growth spanning numerous grade levels and four subjects. This is a methodological statement rather than a measured effect.

"we use seasonal comparisons to examine the possibilities that schooling exacerbates, reduces, or reproduces overall skill inequality in math, reading, language use, and science with recent national data"
