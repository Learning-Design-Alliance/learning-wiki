## Evidence

### van der Velde 2025

van der Velde, M., Harmsen, W., Veldkamp, B. P., Feskens, R., Keuning, J., & Swart, N. (2025). Speech Enabled Reading Fluency Assessment: a Validation Study. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00480-y

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational validation of passage reading item-level speed scores against orthographic transcriptions by two Linguistics graduates using PRAAT; item-level speed was defined as the duration of the final reading attempt for a prompt. Correlations of r = 0.61, r = 0.57 and r = 0.59 all exceed the r = 0.37 threshold for a large correlation.

> "We found moderate to strong correlations between the ASR and transcriber 1 (r = 0.61), transcriber 2 (r = 0.57) and both transcribers (r = 0.59)."
