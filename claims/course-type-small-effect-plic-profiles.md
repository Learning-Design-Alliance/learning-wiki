## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Chi-square tests show significant but small association between course type and profile composition at pre- and post-test. [→ Marcus Kubsch 2026](#marcus-kubsch-2026)

## Evidence

### Marcus Kubsch 2026

Marcus Kubsch, Natasha G. Holmes, Antti Lehtinen. (2026). Multidimensional Profiles of Critical Thinking in Physics Labs: Latent Structure, Instructional Change, and Connections to Physics Identity. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.11598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.11598)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests of association across the three largest course types (Introductory Calculus, Introductory Algebra, Sophomore-level; N=5,457 with complete profile assignments). The article reports effect sizes of "Cramér'sV=.108 at pre-test" and .100 at post-test, both small.

> "Course type was significantly associated with profile composition at both timepoints (pre:χ 2(2) =63.40, p< .001; post:χ 2(2) =55.10,p< .001). Effect sizes, however, were small (Cramér'sV=.108 at pre-test; .100 at post-test)"
