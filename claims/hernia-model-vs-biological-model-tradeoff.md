`q1 i?` The authors state their model showed less sensitivity to dissection than a biological tissue model while maintaining higher anatomical representation and allowing repeated simulations without per-model preparation. [→ de Barros PHF 2024](#de-barros-phf-2024)

## Evidence

### de Barros PHF 2024

de Barros PHF, Borges CS, Ferreira CPdC, Hernani BL, Abreu IPN, Tastaldi L, Cavazzola LT. (2024). Hernia 3D training model: a new inguinal hernia 3D-printed simulator. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2024AO0620

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' discussion-section comparison of their 3D-printed model with the biological tissue model reported by Ivakhov et al.; no head-to-head evaluation was performed. The article notes the biological model "requires the preparation of pig stomachs for each simulation".

> "In contrast to biological tissue models, as presented by Ivakhov et al.,(7) our model showed less sensitivity to dissection while maintaining a higher level of anatomical representation. Furthermore, our model is easier to manipulate, as performing a greater number of simulations using the same model is possible."
