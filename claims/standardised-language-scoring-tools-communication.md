### Alfstad E 2025

Alfstad E, Lockertsen V, Johannessen A-K, Werner A. (2025). The applicability of a structured learning programme focusing on improving observational competencies to strengthen patient safety: a qualitative study with professionals in primary healthcare services. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12692-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding from the qualitative cross-case analysis; an RN describes how shared knowledge of "NEWS and ABCDE" shifted interactions from one-way instructions to collaboration grounded in shared reasoning.

> "It's more like we're speaking the same language. Before, when I knew NEWS and ABCDE, but the team didn't, it was more like giving instructions: 'Do this and that!' Now, it's more collaborative, instead of me just telling them what to do without them understanding the reasoning behind it."
