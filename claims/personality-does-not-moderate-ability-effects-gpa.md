`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Series of two-way interaction regression models in the 227-student sample testing C, O, and A as moderators of VI and strategic EI effects on GPA; all interaction terms were nonsignificant, so branch-level versions were not tested.

> "Regardless of that, moderating effects were nonsignificant for all three inspected personality traits, which is why the moderation models were not further tested with EU and EM instead of overall strategic EI."
