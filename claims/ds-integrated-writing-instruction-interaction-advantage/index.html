Evidence¶

Balaman 2018¶

Balaman, S. (2018). Digital storytelling: A multimodal narrative writing genre. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(3), 202-212. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1193109

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

A quasi-experimental 2×2 mixed ANOVA on pre- and post-test writing scores of 43 EFL English-major students in two intact classes (one experimental, one control) over a 14-week treatment. The article reports "a significant interaction between the time points and the group variables" with partial η2 = .366.

"a 2x2 mixed ANOVA was calculated and indicated that there was a significant interaction between the time points and the group variables, F (1, 41) = 23.673, p < .001, partial η 2 = .366"
