## Evidence

### Dolan 2026

Dolan, S., L. Nowell, N. Moules, A. Kaba, and M. Brockway. 2026. “Investigating Healthcare Educators' Interprofessional Socialisation Following an Interprofessional Simulation Facilitator Training Programme: A Mixed Methods Study.” Journal of Advanced Nursing 82, no. 6: 6726–6749. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70267

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interpretive description analysis of semi-structured interviews with 17 SimSkills participants recruited from PIAT day attendees, with constant comparative analysis and member checking. Educators conveyed how socialising with other professions made scope of practice more evident and broadened their perspective of the healthcare team.

> "When educators socialised, they were able to better understand other educators teaching strategies and common challenges that exist within a complex healthcare system."
