## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Maik Beege 2020

Maik Beege, Manuel Ninaus, Sascha Schneider, Steve Nebel, Julia Schlemmel, Jasmin Weidenmüller, Korbinian Moeller, Günter Daniel Rey. (2020). Investigating the effects of beat gestures and deictic gestures of a lecturer in educational videos. Psychology of learning with digital media, Chemnitz University of Technology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103955

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 results with 108 university students in a three-condition between-participant design. The results section states "beat gestures did not significantly foster learning outcomes" relative to the control condition; equivalence was not tested.

> "In contrast, beat gestures did not significantly foster learning outcomes. Thus, H1 was not supported. Regarding the research question, the current results showed that the deictic condition outperformed the beat condition in terms of retention."
