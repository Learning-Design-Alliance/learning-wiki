The commentary reports on de Mooij et al.'s (2025) systematic review mapping SRL studies onto the SMA grid, examining which combinations of data streams and analytical approaches (unimodal, horizontal, vertical, integrated) were employed. The review finds "motivational and affective processes remain underrepresented".

> "The mapping on the SMA grid shows more integrated use of multimodal data and the dynamics between the CAMM processes, although motivational and affective processes remain underrepresented."
