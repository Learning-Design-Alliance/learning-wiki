## Evidence

### Xusheng Dai 2025

Xusheng Dai, Zhaochun Wen, Jianxiao Jiang, Huiqin Liu, Yu Zhang. (2025). How Students Use AI Feedback Matters: Experimental Evidence on Physics Achievement and Autonomy. Preprint, under review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.08672](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.08672)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup result from Experiment 2, a five-week randomized trial (266 valid participants) comparing full student control (Group D), shared student-system control (Group E), and control. The article reports the gain "for high-achieving students (d = 0.378, p < 0.05)" in the full-control condition.

> "fully learner-controlled AI feedback significantly enhanced academic performance for high-achieving students (d = 0.378, p < 0.05) without negatively impacting their autonomy"
