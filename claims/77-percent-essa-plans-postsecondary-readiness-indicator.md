## Evidence

### Mitchell P. Herz 2023

Mitchell P. Herz, Lamar L. Johnson, and Eric W. Crane. (2023). Accountability for Postsecondary Readiness: A 50-State Analysis. Region 15 Comprehensive Center at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED633301

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

A 50-state document analysis of all ESSA plans on the U.S. Department of Education website as of May 1, 2023, with two raters coding measures and state agency staff verifying the data. The review found "41 out of 53 ESSA plans (77%) included a postsecondary readiness indicator."

> "In total, 41 out of 53 ESSA plans (77%) included a postsecondary readiness indicator as a scoring element that contributed to identifying schools for additional support."
