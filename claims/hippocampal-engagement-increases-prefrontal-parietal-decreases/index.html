Evidence¶

Qin Shaozheng 2014¶

Qin Shaozheng, Cho Soohyun, Chen Tianwen, Rosenberg-Lee Miriam, Geary David C., Menon Vinod. (2014). Hippocampal-neocortical functional reorganization underlies children's cognitive development. Nature Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3788

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal block-design fMRI in 28 children; ROI analysis of anatomically defined hippocampus showed significant longitudinal increase in engagement, and 23 of 28 children showed increased hippocampal activation. No effect size is printed.

"Analysis of a priori anatomically-defined ROIs (Fig. 2c) spanning the entire long axis of the hippocampus also revealed a significant increase in hippocampal engagement (left: t(27)=2.39, P=0.02; right: (t(27)=3.26, P<0.01)"
