Evidence¶

Shigen Shimojo 2025¶

Shigen Shimojo, Yugo Hayashi. (2025). Age-related differences in explanatory activities during collaborative learning with concept maps: experimental investigation using epistemic network analysis. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09456-5

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Experiment with 26 younger and 26 older adults learning attribution theory via a concept-map CSCL task. Mann–Whitney U test on posttest minus pretest scores: "young adults scored higher than older adults" (U = 188.50, p =.004), a printed result with no standardized effect size beyond the reported r statistic.

"A Mann–Whitney U test revealed a significant difference between young adults (median = 1.00) and older adults (median = 0.00) (U = 188.50, p =.004, r = 2.04). In other words, it was found that young adults scored higher than older adults."

Shigen Shimojo 2025 (2)¶

Shigen Shimojo, Yugo Hayashi. (2025). Age-related differences in explanatory activities during collaborative learning with concept maps: experimental investigation using epistemic network analysis. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09456-5

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Within-group analysis in the Findings section: younger adults' medians rose from 3 (pretest) to 4 (posttest), U = 140.00, p =.002, r = 0.61, showing "an improvement from the pretest to the posttest." Older adults were split at median 0, described as no elaboration.

"In addition, the median score for younger adults was 3 for the pretest and 4 for the posttest, indicating an improvement from the pretest to the posttest (U = 140.00, p =.002, r = 0.61)."
