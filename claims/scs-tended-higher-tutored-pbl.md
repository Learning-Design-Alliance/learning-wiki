## Evidence

### Hayashi Shogo 2013

Hayashi Shogo, Tsunekawa Koji, Inoue Chikako, Fukuzawa Yoshitaka. (2013). Comparison of tutored group with tutorless group in problem-based mixed learning sessions: a randomized cross-matched study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-158

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-tier self-evaluation questionnaire administered with the written examination in the cross-matched PBL1 study. SCS averages were 4.08 vs 3.88 (2007) and 4.37 vs 4.29 (2008); variance differences were "inconsistent" and non-significant.

> "In both years, the average score tended to be lower in the tutorless group but this was not significant (2007, p = 0.092; 2008, p = 0.42)."
