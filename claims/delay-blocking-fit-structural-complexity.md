### Gregory L. Heileman 2018 (2)

Gregory L. Heileman, Chaouki T. Abdallah, Ahmad Slim, Michael Hickman. (2018). Curricular Analytics: A Framework for Quantifying the Impact of Curricular Reforms and Pedagogical Innovations. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1811.09676](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1811.09676)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Analytical observation from the simulation study of balanced curricula: the article reports that reachability, degrees-of-freedom and centrality are linearly dependent on delay and blocking factors, though it believes they remain helpful for providing insights about curricula.

> "The reason for this is that the three other structural factors we defined—reachability, degrees-of-freedom and centrality—are all linearly dependent on the delay and blocking factors, and therefore with regards to computing structural complexity, they provide redundant information."
