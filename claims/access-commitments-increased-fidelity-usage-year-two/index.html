Evidence¶

Brasiel 2016¶

Brasiel, S., Jeong, S., Ames, C., Lawanto, K., Yuan, M., & Martin, T. (2016). Effects of educational technology on mathematics achievement for K-12 students in Utah. Journal of Online Learning Research, 2(3), 205-226. https://www.learntechlib.org/j/JOLR/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The authors' report of implementation changes and mid-year usage monitoring in the second year of the Utah STEM Action Center grant program, based on what was learned from the first year's low fidelity rates. This is an uncontrolled before-after observation, not a tested comparison.

"This second year (2015-16) grants were limited to schools with usage during year 1, and the grant application required a letter of commitment from the school principal for access to technology for a minimum of 45 minutes per week for students using the educational technology. Mid-year the second year, close to 50% of students were meeting the recommended level of usage."
