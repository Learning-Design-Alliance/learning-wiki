### Jerrim 2017

Jerrim, J., Macmillan, L., Micklewright, J., Sawtell, M., and Wiggins, M. (2017). Does teaching children how to play cognitively demanding games improve their educational attainment? Evidence from a Randomised Controlled Trial of chess instruction in England. UCL Institute of Education. https://controlled-trials.com/ISRCTN33648117

`q1 · i1` · `causal · r3`

The article reports, citing the Sala and Gobet (2016) meta-analysis of 24 studies, that "the final results reporting an average effect size of +0.34 standard deviations". This is second-hand; the article stresses the median sample size of these studies was just 54 children and that designs were weak.

> "The effect sizes reported are mostly positive, with the final results reporting an average effect size of +0.34 standard deviations."
