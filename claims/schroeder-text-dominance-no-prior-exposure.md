## Evidence

### Katherine D. Rainey and Bethany R. Wilcox 2020

Katherine D. Rainey and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2020). Faculty survey on upper-division thermal physics content coverage. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.rainey](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.rainey)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on textbook use among 59 respondents. Schroeder's text was reported by "(72%, N=44)" of respondents, with Kittel and Kroemer second at 16% and all other texts at 7% or below.

> "The majority of respondents (72%, N=44) reported using An Introduction to Thermal Physics by Daniel V . Schroeder6. Thermal Physics by Charles Kittel and Herbert Kroemer4 was the second most frequently cited text (16%, N=10)."

### Katherine D. Rainey and Bethany R. Wilcox 2020 (2)

Katherine D. Rainey and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2020). Faculty survey on upper-division thermal physics content coverage. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.rainey](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.rainey)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on assumed prior exposure among 59 respondents; "(74%, N=45)" assumed little to no prior exposure. The authors infer that pretest administration of an upper-division assessment may not produce meaningful measurements before the course.

> "Most of the instructors (74%, N=45) teach with the assumption that their students have little to no prior exposure to thermal physics content."
