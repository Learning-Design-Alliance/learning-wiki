# Some upper-division students struggle to apply the state function property of entropy to cyclic processes, requiring instructor intervention to conclude that entropy change over a cycle is zero

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Two students working through the Heat Engines tutorial could not readily apply the state function property of entropy to a complete cycle, reaching the correct conclusion only after direct instructor intervention. [→ Trevor I. Smith 2015](#trevor-i-smith-2015)

## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2015

Trevor I. Smith, Warren M. Christensen, Donald B. Mountcastle, and John R. Thompson. (2015). Identifying Student Diﬃculties with Entropy, Heat Engines, and the Carnot Cycle. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020116](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020116)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Videotaped tutorial episode at School 1 in which a student pair first proposed ΔSws = (QH − QL)/T without knowing which temperature T represented; only after instructor intervention did they conclude the cycle's entropy change was zero. The article reports this is consistent with Bucy et al.'s findings. No effect size is printed.

> "Bonnie and Claude's video data and generally poor performance on parts (b) and (d) of the EEQ pretest (which ask about the change in entropy of the working substance for each engine) provide evidence that students struggle with the state function property of entropy and how it relates to cyclic processes."
