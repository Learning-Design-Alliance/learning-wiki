Evidence¶

CREDO 2020¶

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2020). Charter School Performance in the State of Washington. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-the-state-of-washington-2/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subset analysis limited to charter students who first enrolled between 2015-16 and 2017-18 and their VCRs, to measure the effect of continued enrollment; the report cautions these results should not be directly compared with other findings in the report.

"Figure 4 shows that Washington State charter school students experience learning growth in the first, second, and third year of charter attendance that is not statistically different from that of students (VCR) enrolled in traditional public school settings."
