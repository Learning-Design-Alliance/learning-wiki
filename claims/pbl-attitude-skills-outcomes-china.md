## Evidence

### Ding Xiaojie 2014

Ding Xiaojie, Zhao Liping, Chu Haiyan, Tong Na, Ni Chunhui, Hu Zhibin, Zhang Zhengdong, Wang Meilin. (2014). Assessing the Effectiveness of Problem-Based Learning of Preventive Medicine Education in China. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep05126

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of questionnaire-survey outcomes from 3–6 studies per outcome (426–851 subjects). All four pooled odds ratios favoured PBL; the collaborative-skill interval (0.96–18.45) crosses 1 and heterogeneity was high for problem solved (I² = 82.6%) and collaborative skill (I² = 87.5%).

> "the pooled PBL effects were also significant (OR = 3.62, 95% CI = 2.40–5.16, Pheterogeneity = 0.444 for learning attitude; OR = 4.80, 95% CI = 2.01–11.46, Pheterogeneity < 0.001 for problem solved skill; OR = 5.81, 95% CI = 3.11–10.85, Pheterogeneity = 0.048 for self-directed learning skill; and OR = 4.21, 95% CI = 0.96–18.45, Pheterogeneity < 0.001 for collaborative skill)"
