## Evidence

### Alba López-Moraga 2024

Alba López-Moraga, Laura Luyten & Tom Beckers. (2024). A history of avoidance does not impact extinction learning in male rats. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00223-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered between-subjects rodent experiments comparing Avoiders (platform available during acquisition) with Yoked contingency-matched controls. Freezing dropped over extinction sessions, but the article reports "no signiﬁcant differences between groups were found"; no group effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Freezing was signiﬁcantly reduced after one extinction session in Experiment 1 ( Q(3, 11.33) = 87.44, p < 0.001) and Experiment 2 ( Q(3, 9.6) = 30.56, p < 0.001); no signiﬁcant differences between groups were found."
