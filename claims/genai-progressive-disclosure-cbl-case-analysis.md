### Waseem J 2026

Waseem J, Ritter Nicola L, McMordie J, Su P, Hu M, Chen C, Xu S. (2026). Generative AI–Assisted Progressive-Disclosure Case-Based Learning for Clinical Reasoning in Occupational Medicine: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/103584

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Nonrandomized posttest quasi-experimental study of 224 undergraduates assigned by administrative class. The intervention group scored higher on the standardized case-analysis assignment, with a large effect size (Cohen d=1.51).

> "the intervention group achieved significantly higher scores than the control group (mean 88.41, SD 3.85 vs mean 82.51, SD 3.94; P<.001), with a large effect size (Cohen d=1.51, 95% CI 1.23-1.83)"
