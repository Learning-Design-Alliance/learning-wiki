The review reports, citing the 1998 AAUW Gender Gaps report, that gaps still exist "especially in the area of technology and computing". In 1993 women received 35% of physical science and about 16% of engineering bachelor's degrees.

> "As a result of these efforts to level the playing field, the gender gap in the natural sciences in college has narrowed since the AAUW Report in 1992. (17)"
