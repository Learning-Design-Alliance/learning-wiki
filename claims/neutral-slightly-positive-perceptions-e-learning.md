## Evidence

### Kim Kyong-Jee 2021

Kim Kyong-Jee, Lee Yeon Ji, Lee Mi Jin, Kim Young Hyo. (2021). e-Learning for enhancement of medical student performance at the Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253860

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey of 35 respondents (81.4% response rate) using a 36-item questionnaire with Cronbach's alpha 0.90 for perception items. Descriptive means show agreement that videos "aided learning clinical performance" (M = 3.66); gender and age comparisons were non-significant (p = .73, p = .61).

> "Students tended to agree with the statement that “the clinical videos aided learning clinical performance” (M = 3.66, SD = 0.76), and with the statement that “the clinical videos helped me prepare for the OSCE” (M = 3.54, SD = 0.92)."
