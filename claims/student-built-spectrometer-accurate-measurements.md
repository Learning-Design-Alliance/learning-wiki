## Evidence

### Robert Fischer 2012

Robert Fischer. (2012). The true colours of white light: hands-on optical spectrometry. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1204.6072](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1204.6072)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Informal competition with 16 to 18 year old students who built the spectrometer and measured 4 spectral bands of a compact fluorescent light bulb in 15 minutes; the article reports the winning group's "root-mean square error" was "less than 13 nm" against a CCD spectrometer. No effect size is printed.

> "the majority of groups managed to get good measurement results with at times astonishing accuracy: The root-mean square error over the 4 spectral bands of the winning group was less than 13 nm compared to the values measured by a CCD based spectrometer commonly used in industry."
