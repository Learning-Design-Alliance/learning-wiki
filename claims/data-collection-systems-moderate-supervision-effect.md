# Supervision alone is positively associated with clinical quality in facilities with data collection systems

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In facilities with a system to regularly collect health services data, supervision alone was significantly and positively related to quality of care; without such a system it was not. [→ Li Mengyao 2026](#li-mengyao-2026)

## Evidence

### Li Mengyao 2026

Li Mengyao, Zhou Zhongliang, Mitchell Rebecca, Wang Dan, Boyle Brendan, Wang Wenhua. (2026). Enhancing health professional clinical performance through supervision strategies: from 5311 facilities across eight countries in sick child healthcare. Journal of Global Health. https://doi.org/10.7189/jogh.16.04123

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Subgroup multilevel regression comparing facilities with a data collection system (n = 17825) against those without (n = 1346); the without-group coefficient was −0.166 and non-significant. Over 91% of facilities had data collection systems.

> "Likewise, supervision alone in facilities that implemented data collection systems was significantly and positively related to quality of care (coefficient = 0.130; 95% CI = 0.02, 0.24)."
