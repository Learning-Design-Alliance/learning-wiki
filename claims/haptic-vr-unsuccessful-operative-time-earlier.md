### Duncombe Justin M.T. 2026

Duncombe Justin M.T., Camaly de Brosses Pierre, Kassam Al-Amin M., Harris David J., Buckingham Gavin. (2026). Investigating Hip Arthroplasty Femur Preparation Training Using a Haptic-Enabled Virtual Reality Simulation. Surgical Innovation, 33(2), 169-180. https://doi.org/10.1177/15533506251383830

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Operative time for unsuccessful procedures (IDerror > 15 mm) was analysed independently in the randomized SawBone assessment. The Mann-Whitney U test showed "the VR group (median = 1022 s, IQR = 497) stopped the procedure significantly earlier than the control group (median = 1200 s, IQR = 59)" (r = 0.693).

> "A subsequent Mann-Whitney U test indicated that the VR group (median = 1022 s, IQR = 497) stopped the procedure significantly earlier than the control group (median = 1200 s, IQR = 59), z = 2.298, P = .022, r = 0.693."
