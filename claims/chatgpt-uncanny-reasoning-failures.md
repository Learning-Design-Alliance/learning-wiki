## Evidence

### Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic 2024

Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT on the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of ChatGPT's responses to item 17, where the reasoning expressed contradictory claims about velocity graphs under constant non-zero acceleration. The authors describe such behaviour as uncanny and atypical of students.

> "Item 17, shown in Fig. 7, is especially interesting because it was the least difficult for students (70% correct), while being among the most difficult for ChatGPT (5% correct). Note that its visual interpretation of the graph is correct in this case."
