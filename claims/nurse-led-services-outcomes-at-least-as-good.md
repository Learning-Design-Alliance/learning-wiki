## Evidence

### Pollard D 2020

Pollard D, Harrison C, Dodgson S, Holland M, Khair K. (2020). The UK haemophilia specialist nurse: Competencies fit for practice in the 21st century. Haemophilia. https://doi.org/10.1111/hae.14002

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of several systematic reviews of advanced nursing practice (nurse practitioners and clinical nurse specialists) across specialties, reported in the article's review section. The article states nurse-led services are "at least as effective and less or equally expensive" but notes methodological shortcomings.

> "The overall trend suggests that nurse‐led services, where nurses can autonomously review and treat patients are associated with outcomes at least as good as those associated with traditional medical models in other acute hospital settings."
