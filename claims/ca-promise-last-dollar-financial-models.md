# Most California College Promise programs use a last-dollar financial aid model, with coverage varying across tuition, fees, and books

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The most common financial support structure is the last-dollar model, used by 33 of the 42 programs, with varying coverage of tuition, fees, and books. [→ Rauner 2018](#rauner-2018)
`q2 i?` Five programs provide one-time first-dollar scholarships, and a few provide multi-year awards of up to four years. [→ Rauner 2018 (2)](#rauner-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Rauner 2018

Rauner, M., Perna, L. W., & Kanter, M. J. (2018). California College Promise: Program Characteristics and Perceptions from the Field. WestEd. https://californiacollegepromise.WestEd.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/College-Promise-Landscape-Scan-2018.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Database findings on the 42 programs: the last-dollar model determines support after factoring in other aid such as the CCPG; 33 programs use it with the printed breakdown of coverage types.

> "Of the 33 last-dollar programs in California, 17 cover tuition, fees, and books, 9 cover tuition only, and 7 cover tuition and fees."

### Rauner 2018 (2)

Rauner, M., Perna, L. W., & Kanter, M. J. (2018). California College Promise: Program Characteristics and Perceptions from the Field. WestEd. https://californiacollegepromise.WestEd.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/College-Promise-Landscape-Scan-2018.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Database findings describing first-dollar awards, in which students receive an award regardless of other financial support, plus multi-year awards of $1,000-$1,500 per year and one first-dollar program giving up to $4,000 per year.

> "Five of the 42 California programs provide a one-time first-dollar scholarship, and 2 of the 5 determine the amount of the scholarship based on the recipient's tuition fees."
