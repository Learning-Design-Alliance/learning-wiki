`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear regression on the continuous APS score in a cross-sectional survey of 91 first-semester dental and nursing students, with purpose-specific screen use entered after total duration and the reverse ordering also fitted. The article reports "Purpose-specific screen use added 19.0% of explained variance beyond total duration" and that total duration "added 2.2% beyond purpose and was not significant".

> "Purpose-specific screen use added 19.0% of explained variance beyond total duration (F(3,81) = 6.78, p < 0.001), whereas total duration added 2.2% beyond purpose and was not significant (F(1,81) = 2.37, p = 0.127)."
