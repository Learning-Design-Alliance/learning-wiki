### Hernansanz A 2023

Hernansanz A, Rovira R, Basomba J, Comas R, Casals A. (2023). EndoTrainer: a novel hybrid training platform for endoscopic surgery. International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-023-02837-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative analysis of trial subsets in the 80-gynaecologist, 30-trial study: mean accuracy rose from 78.553 (initial) to 87.885 (middle trials), and all evaluated indices showed statistically significant initial-to-middle differences. The article states "all gain is obtained in this interval" for accuracy.

> "The major gains in all evaluation indices are concentrated in this interval, Table 2, obtaining statistically significant differences. Particularly remarkable is the accuracy: all gain is obtained in this interval."
