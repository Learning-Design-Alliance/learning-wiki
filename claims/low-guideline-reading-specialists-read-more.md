## Evidence

### Çifci İ. 2025

Çifci İ., Gök U., Yorgancıoğlu A. (2025). A current assessment of family physicians’ knowledge and attitudes toward asthma management in primary care in Turkey according to the GINA strategy report. NPJ Primary Care Respiratory Medicine 36, 5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41533-025-00468-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive and chi-square analysis in the cross-sectional survey of 596 physicians: "25.8%" had read an asthma guideline in the past year and 22% had attended a scientific meeting; only specialization level significantly affected guideline reading (Pearson Chi-square, p = 0.006), with specialists higher.

> "In the past year, 25.8% have read a guideline pertaining to asthma, and 22% have attended a scientific meeting on the subject. Among the factors examined, only the level of specialization of physicians significantly affected whether they had read asthma guidelines, with family medicine specialists showing higher rates of guideline reading (Pearson Chi-square, p = 0.006)."
