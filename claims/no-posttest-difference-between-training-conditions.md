## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Posttest problem-solving performance did not differ between the heuristic and algorithmic training conditions. [→ Raaijmakers 2018](#raaijmakers-2018)

## Evidence

### Raaijmakers 2018

Raaijmakers, S. F., Baars, M., Schaap, L., Paas, F., van Merriënboer, J., & van Gog, T. (2018). Training self-regulated learning skills with video modeling examples: Do task-selection skills transfer? Instructional Science, 46, 273–290. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-017-9434-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Median test comparing heuristic (M = 2.70, SD = 1.05) and algorithmic (M = 2.69, SD = 1.30) conditions on the posttest found no significant difference (r = .09); equivalence was not tested.

> "However, problem-solving performance did not differ between training conditions, v2(1) = 0.658, p = .417, r = .09."
