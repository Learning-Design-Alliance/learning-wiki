`q3 i3` Students' engagement in MindTap increased significantly from before to during the persuasive intervention on all three measures: number of logins, time spent, and engagement score. [→ Orji 2021](#orji-2021)

## Evidence

### Orji 2021

Orji, F. A., Greer, J., & Vassileva, J. (2021). Evaluating a Persuasive Intervention for Engagement in a Large University Class. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00260-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

A one-way within-subject ANOVA tested the change in MindTap engagement before and during the intervention for 208 students. The article reports "statistically significant difference in students’ engagement in learning activities for all the three measures": number of logins (η2 = .774), time spent (η2 = .401), and engagement score (η2 = .944).

> "The results show statistically significant difference in students’ engagement in learning activities for all the three measures: number of logins (F1,206 = 428.319, p = .0001, η2= .774), time spent (F1,206 = 98.610, p = .0001, η2 = .401), and engagement score (F1,206 = 2499.862, p = .0001,η2 = .944)."
