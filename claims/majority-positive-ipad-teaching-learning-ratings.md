Archibald D, Macdonald Colla J, Plante J, Hogue RJ, Fiallos J. (2014). Residents' and preceptors' perceptions of the use of the iPad for clinical teaching in a family medicine residency program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-174

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive survey results from the pilot's mid-evaluation on 11 items about the iPad's contribution to teaching and learning. The article reports "76% of the ratings for these 11 items fell under the 'agree' or 'strongly agree' options".

> "Results from preceptors and residents as a whole indicate 76% of the ratings for these 11 items fell under the "agree" or "strongly agree" options, meaning the majority were positive about the contribution of the iPad to effective teaching and learning."
