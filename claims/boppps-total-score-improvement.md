## Evidence

### Khalafi Ali 2026

Khalafi Ali, Parhizkar Mahdieh, Albooghobeish Masoumeh, Sarvi-sarmeydani Nooshin. (2026). Effect of the BOPPPS model on pre-anesthesia care education for nurse anesthesia students in Iran: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08696-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Composite outcome analysis in the quasi-experiment: knowledge (0–15) and skill (0–72) scores were standardized to a 0–100 scale and averaged. The intervention group showed "significantly greater improvement in total scores compared to the control group" (26.47 points, d = 4.12).

> "The intervention group showed significantly greater improvement in total scores compared to the control group (mean difference: 26.47 points, 95% CI [24.40, 28.54], p < 0.001, d = 4.12), indicating a substantial overall effect of the BOPPPS model on pre-anesthesia care competency."
