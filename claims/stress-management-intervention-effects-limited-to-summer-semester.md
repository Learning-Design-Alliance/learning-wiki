# The stress-management intervention (IVSM) reduced perceived stress relative to control mainly in the second (summer) semester, not in the first

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For students in the IVSM, lower stress relative to CG was observed in the summer semester, while application of the techniques during the winter semester was apparently difficult. [→ Faure Theresa 2024](#faure-theresa-2024)

## Evidence

### Faure Theresa 2024

Faure Theresa, Weyers Imke, Voltmer Jan-Bennet, Westermann Jürgen, Voltmer Edgar. (2024). Test-reduced teaching for stimulation of intrinsic motivation (TRUST): a randomized controlled intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05640-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors' interpretation of the longitudinal randomized data: the one-day addisca stress-management workshop showed stress-reducing effects in the IVSM versus CG only in the summer semester; no effect size is printed.

> "However, in the summer semester and up to the oral and written examinations at the end of the semester, a lower proportion of the IVSM felt highly stressed than in the CG."
