## Evidence

### Rai Amar 2024

Rai Amar, Shukla Shivani, Mehtani Nikita, Acharya Vikas, Tolley Neil. (2024). Does a junior doctor focused 'Bootcamp' improve the confidence and preparedness of newly appointed ENT registrars to perform their job roles? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05691-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Station-level pre-post analysis (n=29) reported in Table 3 and Figs. 1-2: Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed significant increases across "ward based, elective and emergency settings" for preparedness and confidence, with medians reported per station. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Participants self-reported a significant increase in preparedness to complete inpatient ward rounds (p < 0.001), elective procedures in completing a neck access (p < 0.001), SPA ligation (p < 0.0001), tonsillectomy (p < 0.0001), HoloLens (p < 0.0001), rigid bronchoscopy (p < 0.0001) and cortical mastoidectomy (p < 0.0001) (Table 3)."

### Rai Amar 2024 (2)

Rai Amar, Shukla Shivani, Mehtani Nikita, Acharya Vikas, Tolley Neil. (2024). Does a junior doctor focused 'Bootcamp' improve the confidence and preparedness of newly appointed ENT registrars to perform their job roles? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05691-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Discussion of Table 3 data: among the ten stations, "the greatest improvements in self-preparedness in the rigid bronchoscopy station and the greatest improvement in self-confidence in the sphenopalatine artery (SPA) ligation station compared to the other stations".

> "ENT ST3s reported the greatest improvements in self-preparedness in the rigid bronchoscopy station and the greatest improvement in self-confidence in the sphenopalatine artery (SPA) ligation station compared to the other stations (Table 3)."
