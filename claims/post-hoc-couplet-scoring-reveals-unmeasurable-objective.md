# Applying couplet scoring post development can reveal items that cannot measure a targeted objective: all FCI item 18 options earn a point for principle L4

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In the post hoc couplet scheme for FCI item 18, every available answer option earns a point for principle L4, so the item as written cannot measure student proficiency regarding L4. [→ Michael Vignal 2023](#michael-vignal-2023)

## Evidence

### Michael Vignal 2023

Michael Vignal, Gayle Geschwind, Marcos D. Caballero, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2023). Overview of couplet scoring in content-focused physics assessments. arXiv:2307.03099. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.03099](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.03099)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Analysis of the post hoc couplet scoring of FCI item 18: because every selectable option includes the downward gravitational force, all options earn a point for L4, and the authors conclude the item "cannot serve as a measurement of student proficiency regarding L4" and would remove L4 as an AO for this item. No effect size is reported.

> "It is notable that all the available answer options earn a point for L4, as students are not allowed to select an option that does not include a downward gravitational force."
