## Evidence

### Hu Yousong 2025

Hu Yousong, Weng Yuqian, Ning Ping, Han Li, Zhang Youxue. (2025). The relationship between psychological capital and subjective academic achievement among middle school students: the chain mediating role of adolescent internalizing problems and life attitude. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03495-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path coefficients from the same PROCESS Model 6 chain mediation analysis of 1,019 students. Psychological capital "negatively predicts adolescent internalizing problems (β = -0.38, t = -12.12, p < 0.001)" and positively predicts life attitude (β = 0.65, t = 27.16, p < 0.001).

### Hu Yousong 2025 (2)

BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03495-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same chain mediation model, second mediator path: psychological capital "positively predicts life attitude (β = 0.65, t = 27.16, p < 0.001)". Internalizing problems were also negatively associated with life attitude (β = -0.10, t = -4.46, p < 0.001).
