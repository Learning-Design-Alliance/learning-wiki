## Evidence

### Agusala Vijay 2018

Agusala Vijay, Vij Priyanka, Agusala Veena, Dasari Vivekanand, Kola Bhargavi. (2018). Can interactive parental education impact health care utilization in pediatric asthma: A study in rural Texas. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060518773621

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional baseline classification of the enrolled sample (n = 100 classifiable of 102) using Expert Panel Report 3 criteria by age band; χ2 = 7.9009, P < 0.019. Descriptive baseline finding, no effect size printed.

> "There was a statistically significant difference in the severity of asthma amongst the three age groups (Table 4): 17.2% (5/29), 42.4% (14/33), and 50.0% (19/38), in age groups 1, 2 and 3, respectively, had intermittent asthma"

### Agusala Vijay 2018 (2)

Agusala Vijay, Vij Priyanka, Agusala Veena, Dasari Vivekanand, Kola Bhargavi. (2018). Can interactive parental education impact health care utilization in pediatric asthma: A study in rural Texas. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060518773621

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same baseline cross-sectional classification (61 male, 39 female of 100 classifiable); χ2 = 0.5910, reported as no significant between-group difference (P ≥ 0.05). Equivalence was not tested beyond this χ2; no effect size printed.

> "Although there were more male patients with asthma than female patients, there was no significant difference in the severity between the sexes"
