# Directly assessed effortful control at 48 months predicts prekindergarten math but not reading, and not growth in either

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Directly assessed effortful control (Dinky Toys) is positively associated with prekindergarten math (ES = .16) but unrelated to math growth. [→ Blair 2015](#blair-2015)
`q3 i?` Directly assessed effortful control is not associated with letter–word knowledge at prekindergarten. [→ Blair 2015 (2)](#blair-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Blair 2015

Blair, C., Ursache, A., Greenberg, M., Vernon-Feagans, L., & The Family Life Project Investigators. (2015). Multiple Aspects of Self-Regulation Uniquely Predict Mathematics but Not Letter–Word Knowledge in the Early Elementary Grades. Developmental Psychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5264531/

`q3 · i1`

Model A growth model. Directly assessed effortful control (Dinky Toys task at 48 months) predicted prekindergarten math: "B = 1.73, SE = 0.34, p < .001, ES = .16". No growth effect.

> "Effortful control as measured by direct assessment was also positively associated with math achievement at prekindergarten (B = 1.73, SE = 0.34, p < .001, ES = .16) but was unrelated to acquisition of math skills during the elementary years."

### Blair 2015 (2)

Blair, C., Ursache, A., Greenberg, M., Vernon-Feagans, L., & The Family Life Project Investigators. (2015). Multiple Aspects of Self-Regulation Uniquely Predict Mathematics but Not Letter–Word Knowledge in the Early Elementary Grades. Developmental Psychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5264531/

`q3 · i?`

Model D growth model. Direct effortful control assessment was not associated with letter–word knowledge, while parent and teacher report was ("B = 1.90, SE = 0.66, p < .01, ES = .13"). No effect size printed for the direct-assessment null.

> "Effortful control as rated by parent and teacher was positively associated with letter–word knowledge at prekindergarten (B = 1.90, SE = 0.66, p < .01, ES = .13) while direct assessment of EC was not."
