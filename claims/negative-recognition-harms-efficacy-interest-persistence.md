### Yangqiuting Li and Chandralekha Singh 2023

Yangqiuting Li and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). The impact of perceived recognition by physics instructors on women's self-efficacy and interest. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020125)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of 38 female physics students; the abstract-level finding is instantiated in the results, e.g., a student whose interest decreased and who stopped attending office hours saying it felt like she was not supposed to be there, and a student who withdrew from a lab course after weeks of marginalization.

> "We find that female students' negative and positive perceived recognition from instructors and teaching assistants (TAs) greatly influenced their self-efficacy and interest and even impacted their desire to persist in STEM majors."
