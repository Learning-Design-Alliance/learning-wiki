## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At the after-test, the control group had significantly higher instrument speed than both SA groups, yet did not achieve a faster task time, and the periodic SA group was significantly smoother than the control group. [→ Hillemans V 2025](#hillemans-v-2025)

## Evidence

### Hillemans V 2025

Hillemans V, Verhoeven DJ, van de Mortel X, Bökkerink GMJ, Botden SMBI, Joosten M, de Blaauw I. (2025). Optimizing skill acquisition: the role of self-assessment during a continuous training program for laparoscopic suturing. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11682-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

After-test group comparisons in the same three-group experiment. "significantly higher speed was found for the control group than both the periodic SA group and the continuous SA group", but the control group did not achieve a faster time, which the authors read as less efficient movement; periodic SA was also significantly smoother than control (p = 0.023).

> "At the after-test, significantly higher speed was found for the control group than both the periodic SA group and the continuous SA group (control group 22.4 mm/s, periodic SA group 8.5 mm/s, continuous SA group 9.8 mm/s, 0.013 ≤ p ≤ 0.049)"
