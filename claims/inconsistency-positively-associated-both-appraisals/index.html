Evidence¶

Chen Y 2026¶

Chen Y (2026) When universities advocate GAI but practice falls short: student appraisals and creative process engagement in art education. Front. Psychol. 17:1955192. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1955192

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Cross-sectional online survey of 425 art students with recent GAI-assisted art-learning experience, analyzed with PLS-SEM (SmartPLS 4.1, 5,000-resample bootstrapping). The model showed inconsistency "positively associated with hindrance appraisal" and with challenge appraisal.

"GAI advocacy–practice inconsistency was positively associated with hindrance appraisal (β = 0.431, p < 0.001) and challenge appraisal (β = 0.496, p < 0.001), supporting H1a and H2a."
