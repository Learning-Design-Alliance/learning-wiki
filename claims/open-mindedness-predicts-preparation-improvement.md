## Evidence

### Pu Dan 2019

Pu Dan, Ni Juhua, Song Demao, Zhang Weiguang, Wang Yuedan, Wu Liling, Wang Xian, Wang Yun. (2019). Influence of critical thinking disposition on the learning efficiency of problem-based learning in undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1418-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Stepwise multiple linear regression (n = 102) with the five PBL performance dimensions as successive dependent variables and CT subscales as independents. Table 7 prints R = 0.250, R square change = 0.063, F change = 6.667, Sig F change = 0.011 for open-mindedness on preparation.

> "It was found that the open-mindedness subscale of CT disposition was the primary factor that influences the improvement of the Preparation dimensions in the PBL process"
