## Evidence

### Sarah Collier Villaume 2025

Sarah Collier Villaume, Amy Auletto, & Kadir Bahar. (2025). Investing Early: The Impact of State Prekindergarten Funding on Kindergarten Academic Outcomes. E4 Center. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2025/06/30/investing-early-the-impact-of-state-prekindergarten-funding-on-kindergarten-academic-outcomes/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of district kindergarten offerings gathered from district websites, by state category (Figure 5). The report found "no statistically significant links between district-level kindergarten offerings and kindergarten math growth," attributing this to lacking student-level participation data.

> "We did not observe any statistically significant links between district-level kindergarten offerings and kindergarten math growth. This is likely due to the fact that we did not have access to student-level information on kindergarten participation, but rather what was offered in each student’s district."
