Evidence¶

Hornstein 2025¶

Hornstein, J., Keller, M. V., Greisel, M., Dresel, M., & Kollar, I. (2025). Enhancing the Peer-Feedback Process Through Instructional Support: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10017-3

q4 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analysis of 13 studies (k = 47 effect sizes) for content-specific provision support on quality of feedback provision: g = 0.75, 95% CI [0.11, 1.38], p < 0.05; on knowledge g = 0.34, p = 0.14 (Table 4).

"we found a positive medium-sized effect of content-specific support during feedback provision on the quality of feedback provision but no significant effect on the quality of feedback reception, nor on subject-matter-related knowledge"

Hornstein 2025 (2)¶

Hornstein, J., Keller, M. V., Greisel, M., Dresel, M., & Kollar, I. (2025). Enhancing the Peer-Feedback Process Through Instructional Support: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10017-3

q4 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analysis of 8 studies (k = 45 effect sizes) for generic provision support on quality of feedback provision: g = 0.70, 95% CI [0.15, 1.25], p < 0.05; on knowledge 7 studies, k = 20: g = 0.55, 95% CI [0.13, 0.98], p < 0.05 (Table 4).

"For generic support during feedback provision, we found positive medium-sized effects on the quality of feedback provision and on subject-mat-ter-related knowledge but no significant effect on the quality of feedback reception"
