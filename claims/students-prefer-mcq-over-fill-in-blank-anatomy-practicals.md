`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same survey cohort (n = 114); 107 students (93.9%) preferred MCQs over fill-in-the-blank, a statistically significant preference by chi-square test. Reasons included relative ease and consistency with standardized exams.

> "107 (93.9%) preferred MCQs, while seven (6.1%) preferred fill‐in‐the‐blank (χ2(1) = 87.72, p < 0.001, Figure 1B)"
