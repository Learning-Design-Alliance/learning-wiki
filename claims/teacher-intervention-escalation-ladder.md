Exploring K-12 Teachers' Perceptions of Students' Relationships with AI Companions: Boundaries, Intervention Strategies, and Design Implications. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13479 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.13479](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.13479)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview finding (RQ2). Proactive teaching belonged almost entirely to the academic scenarios; teachers described one-on-one conversations opened "with curiosity rather than judgment" and referral as being "an extra set of eyes".

> "The four strategies teachers described form an escalation ladder: proactive instruction before any problem appears, quiet watching while nothing is yet wrong, a private conversation once effects surface, and referral when the case exceeds what a conversation can hold."
