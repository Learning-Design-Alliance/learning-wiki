Evidence¶

Zi-Wei Liang 2025¶

Zi-Wei Liang, Zhi-Jun Zhan, Ying-Chen Liu, Hong-Zhou Xu & Jing Yu. (2025). Interpersonal neural synchronization underlies interactive concept learning in older adults. npj Science of Learning 10:75. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00368-5

q3 · i3 · causal · r?

fNIRS hyperscanning of 58 real dyads in Study 1; wavelet transform coherence computed instructor-learner INS. For older learners, passive-learning frontopolar INS "positively correlated with learning outcomes for older learners" (r = 0.50, p = 0.01); the active-learning correlation was not significant. The age × approach INS interaction at CH12/CH19 had ηp2 = 0.08, and permutation analysis confirmed real dyads exceeded pseudo dyads (ps < 0.05).

"Pearson correlations further revealed that frontopolar INS during passive learning positively correlated with learning outcomes for older learners (r =0 . 5 0 ,p =0 . 0 1 ; F i g .2a), whereas no such correlation was found in the active learning (r = 0.33, p = 0.09)."
