## Evidence

### Salarvand 2022

Salarvand, L., Guimaraes, N., & Balagholi, Z. (2022). Instructional Strategies' Impacts on EFL Learners Reading Fluency: A Review. THAITESOL JOURNAL, 35(1), 77-90. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1340892

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Six-week RT study with 38 first-year university students (CEFR below B1) performing an adapted The Picture of Dorian Gray script, assessed with the modified Multidimensional Fluency Scale. The review reports "an average 1.02-point increase" in phrasing between Week 3 and Week 6 performances.

> "Among the four fluency areas on the rubric including expression and volume, phrasing, accuracy and pace, participants showed evident progress in phrasing with an average 1.02-point increase."

### Salarvand 2022 (2)

Salarvand, L., Guimaraes, N., & Balagholi, Z. (2022). Instructional Strategies' Impacts on EFL Learners Reading Fluency: A Review. THAITESOL JOURNAL, 35(1), 77-90. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1340892

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative strand of the same Lekwilai (2016) study using self-reflection forms. The review reports progress across all fluency aspects, "high motivation to practice the script outside the class", and unanimous positive attitudes toward RT.

> "Data from the self -reflection forms also revealed progress not only in all aspects of participants' oral reading fluency, but also their high motivation to practice the script outside the class and their unanimous positive attitude towards the above-mentioned RT activity."
