### Gielen 2015

Gielen, M. & De Wever, B. (2015). Scripting the role of assessor and assessee in peer assessment in a wiki environment: Impact on peer feedback quality and product improvement. Computers & Education, 88, 370-386. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.07.012

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire with 5-point Likert items analyzed by ANOVA across the four conditions. The results report that "both the request (M= 3.90, SD=0.83, p=.043) and content (M= 3.86, SD=0.87 , p=.024) condition perceived the PFB request significantly more useful" than the combination condition.

> "both the request (M= 3.90, SD=0.83, p=.043) and content (M= 3.86, SD=0.87 , p=.024) condition perceived the PFB request significantly more useful to formulate feedback, compared to students in the combination condition (M= 3.21, SD=1.14)"
