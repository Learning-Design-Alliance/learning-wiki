Mehrnoosh Karimi, Raouf Hamzavi. (2017). The Effect of Flipped Model of Instruction on EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension: Learners' Attitudes in Focus. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 8(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.1p.95

Background-literature attribution: the article reports, citing Al-Harbi and Alshumaimeri (2016), a study of 43 secondary students in two groups where attitudes were positive but the grammar performance difference was not statistically significant. By contrast, it attributes significant listening and reading gains to Samah and Saka (2016) and Abaeian and Samadi (2016).

> "the results showed that the flipped classroom strategy play a key role in improving the students' grammar performances, but this difference was not statistically significant."
