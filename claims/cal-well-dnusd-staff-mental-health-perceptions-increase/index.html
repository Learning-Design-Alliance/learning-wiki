Evidence¶

WestEd 2024¶

WestEd. (2024). Del Norte Unified School District: A Comprehensive Approach to Mental Health Services Through Project Cal-Well. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655967

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive results from the School Staff Survey administered by DNUSD, as reported in the brief. Staff agreement about communicating mental health supports rose from "44 percent in 2021/22 to 71 percent in 2022/23"; emphasis rose from 55 to 63 percent, and multiple referrals rose from 29 to 60 percent. No inferential tests or effect sizes are reported.

"The percentage of school staff agreeing that their school communicates to students about the availability of mental health supports increased from 44 percent in 2021/22 to 71 percent in 2022/23."
