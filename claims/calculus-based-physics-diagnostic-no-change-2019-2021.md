### Eric W. Burkholder 2022

Eric W. Burkholder, Carl E. Wieman. (2022). Absence of a COVID-induced academic drop in high-school physics learning. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.023102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.023102)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of summer 2019 (N = 597) and summer 2021 (N = 757) diagnostic scores for students intending calculus-based physics, tested with a t-test and Cohen's d. The article reports "d = -0.061 and is not statistically significant (p = 0.27)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "In 2019, the average score was 24.1 out of 36 (standard deviation = 7.44 points; N = 597). In 2021, the average score was 23.6 points (s. d. = 7.31 points; N = 757). The difference between these two means is d = -0.061 and is not statistically significant (p = 0.27)."
