### Ibukunoluwa Bukola 2026

Ibukunoluwa Bukola, Eric Brewe, Adrienne L. Traxler, and Meagan Sundstrom. (2026). Characterizing adopters of named active learning methods in physics: An interview study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.14311](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.14311)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview coding of initial method exposure (Table IV, Fig. 5a). The article reports direct engagement and professional development avenues "were the most common ways" instructors in the sample became aware of their named method.

> "Direct engagement with the method (e.g., serving as a TA for a course using the method or working with an advisor who uses or conducts research on the method as a graduate student) and professional development avenues (e.g., workshops and conferences) were the most common ways the instructors in our sample became aware of their named method"
