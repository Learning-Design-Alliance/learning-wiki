## Evidence

### Vu 2021

Vu, T., Magis-Weinberg, L., Jansen, B. R. J., van Atteveldt, N., Jansen, T. W. P. J., Lee, N. C., van der Maas, H. L. J., Raijmakers, M. E. J., Sachisthal, M. S. M., & Meeter, M. (2021). Motivation-Achievement Cycles in Learning: a Literature Review and Research Agenda. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09616-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of non-longitudinal studies reports that with effort as quality of learning "there is some evidence for a positive link between academic achievement and effort" (Trigwell et al., 2013), but quantity-of-learning effort shows weak, conditional, or even negative relations (labour-in-vain effect). Longitudinal mediator studies (Marsh et al., 2016; Pinxten et al., 2014; Trautwein et al., 2009) yielded mixed findings on subjective effort.

> "However, when effort is measured as a quantity of learning (such as study time, practice time, time-on-task, persistence, etc.), this relationship seems either weak or only significant after controlling for quality of learning"
