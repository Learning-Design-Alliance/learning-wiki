Dorris 2024¶

Dorris, C., Winter, K., O'Hare, L., & Lwoga, E. T. (2024). A systematic review of mobile device use in the primary school classroom and impact on pupil literacy and numeracy attainment: a systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews, 20, e1417. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1417

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review's own moderator analysis plan (age, gender, screen size, dosage, SAMR-based implementation features) could not be powered: "too few studies identified to support quantitative analysis of sufficient power". No effect sizes are printed for any moderator.

"There were too few studies identified to support quantitative analysis of sufficient power to draw robust conclusions on moderating factors, and insufficient data to determine impact beyond immediate post‐test period."
