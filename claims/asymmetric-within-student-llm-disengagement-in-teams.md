# Within-student transitions are asymmetric: 18.9% of consistent individual users stop using LLMs in teams while only 3.2% start, with framing shifts concentrated in movement to non-use

Within-student transition analysis classifying each of 95 students by dominant HW and TP usage pattern; the quote reports the 18.9% and 3.2% transition rates. A Wilcoxon signed-rank test confirmed the asymmetry (W = 254.0, p < .0001); no standardized effect size is printed.
