## Evidence

### Jourde 2026

Jourde, H.R., Sita, K.Z., Eyqvelle, Z., Brooks, M., & Coffey, E.B.J. (2026). Modulating sleep: slow oscillation and spindle stimulation effects on physiology and memory. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00383-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In a randomized between-subjects nap study with 102 healthy young adults, stimulation during SO up-states produced a clear evoked slow oscillation with "statistically-significant differences from sham" between 0.5 and 1.5 seconds post stimulation, replicating prior CLAS findings.

> "A clearevokedslowoscillationwasobservedwhensoundwas delivered during SO-up-states, with statistically-significant differences from sham (‘Sleep’ condition) between 0.5 and 1.5 seconds post stimulation"
