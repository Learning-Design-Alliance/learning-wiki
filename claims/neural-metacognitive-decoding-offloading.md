The review reports Boldt and Gilbert (2022), an fMRI study using multivariate pattern analysis to decode metacognitive monitoring and control during an intention offloading paradigm. No effect size is printed.

> "a pattern classifier that had only ever been trained to classify high versus low desire for a reminder also accurately clas- sified the difference between a brain with high versus low confidence, even though it was presented with novel data and had never been trained on this distinction"
