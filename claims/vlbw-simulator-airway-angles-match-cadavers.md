CT-based anatomic comparison in a prospective multi-center study: airway angles of four VLBW simulators were measured on sagittal midplane reconstructions and compared with anatomical preparations of stillborn premature cadavers of corresponding weight categories. The article reports that "most angles were within the range of angles measured in the corresponding reference cadavers".

> "Figure 2 shows that most angles were within the range of angles measured in the corresponding reference cadavers (red ribbon)."
