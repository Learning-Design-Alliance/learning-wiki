## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Winch observes that when driven from intellectual advantage, the defense of early entry retreats to moral advantage and then to advantage in attention development. [→ Winch 1914](#winch-1914)

## Evidence

### Winch 1914

This is Winch's author interpretation of English educational debate: belief in early entry persists despite evidence, relocating its justification successively to moral advantage and to attention development. The article offers no data for this observation itself.

> "Driven from 'intellectual advantage,' its defense entrenches itself behind 'moral advantage,' and expelled from that redoubt once more makes a stand behind 'advantage in the development of the attention.'"
