Weighted descriptive analysis of the 2022/23 MO DESE mentoring survey (analytic sample 297; n = 258 for this item), reported in table 1. The report states "Over 80 percent of the teachers reported that their meetings lasted less than 30 minutes on average" and that over 70 percent met several times a month.

"Over half of first-year teachers (56 percent) both met with their mentors several times a month and met for less than 30 minutes on average each time."
