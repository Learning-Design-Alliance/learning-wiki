## Evidence

### Crooke 2014

Crooke, A. H. D., & McFerran, K. S. (2014). Recommendations for the investigation and delivery of music programs aimed at achieving psychosocial wellbeing benefits in mainstream schools. Australian Journal of Music Education, 2014(1), 15-37. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1061963

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post survey evaluation of a weekly five-week school-based music therapy program for six bereaved students, with surveys and interviews conducted with five students. The article reports "no scale results were statistically significant" despite interview-reported benefits.

> "No PSWB benefits were captured in the analysis of the survey data. Mean change scores did show improvement on 5 scales, yet no scale results were statistically significant."
