Gormally C., Brickman P., Lutz M. (2012). Developing a Test of Scientific Literacy Skills (TOSLS): Measuring Undergraduates' Evaluation of Scientific Information and Arguments. CBE—Life Sciences Education 11(4):364–377. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-03-0026

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Statistical characterization of the final TOSLS administered pre- and postsemester in a traditional lecture-based nonmajors course (n = 296). The article reports "The internal reliability of the TOSLS was 0.731and 0.748" and separately reports average item difficulty of 0.59 (pre) and 0.68 (post) and average item discrimination of 0.26–0.27.

> "The internal reliability of the TOSLS was 0.731and 0.748 on the pretest and posttest, respectively (Table 5). These scores fall within the acceptable range of reliability."
