What Works Clearinghouse 2010¶

The WWC report describes a quasi-experimental study across 98 Florida schools comparing 6,070 low-achieving intervention students with 7,058 comparison students, with outcomes after six months. The report states "The WWC-cal -culated effect was small (0.06) but statistically significant."

"Reading Plus (2008) found a statistically significant positive effect of Reading Plus ® on the reading portion of the FCAT for low-achieving students. The WWC-cal -culated effect was small (0.06) but statistically significant."
