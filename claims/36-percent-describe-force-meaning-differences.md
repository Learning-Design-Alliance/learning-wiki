### Salomon F. Itza-Ortiz 2003

Salomon F. Itza-Ortiz, N. Sanjay Rebello, Dean Zollman and Manuel Rodriguez-Achach. (2003). The Vocabulary of Introductory Physics and Its Implications for Learning Physics. Originally published 2002. [doi:10.1119/1.1607802](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1607802)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-survey of 154 students in “The Physical World I” course at Kansas State, administered after the term force was introduced, with responses classified into three categories validated by an independent researcher. The article reports that “Thirty-six percent of the students in the second survey were in categories 1 and 2” and that the remaining 64% were category 3.

> "Thirty-six percent of the students in the second survey were in categories 1 and 2, i.e. they described the similarities and differences between the meaning of the word “force” in the given sentences and its physics meaning."
