# A 7-day virtual simulation game on CAUTI prevention produced higher post-test knowledge scores than standard training presentations in nursing students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Nursing students who used the VSG for 7 days scored higher on a post-test knowledge measure of CAUTI prevention than control students who reviewed presentations and videos, with a medium effect size (d = 0.618). [→ Mercan Annak 2026](#mercan-annak-2026)

## Evidence

### Mercan Annak 2026

Mercan Annak, İ., and Bulut, H. 2026. "The Effect of Virtual Simulation Game on the Development of Nursing Students' Knowledge and Skills: A Randomized Controlled Trial." Nursing Open 13, no. 5: e70566. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70566

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Parallel-group randomized controlled trial; 75 third-year nursing students (38 VSG, 37 control) completed a 24-item knowledge test after both groups received standardized CAUTI prevention training. The VSG group scored higher post-test, "at a medium level (d = 0.618)"; pre-test difference was not significant (p = 0.822).

> "However, the knowledge score of the VSG group was found to be higher than that of the control group in the post‐test measurement (p = 0.009). The effect size of the between‐group difference in knowledge level was at a medium level (d = 0.618)"
