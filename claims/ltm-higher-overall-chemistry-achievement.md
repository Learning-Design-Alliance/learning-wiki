## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Seventh-standard students taught chemistry through the Laboratory Training Model scored significantly higher on overall achievement than students taught by the traditional method (t = 21.847, p = .000). [→ Badeleh 2011](#badeleh-2011)

## Evidence

### Badeleh 2011

Alireza Badeleh. (2011). The Effect of Laboratory Training Model of Teaching and Traditional Method on Knowledge, Comprehension, Application, Skills-Components of Achievement, Total Achievement and Retention Level in Chemistry. i-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology, Vol. 7, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102743

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test parallel group experiment; independent sample t-test on post scores. Table 6 prints means of 72.5333 (Laboratory) vs 38.3292 (Traditional), n = 60 per group, "t 21.847 118 .000". The article reports a statistically significant difference; no effect size is printed.

> "the t-value of 21.847 was found to be statistically significant as the p-value of .000 was smaller than the cut-off .05. This showed that there was significant difference between students taught by LTM, and traditional methods with respect to post scores in overall achievement in chemistry"
