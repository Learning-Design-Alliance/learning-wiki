Egüz 2019¶

Egüz, Ş., Öztürk, C. & Kesten, A. (2019). Technology-supported global education: A mixed method study. Pegem Eğitim ve Öğretim Dergisi, 9(4), 1209-1244. http://dx.doi.org/10.14527/pegegog.2019.040

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Semi-experimental unbalanced pre-test post-test control group study with 44 experimental and 41 control 6th graders; Mann-Whitney U comparison of post-test scores showed "a significant difference ... in favor of the experimental group" (rank means 56.41 vs 28.61); no effect size printed.
