# Monitoring support delivered via a scripted conversational agent significantly improves goal attainment compared with reflection-only and control conditions

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Participants in the Monitoring Only and Monitoring & Reflection conditions reported significantly higher goal attainment than those in Reflection Only and Control conditions. [→ Martins van Jaarsveld 2025](#martins-van-jaarsveld-2025)

## Evidence

### Martins van Jaarsveld 2025

Martins van Jaarsveld, G., Wong, J., Baars, M., Specht, M., & Paas, F. (2025). Enhancing goal attainment in higher education with a scripted conversational agent: Effects of monitoring and reflection support in digital learning. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105441

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA on weekly self-reported goal attainment in an RCT with 84 undergraduates randomly assigned to four conditions over five weeks; the main effect of condition was significant with η2p = .287, "indicating a large effect".

> "The results showed a significant main effect of condition, F(3, 80) = 10.728, p < .001, η2p = .287, indicating a large effect. Results of pairwise comparisons with Bonferroni corrections showed significant differences in goal attainment across conditions."
