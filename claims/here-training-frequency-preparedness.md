### Karniej Piotr 2025

Karniej Piotr, Dissen Anthony, Pietrzykowski Łukasz, Juárez-Vela Raúl, Sabater Antonio Martinez, Kulińska Janina, Zieliński Jakub, Czapla Michał. (2025). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06744-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear regression of the Spanish cohort on LGBT-DOCSS subscales; clinical preparedness rose with training frequency, with the largest increase among those attending "3 or more trainings" (B = 1.659).

> "The largest increase was observed among participants who had attended 3 or more trainings, who scored 1.659 points higher than those who had never attended (B = 1.659)."

### Karniej Piotr 2025 (2)

Karniej Piotr, Dissen Anthony, Pietrzykowski Łukasz, Juárez-Vela Raúl, Sabater Antonio Martinez, Kulińska Janina, Zieliński Jakub, Czapla Michał. (2025). Lesbian, gay, bisexual, and transgender clinical competence of health professionals in Poland and Spain: results of the health exclusion research in Europe (HERE) study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06744-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear regression of the Polish cohort; attending training "1–3 times" was associated with a 0.388-point increase in total LGBT-DOCSS score versus never attending.

> "Additionally, participation in LGBT-related training 1–3 times resulted in a 0.388-point increase compared to those who had never attended such training."
