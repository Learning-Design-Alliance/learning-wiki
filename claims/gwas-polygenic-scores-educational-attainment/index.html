Evidence¶

H. Moriah Sokolowski and Daniel Ansari 2018¶

H. Moriah Sokolowski and Daniel Ansari. (2018). Understanding the effects of education through the lens of biology. npj Science of Learning 3:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0032-y

q3 · i? · review · r3

GWAS of 1.1 million individuals identified 1271 independent SNPs for educational attainment; the derived polygenic score explained "11– 13% of the variance in educational attainment" and 7–10% of cognitive-performance variance in independent samples. No standardized effect size is printed.

"Most recently, a GWAS study with 1.1 million individuals identi ﬁed 1271 independent SNPs associated with educational attainment. 88 These SNPS were used to compute a polygenic prediction score that explained 11– 13% of the variance in educational attainment and 7 – 10% of the variance in cognitive performance in independent samples."

H. Moriah Sokolowski and Daniel Ansari 2018 (2)¶

H. Moriah Sokolowski and Daniel Ansari. (2018). Understanding the effects of education through the lens of biology. npj Science of Learning 3:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0032-y

q2 · i0 · review · r2

The review reports that the three SNPs from the original years-of-education GWAS have "coef ﬁcient of determination R(2) ≈0.02%", about 1 month of schooling per allele, replicated in a study with a threefold larger sample; this per-SNP effect is negligible.

"Critically, the estimated effects sizes of these three SNPs are small (coef ﬁcient of determination R(2) ≈0.02%; this equals approximately 1 month of schooling per allele)."

Related Claims¶

  • The number of genome-wide significant loci associated with educational attainment increases with GWAS discovery sample size — related
  • A polygenic score for educational attainment explains about 15% of individual differences in out-of-sample prediction among people of European descent, with a correlation of approximately 0.4 with outcomes — related
  • Polygenic score predictive power for educational attainment increases with discovery sample size and is projected to approach the SNP heritability bound — related
  • Polygenic risk scores for educational attainment and cognitive performance show the most consistent associations with language traits, and these associations are stronger for English than Chinese literacy phenotypes — related
