### Gram P 2024

Gram P, Purup T, Eriksen EM, Søbye S, Wielandt SK, Astorp K, Jensen MB, Andersen CA. (2024). Ultrasound education, competencies and expected future use among medical interns in Denmark: a national cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06510-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Among the survey's 192 ultrasound-using interns, descriptive results show 91.8% experienced barriers, led by "insufficient scanning routine (70.5%, 65.8–74.9)". Percentages are proportions with confidence intervals; no effect size is printed.

> "Most (91.8%, 88.7–94.3) experienced barriers for using ultrasound, including insufficient scanning routine (70.5%, 65.8–74.9), lack of supervision (46.7%, 41.7–51.7), insufficient knowledge (41.4%, 36.6–46.4), time constraints (37.0%, 32.2–41.9)"
