`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

This is the authors' reflective account from the First Wave section of a network retrospective, based on an April 2023 Chicago convening. The paper states a considerable first-wave effort went to finding a shared core "midway between a singular, unified approach and a loose federation of mostly independent efforts."

> "Reflecting on the first wave, the greatest challenge was developing the right levels of mutual knowledge and coherence, yet with respect for differences in each of the six projects of the Wave 1 network."
