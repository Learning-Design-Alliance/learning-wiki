## Evidence

### Newell 2008

Newell, T. S. (2008). Examining Information Problem-Solving, Knowledge, and Application Gains within Two Instructional Methods: Problem-Based and Computer-Mediated Participatory Simulation. School Library Media Research. https://www.ala.org/aasl/slmr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized two-group trial, the knowledge-section gain comparison reversed the overall result: "The PB learners experienced significantly larger knowledge gains (T = -3.664, p = .00*)"; PS pretest and posttest knowledge means were essentially unchanged (e.g., 36.25 to 36.67 percent).
