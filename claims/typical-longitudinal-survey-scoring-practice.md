## Evidence

### Megan Kuhfeld 2020

Megan Kuhfeld, James Soland. (2020). Avoiding bias from sum scores in growth estimates: An examination of IRT-based approaches to scoring longitudinal survey responses. Psychological Methods. https://doi.org/10.1037/met0000367

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The article describes standard practice in longitudinal growth modeling of psychological and social-emotional constructs: a "consistent set of self-report survey items across multiple school years" scored as sum scores or IRT scale scores. This is a descriptive statement of common practice, not a tested result.

> "students are typically administered a consistent set of self-report survey items across multiple school years, and growth is measured either based on sum scores or scale scores produced based on item response theory (IRT) methods"
