Myers 1960¶

The article describes a methodological demonstration in which sequential analysis estimates television program efficiency. The authors state it is "a quick and economical technique of sequential analysis to estimate the efficiency of a television program."

"To describe and demonstrate a quick and economical technique of sequential analysis to estimate the efficiency of a television program. Efficiency is defined as the cost in.dollars to reach 1000 households."
