### Trevor I. Smith 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Numerical comparison for exemplar items including items 22 and 34 in Table I. The article reports the stark pattern for seven items where the most common incorrect response is highest-ranked for DS2full but lowest or tied for lowest for DS1full.

> "This difference is quite stark for seven of the items (including items 22 and 34) in that the most commonly chosen incorrect response is the highest-ranked incorrect response for DS2full, but the lowest (or tied for lowest) for DS1full."
