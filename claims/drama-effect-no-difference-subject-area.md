Batdi, V. & Elaldi, S. (2020). Effects of drama method on social communication skills: A comparative analysis. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 6(3), 435-457. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1258528

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Mixed-effect moderator analysis of the 24 studies grouped by subject area (Table 4). The article reports that "except for Mathematics (gMathematics=0.734), all the groups had a large effect size (gTotal=1.025)", with Science highest at g = 1.113.

> "Based on this, it can be stated that there were no significant differences amongst the inter groups in terms of subject area of the studies included in the meta analysis (Z= 8.437; P= 0.860)."
