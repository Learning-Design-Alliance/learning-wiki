## Evidence

### Anschuetz Lukas 2025

Anschuetz Lukas, Tschopp Samuel, Trippel Mafalda, Huwendiek Sören, Trepp Roman, Caversaccio Marco, von Werdt Moritz, Borner Urs. (2025). A Novel Training Model to Improve Diagnostic Accuracy of Cervical Fine‐Needle Aspiration. Head & Neck. https://doi.org/10.1002/hed.28039

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mixed-effect model over the three training days with fixed effects for training day and influencing factor and a random effect per participant (15 residents); no factor significantly affected the learning curve. No effect sizes are printed.

> "We tested the influence of gender, preferred ultrasound orientation, age, and experience on the learning curve using a mixed‐effect model. Fixed effects were used for the training day and influencing factor, and a random effect was introduced for each participant. We found no evidence of any influence of these factors on the learning curve."

### Anschuetz Lukas 2025 (2)

Anschuetz Lukas, Tschopp Samuel, Trippel Mafalda, Huwendiek Sören, Trepp Roman, Caversaccio Marco, von Werdt Moritz, Borner Urs. (2025). A Novel Training Model to Improve Diagnostic Accuracy of Cervical Fine‐Needle Aspiration. Head & Neck. https://doi.org/10.1002/hed.28039

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Linear regression of influencing factors on clinical FNA results (age, gender, preferred plane, years of experience, competence, preferred technique); experience with ultrasound or FNA had no significant impact (p > 0.05), while the gender contrast showed a 21% effect (95% CI 8%–33%). This percentage-point difference is not a standardized effect size.

> "Interestingly, men benefit more from training than women. The effect is 21% 95%CI 8%–33%, p < 0.001."
