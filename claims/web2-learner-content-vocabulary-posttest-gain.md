## Evidence

### Eren 2015

Omer Eren. (2015). Vocabulary Learning on Learner-Created Content by Using Web 2.0 Tools. Contemporary Educational Technology, 6(4), 281-300. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105759

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study with 23 experimental and 22 control students analyzed by independent samples t-test in SPSS 15.0; Table 4 reports means of 23.17 vs 17.5, t = 3.63, p = .001, df = 43. The article reports "this difference is statistically significant".

> "The post-test results show that there is a difference between mean scores of the experimental group and the control group , and this difference is statistically significant."
