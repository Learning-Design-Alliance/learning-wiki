Evidence¶

Maikki Pouta 2020¶

Maikki Pouta, Erno Lehtinen & Tuire Palonen. (2020). Student Teachers' and Experienced Teachers' Professional Vision of Students' Understanding of the Rational Number Concept. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09536-y

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Qualitative coding of fraction-related CRR episodes into descriptive, evaluative and interpretive levels, double-coded with 79–86% inter-rater reliability. The article reports a significant group difference with printed percentages; no effect size is printed.

"Student teachers' notions were mostly interpretative (27.3 %) and evaluative (63.6%), whereas experienced teachers' explanations were predominantly evaluative (41.7%) and descriptive (44.4%). The difference in interpretation levels was significant, χ2 (2, N =6 9 )= 10.93, p < 0.01."
