## Evidence

### Sheppard Gillian 2023

Sheppard Gillian, Williams Kerry-Lynn, Metcalfe Brian, Clark Marcia, Bromley Mark, Pageau Paul, Woo Michael, Yi Yanqing, Devasahayam Augustine Joshua, Dubrowski Adam. (2023). Using Kane's framework to build an assessment tool for undergraduate medical student's clinical competency with point of care ultrasound. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04030-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multi-center mixed-methods study in which seven blinded expert raters scored videos of 59 participants performing abdominal, cardiac, and aorta POCUS scans. The article reports Fleiss' kappa "ranged from fair to moderate" across the five pre-selected competence items, with 95% CIs as printed.

> "The level of agreement between seven expert raters for all participants on five questions on clinical competency using Fleiss' kappa ranged from fair to moderate (Abdomen Q 6: 0.52, CI 0.29–0.75; Abdomen Q 8:0.55, CI 0.33–0.77; Abdomen Q 9: 0.32, CI 0.07–0.57; Cardiac Q 9: 0.54, CI 0.32–0.77; and Aorta Q 7: 0.48, CI 0.24–0.73) (n = 59)"
