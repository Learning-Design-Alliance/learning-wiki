Retention questionnaire with 22 trial-1 participants ten months after the module. The article reports "All students were able to write the numbers: billion, million, and ten thousand in powers of ten" and 77% recalled the Ten Times Alice rule; the Powers of the Universe book was the most remembered activity at 64%.

> "All students were able to write the numbers: billion, million, and ten thousand in powers of ten (Q2). Three quarters of students (77%) remembered the rules about dividing by ten as expressed in the songs in the Ten Times Alice play"
