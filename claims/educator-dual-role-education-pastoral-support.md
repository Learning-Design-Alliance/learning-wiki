### Alexander 2026

Alexander, L., Burdeu, G., Cappello, A., & Griffin, K. (2026). Qualified but Unprepared: The Readiness Paradox in Early Career Mental Health Nurses—A Descriptive Qualitative Study. International Journal of Mental Health Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/inm.70285

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 4 interview accounts: the educator role had "expanded beyond its formal education remit to include substantial emotional and relational labour". One educator questioned whether contacting an ECN after hours "crossed a boundary". Escalation policies improved clarity but did not remove emotional labour.

> "it's not that different (to nursing patients) …other educators have really struggled with it"
