## Evidence

### Accelerated Reader™ 2010

Accelerated Reader™. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Accelerated Reader™, Adolescent Literacy. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.renlearn.com/ar/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Randomized controlled trial in one Oregon elementary school: 39 treatment students compared with 43 control students in grades 4 and 5 after 10 weeks. The WWC-calculated average effect size was "not large enough to be considered substantively important" (below 0.25).

> "The author did not find statistically significant effects of Accelerated Reader ™ on the Dynamic Indicators of Basic Early Literacy Skills (DIBELS), Oral Reading Fluency subtest across grades 4 and 5."
