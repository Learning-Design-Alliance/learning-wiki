### i-Ready Personalized Instruction 2025

i-Ready Personalized Instruction. (2025). Evidence for ESSA program description. https://www.curriculumassociates.com/professional-development

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r?`

Quasi-experimental study of 5th graders in six Massachusetts school districts during 2020-2021; treatment defined as completing at least one i-Ready lesson in the first quarter, with matching on achievement and demographics. The article reports "significantly higher ELA MCAS scores" with a printed effect size of ES = +0.12.

> "Over 2,000 students were included in the analysis and those who received i-Ready Personalized Instruction had significantly higher ELA MCAS scores compared to the comparison group (ES = +0.12)"
