Evidence¶

L.M. Richter et al 2021¶

L.M. Richter et al. (2021). Measuring and forecasting progress in education: what about early childhood? npj Science of Learning 6:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00106-7

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Simulation of present discounted value of forgone lifetime earnings (net of programme costs) for 134 countries with populations of over 6.3 billion, using 2018 UNESCO gross enrolment rates, an 8% earnings impact, 3% discount rate and PPP-adjusted costs. Median COIs are "tending to be greater in lower-income countries" and often exceed governmental education expenditures (4.1–5% of GDP); no standardised effect size is printed.

"Figure 3 gives the median COIs for not reaching SDG 4.2.2 for 1 year for country groups: high (0.58% of GDP), upper-middle (2.54% of GDP), lower-middle (6.24% of GDP) and low-income (9.06% of GDP). The association with income is inverse"
