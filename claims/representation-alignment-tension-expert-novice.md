### Danxuan Liang 2026

Danxuan Liang, Chun Yin Li, Zheng Wei, Xian Xu, Meng Xia, Huamin Qu, Wai Tong. (2026). LumiNote: LLM-Assisted Multimodal Instruction for VR Stage Lighting Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17335 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.17335](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.17335)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-phase exploratory study comparing instructor interviews and teaching observations with student questionnaires, task performance, and interviews across 24 students. The authors found "representations that helped instructors externalize expert reasoning were not always those that novices found easiest to follow."

> "Finally, instructor and learner evidence revealed a representation-alignment tension: representations that helped instructors externalize expert reasoning were not always those that novices found easiest to follow."
