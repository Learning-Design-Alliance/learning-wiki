## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Univariate ANOVA showed non-Western students scored significantly higher than Dutch students on autonomous motivation (p < 0.001) and deep strategy; no differences on controlled motivation, surface strategy, achieving strategy, GPA, or clerkship performance. [→ Isik U. 2018](#isik-u-2018)

## Evidence

### Isik U. 2018

Isik U., Wilschut J., Croiset G., Kusurkar R.A. (2018). The role of study strategy in motivation and academic performance of ethnic minority and majority students: a structural equation model. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-018-9840-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate ANOVA with Bonferroni-corrected post hoc comparisons across the three ethnic groups (N = 618, Table 2); printed means include AM 4.47 (non-Western) vs 4.25 (Dutch) and DS 3.35 vs 3.18. No effect size was printed.

> "The autonomous motivation of non-Western students was significantly higher than that of Dutch students (p < 0.001). The deep strategy of non-Western students was significantly higher than that of Dutch students."
