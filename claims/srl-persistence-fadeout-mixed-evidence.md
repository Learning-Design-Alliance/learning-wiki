`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing de Boer et al. (2018), that among 48 long-term studies there was "no evidence of fadeout for interventions targeting metacognitive strategy use"; effort-management interventions showed the largest effects and rehearsal-focused ones negative effects, based on small numbers of studies.

> "a meta-analysis (de Boer et al., 2018) of 48 long-term studies found no evidence of fadeout for interventions targeting metacognitive strategy use; in fact, effects on academic achievement appeared to increase over time."
