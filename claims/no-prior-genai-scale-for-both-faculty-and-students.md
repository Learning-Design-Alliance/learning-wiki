## Evidence

### Stella Y. Kim 2026

Stella Y. Kim, Ji Yae Bong, Daniel Maxwell, Beth Oyarzun. (2026). Development and validation of Generative Artificial Intelligence Readiness and Perception (GenAI-RP) Scale for faculty and students in higher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10713-z

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

This is the authors' literature-review assertion, not a tested result: the review reports that prior instruments such as Chan and Zhou's EVT-based scale and Luo and Zou's ChatGPT readiness scale used student-only samples, while Amani et al.'s dual-population survey reported no formal construct map or validity estimates.

> "To our knowledge, no measurement scale has been developed specifically for both instructors and students in the context of AI-enhanced education. The current literature tends to focus on either instructors (Akanzire et al., 2025; Zhang & Villanueva, 2023) or students (Lemke et al., 2023), rather than both."
