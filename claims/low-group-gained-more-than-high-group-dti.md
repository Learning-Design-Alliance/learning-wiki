## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The low pre-DTI group improved significantly with a high effect size (d = 1.09), as did the high group (d = 0.96), and the low group showed a larger raw gain (19.05 vs 10.06 points), but the between-group comparison did not reach significance. [→ Si Jihyun 2024](#si-jihyun-2024)
`q2 i2` Only the low group showed a significant increase in the FT subscale (d = 0.76). [→ Si Jihyun 2024 (2)](#si-jihyun-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Si Jihyun 2024

Si Jihyun. (2024). Fostering clinical reasoning ability in preclinical students through an illness script worksheet approach in flipped learning: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05614-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Dependent t-tests comparing pre- and post-DTI in the low group (n = 19) split by pre-DTI scores; the article reports "t (18) = 4.76, p < .001, d = 1.09, revealing a high level of effect size". The high group (n = 17) also improved significantly (t (16) = 3.97, p = .001, d = 0.96).

> "in the low group, there were significant differences in the total DTI score, t (18) = 4.76, p < .001, d = 1.09, revealing a high level of effect size"

### Si Jihyun 2024 (2)
