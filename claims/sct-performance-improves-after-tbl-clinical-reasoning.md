# Script concordance test performance rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in fourth-year medical students in one uncontrolled pre-post study of a team-based learning course on clinical symptoms

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Student performance on the SCT rose from pre-test to post-test in both randomly assigned test sets after the TBL educational intervention, in one uncontrolled pre-post study. [→ Ishizuka K 2025](#ishizuka-k-2025)

## Evidence

### Ishizuka K 2025

Ishizuka K, Shikino K, Takada N, et al. (2025). Enhancing clinical reasoning skills in medical students through team-based learning: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06784-w

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Before-and-after evaluation in a TBL course without a control group; 89 of 92 students had complete data. Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed significant SCT gains on both test sets, with effect sizes "r = 0.243" and "r = 0.211".

> "According to SCT results, student performance improved significantly after the educational intervention (A test: pre-test: 16.5 ± 4.4, post-test: 18.7 ± 4.5, p = 0.019, r = 0.243; B test: pre-test: 18.1 ± 3.7, post-test: 19.8 ± 4.4, p = 0.028, r = 0.211)"
