# Watching Arthur improves English narrative skill development in kindergarten English language learners more than an alternative educational program (potentially positive effects)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Kindergarten English language learners who watched Arthur showed greater improvement in narrative skill development than comparison-group students watching Between the Lions, with a WWC-calculated effect size of 0.44 on the combined narrative measure that was not statistically significant but substantively important. [→ Arthur (WWC Intervention Report) 2006](#arthur-wwc-intervention-report-2006)

## Evidence

### Arthur (WWC Intervention Report) 2006

Arthur (WWC Intervention Report). (2006). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://pbskids.org/arthur/index.html

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Randomized controlled trial in which 108 kindergarten Spanish-speaking English language learners were assigned to watch Arthur or Between the Lions; on the combined narrative measure the Arthur group's mean difference was 1.79 with effect size 0.44, rated ns. The report states the "average effect was large enough to be considered substantively important."

> "Uchikoshi reported that students watching Arthur showed greater improvement in nar-rative skill development than students in the comparison group. Although the individual and average effects (as calculated by the WWC) were not statistically significant, the average effect was large enough to be considered substantively important."
