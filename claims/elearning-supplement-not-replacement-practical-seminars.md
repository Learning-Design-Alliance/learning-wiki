# Students view e-learning as a useful supplement to, not a replacement for, practical seminars and lectures in local anaesthesia teaching

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The 25 students who used e-learning confirmed it was a useful supplement to the course (mean score = 2.56, SD = 2.53) but did not think it could replace the practical seminars (mean score = 9.32, SD = 1.46) or the lecture (mean score = 6.56, SD = 2.14). [→ Bock Anna 2021](#bock-anna-2021)

## Evidence

### Bock Anna 2021

Bock Anna, Kniha Kristian, Goloborodko Evgeny, Lemos Martin, Rittich Anne Barbara, Möhlhenrich Stephan Christian, Rafai Nicole, Hölzle Frank, Modabber Ali. (2021). Effectiveness of face-to-face, blended and e-learning in teaching the application of local anaesthesia: a randomised study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02569-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Evaluation of the e-learning programme by participants of both groups using it (n = 25), on a ten-point Likert scale. "Both groups confirmed that the e-learning is a useful supplement to the course" while rejecting replacement of the practical seminars.

> "Both groups confirmed that the e-learning is a useful supplement to the course (mean score = 2.56, SD = 2.53). They did not think that e-learning could replace the practical seminars (mean score = 9.32, SD = 1.46)."
