## Evidence

### Jere-Folotiya 2014

Jere-Folotiya, J., Chansa-Kabali, T., Munachaka, J. C., Sampa, F., Yalukanda, C., Westerholm, J., Richardson, U., Serpell, R., & Lyytinen, H. (2014). The effect of using a mobile literacy game to improve literacy levels of grade one students in Zambian schools. Education Tech Research Dev, 62, 417-436. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-014-9342-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA comparing control against individual intervention groups (C vs LG, LG&TG, LG&TIG, TIG, TG) on spelling posttest with pretest covariate. The Bonferroni-adjusted contrast C vs. LG+TIG was significant at p < .05 with mean difference 2.00; no effect size is printed.

> "Intervention conditions in which both the students and the teachers were exposed to the game produced the greatest effect on the decoding test."
