# Self-efficacy profiles differ in AI use for educational support (η² = .36), the highest-rated support type in the high profile

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` AI use for educational support differed significantly across profiles, F(2,99) = 27.84, p < .001, η2 = .36, with the High profile highest (M = 4.68) and the Low profile lowest (M = 2.41). [→ Suriá-Martínez R 2026](#suria-martinez-r-2026)

## Evidence

### Suriá-Martínez R 2026

Suriá-Martínez R, García-Castillo F, López-Sánchez C and García del Castillo JA (2026). Between perceived competence and artificial intelligence: academic self-efficacy profiles in university students with motor disabilities. Front. Educ. 11:1956192. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1956192

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

One of three one-way ANOVAs using self-efficacy profile as independent variable and the three AI support modalities as dependent variables (N = 102). The quote fragment "AI use for educational support" grounds this measure; means were 4.68 (High), 3.56 (Moderate), 2.41 (Low).

> "The ANOVA results confirmed statistically significant differences among profiles, F(2,99) = 27.84, p < .001, η2 = .36, indicating a significant association between academic self-efficacy profile and AI use for educational support."
