### Latino Francesca 2023

Latino Francesca, Tafuri Francesco, Saraiello Emma, Tafuri Domenico. (2023). Classroom-Based Physical Activity as a Means to Improve Self-Efficacy and Academic Achievement among Normal-Weight and Overweight Youth. Nutrients 15(9):2061. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15092061

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Two-way repeated-measures ANOVA in the same 100-student controlled trial on the Amos 8-15 objective study tests (text study plus title choice, open and true/false questions) showed a "meaningful “Time × Group” interaction" with η2p = 0.69; post hoc t = 10.26, d = 1.45.

> "The results of a two-factor repeated measure ANOVA showed meaningful “Time × Group” interaction for objective study tests (F1,98 = 220.36, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.69, large effect size)."
