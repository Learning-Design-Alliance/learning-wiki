### transfer-funnel (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Interview finding from the same field study: participants P09 and P10 wanted saved problems organized into a knowledge-map wrong-book for weekend spaced retrieval, which telemetry on selective saving corroborated.

> "Students like KX (P09) and TYK (P10) still wanted AITutor to organize these problems into a knowledge-map "wrong-book" for delayed retrieval practice during weekend reviews."
