## Evidence

### García-Crespo 2021

García-Crespo, F. J., Fernández-Alonso, R., & Muñiz, J. (2021). Academic resilience in European countries: The role of teachers, families, and student profiles. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253409

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Second-iteration per-country multilevel logistic models of PIRLS 2016 data. The article reports resilient students with "a strong sense of belonging to the school" had "up to 40 percentage points higher" chances, with the sense-of-belonging coefficient significant in 52.2% of countries (Table 7). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Students who had a strong sense of belonging to the school they attended generally had a better chance of being resilient than those who did not, up to 40 percentage points higher, as in Ireland."
