## Evidence

### Ebadi and Pourzandi 2015

Saman Ebadi, Mahsa Pourzandi. (2015). The Effects of Explicit and Implicit Pragmatic Instruction on the Development of Compliments and Compliment Responses. li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 5, No. 4, October - December. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=The+Effects+of+Explicit+and+Implicit+Pragmatic+Instruction+on+the+Development+of+Compliments+and+Compliment+Responses

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study of 56 intermediate EFL learners in three intact classes at a language center; two groups received three weeks of explicit or implicit instruction on Cs and CRs and one received none. The article reports a statistically significant improvement in both instructed groups with no change in the control group; no effect size is printed.

> "The results of the study evidenced that both implicit and explicit instruction groups' responses significantly improved while the responding patterns of control group without any instruction did not change."
