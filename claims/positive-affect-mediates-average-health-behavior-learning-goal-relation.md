# Positive affect mediates the relation of average sleep quality and average physical activity with learning goal achievement; negative affect does not

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Average positive affect partially mediated the sleep quality–learning goal relation (26%) and mediated the physical activity relation (88%), while negative affect did not significantly mediate either. [→ Flueckiger Lavinia 2014](#flueckiger-lavinia-2014)

## Evidence

### Flueckiger Lavinia 2014

Flueckiger Lavinia, Lieb Roselind, Meyer Andrea H., Mata Jutta. (2014). How Health Behaviors Relate to Academic Performance via Affect: An Intensive Longitudinal Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0111080

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel mediation models on 32 days of data from 72 students. Positive affect mediated 26% of the sleep-quality total effect and "88% of the total effect between average physical activity and learning goal achievement"; negative affect mediation was not significant for either predictor.

> "Positive affect mediated 88% of the total effect between average physical activity and learning goal achievement. Negative affect did not mediate the relation between physical activity and learning goal achievement."
