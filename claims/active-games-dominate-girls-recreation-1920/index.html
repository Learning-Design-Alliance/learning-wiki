Evidence¶

Wheeler 1920¶

Wheeler, T. (1920). A Study of Certain Recreational Reading and Vocational Phases in the Lives of Young Girls. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. XI, No. 9. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0064614

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive analysis of informal interviews with eight hundred girls aged six to twenty, grouped by age. The author reports that active games and sports "constitute at least half the total number of preferences" across all ages, exceeding half among girls under thirteen. No effect size is reported.

"Thoughout the entire age range they constitute at least half the total number of preferences, and with the girls under thirteen years, considerably more than that."
