Marvin Roski, Ratan Sebastian, Ralph Ewerth, Anett Hoppe, Andreas Nehring. (2024). Learning analytics and the Universal Design for Learning (UDL): A clustering approach. Computers & Education 214 (2024) 105028. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105028

Descriptive comparison of z-standardized pre/post-test and survey variables across the six clusters (Fig. 6). The article reports apparent differences in self-concept and interest between Clusters 2 and 5, higher reading ability in Cluster 0, and below-average post-test scores in Cluster 1; no inferential statistics are printed.

"It is evident, for example, that Cluster 2 and 5 have apparent differences in their self-concept and interest in chemistry (Fig. 6a and b). In addition, Cluster 0, for example, stands out from the others regarding reading ability (Fig. 6d), and Cluster 1 shows below-average post-test scores (Fig. 6h)."
