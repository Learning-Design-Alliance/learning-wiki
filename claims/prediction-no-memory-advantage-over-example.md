## Evidence

### Garvin Brod and Jasmin Breitwieser 2019

Garvin Brod and Jasmin Breitwieser. (2019). Lighting the wick in the candle of learning: generating a prediction stimulates curiosity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0056-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered two-tailed paired t test in the 29-participant within-subjects experiment testing overall memory accuracy between conditions; the article reports no significant difference and equivalence was not tested. Participants reported connecting facts with prior knowledge more often in the example condition.

> "Memory performance did not differ signiﬁcantly between the two conditions, however (prediction: M = 60.42 and SD = 15.81; example: M = 59.52, SD = 16.78), t(28) = 0.41, p = 0.682, and d = 0.077"
