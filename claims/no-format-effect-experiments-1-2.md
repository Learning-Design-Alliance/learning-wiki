# Initial retrieval practice format (short-answer, multiple-choice, hybrid-massed, hybrid-spaced) produced little or no difference in delayed meaningful learning in Experiments 1 and 2 and in a combined analysis

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In Experiment 1, a 4 (retrieval format) × 2 (question type) ANOVA on the four retrieval groups showed no main effect of retrieval format on final performance. [→ Smith 2014](#smith-2014)
`q3 i?` In Experiment 2, there was no main effect of retrieval format on final performance. [→ Smith 2014 (2)](#smith-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Smith 2014

Megan A. Smith and Jeffrey D. Karpicke. (2014). Retrieval practice with short-answer, multiple-choice, and hybrid tests. Memory, 22(7), 784-802. https://doi.org/10.1080/09658211.2013.831454

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 final-assessment ANOVA across short-answer, multiple-choice, hybrid-massed, and hybrid-spaced conditions found "no main effect of retrieval format (F B1)"; the format of initial retrieval practice did not affect meaningful learning.

> "A 4 (retrieval format)2 (question type) ANOVA was performed on the four groups that practised retrieval. There was no main effect of retrieval format (F B1); the format of initial retrieval practice did not have an effect on meaningful learning."

### Smith 2014 (2)

Megan A. Smith and Jeffrey D. Karpicke. (2014). Retrieval practice with short-answer, multiple-choice, and hybrid tests. Memory, 22(7), 784-802. https://doi.org/10.1080/09658211.2013.831454

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 final-assessment ANOVA on the four retrieval groups found no main effect of retrieval format, F(3, 76) = 1.59, p = .20, and no interaction; format did not matter for learning.

> "There was no main effect of retrieval format, F(3, 76) 1.59, p .20, or question type, F(1, 76) 1.88, p .17, and no interaction, F(1, 76) 1.08, p .36."

### Smith 2014 (3)

Megan A. Smith and Jeffrey D. Karpicke. (2014). Retrieval practice with short-answer, multiple-choice, and hybrid tests. Memory, 22(7), 784-802. https://doi.org/10.1080/09658211.2013.831454

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Combined analysis across Experiments 1 and 2 (144 subjects, 36 per retrieval condition) found "no differences among the four retrieval practice conditions (F B1)", even with the increased power of the pooled sample.

> "A 4 (retrieval format) 2 (question type) ANOVA showed that there were no differences among the four retrieval practice conditions (F B1)."
