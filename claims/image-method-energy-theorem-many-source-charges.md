## Evidence

### M.M. Taddei 2009

M.M. Taddei, T.N.C. Mendes, C. Farina. (2009). Subtleties on energy calculations in the image method. https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4497 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0903.4497](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0903.4497)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Second theorem proved analytically in Section 4.2 by the same surface-term argument. The article interprets eq.(20) pairwise: real-real pairs enter integrally, real-image pairs take a factor one half, and pairs of image charges are absent from the expression.

> "The electrostatic energy of a set of M point charges q1, q2, ..., qM near N perfect conductors of arbitrary shapes, each conductor bei ng either neutral or grounded, is the Coulombic interaction energy between th e real point charges plus half the sum, from i = 1 to i = M, of the Coulombic energies between charge qi and each image charge."
