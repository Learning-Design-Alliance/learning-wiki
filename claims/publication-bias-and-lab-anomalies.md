## Evidence

### Gobet Fernand 2022

Gobet Fernand, Sala Giovanni. (2022). Cognitive Training: A Field in Search of a Phenomenon. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/17456916221091830

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Estimate from the second-order meta-analysis of cognitive-training data; publication bias is characterized as minor relative to other artifacts.

> "In our second-order meta-analysis, we estimated a small publication-bias effect (0.05–0.10 standardized mean differences). Publication bias thus seems to be a minor issue in the cognitive-training literature."

### Gobet Fernand 2022 (2)

Gobet Fernand, Sala Giovanni. (2022). Cognitive Training: A Field in Search of a Phenomenon. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/17456916221091830

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meta-analytic identification of two anomalous laboratories: the Padua laboratory's CWMS regimen used biographical-questionnaire filler controls rather than cognitively demanding active controls, and Green and Bavelier's video-game studies showed distributional asymmetry attributed to suppressed or misreported effect sizes.

> "The effect sizes reported by these laboratories, which are unusually large compared with those found by other laboratories, are a nonnegligible source of variability in the cognitive-training literature"
