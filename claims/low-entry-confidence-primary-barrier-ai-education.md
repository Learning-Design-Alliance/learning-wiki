### Valentina Kuskova 2026

Valentina Kuskova, Dmitry Zaytsev, and Richard Patrick Johnson. (2026). Learning AI Without a STEM Background: Mixed-Methods Evidence from a Diverse, Mixed-Cohort AIED Program. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29788-4_36](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29788-4_36)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Baseline survey of the first Data Crossings cohort (12 undergraduates, 7 adult learners) administered at program entry; Likert-style items with open-ended prompts. The authors interpret that "confidence, rather than motivation, represents a primary barrier." No effect size is reported.

> "Early survey data indicate that participants entered the program with low to moderate confidence in their ability to engage with AI concepts, despite high levels of interest. Many respondents reported discomfort interpreting AI outputs or assessing system limitations, reinforcing the notion that confidence, rather than motivation, represents a primary barrier to participation in AI education."
