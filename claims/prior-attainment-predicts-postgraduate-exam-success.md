## Evidence

### Ellis Ricky 2025

Ellis Ricky, Knapton Andy, Cannon Jane, Lee Amanda J., Cleland Jennifer. (2025). Differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical examinations: examining the relationship between sociodemographic differences and examination performance. BMC Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-025-04034-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

All-candidates multivariate logistic regression on UKMED data; prior attainment was entered as continuous z-scores relative to each examination's pass mark, and remained significant in UKG-only models (OR 1.35 written; OR 1.12 clinical).

> "As per previous studies, prior academic attainment (individual performance on the PLAB or UCAS tariff) remained a predictor of future success at medical written and clinical examinations (OR 1.39 (95% CI 1.37 to 1.42) and OR 1.18 (95% CI 1.14 to 1.21), respectively)."
