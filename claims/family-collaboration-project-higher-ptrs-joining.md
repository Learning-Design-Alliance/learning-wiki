## Evidence

### Accardo 2020

Accardo, A. L., Xin, J. F., & Shuff, M. (2020). Special Education Teacher Preparation and Family Collaboration. School Community Journal, 30(2). http://www.schoolcommunitynetwork.org/SCJ.aspx

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 77 teacher candidates across two semesters using the Parent-Teacher Relationship Scale as a pre-post survey. The intervention group completed the Family Collaboration Project while the comparison group completed the traditional Pupil Impact Assignment. The article reports "a significant difference (F = 8.69, p < .05)" on the joining subscale.

> "Results also show that there was a significant difference (F = 8.69, p < .05) between the two groups on post survey scores of Factor 1: joining."
