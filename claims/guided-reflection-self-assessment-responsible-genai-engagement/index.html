Pedlow 2026¶

Pedlow, M., & Maldon, J. (2026). Raising Ethical Awareness of GenAI Use Through Student Self-Assessment in the Transition to Higher Education. Journal of University Teaching and Learning Practice. https://doi.org/10.53761/39ey1895

q2 · i? · design · r2

Study-level finding from pre- and post-semester surveys of commencing university students, with open-text reflections thematically analysed; the article states the approach "can support students to engage responsibly and confidently with GenAI".

"This study investigates how guided reflection embedded within self‑assessment can support students to engage responsibly and confidently with GenAI in learning and assessment, while maintaining academic integrity."
