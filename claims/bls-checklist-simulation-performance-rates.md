Tobase L, Peres HHC, Gianotto-Oliveira R, Smith N, Polastri TF, Timerman S. (2017). The effects of an online basic life support course on undergraduate nursing students' learning. Int J Med Educ 8:309-313. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5985.cbce

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Practical simulation test in pairs, scored by two instructors with a printed checklist and a simulator with feedback devices; data were analysable for 52 participants. The article prints per-step percentages and a practical mean of 9.1 ± 0.95.

> "following 90% of the participants checked for responsiveness, 97% checked for breathing, 76% activated the emergency response system, 92% requested for a defibrillator, 77% checked for a carotid pulse, 87% used proper hand placement, 95% performed 30 compressions per cycle"
