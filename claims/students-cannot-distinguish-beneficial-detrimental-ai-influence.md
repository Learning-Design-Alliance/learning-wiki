In the pilot study of 185 students, "Pairwise comparisons using Mann-Whitney U tests showed that both students whose diagnostic accuracy improved after AI use and those whose accuracy worsened rated AI as significantly more influential than students whose diagnostic rankings did not change."

> "Pairwise comparisons using Mann-Whitney U tests showed that both students whose diagnostic accuracy improved after AI use and those whose accuracy worsened rated AI as significantly more influential than students whose diagnostic rankings did not change (Z = -2.74, P = .006 and Z = -3.23, P = .001, respectively)"
