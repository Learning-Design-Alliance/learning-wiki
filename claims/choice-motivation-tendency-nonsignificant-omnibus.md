# Choice shows a non-significant tendency toward higher motivation, with a significant pairwise difference only under uncorrected testing

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The motivation-score omnibus effect of choice was not significant, though a tendency appeared and the ZCO-Predef pairwise difference was significant only under LSD, not Bonferroni. [→ Clément 2024](#clement-2024)

## Evidence

### Clément 2024

Clément, B., Sauzéon, H., Roy, D., & Oudeyer, P.-Y. (2024). Improved Performances and Motivation in Intelligent Tutoring Systems: Combining Machine Learning and Learner Choice. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.01669](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.01669)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on Vallerand's motivation score in the 265-child RCT; pairwise comparison between ZCO and Predef significant by LSD (p − value = 0.034) but not Bonferroni (p − value = 0.205); marginal means ZCO 15.353 vs Predef 13.726.

> "There is no significant effect but we can observe a tendency showing a difference between students who have choices and student without choice (Choice, [F (1, 261) = 3.449, p − value = 0.064, η2 = 0.013])."
