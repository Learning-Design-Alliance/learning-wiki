### Holman 2024

Holman, Kenneth. (2024). Exploring Fraction Comprehension and Interest in Elementary Education Through AI-Powered Personalized Learning. https://stars.library.ucf.edu/etd2024/32

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental repeated-measures study of 22 fourth and fifth graders from a suburban school in the southeastern United States, comparing a Mathbot group to a business-as-usual control over five consecutive school days. The article reports that "the BAU group slightly outperformed Mathbot users in post-test assessments." No effect size is printed.

> "The results indicated that while both groups demonstrated gains in fraction comprehension, the BAU group slightly outperformed Mathbot users in post-test assessments."
