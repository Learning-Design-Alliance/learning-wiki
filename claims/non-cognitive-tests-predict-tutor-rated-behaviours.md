## Evidence

### Adam Jane 2012

Adam Jane, Bore Miles, McKendree Jean, Munro Don, Powis David. (2012). Can personal qualities of medical students predict in-course examination success and professional behaviour? An exploratory prospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-69

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective cohort of the entire 2007 entry cohort (146 students; 137 completed all three non-cognitive tests, 122 also had UKCAT scores). Pearson correlations showed "statistically significant relationships with ratings of behaviour made by tutors"; only p-values were printed, no effect sizes.

> "Personal qualities measured by non-cognitive tests showed a number of statistically significant relationships with ratings of behaviour made by tutors, with performance in each year’s objective structured clinical examinations (OSCEs), and with themed written summative examination marks in each year."
