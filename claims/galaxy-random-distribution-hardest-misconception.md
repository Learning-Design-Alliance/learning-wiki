## Evidence

### Andrej Favia 2013

Andrej Favia, Neil F. Comins, Geoffrey L. Thorpe. (2013). The Elements of Item Response Theory and its Framework in Analyzing Introductory Astronomy College Student Misconceptions. I. Galaxies. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1206.2302](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1206.2302)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

IRT analysis with the graded response model (MULTILOG) of recoded AMI responses from 403 college students shows sA226 is “the hardest of all the galaxy statements,” with high retention even among low-endorsement students. No effect size is printed.

> "We observe that this item represents the hardest of all the galaxy statements, because even those students who are generally low on endorsing misconceptions are likely to respond with “2” or “3,” indicating high retention of the misconception."
