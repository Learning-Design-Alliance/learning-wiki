## Evidence

### Ömer Sinan Can 2016

Ömer Sinan Can, Tevfik İşleyen. (2016). Teaching Probability to Pre-Service Teachers with Argumentation Based Science Learning Approach. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.33. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1122873

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test on the experimental group's (n=20) pre-test and post-test Probability Achievement scores, used because pre-test scores were not normally distributed. No effect size printed.

> "statistical significant di fferences were found between the pre-test and post-test scores of the experimental group (z=-3.923 , p=.000<.050). As a result of the teaching probability with the Argumentation Based Science Learning Approach, th e pre- service teachers’ achievements on the subject of probability increased"

### Ömer Sinan Can 2016 (2)

Ömer Sinan Can, Tevfik İşleyen. (2016). Teaching Probability to Pre-Service Teachers with Argumentation Based Science Learning Approach. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.33. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1122873

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test on the control group's (n=21) pre-test and post-test scores after traditional-method instruction. No effect size printed.

> "statistical significant differences were found between the Probability Achievement pre-test and post-test scores of the control group (z=- 4.021, p=.000<.050). As a result of the teaching probability with the traditional method, the achievements of all the  pre-service teachers on the subject of probability increased"
