## Evidence

### Orprayoon 2014

Orprayoon, S. (2014). Effects of Cooperative Learning on Learning Achievement and Group Working Behavior of Junior Students in Modern French Literature Course. The Journal of Effective Teaching, Vol. 14, No.1, 80-98. https://uncw.edu/jet

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Opinions toward cooperative learning were surveyed with a questionnaire analyzed by frequency on a Five-point Likert Scale after the treatment. Descriptive group means are printed; no inferential test is reported.

> "the first group was satisfied by the method at the highest level (The mean is 4.76), while the second and the third groups were satisfied by the method at a high level (The mean is 4.44 and 4.38, respectively)."

### Orprayoon 2014 (2)

Orprayoon, S. (2014). Effects of Cooperative Learning on Learning Achievement and Group Working Behavior of Junior Students in Modern French Literature Course. The Journal of Effective Teaching, Vol. 14, No.1, 80-98. https://uncw.edu/jet

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item rankings aggregated across the three groups from the opinion questionnaire; the top-ranked items concerned benefit, critical thinking development, and suitability of the technique. Means are descriptive only.

> "They pointed out that, firstly, LT technique was the most beneficial especially when it helped them “learn the real values of various things through cooperative learning” (The mean of the three groups is 4.67). Secondly, they found LT technique “encouraged their development of critical thinking skills” (The mean of the three groups is 4.58)."
