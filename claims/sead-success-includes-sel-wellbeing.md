## Evidence

### Duchesneau 2020

Duchesneau, N. (2020). Social, Emotional, and Academic Development Through an Equity Lens (#ReEnvisionSEL). The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/resource/social-emotional-and-academic-development-through-an-equity-lens/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative focus groups with 70 students and 70 family members of color across eight locations in five states. Participants spoke more frequently about social and emotional components to success than financial or career outcomes, envisioning "empathy, respect, positive relationships, giving back to the community."

> "When asked what success is, participants consistently said that it's more than meeting academic standards and getting a well-paying job. A number of students and family members said happiness and well-being are more critical to success than academic or career outcomes"
