## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Interactive tutorial to improve student understanding of single photon experiments involving a Mach-Zehnder Interferometer. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/37/2/024001](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/37/2/024001)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pretest question 2 in upper-level undergraduate and Ph.D. quantum mechanics courses presented a student conversation contrasting a split-photon model with a must-take-one-path model. The study reports "43% of the undergraduate students and 36% of the Ph.D. students incorrectly agreed" with the must-take-one-path statement; 32% (UG) and 24% (G) agreed the photon splits.

> "Furthermore, 43% of the undergraduate students and 36% of the Ph.D. students  incorrectly agreed with Student 2 in Question 2 on the pretest claiming that a p hoton must take either the U or L path."
