## Evidence

### Dexin Lu 2007

Dexin Lu, Dong Ruan, Wang Xu, Nianle Wu, Minwen Xiao, Yu An. (2007). An Experiment of Research-Oriented Teaching/Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.2042 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0801.2042](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0801.2042)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Program self-report of a renewed evaluation operation procedure introduced in 2006, including an appeal item and severe plagiarism deductions. The printed figures are two weeks versus three days for "256 papers" and "29.2%" scorer coincidence; no statistical test is reported.

> "At first five TAs and three instructors took two weeks to complete the evaluation of 256 papers. Using the new system, in next semester we took three days finished the same work. TA group and instructor group offered quite similar scores of as 29.2% as high coincidence."
