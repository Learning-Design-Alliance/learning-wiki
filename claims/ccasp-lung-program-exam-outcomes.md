Descriptive analysis of exam records from the single lung cancer cohort (CAS LU1) from ESO archives. Grading categories showed participants scored Very Good, Good, and Satisfactory in 56%, 31%, and 13% respectively, and "All participants passed the final certification exam."

> "The mean percentage pass of the four-module tests was 81% (range 79–82%), and that of the certification exam was 100%"
