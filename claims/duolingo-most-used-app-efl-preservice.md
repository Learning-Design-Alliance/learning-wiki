`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from an online survey of 206 EFL pre-service teachers at Polish and Turkish state universities reporting application usage frequencies; "Duolingo was the most widely used app [79.1%]" and Wext had the lowest usage rate [23.8%].

> "In total, fourteen applications were examined, and among these, Duolingo was the most widely used app [79.1%]. Duolingo was followed by Quizlet and Cambly, with usage rates of 76.7 and 68.4%, respectively."
