`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument by the author reviewing the history of the field since the 1970s, when behavioral and cognitive-behavioral perspectives dominated. He describes the shift as the "Copernican turn" of putting the learner at the center; no empirical data are offered for this historical claim.

> "Today, the focus across many of our most prominent motivation theories, including those represented in this SI, is on promoting student engagement, interest, agency, and feeling of competence."
