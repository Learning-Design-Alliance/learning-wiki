# Identification and categorization of performance problems and components improve significantly across academic years

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Identification of performance problems in IADL and social participation increased significantly between AY1 and AY4 (p = 0.005), and leisure identification increased constantly across education (p = 0.001). [→ Moruno-Miralles P. 2020](#moruno-miralles-p-2020)
`q3 i?` Identification of performance components (signs and symptoms, performance patterns, environment) increased significantly between first and fourth years (p = 0.001). [→ Moruno-Miralles P. 2020 (2)](#moruno-miralles-p-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Moruno-Miralles P. 2020

Moruno-Miralles P., Reyes-Torres A., Talavera-Valverde M.-Á., Souto-Gómez A.-I., Márquez-Álvarez L.-J. (2020). Learning and Development of Diagnostic Reasoning in Occupational Therapy Undergraduate Students. Occupational Therapy International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/6934579

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparisons among academic years (AY1 n = 96, AY3 n = 85, AY4 n = 66) in the cross-sectional multicentre study; Table 6 also shows leisure identification rising from 1% (AY1) to 84.8% (AY4) with p = 0.001 across all comparisons.

> "there was a significant increase in the identification of performance problems in the IADL and social participation areas (p = 0.005) between academic years (hereafter, AY) 1 and 4."

### Moruno-Miralles P. 2020 (2)

Moruno-Miralles P., Reyes-Torres A., Talavera-Valverde M.-Á., Souto-Gómez A.-I., Márquez-Álvarez L.-J. (2020). Learning and Development of Diagnostic Reasoning in Occupational Therapy Undergraduate Students. Occupational Therapy International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/6934579

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-year chi-square comparisons (Table 6) in the same study; comparing AY1 and AY3 also showed significant increases in categorization of symptoms/signs (p = 0.004) and performance patterns (p = 0.001).

> "An increase in the identification of the performance components was also identified, specifically in the signs and symptoms, in the performance patterns, and in the environment (p = 0.001) when comparing the first and fourth academic years."
