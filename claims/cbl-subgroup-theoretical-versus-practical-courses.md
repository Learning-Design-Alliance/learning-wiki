### Dong Haitao 2022

Dong Haitao, Guo Chunlan, Zhou Lian, Zhao Jizhi, Wu Xiaomin, Zhang Xinyuan, Zhang Xin. (2022). Effectiveness of case-based learning in Chinese dental education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-048497

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis reported in Table 2 of the meta-analysis. Theoretical courses showed larger pooled effects (e.g. knowledge SMD 1.78, 14 studies) than practical courses (knowledge SMD 1.1, 7 studies); skill-score heterogeneity was non-significant in theoretical courses. No single effect size is printed for the overall subgroup contrast.

> "The outcome revealed that there were significant differences in the effects of CBL on knowledge scores, skill scores and comprehensive ability scores when comparing theoretical and practical courses."
