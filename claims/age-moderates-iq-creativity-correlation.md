# Age moderates the IQ–creativity correlation: elementary-school correlations (r = .086) are significantly lower than those for middle school, high school, and adult groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The elementary school group's mean correlation (r = .086) was significantly lower than the middle school (r = .210), high school (r = .261), and adult (r = .205) groups, and age was a significant independent moderator in weighted regression (β = .03917, p < .0001). [→ Kim 2005](#kim-2005)

## Evidence

### Kim 2005

Kim, K. H. (2005). Can Only Intelligent People Be Creative? A Meta-Analysis. The Journal of Secondary Gifted Education, XVI(2/3), 57-66. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Can+Only+Intelligent+People+Be+Creative

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis across 251 elementary, 27 middle school, 105 high school, and 53 adult correlation coefficients. Elementary correlations were significantly lower than all older groups; in the weighted regression age had a significant independent effect (β = .03917, z = 6.19332, p < .0001).

> "the elementary school group (r = .086) and the high school group (r = .261) were statistically different, χ2 = 110.148, p < .0001. The elementary school group (r = .086) and adults (r = .205) were statistically different, χ2 = 126.945, p < .0001."
