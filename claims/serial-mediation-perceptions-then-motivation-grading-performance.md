# Learning environment perceptions and intrinsic motivation serially mediated the relationship between grading system and academic performance, while neither mediated it alone

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The indirect effect of grading system on academic performance through learning environment perceptions then intrinsic motivation was significant (effect 0.295, 95% bootstrapped CI 0.021 to 0.665). [→ Kim Min-Kyeong 2024](#kim-min-kyeong-2024)
`q2 i0` Neither learning environment perceptions alone nor intrinsic motivation alone showed a significant indirect effect between grading system and performance. [→ Kim Min-Kyeong 2024 (2)](#kim-min-kyeong-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Kim Min-Kyeong 2024

Kim Min-Kyeong, Kim Hae Won. (2024). Impact of a change from A–F grading to honors/pass/fail grading on academic performance at Yonsei University College of Medicine in Korea: a cross-sectional serial mediation analysis. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2024.21.20

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Serial mediation analysis (PROCESS macro model 6, 95% bootstrapped CI with 10,000 resamplings, gender and entrance type controlled) in 238 students. The serial pathway was significant; the article reports "effect, 0.295; 95% bootstrapped CI, 0.021 to 0.665".

> "Learning environment perceptions and intrinsic motivation sequentially mediated the association between the grading system and academic performance (effect, 0.295; 95% bootstrapped CI, 0.021 to 0.665)."

### Kim Min-Kyeong 2024 (2)

Kim Min-Kyeong, Kim Hae Won. (2024). Impact of a change from A–F grading to honors/pass/fail grading on academic performance at Yonsei University College of Medicine in Korea: a cross-sectional serial mediation analysis. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2024.21.20

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Same bootstrapped mediation analysis: the two simple-mediation pathways had confidence intervals containing zero (0.136, CI -0.492 to 0.733; 0.395, CI -0.012 to 1.046), so "there were no significant indirect effects" through either mediator alone.

> "However, there were no significant indirect effects when only learning environment perceptions or intrinsic motivation was used as a mediator (effect, 0.136; 95% bootstrapped CI, -0.492 to 0.733; effect, 0.395; 95% bootstrapped CI, -0.012 to 1.046, respectively)."
