### Ambe Peter C. 2016

Ambe Peter C., Wassenberg Dirk R. (2016). Is sutureless thyroid surgery safe in the hands of surgical trainees. A single centre retrospective study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-016-1940-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results-section comparison of mean tissue weight in the retrospective series: consultants' cases were heavier (47.6 ± 51.9 g) than trainees' (36.9 ± 21.3 g), significant at p = 0.03. No effect size was printed.
