## Evidence

### Miller‐Cotto 2026

Miller‐Cotto, D., & Medrano, J. (2026). Does working memory moderate the effect of fading on math performance? British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 108–126. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12781

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pooled linear regression on the randomized homework study (N = 114 assigned, n = 73 completing), posttest regressed on pretest and condition dummies with BAU as reference; the fading condition outperformed BAU (β = .60, p = .03) while "there were no significant differences between the BAU condition and the worked example or fading with self‐explanation conditions (ps > .05)."

> "Pretest scores were a significant predictor of posttest performance (β = .72, p < .001), and students in the fading condition demonstrated significantly higher posttest scores compared with the BAU condition (β = .60, p = .03)."
