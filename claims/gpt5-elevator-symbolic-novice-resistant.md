### Gerd Kortemeyer 2026 (2)

Gerd Kortemeyer. (2026). The Boiling-Frog Problem of Physics Education. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1119/5.0296601](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0296601)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prompting probe in which requests to act like an undergraduate changed only the register of exposition; the generated solutions "remain algebra-first and verification-oriented rather than plug-and-chug."

> "GPT-5 Thinking appears robust to prompts intended to elicit novice-like behavior: requests such as "act like an undergraduate student" have little effect on the problem-solving approach, beyond shifting the exposition toward a more casual, Gen-Z–tinged register."
