### Jaya Shivangani Kashyap and Chandralekha Singh 2025

Jaya Shivangani Kashyap and Chandralekha Singh. (2025). Strategies educators can use to counter misinformation related to the quantum information revolution. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/adbeb1](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/adbeb1)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured think-aloud interviews with five college QIST educators (two with undergraduate and post-graduate teaching, three undergraduate; none conducting physics education research) elicited their views on misinformation. The article reports the educators discussed misinformation such as "quantum teleportation being like Star Trek" and entanglement implying faster-than-light communication.

> "These educators discussed misinformation, e.g., about quantum entanglement implying that we can do faster than light communication, quantum teleportation being like Star Trek (material instead of quantum information being teleported from one place to another), and quantum computers being several classical computers running in parallel"
