### Kızılırmak Burcu 2025

Kızılırmak Burcu, Gözen Mehmet, Güntekin Neslihan. (2025). Evaluation of dental students’ tooth preparation skills using conventional phantom labs versus advanced simulation technologies: a comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08020-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of the randomized trial's post hoc results. Table 2 shows simulation post-test means of 6.91 for taper and 7.08 for finish line versus conventional 6.26 and 5.94; no per-criterion effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The post-test scores of the simulation group were also found to be significantly higher than both their own pre-test scores and the post-test scores of the conventional group (Table 3). These findings show that digital feedback systems increase accuracy not only in a single parameter of tooth preparation but in many dimensions."
