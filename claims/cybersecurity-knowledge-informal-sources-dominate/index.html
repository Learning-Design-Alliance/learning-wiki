Evidence¶

Adama 2026¶

Adama, V., Biddle, R., Arachchilage, N., & Lottridge, D. (2026). Confident yet Concerned: Inconsistencies in Computing Students' Attitudes on Cybersecurity. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18541

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey distribution question on knowledge sources among 236 computing students, reported with Figure 4. The article states "The most common sources of knowledge were word of mouth, social media, and financial institutions, with 51%, 45%, and 44% respectively"; Reddit was the most-mentioned social media source.

"The most common sources of knowledge were word of mouth, social media, and financial institutions, with 51%, 45%, and 44% respectively (Figure 4)."

Same survey's source-of-knowledge distributions. The article notes "academic institutions were not the primary source of their knowledge. Only 34% indicated these were a source of knowledge", with news outlets (27%), online advertisements (21%), and workplaces (19%) also low.

"Notably, academic institutions were not the primary source of their knowledge. Only 34% indicated these were a source of knowledge."
