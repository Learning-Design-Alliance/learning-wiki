## Evidence

### Manetti Previero Bruno 2026

Manetti Previero Bruno, Spataru Ana, Pinto Ruxandra, Piquette Dominique. (2026). Rapid cycle deliberate practice: a complement to clinical exposure in critical care education. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.1093/atsscholar/aapaf003

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Between-group comparisons in the observational study of 23 assessed learners using Fisher's exact test for percentages and Wilcoxon rank-sum for scores. The article reports no difference in achievement by specialty, training level, or sessions attended; mean scores also did not differ (Appendix S8).

> "There was no difference in the proportion of learners who achieved a performance score of ≥80% based on speciality (P = .54), training level (P = .70), or number of previous RCDP simulations attended (P = .56)."
