## Evidence

### Marcos D. Caballero and Steven J. Pollock 2013

Marcos D. Caballero and Steven J. Pollock. (2013). Assessing Student Learning in Middle-Division Classical Mechanics/Math Methods. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.008](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.008)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Post-test administration of the latest CCMI version at CU over three semesters with N=167 students; score distributions were normal or nearly so with similar variances, justifying pooling. The article reports the pooled mean of "52.9 ± 1.6 %" and that a small graduate-student comparison sample (N=5) averaged 74.5 ± 3.4%.

> "For the pooled data, the mean score over all three semesters was 52.9 ± 1.6 %. The CCMI is a challenging test, and the grading rubric is strict. However, CM 1 students earned a wide range of scores (Fig. 2); some outperformed a sample of ﬁrst-year graduate students at CU (avg. 74.5 ± 3.4 %, N=5)."
