### Josephine C. Meyer 2026

Josephine C. Meyer, Steven J. Pollock, Bethany R. Wilcox, and Gina Passante. (2026). Computer-generated QIS tutorial feedback is valued by students, but does not replicate in-class collaboration. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.15444](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.15444)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Self-report feedback from seven think-aloud interviews in an upper-division quantum computing course, spring 2026, with students quasi-randomly assigned to solo AcePhysics or group paper conditions. The article reports "overwhelmingly positive for both modalities" and that interviewees expressed a desire for similar homework activities.

> "While students had suggestions to improve the UI (e.g. adding an equation editor), overall impressions of the tutorial experience were overwhelmingly positive for both modalities."
