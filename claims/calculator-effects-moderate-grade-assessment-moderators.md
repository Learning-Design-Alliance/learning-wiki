### Young 2016

Young, J.R. (2016). Unpacking TPACK in Mathematics education research: A systematic review of meta-analyses. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 2(1), 19-29. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.2.1.19

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's synthesis of calculator meta-analyses (Ellington 2006, ES .30; Nikolaou 2001, ES .50; Tokpah 2008, ES .38). The article states calculator effect sizes "tend to converge at the moderate level of effectiveness" against Cohen's benchmarks, and that "The statistically significant moderators of calculator effec ts on mathematics achievement established in the literature are grade level and assessment."

> "The effect sizes for calculator use tend to converge at the moderate level of effectiveness, based on effect size benchmarks proposed by Cohen (1992): .20 (small), .50 (medium), and (.80) large."
