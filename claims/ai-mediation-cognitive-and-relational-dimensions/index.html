q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Inductive thematic analysis of co-design workshop transcripts and written artifacts from 12 university students in four interdisciplinary groups who first completed a 15-minute Discord probe session; the analysis yielded two overarching themes, one of which was that beyond cognitive organization, participants "valuedSpritz for softening the interpersonal pressure surrounding disagreement". No effect sizes are reported.

Related Claims¶

  • Participants perceived the AI probe as a neutral mediator whose private prompting surfaced implicit reasoning while public reorganization made disciplinary positions easier to compare — a narrower finding that bears on this claim

  • Participants valued the AI as a relational buffer that softened interpersonal pressure when disagreement required concession, without expecting it to resolve conflict — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
