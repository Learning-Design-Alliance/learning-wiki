## Evidence

### Phillips SC 2019

Phillips SC, Hawley CE, Triantafylidis LK, Schwartz AW. (2019). Geriatrics 5Ms for Primary Care Workshop. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10814

`q2 · i0` · `design · r2`

Six knowledge questions corresponding to the Geriatrics 5Ms were administered pre- and postworkshop and to 10 control learners. Scores were "82% correct on both the pre- and postworkshop assessments" and 78% for controls, with no statistically significant differences; the near-zero change reflects a ceiling effect the authors acknowledge.

> "Learners scored very highly on the knowledge questions before and after the workshop, with an average score of 82% correct on both the pre- and postworkshop assessments. Control learners also scored very high on the knowledge questions, with 78% correct responses."
