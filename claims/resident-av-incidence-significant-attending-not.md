## Evidence

### Abu Al-Burak Salem 2025

Abu Al-Burak Salem, Butt Fahad, Li Xiaole, Garg Amit X, Hutnik Cindy ML, Malvankar-Mehta Monali S. (2025). Anterior vitrectomy incidence in cataract surgery among experienced surgeons and residents: A systematic review and meta-analysis. European Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.1177/11206721251380886

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis in STATA v. 18.0 of five studies (four retrospective, one prospective cohort) covering 4,918 surgeries and 208 AV cases; the review reports "a clinically significant difference was observed for residents" with ES = 0.04 versus a non-significant ES = 0.03 for attendings.

> "A clinically significant difference was observed for residents performing cataract surgery (ES = 0.04; 95% CI: [0.01, 0.06]). In contrast, a clinically non-significant difference was observed for attending surgeons (ES = 0.03; 95% CI: [−0.03, 0.09])."
