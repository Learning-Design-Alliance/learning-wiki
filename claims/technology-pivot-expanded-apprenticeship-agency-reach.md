Qualitative findings from virtual discussion groups with apprenticeship administrators in eight states, as reported in the brief's key takeaways. Administrators said technology let them "pivot their work online quickly" and that working virtually meant agencies "expanded their reach and responsiveness."

> "Leveraging technology allowed apprenticeship administrators to pivot their work online quickly and easily and to streamline work processes and by working virtually, state apprenticeship agencies continued supporting partners and expanded their reach and responsiveness."
