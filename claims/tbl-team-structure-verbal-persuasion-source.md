## Evidence

### Brokk Toggerson 2020

Brokk Toggerson, Samyukta Krishnamurthy, Emily E. Hansen, and Chasya Church. (2020). Positive Impacts on Student Self-Efficacy from an Introductory Physics for Life Science Course Using the Team-Based Learning Pedagogy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' interpretive analysis drawing on end-of-semester team-satisfaction Likert data (N = 206, high averages shown in Figure 8) and student comments. They cite Zeldin and Pajares' suggestion that she-identifying people respond more significantly to verbal persuasion, and offer the team structure as a hypothesized contributor, not a tested mechanism.

> "We therefore posit that these large, long-lasting teams may be contributors as to why we see signiﬁcant shifts in self eﬃcacy from verbal persuasion in she-identifying individ- uals in our study and suggest this as an area for future research."
