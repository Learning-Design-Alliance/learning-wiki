## Evidence

### Bonner C 2019

Bonner C, Patel P, Fajardo MA, Zhuang R, Trevena L. (2019). Online decision aids for primary cardiovascular disease prevention: systematic search, evaluation of quality and suitability for low health literacy patients. BMJ Open 9(3):e025173. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025173

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

PEMAT-P ratings of the 25 identified decision aids by two independent searchers with conflicts resolved by a third rater; a 70% threshold defined understandable/actionable. The aids were "generally scored well on understandability but lower on actionability". Descriptive means only; no effect size printed.

> "The average understandability score was 87% (SD=7.1%) and actionability was 61% (SD=24.6%)."
