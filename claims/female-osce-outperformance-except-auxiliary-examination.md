## Evidence

### Zhang Huiqun 2025

Zhang Huiqun, Li Shanshan, Zheng Guoquan, Chen Xiaoyun, Zhong Lujie, Li Jiaying, Li Yun. (2025). Network analysis of an OSCE-based graduation skills assessment for clinical medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07091-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent samples t-tests on OSCE scores of 3,710 undergraduates at one Guangzhou medical institution compared genders across modules and total score. The study reports "significant differences (all p < 0.001) except for auxiliary examination analysis" (p = 0.169 for that module).

> "Female students scored significantly higher than male students on all modules and overall scores except for auxiliary examination analysis (see Table 4; Fig. 1)."
