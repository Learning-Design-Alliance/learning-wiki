### Flores-Cohaila Javier A. 2025

Flores-Cohaila Javier A., Miranda-Chavez Brayan, Copaja-Corzo Cesar, Benavente-Chalco Xiomara C., Rios-García Wagner, Moreno-Ccama Vanessa P., Samanez-Obeso Angel, Rivarola-Hidalgo Marco. (2025). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06665-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear mixed-effect model of 28,872 applicants (2019–2023) with specialty, setting, modality, and year as random effects; the model's intercept was 12.19 and random effects explain 17% of score variability. The article reports the ENARM score "increases with each level of the ENAM, regardless of receiving honors or specialty of application."

> "For the fixed effects (level 1), the ENAM score had a non-linear relationship with the ENARM score, showing a positive linear relationship and a negative quadratic relationship. Receiving honors in medical school was associated with the ENARM score."
