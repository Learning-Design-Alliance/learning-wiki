### Mutmainna 2026

Exploring Students’ perceptions of their learning experience and self-efficacy in physics online class with project-based learning: An interpretative phenomenological analysis. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.13251](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.13251)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Participant interview statement from Theme 2 findings on lecture material formats. Zahra valued "videos that I can play back" for repeated listening; Kanaya similarly said videos let her review missed material and benchmark the next assignment. Self-reported preference; no quantitative measure.

> "I like online learning accompanied by videos that I can play back because for me to be able to understand the material explained by the teacher, it cannot be just once, but it needs to be listened to repeatedly."
