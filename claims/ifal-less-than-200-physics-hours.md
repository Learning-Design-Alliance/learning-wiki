The article gives this as a quantitative example of the overloaded-curriculum problem: in less than 200 hours teachers must cover the complete content of classical physics, including mechanics, optics, thermodynamics, and electromagnetism.

> "students graduating in 2016 from the secondary technical course at IFAL, an institution run by the federal government, should have attended 267 hours of physics over 3 years. Due to exams and administrative tasks, such as verifying the presence of each student, they eﬀectively were oﬀered less than 200 hours of physics"
