## Evidence

### McCaffrey 2011

McCaffrey, D. F. and Lockwood, J. R. (2011). Missing data in value-added modeling of teacher effects. The Annals of Applied Statistics, 5(2A), 773-797. https://doi.org/10.1214/10-AOAS405

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Posterior estimates from the random effects selection model fitted to the district mathematics data. The printed "posterior mean and standard deviation for β were −0.83 and 0.03", with completion probabilities of 0.31 and 0.12 for students one SD above and below the mean; only descriptive posteriors are printed, so no standardized effect size.

> "The posterior mean and standard deviation for β were −0.83 and 0.03, respectively. At the mean for β, a student with an eﬀect of δ = 0.72 (one standard deviation above the prior mean of zero) would have a probability of 0.31 of completing all ﬁve years of testing"
