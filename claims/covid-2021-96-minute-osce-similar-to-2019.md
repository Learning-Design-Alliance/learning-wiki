`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-hoc pairwise comparisons following ANOVA on anonymised results of the 2020 (n=200) and 2021 (n=205) cohorts versus the 2019 cohort (n=200). The total ACS mark difference 2021 v. 2019 was −0.48 (95% CI −1.57, 0.61, p=0.39); no effect size is printed.

> "The 2021 OSCE demonstrated a similar overall mark compared with the pre-COVID 2019 OSCE, with only one discipline (Paediatrics) noting a lower mark (3.15%, p < 0.001)"
