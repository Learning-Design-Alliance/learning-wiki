Evidence¶

Namaziandost 2018¶

Namaziandost, E., Esfahani, F. R., & Hashemifarnia, A. (2018). The Impact of Using Authentic Videos on Prosodic Ability among Foreign Language Learners. International Journal of Instruction, 11(4), 375-390. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1191678

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation of the expert raters' component-level reports after the 12-session treatment: "The most astounding advancement was in students' utilization of stress, and pronunciation", with least advance in articulation of sounds absent from the mother tongue. No per-component statistics are printed.

"The most astounding advancement was in students' utilization of stress, and pronunciation. Nonetheless, the least advance was in articulation of speech sounds, particularly in pronouncing those consonants and vowels that do not stand in the students' mother tongue."
