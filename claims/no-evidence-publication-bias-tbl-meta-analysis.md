## Evidence

### Fu Jie 2026

Fu Jie, Chen Xiu, Li Jinglun, Peng Lilei. (2026). TBL alone or combined with other teaching methods for neurology education in China: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09468-1

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication-bias assessment for the two primary outcomes using funnel plots, Egger's test, and trim-and-fill. Trim-and-fill imputed no missing studies on either side, and the pooled effect size remained unchanged after adjustment.

> "funnel plots of both theoretical knowledge scores and practical skill scores demonstrated the asymmetric distribution of scattered dots, but Egger's tests were not significant (p = 0.43 and 0.24, respectively)."
