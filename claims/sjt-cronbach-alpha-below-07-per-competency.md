## Evidence

### Obi Uche Shalom 2024

Obi Uche Shalom, Chukwuma Adanna, Agu Ifunanya, Eigbiremolen Godstime, Mbachu Chinyere. (2024). Development and validation of situational judgement test for assessment of behavioural competencies required for effective medical practice in Nigeria. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06298-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Generalization-inference analysis of the pilot SJT (N=303). Alphas per competency ranged from 0.20 to 0.33 in Table 5; re-estimation using only the 36 items with good discrimination gave "mostly similar" results. The authors cite the literature that low alphas are typical for SJTs.

> "Cronbach's alpha estimation across competencies return alpha estimates that are less than 0.7, which suggests low internal consistency among test items in each category or competency. However, it is important to note that low Cronbach’s alpha values for SJTs do not necessarily indicate poor precision of measurement; rather it reflects the heterogeneity of the domain being measured [25]."
