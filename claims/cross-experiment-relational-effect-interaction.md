# Cross-experiment analysis shows reward effects on feature relationships depend jointly on search mode and target-distractor similarity

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` A two-way ANOVA on ΔRT in the relation-match condition revealed a significant search mode main effect (ηp2 = 0.033) and a significant search mode × similarity interaction (ηp2 = 0.027), while feature-value effects were comparable across experiments. [→ Oudeng Jia 2025](#oudeng-jia-2025)

## Evidence

### Oudeng Jia 2025

Oudeng Jia, Qingsong Tan, Sihan Zhang, Ke Jia & Mengyuan Gong. (2025). The precision of attention selection during reward learning influences the mechanisms of value-driven attention. npj Science of Learning 10:49. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00342-1

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Cross-experiment analysis (Fig. 4) pooling Experiments 1a, 1b, 2a and 2c (N = 40 each). The relation-match ΔRT ANOVA showed "a signiﬁcant interaction effect (F(1,156) = 4.35,p = 0.039" with printed small effect sizes ηp2 = 0.033 (search mode) and ηp2 = 0.027 (interaction), and planned comparisons found Experiment 2c differed from all other experiments (ps < 0.006).

> "the same two-way ANOVA conducted on ΔRT in the relation-match condition showed a signi ﬁcant main effect of search mode (F(1,156) = 5.40, p = 0.021, ηp 2 = 0.033), and more importantly, a signiﬁcant interaction effect (F(1,156) = 4.35,p = 0.039, ηp 2 = 0.027)."
