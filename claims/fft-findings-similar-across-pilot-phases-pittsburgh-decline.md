## Evidence

### Stephen Lipscomb 2015

Stephen Lipscomb, Jeffrey Terziev, Duncan Chaplin. (2015). Measuring Teachers' Effectiveness: A Report from Phase 3 of Pennsylvania's Pilot of the Framework for Teaching. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/measuring-teachers-effectiveness-a-report-from-phase-3-of-pennsylvanias-pilot-of-the-framework

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of 2012-2013 results with 2011-2012 data from the previous pilot phase. The report found more than 90 percent in the top two categories in both phases, with a somewhat smaller fraction in Pittsburgh.

> "More than 90 percent of teachers were rated in the top two performance categories in both phases, although the fraction of ratings in the top two categories decreased somewhat in Pittsburgh (which contributed more teachers to the pilot than any other district)."

### Stephen Lipscomb 2015 (2)

Stephen Lipscomb, Jeffrey Terziev, Duncan Chaplin. (2015). Measuring Teachers' Effectiveness: A Report from Phase 3 of Pennsylvania's Pilot of the Framework for Teaching. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/measuring-teachers-effectiveness-a-report-from-phase-3-of-pennsylvanias-pilot-of-the-framework

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-phase comparison of FFT-VAM correlations. The report states correlations were "almost always positive but also below 0.30 in both phases", with the lowest 2011-2012 correlations improving slightly the next year.

> "The correlations between parts of the FFT and VAM scores were almost always positive but also below 0.30 in both phases. The lowest correlations in 2011–2012 improved slightly in 2012–2013."
