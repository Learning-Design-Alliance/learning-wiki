## Evidence

### Fisher H 2022

Fisher H, Zabar S, Chodosh J, Langford A, Trinh-Shevrin C, Sherman S, Altshuler L. (2022). A novel simulation-based approach to training for recruitment of older adults to clinical trials. BMC Medical Research Methodology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12874-022-01643-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

SP assessment of 39 completed 11-item behaviorally anchored communication checklists across three cases (internet challenges limited completion at the first session). The article reports "the % well-done of items by case ranged from 25–85%"; no effect size is printed.

> "There was a range of scores: the % well-done of items by case ranged from 25–85%."

### Fisher H 2022 (2)

Fisher H, Zabar S, Chodosh J, Langford A, Trinh-Shevrin C, Sherman S, Altshuler L. (2022). A novel simulation-based approach to training for recruitment of older adults to clinical trials. BMC Medical Research Methodology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12874-022-01643-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item-level SP checklist results across all cases (N = 39). The article reports 85% "did not interrupt and allowed time to express thoughts fully" while only 25% "checked your understanding through specific questioning"; no effect size is printed.

> "For example, SPs reported that 85% of research staff "did not interrupt and allowed time to express thoughts fully" 25% checked your understanding through specific questioning and/or asking you to repeat back information."
