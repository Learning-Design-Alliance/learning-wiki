## Evidence

### Rees Philippa 2025

Rees Philippa, Dronavalli Mithilesh, Carter Ben, Dickson Michelle, Green Charles, Lawler Kate, Lee Evelyn, Uebel Hannah, Gale Chris, Oei Ju Lee. (2025). School Performance of Preterm-Born Children After Intraventricular Hemorrhage. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.47584

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Subgroup linear regression by individual IVH grade (eTable 9 sensitivity analysis) in the same cohort found grade 2 IVH "significantly lower total z scores at age 8 to 9 years compared with very preterm controls (AMD, −0.20; 95% CI, −0.36 to −0.04)"; grade 1 IVH showed no difference. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "children with grade 2 IVH had significantly lower total z scores at age 8 to 9 years compared with very preterm controls (AMD, −0.20; 95% CI, −0.36 to −0.04), while no difference was found for children with grade 1 IVH"
