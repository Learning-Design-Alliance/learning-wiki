Elaine Allensworth and John Q. Easton 2001¶

Comparison of cohort rates computed from CPS drop files (Tables 5–6) against Master Files rates. The report treats this as a sensitivity analysis of the classification decision; no inferential statistics are printed.

"the drop files do suggest: (a) somewhat higher absolute dropout rates than those calculated with the Master Files (about 4 percent higher at age 19), and (b) no trend downward over time."
