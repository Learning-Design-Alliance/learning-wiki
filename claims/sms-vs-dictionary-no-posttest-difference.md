## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After 16 weeks, the SMS group (M=25.17) and dictionary group (M=22.64) did not differ significantly on the immediate 40-item vocabulary post-test (t=1.48, p=.42). [→ Alemi et al. 2012](#alemi-et-al-2012)

## Evidence

### Alemi et al. 2012

Minoo Alemi, Mohammad Reza Anani Sarab & Zahra Lari. (2012). Successful Learning of Academic Word List via MALL: Mobile Assisted Language Learning. International Education Studies, 5(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n6p99

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test on immediate post-test scores of 45 non-English-major freshmen at Sharif University of Technology: experimental SMS group (N=28, M=25.17, SD=5.74) vs control dictionary group (N=17, M=22.64, SD=5.18). The study reports "not any significant difference between the experimental and control groups in the post-test."

> "The t-test result (t=1.48, p= .42) shows that there was not any significant difference between the experimental and control groups in the post-test."
