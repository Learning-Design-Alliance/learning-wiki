Descriptive analysis of the 30 control-group students' responses to the 12 WAI statements (Table 4); scores averaged above 3 and 4, indicating a high level of writing anxiety.

"the mean of the third statement was M = 4.56 which indicated that writing under time limit was a major source of the students' anxiety; the students' responses to the fifth statement, the mean was M= 4. 00, showed that students had negative feelings towards writing and, eventually, evaluation was considered as a source for provoking students' writing anxiety."
