## Evidence

### Flowers Elena 2019

Flowers Elena, Martin Margaret, Abid Hamza, Binford Sasha, Mackin Lynda. (2019). Pairing pedagogical and genomic advances to prepare advanced practice nurses for the era of precision health. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1542-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive self-assessment survey (5-point Likert, standardized to −1 to 1) administered at four time-points during the 2016–2017 three-course series. Scores are descriptive standardized means; no effect size or significance test is printed.

> "Average agreement across all individual competencies at the beginning of the first course was − 0.3 (range − 1, 1). At beginning of the second course, average agreement increased to 0.1. At both beginning and completion of the third course, average agreement increased to 0.5."

### Flowers Elena 2019 (2)

Flowers Elena, Martin Margaret, Abid Hamza, Binford Sasha, Mackin Lynda. (2019). Pairing pedagogical and genomic advances to prepare advanced practice nurses for the era of precision health. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1542-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-assessment data from the same cohort, reported in Figs. 1 and 2 by competency theme; the article states "all students reported agreement scores with all of the 38 individual competencies" after the second course.

> "Upon completion of the second course, all students reported agreement scores with all of the 38 individual competencies (Fig. 1, Fig. 2)."
