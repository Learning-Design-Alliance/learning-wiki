### Hornung 2026

Hornung, I., Marasinghe Arachchige, D., Kumarage, T., Agrawal, G., Deng, Y., Chen, Y.-C., & Liu, H. (2026). CyberAGENTS: Structured Autonomy for Agentic Gamified Learning in Cybersecurity. Preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07965 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.07965](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.07965)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of open-ended post-study survey responses from the 24-participant classroom deployment. Participants "consistently valued the realism of the scenarios and the BuddyAgent's guided support" and requested shorter, more structured responses and more immersive interaction formats.

> "Open-ended responses revealed four recurring themes: trust in AI scaffolding, clarity of feedback, engagement and interaction design, and authenticity of tasks"
