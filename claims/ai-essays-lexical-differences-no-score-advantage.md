### Will Yeadon 2024

Will Yeadon, Elise Agra, Oto-obong Inyang, Paul Mackay, Arin Mizouri. (2024). Evaluating AI and Human Authorship Quality in Academic Writing through Physics Essays. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad669d](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad669d)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Lexical analysis of the essay corpus found AI essays used more unique words (159.47, SD = 15.10) and longer words (5.78, SD = 0.24) than human essays. The article states this "lexical diversity and sophistication failed to translate into better scores".

> "Statistically significant differences were observed for both metrics, with p-values of 5 .594 × 10−3 for unique words and 3.454 × 10−38 for average word length, indicating a slightly richer vocabulary in the AI-generated texts and a preference for longer words."
