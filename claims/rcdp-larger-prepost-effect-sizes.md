## Evidence

### Xie Lichun 2025

Xie Lichun, Mo Yongkang, Ma Jiejia, Xiao Yubin, Wu Xiaojia, Ma Xilun, Sun Danmiao, Ke Dongping, Mei Wei, Huang Ruibin. (2025). Rapid-cycle deliberate practice with an ex vivo porcine model of intussusception improves radiology residents' competence in air enema reduction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08339-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-post paired analysis in the RCT: the RCDP group's theoretical gain was 11.50 ± 1.16 (P = 0.004) and practical gain 20.40 ± 3.02 (P = 0.012); the article states the printed effect sizes "indicated substantially greater improvements in the RCDP group compared to the TTM group".

> "Although the TTM group also showed measurable gains (theoretical score: mean difference: 6.57 ± 1.55, 95% CI :3.06, 9.93, P = 0.030; practical score: mean difference: 13.40 ± 1.27, 95% CI :10.70, 18.15, P = 0.011), the effect sizes (RCDP: ES = 2.06 and 1.94, TTM: ES = 1.16 and 1.20) indicated substantially greater improvements in the RCDP group compared to the TTM group."
