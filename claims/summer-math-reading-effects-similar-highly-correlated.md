# Summer program effects on math and reading outcomes are similar, and studies' math and reading effect sizes are highly correlated (unweighted r=0.93).

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A within-study fixed effect comparison shows summer program effects did not differ significantly for math versus reading (difference d=-0.024, CI -0.059 to 0.011). [→ Quinn 2014](#quinn-2014)
`q3 i3` Across studies with both outcomes, math and reading effect sizes are highly correlated, unweighted Pearson's r=0.93 (weighted r=0.98; Spearman's rho=0.94). [→ Quinn 2014 (2)](#quinn-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Quinn 2014

Quinn, D. M., Lynch, K., & Kim, J. S. (2014). Replicating the Moderating Role of Income Status on Summer School Effects across Subject Areas: A Meta-analysis. SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562949

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Fixed effect within-study comparison of pooled reading (d=0.02, k=9) and math (d=0.051, k=9) effects from programs including both components (Table 2); the difference is -0.024 (CI -0.059 to 0.011), non-significant.

> "These results indicate that program effects on math and reading were similar."

### Quinn 2014 (2)

Quinn, D. M., Lynch, K., & Kim, J. S. (2014). Replicating the Moderating Role of Income Status on Summer School Effects across Subject Areas: A Meta-analysis. SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562949

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Scatter plot of math on reading effect sizes (Figure 1) for studies with both outcomes, bubbles weighted by inverse variance of the math effect size. The article reports an "unweighted Pearson’s r of 0.93" (weighted r=0.98; Spearman's rho=0.94).

> "Effect sizes are highly correlated, with an unweighted Pearson’s r of 0.93 (weighted r=0.98; Spearman’s rho= 0.94)."
