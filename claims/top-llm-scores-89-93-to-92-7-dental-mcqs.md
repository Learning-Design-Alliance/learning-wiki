The Discussion summarises the study's own two-session results on 150 dental MCQs: the top three models scored "between 89.3 and 92.7%" and the other two between 86.7% and 88.7%. Percentages are descriptive; no effect size is printed.

> "All models demonstrated high accuracy, with ChatGPT-4, Grok XI and Qwen 2.5 achieving the highest scores, ranging from 89.3 to 92.7%. DeepSeek-V3 and Gemini followed with slightly lower, yet still strong, performances between 86.7% and 88.7%."
