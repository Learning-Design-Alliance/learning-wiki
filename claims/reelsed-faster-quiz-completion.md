### Lazaros Stavrinou 2025

Lazaros Stavrinou, Argyris Constantinides, Marios Belk, Vasos Vassiliou, Fotis Liarokapis, and Marios Constantinides. 2025. The Reel Deal: Designing and Evaluating LLM-Generated Short-Form Educational Videos. CHIGreece 2025. https://doi.org/10.1145/3749012.3749048

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same 62-student between-subjects experiment, the LLM group's mean quiz duration was "M = 328.77 seconds" versus 446.23 seconds for the No-LLM group; the Mann-Whitney U test was statistically significant (U = 219.00, p = 0.0002). No effect size is printed.

> "The mean quiz duration for the LLM group was M = 328.77 seconds (5:28), SD = 104.26, compared to M = 446.23 seconds (7:26), SD = 132.87 for the No-LLM group, as illustrated in Figure 3 (middle). A Mann-Whitney U test indicated that this difference was statistically significant, U = 219.00, p = 0.0002"
