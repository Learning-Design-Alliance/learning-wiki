## Evidence

### Lee 2017

Lee, J., Lim, C., & Kim, H. (2017). Development of an instructional design model for flipped learning in higher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-016-9502-1

`q2 · i3` · `design · r3`

External field evaluation of a 15-week flipped algebra course designed with the final model; 17 students completed the VAMS. The article reports "signiﬁcantly higher scores in the post-VAMS" with r = 0.56.

> "Both Wilcoxon signed rank test and paired t test indicated that participants showed signiﬁcantly higher scores in the post-VAMS ( M = 3.59, SE = 0.085) than in the pre-VAMS (M = 3.28, SE = 0.061), ( Z = 2.33, t(17) = 2.774, p \ 0.05, r = 0.56)."
