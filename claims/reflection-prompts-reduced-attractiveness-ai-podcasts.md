`q2 i2` The Standard condition rated the experience significantly more favorably on the UEQ Attractiveness subscale than the Reflection condition (t(34) = 2.26, p = 0.03, D = 0.75). [→ Vishnu Menon 2025](#vishnu-menon-2025)

## Evidence

### Vishnu Menon 2025

Vishnu Menon, Andy Cherney, Elizabeth B. Cloude, Li Zhang, Tiffany D. Do. (2025). Evaluating the Impact of LLM-guided Reflection on Learning Outcomes with Interactive AI-Generated Educational Podcasts. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.04787](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.04787)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Two-sample t-test on the UEQ Attractiveness subscale in the same 36-participant randomized podcast experiment; descriptive means were 29.56 (Standard) versus 26.22 (Reflection), with the Standard condition rated "more favorably than the Reflection condition".

> "The results suggested there was a significant difference in Attractiveness, t(34) = 2.26, p= 0.03, D= 0.75, where the Standard condition rated the experience more favorably than the Reflection condition"
