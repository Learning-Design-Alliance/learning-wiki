Brame Cynthia J 2016¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

The essay reports, citing Lawson et al. (2006), a study in an introductory psychology class where some sections received eight guiding questions while watching a social psychology video: "scored significantly higher on a later test".

"The students who answered the guiding questions while watching the video scored significantly higher on a later test."

Brame Cynthia J 2016 (2)¶

Brame Cynthia J. (2016). Effective Educational Videos: Principles and Guidelines for Maximizing Student Learning from Video Content. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-03-0125

q2 · i? · review · r2

The essay reports, citing Zhang et al. (2006), a comparison of interactive and noninteractive video in a computer science course: students who controlled movement "demonstrated better achievement of learning outcomes and greater satisfaction".

"Students who were able to control movement through the video, selecting important sections to review and moving backward when desired, demonstrated better achievement of learning outcomes and greater satisfaction."
