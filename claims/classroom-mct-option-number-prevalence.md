`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Content-agnostic coding of option-number types across all items in the 182-test corpus, showing "a clear and persistent gap between recommendations for best-practices in high-stakes MCTs and in-practice deployment of classroom tests."

> "Among all items, 31% are found to be of the 5-option type, 56% are 4-option, and only 6% are of 3-option, with 7% of items remaining uncategorized or miscoded."
