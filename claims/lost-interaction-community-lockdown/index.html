Evidence¶

Dilani Gedera · Dianne Forbes · Cheryl Brown · Maggie Hartnett · Ashwini Datt 2023¶

Dilani Gedera · Dianne Forbes · Cheryl Brown · Maggie Hartnett · Ashwini Datt. (2023). Learning during a pandemic: an Activity Theory analysis of the challenges experienced by Aotearoa/New Zealand university students. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10284-3

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative theme from questionnaire open-ended responses, individual interviews and focus groups with 43 students. The article also reports "many (45%) experiencing isolation" and that group work was challenging due to absent and unresponsive peers.

"students expressed that a lack of interpersonal engagement, interaction and collaboration impacted their learning. Students highlighted that “engagement with the lecturers was just a voice and slides on a screen”"
