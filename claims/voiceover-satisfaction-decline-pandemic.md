## Evidence

### Chou E 2026

Chou E, Cooper D, Goel P, Tang J, Henkle G, Zemeida S, Wolfson H, Shyu YL, Chang E, Abram A, Gerriets VA, Cusick JK. (2026). The use of voiceovers and review games in teaching immunology. ImmunoHorizons. https://doi.org/10.1093/immhor/vlag042

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Nonparametric Mann-Whitney U tests on Likert-scale survey responses stratified by academic year and matriculation year. The study reports a "statistically significant decrease in satisfaction with voiceovers" across both stratifications, which the authors attribute to the COVID-19 pandemic's effect on prior educational experiences.

> "students from the 2023–24 academic year noted a statistically significant decrease in satisfaction with voiceovers compared with student feedback obtained from the 2021–22 academic year (P = 0.039) (Fig. 2B). We also stratified student responses by year of matriculation, and a similar trend was obtained, because students who matriculated in 2023 expressed a statistically significant decrease in satisfaction with voiceovers compared with students who matriculated in 2020 (P = 0.0079) (Fig. 2C)."
