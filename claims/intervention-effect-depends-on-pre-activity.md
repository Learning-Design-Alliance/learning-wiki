## Evidence

### Lim Jaeseo 2022

Lim Jaeseo, Ko Hyunwoong, Park Jooyong, Ihm Jungjoon. (2022). Effect of active learning and online discussions on the academic performances of dental students. BMC Medical Education, 22, 312. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03377-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the online experiment's group means: among lecture groups the worksheet intervention showed positive effects, but among self-study groups intervention appeared to lower scores. The printed significant contrast supporting a direction is SW exceeding LD on transfer items, "t (78) = 2.33, P = 0.022, d = 0.770".

> "The LW group had higher scores than the LD group, and the SW group had lower scores than the SSD group, which indicated that when using discussions in the classroom, the instructors need to employ different types of intervention depending on the student learning environments or conditions."
