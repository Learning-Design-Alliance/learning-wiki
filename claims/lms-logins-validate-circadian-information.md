## Evidence

### Smarr 2018

Smarr, B. L., & Schirmer, A. E. (2018). 3.4 million real-world learning management system logins reveal the majority of students experience social jet lag correlated with decreased performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-23044-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Validation analysis of LMS login data from 14,894 students. Women showed more evening activity (F = 143, p = 6 × 10−33), men more night activity (F = 47, p = 9 × 10−12), older students showed earlier phases, and fall vs spring semesters differed (F = 10.6, p = 0.001), matching known circadian patterns.

> "In summary, non-class days are validated as containing biological circadian information because they show expected changes with gender, age, and season."
