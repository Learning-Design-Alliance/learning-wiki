Evidence¶

Dominik M. Piehlmaier & Dee Warmath 2025¶

Dominik M. Piehlmaier & Dee Warmath. (2025). The challenge of explicit learning in life skill education. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00375-6

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Bayesian analysis of the five-week RCT at University of Wisconsin-Madison with 96 undergraduates randomized to three lecture conditions. The financial education arm's knowledge gain had pM 0.685, pSD 0.302, 95% HPD [0.09; 1.277]; the other arms showed "no substantial impact".

"participants in the ﬁnancial education arm were the only group to experi- ence an increase in ﬁnancial knowledge (posterior mean of initial vs sub- sequent percentage of correct answers to theﬁnancial knowledge quiz [pM]: 0.685"
