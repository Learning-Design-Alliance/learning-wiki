## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Among 250 pre-clinical medical students, MBEA scores showed a weak positive correlation with percussion competency (r≈0.18, R²=0.033, p=0.003). [→ Chopra Jasneep Singh 2025](#chopra-jasneep-singh-2025)

## Evidence

### Chopra Jasneep Singh 2025

Chopra Jasneep Singh, Tyagi Ruchi, Reddy Saravanan Sampoornam Pape. (2025). Assessing the impact of musical proficiency on percussion note identification skills in undergraduate medical students: an analysis using the Montreal Battery of Evaluation of Amusia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06679-w

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlation/regression analysis of 250 pre-clinical medical students. The regression model for MBEA scores versus percussion competency had R² = 0.03383 and a slope significantly non-zero (F = 8.685, P = 0.0035), described as "a weak model fit".

> "the correlation between MBEA scores and percussion competency was weak (r ≈ 0.18, p < 0.01), highlighting that while general musical ability is related to percussion skills, the effect is relatively small"
