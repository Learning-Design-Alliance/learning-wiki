### Chen Shengdong 2025

Chen, S., Xu, H., Jin, C., Yin, K., Wang, X., & Wang, T. (2025). Social support and english academic achievement: the mediating role of foreign language emotions and the moderating effect of academic self-concept. BMC Psychology, 13, 1286. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03617-9

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive and correlation analysis (Table 1, N = 1543). Enjoyment–achievement r = .286, boredom–achievement r = −.240, anxiety–achievement r = −.167, self-concept–achievement r = .605, support–achievement r = .142, all p < .01. The largest, self-concept–achievement r = .605, is large (impact coded from printed values).
