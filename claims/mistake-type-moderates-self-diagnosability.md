## Evidence

### Andrew Mason 2009

Andrew Mason, Elisheva Cohen, Chandralekha Singh, and Edit Yerushalmi. (2009). Self-Diagnosis, Scaffolding and Transfer: A Tale of Two Problems. [doi:10.1063/1.3266736](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266736)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of students' mistakes in applying physics principles across the two quizzes, reported in the Discussion: on quiz 6 roughly 60% (including all groups) could not apply an invoked principle correctly, while on quiz 7 more than 90% could. The authors also note difficulty self-diagnosing invocation mistakes of the momentum conservation principle was common among students of all groups on quiz 7.

> "approximately 60% of the students (including all groups) w ere unable to apply the physics principles correctly even if they invoked it in quiz 6 whereas for quiz 7, more than 90% of the students who invoked a physics principle were able to apply it correctly."
