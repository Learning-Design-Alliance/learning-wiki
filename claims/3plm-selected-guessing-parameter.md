# The three-parameter logistic model was selected for estimating SEL and knowledge-point difficulty because lower-scoring students showed more Guttman errors, indicating guessing

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Cao Zhen-Yu 2024

Cao Zhen-Yu, Lin Feng, Feng Chun. (2024). Interpretation of course conceptual structure and student self-efficacy: an integrated strategy of knowledge graphs with item response modeling. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05401-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Model-comparison analysis of logistic IRT models fitted to the 50-item scale. The article states the 3PLM "did not have a significant difference with 1PLM" while adding a guessing parameter; several KPs had non-zero guessing values.

> "The 3PLM was ultimately chosen, which did not have a significant difference with 1PLM and additionally incorporated a guessing parameter compared to the 1PLM."

### Cao Zhen-Yu 2024 (2)

Cao Zhen-Yu, Lin Feng, Feng Chun. (2024). Interpretation of course conceptual structure and student self-efficacy: an integrated strategy of knowledge graphs with item response modeling. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05401-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of scale total scores against Guttman errors. The article reports a statistically significant negative correlation, indicating "students with lower scores tended to demonstrate more Guttman errors", implying guessing on harder items.

> "the total scores of the 50-item scale for each student were negatively correlated with the number of Guttman errors (p = 1.51 × 10− 22)"
