## Evidence

### Zhao Xin 2020

Zhao Xin, Wang Haien, Maes Joseph H. R. (2020). Training and transfer effects of extensive task-switching training in students. Psychological Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-018-1059-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post ANOVA on the untrained switching task in the trained group (n=31), following a significant Group × CSI × Session three-way interaction. The quote reports the CSI-specific switch-cost reductions (ηp2 = 0.47 at 300 ms; ηp2 = 0.13 at 600 ms).

> "The interaction reflected a significantly smaller switch cost for the trained participants during the post- than pre-training assessment under the CSI 300 ms, F(1, 30) = 26.54, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.47, and CSI 600 ms, F(1, 30) = 4.30, p = 0.047, ηp2 = 0.13, conditions, but not the CSI 800 ms condition, p = 0.18."

### Zhao Xin 2020 (2)

Zhao Xin, Wang Haien, Maes Joseph H. R. (2020). Training and transfer effects of extensive task-switching training in students. Psychological Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-018-1059-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post ANOVA on transfer-task mixing cost comparing trained (n=31) and control (n=29) groups; no Group × Session effect for mixing cost in any CSI condition.

> "Thus, training did not result in a larger decrease in mixing cost from pre- to post-training assessment session than that observed in the control group, for any of the CSI conditions."
