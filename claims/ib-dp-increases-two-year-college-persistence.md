### International Baccalaureate Diploma Program students in Chicago's neighborhood high schools more likely to go to college 2007

International Baccalaureate Diploma Program students in Chicago's neighborhood high schools more likely to go to college, persist. (2007). University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research press release. http://www.ccsr.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Quantitative propensity-matched analysis of college persistence for the classes of 2003-2007 finds IB DP participants "significantly more likely to persist in college for at least two years." No effect size is printed. The authors note they had not previously seen coursework affect persistence two years after graduation.

> "In addition, IB DP students from CPS who attended four-year colleges were significantly more likely to persist in college for at least two years."
