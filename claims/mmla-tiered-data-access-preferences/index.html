q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Interview study on accountability (RQ2) with 14 students; thematic analysis of data-access preferences. Twelve of 14 favoured whole-class access for "self-reflection and collaborative learning purposes"; only six favoured sharing with other classes.

"A majority of the participants (n = 12) believed that the whole class should have access to their data, primarily for self-reflection and collaborative learning purposes."

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Same interview study, educator-access theme. Ten supported teacher access and 13 coordinator access, but some participants such as P8 and P11 "preferred differentiating access levels between video, audio, and visualizations."

"Most participants (n = 10; n = 13) supported granting data access to the unit coordinator and other teachers to enhance teaching and assessment methods."
