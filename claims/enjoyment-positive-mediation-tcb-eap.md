## Evidence

### Shao Y 2025

Shao Y, Zhu H, Kong W, Li W, Zhang C. (2025). How teacher caring behavior affects high school students’ English academic performance: the mediating role of academic emotions. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000042751

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped SEM mediation analysis (5000 resamples) among 702 high school students. The indirect pathway through enjoyment was significant with a 95% CI of "0.331–1.472"; enjoyment constituted 48.9% of the total indirect effect, the largest of the three mediators.

> "the mediation effect of EJ was significant (P < .01), with a 95% CI not containing 0 (0.331–1.472), suggesting that the “TCB → EJ → EAP” indirect pathway is established"
