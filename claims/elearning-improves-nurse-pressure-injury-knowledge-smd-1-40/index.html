Evidence¶

Ding 2024¶

Ding, Y., Qian, J., Zhou, Y., & Zhang, Y. (2024). Effect of e‐learning program for improving nurse knowledge and practice towards managing pressure injuries: A systematic review and meta‐analysis. Nursing Open, 11, e2039. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.2039

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of four studies reporting nurse knowledge outcomes, pooled as standardized mean differences. The review reports "pooled SMD was 1.40" with p < 0.004; no standardized effect-size category beyond the SMD is printed, so impact is unclassified. Sensitivity analysis showed no single-study effects.

"The pooled SMD was 1.40 (95%CI: 0.45–2.35; I 2 = 93.1%). This shows that nurses receiving e‐learning had significantly higher knowledge gain for managing pressure injuries compared to nurses that did not receive e‐learning, with p‐value of <0.004"
