## Evidence

### Rémeau Margot 2026

Rémeau Margot, Raffy Gaël, Borst Grégoire. (2026). Academic Resilience in Contexts of Inequality: Motivational and Self‐Efficacy Profiles of Disadvantaged High Achievers. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1002/jad.70190

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bayesian ANCOVA on a nationally representative French panel of 8934 students compared intrinsic motivation across nine SES × achievement profiles, controlling for age and sex. The article reports that "resilient students showed higher levels of intrinsic motivation than low‐SES low achievers and low‐SES mid achievers" with Bayes factors favoring differences, while comparisons with advantaged high achievers "yielded little to no evidence for differences."

> "resilient students showed higher levels of intrinsic motivation than low‐SES low achievers and low‐SES mid achievers (BF10s > 1), indicating differences within the same level of socioeconomic disadvantage. In contrast, comparisons between low‐SES high achievers and other high‐achieving students from middle‐ or high‐SES backgrounds yielded little to no evidence for differences (BF10 = 1 or <1), suggesting that resilient students reached intrinsic motivational levels comparable to those of more socioeconomically advantaged high achievers."
