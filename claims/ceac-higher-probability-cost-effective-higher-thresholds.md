## Evidence

### Janjua Aisha 2018

Janjua Aisha, Roberts Tracy, Okeahialam Nicola, Clark T Justin. (2018). Cost-effective analysis of teaching pelvic examination skills using Gynaecology Teaching Associates (GTAs) compared with manikin models (The CEAT Study). BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-015823

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Probabilistic sensitivity analysis using two-dimensional bootstrapping to generate cost-effectiveness acceptability curves (figure 2) for the three competence thresholds. The article states there is "an increased probability of cost-effectiveness" at the higher thresholds relative to 50%.

> "The CEACs (figure 2) demonstrate that there is an increased probability of cost-effectiveness with using GTAs to train students to a 60% or 70% competence level, as compared with a 50% competence level."
