## Evidence

### Frank Reichert 2020

Frank Reichert, Nancy W. Y. Law, Gary K. W. Wong, Jimmy de la Torre. (2020). Exploring the structure of digital literacy competence assessed with authentic software applications. Education Tech Research Dev, 68, 2991–3013. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09825-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multiple-group analysis in Results comparing P5 and S2 cohorts: partial measurement invariance with unconstrained means fit better (RMSEA = .048, CFI = .931) than full invariance, so "the same dimensional structure of DL was adopted for both age cohorts".

> "The assumption of full measurement invariance was not quite supported (RMSEA = .056, CFI = .914, SABIC = 15,963), but a model where all parameters except the mean levels of student performance were held equal showed a much better fit to the data (RMSEA = .048, CFI = .931, SABIC = 15,710)."
