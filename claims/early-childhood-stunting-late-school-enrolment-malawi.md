## Evidence

### Sunny BS 2018

Sunny BS, DeStavola B, Dube A, Kondowe S, Crampin AC, Glynn JR. (2018). Does early linear growth failure influence later school performance? A cohort study in Karonga district, northern Malawi. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0200380

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cohort analysis of 1,044 children born 2002–2004 in Karonga district, Malawi, using multinomial logistic regression adjusted for sex, parental education and household asset index. The article reports "30% less likely (aOR = 0.66) to be underage at enrolment" and "about twice as likely (aOR = 1.85) to be overage than on time".

> "Compared to those who were not stunted, those stunted in early childhood were 30% less likely (aOR = 0.66) to be underage at enrolment, and about twice as likely (aOR = 1.85) to be overage than on time at the point of entry, after controlling for potential confounders."
