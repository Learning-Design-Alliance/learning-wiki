## Evidence

### Howe 2018

Howe, C., Hennessy, S., Mercer, N., Vrikki, M., & Wheatley, L. (2018). Teacher-Student Dialogue during Classroom Teaching: Does it Really Impact upon Student Outcomes? To appear in The Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://gtr.ukri.org/projects?ref=ES/M007103/1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Validation exercise in which five transcripts varying in Elaborated, Reasoned and the two Ratios were ranked for educationally productive dialogue by eight leading scholars who had endorsed most themes. The mean rank order correlated "rho) .76" with the Ratio-predicted order versus .23 for absolute frequencies.

> "The mean rank order across scholars correlated (Spearman rho) .76 with the rank order predicted by the Ratios but only .23 with the rank order predicted by the absolute frequencies."
