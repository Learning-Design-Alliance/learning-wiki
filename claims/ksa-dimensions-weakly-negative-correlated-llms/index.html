Correlation analysis across ten evaluation dimensions over seven frontier models (Pearson r across 7 models, Figure 6c). The article reports "r=−0.51" between Knowledge and Skills, r=−0.63 between Knowledge and Attitude, and r=0.22 between Skills and Attitude; no significance tests are printed.

"The cor- relation between Knowledge (average of Content and Peda- gogy) and Skills (average of six centers) isr=−0.51, indi- cating that models strong in factual knowledge do not neces- sarily excel in practical educational tasks."
