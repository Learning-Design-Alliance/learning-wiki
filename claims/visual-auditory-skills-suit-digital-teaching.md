## Subclaims

## Evidence

### O’Brien A 2023

O’Brien A, Forde C. (2023). Health science staff and student experiences of teaching and assessing clinical skills using digital tools: a qualitative study. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2023.2256656

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative findings from the DEPTH interview study: participants described competency in skills needing "visual and auditory feedback" taught via recorded materials and videoconferencing, with students reporting confidence in auscultation and ketone analysis. No effect size is reported.

> "Clinical skills requiring visual and auditory feedback could be effectively taught using visual and auditory resources. Students felt confident performing ketone analysis and auscultations after watching and listening to recorded online materials"
