# Adding peripheral data raises specific transition probabilities in the SRL process map, e.g., Elaboration-to-Monitoring from 24% to 44%

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The HC.E to MC.M transition probability rose from 24% (Nav_only) to 44% (Enhanced_log); First-reading to Organisation rose from under 1% to 16%, and back to First-reading from under 1% to 79%. [→ Yizhou Fan 2022](#yizhou-fan-2022)

## Evidence

### Yizhou Fan 2022

Yizhou Fan, Lyn Lim, Joep van der Graaf, Jonathan Kilgour, Mladen Raković, Johanna Moore, Inge Molenaar, Maria Bannert, Maria Bannert, Dragan Gašević. (2022). Improving the measurement of self-regulated learning using multi-channel data. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09304-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of first-order Markov models built with pMineR from the three channels for all 25 participants. Transition probabilities were used for comparison because raw transition counts increase mechanically when channels are added.

> "the probability of transition from Elaboration (HC.E) to Monitoring (MC.M) increased from 24% (based on Nav_only) to 44% (based on Enhanced_log)."
