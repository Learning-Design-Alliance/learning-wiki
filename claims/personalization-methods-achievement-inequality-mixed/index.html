von Stumm 2021¶

von Stumm, S. & Wertz, J. (2021). Who's learning? Using within-family studies to understand personalized learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00082-4

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The review attributes this mixed-evidence point to prior literature (citing Hanushek & Wößmann for tracking and McQuarrie et al. for differentiated instruction); the commentary itself reports no data. It frames personalized learning as having been hailed as an approach to reduce educational inequities.

"some methods of personalizing education, for example, ability tracking or streaming, might increase achievement gaps and educational inequality 3, although the empirical evidence for this is mixed 20"

  • The review identifies data privacy, algorithmic fairness, academic integrity, and unequal access as interconnected ethical concerns in AI-supported mathematics education — related
  • Differentiated instruction and tracking are studied as both remedy and catalyst for unequal achievement depending on discipline — related
