### Buljan Ivan 2021

Buljan Ivan, Marušić Matko, Tokalić Ružica, Viđak Marin, Peričić Tina Poklepović, Hren Darko, Marušić Ana. (2021). Cognitive levels in testing knowledge in evidence-based medicine: a cross sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02449-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional test administration comparing proportions of participants reaching the minimum passing score: 26 of 119 first-year students (21.8%, 95% CI = 14.3 to 32.0%), 14 of 70 third-year students (20.0%, 95% CI = 10.9 to 33.6%), and 9 of 14 experts (64.3%, 95% CI = 38.8 to 83.7%). "A significantly higher proportion of experts achieved the MPS in comparison with first- and third-year students."

> "A significantly higher proportion of experts achieved the MPS in comparison with first- and third-year students (χ2 = 13.3; P < 0.001, Bonferroni Holm post-hoc comparison)."
