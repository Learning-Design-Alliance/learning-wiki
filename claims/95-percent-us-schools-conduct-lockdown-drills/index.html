A research brief reporting national prevalence, citing Musu-Gillette et al. (2018): "95 percent of U.S. schools perform lockdown drills as part of their emergency response plans each year". The number of required drills varies by state; the brief gives New York's requirement of four annually as an example.

"Today, 95 percent of U.S. schools perform lockdown drills as part of their emergency response plans each year (Musu-Gillette et al., 2018), though the number required varies by state."
