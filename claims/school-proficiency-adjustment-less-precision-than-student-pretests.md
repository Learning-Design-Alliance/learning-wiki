Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The report compares precision gains from covariate adjustment using student-level pretest scores against gains from publicly available school-level proficiency data, drawing on five large-scale randomized control trials conducted for IES. It reports that school-level proficiency adjustment performs worse on average, with no effect size printed.

> "On average, adjusting for school-level proficiency does not increase statistical precision as well as student-level baseline test scores."
