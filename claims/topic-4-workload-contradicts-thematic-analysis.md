## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In both academic years, the multilevel regression results for Topic 4 contradicted the thematic analysis: perceived workload was not positively associated with the topic probability of the topic named 'Dissatisfaction with workload', leading the authors to recommend discarding or further investigating that topic. ## Evidence

### Hujala 2020

Hujala, M., Knutas, A., Hynninen, T., & Arminen, H. (2020). Improving the quality of teaching by utilising written student feedback: A streamlined process. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103965

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multilevel regression analysis of the 2017-18 sample showing that the estimated coefficient for perceived workload on Topic 4's probability was not statistically significant, contradicting the thematic analysis.
