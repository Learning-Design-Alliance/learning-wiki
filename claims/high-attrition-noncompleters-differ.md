## Evidence

### Sabatini John P. 2011

Sabatini John P., Shore Jane, Holtzman Steven, Scarborough Hollis S. (2011). Relative Effectiveness of Reading Intervention Programs for Adults with Low Literacy. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3269067/

`q3 · i?`

Of 300 adults assigned to supplemental interventions, 148 completed at least 10 sessions and all post-tests while 152 dropped out before post-testing. "about 50% of the students dropped out of interventions before final post-testing," so results apply only to persisters.

> "For example, in the present study, about 50% of the students dropped out of interventions before final post-testing. Thus, we interpreted our results only with respect to adults who persisted in our implemented programs."

### Sabatini John P. 2011 (2)

Sabatini John P., Shore Jane, Holtzman Steven, Scarborough Hollis S. (2011). Relative Effectiveness of Reading Intervention Programs for Adults with Low Literacy. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3269067/

`q3 · i?`

Table 2 group comparisons show "The Non-Completers were significantly lower in Word Attack than the Non-Intervention group, but otherwise their mean scores resembled those for the Non-Intervention group." Attrition rates were similar across the three interventions.

> "The Non-Completers were significantly lower in Word Attack than the Non-Intervention group, but otherwise their mean scores resembled those for the Non-Intervention group."
