# Self-reported motivation to use virtual patients showed no significant changes from pre- to post-test, with some items scoring lower after training

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There were no significant changes between pre- and post-test self-reports of motivation to use VPs for training among the 11 PCPs. [→ Ekblad Solvig 2013](#ekblad-solvig-2013)
`q2 i?` Motivation items 9, 11 and 13 scored lower in the post-test, which the authors interpret as an expertise reversal effect driven by high pre-training expectations. [→ Ekblad Solvig 2013 (2)](#ekblad-solvig-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Ekblad Solvig 2013

Ekblad Solvig, Mollica Richard F, Fors Uno, Pantziaras Ioannis, Lavelle James. (2013). Educational potential of a virtual patient system for caring for traumatized patients in primary care. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-110

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive pre/post Likert questionnaire (N = 11) on motivation to use VPs; the article reports "no significant changes between the pre- and post-tests" and notes high pre-test values on several items.

> "Table 2 shows results of self-reports of motivation to use the VP before and after the simulation exercise. There were no significant changes between the pre- and post-tests."

### Ekblad Solvig 2013 (2)

Ekblad Solvig, Mollica Richard F, Fors Uno, Pantziaras Ioannis, Lavelle James. (2013). Educational potential of a virtual patient system for caring for traumatized patients in primary care. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-110

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of post-test reductions on items 9, 11 and 13 (understanding mental health, social problems, better care for all patients), attributed to the expertise reversal effect in experienced learners; no effect size is printed.

> "The reduction in post motivation scores in items 9, 11 & 13 seems to show that the VP training was negative in some aspects, mainly because of the high expectations before the training procedure. This is called expertise reversal effect"
