Systematic review following Cochrane guidelines of 21 peer-reviewed articles on serious games or gamified apps in medical education (2011–2015), scored with MERSQI. The review reports articles "averaged 10.8 points" of a maximum 18, range 4.5–15.5.

> "According to Table 2, the maximum MERSQI score allowed is 18 points. The articles averaged 10.8 points; the highest score for an article was 15.5, and the lowest was 4.5."
