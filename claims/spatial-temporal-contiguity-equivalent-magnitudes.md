## Evidence

### Ginns 2006

Ginns, P. (2006). Integrating information: A meta-analysis of the spatial contiguity and temporal contiguity effects. Learning and Instruction, 16(6), 511-525. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2006.10.001

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis comparing the 13 temporal contiguity effects with the 37 spatial contiguity effects in the meta-analysis. The article reports "zcontrast = 0.63, p = 0.526", a non-significant difference between the two types of contiguity.

> "the mean weighted effect size for the temporal contiguity studies ( d = 0.78, 95% c onfidence interval 0.63 – 0.92) did not differ significantly from that for spatial contiguity effect studies (d = 0.72, 95% confidence interval 0.61 – 0.82), zcontrast = 0.63, p = 0.526."
