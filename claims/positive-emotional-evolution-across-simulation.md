### García Carpintero-Blas E 2025

García Carpintero-Blas E, Del Pozo-Herce P, Hernández-Cediel MDC, Rodríguez-García M, Navas-Echazarreta N, Chover-Sierra E, Martínez-Sabater A, Juárez-Vela R, Tovar-Reinoso A. (2025). First Contact With Care Through Clinical Simulation in Nursing Students: Qualitative Study. JMIR Nursing. https://doi.org/10.2196/81617

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group and reflective-narrative data from the same qualitative study of 59 first-year nursing students. The thematic analysis reports "a transition from initial nervousness to a greater sense of calm, control, and security," attributing it to progressive familiarization, debriefing, and emotional self-regulation strategies such as conscious breathing.

> "As the SBE progressed, a process of positive emotional evolution was evident in many participants, characterized by a transition from initial nervousness to a greater sense of calm, control, and security."
