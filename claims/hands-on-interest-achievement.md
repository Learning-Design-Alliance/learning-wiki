A narrative review section reports, citing Häussler et al. (1998), Swarat et al. (2012), Wise & Okey (1983) and Schroeder et al. (2007), that hands-on activities heighten interest and enhance achievement; the article prints no effect sizes for these findings.

> "They cite studies by Häussler and colleagues (1998) and Swarat and colleagues (2012) to confirm that interest in science, in particular, is heightened by hands-on activities. Beyond interest, science achievement is also strongly enhanced by hands-on activity"
