Educator Diversity State Profile: Tennessee 2020¶

Educator Diversity State Profile: Tennessee. (2020). EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED622856

q1 · i? · associational · r1

The brief's opening framing statement asserts, citing research without citation details, that "TEACHER DIVERSITY BENEFITS ALL STUDENTS, REGARDLESS OF RACE OR ETHNICITY" and contrasts this with a national teaching force that is only about 20% of color. No study design or effect size is printed.

"RESEARCH SAYS THAT TEACHER DIVERSITY BENEFITS ALL STUDENTS, REGARDLESS OF RACE OR ETHNICITY. HOWEVER, WHILE THE MAJORITY OF STUDENTS IN THE U.S. ARE OF COLOR, ONLY ABOUT 20% OF TEACHERS ARE OF COLOR."
