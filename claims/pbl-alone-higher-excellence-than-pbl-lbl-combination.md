## Evidence

### Zhang Yanqi 2015

Zhang Yanqi, Zhou Liang, Liu Xiaoyu, Liu Ling, Wu Yazhou, Zhao Zengwei, Yi Dali, Yi Dong. (2015). The Effectiveness of the Problem-Based Learning Teaching Model for Use in Introductory Chinese Undergraduate Medical Courses: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0120884

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis of excellence rates by teaching pattern. The PBL vs LBL subgroup (3 studies) showed RR = 2.02 (95% CI 1.21–3.39) with moderate heterogeneity (I2 = 57%); the PBL+LBL vs LBL subgroup showed RR = 1.57 (95% CI 1.22–2.03).

> "random effects model results reveal that the PBL model generated higher course examination excellence rates than the traditional teaching model [RR: 2.02, 95% CI: (1.21, 3.39)]"
