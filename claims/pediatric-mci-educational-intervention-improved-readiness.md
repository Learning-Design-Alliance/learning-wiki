# A structured educational intervention improved pediatric emergency nurses' mass casualty incident readiness

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quality improvement project with pre–post intervention design, one group (Melony Murray 2025, United States), including didactic review of MCI protocols, review of supply locations, and functional simulation drills focused on zone leader roles, among pediatric ED registered nurses (pre n = 63; post n = 64). The review reports "p < 0.001"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Statistically significant improvement in knowledge (p < 0.001); 92% correctly identified supply location; 98% correctly identified zone leader role; 100% reported at least neutral preparedness; >85% achieved <10‐minute triage setup benchmark."
