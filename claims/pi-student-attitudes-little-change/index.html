Evidence¶

Crouch 2003¶

Crouch, C. H., Watkins, J., Fagen, A. P., & Mazur, E. (2003). Peer Instruction: Engaging Students One-on-One, All At Once. Research-Based Reform of University Physics. https://scholar.google.com

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Attitude survey using the concept and reality link clusters of the MPEX administered to students in the 1998 algebra-based course before and after the semester. Favorable responses "remained exactly the same" at 68% and 67%, with unfavorable responses increasing slightly; the authors conclude there was very little change in class attitudes.

"we found that the percentage of favorable responses remained exactly the same from the pre-course to the post-course survey (68% for concepts and 67% for reality link)"
