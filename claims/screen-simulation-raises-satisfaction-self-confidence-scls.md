## Evidence

### Bayram Hilal Çelik 2025

Bayram Hilal Çelik, Ergün Sibel. (2025). Effect of screen-based simulation on pediatric drug administration among nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2025-0249en

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison in the randomized controlled trial (n = 99 per group) using the Student Satisfaction and Self Confidence in Learning Scale (Cronbach's alpha 0.93 in this study). Independent-samples t-test significant at p < 0.05 (t:–15.61; p:0.00 in Table 3).

> "It was determined that the SCLS overall post-test mean scores of the intervention group (60.13 ± 5.09) were higher than those of the control group (45.53 ± 7.79)"

### Bayram Hilal Çelik 2025 (2)

Bayram Hilal Çelik, Ergün Sibel. (2025). Effect of screen-based simulation on pediatric drug administration among nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2025-0249en

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-test within the intervention group (n = 99) at the 20-day post-test in the randomized controlled trial. The general SCLS repeated-measures result is t:–15.94; p:0.01 in Table 3. No effect size printed.

> "A statistically significant difference was found between the SCLS general pre- and post-test mean scores of the intervention group (p < 0.05). When the mean values were examined, it was determined that the pre-test mean scores of the intervention group (44.35 ± 9.10) were lower than the post-test mean scores (60.13 ± 5.09)"
