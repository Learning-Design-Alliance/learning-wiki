# Teacher-child closeness shows concurrent benefits but no prospective spillover to other domains once conflict covariation is controlled

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Portilla Ximena A. 2014

Portilla Ximena A., Ballard Parissa J., Adler Nancy E., Boyce W. Thomas, Obradović Jelena. (2014). An Integrative View of School Functioning: Transactions between Self-Regulation, School Engagement, and Teacher-Child Relationship Quality. Child development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12259

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-time covariation results (Table 4) showed closeness related to engagement concurrently at each wave but the theorized prospective benefits of closeness did not emerge in cross-domain pathways; the authors attribute prior positive findings to uncontrolled conflict covariation.

> "closeness was positively related to school engagement at all three time points (kindergarten fall: B = .10, β = .32, p < .001; kindergarten spring: B = .10, β = .42, p < .001; 1st grade: B = .14, β = .38, p < .001) but negatively related to inattention and impulsivity only at the end of 1st grade"
