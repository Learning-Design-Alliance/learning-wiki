# Sound Partners tutoring has positive effects on the alphabetics of beginning readers in grades K–3

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across seven studies meeting WWC standards, Sound Partners produced positive effects in the alphabetics domain, with an average improvement index of +21 percentile points. [→ Sound Partners 2010](#sound-partners-2010)

## Evidence

### Sound Partners 2010

Sound Partners. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Sound Partners. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.wri-edu.org/partners/sound-partners.htm

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The WWC's synthesis of seven studies (four RCTs meeting standards, three meeting standards with reservations) covering 442 kindergarten and first-grade students reports that "the average improvement index for alphabetics is +21 percentile points across the seven studies."

> "The average improvement index for alphabetics is +21 percentile points across the seven studies, with a range of –6 to +39 percentile points across findings."
