# Generative instructional conditions (inquiry and explanation) outperformed receptive conditions on far transfer, with inquiry best on far transfer problems

### Ton de Jong 2024

Ton de Jong, Ard W. Lazonder, Clark A. Chinn, Frank Fischer, Janice Gobert, Cindy E. Hmelo-Silver, Ken R. Koedinger, Joseph S. Krajcik, Eleni A. Kyza, Marcia C. Linn, Margus Pedaste, Katharina Scheiter, Zacharias C. Zacharia. (2024). Beyond inquiry or direct instruction: Pressing issues for designing impactful science learning opportunities. Educational Research Review, 44. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100623

`q3 · i?` · `review · r1`

A computer-delivered experiment in probability theory with 613 secondary education students compared hypermedia, observational, inquiry, and explanation-based conditions. Explanation-based students outperformed all others on the posttest, while the inquiry condition "outperformed students from all other conditions on far transfer problems."

> "students in the inquiry-based learning condition performed better than those in the hypermedia and observational learning conditions, and outperformed students from all other conditions on far transfer problems."

### Ton de Jong 2024 (2)

Ton de Jong, Ard W. Lazonder, Clark A. Chinn, Frank Fischer, Janice Gobert, Cindy E. Hmelo-Silver, Ken R. Koedinger, Joseph S. Krajcik, Eleni A. Kyza, Marcia C. Linn, Margus Pedaste, Katharina Scheiter, Zacharias C. Zacharia. (2024). Beyond inquiry or direct instruction: Pressing issues for designing impactful science learning opportunities. Educational Research Review, 44. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100623

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

A follow-up think-aloud study (N = 40) using the same four environments found students in the generative conditions engaged in significantly more elaborative processes, indicating more schema construction activity than direct-instruction students. No effect size is printed.

> "Second, in the latter two conditions the relative number of elaborative processes was significantly higher. This means that students in the generative conditions were more involved in schema construction activities (relating and integrating) than students in the two direct instruction conditions."
