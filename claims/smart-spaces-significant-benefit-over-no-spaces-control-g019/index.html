O'Hare et al. 2017¶

Dr Liam O'Hare, Dr Patrick Stark, Professor Carol McGuinness, Dr Andy Biggart, Professor Allen Thurston. (2017). Spaced Learning: The Design, Feasibility and Optimisation of SMART Spaces — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581430

Optimisation study: small RCT in 12 schools with pre- and post-testing on a bespoke measure of past GCSE science questions. The report prints the effect size "ES g = 0.19" for the content benefit over the no spaces/materials control.

"The content of the SMART Spaces programme was found to have a significant benefit over a no spaces/materials control in the optimisation study, with ES g = 0.19 on total scores on an attainment test using past GCSE questions."
