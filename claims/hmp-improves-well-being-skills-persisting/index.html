Evidence¶

Hirshberg Matthew J. 2022¶

Hirshberg Matthew J., Frye Corrina, Dahl Cortland J., Riordan Kevin M., Vack Nathan J., Sachs Jane, Goldman Robin, Davidson Richard J., Goldberg Simon B. (2022). A Randomized Controlled Trial of a Smartphone-Based Well-Being Training in Public School System Employees During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000739

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

Piecewise linear mixed effects models with FDR correction on the N=662 intention-to-treat sample. "assignment to HMP predicted significant improvements compared to the WLC" in mindful action (d=0.21), a small effect.

"assignment to HMP predicted significant improvements compared to the WLC in mindful action (d=0.21 [0.06 to 0.36], pFDR=.001)"

Hirshberg Matthew J. 2022 (2)¶

Hirshberg Matthew J., Frye Corrina, Dahl Cortland J., Riordan Kevin M., Vack Nathan J., Sachs Jane, Goldman Robin, Davidson Richard J., Goldberg Simon B. (2022). A Randomized Controlled Trial of a Smartphone-Based Well-Being Training in Public School System Employees During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000739

q3 · i2 · causal · r3

Same FDR-corrected mixed effects models on N=662 at post-test. Effects on self-compassion (d=0.40), loneliness (d=−0.34), and cognitive defusion (d=0.40) were medium magnitude; perseverative thinking (d=−0.35), well-being (d=0.42), and meaning in life (d=0.28) also improved.

"self-compassion (d=0.40 [0.25 to 0.55], pFDR<.001), loneliness (d=−0.34 [−0.49 to −0.19], pFDR<.001), cognitive defusion (d=0.40 [0.25 to 0.56], pFDR<.001)"

Follow-up (T6) estimates from the same N=662 models: "all significant HMP group intervention period improvements persisted over the three-month follow-up" (e.g., self-compassion d=0.26), except mindful action (d=0.14, pFDR=.074).

"all significant HMP group intervention period improvements persisted over the three-month follow-up (self-compassion d=0.26 [0.11 to 0.41], pFDR=.003; loneliness d=−0.32 [−0.47 to −0.17], pFDR=.002; cognitive defusion d=0.35 [0.20 to 0.50], pFDR=.002"

Related Claims¶

  • Most participants assigned to the app downloaded it, but fewer engaged, averaging about 11 days and 128 minutes of practice over four weeks — related
  • A four-week smartphone meditation app reduces psychological distress in school system employees at post-intervention and three-month follow-up — related
  • The meditation app showed no evidence of elevated adverse events, with lower odds of symptom worsening at post-test — related
  • A practice brief reviewing self-regulation interventions reports substantial benefits of mindfulness programs for cognitive and emotional regulation, stress and mental health in adolescents and young adults — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Mindfulness and meditation interventions supported teacher well-being in 10 of 12 studies, with two studies showing non-significant changes — a broader claim this one bears on
