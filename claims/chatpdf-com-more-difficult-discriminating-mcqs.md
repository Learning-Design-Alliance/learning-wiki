# ChatPDF.com generates more difficult and discriminating MCQs than ChatGPT 3.5 and ChatGPT 4

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ChatPDF.com had a lower difficulty index (easier questions) and higher item discrimination index than ChatGPT 3.5 and ChatGPT 4. [→ Riehm Lauren 2026](#riehm-lauren-2026)

## Evidence

### Riehm Lauren 2026

Riehm Lauren, Nanji Keean, Lakhani Moiz, Pankiv Evelina, Hasanee Dean, Pfeifer Wesla. (2026). The use of large language models in generating multiple choice questions for health professions education: A systematic review and network meta-analysis. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0340277

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis found significant differences in difficulty index (chi-squared: 116.72; df = 3, p < 0.01). Post-hoc tests showed "ChatPDF.com had a lower difficulty index than ChatGPT 3.5 (p < 0.001), ChatGPT 4 (p = 0.010), and humans (p < 0.001)". ChatPDF.com also had higher discrimination.

> "Exploratory post-hoc t-tests demonstrated that ChatPDF.com had a lower difficulty index than ChatGPT 3.5 (p < 0.001), ChatGPT 4 (p = 0.010), and humans (p < 0.001)."
