# Student burnout is positively associated with dropout intentions, with a pooled meta-analytic correlation of r+ = .46

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across 37 effect sizes from studies of dropout intentions, burnout showed a significant positive pooled association with dropout intentions (r+ = .46, 95% CI = .44 to .55). [→ Daniel J. Madigan 2026](#daniel-j-madigan-2026)

## Evidence

### Daniel J. Madigan 2026

Daniel J. Madigan, Hanna L. Glandorf. (2026). Student Burnout and Educational Dropout: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Dropout Behavior and Intentions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10191-y

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Multilevel meta-analysis pooling Fisher's z converted to Pearson's r from 37 effect sizes on dropout intentions. The printed effect size is r+ = .46 (95% CI = .44 to .55, p < .001), which the authors characterize as large and comparable to meta-analyses in workplace contexts.

> "The overall pooled effect for the association between burnout and dropout intentions was r+ = .46 (95% CI = .44 to .55, p < .001, z+ = .49, k = 37, see Figure S1 for forest plot)."
