### Mahamad 2025

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Case study of a MOOC platform (akin to edX) using Transformer-based NLP for automated grading of open-ended responses in courses spanning 2021 to 2023, reporting "a 70% reduction in grading time and a 15% increase in learner satisfaction". Percentages are reported outcomes; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Outcomes included a 70% reduction in grading time and a 15% increase in learner satisfaction, but addressing bias and computational efficiency remains critical for future iterations."
