## Evidence

### Vadla MS 2023

Vadla MS, Mduma ER, Kvaløy JT, Mdoe P, Hhoki BH, Sarangu S, Michael P, Oftedal B, Ersdal H. (2023). Increase in Newborns Ventilated Within the First Minute of Life and Reduced Mortality After Clinical Data-Guided Simulation Training. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000740

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of resuscitation practices across the four study periods among all 12,678 births, tested with chi-square (P < 0.001 for each measure). The article reports stimulation, suction, and BMV all "increased from baseline to the intervention period" and reversed afterward.

> "Both stimulation (34.5%–40.4%), suction (28.1%–31.4%), and BMV (8.5%–12.9%) increased from baseline to the intervention period (Table 3). Stimulation (40.4%–37.2%–26.2%) and BMV (12.9%–10.6%–9.4%) decreased from the intervention period to postintervention (Table 3)."
