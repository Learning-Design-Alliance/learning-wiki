## Evidence

### Katz 2013

Jennifer Katz. (2013). The Three Block Model of Universal Design for Learning (UDL): Engaging students in inclusive education. Canadian Journal of Education, 36(1). https://journals.sfu.ca/cje/index.php/cje

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA of observed engaged behavior by grade in the same quasi-experiment; Table 7 reports significant treatment effects for both elementary (partial η = .528 overall) and secondary (partial η = .691 overall) groupings.

> "However, student grade levels did impact engaged behavior, F (2, 85) = 10.977, p < .001, such that students in higher grades spent more time passively engaged"

### Katz 2013 (2)

Jennifer Katz. (2013). The Three Block Model of Universal Design for Learning (UDL): Engaging students in inclusive education. Canadian Journal of Education, 36(1). https://journals.sfu.ca/cje/index.php/cje

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Examination of means for secondary students in the same study; the article notes the secondary sample was smaller, which may have mitigated some results, and that students in secondary UDL classes become actively engaged when tasks are assigned while control students disengage.

> "High school students in UDL classes spent 44/60 minutes actively engaged, while those in control classes spent 16/60 minutes actively engaged."
