### Tiffany Jones and Andrew Howard Nichols 2020

Tiffany Jones and Andrew Howard Nichols. (2020). Hard Truths: Why Only Race-Conscious Policies Can Fix Racism in Higher Education. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED603265

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ed Trust analysis of the U.S. Department of Education's 2012-13 Beginning Postsecondary Students Longitudinal Study, Second Follow-up (BPS:12/17), for dependent full-time, first-time students at four-year institutions. The analysis found "In all four income groups, White students are at least 11 percentage points more likely to complete a college degree than Black students." The largest gap was 17 points in the lower middle income group (67% vs. 50%).

> "In all four income groups, White students are at least 11 percentage points more likely to complete a college degree than Black students at four-year institutions (Table 1)."
