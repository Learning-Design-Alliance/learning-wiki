Chaiwat Waree 2017¶

Chaiwat Waree. (2017). An Increasing of Primary School Teachers' Competency in Brain-Based Learning. International Education Studies, 10(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n3p176

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Expert panel evaluation of the developed CBT curriculum, reported descriptively with no statistics. The article states contents had the highest quality, followed by training duration, evaluation, supporting documents, objectives, structure, and media.

"From the results of evaluation of experts on the efficiency of CBT curriculum implementation of primary teachers on brain-based learning management, it was found that, in overall, the efficiency of curriculum implementation was in the highest level."
