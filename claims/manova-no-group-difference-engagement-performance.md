## Evidence

### Angela Y. He 2017

Angela Y. He. (2017). Overcoming Barriers to Self-Directed Student Engagement with Educational Games: A Mixed-Methods Case Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.04491](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.04491)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Quantitative group comparison in the results: group A (five students) played the static-visual-novel version and group B (five students) the more interactive version of Grey Plague. The article reports the omnibus test was not significant, and univariate tests showed "a stronger effect on engagement (F(1, 9) = 1.42, p = 0.27) than on performance (F(1, 9) = 0.70, p = 0.43)."

> "A one-way MANOVA, which indicated no significant difference between groups on engagement or performance (F(2, 8) = 0.79, p=0.49), substantiates the supposition of difference inadequacy between games."
