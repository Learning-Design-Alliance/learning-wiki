`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation comparing this RCT's effect sizes with prior First Step RCTs (e.g., Feil et al., 2014, where teacher-measure effect sizes ranged approximately 0.7 to 0.9 and parent measures 0.3 to 0.4). This is an interpretive claim, not a direct statistical comparison within the study.
