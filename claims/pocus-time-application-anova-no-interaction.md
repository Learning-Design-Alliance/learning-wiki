## Evidence

### Buhumaid R 2025

Buhumaid R, Kilian P, Zidan MA, Salman H, AlSharhan R, Khansaheb H, Zary N. (2025). From novice to proficient: a longitudinal study of POCUS skill development through a hybrid certification program in the United Arab Emirates. BMC Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07138-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-way repeated measures ANOVA with time and POCUS application as within-subjects factors, run separately for confidence and UCAT scores (Figs. 6 and 7). The competency analysis showed the same pattern: time significant, applications and interaction not.

> "The two-way repeated measures ANOVA analysis results indicated that there was a significant improvement in the confidence scores throughout the course time (p = 0.024), but there was no significant difference between the applications (p = 0.484), and no interaction between the time and the applications (p = 0.533)"
