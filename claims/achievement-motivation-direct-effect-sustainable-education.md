### Alismail Abdulaziz Mohammed 2025

Alismail Abdulaziz Mohammed, Almulla Mazen Omar. (2025). Exploring the interplay between achievement motivation, students’ satisfaction with their major, and successful intelligence in supporting the sustainability of university education in SAUDI ARABIA. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03835-1

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

SEM of the cross-sectional survey data (n = 529). The direct path from achievement motivation to sustainable education was significant, "β = 0.28, p <.001", with partial mediation via major satisfaction also reported.

> "Achievement motivation also predicts sustainable university education outcomes directly (β = 0.28, p <.001), but this effect is partially mediated by major satisfaction (β = 0.15, 95% CI [0.09, 0.22])."
