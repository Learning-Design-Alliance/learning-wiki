## Evidence

### Cheung H. C. Kristy 2026

Cheung H. C. Kristy, Zhong Lily Shengjia, Wall Shelley L., Lisk Kristina. (2026). Exploring the impact of virtual reality anatomy training on preparing biomedical illustrators for drawing anatomical structures. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70150

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc t-test on rubric criterion scores from the crossover study (VR_1st group). The article also reports better layout with 2D versus VR (difference = 0.56, ES = 1.18) and "no differences in performance across all modalities in terms of anatomical accuracy, style and visual clarity."

> "they were better at depicting the depth of anatomical structures when drawing using prosection compared with VR (difference = 0.62, t(7) = 3.42, p < 0.05, ES = 0.59)."
