## Evidence

### Roye-Green Karen J. 2025

Roye-Green Karen J., Vickers Ivan, Priestley Sharon, Walker Jerome, Willis Rohan. (2025). Knowledge, practice and attitudes of healthcare students to sepsis management in Jamaica. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07122-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 292 Jamaican healthcare students (210 medical, 82 nursing) using a 25-item questionnaire; four tabulated scores compared between groups with t-tests. Nursing students' "‘correct practice’ scores (6.9±2.2 vs 4.8±2.7, p < 0.001)" exceeded medical students'; incorrect practice was lower and incorrect knowledge did not differ.

> "Nursing students had higher mean ‘correct knowledge’ (3.3±1.7 vs. 2.6±1.5, p = 0.001) and ‘correct practice’ scores (6.9±2.2 vs 4.8±2.7, p < 0.001) compared to medical students."
