Thematic analysis of disclosure-response text boxes: untrained clinicians 'most often provided responses under the theme of Procedural response', referring patients to another clinician such as social work rather than responding directly.

> "Free-text respondents with No Training in family violence (18.58% of response set) most often provided responses under the theme of Procedural response, indicating that they would respond to a disclosure of family violence by referring the patient to another clinician (i.e. social work)."
