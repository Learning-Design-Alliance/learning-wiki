`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Process evaluation based on surveys returned by 12 headteachers, 16 mentors, and 24 teachers, plus telephone interviews with six teachers, eight mentors, and three headteachers, and case-study visits to three intervention schools. Findings are self-reported perceptions, not tested attainment effects.

> "The process evaluation showed that the Dialogic Teaching approach was highly valued by participating schools. Teachers reported positive effects on pupil engagement and confidence."
