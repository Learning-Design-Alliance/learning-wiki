### Ishizuka Kosuke 2026

Ishizuka Kosuke, Nishizaki Yuji, Sekine Miwa, Shimizu Taro, Shikino Kiyoshi, Yamamoto Yu, Fukui Sho, Nojima Masanori, Ohta Mitsuyasu, Tokuda Yasuharu. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across the three regional groups on the Disease-Specific Topics category of the 2023 GM-ITE; Table 2 reports means of 19.9 ± 3.6 (Japan), 17.3 ± 2.7 (Non-Japan Asia), and 19.1 ± 4.2 (Europe and Other).

> "However, a statistically significant difference among the three regional groups was observed in the Disease-Specific Topics category (p = 0.003), with higher mean scores in the Japan and Europe and Other groups than in the Non-Japan Asia group."
