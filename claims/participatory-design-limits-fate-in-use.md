### Bhimdiwala 2022

Bhimdiwala, A., Neri, R. C., & Gomez, L. M. (2022). Advancing the Design and Implementation of Artificial Intelligence in Education through Continuous Improvement. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00278-8

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument from the Current Landscape of FATE in AIEd section. The authors state these approaches engage users "primarily in early stages of the design process" and argue that FATE solutions will also require practice-related adaptations built on local expertise.

> "this set of approaches tends to engage users primarily in early stages of the design process with the goal of using feedback to redesign the technology, which can limit our ability to recognize and address FATE-related issues that arise in practice and through use."
