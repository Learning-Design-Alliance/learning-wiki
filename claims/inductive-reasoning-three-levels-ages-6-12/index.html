Evidence¶

Demetriou 2011¶

Demetriou, A., Spanoudis, G., & Mouyi, A. (2011). Educating the Developing Mind: Towards an Overarching Paradigm. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-011-9178-3

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review reports, citing Mouyi 2008, a detailed reasoning study finding "three main levels" of inductive reasoning between ages 6 and 12: single-dimension patterns, hidden or implied relations, and theoretical supposition. No effect size is printed.

"Mouyi ( 2008) (Demetriou et al . 2010a, b) showed, in a very detailed study of reasoning, that inductive reasoning develops in three main levels from the age of 6 to the age of 12 years."
