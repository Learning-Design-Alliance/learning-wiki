## Evidence

### Matos Jason 2017

Matos Jason, Petri Camille R., Mukamal Kenneth J., Vanka Anita. (2017). Spaced education to improve competency in medical knowledge. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0181418

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized, self-matched trial at a single academic medical center in which Pearls were randomly assigned to reinforcement. Results show "the relative risk of answering a reinforced question incorrectly" was 0.90 (95% CI, 0.79–1.08; p = 0.14), not significant.

> "In analyses accounting for clustering and adjusted for post-graduate year, the relative risk of answering a reinforced question incorrectly compared with an unreinforced question was 0.90 (95% CI, 0.79–1.08; p = 0.14)."
