# Prior knowledge (portion A) scores correlate with transfer (portion C) scores for Fraction Division but not for Fraction Generalization

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The correlation between prior knowledge and transfer portions was significant for Fraction Division (p < 0.001) but not significant for Fraction Generalization. [→ Roschelle 2020](#roschelle-2020)

## Evidence

### Roschelle 2020

Roschelle, J., Cheng, B. H., Hodkowski, N., Haldar, L., & Neisler, J. (2020). Transfer for future learning of fractions within Cignition's microtutoring approach [Project Report]. Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/95

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Exploratory correlational analysis examining internal validity of the TFL task structure. The relationship "was not significant for the "Fraction Generalization" task but was significant for the "Fraction Division" task, p < 0.001."

> "Similarly, the relationship between the prior knowledge (portion A) of the task and only the transfer (portion C) of the task, the relationship was not significant for the "Fraction Generalization" task but was significant for the "Fraction Division" task, p < 0.001."
