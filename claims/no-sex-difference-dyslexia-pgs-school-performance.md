`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sex-stratified ordinal mixed-effect models with a PGS*sex interaction term tested whether PGS effects on school performance differed between males and females in the INSchool cohort. No significant interaction was found for any partition.

> "When stratifying by sex, no differences were found on the effect of the genetic liability of dyslexia dissected by its relationship with EA on school performance (Interaction P-value > 0.05)"
