## Evidence

### Beaumont 2022

Beaumont, J., Putwain, D. W., Gallard, D., Malone, E., Marsh, H. W., & Pekrun, R. (2022, in press). Students' emotion regulation and school-related wellbeing: Longitudinal models juxtaposing between- and within-person perspectives. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://osf.io/W5CPE

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Cross-lagged paths from the three-wave CLPM among 2,365 students; the RI-CLPM showed the same directional pattern. The article had left the reappraisal-suppression link as an exploratory question because prior adolescent findings were inconsistent. No standardized coefficient is printed for this path in the provided text.
