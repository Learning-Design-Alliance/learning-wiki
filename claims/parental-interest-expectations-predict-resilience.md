## Evidence

### Yan Yan 2022

Yan Yan, Gai Xiaosong. (2022). High Achievers from Low Family Socioeconomic Status Families: Protective Factors for Academically Resilient Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315882

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression (Table 4): interest-based expectation OR = 1.14 (grade 6) and 1.11 (grade 9), both p < 0.05; grade 11 OR = 1.09, p = 0.174. Contribution-based expectation was non-significant in all grades. No standardized effect size is printed.
