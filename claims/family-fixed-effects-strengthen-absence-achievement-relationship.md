## Evidence

### Michael A. Gottfried 2011

Michael A. Gottfried. (2011). The Detrimental Effects of Missing School: Evidence from Urban Siblings. American Journal of Education 117(2). https://doi.org/10.1086/657886

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Family fixed effects models on the sibling sample for SAT9 reading (n = 6,048), with grade, year, and school fixed effects added across specifications. The article reports coefficients on absences are larger in the family fixed effects table than in the baseline table, with effect sizes hovering around ⫺0.08j, and that "those unobserved influences relating to the family or household tend to underestimate the true negative estimate of missing school."

> "after controlling for those household unobservable factors through family fixed effects models, the relationship between absences and achievement becomes even stronger."
