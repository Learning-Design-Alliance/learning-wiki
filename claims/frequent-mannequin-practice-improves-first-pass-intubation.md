## Evidence

### Agrawal 2026

Agrawal, S., Rakshasbhuvankar, A., Rath, C., Brown, J. A., McEvoy, P. M., Rao, S., & Patole, S. (2026). Neonatal endotracheal intubation: development of a comprehensive educational program for procedural skills. Pediatric Research, 99, 484–489. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-025-04217-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective study in which 16 medical staff at a mixed neonatal and paediatric retrieval service performed simulated intubation at the start of each retrieval shift, compared with a historical cohort. "First-pass intubation rates improved from 59% to 78% in neonatal patients (P = 0.032)"; no effect size is printed.

> "First-pass intubation rates improved from 59% to 78% in neonatal patients (P = 0.032), 58% to 65% in paediatric patients (P = 0.68) and from 58% to 74% overall (P = 0.043)."
