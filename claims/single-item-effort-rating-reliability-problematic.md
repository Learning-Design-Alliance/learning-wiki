## Evidence

### Scheiter 2020

Scheiter, K., Ackerman, R., & Hoogerheide, V. (2020). Looking at Mental Effort Appraisals through a Metacognitive Lens: Are they Biased? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09555-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

A reanalysis of data from Paas (1992) and Paas and van Merriënboer (1994b) determined internal consistency by administering multiple test problems with effort ratings after each. The review reports "Cronbach 's alpha across these multiple effort ratings was .90" and .84, but notes that "determining the reliability of the single-item effort appraisal is problematic."

> "Cronbach 's alpha across these multiple effort ratings was .90 (Paas 1992) and .84 (Paas and van Merriënboer 1994b), respectively."
