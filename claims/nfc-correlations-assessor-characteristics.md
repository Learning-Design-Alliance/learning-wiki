# Need for cognitive closure correlates with final ratings and rating changes differently for minority and majority students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` NFC correlated significantly and positively with final ratings of ethnic minority students for both performance levels, and for majority students correlated positively with final ratings and negatively with rating changes in descending performances; IMS, EMS and SDO showed no significant correlations with outcome variables. ## Evidence

### Steinmann JJ 2025

Steinmann JJ, Otto I, Born MP, Derous E, Woltman AM, van den Broek WW, Stegers-Jager KM. (2025). Adjustment of Assessors’ First Impressions Differs by Student Ethnicity. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2196

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory bi-variate Pearson correlation analysis of assessor factors (function, experience, NFC, SDO, IMS, EMS) with outcome variables at the individual level; coefficients are in Appendix D. The authors suggest assessors with higher need for closure were less likely to negatively adjust scores for majority students.

> "The need for cognitive closure(NFC) showed significant positive correlation with final ratings of ethnic minority students for both ascending and descending performances. For majority students, NFC correlated significantly and positively with the final ratings and negatively with the rating changes in descending performances."
