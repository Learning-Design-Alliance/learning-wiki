## Evidence

### Cuerva Marcos Javier 2026

Cuerva Marcos Javier, Durá Candela, Aramendia Andrés, Cortes Marta, Lopez Francisco, Bartha José Luis. (2026). Vacuum 5-step technique versus OdonAssist: Which is easier to learn for professionals without experience in assisted vaginal births? A simulation study. International Journal of Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70836

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Training-session-level paired comparisons (Wilcoxon signed-rank) in the same crossover study; effect sizes r = 0.74 for perceived difficulty and r = 0.57 for total errors, both favoring OdonAssist.

> "Perceived difficulty of the training session was higher for vacuum AVB compared with OdonAssist (3 (IQR 2–4) versus 2 (IQR 2–2); Z = −2.55, P = 0.011). The total number of errors per training session was also higher for vacuum AVB (1.5 (IQR 0.25–3.0) versus 1.0 (IQR 0.0–1.0); Z = −1.99, P = 0.046)"
