## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2006

Michael C. Wittmann, Jeffrey T. Morgan, Roger E. Feeley. (2006). Laboratory-Tutorial activities for teaching probability. Submitted to Physical Review Special Topics Physics Education Research. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Ungraded lab-tutorial pretest given before any instruction on probability, asking where a constantly speeding raindrop would most likely appear in 100 random photographs. The article reports "One-third of the 42 students answering the question gave the right answer with the correct reasoning," and 2 students (5%) chose the middle region B.

> "Slightly more than 1/3 (15 of 42) of the students said that the drop was equally likely to be found in each region. The reasoning given was either that equal sized bins lead to equal probabilities or that the random nature of the observation made each region equally likely."
