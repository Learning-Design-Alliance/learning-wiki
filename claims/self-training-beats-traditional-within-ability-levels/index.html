Evidence¶

Ismail et al. 2018¶

Mohd Nasir Ismail, Nurfaezah Mamat, Adnan Jamaludin. (2018). The Effects of Webopac Self Training Tool with Guided Exploration on Information Literacy Skills among First Year Degree Students. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165734

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Post hoc Tukey HSD comparisons among HLT students across the three methods (Table 6). The article reports "Mean Difference = 1.63, p-value= 0.105" for WSTTG vs WSTT, and significant differences versus T of 5.25 (p=0.000) and 3.63 (p=0.017).

"For HLT students, there is no significant difference in the gain score for information literacy skill between students taught via WSTTG and WSTT instructional methods (HLT WSTTG vs HLTWSTT, MeanHLT - WSTTG : 21.13, MeanHlT-WSTT : 19.50 ; Mean Difference = 1.63, p-value= 0.105)."

Ismail et al. 2018 (2)¶

Mohd Nasir Ismail, Nurfaezah Mamat, Adnan Jamaludin. (2018). The Effects of Webopac Self Training Tool with Guided Exploration on Information Literacy Skills among First Year Degree Students. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165734

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Post hoc Tukey HSD comparisons among LLT students across the three methods (Table 7). The article reports "Mean Difference = 0.33, p-value= 0.945, >0.05" for WSTTG vs WSTT, and significant differences versus T of 2.42 (p=0.036) and 2.09 (p=0.005).

"For LLT students, there is no significant difference in the gain score for information literacy skill between students taught via WSTTG and WSTT instructional methods (LLT WSTTG vs LLTWSTT, MeanlLT - WSTTG : 14.38, MeanLlT-WSTT: 14.05; Mean Difference = 0.33, p-value= 0.945, >0.05)"
