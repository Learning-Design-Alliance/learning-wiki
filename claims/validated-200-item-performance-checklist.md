Kazemi-Arpanahi, H. et al. (2025). Effectiveness of tailored educational content on improving nurses’ clinical performance in cardiac surgery wards: a mixed-methods study. Scientific Reports 15, 23616. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-08728-2

Checklist development phase: 350 competencies extracted from literature, reduced to 250 by Delphi (20 experts), then CVI (≥ 0.78 acceptable), Kappa (> 0.74 excellent), and CVR (> 0.51 per Lawshe Table) filtering. The article reports the final checklist was "developed with 200 items (Table 4)".
