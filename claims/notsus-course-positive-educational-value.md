## Evidence

### Aydın Abdullatif 2020

Aydın Abdullatif, Griffin Cora M., Brunckhorst Oliver, Al-Jabir Ahmed, Raison Nicholas, Aya Haleema, McIlhenny Craig, Brewin James, Shabbir Majid, Palou Redorta Joan, Khan Muhammad Shamim, Dasgupta Prokar, Ahmed Kamran. (2020). Non-technical skills for urological surgeons (NoTSUS): development and evaluation of curriculum and assessment scale. World Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-020-03406-6

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-simulation evaluation questionnaire with 5-point Likert items and comment boxes completed by course participants. The article reports a mean of "4.5 out of 5" across parameters and an average realism score of 4.4 for the full immersion simulation, with qualitative feedback describing it as "a highlight" of training.

> "The mean score on a 5-point Likert scale for all parameters asked about in the post-simulation survey was 4.5 out of 5. Individual aspects of FIS received an average mean Likert score of 4.4 for realism, including for lighting (4.24/5), sound (4.34/5), posters (4.10/5), anaesthetist (4.66/5), scrub nurse (4.56/5) and scenario (4.65/5)."
