Patrick 2024¶

Patrick, S. K. (2024). How preparation predicts teaching performance assessment results in California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/544.849

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Subsample analysis of 2,378 student teachers, 325 residents, and 780 interns from the CTC program completer survey (2021–22 and 2022–23, excluding the California State University system) linked to TPA records; figure 2 prints initial passing rates of 88%, 83%, and 72%. No effect size or significance test is printed for this contrast.

"As shown in figure 2, residents in this subsample were more likely to pass a TPA on their first attempt than student teachers or interns."
