# A school's School Climate Index is more strongly associated with its probability of beating the achievement odds than its personnel resources

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The probability of beating the odds increased exponentially with SCI, reaching 10.6 percent at two standard deviations above the mean, about ten times the average-school probability. [→ Voight 2013](#voight-2013)
`q2 i?` Increases in personnel resources were also associated with higher probabilities of beating the odds, but the effect was much smaller (2.8 percent at two standard deviations above the mean). [→ Voight 2013 (2)](#voight-2013-2)
