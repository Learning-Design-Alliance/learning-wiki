### Martínez-Martínez 2020

Martínez-Martínez, A.M.; López-Liria, R.; Aguilar-Parra, J.M.; Trigueros, R.; Morales-Gázquez, M.J.; Rocamora-Pérez, P. (2020). Relationship between Emotional Intelligence, Cybervictimization, and Academic Performance in Secondary School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health 17, 7717. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17217717

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of 3451 secondary students; the cybervictimization latent variable included impersonation, online exclusion, visual, and written/verbal cybervictimization indicators, and EI included attention, clarity, and regulation indicators.

> "In other words, EI favors academic performance of high school students and makes them less susceptible to cybervictimization."
