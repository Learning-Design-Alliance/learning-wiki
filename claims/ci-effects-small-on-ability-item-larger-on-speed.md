# Adding collateral information has little effect on ability and item parameter estimates but more effect on speed parameter estimates

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Differences among models had little effect on theta (ability) parameter estimates for both tests. [→ Wang 2011](#wang-2011)
`q2 i?` Model differences had more effect on zeta (speed) parameter estimates than on theta estimates, and small effects on item b and lambda parameters. [→ Wang 2011 (2)](#wang-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Wang 2011

Wang, S., & Jiao, H. (2011). Incorporating Person Covariates and Response Times as Collateral Information to Improve Person and Item Parameter Estimations. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, New Orleans, LA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/incorporating-person-covariates-and-response-times-as-collateral-information-to-improve-person-and-item-parameter-estimations/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison of person parameter means, standard deviations and standard errors across models (Table 3, Figures 1-2); NS34 theta was negatively skewed, LI52 theta near normal.

> "The difference among models have a little effect on theta parameter estimations for both NS34 and LI52 tests."

### Wang 2011 (2)

Wang, S., & Jiao, H. (2011). Incorporating Person Covariates and Response Times as Collateral Information to Improve Person and Item Parameter Estimations. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, New Orleans, LA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/incorporating-person-covariates-and-response-times-as-collateral-information-to-improve-person-and-item-parameter-estimations/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same descriptive comparison extended to speed parameters (Figures 3-4) and item parameters (Table 4): "For both b- and lambda-parameters, the model effects are small."

> "The difference among models have more effect on zeta parameter estimations for both NS34 and LI52 tests than that on theta parameter estimations."
