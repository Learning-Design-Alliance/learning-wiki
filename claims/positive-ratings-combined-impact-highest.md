Novel educational strategies to improve the telemedicine clinical skills of medical students. Primary Health Care Research & Development. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1463423625000040

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-study survey ratings in the pilot (n = 12; VCI ratings n = 6). All students rated the interventions as helpful and appropriate in length; the printed Likert means are descriptive and no statistical test is reported.

> "students in the OLP+VCI group indicated having both OLP and VCI improved their TM skills (2.5 on a 5-point Likert scale) compared to OLP alone (2.0 on a 5-point Likert scale) or VCI alone (2.3 on a 5-point Likert scale)."
