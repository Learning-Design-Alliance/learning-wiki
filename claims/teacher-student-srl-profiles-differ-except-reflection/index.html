Evidence¶

Avivit Arvatz 2025¶

Avivit Arvatz, Roee Peretz, Yehudit Judy Dori. (2025). Self-regulated learning and reflection: a tool for teachers and students. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09415-3

q2 · i? · design · r2

Cross-group comparison in the Discussion of the factor distributions from the teacher (N = 154) and student (N = 208) samples; the authors argue teachers' deficits in critical reflection, goal-setting and help-seeking may hinder their ability to foster students' reflective skills, establishing a negative reinforcing loop.

"Comparing the teachers' and students' high and low self-assessments of SRL skills reveals that they differ more than they are alike, except for the common insufficient practice of reflection"
