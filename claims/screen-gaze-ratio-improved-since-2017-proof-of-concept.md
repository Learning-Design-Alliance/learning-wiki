### J. Mercier 2023

J. Mercier, N. Chabloz, G. Dozot, C. Audrin, O. Ertz, E. Bocher, D. Rappo. (2023). Impact of Geolocation Data on Augmented Reality Usability: A Comparative User Test. [doi:10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors' own cross-study comparison in the conclusions, explicitly hedged as incomparable because the tests took place five years apart under different conditions. It suggests reduced screen dominance relative to the 2017 test.

> "Although the two experiments cannot be properly compared, because the tests took place 5 years apart under different conditions, we note that participants spent 69.2%–73.3% of the time looking at the tablet screen, which seems to be a meaningful longitudinal progress from the measurement that was made on our 2017 proof-of-concept, where participants interacted with the screen for 88.5 % of the time"
