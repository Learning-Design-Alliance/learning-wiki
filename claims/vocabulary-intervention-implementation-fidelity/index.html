Evidence¶

Michael R. Vitale & Nancy R. Romance 2013¶

Michael R. Vitale & Nancy R. Romance. (2013). Accelerating Vocabulary Development and Reading Comprehension in Grades 3-4-5 through an Inductive Vocabulary Model. SREE Fall 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563063

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Fidelity data obtained through classroom observation averaging 3.4 visits per teacher plus inspection of teaching plans, across 22 teachers and 39 classrooms. Mean fidelity ratings "ranged from 82 to 92"; inter-rater reliability ranged from .88 to .95.

"Mean ratings of fidelity of implementation ranged from 82 to 92 expressed on a 100 point scale, in which a rating of 80 percent or more indicated consistent model implementation."
