### Chen H 2026

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderated mediation analysis with 5,000 bootstrap samples in the 526-student survey: indirect effect significant at low mindset (0.069, CI [0.027, 0.119]) but nonsignificant at high mindset (0.004, CI [−0.033, 0.042]); the index of moderated mediation was significant (Index = −0.037, CI [−0.075, −0.008]).

> "the indirect eﬀect of AI employment threat perception on career decision-making anxiety through perceived employability was signiﬁcant under low growth mindset, Eﬀect= 0.069, Boot SE= 0.023, 95% CI [0.027, 0.119].
