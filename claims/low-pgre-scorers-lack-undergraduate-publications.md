## Evidence

### Emily M. Levesque 2015

Emily M. Levesque, Rachel Bezanson, & Grant R. Tremblay. (2015). Physics GRE Scores of Prize Postdoctoral Fellows in Astronomy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of undergraduate publication records among the survey's low-scoring respondents, compared with the overall respondent rate. The 20% rate among fellows below the 30th percentile agrees with the overall 23% (34 of respondents), so strong undergraduate records do not counteract low scores.

> "of the 15 fellows who scored <30% on the PGRE, only 3 (20%) had published one or more ﬁrst author papers during their undergraduate careers. It is also worth noting that this is in agreement with the overall percentage of fellows who reported publishing one or more ﬁrst author papers as undergraduates (34, or 23%)."
