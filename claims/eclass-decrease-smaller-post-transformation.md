### Doucette 2024

Doucette, D., D'Urso, B., & Singh, C. (2024). Lessons from Transforming Second-Year Honors Physics Lab. arXiv:2403.01525. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.01525](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.01525)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post E-CLASS survey comparison across years in the honors physics lab. The article reports that "a larger decrease in E-CLASS scores the year prior to the transformation, 2015" had "d = 0 .67" while "a smaller decrease during post-transformation years" had "d = 0.10."

> "our results suggest a larger decrease in E-CLASS scores the year prior to the transformation, 2015, (effect-size given by Cohen's d = 0 .67, t-test p = 0 .08 [33]) and a smaller decrease during post-transformation years, ( d = 0.10, p = 0.31)."
