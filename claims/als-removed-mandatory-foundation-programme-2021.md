### Baker Adam 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Narrative synthesis in this opinion article describing the post-2021 patchwork of local provision. Preparedness depends on local opportunity, with variation in study leave, simulation and standardised assessment despite ARCP requiring life support capability evidence.

> "In the best settings, foundation doctors have access to funded courses, regular simulation and supervisors who directly observe and debrief emergencies. In the worst, study leave is difficult, simulation is sporadic and resuscitation capability is inferred from portfolio entries"
