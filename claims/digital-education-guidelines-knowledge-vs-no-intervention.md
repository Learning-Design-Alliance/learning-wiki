`q3 i2` Pooled analysis of three RCTs showed a moderate, statistically significant knowledge advantage for digital education over no intervention (SMD 0.85, 95% CI 0.16–1.54, I2 83%, moderate quality). [→ Tudor Car Lorainne 2019](#tudor-car-lorainne-2019)

## Evidence

### Tudor Car Lorainne 2019

Tudor Car Lorainne, Soong Aijia, Kyaw Bhone Myint, Chua Kee Leng, Low-Beer Naomi, Majeed Azeem. (2019). Health professions digital education on clinical practice guidelines: a systematic review by Digital Health Education collaboration. BMC Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-019-1370-1

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review meta-analysis (random-effects) of three RCTs comparing digital education to no intervention on post-intervention knowledge. The review reports a "large beneficial effect" with SMD 0.85 and high heterogeneity (I2 83%), rated moderate quality by GRADE.

> "The pooled analysis of these studies showed large beneficial effect of digital education interventions for clinical practice guideline adoption on knowledge scores (SMD = 0.85, 95% CI 0.16, 1.54; I2 = 83%, moderate quality of evidence)"
