# In a pilot with 155 graders 4–6, children were overwhelmed by a large non-structured set of inquiry assignments and lacked planning skills, leading to a reduced, predefined-schedule assignment set

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Tessa H. S. Eysink 2020

Tessa H. S. Eysink, Alieke M. van Dijk, Ton de Jong. (2020). BE COOL! a digital learning environment to challenge and socially include gifted learners. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09754-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot study in which four complete sets of inquiry assignments matched to ability level were tried with 155 fourth, fifth, and sixth graders of varying ability levels, using observations and interviews with teachers and children. The article reports that "children were overwhelmed by the large non-structured set of assignments."

> "Teacher evaluations, however, showed that children were overwhelmed by the large non-structured set of assignments and did not have enough planning skills to work through the assignments as intended."
