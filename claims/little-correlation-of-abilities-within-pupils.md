## Evidence

### Chambers 1909

Chambers, W. G. (1909). Individual Differences in Grammar Grade Children: A Comparative Study of Forty-Four Seventh and Eight Grade Pupils. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070330

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Per-pupil analysis of rank variation across the ten tests, with five typical pupil profiles plotted in Fig. III. The article reports that even the least variable student "occupied every rank from IV to VIII inclusive" and that in five cases a pupil ranked first in one test and twelfth in another.

> "without exception, the abilities of each individual student in the different tests were found to be distributed through a considerable number of ranks. The student who showed least variation occupied every rank from IV to VIII inclusive."
