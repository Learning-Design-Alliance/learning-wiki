## Evidence

### Rupprecht 2017

Rupprecht, S., Paulus, P. & Walach, H. (2017). Mind the teachers! The impact of mindfulness training on self-regulation and classroom performance in a sample of German school teachers. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(4), 565-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.4.565

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Practice logs from 15 of 18 intervention participants over the 5-week course with 30 possible practice days. Sixteen of seventeen attendees (94.1%) found the programme rather or very beneficial; 82.3% would recommend it highly to fellow teachers.

> "Formal meditation exercises were practiced on average on 24.1 days (SD=5.2) or 80% of designated practice days. On average, participants eng aged in 29.7 minutes of formal practice per day (SD=10.2)."
