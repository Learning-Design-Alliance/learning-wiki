## Evidence

### Stephen Akinyemi Omotayo & Joshua Oluwatoyin Adeleke 2017

Stephen Akinyemi Omotayo & Joshua Oluwatoyin Adeleke. (2017). The 5E Instructional Model: A Constructivist Approach for Enhancing Students' Learning Outcomes in Mathematics. JISTE, Vol. 21, No. 2, 2017. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1176946

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest-posttest quasi-experiment; 88 students taught with the 5E model and 84 with traditional methods for six weeks. Independent t-test showed the 5E group's posttest mean of 13.15 exceeded the traditional group's 11.32, "t(170) = 4.45, p < 0.05". No effect size printed.

> "Table 4 shows that there was a significant effect of treatment on students' achievement in mathematics, t(170) = 4.45, p < 0.05. The table reveals that the mean scores of students taught with the 5E instructional approach (M = 13.15, SD = 2.36) is higher than those taught with the traditional method (M = 11.32, SD = 3.00)."
