Sayda Martinez-Alvarado 2025¶

Sayda Martinez-Alvarado. (2025). A Scan of State Universal FAFSA Policies. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED672497

q2 · i? · associational · r1

EdTrust's analysis of Federal Student Aid's FAFSA Completion by High School data reports Alabama's rates for the first cohort under its 2021 Board of Education requirement: "a 13.6% jump in FAFSA submissions, and an 11.8% increase in FAFSA completions." No effect size or significance test is printed.

"Among the high school class of 2022, the first cohort required to submit the FAFSA, Alabama experienced a 13.6% jump in FAFSA submissions, and an 11.8% increase in FAFSA completions."
