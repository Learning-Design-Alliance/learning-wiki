van Eeden 2017¶

van Eeden, R. & van Vuuren, J., 2017, 'The cognitive processing potential of infants: Exploring the impact of an early childhood development programme', South African Journal of Childhood Education 7(1), a499. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajce.v7i1.499

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Exploratory analyses preceding the main comparisons examined whether age and gender affected performance, to exclude alternative explanations; none were significant, but the article flags the combined effect for study with a larger sample.

"No significant differences were found for the intervention or the control group. However, trends in the data indicated that the combined effect of age and gender had to be further explored. A larger sample would be preferable for such analysis."
