## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Frequency and time of map visits correlated positively with orientation ratio (rho = 0.57 and 0.62) and access ratio (rho = 0.65 and 0.68). [→ Stanton 1997](#stanton-1997)
`q2 i?` Despite the positive correlations, orientation and access were the ratios with the lowest values overall (0.13 and 0.16), so the map did not produce effective orientation or access. [→ Stanton 1997](#stanton-1997)

## Evidence

### Stanton 1997

Stanton, N., Correia, A. P. and Dias, P. (1997). Efficacy of a map on search, orientation and access behaviour in a hypermedia system. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Efficacy+of+a+map+on+search+orientation+and+access+behaviour+in+a+hypermedia+system

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis in the same log-based study of 22 students. The printed effect sizes are rho = 0.57, rho = 0.62 (orientation) and rho = 0.65, rho = 0.68 (access); the largest, |r| = .68, is a large correlation. The authors note these ratios "have the lowest values (0.13 and 0.16, respectively)" in Table 2.

> "in spite of a reasonable positive correlation between frequency/time of visit to the map and orientation ratio (0.57 and 0.62, respectively) as between frequency/time of visit to the map and access ratio (0.65 and 0.68, respectively)"
