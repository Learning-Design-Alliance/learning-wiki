## Evidence

### Seehra Jaspreet Kaur 2025

Seehra Jaspreet Kaur, Ellis Ricky, Doleman Brett, McLarty Esther, Lund Jonathan. (2025). Fair access to higher surgical training in the UK: an equity, diversity and inclusion analysis of national selection in 2024. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-106487

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Specialty-stratified multivariable analysis of 522 trauma and orthopaedics applicants, with age dichotomised at the within-specialty mean; exploratory continuous-age modelling yielded consistent findings. The article reports a "56% reduction" in odds for older applicants, with an adjusted odds ratio of aOR=0.44 (95% CI 0.26 to 0.72).

> "Older applicants (>mean age) also had significantly lower odds (56% reduction) compared with younger peers (aOR=0.44, 95% CI 0.26 to 0.72, p=0.001)."
