## Evidence

### Efrat 2017

Merav W. Efrat. (2017). Exploring Strategies That Influence Children's Physical Activity Self-Efficacy. Contemporary Issues in Education Research, 10(2). https://clutejournals.com/index.php/cier

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomized ANCOVA analysis of the 161 fourth graders: the comparison group exceeded the modeling group on adult encouragement barrier self-efficacy, p = .006, indicating the modeling treatment produced no significant gains on this measure.

> "No treatment, compared to modeling, was more effective at influencing a child's confidence in their ability to be less dependent on an adult's encouragement to prompt them to be active during recess, p = 006."
