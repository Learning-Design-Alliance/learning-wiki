### Mielke 2025

Mielke, I., Breil, S. M., Hissbach, J., Ehrhardt, M., & Knorr, M. (2025). Predicting undergraduate OSCE performance using traditional and construct-driven situational judgment tests at admission. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 30(3), 777–794. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10379-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of the low-stakes 2019 cohort (nLS = 159), where SJTs were voluntary research measures with no admission weight. All six SJT–OSCE correlations "remained insignificant"; no effect sizes beyond the printed correlations are reported.
