## Evidence

### Lince 2016

Lince, R. (2016). Creative Thinking Ability to Increase Student Mathematical of Junior High School by Applying Models Numbered Heads Together. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(6), 206-212. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1092494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control group study of 130 eighth-grade students in Ambon. NHT students scored higher on pretest (11.21 vs 8.88) and posttest (28.09 vs 24.88) than conventional learning students, indicating "increase in the medium category" per Hake's criteria.

> "Overall, students who obtain learning by NHT has KBKM pretest mean of 11.21 is higher than the average pretest KBKM students who obtain the PK of 8.88, and the mean posttest KBKM students who obtain the NHT learning at 28.09, higher than postes mean students who received PK at 24.88."
