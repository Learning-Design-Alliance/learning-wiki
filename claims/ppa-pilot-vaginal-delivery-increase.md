## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across 27 hospitals, the estimated percentage of vaginal deliveries rose from 33.0% in Period 1 to 38.1% in Period 2 and 42.6% in Period 3, with the Period 3 versus Period 1 difference significant (p<0.001) and Period 3 versus Period 2 also significant (p=0.004). Gamma-distribution generalized estimating equation analysis of monthly hospital reports from 27 hospitals, with 190, 335, and 295 observations in Periods 1-3 (April 2014 to June 2017). Estimated vaginal delivery percentages were "33.0 (29.0; 37.6)", "38.1 (35.2; 41.3)", and "42.6 (39.8; 45.6)", with p-values Bonferroni-corrected.
