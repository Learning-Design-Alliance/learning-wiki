## Evidence

### Cerutti B 2016

Cerutti B, Blondon K, Galetto A. (2016). Long-menu questions in computer-based assessments: a retrospective observational study. BMC Medical Education 16:55. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0578-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective observational analysis of 13 exam sessions of the paediatric CBA (855 examinees, 558 items), comparing Long-menu, Pick N and Type A formats with multiple-way analyses of variance including year and session as factors. The article reports "a significant format effect" on both outcomes.

> "The analysis of variance showed a significant format effect regarding both the level of difficulty (p-value = 0.0045) and the power of discrimination (p-value < 0.0001)."
