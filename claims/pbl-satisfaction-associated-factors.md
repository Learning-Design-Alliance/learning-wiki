Problem based learning approach increases the academic satisfaction of health science students in Ethiopian universities: a comparative cross sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03397-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple logistic regression among the 425 PBL students; the quote prints the adjusted odds ratios and confidence intervals for the five retained factors. Odds ratios below 1 apply to students not satisfied with the named factor.

> "Experience of classroom distress (AOR = 1.93, 95% CI = 1.22, 3.06), quality of teaching (AOR = 0.54, 95% CI = 0.34, 0.86), relationship with classmates (AOR = 0.33, 95% CI = 0.13, 0.80), course content (AOR = 0.56, 95% CI = 0.33, 0.93) and accessibility of technology in the campus (AOR = 0.62, 95% CI = 0.40, 0.96) were the significant factors of academic satisfaction of problem based learning students."
