q3 · i? · associational · r3

Country-level results from the four-way decomposition (Fig. 2, left panel), described in the Results text as showing cross-national variability in the proportion mediated for math scores.

"such as Chile, Indonesia, Australia, Brazil, and Jamaica, where the proportion mediated reaches a maximum of 0.11 — the majority of countries fall within the range of 0 to 0.08"

Related Claims¶

  • Growth mindset mediates only a small share (about 2.9–3.2%) of the effect of SES on academic achievement across 73 countries — related
  • The mediated interaction between SES and growth mindset is small but positive (about 0.006), indicating only minimal moderation of the SES effect — related
  • Children from low-SES backgrounds hold more negative academic self-views, which partially mediate the SES-achievement association across 70 countries — related
  • The direct effect of SES on achievement dominates: the controlled direct effect accounts for about 98% of the total SES effect in math — related
