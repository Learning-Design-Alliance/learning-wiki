## Evidence

### Visher 2016

Visher, M. G., Mayer, A. K., Johns, M., Rudd, T., Levine, A., and Rauner, M. (2016). Scaling academic planning in community college: A randomized controlled trial (REL 2017–204). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory West. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Cost-effectiveness analysis using Saddleback College expenditure data and California State Controller salary information, in 2014 dollars. Table 3 prints per-student costs of $27 (workshop), $46 (one-on-one), $24 (control) and cost per plan completed of $70, $109, and $126; the article concludes "the workshop group is the most cost-effective counseling group (lowest cost per desired outcome)."

> "The workshop group had a lower cost per student outcome than the one-on-one group and a similar cost per student outcome to the control group."
