## Subclaims

`q2 i?` Faculty members used a diverse set of categories for quantum mechanics problems, in contrast to the highly uniform categorization they showed for introductory physics problems in prior work. [→ Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2016](#shih-yin-lin-and-chandralekha-singh-2016)

`q2 i?` Unlike introductory physics problems, where professors' categorizations are uniform, professors' categorizations of quantum mechanics problems were varied across individuals. [→ Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2016](#shih-yin-lin-and-chandralekha-singh-2016)
