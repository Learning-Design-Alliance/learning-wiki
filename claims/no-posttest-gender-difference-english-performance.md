## Evidence

### Meshesha Yeshitla Meaza 2026

Meshesha Yeshitla Meaza, Weldmeskel Fisseha Mikre. (2026). Gender differences in causal attributions, self-regulation, achievement motivation, and academic performance following an intervention. BMC Psychology, 14, 588. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04357-0

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on post-test English achievement (males n = 37, M = 22.95, SD = 6.52; females n = 47, M = 23.79, SD = 7.52) was non-significant, t = −0.54, p = .591, Cohen's d = −0.12, which the authors call "very small and not practically meaningful".

> "there is no statistically significant gender difference in post-test English academic performance, as indicated by the nonsignificant t-value (t = –0.54, p = 0.591)"
