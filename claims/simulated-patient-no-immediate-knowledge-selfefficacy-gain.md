## Evidence

### Avau B 2022

Avau B, Vanhove A-C, Scheers H, Stroobants S, Lauwers K, Vandekerckhove P, De Buck E. (2022). Impact of the Use of Simulated Patients in Basic First Aid Training on Laypeople Knowledge, Skills, and Self-efficacy. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000657

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Nonrandomized controlled experimental study of 949 complete cases (334 simulated patient, 615 control) within Belgian Red Cross occupational first aid trainings; generalized linear mixed model analysis. Knowledge rose from median 4 (2–5) to "a median of 7 (6–8) in both groups"; no group difference.

> "The knowledge scores after following training (T1) increased to a median of 7 (6–8) in both groups. The mixed model analysis indicated that a simulated patient had no impact on the increase in first aid–related knowledge scores (P = 0.15"
