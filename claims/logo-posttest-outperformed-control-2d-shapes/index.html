Evidence¶

Boo Jia Yi 2016¶

Boo Jia Yi, Leong Kwan Eu. (2016). Effect of Using Logo on Pupils' Learning in Two-Dimensional Shapes. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 4(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1106476

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Non-equivalent quasi-experiment with 18 pupils per group; independent samples t-test on post-test scores. Experimental group M = 73.28 (SD = 5.92) vs control M = 60.06 (SD = 6.23); "t(34) = 6.23, p < .05". The article reports effect size (2.17) as large by Cohen's (1988) criteria.

"The independent t-test found statistically significant difference in post -test mean scores between the control and experimental group, t(34) = 6.23, p < .05). Thus the null hypothesis that the post-test mean scores were not different can be rejected; pupils in the experimental group performed better than the control group."
