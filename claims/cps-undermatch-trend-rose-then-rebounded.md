# Undermatch among CPS four-year college-goers rose from 42.7 percent in 2003 to 57.7 percent in 2013 before rebounding after 2013

## Evidence

### Roderick 2018

Roderick, M., Holsapple, M., Clark, K., & Kelley-Kemple, T. (2018). From High School to the Future: Delivering on the Dream of College Graduation. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-delivering-dream-college-graduation

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Trend analysis of CPS graduates 2003-2013 (n=119,550 two- and four-year college-goers): undermatch rose from 42.7 percent to 57.7 percent; of 3,812 two-year college attendees in 2013, 74 percent had qualifications for a four-year college. Match rates rebounded after 2013, reaching 32 percent for selective-access students by 2015.

> "In 2003, 42.7 percent of CPS four-year college-goers undermatched in their college choice; by 2013, that undermatching had risen to 57.7 percent of college-goers."
