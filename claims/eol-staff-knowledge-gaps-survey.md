### Brezis Mayer 2017

Brezis Mayer, Lahat Yael, Frankel Meir, Rubinov Alan, Bohm Davina, Cohen Matan J, Koslowsky Meni, Shalomson Orit, Sprung Charles L, Perry-Mezare Henia, Yahalom Rina, Ziv Amitai. (2017). What can we learn from simulation-based training to improve skills for end-of-life care? Insights from a national project in Israel. Israel Journal of Health Policy Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13584-017-0169-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Self-reported questionnaire and discussion data from workshop participants on palliative care knowledge; the article notes this reluctance contradicts the literature on opioids for dyspnea and that many did not know palliative care has been associated with improved survival. Percentages are descriptive self-reports; no effect size.

> "Nearly two-third would not use opioids to relieve dyspnea for fear of respiratory depression (and few more would use nebulized opioids) despite the literature."

### Brezis Mayer 2017 (2)

Brezis Mayer, Lahat Yael, Frankel Meir, Rubinov Alan, Bohm Davina, Cohen Matan J, Koslowsky Meni, Shalomson Orit, Sprung Charles L, Perry-Mezare Henia, Yahalom Rina, Ziv Amitai. (2017). What can we learn from simulation-based training to improve skills for end-of-life care? Insights from a national project in Israel. Israel Journal of Health Policy Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13584-017-0169-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Self-reported knowledge of the Israeli Dying Patient Act among workshop participants, described as previously reported elsewhere. A separate theme reports "About 20% of them reported often performing 'slow codes'". Both are descriptive self-reports from discussions and questionnaires; no effect size is printed.

> "Only half of participants knew that this law requests staff to support patients’ families."
