In the interference-optics lesson analysis, arising from one-dimensional observation patterns, "students often did not recognize that the difference of the optical path length are even or odd multiples of the wave length"; the equalization of retardation with optical path length makes the path understood as a type of resonator.

> "In response to corresponding in- quiries, students often did not recognize that the difference of the optical path length are even or odd multiples of the wave length."
