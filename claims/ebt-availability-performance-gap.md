Descriptive survey results on endoscopic bariatric therapies from the 273-respondent cross-sectional study. A second passage reports that respondents declared performing IGB, ESG, and TORe at rates of 34.6%, 12.5%, and 8.1%, respectively.

> "While 52.2% recommend EBTs as treatment option, only 23.9% perform it by themselves. Intragastric balloon (IGB) placement was the most commonly available procedure (60.7%) although only 34.6% of respondents perform it."
