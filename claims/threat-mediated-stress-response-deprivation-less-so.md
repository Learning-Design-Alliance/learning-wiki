### Karen E. Smith and Seth D. Pollak 2020

Karen E. Smith and Seth D. Pollak. (2020). Early life stress and development: potential mechanisms for adverse outcomes. Journal of Neurodevelopmental Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s11689-020-09337-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review synthesis of the rapidly expanding deprivation/threat literature, reporting a pattern of differential mediation. The review states this literature "appears to find more consistent evidence for the association between “threat” and psychopathology being mediated by alterations in stress response systems" while finding less evidence for deprivation. No effect sizes are printed.

> "this literature appears to find more consistent evidence for the association between “threat” and psychopathology being mediated by alterations in stress response systems (including autonomic and HPA reactivity). In contrast, it finds less evidence for the association between “deprivation” and psychopathology being mediated by alterations in stress response systems"
