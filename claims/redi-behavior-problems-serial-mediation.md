`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Serial mediation path model with bootstrapped confidence intervals (1000 data sets drawn) on the longitudinal RCT data, reported in Results §3.3. A fourth path ran from intervention to enhanced parent involvement to reduced behavior problems.

> "Four significant mediation paths emerged (see Figure 2, Table S6). Two significant pathways began with intervention boosts in preschool emotion knowledge, which then progressed through grade school social adjustment and parent involvement to reduced behavior problems."
