## Evidence

### Wu Bian 2014

Wu Bian, Wang Minhong, Johnson Janice M., Grotzer Tina A. (2014). Improving the learning of clinical reasoning through computer-based cognitive representation. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v19.25940

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Single-group pre-post comparison of dual maps for the first and last of four cases, blind-coded by two domain experts (inter-rater reliability 0.91). The study reports "a significant improvement in the overall performance" with effect size 1.17.

> "A significant improvement in the overall performance was found (first case mean=0.24; last case mean=0.38; first to last case: t(28)=5.72, p=0.000), with the effect size1 of 1.17, indicating substantial progress from the first to the last case."
