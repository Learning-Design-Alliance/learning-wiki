## Evidence

### Madjar Nir 2012

Madjar Nir, Bachner Yaacov G, Kushnir Talma. (2012). Can achievement goal theory provide a useful motivational perspective for explaining psychosocial attributes of medical students? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression analysis of questionnaire data from 143 first-year medical students, with PMI and LFT as dependent variables and three goal orientations entered in steps. The regression showed "β = .16, p < .05" for mastery goals on PMI-S; no standardized effect size beyond β was printed.

> "The mastery goal orientation was significantly associated with perceived psychosocial abilities (PMI-S) (β = .16, p < .05) but negatively associated with LFT (β = -.23, p < .01)"
