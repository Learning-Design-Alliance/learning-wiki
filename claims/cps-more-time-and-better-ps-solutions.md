## Evidence

### Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai 2023

Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai. (2023). Can CPS better prepare 8th graders for problem-solving in electromagnetism and bridging the gap between high- and low-achievers than IPS? Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09407-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A two-factor MANOVA on online performance across 11 topics (Wilk's Λ = 0.82, F = 7.58, p < 0.001 for problem-solving type) found "the CPS group significantly outperformed the IPS group in the performance of their PS solution performance"; in the discussion the authors report CPS outperformed IPS in 10 of the 11 topics, significant in four.

> "the CPS group significantly outperformed the IPS group in the performance of their PS solution performance (F = 14.42, p < 0.001), regardless of whether they were high or low-achievers"

### Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai 2023 (2)

Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai. (2023). Can CPS better prepare 8th graders for problem-solving in electromagnetism and bridging the gap between high- and low-achievers than IPS? Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09407-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A two-factor MANOVA on time allocation (Wilk's Λ = 0.50, F = 53.13, p < 0.001 for type) found "the CPS group allocated significantly more time to PS solutions, EB reasoning, and simulation and data recording than the IPS group" across all three stages, with printed means of 181.82 vs 123.21 (PS solution time) for CPS and IPS.

> "the CPS group allocated significantly more time to PS solutions, EB reasoning, and simulation and data recording than the IPS group (F = 65.05, p < 0.001; F = 119.66, p < 0.001; F = 32.12, p < 0.001)"
