## Evidence

### O'Neill Lotte Dyhrberg 2014

O'Neill Lotte Dyhrberg, Norberg Karen, Thomsen Maria, Jensen Rune Dall, Brøndt Signe Gjedde, Charles Peder, Mortensen Lene Stouby, Christensen Mette Krogh. (2014). Residents in difficulty—just slower learners? a case–control study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0276-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative t-test analyses of administrative medical school records for 89 cases and 343 controls (Table 2). The article interprets the pattern as cases being "slower learners" who reached similar competence given extra time and attempts.

> "cases had significantly more examination attempts (p = 0.036) and took significantly longer time to complete medical school (p = 0.013) compared to controls. However, the final competency levels as expressed by the average medical school grades were not significantly different for cases and controls by the end of medical school (p = 0.246)."
