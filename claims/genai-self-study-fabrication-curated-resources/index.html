Zhao J 2026¶

Zhao J, Yang K, Gao H and Jiang G (2026). Generative artificial intelligence and the transformation of medical education: a scoping review. Front. Educ. 11:1907589. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1907589

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports this behavioral comparison from Tuttle et al. as a stark example of hallucination becoming learned misinformation during ungrounded self-study.

"Only 6 of 23 students correctly learned the Randleman criteria during an initial ChatGPT-3.5 phase; all 23 answered correctly in a subsequent internet-search phase, and the model had fabricated a similarly named syndrome (Tuttle et al., 2024)."

Zhao J 2026 (2)¶

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports this comparison among negative and null results showing the risk of poorly grounded self-study; other comparisons found ChatGPT faster without knowledge improvement.

"Blended urology teaching outperformed ChatGPT-only learning (Digiacomo et al., 2025)."
