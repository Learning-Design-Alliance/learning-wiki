## Evidence

### Delavari S 2024

Delavari S, Barzkar F, Rikers MJP, Pourahmadi M, Soltani Arabshahi SK, Keshtkar A, Dargahi H, Yaghmaei M, Monajemi A. (2024). Teaching and learning clinical reasoning skill in undergraduate medical students: A scoping review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309606

`q3 · i3` · `review · r3`

The review reports this finding from an included study (attributed to reference [4]) comparing self-explanation with prompts against a word-puzzle control group, with printed effect size SMD = 1.601 (confidence interval 0.85–2.353) for diagnostic performance.

> "Diagnostic performance was statistically significantly affected by resident self-explanation with prompts in contrast to solving word puzzles (control group) (SMD, 1.601[0.85–2.353])"
