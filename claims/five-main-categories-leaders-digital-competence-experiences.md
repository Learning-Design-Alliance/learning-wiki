### Laakkonen Noora 2024

Laakkonen Noora, Jarva Erika, Hammarén Mira, Kanste Outi, Kääriäinen Maria, Oikarinen Anne, Mikkonen Kristina. (2024). Digital Competence among Healthcare Leaders: A Mixed-Methods Systematic Review. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/8435248

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Mixed-methods systematic review following JBI guidelines; qualitative studies were analyzed with content analysis to synthesise leaders' digital competence experiences. The review reports that "The synthesis of qualitative data identified five main categories that describe healthcare leaders' experiences with digital competencies."

> "The synthesis of qualitative data identified five main categories that describe healthcare leaders' experiences with digital competencies: (1) the need for developing leader's own, professionals', and patients' competence in the digitalisation of healthcare, (2) the need for expertise in the health IT implementation process, (3) positive perceptions towards technology, (4) negative perceptions towards technology, and (5) ability to act as an advocate to implement technology into practice."
