q3 · i? · design · r2

Human evaluation study: seven middle-school science teachers answered 64 MCQs generated from eight AP Environmental Science learning objectives, each with a 'none of the above' option added. Their Fleiss' kappa of 0.70 indicates "the generated MCQs are generally answerable and unambiguous".

"The seven human judges achieved a substantial agreement [21] with each other (Fleiss' kappa = 0.70), indicating that the generated MCQs are generally answerable and unambiguous."
