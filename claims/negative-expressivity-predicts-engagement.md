### Hernández 2018

Hernández, M. M., Eisenberg, N., Valiente, C., Thompson, M. S., Spinrad, T. L., Grimm, K. J., VanSchyndel, S. K., Berger, R. H., Silva, K. M., Pina, A. A., Southworth, J., & Gal, D. E. (2018). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Growth model predicting spring first-grade observed engagement, controlling for fall-K engagement and background covariates (N = 301). The article reports the intercept and slope "significantly and inversely predicted school engagement" with the printed coefficients.

> "In this model, the intercept (b1 = −1.82, p = .01) and linear slope (b2 = −4.72, p = .02) of negative expressivity significantly and inversely predicted school engagement."
