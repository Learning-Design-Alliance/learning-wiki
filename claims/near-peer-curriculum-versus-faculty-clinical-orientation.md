## Evidence

### Hari Roman MD 2025

Hari Roman MD, MME; Oppliger Sarah; Dolmans Diana H.J.M. PhD; Huwendiek Sören MD, MME, PhD; Stalmeijer Renée E. MSc, PhD. (2025). Comparison of Practical Skills Teaching by Near-Peers and Faculty. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006003

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group perceptions study at the University of Bern (8 FGs; students n = 26, near-peers n = 22). Results state "Near-peer teaching was oriented toward the formal curriculum and students’ learning needs," while faculty teaching toward clinical practice "could overwhelm novice students."

> "Near-peer teaching was oriented toward the formal curriculum and students’ learning needs, resulting in more tailored explanations focused on examination-relevant content. Faculty oriented their teaching toward clinical practice, which helped students transition to clinical practice but could overwhelm novice students."
