## Evidence

### Aquino-Blanco A 2025

Aquino-Blanco A, Quiroz-Cárdenas F, Montenegro-Espinosa JA, Martín-Calvo N, Yañéz-Sepúlveda R, Gutiérrez-Espinoza H, Olivares-Arancibia J, Rahmati M, Stubbs B, Smith L, López-Gil JF, Jiménez-López E. (2025). Social networks, messaging apps, addictive use, and academic performance in adolescents: findings from the EHDLA study. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-025-02180-8

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional analysis of 583 EHDLA adolescents; addictive behaviors measured with the validated SNAddS-6S (KR-20 = 0.65) and GPA from official school records, modeled with robust generalized linear models adjusted for eight covariates. The article reports "This negative association is statistically significant" with β = -0.11, a small standardized magnitude.

> "After adjusting for sex, age, socioeconomic status, BMI, physical activity, sedentary behaviors, sleep duration, and energy intake, for each unit increase in addictive behaviors to SNs use, there is an associated decrease of 0.15 points in GPA. This negative association is statistically significant (B = -0.15, 95% CI -0.26, -0.03; p = 0.014; β = -0.11)."
