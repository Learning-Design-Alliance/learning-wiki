`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of two focus groups (16 final-year pharmacy students), deductively coded to the Kirkpatrick Model with Cohen's kappa = 0.79. This finding is from the Level 1 (Reaction) theme.

> "students demonstrated strong emotional and cognitive engagement with the campaign, highlighting its authenticity and impact. The hands-on nature, through role-play, interactive activities, and real-world health communication, shifted learners from passive recipients to active contributors."
