Solís 2017¶

Solís, M., Scammacca, N., Barth, A. E., & Roberts, G. J. (2017). Text-based Vocabulary Intervention Training Study: Supporting Fourth Graders with Low Reading Comprehension and Learning Disabilities. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 15(1), 103-115. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1141988

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Fidelity observation analysis: fourteen observations across both tutors rated each component on a 4-point Likert scale; the mean implementation score was 3.81 (SD = 0.64) and global quality was 3.85 (SD = 0.40).

"The mean implementation score across instructional components and across tutors was 3.81 (SD = 0.64, range 0.00 to 4.00). The mean implementation score for global observations of quality was 3.85 (SD = 0.40, range 2.00 to 4.00)."
