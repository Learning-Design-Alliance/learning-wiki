`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni-Holm-corrected post hoc correlations in the 86-student experiment supported H3.3 only for VPs; the overall combined correlation (r=0.18; P=.05) did not support the hypothesis. VP quality of evidence was also higher than SP quality (t74=–2.47; P=.02; d=0.29).

> "Corrected post hoc analyses showed, however, that the quality of evidence generation was positively related to diagnostic accuracy for VPs (r=0.38; P<.001); this finding did not hold for SPs (r=0.05; P=.32)."
