`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Pre- and post-program surveys of 21 participants across 12 skill domains rated on a 0–5 scale showed average gains of +2.0 points; "Improvements were especially pronounced in nuclear physics (∆M = 2.9, d = 2.51), phyphox (∆M = 2.6, d = 2.52), and basic electronics (∆M = 2.7, d = 2.43)."
