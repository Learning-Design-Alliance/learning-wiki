## Evidence

### Ehrlich 2018

Ehrlich, S.B., Pacchiano, D.M., Stein, A.G., Wagner, M.R., Luppescu, S., Park, S., Frank, E., Lewandowski, H., & Young, C. (2018). Organizing early education for improvement: Testing a new survey tool. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research and the Ounce of Prevention Fund. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/organizing-early-education-improvement-testing-new-survey-tool

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Descriptive analysis of CLASS Pre-K observation data provided by CPS and DFSS for classrooms across 37 school-based sites (120 classrooms) and 40 community-based sites (150 classrooms). The sample showed "an average score of 6.13 on Emotional Support" and 3.16 on Instructional Support, a pattern the authors note is consistent with nationwide patterns. No inferential test was reported for this contrast.

> "Overall, classrooms in our sample had an average score of 6.13 on Emotional Support (SD = 0.69), 5.99 on Classroom Organization (SD = 0.97), and 3.16 on Instructional Support (SD = 1.16). The lower scores and wider spread on Instructional Support is consistent with nationwide patterns."
