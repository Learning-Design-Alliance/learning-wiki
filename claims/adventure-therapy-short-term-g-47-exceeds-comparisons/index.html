Evidence¶

Daniel J. Bowen and James T. Neill 2013¶

Daniel J. Bowen and James T. Neill. (2013). A Meta-Analysis of Adventure Therapy Outcomes and Moderators. The Open Psychology Journal, 2013, 6, 28-53. http://www.daniel-bowen.com.au/meta-analysis

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis of 2,908 effect sizes from 206 unique samples; the Pre-Post comparison yielded an effect size of g = .47 for adventure therapy versus .14 (alternative treatment) and .08 (no treatment), described as "moderate, positive and significant (.47)". Based on a Fisher z transformation the authors equate .47 with a 23.5% increase in measured outcomes.

"The Pre-Post Adventure Therapy effect size was moderate, positive and significant (.47), and larger than the small positive significant effects for Alternative Treatment (.14) and No Treatment (.08)."
