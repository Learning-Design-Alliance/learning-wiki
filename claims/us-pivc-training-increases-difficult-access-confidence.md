### Pham Mia 2024

Pham Mia, Aldous Robert, Brincat Stephan. (2024). Implementation of ultrasound-guided cannulation training for foundation doctors. Clinical Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinme.2024.100256

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-/post-course questionnaire study of 65 foundation and trust-grade doctors at St George's Hospital; pre- and post-course Likert scores compared using the Wilcoxon signed rank test, showing a "significant" confidence increase with no effect size printed.
