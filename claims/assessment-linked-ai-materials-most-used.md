Survey usage-ranking data from the same mixed-methods study (N=38 analyzable responses). Assignment 1 materials were "ranked first by nearly half (n=18) of students" and lesson worksheet videos, most indirectly related to assessments, had the lowest usage rate.

> "As presented in Figure 4, “Assignment 1 infographics, reports and videos” was dominant, ranked first by nearly half (n=18) of students, followed by “Homework reports and videos.” Conversely, “Lesson worksheet videos,” which are most indirectly related to assessments, had the lowest usage rate."
