## Evidence

### Wechsler 2024

Wechsler, M. E., Patrick, S. K., Thompson, C., & Levin, S. (2024). On the path to leadership: California's administrator induction programs. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/636.224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Statewide completer survey analysis (6,812 completers) reports that "completers from district-run induction programs were the most likely to rate their programs as very effective." No effect size or test statistic is printed.

> "Induction programs run by school districts are well positioned to integrate induction into the broader set of supports available for newly credentialed administrators, and completers from district-run induction programs were the most likely to rate their programs as very effective on the statewide survey."

### Wechsler 2024 (2)

Wechsler, M. E., Patrick, S. K., Thompson, C., & Levin, S. (2024). On the path to leadership: California's administrator induction programs. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/636.224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The report's synthesis of survey and case study data describes benefits of preparation–induction alignment at the same institution, including a seamless learning experience and long-term staff and coach relationships. Qualitative and descriptive evidence.

> "In addition, administrators who attended the same institution for both their preparation and induction programs often benefited from a more seamless learning experience and had opportunities to develop long-term relationships with program staff and coaches working across both programs."
