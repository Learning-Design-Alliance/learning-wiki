`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The review reports a survey of medical professionals (Begeny et al., BMJ Open) finding overestimation of women's representation; men's overestimation predicted lower support for gender equality initiatives (p < 0.001) while women's estimations were unrelated to support (p = 0.92). No effect size is printed.

> "The GIWL research indicates that a man's degree of overestimation predicted lower support for gender equality initiatives (with a p-value less than 0.001). Women's (over)estimations were unrelated to their level of support (with a p value of 0.92) [16]."
