## Evidence

### Kothe Christian 2013

Kothe Christian, Hissbach Johanna, Hampe Wolfgang. (2013). The Hamburg Selection Procedure for Dental Students – Introduction of the HAM-Nat as subject-specific test for study aptitude. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000889](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000889)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression models predicting ZVP examination grades (anatomy, physiology, biochemistry) in three cohorts; the advantage for HAM-Nat appeared only in WiSe 2009/10, where "the percentage of predicted academic performance was 10% higher in anatomy and biochemistry and 6.4% higher in physiology for the HAM-Nat".
