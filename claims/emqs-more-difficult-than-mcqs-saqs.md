## Evidence

### Karamdokht Parya 2026

Karamdokht Parya, Babaee Hemmati Yasamin, Dadgaran Ideh, Falahchai Mehran. (2026). Item analysis and performance of EMQ, MCQ, and SAQ in dental education and students’ perception of extended-matching questions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09791-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Item analysis in a cross-sectional study of 29 senior dental students across four courses; ANOVA of difficulty factor showed EMQs (mean 49.26 ± 27.06) were harder than SAQs (76.69 ± 18.48) and MCQs (71.33 ± 22.42), with "no significant difference between SAQs and MCQs".

> "the difficulty factor of the EMQs was significantly different from that of SAQs and MCQs (P < 0.001), such that the difficulty factor of the EMQs was lower than the other two question formats, but there was no significant difference between SAQs and MCQs (P = 0.364)"
