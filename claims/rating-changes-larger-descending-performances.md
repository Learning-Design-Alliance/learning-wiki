## Evidence

### Steinmann JJ 2025

Steinmann JJ, Otto I, Born MP, Derous E, Woltman AM, van den Broek WW, Stegers-Jager KM. (2025). Adjustment of Assessors’ First Impressions Differs by Student Ethnicity. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2196

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Expanded LMM analysis adding performance level as a second within-subjects variable after both ethnicity effects were significant. The interaction effect between ethnicity and performance level was not significant, t(247) = –.51, p = .61.

> "performance level (ascending/descending) was found to be significant, t(247) = –5.65, p < .001, indicating that rating changes were larger for descending performances than ascending performances"
