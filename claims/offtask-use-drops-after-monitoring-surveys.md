## Evidence

### Day Alison J. 2021

Day Alison J., Fenn Kimberly M., Ravizza Susan M. (2021). Is it worth it? The costs and benefits of bringing a laptop to a university class. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251792

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-semester comparison of tracked off-task durations before versus after the two surveys, interpreted as a possible social-desirability or awareness effect. The article reports the decrease as "significantly by about 7 minutes in the intervals after the surveys were given, t(102) = 3.95, p < .001".

> "On average, the duration of off-task activities decreased significantly by about 7 minutes in the intervals after the surveys were given, t(102) = 3.95, p < .001."

### Day Alison J. 2021 (2)

Day Alison J., Fenn Kimberly M., Ravizza Susan M. (2021). Is it worth it? The costs and benefits of bringing a laptop to a university class. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251792

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Split-semester correlational check showing the off-task–exam relationship persisted despite duration changes; the article reports "negative correlations with their respective exam scores were observed both before, r = -0.16, and after, r(101) = -0.18".

> "negative correlations with their respective exam scores were observed both before, r = -0.16, and after, r(101) = -0.18, students completed the surveys"
