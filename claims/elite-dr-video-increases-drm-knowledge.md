## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After watching the three-part self-learning video, participants' overall DRM knowledge score rose from 6.99 ± 2.65 to 13.31 ± 5.21, a statistically significant increase (p < 0.001). [→ Saiboon Ismail M. 2021](#saiboon-ismail-m-2021)

## Evidence

### Saiboon Ismail M. 2021

Saiboon Ismail M., Zahari Fareena, Isa Hisham M., Sabardin Dazlin M., Robertson Colin E. (2021). E-Learning in Teaching Emergency Disaster Response Among Undergraduate Medical Students in Malaysia. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.628178

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective cross-sectional interventional study with pre- and post-test design; 168 pre-clinical students completed the study after 7 days of access to the self-learning videos. Paired t-test showed the increase was significant: "6.99 ± 2.65 and 13.31 ± 5.21, respectively (p < 0.001)". No effect size printed.

> "The mean pre- and post-test marks for knowledge of DRM were 6.99 ± 2.65 and 13.31 ± 5.21, respectively (p < 0.001)."
