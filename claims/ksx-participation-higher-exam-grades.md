## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Active MOOC∩KSX participants scored significantly higher final exam grades than active MOOC-only participants, and the difference survived propensity score matching. [→ Heusler Andreas 2019](#heusler-andreas-2019)

## Evidence

### Heusler Andreas 2019

Heusler Andreas, Molitor Dominik, Spann Martin. (2019). Knowledge Stock Exchanges increase student success in Massive Open Online Courses. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0223064

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-sample t-test among 4,498 students who passed all quizzes, comparing 3,885 MOOC-only with 613 MOOC∩KSX participants; the printed means and t-statistic support the grade difference. Matching (kernel ATT difference 0.758, T-stat 3.09) addresses self-selection.

> "A two-sample t-test (t = -3.902, p < 0.01) indicates that the average final exam grades of 3,885 active MOOC-only participants (M = 24.26, SD = 6.43) are significantly lower (4.4%) compared to 613 active MOOC∩KSX participants (M = 25.33, SD = 5.35)."
