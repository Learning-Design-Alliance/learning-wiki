Meta-analysis of journal articles and dissertations from 1990 to 2012 (k = 14) comparing SCMC and FTF interaction. The review reports that "interaction in SCMC and FTF had a significant impact on second language (L2) development," consistent with earlier syntheses it cites. No pooled effect size is printed in the available text.
