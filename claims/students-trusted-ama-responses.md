`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the post-survey question "Do you trust AMA responses?", "The students trusted AMA's responses as similar to those from teachers and friends"; five 7th graders trusted it like a teacher or friend and eight generally trusted it, while three 6th graders expressed uncertainty (Figure 8).

> "The students trusted AMA's responses as similar to those from teachers and friends, as well as direct responses from AMA."
