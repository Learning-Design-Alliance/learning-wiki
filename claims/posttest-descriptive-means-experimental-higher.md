Tuncel, İ. (2019). The effectiveness of intercultural sensitivity development activities integrated with scientific research methods course. European Journal of Educational Research, 8(1), 301-312. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.8.1.301

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive statistics from Table 4 for 43 experimental and 43 control students: pretest means of 87.04 and 87.93 were moderate, and the posttest means were 99.20 (experimental) versus 89.27 (control).

> "the students in the experimental group ( x =99.20) had a higher level of intercultural sensitivity perception than the control group ( x =89.27) when the arithmetic means of the scores obtained from the intercultural sensitivity scale were compared."
