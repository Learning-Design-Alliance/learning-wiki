## Evidence

### Scior Katrina 2014

Scior Katrina, Bradley Caroline E, Potts Henry W W, Woolf Katherine, de C Williams Amanda C. (2014). What predicts performance during clinical psychology training? The British Journal of Clinical Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjc.12035

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Of the 12 trainees with poor placement performance, only three were among the 58 who received at least one major revision or fail on case reports. Case report score correlated negatively with all four exams (e.g. exam 4: τb = −0.23, p < .001) and with viva outcome (U = 1165.5, p < .001).

> "Poor placement performance was related to poor exam performance, but there was no association between poor placement performance and case report marks, which raises the question of the extent to which case reports measure clinical knowledge and/or skills."
