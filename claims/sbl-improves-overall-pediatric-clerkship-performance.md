### Elamin Abdelaziz 2025

Elamin Abdelaziz, Ali Maryam Fuad, Almarabheh Amer. (2025). Impact of simulation-based learning on the academic performance of medical students during pediatric clerkships. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07911-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective analysis of 2,251 fifth-year medical students over 12 academic years, six before and six after SBL integration. The article reports "Overall academic performance increased by 5.75%" with effect size 0.671 in Table 2.

> "Overall academic performance increased by 5.75%, with the mean rising from 69.66 ± 8.13 to 75.41 ± 8.90, also reaching statistical significance (p < 0.001)."
