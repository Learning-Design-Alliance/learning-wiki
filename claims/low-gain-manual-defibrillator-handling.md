## Evidence

### dos Santos Emílio Carlos Alves 2021

dos Santos Emílio Carlos Alves, Fontes Cor Jesus Fernandes, D'Artibale Eloana Ferreira, Miravete Jocilene de Carvalho, Ferreira Gimerson Erick, Ribeiro Mara Regina Rosa. (2021). Simulation for teaching cardiorespiratory resuscitation by teams: setting and performance assessment. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.3932.3406

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level analysis of pre/post knowledge test scores (Table 2): defibrillator handling rose from 44.4 to 51.6. The authors attribute this to scarce prior training and to the existence of more than five brands of defibrillator, which makes fixing handling learning difficult.

> "It was identified in this study that there was a low increase in the mean of correct answers in the pre- and post-assessments, in relation to the handling of the manual external defibrillator."
