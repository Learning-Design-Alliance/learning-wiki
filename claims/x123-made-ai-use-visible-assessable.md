## Evidence

### Riasat Islam and Thomas Roelleke 2026

Riasat Islam and Thomas Roelleke (2026). Assessment Design in the GenAI Era: The X1-X2-X3 Assessment Pattern for Testing Students' AI Literacy, Learning Outcomes, and Reflection. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.12351](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.12351)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Authors' structural observation from the deployed assessment in the database systems module: the response format turned GenAI use into "part of the assessed performance itself", requiring a source trace, evidence of the interaction, a revised answer, and justification for full credit.

> "the combination of AI-aware question design and the X1-X2-X3 response structure changed GenAI use from an unrecorded part of students' drafting process, visible to markers only indirectly through the final answer, into part of the assessed performance itself."
