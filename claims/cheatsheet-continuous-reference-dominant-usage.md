## Evidence

### Helen Weixu Chen 2026

Helen Weixu Chen, Victoria Sakhnini, and Lesley Istead. (2026). Make or Take: How Students Navigate Self-Created and Instructor-Provided Cheat Sheets. ACM Trans. Comput. Educ. https://doi.org/10.1145/3834566

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey responses on consultation frequency across both exams; occasional use predominated, indicating the sheet served "more as a backup reference than as a central tool" for most participants.

> "the majority of participants said they "occasionally" used it, with around 64% for the midterm and 50% for the final exam."

### Helen Weixu Chen 2026 (2)

Helen Weixu Chen, Victoria Sakhnini, and Lesley Istead. (2026). Make or Take: How Students Navigate Self-Created and Instructor-Provided Cheat Sheets. ACM Trans. Comput. Educ. https://doi.org/10.1145/3834566

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-reported usage strategies for the final exam; continuous referral (73%) far exceeded skimming at the start (23%) or referral only for uncertain questions (13%).

> "The most popular strategy for using the cheat sheet was to "refer to the cheat sheet continuously as I worked through the exam, " reported by 73% of participants."
