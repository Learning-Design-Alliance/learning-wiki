## Evidence

### Namaziandost 2018

Namaziandost, E., Esfahani, F. R., & Hashemifarnia, A. (2018). The Impact of Using Authentic Videos on Prosodic Ability among Foreign Language Learners. International Journal of Instruction, 11(4), 375-390. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1191678

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired samples t-test on the experimental group's pre- and post-test totals (Table 10): mean paired difference 12.400, t = 5.770, df 19, sig. .000 — "the difference between the post -test and pre -test of the experimental group is significant". No effect size printed.

> "But, since the sig. (.000) for experimental group i s less than 0.05 (p<0.05); the difference between the post -test and pre -test of the experimental group is significant."

### Namaziandost 2018 (2)
