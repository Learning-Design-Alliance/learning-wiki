### Reinhard Pekrun 2022

Reinhard Pekrun, Herbert W. Marsh, Felix Suessenbach, Anne C. Frenzel, Thomas Goetz. (2022). School grades and students' emotions: Longitudinal models of within-person reciprocal effects. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101626

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Correlations of within-person centered scores extracted from the RI-CLPMs, PALMA sample (N = 3,425). The article prints the range "r = .12 to .55" with all ps < .001 for enjoyment and pride with grades.

> "In addition, enjoyment and pride showed significant positive within-person correlations with grades (range r = .12 to .55; all ps < .001)."

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

> "Anger, anxiety, shame, boredom, and hopelessness showed significant negative within-person correlations with grades (range r =  .17 to  .44; all ps < .001)."
