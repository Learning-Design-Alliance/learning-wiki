Katherine D. Rainey and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2020). Faculty survey on upper-division thermal physics content coverage. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.rainey](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.rainey)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of which topics were selected by all instructors across the three topic groups in the survey of 59 respondents. The authors state the results "support the anecdotal claim" of variability across institutions and between instructors, with less variability within institutions.

> "These results support the anecdotal claim that upper-division thermal physics content coverage varies both across institutions and between instructors at the same institution (though to a lesser extent)."
