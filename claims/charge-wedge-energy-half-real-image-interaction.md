## Evidence

### M.M. Taddei 2009

M.M. Taddei, T.N.C. Mendes, C. Farina. (2009). Subtleties on energy calculations in the image method. https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4497 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0903.4497](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0903.4497)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical symmetry argument in Section 2 for a wedge whose aperture angle equals π/n, where the space divides into 2n sectors each holding one charge. The apparent n-dependent prefactor cancels against the double summation, leaving "half of the interac tion energy between the real charge and each image".

> "and once more the energy of the conﬁguration is half of the interac tion energy between the real charge and each image."
