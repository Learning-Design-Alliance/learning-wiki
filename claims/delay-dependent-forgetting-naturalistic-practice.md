## Evidence

### Aakriti Kumar 2022

Aakriti Kumar, Aaron S. Benjamin, Andrew Heathcote and Mark Steyvers. (2022). Comparing models of learning and relearning in large-scale cognitive training data sets. npj Science of Learning 7:24. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00142-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of Lumosity retention data plotting first- and second-gameplay performance as a function of retention interval, from a few days to over 2 years, showing "a delay of 100 days leads to a 20% reduction in performance". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "For the first game within the session, delay has a substantial effect on the proportional change in performance: a delay of 100 days leads to a 20% reduction in performance and a delay of 400 days leads to a 50% reduction."
