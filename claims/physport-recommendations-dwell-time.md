PhysPort use and growth: Supporting physics teaching with research-based resources since 2011. Submitted to the Physics Teacher. [doi:10.1119/10.0002062](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0002062)

Google Analytics dwell-time analysis of Expert Recommendations pages in 2018, for users who later visit another PhysPort page. The authors infer "these recommendations are capturing the attention of PhysPort visitors", noting 80% of web users leave a typical page within 70 seconds.

> "PhysPort's most popular recommendation in 2018, "Effect size: What is it and when and how should I use it?"14, had 9,300 unique page views and an average time on page of nine minutes."
