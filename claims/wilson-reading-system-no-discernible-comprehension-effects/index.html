What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Wilson Reading System®. What Works Clearinghouse, Beginning Reading. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/546

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The comprehension findings table reports GRADE passage comprehension effect size 0.28 (ns, improvement index +11) and WRMT-R passage comprehension effect size 0.07 (ns, +3), for a domain average effect size of 0.17, neither statistically significant nor substantively important under WWC criteria.

"Torgesen et al. (2006) examined two outcomes in this domain (the WRMT–R passage comprehension subtest and the GRADE passage comprehension subtest) and reported no statistically significant effects."
