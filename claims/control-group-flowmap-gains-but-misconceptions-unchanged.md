## Evidence

### Ceylan 2018

Ceylan, Ö., & Yiğit, E. A. (2018). Analyzing the Effect of Concept Cartoon Usage on Students’ Cognitive Structures Developments and Science Achievements through Flow Maps. Science Education International, 29(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1205414

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test on flow map size parameter (number of sentences expressed) for the 27 control students. Positive ranks favored the post-test, indicating the level of knowledge about the particulate nature of matter unit increased.

> "the control group students had a statistically significant difference (z = 4.111, ρ < 0.05) between the pre-test scores of the size parameter after 8 weeks of study"

### Ceylan 2018 (2)

Ceylan, Ö., & Yiğit, E. A. (2018). Analyzing the Effect of Concept Cartoon Usage on Students’ Cognitive Structures Developments and Science Achievements through Flow Maps. Science Education International, 29(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1205414

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test on flow map correctness parameter (misconception rate) for control students over 8 weeks. No statistically significant change was found, so misconceptions could not be removed by teaching with the current program.

> "There was no statistically significant difference (z = 0.488, ρ > 0.05) between the correctness parameter pre-post test scores of the control group at the end of the 8-week period."
