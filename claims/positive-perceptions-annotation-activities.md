### Sahota Michael 2016

Sahota Michael, Leung Betty, Dowdell Stephanie, Velan Gary M. (2016). Learning pathology using collaborative vs. individual annotation of whole slide images: a mixed methods trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0831-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Grounded-theory analysis of open-ended questionnaire responses from junior students; themes were coded independently by two investigators with consensus resolution. The most frequent positive themes were "the ability to annotate" and "the user-friendly Slice™ interface".

> "Positive feedback comments (n = 73) from these students indicated that the ability to annotate (n = 36), the user-friendly Slice™ interface (n = 22), improved understanding (n = 19), collaboration with peers (n = 17) and the engagement/interactivity provided by the activities (n = 15) were the most enjoyable aspects of the intervention"
