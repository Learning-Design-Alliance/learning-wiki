The report states, citing Willis, Doutre, & Berg-Jacobson (2019), that "differentiating resources in these ways can lead to improved outcomes for students with disabilities," referring to funding differentiated by disability, disability category, or student need. The study's design is not described in the report.

Krausen 2020 (2)¶

Krausen, K., Caparas, R., Ripma, T., & Willis, J. (2020). Strategic resource allocation for the 21st century: How state leaders can address the silent recession. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/strategic-resource-allocation/

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report describes the Maryland adequacy study's new metric — "progress toward each student's Individualized Education Program (IEP) goals, adjusted for the amount of time since each IEP goal was developed" — used with cost function analysis to calculate short-term and long-term investment recommendations.

"Maryland's recent adequacy study (Willis, Doutre, & Berg-Jacobson, 2019) established a new metric for studying outcomes of students with disabilities: progress toward each student's Individualized Education Program (IEP) goals, adjusted for the amount of time since each IEP goal was developed."
