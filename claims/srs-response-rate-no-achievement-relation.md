`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Blood (2010), ABABC single-subject design with five high-school special-education students; the review reports response-rate increases during SRS but "no functional relation was detected" for on-task behavior or achievement. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Although authors note increases in response rates for students during the use of SRS, no functional relation was detected for student on-task behavior or academic achievement (as measured by daily quizzes or end-of-phase test scores)."
