## Evidence

### Iaroseski Júlia 2026

Iaroseski Júlia, Bigolin André Vicente, Galvão Lima Sofia, Cazella Silvio César. (2026). Quality over quantity: early performance predicts suturing proficiency in robotic surgery simulation. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03702-0

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation from the 57-participant retrospective cohort (Table 2). The article frames this as evidence that extra time-on-task alone did not improve performance; the correlation is small (r = 0.274) and the design is observational.

> "Notably, longer time spent in the Fundamentals module was associated with longer total suturing time (r = 0.274, p = 0.039), reinforcing that additional time-on-task did not, by itself, translate into better performance."
