Evidence¶

Sebring 2003¶

Sebring, Penny Bender. (2003). Research Agenda 2004-2008. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org/publications

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The academic productivity section reports the Consortium's Phase I value-added analyses of elementary schools using ITBS data, in which it "found a disturbing pattern of weak improvements" in a subset of very disadvantaged, racially isolated African-American schools; no effect size is printed.

"in our Phase I analyses, we found a disturbing pattern of weak improvements in a subset of very disadvantaged, racially isolated African- American schools. Many of these schools were the explicit targets of Phase II reforms."
