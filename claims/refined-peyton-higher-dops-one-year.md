## Evidence

### Zhang Dandan 2024

Zhang Dandan, Wang Xiaoxue, Chen Xueting, Lou Yingze, Zhou Xin, Zhang Kai. (2024). Optimizing resident training in obstetrics and gynecology: a new perspective on the refined Peyton four-step teaching method. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05851-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-year post-training DOPS assessment of both cohorts on 11 items scored out of 9 by two blinded associate-chief-physician examiners. The article reports the RP group "scored higher than the TTM group in DOPS evaluations, with statistically significant differences in all assessed items (P < 0.001)". No effect size is printed.

> "One year after training, the RP group scored higher than the TTM group in DOPS evaluations, with statistically significant differences in all assessed items (P < 0.001). The RP group exhibited better performance in obtaining patient consent, providing appropriate comfort and pain relief, skill operation, aseptic technique, seeking assistance as needed, communication skills with patients, professional demeanor/humanistic care, and overall performance, as detailed in Table 3."
