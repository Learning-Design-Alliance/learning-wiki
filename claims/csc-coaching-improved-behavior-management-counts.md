# Delayed CSC coaching significantly improved waitlist control teachers' standardized behavior management strategy counts (d = .42)

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Waitlist control teachers receiving delayed CSC showed standardized BMS Counts change scores significantly greater than zero, a medium effect. [→ Dudek et al. 2019](#dudek-et-al-2019)

## Evidence

### Dudek et al. 2019

Christopher M. Dudek, MEd, Linda A. Reddy, PhD, Adam Lekwa, PhD, Anh N. Hua, PhD, and Gregory A. Fabiano, PhD. (2019). Improving Universal Classroom Practices Through Teacher Formative Assessment and Coaching. Assessment for Effective Intervention. https://doi.org/10.1177/1534508418772919

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Within-group one-sample t test on standardized change scores for 32 waitlist control elementary teachers after the CSC 4 Session Model. The article reports the BMS change score mean of .66 and "t (29) = 2.32, p = .028, d = .42".

> "On the contrary, for BMS Counts, the standardized change scores between baseline assessment and postintervention assessment (M = .66, SD = 1.56) were significantly greater than zero, t (29) = 2.32, p = .028, d = .42."
