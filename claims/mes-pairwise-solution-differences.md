# Ethanol–phenol-embalmed tissue received the highest MES suitability ratings, while ICL tissue rated lowest on color and texture domains

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` EP-embalmed tissue was rated significantly higher on the suitability domain than all other solutions (partial η² = 0.035). [→ Darie S. 2026](#darie-s-2026)

## Evidence

### Darie S. 2026

Darie S., Wainman B.C., Wohl G.R., Wang A.Y., Zhang B.Z., Li A., Ahmad E., Palombella A.S., Rockarts J.L., de SA D.L., Nguyen L.N., DeCarlo B., Downer N., Brewer‐Deluce D. (2026). Preliminary insights on high‐fidelity embalming solutions for surgical skills training—An evaluation using the McMaster Embalming Scale and mechanical tests. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70185

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Post-hoc pairwise comparisons following the two-way MANOVA of MES domain ratings in the fully crossed resident-rating study. The article reports "suitability domains (EP > all; p = 0.020, partial η 2 0.035)" and ICL rated below EP and SSS on color and below SRF and EP on texture.

> "Solution‐based differences (Figure 3) were significant for the color (ICL < EP, SSS; p = 0.027, partial η 2 = 0.032), texture (ICL < SRF, EP; p = 0.006, partial η 2 = 0.044), and suitability domains (EP > all; p = 0.020, partial η 2 0.035)."
