### Munyaw Yuda 2022

Munyaw Yuda, Gidabayda Joshua, Yeconia Anita, Guga Godfrey, Mduma Esto, Mdoe Paschal. (2022). Beyond research: improved perinatal care through scale-up of a Moyo fetal heart rate monitor coupled with simulation training in northern Tanzania for helping babies breathe. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-022-03249-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post OSCE assessment of 107 health care workers trained with NeoNatalie simulators, analyzed with paired t-tests (Table 2 prints p < 0.001 for both OSCE A and B). No effect size is printed.

> "Skill gain was 25.5 and 38.2% for OSCE A and B respectively with significant differences between pre-test and post-test scores."
