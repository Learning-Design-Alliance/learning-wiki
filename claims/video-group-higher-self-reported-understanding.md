### Jose J. Vazquez & Eric P. Chiang 2016

Jose J. Vazquez & Eric P. Chiang. (2016). Preparing Students For Class: A Clinical Trial Testing The Efficacy Between Multimedia Pre-lectures And Textbooks in an Economics Course. Journal of College Teaching & Learning, 13(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1095763

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Participant opinion survey after the learning modules, asking how confident students felt about their understanding; responses shown in Figure 3. The percentages are descriptive self-reports; no statistical test of the difference is reported.

> "Of the students in the Video group, 44% felt they understood the concept either well or very well. This is in comparison to just 36% in the Text group. Therefore, the results suggest that students in the Video group not only scored better, but also self-reported better understanding of the material."
