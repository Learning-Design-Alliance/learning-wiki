q2 · i? · review · r3

Review's interpretation of three studies (Alamer and Alharbi; Nast et al; Popovic et al) where attendance rose alongside recorded lectures, synchronous online teaching or Moodle introduction; only one of these reached statistical significance.

"These three studies suggest that the use of electronic resources as an adjunct to in-person teaching may be the reason for an increase in attendance observed in these studies."
