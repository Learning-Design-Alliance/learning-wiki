Baseline check in the user study using the short 20-item Purdue Visualization of Rotations Test; Table 2 reports pre-test means of 64.14 (AR) and 65 (non-AR). Equivalence beyond the non-significant test was not established.

> "The between-subjects ANOVA conducted on the PVRT pre -test scores did not show a significant difference between the AR and non-AR groups (p = 0.8), which indicates that students of the two groups had similar mental visualization skills before participating in the workshops"
