Evidence¶

Komachali and Khodareza 2012¶

Maryam Eslahcar Komachali, Mohammadreza Khodareza. (2012). The Effect of Using Vocabulary Flash Card on Iranian Pre-University Students' Vocabulary Knowledge. International Education Studies, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n3p134

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-samples t-test of the control group's (n = 25) pretest and posttest scores under traditional instruction (synonyms, definitions, mini contexts); the article reports observed t less than critical, so "the third null hypothesis was supported". Equivalence was not tested; no effect size is printed.

"It is clear in Table 4 there is a little progress between the performances of the control group from pretest to posttest. The t observed is less than the t critical and it was concluded that the third null hypothesis was supported."
