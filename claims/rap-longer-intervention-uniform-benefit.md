## Evidence

### Leidig 2018

Leidig, T., Grünke, M., Urton, K., Knaak, T., & Hisgen, S. (2018). The Effects of the RAP Strategy Used in a Peer-tutoring Setting to Foster Reading Comprehension in High-Risk Fourth Graders. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 16(2), 231-253. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1194573

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of score dispersion at t3 between group 1 (SD=1.47) and group 2 (SD=2.39). The authors interpret this as students benefiting more uniformly from a longer intervention with the motivational system incorporated mid-treatment rather than from the start.

> "the average distance of each data point from the mean of the data series in group 2 (SD=2.39) was 162.59% higher in group 2 at t3 than it was in group 1 (SD=1.47) at the same time."
