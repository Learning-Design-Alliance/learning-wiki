# The sports-team–grades association varies by grade: in grade 9 only two or more teams, and in grade 12 only three or more teams, were significantly associated with better grades

## Evidence

### Chen Sitong 2021

Chen Sitong, Li Xiaoyun, Yan Jin, Ren Zhanbing. (2021). To Be a Sportsman? Sport Participation Is Associated With Optimal Academic Achievement in a Nationally Representative Sample of High School Students. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.730497

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Grade-stratified fully adjusted logistic regression on the YRBS 2019 sample (Table 3). One team was not significant in grade 9; printed odds ratios were OR = 2.13 for two teams and OR = 3.42 for three or more teams versus zero teams.

> "In grade 9 students, only having two or three team sports (2 teams OR = 2.13, 95%CI: 1.42–3.20; 3 or more teams OR = 3.42, 95%CI: 1.91–6.12) were more likely to report better academic performance."

### Chen Sitong 2021 (2)

Chen Sitong, Li Xiaoyun, Yan Jin, Ren Zhanbing. (2021). To Be a Sportsman? Sport Participation Is Associated With Optimal Academic Achievement in a Nationally Representative Sample of High School Students. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.730497

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Grade-stratified fully adjusted logistic regression (Table 3). In grade 12, one and two teams were not significant; the printed odds ratio was OR = 4.44 (95%CI: 1.38–14.28) for three or more team sports versus zero teams.

> "However, in grade 12 students, only participating in three or more team sports was associated with better academic performance (OR = 4.44, 95%CI: 1.38–14.28)."
