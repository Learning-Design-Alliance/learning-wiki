## Evidence

### Çakiroğlu 2025

Çakiroğlu, Ü., Güven, O., Yaman, H., & Mazlum Güven, E. (2025). Scientific habits of arguing minds: enhancing online argumentation to foster SHOM. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10474-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test comparing draft and final argument rubric scores of 33 teacher candidates scored with the Weng et al. (2017) rubric by three researchers; total scores rose from 3.72 to 5.8 with eta squared = 0.67 reported as large.

> "there was a statistically significant difference between the mean rubric scores of draft argumentations (M = 3.72, SD = 1.35) and final argumentations (M = 5.8, SD = 1.45), t(33) = − 12.336, p > 0.000. The eta squared statistic (0.67) indicated a large effect size."

### Çakiroğlu 2025 (2)

Çakiroğlu, Ü., Güven, O., Yaman, H., & Mazlum Güven, E. (2025). Scientific habits of arguing minds: enhancing online argumentation to foster SHOM. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10474-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Component-level paired t-tests on rubric scores for claim-ground, warrant, backing and rebuttal; all four increased significantly with eta squared values of 0.67, 0.63, 0.79 and 0.64 respectively.

> "claim and ground (M = 1.21, SD = 0.37 for draft and M = 1.56, SD = 0.41 for final; t(33) = − 8.056, p > 0.00; eta squared = 0.67), warrant (M = 0.77, SD = 0.48 for the draft and M = 1.42, SD = 0.42 for final; t(33) = − 7.376, p > 0.00; eta squared = 0.63), backing (M = 0.85, SD = 0.54 for the draft and M = 1.50 SD = 0.41 for final; t(33) = − 10.982, p > 0.00; eta squared = 0.79) and rebuttal (M = 0.88, SD = 0.41 for draft and M = 1.32, SD = 0.49 for final; t(33) = − 7.598, p > 0.00; eta squared = 0.64) increased statistically significantly and the effect size was large for all those skills."
