### Taşdemir Murat 2026

Taşdemir Murat, Keskin Büşra Ekin, Aydoğan Ayaz Elif, Çelik Davut. (2026). Adverse outcomes of post-core restorations performed by undergraduate and postgraduate dental students: a retrospective comparison. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09270-z

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparison of adverse outcomes by operator education level among 160 restorations (40 undergraduate, 120 postgraduate). The article reports a significant association "with a small effect size" (φ = 0.176); Table 3 shows 37.5% versus 20.0% adverse outcomes.

> "A statistically significant association was observed between operator education level and adverse outcomes (χ²(1) = 4.984, p = 0.026, φ = 0.176), with a small effect size"
