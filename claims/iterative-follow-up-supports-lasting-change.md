### Lotan 2019

Lotan, R. A., & Burns, D. (2019). The Instructional Leadership Corps: Teachers leading sustainable professional learning in their communities. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/instructional-leadership-corps-teachers-leading

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Cross-case observational finding from the Lessons Learned chapter (Lesson 4); the authors report this from observing ILC teams across four sites. Follow-up frequency and quality were described as "variable yet indispensable."

> "We observed that lasting changes in pedagogy are more likely when teachers have the opportunity to try out new strategies, receive feedback, address challenges in implementation, and iteratively improve over the course of multiple workshops, with advisors and coaches at hand."
