Null interaction result from the PROCESS Model 8 moderated mediation analysis in the 5764-participant survey; the printed test shows the interaction "was not significant (b = −0.006" with the full confidence interval spanning zero. Equivalence was not tested.

"For the direct association between AI dependence and self-reported creativity, the interaction of AI dependence and perceived AI literacy was not significant (b = −0.006, SE = 0.007, t = −0.906, p = 0.365, 95% CI [−0.019, 0.007])."
