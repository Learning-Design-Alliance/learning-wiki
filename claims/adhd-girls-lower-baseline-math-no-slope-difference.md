## Evidence

### Henry 2025

Henry, L. N., Gross, R. A., & Hinshaw, S. P. (2025). Mathematics Achievement in Women with and without ADHD: Childhood Predictors and Developmental Trajectories into Adulthood. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 58(6). https://doi.org/10.1177/00222194241301044

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent growth curve modeling (LGCM) of WIAT Math Reasoning standard scores across four waves, covarying SES and full-scale IQ, in the longitudinal sample of 140 girls with ADHD and 88 comparisons. The slope coefficient was "the non-significant slope coefficient β = −0.16, p = 0.26", so the ADHD group did not decline significantly faster.

> "the negative intercept coefficient of β = −0.17, p = 0.004 indicates that the expected math standard score at the first age cluster was lower for girls with ADHD than for comparisons. No variables significantly predicted the latent slope."
