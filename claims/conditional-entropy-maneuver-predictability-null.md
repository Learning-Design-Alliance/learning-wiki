## Evidence

### Vedula S. Swaroop 2016

Vedula S. Swaroop, Malpani Anand O., Tao Lingling, Chen George, Gao Yixin, Poddar Piyush, Ahmidi Narges, Paxton Christopher, Vidal Rene, Khudanpur Sanjeev, Hager Gregory D., Chen Chi Chiung Grace. (2016). Analysis of the Structure of Surgical Activity for a Suturing and Knot-Tying Task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149174

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Conditional entropy analysis of maneuver transition probability matrices for 135 dVSS trials by 18 surgeons, with 95% bootstrap intervals from 1000 bootstrap samples. The printed difference was -0.24 (95% BI -0.95 to 0.47); no standardized effect size is reported.

> "Experts performed the task using a more predictable sequence of maneuvers compared with novice surgeons but the 95% BI included the null value suggesting that the difference was not statistically significant. The difference in the conditional entropy for probability distribution of transitions among maneuvers for trials by expert and novice surgeons was -0.24 (95% BI = -0.95 to 0.47)."
