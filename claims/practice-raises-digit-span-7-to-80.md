## Evidence

### Ericsson 1993

K. Anders Ericsson and Walter Kintsch. (1993). Long-Term Working Memory. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Long-Term+Working+Memory

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Chase & Ericsson (1982) and Staszewski (1988a), studies of extended practice on the digit-span task: "two subjects were able to increase their memory performance from around 7 digits" to over 80 digits. Other subjects acquired spans over 20 digits within 50 hours of practice. The review attributes the gains to storage in LTM with retrieval cues.

> "After hundreds of hours of practice on this task two subjects were able to increase their memory performance from around 7 digits, which is the typical performance of untrained subjects, to over 80 digits"
