## Evidence

### Samuel Fynes-Clinton 2022

Samuel Fynes-Clinton, Chase Sherwell, Maryam Ziaei, Ashley York, Emma Sanders O'Connor, Kylee Forrest, Libby Flynn, Julie Bower, David Reutens and Annemaree Carroll. (2022). Neural activation during emotional interference corresponds to emotion dysregulation in stressed teachers. npj Science of Learning 7:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00123-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Brain-behaviour PLS analysis of DERS total scores with Stroop-task activation (LV p < 0.001, 51.38% covariance) in 49 stressed teachers. "greater activation in the aforementioned regions was associated with higher DERS" during GenNeut, r = −0.61; marginal reversals during SpeNeg (r = 0.19) and control (r = 0.16).

> "Pearson 's correlation revealed that during the GenNeut condition, greater activation in the aforementioned regions was associated with higher DERS (re ﬂecting greater emotion dysregulation) whereas low DERS scores related to reduced activation in these regions, r = −0.61 (Fig. 2B)."
