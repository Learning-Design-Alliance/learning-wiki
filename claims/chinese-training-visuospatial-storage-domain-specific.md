## Evidence

### Demetriou 2011

Demetriou, A., Spanoudis, G., & Mouyi, A. (2011). Educating the Developing Mind: Towards an Overarching Paradigm. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-011-9178-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Demetriou et al. 2005, a cross-cultural comparison of Chinese and Greek children's storage capacities. The visuospatial advantage of "about three units" did not extend to phonological storage or executive operations; no effect size is printed.

> "the capacity of visuospatial short-term storage of Chinese children exceeded the corresponding capacity of Greek children by about three units of information throughout the period from 8 to 14 years of age, whereas their working memory for words and numbers was the same"
