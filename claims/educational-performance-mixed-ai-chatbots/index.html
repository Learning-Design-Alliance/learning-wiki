Evidence¶

Potel 2026¶

Potel, J. T., & Kumashiro, M. (2026). Artificial Intelligence as a Personal Coach: A Narrative Review of Benefits and Risks in Educational and Health Contexts. Behavioral Sciences, 16(8), 1431. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16081431

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of educational-performance studies: nursing students with an AI chatbot coach showed no significant differences versus controls; programming students performed best with non-AI digital materials; a story-time chatbot showed lower productivity than controls.

"Thus, these studies do not support a general performance advantage for AI coaching over comparison conditions."
