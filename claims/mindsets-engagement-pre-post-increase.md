## Evidence

### Gage 2025

Gage, N. (2025). The impact of the Mindsets mathematics program on student engagement, ability to reason, and repeated reasoning. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677579

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre–post analysis of the Mindsets engagement dataset using a cumulative link mixed model with random intercepts for classroom and challenge (n = 1,982). The report states "The odds of being in a higher engagement category at post-intervention were approximately 7.47 times greater than at pre-intervention" and converts the log-odds to d ≈ 1.11.

> "Results indicated a statistically significant increase in engagement from pre- to post-intervention, b = 2.01, SE = 0.26, z = 7.74, p < .001"
