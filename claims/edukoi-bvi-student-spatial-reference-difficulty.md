### Guiotto Nai Fovino 2023

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case-study feedback collected from one BVI student and his support teacher during the first classroom test. Because the user must move a hand on a plane parallel to the screen in front of the camera, "it was difficult for the BVI student to understand where the camera and the screen were".

> "He was enthusiastic about the tasks and very focused, although he found it very difficult to find the sonified areas, as any kind of spatial reference was lacking."
