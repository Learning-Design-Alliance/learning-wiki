## Evidence

### Madkour and Mohamed 2016

Magda Madkour & Rafik Ahmed Abdel Moati Mohamed. (2016). Identifying College Students’ Multiple Intelligences to Enhance Motivation and Language Proficiency. English Language Teaching, 9(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n6p92

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test comparing male (n=58) and female (n=50) students across eight intelligence scales. The article reports significance "in favor of the males in the spatial intelligence and intrapersonal intelligence" with t values of 01.95 and 2.11.

> "there is statistical significance between the mean scores of the males and females at the level of 0.5 in favor of the males in the spatial intelligence and intrapersonal intelligence where the t values are (01.95) and (2.11), respectively."
