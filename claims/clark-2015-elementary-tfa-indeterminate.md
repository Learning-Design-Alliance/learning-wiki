## Evidence

### Teach For America 2016

Teach For America. (2016). WWC Intervention Report: Teach For America. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.teachforamerica.org

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

This RCT across 36 elementary schools randomly assigned students within grade to TFA or non-TFA teachers, with analytic samples of 2,065 students for math and 2,123 for reading. The report states: "The authors reported, and the WWC confirmed, that the difference between the TFA group and the comparison group was not statistically significant", and the effect size was not large enough to be substantively important.
