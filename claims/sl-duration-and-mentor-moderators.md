### Toronyi 2020

Toronyi, A. (2020). Preservice Teachers Working with English Language Learners: A Meta-Synthesis of Service-Learning as an Effective Pedagogical Tool. Journal of Service-Learning in Higher Education, 11. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1267630

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Limitation noted in the meta-synthesis; as Haddix (2014) posits, relationship building takes time to develop trust between participants.

> "most of the studies did not go beyond 10-20 hours of SL which were generally conducted within the length of a semester while PSTs completed their methods courses"

### Toronyi 2020 (2)

Toronyi, A. (2020). Preservice Teachers Working with English Language Learners: A Meta-Synthesis of Service-Learning as an Effective Pedagogical Tool. Journal of Service-Learning in Higher Education, 11. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1267630

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`
