### Pasqualotto Eric 2026

Pasqualotto Eric, Pasqualotto Tales, Lopes Victor Hugo Pinheiro, Lunelli Laura, Santos Lucas Loiola. (2026). Virtual reality surgical simulator training of residents and early-career surgeons for pedicle screw placement: a systematic review and meta-analysis. European Journal of Orthopaedic Surgery & Traumatology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00590-026-05008-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of six RCTs comparing VR simulator training with traditional training for pedicle screw placement; pooled effect for optimal Grade I placement was "RR 1.36; 95% CI 1.15 to 1.61; p < 0.01; I2 = 76%". GRADE rated this outcome very low-certainty.

> "residents and early-career surgeons trained with VR simulation achieved a higher rate of Grade I screw placement compared with traditional approaches (RR 1.36; 95% CI 1.15 to 1.61; p < 0.01; I2 = 76%; Fig. 2A)"
