Independent-sample t-tests and chi-square crosstabulations across demographic groups in the cross-sectional survey; printed p-values were 0.574 (gender), 0.281 (living condition), 0.563 (family doctor), and 0.671, 0.190, 0.881 for the crosstabulations. Equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference between male and female or hosteller and day scholar students regarding their readiness for self-directed learning.
