Evidence¶

Shiza Khaqan 2024¶

Shiza Khaqan, Gisela Redondo-Sama. (2024). A systematic review of the role of dialogic leadership: Characterization and impacts. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100618

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Deakins (2007), an action-research case in an early childhood organization where staff engaged in open dialogue and decision-making: "participants felt empowered and showed increased self-esteem". Elderly participants also showed improved mental and physical well-being. No effect size is printed.

"After engaging in dialogic interactions and being a part of the decision-making, participants felt empowered and showed increased self-esteem (Deakins, 2007)."

Shiza Khaqan 2024 (2)¶

Shiza Khaqan, Gisela Redondo-Sama. (2024). A systematic review of the role of dialogic leadership: Characterization and impacts. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100618

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing García-Carrión and Díez-Palomar (2015), a longitudinal case study in which "participation in a classroom intervention based on dialogic interactions resulted in improved self-efficacy and confidence" among 5th-grade children. No effect size is printed.

"In a longitudinal case study, García-Carri´on and Díez-Palomar (2015) found that within children in the 5th grade, participation in a classroom intervention based on dialogic interactions resulted in improved self-efficacy and confidence."
