### Omar David Perez & Gonzalo P. Urcelay 2025

Omar David Perez & Gonzalo P. Urcelay. (2025). Delayed rewards weaken human goal directed actions. npj Science of Learning 10:36. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00325-2

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered 2 (Delay) × 2 (Val) within-subjects ANOVA on pooled test data from all three experiments (N = 290): "η2 =0 . 0 2" for the Delay × Val interaction, p = 0.018, F(1, 289) = 5.69. Devaluation sensitivity was weaker in the delayed context.

> "Importantly, the interaction betweenDelay and Val was signi ﬁcant, [ F (1, 289) = 5.69,p = 0.018, η2 =0 . 0 2 ] , showing that the inﬂuence of devaluation on response rate was moderated by the delay condition: participants were less sensitive to devaluation in the delay context compared to the immed iate context"

### Omar David Perez & Gonzalo P. Urcelay 2025 (2)

Omar David Perez & Gonzalo P. Urcelay. (2025). Delayed rewards weaken human goal directed actions. npj Science of Learning 10:36. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00325-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up comparisons on the pooled test data: responding differed between valued and devalued actions in the immediate context, t(289) = 2.91, p = 0.004, but not in the delay context, t(289) = 0.17, p = 0.866. No effect size is printed for either comparison.
