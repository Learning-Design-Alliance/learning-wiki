## Evidence

### Drost-de Klerck Amanda M 2020

Drost-de Klerck Amanda M, Olgers Tycho J, van de Meeberg Evelien K, Schonrock-Adema Johanna, ter Maaten Jan C. (2020). Use of simulation training to teach the ABCDE primary assessment: an observational study in a Dutch University Hospital with a 3–4 months follow-up. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-032023

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pattern observed in the Wilcoxon follow-up comparisons of separate skills (table 2), where the declining items include CRM skills like "Communicates clearly", "Shows confidence" and "Shows good leadership". The authors suggest the lower score may reflect 'not thinking out loud' rather than failing to recognise a life-threatening condition.

> "Strikingly, most skills that decrease over time are CRM skills (table 2). This is illustrated by a decrease in time of 'recognition of life-threatening conditions in the C', while the scores on the resuscitation skills did not decline."
