# RI-CLPM on simulated PALMA-like data shows positive reciprocal cross-lagged effects between within-individual math achievement and self-concept

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a RI-CLPM fitted to simulated data resembling Marsh et al.'s PALMA data, prior within-individual achievement positively predicted subsequent within-individual self-concept and vice versa. [→ Kimmo Sorjonen 2025](#kimmo-sorjonen-2025)

## Evidence

### Kimmo Sorjonen 2025

Kimmo Sorjonen, Bo Melin, Gustav Nilsonne. (2025). Inconclusive Evidence for a Prospective Effect of Academic Self-Concept on Achievement: A Simulated Reanalysis and Comment on Marsh et al. (2024). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10008-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of simulated data with the same correlations and sample size (N = 3527) as the PALMA data used by Marsh et al. (2024). The RI-CLPM showed "a good fit" and positive cross-lagged effects: achievement on self-concept b = 0.119 [0.097; 0.141], p < 0.001, and vice versa b = 0.146 [0.121; 0.171], p < 0.001.

> "The RI-CLPM (Fig. 1A) had a good fit (χ 2 = 505, DF = 46, CFI = 0.981, TLI = 0.981, RMSEA = 0.053). In agreement with Marsh et al. (2024) and the reciprocal effects model, prior within-individual achievement had a positive effect on subsequent within-individual self-concept"
