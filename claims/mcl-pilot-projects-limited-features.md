The authors' review of related work and background (Section 2 and conclusion) argues that prior work targets specific fields or limited collaboration aspects and does not handle issues required for effective MCL, such as video-on-demand delivery and archive updating.

> "Most of the mobile learning applications are limited to short-funded pilot projects, which were developed for special purposes or individual courses offered by their institutions."
