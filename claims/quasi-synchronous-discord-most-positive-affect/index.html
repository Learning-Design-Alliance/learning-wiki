Evidence¶

Duncan Culbreth · Florence Martin 2025¶

Duncan Culbreth · Florence Martin. (2025). Exploring the role of synchrony in asynchronous, synchronous, and quasi-synchronous online learner engagement. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10504-y

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Sentiment analysis (AFINN, BING, NRC lexicons) of debate chatlogs from one course cohort; Table 4 prints ratios of 1.2 (async), 1.31 (sync), and 1.71 (quasi-sync), and Discord had the "highest ratio of positive to negative tokens".

"Like AFINN, all modalities had more positive values than negative, but quasi-synchronous Discord had the highest ratio of positive to negative tokens, followed by synchronous Zoom, then the asynchronous forum."
