# In the main study, the DDR script induced far more talk-page discussion contributions and fewer article edits than the BRD script

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` In a two-week field experiment with two DokuWiki groups, DDR participants engaged in many more discussions (d = 1.68) and made fewer edits (d = 0.51) than BRD participants. [→ Heimbuch 2018](#heimbuch-2018)

## Evidence

### Heimbuch 2018

Heimbuch, S., Ollesch, L., & Bodemer, D. (2018). Comparing effects of two collaboration scripts on learning activities for wiki-based environments. University of Duisburg-Essen. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9283-0

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Main-study two-group field experiment with DokuWiki (N = 69 completing all stages); Welch's t-tests on wiki log metrics: edits BRD M = 12.47 vs DDR M = 7.43, t(63.09) = 2.11, p = .019, d = 0.51; discussions BRD M = 0.28 vs DDR M = 13.87, t(36.45) = 7.48, p < .001, d = 1.68.

> "Regarding the wiki contributions, students in the DDR group participated much more frequently in talk page discussions and edited less to the wiki articles than students in the BRD group (cf. Table 6)."
