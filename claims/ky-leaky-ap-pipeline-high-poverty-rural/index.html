Kristen Hengtgen 2023¶

Kristen Hengtgen, Hector Biaggi. (2023). Increasing Access to Advanced Coursework in Kentucky. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641971

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Drop-off analysis tracking students from AP enrollment through completion, test-taking, and qualifying scores, by district poverty level and locale. The report states "only 1 in 100 earned a passing score on an AP exam and got college credit" in high-poverty districts.

"In districts that serve high numbers of students from low-income backgrounds (the grey line above), only 1 in 20 students were enrolled in an AP class in 2021-22, and only 1 in 100 earned a passing score on an AP exam and got college credit."
