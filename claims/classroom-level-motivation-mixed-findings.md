Longitudinal study within the review's 15 classroom-level motivation studies (N range 144-4847). In contrast, the review reports a large-scale cross-sectional study found students' perceptions of all three domains positively related to emotional engagement (Havik & Westergård, 2020).

> "For instance, in a study by Rimm-Kaufman et al. ( 2014), longitudinal associations of instructional support, classroom organization, and emotional support with aspects of regulation of motivation were either very small and non-significant or no effects were reported."
