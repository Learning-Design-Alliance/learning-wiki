### Imlach 2017

Imlach, A.-R., Ward, D. D., Stuart, K. E., Summers, M. J., Valenzuela, M. J., King, A. E., Saunders, N. L., Summers, J., Srikanth, V. K., Robinson, A. & Vickers, J. C. (2017). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the initial multiple regression on the genetic subgroup (N = 181), none of APOE, BDNF Val66Met, KIBRA, COMT Val158Met or SERT 5-HTTLPR significantly predicted GPA (all p > 0.15 in Table 2). The authors conclude a candidate gene approach to predicting later-life academic success may not succeed.

> "as the initial regression identiﬁed that no gene polymorphism was signiﬁcantly associated with GPA, a separate hierarchical multiple linear regression was conducted within the full sample"
