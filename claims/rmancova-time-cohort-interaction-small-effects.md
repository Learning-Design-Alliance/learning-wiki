RM-ANCOVA with cumulative pre-intervention academic averages as covariate. The article reports "ηp²=0.025" for the interaction and "ηp²=0.057" for the cohort effect, both small.

> "the magnitude of the between-cohort difference varied across the two committees (time × cohort: F(1,183) = 8.06, p = 0.005, ηp²=0.025) and that the EC showed higher adjusted performance across committees overall (cohort effect: F(1,183) = 19.14, p = 0.001, ηp²=0.057)"
