Multiple-group SEM indirect-effect comparison with bias-corrected bootstrap confidence intervals (10,000 samples). The article reports three indirect paths "signi ﬁcantly stronger in female adolescents than in males", with the printed difference coefficients and p-values; no standardized effect size for the differences is printed.

"The indirect effects from CER to WOR through OSE (D CER → OSE → WOR = 0.16, p < 0.05, see Table 2), DSE to WOR through OSE (D DES → OSE → WOR = −0.11, p < 0.05), and PCR to WOR through DSE and OSE (D PCR → DES → OSE → WOR = 0.02, p < 0.05) were signi ﬁcantly stronger in female adolescents than in males."
