`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA with Bonferroni-corrected pairwise comparisons of PPVT-4 vocabulary and grammar scores across the three language groups, reported in the group-comparisons results. The article reports "a strong advantage for the L1E group" on both proficiency indices, with the stated large and medium pairwise effect sizes; no numeric effect size is printed in this sentence.

> "On the indices of English language proficiency, by contrast, there was a strong advantage for the L1E group. On vocabulary, it outperformed both the EAL and EFL groups with large effect sizes, and on grammar, it outperformed them with a large and medium effect size, respectively."
