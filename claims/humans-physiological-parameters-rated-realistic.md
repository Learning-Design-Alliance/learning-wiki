`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same validation study with 15 prehospital experts rating 9 simulated patients at five time points (T0 to T4) on a 5-point Likert scale. Overall physiological parameters had a "median rating of 5 (absolutely realistic; IQR 3-5)"; 79% of individual physiological parameters were rated 5, 17% rated 4, and 4% rated 3.

> "Overall physiological parameters of all patients had a median rating of 5 (absolutely realistic; IQR 3-5). The majority of individual physiological parameters (n=43, 79%) had a median rating of 5, with (n=9, 17%) rated 4, and only (n=2 ,4%) rated 3."
