New research: Chicago showed instructional improvements under standards reform 2022¶

New research: Chicago showed instructional improvements under standards reform, while many other places did not. (2022). UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

A districtwide study of Chicago Public Schools used student and teacher surveys, interviews, and achievement data from 2014-15 to 2017-18. The release reports that "Math and science instruction improved in Chicago during the years of standards reform" per student survey reports. No effect size is printed.

"Math and science instruction improved in Chicago during the years of standards reform, based on student survey reports of their classroom experiences from before and after standards reform."
