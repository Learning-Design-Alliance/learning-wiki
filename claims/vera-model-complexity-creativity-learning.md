## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The number of hypotheses generated correlated strongly with model complexity (r=0.66), while complexity and creativity were only weakly related (r=0.18). [→ An 2022](#an-2022)

## Evidence

### An 2022

An, S., Bates, R., Rugaber, S., Hammock, J., Weigel, E., & Goel, A. K. (2022). Cognitive Assistance for Inquiry-Based Modeling. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.02576](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.02576)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of the 15 lab participants' constructed models. Complexity was calculated as the number of components plus the total number of relationships. The article reports a "strong correlation (r=0.66)" between hypotheses generated and model complexity.

> "The number of hypotheses generated and the complexity of models constructed has a strong correlation (r=0.66)."

### An 2022 (2)

An, S., Bates, R., Rugaber, S., Hammock, J., Weigel, E., & Goel, A. K. (2022). Cognitive Assistance for Inquiry-Based Modeling. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.02576](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.02576)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis linking model quality measures to learning gains in the lab experiment. The article reports complexity related to biological knowledge gains (r=0.29) and creativity related to modeling-process gains (r=0.43).

> "Gain in learning biological knowledge is related to the complexity of constructed models (r=0.29) whereas understanding the process of modeling is related to creative models (r=0.43)."
