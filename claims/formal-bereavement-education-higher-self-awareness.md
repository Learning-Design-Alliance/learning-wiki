## Evidence

### Garavito C 2026

Garavito C, Karki S, Rose SJ, Hartnett J. (2026). Bereavement practices and staff competencies on perinatal loss at a community-based teaching hospital. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0347348

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent t-test sub-analysis comparing PBCCS subscale means between staff with and without formal bereavement education. Self-awareness differed significantly (4.23 vs 3.39, p = 0.001); knowledge (p = 0.13), skills (p = 0.15) and organizational support (p = 0.19) were not significant in the printed table.

> "Those reporting having a formal education were more likely to have greater self-awareness (p = 0.001) and bereavement support skills (4.23) as compared to those who did not (3.39) (Table 5)."
