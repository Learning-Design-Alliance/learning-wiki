## Evidence

### Farey John E. 2018

Farey John E., Bui David T., Townsend David, Sureshkumar Premala, Carr Sandra, Roberts Chris. (2018). Predictors of confidence in anatomy knowledge for work as a junior doctor: a national survey of Australian medical students. BMC Medical Education 18:174. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1280-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable ordinal regression in the same 1101-student national survey. Integration of anatomy with basic sciences carried adjusted OR 1.97 (95% CI 1.52–2.56) for self-reported confidence; the unadjusted OR was 2.85.

> "integration of anatomy with other basic sciences (OR 1.97 [1.52–2.56]) and clinical teaching (OR 1.90 [1.46–2.48])"
