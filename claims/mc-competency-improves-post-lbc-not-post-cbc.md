## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Second-year medical students' perceived medical communication competency improved significantly post-LBC compared to pre-LBC (t=-6.84, p<0.01). [→ Yoo Hyo Hyun 2018](#yoo-hyo-hyun-2018)
`q3 i?` Perceived MC competency post-CBC was also higher than pre-LBC (t=-5.83, p<0.01), but there was no significant difference between post-LBC and post-CBC. [→ Yoo Hyo Hyun 2018](#yoo-hyo-hyun-2018)

## Evidence

### Yoo Hyo Hyun 2018

Yoo Hyo Hyun, Shin Sein, Lee Jun-Ki. (2018). Effects of medical communication curriculum on perceptions of Korean medical school students. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2018.106

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests comparing 94 students' self-assessments across the three survey waves (pre-LBC, post-LBC, post-CBC) showed "MC competency improved post-LBC when compared to pre-LBC (t=-6.84, p<0.01)". No effect size was printed.

> "MC competency improved post-LBC when compared to pre-LBC (t=-6.84, p<0.01), and competency was higher post-CBC when compared to pre-LBC (t=-5.83, p<0.01). There was no significant difference in perceived MC competency post-LBC or post-CBC."
