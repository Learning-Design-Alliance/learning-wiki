## Evidence

### Fies 2021

Fies, C., & Packham, C. (2021). Interdisciplinary Teams for Teacher Professional Development. ASP Conference Series. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.04878](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.04878)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Feedback from the iteration that first opened registration to current and future teachers and intentionally teamed them. All participants "appreciated being part of mixed teams consisting of pre- and inservice teachers", while inservice teachers saw benefits in inservice-only grouping for exchanging classroom tips and co-planning lessons.

> "All participants stated that they appreciated being part of mixed teams consisting of pre- and inservice teachers. However, inservice teachers also noted that there would have been benefits in being grouped with other inservice teachers."
