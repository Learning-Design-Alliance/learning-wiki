Patricia D. Simon 2026¶

Patricia D. Simon, Lily Min Zeng, Luke K. Fryer. (2026). A systematic review of student engagement research in adaptive learning platforms. Computers and Education Open. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100360

The review reports, citing an included study, that "providing elaborate explanations and summary interactives instead of just scores increases students ' perceptions of engagement"; the discussion adds evidence that elaborated feedback has a large impact on learning.

"Furthermore, providing elaborate explanations and summary interactives instead of just scores increases students ' perceptions of engagement and understanding and reduces mind wandering [67]."
