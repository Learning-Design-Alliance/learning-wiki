Urban Eriksson, Cedric Linder, John Airey, Andreas Redfors. (2014). Introducing the anatomy of disciplinary discernment: an example from astronomy. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, Vol. 2, No. 3, 167-182. [doi:10.30935/scimath/9409](https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9409)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Conclusion drawn from the comparative analysis of lecturer and student descriptions of the same simulation clips; the discussion notes that disciplinary experts have often lost the ability to see things as students might see them, and that undergraduates often focus on the wrong things in representations.
