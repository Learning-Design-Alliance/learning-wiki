## Evidence

### Paul Alstein 2021

Paul Alstein, Kim Krijtenburg-Lewerissa, and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2021). Teaching and learning special relativity theory in secondary and lower undergraduate education: A literature review. Physical Review Physics Education Research 17, 023101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.17.023101

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Scherr et al., an evaluation of two tutorials using a three-step design (pretest eliciting conceptions, recognition of discrepancy, guided resolution), with "three pre-post tests (N ¼ 300)" plus interviews and classroom interactions. No effect size is printed.

> "Analysis of three pre-post tests (N ¼ 300) and excerpts from taped interviews and classroom interactions showed that the materials help to confront incompatibility of deeply held student conceptions about simultaneity and improve students' ability to recognize and resolve some of the classic paradoxes of SRT."
