### Kang Xue 2022

Kang Xue, Elizabeth Barker. (2022). Using Artificial Intelligence (AI) to improve math accessibility for students with visual impairments. https://www.nwea.org/resources/using-artificial-intelligence-ai-to-improve-math-accessibility-for-students-with-visual-impairments/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Observational analysis of MAP Growth math assessment data from about 29.5 million students on standard versions and 48,845 students on accessible versions. The study "identified high-quality items, those that measured achievement for both students with and without VI equally well, and low-quality items, which showed differences between the two groups of students."

> "Using data from about 29.5 million students taking standard versions of the MAP Growth math assessment, and 48,845 students taking accessible versions, we identified high-quality items, those that measured achievement for both students with and without VI equally well, and low-quality items, which showed differences between the two groups of students."
