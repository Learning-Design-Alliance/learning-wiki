### Poudyal 2026

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Comparative rates of AI-action predicates across the four operational representations in the corpus. The study reports "Tool records averaged 119.2 AI-action predicates per hundred records", the highest of the four representations, indicating tool framing does not reduce agentive description. Descriptive rates; no effect size.

> "Tool records averaged 119.2 AI-action predicates per hundred records, proxy records averaged 89.1, interlocutor records averaged 87.5, and infrastructure records averaged 75.4."

### Poudyal 2026 (2)

Poudyal, B. (2026). Who Acts, Who Knows, Who Answers? A Corpus-Assisted Discourse Analysis of Agency, Epistemic Responsibility, and Accountability in Generative AI Higher Education Research. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7210-5480

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Cross-representation comparison of verification-judgement association rates and responsibility-nominalization rates. Proxy discourse led verification-judgement combinations at "26.1 associations per hundred records"; infrastructure trailed at 6.3 and averaged the highest nominalization rate at approximately 3.98 per document. Descriptive; no effect size.

> "Verification-judgement combinations were greatest in proxy discourse (26.1 associations per hundred records), consistent with the need to determine whether a submitted performance remains the learner’s own."
