## Evidence

### Abegglen S 2020

Abegglen S, Krieg A, Eigenmann H, Greif R. (2020). Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing (OSAD) in simulation-based medical education: Translation and validation of the German version. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244816

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

CFA standardised loadings for the 57-debriefing sample (Fig 1) show weak loadings for these two categories, which Table 2 also shows had the lowest category ICCs (e.g. 'application phase' single-rater ICC = 0.274). Item discrimination for 'reaction phase' was rjt = 0.44, below the satisfactory range. No effect size printed.

> "Thus, in the final model, two categories (i.e., 'reaction phase', β = 0.200; 'application phase', β = 0.385) were little correlated with the latent structure."
