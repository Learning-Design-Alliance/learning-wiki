# The Saga blended technology-enhanced tutoring model significantly improves standardized math scores, math GPA, and reduces math course failures (+0.19)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In the third RCT of the blended model (tutor every other day at 2:1, ALEKS on off days), students assigned to Saga performed significantly higher on standardized math measures (+0.19), with significant increases in math GPA and decreases in math course failures. [→ Saga Math (Evidence Summary) 2020](#saga-math-evidence-summary-2020)

## Evidence

### Saga Math (Evidence Summary) 2020

Saga Math (Evidence Summary). (2020). Evidence for ESSA program evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/saga-math/

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Third RCT (7 schools, Chicago and NYC, 2018-19/2019-20) randomized approximately 2,000 9th graders to daily blended-learning high-dosage tutoring or business-as-usual; results focus on the 2018-19 cohort. Saga students "performed significantly higher on standardized math measures... (effect size= +0.19)".

> "After controlling for pretests and other baseline demographics, students assigned to receive Saga performed significantly higher on standardized math measures at the end of the school year (effect size= +0.19) than control students"
