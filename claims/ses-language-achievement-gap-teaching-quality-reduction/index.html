Evidence¶

Lena Asp 2026¶

Lena Asp, Alli Klapp, & Victoria Rolfe. (2026). Associations between student-perceived teaching quality and students’ mathematics confidence and mathematics achievement: A study of Swedish grade 4 TIMSS 2019. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09767-y

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Comparison of Model C5 against reference models in the Swedish grade 4 TIMSS 2019 MSEM analyses. The study reports BOOKS at "0.30w; 0.66 b" in C5 versus 0.81***b in the reference model, suggesting "teaching quality could reduce some of the influence of SES"; only standardised path coefficients are printed, no effect size.

"The relations between SES (BOOKS) and mathematics achievement are significant and substantial at both levels (Model C5 = 0.30w; 0.66 b). However, the aggregated BOOKS estimate is lower than the reference model, where BOOKS had a highly significant relation to mathematics achievement (0.81***b). This result would suggest that teaching quality could reduce some of the influence of SES."
