# UMAT practice gains are concentrated in the lowest initial-performance quartile while the highest quartile's scores decrease on re-sitting

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2`–`r3` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2014 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Candidates in the lowest quartile of initial performance gained 16.2–24.8 percentiles on re-sitting, whereas those in the highest quartile lost 1.4–9.3 percentiles across total UMAT and its sections. [→ Puddey Ian B 2014](#puddey-ian-b-2014)
`q3 i?` The highest initial-performance quartile showed significant decreases on re-sitting, e.g. 1.4 ± 0.3 percentiles for total UMAT and 9.3 ± 0.3 for UMAT-2. [→ Puddey Ian B 2014 (2)](#puddey-ian-b-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Puddey Ian B 2014

Puddey Ian B, Mercer Annette, Andrich David, Styles Irene. (2014). Practice effects in medical school entrance testing with the undergraduate medicine and health sciences admission test (UMAT). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-48

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired comparison within the lowest initial-performance quartile, plotted in Figure 2. "In those in the lowest quartile of initial performance the increases were by 16.2 ± 0.3 percentiles for total UMAT score (Paired T-test P < 0.001)", with all three sections also significant.

> "In those in the lowest quartile of initial performance the increases were by 16.2 ± 0.3 percentiles for total UMAT score (Paired T-test P < 0.001), 16.8 ± 0.3 percentiles for UMAT-1 (Paired T-test P < 0.001), 21.1 ± 0.3 percentiles for UMAT-2 (Paired T-test P < 0.001) and 24.8 ± 0.4 percentiles for UMAT-3 (Paired T-test P < 0.001)."

### Puddey Ian B 2014 (2)

Puddey Ian B, Mercer Annette, Andrich David, Styles Irene. (2014). Practice effects in medical school entrance testing with the undergraduate medicine and health sciences admission test (UMAT). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-48

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Paired comparison within the highest initial-performance quartile (Figure 3), showing the opposite direction. "For those in the highest quartile of initial performance there were decreases by 1.4 ± 0.3 percentiles for total UMAT score (Paired T-test P < 0.001)" and parallel significant decreases in each section.

> "For those in the highest quartile of initial performance there were decreases by 1.4 ± 0.3 percentiles for total UMAT score (Paired T-test P < 0.001), 3.6 ± 0.3 percentiles for UMAT-1 (Paired T-test P < 0.001), 9.3 ± 0.3 percentiles for UMAT-2 (Paired T-test P < 0.001) and 8.5 ± 0.3 percentiles for UMAT-3 (Paired T-test P < 0.001)."
