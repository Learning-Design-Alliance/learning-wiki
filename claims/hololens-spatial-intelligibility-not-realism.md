## Evidence

### Lin Joseph 2026

Lin Joseph, Ghazi Sarmad, Kuss Ethan, Shehata Sarah. (2026). Medical students’ experiences of mixed-reality-based anatomy instruction in a non-cadaveric curriculum: a qualitative case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09881-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 12 first-year medical students in a non-cadaveric curriculum found students valued holograms for "orientation, scale, layering, and the relative positioning of structures" while calling them too simplified, brightly colored, or "cartoon-like" to approximate real anatomy.

> "What I really like about the HoloLens is you can actually see the 3D version of it—turn it around, look at it from the side, or from a view that isn’t in books or online."
