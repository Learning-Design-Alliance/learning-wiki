`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup analysis within the 217-respondent LA survey (Table 4). The quote reports the experience-in-oncology contrast; the article also prints parallel significant contrasts for age (> 44 years; P = .0038 and P = .0098) and leadership experience (P = .0083 and P = .0033). No effect sizes are printed.

> "Leaders with 10 or more years of experience in oncology, compared with those with < 10 years of experience, provided higher scores for the competencies of task management (92.84% v 87.24%, respectively; P = .0007) and leading others (84.69% v 79.42%, respectively; P = .0001)."
