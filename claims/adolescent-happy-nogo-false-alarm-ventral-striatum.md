`q3 · i?`

An fMRI study using an appetitive go/no-go variant with happy and calm face cues across children, adolescents and adults. The review reports the "disproportionately greater" adolescent false alarm rate on happy no-go trials, paralleled by heightened VS activity, consistent with subcortical-driven behaviour during adolescence.

> "the false alarm rate on no-go ‘happy’ trials relative to no-go neutral trials was disproportionately greater for adolescents (aged 13–17) than for either children (aged 6–12) or adults (18–29) (Somerville et al., 2011). This adolescent-specific performance dip was paralleled by heightened activity in the VS"
