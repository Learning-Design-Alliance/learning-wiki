Al-Asmar Ayah A., Oweis Yara, Ismail Noor H., Sabrah Alaa H. A., Abd-Raheam Islam M. (2021). The predictive value of high school grade point average to academic achievement and career satisfaction of dental graduates. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-021-01662-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Electronic survey of 525 recent graduates with 333 completed matched responses; Pearson correlation between employment status and graduating GPA was significant with no effect size printed. Respondents included 44.4% in the private sector and 15.9% unemployed.

> "Correlation results indicated that there was a statistically significant positive correlation between the present employment status and their AA-GPA (p < 0.05)."
