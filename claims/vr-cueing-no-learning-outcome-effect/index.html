Evidence¶

Daniela Decker 2025¶

Daniela Decker, Martin Merkt. (2025). Look at me! Can a pedagogical agent facilitate orientation and support learning in VR? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10531-9

q3 · i? · causal · r3

In the same 91-participant pre-registered between-subjects VR experiment, the knowledge test (22 open-ended questions, two per search object) showed "no significant effect of cueing for the learning outcomes"; no effect size beyond ηp2 =.02 was printed, so no magnitude is asserted.

"In contrast to Hypotheses 2a and 2b, the ANOVA revealed no significant effect of cueing for the learning outcomes (M = 6.68, SD = 3.38), F(2, 88) = 1.10, p = 0.336, ηp2 =.02."
