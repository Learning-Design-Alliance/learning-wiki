## Evidence

### Chen Yiwen 2025

Chen Yiwen, Song Jiali, Li Mengying, He Huijuan, Wang Xiangrong, Zhou Siyu, Wang Ling. (2025). Integrating jigsaw teaching into self-regulated learning instruction: an instructional design to improve nursing students’ self-regulated learning. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1437265

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA with time as within-group factor on intervention-group students' self-assessment across the three jigsaw cycles. The article reports an "increasing trend with prolonging the intervention time" with F = 6.778 (self-assessment); no effect sizes printed.

> "The Self-assessment (F = 6.778, p < 0.001).and group mutual assessment of nursing students (F = 11.037, p < 0.001) in the Intervention group at three time points would show an increasing trend with prolonging the intervention time."

### Chen Yiwen 2025 (2)

Chen Yiwen, Song Jiali, Li Mengying, He Huijuan, Wang Xiangrong, Zhou Siyu, Wang Ling. (2025). Integrating jigsaw teaching into self-regulated learning instruction: an instructional design to improve nursing students’ self-regulated learning. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1437265

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA (Table 3) on mutual-assessment scores of intervention-group students across the three jigsaw cycles (1st excretion, 2nd blood transfusion, 3rd penicillin). The article reports "a gradual increase in the total scores of the mutual assessment form", total F = 11.037, p < 0.001; no effect sizes printed.

> "This suggested a gradual increase in the total scores of the mutual assessment form and the scores of the dimensions of motivation to learn and task completion of the nursing students with time."
