Exploratory multi-group SEM by educational level (undergraduate vs graduate subgroups). The article states these analyses are "strictly exploratory rather than conﬁrmatory, given the limited subgroup sizes" and no formal measurement invariance testing.

"CC negatively predicted PR only among graduate students, whereas its negative direct association with UB appeared exclusively among undergraduates."
