## Evidence

### Boetje 2026

Boetje, J., Sichterman, B., Van Ginkel, S., Smakman, M., & Versendaal, J. (2026). Design Principles for Developing Information Problem Solving Competence in Higher Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543251338057

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Narrative synthesis of controlled pretest-posttest studies using peer-expert modeling videos. The review reports "a significant, medium to large, positive effect on IPS performance" relative to practice-only groups, persisting in a delayed posttest after one week; no standardized effect size value is printed.

> "Studies involving peer-expert videos—showing short fragments of each IPS process step, interspersed with reflective prompts (Frerejean et al., 2018) or explicit instruction (Mateos et al., 2018), and followed by practice—demonstrated a significant, medium to large, positive effect on IPS performance when compared to groups that only practiced"

### Boetje 2026 (2)

Boetje, J., Sichterman, B., Van Ginkel, S., Smakman, M., & Versendaal, J. (2026). Design Principles for Developing Information Problem Solving Competence in Higher Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543251338057

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Narrative synthesis of studies using non-expert peer models. The review attributes mixed results to differing modeling techniques (comedy excerpts vs. live demonstrations) and unvalidated outcome measures, advising caution.

> "The impact of non-expert models varied, showing either no effect on IPS knowledge (Hostetler & Luo, 2021) or a significant, large effect on understanding IPS concepts (Tewell, 2014)"
