`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Item‐level descriptive analysis in the 217‐nurse survey (Table 3). The article reports 13.8% correct for the hydrosol‐versus‐foam dressing item and 19.4% for the tight‐fixation item, and compares the latter with Fu et al.'s reported 9.6%. Percentages are descriptive.

> "Similarly, only 19.4% of nurses in our study correctly answered the item “Medical devices should be tightly fixed to avoid displacement,” indicating a prevalent misconception that may increase the risk of MDRPIs by causing excessive localised pressure."
