## Evidence

### Kula Yosef 2025

Kula Yosef, Wacht Oren, Ben Shlomo Izhar, Gitler Asaf, Gidron Yori. (2025). Does heart rate variability predict and improve performance in pediatric CPR?—a simulation study. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-025-01209-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

t-test comparison in the simulation study between participants with adequate compression rate (n = 31) and inadequate rate (n = 8): "only the SDNN was significantly higher (t (37) =-1.72,p = 0.0.012)". For compression depth (n = 28 vs n = 11), both differences were not significant (P > 0.05).

> "Participants who performed in the recommended compression rate (n = 31) had higher baseline RMSSD (49.7±22.83) and SDNN (50.93±22.78) than those without adequate compression rate (n = 8) RMSSD (38.13±7.20) and SDNN (34.75±6.49). However, only the SDNN was significantly higher (t (37) =-1.72,p = 0.0.012)."
