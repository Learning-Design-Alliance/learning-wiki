DreamBox Math evidence summary 2024¶

DreamBox Math evidence summary. (2024). https://support.dreambox.com/hc/en-us/articles/204906318-System-Requirements-for-Schools

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Randomized study in "three high-poverty charter schools in the San Francisco Bay Area" with over 500 K-1 students (87% Hispanic, 81% ELL) over more than 4 months. DreamBox users outperformed controls on the MAP subtest with "effect size =+0.16" and +0.11 overall.

"Students who used DreamBox Learning gained significantly more than control students on the Measurement and Geometry subtest of the NWEA MAP (effect size =+0.16) and scored higher on the test overall (effect size = +0.11)."
