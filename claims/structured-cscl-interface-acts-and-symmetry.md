Michael J. Baker. (2015). The Negotiation of Meaning in Epistemic Situations. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0050-3

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Comparison of interaction extracts from the Baker and Lund (1997) energy-chain task with 16-17-year-old students communicating at distance by Intranet, one pair per interface. The shorter structured extract contained 17 communicative acts versus 8 in the free-chat extract; graphical actions went the other way (5 versus 2). Counts are descriptive; no effect size is printed.
