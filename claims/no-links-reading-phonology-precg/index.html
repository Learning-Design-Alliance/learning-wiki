Related Claims¶

  • Cortical activation in left STG and lateral inferior precentral gyrus shows non-linear developmental change across three yearly fMRI sessions in 8–11-year-old children — related
  • Left STG developmental activation change interacts with baseline age: younger children peak at session 2, older children show more activation at session 3 — related
  • Lateral inferior pre-CG activation differed significantly between sessions 1 and 2, with other pairwise differences only at uncorrected levels — related
  • Children showing stronger behavioural recalibration effects show more left STG activation in session 2 than session 3, regardless of age — related
  • Phoneme-category response curves along the /aba/–/ada/ continuum remained stable across sessions, with a younger-child response bias toward /aba/ — related
  • No significant behavioural text-based recalibration effect was observed in the MRI scanner environment across the three sessions — related
