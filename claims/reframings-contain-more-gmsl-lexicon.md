## Evidence

### Kunal Handa 2023

Kunal Handa, Margaret Clapper, Jessica Boyle, Rose E Wang, Diyi Yang, David S Yeager, Dorottya Demszky. (2023). "Mistakes Help Us Grow": Facilitating and Evaluating Growth Mindset Supportive Language in Classrooms. [doi:10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.549](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.549)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Log odds ratio analysis (Monroe et al. measure) of n-grams across the four response types, with top n-grams reported in Table 2. Model reframings show "even more explicit GMSL" than expert reframings.

> "Both types of model reframings exhibit even more explicit GMSL, as ev- idenced by autonomy-supportive language (e.g., let's consider), positioning as a collaborative re- source (e.g., working together, let's collaborate), hope for change (e.g., continue exploring), and em- pathic validation (e.g., great effort and appreciate input)."
