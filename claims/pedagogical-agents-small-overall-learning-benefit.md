### Castro-Alonso 2021

Castro-Alonso, J. C., Wong, R. M., Adesope, O. O., & Paas, F. (2021). Effectiveness of Multimedia Pedagogical Agents Predicted by Diverse Theories: a Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09587-1

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis pooling 32 effect sizes from studies of multimedia pedagogical agents, total N = 2104. The printed pooled effect size is g+ = 0.20, a small effect favoring learning with agents over learning without them.

> "Our meta-analysis of 32 effect sizes ( N = 2104) revealed a small overall effect ( g + = 0.20), showing that learning with multimedia pedagogical agents was more effective than learning without these agents."
