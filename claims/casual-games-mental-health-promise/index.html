Evidence¶

Kurt Squire 2023¶

Kurt Squire, Garrison Wells, Maria J. Anderson-Coto, and Constance Steinkuehler. 2023. Casual Games, Cognition, and Play across the Lifespan: A Critical Synthesis. ACM Games: Research and Practice, Volume 1, Issue 2, Article 14. https://doi.org/10.1145/3594534

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Pine and colleagues [2020], a meta-analysis of 13 studies on casual games and mental health finding "real promise for mitigating the entire range of mental health issues examined". No pooled effect size is printed in the review.

"In a meta-analysis of 13 studies examining the effects of casual games on anxiety, depression, stress, and low mood, Pine and colleagues [2020] found that casual games show real promise for mitigating the entire range of mental health issues examined."
