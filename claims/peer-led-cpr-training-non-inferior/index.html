Lee Hyeon-Ji 2026¶

Lee Hyeon-Ji. (2026). Training interval, feedback, and retention in cardiopulmonary resuscitation education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09538-4

q3 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of the target-population theme across 20 studies; the review states "peer-led training was non-inferior to instructor-led approaches, supporting the scalability of alternative training models," and Table 3 records a school-student non-inferiority RCT on CPR pass rate. Younger-population studies showed successful basic skill acquisition.

"In particular, randomized trials reported that peer-led training was non-inferior to instructor-led approaches, supporting the scalability of alternative training models."
