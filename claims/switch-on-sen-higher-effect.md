## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The effect size for SEN pupils (+0.33 on gain scores) is considerably higher than for non-SEN pupils. [→ Gorard et al. 2014](#gorard-et-al-2014)

## Evidence

### Gorard et al. 2014

Stephen Gorard, Beng Huat See, Nadia Siddiqui. (2014). Switch-on Reading: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/evaluation

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Subgroup analysis of the randomised trial: SEN pupils (n=225) showed +0.33 (CI 0.04 to 0.57) on gain scores; the report calls it "intriguing that the intervention appears to be especially effective for them".

> "A slightly different picture emerges from consideration of pupils reported by their schools to have special needs or specific learning challenges (SEN). The effect size for these pupils is considerably higher than for the rest"
