## Evidence

### Conroy Elizabeth J. 2015

Conroy Elizabeth J., Kirkham Jamie J., Bellis Jennifer R., Peak Matthew, Smyth Rosalind L., Williamson Paula R., Pirmohamed Munir. (2015). A pilot randomised controlled trial to assess the utility of an e‐learning package that trains users in adverse drug reaction causality. International Journal of Pharmacy Practice. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijpp.12197

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome of the same pilot RCT: the route taken on the LCAT flowchart was scored against a route defined by a multidisciplinary expert panel. Means were 5.89 (training) versus 4.88 (control); the printed effect was "1.01 correct classifications (95% CI, −0.3 to 2.3)", with no significance test or effect size reported.

> "The secondary outcome, score based on correct route, ranged from a minimum of 2 to a maximum of 8 out of 20 across both arms. The effect of the training package increased the score by correct route by 1.01 correct classifications (95% CI, −0.3 to 2.3)."
