## Evidence

### Fraser S 2019

Fraser S, Wright AD, van Donkelaar P, Smirl JD. (2019). Cross-sectional comparison of spiral versus block integrated curriculums in preparing medical students to diagnose and manage concussions. BMC Medical Education 19(17). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1439-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results of the cross-sectional survey: chi-square tests on the long-term consequences items all favored the spiral group; Table 2 prints percentages of 89.7 vs 65.7 (dementia), 79.5 vs 57.1 (second impact), 47.4 vs 17.1 (Parkinsonism), 73.1 vs 50 (CTE).

> "students in the spiral curriculum identified dementia (X21, 148 = 12.57, p < 0.001), second impact (X21, 148 = 8.60, p = 0.003), Parkinsonism (X21, 148 = 14.87, p < 0.001), and CTE (X21, 148 = 8.35, p = 0.004) significantly more frequently than those in the block curriculum"
