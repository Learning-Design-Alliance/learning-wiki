## Evidence

### Conroy 2018

Conroy, M. A., Sutherland, K. S., Algina, J., Werch, B., & Ladwig, C. (2018). Prevention and Treatment of Problem Behaviors in Young Children: Clinical Implications From a Randomized Controlled Trial of BEST in CLASS. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858417750376

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple-interaction decomposition of the significant three-way Condition × Pretest × State interaction on SSIS-RS Social Skills in the cluster-randomized trial. In State B the simple interaction was not significant, though the condition effect there was significant (SRD = −.161).

> "A simple interaction analysis resulted in a significant Condition × Pretest interaction for State A (z = −2.104, p  = .035), with a significant condition effect for children in the clinical or borderline range at pretest (z  = −3.112, p  = .002, SRD = −.277)."
