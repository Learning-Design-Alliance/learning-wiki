Jacob Whitehill and Margo Seltzer. (2017). A Crowdsourcing Approach To Collecting Tutorial Videos – Toward Personalized Learning-at-Scale. [doi:10.1145/3051457.3053973](https://doi.org/10.1145/3051457.3053973)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of the Experiment I corpus, reported as a diversity examination across dimensions of presentation format, language, and pedagogical approach. The article lists the five most common presentation formats and notes dialect variability, e.g. the Indian-origin multiplication phrasing "5 into x".

> "The five most common formats include: (1) a video of the teacher writing on paper (2) a video of the teacher’s computer screen that is used as an electronic notepad; (3) a video of the teacher speaking directly to the learner in a face video along with written materials"
