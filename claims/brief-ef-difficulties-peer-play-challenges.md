`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal multiple regression predicting kindergarten-exit parent-reported PIPPS peer play scores from entry BRIEF-II in the followed sample. The article reports higher parent-reported EF difficulties predicted more disconnected and disruptive play; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Longitudinal analyses revealed that BRIEF‐II at kindergarten entry was positively associated with PIPPS Disconnection (B = 0.78, SE = 0.19, p < .001) and Disruption (B = 0.56, SE = 0.15, p = .001) at kindergarten exit."

Concurrent multiple regression at kindergarten entry relating parent-reported BRIEF-II to parent-reported PIPPS domains. The article reports "positively associated with current PIPPS Disconnection" and Disruption; both measures are parent reports, and no standardized effect size is printed.

> "BRIEF‐II was positively associated with current PIPPS Disconnection (B = 1.08, SE = 0.28, p = .001) and Disruption (B = 0.57, SE = 0.16, p = .001; higher PIPPS scores indicate more impairment for these two domains)."
