Robel S, Tigist W, Feleke Y, Moges M. (2025). Exploring pediatric residents’ readiness for point-of-care ultrasound in two teaching hospitals in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia: insights into training, confidence, and practice. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07959-1

Descriptive survey results from Table 3 of the 97-resident cross-sectional study. Barriers listed include lack of training (72.2%), lack of equipment (14.4%), and qualified faculty as educators (12.4%); the top preferred intervention was "dedicated practice with an ultrasound machine (40.2%)".

> "The most significant perceived barrier to POCUS practice is a lack of training (72.2%). The most preferred intervention to improve POCUS proficiency is dedicated practice with an ultrasound machine (40.2%)."
