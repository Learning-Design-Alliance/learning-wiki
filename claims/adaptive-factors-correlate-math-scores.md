## Evidence

### Xia et al. 2022

Qi Xia, Hongbiao Yin, Ruonan Hu, Xiuhan Li, and Junjie Shang. (2022). Motivation, Engagement, and Mathematics Achievement: An Exploratory Study Among Chinese Primary Students. SAGE Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/21582440221134609

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Table 2, n=1,538) relating the four MES higher-order factors to the end-of-semester mathematics exam score. The printed correlations include MATH-AE r=.136**, MATH-AM r=.225**, MATH-ME r=-.207**, MATH-MM r=-.267**, all significant.

> "The results suggest that adaptive factors (AM and AE) were positively related to mathematics scores, while the two maladaptive factors (MM and ME) were negatively related to mathematics scores"
