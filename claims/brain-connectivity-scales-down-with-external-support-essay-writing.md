## Evidence

### Nataliya Kosmyna 2025

Nataliya Kosmyna, Eugene Hauptmann, Ye Tong Yuan, Jessica Situ, Xian-Hao Liao, Ashly Vivian Beresnitzky, Iris Braunstein, Pattie Maes. (2025). Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task. Preprint, under review. https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08872

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

EEG analysis (dDTF) of participants in the LLM, Search Engine, and Brain-only groups writing essays, reported in the study's summary of results. The paper states "Brain connectivity systematically scaled down with the amount of external support" across the three assigned tool conditions. No effect size is printed for this group contrast.

> "Brain connectivity systematically scaled down with the amount of external support: the Brain‑only group exhibited the strongest, widest‑ranging networks, Search Engine group showed intermediate engagement, and LLM assistance elicited the weakest overall coupling."
