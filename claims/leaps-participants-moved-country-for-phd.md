`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of alumni career data collected from program archives and online searches. Of 122 participants with known pre- and post-LEAPS affiliations, "74 students (61%) choosing a different country for the Ph.D.". The authors frame this as LEAPS possibly providing additional motivation for international careers; no effect size is printed.

> "The number of LEAPS participants that have undertaken or are undertaking a Ph.D., and for which we have information about their affiliation both before and after LEAPS, is 122. Of them, we recorded 74 students (61%) choosing a different country for the Ph.D. than that of the affiliation before LEAPS."
