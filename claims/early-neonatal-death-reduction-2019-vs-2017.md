### Munyaw Yuda 2022

Beyond research: improved perinatal care through scale-up of a Moyo fetal heart rate monitor coupled with simulation training in northern Tanzania for helping babies breathe. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way ANOVA with Tukey post hoc comparisons of DHIS2 perinatal outcome rates by year (Table 4 prints F = 6.067, p = 0.0362 for 24 h and F = 6.096, p = 0.0359 for 7 days; Tukey 2019–2017 p = 0.0304650 and 0.0312455). No effect size is printed.
