Analysis of the Y1 ScoutRoach focal group's work led the design team to this conclusion; the group needed targeted teacher support to meet the engineering design goals, and another student created its program.

"it became clear to the design team that while the curriculum’s engineering design goals were perhaps appropriate for a single-discipline curriculum, they were too extensive for an integrated project."
