### Angrist N 2020

Angrist N, Bergman P, Evans DK, Hares S, Jukes MCH, Letsomo T. (2020). Practical lessons for phone-based assessments of learning. BMJ Global Health. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2020-003030

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive results from the first wave of the Botswana phone-based assessment. The authors note the 'understands' judgement "is a subjective judgement made by the assessor and so is less objective than the rate of correct answers"; no effect size is printed.

> "Data from our first wave of phone-based results reveal that over 75% of students understood and answered all the problems, and 24% understood all the problems but could only answer some."
