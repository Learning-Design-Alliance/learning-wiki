### Okada 2024

Okada, A., Sherborne, T., Panselinas, G., & Kolionis, G. (2024). AI in Education: A Cross-National Study of Open Schooling Using the CARE-KNOW-DO Framework for Sustainable Development Goals. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3960164/v1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quantitative analysis of Likert-scale questionnaire data using SPSS 27; the article reports "a high Cronbach’s alpha of 0.916", a "KMO measure of 0.906", and seven clusters from Varimax-rotated EFA.

> "A high Cronbach’s alpha of 0.916 demonstrated the instrument's reliability and internal consistency. An adequate sample size was conrmed by a KMO measure of 0.906 and Bartlett’s test chi-square statistic, validating the suitability of the factor analysis. Varimax rotation in EFA identied seven clusters aligning with the CARE-KNOW-DO framework's core and subconstructs."
