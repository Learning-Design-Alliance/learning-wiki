Paul W. Irving and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2015). Becoming a Physicist: The Roles of Research, Mindsets, and Milestones in upper-division student perceptions. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020120)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenographic analysis of 45-minute semi-structured interviews with 21 upper-division physics students at Kansas State University. The authors report the themes emerging from the category analysis: "research and its association with being a physicist; diﬀerences in mindset; and exclusivity of accomplishments." No effect size is applicable to this qualitative finding.
