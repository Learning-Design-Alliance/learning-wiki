## Subclaims
`q2 i?` K-means clustering of duration, velocity, stops and size separated eleven consonants into an easy cluster and a difficult cluster containing b, f, g and p. [→ Danna 2021](#danna-2021)

## Evidence

### Danna 2021

Danna, J., Longcamp, M., Nalborczyk, L., Velay, J.-L., Commengé, C., & Jover, M. (2021). Interaction between orthographic and graphomotor constraints in learning to write. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3596-880X

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-experiment in which 19 Grade 1 students copied eleven consonant-vowel bigrams in cursive on a digitizing tablet; z-scored duration, velocity, stops and size were entered in a K-means cluster analysis with inter-cluster Euclidian distance of 0.74. The article states "b, f, g, and p are more difficult to trace than the other tested consonants".

> "This pre-experiment confirmed that b, f, g, and p are more difficult to trace than the other tested consonants for 1st grade students."
