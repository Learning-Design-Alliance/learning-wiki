## Evidence

### Romanovska 2022

Romanovska, L., Janssen, R. & Bonte, M. (2022). Longitudinal changes in cortical responses to letter-speech sound stimuli in 8–11 year-old children. npj Science of Learning 7:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00118-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal fMRI study of 18 children scanned yearly with a text-based recalibration task. RM ANOVA on exposure-block activation found session effects only in "the left STG and lateral inferior precentral gyrus" at whole-brain FDR q < 0.05; plotted individual betas indicated non-linear changes.

> "RM ANOVA analyses investigating changes in cortical activation across sessions, revealed significant clusters only in the left STG and lateral inferior precentral gyrus (pre-CG; Fig. 4; whole-brain FDR threshold q < 0.05)."
