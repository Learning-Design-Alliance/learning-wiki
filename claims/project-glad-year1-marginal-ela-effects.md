## Evidence

### Deussen et al. 2014

Theresa Deussen, Elizabeth Autio, Angela Roccograndi, Makoto Hanita. (2014). The Impact of Project GLAD on Students' Literacy and Science Learning: Year 1 Results from a Cluster-Randomized Trial of Sheltered Instruction. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

EL subgroup HLM analysis (current and recently reclassified ELs, 13% of the analytic sample) in the cluster randomized trial. Both GM measures showed "marginal positive effects" at p<.10 with g = 0.24 and g = 0.21.

> "Both the GM reading comprehension and vocabulary measures had marginal positive effects (b=6.87, p=0.099, Hedges g = 0.24 for comprehension and b=5.72, p=0.092, Hedges g =  0.21 for vocabulary)."
