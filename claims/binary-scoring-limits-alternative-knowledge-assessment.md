### Bao 2006

L. Bao and E. F. Redish. (2006). Model analysis: Representing and assessing the dynamics of student learning. Phys. Rev. ST Phys. Educ. Res. 2(1). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.2.010103

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

The article's methodological argument that binary scoring 'greatly limits the capacity' of standardized multiple-choice tests to assess alternative knowledge. This is the authors' analytic critique, not a measured comparison.

> "many existing quantitative analysis methods often focus on a binary question of whether a student answers a question correctly or not. This greatly limits the capacity of using the standardized multiple-choice tests in assessing students' alternative knowledge."
