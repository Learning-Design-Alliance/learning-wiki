Evidence¶

Pablo Egana-delSol 2023¶

Pablo Egana-delSol. (2023). Author Correction: The impacts of a high-school art-based program on academic achievements, creativity, and creative behaviors. npj Science of Learning 8:47. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00196-5

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

This is the author correction notice itself, not an empirical study. It states that the original article's title and one sentence mislabeled the studied program's school level, and that "high-school" is the correct descriptor. No data or effect sizes are reported.

"In this article the title was incorrectly given as ‘The impacts of a middle-school art-based program on academic achievements, creativity, and creative behaviors ’ but should have been ‘The impacts of a high-school art-based program on academic achievements, creativity, and creative behaviors ’."
