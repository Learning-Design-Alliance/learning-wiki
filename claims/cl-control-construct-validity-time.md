### Massey Patrick A. 2025

Massey Patrick A., Scalisi Wayne, Barton Richard Shane, Hansen Austin, Montgomery Carver, Solitro Giovanni F. (2025). Validity Assessment of a Three-Dimensional Printed Arthroscopic Shoulder Simulator: An Experimental Evaluation of Construct Validity and Educational Value. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-25-00225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled trial arm with five medical students and five expert surgeons on the commercial control simulator (Alex 3, Sawbones). Students "had longer time to complete the arthroscopic tasks than the expert surgeons on the control simulator" (729 s ± 197 versus 295 s ± 120, P = 0.003).
