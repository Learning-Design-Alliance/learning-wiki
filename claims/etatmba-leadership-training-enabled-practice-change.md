Can training in advanced clinical skills in obstetrics, neonatal care and leadership, of non-physician clinicians in Malawi impact on clinical services improvements (the ETATMBA project): a process evaluation. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Second interview round, 6 months after module 2 on clinical leadership, with an opportunistic sample of 12 trainees from the seven intervention districts. District Health Officers reported trainees "were taking leading roles in improving healthcare practice" and trainees described empowerment to approach management and redirect resources.

> "Of the 12 trainees interviewed, 10 talked about how the leadership training had helped them work better with those around them."
