### Megan Ivory 2023 (2)

Megan Ivory, Alisa Bettale, Rachel Boren, Ashlyn D. Burch, Jake Douglass, Lisa Hackett, Boris Kiefer, Alina Kononov, Maryanne Long, Mekena Metcalf, Tzula B. Propp, Mohan Sarovar. (2023). Quantum Computing, Math, and Physics (QCaMP): Introducing quantum computing in high schools. [doi:10.1109/qce57702.2023.20318](https://doi.org/10.1109/qce57702.2023.20318)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructor observation of the same final project: students grasped device characterization and running Quantum Composer readily, and the authors attribute this to the substantial preparation from the week's curriculum. No quantitative evaluation was performed.

> "Students best grasped how to characterize quantum devices and how to get results by running Quantum Composer. Quantum circuit implementation and device characterization proved to be one of the least challenging aspects of the activity due to the substantial preparation from the week’s curriculum."
