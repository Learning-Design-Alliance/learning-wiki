Authors' interpretation of the RFR partial dependence results (Figs. 2 and 3) for the national cohort. The article states the association "is not linear" and that meeting the criterion-referenced fitness standard correlates with increased likelihood of higher academic scores; no effect size is printed.

> "It is important to highlight that the association between academic achievement and CRF, as revealed by the RFR, is not linear."
