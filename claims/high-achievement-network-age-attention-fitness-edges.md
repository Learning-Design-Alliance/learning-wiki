Edge weights from the GGM network for the high-achievement subgroup, reported alongside Figure 1b. The authors call the negative age–attention association "counterintuitive" and suggest it may reflect greater attention fluctuations or mind-wandering in older students in this setting.

> "In the high achievement group (Figure 1b), age was negatively correlated with sustained (−0.58) and alternating attention (−0.47), while physical fitness was positively correlated with motor coordination (0.39) and physical activity (0.32)."
