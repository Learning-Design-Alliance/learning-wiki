### Ricoy M.-C. 2022

Ricoy M.-C., Martínez-Carrera S., Martínez-Carrera I. (2022). Social Overview of Smartphone Use by Teenagers. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 19(22), 15068. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192215068

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the drawbacks category of the content analysis, health effects totalled 973/1704, led by addiction/dependency (386), stress (216), insomnia (114) and obesity (92); psychosocial codes included sexting (284), nomophobia (252) and phubbing (102).

> "Multiple newspaper reports (386/1704) alert the public to the dependence or addiction behaviours that excessive use of smartphones can generate in teenagers; and indicate that it tends to cause low academic performance and deterioration of their interpersonal relationships."
