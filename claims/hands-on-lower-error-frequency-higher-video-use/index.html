Zhanyong Qi, Jiayi Wang, Yue Du, Xinyue Zhang. (2026). The effect of blended learning approaches on vocational school students' practical skills performance and learning behaviors: virtual simulations or hands-on activities while accessing instructional videos. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09793-4

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive frequency comparison of video-control behaviors (play, pause, fast-forward, rewind, speed) between the two video-review conditions, from the coded log data of the 2x2 experiment.

"It was further noted that participants in hands-on activity + video review demonstrated a higher frequency of video operations compared to those in virtual simulation + video review, indicating a preference for video-assisted learning during practical tasks."
