Poelman A. A. M., Cochet-Broch M., Beelen J., Wiggins B., Heffernan J. E., & Cox D. N. (2021). Teacher Evaluation of an Experiential Vegetable Education Program for Australian Primary Schools: Does Face-to-Face Training Add Value above Digital Training? `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of 11 repeated statements between NSW teachers in the pilot evaluation and NSW teachers in the current study. The article reports that "the teachers rated the current program higher on the usefulness of the supporting materials (U = 2.5, p = 0.01) and the preparation efforts needed for the program (U = 2.8, p < 0.01)".

> "Compared to this earlier version of the program, the teachers rated the current program higher on the usefulness of the supporting materials (U = 2.5, p = 0.01) and the preparation efforts needed for the program (U = 2.8, p < 0.01) (Figure 1), with no statistical differences between the two versions for the other statements."
