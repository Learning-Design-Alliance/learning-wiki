## Evidence

### Yasuda Yukinori 2026

Yasuda Yukinori, Takehara Shoichiro, Inoue Soichiro. (2026). Quantitative analysis of gaze and body movement differences by proficiency in direct and video laryngoscope intubation. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44432-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Motion-capture analysis of minimum head-to-mouth distance with a direct laryngoscope in seven experts and eight novices; experts 0.529 ± 0.104 m versus novices 0.331 ± 0.055 m, "a statistically significant difference". A height comparison showed no significant difference.

> "A t-test conducted at a significance level of 0.05 revealed a statistically significant difference between the two groups (seven experts and eight novices) [t (13) = 4.339, p = 0.001]."
