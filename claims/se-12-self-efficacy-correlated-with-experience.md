## Evidence

### Axboe Mette K. 2016

Axboe Mette K., Christensen Kaj S., Kofoed Poul-Erik, Ammentorp Jette. (2016). Development and validation of a self-efficacy questionnaire (SE-12) measuring the clinical communication skills of health care professionals. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0798-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional test of the second construct-validity hypothesis on the 292-clinician sample, comparing sum scores across seniority bands. The rank test was statistically significant, which the authors say confirmed that self-efficacy is highly correlated to experience in the field.

> "Participants with the most experience within their field had a higher self-efficacy sum score compared to participants with less experience. A Kruskal-Wallis equality-of-populations rank test was performed (chi-square = 12.94 with 5 degrees of freedom; P = 0.024)."
