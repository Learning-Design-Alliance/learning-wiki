Path analysis within a two-wave prospective SEM study of 243 Chinese collegiate basketball players; the two perfectionistic-concerns facets showed opposite-signed significant direct paths to self-reported extra training, with the quoted standardized coefficients. The DA direction contradicted hypothesis 1a.

> "results revealed a significant negative association of concern over mistake (β = −0.13, SE = 0.06, p = 0.021), and a positive association of doubts about action (β = 0.18, SE = 0.06, p = 0.005) with extra training"
