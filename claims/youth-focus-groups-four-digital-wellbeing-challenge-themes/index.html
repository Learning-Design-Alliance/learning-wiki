Karim 2025¶

Karim, S., Liberman, B., & Trettin, S. (2025). Tech Talk: Co-Designing Youth Digital Well-Being Solutions. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/264

q2 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative findings from two 1-hour focus groups with youth ages 14-18, facilitated by youth advisors with ideas captured on Padlet. The report states youth shared that "their excessive screen time and constant connectivity have led to increased anxiety, stress, and even "drama" with peers."

"Addiction and compulsive use: Youth shared that they and their peers use social media often and that their excessive screen time and constant connectivity have led to increased anxiety, stress, and even "drama" with peers."
