## Evidence

### Star 2013

Star, J. R., Rittle-Johnson, B., Durkin, K., Newton, K., Pollack, C., Lynch, K., & Gogolen, C. (2013). The Impact of a Comparison Curriculum in Algebra I: A Randomized Experiment. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED564086

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Year-long randomized control trial with 77 teachers and 1,661 students in 57 Massachusetts public schools; two-level hierarchical linear models estimated condition effects on two outcomes. The article reports "a negligible impact on both outcomes."

> "Model estimates indicated that students in the treatment condition scored about 2 percentage points higher on the CC measure and about 5 points higher on the Acuity measure, on average, than students in the control condition (see Table 2)."
