`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis of the same Sicilian GP pre-post cohort restricted to smokers (indicator 3). The article reports values "higher than those recorded in HSD (65.3%)"; Table 4 shows p<0.001 at both follow-ups versus baseline.

> "By restricting the analysis only to smokers, the proportion of COPD patients with a spirometry lifetime increased from 70.5 to 77.7% and 81.0% over time; these values were higher than those recorded in HSD (65.3%)."
