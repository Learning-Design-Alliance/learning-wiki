## Evidence

### de Villiers Scheepers 2015

de Villiers Scheepers, M. J. & Maree, L. (2015). Fostering Team Creativity in Higher Education Settings. e-Journal of Business Education & Scholarship of Teaching, 9(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1167371

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA on the same quasi-experimental student sample. Problem-solving means rose from 3.400b at initiation to 3.988a after the workshop and were 3.820a three months later, with no significant decline. The authors attribute retention to "specific processes and guidelines practiced during the workshops".

> "The ANOVA indicate a significant effect for problem-solving skills for the different stickiness stages as well [Wilks Lambda=0.31, F(3, 46) =34.521, p<0.0005, multivariate partial eta squared=.69]"
