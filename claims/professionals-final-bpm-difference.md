### Mocellin D 2026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA and post hoc Bonferroni comparison of mean ventilation rate across the 3 simulator configuration groups among health professionals during the final evaluation session, reported in the Results section. The article reports the printed group means, ANOVA P value, and post hoc P value.

> "In contrast, a significant difference was observed in mean BPM across groups (mean [SD] BPM: medium-fidelity passive group, 42.8 [7.0] breaths/min; high-fidelity passive group, 39.9 [8.3] breaths/min; high-fidelity active group, 48.8 [8.9] breaths/min; ANOVA P = .04), with the high-fidelity passive group achieving values closest to the reference rate of 40 breaths/min. Post hoc Bonferroni analysis identified a significant difference between high-fidelity passive and high-fidelity active groups (P = .04)"
