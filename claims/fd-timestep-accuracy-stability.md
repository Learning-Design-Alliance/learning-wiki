### Peter Hugo Nelson 2012

Peter Hugo Nelson. (2012). Teaching introductory STEM with the Marble Game. [doi:10.1016/j.bpj.2012.11.2943](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpj.2012.11.2943)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Module 3 classroom activity: students implement the FD method in a spreadsheet and graphically compare its predictions with an analytical equation for the normalized order parameter u(t) across timestep sizes. Through this comparison they "discover that the accuracy and stability of the FD method depends on the size of the timestep"; the article reports no quantitative test of this discovery.

> "Students graphically compare the predictions of the FD model with the analytical equation for various timestep sizes in the FD method and then discover that the accuracy and stability of the FD method depends on the size of the timestep."
