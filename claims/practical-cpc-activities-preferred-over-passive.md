## Evidence

### Knox Shane 2014

Knox Shane, Cullen Walter, Dunne Colum. (2014). Continuous Professional Competence (CPC) for Irish paramedics and advanced paramedics: a national study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-41

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey ratings of 23 potential CPC activities (Table 2). Respondents rated practical hands-on activities highest; the highest 'not relevant' responses were "e-learning modules only and no related practice 36% (210), project work 27% (166), appraisal of journal publications 24% (147)."

> "Most respondents considered practical-type learning relevant to their roles (Table 2): practical training scenarios 94% (582), annual cardiac re-certification 92% (566), access to e-learning followed by related practice 90% (566), and training on simulation manikins 88% (535)."
