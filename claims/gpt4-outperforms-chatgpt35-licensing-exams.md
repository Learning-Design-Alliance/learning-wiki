## Evidence

### Jin Hye Kyung 2024

Jin Hye Kyung, Lee Ha Eun, Kim EunYoung. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT-3.5 and GPT-4 in national licensing examinations for medicine, pharmacy, dentistry, and nursing: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05944-8

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects subgroup analysis comparing the two ChatGPT model versions across the included studies. GPT-4 pooled accuracy was "80.4% (95% CI, 78.6-82.0%)" versus 58.9% for ChatGPT-3.5; the difference was significant (Q = 177.027, p < 0.001).

> "The effect size for the percentage of performance with regard to the ChatGPT models was 58.9% (95% CI, 56.2-61.6%) for ChatGPT-3.5 and 80.4% (95% CI, 78.6-82.0%) for GPT-4."
