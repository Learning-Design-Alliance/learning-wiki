`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Individual-level LMM analysis of Scenario A data showed no significant effects of synchrony or arousal on any of the three outcomes. The study reports no effect sizes, so the null is not evidence of equivalence.

> "In Scenario A, neither PSind (γ10) nor PAind (γ20) had a signiﬁcant impact on post-stress levels, task performance satisfaction, or collaboration performance satisfaction (Table 3)."

### Lixiang Yan · Dragan Gašević · Vanessa Echeverria · Linxuan Zhao · Yueqiao Jin · Xinyu Li · Roberto Martinez-Maldonado 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Group-level LMM model comparisons for Scenario A found no significant fit improvements when synchrony, arousal, or their interaction were added for any post-simulation outcome.

> "Model comparisons, starting with the null model (Model 2.0), showed no signiﬁcant improvements in model ﬁt when PSgrp (Model 2.1), PAgrp (Model 2.2), or their interaction (Model 2.3) were added."
