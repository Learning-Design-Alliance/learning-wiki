### Muntean Lorena Mihaela 2022

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square association analysis in the same prospective pilot study of 179 medical students relating the DECAS extraversion dimension to National Residency Exam score. The article reports "the chance that a student with low extraversion will get a better grade is 5.12 times higher" with OR = 0.512, p = 0.03, and d = −0.369.
