The paper attributes this position to Barkatoolah (1990) in its adult learning section, presenting it as current thinking in adult education rather than as a tested finding; no study design or effect size is reported.

> "Today, in fact, it is thought that the adult learns better when the matter being learned or the methods used can be linked to his previous experience and that this experience can be reinvested in the new learning process."
