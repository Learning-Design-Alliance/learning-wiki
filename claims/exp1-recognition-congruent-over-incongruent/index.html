"Participants ( N = 29) were, on average, more likely to remember the congruent new-words (congruent: 62.4% vs. incongruent: 38.9%, main effect of congruence: χ2(1) = 78.32 p < 0.0001) after 24 h"

  • In the laboratory experiment, feedback order effects on next-day recognition were specific to congruent trials, with a trend for feedback boosting incongruent-item memory regardless of timing — related
  • In the laboratory experiment, withdrawing feedback in the second half of learning significantly lowered next-day recognition of congruent word meanings, while adding feedback later did not impair recognition — related
  • In an online replication, adding feedback in the second half improved immediate extraction of concrete word meanings for congruent trials only, replicating the laboratory effect — related
