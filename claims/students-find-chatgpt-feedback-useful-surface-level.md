`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive thematic analysis of 13 handwritten student reflections in a Saudi undergraduate technical communication course; Theme 1 was explicitly articulated by all 13 participants per Table 1. Students described feedback as "clear," "direct," "well-organized," and "objective."

> "All thirteen participants described the ChatGPT-generated feedback as useful, particularly valuing its identification of specific issues, for example , a grammar error or a disorganised paragraph, rather than vague evaluative comments such as “needs improvement.”"
