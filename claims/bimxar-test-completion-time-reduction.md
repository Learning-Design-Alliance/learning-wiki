## Evidence

### Ziad Ashour 2022

Ziad Ashour, Zohreh Shaghaghian, Wei Yan. (2022). BIMxAR: BIM-Empowered Augmented Reality for Learning Architectural Representations. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.03207](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.03207)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Completion-time analysis of the pilot study, with two participants excluded as pretest-duration outliers, found mean SBST completion time "improved (reduced) by 35.74%" and mean ART completion time "improved (reduced) by 38.11%".

> "The mean completion times of the SBST (mean Pretest = 12.95 minutes, mean Posttest = 8.32 minutes) improved (reduced) by 35.74%. The mean completion times of the ART (mean Pretest =20.87 minutes, mean Posttest = 12.91 minutes) improved (reduced) by 38.11%."
