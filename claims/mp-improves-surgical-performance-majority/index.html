Evidence¶

Snelgrove Huon; Gabbott Ben 2020¶

Snelgrove Huon; Gabbott Ben. (2020). Critical analysis of evidence about the impacts on surgical teams of ‘mental practice’ in systematic reviews: a systematic rapid evidence assessment (SREA). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02131-3

q2 · i? · review · r3

A systematic rapid evidence assessment of six systematic reviews of mental practice in surgery (searches July–December 2018). The review of reviews reports that of 73 experimental reports, "fifty-one reported positive impacts of MP while 16 described no effect, or negative effects on performance".

"Of 73 experimental reports contained in the reviews, fifty-one reported positive impacts of MP while 16 described no effect, or negative effects on performance."

Snelgrove Huon; Gabbott Ben 2020 (2)¶

Snelgrove Huon; Gabbott Ben. (2020). Critical analysis of evidence about the impacts on surgical teams of ‘mental practice’ in systematic reviews: a systematic rapid evidence assessment (SREA). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02131-3

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis of the six included reviews, categorising reported outcomes on a modified Kirkpatrick typology. The authors report that at levels 2a and 2b "intervention groups significantly outperformed controls", measured with OSATS, Global Rating Scales and stress measures; no effect size is printed.

"the majority of outcomes are at level 2a (Learner perceptions) and 2b (Knowledge & Skills acquisition) where the reported evidence base is stronger and intervention groups significantly outperformed controls"
