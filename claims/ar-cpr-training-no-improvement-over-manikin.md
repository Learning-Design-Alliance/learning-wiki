## Evidence

### Sabery Mahdieh 2026

Sabery Mahdieh, Batooli Zahra, Sabbarifard Ebrahim, Ghaffari Faezeh. (2026). Simulation-based feedback systems in cardiopulmonary resuscitation training: a scoping review. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-026-01553-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Leary et al. (2020), a comparison of an AR CPR training application (CPReality) with a standard AV feedback manikin in healthcare providers; the AR system had slightly higher compression rates but lower depths, and both methods yielded low percentages within guideline recommendations.

> "The AR CPR training application did not improve CPR quality compared to the standard audio-visual feedback manikin in terms of chest compression rate and depth."
