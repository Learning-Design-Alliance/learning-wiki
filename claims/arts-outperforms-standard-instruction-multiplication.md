Everett Mettler, Christine M. Massey, Philip J. Kellman. (2011). Improving Adaptive Learning Technology through the Use of Response Times. Proceedings of the 33rd Annual Meeting of the Cognitive Science Society. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED549436
