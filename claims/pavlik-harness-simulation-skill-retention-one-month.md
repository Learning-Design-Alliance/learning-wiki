### Moktar Joel MD 2016

Moktar Joel MD, Bradley Catharine S. BScPT MSc, Maxwell Alexandra BSc, Wedge John H. MD FRCS(C), Kelley Simon P. MBChB FRCS(Tr&Orth), Murnaghan M. Lucas MD MEd FRCSC. (2016). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA across baseline, post-intervention, and one-month retention time points for both trained groups. All participants in both groups were scored as competent post-intervention and at retention; the discussion states retention "with no evidence of decay in competency".

> "Repeated-measures ANOVA of the scores of the resident and allied health groups demonstrated a significant difference between baseline and post-intervention testing and between baseline and retention testing for all three measures (p < 0.001)."
