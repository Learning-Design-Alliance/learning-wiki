## Evidence

### Ketcheson et al. 2017

Leah Ketcheson, Janet Hauck and Dale Ulrich. (2017). The effects of an early motor skill intervention on motor skills, levels of physical activity, and socialization in young children with autism spectrum disorder: A pilot study. Autism 21(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361316650611

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANCOVA comparing 11 experimental and 9 control children with ASD aged 4–6 years, covarying cognitive t score, adaptive behavior, and calibrated severity. The article reports "statistically significant differences between the experimental and control groups in locomotor" with partial η2 = 0.42.

> "For motor outcomes, results revealed statistically significant differences between the experimental and control groups in locomotor (F(1, 14) = 10.07, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.42)"
