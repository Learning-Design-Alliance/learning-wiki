Popenici, S. A. D., & Kerr, S. (2017). Exploring the impact of artificial intelligence on teaching and learning in higher education. Research and Practice in Technology Enhanced Learning, 12:22. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41039-017-0062-8

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' exploratory literature-based analysis argues that current AI handles automatable tasks while "cannot be yet envisaged as a solution for more complex tasks of higher learning", citing difficulty detecting irony, sarcasm and humor. No empirical test is reported.

> "Artificial intelligence solutions relate to tasks that can be automated, but cannot be yet envisaged as a solution for more complex tasks of higher learning."
