# Standard creativity measures show a statistically significant SES gap among Brazilian 6-9th graders, with high-SES students scoring 0.154 s.d. higher than low-SES students

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Guilherme Lichand 2026

Guilherme Lichand, Lêticia Lopes, Sachin Allums. (2026). Reading ability conflates SES creativity gaps. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00404-y

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Observational study of 523 students in 8 Rio de Janeiro middle schools (May-Jul/2025) completing computer-based AUT and DAT tasks; SES proxied by number of full bathrooms (366 low-SES, 157 high-SES). The article reports a "sizable and statistically significant SES creativity gap" with the 0.154 s.d. difference (p = 0.023).

> "Figure 1 documents a sizable and statistically significant SES creativity gap based on the summary measure of conventional creativity measures (the AUT and the DAT). Concretely, high-SES students score 0.154 s.d. higher than low-SES students (p-value of difference = 0.023)."
