### Aydın Abdullatif 2020

Aydın Abdullatif, Griffin Cora M., Brunckhorst Oliver, Al-Jabir Ahmed, Raison Nicholas, Aya Haleema, McIlhenny Craig, Brewin James, Shabbir Majid, Palou Redorta Joan, Khan Muhammad Shamim, Dasgupta Prokar, Ahmed Kamran. (2020). Non-technical skills for urological surgeons (NoTSUS): development and evaluation of curriculum and assessment scale. World Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-020-03406-6

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Validity and reliability analysis comparing the modified NoTSUS scale against the gold-standard NoTSS scale, with assessments by two NTS-trained experts. The article reports correlation coefficients of "r = 0.93 and r = 0.88" (both p < 0.0001) and a non-significant mean difference of 3.3 points between raters.

> "Correlation of the NoTSS and NoTSUS scores for each participant gave a correlation coefficient of r = 0.93 and r = 0.88, respectively, for expert raters A (n = 43, p < 0.0001) and B (n = 26, p < 0.0001) (Fig. 2). The two expert raters did not significantly differ in their scoring (Fig. 3), with a mean difference of 3.3 points in the overall total, as confirmed by a paired t-test of candidate scores (n = 36, p = 0.10, r = 0.53)."
