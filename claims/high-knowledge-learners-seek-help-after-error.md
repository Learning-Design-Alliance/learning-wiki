## Evidence

### Wood 1999

Wood, H., & Wood, D. (1999). Help seeking, learning and contingent tutoring. Computers & Education, 33, 153-169. https://www.elsevier.com/locate/compedu

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Error-then-help analysis of the QUADRATIC logs (§3.6). The Low group made twice as many errors and self-corrected less often; the article reports "children in the high group being more likely to seek help after error (0.27 against 0.11, P < 0.02)", while probability of success after help showed no significant difference (0.62 against 0.60).
