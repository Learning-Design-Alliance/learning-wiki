## Evidence

### Tsikas Stefanos A. 2024

Tsikas Stefanos A., Dauer Karina. (2024). Examining interviewer bias in medical school admissions: The interplay between applicant and interviewer gender and its effects on interview outcomes. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309293

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Predictive admission probabilities calculated for each level of Abitur grades (Fig 5, based on regression equation (3) in the S1 File). For male candidates in FF committees the probability was significantly higher for all Abitur grade levels below 13 points compared to the other interviewer-interviewee pairs.

> "For five of the six interviewer-interviewee pairs, we find nearly identical results: for below-average grades, the probability of admission is below 10%, but it increases significantly with better academic performance. Only in the case of male candidates in FF committees is a different relationship observed."
