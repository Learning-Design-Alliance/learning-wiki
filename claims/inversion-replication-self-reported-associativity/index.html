Evidence¶

Joanne Eaves 2018¶

Joanne Eaves, Nina Attridge, Camilla Gilmore. (2018). Increasing the use of conceptually-derived strategies in arithmetic: using inversion problems to promote the use of associativity shortcuts. Learning and Instruction. https://figshare.com/s/230ee9491e0b67d9d3f3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-registered between-subjects classroom experiment with 52 undergraduates on a management statistics course. All 20 intervention problems were 'a + b – b' format. Chi-square showed "more shortcut users in the inversion condition" (phi = 0.30); Bayesian BF10 = 2.96 (anecdotal); no other effect size printed.

"A chi-square test found that the frequencies of users to non-users was significantly different between the two conditions, X2(1, N = 51) = 4.46, p = 0.035, phi = 0.30, with more shortcut users in the inversion condition."
