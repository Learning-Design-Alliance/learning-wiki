Observational result from the same pilot's Lawson diagnostic: failure rates rose as reasoning level fell (concrete 52.6%/54.3%, transitional 45.0%/31.5%, formal 18.8%/21.1% across modalities), supporting the correlation stated in the quote.

> "The reasoning skills level is correlated to the probability of passing the course: concrete students were approximately 1.7 less likely to pass the course than transitional students and 2.5 less likely than formal students."
