## Evidence

### Alturki 2017

Nada Alturki. (2017). The effectiveness of using group story-mapping strategy to improve reading comprehension of students with learning disabilities. Educational Research and Reviews. https://www.academicjournals.org/ERR

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test-only comparison of two groups of two sixth-grade ESL students with learning disabilities each; the experimental group completed the comprehension test after the group story-mapping intervention. Experimental scores were 7 and 10 of 10 versus 5 of 10 for both control participants, described as "higher performance for the participants in the experimental group".

> "Jennifer and Kevin gained the same score on the comprehension skills test with only 5 correct answers out of 10. However, the figure indicated higher performance for the participants in the experimental group who were given the comprehension skills test after the intervention. Yovan got 7correct answers while Hilary got 10 correct answers out of 10."
