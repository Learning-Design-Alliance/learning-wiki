## Evidence

### Crawford and Skipp 2014

Claire Crawford, Amy Skipp. (2014). LIT Programme Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581139

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Attrition analysis in the impact evaluation section: four of 19 control schools and three of 22 treatment schools were lost, mostly due to testing difficulties rather than formal withdrawal. Table 2 shows academy sponsor-led status differed significantly between dropouts and non-dropouts (31.1%, significant at 5% level).

> "different school types had very different propensities to remain in the trial, with, for example, almost half (3/7) of sponsor-led academy schools dropping out but fewer than a fifth (3/16) of community schools doing so"
