## Evidence

### Yu Huan 2026

Yu Huan, Li Jing, Jia Yan, Zhang Jiayang, Wang Zhitao, Zou Huiru. (2026). Enhancing online teaching of “subgingival scaling and root planing” through periodontal endoscopy assistance: a study on its application in a teaching-practicing module. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-07706-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of identical pre- and after-class quizzes for all 178 participants in a three-group quasi-experiment. Each group improved significantly (P = 002, P = 0.020, P = 0.015), while between-group comparison showed "no statistically significant difference was detected (P > 0.05)"; no effect size printed.

> "Statistical analysis revealed that all three groups demonstrated significant improvements in their quiz scores following the learning intervention (P = 002, P = 0.020, P = 0.015, respectively). However, when comparing the scores of the pre-class and after-class quizzes among the three groups, no statistically significant difference was detected (P > 0.05)"
