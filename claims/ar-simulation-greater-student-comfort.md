## Evidence

### Felten Renaud 2025

Felten Renaud, Bigaut Kevin, Wirth Thomas, Kremer Laurent, Gauer Lucas, Arnold Cécile, Ollivier Irène, Godet Julien, Scherlinger Marc, Dubois Maxime, Sebbag Eden, De Sèze Jerome, Gottenberg Jacques-Eric. (2025). Advancing medical training with augmented reality and haptic feedback simulator: outcomes of a randomized controlled trial on lumbar puncture. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07536-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student self-assessed comfort (0–10 Likert) during the procedure in the randomized trial: LP-performance comfort significantly higher in the simulation group (p = 0.04); other comfort items (material preparation, installation, sanitization, communication) showed no significant differences. No effect size printed.

> "Lastly, in terms of the LP performance, the overall median comfort score was 6.0 (IQR 5.5-7.0), with a significant difference between the two groups, the simulation group scoring 7.0 (IQR 6.0–7.0) and the control group scoring 6.0 (IQR 5.0–7.0) (p = 0.04)."
