### Pinto-Escalona Tania 2024

Effects of a school-based karate intervention on academic achievement, psychosocial functioning, and physical fitness: A multi-country cluster randomized controlled trial. Journal of Sport and Health Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2021.10.006

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`
