# Purpose interventions shift new users' interest areas toward creative arts and social change and away from computer and data science, without affecting business interest

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The self-transcendent intervention increased new users' interest in creative arts and social change and decreased interest in computer and data science. [→ Chen 2024](#chen-2024)
`q3 i?` Neither intervention significantly changed new users' interest in business. [→ Chen 2024 (2)](#chen-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Chen 2024

Chen, Y., Iyer, P., & Kizilcec, R. F. (2024). Reflection on Purpose Changes Students' Academic Interests: A Scalable Intervention in an Online Course Catalog. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.19035](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.19035)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Topic-model-derived interest probabilities for new users in the randomized field experiment: creative arts rose "from 20.1% to 24.8%", social change from 10.1% to 13.9%, and computer and data science fell from 22.6% to 16.2%; all significant, no standardized effect size printed.

> "For new users, the self-transcendent purpose intervention increased their interest significantly in creative arts from 20.1% to 24.8% (t(1097) = 2.19, p = 0.03) and in social change from 10.1% to 13.9% (t(1097) = 2.23, p = 0.03). In contrast, it led students to be significantly less interested in computer and data science from 22.6% to 16.2% (t(1097) = -2.99, p < 0.01)"

### Chen 2024 (2)

Chen, Y., Iyer, P., & Kizilcec, R. F. (2024). Reflection on Purpose Changes Students' Academic Interests: A Scalable Intervention in an Online Course Catalog. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.19035](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.19035)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The self-transcendent intervention's effect on new users' business-topic probability was null ("percentage point change = -0.8%", p = 0.71); equivalence was not tested.

> "It did not cause a significant change of interest in business (percentage point change = -0.8%, t(1097) = -0.37, p = 0.71)."
