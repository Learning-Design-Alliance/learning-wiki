### Sophia E. Economou and Edwin Barnes 2022

Sophia E. Economou and Edwin Barnes. (2022). Hello Quantum World! A rigorous but accessible first-year university course in quantum information science. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.02868](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.02868)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' report of their semester-long freshman course Hello Quantum World!, based on years of refining an outreach approach; they state students learn "the fundamentals of quantum information and computation" rigorously with only high-school math. No formal evaluation data are reported.

> "with only high-school level math, mostly addition, multiplication, a very small amount of algebra, and a basic understanding of probability, students can learn in a rigorous and, we would argue, much more intuitive way the fundamentals of quantum information and computation."
