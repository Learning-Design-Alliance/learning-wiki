### Burmeister Jay 2014

Burmeister Jay, McSpadden Erin, Rakowski Joseph, Nalichowski Adrian, Yudelev Mark, Snyder Michael. (2014). Correlation of admissions statistics to graduate student success in medical physics. Journal of Applied Clinical Medical Physics 15(1):375-385. https://doi.org/10.1120/jacmp.v15i1.4451

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective correlational analysis of 108 matriculants comparing admissions statistics with four performance metrics. "undergraduate GPA was not correlated with faculty rating (p=0.998)"; the article reports no statistically significant correlation between undergraduate GPA and any success metric investigated.

> "While undergraduate GPA was not correlated with faculty rating (p=0.998), undergraduate degree type was."
