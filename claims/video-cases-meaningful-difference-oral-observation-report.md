## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Geerts 2018

Geerts, W. M., Steenbeek, H. W., & van Geert, P. L. C. (2018). Effect of Video-Cases on the Acquisition of Situated Knowledge of Teachers. International Education Studies, 11(1), 64. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p64

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Oral observation report data: the experimental group's mean number of design patterns increased from pre-test (M = 10.44, SD = 3.0) to post-test (M = 11.33, SD = 2.65), while the control group declined from M = 13.13 (SD = 4.45) to M = 11.38 (SD = 3.34), with a meaningful difference (d = 0.867, p = 0.049).

> "Examining the differences in scores in the experimental group and the control group, it can be seen that there is a meaningful difference (d = 0.867, p = 0.049)."
