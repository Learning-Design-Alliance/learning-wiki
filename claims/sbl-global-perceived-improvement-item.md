## Evidence

### Gomwe Howard 2026

Gomwe Howard, Phiri Lesego, Marange Chioneso Show, Kgongwana Thokozile Harriet, Zikalala-Mabope Lindi, Mothapo Rahab, Dyani Xolelwa. (2026). Simulation-based learning enhances interprofessional competencies in undergraduate health students: a retrospective pre-post study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09125-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single retrospective global item (item 21 of the ICCAS) administered at the end of the program; 450 of 540 participants responded, and the article reports "a combined 94.4% of respondents who perceived at least some improvement". Percentages are descriptive; no effect size applies.

> "A total of 344 participants (76.4% of respondents) indicated their ability was “Much better now,” and 81 (18.0%) indicated “Somewhat better now,” representing a combined 94.4% of respondents who perceived at least some improvement."
