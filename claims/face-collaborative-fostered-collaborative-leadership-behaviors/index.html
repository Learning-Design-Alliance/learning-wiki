Region 15 Comprehensive Center 2022¶

Region 15 Comprehensive Center. (2022). Advancing Family and Community Engagement: A Regional Collaborative. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED651087

q1 · i? · design · r1

Practitioner report by R15CC describing the outcomes of its four-state FACE Collaborative. The authors state the Collaborative's efforts "resulted in a regional network of leaders" who supported each other in building a practice-centered knowledge base; no evaluation data or effect measures are reported.

"The Collaborative's efforts resulted in a regional network of leaders who • Supported each other: Leaders supported each other in building an active practice- centered knowledge base."
