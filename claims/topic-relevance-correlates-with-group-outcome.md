## Evidence

### Hang Su 2023

Hang Su, Borislav Dzodzo, Changlun Li, Danyang Zhao, Hao Geng, Yunxiang Li, Sidharth Jaggi, and Helen Meng. (2023). Learning Analytics from Spoken Discussion Dialogs in Flipped Classroom. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2301.12399](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2301.12399)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Statistical analysis (correlation and ANOVA) of 90 group discussions from 10 groups over 9 weeks, with group-level features min-max normalized weekly. The article reports that topic-relevance features are "positively correlated with the group learning outcome," and that low learning outcome groups tend to have more relaxing off-topic leisure words.

> "the mean of average number of math terms spoken by each in-group student, the mean of average topic relevance score of each in-group student, and the sum of the number of quantity unit words in an entire discussion dialog are positively correlated with the group learning outcome"
