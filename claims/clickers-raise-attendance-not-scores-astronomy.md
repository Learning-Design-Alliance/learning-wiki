## Evidence

### James Schombert 2010

James Schombert. (2010). Introductory Astronomy as a Measure of Grade Inﬂation. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1008.4410](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1008.4410)

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental two-term comparison (Fig. 6) in an introductory cosmology course, one term with and one without clickers, teaching identical material. Attendance increased "from a mean of 40% per lecture to 85%" and evaluations increased 30%, yet scores were identical across terms.

> "Regardless of the signiﬁcant improvement in th e classroom environment, the exam and on-line quiz scores were identical for both terms (in fact, t here was a slight increase in students with failing scores for the clicker class)."
