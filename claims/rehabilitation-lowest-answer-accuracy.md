### Sun Shiying 2026

Sun Shiying, Xu Shenglin, Wang Xue. (2026). Classroom behavioral indicators predict academic performance in large-class clinical instruction: a cohort study using real-time behavioral data. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09340-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Class-level one-way ANOVA with Tukey's post hoc tests comparing answer accuracy across ten teaching classes (n = 1,178 students). The article reports "One-way ANOVA confirmed that the differences in answer accuracy across classes were statistically significant", with Rehabilitation classes lowest at 66% (2020) and 75% (2021).

> "One-way ANOVA confirmed that the differences in answer accuracy across classes were statistically significant (F (9, 1168) = 12.45, p < 0.001)"
