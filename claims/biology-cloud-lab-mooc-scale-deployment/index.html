Hossain 2018¶

Hossain, Z., Bumbacher, E., Brauneis, A., Diaz, M., Saltarelli, A., Blikstein, P., & Riedel-Kruse, I. H. (2018). Design Guidelines and Empirical Case Study for Scaling Authentic Inquiry-based Science Learning via Open Online Courses and Interactive Biology Cloud Labs. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0150-3

q2 · i? · design · r3

Observational analysis of logged user interactions across six course offerings with 325 students who created cloud lab accounts. The log data shows "over 2,300 experiments were executed" with median wait time of 4.8 seconds, demonstrating the system's scalability.

"A total of over 2,300 experiments were executed, with 7 experiments on average per student (students who completed the course ran 12 ± 6 experiments versus students who did not complete the course ran 5±6). During these 6 weeks, students hardly experienced any wait-time (median: 4.8 secs"
