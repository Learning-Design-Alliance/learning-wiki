## Evidence

### Nafis I. Karim 2018

Nafis I. Karim, Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Do evidence-based active-engagement courses reduce the gender gap in introductory physics? https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.14.010103

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intra-group FCI pre/posttest analysis (Table I) of pooled algebra-based EBAE and LB courses. Gender gaps of 11-13% persisted from pretest to posttest in both course types, with male-female differences statistically significant and effect sizes typically in the medium range.

> "Thus, it appears that in algebra-based courses, using evidence-based pedagogies helped both female and male students learn more, but did not result in a reduction of the gender gap."
