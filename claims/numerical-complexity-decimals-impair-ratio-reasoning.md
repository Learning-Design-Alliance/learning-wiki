## Evidence

### Suzanne White Brahmia 2016

Suzanne White Brahmia, Andrew Boudreaux, Stephen E. Kanim. (2016). Obstacles to mathematization in introductory physics. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.01235](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.01235)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired-item comparison in the large-N assessment: items VIIa and VIIb are nearly identical recipe-context tasks differing only in whether quantities are whole numbers or decimals. The authors describe that students are apparently distracted from or less likely to cue appropriate ratio reasoning with decimal and fractional values, quoting "significantly higher than that on VIIb."

> "The correct response rate on item VIIa was significantly higher than that on VIIb; it seems that for some of the engineering students, reasoning arithmetically with decimal numbers presents an obstacle not present when reasoning with whole numbers."
