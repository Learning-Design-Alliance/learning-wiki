Evidence¶

Walsh et al. 2015¶

Mary Walsh, Anastasia Raczek, Erin Sibley, Terrence Lee-St. John, Chen An, Bercem Akbayin, Eric Dearing, Claire Foley. (2015). City Connects: Building an Argument for Effects on Student Achievement with a Quasi-Experimental Design. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562168

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Study 3 used a comparative regression discontinuity design exploiting the kindergarten entrance birth-date cut-off, with outcomes of grades 3 to 5 English/Language Arts and Math MCAS scores and proficiency levels. The article reports Cohen's effect sizes of approximately .20 to .28 and proficiency likelihood increases of approximately 37% to 43%.

"Positive City Connects effects were found in both 3rd and 5th grade math and reading scores. Cohen’s effect sizes for analyses using MCAS raw scores as outcomes ranged from approximately .20 to .28. Increases in the likelihood of achievement at the proficient level or higher ranged from approximately 37% to 43%."
