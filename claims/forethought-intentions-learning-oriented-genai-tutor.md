Rania Abdelghani, Peter Kaiser, and Kou Murayama. (2026). Regulating the AI Tutor: Intentions, Help-Seeking, and Self-Regulated Learning in Adolescent GenAI Use. Accepted at the NextGen Learning Interfaces Workshop, AIED 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08568 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.08568](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.08568)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary AI-reported descriptive results from the forethought phase of the classroom study, in which 98 Grade-9 students selected learning goals before chatting. The article reports these as descriptive percentages with no inferential test, quoting that choices were "strongly learning-oriented".

> "students selected on average4.66of 8 goals, suggesting broad rather than focused intentions. Choices were strongly learning-oriented: step-by- step examples (82.9%), tips and strategies (80.3%), practice problems (71.1%), concept explanations (69.7%), and checking understanding (69.7%). Only11.8% selected “just give me the final solutions”"
