## Evidence

### Pam Hanley 2015

Pam Hanley, Robert Slavin, Louise Elliott. (2015). Thinking, Doing, Talking Science: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN54969918

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pupil attitude questionnaire (adapted from Kind, Jones and Barmby, 2007) analysed by paired comparison of school-level means across 19 matched school pairs; no effect sizes reported. In 16 of 19 pairs, importance of learning science was expressed more strongly in the intervention school.

> "Pupils in classes following the approach reported having more positive attitudes towards science on the vast majority of measures, and were more likely to believe that it was important to learn science."
