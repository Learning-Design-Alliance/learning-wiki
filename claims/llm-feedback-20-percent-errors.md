### Holger Maus 2026

Holger Maus, Fabian Kieser, Stefan Petersen, Peter Wulff, and Paul Tschisgale. (2026). Developing an LLM-Based Feedback System Grounded in Evidence-Centered Design to Support Physics Problem Solving. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1142/s2661339526500125](https://doi.org/10.1142/s2661339526500125)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Independent error examination of all generated feedback by the first author and a graduate student assistant, consolidated by discussion. The raters found the feedback "physically cor- rect in 51 of 64 cases (≈80%)"; the remaining 13 cases contained 14 minor to substantial errors, including calculation errors, missing or incorrect terms, and misclassified correct alternative approaches.

> "Overall, an analysis of correctness by two human raters revealed that the feedback was physically cor- rect in 51 of 64 cases (≈80%)."
