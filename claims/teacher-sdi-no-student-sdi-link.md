## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Teachers' self-determination did not predict individual students' self-determination (ß = .08, p > .05), and no teacher regulation predicted the corresponding student regulation. [→ Taylor 2007](#taylor-2007)

## Evidence

### Taylor 2007

Taylor, I. M., & Ntoumanis, N. (2007). Teacher motivational strategies and student self-determination in physical education. Journal of Educational Psychology, 99, 747-760. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.747

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression with teacher SDI as a level-2 predictor of student SDI (51 teachers, 787 students). The article reports a non-significant effect (ß = .08) and non-significant regulation-level effects, e.g. "intrinsic motivation (ß = .09, p > .05)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "teachers' self-determination did not predict individual students' self-determination (ß = .08, p > .05). Furthermore, non-significant relationships were also found between teacher and student self-reports of intrinsic motivation (ß = .09, p > .05)"
