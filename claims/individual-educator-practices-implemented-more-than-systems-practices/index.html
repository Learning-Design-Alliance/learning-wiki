Descriptive analysis of 2022 survey component scales across 271 schools (figure 1) shows "relationship building (mean = 4.02, standard deviation = 0.60)" and skill instruction highest; lowest were planning and coordination (3.11), support services (3.48), and policy considerations (3.49).

"Average ratings were highest for implementation of components that individual educators could directly implement in their classrooms without a high level of coordination at the school or district level: relationship building (mean = 4.02, standard deviation = 0.60) and trauma-relevant skill instruction (mean = 3.90, standard deviation = 0.66"
