## Evidence

### Zouakia Z 2026

Zouakia Z, Logak E, Szymczak A, Jais J-P, Burgun A, Tsopra R. (2026). AI-Driven Objective Structured Clinical Examination Generation in Digital Health Education: Comparative Analysis of Three GPT-4o Configurations. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/82116

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Expert checklist evaluation of 24 OSCE stations (8 topics x 3 configurations) against 27 binary format criteria from the OSCE Vademecum. The article reports "the simulated-agents GPT showed the highest format compliance" and that personalized GPT compliance was "full only for 3 criteria of the 'vignette' component".

> "Overall, the simulated-agents GPT showed the highest format compliance, followed by the standard GPT, while the personalized GPT exhibited the lowest performance (Table 2)."
