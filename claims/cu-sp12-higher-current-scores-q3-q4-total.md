Preliminary comparison of the CURrENT given in SP12 at CU (N=24) and at a small selective engineering college (N=11), scored with a consistent rubric. The article reports "the average CU SP12 scores were significantly higher for Q3 and Q4" and for the total score; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Responses from both institutions were scored using a consistent rubric, and the average CU SP12 scores were significantly higher for Q3 and Q4 (p ≤ 0.001), and for the total score (p < 0.05)."
