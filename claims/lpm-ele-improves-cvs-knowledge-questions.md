## Evidence

### Warriner DR 2017

Warriner DR, Bayley M, Shi Y, Lawford PV, Narracott A, Fenner J. (2017). Computer model for the cardiovascular system: development of an e-learning tool for teaching of medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1058-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Quantitative assessment of learning used six true/false and extended-matching questions derived from the UoS curriculum, tested before and after optional two-week access to the e-learning environment. The study reports "a statistically significant increase in their performance" and gives the size of the change as 33% and 22% more questions answered correctly, but no standardised effect size, so impact is coded i?. Access was optional and there was no comparison group, so the gain is observed after exposure, not attributed to it.

> "There was a statistically significant increase in their performance after exposure to the environment, with a 33% and 22% increase in the number of questions answered correctly, p < 0.0001 and p < 0.001 respectively"
