# Question randomization is associated with reduced CAMEO harvesting

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2017 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A submission to a randomized question is less likely to be harvested than a submission to a non-random question (p < 0.001). [→ Alexandron 2017](#alexandron-2017)

## Evidence

### Alexandron 2017

Alexandron, G., Ruipérez-Valiente, J. A., Chen, Z., Muñoz-Merino, P. J., Pritchard, D. E. (2017). Copying@Scale: Using Harvesting Accounts for Collecting Correct Answers in a MOOC. Computers & Education, 108, 96-114. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.01.015

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of harvesting rates on randomized (4.06% of submissions) versus non-random questions (6.07%) in the physics MOOC (Table 3); the t-test shows submissions to random questions are less likely to be harvested.

> "A t-test confirms that a submission to a random question is less likely to be harvested, with p-value < 0.001."
