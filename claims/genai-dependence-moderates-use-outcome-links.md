## Evidence

### Shojaei SA 2026

Shojaei SA, Armosh F, Lihe R, Hamacha R and Labib J (2026) Helpful or harmful? generative AI dependence, self-reported critical thinking disposition, and self-perceived employability among business students. Front. Educ. 11:1885990. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1885990

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Latent interaction estimated via a two-stage procedure in PLS-SEM with 412 respondents; f2 values were 0.070 and 0.065 (small). CB-SEM with matched-pair product indicators estimated −0.391 and −0.262, same direction and significance.

> "The interaction term GenAI use × GenAI dependence was negative and significant for self-reported critical thinking disposition (β = −0.239, p < 0.001; H4) and self-perceived employability (β = −0.191, p < 0.001; H5), indicating attenuation of the GenAI use–outcome association at higher dependence levels"

### Shojaei SA 2026 (2)

Shojaei SA, Armosh F, Lihe R, Hamacha R and Labib J (2026) Helpful or harmful? generative AI dependence, self-reported critical thinking disposition, and self-perceived employability among business students. Front. Educ. 11:1885990. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1885990

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple-slopes analysis at −1 SD, mean, and +1 SD of dependence (Figure 3). For critical-thinking disposition the slope decreased from 0.424 at low dependence to −0.054 at high; for employability from 0.446 to 0.064. The article describes "progressive attenuation rather than a uniform reversal".

> "The slopes at low and mean levels of GenAI dependence are statistically different from zero for both outcomes, whereas the slopes at high dependence are not, as their confidence intervals include zero"
