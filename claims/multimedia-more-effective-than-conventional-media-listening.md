## Evidence

### Tagor Pangaribuan 2017

Tagor Pangaribuan, Andromeda Sinaga & Kammer Tuahman Sipayung. (2017). The Effectiveness of Multimedia Application on Students Listening Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 10(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n12p212

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental classroom study with three experimental and three control classes of 20 students each, analyzed with a t-test. The article reports "Tcount = 14.68 > Ttable = 2.02" at α 0.05 with df 38, concluding multimedia was more effective than conventional media. No effect size is printed.
