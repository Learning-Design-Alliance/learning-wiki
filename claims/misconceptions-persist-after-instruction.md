## Evidence

### Hyunju Lee & Stephen E. Schneider 2015

Hyunju Lee & Stephen E. Schneider. (2015). Using Astronomical Photographs to Investigate Misconceptions about Galaxies and Spectra: Question Development for Clicker Use. Physical Review Special Topics Physics Education Research. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.11.020101

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Survey 3 (38 finalized questions, class A n=93, class B n=121, end of semester) showed low performance: means of 12.4 and 9.2 versus a pure guessing score of 8.9, with bell-shaped distributions peaking at 6~10.

> "The mean scores of the students’ performance on the Survey 3 were 12.4 for the Fall 2011 class and 9.2 for the Spring 2012 class. The distribution of their scores showed a similar bell-shape with peaked at the range of 6~10 between the two classes"

### Hyunju Lee & Stephen E. Schneider 2015 (2)

Hyunju Lee & Stephen E. Schneider. (2015). Using Astronomical Photographs to Investigate Misconceptions about Galaxies and Spectra: Question Development for Clicker Use. Physical Review Special Topics Physics Education Research. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.11.020101

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Proportional similarity analysis of Survey 3 answer-choice distributions: average 0.844 across all 38 questions; 30 questions exceeded 0.8 and the lowest was 0.691, despite different instructors.

> "On average for all 38 questions, the proportional similarity between the two classes was 0.844, which indicates that the percentages of students who chose certain misconception are similar in both classes"
