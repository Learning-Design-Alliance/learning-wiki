> "The colonoscopy withdrawal time among cases with no polyps significantly increased in the feedback group (4.9–5.9 minutes, P < 0.01). However, the CIR did not improve significantly after Endo.Adm audit and feedback (94.2%–96.6%, P = 0.077, OR 0.59, 95% CI 0.329–1.059)"
