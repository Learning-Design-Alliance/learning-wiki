Sharon Greenberg & Anthony S. Bryk 2024¶

Sharon Greenberg & Anthony S. Bryk. (2024). Change that matters: Progress made by Chicago Public Schools shows that successful change takes time, focus, and patience. Kappan, V105 N6. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/change-that-matters

q2 · i? · review · r2

The article reports district-level outcomes documented in the authors' 2023 history of Chicago school reform, citing research that in 2017 found the district among the nation's most improved, with "Graduation rates had climbed from 50% to 80%".

"Elementary students were gaining 1.2 years of learning for each year of instruction. English learners were making rapid progress. Graduation rates had climbed from 50% to 80%, even as academic requirements became more rigorous. Students of color and those from low-income families made the biggest gains."
