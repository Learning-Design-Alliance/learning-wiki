## Evidence

### Glodowski 2025

Glodowski, K. R., & Hayashi, Y. (2025). Toward cross‐disciplinary translation of the testing effect: A systematic replication. Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, 58(3), 653–663. https://doi.org/10.1002/jaba.70012

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within-subject classroom study of 15 college students in an Advanced Conditioning and Learning course; quiz conditions were quasirandomly assigned within units and exam scores compared by repeated-measures ANOVA. The omnibus test showed "F(1.19, 15.44) = 14.53, p = .001, ηp2 = .528".

> "the results of the repeated‐measures ANOVA revealed a statistically significant difference across three quiz conditions, F(1.19, 15.44) = 14.53, p = .001, ηp2 = .528"

### Glodowski 2025 (2)

Glodowski, K. R., & Hayashi, Y. (2025). Toward cross‐disciplinary translation of the testing effect: A systematic replication. Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, 58(3), 653–663. https://doi.org/10.1002/jaba.70012

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subset analyses comparing pairs of exams (Exams 1 and 2, 1 and 3, 2 and 3) were conducted to address concerns that exam difficulty varied across quiz conditions; "the main findings were replicated with all subsets".

> "the results of the subset analyses revealed that the main findings were replicated with all subsets (p < .05, ηp2 > .355)"
