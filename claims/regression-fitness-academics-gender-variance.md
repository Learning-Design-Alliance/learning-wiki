## Evidence

### Ryu Jong-Sik 2021

Ryu Jong-Sik, Chung Hae Ryong, Meador Benjamin M., Seo Yongsuk, Kim Kyung-O. (2021). The Associations between Physical Fitness, Complex vs Simple Movement, and Academic Achievement in a Cohort of Fourth Graders. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18052293

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Best-subsets automatic linear modeling in SPSS for the female subsample; predictors in order of importance were soda pop foot times, physical activity, Illinois agility, sit-and-reach, and VO2max. "3 of the 50 subjects were excluded from the model as outliers."

> "In females, the regression model for total grade scores was able to explain 30.5% of the variance. In total, 3 of the 50 subjects were excluded from the model as outliers."

### Ryu Jong-Sik 2021 (2)

Ryu Jong-Sik, Chung Hae Ryong, Meador Benjamin M., Seo Yongsuk, Kim Kyung-O. (2021). The Associations between Physical Fitness, Complex vs Simple Movement, and Academic Achievement in a Cohort of Fourth Graders. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18052293

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Best-subsets automatic linear modeling for the male subsample; soda pop hand scores were the only contributor and "no other activity tests significantly improved the model fit".

> "In males, the regression model for total grade scores was able to explain 4.3% of the variance. In total, 1 of the 60 subjects was excluded from the model as an outlier."
