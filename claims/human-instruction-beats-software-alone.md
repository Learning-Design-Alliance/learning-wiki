## Evidence

### Yang Gao and Barbara E. Hanna 2016

Yang Gao and Barbara E. Hanna. (2016). Exploring optimal pronunciation teaching: Integrating instructional software into intermediate-level EFL classes in China. CALICO Journal. https://doi.org/10.1558/cj.v33i2.26054

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t test comparing score-change means of Group 1 (human-aided instruction) and Group 2 (software-only), 20 students each: "t (38) = 1.99, p = 0.027" with "a medium-sized effect r = 0.31"; Group 1 saw the greater improvement.

> "the mean of score differences for Group 1 was 2.35 (SD = 0.88), whereas for Group 2 it was 1.78 (SD = 0.95). This difference was significant at a level of 0.05 (t (38) = 1.99, p = 0.027) with a medium-sized effect r = 0.31."
