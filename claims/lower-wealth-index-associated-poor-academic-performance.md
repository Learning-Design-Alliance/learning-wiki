## Evidence

### Adam Sagal Mohamed 2024

Adam Sagal Mohamed, Teshoma Melese Sinaga, Ahmed Awale Sh. Dahir, Tamiru Dessalegn. (2024). Household food insecurity and its association with academic performance among primary school adolescents in Hargeisa City, Somaliland. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0303034

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the cross-sectional study (n = 630), with the wealth index generated by principal component analysis of household assets (Cronbach's alpha = 0.864) and categorized into tertiles; middle wealth had "AOR = 0.40, 95%CI = 0.21–0.76" and higher wealth "AOR = 0.51, 95%CI = 0.30–0.88".

> "Our results imply that having a lower household wealth index has a negative impact on academic performance. This indicates that adolescents from lower wealth households had poor academic performance as compared to their peers from high- and middle-wealth households."
