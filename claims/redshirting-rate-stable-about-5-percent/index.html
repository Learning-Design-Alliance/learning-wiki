The fact sheet reports trend data on redshirting prevalence, stating the rate has "staying at about 5% of kindergarteners redshirted on average each year" and remains comparable to 1990s and 2010s rates of 5–7%.

"The rate of redshirting has barely budged in the last two decades, staying at about 5% of kindergarteners redshirted on average each year. These rates are quite comparable to redshirting rates from the 1990s and 2010s (5–7% of kindergarteners)"
