Roche Adam F., Condron Claire M., Eppich Walter J., O'Connor Paul E. (2023). A Mixed Methods Study Identifying the Competencies of Health Care Simulation Technicians. Simulation in Healthcare 18(5). https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000682

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Documentary analysis of online HST job advertisements conducted in January 2021 using specified search terms. Of 124 records, 18 met eligibility criteria; the article reports that "The 67 competencies identified from the descriptions were classified into 9 domains across KSAs." Advertised roles were in the US (n = 17) and Saudi Arabia (n = 1).

> "A total of 124 records were identified from the Internet search, of which 18 job descriptions were found to meet the eligibility criteria in this analysis (Fig. 1)."
