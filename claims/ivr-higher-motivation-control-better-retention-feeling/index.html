Trinidad-Fernández 2026¶

Trinidad-Fernández, M., Bossavit, B., Fuentes-Abolafio, I. J., García-Conejo, C., Mayorga-Vega, D., Díaz-Balboa, E., & Cuesta-Vargas, A. (2026). Immersive virtual reality–based manual therapy training: a mixed-methods trial. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10620-3

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Ad-hoc 5-point Likert satisfaction questions administered to both groups after the session showed the iVR group felt more motivated: "the EG significantly felt more motivated to learn manual therapy (p = .045, d = 0.79)".

Trinidad-Fernández 2026 (2)¶

Trinidad-Fernández, M., Bossavit, B., Fuentes-Abolafio, I. J., García-Conejo, C., Mayorga-Vega, D., Díaz-Balboa, E., & Cuesta-Vargas, A. (2026). Immersive virtual reality–based manual therapy training: a mixed-methods trial. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10620-3

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

On the same ad-hoc satisfaction survey, the control group rated their retention and understanding higher: "the CG showed a significantly higher feeling of having effectively retained and understood manual therapy concepts (p = .008, d = 1.02)".

Related Claims¶

  • Undergraduate physiotherapy students who used iVR alongside traditional teaching scored higher on overall manual therapy performance one week later — related
  • Motion sickness after the iVR experience was negligible for most participants, but a minority felt significant discomfort — related
  • iVR users showed better synchronization of angulation and translation movements in the mobilization technique — related
  • Traditional teaching was rated easier, more efficient, and clearer, while iVR was rated more inventive and leading-edge — related
  • Qualitative analysis found predominantly positive perceptions of iVR, with real-time feedback the most valued element and lack of realism the main concern — related
  • Prior single-exposure IVR studies show mixed cognitive outcomes: affective gains without cognitive benefits (Parong & Mayer) and short-lived retention gains (Christopoulos) — related
  • Value-added studies combining IVR with generative learning strategies (learning-by-teaching, enactment) found particular benefits, suggesting theoretically driven design elements maximize the medium's affordances — related
