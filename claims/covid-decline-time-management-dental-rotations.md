## Evidence

### Shukla A 2025

Shukla A, Amrutham BV. (2025). Impact of coronavirus disease 2019 pandemic on dental student's performance across disciplines during community site rotations: A comparative analysis. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13707

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre/post t-test of time management scores in Results §3.3. The relative ranking of faculty versus student scores reversed across the pandemic: "faculty‐assigned scores were slightly lower than students' self‐reported scores" post-COVID.

> "student mean scores decreased from 4.43 in 2018–2020 to 4.10 in 2020–2022, also showing a statistically significant decline (p < 0.05). Pre‐COVID, faculty mean scores were marginally higher than students' self‐reported scores, whereas post‐COVID, faculty‐assigned scores were slightly lower than students' self‐reported scores."

### Shukla A 2025 (2)

Shukla A, Amrutham BV. (2025). Impact of coronavirus disease 2019 pandemic on dental student's performance across disciplines during community site rotations: A comparative analysis. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13707

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cohort ANOVA in Results §3.3. The class of 2022, taught online in preclinical years, showed a partial recovery that still remained below earlier cohorts, per the article's discussion.

> "student mean scores declined from 4.43 for the class of 2019, to 4.01 for the class of 2021, and then increased to 4.31 for the class of 2022. In contrast, faculty scores declined from 4.45 in 2019 to 3.93 in 2022."
