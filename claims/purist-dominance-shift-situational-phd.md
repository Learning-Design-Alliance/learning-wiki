Alan S. Cornell and Kershree Padayachee. (2021). Revealing shifts from mastery of knowledge to problem solving in assessments of a tertiary physics programme. arXiv. [doi:10.17159/2520-9868/i83a06](https://doi.org/10.17159/2520-9868/i83a06)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative phase 2 analysis examined the topography of knowledge practices in assessments from different years of study on the epistemic plane. It "revealed the dominance of purist insights (OR+, DR+)" except at PhD level, where cross-field projects shift into situational insight (Figure 2).

> "This examination revealed the dominance of purist insights (OR+, DR+) at all levels except PhD, where students may either choose to remain within the ﬁeld of theoretical physics or opt for projects requiring the application of disciplinary knowledge into diﬀerent intellectual ﬁelds"
