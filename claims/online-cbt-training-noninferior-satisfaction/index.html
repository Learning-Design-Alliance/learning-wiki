Evidence¶

Mohd Amiruddin Mohd Kassim 2025¶

Mohd Amiruddin Mohd Kassim, Sidi Muhammad Yusoff Azli Shah, Jane Tze Yn Lim, Tuti Iryani Mohd Daud. (2025). Online-Based and Technology-Assisted Psychiatric Education for Trainees: Scoping Review. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/64773

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review reports a quasiexperimental study in Germany comparing online (inverted-classroom via Moodle plus Zoom discussions) and in-person CBT training with identical content, duration, and trainer: "It was found that evaluations of the online training group were noninferior to those of the in-person group in terms of information content, didactic presentation, assessment of the trainer as a suitable role-model, working atmosphere, own commitment, and practical relevance."
