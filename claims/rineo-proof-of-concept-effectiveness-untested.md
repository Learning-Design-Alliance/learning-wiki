## Evidence

### Coduri Mara 2023

Coduri Mara, Calandrino Andrea, Addiego Mobilio Giulia, Casadio Maura, Ricci Serena. (2023). RiNeo MR: A mixed reality simulator for newborn life support training. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294914

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' own statement of study scope: the 16-subject evaluation covered usability and user experience only. They plan a larger study comparing the simulator to manikin-based training on short- and long-term learning outcomes; gesture speed and precision were not assessed because participants lacked a medical background.

> "Our study is a pilot study on the design and development of a system combining VR with a physical manikin for NLS training. Consequently, we have chosen to concentrate our efforts on usability and user experience"
