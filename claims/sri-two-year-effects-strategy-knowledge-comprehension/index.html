Evidence¶

Droop 2016¶

Droop, M.; van Elsäcker, W.; Voeten, M. J. M.; Verhoeven, L. (2016). Long-Term Effects of Strategic Reading Instruction in the Intermediate Elementary Grades. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2015.1065528

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multilevel model (Model 2) of the two-year cluster-randomized trial shows the strategy-knowledge intervention effect increased by the end of Grade 4; the article reports the mean difference in learning gain "was 2.946 in favor of the experimental group."

"after two years of intervention, the mean difference in learning gain on the reading strategies questionnaire between the experimental group and the control group was 2.946 in favor of the experimental group."

Droop 2016 (2)¶

Droop, M.; van Elsäcker, W.; Voeten, M. J. M.; Verhoeven, L. (2016). Long-Term Effects of Strategic Reading Instruction in the Intermediate Elementary Grades. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2015.1065528

q3 · i? · causal · r2

"At the end of Grade 4, there were also positive intervention effects on reading comprehension."
