## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In Experiment 2, the deictic condition reported higher accuracy of persona-relevant information than the control (ηp2 = .07), and a small non-significant tendency toward higher evaluation than the beat condition. [→ Maik Beege 2020](#maik-beege-2020)

## Evidence

### Maik Beege 2020

Maik Beege, Manuel Ninaus, Sascha Schneider, Steve Nebel, Julia Schlemmel, Jasmin Weidenmüller, Korbinian Moeller, Günter Daniel Rey. (2020). Investigating the effects of beat gestures and deictic gestures of a lecturer in educational videos. Psychology of learning with digital media, Chemnitz University of Technology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103955

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 PSI univariate analysis (N = 121), printed effect size ηp2 = .07 for accuracy of persona-relevant information. "students in the deictic condition also reported higher accuracy than students in the control condition (M d if f = 0.91; p = .01)"; evaluation showed only a descriptive tendency (Mdiff = 0.53; p = .08) and logical understanding was not significant.

> "There also was a significant effect with a medium effect size for accuracy of persona-relevant information; F(2, 118) = 4.65, p = .01, ηp2 = .07. Sidak-corrected post-hoc analyses indicated that students in the deictic condition also reported higher accuracy than students in the control condition (M d if f = 0.91; p = .01)."
