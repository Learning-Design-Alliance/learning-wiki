### Heldring Sara 2025

Heldring Sara, Jirwe Maria, Wihlborg Jonas, Lindström Veronica. (2025). Acceptability and applicability of using virtual reality for training mass casualty incidents- a mixed method study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07319-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mixed-methods evaluation with 95 Swedish healthcare professionals and nursing students using the GoSaveThem VR MCI triage scenario, combining Likert ratings, open-ended questionnaire answers, interviews, and computer-recorded triage data. The Results state "the acceptability and applicability of using VR for training MCIs were high overall in all examined dimensions for most users, with some exceptions."
