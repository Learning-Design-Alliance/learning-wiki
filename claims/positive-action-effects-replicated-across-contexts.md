## Evidence

### Brian R. Flay 2014

Brian R. Flay, D.Phil. (2014). Replication of effects of the Positive Action program in randomized trials in Hawai'i and Chicago schools. SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/NCT01025674

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article's own cross-site comparison of the two matched-pair cluster-randomized trials. It also notes program implementation was probably higher in Hawai'i than in Chicago, and that the replicated pattern suggests findings are likely generalizable across a wide range of settings.

> "Overall, major findings from Hawai'i were replicated in Chicago, although often with smaller effect sizes. We attribute this to the more challenging circumstances of disadvantaged students in inner-city schools."
