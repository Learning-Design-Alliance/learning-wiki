## Evidence

### Rotthoff Thomas 2014

Rotthoff Thomas, Ostapczuk Martin S., Kröncke Klaus D., Zimmerhofer Alexander, Decking Ulrich, Schneider Matthias, Ritz-Timme Stefanie. (2014). Criterion validity of a competency-based assessment center in medical education – a 4-year follow-up study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v19.25254

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analyses comparing the Year 1 self-report questionnaire (Cronbach's α=0.85) with all follow-up measures. The highest associations were moderate but insignificant: r=0.34 with the communication OSCE and r=0.41 with peer feedback self-assessment, another self-report measure.

> "Note that questionnaire scores did not correlate significantly with any of the criterion-related measures, although it showed some moderate, yet insignificant associations with the communication OSCE average score [r=0.34, p=0.06 (one-tailed)] and peer feedback self-assessment [r=0.41, p=0.09 (one-tailed)]."
