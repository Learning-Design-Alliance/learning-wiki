Soland 2019¶

(The Collaborative for Student Growth at NWEA Working Paper). https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/reconciling-long-term-education-policy-goals-with-short-term-school-accountability-models-paper/

q1 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' stated limitations: the sample is from one state using MAP Growth, a low-stakes assessment, and lacks student-level SES, special education, and English learner status, though school-level NCES covariates were included. No effect sizes are attached, so impact is null.

"we do not have student-level information on socioeconomic status like free- and reduced-price lunch status. Therefore, we cannot be sure if adding those covariates would change our findings."
