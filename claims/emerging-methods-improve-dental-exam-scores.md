## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Pang Xuefei 2024

Pang Xuefei, Li Ling, Liu Xu, Wang Yan, Yang Bo. (2024). Application of Emerging Teaching Models in Dental Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2024.05.016

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 29 randomized controlled trials including 3502 dental students, analyzed with a random effects model in Stata 14.0 using standard mean deviation. The pooled estimate showed "significantly improve students’ exam scores" with SMD = 0.48 and substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 66.4%).

> "A meta-analysis incorporating 29 studies showed that compared to traditional teaching method LBL, emerging teaching methods can significantly improve students’ exam scores (SMD = 0.48, 95% CI = 0.34-0.62, I2 = 66.4%, P < .001)"
