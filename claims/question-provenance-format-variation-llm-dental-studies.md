Descriptive characterization of the 39 included studies' question sources and formats. MCQ and open-ended formats were each used in 17/39 studies (43.6%), and 38/39 (97.4%) evaluated text-only questions. These variables were reported descriptively, not as prespecified meta-regression moderators.

> "Question provenance most commonly consisted of author-constructed question sets (n = 21/39, 53.8%), followed by licensing or official examination materials (n = 13/39, 33.3%)."
