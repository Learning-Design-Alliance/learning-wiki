# Resident experience level predicted training assessment scores but not final assessment scores after simulation training

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` At the training assessment, year of residency was significantly related to key action score (β = 0.47, P < 0.05) and GRS (β = 0.36, P < 0.001), but no such relationship was observed at the final assessment (GRS: P = 0.9; key action score: P = 0.8). [→ Spadaro S 2017](#spadaro-s-2017)

## Evidence

### Spadaro S 2017

Spadaro S, Karbing DS, Fogagnolo A, Ragazzi R, Mojoli F, Astolfi L, Gioia A, Marangoni E, Rees SE, Volta CA. (2017). Simulation Training for Residents Focused on Mechanical Ventilation: A Randomized Trial Using Mannequin-Based Versus Computer-Based Simulation. Simulation in Healthcare 12(6):349-355. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000249

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Univariate linear regression across both groups at the training assessment relating residency year (CA1–CA4) to scores. The article reports "a strong effect size with β value of 0.47" for key action score and "a moderate effect size with β value of 0.36" for global score; the final-assessment regressions were not significant (P = 0.9 and P = 0.8).

> "The univariate linear regression across both groups showed statistically significant relationships between the score and the year of training (P < 0.001), a strong effect size with β value of 0.47 (P < 0.05) for key action score, and a moderate effect size with β value of 0.36 for global score."
