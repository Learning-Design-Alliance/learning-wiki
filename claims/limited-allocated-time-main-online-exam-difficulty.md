> "Figure 1 shows that the major reported difficulty concerning the online exams was the limited allocated time for each exam (n = 308, 22.47%). On the other hand, few participants (n = 53, 3.87%) reported that the source of difficulty was the noisy or uncomfortable setting during the online exam."
