### Couto Thomaz Bittencourt I 2015

High-fidelity simulation versus case-based discussion for teaching medical students in Brazil about pediatric emergencies. Clinics. [doi:10.6061/clinics/2015(06)02](https://doi.org/10.6061/clinics/2015(06)02)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of demographic and educational characteristics against test results in the non-randomized cohort; the prior adult ED rotation was the only characteristic influencing results, and the difference was again present at retention (46.5% vs 39.5%, p=0.04).

> "This subgroup had higher mean pre-test scores for SVT (38.7%) than group 1 (31.6%; p=0.06) and the rest of group 2 (30.1%; p=0.02) did."
