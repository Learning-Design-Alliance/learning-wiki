### Chrispeels H. E. 2019

Chrispeels H. E., Chapman J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2019). Peer Teaching Increases Knowledge and Changes Perceptions about Genetically Modified Crops in Non–Science Major Undergraduates. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-08-0169

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of coded knowledge level against perception category on pre-SLP responses (n = 37), Fisher's exact test with Bonferroni-corrected pairwise analysis at p = 0.0014.

> "Pairwise post hoc analysis with Bonferroni correction showed a significant correlation for students who perceived GMOs as harmful or suspect and those whose responses contained incorrect knowledge (p = 0.0014)."
