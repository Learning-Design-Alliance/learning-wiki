`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same 12-surgeon multi-institution annotation study, 48 suprapancreatic images per group; the trainer-versus-senior-trainee contrast did not reach significance ("p = 0.054"). No effect size printed for this comparison.

> "In the suprapancreatic area, corresponding rates were 18/48 images (38, 95 CI 25.2–51.6%) in the JT group, 8/48 images (17, 95 CI 8.7–29.6%) in the ST group, and 2/48 images (4, 95 CI 1.2–14.0%) in the T group, with the T group demonstrating significantly lower rates than the JT group (p < 0.001) but not ST group (p = 0.054)."
