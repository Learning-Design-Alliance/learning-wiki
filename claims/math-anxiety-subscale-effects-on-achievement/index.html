Evidence¶

Wu SS 2012¶

Wu SS, Barth M, Amin H, Malcarne V and Menon V (2012) Math anxiety in second and third graders and its relation to mathematics achievement. Front. Psychology 3:162. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00162

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Subscale correlational analyses in the 162-child sample controlling for FSIQ, shown in Figure 3. The article reports the printed ρ and p values for both subscales against the Math Composite.

"the Numerical Processing Anxiety subscale of SEMA was significantly and negatively correlated with WIAT-II Math Composite scores ( ρ =− 0.21, p < 0.05) as was the Situational and Performance Anxiety subscale ( ρ =− 0.19, p < 0.05)."

Wu SS 2012 (2)¶

Wu SS, Barth M, Amin H, Malcarne V and Menon V (2012) Math anxiety in second and third graders and its relation to mathematics achievement. Front. Psychology 3:162. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00162

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Follow-up subscale analyses against the two WIAT-II math subtests in the same sample. The article reports the printed ρ and p values for each subscale against each subtest.

"Numerical Processing Anxiety and Situational and Performance Anxiety were correlated with Mathematical Reasoning ( ρ =− 0.27, p < 0.01; ρ =− 0.28, p < 0.01, respectively) but not Numerical Operations ( ρ =− 0.13, p = 0.15; ρ =− 0.09, p = 0.32, respectively)."
