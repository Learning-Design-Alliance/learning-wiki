## Evidence

### Suya Liu 2026

Suya Liu, Ziyan Lin, Oi Lam Ng, Yijian Yang, Yun Dai. (2026). AI literacy interventions in education: A meta-analysis of effects and moderators. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2026.100813

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Three-level random-effects meta-analysis of 59 (quasi-)experimental studies with 172 effect sizes from 7211 participants, using Hedges' g and REML estimation. The pooled estimate was "g = 0.837 (SE = 0.073, 95% CI [0.692, 0.982], t = 11.393, p < .001)".

> "the three-level random-effects model yielded a large and statistically significant average effect of educational interventions on AI literacy, g = 0.837 (SE = 0.073, 95% CI [0.692, 0.982], t = 11.393, p < .001)"
