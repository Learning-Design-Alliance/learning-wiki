# Students' individual interest in social sciences correlated with their situational interest during the OSL visit (catch r = .27; hold r = .26)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Individual interest in the subject showed small positive correlations with both catch and hold situational interest, justifying its use as covariate. [→ Valentina Nachtigall 2021](#valentina-nachtigall-2021)

## Evidence

### Valentina Nachtigall 2021

Valentina Nachtigall, Nikol Rummel. (2021). Investigating students' perceived authenticity of learning activities in an out-of-school lab for social sciences: a replication study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09556-3

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Secondary correlational analysis in the MANCOVA section of the quasi-experimental OSL study. Individual interest (adapted Sparfeldt et al. 2004 scale, α = .87) correlated with situational interest catch (r = .27, p = .001) and hold (r = .26, p = .002), small effects.

> "We included students' interest in the social sciences as a covariate due to correlations with situational interest (catch: r = .27, p = .001; hold: r = .26, p = .002)."
