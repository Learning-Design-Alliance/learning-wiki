# Task instruction significantly affected verbalized local-level selection reasons, with the linguistic task yielding more matching verbalizations than the other two tasks

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A chi-square test showed a significant relation between selection task and verbalization at the local task level (X2(df=2)=9.17, p<.025), with the linguistic task producing relatively more matching verbalizations than the educational and personal tasks. [→ Gonny L. M. Schellings & Hein Broekkamp 2011](#gonny-l-m-schellings-hein-broekkamp-2011)

## Evidence

### Gonny L. M. Schellings & Hein Broekkamp 2011

Gonny L. M. Schellings & Hein Broekkamp. (2011). Signaling task awareness in think-aloud protocols from students selecting relevant information from text. Metacognition and Learning 6:65–82. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-010-9067-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square analysis of 72 definite selection reasons coded across the 36 think-aloud protocols (12 per task). The test of task by match/mismatch of verbalization was significant at the local task level; no effect size is printed.

> "There was a significant relation between task and verbalization at the local task level ( X2(df=2)=9.17 p<.025), implying that the linguistic task resulted in relatively more matching verbalizations, compared to the other two tasks."
