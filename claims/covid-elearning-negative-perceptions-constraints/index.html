Systematic review synthesis of negative-perception items across the included cross-sectional studies. The review reports negative perceptions "focused on access to the internet", health problems from e-learning use, and "difficulty in developing clinical skills online", with the printed percentage ranges per item.

"Negative perceptions in the included studies focused on access to the internet in 2 studies (21.5% to 35.9%) [15,16], health problems caused by the use of e-learning in 2 studies (62.5% to 67%) [18,24], and difficulty in developing clinical skills online in 2 studies (82.2% to 84.2%) [15,17]."
