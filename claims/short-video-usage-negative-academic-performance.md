## Evidence

### Gong Qiong 2024

Gong Qiong, Tao Ting. (2024). The relationship between short video usage and academic achievement among elementary school students: The mediating effect of attention and the moderating effect of parental short video usage. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309899

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of questionnaire and exam-score data from 1,052 elementary students in Shenzhen, China, found short video usage "is negatively related to academic performance (standard path coefficient = -0.205, critical ratio = -4.096, p < 0.001)". Correlations also showed r = -.271 between usage and performance (p < .01).

> "short video usage is negatively related to academic performance (standard path coefficient = -0.205, critical ratio = -4.096, p < 0.001), indicating that frequent use of short videos may have detrimental effects on academic performance."
