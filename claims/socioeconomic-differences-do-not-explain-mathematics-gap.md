## Evidence

### Al-Bakri Moug 2023

Al-Bakri Moug, Skovgaard Anne Mette, Bach-Holm Daniella, Larsen Dorte Ancher, Siersma Volkert, Kessel Line. (2023). School performances in children with cataract: results from a population-based cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-072984

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted multivariable linear regression including the child's geographical birth origin, somatic comorbidity, mental disorders, parental socioeconomic status and parental mental disorders. The cataract cohort had lower parental education (p value=0.0025) and income (p value=0.021), yet the adjusted mathematics difference persisted.

> "The socioeconomic differences between the cataract and control groups did, however, not explain the difference in tests scores in mathematics, and they did not influence the results on reading either (adjusted analyses, see table 2)."
