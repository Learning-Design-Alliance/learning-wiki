### Yimeng Sun 2026

Yimeng Sun, Haiyang Xin, Qiannan Niu, Shuang Li, Lingyun Huang, Gaowei Chen. (2026). Computational Thinking Development in AI Agent Creation: A Mixed-Methods Study. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14330

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression on N=80 students using Days 4-5 platform behavioral logs; iterative testing engagement was the sole behavioral predictor of self-efficacy gains, a statistically significant regression coefficient with no standardized effect size printed, bootstrap validation (BCa 95% CI [1.10, 116.78]) and cross-validation (R² = .38) reported as confirming model stability.
