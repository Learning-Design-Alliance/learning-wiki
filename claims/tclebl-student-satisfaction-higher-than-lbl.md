# Medical postgraduate students rated the TCLEBL model significantly higher than LBL on eight satisfaction dimensions including interactivity, knowledge mastery, and perceived research-ability gains

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students in the TCLEBL group rated classroom interactivity, learning efficiency, knowledge mastery, self-directed learning ability, teamwork skills, preference for the model, clinical reasoning, and research abilities significantly higher than LBL students (p < 0.001 for all). [→ Huang Tianlong 2024](#huang-tianlong-2024)

## Evidence

### Huang Tianlong 2024

Huang Tianlong, Zhou Shun, Wei Qiaoyan, Ding Chun. (2024). Team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning in medical postgraduates training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05650-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Student questionnaire (10-point VAS) comparing the TCLEBL group (n = 21) with the LBL group (n = 20) after course completion, analyzed by independent t-test. All eight items favored TCLEBL at p < 0.001; e.g., "classroom interactivity (7.714 ± 0.902 vs. 5.75 ± 0.91, p < 0.001)". No effect sizes printed.

> "students in the TCLEBL group rated significantly higher than the LBL group in terms of classroom interactivity (7.714 ± 0.902 vs. 5.75 ± 0.91, p < 0.001), learning efficiency (6.857 ± 1.062 vs. 4.1 ± 0.912, p < 0.001)"
