Regression analysis (Table 6) with Behavioral Intention as dependent variable and the five constructivist outcomes as predictors; all predictor p-values exceeded 0.05. "The value of R-squared (0.007)" and the non-significant F-test indicate a very weak model; VIFs near 1 showed no multicollinearity.

> "The value of R-squared (0.007) indicates that only 0.7% variation in behavioral intention is accounted for by these predictors, reflecting a very weak model. Moreover, the F -value (0.531) and p-value (0.753) also validate that the model as a whole is not statistically significant."
