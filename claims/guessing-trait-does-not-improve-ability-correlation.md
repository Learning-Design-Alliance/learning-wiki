## Evidence

### Emre Gönülateş and Gerd Kortemeyer 2015

Emre Gönülateş and Gerd Kortemeyer. (2015). A New Item Response Theory Model for Open-Ended Online Homework with Multiple Allowed Attempts. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.010118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.010118)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reduced 2P3TL model (χj ≡ 0) fitted to first-attempt homework data from the 256-student physics course and correlated with exam-based 2PL ability. The guessing-only ability fit shows R² = 0.40883, and the article reports that "The correlation between homework and exam ability, however, did not improve."

> "The correlation between homework and exam ability, however, did not improve: the model has more variability, but explains slightly less of it."
