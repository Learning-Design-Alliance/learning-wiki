## Evidence

### Chatziisaak D 2024

Chatziisaak D, Sparn M, Krstic D, Bauci G, Warschkow R, Brunner W, Schmied B, Hahnloser D, Bischofberger S. (2024). Be prepared! Impact of structured video-assisted coaching on performance in a simulated bleeding exercise during laparoscopic surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11173-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Reliability analysis of the 54 recorded exercise videos assessed by 7 experienced minimally invasive surgeons using the blinded GOALS score; the printed "ICC = 0.72, 95%CI: 0.63–0.81" supports moderate-to-good reliability for the sum score.

> "The Intraclass Correlation Coefficient (ICC) indicate a moderate to good inter-rater reliability for the GOALS sum score (ICC = 0.72, 95%CI: 0.63–0.81, P < 0.001). In the sub-scores for the skills assessed by the GOALS sum score, a moderate reliability was observed."
