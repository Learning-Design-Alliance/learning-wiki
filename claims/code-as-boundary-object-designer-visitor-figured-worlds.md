### Pratim Sengupta & Marie-Claire Shanahan 2016

Pratim Sengupta & Marie-Claire Shanahan. (2016). STEM as Public Computation and Boundary Play. STEM2016, University of Calgary. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1610.06658](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1610.06658)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic, phenomenographic analysis of visitor interactions in the single-case study. The authors interpret the open source code as an ill-structured boundary object that was localized differently by designers, whose figured world was realistic bird flocking, and visitors, whose figured worlds were 'Boid prisons' and jail cells.

> "In our analysis, fragments of the underlying code therefore serve as boundary objects between the figured worlds of the exhibit designers and the visitors. The figured worlds of the visitors, however, are malleable, and so are the boundary objects."
