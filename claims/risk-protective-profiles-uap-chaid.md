# CHAID analysis identifies a risk profile (low/medium intrinsic motivation to experience stimulation plus low/medium self-efficacy) and a protective profile (high self-efficacy) for UAP

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The risk profile for low UAP combines low/medium IMS and low/medium self-efficacy; the protective profile for high UAP is high self-efficacy. [→ Deroncele-Acosta Angel 2025](#deroncele-acosta-angel-2025)

## Evidence

### Deroncele-Acosta Angel 2025

Deroncele-Acosta Angel, Norabuena-Figueroa Roger Pedro, Norabuena-Figueroa Emerson Damian. (2025). Women’s mental health in the doctoral context: Protective function of the psychological capital and academic motivation. Women’s Health. https://doi.org/10.1177/17455057251315318

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Decision-tree analysis using the CHAID algorithm on the 108-woman survey data to identify profiles associated with UAP; the article identifies the protective profile by "a high level of self-efficacy of psychological capital."

> "the protection profile that contributes to obtaining a high level of UAP, which is identified by women who present a high level of self-efficacy of psychological capital."
