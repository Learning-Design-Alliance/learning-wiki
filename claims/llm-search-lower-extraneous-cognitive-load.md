# Students using ChatGPT 3.5 experience lower extraneous cognitive load than students using Google during a web research task

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium
> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students in the LLM condition reported significantly lower extraneous cognitive load than students in the web search condition, adjusted for prior knowledge (η² = 0.09). [→ Matthias Stadler 2024](#matthias-stadler-2024)

## Evidence

### Matthias Stadler 2024

Matthias Stadler, Maria Bannert, Michael Sailer. (2024). Cognitive ease at a cost: LLMs reduce mental effort but compromise depth in student scientific inquiry. Computers in Human Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2024.108386

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA comparing group means controlling for prior knowledge in a randomized experiment with 91 university students (web search n=47, LLM n=44). The LLM group's mean ECL was 3.00 (SD 1.67) versus 3.96 (SD 1.55) for web search, with "8.26 0.005 0.09", i.e., η² = 0.09, a small effect.

> "H1.1-ECL 3.96(1.55) 3.00(1.67) 8.26 0.005 0.09"
