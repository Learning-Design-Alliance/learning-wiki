Evidence¶

Valiente Carlos 2011¶

Valiente Carlos, Eisenberg Nancy, Haugen Rg, Spinrad Tracy L., Hofer Claire, Liew Jeffrey, Kupfer Anne. (2011). Children's Effortful Control and Academic Achievement: Mediation Through Social Functioning. Early Education and Development, 22(3), 411-433. https://doi.org/10.1080/10409289.2010.505259

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Zero-order correlations (Table 1) in the 6-year longitudinal sample supported the mediation requirements: reported and observed EC were positively related to social competence, negatively related to externalizing problems, and positively related to achievement measures. No standardized effect sizes are printed for these prospective paths.

"In addition, social competence (positively) and externalizing problems (negatively) were prospectively related to academic achievement."
