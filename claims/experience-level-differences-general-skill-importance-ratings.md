## Evidence

### Cai Bohong 2025

Cai Bohong, Wang Zihan, Lyu Xi, Qiao Yiran, Liu Yuhan, Wu Danting, Li Sihang, Yao Qi, Chen Baixiang, Fu Fujian, Jiang Wei. (2025). Surgeon perceptions of essential skills and simulation for percutaneous transforaminal endoscopic discectomy training. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34397-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bonferroni-corrected Welch ANOVA of five general-skill importance ratings across four experience levels (n = 154). Only imaging anatomy (F = 2.858, P = 0.039) and spatial perception (F = 3.339, P = 0.021) showed significant group differences.

> "Post-hoc tests indicated that both junior specialists and consultants rated knowledge of imaging anatomy significantly higher than postgraduates/residents/fellows. For spatial perception, senior consultants, junior specialists, and consultants all assigned significantly higher ratings than the postgraduate/resident/fellow group."
