## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Çetinkaya Levent 2024

Çetinkaya Levent, Keser İlke, Yildirim Serkan, Keser Hafize. (2024). The effect of case-based mobile virtual patient application on students’ academic achievement in clinical reasoning skills. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2322223

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive effect-size comparison from the same quasi-experimental study (n = 26 per group): control means rose from 5.58 to 6.58, experimental from 5.54 to 9.46 on the 17-item pretest form. The article states the effect "was determined to be large (d = 1.215) for those in the experimental group".

> "it was seen that while the effect size of the students in the control group was small (d = .464), it was determined to be large (d = 1.215) for those in the experimental group"
