Demir 2021¶

Demir, Bora. (2021). Working Memory Model and Language Learning. Shanlax International Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.34293/education.v9iS2-Sep.4366

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Narrative review statement attributed to Baddeley (1996) and Morris & Gruneberg (1994). The CE is described as the most complex and powerful WM component, a flexible but limited-capacity workspace; no effect size is printed.

"This system is responsible for the specific functions of learning a language such as a strategy switching, selective attention, retrieval from long-term memory and dual-task coordination, which represent higher-level cognition tasks."
