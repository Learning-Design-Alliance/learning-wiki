# Shared mental model development mediates the effect of team learning behaviors on actual team performance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The effect of team learning behaviors on actual team performance is mediated by shared mental model development; mediation conditions were not met for perceived effectiveness. [→ Van den Bossche 2011](#van-den-bossche-2011)

## Evidence

### Van den Bossche 2011

Van den Bossche, P., Gijselaers, W., Segers, M., Woltjer, G., & Kirschner, P. (2011). Team learning: building shared mental models. Instructional Science, 39, 283-301. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-010-9128-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Baron-and-Kenny mediation analyses on team-level data: adding team learning behaviors after the shared mental model failed to add significant variance for equity and goodwill (e.g., ΔR2 = 0.063, p = 0.57); Sobel tests supported indirect effects on goodwill (p = 0.05) and equity (p < 0.10).

> "To summarize, these analyses give substantial support for the idea that the effect of team learning behaviors on the actual team performance is mediated by the development of a shared mental model."
