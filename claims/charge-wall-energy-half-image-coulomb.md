# For a point charge near a perfectly conducting wall, the electrostatic energy is half the Coulombic energy between the real charge and its image charge.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The energy of the point charge-conducting wall system equals half the energy of the corresponding real-image charge pair, consistent with the force obtained from the image method. [→ M.M. Taddei 2009](#mm-taddei-2009)

## Evidence

### M.M. Taddei 2009

M.M. Taddei, T.N.C. Mendes, C. Farina. (2009). Subtleties on energy calculations in the image method. https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4497 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0903.4497](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0903.4497)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivation in Section 2 for a point charge q at (0,0,z) near a conducting wall filling z<0. Computing U as the external work via the image-method force shows the system's energy is "half the electrostatic energy" of the q, −q pair; the naive expression is shown inconsistent by taking its gradient.

> "In other words, the electrostatic energy of the system formed by point charge q and the conducting region is half the electrostatic energy of a point charge q located at (0, 0, z) and a point charge −q located at (0 , 0, −z)."
