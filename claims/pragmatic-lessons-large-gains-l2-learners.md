## Evidence

### Bachelor 2019

Bachelor, J. W., & Barros García, M. J. (2019). A comparison of heritage learners and L2 learners of Spanish: A study on compliment sequences in the classroom. Journal of Foreign Language Education and Technology, 4(1). http://jflet.com/jflet/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t test on MCDCT scores of the L2 experimental group (15 students at site one) after three pragmatic lessons. The article reports a statistically significant difference between pre and post scores; no standardized effect size is printed for this paired test.

> "a statistically significant difference was found in the L2 experimental group between the pre test scores (M=33.33, SD=20. 59) and the post test scores (M=63.33, SD=17.18); t(14)=6.40, p=0.0001."

### Bachelor 2019 (2)

Bachelor, J. W., & Barros García, M. J. (2019). A comparison of heritage learners and L2 learners of Spanish: A study on compliment sequences in the classroom. Journal of Foreign Language Education and Technology, 4(1). http://jflet.com/jflet/

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Unpaired t test comparing L2 control (M=34.29, SD=19.02) and L2 experimental (M=63.33, SD=17.18) post-test scores was significant, t(20)=3.57, p=0.0019; the printed Cohen's d of 1.6 is called a "very large effect".

> "As calculated, Cohen’s d=(63.33-34.29) ⁄ 18.123366=1.6. As such, an effect size of 1.6 is considered to be a “very large effect.”"
