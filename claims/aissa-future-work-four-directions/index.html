Authors' forward-looking statement in the conclusion: planned work includes comparing LLM and human teacher assessments, studying pedagogical impact systematically, comparing different LLMs and extending extracted features, and examining selective uptake of recommendations. These are stated intentions, not tested results.

"First, we will compare AISSA’s LLM-based evaluations with assessments provided by human teachers, examining both the numerical scores assigned at rubric-item and overall level and the qualitative feedback associated with each presentation."
