Evidence¶

Shariman and Talib 2017¶

Tenku Putri Norishah Tenku Shariman and Othman Talib. (2017). OCRA, A Mobile Learning Prototype for Understanding Chemistry Concepts. 14th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2017). https://www.worldcat.org/isbn/978-989-8533-68-5

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Questionnaire survey of 100 students (54 from an earlier cohort and 46 current) using a 1-to-4 Likert scale across 4 sections; item means ranged from 4.21 to 4.58 with an overall mean of 4.44. The article reports descriptive means only, with no inferential test.

"The total average mean for this part is 4.44; the results of t he survey confirm that majority of students have a positive perception on the suitability of OCRA to positively impact their experiences, motivation and engagement in learning organic chemistry concepts."
