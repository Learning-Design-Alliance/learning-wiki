## Evidence

### Lee Gun Tak 2022

Lee Gun Tak, Park Jong Eun, Woo Sook-young, Shin Tae Gun, Jeong Daun, Kim Taerim, Lee Se Uk, Yoon Hee, Hwang Sung Yeon. (2022). Defining the learning curve for endotracheal intubation in the emergency department. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-19337-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same multivariable GLMM on the registry dataset. Cardiac arrest (adjusted odds ratio = 0.677) and difficult airway (adjusted odds ratio = 0.324) were each significantly associated with decreased FAS, the latter a large effect. Difficult-airway assessment was acknowledged as "highly susceptible to subjectivity and interobserver variability".

> "Conversely, ETI in cardiac arrest (vs. non-cardiac arrest; aOR 0.677; 95% CI 0.538–0.852; p < 0.001) and the presence of a difficult airway (vs. non-difficult airway; aOR 0.324; 95% CI 0.254–0.412; p < 0.001) were significantly associated with decreased FAS."
