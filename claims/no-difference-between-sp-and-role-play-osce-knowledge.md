## Evidence

### Park Kye-Yeung 2019

Park Kye-Yeung, Park Hoon-Ki, Hwang Hwan-Sik. (2019). Group randomized trial of teaching tobacco-cessation counseling to senior medical students: a peer role-play module versus a standardized patient module. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1668-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group Student's t-tests in the group-randomized trial (SP group N = 57, RP group N = 56) found "post-intervention OSCE scores and smoking-knowledge scores were not statistically different between two groups"; the printed total-score p-values were 0.64 (OSCE) and 0.84 (knowledge).

> "In the post-intervention comparisons, post-intervention OSCE scores and smoking-knowledge scores were not statistically different between two groups."
