## Evidence

### Tacoma 2020

Tacoma, S., Heeren, B., Jeuring, J., & Drijvers, P. (2020). Intelligent Feedback on Hypothesis Testing. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00218-y

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled experiment embedded in a first-year psychology Methods and Statistics course, comparing an experimental group (N=163) receiving ITS feedback with a control group (N=151) receiving verification feedback. The experimental group made significantly fewer logic errors, with "Cohen’s d = 0.36".

> "students in the experimental group made 1.12 (SD = 0.79) different errors per solution, while students in the control group made 1.42 (SD = 0.86) different errors, which was significantly more (t(312) = 3.22, p =. 0 0 1 , Cohen’s d = 0.36)"
