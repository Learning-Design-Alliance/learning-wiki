Evidence¶

Gandolfi 2026¶

Gandolfi, E., Soyturk, I., Kosko, K. W., Ferdig, R. E., & Clements, R. (2026). Measuring presence in virtual world teacher education: validation of the eXtended Reality Presence Scale. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10619-w

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Rasch (polytomous model, Winsteps) analysis of XRPS responses from 91 pre-service teachers after viewing a Minecraft.edu 360 video; the article reports "robust evidence for the reliability and validity of the scale" in this sample, with subscale eigenvalues, reliabilities, and fit statistics in the Results.

"all the results together on the Rasch Analysis provided robust evidence for the reliability and validity of the scale to use in the current sample"
