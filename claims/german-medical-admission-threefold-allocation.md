Narrative review section on the German application process, drawing on official trust publications. The review reports that "the Stiftung fuer Hochschulzulassung distributes the available over 9,000 spots in German medical schools threefold", with the remaining 20% admitted by waiting semesters. For 2016/17 the state-ranking GPA cutoff was 1.0 or 1.1 and waiting-list admission required a minimum wait of 14 semesters.

> "the Stiftung fuer Hochschulzulassung distributes the available over 9,000 spots in German medical schools threefold [18]: 20% of all spots are given to those with the highest GPAs in state rankings. 60% are accepted via the internal process of medical faculties"
