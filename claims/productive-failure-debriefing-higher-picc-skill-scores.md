# Productive failure plus debriefing training yields significantly higher PICC theory and operation scores than traditional training in oncology specialist nurses

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Post-intervention, the intervention group scored significantly higher than the control group on PICC skills theory, piping operation, maintenance operation, and complications practice scores (P < 0.001), with very large effect sizes (e.g. theory d = 2.75). ## Evidence

### Yue Zhiying 2025
