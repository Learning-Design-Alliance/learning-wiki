# Face-to-face RE-SB consultation reduces self-downing attitudes and authoritarian attitudes toward students but not low frustration tolerance or school organization attitudes

## Evidence

### Warren et al. 2013

Jeffrey M. Warren, Edwin R. Gerler, Jr. (2013). Effects of School Counselors' Cognitive Behavioral Consultation on Irrational and Efficacy Beliefs of Elementary School Teachers. The Professional Counselor, 3(1), 6-15. https://doi.org/10.15241/jmw.3.1.6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

TIBS subscale analysis in the same quasi-experimental study showed a significant omnibus ANOVA for self-downing attitudes, F(2, 35) = 5.97, p = .006, with the face-to-face group scoring lower than control. Table 2 reports d = 1.58 and 22% of variance explained.

> "Post hoc comparisons indicated the mean for the face-to-face group (M = 16.89, SD = 4.57) statistically differed from the control group (M = 22.95, SD = 4.49) in terms of SDA."

### Warren et al. 2013 (2)

Jeffrey M. Warren, Edwin R. Gerler, Jr. (2013). Effects of School Counselors' Cognitive Behavioral Consultation on Irrational and Efficacy Beliefs of Elementary School Teachers. The Professional Counselor, 3(1), 6-15. https://doi.org/10.15241/jmw.3.1.6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Authoritarian attitudes toward students showed a significant omnibus ANOVA, F(2, 33) = 6.35, p = .004, with the face-to-face group scoring significantly lower than control. Table 2 reports d = 1.73 and 28% of variance explained for this subscale.

> "Post hoc comparisons using the Tukey HSD analysis indicated the mean scores of the face-to-face treatment (M = 10.78, SD = 3.67) were significantly different from the control group (M = 15.43, SD = 4.07)."

### Warren et al. 2013 (3)

Jeffrey M. Warren, Edwin R. Gerler, Jr. (2013). Effects of School Counselors' Cognitive Behavioral Consultation on Irrational and Efficacy Beliefs of Elementary School Teachers. The Professional Counselor, 3(1), 6-15. https://doi.org/10.15241/jmw.3.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Low frustration tolerance attitudes showed only a trend toward significance (p = .057), and attitudes to school organization were significant on the omnibus test, F(2, 33) = 4.78, p = .015, but Tukey HSD post hoc criteria were not met for either treatment versus control. The authors note Type II errors may exist due to small sample size.

> "An omnibus ANOV A indicated that means for low frustration tolerance attitudes (LFTA) were not significantly different across groups, although a slight trend toward significance was present, F(2, 33) = 3.13, p = .057."
