Minkang Kim 2021¶

Minkang Kim, Jean Decety, Ling Wu, Soohyun Baek and Derek Sankey. (2021). Neural computations in children's third-party interventions are modulated by their parents' moral values. npj Science of Learning 6:38. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00116-5

Bayesian structural equation modelling (Model 1, within-child brain–behaviour model, ppp = 0.44) using AMOS 26 with MCMC sampling. The Central P2 diff → Child's protest path had posterior mean 0.349 with a 90% CI of 0.020 to 0.695; no standardised effect size is printed.
