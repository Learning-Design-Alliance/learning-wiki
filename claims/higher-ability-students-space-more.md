## Evidence

### Paulo F. Carvalho 2020

Paulo F. Carvalho, Faria Sana and Veronica X. Yan. (2020). Self-regulated spacing in a massive open online course is related to better learning. npj Science of Learning 5:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0061-1

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

A between-participant regression on the MOOC data used final exam grade as an ability proxy; "students who performed better on the final exam were also more likely to space their study more, β = 0.06". Similar results held using pretest score.

> "The results (see Supplementary Table 2) reveal that those students who performed better on the final exam were also more likely to space their study more, β = 0.06, SE = 0.02, t(787.7) = 2.82, p = 0.005."
