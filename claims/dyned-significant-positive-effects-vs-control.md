## Evidence

### Sarah Humpage and Horacio Álvarez-Marinelli 2014

Sarah Humpage and Horacio Álvarez-Marinelli. (2014). Teachers’ Helpers: Experimental Evidence from Costa Rica on Computers for English Language Learning. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562994

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-difference estimates from a randomized controlled trial in Alajuela, Costa Rica, using the restricted sample of schools with English teachers (n=249 for DynEd vs. control). Table 4a shows Dyned*t coefficients of 0.337* (picture vocabulary) and 0.507*** (understanding directions) at end of year one; the article reports "DynEd has significant positive effects compared to the control group".

> "These tables show that DynEd has significant positive effects compared to the control group, while Imagine Learning has no positive effects."
