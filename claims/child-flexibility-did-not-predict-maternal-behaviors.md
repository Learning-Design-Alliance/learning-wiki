### Zeytinoglu 2019

Zeytinoglu, S., Calkins, S. D., & Leerkes, E. M. (2019). Maternal emotional support but not cognitive support during problem-solving predicts increases in cognitive flexibility in early childhood. International Journal of Behavioral Development, 43(1), 12-23. https://doi.org/10.1177/0165025418757706

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reverse cross-lagged paths in the final panel model (N = 278) testing child-to-parent effects were non-significant (Table 4 prints p values from .064 to .927); the article reports "Child cognitive flexibility did not predict maternal behaviors." No effect size was printed.

> "Child cognitive flexibility did not predict maternal behaviors. The same paths remained statistically significant when the outliers on the cognitive flexibility task were removed from the sample."
