## Evidence

### Shi Xu-Ying 2025

Shi Xu-Ying, Lu Bing-Rui, Yin Qing, Wang Qiu-Wen, Fang Yong-Qi, Sun Zhi-Gang. (2025). Whether case-based teaching combined with the flipped classroom is more valuable than traditional lecture-based teaching methods in clinical medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07465-4

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Sensitivity analysis sequentially excluding each study to evaluate robustness of the combined effect size. Excluding Cai et al. and Yang et al. reduced heterogeneity dramatically and lowered the effect size, though significance was retained.

> "After excluding these two studies, the I2 statistic for the combined Cohen’s d decreased to 1.4%, and the heterogeneity p-value increased to 0.419. The revised combined effect size was Cohen’s d = 0.50 (95% CI: 0.38 to 0.62, P < 0.001), which did not alter the outcome of the original analysis."
