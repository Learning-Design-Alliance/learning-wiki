## Evidence

### William R. Evans and Mats A. Selen 2016

William R. Evans and Mats A. Selen. (2016). Investigating the Use of Mastery-Style Online Homework Exercises in Introductory Algebra-based Mechanics in a Controlled Clinical Study. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

First clinical trial: 31 algebra-based mechanics students randomized to mastery (16) or traditional (15) homework on friction before the first midterm, then given a twelve-question free-response post-test. The article reports the mastery condition outperformed on "9 of the 12 questions on the post-test, signiﬁcantly so on 3 of them (p < 0.05, one-tailt-test)"; no effect size is printed.

> "On average, students in the mastery homework condition outperformed students in the traditional homework condition on 9 of the 12 questions on the post-test, signiﬁcantly so on 3 of them (p < 0.05, one-tailt-test)."
