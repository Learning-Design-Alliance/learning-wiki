## Evidence

### Gautam Yadav 2024

Gautam Yadav, Paulo F. Carvalho, Elizabeth A. McLaughlin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2024. Beyond Repetition: The Role of Varied Questioning and Feedback in Knowledge Generalization. In Proceedings of the Eleventh ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3657604.3664688

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

GLMM analysis of 30,198 opportunities from 32 students in a hybrid graduate-level course, using the quiz-adjusted KC model. The model printed a coefficient of 0.092 for unique opportunities (z = 7.020, p < .001), and the authors conclude "students benefit considerably more from engaging with new material".

> "Unique learning opportunities showed a significant positive effect on students' ability to generalize knowledge to unseen items (p < .001)."
