## Evidence

### Gove 2017

Gove, A., Brunette, T., Bulat, J., Carrol, B., Henny, C., Macon, W., Nderu, E., & Sitabkhan, Y. (2017). Assessing the impact of early learning programs in Africa. New Directions for Child and Adolescent Development, 158, 25–41. https://doi.org/10.1002/cad.20224

`q3 · i1` · `review · r2`

The LTTP II staggered entry cohort design randomized school selection at the cluster level, with Cohort 2 serving as control between baseline and midterm. Endline results indicated gains made by Cohort 1 were not sustained between midterm and endline.

> "Between baseline and midline, students in Cohort 1 improved from 4.8 to 14.2 correct words per minute on oral reading fluency, relative to almost no gains for Cohort 2 students, an effect size of 0.3 SD."
