The review reports 21 of 33 papers (63.64%) defined trust and 12 did not; the most cited definitions come from automation and organizational psychology, and the review notes a possible trend toward greater definitional transparency versus Bach et al.'s 30.43%.

> "Across the 21 papers that reported a trust deﬁnition, nine distinct deﬁnition sources were identiﬁed, indicating a lack of consensus on a single deﬁnition of trust within the reviewed literature."
