Evidence¶

Jiayu Huang 2025¶

Jiayu Huang, Ruoxin Ritter Wang, Jen-Hao Liu, Boming Xia, Yue Huang, Ruoxi Sun, Jason (Minhui) Xue, Jinan Zou. (2025). A Meta-Analysis of LLM Effects on Students Across Qualification, Socialisation, and Subjectification. A PREPRINT. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22725

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis within the socialisation domain (k = 11 studies, a small evidence base) found duration and learning strategy decisive; personalised learning g = 1.299 and reflective learning g = 0.921 contrasted with contextual learning g = 0.168.

"A clear “dose–response” relationship emerged for duration: interventions lasting more than eight weeks yielded exceptionally large gains (g= 1.204 ), whereas those under four weeks produced negligible effects."
