## Evidence

### Cortes 2022

Cortes, R. A., Peterson, E. G., Kraemer, D. J. M., Kolvoord, R. A., Uttal, D. H., Dinh, N., Weinberger, A. B., Daker, R. J., Lyons, I. M., Goldman, D., & Green, A. E. (2022). Transfer from spatial education to verbal reasoning and prediction of transfer from learning-related neural change. Science Advances. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abo3555

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple regression model comparisons in the neuroimaging sample; likelihood ratio tests compared neural change predictors against performance-based assessments including change on the same EFT task. Adding neural variables to the best performance-based combinations also significantly improved fit (all P ≤ 0.043).

> "The two neural change predictor variables (ΔaIPS_EFT and ΔIPL_EFT) each individually yielded significantly better model fit for predicting ΔReasoning than any of the performance-based assessments in the full neuroimaging sample and in the sample of Geospatial students only [ΔEFT, ΔMRT, change in spatial habits of mind, PSAT, GPA, Geospatial course grade (Geospatial students only), and gender; likelihood ratio tests: all P < 0.001]."

### Cortes 2022 (2)

Cortes, R. A., Peterson, E. G., Kraemer, D. J. M., Kolvoord, R. A., Uttal, D. H., Dinh, N., Weinberger, A. B., Daker, R. J., Lyons, I. M., Goldman, D., & Green, A. E. (2022). Transfer from spatial education to verbal reasoning and prediction of transfer from learning-related neural change. Science Advances. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abo3555

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Data-driven ensemble boosting analysis including ΔEFT, ΔMRT, spatial habits of mind change, PSAT, GPA, course grade, and gender; of performance-based variables, ΔEFT received the highest importance (16%). Analyses with course grades included converged on the same neural predictors.

> "The prediction analysis identified the two neural change predictor variables as the variables that contributed most to prediction of ΔReasoning (table S33): ΔIPL_EFT (accounting for 38% of predictor "importance") and ΔaIPS_EFT (accounting for 28%)."

### Cortes 2022 (3)

Cortes, R. A., Peterson, E. G., Kraemer, D. J. M., Kolvoord, R. A., Uttal, D. H., Dinh, N., Weinberger, A. B., Daker, R. J., Lyons, I. M., Goldman, D., & Green, A. E. (2022). Transfer from spatial education to verbal reasoning and prediction of transfer from learning-related neural change. Science Advances. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abo3555

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc model comparison: the best prediction combined neural and traditional measures, indicating neural change should bolster rather than replace traditional assessments. Adjusted R2 is not a standardized effect size, so impact is null.

> "the strongest fit among all models was achieved by a model combining the two neural change variables (ΔaIPS_EFT and ΔIPL_EFT) with three performance-based assessments (ΔEFT, ΔMRT, and PSAT; adjusted R2 = 0.39; table S53)."
