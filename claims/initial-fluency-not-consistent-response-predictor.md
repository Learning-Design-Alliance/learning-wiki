## Evidence

### Ritchey et al. 2017

Kristen D. Ritchey, Kimberly Palombo, Rebecca D. Silverman, and Deborah L. Speece. (2017). Effects of an Informational Text Reading Comprehension Intervention for Fifth-Grade Students. Learning Disability Quarterly, 40(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948716682689

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory descriptive analysis of the intervention group only (fluency scores ranged from approximately 50 to 170 wpm); no statistical test or effect size is reported. Lower-fluency students had lower post-test TOSREC and QRI scores but not WIAT or ASKIT scores.

> "Students with lower fluency scores (based on a median split) did not consistently respond in ways that were different from students with higher fluency scores."
