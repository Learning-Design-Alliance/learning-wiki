Evidence¶

Roy Lyster 2013¶

Roy Lyster, Kazuya Saito, and Masatoshi Sato. (2013). Oral corrective feedback in second language classrooms. Language Teaching, Volume 46, Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0261444812000365

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's theoretical section presents perspectives supporting CF and explicitly frames three contentious issues: "the role of learner uptake", the role of instruction, and whether CF's purpose is "to initiate the acquisition of new knowledge or to consolidate already acquired knowledge".

"Next, theoretical perspectives in support of CF are presented and some contentious issues addressed related to the role of learner uptake, the role of instruction, and the overall purpose of CF: to initiate the acquisition of new knowledge or to consolidate already acquired knowledge."
