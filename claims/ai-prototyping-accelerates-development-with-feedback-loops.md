Practitioner field note reporting one team's lessons learned while prototyping an AI-enabled literacy workflow and decision support tool for K-2 educators. The team reports that "AI enabled the rapid generation of multiple feature variations" but progress slowed without a feedback system, which they addressed with weekly educator and expert sessions.

> "AI enabled the rapid generation of multiple feature variations, for example different ways to present student data in a dashboard. But progress slowed when there was no clear system for getting feedback."
