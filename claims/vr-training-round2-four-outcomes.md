## Evidence

### Lin Pei-Chao 2024

Lin Pei-Chao, Wung Shu-Fen, Lin Pei-Chen, Lin Yi-Ching, Lin Cheng-Yu, Huang Hsiao-Ling. (2024). Virtual reality-based simulation learning on geriatric oral health care for nursing students: a pilot study. BMC Oral Health 24, Article 627. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-024-04249-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

GEE analysis of the pilot RCT after the second training round at week 4, controlling for gender, academic year, VR game experience, and oral health learning experience. All four outcome contrasts favored the experimental group with printed effect sizes.

> "The higher geriatric oral health care knowledge training [β: 2.04, 95% CI: 1.57, 3.55; p < 0.001; effect size: 1.40], attitudes [β: 1.56, 95% CI: 0.25, 2.88; p = 0.020; effect size: 0.64], self-efficacy [β: 6.04, 95% CI: 2.93, 9.16; p < 0.001; effect size: 1.05], and intention to assist oral health care for older adult scores [β: 3.28, 95% CI: 0.32, 6.24; p = 0.030; effect size: 0.60] among participants in the experimental group than those in the control group persisted after the second round of training."
