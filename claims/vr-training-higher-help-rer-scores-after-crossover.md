## Evidence

### Falcone Veronica 2024

Falcone Veronica, Catic Anja, Heinzl Florian, Steinbauer Philipp, Wagner Michael, Mikula Fanny, Dorittke Tim, Roessler Bernhard, Farr Alex. (2024). Impact of a virtual reality-based simulation training for shoulder dystocia on human and technical skills among caregivers: a randomized-controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-57785-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized crossover trial of 61 caregivers at a university obstetric unit; after the 12-week washout and crossover, the group receiving 360°-VR training scored higher on the modified ALSO checklist, with medians "7 vs. 6.5; (p = 0.01)". No effect size is printed.

> "However, after crossover, the control group yielded significantly higher HELP-RER scores [7 vs. 6.5; (p = 0.01)], with lower diagnosis-to-delivery-time [85.5 vs. 99 s; (p = 0.02)], and TLX scores [57 vs. 68; (p = 0.04)]."
