Evidence¶

Hemmeter et al. 2015¶

Mary Louise Hemmeter, Jessica K. Hardy, Alana G. Schnitz, Jessie Morris Adams, and Kiersten A. Kinder. (2015). Effects of Training and Coaching With Performance Feedback on Teachers' Use of Pyramid Model Practices. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121415594924

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Single-case multiple probe design across three sets of Pyramid Model practices replicated across three teachers in blended preschool classrooms. Checklists scored percentage of indicators present; all three teachers showed immediate level shifts after coaching began, which the authors describe as "establishing a functional relation".

"All teachers in this study increased their use of targeted teaching practices. Thus, there were nine demonstrations/replications across the three teachers, establishing a functional relation."
