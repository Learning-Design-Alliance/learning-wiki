`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MCQ analysis across all 971 students in the randomized between-subjects study; chi-square tests found no significant differences in MCQ accuracy between conditions (p > .05). Table 1 shows, for example, sumArray accuracy of 88.84% (Diverse) versus 87.84% (Generic).

> "In MCQ assessments (see Table 1), which tested students' grasp of programming concepts, accuracy was consistently high (>87%) across all conditions, with negligible differences between diverse and generic explanations."
