Evidence¶

Symonds 2021¶

Symonds, J. E., Schreiber, J. B., & Torsney, B. M. (2021). Silver linings and storm clouds: Divergent profiles of student momentary engagement emerge in response to the same task. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000605

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive statistics from the seven-profile latent profile solution for the same 196-student single-task sample show internally divergent profiles: "the attentive disinterested (n = 62, 32%) profile had moderate levels of emotional and cognitive engagement" despite high behavioral engagement; the distracted motivated profile (n = 12, 6%) showed the reverse pattern.

"the attentive amotivated (n = 18, 9%) profile also had low lower levels of emotional engagement, the attentive disinterested (n = 62, 32%) profile had moderate levels of emotional and cognitive engagement, and the attentive disaffected (n = 12, 6%) profile had low emotional and cognitive engagement."

q2 · i? · associational · r2

A simple cross-tabulation of profile membership by classroom (Table 7) in the same sample showed "nearly all classrooms had at least some instances of every profile", meaning students in each profile sat alongside students in other profiles during the task.

"Visual examination of the statistics indicated that nearly all classrooms had at least some instances of every profile."
