## Evidence

### Homa Babai Shishavan and Mahdi Jalili 2020

Homa Babai Shishavan and Mahdi Jalili. (2020). Responding to student feedback: Individualising teamwork scores based on peer assessment. [doi:10.1016/j.ijedro.2020.100019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijedro.2020.100019)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-and-after comparison of written Course Evaluation Survey feedback from 151 students (27 teams) in 2018 and 129 students (23 teams) in 2019 in RMIT engineering design courses. The proportion appreciating teamwork "increased to 34% post peer assessment" from 24%, which the Results section also describes as "a 42% increase in students' positive feedback".

> "While only 24% of the students appreciated teamwork aspects of the course before the peer assessment, it increased to 34% post peer assessment."
