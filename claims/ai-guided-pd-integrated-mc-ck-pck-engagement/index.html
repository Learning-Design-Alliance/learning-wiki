Embedded case study of eight Grade 5-6 rural teachers completing ten AI-guided modules; iterative thematic analysis of dialogue logs, artifacts, and interviews found "teachers engaged with MC, CK, and PCK as interconnected forms of reasoning rather than as isolated domains."
