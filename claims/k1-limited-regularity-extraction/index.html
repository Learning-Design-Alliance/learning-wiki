Evidence¶

Duan 2025¶

Duan, R., Sun, Q., & Tong, X. (2025). Non-linear development in statistical learning of visual orthographic regularities. npj Science of Learning 10, 6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00298-2

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-sample t-tests against the 50% chance level on recognition accuracy by age group. All groups exceeded chance overall (Cohen's ds > 0.52); the article reports K1 children could merely extract positional regularities at high and moderate levels, with their low-consistency t-test not significant (p = 0.140).

"The overall mean accuracy rates i n the recognition phase were also signiﬁcantly larger than chance level (ts > 3.35,ps < 0.01, Cohen’s ds > 0.52)."
