## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The intervention generated statistically significant impacts on counting, patterning, geometry, and measurement subdomains, with end-of-pre-K treatment-control differences of .63 SD. [→ Bailey 2018](#bailey-2018)

## Evidence

### Bailey 2018

Bailey, D. H., Duncan, G. J., Watts, T., Clements, D. H., & Sarama, J. (2018). Risky Business: Correlation and Causation in Longitudinal Studies of Skill Development. American Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1037/amp0000146

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized TRIAD evaluation; end-of-preschool knowledge was assessed with the REMA, grouped into four subdomains scored as proportion correct and standardized. The article reports significant impacts "on all four subdomains" with the printed β and SE values, and a .63 SD overall end-of-pre-K impact.

> "The intervention generated statistically significant impacts on all four subdomains: counting (β= 0.45, SE=0.06), patterning (β= 0.36, SE=0.06), geometry (β= 0.67, SE=0.06), and measurement (β= 0.20, SE=0.06)"
