Evidence¶

Li 2024¶

Li, Z., Wang, J., Chen, Y., Li, Q., Yin, S. & Chen, A. (2024). Attenuated conflict self-referential information facilitating conflict resolution. npj Science of Learning 9, 47. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00256-4

q3 · i? · causal · r1

MVPA decoding of self-referential information from the 33-participant EEG experiment: incongruent-self-reference vs. incongruent-non-self-reference was decodable from 100 to 420 ms and congruent-self-reference vs. non-self-reference from 80 to 580 ms (p < 0.001, cluster-corrected). The authors note the decoding window extends significantly beyond the N2 time window, suggesting self-referential processing also encompasses conflict monitoring.

"MVPA revealed a signiﬁcant above-chance difference between classes of congruent-self-reference and incongruent-non-self-reference from 80 to 580 ms after stimulus onset ( p < 0.001, cluster-corrected, right-sided)."
