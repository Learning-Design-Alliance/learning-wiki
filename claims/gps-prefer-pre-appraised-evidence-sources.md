## Evidence

### Galbraith Kevin 2017

Galbraith Kevin, Ward Alison, Heneghan Carl. (2017). A real-world approach to Evidence-Based Medicine in general practice: a competency framework derived from a systematic review and Delphi process. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5415750/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Cross-sectional survey data synthesised in the systematic review of GP engagement with EBM. The review reports that "evidence-based guidelines (55%) and journals containing evidence summaries (52%)" were rated most useful, while MEDLINE and primary research articles were rated useful by fewer GPs. This supports the pragmatism competency's emphasis on pre-appraised sources.

> "One survey found the resources endorsed by the greatest proportion of GPs as 'very useful' were evidence-based guidelines (55%) and journals containing evidence summaries (52%); access to MEDLINE, and primary research articles were both rated 'very useful' by fewer GPs (27% and 25% respectively)"
