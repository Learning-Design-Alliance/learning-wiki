Curiosity Corner 2009¶

Curiosity Corner. (2009). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.successforall.org/early/early_curiosity.htm

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial (PCER Consortium, 2008); phonological processing assessed with the Pre-CTOPPP Elision subtest in 18 preschools. The report confirms "no discernible effects on phonological processing."

"The study shows, and the WWC confirms, no statistically significant or substantively important differences between children who participated in Curiosity Corner and those in the control group, and thus, no discernible effects on phonological processing."
