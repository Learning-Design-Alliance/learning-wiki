## Subclaims
`q3 i3` PBL group means were significantly higher on concept relationships (d = 0.81), hierarchy levels (d = 0.61), and cross linkages (d = 0.62). [→ Hung Chia-Hui 2015](#hung-chia-hui-2015)

## Evidence

### Hung Chia-Hui 2015

Hung Chia-Hui, Lin Chen-Yung. (2015). Using concept mapping to evaluate knowledge structure in problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0496-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests comparing concept-map scoring parameters between the PBL (n = 26) and LBL (n = 26) groups after 6 weeks of differential instruction. The article reports the results as "a medium to large effect size in the three parameters", with d = 0.81 for concept relationships.

> "Independent-samples t-tests show significant differences between the PBL and LBL groups in terms of the relationships among the concepts (t (50) = 2.93, p =0.005; d = 0.81; power =0.93), hierarchy levels (t (50) = 2.25, p =0.029; d = 0.61; power =0.86), and cross linkages (t (50) = 2.30, p =0.026; d = 0.62; power =0.87)."
