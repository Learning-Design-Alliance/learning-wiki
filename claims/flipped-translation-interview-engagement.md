## Evidence

### Deng 2018

Lijun Deng. (2018). The Project-Based Flipped Learning Model in Business English Translation Course: Learning, Teaching and Assessment. English Language Teaching, 11(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n9p118

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent interviews on 10 students from each class, each lasting 5-7 minutes and conducted by two uninvolved teachers; students also called the translation log "a very productive means" to observe and reflect on their process.

> "Students from the experimental class expressed strong interest and greater initiatives in the flipped learning methodology. The on-line study checklist exercises and the division of client and translator groups in and out of class were acknowledged by the students as very useful in engaging them"
