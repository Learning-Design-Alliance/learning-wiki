Evidence¶

Xinyue Wang 2024¶

Xinyue Wang, Kelong Lu, Yingyao He, Xinuo Qiao, Zhenni Gao, Yu Zhang & Ning Hao. (2024). Dynamic brain networks in spontaneous gestural communication. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00274-2

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Dynamic brain network analysis (sliding windows and k-means clustering of WTC-based ISFC, 27 dyads; Table 1). The article reports that under BOTH and ONE, "State 1 had signiﬁcantly increased ISFC that concentrated in the mirro ring and mentalizing systems" while States 2 and 3 did not.

"under the ‘BOTH’and ‘ONE’conditions, State 1 had signiﬁcantly higher globE, Cp and signiﬁcantly lower Lp than State 2"

Xinyue Wang 2024 (2)¶

Xinyue Wang, Kelong Lu, Yingyao He, Xinuo Qiao, Zhenni Gao, Yu Zhang & Ning Hao. (2024). Dynamic brain networks in spontaneous gestural communication. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00274-2

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Correlational analysis linking occurrence rates of dISFC states to behavioural indices after validation analysis in the 27-dyad study. Both printed correlations support that State 1 "might represent an efﬁcient state of information exchange and cognitiv e alignment among communicators".

"the occurrence rate of State 1 was positively correlated with RPP originality and sqrt (IOC) under the ‘BOTH’condition (Originality: r = 0.43, P = 0.041; sqrt [IOC]: r =0 . 4 4 ,P = 0.020;"
