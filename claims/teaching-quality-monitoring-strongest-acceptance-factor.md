Miaoyun Li, Chun Lu, Harrison H. Yang, Di Wu, Xiao Yang. (2023). The influence of organizational factors on the acceptance of online teaching among college faculty during the COVID-19 pandemic: a nationwide study in mainland China. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10245-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' summary interpretation of the MSEM results from the nationwide survey (Discussion section). Teaching quality monitoring is described as "the most influential variable affecting faculty's acceptance of online teaching" because it significantly predicted both perceived usefulness (β = .024) and behavioral intention (β = .016).

> "By drawing data from a nationwide survey in mainland China, this study found that teaching quality monitoring had the strongest association with faculty's acceptance of online teaching via the direct positive influence on both behavioral intention and perceived usefulness."
