### Biagioni Gabriela 2026

Biagioni Gabriela, Comodo Fernanda, Coelho Marcelo Santos. (2026). Technical Quality and Students' Perception of Endodontic Preclinical Training Using Natural or LikeReal Artificial Teeth. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective comparison of 408 teeth (204 per group) used by two consecutive dental classes during preclinical endodontic training. Ledges occurred in 8.33% of artificial teeth versus 2.94% of natural teeth; root fractures in 5.88% versus 0.98%, both statistically significant at p < 0.05.

> "Ledges and root fractures were more frequent in the ATG (8.33%) than in the NTG (2.94%), which is similar to a recent clinical study [13]."
