Evidence¶

Matey Yordanov 2026¶

Matey Yordanov, Mikhail Bychkov, and Andrei Kuchma. (2026). The Effect of High-Frequency, Automatically-marked Formative Assessments on Student Outcomes in A-Level Sciences. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23566

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

ANCOVA on post-test mock scores in a 16-week quasi-experiment with intact classes (intervention N=72, control N=70), Z-scored pre-test as covariate and cluster-robust standard errors. The article reports a statistically significant main effect with effect size η²p = .149, and adjusted means of 81.2% (SE=1.2) versus 64.4% (SE=1.4).

"The analysis revealed a statistically significant main effect,F(1,139) = 24.32,p<.001,η 2 p =.149."
