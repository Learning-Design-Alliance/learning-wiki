### Zhao 2026

Zhao, L., Echeverria, V., Gašević, D., Yan, L., Alfredo, R., Li, X., & Martinez-Maldonado, R. (2026). Automated multimodal visual analytics of social and epistemic dimensions in team communication for reflective practice in healthcare simulation. Computers & Education, 252, 105655. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105655

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive qualitative analysis of 28 audio- and video-recorded debriefing sessions (DS1–DS28) identified three higher-order themes of social network use. The article reports engagement in "19/28 sessions, 38 quotes", roles in "25/28 sessions, 87 quotes", and shared mindset in "9/28 sessions, 21 quotes".

> "Across the 28 analysed debriefing sessions, educators utilised social networks to guide learners to reflect on three higher-order teamwork constructs. Specifically, these constructs include learners' engagement in communication (19/28 sessions, 38 quotes), roles in the simulation session (25/28 sessions, 87 quotes), and establishing a shared mindset (9/28 sessions, 21 quotes)."
