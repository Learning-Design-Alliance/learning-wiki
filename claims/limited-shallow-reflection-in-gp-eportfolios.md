`q2 i?` Reflective forms were present in 54.4% of e-portfolios, and 83.7% of present entries could not be qualified as reflective; no dialogic or critical reflection was found. [→ van der Gulden R 2020](#van-der-gulden-r-2020)

## Evidence

### van der Gulden R 2020

van der Gulden R, Heeneman S, Kramer AWM, Laan RFJM, Scherpbier-de Haan ND, Thoonen BPA. (2020). How is self-regulated learning documented in e-portfolios of trainees? A content analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02114-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quantitative content analysis of 90 GP trainee e-portfolios, rated with a literature-based codebook. "forms aimed at reflection were present in just over half of the e-portfolios (54.4%)"; only 8 of 49 entries reached descriptive reflection, with 0% dialogic and 0% critical reflection.

> "forms aimed at reflection were present in just over half of the e-portfolios (54.4%). In case an entry was present it could often (83.7%) not be qualified as reflective, because only experiences and events were described."
