## Evidence

### Colin G. West 2023

Colin G. West. (2023). AI and the FCI: Can ChatGPT project an understanding of introductory physics?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2303.01067](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2303.01067)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Administration of the 23 usable modified FCI multiple-choice items to ChatGPT3.5 with the BASIC prompt in February 2023; the model "gave a correct answer for ﬁfteen of them," with correct answers spread across both unaltered and figure-modified items.

> "When prompted with the 23 usable FCI questions us- ing the “BASIC” prompt format, ChatGPT3.5 gave a correct answer for ﬁfteen of them."

### Colin G. West 2023 (2)

Colin G. West. (2023). AI and the FCI: Can ChatGPT project an understanding of introductory physics?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2303.01067](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2303.01067)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison against the end-of-semester FCI post-test score distribution from the author's previous class, in which "the median student score was a 56%"; ChatGPT3.5 falls just below or somewhat above the typical student depending on how its unanswerable figure items are treated.

> "In that distribution, the median student score was a 56%, meaning that depending on how charitably one treats its partial results, ChatGPT was either just below the performance of a typical student or else perhaps a nontrivial cut above average."
