### Holger Maus 2026

Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1142/s2661339526500125](https://doi.org/10.1142/s2661339526500125)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Nonparametric two-sided Mann–Whitney U test on the 36 ratings of the most frequently edited problem (27 correct vs. 9 incorrect feedback cases; M1 = 4.3 vs. M2 = 4.0), conducted to ensure independence of observations. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The test revealed no significant differ- ence in perceived correctness ratings between stu- dents receiving correct feedback and those receiv- ing incorrect feedback (U= 148.5,p= 0.2805)."
