## Evidence

### Imlach 2017

Imlach, A.-R., Ward, D. D., Stuart, K. E., Summers, M. J., Valenzuela, M. J., King, A. E., Saunders, N. L., Summers, J., Srikanth, V. K., Robinson, A. & Vickers, J. C. (2017). Age is no barrier: predictors of academic success in older learners. npj Science of Learning 2:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0014-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the initial regression (N = 181), working memory was a significant primary predictor (β = 0.192, p = 0.028). After re-examination in the full sample (N = 329) with secondary predictors added, its effect fell to β = 0.109, p = 0.055. The effect therefore does not replicate across models.

> "The predictive capacity of the model increased by 2.3% of the variance in GPA due to the inclusion of secondary predictors, and working memory was no longer signiﬁcantly predictive."
