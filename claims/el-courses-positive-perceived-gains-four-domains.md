## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across three course iterations, 87%–96% of students agreed or strongly agreed with Likert prompts measuring perceived gains in teamwork, career preparedness, critical thinking and problem solving, and application of theory to practice. [→ Arnot Michelle 2025](#arnot-michelle-2025)

## Evidence

### Arnot Michelle 2025

Arnot Michelle, Kim Jinhee, French Michelle, Lin Sonia Y., Pashley Charlotte, Laposa Rebecca R. (2025). Students Perceive Similar Gains in Collaboration, Communication and Professional Skills in Two Distinct Experiential Learning Courses. Pharmacology Research & Perspectives. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.70095

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mixed-methods survey of 152 of 153 students enrolled in two experiential learning electives over three academic years (2021–2024), with Likert and open-ended questions analyzed by research assistants not involved in teaching. The article reports "87%–96% either agreed or strongly agreed" across the four domains.

> "Overall student responses were positive to Likert prompts (87%–96% either agreed or strongly agreed) exploring these domains."
