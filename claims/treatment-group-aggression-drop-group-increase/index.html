Descriptive statistics for the 33 treatment-group students (Table 6), comparing first-half and second-half coded incident frequencies during the curriculum implementation. Four of five incident types decreased; group incidents increased from 12 to 15.

"The greatest percentage decrease in the treatment group was in the frequency of aggressive incidents, which dropped from 14 to 1, a reduction of 92.86%. The greatest decrease in occurrences was in the teacher sent frequency, which dropped from 116 to 63, a reduction of 53 occurrences."
