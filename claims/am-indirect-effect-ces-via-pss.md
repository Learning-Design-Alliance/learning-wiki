Psychological resilience as a selective perceptual amplifier in post-crisis learning: a dual-pathway model of academic motivation, perceived school support, and cognitive engagement – a multi-country study based on PISA 2022. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04665-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling with bootstrap confidence intervals (1,000 resamples, 969 successful convergences) on PISA 2022 data. The indirect effect of AM on CES through PSS was significant: "The indirect effect accounted for 53.6% of the total effect". Note the article states this mediation analysis was conducted without survey design correction.

> "The indirect effect accounted for 53.6% of the total effect (bootstrap 95% CI [0.102, 0.104])."
