# Performance goals increase maladaptive, noninquisitive help seeking among sixth graders but not third graders

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among sixth graders, performance-goal students were more likely than learning-goal students to exhibit maladaptive help seeking; among third graders the goal difference was not significant. [→ Newman 1995](#newman-1995)

## Evidence

### Newman 1995

Richard S. Newman and Mahna T. Schwager. (1995). Students' Help Seeking During Problem Solving: Effects of Grade, Goal, and Prior Achievement. American Educational Research Journal, 32(2), 352-376. https://www.jstor.org/stable/1163435

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Logistic regression of dichotomized MALADAP frequencies over 10 Day 3 problems showed a significant grade x goal interaction, chi-square (1) = 8.22, p < .005, with the printed percentages of students showing at least one maladaptive pattern.

> "Among sixth graders, students given performance goals were more likely than those given learning goals to exhibit noninquisitiveness (percentages of students with at least one occurrence of MALADAP were .71 versus .42 for performance and learning goals, respectively). Among third graders, the difference according to goal was not significant (percentages of students with at least one occurrence of MALADAP were .77 versus .59)."
