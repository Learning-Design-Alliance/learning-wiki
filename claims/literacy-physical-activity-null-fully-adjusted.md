# The association of total literacy with physical activity is not statistically significant after adjustment for income and chronic medical conditions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Higher literacy was associated with more frequent physical activity in the core model, but the association was no longer significant in the fully adjusted model, and financial literacy was not associated with physical activity. [→ Bennett JS 2012](#bennett-js-2012)

## Evidence

### Bennett JS 2012

Bennett JS, Boyle PA, James BD, Bennett DA. (2012). Correlates of health and financial literacy in older adults without dementia. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2318-12-30

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional regression of physical activity (hours per week, five activities over the past two weeks) in 556 older adults. The core-model association (p = .025 in Table 2) was "no longer significant" after adjusting for income and chronic conditions; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, in the fully adjusted model, the association was similar but was no longer significant. In additional analyses, higher health literacy was associated with more frequent physical activity in the fully adjusted model, but financial literacy was not associated with physical activity."
