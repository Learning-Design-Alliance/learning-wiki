# In a pooled multivariate model, the combined self-efficacy/self-management growth predicts about 80% lower odds of suspension, while the growth mindset intercept unexpectedly increases suspension odds

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A one-SD increase in the combined self-efficacy/self-management slope is associated with suspension odds about 80% lower than students with average growth. [→ Soland 2019](#soland-2019)
`q2 i?` The growth mindset intercept is associated with increased odds of suspension, an effect that appears to be a covariate artifact. [→ Soland 2019 (2)](#soland-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Soland 2019

Soland, J. & Kuhfeld, M. (2019). Identifying students who are off-track academically in 9th Grade: the role of social-emotional learning trajectories. (NWEA Collaborative for Student Growth Research Brief). https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/identifying-students-who-are-off-track-academically-in-9th-grade-the-role-of-social-emotional-learning-trajectories/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Alternative multivariate model (Figure 5/Table 6) pooling self-efficacy and self-management intercept and slope variances to address multicollinearity (slopes correlated .95), regressed on each off-track indicator with background covariates.

> "For whether the student was ever suspended, the combined self-efficacy/self-management intercept and slope were both significant, with a one standard deviation unit increase in the slope term associated with odds of being suspended that were about 80% lower than students with average growth, controlling for the other background variables."

### Soland 2019 (2)

Soland, J. & Kuhfeld, M. (2019). Identifying students who are off-track academically in 9th Grade: the role of social-emotional learning trajectories. (NWEA Collaborative for Student Growth Research Brief). https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/identifying-students-who-are-off-track-academically-in-9th-grade-the-role-of-social-emotional-learning-trajectories/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same pooled-model analysis (Table 6, growth mindset intercept OR = 1.845, p = .002). The article reports that without covariates such as test scores the growth mindset logits were near zero and nonsignificant, suggesting the effect partially reflects the covariates.

> "Surprisingly, the growth mindset intercept is associated with an increase in the odds that a student was suspended."
