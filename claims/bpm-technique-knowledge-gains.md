## Evidence

### Patel Mandeep K. 2026

Patel Mandeep K., Timko‐Swaim Lynn, Conklin Perrilynn, Lu Wei‐Hsin. (2026). Curriculum Integration of an Interactive Blood Pressure Measurement Module to Improve Healthcare Students' Knowledge, Confidence, and Skills. The Journal of Clinical Hypertension. https://doi.org/10.1111/jch.70234

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-squared analyses of pre/post technique knowledge in the pre–post study. Medical students' all-six-correct rose from 28% to 51.9% (χ² = 22.6, p < 0.001); PA students' from 0% to 44.8% (χ² = 62.8, p < 0.001).

> "51.9% of medical students correctly identified the six techniques after receiving the BPM training which is significantly higher than the 28% at the beginning of the training [χ [2] = 22.6, p < 0.001]. Significant increase in the percentage of PA students getting all six techniques was also seen (pre: 0% vs. post: 44.8%) [χ [2] = 62.8, p < 0.001]"
