## Evidence

### Maria Adelina Ianos 2026

Maria Adelina Ianos, Andrei Rusu, Ramona Paloş, Cristina Petreñas, Clara Sansó. (2026). Linking Language Attitudes to Proficiency Across Learning Contexts: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Correlational Evidence. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10193-w

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level moderator analysis of proficiency measurement approach across 132 effects. The self-assessment subgroup showed "r =.35, 95% CI [.30, .40]" versus r = .28 for external measures, consistent with H3a.

> "Studies using self-assessments (k = 78, r =.35, 95% CI [.30, .40]) yielded larger associations than those using external measures ( k = 54, r =.28, 95% CI [.18, .37]), with a three-level differential of β = 0.08."
