Hartikainen 2026¶

Hartikainen, S., Nokelainen, P., Puhakka, I., & Kangaslampi, R. (2026). Emotions in flipped and lecture-based math courses. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102474

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel binomial logistic regression across 546 learning events (Table 3, Model 4) prints a teaching implementation coefficient of 0.02 with a confidence interval spanning zero, consistent with the reported null. Equivalence was not tested; the simulation-based power analysis reported power of 49.4% for H4 (flipped more positive than lecture-based).
