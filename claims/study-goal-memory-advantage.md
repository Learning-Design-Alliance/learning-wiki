## Evidence

### Moort 2022

Moort, M. L. van, Koornneef, A. W., Wilderjans, T. F., & Broek, P. W. van den. (2022). Validation processes and reading purpose: Is validation against knowledge and prior text influenced by reading goal? Journal of Educational Psychology, 114(7), 1533-1552. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000769

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects logistic regression on recognition memory probability-correct scores for target sentences showed "a main effect of goal" (OR = 1.32, p = .009), indicating that participants instructed to read for study recognized target information better the next day than those reading for general comprehension.

> "On memory scores (see Table 5 for descriptive statistics) we observed a main effect of goal ( b = .29, SE = .11, z = 2.60, p = .009, OR = 1.32)"
