## Evidence

### Gilmore Camilla K. 2010

Gilmore Camilla K., McCarthy Shannon E., Spelke Elizabeth S. (2010). Non-symbolic arithmetic abilities and achievement in the first year of formal schooling in mathematics. Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2010.02.002

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Experiment 2 gave 62 kindergarten children from a different lower-middle socioeconomic community a modified non-symbolic addition task with easier ratios, plus verbal and Arabic number knowledge tests, and end-of-year school tests of mathematics, reading (DIBELS) and verbal intelligence (PPVT-III).

> "performance of non-symbolic addition again was correlated with academic achievement in mathematics (r = .376, df = 59, p =. 003; Figure 4b), and the correlation remained after controlling for verbal intelligence and achievement in literacy (r = .276, df = 57, p = .034)"
