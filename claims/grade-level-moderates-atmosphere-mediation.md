## Evidence

### Zhang Wanting 2025

Zhang Wanting, Sun Junhua, Yao Haichang. (2025). The impact of learning efficiency on teaching evaluation in urban-rural interactive classrooms: a moderated mediation model. BMC Psychology, 13. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03313-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderated mediation test (PROCESS Model 7) on the 449-student sample; the grade-level interaction on the mediator was β = -0.148, t = -2.168, significant at p < 0.05, establishing grade moderation of the first-stage path.

> "the interaction term between student grade level (moderator) and learning efficiency (predictor) exerted a significant negative predictive effect on classroom atmosphere (mediator) (β = -0.148, p < 0.05)"

### Zhang Wanting 2025 (2)

Zhang Wanting, Sun Junhua, Yao Haichang. (2025). The impact of learning efficiency on teaching evaluation in urban-rural interactive classrooms: a moderated mediation model. BMC Psychology, 13. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03313-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PROCESS Model 7 decomposition on the same sample: the indirect effect via classroom atmosphere fell from 0.208 (lower grades) to 0.132 (higher grades), IMM = -0.034, while the direct effect was invariant, confirming H4.

> "for lower-grade students, the indirect effect was 0.208, whereas for higher-grade students, it declined to 0.132, yielding an index of moderated mediation (IMM) of -0.034. The direct effect of learning efficiency on teaching evaluation remained statistically stable and invariant (β = 0.437, p < 0.001), demonstrating no moderation by grade level."
