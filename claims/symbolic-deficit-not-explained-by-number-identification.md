## Evidence

### Bert De Smedt 2011

Bert De Smedt, Camilla K. Gilmore. (2011). Defective number module or impaired access? Numerical magnitude processing in first graders with mathematical difficulties. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology, 108, 278–292. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2010.09.003

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Re-analysis of the approximate addition ANOVA with number identification accuracy as covariate. The "group × task interaction remained" significant, and TA children still outperformed LA and MLD on the symbolic task, indicating poorer symbolic knowledge does not fully account for the deficit.

> "When controlling for this variable, the group × task interaction remained (F(2,59) = 4.46, p = .02, ηp² = .13). Post-hoc t-test confirmed that TA children performed significantly more accurately on the symbolic task than children with LA (t(47) = 3.69, p < .01, d = 1.21) and children with MLD (t(43) = 3.47, p < .01, d = 1.14)"
