### Winter Lone 2024

Winter Lone, Neubauer Ricarda, Weimer Johannes, Dietrich Christoph F., Wittek Agnes, Schiestl Lina, Marinova Milka, Schäfer Valentin Sebastian, Strizek Brigitte, Recker Florian. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review attributes this to Celebi et al., reporting equivalence across internship, course, and combined training formats for prospective peer teachers, with no significant difference in their subsequent instruction effectiveness.

> "When comparing different training approaches, Celebi et al. showed that the training of peer tutors through internships, conventional courses, or a combination of both leads to an equivalent learning outcome for future peer instructors."
