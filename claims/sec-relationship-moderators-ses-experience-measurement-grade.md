Meta-regression and subgroup analyses within the three-level meta-analytic dataset tested gender, SES, teaching experience, measurement modality, and grade level as moderators; the article reports moderating effects for SES, experience, measurement method, and grade level, with no moderator coefficients printed in the available text.
