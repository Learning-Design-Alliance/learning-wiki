### Miller-Matero Lisa Renee 2019

Miller-Matero Lisa Renee, Tobin Erin T., Fleagle Elizabeth, Coleman Joseph P., Nair Anupama (2019). Motivating residents to change communication: the role of a brief motivational interviewing didactic. Primary Health Care Research & Development, 20, e124. https://doi.org/10.1017/S146342361900015X

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level paired samples t-test from the utilization scale (Table 2), pre-test to 1-month follow-up. Means were nearly unchanged ("4.29 (0.46)" to "4.33 (0.58)") and the difference was not significant (p = 0.75); equivalence was not tested.

> "3. How often do you use open-ended questions in your clinical practice? 4.29 (0.46) 4.33 (0.58) −0.33 0.75"

### Miller-Matero Lisa Renee 2019 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`
