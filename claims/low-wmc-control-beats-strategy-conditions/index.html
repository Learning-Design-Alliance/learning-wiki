Evidence¶

Swanson et al. 2013¶

H. Lee Swanson, Michael J. Orosco, Cathy Lussier. (2013). Does Cognitive Strategy Training on Word Problems Compensate for Working Memory Capacity in Children with Math Difficulties? SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563039

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Adjusted posttest means at the low pretest WMC level (z = -.50) in the randomized ANCOVA, collapsed across ability group. The article reports "Post-test scores were higher for the control condition than the strategy conditions", consistent with the hypothesis that low-WMC children may be overtaxed by strategies.

"For the low WMC level, the adjusted z-post-test means (collapsed across ability group) were .26, .59, .36, and .75 for verbal-only, verbal+visual, visual-only and control conditions, respectively. Post-test scores were higher for the control condition than the strategy conditions."
