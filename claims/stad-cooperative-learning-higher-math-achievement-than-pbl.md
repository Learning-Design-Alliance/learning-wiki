## Evidence

### Tawachai Rattanatumma and Dr.Vichian Puncreobutr 2016

Tawachai Rattanatumma and Dr.Vichian Puncreobutr. (2016). Assessing the Effectiveness of STAD Model and Problem Based Learning in Mathematics Learning Achievement and Problem Solving Ability. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.12. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099565

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental randomized control-group pretest-posttest study with 25 students per group. One-Way ANCOVA on post-test mathematics achievement scores, controlling for pre-test scores, showed a significant effect of teaching method favoring STAD (F = 434.716, p < .01), with R Squared = .924.

> "The results of One-Way ANCOV A indicates that there is a significant effect of teaching methods on Mathematics learning achievement of the students after controlling their normal scores of mathematics learning achievement before taught using the specific methods, (F, .750), P>0.05 and after using the methods (F, 434.716), P<0.01"
