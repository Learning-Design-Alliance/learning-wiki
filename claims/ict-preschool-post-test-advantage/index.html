Evidence¶

Oluwadare 2015¶

Oluwadare, F. A. (2015). ICT Use in Preschool Science Education: A Case Study of Some Private Nursery Schools in Ekiti State. Journal of Education and Practice, 6(31). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083397

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-test on post-test EMSAT scores of the experimental group (132 children) and control group (128 children) after the four-week teaching intervention. The article reports "t = -2.007, p = 0.046" as significant between the groups; no effect size is printed.

"Finally, an independent sample t-test was conducted . The t-test for equality of means was significant (t = -2.007, p = 0.046), indicating significant differences, in the scores between the experimental and control groups."
