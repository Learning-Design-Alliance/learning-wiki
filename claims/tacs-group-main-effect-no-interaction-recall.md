## Evidence

### Limor Shtoots 2024

Limor Shtoots, Asher Nadler, Roni Partouche, Dorin Sharir, Aryeh Rothstein, Liran Shati & Daniel A. Levy. (2024). Frontal midline theta transcranial alternating current stimulation enhances early consolidation of episodic memory. npj Science of Learning 9:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00222-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus repeated-measures ANOVA on post-intervention free recall (tests 4–6) in the four-group experiment, with Greenhouse-Geisser correction used at baseline. The article prints a main effect of group, F(3,68) = 6.475, p = 0.001, "partial η2 = 0.222," plus a main effect of test reflecting natural forgetting; no interaction was found.

> "Repeated measures ANOVA for free recall performance on tests 4– 6 indicated a main effect of the test, F(2136) = 65.00, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.489 (re ﬂecting natural forgetting over time, as anticipated), and a main effect of group F(3,68) = 6.475 p = 0.001, partial η2 = 0.222."
