`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests compared the 50 students' mean iRAT score for Themes 1–4 (pre) versus Themes 5–7 (post) in the analytics-enabled 2024 cohort. The mean iRAT score increased from 3.67 to 4.38 with intervention (p < 0.0001), whereas the matched 2023 cohort without intervention showed no improvement.

> "Among the bottom 50 students identified, the mean iRAT score increased from 3.67 (themes 1–4) to 4.38 (themes 5–7) with intervention (p < 0.0001) (Fig. 5A), whereas the matched 2023 cohort without intervention showed no improvement, and, in fact, performance decreased throughout the semester"

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-semester performance was compared between the at-risk subgroup in 2023 and 2024 and between whole-cohort means to monitor for cohort-wide grade inflation. Students who received intervention in 2024 achieved a mean score of 56% compared with 51% for the equivalent non-intervention cohort in 2023, representing a significant improvement.

> "students who received intervention in 2024 achieved a mean score of 56%, compared with 51% for the equivalent non-intervention cohort in 2023, representing a significant improvement. Importantly, whole cohort mean scores remained stable across years"
