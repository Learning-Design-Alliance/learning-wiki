## Evidence

### Puddester Derek 2015

Puddester Derek, MacDonald Colla J., Clements Debbie, Gaffney Jane, Wiesenfeld Lorne. (2015). Designing faculty development to support the evaluation of resident competency in the intrinsic CanMEDS roles: practical outcomes of an assessment of program director needs. BMC Medical Education 15:100. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0375-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Under the 'Challenges' theme, directors cited "Lack of time", worried reduced attention to Medical Expert competency deprived training, and noted about fifty of sixty PDs saw higher value placed on the Medical Expert role; on incentives one PD said "Financial incentives always work".

> "Approximately fifty of the sixty PDs reported the Faculty of Medicine culture and residents tend to place higher value on the Medical Expert role."
