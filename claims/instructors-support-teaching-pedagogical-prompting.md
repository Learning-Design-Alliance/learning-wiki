## Evidence

### Ruiwei Xiao 2025

Ruiwei Xiao, Xinying Hou, Runlong Ye, Majeed Kazemitabaar, Nicholas Diana, Michael Liut and John Stamper. (2025). Improving Student-AI Interaction Through Pedagogical Prompting: An Example in Computer Science Education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.19107](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.19107)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Formative survey of 36 instructors of early undergraduate CS courses, recruited via mailing lists with snowball sampling in November 2024; respondents came from 8 countries and mostly public universities.

> "More than 90% of instructors have implemented AI usage policies targeting popular tools like ChatGPT and Copilot, and nearly 80% of instructors allow different levels of AI usage."

### Ruiwei Xiao 2025 (2)

Ruiwei Xiao, Xinying Hou, Runlong Ye, Majeed Kazemitabaar, Nicholas Diana, Michael Liut and John Stamper. (2025). Improving Student-AI Interaction Through Pedagogical Prompting: An Example in Computer Science Education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.19107](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.19107)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same 36-instructor survey: reported concerns as printed, alongside the finding that only 5 instructors (14%) considered learning pedagogical prompting not at all important to novices.

> "instructors reported their primary concerns asfollows: students relying on AI tools without truly learning the material (50%), blindly copying solutions (19%), and engaging in academic dishonesty or cheating (14%)."
