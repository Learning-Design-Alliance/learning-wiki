Manuel Rausch 2023¶

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Kolmogorov–Smirnov tests on summary statistics computed per simulated participant across the 5000 resampled experiments; log transformation reduced the deviation rates to 28.24% and 30.06% respectively.

"We detected a significant deviation from the normal distributions in 47.54% of simulations for meta-d′/d′ and 51.46% for meta-d a/da"

Related Claims¶

  • T-testing raw meta-d′/d′ or meta-da/da yields a false positive rate below 5%; log transformation or the Mann–Whitney U-test restores an adequate rate — related
  • Summary-statistic measures of metacognitive accuracy (gamma, type 2 ROC, meta-d′, meta-da) maintain a false positive rate consistent with the nominal 5% alpha level in group comparisons — related
  • HMeta-d's false positive rate depends on study design and prior specification: independent group priors inflate it, priors on the group difference fix it, and default priors for continuous predictors yield a rate below 5%
  • Logistic mixed-model regression of confidence on accuracy shows dramatically inflated false positive rates when random slopes are omitted, but an adequate rate when random slopes are included — related
