### Julien-Pooya Weihs 2024

Julien-Pooya Weihs, Helge Drange, Vegard Gjerde. (2024). From Novice to Expert in Cloud Physics: a Network-Based Analysis of Learner Understanding. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.06479](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.06479)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional network analysis of hand-drawn concept maps comparing participant groupings by experience, discipline, and academic level. The authors report that "disciplinary experience is the most statistically significant independent variable by which to group our participants", contrasting with discipline or level.

> "According to our analysis, disciplinary experience is the most statistically significant independent variable by which to group our participants."
