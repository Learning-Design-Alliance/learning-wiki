`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The systematic review's study sample (267 studies identified, 37 eligible, 20 meeting WWC standards) reports that "The review did not identify which interventions were culturally relevant for CNMI", a stated limitation on generalizability of findings to the CNMI PSS.

> "None of the studies eligible for review was conducted in CNMI or in other Pacific islands; all eligible studies were conducted in the United States."
