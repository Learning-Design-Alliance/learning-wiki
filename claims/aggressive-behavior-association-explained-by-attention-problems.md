# The negative association between childhood aggressive behavior and academic attainment attenuates to non-significance once comorbid attention problems are accounted for

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Aggressive behavior at 1½ and 3 years was significantly associated with lower attainment before adjustment (−0.04 SD per SD at 1½ years), but intercepts at 1½, 3, 5 and 10 years were no longer significant after adjusting for attention problems. [→ Tamayo Martinez N 2021](#tamayo-martinez-n-2021)

## Evidence

### Tamayo Martinez N 2021

Tamayo Martinez N, Tiemeier H, Luijk MPCM, Law J, van der Ende J, Verhulst F, Jansen PW. (2021). Aggressive behavior, emotional, and attention problems across childhood and academic attainment at the end of primary school. Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00127-021-02039-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Objective-3 linear regression models in the Generation R cohort repeated with attention problem intercept and slope as additional covariates; the article reports the aggressive-behavior intercepts were "no longer significantly associated with academic attainment".

> "For aggressive behavior, the intercepts—whether set at 1½, 3, 5 or 10 years—were no longer significantly associated with academic attainment."

### Tamayo Martinez N 2021 (2)

Tamayo Martinez N, Tiemeier H, Luijk MPCM, Law J, van der Ende J, Verhulst F, Jansen PW. (2021). Aggressive behavior, emotional, and attention problems across childhood and academic attainment at the end of primary school. Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00127-021-02039-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Objective-1 regression with the growth-model intercept set at 1½ years; the article prints a coefficient of "− 0.04 SD" (95% CI − 0.08, − 0.01), a standardized regression coefficient rather than a printed effect size.

> "A 1 SD increase in the aggressive behavior score at 1½ years reduced the academic attainment score by − 0.04 SD (95% CI − 0.08, − 0.01), when taking into account the trajectory of the problems."
