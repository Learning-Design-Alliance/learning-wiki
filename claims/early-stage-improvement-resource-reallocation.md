## Evidence

### Thomaschewski M 2021

Thomaschewski M, Heldmann M, Uter J C, Varbelow D, Münte T F, Keck T. (2021). Changes in attentional resources during the acquisition of laparoscopic surgical skills. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zraa012

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, not a separately tested result; it rests on the reduced P300 to auditory targets alongside unchanged secondary-task accuracy and reaction times. This is the authors' stated reading of "reallocation of mental resources".

> "It seems reasonable to conclude that, in this early training stage, improvement is mainly due to reallocation of mental resources."
