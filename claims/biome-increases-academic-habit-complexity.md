## Evidence

### Wilton Mike 2021

Wilton Mike, Katz Daniel, Clairmont Anthony, Gonzalez-Nino Eduardo, Foltz Kathy R., Christoffersen Rolf E. (2021). Improving Academic Performance and Retention of First-Year Biology Students through a Scalable Peer Mentorship Program. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-02-0039

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Latent regression on Rasch-modeled responses to the 22-item academic habit complexity survey (2018–2019 cohorts, ~35–40% response rate, no significant demographic differences between BIOME and non-BIOME respondents). Table 3 reports BIOME at "0.29 (.14)", p = 0.019. The article reports a statistically significant difference with no standardized effect size.

> "The latent regression revealed a significant positive difference for the BIOME group in academic habit complexity of 0.3 logits; that is, BIOME participants showed a level of academic habit complexity roughly 5% higher than that of the comparison group at the end of the Fall quarter."
