# Experimental and control groups did not differ significantly on the listening pretest

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pretest listening comprehension scores showed no statistically significant difference between the control and experimental groups (t-cal -.048 < t-table 2.000, df 70). [→ Andi Tenri Ampa 2015](#andi-tenri-ampa-2015)

## Evidence

### Andi Tenri Ampa 2015

Andi Tenri Ampa. (2015). The Implementation of Interactive Multimedia Learning Materials in Teaching Listening Skills. English Language Teaching, 8(12). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n12p56

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest comparison in the same quasi-experiment: independent-samples t-test gave t-cal -.048 at df 70, p .962, with Levene's sig. 0.695 indicating homogeneous variances. Equivalence was supported by the printed test, not established as strict equivalence.

> "The results of statistical analysis in Tabel 1 present the mean scores of pretest of control and experimental groups (47.42 and 47.25). It indicates that the two groups are not significantly different."
