# Examinees spend the most review time on changes that do not affect score (R-R, W-W) and the least on W-R changes

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Estimated marginal mean revisiting time was longest for R-R changes (58.2 s), second longest for W-W (50.4 s), and lowest for R-W (46.4 s) and W-R (44.0 s), with all change types differing significantly from R-W. [→ Ouyang Wenli 2019](#ouyang-wenli-2019)

## Evidence

### Ouyang Wenli 2019

Ouyang Wenli, Harik Polina, Clauser Brian E., Paniagua Miguel A. (2019). Investigation of answer changes on the USMLE® Step 2 Clinical Knowledge examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1816-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear mixed-effects model of block four revisiting duration with change type and English proficiency as fixed effects, examinee as random term, adjusted for ability and item difficulty; effects of change type, ability, difficulty, and proficiency were all significant (all p < 0.001).

> "the estimated marginal mean time spent reconsidering item responses was longest for the R-R changes (58.2 s), second longest for the W-W changes (50.4 s), and lowest for the R-W (46.4 s) and W-R changes (44.0 s)."
