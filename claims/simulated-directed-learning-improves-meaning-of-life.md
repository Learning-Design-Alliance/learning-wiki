## Evidence

### Tsai Fu-Ju PhD 2019

Tsai Fu-Ju PhD, Hu Yih-Jin PhD, Chen Cheng-Yu PhD, Yeh Gwo-Liang PhD, Tseng Chie-Chien PhD, Chen Si-Chi PhD, Tusconi Massimo. (2019). Simulated directed-learning in life-education intervention on the meaning of life, positive beliefs, and well-being among nursing students: A Quasi-experimental study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000016330

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

GEE analysis of a quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test/post-post-test design with 54 experimental and 56 control third-year nursing students. The article reports "significant differences on meaning of life" at both time points; the β coefficients are unstandardized GEE estimates, so no effect size is printed.

> "The GEE analysis revealed that all of the nursing students in both groups had significant differences on meaning of life on the pre-post-test (β = 16.40, P < .001) and pre-post post-test (β = 25.94, P < .001)"

### Tsai Fu-Ju PhD 2019 (2)

Tsai Fu-Ju PhD, Hu Yih-Jin PhD, Chen Cheng-Yu PhD, Yeh Gwo-Liang PhD, Tseng Chie-Chien PhD, Chen Si-Chi PhD, Tusconi Massimo. (2019). Simulated directed-learning in life-education intervention on the meaning of life, positive beliefs, and well-being among nursing students: A Quasi-experimental study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000016330

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparative mean scores (experimental minus control) from Table 4 of the quasi-experiment, showing the experimental group moving from below to above the control group on meaning of life across the three measurement points.

> "In terms of meaning of life, the experimental group showed a comparative mean score on the pre-test of −11.84 (SD 3.20), post-test of 4.56 (SD 3.08), and post-post-test of 14.09 (SD 3.16)"
