## Evidence

### Poos 2017

Poos, J., van den Bosch, K. & Janssen, C.P. (2017). Battling bias: Effects of training and training context. Computers & Education, vol. 111, pp. 101-113. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.04.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same between-subjects experiment, the ANOVA on confirmation-bias in-context measurements of murder case two detected no significant Training or Context effects (equivalence was not tested). The authors suggest the rich game/story narratives may have made it too difficult to inhibit confirmatory thinking during these measurements.

> "Analysis revealed no signi ﬁcant main effects of Training ( F (1,73) ¼ 1.94, p ¼ 0.1 7), and no effect of Context ( F (1,73) ¼ 1.01, p ¼ 0.32). In addition, no signi ﬁcant interaction effect between Training and Context was found ( F (1,73) ¼ 0.16, p ¼ 0.69, h2 < 0.01)."
