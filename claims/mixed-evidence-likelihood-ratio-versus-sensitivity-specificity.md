## Evidence

### Whiting Penny F 2015

Whiting Penny F, Davenport Clare, Jameson Catherine, Burke Margaret, Sterne Jonathan A C, Hyde Chris, Ben-Shlomo Yoav. (2015). How well do health professionals interpret diagnostic information? A systematic review. BMJ Open. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4521525/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of six studies assessing LR formats for post-test probability estimation. The review reports two studies found less correct responses with LRs compared with sensitivity and specificity presented numerically, two found more correct responses with LRs, and one found similarly poor estimation for both formats.

> "Two studies demonstrated less correct responses for post-test probability estimation with LRs (described in words in one and numerical in the other) compared with sensitivity and specificity presented numerically."
