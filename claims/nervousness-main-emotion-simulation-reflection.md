Wono, A. L., Bell, A., & Bourne, E. (2026). Student Reflections in Simulated Speech and Language Therapy Placements Using Telehealth and In‐Person Formats: A Qualitative Study. *International Journal of Language & Communication Disorders, 61*(2), e70221. [doi:10.1111/1460-6984.70221](https://doi.org/10.1111/1460-6984.70221)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative finding from reflexive thematic analysis of discussion posts in both simulation formats, within the emotions theme (subthemes: client emotions, own personal emotions, empathy). Students linked nervousness to speaking too quickly and over-relying on notes. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The main emotion identified by students was nervousness, with students acknowledging that nerves detrimentally influenced their interactions with the client."
