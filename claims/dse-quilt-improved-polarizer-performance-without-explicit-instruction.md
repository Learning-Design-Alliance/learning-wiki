# Both undergraduates and graduates improved significantly on DSE polarizer questions after the DSE QuILT, which never discussed polarizers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Undergraduate performance on DSE polarizer questions rose from 57% to 88% after the DSE QuILT (p<0.001, Cohen's d=1.09), and graduate performance rose from 60% to 75% (p=0.018, d=0.47), despite the tutorial not addressing polarizers. [→ Alexandru Maries 2017](#alexandru-maries-2017)

## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries 2017

Alexandru Maries, Ryan Sayer, and Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Effectiveness of interactive tutorials in promoting “which-path” information reasoning in advanced quantum mechanics. Physical Review Physics Education Research 13, 020115. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.13.020115

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Simple t tests comparing performance before and after the DSE QuILT for 46 undergraduates and 59 graduate students, graded with the DSE polarizer rubric. The article notes "The effect is large for undergraduate students, but only medium for graduate students."

> "UG-before QuILT 46 57% 35% UG-after QuILT 46 88% 20% UG p value <0.001 UG effect size 1.09 GS-before QuILT 59 60% 35% GS-after QuILT 59 75% 28% GS p value 0.018 GS effect size 0.47"
