## Evidence

### Causby RS 2017

Causby RS, McDonnell MN, Reed L, Fryer CE, Hillier SL. (2017). A qualitative evaluation of scalpel skill teaching of podiatry students. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13047-017-0202-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative study of semi-structured interviews with 16 educators from eight university podiatry programs in Australia and New Zealand, analysed with conventional content analysis. The article reports that approaches "appear to be consistent between university programs in Australia and New Zealand". No effect size is printed.

> "Approaches to teaching manual clinical skills, in particular scalpel skills, appear to be consistent between university programs in Australia and New Zealand in utilising didactic-style content, demonstration, physical practice on inanimate objects and real skin, and often the use of supplementary audio-visual material."
