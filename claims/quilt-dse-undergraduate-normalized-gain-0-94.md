## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Undergraduate students in an upper-level quantum mechanics course improved from an average pre-test score of 23% to an average post-test score of 95% (p<0.001) with a normalized gain of 0.94 after working through the DSE QuILT. [→ Sayer 2015](#sayer-2015)

## Evidence

### Sayer 2015

Sayer, R., Maries, A., & Singh, C. (2015). Developing and evaluating a tutorial on the double-slit experiment. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.070](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.070)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre/post evaluation of the QuILT in an upper-level quantum mechanics course (N=46). Table III reports U pre 23%, post 95%, p-value <0.001, normalized gain 0.94, printed as "normalized gains". Improvement is large but the design has no control group.
