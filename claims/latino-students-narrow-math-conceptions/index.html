Evidence¶

Maura Varley Gutiérrez 2011¶

Maura Varley Gutiérrez, Craig Willey, & Lena L. Khisty. (2011). (In)equitable Schooling and Mathematics of Marginalized Students: Through the Voices of Urban Latinas/os. Journal of Urban Mathematics Education, 4(2), 26–43. http://education.gsu.edu/JUME

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Critical ethnographic analysis of students' comments across three years of after-school mathematics clubs at two urban sites. Students rarely broke out of the 'mathematics-is-numbers' box; one student described seeing 'a whole bunch of numbers, and problems go around me, and I get dizzy.'

"When students were asked to consider what it takes to be “good at mathematics,” they largely gravitated toward external measures of mathematical success, such as grades or test scores."
