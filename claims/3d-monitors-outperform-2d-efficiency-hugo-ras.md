## Evidence

### Østdal TB 2024

Østdal TB, Tang DHY, Olsen RG, Olsen LM, Konge L, Bjerrum F. (2024). 3D monitors improve performance on the HUGO™ RAS system: a randomised trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11615040/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised trial of 31 medical students (16 3D, 15 2D) trained on the Medtronic Hugo RAS simulator over two sessions; mixed two-way repeated-measures ANOVA on LOG-transformed data. The 3D group "consistently outperformed the control group (2D)" on both efficiency parameters, e.g. Exercise 1 main effect of group F(1,29) = 67.77, p < 0.001.

> "For the efficiency parameters, Time to complete exercise and Economy of motion (p < 0.001) (p = < 0.001–0.003), we found that participants got better with more attempts and that the intervention group (3D) consistently outperformed the control group (2D)"
