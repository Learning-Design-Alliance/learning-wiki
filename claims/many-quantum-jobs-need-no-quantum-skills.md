`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Closed-response survey of 57 companies; important skills defined by consensus as those chosen by more than 50% of respondents for a job. Fig. 2 and Table I list important quantum and non-quantum skills per job role. The authors conclude the industry "can obtain personnel from the cur- rent non-quantum workforce."
