## Evidence

### Liu Zhipeng 2024

Liu Zhipeng, Na Guanqiong, Liu Lihua, Tian Sicong, Shan Yujuan. (2024). Investigation and implementation of case-based learning in the sino-foreign joint program of preventive medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06372-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated instructor evaluation across the five CBL cases, reported in the Results with Fig. 1A. The average rose from 7.846 to 8.541 points as cases progressed. No effect size is printed for this trend. The article notes "starting with the fourth case class, there was a deceleration in the rate of improvement of students' results."

> "the average score of students in the initial case was 7.846 points. As the CBL courses progress, students' scores gradually rose, and the average score of students in the final case was 8.541 points"

### Liu Zhipeng 2024 (2)

Liu Zhipeng, Na Guanqiong, Liu Lihua, Tian Sicong, Shan Yujuan. (2024). Investigation and implementation of case-based learning in the sino-foreign joint program of preventive medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06372-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dimension-level analysis of the instructor evaluation form, reported in the Results with Fig. 1B. Each dimension score improved significantly relative to the first case (P < 0.05); only the p-value threshold is printed, no effect size. The instructor form assessed participation, inquiry skills, organizational abilities, communication and teamwork, scored out of 10 points.

> "in comparison to the initial case study, the students' scores in each dimension exhibited substantial improvement with statistical significance (Fig. 1B, P < 0.05)"
