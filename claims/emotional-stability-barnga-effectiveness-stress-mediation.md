## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hofhuis 2020

Hofhuis, J., Schilderman, M. F., & Verdooren, A. (2020). Multicultural personality and effectiveness in an intercultural training simulation: The role of stress and pro‐active communication. International Journal of Psychology, 55(5), 812–821. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijop.12647

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model with latent growth curves among 116 workshop participants; no direct effect of emotional stability was found, but the indirect effect through stress on self-reported effectiveness was significant, "b* = .11; SE = .05; p = .033".

> "emotional stability has a negative effect on stress, which in turn has a negative effect on the intercept of self‐reported effectiveness. This indirect effect through stress is significant (b* = .11; SE = .05; p = .033)"

### Hofhuis 2020 (2)

Hofhuis, J., Schilderman, M. F., & Verdooren, A. (2020). Multicultural personality and effectiveness in an intercultural training simulation: The role of stress and pro‐active communication. International Journal of Psychology, 55(5), 812–821. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijop.12647

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same LGCM of 116 Barnga players, emotional stability indirectly related to the peer-evaluated effectiveness intercept through stress, with "b* = .14; SE = .05; p = .005", confirming hypothesis 1b and the stress mediation (H3).

> "Again, an indirect effect is found through stress (b* = .14; SE = .05; p = .005). This confirms hypothesis 1b"
