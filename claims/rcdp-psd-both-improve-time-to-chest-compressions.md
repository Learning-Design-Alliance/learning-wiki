## Evidence

### Coelho Laila Pinto et al 2024

Coelho Laila Pinto et al. (2024). Rapid cycle deliberate practice versus postsimulation debriefing in pediatric cardiopulmonary resuscitation training: a randomized controlled study. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2024AO0825

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study of 16 groups of pediatric residents; video-recorded scenarios analyzed by blinded evaluators. The within-group decrease in time to initiation of chest compressions was significant in both arms, e.g. "from 36.38 seconds during scenario 1 to 23.1 seconds during scenario 3 in the RCDP Group (p=0.002)". No effect size printed.

> "The time to initiation of chest compressions decreased from 36.38 seconds during scenario 1 to 23.1 seconds during scenario 3 in the RCDP Group (p=0.002) and from 31.4 seconds during scenario 1 to 19.1 seconds during scenario 3 in the PSD Group (p=0.027)."
