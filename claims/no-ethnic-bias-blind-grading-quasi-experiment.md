## Evidence

### Bischoff Carina Saxlund 2021

Bischoff Carina Saxlund, Ejrnæs Anders, Rubin Olivier. (2021). A quasi-experimental study of ethnic and gender bias in university grading. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254422

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OLS regression (Model 3) on the same 896-exam quasi-experiment, with an ethnic*open interaction term (β6 = 0.35, p = 0.64). The article reports the high p-value means the slight positive bias "is far from significant."

> "The coefficient for the interaction term β5b (ethnic*open) would indicate a slight positive bias toward ethnic minorities in the sample, but the high p-value suggests that it is far from significant."
