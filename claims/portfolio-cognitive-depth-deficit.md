### Turaev 2026

Turaev, S., Aldabet, S., John, M., Musthafa, N., Awad, M., Zaki, N., & Shuaib, K. (2026). Measuring Curriculum–Labor Market Alignment at the Scale of a Program Portfolio. Preprint. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.7122139](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7122139)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Per-domain Bloom-depth differential analysis of the 1,922 CLO supply against the demand corpus (Figure 7), with printed effect size "Cliff’s δ=−0.45". The deficit is significant in nine of eleven domains after false-discovery-rate control, and shallowest to the point of non-significance in Web and Mobile Development and Emerging Technologies.

> "Across the portfolio the curriculum is pitched at a mean Bloom level of 2.68 against the market’s 3.63, a differential of −0.95 of a level (95% CI [−1.00,−0.89] ; Cliff’s δ=−0.45 ), so the curriculum sits roughly a full cognitive level below market demand."
