### Paul W. Irving and Eleanor C. Sayre 2015

Paul W. Irving and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2015). Becoming a Physicist: The Roles of Research, Mindsets, and Milestones in upper-division student perceptions. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020120)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Longitudinal case analysis of three physics majors who undertook undergraduate research between the two interview sets. The article reports "Their perceptions have since changed," and Will's second interview reorients his threshold from knowledge mastery toward research as what makes a good physicist. Qualitative finding; no effect size printed.

> "In the time between the ﬁrst and second interviews, Charlie, Will, and Oliver (all physics majors) have undergraduate research experiences. Their perceptions have since changed."
