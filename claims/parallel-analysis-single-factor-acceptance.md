## Evidence

### Zhorabekova A 2026

Zhorabekova A, Duisenova M, Kurmasheva M and Yesnazar A (2026). Acceptance of AI-gamified adaptive learning in EFL: bifactor evidence for essential unidimensionality and the limits of subscale scoring. Front. Educ. 11:1929123. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1929123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis with Horn's parallel analysis on the randomly split exploratory half (n = 200; KMO = .935). The article reports the second eigenvalue "fell below the corresponding parallel value (1.41)" and the one-factor solution accounted for 66.4% of variance.

> "Horn's parallel analysis retained a single factor: only the first observed eigenvalue (8.63) exceeded its parallel-analysis 95th-percentile value (1.55), whereas the second observed eigenvalue (1.19) fell below the corresponding parallel value (1.41)."
