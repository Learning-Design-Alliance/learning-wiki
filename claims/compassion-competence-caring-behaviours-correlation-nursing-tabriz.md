Pearson correlation analysis in a cross-sectional survey of 200 undergraduate nursing students at Tabriz (September–December 2023) found a "positive significant correlation" between compassion competence and caring behaviours, r = 0.70, p < 0.001.

> "The results showed a positive significant correlation between the mean total score of compassion competence and the mean total score of caring behaviours (r = 0.70, p < 0.001)"
