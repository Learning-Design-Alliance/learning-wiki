## Evidence

### Williams 2014

Williams, Brett; Fellows, Holly; Eastwood, Kathryn, and Wallis, Jaime. (2014). Peer teaching experiences of final year paramedic students: 2011–2012. Journal of Peer Learning, 7, 81-91. http://ro.uow.edu.au/ajpl/vol7/iss1/7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of final clinical examination grades between 41 peer teachers and a random selection of 41 final year students not involved in the PAL program, analysed with an independent samples t-test. The article reports "higher clinical marks" in both years with p < .001; no effect size is printed.

> "In both 2011 and 2012, students who participated in the PAL project as peer teachers obtained higher clinical marks on their final clinical examination than their non-PAL counterparts (2011 76.5% vs. 71.0%, p < .001, and 2012, 75.2% vs. 72.7%, p < .001)."
