## Evidence

### Mascio R 2026

Mascio R, Lynch S, Phillips J, Best M. (2026). Predictors of nurses’ spiritual care competence: A replication study with Australian palliative care nurses. Palliative & Supportive Care. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1478951526102119

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory supplementary Mann–Whitney comparisons of nurse characteristics between the palliative (n = 21) and general (n = 66) samples; the article prints Z statistics and p-values only, with no effect sizes. Training adequacy, nursing experience, and sex did not differ significantly.

> "This table also shows that frequency of spiritual care (Z = 3.12, p < 0.001), personal spirituality (Z = 3.95, p < 0.001), confidence (Z = 4.16, p < 0.001), and comfort (Z = 4.13, p < 0.001) were significantly higher in the palliative care sample than in the general sample."
