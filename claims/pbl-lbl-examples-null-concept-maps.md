## Evidence

### Hung Chia-Hui 2015

Hung Chia-Hui, Lin Chen-Yung. (2015). Using concept mapping to evaluate knowledge structure in problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0496-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test of the examples scoring parameter in the same 52-student quasi-experiment (PBL n = 26, LBL n = 26). No statistically significant difference was detected; equivalence was not tested. The table reports PBL M = 8.9, LBL M = 6.0 descriptively.

> "Although the mean of “examples” was higher in the PBL group, the difference was not statistically significant (t (50) = 1.14, p =0.26)."
