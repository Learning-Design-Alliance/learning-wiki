## Evidence

### Siva Raja Sindiramutty 2026

Siva Raja Sindiramutty. (2026). A Comparative Study on the Impact of Traditional Learning and Interactive Learning on Students' Academic Performance and Emotional Well-Being. Taylor's University. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

PANAS-based affect scale administered after the intervention in the randomized two-group experiment. Positive emotion in the interactive group (M = 4.00) exceeded the traditional group (M = 2.40); the study reports "t(48) = 5.66, p < .001, d = 1.60" and a 66.67% increase.

> "Positive emotional states , such as enjoyment and enthusiasm, were higher in the interactive group ( M = 4.00 ) compared to the traditional group ( M = 2.40 ), t(48) = 5.66, p < .001, d = 1.60 , representing a 66.67% increase"

### Siva Raja Sindiramutty 2026 (2)

Siva Raja Sindiramutty. (2026). A Comparative Study on the Impact of Traditional Learning and Interactive Learning on Students' Academic Performance and Emotional Well-Being. Taylor's University. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Same PANAS-based affect scale, negative-affect subscale. The interactive group reported lower negative emotion (M = 2.56) than the traditional group (M = 4.00), with "t(48) = -4.88, p < .001, d = -1.38"; the authors suggest well-designed digital environments reduce performance pressure via safe trial-and-error learning.

> "negative emotions  such as frustration, anxiety, and confusion were significantly lower in the interactive group (M = 2.56 ) than in the traditional group ( M = 4.00), t(48) = -4.88, p < .001, d = -1.38, a 36.00% reduction"

### Siva Raja Sindiramutty 2026 (3)

Siva Raja Sindiramutty. (2026). A Comparative Study on the Impact of Traditional Learning and Interactive Learning on Students' Academic Performance and Emotional Well-Being. Taylor's University. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.15335)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

> "Flow state, reflecting immersion and psychological engagement, was also higher in the interactive group ( M = 4.00) than in the traditional group ( M = 2.44 ), t(48) = 5.62, p < .001, d = 1.59"
