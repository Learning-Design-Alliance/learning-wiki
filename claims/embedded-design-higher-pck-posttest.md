# Pre-service teachers in an embedded-design inclusive education course recorded significantly higher pedagogical content knowledge posttest scores than peers in an applied-experience condition

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The embedded design condition outperformed the applied experience condition on the cumulative mastery posttest (effect size 1.35, p < .0001). [→ Lancaster 2019](#lancaster-2019)

## Evidence

### Lancaster 2019

Lancaster, J., & Bain, A. (2019). Designing University Courses to Improve Pre-Service Teachers' Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Evidence-Based Inclusive Practice. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 44(2). https://ro.ecu.edu.au/ajte/vol44/iss2/4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison group study of 40 volunteer second-year pre-service teachers matched on demographics and pretest across two campuses. Posttest means were 27.21 (SD 4.04) for embedded design versus 18.42 (SD 6.48) for applied experience; the article reports "effect size = 1.35".

> "The two-factor analysis of variance revealed statistically significant differences between the post-tests where higher scores were recorded by the pre-service teachers in the embedded design condition (F) 1.36 = 25.12, p= <.0001), effect size = 1.35."
