Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke 2025¶

Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke. (2025). Pavlovian to instrumental transfer of control over fight or flight decisions. npj Science of Learning 10:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00331-4

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Study 3 manipulation-check analysis (n=82, one missing value) of effectiveness ratings on a bipolar scale from 1 (fight) to 5 (flight); the interaction was significant with a large printed effect size, partial eta squared ηp2 = .226 (ηp² = .226). Reversal participants judged the alternative defensive action as more effective than the standard-instruction group.

"The main effects were not signi ﬁcant ( Fs < 1), but the interaction effect was signi ﬁcant, F(1, 80) = 23.29, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = .226."
