## Evidence

### Fan Na 2025

Fan Na, Yin Yan-Ling, Zhang Sai-Na, Yan Rui, Han Shi-Fan. (2025). Developing a Competency Model for Mobile Nurses in Chinese Tertiary Hospitals: A Delphi-AHP Approach. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/5561680

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

First-round Delphi consultation with the expert panel refined the preliminary model of 3 primary, 12 secondary, and 49 tertiary indicators. The article reports "two were eliminated due to conceptual redundancy (e.g., \"management ability,\" mean: 3.13, CV: 0.38)" and that 7 of 49 tertiary indicators were removed, including "medical informatics" (mean: 3.20).

> "Among the 12 secondary indicators, two were eliminated due to conceptual redundancy (e.g., "management ability," mean: 3.13, CV: 0.38) or low practical applicability, while others were refined; for instance, "teaching ability" was expanded to "teaching and research ability""
