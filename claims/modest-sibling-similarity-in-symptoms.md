### Demange Perline A. 2026

Descriptive analysis in the children-of-siblings samples. Intraclass correlations were higher in pregnancy than at child age 8 (e.g. 0.13 vs. 0.07 for anxiety-depression), "suggesting reduced familial clustering over time."

> "The intraclass correlations ranges from 0.07 to 0.15, reflecting modest sibling similarity: only 7–15% of the variation in symptoms is shared between siblings."
