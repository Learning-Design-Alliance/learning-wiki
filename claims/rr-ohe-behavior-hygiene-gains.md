## Evidence

### Haleem Abdul 2016

Haleem Abdul, Khan Muhammad Khalil, Sufia Shamta, Chaudhry Saima, Siddiqui Muhammad Irfanullah, Khan Ayyaz Ali. (2016). The role of repetition and reinforcement in school-based oral health education-a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-2676-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GEE analysis of cluster randomized controlled trial data comparing oral health behavior sustainability between RR-OHE and one-time OHE, with baseline as reference. RR-OHE effect was about 100% higher (OR: 2.22, CI: 2.08–2.37).

> "the sustainability of the effect of RR-OHE on the OHB of the study participants over six-month period was about 100 % higher (OR:2.22, CI: 2.08–2.37) than that of the effect produced by one-time OHE (OR: 1.18, CI:1.11–1.25)"

### Haleem Abdul 2016 (2)

Haleem Abdul, Khan Muhammad Khalil, Sufia Shamta, Chaudhry Saima, Siddiqui Muhammad Irfanullah, Khan Ayyaz Ali. (2016). The role of repetition and reinforcement in school-based oral health education-a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-2676-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Oral hygiene status measured by OHS-Composite index showed sustained improvement after RR-OHE, remaining 37% above baseline at six months and 49% above baseline at one year.

> "the adolescents' OHS-Composite index score, as a result of RR-OHE, remained 37 % and 49 % higher than the respective score at baseline over the period of six-month and 1 year respectively"
