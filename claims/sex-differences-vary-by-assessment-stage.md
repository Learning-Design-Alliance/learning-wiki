BMC Medicine 11:242. https://doi.org/10.1186/1741-7015-11-242

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sex-comparison analysis of the UCLMS Cohort Study (288 male, 441 female students), reported in the Results sex and ethnicity subsection with details in Additional file 2. The article reports stage-specific reversals: males "performed better at BMS examinations, but then performed less well on clinical assessments"; no effect sizes are printed for these contrasts.

> "Males underperformed somewhat at GCSE, and taking that into account, did slightly better at A-level. Males performed better at BMS examinations, but then performed less well on clinical assessments, while once again performing better at MRCP(UK) Part 1."
