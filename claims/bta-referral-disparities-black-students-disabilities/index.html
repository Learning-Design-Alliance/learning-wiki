DePaoli 2025¶

DePaoli, J. L., & Loewe, S. B. (2025). Keeping schools safe? The research on behavioral threat assessments. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/808.437

q2 · i? · review · r2

Descriptive analysis of 1,865 threat assessment cases in statewide Virginia implementation found no differences in disciplinary outcomes by gender or race, but referral disparities: "male students, Black students, and students with disabilities were more likely initially to receive a threat assessment referral."

"However, the authors did find that male students, Black students, and students with disabilities were more likely initially to receive a threat assessment referral compared with their peers."

DePaoli 2025 (2)¶

DePaoli, J. L., & Loewe, S. B. (2025). Keeping schools safe? The research on behavioral threat assessments. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/808.437

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's synthesis of Florida outcome studies (district data from 90% of districts, 2021–22) states that "Black students were disproportionately referred for BTAs, as were students with disabilities." No effect sizes are printed.

"Black students were disproportionately referred for BTAs, as were students with disabilities."
