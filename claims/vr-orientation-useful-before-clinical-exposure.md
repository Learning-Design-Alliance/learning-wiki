## Evidence

### Nabeela C 2025

Nabeela C, Shykhon M, Milward M, Yonel Z. (2025). The use of 360-videos to bridge the gap between pre-clinical and clinical dental teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06946-w

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group finding from the theme of familiarity with the clinical environment: students said VR orientation "gave an idea of the layout of the clinic and where to find instruments or materials," but those already exposed to clinics felt it did not offer a great deal extra.

> "Where student hadn’t already been exposed to clinics, they felt the use of the VR headsets would be very useful as it gave an idea of the layout of the clinic and where to find instruments or materials."
