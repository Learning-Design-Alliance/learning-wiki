## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Some misconceptions increased after traditional instruction, e.g., 'Low pressure is formed where temperature is high' rose from 15.6% to 35.6% in the control group while falling from 35.6% to 11.1% experimentally. [→ Akbaş 2011](#akbas-2011)

## Evidence

### Akbaş 2011

Akbaş, Y., & Gençtürk, E. (2011). The Effect of Conceptual Change Approach to Eliminate 9th Grade High School Students' Misconceptions about Air Pressure. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 11(4), 2217-2222. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ962696

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-level pre/post percentages show traditional instruction was associated with an increase in this misconception while conceptual change instruction reduced it.

> "misconception "Low pressure is formed where temperature is high" increased 15,6% to 35,6% in post test. The same misconception decreased from 35,6% to 11,1% in experiment group."
