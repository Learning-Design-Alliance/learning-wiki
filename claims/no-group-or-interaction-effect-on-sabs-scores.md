Mixed repeated-measures ANOVA in the randomized controlled trial (three groups, n = 17 each) tested the SABS change over time; both the group effect and the group × time interaction were "not significant", with no effect size printed. Equivalence between methods was not formally tested.

> "However, the group effect (F = 0.836, p = 0.440) and the group × time interactio n (F = 0.304, p = 0.739) were not significant."
