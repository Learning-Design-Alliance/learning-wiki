## Evidence

### Agusala Vijay 2018

Agusala Vijay, Vij Priyanka, Agusala Veena, Dasari Vivekanand, Kola Bhargavi. (2018). Can interactive parental education impact health care utilization in pediatric asthma: A study in rural Texas. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060518773621

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pre/post survey of 102 rural pediatric asthma patients and caregivers before and after an interactive education program; ED (χ2 = 25.58, P < 0.001), hospitalization (χ2 = 6.20, P = 0.014), and absence (χ2 = 15.56, P < 0.001) reductions were statistically significant; no standardized effect size printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect). Caregiver reports were cross-checked against county hospital ICD-9 records.
