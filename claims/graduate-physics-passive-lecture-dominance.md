Kevin Coldren, Nkodia Ngondala, Audrey Claar, Mike Verostek, and Diana Sachmpazidi. (2026). Student Perspectives on Traditional Pedagogy Used in Graduate Physics Coursework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.10955](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.10955)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of five semi-structured interviews with graduate students in one R1 physics Ph.D. program. All five students described traditional lecture instruction in which instructors write on the board while "students passively receive this information." The only deviation noted was two discussion-based astrophysics electives.

> "all five of the interviewed students responded that both required and elective classes were taught in a traditional lecture model with minimal deviations. Instructors would write information on the board with accompanying verbal explanations, while students passively receive this information."
