### Yıldız 2021

Yıldız, M. (2021). Language choices of language teachers and learners: Meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 17(Special Issue 1), 472-492. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1285162

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis finding on language context and content factors. The article quotes a teacher in Bilgin (2016): "I use Turkish most while teaching grammar". In multilingual contexts where students do not share the same native language, the article states it seems obligatory to use the target language. Qualitative evidence; no effect size.

> "Similarly, monolingual classes, compared to multilingual classes, and grammar lessons also provide more opportunities for the use of L1."
