## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Comments reflecting praise were accompanied by higher quantitative ratings, with a standardized regression coefficient of β = 0.46 (P < .001). [→ Tekian A 2019](#tekian-a-2019)

## Evidence

### Tekian A 2019

Tekian A, Park YS, Tilton S, Prunty PF, Abasolo E, Zar F, Cook DA. (2019). Competencies and Feedback on Internal Medicine Residents' End-of-Rotation Assessments Over Time: Qualitative and Quantitative Analyses. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000002821

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects regression associating coded comment orientation with average quantitative ratings across all competencies and years for 828 comments on one cohort; the coefficient was "moderately positive (β = 0.46, P < .001)". Associations by year and competency were of similar magnitude and significance.

> "the standardized regression coefficient (which can be interpreted analogous to a correlation coefficient) was moderately positive (β = 0.46, P < .001). In other words, comments that reflected praise were accompanied by higher quantitative ratings."
