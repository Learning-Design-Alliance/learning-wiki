## Evidence

### Puppart 2025

Puppart, B., & Aru, J. (2025). A Brief AI Literacy Intervention Does Not Significantly Reduce Over-Reliance and Increases Under-Reliance on ChatGPT: A Randomized Study. University of Tartu. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.10556](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.10556)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Behavioral task in a randomized study in which 36 Estonian 12th-graders solved eight Kangaroo math puzzles with ChatGPT-4o advice, incorrect in four puzzles. The article reports "Over-reliance occurred in 52.1% (SD = 28.90) of trials" and under-reliance of 28.5%; most participants (94.4%) accepted at least one incorrect recommendation.

> "Over-reliance occurred in 52.1% (SD = 28.90) of trials with incorrect recommendations, and under-reliance in 28.5% (SD = 24.00) of trials with correct recommendations."
