Intention-to-treat sensitivity analysis with 33 matched pairs after reperforming PSM. The article reports "mean difference 5.27, 95% CI 1.52-9.02; P=.01; Cohen d=0.70", which the authors interpret as tentative evidence that motivational bias did not inflate the effect.

> "revealing that group A’s MHT total scores remained significantly higher than those of group B (mean difference 5.27, 95% CI 1.52-9.02; P=.01; Cohen d=0.70)"
