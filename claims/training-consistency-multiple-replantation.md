## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In multiple fingertip replantation the control group had longer operative times (151.4 vs 132.7 min, P < 0.001), lower survival (54.1% vs 75.6%, P = 0.018) and higher arterial insufficiency (48.6% vs 10.5%, P < 0.001), venous congestion (40.5% vs 18.6%, P = 0.010) and pulp atrophy (51.4% vs 25.6%, P = 0.005). ### He Xuecheng MD 2025

He Xuecheng MD, Gao Tao MD, Tang Jianfei MD, Jiang Zengxin MD, PhD, Zhang Zeng MD, PhD, Lin E. MD, Yuan Hengfeng MD, PhD. (2025). Assessment of simulation training efficacy in improving microsurgical skills: a retrospective analysis. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000002259

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Stratified analysis of 22 trained patients with 47 digits and 18 control patients with 37 digits (Table 3). "The trained group maintained consistent survival rates, operative times, and complication rates regardless of whether a single or multiple fingertips were replanted"; control survival fell from 75.6% to 54.1% (P = 0.018).

> "The trained group maintained consistent survival rates, operative times, and complication rates regardless of whether a single or multiple fingertips were replanted. The control group showed significant variability."
