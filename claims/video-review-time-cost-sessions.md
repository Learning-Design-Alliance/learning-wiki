### Robbrecht M 2025

Robbrecht M, Van Winckel M, Mulder A, Embo M. (2025). What is the learning effect of video review in postgraduate medical education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06515-7

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's secondary outcome analysis of costs found "substantiate time effort" across five studies, with sessions ranging from 16 to 90 minutes. Only one study (Son et al.) described monetary costs, specifying $1500 for recording equipment.

> "Only five studies gave an indication of the temporal cost by describing the duration of review sessions, which showed substantiate time effort: 30–45 min [35], 16–90 min with a mean of 44 min [29], approximately 45 min [30], 60–90 min [10], and 90 min [33]."
