Gyeong-Geon Lee and Xiaoming Zhai. (2025). Realizing Visual Question Answering for Education: GPT-4V as a Multimodal AI. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/s11528-024-01035-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11528-024-01035-z)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Demonstration task in Chapter III: five student drawings from the Test About Particles in a Gas were scored against structure (particulate-continuous-other) and distribution (expanded-concentrated-other) criteria. The article reports unmatched cases involved anthropomorphic or artistic depictions, and that GPT-4V said such drawings might reveal common misconceptions or creative interpretations that could be addressed or encouraged in further instruction.

> "GPT-4V assessed most aspect correctly - 60% accurate for the structure aspect and 80% accurate for distribution aspect, in comparison with human coders' decision."
