## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Discussions of revised papers contained significantly more analysis units than discussions of unrevised papers (P < .001, Cohen's d = 1.08). [→ Koops Willem JM 2012](#koops-willem-jm-2012)

## Evidence

### Koops Willem JM 2012

Koops Willem JM, van der Vleuten Cees PM, de Leng Bas A, Snoeckx Luc HEH. (2012). Computer supported collaborative learning in a clerkship: an exploratory study on the relation of discussion activity and revision of critical appraisal papers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-79

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory observational comparison of blinded content-analysed postings from 47 sixth-year students in 16 groups; 1028 units in revised-paper discussions versus 554 in unrevised, Mann–Whitney U test P < .001, effect size d = 1.08. The study was "an exploratory study" of voluntary participants.

> "In the discussions of revised papers (n = 24), the number of analysis units was almost twice as high as in the unrevised paper discussions (n = 23) (P < .001)."
