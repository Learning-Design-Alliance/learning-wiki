## Evidence

### Tribello 2024

Tribello, G. A., et al. (2024). PLUMED Tutorials: a collaborative, community-driven learning ecosystem. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.03595](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.03595)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

A single-case experience report from the 2023 PLUMED school using a flipped format: online Masterclass completion before the meeting, then collaborative projects during it. The authors report they "were amazed by what the students were able to achieve within the short time available during the meeting." No outcome measures are reported.

> "at the recent 2023 in-person PLUMED school, students were asked to complete some of the PLUMED Masterclass prior to the meeting. Students could then spend the time working together on short research projects and discussing what they had learned"
