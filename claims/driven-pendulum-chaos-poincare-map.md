### H. Hauptfleisch 2009

H. Hauptfleisch, T. Gasenzer, K. Meier, O. Nachtmann, and J. Schemmel. (2009). A computer controlled pendulum with position readout. arXiv:0905.2015v1 [physics.pop-ph]. https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.2015 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0905.2015](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0905.2015)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Experimental demonstration of chaotic motion of the driven pendulum (Fig. 8) contrasted with a nonlinear but periodic oscillation (Fig. 7). The article states "A chaotic oscillation can only be reached if the pendulum turns over the top position at least once"; near the top, dependence on initial conditions makes long-term prediction practically impossible.

> "A chaotic oscillation can only be reached if the pendulum turns over the top position at least once."
