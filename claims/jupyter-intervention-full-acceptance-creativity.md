## Evidence

### Jozef Hanˇc 2020

Jozef Hanˇc, Peter ˇStrauch, Eva Paˇnkov´a and Martina Hanˇcov´a. (2020). Teachers' perception of Jupyter and R Shiny as digital tools for open education and science. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.11262](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.11262)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Second phase of the qualitative longitudinal case study (June 2017-June 2020) with three remaining Ph.D. participants. The authors report that after exchanging the point-and-click environment for Jupyter and continual advising, "we saw the full acceptance of data science tools which led also to creativity of participants."

> "from the point-and-click environment (R Commander) to interactive multimedia documents (Jupyter Notebooks), and the following continual advising, watching progress of our participants, we saw the full acceptance of data science tools which led also to creativity of participants."
