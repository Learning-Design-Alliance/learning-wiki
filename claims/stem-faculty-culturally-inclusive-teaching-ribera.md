`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Ribera et al. (2018)'s analysis of a 2017 FSSE topical module administered at 30 colleges measuring self-reported culturally inclusive teaching. Inside STEM, Black and Latiné faculty effects "neither of these latter effects achieved statistical significance," with each group only ~2% of the 811 STEM faculty participants.

> "However, inside STEM, only Asian and multi-racial identities were also significantly associated with greater self-reported use of these practices. Black and African American faculty self-reported using culturally inclusive teaching practices at higher rates than White faculty, while Latiné faculty self-reported using these practices at lower rates than White faculty, but neither of these latter effects achieved statistical significance, perhaps because of the extremely low numbers of Black and Latiné faculty in the sample (each constituting only ~ 2% out of the 811 STEM faculty participants; Ribera et al., 2018)."
