Qualitative reflection on the evolutionary development of the task: problems "became apparent when the storyboards were shared with peers", exposing rigidity arising from technological attempts to assist learners with translating natural language into formal notation.

> "These problems became apparent when the storyboards were shared with peers and presented at seminars. The interface design embodied the anticipated learning scenarios in a rather rigid manner and so lacked the flexibility to accommodate much variation."
