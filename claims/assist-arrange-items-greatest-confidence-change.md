### Sturgiss Elizabeth 2017

Sturgiss Elizabeth, Haesler Emily, Elmitt Nicholas, van Weel Chris, Douglas Kirsty. (2017). Increasing general practitioners' confidence and self-efficacy in managing obesity: a mixed methods study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014314

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive pre/post Likert survey results presented in figure 3 of the pilot study, covering the Assist and Arrange phases of the 5As framework; median scores improved across all questions. The authors interpret in discussion that the Assist items "showed the greatest change in GP confidence".

> "Figure 3 has items relating to the ‘Assist’ and ‘Arrange’ phases of the 5As with improvements in the median Likert score across all questions, including identifying barriers, tailoring a plan to an individual and addressing obstacles to change."
