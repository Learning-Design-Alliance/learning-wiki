Oakes 2017¶

q3 · i? · review · r2

A study of school improvement supports used longitudinal data from a Chicago reform, finding shared leadership builds professional community that improves instructional practice and student achievement; the ten-fold likelihood contrast is reported as printed.

"Schools that were strong in five essential supports (including leadership, parent-community ties, professional capacity, student-centered learning climate, and ambitious instruction) were at least ten times more likely than schools weak in most of the supports to show substantial gains in both reading and mathematics."
