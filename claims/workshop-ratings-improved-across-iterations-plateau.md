## Evidence

### Gough Melissa 2020

Gough Melissa, Solomou Georgios, Khan Danyal Zaman, Kamel Mohammed, Fountain Daniel, Kumaria Ashwin, Ashpole Richard, Sinha Saurabh, Mendoza Nigel. (2020). The evolution of an SBNS-accredited NANSIG simulated skills workshop for aspiring neurosurgical trainees: an analysis of qualitative and quantitative data. Acta Neurochirurgica. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-020-04325-6

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative Likert-scale feedback comparison across three NANSIG workshops (2017–2019). A two-way ANOVA showed a statistically significant difference (p = 0.000) between the three sets of feedback; post hoc tests showed workshop 1 differed from workshops 2 and 3 (p = 0.001 each), while workshops 2 and 3 did not differ (p = 0.834).

> "Across the three workshops hosted by NANSIG, the overall ratings using 5-point Likert scale feedback (Appendices 4 and 5) from every delegate at each event show overall improvement and plateau at a high level (workshop 1 = 3.27 (n = 11); workshop 2 = 4.89 (n = 9); workshop 3 = 4.8 (n = 10); Fig. 5)."
