# Final course grade does not predict switching from less common to most common answers, but does predict switching from most common to less common answers when the bar graph is shown

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no significant effect of student grade on the percentage of students switching from LC to MC answers. [→ Perez Kathryn E. 2010](#perez-kathryn-e-2010)
`q3 i?` Grade significantly affected switching from MC to LC answers: D/F students showed a 23% decrease and A students a 17% increase in such switching when they saw the histogram. [→ Perez Kathryn E. 2010 (2)](#perez-kathryn-e-2010-2)

## Evidence

### Perez Kathryn E. 2010

Perez Kathryn E., Strauss Eric A., Downey Nicholas, Galbraith Anne, Jeanne Robert. (2010). Does Displaying the Class Results Affect Student Discussion during Peer Instruction? CBE Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.09-11-0080

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mantel–Haenszel chi-square analysis of switchers only, grouped by final course grade, found no significant grade effect on LC-to-MC switching (χ2 = 1.04; p = 0.3087; N = 625). The authors write that "Students of all grade levels seemed to be influenced by seeing the most common answer."

> "We did not find a significant effect of student grade on the percentage of students who switched from an LC to the MC answer (χ2 = 1.04; p = 0.3087; N = 625; Figure 6A)."

### Perez Kathryn E. 2010 (2)

Perez Kathryn E., Strauss Eric A., Downey Nicholas, Galbraith Anne, Jeanne Robert. (2010). Does Displaying the Class Results Affect Student Discussion during Peer Instruction? CBE Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.09-11-0080

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same switcher-only analysis found a significant grade effect on MC-to-LC switching (χ2 = 4.45; p = 0.035; N = 293): a 23% decrease for D/F students and a 17% increase for A students when the histogram was seen.

> "However, we did find a significant effect of grade on the percentage of students who switched from the MC to an LC answer (χ2 = 4.45; p = 0.035; N = 293; Figure 6B)."
