## Evidence

### Ma Xingming 2026

Ma Xingming, Liu Xianting, Sun Haojie. (2026). The effectiveness of GAI-assisted teaching methods in medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1813108

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 50 studies (2,194 control, 2,088 experimental participants) reported in the practical examination scores section, showing "a significant improving effect on experimental skills scores" with high heterogeneity (I2 = 94%).

> "Compared with the control group, the pooled effect of the studies (SMD = 1.48, 95%CI: 1.20–1.77, p < 0.00001) showed a significant improving effect on experimental skills scores in the group of GAI-assisted experiment teaching group."
