## Evidence

### Valdemar Stenberdt 2025

Valdemar Stenberdt, Ban Mouid Shiwalia, Guido Makransky. (2025). Re-Experiencing vs. Self-Explaining: Comparing Generative Learning Activities in VR. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10096-2

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Accuracy analysis on 139 participants in the VR serial dilution experiment. Post-hoc t-tests could not detect a significant improvement for re-experiencing (p = 0.379, d = 0.151) or self-explaining (p = 0.226, d = 0.204); no group difference or interaction. The article says the accuracy effects "should be interpreted cautiously".

> "showed a sig- nificant, small increase in participants’ accuracy over time, F(1, 137) = 5.978, p = 0.016, ηp2 = 0.042."
