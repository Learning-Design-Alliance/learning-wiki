## Evidence

### Xie Jingtao 2026

Xie Jingtao, Chen Ziyan, Lin Duhuang, Zhuang Guansheng, Zheng Ziyuan, Su Yun, Xin Gang. (2026). Peer-assisted learning combined with problem-based learning enhances clinical reasoning and knowledge application among medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08659-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomized controlled trial in the Infection and Immunity module: 134 PA-PBL students versus 389 lecture-only controls, compared with the Mann-Whitney U test after two semesters. The article reports that "students in the experimental group exhibited significantly higher scores than the control group" on all four examination measures. Mean differences with 95% CIs are printed in Table 3; no standardized effect size is reported.

> "students in the experimental group exhibited significantly higher scores than the control group in the final examination total score, as well as in the knowledge-recall MCQs, case-based MCQs, and MEQs (P < 0.05, Table 3)"
