## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Dvorak Martin 2024

Dvorak Martin. (2024). Inhibitory control and academic achievement – a study of the relationship between Stroop Effect and university students’ academic performance. BMC Psychology 12:498. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01984-3

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Pearson bivariate correlation of Stroop Effect values with fail rates in the same 107-student sample found "basically no correlation between the former and participants’ fail rate, r(107) = 0.04, p = .68"; the printed effect size r = 0.04 is negligible (below r = .10).
