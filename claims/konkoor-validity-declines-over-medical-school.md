## Evidence

### Farrokhi-Khajeh-Pasha Yasin 2012

Farrokhi-Khajeh-Pasha Yasin, Nedjat Saharnaz, Mohammadi Aeen, Rad Elaheh Malakan, Majdzadeh Reza, Monajemi Farshid, Jamali Ehsan, Yazdani Shahryar. (2012). The validity of Iran’s national university entrance examination (Konkoor) for predicting medical students’ academic performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-60

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations between the eight Konkoor sub-scores and the three outcome measures in the national 2003 cohort, showing smaller coefficients for the later-stage CPIE and msGPA outcomes than for the CBSE; the largest printed exception is Arabic language with msGPAs at r = 0.323.

> "Conversely, other than the correlation coefficients of Arabic language (r = 0.323) and biology (r = 0.300) with msGPAs, all of the Konkoor sub-score predictive validity coefficients were small (range: r = 0.138 to 0.261) for both the CPIE and msGPAs."
