## Evidence

### Gates 1922

Gates, Arthur I. (1922). Educational psychology at the Princeton meeting of the American Psychological Association. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Conference report of consecutive individual measurements from Bird T. Baldwin and L. Stecher's Iowa research on mental and physical growth; the printed probable error of 2.5 centimeters is reported as printed.

> "These workers have found it possible to predict the stature at sixteen years of age from the measurements secured at ten with a PE of approximately 2.5 centimeters."

### Gates 1922 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Same Iowa growth study reported at the meeting; the printed probable error of 6.3 IQ points is reported as printed, and the report notes similar growth curves for physical and mental traits.

> "The IQ can be predicted over a similar period with a PE of estimate of 6.3 points. In general, the curves of growth for physical and mental traits are very similar."
