`q1 i?` The authors identify seven specific dilemmas in which guidelines derived from 4C/ID, ARCS and CLT contradict guidelines derived from CTML, covering situational interest, non-essential information, learner control, peer model selection, segmentation, durability of the multimedia principle, and split attention. `q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical analysis in the dilemmas section. The article enumerates seven dilemmas, e.g. learner control is "deemed extraneous by Mayer and Moreno (2003)" yet "required to foster intrinsic motivation"; and Schweppe et al. (2015) found only limited retention of multimedia-learned information after one and two weeks, questioning the multimedia principle's durability.

> "In general, motivational and complex skill developmental guidelines derived from 4C/ID, ARCS and Cognitive Load Theory (CLT) contradict with multimedia learning guidelines derived from the CTML"
