In a semi-structured interview reported in the RQ1 results, Educator 2 contrasts standard quantum mechanics treatments, where measurements are described by observables with eigenvectors and eigenvalues, with the QIST focus: "what's important about a measurement is almost always the eigenvectors."

> "From the quantum information perspective, what's important about a measurement is almost always the eigenvectors and hardly ever the eigenvalues."
