Independent t-test comparing boys and girls in the 192-child sample (75.50% male). The test showed "no significant sex difference in CTt performance", though descriptively girls scored about 1 point less than boys.

> "To evaluate sex differences in performance on the CTt, we ran an independent t-test (t (190) = 1.790, p = .075, d =.311). These results indicate no significant sex difference in CTt performance."
