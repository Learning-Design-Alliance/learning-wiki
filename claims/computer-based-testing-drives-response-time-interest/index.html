Framing statement of the commentary: computer-based testing brought "the capability to routinely capture the time that test takers spend on individual test items", which led to increased interest in response-time applications. Descriptive, no effect size.

"The growing presence of computer-based testing has brought with it the capability to routinely capture the time that test takers spend on individual test items. This, in turn, has led to an increased interest in potential applications of response time in measuring intellectual ability and achievement."
