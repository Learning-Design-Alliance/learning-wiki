# Students' self-efficacy in English rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in the reading and writing domains but did not change in listening or speaking, in one single-group pre-post study

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kim Kyong-Jee 2019

Kim Kyong-Jee. (2019). Enhancing students' active learning and self-efficacy using mobile technology in medical English classes. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2019.118

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post questionnaire of 46 students using the 20-item Korean self-efficacy in English instrument (α 0.87–0.93). Reading rose from 3.16±0.54 to 3.44±0.61 (t=1.13, p<0.05) and writing from 3.32±0.68 to 3.56±0.46 (t=1.25, p<0.05); listening (p=0.49) and speaking (p=0.72) did not change. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Students' self-efficacy in reading and writing in English increased significantly (p<0.05), whereas their self-efficacy in listening and speaking in English did not change."
