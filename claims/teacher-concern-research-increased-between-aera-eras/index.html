Evidence¶

Quesen 2026¶

Quesen, S. (2026). What educational research looked like when scores were rising: A comparative analysis of AERA conference programs, 2005–2014 and 2022–2025. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681368

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Document analysis of AERA annual meeting program pdfs (10 files for 2005–2014, 4 for 2022–2025) using exact string matching in R, reporting per-year averages. Mental health rose from 24.4 to 188.0 mentions per year; technology/AI from 30.1 to 73.8; chronic absenteeism from 1.4 to 11.8.

"mental health research increased 670 percent, technology and AI research increased 145 percent, chronic absenteeism research increased 743 percent, student behavior research increased 19 percent, and teacher retention research increased 14 percent"
