Interview theme on hidden productivity costs: P14 must "explain the context repeatedly" in complex environments; participants found iterative refinement "almost annoying" [P10] and largely invisible in productivity analysis.

"In addition to the time spent verifying outputs, participants described the need to write multiple prompts and to refine their prompts repeatedly for the same task."
