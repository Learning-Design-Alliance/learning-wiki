## Evidence

### Svensson C 2020

Svensson C, Wickström H, Emanuelson U, Bard AM, Reyher KK, Forsberg L. (2020). Training in motivational interviewing improves cattle veterinarians' communication skills for herd health management. The Veterinary Record. https://doi.org/10.1136/vr.105646

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multivariable hierarchical regression analysis of pre/post MITI scores from 31 veterinarians, including Training × Years in VHHM interactions. The article reports significant interactions with Years in VHHM for Cultivating Change Talk, Giving Information, Persuade and Seeking Collaboration, and states that the least and most experienced veterinarians did not improve in Cultivating Change Talk.

> "Veterinarians with very little (<1 year) or very substantial (>15 years) experience in VHHM did not improve in practising Cultivating Change Talk."
