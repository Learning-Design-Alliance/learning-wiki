## Evidence

### Gikandi MI 2020

Gikandi MI, Singh BS, Shuchi S, Shailja V, Megha S, Vishal D, Tamhankar AJ, Stålsby Lundborg C, Pathak A. (2020). Identifying the know-do gap in evidence-based neonatal care practices among informal health care providers—a cross-sectional study from Ujjain, India. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-020-05805-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate quantile regression on knowledge scores of 830 IHCPs with 500 bootstrap samples; the article reports the significant positive association was confined to "q75th and q90th".

> "More than 5 years of practice experience and treating more than 20 patients per day had a statistically significant positive association with the knowledge score only at higher quantiles (q75th and q90th) and had no statistically significant association at lower quantiles (q10th and q25th) and at median quantile (50th)."
