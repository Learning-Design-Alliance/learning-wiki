## Evidence

### Lujain Ibrahim 2026

Lujain Ibrahim, Franziska Sofia Hafner, Myra Cheng, Cinoo Lee, Rebecca Anselmetti, Robb Willer, Luc Rocher, Diyi Yang. (2026). Sycophantic AI makes human interaction feel more effortful and less satisfying over time. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07912 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.07912](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.07912)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Between-subjects experiment (N=228 Prolific participants) where participants rated the importance of four support types from either a close person or an AI chatbot. The source×support type interaction was significant (F(3,678)=17.69, p<0.001, partial η²=0.073), with "emotional (d= 0.77" and esteem support favored for humans.

> "People valued far more emotional (d= 0.77, p <0.001) and esteem support (d= 0.64,p <0.001) from humans than from AI systems, valued certainty somewhat more from humans (d= 0.29,p= 0.027), and showed no statistically significant difference for informational support (d=−0.04,p= 0.789)."
