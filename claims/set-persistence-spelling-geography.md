## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zirbes 1919

Zirbes, L. (1919). A Problem for Careful Research: How Does the Ability to Make Rapid Mental Adjustments Affect Test Scores and Pupil Achievements? The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22How+Does+the+Ability+to+Make+Rapid+Mental+Adjustments+Affect+Test+Scores+and+Pupil+Achievements%22+Zirbes

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

Brief illustration from spelling: pupils who spell well when spelling is uppermost in consciousness become very inefficient spellers when other factors are demanded.

> "Words learned for a given test or lesson can often not be recalled because the "set" in which they were acquired was not conducive to recall under other conditions."

### Zirbes 1919 (2)

Zirbes, L. (1919). A Problem for Careful Research: How Does the Ability to Make Rapid Mental Adjustments Affect Test Scores and Pupil Achievements? The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22How+Does+the+Ability+to+Make+Rapid+Mental+Adjustments+Affect+Test+Scores+and+Pupil+Achievements%22+Zirbes

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Geography examples: pupils asked to outline topics in their order of occurrence — 'Over half of them arranged topics with reference to another order', the order of the preceding states-in-industry problem persisting. Another pupil answered a steps-in-a-process question in terms of the previous discussion.

> "Over half of them arranged topics with reference to another order."
