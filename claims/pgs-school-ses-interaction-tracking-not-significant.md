## Subclaims

## Evidence

### K. Paige Harden 2020

K. Paige Harden, Benjamin W. Domingue, Daniel W. Belsky, Jason D. Boardman, Robert Crosnoe, Margherita Malanchini, Michel Nivard, Elliot M. Tucker-Drob and Kathleen Mullan Harris. (2020). Genetic associations with mathematics tracking and persistence in secondary school. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0060-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cumulative link model with PGS-by-school-SES product terms (Supplementary Table 3), reported in Results. Descriptively, a student with education-PGS of +1 had a 33.1% probability of Geometry placement in a high-status school, whereas a low-status-school student would need a PGS of +2.0 for the same probability; equivalence was not tested.

> "The interaction term was positive, suggesting that the education-PGS predicted 9th-grade tracking more strongly among students in higher-status schools than in lower-status schools, but this effect was not statistically signi ﬁcant (interaction b = 0.59, SE = 0.291, 95% CI [ −0.007, 1.11]);"
