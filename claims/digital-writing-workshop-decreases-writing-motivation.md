## Evidence

### Nurhan Aktaş & Hayati Akyol 2020

Nurhan Aktaş & Hayati Akyol. (2020). Effect of Digital Writing Workshop Activities on Writing Motivation and Development of Story Writing Skills. https://doi.org/10.29329/ijpe.2020.248.20

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-factor ANOVA for repeated measures on Motivation to Write Scale scores; the interaction was significant, F(1-28)= 5.90, p <0.05, while the group and measurement main effects were not significant.

> "when the table is examined with regard to the joint effect of the scale time (pretest-posttest) and group (experimental and control) variables, a statistically significant difference was found between writing motivation scores"

### Nurhan Aktaş & Hayati Akyol 2020 (2)

Nurhan Aktaş & Hayati Akyol. (2020). Effect of Digital Writing Workshop Activities on Writing Motivation and Development of Story Writing Skills. https://doi.org/10.29329/ijpe.2020.248.20

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-groups t-tests: experimental motivation fell from 56.53 to 53.13, t (14)=2.75; p=.01; the control group showed no significant change, t (14)=.-55; p=.59.

> "As can be seen in Table 10, a statistically significant difference was found between pretest and posttest scores in the experimental group (t (14)=2.75; p=.01). However, this difference is due to the fact that writing motivation scores in the experimental group decreased."
