### Ceri Ngai and Sam J. Gilbert 2026

Ceri Ngai and Sam J. Gilbert (2026). Metacognitive training facilitates optimal cognitive offloading. Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-026-00714-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Pre-registered one-way ANOVA with linear contrast on signed metacognitive bias in Experiment 2 (N = 416). The analysis "did not show a significant group effect" or linear trend; numerically, Group 4 (M = -11.6, SD = 21.1) showed greater underconfidence than Group 1 (M = -5.5, SD = 30.9).

> "Analysis of signed metacognitive bias scores did not show a significant group effect (F(3, 412) = 1.58, p = .194), nor a significant linear trend (F(1, 412) = 1.58, p = .209)."
