Evidence¶

Bruce 2022¶

Bruce, J., & Kim, H. K. (2022). Behavioral and Electrophysiological Indices of Inhibitory Control in Maltreated Adolescents and Nonmaltreated Adolescents. Development and Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0954579420001819

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA on go/no go accuracy in 230 adolescents (128 maltreated, 102 nonmaltreated) recruited via child welfare system and middle schools. Simple main effects of group: "the nonmaltreated adolescents had a higher percentage of correct responses than the maltreated adolescents" on both trial types, with partial η2 = .02 (go) and .03 (no go).

"Post hoc paired comparisons examining the simple main effect of group indicated that the nonmaltreated adolescents had a higher percentage of correct responses than the maltreated adolescents on the go trials and no go trials, F(1, 228) = 4.13, p = .043, partial η2 = .02, and F(1, 228) = 7.55, p = .006, partial η2 = .03, respectively."

Bruce 2022 (2)¶

Bruce, J., & Kim, H. K. (2022). Behavioral and Electrophysiological Indices of Inhibitory Control in Maltreated Adolescents and Nonmaltreated Adolescents. Development and Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0954579420001819

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Repeated measures ANCOVA controlling for FSIQ confirmed the group main effect and trial type x group interaction on accuracy. The article reports the interaction as significant (partial η2 = .03 in Table 3) and that results "remained significant even after controlling for general intellectual ability."

"the main effect of group and interaction between trial type and group remained significant even after controlling for general intellectual ability."
