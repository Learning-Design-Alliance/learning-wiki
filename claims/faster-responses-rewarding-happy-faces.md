## Evidence

### Fuhrmann Delia 2019

Fuhrmann Delia, Schweizer Susanne, Leung Jovita, Griffin Cait, Blakemore Sarah-Jayne. (2019). The neurocognitive correlates of academic diligence in adolescent girls. Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1080/17588928.2018.1504762

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Behavioral results from the emotional go-no-go task in the same sample; post-scan liking ratings confirmed happy faces were perceived as rewarding (M = 0.58) versus neutral faces (M = −0.22). False alarm rates did not differ between happy and neutral faces (χ2(1) = 0.96, p = .328).

> "Reaction times to happy go trials (M = 582.83 ms, SE = 22.71 ms) were significantly faster than to neutral go trials (M = 622.21 ms, SE = 28.16 ms), indicating that participants respond faster to rewarding social cues than to neutral ones."
