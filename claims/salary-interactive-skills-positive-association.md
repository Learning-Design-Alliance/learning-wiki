### Støve 2026

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of k = 5 studies (N = 1020): r = .221, 95% CI [0.04, 0.41], p = .020. The review notes salary "is often closely linked to qualifications and educational level" and may reflect broader center-level quality.

> "Our meta-analysis found a small, significant, positive association between salary and interactive skills."
