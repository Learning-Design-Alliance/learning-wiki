Elementary teacher survey and principal survey data: 53 percent of board-certified teachers and 36 percent of candidates reported leadership roles versus 25 percent of other teachers; principals reported most led professional development sometimes, often, or regularly (Table 16).

> "Board-certified teachers were twice as likely as other teachers to hold leadership roles, such as Local School Council representative, reading specialist or facilitator, lead teacher, or union delegate. Principals stated that about two-thirds of these teachers led professional development activities."
