## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Homework completion and reading assignment completion were better predictors of final exam performance in the formative format than in the original format, though correlation coefficients remained relatively low. [→ Bailey E. G. 2017](#bailey-e-g-2017)

## Evidence

### Bailey E. G. 2017

Bailey E. G., Jensen J., Nelson J., Wiberg H. K., Bell J. D. (2017). Weekly Formative Exams and Creative Grading Enhance Student Learning in an Introductory Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-02-0104

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear regression with final exam score as dependent variable across five semesters (Table 1); e.g., homework completion r² rose from 0.04/0.01 to 0.10–0.16 and reading completion from 0.01 to 0.06–0.15. Midterm scores were the best predictor in both formats.

> "Interestingly, homework completion and reading assignment completion were better predictors of final exam performance as part of the formative course format compared with the original course format, even though these activities were identical."
