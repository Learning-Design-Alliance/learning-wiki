## Evidence

### Xu Bu 2025

Xu Bu, Xing Xiaoli, Zhang Guangyun, Zhang Wenting, Zhang Min, Gan Yan, Wei Tao, Xia Xiaodan. (2025). Effect of the situation-background-assessment-recommendation model combined with the mini-CEX assessment on the clinical communication, clinical thinking and comprehensive clinical abilities of nursing interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08022-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison on the Nurses' Clinical Communication Ability Scale (28 items, six dimensions, Cronbach's α = 0.92) in the RCT of 405 interns. Table 2 prints total score 75.19 ± 8.05 vs. 73.14 ± 10.41 (t = −2.214, p = 0.027) and for participate jointly t = −1.448, p = 0.148. No effect sizes are printed.

> "After intervention, interns in the observation group had significantly improved clinical communication skills in five dimensions (establishing harmonious relationships, listening keenly, confirming patient problems, transmitting effective information and verifying feelings) compared with the control group (p < 0.05); however, there was no statistically significant difference in the dimensions of participating jointly (p > 0.05)."
