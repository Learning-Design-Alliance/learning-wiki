## Evidence

### Hao 2025

Hao, Z., Jiang, J., Yu, J., Liu, Z., & Zhang, Y. (2025). Student-AI Interaction in an LLM-Empowered Learning Environment: A Cluster Analysis of Engagement Profiles. arXiv:2503.01694v3. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA on average quiz scores with pretest as covariate, F(2,125)=7.20, p=.001, comparing Clusters 2, 3, and 4 (Cluster 1 excluded for having no quiz data).

> "Post-hoc tests further revealed that students in Cluster 4 achieved significantly higher average quiz scores (M= 7.18 , SD= 1.23 ) than those in Cluster 2 (M= 6.00 , SD= 1.63 ) and 3 (M= 6.07 , SD= 1.40 ), t= 2.84, p=.02 and t= 3.23, p=.005 respectively."
