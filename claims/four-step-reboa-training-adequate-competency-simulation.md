## Evidence

### Brede Jostein Rødseth 2019

Brede Jostein Rødseth, Lafrenz Thomas, Krüger Andreas J, Søvik Edmund, Steffensen Torjus, Kriesi Carlo, Steinert Martin, Klepstad Pål. (2019). Resuscitative endovascular balloon occlusion of the aorta (REBOA) in non-traumatic out-of-hospital cardiac arrest: evaluation of an educational programme. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-027980

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive simulation-based assessment of seven physicians after the four-step training programme, scored on video by two independent observers using the OSAPRA chart. Mean score was "41.8 (39–43.5, SD 1.4) points out of 48", above the agreed cut score of 30; no formal sample testing was performed.

> "The total objective assessment scores of the candidates’ competency was 41.8 (39–43.5, SD 1.4) points out of 48 (figure 5). All scores are mean values from the two observers."
