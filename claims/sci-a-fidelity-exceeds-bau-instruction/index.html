Stichter 2018¶

Stichter, J. P., Herzog, M. J., Kilgus, S. P., & Schoemann, A. M. (2018). Exploring the Moderating Effects of Cognitive Abilities on Social Competence Intervention Outcomes. Behavior Modification, 42(1), 84-107 (published online 2017). https://doi.org/10.1177/0145445517698654

Direct-observation fidelity-of-core-components coding (partial interval, minimum ten 15-min observations per setting; interobserver agreement 93.3%–99.3%) compared SCI-A and BAU instruction. All components differed significantly; e.g., specific verbal feedback t(1,036) = 19.88, p < .001, and BAU nonsocial time M = 0.797 vs 0.070.

"SCI-A teachers utilized more specific verbal feedback about students’ social knowledge and performance (SCI-A: M = 0.096, BAU: M = 0.008), t(1,036) = 19.88, p < .001."
