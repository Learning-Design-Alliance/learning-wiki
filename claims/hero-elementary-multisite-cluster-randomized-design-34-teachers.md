# The study enrolled 34 second-grade teachers with 810 students across 20 California schools, with 318 students completing both assessments in the impact sample.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The study randomly assigned 34 second-grade teachers (n=810 students) from 20 California schools serving economically disadvantaged students to treatment or control, using Mahalanobis matching for schools with odd numbers of participating teachers. [→ Li 2020](#li-2020)
`q3 i?` Of 793 students in the study, 754 completed the pre-assessment and 318 completed both pre- and post-assessment, comprising the impact sample. [→ Li 2020 (2)](#li-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Li 2020

Li, L., Luttgen, K., Huang, K., Weiser, G., Cornfield, O., Hess, H., Chow, E., & Schneider, M. (2020). Hero Elementary Playlist RCT Study in Formal School and Distance Learning Contexts. WestEd. https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/dd/index.asp

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study design description from the Methods section. Random assignment was at the teacher level; for schools with odd numbers of participating teachers "the research team applied the optimal Mahalanobis matching method to pair up schools" on four demographic variables. Treatment implemented Hero Elementary; control implemented business-as-usual science.

> "The study used a multi-site cluster randomized, experimental design, which randomly as-signed 34 second-grade teachers (n=810 students) from 20 schools in California"

### Li 2020 (2)

Li, L., Luttgen, K., Huang, K., Weiser, G., Cornfield, O., Hess, H., Chow, E., & Schneider, M. (2020). Hero Elementary Playlist RCT Study in Formal School and Distance Learning Contexts. WestEd. https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/dd/index.asp

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

CONSORT-reported sample flow in the Student Participants section: 793 students remained after opt-outs, "754 students" completed the pre-assessment, and 318 completed both assessments forming the impact sample. Attrition followed COVID-19 school closures.

> "Seventeen students in total opted out of the study, leaving 793 students in the study. Among these students, 754 students (not directly shown in the CONSORT figure) completed the pre-assessment (the baseline sample), and 318 students completed both pre- and post-assessment (matched pre- and post-sample), which comprised the impact sample."
