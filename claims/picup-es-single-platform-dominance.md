### W. Brian Lane 2021

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Census of programming platforms used in code templates and completed codes (Figures 11 and 12). Despite editors recommending at least three platforms, the article reports "over two-thirds of the 62 ESs use only a single platform".

### W. Brian Lane 2021 (2)

W. Brian Lane. (2021). Analysis of the PICUP Collection: Strengths and Areas for Development. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.02408](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.02408)

Classification of Exercise Sets as platform-specialized or platform-agnostic (Figure 13). The analysis found "nearly all of the ESs (57 out of 62) are platform agnostic", meaning they could be extended to additional platforms.

> "even though the majority of ESs include implementations in only a single platform, nearly all of the ESs (57 out of 62) are platform agnostic"
