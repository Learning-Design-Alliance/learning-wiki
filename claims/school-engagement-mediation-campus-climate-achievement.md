## Evidence

### Cruz 2025

Cruz, I. N., Moreno, K. A., Stovall, B., de la Vega, L. P., & Chavira, G. (2025). Connecting the dots: Examining stressful life events, campus climate, and school engagement on academic achievement and psychological distress in a predominantly Latine sample. Journal of Research on Adolescence, 35, e70096. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.70096

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional path analysis (lavaan, FIML) in a sample of 293 ninth- and tenth-grade students; the model fit the data well (CFI = 0.997, RMSEA = 0.049, SRMR = 0.011). The direct effect of campus climate on achievement was nonsignificant (β = −.04, p = .567), while the indirect effect through engagement was significant, "indicating school engagement mediated the relationship between campus climate and academic achievement."

> "campus climate had a significant positive indirect effect on academic achievement through school engagement (B = 0.27, β = .15, SE = 0.07, p < .001), indicating school engagement mediated the relationship between campus climate and academic achievement."
