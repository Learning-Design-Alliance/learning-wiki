Evidence¶

Doutre 2021¶

Doutre, S. M., Barrett, T., & Rice, J. (2021). California's Special Education Local Plan Areas: Funding patterns, inclusion rates, and student outcomes. Regional Educational Laboratory West at WestEd. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive SELPA-level comparison (N = 42 single-district, N = 83 multidistrict) of 2016/17 IDEA Section 618 LRE data; table 2 reports means of "25 (8–39)" versus "18 (2–34)" with a printed difference statistic of 0.92, marked as meaningful above the 0.50 SD threshold. The authors interpret this as a more restrictive environment for some K–12 students.

"K–12 – percent of students who spend less than 40 percent of the school day in a general education setting 21 (2–39) 25 (8–39) 18 (2–34) 0.92*"
