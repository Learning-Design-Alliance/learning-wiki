## Evidence

### Rimfeld 2016

Rimfeld, K., Ayorech, Z., Dale, P. S., Kovas, Y. & Plomin, R. (2016). Genetics affects choice of academic subjects as well as achievement. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep26373

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariate ACE model-fitting results (Fig. 2) for individual subject grades in the TEDS twin sample. The article reports the range "from 35% for history to 76% for chemistry" but states the sample was underpowered, with overlapping confidence intervals; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Although heritabilities differed across subjects from 35% for history to 76% for chemistry, the sample size was too small to provide adequate power to detect such differences, which can be seen in the estimates’ overlapping confidence intervals"
