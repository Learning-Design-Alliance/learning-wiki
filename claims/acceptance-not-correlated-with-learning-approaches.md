## Evidence

### Marco Lionel Di 2017

Marco Lionel Di, Venot Alain, Gillois Pierre. (2017). Does the acceptance of hybrid learning affect learning approaches in France?. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2017.14.24

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis in the same midwifery survey relating HELAM-L acceptance scores to R-SPQ-2F approach scores. The article reports "no significant correlation between acceptance and learning approaches"; equivalence was not tested, and no effect size is printed for this non-significant finding.

> "There was no significant correlation between acceptance and learning approaches (rs<0.2, P>0.05; not significant [NS]). Stronger correlations were found with an acceptance below the average score, but this was not significant (rs= 0.42, P> 0.05; NS)"
