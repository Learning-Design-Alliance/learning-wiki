Shahin Hossain. (2026). Four Types of LLM Reliance and Their Predictors Among Undergraduate Writers: A Mixed-Methods Study at a Minority-Serving R1 University. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28749 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.28749](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.28749)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Known-groups validity analysis via multinomial logistic regression in the main survey study (N=382). The article prints an odds ratio of OR = 0.11 with p < .001 for Dependent versus Strategic classification per unit of AI literacy; no standardized effect size is printed, so no magnitude code is assigned.

> "each unit increase in AI literacy was associated with approximately a tenfold reduction in the odds of Dependent versus Strategic classification (𝑂𝑅= 0.11, 𝑝<. 001), confirming that high-AI-literacy students disproportionately exhibit Strategic reliance."
