Evidence¶

Ozcelik 2013¶

Ozcelik, E., Ercil Cagiltay, N., & Sahin Ozcelik, N. (2013). The effect of uncertainty on learning in game-like environments. Computers & Education, 67, 12–20. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2013.02.009

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Pearson correlation analyses (n = 140) on the game experiment's behavioral measures found "higher total response time to the questions, accuracy, and motivation were correlated with higher post-test scores"; Table 4 prints r = 0.27, r = 0.37, and r = 0.22 respectively, while reading time was not significant (r = 0.10).

"The higher total response time to the questions, accuracy, and motivation were correlated with higher post-test scores. However, the correlation between the total reading time of explanations and the post-test found not to be statistically signiﬁcant."
