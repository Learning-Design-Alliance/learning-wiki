## Evidence

### Reeves Todd D. 2018

Reeves Todd D., Hake Laura E., Chen Xinnian, Frederick Jennifer, Rudenga Kristin, Ludlow Larry H., O'Connor Clare M. (2018). Does Context Matter? Convergent and Divergent Findings in the Cross-Institutional Evaluation of Graduate Teaching Assistant Professional Development Programs. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-03-0044

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA treating GTA as a random effect on the 19 student rating items; random GTA effects were statistically significant for instructional practice overall and for all individual items, with ηp2 values ranging from 0.10 to 0.23 and the largest ηp2 = 0.23 for providing clear explanations. The largest GTA-to-GTA differences pertained to clear explanations, answering questions, and teaching diverse students.

> "The amount of variance across GTAs was moderate to large, with ηp2 values ranging from 0.10 to 0.23."
