# In an RCT, DCMP significantly increased completion of the developmental math sequence and passing of college-level math

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q4` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` In the Rutschow et al. (2019) randomized controlled trial (1,411 students), DCMP significantly increased completion of the developmental math sequence (57.00% vs. 33.50%, effect size 0.59) and ever passing a college-level math class (25.30% vs. 18.50%, effect size 0.24). ## Evidence

### rutschow-rct

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021, June). Dana Center Mathematics Pathways. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/718.

Randomized controlled trial with low attrition across four Texas community colleges; 856 students randomly assigned to DCMP and 555 to standard developmental math, outcomes from administrative records with no attrition; effect size 0.59, p < .01.
