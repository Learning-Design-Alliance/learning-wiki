## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A G study of 22 short debriefings rated by two raters yielded an absolute generalizability coefficient φ = 0.80 and a decision coefficient φ-λ3 = 0.82, indicating high reliability. [→ Rivière Etienne 2019](#riviere-etienne-2019)

## Evidence

### Rivière Etienne 2019

Rivière Etienne, Aubin Etienne, Tremblay Samuel-Lessard, Lortie Gilles, Chiniara Gilles. (2019). A new tool for assessing short debriefings after immersive simulation: validity of the SHORT scale. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1503-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Generalizability-theory analysis (G study) of two raters independently assessing 22 filmed short debriefings from Laval University's regular simulation activities, using a mixed R x I x O:B design analyzed with EduG 6.1-f. The study reports "φ = 0.80 and φ-λ3 = 0.82"; without the holistic rating the coefficients are slightly lower.

> "The calculated G coefficients are: φ = 0.80 and φ-λ3 = 0.82. If the holistic rating is removed from the analysis (i.e., only items 1 to 5 are included), the calculated G coefficients are: φ = 0.78 and φ-λ3 = 0.79."
