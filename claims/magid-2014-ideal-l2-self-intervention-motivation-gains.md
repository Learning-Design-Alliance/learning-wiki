## Evidence

### Magid 2014

Michael Magid. (2014). An Application of the L2 Motivational Self System to Motivate Elementary School English Learners in Singapore. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v2i1.232

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quantitative comparison of pre- and post-workshop questionnaire ratings in a four-month intervention with 16 grade-five learners (8 per group); the author compared means because there were not enough participants to test for statistical significance. The quote reports the headline percentages.

> "The main findings from the quantitative data were that 90% of the participants in the experimental group became more motivated to learn English, more confident in their English, and exhibited more positive attitudes toward learning English as a result of the program."

### Magid 2014 (2)

Michael Magid. (2014). An Application of the L2 Motivational Self System to Motivate Elementary School English Learners in Singapore. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v2i1.232

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same questionnaire-based comparison, reporting the control group's percentages for motivation, confidence, and attitudes; the author states means were compared without statistical significance testing due to the small sample.

> "In the control group, only 50% of the participants became motivated, 75% became more confident, and 90% improved their attitudes toward learning English."
