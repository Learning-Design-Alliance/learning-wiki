# GEMS education produced higher post-test overall problem solving scores in science education than the control program (d = 1.41)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Children receiving GEMS education scored significantly higher on the overall PSSSE at post-test than control children (t = 3.5, p = .002, Cohen's d = 1.41). [→ Unal 2018](#unal-2018)

## Evidence

### Unal 2018

Unal, M., & Saglam, M. (2018). Examination of the effect of the GEMS program on problem solving and science process skills of 6 years old children. European Journal of Educational Research, 7(3), 567-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.7.3.567

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Semi-experimental pre-test/post-test control group study of 25 six-year-olds (11 experimental, 14 control) in Malatya kindergartens; post-test total PSSSE compared by independent samples t test, t = 3.5, p = .002, effect size "1.41" (Cohen's d). The experimental mean was 2.72 vs control 2.50.

> "When Table 5 was examined, the change observed in the problem solving scores of the children receiving education within the scope of GEMS Education before and after the application was found to be higher compared to the control group"
