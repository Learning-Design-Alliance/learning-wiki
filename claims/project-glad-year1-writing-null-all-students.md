# Project GLAD showed no statistically significant Year 1 effects on any of six writing traits for all students, with a marginal effect for the trait of ideas

## Evidence

### Deussen et al. 2014

Theresa Deussen, Elizabeth Autio, Angela Roccograndi, Makoto Hanita. (2014). The Impact of Project GLAD on Students' Literacy and Science Learning: Year 1 Results from a Cluster-Randomized Trial of Sheltered Instruction. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

HLM analysis of 6+1 Traits writing assessments (scored per trait, no pretest; fall GM comprehension used as covariate) in the cluster randomized trial. The ideas trait was "marginally significant" with b=0.14, p=.062, Hedges g = 0.22.

> "There were no statistically significant treatment effects for any of the six traits (Table 8). However, the result for the trait of "ideas" was marginally significant and was the largest effect size of any of the literacy measures (b=0.14, p=.062 and Hedges g= 0.22)."
