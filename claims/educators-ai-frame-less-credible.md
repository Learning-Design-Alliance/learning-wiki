## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within educators only, the AI condition had significantly lower mean credibility ranks than the educational psychology condition; other pairwise comparisons were not significant. [→ Mutlu Cukurova & Rosemary Luckin & Carmel Kent 2020](#mutlu-cukurova-rosemary-luckin-carmel-kent-2020)

## Evidence

### Mutlu Cukurova & Rosemary Luckin & Carmel Kent 2020

Mutlu Cukurova & Rosemary Luckin & Carmel Kent. (2020). Impact of an Artificial Intelligence Research Frame on the Perceived Credibility of Educational Research Evidence. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00188-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis subgroup analysis of educators (n = 20 neuroscience, n = 20 educational psychology, n = 26 AI) found significantly lower mean ranks for the AI condition versus educational psychology only; no effect size was printed.

> "This post hoc analysis revealed statistically significant differences in the credibility scores’ mean ranks between the AI condition (25.37) and the EdPsy condition (39.28) ( p = .044), but not between the other post hoc pairwise comparisons."
