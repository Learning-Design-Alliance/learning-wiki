## Evidence

### Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang 2024

Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang. (2024). Interbrain neural correlates of self and other integration in joint statistical learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00280-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Target-onset P3 analysis of actors' EEG on self-relevant trials across learning-phase blocks: significant phase × context interaction (β = −0.11, P = 0.045), steeper decline in the joint context; the quadratic modulating-phase model also fit better; no printed effect size.

> "However, the two-way interaction effects of phase × context were signi ﬁcant (β = −0.11, SE = 0.05, t418 = −2.01,P = 0.045). This was indicated by the slope of the P3 amplitude decreasing from Block 1 to Block 6 being steeper in the joint context compared to the baseline context"
