## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Studies the authors review found bridge participants more likely to persist to the second year with higher GPAs, and several reported significantly higher retention, persistence, and graduation rates. [→ Gonzalez Quiroz 2018](#gonzalez-quiroz-2018)

## Evidence

### Gonzalez Quiroz 2018

Gonzalez Quiroz, A., & Garza, N. R. (2018). Focus on Student Success: Components for Effective Summer Bridge Programs. Journal of Hispanic Higher Education, 17(2), 101-111. https://doi.org/10.1177/1538192717753988

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Narrative literature review attributing this finding to Pascarella and Terenzini (2005) and Walpole et al. (2008); the review also cites Cabrera et al. (2013) reporting retention (p < .05) and persistence (p < .001) significantly higher for participants, and Douglas and Attewell (2014) reporting higher graduation and second-year retention for bridge students in a cohort of more than 10,000.

> "Both studies found that summer bridge participants were more likely to persist to the second year and had a higher grade point average (GPA) than those who did not participate in a summer bridge program."
