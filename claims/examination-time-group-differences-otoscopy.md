## Evidence

### Albrecht Tobias 2024

Albrecht Tobias, Fehre Nathalie, Ramackers Wolf, Nikendei Christoph, Offergeld Christian. (2024). “Seeing inside out”: revealing the effectiveness of otoscopy training in virtual reality enhanced practical exams - a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05385-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simulator-recorded timing during the practical exam in this cluster-randomized trial; the overall average examination time was 83 seconds (range 15–196). IG1 averaged 95 ± 32 seconds, significantly longer than IG2 and CG, without examining a significantly larger tympanic membrane area.

> "The mean examination times (in seconds) for the individual groups were IG1 (95 ± 32), IG2 (79 ± 33), IG3 (81 ± 28) and CG (70 ± 25), with a significant longer examination time of IG1 compared to IG2 (p = 0.03) and CG (p = 0.008)"
