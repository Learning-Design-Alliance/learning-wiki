## Evidence

### Flowers Elena 2019

Flowers Elena, Martin Margaret, Abid Hamza, Binford Sasha, Mackin Lynda. (2019). Pairing pedagogical and genomic advances to prepare advanced practice nurses for the era of precision health. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1542-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Standardized school-wide online course evaluations with a four-point Likert scale, administered at completion of each term for the three hybrid courses. Scores are descriptive; the third course scored 3.50 against a school-wide average of 3.40.

> "Overall course satisfaction was 3.25 (range 3, 4) for the first course compared to school-wide average satisfaction for all courses in the term of 3.46. Overall satisfaction was 3.78 (range 3, 4) for the second course compared to school-wide average satisfaction of 3.46 for all courses in the term."

### Flowers Elena 2019 (2)

Flowers Elena, Martin Margaret, Abid Hamza, Binford Sasha, Mackin Lynda. (2019). Pairing pedagogical and genomic advances to prepare advanced practice nurses for the era of precision health. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1542-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of anonymized free-text evaluation comments from the same course evaluations; the authors interpret the recurrent critique as a common challenge for online learning environments.

> "Critiques of the courses had a specific recurrent theme of wanting more in-person class sessions and/or more engagement in the online learning forum. This is a common challenge for online learning environments."
