## Evidence

### Arnaud Stanczak 2022

Arnaud Stanczak, Céline Darnon, Anaïs Robert, Marie Demolliens, Camille Sanrey, Pascal Bressoux, Pascal Huguet, Céline Buchs, Fabrizio Butera, and PROFAN Consortium. (2022). Do Jigsaw classrooms improve learning outcomes? Five experiments and an internal meta-analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology, 114 (6), pp.1461-1476. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000730

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1, a two-hour randomized lesson with 252 sixth graders assigned by class to jigsaw (n = 127) or individualistic (n = 125) conditions on the same mathematics material. The independent t-test showed "did not outperform" with d = -0.04, 95% CI [-0.29, 0.20].

> "participants in the jigsaw condition (M = 10.83, SD = 4.28) did not outperform participants in the individualistic condition (M = 11.01, SD = 4.13), t(250) < 1, p = .628¸ d = -0.04, 95 % CI [-0.29, 0.20]"
