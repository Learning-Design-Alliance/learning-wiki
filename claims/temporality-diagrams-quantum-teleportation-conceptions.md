### Sebastian Kilde-Westberg 2026

Sebastian Kilde-Westberg, Andreas Johansson, Anna Pearson, and Jonas Enger. (2026). Making sense of quantum teleportation: An intervention study on students' conceptions using a diagrammatic approach. arXiv:2511.21443 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00594-9](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00594-9)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The phenomenographic analysis found that in categories 1 and 2 'there seem not to be a direction of time in the diagrams per se', whereas in categories 3 and 4 temporal recognition in diagrams is connected to a physical experimental situation.

> "In the first two categories, where the process of 'teleportation' includes some physical transportation or re-constitution of a quantum particle, there seem not to be a direction of time in the diagrams per se"
