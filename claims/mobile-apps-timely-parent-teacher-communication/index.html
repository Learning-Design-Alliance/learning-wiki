Evidence¶

Orta 2022¶

Orta, D., & Gutiérrez, V. (2022). Improving school-family communication and engagement: Lessons from remote schooling during the pandemic. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/improving-school-family-communication-and-engagement

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Focus-group finding from a high school teacher describing app-based messaging during remote schooling; the teacher recounted texting a parent during a midterm and the student immediately turning her screen on. Parents reported appreciating attendance notifications received through apps.

"We have been finding that a quick text message [via the app] is something that parents are more apt to see, and immediately respond to rather than leaving a voicemail."
