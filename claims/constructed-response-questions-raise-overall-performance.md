### Wright 2016

Cognitive Difficulty and Format of Exams Predicts Gender and Socioeconomic Gaps in Exam Performance of Students in Introductory Biology Courses. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Main effect from the multilevel model with percent CR as the exam-characteristic predictor. A middle/high-SES male with cumulative GPA 3.27 is predicted to score 14.54% lower on a purely restricted-response exam than on a purely constructed-response exam.

> "For middle/high-SES male students, there is predicted to be a positive effect of increasing the percentage of constructed-response questions on exams (β = 0.0789 ± 0.00668 [SE], p value < 0.0001; Table 4)"
