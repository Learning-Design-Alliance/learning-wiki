## Evidence

### L Hahn 2024

L Hahn, S Blaue, P Klein. (2024). A research-informed graphical tool to visually approach Gauss' and Stokes' theorems in vector calculus. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad2390](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad2390)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Seven-item perceived educational impact scale (Cronbach's alpha 0.90) administered after the intervention phases in the university recitation implementations (N = 65). The article reports the "scale mean of 0 .65 ± 0.22" and that the tool was considered helpful for understanding and recommended for first-year students and university courses. No effect size is printed.

> "Further, students indicated a scale mean of 0 .65 ± 0.22 for the perceived educational impact of the tool ( N = 65; Fig. 7). More precisely, the graphical tool is considered helpful for understanding (EI1, EI4, EI6) and recommended for first-year students and for its use in university courses in undergraduate physics"
