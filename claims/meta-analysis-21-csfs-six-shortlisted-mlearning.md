`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Systematic meta-analysis of 19 quantitative studies published since 2007 on m-Learning in higher education. The authors report that "a total of 21 factors were discovered" and then applied a threshold of six appearances to shortlist factors for statistical pooling.

> "A total of 21 factors were discovered in the studies that researchers considered to be important  for the success of m-Learning in higher educational  institutions."
