## Evidence

### Lohela Terhi Johanna 2016

Lohela Terhi Johanna, Nesbitt Robin Clark, Manu Alexander, Vesel Linda, Okyere Eunice, Kirkwood Betty, Gabrysch Sabine. (2016). Competence of health workers in emergency obstetric care: an assessment using clinical vignettes in Brong Ahafo region, Ghana. BMJ Open. [doi:10.1136/bmjopen-2015-010963](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-010963)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regression linking facility-level workload (average annual deliveries per SBA, from surveillance and facility data) to vignette score in 60 facilities; the Results section reports p=0.03 for the association. The abstract describes workload "ranging from 0 to 184 deliveries per skilled birth attendant, with higher workload associated with higher vignette scores".

> "The average annual workload was very unevenly distributed among facilities, ranging from 0 to 184 deliveries per skilled birth attendant, with higher workload associated with higher vignette scores."
