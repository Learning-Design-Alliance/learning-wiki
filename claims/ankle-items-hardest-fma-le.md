`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-level analysis of all 17 FMA-LE items across both groups; binomial tests against a 0.50 benchmark and FDR-adjusted Fisher's exact tests. Reflex items showed the highest accuracy (87.5%–98.5%) and the ankle items the lowest (30.8%–36.0%).

> "binomial tests showed that the proportion of correct responses for these two items was significantly below 0.50 in both groups (adjusted p < .001). After Fisher's exact tests with multiple-comparison adjustment, no significant between-group differences were observed for any individual item"
