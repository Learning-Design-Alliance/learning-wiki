`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Observational comparison of attrition rates by staff race/ethnicity across job categories in Pennsylvania public schools. The brief reports gaps "generally higher" for staff of color pre-pandemic that grew during the pandemic, with the exception of school administrators; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Pre-pandemic attrition rates for staff of color were generally higher than for non-Hispanic White staff, and these differences grew during the pandemic except for school administrators, where they narrowed."
