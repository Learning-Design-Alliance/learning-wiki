## Evidence

### Lee 2025

Lee, J., Nam, K., Suh, Y., Lee, Y., and Lee, D. 2025. "Factors Associated With Patient Safety Activities of Clinical Nurses: A Cross‐Sectional Secondary Data Analysis." International Nursing Review 72, no. 4: e70127. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70127

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression (Table 3) with education sessions as a predictor (reference: 4 or more times): the 1-time category had B = −0.13, β = −0.11, t = −1.98, p = 0.049 among 291 nurses. The abstract states receiving education only once was negatively associated with activities.

> "Participants who received patient safety nursing education only once a year demonstrated lower engagement in patient safety activities than those who received it four or more times a year."
