`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pragmatic randomized controlled trial; 39 clinicians completed either in-person simulation (n = 17) or telesimulation training (n = 22). Two-way mixed ANOVA on the clinical vignette measure showed "a significant improvement across timepoints" with partial ƞ2=0.219; post-hoc change was significant between pre- and 4-weeks post (p < 0.001).
