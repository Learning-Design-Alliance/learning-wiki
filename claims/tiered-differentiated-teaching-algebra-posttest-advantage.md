Bal, A. P. (2016). The effect of the differentiated teaching approach in the algebraic learning field on students’ academic achievements. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 63, 185-204. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.63.11

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Semi-experimental pretest–posttest control-group study: 33 experiment and 24 control sixth graders taught four weeks by the same teacher. Corrected posttest means were 17.35 versus 13.38; the ANCOVA grouping main effect was "F (1,54)=22,212; p<.01".
