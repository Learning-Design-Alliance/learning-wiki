## Evidence

### Cox Morgan L. 2020

Cox Morgan L., Deng Zhi-De, Palmer Hannah, Watts Amanda, Beynel Lysianne, Young Jonathan R., Lisanby Sarah H., Migaly John, Appelbaum Lawrence G. (2020). Utilizing transcranial direct current stimulation to enhance laparoscopic technical skills training: A randomized controlled trial. Brain Stimulation. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brs.2020.03.009

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary sub-analyses and correlations in the randomized trial found no significant demographic or behavioral moderators of performance; the article also reports age, caffeine, and sleep "did not correlate with any of the task metrics (all p > 0.05)". No effect sizes printed.

> "There was no difference in performance metrics between male and female subjects. Similarly, no differences were observed in performance between individuals who self-reported as athletes, musicians, artists, or video gamers versus those reporting not to belong to these classifications (all p > 0.05)"
