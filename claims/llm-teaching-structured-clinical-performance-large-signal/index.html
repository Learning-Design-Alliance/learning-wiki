BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09879-0

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Supplementary random-effects meta-analysis of structured or examination-based clinical performance outcomes from 2 studies (38 vs. 39 participants). The printed effect size is SMD 3.39 (95% CI 1.43 to 5.35). The review warns this is "a small-study signal from only 77 participants"; selective reporting and measurement artifact cannot be excluded; certainty was low.

"Structured clinical performance included 2 studies (38 vs. 39 participants), with a pooled effect of SMD 3.39 (95% CI 1.43 to 5.35) (Fig. 6)."
