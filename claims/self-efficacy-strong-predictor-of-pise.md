> "Model 3 in Table 4 suggests that physics self -efficacy is a strong predictor of PISE, explaining 32% of the variance before pandemic and 38% of the variance during pandemic. When physics self -efficacy is included in the analysis (Model 4), both gender and grade become non-significant predictors during the pandemic."
