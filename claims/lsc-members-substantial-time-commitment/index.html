Susan Ryan 1997¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey of LSC members asked to list monthly hours spent at the school for formal duties; the authors treat this time as a proxy for members' specific knowledge about the school. "half the parent and community members report spending five to ten hours a month at school."

"For formal LSC duties, half the parent and community members report spending five to ten hours a month at school (see Table 6)."
