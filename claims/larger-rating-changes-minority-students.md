# Rating changes between first impressions and final ratings are larger for ethnic minority than ethnic majority students, for both ascending and descending performances

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Rating changes were significantly larger for ethnic minority students than majority students in ascending performances (d = .37) and in descending performances (d = .24). [→ Steinmann JJ 2025](#steinmann-jj-2025)

## Evidence

### Steinmann JJ 2025

Steinmann JJ, Otto I, Born MP, Derous E, Woltman AM, van den Broek WW, Stegers-Jager KM. (2025). Adjustment of Assessors’ First Impressions Differs by Student Ethnicity. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2196

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

LMM analysis of mean absolute rating changes in the 81-physician video experiment. The descending performances showed a similar result, with significantly smaller changes for majority students (M = 3.05) than minority students (M = 3.442), F(1,79) = 5.541, p = .02, d = .24.

> "the rating changes were significantly smaller for ethnic majority students (M = 2.07, SD = .13) than for ethnic minority students (M = 2.53, SD = .13), F(1,83) = 11.65, p < .001, d = .37"
