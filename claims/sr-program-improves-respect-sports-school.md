## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the educational program, the experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group on the respect component of social responsibility (t(50) = 2.31; p = .02). [→ Malinauskas and Juodsnukis 2017](#malinauskas-and-juodsnukis-2017)
`q2 i?` Within the experimental group, respect scores rose significantly from before to after the program (t(50) = -2.78; p = .008), while the control group showed no significant change. [→ Malinauskas and Juodsnukis 2017 (2)](#malinauskas-and-juodsnukis-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Malinauskas and Juodsnukis 2017

Romualdas K. Malinauskas, Dalius R. Juodsnukis. (2017). Education of Social Responsibility among Sports Schools Students. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2017.2.289

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group comparison in the Results section of the educational experiment with 26 experimental and 26 control football sports school students, using Student t tests for independent samples. The article reports a significant post-program difference in respect, "t (50) = 2.31; p = .02"; no effect size is printed.

> "After experiment significant differences were found between experimental and control groups in ―respect‖ (t (50) = 2.31; p = .02) and in ―caring and helping‖ ( t (50) = 2.00; p = .04)."

### Malinauskas and Juodsnukis 2017 (2)
