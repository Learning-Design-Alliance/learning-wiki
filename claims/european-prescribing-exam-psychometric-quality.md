Donker, E. M., Piët, J. D., Brinkman, D. J., Richir, M. C., Papaioannidou, P., Likic, R., Sanz, E. J., Christiaens, T., Costa, J. N., De Ponti, F., Böttiger, Y., Kramers, C., van Agtmael, M. Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeutics. https://doi.org/10.1002/cpt.70063

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric analysis of two pilot exams (94 questions). Cronbach's alpha ranged 0.61–0.87, SEMs ranged "2.5% to 2.75%", no Rir-values were negative, and an expert panel (28 experts from 13 countries) rated 92.6% of questions positively on content validity.

> "The independent panel of experts considered that most questions assessed essential knowledge or skills: 87 (92.6%) of the questions had a positive CVR and 60 (63.8%) had a good CVR according to the reference values described by Lawshe."
