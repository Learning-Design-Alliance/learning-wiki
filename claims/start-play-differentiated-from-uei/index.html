Evidence¶

Mihee An 2020¶

Mihee An, Jayden Nord, Natalie A Koziol, Stacey C Dusing, Audrey E Kane, Michele A Lobo, Sarah W McCoy, Regina T Harbourne. (2020). Developing a fidelity measure of early intervention programs for children with neuromotor disorders. Developmental Medicine & Child Neurology. https://doi.org/10.1111/dmcn.14702

q2 · i? · design · r3

Phase II program differentiation analysis of 103 videos (64 START-Play, 39 uEI) using general linear mixed-effects modelling accounting for variance between coders and videos. The article reports p<0.001 for all behavior-use contrasts between the two named groups.

"Regarding individual behavior use, rates of START-Play behaviors were higher in the START-Play group ( p<0.001 for all behaviors) and rates of non-START-Play behaviors were higher in the uEI group ( p<0.001 for all behaviors)."
