## Evidence

### Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Surveying Graduate Students' Attitudes and Approaches to Problem Solving. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/37/5/055704](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/37/5/055704)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey item (20) across groups: faculty reflection was close to 75% for both introductory and graduate-level problem solving, which the authors call lower than expected; only approximately 25% of introductory physics students reported reflecting and learning from problem solutions.

> "Figure 11 shows that in response to question (20), only approximately 55% of the graduate students (for both introductory and advanced level prob- lems) noted that, after they solve homework problems, they take the time to reﬂect and learn from the solution."
