## Evidence

### Tao 1997

Tao, Ping-Kee; Gunstone, Richard F. (1997). Conceptual Change in Science through Collaborative Learning at the Computer. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the National Association for Research in Science Teaching (70th, Oak Brook, IL, March 21-24, 1997). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED406151

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case-study analysis of conversational interactions of 7 dyads working on the Force and Motion Microworld during a 10-week Grade 10 physics unit, with conceptual change assessed by pre-, post- and delayed post-test gains. The study reports that high-quality collaboration "helped foster conceptual change but did not necessarily ensure change."

> "Collaborative learning with high joint on-task engagement which was high in equality and mutuality helped foster conceptual change but did not necessarily ensure change. To achieve conceptual change it appeared that students needed to reflect on and reconstruct their conceptions."
