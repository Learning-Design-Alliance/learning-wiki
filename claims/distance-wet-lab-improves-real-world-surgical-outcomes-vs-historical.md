# Residents who took the course showed a 17% absolute increase in day one BCVA ≥20/60 outcomes compared with residents in the previous two years

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-course surgical outcomes of course participants were significantly better than those of senior-year residents in the two years before the course began. [→ Geary Amelia 2021](#geary-amelia-2021)

## Evidence

### Geary Amelia 2021

Geary Amelia, Wen Qing, Adrianzén Rosa, Congdon Nathan, Janani R., Haddad Danny, Timbo Clare Szalay, Khalifa Yousuf M. (2021). The impact of distance cataract surgical wet laboratory training on cataract surgical competency of ophthalmology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02659-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective two-proportion test comparing day one post-operative outcomes of the first surgeries by course participants against outcomes of senior residents in the two years before the course: "a 22.7% relative improvement in surgical outcomes" with an absolute increase of 17% (95% CI 1.5–32.5%, P = 0.012).

> "This represents a 22.7% relative improvement in surgical outcomes, (an absolute increase of 17% (95% CI 1.5–32.5%, P = 0.012, two-proportions test)) for second and final year residents having taken the course over final year residents in the previous 2 years."
