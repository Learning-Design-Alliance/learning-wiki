### Katherine L. Jones 2022

Katherine L. Jones, Mei Zhou and Dhanisha J. Jhaveri. (2022). Dissecting the role of adult hippocampal neurogenesis towards resilience versus susceptibility to stress-related mood disorders. npj Science of Learning 7:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00133-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review of the few gain-of-function studies. The review reports neural precursor cell-selective Bax deletion expanded adult-born neurons with no baseline mood improvement, while chemogenetic stimulation of adult-born neurons alleviated depressive-like behaviour following uncontrolled chronic mild stress.

> "Although no baseline improvement in mood was found, enhancing the level of neurogenesis using this approach was shown to promote behavioural resilience to chronic corticosterone administration46 and chronic social defeat stress88."
