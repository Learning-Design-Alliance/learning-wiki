## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Aziz Amaaz 2024

Aziz Amaaz, Abderrahman Mouradi, Moahamed Erradi, Ali Allouch. (2024). Moroccan pre-service elementary teachers: attitudes toward STEM education and mobile devices. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE). https://doi.org/10.11591/ijere.v13i5.28205

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Four Mann-Whitney U tests comparing 81 second-year and 145 third-year BEES students; Table 5 reports overall attitude U=5416, z=-0.971, p=0.332, with no significant differences on any dimension.

> "The first test indicated no statistically significant difference between the mean scores of the second - and third -year students' responses to all of the items used to measure attitudes toward integrated STEM education."
