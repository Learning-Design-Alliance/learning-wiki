Danni Chen 2024¶

Danni Chen, Tao Xia, Ziqing Yao, Lingqi Zhang & Xiaoqing Hu. (2024). Modulating social learning-induced evaluation updating during human sleep. npj Science of Learning 9:43. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00255-5

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Delayed-phase ANOVA comparing cued vs. uncued snacks three days after learning: the TMR effect was a "non-signi ﬁcant trend," with cued snacks numerically higher in evaluation change. Neither feedback nor interaction effects were significant at this phase.

Danni Chen 2024 (2)¶

Danni Chen, Tao Xia, Ziqing Yao, Lingqi Zhang & Xiaoqing Hu. (2024). Modulating social learning-induced evaluation updating during human sleep. npj Science of Learning 9:43. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00255-5

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Familiarity-rating ANOVA at the 3-day delayed session: cueing "signiﬁcantly enhanced familiarity ratings" of cued snacks, F(1, 33) = 8.28, p = 0.007, η²G = 0.03, but not at post-learning or post-TMR tests (ps > 0.116). The authors attribute the numerically higher delayed evaluations of cued snacks to this familiarity.
