`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-level results from the 402-participant cross-sectional survey (Table 2). The authors suggest higher recognition of CPR ratio and compression location may stem from passive observational learning during wartime resuscitations.

> "High BLS knowledge was observed in areas such as the meaning of AED (84.08%), the adult chest compression-to-breath ratio (91.04%), and the management of myocardial infarction (84.83%). Moderate understanding was found in items such as the initial penetrating trauma response (68.41%) and airway management in neck injuries (72.64%)."
