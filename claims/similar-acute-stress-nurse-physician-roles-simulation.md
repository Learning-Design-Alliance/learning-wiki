## Evidence

### Dias Roger Daglius 2017

Dias Roger Daglius, Scalabrini-Neto Augusto. (2017). Acute stress in residents playing different roles during emergency simulations: a preliminary study. Int J Med Educ 8:239-243. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5929.60f1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional study of 16 residents in 24 simulations; Mann-Whitney U tests compared percent variation of stress markers from baseline (T1) to post-scenario (T2). Cortisol and α-amylase comparisons were "U = 64.0, p=0.08" and "U = 23.0, p=0.08"; heart rate, blood pressures and interleukin-1β were also non-significant. No effect sizes were printed.

> "cortisol: 35.3% (IQR: 22.2-83.5) versus 42.3% (IQR: 12.4-133.8), diff: -7.0 (95%CI -80.5 to 66.5), U = 64.0, p=0.08; α-amylase: -5.4% (IQR: -62.70-73.90) versus 37.5% (IQR: 11.0-186.8), diff: -42.9 (95%CI -186.3 to 100.6), U = 23.0, p=0.08"
