Theoretical argument in the editorial: even if extremists were right that many brain cells could be developed this way, the author argues the net result would not augment intelligence. No empirical evidence is offered for this claim.

> "To take the doctrine so literally as to advocate the presentation to the infant of as many as possible of the thousands of sense impressions themselves (the 11,000 tones, the 35,000 colors, the four tastes, etc.) is a manifest absurdity"
