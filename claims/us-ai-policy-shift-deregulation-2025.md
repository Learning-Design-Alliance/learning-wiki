Documentary analysis of US AI regulatory documents found the Biden-era AI Bill of Rights (2022) and Executive Order 14110 (2023) were revoked in January 2025. The replacement order "emphasized sustaining and enhancing America's global AI dominance" and does not establish binding regulations.

> "in January 2025, Executive Order 14110 was revoked by President Trump. On January 23, 2025, a new Executive Order titled Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence was issued"
