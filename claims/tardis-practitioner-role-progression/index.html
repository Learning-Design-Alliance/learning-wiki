The article's observational account of the TARDIS project, which used knowledge elicitation across three yearly stages to build practitioners' self-observation and self-reporting skills. Practitioners' roles "changed from those of willing informants (the beginning of the project), through advisors and co-designers of the TARDIS system (middle of the project)" toward independent lead-practitioners. No objective measures are offered; the author labels the concluding interpretation an opinion.
