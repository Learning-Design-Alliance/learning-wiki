## Evidence

### de Zeeuw Eveline L. 2020

de Zeeuw Eveline L., Hottenga Jouke-Jan, Ouwens Klaasjan G., Dolan Conor V., Ehli Erik A., Davies Gareth E., Boomsma Dorret I., van Bergen Elsje. (2020). Intergenerational Transmission of Education and ADHD: Effects of Parental Genotypes. Behavior Genetics, Volume 50, Issue 4. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10519-020-09992-w

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Model 2 regressions of the three childhood outcomes on transmitted and non-transmitted ADHD PGSs: the transmitted ADHD PGS "was not associated with academic achievement (β = − 0.08, p = 0.022)" at the adopted alpha of 0.01, but significantly predicted symptoms at home (β = 0.17) and school (β = 0.13).

> "was not associated with academic achievement (β = − 0.08, p = 0.022), but had a significant influence on ADHD symptoms at home (β = 0.17, p < 0.001) and ADHD symptoms at school (β = 0.13, p < 0.001)"
