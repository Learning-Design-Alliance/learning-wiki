CP growth models (Table 5) estimated summer change between 4th and 5th grade under three adjustment approaches. The article reports "an average drop of 1.68 RIT points over the summer" unadjusted versus 1.31 filtered and 1.47 effort-moderated, significant only for filtering.

"students in the unadjusted sample have an average drop of 1.68 RIT points over the summer compared to an average drop of 1.31 RIT points in the filtered sample and 1.47 points in the effort-moderated model."
