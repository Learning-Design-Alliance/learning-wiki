## Evidence

### Julien Bobroff 2015

Julien Bobroff, Frédéric Bouquet. (2015). A project-based course about outreach in a physics curriculum. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/37/4/045704](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/37/4/045704)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective survey of the outreach course sent to all 52 participants over three years; 25 answered. Students rated course interest at "an average of 4.5(0.6) on a Likert scale from 1 to 5" and 96% would not have chosen another elective. No effect size is reported.

> "Students assessed their interest in the course at an average of 4.5(0.6) on a Likert scale from 1 to 5 [17]. When asked if they would have preferred to choose another elective instead, 96% answered ‘no’ and 4% ‘without opinion’."
