### Brivael Hémon 2025

Brivael Hémon, Kevin de Checchi, Amaël Arguel, Bastien Trémolière, & Franck Amadieu. (2025). Relationships Between Critical Thinking Dispositions and Evaluation of Videos with Variation in the Level of Expertise of the Source Among Middle and High School Students. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105274

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analyses (footnote 2, Results §4.2) applying the same regressions to the decision to use videos in a school presentation; "the use of videos featuring laypersons was only predicted by the age of the student (β = -.17, p = .003)". No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The use of videos featuring experts was only predicted by need for evidence for facts (β = .17, p = .011) and the use of videos featuring laypersons was only predicted by the age of the student (β = -.17, p = .003)"
