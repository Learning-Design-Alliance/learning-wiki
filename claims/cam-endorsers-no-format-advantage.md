### Swire-Thompson 2025

Swire-Thompson, Briony, Butler, Lucy, & Rapp, David N. (2025). The truth sandwich format does not enhance the correction of misinformation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 117(6), 851–862. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000961

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Subgroup reanalysis of Experiment 1 restricted to participants in "the top tertile of the CAMBI-NH" replicated all findings, "indicating benefits of both correction formats but no differential effects"; the same pattern held in Experiment 2.

> "When analyses were isolated to only participants who scored in the top tertile of the CAMBI-NH, all findings replicated, indicating benefits of both correction formats but no differential effects (see Supplement D for full details)."
