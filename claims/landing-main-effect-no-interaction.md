## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The landing task showed a significant main effect of training method (η² = 0.35) but no significant training method × measurement time interaction (η² = 0.03). [→ Zhang Yang 2025](#zhang-yang-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhang Yang 2025

Zhang Yang, Chen Jie, Zhong Xiao, Shao Yongcong, Fu Weiwei. (2025). Evaluating the effectiveness of flight simulator training on developing perceptual-motor skills among flight cadets: a pilot study. Scientific Reports 15, 28062. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-12929-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA of landing scores in the 2×8 randomized design (n = 40). The article reports the training-method main effect "η² = 0.35" and "no significant interaction", adding that "as flight task difficulty increased, both groups showed slower performance improvements".

> "significant main effects for training method (F (1, 38) = 20.05, p <0.001, η² = 0.35, 95% CI [0.151, 0.511]), measurement time (F (3.20, 121.75) = 108.55, p < 0.001, η² = 0.74, 95% CI [0.699, 0.772]), and no significant interaction between training method and measurement time"
