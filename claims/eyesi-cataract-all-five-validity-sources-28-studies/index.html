Lee Roxanne 2020¶

Lee Roxanne, Raison Nicholas, Lau Wai Yan, Aydin Abdullatif, Dasgupta Prokar, Ahmed Kamran, Haldar Shreya. (2020). A systematic review of simulation-based training tools for technical and non-technical skills in ophthalmology. Eye. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41433-020-0832-1

The review's synthesis of the Eyesi cataract evidence base in its virtual reality results section, aggregating 28 studies against Messick's five validity parameters with the review's own 0–2 rating scale; response processes scored 1 and all other parameters scored 2.

"Twenty-eight studies assessed the Eyesi cataract training modules, collectively demonstrating all five sources of validity evidence, with data strongly supporting each parameter (score = 2) except for response processes, which had more limited evidence (score = 1) [11–38]."

Related Claims¶

  • Deuchler et al. found learning occurs with EyeSi simulator training but is higher when combined with an online theory platform — related
