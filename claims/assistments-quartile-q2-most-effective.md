# Achievement-quartile analysis shows the intervention was most effective for students in the 2nd quartile (25th–50th percentiles) of baseline achievement

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Q2 students demonstrated significantly higher math achievement (p = 0.045; effect size = 0.16); Q1 gains were marginal (p = 0.068; effect size = 0.10); Q3 and Q4 did not differ significantly from comparison peers (p = 0.288 and 0.104). [→ Feng 2025](#feng-2025)

## Evidence

### Feng 2025

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments companion report: Implementation analysis of the ASSISTments intervention. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677784

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quartile analysis of the quasi-experimental sample splitting students into four baseline MAP Growth quartiles. Only Q2 reached significance (p = 0.045, effect size = 0.16); the article concludes the intervention "was most effective for students in the 2nd quartile."

> "Students in Q1 ended the year with math scores marginally higher than those in the comparison group (p = 0.068; effect size = 0.10). Students in Q2 demonstrated significantly higher math achievement (p = 0.045; effect size = 0.16). By contrast, students in Q3 and Q4 did not differ significantly from their peers in the comparison group (p = 0.288 and 0.104, respectively)."
