# Touching objects heightens negative affect during exhibition visit

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Negative affect increased substantially from before the exhibition to after visiting the showrooms, with a large main effect of showroom (ηp² = 0.48), consistent with the abstract's report of heightened negative affect due to the haptic experience. [→ Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan 2021](#magdalena-novak-stephan-schwan-2021)

## Evidence

### Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan 2021

Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan. (2021). Does Touching Real Objects Affect Learning? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09551-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Three-way mixed ANOVA (vision × haptics × showroom) on PANAS negative affect showed "a significant main effect for factor showroom" with ηp² = 0.48; negative affect rose from before the exhibition (e.g., M = 1.21–1.43) to after showroom 1 (M = 1.81–2.23). No significant main effects of vision or haptics were found.
