## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among single-assessment-type studies, mean MERSQI scores differed significantly by assessment type (p < 0.001); knowledge studies had a median MERSQI of 11.5 versus 9.5 for perception and 8 for other, with Tukey's HSD differences between knowledge and perception (p = 0.004) and knowledge and other (p < 0.001). ## Evidence

### Taylor 2023

Taylor, T.A.H., Kemp, K., Mi, M., & Lerchenfeldt, S. (2023). Self-directed learning assessment practices in undergraduate health professions education: a systematic review. Medical Education Online, 28(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2023.2189553

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

One-Way ANOVA with Tukey's HSD on the 69 studies using one assessment type (42 perception, 13 knowledge, 14 other). "There was a statistically significant difference of mean MERSQI quality scores between study types (p < 0.001)".

> "There was a statistically significant difference of mean MERSQI quality scores between study types (p < 0.001). Studies classified as perception had a median (IQR) of 9.5 (10.5 to 7.6), whereas studies classified as knowledge had a median (IQR) of 11.5 (12.5 to 10.5) and other had a median (IQR) of 8 (10 to 7)."
