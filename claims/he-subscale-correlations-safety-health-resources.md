Follow-up exploratory Pearson correlations (Supplemental Figure 3) examined H/E subscales against outcomes predicted by the H/E index, conducted with the NSSD and unaffected samples as separate groups; the WMI correlation was r=.388 and the Spelling correlation was r=.337.

> "In the NSSD group, Safety-Related Resources were significantly related to the Wechsler WMI (r=.388, p=.021). In the unaffected group, Health Resources were significantly related to Spelling (r=.337, p=.006)."
