## Evidence

### Kasvosve I 2014

Kasvosve I, Ledikwe JH, Phumaphi O, Mpofu M, Nyangah R, Motswaledi MS, Martin R, Semo B-w. (2014). Continuing professional development training needs of medical laboratory personnel in Botswana. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1478-4491-12-46

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Format-preference results of the 275-respondent cross-sectional survey (Figure 1), in which participants selected any three of seven listed delivery options. The survey reports "training workshops (218/275 participants)" as the top choice and workplace journal clubs least preferred at 32 selections.

> "The top three preferred CPD delivery modes, in rank order, were training workshops (218/275 participants), hands-on workshops (163/275 participants), and internet-based learning (156/275 participants) (Figure 1). A journal club at the workplace was the least preferred method of delivery and was selected by only 32 participants."
