Narrative review synthesizing empirical research on ultrasound simulation; the review reports trainees "demonstrate significant improvements in image acquisition, interpretation, and procedural skills" and reduced performance anxiety among novices.

### Recker F 2024 (2)

Recker F, Neubauer R, Dong Y, Gschmack AM, Jenssen C, Möller K, Blaivas M, Ignacio Prats M, Lucius C, Ruppert J, Sänger SL, Sirli R, Weimer J, Westerway SC, Zervides C, Dietrich CF. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review describing feedback mechanisms of simulation tools; combined mannikin- and computer-based tools compare acquired image angles with a standard image, offering constant feedback, in contexts where trainer shortages limit individual feedback.

> "In contrast, simulators offer continuous and immediate feedback for the students [38]. Combined mannikin- and computer-based simulation tools are able compare the angle of the acquired images with the angle of a standard image and calculate any differences offering constant feedback on the quality of acquired images."
