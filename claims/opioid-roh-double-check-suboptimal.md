`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of double-check behaviour across seven standardised items after each of the 86 paired preparation exercises, scored by the two educator-assessors. The authors interpret this as reflecting "a lack of structured and systematic approach".

> "The double-check performance was low, with a median of 1 out of 7 items performed. The dosage check was the most frequently completed item (48%), and all remaining items were completed by fewer than 30% of participants."
