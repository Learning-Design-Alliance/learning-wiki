`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported confidence on a 1–10 scale in the same two-session within-subject study of 40 dental students; medians rose at every caries depth and overall, all significant at p < 0.001 (Wilcoxon test).

> "Students’ confidence improved notably for enamel caries (4.0 to 6.0), dentin caries (5.0 to 7.0), and pulp-involved caries (8.0 to 8.5), as well as overall detection (5.0 to 7.0) (p < 0.001)."
