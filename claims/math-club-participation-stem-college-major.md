# Math club participation is associated with higher odds of declaring a STEM major in college, remaining significant after propensity score matching

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` High school math club participation is positively associated with the probability of having a STEM college major, with odds tempered from roughly 3-to-1 to roughly 2-to-1 after covariates and still significant in all three matching models. [→ Gottfried 2013](#gottfried-2013)

## Evidence

### Gottfried 2013

Gottfried, M. A. & Williams, D. (2013). STEM Club Participation and STEM Schooling Outcomes. Education Policy Analysis Archives, 21 (79). http://epaa.asu.edu/ojs/article/view/1361

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression and propensity-matched models predicting STEM major selection from Table 7. Math club odds ratios were 2.766 (no covariates), 2.187 (full model), and 1.232, 1.404, and 1.260 under block, nearest-neighbor, and kernel matching, all statistically significant.

> "The inclusion of a full set of covariates, however, did temper the effect math or club participation's positive association in both subject areas from approximately 3-to-1 odds to approximately 2-to-1 odds for math club. Nonetheless, the coefficients remained statistically significant."
