## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Most of 118 surveyed research-active mathematicians agreed that publication in a journal, journal quality, or a trusted authority increased their confidence that a proof is correct. [→ Keith Weber 2013](#keith-weber-2013)

## Evidence

### Keith Weber 2013

Keith Weber, Matthew Inglis & Juan Pablo Mejia-Ramos. (2013). How mathematicians obtain conviction: Implications for mathematics instruction and research on epistemic cognition. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2013.865527

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A survey of 118 research-active mathematicians (Mejia-Ramos & Weber) asked agreement on five-point Likert items about sources of confidence in proofs. The authors report "72% of the participants agreed with C1" and 83% agreed with C3; no effect size is printed.

> "We found that 72% of the participants agreed with C1 (12% disagreed), 67% agreed with C2 (17% disagreed), and 83% agreed with C3 (7% disagreed)."
