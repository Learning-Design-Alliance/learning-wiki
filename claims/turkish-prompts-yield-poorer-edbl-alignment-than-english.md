### Karaismailoglu 2026

Karaismailoglu, F., Surmeli, H., & Yildirim, M. (2026). Suitability of Artificial Intelligence Supported Lesson Plans from the Perspective of Science Education Experts. Journal of Science Education and Technology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10956-026-10342-4

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot observation during prompt development, reported in the Methodology. Both platforms prompted in Turkish produced plans that "did not reflect the iterative, process-oriented nature of EDBL," so the final prompt was written in English. No systematic test was reported.

> "pilot testing revealed that both AI platforms (ChatGPT-4 and Teacher's Buddy) failed to adequately interpret the EDBL framework when prompted in Turkish. The generated lesson plans did not reflect the iterative, process-oriented nature of EDBL."
