A meta-analytic review's own synthesis of nine single-case TCSM studies: the reviewers calculated PND, PEM and Phi indices because original authors reported almost none. The review reports "Phis ranged from 0.18 to 0.89 (Md = 0.65)" with mostly large magnitudes, but prints no standardized effect size in d/g/r terms, so no magnitude code is assigned.
