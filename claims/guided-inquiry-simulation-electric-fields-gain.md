## Evidence

### Espinoza 2020

Espinoza, F. (2020). Impact of Guided Inquiry with Simulations on Knowledge of Electricity and Wave Phenomena. European Scientific Journal, ESJ, 16 (33), 1. https://doi.org/10.19044/esj.2020.v16n33p1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Study II: 24 students completed an 8-question electric fields pre/post assessment after sketching field lines in an online simulator and playing Electric Field Hockey. The paired t-test was significant, "t = 6.70, p <.001", with a large Cohen's d = 1.98 reported in Table 5.

> "for the Electric Fields it is t = 6.70, p <.001, and for the wave characteristics  t = 5.28, p <.001"
