Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir. (2025). ChatGPT-Assisted Lesson Planning for Children's STEAM Arts Education: An Experimental Study on Benefits, Challenges, Methods, and a Prompt Framework. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00513-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire and interview data on teachers' perceptions of ChatGPT's role in innovation. The article reports users were neutral on innovation and that "ChatGPT is more likely to serve as a source of inspiration", attributing this to its Transformer architecture trained on internet-collected data.

> "Rather than being able to generate entirely inno - vative content, ChatGPT is more likely to serve as a source of inspiration."
