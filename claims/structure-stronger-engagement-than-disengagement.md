This comparison across the meta-analytic syntheses of correlational and intervention studies contrasted the engagement findings (r = .28; g = 0.46) with disengagement findings (-.08 non-significant; g = -0.34), reporting "stronger for engagement than disengagement". No separate effect size for the contrast itself is printed.
