## Evidence

### Jayasinghe S 2016

Jayasinghe S. (2016). Describing complex clinical scenarios at the bed-side: Is a systems science approach useful? Exploring a novel diagrammatic approach to facilitate clinical reasoning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0787-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of CRM with 289 fifth- and fourth-year medical students, reported in the utility section. The article states "245 (85.5 %) perceived CRM to be useful to improve their understanding of the case" because it clearly presented linkages and summarized information. The same section reports no clear recall advantage over diagnosis lists and that 83.9% were keen to learn the technique.
