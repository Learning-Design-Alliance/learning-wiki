## Evidence

### Connolly Murray 2026

Connolly Murray, Iohom Gabriella, O’Brien Niall, Volz James, O’Muircheartaigh Aogán, Serchan Paschalitsa, Shorten George. (2026). Delivering remote procedural training on peripheral intravenous cannulation to medical students using the Microsoft Hololens 2: a mixed-methods evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08648-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear regression and Pearson correlation analysis of MRT-A scores against competency-score change for the eight HL-group students. The correlation was non-significant (r = 0.29, p = 0.48, R² = 0.08); no effect size beyond the correlation is printed.

> "Students’ visuospatial skills, as measured by the MRT-A, were not significantly associated with (pre-to post training) change in assessment scores, as illustrated in Fig. 3. Correlation coefficient was calculated as 0.29, p = 0.48. R-squared was calculated as 0.08."
