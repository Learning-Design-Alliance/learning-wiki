Evidence¶

Man Su 2025¶

Man Su, Belle Dang, Andy Nguyen & Tomohiro Nagashima. (2025). Choice-making in an adaptive learning system with motivational pedagogical agents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00366-7

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Two-way ANOVA on posttest scores in the 49-student classroom study, with condition (Agent vs. Non-Agent) and prior knowledge (HPK vs. LPK) as factors. Prior knowledge was a significant main effect; the condition main effect and the condition × prior knowledge interaction were not significant.

"Results indicated a signi ﬁcant main effect of prior knowledge on posttest scores, F(1, 45) = 16.93,p < 0.001, showing that HPK students achieved signiﬁcantly higher posttest scores compared to LPK peers."

Man Su 2025 (2)¶

Man Su, Belle Dang, Andy Nguyen & Tomohiro Nagashima. (2025). Choice-making in an adaptive learning system with motivational pedagogical agents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00366-7

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Two-way ANOVA on pre-post learning gains in the same 49-student classroom study. The main effect of prior knowledge was significant, with LPK students demonstrating "greater improvements due to lower baseline scores".

"For learning gains, there was also a significant main effect of prior knowledge, F(1, 45) = 14.98, p < 0.001, with LPK students demonstrating greater improvements due to lower baseline scores (see Table1)."
