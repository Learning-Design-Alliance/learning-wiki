## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, Y., & Dyson, B. J. (2026). Recover or discover: How outcome feedback shapes response patterns within game spaces. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00414-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learning x Phase interaction in Experiment 1's three-way repeated measures ANOVA (n = 76): OBR dropped when the win rate was fixed at 50% and recovered when exploitable structure returned; no effect size printed.

> "OBR was significantly reduced from Pre to During phases, consistent with the reduction in win rate from Pre to During phases. OBR also significantly rose from During to Post, consistent with the release of the fixed win rate."

### Zhang 2026 (2)

Zhang, Y., & Dyson, B. J. (2026). Recover or discover: How outcome feedback shapes response patterns within game spaces. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00414-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 (25% condition, n = 72 analysed) ANOVA produced significant main effects of Phase and Outcome only; the absence of interaction with Learning supported the claim that severe punishment eliminated the relearning-old advantage.

> "OBR was also higher in both Pre- (66.71%) and Post- (68.88%) phases relative to During (56.13%; Tukey's HSD, p < .05)."
