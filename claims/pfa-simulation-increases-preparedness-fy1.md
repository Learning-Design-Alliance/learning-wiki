BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08477-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post questionnaire analysis (N = 248) of preparedness for starting FY1, analysed with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test. The article reports "Standardised effect size was large (0.572)" and a 1-point median improvement.

> "Students reported feeling more prepared for starting work as an FY1 after the course (Mdn = 4, somewhat agree; IQR1 = 4.00, IQR3 = 4.00) compared to before the course (Mdn = 3, neither agree nor disagree; IQR1 = 3.00, IQR3 = 4.00) using the Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Test (W = 278.50, z = 9.02, p < 0.001)"
