`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The editorial cites the author's own pilot study (Shiner) on first-year diagnostic radiography students' emotional preparedness. On unpacking in debriefing, students reported a wish to 'step up, not step back'. The editorial concludes the affective domain can derail the cognitive and psychomotor domains.

> "When presented with simulated patients suffering open wounds (simulated using moulage) and in pain, students shifted into observational roles, limiting their communication with the patient in fear of making the situation worse."
