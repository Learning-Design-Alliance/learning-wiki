Stelling H 2026¶

Stelling H, Burford B, Park S, McGurk L, Brown M E L, Vance G. (2026). How do doctors build knowledge around obstetric emergencies? A scoping review. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2026-119725

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis across the scoping review's training domain: simulation spans "low-cost, improvised models" to "advanced, immersive virtual reality systems", with standardised commercial models being most common. No formal quality appraisal was undertaken.

"Simulation—delivered in both educational and workplace contexts—dominates the literature (online supplemental file S6A), encompassing methods from low-cost, improvised models (OE1368, OE130,9 OE215)10 to advanced, immersive virtual reality systems"

Stelling H 2026 (2)¶

Stelling H, Burford B, Park S, McGurk L, Brown M E L, Vance G. (2026). How do doctors build knowledge around obstetric emergencies? A scoping review. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2026-119725

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis in the training domain: the review reports that "evidence for sustained behaviour change and clear demonstration of clinical impact remains limited". Simulation can improve team performance and show potential to contribute to better clinical outcomes per the review.

"However, evidence for sustained behaviour change and clear demonstration of clinical impact remains limited (OE207,15 OE28,16 online supplemental file S6B)."
