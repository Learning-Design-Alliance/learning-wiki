q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interaction Analysis case study of Group 1 in the Black-accent condition. The group probed Phoenix's role during the icebreaker, later treated it as a validating authority, and after persistent ranking errors questioned its competence, lowered expectations, and disengaged.

"However, Pheonix's use as validating authority ultimately led to a trust breakdown when it inaccurately tracked the group's decisions."
