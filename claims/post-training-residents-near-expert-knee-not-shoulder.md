## Evidence

### Rahm Stefan 2018

Rahm Stefan, Wieser Karl, Bauer David E., Waibel Felix WA, Meyer Dominik C., Gerber Christian, Fucentese Sandro F. (2018). Efficacy of standardized training on a virtual reality simulator to advance knee and shoulder arthroscopic motor skills. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-018-2072-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of five fellowship-trained experts against 20 post-training residents. Knee z-scores did not differ significantly (p = 0.192); in shoulder tasks the experts were significantly better in all but one parameter, with the article reporting p < 0.001 for the overall shoulder z-score contrast.

> "The experts reached a higher mean knee z-score with 0.00 (range, − 0.69 to 0.83; SD 0.61) than the post training residents result with − 0.87 (range, − 4.48 to 0.66; SD 1.50) not reaching statistical significance; p = 0.192."
