## Evidence

### Ana Cebollero-Salinas 2022

Ana Cebollero-Salinas, Jacobo Cano-Escoriaza, Santos Orejudo. (2022). Are socio-emotional e-competencies protective factors against habitual digital behaviors (media multitasking, phubbing, cybergossip) in Spanish secondary students?. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104464

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group structural equation model (ADF estimation) on n = 776 students, with the three behaviors as one endogenous factor. Standardised path weights show e-self-control's predictive role decreases with age, consistent with the authors' interpretation that impulse regulation becomes less discriminating as cognitive regulation matures.

> "The most important ones are emotional e-regulation, both for boys and for girls (β = 0.39), as well as for the two academic year age groups (β = 0.34), as well as e-regulation of impulsiveness, with the same factorial weight according to gender (β = 0.35), but with a greater relevance for 1st/2 nd year students (β = 0.45) than for 3rd/4th year students (β = 0.24)."
