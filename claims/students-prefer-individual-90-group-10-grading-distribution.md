## Evidence

### Nguyen 2026

Nguyen, A. H., Esparza, C., Darmadi, M., Boedeker, P. J., & Pinkas, A. (2026). Medical Student Preferences on the Anatomy of a Practical Exam. Clinical Anatomy. https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.70114

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of the same cohort (n = 114) using a proportional allocation scale; I90/G10 was the most frequently chosen grading distribution, followed by I50/G50 and I70/G30, a statistically significant distribution by chi-square test.

> "the three most common answers were I90/G10 (48 participants, 42.1%), I50/G50 (26 participants, 22.8%), and I70/G30 (10 participants, 8.8%) (χ2(8) = 114.97, p < 0.001, Figure 1D)"
