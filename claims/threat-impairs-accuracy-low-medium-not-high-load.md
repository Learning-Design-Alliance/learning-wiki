# Threat of an aversive scream lowers verbal working memory accuracy at low and medium load but not at high load in both adolescents and adults

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Accuracy was lower during threat than safe conditions in the 1-back and 2-back tasks, but did not differ between conditions in the 3-back task, across age groups. [→ Patel Nilam 2016](#patel-nilam-2016)

## Evidence

### Patel Nilam 2016

Patel Nilam, Vytal Katherine, Pavletic Nevia, Stoodley Catherine, Pine Daniel S., Grillon Christian, Ernst Monique. (2016). Interaction of Threat and Verbal Working Memory in Adolescents. Psychophysiology. https://doi.org/10.1111/psyp.12582

`q3 · i?`

Laboratory experiment with 25 adolescents and 25 adults performing a verbal n-back task under threat-of-scream and safe blocks in a within-subjects design. Follow-up tests of the Condition by Load interaction showed "accuracy was lower for the threat than safe conditions in the 1-back and 2-back tasks (ps < .05)" with no difference at 3-back.

> "When each load was examined separately, we found that accuracy was lower for the threat than safe conditions in the 1-back and 2-back tasks (ps < .05), but not for the 3-back task (p > .1), in which accuracy was similar for the threat and safe conditions"
