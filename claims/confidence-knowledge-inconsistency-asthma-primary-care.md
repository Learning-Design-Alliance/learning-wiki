Çifci İ., Gök U., Yorgancıoğlu A. (2025). A current assessment of family physicians’ knowledge and attitudes toward asthma management in primary care in Turkey according to the GINA strategy report. NPJ Primary Care Respiratory Medicine 36, 5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41533-025-00468-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation (discussion) of attitude items in the survey of 596 physicians: only 27.7% felt confident diagnosing adult asthma, 11.9% felt they had sufficient medication knowledge, and 6.7% actively participated in follow-up including medication changes. This is an interpretive claim, not a tested result.

> "One of the most important findings in our study is the inconsistency between participants’ high self-perceptions of managing asthma treatment and their low self-confidence in diagnosing or treating the disease."
