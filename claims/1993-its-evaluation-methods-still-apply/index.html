Jim Greer & Mary Mark 2015¶

Jim Greer & Mary Mark. (2015). Evaluation Methods for Intelligent Tutoring Systems Revisited. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:387–392. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0043-2

q2 · i? · review · r2

The authors' retrospective assessment, as a survey paper, of their own 1993 IJAIED article: it "still holds up well today" and its basic techniques remain in use, now applied to adaptive learning environments beyond stand-alone ITSs.

"The 1993 paper in IJAIED on evaluation methods for Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS) still holds up well today. Basic evaluation techniques described in that paper remain in use."
