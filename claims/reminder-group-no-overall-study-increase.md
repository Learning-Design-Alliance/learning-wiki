## Evidence

### Lea Nobbe 2024

Lea Nobbe, Jasmin Breitwieser, Daniel Biedermann & Garvin Brod. (2024). Smartphone-based study reminders can be a double-edged sword. npj Science of Learning 9:40. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00253-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group logistic mixed-effects model on daily log-file data in the same preregistered intervention (n = 42 Control, n = 43 Reminder). The article reports the groups "did not differ in their likelihood to study on a given day" (p = 0.106); no effect size is printed.

> "the Reminder and Control Group did not differ in their likelihood to study on a given day ( b = −0.64, 95% CI [−1.41, 0.14], χ2(1) = 2.56,p = 0.106)."
