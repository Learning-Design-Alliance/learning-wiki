### Ian Robottom and Sharifah Norhaidah 2008

Ian Robottom and Sharifah Norhaidah. (2008). Western science and Islamic learners: When disciplines and culture intersect. Journal of Research in International Education 7(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/1475240908091302

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey findings from the Malaysian study of 549 Form 4 students, in which epistemological beliefs were elicited with Schommer's 63-item questionnaire and discord with a purpose-built questionnaire. The article reports that "discord scores were inversely related to epistemological belief scores."

> "The findings of this first study show that discord scores were inversely related to epistemological belief scores. Students with more sophisticated epistemological belief scores show less discord while students with naïve epistemological belief scores (low sophistication) show more discord."
