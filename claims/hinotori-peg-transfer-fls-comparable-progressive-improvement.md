### Iwabuchi Serena 2025

Peg-transfer FLS results from the ten-surgeon crossover simulation study; medians are reported with Wilcoxon signed-rank p values and no effect size. The article states "the Robo group demonstrated a trend toward progressive improvement across trials" (Fig. 2).

> "FLS scores did not differ significantly between the Robo and Lap groups across the three trials (1st: 167.5 vs 174, p = 0.21; 2nd: 174.5 vs 198.5, p = 0.15; 3rd: 178 vs 191, p = 0.26; total: 519.5 vs 531, p = 0.24)"
