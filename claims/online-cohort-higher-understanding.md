## Evidence

### Callaghan 2025

Callaghan, K., Kestin, G., Klales, A., McCarty, L., & Deslauriers, L. (2025). Active Learning Through Flexible Collaborative Exams: Improving STEM Assessments. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.01994](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.01994)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Understanding self-reflection ratings (out of 4 problems) collected immediately after the Midterm 2 collaborative exam in the quantum mechanics course. Online: 3.87 ± 0.07; in-person: 3.48 ± 0.18; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "the online cohort from Midterm 2 perceived a higher level of understanding following the collaborative exam (3.87 ± 0.07 out of 4 problems) in contrast to the in-person group (3.48 ± 0.18 out of 4 problems), with a p-value less than 0.05."
