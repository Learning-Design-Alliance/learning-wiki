Wilkes S. 2026¶

Wilkes S., Maggio L. A., Martin P. C., Melton J., Zheng B. (2026). Self-Directed Learning in Health Professions Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2128

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression moderator analysis across 74 effect sizes comparing studies where SDL was the experimental condition versus the control condition. The difference was "statistically significant (F(1, 72) = 8.77, p = 0.004)".

"SDL treated as experimental groups showed a significantly larger effect (d = 0.54, 95% CI [0.24, 0.84], r = 0.50) compared to comparator groups (d = –0.27, 95% CI [–0.75, 0.22], r = –0.26), with this difference being statistically significant (F(1, 72) = 8.77, p = 0.004)."
