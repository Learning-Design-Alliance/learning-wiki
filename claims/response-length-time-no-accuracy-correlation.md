`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis across the pooled chatbot responses on the DUS benchmark. The printed correlations are small: r = −0.032 (length–accuracy, ns), r = −0.043 (time–accuracy, ns), and a weak significant positive r = 0.093 between response time and question length.

> "no significant correlation was observed between response length and accuracy (r = − 0.032, p = 0.215), suggesting that increased response length did not necessarily correspond to higher accuracy. Likewise, the association between response time and accuracy was not statistically significant (r = − 0.043, p = 0.095)."
