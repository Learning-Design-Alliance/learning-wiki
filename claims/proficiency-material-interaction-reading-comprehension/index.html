Gao X 2026¶

Gao X (2026) From unified to differentiated materials: generative AI–supported adaptation of EAP reading materials. Front. Psychol. 17:1887565. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1887565

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Two-way ANOVA (Table 7) on reading comprehension with proficiency, material condition, and their interaction in a 3×3 between-subjects design (N = 135). The interaction "F(4, 126) = 7.43, p < 0.001, and η2 p = 0.191" located heterogeneity across proficiency strata.

"reading comprehension varied by proﬁciency, F(2, 126) = 56.81, p < 0.001, and η2 p = 0.474, material condition, F(2, 126) = 8.81, p < 0.001, and η2 p = 0.123, and Proﬁciency× Material, F(4, 126) = 7.43, p < 0.001, and η2 p = 0.191."
