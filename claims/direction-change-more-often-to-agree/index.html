Evidence¶

Schnaubert 2021¶

Schnaubert, L., Krukowski, S., & Bodemer, D. (2021). Assumptions and confidence of others: the impact of socio-cognitive information on metacognitive self-regulation. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09269-5

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Within-subject experiment comparing change-to-agree versus change-to-disagree occurrences per participant. Learners changed on average 2.3 times to agree and 1.13 times to disagree; the paired t-test found people changed "significantly more often to agree with a partner than to disagree (t(59) = 4.44, p < 0.001, d = 0.58)".

"We conducted a t-test for paired samples to test for differences in occurrence and found a highly significant difference with people changing their assumptions significantly more often to agree with a partner than to disagree (t(59) = 4.44, p < 0.001, d = 0.58)."
