In the RCT's comparative analysis of the Standard Cases Assessment Questionnaire, the PainScan group's median post-intervention SCA total was 9 (8, 9) versus 7 (6, 8) in the on-site group, with the authors reporting "superior skill development" for PainScan.

> "Compared to traditional on‐site training, the PainScan showed superior skill development (Z = −3.644, p < 0.001)"
