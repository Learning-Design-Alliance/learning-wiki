Zenani, N.E., Bello, B., Molekodi, M., & Useh, U., 2022, 'Effectiveness of school-based CPR training among adolescents to enhance knowledge and skills in CPR: A systematic review', Curationis 45(1), a2325. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v45i1.2325

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Characteristics synthesis of the 14 included studies. The review reports the majority used instructor-led training with video simulations or theoretical training, and that "the outcome measure used by most of the studies was subjective questionnaires"; few studies used virtual reality and video gaming.

> "In the participating schools, the interventions used to assess the effects of CPR among adolescents in the majority of the studies were mainly instructor-led basic CPR training with video simulations of chest compressions or basic CPR theoretical training."
