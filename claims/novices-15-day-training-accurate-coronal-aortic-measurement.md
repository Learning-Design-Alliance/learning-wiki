## Evidence

### Nguyen Anh TV 2013

Nguyen Anh TV, Hill Geraldine B, Versteeg Matthew PT, Thomson Ian A, van Rij Andre M. (2013). Novices may be trained to screen for abdominal aortic aneurysms using ultrasound. Cardiovascular Ultrasound. https://doi.org/10.1186/1476-7120-11-42

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Double-blinded prospective longitudinal study in which three novices trained for 15 days independently scanned 215 participants and were compared with experienced vascular sonographers. In the detailed results, 85-97% of novices' maximal coronal measurements were within 0.5 cm of the assessors', with variability of 0.47-0.50 cm (the abstract's "0.46-0.52 cm" matches the suprarenal mean difference in Table 1, not the coronal results); no standardised effect size is printed.

> "When imaging the maximal coronal aortic diameter, the novice's measurements were within 0.5 cm 85-97% of the time, with a variability ranging between 0.47-0.50 cm."
