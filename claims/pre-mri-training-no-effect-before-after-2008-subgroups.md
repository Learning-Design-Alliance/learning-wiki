Li Jie BS, Li Qiancheng MD, Dai Xiuhong MD, Li Jiong MD, Zhang Xinxian MD. (2019). Does pre-scanning training improve the image quality of children receiving magnetic resonance imaging? A meta-analysis of current studies. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000014323

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Subgroup meta-analysis of successful MRI scanning split by publication year, motivated by the authors' observation that newer machines cost less time and new sequences minimize artifacts. No differences were found in either subgroup; the authors note too few studies.

> "The respective analysis of studies before and after 2008 revealed no differences in successful MRI."
