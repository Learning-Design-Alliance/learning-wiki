### Preston Jones 2007

Preston Jones, Gerardo Munoz, Michael Ragsdale, Douglas Singleton. (2007). The general relativistic infinite plane. https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.2906 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0708.2906](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0708.2906)

Analytical derivation of geodesic equations in Section III for both metrics, showing velocity-dependent acceleration terms. The derivation finds the accelerations "have a depen dence on the velocity" and so do not generally reproduce the Newtonian result; no effect size applies.

> "However, unlike the Newtoni an case, both the 4-acceleration ( 16) as well as the acceleration ( 18) measured with clocks ﬁxed relative to the plane have a depen dence on the velocity, and do not therefore reproduce the Newtonian result in general."
