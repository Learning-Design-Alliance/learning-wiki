Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/roads-to-success-estimated-impacts-of-an-education-and-career-planning-program-during-middle-school

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The report describes these as exploratory analyses, not confirmatory main findings, for eighth graders from high-poverty rural schools exposed to Roads to Success. It reports "positive impacts on talking to school staff about career/school plans"; no effect size is printed.

> "However, exploratory analyses suggest positive impacts on talking to school staff about career/school plans and on gaining confidence in two areas: choosing careers and understanding what is required for career success."
