Evidence¶

Phuong 2017¶

Phuong, A. E., Nguyen, J., & Mariea, D. (2017). Evaluating an Adaptive Equity-Oriented Pedagogy: A Study of its Impacts in Higher Education. The Journal of Effective Teaching, 17(2), 5-44. https://uncw.edu/jet

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two sections of the same course with the same instructors and assessments. The article reports "statistically significant differences on the final assessment scores" favoring treatment (p<.0001); a companion sentence reports r=0.78, p<.0001 between final score and group.

"We found statistically significant differences on the final assessment scores in favor of students who were in the classroom that implemented t he adaptive equity -oriented pedagogy (i.e., treatment) (p<.0001)."

Phuong 2017 (2)¶

Phuong, A. E., Nguyen, J., & Mariea, D. (2017). Evaluating an Adaptive Equity-Oriented Pedagogy: A Study of its Impacts in Higher Education. The Journal of Effective Teaching, 17(2), 5-44. https://uncw.edu/jet

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive final-score results from the same two-section comparison: treatment mean "around 97 points ( SD=1.98)" versus control "approximately 82 points ( SD=8.28)"; all treatment students and 22% of control students scored above 90. No effect size beyond the correlation is printed for this contrast.

"The average final score for the treatment was around 97 points ( SD=1.98) and the average final score for the control was approximately 82 points ( SD=8.28). 100% of the students in the tre atment scored above 90 points, whereas 22% of the students in the control scored above 90 points."

Related Claims¶

  • The final-score advantage of the equity-oriented pedagogy held across gender, disability, and immigration-status subgroups — a narrower finding that bears on this claim

  • Weekly pre-test scores showed no significant treatment-control differences in eight of ten weeks; control scored higher in the first two weeks (p<.05) — related
  • Students in the equity-oriented pedagogy reported positive psychosocial outcomes, a growth mindset, and reduced stereotype threat — related
