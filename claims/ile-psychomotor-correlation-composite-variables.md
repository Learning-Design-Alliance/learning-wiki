Correlation analysis of the review's coded corpus, reported in the Results composite-variables subsection. The review names six composites formed by linking paired variables with positive correlations and similar goals; IHE shows the strongest correlation with three very strong positive correlations, and the review notes there were no strong positive correlations among included pairs.

> "Figure 9 presents six composite variables derived from our analysis: immersive haptic experience (IHE), post-session textual summary (PTS), repetitive large muscle responses (RLMR), haptic-motion interaction (HMI), immersive display technology (IDT), and concurrent continuous feedback (CCF)."
