## Evidence

### Rohmer 2022

Rohmer, O., Doignon‐Camus, N., Audusseau, J., Trautmann, S., Chaillou, A.‐C., & Popa‐Roch, M. (2022). Removing the academic framing in student evaluations improves achievement in children with dyslexia: The mediating role of self‐judgement of competence. Dyslexia, 28(3), 309–324. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1713

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Bias-corrected bootstrap SEM mediation models (Mplus) on the 183-student sample showed "a significant indirect effect of dyslexia on performance through self‐judgement of competence" for both academic literacy (β = −.49) and academic mathematics (β = −1.05), with confidence intervals excluding zero.

> "they both showed a significant indirect effect of dyslexia on performance through self‐judgement of competence (CIs for the associated parameter do not include 0) for academic literacy (β = −.49, 95% CI = [−1.93; −.06]) and academic mathematics (β = −1.05, 95% CI = [−3.00; −.27])."

### Rohmer 2022 (2)

Rohmer, O., Doignon‐Camus, N., Audusseau, J., Trautmann, S., Chaillou, A.‐C., & Popa‐Roch, M. (2022). Removing the academic framing in student evaluations improves achievement in children with dyslexia: The mediating role of self‐judgement of competence. Dyslexia, 28(3), 309–324. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1713

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same mediation analysis, the indirect pathway through self-judgement of competence did not reach significance for the non-academic literacy tasks; the printed confidence interval included zero, so no indirect effect was detected.

> "However, the indirect effect for non‐academic literacy was not significant (β = −.53, 95%, CI = [−1.11; .04])."
