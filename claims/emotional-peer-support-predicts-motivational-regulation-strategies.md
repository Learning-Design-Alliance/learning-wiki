## Evidence

### Villar 2025

Villar, E., Real‐Deus, E., Martínez‐López, Z., Mayo, M. E., & Tinajero, C. (2025). Perceived peer support, motivational self‐regulation and academic achievement in adolescents. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1325–1346. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12783

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression mediation analysis of self-report data from 463 compulsory secondary students (M age = 13.50), with sex, age, SEN, repeated courses, private classes and COVID-19 impact as covariates. Emotional support "was positively related to" the three strategies with the printed standardised coefficients.

> "emotional support was positively related to the use of personally referred performance self‐talk (β = .26, t = 3.76, p < .001), self‐efficacy enhancement (β = .44, t = 4.92, p < .001) and enhancement of situational interest (β = .37, t = 4.38, p < .001)"
