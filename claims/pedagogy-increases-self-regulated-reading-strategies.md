# Pedagogical interventions and scaffolds increase learners' use of self-regulated reading strategies

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hu 2017

Hu, Jingjing; Gao, Xuesong (Andy). (2017). Using think-aloud protocol in self-regulated reading research. Educational Research Review, v.22, pp. 181-193. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.09.004

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Rogers (1991), that an instructional unit with eight ninth-grade students involving peer discussion and reflection led to increased use of self-regulated reading strategies.

> "The results showed that after participating in the instructional unit, students used more self-regulated strategies while reading, such as questionning and evaluating."

### Hu 2017 (2)

Hu, Jingjing; Gao, Xuesong (Andy). (2017). Using think-aloud protocol in self-regulated reading research. Educational Research Review, v.22, pp. 181-193. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.09.004

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Moos and Azevedo (2008) with 37 participants, that conceptual scaffolds increased planning strategy use during hypermedia reading; tutor facilitation similarly increased monitoring and adaptive help seeking (Azevedo et al. 2008).

> "Results indicated that students who were provided with conceptual scaffolds used more of planning during the hypermedia reading task than students who were not provided with the scaffolds."
