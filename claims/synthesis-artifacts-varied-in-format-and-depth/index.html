Evidence¶

Xinran Zhu · Bodong Chen · David DeLiema 2026¶

Xinran Zhu · Bodong Chen · David DeLiema. (2026). Advancing collaborative discourse through knowledge synthesis. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09463-6

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive analysis of the ten consenting students' individual synthesis artifacts in the second synthesis week. The authors report that "most syntheses remained at a surface level", with group 2 students creating more elaborate syntheses emphasizing connections across readings and peers' annotations.

"As presented in Table 2, most syntheses remained at a surface level, often listing key points without integrating themes or connections."

Xinran Zhu · Bodong Chen · David DeLiema 2026 (2)¶

Xinran Zhu · Bodong Chen · David DeLiema. (2026). Advancing collaborative discourse through knowledge synthesis. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09463-6

q2 · i? · design · r2

Open-ended pre-intervention survey responses analyzed descriptively. Responses revealed three orientations, from "synthesis as aggregation" to "knowledge advancement", which the authors offer as a possible explanation for artifact differences, e.g., Sophia's keyword list versus Chloe's elaborated essay.

"synthesis as aggregation, in which students simply collected ideas without much elaboration; (2) synthesis as connection-building, where students attempted to link concepts into a coherent whole"
