## Evidence

### Wang D 2016

Wang D, Cunningham R, Boom J, Amith M, Tao C. (2016). Towards a HPV Vaccine Knowledgebase for Patient Education Content. Studies in Health Technology and Informatics. [doi:10.3233/978-1-61499-658-3-432](https://doi.org/10.3233/978-1-61499-658-3-432)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Author reflection on the ontology construction process (interpretation, not a tested result). The authors describe creating subclasses such as "Adjuvant" and "DNA Virus", deciding to include information "determined to be beneficial and helpful to the patient", and note that ambiguous terms can mislead patients who cannot discern degrees of illness.

> "Additionally, some of the medical terms in the VIS documents are subject to interpretation and could be ambiguous to patients, such as the terms "mildly ill" versus "moderately ill"."
