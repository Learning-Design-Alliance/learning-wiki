Claims

Adding a whole-number-strategy misconception to a fraction tutor's student model improved its fit to the data and gave per-student misconception estimates that tracked pre-test errors

In logged data from 40 students using a fraction arithmetic tutor, a knowledge component coded +1 where the independent whole-number strategy gives the right answer and -1 where it gives a wrong one improved the Additive Factors Model's fit, and each student's estimated misconception strength correlated with the share of their pre-test errors that followed that strategy (Spearman R = 0.46).
