Scoping review synthesis of studies on time demands. The review reports both students and societal partners tended to overestimate the time the other party could commit, and that building relationships and ensuring shared understanding were more time-consuming than in other types of learning.

> "Educational staff also noted that they invested significantly more time and energy in transdisciplinary courses than in traditional coursework (Lake et al., 2022)."
