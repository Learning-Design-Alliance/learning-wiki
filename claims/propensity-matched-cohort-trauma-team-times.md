## Evidence

### Hong Yucai 2017

Hong Yucai, Cai Xiujun. (2017). Effect of team training on efficiency of trauma care in a Chinese hospital. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060517717401

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Propensity-score analysis using nearest-neighbor matching matched 51 of 93 control subjects to the 51 team subjects; Table 4 additionally reports hemostatic administration at P = 0.03. The authors state the matched cohort produced similar results to the raw cohort.

> "The team group showed significantly shorter times to blood routine report (8 [5–11] vs. 13 [10–21]; P < 0.01), CT scan (29.5 [18.5–36.5] vs. 47 [35.5–77] min; P = 0.01) and hemostasis (31 [13.5–58.5] vs. 107 [58.5–121] min; P =0.04) than the control group."
