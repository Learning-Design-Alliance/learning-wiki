Madigan 2019¶

Madigan, D. J. (2019). A meta-analysis of perfectionism and academic achievement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09484-2

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis of the same 37 studies found perfectionistic concerns showed "a small negative relationship with academic achievement". The printed pooled effect size was r+ = −.08 (95% CI −.12, −.05); the 80% credibility interval (−.22, .10) spans zero.
