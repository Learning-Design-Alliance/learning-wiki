`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni-adjusted post hoc robot comparisons in the N = 81 repeated-measures experiment; estimated marginal means were R1: M = 3.17, R2: M = 3.17, R3: M = 3.10. The article concludes "the type of robot did not meaningfully influence participants' trust". No effect size is printed for these comparisons.

> "Pairwise comparisons between robots revealed no statistically significant differences in trust. Differences between R1–R2 (p<.05), R1–R3 (p = .069), and R2–R3 (p = .062) did not reach significance after Bonferroni adjustment."
