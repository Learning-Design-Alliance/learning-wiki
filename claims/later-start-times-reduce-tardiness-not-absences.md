The review contrasts two attendance-related outcomes across delayed-start studies: absences showed no significant variation, whereas "a considerable reduction of marks for lateness" appeared in delayed-start samples. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Studies examining the effects of starting times on absences from school did not indicate significant variations related to starting times [108,113,124]. Conversely, samples of students with delayed start times showed a considerable reduction of marks for lateness [109,110,124]."
