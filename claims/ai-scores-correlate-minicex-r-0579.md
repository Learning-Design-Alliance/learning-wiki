### Hu Shuai 2026

Hu Shuai, Du Fei, Fang Can, Zheng Xiyan, Shi Xianjie, Zhou Zheng. (2026). Effectiveness of an AI-augmented teacher feedback model in improving medical students' clinical documentation skills: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09953-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Phase 2 of the validation analyzed the correlation between post-rotation AI scores and Mini-CEX scores for all 50 interns. Pearson correlation showed a "significant moderately strong positive correlation" (r = 0.579, 95% CI: 0.359–0.739, p < 0.001).

> "Pearson correlation analysis showed a significant moderately strong positive correlation between post-rotation AI scores and total Mini-CEX scores (r = 0.579, 95% CI: 0.359–0.739, p < 0.001), indicating that the AI scores effectively reflected students' true clinical competence."
