## Evidence

### Salem 2013

Ashraf Atta M. S. Salem. (2013). The Effect of Using Writer's Workshop Approach on Developing Basic Writing Skills (Mechanics of Writing) of Prospective Teachers of English in Egypt. English Language Teaching, 6(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n7p33

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pre-post quasi-experiment with 40 prospective English teachers taught with a writing workshop program for eight weeks. Pretest mean was 23.5 (SD 10.78) and posttest mean 76.75 (SD 11.67), with observed t-value 28.49 and effect size 0.976, described as "statistically significant difference between mean scores".

> "As it is shown in table 2 it is revealed that there is a statistically significant difference between mean scores of the subjects of the study in the pretest and posttest on the basic writing test, favoring the posttest."
