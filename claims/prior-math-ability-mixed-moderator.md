## Evidence

### Jing Chen 2026

Jing Chen. (2026). A systematic review of using tablet games to promote early math learning in preschool settings: effectiveness and influencing factors. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10626-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review synthesis of 16 articles coding prior math ability: some found lower-ability children gained most, others found high-level (DePascale et al., 2024; Schacter et al., 2016) or medium-level (Thai et al., 2022) prior ability children improved more, and others found effects similar regardless of baseline.

> "Several studies have found that children with lower prior abilities made better progress from tablet-based games than children with other level abilities"
