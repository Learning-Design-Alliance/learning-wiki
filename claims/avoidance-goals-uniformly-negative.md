## Evidence

### Senko 2011

Senko, C., Hulleman, C. S., & Harackiewicz, J. M. (2011). Achievement Goal Theory at the Crossroads: Old Controversies, Current Challenges, and New Directions. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2011.538646

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of the approach-avoidance distinction reports that "the two avoidance goals have been almost uniformly negative" in Western cultures, with performance-avoidance goals tied to anxiety, disorganized study, and low achievement. No effect size is printed.

> "The findings for the two avoidance goals have been almost uniformly negative, at least in Western cultures (Hulleman et al., 2010). Performance-avoidance goals are typically associated with high anxiety, disorganized study habits, help-avoidance, self-handicapping, and often low achievement and interest as well"
