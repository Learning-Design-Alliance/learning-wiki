## Evidence

### Pavlo D. Antonenko 2007

Pavlo D. Antonenko, John Jackman, Piyamart Kumsaikaew, Rahul R. Marathe, Dale S. Niederhauser, C. A. Ogilvie, and Sarah M. Ryan. (2007). Understanding Student Pathways in Context-rich Problems. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0701284](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0701284)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of problem 1 PSLP tracking data. The article reports clustering correlated with fresource at "r = 0.552 and p = 0.01", and separately reports frelevant vs fresource at r = -0.406 with p = 0.01, meaning more selective teams opened fewer total resources.

> "For problem 1 this  correlation was very strong: r = 0.552 and p = 0.01. Thus, the likelihood that st udents would view resources in clusters increased with the amount of resources that were opened by the students."

### Pavlo D. Antonenko 2007 (2)

Pavlo D. Antonenko, John Jackman, Piyamart Kumsaikaew, Rahul R. Marathe, Dale S. Niederhauser, C. A. Ogilvie, and Sarah M. Ryan. (2007). Understanding Student Pathways in Context-rich Problems. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0701284](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0701284)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis linking qualitative-analysis timing to resource selectivity across teams. The article reports "r = -0.389, p = 0.01" between earlier qualitative-analysis submission and higher frelevant.

> "We also found that teams that submitted their qu alitative analysis earlier tended to have a higher value for frelevant (r = -0.389, p = 0.01)."
