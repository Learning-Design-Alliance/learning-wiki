Comparative study synthesised by the review (Wyles et al. 2011; 66 body donor and 37 porcine model participants per its table) comparing FFBD and live anaesthetised porcine models for colorectal surgery training. Both directions of the significant difference are reported; no effect size printed.

> "The participants rated the FFBD model significantly superior for realistic simulation for port placement and anatomy on the five-point Likert scale. Trainees, however, rated the porcine model significantly better in terms of tissue quality, persistence of air leak, disturbance by odour."
