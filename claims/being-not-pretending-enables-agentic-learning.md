## Evidence

### Spence AD 2025

Spence AD, Carr D, Kajamaa A, Gormley GJ. (2025). 'Overcoming and owning challenges': A qualitative study exploring the manifestation of agency in learners. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.15631

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cross-dataset interview finding: artefacts such as hospital curtains, trolleys and beds evoked a sense of place, and the high-acuity scenario mediated suspension of disbelief, supporting being "present with a more real‐to‐life experience" rather than pretending.

> "Shifting their sense that they were being present with a more real‐to‐life experience linked to their professional context, rather than just pretending, provided an important basis to intentionally drive forward their learning."
