Sakai, K., Kitaguchi, K. & Hikosaka, O. (2003). Chunking during human visuomotor sequence learning. Exp Brain Res 152, 229–242. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00221-003-1548-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Hand-transfer manipulation in the human visuomotor sequence learning experiment showed asymmetric transfer of temporal performance patterns: transfer occurred "from the nondominant to dominant hand, but not vice versa." No effect size is printed.

> "We also found that the temporal patterns of sequence performance transferred from the nondominant to dominant hand, but not vice versa. This may suggest a role of the dominant hemisphere in storage of learned chunks."
