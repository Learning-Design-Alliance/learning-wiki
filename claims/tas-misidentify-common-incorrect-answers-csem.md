### Nafis I. Karim 2017

Nafis I. Karim, Alexandru Maries, and Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Teaching assistants' performance at Identifying Common Introductory Student Difficulties Revealed by the Conceptual Survey of Electricity and Magnetism. 2017 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.047

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of TA predictions with CSEM post-test data from approximately 400 introductory algebra-based physics students reported in Ref. [1]. The study found "only 11% of students actually selected this choice" while 49% of TAs expected it to be most common. No effect size is printed.

> "Answer choice D was selected by 49% of the TAs as the most common incorrect answer choice of students, but only 11% of students actually selected this choice according to Ref. [1]."
