Motivation-section MSLQ survey (18 items on a seven-point Likert scale) administered online via Canvas during the first week of class, covering intrinsic goal orientation, extrinsic goal orientation, task value, and self-efficacy. Most items were consistent with a neutral response (4) or slightly positive.

> "Students’ responses on the MSLQ show modest perception of intrinsic value and higher levels of extrinsic motivation. Students had particularly low interest in physics for its own sake but particularly high emphasis on grade importance."
