Study 2, part of the multi-study mixed method design, collected "concurrent think-aloud protocols and retrospective interviews" from 20 university students learning science content with conceptual discrepancies, extending Study 1's behavioral measures with explanations of discrepancy detection and resolution.

"In Study 2 (n = 20), we collected concurrent think-aloud protocols and retrospective interviews to further explore why discrepancies were noticed (or not) and how they were resolved."
