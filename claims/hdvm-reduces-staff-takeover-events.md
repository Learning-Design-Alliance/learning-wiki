### Mendez Adrian 2014

Mendez Adrian, Seikaly Hadi, Ansari Kal, Murphy Russell, Cote David. (2014). High definition video teaching module for learning neck dissection. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/1916-0216-43-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same prospective pre-post study, a study team member present at every procedure documented attending takeover events. Takeovers fell from 66 to 24, "a 64% decrease following exposure to the HDVM that was statistically significant".

> "The total number of 'take over' events by the surgical attending before and after exposure to the HDVM was 66 and 24 respectively (Figure 5). This represented a 64% decrease following exposure to the HDVM that was statistically significant"
