## Evidence

### Floris M. van Blankenstein 2019

Floris M. van Blankenstein, Gabriela-Olivia Truțescu, Roeland M. van der Rijst, Nadira Saab. (2019). Immediate and delayed effects of a modeling example on the application of principles of good feedback practice: a quasi-experimental study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-019-09482-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney exact test on the direct measure compared assessment-criteria references between the modeling example and practice conditions. The modeling condition "generated significantly more feedback referring to assessment criteria", with medians of 7.00 versus 5.00.

> "the modeling example condition generated significantly more feedback referring to assessment criteria than the practice condition (U = 413.00, z = 5.464, p < 0.0001, Mdn = 7.00 vs. Mdn = 5.00)"
