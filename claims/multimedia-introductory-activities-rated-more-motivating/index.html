Evidence¶

Lauc 2020¶

Lauc, T., Jagodić, G. K., & Bistrović, J. (2020). Effects of Multimedia Instructional Message on Motivation and Academic Performance of Elementary School Students in Croatia. International Journal of Instruction, 13(4), 491-508. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13431a

q3 · i? · causal · r2

After each of 16 lessons, both groups rated the introductory activity on 5-point Likert scales for instructive, interesting and fun. T-tests on summed ratings showed significant differences favouring the experimental group in both parts of the study (e.g. Instructive 1: t = 3.99, p = .001; Fun 2: t = 4.99, p = .001).

"students whose teachers used instructive media in the introductory part of the lecture found those activities more instructive, more interesting and more fun than students whose teachers used traditional materials"
