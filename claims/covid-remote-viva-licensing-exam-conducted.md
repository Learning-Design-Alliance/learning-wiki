## Evidence

### Tan Chee-Kiat 2021

Tan Chee-Kiat, Chua Wei-Ling, Vu Charles Kien-Fong, Chang Jason Pik-Eu. (2021). High-stakes examinations during the COVID-19 pandemic: to proceed or not to proceed, that is the question. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2020-139241

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case report by the examination board of their own licencing examination during the pandemic. Candidates were socially distanced in individual rooms for the MCQ, examiners joined from their own offices, and an IT technician was on standby; the format was 'identical to that in all previous years except that it is now conducted remotely'.

> "The viva voce examination was conducted in four different rooms within the examination venue itself and used a video conferencing software on equivalent laptop set ups. Thus, there was an equivalent technological environment for all the candidates."
