### Wittich CM 2017

Wittich CM, Agrawal A, Cook DA, Halvorsen AJ, Mandrekar J, Chaudhry S, Dupras DM, Oxentenko AS, Beckman TJ. (2017). E-learning in graduate medical education: survey of residency program directors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0953-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Resource results from the national APDIM e-learning survey (Table 2, N = 214 responders): 78.5% no budget, 55.6% no faculty development, and 29.9% insufficient faculty development. Frequencies are descriptive; no inferential test is printed for these resource questions.

> "Most programs (119; 56%) do not have faculty development for e-learning, and many PDs (64; 30%) reported that their program's faculty development for e-learning is insufficient. Most programs (168; 79%) do not have a budget to integrate e-learning into their educational curricula."
