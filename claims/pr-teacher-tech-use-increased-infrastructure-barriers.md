# Teachers' technology use increased during COVID-19 remote learning, but infrastructure and device barriers hindered effective integration

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers in Puerto Rico reported significantly increased technology use compared to pre-COVID years, while citing electrical and broadband infrastructure and lack of devices as key barriers to effective integration. [→ Carol Lopez 2021](#carol-lopez-2021)

## Evidence

### Carol Lopez 2021

Carol Lopez, Odelia Younge. (2021). Creating Conditions for Powerful Technology Use: Teachers' Technology Journey in Puerto Rico. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/bcbf5887-7cf2-48be-b776-c19cb5ef979f

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case study focus groups with 14 Puerto Rico teachers (December 2020–June 2021) found that "Teachers' technology use significantly increased in comparison to pre-COVID school years"; teachers cited electrical and broadband infrastructure, exacerbated by hurricanes and earthquakes, as key barriers, and two participants obtained laptops through donations.

> "Teachers' technology use significantly increased in comparison to pre-COVID school years, and they needed to find new tools and resources that would help them reach their students."
