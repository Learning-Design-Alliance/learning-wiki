# Group, time, and group-by-time interaction all significantly influenced normal vaginal birth skill scores over three semesters

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` GEE analysis showed skill scores changed across semesters (p < .001), differed by group (p < .001), and the group * time interaction affected skill changes (p < .001). [→ Pajohideh Zahra Sadat 2023](#pajohideh-zahra-sadat-2023)

## Evidence

### Pajohideh Zahra Sadat 2023

Pajohideh Zahra Sadat, Mohammadi Solmaz, Keshmiri Fatemeh, Jahangirimehr Azam, Honarmandpour Azam. (2023). The effects of normal vaginal birth simulation training on the clinical skills of midwifery students: a quasi-experiment study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04319-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Generalized Estimating Equations analysis of the same 61 students assessed over the fourth, fifth, and sixth semesters. The omnibus group * time interaction was significant (p < .001); Table 4 reports partial eta squared values of 0.509 (group), 0.786 (time), and 0.082 (group * time).

> "students’ skill scores have changed in general in different periods (time-variable) (p < .001) and also under the influence of different investigated groups (p < .001) and also, the studied groups in different periods (interaction effect of group * time) had an effect on the changes occurred in the students’ skills at the 95% confidence level (p < .001)"
