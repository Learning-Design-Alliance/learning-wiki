## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Haley Noh 2026

Haley Noh, Aarna Chowdhary, Jeroen Ooge, Vincent Aleven, Conrad Borchers. (2026). Simulating Learners' Task-Selection Strategies and System Constraints in Mastery Learning. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21613 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.21613](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.21613)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Simulation results (Figure 3) comparing constrained versus unconstrained task-selection for the Minimize Worst Case Loss strategy across both datasets. The printed effect sizes are "large respective Cohen's d effect sizes of 1.87 and 1.86". Constraints bounded rather than removed learner choice.

> "when the risk-averse strategy (Minimize Worst Case Loss) was guided toward skills closer to mastery, the excess overpractice was sharply reduced from 30.28 to 1.83 times in equation-solving and 28.93 to 3.22 times in graph-interpretation relative to the unconstrained baseline, with large respective Cohen's d effect sizes of 1.87 and 1.86"
