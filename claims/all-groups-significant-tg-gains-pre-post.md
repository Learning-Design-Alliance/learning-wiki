## Evidence

### Gül Kaleli Yılmaz 2015

Gül Kaleli Yılmaz. (2015). The Effect of Dynamic Geometry Software and Physical Manipulatives on Candidate Teachers’ Transformational Geometry Success. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.5.2610

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-test analysis of pre- and post-test TGAT scores within each of the three groups; the article reports p < .001 for the Computer, Manipulatives, and Traditional Groups on the whole test and each section, with no effect size printed.

> "This evaluation suggests that as a result of the treatments, there was a significant difference in the students’ transformational geometry achievement for all groups."
