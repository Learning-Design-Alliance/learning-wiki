## Evidence

### Min Han 2015

Min Han. (2015). An Empirical Study on the Application of Cooperative Learning to English Listening Classes. English Language Teaching, 8(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n3p177

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative interviews conducted throughout the semester with experiment-class students; the article quotes students saying CL "makes us less anxious" and that it "develops my listening and oral skills". One student reported disliking CL as an independent learner.

> "While in CL, we are the center of the class which makes us less anxious."
