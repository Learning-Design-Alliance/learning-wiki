Pearson correlation analysis in the 691-student sample (Table 1) showed growth mindset correlated positively with grit (r = 0.31, p < 0.01); grit also correlated with mathematics grades (r = 0.18, p < 0.01) per the same table.

> "growth mindset was significantly positively correlated with mastery-approach goals, mastery-avoidance goals, performance-approach goals, and grit (r = 0.16, 0.12, 0.15, 0.31, p < 0.01)"
