### Hew Khe Foon 2016

Hew Khe Foon, Tan Cheng Yong. (2016). Predictors of Information Technology Integration in Secondary Schools: Evidence from a Large Scale Study of More than 30,000 Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0168547

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

HLM analysis of PISA 2012 data showed that parental academic pressure was a significant negative predictor of IT integration across models. The authors suggest teachers under pressure for students to score high on tests may prefer didactic presentation over student exploration due to time constraints.

> "students in schools with higher levels of parental pressure for academic achievement (ParentExp, γ = -0.02, p < .05) reported lower levels of IT integration"
