## Evidence

### Kakiuchi Yoshihiko 2024

Kakiuchi Yoshihiko, Kuroda Shinji, Yoshida Yusuke, Kanaya Nobuhiko, Kashima Hajime, Kikuchi Satoru, Kagawa Shunsuke, Fujiwara Toshiyoshi. (2024). An effective surgical educational system in the era of robotic surgery: “Double-Surgeon Technique” in robotic gastrectomy for minimally invasive surgery. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03593-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of early RDG cases (5, 6, and 4 cases by DSTs-1, Cs-1, Cs-2) limited to the first 15 cases after RG initiation; DSTs-1 had DST experience but no ESSQS qualification while the comparators had ESSQS qualification. No effect sizes are printed.

> "No significant differences were observed in the time assessed from console start to picking up the stomach, bleeding, postoperative hospital stay, or frequency of complications of CD Grade ≥ III among the groups."
