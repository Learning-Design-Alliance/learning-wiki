## Evidence

### Karma Wangda 2020

Karma Wangda, Kinzang Dorji, Kinley. (2020). Teachers and Students Perception on the Impact of Kagan Cooperative Learning Structures at Higher Secondary School. ASEAN Journal of Open and Distance Learning, 12(2). https://rcl.sce.rub.edu.bt/ajod/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Theme-two questionnaire items on factors affecting KCLS, analysed descriptively for the same 650-participant survey, showed "an average mean value of 2.0, SD 0.12 for teachers" and 2.30 (SD 0.17) for students, both in the Low band. The authors interpret this as indicating practical implementation challenges.

> "for theme two on teachers and students' perceptions on factors affecting KCLS indicated an average mean value of 2.0, SD 0.12 for teachers and an average mean value of 2.30, SD 0.17 for students with 'Low' level of perceptions"
