## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Interviewed experimental-group participants unanimously judged face-to-face tasks more useful than online tasks, preferring synchronous interaction. [→ Ebadi 2018](#ebadi-2018)

## Evidence

### Ebadi 2018

Ebadi, S., & Asakereh, A. (2018). Using Voice Thread to Enhance Speaking Accuracy: A Case Study of Iranian EFL Learners. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 8(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1198886

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semi-structured interviews with 4 experimental-group participants (2 male, 2 female) conducted in Persian, coded via grounded theory; participants said online tasks "were not natural" and lacked discussion and synchronous feedback.

> "They, however, found some problems with online tasks and they all unanimously believed that face-to-face tasks were more or less useful than online tasks."
