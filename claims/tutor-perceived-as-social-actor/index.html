q1 · i? · design · r2

Authors' interpretation of the field study and interview data, reported in the reflection on the evaluation studies. The article attributes the perceived social-actor status to the chatbot-based human–computer interface; this is an interpretation rather than a tested result.

"It is particularly noteworthy that the students spoke of the digital tutor as if it were a human being. It seems that the digital tutor is perceived as a social actor—in line with the CASA principle (Nass & Moon, 2000; Nass et al., 1995)."
