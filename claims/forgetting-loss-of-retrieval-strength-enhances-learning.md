Theoretical exposition of the authors' own framework in the history section. The article states that "forgetting (loss of retrieval strength) can enhance learning (the gain in storage strength)" and names spacing and variation as manipulations that reduce retrieval strength and improve delayed performance. No empirical test is reported here.

> "Thus, forgetting (loss of retrieval strength) can enhance learning (the gain in storage strength), which is why, in the theory, manipulations such as spacing and variation, which reduce retrieval strength, can enhance learning, as measured by performance at a delay."
