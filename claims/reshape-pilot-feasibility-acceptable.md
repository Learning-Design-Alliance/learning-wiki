`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Feasibility outcomes from a pilot cluster randomized clinical trial in Nepal with 34 facilities randomized. Of 15 PWLE trained in PhotoVoice, 11 (73.3%) cofacilitated; the article reports both trainings were "above the 75% fidelity benchmark" and no serious adverse events for PWLE, PCPs, or patients.

> "Of 15 PWLE trained as cofacilitators, 11 (73.3%) participated throughout the 3 months of PCP trainings."
