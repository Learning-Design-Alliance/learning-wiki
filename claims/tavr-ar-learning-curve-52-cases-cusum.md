# CUSUM analysis identifies a procedure-time learning-curve inflection at case 52 and a fluoroscopy-time inflection at case 43 for TAVR in native aortic regurgitation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` By CUSUM plot inspection, decreasing procedure time began at the 52nd operation and decreasing fluoroscopy time at the 43rd operation for a single surgeon performing J-valve TAVR for native AR. [→ Liu L  2020](#liu-l-2020)

## Evidence

### Liu L  2020

Liu L , Zhang J , Peng Y , et al. (2020). Learning curve for transcatheter aortic valve replacement for native aortic regurgitation: Safety and technical performance study. Clinical Cardiology. 2020;43:475–482. https://doi.org/10.1002/clc.23332

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CUSUM analysis of prospectively collected procedure and fluoroscopy times from 134 consecutive native AR patients treated by one novice surgeon; the plot cross-points defined the early (first 52 cases) and late (next 82 cases) experience groups. This is a "decreasing point" identified by visual inspection of the CUSUM plots, not an analytic derivation.

> "By visually inspecting the CUSUM plots, a decreasing point for total procedure time began at the 52nd operation (Figure S1A), and a similar trend was observed for fluoroscopy time at the 43rd operation (Figure S1B)."
