## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Trait and state test boredom show significant negative correlations with math grades, while no significant relations with the math test score were found. [→ Goetz 2023](#goetz-2023)

## Evidence

### Goetz 2023

Goetz, T., Bieleke, M., Yanagida, T., Krannich, M., Roos, A.-L., Frenzel, A. C., Lipnevich, A. A., & Pekrun, R. (2023). Test boredom: Exploring a neglected emotion. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://osf.io/ftr4g/?view_only=beac4ca87987492294aeac4a1bb96c86

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 1 latent correlations with achievement outcomes (math test score and inverted midterm grades) in 208 8th graders. The article reports "negative correlations with math grades" for both trait and state boredom; the null test-score relation had no printed effect size, and equivalence was not tested.

> "No significant relations between boredom and the score in the math test were found. However, both for the trait and state assessments, the overall boredom score showed negative correlations with math grades."
