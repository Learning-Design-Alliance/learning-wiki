## Evidence

### Zhou Quan 2024

Zhou Quan, Gidziela Agnieszka, Allegrini Andrea G., Cheesman Rosa, Wertz Jasmin, Maxwell Jessye, Plomin Robert, Rimfeld Kaili, Malanchini Margherita. (2024). Gene-environment correlation: the role of family environment in academic development. Molecular Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-024-02716-0

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Correlational analyses in the TEDS twin sample across four waves (ages 7, 9, 12, 16) relating five polygenic scores to teacher- and GCSE-based achievement composites. The study reports the EA PGS correlation "increased from 0.20 at age 7 to 0.36 at age 16" (r = 0.36 at age 16).

> "For example, the correlation between the EA PGS and academic achievement increased from 0.20 at age 7 to 0.36 at age 16 (Fig. 1 and Supplementary Table 3)."
