## Evidence

### Simpson Paul L 2014

Simpson Paul L, Scicluna Helen A, Jones Philip D, Cole Andrew MD, O’Sullivan Anthony J, Harris Peter G, Velan Gary, McNeil H Patrick. (2014). Predictive validity of a new integrated selection process for medical school admission. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-86

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression and correlation analyses of 2004–2005 cohorts (N = 240–301 across criteria). UMAT total correlated only with WAM phase 1 (0.15, p < 0.01) and knowledge exam phase 1 (0.12, p < 0.05); no UMAT dimension was significant in any regression model.

> "The UMAT was not a significant predictor of any outcome."
