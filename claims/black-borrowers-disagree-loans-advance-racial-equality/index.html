Mustaffa 2021¶

Mustaffa, J. B., & Davis, J. C. W. (2021). Jim Crow Debt: How Black Borrowers Experience Student Loans. The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/black-student-debt-study/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey findings reported in Finding 1 and Figure 1 of the study of 1,272 Black borrowers. "only 33% of respondents believe student loans improve life opportunities for Black borrowers," while majorities disagreed on racial equality and wealth-building.

"More than half of the Black borrowers in our study said they do not believe that student loans advance racial equality for Black borrowers (58%) or increase Black borrowers’ ability to build wealth (61%). (See Figure 1.) In fact, only 33% of respondents believe student loans improve life opportunities for Black borrowers."
