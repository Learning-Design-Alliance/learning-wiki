## Evidence

### Lima Rodrigo A. 2020

Lima Rodrigo A., Stodden David F., Pfeiffer Karin A., Larsen Lisbeth R., Barros Mauro V. G., Bugge Anna, Andersen Lars B. (2020). Dynamic Balance, but Not Precision Throw, Is Positively Associated with Academic Performance in Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17082790

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sex-stratified SEM in the same longitudinal sample. The article reports for boys βdirect = 0.101 (95% CI: 0.051 to 0.152) and βtotal = 0.113 (95% CI: 0.050 to 0.176), and that "waist circumference did not mediate the association".

> "In boys, dynamic balance was associated with academic performance (βdirect = 0.101; 95% CI: 0.051 to 0.152; βtotal = 0.113; 95% CI: 0.050 to 0.176), but waist circumference did not mediate the association."
