## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In 2016, the median Added Value was higher for online-homework-preferring students (11.24) than clickers-preferring students (−2.9), but neither the u-test (p = 0.5641) nor the t-test (p = 0.5599) detected a statistically significant difference.
