## Evidence

### Springvloet Linda 2014

Springvloet Linda, Lechner Lilian, Oenema Anke. (2014). Planned development and evaluation protocol of two versions of a web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention aimed at adults, including cognitive and environmental feedback. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-14-47

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Evaluation plan of the study protocol: a "three-group randomized controlled trial" with measurements at baseline (T0) and 1, 4 and 9 months post-intervention using online questionnaires. 2,000 adults will be recruited (900 high-educated, 1,100 lower-educated), with a power calculation stating 1,400 participants sufficient to detect a small effect with power 0.80 at p < 0.05. Trial registered as NTR3396.

> "A three-group randomized controlled trial will be conducted to study the effects of both versions of the intervention compared to a control group that will receive generic information about fruit, vegetables, high-energy snacks and fat."

### Springvloet Linda 2014 (2)

Springvloet Linda, Lechner Lilian, Oenema Anke. (2014). Planned development and evaluation protocol of two versions of a web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention aimed at adults, including cognitive and environmental feedback. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-14-47

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Stated hypothesis of the planned trial: "participants in both intervention conditions will have a higher increase in fruit and vegetable consumption and a higher decrease of high-energy snacks and fat" than control. Secondary outcomes are self-reported BMI, general self-control and general self-regulation; mediating variables and mediation via the joint significance test will also be examined. This is a hypothesis, not yet a result.

> "It is hypothesized that after exposure to the intervention participants in both intervention conditions will have a higher increase in fruit and vegetable consumption and a higher decrease of high-energy snacks and fat (i.e. the primary outcome measures) compared to the control condition."

### Springvloet Linda 2014 (3)

Springvloet Linda, Lechner Lilian, Oenema Anke. (2014). Planned development and evaluation protocol of two versions of a web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention aimed at adults, including cognitive and environmental feedback. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-14-47

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The protocol states a moderation hypothesis: "the plus version is more effective for low-educated participants than for high-educated participants, since environmental factors are suggested to be more important among low SES groups". Interaction with educational level will be explored in the analyses. This expectation rests on cited prior literature, not on data reported here.

> "We expect that the plus version is more effective for low-educated participants than for high-educated participants, since environmental factors are suggested to be more important among low SES groups"
