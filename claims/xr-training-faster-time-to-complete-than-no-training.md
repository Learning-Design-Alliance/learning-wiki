## Evidence

### Bickford Michael 2025

Bickford Michael, Alruwaili Fayez, Ragab Sara, Rothenberg Hanna, Abedin-Nasab Mohammad (2025). Impact of extended reality on robot-assisted surgery training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-025-02559-z

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of randomized and prospective studies comparing XR training to no additional training on time to complete. The analysis found "statistically significant less time to complete tasks" with Cohen's d = -0.95 and a 95% CI of [-1.73, -0.16].

> "A random-effects analysis was used for this meta-analysis and showed that participants with virtual reality training took statistically significant less time to complete tasks than participants with no additional training (Cohen’s d = −0.95, 95% CI [−1.73, −0.16], p = 0.02)"
