Evidence¶

Green Dot Public Schools 2018¶

Green Dot Public Schools. (2018). WWC Intervention Report: Green Dot Public Schools, Charter Schools. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/686

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The WWC review reports that the single eligible quasi-experimental study, matching Green Dot students to comparison students via propensity scores, found a "statistically significant positive effect" on first-year CST Algebra I and Geometry scores across all three cohorts. The WWC rating is potentially positive effects based on a small extent of evidence.

"Herman et al. (2012) found a statistically significant positive effect of attending Green Dot Public Schools on California Standards Test (CST) scores in Algebra I and Geometry in the first year of high school for students across all three cohorts."
