# PARSER's segmentation performance extends to a language composed of one- to five-syllable words, with no detrimental effect of varied word length

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Perruchet 1998

Perruchet, P., & Vinter, A. (1998). PARSER: A Model for Word Segmentation. Journal of Memory and Language, 39, 246-263. https://doi.org/10.1006/jmla.1998.2576

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical simulation (Study 4) using six words from one to five syllables, including the one-syllable word bu, which was the most frequent syllable and a priori increased difficulty. The article reports the strict criterion mean of 3338 syllables and concludes varied length has no detrimental effect.

> "The loose criterion was reached after processing a mean of 1910 syllables, with a range of 613 to 4782 syllables."
