The Player Experience Inventory (PXI) was used to assess user experience across ten constructs on a 7-point Likert scale. Audiovisual appeal scored highest (AVG=1.86, SD=2.01), followed by goal clarity, progress feedback, and immersion, while mastery and meaning obtained lower average scores.

> "The results indicate a moderately high level for almost all categories. Among the constructs, the audiovisual appeal was scored as the highest, followed by goal clarity, progress feedback, and immersion."
