### Oriyama Sanae 2025

Oriyama Sanae. (2025). Influence of the COVID-19 pandemic on nursing skills experience and subjective attainment among final-year nursing students in Japan: a questionnaire-based multicenter cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07832-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pre/during-pandemic comparison of two questionnaire samples (237 before, 303 during). The Results section reports the drop in skills performed in clinical practice, with four skills not differing significantly between groups.

> "Of the 31 nursing skills, more than 50% of the students were able to perform 23 (74.2%) in clinical practice before compared with 17 (54.8%) during the pandemic."
