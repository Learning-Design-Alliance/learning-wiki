## Evidence

### Jiang Dingyuan 2025

Jiang Dingyuan, Huang Danpei, Wan Hua, Fu Wuliang, Shi Weidong, Li Jin, Zou Huan, Hou Niannan, Li Qing, Li Nan. (2025). Effect of integrated case-based and problem-based learning on clinical thinking skills of assistant general practitioner trainees: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06634-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RCT outcome measured with two equal-difficulty examination papers built on Bloom's Taxonomy; baseline scores were equivalent (62.114 vs 62.515, p = 0.784). The article reports the significant post-course difference with t = 9.585, p < 0.001. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The post-course test difference between the two groups was statistically significant (t = 9.585, p < 0.001), signifying a markedly greater enhancement in the CBL-PBL group than that in the LBL group."
