`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Year-by-year weighted-frequency analysis of the same NVivo exam-question corpus, reported per academic year; it "identified a pattern of increasing use" of four verbs and inconsistent or no change for three others.

> "Finally, action verb frequency across programme years identified a pattern of increasing use of the words “discuss”, “outline”, “include” and “write” but inconsistent or no change in the use of the words “describe”, “explain”, and “give” (Figure 5)."
