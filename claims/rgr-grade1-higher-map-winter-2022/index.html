Really Great Reading 2023¶

Really Great Reading. (2023). Really Great Reading. https://www.reallygreatreading.com

q2 · i1 · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental study in one urban charter school network during 2021–22 and 2022–23, comparing students whose teachers completed RGR professional development and used the platform to peers of teachers who did not. Grade 1 showed "effect size = +0.30" on the winter 2022 NWEA MAP.

"Grade 1 and grade 2 students receiving RGR instruction scored higher on the NWEA MAP® Reading Growth assessment in winter 2022 (effect size = +0.30, +0.24)."
