## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin 2013

Shih-Yin Lin, Charles Henderson, William Mamudi, Chandralekha Singh, and Edit Yerushalmi. (2013). Teaching assistants' beliefs regarding example solutions in introductory physics. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.9.020115

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Open coding of GAIQ worksheets from 24 TAs produced seven goal categories (G1-G7). For G1 physics understanding the article reports the goal was expressed "by 23 and 18 TAs, respectively" in the general and concrete contexts.

> "This goal is expressed in both the general and concrete contexts, by 23 and 18 TAs, respectively ."

### Shih-Yin Lin 2013 (2)

Shih-Yin Lin, Charles Henderson, William Mamudi, Chandralekha Singh, and Edit Yerushalmi. (2013). Teaching assistants' beliefs regarding example solutions in introductory physics. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.9.020115

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

For G4 cognitive engagement, the coded GAIQ responses show the goal "expressed to a much lesser extent in the general context (by 6 TAs)" than in the concrete context (21 TAs), with slightly different meanings across contexts.

> "This goal is also expressed to a much lesser extent in the general context (by 6 TAs) as compare to the concrete context (by 21 TAs)"
