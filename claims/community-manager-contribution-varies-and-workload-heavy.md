## Evidence

### Dilrukshi Gamage and Mark E Whiting 2021

Dilrukshi Gamage and Mark E Whiting. 2021. Together we learn better: leveraging communities of practice for MOOC learners. In Asian CHI Symposium 2021, May 8–13, 2021, Yokohama, Japan. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3429360.3468176

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Field-study log analysis of manager posting shares across the five rapid communities. Managers' most frequent actions were regular comments, encouraging comments, and welcoming members; two managers held Zoom meetings outside the platform.

> "Community managers played different roles across communities, contributing 23% of the conversation on average: in C++ almost all posts were written by the community manager, while the next highest manager contribution was Design Thinking Community 1 at 12%"

### Dilrukshi Gamage and Mark E Whiting 2021 (2)

Dilrukshi Gamage and Mark E Whiting. 2021. Together we learn better: leveraging communities of practice for MOOC learners. In Asian CHI Symposium 2021, May 8–13, 2021, Yokohama, Japan. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3429360.3468176

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-study interviews with community managers in the field study. One manager wished the system would show who had replied rather than digging through conversations; some members said personal welcoming made them feel accountable to contribute.

> "During our interviews with the community managers, they reflected the heavy workload they had administrating the work, they particularly highlighted scheduling meetings, and reaching out to members who were not active in the community."
