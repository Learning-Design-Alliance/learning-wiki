Cekiso 2012¶

Cekiso, M. (2012). Effects of strategy instruction on the reading comprehension and strategy awareness of Grade 11 English Second Language learners in the Eastern Cape. Reading & Writing 3(1), Art. #23. https://doi.org/10.4102/rw.v3i1.23

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Comparison of Reading Strategies Questionnaire post-test means (N = 30 per group) in the quasi-experiment. Table 2 marks strategies with p < 0.05 and d-values ranging from 0.30 to 1.50, e.g. making predictions (d = 1.50) and checking anticipated information (d = 1.21).

"The post-test results, however, indicated that the learners in the experimental group used certain strategies statistically (p < 0.05), as well as practically, significantly (small to large effect sizes), more often than the learners in the control group"
