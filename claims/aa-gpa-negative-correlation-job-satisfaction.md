### Al-Asmar Ayah A. 2021

Al-Asmar Ayah A., Oweis Yara, Ismail Noor H., Sabrah Alaa H. A., Abd-Raheam Islam M. (2021). The predictive value of high school grade point average to academic achievement and career satisfaction of dental graduates. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-021-01662-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Web-survey of 333 matched graduates; Pearson correlations of AA-GPA with first-choice career, life enjoyment, and income satisfaction were negative (Table 6 reports values of -0.05, -0.01, -0.06), though the narrative reports no tested significance for these individual items.

> "There was a negative correlation between was dentistry your first choice as a profession, are you able to enjoy your life and are you satisfied with your income and the university AA-GPA."
