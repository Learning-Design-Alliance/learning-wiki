## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental group's reading comprehension improved significantly from pretest to posttest after strategy training, while the control group's change was not significant. [→ Yang 2017](#yang-2017)

## Evidence

### Yang 2017

Xiaoling Yang. (2017). On Non-English Majors' Strategy Training in English Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 10(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n10p153

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Five-week quasi-experiment with two groups of second-year non-English majors; the experimental group received strategy training and the control group a traditional approach. The article reports "a significant difference between the pretest and the posttest" for the experimental group (t = 4.764, P ≤ 0.001, DF 57).

> "Comparing with the pretest, students in the experimental group made progress in the posttest (68.279 vs. 74.918 in the mean score). There was a significant difference between the pretest and the posttest."
