agency-theme¶

Code as Anchor, Memory and Metaphor as Support: Learner Experiences with Multi-View Visualizations. ICER 2026 Vol. 1. https://doi.org/10.1145/3765964.3811662

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Reflexive thematic analysis of think-aloud and interview transcripts from the 19-participant within-subjects study; the agency theme was constructed inductively from accounts such as P6A preferring to trace on his own to feel more in control. Qualitative interpretive finding; no effect size.

"The high frequency of Memory↔Code transitions (11k+) supports this interpretation: students used visualizations forverificationof their own reasoning rather than as primary tools for understanding execution."
