## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Poster-phase gaze similarity correlates with game-phase gaze similarity (r = 0.49), as do transition similarity (r = 0.55) and both AOI transition directions (r = 0.56 and 0.50). [→ Sharma Kshitij 2020](#sharma-kshitij-2020)

## Evidence

### Sharma Kshitij 2020

Sharma Kshitij, Leftheriotis Ioannis, Giannakos Michail. (2020). Utilizing Interactive Surfaces to Enhance Learning, Collaboration and Engagement: Insights from Learners’ Gaze and Speech. Sensors (Basel). https://doi.org/10.3390/s20071964

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Cross-task correlations comparing the physical poster phase with the digital game phase for 18 dyads; the printed effect size is "r (16) = 0.49, p < .04". Game-phase gaze similarity was taken from the collaborative version only.

> "we observe a significant correlation between the gaze similarity during the poster phase and the gaze similarity during the game phase (r (16) = 0.49, p < .04). The pairs having high gaze similarity during the poster session also have high gaze similarity during the game phase"
