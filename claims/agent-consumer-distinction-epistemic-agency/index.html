Brady D. Lund and Zoë Abbie Teel 2026¶

Brady D. Lund and Zoë Abbie Teel. (2026). AI Literacy: An Exercise in Power-Knowledge. University of North Texas. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27547

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Conceptual argument drawing the paper's firm distinction between consumers, who receive and evaluate outputs within an unshaped frame, and agents, who understand the frame itself, interrogate its assumptions, and participate in governance. The distinction has direct implications for how AI literacy should be taught and assessed.

"A highly technically sophisticated user who uses their skills entirely within the logic of AI as a productivity tool is, in the relevant sense, still a consumer."
