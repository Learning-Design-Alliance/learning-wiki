# Immersion in inquiry-based learning significantly increases student teachers' value for procedural knowledge goals and self-efficacy for inquiry, but not substantive knowledge goals

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Student teachers' value for procedural knowledge goals increased significantly from pre- to posttest after a four-hour technology-enhanced inquiry activity. [→ Voet 2018](#voet-2018)
`q3 i?` Student teachers' self-efficacy for conducting historical inquiries increased significantly from pre- to posttest. [→ Voet 2018](#voet-2018)
`q3 i?` Student teachers' beliefs about substantive knowledge goals did not change significantly from pre- to posttest. [→ Voet 2018 (2)](#voet-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Voet 2018

Voet, M. & De Wever, B. (2018). Effects of immersion in inquiry-based learning on student teachers' educational beliefs. Instructional Science, 46(3), 383-403. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-017-9439-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A multivariate multilevel analysis of difference scores (N=298) with students nested in dyads and university colleges found the increase in procedural knowledge goals and self-efficacy for inquiry was significantly different from 0. No effect size was printed.

> "the significant intercepts for procedural knowledge goals (X2= -56.65, df=1, p<.001) and self -efficacy for inquiry (X2=23.45, df=1, p<.001) indicate that the increase in scores is significantly different from 0"

### Voet 2018 (2)

Voet, M. & De Wever, B. (2018). Effects of immersion in inquiry-based learning on student teachers' educational beliefs. Instructional Science, 46(3), 383-403. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-017-9439-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same multivariate multilevel model showed no significant pre-post change in beliefs about substantive knowledge goals (X2=1.03, df=1, p=0.31). The authors note this was "in line with what could be logically expected". Equivalence was not tested.

> "In contrast, student teacher beliefs about substantive knowledge goals did not change significantly from pre- to posttest (X2=1.03, df=1, p=0.31)."
