## Evidence

### Walck-Shannon 2019

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Cahill, M. J., McDaniel, M. A., & Frey, R. F. (2019). Participation in Voluntary Re-quizzing Is Predictive of Increased Performance on Cumulative Assessments in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 18(2). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-08-0163

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression in a large introductory biology course (final n = 310) predicting posttest score. Step 2 added number of quizzes retaken beyond pretest and chemistry finals; "a significant increase in the explanatory power of our model"; the article reports a student retesting all 10 quizzes scored about 6% higher on the posttest.

> "We found that there was a significant increase in the explanatory power of our model when adding in the delayed re-quizzing variable (F(1, 306) = 8.790, p = 0.003, ΔR2 = 0.018; see also step 2 of Table 1)."
