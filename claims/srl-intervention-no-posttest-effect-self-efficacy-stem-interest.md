## Evidence

### Granello 2026

Granello, F., Cuder, A., Doz, E., Pellizzoni, S., & Passolunghi, M. C. (2026). “I can do math!”: A self‐regulated learning intervention to enhance math‐related motivational factors and performance in middle school. The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96(2), 501–521. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70034

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel models controlling pretest and gender found no significant post-test group difference on either measure; printed effect sizes were "d = .038" for self-efficacy and "d = .157" for STEM vocational interest. Equivalence was not tested.

> "No statistically significant differences were found at post‐test on math self‐efficacy (B = .173, p = .382, d = .038) or STEM vocational interest (B = .474, p = .259, d = .157)."
