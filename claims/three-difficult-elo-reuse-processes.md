Two case studies of students' ability to assess and re-use each other's ELOs, reported in the student-experiences section, found students appreciated inspecting peers' work but struggled with these three reuse processes; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Three processes in particular appeared to be difficult for students: deciding what information to look for, selecting relevant ELOs, and judging the quality of these ELOs."
