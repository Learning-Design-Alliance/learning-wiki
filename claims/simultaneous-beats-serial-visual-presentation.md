## Evidence

### Lukics Krisztina Sára 2022

Lukics Krisztina Sára, Lukács Ágnes. (2022). Modality, presentation, domain and training effects in statistical learning. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-24951-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Second three-way ANOVA (Presentation Type, Domain, Training Type) on the visual conditions of the 360-participant experiment: "performance was higher with simultaneous presentation than with serial presentation" (ηp2 = 0.08).

> "The effect of Presentation Type was significant, F(1,228) = 20.38, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.08, BF10 = 1092.94; performance was higher with simultaneous presentation than with serial presentation."
