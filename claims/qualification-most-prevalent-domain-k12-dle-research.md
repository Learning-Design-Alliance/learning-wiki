### Fagerlund 2025

Fagerlund, J., Palsa, L., & Mertala, P. (2025). Exploration of domains of educational purpose in K-12 data literacy education research. Educational Research Review, 46, Article 100663. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100663

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Integrative qualitative review of K-12 DLE research; peer reconciliation of coded purpose expressions against Biesta's three domains. The review reports "470 expressions pertaining to qualification (expressed in 100% of the studies)" versus 180 socialization (82%) and 171 subjectification (71%).

> "15% of the interpretations (131 out of 847) were modified in the reconciliation process, ultimately resulting in 470 expressions pertaining to qualification (expressed in 100% of the studies), 180 pertaining to socialization (in 82%), and 171 pertaining to subjectification (in 71%)."
