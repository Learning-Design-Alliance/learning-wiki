Nasabi Narjes Alsadat, Yusefi Ali Reza, Bordbar Najmeh. (2026). The impact of creative thinking on organizational maturity and the mediating role of experiential learning among knowledge management professionals. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39905-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 505 knowledge management professionals at an Iranian medical university, analyzed with Pearson correlations. The study reports creative thinking correlated with experiential learning at "r = 0.61, p < 0.001".

> "creative thinking was significantly correlated with experiential learning (r = 0.61, p < 0.001). This suggests that individuals with higher creative thinking skills are more likely to engage deeply in experiential learning processes."
