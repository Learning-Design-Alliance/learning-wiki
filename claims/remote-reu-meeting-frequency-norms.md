### Dina Zohrabi Alaee and Benjamin M. Zwickl 2022

Dina Zohrabi Alaee and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2022). Toward a Longitudinal Multifaceted View of Remote Undergraduate Research Experiences During the COVID-19 Pandemic: The perspectives of doing research online. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2207.02733](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2207.02733)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phenomenographic analysis of lab norms found "Weekly check-ins were vital to support mentees' research goals and help them develop more open communication with their mentors," with meeting frequency varying from multiple weekly meetings to mostly email exchange for an international mentee in a different time zone.

> "Overall, most mentees (N=6) had meetings more than once a week, some ( N=3) met their mentor only once a week, and only Ivan met less frequently than once per week and mostly communicated through email."
