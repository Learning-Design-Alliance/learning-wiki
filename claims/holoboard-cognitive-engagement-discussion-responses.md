## Evidence

### Jiangtao Gong 2021

Jiangtao Gong, Teng Han, Siling Gong, Jiannan Li, Siyu Zha, Liuxin Zhang, Feng Tian, Qianying Wang, and Yong Rui. (2021). HoloBoard: a Large-format Immersive Teaching Board based on pseudo HoloGraphics. In The 34th Annual ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology (UIST '21). https://doi.org/10.1145/3472749.3474761

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Transcript analysis of the two classes with four trained coders (inter-coder Kappa greater than 0.81), coding speech types and Bloom's cognitive levels. The article reports assertions and questions per minute were "both significantly larger in the HoloBoard group"; only p-values are printed.

> "The results indicated that the numbers of students' assertions (p < .001) and questions (p = .005) per minute were both significantly larger in the HoloBoard group than the one in the normal group."

### Jiangtao Gong 2021 (2)

Jiangtao Gong, Teng Han, Siling Gong, Jiannan Li, Siyu Zha, Liuxin Zhang, Feng Tian, Qianying Wang, and Yong Rui. (2021). HoloBoard: a Large-format Immersive Teaching Board based on pseudo HoloGraphics. In The 34th Annual ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology (UIST '21). https://doi.org/10.1145/3472749.3474761

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same transcript analysis coded with Bloom's taxonomy of the cognitive domain. Remembering and understanding responses were significantly higher in the HoloBoard group (both p < .001), while no significant differences appeared at higher cognitive levels; no effect sizes are printed.

> "the numbers of remembering responses (p < .001) and understanding responses (p < .001) were significantly higher in the HoloBoard group than in the normal group. No significant differences were found in the number of responses at any higher cognitive level (e.g., applying and analyzing responses) between the two groups."
