## Evidence

### Ercan 2017

Ercan, O., Ural, E., & Köse, S. (2017). The Effect of Web Assisted Learning with Emotional Intelligence Content on Students' Information about Energy Saving, Attitudes Towards Environment and Emotional Intelligence. Science Education International, 28(1), 78-94. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1143753

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-tests on pre-test scores of the 60 experimental and 60 control students. The AAT difference favored the control group; EIS and EAS pre-tests showed no statistically significant differences, so groups were described as similar on EI and attitudes.

> "there is a statistically significant difference between the AAT scores of the groups [t(118)=2.621; p=0.010<0.05] in favor of the control group, but there is not a statistically significant difference between EIS scores [t(118)=0.697; p=0.487>0.05] and EAS scores [t(118)=1.949; p=0.054>0.05]"
