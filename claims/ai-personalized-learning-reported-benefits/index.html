Martin Sposato 2025¶

Martin Sposato. (2025). Artificial intelligence in educational leadership: a comprehensive taxonomy and future directions. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-025-00517-1

q1 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Tang et al. (2020), that adaptive learning platforms adjust content delivery in real time based on student performance patterns and that "AI-driven personalization can lead to significant improvements in student engagement and learning outcomes". No effect sizes or study designs are printed in the review.

"Studies have demonstrated that AI-driven personalization can lead to significant improvements in student engagement and learning outcomes (Tang et al., 2020)."

Martin Sposato 2025 (2)¶

Martin Sposato. (2025). Artificial intelligence in educational leadership: a comprehensive taxonomy and future directions. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-025-00517-1

q1 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Essel et al. (2022), that intelligent tutoring systems using natural language processing and cognitive modeling continuously adapt to student responses and have "shown particular success in supporting diverse learning styles and needs". The review does not report quantitative results for this attribution.

"Recent implementations have shown particular success in supporting diverse learning styles and needs (Essel et al., 2022)."
