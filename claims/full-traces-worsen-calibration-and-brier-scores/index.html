Fernandes 2026¶

Fernandes, D., Buschek, D., Tankelevitch, L., Kosch, T., & Welsch, R. (2026). Explaining Too Much? Understanding How Large Language Model Reasoning Traces Influence Performance and Metacognition. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25856 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.25856

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

Trial-level calibration index (mean confidence minus accuracy) differed across conditions with η² = .029; Tukey HSD showed Full-trace (M = 0.265) more overconfident than Answer-only (M = 0.189, p < .001), with Summary-trace between and not reliably different from either.
