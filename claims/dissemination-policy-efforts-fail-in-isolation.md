Joel C. Corbo, Daniel L. Reinholz, Melissa H. Dancy, Stanley Deetz, and Noah Finkelstein. (2014). A Framework for Transforming Departmental Culture to Support Educational Innovation. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1412.3034](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1412.3034)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

This is the article's own analytical synthesis (Section III.G) of why some change efforts fail, drawing on the reviewed literature; it is an interpretive argument rather than a new empirical test. The article states dissemination efforts focus on "changing practices, but fail to account for underlying" beliefs, culture, and incentive structures, and this analysis "supports the claim that change efforts should draw from all six change perspectives."
