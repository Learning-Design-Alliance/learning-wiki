q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

The report's retrospective case study of the 2012 IES-funded ASSISTments efficacy trial in Maine, a large classroom-level randomized controlled trial, reports that "students with lower prior mathematics achievement (or any of its correlates) gained more from ASSISTments implementation." No effect size is printed in the report.

"These impacts were positive: students with lower prior mathematics achievement (or any of its correlates) gained more from ASSISTments implementation than did students with higher prior mathematics achievement (Murphy et al., 2020)."
