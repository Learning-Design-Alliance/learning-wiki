Qualitative analysis of week-06 brainstorming audio recordings from 14 student groups in an ED-based physics lab. The authors report the distribution of initial solution ideas, noting "The most popular approach involved launching the load us- ing a catapult or trebuchet, closely followed by the use of drones and ziplines."

> "The most popular approach involved launching the load us- ing a catapult or trebuchet, closely followed by the use of drones and ziplines. Other innovative ideas included raft sys- tems, automatic underground tubing systems, amphibious au- tomated guided vehicles, autonomous sky-cranes, boats, tug- boat ferry systems, pressure guns, and airplane drops, among others."
