## Evidence

### Wiggins Benjamin L. 2017

Wiggins Benjamin L., Eddy Sarah L., Wener-Fligner Leah, Freisem Karen, Grunspan Daniel Z., Theobald Elli J., Timbrook Jerry, Crowe Alison J. (2017). ASPECT: A Survey to Assess Student Perspective of Engagement in an Active-Learning Classroom. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0244

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Linear mixed-effects model results (Table 3) from the repeated-measures comparison of short clicker-based versus long worksheet activity days in introductory biology, showing activity-type and ethnicity effects on the Value construct with white students and the short activity as reference levels.

> "on average, students value the long activity less than the short activity (Table 3, a–c), Asian-American students value the activity more than white students (Table 3, b and c), and Asian-American students and international students both value the long activity more than white students (Table 3c)."
