## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Concern that internet resources cannot be distinguished as good or bad decreased (54.7% in 2020 vs. 35% in 2022) while inability to communicate face to face increased (35.9% vs. 45.5%), and each disadvantage was cross-associated with different teaching-method preferences.
