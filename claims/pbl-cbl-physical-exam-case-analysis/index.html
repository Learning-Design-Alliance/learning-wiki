Evidence¶

Xiong Xianghua 2025¶

Xiong Xianghua, Xu Jiongnan, Luo Mingyan, Niu Dongsheng, Bi Qing, Wang Zhaofu, Zhang Jun. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning combined with case-based learning versus lecture-based learning in orthopedic education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07741-3

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 3 studies (202 students) comparing physical examination scores. The pooled estimate favored PBL-CBL: "SMD = 1.64, 95% CI: 0.83–2.44, P < 0.0001" with high heterogeneity (I2 = 82%).

"The PBL-CBL group had significantly higher physical examination scores than the LBL group (SMD = 1.64, 95% CI: 0.83–2.44, P < 0.0001, I2 = 82%)"

Xiong Xianghua 2025 (2)¶

Xiong Xianghua, Xu Jiongnan, Luo Mingyan, Niu Dongsheng, Bi Qing, Wang Zhaofu, Zhang Jun. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning combined with case-based learning versus lecture-based learning in orthopedic education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07741-3

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 4 studies (248 students) on case analysis scores. The pooled estimate favored PBL-CBL: "SMD = 1.30, 95% CI: 0.65–1.95, P < 0.0001" with high heterogeneity (I2 = 81%).

"The PBL-CBL group demonstrated significantly higher case analysis scores than the LBL group (SMD = 1.30, 95% CI: 0.65–1.95, P < 0.0001, I2 = 81%)"
