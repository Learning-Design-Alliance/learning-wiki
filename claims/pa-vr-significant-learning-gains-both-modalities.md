## Evidence

### Aimilios Hadjiliasi 2024

Aimilios Hadjiliasi, Louis Nisiotis, Irene Polycarpou. (2024). A Comparative Assessment of Technology Acceptance and Learning Outcomes in Computer-based versus VR-based Pedagogical Agents. [doi:10.1109/ismar-adjunct64951.2024.00148](https://doi.org/10.1109/ismar-adjunct64951.2024.00148)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparative experiment with 92 undergraduates randomly split into control (3D desktop) and experimental (VR) groups of 46 each; pre/post difference values were inspected and, after a Shapiro-Wilk normality violation, tested with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test, which revealed "statistical signiﬁcance" for both groups.

> "The test revealed that this difference, for both groups, is of statistical signiﬁcance, indicating that lea rning has been effectively achieved in the use of both groups for learn ing."
