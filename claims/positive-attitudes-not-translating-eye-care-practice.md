## Evidence

### Y. Zhang 2025

Y. Zhang, C. Li, Y. Zhou, and X. Yu. (2025). Knowledge, Attitudes and Practices of Critical Care Nurses Regarding Eye Care Across 17 Hospitals: A Multicentre Cross‐Sectional Survey. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70120

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Attitude dimension results from the survey (mean 31.48 ± 4.39; 83.26% at excellent level ≥ 28 points) show that "only 53.83% prioritised eye care in clinical practice" despite high importance ratings. The lowest-scoring attitude items concerned priority given to eye care, willingness, and standard endotracheal suctioning.

> "Specifically, 67.33% considered such care 'extremely important', and 58.14% expressed strong willingness to perform it. However, only 53.83% prioritised eye care in clinical practice."
