## Evidence

### Archana Prabu Kumar 2026

Archana Prabu Kumar, Padmavathi R, Maheshkumar K, Abirami Omprakash, Prabu Kumar Chokkalingam Mani, B W C Sathiyasekaran, Maruthy K N. (2026). E-Learning Modules Based on Bloom Taxonomy and the Miller Pyramid for First-Year Indian Medical Students: Randomized Controlled Study in Medical Education. JMIR Human Factors. https://doi.org/10.2196/84339

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized controlled study (N=690), OSCE manipulation scores (6 marks) were compared between groups. The authors report "superior psychomotor performance (d=0.35; 4.55 vs 4.10; P<.001)" and attribute it to visual demonstrations and procedural guidance in the modules.

> "The intervention group demonstrated superior psychomotor performance (d=0.35; 4.55 vs 4.10; P<.001), suggesting that visual demonstrations and procedural guidance in e-modules effectively enhanced motor skill acquisition."
