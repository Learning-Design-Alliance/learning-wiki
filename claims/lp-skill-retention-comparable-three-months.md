Cross-sectional follow-up comparison among the 42 of 88 participants who returned at 3 months (19 VRT, 23 CT), none reporting interim LP exposure. "the total number of key points completed did not differ significantly between groups"; rating score (P = 0.789) and time (P = 0.140) were also comparable.
