# During feedback provision, both content-specific support (g = 0.48) and generic support (g = 0.55) positively affect peer-feedback processes and outcomes, with no significant difference between them

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Content-specific support during feedback provision has a positive effect vs. no support (g = 0.48). [→ Hornstein 2025](#hornstein-2025)
`q4 i2` Generic support during feedback provision has a positive effect vs. no support (g = 0.55). [→ Hornstein 2025 (2)](#hornstein-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Hornstein 2025

Hornstein, J., Keller, M. V., Greisel, M., Dresel, M., & Kollar, I. (2025). Enhancing the Peer-Feedback Process Through Instructional Support: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10017-3

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 20 studies (k = 99 effect sizes) for content-specific support during feedback provision: g = 0.48, 95% CI [0.09, 0.87], p < 0.05 (Table 3).

> "Regarding RQ3b, we found small to medium-sized positive effects for content-specific and generic support during the feedback provision phase compared to peer-feedback without instructional support during the feedback provision phase."

### Hornstein 2025 (2)

Hornstein, J., Keller, M. V., Greisel, M., Dresel, M., & Kollar, I. (2025). Enhancing the Peer-Feedback Process Through Instructional Support: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10017-3

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 13 studies (k = 79 effect sizes) for generic support during feedback provision: g = 0.55, 95% CI [0.26, 0.83], p < 0.01 (Table 3).

> "Regarding RQ3b, we found small to medium-sized positive effects for content-specific and generic support during the feedback provision phase compared to peer-feedback without instructional support during the feedback provision phase."

### Hornstein 2025 (3)

Hornstein, J., Keller, M. V., Greisel, M., Dresel, M., & Kollar, I. (2025). Enhancing the Peer-Feedback Process Through Instructional Support: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10017-3

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-regression comparison of content-specific vs. generic support effects during feedback provision found no significant difference (F(1, 3.84) = 0.14, p = 0.72).

> "the effects regarding the comparisons between content-specific support during peer-feedback and peer-feedback without instructional support on the one hand and generic support during peer-feedback and peer-feedback without instructional support on the other hand were not significantly different from each other during the feedback provision phase (F(1, 3.84) = 0.14, p = 0.72)"
