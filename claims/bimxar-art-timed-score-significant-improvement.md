# ART timed scores improved significantly after the BIMxAR learning session

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After a self-learning session with BIMxAR, participants' ART timed scores (score divided by completion time) improved significantly from pretest to posttest. [→ Ziad Ashour 2022](#ziad-ashour-2022)
`q2 i?` The mean ART timed score improved by 52.26% from pretest to posttest. [→ Ziad Ashour 2022 (2)](#ziad-ashour-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Ziad Ashour 2022

Ziad Ashour, Zohreh Shaghaghian, Wei Yan. (2022). BIMxAR: BIM-Empowered Augmented Reality for Learning Architectural Representations. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.03207](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.03207)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Timed-score analysis (score divided by completion time) in the pilot study's posttest phase: the Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed-rank test for ART timed scores "revealed a significant improvement from the pretest to the posttest sessions (p = 0.0313)".

> "Similarly, the results from the Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed-rank test for the ART timed scores with a significance level of 0.05 revealed a significant improvement from the pretest to the posttest sessions (p = 0.0313)."

### Ziad Ashour 2022 (2)

Ziad Ashour, Zohreh Shaghaghian, Wei Yan. (2022). BIMxAR: BIM-Empowered Augmented Reality for Learning Architectural Representations. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.03207](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.03207)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive timed-score results from the same pilot study: "the mean timed score in the ART (mean pretest = 4.60, mean posttest = 7.01) improved by 52.26%".

> "Additionally, the mean timed score in the ART (mean pretest = 4.60, mean posttest = 7.01) improved by 52.26%."
