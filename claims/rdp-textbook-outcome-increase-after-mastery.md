### Müller PC 2024

Comparison of textbook outcome before (n = 952) and after (n = 157) the mastery cutoff; the article reports the rate of such outcomes "increasing thereafter (63.4% vs 70.7%; P = 0.028)".

> "Analysis of textbook outcome revealed a cutoff for mastery at 73 RDP procedures, with the rate of such outcomes increasing thereafter (63.4% vs 70.7%; P = 0.028; Fig. 2C)."
