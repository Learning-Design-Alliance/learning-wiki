## Evidence

### Mannarino Candace MD 2020

Mannarino Candace MD, Bradley Erin MD, Puro Amanda MD, Sung Deborah MD, Wolfe Katie MD. (2020). Pathophysiology for the Pediatric Critical Care Fellow: Three Representative Simulation Cases. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10931

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous Likert evaluations from the six fellows participating in the status epilepticus simulation case. Five of six strongly agreed across the rated items; the article reports no statistical test or effect size for this difference from the other cases.

> "For case 3, five of the six fellow learners strongly agreed that the content was relevant, the case was challenging, the simulation would improve their clinical practice, and the debriefing allowed all learners the opportunity to speak, as well as addressed teamwork and communication issues."
