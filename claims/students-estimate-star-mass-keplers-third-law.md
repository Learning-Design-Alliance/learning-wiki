## Evidence

### Ollé 2022

Ollé, H. and Kovács, T. (2022). What Hides within the Photograph: the Analysis of a Light Curve in the Classroom. Physics Education. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac68c3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac68c3)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Kepler's-third-law calculation using the student-derived orbit and period, with the planet's mass neglected for simplicity; the result was "1.4823± 0.4107 solar mass", against the printed real value of 1.500±0.03 solar masses.

> "The mass of the star was thus 1.4823± 0.4107 solar mass, in reality it is 1.500± 0.03 solar mass [12, 13]."
