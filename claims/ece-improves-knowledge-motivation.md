## Evidence

### Shaban Eman E. 2025

Shaban Eman E., Elmelliti Hussam, Shaban Amira, Shaban Ahmed, Zaki Hany A. (2025). Impact of early clinical exposure on medical students’ competency development: a systematic review of evidence from Asian and African countries. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08103-9

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review with narrative synthesis (15 studies, 2,579 medical students in Asian and African countries; a meta-analysis was planned but not run because of heterogeneity). The knowledge-score finding comes from one included study, Warkar and Asia (2021), an experimental interventional study of 150 first-year students in India, whose mean test scores rose from 48% to 66% after ECE. The motivation finding comes from students' perceptions in several included studies (Fig. 2). The review notes that most outcomes it synthesised were self-reported.

> "Warkar and colleagues reported that the mean test scores significantly increased from 48% to 66% after ECE intervention, suggesting that ECE has a significant effect on the learning gain of medical students"
