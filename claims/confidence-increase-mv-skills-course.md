## Evidence

### Rocha de Macedo Bruno 2025

Rocha de Macedo Bruno, Lima Carolina Saldanha, Haydar Ahmed, Holanda Marcelo Alcantara, Hayashi Fatima Kiyoko, Ferreira Juliana Carvalho. (2025). Impact of a Competency-based Mechanical Ventilation Course Using Virtual Simulation. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.34197/ats-scholar.2024-0083OC

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey data (Kirkpatrick level 1) using a five-level Likert confidence scale collected at baseline and course end from the 81 completing residents. Recognition of asynchrony confidence rose from 19% to 44.8% fairly or completely confident; "Feeling 'not confident at all' was not reported at the end of the course". Percentages are descriptive; no effect size is printed.

> "Confidence increased for all skills except for setting the parameters for patients with obstructive lung disease. At baseline, 19% of participants felt fairly or completely confident in recognizing patient–ventilator asynchrony, increasing to 44.8% at the end."
