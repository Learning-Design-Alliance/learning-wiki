# Players who completed the game reported enjoying the game more and finding it more humorous, but completion was not significantly associated with liking Jo

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Players who completed the game reported enjoying the game more (χ²(df=1)=23.56, p<.0001) and finding the game more humorous (F(2,7113)=23.56, p=.0005) than students who had not completed it. [→ Gagnon 2022](#gagnon-2022)
`q3 i?` Player completion was not statistically significantly associated with how much they liked Jo (χ²(df=1)=2.54, p=.111). [→ Gagnon 2022 (2)](#gagnon-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Gagnon 2022

Gagnon, D. J., Baker, R. S., Gagnon, S., Swanson, L., Spevacek, N., Andres, J., Harpstead, E., Scianna, J., Slater, S., San Pedro, M. O.C.Z. (2022). Exploring players' experience of humor and snark in a grade 3-6 history practices game. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09906](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09906)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis within the randomized experiment relating player attitudes to in-game completion measures. The article states these findings match Dormann & Biddle (2006) and lend additional evidence for humor sustaining engagement in educational games.

> "players who completed the game reported enjoying the game more, χ2 (df = 1) = 23.56, p <.0001, as well as finding the game more humorous, F(2, 7113) = 23.56, p = .0005]"

### Gagnon 2022 (2)

Gagnon, D. J., Baker, R. S., Gagnon, S., Swanson, L., Spevacek, N., Andres, J., Harpstead, E., Scianna, J., Slater, S., San Pedro, M. O.C.Z. (2022). Exploring players' experience of humor and snark in a grade 3-6 history practices game. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09906](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09906)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Test of the association between Jo's likeability and game completion in the same correlational analysis. No statistically significant association was detected; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, player completion was not statistically significantly associated with how much they liked Jo, χ 2 (df = 1) = 2.54, p = .111."
