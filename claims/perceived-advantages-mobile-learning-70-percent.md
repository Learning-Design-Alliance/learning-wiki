Abdulhafeth A. Khrisat & Salameh Saleem Mahmoud. (2013). Integrating Mobile Phones into the EFL Foundation Year Classroom in King Abdulaziz University/KSA: Effects on Achievement in General English and Students' Attitudes. English Language Teaching, 6(8). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n8p162

Descriptive questionnaire results from the same forty-student sample, covering items on knowledge gain, flexibility of time and place, cost, and self-learning. The article reports "70.3% responded positively"; no inferential test is reported.

> "A summary of the percentages of the seven items shows that 14.2% of the sample responded negatively to the seven items of the second subgroup that is “the advantages of using mobile phones in the EFL classroom", whereas 15.3% were neutral and 70.3% responded positively"
