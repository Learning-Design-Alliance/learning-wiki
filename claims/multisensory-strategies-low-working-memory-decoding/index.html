Research and the Promise of Personalized Learning 2015¶

Research and the Promise of Personalized Learning. (2015). Digital Promise Global, Making Learning Personal for All series. https://global.digitalpromise.org

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The white paper asserts, citing research without naming a study or printing an effect size, that multisensory strategies such as audio books and manipulatives help students with low working memory decode better by "activating different parts of the brain."

"Multisensory literacy strategies, such as audio books, manipulatives, word games and songs, help students with low working memory become better at decoding by activating different parts of the brain."
