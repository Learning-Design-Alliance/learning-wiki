## Evidence

### Torppa 2025

Torppa, M., Psyridou, M., Khanolainen, D., Koponen, T., & Salminen, J. (2025). Role of home math environment in the intergenerational transmission of math skills. Learning and Instruction, 97, Article 102080. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102080

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model (Model 1G) on the Finnish VUOKKO longitudinal sample (N = 402) with latent factors of parental tested skills predicting children's Grade 1 and 2 arithmetic fluency; the model "fit the data well" (χ2(29) = 34.11, p = .24, RMSEA = .02, CFI = 1).

> "suggested that both mothers’ and fathers’ math skills significantly pre- dicted children’s arithmetic skills in Grade 1. Grade 2 arithmetic skill variance was strongly predicted by Grade 1 arithmetic skills, suggesting high stability in the skills. However, fathers’ math skills (but not mothers’) remained a significant predictor of Grade 2 arithmetic fluency"
