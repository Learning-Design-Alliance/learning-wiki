# Students’ environmental knowledge scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test after eight weeks of blog activities, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students’ environmental knowledge scores increased significantly after eight weeks of blog activities (t(55) = -2.90, p = 0.005). [→ Saltan 2017](#saltan-2017)

## Evidence

### Saltan 2017

Saltan, F., & Divarci, O. F. (2017). Using Blogs to Improve Elementary School Students’ Environmental Literacy in Science Class. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(3), 347-355. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.3.347

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures t-test on 56 students’ environmental knowledge subscale scores before and after the eight-week blog intervention. Post-test mean (5.32) was higher than pre-test mean (4.82), a statistically significant difference (t(55) = -2.90, p = 0.005).

> "The results have shown that there is a statistically significant difference between pre-test (m=4.82, sd=1.69) and post-test(m=5.32, sd=1.95) scores in terms of the environmental knowledge of participants (t(55)= -2.90, p=0.005)."
