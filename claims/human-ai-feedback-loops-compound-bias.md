`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The chapter reports, citing Lum and Isaac (2016), a simulation study of the PredPol predictive policing software: it targeted Black people at twice the rate of white people, and a simulated feedback loop in which policing activity affects future crime data further amplified existing biases. The chapter uses this as an analogy for AIEd feedback loops.

> "Lum and Isaac (2016) showed that PredPol, a popular predictive policing software, reinforced existing biases in the dataset and targeted Black people at disproportionately high rates (twice as high as white people, even though drug use was roughly equal between the two groups)"
