`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meta-analysis of 26 single-case design studies (Bowman-Perrott et al., 2013) applying What Works Clearinghouse standards, examining peer tutoring effects across reading, spelling, math, vocabulary, and social studies for grades 1-12.

> "They noted findings that suggested that peer tutoring is an effective intervention regardless of dosage, grade level, or disability status and that among students with disabilities those with EBD benefitted most."
