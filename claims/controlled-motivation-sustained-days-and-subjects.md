## Evidence

### Malmberg and & Martin 2019

Lars-Erik Malmberg, Andrew J. Martin. (2019). Processes of Students' Effort Exertion, Competence Beliefs and Motivation: Cyclic and Dynamic Effects of Learning Experiences within School Days and School Subjects. Contemporary Educational Psychology (manuscript CEDPSYCH 2018-216-R2). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.03.013

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Joint MSEM (Model 3). Controlled motivation showed significant autoregressive effects of similar magnitude for both lag types; the difference between them was not significant. Authors interpret this as consistency of teachers' expectations.

> "controlled motivation at time T was equally sustained within days (ρ = .37; p < .001) as within school-subjects (ρ = .34; p < .001), not significantly different from each other (∆B = .05; p = .662)."
