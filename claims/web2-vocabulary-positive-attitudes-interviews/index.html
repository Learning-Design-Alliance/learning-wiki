Evidence¶

Eren 2015¶

Omer Eren. (2015). Vocabulary Learning on Learner-Created Content by Using Web 2.0 Tools. Contemporary Educational Technology, 6(4), 281-300. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105759

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Semi-structured interviews conducted after the post-test with 18 experimental-group students; transcriptions and notes were coded into two themes. The article reports "A total of 16 students out of 18" agreed the tools were very useful for vocabulary.

"Findings for the interview question 1 suggest that almost all students consider Web 2.0 tools very useful for improving their vocabularies. A total of 16 students out of 18 agree on this point."
