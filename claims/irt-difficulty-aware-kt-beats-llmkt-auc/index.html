Related Claims¶

  • LLMKT outperforms existing knowledge tracing methods at predicting student turn correctness in the CoMTA and MathDial tutoring dialogue datasets, and generally outperforms DKT-Sem. — related
  • Knowledge tracing performance on tutoring dialogues is relatively low, with a maximum of around 76% AUC, which the authors take to show dialogueKT is a challenging task. — related
  • DKT-Sem, a DKT variant using semantic text embeddings, performs better than existing KT methods on tutoring dialogues, with a smaller margin on the larger MathDial dataset. — related
