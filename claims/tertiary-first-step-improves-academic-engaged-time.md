# Tertiary First Step improves academic engaged time within the intervention group

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Within the intervention group, academic engaged time improved from a mean of 59% (SD = 17%) at baseline to 75% (SD = 16%) at post-intervention, a large within-subject effect (partial r = .82). [→ Frey 2015](#frey-2015)

## Evidence

### Frey 2015

Frey, A. J., Small, J. W., Lee, J., Walker, H. M., Seeley, J. R., Feil, E. G., & Golly, A. (2015). Expanding the range of the First Step to Success intervention: Tertiary-level support for children, teachers, and families. Early Childhood Research Quarterly 30 (2015) 1–11. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2014.05.002

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject ANOVA on direct-observation Academic Engaged Time data (three 20-minute observations per time point) for the 33 intervention students. The article reports AET "improved on average from 59% (SD = 17%) to 75% (SD = 16%)" with partial r = .82, a large effect.

> "Within-subject effect sizes for the academic domain were .25 for academic competence (F[1,29] = 1.80, p = .190) and .82 for AET (F[1,32] = 62.17, p < .001). After receiving the intervention, student AET improved on average from 59% (SD = 17%) to 75% (SD = 16%)"
