Qualitative framework analysis of staff interviews, observations and Stakeholder Groups across all six cases, organised by NPT constructs. The article reports that "Staff felt forced to prioritise within priorities", concentrating effort on service-user rather than carer contacts, and that all staff were working at or beyond capacity.

> "Staff felt forced to prioritise within priorities, leading to concentration of efforts on measureable outcomes (service user contacts rather than carer contacts), with financial incentives attached."
