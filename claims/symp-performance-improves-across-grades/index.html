Brankaer 2017¶

Brankaer, C., Ghesquière, P., & De Smedt, B. (2017). Symbolic magnitude processing in elementary school children: A group administered paper-and-pencil measure (SYMP Test). Behavior Research Methods, 49, 1361–1373. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-016-0792-3

q3 · i3 · design · r3

One-way ANOVA on the full sample (n = 1,588) comparing grades on the one-digit subtest, F(5,1582) = 538.55, ηp² = .63, with Tukey post-hoc tests showing every grade differed from every other. A similar pattern held for the two-digit subtest (ηp² = .57).

Related Claims¶
