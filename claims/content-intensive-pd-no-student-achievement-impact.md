## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Garet 2016

Garet, M. S., Heppen, J. B., Walters, K., Parkinson, J., Smith, T. M., Song, M., Garrett, R., Yang, R., & Borman, G. D. (2016). Focusing on teachers' mathematical knowledge: The impact of content-intensive professional development (NCEE 2016-4010). Washington, D.C.: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs/20164010

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Spring 2014 outcomes: study-administered NWEA-aligned adaptive assessment (806 treatment, 891 control students) and state math assessment in Normal Curve Equivalents (1,760 treatment, 1,917 control students). The state-assessment difference was statistically significant at the 0.05 level; the study-administered one was not. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "On average, treatment teach- ers' students scored 2 percentile points lower than control teachers' students on both spring 2014 stu- dent achievement measures (see Figure 3)."
