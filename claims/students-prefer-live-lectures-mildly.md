## Evidence

### Brockfeld 2018

Brockfeld, T., Müller, B., & de Laffolie, J. (2018). Video versus live lecture courses: a comparative evaluation of lecture types and results. Medical Education Online, 23(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2018.1555434

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preference ratings on a bipolar scale from 271 respondents in the Göttingen crossover course, reported in Results with the full frequency distribution in Table 2. The article states "the students obviously prefer live lessons".

> "One hundred and thirty out of 271 (48%) prefer live lessons, 69 (25%) rated ‘neutral’ and 72 (27%) prefer the video lessons."
