Ehrlich 2014¶

Ehrlich, S. B., Gwynne, J. A., Pareja, A. S., & Allensworth, E. M. (2014). Preschool Attendance in Chicago Public Schools: Relationships with Learning Outcomes and Reasons for Absences. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED555650

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal analysis tracking CPS students from preschool through second grade. The report finds "chronically absent preschool children are five times more likely to be chronically absent in second grade than their non-chronically absent preschool peers."

"The relationship between preschool attendance and attendance in later years continues into the elementary grades: chronically absent preschool children are five times more likely to be chronically absent in second grade than their non-chronically absent preschool peers."
