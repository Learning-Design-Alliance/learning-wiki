## Evidence

### Yen-Fen Lee 2025

Yen-Fen Lee, Gwo-Jen Hwang, Pei-Ying Chen. (2025). Technology-based interactive guidance to promote learning performance and self-regulation: a chatbot-assisted self-regulated learning approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10478-x

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of posttest learning motivation (Wang & Chen questionnaire, Cronbach's alpha 0.79) adjusting for pretest in the two-class quasi-experiment; experimental adjusted mean 4.02 vs control 3.70, "a significant level (F = 4.10, p < 0.05)", η² = .09.

> "the variance caused by the group reached a significant level (F = 4.10, p < 0.05), indicating that the scores of the posttest questionnaire on learning motivation differed significantly depending on the learning mode students adopted."
