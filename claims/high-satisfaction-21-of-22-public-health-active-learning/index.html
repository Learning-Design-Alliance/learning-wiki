"Across studies, student satisfaction was high, with 21 of 22 studies (95.4%) reporting positive satisfaction outcomes. Improvements were noted in critical thinking and problem-solving (14 studies; 63.6%) and communication skills (12 studies; 54.5%; table 2)."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Of 22 included studies on active learning in public health education, 11 used PBL only, 6 used TBL, 3 integrated PBL and CBL, and 2 used CBL only — related
  • No included studies applied AI-based modalities specifically to public health education, though external reviews report positive satisfaction with AI-assisted PBL and CBL — related
  • Student preference for CBL, PBL or TBL over traditional teaching was reported in 15 of 22 studies (68.1%), but only two studies used a controlled comparison with lecture — related
  • Student-centered innovative strategies (PBL, EBL, simulation, TBL, blended and online learning) showed positive effects in pharmaceutical care learning — related
  • Problem-based learning shows a large positive pooled effect on academic achievement (REM ES = 1.560), with heterogeneous study effects — related
  • Findings from other postgraduate specialties suggest PBL and CBL improve performance, knowledge and confidence, but overall RCT quality in this field is deemed unsatisfactory — related
  • CBL, PBL, and TBL each significantly improve nursing students' problem-solving abilities — related
  • TBL increases student satisfaction and engagement in ethics education — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
