### Arnold 2012

Arnold, D. H., Kupersmidt, J. B., Voegler-Lee, M. E., & Marshall, N. (2012). The Association between Preschool Children's Social Functioning and Their Emergent Academic Skills. Early Childhood Research Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2011.12.009

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

HLM analysis of 467 preschoolers nested in 84 classrooms regressed each academic outcome on inattention, aggression, and social skills. The article reports "Attention problems were associated with both lower preliteracy and lower language scores, controlling for aggression and social skills" (Table 3 standardized weights).
