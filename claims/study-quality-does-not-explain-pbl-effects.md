## Evidence

### Dolmans Diana H. J. M. 2015

Dolmans Diana H. J. M., Loyens Sofie M. M., Marcq Hélène, Gijbels David. (2015). Deep and surface learning in problem-based learning: a review of the literature. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9645-6

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Vote-count comparison by methodological quality in the review: high-quality studies (n = 8) yielded deep-approach ES = .13 and surface ES = −.01; medium–low quality studies (n = 13) yielded deep ES = .07 and surface ES = .17. All sign tests were not significant.

> "The effect size of .07 indicates there is no or a very small effect."
