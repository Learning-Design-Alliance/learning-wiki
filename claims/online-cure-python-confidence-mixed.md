# Student confidence in Python coding skills was lower and more variable than confidence in other course skills

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students were less confident performing actions in Python than in other course activities, with the widest spread of survey responses on Python items and least confidence manipulating arrays. [→ Hom 2024](#hom-2024)

## Evidence

### Hom 2024

Hom, J., Patience, J., Knierman, K., Simon, M. N., and Austin, A. (2024). A Pilot Study from the First Course-Based Undergraduate Research Experience for Online Degree-Seeking Astronomy Students. [doi:10.32374/2024.4.1.087aep](https://doi.org/10.32374/2024.4.1.087aep)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course survey (Question 12) of the SES 294 pilot, N=8. Python sub-item means ranged from 2.75 (create and manipulate arrays) to 3.50 (assign variables) on a 1-5 scale, with standard deviations up to 1.51; descriptive only, no effect sizes.

> "Overall, students were less confident in performing actions in Python compared to other activities in the course."
