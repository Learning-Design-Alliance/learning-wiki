## Evidence

### Filippou 2018

Filippou, F., Rokka, S., Pitsi, A., Gargalianos, D., Bebetsos, E., & Filippou, D. A. (2018). Interdisciplinary Greek Traditional Dance Course: Impact on Student Satisfaction and Anxiety. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 363-374. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11325a

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis among experimental-group students relating satisfaction to the anxiety inventory subscales. The article reports "a positive correlation between satisfaction and self -confidence (r=.546, p<.01)", plus negative correlations with somatic anxiety (r=-.445) and cognitive anxiety (r=-.514), both p<.01.
