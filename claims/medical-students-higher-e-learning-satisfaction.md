### Cofini Vincenza 2022

Cofini Vincenza, Perilli Enrico, Moretti Annalucia, Bianchini Valeria, Perazzini Matteo, Muselli Mario, Lanzi Sabrina, Tobia Loreta, Fabiani Leila, Necozione Stefano. (2022). E-Learning Satisfaction, Stress, Quality of Life, and Coping: A Cross-Sectional Study in Italian University Students a Year after the COVID-19 Pandemic Began. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19138214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate one-way ANOVA in a cross-sectional survey of 471 Italian university students. E-learning satisfaction "was significantly related to course attendance (F = 5.35; p = 0.005)" with Bonferroni-adjusted differences between medicine and other courses. No standardized effect size was printed for this finding.

> "it was significantly related to course attendance (F = 5.35; p = 0.005), as significant differences were found between students in medicine and other courses (p Bonferroni < 0.05)."
