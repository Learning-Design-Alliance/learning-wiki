`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of final academic performance between the two cohorts; course evaluation (6 quizzes 30%, exam 35%, project 35%) was identical across years. The authors explicitly hedge the grade difference due to the small sample.

> "Finally, EC had the best overall academic performance with an average final grade of 61% compared to C G's 53%.  However, due to the relatively low number of participants, additional studies are needed to identify possible correlations between gamification and academic performance."
