## Evidence

### Sampasa-Kanyinga 2022

Sampasa-Kanyinga, H., Hamilton, H. A., Goldfield, G. S., & Chaput, J.-P. (2022). Problem Technology Use, Academic Performance, and School Connectedness among Adolescents. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 19(4), 2337. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19042337

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Ordered logistic regression (Table 4, Model 2, adjusted covariates) in the same cross-sectional sample of 4837 Ontario secondary students. School connectedness was a three-item composite (feeling close to people at school, part of the school, safe) with Cronbach's alpha 0.68. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "Having moderate-to-serious symptoms of problem technology use was associated with lower levels of school connectedness among males (AOR = 0.65, 95% CI = 0.50−0.86) and females (AOR = 0.63, 95% CI = 0.51−0.78)."
