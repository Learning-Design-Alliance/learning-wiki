### Martin O. Archer 2021

Martin O. Archer, Jennifer DeWitt, Charlotte Thorley, and Olivia Keenan. (2021). Evaluating participants' experience of extended interaction with cutting-edge physics research through the PRiSE 'research in schools' programme. [doi:10.5194/gc-4-147-2021](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-4-147-2021)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of PRiSE survey ratings against the Vennix et al. (2017) benchmark of 729 high-school students and 35 teachers across 12 STEM outreach activities. The one-sample Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed PRiSE "perceived considerably more positively than usual" for both students (p = 1 × 10−15) and teachers (p = 1 × 10−7).

> "This comparison reveals that PRiSE seems to be perceived considerably more positively than usual by both students (benchmark average 3.66 ± 0.01, p = 1 × 10−15 in a one-sample Wilcoxon signed-rank test) and teachers (benchmark average 3.84 ± 0.08, p = 1 × 10−7)"
