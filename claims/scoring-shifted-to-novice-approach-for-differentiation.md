A. Mason, E. Cohen, E. Yerushalmi, and C. Singh. (2007). Identifying Differences In Diagnostic Skills Between Physics Students: Developing A Rubric. [doi:10.1063/1.3021239](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3021239)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Scoring-section account of the researchers' own prioritization during rubric use: scoring began under the first approach, in which each ideal subcategory is worth 1 point if + and 0 if -, and was later changed to the second approach for the RSD. The article states the reason in its own words; no quantitative comparison is printed.

> "Initially, we took the 1 st approach when we scored the quiz solution and the self-diagnosis (RSD). Later on, we took the 2 nd approach for the RSD as it allowed better differentiating between students."
