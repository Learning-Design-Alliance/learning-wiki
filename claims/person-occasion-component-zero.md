## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Aggregate performance averaged over tasks did not vary from one occasion to another, despite occasion-level changes in approach. [→ Shavelson et al. 1999](#shavelson-et-al-1999)

## Evidence

### Shavelson et al. 1999

Richard J. Shavelson, Maria Araceli Ruiz-Primo, Edward W. Wiley. (1999). Note on Sources of Sampling Variability in Science Performance Assessments. Journal of Educational Measurement, 36(1), 61-71. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Note+on+Sources+of+Sampling+Variability+in+Science+Performance+Assessments

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Across both retest G studies (Tables 1 and 2), the authors report that "the estimated variance component for the person x occasion effect is zero", meaning students' average performance level was stable across occasions even as their task approaches changed.

> "Note, however, that the estimated variance component for the person x occasion effect is zero (Tables 1 and 2)."
