# Genetic variance in learning motivation fully mediates the gene-by-SES interaction on four-year-olds' math achievement

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Once genetic variance in motivation was accounted for, the residual gene-by-SES interaction on math achievement was not statistically significant, indicating full mediation. [→ Tucker-Drob 2011](#tucker-drob-2011)

## Evidence

### Tucker-Drob 2011

Tucker-Drob, Elliot M., & Harden, K. Paige. (2011). Learning Motivation Mediates Gene-by-Socioeconomic Status Interaction on Mathematics Achievement in Early Childhood. Learning and Individual Differences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2011.11.015

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate Cholesky model with motivation as predictor and math as outcome in the ECLS-B twin sample. The ay′ parameter was not statistically significant, and the authors state the interaction was "fully mediated by genetic differences in learning motivation."

> "There is no residual gene-by-SES interaction on Math after accounting for the effects of motivation. Thus, in the current sample, the previously documented gene-by-SES interaction on Math Achievement was fully mediated by genetic differences in learning motivation."
