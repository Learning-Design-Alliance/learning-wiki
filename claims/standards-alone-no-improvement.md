## Evidence

### Azkiyah et al. 2014

S.N. Azkiyah, Simone Doolaard, Bert P. M. Creemers, M.P.C. (Greetje) Van Der Werf. (2014). The Effects of Two Intervention Programs on Teaching Quality and Student Achievement. Journal of Classroom Interaction, 49(1), 4-11. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1100411

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experimental comparison in which experimental group 1 received only the elaborated standards document plus a one-day workshop; no significant advantage over control emerged on observed teaching quality or multilevel-analyzed student achievement. Equivalence was not tested.

> "This study has proved that education standards on its own did not lead to the improvement of either teaching quality or student achievement."
