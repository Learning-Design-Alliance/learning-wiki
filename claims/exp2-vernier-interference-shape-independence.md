# Experiment 2 replicates Vernier anterograde interference and Shape independence with no retrograde interference

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a two-task design, sequence position impeded initial Vernier performance, left shape search unaffected, and produced no retrograde interference in either task. [→ Jia Yang 2025](#jia-yang-2025)

## Evidence

### Jia Yang 2025

Jia Yang, Fang-Fang Yan, Tingting Wang, Zile Wang, Qingshang Ma, Jinmei Xiao, Xianyuan Yang, Zhong-Lin Lu & Chang-Bing Huang. (2025). Anterograde interference in multitask perceptual learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00312-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 (N = 14, two groups trained in Vernier and shape search in opposite orders, with next-day re-test): "anterograde interference was evident in the Vernier task, while independence was observed in the Shape task"; the Vernier sequence-position coefficient on initial performance was −0.09.

> "In summary, anterograde interference was evident in the Vernier task, while independence was observed in the Shape task. Furthermore, no retrograde interference was detected in either task."
