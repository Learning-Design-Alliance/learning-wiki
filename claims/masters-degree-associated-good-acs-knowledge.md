# Nurses with a master's degree were significantly more likely to have good knowledge of ACS than Bachelor degree holders (AOR=3.801)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In multivariable logistic regression, having a master's degree was significantly associated with good knowledge of ACS compared to being a Bachelor degree holder (AOR=3.801, 95% CI 1.314–10.996, P=0.014). [→ Lakew Bizuayehu Ashine 2023](#lakew-bizuayehu-ashine-2023)

## Evidence

### Lakew Bizuayehu Ashine 2023

Lakew Bizuayehu Ashine, Bekele Daniel M, Alemu Kassa Wudma, Dugassa Tolera Boka. (2023). Suboptimal Knowledge and Care of Patients with Acute Coronary Syndrome Among Nurses Working in Selected Hospitals in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia 2021. Vascular Health and Risk Management. https://doi.org/10.2147/VHRM.S422339

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression within the cross-sectional survey of 252 acute care nurses; master's degree remained significantly associated with good ACS knowledge after adjustment, with the article reporting "AOR=3.801, 95% CI (1.314–10.996), P =0.014".

> "having master's degree [AOR=3.801, 95% CI (1.314–10.996), P =0.014] and having clinical practice guidelines in their working unit [AOR= 10.998, 95% CI (2.478–48.805), P =0.002] were found more likely to have good knowledge regarding ACS"
