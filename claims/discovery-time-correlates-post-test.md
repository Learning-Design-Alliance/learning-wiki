## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The discovery-then-instruction group's time spent correlated moderately with post-test score (r = 0.42, p = 0.07) but not retention test score (r = 0.17, p = 0.51). [→ Xu J 2018](#xu-j-2018)

## Evidence

### Xu J 2018

Xu J, Campisi P, Forte V, Carrillo B, Vescan A, Brydges R. (2018). Effectiveness of discovery learning using a mobile otoscopy simulator on knowledge acquisition and retention in medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-018-0317-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

A correlational analysis within the discovery-then-instruction group (n = 19) found time spent "correlated moderately with their post-test score (r = 0.42, p = 0.07)". The correlation with retention was non-significant.

> "We did find that the discovery then instruction group's time spent with the simulator correlated moderately with their post-test score (r = 0.42, p = 0.07), but not their retention test score (r = 0.17, p = 0.51)."
