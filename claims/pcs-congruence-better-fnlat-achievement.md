# Learning achievement in the fundamental nursing test is significantly better when students' preferred and perceived learning environments are congruent by the PCS distance method

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students whose preferred environment was aligned with their perceived environment (PrAPc_PCS) performed significantly better on the FNLAT achievement test than students whose environments were discordant (PrDPc_PCS). [→ Yeh Ting-Kuang 2016](#yeh-ting-kuang-2016)

## Evidence

### Yeh Ting-Kuang 2016

Yeh Ting-Kuang, Huang Hsiu-Mei, Chan Wing P, Chang Chun-Yen. (2016). Effects of congruence between preferred and perceived learning environments in nursing education in Taiwan: a cross-sectional study. BMJ Open 6(5): e009925. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-009925

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-tailed t test comparison of FNLAT post-test scores between the congruent and discordant groups formed by the preferred-perceived spaces (PCS) distance method; the congruent "group performed significantly better in FNLAT". Group sizes were equal halves of the sample by PCS score.

> "Learning achievement, self-efficacy in nursing, and attitudes towards nursing were better when the instructional activities and students' preferred learning environment were congruent. The PrAPc_PCS group performed significantly better in FNLAT than did the PrDPc_PCS group, as shown in table 2."
