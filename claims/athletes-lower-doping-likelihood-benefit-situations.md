## Evidence

### Deng Zhangyan 2022

Deng Zhangyan, Guo Jinyang, Wang Dong, Huang Tao, Chen Zuosong. (2022). Effectiveness of the world anti-doping agency's e-learning programme for anti-doping education on knowledge of, explicit and implicit attitudes towards, and likelihood of doping among Chinese college athletes and non-athletes. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13011-022-00459-1

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Between-group ANOVA on the doping likelihood scale in the 64-participant quasi-experiment; the main effect of competitive level printed η2 = 0.10 and was significant in the benefit situation, with "the athletes reported lower doping likelihood scores in the benefit situation than the non-athletes."

> "ANOVA results indicated a significant main effect of the competitive level (F(1, 60) = 6.99, p < 0.05, η2 = 0.10) in the benefit situation. The athletes reported lower doping likelihood scores in the benefit situation than the non-athletes."
