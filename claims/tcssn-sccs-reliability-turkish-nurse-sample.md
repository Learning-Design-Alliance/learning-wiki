### Akpinar Havva 2025

Akpinar Havva, Ozcan Mircali Fatmanur, Bulut Ugurlu Nezihe. (2025). The Relationship Between Therapeutic Communication Skills and Spiritual Care Competencies of Nurses: The Example of Türkiye. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-025-02348-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Internal-consistency analysis of both instruments within the 215-nurse sample. The SCCS alphas in this study were α = 0.91 total, 0.93, 0.84, and 0.95 for its sub-dimensions. The article treats α ≥ 0.70 as indicating a reliable measurement tool.

> "In this study, the reliability analysis showed higher Cronbach's alpha values with α = 0.90 for the total scale, α = 0.80 for non-therapeutic communication skills, α = 0.78 for therapeutic communication skills I, and α = 0.78 for therapeutic communication skills II."
