### Muthulakshmi and Veliappan 2015

P. Muthulakshmi, A. Veliappan. (2015). Effectiveness of an Interactive Multimedia Learning Package in Developing Attitude Towards Mathematics. i-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology, Vol. 11, No. 3, December 2015 - February 2016. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131820

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test comparison in the same 42-student equivalent-group experiment. Table 3 reports means of 189.62 (control) and 189.19 (experimental), t = 0.056, p = 0.956, NS at 5% level.

> "the calculated t-value (0.056) is less than table value (0.5197) for df (20) at 5% level of significance. Hence, the null hypothesis is accepted. It shows that, there is no significant difference in the pretest scores"
