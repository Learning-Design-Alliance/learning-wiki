## Evidence

### Jun Wu 2022

Jun Wu, Shuoli Qi and Yueshan Zhong. (2022). Intrinsic motivation, Need for cognition, Grit, Growth Mindset and Academic Achievement in High School Students: Latent Profiles and Its Predictive Effects. [doi:10.31234/osf.io/mheg7](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/mheg7)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression with moderate students as reference group examined demographic predictors. Maternal education and race significantly distinguished low-grit from moderate students, while gender and social-economic ranking distinguished high-grit students.

> "When comparing the low grit students and moderate students, the differences in maternal education are significant (Logit = -.774), and the difference in race is also relatively significant (Logit = -.774)."
