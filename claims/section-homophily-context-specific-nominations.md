## Evidence

### Meagan Sundstrom 2022

Meagan Sundstrom, Ashley B. Heim, Barum Park, and N. G. Holmes. (2022). Introductory physics students' recognition of strong peers: Gender and racial/ethnic bias differ by course level and context. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020148)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ERGM homophily variables across all six networks. The authors conclude students learn about peers' lecture-related strengths in both discussion and lab sections, but learn about lab-related strengths only in lab section.

> "students tend to nominate peers in their discussion section as strong in the lecture material, but they do not systematically nominate discussion peers as strong in the lab material."
