Evidence¶

Fandakova Yana 2021¶

Fandakova Yana, Gruber Matthias J. (2021). States of curiosity and interest enhance memory differently in adolescents and in children. Developmental Science, 24(1), e13005. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.13005

q3 · i2 · associational · r1

ANOVA on recall proportions with within-subjects factor post-answer interest (high vs. low) and age group. The study reports "a significant age group-by-post-answer interest interaction (F(1,58) = 4.92, p = .03, ηp2 =.08)"; post-hoc tests showed Mdifference = 4% (padj = .24) in children versus Mdifference = 12% (padj < .001) in adolescents.

"revealed a main effect of interest (F(1,58) = 17.60, p < .001, ηp2 = .23) along with a significant age group-by-post-answer interest interaction (F(1,58) = 4.92, p = .03, ηp2 =.08)"

Fandakova Yana 2021 (2)¶

Fandakova Yana, Gruber Matthias J. (2021). States of curiosity and interest enhance memory differently in adolescents and in children. Developmental Science, 24(1), e13005. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.13005

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Follow-up correlation treating age as continuous confirmed the group analysis: the interest-driven memory enhancement was "positively correlated with participants’ age (Pearson’s r = .31, pone-tailed = .01)".

"the post-answer interest-driven memory enhancement (proportion correct recall for high versus low post-answer interest) was positively correlated with participants’ age (Pearson’s r = .31, pone-tailed = .01; Figure 2C)"
