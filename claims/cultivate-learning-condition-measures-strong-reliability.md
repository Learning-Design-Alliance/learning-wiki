# All nine Cultivate learning condition measures show strong Rasch person reliability (0.82-0.87) in CPS administrations

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Each of the nine Cultivate learning condition measures has a Rasch person reliability index of 0.82 or higher, exceeding the 0.80 strong-reliability threshold. [→ Porter 2025](#porter-2025)

## Evidence

### Porter 2025

Porter, S., Shyjka, A., & Kling, K. (2025). Early insights from the revalidation of the Cultivate Survey: Learning conditions shape students' beliefs about being valued. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/early-insights-from-the-revalidation-of-the-cultivate-survey

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Revalidation psychometric analysis of Cultivate survey responses from 504 schools in 2022-23 and 587 schools in 2023-24 (grades 5-12). Table 1 reports Rasch person reliability indices from 0.82 (Well-Organized Classroom, Affirming Identities) to 0.87 (Feedback for Growth); the article states "reliabilities over 0.70 are evidence that a measure consistently captures the intended dimension."

> "Cultivate learning condition measures demonstrate exceptional measures properties—reliabilities over 0.70 are evidence that a measure consistently captures the intended dimension of classroom conditions."
