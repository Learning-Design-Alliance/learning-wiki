Intervention study comparing an apprenticeship condition (more skilled adult partners) with a same-ability-paired comparison group on a new topic, assessed by individually constructed dialog and individual essay. The abstract reports greater skill in "generating advanced counterarguments" in the experimental group. No effect size is printed.

> "the experimental group showed greater skills in using evidence to support a claim, generating advanced counterarguments, and constructing integrative critical arguments coordinating two contrasting claims, relative to the comparison group."
