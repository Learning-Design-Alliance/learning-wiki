Evidence¶

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett 2026¶

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett. (2026). Knowledge Reshapes Inquiry: Changes in Question Asking Ability and its Impact on Academic Assessment. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00402-0

q3 · i1 · associational · r1

Paired-samples t-test on longitudinal data from undergraduates in a semester-long Intro to Psychology course, comparing DS-AQT fluency at semester start and end. The article reports a "significant increase in DS-AQT fluency" with Cohen’s d = 0.2.

"We found a significant increase in DS-AQT fluency from Time 1 (M = 6.1, SD = 2.3) to Time 2 (M = 6.6, SD = 3), t(65) = 1.67, p < .05, Cohen’s d = 0.2."

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett 2026 (2)¶

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett. (2026). Knowledge Reshapes Inquiry: Changes in Question Asking Ability and its Impact on Academic Assessment. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00402-0

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Paired-samples t-test (Time 1 vs Time 2) in the same longitudinal course study shows a "significant increase in DS-AQT originality", Cohen’s d = 0.440, from M = 2.06 to M = 2.29.

"We also found a significant increase in DS-AQT originality from Time 1 (M = 2.06, SD = 0.47) to Time 2 (M = 2.29, SD = 0.57), t(65) = 3.575, p < .001, Cohen’s d = 0.440."

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett 2026 (3)¶

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett. (2026). Knowledge Reshapes Inquiry: Changes in Question Asking Ability and its Impact on Academic Assessment. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00402-0

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Paired-samples t-test of Bloom-based DS-AQT complexity across the semester reports a "significant increase" from M = 3.70 to M = 3.79, t(65) = 2.720, Cohen’s d = 0.333.

"Finally, a significant increase was also observed for DS-AQT complexity, which rose from Time 1 (M = 3.70, SD = 0.27) to Time 2 (M = 3.79, SD = 0.36), t(65) = 2.720, p = .008, Cohen’s d = 0.333."
