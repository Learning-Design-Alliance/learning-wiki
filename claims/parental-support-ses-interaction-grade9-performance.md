### Yeung Jerf W. K.; Xia Lily L. L 2023

Yeung Jerf W. K.; Xia Lily L. L. (2023). Family and Individual Contexts of Middle-School Years and Educational Achievement of Youths in Middle-Aged Adulthood. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20043279

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction analysis within the second structural equation model of LSAY cohort-2 longitudinal data. The article reports "βint = 0.002, p < 0.05" for the parental-support-by-SES interaction on grade-9 educational performance, characterized as promotive rather than buffering.

> "interaction analysis found that grade-7 parental support for college education significantly interacted with family SES in prediction of youths' better grade-9 educational performance (Table 5), βint = 0.002, p < 0.05."
