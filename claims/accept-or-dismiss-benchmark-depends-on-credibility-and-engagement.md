## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Edwards 2025

Edwards, R. J., Yeates, P., Lefroy, J., & McKinley, R. (2025). Understanding contexts and mechanisms through which video based benchmarking promotes alignment of examiners' scoring in objective structured clinical exams. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10454-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Realist interview analysis of the 16 examiners' responses to discrepant benchmark judgements; panel credibility and justification clarity were reported as "critical to the credibility of the benchmark", alongside amenability, discussion opportunity and cognitive resources.

> "The credibility of the benchmark panel (i.e. composed of individuals with the requisite expertise to judge) and the explanatory clarity of the justifications (participants could logically see how the justification related to the performance and the marking scheme) were both critical to the credibility of the benchmark."
