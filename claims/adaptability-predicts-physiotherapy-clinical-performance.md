## Evidence

### Reubenson Alan A. 2025

Reubenson Alan A., Riddell Hugh, Brewer Margo L., Ng Leo, Gucciardi Daniel F. (2025). Individual differences and self-regulatory factors are credible determinants of physiotherapy student performance on clinical placement: Insights from a measurement burst design study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10453-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel structural equation modelling of weekly survey data from 97 final-year physiotherapy students across four 5-week placements, with APP supervisor scores as outcome. The article reports adaptability "had a positive association with clinical performance between-persons and between-placements"; only unstandardised estimates with 95% credible intervals were printed, no effect size.

> "Adaptability had a positive association with clinical performance between-persons and between-placements, and professional performance between-placements. Students with higher average adaptability performed better in the clinical dimension than students with low average adaptability."

### Reubenson Alan A. 2025 (2)

Reubenson Alan A., Riddell Hugh, Brewer Margo L., Ng Leo, Gucciardi Daniel F. (2025). Individual differences and self-regulatory factors are credible determinants of physiotherapy student performance on clinical placement: Insights from a measurement burst design study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10453-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Location-scale component of the same multilevel models on the professional APP dimension. The article reports scores "tended to fluctuate less from placement-to-placement" for higher-adaptability students; no standardised effect size printed.

> "Adaptability also negatively predicted intra-individual variability in professional performance, such that scores in the professional dimension tended to fluctuate less from placement-to-placement in students who reported higher levels of adaptability."
