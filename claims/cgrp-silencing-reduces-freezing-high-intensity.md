## Evidence

### Hanny Hayeon Lee 2026

Hanny Hayeon Lee, Eul Han & Yong Sang Jo. (2026). CGRP inhibition in the PBN shifts defensive strategy from freezing to active avoidance in a platform-based active avoidance paradigm. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00453-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Freezing analysis in the CGRP-silencing experiment (TetTox vs eYFP controls, n = 7 per intensity cohort) during retention tests: at 0.4 and 0.5 mA "the TetTox mice maintained signiﬁcantly lower freezing levels" than controls.

> "However, at 0.4 and 0.5 mA, the control group exhibited signiﬁcant increases in freezing, while the TetTox mice maintained signiﬁcantly lower freezing levels (Fig. 4h)."

### Hanny Hayeon Lee 2026 (2)

Hanny Hayeon Lee, Eul Han & Yong Sang Jo. (2026). CGRP inhibition in the PBN shifts defensive strategy from freezing to active avoidance in a platform-based active avoidance paradigm. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00453-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus two-way ANOVA on retention test 3 freezing percentage (Fig. 4h) reported a significant CGRP manipulation × Footshock Intensity interaction, F(4,48) = 11,75, ***p < 0.001, with Bonferroni post-hoc group differences at 0.4 and 0.5 mA.

> "Two-way ANOVA: signi ﬁcant main effects of CGRP manipulation [ F(1,12) = 21.77, ***p < 0.001], Footshock Intensity [F(4,48) = 37.50, ***p < 0.001], and their interaction [ F(4,48) = 11,75, ***p < 0.001]."
