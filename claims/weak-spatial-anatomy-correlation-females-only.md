## Evidence

### Erli Sarilita 2022

Erli Sarilita, Lita Yurika Ambar, Firman Dani Rizali, Tracey Wilkinson, Sri Susilawati, Risti Saptarini, Dudi Aripin, Endang Sjamsudin. (2022). Spatial ability and anatomy learning performance among dental students. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2022.239

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations on pooled 1st- and 2nd-year dental students' anatomy grades and spatial test scores; the article reports r=0.343 (p<0.001) for PSVT-R and r=0.184 (p=0.015) for MRT. The authors note the correlations were weak.

> "Furthermore, significant, but weak positive correlations between PSVT-R and gross anatomy (r=0.343, p<0.001), and MRT and gross anatomy scores (r=0.184, p=0.015) were found."

### Erli Sarilita 2022 (2)

Erli Sarilita, Lita Yurika Ambar, Firman Dani Rizali, Tracey Wilkinson, Sri Susilawati, Risti Saptarini, Dudi Aripin, Endang Sjamsudin. (2022). Spatial ability and anatomy learning performance among dental students. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2022.239

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the same pooled analysis, Table 4 reports the PSVT-R vs. anatomy and MRT vs. anatomy correlations as NS for the male group; the authors note agreement with Hegarty et al. (2009). No effect size is printed for this null finding.

> "Correlation was not found between any of the spatial ability tests and anatomy scores in the male group. This finding was in agreement with the previous study by Hegarty et al. [7] in 2009, which showed that anatomy class grades did not correlate with any of the spatial ability tests."
