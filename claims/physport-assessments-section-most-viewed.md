## Evidence

### Sarah B. McKagan 2020

Sarah B. McKagan, Linda E. Strubbe, Lyle J. Barbato, Adrian M. Madsen, Eleanor C. Sayre, Bruce A. Mason. (2020). PhysPort use and growth: Supporting physics teaching with research-based resources since 2011. Submitted to the Physics Teacher. [doi:10.1119/10.0002062](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0002062)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Google Analytics page-view analysis by site section for 2018. Of 97 published assessments, 65 had more than 100 unique page views and nine had more than 1,000; the FCI "had 11,000 unique page views and was downloaded 1,400 times in 2018", with almost 36,000 lifetime assessment downloads.

> "The Assessments section, where users can learn about and download research-based assessments, received the most page views on PhysPort in 2018. Of the 97 assessments published on PhysPort, 65 had more than 100 unique page views12 and nine had more than 1,000 unique page views in 2018."
