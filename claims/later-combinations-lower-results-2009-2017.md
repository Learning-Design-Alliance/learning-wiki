## Evidence

### Enzo Bonacci 2021

Enzo Bonacci. (2021). Proposals for High School Teaching of Quantum Physics. ATINER's Conference Paper Proceedings Series PHY2018-0082, Athens. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2112.01219](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2112.01219)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multi-year classroom experience from 2009-2017 in which the author varied which of the six critical aspects were addressed, at most three at a time. The article reports lower results than the two pilot years and leaves the combination question open, inviting colleague feedback.

> "we tackled the critical aspects (always in the maximum number of three)trying different combinations with respect to 1–3–5 of the pilot year 2007-2008 and 2–4–6 of 2008-2009, but with overall lower results. We cannot say whether this is indicative of an optimum achievable only when to be faced together are certain problems and not others."

### Bonacci 2020

Bonacci, E. (2020). On Teaching Quantum Physics at High School. Athens Journal of Education, 7(3), 313-330. https://doi.org/10.30958/aje.7-3-5

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Observational report across the author's pre-university classes from 2009-2018, after the question of teaching materials had been resolved; the author reports trying different combinations of critical aspects "but with overall lower marks" than the two pilot-year combinations.

> "we tackled the critical aspects (always in the maximum number of three) trying different combinations with respect to 2–4–6 of the pilot year 2007 -2008 and 1–3–5 of 2008-2009, but with overall lower marks."
