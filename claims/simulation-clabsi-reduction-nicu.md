### Yousef Nadya 2022

Yousef Nadya, Moreau Romain, Soghier Lamia. (2022). Simulation in neonatal care: towards a change in traditional training? European Journal of Pediatrics 181:1429–1436. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-022-04373-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a NICU study in which simulation-based training was added to a CLABSI prevention bundle, with rates falling "from 13.9 per 1000 catheter days to 4.7" (P < 0.0001). The review attributes this to studies from 2012 (Steiner et al.); no effect size is printed.

> "Studies from 2012 have shown that CLABSI rates can decrease significantly from 13.9 per 1000 catheter days to 4.7 (P < 0.0001) in a NICU after integrating simulation-based training into a CLABSI prevention bundle"
