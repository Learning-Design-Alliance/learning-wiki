## Evidence

### Bialystok Ellen 2009

Bialystok Ellen, Viswanathan Mythili. (2009). Components of Executive Control with Advantages for Bilingual Children in Two Cultures. Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2009.06.014

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cost analysis of the faces task in ninety 8-year-olds across three language groups. The article adds that "the bilinguals showed no cost of switching, with RT in blocked and mixed trials not significantly different, Fs < 1."

> "Switching costs, calculated as the RT difference between blocked and mixed presentations (comparing Figures 2a and 3a with Figures 2b and 3b), led to a significant effect of language group, F (2, 87) = 102.55, p < .0001, again because of greater costs for the monolinguals"
