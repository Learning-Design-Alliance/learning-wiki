# The evidence base for Everyday Mathematics® on primary mathematics achievement is small: one study of 3,436 students met WWC standards, and 33 of 34 eligible studies did not meet group design standards

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### evidence-base

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2015, November). Primary Mathematics intervention report: Everyday Mathematics®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/166

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The WWC systematic review screened 92 identified studies; 34 were eligible for review against group design standards. Thirty-three eligible studies did not meet WWC group design standards, most commonly because "equivalence of the analytic intervention and comparison groups is necessary and not demonstrated." The extent of evidence is rated small: one study with 3,436 students in 18 schools.

> "The WWC identified 34 eligible studies that investigated the effects of Everyday Mathematics® on mathematics achievement for primary stu-dents. An additional 58 studies were identified but do not meet WWC eligibility criteria for review in this topic area."
