Cross-sectional questionnaire study; Table 11 reports 189 (47.6%) Bulgarian students preferring oral examination versus 31 (25.4%) international students, while 31 (54.4%) of academic staff preferred test-based examination. Overall comparison between respondent groups: χ² = 16.7, p < 0.001.
