## Evidence

### Reubenson Alan A. 2025

Reubenson Alan A., Riddell Hugh, Brewer Margo L., Ng Leo, Gucciardi Daniel F. (2025). Individual differences and self-regulatory factors are credible determinants of physiotherapy student performance on clinical placement: Insights from a measurement burst design study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10453-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-placement analysis using a 20-item study-specific clinical self-efficacy scale completed weekly during each 5-week placement, modelled against APP scores in multilevel SEM. The article reports students "performed better on both clinical and professional dimensions"; no standardised effect size printed.

> "Conversely, students performed better on both clinical and professional dimensions in placements where they perceived or experienced greater clinical self-efficacy."
