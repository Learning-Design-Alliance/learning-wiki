## Evidence

### Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes 2026

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes. (2026). Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08849 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.08849](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2607.08849)

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Post-learning survey measure from the randomized experiment; the enjoyment effect is marginal (p = 0.063), which the authors describe as AI making learning more enjoyable. The article also reports the share of students rating enjoyment at or above the sample median rises by 12.1 pp (p= 0.082), while the effect on feeling skilled or effective is 0.06 points (p= 0.834).

> "Enjoyment of the learning task rises by ˆβ= 0.58points on the 0–10 scale (p= 0.063), an 11 percent improvement over the control mean of 5.22."

### Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes 2026 (2)

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes. (2026). Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08849

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Null result on the self-assessed knowledge outcome (0–10 scale) from Session One of the randomized experiment; objective test performance in the same session does respond to AI access. A similar null appears in Session Two (ITT 0.22 points, p= 0.250).

> "Self-assessed knowledge in the control group rises sharply, from 1.56 to 4.79 on the 0–10 scale (p <0.001). The rise is nearly identical among treated students, leaving an ITT ofˆβ=−0.10points, which is statistically indistinguishable from zero."
