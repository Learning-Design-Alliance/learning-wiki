## Evidence

### Thompson Jessica; White Simon; Chapman Stephen 2020

Thompson Jessica; White Simon; Chapman Stephen. (2020). Virtual patients as a tool for training pre-registration pharmacists and increasing their preparedness to practice: A qualitative study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238226

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative telephone-interview study of twenty pre-registration pharmacist trainees (nine VP group, eleven NI group) analysed with the five-stage framework approach. Trainees self-reported skills: "Over half of the trainees in the VP group reported that the simulations helped improve their communication skills", whereas calculation development was reported only by NI users.

> "When trainees were asked about the skills they had developed from completing the case studies, the most commonly reported were communication and calculation. Over half of the trainees in the VP group reported that the simulations helped improve their communication skills."
