Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du 2026¶

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du. (2026). Technology-Supported Interventions and Learners' Metacognition: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10227-3

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Descriptive characterisation of the evidence base (RQ1) from the study-level evidence map; the authors state the quantitative evidence primarily reflects metacognitive-regulation outcomes.

"Metacognitive regulation was represented in 28 of 33 studies (84.8%) and accounted for 77 of 94 effect sizes (81.9%)."

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du 2026 (2)¶

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du. (2026). Technology-Supported Interventions and Learners' Metacognition: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10227-3

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Measurement-format audit in the same evidence-base characterisation; the authors conclude the pooled evidence predominantly concerns changes in learners' self-reported metacognition rather than independently demonstrated monitoring or regulatory performance.

"Self-report measures were used in 31 of 33 studies (93.9%) and generated 90 of 94 effect sizes (95.7%)."

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du 2026 (3)¶

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du. (2026). Technology-Supported Interventions and Learners' Metacognition: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10227-3

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Regulatory-support coding of each intervention against four processes (planning, monitoring, evaluation/reflection, strategic control); the distribution was not uniform, with explicit planning support in only slightly more than half of studies.

"Evaluation/reflection was the most frequently represented regulatory-support process, occurring in 29 of 33 studies (87.9%). Monitoring and strategic control were each represented in 26 studies (78.8%), whereas planning was represented in 19 studies (57.6%)."
