Within-subjects preliminary study measuring happiness before and after Kinetiq use; a one-tailed paired-samples t-test confirmed the increase was significant (p<0.01). Means rose from M=5.0 to M=6.0; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "They also reported higher happiness after using Kinetiq (M = 6.0, SD = 0.76) than before (M=5.0, SD = 1.07). A one-tailed paired-samples t-test confirmed that this increase was statistically significant (p<0.01)."
