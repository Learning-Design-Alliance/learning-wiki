Birjandi and Tamjid 2010¶

Parviz Birjandi, Nasrin Hadidi Tamjid. (2010). The Role of Self-assessment in Promoting Iranian EFL Learners' Motivation. English Language Teaching, 3(3). https://www.ccsenet.org/elt

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired sample t-test on the control group (n=30), which received the same content, instructor and method but no self-assessment training. Means moved only from 143.93 to 145.56; t(29)=-.388, p>.05. Equivalence was not tested.
