# Tests whose item characteristic curves are well separated show very low answer change, with a maximum of 0.25/N for N separated questions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a test with N separated questions the maximal proportion of answer change is 0.25/N, about 1% for a 30-question test, so gains and losses remain very small when no learning occurs. [→ Jean-François Parmentier and Brahim Lamine 2015](#jean-francois-parmentier-and-brahim-lamine-2015)

## Evidence

### Jean-François Parmentier and Brahim Lamine 2015

Jean-François Parmentier and Brahim Lamine. (2015). Interpreting gains and losses in conceptual test using Item Response Theory. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1509.03878](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1509.03878)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical/numerical comparison of three-item tests A, B and C (Fig. 5): overlapping curves (test A) give about 15% change at a 50% score, while non-overlapping curves (test C) give about 0.08, described as "much smaller than for test A".

> "In a test withN separated questions, the maximal value of IC i is 0.25/N and is obtained for values of S = 0.5/N, 1.5/N, ... , (N− 0.5)/N."
