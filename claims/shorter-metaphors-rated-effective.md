Ha Monica MD, Rowe Allyson MD, Hendrix Katlynn, Anwar Summer BS, Mang Khup BS, Wen Frances PhD, Coon Kim EdD, Bragg Jedediah MSW, PhD, Foulks Kristin MPH, Miller-Cribbs Julie MSW, PhD, Jelley Martina MD, MSPH. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive statistical analysis of metaphor durations coded from the 122 simulation videos, using the study rubric. The article reports effective uses averaged "31.8 s, SD = 14.7" versus "39 s, SD = 34.7" for ineffective ones; effective uses also had the strongest coefficient of determination (R 2 = 0.897).

> "Interestingly, metaphor conversations rated as effective were shorter and less variable in duration on average (31.8 s, SD = 14.7) than those rated as ineffective (39 s, SD = 34.7)."
