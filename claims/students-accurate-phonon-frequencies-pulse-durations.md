## Evidence

### Ilana J. Porter 2021

Ilana J. Porter, Michael W. Zuerch, Anne M. Baranger, Stephen R. Leone. (2021). Coherent Phonons in Antimony: an Undergraduate Physical Chemistry Solid-State Ultrafast Laser Spectroscopy Experiment. Journal of Chemical Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2110.11423](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2110.11423)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of numerical results from 18 consenting students' reports (Table 1), comparing calculated values against the true pulse duration of approximately 22-23 fs and true phonon frequency of approximately 4.5 THz. "All students were able to obtain accurate phonon frequencies" while some pulse-duration errors stemmed from forgetting the double path traversal, a missing factor of √2, or a missing 0.707 Gaussian-to-FWHM factor.

> "All students were able to obtain accurate phonon frequencies, and 11 of the 18 students got accurate pulse durations, with a couple of students (number 3 and 9) obtaining pulse durations that were off by a factor of 2"
