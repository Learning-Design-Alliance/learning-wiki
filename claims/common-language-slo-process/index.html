Slotnik 2015¶

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2015). Change in Practice in Maryland: Student Learning Objectives and Teacher and Principal Evaluation. Mid-Atlantic Comprehensive Center (WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED568386

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Statewide survey results across all 24 local school districts (19,022 respondents). The report describes a common language about instruction as a factor that enhances implementation of the evaluation system and its components.

"In every district, more teachers and principals agree than disagree that they have a common language to describe the SLO process, and that expectations are clear."
