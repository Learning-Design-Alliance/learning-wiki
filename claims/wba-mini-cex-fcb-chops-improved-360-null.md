### Ma Chen-ying 2026

Evaluating a specialty-specific objective structured clinical examination and workplace-based assessment curriculum for radiation oncology postgraduate training: a prospective educational intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09554-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-cohort pre/post workplace-based assessment in the intervention cohort; the article reports mean changes with P values and, in Table 2, an effect size of 1.06 for clinical reasoning.

> "The mini-CEX overall score improved from 6.4 ± 0.8 at baseline to 6.7 ± 0.8 at the end of rotation (P = 0.006). The largest gain was seen in clinical reasoning (6.1 ± 0.8 vs. 6.9 ± 0.7, P < 0.001)"

### Ma Chen-ying 2026 (2)

BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09554-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post change on the department-adapted FCB-CHOPS checklist completed during plan-review sessions in the intervention cohort; the article reports the change with means and P value.

> "FCB-CHOPS score improved from 21.9 ± 3.7 vs. 23.2 ± 3.5 (P = 0.008), suggesting a more structured approach to plan review."

### Ma Chen-ying 2026 (3)

Ma Chen-ying, Chen Jie, Fu Yi, Liu Lou, Zhang Jing-wen, Zhou Ju-ying. (2026). Evaluating a specialty-specific objective structured clinical examination and workplace-based assessment curriculum for radiation oncology postgraduate training: a prospective educational intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09554-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-cohort multisource 360-degree evaluation in the intervention cohort; the article reports non-significant changes with means and P values, and no effect size in this sentence.

> "The 360-degree overall score increased numerically from 4.03 ± 0.33 vs. 4.11 ± 0.31 but did not reach statistical significance (P = 0.063), while professional accountability showed no clear change over the rotation period (4.11 ± 0.31 vs. 4.17 ± 0.29, P = 0.135)."
