## Evidence

### Okyay 2017

Okyay, O. & Kandir, A. (2017). Impact of the Interactive Story Reading Method on Receptive and Expressive Language Vocabulary of Children. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(3), 395-406. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1149725

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon analysis for the 26-child control group receiving the Ministry of Education's Preschool Education Program; receptive z= -4.284 and expressive z= -3.675, both p<0.05. The authors attribute control-group growth to effective Turkish language activities, drama, pre-literacy and mathematics activities, and out-of-school language exposure.

> "a statistically significant difference is observed between the pre -test and post -test scores of the children in the control group, in the receptive language test (z= -4.284, p<0.05)"
