### Ehrlich 2014

Ehrlich, S. B., Gwynne, J. A., Pareja, A. S., & Allensworth, E. M. (2014). Preschool Attendance in Chicago Public Schools: Relationships with Learning Outcomes and Reasons for Absences. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED555650

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of attendance logs recorded over a nine-week period documenting reasons for preschool absences. The report finds "more than half of all the days missed in preschool were due to children being sick" and "another 18 percent of days missed were due to a range of reported logistical obstacles."

> "More than half of all the days missed in preschool were due to children being sick, according to attendance logs recorded over a nine-week period. Another 18 percent of days missed were due to a range of reported logistical obstacles for families."
