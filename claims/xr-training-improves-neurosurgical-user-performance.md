## Evidence

### Iop A. 2022

Iop A., El-Hajj V.G., Gharios M., de Giorgio A., Monetti F.M., Edström E., Elmi-Terander A., Romero M. (2022). Extended Reality in Neurosurgical Education: A Systematic Review. Sensors. https://doi.org/10.3390/s22166067

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of the review's 31 included studies: eight adopted an 'XR vs. non-XR' controlled design comparing UP between groups, and the review reports XR use "was associated with improved UP and contributed to better outcomes". No pooled effect size was computed.

> "Eight of 26 studies (31%) adopted an “XR vs. non-XR” controlled design, assessing and comparing UP between groups assigned to different conditions. The use of XR (VR in six cases and AR in two) was associated with improved UP and contributed to better outcomes."
