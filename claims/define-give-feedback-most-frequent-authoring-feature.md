## Evidence

### Diego Dermeval 2018

Diego Dermeval, Ranilson Paiva, Ig Ibert Bittencourt, Julita Vassileva, Daniel Borges. (2018). Authoring Tools for Designing Intelligent Tutoring Systems: a Systematic Review of the Literature. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0157-9

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

RQ3 analysis identifying 21 categories of features that aid the authoring process across the included studies. The review reports "Define/Give feedback (27.27%; 9 studies)" as most frequent, followed by Define problem solutions, with most features tied to the pedagogical and domain models.

> "The most frequent feature identified was the Define/Give feedback (27.27%; 9 studies), followed by the Define problem solutions (18.18%; 6 studies)."

### Diego Dermeval 2018 (2)

Diego Dermeval, Ranilson Paiva, Ig Ibert Bittencourt, Julita Vassileva, Daniel Borges. (2018). Authoring Tools for Designing Intelligent Tutoring Systems: a Systematic Review of the Literature. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0157-9

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

RQ3 analysis of features targeting general aspects of authoring tools rather than a specific ITS component. The review describes "Reuse/Export tutor design" as enabling reuse of previous authoring decisions, saving author time and possibly favouring reuse of validated tutors, alongside viewing learners' statistics, mobile authoring and class lists.

> "The Reuse/Export tutor design feature enables authors to reuse or export previous authoring decisions in a new tutor."
