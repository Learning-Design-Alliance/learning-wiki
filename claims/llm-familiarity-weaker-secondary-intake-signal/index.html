Yongkyung Oh 2026¶

Yongkyung Oh, Lynn Talton, Alex Bui. (2026). Engagement Intensity as a Learner-Modeling Signal for Adaptive AI Ethics Instruction. KDD 2026 AI for Education Day, at the ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (KDD '26). https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18548

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Same pre-instruction survey analysis (Spearman correlations, Holm correction across the five-outcome family) in 91 trainees. Printed correlations ρ=.294, ρ=.278, and ρ=−.254 fall in the medium range; accuracy trust (pHolm=.069) and training interest did not survive correction.

"Self-rated familiarity shows a weaker pattern and survives Holm correction for distinguishing capability (𝜌=.294 ,𝑝Holm =.023 ), complex-task trust (𝜌=.278 , 𝑝Holm =.031 ), and critical-thinking risk ( 𝜌=−.254 , 𝑝Holm =.045 )."

Yongkyung Oh, Lynn Talton, Alex Bui. (2026). Engagement Intensity as a Learner-Modeling Signal for Adaptive AI Ethics Instruction. KDD 2026 AI for Education Day, at the ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (KDD '26). https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18548

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Authors' descriptive observation of the median-by-familiarity-level medians in the same survey analysis; the printed Table 2 medians show identical upper-level medians. The article notes the single Not-at-all respondent limits interpretation.

"The familiarity pattern is bottom-anchored rather than smoothly graded. Separation is clearest at the lower end of the scale, while the medians for the “Somewhat familiar” and “Very familiar” groups are often identical."
