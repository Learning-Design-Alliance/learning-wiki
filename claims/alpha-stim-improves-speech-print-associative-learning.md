# Alpha-rate (10 Hz) but not theta-rate (5 Hz) sensory stimulation improves accuracy of speech-print associative learning compared to a null-stimulation control

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Alpha-rate (10 Hz) sensory entrainment significantly improved response accuracy in paired associative learning compared to the Null condition. [→ Zhijun Liao 2024](#zhijun-liao-2024)
`q3 i?` Theta-rate (5 Hz) stimulation did not significantly modulate paired associative learning accuracy relative to the Null condition. [→ Zhijun Liao 2024 (2)](#zhijun-liao-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Zhijun Liao 2024

Zhijun Liao, Xiya Ao, Yulu Sun, Manli Zhang & Xiangzhi Meng. (2024). Alpha sensory stimulation modulates theta phase during speech-print associative learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00263-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-subjects experiment: 36 Chinese undergraduates were randomly assigned to a 10 Hz tone-flash entrainment condition and performed the PAL task; independent-samples t-test against the Null control group showed "t41 = −2.39, p = 0.021" with mean accuracy 0.95 vs 0.91. Only a t statistic was printed, so no effect size is reported here.

> "First, α-rate sensory entrainment (M ± SD = 0.95 ± 0.03; t41 = −2.39, p = 0.021) significantly improved the response accuracy compared to the Nullcondition [0.91 ± 0.06]"

### Zhijun Liao 2024 (2)

Zhijun Liao, Xiya Ao, Yulu Sun, Manli Zhang & Xiangzhi Meng. (2024). Alpha sensory stimulation modulates theta phase during speech-print associative learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00263-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same between-subjects PAL experiment: the 5 Hz theta stimulation group's accuracy (0.92 ± 0.08) did not differ from the Null condition, "t41 = 0.60, p = 0.595". A non-significant test with no printed effect size; equivalence was not tested.

> "while θ-rate stimulation (0.92 ± 0.08;t41 = 0.60, p = 0.595) did not modulate the performance of associative learning (Fig. 1b)."
