## Evidence

### Prast 2018

Prast, E. J., Van de Weijer-Bergsma, E., Kroesbergen, E. H., & Van Luit, J. E. H. (2018). Differentiated instruction in primary mathematics: Effects of teacher professional development on student achievement. Learning and Instruction, 54, 22-34. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.009

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-report evaluation questionnaire (15 items, five-point Likert scale) completed by 76 Cohort 1 teachers at the end of Year 1 and 73 Cohort 2 teachers at the end of Year 2. Scores were above the scale midpoint for all questions; perceived effects on motivation and achievement are teacher perceptions, not measured outcomes.

> "Moreover, the majority of teachers (76.3% and 76.7% of teachers who completed the questionnaire in Cohort 1 and 2, respectively) mostly or fully agreed that they actually used what they had learned in the PD for the preparation and teaching of their mathematics lessons."
