## Evidence

### Sanders 2012

Sanders, N. E., Kohler, S., Newton, E., & the Astrobites collaboration. (2012). Preparing Undergraduates for Research Careers: Using Astrobites in the Classroom. [doi:10.3847/aer2012030](https://doi.org/10.3847/aer2012030)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

An informal September 2011 reader survey with 125 respondents, plus Google Analytics tracking of about 300 regular readers per day, underpins the readership estimate: the survey "showed that 20% of the respondents were undergraduate students." The estimate is the authors' own inference from those data.

> "We therefore estimate that Astrobites' daily readership already includes >150 students in our target demographic of undergraduates in a physical science field."

### Sanders 2012 (2)

Sanders, N. E., Kohler, S., Newton, E., & the Astrobites collaboration. (2012). Preparing Undergraduates for Research Careers: Using Astrobites in the Classroom. [doi:10.3847/aer2012030](https://doi.org/10.3847/aer2012030)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The same informal September 2011 reader survey asked undergraduates about career goals; the article reports that more than 80% of undergraduate respondents listed their career goal as researcher in astrophysics or a related field. No test statistics are printed for these descriptive survey results.

> "In the same reader survey, >80% of undergraduate respondents listed their career goal as "researcher" in astrophysics or a related field."
