Scheiter 2007¶

Scheiter, K., & Gerjets, P. (2007). Learner Control in Hypermedia Environments. Educational Psychology Review 19, 285–307. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-007-9046-3

The review's analysis identifies usability problems as the first of four reasons effectiveness is hard to demonstrate, naming "disorientation, distraction, cognitive overload" as the specific problems. No effect sizes are reported in the available text.

"the potential effectiveness of self-controlled learning with hypermedia might be difficult to demonstrate due to (1) usability problems (i.e., disorientation, distraction, cognitive overload)"
