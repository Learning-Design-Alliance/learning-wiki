### Das Bibek 2024

Das Bibek, Ledesma Frances, Naik Ravi, Law Sarah, Soleimani-Nouri Payam, Khan Omar A, Mylonas George, Pai Madhava, Ashrafian Hutan, Spalding Duncan, Fehervari Matyas. (2024). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised comparison within a prospective cohort study of 20 novice learners allocated to F2F (n=8) or virtual (n=12) teaching. The article reports the online group "completed the task faster than the F2F group (11.25 min vs. 16.88 min; P=0.015)" and no difference in pegs dropped (P=0.961).

> "On average, participants in the online training group completed the task faster than the F2F group (11.25 min vs. 16.88 min; P=0.015) with no difference in number of pegs dropped (P=0.961)."
