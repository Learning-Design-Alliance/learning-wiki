## Evidence

### Giang Pham 2026

Giang Pham, Franziska Maria Locher, Dimitra Kolovou, Eliane Arnold. (2026). COVID-19: A cross-national analysis with PISA data. Learning and Instruction 105 (2026) 102366. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102366

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression on the potential-outcome-based Pandemic-Related Performance Change (N = 47 countries), contrasting Cluster 2 vs Cluster 3. The article reports "6.9 PISA points in mathematics, p = .04" and 9.9 points in reading (p = .03); the science difference (p = .11) was not significant.

> "Pandemic-Related Performance Change was more positive in Cluster 2 countries (Accentuated Teacher-Led Synchronous Instruction) compared with Cluster 3 countries (Accentuated Instructional Disruption) across all domains (6.9 PISA points in mathematics, p = .04; 9.9 points in reading, p = .03; 5.4 points in science, p = .11)."

### Giang Pham 2026 (2)

Giang Pham, Franziska Maria Locher, Dimitra Kolovou, Eliane Arnold. (2026). COVID-19: A cross-national analysis with PISA data. Learning and Instruction 105 (2026) 102366. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102366

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same regression framework contrasting Cluster 2 vs Cluster 1 on pandemic-related performance change. The science difference was significant (21.3 points, p = .02); reading (p = .07) was marginal; mathematics and all Cluster 1 vs Cluster 3 contrasts were not significant.

> "A more positive effect of belonging to Cluster 2 over Cluster 1 (Family-Supported Asynchronous Instruction) was also observed in reading (12.9 points, p = .07) and science (21.3 points, p = .02). No significant differences were found between Cluster 1 and Cluster 2 in mathematics, nor between Cluster 1 and Cluster 3 across all domains."
