Evidence¶

WWC Intervention Report: ALAS 2006¶

WWC Intervention Report: ALAS. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.alasdropoutprevention.com

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial of 94 high-risk seventh graders (46 ALAS, 48 control) in one Los Angeles junior high. Appendix A3.1 reports an effect size of 1.39 (Cox index) and an improvement index of 42, statistically significant at α = 0.05.

"Larson and Rumberger (1995) reported that, at the end of the intervention (the end of ninth grade), ALAS students were significantly more likely than control students to be enrolled in school (98% compared with 83%)."
