Coursemojo evidence summary 2024¶

Coursemojo evidence summary. (2024). Coursemojo: AI-powered ELA teaching platform, Moderate evidence rating. https://www.coursemojo.com

q2 · i? · causal · r?

From the same Tennessee quasi-experiment of 2,045 sixth graders: students with disabilities "narrowed the achievement gap with general education peers from 16 to 5 points"; economically disadvantaged students reduced their gap by more than half. No effect size printed for these gap changes.

"Students with disabilities narrowed the achievement gap with general education peers from 16 to 5 points, and economically disadvantaged students reduced their gap by more than half."
