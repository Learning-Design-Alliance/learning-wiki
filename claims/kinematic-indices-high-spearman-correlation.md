`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Wet-lab accuracy verification: kinematic indices (path length, depth path length, velocity, acceleration, jerk, idle/low/middle/high time percentages, angular lengths) were calculated from both systems' data and compared via Spearman's rank correlation coefficients (Table 2), e.g., grasping forceps PL = 0.982.

> "the absolute values of the correlation coefficients of all the kinematic indices exceed 0.7, and the coefficients of most of the indices exceed 0.9. This result indicates that the developed system can evaluate the skills of surgeons to the same extent as the 6-camera system."
