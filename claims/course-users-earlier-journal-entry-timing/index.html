Evidence¶

Sven Scheu 2026¶

Sven Scheu, Simone N. Loeffler and Alexander Maedche. (2026). Designing a mobile chatbot-based learning journaling system for intrinsic motivation and engagement. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00589-7

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Exploratory analysis of notification reliance among the 157 active participants: early users created entries before the 9 P.M. reminder more than 50% of the time. Fisher exact tests on the contingency tables (Table 1) showed course users were significantly more often early (OR = 3.45), while assistant access showed no significant timing difference (OR = 1.55, p = 0.11).

"Fisher exact tests revealed that users with the course were significantly more often early ( OR =3 .45,p < 0.001). The assistant users' results were not significant (OR =1 .55,p =0 .11)."
