Evidence¶

Juan C. Castro-Alonso 2024¶

Juan C. Castro-Alonso, Paul Ayres, Shirong Zhang, Björn B. de Koning, Fred Paas. (2024). Research Avenues Supporting Embodied Cognition in Learning and Instruction. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09847-4

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The review reports, citing Li et al. (2019), a study with 123 undergraduates (85% females) learning about neuron synapsis comparing specific pointing, nonspecific pointing, and non-pointing general gestures by pedagogical agents. No effect size is printed.

"multimedia pedagogical agents doing specific pointing to a key area of the depiction were more effective than those doing nonspecific pointing to all the depiction, which were also more effective than those showing non-pointing general gestures."
