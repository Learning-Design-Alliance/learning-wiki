## Evidence

### Pérez-Chicharro María 2025

Pérez-Chicharro María, González-Olmo María José, Nieto-Moraleda Carolina, Obeso-Benítez Paula, Martínez-Piédrola Rosa M, Sánchez-Herrera-Baeza Patricia, Pérez-de-Heredia-Torres Marta, Romero-Maroto Martín, Huertas-Hoyas Elisabet. (2025). Improving dental students’ dexterity and strength: an intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08015-8

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon pre-post analysis of 37 first-year dental students who completed the eight-week home programme. Nine Hole times decreased (e.g., dominant 18.42 to 17.25 s) and Box and Block counts increased, with the article reporting "a significant improvement in dexterity with a small effect size (r >.26, p <.05)". No control group, so attribution to the intervention is limited.

> "In addition, we found a significant improvement in dexterity with a small effect size (r >.26, p <.05) in the Nine Hole for both hands, and in B&B, we see that both the dominant and non-dominant hands showed significant increases."
