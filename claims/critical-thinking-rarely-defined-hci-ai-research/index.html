q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's own critical synthesis of its 80-study corpus of empirical HCI research on critical thinking with or about AI. The authors report that "critical thinking is rarely defined in HCI research about AI" and identify construct and criterion validity challenges across the line of research.

"Our findings demonstrate that critical thinking is rarely defined in HCI research about AI, and there are fundamental construct and criterion validity challenges in this line of research."
