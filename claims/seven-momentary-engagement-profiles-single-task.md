# Seven profiles of momentary (dis)engagement emerge among adolescents completing the same ten-minute literacy task

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Latent profile analysis of behavioral, emotional and cognitive momentary engagement during one standardized task yielded seven profiles: engaged, attentive amotivated, attentive disinterested, attentive disaffected, distracted motivated, disengaged, and deeply disengaged. [→ Symonds 2021](#symonds-2021)

## Evidence

### Symonds 2021

Symonds, J. E., Schreiber, J. B., & Torsney, B. M. (2021). Silver linings and storm clouds: Divergent profiles of student momentary engagement emerge in response to the same task. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000605

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis in Mplus of three momentary engagement indicators (self-reported emotion and cognition; observed on-task behavior) from 196 adolescents doing the same ten-minute literacy task. "A total of 196 cases were classified into the seven profiles", with the engaged profile comprising 28% of the sample.

> "A total of 196 cases were classified into the seven profiles. Below, we report on the most engaged to the least engaged profiles. The engaged profile (n = 54, 28%) had high levels of behavioral, emotional, and cognitive momentary engagement."
