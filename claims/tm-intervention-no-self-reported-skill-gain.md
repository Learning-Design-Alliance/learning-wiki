## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Study 1, comparisons of treatment and control change within each skill bucket showed no significant interactions on time management facets. [→ Burrus et al. 2013](#burrus-et-al-2013)

## Evidence

### Burrus et al. 2013

Jeremy Burrus, Teresa Jackson, Steven Holtzman, and Richard D. Roberts, Terri Mandigo. (2013). Examining the Efficacy of a Time Management Intervention for High School Students. ETS Research Report Series, ETS RR-13-25. https://search.ets.org/researcher/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study 1 quasiexperiment with 143 ninth-graders assigned by class to treatment or focal-local control; mixed-model ANOVAs compared facet changes by skill bucket. The article reports "no significant interactions, all ps >.17" across buckets.

> "Once again, for this analysis there were no significant interactions, all ps >.17. This may not be surprising because, outside of the feedback, the intervention (i.e., homework assignments) was identical for all skill categories."
