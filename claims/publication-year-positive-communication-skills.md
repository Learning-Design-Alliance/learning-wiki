### Jiang N 2024

Jiang N, Zhang Y, Liang S, Lyu X, Chen S, Huang X, Pan H. (2024). Effectiveness of Virtual Simulations Versus Mannequins and Real Persons in Medical and Nursing Education: Meta-Analysis and Trial Sequential Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/56195

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Univariate random-effects meta-regression across the 5 communication-skills RCTs. Publication year was the only significant moderator (β=.09; P=.02); age, nursing discipline, undergraduate level, and real person comparison had no effects. The article attributes this to technology advances overcoming lack of realism.

> "Univariate meta-regression revealed that year of publication (β=.09; P=.02) had a positive effect on communication skill scores"
