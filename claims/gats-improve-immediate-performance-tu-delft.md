`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment in a 9-week Java CS1 course at TU Delft (pooled N=151; While-loop N=138), analyzed with linear mixed models controlling for topic and prior experience; Table 1 row 21 reports Δ=5.420, p=0.003, padj=0.017, and the While-loop row reports Δ=8.227, p=0.003, padj=0.0159. Other topics showed positive but non-significant differences that failed correction.
