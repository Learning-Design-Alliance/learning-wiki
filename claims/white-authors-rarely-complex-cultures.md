# White authors rarely portray complex cultures of color: 41 of 64 limited-representation books have at least one White author, versus 15 of 36 complex-representation books

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Books with limited representation of groups or cultures of color more often have at least one White author than books with complex representation (41 of 64 versus 15 of 36). [→ Tanji Reed Marshall 2023](#tanji-reed-marshall-2023)

## Evidence

### Tanji Reed Marshall 2023

Tanji Reed Marshall, Ph.D., and William H. Rodick, Ph.D. (2023). The Search for More: Complex Racial and Ethnic Representation in Grade School Books. The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/booktool

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authorship-by-representation cross-tabulation in the Culture section of the 300-book analysis, motivated by the observation that White authors write most ELA texts. The report prints both counts and illustrates with Sharon Creech's Walk Two Moons as limited representation by a White author.

> "Of the 64 books in our sample that include groups or cultures with limited representation, 41 of them have at least one author who is White. Of the 36 books in our sample that contain groups or cultures with complex representation, only 15 have at least one author who is White."
