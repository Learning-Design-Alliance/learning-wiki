### Wijaya Tommy Tanu 2023

Wijaya Tommy Tanu, Yu Boran, Xu Fei, Yuan Zhiqiang, Mailizar Mailizar. (2023). Analysis of Factors Affecting Academic Performance of Mathematics Education Doctoral Students: A Structural Equation Modeling Approach. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20054518

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 147 Indonesian mathematics education doctoral students analyzed with PLS-SEM (bootstrap with 5000 subsamples, significance level 0.05). The structural model gave teacher support the largest significant path to academic performance, which the authors word as "the biggest factor significantly improving" it; a cross-sectional path model cannot show improvement.

> "Meanwhile, teacher support was the biggest factor significantly improving the academic performance of mathematics education doctoral students."
