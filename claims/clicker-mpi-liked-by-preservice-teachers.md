## Evidence

### Miller 2013

Miller, T. K. (2013). Pre-service Elementary Teachers' Perceptions of a Modified Peer Instruction Implementation of Clickers in their Mathematics Content Course. IUMPST: The Journal, Vol 3 (Technology). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1043050

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

End-of-semester anonymous questionnaire administered to all four course sections near semester's end; basic statistics were calculated because the data set was small. The survey found "117 (95.9%), indicated that they generally liked the use of clickers in the course."

> "of the 122 students from the four sections using clickers, 117 (95.9%), indicated that they generally liked the use of clickers in the course, with only three (2.4%) disliking the implementation and two (1.6%) unsure"
