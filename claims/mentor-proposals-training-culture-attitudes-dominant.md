`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phase 2 open-ended survey with 63 of the 173 phase 1 mentors (36.4%) from 7 countries; content analysis coded meaning units into categories and themes. Training was the most productive theme (127 ideas, 36%), followed by attitudes and environmental factors (83, 23.5%).

> "The overall productivity was 353 ideas or meaning units grouped into 9 joint themes. More than half (210/353, 59.5%) were related to training activities, environmental and structural conditions (safety culture and work climate), and individual attitudes."
