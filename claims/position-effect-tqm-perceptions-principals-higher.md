## Evidence

### Altunay 2016

Esen Altunay. (2016). The Effect of Training with TQM on the Perceptions of Teachers about the Quality of Schools. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2126-2133. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040925

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis H tests comparing teachers (n = 11), assistant principals (n = 8) and principals (n = 6) on post-test scores; management structure X2 = 11.104, p = .004 and overall perceptions X2 = 7.224, p = .027 were significant, cultural structure was not (p = .140).
