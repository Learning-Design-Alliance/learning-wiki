## Evidence

### Shikino K 2025

Shikino K, Nishizaki Y, Fukui S, Kataoka K, Yokokawa D, Shimizu T, Yamamoto Y, Nagasaki K, Kobayashi H, Tokuda Y. (2025). Resident Physician Recognition of Tachypnea in Clinical Simulation Videos in Japan: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/72640

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional observational analysis of 5105 GM-ITE 2022 participants, with 959 propensity-matched pairs balanced on sex, PGY, rotations, and workload (SMD <0.1). The matched "mean difference, 1.9" points favored the detection group. No standardized effect size is printed, so impact is unreported.

> "Post-matching, the detection group achieved significantly higher GM-ITE scores (mean [SD], 47.6 [8.4]) than the non-detection group (mean [SD], 45.7 [8.1]; mean difference, 1.9; 95% CI, 1.1‐2.6; P=.041)."
