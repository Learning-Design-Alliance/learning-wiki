### Delgado-Floody P 2024

Delgado-Floody P, Cristi-Montero C, Jerez-Mayorga D, Ruiz-Ariza A, Guzmán-Guzmán IP, Álvarez C, Gómez-López M, Carter-Thuillier B, Caamaño-Navarrete F. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (PROCESS v3.3, 5,000 resamples) on the total sample of 4,990 schools with school climate as the dependent variable; the article reports the printed indirect effect and a mediation percentage of 35%.

> "Finally, the indirect effect confirmed that PSPA was a partial mediator of school climate (IE = –0.46; SE = 0.52; 95%CI, −0.56, −0.35, Med%: 35%) (Figure 3C)."
