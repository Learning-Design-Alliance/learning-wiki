## Evidence

### Rogers 2025

Rogers, L. K., Nguyen, T. D., Timmer, M., & Samson, J. (2025). Principal Burnout: A Systematic Review. Working paper. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED666666

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review coding of antecedent categories across 99 studies. Job/organizational conditions (67%) were the most studied, followed by personal factors and environmental conditions (12%). Workload appeared in 26% of studies; teacher interactions in 19%; school structure in 26%.

> "Sixty-seven percent of studies included a focus on job and organizational conditions."
