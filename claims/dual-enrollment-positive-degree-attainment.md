## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2017

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2017). Dual Enrollment Programs. WWC Intervention Report, Transition to College. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/671

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review synthesis of five studies (two RCTs without reservations, three studies with reservations) including 77,249 students across the United States. Table 1 reports an average improvement index of +25 percentile points (range +6 to +42) for degree attainment, a "rating of positive effects"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Thus, for the degree attainment (college) domain, five studies found statistically significant positive effects. This results in a rating of positive effects, with a medium to large extent of evidence."
