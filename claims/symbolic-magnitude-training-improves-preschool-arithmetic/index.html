Evidence¶

Honoré N 2016¶

Honoré N, Noël M-P (2016) Improving Preschoolers' Arithmetic through Number Magnitude Training: The Impact of Non-Symbolic and Symbolic Training. PLoS ONE 11(11): e0166685. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0166685

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Randomized three-group training study with 56 preschoolers (non-symbolic, symbolic, control). In the exact addition task, the time × group interaction was significant, "F(2,52) = 4.38;p = .017, η2 = 0.144", and only the symbolic group improved significantly (η² = 0.265).

"the interaction between time and group was significant,F(2,52) = 4.38;p = .017, η2 = 0.144 (Fig 9). Improvement in performance over time was significant in the symbolic group only,t(52) = 4.18; p< .001, η2 = 0.265"
