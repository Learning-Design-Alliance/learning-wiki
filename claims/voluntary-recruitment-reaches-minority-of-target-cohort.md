Effectiveness of a Low-Cost, Graduate Student–Led Intervention on Study Habits and Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0004

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Recruitment analysis of the Fall 2014 lecture enrollment (511 students; 307 in the target cohort; 54 enrolled). The article reports "only 16.94% of the target cohort enrolled" despite the goal of recruiting 100% of that cohort.

> "This means that only 16.94% of the target cohort enrolled, but also that only 3.07% of students who did enroll were not from the target cohort (Table 1)."
