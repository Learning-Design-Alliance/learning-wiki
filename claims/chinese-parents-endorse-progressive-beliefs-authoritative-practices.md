## Evidence

### Ren 2024

Ren, L., Chen, M., Hu, B. Y., & Sun, J. (2024). Parenting beliefs and practices as precursors to academic outcomes in Chinese children. Family Process, 63(4), 2000–2015. https://doi.org/10.1111/famp.13006

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparisons in the SEM sample of 206 parent–child dyads from Zhongshan, Guangdong, using paired t-tests on Parental Modernity Scale and PSDQ subscale means; the article reports "t(205) = 16.76, p < 0.001" for beliefs and "t(205) = 30.35, p < 0.001" for practices.

> "parents reported greater endorsement of progressive beliefs as well as more frequent use of authoritative parenting compared to traditional beliefs (t(205) = 16.76, p < 0.001) and authoritarian parenting (t(205) = 30.35, p < 0.001), respectively"
