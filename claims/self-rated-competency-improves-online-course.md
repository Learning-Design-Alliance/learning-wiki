### Fehervari Matyas 2022

Fehervari Matyas, Das Bibek, Soleimani-Nouri Payam, Ahmad Manal, Fadel Michael G., Deputy Mohammed, Morgan Catrin, Burke Joshua R., Mason John D., Nott David, Spalding Duncan. (2022). Can surgical skills be taught using technological advances online? A comparative study of online and face-to-face surgical skills training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-022-09170-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post comparison of delegate self-rated competency (0–10) across skills including suturing, scrubbing-in, knot tying, tendon repair, instrument recognition and vascular anastomosis, analysed with the Wilcoxon matched pairs signed-rank test; scores "improved for all technical skills". Only a p-value printed, no effect size.

> "Overall self-rated competency scores improved for all technical skills (p < 0.001) at the completion of the course (Fig. 3)."
