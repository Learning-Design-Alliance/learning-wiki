## Evidence

### Jacob T. Stanley 2017

Jacob T. Stanley, Weifeng Su, and H. J. Lewandowski (2017). Using lab notebooks to examine students' engagement in modeling in an upper-division electronics lab course. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Notebook coding of the highly scaffolded voltage divider activity in the transformed electronics lab course, with 45 students across three sections. The coding results show "74% of students completed at least one of the four modeling cycles" and 37% completed all four; percentages are descriptive, no effect size printed.

> "Overall, 74% of students completed at least one of the four modeling cycles, 65% completed at least two, 51% completed at least three, and 37% completed all four modeling cycles in the activity."

### Jacob T. Stanley 2017 (2)

Jacob T. Stanley, Weifeng Su, and H. J. Lewandowski (2017). Using lab notebooks to examine students' engagement in modeling in an upper-division electronics lab course. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Notebook coding of the moderately scaffolded room light photometer activity, a single modeling cycle with student-chosen revision pathways. "53% of students completed the modeling process"; students on average made 2 to 3 proposals and performed 1 to 2 revisions. Descriptive percentages only.

> "In this activity 53% of students completed the modeling process. On average, students made 2 to 3 proposals to revise the model/system and performed 1 to 2 revisions."

### Jacob T. Stanley 2017 (3)

Jacob T. Stanley, Weifeng Su, and H. J. Lewandowski (2017). Using lab notebooks to examine students' engagement in modeling in an upper-division electronics lab course. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Notebook coding of the least scaffolded voltage-controlled electromagnet activity, where students chose their own model of the magnetic field to test. "68% of students completely docu- mented a modeling cycle"; descriptive percentage, no effect size printed.

> "In this activity, 68% of students completely docu- mented a modeling cycle."
