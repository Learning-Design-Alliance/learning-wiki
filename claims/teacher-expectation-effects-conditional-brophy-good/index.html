Daniel Robinson 2009¶

Daniel Robinson. (2009). Editor's Note: Remembering Jere Brophy. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-009-9115-x

q2 · i? · theoretical · r1

Tom Good's tribute recounts the joint Brophy–Good teacher expectation research: the dispute produced articles concluding that some teachers differentially treat students by perceived ability while others do not. No effect sizes are printed.

"Our first common research activity evolved around a disagreement of the extent to which teachers would vary in how they interacted with students they believed to be more and less capable. This dispute led to a spate of articles that in the end proved both of us right and wrong. Some teachers exhibit markedly different behavior and opportunities based upon perceived level of student ability — others do not."
