## Evidence

### Lilin Zhu and Gang Xiang 2021

Lilin Zhu and Gang Xiang. (2021). Investigating student understanding of heat engine: a case study of Stirling engine. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac342b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac342b)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-by-case analysis of written answers found four error types; misunderstanding regenerator percentages were 61.5% (Wu), 50.0% (Xiang) and 64.2% (Zhu). Interviews showed students could not describe how the regenerator works; only three students gave a correct description. No effect size is printed.

> "in Xiang class, 52.1% (25) participants made engineering errors, 50% (24) participants made the error of misunderstanding regenerator, and 2.1% (1) participant made the error of misunderstanding cycle process, respectively."
