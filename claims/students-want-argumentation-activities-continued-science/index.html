Content analysis of experimental-group students' answers on whether similar activities should be used in other units or courses. Table 6 prints 'Only suitable to Science classes' at 28.6%, 'Applicable to all subjects' at 19.0%, and smaller percentages for social studies, Turkish, Maths and English; two students (9.5%) did not want them.

"Most of the students stated that argumentation-based activities are only suitable for science lessons. Some of them stated that they would like to have similar activities in all courses."
