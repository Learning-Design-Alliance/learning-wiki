Reshaping Undergraduate Computer Science Education in the Generative AI Era 2026¶

Reshaping Undergraduate Computer Science Education in the Generative AI Era. (2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07545 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.07545

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Qualitative consensus from the January 2026 one-day student workshop with 60 participants, including students and recent graduates, roughly half from Singapore-based institutions. Reports participant perceptions, not tested outcomes.

"The consensus was that AI’s role isfluid: a shortcut for tasks with clear, predictable outcomes (e.g., information search, simple automation) and a collaborator for uncertain, creative tasks where students must inject their own critical thinking and opinions."
