# Forum network centrality is positively correlated with final course grade in the two semesters with denser forum networks

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In Semester 1, PageRank (r = 0.18) and Target Entropy (r = 0.29) correlate positively with final grade and Hide negatively (r = -0.27), all significant. [→ Adrienne Traxler 2018](#adrienne-traxler-2018)

## Evidence

### Adrienne Traxler 2018

Adrienne Traxler, Andrew Gavrin, and Rebecca Lindell. (2018). Talking by the numbers: Networks identify productive forum discussions. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020107)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Bootstrap permutation correlation analysis of the Semester 1 full-semester forum network (156 nodes, density 0.32) against final course grade, reported in Results Table II. Printed effect sizes: r = 0.18 (PageRank, p < 0.05), r = 0.29 (Target Entropy, p < 0.01), r = -0.27 (Hide, p < 0.01), described as "small eﬀect sizes" using Cohen's thresholds.

> "In the ﬁrst semester, PageRank and Target En- tropy are positively correlated with ﬁnal grade and Hide is negatively correlated, all at small eﬀect sizes"

### Adrienne Traxler 2018 (2)

Adrienne Traxler, Andrew Gavrin, and Rebecca Lindell. (2018). Talking by the numbers: Networks identify productive forum discussions. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020107)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Same bootstrap correlation analysis for Semester 3 (145 nodes, density 0.28), Table II printing r = 0.34 (PageRank, p < 0.001), r = 0.28 (Target Entropy, p < 0.01), r = -0.31 (Hide, p < 0.01). The article states the PageRank and Hide correlations are "above the threshold for medium eﬀect size".

> "The third semester repeats the pattern of semester 1, with the PageRank and Hide correlations now above the threshold for medium eﬀect size."
