## Evidence

### Thomsen JLD 2022

Thomsen JLD, Mathiesen O, Hägi‐Pedersen D, Skovgaard LT, Østergaard D, Gätke MR; the INVERT collaborator group. (2022). Improving neuromuscular monitoring and reducing residual neuromuscular blockade via e‐learning: A multicentre interrupted time‐series study (INVERT study). Acta Anaesthesiol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aas.14038

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Department-level segmented regression of succinylcholine-only monitoring; department 4 showed a significant sudden change plus increasing trend (p = .005). A parallel finding for department 2's non-depolarising monitoring showed a −37.0% per year declining trend after the change. The authors note small department effects could reflect multiple testing.

> "After the intervention, there was a significant sudden change in departments 2 and 4, and in department 4 this change was followed by a highly significant increase in the trend to estimated 109.4% per year (p = .005)."
