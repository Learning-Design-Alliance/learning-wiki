Evidence¶

Fulton 2010¶

Fulton, K., Doerr, H., & Britton, T. (2010). STEM Teachers in Professional Learning Communities: A Knowledge Synthesis. National Commission on Teaching & America's Future / WestEd. https://www.nctaf.org/resources/events/STEMWingspreadConference.htm

q2 · i? · review · r3

Knowledge synthesis finding, reported in the Overview of Key Findings from the reviewed Types 1 and 2 studies. The review reports that participation in PLCs can successfully engage teachers in discussion about content knowledge, "positively impacting their understanding of or preparedness to teach content, or attitudes toward teaching methods". No effect sizes are printed.

"Participation in PLCs can successfully engage teachers in discussion about content knowledge or knowledge about how to teach it (pedagogical content knowledge or PCK), positively impacting their understanding of or preparedness to teach content, or attitudes toward teaching methods."

Fulton 2010 (2)¶

Fulton, K., Doerr, H., & Britton, T. (2010). STEM Teachers in Professional Learning Communities: A Knowledge Synthesis. National Commission on Teaching & America's Future / WestEd. https://www.nctaf.org/resources/events/STEMWingspreadConference.htm

q2 · i? · review · r3

The synthesis's own strength-of-knowledge judgment in the section on the source of knowledge: claims about teacher belief and content/PCK change are "moderately substantiated", and it states that "More evidence of changes in students' learning or achievement clearly is needed." No effect sizes are printed.

"Claims seem moderately substantiated that STEM PLCs can contribute to changes in teacher beliefs or changing teachers'knowledge of STEM content or content pedagogical knowledge (PCK). More evidence of changes in students' learning or achievement clearly is needed."
