## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Regression analyses found no significant association between any demographic covariate and any of the four outcome measures; the training itself was the differentiating factor. [→ Li Y 2025](#li-y-2025)

## Evidence

### Li Y 2025

Li Y, Cheng S, Liu D, Jiang W. (2025). “Learning, Thinking & Conveying” training with experiential learning in the emergency department for undergraduate nursing students: A quasi-experimental study. Medicine 2025;104:28(e43231). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000043231

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multivariate linear regression in the 90-student study, controlling for gender, age, place of birth, student cadre status and voluntary choice of profession, found no significant associations with theoretical exam, practical exam, total SRSSDL or total CTDI-CV scores.

> "No effect of gender, age, place of birth, student cadre or voluntary choice of nursing profession was found."
