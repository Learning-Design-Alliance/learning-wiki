Evidence¶

Coyne 2019¶

Coyne, M. D., McCoach, D. B., Ware, S., Austin, C. R., Loftus-Rattan, S. M., & Baker, D. L. (2019). Racing Against the Vocabulary Gap: Matthew Effects in Early Vocabulary Instruction and Intervention. Exceptional Children, 85(2), 163–179. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918789162

q3 · i2 · causal · r3

Multilevel analysis of listening comprehension outcomes in the efficacy and replication studies. The article reports an overall mean effect size of 0.41, rising from 0.26 (5 points below the mean) to 0.56 (5 points above the mean).

"The effect of treatment on listening comprehension was statistically significant for at-risk students whose initial PPVT scores were less than 7 points below the mean but not statistically significant for students whose initial PPVT scores were more than 7 points below the mean."
