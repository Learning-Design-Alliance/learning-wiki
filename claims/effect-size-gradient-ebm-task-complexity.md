Zand Vahid, Bazi Ramin, Keshmiri Fatemeh. (2026). Game-based blended learning versus traditional lecture for evidence-based medicine (EBM) competencies in undergraduate medical students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09628-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent t-tests across four ACE domains showed the intervention group scored notably higher in literature search (1.92 vs 1.64; p = 0.031), evidence appraisal (6.07 vs 4.54; p < 0.001), and application of evidence (3.33 vs 2.25; p < 0.001), but "no significant difference between the two groups in the 'question formulation' domain".
