## Evidence

### Turhan ÇETİN & Mustafa TAHİROĞLU 2012

Turhan ÇETİN & Mustafa TAHİROĞLU. (2012). The Attitude of the Students towards the Value of “Paying Attention to Being Healthy” in 4th Grade Elementary Social Sciences Course. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 12(2). www.edam.com.tr/estp. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ987865

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A pre-test/post-test control group quasi-experiment with 22 experiment and 23 control 4th-grade students. The post-test showed a "1,20 point difference in favour of the experiment groups" with t(43)=11.949, p<.05.

> "There is a 1,20 point difference in favour of the experiment groups in the mean scores of the groups from the Attitude Scale of Being Healthy post-test."
