## Evidence

### Rashid Basima 2026

Rashid Basima, Janke Natasha, Gohar Basem, Blokland Kirsten, Taylor Becky, Coe Jason B. (2026). A professional skills and knowledge training programme enhances the communication confidence of registered veterinary technologists/technicians. The Veterinary Record. https://doi.org/10.1002/vetr.70493

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Manifest content analysis of open-ended responses from 28 of the 29 participants in the 2-month follow-up survey; the analysis identified two subcategories of implementation success, with seven participants unable to implement and 14 able to implement their new skills and knowledge.

> "Of these seven individuals, three did not mention their view of their job (43%), while four (57%) expressed a less‐favourable view of their job due to being restricted from implementing the skills and knowledge they gained from attending the training programme."
