### Alexander Mayer 2014

Alexander Mayer, MDRC. (2014). Performance-Based Scholarships: Replication at Six Sites Using Randomized Controlled Trials. SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562816

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trials at six sites with more than 12,000 low-income students randomly assigned to program or control groups; transcript-based outcomes. The abstract reports "These increases are sizeable," with first-term gains of 7.0 percentage points in Ohio to 9.6 in Florida.

> "In all sites except New York, students in the program group met the academic benchmark at higher rates than students in the control group in one of the two semesters. These increases are sizeable."
