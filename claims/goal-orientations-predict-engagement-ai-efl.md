## Evidence

### Yongliang Wang & Yaobo Wang 2026

Yongliang Wang & Yaobo Wang. (2026). Explaining learning engagement in AI-assisted learning through teacher support and achievement goals: insights from goal-setting theory. Humanities and Social Sciences Communications 13:1215. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-026-07636-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM results (Table 3) show supported paths from mastery-approach (β = 0.312), performance-approach (β = 0.302), and performance-avoidance (β = 0.209) goals to engagement, all p < 0.001; the mastery-avoidance path (β = 0.072, p = 0.056) was unsupported, consistent with the article's statement that these three goal types "significantly predict learners' engagement".

> "Mastery-approach, performance-approach, and performance-avoidance goals were all found to significantly predict learners' engagement in AI-assisted English learning."
