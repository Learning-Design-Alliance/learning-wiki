This is the study design as specified in the protocol's Methods and discussion section, not a completed comparison. The protocol specifies a multi-centre non-inferiority design "with historical controls" and a one-week home-use period for 69 healthcare staff and students. No results are reported because no datasets were generated or analysed.

> "A multi-centre, non-inferiority educational interventional study with historical controls will be used to evaluate the novel VR training package. A group of 69 healthcare staff and students will have one week to use the educational intervention as often as necessary to learn paediatric emergency tracheostomy skills."
