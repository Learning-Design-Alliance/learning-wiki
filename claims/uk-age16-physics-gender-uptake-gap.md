A 2020 analysis cited by this UK review shows stark gender differences across all four nations in numbers choosing physics at age 16, with physics "the second most popular A-level choice for boys, but the 15th most popular choice for girls". Table 1 prints percentages: England 8.6% of boys vs 2.6% of girls; Wales 9% vs 2%; Northern Ireland 2.64% vs 1.02%; Scotland Higher physics 72.5% vs 27.5%.
