## Evidence

### Kaijomaa M 2023

Kaijomaa M, Gissler M, Äyräs O, Sten A, Grahn P. (2023). Impact of simulation training on the management of shoulder dystocia and incidence of permanent brachial plexus birth injury: An observational study. BJOG. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.17278

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective observational study of 113,785 vertex deliveries at Helsinki University Women's Hospital, comparing 2010-2014 pre-training with 2015-2019 post-training periods using register data. The study reports that "the number of children with permanent BPBI decreased from 0.05% (0.5/1000 deliveries) to 0.02% (0.2/1000 deliveries)" with p < 0.001, despite increasing SD incidence.
