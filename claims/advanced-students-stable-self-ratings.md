## Evidence

### Ghimire 2025

Ghimire, A. and Singh, C. (2025). Perspectives of Women and Men Students and Faculty on Conceptual and Quantitative Problem-Solving in Physics from Introductory to Graduate Levels. [doi:10.3390/educsci15121602](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci15121602)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Pre/post t-tests in upper-level undergraduate and graduate courses; Table 7 prints negligible effect sizes (e.g., −0.08 upper-level conceptual, −0.17 graduate conceptual) with none statistically significant. The article notes the smaller graduate sample should be noted; equivalence was not tested.

> "No statistically significant changes were observed in either concep- tual or quantitative skills, suggesting that advanced students may have stable self-percep- tions that are less influenced by short-term instructional experiences."
