## Evidence

### Yoo H 2025

Yoo H, Zhou X, Phillips B. (2025). Predictive validity of preadmission assessment on early nursing academic readiness: impact of COVID-19. Nurse Educ. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000001891

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of more than 100 000 students who took both TEAS and Fundamentals between January 2018 and June 2024 (Table 1). Printed effect sizes: Science r = 0.353, Reading r = 0.348, English r = 0.309, Mathematics r = 0.292 with Fundamentals; the study reports "All correlations were statistically significant (P < .001)."

> "All correlations were statistically significant (P < .001). For the total sample, the Science and Reading scores had the higher correlation coefficients than the English and Math. This pattern was consistent among both the ADN and BSN subsamples."
