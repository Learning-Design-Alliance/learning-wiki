Active Commute in Relation to Cognition and Academic Achievement in Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review and Future Recommendations. `q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Systematic review synthesis of studies measuring ACS and cognition among adolescents. The review reports that "three studies found a positive association with at least one cognitive measure" while two found no significant association with any cognitive variable and one was mixed across measures.

> "Overall, three studies found a positive association with at least one cognitive measure [42,45,47]. Two studies did not find any significant association with any of the cognitive variables [48,53], and one study found no associations with some cognitive variables but not others [42]."
