## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Metalinguistic explanation yields large positive effect sizes (d = 0.914 immediate; d = 0.879 delayed) on English article acquisition. [→ Kao 2013](#kao-2013)

## Evidence

### Kao 2013

Kao, C.-W. (2013). Effects of Focused Feedback on the Acquisition of Two English Articles. The Electronic Journal for English as a Second Language, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1007571

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of five metalinguistic-explanation effect sizes from seven focused feedback studies. Immediate d = 0.914 and delayed d = 0.879, both with confidence intervals excluding zero, described as "a large positive effect size".

> "The random-effects model produces a large positive effect size (0.914), with the 95% confidence interval of 0.568 to 1.261 in immediate post-tests and a large positive effect size (0.879) with the confidence interval of 0.576 to 1.182 in delayed post-tests."
