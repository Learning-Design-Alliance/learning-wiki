# The combined theory-driven and data-driven validation approach improved the match rate between trace-based SRL processes and think aloud data from 38.97% to 54.24% (training) and 34.54% to 55.09% (testing)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The validation approach improved the match rate between SRL processes extracted from trace data and think aloud data in both training and testing sets. [→ Fan 2022](#fan-2022)

## Evidence

### Fan 2022

Fan, Y., van der Graaf, J., Lim, L., Raković, M., Singh, S., Kilgour, J., Moore, J., Molenaar, I., Bannert, M., & Gašević, D. (2022). Towards investigating the validity of measurement of self-regulated learning based on trace data. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09291-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Laboratory study with 44 learners studying about artificial intelligence in education in a technology-enhanced learning environment for 45 minutes. The improved match rate quantifies the extent to which validity improved by combining empirical evidence from think aloud data and rationale from the theoretical framework of SRL.

> "we achieved an improved match rate between SRL processes extracted from trace-data and think aloud data (training set: 54.24%; testing set: 55.09%) compared to the match rate before applying the validation approach (training set: 38.97%; test set: 34.54%)."
