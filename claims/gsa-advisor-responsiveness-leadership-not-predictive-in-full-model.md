This finding comes from the study's comparison of bivariate correlations against the full three-wave path model among 627 youth in 51 GSAs. The article reports that "Advisor responsiveness and GSA leadership correlated with wellbeing and academic engagement in bivariate analyses but were not predictive in the comprehensive model."
