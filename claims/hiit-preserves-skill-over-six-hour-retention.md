## Evidence

### Eleanor M. Taylor 2024

Eleanor M. Taylor, Claire J. Cadwallader, Dylan Curtin, Trevor T.-J. Chong, Joshua J. Hendrikse & James P. Coxon. (2024). High-intensity acute exercise impacts motor learning in healthy older adults. npj Science of Learning 9:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00220-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Retention analysis in the same randomised between-groups study, tested ~6 hours after acquisition. The article reports the Group × Time interaction "(F(1, 21) = 5.98, p = 0.03)"; skill scores did not differ significantly between groups at end of acquisition or start of retention. No effect size printed.

> "there was a Group by Time interaction (F(1, 21) = 5.98, p = 0.03), with participants in the rest group showing a reduction in skill between learning and retention"
