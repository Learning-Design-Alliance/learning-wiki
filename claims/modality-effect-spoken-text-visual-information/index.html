Evidence¶

Sweller 2019¶

Sweller, J., van Merriënboer, J. J. G., & Paas, F. (2019). Cognitive Architecture and Instructional Design: 20 Years Later. Educational Psychology Review, 31, 261–292. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09465-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

Mousavi et al.'s (1995) geometry-learning experiment, first test of the modality effect per this review, compared a diagram with integrated written text versus auditory spoken text; the spoken-text combination was most effective, and the review cites Ginns (2005a) meta-analysis.

"They indeed found the modality effect that has since been replicated in many other experiments."
