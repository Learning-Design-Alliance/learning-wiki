## Evidence

### Janneke van de Pol 2020

Janneke van de Pol, et al. (2020). Mapping and Drawing to Improve Students’ and Teachers’ Monitoring and Regulation of Students’ Learning from Text: Current Findings and Future Directions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09560-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's conclusion section states this boundary condition for concept mapping and drawing, noting that for diagramming extensive instructions are not needed because the pre-structured diagram already focuses attention on relations in the text.

> "Only when students are instructed to focus on relations in the text in their concept maps or drawings, do the students ’ products seem to contain diagnostic cues which are needed for accurate judgments."

### Janneke van de Pol 2020 (2)

Janneke van de Pol, et al. (2020). Mapping and Drawing to Improve Students’ and Teachers’ Monitoring and Regulation of Students’ Learning from Text: Current Findings and Future Directions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09560-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's conclusion across delayed-approach (e.g., Van Loon et al., 2014) and concurrent-approach (e.g., Thiede et al., 2019) studies, stating that some form of retrieval, at least when making the judgments, might be necessary for promoting monitoring accuracy.

> "Yet, only when students did not have access to the products of their generative activities when making judgments, did their monitoring accuracy benefit."
