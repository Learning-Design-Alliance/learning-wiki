## Evidence

### Keselman 1998

Keselman, H. J., Huberty, C. J., Lix, L. M., Olejnik, S., Cribbie, R. A., Donahue, B., Kowalchuk, R. K., Lowman, L. L., Petoskey, M. D., Keselman, J. C., & Levin, J. R. (1998). Statistical Practises of Educational Researchers: An Analysis of Their ANOVA, MANOVA and ANCOVA Analyses. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Statistical+Practises+of+Educational+Researchers

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Content analysis of 61 between-subjects univariate articles from prominent educational journals. The authors report that "In 12 articles (19.7%), the author(s) indicated some concern for distributional assumption violations," with normality assessed by descriptive measures only and variance homogeneity evaluated in five articles.

> "In 12 articles (19.7%), the author(s) indicated some concern for distributional assumption violations. Normality was a consideration in seven articles, although no specific tests for violations of this assumption were reported"
