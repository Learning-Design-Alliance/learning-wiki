## Evidence

### Thomaschewski M 2025

Thomaschewski M, Vonthein R, Keck T, Laubert T, Benecke C. (2025). Laparoscopic simulation training improves operating room performance of surgical residents: a multicenter randomized trial (NOVICE). International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000002304

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary-endpoint analysis of individual GOALS domains in the FAS (n = 18), reported in Table 5 with median-of-differences and Hodges-Lehman confidence intervals. The article reports "statistically significantly higher" depth-perception gains for the LTB group only.

> "While the improvements in the domain “depth perception” of the GOALS score were statistically significantly higher in the LTB group in comparison to the control group, the differences in the other domains of the GOALS score between both groups were not statistically significant."
