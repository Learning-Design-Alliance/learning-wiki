Evidence¶

Ceylan 2018¶

Ceylan, Ö., & Yiğit, E. A. (2018). Analyzing the Effect of Concept Cartoon Usage on Students’ Cognitive Structures Developments and Science Achievements through Flow Maps. Science Education International, 29(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1205414

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Mann–Whitney U-test on achievement post-test scores in the semi-experimental pre-test/post-test design (27 experimental, 27 control). Mean ranks (34.41 vs 17.59) show the experimental group higher, supporting that "the teaching with concept cartoons was effective in enhancing the success of teaching."

"it is seen that there is a significant difference (U = 97.00, ρ < 0.05) between the achievement test scores of the students of the experimental group and the control group"
