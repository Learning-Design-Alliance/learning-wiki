## Evidence

### Peter Hu 2023

Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, Roger S. K. Mong, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Student Understanding of the Bloch Sphere. https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad2393

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post classroom study (N = 28) on questions 4a-c, which evaluated knowledge that when θ is the angle between the given state and a basis state, the probability of that basis state is cos²(θ/2); the tutorial has a section dealing with measurements made in arbitrary bases.

> "On the pre-test, many students gave multiple incorrect answers. After engaging with the tutorial, the vast majority of students gave answers in the correct form, with some only forgetting to divide the appropriate 𝜃 by 2, for which they were given partial credit."
