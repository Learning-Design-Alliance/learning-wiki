## Evidence

### Li Y 2025

Li Y, Cheng S, Liu D, Jiang W. (2025). “Learning, Thinking & Conveying” training with experiential learning in the emergency department for undergraduate nursing students: A quasi-experimental study. Medicine 2025;104:28(e43231). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000043231

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 90 nursing students (45 per group) in a tertiary hospital emergency department, assigned by rotation month. Regression on theoretical exam results showed "β = 0.731, P < .001" (multivariate β = 0.727, P < .001); the experimental group scored 90.69 ± 3.895 versus 81.73 ± 4.545 (t = 10.037).

> "An impact of “Learning, Thinking & Conveying” Training with Experiential Learning on theoretical exam results (β = 0.731, P < .001) was shown by univariate linear regression analysis and this relationship remained after mutual adjustment by multivariate linear regression"
