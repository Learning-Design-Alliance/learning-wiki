## Evidence

### Premo Joshua 2018

Premo Joshua, Cavagnetto Andy, Davis William B. (2018). Promoting Collaborative Classrooms: The Impacts of Interdependent Cooperative Learning on Undergraduate Interactions and Achievement. CBE—Life Sciences Education 17(2). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-08-0176

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

BEG coding of audio/video recordings of 72 students (36 per condition, 12 at each of three time points), coded every 10 seconds with Cohen's kappa = 0.84–0.85; unpaired t tests with Bonferroni correction showed more task discussion and peer support in the intervention condition.

> "students in the intervention condition dedicated proportionally more time to discussing the answers and underlying concepts of the question sets with their laboratory groups (p < 0.0001, R2 = 0.24) and being socially supportive of their peers (p < 0.001, R2 = 0.14) than did students in the comparison condition."

### Premo Joshua 2018 (2)

Premo Joshua, Cavagnetto Andy, Davis William B. (2018). Promoting Collaborative Classrooms: The Impacts of Interdependent Cooperative Learning on Undergraduate Interactions and Achievement. CBE—Life Sciences Education 17(2). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-08-0176

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression on BEG-coded behavior of the same 72-student subsample showed comparison-classroom students spent more time not working with their four-member table group in both intervention phases (R2 = 0.24 and 0.25).

> "student presence in a comparison classroom predicted increased time not working with their table group (four members) compared with the intervention students during both phase 1 (p < 0.0001, R2 = 0.24) and phase 2 (p < 0.0001, R2 = 0.25)."
