Scoping review coding of educational objects across the 53-study corpus distinguished six categories (Table 4), including language, literacy, and narrative development, logical and problem-solving development, cognitive development, emotional and relational development, AI and digital literacy, and self-directed learning. The review cautions the categories "do not indicate demonstrated learning gains."

> "These categories describe intended learning purposes or the focus of a needs or interaction-analysis study; they do not indicate demonstrated learning gains."
