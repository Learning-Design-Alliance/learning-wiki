# In the model's simulations, group performance increases through discussion even when no group member initially knows the correct answer, provided initial opinions sufficiently differ

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Simulated groups improve their average score through iterated discussion regardless of initial condition (NH, EH, SH), and improvement is larger when initial opinions are more diverse. [→ Jibeom Seo 2025](#jibeom-seo-2025)

## Evidence

### Jibeom Seo 2025

Jibeom Seo, Beom Jun Kim. (2025). Opinion dynamics model of collaborative learning. [doi:10.1016/j.physa.2025.130670](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physa.2025.130670)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Monte Carlo simulation of the proposed agent-based model (N = 4, α = β = 0.5, T = 10, tf = 100), ensemble-averaged over 1,000 independent runs, shown in Fig. 2. The article reports that "regardless of initial conditions, all three (NH, EH, and SH) clearly exhibit improvement in group performance."

> "regardless of initial conditions, all three (NH, EH, and SH) clearly exhibit improvement in group performance, which implies that group discussions help the students to get to the correct answer as the discussion sessions are iterated."
