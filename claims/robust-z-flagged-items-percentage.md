### He 2022

He, W. (2022). MAP Growth item parameter drift study. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-item-parameter-drift-study/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robust Z analysis (two-sided tests, significance level 0.05) of difficulty differences from two calibrations separated by at least four years. The report states the percentages, except for science items, are slightly larger than the 5% nominal error rate, suggesting some drift occurring on each scale.

> "As shown in Table 3.2, between 4.2% and 6.9% of items across subjects were flagged as unstable by the Robust Z procedure."
