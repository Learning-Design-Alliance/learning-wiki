`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Path analysis in the classroom study found pre-test scores did not significantly predict %KBR; the article reports the coefficients "were not statistically significant" and suggests knowledge-building may be learned or prompted through adaptive follow-up questioning.

> "the regression coefficients from both conceptual and procedural pre-test scores to %KBR were not statisti- cally significant (CKT pre : β = .440,p = .10; PST pre : β = .206,p = .16)."
