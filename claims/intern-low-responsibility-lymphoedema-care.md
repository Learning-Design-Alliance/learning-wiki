Kruger N, Plinsinga M, Window P, Hayes S, Bunzli S. (2025). Comfortable knowing little about lymphoedema? A qualitative study of medical interns in Australia. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-089789

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 4 of the thematic analysis: interns deferred to senior colleagues, who often advised seeking support elsewhere ("refer to physio"), and participants were unsure which specialists owned lymphoedema care or how to access them.

> "The majority of participants described a low sense of responsibility towards lymphoedema care. Most felt that they would need to defer lymphoedema care to others compared to other conditions where they felt more experienced"
