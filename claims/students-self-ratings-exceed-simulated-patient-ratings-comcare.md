## Evidence

### Gärtner Julia 2021

Gärtner Julia, Prediger Sarah, Harendza Sigrid. (2021). Development and pilot test of ComCare – a questionnaire for quick assessment of communicative and social competences in medical students after interviews with simulated patients. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001464](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001464)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the simulated consultation hour assessment, students' self-assessment (ComCareD, total 4.16±.34) was "significantly (p<.001) higher than" the simulated patients' ComCareP total (2.79±.38). The comparison held for both sexes and all three thirds of final-year advancement.

> "All item means as well as the total mean score of the ComCareD (4.16±.34) were significantly (p<.001) higher than all item means and the total mean score of the ComCareP."
