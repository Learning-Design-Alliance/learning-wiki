## Evidence

### Do Thang Xuân 2022

Do Thang Xuân, Tran Huong Thi Lan, Le Thuy Thu. (2022). Factors influencing the E-learning system usage during the COVID-19 pandemic in Vietnam. PLOS ONE 17(12): e0278109. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0278109

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression results (Table 4) from the 1612-response survey; the system & technical dimension carried a standardized coefficient of "β = 0.241, Sig <0.005"; no separate standardized effect size is printed.

> "perceived usefulness (β = 0.610, Sig <0.005) and perceived ease of use (β = 0.350, Sig <0.005) showed the greatest effects, following by system & technical dimension (β = 0.241, Sig <0.005)."

### Do Thang Xuân 2022 (2)

Do Thang Xuân, Tran Huong Thi Lan, Le Thuy Thu. (2022). Factors influencing the E-learning system usage during the COVID-19 pandemic in Vietnam. PLOS ONE 17(12): e0278109. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0278109

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion-section observation from the same survey: functionality items including "lecture recording" and prompt posting of materials received low ratings, and the authors note transmission quality or device problems could interrupt online learning.

> "However, factors related to the system’s functionality including lecture recording, materials posted online promptly were rated at low score."
