Chun-Yi Lee 2015¶

Chun-Yi Lee. (2015). The Effects of Online Peer Assessment and Family Entrepreneurial Experience on Students' Business Planning Performance. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057346

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Series of paired t-tests across the three review rounds (Table 5 reports t values such as -25.911*** for the reviewer group round 1); all increases were significant at p < .001, with no effect size printed.
