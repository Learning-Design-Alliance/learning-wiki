`q2 i?` Of 23 graduate students taking the 5-bulbs pretest before reading the research literature, 13 (57%) gave the correct ranking with correct reasoning; 12 of 15 with physics backgrounds (80%) were correct versus 1 of 8 with non-physics backgrounds (13%).
