## Evidence

### Kimmo Sorjonen 2025

Kimmo Sorjonen, Bo Melin, Gustav Nilsonne. (2025). Inconclusive Evidence for a Prospective Effect of Academic Self-Concept on Achievement: A Simulated Reanalysis and Comment on Marsh et al. (2024). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10008-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Modified LCSM fitted on the simulated PALMA-like data (acceptable fit: χ2 = 1324, DF = 46, CFI = 0.947, RMSEA = 0.089). The article reports "a positive effect of change in achievement on contemporaneous change in self-concept" (b = 0.277 [0.265; 0.288], p < 0.001); the modified STARTS also fit well (χ2 = 266, CFI = 0.991).

> "This interpretation was validated by a modified LCSM (Fig. 2B), where latent change in achievement had a positive effect on contemporaneous latent change in self-concept (b = 0.277 [0.265; 0.288], p < 0.001)"
