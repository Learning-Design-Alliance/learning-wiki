# Caudate–claustrum functional connectivity during retrieval is lower after short-video learning, with no group differences in MTG-involving connectivity

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Task-related functional connectivity between the caudate and claustrum was significantly lower in the short video group (d = -0.794). [→ Wei 2026](#wei-2026)
`q3 i?` No significant group differences were found for caudate–MTG or MTG–claustrum connectivity. [→ Wei 2026 (2)](#wei-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Wei 2026

Wei, M., Liu, J., Wang, H., Li, Q. & Dong, G.-H. (2026). Fragmented learning from short videos modulates neural activity and connectivity during memory retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00399-y

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Generalized PPI analysis (CONN toolbox) using the GLM activation clusters as seeds, comparing task-related connectivity between the randomized groups. "FC between the caudate and claustrum" was significantly lower in the short video group, "Cohen’s d = -0.794".

> "FC between the caudate and claustrum (Fig. 2a) was significantly lower in the short video group than in the long video group (t(55) = -2.998, p = .004, Cohen’s d = -0.794; see Fig. 2b)."

### Wei 2026 (2)

Wei, M., Liu, J., Wang, H., Li, Q. & Dong, G.-H. (2026). Fragmented learning from short videos modulates neural activity and connectivity during memory retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00399-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same PPI analysis: connectivity involving the MTG did not differ between groups (MTG–claustrum d = 0.146; caudate–MTG d = -0.023). The connectivity effect is therefore specific to the caudate–claustrum pair; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "No significant group differences were found for the FC between the left middle temporal gyrus (MTG) and claustrum (t(55) = 0.549, p = .585, Cohen’s d = 0.146), or between the caudate and MTG (t(55) = -0.087, p = .931, Cohen’s d = -0.023)."
