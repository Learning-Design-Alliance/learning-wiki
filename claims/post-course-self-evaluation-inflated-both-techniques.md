## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Average self-evaluation grades increased from 2.6 ± 0.6 before the course to about 3.6–3.7 after the course, with no significant difference between teaching techniques (P = 0.616). [→ Jenko Matej 2012](#jenko-matej-2012)

## Evidence

### Jenko Matej 2012

Jenko Matej, Frangež Maja, Manohin Aleksander. (2012). Four-stage teaching technique and chest compression performance of medical students compared to conventional technique. Croatian Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.3325/cmj.2012.53.486

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire administered before and after the course to all 126 students, analyzed with the Mann-Whitney U test. Post-course grades of "3.7 ± 0.47" and "3.6 ± 0.48" followed a pre-course average of "2.6 ± 0.6" in both groups (P = 0.805).

> "The average grade after the course was 3.7 ± 0.47 in 2-stage approach and 3.6 ± 0.48 in 4-stage approach, with no significant difference between the groups (P = 0.616, Mann-Whitney test U)"
