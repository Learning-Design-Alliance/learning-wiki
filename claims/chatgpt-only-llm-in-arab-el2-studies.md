# ChatGPT is the only LLM found in use in Arab university EL2 classrooms in the reviewed 2023–2025 studies, despite the review's aim to capture Gemini and DeepSeek

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of the LLMs launched 2023–2025, only ChatGPT was utilized in the Arab university EL2 studies meeting inclusion criteria; Gemini and DeepSeek received no included studies. [→ Mohammed Q. Shormani; Muneef Yehia Alshawsh 2026](#mohammed-q-shormani-muneef-yehia-alshawsh-2026)

## Evidence

### Mohammed Q. Shormani; Muneef Yehia Alshawsh 2026

Mohammed Q. Shormani; Muneef Yehia Alshawsh. (2026). AI tools in Arab University English classrooms: Looking back and forward. Preprint, Ibb University, Yemen. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0138-4793

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Result of the review's database synthesis: although the search window was chosen to catch ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek, only ChatGPT appeared among the 11 included studies, alongside tools such as Grammarly. The review speculates other-LLM studies were excluded by the criteria.

> "However, we found that the only LLM utilized in Arab University EL2 classrooms is ChatGPT, though other AI-powered tools such as Grammarly are employed in studies involved in our study."
