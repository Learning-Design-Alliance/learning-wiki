### Thielmann Anika 2024

Thielmann Anika, Schmitz Marie-Therese, Welchowski Thomas, Weltermann Birgitta. (2024). Effectiveness of the online-eLearning program KeepCoool at improving the vaccine cold chain in general practices. PLoS ONE 19(4): e0301847. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0301847

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective observational baseline assessment of 64 practices with 75 refrigerators using one-minute continuous temperature logging; 68% of refrigerators were outside the target range and 15% had reached critically low temperatures <0°C for up to 7 days.

> "At baseline, 73.4% of the practices (47 of 64) showed cold chain breaches in ≥ 1 refrigerator and were offered the eLearning."
