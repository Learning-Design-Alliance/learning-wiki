### Yangchu Huang 2026

Yangchu Huang, Xinyi Du & Shanshan Zhen. (2026). Deciding for others diminishes model-based decision-making but depends on individual prosociality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00397-0

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

RLDDM parameter comparison in the same 92-participant task. The article reports a "significantly higher model-free learning rate when deciding for oneself than for others" with printed effect size d = 0.29 (self M ± SD = 0.65 ± 0.22; others M ± SD = 0.57 ± 0.28), indicating slower adaptation to recent outcomes in prosocial contexts.
