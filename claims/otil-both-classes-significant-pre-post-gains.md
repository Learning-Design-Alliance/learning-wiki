## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Paired-samples T-tests showed significant pre-test to post-test differences in both classes (P=0.000), with a larger paired difference in the experimental class (-18.26) than the control class (-9.17). [→ Xingbin Tian & Suksan Suppasetseree 2013](#xingbin-tian-suksan-suppasetseree-2013)

## Evidence

### Xingbin Tian & Suksan Suppasetseree 2013

Xingbin Tian & Suksan Suppasetseree. (2013). Development of an Instructional Model for Online Task-Based Interactive Listening for EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n3p30

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples T-test on each class (N=46 per class). Experimental class paired difference mean -18.26 (SD=4.855, t=-25.50, df=45); control class -9.17 (SD=4.312, t=-14.43, df=45); both Sig. .000.

> "In Table 3, both experimental and control classes had significant differences between the pre-test and post-test (P=0.000, P ≤0.05). Therefore, both classes of students made a great progress in English listening learning."
