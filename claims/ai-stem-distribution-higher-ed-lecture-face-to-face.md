`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's distribution analysis of 63 studies found 43 of 59 level-reporting studies focused on higher education, with medium scale (N = 24) and large scale (N = 21) samples outnumbering small scale (N = 10). Instructors most frequently used lecturing (N = 27), computer system resource was the most used medium (N = 28), and face-to-face environment dominated (N = 33 of 51).

> "Third, a majority of AI techniques (except educational robots) were applied in higher education with medium and large scale of learners. The most fre- quently used AI in higher education were learning predic- tion and ITSs."
