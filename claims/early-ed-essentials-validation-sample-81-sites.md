# The validation study sample comprised 81 Chicago ECE sites with high teacher and parent survey response rates

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A stratified random sample of eligible Chicago ECE sites yielded a final sample of 81 sites (41 school-based, 40 community-based), with 746 teacher surveys and 2,464 parent surveys collected. [→ Ehrlich 2018](#ehrlich-2018)
`q2 i?` An average of 75 percent of teachers and 31 percent of parents responded across sites, and about 85 percent of sites met teacher survey criteria while 95 percent met parent survey criteria. [→ Ehrlich 2018 (2)](#ehrlich-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Ehrlich 2018

Ehrlich, S.B., Pacchiano, D.M., Stein, A.G., Wagner, M.R., Luppescu, S., Park, S., Frank, E., Lewandowski, H., & Young, C. (2018). Organizing early education for improvement: Testing a new survey tool. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research and the Ounce of Prevention Fund. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/organizing-early-education-improvement-testing-new-survey-tool

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stratified random sampling (PROC SURVEYSELECT in SAS, stratified by percent of pre-k students speaking Spanish) of Chicago programs with at least three pre-k classrooms and six teachers. "Of the sites selected and contacted for recruitment (n=146), 55 percent agreed to participate," yielding 81 sites. Participation was higher among majority-Latino sites (59 percent) than majority-Black sites (51 percent).

> "Of the sites selected and contacted for recruitment (n=146), 55 percent agreed to participate. Participation rates were 5 7 percent and 54 percent for school - and community -based settings, respectively, resulting in a  final sample of 81 sites— 41 school -based and 40 community -based."

### Ehrlich 2018 (2)

Ehrlich, S.B., Pacchiano, D.M., Stein, A.G., Wagner, M.R., Luppescu, S., Park, S., Frank, E., Lewandowski, H., & Young, C. (2018). Organizing early education for improvement: Testing a new survey tool. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research and the Ounce of Prevention Fund. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/organizing-early-education-improvement-testing-new-survey-tool

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey administration during spring 2016 across the 81 validation sites, with teacher surveys online in English and parent surveys online and on paper in English and Spanish. "An average of 75 percent of teachers responded to the survey" and 31 percent of parents; about 85 percent of sites met teacher survey criteria and 95 percent met parent survey criteria.

> "Across all sites, an average of 75 percent of teachers responded to the survey (79 percent in school -based and 71 per cent in community- based) and 31 percent of parents responded to the survey (32 percent in school -based and 31 percent in community -based)."
