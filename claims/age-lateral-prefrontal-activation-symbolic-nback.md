## Evidence

### Leopold D.R. 2024

Leopold D.R., Kim H., Carlson K.W., Rowe M.A., Groff B.R., Major M.P., Willcutt E.G., Cutting L.E., Banich M.T. (2024). Stimulus shapes strategy: Effects of stimulus characteristics and individual differences in academic achievement on the neural mechanisms engaged during the N-back task. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2024.101372

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain fMRI analysis treating age as a covariate of interest in 126 youth aged 10-16. The study found "increased activation in a portion of left lateral prefrontal cortex" associated with older age only for the whole-task symbolic contrast; no regions were identified for the 2>0-back contrast. No effect size printed.

> "However, when examining the symbolic contrast across the entire task (i.e., 0-back+2-back>fixation), increased activation in a portion of left lateral prefrontal cortex – posterior inferior frontal sulcus into precentral gyrus – was associated with older age (Fig. 4C; Table S7)."
