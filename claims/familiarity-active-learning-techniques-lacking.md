## Evidence

### Heng W 2025

Heng W, Ho MH, Mah XH, Lim JJ, Syamsury NBHA, Haagensen E, Ong ELC, Hubbard P. (2025). Exploring pre‐clinical medical students' perception of and participation in active learning: A mixed‐methods transnational study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.15611

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Likert questionnaire item analysed descriptively and with Mann–Whitney U tests. "67.8% of students agreed" they were familiar with techniques; 19.9% were neutral, versus only 1.5% neutral on importance. No significant campus (p = 0.089, p = 0.769) or year-group differences.

> "When asked ‘I am familiar with active learning techniques’ 67.8% of students agreed with this statement (Figure 3b), with a large proportion of students choosing the neutral option (19.9%) for this question"
