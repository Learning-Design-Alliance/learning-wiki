Descriptive subgroup comparison from the same pre/post survey data (N=13); the article prints gains of 66% versus 31% but no test statistic or effect size for this contrast.

> "Generally, those students who had a better grasp of the material upon entering this class made greater improvements than those who were weaker at entry.  (The half of the class that performed best on the pre-test made average gains of 66%; whereas, the bottom half of the class made gains of 31%.)"
