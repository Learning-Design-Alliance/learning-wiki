Bennun, L., & Santibanez, M. (2015). Analysis of the benefits of designing and implementing a virtual didactic model of multiple choice exam and problem-solving heuristic report, for first year engineering students. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1506.03719](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1506.03719)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Second-exam results in the same case study: 19 of 38 platform users passed (68%) versus 64% of 162 non-users. The article also reports user average 4.6 versus non-user 4.1. No significance test is printed; the between-group gap is far smaller than on the first exam.
