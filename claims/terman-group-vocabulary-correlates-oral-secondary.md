## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among 57 girls in a secondary school, scores from a written group administration of the Terman vocabulary test correlated +0.7487 with scores from individual oral administration. [→ Weeks 1919](#weeks-1919)

## Evidence

### Weeks 1919

Weeks, A. L. (1919). Terman Vocabulary as a Group Test. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A method-comparison study in one girls' secondary school (n=57): the Terman vocabulary list was given in written group form and, afterward, in oral individual form to the same pupils, using alternate word series. The rank-order correlation between the two formats was "+ 0.7487".

> "The correlation of the results obtained in group and individual tests was + 0.7487, which, though not very high, is sufficient to warrant the use of the group method of vocabulary testing in secondary schools."
