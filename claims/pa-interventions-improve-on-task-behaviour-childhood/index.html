Evidence¶

Vasilopoulos 2023¶

Vasilopoulos, F., Jeffrey, H., Wu, Y., & Dumontheil, I. (2023). Multi-Level Meta-Analysis of Physical Activity Interventions During Childhood: Effects of Physical Activity on Cognition and Academic Achievement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09760-2

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Three-level meta-analysis of on-task behaviour outcomes from five studies (six effect sizes, 804 participants) in the review's Table 4, showing a summary Hedges' g of "1.04" with 95% CI 0.08–2.00 and p = 0.03. The authors rate this very low-certainty evidence under GRADE.
