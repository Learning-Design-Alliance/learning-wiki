Evidence¶

Tupe 2015¶

Tupe, N. (2015). Multimedia Scenario Based Learning Programme for Enhancing the English Language Efficiency among Primary School Students. International Journal of Instruction, 8(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085304

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pre-test post-test control group study in one Marathi-medium primary school; two intact divisions of 51 students each. "The Remedial Teaching through the MSBL has been implemented in the Experimental group for three months" while the control received traditional teaching; post-test t-tests rejected the null hypothesis of equal effectiveness.

"Their gain score differences as a result of treatment in Experimental and Control groups are significant."
