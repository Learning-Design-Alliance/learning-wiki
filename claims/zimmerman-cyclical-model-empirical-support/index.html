Evidence¶

Panadero E 2017¶

Panadero E. (2017). A Review of Self-regulated Learning: Six Models and Four Directions for Research. Front. Psychol. 8:422. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.00422

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Experimental training study reviewed by the article as support for the cyclical model: 50 college students in five conditions training one to three SRL phases plus two controls; "The results showed a linear trend: the more phases trained the better the participants' scores." No effect size printed.

"Cleary et al. (2006) trained 50 college students in basketball free throws organized in ﬁve diﬀerent conditions: one-phase SRL, two- phases SRL, three-phases SRL, control group practice-only and control group no-practice. The results showed a linear trend: the more phases trained the better the participants' scores."

Panadero E 2017 (2)¶

Panadero E. (2017). A Review of Self-regulated Learning: Six Models and Four Directions for Research. Front. Psychol. 8:422. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.00422

q2 · i? · review · r2

Comparative study of expert and non-expert college women in volleyball; the review reports "the SRL skills predicted 90% variance in serving skills." Variance explained is not a standardized effect size, so impact is null.

"Kitsantas and Zimmerman (2002) compared college women that were experts and non-experts in volleyball, ﬁnding that the SRL skills predicted 90% variance in serving skills."
