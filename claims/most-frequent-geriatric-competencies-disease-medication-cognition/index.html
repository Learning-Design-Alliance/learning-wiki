Evidence¶

Yang Deng-Chi 2025¶

Yang Deng-Chi, Yu Yung-Chen, Lee Yi-Hsuan, Su Ruei-Ning, Lin Wei-Hung, Lai Pei-Chun. (2025). Geriatric core competencies for non-geriatricians and nurses: a scoping review. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-025-06806-8

q2 · i? · review · r2

Scoping review synthesis of ten expert-consensus/Delphi studies (1989–2017) yielding 257 competencies across 18 domains. The review reports that "the geriatric core competencies most frequently mentioned by physicians, medical students, and nurses were in the domains of disease and comorbidities, pharmacologic/medication management, and cognitive/dementia problems."

"Overall, the geriatric core competencies most frequently mentioned by physicians, medical students, and nurses were in the domains of disease and comorbidities, pharmacologic/medication management, and cognitive/dementia problems."
