## Evidence

### Nelson 2014

Nelson, L. P., & Crow, M. L. (2014). Do Active-Learning Strategies Improve Students’ Critical Thinking? Higher Education Studies, 4(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v4n2p77

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures analysis across both conditions (N=190) found "an effect of time (pre- vs. post-treatment)", with post-test means higher than pre-test in almost every analysis except the Management Vignette. Table 1 reports significant time F values for Legal, Motivation, and Total scores.

> "However, we did find an effect of time (pre- vs. post-treatment), with post-test scores being higher than the pre-test score means in almost every analysis except within the Management Vignette."
