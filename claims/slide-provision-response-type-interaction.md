## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Samuel P. León 2021

Samuel P. León, Inmaculada García-Martínez. (2021). Impact of the provision of PowerPoint slides on learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104283

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A 2 (PPT availability) x 3 (response type) ANOVA on response patterns; correct responses were 53.52% in N-ppt versus 46.89% in Y-ppt, and blank responses differed by 12%. The printed interaction effect size was η2 p = .067, and "the interaction uncovered was due to the higher number of correct answers and lower number of blank answers given in the N-ppt condition".

> "the presence of a two-way interaction (PPT x response type, F(2, 320) = 11.51, p < .001, η2 p = .067, CI95% [0.021–0.122])."
