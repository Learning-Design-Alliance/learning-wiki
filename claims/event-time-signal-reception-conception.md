## Evidence

### Paul Alstein 2021

Paul Alstein, Kim Krijtenburg-Lewerissa, and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2021). Teaching and learning special relativity theory in secondary and lower undergraduate education: A literature review. Physical Review Physics Education Research 17, 023101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.17.023101

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Scherr et al., an analysis of physics and nonphysics students' responses to a written questionnaire (N = 800) finding that "students tend to associate the time of an event with the time at which a light signal from the event is received". No effect size is printed.

> "It was found that students tend to associate the time of an event with the time at which a light signal from the event is received by the observer."
