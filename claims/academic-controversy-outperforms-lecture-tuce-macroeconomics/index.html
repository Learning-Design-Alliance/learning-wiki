Evidence¶

Craig F. Santicola 2015¶

Craig F. Santicola. (2015). Academic Controversy In Macroeconomics: An Active And Collaborative Method To Increase Student Learning. American Journal Of Business Education – Third Quarter 2015 Volume 8, Number 3. https://www.clutejournals.com/index.php/AJBE

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with 34 post-secondary students at a Mid-Atlantic community college, 17 per group, both taught by the researcher; independent samples t-test on TUCE overall scores. The academic controversy group "significantly outperformed" the lecture group; no effect size is printed.

"students receiving the academic controversy treatment (M = 18.35, SD = 5.17) significantly outperformed the students who received traditional lecture (M = 14.29, SD = 4.41), t(32) = 2.46, p = .02"
