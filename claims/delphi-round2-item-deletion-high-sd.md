## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Three Round 2 items — nutrition and exercise counseling and prescription, provision of psychological support, and therapeutic mediation with caregivers — were deleted because their SDs exceeded 0.8 and they were judged less relevant to shared physician–nurse roles. ## Evidence

### Lee Jin Young 2025

Lee Jin Young, Min Yul Ha, Yim Jun, Park Kwi Hwa, Yune So Jung. (2025). Core shared competencies between physicians and nurses in primary care: a Delphi study in Korea. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.359

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Round 2 of the Delphi evaluated 16 items derived from Round 1 open-ended responses; all showed means of 4.03–4.86 and SDs of 0.38–0.93. The three deleted items were also considered conceptually overlapping with other items. No effect size is printed.

> "After meeting the minimum CVR criterion (0.34), three items (‘nutrition and exercise counseling and prescription,’ ‘provision of psychological support,’ ‘therapeutic mediation with caregivers’) were deleted due to SDs above 0.8, and 13 items were integrated and refined."
