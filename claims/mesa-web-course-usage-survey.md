`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

An anonymous survey reported in the classroom-use section. It found roughly equal undergraduate and graduate course adoption, mostly once-yearly teaching, with the printed splits of 62% repeated use versus 38% single use and 55% versus 45% on course size.

> "In 62% of these courses, MESA-Web is used repeatedly during the term while the remainder (38%) used it once. About 55% of the respondents said their courses had no more than 20 learners and 45% said their courses had more than 20 learners."
