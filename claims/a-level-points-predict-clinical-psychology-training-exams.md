## Evidence

### Scior Katrina 2014

Scior Katrina, Bradley Caroline E, Potts Henry W W, Woolf Katherine, de C Williams Amanda C. (2014). What predicts performance during clinical psychology training? The British Journal of Clinical Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjc.12035

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analyses of 274 trainees from seven cohorts found A-level points significantly predicted exam 1 (B = 0.002, p = .015), exam 2 (B = 0.002, p = .021), exam 3 (B = 0.009, p = .007) and exam 4 (B = 0.022, p = .036) marks. The article notes A-levels were "the most important predictor of performance during training" and "the only data that showed a reasonable range".

> "They predicted marks on all four of the exams independently of other pre-course variables, and were univariately associated with clinical placement problems."
