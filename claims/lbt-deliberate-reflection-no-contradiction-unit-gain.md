## Evidence

### Kuhn Josepha 2022

Kuhn Josepha, Mamede Silvia, van den Berg Pieter, Zwaan Laura, van Peet Petra, Bindels Patrick, van Gog Tamara. (2022). Learning deliberate reflection in medical diagnosis: does learning-by-teaching help? Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10138-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Think-aloud protocols from the test phase were coded into idea units and deliberate-reflection steps; the ANOVA on mean proportion of contradiction units found "no main effects" on "the proportion of contradiction units, F (1, 43) = 0.37, p = 0.544, ηp2 = 0.01". Means were .31 (learning-by-teaching) vs .29 (control), descriptively.

> "or on the proportion of contradiction units, F (1, 43) = 0.37, p = 0.544, ηp2 = 0.01. Note that only 45 residents were included in the latter analysis because for 11 residents the think-aloud task was not recorded correctly."
