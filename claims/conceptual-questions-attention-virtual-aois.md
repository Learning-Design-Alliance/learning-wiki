## Evidence

### Atakan Coban 2025

Atakan Coban, David Dzsotjan, Stefan Küchemann, Jürgen Durst, Jochen Kuhn & Christoph Hoyer. (2025). AI Support Meets AR Visualization for Alice and Bob: Personalized Learning Based on Individual ChatGPT Feedback in an AR Quantum Cryptography Experiment for Physics Lab Courses. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00310-z](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00310-z)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired sample t-tests on normalized total fixation durations for groups receiving feedback on Q1 (N=5), Q2 (N=6) and Q4 (N=3); all intervals showed "significantly higher" virtual AOI attention (e.g. Q4 Interval 1: t=-15.813, p=0.004). No effect size is printed in the quoted result sentence.

> "The results indicate that the normalized total fixation duration on virtual components is significantly higher than that on physical components during both intervals."
