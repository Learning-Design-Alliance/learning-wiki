Matey Yordanov 2026¶

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Independent samples t-test on post-intervention survey data; composite Likert-scale constructs adapted from Bandura's self-efficacy scales with Cronbach's α > .80. The article reports the printed statistics above, including effect size d = 0.76, and qualitative feedback that ECF validation "systematically reduced mathematics anxiety."

"Self-reported “Exam Confidence“ was significantly higher in the intervention group (M= 4.3/5.0) compared to the control group (M= 3.4/5.0),t(140) = 4.56,p < .001,d= 0.76."
