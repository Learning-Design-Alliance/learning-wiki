### M. Schoo 2015

M. Schoo, S. Lawn, E. Rudnik, C. Litt. (2015). Teaching health science students foundation motivational interviewing skills: use of motivational interviewing treatment integrity and self-reflection to approach transformative learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0512-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative descriptive analysis of 22 students' self-reported MITI scores after one lecture, two tutorials and a self-rated simulated interview. The article reports "Global Spirit scores ranged between 2.67 and 4.67 (M = 3.73, SD .5)" with no effect size or inferential test printed.
