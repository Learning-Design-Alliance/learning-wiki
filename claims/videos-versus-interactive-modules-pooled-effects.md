`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis by intervention type reported in the review's results (Fig. 2 forest plot). Videos and interactive modules each favour digital teaching, with the printed mean differences of 1.64 and 2.27 respectively; the review notes "a high amount of statistical heterogeneity in the results".

> "Overall video tools showed a beneficial effect with a mean difference of 1.64 (95% CI 0.22 to 3.06), while interactive modules showed a beneficial effect with a mean difference of 2.27 (95% CI 1.28 to 3.25)."
