## Evidence

### Hart 2015

Hart, Sara A.; Logan, Jessica A.R.; Thompson, Lee; Kovas, Yulia; McLoughlin, Gráinne; & Petrill, Stephen A. (2015). A latent profile analysis of math achievement, numerosity, and math anxiety in twins. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000045

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Affected-status agreement statistics from the twin design showed class 4 (n = 295) at 76% concordance (familial resemblance), classes 2 and 3 at 0% and 14% (individual-specific), and classes 1 and 5 at 33% and 46% with confidence intervals overlapping .50.

> "For individuals in class 4, the affective status agreement statistic indicated that class membership was associated with a higher chance of both twins being assigned to the same class, in that of all individuals in the class, 76% were concordant by twin pair."
