## Evidence

### Hans van der Meij 2018

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij, Tessa Voerman, Evert Duipmans. (2018). Supporting motivation, task performance and retention in video tutorials for software training. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 597–614. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9560-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA over pre-test, training, immediate and delayed post-test performance scores for 65 elementary students learning six Word formatting tasks. The main effect of time was significant; "no main effect of condition and no interaction between time and condition" was found.

> "A repeated measures ANOVA with condition as the independent variable and the regular task tests as four measurement points, showed a main effect of time, F(3,183) = 202.74, p \ 0.001."

### Hans van der Meij 2018 (2)

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij, Tessa Voerman, Evert Duipmans. (2018). Supporting motivation, task performance and retention in video tutorials for software training. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 597–614. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9560-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc tests on the same repeated measures ANOVA of performance test percentages. Scores rose from a pre-test mean of 11.7% to 79.7% at training, then declined significantly to 62.5%; immediate and delayed post-tests did not differ (p = 0.43).

> "Post hoc analyses revealed a signiﬁcant change from pre-test to training ( p \ 0.000) with scores reaching an average of 79.7% (see Table 4). There was also a signiﬁcant change from training to immediate post-test (p \ 0.001), with scores declining to 62.5%."
