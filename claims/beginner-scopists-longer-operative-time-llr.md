## Evidence

### Park Sunghae 2026

Park Sunghae, Oh Namkee, Rhu Jinsoo, Choi Gyu-Seong, Kim Jongman. (2026). Comparison of learning curve difference of laparoscopist according to controller type of flexible scope during laparoscopic liver resection. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12909-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective comparison of 161 LLRs by a single surgeon (82 expert-assisted, 79 beginner-assisted). The study reports "significantly longer in the beginner group than in the expert group" for median operation time; only a p-value is printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "Median operation time was significantly longer in the beginner group than in the expert group (178.0 [140.0–206.0] vs 139.5 [103.5–184.0] minutes, p < 0.001)."
