# Students' math scores on transformation matrices rose significantly from pre-test to post-test within both AR and non-AR workshop groups, with a larger observed rise in the AR group

## Evidence

### Zohreh Shaghaghian 2022

Zohreh Shaghaghian, Heather Burte, Dezhen Song, and Wei Yan. (2022). An Augmented Reality Application and User Study for Understanding and Learning Spatial Transformation Matrices. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2212.00110](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2212.00110)

User study comparing AR (N=29) and non-AR (N=30) workshops with pre- and post-tests; paired t-tests showed significant improvement in both groups. Table 3 reports mean improvement "with more score improvement in the AR group (29.33%) compared to the non-AR group (20.83%)."

> "The paired t-test showed that students’ math scores significantly improved in the post-sessions of AR (p < 0. 001) and non-AR (p < 0. 001)."
