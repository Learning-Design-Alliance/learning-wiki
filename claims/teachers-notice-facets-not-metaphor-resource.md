## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2018

Michael C. Wittmann. (2018). Research in the Resources Framework: Changing environments, consistent exploration. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/revperv2.5.5](https://doi.org/10.1119/revperv2.5.5)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of a large-group discussion in a Maine Physical Sciences Partnership professional development meeting, where teachers explained why students might answer that the hiker on the shortest, steepest path has the most potential energy; teachers named all typical incorrect responses, including nuances not previously described in the literature.

> "What concerned us was that the teachers had only noticed the problematic application of these “facet” resources. The more general resource, the “metaphor” resource which was the basis of those “facet” resources, was not discussed as valuable."
