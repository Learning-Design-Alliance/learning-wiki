Manuela Macedonia 2026¶

Manuela Macedonia, Lihui Hu, Brian Mathias. (2026). Why Some Gestures Work Better than Others: Toward a Model of Gesture-Supported Vocabulary Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10222-8

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review reports item-level analyses from Macedonia and Klimesch (2014) in which "nunun was the most memorable word in the corpus, accounting for 9.59% of all retrieved items across testing time points." The gesture involved dynamic whole-body movement and evoked laughter.
