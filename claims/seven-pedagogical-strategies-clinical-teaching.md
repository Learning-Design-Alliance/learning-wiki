## Evidence

### Nilsson Maria Skyvell 2010

Nilsson Maria Skyvell, Pennbrant Sandra, Pilhammar Ewa, Wenestam Claes-Göran. (2010). Pedagogical strategies used in clinical medical education: an observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Ethnographic non-participant observations and informal interviews over four months at a Swedish teaching hospital, with 27 observation occasions involving nine medical students and twelve clinical teachers, yielded seven strategies through qualitative coding of meaning units. The analysis found "Seven pedagogical strategies were found to be applied".

> "Seven pedagogical strategies were found to be applied, namely: 1) Questions and answers, 2) Lecturing, 3) Piloting, 4) Prompting, 5) Supplementing, 6) Demonstrating, and 7) Intervening."
