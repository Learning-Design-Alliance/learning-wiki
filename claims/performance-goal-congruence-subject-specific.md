# Congruence between perceived parent and teacher performance goals is associated with mathematics performance but not with language performance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Congruence in perceived parent and teacher performance goals shows a linear association with mathematics achievement, with discrepancy also significant. [→ Zhao 2025](#zhao-2025)
`q2 i?` The congruence of performance goals did not correlate with improved language performance; the linear congruence slope was nonsignificant. [→ Zhao 2025 (2)](#zhao-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Zhao 2025

Zhao, N., Dou, D., Chen, X., Chen, F., Luo, R., Zhu, X., & Xiang, G.-X. (2025). Exploring the congruence between perceived parent–teacher achievement goals and student academic outcomes: A study using polynomial regression with response surface analysis. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 603–626. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12742

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Response surface analysis for performance goals and mathematics in the 4944-student survey: "A significant a 1 with a nonsignificant a 2 suggests the effect along the line of congruence", and the significant negative a4 indicates discrepancy matters.

> "The regression results were as follows (see Table 8): a 1 = 0.07 (SE = 0.025, p = .008), a 2 = −0.03 (SE = 0.021, p = .149), a 3 = 0.08 (SE = 0.027, p = .005) and a 4 = −0.09 (SE = 0.030, p = .002)."

### Zhao 2025 (2)

Zhao, N., Dou, D., Chen, X., Chen, F., Luo, R., Zhu, X., & Xiang, G.-X. (2025). Exploring the congruence between perceived parent–teacher achievement goals and student academic outcomes: A study using polynomial regression with response surface analysis. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 603–626. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12742

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Response surface analysis for performance goals and language: a1 was nonsignificant (p = .151) while a2, a3 and a4 were significant, indicating a quadratic rather than linear congruence effect and significant incongruence effects.

> "For language performance, the results of the polynomial regression analysis were as follows: a 1 = 0.02 (SE = 0.011, p = .151), a 2 = −0.03 (SE = 0.008, p = .004), a 3 = 0.06 (SE = 0.012, p < .001) and a 4 = −0.03 (SE = 0.013, p = .030)."
