Yavuz Akpinar 2018¶

Yavuz Akpinar, Özlem Şengül. (2018). Two Modalities of the Contextualized Courseware in Three Modalities of Classroom Use. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165740

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Post hoc Bonferroni tests following the combined-knowledge ANOVA [F(3, 147)= 12,415; p=0,0001] in the quasi-experimental comparison. Both teacher-supported courseware groups "outperformed the group B" and the teacher-only group D.

"achievement of combined knowledge of Logic unit of the group A (M=3,2; SD=2,802) is significantly higher than the group B (M=,634; SD=2,527) and the group D (M=,500; SD=2,470), whereas the group C (M=2,933; SD=2,242) outperformed the group B (M=,634; SD=2,527) and the group D (M=,500; SD=2,470)"
