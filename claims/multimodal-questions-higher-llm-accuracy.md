## Evidence

### Nouri Haniyeh 2025

Nouri Haniyeh, Mahdavi Abdollah, Abedi Ali, Mohammadnia Alireza, Hamedan Mahnaz, Amanzadeh Masoud. (2025). Performance of large language models in medical licensing examinations: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.36

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis by question format across the included studies (text-only 47 subgroup entries, text-and-image 16). The review reports 78% versus 71% with P=0.03, and attributes the improvement to multimodal prompting, particularly in GPT-4.

> "When stratified by question format, studies incorporating both text- and image-based questions achieved slightly higher pooled accuracy compared with text-only studies (78% vs. 71%, P=0.03)."
