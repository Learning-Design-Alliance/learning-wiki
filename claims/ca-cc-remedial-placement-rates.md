## Evidence

### Venezia 2010

Venezia, A., Bracco, K. R., & Nodine, T. (2010). One-shot deal? Students' perceptions of assessment and course placement in California's community colleges. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566386

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The report cites a California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office (2009) survey of assessment results, reporting that "over 83 percent of incoming community college students place into remedial-level mathematics" and 72 percent into remedial-level English. These are second-hand system statistics, not data collected in this study.

> "over 83 percent of incoming community college students place into remedial-level mathematics (with 6 1 percent placing two or more levels below college-level mathematics), and 72 percent place into remedial-level English"
