### Escribano Nuria 2023

Escribano Nuria, Belliard Virginia, Baracco Bruno, Da Silva Dayana, Ceballos Laura, Fuentes M. Victoria. (2023). Rubric vs. numeric rating scale: agreement among evaluators on endodontic treatments performed by dental students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04187-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blinded assessment study in which four evaluators rated 42 randomly selected portfolios of preclinical root canal treatments with both methods; agreement measured by intraclass correlation coefficients. The study reports "inter-evaluator agreement was substantial" with the rubric versus moderate to fair with the numeric rating scale.

> "When the analytic rubric was used, inter-evaluator agreement was substantial for radiographic assessment, access preparation, shaping procedure, obturation, and overall scores. Inter-evaluator agreement ranged from moderate to fair with the numeric rating scale."
