## Evidence

### Poh Geik Tieng & Leong Kwan Eu 2013

Poh Geik Tieng & Leong Kwan Eu. (2013). Improving Students' Van Hiele Level Of Geometric Thinking Using Geometer's Sketchpad. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 2(3). www.mojet.net. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086363

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-tests for the control group (n = 15) taught by traditional method. Obtuse angles: t(14) = 5.12, p < 0.01, "The effect size is 0.65"; acute angles t(14) = 4.80, effect size 0.62; right angles t(14) = 2.87, p = .01, effect size 0.37.

> "They improved significantly after the teaching of obtuse angles using traditional approach in posttest 3 ( M = 1.93, SD = 0.26), t (14) = 5.12, p< 0.01. The effect size is 0.65."
