## Evidence

### Sørensen Jette Led 2015

Sørensen Jette Led, Navne Laura Emdal, Martin Helle Max, Ottesen Bent, Albrecthsen Charlotte Krebs, Pedersen Berit Woetmann, Kjærgaard Hanne, van der Vleuten Cees. (2015). Clarifying the learning experiences of healthcare professionals with in situ and off-site simulation-based medical education: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. [doi:10.1136/bmjopen-2015-008345](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-008345)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group study of 25 healthcare professionals recruited from a randomised ISS/OSS trial, analysed with conventional content analysis. Participants reported an initial preference for ISS: "they had a preconceived preference for participating in ISS because they believed that ISS better matched reality". The preference shifted as discussion progressed.

> "When asked about their expectations in the beginning participants predominantly responded that prior to participating in the randomised controlled trial intervention they had a preconceived preference for participating in ISS because they believed that ISS better matched reality"
