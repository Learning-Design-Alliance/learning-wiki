Analysis of MAP Growth assessment data from nearly 4.4 million students in grades 3-8 tested in fall 2020, compared with fall 2019 same-grade performance. The brief reports "5 to 10 percentile points lower" math achievement relative to pre-COVID same-grade students; no standardized effect size is printed.
