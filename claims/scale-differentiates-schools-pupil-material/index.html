Pressey 1919¶

Pressey, L. W. (1919). A Group Scale of Intelligence for Use in the First Three Grades. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072897

q2 · i? · design · r3

School-comparison validation in City B contrasting a school attended by children of professional men with one attended by factory and quarry workers' children; percentages only, no effect size printed.

"Only 24 per cent, of the children in the second school tested above the age medians of the children attending the first school; there was only one child in the second school who tested above the seventy-five percentile for children of his age in the first school."
