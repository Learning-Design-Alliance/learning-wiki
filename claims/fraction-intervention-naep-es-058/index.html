Evidence¶

Lynn S. Fuchs 2017¶

Lynn S. Fuchs, Amelia S. Malone, Robin F. Schumacher, Jessica Namkung, and Amber Wang. (2017). Fraction Intervention for Students With Mathematics Difficulties: Lessons Learned From Five Randomized Controlled Trials. Journal of Learning Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022219416677249

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Aggregate across the five RCTs on a generalized outcome measure balancing fractions magnitude and part-whole items, using 19 released NAEP items from fourth- and eighth-grade assessments. The article reports "the mean ES was 0.58".

"Here, too, intervention students significantly outperformed control students each year (see Figure 1 for ES by study year; i.e., by RCT). Across RCTs, the mean ES was 0.58."

Lynn S. Fuchs 2017 (2)¶

Lynn S. Fuchs, Amelia S. Malone, Robin F. Schumacher, Jessica Namkung, and Amber Wang. (2017). Fraction Intervention for Students With Mathematics Difficulties: Lessons Learned From Five Randomized Controlled Trials. Journal of Learning Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022219416677249

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Aggregated gap analysis on the NAEP outcome: a 0.52 SD decrease for intervention versus a 0.04 SD increase for control, an advantage of 0.56 SD in narrowing the achievement gap.

"the NAEP achievement gap narrowed markedly for intervention students (a decrease in ES of 0.52 SD units from pre- to posttest), while the gap remained approximately constant for the control group (an increase of 0.04 SD)."
