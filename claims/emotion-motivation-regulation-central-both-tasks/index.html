q2 · i? · associational · r1

T/ONA network analysis of both task networks. The article reports emotion–motivation regulation as "the code most often connected to other codes" in both tasks, with balanced links to share/compare, negotiate, and test/modify in SimSketch, and stronger share/compare links plus self-transitions in Simua.

"Nevertheless, for both networks, emotion–motivation regulation is the code most often connected to other codes, indicating its importance for both tasks. SimSketch demonstrated a balanced interaction of emotion–motivation regulation with CKC phases "share/compare," "negotiate," and "test/modify""
