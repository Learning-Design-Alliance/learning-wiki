Evidence¶

Roessingh 2006¶

Roessingh, H. (2006). BICS-CALP: An Introduction for Some, a Review for Others. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 23(2), Spring 2006. https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v23i2.57

q1 · i? · review · r1

The review attributes these figures to Moskowitz (1978), a study it describes as still widely cited, reporting around 2,500 words at school entry and about 3,000 words acquired per year. The reviewed study's design is not described.

"Moskowitz (1978), in a study that is still widely cited, puts the figure at around 2,500. The child acquires about 3,000 words a year for the first several years of school."
