# Repeated weekly viewing of instructional videos yields significantly better observed Bass technique precision than single viewing in preclinical dental students

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Dental students who viewed the videos four times demonstrated significantly better Bass technique precision (brush position, pressure, movement) than single viewers, with printed effect sizes around −0.3 to −0.49. [→ Dervisbegovic Selma 2025](#dervisbegovic-selma-2025)

## Evidence

### Dervisbegovic Selma 2025

Dervisbegovic Selma, Laky Markus, Tur Dino, Grundnig Julia, Rausch-Fan Xiaohui, Moritz Andreas, Holzinger Anita. (2025). Educational videos as a teaching approach to enhance dental students’ practical skills in preclinical courses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07807-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Practical test (N=81) scored by blinded examiners on plastic models; Mann–Whitney U tests compared the single-viewing group (N=40) with the repeated-viewing group (N=41) on six Bass technique items, all significant (p ≤ 0.004), with "effect size" values of −0.486 to −0.298 as printed in Table 1.

> "Group 1 (single video viewing) differed significantly from Group 2 (repeated video viewing). Group 2 demonstrated significantly better results in observed precision regarding practical skills in both exam parts."
