## Evidence

### Chou 2013

Chou, C. (2013). A study on the effectiveness of applying "Readers' Theater" as English remedial instruction for underachievers. Taiwan Journal of TESOL, 10(1), 77-103. http://english.moe.gov.tw/ct.asp?xItem=7773&ctNode=516&mp=1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study comparing semester grades of 25 RT-exposed and 24 control underachievers via paired- and independent-samples t-tests. The article reports "the t -value is 4.13" at the .001 level for the post-test grade comparison.

> "it is clear from the independent t -test post -test results of the two groups’ second semester grades in Table 6 that the t -value is 4.13, which reached the .001 significance level and indicates that the English learning achievement (M=126.40) of the RT -exposed experimental group students is significantly greater than that of students in the control group (M=103.25)."
