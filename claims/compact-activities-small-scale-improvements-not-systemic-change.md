Perception-based findings from interviews and site visits conducted for the district-charter collaboration grant evaluation through December 2015. Improvements in instructional quality, human capital practices, and information availability were "perceived" by respondents; systemic change was not achieved. No effect size is reported.

> "Compact collaboration activities have not yielded systemic change within the grant period but were perceived to have resulted in small-scale improvements in instructional quality and human capital practices and increased availability of information about the relative effectiveness of individual schools across all sectors."
