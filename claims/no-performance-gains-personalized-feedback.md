## Evidence

### Jae-Eun Russell 2026

Jae-Eun Russell, Anna-Marie Smith, Salim George, Adam Brummett. (2026). Impact of learning analytics-based personalized feedback: an experimental study on student behavior in a large introductory STEM course. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10728-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment comparing exam performance across feedback conditions; intervention-phase means were 69.9% (control) versus 70.6% (experimental), with no significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "There were no significant differences between groups in baseline exam performance (Exam 1) or in post-intervention exam performance (mean of Exams 2–Final; see Table 4)."

### Jae-Eun Russell 2026 (2)

Jae-Eun Russell, Anna-Marie Smith, Salim George, Adam Brummett. (2026). Impact of learning analytics-based personalized feedback: an experimental study on student behavior in a large introductory STEM course. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10728-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of D/F rates between groups; the printed test was χ2(1) = 2.19, p = 0.14, so the near-halving of failure rate was "not statistically significant" and the authors say it should be interpreted cautiously pending further research.

> "although the D and F rate of the experimental group was nearly half that of the control group (control = 4.7%, experimental = 2.5%), this difference was not statistically significant."
