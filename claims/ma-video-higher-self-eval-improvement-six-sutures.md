## Evidence

### Cheng Meng 2025

Cheng Meng, Wang Xinle, Zhang Man, Li Sainan, Geng Cuizhi. (2025). Video based on the combination of animation and modules is effective in teaching suture technologies to novices without faculty involvement: a new surgical video course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06915-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized three-group study (lottery-based assignment) of 60 preclinical medical students with no prior suture training; pre-to-post self-evaluation score differences were compared across the Module and Animation (MA), Module Video (MV), and Traditional Education (TE) groups. The article reports "significantly higher score improvements" for MA on these six techniques, with Table 2 printing p-values such as p < 0.001 for MA vs. MV on VMS.

> "In the majority of suturing techniques (SCS, VMS, HMS, CHMS, LS, and HS), Group MA exhibited significantly higher score improvements compared to the other two groups."
