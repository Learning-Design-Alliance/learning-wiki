## Evidence

### Nikendei C 2014

Nikendei C, Huber J, Stiepak J, Huhn D, Lauter J, Herzog W, Jünger J, Krautter M. (2014). Modification of Peyton's four-step approach for small group teaching – a descriptive study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-68

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive study of three skills-lab sessions on IV catheter insertion, each with three first- or second-year medical students (n = 9). Five-item self-efficacy ratings on a six-point Likert scale were collected pre and post; the article reports the change "turned out to be significant (p < .001)" with no effect size.

> "Comparison of pre (3.7 ± 1.5) and post (1.6 ± 0.5) self-efficacy ratings (1 = "I completely agree" to 6 = "I completely disagree) of participating trainees turned out to be significant (p < .001)."
