## Evidence

### Lynch 2025

Lynch, K., Gonzalez, K., Hill, H., & Merritt, R. (2025). A Meta-Analysis of the Experimental Evidence Linking Mathematics and Science Professional Development Interventions to Teacher Knowledge, Classroom Instruction, and Student Achievement. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584251335302

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 46 randomized experiments of PK-12 mathematics and science PD, using correlated and hierarchical effects models with robust variance estimation. The pooled average impact estimate — a printed effect size of "+0.52 SD" — favored treatment teachers over controls on teacher-level outcomes.

> "Compared with controls, treatment group teachers in PK–12 mathematics and science PD intervention studies demonstrated stronger performance on teacher-level outcomes (pooled average impact estimate: +0.52 SD)."
