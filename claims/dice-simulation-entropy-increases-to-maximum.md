## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the dice simulation, the generalized entropy increases monotonically, reaches its maximum at equilibrium, and remains there apart from fluctuations that shrink as system size grows. [→ Koo-Chul Lee 1999](#koo-chul-lee-1999)

## Evidence

### Koo-Chul Lee 1999

Koo-Chul Lee. (1999). How to teach statistical thermal physics in the introductory physics course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/9908046](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/9908046)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Simulation result from applet 2, which thermalizes a dice system via microcanonical MC steps and plots the entropy calculated from occupation numbers at every instance. The article reports the plotted entropy "increase monotonically" to the equilibrium maximum, with fluctuations shrinking as system size grows.

> "As the time progresses we will see the “entropy” in- crease monotonically and hit the maximum value eventu- ally and remains there “forever” apart small ﬂuctuation which tends smaller as the system size grows larger. This is the 2nd law of thermodynamics !"
