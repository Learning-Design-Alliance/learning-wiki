`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate comparisons in the cross-sectional preterm cohort found no differences for ROP, ophthalmological changes and ventilation days; the article also reports no Apgar-score differences and, in the discussion, "no association between lower family income, lower maternal schooling and poor performance in WISC or in the psychoeducational evaluations."

> "There were no differences in the frequency of changes in assessment tests at school age when compared to the presence of the variables: retinopathy of prematurity (ROP), ophthalmological changes and days in invasive mechanic ventilation."
