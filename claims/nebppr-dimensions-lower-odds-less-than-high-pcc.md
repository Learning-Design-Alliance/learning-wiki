## Evidence

### Ozturk Melek Aksut 2026

Ozturk Melek Aksut, Oner Beratiye. (2026). The Relationship Between Nurses' Ethical Behaviours for Protecting Patients' Rights and Patient‐Centred Care Competencies: A Cross‐Sectional Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70744

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 397 nurses in Turkiye; five separate binary logistic regression models (one per NEBPPR subscale) each controlling for gender, experience, and education, with PCC competency dichotomized high vs. less-than-high. The article reports "all ORs < 1, all p‐values < 0.05".

> "In all models, higher scores on ethical behaviour subscales were associated with lower odds of less‐than‐high PCC competency, indicating a protective relationship between ethical behaviour and PCC competency (all ORs < 1, all p‐values < 0.05)."
