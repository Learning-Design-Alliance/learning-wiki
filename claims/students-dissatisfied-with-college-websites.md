## Evidence

### Nodine 2012

Nodine, T., Jaeger, L., Venezia, A., and Bracco, K. R., with research support from Public Agenda. (2012). Connection by Design: Students' Perceptions of Their Community College Experiences. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED541651

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the focus group discussions, participants reported that static website information was often out of date and not easily searchable, and that sites lacked interactive formats such as education-plan tracking. As the report summarizes, "Many students are dissatisfied with college websites; they want sites to be more interactive, with online information that is dynamic, accurate, and timely."
