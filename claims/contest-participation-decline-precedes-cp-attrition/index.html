Azuad Islam Ruhan 2026¶

Azuad Islam Ruhan, Golam Mostofa Naeem, Rakibul Islam Rafi, Sherin Afrin Mim, Nazira Bani Opi, Dewan Fahad Chowdhury, and Md. Rejaul Korim Sadi. (2026). Predicting Student Attrition in Competitive Programming: A Large-Scale Study Integrating Survey Insights and Global Behavioral Logs. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28618 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.28618

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational comparison of 908 active and 908 true attrition users from a balanced Codeforces log dataset (drawn from 6,927 profiles), using an independent samples t-test. The article reports "an 83.71% reduction" in contest participation, p <0.001, calling it "the strongest single signal of impending disengagement."

"Active users participated in an average of 7.27 contests per month, compared to just 1.19 for true attrition users—an 83.71% reduction (p <0.001, independent samples t-test)."
