Evidence¶

Arnaud Stanczak 2022¶

Arnaud Stanczak, Céline Darnon, Anaïs Robert, Marie Demolliens, Camille Sanrey, Pascal Bressoux, Pascal Huguet, Céline Buchs, Fabrizio Butera, and PROFAN Consortium. (2022). Do Jigsaw classrooms improve learning outcomes? Five experiments and an internal meta-analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology, 114 (6), pp.1461-1476. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000730

q3 · i0 · causal · r3

Experiment 3A, a pre-registered within-participant crossover with 110 sixth graders over 16 one-hour sessions taught by their regular teacher, comparing jigsaw and TAU periods on the same material. Jigsaw scores were "not higher" than TAU scores.

"learning outcomes on the learning test were not higher when participants were in the jigsaw condition (M = 10.78, SD = 3.58) compared to the teaching-as-usual condition (M = 11.15, SD = 3.34), t(110) = 0.76, p = .777, d = - 0.07, 95% CI [-0.26, 0.11]"
