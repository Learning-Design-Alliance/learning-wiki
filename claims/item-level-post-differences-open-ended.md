## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Open-ended versus guided laboratory activities: Impact on students' beliefs about experimental physics. arXiv:1608.01621. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Item-by-item comparison (Fig. 2) of open-ended vs guided courses in the aggregate data set; the largest difference was Item 14 ("When doing an experiment I usually think up my own questions to investigate"), d = 0.4, with d < 0.3 for the remaining 23 items.

> "24 of the 30 items showed statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerences after instruction with students in courses using open-ended activities outscoring students in courses using only traditional guided labs in all cases."
