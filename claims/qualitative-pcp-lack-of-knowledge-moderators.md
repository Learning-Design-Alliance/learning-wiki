### Vos Julien A. M. 2023

Vos Julien A. M., Wollersheim Barbara M., Cooke Adelaide, Ee Carolyn, Chan Raymond J., Nekhlyudov Larissa. (2023). Primary care physicians’ knowledge and confidence in providing cancer survivorship care: a systematic review. Journal of Cancer Survivorship. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11764-023-01397-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of the review's qualitative studies (interviews and one focus group); the review reports "a lack of knowledge to provide survivorship care" in nine studies, plus education needs on follow-up plans and late effects.

> "Many studies described a lack of knowledge to provide survivorship care (n = 9) [41, 43–49, 51]. PCPs felt that they need to be educated regarding the follow-up plan/surveillance guidelines, including the interpretation of follow-up test results (n = 4)"

### Vos Julien A. M. 2023 (2)

The review attributes these moderator findings to other included studies; the review itself did not address these factors, so this is an untested second-hand assertion within the review.

> "While our study did not address these factors, others have found lower confidence levels related to fewer (years of) experience [26, 31–34, 38, 39] and female sex [26, 30, 33, 39]."
