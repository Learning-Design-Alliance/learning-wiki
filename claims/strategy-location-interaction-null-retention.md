Authors' interpretation of the two-way ANCOVA in Table 4, where the Strategy X Location interaction is marked NS (F = 0.889). The article attributes the similar retention to problem solving being an activity method of teaching, citing Tenebaum (1986) and Okebukola (1986).

> "In retention test, (Table 4) location was not significant. Also the interaction (strategy X location) is not significant. This shows that both urban and rural subjects in the PF, PM and PC groups retained similarly."
