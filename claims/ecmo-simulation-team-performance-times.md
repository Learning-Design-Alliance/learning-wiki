### Yousef Nadya 2022

Yousef Nadya, Moreau Romain, Soghier Lamia. (2022). Simulation in neonatal care: towards a change in traditional training? European Journal of Pediatrics 181:1429–1436. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-022-04373-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The review describes Di Nardo et al.'s study of team performance in an Italian pediatric intensive care unit after high technology simulation training for ECMO emergencies, reporting only test statistics with no effect size.

> "The study showed a decrease in oxygenator change time after failure from 5.3 to 3.9 min (P = 0.02), a decrease in the time to respond to an air embolism (P = 0.048), and an increase in behavioral scores after simulation-based training (P < 0.01)"

### Yousef Nadya 2022 (2)
