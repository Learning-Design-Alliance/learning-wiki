### Drovandi Aaron 2026

Drovandi Aaron, Sheader Elizabeth, Kingston Margaret. (2026). Integrated Team-Based Learning in a UK Undergraduate Medical Programme. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70470

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of Year 1 medical students (n = 253; 58% response) conducted via Mentimeter during Semester 2 of the first TBL rollout year. Findings show nearly three-quarters (184; 72.7%) had a 'positive' or 'very positive' experience.

> "Overall, students had positive perceptions of TBL, with nearly three‐quarters (184; 72.7%) having a ‘positive’ or ‘very positive’ experience."
