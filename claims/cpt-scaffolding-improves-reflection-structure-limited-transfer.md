### Seyed Parsa Neshaei 2026

Seyed Parsa Neshaei, Richard Lee Davis, and Tanja Käser. (2026). Moving Beyond Review: Applying Language Models to Planning and Translation in Reflection. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_6)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects analysis of Gibbs-cycle structure scores (0-6) across Pre, Tool, and Post stages in the 93-student between-subjects study. Stage effect was significant (p < 2e-16); delayed post-test gains (e.g., "2.13to2.40;p=.409") were not significant in either group.

> "the structure score increased substantially from Pre to Tool (TG:2.13to4.38; CG:2.10to4.71; both contrastsp < .0001), indicating that Pensée improved the writing structure while they used the system"
