Evidence¶

Guzel 2016¶

Hatice Guzel. (2016). The effect of brightness of lamps teaching based on the 5E model on students' academic achievement and attitudes. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.2915

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test on BLAT posttest scores in a quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with an experimental group of 29 and control group of 33 students; the article reports means of 8.31 and 5.73 and "p = 0.037 <0.05". No effect size is printed.

"Table 2 shows that there is a considerable difference between the posttest scores of the control group and the experimental group (p = 0.037 <0.05). In the post test, the experimental group was more successful than the control group."
