### Kreiter Clarence D 2013

Kreiter Clarence D. (2013). A measurement perspective on affirmative action in U.S. medical education. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v18i0.20531

The review summarizes the clinical-versus-statistical prediction literature, reporting that "statistical (actuarial/formulistic) methods based on simple statistical principles work as well or better than holistic ratings". Hanson et al. found independent information within admission files was lost with holistic ratings.
