### Kruger N 2025

Kruger N, Plinsinga M, Window P, Hayes S, Bunzli S. (2025). Comfortable knowing little about lymphoedema? A qualitative study of medical interns in Australia. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-089789

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the 12-interview dataset found all participants described knowledge gaps; most related lymphoedema to breast cancer or its treatment, and participants described gaps in "assessment, diagnosis and management of lymphoedema".

> "While all participants described gaps in their knowledge of lymphoedema, the majority felt comfortable in their understanding of the anatomy and physiology of the lymphatic system"
