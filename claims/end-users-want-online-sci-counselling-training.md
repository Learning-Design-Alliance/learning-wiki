Needs-analysis survey (n = 130) of counsellors, physiotherapists, recreation and occupational therapists, peer mentors and fitness trainers conducted as the first TEL-framework development activity; percentages are descriptive. The article also reports "lack of time (63%), lack of reimbursement (38%), and lack of resources (18%)" as top barriers.

> "Almost all participants (93%) reported that they would be interested in an online training on the best practices for SCI physical activity counselling."
