## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Impact of instructional approach on students' epistemologies about experimental physics. arXiv:1609.05758. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

3-way ANCOVA on the N = 3481 matched subsample split by instructional approach, controlling for pre-instruction score and major. In Traditional courses, adjusted post-instruction means for men were significantly higher than for women (F-test, p < 0.01); no effect size is printed.

> "For students in Traditional courses, adjusted post-instruction means for men were signiﬁcantly higher than the adjusted means for women (F-test, p < 0.01);"

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Impact of instructional approach on students' epistemologies about experimental physics. arXiv:1609.05758. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same approach-split 3-way ANCOVAs of the matched E-CLASS data. In Transformed courses, post-instruction means for men and women were the same (F-test, p = 0.4); only the test statistic is printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "however, for students in Transformed courses, post-instruction means for men and women were the same (F-test, p = 0 .4)."
