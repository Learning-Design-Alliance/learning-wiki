`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

> "Results from the analyses of math data show no significant difference overall."

### math-items-1

Dominguez, X., Vidiksis, R., Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Presser, A., Bueno, M., & Orr, J. (2023, April). Integrating science, mathematics, and engineering: Linking home and school learning for young learners. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/180

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level analyses of the math assessment in the same field study. The report states items measuring "visual spatial vocabulary and nonstandard measurement" showed significant improvement for treatment children, though total scores showed no significant difference.

> "Although no statistically significant difference was evident using total scores, a few items (measuring visual spatial vocabulary and nonstandard measurement, which are less often promoted in preschool relative to counting) showed significant improvement for children in the treatment condition, relative to children in the comparison condition."
