`q2 i?` In School B, groups equal in preliminary reasoning (494 units each) diverged in the final tests, the practised group scoring 532 units against 499, but two non-practised sections were still superior section by section. [→ W. H. Winch 1910](#w-h-winch-1910)

## Evidence

### W. H. Winch 1910

W. H. Winch. (1910). Accuracy in school children. Does improvement in numerical accuracy 'transfer'? The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Winch+accuracy+school+children

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment in a Standard II municipal girls' class (average age 10) where the practised group worked rule sums while the non-practised group did drawing. Group means moved from 22.5 to 24.2 (practised) versus 22.5 to 22.7 (non-practised). The author calls this "a reverse relationship to that found in School 'S.'"

> "In this school the practised group and the non-practised group, which each scored 494 units of correct reasoning in the preliminary problem tests, are, as in the preceding school, not alike in the final tests, for now the former scores 532 units as against 499 for the latter."

### Winch 1910

Winch, W. H. (1910). Accuracy in school children. Does improvement in numerical accuracy "transfer"? The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072557

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Matched-groups experiment in School B (1909): two equal groups, each scoring 494 preliminary reasoning units, one practised in rule sums over six sessions. In the final tests the practised group "scores 532 units as against 499 for the latter".

> "are, as in the preceding school, not alike in the final tests, for now the former scores 532 units as against 499 for the latter."
