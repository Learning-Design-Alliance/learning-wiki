Narrative synthesis (Section 3.2.1) of the scoping review's 43 included studies, comparing theorised and atheorised models. The review reports that "an explicit theoretical foundation in intervention was found to be associated with more robust outcomes," citing Social Cognitive Theory-grounded models as improving refusal self-efficacy and reduced substance use risk. No effect size is printed.
