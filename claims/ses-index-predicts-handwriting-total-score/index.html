q3 · i? · associational · r2

Mixed-model analysis of the same sample found a significant socioeconomic index effect, with the article reporting "B = 2.249" points per standard deviation increase in the index.

"The analysis also revealed a significant effect of school socioeconomic index on handwriting total score (B = 2.249, CI 95 % [0.946, 3.552], t(1355) = 3.383, p = .001) indicating that for each standard deviation increases in socioeconomic index, the handwriting total score increases by 2.249 points"
