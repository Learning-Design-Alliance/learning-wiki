## Evidence

### Lorenzo G. Rimoldini and Chandralekha Singh 2005

Lorenzo G. Rimoldini and Chandralekha Singh. (2005). Student understanding of rotational and rolling motion concepts. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Demonstration-based interview task with a paper rotor: all students predicted rotation, but per the interview data "only a few (less than 20%) could explain why". Most students spoke of forces that do not balance rather than using the concept of torque, and many believed the drag force acted completely vertically.

> "Although all of the interviewed students predicted that the paper helicopter would start to rotate, only a few (less than 20%) could explain why the helicopter, which was initially at rest, w ould start to rotate"
