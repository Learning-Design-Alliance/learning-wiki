# Perceived usefulness of activities and self-reported soft-skill proficiency differ by country, with student and teacher surveys showing different country-effect patterns

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ANOVA showed country differences in student-reported usefulness of YPT-related activities for all soft-skill types, but in teacher responses only for the ability to locate and use information; teachers, but not students, reported differences for other extracurricular activities. [→ Aneva 2022](#aneva-2022)

## Evidence

### Aneva 2022

Aneva, B., Faletič, S., Hömöstrei, M., Jenei, P., Kundracik, F., Kyuldjiev, A., Lindner, T., Němec, H., Puhr, H., & Plesch, M. (2022). Development of Soft Skills via IYPT. Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ANOVA (Value ~ Country) on student and teacher survey responses; printed student results include YPT Presentation skills F = 16.316, p = 0.000, and teacher results show YPT Ability to locate and use information F = 2.763, p = 0.048.

> "While we find country-differences in the student-reported usefulness of YPT-related activities for all types of soft skills, for responses by teachers, we observe these differences only in the case of the “Ability to locate and use information”."
