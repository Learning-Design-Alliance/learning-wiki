## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The absolute difference between resident self-assessed and faculty-assessed RAT scores improved (reduced) by 0.8 more in the control arm than the eye-tracking arm, a non-significant difference (P = 0.19) in the direction opposite to what was hypothesized. [→ Braund H 2024](#braund-h-2024)

## Evidence

### Braund H 2024

Braund H, Hall AK, Caners K, Walker M, Dagnone D, Sherbino J, Sibbald M, Wang B, Howes D, Day AG, Wu W, Szulewski A. (2024). Evaluating the Value of Eye-Tracking Augmented Debriefing in Medical Simulation—A Pilot Randomized Controlled Trial. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000825

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the RCT, residents completed Resuscitation Assessment Tool self-assessments and debriefers rated each resident; change in absolute SA-FA difference was compared between arms by linear regression controlling for site and baseline. The difference of "0.8 (95%, −0.4 to 2.0, P = 0.19)" was not significant.

> "After controlling for site and baseline, this absolute difference improved (ie, reduced) by 0.8 (95%, −0.4 to 2.0, P = 0.19) more in the control arm than the eye-tracking arm."
