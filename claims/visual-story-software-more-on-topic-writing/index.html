Evidence¶

M. Madden 2007¶

M. Madden, P.W.H. Chung and C.W. Dawson. (2007). Cartoon beyond clipart: a computer tool for storyboarding and storywriting. Loughborough University. https://dspace.lboro.ac.uk/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Small-scale formative school-based study with Year 5 children (ages 9-10); the article reports the teacher-leader judged the experimental group text contained "more on-task descriptions appropriate to retelling the story".

"the evidence suggests that the con tent of the experimental group's stories is more on-topic than the control group's"
