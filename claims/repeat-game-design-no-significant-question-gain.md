## Evidence

### Jason Paul Siko 2011

Jason Paul Siko. (2011). Homemade PowerPoint Games. http://it.coe.uga.edu/wwild/pptgames

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Independent t-test comparing percentage of Knowledge-level questions on the second unit between 14 first-time groups (mean 70.7, SD 10.1) and 16 repeat groups (mean 64.6, SD 10.5); "t(28) = 1.60; p = .12" was not significant.

> "However, the difference was not determined to be statistically significant, t(28) = 1.60; p = .12."
