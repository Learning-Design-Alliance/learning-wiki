A narrative review of summaries of the methodological literature on cluster randomized trials, plus a search for practical applications in school health research, yielded this enumerated list of six decisions. The article states the review "identified 6 key issues or decisions" spanning design, sampling, consent, and analysis.

> "The review identified 6 key issues or decisions school health researchers must address when designing, conducting, and analyzing data from a cluster randomized trial: (1) reasons to use a clustered design, (2) sample size calculation, (3) the use of matching or stratification, (4) definition of the school and student samples, (5) consent gathering, and (6) analysis methods."
