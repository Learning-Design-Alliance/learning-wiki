## Evidence

### Rotthoff Thomas 2014

Rotthoff Thomas, Ostapczuk Martin S., Kröncke Klaus D., Zimmerhofer Alexander, Decking Ulrich, Schneider Matthias, Ritz-Timme Stefanie. (2014). Criterion validity of a competency-based assessment center in medical education – a 4-year follow-up study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v19.25254

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal correlational analysis of n=21 students who completed the fourth-year communication OSCE, correlating Year 1 AC scores with OSCE average scores. The study reports "a very strong convergent association" for social-ethical competency (r=0.60) and moderate correlations for the AC average and teaching competency.

> "As to the fourth-year measures, the AC average score and teaching competency correlated moderately with the communication OSCE average score [r=0.41, p=0.03, and r=0.38, p=0.04 (one-tailed), respectively]. Social-ethical competency showed a very strong convergent association with the communication OSCE average score [r=0.60, p<0.01 (one-tailed)]."
