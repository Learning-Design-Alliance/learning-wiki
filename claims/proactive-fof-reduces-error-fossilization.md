# Preselection of grammar points in proactive FOF reduced learners' repetition and fossilization of erroneous forms

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors interpret proactive pre-selection of grammar points as preventing learners from fossilizing their own and peers' erroneous forms, unlike delayed reactive correction. [→ Naeimeh Bakshiri 2011](#naeimeh-bakshiri-2011)

## Evidence

### Naeimeh Bakshiri 2011

Naeimeh Bakshiri, Arshya Keyvanfar. (2011). Time Matters: Proactive vs. Reactive FOF. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 2, April - June 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071093

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' observational interpretation from the beginner reactive group, where delayed elaboration exposed learners to many erroneous forms that some took three to five sessions to correct; no effect size is printed for this claimed mechanism.

> "Indeed, the proactive learners of grammar less than the reactive learners repeated other learner's errors. Therefore, it can be suggested that the pre-selection of grammar points in the proactive FOF prevented learners from fossilizing their peers' as well as their own erroneous forms."
