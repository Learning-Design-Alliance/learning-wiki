Observational comparison of MAP Growth median percentile ranks between a spring 2019 pre-COVID sample and a spring 2022 COVID sample of grades 1–2 students, showing "lower spring 2022 achievement relative to spring 2019". Table 3 reports means, standard deviations, and standardized differences by grade and subject.

> "we observed lower spring 2022 achievement relative to spring 2019 for first- and second-grade students, with the differences ranging in magnitude from 6 to 7 percentile points in reading and 3 to 8 percentile points in math"
