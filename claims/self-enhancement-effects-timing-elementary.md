## Evidence

### Weidinger 2019

Weidinger, A. F., Steinmayr, R., & Spinath, B. (2019). Ability Self-Concept Formation in Elementary School: No Dimensional Comparison Effects Across Time. Developmental Psychology, 55(5), 1005–1018. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0000695

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

The 4-Months Interval RI/E model showed that children's math ability self-concepts began predicting subsequent math grades only from mid-Grade 3 (β = .09–.29, p ≤ .003), consistent with self-concepts needing to be established before they can influence achievement.

> "Positive self-enhancement effects for the 4-months intervals in math did not occur before the middle of Grade 3 (.05 ⱕ ␤ ⱕ .08, p ⱕ .268) but did occur from the middle of Grade 3 onward (.09 ⱕ ␤ ⱕ .29, p ⱕ .003"
