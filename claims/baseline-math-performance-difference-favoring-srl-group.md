### Granello 2026

Granello, F., Cuder, A., Doz, E., Pellizzoni, S., & Passolunghi, M. C. (2026). “I can do math!”: A self‐regulated learning intervention to enhance math‐related motivational factors and performance in middle school. The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96(2), 501–521. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70034

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline multilevel models with classroom random intercepts tested group differences on each pretest outcome. No significant baseline differences emerged on the motivational measures, but math performance was "higher scores in the SRL intervention group" (B = 2.836, p = .014).

> "However, a statistically significant difference emerged for math performance (B = 2.836, p = .014), with higher scores in the SRL intervention group compared to the control group at baseline."
