# At baseline, lifestyle habits correlate with cognitive control in both genders, including video games use with 2-back accuracy in females (r = −0.40 to −0.44) and sleep onset latency with Flanker MRT interference in males (r = 0.59)

## Evidence

### Dubuc Marie-Maude 2019

Dubuc Marie-Maude, Aubertin-Leheudre Mylène, Karelis Antony D. (2019). Lifestyle Habits Predict Academic Performance in High School Students: The Adolescent Student Academic Performance Longitudinal Study (ASAP). International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17010243

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Baseline Pearson's partial correlations between lifestyle habits and cognitive control (Flanker and N-back tasks) in the ASAP cohort, controlled for age, pubertal status, socioeconomic status, and ethnicity. In males, the authors print sleep onset latency with Flanker MRT interference (r = 0.59, p < 0.001) and television use on WE with Flanker MRT interference (r = 0.46, p = 0.001).

> "Video games use with 2-back accuracy (WD: r = −0.40, p = 0.001; WE: r = −0.44, p < 0.001), with 2-back MRT (WD: r = 0.29, p = 0.02; WE: r = 0.26, p = 0.03), and with d’ (WD: r = −0.25, p = 0.03)"
