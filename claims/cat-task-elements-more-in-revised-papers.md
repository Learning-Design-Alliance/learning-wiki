## Evidence

### Koops Willem JM 2012

Koops Willem JM, van der Vleuten Cees PM, de Leng Bas A, Snoeckx Luc HEH. (2012). Computer supported collaborative learning in a clerkship: an exploratory study on the relation of discussion activity and revision of critical appraisal papers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-79

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparison of CAT task elements identified in revised (N = 24) versus unrevised (N = 23) paper discussions; e.g. Study design 15 vs 0 (P < .000), Strategy of the literature search 24 vs 12 (P < .000), Relevant clinical conclusion 18 vs 6 (P < .001).

> "In the revised paper discussions, every prescribed CAT topic and corresponding CAT task element was identified, where in the unrevised CAT paper discussions, the elements of topic (2): 'Study design' and 'Study outcome' were not identified as elements under discussion."
