# Monitoring discrimination and overconfidence improve with age between kindergarten and second grade

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Second graders discriminated better between correct and incorrect answers than kindergartners (discrimination main effect of age, ηp² = .10). [→ Sophie Wacker 2024](#sophie-wacker-2024)
`q3 i2` Older children showed less overconfidence than younger children (bias index main effect of age, ηp² = .12). [→ Sophie Wacker 2024 (2)](#sophie-wacker-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Sophie Wacker 2024

Sophie Wacker, Claudia M. Roebers. (2024). Motivating children to (pre)monitor: positive effects on monitoring accuracy? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09351-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Age main effect in the ANOVA on the discrimination score: second graders (M = .83) "outperformed kindergartners (M = -.40) in terms of metacognitive discrimination", ηp² = .10.

> "revealed a significant main effect of age (F (1, 301) = 34.50, p < .001, η p 2 = .10). Second graders (M = .83, SD = 1.9) outperformed kindergartners (M = -.40, SD = 1.8) in terms of metacognitive discrimination."

### Sophie Wacker 2024 (2)

Sophie Wacker, Claudia M. Roebers. (2024). Motivating children to (pre)monitor: positive effects on monitoring accuracy? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09351-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Age main effect on the bias index in the same experiment: older children (M = .27) "show less overconfidence than younger children (M = .42)", ηp² = .12.

> "The ANOVA with age and condition as between-subject factors revealed a main effect of age (F (1, 301) = 39.77, p < .001, ηp 2 = .12). In line with our assumption, older children (M = .27, SD = .19) show less overconfidence than younger children (M = .42, SD = .20)."
