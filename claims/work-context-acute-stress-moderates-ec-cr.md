Joly Louise, Nunes de Sousa Alexandra, Bardiau Marjorie, Dory Valérie, Bayot Marie, Lenoir Anne-Laure. (2026). Impact of emotional competence on clinical reasoning: a scoping review. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-113483

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's own interpretive hypothesis from its moderator synthesis (Section 2), stated as congruent with the cognitive-load literature; it is an authors' interpretation, not a tested result, and no effect size is printed.

> "The first hypothesis supports the fact that the link between EC and CR is moderated by the level of acute stress. The higher the level of acute stress, the greater the effect of EC on CR, likely by helping individuals to manage their cognitive load."

### Joly Louise 2026 (2)

Joly Louise, Nunes de Sousa Alexandra, Bardiau Marjorie, Dory Valérie, Bayot Marie, Lenoir Anne-Laure. (2026). Impact of emotional competence on clinical reasoning: a scoping review. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-113483

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of the review's CR-development studies; the review reports an inverse correlation in students facing emergency situations, with clinical exposure a stronger predictor of performance than EC scores.

> "Poor stress regulation abilities have been inversely correlated with the ability to learn new skills, establish a differential diagnosis and determine treatment in students facing emergency situations—especially when their clinical experience is limited."
