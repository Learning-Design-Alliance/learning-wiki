Meta-analysis of three CWA studies under REM. The article reports "the average effect size is found to be 1.150 and in favor of CWA" while also stating "there is no statistical significance in the analysis" (z = 3.521, p = 0.34038).

> "Thus, the average effect size is found to be 1.150 and in favor of CWA. Moreover, the calculated z -test value is found to be z=3.521, while the value of p is 0.34038. These values reveal that there is no statistical significance in the analysis."
