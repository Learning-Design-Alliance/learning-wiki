Latent Profile Analysis combining 90 COPUS observations from this study with 133 comparable observations from two prior studies (223 total). The article reports "Four profiles of classroom activities emerged from our analysis", with entropy 0.97 indicating reliable classification.

> "Four profiles of classroom activities emerged from our analysis: (1) “Lecture,” where the instructor lectures for most of the class time (blue dots in Fig. 2b), (2) “Clickers,” where lecture is supplemented with group clicker questions"
