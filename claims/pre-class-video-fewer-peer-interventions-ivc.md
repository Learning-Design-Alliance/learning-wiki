# Students who watched the IVC video before class received fewer peer interventions during triad practice, but intervention count was not correlated with skill performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pre-class watchers received fewer interventions from triad peers during IVC practice (2.9 ± 1.8 vs 4.3 ± 2.9; p < 0.05). [→ Chan Enoch 2021](#chan-enoch-2021)
`q3 i?` The number of interventions received was not significantly correlated with performance score for either skill (BMV: tau-b = 0.141, p = 0.065; IVC: tau-b = -0.046, p = 0.512). [→ Chan Enoch 2021 (2)](#chan-enoch-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Chan Enoch 2021

Chan Enoch, Botelho Michael George, Wong Gordon Tin Chun. (2021). A flipped classroom, same-level peer-assisted learning approach to clinical skill teaching for medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0258926

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of intervention counts during IVC triad practice between pre-class watchers and non-watchers. The article reports "2.9 ± 1.8" versus "4.3 ± 2.9" with p < 0.05; no effect size is printed.

> "those who watched the video before class received less interventions from their peers during triad practice (those who watched before class: 2.9 ± 1.8; those who did not: 4.3 ± 2.9; p < 0.05)"

### Chan Enoch 2021 (2)

Chan Enoch, Botelho Michael George, Wong Gordon Tin Chun. (2021). A flipped classroom, same-level peer-assisted learning approach to clinical skill teaching for medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0258926

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kendall's tau-b correlations between interventions received and performance score, reported as null for both skills. The article concludes that "receiving less intervention from peers do not equal better skill performance"; no effect size is printed.

> "The correlation between the number of interventions received and skill performance was determined by Kendall’s tau-b, and there were no statistically significant correlations (BMV: tau-b = 0.141, p = 0.065, n = 100; IVC: tau-b = -0.046, p = 0.512, n = 107)."
