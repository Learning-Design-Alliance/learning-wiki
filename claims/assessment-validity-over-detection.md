### Qusay H. Mahmoud 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review cites cross-course and cross-national evidence that interpretations of AI use and rules vary across courses and between students and educators, making detection an unstable organizing strategy for assessment.

> "Students and educators differ in how they interpret AI use and its impact on assessment [Zastudil et al., 2023, Kizilcec et al., 2024], and students encounter different rules across courses [Keuning et al., 2024, Yabaku and Ouhbi, 2024]."
