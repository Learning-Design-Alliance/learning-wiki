## Evidence

### Rotgans 2011

Rotgans, J. I., & Schmidt, H. G. (2011). Cognitive engagement in the problem-based learning classroom. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 16(4), 465–479. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9272-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Construct validation study using confirmatory factor analysis on an exploration sample (N = 61) and a cross-validation sample (N = 312) of polytechnic students. Fit indices were Chi-square/df = .17 and .02, CFI = 1.00, RMSEA = .00, and "the four-item instrument is a reliable and valid measure". No standardized effect size is printed for this validation finding.

> "The results of the construct validation and cross-validation study suggest that the four-item instrument is a reliable and valid measure to determine students' situational cognitive engagement in the classroom."
