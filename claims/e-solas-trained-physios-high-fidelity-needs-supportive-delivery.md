## Evidence

### Hurley DA 2019

Hurley DA, Keogh A, Mc Ardle D, Hall AM, Richmond H, Guerin S, Magdalinski T, Matthews J. (2019). Evaluation of an E-Learning Training Program to Support Implementation of a Group-Based, Theory-Driven, Self-Management Intervention For Osteoarthritis and Low-Back Pain: Pre-Post Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/11123

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Behavior-level evaluation: audio recordings of one class per physiotherapist coded by blinded expert raters, plus physiotherapist-completed checklists. The article reports "The total mean %fidelity score (93.5%; SD 4.9%)" and the HCCQ/CCBS medians quoted here; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Physiotherapists delivered SOLAS in a needs supportive manner consistent with a self-determination theory-based communication style (HCCQ: median 5.2, interquartile range 1.3, min-max 3.7-5.8; CCBS: median 6.6, interquartile range 1.0, min-max 5.6-7.0; Table 4)."
