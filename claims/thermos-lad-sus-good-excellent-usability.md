`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

A survey of 42 participants from various study programs completed the ten-item SUS embedded in the adjusted LAD. The score "equals 78.25%, interpretable as good–excellent usability"; item 1 on frequent use scored relatively low (M = 3.00).

> "Usability reassessment with the SUS resulted in a score of M = 4.13 and SD = 0.46 . This score equals 78.25%, interpretable as good–excellent usability (Bangor et al., 2009)."
