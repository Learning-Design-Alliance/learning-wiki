Evidence¶

Valdés Aguirre 2016¶

Valdés Aguirre, B., Ramírez Uresti, J. A., & du Boulay, B. (2016). An Analysis of Student Model Portability. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0113-0

q2 · i? · design · r2

This is the article's analysis of its own graded survey of student models from AIED 2013, EDM 2014 and ITS 2014, presented with a histogram of models per portability-restriction class (Fig. 2). The authors state that "The fact that 53 % of the models have low or mid portability restrictions can be interpreted as half the models show promise to be ported".

"We observe that 34 % of the classified models have low restrictions, almost 19 % of them have medium restrictions, and 40 % the SM (24 % and 16 % respectively) are hard or very hard to port."
