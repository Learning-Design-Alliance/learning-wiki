## Evidence

### Beres Adam 2026

Beres Adam, Ramagiri Lahari, Zhou Jonathan, Prats Michael I, Bahner David. (2026). Transesophageal Echocardiography – Student Early Engagement in Didactic Sonography (TEE-SEEDS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09600-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Phase 1 paired t-test analysis of 10 second-year students' confidence surveys across four categories. All increases were significant (e.g., TEE p < 0.0001), with the largest relative gain in TEE confidence.

> "second-year students significantly increased their confidence across all categories, including a 16.9% increase in cardiac anatomy 6.5/10 (SD = 1.43) to 7.6/10 (SD = 1.35), a 29.8% increase in general ultrasound from 4.7/10 (SD = 1.25) to 6.0/10 (SD = 1.10), a 47.6% increase in cardiac ultrasound from 4.2/10 (SD = 1.55) to 6.1/10 (SD = 1.23), and a 400% increase in TEE from 1.3/10 (SD = 0.82) to 6.4/10 (SD = 2.12)."

### Beres Adam 2026 (2)

Beres Adam, Ramagiri Lahari, Zhou Jonathan, Prats Michael I, Bahner David. (2026). Transesophageal Echocardiography – Student Early Engagement in Didactic Sonography (TEE-SEEDS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09600-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Phase 1 paired t-test analysis of 7 fourth-year students' confidence surveys. All four categories showed significant increases, with the largest relative gain in TEE confidence (446%, p < 0.0001).

> "Fourth-year students reported even greater significant rises in confidence for each category, including a 37.7% increase in cardiac anatomy from 6.1/10 (SD = 1.46) to 8.4/10 (SD = 0.53), a 40.8% increase in general ultrasound from 4.9/10 (SD = 1.57) to 6.9/10 (SD = 0.69), a 65.0% increase in cardiac ultrasound from 4.0/10 (SD = 0.82) to 6.6/10 (SD = 1.51), and a 446% increase in TEE from 1.3/10 (SD = 0.49) to 7.1/10 (SD = 1.21)."

### Beres Adam 2026 (3)

Beres Adam, Ramagiri Lahari, Zhou Jonathan, Prats Michael I, Bahner David. (2026). Transesophageal Echocardiography – Student Early Engagement in Didactic Sonography (TEE-SEEDS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09600-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Phase 2 paired t-test analysis of 42 first-year medical students' confidence surveys. All four categories showed significant increases, with the greatest growth in TEE confidence (252.9%, p < 0.0001).

> "first-year students showed a significant increase in confidence in cardiac anatomy (20.0%), general ultrasound (34.6%), cardiac ultrasound (51.1%), and TEE (252.9%)."
