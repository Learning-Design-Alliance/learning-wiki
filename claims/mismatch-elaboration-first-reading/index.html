Fan 2022¶

Fan, Y., van der Graaf, J., Lim, L., Raković, M., Singh, S., Kilgour, J., Moore, J., Molenaar, I., Bannert, M., & Gašević, D. (2022). Towards investigating the validity of measurement of self-regulated learning based on trace data. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09291-1

q3 · i? · design · r2

Analysis of mismatch durations in the first alignment between theory-driven trace-based SRL processes and think aloud codes. The mismatch between Elaboration/Organisation and First-reading accounted for the largest share of mismatch time, helping identify invalid or over-interpretations in the process library.

"A total of 41.69% of all mismatch time was caused by the mismatch between HC.E/O and LC.F."
