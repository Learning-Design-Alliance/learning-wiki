NWEA 2025¶

NWEA. (2025). Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Content Guide. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/spanish-map-reading-fluency-content-guide/

q2 · i? · design · r1

A narrative research-synthesis statement in the Test Design chapter, attributed by the guide to the transparent orthography of Spanish with decoding developing more rapidly than in opaque orthographies (citing Tapia, 2016). No effect size is printed.

"While both decoding and language comprehension play a role in Spanish as well, the better predictor of passage comprehension is not decoding accuracy but decoding rate, or ﬂuency."
