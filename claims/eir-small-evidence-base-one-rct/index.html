Evidence¶

Early Intervention in Reading® 2008¶

Early Intervention in Reading®. (2008). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.earlyinterventioninreading.com

q2 · i? · review · r3

The WWC report's research section states that "Seven studies reviewed by the WWC investigated the effects of EIR®" and only one met evidence standards. The remaining six did not meet standards or eligibility screens, mostly because effects could not be attributed solely to the intervention.

"Seven studies reviewed by the WWC investigated the effects of EIR®. One study (Taylor, Frye, Short, & Shearer, 1991) is a randomized controlled trial that meets WWC evidence standards."
