Brundage, M. J., Maries, A., & Singh, C. (2023). Using the Energy and Momentum Conceptual Survey to investigate progression in student understanding from introductory to advanced levels. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020132)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional pre/post analysis of EMCS post-test response distributions in a traditionally taught upper-level classical mechanics course (N=42 post-test) compared with introductory students (N=336 post-test). The Results section reports "13 questions on which the performance of the upper-level students is below two-thirds correct" and states that on all questions where introductory students scored below 50% post-test, upper-level students scored below two-thirds.
