Evidence¶

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte 2020¶

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte. (2020). The Effect of School Closures on Standardised Student Test Outcomes. KU Leuven Faculty of Economics and Business Discussion Paper Series DPS20.17. https://www.kuleuven.be/dps20.17

q2 · i1 · causal · r1

Difference-in-differences estimation for science scores on the 2019-2020 and 2017-2020 sub-samples of Flemish schools; the printed effect sizes span 0.22 to 0.33 standard deviations (up to d = 0.33), with a 0.32 SD decrease in the fully saturated 2017-2020 specification.

"In all sub-samples and speciﬁcations, the COVID-19 coefﬁcient is negative and statistically signiﬁcant, ranging from a decrease of 0.22 standard deviations to a decrease of 0.33 standard deviations."
