Evidence¶

Schildkraut 2019¶

Schildkraut, J., & Grogan, K. (2019). Are metal detectors effective at making schools safer? San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/are-metal-detectors-effective-at-making-schools-safer

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief reports NYC data: "57 percent were found without using scanning devices" of weapons confiscated in a single school year, suggesting most finds came from manual searches.

"New York City schools, for example, reported that of the weapons they confiscated in a single school year, 57 percent were found without using scanning devices"

Schildkraut 2019 (2)¶

Schildkraut, J., & Grogan, K. (2019). Are metal detectors effective at making schools safer? San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/are-metal-detectors-effective-at-making-schools-safer

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief cites NYC scanning data showing an extremely low yield: "for every 23,034 students scanned in the five boroughs, just one dangerous item ... was found." The brief cautions these data cannot show any deterrent effect.

"other data indicate that for every 23,034 students scanned in the five boroughs, just one dangerous item (including, but not limited to, firearms) was found"
