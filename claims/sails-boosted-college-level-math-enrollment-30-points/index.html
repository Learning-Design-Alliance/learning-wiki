q3 · i? · causal · r2

Difference-in-difference analysis of first-year community college students under the SAILS waiver. Enrollment in college-level math rose "by nearly 30 percentage points, with nearly half of new enrollees passing the college‐level course." No standardized effect size is printed.

"Using a difference‐in‐difference design, we find that completing SAILS boosted enrollment in college‐level math among first‐year community college students by nearly 30 percentage points, with nearly half of new enrollees passing the college‐level course."
