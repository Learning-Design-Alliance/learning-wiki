### Schwab Jessica F. 2016

Schwab Jessica F., Lew-Williams Casey. (2016). Language learning, socioeconomic status, and child-directed speech. Wiley interdisciplinary reviews. Cognitive science. https://doi.org/10.1002/wcs.1393

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports studies showing younger infants (4–6 months) prefer CDS while those older than 7 months often do not, with preference re-emerging after 14 months, and one study showing no word-learning advantage of CDS at 27 months. No effect sizes are printed.

> "One study showed that 27-month-old children were equally able to learn new words in both CDS and ADS."
