### Delbene Lara 2026

`q3 · i3` · `review · r2`

Randomized controlled trial (Eweida et al. 2025, Egypt) of a Video-Based Climate Change Program of five themed sessions delivered via 30–45 minute videos with interactive discussions, in 140 undergraduate nursing students (70 per group). The review reports large effects including "d ≈ 1.40–1.45" for climate change perceptions; the review classifies this study as high quality.

> "Compared with control, the intervention significantly improved climate change perceptions (total CCP: large effects, d ≈ 1.40–1.45 at T1–T2; very large gains in “causes” d ≈ 2.17 and “valence of consequences” d ≈ 1.70–1.77) and significantly increased environmental self‐efficacy (ESE: d ≈ 1.44 at T1; d ≈ 1.60 at T2). It also significantly reduced total climate anxiety (d ≈ 0.96 at T1; d ≈ 0.94 at T2) and cognitive‐impairment anxiety (d ≈ 1.31–1.40), while functional‐impairment anxiety did not change significantly."
