## Evidence

### Martín-Coca A 2025

Martín-Coca A, Martín Herrero JA. (2025). Training program on motivational interviewing for prison professionals. Revista Española de Sanidad Penitenciaria. https://doi.org/10.18176/resp.00108

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures analysis in the quasi-experimental study of 25 prison professionals shows compatible adherence rose from 3.34 to 3.94 then fell to 3.12; the printed effect size for the initial increase was η² = 0.267 (rendered "h2 = 0.267" in the English text) and the article reports "consolidation was not maintained in the final phase".

> "There was a significant increase between moment 1 and moment 2 (F = 8.721, p <0.001, h2 = 0.267, observed power = 0.961), followed by a drop in moment 3. This pattern was also confirmed by the Friedman test (c2 = 9.939, p = 0.007)"
