# Conditional process analysis shows HF-HRV moderates the mediating role of state anxiety, indicating mechanistic heterogeneity

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` HF-HRV significantly moderates the indirect effect of math anxiety on math performance via state anxiety (Index of Moderated Mediation = 0.151, 95% CI [0.077, 0.284]); state anxiety explains ~94% of the link at low HF-HRV but only ~14% at high HF-HRV. [→ Daker 2023](#daker-2023)

## Evidence

### Daker 2023

Daker, R.J., Gattas, S.U., Necka, E.A., Green, A.E. & Lyons, I.M. (2023). Does anxiety explain why math-anxious people underperform in math? npj Science of Learning, 8:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00156-z

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Conditional process analysis (pruned model, b-path moderation removed after preliminary analysis showed non-significant b-path moderation β = 0.142, p = 0.163). At low HF-HRV, indirect effect accounts for 94% of total effect; at high HF-HRV, only 14% (direct effect 86%).

> "HF-HRV significantly moderated not just the a path, but also the indirect effect ( ab path: Index of moderated mediation = 0.151, 95% CI [0.077, 0.284])."
