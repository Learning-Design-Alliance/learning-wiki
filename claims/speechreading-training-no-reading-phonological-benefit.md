## Evidence

### Pimperton 2019

Pimperton, H., Kyle, F., Hulme, C., Harris, M., Beedie, I., Ralph-Lewis, A., Worster, E., Rees, R., Donlan, C., & MacSweeney, M. (2019). Computerized Speechreading Training for Deaf Children: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research. https://doi.org/10.1044/2019_JSLHR-H-19-0073

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Intention-to-treat ANCOVAs across the language and literacy battery in the RCT reported that "effects of training on phonological awareness and word reading were all small and nonsignificant" (ds = 0.08–0.30), and no effects emerged for letter-sound knowledge or number skills (ds = −0.06 to 0.12). Reading trajectories were highly stable relative to the sample.

> "The effects of training on phonological awareness and word reading were all small and nonsignificant (ds = 0.08–0.30). Finally, there was no evidence of an effect of intervention group on Letter–Sound Knowledge (T2/T3) or Number Skills (T2/T3/T4) (ds = −0.06 to 0.12)."
