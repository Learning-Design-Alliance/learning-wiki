## Evidence

### Ruchay Zino 2024

Ruchay Zino, Julian Pape, Julia-Sophie Cordt, Carolina Kerres, Sebastian Siehl, Karina Jansone, Johannes Ackermann, Veronika Guenther, Liselotte Mettler, Leila Allahqoli, Nicolai Maass, Frauke Nees, Ibrahim Alkatout. (2024). Does an aptitude for surgery exist and can we predict it? An experimental study. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000001577

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of the student cohort (n=237) in the randomized crossover training trial modeled psychomotor skills, personality and motivation as latent predictors of training performance; the article reports "motivation and personality contributing most to the students' surgical training performance" with adjusted R2=0.082.

> "The latent factors of motivation (explained variance, 8.4%; R2=0.109; R2 corrected=0.84), personality (explained variance, 4.5%; R2=0.074; R2 corrected = 0.045), and psychomotor skills (explained variance, 1.1%; R2=0.042; R2 corrected=0.011) are weighted differently, with motivation and personality contributing most to the students' surgical training performance."
