Evidence¶

Panadero 2025¶

Panadero, E., Delgado, P., Zamorano, D., Pinedo, L., Fernández-Ortube, A., & Barrenetxea-Mínguez, L. (2025). Putting excellence first: How rubric performance level order and feedback type influence students' reading patterns and task performance. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102168

q3 · i? · causal · r2

GLM analysis of PL4-picture gaze transition changes across feedback conditions in the RCT. The study found "only students who received rubric-based feedback significantly increased their transitions from PL4 to the picture"; the effect was not moderated by rubric PL order.

"only students who received rubric-based feedback significantly increased their transitions from PL4 to the picture (t = 2.19, p = .03), whereas no such increase was observed in the other feedback conditions"

Panadero 2025 (2)¶

Panadero, E., Delgado, P., Zamorano, D., Pinedo, L., Fernández-Ortube, A., & Barrenetxea-Mínguez, L. (2025). Putting excellence first: How rubric performance level order and feedback type influence students' reading patterns and task performance. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102168

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Among all Phase 4 gaze measures, "only the number of PL4-picture transitions significantly predicted students' performance in written analysis 2" after controlling for University Entry scores. Feedback type and its interaction with order did not affect visits or fixation time on PL4.

"only the number of PL4-picture transitions significantly predicted students' performance in written analysis 2 (t = 2.01, p = .049), even after controlling for University Entry scores"
