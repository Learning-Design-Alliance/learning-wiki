## Evidence

### Molitor Vincent 2026

Molitor Vincent, Seiters Johanna Christina, Hoch Jonas, Thürmann Petra, Holle Bernhard, Vollmar Horst Christian, Palm Rebecca. (2026). Improving delirium knowledge and recognition confidence in nursing homes through an e-learning program: a pre–post study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09297-2

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory subgroup analyses in the same 80-nurse pilot, reported in Table 2 by management function, direct care involvement, and previous delirium training. The article states "a significant p-value < 0.05 was found in all the subgroups" with all effect sizes above r = 0.50. Subgroup sizes were small (e.g., n = 17 with prior training).

> "Overall, a significant p-value < 0.05 was found in all the subgroups, and the effect sizes were all above r = 0.50. This indicates a strong effect size. Subgroups with low median values in the pretest tend to show the greatest increase compared those with higher baseline values."
