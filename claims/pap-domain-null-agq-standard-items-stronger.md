## Evidence

### Van Yperen NW 2014

Van Yperen NW, Blaga M, Postmes T (2014). A Meta-Analysis of Self-Reported Achievement Goals and Nonself-Report Performance across Three Achievement Domains (Work, Sports, and Education). PLoS ONE 9(4): e93594. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0093594

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Domain moderator analysis for PAp goals (Table 4): education r = .10, work r = .13, sports r = .15, with no significant between-class effect.

> "Table 4 shows that the overall positive correlation between PAp goals and performance was not qualified by achievement domain."

### Van Yperen NW 2014 (2)

Van Yperen NW, Blaga M, Postmes T (2014). A Meta-Analysis of Self-Reported Achievement Goals and Nonself-Report Performance across Three Achievement Domains (Work, Sports, and Education). PLoS ONE 9(4): e93594. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0093594

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

AG measure moderator analysis for PAp goals (Table 4). The article reports higher correlations for AGQ-4 (r = .19) and AGQ-S (r = .18) versus measures containing affect or self-presentation items.

> "follow-up contrasts analyses revealed significantly higher correlations between PAp goals and performance in studies that used the AGQ-4 (primarily in the classroom) and the AGQ-S (on the sports field). These PAp subscales only comprise goal-relevant items that refer explicitly to other-referenced standards of competence"
