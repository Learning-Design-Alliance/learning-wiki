## Evidence

### Altani 2019

Altani, A., Protopapas, A., Katopodi, K., & Georgiou, G. K. (2019). From Individual Word Recognition to Word List and Text Reading Fluency. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000359

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis across grades (Table 4) in 710 children; z-transformed comparisons showed discrete word reading correlated significantly less with serial word reading and text reading in Grades 3 and 5 than Grade 1. Grade 1 r values: Greek .84, English .78; Grade 5: Greek .56, English .49.

> "discrete word reading and serial word reading correlated strongly among Grade 1 children in both languages (Greek: r = .84; English: r = .78), but only moderately among Grade 5 children (Greek:  r = .56; English: r = .49; down by about .3 from Grade 1 in both languages)."
