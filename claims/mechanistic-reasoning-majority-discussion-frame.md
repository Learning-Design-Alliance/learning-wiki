Physics Education Research Conference proceedings record. https://www.compadre.org/PER/ [doi:10.1063/1.2820949](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2820949)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Summary of coded mechanistic reasoning across all 6 video clips (Table 2 of the article), showing the distribution of codes across the four behavior modes plus mixed modes. The article reports 53%, 18%, and 15% for discussion, worksheet, and receptive-to-TA frames respectively.

> "Notably, the majority of mechanistic reasoning occurred during the “animated discussion” frame (53%).  A significant amount of mechanistic reasoning also occurred during the “completing the worksheet” frame (18%), and the “receptive to TA” frame (15%)."
