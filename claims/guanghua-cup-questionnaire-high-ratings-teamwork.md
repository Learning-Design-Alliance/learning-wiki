### Li Xiaolan 2021

The national dental undergraduate clinical skills competition known as the Guanghua Cup: an innovative and effective program that promoted undergraduate dental education in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02841-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire survey of referees, team leaders, participating students, and volunteers from the 2nd and 3rd Cups, rated on a 10-point scale. Collegiality and teamwork (Q5) scored 9.10 ± 1.27 overall; improving clinical skills (Q3) scored 9.12 ± 1.20; overall evaluation (Q12) scored 8.93 ± 1.10.

> "In addition, 338 valid questionnaires were collected, for a response rate of 87.79 %. Overall, the participants spoke highly of the Guanghua Cup."
