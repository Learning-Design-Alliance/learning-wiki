## Evidence

### Ginzburg Denise M. 2012

Ginzburg Denise M., Bohn Christiane, Höfling Volkmar, Weck Florian, Clark David M., Stangier Ulrich. (2012). Treatment specific competence predicts outcome in cognitive therapy for social anxiety disorder. Behaviour Research and Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2012.09.001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the HLM models with all three predictors entered, adherence and patient difficulty were non-significant for both outcomes; for LSASres their β values were .09 (p = .59) and .24 (p = .20). No statistically significant difference was detected for either predictor.

> "β values relating adherence and patient difficulty to CGI-I were .02 (p = .85) and .15 (p = .41) respectively."
