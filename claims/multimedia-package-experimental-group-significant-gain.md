## Evidence

### G. Singaravelu 2014

G. Singaravelu. (2014). Efficacy of Multimedia Package in Communicative Skill in English. i-manager's Journal on English Language Teaching, Vol. 4 No. 4, October - December. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068384

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study; Table 3 reports the experimental group (n = 38) moving from a pre-test mean of 6.81 (SD 3.71) to a post-test mean of 15.62 (SD 4.08), with t = 8.42 "Significant at 0.05 level".

> "The calculated value is (8.42) greater than Table 3 value (2.00). Hence null hypothesis is rejected at 0.05 level. It shows that there is a significant difference in achievement mean score between the pre-test of Experimental group and the post-test of Experimental group."
