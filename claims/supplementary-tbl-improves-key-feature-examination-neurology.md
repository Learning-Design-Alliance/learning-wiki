## Evidence

### Meike Jost 2017

Meike Jost, Peter Brüstle, Marianne Giesler, Michel Rijntjes, Jochen Brich. (2017). Effects of additional team-based learning on students’ clinical reasoning skills: a pilot study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-017-2614-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Static-group comparison in one neurology course: 11 voluntary TBL participants versus 15 non-TBL students took a formative key-feature problem examination of 12 problems (47 key features). Means were "28.0 points" versus "24.9 points", significant at "p = 0.026" with Cohen's d = 0.84; examination reliability Cronbach's α was 0.63.

> "The 11 TBL-class-participants scored a mean of 28.0 points. The 15 students of the control group scored a mean of 24.9 points (Table 1). This difference was statistically significant (p = 0.026) (Table 1). The calculated effect size (Cohen’s d) was 0.84."
