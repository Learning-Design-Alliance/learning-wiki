Evidence¶

Doshmangir Leila 2024¶

Doshmangir Leila, Alipouri Sakha Minoo, Mostafavi Hakimeh, Kabiri Neda, Ghaffarifar Saeideh, Takian Amirhossein. (2024). Essential core competencies for health policy graduates: a multi-method consensus type study. Health Research Policy and Systems. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12961-024-01221-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative study using heterogeneous and snowball sampling of health policy experts, students and stakeholders, mostly in Iran plus participants in other countries interviewed via Google Meet. Interviews averaged approximately 50 minutes; qualitative content analysis by two authors identified the competencies, with data saturation reached.

"In phase 2, six core competencies were identified as being essential for graduates in health policy, namely, research, education, policy analysis, decision-making, globalization and communication."
