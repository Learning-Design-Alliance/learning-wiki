The review's synthesis of the 13 adaptive-learning studies, many adopting a three-component model (learner, content, instructional models) in which algorithms translate performance, interaction, self-report, and motivational data into instructional adaptations. Positive effects are reported as the reviewed studies' findings, attributed by the review.

"Adaptive learning appeared in 13 studies and was consistently described as the primary mechanism through which personalization is operationalized, dynamically adjusting task difficulty, learning sequences, feedback, and pacing based on learner data."
