# Student reports of safety did not improve faster in Culture of Calm schools than in other similar high schools

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Between 2009 and 2011, student perceptions of Safety improved at the same rate in CoC and non-CoC schools; the difference in change was not statistically significant. [→ Rachel Levenstein 2011](#rachel-levenstein-2011)

## Evidence

### Rachel Levenstein 2011

Rachel Levenstein, Sue Sporte, Elaine Allensworth. (2011). Findings from an Investigation into the Culture of Calm Initiative. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/findings-investigation-culture-calm-initiative

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

HLM analysis of student Safety measures from the My Voice, My School survey (2005-2011) found both groups improved "but at the same rate"; the student Safety measure "did not change significantly between 2009 and 2011 for CoC schools compared to non-CoC schools." No effect size is printed.

> "Between 2009 and 2011, both CoC and non-CoC schools improved in Safety—but at the same rate. See Figure 1 for the trends since 2005, controlling for the variables specified above."
