# Relational-domain programs are argued to be workable only under highly individualized instruction matched to learners' concrete-abstract developmental stage

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Because students differ in position on the concrete-abstract continuum, the paper argues different experiences are warranted, and educational programs incorporating the relational domain would be workable only under highly individualized instruction. [→ Brandhorst 1976](#brandhorst-1976)

## Evidence

### Brandhorst 1976

Brandhorst, Allan R. (1976). Toward a Taxonomy of Educational Objectives in the Relational Domain. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED134505

`q1 · i?`

Theoretical argument in the individualized-learning section, applying Harvey's developmental stages: stage one individuals may need social- and interdependent-orientation training instead; stage three individuals would need conceptualization of leadership under the interdependent orientation and evaluation of self and group situations. No empirical data are reported.
