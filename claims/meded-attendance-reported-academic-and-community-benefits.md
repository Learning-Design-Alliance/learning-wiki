Descriptive analysis of an anonymous Qualtrics survey of 66 students (19 pre-clinical years 1–2, 47 clinical years 3–6) who attended MedED events in 2022-23. Likert-type responses summarised in Fig. 1; free-text benefits led with "Knowledge" (12/19 pre-clinical, 39/47 clinical).

> "Most students strongly agreed with statements that attending MedEd lectures improved their medical knowledge and skills, confidence as a medical student and future doctor, and motivation, as well as helping them feel more academically supported and part of a community"
