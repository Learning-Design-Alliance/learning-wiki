Descriptive observation of both genAI groups' audio recordings found most questions were closed-ended, often in jargon or formal medical tone; a Group 1 student asked the tutor "Can we give him advice or is it [the AI] just, like, a question base?" Group 1 was more open and experimental than Group 2.

> "In general, students seemed more focused on how to gather history information efficiently than on approximating interaction with a real human."
