### Al-Zahrani Sami H. MD 2015

Al-Zahrani Sami H. MD, Al-Khail Bahaa A. Aba MD, DPH. (2015). Resident physician's knowledge and attitudes toward biostatistics and research methods concepts. Saudi Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.15537/smj.2015.10.11842

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey comparison of knowledge scores by journal-reading practice: readers scored lower than non-readers (p=0.001) with attitudes not significantly different (p=0.22). The direction is counterintuitive and no effect size is printed.

> "Physicians who read medical journals scored lower than those who never read journals (p=0.001), and their attitudes toward biostatistics were not significantly different from those of their colleagues who reported never reading medical journals (p=0.22)."
