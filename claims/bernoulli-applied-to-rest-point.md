## Evidence

### Alvaro Suarez 2017

Alvaro Suarez, Sandra Kahan, Genaro Zavala, and Arturo C. Marti. (2017). Student Conceptual Difficulties in Hydrodynamics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Error categorization of written exam solutions from the May 2015 midterm, where students determined the force on a plug closing a pipe. "20% of the solutions presented the aforementioned error" of applying Bernoulli's equation between a moving-fluid point and a point at rest; the authors state this error was the most commonly detected in all the tests.

> "20% of the solutions presented the aforementioned error: to determine the pressure inside the plug, students applied Bernoulli's equation between a point at the inlet of pipe 1 and a point in the middle of the inner side of the plug."
