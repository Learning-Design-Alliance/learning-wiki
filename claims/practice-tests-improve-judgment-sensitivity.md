`q2 · i?`

Hierarchical regression in Study 1 (N = 201, no feedback) predicting posttest sensitivity; Table 3 shows step 2 ΔR² = .03*, β = .17*. In Study 2 (with feedback), sensitivity was also significantly predicted (β = .24*, ΔR² = .06*).
