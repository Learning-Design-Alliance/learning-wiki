Comparison of pacing trajectories between top-quartile and bottom-quartile schools on the professional community index (Figure 10). The difference does not significantly affect grades K-4 but students in succeeding grades experience slower pacing in weak-community schools. Figure 11 confirms the gap within high-poverty schools only.

"students who attend an elementary school where faculty enjoy strong professional community tend to receive instruction closely aligned with the ITBS math content standards (i.e., consistently at or above the ITBS grade-level mastery line)."
