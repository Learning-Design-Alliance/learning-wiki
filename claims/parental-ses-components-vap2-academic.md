## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Father's education (η2 = 0.18) and mother's income (η2 = 0.21) are associated with VAP2 participation, and mother's education and income relate to academic performance across subjects. [→ Deer Genman 2024](#deer-genman-2024)

## Evidence

### Deer Genman 2024

Deer Genman, Wu Hao, Zhang Li, Tadesse Endale, Khalid Sabika, Duan Congyu, Tian Wang, Gao Chunhai. (2024). Effect of out-of-school visual art activities on academic performance. The mediating role of socioeconomic status. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0298901

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Two-way MANOVA with Tukey post hoc tests on the Guizhou sample. Father's education (η2 = 0.18) and mother's income (η2 = 0.21) predicted VAP2; mother's education and income related to Chinese, English, and math performance (e.g., η2 = 0.28 for Chinese).

> "the father's educational level (F (4) = 12.78, P< .05, η2 = 0.18) and mother's monthly income (F (4) = 12.98, P< .01, η2 = 0.21) are associated with children participating in VAP2."
