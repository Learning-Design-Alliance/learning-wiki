Descriptive statistics from the survey of 3003 pre-service teachers across 12 Chinese provinces, reported in the results section. The article reports "innovation practice (M = 3.68, SD = 0.72) was relatively lower" than the other two subdimensions; no effect size is printed for this descriptive pattern.

> "Pre-service teachers' innovative competence generally fell in the upper-middle range (M = 3.79, SD = 0.60). Among its subdimensions, innovation willingness (M = 3.87, SD = 0.62) and innovation readiness (M = 3.83, SD = 0.64) were relatively higher, while innovation practice (M = 3.68, SD = 0.72) was relatively lower."
