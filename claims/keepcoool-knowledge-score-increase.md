## Evidence

### Thielmann Anika 2024

Thielmann Anika, Schmitz Marie-Therese, Welchowski Thomas, Weltermann Birgitta. (2024). Effectiveness of the online-eLearning program KeepCoool at improving the vaccine cold chain in general practices. PLoS ONE 19(4): e0301847. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0301847

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Knowledge test results reported for participants of the KeepCoool study, using the same 11-item questionnaire before and after the eLearning, as published in a companion analysis cited by this article.

> "Average knowledge improved to 9.8 ± 1.2 with an increase of 4.2 points (p<0.001). In physicians, the mean knowledge score at follow-up increased by 4.6 points in physicians (p<0.001) and by 4.0 points in practice assistants (p < 0.001)"
