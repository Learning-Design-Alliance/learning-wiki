### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2017

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2017). Students’ views about the nature of experimental physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020110](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020110)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparisons of FY versus BFY score distributions across the national data set; the article reports BFY students scored higher overall and that "24 of the 30 E-CLASS items showed a sta- tistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence."

> "Additionally, 24 of the 30 E-CLASS items showed a sta- tistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence between the score distri- butions from students in diﬀerent level courses."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2017 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2017). Students’ views about the nature of experimental physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020110](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020110)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-level comparison across course levels; Q24 was the single item where "FY students scored higher both statistically and practically (d > 0.1)". The authors attribute lower BFY scores to harder upper-level experiments and attrition-driven self-selection.

> "Of these items, FY students scored higher both statistically and practically (d > 0.1) in only one case — Q24, “Nearly all students are capable of doing physics experiments if they work at it.”"
