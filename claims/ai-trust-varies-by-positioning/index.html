Hillary Greene Nolan 2024¶

Hillary Greene Nolan, Ph.D, Merijke Coenraad, Ph.D, Viki Young, Ph.D. (2024). Teaching Partner, Grading Assistant, Substitute Teacher: Three Ways Teachers Positioned an Artificial Intelligence Tool in Writing Instruction. Digital Promise, Center for Inclusive Innovation. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/4f2d86a3-9b66-4538-a5ad-977bb8fbacf7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interview finding on grading practices. Partner teachers showed skepticism, e.g., "I've noticed the paraphrasers don't get the points the quoters do...the algorithm wants the quotes," while one substitute said "I felt like [the AI] was spot-on."

"Teachers who positioned AI as a substitute tended to use the grades directly, neither factoring in effort nor accounting for students who did not produce enough writing to receive a score."
