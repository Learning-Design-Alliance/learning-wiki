# Schools vary widely in freshman on-track rates even among students with similar backgrounds

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Schools' freshman on-track rates vary from just over 30 percent to just over 90 percent, and for students with typical CPS backgrounds the rate differs by almost 20 percentage points among the middle three-fourths of schools. [→ Allensworth 2005](#allensworth-2005)

## Evidence

### Allensworth 2005

Allensworth, E. M., & Easton, J. Q. (2005). The On-Track Indicator as a Predictor of High School Graduation. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/track-indicator-predictor-high-school-graduation

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Observational comparison of CPS high schools, reported with Figure 1 including adjusted rates that remove differences explainable by entering student characteristics. The report concludes students' likelihood of being on-track depends quite a bit on which school they attend.

> "The on-track rate for students with background characteristics that are typical for CPS (e. g. average achievement and average economic status) differs by almost 20 percentage points (from 56 to 75 percent) among the middle three-fourths of schools, and by 40 percentage points if all schools are compared."
