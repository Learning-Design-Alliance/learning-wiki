`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case evaluation questionnaire across all eight PBL cases (N = 1,072). The article reports "the dimension of 'Integration' achieved the highest grand mean (M = 4.61, SD = 0.15)" and that 'Interest' received the lowest rating (M = 4.15, SD = 0.07) with the smallest standard deviation.

> "Across all eight cases, the dimension of "Integration" achieved the highest grand mean (M = 4.61, SD = 0.15). This was followed by "Fidelity & Relevance" (M = 4.54, SD = 0.16), "Challenge" (M = 4.39, SD = 0.18), "Logic & Flow" (M = 4.38, SD = 0.16), and "Interest" (M = 4.15, SD = 0.07)."
