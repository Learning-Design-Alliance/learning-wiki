# Students using a voluntary virtual patient module achieved significantly higher BLS-AED knowledge post-test scores than controls (ES = 0.73)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Experimental-group students using virtual patients scored significantly higher on the BLS-AED knowledge post-test than the control group (p < 0.001; ES = 0.73). [→ Kononowicz AA 2012](#kononowicz-aa-2012)

## Evidence

### Kononowicz AA 2012

Kononowicz AA, Krawczyk P, Cebula G, Dembkowska M, Drab E, Frączek B, Stachoń AJ, Andres J. (2012). Effects of introducing a voluntary virtual patient module to a basic life support with an automated external defibrillator course: a randomised trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-41

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomised trial of 226 first-year medical students; knowledge measured with 60 true-or-false questions pre- and post-course and compared by ANOVA for repeated measures. The VP-using subgroup (G3, n = 47) outperformed controls (G1, n = 75), with "significantly higher than in the control group (p < 0.001; ES = 0.73)".

> "Post hoc tests revealed that students’ results in the post-test in the experimental group using virtual patients were significantly higher than in the control group (p < 0.001; ES = 0.73)."
