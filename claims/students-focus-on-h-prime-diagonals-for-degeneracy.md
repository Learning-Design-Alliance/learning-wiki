# Students incorrectly use the diagonal elements of the perturbation H′ to decide whether degenerate perturbation theory is needed

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After traditional instruction, many students incorrectly focused on the diagonal elements of the perturbation H′, rather than the degeneracy of H0, to determine whether to use DPT. [→ Christof Keebaugh 2017](#christof-keebaugh-2017)

## Evidence

### Christof Keebaugh 2017

Christof Keebaugh, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Developing and evaluating an interactive tutorial on degenerate perturbation theory. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.041](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.041)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Investigation of student difficulties with written free-response and multiple-choice questions from 32 upper-level undergraduates plus think-aloud interviews with 10 students, after traditional instruction. The article reports that "many students incorrectly focused on the diagonal elements of th e perturbation ˆH ′" to judge degeneracy.

> "In fact, we ﬁnd that many students incorrectly focused on the diagonal elements of th e perturbation ˆH ′ to determine whether there was degeneracy in the system and whether they should use DPT."
