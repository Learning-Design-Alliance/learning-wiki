# Spatial assembly skill measured at age 3 (TOSA) strongly correlates with mathematics performance one year later at age 4

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` TOSA scores at age 3 correlated r = .71 (p < .001) with WIAT math scores at age 4, and TOSA remained a significant predictor in the final regression (std. = .346, t = 2.94, p = .005). [→ Verdine 2014](#verdine-2014)
`q3 i3` Partial correlations controlling the other three spatial/EF predictors remained positive for TOSA (r = .43, p = .005), VMI (r = .43, p = .005), and Tap Test (r = .40, p = .010), with FIST marginal (r = .27, p = .093). [→ Verdine 2014 (2)](#verdine-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Verdine 2014

Verdine, B. N., Irwin, C. M., Golinkoff, R. M., & Hirsh-Pasek, K. (2014). Contributions of Executive Function and Spatial Skills to Preschool Mathematics Achievement. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology, 126, 37-51. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2014.02.012

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis in the longitudinal sample of 44 children, relating age-3 TOSA and age-4 EF, spatial, and math measures. TOSA showed the largest correlation with WIAT (r = .71, p < .001); the TOSA was given a year before the WIAT.

> "Bivariate correlations were analyzed between predictive variables and the dependent variable (WIAT) showing positive, sizeable correlations with the EF (FIST r = .60; p <.001; Tap Test r = .58, p <.001) and spatial measures (VMI r = .67, p <.001; TOSA r = .71, p <.001)."

### Verdine 2014 (2)

Verdine, B. N., Irwin, C. M., Golinkoff, R. M., & Hirsh-Pasek, K. (2014). Contributions of Executive Function and Spatial Skills to Preschool Mathematics Achievement. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology, 126, 37-51. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2014.02.012

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Partial correlation analysis controlling the other three predictors, reported in the Results. TOSA and VMI each correlated r = .43 (p = .005) with WIAT, the Tap Test r = .40 (p = .010), and the FIST a marginal r = .27 (p = .093).

> "Partial correlations between the WIAT and each of the spatial and EF independent variables (TOSA, VMI, Tap Test, and FIST), controlling for the other three independent variables, continued to show positive, mid-level correlations for the TOSA (r = .43; p = .005), VMI (r = .43; p = .005), and Tap Test (r = .40; p = .010) and a marginal correlation for the FIST (r = .27; p = .093)."
