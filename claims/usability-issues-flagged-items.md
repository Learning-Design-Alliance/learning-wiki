Development of the Feedback Quality Instrument: a guide for health professional educators in fostering learner-centred discussions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02722-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Usability analysis conducted while raters administered the provisional instrument to feedback videos, using contemporaneous written comments and recorded team discussions; eight items were flagged for problems including "overlapping items, ambiguous phrasing". No effect size.

> "Raters reported issues related to items 1, 7, 11, 12, 13, 17, 18, 19, such as overlapping items, ambiguous phrasing, restricted applicability or difficulty utilising rating categories, so these items were flagged for review."
