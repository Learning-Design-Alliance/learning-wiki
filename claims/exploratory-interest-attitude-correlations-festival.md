# Exploratory correlations show pre-visit interest strongly tracks pre-visit attitude (r = 0.52) and change in interest moderately tracks change in attitude (r = 0.37).

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Participants' interest before visiting the exhibit correlates strongly and positively with their attitude before visiting (r = 0.52). [→ Koeman 2026](#koeman-2026)
`q2 i3` Participants' change in interest correlates moderately and positively with their change in attitude (r = 0.37). [→ Koeman 2026 (2)](#koeman-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Koeman 2026

Koeman, V., Romp, S., Willems, S. J. W., & Cramer, J. (2026). Quantum at a Music Festival: the Impact of an Exhibit about Quantum Science and Technologies on Festival Visitors. Preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00583-y](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00583-y)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory Pearson correlation analysis of pre-measures in the same festival survey. The article found 'a strong, positive correlation' between pre-visit interest and pre-visit attitude, r = 0.52 (printed as r(795)), p < 0.001.

> "A strong, positive correlation was found between the participants’ level of interest before visiting the exhibit and the participants’ attitude before visiting the exhibit (r(795) = 0.52, p < 0.001)."

### Koeman 2026 (2)

Koeman, V., Romp, S., Willems, S. J. W., & Cramer, J. (2026). Quantum at a Music Festival: the Impact of an Exhibit about Quantum Science and Technologies on Festival Visitors. Preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00583-y](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-026-00583-y)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Same exploratory analysis applied to pre-post change scores. The article found 'a moderate, positive correlation' between change in interest and change in attitude, r = 0.37 (printed as r(781)), p < 0.001.

> "A moderate, positive correlation was found between participants’ change in interest and their change in attitude (r(781) = 0.37, p < 0.001)."
