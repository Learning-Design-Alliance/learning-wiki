## Evidence

### Sellitto Giovanni 2026

Sellitto Giovanni, Galeoto Giovanni, Deodato Manuela, Di Bari Mauro, Garaventa Daniela, Testa Marco, Pelosin Elisa, Pillastrini Paolo, Gatti Roberto, Trucco Marco. (2026). Exploring the Case-Based Learning Challenge (CBLC): a digitally mediated, inter-university simulation to enhance clinical reasoning in physiotherapy students - a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08610-3

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

One-group repeated-measures analysis of paired T0–T1 responses from the Competence Questionnaire administered immediately before and after the CBLC session. Paired-sample t-tests showed the "mean scores increasing from 3.59 ± 1.043 at baseline to 4.02 ± 1.414 post-intervention", with "Cohen's d = 0.33".

> "a statistically significant improvement was observed in the General Section of the Competence Questionnaire, with mean scores increasing from 3.59 ± 1.043 at baseline to 4.02 ± 1.414 post-intervention (p < 0.001). The corresponding effect size was small (Cohen's d = 0.33; 95% CI: 0.21 to 0.45)"
