## Evidence

### White Brahmia 2021

White Brahmia, S. W., Olsho, A., Smith, T. I., Boudreaux, A., Eaton, P., and Zimmerman, C. (2021). The Physics Inventory of Quantitative Literacy: A tool for assessing mathematical reasoning in introductory physics. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129)

`q2 · i1` · `design · r3`

Analysis of PIQL v2.2 administrations across the calculus-based introductory sequence at Institution 1 compares pre/post course scores. The article reports effect sizes "d = 0.3 from PreMech to PostMech and d = 0.1 from PostMech to PostEM" and notes this is below the d about 1 typical of conceptual assessments.

> "Effect sizes from PIQL data are significantly lower:d = 0.3 from PreMech to PostMech andd = 0.1 from PostMech to PostEM, despite data being collected in courses that make heavy use of research-based instructional materials."

### White Brahmia 2021 (2)

White Brahmia, S. W., Olsho, A., Smith, T. I., Boudreaux, A., Eaton, P., and Zimmerman, C. (2021). The Physics Inventory of Quantitative Literacy: A tool for assessing mathematical reasoning in introductory physics. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Score distributions across 2,758 response sets from 2,078 individuals on PIQL v2.2 show descriptive means rising slightly across the sequence; the article states that "many students have not achieved high levels of PQL after two introductory courses." No effect size is printed for the descriptive contrast.

> "the PreMech mean and stan- dard deviation are 9.6 ± 3.7 out of 20. Students’ PQL in- creases slightly with instruction, with mean scores 10.8 ± 4.3 for PostMech and 11.3 ± 4.5 for PostEM"

### White Brahmia 2021 (3)

White Brahmia, S. W., Olsho, A., Smith, T. I., Boudreaux, A., Eaton, P., and Zimmerman, C. (2021). The Physics Inventory of Quantitative Literacy: A tool for assessing mathematical reasoning in introductory physics. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

A one-way ANOVA on PIQL v2.2 data from four academic terms at Institution 1 found no statistically significant term-to-term difference; the article reports F(3, 2754) = 1.6, p = 0.2, with no printed effect size.

> "A one-way analysis of variance showed no signiﬁcant difference between the four academic terms,F (3, 2754) = 1.6,p = 0.2."
