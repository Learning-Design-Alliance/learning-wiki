Darling-Hammond 2018¶

Darling-Hammond, L., & Cook-Harvey, C. M. (2018). Educating the whole child: Improving school climate to support student success. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/educating-whole-child

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report cites cognitive scientists at MIT and Harvard who found that conversation between an adult and a child appears to change the child's brain, and that "the number of “conversational turns” was more important than the quantity of words" in explaining differences in brain physiology and language skills.

"The researchers found that the number of “conversational turns” was more important than the quantity of words in accounting for differences in brain physiology and language skills among children."
