### Lesley G.A. de Putter and Marloes M.H.G. Hendrickx 2025

Lesley G.A. de Putter and Marloes M.H.G. Hendrickx. (2025). Teachers of bachelors' lab courses collaborating to promote open inquiry: a case study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.10379](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.10379)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of 14 of 30 NFLC meetings (October 2022 to June 2023), with emails, meeting notes, Miro boards and annotated documents coded bottom-up into three categories by two researchers. The article reports the phase pattern shown in figure 1, with "bringing information more than taking and consensus" in the start phase and consensus predominant by June 2023.

> "A start phase from September to December 2022 where bringing information more than taking and consensus take place; a change -phase in January and February 2023 where the bringing lessens and taking and consensus start occurring"
