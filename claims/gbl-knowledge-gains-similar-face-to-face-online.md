## Evidence

### Daniel López-Fernández 2023

Daniel López-Fernández, Aldo Gordillo, Jennifer Pérez, Edmundo Tovar. (2023). Learning and Motivational Impact of Game-Based Learning: Comparing Face-To-Face and Online Formats on Computer Science Education. IEEE Transactions on Education. https://doi.org/10.1109/TE.2023.3241099

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples T-tests compared the two groups' test scores and gains in the quasi-experiment (N=217 face-to-face, N=104 online). Learning gains were "slightly greater in the online format" with d = 0.05; Table II reports gains of 1.73 (SD 1.79) vs 1.82 (SD 2.03), p = 0.74.

> "Lastly, regarding the learning gains, these were slightly greater in the online format than in the face-to-face format, but this difference was not statistically significant, and its effect size was negligible (Cohen’s d = 0.05)."
