### Omar David Perez & Gonzalo P. Urcelay 2025 (2)

Omar David Perez & Gonzalo P. Urcelay. (2025). Delayed rewards weaken human goal directed actions. npj Science of Learning 10:36. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00325-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiments 2 (online, N = 39 undergraduates) and 3 (preregistered online, N = 198 Prolific participants) each showed higher response rates with immediate feedback: "t(38) = 3.41,p = 0.002" and "t(197) = 4.71,p < 0.001" respectively. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Similarly, in Experiment 2, response rates were higher in the immediate condition compared to the delayed condition, [t(38) = 3.41,p = 0.002, 95%CI[2.64, 10.36]].
