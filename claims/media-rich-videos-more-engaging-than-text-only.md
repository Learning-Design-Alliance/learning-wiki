`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article reports, citing Teng et al. (2009), a study of 113 students on YouTube videos shown in class. The media-rich videos were deemed more creative and engaging, and media richness influenced viewers' perceptions, motivation and learning. The study found participants "rated the videos with a combination of text, pictures and voicing as more engaging".

> "Participants rated the videos with a combination of text, pictures and voicing as more engaging than the text-only videos, which were seen as informative but dull."
