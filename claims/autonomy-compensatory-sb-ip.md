Swagata Sarkar and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Sense of Belonging and Intent to Persist: Mediating Role of Motivation and Moderating Role of Gender in Physics and Astronomy Graduate Students. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sarkar](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sarkar)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup probit regressions after a median split on autonomy, reported in the findings section and Fig. 2. The low-autonomy slope (0.518) exceeded the high-autonomy slope (0.365); the authors interpret this as "a compensatory pattern" rather than enhancing mediation.

> "Interestingly, the slope is slightly steeper for students with low Autonomy (0.518) than those with high Autonomy (0.365). Contrary to theoretical expectations, higher Autonomy does not amplify the effect of SB."
