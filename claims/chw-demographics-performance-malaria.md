### Mwiinga CH 2018

Mwiinga CH, Zulu JM, Jacobs C, Chongwe G, Chola M, Halwiindi H, Zgambo J, Michelo C. (2018). Towards a framework for analyzing determinants of performance of community health workers in malaria prevention and control: a systematic review. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-018-0284-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Thematic finding on CHW characteristics drawn from Kawakatsu et al. 2015, Bagonza et al. 2014, and Wanduru et al. 2016. The review reports secondary education helped CHWs read and write monthly reports and longer experience built confidential community relationships.

> "Being a female CHW was associated with performance as some female community members who were found home during CHW visits felt freer to be served by a fellow female CHW [11]. Being married also showed to have an influence on performance due to availability of supporters within the household of the CHWs to enable him or her perform her malaria-related duties [11, 15]."
