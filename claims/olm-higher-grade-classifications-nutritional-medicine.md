## Evidence

### Trutwin Benjamin Caspar Raphael 2026

Trutwin Benjamin Caspar Raphael, Eilers Jantje, Herrmann Hans Joachim, Neurath Markus Friedrich, Kohl Matthias, Zopf Yurdagül, Burgard Leonie Cordelia. (2026). In-Person vs. Virtual: A Comparative Study of Teaching Methods in Nutritional Medicine. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu18050821

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Grade conversion of the 40-point single-choice knowledge test in the same randomized post-test trial. The test "indicated significantly higher grades in the OLM group" with r = 0.29, despite equal medians of 3.0.

> "Although the median grade was 3.0 in both groups, the Wilcoxon–Mann–Whitney test indicated significantly higher grades in the OLM group (difference in location = 0.5, 95% CI [0.0, 1.0]; W = 998, p = 0.011; effect size r = 0.29)."
