## Evidence

### Hew Khe Foon 2016

Hew Khe Foon, Tan Cheng Yong. (2016). Predictors of Information Technology Integration in Secondary Schools: Evidence from a Large Scale Study of More than 30,000 Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0168547

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The final HLM model of PISA 2012 data found no significant relationships between IT integration and six sets of variables. The authors note it may be premature to definitively conclude these variables are unrelated, as some may have indirect effects (e.g., principal leadership may operate through influencing teacher beliefs).

> "Class sizes (ClassSize, γ = 0.00, p = .30), school learning climate attributable to student factors (ClimateStu, γ = -0.01, p = .17), principal leadership (Academic, γ = 0.01, p = .39; React, γ = 0.00, p = .47; Monitor, γ = -0.01, p = .35), schools' public posting of achievement data (DataPublic, γ = -0.01, p = .28), tracking of school's achievement data by administrative authorities (DataTracked, γ = 0.01, p = .25), and pedagogical and curricular differentiation in mathematics lessons (Differentiate γ = 0.00, p = .68) were all not related to levels of IT integration"
