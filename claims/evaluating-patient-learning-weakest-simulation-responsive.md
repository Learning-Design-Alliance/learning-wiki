### Aparicio M. 2026

Aparicio M., López-Herce J., Montalvo N., Solana M. J., Vázquez P., Mencía S., López-Herce Jesus. (2026). Simulation based learning as a strategy for patient education training in medical students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-57950-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Domain-level analysis of the practical assessment (part III, assessment of patient learning). The simulation group outperformed both other groups, with 14/23 and 11/22 students in groups 1 and 3 scoring zero; the authors call this "one of the most important findings of the study".

> "The Group 2 (PICU) students scored better (8.46 [0.57]) than did the Group 1 (PCP) (2.53 [0.82] p < 0.001) (14/23 students received a score of 0 out of 10 from the four tutors) and Group 3 (PER) (4.00 [0.95]; p = 0.002)"
