## Evidence

### Domen 2020

Domen, J., Hornstra, L., Weijers, D., van der Veen, I., & Peetsma, T. (2020). Differentiated need support by teachers: Student‐specific provision of autonomy and structure and relations with student motivation. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 90(2), 403–423. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12302

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within‐level path from a two‐level multilevel mediation model of autonomous motivation estimated in Mplus on data from 24 teachers and 506 students, with gender and ethnic background as covariates. The printed coefficient was b = .08, p = .004; the model fit well (RMSEA = .034; CFI = .991).

> "At the student (within) level, we could confirm the hypothesized positive relation between teacher perceptions of student‐specific autonomy support and students' autonomous motivation (b = .08, p = .004)."
