## Evidence

### Back David A 2014

Back David A, Haberstroh Nicole, Antolic Andrea, Sostmann Kai, Schmidmaier Gerhard, Hoff Eike. (2014). Blended learning approach improves teaching in a problem-based learning environment in orthopedics - a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-17

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous evaluation questionnaires completed by all 35 users (17 Likert-scale and short-answer items); the article also reports strong support for continuing the approach (91%). Percentages are descriptive survey results with no effect size.

> "Students who used NESTOR were very satisfied with its offering (92%), approved its overall structure (83%), and had fun learning with it (91%). The blended learning concept was very positively accepted (97%) and NESTOR was considered to be helpful in preparing for clinical situations (74.3%)."
