## Evidence

### Baherimoghadam Tahereh 2025

Baherimoghadam Tahereh, Naseri Navid, Tabesh Amir, Hamedani Shahram, Mousavi Seyedeh Fatemeh. (2025). The influence of learning styles on the efficacy of flipped classroom in dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07916-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wis comparison of wire-bending scores by ILS dimension (Table 8): active vs reflective significant in both groups (p = 0.03 flipped, p = 0.041 face-to-face); sequential vs global significant in flipped (p = 0.001) and sequential flipped vs sequential face-to-face significant (p = 0.016); perception and input dimension comparisons were not significant.

> "in the information processing dimension, in both groups, students with active information processing scored significantly higher than students with reflective information processing. In the flipped class, the grades of students with intuitive perception of information were significantly higher than students with sensory perception of information."
