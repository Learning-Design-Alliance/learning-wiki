## Evidence

### Teng 2015

Teng, F. (2015). Assessing the Relationship between Vocabulary Learning Strategy Use and Vocabulary Knowledge. PASAA, 49, 39-64. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077911

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics of the 5-point Likert strategy questionnaire for the 145 participants, answering research question 1. The direct memory applying strategy had the highest mean (3.77), while indirect categories ranged from 1.71 to 1.86; students 'tend to use more direct vocabulary learning strategies than indirect vocabulary learning strategies.'

> "According to the data in Table 1, it can be concluded that EFL students tend to use more direct vocabulary learning strategies than indirect vocabulary learning strategies. The most frequently used strategy is the direct memory applying strategy."

### Teng 2015 (2)

Teng, F. (2015). Assessing the Relationship between Vocabulary Learning Strategy Use and Vocabulary Knowledge. PASAA, 49, 39-64. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077911

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired-samples t-test comparing direct (M=3.05, S.D.=1.15) and indirect (M=1.78, S.D.=0.99) strategy use across the 145 participants, t=6.15, df=69, 'a significant difference in the use of direct and indirect strategies ( p<0.001)'.

> "The results from Table 2 showed that there was a significant difference in the use of direct and indirect strategies ( p<0.001)."
