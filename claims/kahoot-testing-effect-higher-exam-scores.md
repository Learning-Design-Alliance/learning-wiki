## Evidence

### Iwamoto 2017

Iwamoto, D. H., Hargis, J., Taitano, E. J., & Vuong, K. (2017). Analyzing the efficacy of the testing effect using Kahoot on student performance. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 18(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1145220

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot quasi-experimental action research study with 49 undergraduate psychology students in two sections, one assigned by coin flip to Kahoot quizzes. The Independent-Samples T-Test found significance "at the p<.05 level (F(1, 47) = 7.801, p = .008)"; no effect size was printed.

> "An Independent-Samples T -Test was conducted to compare the effects of the online application Kahoot™ on the mean test scores in the experimental and control groups. Significance was determined between the two groups at the p<.05 level (F(1, 47) = 7.801, p = .008)."
