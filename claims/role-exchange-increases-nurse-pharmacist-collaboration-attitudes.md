# Integrating profession-role exchange into the IPE activity achieves further improvement in nursing and pharmacy students' attitudes toward nurse-pharmacist collaboration beyond the control activity, especially on roles and responsibilities for collaborative practice

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Despite control-group gains, the integration of profession-role exchange achieved further improvement in total nurse-pharmacist collaboration attitude scores (p < 0.05 vs. control). [→ Wang J 2020](#wang-j-2020)
`q2 i?` Attitudes toward Roles/responsibilities for collaborative practice were significantly enhanced by the exchange (44.5% in pharmacy and 54.4% in nursing intervention students vs. 19.1% and 37.3% in control students). [→ Wang J 2020 (2)](#wang-j-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Wang J 2020

Wang J, Guo J, Wang Y, Yan D, Liu J, Zhang Y, Hu X. (2020). Use of profession-role exchange in an interprofessional student team-based community health service-learning experience. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02127-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post survey of nursing and pharmacy students using a self-developed 25-item nurse-pharmacist collaboration scale in the same quasi-experiment; the between-group comparison reached "p < 0.05, vs. control group". No effect size is printed.

> "the integration of profession-role exchange experience into the IPE activity achieved further improvement (p < 0.05, vs. control group)"

### Wang J 2020 (2)

Wang J, Guo J, Wang Y, Yan D, Liu J, Zhang Y, Hu X. (2020). Use of profession-role exchange in an interprofessional student team-based community health service-learning experience. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02127-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subscale analysis in the same study comparing intervention and control percentage gains on the roles/responsibilities factor; the article reports the printed percentages and describes the enhancement as significant. No effect size is printed.

> "the nursing and pharmacy students’ attitudes toward “Roles/responsibilities for collaborative practice” were significantly enhanced by the profession-role exchange experiences (44.5% in pharmacy students and 54.4% in nursing students of the intervention group vs. 19.1% in pharmacy students and 37.3% in nursing students of control group)"
