Evidence¶

Saiz et al. 2015¶

Carlos Saiz, Silvia F. Rivas and Sonia Olivares. (2015). Collaborative learning supported by rubrics improves critical thinking. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 15, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v15i1.12905

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pre-post study of 144 first-year psychology students taking ARDESOS v.2; Student's t tests on PENCRISAL pre-post scores. The total scale rose from M=28.58 to 31.70 (t=-5.87, p=.000, d=.48); practical reasoning rose from 6.31 to 8.40 (d=.83). Decision making showed no significant pre-post change (p=.063).

"the optimized v.2 ARDESOS program was more effective since it significantly improved the performance on the post- measurements across the whole scale and in all the factors, with the exception of decision making"
