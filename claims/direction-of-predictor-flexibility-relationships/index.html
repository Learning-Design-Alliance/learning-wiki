Bülent GÜNDÜZ 2013¶

Bülent GÜNDÜZ. (2013). The Contributions of Attachment Styles, Irrational Beliefs and Psychological Symptoms to the Prediction of Cognitive Flexibility. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 13(4), 2079-2085. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2013.4.1702

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Interpretation of the regression results in the Results section of the 436-student study. The Discussion separately notes "a positive relationship between negligent attachment and flexibility is thought provoking" and links autonomy in negligent attachment to solving problems alone. The word cause is the article's own; the design is correlational.

"it can be said that the excesses of these three variables cause the cognitive flexibility to decline"
