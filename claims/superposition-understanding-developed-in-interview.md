### Ying Cao and Bárbara M. Brizuela 2016

Ying Cao and Bárbara M. Brizuela. (2016). High school students' representations and understandings of electric fields. Physical Review Physics Education Research 12, 020102. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.12.020102

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Guanhua initially represented interactions with multiple charges sequentially but during the interview drew forces from multiple charges in the same frame, demonstrating emergent understanding that forces act simultaneously.

> "Guanhua developed more sophisticated ideas of the superposition of electric forces while he was explaining his work during the interview. For sections that had multiple charges exert forces on the little test charge, Guanhua drew the forces in the same frame and showed the idea that forces act simultaneously."
