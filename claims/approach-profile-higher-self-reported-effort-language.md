## Evidence

### Hornstra 2017

Hornstra, Lisette; Majoor, Marieke; Peetsma, Thea. (2017). Achievement goal profiles and developments in effort and achievement in upper elementary school. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 87(4), 606-629. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12167

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal multilevel analysis of three waves of self-reported effort in language from 722 students, controlling for gender, ethnicity, SES and cognitive ability. The study reports "The effect size for the mean difference in self‐reported effort between approach‐ and multiple goals students can be considered medium (d = .61)" and d = .31 for moderate vs multiple goals.

> "students with an approach‐oriented and a moderate/indifferent profile scored significantly higher than students with a multiple goals profile (b = .64, p < .001; b = .43, p = .010, respectively), but both groups also showed a more negative development in self‐reported effort compared to students with a multiple goals profile"
