## Evidence

### Xingyu Li 2021

Xingyu Li, Difan Song, Miaozhe Han, Yu Zhang, and René F. Kizilcec. (2021). On the limits of algorithmic prediction across the globe. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.15212](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.15212)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Country-level correlation between U.S.-model balanced accuracy and the UN Human Development Index across 65 PISA countries, shown in Fig. 1. The article prints the effect size "r = 0.86, P < 0.001, n = 65". In Indonesia, Vietnam, and Moldova accuracy dropped to no better than chance.

> "We found that the model’s accuracy drops linearly in less and less developed countries [r = 0.86, P < 0.001, n = 65] (Fig. 1)."
