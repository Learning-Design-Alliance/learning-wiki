## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The statement that the Universe started from one place is simply wrong: space-time is generated by matter itself, so with no matter there could not have been any place anywhere to define. [→ Kevin A. Pimbblet 2003](#kevin-a-pimbblet-2003)

## Evidence

### Kevin A. Pimbblet 2003

Kevin A. Pimbblet, John C. Newman. (2003). Ex-nihilo II: Examination Syllabi and the Sequencing of Cosmology Education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1088/0031-9120/38/3/307](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9120/38/3/307)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors analyse an AQA GCSE exemplar syllabus statement using general relativity, arguing that for flat space-time the Gµν components vanish, so space-time is generated by matter itself. They note AQA deleted the phrase from one place for the 2004 syllabus.

> "This is a common misconception. From Einsteins ﬁeld equations of general relativity (e.g. Einstein 1950), it is known that Gµν = 8πGc−4Tµν."
