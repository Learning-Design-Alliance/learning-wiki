`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

K-means clustering of 204 students with complete responses identified two phenotypes; Cluster 1 (n = 77) was "mostly composed of students belonging to the in-person group (69%)" with 85% males, while Cluster 2 (n = 127) was majority online (57%) with 67% males and high satisfaction.

> "Cluster 1 is composed of 77 students, and is mostly composed of students belonging to the in-person group (69%), and is also composed of 85% of male students and present high proportions of “neutral” or “disagree” answers"
