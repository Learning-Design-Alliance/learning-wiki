### Gehlbach 2004

Gehlbach, Hunter. (2004). A New Perspective on Perspective Taking: A Multidimensional Approach to Conceptualizing an Aptitude. Educational Psychology Review 16 (3). https://doi.org/10.1023/B:EDPR.0000034021.12899.11

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Bernstein and Davis's (1982) video-tape procedure study of exposure duration, where a longer tape (about 7:30 minutes) yielded a non-significant trend (p < .20) toward greater accuracy than a shorter tape (about 4:00 minutes). No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "When perspective takers viewed a longer tape (about 7:30 minutes) they tended to be more accurate than when viewing a shorter tape (about 4:00 minutes), although their results did not achieve statistical significance (p < .20)."
