## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Overall 50% of examinees expressed alternative concepts, varying only slightly with level (59% elementary, 47% high school, 48% college), while 32% overall did not express them, rising from 21% to 36% to 42% across levels. [→ Zdeslav Hrepic 2011](#zdeslav-hrepic-2011)

Conclusions of the 1997 written open-ended survey of 287 pupils and students in Split, Croatia, tested immediately after finishing instruction on sound. The author reports that "this percentage varies only slightly with level" while those not expressing alternative concepts rise with level.

> "Another problem is relatively big percentage of students who do express alternative concepts (overall is 50%) and this percentage varies only slightly with level 59% (El.)w 47% (H.S.)w 48% (Col.)."
