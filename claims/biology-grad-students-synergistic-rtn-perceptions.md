### Reid Joshua W. 2020

Reid Joshua W., Gardner Grant E. (2020). Navigating Tensions of Research and Teaching: Biology Graduate Students' Perceptions of the Research–Teaching Nexus within Ecological Contexts. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-11-0218

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of biology graduate students in North America (n = 255 complete responses after exclusions). Descriptive results show "very synergistic perceptions of the RTN (50.2%; Figure 2)" with few antagonistic responses (6.7%, 2.0%, 1.5%); the abstract reports 65.5% overall synergistic.

> "Biology graduate students primarily held very synergistic perceptions of the RTN (50.2%; Figure 2). Notable here is how few graduate students reported slightly antagonistic (6.7%), antagonistic (2.0%), and very antagonistic (1.5%) perceptions."
