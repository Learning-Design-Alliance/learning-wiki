## Evidence

### Bruns et al. 2017

Julia Bruns, Lars Eichen, Hedwig Gasteiger. (2017). Mathematics-related Competence of Early Childhood Teachers Visiting a Continuous Professional Development Course: An Intervention Study. Mathematics Teacher Education and Development, 19(3), 76-93. MERGA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1163878

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measure ANOVA on Rasch-scaled MCK test scores (24 items, secondary school level) in the quasi-experimental study found no significant main effects of time of measurement or treatment group and no interaction effect. The authors attribute this null result to the test measuring MCK at secondary school level rather than the early-childhood-related knowledge addressed in the course.

> "Looking at mathematical content knowledge the results of the repeated measure ANOVA revealed no significant main effect, neither of the time of measurement (F(1, 37) = .23, p > 0.05) nor the treatment group (F(1, 37) = .639, p > 0.05). There was also no interaction effect (F(1, 37) = .45, p > 0.05)."
