### Kay Kelly 2023

Kay Kelly, Metersky Kateryna, Smye Victoria, McGrath Colleen, Johnson Karen, Astell Arlene, Sun Winnie, Bartfay Emma. (2023). A scoping review to inform the development of dementia care competencies. Dementia. https://doi.org/10.1177/14713012231165568

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

A multi-centre-controlled pretest/post-test design with randomized cohort allocation evaluated a communication skills training program with n = 38 community aged care staff (training n = 22; control n = 16). The review reports "Improved knowledge scores from baseline found for training group" with the printed p values; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Improved knowledge scores from baseline found for training group immediately post training (p = 0.002) and at 3-months follow up (p = 0.001). Training effect for self-efficacy (p = 0.024), strain in nursing care (0.023) and preparedness to provide care (p = 0.007). No significant difference for the control group on any measures."
