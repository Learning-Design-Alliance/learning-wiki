## Evidence

### Laaksonen M 2025

Laaksonen M, Paavilainen E, Koivisto A-M, Halkoaho A. (2025). Genomic competence among nurses: A spotlight on ethics. Nursing Ethics, 32(8). https://doi.org/10.1177/09697330251366594

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 234 Finnish registered nurses using the GGNPS-CA. Self-rated understanding of general genomics/genetics: "79 % (n = 184) assessed their understanding as poor." A separate result line reports the mean KS was 9.12 (SD = 1.44) among the 216 nurses answering all knowledge items.

> "Of the respondents, 21% (n = 49) assessed their general genetic/genomic understanding in two-level variable as excellent/good, while 79 % (n = 184) assessed their understanding as poor."
