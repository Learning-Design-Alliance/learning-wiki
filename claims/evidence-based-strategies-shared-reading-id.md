# Least-to-most prompting and teaching wh-word meanings meet evidence-based criteria; explicit comprehension-strategy instruction is potentially evidence-based

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

The review's synthesis applying Council for Exceptional Children criteria (at least five methodologically sound single-case studies with positive effects and at least 20 participants) to the coded instructional strategies across the included studies.

> "two instructional strategies can be identified as evidence based: (a) least to most intrusive prompts (including rereads and often reminders of the strategy taught) to help students answer comprehension questions (supported by 15 studies) and (b) teaching the meaning of wh-words (supported by seven studies). Teaching comprehension strategies explicitly can be considered potentially evidence based because it is supported by five methodologically sound studies with positive effects but encompassing fewer than 20 participants across studies."
