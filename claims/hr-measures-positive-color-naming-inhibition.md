### Muntaner-Mas Adrià 2020

Muntaner-Mas Adrià, Vidal-Conti Josep, Salmon Jo, Palou-Sampol Pere. (2020). Associations of Heart Rate Measures during Physical Education with Academic Performance and Executive Function in Children: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17124307

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of 130 children relating heart rate measures from PE lessons to Stroop test conditions; the results section reports the three HR measures "were positively related to color naming" only.

> "The minimum, average, and maximum HRs were positively related to color naming. No significant associations were found for the rest of the variables examined."
