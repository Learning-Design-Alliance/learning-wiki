## Evidence

### Klingenberg 2024

Klingenberg, S., Bosse, R., Mayer, R. E., & Makransky, G. (2024). Does Embodiment in Virtual Reality Boost Learning Transfer? Testing an Immersion-Interactivity Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09956-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the 2x2 experiment, the main outcome was a real-life transfer test in which students performed the serial dilution with concrete objects, rated on 22 items by two independent raters. The article reports "no significant effects of immersion or interactivity on any of the transfer measures"; no effect sizes are printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "However, there were no significant effects of immersion or interactivity on any of the transfer measures."
