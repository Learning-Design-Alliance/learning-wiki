Evidence¶

Alexander Skulmowski 2023¶

Alexander Skulmowski. (2023). The Cognitive Architecture of Digital Externalization. Educational Psychology Review, 35:101. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09818-1

q1 · i? · review · r3

The review's theoretical argument about generative AI and externalization: "Just as having access to a wealth of information using one's smartphone can induce the illusion of being knowledgeable, a tool that can easily generate texts could result in convincing users of being good writers." The article offers this as a reasoned prediction, not a tested result.
