# In the interest-development context, naming the theory without full references produced the most practical and usable codebook, while supplying full papers enhanced theoretical alignment but reduced applicability

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among four prompting strategies (no theory, theory named, references listed, full papers supplied), naming the framework without references yielded the most practical and usable codebook in human evaluations. [→ Zambrano 2026](#zambrano-2026)
`q2 i?` Using the SRL prompt structure with the more abstract interest-development theory produced theoretically aligned but less practical results. [→ Zambrano 2026 (2)](#zambrano-2026-2)

### Zambrano 2026

Data Plus Theory Equals Codebook: Leveraging LLMs for Human-AI Codebook Development. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 18, No 1. https://osf.io/g3z4x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Study 2 compared four prompt-engineering strategies for generating a codebook guided by Hidi & Renninger's four-phase Interest Development model using 144 interview transcripts from 14 middle-school students; human evaluations favored the naming-only strategy.

> "Human evaluations show that naming the theory without including full references produced the most practical and usable codebook, while supplying full papers to the prompt enhanced theoretical alignment but reduced applicability."
