### Ratekin Carly Schade 2025

Adjusted logistic regression of mortality by ventilation status among 553 recorded births; table 5 prints OR 0.26 (95% CI 0.12 to 0.59, p=0.00) for receiving bag and mask ventilation at birth. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "this study found that 95.10% of neonates lived without receiving bag and mask ventilation, while only 84.28% of neonates lived after receiving bag and mask ventilation."
