A systematic review and network meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06291-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analyses by region, curricula, and educational background within the NMA. The article also reports "CBL emerged as the most effective method for improving proficiency in clinical course knowledge with a probability of 97.7%" and superior performance regardless of educational background; further subgroups could not be conducted due to limited studies.

> "Notably, PBL was found to be particularly effective in enhancing theoretical test scores in developing countries with a probability of 86.6%, as well as in basic courses with a probability of 78.3%."
