## Evidence

### Di Mitri Daniele 2019

Di Mitri Daniele, Schneider Jan, Specht Marcus, Drachsler Hendrik. (2019). Detecting Mistakes in CPR Training with Multimodal Data and Neural Networks. Sensors (Basel, Switzerland). https://doi.org/10.3390/s19143099

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Figure 6 class-distribution plots for classRate and classDepth are interpreted in running text: individual participants clustered in two of three classes, so "a more even distribution of mistakes can only be achieved when collecting data from multiple participants".

> "Participants tended to make only one type of mistake for a particular target, or, in other words, either they were too fast or too slow, but not a combination of both. The performance of a single participant is therefore not representative for the full span of possible mistakes that may occur during training."
