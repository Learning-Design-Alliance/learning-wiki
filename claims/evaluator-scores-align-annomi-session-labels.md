`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

External comparison analysis of Evaluator-assigned global MI scores against coarse, metadata-derived high/low session-level quality labels in the publicly available AnnoMI corpus (133 transcripts). The authors treat this as preliminary external grounding rather than validation against expert fidelity coders.

> "Using a threshold of 2, where scores ≤2 were classified as low quality and scores >2 were classified as high quality, yielded 91.7% accuracy, 95.4% precision, 94.5% recall, and an F1-score of 95.0%."
