## Evidence

### Clements 2013

Clements, D. H., Sarama, J., Wolfe, C. B., & Spitler, M. E. (2013). Longitudinal Evaluation of a Scale-Up Model for Teaching Mathematics With Trajectories and Technologies: Persistence of Effects in the Third Year. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831212469270

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Intent-to-treat HLM analysis of the cluster randomized trial (N = 1,079 across 42 schools), with pre-K pretest as covariate. The article reports TRIAD-FT "significantly outperformed" control with "g = .38" at end of first grade.

> "Children within the TRIAD-FT condition significantly outperformed both the TRIAD-NFT (LP: b = .124; SE = .056, p = .03, g = .18) and control (RP: b = .264; SE = .057, p \ .001, g = .38) conditions within the ITT sample on the mathematics outcome at the end of first grade."
