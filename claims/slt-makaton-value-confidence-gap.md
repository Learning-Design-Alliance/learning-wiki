## Evidence

### Mc Geer 2026

Mc Geer, C., Milton, C., Vorster, C., & Graham, M.A. (2026). South African speech-language therapists’ perceived competencies and use of Makaton. South African Journal of Communication Disorders, 73(1), a1154. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajcd.v73i1.1154

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional mixed-methods online survey of 57 South African SLTs with at least Level 1 Makaton training. The article reports that "87.7% of participants viewed Makaton as valuable, only 5.5% reported feeling very confident using it"; no effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "While 87.7% of participants viewed Makaton as valuable, only 5.5% reported feeling very confident using it."
