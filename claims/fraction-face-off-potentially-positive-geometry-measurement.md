# Fraction Face-Off! shows potentially positive effects on fourth-grade students' geometry and measurement outcomes, with a WWC-calculated average effect size of 0.96 and improvement index of +33

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q4` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q4 i3` In one RCT meeting WWC standards without reservations, Fraction Face-Off! produced a positive and statistically significant effect on geometry and measurement (average effect size 0.96, improvement index +33, 212 students). [→ geom-1](#geom-1)

## Evidence

### geom-1

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, March). Fraction Face-Off!. Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/702

`q4 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Findings from the one RCT meeting WWC group design standards without reservations, on researcher-designed fraction magnitude measures with 212 fourth-grade students. The fraction magnitude comparison measure showed an effect size of 1.15 and improvement index of +38, p < .01; the domain average effect size was 0.96.
