### Paul Stonaha 2024

Paul Stonaha, Stephanie T. Douglas. (2024). Efficacy of a hybrid take home and in class summative assessment for the postsecondary physics classroom. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad7fc0](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad7fc0)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey comparison of control (NC=31) and experimental (NE=59) cohorts in one Physics II course, aggregated over three post-test surveys. Table 1 reports Q14 means of 3.61 (control) and 4.07 (experimental), rMW = 0.224, pMW = 0.031; the difference is "significant (pMW = 0.031)".

> "While both the control and experimental cohorts concurred with the statement “preparing for the exams increased their mastery of physics” (Q14), the experimental cohort agreed more strongly with that statement, and the difference between the two cohorts is significant (pMW = 0.031)."
