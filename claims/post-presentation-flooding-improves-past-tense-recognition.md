## Evidence

### Rikhtegar 2015

Rikhtegar, O., & Gholami, J. (2015). The Effects of Pre- versus Post-Presentation Input Flooding via Reading on the Young Iranian EFL Learners' Acquisition of Simple Past Tense. English Language Teaching, 8(3), 80-88. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n3p80

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same ANCOVA on post-test scores with pre-test covariate; the post-presentation group (n = 20) differed from the control group (n = 20) by a mean of "9.74", significant at the 0.001 alpha level.

> "The estimated mean difference of post-presentation and control groups (9.74) is significant at (0.001) alpha level. Thus, the post-presentation input flooding via reading has positive and significant effect on the acquisition of past tense."
