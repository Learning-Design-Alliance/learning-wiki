Bingjun Li 2025¶

Bingjun Li, Christopher Buzaid, Weihao Qu. (2025). Security Education in Higher Education through AI-Powered Gamification. Journal of Cybersecurity, Digital Forensics and Jurisprudence. https://doi.org/10.65879/3070-5789.2025.01.07

q2 · i? · design · r2

Tier 1 technical validation in which nine Computer Science students in an advanced Cybersecurity course played all five subgames and voted on the most effective game mechanics. The TikTok mini-game drew "the highest engagement rating (7 out of 9 votes)"; vote counts are descriptive.

"The TikTok mini-Game received the highest engagement rating (7 out of 9 votes), with experts citing its fast-paced nature as highly effective for "attention capture.""

"The Text Phishing Detective mini-game received 5 votes for "Best Learning Value," as experts noted it effectively taught users to identify URL spoofing and subtle social engineering cues."
