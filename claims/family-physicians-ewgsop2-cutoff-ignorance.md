Survey results on EWGSOP2 criteria (Table 5): correct-response rates were 9.9% for grip strength cutoffs, 3.7% for calf circumference (<31 cm), and 4.9% for gait speed (<0.8 m/s), with no-opinion rates of 84.2–86.4%. Descriptive percentages only.

> "Regarding the cutoff values for low grip strength, only 9.9% correctly identified the thresholds as <27 kg for men and <16 kg for women. A substantial 84.2% indicated no opinion on this matter."
