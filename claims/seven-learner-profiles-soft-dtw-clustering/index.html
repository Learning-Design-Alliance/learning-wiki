Evidence¶

Wei Ding 2026¶

Wei Ding, Anjuli Niyogi, Jordan A. Taylor & Jonathan S. Tsay. (2026). Hypothesis Testing Governs Strategic Motor Learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00428-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Soft-DTW clustering restricted to successful learners (N = 476; 85% learner rate). Clusters C1–C3 converged on the correct rotational solution (C1: 62.2% of learners), C4–C6 settled on sign-flip solutions, C7 defaulted to straight-to-target movements; outer geometries produced a higher proportion of rapid-converging C1 learners.

"Critically, the dissociable impact of target geometry on learning and generalization emerged from a continuous shift in the mixture of strategies ( Figure 4d; Table S4; χ²(18) = 75.8, p < .001)"
