## Evidence

### Amrita Bains 2023

Amrita Bains, Carina Spaulding, Jessie Ricketts and Saloni Krishnan. (2023). Using a willingness to wait design to assess how readers value text. npj Science of Learning 8:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00160-3

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Mixed effects logistic regression with wait choice as dependent variable and enjoyment as fixed effect, random intercepts and slopes for participant and synopsis (N = 40). The model suggests "1 standard deviation (SD) increase in enjoyment was associated with a participant being 2.57 times more likely to wait".

> "In Experiment 1, as hypothesised, enjoyment of a synopsis predicted participants’ likelihood to wait to learn more information about the corresponding book, β = 0.94, SE = 0.11, z = 8.73, p < 0.001"
