## Evidence

### Okulu 2018

Okulu, H. Z., & Oğuz Ünver, A. (2018). The Process of Facilitating Knowledge Acquisition and Retention: An Inquiry into Magnetic Poles with Challenging Questions. International Education Studies, 11(5), 25-37. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n5p25

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest analysis of 65 pre-service teachers' responses on the number and location of poles across five magnet shapes. The study reports that 62 participants correctly located the ends of a bar magnet and 53 the ends of a horseshoe magnet, while for the disc magnet 23 located poles at upper and lower points and for the ring magnet 17 at internal and external points.

> "The participants used the end variable to determine the poles of bar and horseshoe magnets. However, since the disc and ring magnets do not have ends, the participants tried to determine the locations of the poles using only surface variables for these two magnets."
