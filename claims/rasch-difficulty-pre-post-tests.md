## Evidence

### Evgenia Paxinou 2017

Evgenia Paxinou, Vasilis Zafeiropoulos, Athanasios Sypsas, Chairi Kiourt, and Dimitris Kalles. (2017). Assessing the Impact of Virtualizing Physical Labs. Hellenic Open University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

IRT (Rasch model) analysis of the 43 students' dichotomous responses, computed in R with the TAM package; Table I prints average difficulty parameters (e.g. Pre-Test 1st group -1.807 ± 1,277; 3rd group 0.035 ± 1,041). The authors state the tests are "designed in augmented difficulty from 1 st to the 3 rd group".

> "Table I provides evidence that the tests are designed in augmented difficulty from 1 st to the 3 rd group of students."

### Evgenia Paxinou 2017 (2)

Evgenia Paxinou, Vasilis Zafeiropoulos, Athanasios Sypsas, Chairi Kiourt, and Dimitris Kalles. (2017). Assessing the Impact of Virtualizing Physical Labs. Hellenic Open University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Rasch-model difficulty estimates from the same analysis (Table I). The authors report that "a ll Post-Tests seem to be less difficult compared to their corresponding Pre -Tests", attributing this to administration immediately after the tutorial. Students' ability was assessed but not shown.

> "According to Table I a ll Post-Tests seem to be less difficult  compared to their corresponding Pre -Tests, something that was expected , as Post -Tests were administered right away after the face -to-face tutorial."
