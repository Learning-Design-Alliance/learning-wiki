## Evidence

### Carmen Yot-Domínguez and Carlos Marcelo 2017

Carmen Yot-Domínguez and Carlos Marcelo. (2017). University students' self-regulated learning using digital technologies. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education 14:38. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-017-0076-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Principal components analysis with varimax rotation on 33 survey items from 711 students; Bartlett's test (p = .000) and KMO (.886) supported factorability. Factors were named share information, active presence, documentation and classification, superficial use, expansion, monitoring and feedback, personal management, self-evaluation, and collaborative learning.

> "This exploratory analysis determined that the number of factors should be 9, and would therefore explain a variation of 54.904% of the data. To shape the factors, factorial loads greater than 0.40 were considered."
