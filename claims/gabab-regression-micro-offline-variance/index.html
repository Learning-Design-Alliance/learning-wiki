Evidence¶

Pasquale Cardellicchio & Sara Borgomaneri 2025¶

Pasquale Cardellicchio & Sara Borgomaneri. (2025). Level of M1 GABAB predicts micro offline consolidation of motor learning during wakefulness. npj Science of Learning 10:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00299-1

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Bayesian linear regression (JASP, JZS prior r scale = 0.354) testing whether LICI predicts individual learning-fitting estimates and micro-online/micro-offline performance in the 16-participant experiment. R² and B are regression quantities, not standardized effect sizes, so no impact value is assigned.

"The model with Micro-ofﬂine-T learning explained a moderate proportion of the variance R2 =0 . 6 9 ( B F10 > 100) (Table 2). Micro-ofﬂine-T learning (B = −0.58, BF inclusion= 52.6) provides moderate evidence for being included in the model"

Related Claims¶

  • Lower resting GABAB-mediated inhibition in M1 (measured as LICI) is strongly negatively associated with micro-offline motor consolidation gains during early learning — related
  • No other neurophysiological measure (MEP, SICI, ICF, sICF) showed a significant correlation with micro-online or micro-offline learning — related
  • The GABAB–micro-offline correlation is significantly stronger than the corresponding GABAA (SICI) correlation — related
  • The magnitude of micro-online and micro-offline gains is strongly related across participants (r = -0.96 between subjects) — related
