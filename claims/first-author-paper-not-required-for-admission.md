## Evidence

### Emily M. Levesque (chair) 2025

Emily M. Levesque (chair), Rachel Ivie, Christopher Johns-Krull, Grace Krahm, Laura A. Lopez, Meredith A. MacGregor, Sebastian Monzon, Daniel R. Piacitelli, Seth Redfield, and Tom Rice. (2025). AAS Graduate Admissions Task Force: Final report and recommendations. American Astronomical Society. [doi:10.3847/25c2cfeb.53ec1fbc](https://doi.org/10.3847/25c2cfeb.53ec1fbc)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Applicant survey result on publication records at time of application. The report states this is "at odds with the perception that a first-author paper is becoming a de facto requirement for admission".

> "while 93% of our respondents were admitted to grad school, only 27% of our respondents had a first-author paper by the time they applied to grad school."

### Emily M. Levesque (chair) 2025 (2)

Emily M. Levesque (chair), Rachel Ivie, Christopher Johns-Krull, Grace Krahm, Laura A. Lopez, Meredith A. MacGregor, Sebastian Monzon, Daniel R. Piacitelli, Seth Redfield, and Tom Rice. (2025). AAS Graduate Admissions Task Force: Final report and recommendations. American Astronomical Society. [doi:10.3847/25c2cfeb.53ec1fbc](https://doi.org/10.3847/25c2cfeb.53ec1fbc)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Program survey result: while 83% (86%) of programs saw first authorship (co-authorship) on a submitted refereed paper as important, most admitted students lack refereed publications.

> "83% of responding programs indicated that most of their admitted students do not have a refereed publication."
