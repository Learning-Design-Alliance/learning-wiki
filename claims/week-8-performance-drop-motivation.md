## Evidence

### Glöckner 2013

Glöckner, A., Towfigh, E., & Traxler, C. (2013). Development of legal expertise. Instructional Science, 41, 989–1007. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-013-9266-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Panel regression with fixed effects on 71,405 exams estimating "an average drop of 0.24 grading points in week 8". The drop "remains statistically significant and substantial in magnitude" and "washes away the improvement that comes with an experience of 7.5 exams." A second analysis found "if the weather was good before the exam—i.e., if there was a day without any clouds on the sky—the drop in the grade was even more pronounced (0.97 grading points instead of 0.24)."

> "Accounting for all other inﬂuences, we estimate an average drop of 0.24 grading points in week 8 after the ﬁrst exam."
