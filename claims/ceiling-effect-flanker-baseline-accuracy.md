Authors' interpretation in the discussion of the cluster-RCT, noting baseline accuracy of 92–94% for congruent and 83–88% for incongruent trials. The article states "the lack of findings is due to a ceiling effect in the present sample" as a possibility.

> "it is possible that the lack of findings is due to a ceiling effect in the present sample; this assumption is supported, as the largest improvements in cognitive performance outcomes were observed among the 2 groups with the lowest baseline performance scores"
