# Old high performers show larger target-locked P2/N2 amplitudes than old low performers, exceeding middle-aged or young adults in some conditions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The P2/N2 complex was consistently larger in old high than old low performers, with some conditions showing larger amplitudes than young adults. [→ Patrick D. Gajewski 2020](#patrick-d-gajewski-2020)

## Evidence

### Patrick D. Gajewski 2020

Patrick D. Gajewski, Michael Falkenstein, Sven Th€ones, Edmund Wascher. (2020). Stroop task performance across the lifespan: High cognitive reserve in older age is associated with enhanced proactive and reactive interference control. NeuroImage 207 (2020) 116430. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.116430

`q3 · i?`

Target-locked ERP analysis of the P2/N2 complex at FCz. A main effect of Group (ηp2 = 0.064) and a significant Group x Block x Congruity interaction (ηp2 = 0.026) showed "consistently larger P2/N2 in OH than OL", and specific conditions showed larger amplitudes in OH than YA or OM.

> "Bonferroni corrected post-hoc tests yielded consistently larger P2/N2 in OH than OL ( /C0 6.2 vs. /C0 5.2 μV; p < .001), whereas the difference did not reach signi ﬁcance for other group comparisons."
