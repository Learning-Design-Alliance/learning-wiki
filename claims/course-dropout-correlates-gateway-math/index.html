Evidence¶

Daria Katharina Benden and Fani Lauermann 2021¶

Daria Katharina Benden and Fani Lauermann. (2021). Students' Motivational Trajectories and Academic Success in Math-Intensive Study Programs: Why Short-Term Motivational Assessments Matter. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000708

q3 · i? · associational · r?

Bivariate correlational analysis in Study 1a preliminary results (N = 1,004). Course dropout, operationalized as lack of attendance at end of semester, showed correlations with SES, GPA, preparatory course participation, and initial motivations as reported.

"course dropout was linked to lower SES (r = −.11, p = .002), lower high school GPA (r = −.39, p < .001), lower likelihood of participation in preparatory math courses (r = −.23, p < .001), and less adaptive initial motivations."
