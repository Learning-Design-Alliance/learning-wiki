Mohammadian et al. 2018¶

Amir Mohammadian, Amin Saed, Younes Shahi. (2018). The Effect of Using Video Technology on Improving Reading Comprehension of Iranian Intermediate EFL Learners. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 9(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.9n.2p.17

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-samples t-test within the 15-member experimental group using educational video files. Means rose from 19.07 to 23.80; the article reports the difference as significant at the 0.01 level. No effect size is printed.
