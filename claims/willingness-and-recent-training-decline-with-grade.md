`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary analysis across grades among the 397 unqualified students (Table 2). Both willingness to perform CPR and receipt of training within 6 months declined significantly across Grade One, Two and Three, attributed by the authors to college entrance examination pressure.

> "Willingness to implement CPR decreased as school grade increased (72.4% vs. 66.2% vs. 54.8%, P = 0.009), and training intervals <6 months decreased as school grade increased (23.8% vs. 15.8% vs. 6.8%, P = 0.001)"
