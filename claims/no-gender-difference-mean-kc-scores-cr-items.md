## Evidence

### Federer Meghan Rector 2016

Federer Meghan Rector, Nehm Ross H., Pearl Dennis K. (2016). Examining Gender Differences in Written Assessment Tasks in Biology: A Case Study of Evolutionary Explanations. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mean raw-score analysis of ACORNS constructed-response responses from undergraduate biology majors and nonmajors (e.g., ELSP strand n = 680, MSLG n = 184, SRPE n = 640) compared independent t tests of male and female KC scores. The article reports "no significant difference in mean KC scores"; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in mean KC scores between males and females on individual CR items or on strands of items (i.e., four-item sequence, grouped by item features; Table 3)."
