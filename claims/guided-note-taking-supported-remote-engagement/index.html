Evidence¶

Means 2020¶

Means, B., Peters, V., Neisler, J., & Griffiths, R. (2020). STEM Courses During the COVID Pandemic: Lessons from Spring 2020. Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7900cb79-6ea5-44d0-aad6-34aa81e9bf4b

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative case studies of 28 STEM courses at nine institutions; interviews and focus groups with 92 students. Instructors assigned for-credit guided lecture outlines submitted as attendance; one biology instructor said the assignment "went from passive to active."

"Focus group students in courses incorporating guided note-taking or other active learning activities during lectures did not report the same level of difficulty with motivation or staying engaged that other students did."
