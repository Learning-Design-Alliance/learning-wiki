### Boxuan Ma 2026

Boxuan Ma, Huiyong Li, Gen Li, Li Chen, Cheng Tang, Atsushi Shimada, and Shin'ichi Konomi. (2026). Three Years with Classroom AI in Introductory Programming: Shifts in Student Awareness, Interaction, and Performance. [doi:10.1145/3862769](https://doi.org/10.1145/3862769)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal questionnaire comparisons across cohorts (RQ1, Fig. 1(a)) in an introductory Python course at a Japanese university. The survey shows high familiarity at "only5.2%ofre- spondents" in 2023, rising to 47.2% in 2024 and 60% in 2025; descriptive percentages only, no effect size printed.

> "In2023,only5.2%ofre- spondents reported high familiarity, while the majority fell into the medium cat- egory (68.4%) and over a quarter reported low familiarity (26.3%)."

### Boxuan Ma 2026 (2)

Boxuan Ma, Huiyong Li, Gen Li, Li Chen, Cheng Tang, Atsushi Shimada, and Shin'ichi Konomi. (2026). Three Years with Classroom AI in Introductory Programming: Shifts in Student Awareness, Interaction, and Performance. [doi:10.1145/3862769](https://doi.org/10.1145/3862769)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort-level questionnaire results on GenAI usage (Fig. 1(b)). The article reports active use at 36.8% in 2023, 63.9% in 2024, and that "GenAI use became nearly universal: 91.7% reported active use" in 2025; descriptive percentages, no effect size.

> "In 2023, only 36.8% reported active use, and a substantial portion reported no use. By 2024, activeuseincreasedmarkedly(63.9%),andtheshareofstudentsreportingnouse dropped to 11.1%."
