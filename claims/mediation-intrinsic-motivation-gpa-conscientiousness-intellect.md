## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Bootstrap mediation showed significant indirect effects of intrinsic motivation on GPA via intellect (0.02, CI 0.002–0.047) and via conscientiousness (0.04, CI 0.01–0.07); the conscientiousness pathway was significant only for male students. [→ Kuśnierz Cezary 2020](#kusnierz-cezary-2020)

Kuśnierz Cezary, Rogowska Aleksandra M., Pavlova Iuliia. (2020). Examining Gender Differences, Personality Traits, Academic Performance, and Motivation in Ukrainian and Polish Students of Physical Education: A Cross-Cultural Study. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PROCESS Model 4 and Model 14 moderated mediation with 10,000 bootstrap samples in the same survey; the moderated mediation model explained 9% of GPA variance (R² = 0.09, F(4, 419) = 10.37), and "The indirect effect of the IM on GPA via C was statistically significant only for male PE students".

> "The indirect effect of the IM on GPA via C was statistically significant only for male PE students, 0.002, SE = 0.001, bootstrap CI = (0.001, 0.004), whereas for female students, it was not statistically significant, 0.000, SE = 0.001, bootstrap CI = (−0.001, 0.001)."
