Descriptive survey finding reported in the conclusion: 127 of 151 respondents reported using GenAI tools in their academic work. The article states the tools are "already widely used in students’ academic work" at the case institution.

"At the same time, GenAI tools are already widely used in students’ academic work. Among the respondents who reported using GenAI tools, 92 out of 127 students (72.44%) indicated that they were uncertain whether their usage complies with institutional regulations."
