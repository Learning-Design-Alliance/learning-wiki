Matthew T. Johnson 2015¶

Matthew T. Johnson, Stephen Lipscomb, Brian Gill. (2015). Sensitivity of Teacher Value-Added Estimates to Student and Peer Control Variables. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness, vol. 8, issue 1. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/journal-article-sensitivity-of-teacher-valueadded-estimates-to-student-and-peer-control-variables

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Comparative analysis across VAM specification variations, including the two variations the article introduces as previously unexplored: replacing classroom peer characteristics with teacher-level averages and allowing demographics to influence the relationship between current and prior achievement. The article states "Other modeling choices have smaller practical consequences." No effect sizes are printed.

"Other modeling choices have smaller practical consequences, and none are as important as selecting which assessment to use as the outcome measure."
