## Evidence

### Ruhollah Khanbeiki; Seyed Jalal Abdolmanafi-Rokni 2015

Ruhollah Khanbeiki; Seyed Jalal Abdolmanafi-Rokni. (2015). The Effect of Explicit vs. Implicit Instruction on the Learnability of English Consonant Clusters by Iranian Learners of English. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, Vol. 6 No. 2. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.2p.103

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A one-way ANOVA on post-test scores of 60 learners across three groups reported "a strong significant relationship between the type of instruction and the students' accuracy in correctly pronouncing the words containing consonant clusters".

> "the results of the ANOVA analysis proved that there was a strong significant relationship between the type of instruction and the students' accuracy in correctly pronouncing the words containing consonant clusters"

## Discussion

The paper's ANOVA table reports a within-groups df of 57 (60 learners in three groups) but a mean square of 30.690, which is 2,670 / 87, not 2,670 / 57; the F of 74.618 in the text was computed with that df of 87. Recomputed from the table, F(2, 57) ≈ 48.9. The group difference stays highly significant, but the reported F overstates it.
