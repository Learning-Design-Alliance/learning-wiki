# Adolescents with co-occurring ADHD and ODD symptoms show the greatest deficits in educational attainment by age 32, for both males and females

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among males, the ADHD+ODD group was far more likely than controls to have comprehensive school as their highest attainment (23.0% vs 6.3%; adjusted OR 2.03, 95% CI 1.18–3.48) and far less likely to reach higher education (adjusted OR 0.22, 95% CI 0.11–0.43). [→ Seppä S 2023](#seppa-s-2023)
`q3 i?` Among females, the ADHD+ODD group was more likely than controls to stop at comprehensive school (adjusted OR 2.34, 95% CI 1.05–5.23) and less likely to reach higher education (adjusted OR 0.20, 95% CI 0.11–0.36). [→ Seppä S 2023 (2)](#seppa-s-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Seppä S 2023

Seppä S, Halt A-H, Nordström T, Hurtig T. (2023). Effects of Symptoms of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD) on Academic Performance and Educational Attainment. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-023-01598-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multinomial logistic regression of register-based educational attainment at 32 in the NFBC1986 cohort, with upper secondary school as reference. The male ADHD+ODD versus control contrast for stopping at comprehensive school was significant unadjusted ("OR was 2.82 with a 95% CI of 1.76–4.52") and remained significant adjusted (OR 2.03, 95% CI 1.18–3.48).

> "Where 6.3% of the male participants in the control group had comprehensive school as their highest educational attainment, this was the case for 23.0% of those in the ADHD + ODD group. In the unadjusted model the OR was 2.82 with a 95% CI of 1.76–4.52"

### Seppä S 2023 (2)

Seppä S, Halt A-H, Nordström T, Hurtig T. (2023). Effects of Symptoms of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD) on Academic Performance and Educational Attainment. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-023-01598-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Register-based multinomial logistic regression for females in NFBC1986; the female ADHD+ODD group reached higher education at 21.4% versus 60.4% of controls, with the adjusted OR of 0.20 (95% CI 0.11–0.36) reported in Table 5. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "60.4% of the female participants in the control group had an institution of higher education as their ultimate educational attainment, as opposed to 30.0% of those in the ADHD group (adjusted OR 0.37 with a 95% CI of 0.24–0.57) and 21.4% of those in the ADHD + ODD group"
