`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired analysis in a randomized controlled trial of 74 surgical clerkship students (37 per group) comparing the numerical difference between preintervention and postintervention DOPS scores. The study found the score increase "was not significantly different" between groups (P=.48); equivalence was not formally tested.

> "Based on this analysis, we found that the score increase between the FTF and LS groups was not significantly different (FTF: mean 27.5, SD 20.6 vs LS: mean 24.4, SD 16.7; P=.48)."
