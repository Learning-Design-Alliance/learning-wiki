## Evidence

### Bjørngaard Hilde 2022

Bjørngaard Hilde, Koksvik Hege Svean, Jakobsen Bente, Grønning Kjersti. (2022). Nurses experience increased clinical and organisational competence by working with a medical quality register, RevNatus – a qualitative study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-022-08595-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the interview analysis, nurses reported that "the registration form was an excellent template for structuring the consultations with the women" and that its questions acted as a checklist covering blood tests, pain, fatigue and disease activity. No effect size is applicable to this qualitative finding.

> "The questions in the registration acted as a checklist for important aspects necessary to monitor during pregnancy, such as abnormal blood tests, pain, fatigue and symptoms of increased disease activity."
