### S. B. McKagan 2007

S. B. McKagan, W. Handley, K. K. Perkins, and C. E. Wieman. (2007). A Research-Based Curriculum for Teaching the Photoelectric Effect. https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2165 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0706.2165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0706.2165)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of a random subset of 47 students per semester responding to the second exam question, coding correct observations (Fig. 7), correct inferences (Fig. 8), and reasoning quality (Fig. 9) as no, partial, or good reasoning; the quote states "Only about a third of the students were able to fully explain".

> "Only about a third of the students were able to fully explain how the observations led to the in- ferences. Further, many students were confused by the diﬀerence between observations and inferences, giving in- ferences in response to questions about observations and vice versa."
