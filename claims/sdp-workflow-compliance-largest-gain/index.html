Hossein Talebzadeh 2025¶

Hossein Talebzadeh. (2025). The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Green Education: Optimizing Teacher Workflow and Enhancing Pedagogical Design under Sustainable Development Pedagogy (SDP) Constraints. 3rd National Conference on Green Industries Innovation, University of Mazandaran. https://conf.umz.ac.ir/gii

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired t-test on the SDP Workflow Compliance index (adherence to paperless mandates and digital tool integration) for the same 28 teams. Table 2 reports Baseline M = 3.00 (SD 0.45) versus SDP M = 4.83 (SD 0.47), t(27) = 16.53, p < 0.001, d = 3.50. The article reports this as the largest sub-index gain.

"the most pronounced design leaps occurred within the specific sub-indices of SDP Workflow Compliance (d = 3.50, Δ = +1.83) and Strategic Reflection (d = 3.40, Δ = +1.80)"
