Evidence¶

Francisco González-Gómez 2011¶

Francisco González-Gómez, Jorge Guardiola, Óscar Martín Rodríguez, Miguel Ángel Montero Alonso (University of Granada, Spain). (2011). Gender differences in e-learning satisfaction. FEG Working Paper Series. http://www.ugr.es/~biblio/biblioteca_ugr/bibliotecas_centros/economicas/index.html

q2 · i? · associational · r2

A t-test on global satisfaction ratings from 1,185 students on 27 e-learning courses at the Universidad de Granada found "female students give a higher average global rating to e-learning than male students (p-value=0.0196)". Table 2 reports male mean 4.08, female mean 4.18, difference -0.10, p-value 0.02. No effect size is printed.

"Results lead us to conclude that female students give a higher average global rating to e-learning than male students (p-value=0.0196)."
