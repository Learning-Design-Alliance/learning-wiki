Heng Zhang, Yuhan Liu, Meilin Jiang, Juanjuan Chen, Minhong Wang, Fred Paas. (2025). Emotional Artificial Intelligence in Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10086-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Cross-category comparison from the meta-analysis of 54 studies: Category 1 (integrated support, 17 studies) versus Category 2 (cognitive-only, 35 studies). The comparison contrasts "integrated support" with "cognitive-only support" across knowledge, perception, and affect outcomes; no effect size is printed for the difference itself.

> "Integrated support was found to be more effective for knowledge acquisition than cognitive-only support, but less effective for perception, with similar effect on affect. Evidence for emotional-only support is limited (two studies)."
