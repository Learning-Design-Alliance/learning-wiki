## Evidence

### Christina Luke 2016

Christina Luke, Christa M. Simone, Aubrey T. Francisco. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Summer Ed-Tech Programs. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8017846c-4099-4af3-92e4-462cd5d6262d

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Product usage data from the Kettle Moraine TenMarks pilot, shown in Figure 3; the report adds that usage declined from week to week, suggesting motivation dwindled, though active weekly users remained relatively stable.

> "students at Kettle Moraine engaged with TenMarks for an average of 54 minutes per week over 11 weeks in the summer, which is less than a third of the recommended three hours per week"

### Christina Luke 2016 (2)

Christina Luke, Christa M. Simone, Aubrey T. Francisco. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Summer Ed-Tech Programs. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8017846c-4099-4af3-92e4-462cd5d6262d

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Elizabeth Forward Amplify usage data shown in Figure 8; the report states these low rates of usage may have contributed to the lack of gains in student learning, and that logins decreased dramatically over the five-week program.

> "the amount of time using the tool each week decreased from close to 3.5 hours to just over one hour a week by the end of the summer"
