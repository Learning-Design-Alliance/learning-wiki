## Evidence

### Sayed Azher 2024

Sayed Azher, Aralia Mills, Jinzhi He, Taliah Hyjazie, Junko Tokuno, Andrea Quaiattini, and Jason M. Harley. (2024). Findings Favor Haptics Feedback in Virtual Simulation Surgical Education: An Updated Systematic and Scoping Review. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/15533506241238263

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's own qualitative synthesis of 51 included studies comparing haptic versus non-haptic virtual simulation conditions. The review reports that "34 studies had results that, overall, favored the haptic condition over non-haptic" with only 3 favoring non-haptics. No meta-analysis was performed due to heterogeneity.

> "34 studies had results that, overall, favored the haptic condition over non-haptic while the summative results of only 3 studies59,66,86 favored the non-haptic condition. The remaining studies either showed the two conditions to be equal/similar (n = 11)39,42,44,45,50,54,67,74,77,80,83 or had mixed results (n = 3)52,68,81"
