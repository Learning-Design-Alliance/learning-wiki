Descriptive statistics from the Findings section of the true-experimental study: experimental means 2.01 to 4.01, control means 2.07 to 2.7. The article reports the experimental post-test as "high category" and the control post-test as average category. Standard deviations of pre- and post-test were 3.47 and 3.72.
