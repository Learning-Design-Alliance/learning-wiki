Comment-categorization analysis across the 11 serial TBL classes, with Pearson's chi-square for trend across classes (74.36, p<0.001 in Table 2). The article states the compatible-comment proportion "increased moderately" while the incompatible proportion "decreased consistently".

> "During the 11 TBL classes, the proportion of comments that were compatible with the criteria provided to the students increased moderately, while the proportion of comments that were not compatible decreased consistently (Table 2)."
