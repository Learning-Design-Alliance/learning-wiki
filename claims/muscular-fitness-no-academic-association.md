## Evidence

### de Greeff 2014

de Greeff, J. W.; Hartman, E.; Mullender-Wijnsma, M. J.; Bosker, R. J.; Doolaard, S.; & Visscher, C. (2014). Physical fitness and academic performance in primary school children with and without a social disadvantage. Health Education Research, 29(5), 853-860. https://doi.org/10.1093/her/cyu043

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null multilevel-analysis result in the 544-child sample: muscular fitness, measured with muscular fitness tests, was not associated with mathematics, spelling or reading scores. The article reports "No associations were found between muscular fitness and academic performance." No effect sizes are printed for these null results.
