## Evidence

### Li Gengchun 2025

Li Gengchun. (2025). Understanding the roles of grit and well-being in L2 achievement among Chinese university english learners. BMC Psychology, 13, 576. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-02933-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary analyses in the same 343-participant sample tested demographic moderators. Gender (t-tests) and age and years of English learning (Kruskal-Wallis tests) showed no significant effects (p > 0.05) on any study variable. No effect sizes were printed.

> "Independent samples t-tests revealed that participants' gender did not significantly affect the two dimensions of grit, the four elements of well-being, and L2 achievement (p > 0.05). The Kruskal-Wallis test showed that their age and years of English learning did not significant influence the above variables, either (p > 0.05)"
