Mikko Kainulainen, Jake McMullen, and Erno Lehtinen (2017). Early Developmental Trajectories Toward Concepts of Rational Numbers. Cognition and Instruction, 35:1, 4-19. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1251287

A chi-squared test of grade level against the eight most frequent latent transition pathways (each taken by at least 2% of the sample, together 66% of the sample) showed significant grade differences, χ²(16) = 89.45, p < .001.

> "There were significant differences in the distribution of third, fourth, and fifth graders across the developmental pathways identified, χ ² (16) = 89.45, p < .001, N = 251."
