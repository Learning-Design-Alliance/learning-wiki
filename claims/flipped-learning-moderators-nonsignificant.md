`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator (homogeneity) analysis of the 28 datasets: subgroups were associate/undergraduate (n=19) versus primary/secondary/high school (n=9), article (14) versus thesis (14), and years 2016-2018; none reached significance.

> "According to the results of the homogeneity test in Table 5, there is no statistically significant difference between groups according to the level of education (Q=0.377, p>0.05), study type (Q=0.590, p>0.05), and year of the study (Q=4.598, p>0.05)."
