## Evidence

### Douwes‐van Ark 2026

Douwes‐van Ark, I. M. E., Fokkens‐Bruinsma, M., Deinum, J. F., & Korpershoek, H. (2026). University teachers' self‐efficacy beliefs. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70088

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate regression analysis on the UNITSS among 472 university teachers; the overall model was statistically significant (R² = .13, F(3, 468) = 23.31, p < .001), and "Years of teaching experience significantly predicted UNITSS".

> "Years of teaching experience significantly predicted UNITSS (β = .20, p = <.001). Informal professional development also predicted UNITSS (β = .27, p = <.001). However, formal professional development did not significantly predict UNITSS (β = .02, p = .71)."
