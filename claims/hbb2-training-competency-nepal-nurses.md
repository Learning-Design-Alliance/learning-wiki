## Evidence

### Gurung Rejina 2019

Gurung Rejina, Gurung Abhishek, Sunny Avinash K., Basnet Omkar, Shrestha Shree Krishna, Gomo Øystein Herwig, Myklebust Helge, Girnary Sakina, KC Ashish. (2019). Effect of skill drills on neonatal ventilation performance in a simulated setting- observation study in Nepal. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-019-1723-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational cohort study in a Nepali referral hospital; competency measured with a skill-based checklist and OSCE A and B after one-day HBB 2.0 training. The article reports "an average score of 12.73 ± 1.09 out of 14 (p < 0.001)" and a knowledge improvement "by 1.77 (95% CI: 1.24–2.30)". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Among the total of 60 nursing staffs, all of them were competent with an average score of 12.73 ± 1.09 out of 14 (p < 0.001) on bag-and-mask ventilation skill checklist."
