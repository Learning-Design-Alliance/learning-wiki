Salem Saleh Khalaf Ibnian 2010¶

Salem Saleh Khalaf Ibnian. (2010). The Effect of Using the Story-Mapping Technique on Developing Tenth Grade Students' Short Story Writing Skills in EFL. English Language Teaching, Vol. 3, No. 4. http://www.ccsenet.org/elt

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Within-group pre-post comparison of the 42-student experimental group over the three-month treatment period. Table 7 prints means of 30.91 (pre) and 36.83 (post), with "T value (11.517), η2 value (0.715)" — the printed effect size η² = 0.715 — and p = 0.000.
