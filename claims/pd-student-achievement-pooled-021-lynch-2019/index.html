Lynch 2025¶

q2 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r?

The review reports, citing its own prior work (Lynch et al., 2019), a pooled mean effect size of "0.21 SD" on student achievement for mathematics and science PD and curriculum improvement interventions — a small printed effect size.

"conducted a related meta-analysis of the student-level impacts of teacher professional development and curriculum improvement interventions in PK–12 mathematics and sci- ence, finding a pooled mean effect size on student achievement of 0.21 SD."

Related Claims¶

  • Prior meta-analyses report moderate positive pooled effect sizes of teacher PD on instructional practice and teacher knowledge outcomes — related
  • Mathematics and science PD interventions causally improve in-service teachers' knowledge and classroom instruction, with a pooled average impact estimate of +0.52 SD across randomized studies — related
  • Teacher coaching programs improve teachers' instructional practice by a pooled 0.49 SD across 43 causal studies — related
  • Teacher coaching raises student achievement on standardized tests by a pooled 0.18 SD across 31 causal studies — related
  • PD programs with larger impacts on teacher-level outcomes tended to have significantly larger mean impacts on student achievement — related
  • A practitioner review reports a meta-analysis of causal coaching studies as finding positive effects of coaching on teachers' instructional practice and student achievement, though one contrasting study found coached teachers no more effective — related
  • The Math Solutions video-based PD program showed limited impact on teacher knowledge and no impact on instructional practice or student achievement — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
