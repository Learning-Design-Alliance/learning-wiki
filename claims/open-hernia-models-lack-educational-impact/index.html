Evidence¶

Pelly T. 2021¶

Pelly T., Vance-Daniel J., Linder C. (2021). Characteristics of laparoscopic and open hernia repair simulation models: a systematic review. Hernia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8251686/

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review synthesis across the six open hernia repair models identified (silicon, animal and VR types); the review reports "fewer attempts to measure educational impact in the open hernia repair models than the laparoscopic models". The abstract states none of the six demonstrated educational impact.
