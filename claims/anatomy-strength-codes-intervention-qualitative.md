Chi-square test of independence comparing aggregated strength versus weakness codes between control and intervention groups within each instructional domain. The Anatomy domain showed "a significantly higher proportion of strength codes" in the intervention group (70.3% vs 39.3%, p = 0.014), while Special Tests (p = 0.55) and Video Format (p = 0.40) showed no significant differences.
