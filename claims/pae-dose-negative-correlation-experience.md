`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation across 250 consecutive PAE patients against number of patients treated. The article prints "Pearson’s r = − 0.240", r = − 0.269, and r = − 0.504 for DSA series, fluoroscopy time, and procedure time respectively, all p < 0.001.

> "Number of digital subtraction angiography series, FT, and procedure time decreased with increasing operator experience (Pearson’s r = − 0.240, p < 0.001, r = − 0.269, p < 0.001, and r = − 0.504, p < 0.001, respectively)."

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same retrospective cohort analysis. The article reports a moderate negative DAP correlation (r = − 0.396, p < 0.001) and separately reports ESD decreased with operators' experience (Pearson's r = − 0.334, p < 0.001).

> "Overall, there was a moderate correlation between DAP and the number of patients treated (Pearson’s r = − 0.396, p < 0.001)"
