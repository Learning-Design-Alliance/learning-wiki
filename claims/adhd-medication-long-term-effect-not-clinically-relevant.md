### Varnet Pérez 2025

Varnet Pérez, Tomás; Øvergaard, Kristin Romvig; Frigessi, Arnoldo; Biele, Guido. (2025). Long-term effect of pharmacological treatment on academic achievement of Norwegian children diagnosed with ADHD: a target trial emulation. International Journal of Epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyaf010

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the main analysis results: "bridges between 3.9% and 20.5% of the average gap between the grade eight scores of the ADHD sample relative to the general sample". The article notes this is a slight overestimation because the gap is calculated with scores of medicated children.

> "Informally, the estimated compatibility intervals suggest that medication initiation bridges between 3.9% and 20.5% of the average gap between the grade eight scores of the ADHD sample relative to the general sample"
