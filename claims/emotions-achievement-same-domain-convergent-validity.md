## Evidence

### Goetz et al. 2007

Thomas Goetz, Anne C. Frenzel, Reinhard Pekrun, Nathan C. Hall, and Oliver Lüdtke. (2007). Between- and Within-Domain Relations of Students' Academic Emotions. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.715

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM models including midterm grades as manifest achievement variables alongside latent domain-specific emotion factors (5 emotions × 4 domains; 542 students). The article reports that "the strongest relations were found between emotions and achievement scores referring to the same domain," and that positive emotions related positively and negative emotions negatively to achievement, indicating "high convergent and discriminant validity."

> "Concerning relations between latent domain-specific emotion and achievement scores, with few exceptions, the strongest rela- tions were found between emotions and achievement scores refer- ring to the same domain (see Table 2)."
