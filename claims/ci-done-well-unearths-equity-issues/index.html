Evidence¶

Valdez 2020¶

Valdez, A., Takahashi, S., Krausen, K., Bowman, A., & Gurrola, E. (2020). Getting better at getting more equitable: Opportunities and barriers for using continuous improvement to advance educational equity. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED606089

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview study of 21 California education leaders conducted in fall 2019. A leader stated that "if continuous improvement is done well, it actually unearths issues of equity," reflecting the strong belief among interviewees in its potential for advancing equity.

"For me, absolutely, con - tinuous improvement is a strategy, a tool, a mind - set, that has the potential to signifcantly change education if it's done well . . . We've seen that if continuous improvement is done well, it actually unearths issues of equity."
