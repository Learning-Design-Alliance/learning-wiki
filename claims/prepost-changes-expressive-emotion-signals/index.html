Hung-Yue Suen and Kuo-En Hung 2026¶

Hung-Yue Suen and Kuo-En Hung. (2026). An Interpretable Closed-Loop Intelligent Tutoring System for Multimodal Affective Feedback in Asynchronous Presentation Training. IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1109/TLT.2026.3693864

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive paired-samples analyses of facial, vocal, and lexical emotion patterns in the 30-day field study (N = 204); the article reports significance without effect sizes and characterizes the changes as suggesting potential regulation of emotional expression.

"Paired-samples tests revealed reliable pre–post differences across facial, vocal, and lexical signals, encompassing both negative and high-arousal expressions reflected in facial cues, vocal delivery, and word usage (e.g., facial anger and surprise; vocal anger and happiness; affectively charged lexical patterns; all p < .05)."
