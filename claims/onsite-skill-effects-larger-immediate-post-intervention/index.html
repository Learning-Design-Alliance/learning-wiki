Pumsopa 2025¶

Pumsopa, N., Jirapongsuwan, A., Kalampakorn, S., Siri, S., and Klainin‐Yobas, P. (2025). The Effectiveness of Community Nurse Training Programs to Improve Nurses' Competency in Community Settings: a Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis. International Journal of Nursing Practice. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijn.70090

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup analysis based on assessment points found a significant difference for onsite training, "with immediate post‐intervention showing a larger effect size (SMD = 0.51, 95% CI = 0.35, 0.67)", while hybrid training showed no significant difference between assessment points.

"For onsite training, nurses' skills varied significantly between the two subgroups (Chi2 = 4.76, p = 0.030; Figure 8), with immediate post‐intervention showing a larger effect size (SMD = 0.51, 95% CI = 0.35, 0.67). For hybrid training, the two assessment points did not differ significantly in nurses' skills (Chi² = 0.13, p = 0.720)."
