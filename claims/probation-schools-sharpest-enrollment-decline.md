School-by-school observational analysis of enrollment change 1995-96 to 1999-00 (Section I, Figure 2). Probation schools averaged a 20.6 percent loss; the report adds that "Neighborhood schools lost an average of 10.4 percent" and nearly every neighborhood and probation school declined.
