Predictors of students' academic achievements in allied health professions at King Saud University: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02525-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis across the 13 undergraduate programs in the cohort (Table 2) reported the highest GPA mean in occupational therapy ("4.3 ± 0.42") and the lowest in biomedical instrumentation ("3.7 ± 0.45"), with cohort GPA overall 4.17 ± 0.48.

> "The mean GPA, HSGA, APT and ACT scores the highest was in the occupational therapy program 4.3 ± 0.42, 97.2 ± 2.45, 83.7 ± 4.86 and 77.7 ± 5.67 respectively. However, the lowest score in GPA was in the biomedical instrumentation program (3.7 ± 0.45)"
