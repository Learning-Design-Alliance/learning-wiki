### Bao Xuemei 2026

Bao Xuemei, Liu Shuhan, Hu Xiaoxiao, Zhang Qinghua, Jiang Mei, Gan Yingting and Wang Yu. (2026). Development and psychometric validation of a health literacy competency scale for nurses, and its application in a cross-sectional survey: a methodological study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1978033

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Internal consistency analysis in the reliability subsample of 393 nurses. The quote reports the printed coefficients: dimension α "from 0.820 to 0.958" and total scale α of 0.958, exceeding the 0.8 threshold.

> "Cronbach’s α coefficients ranged from 0.820 to 0.958 for the five dimensions, with a total scale α of 0.958. Split-half reliability for the total scale was 0.827"

### Bao Xuemei 2026 (2)

Bao Xuemei, Liu Shuhan, Hu Xiaoxiao, Zhang Qinghua, Jiang Mei, Gan Yingting and Wang Yu. (2026). Development and psychometric validation of a health literacy competency scale for nurses, and its application in a cross-sectional survey: a methodological study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1978033

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Test–retest assessment with 81 nurses re-completing the HLC-N after a two-week interval. The quote reports the total-scale coefficient of 0.927 and dimension range 0.622–0.758; the article attributes lower attitude and knowledge stability to fluctuating work pressure and forgetting.

> "Test–retest reliability for the total scale was 0.927 (dimensions: 0.622–0.758), all exceeding or approaching the recommended threshold of 0.7–0.8"
