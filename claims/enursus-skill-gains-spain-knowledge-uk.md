## Evidence

### Álvarez-García C. 2019

Álvarez-García C., Álvarez-Nieto C., Kelsey J., Carter R., Sanz-Martos S., López-Medina I.M. (2019). Effectiveness of the e-NurSus Children Intervention in the Training of Nursing Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16214288

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Country-specific pre-post improvement percentages from the quasi-experimental time-series study. The article reports that "In Spain, environmental health skills improved the most (42.33%)" while UK knowledge led at 40.38%. These are descriptive improvement percentages, not standardised effect sizes.

> "In Spain, environmental health skills improved the most (42.33%), followed by knowledge (37.61%), and attitudes (22.96%). In the United Kingdom, environmental health knowledge (40.38%) improved the most, followed by skills (21.70%), and attitudes (11.06%)."
