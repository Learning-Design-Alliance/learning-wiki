McWayne 2016¶

McWayne, C. M., Melzi, G., Limlingan, M. C., & Schick, A. (2016). Ecocultural Patterns of Family Engagement among Low-income Latino Families of Preschool Children. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0040343

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Profile-form comparison across the two language subsamples in the LPA results and discussion. The Spanish sample's high profile (18% of participants) was marked by Supplemental Education and School Participation one standard deviation above the mean; the English sample's high profile (48%) by Supplemental and Foundational Education.

"Specifically, having high levels of school participation (along with high levels of supplemental education) was a differentiating feature for Spanish-speaking parents."
