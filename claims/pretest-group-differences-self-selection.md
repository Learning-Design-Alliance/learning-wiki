## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Experimental students scored higher on the pretest (mean 1.55 vs 1.17) and reported more science interest, environmental concern, and protective actions than controls. [→ Reichert et al. 2015](#reichert-et-al-2015)

## Evidence

### Reichert et al. 2015

Collin Reichert, Cinzia Cervato, Dale Niederhauser, and Michael D. Larsen. (2015). Understanding Atmospheric Carbon Budgets: Teaching Students Conservation of Mass. Journal of Geoscience Education 63, 222–232. https://doi.org/10.5408/14-055.1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Group-comparison t-tests on the pretest and interest/belief scales among the 176 LAS students. The article reports t(174) = 2.23, p < 0.05 for the pretest difference, plus Table II differences in science interest (t(174) = 3.59, p < 0.001), environmental concern, and actions taken.

> "There was a significant difference in performance on pretest budget questions between groups ( t(174) = 2.23, p < 0.05). Students in the experimental group (mean = 1.55, SD = 1.08) scored higher than the control group (mean = 1.17, SD = 0.98) on the pretest measure."
