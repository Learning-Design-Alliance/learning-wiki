Hertanti Nuzul Sri, Nguyen Nga Thi Thuy, Chi Do Thi Kim, Chuang Yeu-Hui. (2026). Psychometric evaluation of the Indonesian version of the confidence scale among primary healthcare nurses in dengue-related prevention practice. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09471-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Known-groups validity was assessed by an independent samples t-test comparing C-Scale-I total scores between guideline-familiar and non-familiar nurses in the psychometric sample. The comparison reached statistical significance (t = − 4.73, p < 0.001); no effect size was printed.

> "Nurses familiar with the guidelines had higher C-Scale-I scores (Mean = 20.05, SD = 3.45) than those who were not familiar (Mean = 18.47, SD = 3.52) (t = − 4.73, p < 0.001)."
