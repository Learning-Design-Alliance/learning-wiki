## Evidence

### Michael Batashvili 2022

Michael Batashvili, Rona Sheaffer, Maya Katz, Yoav Doron, Noam Kempler and Daniel A. Levy. (2022). Behavioural reconsolidation interference not observed in a within-subjects design. npj Science of Learning 7:26. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00143-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The general discussion summarises the three experiments reported here (associative recognition and two free-recall experiments), together with three prior item-recognition experiments, as showing no behavioural reconsolidation interference in within-subjects paradigms.

> "Nonetheless, provid- ing a reminder before introducing retroactive interference did not impair memory compared to simple interference in any of these paradigms."
