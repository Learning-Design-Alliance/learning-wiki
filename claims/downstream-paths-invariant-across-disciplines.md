## Evidence

### Tian Qi 2026

Tian Qi, Huang Yinpin, Xiong Jun. (2026). Psychological quality and entrepreneurial competence: the mediating role of professional recognition and moderating effect of academic discipline among Chinese university students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03954-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square difference tests in the multi-group SEM (n = 741) found no significant path differences: "the path from Professional Recognition to Entrepreneurial Competence (Δχ² = 4.894, p = 0.180) and the path from Psychological Quality to Entrepreneurial Competence (Δχ² = 5.414, p = 0.144) were statistically invariant across disciplines"; no effect sizes are printed for these null contrasts.

> "the path from Professional Recognition to Entrepreneurial Competence (Δχ² = 4.894, p = 0.180) and the path from Psychological Quality to Entrepreneurial Competence (Δχ² = 5.414, p = 0.144) were statistically invariant across disciplines."
