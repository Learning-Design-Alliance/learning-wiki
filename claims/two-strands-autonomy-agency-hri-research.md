## Evidence

### Glawe 2025

Glawe, F., Schmeckel, T., Brauner, P., & Ziefle, M. (2025). Human Autonomy and Sense of Agency in Human-Robot Interaction: A Systematic Literature Review. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22271

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review (PRISMA) of 22 empirical HRI studies published 2011–early 2024, identified via five databases. The review's synthesis reports that "separate methodological strands emerged" for the two concepts, with autonomy studied via psychometric scales and agency primarily via intentional binding.

> "Across both concepts, separate methodological strands emerged: Self-determination theory-based measures of autonomy via psychometric scales and neuroscientific measures of agency, primarily intentional binding, with little integrative work bridging the two."
