q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

In the dissatisfaction NMA (42 studies, 2921 trainees), ten interventions each showed significantly fewer dissatisfied students than LBL, with printed ORs and 95% CIs as quoted; students most disliked LBL (SUCRA 0.7%).

"PBL + Wechat (OR = 0.06, 95% CI [0.01, 0.27]), TBL (OR = 0.11, 95% CI [0.02, 0.52]), CBL + Wechat (OR = 0.13, 95% CI [0.06, 0.3]), CBL + FC (OR = 0.13, 95% CI [0.02085]), PBL + MDT (OR = 0.18, 95% CI [0.06, 0.5]), PBL + CBL (OR = 0.19, 95% CI [0.12, 0.3]), CBL (OR = 0.19, 95% CI [0.08, 0.44], PBL + EBM (OR = 0.2, 95% CI [0.06, 0.65]), PBL (OR = 0.24, 95% CI [0.12, 0.47]) and PBL + CP (OR = 0.25, 95% CI [0.11, 0.59]) have significant advantages"
