### Thompson Jessica; White Simon; Chapman Stephen 2020

Thompson Jessica; White Simon; Chapman Stephen. (2020). Virtual patients as a tool for training pre-registration pharmacists and increasing their preparedness to practice: A qualitative study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238226

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative theme 'Feedback' from the interviews. VP users found "the animated, individualised feedback was a particularly helpful addition" because it showed exactly what they had missed; NI users wanted personalisation, such as their own answer shown beside the model answer.

> "All pre-registration trainees reported that the immediate feedback provided at the end of each case study was useful. Those who completed the VP cases reported that the animated, individualised feedback was a particularly helpful addition to their learning"
