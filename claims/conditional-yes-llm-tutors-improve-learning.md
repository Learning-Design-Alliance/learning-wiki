`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of controlled human studies (Section 6.3), citing Kestin et al. 2025, Pardos and Bhandari 2024, Fischer et al. 2025, and Chung et al. 2026; the reviewed studies' designs are not described here in detail.

> "Several controlled studies show that LLM-based tutoring can improve learn- ing when assistance is pedagogically structured or adapted to learner needs (Kestin et al., 2025; Pardos and Bhandari, 2024; Fischer et al., 2025; Chung et al., 2026)."

### Sayantan Adak 2026 (2)

Sayantan Adak, Rima Hazra, Koyel Mukherjee, Digbalay Bose, Somnath Banerjee. (2026). Do LLM Tutors Actually Teach? A Process-Centered Synthesis of the Tutoring Loop. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.15692](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.15692)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Schmucker et al. 2024, that the Ruffle&Riley dialogue tutor raised engagement yet showed no significant short-term learning advantage over reading; equivalence was not tested.

> "positive interaction quality does not guarantee a learning advantage: Ruffle&Riley increased engagement without producing a significant short-term learning advantage over reading (Schmucker et al., 2024)."

### Sayantan Adak 2026 (3)

Sayantan Adak, Rima Hazra, Koyel Mukherjee, Digbalay Bose, Somnath Banerjee. (2026). Do LLM Tutors Actually Teach? A Process-Centered Synthesis of the Tutoring Loop. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.15692](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.15692)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports large-scale deployment evidence, citing two NBER working papers: availability of a capable tutor separated from productive use, and immediate success diverging from delayed assessment outcomes.

> "Long-term access to Khanmigo did not automatically produce sub- stantive tutoring interaction (Oreopoulos and Low, 2026), while delayed assessment shows that imme- diate success and durable learning need not move together (Oreopoulos et al., 2026)."
