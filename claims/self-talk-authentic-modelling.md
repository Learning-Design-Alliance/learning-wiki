## Evidence

### Stewart 2026

Stewart, T. M., McCluskey, G., Johnson, H., Fry, D., McAra, L., & Booth, J. N. (2026). Co-developing the Child and Adolescent Mentally Healthy Classroom (CAMH Class) intervention for use in routine teaching in secondary schools. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0359563

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative finding from the young person co-development workshop (n = 7) and teacher workshops. Overall, self-talk was perceived as feasible, acceptable and of recognised benefit through grounding in modelling realistic authenticity and whole-class design.

> "Young people noted that exaggerated positivity felt unhelpful and instead emphasised that modelling authentic and more balanced language with some realistic positivity, would have a greater positive impact on their mental health and wellbeing."
