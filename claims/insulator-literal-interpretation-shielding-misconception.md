## Evidence

### Joshua Bilak and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Joshua Bilak and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students’ Conceptual Understanding of Conductors and Insulators. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07390](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07390)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results for multiple-choice question 3 about an aluminum ball encased in a Styrofoam cylinder; both groups obtained less than 50% with no significant difference between them. Think-aloud interviews corroborated the literal reading of insulator.

> "Surprisingly, approximately one-third of the graduate students invoked the notion of shielding and believed that no force would be felt by the aluminum ball from the plastic rod outside."
