## Evidence

### Rupprecht 2017

Rupprecht, S., Paulus, P. & Walach, H. (2017). Mind the teachers! The impact of mindfulness training on self-regulation and classroom performance in a sample of German school teachers. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(4), 565-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.4.565

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Post-test AVEM engagement results in Table 3 show Cohen's d of -1.52 for willingness to work to exhaustion and -0.48 for striving for perfection. The article calls these "surprising results" as engagement decreased in the intervention group.

> "Changes in teacher engagement yielded surprising results. Teachers in the intervention group reported decreases in professional engagement in all four scales with two of them reaching statistical significance. At post -test, the willingness to wor k to exhaustion decreased statistically significantly in the intervention group, while it increased at the same time in the control group."
