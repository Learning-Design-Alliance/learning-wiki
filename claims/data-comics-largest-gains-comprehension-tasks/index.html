Evidence¶

Zirui Shan 2026¶

Zirui Shan, Vanessa Echeverria, Yuheng Li, Yi-Shan Tsai, and Roberto Martinez-Maldonado. (2026). Data Comics for Education: Evaluating Effectiveness, Benefits, and the Ethics of AI-Assisted Creation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02361

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

RQ2 analysis of the within-subjects comparison, splitting the 16 questions per condition by Bloom's taxonomy level. The retrieval contrast reached significance with "a moderate effect size (U= 1601.5,Z= 2.642,p= 0.033,r= 0.341)", an average increase of 0.156.

"For information retrieval tasks, participants achieved a significantly higher correct rate in the DC condition (median = 0.667, IQR = 0.333) compared to conventional visualisations (CV) (median = 0.667, IQR = 0.334), with a moderate effect size (U= 1601.5,Z= 2.642,p= 0.033,r= 0.341)"

Zirui Shan 2026 (2)¶

Zirui Shan, Vanessa Echeverria, Yuheng Li, Yi-Shan Tsai, and Roberto Martinez-Maldonado. (2026). Data Comics for Education: Evaluating Effectiveness, Benefits, and the Ethics of AI-Assisted Creation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02361

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Same RQ2 analysis, comprehension task level. The article reports DC "showing a large effect size (U= 1727.5,Z= 5.383,p<0.001,r= 0.695)", with data comics increasing the correct rate by a mean of 0.417.

"For comprehension tasks, participants also performed significantly better with DC (median = 0.667, IQR = 0.667) than with CV (median = 0.000, IQR = 0.333), showing a large effect size (U= 1727.5,Z= 5.383,p<0.001,r= 0.695)"

Zirui Shan 2026 (3)¶

Zirui Shan, Vanessa Echeverria, Yuheng Li, Yi-Shan Tsai, and Roberto Martinez-Maldonado. (2026). Data Comics for Education: Evaluating Effectiveness, Benefits, and the Ethics of AI-Assisted Creation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02361

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

RQ2 comprehension subtype analysis. For multiple-insight questions DC "showed an even larger improvement" over CV with a large effect; single-insight questions also favoured DC (r = 0.520, average increase 0.322).

"Formultiple-insightquestions, DC showed an even larger improvement (median = 1.000, IQR = 0.667) compared to CV (median = 0.333, IQR = 0.333), with a large effect (U= 1690.0,Z= 4.564,p <0.001,r= 0.589)"
