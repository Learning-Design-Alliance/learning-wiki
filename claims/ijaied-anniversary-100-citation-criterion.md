# A 100-citation Google Scholar threshold was used as the objective inclusion criterion for anniversary articles

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · review `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The editors set 100 Google Scholar citations as the inclusion criterion for selecting the journal's highly-cited articles for the anniversary issue. [→ Monique Grandbastien 2016](#monique-grandbastien-2016)

## Evidence

### Monique Grandbastien 2016

Monique Grandbastien, Rosemary Luckin, Riichiro Mizoguchi, Vincent Aleven. (2016). Preface to the IJAIED 25th Anniversary Issue. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0092-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

This is a design decision reported by the guest editors in the preface itself: to make highly-cited status objective, they "settled on 100 citations as listed in Google Scholar as our inclusion criterion". It is an editorial selection rule, not an empirical test.

> "Rigorous scholarly standards demanded an objective definition of what it means to be highly-cited! We settled on 100 citations as listed in Google Scholar as our inclusion criterion."
