### Nguyen 2018

Nguyen, T.D., Kramer, J.W., & Evans, B.J. (2018). The Effects of Grant Aid on Student Persistence and Degree Attainment: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of the Causal Evidence (CEPA Working Paper No. 18-04). Stanford Center for Education Policy Analysis. http://cepa.stanford.edu/wp18-04

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Authors' assessment of contoured enhanced funnel plots for persistence (Figure 7) and degree completion (Figure 8); the plots show a substantial number of non-significant effect estimates, so small studies appear not to be missing. This is the authors' interpretive analysis, not a formal test statistic.

> "Figures 7 and 8 both provide no evidence that our analysis lacks studies with small effect estimates; we observe a substantial number of studies that have effect estimates that are non-significant."
