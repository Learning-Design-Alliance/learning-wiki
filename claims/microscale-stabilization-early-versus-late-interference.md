## Evidence

### Marlene Bönstrup 2020

Marlene Bönstrup, Iñaki Iturrate, Martin N. Hebart, Nitzan Censor and Leonardo G. Cohen. (2020). Mechanisms of offline motor learning at a microscale of seconds in large-scale crowdsourced data. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0066-9

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2: participants (N = 118 early, N = 126 late, N = 129 no interference) practiced a target sequence and were interfered by a second sequence either immediately or 10 s after each practice period. Learning-curve modeling showed a shallower rise with early interference; the printed effect size of the group difference was "Cohen ’s d 0.15", and the effect on number of correct sequences was significant at P < 0.05.

> "The late interference group showed a higher learning rate compared wit h the early interference group (late: 0.26 ± 0.23, early: 2.15 ± 0.20, P = 0.04). The effect size of the group difference was small to medium (Cohen ’s d 0.15)"
