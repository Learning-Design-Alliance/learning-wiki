## Evidence

### Baracho Valéria da Silva 2020

Baracho Valéria da Silva, Chaves Maria Emília de Abreu, Lucas Thabata Coaglio. (2020). Application of the educational method of realistic simulation in the treatment of pressure injuries. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.3946.3357

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study with 77 nursing professionals in a Brazilian philanthropic hospital; knowledge measured with an adapted Pieper Pressure Ulcer Knowledge Test and analyzed with the unweighted Kappa index. The article reports the index "went from 0.56 (p=0.002) in the pre-test to 0.87 (p=0.001) in the post-test".

> "Regarding the knowledge of primary and secondary coverage techniques, the Kappa index went from 0.56 (p=0.002) in the pre-test to 0.87 (p=0.001) in the post-test."
