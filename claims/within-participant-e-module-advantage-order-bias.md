## Evidence

### Kwant Kelly J. 2015

Kwant Kelly J., Custers Eugene J. F. M., Jongen-Hermus Femke J., Kluijtmans Manon. (2015). Preparation by mandatory E-modules improves learning of practical skills: a quasi-experimental comparison of skill examination results. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0376-4

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Within-participant paired comparison of 332 students each tested on one text-based and one e-module station. The article reports the difference was significant, t = 2.48, p = 0.01, d = 0.15.

> "The within-subject comparison showed a station average of 3.50 ± 0.68 for the stations with a text based preparation and an average of 3.60 ± 0.66 for stations with an e-module preparation. This difference was significant, (t = 2.48 p = 0.01; Cohen’s d = 0.15)."
