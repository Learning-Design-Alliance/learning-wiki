## Evidence

### Mou Qiaoxing 2024

Mou Qiaoxing, Zhuang Jie, Wu Qunhong, Zhong Yaqin, Dai Qianqian, Cao Xin, Gao Yuexia, Lu Qingyun, Zhao Miaomiao. (2024). Social media addiction and academic engagement as serial mediators between social anxiety and academic performance among college students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01635-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (5,000 resamples) of the survey data found "the other specific pathway of social anxiety → academic engagement → academic performance was significant (effect= -0.067, 95% CI: -0.084 to -0.052)". The total indirect effect was -0.094 (95% CI -0.117 to -0.071), 49.2% of the total effect.
