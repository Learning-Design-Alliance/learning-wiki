## Evidence

Gulhiz Pilten. (2016). The Evaluation of Effectiveness of Reciprocal Teaching Strategies on Comprehension of Expository Texts. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i10.1791

Semi-structured interview with the single experiment group teacher after the 11-week implementation, analysed descriptively across pre-, while- and post-teaching dimensions (Table 11). This is one teacher's opinion, not a measured outcome.
