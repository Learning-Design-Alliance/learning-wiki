Policy analysis of Brazilian regulatory documents from the Conselho Federal de Medicina, Associação Médica Brasileira, and Colégio Brasileiro de Cirurgiões. The review reports that these regulations 'are not specific to cardiac surgery' and lack detailed criteria for volumes, competency assessment, and progression.

> "However, these regulations are not specific to cardiac surgery and do not establish detailed criteria regarding minimum procedural volumes, objective competency assessment, or structured progression of training."
