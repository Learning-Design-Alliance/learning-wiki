## Evidence

### Cetron 2026

Cetron, J.S., Hillis, M.E., Diamond, S.G., May, V.V. & Kraemer, D.J.M. (2026). Neural patterns reflect conceptual grasp of novice students following first class learning in physics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00394-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed-effects regression of individual neural scores on behavioral scores across 160 parcels, with permutation tests per parcel (1,000 randomized mappings). Significant parcels printed in the article include left ITG (β = 0.29, Z = 1.70) and left IPS (β = 0.28, Z = 2.08); the beta values are unstandardized model parameters, so no standardized effect size is asserted.

> "Six parcels were identified where observed beta values for individual neural score as a predictor of behavioral score was greater than 1.65 standard deviations above the permuted null distribution."
