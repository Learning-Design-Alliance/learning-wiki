### Menso Julia E. 2024

Menso Julia E., Rahimi A. Masie, Zwart Maurice J. W., Daams Freek, de Hondt Joey, Karadza Emir, Montorsi Roberto M., Nickel Felix, Bonjer H. Jaap, van Dijkum Els J. M. Nieveen, Besselink Marc G. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled crossover trial of 20 robotic novices randomized to start with SimNow robotic simulation (n = 10) or suture-pad dry-lab training (n = 10), each followed by crossover and a robotic hepaticojejunostomy exam. The "mean combined OSATS score was significantly higher in the robotic simulation-first group" (3.3 ± 0.9 vs 2.5 ± 0.8; p = 0.049), scored by two blinded raters.

> "In the second exam, the mean combined OSATS score was significantly higher in the robotic simulation-first group (3.3 ± 0.9 vs 2.5 ± 0.8, respectively; p = 0.049)"
