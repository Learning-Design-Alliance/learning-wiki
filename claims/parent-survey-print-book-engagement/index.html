Kim et al. 2019¶

James S. Kim, Catherine A. Asher, Mary Burkhauser, Laura Mesite, & Diana Leyva. (2019). Using a Sequential Multiple Assignment Randomized Trial (SMART) to Develop an Adaptive K–2 Literacy Intervention With Personalized Print Texts and App-Based Digital Activities. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419872701

q2 · i? · causal · r2

A small phone survey of 12 of 16 randomly sampled parents (75% response rate) conducted in Fall 2018. The article notes the survey is limited by sample size; combining app users and nonusers, parents reported reading on average 7 of the 10 books provided.

"the results indicate that parents read an average of 7.7 MORE books to their children and reported their children independently read 6.6 MORE books."
