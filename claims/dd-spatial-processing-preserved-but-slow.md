## Evidence

### Szucs Denes 2013

Szucs Denes, Devine Amy, Soltesz Fruzsina, Nobes Alison, Gabriel Florence. (2013). Developmental dyscalculia is related to visuo-spatial memory and inhibition impairment. Cortex, 49(10). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cortex.2013.06.007

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the case-control study, accuracy equaled between groups on the spatial symmetry and mental rotation tasks while solution times were longer in DD (mental rotation: DD 66.7 ± 4.4 sec vs Control 56.0 ± 3.5 sec; trail-making A: 58.3 ± 5.4 vs 41.3 ± 2.0 sec).

> "The accurate performance on the symmetry and rotation tasks suggests that spatial skills were available to DD albeit at a slower speed than to controls."
