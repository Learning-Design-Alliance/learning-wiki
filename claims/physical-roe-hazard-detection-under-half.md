The article reports, citing Zimmermann et al., an evaluation of the physical Room of Error across 13 Swiss hospitals as a low-fidelity situational awareness simulation; the review of prior work is described in the article's introduction, not re-analyzed here. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Zimmermann et al. [56] evaluated it as a low-fidelity SA simulation in 13 Swiss hospitals, where participants detected fewer than half of the embedded hazards – highlighting limitations in existing SA training. Interprofessional group interactions, however, enhanced error detection, stressing the value of collaborative components."
