### Inusah Salifu · Usman Kojo Abonyi 2023

Inusah Salifu · Usman Kojo Abonyi. (2023). Managing large classes in virtual teaching: experiences of university teachers in Ghana during COVID-19. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10151-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cross-case interview finding. In Case 4, participants P11 and P12, who had previous virtual teaching experience, claimed they did not face challenges, while P10, for whom virtual teaching was new, described difficulty adopting new technology and said training would have helped.

> "Interestingly, the participants whose threats included difficulty using virtual tools were those without virtual teaching experience before the COVID period."
