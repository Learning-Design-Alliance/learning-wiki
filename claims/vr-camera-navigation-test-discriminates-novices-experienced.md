## Evidence

### Yu Jiongbiao 2025

Yu Jiongbiao, Yang Yunran, Tang Lidong, Wang Wentao, Ma Jinhuo, Li Xiaowu, Zheng Wang, Lars Konge, Liu Wei. (2025). Using virtual reality simulation to ensure laparoscopic camera navigation skills of new surgical assistants—a validation study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12073-w

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-center validation trial comparing 12 novice medical students with 12 experienced residents, each tested twice on the LapSim camera navigation task. The study reports "average test scores ± SD for the novice and experienced groups were 51.8 ± 13.9 and 81.7 ± 6.0" with an independent-samples t-test at p < 0.001.

> "Validity evidence for relationship to other variables was established as the average test scores ± SD for the novice and experienced groups were 51.8 ± 13.9 and 81.7 ± 6.0, respectively: p < 0.001"
