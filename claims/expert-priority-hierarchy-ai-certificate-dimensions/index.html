AHP analysis of structured pairwise comparisons from 18 domain experts across Chinese universities, aggregated by geometric mean with eigenvalue-method weights. The results section reports the dimension hierarchy, with curriculum design at "weight = 0.3878" and cross-cultural adaptability lowest at 0.0735.
