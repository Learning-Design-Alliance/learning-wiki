Cost estimate reported in the executive summary. The report adds that Butterfly teaching staff cost £35-40 for a one-hour class and specialist training from Irina Tyk cost £600 for half a day; the overall cost rating was ££.

> "The cost of Butterfly Phonics as it was delivered in this evaluation is estimated at £108.50 per pupil. This estimate is based on the assumption that there are eight pupils in each Butterfly Phonics class, and includes the salaries of the Butterfly Phonics teaching staff, training costs and the cost of course books (about £10 each)."
