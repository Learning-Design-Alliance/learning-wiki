## Evidence

### Choi Kai Yip 2026

Choi Kai Yip, Chan Ka Wing, Yau Hiu Ching, Lau Yuet Yan, Leung Tsz Wing. (2026). Perceptual learning enhances diabetic retinopathy detection skills in novice optometry students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08691-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests on eye-tracking data (Tobii Pro Fusion, 250 Hz) from the PL group pre- vs post-training. At baseline the PL group directed significantly fewer fixations to relevant AOIs than the SS group (32.5% ± 12.7% vs 64.8% ± 27.9%, d = 1.38); post-training "fixation hits on AOIs significantly doubled" and normalized fixation duration also rose significantly.

> "Following the four-week training, the PL group's percentage of fixation hits on AOIs significantly doubled to 65.6% ± 23.4% (t(19) = 9.40, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 1.42), as well as the normalized fixation duration to 16.0% ± 6.8% (t(39) = 6.73, p < 0.001, Cohen'd = 0.97)."
