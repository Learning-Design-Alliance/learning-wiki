## Evidence

### Tsai 2020

Tsai, Yi-Shan, et al. (2020). Learning Analytics in European Higher Education – Trends and Barriers. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103933

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey and interview data from senior managers at 83 institutions in 24 European countries show "teaching and support staff are found to be the main users of LA and the target audience of training support".

> "we observe that LA is primarily perceived as a tool to enhance teaching and institutional management. As a result, teaching and support staff are found to be the main users of LA and the target audience of training support."
