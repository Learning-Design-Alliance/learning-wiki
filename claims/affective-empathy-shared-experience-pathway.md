## Evidence

### Alia Hamdan 2024

Alia Hamdan, Ash Bista, Dina Newman, Scott Franklin. (2024). A Conceptual Framework for Understanding Empathy in Physics faculty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020148)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview study, Results Section IV.B: analysis of faculty narratives (PM, Kali) found affective empathy relies on familiarity with an experience; experience acts as mediator between attention/noticing and affective empathy. The authors report it was challenging to elicit affective-empathy examples, possibly because interviews were personal or the protocol asked late.

> "Faculty members interviewed largely agreed that affective empathy is easier than cognitive empathy and often characterized it as a reflexive, automatic response triggered by personal familiarity."
