Evidence¶

Kaya 2016¶

Kaya, S., & Avci, R. (2016). Effects of cognitive-behavioral-theory-based skill-training on university students' future anxiety and trait anxiety. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 66, 281-298. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.66.16

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples t-tests comparing experimental-group posttest and four-week follow-up scores on all three measures; none differed significantly (e.g., t(14)=.343, p>.05 for PFEI). The article interprets this as effects that "continued for four weeks."

"the PFEI, TAI, and AMM scores of the experimental group showed no significant difference with the mean of posttest and follow -up test (t(14)=.343, p>.05; t(14)= -.733, p>.05; t(14)=.688, p>.05), respectively. These results indicate that the effects of the skill -training program continued for four weeks."
