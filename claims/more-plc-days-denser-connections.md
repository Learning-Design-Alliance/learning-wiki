## Evidence

### Eric Brewe 2012

Eric Brewe, Laird Kramer, and Vashti Sawtelle. (2012). Investigating student communities with network analysis of interactions in a physics learning center. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 8, 010101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.8.010101

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Visual interpretation of the Fig. 3 sociogram of self-reported collaboration ties among 99 PLC survey respondents, grouped by days per week in the PLC. The authors describe the pattern as tending toward greater density for frequent visitors.

> "the students represented with purple diamonds (6–7 days per week) tend to be farther on the left-hand side of the circle and tend to have a greater density of connections with other students"
