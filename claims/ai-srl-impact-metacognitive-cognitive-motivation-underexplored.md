`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review categorized direct impacts of AI on SRL into cognition, metacognition, and motivation (Schraw et al., 2006), finding "AI’s impact on the metacognitive aspect of SRL emerged as the primary area of focus in 73 studies. In contrast, AI’s impact on the motivational aspect of SRL was examined only in 20 studies". Some studies addressed one aspect only, others two or all three.
