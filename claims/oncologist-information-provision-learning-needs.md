## Evidence

### Stuij Sebastiaan M. 2020

Stuij Sebastiaan M., Drossaert Constance H. C., Labrie Nanon H. M., Hulsman Robert L., Kersten Marie José, van Dulmen Sandra, Smets Ellen M. A. (2020). Developing a digital training tool to support oncologists in the skill of information-provision: a user centred approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1985-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative focus group study: four focus group sessions (n = 13 across sessions of 5, 3, 3, and 2) plus three individual interviews, analysed by thematic content analysis. It found "the most prominent educational needs" were tailoring, structuring, and dealing with patients' emotions.

> "it should be highlighted that the most prominent educational needs perceived by the health care professionals included how to 1) tailor their information to the needs of the individual patient, 2) structure and prioritise information during a consultation and 3) deal with patients’ emotions."

### Stuij Sebastiaan M. 2020 (2)

Stuij Sebastiaan M., Drossaert Constance H. C., Labrie Nanon H. M., Hulsman Robert L., Kersten Marie José, van Dulmen Sandra, Smets Ellen M. A. (2020). Developing a digital training tool to support oncologists in the skill of information-provision: a user centred approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1985-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The same focus group study reported differential interest by seniority: "Oncologists in training, rather than senior oncologists, appeared to be most interested" in digital training; no effect size is printed.

> "Oncologists in training, rather than senior oncologists, appeared to be most interested in considering digital as opposed to more conventional face-to-face communication skills trainings"
