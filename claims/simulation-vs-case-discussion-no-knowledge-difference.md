## Evidence

### Couto Thomaz Bittencourt I 2015

Couto Thomaz Bittencourt I, Farhat Sylvia C.L., Geis Gary L, Olsen Orjan, Schvartsman Claudio. (2015). High-fidelity simulation versus case-based discussion for teaching medical students in Brazil about pediatric emergencies. Clinics. [doi:10.6061/clinics/2015(06)02](https://doi.org/10.6061/clinics/2015(06)02)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomized controlled crossover study of 108 final-year FMUSP medical students completing all steps; MCQ post-test and 4–6-month retention test compared between methods. Anaphylaxis post-test p=0.13 and retention p=0.19; SVT post-test p=0.09 and retention p=0.47, so "no significant difference was observed between the post-test and the retention test".

> "The mean scores for simulation versus case-based discussion were respectively 43.6% versus 46.6% for the anaphylaxis pre-test (p=0.42), 63.5% versus 67.8% for the post-test (p=0.13) and 61.5% versus 65.5% for the retention test (p=0.19)."
