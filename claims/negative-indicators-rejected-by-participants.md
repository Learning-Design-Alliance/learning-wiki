## Evidence

### Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario 2014

Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario. (2014). Involving Users to Improve the Collaborative Logical Framework. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/893525

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Layer 3 (modeling of the current state of the world) of the 2009 study: students were asked whether they agreed with the collaboration indicators computed by the system. The article reports "100% disagreement" for non-collaborative and unsecure and 66% for gossip, with other indicators accepted in more than 80% of cases.

> "there was discord on those aspects focused on indicators with a negative significance ("non-collaborative": 100% disagreement; "gossip": 66% disagreement; "unsecure": 100% disagreement) probably because participants do not accept to be characterized with adverse skills. The other indicators were accepted in more than 80% of cases."
