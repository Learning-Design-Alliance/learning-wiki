### Jörg 2007

Jörg, T., Davis, B. & Nickmans, G. (2007). Towards a new, complexity science of learning and education. Educational Research Review, 2(2), 145–156. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2007.09.002

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Conceptual argument in Section 4.1, which enumerates six properties of complex systems (superposition, substance-process synthesis, multi-layered multi-scaled organisation, reciprocal causality, distributed properties, and functioning self-images) and applies them to classrooms and formal education.

> "The assertion is that education is a complex system, with all the properties that are characteristic of a complex system, and that it is necessary for researchers, policy makers and educators to adapt their educational actions and decisions accordingly."
