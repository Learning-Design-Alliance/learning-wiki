Evidence¶

Kaplan 2015¶

Kaplan, A., Ozturk, M., & Ocal, M. F. (2015). Relieving of misconceptions of derivative concept with derive. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 1(1), 64-74. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105126

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental experiment group taught with Derive, one of the CAS software, working individually on computers; pre-test and post-test category percentages compared. Reported gains include a 63% understanding increase in the second attainment and a 69% increase in the sixth.

"According to the pre -test and post-test results, there was a decrease among the students who received computer assisted method for all attainments in the number of students who had misconceptions. In addition, there was an increase in the “understanding” c ategory for all attainments."
