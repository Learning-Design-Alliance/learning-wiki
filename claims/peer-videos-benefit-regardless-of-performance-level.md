## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hunukumbure Agra Dilshani 2017

Hunukumbure Agra Dilshani, Smith Susan F, Das Saroj. (2017). Holistic feedback approach with video and peer discussion under teacher supervision. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1017-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic interview analysis; students realised mistakes were not unique to themselves, which improved self-confidence. Better performances motivated emulation; worse ones offered mistakes to avoid.

> "Our study showed that observing peer videos had a positive influence on students regardless of whether it was a better or worse performance than their own."
