## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The apparatus records position-versus-time data that allow fitting an exponential decay envelope to extract the decay constant and oscillation frequency of a simple physical pendulum. [→ Vadas Gintautas and Alfred Hübler 2009](#vadas-gintautas-and-alfred-hubler-2009)

## Evidence

### Vadas Gintautas and Alfred Hübler 2009

Vadas Gintautas and Alfred Hübler. (2009). A simple, low-cost, data-logging pendulum built from a computer mouse. https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.4319 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0901.4319](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0901.4319)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Demonstration experiment with the authors' own apparatus: position-versus-time data were logged for a pendulum released from rest, and turning points were fit to an exponential decay envelope, obtaining "δ = 0.415 with R2 = 0.997 for the fit". The authors note this investigation would be "next to impossible using only photogates".

> "Fig. 3 shows a plot of position versus time data for the pendulum when released from rest ). The dashed line shows a fit of the turning points to an exponential decay envelope. For the pendulum, we obtain δ = 0.415 with R2 = 0.997 for the fit."
