## Evidence

### Thomsen JLD 2022

Thomsen JLD, Mathiesen O, Hägi‐Pedersen D, Skovgaard LT, Østergaard D, Gätke MR; the INVERT collaborator group. (2022). Improving neuromuscular monitoring and reducing residual neuromuscular blockade via e‐learning: A multicentre interrupted time‐series study (INVERT study). Acta Anaesthesiol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aas.14038

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interrupted time-series analysis of all departments combined; for the non-depolarising group the rate at intervention was 92.8% with no detectable post-intervention trend change. The succinylcholine-only group showed a pre-intervention trend of 7.5% per year (p < .001). The authors attribute the rising trend to years of national focus on the topic.

> "At the time of the intervention, acceleromyography was used in 92.8% of all cases involving non‐depolarising NMBAs and there was no detectable change in trend after the intervention."
