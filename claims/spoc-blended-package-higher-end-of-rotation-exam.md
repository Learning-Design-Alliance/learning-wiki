## Evidence

### Chen J 2026

Chen J, Cui J, Li X, Yang J, Yang X, Zhong H, Wang F, Gu Q, Duan C. (2026). Application of a SPOC-based blended teaching system in full-cycle training for medical imaging interns: A randomized controlled educational intervention study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000049908

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Department-specific end-of-rotation examination with theoretical and practical components, completed under standardized conditions; the practical component was independently scored by 2 blinded senior physicians with inter-rater reliability of 0.92. The "unadjusted mean difference of 4.63 points" favored the experimental group; Table 4 reports Cohen d = 1.44 for this comparison.

> "The mean score on the department-specific end-of-rotation examination was 90.87 ± 2.60 in the experimental group and 86.23 ± 3.73 in the control group, corresponding to an unadjusted mean difference of 4.63 points (95% CI, 2.97–6.30; P < .001)"
