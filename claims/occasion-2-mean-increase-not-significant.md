### Maria Araceli Ruiz-Primo 1993

Maria Araceli Ruiz-Primo, Gail P. Baxter, and Richard J. Shavelson. (1993). On the Stability of Performance Assessments. Journal of Educational Measurement, 30(1), 41-53. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/17453984

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA comparison of mean scores across the two occasions (Table 2) for 29 students. Means rose on Occasion 2 for all investigations and methods, but "these differences were not statistically significant" (both p > .05). No effect size was printed.

> "Although all three means for investigations and methods (observed and notebook surrogate) were higher on the second occasion (Table 2), these differences were not statistically significant for either observed or notebook data (MANOVA: F2.51.569, p \> .05; F251= 1.246, p \> .05)."
