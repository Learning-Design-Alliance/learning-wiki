## Evidence

### Pagerols M 2022

Pagerols M, Prat R, Rivas C, Español-Martín G, Puigbó J, Pagespetit È, Haro JM, Ramos-Quiroga JA, Casas M, Bosch R. (2022). The impact of psychopathology on academic performance in school-age children and adolescents. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-08242-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional regression analysis of 7036 school students (model 2, adjusted for all CBCL scales and sociodemographics) in Tables 2–4. Attention problems remained significant, e.g. first language OR = 1.28 (1.25–1.31), supporting that "students with increased levels of attention problems were the most likely to perform poorly on all academic subjects".
