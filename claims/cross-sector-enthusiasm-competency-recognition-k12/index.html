Qualitative findings section of a mixed-methods landscape study with listening tours and cross-sector design sessions in two communities reports that stakeholders across all groups "were all enthusiastic about shifts to competency-based learning and assessment models."

"Students, caregivers, educators, as well as postsecondary and workforce partners, were all enthusiastic about shifts to competency-based learning and assessment models and the use of technology to recognize and share skills."
