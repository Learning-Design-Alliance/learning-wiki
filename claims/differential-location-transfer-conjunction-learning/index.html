Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik 2024¶

Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik. (2024). Visual perceptual learning of feature conjunctions leverages non-linear mixed selectivity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00226-w

Sign-rank tests of transfer indices at near and far locations (3 and 6 dva) after training, comparing feature-wise spatial transfer. "color having larger TIs than orientation" at the near location (Experiment 1: p = 0.002, d = 1.1); the far-location contrast was not significant in Experiment 1.

Related Claims¶

  • Months after conjunction training, the global-feedback group showed significantly larger non-linear mixing on an XOR transfer task than the feature-specific group, though average XOR accuracy did not differ between groups — related
  • Global conjunction-level feedback yields faster and greater conjunction learning than feature-specific feedback in orientation-color conjunction training — related
  • Reaction times on the XOR task reveal a strategy difference: the feature-specific feedback group was slower on XOR relative to conjunction than the global-feedback group — related
  • Orientation learning with salient task-irrelevant color (Experiment 4) is smaller than orientation learning under global feedback (Experiment 1) or attended single-feature training (Experiment 3), but not smaller than feature-specific feedback (Experiment 2) — related
  • Under global feedback, learning reflects significant non-linear mixing of color and orientation, while the linear mixing term does not differ between feedback regimes — related
