The article's methodological argument for its ten-step workflow combining GPT-4o-generated inductive codes with iterative human review, refinement, and independent researcher coding for triangulation. No effect sizes are reported; this is the authors' evaluation of their own workflow.

Data preparation step: transcripts were segmented into topic-based files (approximately 324 to 5,758 words) to stay within the GPT-4o token limit of approximately 10,000 tokens per prompt, a strategy the authors say may improve LLM output accuracy.

"the full dataset was segmented into 17 topic -based files corresponding to specific WAT-C metrics and structured focus group activities"
