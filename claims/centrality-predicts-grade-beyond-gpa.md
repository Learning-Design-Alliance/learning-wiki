## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Bootstrapped multiple regressions show outdegree, indegree, and closeness centrality predict final grade while controlling for prior GPA, with full models explaining up to 47% of variance versus about 20% for GPA alone. [→ Williams 2017](#williams-2017)

## Evidence

### Williams 2017

Williams, E. A., Zwolak, J. P., Dou, R., & Brewe, E. (2017). Linking engagement and performance: The social network analysis perspective. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1706.04121](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1706.04121)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational bootstrapped multiple regression analysis of five survey-collected interaction networks from 73 first-year students, comparing full models (final grade ~ GPA + centrality) against the GPA benchmark. Full models explained "up to 47 % of the variance in course grade" versus about 20% for GPA alone; likelihood ratio tests confirmed the full models were significantly better.

> "the R2ad j values reveal that all these models predict future performance better than the benchmark does, explaining up to 47 % of the variance in course grade, compared to the benchmark's explanatory power of only about 20 %"
