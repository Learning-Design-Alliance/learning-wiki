## Evidence

### Mitra Nilesh Kumar 2015

Mitra Nilesh Kumar, Barua Ankur. (2015). Effect of online formative assessment on summative performance in integrated musculoskeletal system module. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0318-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-randomized post-test-only trial: group E (n = 65) took three online formative MCQ tests with automated feedback; group C (n = 102) took one supervised paper-based formative test with face-to-face feedback. Independent Samples t-test on summative MCQ scores showed "not significantly different" (t(165) = 0.254, p > 0.05).

> "However, the means score of the summative assessment in group E exposed to computer based formative test with online feedback was not significantly different from that in group C exposed to paper-based formative test with face-to face feedback [t(165) =0.254, p > 0.05]."
