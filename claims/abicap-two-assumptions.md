### Rismanchian 2024 (2)

Rismanchian, S., & Doroudi, S. (2024). Reconciling Different Theories of Learning with an Agent-based Model of Procedural Learning. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2408.13364](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2408.13364)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article's second, more speculative modeling assumption: constructive learning builds relational, conceptual knowledge modeled implicitly as edges between procedural nodes. The authors flag it as potentially an oversimplification.

> "When a learner practices a procedural skill constructively or interactively, they do not simultaneously learn other skills, but rather they can increase in their ability to connect the practiced skill to related skills."
