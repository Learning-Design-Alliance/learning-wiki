### Weimer Johannes 2024

Weimer Johannes, Ruppert Johannes, Vieth Thomas, Weinmann-Menke Julia, Buggenhagen Holger, Künzel Julian, Rink Maximilian, Lorenz Liv, Merkel Daniel, Ille Carlotta, Yang Yang, Müller Lukas, Kloeckner Roman, Weimer Andreas. `q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pairwise correlation analysis among subjective competency self-ratings in the prospective observational cohort (n = 141). The printed correlation between self-assessed ultrasound skills and topographical understanding is R = 0.53 (p = 0.005).

> "the self-assessment of ultrasound skills correlated significantly and positively with the self-assessment of topographical understanding at T1 (R = 0.53, p = 0.005)."

### Weimer Johannes 2024 (2)

Weimer Johannes, Ruppert Johannes, Vieth Thomas, Weinmann-Menke Julia, Buggenhagen Holger, Künzel Julian, Rink Maximilian, Lorenz Liv, Merkel Daniel, Ille Carlotta, Yang Yang, Müller Lukas, Kloeckner Roman, Weimer Andreas. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05608-7

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pairwise correlation between objective ultrasound skills/understanding results and tube-figure (visual-spatial) test results in the same observational cohort (n = 141). The printed correlation is R = 0.32 (p = 0.007).

> "The objective results of ultrasound skills/understanding correlated significantly positively with the results of the tube figures (R = 0.32, p = 0.007)."
