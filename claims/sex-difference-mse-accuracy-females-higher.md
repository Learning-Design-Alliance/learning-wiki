## Evidence

### Mikkelsen N 2025

Mikkelsen N, Larsen EB, Arnfred SM. (2025). Authentic Versus Simulated Patient Videos: Effect on Mental Status Examination—An Educational Randomized Controlled Trial. Academic Psychiatry 49(3):222–230. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40596-024-02111-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc two-factor ANOVA including allocation group and sex in the cluster-RCT (N = 190 tested). Table 2 shows mean MSE test scores of 10.3 for female and 9.3 for male students. Only F statistics and p-values are printed; no effect size, so no magnitude is reported.

> "female students demonstrated higher MSE accuracy compared to male students across intervention groups (F1,188, 6.294; p = 0.01), and particularly for one subtest video a marked sex difference was observed in the Limited group (F1,88, 6.765; p = 0.01)"
