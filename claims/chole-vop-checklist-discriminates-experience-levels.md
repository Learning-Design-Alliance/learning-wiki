## Evidence

### Kim Erin 2025

Kim Erin, Tchinde Marius J., Rooney Deborah M., Ngam Blessing N., Rettig R. Luke, Gross Christopher L., Snell Mark J., Barnard Melanie L., El-Hayek Kevin, Jeffcoach David R., Kim Grace J. (2025). A novel tool for assessing psychomotor proficiency in laparoscopic cholecystectomy using simulation-based training. Surgical Endoscopy 39(5):3386–3395. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11708-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis comparison of summed CHOLE-VOP checklist scores across three laparoscopic experience levels among 52 module users (novices n = 14, intermediates n = 18, experts n = 17). The article reports "P < 0.001" and describes "high practical effect, ɳ2 < 0.11" without printing a single effect-size value.

> "Novice users scored lower (M = 28.16 (SD = 7.62) than intermediate users (M = 31.33 (3.76), and expert users (M = 32.66 (3.43), P < 0.001, with high practical effect, ɳ2 < 0.11."
