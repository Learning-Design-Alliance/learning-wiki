## Evidence

### Hollands 2020

Hollands, F., & Escueta, M. (2020). How research informs educational technology decision-making in higher education: the role of external research versus internal research. Education Tech Research Dev, 68, 163–180. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09678-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study finding on internal comparison studies, reported by approximately one-sixth of interviewees. The article states the "level of rigor of these internal studies was often questionable" for the quoted reasons, and that subjects were not assigned to treatment and control conditions at random.

> "the level of rigor of these internal studies was often questionable because they relied on volunteer or "first-mover" faculty members who might not be representative of typical faculty members, did not include any form of pre-test"
