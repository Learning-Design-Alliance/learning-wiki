An analysis of educational needs to enhance the response competency of infectious disease control personnel to emerging infectious diseases. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.115

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group interviews with 34 personnel across occupational groups; qualitative content analysis found role-specific needs. The article concludes a "one-size-fits-all" model is ineffective. No effect sizes reported.

> "Infection control professionals required advanced simulation-based training with standardized scenarios, while nurses prioritized practical training on clinical procedures for critically ill patients. Administrative staff sought training to improve their understanding of hospital systems and facilitate interdepartmental collaboration."
