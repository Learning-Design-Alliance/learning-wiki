## Evidence

### Gaudet Andrew D. 2010

Gaudet Andrew D., Ramer Leanne M., Nakonechny Joanne, Cragg Jacquelyn J., Ramer Matt S. (2010). Small-Group Learning in an Upper-Level University Biology Class Enhances Academic Performance and Student Attitudes Toward Group Work. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0015821

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis of the SAGE entrance-exit survey data comparing seven term high achievers with all survey completers using an unpaired t test (p<0.05 reported, no effect size); addresses the Hitch Hiker concern that high achievers are frustrated by group work.

> "term high achievers' remarkable positive shift in attitude towards peer support over the term was significantly higher than the shift recorded from all students that completed the surveys (Figure 5c) (exit survey minus entrance survey; All students: +0.24±0.08; Term high achievers: +0.6±0.1)"
