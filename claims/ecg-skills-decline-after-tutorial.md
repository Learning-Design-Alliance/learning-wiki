## Evidence

### Rolskov Bojsen S 2015

Rolskov Bojsen S, Räder SBEW, Holst AG, Kayser L, Ringsted C, Hastrup Svendsen J, Konge L. (2015). The acquisition and retention of ECG interpretation skills after a standardized web-based ECG tutorial–a randomised study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0319-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised retention analysis with retention-tests at 2–4, 10–12, or 18–20 weeks; students had no tutorial access in between. The article reports significant declines in all three groups (p < 0.001 for all) with the printed means; no effect size is printed.

> "All three follow-up groups experienced a decrease in test score between post-test and retention-test: from 67.4 (SD 12.3) to 60.2 (SD 8.3) in the short follow-up group, from 71.4 (SD 12.0) to 60.8 (SD 8.9) in the medium follow-up group, and from 66.1 (SD 12.1) to 58.6 (SD 8.6) in the long follow-up group (p < 0.001 for all)."
