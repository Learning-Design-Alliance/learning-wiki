## Evidence

### Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition 2007

Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition. (2007). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED405428

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Exit-criterion finding for English language development from the same quasi-experiment, Appendix A4.2, 118 third graders, printed effect size 0.53, ns, improvement index +20. The WWC could not confirm the effect size because subgroup standard deviations were not reported.

> "At the end of third grade, 39% of students in the BCIRC group scored above the 40th percentile on the Norm-Referenced Assessment Program for Texas (NAPT) language test, qualifying them to exit their bilingual education program. By contrast, 21% of students in the comparison group met this criterion."
