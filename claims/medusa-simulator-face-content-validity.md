## Evidence

### Gmeiner M 2025

Gmeiner M, Schrempf A, Thurner T, Fenz W, Sabrowsky-Hirsch B, Giretzlehner M, Prückl R, Schaffelhofer S, Major Z, Lämmermann S, Baumgartner M, Drabauer L, Nagy J, Esposito G, Colombo E, Stroh-Holly N, Gruber A. (2025). Development and external validation of a mixed-reality aneurysm clipping simulator. Neurosurgical Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10143-025-03846-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

External validation study at the Zurich Aneurysm Clipping Course (n = 22) and the 11th Annual EANS Vascular Section Meeting (n = 18); 40 neurosurgeons rated a 32-item questionnaire on a 5-point Likert scale, with "mean scores between 3.13 and 4.25". Participants included 11 novices, 17 intermediates, and 12 experts.

> "We conducted an external validation with 40 neurosurgeons at two international events. Participants completed a 32-item questionnaire evaluating face and content validity on a 5-point Likert scale. Participants' surgical experience ranged from novice to expert (> 15 years). Average ratings for simulator realism and educational value were high, with mean scores between 3.13 and 4.25."
