`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire survey with 100% response rate (25 per group), 10 items scored 10 points each. On stimulating learning initiative, 24 of 25 experimental versus 14 of 25 control residents responded affirmatively (χ2 = 8.88, p = 0.002). Satisfaction with teaching content showed no significant difference (p = 0.247).
