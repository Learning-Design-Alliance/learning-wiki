## Evidence

### Isik U. 2018

Isik U., Wilschut J., Croiset G., Kusurkar R.A. (2018). The role of study strategy in motivation and academic performance of ethnic minority and majority students: a structural equation model. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-018-9840-3

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model of the cross-sectional SMS survey data (n = 618) with GPA and clerkship performance as outcomes; the model "had a good fit (df = 1, Chi square = 0.64, p = 0.43, RMSEA = 0.000 (< 0.05), and CFI = 1.000, > 0.9)". The printed path coefficient was β = 0.11.

> "Autonomous motivation was not directly related to GPA, but only via achieving strategy (β = 0.11, SE = 0.02, p < 0.001)."
