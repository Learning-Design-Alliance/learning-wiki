## Evidence

### Si Jihyun 2024

Si Jihyun. (2024). Exploring the responses of preclinical medical students and professors to flipped learning for the development of clinical reasoning. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2024.297

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Online survey after a flipped 'clinical reasoning method' course at one Korean medical school; 37 of 42 students (88%) responded; 10 Likert items plus open-ended questions; student survey reliability was Cronbach's α of 0.90. The article reports "the mean score for all items is 3.13".

> "For the student survey, the mean score for all items is 3.13. In response to the open-ended question about whether the course helped develop clinical reasoning skills, 25 students answered affirmatively"
