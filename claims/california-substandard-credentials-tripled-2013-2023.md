### Carver-Thomas 2024

Carver-Thomas, D., Leung-Gagné, M., & Jeannite, D. (2024). Tackling teacher shortages: What we know about California's teacher workforce investments. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/137.196

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Learning Policy Institute analysis of California Commission on Teacher Credentialing teacher supply dashboard data (2012–13 to 2022–23), plotted in Figure 2. The report states "the least prepared teachers are growing at the fastest rate" and that substandard credentials made up more than half of new authorizations in 2023.

> "Substandard credentials and permits tripled between 2013 and 2023 (see figure 2). further, this increase has been driven by a dramatic growth in provisional and short-term permits, which have increased more than eightfold since 2013."

### Carver-Thomas 2024 (2)

Carver-Thomas, D., Leung-Gagné, M., & Jeannite, D. (2024). Tackling teacher shortages: What we know about California's teacher workforce investments. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/137.196

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The report cites a 2021 study (second-hand attribution) on turnover among underprepared California teachers, noting interns also had higher turnover and per-teacher replacement costs of about $25,000 in a large district.

> "A 2021 report found higher turnover among underprepared teachers in California, with 40% of new teachers hired on permits or waivers leaving teaching altogether by the end of their third year."
