### Youjie Chen 2026

Youjie Chen, Xixi Shi, Xinyu Liu, Shuaiguo Wang, Tracy Xiao Liu, Dragan Gašević. (2026). Not All Students Engage Alike: Multi-Institution Patterns in GenAI Tutor Use. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00447

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the session-level clustering results, in which shallow engagement was 10.4% of the 113,255 sessions and exam-driven engagement (31.2% of sessions) "exhibited the least copy-pasting behaviors." The authors conclude students often seek broad learning support rather than simple answer extraction.

> "We show that shallow engagement, as commonly characterized by copy-pasting behaviors and direct answer requests, did occur but was not the dominant mode of GenAI Tutor use."
