## Evidence

### Ger Ebru 2023

Ger Ebru, Roebers Claudia M. (2023). The Relationship between Executive Functions, Working Memory, and Intelligence in Kindergarten Children. Journal of Intelligence. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10143737/

`q3 · i?`

Exploratory GLMM on trial-level accuracy in the N = 147 sample, chunking every six trials. Correct-response probability decreased in the mixed block "by 1% (p < .0001)" per trial-chunk but did not significantly change in the flowers block. Authors suggest fatigue in the longer mixed block as a common cause.

> "We found that, with each proceeding trial, the probability of giving a correct response decreased in the mixed block by 1% (p < .0001)1, while it did not significantly change in the flowers block despite showing an increasing trend of 0.8% (p = .11)"
