### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Teaching Assistants Using the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Summary analysis of the PCK task in which 25 graduate students selected the most common incorrect answer choice on each TUG-K item, scored by the fraction of introductory students choosing that distractor and compared to a generated random-guesser population via t-tests. The article reports they "performed better than random guessing on eight of these 17 questions" and no effect size is printed.

> "There are 17 questions on the TUG-K which uncover moderate (nine questions) or major (eight questions) introductory student difficulties, and the graduate students performed better than random guessing on eight of these 17 questions."
