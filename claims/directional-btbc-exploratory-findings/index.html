Evidence¶

Yafeng Pan 2022¶

Yafeng Pan, Suzanne Dikker, Yi Zhu, Cuirong Yang, Yi Hu and Pavel Goldstein. (2022). Instructor-learner body coupling reflects instruction and learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00131-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory analyses of directional BtBC: learner-leading showed a significant scaffolding advantage (0.15 ± 0.005 vs 0.13 ± 0.005); instructor-leading was trending (β = 0.02, p = 0.07). A separate SVR found no significant prediction of learning outcome from instructor-leading BtBC under explanation (r = 0.17, p = 0.42).

"For learner-leading BtBC (BtBC averaged across all positive lags), we observed a main effect of Instructional Approach (β = 0.02, SE = 0.01, t = 2.11, p = 0.047, R2 β = 0.17)"
