`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prototype development phase of a developmental research study. Authors report that LLM-based conversational AI with a RAG framework using a vector database supported dynamic history taking with avatar-based virtual patients, generating contextually appropriate responses in real time while preserving scenario fidelity through predefined expert-reviewed case data.

> "Integration of LLMs into virtual patient scenarios enabled realistic, context-sensitive medical interviews, facilitating interactive dialogue between examinees and simulated patients."
