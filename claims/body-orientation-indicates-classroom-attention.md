## Evidence

### Chen Yuqing 2025

Chen Yuqing, Li Jiawen, Liu Yixin, Jiang Fei. (2025). A Novel Student Engagement Analysis of Real Classroom Teaching Using Unified Body Orientation Estimation. Sensors (Basel, Switzerland). https://doi.org/10.3390/s25206421

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of a self-collected dataset of 10 complete classroom videos of about 40 minutes each, spanning kindergarten to university and multiple seating layouts, with faces blurred. The authors observe attention directions are "almost consistent with their body orientations". No effect size is printed.

> "From Figure 9, we observe that students' attention directions are almost consistent with their body orientations; thus, student engagement can be inferred from their body orientations."

### Chen Yuqing 2025 (2)

Chen Yuqing, Li Jiawen, Liu Yixin, Jiang Fei. (2025). A Novel Student Engagement Analysis of Real Classroom Teaching Using Unified Body Orientation Estimation. Sensors (Basel, Switzerland). https://doi.org/10.3390/s25206421

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case analysis of continuous course visualization (Figure 10, case B, minutes 14–16) in which the teacher moved from podium to student rows. The article reports students "hardly changed their orientation", interpreted as high engagement. This is the authors' interpretation of visualized data, not a tested result.

> "During this period, all the students hardly changed their orientation, demonstrating a high level of engagement, while the teacher's movement path showed attention to each row of students."
