### de Barros PHF 2024

de Barros PHF, Borges CS, Ferreira CPdC, Hernani BL, Abreu IPN, Tastaldi L, Cavazzola LT. (2024). Hernia 3D training model: a new inguinal hernia 3D-printed simulator. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2024AO0620

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-training questionnaire from a simulation exercise in which 28 of 30 invited surgeons performed a simulated TAPP repair and answered 5-point Likert items stored in RedCap; medians were used because "the sample size was small, and the results were not normally distributed". Table 2 shows medians of 4 (3–5) for realism, anatomy simulation, and ease of use.

> "The model was deemed easy to use, realistic, and anatomically precise, establishing it as a valuable supplement to minimally invasive surgery training."
