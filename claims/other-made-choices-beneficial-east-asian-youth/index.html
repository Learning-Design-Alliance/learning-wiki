Evidence¶

Bong 2026¶

Bong, M., Jeong, D., Park, S., Suh, J., Kim, S. G., Park, J. C., & Kim, J. (2026). Cultural Insights into the Motivation Paradox of East Asian Learners. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10140-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Iyengar and Lepper's (1999) experiments with Anglo-American and Asian American children on anagram performance under personal-choice versus other-made-choice conditions. The review attributes the finding that 'Asian Ameri can children solved anagrams best' under maternal choice, interpreting it as evidence that freedom of choice may hold less significance in East Asian cultures.

"Anglo-American children performed best and spent significantly more time on anagrams in the personal choice condition, where they themselves chose the type of anagrams to solve and the color of the marker to use. In contrast, Asian Ameri can children solved anagrams best and were most intrinsically motivated when they believed these choices were made for them by their mothers."
