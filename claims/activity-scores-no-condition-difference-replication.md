## Evidence

### Smit 2025

Smit, N., de Kleijn, R., Wicherts, J. M., & van de Pol, J. (2025). What it takes to tutor—A preregistered direct replication of the scaffolding experimental study by D. Wood et al. (1978). Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000965

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered ANOVA on activity scores (total operations per child, aided and unaided, correct and incorrect) in the randomized replication; the article reports F(3, 281) = 1.18, p = .318 and "BF = 13.6". All planned contrasts were nonsignificant. Similar control-condition means across studies suggest comparable coding granularity.

> "the number of child operations (activity scores) did not differ signiﬁcantly between conditions, F(3, 281) = 1.18, p = .318, with the Bayes factor showing strong evidence in favor of the null hypothesis (BF = 13.6)"
