## Evidence

### Matthes Julia 2025

Matthes Julia, Scherrer Vsevolod, Preckel Franzis. (2025). Need for Cognition Predicts Academic Interest Development but Not the Other Way Around: A Longitudinal Study of Secondary School Students. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.14262

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Autoregressive cross-lagged panel models (Model 1) on four waves of data from 922 academic-track students in Grades 5–7. The study reports that "previous NFC was positively related to subsequent academic interest" in mathematics and English at all points, and in German only at T2; the abstract prints cross-lagged β = 0.03 to β = 0.17.

> "For Model 1, in mathematics and English, previous NFC was positively related to subsequent academic interest over all measurement points. In German, previous NFC was only positively related to subsequent academic interest at T2."
