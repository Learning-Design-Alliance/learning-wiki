Evidence¶

Yao Gege 2025¶

Yao Gege, Zhang Junlong, Soh Kim Geok, Bai Xiaorong, Xiao Wensheng, Anuar Mohd Ashraff Mohd, Bao Lixia. (2025). Effective implementation of the Sport Education Model in physical education: A meta-analysis of participant and intervention characteristics. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331228

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of cognitive-dimension outcomes (11 cognitive indicators across included studies). The article reports "Effect size = 0.411" with 95% CI 0.015–0.807, Z = 2.034, P = 0.042, a small statistically significant effect.

"SEM had a small positive effect on students' learning outcomes in the cognitive dimension (Effect size = 0.411, P < 0.042)"

Yao Gege 2025 (2)¶

Yao Gege, Zhang Junlong, Soh Kim Geok, Bai Xiaorong, Xiao Wensheng, Anuar Mohd Ashraff Mohd, Bao Lixia. (2025). Effective implementation of the Sport Education Model in physical education: A meta-analysis of participant and intervention characteristics. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331228

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of non-cognitive-dimension outcomes (38 non-cognitive indicators, e.g., motivation). The article reports "SMD = 0.641" with 95% CI 0.265–1.016, Z = 3.345, P = 0.001, a moderate statistically significant effect.

"In the non-cognitive dimension, SEM demonstrated a moderate positive effect on students' learning outcomes (SMD = 0.641, P < 0.01)"
