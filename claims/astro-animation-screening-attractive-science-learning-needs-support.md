## Evidence

### Arcadias 2020

Arcadias, L., Corbet, R.H.D., McKenna, D., & Potenziani, I. (2020). Astro-animation - A case study of art and science education. [doi:10.1386/ap3_000018_1](https://doi.org/10.1386/ap3_000018_1)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

NEA grant-supported evaluation using surveys and interviews with scientists, animation students, and non-specialist audiences. The authors report that screening alone "has a visual impact" but that specific science learning benefits from added scientific presentation.

> "We also found that simply screening the animations on their own has a visual impact. However, for more specific science learning it is valuable to have some additional scientific presentation."
