## Evidence

### Dejonckheere 2016

Dejonckheere, P. J. N., De Wit, N., Van de Keere, K., & Vervaet, S. (2016). Exploring the classroom: Teaching science in early childhood. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 8(4), 537-558. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1109852.

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study with 57 children across four preschool classrooms; intervention group (n=27) received 15 inquiry activities over 7 weeks while controls (n=30) followed normal classes. The MANOVA on informative explorations showed a "main effect of group, F(1, 52) = 7.8; p<0.007, partial η²= 0.13".
