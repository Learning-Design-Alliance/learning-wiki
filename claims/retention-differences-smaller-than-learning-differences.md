## Evidence

### Pyle 1917

Pyle, W. H. (1917). Retention as Related to Repetition. The Journal of Educational Psychology.

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

This generalization is drawn from the author's own experiments and from reviewing prior work by Muller and Schumann, Ogden, Whitehead, Henderson, and Norsworthy, all cited in the article as pointing to positive correlation between learning speed and retention. The author states that "the differences in reten- tion of a number of individuals are not so great as their dif- ferences in time for complete learning."
