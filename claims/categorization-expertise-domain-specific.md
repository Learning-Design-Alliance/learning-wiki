## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Rethinking Tools for Training Teaching Assistants. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02946](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02946)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of a graduate student's self-reported analogy: like introductory students with mechanics problems, he struggled to connect graduate-level homework questions to class material, while his instructor connected them effortlessly. Interpretive, no effect size.

> "These comments are consistent with the fact that a graduate student may be an expert in the introductory physics material related to electricity and magnetism but not necessarily an expert in the material at the Jackson level course."
