Evidence¶

Linda Bobo 2009¶

Linda Bobo, Amanda Andrews. (2009). Using video feedback to measure self-efficacy. Journal of Instructional Pedagogies. https://www.aabri.com/journal-of-instructional-pedagogies/

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

One-way ANOVA with repeated measures on five self-efficacy trials from an intact cohort of eight athletic training students in a one-day pilot with no control group. The article reports "partial η2 = .826" for the significant pairwise differences and states the result showed weak support.

"The difference in means between trials one and four, one and five, and two and five for self-efficacy was .174, F(4,4) = 4.763, p < .08, partial η2 = .826. The result of these comparisons showed weak support of the research hypothesis."
