## Evidence

### Zheng Que 2022

Zheng Que, Cheng Yu Yan, Sonuga-Barke Edmund, Shum Kathy Kar-man. (2022). Do Executive Dysfunction, Delay Aversion, and Time Perception Deficit Predict ADHD Symptoms and Early Academic Performance in Preschoolers. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-022-00937-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression analysis predicting ADHD symptoms from Time 1 neuropsychological measures in 131 Hong Kong preschoolers. The authors conclude this "lent support to the proposition that time perception deficit is an important neuropsychological component in ADHD among preschoolers".

> "Time discrimination, in particular, was found to predict IA at both Time 1 (ß = 0.17, p = .05) and Time 2 (ß = 0.21, p = .05), and HI at Time 2 (ß = 0.26, p = .02), and remained a unique predictor of IA and HI at Time 2 when all tasks were considered."
