## Evidence

### Tokumine Joho 2025

Tokumine Joho, Yorozu Tomoko, Moriyama Kiyoshi, Suzuki Teruko, Okada Chikako. (2025). Outcome-based simulation training for ultrasound-guided central venous catheter placement: clinical impact on preventing mechanical complications. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06739-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of a hospital-wide nurse-recorded reporting system covering 24,915 placements from October 2008 to March 2024, calculated excluding PICCs. The study reports the rate "dropped to 1.2% in 2023" after the training program; no inferential test or effect size is printed.

> "Similarly, the incidence of mechanical complications decreased from 3.9% in 2008 to 2.2% in 2015, and after implementing the training program, the mechanical complication rate dropped to 1.2% in 2023"
