Random-effects multilevel meta-analysis (intercept-only model) of 440 studies, 731 samples, and 1,210 effect sizes with an estimated 15,436,793 participants, following PRISMA guidelines. The article reports "d = 0.083, 95% CI [0.060, 0.107]" with substantial heterogeneity (Q(1209) = 707,116.22, p < .001).
