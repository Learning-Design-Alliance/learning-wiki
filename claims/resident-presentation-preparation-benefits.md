## Evidence

### Horisberger Karoline MD 2022

Horisberger Karoline MD, Di Natale Samuela MD, Gutschow Christian A. MD, Clavien Pierre-Alain MD, PhD. (2022). Towards a Virtual “Global Academia of Surgeons”. Annals of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLA.0000000000005642

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-report survey of local presenters and advisors, part of the hospital-internal survey. The article reports that "All presenters declared to have learned a lot preparing the presentation", with knowledge refreshing (50%) and public speaking (42%) cited as best aspects. Self-report only, with no objective measure of presentation skill and no control condition; the article does not give the number of presenters or advisors.

> "All presenters declared to have learned a lot preparing the presentation. Half of the presenters (50%) stated that one of the best things about the presentation was refreshing their knowledge, and according to 42% the exercise to speak in front of an audience."
