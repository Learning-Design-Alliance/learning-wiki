## Evidence

### Joekes K 2011

Joekes K, Noble LM, Kubacki AM, Potts HWW, Lloyd M. (2011). Does the inclusion of 'professional development' teaching improve medical students' communication skills? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-41

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of year 5 OSCE totals (reduced sample n = 73 due to attrition) against early Interview Rating Scale scores. The article reports the inverse relationship only for traditional curriculum students; no relationship with year 3 OSCE results was found for either cohort.

> "A significant inverse relationship was identified between the Interview Rating Scale total scores in year 1 and the overall year 5 OSCE scores for traditional curriculum students (r = -0.36, p = 0.040, n = 33). No such relationship was found for professional development curriculum students."
