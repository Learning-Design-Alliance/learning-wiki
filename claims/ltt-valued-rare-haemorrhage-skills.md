## Evidence

### Swain Cara 2025

Swain Cara, Cohen Hugo, Routh Jennifer, Helgesson Gert, Rickard Rory, Karlgren Klas. (2025). Optimising the educational utility of live tissue training in trauma surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07846-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study (15 physicians, Framework Method analysis). Participants reported that LTT lets learners "practice technical skills, specifically rare skills associated with haemorrhage control" they lack clinical exposure to, trying procedures for the first time without risking human patients' lives.

> "Participants felt that LTT is beneficial for surgical learners to practice technical skills, specifically rare skills associated with haemorrhage control, which they are not otherwise exposed to in clinical practice, and lack confidence in executing."
