### Melissa Roderick 2013

Melissa Roderick, Vanessa Coca, Eliza Moeller, and Thomas Kelley-Kemple. (2013). From High School to the Future: The Challenge of Senior Year in Chicago Public Schools. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-challenge-senior-year-chicago-public-schools

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of coursetaking by school for 2006-09 graduates (N=50,053) shows seniors with the same college qualifications falling into Advanced Placement, Elective Heavy, or Vocationally Focused schedules depending on school; the report says this was "largely determined by what high school they attended."

> "Among students who are qualified to attend a somewhat selective college, the proportion of students taking AP varies from a low of less than 10 percent in some high schools to a high of nearly 90 percent."
