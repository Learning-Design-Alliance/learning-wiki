## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Some students maintained their own expectations about learning biology and resisted or misinterpreted reform activities, for example by diligently memorizing even the concepts and generalizations the reform intended them to reason with. [→ Hall 2011](#hall-2011)

## Evidence

### Hall 2011

Hall, K. L., Watkins, J. E., Coffey, J. E., Cooke, T. J., & Redish, E. F. (2011). Examining the Impact of Student Expectations on Undergraduate Biology Education Reform. AERA 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1105.6349](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1105.6349)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview analysis in the reformed course: students may reject reform activities as unhelpful and pointless, as in Ashlyn's diffusion example, or misinterpret their role, as when Ashlyn reported methodically memorizing the "concepts and generalizations."

> "Our interview data also indicate that some students have robust expectations about learning that impede the successful implementation of even well orchestrated reforms."
