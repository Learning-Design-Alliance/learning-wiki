## Evidence

### Mari G. Strand Cary 2017

Mari G. Strand Cary, Ben Clarke, Christian T. Doabler, Keith Smolkowski, Hank Fien, and Scott K. Baker. (2017). A Practitioner Implementation of a Tier 2 First-Grade Mathematics Intervention. Learning Disability Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714715

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Time-by-condition mixed-model analyses of fall-to-spring gains on the EN-CBM total score and the SAT-10 procedures subtest; both effects were positive but did not reach statistical significance.

> "Positive nonsignificant effects were found on the distal EN-CBM (p = .13, g = .34) and SAT-10 procedures (p = .71, g = .13) measures."

### Mari G. Strand Cary 2017 (2)

Mari G. Strand Cary, Ben Clarke, Christian T. Doabler, Keith Smolkowski, Hank Fien, and Scott K. Baker. (2017). A Practitioner Implementation of a Tier 2 First-Grade Mathematics Intervention. Learning Disability Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714715

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same time-by-condition analyses for easyCBM and a mixed-model ANCOVA (ProFusion pretest as covariate) for SAT-10 problem solving; both effects were negative and nonsignificant, and the ANCOVA was interpreted cautiously by the authors.

> "Negative nonsignificant results were found on the distal easyCBM (p = .49, g = −.21) and SAT-10 problem solving (p = .51, g = −.27) measures."
