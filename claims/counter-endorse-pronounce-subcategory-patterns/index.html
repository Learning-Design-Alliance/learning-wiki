Fine-grained descriptive analysis (Table 8) of heteroglossic subcategories, used because some categories were unevenly distributed, reported that "Endorse resources increased substantially in peer-feedback revisions" while declining in AI revisions; Counter rose in both conditions and Pronounce fell in both.
