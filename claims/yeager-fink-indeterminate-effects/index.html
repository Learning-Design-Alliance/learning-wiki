Growth Mindset Interventions 2022¶

Growth Mindset Interventions. (2022). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, NCEE 2022-006. https://whatworks.ed.gov

q4 · i? · causal · r?

RCT at a public university in Texas with incoming students blocked on SAT score, race and ethnicity, and gender; the WWC calculated effect sizes and p-values using the dichotomous method, characterizing effects as indeterminate because the mean effects are not statistically significant.

Growth Mindset Interventions 2022 (2)¶

Growth Mindset Interventions. (2022). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, NCEE 2022-006. https://whatworks.ed.gov

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Compromised RCT in a General Chemistry course at a private university, meeting WWC standards with reservations because excluding students who did not participate "compromised the integrity of random assignment"; the effect on final exam score was indeterminate (not statistically significant).
