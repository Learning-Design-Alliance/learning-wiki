## Evidence

### Tamayo Martinez N 2022

Tamayo Martinez N, Xerxa Y, Law J, Serdarevic F, Jansen PW, Tiemeier H. (2022). Double advantage of parental education for child educational achievement: the role of parenting and child intelligence. European Journal of Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/eurpub/ckac044

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Four structural equation models (Models 4–7) combining early and mid-childhood assessments tested whether parenting and child intelligence jointly, in temporal sequence, mediate the parental education–achievement association. All indirect effects were non-significant.

> "There was no mediated effect through the sequential paths of early child IQ and mid-childhood parenting practice or through the sequential paths of early parenting practice and mid-childhood child IQ."
