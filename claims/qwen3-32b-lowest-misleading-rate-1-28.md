Misleading rates were determined through binary expert scoring, defined as the proportion of medically inaccurate responses based on information not derived from clinical case reports. The "single-LLM Qwen3-32B baseline showed significantly higher misleading rates than the multiagent frameworks based on Qwen3-32B (adjusted P=.003)". All methods demonstrated misleading rates under 5%.
