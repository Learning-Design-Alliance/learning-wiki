Madeleine Eastwood 2026¶

Madeleine Eastwood, Harshith Narne, Joseph Hilby, Paul Denny, Ashish Aggarwal, and Amanpreet Kapoor. (2026). Guardrails or Roadblocks? Effects of Pedagogical Style and Context Awareness in AI Teaching Assistants for Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29995

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Post-survey perception data from the 2x2 randomized controlled experiment in an introductory Python course; Kruskal-Wallis H test across the four AI TA conditions found a significant effect on task-completion support, "𝜒 2(3)= 12.14, 𝑝=. 007". The Socratic + Full Context condition rated lowest (mean rank 48.63, μ=3.53) versus Direct + Full Context (76.85, μ=4.27).

"A Kruskal–Wallis H test revealed a statistically significant effect of condition on perceived support of the AI TA fortask completion (𝜒 2(3)= 12.14, 𝑝=. 007, 𝑁= 132)."
