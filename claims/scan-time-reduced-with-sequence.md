`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis of scan times in the randomized trial, analyzed with a generalized estimating equation across the four sequentially scanned eyes of two standardized patients; Table A5 prints sequence estimates of −154 and −147 seconds for second and fourth versus first order. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "There was a statistically significant reduction in scan time for the second-order scan eye (left eye of SP1) and fourth-order scan eye (left eye of SP2) compared with the first-order scan eye (right eye of SP1; Table A5. p < 0.001)."
