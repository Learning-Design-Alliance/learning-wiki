## Evidence

### Fullerton Dayna J. 2021

Fullerton Dayna J., Zhang Lisa M., Kleitman Sabina. (2021). An integrative process model of resilience in an academic context: Resilience resources, coping strategies, and positive adaptation. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0246000. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246000

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis (Maximum Likelihood) in 306 undergraduates with Big 5 personality, intelligence, and financial/living covariates retained; the article reports "the resilience resources factor strongly predicted well-being and adjustment (β = .62 and .49, p < .001)". The final model accounted for 58.1% variance in well-being and 38.7% in adjustment.
