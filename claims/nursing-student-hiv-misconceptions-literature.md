### Ngcobo 2019

Ngcobo, S.J. & Mchunu, G.G., 2019, 'Bachelor of Nursing students' HIV and AIDS knowledge in KwaZulu-Natal province: An evaluation study', Curationis 42(1), a1928. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v42i1.1928

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Narrative literature review attributing these findings to Bektaş & Kulakaç (2007), Abolfotouh et al. (2013) and Mahat & Eller (2009); the review also reports cited studies finding beliefs in quarantine of HIV-positive individuals and failure to correctly identify HIV and AIDS medication.

> "One finding from the studies reviewed, for example, is that some nursing students hold true that infection with HIV can be prevented by the administration of a vaccine, while others believe that a cure is readily available for the virus"

### Ngcobo 2019 (2)

Ngcobo, S.J. & Mchunu, G.G., 2019, 'Bachelor of Nursing students' HIV and AIDS knowledge in KwaZulu-Natal province: An evaluation study', Curationis 42(1), a1928. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v42i1.1928

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article reports this figure from studies it cites (Dharmalingam et al. 2015; Ouzouni & Nakakis 2012), noting students obtain HIV knowledge from varied sources including the Internet, magazines, friends, television and high school teachers, with problematic accuracy.

> "77% of the undergraduate nursing students surveyed for the current study also conveyed that they had not been taught anything regarding HIV and AIDS, or the treatment thereof, throughout the extent of their nursing training"
