## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries 2018

Alexandru Maries, Nafis I. Karim, and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Is agreeing with a gender stereotype correlated with the performance of female students in introductory physics? Physical Review Physics Education Research 14, 020119. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.14.020119

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis across two years of data from over 3300 Pitt and over 900 UC students in large introductory physics courses; the FCI gender gap was typically 10%–20%, while the CSEM gap was typically smaller, especially on pretests where scores were near random guessing (20% average).

> "in all classes regardless of whether they were algebra based or calculus based, or whether they were introductory physics I or II, large gender differences were found in our investigation between the performance of male and female students both on the pretest and on the post-test. The gender gap on the FCI was typically 10% –20%"
