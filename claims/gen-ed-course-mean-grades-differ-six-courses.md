## Evidence

### Nixon Ryan S. 2025

Nixon Ryan S., Bailey Elizabeth Gibbons. (2025). Comparing Academic Performance of Elementary Education Majors in General Education Science Courses. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0320137

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the full archival data set of 195860 grades across six courses. The omnibus test of course mean grades was significant, F(5, 195859) = 1142, p < .001, with a printed effect size of η² = .028 (reported as "h2 =  .028"), a small effect. The authors also report the lowest means in History and Statistics.

> "There is a statistically significant difference among the mean grades in these courses, F(5, 195859) =  1142, p < .001, h2 =  .028."
