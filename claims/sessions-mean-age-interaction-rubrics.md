## Evidence

### Panadero 2023

Panadero, Ernesto; Jonsson, Anders; Pinedo, Leire; Fernández-Castilla, Belén. (2023). Effects of Rubrics on Academic Performance, Self-Regulated Learning, and self-Efficacy: a Meta-analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09823-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Posteriori secondary meta-regression on standardized mean differences for academic performance, entering centered sessions, centered mean age, and their interaction (shown in Fig. 10). When mean age imputed was used, the interaction remained negative but was not significant (p = 0.677).

> "producing a statistically significant and negative interaction effect (B = -0.041, SE = 0.001, t = -62.1, p < 0.001, 95% CI [-0.05, -0.03]), which means that the relationship between the number of sessions and the standardized mean differences decreases as the mean age increases"
