`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Descriptive comparison of chat logs per condition (Table 5): control 3.959 words per message and 32.2 minutes versus treatment 5.716 words per message and 39.6 minutes; independent samples t-test, p <.001. No effect size printed.

> "Independent samples t-test revealed that treatment dyads exchanged messages with a higher average word count and also spent more time on the chat compared to control dyads ( p <.001), which suggests an increase in active participation."
