Evidence¶

Woo 2009¶

Woo, H.L. (2009). Designing multimedia learning environments using animated pedagogical agents: factors and issues. Journal of Computer Assisted Learning, 25, 203-218. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2729.2008.00299.x

q3 · i? · review · r2

Review reports Buisine and Martin's (2007) comparison of two modes of speech-gesture cooperation in animated agents. Redundancy outperformed complementarity on the three named measures; the review notes this behaviour is uncommon in human communication. No effect sizes are printed.

"Buisine and Martin's study found that redundancy is better than complementarity in terms of improving recall in verbal information, quality of presentation, and social perception of the agents"
