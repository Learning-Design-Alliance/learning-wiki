## Evidence

### Hauk and Matlen 2017

Shandy Hauk, Bryan Matlen. (2017). Exploration of the Factors that Support Learning: Web-based Activity and Testing Systems in Community College Algebra. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567766

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-report comparison of the study's 30 instructors (19 Control, 11 Treatment) after the practice semester. Treatment instructors reported increased technology ability while Control instructors did not; the difference was "not statistically significant (p = .12)". No effect size is printed.

> "Indeed, after a semester of practice, Treatment but not Control instructors in our study reported an increase in their ability to use technology for teaching mathematics. Though not statistically significant (p = .12), the difference was consistent with the conference participants' hypothesis."
