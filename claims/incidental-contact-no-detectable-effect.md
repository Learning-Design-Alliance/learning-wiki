## Evidence

### Custers 2010

Eugène J. F. M. Custers. (2010). Long-term retention of basic science knowledge: a review study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-008-9101-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review cites this to qualify its rehearsal account of between-discipline retention differences: students only incidentally confronted with disciplines such as biochemistry during clinical years will show losses like those after a true nonuse interval. No effect size printed.

> "it is worth noticing that there is no evidence that incidental contact with a domain—in the order of a few hours during an RI that is measured in months or years—has a detectable effect on retention (Bahrick 1984; Weitman 1964)."
