### Conlin 2019

Conlin, L. D., Kuo, E., and Hallinen, N. R. (2019). Nothing's plenty: The significance of null results in physics education research. Preprint. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020104)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Classroom experiment in five middle-school science classrooms randomly assigning students to GP or CV instruction over 7 class periods; the ramp posttest showed "no statistically significant association between condition (CV or GP) and answering correct" with χ²(1, N=100)=.17, p>.05.
