## Evidence

### Wilton Alexander Awil 2022

Wilton Alexander Awil, Pananwala Hasitha. (2022). Publication in the Australian medical student journal is associated with future academic success: a matched-cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03607-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable conditional logistic regression (R, clogit) among the 55 AMSJ authors and 110 controls. The quote reports AMSJ publication OR 2.56 (CI 1.22–5.39, P = 0.01); Table 4 also prints higher degree OR 3.75 (P < 0.001) and academic position OR 9.77 (P = 0.004).

> "A multivariable conditional logistic regression model was created to account for potentially confounding variables. AMSJ authors were found to be more likely to achieve PubMed® indexed citations independently of whether they attained a higher degree or faculty position (OR 2.56, CI 1.22–5.39, P = 0.01)."
