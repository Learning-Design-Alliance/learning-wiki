## Evidence

### Schneid Stephen D. 2022

Schneid Stephen D., Kelly Carolyn J., Brandl Katharina. (2022). Relationships between preadmission variables and academic outcomes for postbaccalaureate students in medical school. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10129-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three-group comparison (UCSD PBPM n = 25, other PBPM n = 34, no PBPM n = 329) of USMLE Step 1 scores and pass rates using one-way ANOVA with Tukey post-hoc test and chi-squared tests. The study reports significantly lower scores but "no significant differences" in passing rates; no effect size is printed.

> "Both groups of PBPM program participants scored significantly lower on USMLE Step 1 exam compared to students who did not participate in any PBPM program. However, no significant differences were found regarding USMLE Step 1 passing rates between the three groups and none of the UCSD PBPM students failed the USMLE Step 1 exam on their first attempt"
