## Evidence

### Walter L. Leite 2021

Walter L. Leite, Zeyuan Jing, Huan Kuang, Dongho Kim & A. Corinne Huggins-Manley (2021). Multilevel Mixture Modeling with Propensity Score Weights for Quasi-Experimental Evaluation of Virtual Learning Environments. Structural Equation Modeling: A Multidisciplinary Journal, 28:6, 964-982. https://doi.org/10.1080/10705511.2021.1919895

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Joint-effects moderation analysis from the third multilevel mixture model with IPWs at both levels; two significant interaction effects appeared in Table 10. The authors infer that high classroom engagement helped remediation students improve scores.

> "An interaction of 17.937 (SE=4.294, p < 0.001, Hedges g = 1.870) shows that in classrooms with low student but high teacher engagement (D2), students the pre-Algebra focused (C3) class performed much better than in classrooms with low student and teacher engagement (i.e., D1)."
