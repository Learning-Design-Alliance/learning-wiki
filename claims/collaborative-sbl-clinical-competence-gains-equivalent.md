## Evidence

### Cuyul-Vásquez I 2026

Cuyul-Vásquez I, Aravena-Páez H, Araya-Quintanilla F, Guinez-Molinos S, Núñez-Cortés R, Gutiérrez Espinoza H. (2026). Collaborative Simulation-Based Learning to Develop Competencies in Physical Therapy Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/85442

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial; 2×2 mixed ANOVA on 23 completers (12 collaborative, 11 individual). Main effect of time on MTCCP: ηp²=0.739, P<.001. Descriptive gains were +6.17 (collaborative) and +5.39 (individual) points, "demonstrating substantial pre-to-post improvement in clinical competencies across both groups."

> "The mixed ANOVA revealed a large main effect of time on MTCCP scores (F1,21=59.47, ηp²=0.739; P<.001), demonstrating substantial pre-to-post improvement in clinical competencies across both groups."

### Cuyul-Vásquez I 2026 (2)

Cuyul-Vásquez I, Aravena-Páez H, Araya-Quintanilla F, Guinez-Molinos S, Núñez-Cortés R, Gutiérrez Espinoza H. (2026). Collaborative Simulation-Based Learning to Develop Competencies in Physical Therapy Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/85442

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Same mixed ANOVA: the group × time interaction (the primary test of differential efficacy) was nonsignificant, ηp²=0.012, P=.61. Between-group post hoc comparisons at pre (d=−0.346) and post (d=−0.211) were nonsignificant after Bonferroni correction (both PBonf=1.00).

> "The main effect of group was nonsignificant (F1,21=0.595, ηp²=0.028; P=.45), and the group × time interaction did not reach statistical significance (F1,21=0.264, ηp²=0.012; P=.61)."
