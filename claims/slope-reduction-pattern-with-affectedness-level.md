## Evidence

### Gibbs 2019

Gibbs, L., Nursey, J., Cook, J., Ireton, G., Alkemade, N., Roberts, M., Gallagher, H. C., Bryant, R., Block, K., Molyneaux, R., & Forbes, D. (2019). Delayed Disaster Impacts on Academic Performance of Primary School Children. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13200

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Subpopulation analyses in Mplus using the Complex data command produced the comparative standardized beta weights shown in Figure 1 for each affectedness region. The article notes numeracy slopes "plateau between the 'moderate' and 'high' affected regions" yet remain significantly below the low region.

> "As we can see for the domains of reading and numeracy there is a pattern of reduction in the slope values with the increase in levels of bushfire affectedness."
