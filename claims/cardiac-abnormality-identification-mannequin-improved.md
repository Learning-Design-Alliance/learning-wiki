Mannequin evaluation of heart function and cardiac-abnormality identification before and after training (Table 4). For example, correct identification of left ventricular failure rose from '2 (7.1)' to '26 (92.9)' participants, and pericardial effusion/tamponade from 5 (17.9%) to 26 (92.9%), all p<10-3 as printed.

> "severe hypovolemia, left ventricular failure right ventricular failure, pericardial effusion/tamponade,homogeneous or heterogeneous, left ventricular contraction) before and after training during the practice of echocardiographic views by medical residents on the mannequin showed a significant improvement in all of these parameters (p< 10-3 )"
