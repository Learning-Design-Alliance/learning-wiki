Zvereva 2026¶

Zvereva, G., Guillén, G., Al-Msallam, S., Hamari, J., & Xi, N. (2026). Game-based learning for ethics: benefits, challenges, and dilemmas. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10678-z

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Challenge theme from the performance-phase analysis of post-game interviews. The article reports several participants "questioned whether such activities risk encouraging unethical behavior", and some who adhered to their ethical standards acknowledged they might act differently if repeating the game.

"In the absence of explicit cues distinguishing ethical from unethical choices (C10), some players failed to recognize the intended dilemma, leading not only to missed nuances but also to unintended interpretations."
