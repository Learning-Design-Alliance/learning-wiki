## Evidence

### Mwakanyanga Emelia T. 2018

Mwakanyanga Emelia T., Masika Golden M., Tarimo Edith A. M. (2018). Intensive care nurses' knowledge and practice on endotracheal suctioning of the intubated patient: A quantitative cross-sectional observational study. PLoS ONE 13(8): e0201743. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0201743

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of observed practice scores for 35 nurses by ICU training, education, and experience; "no significant difference in practice between those with ICU training and those without (p = 0.812)". No effect size printed; equivalence not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in practice between those with ICU training and those without (p = 0.812), across the three levels of nursing education (p = 0.356) and across the three strata of nurses' work experiences (p = 0.791)."
