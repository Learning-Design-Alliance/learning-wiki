## Evidence

### Willingham 2008

Daniel T. Willingham. (2008). Critical Thinking: Why Is It So Hard to Teach? Arts Education Policy Review, 109(4), 21-32. https://doi.org/10.3200/AEPR.109.4.21-32

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

In the same band-problem experiment, a hint naming the similarity increased solvers to 35 percent versus 19 percent without the hint. The article uses this to argue metacognitive strategies suggest what to do but do not supply the knowledge to do it.

> "when experimenters told subjects working on the band problem that it was similar to the garden problem, more subjects solved the problem (35 percent, compared to 19 percent without the hint), but most subjects, even when told what to do, weren't able to do it."
