Evidence¶

Ulla Hemminki-Reijonen 2025¶

Ulla Hemminki-Reijonen, Noha M. A. M. Hassan, Minna Huotilainen, Jaana-Maija Koivisto & Benjamin Ultan Cowley. (2025). Design of generative AI-powered pedagogy for virtual reality environments in higher education. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00326-1

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative descriptive needs analysis combining 30 meetings with 31 university teachers and 35 written ChatGPT ideas from educators, analyzed by inductive thematic analysis; the authors state the conclusion "is presented as three main categories" in the categorization matrix (Table 3).

"The conclusion is presented as three main categories: AI acting as character, Emerging tech- nology to assist students ’ learning,a n d Emerging technology to help the teacher."
