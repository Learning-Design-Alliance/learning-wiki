`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interview finding from the process evaluation: all eight teachers interviewed agreed the programme gave them the opportunity to think and plan ‘outside of the box’ and enjoyed theme-based teaching reinforcing links with ‘real life’. Qualitative, small-sample self-report.

> "All eight teachers interviewed agreed that SHINE in Secondaries gave them the opportunity to think and plan ‘outside of the box’ and they enjoyed teaching around a particular theme, which helped reinforce links with ‘real life’."
