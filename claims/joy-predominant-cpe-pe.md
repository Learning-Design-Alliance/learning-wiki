(2026). “I didn't want physical education days to come”: Emotional experiences in physical education of prospective primary education teachers. Learning and Instruction 104 (2026) 102382. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102382

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of written autobiographical narratives from 56 female prospective teachers; 45 participants addressed joy in CPE. Joy was “manifested through the heterogeneity of the content and, especially, through popular games and cooperative challenges”, with catharsis and free play also cited.

> "The most prominent emotion during CPE PE classes as expressed by prospective teachers was joy. In most cases, joy was manifested through the heterogeneity of the content and, especially, through popular games and cooperative challenges (contents)"
