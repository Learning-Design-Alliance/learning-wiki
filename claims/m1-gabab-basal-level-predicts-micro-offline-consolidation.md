## Evidence

### Pasquale Cardellicchio & Sara Borgomaneri 2025

Pasquale Cardellicchio & Sara Borgomaneri. (2025). Level of M1 GABAB predicts micro offline consolidation of motor learning during wakefulness. npj Science of Learning 10:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00299-1

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Experiment with 16 right-handed adults performing a sequential finger-tapping task with 10-s practice and 10-s rest blocks; at-rest pp-TMS indices were collected before learning. Bayesian correlation showed a negative LICI–micro-offline-T relationship ("R = −0.834; BF10 = 468"), i.e. "a lower level of GABA B neurotransmitter (disinhibition) have a better motor consolidation".
