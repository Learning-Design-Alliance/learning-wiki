## Evidence

### Liu W 2026

Liu W (2026) AI-peer integrated feedback in second language writing classes: exploring students' engagement and writing performance. Front. Psychol. 17:1871951. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1871951

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANCOVA on posttest behavioral engagement with pretest covariate in the quasi-experiment (experimental n = 61, control n = 61). Adjusted means were 3.94 versus 3.07, "mean difference = 0.87, 95% CI [0.59, 1.15]"; partial η2 = 0.28.

> "After adjusting for pretest scores, ANCOV A indicated a significant main effect of group on posttest behavioral engagement ( F(1, 119) = 47.15, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.28)."
