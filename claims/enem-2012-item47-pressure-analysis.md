# Enem 2012 item 47 (pressure–force–area) is difficult with good discrimination: about 44% correct, with most students knowing pressure must be reduced but fewer holding a clear conceptual explanation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` About 44% of students chose the correct answer connecting increased area to lower pressure, while 92% chose options mentioning reduced pressure. [→ Marta F. Barroso 2017](#marta-f-barroso-2017)
`q2 i?` The item's 3PL parameters are difficulty 0.8, discrimination 2.1 and pseudo-chance 0.23, with empirical data reasonably fitted by the model. [→ Marta F. Barroso 2017 (2)](#marta-f-barroso-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Marta F. Barroso 2017

Marta F. Barroso, M. S. O. Massunaga and G. Rubini. (2017). Brazilian National High School Examination: assessing student's learning in Physics. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1707.04181](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1707.04181)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Distractor analysis of NS2012-Q47 (Table 9): options B and E, which also mention decreased pressure, drew 33% and 17%; the article concludes "less than half of them seem to have a clear  conceptual explanation on this knowledge".

> "Circa 44% of the students choose the correct answer (A), connecting an increase in the area to lowering the pressure exerted by a constant force."

### Marta F. Barroso 2017 (2)

Marta F. Barroso, M. S. O. Massunaga and G. Rubini. (2017). Brazilian National High School Examination: assessing student's learning in Physics. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1707.04181](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1707.04181)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item characteristic curve for item 47 comparing the mirt 3PL model curve with empirical data from ten equal score groups (Fig. 8); the fit is described as reasonable.

> "The ICC shows that the item is difficult (item diffi culty 0.8) and its discrimination is a good one (discrimination 2.1). Also, pseudo-chance is very high, 0.23."
