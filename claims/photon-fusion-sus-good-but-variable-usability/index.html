Iwai Wataru 2026¶

Iwai Wataru, Duc V . Nguyen. (2026). Design and Implementation of a Real-Time Multi-Site Immersive Learning System Using Photon Fusion. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10325 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.10325

SUS questionnaire administered after the one-hour lab session with 10 participants using Meta Quest 3 headsets. The average was "69.7, which corresponds to a grade of B", with scores from 50.0 to 92.5. No effect size is printed.

"The average SUS score was 69.7, which corresponds to a grade of B, indicating good usability. However, scores ranged widely from 50.0 (D) to 92.5 (A), showing significant variation in user evaluations."
