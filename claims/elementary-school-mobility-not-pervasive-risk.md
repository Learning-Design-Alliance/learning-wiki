### Deborah Lowe Vandell 2021

Deborah Lowe Vandell, Megan Kuhfeld, Elizabeth Gershoff, Robert Crosnoe. (2021). Predictors and consequences of school mobility in middle childhood. Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology, 76. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/predictors-and-consequences-of-school-mobility-in-middle-childhood/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Overall conclusion of the prospective longitudinal study (N = 1094, ages 5.6 to 11.1) integrating the move-timing and cumulative-move analyses. The authors state mobility "did not appear to be a pervasive risk" while noting the scope limit that very high mobility rates could not be studied due to very small sample sizes.
