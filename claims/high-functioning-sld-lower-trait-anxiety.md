## Evidence

### Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa 2025

Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa. (2025). Relationships of trait anxiety, test anxiety, and academic performance of Chinese undergraduates with typical developments and high- and typical-functioning specific learning disabilities. Annals of Dyslexia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-025-00328-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANCOVAs controlling for age and gender compared trait anxiety across 134 typically developing, 54 typical-functioning SLD, and 51 high-functioning SLD undergraduates. The article reports "significant differences among the three groups, F(2, 234) = 5.86, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.05", with post hoc tests showing the high-functioning group lower than both others.

> "For trait anxiety, the ANCOVA, controlling for age and gender, revealed significant differences among the three groups, F(2, 234) = 5.86, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.05."
