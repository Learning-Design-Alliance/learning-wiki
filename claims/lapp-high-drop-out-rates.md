### Ulloa G 2022

Ulloa G, Neyem A, Escalona G, Ortiz C, Varas J. (2022). Remote asynchronous feedback for unsupervised laparoscopic training: the "Lapp" platform. ABCD Arq Bras Cir Dig. https://doi.org/10.1590/0102-672020220002e1712

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Of 420 recruited participants (288 group A, 132 group B), only 169 completed the course. The article states "54% of participants from group A and 70% of participants from group B were not able to complete the procedure" and calls this elevated drop-out rate a matter for future studies.

> "It is relevant to address the fact that despite the positive results presented, 54% of participants from group A and 70% of participants from group B were not able to complete the procedure."
