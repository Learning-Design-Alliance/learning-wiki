## Evidence

### Toru Ishihara 2021

Toru Ishihara, Noriteru Morita, Toshihiro Nakajima, Koji Yamatsu, Koichi Okita, Masato Sagawa and Keita Kamijo. (2021). Differential effects of changes in cardiorespiratory fitness on worst- and best- school subjects. npj Science of Learning 6:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00086-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson's correlation and multiple regression analyses in the 469-student longitudinal study; Table 2 reports β = 0.16, p < 0.001 for lowest GP and β = 0.22, p < 0.001 for highest GP. "changes in learning time were positively associated with changes in the lowest and highest GPs." No standardized effect size is printed.

> "However, changes in learning time were positively associated with changes in the lowest and highest GPs, whereas changes in cardiorespira- tory ﬁtness were positively associated with only changes in the lowest GP."
