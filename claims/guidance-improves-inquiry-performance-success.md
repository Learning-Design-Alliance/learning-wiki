## Evidence

### Lazonder 2016

Lazonder, A. W., & Harmsen, R. (2016). Meta-analysis of inquiry-based learning: Effects of guidance. Review of Educational Research, 86(3), 681-718. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315627366

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Random-effects meta-analysis of studies assessing performance success, defined as "what learners managed to achieve during the inquiry, as evidenced by the products they created throughout the learning process." The summary effect was d = 0.71 with a confidence interval excluding zero.

> "Results showed facilitative overall effects of guidance on learning activities (d = 0.66, 95% CI [0.44, 0.88]), performance success (d = 0.71, 95% CI [0.52, 0.90]), and learning outcomes (d = 0.50, 95% CI [0.37, 0.62]). Type of guidance moderated the effects on performance success but not on the other two outcome measures."
