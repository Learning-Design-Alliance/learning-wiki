# Peer rejection shows a significant negative correlation with language competence but no main effect on later language competence after controls

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Peer rejection correlated negatively with language competence, but the multilevel models showed no main effect of peer rejection on language competence once control variables were included. [→ Femke van der Wilt 2025](#femke-van-der-wilt-2025)

## Evidence

### Femke van der Wilt 2025

Femke van der Wilt, Jantine Spilt, Sharisse van Driel, Hilde Colpin. (2025). The moderating role of the teacher-child relationship in the association between peer rejection and language competence in early childhood education. Learning and Instruction 100 (2025) 102217. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102217

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the multilevel results in the N = 328 longitudinal sample: a "significant negative correlation" was found, but no main effect of peer rejection after controls; Table 2 prints the peer rejection T1 parameter at p = .219. No effect size is printed for the null main effect.

> "Outcomes indicated that there was a significant negative correlation between peer rejection and language competence, but there was no main effect of peer rejection on language competence."
