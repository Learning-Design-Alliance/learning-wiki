## Evidence

### Cárdenas-Gutiérrez 2021

Cárdenas-Gutiérrez, A. R., Bernal-Guerrero, A., & Montoro-Fernández, E. (2021). Construction and validation of the Basic Scale of Entrepreneurial Competencies for Secondary Education level. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0249903

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability analysis of the BSEC subscales and total scale computed on the validation sample; the article interprets these values as good, alongside composite reliability coefficients above .7.

> "The Cronbach´s α coefficients of the three subscales have ranged between.79 and.90. The Cronbach´s α for the whole scale reached a value of.82."

### Cárdenas-Gutiérrez 2021 (2)

Cárdenas-Gutiérrez, A. R., Bernal-Guerrero, A., & Montoro-Fernández, E. (2021). Construction and validation of the Basic Scale of Entrepreneurial Competencies for Secondary Education level. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0249903

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Test-retest stability study in which 50 subjects completed the scale at two measurement moments fourteen days apart; scores above .75 on the ICC were considered good by the authors' criteria.

> "The scale’s stability was appropriate, having an ICC = .92."
