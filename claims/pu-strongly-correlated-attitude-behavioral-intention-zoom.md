## Evidence

### Alfadda 2021

Alfadda, Hind Abdulaziz, & Mahdi, Hassan Saleh. (2021). Measuring Students’ Use of Zoom Application in Language Course Based on the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM). Journal of Psycholinguistic Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-020-09752-1

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of questionnaire responses from 75 Saudi undergraduate EFL students using Zoom during COVID-19 closures, reported in the Results and Discussion section. The printed Pearson correlations are r = .82 for PU with attitude and r = .81 for PU with behavioral intention: "a strong positive relationship between PU and Attitude (.82) and between PU and Behavioral Intention (.81)".

> "The results also show that there is a strong positive relationship between PU and Attitude (.82) and between PU and Behavioral Intention (.81)."
