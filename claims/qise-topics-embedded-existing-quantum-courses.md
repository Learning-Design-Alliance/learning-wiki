## Evidence

### El-Adawy 2025

El-Adawy, S., Piña, A. R., Zwickl, B. M., & Lewandowski, H. J. (2025). Insights from Educators on Building a More Cohesive Quantum Information Science and Engineering Education Ecosystem. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/tfmb-hnvz](https://doi.org/10.1103/tfmb-hnvz)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of 15 QISE educators; Results section on course development. Five educators discussed embedding QISE topics into existing quantum courses, e.g. adding quantum computing principles as applications within modern physics content — "a pragmatic entry point for educators".

> "A key strength in current QISE course development was the integration of QISE topics into existing quantum courses . This approach offered a pragmatic entry point for educators because it allowed them to introduce students to QISE concepts without needing to build entirely new courses from scratch."
