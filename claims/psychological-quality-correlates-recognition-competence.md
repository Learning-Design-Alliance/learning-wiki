## Evidence

### Tian Qi 2026

Tian Qi, Huang Yinpin, Xiong Jun. (2026). Psychological quality and entrepreneurial competence: the mediating role of professional recognition and moderating effect of academic discipline among Chinese university students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03954-3

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of cross-sectional survey data from 741 undergraduates at four public universities in Hubei Province, China. "Psychological Quality was positively correlated with Professional Recognition (r = 0.482, p < 0.001) and Entrepreneurial Competence (r = 0.586, p < 0.001)"; means were 3.58, 3.85 and 3.69 on 5-point scales.

> "Psychological Quality was positively correlated with Professional Recognition (r = 0.482, p < 0.001) and Entrepreneurial Competence (r = 0.586, p < 0.001), while Professional Recognition was positively correlated with Entrepreneurial Competence (r = 0.535, p < 0.001)."
