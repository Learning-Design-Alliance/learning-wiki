## Evidence

### Abenavoli 2014

Abenavoli, R. M., Harris, A. R., Katz, D. A., Jennings, P. A., & Greenberg, M. T. (2014). Mindfulness promotes educators’ efficacy in the classroom. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://sree.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/Abenavoli-et-al-SREE-2014.pdf

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis in the same 30-educator longitudinal sample found that "both subscales of the IMTS and the Observing and Acting with Awareness subscales of the FFMQ" predicted change in classroom management efficacy.

> "Change in classroom management efficacy was significantly or marginally significantly predicted by both subscales of the IMTS and the Observing and Acting with Awareness subscales of the FFMQ."
