# Teacher-reported mathematics instructional quality in pooled TALIS 2013 data from seven countries has three dimensions: classroom disciplinary climate, teacher support, and cognitive activation

## Evidence

### Xin Liu 2023

Xin Liu, Kajsa Yang Hansen, Jan De Neve, Martin Valcke. (2023). Teacher versus student perspectives on instructional quality in mathematics education across countries. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09652-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of pooled TALIS 2013 teacher data (12 items on teaching practices, classroom climate, and mathematics teaching approaches) from the rTPL database. CFA "identified: classroom disciplinary climate (DC-t), teacher support (TS-t), and cognitive activation (CA-t)" in the pooled seven-country data.

> "Building on the pooled data of the seven countries, three dimensions of mathematical instructional quality could be identified: classroom disciplinary climate (DC-t), teacher support (TS-t), and cognitive activation (CA-t)."

### Xin Liu 2023 (2)

Xin Liu, Kajsa Yang Hansen, Jan De Neve, Martin Valcke. (2023). Teacher versus student perspectives on instructional quality in mathematics education across countries. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09652-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-fit indices for the pooled three-factor teacher model estimated with MLR in Mplus 8.3. The article reports the model "fits the data well, with CFI = 0.99, RMSEA = 0.02, SRMR = 0.03".

> "This model fits the data well, with CFI = 0.99, RMSEA = 0.02, SRMR = 0.03, and Chi-square = 90.86 with 51 df."
