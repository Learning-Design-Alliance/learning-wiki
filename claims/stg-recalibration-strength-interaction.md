## Evidence

### Romanovska 2022

Romanovska, L., Janssen, R. & Bonte, M. (2022). Longitudinal changes in cortical responses to letter-speech sound stimuli in 8–11 year-old children. npj Science of Learning 7:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00118-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc mixed regression in the same longitudinal fMRI sample. The session × recalibration effect interaction was significant (F(2,17) = 5.808, p < 0.05) in the left STG cluster; the authors link it to emergence of the recalibration effect across sessions.

> "regardless of age, children who show a (stronger) recalibration effect across measurement sessions, show more activation in the left STG cluster during session 2 compared to session 3 (mean difference = 0.603, t(12) = 2.791, p < 0.05)."
