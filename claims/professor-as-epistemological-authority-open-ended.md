## Evidence

### O. I. González-Peña 2021

O. I. González-Peña, G. Morán-Soto, R. Rodríguez-Masegosa, and B. M. Rodríguez-Lara. (2021). Effects of a thermal inversion experiment on STEM students learning and application of damped harmonic motion. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.3390/su13020919](https://doi.org/10.3390/su13020919)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Questionnaire responses from the same cohort of 26 students showed "Most students (20) reported that they asked for their professor’s help during the designing and development of their thermal inversion experiment"; only one student completed it without asking for help. The authors interpret this as searching for an epistemological authority.
