## Evidence

### Spielmann V 2026

Spielmann V, Ebinger M, Jaki C. (2026). Development of a digital application to train and debrief situational awareness in interprofessional teams: A simulation-based approach to mitigate patient safety hazards with a virtual room of error. GMS J Med Educ 43(2):Doc24. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume43/zma001818

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory post-training survey during a demonstration session with five final-year medical students and nine Health and Nursing Science students (n=14) in three interprofessional teams, evaluated via the Kirkpatrick model. The article reports "86% found the embedded hazards relevant" and unanimous perceived benefit from interprofessional exchange; no effect sizes were printed.

> "All participants reported benefiting from interprofessional exchange, and 86% found the embedded hazards relevant to their professional context. Over two-thirds strongly agreed the training was educational, and most would recommend the format."
