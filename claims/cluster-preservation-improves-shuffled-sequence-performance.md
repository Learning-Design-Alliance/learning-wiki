`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Transfer test in the human sequence-learning experiment: learned sequences were split into three segments with clusters preserved or destroyed and then shuffled. Preserved-cluster performance was "more accurate and quicker" than destroyed-cluster performance. No effect size is printed.

> "We then split a learned sequence into three segments, while preserving or destroying the clusters in the learned sequence, and shuffled the segments.
