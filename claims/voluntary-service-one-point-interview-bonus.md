OLS regressions for the overall interview grade and four conversation categories (Table 3), controlling for Abitur grade, waiting time, and year fixed effects. The voluntary-service coefficient was statistically significant (p<0.05) in all five specifications; the article prints coefficients of approximately 0.88 to 1.16 points.
