## Evidence

### Ole Eggers Bjaelde 2015

Ole Eggers Bjaelde, Mads Kock Pedersen, Jacob Sherson. (2015). Gamification of Quantum Mechanics Teaching. E-Learn 2014 - New Orleans, LA, United States, October 27-30, 2014. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1506.08128](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1506.08128)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Binned analysis of the pretest/posttest data from the same 64-student gamified intervention. The printed binned gains are 0.33, 0.18 and 0.05; a check of individual answers showed low scorers "answered the same questions correctly in the pretest and posttest", suggesting they learned new content rather than guessing randomly.

> "students with 0-3 correct in the pretest score a gain of 0.33, students with 4-6 correct in pretest score a gain of 0.18, while students with 7-9 correct in pretest score a gain of 0.05."
