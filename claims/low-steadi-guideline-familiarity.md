Tool-usage item in the cross-sectional survey (N = 384). The Berg Balance Scale (55.5%) and TUG test (52.3%) dominated, while the CDC-endorsed STEADI toolkit was used by only 10.2% (n = 39); 40.6% also reported unfamiliarity with formal guidelines.

> "Notably, only 10.2% (n = 39) of respondents reported using the Stopping Elderly Accidents, Deaths & Injuries (STEADI) toolkit, a comprehensive evidence-based resource endorsed by the CDC for fall risk screening and intervention."
