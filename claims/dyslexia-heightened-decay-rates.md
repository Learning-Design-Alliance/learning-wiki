## Evidence

### Gabay 2025

Gabay, Y., Jacob, L., Mansour, A. & Hertz, U. (2025). Computational markers show specific deficits for dyslexia and ADHD in complex learning settings. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00323-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Computational modeling of choice sequences with the hybrid reinforcement learning/Bayes model in the dyslexia comparison. The decay rate was higher in the dyslexia group on the No-Explicit-Assist task; Table 1 prints control 0.38 (0.14) vs dyslexia 0.52 (0.19), t = −2.75, p = 0.004, significant after Bonferroni correction. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "We observed a signiﬁcant difference in the decay rate parameter between the two groups on the No-Explicit Assisttask, such that the parameter was higher in the dyslexia group (see Table1). This parameter reﬂects the rapid diminishment of the inﬂuence of past experiences or learned information over time."
