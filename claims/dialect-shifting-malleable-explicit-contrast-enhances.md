### Thomas-Tate et al. 2013

Shurita Thomas-Tate, Carol McDonald Connor, & Lakeisha Johnson. (2013). Design Experiments: Developing and Testing an Intervention for Elementary School-age Students who use Non-mainstream American English Dialects. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563056

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' conclusions from the randomized design experiment (n = 116). The two stated findings are malleability of dialect shifting and an advantage for explicit contrast; the latter rests on the Dialect Awareness group's "significantly better outcomes on all post-intervention measures". No effect sizes printed.

> "First dialect shifting is malleable. Overall, students in both treatment conditions demonstrated greater knowledge and use of three MAE/NMAE contrastive features in written tasks than students in the control group. Second, explicitly contrasting home and school language enhanced dialect-shifting instruction"
