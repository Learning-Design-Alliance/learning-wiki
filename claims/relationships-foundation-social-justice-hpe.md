## Evidence

### Gerdin Göran 2020

Gerdin Göran, Larsson Lena, Schenker Katarina, Linnér Susanne, Mordal Moen Kjersti, Westlie Knut, Smith Wayne, Philpot Rod. (2020). Social Justice Pedagogies in School Health and Physical Education—Building Relationships, Teaching for Social Cohesion and Addressing Social Inequities. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17186904

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 20 HPE lesson observations and post-lesson stimulated recall interviews with 13 teachers in Sweden, Norway, and New Zealand. The article reports that "All teacher participants recognised the importance of building relationships" and that teachers used caring strategies such as knowing names and families.

> "All teacher participants recognised the importance of building relationships. Many of them talked about the importance of getting to know their student(s)."
