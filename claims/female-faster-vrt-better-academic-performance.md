### Prabu Kumar Archana 2020

Prabu Kumar Archana, Omprakash Abirami, Kuppusamy Maheshkumar, K.N. Maruthy, B.W.C. Sathiyasekaran, P.V. Vijayaraghavan, Ramaswamy Padmavathi. (2020). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Student's t-test comparison between the 345 female and 273 male participants in the cross-sectional sample showed females' VRT mean of 243.97 msec versus 274.86 msec for males, and academic performance means of 56.16% versus 48.02%.

> "Female students (n = 345) had a faster VRT (mean = 243.97, SD = 83.87) than male students (n = 273, mean = 274.86, SD = 96.97) (p = 0.001), and they demonstrated better academic performance (mean = 56.16, SD = 19.66) when compared to male students (mean = 48.02, SD = 13.13) (p < 0.001)"
