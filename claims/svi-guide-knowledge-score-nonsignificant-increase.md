## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mayer-Hirshfeld Ilana 2019

Mayer-Hirshfeld Ilana, Agarwal Gauri G., Jackson Jennifer S. (2019). SVI Self-Study Guide: A Study Tool for Independent Preparation for the AAMC Standardized Video Interview. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10805

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-tailed t test comparing overall percentage scores on four knowledge-based questions (N = 10 pre, N = 8 post). The article reports the increase was "not significant"; equivalence was not tested. The authors note prequiz scores were already 72.50% because these topics are mandated in medical school curricula.

> "There was no significant difference between the pre- and postquiz average score on the four knowledge-based questions (p = .29). However, the average score increased from 72.50% to 93.25% (Table 3)."
