## Evidence

### Hegarty Josephine 2019

Hegarty Josephine, Howson Victoria, Wills Teresa, Creedon Sile A., Mc Cluskey Pat, Lane Aoife, Connolly Aine, Walshe Nuala, Noonan Brendan, Guidera Fiona, Gallagher Anthony G, Murphy Siobhan. (2019). Acute surgical wound‐dressing procedure: Description of the steps involved in the development and validation of an observational metric. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.13072

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Video analysis phase in which two team members independently watched recordings in silence up to three times before scoring agreement. Reliability was expressed by Cohen's Kappa, with κ scores ≥0.85 considered appropriate for this study.

> "The process of independent viewing, scoring, and revising the step and error metrics was continued until the metrics group was satisfied that the metric accurately characterised the procedural steps of an acute surgical wound‐dressing procedure with a consistent inter‐rater reliability ≥0.85."
