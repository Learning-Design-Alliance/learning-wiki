## Evidence

### Sandelowsky H 2018

Sandelowsky H, Natalishvili N, Krakau I, Modin S, Ställberg B, Nager A. (2018). COPD management by Swedish general practitioners – baseline results of the PRIMAIR study. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2018.1426148

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Baseline questionnaire analysis across 34 PHCCs: no correlation between question scores and presence of an asthma/COPD clinic, except on question 5. Equivalence was not tested beyond this reported absence of correlation.

> "There was no correlation between scores on individual questions and whether or not the PHCC had an asthma/COPD clinic, except on one question (question 5)."

### Sandelowsky H 2018 (2)

Sandelowsky H, Natalishvili N, Krakau I, Modin S, Ställberg B, Nager A. (2018). COPD management by Swedish general practitioners – baseline results of the PRIMAIR study. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2018.1426148

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results on question 5 (follow-up after exacerbation): GPs with clinic access scored worse than those without (1.10 vs. 1.42 points, p < .001). The authors suggest responsibilities may have been transferred to specialised nurses.

> "The GPs with access to an asthma/COPD clinic at the PHCC scored worse on question 5 than those without access to such clinic (1.10 vs. 1.42 points, p < .001)."
