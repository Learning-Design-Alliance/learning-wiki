Evidence¶

El-Sakka 2016¶

El-Sakka, S. M. F. (2016). Self-Regulated Strategy Instruction for Developing Speaking Proficiency and Reducing Speaking Anxiety of Egyptian University Students. English Language Teaching, 9(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n12p22

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Same one-group quasi-experiment (forty third-year English majors), measured with a researcher-adapted twenty-item speaking anxiety scale (Cronbach's Alpha .89). The paired samples t-test was significant; no effect size is printed for this finding.

"As shown in Table 2, the paired samples t-test revealed that a statistically significant difference existed in the mean scores of the experimental group between the pre- posttest of speaking anxiety (t=15.28, p<0.05)."
