Torgerson 2014¶

Torgerson, D., Torgerson, C., Jefferson, L., Buckley, H., Ainsworth, H., Heaps, C., & Mitchell, N. (2014). Discover Summer School Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581103

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Process evaluation based on non-participant observations of two sessions (one poet-led, one teacher-led), undertaken by a researcher independent of delivery. The article states formal qualitative analysis of these observations was not conducted, so these are observational impressions rather than tested outcomes.

"Children for the most part appeared well engaged and to enjoy these sessions. The interactive approach in poetry sessions in particular appeared to motivate pupils in their writing."
