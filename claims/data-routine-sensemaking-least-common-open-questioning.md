### McElhaney 2025

Promoting Mathematical Reasoning in OpenSciEd Middle School Curriculum Units. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/4ea4b3c5-c663-4484-8727-258bff346d2d

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Classroom-observation coding of Data Routine enactment across the 43 observed lessons found teachers enacted at least one element in 100% of lessons and all four in 24%, with "Orienting to the Data (58%) and Sensemaking about the Data (24%)" least common.

> "Framing Questions (88%) and Exploring the Data (97%) were commonly addressed, while Orienting to the Data (58%) and Sensemaking about the Data (24%) were less common."

### McElhaney 2025 (2)

McElhaney, K. W., Hodkowski, N. M., McMahon, L. H., Jacobs, J., Penuel, W. R., Anton, G., Henson, K., Urban, R., & Parker, D. A. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Coding of each lesson activity for questioning approach found teachers who facilitated at least one sensemaking activity used open-ended questioning much more frequently; "75% of activities that incorporated sensemaking used primarily open-ended questioning, compared to just 40% of other activities."
