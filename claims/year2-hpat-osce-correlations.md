Pearson correlations in the Year 2 cohort between selection criteria and OSCE Z-scores. The article reports "moderate, significant associations" with OSCE Communication Z-scores of r = .27 and .29, and further significant correlations with Clinical and Total OSCE Z-scores (r = .21–.28). These r values fall in the small-to-moderate range.

> "In Year 2 moderate, significant associations emerged between HPAT- Ireland 2 and Total HPAT- Ireland, and OSCE Communication Z-scores (r = .27, .29 respectively; all p values < .01)."
