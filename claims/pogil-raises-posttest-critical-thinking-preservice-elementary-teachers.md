## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After six weeks, the POGIL group outperformed the traditional-lecture control group on overall posttest critical thinking skills (U=37.500; p=.000). [→ Irwanto 2018](#irwanto-2018)
`q2 i?` The posttest critical thinking advantage appeared in all five sub-skills (elementary clarification, bases for a decision, inference, advanced clarification, supposition and integration). [→ Irwanto 2018](#irwanto-2018)

## Evidence

### Irwanto 2018

Irwanto, Saputro, A. D., Rohaeti, E., & Prodjosantoso, A. K. (2018). Promoting Critical Thinking and Problem Solving Skills of Preservice Elementary Teachers through Process-Oriented Guided-Inquiry Learning (POGIL). International Journal of Instruction, 11(4), 777-794. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1191708

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with 24 POGIL and 24 control students taught by the same lecturer; Mann-Whitney U-test on posttest critical thinking scores showed "a significant difference in CTS between experimental and control groups (U=37.500; p=.000)". Descriptive means rose from 10.08 to 17.96 (POGIL) versus 10.13 to 14.75 (control).

> "Overall, there was a significant difference in CTS between experimental and control groups (U=37.500; p=.000). It was seen from the mean rank that difference was also observed in all sub-skills"
