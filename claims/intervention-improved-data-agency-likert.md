# A four-hour technology-supported intervention improved 5th and 8th graders' perceived data agency across all 11 questionnaire statements, with medium effect sizes on several

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Learners' self-rated data agency improved significantly from pre- to post-test, with medium effect sizes on understanding of data collection (d=0.63), engagement maximization (d=0.54), recommendation awareness (d=0.46), other users' influence (d=0.44), and data-use purposes (d=0.40). Classroom Activities and New Classroom Apps for Enhancing Children's Understanding of Social Media Mechanisms. ACM Trans. Comput. Educ. 1, 1, Article 1 (January 2024). Originally published 2024. `q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Pre/post Likert questionnaire (11 statements, 5-point scale) administered to 209 Finnish 5th and 8th graders before and after the two workshops, analyzed with paired samples t-test and Cohen's d. Positive responses on understanding data collection rose from "65% to 91%", d=0.63***; other statements showed d=0.54, 0.46, 0.44, 0.40.

> "medium effect size (d=0.63***) on statements on understanding how social media collects information about users (the number of positive responses increased from 65% to 91%)"
