# Adding a serious game to standard burn training produces a large improvement in Mini-CEX total clinical competence scores relative to standard training alone

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` After a one-week burn rotation, the intervention group's adjusted mean Mini-CEX total score exceeded the control group's by 23.21 points (95% CI: 21.10–25.33, P < 0.0001, partial η² = 0.877). [→ Deldar Kolsoum 2026](#deldar-kolsoum-2026)

## Evidence

### Deldar Kolsoum 2026

Deldar Kolsoum, Tavousi Seyed Hasan, Ahmadabadi Ali, Rakhsha Mohsen, Khodabandeloo Farnoosh, Froutan Razieh. (2026). Enhancing clinical competence in burn management through a serious educational game: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09970-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

A parallel-group randomized controlled trial of 70 medical students (35 per group) at a burn department; ANCOVA adjusted for pre-test scores with Holm-Bonferroni correction showed the post-intervention total score difference "strongly favoring the intervention" with a printed effect size of η² = 0.877, described as a large effect.

> "The adjustment mean difference between the groups was 23.21 points (95% CI: 21.10–25.33), strongly favoring the intervention. As determined by ANCOVA, this difference was statistically significant (P-value < 0.0001). The effect size was 0.877. This represents a large effect"
