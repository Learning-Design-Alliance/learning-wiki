### Morrison Nariell 2019

Morrison Nariell, Machado Michelle, Blackburn Clare. (2019). Student perspectives on barriers to performance for black and minority ethnic graduate-entry medical students: a qualitative study in a West Midlands medical school. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-032493

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of four semi-structured focus groups with 24 BME graduate-entry MBChB students at one West Midlands medical school. Participants described allies among other ethnic minority students, with one noting allies "kind of gives you a boost".

> "Most BME students reported feeling isolated, explaining that each cohort consisted of predominantly white students. Students felt that they were not represented within the student body."
