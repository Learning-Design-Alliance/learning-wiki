# Textual prompts produce higher first-attempt practice accuracy, while pictorial prompts produce higher second-attempt accuracy

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Textual prompt groups solved significantly more unsolved practice steps correctly at the first attempt, F(1,47) = 12.00, p < .01. [→ Reisslein 2004](#reisslein-2004)
`q2 i?` Pictorial prompt groups had a significantly higher probability of solving practice steps on the second trial, F(1,47) = 12.77, p < .01. [→ Reisslein 2004 (2)](#reisslein-2004-2)

## Evidence

### Reisslein 2004

Reisslein, J., Atkinson, R. K., & Reisslein, M. (2004). Exploring the Presentation and Format of Help in a Computer-Based Electrical Engineering Learning Environment. Arizona State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED484994

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

En route practice accuracy recorded by the computer module in the 51-participant factorial experiment showed a "significant main effect on first anticipation for format of prompts"; textual groups solved 93% of unsolved steps at first attempt versus 82% for pictorial groups.

> "The ANOVA revealed that this difference corresponds to a significant main effect on first anticipation for format of prompts, F(1,47) = 12.00, MSE = .01, p< .01."

### Reisslein 2004 (2)

Reisslein, J., Atkinson, R. K., & Reisslein, M. (2004). Exploring the Presentation and Format of Help in a Computer-Based Electrical Engineering Learning Environment. Arizona State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED484994

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same en route practice ANOVA in the 51-participant experiment found pictorial-prompt participants "had a significantly higher probability of accurately solving the practice problems on the second trial" than text-based participants.

> "the participants who received pictorial -based prompts had a significantly higher probability of accurately solving the practice problems on the second trial as compared to participants who were assigned to the te xt-based prompts groups, F(1,47) = 12.77, MSE = .01, p< .01."
