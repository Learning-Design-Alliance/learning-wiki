## Evidence

### Reynolds Arthur J. 2017

Reynolds Arthur J., Hayakawa Momoko, Ou Suh-Ruu, Mondi Christina F., Englund Michelle M., Candee Allyson J., Smerillo Nicole E. (2017). Scaling and Sustaining Effective Early Childhood Programs Through School-Family-University Collaboration. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12901

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental propensity-score matched analysis of the Midwest CPC expansion cohort of 2,364 CPC participants in 26 sites and 1,212 comparison participants. Controlling for baseline and background characteristics, the article reports a school readiness effect of ".47 standard deviations" and a parent involvement effect of .33 standard deviations.

> "the mean effect size for school readiness skills at the end of preschool for Midwest CPC participants in Chicago (based on TS-Gold total scores) and Saint Paul (based on PALS alphabet recognition) was .47 standard deviations"
