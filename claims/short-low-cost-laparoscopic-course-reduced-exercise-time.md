## Evidence

### Takegawa Paula Haveroth 2022

Takegawa Paula Haveroth, Kalil Jefferson, Bustorff-Silva Joaquim Murray, Miranda Márcio Lopes. (2022). Effectiveness of an inexpensive short-term theoretical-practical course on videosurgery for surgeons in training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03594-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post paired comparison of timed laparoscopic stitch performance by 36 first-year surgery residents, scores converted from completion time (0–6 scale) and compared with the Wilcoxon test. The article reports "statistically significant (p = 0.0296)" improvement, reflecting decreased time spent on the task.

> "Improvement in the time to complete the practical exercise was statistically significant (p = 0.0296) after participating in the theoretical-practical course."
