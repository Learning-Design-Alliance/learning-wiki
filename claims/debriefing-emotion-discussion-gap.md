## Evidence

### Grewal 2026

Grewal, K., Azher, S., Moreno, M., Pekrun, R., Wiseman, J., Flake, J., Lajoie, S., Sun, N.‐Z., Fried, G. M., Khalil, E., & Harley, J. M. (2026). Medical trainees' emotions and their effects on perceptions of performance and team mood in team‐based simulations. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 306–336. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70017

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Qualitative content analysis of 13 debriefings found "negative activating emotions were found to be expressed the most during the debriefings despite positive activating emotions being the most reported" in the SERQ; the Results section reports 14 negative activating and 2 positive activating debriefing emotion observations, with no deactivating emotions.

> "Though debriefing frameworks explore trainees' emotional reactions, negative activating emotions were found to be expressed the most during the debriefings despite positive activating emotions being the most reported at intensities of moderate or higher in the SERQ."
