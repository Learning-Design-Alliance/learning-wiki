Evidence¶

Gorard et al. 2014¶

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui, Beng Huat See. (2014). Response to Intervention: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581264

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Waiting-list RCT in 61 English primary schools (30 treatment, 31 control) evaluating RTI for Year 6 pupils at risk of not reaching Level 4 English; NGRT post-test comparison of RTI-supported pupils with similar non-supported pupils. The report prints "an estimated effect size of +0. 19" but deems the trial spoilt.

"The first had an estimated effect size of +0. 19 . This headline result suggest s that this could programme have an impact equivalent to 3 months of additional progress in one year."
