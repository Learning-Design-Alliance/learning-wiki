Kozibroda et al. 2020¶

The review reports, citing Kiel, Braun, Muckenthaler, Heimlich and Weiss (2019), a connection between teachers' self-efficacy and inclusion implementation; no effect size is printed. Types of self-efficacy are said to determine implementation state and cannot coexist.

"The experience of inclusion in Germany (Kiel, Braun, Muckenthaler, Heimlich, & Weiss, 2019) indicates the connection between teachers’ self -efficacy and the effectiveness of inclusion implementation:the highest level of self-efficacy corresponds to the highest level of implementation of inclusive educational curricula"
