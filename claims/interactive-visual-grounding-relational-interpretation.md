### LUO Lan 2024

LUO Lan, WANG Anqi, ZHOU Muzhi, ZHU Junhua, CAI Jie, YU Ao, and PAN Hui. (2024). ParaTutor: Coordinating Parent-Child Math Tutoring through Role-Separated LLM Scaffolding. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18030 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.18030](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.18030)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

User study finding from 23 parent-child dyads using ParaTutor modes. Children used generated diagrams to drag age entities, adjust time sliders, and test whether interpretations were consistent with problem relations. Parents reported this helped children's sense-making.

> "These accounts suggest that interactive visual grounding helped children interpret word problems by making hidden relational constraints manipulable and visible."
