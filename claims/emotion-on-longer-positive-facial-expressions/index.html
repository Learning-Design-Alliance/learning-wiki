Debanjana Kar 2025¶

Within-subject user study with 30 participants (aged 15-55) using MathBuddy in Emotion ON and OFF conditions; Wilcoxon paired t-tests on mean per-participant facial emotion durations (Table 4). Negative: p=.537; Neutral: p=.248.
