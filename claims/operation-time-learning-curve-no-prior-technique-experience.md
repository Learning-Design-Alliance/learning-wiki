## Evidence

### Conroy Elizabeth J 2025

Conroy Elizabeth J, Blazeby Jane M, Burnside Girvan, Cook Jonathan A, Gamble Carrol. (2025). Investigating the presence of surgical learning in the Timing of Primary Surgery for cleft palate randomised trial. Clinical Trials. https://doi.org/10.1177/17407745241302488

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Visual within-surgeon analysis (moving average plots, Order 5) of the two surgeons with no prior Sommerlad experience, Surgeons 1 and 10, in the TOPS trial. The plots showed "a downwards trend in operation time" evident for both treatment arms despite their prior experience operating on infants aged 12 to 18 months.

> "The moving average plots for these surgeons suggested a downwards trend in operation time, with these surgeons getting faster at performing the technique during the trial by approximately 1 hr"

### Conroy Elizabeth J 2025 (2)

Conroy Elizabeth J, Blazeby Jane M, Burnside Girvan, Cook Jonathan A, Gamble Carrol. (2025). Investigating the presence of surgical learning in the Timing of Primary Surgery for cleft palate randomised trial. Clinical Trials. https://doi.org/10.1177/17407745241302488

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-level multilevel linear model of operation time for 516 infants with surgeon as random effect. The operation sequence coefficient was significant (β = 0.568; p < 0.0001); with an interaction term added, all variables including X1 × X2 (β = 0.885; p < 0.001) were significant at the 0.05 level, indicating different slopes for surgeons with and without prior technique experience.

> "Model B (adjusted for learning and technique), which extends Model A to incorporate operation sequence and technique experience, indicates that operation time decreases by approximately 1 min for every two operations performed (β = 0.568; 95% CI: −0.709 to −0.427; p < 0.0001)"
