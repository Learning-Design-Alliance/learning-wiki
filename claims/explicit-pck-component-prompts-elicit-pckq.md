## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Initial open-ended prompts produced LA answers that were too vague and did not reflect the PCK level observed in classroom videos; refining the prompts to ask explicitly about specific PCK components better elicited that PCK. [→ Beth Thacker 2023](#beth-thacker-2023)

## Evidence

### Beth Thacker 2023

Beth Thacker, Stephanie Hart, Kyle Wipfli, Jianlan Wang. (2023). The development of free-response questions to assess learning assistants' PCK in the context of questioning. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.14285](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.14285)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Instrument-development observation from iterative refinement of the free-response prompts: initial LA answers were "too vague and did not reflect the level of PCK observed in the videos", so prompts were refined to ask explicitly about specific PCK components.

> "Initially, the answers from LAs were too vague and did not reflect the level of PCK observed in the videos. Therefore we refined the open ended questions by explicitly asking about specific components of PCK to better elicit components of PCK as demonstrated in the videos."
