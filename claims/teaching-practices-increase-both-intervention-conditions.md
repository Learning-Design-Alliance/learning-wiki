### Hughes 2026

Hughes, A., Gerson, S. A., & van Schaik, J. E. (2026). Teacher-led robot intervention in early primary school classrooms improves pupil and teacher outcomes. Computers & Education, 242: 105510. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105510

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of teaching practices subscale scores served as a manipulation check of curriculum implementation; printed interactions were Intervention+ vs Control β = 4.51, p = 0.01 and Intervention vs Control β = 3.10, p = 0.03. The article reports "a significant interaction effect of condition and time on teachers’ practice scores".

> "There was a significant interaction effect of condition and time on teachers’ practice scores. Both Intervention+ and Intervention had significantly higher increases in practice scores compared to Control. Scores in Intervention + did not differ significantly from the Intervention."
