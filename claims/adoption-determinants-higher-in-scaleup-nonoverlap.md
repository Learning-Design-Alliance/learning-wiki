## Evidence

### Newman 2016

Newman, D., Lazarev, V., & Zacamy, J. (2016). Comparing a Program Implemented Under the Constraints of an RCT and in the Wild. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567633

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of RCT and Scale-up survey means on adoption determinants drawn from Zacamy, Newman, Lazarev, & Lin (2015); Table 3 prints Level of responsibility 3.45 vs 3.28 (p = 0.03) and Level of commitment 4.14 vs 2.43 (p < 0.001).

> "the set of variables associated with student outcomes does not overlap with those identified in our earlier study set of determinants of successful adoption: reported by teachers attendance of program meetings, levels of responsibility for and commitment to the success of the program at the school level. These two characteristics have higher values in Scale-up schools where teachers volunteered to participate in the study (Table 3, last two rows)."

### Newman 2016 (2)

Newman, D., Lazarev, V., & Zacamy, J. (2016). Comparing a Program Implemented Under the Constraints of an RCT and in the Wild. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567633

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' concluding statement integrating the adoption analysis (gain or loss in participating teachers per school) with the value-added correlates analysis; the two characteristic sets did not overlap.

> "But schools that were successful in growing participating internally were not more likely to have the characteristics associated with achievement gains."
