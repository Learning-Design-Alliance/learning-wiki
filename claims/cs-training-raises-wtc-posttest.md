## Evidence

### Mesgarshahr and Abdollahzadeh 2014

Abulfazl Mesgarshahr, Esmaeel Abdollahzadeh. (2014). The impact of teaching communication strategies on EFL learners’ Willingness to Communicate. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2014.4.1.4

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 8 intact pre-intermediate classes (experimental N = 58, control N = 62) at a private language institute; independent-samples t-test on post-test WTC questionnaire scores showed the experimental group (M = 59.21, SD = 27.31) exceeded the control group, and the article prints the effect size eta squared = .10, calling "the magnitude of the difference in the means was large (eta squared = .10)".

> "and control group ( M = 42.79, SD = 27.64), with t(99) = 3.48, p< .05. Furthermore, the magnitude of the difference in the means was large (eta squared = .10)."
