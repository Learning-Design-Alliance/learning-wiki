## Evidence

### Mosling Marte 2026

Mosling Marte, Horn Julie, Xanthoulis Athanasios. (2026). Stepping into the virtual ward: a qualitative study on first-year residents' experiences with VR simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08729-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study (five individual in-depth interviews and two focus groups with 2–3 participants) of eleven first-year residents at Levanger Hospital; reflexive thematic analysis. Participants reported feeling safe in simulation and dared to "challenge themselves more than they would have done in a real emergency situation".

> "The findings showed that the participants considered VR simulation to be a safe and flexible learning tool for training in emergency care."
