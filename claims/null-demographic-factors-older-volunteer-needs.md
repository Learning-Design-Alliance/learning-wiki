### Yang 2025

Yang, L.-C., LAI, C.-Y., Chen, K.-M., Chen, M.-C., Chang, C.-C., Lin, T.-Y., & Kuo, C.-F. (2025). Competencies and needs of older volunteers: A comparative survey. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000712

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null findings from the 1,000-participant ANOVA survey (Table 3 reports gender F = 0.52, p = .470; living status F = 0.18, p = .675; chronic disease F = 2.41, p = .121; marital status F = 1.36, p = .244). Equivalence was not tested.
