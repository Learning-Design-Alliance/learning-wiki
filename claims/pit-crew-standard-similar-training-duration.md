## Evidence

### Couper K 2018

Couper K, Velho RM, Quinn T, Devrell A, Lall R, Orriss B, Yeung J, Perkins GD. (2018). Training approaches for the deployment of a mechanical chest compression device: a randomised controlled manikin study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-019009

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Delivery data from the randomised manikin trial: mean duration 49.0 min (95% CI 44.0 to 54.0) for pit-crew vs 45.3 min (95% CI 40.5 to 50.1) for standard training, mean difference 3.1 (95% CI −2.7 to 10.1), P=0.244. "all training interventions were delivered as planned".

> "There were no study adverse events, and all training interventions were delivered as planned, with time taken to deliver training interventions being similar between groups"
