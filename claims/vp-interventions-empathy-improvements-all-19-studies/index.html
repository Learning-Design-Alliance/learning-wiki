Yamada Rie 2025¶

Yamada Rie, Futakawa Kaori, Kondo Satoshi, Xu Kuangzhe, Okazaki Satoshi. (2025). A scoping review on the use of virtual patients for enhancing empathy in medical students. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2607825

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of the scoping review's outcome mapping (RQ6), covering 19 studies of virtual patient interventions in medical students. The review reports that "Improvements in medical students’ empathy through the use of virtual patients were reported in all 19 included studies", with 17 studies showing immediate enhancement and two showing sustained effects at six months and one year.

"Improvements in medical students’ empathy through the use of virtual patients were reported in all 19 included studies (Table 1). Regarding the timing of these improvements, 17 studies [25,26,31,32,56,63–74] reported enhanced empathy immediately following the virtual patient intervention, while two indicated that the effects were sustained at six months [47] and one year [62], respectively."
