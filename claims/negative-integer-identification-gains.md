## Evidence

### Bofferding 2015

Bofferding, L., Hoffman, A., Suazo, E., & Lisy, N. (2015). Number Line Estimation with Negatives. Proceedings of the 37th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED584204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Integer identification task in the first-grade pretest/posttest interviews: game-group identification of negatives rose to 95% versus 32% in control. In kindergarten, "six students (26%) in the game group could correctly identify the majority" while no control kindergarteners could.

> "By the posttest, eight students in the control group (32%) and 21 (95%) students in the game group did so."
