## Evidence

### Knoop‐van Campen CAN 2018

Knoop‐van Campen CAN, Segers E, Verhoeven L. (2018). The modality and redundancy effects in multimedia learning in children with dyslexia. Dyslexia. 2018;24:140–155. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1585

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANCOVA of learning time in the redundancy analysis (text vs. text-and-audio vs. audio conditions) in the same within-subjects experiment. The dyslexia subgroup showed a large printed effect size η2 p = .346 (F(1, 14) = 7.40, p = .017); the interaction with group was F(1, 42) = 14.15, p = .001, η2 p = .252.

> "These analyses showed that children with dyslexia spent significantly more time in the text condition than in the combined text‐and‐audio condition, F(1, 14) = 7.40, p = .017, η2 p = .346, pairwise comparisons p = .026"
