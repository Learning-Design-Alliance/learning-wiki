Evidence¶

Laninga-Wijnen 2018¶

Laninga-Wijnen, L., Ryan, A., Harakeh, Z., Shin, H., & Vollebergh, W. A. M. (2018). The Moderating Role of Popular Peers' Achievement Goals in 5th- and 6th-Graders' Achievement-Related Friendships: A Social Network Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology, vol. 110, no. 2, pp. 289-307. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000210

q3 · i? · associational · r2

RSiena endowment (maintenance) effects estimated separately for classrooms with low, moderate, and high performance goal popularity norms in a longitudinal study of 46 classrooms. The abstract states this maintenance contrast; the article notes maintenance processes have been underinvestigated in prior work.

"Conversely, adolescents remained friends with similarly achieving peers in classrooms with low performance goal popularity norms but not in classrooms with high performance goal popularity norms."
