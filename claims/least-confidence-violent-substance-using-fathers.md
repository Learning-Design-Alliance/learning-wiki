Descriptive confidence ratings across 13 areas from 205 practitioners (Table 2). Most confidence was reported for communicating with fathers (80.9%), remaining neutral (72.2%), and eliciting fathers' expectations and goals (66.3%).

> "Practitioners were least confident (% extremely or very confident) in: working with fathers who have been violent or abusive (22.4%), working with fathers with substance use issues (26.9%), and dealing with resistance from fathers (39.0%)."
