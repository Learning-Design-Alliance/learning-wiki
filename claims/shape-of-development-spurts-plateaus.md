## Evidence

### Dawson-Tunik 2005

Dawson-Tunik, T. L., Commons, M., Wilson, M., & Fischer, K. W. (2005). The shape of development. European Journal of Developmental Psychology, 2(2), 163–195. https://doi.org/10.1080/17405620544000011

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Rating scale Rasch analysis of 747 LAS-scored moral judgment interviews (ages 5–86); Figure 6 shows the expected-score function. The article reports the pattern is "consistent with Fischer & Rose's (1999) characterization" of "consolidation (plateaus)" and "spurts".

> "proceeding through successive periods of consolidation (plateaus) during which performance within a domain is predominantly at a single level followed by transitional periods (spurts) characterized by vacillation between the modal stage and its successor"
