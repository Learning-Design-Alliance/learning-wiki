The primer warns, citing Deci and Ryan (1985) and Radkowitsch et al. (2020), that "Highly prescriptive interaction scripts can negatively impact learner motivation by undermining their sense of agency." Practitioners are told to be aware of this potential consequence.

"Highly prescriptive interaction scripts can negatively impact learner motivation by undermining their sense of agency (Deci and Ryan, 1985; Radkowitsch et al., 2020)."
