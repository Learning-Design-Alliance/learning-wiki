Maadi Mahdi Alajmi & Hanan Abdullah Al-Hadiah 2017¶

Maadi Mahdi Alajmi & Hanan Abdullah Al-Hadiah. (2017). Effectiveness of using the iPad in Learning to Acquire the Mental and Performance Skills in Teaching Social Studies Curriculum. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v6n1p1

Pre-test T-test on the same 48-student sample: experimental mean 8.33 (SD 0.96) versus control 8.11 (SD 0.84), T-test value 0.827. The table prints a significance level of .0413 while the text states there are no significant differences; the article does not explain this discrepancy.

"showed that there are no statistically significant differences between the averages of the experimental group and the control group in acquisition of the mental skills in the pre-test."
