### Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer 2007

Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer. (2007). Student Behavior and Epistemological Framing: Examples from Collaborative Active-Learning Activities in Physics. Submitted to Cognition and Instruction. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Student+Behavior+and+Epistemological+Framing

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Observational video analysis of student tutorial groups in an introductory physics course. The authors report that the four co-occurring behavior clusters are distinct and that "independent coders achieve 90% agreement in classifying 5-second video segments into one of four clusters."

> "These clusters of co-occurring behaviors stand out clearly; having had them described, and with very little training, independent coders achieve 90% agreement in classifying 5-second video segments into one of four clusters."
