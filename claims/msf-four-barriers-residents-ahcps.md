### Yama Brie A. 2018

Yama Brie A., Hodgins Michael, Boydell Katherine, Schwartz Sarah B. (2018). A qualitative exploration: questioning multisource feedback in residency education. BMC Medical Education 18. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1270-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of three focus groups with junior residents, senior residents, and AHCPs in a pediatric inpatient unit, conducted before MSF implementation. The analysis "revealed concerns about (i) (mis) understanding of roles and responsibilities" alongside the three other named barrier themes.

> "Our analysis revealed concerns about (i) (mis) understanding of roles and responsibilities, (ii) perceptions of expertise, (iii) hospital culture/interprofessionalism, and (iv) power dynamics as potential barriers to the acceptance and provision of feedback."
