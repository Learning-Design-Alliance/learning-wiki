### Koppenborg 2026

Koppenborg, Markus, Hüffmeier, Joachim, & Klingsieck, Katrin B. (2026). Is procrastination among students lower in group work? Evidence from a registered field experiment. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70069

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered OLS regression (Model 6) on task procrastination comparing additive group work to individual work among high trait-procrastinators. Both overall fit and condition coefficient were non-significant, answering RQ1a with no detected difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "Results revealed a non‐significant overall model fit and a non‐significant coefficient for additive group work (see Model 6 in Table 7). In view of RQ1a, task procrastination does not differ between additive group work and individual work."
