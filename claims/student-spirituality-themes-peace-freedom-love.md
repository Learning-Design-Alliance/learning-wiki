### Domènech-Sorolla 2025

Domènech-Sorolla, J., Martínez-Rodríguez, L., Pedregosa-Fauste, S., Muns-Orenga, A., García-Díaz, F., & Fernández-Pascual, M. D. (2025). Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-025-02285-8

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Lexicometric content analysis with IRAMUTEQ (Reinert's descending hierarchical classification) of students' responses to 'What does spirituality mean to you?'; Table 6 reports four concept classes with participant quotes; highest-frequency words included life (24), person (20), and peace (17).

> "Qualitative results indicated that for students, the concept of spirituality is shaped by life, as a feeling of surrendering, sometimes with a sense of fear, in order to reach the soul and hope. Students described a person's spirituality as rooted in freedom, love, and tranquility."
