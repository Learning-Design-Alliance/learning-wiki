Split-plot ANOVA on tooth volume loss difference scores in the RCT (n = 30) shows a significant main effect of access technique, F(1, 28) = 10.46, p = 0.003, with the training-method main effect non-significant (F = 2.19, p = 0.15).

> "A significant main effect of access cavity preparation technique was observed, F(1, 28) = 10.46, p = 0.003, suggesting that the type of access cavity preparation significantly influenced the amount of tooth structure removed."
