`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regressions of assessment marks onto gender pre- and post-implementation (Table 6) in the archival dataset. The article reports variance explained by gender "approximately halved across the board since implementation", but calls the change very small in practical terms.

> "this only amounts to a mean reduction in marks on these assessments of 0.95 of a percentage point, a very small amount in practical terms."
