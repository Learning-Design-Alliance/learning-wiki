## Evidence

### Liang 2026

Liang, F., Zhu, Y., Li, R. & Fu, Q. (2026). Foil Type Modulates Developmental Changes in Statistical Learning Across Childhood to Adulthood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00434-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on confidence response proportions with Group (Grades 1, 3, 5) as between-subjects factor; the main effect of Confidence was significant (F(1.92, 180.90) = 18.80, p < 0.001, ηp² = 0.167), but the article concludes metacognitive labeling showed no developmental or trial-based modulation.

### Liang 2026 (2)

Liang, F., Zhu, Y., Li, R. & Fu, Q. (2026). Foil Type Modulates Developmental Changes in Statistical Learning Across Childhood to Adulthood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00434-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed ANOVA on confidence response proportions comparing Grade 5 and adults; the main effect of Confidence was significant (F(1.58, 101.11) = 26.64, p < 0.001, ηp² = 0.294) but group-related effects were null, indicating metacognitive labeling does not undergo substantial developmental change in this range.

> "the Group × Confidence interaction was not significant, 𝐹(1.58, 101.11) = 2.63, 𝑝 = 0.089, 𝜂𝑝 2 = 0.039, 𝐵𝐹10 = 0.292, and the three-way Group × FoilType × Confidence interaction also favored the null model"
