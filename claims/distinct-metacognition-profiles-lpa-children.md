# Elementary school children show distinct latent profiles of procedural metacognition combining monitoring accuracy, restudy effectiveness, and decision accuracy across tasks

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Latent profile analyses identified distinct metacognition profiles at both grade levels and both measurement points, confirming heterogeneity in the quality of monitoring and control skills. [→ Mariëtte van Loon 2024](#mariëtte-van-loon-2024)

## Evidence

### Mariëtte van Loon 2024

Mariëtte van Loon, Claudia M. Roebers. (2024). Development of metacognitive monitoring and control skills in elementary school: a latent profile approach. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09400-2

`q2 · i?`

Latent profile analyses of six z-standardized indicators (monitoring accuracy, effective restudy, decision accuracy for text and Kanji tasks) in 151 second and 176 fourth graders. Three-profile solutions were selected per grade at each time point, "confirming H1" that profiles differ in metacognitive quality.

> "In sum, for both age groups and at both time points, identified profiles showed differences in the quality of metacognitive monitoring and control skills (confirming H1)."
