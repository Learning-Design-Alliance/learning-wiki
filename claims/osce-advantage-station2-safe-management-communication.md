## Evidence

### Peng Shuang 2026

Peng Shuang, Wei Yongbao, Dai Caixia, Liang Yuxuan, Huang Li. (2026). Impact of a vertically integrated, symptom-oriented teaching program for hematuria management on clinical competency transfer among urology clerkship students. BMC Medical Education, 26, Article 1348. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09680-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Station-level Welch t-test decomposition of the modified OSCE in the same quasi-experimental cohort. The difference was more concentrated in station 2, management and communication (42.95 ± 2.30 vs. 39.64 ± 3.82), while station 1 also showed a difference.

> "the difference was more concentrated in station 2, management and communication (42.95 ± 2.30 vs. 39.64 ± 3.82, P = 0.000015), while station 1, outpatient initial decision making, also showed a difference (42.44 ± 3.42 vs. 40.35 ± 3.44, P = 0.0081)"

### Peng Shuang 2026 (2)

Peng Shuang, Wei Yongbao, Dai Caixia, Liang Yuxuan, Huang Li. (2026). Impact of a vertically integrated, symptom-oriented teaching program for hematuria management on clinical competency transfer among urology clerkship students. BMC Medical Education, 26, Article 1348. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09680-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Domain-level Welch t-tests with Holm adjustment in the same quasi-experimental cohort. Safe management and communication/follow-up remained significant after Holm adjustment; investigation interpretation had a slightly higher mean but was not statistically significant (21.10 ± 2.00 vs. 20.21 ± 2.84).

> "Investigation interpretation had a slightly higher mean in the intervention group but was not statistically significant (21.10 ± 2.00 vs. 20.21 ± 2.84, P = 0.110)"
