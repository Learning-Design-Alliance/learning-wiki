## Evidence

### Perotti Luis 2025

Perotti Luis, Stamm Oskar, Ferizaj Drin, Dietrich Michael, Buchem Ilona, Müller-Werdan Ursula. (2025). Evaluation of an e-learning platform promoting electronic personal health record competence: a pilot trial in older adults. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22242-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U test comparing knowledge-test mean differences between the YOA and OA groups in the pilot trial. The article reports "no significant difference was present in the knowledge test"; equivalence was not tested.

> "When comparing the values for the mean differences of correct answers between the two groups, no significant difference was present in the knowledge test between the baseline and follow-up assessments (U = 64.50, Z = −1.586, p = 0.125) (Table 4)."

### Perotti Luis 2025 (2)

Perotti Luis, Stamm Oskar, Ferizaj Drin, Dietrich Michael, Buchem Ilona, Müller-Werdan Ursula. (2025). Evaluation of an e-learning platform promoting electronic personal health record competence: a pilot trial in older adults. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22242-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U test comparing skill-test completion-time differences between the two age groups. The article reports the differences "were not significant"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Similar to the results of the knowledge test, the differences in processing time in the pre-post comparison between the two groups were not significant (U = 173.00, Z = −1.378, p = 0.178)."
