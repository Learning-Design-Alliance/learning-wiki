## Evidence

### seet-participation

McElhaney, K. W., Mills, K., Kamdar, D., Baker, A., & Roschelle, J. (2023, March). A summary and synthesis of initial OpenSciEd research: Updated version [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/171

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports field-test SEET survey analyses from the 2018-2019 OpenSciEd study, finding "students from different race, gender, and linguistic backgrounds report they contribute to class discussions at high levels"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Students from different race, gender, and linguistic backgrounds report they contribute to class discussions at high levels and say their ideas influence the class and help others (OpenSciEd, 2019)."

### teacher-accessibility

McElhaney, K. W., Mills, K., Kamdar, D., Baker, A., & Roschelle, J. (2023, March). A summary and synthesis of initial OpenSciEd research: Updated version [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/171

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Teacher self-report survey data from the 2018-2019 field test, summarized by the review; these are descriptive percentages of teacher perceptions, not direct measures of student engagement.

> "84% of teachers said that they were accessible to struggling readers, 76% of teachers said they were accessible to students with Individual Education Plans, and 68% of teachers said they were accessible to multilingual learners (OpenSciEd, 2019)."
