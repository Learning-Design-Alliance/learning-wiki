## Evidence

### Huang 2026

Huang, S. C.-F., Chen, Z.-H., Lin, Y.-T., & Yeh, M. K. (2026). Bridging strategy gaps in programming: the role of visual scaffolding for learners with low cognitive ability. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10684-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Lag sequential analysis of gaze movement across AOIs for high- and low-EF learners in the EG, with z > 1.96 treated as significant. The article reports z-scores of 8.57 (high-EF) and 8.56 (low-EF) for top-down-to-tool transitions, and states "low-EF learners tend to use the same strategy as high-EF students".

> "Both of them show significant scan patterns of back and forth between the top-down tracking window and the programming tool. The z-score from the top-down tracking window to the programming tool of high-EF learners is 8.57, while low-EF learners is 8.56"
