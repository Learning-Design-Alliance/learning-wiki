## Evidence

### Hyewon Jang 2026

Hyewon Jang. (2026). Measurement of the effect of member performances on team outcomes in two-stage exams. Physical Review Physics Education Research (submitted). Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Variance decomposition from the regression models on 44 team scores: R2 was 0.48 (best), 0.35 (mean), 0.13 (standard deviation), and 0.41 for the combination of mean and standard deviation. The author concludes "approximately half of the variation in team scores is attributable to factors dependent on the individual scores".

> "Table II shows that member performances explain less than 50% of the variance in the team outcome. Forty-eight percent of the variation in team performance after the first collaboration can be explained by the best of member performances, whereas 35% of the variation in team scores after the first collaboration can be explained by the average of member performances."
