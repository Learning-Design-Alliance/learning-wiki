## Evidence

### Hu Wen-Gang 2015

Hu Wen-Gang, Feng Jia-Yu, Wang Jin, Song Ya-Jun, Xu Xiao-Ting, Zhou Hong, Huang Chi-Bing. (2015). Ureteroscopy and cystoscopy training: comparison between transparent and non-transparent simulators. BMC Medical Education 15:93. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0380-8

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

This is the authors' discussion-section argument addressing the potential concern that transparent materials are prohibitively costly. They report the printed material prices — "The acrylic used to manufacture our device cost ~ $60 per kilogram" versus about $50 for ABS — and conclude the per-simulator discrepancy is almost negligible. No cost analysis was performed.

> "The acrylic used to manufacture our device cost ~ $60 per kilogram, whereas the common opaque plastic ABS resin that might have been used to manufacture an equivalent opaque device costs ~ $50 per kilogram. The discrepancy between the prices of the transparent and the non-transparent materials in the manufacture of each simulator is almost negligible."
