## Evidence

### Solberg Runar Barstad 2021

Solberg Runar Barstad, Steene-Johannessen Jostein, Anderssen Sigmund Alfred, Ekelund Ulf, Säfvenbom Reidar, Haugen Tommy, Berntsen Sveinung, Åvitsland Andreas, Lerum Øystein, Resaland Geir Kåre, Kolle Elin. (2021). Effects of a school-based physical activity intervention on academic performance in 14-year old adolescents: a cluster randomized controlled trial – the School in Motion study. BMC Public Health 21, 871. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-021-10901-x

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Same cluster RCT, standardized national reading tests at baseline and follow-up analyzed with linear mixed models adjusting for sex. The article reports "0.9 (95% CI 0.2 to 1.6; Cohen’s d = 0.06)" for PAL and "1.1 (95% CI: 0.3 to 1.9; Cohen’s d = 0.18)" for DWBH versus controls — both very small effects by the article's classification.

> "Similar results were found for reading, where the mean difference in change was 0.9 (95% CI 0.2 to 1.6; Cohen’s d = 0.06) and 1.1 (95% CI: 0.3 to 1.9; Cohen’s d = 0.18) points in favour of students in the PAL and DWBH interventions, respectively."
