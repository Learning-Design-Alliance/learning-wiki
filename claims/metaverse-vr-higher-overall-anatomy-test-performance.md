## Evidence

### Ito T 2026

Ito T, Kawasaki H, Yamazaki A, Mizoguchi Y, Yamaguchi K, Kaneko E, Tsutsumi T. (2026). Metaverse‐based virtual reality and spatial learning in cervicofacial anatomy: A curriculum‐embedded comparative study in undergraduate medical students. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70277

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Nonrandomized curriculum-embedded comparison of intact cohorts: fourth-year Zoom lecture (n = 73) versus sixth-year metaverse VR session (n = 40), assessed immediately after teaching with a 12-item test. The article reports a "mean difference 22.4 percentage points" with Hedges' g = 1.23.
