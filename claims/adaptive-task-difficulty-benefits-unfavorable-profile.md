## Evidence

### Bach 2025

Bach, K. M., Reinhold, F., & Hofer, S. I. (2025). Unlocking math potential in students from lower SES backgrounds – using instructional scaffolds to improve performance. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00358-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed model on post-test fractions-on-number-line knowledge (log-transformed) with condition, profile, and school type as fixed effects and class as random effect. Students were randomly assigned to conditions; the outcome is placement error, so lower is better. The adaptive task difficulty × inattentive unfavorable profile interaction was significant (Table 8: β = −0.79, SE = 0.34, p = .022), while the main effect of adaptive task difficulty was not (p = .090). The β is a regression weight on a log error scale, not a standardized mean difference, so impact is coded `i?` (not reported on a comparable scale), which does not mean the effect is null. The model's conditional R2 = 0.53.

> "The interaction effect between the adaptive task dif ﬁculty condition and the inattentive unfavorable proﬁle was signiﬁcant and negative, sug- gesting that students assigned to this pro ﬁle showed signiﬁcantly higher performance on the fraction on number line knowledge test in this condi- tion compared to the control condition."
