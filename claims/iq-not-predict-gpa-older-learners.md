## Evidence

### Imlach 2017

Imlach, A.-R., Ward, D. D., Stuart, K. E., Summers, M. J., Valenzuela, M. J., King, A. E., Saunders, N. L., Summers, J., Srikanth, V. K., Robinson, A. & Vickers, J. C. (2017). Age is no barrier: predictors of academic success in older learners. npj Science of Learning 2:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0014-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the initial multiple regression (N = 181), WAIS full-scale IQ was non-significant (β = 0.007, p = 0.936, sr² = 0.000). The authors note substantial IQ variance remained in the sample (M = 119.96, range = 85–155), so restriction of range does not explain the null.

> "In this study, IQ was not a predictor of success in older learners."
