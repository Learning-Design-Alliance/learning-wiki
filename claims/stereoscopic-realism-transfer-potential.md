`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review statement citing multiple disciplines' findings, especially for complex scenes with high levels of detail (van Beurden et al., 2010). The review reports these as second-hand findings; no effect sizes printed.

> "Across several disciplines, it has been found that stereoscopic viewing has a major advantage over flat, non-stereoscopic presentations for tasks involving spatial perception (e.g. Hackett & Proctor, 2016; McIntire et al., 2012, 2014)."
