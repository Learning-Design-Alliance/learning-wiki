`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Results of the main preregistered multilevel model (Table 4), with T1 status as predictor of T2 outcomes controlling for initial adjustment. The article reports defended and nondefended victims "did not differ" on these outcomes; equivalence was not tested. No effect sizes were printed, so impacts are null.

> "victims who were defended at T1 did not differ from nondefended victims in self-esteem, depressive symptoms, and severity of victimization at T2. Nonvictims at T1 had higher self-esteem, lower depressive symptoms, and lower victimization at T2 compared with defended victims."
