# Reading Apprenticeship® showed a statistically significant positive effect on the GRADE reading comprehension subtest for Cohort 2 ninth-grade students

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` For Cohort 2 students, the study found, and the WWC confirmed, a statistically significant positive effect on the GRADE reading comprehension subtest (effect size 0.14, improvement index +5). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2010](#what-works-clearinghouse-2010)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2010

What Works Clearinghouse. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Reading Apprenticeship®. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. http://www.wested.org/ReadingApprenticeship

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r?`

Findings from the randomized controlled trial's second cohort (645 intervention and 470 control ninth-graders reading at least two years below grade level), assessed after 7.5 to 9 months. Appendix A3 prints an effect size of 0.14 and an improvement index of +5 for the comprehension subtest. The effect size falls below d = 0.2.

> "For Cohort 2 students, the authors did not find statistically significant effects of Reading Apprenticeship ® on the GRADE vocabulary subtest, but found, and the WWC confirmed, a statistically significant positive effect of Reading Apprentice -ship® on the GRADE reading comprehension subtest."
