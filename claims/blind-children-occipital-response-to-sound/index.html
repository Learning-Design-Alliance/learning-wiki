Bedny 2015¶

A neuroimaging study comparing blind and sighted children listening to auditory stimuli found that "visual" cortices of young blind (but not sighted) children respond to sound. No effect size is printed for this group contrast.

"We find that “visual” cortices of young blind (but not sighted) children respond to sound."
