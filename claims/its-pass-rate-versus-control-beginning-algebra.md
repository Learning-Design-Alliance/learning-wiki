## Evidence

### Copus 2016

Copus, C., & McKinney, B. (2016). Early Integration of Tutorial Support in Beginning Algebra. Journal of Developmental Education, 40(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1152251

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison over four semesters (Fall 2014–Spring 2016) of ITS completers against a non-ITS control group in the same instructor's sections, with success defined as "a final grade of C- or better". No statistical test or effect size is reported.

> "Pass rates among participants who did not withdraw is 65.6 %. This is notable when compared to the pass rates of all students in Beginning Algebra (56.6 %; GRCC Analytics, 2016) and, particularly, to students from the control group (22.2%)."
