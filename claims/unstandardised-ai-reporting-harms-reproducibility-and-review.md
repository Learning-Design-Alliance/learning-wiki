### Allison 2026

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Editorial argument by the author, grounded in observed manuscript deficiencies: submissions often omit which model was used, how it was configured, the AI's role, human oversight, and how limitations or ethical risks were addressed. This is a reasoned editorial claim, not an empirical test; no effect size is reported.

> "A lack of standardised reporting creates problems for reproducibility, peer review, meta-analysis, and ultimately, impact. In this context, the RAISE framework offers a shared vocabulary and minimal reporting standard to elevate the quality and consistency of published research."
