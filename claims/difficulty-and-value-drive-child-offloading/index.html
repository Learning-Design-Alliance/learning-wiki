Evidence¶

Alexander Skulmowski 2023¶

Alexander Skulmowski. (2023). The Cognitive Architecture of Digital Externalization. Educational Psychology Review, 35:101. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09818-1

q2 · i? · review · r3

Three studies of 11-year-old children memorizing word pairs on laptops (Dong et al., 2022, as reviewed here). In the first study, "More difficult (i.e., unrelated) word pairs were offloaded more than easier pairs, but an assigned value (one or five stars) did not affect offloading behavior." In a second study with constant difficulty, higher value increased offloading; a third study showed value and difficulty jointly determine offloading when value is emphasized.
