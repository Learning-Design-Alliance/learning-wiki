`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot randomized educational trial of 52 nursing students randomized 2:1 to SimZone 0 interactive video plus SimZone 1 seminar or SimZone 1 alone; Fisher exact test at 6 months showed "the experimental group showing markedly higher competence retention" (96% vs 62%).

> "At 6 months, the difference was statistically significant (P=.02; OR 13.75, 95% CI 1.39-136.39), with the experimental group showing markedly higher competence retention."
