## Evidence

### David 2026

David, L., Biwer, F., Crutzen, R., & de Bruin, A. (2026). Understanding and supporting university students' use of distributed practice via implementation intentions. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00448-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the post-questionnaire, 25% of the II group (n = 6) and 23.8% of controls (n = 10) said they had not used distributed practice, versus 3% and 10% in daily data; the authors describe "a discrepancy between retrospective self-reported use of distributed practice and actual use".

> "This difference between the post- and daily questionnaire indicates a discrepancy between retrospective self-reported use of distributed practice and actual use measured through the daily questionnaire, suggesting that students may underestimate their use of this strategy when reﬂecting retrospectively."
