### Abhilash Nair 2018

Abhilash Nair, Paul Irving, and Vashti Sawtelle. (2018). Using disciplinary perspectives to refine conceptions of the "real world". arXiv:1808.10429. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.062](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.062)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In Maria's second semi-structured interview (case-study interview data), she explains that her cross-disciplinary connection — physics explaining the chemistry interactions driving flagellar movement in microbiology — emerged only when work, torque, and force appeared "in a field that I know about" in her concurrent microbiology course.

> "taking this course, the microbio course along-side physics where things like work and torque and force are coming up in a ﬁeld that I know about, it helps you see."
