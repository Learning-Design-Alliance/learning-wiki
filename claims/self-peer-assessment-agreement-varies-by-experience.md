## Evidence

### Kim Erin 2025

Kim Erin, Tchinde Marius J., Rooney Deborah M., Ngam Blessing N., Rettig R. Luke, Gross Christopher L., Snell Mark J., Barnard Melanie L., El-Hayek Kevin, Jeffcoach David R., Kim Grace J. (2025). A novel tool for assessing psychomotor proficiency in laparoscopic cholecystectomy using simulation-based training. Surgical Endoscopy 39(5):3386–3395. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11708-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Agreement analysis of self- versus peer-assessments using the same CHOLE-VOP across all experience levels in the 52-user pilot. The article reports Spearman correlations and labels agreement moderate, poor, and moderate respectively.

> "Spearman rank correlations between "self" and "peer" assessments for users of all experience levels showed moderate agreement for summed checklist scores (ρ = 0.52), poor agreement for summed GRS (ρ = 0.44), and moderate for final rating (ρ = 0.60)."

### Kim Erin 2025 (2)

Kim Erin, Tchinde Marius J., Rooney Deborah M., Ngam Blessing N., Rettig R. Luke, Gross Christopher L., Snell Mark J., Barnard Melanie L., El-Hayek Kevin, Jeffcoach David R., Kim Grace J. (2025). A novel tool for assessing psychomotor proficiency in laparoscopic cholecystectomy using simulation-based training. Surgical Endoscopy 39(5):3386–3395. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11708-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Averaged two-way mixed ICC analysis comparing novice self-assessments with peer-assessments from more experienced users, and intermediate self-assessments with expert peer-assessments. Novice checklist agreement was poor (0.24); intermediate checklist agreement was good (0.79).

> "When comparing scores between novice self-assessment scores with peer-assessment scores given by intermediate or expert users, agreement was poor for the checklist (0.24), moderate for the GRS (0.55), and moderate for the final rating (0.65). Intermediate users demonstrated good agreement on the checklist (0.79) and moderate agreement on the GRS (0.52) and the final rating (0.45)."
