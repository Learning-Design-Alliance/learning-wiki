## Evidence

### Tefera Aklile Semu 2024

Tefera Aklile Semu, Melaku Ermiyas Endewunet, Urgie Besufekad Mulugeta, Hassen Erzik Muhammed, Tamene Tilahun Deresse, Gebeyaw Enguday Demeke. (2024). Barriers to implementing problem-based learning at the school of medicine of Debre Berhan University, Ethiopia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05252-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Student FGDs in the qualitative study reported that "most tutors do not use the assessment check list" and rely on subjective judgment. Students also complained that tutors evaluated them at the end of the module rather than providing continuous assessment and feedback. Tutors found the tool too complex and time consuming.

> "most tutors do not use the assessment check list; they simply evaluate us based on their subjective judgment and that creates disappointment for students"
