## Evidence

### Barros García 2018

Barros García, M. J., & Bachelor, J. W. (2018). Pragmatic Instruction May Not Be Necessary Among Heritage Learners of Spanish: A Study on Requests. Journal of Foreign Language Education and Technology, 3(1). https://jflet.com/jflet/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Brief descriptive analysis of the 8-student L2 control group, which received regular instruction without the pragmatic lessons. The article reports growth of "3.75%" (written) and "0%" (oral) and offers the authors' suggestion that other variables were unlikely causes; no statistical test is reported.

> "A growth of 3.75% occurred in this group between pre- and post-written DCTs and by 0% between pre- and post-oral DCTs. With this in mind, it can be suggested that other variables did not likely play a role in group 2's growth between pre- and post-tests."
