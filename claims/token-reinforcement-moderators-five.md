# Five moderators of token reinforcement effect sizes: study type, outcome type, effect size direction, publication year, and variance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Surveys/questionnaires showed significantly higher effect sizes (p = .007); behavior outcomes were significantly higher (p = .021) and performance significantly lower (p = .048); positive-direction studies improved significantly over time (p = 0.025); 1990s-2000s studies had higher effect sizes (p = 0.047); higher-variance studies had higher effect sizes (p < .05). [→ Kelly C. Dreger 2022](#kelly-c-dreger-2022)

## Evidence

### Kelly C. Dreger 2022

Kelly C. Dreger, Ed.D. and Steve Downey, Ph.D. (2022). Reinforcement Practicality for Middle School Students: A Meta-Analysis. Valdosta State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1355660

`q2 · i?`

Moderator testing within each category for the 31 studies. The article reports the survey/questionnaire moderator at "p = .007", plus outcome type (behavior p = .021, performance p = .048), effect size direction (p = 0.025), year (p = 0.047), and variance (p < .05) as significant moderators.

> "A moderator existed where studies that had surveys or questionnaires had a significantly higher effect size during treatment and control phases (p = .007)."
