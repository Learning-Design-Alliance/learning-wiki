## Evidence

### Hong Yucai 2017

Hong Yucai, Cai Xiujun. (2017). Effect of team training on efficiency of trauma care in a Chinese hospital. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060517717401

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Raw-cohort before-and-after comparison of times from ER arrival to tests, using the rank-sum test. The trained team's median time to CT scan was 29.5 minutes versus 58.5 minutes for traditional practice, with P = 0.01 as printed in the Results text.

> "CT scan (29.5 [20.25–65] vs. 58.5 [30.25–71.25] min; P = 0.01)"
