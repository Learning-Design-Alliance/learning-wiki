Min Jou 2026¶

Min Jou, Yungwei Hao. (2026). Dual empowerment in VR-based technical training: longitudinal evidence of developmental dynamics in learning outcomes and skill transfer. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10654-7

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Three-wave CLPM (T1 Week 2, T2 Week 10, T3 Week 18; N = 216) estimated with covariance-based SEM including autoregressive stability paths, cross-lagged paths, and within-wave correlations. The article reports the asymmetrical LO→STR dominance; no printed cross-lagged coefficient is available in the supplied text.

"Longitudinal findings further revealed a bidirectional but developmentally asymmetrical relationship between LO and STR, in which prior learning outcomes exerted a stronger forward influence on later skill transfer than the reverse pathway, after accounting for temporal stability."
