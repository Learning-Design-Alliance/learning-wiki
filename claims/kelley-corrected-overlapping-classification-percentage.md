## Evidence

### Truman L. Kelley; Ernest C. Witham 1918

Truman L. Kelley; Ernest C. Witham. (1918). Communications and Discussions. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

Kelley's analytical correction applied to previously reported grade-overlapping figures, described in the article's own terms as the overlapping "reported by F.J. Kelly". It shows 33 per cent correct classification rather than 17 per cent, and 19.5 rather than 33 per cent beyond the adjacent grade's median.

> "Therefore according to the overlapping reported by F.J. Kelly there is 33 per cent, instead of 17 per cent, of correct classification and, referring to the frequencies given in a normal distribution, 19.5, instead of 33 per cent, exceed or fall short of the median accomplishment of the grade above or below."
