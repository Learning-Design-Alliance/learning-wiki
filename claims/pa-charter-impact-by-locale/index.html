Figure 6 disaggregation of charter impacts by NCES locale type (city, suburb, town, rural), each compared to its own VCR peers. Urban charters show "35 more days of learning" in reading; suburban and town charters show significantly weaker growth in both subjects; rural charters show similar reading but weaker math growth. No standardized effect sizes are printed.
