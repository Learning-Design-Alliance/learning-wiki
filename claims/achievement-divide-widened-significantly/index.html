Karyn Lewis 2022¶

Karyn Lewis, Megan Kuhfeld, Meredith Langi, Scott J. Peters, Erin Fahle. (2022). The widening achievement divide during COVID-19. NWEA Research brief. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/the-widening-achievement-divide-during-covid-19/

Based on comparing spring 2022 MAP Growth results with a similar sized pre-pandemic sample, the authors conclude "the achievement divide that has widened significantly over the last two years." No effect size is printed in the available text.

"suggest a critical need for focusing support to help the lowest achieving students catch up to address the achievement divide that has widened significantly over the last two years"
