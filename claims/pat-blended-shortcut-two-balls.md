Qualitative case study of one interviewed student's think-aloud solution to the Two Balls Problem. Pat reasoned that since both balls gain the same speed, the initial difference of 2 m/s persists, describing the article's "shortcut" solution without explicit calculation.

> "Pat, by contrast, blends symbolic equations with conceptual reasoning about physical processes to find a “shortcut” solution."
