# Inquiry-based science activities increased spontaneous exploratory play in preschoolers relative to controls

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` After a 7-week inquiry-based intervention, experimental children explored significantly more than controls at post-test (partial η² = 0.20). [→ Dejonckheere 2016](#dejonckheere-2016)

## Evidence

### Dejonckheere 2016

Dejonckheere, P.J.N., De Wit, N.L., Van de Keere, K., & Vervaet, S. (2016). Exploring the classroom: Teaching science in early childhood. European Journal of Educational Research, 5(3), 149-164. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.5.3.149

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

A MANOVA on total explorations (informative plus uninformative) in a quasi-experimental classroom study with 57 children found a significant group effect at post-test, with experimentals (M = 6.63) exceeding controls (M = 4.30), "partial η² = 0.20". No group difference existed at pretest.

> "An effect of group on the number of explorations was found in the post -test ( Mcontr = 4.30; SD = 3.13; Mexp = 6.63; SD = 2.54), F(1, 56) = 9.74, p < 0.003, partial η² = 0.20, but not in the pretest, F < 4."
