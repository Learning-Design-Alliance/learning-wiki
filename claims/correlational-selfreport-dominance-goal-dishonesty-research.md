## Evidence

### Tanja Fritz 2023

Tanja Fritz, Hernán González Cruz, Stefan Janke, Martin Daumiller. (2023). Elucidating the Associations Between Achievement Goals and Academic Dishonesty: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09753-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Descriptive analysis of the 163 coded effect sizes from 33 studies: "self-reports of students’ engagement in academic dishonesty (90.18%)" dominated, with only seven behavioral effect sizes; 84.66% of goal measures were personal goals.

> "Most academic dishonesty measures were self-reports of students’ engagement in academic dishonesty (90.18%), nine effect sizes represented intentions to cheat (5.52%) and seven effect sizes were behavioral measures of actually committed dishonesty (4.29%)"
