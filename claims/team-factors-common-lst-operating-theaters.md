Analysis of coded postcourse reports from 21 hospital sites. The results report that "Opportunities to improve the distribution of tasks between team members were frequently reported (45% of reports, 67% of sites)", with team factors overall in 59 reports (77%) and 20 sites (95%); verbal communication gaps appeared in 49% of reports.
