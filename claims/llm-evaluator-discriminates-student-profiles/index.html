Po-Chin Chang 2026¶

Po-Chin Chang, Nicholas Hogan, Aske Plaat, Michiel T. van der Meer. (2026). Learning to Prompt: Improving Student Engagement with Adaptive LLM-based High-School Tutoring. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20138 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.20138

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Simulation validity check across the three simulated-student profiles (Motivated, Mediocre, Unmotivated). Motivated students "yield the highest mean score (0.733)" while unmotivated students average 0.544 with a wider distribution.

"Statistical significance was confirmed via inde- pendent t-tests, showing highly significant differ- ences between all groups (p<0.001 ), confirming that the LLM evaluator successfully discriminates between simulated-student states."
