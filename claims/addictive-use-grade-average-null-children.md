## Evidence

### Tim Schulz van Endert 2021

Tim Schulz van Endert. (2021). Addictive use of digital devices in young children: Associations with delay discounting, self-control and academic performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253058

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis of DASC score against most recent semester grade average (1.0 best to 6.0 worst) among 75 children; the hypothesised negative association was not found ("The DASC scores and grade averages were not correlated").

> "The DASC scores and grade averages were not correlated (r = 0.15, p = 0.197). However, there was a positive correlation between self-reported usage of digital devices and grade average (r = 0.43, p<0.001)."
