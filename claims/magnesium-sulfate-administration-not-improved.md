## Evidence

### Raney Julia H. 2019

Raney Julia H., Morgan Melissa C., Christmas Amelia, Sterling Mona, Spindler Hilary, Ghosh Rakesh, Gore Aboli, Mahapatra Tanmay, Walker Dilys M. (2019). Simulation-enhanced nurse mentoring to improve preeclampsia and eclampsia care: an education intervention study in Bihar, India. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-019-2186-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Video analysis of matched simulation pairs (Table 2 reports 86.4% first vs 82.1% last, p = 0.74). The authors note the 76% administration rate was much higher than in the CLIP study, where intravenous magnesium sulfate was never administered by nurses in PHCs.

> "While the total number of 'management steps' completed by mentees increased, it is notable that magnesium sulfate administration did not improve. This finding is in contrast to previous evaluations of PE/E simulation training in high-resource settings [31, 32]."
