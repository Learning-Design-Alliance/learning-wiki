Evidence¶

Aljamal Husam 2025¶

Aljamal Husam, Alawneh Rama, Derbas Afnan, Edaibes Mohammad, Ahmed Aya, Amer Lama, Alzoubi Hiba, Serhan Hashem Abu Habuser. (2025). Efficacy of mind maps and concept maps in enhancing academic performance among undergraduate medical students in the preclinical stage: a systematic review. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10437-4

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Randomized crossover RCT of 65 Year 1–2 medical students given access to online testable pathogenesis maps or existing online resources in 2-week blocks. The review reports significant quiz improvements with "Cohen's d = 0.8" for renal and "Cohen's d = 0.7" for hepatic disease quizzes.

"Ho et al. (2014) found significant score improvements in quizzes on renal (mean score: 56.0% vs. 46.1%, p < 0.01, Cohen's d = 0.8) and hepatic diseases (mean score: 66.1% vs. 56.8%, p < 0.01, Cohen's d = 0.7)"
