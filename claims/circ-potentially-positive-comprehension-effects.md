# CIRC has potentially positive effects on comprehension for beginning readers, with an average improvement index of +12 percentile points across two studies

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r1` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Across two studies totaling 712 students, CIRC showed a statistically significant positive effect in one study and an indeterminate effect in another, yielding a domain rating of potentially positive effects for comprehension. [→ wwc-summary-1](#wwc-summary-1)

## Evidence

### wwc-summary-1

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2012, June). Beginning Reading intervention report: Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/109

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

WWC synthesis of two studies (one RCT, one quasi-experiment) covering 712 students in grades 3 and 4 in Ohio and Pennsylvania. Table 1 reports an average comprehension improvement index of +12 (range +1 to +30) and a medium to large extent of evidence; the domain average effect size across studies was 0.31.

> "Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition ® was found to have potentially positive effects on comprehension and no discernible effects on general reading achievement for beginning readers."
