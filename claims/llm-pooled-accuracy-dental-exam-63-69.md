Meta-analysis of 39 studies of LLMs answering dental examination questions, using a random-effects model with Freeman-Tukey double arcsine transformation. The pooled answer rate was "63.69% (95% CI: 60.26%-67.06%)" with I² = 91.5%. No effect size beyond the pooled proportion is printed.

> "Under the random-effects model, the meta-analysis of 39 studies yielded an overall pooled answer rate of 63.69% (95% CI: 60.26%-67.06%), with substantial between-study heterogeneity (I² = 91.5%, τ² = 0.0287; P < .0001)."
