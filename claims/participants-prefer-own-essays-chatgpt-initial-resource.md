## Evidence

### Angela Oates · Donna Johnson 2025

Angela Oates · Donna Johnson. (2025). ChatGPT in the Classroom: Evaluating its Role in Fostering Critical Evaluation Skills. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00452-8

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-hour semi-structured focus group with the ten participants, analysed thematically. "participants' preference was for creating their own essays"; they viewed ChatGPT as an initial resource for grasping fundamentals or sparking ideas.

> "While the focus group revealed that participants' preference was for creating their own essays over using ChatGPT, it also highlighted the perception that the tool could provide a degree of flexibility by accommodating diverse personal preferences and learning styles."
