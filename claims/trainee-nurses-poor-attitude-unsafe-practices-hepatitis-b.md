## Evidence

### Aniaku Joshua Kwabena BPH 2019

Aniaku Joshua Kwabena BPH, Amedonu Edem Kojo BPH, Fusheini Adam PhD. (2019). Assessment of Knowledge, Attitude and Vaccination Status of Hepatitis B among Nursing Training Students in Ho, Ghana. Annals of Global Health 85(1): 18. https://doi.org/10.5334/aogh.750

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square association analysis of knowledge score with attitudes in the cross-sectional survey. The article reports 68.8% of participants recapped needles and that knowledge "could not deter them from the practice of recapping needles" (P = 0.012).

> "It was interesting to note that participants' knowledge could not deter them from the practice of recapping needles because 33.1% of respondents with good knowledge and 58.1% with average knowledge (P = 0.012) recapped needles."
