# Most theorized individual difference constructs have been examined in sourcing research, but several motivation and personality constructs remain unstudied

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Anmarkrud 2022

Anmarkrud, Ø., Bråten, I., Florit, E., & Mason, L. (2022). The Role of Individual Differences in Sourcing: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09640-7

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review of studies published 1991–2020 identified 72 studies coding individual differences against sourcing measures. The review reports that "14 of these individual difference constructs were examined in the 72 studies included in this review", out of 19 constructs proposed in contemporary conceptualizations.

> "As displayed in Table 2, 14 of these individual difference constructs were examined in the 72 studies included in this review."

### Anmarkrud 2022 (2)

Anmarkrud, Ø., Bråten, I., Florit, E., & Mason, L. (2022). The Role of Individual Differences in Sourcing: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09640-7

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's analysis of Table 2 shows gaps in the empirical literature: "achievement goals and self-concept of ability" were never examined, and only two of four personality constructs (need for cognition, growth vs. fixed mindset) had been studied, in a total of three studies.

> "Two (i.e., achievement goals and self-concept of ability) of the five motivation and engagement constructs highlighted in conceptualizations of multiple document representation and use had not been empirically examined in any of the studies included in this review."
