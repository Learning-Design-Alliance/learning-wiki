### Higgins Joyce Alanna 2019

Higgins Joyce Alanna, Raman Maya, Beaumont Jennifer L., Heiman Heather, Adler Mark, Schmidt Suzanne M. (2019). A survey comparison of educational interventions for teaching pneumatic otoscopy to medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1507-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Free-text comments from the end-of-clerkship survey of 115 students. Students reported attendings not using insufflation, being asked not to perform pneumatic otoscopy, and being told it hurt patients; barriers also included cerumen impaction, time, anxiety, and patient hold.

> "Student feedback results described a culture where pneumatic otoscopy was neither practiced nor valued by multiple supervising physicians during their rotations (Table 3). One student noted “no attendings used insufflation in practice, and I think this was the biggest barrier to using the insufflator… when I was in a room alone, I was more likely to use the insufflator.”"
