Evidence¶

Backfisch 2021¶

Backfisch, I., Lachner, A., Stürmer, K., & Scheiter, K. (2021). Variability of teachers’ technology integration in the classroom: A matter of utility!. Computers & Education, 166, 104159. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104159

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Variance component analysis of experience sampling data from 18 in-service teachers over six weeks found that most variance in technology-related motivation was attributable to lesson-specific instructional context rather than stable traits. The article reports that "approximately 30% of the motivation could be explained by stable traits, 60 - 70% were determined by specific instructional contexts".

"Even though, approximately 30% of the motivation could be explained by stable traits, 60 - 70% were determined by specific instructional contexts of the different lessons."
