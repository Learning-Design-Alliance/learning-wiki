Feil 2020¶

Feil, E. G., Walker, H. M., Frey, A. J., Seeley, J. R., Small, J. W., Golly, A., Lee, J., & Forness, S. R. (2020). Efficacy Validation of the Revised First Step Program: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Exceptional Children. https://journals.sagepub.com/home/ecx

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Process measures collected from intervention-condition participants at the end of implementation: coach and teacher alliance surveys and 13-item teacher and 12-item parent satisfaction measures (both α = .92). Teacher satisfaction averaged 4.36 (SD = .61) and parent satisfaction 4.28 (SD = .52).

"Both coaches ( M = 4.62 on a 5-point scale) and teachers ( M = 4.94) reported high levels of alliance with one another. Teachers and parents also reported favorable satisfaction ratings."
