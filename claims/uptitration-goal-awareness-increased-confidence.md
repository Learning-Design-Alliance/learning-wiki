### Latif Zahira P. 2018

Latif Zahira P., Nakafero Georgina, Jenkins Wendy, Doherty Michael, Abhishek Abhishek. (2018). Implication of nurse intervention on engagement with urate-lowering drugs: A qualitative study of participants in a RCT of nurse led care. Joint, Bone, Spine. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbspin.2018.10.008

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same qualitative interview study, participants previously prescribed fixed low-dose ULT that failed to prevent flares were reluctant to re-engage until informed about up-titration; "awareness of the goal of up-titration increased patients’ confidence". No effect size reported.

> "awareness of the goal of up-titration increased patients’ confidence in the potential efficacy of ULT, encouraging them to begin therapy and engage with the up-titration process."
