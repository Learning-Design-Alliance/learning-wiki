### Atlas Lauren Y 2022

Atlas Lauren Y, Dildine Troy C, Palacios-Barrios Esther E, Yu Qingbao, Reynolds Richard C, Banker Lauren A, Grant Shara S, Pine Daniel S. (2022). Instructions and experiential learning have similar impacts on pain and pain-related brain responses but produce dissociations in value-based reversal learning. eLife 11. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73353

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Second mediation analysis testing regions responding to original contingencies while controlling for current contingencies (Figure 6, fMRI sample). No regions survived correction within pain modulatory ROIs and there were no effects of original cues on NPS or SIIPS (all p's>0.2).

> "Whole brain correction revealed significant positive Path a effects (original high cue >original low cue) in a brainstem cluster overlapping with the rostroventral medulla (RVM"

### Atlas Lauren Y 2022 (2)

Atlas Lauren Y, Dildine Troy C, Palacios-Barrios Esther E, Yu Qingbao, Reynolds Richard C, Banker Lauren A, Grant Shara S, Pine Daniel S. (2022). Instructions and experiential learning have similar impacts on pain and pain-related brain responses but produce dissociations in value-based reversal learning. eLife 11. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73353

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Trial-level response extraction from Path a regions (Figure 6 supplements); the article also reports that only the RVM maintained original contingencies in both groups.

> "effects in most regions were driven primarily by lack of reversal in the Uninstructed Group, although we did not observe significant moderation by Group in any regions at FDR-corrected thresholds"
