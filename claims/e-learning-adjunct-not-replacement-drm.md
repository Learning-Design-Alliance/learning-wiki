## Evidence

### Saiboon Ismail M. 2021

Saiboon Ismail M., Zahari Fareena, Isa Hisham M., Sabardin Dazlin M., Robertson Colin E. (2021). E-Learning in Teaching Emergency Disaster Response Among Undergraduate Medical Students in Malaysia. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.628178

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Perception survey (Table 6) of the 168 participants. Incorporating e-learning scored PS +0.99, supplementary-to-F2F PS +1.12, recommending the video PS +1.38, while substituting F2F lectures scored PS −0.36: they "“disagree” (PS = −0.36) that DRM through e-learning could, or should, completely replace F2F sessions".
