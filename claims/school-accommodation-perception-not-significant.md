## Evidence

### Russell Kelly 2019

Russell Kelly, Selci Erin, Black Brian, Cochrane Karis, Ellis Michael. (2019). Academic outcomes following adolescent sport-related concussion or fracture injury: A prospective cohort study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0215900

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exit-interview ratings of how accommodating students perceived their school to be during recovery, compared by injury type with the chi-square test: 60 (53.1%) SRC versus 31 (40.3%) SRF students rated their school very accommodating, "not statistically significant (p = 0.082)".

> "Overall, 60 (53.1%) students with SRC rated their school as very accommodating while 31 (40.3%) students with SRF rated their school as very accommodating but this was not statistically significant (p = 0.082)."
