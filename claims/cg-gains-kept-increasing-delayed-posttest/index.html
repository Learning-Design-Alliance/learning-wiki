Evidence¶

Jakub Bielak 2010¶

Jakub Bielak, Mirosųaw Pawlak. (2010). Teaching English tense and aspect with the help of cognitive grammar: An empirical study. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 1(3), 365-400. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136451

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Delayed-posttest comparison in the quasi-experiment. The article reports COG gains "kept increasing from Posttest 1 to Posttest 2 to yield an improvement of 4.6 points", while TRAD deteriorated by 0.47 of a point and CTRL by 0.07 of a point; descriptive values only, no effect size printed.

"This is different from the gains of COG, which were not just maintained but kept increasing from Posttest 1 to Posttest 2 to yield an improvement of 4.6 points."
