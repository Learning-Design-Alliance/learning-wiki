## Evidence

### van der Sande 2023

van der Sande, L., van Steensel, R., Fikrat-Wevers, S., & Arends, L. (2023). Effectiveness of Interventions that Foster Reading Motivation: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09719-3

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Categorical moderator analysis: primary education d = 0.36 versus secondary d = 0.12 on comprehension, Q(1) = 6.08*; affirming motivations did not differ (d = 0.38 vs 0.50, Q(1) = 0.44).

> "Educational stage was a significant moderator of effects on reading comprehension; interventions involving primary schoolers were more effective than interventions involving secondary schoolers."

### van der Sande 2023 (2)

van der Sande, L., van Steensel, R., Fikrat-Wevers, S., & Arends, L. (2023). Effectiveness of Interventions that Foster Reading Motivation: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09719-3

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Categorical moderator analysis: mainly typical readers d = 0.36 versus mainly struggling readers d = 0.12 on comprehension, Q(1) = 5.59*; affirming motivations did not differ (Q(1) = 0.03).

> "The interventions had significantly larger effects on reading comprehension if the sample included mainly typical readers than if it included mainly struggling readers."
