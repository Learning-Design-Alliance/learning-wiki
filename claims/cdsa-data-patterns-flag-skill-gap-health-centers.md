`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective analysis of ePOCT+ consultation logs from 20,085 outpatient pediatric consultations (November 2021 to October 2022) in 16 Rwandan primary health centers, focusing on numerical signs such as temperature, MUAC, weight, height, z-scores, heart rate, respiratory rate and SpO2. The study "retrospectively analyzed data from 20,085 pediatric consultations" to detect unusual patterns reflecting potential skill gaps. No effect size is printed.

> "We retrospectively analyzed data from 20,085 pediatric consultations conducted using an IMCI-based CDSA in 16 primary health centers in Rwanda."
