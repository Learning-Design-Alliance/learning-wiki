### Nair 2016

Nair, S. S., Paré, D. and Vicentic, A. (2016). Biologically based neural circuit modelling for the study of fear learning and extinction. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.15

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Simulation result of the biophysically realistic LA network model described in the review. The authors report that the model unexpectedly reconciled the afferent-plasticity and intra-amygdala-plasticity views: "the fear memory was maintained by post-synaptic increases in synaptic ef ﬁcacy within LA" after training.

> "while increases in the CS responsiveness of auditory thalamic neurons were found to be essential for fear learning in the model, after training they were no longer needed because the fear memory was maintained by post-synaptic increases in synaptic ef ﬁcacy within LA."
