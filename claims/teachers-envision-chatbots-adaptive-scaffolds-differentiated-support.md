### Riahi 2026

Riahi, B., Ozturk, D., Guth, A., Li, J., Singh, D. P., Tian, X., Chiu, J., Lytle, N., Barnes, T., & Cateté, V. (2026). Will It Teach as Intended? How Teachers Configure Educational AI Chatbots. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29993

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Focus-group interview thematic analysis during summer 2026 PD workshops with 27 middle school teachers. Teachers envisioned adjusting the form and level of assistance based on students' needs, with examples including a fractions tutor that could "give hints for answers, correct misconceptions, [and] use visuals."

> "Approximately 15 teachers described chatbots as adaptive instructional supports that could respond to differences in students' prior knowledge, skill level, accessibility needs, or the type of learning support required."
