Skulmowski 2024¶

Skulmowski, A. (2024). AI-Related Threats to Information Sovereignty and Challenges for Research Ethics. Educational Psychology Review, 36, 101. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09939-1

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The author's normative argument in the Ethical and Legal Consequences section: recombination of recorded data streams could enable user re-identification, and leaked cloud or AI training data could be linked to study datasets. No empirical test is reported; the claim is an inference from cited re-identification work.

"Given the decline in participant anonymity resulting from the utilization of devices collecting, analyzing, and transferring user data at an unprecedented scale, it may become necessary to consider all remotely collected data to be potentially discoverable or otherwise linkable to participants’ real identities."
