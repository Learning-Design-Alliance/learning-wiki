## Evidence

### Mahnaz Saeidi & Mahsa Yusefi 2012

Mahnaz Saeidi & Mahsa Yusefi. (2012). The Effect of Computer-Assisted Language Learning on Reading Comprehension in an Iranian EFL Context. EUROCALL Conference, Gothenburg, Sweden, Proceedings. Research-publishing.net. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED574943

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact groups of Iranian female intermediate EFL learners (40 analyzed) taught the same texts over ten sessions, via three software packages versus printed pages. The article reports "a significant difference in post-test scores" favoring the experimental group, t(38) = -5.741, p = .0001; no effect size is printed.

> "there was a significant difference in post-test scores for the experimental group (M = 16.10, SD = 1.17) and control group (M = 13.80, SD = 1.36), t(38) = -5.741, p = .0001"
