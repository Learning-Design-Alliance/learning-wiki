### Kurt Squire 2023

Kurt Squire, Garrison Wells, Maria J. Anderson-Coto, and Constance Steinkuehler. 2023. Casual Games, Cognition, and Play across the Lifespan: A Critical Synthesis. ACM Games: Research and Practice, Volume 1, Issue 2, Article 14. https://doi.org/10.1145/3594534

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of Tetris studies reports that Pilegard and Mayer [2018] found no gains on six cognitive tasks even with explicit instruction across four sessions, and that gains "are most often found through tests that closely resemble the games themselves". No effect size is printed.
