q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Balfanz and Byrnes (2012), a developmental pattern of absenteeism severity across grade levels. The pattern is descriptive; no effect sizes or sample details are printed in the quoted passage.

"absenteeism severity rates in schools tend to spike in kindergarten and first grade, decline during elementary school years, spike again in middle school, and continue to increase through high school, peaking at 12th grade"
