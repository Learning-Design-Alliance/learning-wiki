## Evidence

### Li 2022

Li, B., Yu, Q., & Yang, F. (2022). The effect of blended instruction on student performance: A meta-analysis of 106 empirical studies from China and abroad. Best Evidence in Chinese Education, 10(2):1395-1403. https://doi.org/10.15354/bece.22.ar018

Moderator analysis comparing two learning organization forms coded across the pooled studies. Group learning's effect is above moderate and independent learning's is moderate; the between-group test (p = 0.433 > 0.05) shows no significant difference.
