## Evidence

### Larson et al. 2010

Heidi A. Larson, Mera K. El Ramahi, Steven R. Conn, Lincoln A. Estes, and Amanda B. Ghibellini. (2010). Reducing Test Anxiety Among Third Grade Students Through the Implementation of Relaxation Techniques. Eastern Illinois University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ885222

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-post study of third graders at two Midwestern public elementary schools; the experimental group (partitioned into two subgroups, N = 124 total) received relaxation training. The article reports "significant differences between the pre-test and post-test means" for both subgroups, with t(55) = 2.24, p = .029 and t(67) = 4.07, p = .000.

> "For both experimental subgroups, significant differences between the pre-test and post-test means were found (t(55) = 2.24, p = .029 and t(67) = 4.07, p = .000, respectively)."
