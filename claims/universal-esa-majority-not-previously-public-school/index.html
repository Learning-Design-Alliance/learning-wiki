Griffith 2024¶

Griffith, M., & Burns, D. (2024). Understanding the cost of universal school vouchers: An analysis of Arizona's Empowerment Scholarship Account program. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/682.951

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Enrollment-composition analysis using Arizona Department of Education quarterly reports on students' previous enrollment. The report finds "the large majority (71.2%) did not previously attend a public school" among Universal ESA users.

"Of the students taking advantage of the new Universal ESA, the large majority (71.2%) did not previously attend a public school, thus adding new costs to the state education budget."

Griffith 2024 (2)¶

Griffith, M., & Burns, D. (2024). Understanding the cost of universal school vouchers: An analysis of Arizona's Empowerment Scholarship Account program. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/682.951

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of AdE's 3rd quarter 2022–23 report, the most recent quarter reporting previous enrollment. Students not previously enrolled could include privately educated, homeschooled, or newly arrived students, per the report.

"of the total 61,689 students in the ESA program (both Targeted and Universal), 29,516 students (48%) were previously enrolled in public education (district and charter), and 32,173 students (52%) were not previously enrolled in Arizona public schools."
