## Evidence

### Liu Yutong 2026

Liu Yutong, Ling Zijun, Zhang Zicheng, Peng Xinqi, Liu Minfeng. (2026). Integrating online learning with PBL and CBL: a promising approach to improve comprehensive learning outcomes in breast surgery for Chinese eight-year medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09784-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous self-administered questionnaire with a 100% response rate, assessing five domains on 1–5 Likert scales. Internal consistency analysis revealed "a Cronbach's α coefficient of 0.246 across the five items", which the authors interpret as reflecting dimension independence rather than poor reliability.

> "although the experimental group scored higher on all five dimensions, none of the between-group differences reached statistical significance (all P > 0.05). Given the limited sample size and insufficient statistical power, these null findings do not confirm the absence of an intervention effect."
