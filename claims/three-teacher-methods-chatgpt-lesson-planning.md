# Teachers adopted three distinct methods for AI-assisted lesson planning, with most (60%) preferring to outline a framework first and then use ChatGPT to refine it

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers reported three AI-assisted planning methods (A: outline then refine; B: complete plan then optimize; C: fully AI-generated then adapt), with 60% choosing A, 20% B, and 20% C. [→ Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir 2025](#zhihua-luo-and-rabail-tahir-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir 2025

Zhihua Luo and Rabail Tahir. (2025). ChatGPT-Assisted Lesson Planning for Children's STEAM Arts Education: An Experimental Study on Benefits, Challenges, Methods, and a Prompt Framework. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00513-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative interview and interaction-record analysis of the 13 teachers' AI-assisted planning processes identified three methods; the article reports that "60% of teachers chose Method A, 20% chose B, and a further 20% chose C." Most teachers favored Method A because it retained personal reflection.
