## Evidence

### Nele Claes 2024

Nele Claes, Annique Smeding, Arnaud Carré, Nicolas Sommet. (2024). The social class test gap: A worldwide investigation of the role of academic anxiety and income inequality in standardized test score disparities. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(6), 871-888. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000881

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel analyses of PISA 2012 (480,174 students, 65 countries) and PISA 2015 (519,334 students, 72 countries) regressed performance on each indicator. Study 2a: "β = 0.12 [0.10, 0.14], p < .001, β = 0.09 [0.07, 0.10], p < .001, and β = 0.04 [0.03, 0.05], p < .001"; Study 2b: β = 0.09, β = 0.11, β = 0.03.

> "Consistent with H1a, across Studies 2a and 2b, the analyses revealed that all three social class indicators—parental education, family cultural capital, and family economic capital—were positive predictors of test performance"
