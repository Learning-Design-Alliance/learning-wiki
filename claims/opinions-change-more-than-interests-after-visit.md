Quantitative pre-post analysis (paired-samples sign tests) of sections A and B of the questionnaire. The article reports the printed proportions of significantly changed interest versus opinion statements, with opinions varying more than interests.

> "on average 3 of 12 (25%) interest statements were significantly perceived as more interesting after the visit, whereas 5 of 9 (56%) opinion statements were significantly perceived as more agreeable."
