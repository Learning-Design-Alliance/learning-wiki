## Evidence

### Zhao Xiaojuan 2026

Zhao Xiaojuan. (2026). Psychological characteristics of metacognitive strategy use and vocabulary development paths among vocational university students in a personalized english teaching environment. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04595-2

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis across the full sample (N = 120) at posttest, reported in Table 4. The article interprets this differential pattern descriptively, noting the psychological correlates of each metacognitive dimension are not uniform.

> "self-efficacy demonstrated the strongest positive association with monitoring strategy use (r = .52, p < .001), followed by a moderate link with evaluation (r = .41, p < .001)"

### Zhao Xiaojuan 2026 (2)

Zhao Xiaojuan. (2026). Psychological characteristics of metacognitive strategy use and vocabulary development paths among vocational university students in a personalized english teaching environment. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04595-2

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Same Pearson correlation analysis (N = 120). The anxiety–evaluation link did not reach statistical significance (r = -.17, p = .063), showing the anxiety association is dimension-specific rather than uniform.

> "Learning anxiety showed a significant negative relationship with planning (r = − .44, p < .001) and a weaker but still significant inverse correlation with monitoring (r = − .31, p < .01)"
