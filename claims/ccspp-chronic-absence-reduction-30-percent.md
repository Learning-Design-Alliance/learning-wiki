# CCSPP community schools reduced chronic absence at least 30% more than matched comparison schools in the first implementation year

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the first full year of CCSPP implementation, grantee schools improved chronic absence rates at least 30% more than matched comparison schools, varying by subgroup and school level. [→ Swain 2025](#swain-2025)

## Evidence

### Swain 2025

Swain, W., Leung-Gagné, M., Maier, A., & Rubinstein, C. (2025). Community schools impact on student outcomes: Evidence from California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/541.498

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Matched difference-in-differences analysis of California administrative data (2018-19 to 2023-24) comparing 458 first-cohort CCSPP schools with matched controls; the report states grantee schools showed "at least a 30% larger improvement in chronic absence rates" than comparison schools.

> "Using matched comparison schools with similar pre-implementation characteristics and trends, we find that CCSPP schools demonstrated at least a 30% larger improvement in chronic absence rates (depending on student subgroup and school level) compared to similar schools."
