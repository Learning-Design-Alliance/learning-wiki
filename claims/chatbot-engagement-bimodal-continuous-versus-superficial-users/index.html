Hilliger 2026¶

Hilliger, I., Pérez-Sanagustín, M., González, C., Villalobos, E. and Celis, S. (2026). A GAI-Based Chatbot for Scaffolding Self-Regulated Learning: Insights from a Design-Based Research Approach. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1), 74–88. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9143

q2 · i? · design · r3

Analysis of interaction logs from 15 of 34 enrolled students who voluntarily used the web-based chatbot in Education and Society during first semester 2024. Superficial users (7 of 15) averaged 1.3 messages (95% CI = [0.59, 1.98]) and 0.14 conversational runs.

"a small subset of students (8 out of the 15) interacted continuously throughout the academic period , producing on average 23.6 messages (95% Confidence Interval = [1.89, 45.36]), participating in 10.9 conversational runs"
