## Evidence

### Boscolo-Berto Rafael 2021

Boscolo-Berto Rafael, Tortorella Cinzia, Porzionato Andrea, Stecco Carla, Picardi Edgardo Enrico Edoardo, Macchi Veronica, De Caro Raffaele. (2021). The additional role of virtual to traditional dissection in teaching anatomy: a randomised controlled trial. Surgical and Radiologic Anatomy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00276-020-02551-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Univariate comparison (t-test/Mann–Whitney as appropriate) of the primary outcome, mean variation of final overall score, between the 10 virtual-dissection and 13 textbook students. The means favour virtual dissection descriptively, but the test was underpowered (power 10%) and "not statistically significant"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The scoring obtained at the post-test showed a better performance for the Virtual Dissection Group, even if not statistically significant under a low power comparison (70.6 ± 23.4 vs 66.3 ± 18.0; p = 0.62; power 10%)"
