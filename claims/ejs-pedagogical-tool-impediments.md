## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Adopting source-code tinkering as pedagogy is impeded by established pen-and-paper schooling and assessment, by the dual burden of learning physics content plus programming, by school culture and teacher technology maturity, and by a steep learning curve for both educators and students. [→ Loo Kang Wee & Wai Keong Mak 2012](#loo-kang-wee-wai-keong-mak-2012)

Paper presented at the 3rd Redesigning Pedagogy International Conference, Singapore. Originally published 2009. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1207.0219](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1207.0219)

Authors' stated impediments section (a type (e) interpretation, not a tested result). They also state that established pen and paper education system and assessment makes it "a challenge to create learning experiences through tinkering with the source codes" and that "Established school culture, teacher pedagogical understanding of meaningful learning, technology maturity and modeling skills are barriers". No data were collected.

> "Steep learning curve on both the educators and the students is demanded for learning to modify codes in this new technology constructionism pedagogy."
