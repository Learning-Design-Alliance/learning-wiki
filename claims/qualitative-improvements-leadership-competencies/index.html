Narrative synthesis of qualitative findings reported in four of the included studies. The review reports improvements in nurse managers' knowledge, skills, beliefs, and attitudes, and that "improved ability to engage relevant stakeholders" was recognized through nurse managers' activity. No effect sizes apply.

"Improvements in evidence‐based practice implementation leadership competencies were recognized through the activity of the nurse managers, their improved ability to engage relevant stakeholders (Caramanica and Spiva 2018; Lavoie‐Tremblay et al. 2012; Proctor et al. 2019)."
