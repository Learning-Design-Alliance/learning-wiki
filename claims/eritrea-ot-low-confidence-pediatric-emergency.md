Gyawali Rajendra, Bhayal Bharat Kumar. (2019). Practice scope and job confidence of two-year trained optometry technicians in Eritrea. BMC Medical Education, 19, 303. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1738-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the confidence survey results; Table 4 prints 15 (21.4%) confident in refraction in children (1.0 ± 0.9 out of 3) and 10 (14.3%) confident in management of ocular emergency (0.7 ± 0.8), the lowest skill scores.

> "However, the OTs reported poor confidence in refracting children. The training time and scope for the OTs in pediatric refraction are limited and as the refractive procedure and prescription in children can be daunting and complex than the adults, this lack of confidence is understandable."
