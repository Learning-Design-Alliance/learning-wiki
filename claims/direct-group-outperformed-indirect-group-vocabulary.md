### Naeimi and Foo 2015

Maki Naeimi & Thomas Chow Voon Foo. (2015). Vocabulary Acquisition through Direct and Indirect Learning Strategies. English Language Teaching, 8(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n10p142

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of two experimental groups of 30 pre-intermediate Iranian EFL learners each, one taught with direct strategies and one with indirect strategies over 10 sessions. The article reports the direct group outperformed the indirect group on the parallel vocabulary post-test.

> "The results indicated that the direct group (A) outperformed the indirect group (B). The results showed that direct strategies at pre- intermediate level can lead to higher achievement of vocabulary storage in reading comprehension."
