## Evidence

### Keil 2001

Keil, C. T., & Cortina, J. M. (2001). Degradation of validity over time: A test and extension of Ackerman's model. Psychological Bulletin, 127(5), 673-697. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.127.5.673

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression reanalysis of long-time-span studies (more than 1 day, some over 5 years) found a "significant negative linear trend (R2 = .171, p < .01)" plus significant quadratic and cubic trends, indicating the correlation decreases, levels off near 1 year, and decreases again near 3.8 years.

> "Analysis of the long-time-span studies also yielded a significant negative linear trend (R2 = .171, p \< .01) and a significant quadratic trend (A/?2 = .015, p \< .01), but also yielded a significant cubic trend (\&R2 = .013, p \< .01) between time and the cognitive ability- consistent task performance correlation."
