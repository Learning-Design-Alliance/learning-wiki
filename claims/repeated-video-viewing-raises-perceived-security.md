## Evidence

### Dervisbegovic Selma 2025

Dervisbegovic Selma, Laky Markus, Tur Dino, Grundnig Julia, Rausch-Fan Xiaohui, Moritz Andreas, Holzinger Anita. (2025). Educational videos as a teaching approach to enhance dental students’ practical skills in preclinical courses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07807-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Five-item 5-point Likert questionnaire completed voluntarily (N=82); all four shared items significantly favoured the repeated-viewing group (p 0.001 to 0.021), with printed "effect size" values of −0.334, −0.268, −0.318 and −0.223.

> "Group 2 reported significantly higher levels of perceived security in practical skills Compared to Group 1 (Table 2)."
