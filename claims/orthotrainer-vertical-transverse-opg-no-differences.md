`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Occlusion-analysis outcome of the crossover study: vertical and transverse deviation classifications correlated strongly with the sample solution, "with percentages reaching up to 96.72% in group D and 93.44% in group A", with no significant differences (p > 0.05).

> "The intermaxillary analysis of the vertical and transverse deviations demonstrated a strong correlation between the students and the sample solution, with percentages reaching up to 96.72% in group D and 93.44% in group A, with no significant differences (p > 0.05)."
