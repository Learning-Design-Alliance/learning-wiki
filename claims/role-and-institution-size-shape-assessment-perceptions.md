### Adrian Madsen 2015

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, Matthew “Sandy” Martinuk, Alexander Bell, Eleanor C. Sayre. (2015). Research-based assessment affordances and constraints: Perceptions of physics faculty. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

During the interviews the authors noticed perceptions related to role in the department and institution size; Table 3 reports distributions across small and large schools for the four broad categories, and the distribution of faculty from small and large schools was about even across the broad families.

> "The majority of those who discussed issues around the broader context of the their departments were department heads."
