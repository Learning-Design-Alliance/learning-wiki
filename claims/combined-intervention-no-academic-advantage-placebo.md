## Evidence

### Beckmann Johanna 2022

Beckmann Johanna, Nqweniso Siphesihle, Ludyga Sebastian, du Randt Rosa, Gresse Annelie, Long Kurt Z., Nienaber Madeleine, Seelig Harald, Pühse Uwe, Steinmann Peter, Utzinger Jürg, Walter Cheryl, Gerber Markus, Lang Christin. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu14132609

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-model analysis of end-of-year results (mean of home language and mathematics) in the cluster-RCT. The article reports the combined group beat MMNS alone but "not if compared to the PA ... or the placebo condition"; no effect size was printed.

> "Pairwise comparisons reveal that the combined intervention (PA + MMNS) resulted in significantly higher academic achievement than MMNS alone (F(1,16.78) = 22.45, p < 0.001), but not if compared to the PA (F(1,15.90) = 1.38, p = 0.257) or the placebo condition (F(1,18.74) = 0.108, p = 0.747)."
