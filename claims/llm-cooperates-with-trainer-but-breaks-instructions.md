`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the second role play of the autoethnographic study, the trainer asked for a more pseudo-cooperative attitude; the chatbot "understood the instruction and responded appropriately", also improvising realistic fictional fire details not prepared in advance.

### Figueroa Rodrigo A. 2026 (2)

Figueroa Rodrigo A., Bernales Margarita, Hoeboer Chris M., Olff Miranda. (2026). Towards scalable psychological first aid training: an autoethnographic exploration of the potential of large language models in simulation-based learning. European Journal of Psychotraumatology. https://doi.org/10.1080/20008066.2026.2616976

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the third role play, despite structured instructions, the chatbot answered "Yes, I understand the instructions. Let's start the game!" and immediately began as survivor, showing instruction-following was "not free of errors".

> "the LLM did not comply with the explicit directives regarding timing and role boundaries, prematurely assuming the survivor role and commencing the simulation before receiving the explicit signal to start"
