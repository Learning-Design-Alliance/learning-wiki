### Stephen D.H. Hsu 2010

Stephen D.H. Hsu, James Schombert. (2010). Data Mining the University: College GPA Predictions from SAT Scores. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.1589792](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.1589792)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of repeat test-takers in the sample (2,486 took Math and Reading twice, 562 three times, 114 four times, 31 over five times), comparing mean, peak and minimum scores (Figs. 1-2); the article reports re-taking "only improves scores by 20 to 30 points per section."

> "This analysis is shown in Figures 1 and 2, and demonstrates that, on average, re-taking the SAT only improves scores by 20 to 30 points per section."
