This is the authors' interpretive comparison of two strategy tables, not a tested result. Both tables assume the learner is broadly motivated at the start, though may temporarily suffer reduced motivation during the lesson.

> "Table 1 and Table 2 are similar, particularly in terms of including feedback based on the effort expended by the learner, and also in terms of adjusting problem difficulty according to performance."
