q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of 7 studies (15%) comparing the source of feedback (self-regulated versus instructor-guided) in competency-based simulation, using each study's best outcome. The pooled effect was negligible and not statistically significant, with high inconsistency (I2 = 80).

"Figure 5 shows the meta-analysis for the best outcome with a pooled SMD of 0.10 (95% CI = −0.33 to 0.53, P = 0.64). Inconsistency was again high between studies (I 2 = 80) with effect sizes ranging from −1.38 to 1.07"
