## Evidence

### Pavan Inguva 2021

Pavan Inguva, Vijesh J. Bhute, Thomas N.H. Cheng, Pierre J. Walker. (2021). Introducing students to research codes: A short course on solving partial differential equations in Python. Preprint submitted to Education for Chemical Engineers. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1016/j.ece.2021.01.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ece.2021.01.011)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student feedback from the pre/post surveys and focus groups in the 9-student course. Some students struggled due to older versions of Anaconda requiring uninstalling and reinstalling; the team held multiple drop-in sessions and set aside the first hour for setup issues.

> "Despite our best eﬀorts to make the set-up process as seamless as possible, students indicated this still remained a signiﬁcant pain point."

### Pavan Inguva 2021 (2)

Pavan Inguva, Vijesh J. Bhute, Thomas N.H. Cheng, Pierre J. Walker. (2021). Introducing students to research codes: A short course on solving partial differential equations in Python. Preprint submitted to Education for Chemical Engineers. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1016/j.ece.2021.01.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ece.2021.01.011)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Students rated activation barriers/difficulties associated with starting and applying Python on a 0–10 scale (fig. 8); the article reports the majority rating these aspects as high barriers, with no effect size printed.

> "Environment set up, familiarization with syntax, and lac of aim /problem to solve posed high activation barriers to programming according to majority of the students."
