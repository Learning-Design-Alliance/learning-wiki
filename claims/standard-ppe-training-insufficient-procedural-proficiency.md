`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline assessment of 155 medical students, of whom 73 (62.9%) reported additional prior PPE training, found only "Two students met the MPS at baseline assessment." Standard video-based training alone did not produce demonstrable proficiency.

> "All but two subjects failed to meet the MPS at baseline testing for proper PPE use despite the usual medical school and hospital infection control training, ad-hoc clinical instruction on the wards, and mandatory viewing of a CDC video that mirrored our assessment checklist."
