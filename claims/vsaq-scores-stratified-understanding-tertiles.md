# VSAQ scores differ by self-reflected understanding tertile on both attempts (η² = 0.44 and 0.45), and overall understanding scores rose from first to second attempt (d = 0.24)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` One-way ANOVA showed VSAQ scores decreased from the superior to inferior understanding group on both attempts (first: F(2, 91) = 35.03, η² = 0.44; second: F(2, 91) = 36.63, η² = 0.45), and paired t-test showed understanding scores increased from 9.64 ± 4.44 to 10.72 ± 4.68 (d = 0.24). ## Evidence

### Lertsakulbunlue Sethapong 2024

Lertsakulbunlue Sethapong, Kantiwong Anupong. (2024). Development and validation of immediate self-feedback very short answer questions for medical students: practical implementation of generalizability theory to estimate reliability in formative examination designs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05569-x

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Descriptive and inferential analysis of 94 students' two ISF-VSAQ attempts stratified by understanding tertile. Printed effect sizes: η² = 0.44 (first attempt ANOVA, F(2, 91) = 35.03, p = 0.001) and η² = 0.45 (second); paired t-test gave "The overall understanding score increased from 9.64 ± 4.44... to 10.72 ± 4.68", d = 0.24.

> "The overall understanding score increased from 9.64 ± 4.44 in the first attempt to 10.72 ± 4.68 in the second attempt (t(93) = 2.87, p = 0.005), d = 0.24."
