Evidence¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes 2026¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes. (2026). Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08849

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Essays graded blind by three or four master's/PhD-level Prolific graders per essay (309 recruited), averaged with LLM ratings. The article reports the Session Two effect as 0.33 points (0.24 SD, p= 0.083), with the largest Session Two dimension effects on use of evidence and examples (0.35 SD) and writing style and clarity (0.27 SD).

"In Session One, treated essays score ˆβ= 0.45points higher in overall quality (or0.34SD,p= 0.024), an 8 percent increase over the control mean of 5.50 points"

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes 2026 (2)¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes. (2026). Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08849

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Pangram 4 detector applied to essays from the same randomized experiment. The article reports the TOT estimate as 31.8 pp and that by Session Two "The treatment effect collapses from ˆβ= 22.2pp to ˆβ= 0.7pp (p= 0.494)", with no plagiarism-flag differences in either session.

"The fraction of text classified as AI-generated rises byˆβ= 22.2pp in the AI-allowed group (p <0.001), a 337 percent increase over the control mean of 6.6 percent."
