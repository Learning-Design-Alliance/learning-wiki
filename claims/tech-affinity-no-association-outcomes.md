## Evidence

### Sternecker K 2026

Sternecker K, Schmitz C, Berndt M. (2026). Students’ Learning Outcomes in a Virtual Microscopic Online and Face-to-Face Anatomy Course: Cross-Over Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/82012

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Affinity for technology interaction (9 items, Cronbach α=0.87, n=740) showed "no association" with learning outcomes. The authors suggest this may be because students had already practiced with the Histologi@ system for several months, reducing differentiation within an ATI framework.

> "Technological affinity was retained as a predictor in the linear regression models, because no association was detected between students’ technological affinity and their learning outcomes."
