## Evidence

### Espelage 2016

Espelage, D. L., Rose, C. A., & Polanin, J. R. (2016). Social-Emotional Learning Program to Promote Prosocial and Academic Skills Among Middle School Students With Disabilities. Remedial and Special Education, 37(6), 323–332. https://doi.org/10.1177/0741932515627475

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Wave 2 ANCOVA analysis of the disability subsample. The article reports the Hedges' g of .32 "represents a medium effect" and is "equivalent to 1.78 odds ratio (OR)", higher than 19 of 23 significant ORs for bully perpetration in Ttofi and Farrington's meta-analysis.

> "willingness to intervene showed the largest increase (g = .32, 95% confidence interval [CI] [–.14, .77]), but was not statistically significant."
