## Evidence

### Michael B. Bennett 2024

Michael B. Bennett, Joan Étude Arrow, Sasha Novack, and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2024). Investigating Student Participation in Quantum Workforce Initiatives. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured interviews with six QRX students in spring 2024, coded with a four-code emergent codebook. The article reports "Without exception, QRX students articulated" that the program was their first QIS exposure; all six also named a recruiting faculty member.

> "Without exception, QRX students articulated that participation in the program was their first foray into QIS as a field, if even they had encountered “quantum” as a concept before."

### Michael B. Bennett 2024 (2)

Michael B. Bennett, Joan Étude Arrow, Sasha Novack, and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2024). Investigating Student Participation in Quantum Workforce Initiatives. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.14698)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interviews with six Quantum Scholars students in spring 2023. Four reported prior awareness of quantum concepts from as early as middle school; one cited a Pacific Northwest National Laboratory internship; one cited peer discussions leading to a quantum engineering minor.

> "Together, the Quantum Scholars interviews revealed a level of prior exposure to quantum science not observed in the QRX student interviews."
