# With peer feedback groups added to web-based SRL training (Group TDP), students showed statistically significant gains in self-motivation, volition and reflection, beyond the planning and metacognition gains also seen in Group TD; goal-setting and elaboration gains stayed non-significant

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Group TDP showed statistically significant increases in planning (β = 0.80), metacognition (β = 0.88), self-motivation (β = 0.50; p < 0.01), volition (β = 0.48; p = 0.01) and reflection (β = 0.52; p = 0.02). [→ Bellhäuser 2022](#bellhauser-2022)
`q3 i?` Gains in goal-setting and elaboration remained statistically non-significant for Group TDP. [→ Bellhäuser 2022 (2)](#bellhauser-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Bellhäuser 2022

Bellhäuser, H., Liborius, P., & Schmitz, B. (2022). Fostering Self-Regulated Learning in Online Environments: Positive Effects of a Web-Based Training With Peer Feedback on Learning Behavior. Frontiers in Psychology, 13, 813381. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.813381

`q3 · i?`

Planned pre-to-post gain contrasts on SRL subscales. "Group TDP also showed statistically significant increases in planning (β = 0.80; p < 0.001) and in metacognition (β = 0.88; p < 0.001)", plus self-motivation, volition and reflection.

> "Group TDP also showed statistically significant increases in planning (β = 0.80; p < 0.001) and in metacognition (β = 0.88; p < 0.001); in addition, Group TDP showed statistically significant increases in self-motivation (β = 0.50; p < 0.01), volition (β = 0.48; p = 0.01), and reflection (β = 0.52; p = 0.02)."

### Bellhäuser 2022 (2)

Bellhäuser, H., Liborius, P., & Schmitz, B. (2022). Fostering Self-Regulated Learning in Online Environments: Positive Effects of a Web-Based Training With Peer Feedback on Learning Behavior. Frontiers in Psychology, 13, 813381. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.813381

`q3 · i?`

Same planned contrasts for Group TDP: "gains in goal-setting and elaboration remained statistically non-significant for Group TDP." No gain was detected; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, gains in goal-setting and elaboration remained statistically non-significant for Group TDP."
