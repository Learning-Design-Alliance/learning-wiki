## Evidence

### İlknur Özpinar 2017

İlknur Özpinar, Semirhan Gö kç e, Arzu Aydoğan Yenmez. (2017). Effects of Digital Storytelling in Mathematics Instruction on Academic Achievement and Examination of Teacher-Student Opinions on the Process. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 10. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i10.2595

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 30 experimental and 28 control eighth-grade students taught by the same teacher; t-test on posttest Achievement Test scores found no significant difference, with the experimental mean of 17.77 higher than the control mean of 15.89.

> "No significant difference was observed between the groups by the achievement posttest scores [ t(56)=1,342, p>.05]. It is seen in Table 4 that the mean of the control group students obtained in the achievement posttest is X =15.89 and the mean of the experimental group students obtained in the same test is X =17.77."
