# English learners' mathematical performance was positively correlated with English proficiency, more strongly for explanations than calculations

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Correlation matrix showed positive correlations between proficiency and math performance, stronger for the explanation part (e.g., r = .628 with Unit 2 post explanation) than calculation (e.g., r = .462). [→ Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton 2024](#xiaojuan-ke-kristie-j-newton-2024)

## Evidence

### Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton 2024

Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton. (2024). English learners learn from worked example comparison in algebra. Instructional Science (2024) 52:831–858. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09668-6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations across 78 students' pre/post scores and proficiency. Table 7 prints, e.g., r = .628 (proficiency with Unit 2 post explanation) and r = .462 (with Unit 2 post calculation); posttest scores also correlated highly with pretest scores.

> "The correlation matrix (Table 7) showed that ELs’ mathematical performance was positively correlated with their English language proficiency, and this association was stronger in the explanation part than in the calculation part."
