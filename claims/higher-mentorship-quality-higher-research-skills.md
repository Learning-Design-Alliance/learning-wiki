## Evidence

### Jiang Juan 2025

Jiang Juan, Deng Pengbo, Li Ruijia, Yang Qian, Hu Chengping, Li Yuanyuan. (2025). The effect of research training on research skills, academic performance, and stress level among medical undergraduates in China: a cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07809-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Trained undergraduates split by mentorship-quality ratings (<7 vs ≥7 on a 10-point scale). The ≥7 group "presented better self-evaluation scores" on reading (P = 0.041) and writing (P = 0.033); data analysis was a non-significant tendency (P = 0.066).

> "medical undergraduates rating mentorship quality ≥ 7 points presented better self-evaluation scores on scientific literature reading ability (7.0 [5.0–8.0] vs. 6.0 [5.0–7.0], P = 0.041), research data analysis ability (6.0 [4.5-7.0] vs. 5.0 [3.8-6.0], P = 0.066), and scientific paper writing ability (6.0 [5.0-7.5] vs. 5.0 [4.0–7.0], P = 0.033)."
