## Evidence

### Roach Pamela 2022

Roach Pamela, Hernandez Santanna, Carbert Amanda, Jalil Rabiya, Panaccione Remo, Ruzycki Shannon M. (2022). Anti-Indigenous bias of medical school applicants: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03739-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 595 applicant respondents (32.4% response rate) at one Canadian medical school. The article reports applicants "felt warmly toward Indigenous people (median 96; IQR 80–100)" with no explicit preference and a mild implicit European-face preference on the IAT.

> "Overall, medical school applicant participants felt warmly toward Indigenous people (median 96; IQR 80–100), had no explicit preference for white or Indigenous people (median 50, IQR 37–55), and had mild implicit preference for European faces (median latency − 0.22 ms; IQR -0.54 − + 0.08 ms)."
