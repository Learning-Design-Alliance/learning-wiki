## Evidence

### Leckie 2023

Leckie, G., Parker, R., Goldstein, H., & Tilling, K. (2023). Mixed-Effects Location Scale Models for Joint Modeling School Value-Added Effects on the Mean and Variance of Student Achievement. [doi:10.3102/10769986231210808](https://doi.org/10.3102/10769986231210808)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bayesian (MCMC) fitting of MELS and traditional value-added models to National Pupil Database records for 71,321 London students in 465 schools. The article reports that "Model 2 shows a reduction in the DIC of 972 points confirming that this variation in variances is statistically significant."

> "Model 2 shows a reduction in the DIC of 972 points confirming that this variation in variances is statistically significant."

### Leckie 2023 (2)

Leckie, G., Parker, R., Goldstein, H., & Tilling, K. (2023). Mixed-Effects Location Scale Models for Joint Modeling School Value-Added Effects on the Mean and Variance of Student Achievement. [doi:10.3102/10769986231210808](https://doi.org/10.3102/10769986231210808)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Caterpillar plots of the 465 predicted school means and variances of student adjusted achievement from Model 2. The article reports "Only 117 out of the 465 schools (25%) can be statistically separated from the overall average in terms of their school variances" versus 320 schools (69%) for means.

> "Only 117 out of the 465 schools (25%) can be statistically separated from the overall average in terms of their school variances compared to 320 schools (69%) when we consider the school means."
