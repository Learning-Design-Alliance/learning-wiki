## Evidence

### Fan 2001

Fan, X., Chen, M. (2001). Parental Involvement and Students' Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1009048817385

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The meta-analysis's moderator analysis compared involvement components; it found "parental aspiration/expectation for children's education achievement has the strongest relationship" with achievement. No component-specific effect sizes are printed in the available text.

> "Through moderator analysis, it was revealed that parental aspiration/expectation for children's education achievement has the strongest relationship, whereas parental home supervision has the weakest relationship, with students' academic achievement."
