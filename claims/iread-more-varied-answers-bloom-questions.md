Xiaoyu Zhang, Vincent Dörig, Peng Cui, Vilém Zouhar, Torbjørn Netland, Mrinmaya Sachan. (2025). Harmonizing Assistance: Moderating Visual and Textual Aids in AI-Enhanced Textbook Reading with IRead. Analysis of participants' open-ended answers converted to sentence-transformer word embeddings, computing average in-cluster answer distance per group. The figure-reported result states participants using IRead "provided more varied answers compared to those using the baseline system" for 5 of 6 questions; no statistical test or effect size is printed for this measure.

> "The average in-cluster answer distance indicates that participants using IRead (experiment group) were more inspired in their thoughts and thus provided more varied answers compared to those using the baseline system (control group) for 5 out of the 6 content-related questions designed according to Bloom’s Taxonomy."
