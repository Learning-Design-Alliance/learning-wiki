## Evidence

### Galvin Sheila 2026

Galvin Sheila, Lucitt Margaret. (2026). Student Learning Performance and Satisfaction With a Flipped Classroom in Undergraduate Dental Pharmacology Education. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70062

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective cohort comparison of exam results: 97 FC students (2021–2022) versus 129 TC students (2017–2018), same topics, scheduling and assessment format. The FC mean was "significantly greater than that of the TC" with p < 0.001 (unpaired t-test).

> "Student examination performance is shown in Figure 1 with the percentage mean ± SD exam grade in the FC (59.38% ± 1.62%) significantly greater than that of the TC (51.70% ± 1.45%) (p < 0.001)."
