`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive demographic analysis of 166 participating fellows across the three programs, using frequencies, proportions, median and range from ESO archives. Origins were mostly European (58% breast, 75% lymphoma, 56% lung), with the rest mainly from the Middle East/Africa, Latin America, and Asia, and very few from the USA and Australia.

> "Sixty-seven participants attended the breast cancer program of whom 46% were medical oncologists"
