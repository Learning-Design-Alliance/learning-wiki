## Evidence

### Bischoff Carina Saxlund 2021

Bischoff Carina Saxlund, Ejrnæs Anders, Rubin Olivier. (2021). A quasi-experimental study of ethnic and gender bias in university grading. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254422

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OLS regressions split by examiner gender (male examiners N = 666; female examiners N = 232). The male-examiner female*open coefficient was -0.62 (p = 0.31); the female-examiner coefficient was 1.35 (p = 0.22); ethnic*open interactions were also insignificant in both.

> "The analysis in Table 3 reveals that examiner gender plays no role for gender bias in exam grading. The interaction term—female*open—is insignificant in both analyses (Model 2)."
