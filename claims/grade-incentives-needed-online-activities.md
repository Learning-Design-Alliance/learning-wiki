# Without grade incentives, students stopped completing in-class collaborative activities and out-of-class work in flipped and asynchronous online classes

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r3` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2021 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In classes lacking grade incentives for collaborative activities and homework, students stopped doing activities on time, crammed before exams, and a negative feedback loop deflated group work quality; in classes where weekly assignments and collaborative work were graded, students continued participating and felt they had learned well. [→ Danny Doucette 2021](#danny-doucette-2021)

## Evidence

### Danny Doucette 2021

Danny Doucette, Sonja Cwik, and Chandralekha Singh. (2021). "Everyone is new to this": Student reflections on different aspects of online learning. [doi:10.1119/10.0005795](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0005795)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview and survey accounts of fall 2020 flipped and asynchronous physics classes. Where incentives were absent, students "attempted to ‘cram’ before mid-term and ﬁnal exams" by watching videos and browsing posted solutions.

> "However, in classes where weekly assignments and collaborative work were graded, students continued to participate throughout the semester and felt that they had learned well and were better-prepared for their exams."
