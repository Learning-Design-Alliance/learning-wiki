# Gamified activities did not significantly change students' perceived engagement in the online course

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pretest and posttest medians of perceived engagement were not statistically different after exposure to gamified activities. [→ Chen 2018](#chen-2018)

## Evidence

### Chen 2018

Chen, C.-C., Huang, C., Gribbins, M., & Swan, K. (2018). Gamify online courses with tools built into your learning management system (LMS) to enhance self-determined and active learning. Online Learning, 22(3), 41-54. https://doi.org/10.24059/olj.v22i3.1466

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon matched pairs signed-rank tests (n = 44) on five engagement items showed "the pretest and posttest medians were not statistically different" (p > .05 for all items). The authors note this contradicts prior studies reporting engagement gains.

> "Wilcoxon matched pairs signed-rank tests were conducted to determine whether interest levels were different before and after the exposure of gamified activities. The results indicated that the pretest and posttest medians were not statistically different"
