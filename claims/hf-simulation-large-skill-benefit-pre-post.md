# High-fidelity simulation produces a large short-term benefit in neonatal resuscitation skill performance in single-arm pre-post studies (pooled SMD 1.34)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Meta-analysis of five single-arm pre-post studies shows a large benefit of high-fidelity simulation in skill performance (SMD 1.34; 95% CI 0.50–2.18) with substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 76%). [→ Huang Jichong 2019](#huang-jichong-2019)

## Evidence

### Huang Jichong 2019

Huang Jichong, Tang Ying, Tang Jun, Shi Jing, Wang Hua, Xiong Tao, Xia Bin, Zhang Li, Qu Yi, Mu Dezhi. (2019). Educational efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in neonatal resuscitation training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1763-z

`q3 · i3`

Meta-analysis of five single-arm pre-post studies of high-fidelity simulation in neonatal resuscitation training, four measuring individual scores and one team scores. The pooled skill-performance effect size was SMD = 1.34 (95% CI 0.50–2.18), a large benefit, with heterogeneity I2 = 76%.

> "The pooled results by meta-analysis showed a large benefit with high-fidelity simulation in skill performance (SMD 1.34; 95% CI 0.50–2.18) and the evidence of heterogeneity with an I2 = 76% was also shown"
