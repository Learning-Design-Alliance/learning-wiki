## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2021

Martin O. Archer, Natt Day, and Sarah Barnes. (2021). Demonstrating change from a drop-in space soundscape exhibit by using graffiti walls both before and after. [doi:10.5194/gc-4-57-2021](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-4-57-2021)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative linguistics analysis (piecewise regression on rank-frequency plots) of textual graffiti wall responses, n=464 before and n=391 after. The top 10 ranks constituted "62± 2% of words before and 45± 3% after", with the full distributions significantly different by a two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test.

> "The top 10 ranks constitute 62± 2% of words before and 45± 3% after, making the two entire distributions signiﬁcantly different (p = 8× 10−11 in a two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test, see Appendix A). The overall effect is an increased diversity of words resulting following the soundscape."
