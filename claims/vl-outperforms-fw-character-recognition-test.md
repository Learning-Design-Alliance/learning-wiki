## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Accuracy on weekly character recognition tests showed a significant Time × Training group interaction, with the VL group significantly higher than the FW group after the fourth session. [→ Zhao Pei 2015](#zhao-pei-2015)

## Evidence

### Zhao Pei 2015

Zhao Pei, Li Su, Zhao Jing, Gaspar Carl M., Weng Xuchu. (2015). Training by visual identification and writing leads to different visual word expertise N170 effects in preliterate Chinese children. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2015.09.002

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

4 × 2 mixed ANOVA on weekly recognition-test accuracy for 12 trained characters in 29 children: significant Time × Group interaction (F(3,81) = 7.08, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.21), with "the score in the VL group was significantly higher than that in the FW group" at Test 4; the FW group showed no significant changes across tests.

> "the interaction between Time and Training group was significant (F(3,81) = 7.08, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.21). As can be seen in Fig. 4, the score in the VL group was significantly higher than that in the FW group after the 4th training session (Test 4) (p < 0.01)."
