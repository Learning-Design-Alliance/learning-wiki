### Bouwmeester Rianne A. M. 2016

Bouwmeester Rianne A. M., de Kleijn Renske A. M., van Rijen Harold V. M. (2016). Peer-instructed seminar attendance is associated with improved preparation, deeper learning and higher exam scores: a survey study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0715-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of motive items for active participation (Table 4). The article reports 79% for tracking correct answers, 69% for understanding content through peer discussion and 68% for presenting in plenary discussion; no element was highly demotivating, with repetition rated demotivating by 21%.

> "The most important argument, according to 79 % of the students, was that they wanted to keep track of the correct answers themselves, therefore not being too dependent on the wikis."
