## Evidence

### Kao 2013

Kao, C.-W. (2013). Effects of Focused Feedback on the Acquisition of Two English Articles. The Electronic Journal for English as a Second Language, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1007571

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Q-tests comparing the two feedback types across the meta-analyzed studies found no significant difference in immediate post-tests (Q=0.619, p=0.431) or delayed post-tests (Q=0.049, p=0.825), described as "no significant difference between direct correction and meta-linguistic explanation".

> "Although direct correction (0.695) yields a smaller effect size than does metalinguistic explanation (0.914) in immediate post-tests, there is no significant difference between direct correction and meta-linguistic explanation (Q=0.619, p=0.431)."
