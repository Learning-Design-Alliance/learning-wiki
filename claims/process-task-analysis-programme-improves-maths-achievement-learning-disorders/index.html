Alenizi 2016¶

Mogbel Aid K Alenizi. (2016). Assessment of Conventional Teaching Procedures: Implications for Gifted Learners. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 4(3), 8-17. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.4n.3p.8

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experiment with 65 female students with learning disorders (35 test, 30 control) in a KSA learning-disorders centre; the test group studied the statistics and probability unit with the training programme while the control used the ordinary method. The ANCOVA on post-test scores gave an F Value of 6.89 with statistical significance of 0.01, favouring the test group.

"Table 6 shows that there are statistically significant differences at the significance level of 0.05 following the group variables. They were in favour of the test group."
