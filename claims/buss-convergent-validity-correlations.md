`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Convergent validity analysis in Study 2 (n=340), correlating BUSS tenacity and self-composure with five established scales. Table 7 prints correlations including tenacity with resilience (.613**), well-being (.537**) and grit (.461**), all significant at the 0.01 level (2-tailed).

> "That is, both factors are positively correlated with the self-control scale [96]; the grit scale [7]; the CD-RISC 10 [95]; the WEMWBS [97] and the mind-set quiz [94]. This shows evidence of good convergent validity."
