# Only plate tapping and sit and reach significantly predicted GPA in the full hierarchical regression model

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the full model, plate tapping (B = −0.22, p = 0.02) and sit and reach (B = 0.18, p = 0.04) were the only significant predictors of GPA, with plate tapping tending to influence GPA more than sit and reach. [→ Batez Maja 2021](#batez-maja-2021)

## Evidence

### Batez Maja 2021

Batez Maja, Milošević Živan, Mikulić Ivan, Sporiš Goran, Mačak Draženka, Trajković Nebojša. (2021). Relationship between Motor Competence, Physical Fitness, and Academic Achievement in Young School-Aged Children. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/6631365

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear regression on 130 children's GPA with blocks for age/BMI, KTK MQ, and fitness measures; the discussion reports the standardized coefficients "(B = −0.22, p = 0.02)" and "(B = 0.18, p = 0.04)". No effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The linear regression models showed positive association only for plate tapping (B = −0.22, p = 0.02) and sit and reach (B = 0.18, p = 0.04) after excluding the KTK MQ influence and adjusting for age and BMI effects."
