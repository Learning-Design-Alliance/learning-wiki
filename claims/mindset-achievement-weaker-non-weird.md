## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The growth mindset × cultural distance interaction significantly predicted reading (b = -64.95, p = .003) and science scores (b = -45.82, p = .033), and marginally predicted math (b = -31.81, p = .066), indicating a weaker mindset–achievement association in non-WEIRD cultures. ## Evidence

### Wang 2026

Wang, Y., Tang, X., Wu, S., & Sun, X. (2026). Growth mindsets are less endorsed and less associated with academic performance in non-WEIRD cultures. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00406-w

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel moderation analysis (Model B, PISA 2018) treating cultural distance as a moderator of the student-level mindset–achievement slope. The article reports "reading: b = -64.95, β = -.32" and science significant, math only marginal at p = .066.

> "PISA science and reading scores were significantly predicted by the interaction between growth mindset and cultural and psychological distance (reading: b = -64.95, β = -.32, se = 21.65, p = .003; science: b = -45.82, β = -.24, se = 21.47, p = .033). PISA math score was marginally predicted by this interaction (b = -31.81, β = -.18, se = 17.27, p = .066)."
