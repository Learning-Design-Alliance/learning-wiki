Qualitative case study of two districts and two cities, based on interviews with local leaders in spring and summer 2024. The report's cross-site lessons state that "Strong and consistent leadership focused on ECE" with strategic planning supports coherence; in districts this came from an early childhood director with superintendent and school board support.

"Strong and consistent leadership focused on ECE, coupled with long-term strategic planning, are essential elements of local system coherence."
