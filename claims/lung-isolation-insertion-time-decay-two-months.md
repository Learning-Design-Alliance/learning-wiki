## Evidence

### Latif Rana K. 2016

Latif Rana K., VanHorne Edgar M., Kandadai Sunitha Kanchi, Bautista Alexander F., Neamtu Aurel, Wadhwa Anupama, Carter Mary B., Ziegler Craig H., Memon Mohammed Faisal, Akça Ozan. (2016). Teaching basic lung isolation skills on human anatomy simulator: attainment and retention of lung isolation skills. BMC Anesthesiology 16:7. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-015-0169-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-month follow-up of 14 of the original 30 novices in the simulation study; time data are presented as medians with interquartile range (Fig. 2), and the discussion notes a statistically significant decay of insertion speed of 15 s.

> "Compared with their post-testing performance, there was significant skill decay after 2 months of no practice as shown by an increase in time to correct placement."
