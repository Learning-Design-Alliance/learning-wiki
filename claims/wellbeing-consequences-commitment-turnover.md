## Evidence

### Sijing Zhou 2024

Sijing Zhou, Gavin R. Slemp & Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick. (2024). Factors Associated with Teacher Wellbeing: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09886-x

Meta-analytic results on consequences (Table 2): "Overall wellbeing exhibited its strongest asso- ciation with occupational commitment (ρ = .66)". Turnover intentions correlated −.38 to −.51 with the four wellbeing types; job satisfaction was strongest for the other four wellbeing types (.58–.85).
