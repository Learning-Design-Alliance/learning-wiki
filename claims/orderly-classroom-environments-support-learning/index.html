Evidence¶

Angeline S. Lillard 2019¶

Angeline S. Lillard. (2019). Shunned and Admired: Montessori, Self-Determination, and a Case for Radical School Reform. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09483-3

q2 · i? · review · r1

The review reports an experimental study (citing Bullard 2013) in which kindergartners received science lessons in a classroom whose walls alternated between cluttered and sparse; "they also learned significantly less" in the cluttered condition, with attention coded during lessons.

"children were distracted by the cluttered walls: They spent more time looking at them than the teacher, and they also learned significantly less"
