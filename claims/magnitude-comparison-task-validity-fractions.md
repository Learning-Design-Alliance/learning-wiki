### Torbeyns 2015

Torbeyns, J., Schneider, M., Xin, Z., & Siegler, R. S. (2015). Bridging the Gap: Fraction Understanding is Central to Mathematics Achievement in Students from Three Different Continents. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2014.03.002

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analyses across the six Country × Grade groups (Table 3) showed inconsistent relations between the two magnitude tasks; the article states "magnitude comparison was correlated with general math achievement only in the U.S. samples", raising validity questions about the comparison task with fractions.

> "although number line estimation and magnitude comparison are generally assumed to measure the same underlying construct, i.e., students’ magnitude understanding, strong correlations between the two was only present for U.S. 6th and 8th graders and, to a lesser extent, Belgian 6th graders."
