## Evidence

### Xu Binbin 2026

Xu Binbin, Wang Yan, Mo Yun, Zhang Rong, Chen Hongyan, Zhou Xuezhi, Hu Li. (2026). Factors influencing nurses’ knowledge on peripherally inserted central catheter care in Guizhou, China: A web-based cross-sectional study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-55786-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison across hospital levels within the survey of 3528 nurses; the regression in Table 3 additionally reports tertiary hospital employment significantly associated with higher scores (B = 1.214, 95% CI: 0.371 to 2.056, p = 0.005).

> "Nurses in tertiary hospitals exhibited significantly higher scores (50.19 ± 13.41) and pass rates (21.6%) compared to their counterparts in secondary/primary hospitals (47.44 ± 12.42, 13.6%)."
