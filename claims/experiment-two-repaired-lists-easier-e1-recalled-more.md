# Experiment II: E1 subjects outperformed E2 subjects on shortened lists even though E2's re-paired lists were easier, consistent with incremental theory

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` E1 recalled 4.07 items vs E2's 3.75 on the shortened list; the difference was not significant (t just over 1) but favored incremental theory. [→ Underwood 1962](#underwood-1962)
`q2 i?` Re-paired E2 lists were significantly easier than E1 lists (mean difficulty 1.51 vs 1.20, t = 3.48; rescored 1.46 vs 1.20, t = 3.13), so the recall direction runs against list difficulty. [→ Underwood 1962 (2)](#underwood-1962-2)

## Evidence

### Underwood 1962

Underwood, B. J., & Keppel, G. (1962). One-Trial Learning? Journal of Verbal Learning and Verbal Behavior, 1, 1-13. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-verbal-learning-and-verbal-behavior

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment with two groups of 44 subjects each; E1 restudied missed original pairs while E2 restudied re-paired (negative-transfer) items. First-list means were identical (4.61 of 11), and second-test recall was 3.98 (E1) vs 3.55 (E2).

> "In the recall of the shortened list the E1 Ss averaged 4.07 items, the E2 Ss averaged 3.75. This difference is not significant, giving a t of just over 1, but the difference is in the direction which would favor an incre mental theory."

### Underwood 1962 (2)

Underwood, B. J., & Keppel, G. (1962). One-Trial Learning? Journal of Verbal Learning and Verbal Behavior, 1, 1-13. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-verbal-learning-and-verbal-behavior

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-difficulty analysis of Experiment II using first-recall correctness values (0-4) per item; intersubject agreement on item difficulty was r = .31 (121 items) or .61 when grouped into 24 categories. Rescoring items capped at three gave t = 3.13, preserving the conclusion.

> "For the E1 Ss the mean list dif ficulty of the shortened list, consisting of pairs that had been missed on the first test trial, was 1.20. For the re-paired lists of the E2 Ss the mean list value was 1.51. The difference between these two means gives a t of 3.48, far beyond the 1% significance level."
