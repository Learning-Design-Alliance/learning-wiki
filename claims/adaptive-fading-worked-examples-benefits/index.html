Evidence¶

Slava Kalyuga 2010¶

Slava Kalyuga, Alexander Renkl. (2010). Expertise reversal effect and its instructional implications: introduction to the special issue. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-009-9102-0

q3 · i? · review · r1

One laboratory and one classroom experiment compared individualized (adaptive) fading, fixed fading, and standard tutored problem solving with high school students studying geometry in the Cognitive Tutor. The classroom study replicated the effect on the delayed posttest but not on the immediate posttest.

"The results of the lab study showed that the adaptive fading of worked-out examples lead to higher performance scores on immediate and delayed (a week later) posttests."
