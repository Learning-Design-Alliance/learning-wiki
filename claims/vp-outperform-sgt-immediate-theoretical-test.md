## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In a randomized controlled curricular trial, the VP group achieved a highly significant performance advantage over the SGT group on the immediate post-test (p < 0.0001; d = 2.41), with a within-group effect size of dVP = 3.3 versus dSGT = 1.42. ## Evidence

### Seifert Lukas B. 2019

Seifert Lukas B., Socolan Octavian, Sader Robert, Rüsseler Miriam, Sterz Jasmina. (2019). Virtual patients versus small-group teaching in the training of oral and maxillofacial surgery: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1887-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 57 dental students (VP = 32, SGT = 25) in a curricular clerkship; theoretical test of 20 randomized multiple-choice questions. The VP group scored 60.8 points versus 40.3 for SGT, "a highly significant (p < 0.0001; d = 2.41) performance difference in favor of the VP group".

> "However, there was a highly significant (p < 0.0001; d = 2.41) performance difference in favor of the VP group which doubled its test scores with 60.8 points (SD = 9.6) and showed a stronger effect size (dVP = 3.3 vs. dSGT = 1.42) than the SGT group which achieved 40.3 points (SD = 7.2)."
