### Renkl 1997

Renkl, A. (1997). Learning from Worked-Out Examples: A Study on Individual Differences. Cognitive Science, 21(1), 1-29. https://www.cogsci.org/journal/21-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational and regression analyses of think-aloud protocols from 36 university freshmen studying worked-out probability examples showed post-test scores "significantly positively associated with principle-based explanations and anticipative reasoning". The explication of goal-operator combinations was significantly correlated with the post-test as a whole and with medium transfer performance.

> "As Table 6 shows, all post-test scores were significantly positively associated with principle-based explanations and anticipative reasoning."
