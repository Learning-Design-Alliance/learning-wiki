## Evidence

### Qingyang Zhang 2025

Qingyang Zhang, Rose E. Wang, Ana T. Ribeiro, Dora Demszky, Susanna Loeb. (2025). Educator Attention: How computational tools can systematically identify the distribution of a key resource for students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.20135](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.20135)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 3 compares attention to the lower- versus higher-achieving student within pairs by demographic group. The article reports lower-achieving girls receive "1.4 p.p. less attention than their higher-achieving male peers" (t(1279) = −2.32, p = 0.02), while lower-achieving boys receive a 9.1 p.p. increase (t(1262) = 14.54, p <0.001) across all attention categories.

> "In mixed-gender pairs, lower-achieving female students not only fail to receive additional attention, but they receive 1.4 p.p. less attention than their higher-achieving male peers (t(1279) = −2.32, p = 0.02)."
