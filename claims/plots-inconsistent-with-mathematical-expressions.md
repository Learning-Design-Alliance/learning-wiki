## Evidence

### Maries 2016

Maries, A., Lin, S.-Y., and Singh, C. (2016). The impact of students’ epistemological framing on a task requiring representational consistency. 2016 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.048

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview study in which three students solved a Gauss’s law problem requiring an expression and a plot in four regions. The authors found that ‘more often than not, students’ plots for the electric field in different regions’ did not agree with their mathematical expressions.
