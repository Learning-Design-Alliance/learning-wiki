## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Children with SCD performed substantially poorer on reading comprehension than typical readers (d = −2.49 and d = −3.43) and also had weaker vocabulary (d = −1.56 and d = −2.03), but the vocabulary weakness was less severe than the reading comprehension difficulty. [→ Spencer 2019](#spencer-2019)

## Evidence

### Spencer 2019

Spencer, M., Wagner, R. K., & Petscher, Y. (2019). The Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary Knowledge of Children with Poor Reading Comprehension despite Adequate Decoding: Evidence from a Regression-Based Matching Approach. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000274

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Between-group effect size comparison in the Results section of the statewide cohort study of second graders. Children with SCD performed "substantially poorer on reading comprehension" than typical readers with large printed effect sizes under both classification criteria.

> "Compared to typical readers, children with SCD performed substantially poorer on reading comprehension regardless of whether groups were formed based on the 25th and 40th percentiles or were formed based on the 5th and 40th percentiles (d = −2.49 and d = −3.43, respectively)."

### Spencer 2019 (2)

Spencer, M., Wagner, R. K., & Petscher, Y. (2019). The Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary Knowledge of Children with Poor Reading Comprehension despite Adequate Decoding: Evidence from a Regression-Based Matching Approach. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000274

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Within-group comparison in the Results section using cohort-based z-scores of SAT-10 and PPVT. Children with SCD showed reading comprehension "lower than their vocabulary scores" (z difference −0.40; −0.73 under the stringent criterion), opposite the typical-reader pattern (+0.10).

> "children with SCD attained mean scores in reading comprehension that were lower than their vocabulary scores when based on the 25th and 40th percentiles (mean z-score difference = −0.40)"
