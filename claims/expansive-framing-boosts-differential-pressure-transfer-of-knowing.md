## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Randi A. Engle 2011

Randi A. Engle, Phi D. Nguyen, Adam Mendelson. (2011). The influence of framing on transfer: initial evidence from a tutoring experiment. Instr Sci 39:603–628. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-010-9145-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on transfer-of-knowing for the differential pressure principle in the tutoring experiment. The expansive condition transferred M = 78% versus M = 55% for the bounded condition, a "large main effect ( d = .95) of condition".

> "there was a large main effect ( d = .95) of condition on transfer-of-knowing (F(1,20) = 5.42 p = .03), with no statistically reliable main effect of population ( F(1,20) = .34, p = .23) or interaction effect ( F(1,20) = 1.53, p = .57)"
