"the 247 institutions with 5,000 or more distance enrollments represent only 5.1% of all institutions, but 49.1% of the student enrollments ."

IPEDS-based concentration analysis. The report notes decisions of a relatively small number of academic leaders have a very large impact on the overall distance education universe.

"the top 1% of all institutions represents 29.8% of distance enrollments, and the top 10% of institutions represent 64.5%."
