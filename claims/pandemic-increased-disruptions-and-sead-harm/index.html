Blair Wriston 2023¶

Blair Wriston, Nancy Duchesneau. (2023). How School Discipline Impacts Students' Social, Emotional, and Academic Development (SEAD). The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED628035

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief reports NC survey findings that "56% of school leaders said the pandemic led to increased classroom disruptions" and 48% reported more disrespect, attributing the behavior changes to pandemic-related loss, stress, isolation, and disrupted routines.

"The National Center for Education Statistics found that 56% of school leaders said the pandemic led to increased classroom disruptions from student misbehavior. And 48% said it led to more acts of disrespect toward teachers and staff."
