# Contrasting examples, correct examples, erroneous examples, and practice problems produced no differences in learning gains or transfer of unbiased-reasoning skills

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No main effect of Practice Type and no Practice Type × Test Moment interaction on learning items at pretest-to-immediate-posttest or immediate-to-3-week-delayed comparisons. [→ Lara M. van Peppen 2021](#lara-m-van-peppen-2021)
`q3 i?` No main effect of Practice Type and no interaction on transfer items from immediate to 3-week delayed posttest. [→ Lara M. van Peppen 2021 (2)](#lara-m-van-peppen-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Lara M. van Peppen 2021

Lara M. van Peppen, Peter P. J. L. Verkoeijen, Anita E. G. Heijltjes, Eva M. Janssen, Tamara van Gog. (2021). Enhancing students' critical thinking skills: is comparing correct and erroneous examples beneficial? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09559-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVAs on learning items (N = 170 pretest-to-immediate; N = 154 to 3-week delay) printed Practice Type and interaction ηp² values of .01–.02, all non-significant (e.g., interaction p = .592 and p = .984). Equivalence was not tested.

> "In contrast to Hypothesis 3, the results did not reveal a main effect of Practice Type, nor an interaction between Practice Type and Test Moment"

### Lara M. van Peppen 2021 (2)

Lara M. van Peppen, Peter P. J. L. Verkoeijen, Anita E. G. Heijltjes, Eva M. Janssen, Tamara van Gog. (2021). Enhancing students' critical thinking skills: is comparing correct and erroneous examples beneficial? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09559-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on transfer items (N = 155, immediate to 3-week delayed posttest) printed Practice Type F(3,151) = 0.76, p = .520 and interaction p = .211; no effect size exceeded ηp² = .03. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The results revealed no main effect of Test Moment. Moreover, in contrast to Hypothesis 4, the results did not reveal a main effect of Practice Type, nor an interaction between Practice Type and Test Moment"
