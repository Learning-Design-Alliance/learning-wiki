## Subclaims
`q3 i?` ACCg neurons encode other's reward outcomes (some exclusively), and prediction errors about another person's choices are found in ACCg but not ACCs. [→ Jessica Joiner 2017](#jessica-joiner-2017)

## Evidence

### Jessica Joiner 2017

Jessica Joiner, Matthew Piva, Courtney Turrin and Steve W. C. Chang. (2017). Social learning through prediction error in the brain. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0009-2

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review of electrophysiology, neuroimaging, and lesion studies. Monkey ACCg neurons in a social reward allocation task exclusively encoded self reward, other's reward, or both; lesioning "ACCg, but not the ACC sulcus (ACCs), abolishes social valuation in monkeys".

> "prediction errors related to the choices made by another person are found in the ACCg but not in the ACCs."
