### Linnenbrink-Garcia 2018

Linnenbrink-Garcia, L., Wormington, S. V., Snyder, K. E., Riggsbee, J., Perez, T., Ben-Eliyahu, A., & Hill, N. E. (2018). Multiple Pathways to Success: An Examination of Integrative Motivational Profiles Among Upper Elementary and College Students. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7), 1026-1048. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000245

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of state standardized-test growth scores for fifth graders (N = 158) via BCH equality tests. Only "the Very High All profile was associated with greater mathematics growth achievement than the Average All profile"; reading growth showed no significant profile differences.

> "Again, the Very High All profile was associated with greater mathematics growth achievement than the Average All profile; the Moderate-High All and Intrinsic and Confident profiles did not differ in mathematics achievement growth from the other profiles. There were no significant differences in reading growth achievement based on the profiles."
