Bonferroni-adjusted post-hoc comparisons in the repeated-measures study showed the easy-question mode difference was significant (t(763.1) = 4.17, p < 0.001), with "students performed better on easy CBT questions (M = 2.92, 95% CI = 2.24, 3.61) compared to easy PBT questions (M = 2.65, 95% CI = 1.96, 3.34)".
