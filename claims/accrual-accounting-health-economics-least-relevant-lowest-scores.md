## Evidence

### Gholipour Kamal 2018

### Gholipour Kamal 2018 (2)

Gholipour Kamal, Tabrizi Jafar Sadegh, Farahbakhsh Mostafa, Iezadi Shabnam, Ghiasi Akbar, Jahanbin Hasan. (2018). Evaluation of the district health management fellowship training programme: a case study in Iran. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-020603

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pretest and post-test examinations normalised to 0–100 across the fellowship courses. Health information (44) and health resources management and health economics (53) had the lowest post-test scores.

> "Also, health information (44) and health resources management and health economics (53) gained the least score among health managers."
