## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There is no significant effect of node degree on final GPA for third-year students enrolled before 2018 (students who had failed one or more subjects). [→ Cristian Candia 2022](#cristian-candia-2022)

## Evidence

### Cristian Candia 2022

Cristian Candia, Alejandra Maldonado-Trapp, Karla Lobos, Fernando Peña, and Carola Bruna. (2022). Disadvantaged students increase their academic performance through collective intelligence exposure in emergency remote learning due to COVID 19. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.05621](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.05621)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression results for Group III (n=1,647 before filtering; 638 analyzed) in the Results section show no significant node-degree effect on final GPA; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "Finally, there is no signiﬁcant eﬀect between the node degree and ﬁnal GPA for third-year students enrolled before 2018 (Fig. 3 third column)."
