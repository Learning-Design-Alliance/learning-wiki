# The EmMa course showed no significant effect on early childhood teachers' process orientation towards mathematics

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Repeated measure ANOVA revealed no significant main effects and no interaction effect on process orientation towards mathematics. [→ Bruns et al. 2017](#bruns-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Bruns et al. 2017

Julia Bruns, Lars Eichen, Hedwig Gasteiger. (2017). Mathematics-related Competence of Early Childhood Teachers Visiting a Continuous Professional Development Course: An Intervention Study. Mathematics Teacher Education and Development, 19(3), 76-93. MERGA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1163878

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measure ANOVA on process orientation beliefs in the same quasi-experimental study found no significant main effects of time of measurement or treatment group and no interaction effect. The authors note this could be due to high pre-test agreement or that more time and experiences with mathematics may be needed.

> "The results of the analysis of the process orientation revealed no significant main effect neither of the time of measurement (F(1, 39) = .82, p > 0.05) nor the treatment group (F(1, 39) = .01, p > 0.05) and no interaction effect (F(1, 39) = .34, p > 0.05)."
