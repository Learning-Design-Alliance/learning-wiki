## Evidence

### Michael A. Gottfried 2010

Michael A. Gottfried. (2010). Evaluating the Relationship Between Student Attendance and Achievement in Urban Elementary and Middle Schools: An Instrumental Variables Approach. American Educational Research Journal, Vol. 47, No. 2, pp. 434-465. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831209350494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Value-added specification on the Philadelphia panel in which one-year lagged GPA serves as a proxy for individual fixed effects. The article reports reduced coefficients and increased R-squared values but prints no standardized effect size for this model in the quoted text.

> "To begin, the coefficients on number of days present remain positively and significantly related to GPA and consistent with the baseline model. However, incorporating the lagged measure of GPA has reduced the size of the coefficients and effect sizes of attendance in all value-added models."
