## Evidence

### Reid Joshua W. 2020

Reid Joshua W., Gardner Grant E. (2020). Navigating Tensions of Research and Teaching: Biology Graduate Students' Perceptions of the Research–Teaching Nexus within Ecological Contexts. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-11-0218

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis test on survey data (χ2(3) = 17.95, p < 0.001) across desired career types; mean rank perception scores were 1.46 (nonacademic), 1.50 (teaching), 2.10 (balanced), 2.23 (research). The quote names "students with intentions to pursue careers at research-intensive institutions" versus teaching and balanced peers.

> "Post hoc analysis revealed that students with intentions to pursue careers at research-intensive institutions held more synergistic perceptions than biology graduate students pursuing careers at teaching institutions (p = 0.025) and research–teaching balanced institutions (p = 0.006)."
