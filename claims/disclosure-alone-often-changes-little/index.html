Biranchi Poudyal. (2026). Mapping the Authorized Boundary: A Comparative Policy-Vignette Study of Generative AI Governance in Australian Higher Education. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7210-5480

Matched-case comparison analysis across 20 universities, coding paired changes between disclosed and undisclosed versions of the same conduct. The study reports "Removing disclosure left 14 of 20 classifications unchanged for rewriting and paragraph generation".
