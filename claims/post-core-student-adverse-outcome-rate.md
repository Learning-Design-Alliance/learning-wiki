Taşdemir Murat, Keskin Büşra Ekin, Aydoğan Ayaz Elif, Çelik Davut. (2026). Adverse outcomes of post-core restorations performed by undergraduate and postgraduate dental students: a retrospective comparison. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09270-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective analysis of 160 post-core restorations (one per patient) performed 2019–2020 in a university training clinic with 52–60-month radiographic follow-up. The article reports "adverse outcomes were observed in 24.4% of restorations", extraction being the most frequent at 48.7% of the 39 adverse events.

> "During the follow-up period, adverse outcomes were observed in 24.4% of restorations, while 75.6% showed no adverse outcomes. Among the adverse outcomes, tooth extraction was the most frequent event (48.7%), followed by post fracture (17.9%), periapical lesion (17.9%)"
