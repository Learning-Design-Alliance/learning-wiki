# The Brazilian SEPC scale replicates the original three-factor structure and achieves satisfactory confirmatory factor analysis fit once correlations between items' errors are modelled

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a cross-sectional validation study of 111 Brazilian third-year medical students, the base SEPC-Br confirmatory model showed poor fit, and adding correlated item errors (model B) yielded satisfactory fit (CFI 0.945, TLI 0.934, RMSEA 0.079). [→ Gryschek Guilherme 2020](#gryschek-guilherme-2020)

## Evidence

### Gryschek Guilherme 2020

Gryschek Guilherme, Cecilio-Fernandes Dario, Mason Stephen, de Carvalho-Filho Marco Antonio. (2020). Assessing palliative care education in undergraduate medical students: translation and validation of the Self-Efficacy in Palliative Care and Thanatophobia Scales for Brazilian Portuguese. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-034567

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analysis of data from 111 third-year students at one Brazilian medical school in 2017. Model B for SEPC-Br reached CFI 0.945, TLI 0.934 and RMSEA 0.079, and "the model achieved a satisfactory level of model fit"; model A fit was poor (CFI 0.804, RMSEA 0.143).

> "Confirmatory factor analysis (CFA) demonstrated that the base model for the SEPC-Br Scale (model A) displayed poor fit index values, based on the CFI, TLI and RMSEA. When the correlation between the items' errors was added (model B), the model achieved a satisfactory level of model fit"
