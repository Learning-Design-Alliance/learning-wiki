## Evidence

### Devang Kulshreshtha 2022

Devang Kulshreshtha, Muhammad Shayan, Robert Belfer, Siva Reddy, Iulian Vlad Serban, Ekaterina Kochmar. (2022). Few-shot Question Generation for Personalized Feedback in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv. [doi:10.3233/faia220062](https://doi.org/10.3233/faia220062)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Deployment study in the Korbit ITS collecting around 146 distinct student interactions for 550 exercises; learning gain is defined as "the percentage of times a student answer is labelled correctly by the solution checker after they have received a given feedback". Table 6 reports first-attempt gains of 66.67 ± 16.87 for question-based feedback versus 22.58 ± 14.72 (minimal) and 31.25 ± 16.06 (human-personalized).

> "We compare our Personalized Question-based Feedbackwith both simple and strong baselines"
