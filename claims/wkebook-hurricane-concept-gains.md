### Stephanie L. Day 2024

Stephanie L. Day, Jin K. Hwang, Tracy Arner, Danielle McNamara, Carol M. Connor. (2024). Choose Your Own Adventure: Interactive E-Books to Improve Word Knowledge and Comprehension Skills. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The 271 students in the California sample read Hurricane! in six sessions after finishing Dragon's Lair. The paired samples t-test found a "significant difference between students’ pretest and posttest scores" (t(252) = 14.785, p < .001). Table 1 means rose from 8.63 to 11.07; no effect size was printed.

> "For Hurricane!, a paired samples t-test revealed that there was a significant difference between students’ pretest and posttest scores on the Hurricane! Concept Knowledge Task (t(252) = 14.785, p < .001)."
