## Evidence

### Lin Yuhui 2025

Lin Yuhui, He Xiaoqing, Dai Wenjun, Tan Wenpeng, Xu Yunhong. (2025). The application and effectiveness of PBL combined with MDT and flipped classroom teaching method in acute coronary syndrome education for undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08150-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous author-designed yes/no questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha 0.78) administered to all 100 students; all 100 questionnaires were valid. Table 3 prints chi-square values from 5.138 to 10.714 with p values 0.023 to 0.006 across the seven dimensions. Data are self-reported; no effect sizes are reported.

> "the proportion of students in the experimental group who responded positively ("Yes") was greater than that in the control group across all aspects, including learning motivation, effectiveness of theoretical learning, clinical reasoning ability, literature search skills, self-directed learning ability, practical skills, and classroom satisfaction"
