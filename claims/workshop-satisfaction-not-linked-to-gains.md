## Evidence

### Feldstein David A 2010

Feldstein David A, Maenner Matthew J, Srisurichan Rachaya, Roach Mary A, Vogelman Bennett S. (2010). Evidence-based medicine training during residency: a randomized controlled trial of efficacy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-59

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous 10-item post-workshop self-assessment by the 23 workshop participants yielded means of "M = 4.5, SD = 0.5" for overall quality and perceived ability improvement; content-area means ranged 4.4-4.9.

> "Residents consistently rated the workshop very highly (M = 4.5, SD = 0.5) and felt that participation in the workshop improved their ability to practice EBM (M = 4.5, SD = 0.5)."
