# Just over half of centered people of color receive complex representation, while more than 40% receive limited representation, most often lacking agency

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 124 people of color centered in the sample, 53% show complex representation (multidimensionality, agency, positive influence) and more than 40% show limited representation. [→ Tanji Reed Marshall 2023](#tanji-reed-marshall-2023)
`q2 i?` Among people of color with limited representation, 64% lack agency. [→ Tanji Reed Marshall 2023 (2)](#tanji-reed-marshall-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Tanji Reed Marshall 2023

Tanji Reed Marshall, Ph.D., and William H. Rodick, Ph.D. (2023). The Search for More: Complex Racial and Ethnic Representation in Grade School Books. The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/booktool

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Tool-based coding of the 124 people of color among 280 people central to the 300 sampled texts. The report finds "more than 40% of people of color have limited representation"; 53% are coded complex and 3% partial.

> "However, more than 40% of people of color have limited representation, meaning they lack multidimensionality or agency, or they are negatively described."
