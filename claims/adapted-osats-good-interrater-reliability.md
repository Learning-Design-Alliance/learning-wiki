## Evidence

### Ebrahim Sumayyah 2024

Ebrahim Sumayyah, Kinoo Suman Mewa, Naidoo Maheshwar, Van Wyk Jacqueline Marina. (2024). The use of household items to support online surgical knot-tying skills training: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05549-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Blinded scoring of 71 student video attempts by two independent surgeons using an adapted OSATS tool scored out of 21. Median scores were 19.0 (IQR 17.0–20.0) and 18.0 (IQR 17.0–20.0); the Wilcoxon test showed scores were similar between assessors (p = 0.200).

> "The overall scores showed good reliability between assessors based on intraclass correlation (0.86, 95% CI 0.79–0.90, p < 0.001). The non-parametric Wilcoxon test, which assessed how comparable the scores of the same participants were between the two assessors, showed that the scores were similar (p = 0.200, Fig. 3)."
