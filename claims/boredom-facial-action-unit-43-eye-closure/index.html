Evidence¶

Scotty D. Craig 2008¶

Scotty D. Craig, Sidney D'Mello, Amy Witherspoon, and Art Graesser. (2008). Emote aloud during learning with AutoTutor: Applying the Facial Action Coding System to cognitive/affective states during learning. Cognition and Emotion, 22(5), 777-788. https://doi.org/10.1080/02699930701516759

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Association rule mining result for boredom from the same dataset; Table 2 lists AU 43 with 40% coverage and kappa 0.77, marked of secondary importance. The article reports "a significant association with action unit 43 (eye closure)" and no AU-to-AU rules.

"While boredom displayed a significant association with action unit 43 (eye closure), no association rules between AUs were observed, but some weaker non-significant trends between eye movement, such as blinks and eye closure, and AUs related to mouth movement."
