## Evidence

### Graafland Maurits 2017

Graafland Maurits, Bemelman Willem A., Schijven Marlies P. (2017). Game-based training improves the surgeon's situational awareness in the operation room: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-017-5456-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learning-curve analysis of the 12 intervention participants' game sessions (mean 11.8 sessions, SD 1.7), estimated with a linear regression model with random intercepts. Participants solved a mean of 48.1% of problems in the first four sessions and 69.3% in the third four.

> "This shows a 2.3% improvement in the proportion of solved cases per three-min session (p < 0.001)."
