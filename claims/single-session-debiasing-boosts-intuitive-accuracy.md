# A single debiasing training session significantly increases both intuitive and deliberate accuracy on classic reasoning tasks compared to control

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After the first training session, the training group's initial intuitive accuracy rose far more than the control group's, with a significant Block x Group interaction. [→ Nina Franiatte 2024](#nina-franiatte-2024)
`q3 i?` Final deliberate accuracy also increased sharply in the training group relative to control after the first session. [→ Nina Franiatte 2024 (2)](#nina-franiatte-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Nina Franiatte 2024

Nina Franiatte, Esther Boissin, Alexandra Delmas, Wim De Neys. (2024). Boosting Debiasing: Impact of Repeated Training on Reasoning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101845

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered online experiment (120 adults, Prolific) comparing training and control groups on conflict trials across three reasoning tasks, using a composite accuracy measure; initial intuitive accuracy rose +42.0 points in the training group versus +7.9 in control, and the "Block x Group inte raction" was significant.

> "Statistical composite analyses indicated that the Block x Group inte raction also significantly improved the model for the initial responses, χ2 (1) = 34.72, p < .001."

### Nina Franiatte 2024 (2)

Nina Franiatte, Esther Boissin, Alexandra Delmas, Wim De Neys. (2024). Boosting Debiasing: Impact of Repeated Training on Reasoning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101845

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same Session 1 composite analysis: final deliberate accuracy increased +44.2 points in the training group (M = 76.9%) versus +8.9 points in control, with a significant Block x Group interaction on final responses.

> "Statistical composite analyses revealed that the Block x Group interaction significantly improved the model for the final responses, χ2 (1) = 32.12, p < .001."
