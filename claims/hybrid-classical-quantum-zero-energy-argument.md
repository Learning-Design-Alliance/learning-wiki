## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2015

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Erin Ronayne Sohr, Ayush Gupta, and Andrew Elby. (2015). "Classical-ish": Negotiating the boundary between classical and quantum particles. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.023](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.023)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Episode 1 exchange among the same five students. The authors interpret Bob as treating the particle as a hybrid classical/quantum entity, feeding a classical definite-position assumption into the uncertainty principle: 'It has a definite position and a defi- nite momentum, which is impossible.'

> "It has a definite position and a defi- nite momentum, which is impossible. ‘Cause you know its momentum is zero, and you know its po- sition is right there, which is not possible."
