## Evidence

### Demedts 2024

Demedts, D., Magerman, J., Goossens, E., Tricas-Sauras, S., Bilsen, J., De Smet, S., & Fobelets, M. (2024). Using simulation to teach nursing students how to deal with a euthanasia request. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0299049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Findings from group interviews with 17 final-year nursing students after completing the full module. Students said they would revisit the experience if confronted with a euthanasia question in future practice; some expressed a desire to repeat the module from the other role, without consensus.

> "Students indicate that this simulation practice is a substantial added value to their nursing education. Only theory-led education is not enough to gain a proper understanding towards such a sensitive topic as euthanasia."
