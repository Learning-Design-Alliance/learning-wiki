Evidence¶

Vos Julien A. M. 2023¶

Vos Julien A. M., Wollersheim Barbara M., Cooke Adelaide, Ee Carolyn, Chan Raymond J., Nekhlyudov Larissa. (2023). Primary care physicians’ knowledge and confidence in providing cancer survivorship care: a systematic review. Journal of Cancer Survivorship. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11764-023-01397-y

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review synthesis of eight included survey studies on PCP confidence in managing physical effects; the review reports "between 10-37% of PCPs reported (high) confidence". No standardized effect sizes were pooled.

"Overall, between 10-37% of PCPs reported (high) confidence in providing care for physical effects (n = 8) [21, 22, 32, 37, 39, 40, 49, 51]."

Related Claims¶

  • PCPs report stronger skills in detecting cancer recurrences (62–78%) than confidence to screen for recurrence (6–42%) — related
  • Qualitative studies consistently describe PCPs' lack of knowledge for survivorship care, with confidence depending on experience, sex, and survivor population — related
