## Evidence

### Eitel 2025

Eitel, A., Krebs, M.-C., Schöne, C. (2025). Introducing the FoRe-Squares Model: Focus Support and Reward as Key Predictors of Effective Technology-Augmented Instruction. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10045-z

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports a recent study contrasting mandatory versus voluntary learning units: weeding seductive details helped learning only when the reward of payment was tied to the mandatory situation. The review interprets this via the FoRe-Squares logic that weeding alone did not foster learning when overall reward was too low.

> "a recent study by Wesenberg et al. (2025) showed that weeding seductive details (i.e., interesting but irrelevant information) was effective for learning in a mandatory learning unit (for payment) but not  in a voluntary learning unit (payment received regardless of completion)"
