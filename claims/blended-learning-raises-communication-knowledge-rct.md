## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gross Sebastian 2025

Gross Sebastian, Wunderlich Kurt, Arpagaus Armon, Becker Christoph, Gössi Flavio, Bissmann Benjamin, Zumbrunn Samuel K., Wilde Michael, Hunziker Sabina. (2025). Effectiveness of blended learning to improve medical students’ communication skills: a randomized, controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06938-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 164 second-year medical students, gender-matched and randomized 1:1 after a face-to-face lecture; intervention completed a 90-minute video-based online module with knowledge assessment, control completed assessment only. Adjusted difference "17.02, 95%CI 12.95 to 21.1, p < 0.001".

> "The intervention group showed significantly higher communication related knowledge compared to the control group (73.6 ± 10.7 versus 56.7 ± 15.3, adjusted difference 17.02, 95%CI 12.95 to 21.1, p < 0.001)"
