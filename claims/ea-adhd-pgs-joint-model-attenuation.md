# In a joint model including both PGS types, the ADHD PGS effect on academic achievement attenuates to non-significance (β = − 0.02) while the transmitted EA PGS effect remains similar (β = 0.23), and effects on ADHD symptoms diminish

> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` When both EA and ADHD PGSs were entered together, the ADHD PGS effect on academic attainment was attenuated to β = − 0.02 (p = 0.524) while the transmitted EA PGS effect stayed similar. [→ de Zeeuw Eveline L. 2020](#de-zeeuw-eveline-l-2020)
`q2 i0` In the joint model, the transmitted EA and ADHD PGS effects on ADHD symptoms at home were both diminished relative to their separate models. [→ de Zeeuw Eveline L. 2020 (2)](#de-zeeuw-eveline-l-2020-2)

## Evidence

### de Zeeuw Eveline L. 2020

de Zeeuw Eveline L., Hottenga Jouke-Jan, Ouwens Klaasjan G., Dolan Conor V., Ehli Erik A., Davies Gareth E., Boomsma Dorret I., van Bergen Elsje. (2020). Intergenerational Transmission of Education and ADHD: Effects of Parental Genotypes. Behavior Genetics, Volume 50, Issue 4. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10519-020-09992-w

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Model 3 regressions entering transmitted and non-transmitted EA and ADHD PGSs simultaneously: for academic attainment, "the effect of the ADHD PGS (β = − 0.02, p = 0.524) was attenuated" while the transmitted EA PGS effect remained similar (β = 0.23).

> "the effect of the transmitted EA PGS (β = 0.23, p < 0.001) on academic attainment was similar, but the effect of the ADHD PGS (β = − 0.02, p = 0.524) was attenuated"

### de Zeeuw Eveline L. 2020 (2)

de Zeeuw Eveline L., Hottenga Jouke-Jan, Ouwens Klaasjan G., Dolan Conor V., Ehli Erik A., Davies Gareth E., Boomsma Dorret I., van Bergen Elsje. (2020). Intergenerational Transmission of Education and ADHD: Effects of Parental Genotypes. Behavior Genetics, Volume 50, Issue 4. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10519-020-09992-w

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

In model 3, "The effects of the transmitted EA PGS (β = − 0.09, p < 0.001) and ADHD PGS (β = 0.14, p < 0.001) on ADHD symptoms at home were both diminished" relative to the separate models; effects at school were also somewhat lower.

> "The effects of the transmitted EA PGS (β = − 0.09, p < 0.001) and ADHD PGS (β = 0.14, p < 0.001) on ADHD symptoms at home were both diminished."
