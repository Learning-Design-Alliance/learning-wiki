Evidence¶

Chen 2020¶

Chen, Huili; Park, Hae Won; Breazeal, Cynthia. (2020). Teaching and learning with children: Impact of reciprocal peer learning with a social robot on children's learning and emotive engagement. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/J.COMPEDU.2020.103836

q1 · i? · causal · r2

This is the authors' interpretation (type e) of their randomized experiment: they propose that "synergistic effect between tutor and tutee roles" explains why the adaptive peer agent brought the most benefit, rather than a directly tested mediation result.

"This synergistic effect between tutor and tutee roles suggests why our adaptive peer-like agent brought the most benefit to children's vocabulary learning and affective engagement"
