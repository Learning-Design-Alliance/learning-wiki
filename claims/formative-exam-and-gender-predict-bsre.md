Regression analysis of BSRE scores from 61 GP registrars in the quantitative phase. The article reports "The only significant predictors of the BSRE score in the multivariable analysis were the formative clinical examination" and gender; the β coefficients are unstandardised regression weights, so no effect size is coded.

> "The only significant predictors of the BSRE score in the multivariable analysis were the formative clinical examination (β = 0.83, 95% CI: 0.45 to 1.2, p < 0.001) and gender (β = -9.7, 95% CI: -17 to -2.3, p = 0.011)"
