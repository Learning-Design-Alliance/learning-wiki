## Evidence

### Kaushal Kanica 2026

Kaushal Kanica, Aggarwal Priyanka, Saifi Ghazala, Mhatre Siddhesh, Kumar Guresh, Jain Sumi, Shah Urvin Kumar Prafulkumar, Gautam Sumridhi, Sarin Shiv Kumar. (2026). From Policy to Practice: Knowledge gaps and training outcomes related to MASLD guidelines among medical officers in Chhattisgarh, India. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-026-14169-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level pre-post analysis of 207 medical officers (Table 3, McNemar test with Holm adjustment). FIB-4 correct responses rose from 49.3% to 81.2% (p < 0.001); LSM from 23.7% to 51.7%; CAP from 33.8% to 57.5%. No effect sizes printed.

> "Similar progress is evident in defining waist circumference cut-off values, determining the LSM cut-off value for diagnosing fatty liver, and improving responses to questions about calculating liver fibrosis using the FIB-4 Index score."

### Kaushal Kanica 2026 (2)

Kaushal Kanica, Aggarwal Priyanka, Saifi Ghazala, Mhatre Siddhesh, Kumar Guresh, Jain Sumi, Shah Urvin Kumar Prafulkumar, Gautam Sumridhi, Sarin Shiv Kumar. (2026). From Policy to Practice: Knowledge gaps and training outcomes related to MASLD guidelines among medical officers in Chhattisgarh, India. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-026-14169-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level null results from the same pre-post questionnaire (Table 3): estimated MASLD prevalence in India was answered correctly by only 14% pre-test and 15.9% post-test (p = 0.6835); metabolic syndrome parameters p = 0.5154; reporting dates p = 0.3713.

> "Despite participating in a training session, approximately 84% of these medical officers continued to underestimate the burden of MASLD."
