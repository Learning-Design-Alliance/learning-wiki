# Publication-bias tests found no evidence of substantial publication bias in the meta-analytic sample

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Egger's regression test and the conditional PET-PEESE procedure showed no evidence of publication bias; adjusted and unadjusted overall effects were comparable. [→ Mok 2022](#mok-2022)

## Evidence

### Mok 2022

Mok, S. Y., Rupp, D., & Holzberger, D. (2022). What kind of individual support activities in interventions foster pre-service and beginning teachers' self-efficacy? A meta-analysis. Preprint submitted for peer-review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100552

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Publication-bias assessment of the meta-analytic sample using Egger's regression test and the conditional PET-PEESE estimator. Egger's test was non-significant (p = .908); the PET-PEESE intercept (β0 = 0.689) was comparable to the dependency-adjusted g = .678.

> "We found no evidence for an asymmetry of published studies based on the Egger's regression test (b = 0.634, z = -0.115, p = .908, CI[0.409; 0.859])."
