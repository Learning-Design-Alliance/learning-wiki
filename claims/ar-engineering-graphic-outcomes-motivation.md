## Subclaims
`q2 i?` First-year mechanical engineering students who used AR technology in graphic engineering had substantially better academic outcomes and higher motivation than a control group using conventional class notes. [→ Godoy 2022](#godoy-2022)

## Evidence

### Godoy 2022

Godoy, C. H., Jr. (2022). A Review of Augmented Reality Apps for an AR-Based STEM Education Framework. Southeast Asian Journal of STEM Education, 3(1). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.07024](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.07024)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review reports Martin-Gutierrez et al.'s (2014) study of twenty-five first-year Mechanical Engineering students using AR against a control group of twenty-two classmates using conventional notes, with an analysis and two surveys on material quality, satisfaction, and motivation. No effect size is printed.

> "The findings revealed a substantial statistical disparity in their academic outcomes and appeared to be higher in the experimental community; this community also displayed a higher motivation level than the control group."
