## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2020

Martin O. Archer. (2020). Schools of all backgrounds can do physics research: On the accessibility and equity of the PRiSE approach to independent research projects. arXiv. [doi:10.5194/gc-2020-37](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-2020-37)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-years survival analysis (Fig. 5) of schools that began projects: successful completers were "far more likely to participate again at 70 ± 10%" versus non-completers at 26 ± 11%; overall year-on-year drop-off was 45 ± 10% averaged over the first 2 years.

> "schools which have been able to successfully complete at least one year (green) are far more likely to participate again at 70 ± 10% compared to those which have not (purple) at 26 ± 11%."

### Martin O. Archer 2020 (2)

Martin O. Archer. (2020). Schools of all backgrounds can do physics research: On the accessibility and equity of the PRiSE approach to independent research projects. arXiv. [doi:10.5194/gc-2020-37](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-2020-37)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Figure 6 plots each school's years completed against years involved; the authors observe "completion rates increased as schools were involved for more years" but explicitly caution about interpreting possible causation behind this correlation.

> "A clear trend can be seen that completion rates increased as schools were involved for more years."
