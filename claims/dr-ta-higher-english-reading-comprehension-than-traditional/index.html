Evidence¶

Chaemsai 2016¶

Chaemsai, R. & Rattanavich, S. (2016). The Directed Reading-Thinking Activity (DR-TA) and the Traditional Approach Using Tales of Virtue based on His Majesty the King’s Teaching Concepts in Seventh Grade Students’ Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 9(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n9p18

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-way MANOVA comparing experimental (DR-TA) and control groups in a randomized control group pretest-posttest design with 100 students. The experimental group posttest mean was 23.04 versus 20.46 in the control group, and the article reports the experimental group "scored significantly higher than control group in English reading comprehension".

"The experimental group scored significantly higher than control group in English reading comprehension and ethical awareness. (F-statistic = 8.24, p-value = .005 in English reading comprehension; and F-statistic =8.77,p-value = 004 in ethical awareness)."
