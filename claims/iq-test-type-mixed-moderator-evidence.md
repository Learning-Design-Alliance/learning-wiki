## Evidence

### Kim 2005

Kim, K. H. (2005). Can Only Intelligent People Be Creative? A Meta-Analysis. The Journal of Secondary Gifted Education, XVI(2/3), 57-66. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Can+Only+Intelligent+People+Be+Creative

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis across seven IQ test categories (e.g., TCMT, CTMM, WISC, SCAT, STEP, PPVT). The CTMM–STEP contrast was significant, but in the weighted regression IQ tests were not significant (β = -.00170, z = -.53790, p = .59), so the article concludes IQ tests did not independently explain variation.

> "Post hoc contrasts revealed statistically significant differences (p < .0001) between CTMM and STEP. On an a priori basis, CTMM (r = .191) and STEP (r = .096) were also statistically different, χ2 = 27.943, p < .0001."
