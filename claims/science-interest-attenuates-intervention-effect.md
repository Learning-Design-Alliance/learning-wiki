## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adding interest in science as a covariate lowered the experimental-group coefficient from 0.41 to 0.32 so it was no longer statistically significant, but the Sobel test for mediation was nonsignificant (1.54, p = 0.12). [→ Reichert et al. 2015](#reichert-et-al-2015)

## Evidence

### Reichert et al. 2015

Collin Reichert, Cinzia Cervato, Dale Niederhauser, and Michael D. Larsen. (2015). Understanding Atmospheric Carbon Budgets: Teaching Students Conservation of Mass. Journal of Geoscience Education 63, 222–232. https://doi.org/10.5408/14-055.1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear-model covariate analysis (Table III) of gain scores, fitting six models with AIC comparison. The article reports the Sobel test "produced a nonsigniﬁcant test statistic of 1.54 ( p = 0.12)", so interest in science did not qualify as a mediating variable.

> "In the ﬁfth model, the impact of the interest-in-science covariate on the coefﬁcient for experimental group is to decrease the coefﬁcient so that it is not statistically signiﬁcant."
