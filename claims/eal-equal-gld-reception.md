## Evidence

### Whiteside 2017

Whiteside, Katie E.; Gooch, Debbie; Norbury, Courtenay F. (2017). English Language Proficiency and Early School Attainment Among Children Learning English as an Additional Language. Child Development, 88(3), 812-827. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12615

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical logistic regression predicting good level of development on the EYFSP; EAL status was not significant (OR = 1.28, 95% CI 0.87–1.89, p = .207) and the interaction was not significant (p = .258), while CCC-S score was (OR = 0.82, p < .001).

> "This implies that, across the continuum of English language proficiency, children with EAL and monolingual peers with comparable language proficiency were equally likely to achieve a good level of development in reception year."
