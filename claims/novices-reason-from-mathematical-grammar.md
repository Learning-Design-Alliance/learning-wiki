Anecdotal classroom report from a second-semester algebra-based physics class quiz on the electric field definition. The instructor reports that "more than half of nearly 200 students thought that the answer should be (c)", reflecting reasoning from the equation E = F/q as grammar despite quoting that field is independent of test charge.
