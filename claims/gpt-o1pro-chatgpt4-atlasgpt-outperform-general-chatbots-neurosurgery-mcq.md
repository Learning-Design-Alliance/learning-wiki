## Evidence

### Çamlar Mahmut 2025

Çamlar Mahmut, Sevgi Umut Tan, Erol Gökberk, Karakaş Furkan, Doğruel Yücel, Güngör Abuzer. (2025). Comparative performance of neurosurgery-specific, peer-reviewed versus general AI chatbots in bilingual board examinations: evaluating accuracy, consistency, and error minimization strategies. Acta Neurochirurgica 167(1):241. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-025-06628-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Benchmark evaluation of six LLMs on 599 questions (300 English from a Thieme question bank, 299 Turkish Board examination items), analyzed with proportion z-tests. The article reports the top three models surpassed the other three (p < 0.001), with no significant difference between ChatGPT-o1pro and ChatGPT-4 (p = 0.06) or AtlasGPT (p = 0.08).

> "ChatGPT-o1pro demonstrated a significantly higher correct response rate than Gemini, Copilot, and ChatGPT-3.5 (p < 0.001) did. ChatGPT-4 and AtlasGPT also surpassed these three models (p < 0.001)."
