## Evidence

### Bansbach Joachim 2024

Bansbach Joachim, Bentele Michael, Bollinger Matthias, Bentele Stefanie, Langenhan Ronny, Gerber Bianka, Trifunovic-Koenig Milena, Bushuven Stefan. (2024). Self-assessment and learning motivation in emergency point-of-care ultrasound: an online pilot investigation in German physicians. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-024-01154-z

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation in the same survey. The article reports "r = 0.36, p = 0.01" for external regulation and interprets that physicians who overestimated their performance "tend to report greater levels of extrinsic motivation".

> "the self-evaluation to accuracy ratio was significantly positively correlated with external regulation (r = 0.36, p = 0.01), indicating a medium effect size. This implies that physicians who have a higher self-evaluation to accuracy ratio (indicating overestimation of their performance) tend to report greater levels of extrinsic motivation."
