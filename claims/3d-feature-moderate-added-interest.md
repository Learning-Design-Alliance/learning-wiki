## Evidence

### Vrillon Agathe 2022

Vrillon Agathe, Gonzales-Marabal Laurent, Ceccaldi Pierre-François, Plaisance Patrick, Desrentes Eric, Paquet Claire, Dumurgier Julien. (2022). Using virtual reality in lumbar puncture training improves students learning experience. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03317-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey item comparing perceived value of the 3D feature against 2D video, administered to all 168 participants; the Mann-Whitney comparison between the two cohorts was not significant (p = 0.0733). No effect size is printed.

> "The added value of the 3D 180-degree characteristic of video compared to a 2D video was quoted to M = 3.9 ± 1.1 on a 5-point scale and did not differ between groups"
