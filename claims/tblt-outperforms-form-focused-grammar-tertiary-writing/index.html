Evidence¶

Saraç 2018¶

Saraç, H. S. (2018). Completing the Task Procedure or Focusing on Form: Contextualizing Grammar Instruction via Task-Based Teaching. College Quarterly, 21(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1169343

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact course sections (experimental n=27, control n=26) analyzed with an independent samples t-test in SPSS; the article reports "The p value (sig-2 tailed= .000) is less than 0.05" and a mean difference of 12.13248.

"When the mean scores of the experimental and control groups are compared, it is seen that the experimental group achieved a higher mean score (the experimental group = 78.5556 and the control group = 66.4231)."
