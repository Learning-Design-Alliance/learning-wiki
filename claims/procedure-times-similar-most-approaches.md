Mixed linear models of procedure time (seconds) by group with academic year as covariate, from the randomized trial's results section for the interscalenic approach: "no statistically significant differences based on the educational methods used" (simulator β=79.38, P=.01; traditional β=74.36, P=.03).

> "The analysis revealed no statistically significant differences based on the educational methods used. Both intervention groups (traditional education and simulator-based education) exhibited positive effects. The simulator-based group achieved β of 79.38 (95% CI 24.78-164.73; P=.01), whereas the traditional teaching group achieved β of 74.36 (95% CI 9.03-145.23; P=.03"
