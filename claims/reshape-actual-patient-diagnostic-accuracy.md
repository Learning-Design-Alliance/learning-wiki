`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Diagnostic accuracy evaluation 14 to 22 months posttraining in the pilot trial; psychiatrist blinded to PCP diagnosis administered the Nepali-validated CIDI. The article reports "13 of 14 patients diagnosed with psychosis by the control PCPs were false positives, and 8 of 14 patients diagnosed with psychosis by RESHAPE-trained PCPs were false positives."

> "For actual patient diagnoses confirmed with the psychiatrist-administered CIDI, 72.5% (29 of 40) of patients in the RESHAPE group were correctly diagnosed and 34.5% (10 of 29) in the control group."
