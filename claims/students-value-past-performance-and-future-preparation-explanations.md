### Nicholas Mattei 2014

Nicholas Mattei, Thomas Dodson, Joshua T. Guerin, Judy Goldsmith, Joan M. Mazur. (2014). Lessons Learned from Development of a Software Tool to Support Academic Advising. [doi:10.1109/aseezone1.2014.6820659](https://doi.org/10.1109/aseezone1.2014.6820659)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paper survey (ESS) of 65 CS-course and 130 psychology-course students evaluating advice generated for two fictional students. The authors state this "validated our method of advising support", aligning student decision factors with the system's case-based and model-based explanation methods.

> "47 of 62 (75%) in the CS group and 104 of 130 (80%) in the PSY group indicated that they considered how past students in their situation performed and/or how a course would prepare them for future courses to be important when making a decision."
