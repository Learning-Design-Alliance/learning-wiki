# Three months of daily computer-based MSL training in L3 English produced significant pre-post gains for the Test Dyslexic group on L2 and L3 rapid naming and L3 receptive vocabulary tasks, while control groups did not improve

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2015 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The Test Dyslexic group made significant gains over time on DIGRAN-L2, DIGRAN-L3 and PICRAN-L3 rapid naming tasks, whereas the Control Dyslexic group scored significantly lower on post-PICRAN-L2 and post-PICRAN-L3 than on the corresponding pretests. [→ Pfenninger 2015](#pfenninger-2015)
`q2 i?` The Test Dyslexic group made significant gains on L3 receptive vocabulary (LISTEN-L3) to the extent that they caught up with the non-dyslexic readers, while the Control Dyslexic group scored significantly lower than the two non-dyslexic groups on pretest and posttest. [→ Pfenninger 2015 (2)](#pfenninger-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Pfenninger 2015

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon pre-post comparisons within groups in the three-month intervention study; the Test Dyslexic group improved on three rapid naming tests while the Control Dyslexic group "showed the opposite pattern", scoring significantly lower on post-PICRAN-L2 and post-PICRAN-L3.

> "While the Test Dyslexic group was able to make significant gains over time on the DIGRAN-L2 test (z = 2.42, p = .02), the DIGRAN-L3 test (z = 2.78, p = .005) and the PICRAN-L3 test (z = 2.68, p = .007), the Control Dyslexic group showed the opposite pattern"

### Pfenninger 2015 (2)

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post Wilcoxon comparison on the L3 receptive vocabulary sentence-comprehension task (maximum = 6) in the intervention study; the Test Dyslexic group reached ceiling-level performance, and the Control Dyslexic group remained significantly lower (U = 100, p < .001).

> "The Test Dyslexic group made significant gains over time (pre-LISTEN-L3 vs. post-LISTEN-L3: z = -2.78, p = .005), to the extent that they caught up with the non-dyslexic readers"
