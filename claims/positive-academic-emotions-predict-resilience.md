## Evidence

### Yan Yan 2022

Yan Yan, Gai Xiaosong. (2022). High Achievers from Low Family Socioeconomic Status Families: Protective Factors for Academically Resilient Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315882

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression (Table 4): positive emotions OR = 1.40 (grade 6), 1.17 (grade 9), 1.30 (grade 11), all p < 0.001. The article recommends improving learning experiences and creating a positive classroom environment. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "the likelihood of becoming a resilient student increased by 40%, 17%, and 30% (p < 0.001) correspondingly for each unit increase in positive academic emotion among disadvantaged students."
