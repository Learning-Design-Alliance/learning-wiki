`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear regression on complete cases (n = 88) adjusting for gender, age, experience and education; negative coefficients indicate lower knowledge scores, i.e. better knowledge. The 51–60 age coefficient was "β = −1.81, 95% CI: −3.30 to −0.31, p = 0.019" and the >10 years experience coefficient was β = −1.26 (95% CI: −2.45 to −0.07, p = 0.039).

> "nurses in the 51–60 age group demonstrated a statistically significant association compared with the reference group (< 30 years) (β = −1.81, 95% CI: −3.30 to −0.31, p = 0.019)"
