# GPA treatment effects among lower-achieving students were larger in schools whose peer norms supported challenge-seeking

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q4` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` The intervention × behavioural challenge-seeking norms interaction was positive for GPA among lower-achieving adolescents, β = 0.23, P = 0.029. [→ Yeager 2019](#yeager-2019)

## Evidence

### Yeager 2019

Yeager, D. S., Hanselman, P., Walton, G. M., Murray, J. S., Crosnoe, R., Muller, C., Tipton, E., Schneider, B., Hulleman, C. S., Hinojosa, C. P., Paunesku, D., Romero, C., Flint, K., Roberts, A., Trott, J., Iachan, R., Buontempo, J., Yang, S. M., Carvalho, C. M., Hahn, P. R., Gopalan, M., Mhatre, P., Ferguson, R., Duckworth, A. `q4 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Peer norms measured via the make-a-math-worksheet challenge-seeking task aggregated from control-group students at the school level; standardized β = 0.23 (a medium standardized coefficient). Combined CATEs in low/medium-achieving schools were 0.14–0.18 grade points at the third and fourth norm quartiles.

> "the intervention produced a greater difference in end-of-year GPAs relative to the control group when the behavioural norm that surrounded students was supportive of the growth mindset belief system, B = 0.11 (95% confidence interval = 0.01, 0.21), s.e. = 0.05, z = 2.18, n = 6,320, k = 65, P = 0.029, β = 0.23"
