Slavin 2014¶

Slavin, R. E. (2014). Cooperative Learning and Academic Achievement: Why Does Groupwork Work?. Anales De Psicología, 30(3), 785–791. https://doi.org/10.6018/analesps.30.3.201201

The review reports, from its synthesis of practical classroom research on cooperative learning, that evidence "primarily supports the motivational perspective." No pooled effect size is printed; this is the review's own narrative assessment of the evidence base.

"Evidence from practical classroom research primarily supports the motivational perspective, which emphasizes the use of group goals and individual accountability for group success."
