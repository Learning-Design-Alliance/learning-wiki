### Rouble 2023

Rouble, M., Dobbs, M., & Gilbert, A. (2023). WinterLab: Developing a low-cost, portable experiment platform to encourage engagement in the electronics lab. International Journal of Designs for Learning. [doi:10.14434/ijdl.v14i1.33406](https://doi.org/10.14434/ijdl.v14i1.33406)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Field testing of the 2019 pilot laboratory section (16 of 25 pilot students were study volunteers) using anonymized surveys administered during the course. Responses were positive; one student described the platform as "[w]ell explained and easy to understand how to use."

> "student responses to the WinterLab platform were positive throughout the laboratory sessions, and this is bolstered by their responses to the set of anonymized surveys."
