### Bitar Anas 2024

Bitar Anas, Barakat Fady, Hawat Angie, Alsaid Bayan. (2024). Dietary and smoking habits during the exam period and their effect on the academic achievement among Syrian medical students. BMC Medical Education 24:60. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04950-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the multiple linear regression for students' exam scores (n = 608), "Water pipe consumption exhibited a negative association with academic performance in the regression analysis (B = -0.700, 95% CI -1.396 – -0.005, p = 0.049)". The coefficient is unstandardized; no standardised effect size is printed.

### Bitar Anas 2024 (2)

Bitar Anas, Barakat Fady, Hawat Angie, Alsaid Bayan. (2024). Dietary and smoking habits during the exam period and their effect on the academic achievement among Syrian medical students. BMC Medical Education 24:60. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04950-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`
