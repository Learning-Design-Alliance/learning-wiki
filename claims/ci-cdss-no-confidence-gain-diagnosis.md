`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey-based arm-by-time analysis from the same clinic-randomized trial. Overall diagnostic-confidence difference between "CI-CDSS clinics" and usual-care clinics was not significant; the article also reports no differences on specific diagnostic steps.

> "Clinicians in CI-CDSS clinics reported more confidence in diagnosing CI at follow-up than at baseline, but this change was not significantly different from that reported by clinicians in UC clinics (AME = 8.2; OR = 1.56, 95% CI: 0.75, 3.25)."
