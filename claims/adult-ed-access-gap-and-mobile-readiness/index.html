The introduction cites national statistics to frame demand versus capacity, stating programs "can only handle about four million of those 36 million learners." Figures are attributed to cited reports; the brief reports no new analysis.

"Access to quality education programs is limited at best — our current adult education programs can only handle about four million of those 36 million learners."

Designing Technology for Adult Learners: Applying Adult Learning Theory 2016 (2)¶

Designing Technology for Adult Learners: Applying Adult Learning Theory. (2016). https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/bb3f892f-a74d-4da1-8086-0334fd227316

The introduction reports survey data on technology access, stating "an estimated 75 percent of students enrolled in adult education programs own smart phones," which the report uses to argue mobile technology is a viable learning channel for this population.

"According to a Tyton Partners report, an estimated 75 percent of students enrolled in adult education programs own smart phones."
