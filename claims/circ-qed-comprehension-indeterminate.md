## Evidence

### bramlett-comprehension

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2012, June). Beginning Reading intervention report: Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/109

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment of 18 third-grade classrooms (392 students) in eight rural southern Ohio districts, matched on district and teaching experience; only the reading components of CIRC were implemented over one school year. WWC domain average effect size for Bramlett's comprehension outcomes was 0.09, below the 0.25 substantively-important threshold.

> "According to WWC calculations (which account for clustering and multiple comparisons), the difference was not statistically significant."
