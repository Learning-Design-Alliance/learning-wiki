Evidence¶

Baumgartner 2025¶

Baumgartner, V., Daguati, S., Trninic, D., Akveld, M., Caspar, A., Hungerbühler, N., Kapur, M. (2025). Problem-solving before instruction for learning linear algebra in university mathematics. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09709-8

q3 · i1 · design · r3

ANOVA on the percentage of passing grades across baseline and intervention cohorts (overall F(4) = 27.32, p < 0.001, η² = 0.03). Pass rates rose from 55.84% at baseline to 65.66–75.81% in the intervention cohorts (Table 3); e.g. Year 2 contrast d = 0.42, Year 3 d = 0.21.

"Planned contrasts using Tukey's method between the separate intervention years and the baseline revealed that the exam performance in each year of the intervention differed significantly from the baseline, all p ≤ 0.001, with effect sizes of Cohen's d between 0.21 and 0.42."
