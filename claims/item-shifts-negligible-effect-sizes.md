Aggregate analysis averaging students across introductory and beyond-first-year courses at 120 institutions. The article states "Cohen's d <0.2" for all item shifts in both measures, attributing the small sizes to averaging that washes out context-specific changes; conclusions rest on large numbers and low uncertainty.
