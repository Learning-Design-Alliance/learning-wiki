## Evidence

### Katrin Schuessler 2026

Katrin Schuessler, Jenna Koenen, Elke Sumfleth, Marvin Rost. (2026). Variations in Repeated Measures of Cognitive Load and Interest During Complex Learning Tasks. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10105-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the N = 360 repeated-measures sample plotted across measurement points (Fig. 4). The article reports the same significant pattern for perceived task difficulty, interest (higher in introductory sections), and self-assessed understanding (lower in content sections); no effect sizes are printed.

> "In sections introducing new content knowledge (e.g. measurement points 4 to 6 within the worked examples), the amount of mental effort invested is significantly higher than in the first introductory sections."
