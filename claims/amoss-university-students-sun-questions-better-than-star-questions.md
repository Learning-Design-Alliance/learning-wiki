## Evidence

### Bekaert 2020

Bekaert, H., Van Winckel, H., Van Dooren, W., Steegen, A., & De Cock, M. (2020). Design and validation of an instrument to test students' understanding of the apparent motion of the Sun and stars. Physical Review Physics Education Research, 16(2), 020135. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.16.020135

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Second pilot study with 48 university students taking the revised test during the last lesson of an 'Introduction to Astronomy' course in which the test topic was not explicitly addressed. Sun questions again outperformed star questions. No inferential statistic is reported for the Sun-star contrast.

> "The mean score for all participants on all questions was M = 50%, SD = 16% (n = 48), which is a higher score than the secondary school students. On average the seven Sun questions (M = 57%, SD = 16%) are answered more correctly than the seven star questions (M = 44%, SD = 24%)."
