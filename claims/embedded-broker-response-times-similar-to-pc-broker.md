## Evidence

### Kao 2010

Kao, F.-C., Wang, S.-R. & Huang, T.-H. (2010). The Design of Circuit-Measuring Collaborative Learning System with Embedded Broker. IJCSI International Journal of Computer Science Issues, Vol. 7, Issue 1, No. 3. [doi:10.1109/digitel.2010.14](https://doi.org/10.1109/digitel.2010.14)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Performance testing of the system with 40 learners in groups, using the Ethereal network packet analyzer to measure average response time as the number of learners increased. The PC broker results were "132ms," "144ms," "148ms," and "152~155ms" for groups 1 to 4, nearly identical to the embedded broker values.
