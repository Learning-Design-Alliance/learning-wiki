Evidence¶

Tiina Leino Lindell 2026¶

Tiina Leino Lindell, Christian Stöhr. (2026). The AI disruption in engineering education: an analysis of changing student norms through cultural historical activity theory. Journal of Computing in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12528-025-09488-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Reflexive thematic analysis of 11 one-to-one and 6 group interviews with 25 engineering students from 13 subject areas at a technical university in Northern Europe. Students reported using GenAI for writing support, faster data analysis, and idea generation, and were found to be "increasingly integrating GenAI for their personal growth".

"In short, the findings from this group of engineering students show that they are increasingly integrating GenAI for their personal growth due to its ease of use and efficiency compared to traditional educational tools."

Tiina Leino Lindell 2026 (2)¶

Tiina Leino Lindell, Christian Stöhr. (2026). The AI disruption in engineering education: an analysis of changing student norms through cultural historical activity theory. Journal of Computing in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12528-025-09488-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

CHAT-based deductive analysis of the interview themes, illustrated in Fig. 2, locating the contradiction between the new object of self-directed growth and the "limitations of traditional educational tools". One student described ChatGPT processing data faster than Excel and other tools.

"This transformation appears to be driven by contradictions between their object of enhancing understanding and skills through self-direction and efficiency, and the limitations of traditional educational tools in fulfilling these needs (See Fig. 2)."
