## Evidence

### Rogers Gary D 2014

Rogers Gary D, McConnell Harry W, Jones de Rooy Nicole, Ellem Fiona, Lombard Marise. (2014). A randomised controlled trial of extended immersion in multi-method continuing simulation to prepare senior medical students for practice as junior doctors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-90

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial; 44 Intervention vs 36 Control individual participants. Table 3 reports PS3 means of 75.4 (75%) vs 70.1 (70%), difference 5.3 (5%), P = 0.02, scored by an expert blinded to study group.

> "Participation in the extended simulation program was associated with a significantly higher mean score in the prescribing exercise among individual participants at the end of the Year 3 in-school week."
