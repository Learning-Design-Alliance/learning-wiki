# Moll and Greenberg found that Mexican American students judged cognitively deficient in school performed complex cognitive tasks in their home communities, attributing the deficit judgment to culture-laden evaluative mediation

Narrative review attributing this to Moll and Greenberg (1990), who studied Southwestern Mexican American students in school and home community and found students engaged in complex tasks such as repairing farm machinery and managing budgets while being judged as failing in school. The article reports, citing Moll and Greenberg (1990), that students judged 'failing' academically performed complex tasks such as repairing farm machinery and managing budgets at home, and that deficit conclusions stemmed from assessment means 'more congenial to students of European American origin'. Reported here as the review-style attribution it is; the study's design is not described in the article.
