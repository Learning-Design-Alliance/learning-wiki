## Evidence

### Yoon S. 2022

Yoon S., Pei F., Benavides J.L., Ploss A., Logan J., Hamby S. (2022). The Long-Term Effects of Early Childhood Resilience Profiles on School Outcomes among Children in the Child Welfare System. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19105987

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Three-step latent profile analysis of NSCAW-II Waves 1 and 3 (N = 827, ages 3–5 at baseline) comparing three resilience profiles on Woodcock–Johnson III outcomes at the 3-year follow-up, controlling covariates. The multi-domain profile outperformed the low cognitive profile on all three achievement measures, with mean differences of 13.18, 12.60, and 14.82, each p < 0.001.

> "Children with the multi-domain resilience profile showed significantly higher levels of basic reading skills (mean difference = 13.18, p < 0.001), reading comprehension (mean difference = 12.60, p < 0.001), and math reasoning (mean difference = 14.82, p < 0.001), compared to children with the low cognitive resilience profile."
