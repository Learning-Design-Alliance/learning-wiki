## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Question format on the final test had a significant main effect on final test score (F(1,44) = 22.80, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.10), with lower proportional scores on VSAQs (47.2%) than MCQs (61.0%). [→ van Wijk EV 2024](#van-wijk-ev-2024)

## Evidence

### van Wijk EV 2024

van Wijk EV, de Jonge M, van Blankenstein FM, Janse RJ, Langers AMJ. (2024). The battle of question formats: a comparative study of retrieval practice using very short answer questions and multiple choice questions. BMC Medical Education, 24:1547. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06538-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the same within-subjects ANOVA (n = 45), the final test format itself mattered: "lower proportional scores on VSAQs compared to MCQs" with η2 = 0.10, a medium effect. This reflects format difficulty, not a learning difference.

> "Question format on the final test had a significant main effect on the final test score (F(1, 44) = 22.80, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.10) with lower proportional scores on VSAQs compared to MCQs, (M (SD) MCQ: 61.0% (2.2), VSAQ: 47.2% (2.1))."
