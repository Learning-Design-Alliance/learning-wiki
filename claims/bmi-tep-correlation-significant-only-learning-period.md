## Evidence

### Kato Juliana Mika 2017

Kato Juliana Mika, IUAMOTO Leandro Ryuchi, SUGUITA Fábio Yuji, ESSU Felipe Futema, MEYER Alberto, ANDRAUS Wellington. (2017). IMPACT OF OBESITY AND SURGICAL SKILLS IN LAPAROSCOPIC TOTALLY EXTRAPERITONEAL HERNIOPLASTY. Arquivos Brasileiros de Cirurgia Digestiva. https://doi.org/10.1590/0102-6720201700030002

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the same prospective single-surgeon series, subgroup correlation analysis by surgery type showed the BMI–time relationship held only early in the experience: "the first 42 patients who underwent a bilateral hernia repair (p<0,001)". The authors interpret this as an influence of the learning curve on the technical procedure.

> "Nevertheless, the correlation was statistically significant only in the first 14 patients who underwent a unilateral hernia (p=0,083) and in the first 42 patients who underwent a bilateral hernia repair (p<0,001)."
