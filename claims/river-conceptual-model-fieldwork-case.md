### Daniel Churchill 2011

Daniel Churchill. (2011). Conceptual Model Learning Objects and Design Recommendations for Small Screens. Educational Technology & Society, 14(1), 203-216. https://www.worldcat.org/issn/1436-4522

`q2 · i?`

A case study of a conceptual model designed in line with the article's recommendations, used during a geography field trip. Teachers first displayed the model via a projector as a visual aid; students then used it outdoors to support their river study task and later in a PowerPoint report template with conceptually challenging questions. The article reports the case descriptively with no outcome measures.

> "In February 2008, the author traveled to Northern Thailand with a group of 72 secondary school students and two of their Geography teachers from Hong Kong."
