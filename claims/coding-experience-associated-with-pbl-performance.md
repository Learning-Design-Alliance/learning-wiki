Exploratory student-level OLS analysis, visualized in Figure 2 (right panel), relating years of coding experience to course score. The unstandardized slope is "ˆβ= +1.88points per year"; the association is correlational and the authors interpret it conservatively as coding fluency enabling effective GenAI use.

> "Students with more years of coding experience tended to perform better, a finding consistent with an exploratory ordinary least squares (OLS) analysis at the student level (slope ˆβ= +1.88points per year; 95 % CI[+0.30,+3.46];p= 0.022)"
