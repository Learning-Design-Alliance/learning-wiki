David Menendez. (2023). Cues to generality: Integrating linguistic and visual information when generalizing biological information. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000812

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Content analysis of 18 elementary science books (4 textbooks, 14 trade books; 687 images, 611 accompanying texts) coded by trained coders (Cohen's k = 0.78 for images) found "90.8% (n = 624) of all visualizations were rich."

"I found that 90.8% (n = 624) of all visualizations were rich, and there were very few bland visualizations (9.2%, n = 63)."
