### Silventoinen Karri 2023

Silventoinen Karri, Luukkonen Juha, Myrskylä Mikko, Martikainen Pekka. (2023). Birth size, school performance and family social position: a study of 650,000 children. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-023-02757-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linearity testing in population-averaged models on the Finnish register cohort: a squared term added to a linear term for birth weight was significant (p < 0.00001), and "the association leveled off at the higher end of BW distribution". Negative quadratic effects appear in Table 2 for both sexes.

> "For BW, the squared term was statistically significant for boys and girls (p < 0.00001). This was because the association leveled off at the higher end of BW distribution (Supplementary Table 2)."
