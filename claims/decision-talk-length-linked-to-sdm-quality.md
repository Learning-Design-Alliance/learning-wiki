# Longer decision talk was positively associated with observer- and doctor-rated communication quality, but not with patients' assessments

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Observer-rated doctor (r=.69) and dyad (r=.64) quality and observed patient involvement (r=.37) correlated positively with decision-sequence length. [→ Kasper J 2017](#kasper-j-2017)

## Evidence

### Kasper J 2017

Kasper J, Liethmann K, Heesen C, Reissmann DR, Geiger F. (2017). Training doctors briefly and in situ to involve their patients in making medical decisions—Preliminary testing of a newly developed module. Health Expectations. https://doi.org/10.1111/hex.12565

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlations between the length of the pure decision sequences and mean MAPPIN'SDM patient-involvement scores across the 40 consultations. The printed correlations include "MAPPIN‐Odoctor: r=.69" and "MAPPIN‐Odyad: r=.64".

> "In this study, length of decision talk was associated with quality of the communication as assessed by SDM observers and the doctors themselves (MAPPIN‐Odoctor: r=.69, P<.001, MAPPIN‐Opatient: r=.37, P=.03, MAPPIN‐Odyad: r=.64, P<.001, MAPPIN‐Qdoctor: r=.33, P=.052, MAPPIN‐Qpatient: r=.33, P=.052)"

### Kasper J 2017 (2)

Kasper J, Liethmann K, Heesen C, Reissmann DR, Geiger F. (2017). Training doctors briefly and in situ to involve their patients in making medical decisions—Preliminary testing of a newly developed module. Health Expectations. https://doi.org/10.1111/hex.12565

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same correlational analysis of the 40 consultations: the patient questionnaire scale showed no relationship with sequence length (r=.07, P=.700); the printed r=.07 is below the negligible threshold for the patient-perspective finding only.

> "while patients’ assessment of the communication was not related to the length of the decision talk sequence (MAPPIN‐Qpatient: r=.07, P=.700)."
