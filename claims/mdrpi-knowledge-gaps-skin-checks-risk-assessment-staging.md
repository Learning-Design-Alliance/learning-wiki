### Aydinli Ayşe; Deniz Doğan Sevgi 2025

Aydinli Ayşe; Deniz Doğan Sevgi. (2025). Investigation of Nurses' Level of Knowledge on Medical Device‐Related Pressure Injuries: A Descriptive and Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70776

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Item‐level descriptive analysis of correct responses in the 217‐nurse survey (Table 3). The article reports these percentages as printed and links the gaps to the lack of a routinely used MDRPI risk assessment tool; no effect size applies.

> "the three items with the lowest correct response rates were: “To avoid the occurrence of MDRPIs, the skin under and around the medical device should be checked at least once a day” (6.5%), “Risk assessment for MDRPIs should be completed for each patient within 12 h of admission” (7.4%), and “International pressure injury classification stages (e.g., Stage I, Stage II) are used to categorise MDRPI that develop on the mucous membranes” (8.3%)"
