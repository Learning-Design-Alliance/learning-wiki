## Evidence

### Borgonovi Francesca 2025

Borgonovi Francesca, Andrieu Elodie. (2025). The Relationship Between Internet Use, Achievement, and Persistence in Digital Tasks. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1002/jad.12503

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Observational item-level analysis of PISA 2018 mathematics and science items (Model 3) among 153,603 students. The article reports no statistically significant internet-use × item-position interactions, printing "are not statistically significant"; no effect size is reported for this null finding, so equivalence was not tested.

> "estimates presented in Model 3 do not reveal differences across students with low, medium, and high levels of internet use in how much accuracy declines as a function of item position: interaction terms between high/low levels of internet use and item position are quantitatively small (i.e. very close to 1) and are not statistically significant."
