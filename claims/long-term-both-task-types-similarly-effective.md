## Evidence

### Madeleine Hörnlein and Christoph Kulgemeyer 2025

Madeleine Hörnlein and Christoph Kulgemeyer. (2025). Learning Science and the Illusion of Understanding: Exploring the Effects of Integrating Learning Tasks after Explainer Videos. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.02824](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.02824)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Study 2 follow-up test about one month after the intervention with small per-group samples (combined task group and control). The article reports a large correlation r = 0.50 (p = 0.005) in the combined task group versus r = 0.03 (p = 0.28) in the control group, suggesting a positive long-term effect.

> "A correlation analysis for the combined task group revealed a highly significant correlation between the belief of understanding at follow-up and conceptual knowledge in the follow-up test (𝑟=0.50, 𝑝=0.005). However, no such correlation was found in the control group at follow-up (𝑟=0.03, 𝑝=0.28)."
