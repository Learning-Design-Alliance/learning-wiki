## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the best-fitting hierarchical Bayesian DDM, drift rate increased with training as an exponential function of overall trial number (trials 1 through 2800), with every participant's drift rate estimated as increasing. [→ Cochrane 2023](#cochrane-2023)

## Evidence

### Cochrane 2023

Cochrane, A., Sims, C. R., Bejjanki, V. R., Green, C. S., & Bavelier, D. (2023). Multiple timescales of learning indicated by changes in evidence-accumulation processes during perceptual decision-making. npj Science of Learning 8:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00168-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fully Bayesian nonlinear mixed-effects DDM fits to joint RT and accuracy data from 21 participants completing 700 trials per day across 4 days of dot-motion discrimination. Fixed effects show "DR increased with training" with start mean 0.44 and asymptote mean 0.96, time-to-half-of-learning 1261 trials; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Model fixed effects indicated that DR increased with training (start mean = 0.44, se = 0.05; asymptote mean = 0.96, se = 0.11), with a sample-level time- to-half-of-learning of 1261 trials. Every participant 's DR was estimated as increasing"
