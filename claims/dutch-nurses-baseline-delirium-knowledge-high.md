## Evidence

### van de Steeg L 2015

van de Steeg L, IJkema R, Wagner C, Langelaan M. (2015). The effect of an e-learning course on nursing staff's knowledge of delirium: a before-and-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0289-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline assessment of 978 participants who started the test in 17 Dutch hospitals; the printed mean is 79.3 with SD 10.5. The discussion notes other studies found mean scores of 53-61 but used different instruments, so direct comparison may not be possible.

> "Participants' mean score for the baseline test was 79.3 (95% CI 78.5 to 80.1, SD 10.5, n = 650) on a scale of 0 to 100, adjusted for age, sex, function, level of education, experience, type of ward and type of hospital."
