## Evidence

### Eddy Y.C. Lee 2006

Eddy Y.C. Lee, Carol K.K. Chan, & Jan van Aalst. (2006). Students assessing their own collaborative knowledge building. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-006-8997-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Partial correlations controlling for Hong Kong Attainment Test scores among the two portfolio classes (n = 58), with printed correlations r = .44, r = .39, and r = .46 the largest exceeding the r ≥ .37 threshold. Portfolio ratings were also "significantly correlated with essay writing reflecting conceptual understanding ( r = .42, p < .01)"; portfolios were rated on explanation, conceptual quality, and note-cluster selection.

> "Knowledge building portfolio ratings were significantly correlated with ATK inquiry (r = .44, p < .001), ATK visual organization (r = .39, p < .01), and explanation scores (r = .46, p < .001)."
