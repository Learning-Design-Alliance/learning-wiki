## Evidence

### Qi Li 2024

Qi Li, Die Wang, Weilong Xiao, Yingying Tang, Qi Sun, Binghai Sun & Zhishan Hu. (2024). Structured interaction between teacher and student in the flipped classroom enhances learning and interbrain synchrony. npj Science of Learning 9:73. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00286-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on pre/post case-analysis scores of 64 university students randomly assigned to flipped (FT), lecture-based (LT), or control (CT) teaching. The phases-by-lecture-type interaction was significant (F(2,61) = 7.74, p = 0.0010, ηp² = 0.20), and "the FT condition (M = 9.90, SD = 2.68) was significantly higher than the LT condition".
