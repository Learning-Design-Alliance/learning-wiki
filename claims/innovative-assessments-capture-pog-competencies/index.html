Terrell 2025¶

Terrell, J. H., Ahigian, R., Garvey, M., & Barzee, S. (2025). So, you want to create a Portrait of a Graduate? Factors and considerations for the field. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673579

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The authors' assertion in the assessment section of the brief, offered without cited empirical evidence. They argue these methods "encourage students to demonstrate their knowledge and skills in practical, real-world contexts" and are more reflective of POG competencies than standardized tests.

"Innovative assessment measures like performance assessments, portfolios, certifications, and digital badges can provide a more comprehensive view of a student’s abilities related to POG competencies and skills."
