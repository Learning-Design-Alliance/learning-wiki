# FCCL and traditional instruction produce no statistically significant difference in overall theoretical examination scores in anatomy

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yao Qianyin 2025

Yao Qianyin, Zhu Peiyi, Zou Zixi, Cheng Yatao, Liang Yaojian, Huang Wenyu. (2025). Synergising flipped classroom and case-based learning in resource-intensive anatomy education in China: a quasi-experimental study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-102001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of the 40-item anatomy theory course examination (TCE) in the same 64-vs-64 design: FCCL students averaged 60% (SD±16%) versus 56% (SD±14%) for TC, and "this 4% difference was not statistically significant" (p=0.136). No effect size printed.

> "The Mann-Whitney U test showed that this 4% difference was not statistically significant between TC and FCCL students (U=1735.500, p=0.136)"
