## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Higher rank score (OR:1.043) and MAPAS mathematics (OR:1.043) and English (OR:1.042) test scores predicted Bachelor recommendations; lower scores on these measures predicted Not FMHS recommendations (all p < 0.0001). [→ Curtis Elana 2015](#curtis-elana-2015)

## Evidence

### Curtis Elana 2015

Curtis Elana, Wikaire Erena, Jiang Yannan, McMillan Louise, Loto Rob, Airini, Reid Papaarangi. (2015). A tertiary approach to improving equity in health: quantitative analysis of the Māori and Pacific Admission Scheme (MAPAS) process, 2008–2012. International Journal for Equity in Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12939-015-0133-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression models on the 918-applicant MAPAS cohort adjusted for interview year, gender and ancestry. The article reports "lower rank score (OR:0.968, CI:0.963-0.973)" among predictors of Not FMHS; the Bachelor-direction ORs come from the same Table 4 models. No standardised effect sizes printed.

> "A lower performance on the MAPAS mathematics (OR:0.957, CI:0.949-0.965) and English tests (OR:0.972, CI:0.961-0.984); lower rank score (OR:0.968, CI:0.963-0.973) and not having exposure to any 2 sciences (OR:0.84, CI:0.057-0.125) predicted a higher chance of receiving a recommendation of Not FMHS (all p-values <0.0001)"
