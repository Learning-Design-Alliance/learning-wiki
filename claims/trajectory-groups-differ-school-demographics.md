EdWorkingPaper No. 20-233, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/catching-up-or-falling-behind-measuring-middle-school-achievement-trajectories-for-college-readiness/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Summary statistics by trajectory group (Table 1) comparing school demographics of always on-track versus always off-track students in the 363,685-student sample. The quote prints the school-level FRPL and racial/ethnic composition averages for both groups.

> "students who are always on-track in math are in schools with an average of 33% of its student body eligible for FRPL and 33% Black, Hispanic, or Asian students, compared with the always off-track students who are in schools with 56% of its student body eligible for FRPL and 49% Black, Hispanic, or Asian students."
