## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Fenzi Giulio 2024

Fenzi Giulio, Alemán-Jiménez Carolina, López-Ferrándiz Lucia, Leal-Costa Cesar, Díaz-Agea José Luis. (2024). Enhancing Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Training: An Interprofessional Approach With Undergraduate Medicine and Nursing Students Using Self-Learning Methodology in Simulated Environments (MAES)—A Qualitative Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/9470402

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group theme on scenario choice in the qualitative study. Students reported that deciding the scenario "generates more interest and curiosity in the person who chooses" (S2) and that they felt "more responsible towards the knowledge that would otherwise remain superficial" (S6).

> "This practice improved interest and increased motivation as students saw the connection between a real fact and their simulated scenarios."
