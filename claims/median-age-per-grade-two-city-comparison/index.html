Descriptive analysis of age-grade census tables (Table I) for City C (1320 pupils) and City F (1543 pupils), both censused in March, summarized by median age per grade. The printed median differences include .55, .56, .49, .65 year for early grades.

"when it is realized that the children in the first grade of City C are nearly six months older than the same children in City F and that the children in grade 4 are over eight months older than those in City F, then things begin to look serious."
