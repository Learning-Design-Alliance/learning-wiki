## Evidence

### Meyer 2013

Meyer, A., Meyer-Ahrens, I., & Wilde, M. (2013). The Beneficial Effects of Non-received Choice: A Study on Intrinsic Motivation in Biology Education. European Journal of Educational Research, 2(4), 185-190. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086332

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of five intact fifth-grade classes (N=118) in a choice condition (majority vote among four biology topics) versus a no-choice control, using a modified Intrinsic Motivation Inventory. The article reports "significantly higher level of interest/enjoyment for the choice condition". The printed effect size, η² = 0.1 (F(2,109) = 5.58, p < .01), is for the omnibus effect of the three conditions on interest/enjoyment; the choice-versus-no-choice contrast is reported only as CI = .546 ± .327, p < .01, with no effect size, so impact is coded i?.

> "The results showed a significantly higher level of interest/enjoyment for the choice condition (CI: .546±.327; p<.01), as well as for perceived choice (CI: .447±.322, p<.01)"
