## Evidence

### Nawajah Inad 2025

Nawajah Inad. (2025). The statistical relationship between sweet consumption and academic achievement among Palestinian university students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-025-07425-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 220 full-time general-medicine students from four Palestinian universities, stratified by institution and study year; multiple linear regression with three hierarchical models on self-reported GPA. The fully adjusted model showed "attenuated but remained significant statistically" for daily versus less-than-daily sweet consumption, with β = −0.15 (95% CI −0.29, −0.01; p = 0.039).

> "In the model completely adjusted for mental health, frequency of eating breakfast, and physical activity (Model 3), the association was attenuated but remained significant statistically (β = − 0.15; 95% CI: − 0.29, − 0.01; p = 0.039)."
