Narrative review reporting analysis of mini-CEX forms in anaesthetics training in New Zealand (attributed to Weller et al). The review reports "95% of specialists wrote comments under 'things that the trainee did well', 70% recorded comments in 'areas for improvement', and 60% wrote down an 'agreed action'."

> "In the written feedback fields of the Mini-CEX form, 95% of specialists wrote comments under ‘things that the trainee did well’, 70% recorded comments in ‘areas for improvement’, and 60% wrote down an ‘agreed action’ [15]."
