Findings on generating, illustrated with contrasting excerpts: one student reconciled discordant shortness of breath by broadening the differential, another noticed an age-symptom disconnect but did not elaborate; two students reached the same diagnosis via full versus minimal chart review. "Students in our sample generated ideas using three central approaches."
