## Evidence

### Duncan C 2017

Duncan C, Rengasamy ER, Batchelor S, Pope C, Riley S, Cunningham AM. (2017). Using social media to support small group learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1060-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of staff participation during the year 1 implementation found "Staff engagement with the various platforms was variable". Most student groups kept their Facebook groups private and did not invite tutors; web-based resources were reviewed and used by several groups in face-to-face CBL sessions, where staff engaged well.

> "Staff engagement with the various platforms was variable, with some engaged and following student activity, and others not engaging at all."
