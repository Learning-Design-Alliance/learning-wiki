### Alshehri Abdulkarim H. 2025

Alshehri Abdulkarim H., Moaleem Mohammed M. Al, Meshni Abdullah Ahmed, Gadah Thrya S., Alahmari Nasser M., Albar Nassreen H., Adawi Hafiz Ahmed A., AL-Makramani Bandar M. A., Shubayr Mosa A., Nandalur Kulashekar Reddy. (2025). A comparative study of student performance in all-ceramic crown preparation by clinical-phase students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07512-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional double-blinded clinical exam of 72 fifth-year students (30 male, 42 female) scored by two calibrated evaluators using a 39-point rubric. The study reports "nearly identical" overall scores and no significant gender-based differences; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "The overall rubric scores were nearly identical for males (31.03 ± 1.24) and females (31.00 ± 1.11), and no significant gender-based differences (p > 0.05) were observed across most parameters."
