### Iordanou 2022

Iordanou, K. (2022). Supporting strategic and meta-strategic development of argument skill: the role of reflection. Metacognition and Learning, 17, 399–425. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09289-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Microgenetic analysis of metatalk in the electronic dialogs, with utterances categorized as meta-strategic (e.g., monitoring the discourse) or epistemic (e.g., judging evidence quality). Figures 2 and 3 show per-session usage; the article reports the patterns were comparable in sessions 1–4 and diverged after "Dialog Session 4".

> "Reflective activities were introduced in the experimental condition only (A&R) in Dialog Session 4, and from this point the two groups' pattern of usage of meta-level talk differed"
