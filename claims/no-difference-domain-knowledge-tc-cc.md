## Evidence

### Czeskleba A. 2020

Czeskleba A., Holzhausen Y., Peters H. (2020). Clinical reasoning for acute dyspnoea: comparison between final-year medical students from discipline- and competency-based undergraduate programmes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02055-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paper-based 22-item single-choice knowledge test administered to all 60 participants after the clinical decision-making test as a control variable; the t-test showed no significant group differences.

> "In the knowledge test on acute dyspnoea, there were no differences between the two groups in the number of correctly answered items (TC: M = 13.7 (62.1%), SD = 1.8; / CC: M = 13.6 (61.7%), SD = 2.1)."
