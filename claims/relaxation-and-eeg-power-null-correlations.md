## Evidence

### Jingjing Chen 2022

Jingjing Chen, Bing Xu & Dan Zhang. (2022). Inter-brain coupling analysis reveals learning-related attention of primary school students. bioRxiv. https://doi.org/10.1101/2022.06.08.495411

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Control analysis in the same 28-student classroom EEG study using the NeuroSky relaxation index; the article reports "r = -0.278, p = .152" and "r = 0.069, p =.728", both non-significant.

> "Moreover, the correlations between individual’s relaxation index and academic performances also failed to reach significance (Fig. 5c-d, inter-brain relaxation level: r = -0.278, p = .152, n = 28; single-brain relaxation coupling: r = 0.069, p =.728, n = 28)."

### Jingjing Chen 2022 (2)

Jingjing Chen, Bing Xu & Dan Zhang. (2022). Inter-brain coupling analysis reveals learning-related attention of primary school students. bioRxiv. https://doi.org/10.1101/2022.06.08.495411

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analysis of the same classroom dataset using normalized EEG power in five frequency bands (delta 1-4Hz through gamma 30-40 Hz) for both inter-brain and single-brain analyses; no significant correlations with academic performance.

> "When using the EEG power data for analysis, all the correlations failed to reach significance."
