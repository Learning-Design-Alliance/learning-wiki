`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subgroup analysis by question type (Table 4): 12 case-based items versus 176 knowledge-based items. Case-based accuracy means were 1.00 for ChatGPT-4o Plus, Gemini 1.5, and Gemini Advanced; 0.83–0.92 for the other five models. The authors interpret this as competence in clinical reasoning, not only recall.
