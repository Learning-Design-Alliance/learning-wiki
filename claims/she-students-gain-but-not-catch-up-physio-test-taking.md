## Evidence

### Brokk Toggerson 2020

Brokk Toggerson, Samyukta Krishnamurthy, Emily E. Hansen, and Chasya Church. (2020). Positive Impacts on Student Self-Efficacy from an Introductory Physics for Life Science Course Using the Team-Based Learning Pedagogy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Table IV reports end-of-semester comparisons for she- vs he-identifying students in the TBL IPLS course. For test-taking, the post-semester He - She difference is 0.32 ± 0.14 (t = 2.14, p = 0.03); for physiological state, 0.25 ± 0.12 (t = 1.88, p = 0.06). She-identifying students' gains were their largest in test-taking but they "only “caught up” to the starting value of he-identifying students."

> "She-identifying individuals, on average, experienced their largest gains in this dimension. However, they only “caught up” to the starting value of he-identifying students who also experienced a gain in this area."
