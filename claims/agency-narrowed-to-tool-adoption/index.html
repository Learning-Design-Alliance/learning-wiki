Shahin Hossain 2026¶

Shahin Hossain, Sima Ahmadi, Leqi Li, Idowu David Awoyemi, Wei Huang, Chenxi Zhou, Jujia Li, Samaa Haniya, Shapla Khanam, Tasbirun Mashreka Subaha. (2026). Rethinking Generative AI Literacy: An Integrative, Developmental, and Dialectical Framework for K–12 Teacher Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01705 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.01705

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative framework analysis of the AI literacy corpus: agency was defined in the literature as educators' behavioral intention to adopt AI tools, write effective prompts, and make individual tool-use decisions. "The concepts of agency across the reviewed studies were consistently inter- preted in narrow, instrumental terms," which the authors argue does not capture ethical and civic demands.

"The concepts of agency across the reviewed studies were consistently inter- preted in narrow, instrumental terms (Zhou et al., in press)."
