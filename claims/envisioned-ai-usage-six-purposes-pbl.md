# College students envisioned over 100 AI usages in project-based learning serving six purposes, from automating tedious tasks to guiding learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across seven co-design workshops, 18 students produced over 100 desired AI usages in PBL, which thematic analysis grouped into six purpose subthemes: automating repetitive and time-consuming tasks, supporting divergent thinking, supporting selection from alternatives, drafting or directly implementing solutions, feedback on solutions, and guiding students to learn.
