## Evidence

### Ines Zeitlhofer 2026

Ines Zeitlhofer, Joerg Zumbach. (2026). Germane processes moderate the effectiveness of complex desirable difficulty tasks. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09807-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

In the 2×2 factorial experiment (132 participants, Italian conjugation task, posttest two days later), the MANOVA on posttest time and trials showed a moderate complexity effect (η² = 0.09), with "higher complexity leads to less time needed" and fewer trials.

> "A MANOV A with complexity and desirable difficulty as independent variables and time and trials (posttest) as dependent variables revealed a moderate main effect of complexity (F (2, 127) = 6.55, p = .002; η² = 0.09; Hypothesis 1.1)"
