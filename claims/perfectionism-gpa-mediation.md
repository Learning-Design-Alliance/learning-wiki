## Evidence

### Han Chengjiang 2022

Han Chengjiang, Li Feng, Lian Bizhen, Vencúrik Tomas, Liang Wei. (2022). Relationships between Perfectionism, Extra Training and Academic Performance in Chinese Collegiate Athletes: Mediating Role of Achievement Motivation. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191710764

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct paths to semester GPA in the second mediation model of the two-wave study (n = 243); GPA was collected from university records as a credit-weighted average. Motive for success was the only achievement-motivation attribute significantly associated with GPA (β = 0.33, p < 0.001).

> "doubts about action (β = 0.12, SE = 0.06, p = 0.037), personal standard (β = 0.20, SE = 0.06, p = 0.001), and organization (β = 0.20, SE = 0.06, p < 0.001)"

### Han Chengjiang 2022 (2)

Han Chengjiang, Li Feng, Lian Bizhen, Vencúrik Tomas, Liang Wei. (2022). Relationships between Perfectionism, Extra Training and Academic Performance in Chinese Collegiate Athletes: Mediating Role of Achievement Motivation. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191710764

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped indirect effects in the GPA mediation model (n = 243, prospective design). The authors interpret that athletes with higher standards or self-doubt pursue success motivation, yielding better academic results.

> "motive for success was identified as a significant mediator for the associations of personal standard (β = 0.10, p =0.003) and doubts about action (β = 0.06, p = 0.037) with academic performance"
