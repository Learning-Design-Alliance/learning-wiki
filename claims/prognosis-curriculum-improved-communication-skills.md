## Evidence

### Schoenborn Nancy L. 2015

Schoenborn Nancy L., Boyd Cynthia, Cayea Danelle, Nakamura Kelly, Xue Qian-Li, Ray Anushree, McNabney Matthew. (2015). Incorporating prognosis in the care of older adults with multimorbidity: description and evaluation of a novel curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0488-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post Impact Survey of 18 intervention and 29 control residents; paired t-test and inter-group comparisons. Self-reported skills rose "from 5.3 to 6.9 and 5.2 to 6.6 on a 9-point scale (both p < 0.001)"; no effect size printed.

> "self-reported skill levels in both aspects increased significantly from 5.3 to 6.9 and 5.2 to 6.6 on a 9-point scale (both p < 0.001); these responses were significantly different from the control group at follow-up."
