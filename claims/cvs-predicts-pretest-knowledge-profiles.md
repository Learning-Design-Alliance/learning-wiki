## Evidence

### Edelsbrunner et al. 2018

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Lennart Schalk, Ralph Schumacher, Elsbeth Stern. (2018). Variable control and conceptual change: A large-scale quantitative study in elementary school. Learning and Individual Differences 66. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2018.02.003

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional covariate analysis within the latent transition analysis of N = 1809 students, relating CVS scores to pretest profile frequencies while controlling for school grade. The article reports "a positive relation between students' understanding of the CVS and their content knowledge proﬁles at pretest".

> "The higher students' CVS score was, the less likely they were to begin instruction in the high or moderate misconceptions-proﬁles. Instead, with increasing CVS scores, the frequency of students entering instruction in the low misconceptions-proﬁle or in the fragmented proﬁle increased."
