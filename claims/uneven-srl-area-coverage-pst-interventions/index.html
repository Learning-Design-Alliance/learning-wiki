q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Frequency analysis across the 66 coded interventions (RQ1.2) of SRL areas and strategies, based on Pintrich's (2000) model adapted by the authors. The review reports areas were "unevenly distributed", with cognition most promoted and emotion and context rare.

"The areas addressed by the interventions were unevenly distributed (see Fig. 2). Firstly, the most promoted area was cognition (n = 62)."

Related Claims¶

  • Only 13 of 66 interventions promoted pre-service teachers' dual role in SRL, and only three covered all four subcategories of the dual role — related
  • Intervention content related to professional competences is uneven: self-reflection (42%) and technology (38%) are common, while explicit SRL knowledge instruction (25%) and SRL diagnosis practice (9%) are rare — related
  • Among highly effective interventions targeting metacognition, four of eleven focused on a single SRL area (metacognition) and five promoted the dual role — related
  • Among 53 quantitative SRL interventions for pre-service teachers, effect sizes were distributed evenly across highly effective (n = 17), moderately effective (n = 16) and less effective (n = 20) categories — related
  • AI's direct impact on SRL concentrates on metacognitive and cognitive aspects while motivation remains underexplored — related
  • Most direct SRL promotion targeted cognitive capabilities and rarely mentioned the benefit of strategy use — related
