## Evidence

### Malhotra S 2007

Malhotra S, Biswas P, Sharan P, Grover S. (2007). Characteristics of Patients Visiting the Child & Adolescent Psychiatric Clinic: A 26-year Study from North India. J. Indian Assoc. Child Adolesc. Ment. Health 3(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ896858

`q2 · i?`

Outcome analysis reported only for Period III (2000-2005) of the clinic audit. The article reports that of cases followed regularly to one year, "53.3% were rated as partially improved or recovered and 46.7% were rated as unimproved", with unimproved cases mostly diagnosed with mental retardation.

> "Of the cases that followed up regularly till 1 year 53.3% were rated as partially improved or recovered and 46.7% were rated as unimproved. A vast majority of cases rated as unimproved were diagnosed as having mental retardation."
