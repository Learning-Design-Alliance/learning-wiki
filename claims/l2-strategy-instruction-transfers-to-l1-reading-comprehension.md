## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental group gained significantly more than the control group on the Persian reading comprehension test, indicating reverse transfer of reading ability from L2 to L1. [→ Talebi 2012](#talebi-2012)

## Evidence

### Talebi 2012

Seyed Hassan Talebi. (2012). Reading in L2 (English) and L1 (Persian): An Investigation into Reveres Transfer of Reading Strategies. English Language Teaching, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n3p217

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measure ANOVA on pre/post Persian reading test scores (Table 2); the Change x Group interaction was significant (F=438.034; P<.000), with the experimental group gaining 2.81 scores versus .10 for controls, which the authors interpret as reverse transfer from L2 to L1.

> "From the table we find that the experimental group had a gain of 2.81 scores (From 15.87 to 18.68) whereas the control group had a gain of only .10 scores (from 15.68 to 15.78)."
