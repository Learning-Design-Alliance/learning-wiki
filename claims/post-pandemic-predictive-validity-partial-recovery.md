Yoo H, Zhou X, Phillips B. (2025). Predictive validity of preadmission assessment on early nursing academic readiness: impact of COVID-19. Nurse Educ. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000001891

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis by pandemic period (Table 2): post-pandemic correlations were Science 0.349, Reading 0.344, English 0.308, Mathematics 0.306. The study reports "All correlations were statistically significant (P < .001)." Regression indexes echoed this pattern of slight decrease then recovery.

> "Regarding the results from post-pandemic period, the correlations with all content areas were stronger compared with those in the pandemic period, but weaker than observed in the pre-pandemic period. In addition, the correlation differences between the pandemic and post-pandemic periods were significant for Science, Reading, and English, but not for Math."
