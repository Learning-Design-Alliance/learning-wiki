## Evidence

### Ho Martin Patrick 2026

Ho Martin Patrick, Heskin Leonie, Dablouk Lena, Richa Yasmina, Nolan Joshua, Brien Andrew O’, Abrar Samin, Hennessey Derek. (2026). Near-peer teaching of laparoscopic skills among medical students: a randomised feasibility study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08461-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison in the randomised feasibility study (42 students, two blinded scorers). The String station showed the same pattern: "Group 1 outperformed Group 2 by 25.5 points (p = 0.01, Effect size=−0.49)".

> "Group 1 outperformed Group 2 by five points (p = 0.02, Effect size=−0.44)"

### Ho Martin Patrick 2026 (2)

Ho Martin Patrick, Heskin Leonie, Dablouk Lena, Richa Yasmina, Nolan Joshua, Brien Andrew O’, Abrar Samin, Hennessey Derek. (2026). Near-peer teaching of laparoscopic skills among medical students: a randomised feasibility study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08461-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on post-intervention Peg Transfer scores controlling for baseline; Group 1 showed higher adjusted scores (EMM = 44.39) than Group 2 (EMM = 27.7). No standardised effect size is printed for this test.

> "After controlling for baseline performance, the effect of group on improved objective performance was statistically significant, (F(1, 29) = 4.64, p = 0.04)"

### Ho Martin Patrick 2026 (3)

Ho Martin Patrick, Heskin Leonie, Dablouk Lena, Richa Yasmina, Nolan Joshua, Brien Andrew O’, Abrar Samin, Hennessey Derek. (2026). Near-peer teaching of laparoscopic skills among medical students: a randomised feasibility study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08461-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The between-group difference was not significant for Ball Transfer; equivalence was not tested. The article attributes this to station design emphasising dynamic force manipulation that may be difficult to teach verbally.

> "However, Group 1 did not outperform Group 2 in the “Ball Transfer” station (p = 0.77)."
