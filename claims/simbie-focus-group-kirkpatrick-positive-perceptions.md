Khuansiri Narajeenron et al. (2025). Enhancing Team Strategies and Tools to Enhance Performance and Patient Safety Performance Through Medical Movies, Massive Open Online Courses, and 3D Virtual Simulation–Based Interprofessional Education: Mixed Methods Double-Blind Quasi-Experimental Study. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative thematic analysis of 60-90 minute focus groups (5 disciplines per group) coded against the New World Kirkpatrick model and TeamSTEPPS domains; participants reported that games and SimBIE scenarios promoted teamwork and interprofessional understanding where traditional classrooms were found lacking.

> "the analysis of focus group discussions based on Kirkpatrick's model levels 1 to 3 indicated positive effects on satisfaction, engagement, knowledge, skills, attitude, and confidence, enriched by game practice and debriefing sessions."
