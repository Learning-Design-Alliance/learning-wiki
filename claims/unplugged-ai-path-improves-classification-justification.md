## Evidence

### Maria Cristina Carrisi 2025

Maria Cristina Carrisi, Mirko Marras, and Sara Vergallo. (2025). A Structured Unplugged Approach for Foundational AI Literacy in Primary Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.21398](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.21398)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-test analysis of 23 fifth-grade respondents (of 31 enrolled) evaluated by three independent experts against predefined criteria. The percentage answering correctly rose from "9.5% in the initial questionnaire" to 69.57%, with 56.52% giving valid justifications; no effect size is printed.

> "The ability to justify responses (Fig. 2c) improved significantly, with 69.57% answering correctly and 56.52% providing valid justifications, compared to 9.5% in the initial questionnaire."
