Evidence¶

Olaoye DD 2026¶

Olaoye DD, Owolabi HO and Olaoye OT (2026). Automated software scoring of senior school certificate examination mathematical items in economics using a contextual similarity model. Front. Educ. 11:1669504. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1669504

q2 · i? · design · r2

Trial test of the AEEGS software with twenty examinees' written essay responses in a purposively sampled school with characteristics similar to the study area. The article reports alpha of 0.86 and expert examination by three software development experts for face and content validity.

"Cronbach alpha was used to test the internal consistency of the results (scores) obtained through the software, which is 0.86, which means it is reliable because it is greater than the benchmark of 0.75."
