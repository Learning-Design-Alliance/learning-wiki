Pre- and post-course technology acceptance questionnaires in the MAIC pilot. The article reports 'a significant overall increase in technology acceptance (N = 111,t = 3.05,p = 0.002),' with significant improvements in Habit (t = 2.81, p = 0.005), Effort Expectancy (t = 3.98, p < 0.001), and Facilitating Conditions (t = 3.22, p = 0.002).
