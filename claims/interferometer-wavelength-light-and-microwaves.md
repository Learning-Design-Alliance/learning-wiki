`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Laboratory experiment in which students move a reflector and count maxima of meter deflections for microwaves, mirroring the fringe-counting procedure with laser light. The author reports students thereby determine the microwave wavelength with the same equation as for visible laser light.

> "By measuring the reﬂector’s displacement distances and cor- responding numbers of maximum relative intensity the wavelength of microwave radiation can be determined us- ing the same Eq. (3) as for visible laser light."
