Trend analysis of representative German large-scale assessment data comparing the 2016 and 2021 Grade 4 cohorts, with jackknife-2 standard errors and linking error. The article reports "significant negative trends in verbal achievements for non-SEN and SEN-L-R student cohorts in all three domains."

> "In Grade 4, the results indicate significant negative trends in verbal achievements for non-SEN and SEN-L-R student cohorts in all three domains. For SEN-L-S students, mean values were also smaller at T2 than at T1, but significant group differences were only found for orthography."
