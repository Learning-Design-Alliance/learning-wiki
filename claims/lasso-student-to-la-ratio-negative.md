## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen 2015

Ben Van Dusen, Laurie Langdon, and Valerie Otero. (2015). Learning Assistant Supported Student Outcomes (LASSO) study initial findings. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.081](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.081)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Continuous-variable HLM model (model 6) over the pilot-semester course sample (Table 2 reports n=17 courses) with effect size as outcome and student-to-LA ratio as course-level predictor; the printed coefficient is -0.005 with standard error 0.003 and no significance marker.

> "Model 6 shows that as the student to LA ratio increases, there is a decrease in students' average effect size (-0.005 per additional student/LA). This means that students in classes with less students and more LAs do better."
