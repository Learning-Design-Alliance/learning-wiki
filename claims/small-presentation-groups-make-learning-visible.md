## Evidence

### Ahlquist 2020

Ahlquist, E.-M. T., & Gynther, P. (2020). Teaching in the Montessori Classroom: Investigating Variation Theory and Embodiment as a Foundation of Teachers' Development. Journal of Montessori Research, 6(1). https://journals.ku.dk/jmr

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' discussion of the observed change from a 14-child polygon presentation to a five-child quadrilateral presentation. They report the smaller, more active format made it "possible for the teacher to identify how the children perceived the phenomenon."

> "By reducing the number of children in the presentations and letting them play a more active role—letting each child, for example, describe the differences and similarities between geometrical shapes—it was possible for the teacher to identify how the children perceived the phenomenon."
