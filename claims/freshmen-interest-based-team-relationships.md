### Lee Hyo Jeong 2023

Lee Hyo Jeong, Kim Do-Hwan, Kang Ye Ji. (2023). Passivity, task segmentation, and relational capital: a study of interpersonal relationship formation among freshman medical students in team-based activities. Korean J Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2023.261

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

FGI participants said being slightly uncomfortable with each other helped clarify individual responsibilities and enable taking more responsibility for roles during team projects.

> "Students perceived that maintaining a certain distance from team members prevented interference with team assignments and helped each member take responsibility and feel a sense of obligation, which prevented “free riding.”"

### Lee Hyo Jeong 2023 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

> "The participants found that these relationships allowed them to expand their human relations networks through their team members."
