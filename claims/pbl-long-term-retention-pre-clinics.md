# Fifteen months after the course, lecture-based knowledge and confidence scores decreased significantly from post-course to pre-clinics, while problem-based knowledge and confidence scores showed no significant decrease in one group with no comparison group

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pre-clinics knowledge for lecture-based items decreased significantly (p = 0.005) while problem-based knowledge showed no significant change (p = 0.903). [→ Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018](#al-madi-ebtissam-m-2018)
`q3 i?` Pre-clinics confidence for lecture-based items decreased significantly (p < 0.001) while problem-based confidence showed no significant decrease (p = 0.06). [→ Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018 (2)](#al-madi-ebtissam-m-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018

Al-Madi Ebtissam M, Celur Sree Lalita, Nasim Mamoona. (2018). Effectiveness of PBL methodology in a hybrid dentistry program to enhance students’ knowledge and confidence. (a pilot study). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1392-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired 't' tests comparing post-course and pre-clinics MCQ scores 15 months after the course in the same cohort. Lecture-based scores fell significantly; problem-based scores "marginally decreased" with no statistical significance; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The mean knowledge score (lecture based) decreased from 6.76 ± 2.36 to 5.09 ± 1.96 with statistical significance (p = 0.005), and the mean knowledge score (problem based) marginally decreased from 5.97 ± 1.99 to 5.9 ± 2.06 with no statistical significance (p = 0.903)"

### Al-Madi Ebtissam M 2018 (2)

Al-Madi Ebtissam M, Celur Sree Lalita, Nasim Mamoona. (2018). Effectiveness of PBL methodology in a hybrid dentistry program to enhance students’ knowledge and confidence. (a pilot study). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1392-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired 't' tests of confidence scores at post-course versus pre-clinics in the same cohort. Problem-based confidence decreased with "no statistical significance (p = 0.06)", a marginal result; no effect size is printed.

> "The mean confidence score (lecture based) decreased from 33.97 ± 10.17 to 25.24 ± 8.46 with a statistical significance (p < 0.001), and the mean confidence score (problem based) decreased from 29.42 ± 10.4 to 25.39 ± 9.7 with no statistical significance (p = 0.06)"
