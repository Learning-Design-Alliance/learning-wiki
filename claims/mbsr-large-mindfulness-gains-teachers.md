## Evidence

### Rupprecht 2017

Rupprecht, S., Paulus, P. & Walach, H. (2017). Mind the teachers! The impact of mindfulness training on self-regulation and classroom performance in a sample of German school teachers. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(4), 565-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.4.565

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental waitlist-controlled study of 32 German teachers (intervention n=18, control n=14) using the Freiburg Mindfulness Inventory; Table 2 reports Cohen's d of 1.78 (mindful presence) and 1.29 (mindful acceptance) at post-test and 1.11 and 1.20 at follow-up. The article states "The between-group effect sizes were large for both post-test and follow-up".
