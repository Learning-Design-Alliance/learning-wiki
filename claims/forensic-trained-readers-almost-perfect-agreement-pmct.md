Interobserver analysis of four blinded readers independently reporting 10 firearm-death PMCT cases against autopsy. The paper reports "an almost perfect agreement (Cohen's kappa = 0.88)" versus "only moderate agreement (Cohen's kappa = 0.53)"; overall Fleiss' kappa was 0.66.

> "Radiologists with prior PMCT training demonstrated an almost perfect agreement (Cohen's kappa = 0.88), whereas those without forensic experience showed only moderate agreement (Cohen's kappa = 0.53)."
