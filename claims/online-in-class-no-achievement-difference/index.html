Cimermanová 2018¶

Cimermanová, I. (2018). The Effect of Learning Styles on Academic Achievement in Different Forms of Teaching. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 219-232. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11316a

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-sample t-tests run separately for the online (df 26, p=.242) and in-class (df 54, p=.519) groups; the article states "an increase (not statistically significant)" in both. Descriptive means are quoted; no effect size is printed.
