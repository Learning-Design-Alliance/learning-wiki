Study 2 with four senior anthropologists who teach at European universities, using the same evaluation procedure plus open discussions. Responses on enjoyability were mixed (P3 "definitely fun"; P5 "intriguing but not fun"); all four endorsed classroom use only within a pedagogical framing.

> "Participants viewed the game primarily as an educational supplement rather than a standalone learning tool. P5, a senior anthropologist, characterized it as “an introduction to the book”"
