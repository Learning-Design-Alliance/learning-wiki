# Representational competence predicts ECL reduction with DCN visualization, but not accuracy increases or ICL reduction

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Higher representational competence was associated with a significantly larger reduction in extraneous cognitive load with visualization (F(1, 29) = 6.96, p = 0.013). [→ Bley 2026](#bley-2026)
`q2 i?` The RC test instrument did not significantly predict changes in accuracy or intrinsic cognitive load. [→ Bley 2026 (2)](#bley-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Bley 2026

Bley, J., Rexigel, E., Arias, A., Krupp, L., Longen, N., Lukowicz, P., Küchemann, S., Kuhn, J., Kiefer-Emmanouilidis, M., and Widera, A. (2026). Visualization Enhances Problem Solving in multi-Qubit Systems. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/hqzq-fbrb](https://doi.org/10.1103/hqzq-fbrb)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear regression of the ECL difference (with minus without visualization) on RC in the within-subject study (N=31). The regression equation was [ECL inc.] = −(0.16 ± 0.03) − (0.36 ± 0.13) · [RC], so higher RC predicted larger ECL reduction.

> "The statistics for the dependency of reduction in ECL on the RC evaluated at F (1, 29) = 6 .96, p = 0 .013, R2(R2adj.) = 0.19(0.17)."

### Bley 2026 (2)

Bley, J., Rexigel, E., Arias, A., Krupp, L., Longen, N., Lukowicz, P., Küchemann, S., Kuhn, J., Kiefer-Emmanouilidis, M., and Widera, A. (2026). Visualization Enhances Problem Solving in multi-Qubit Systems. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/hqzq-fbrb](https://doi.org/10.1103/hqzq-fbrb)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Regression analyses of accuracy and ICL differences on RC (Table IV) found no significant slopes; the article reports the instrument "was not able to predict the changes in performance or ICL" and suggests further adjustments to the instrument.

> "However, the test instrument was not able to predict the changes in performance or ICL."
