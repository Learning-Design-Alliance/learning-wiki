## Evidence

### Keles 2025

Keles, S., ten Braak, D., Hagen, Å. M., & Melby-Lervåg, M. (2025). Validity of Early-Grade Screening Tools in the Three Tier (RTI/MTSS) Model: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Literacy, Language, and Mathematics. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10077-5

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Descriptive synthesis of sensitivity and specificity data from nine validation studies covering 14 screening tools used for tier assignment. The review reports "The sensitivity range for the tools across domains varied from zero and 100" and cut-offs from 7 to 40%. In literacy, sensitivity was 59–100 and specificity 38–84.2; the single mathematics tool showed specificity of 15–32.

> "The sensitivity range for the tools across domains varied from zero and 100, while the specificity range across domains spanned from 15 to 100. The cut-off used ranged from 7 to 40% across the different studies."
