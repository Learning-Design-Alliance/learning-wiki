## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Imundo 2024

Imundo, M. N., Zung, I., Whatley, M. C., & Pan, S. C. (2024). When Two Learners Are Better Than One: Using Flashcards with a Partner Improves Metacognitive Accuracy. Metacognition and Learning. https://link.springer.com/journal/11409/articles

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 1's final cued-recall test (40 GRE word-definition pairs, 5-min delay, ≥75% spelling-match scoring threshold) showed no significant difference between conditions, contrary to the hypothesis that paired learning would yield higher performance.

> "final test performance was not significantly different between the Individual and Paired conditions, t (150) = 1.29, p = .20, d = 0.21, 95% CI [-.03, .13]."
