## Evidence

### Tseng Yi-Ling 2025

Tseng Yi-Ling, Wu Hua-Shan, Huang Pei-Yu, Hsaio Pei-Shan, Tseng Hui-Chen. (2025). Effects of a Situated Simulation Teaching Strategy on Knowledge of Negative-Pressure Wound Therapy among Surgical Nurses in Taiwan. Advances in Skin & Wound Care. https://doi.org/10.1097/ASW.0000000000000293

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental one-group pretest/posttest study of 31 surgical nurses at a central Taiwan regional hospital, analyzed with a Wilcoxon signed-rank test. The article reports "a significant improvement (z = −4.45, P < .001)" in total NPWT knowledge; no effect size is printed.

> "Their average score on NPWT knowledge was 19.90 before the intervention and 27.84 after the intervention, a significant improvement (z = −4.45, P < .001)."
