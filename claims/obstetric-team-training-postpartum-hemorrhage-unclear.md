# The effect of obstetric team training on severe postpartum hemorrhage and transfusion remains unclear, with conflicting RCT and cohort results

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` One RCT reported increased severe blood loss (OR 2.20, 95% CI 1.24–3.90) and transfusion of four or more units (OR 2.10, 95% CI 1.10–4.01), while observational studies reported OR 1.08 (95% CI 0.96–1.23) and OR 0.63 (95% CI 0.38–1.04); certainty was very low. ## Evidence

### Brogaard L 2022

Brogaard L, Glerup Lauridsen K, Løfgren B, et al. (2022). The effects of obstetric emergency team training on patient outcome: A systematic review and meta‐analysis. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14263

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of eight studies evaluating postpartum hemorrhage outcomes after team training. Certainty of evidence was categorized as very low for both severe blood loss and transfusion outcomes.

> "Severe blood loss was reported in one RCT 16 with an OR of 2.20 (95% CI 1.24–3.90) and in two observational studies 21 , 25 there was a combined OR of 1.08 (95% CI 0.96–1.23) (I 2 = 0%)."
