## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Abass 2011

Bada Tayo Abass. (2011). Computer Instructional Approach and Students' Creative Ability in Sculpture Education in Nigeria Universities: Obafemi Awolowo University as a Case Study. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v1n2p130

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Scheffe post-hoc comparison of post-test mean scores by creative ability level in the 48-student ANCOVA study. The printed mean differences were 11.92 (high vs low) and 8.67 (average vs low), each with calculated significance level .00, below the .05 alpha. No effect size is printed.

> "the students with high creativability performed significantly better than those with low ability, also the students with average ability performed significantly better than those with low creative ability."

### Abass 2011 (2)

Bada Tayo Abass. (2011). Computer Instructional Approach and Students' Creative Ability in Sculpture Education in Nigeria Universities: Obafemi Awolowo University as a Case Study. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v1n2p130

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Scheffe post-hoc comparison in the same study: the mean difference between high and average creative ability students was 3.26 with a calculated significance level of 0.18 alpha, greater than .05, so this contrast was not statistically significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "there was no significant difference between the performances of students with high and average creative ability."
