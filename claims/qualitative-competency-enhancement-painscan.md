In the embedded descriptive qualitative study using semi-structured interviews with 11 PainScan-group nurses analysed thematically, the first theme was "competency enhancement in pain assessment through PainScan training"; one nurse noted scoring criteria became "much more comparable" after training.

> "Participants reported significant improvements in both theoretical understanding and practical application of pain assessment. PainScan integrated theoretical instruction with deliberate case‐based practice to reinforce knowledge."
