Inductive thematic analysis of chatbot prompts and open-ended responses, coded by the first author; sentiment annotation by two independent reviewers reached "substantial agreement (Cohen'sκ= .75)". Usage differed strongly between RL and Always conditions, with 43.2% of RL participants never using AI.

> "First of all, 43.2% in the RL condition did never use AI either because the agent did not think it necessary to allow us-age or because they deliberately choose not to."
