## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wick Katharina 2018

Wick Katharina, Faude Oliver, Manes Susanne, Zahner Lukas, Donath Lars. (2018). I Can Stand Learning: A Controlled Pilot Intervention Study on the Effects of Increased Standing Time on Cognitive Function in Primary School Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15020356

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

2 (group) × 2 (pre/post) ANOVA on digit span in the pilot (n = 38). The article reports "The mean changes and their effect sizes were higher in the IG (IG: 0.46–0.48; CG: 0.26)", but no group effect reached significance (group p-values 0.16–0.74 in Table 3).

> "The group × time interaction was neither significant for the short-term memory (forward digit span) nor for the working memory (backward digit span)."
