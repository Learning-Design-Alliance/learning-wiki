## Evidence

### Sensibaugh Cheryl A. 2017

Sensibaugh Cheryl A., Madrid Nathaniel J., Choi Hye-Jeong, Anderson William L., Osgood Marcy P. (2017). Undergraduate Performance in Solving Ill-Defined Biochemistry Problems. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar63. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-04-0106

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Secondary correlation analysis on the Integrate domain (21/47 unsatisfactory) among the 47 graduating majors. The correlation of r = −0.48 is a printed effect size of large magnitude; as IPSA scores decreased, an unsubstantiated conclusion was more likely part of the response.

> "Further correlation analysis revealed that including unsubstantiated or incorrect conclusions within a response correlated moderately and negatively with IPSA scores in this domain (r = −0.48, p < 0.001)."
