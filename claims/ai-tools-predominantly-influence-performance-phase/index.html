Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • A qualitative review reports that AI applications supported learners' attitudes, motivation and self-efficacy in the forethought phase, but self-efficacy gains were inconsistent across studies, and students with initially low self-efficacy often struggled to use the tools — related
  • Only 3 of 14 AI applications in the reviewed studies provided comprehensive support across all three phases of Zimmerman's SRL model — related
  • Chatbots were the most frequently used AI application type for supporting SRL, followed by evaluation systems — related
