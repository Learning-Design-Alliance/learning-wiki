# Students who find a good basis may still incorrectly believe the first-order energy corrections depend on the initial choice of basis

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Some students thought first-order corrections to the energies would differ depending on whether the coupled or uncoupled representation was chosen as the initial basis. [→ Emily Marshman 2019](#emily-marshman-2019)

## Evidence

### Emily Marshman 2019

Emily Marshman, Christof Keebaugh, and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Student difficulties with the corrections to the energy spectrum of the hydrogen atom for the intermediate field Zeeman effect. arXiv:2006.05000. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.marshman](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.marshman)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview and written-response findings from students who had correctly identified a good basis for the Zeeman effect. The article notes "it does not make sense experimentally that the observed perturbative corrections would depend upon the choice of basis."

> "Many students thought that the ﬁrst order corrections to the energies depend on the initial choice of basis."
