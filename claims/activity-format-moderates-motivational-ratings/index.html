Lauc 2020¶

Lauc, T., Jagodić, G. K., & Bistrović, J. (2020). Effects of Multimedia Instructional Message on Motivation and Academic Performance of Elementary School Students in Croatia. International Journal of Instruction, 13(4), 491-508. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13431a

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Comparing total assessments (average of interesting, instructional, fun) for each of the six introductory activity types, multimedia was favoured for four groups, but quizzes (t = 1.53, p = .126) and picture descriptions (t = .951, p = .343) showed no significant format difference.

"all activities were rated better when they were in multimedia format except for quizzes (M = 4.68, SD = .608 vs M = 4.58, SD = .847, t = 1.53, p = .126)"
