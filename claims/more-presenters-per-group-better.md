### Angel Sánchez & Cristina Brändle 2014

Angel Sánchez & Cristina Brändle. (2014). More network science for teenagers. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1403.3618](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1403.3618)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Staffing observation from the University workshop sessions, where students were split into two groups by their original High School classes; the authors recommend either a third facilitator or two facilitators per group.

> "While the size of the two groups for the workshop session was manageable, it would have worked better with a third person so we could have formed three groups, or else with two people per group so discussions could be followed more closely by the presenters."
