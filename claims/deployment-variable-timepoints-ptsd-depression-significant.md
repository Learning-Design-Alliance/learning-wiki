## Evidence

### Bøg M 2018

Bøg M, Filges T, & Jørgensen A.M.K. (2018). Deployment of personnel to military operations: impact on mental health and social functioning. Campbell Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.4073/csr.2018.6

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

The review's meta-analysis at other (variable) time points since exposure, comparing deployed to non-deployed personnel, found "the average effects of PTSD and depression were positive and statistically significant" with the printed ORs, while the common mental disorder analysis was inconclusive.

> "For the absolute comparison the analysis of common mental disorder was inconclusive, whereas the average effects of PTSD and depression were positive and statistically significant (PTSD odds ratio (OR) was 1.91 (95% confidence interval (CI): 1.28 to 2.85) and OR=1.98 (95% CI: 1.05 to 3.70) for depression)."
