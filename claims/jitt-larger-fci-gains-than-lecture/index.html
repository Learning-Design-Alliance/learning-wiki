Evidence¶

S. P. Formica 2010¶

S. P. Formica, J. L. Easley, and M. C. Spraker. (2010). Transforming common-sense beliefs into Newtonian thinking through Just-In-Time Teaching. Phys. Rev. ST Phys. Educ. Res. 6(2), 020106. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.6.020106

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental comparison of 222 students in introductory physics courses taught by traditional lecture versus Just-in-Time Teaching over five semesters at North Georgia College & State University, using the Force Concept Inventory as pretest and post-test. The study found "a 37.6%±2.0% gain" for JiTT versus 17.9%±2.5% for lecture; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Overall, the gains favor the Just-in-Time teaching method with a 37.6%±2.0% gain compared to the 17.9%±2.5% seen in traditional lecture classes."
