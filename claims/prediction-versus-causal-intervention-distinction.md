This is the paper's central methodological argument, made as a theoretical argument rather than an empirical test. It holds that predictive models answer correlational questions while intervention requires causal ones, and recommends the comparative-effectiveness toolkit: uplift modeling, randomized encouragement designs, staggered rollouts, and regression discontinuity at existing thresholds.

> "A model can predict attrition with high accuracy and still provide no guidance about what to do, because the features that predict best, such as prior GPA, are often the features an institution cannot change."
