## Evidence

### Kuhn 2023

Kuhn, Jochen; Müller, Andreas; Vogt, Patrik. (2023). Newspaper Story Problems and Other Tasks for Context Based Physics Education: A Research Based Report on Classroom Practice. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.11149](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.11149)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental classroom comparison for the electrical energy topic: experimental classes used newspaper story problems, control classes conventional textbook tasks with identical content, difficulty, teacher and lesson plan. The study reports "significantly higher motivation than those in CC during and after treatment, as well as in follow-up measures"; the article's conclusions report an effect size of d > 1.5 for motivation.

> "While motivation (Fig. 6a) was nearly equal in EC and CC before treatment, pupils in EC reported significantly higher motivation than those in CC during and after treatment, as well as in follow-up measures."

### Kuhn 2023 (2)

Kuhn, Jochen; Müller, Andreas; Vogt, Patrik. (2023). Newspaper Story Problems and Other Tasks for Context Based Physics Education: A Research Based Report on Classroom Practice. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.11149](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.11149)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The article's conclusions section reports the Cohen d effect sizes for the intervention, stating "the advantage of NSPs is >  1.5 for motivation and > 0.9 for learning", i.e. an effect size of d > 1.5 for motivation, with details reported in the earlier research publications.

> "The results show large positive effects on pupils’ motivation and learning; in terms of effect sizes 8 (Cohen d), the advantage of NSPs is >  1.5 for motivation and > 0.9 for learning (see Kuhn, 2010 and Kuhn & Müler, 2014 for details)."
