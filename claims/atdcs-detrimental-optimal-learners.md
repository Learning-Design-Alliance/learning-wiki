## Evidence

### Maceira-Elvira 2024

Maceira-Elvira, P., Popa, T., Schmid, A.-C., Cadic-Melchior, A., Müller, H., & Schaer, R. (2024). Native learning ability and not age determines the effect of brain stimulation on motor skill acquisition. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00278-y

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Modeling dataset verum versus unstimulated/placebo contrast showing "the accuracy of the high tier of optimal learners wor-sened signiﬁcantly on theﬁrst day (t20 = 3.887, d = 4.39, P = 0.0009)". The low tier of optimal learners was not significant.

### Maceira-Elvira 2024 (2)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`
