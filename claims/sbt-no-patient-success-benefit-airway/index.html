Evidence¶

Sun Yanxia 2017¶

Sun Yanxia, Pan Chuxiong, Li Tianzuo, Gan Tong J. (2017). Airway management education: simulation based training versus non-simulation based training-A systematic review and meta-analyses. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-017-0313-7

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of four trials assessing learner skill acquisition on patients or volunteers. The "risk ratio (RR) was 1.26 (95% CI: 0.96 to 1.66)", indicating no significant difference between SBT and NSBT groups; equivalence was not tested.

"Four studies [8, 9, 13, 18] provided success rate of procedure completion. The risk ratio (RR) was 1.26 (95% CI: 0.96 to 1.66), indicating no significant difference between SBT and NSBT groups."
