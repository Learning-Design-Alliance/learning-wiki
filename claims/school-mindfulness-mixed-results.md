Narrative review citing a multi-site trial: "no improvement in mental health outcomes" after 8 weeks, with possible anxiety increases in some subgroups. Explanations cited include low at-home compliance and variation in instructor experience. No effect sizes printed.

### Madeline B. Harms and Sherona D. Garrett-Ruffin 2023 (2)

Madeline B. Harms and Sherona D. Garrett-Ruffin. (2023). Disrupting links between poverty, chronic stress, and educational inequality. npj Science of Learning 8:50. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00199-2

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that meta-analyses and cluster RCTs of adolescent school mindfulness interventions show mixed results. It contrasts this with mostly positive effects of broader SEL programs. No pooled effect sizes printed.

> "Results from larger meta-analyses and randomized control trials of mindfulness interventions in adolescents have been mixed, with little evidence for unambiguously positive outcomes 67,68."
