# In one single-group pre-post study, the proportion of interns attending to social, psychological or emotional factors of disease rose from 10.98% to 37.16% after software-based training

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the information collection stage, the proportion of interns attending to social, psychological or emotional factors of disease rose from 10.98% to 37.16% after software-based training (P < 0.001). [→ Sun Chao 2020](#sun-chao-2020)

## Evidence

### Sun Chao 2020

Sun Chao, Zou Junkai, Zhao Lanbo, Wang Qing, Zhang Shaozhi, Ulain Qurat, Song Qing, Li Qiling. (2020). New doctor-patient communication learning software to help interns succeed in communication skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1917-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-controlled pre/post trial of interns at the First Affiliated Hospital of Xi'an Jiaotong University assessed with the SEGUE framework; the information collection item 'Asking about psychological factors' rose from 10.98% to 37.16%, and the article reports this difference as "P < 0.001".

> "In the collection process, compared to the first assessment results, more interns were able to pay attention to the social, psychological or emotional factors associated with disease (37.16% vs. 10.98%; P < 0.001)"
