## Evidence

### Lord 2017

Lord, P., Rabiasz, A., and Styles, B. (2017). 'Literacy Octopus' Dissemination Trial: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN84508118

`q4 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Primary intention-to-treat multilevel (school and pupil) analysis of National Pupil Database KS2 English scores for the 2015/2016 cohort, with KS1 as baseline, across 12,397 analysed schools. The global likelihood ratio test was 3.45 (p = 0.48); "there is no evidence of any effect" of the interventions. Per-arm effect sizes versus control were 0.01 (95% CI -0.01 to 0.03), estimated at 0 months' progress.

> "This yielded a likelihood ratio of 3.45 (p = 0.48) meaning that we do not reject the null hypothesis and there is no evidence of any effect of the various interventions."

### Lord 2017 (2)

Lord, P., Rabiasz, A., and Styles, B. (2017). 'Literacy Octopus' Dissemination Trial: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN84508118

`q4 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Descriptive check of raw unadjusted means from the NPD 2015/2016 KS2 dataset prior to modelling, reported in Table 5b. Means were close across arms (CEM 205.98, IEE 205.98, ResearchEd 206.19, How2s 206.11, control 205.95), consistent with the null modelled result.

> "We also explored raw unadjusted means using Key Stage 2 English attainment data for all pupils in dissemination trial schools in the NPD 2015/2016 KS2 dataset"
