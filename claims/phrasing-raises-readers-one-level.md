`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interpretation of Figure 1, which plots mean first-passage comprehension scores for the three formats by reading level. The text states the improvement is approximately one reading level; the figure comparison is descriptive, and no effect size was printed.

> "From this figure it is evident that when poor readers read passages in a phrased format, their reading comprehension improved to the reading comprehension level typical of average readers reading standard prose passages. In a similar manner, when the average readers read passages in a phrased format, their reading comprehension improved almost to the reading comprehension level typical of good readers reading standard prose passages."
