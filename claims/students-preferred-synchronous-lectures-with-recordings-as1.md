## Evidence

### Saray Busto 2021

Saray Busto, Michael Dumbser, and Elena Gaburro. (2021). A Simple but Efficient Concept of Blended Teaching of Mathematics for Engineering Students during the COVID-19 Pandemic. [doi:10.3390/educsci11020056](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci11020056)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Systematic ex ante survey (AS1) of DICAM students in June 2020 at the end of the emergency online teaching, with n = 445 respondents; the article reports that "the clear preference of the students regarding online teaching was synchronous online lectures" with recordings available afterwards. No effect size is printed.

> "the clear preference of the students regarding online teaching was synchronous online lectures with the recording of the lecture made available to the students via the Moodle–Kaltura platform afterwards."
