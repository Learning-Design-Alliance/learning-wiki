## Evidence

### Tian Qi 2026

Tian Qi, Huang Yinpin, Xiong Jun. (2026). Psychological quality and entrepreneurial competence: the mediating role of professional recognition and moderating effect of academic discipline among Chinese university students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03954-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bias-corrected bootstrap test with 5,000 resamples within the SEM of 741 undergraduates confirmed mediation: "the indirect effect of Psychological Quality on Entrepreneurial Competence through Professional Recognition was statistically significant (β = 0.188, p < 0.001)"; the direct effect remained significant (β = 0.528), yielding a mediation ratio of 26.25%.

> "The indirect effect of Psychological Quality on Entrepreneurial Competence through Professional Recognition was statistically significant (β = 0.188, p < 0.001). This finding was supported by the 95% bias-corrected confidence interval (95% CI = [0.112, 0.275]), which did not include zero."
