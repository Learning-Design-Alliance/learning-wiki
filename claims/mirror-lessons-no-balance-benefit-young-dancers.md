## Evidence

### Notarnicola Angela 2014

Notarnicola Angela, Maccagnano Giuseppe, Pesce Vito, Pierro Silvia Di, Tafuri Silvio, Moretti Biagio. (2014). Effect of teaching with or without mirror on balance in young female ballet students. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/1756-0500-7-426

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized prospective study of 64 young female dancers (32 mirror, 32 non-mirror) assessed with BESS at recruitment and after 6 months. The article reports "No other differences due to the group or to the time of measurement were found (p > 0.05)"; no effect size was printed.

> "The repeated measures ANOVA analysis shows that for BESS total score there is a difference due to the time (F = 3.86; p < 0.05). No other differences due to the group or to the time of measurement were found (p > 0.05)."
