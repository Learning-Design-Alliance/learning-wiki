### Sohail Fadieleh A 2025

Sohail Fadieleh A, Iqbal Marium, Malik Marib Ghulam Rasool, Saeed Sana. (2025). Perspectives of faculty, students and patients on clinical skills teaching in undergraduate medical education in Pakistan: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-103139

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 6 student interview data; students described learning how to talk to patients through mirroring teachers, and separately reported that consultants' bedside teaching shows how experienced physicians think beyond what is in books.

> "We learn how we should talk to the patient. We have to be kind and polite… They (our teachers) teach us empathy and professionalism."
