Evidence¶

Liao 2026¶

Liao, C.-Y. (2026). Beyond a single role: Justifying a role-adaptive framework for ai companions through a comparative study in elementary book talk. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-13958-6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Content analysis of transcripts coded by two researchers against the 4F framework (Cohen's κ = 0.86), with paired t-tests on utterance counts for Grade 4 (n=12). The teacher elicited more utterances in all four categories; no effect size printed.

"Although the AI sessions had a higher proportion of ‘Facts’ utterances, the teacher prompted a significantly higher absolute number of statements in this category ( t(11) = −5.11, p <.001). This superiority of the human teacher extended across all other reflective dimensions: ‘Feelings’ ( t(11) = −3.45, p =.005), ‘Findings’ (t(11) = −3.88, p =.003), and was most pronounced for ‘Future’-oriented talk (t(11) = −4.92, p <.001)."
