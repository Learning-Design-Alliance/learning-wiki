Swagata Sarkar and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Sense of Belonging and Intent to Persist: Mediating Role of Motivation and Moderating Role of Gender in Physics and Astronomy Graduate Students. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sarkar](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sarkar)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup probit regressions after a median split on competence, reported in the findings section and Fig. 3. High-competence students showed a slope of 1.259 versus 0.180 for low-competence students, described as "a meaningful and amplifying mediator."

> "Figure 3 shows a steep SB–IP slope (1.259) for students with high Competence, indicating that as SB increases, so does their intent to persist. In contrast, students with low Competence show a flatter slope (0.180)"
