Greene 1928¶

q1 · i? · design · r2

Definitional/descriptive statement of the test construction procedure. The article reports that of two easy exercises of closely the same value one went in Form A and the other in Form B, and that unit values were converted to point values so the total possible score on a given form is 100 points.

"the two forms comprising ten exercises each would have the same total values. A third consideration was that the two forms should have closely the same inter-exercise interval."
