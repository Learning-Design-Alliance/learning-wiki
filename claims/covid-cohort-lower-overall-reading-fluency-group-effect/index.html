Evidence¶

Kraus 2024¶

Kraus, E. B., Wild, J., Schilcher, A., & Hilbert, S. (2024). Exploring the Short-Term and Long-Term Effects of the COVID-19 School Closures on Reading Fluency in Southern Germany: A Longitudinal Causal Effects Analysis. https://osf.io/bu2ad/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

DiD regression on the Bavarian sample; Table 4 reports β1 = -2.71 (CI -3.98 to -1.44, p < .001) and intercept β0 = 27.66 versus a norm sample mean of 22. The quote reports the "significant negative group effect (β1)"; no standardized effect size was printed.
