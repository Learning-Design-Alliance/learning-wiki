Kunz Kevin, Burkert Mirka, Heindl Felix, Schüttpelz-Brauns Katrin, Giesler Marianne. (2019). The frequency of using certain feedback methods in the teaching of medicine: a survey of teachers at the medical faculties in Baden-Wurttemberg. GMS Journal for Medical Education. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on situations in which instructors give students feedback. "By far, the instructors most often gave feedback in learning groups during course sessions (71% frequently or very frequently)"; encouraging peer feedback followed (40%), feedback in assessments (38%) and during breaks (34%), while 75% never gave feedback on a learning portfolio and 89% never online via a learning platform.
