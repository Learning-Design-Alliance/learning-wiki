## Evidence

### Alian 2018

Alian, J., Khodabandeh, F., & Soleimani, H. (2018). The effect of CALL-based tasks on EFL learners’ grammar learning. Teaching English with Technology, 18(3), 54-68. http://www.tewtjournal.org

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 60 Iranian junior high learners (30 per group) taught six grammar topics over 15 sessions; the post-test comparison favored the CALL-based task group, with means of 17.45 and 15.60 and a significant independent samples t-test ("t = 25.869, p = .000").

> "independent samples test showed significant difference in grammar learning between the two groups (experimental and control groups) on post-test with (t = 25.869, p = .000, p < α)"
