## Evidence

### Gao C 2026

Gao C, Li J, Shui K, Xie X, Peng L. (2026). Effectiveness of Problem-Based Learning (PBL) in specialist physician training for kidney transplantation. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000047684

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Faculty-scored multidisciplinary kidney transplantation case discussions in 4 domains (accuracy of interpretation, completeness of problem identification, clarity of reasoning, integration of multidisciplinary information), same retrospective cohort. A higher PBL proportion reached ≥80 points (83.1% vs 55.4%; χ2 = 10.407, P = .001).

> "The overall discussion performance score was higher in the PBL group compared with the control group (86.0 ± 6.0 vs 79.0 ± 7.0; t = −5.744, P < .001)"
