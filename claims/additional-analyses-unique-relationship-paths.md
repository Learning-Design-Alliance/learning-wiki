# In additional analyses without controlling for other relationship types, closeness with friends directly predicted higher subsequent achievement and closeness with teachers predicted higher school satisfaction

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kiuru 2020

Kiuru, N., Wang, M.-T., Salmela-Aro, K., Kannas, L., Ahonen, T., & Hirvonen, R. (2020). Associations between Adolescents' Interpersonal Relationships, School Well-being, and Academic Achievement during Educational Transitions. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-019-01184-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Additional analyses repeating the models separately for each relationship type without controlling for the other types. The main transactional pattern was similar, but this direct friend-closeness-to-achievement path, a teacher-closeness-to-satisfaction path, and a parent-conflict path appeared only in these models.

> "a significant cross-lagged effect from high closeness with friends on subsequent higher academic achievement in the spring of seventh grade was detected (β = 0.05, SE = 0.02, p = 0.034)"
