## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On procedural items novices performed better on the posttest compared to the pretest, while for advanced learners the pattern was reversed with significant improvement on declarative items only. [→ Wouters 2011](#wouters-2011)

## Evidence

### Wouters 2011

Wouters, P., van der Spek, E. D., & van Oostendorp, H. (2011). Measuring learning in serious games: A case study with structural assessment. Educational Technology Research and Development, 59(6), 741–763. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-010-9183-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Wilcoxon tests on knowledge test scores from 19 participants. The article reports that "on the pro- cedural items novices perform better on the posttest compared to the pretest" (Z = -2.448) while for advanced learners the pattern is reversed (declarative items, Z = -2.000).

> "on the pro- cedural items novices perform better on the posttest compared to the pretest ( Z =- 2.448, p\ .05), while this is not true for the declarative items ( Z =- 1.561, p[ .05). For advanced learners the pattern is reversed: on declarative items they perform better on the posttest compared to the pretest ( Z =- 2.000, p\ .05), while this is not true for the procedural items (Z =- .816, p[ .05)."
