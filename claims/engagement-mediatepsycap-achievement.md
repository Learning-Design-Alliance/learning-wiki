## Evidence

### Wu Yajun 2025

Wu Yajun, Kang Xia, Li Yiyan. (2025). Linking EFL psychological capital and EFL achievement: the chain mediating effect of academic boredom and behavioural engagement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03685-x

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test in the same SEM of 1460 Chinese secondary EFL learners; the engagement pathway had a standardized indirect effect of β3 = 0.076, 95% CIs [0.024, 0.134], excluding zero, "explaining 15.3% and 16.9%" respectively.

> "Each mediation path had a significant effect on EFL achievement (β2 = 0.069, 95% CIs [0.023, 0.120]; β3 = 0.076, 95% CIs [0.024, 0.134]), explaining 15.3% and 16.9% of the total effect of EFL PsyCap on EFL achievement, respectively."
