## Evidence

### Vashti Sawtelle & Chandra Turpen 2014

Vashti Sawtelle & Chandra Turpen. (2014). Leveraging a Relationship with Biology to Expand a Relationship with Physics. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1409.5471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1409.5471)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Longitudinal ethnographic case study of one student (participant observations, ~35 hours of field notes, ~84 hours of video, three interviews) in the NEXUS/Physics course; the authors characterize her incoming and outgoing relationships with physics, finding she became "more of a symbols person" with a love/hate stance. No effect sizes are reported.

> "we see Violet's relationship with physics changing from someone who "hates physics", is "not a symbols person", and thinks "physics just happens" to a person who has a "love/hate" relationship with physics, is "more of a symbols person""
