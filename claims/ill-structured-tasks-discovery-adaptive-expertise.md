`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesis of the task/training characteristics in Table 4 across the nine reviews. The article links this to the concept of change: learners are asked to show how they deal with change by confronting them with ill-structured or non-routine problems, and complex tasks necessitate problem solving in interaction with others.

> "It is found to be important that learners are confronted with a variety of ill-structured and non-routine (real life) tasks, and that they are stimulated to learn and discover by themselves."
