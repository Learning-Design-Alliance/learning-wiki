## Evidence

### Tan Chee-Kiat 2021

Tan Chee-Kiat, Chua Wei-Ling, Vu Charles Kien-Fong, Chang Jason Pik-Eu. (2021). High-stakes examinations during the COVID-19 pandemic: to proceed or not to proceed, that is the question. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2020-139241

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' own account of the operational cost of running the pandemic examination, listing the sources of the additional man-hours. No effect sizes are printed.

> "We required 88 more man-hours than previous years. The additional man-hours were due to the coordination with more agencies, testing the video conferencing software at the examination venue with the examiners in their own offices, additional invigilators as the candidates were in their own individual rooms for the MCQ paper and an onsite IT technician on the day of the examination."

### Tan Chee-Kiat 2021 (2)

Tan Chee-Kiat, Chua Wei-Ling, Vu Charles Kien-Fong, Chang Jason Pik-Eu. (2021). High-stakes examinations during the COVID-19 pandemic: to proceed or not to proceed, that is the question. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2020-139241

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors report a technical hitch during the viva voce; because neither affected candidate failed and the examination was criterion-referenced, they judged the interruption did not unfairly affect outcomes, while acknowledging it 'could have caused emotional distress'.

> "On the day itself, there was a technical hitch resulting in a loss of communications for 2 min in two of the rooms for viva voce. Two candidates were affected and they were compensated with another 2 min for the viva voce."
