# Students' respect for the instructor is affected by the horizontal posture dimension but not the vertical dimension, with pairwise differences no longer significant after correction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i1` small
> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In the preregistered experiment, students' respect was significantly affected only by the horizontal dimension, and corrected pairwise contrasts were not significant. [→ Traulsen 2024](#traulsen-2024)

## Evidence

### Traulsen 2024

Traulsen, S. J., & Zander, L. (2024). Straighten your back, open your arms! Effects of instructor's body postures in educational videos on students' interest and motivation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101959

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered experiment with 434 students; 2×2 ANOVA on respect (two items, α = .75). Only the horizontal dimension was significant (partial η2 = .011); the vertical effect was non-significant (partial η2 = .003).

> "Students' respect for the instructor was significantly affected by the horizontal dimension, F (1, 430) = 4.851, p = .028, partial η2 = .011, but not by the vertical dimension, F (1, 430) = 1.363, p = .244, partial η2 = .003, nor by the interaction of both dimensions, F (1, 430) = 2.810, p = .094, partial η2 = .006."
