# Pilot-test evaluation (n=463) shows students were highly motivated, preferred formative administration and an annual frequency, but perceived limited learning effect

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an 11-item survey after the pilot test, students expressed a desire for the progress test as a permanent part of the programme and for more helpful feedback, voted clearly for formative administration, mostly preferred yearly frequency, and only to a limited extent perceived a learning effect from taking the test. Evaluation survey of the pilot test at eight schools (n=463), conducted by Freiburg University's Competence Center immediately after the test using 11 items on a 4-point scale. The sample comprised "284 (61.3%) were females, 162 (35.0%) were males"; item results appear in Table 5.

> "The total number of students who participated in the evaluation was 463, of which 284 (61.3%) were females, 162 (35.0%) were males and 17 without an indication of their gender."
