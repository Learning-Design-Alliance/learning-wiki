Model evaluation study comparing several NLP models (BERT baseline and fine-tuned, DeBERTa, RoBERTa) on classifying learner-generated questions. The article reports that "DeBERTa achieved the highest overall accuracy (86.36%)" while noting its difficulty with contextual nuance in complex queries.
