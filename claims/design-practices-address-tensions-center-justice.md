Findings across the four cases show practices responding to tensions: Cases 2 and 4 made community partners central to decision making; Cases 1 and 3 built understanding with faculty and experts, e.g., aligning DEI with faculty needs and creating collaborative decision spaces.

> "Tension became a driving force for the design practices that our participants implemented. Design practices refer to what a designer does throughout the course of a project (Reiser, 2001). In this study, those practices focused largely on the interpersonal practices pre- sent within the design team"
