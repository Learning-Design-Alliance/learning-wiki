## Evidence

### Burch 2016

Burch, P., Good, A., & Heinrich, C. (2016). Improving Access to, Quality, and the Effectiveness of Digital Tutoring in K–12 Education. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373715592706

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive tests (mean comparisons, chi-square cross-tabulations) and logistic regression on Dallas ISD digital provider data. The odds of a student with disabilities being tutored with curriculum-based software were 49% higher than for students without disabilities.

> "students with disabilities were more likely to be tutored with curriculum-based software (p = .0256) or a tutor with software combination (p < .0001), while English language learners were also more likely to receive OST tutoring through a combination of tutor and software-driven instruction (p < .0001)."
