### McCracken DJ 2019

Narrative review of EBUS training literature: a systematic review of 27 studies suggested 37–44 procedures for 80% accuracy, but "the number of procedures required to achieve “competence” varied between 10 and 529".

> "Although the definition of competence was somewhat unclear, the number of procedures required to achieve “competence” varied between 10 and 529; however, using a cumulative sum analysis this varied between 10 and 100."
