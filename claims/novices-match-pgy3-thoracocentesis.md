## Evidence

### Huang Sih-Shiang 2024

Huang Sih-Shiang, Lin Chih-Hsien, Lin Shao-Yung, Huang Chien-Tai, Lien Wan-Ching. (2024). How to enhance the novices' learning in ultrasound-guided procedures utilizing handmade phantoms? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06458-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Concurrent comparison in the same prospective study: 36 novices vs 12 PGY-3 EM residents (with >20 real-patient US procedures experience) evaluated on the high-fidelity phantom. No significant difference in global rating; puncture time comparable (13 vs 14 sec median, p = 0.232).

> "achieving a comparable level of proficiency with the PGY-3 emergency medicine residents [5 (4–5) vs. 5 (5), p = 0.105]"
