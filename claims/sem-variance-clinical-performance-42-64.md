Variance decomposition from the emerging CB-SEM model (N = 585) reported that "the resulting variance of clinical performance was 42.64%, which was attributed to SMA, nomophobia, and ASE". No standardized effect size accompanies this variance figure, so it is reported descriptively.

> "Findings demonstrated that SMA contributed 7.80% of the computed variance of nomophobia, while ASE had an R 2 value of 11.28%, measured by both SMA and nomophobia. Finally, the resulting variance of clinical performance was 42.64%, which was attributed to SMA, nomophobia, and ASE."
