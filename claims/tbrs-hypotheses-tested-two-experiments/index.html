Evidence¶

Sébastien Puma 2018¶

Sébastien Puma, Nadine Matton, Pierre-Vincent Paubel, André Tricot. (2018). Cognitive Load Theory and Time Considerations: Using the Time-Based Resource Sharing Model. Educational Psychology Review, 30 (3). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9438-6

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Two experiments reported in the article tested hypotheses derived from the TBRS model. The results showed the model "can assist the investigation of information presentation pace effects during learning", with the stated condition that "prior knowledge is taken into account". No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"In two experiments, we tested hypotheses allowed by the model. Results showed that the Time-Based Resource Sharing model can assist the investigation of information presentation pace effects during learning, as long as prior knowledge is taken into account."
