Evidence¶

Eleanor M. Taylor 2024¶

Eleanor M. Taylor, Claire J. Cadwallader, Dylan Curtin, Trevor T.-J. Chong, Joshua J. Hendrikse & James P. Coxon. (2024). High-intensity acute exercise impacts motor learning in healthy older adults. npj Science of Learning 9:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00220-2

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Linear mixed model analysis of acquisition data from a randomised between-groups study of 24 adults aged 55–75 (12 rest, 11 exercise analysed) practicing the SVIPT. The article reports the three-way interaction: "there was a significant three-way interaction between Group, Block, and Age (F(1, 19.00) = 4.61,p = 0.04)". No effect size printed.
