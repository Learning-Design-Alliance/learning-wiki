## Evidence

### Erkan Gözde Nur 2026

Erkan Gözde Nur. (2026). Long-term impact of anesthesiologist-led active learning on dental students’ clinical autonomy in medical risk management and patient safety: a 12-month prospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09523-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A 12-month prospective cohort study of 192 fourth-year dental students (Group T n=100, Group AL n=92) assessed knowledge retention using a structured assessment tool with Mann–Whitney U and Chi-square tests. The article reports "significantly higher retention of theoretical knowledge" in the active learning group.

> "Students in the anesthesiologist-led active learning group demonstrated significantly higher retention of theoretical knowledge regarding medical history-taking, vital signs monitoring, and patient-specific maximum safe local anesthesia dosing."
