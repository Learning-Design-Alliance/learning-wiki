## Subclaims
`q2 i3` URM status correlated with higher ESC at selective institutions (β = 0.57, p < 0.001), but ESC did not significantly mediate the URM performance gap anywhere (all p ≥ 0.26). [→ Salehi S. 2021](#salehi-s-2021)

SEM mediation analysis found "ESC was not a significant mediator for the effect of URM status on exam scores" at all three institution types; the accompanying selective-institution correlation (β = 0.57 ± 0.13, p < 0.001) is reported in the same results paragraph.

> "ESC was not a significant mediator for the effect of URM status on exam scores for all the three institution types (βCC = 0.01 ± 0.01, p = 0.41; βinclusive = −0.01 ± 0.01, p = 0.26; βselective = −0.008 ± 0.02, p = 0.72). However, the reason for the lack of mediation effect varied by institution type."
