## Subclaims
`q2 i?` PCPs who had never participated in diabetes training had significantly poorer diabetes knowledge than those who had (OR 6.5; P 0.027). [→ Ugwwu Ejiofor 2020](#ugwwu-ejiofor-2020)

## Evidence

### Ugwwu Ejiofor 2020

Ugwwu Ejiofor, Young Ekenechukwu, Nkpozi Marcellinus. (2020). Diabetes care knowledge and practice among primary care physicians in Southeast Nigeria: a cross-sectional study. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-020-01202-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression on questionnaire data from 64 PCPs in a cross-sectional study identified non-participation in diabetes training as a significant predictor of poor knowledge, with "non participation in diabetes training (OR 6.5; P 0.027)".

> "Duration of medical practice more than 10 years (OR 10.1; P 0.034) and non participation in diabetes training (OR 6.5; P 0.027) were significant predictors of poor diabetes knowledge in the study population (Table 3)."
