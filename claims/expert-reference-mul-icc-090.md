### Goedegebuure E.P. 2026

Goedegebuure E.P., Boellaard T.N., van Dijk-de Haan M.C., et al. (2026). Improving membranous urethral length measurements on prostate MRI: a comparison of online training methods. European Radiology 36(8):6419-6427. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00330-026-12468-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ICC analysis of MUL measurements by two independent faculty members on the 20 test cases. 95% limits of interobserver agreement were −4.4 mm and +5.1 mm. A Bland–Altman plot is provided in Supplement 2.

> "Agreement between the two experts (whose average measurements served as the reference standard) was excellent, with an ICC of 0.90 (95% CI 0.76–0.96) and a mean (SD) difference of 0.3 (±2.4) mm."
