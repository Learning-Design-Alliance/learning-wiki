Derso 2026¶

Derso, Y. A., Adisu, M. A., Kitaw, T. A., Dadi, H. H., Beriso, H. B., Abebe, W., Engdaw, H. T., Tigabu, D., Abiye, M. M., Dessie, G. A., Yetwale, H. A. (2026). q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects pooling of four studies on encouragement from clinical staff; the printed effect size is an odds ratio, AOR = 3.95 with a 95% CI (1.04–6.86), with moderate heterogeneity; Egger p = 0.1207 and Begg p = 0.7341 indicated no publication bias.

"The forest plot result of the studies showed that the overall effect size was 3.95, with a 95% CI (1.04–6.86). The heterogeneity measures, including I 2 = 37.32% and p value = 0.25, suggest that presence of moderate variability"
