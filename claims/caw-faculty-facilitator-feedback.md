### O'Neil Jessica 2018

O'Neil Jessica, Croniger Colleen. (2018). Critical Appraisal Worksheets for Integration Into an Existing Small-Group Problem-Based Learning Curriculum. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10682

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Voluntary feedback survey of 15 faculty facilitators comparing the CAW intervention to the prior PBL EBM activity. The article also reports 80% (n = 12) found CAWs more effective at applying statistical knowledge and only 7% (n = 1) found the exercise burdensome.

> "Ninety-three percent (n = 14) agreed or strongly agreed that the CAW encouraged students to use a structured approach to the critical appraisal of health science literature and that CAWs were more effective in doing this compared to the previously used PBL EBM activity."
