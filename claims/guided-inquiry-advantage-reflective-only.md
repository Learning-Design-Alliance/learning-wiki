# Guided inquiry's advantage over conventional teaching holds for reflective but not impulsive students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2019 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among reflective students, guided inquiry significantly outperformed conventional teaching (t = 10.55, p<0.05; means 26.54 vs 21.91). [→ Margunayasa 2019](#margunayasa-2019)
`q3 i?` Among impulsive students, there was no significant difference between guided inquiry and conventional teaching (t = 0.33, p>0.74); equivalence was not tested. [→ Margunayasa 2019 (2)](#margunayasa-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Margunayasa 2019

Margunayasa, I G., Dantes, N., Marhaeni, A.A.I.N., & Suastra, I W. (2019). The Effect of Guided Inquiry Learning and Cognitive Style on Science Learning Achievement. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 737-750. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple-effect t-test following the significant interaction, in reflective students of the same quasi-experiment; guided inquiry mean was 26.54 vs conventional 21.91. The article reports "t = 10.55 and p<0.05".

> "there is a significant difference in science learning achieve ment between the students who learned through guided inquiry learning model and those  who learned through conventional teaching model in those with reflective cognitive style (t = 10.55 and p<0.05)"

### Margunayasa 2019 (2)

Margunayasa, I G., Dantes, N., Marhaeni, A.A.I.N., & Suastra, I W. (2019). The Effect of Guided Inquiry Learning and Cognitive Style on Science Learning Achievement. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 737-750. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple-effect t-test in impulsive students of the same quasi-experiment; guided inquiry mean was 21.66, slightly below conventional 22.83, and the difference was not significant. No effect size was printed.

> "there is no significant difference in science learning achievement between the students who learned through guided learning model and those who learned through conventional teaching model and those with impulsive cognitive style (t = 0.33 and p>0.74)"
