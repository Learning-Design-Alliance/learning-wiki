## Evidence

### Koedinger 2008

Koedinger, K., Aleven, V., Roll, I., & Baker, R. (2008). In vivo experiments on whether supporting metacognition in intelligent tutoring systems yields robust learning. https://learnlab.org/

`q2 · i3`

Correlational analysis of student-tutor log data across Cognitive Tutor units, indicating the help-seeking model captures behavior independent of specific content or student group (r = 0.89). The authors note related results were "not entirely stable across data sets".

> "the correlation between students' frequency of different types of help-seeking errors in an angles unit (in geometry) and a scatterplot unit (in data analysis) was 0.89."
