## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In Study 1, a repeated measures ANCOVA on pre/post video noticing codes found a significant time by subject-focus interaction (F(1,15) = 14.01, p < .01, ηp² = 0.48), with no significant main effect of time. [→ Blair 2024](#blair-2024)

## Evidence

### Blair 2024

Blair, K. P., Banes, L. C., & Martin, L. (2024). Fostering noticing of classroom discussion features through analysis of contrasting cases. Instructional Science, 52:417–452. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09661-z

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Study 1 repeated measures ANCOVA with within-subject factors time, subject-focus, and valence, on 18 preservice teachers' pre/post video noticing reflections. The article reports "a significant interaction of time by subject-focus" with ηp² = 0.48; video clip order was a covariate.

> "There was no significant main effect of time (F(1,15) = 0.37, p > .5), but there was a significant interaction of time by subject-focus ( F(1,15) = 14.01, p < .01"
