Hulsey 2015¶

Hulsey, L., Esposito, A. M., Boller, K., & Osborn, S. (2015). Promise Neighborhoods Case Studies (Issue Brief). Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/promise-neighborhoods-case-studies-issue-brief

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Descriptive multi-site case study of five Promise Neighborhoods grantees conducted in 2014-2015 for the Promise Neighborhoods Institute at PolicyLink. The study documented "how the grantees designed and implemented their programs" and the early successes and challenges experienced during early implementation. No outcome effect sizes are reported.

"Findings from case studies conducted of five sites revealed how the grantees designed and implemented their programs, the services they chose to offer, and the early successes and challenges they had in improving the lives of children in their community."
