Analytical worked solution of the historical leakage problem: with r = 6 Ω per knot, ℓ = 360 knots, c = 970 Ω, b = 1040 Ω, giving a = 2160 Ω, equation (17) yields "x ≈ 114 knots or, approximately,211 km from the English end".

> "Heaviside data were r = 6 Ω per knot (in the context,1 knot = 1 nautical mile = 1852 m), ℓ = 360 knots, c = 970 Ω and b = 1040 Ω. It follows that a = 2160 Ω. Taking these values into equation (17), we see that the physically acceptable solution isx ≈ 114 knots or, approximately,211 km from the English end."
