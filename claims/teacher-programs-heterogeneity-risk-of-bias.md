## Evidence

### Joanne R. Beames 2023

Joanne R. Beames, Samantha Spanos, Anna Roberts, Lauren McGillivray, Sophie Li, Jill M. Newby, Bridianne O'Dea, Aliza Werner-Seidler. (2023). Intervention Programs Targeting the Mental Health, Professional Burnout, and/or Wellbeing of School Teachers: Systematic Review and Meta-Analyses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09720-w

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

I-squared heterogeneity statistics computed across the RCT meta-analyses at post-intervention. The authors attribute high heterogeneity to the cross-disciplinary, emerging nature of the field and varied programs, designs, and measures.

> "Based on 12 statistics (see Table S8 in the Supplementary Material), there was high heterogeneity in the controlled effects at post-intervention for stress, psychological distress, and professional burnout. There was low heterogeneity for anxiety and depression, and moderate heterogeneity for wellbeing."

### Joanne R. Beames 2023 (2)

Joanne R. Beames, Samantha Spanos, Anna Roberts, Lauren McGillivray, Sophie Li, Jill M. Newby, Bridianne O'Dea, Aliza Werner-Seidler. (2023). Intervention Programs Targeting the Mental Health, Professional Burnout, and/or Wellbeing of School Teachers: Systematic Review and Meta-Analyses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09720-w

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

ROB-II assessment of the RCTs found bias mainly from unblinded participants and absent pre-specified analysis plans; ROBINS-I rated 87.5% of nRCTs at serious risk of bias.

> "Roughly half of the RCTs were assessed to have some concerns (n=26, 53.06%) and the other half were assessed to have high risk of bias ( n=23, 49.94%)."
