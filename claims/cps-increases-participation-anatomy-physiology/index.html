Evidence¶

Termos 2013¶

Termos, M. H. (2013). The Effects of the Classroom Performance System on Student Participation, Attendance, and Achievement. International Journal of Teaching and Learning in Higher Education, 25(1), 66-78. http://www.isetl.org/ijtlhe/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison over 12 weeks of lectures at South Texas College: four CPS sections (P02, P03, P05, P18) versus one control section, with participation measured by counting used clickers after every lecture. The article reports "the difference in participation was 20% to 25% higher in the experimental sections."

"The four CPS experimental sections had high percentages of participation, which did not go below 86.8%, while, in the control group, participation did not exceed 68% as shown in Figure 1."
