Foster 2016¶

Descriptive analysis of an eight-question Likert-type satisfaction inventory completed by 126 subjects; the article reports only descriptive statistics (means and standard deviations per item), no inferential test.

"Overall, on a scale of 8–32 points, the mean score for subject satisfaction with the training was 25.62 (Sn-1 = 3.25). Each subject’s ratings were averaged across the eight questions. The overall mean for all subjects was 3.2."
