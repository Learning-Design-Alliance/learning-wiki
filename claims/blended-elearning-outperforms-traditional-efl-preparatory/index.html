Evidence¶

Intakhab Alam Khan 2016¶

Intakhab Alam Khan. (2016). Effectiveness of E-learning for the Teaching of English: A Study of Comparative Strategies. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 7(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.3p.125

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment at KAU Community College: two parallel classes taught by routine method versus e-learning mode for 8 weeks, then tested with a self-developed achievement test covering listening, reading, grammar and vocabulary. The printed means were 3.66 (control) and 4.48 (experimental), described as "a difference in mean values of the two groups".

"The mean value of controlled group is 3.66, but the mean value of experimental is 4.48 which show that there is a difference in mean values of the two groups of the sample used for the study."
