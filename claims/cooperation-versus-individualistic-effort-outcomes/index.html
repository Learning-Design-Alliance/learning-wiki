The project report's rationale section asserts this as established by research on cooperative learning, without citing specific studies or reporting any data from the project itself. The claim is the report's own summary assertion.

"From the research on cooperative learning, we know that cooperation typically results in higher achievement, more positive relationships, and greater self-esteem. All of these outcomes are related to retention."
