# Five persistence definition variants show similar predictiveness of completion, but the third-semester point-in-time definition yields persistence rates up to 14 percentage points higher than the continuous four-term definition

> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among bachelor's degree enrollees, 76% of 2019 graduates were enrolled in the third semester while 66% remained continuously enrolled through semester four, a 10 percentage point difference. [→ Mahaffie 2024](#mahaffie-2024)
`q2 i?` Among community college enrollees, 52% were enrolled at the third semester versus 38% continuously enrolled through semester four, a 14 percentage point difference. [→ Mahaffie 2024 (2)](#mahaffie-2024-2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison (Figure 6) of persistence rates among CPS graduates enrolled in bachelor's degree granting institutions under the existing continuous four-term definition and the proposed third-semester enrollment definition, showing a 10 percentage point difference for the 2019 cohort.

> "76% of 2019 bachelor’s degree enrollees were still enrolled in college in the third semester, while 66% remained continuously enrolled through semester four."

### Mahaffie 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison (Figure 9) of persistence rates among CPS graduates enrolled in community colleges under the third-semester and continuous four-term definitions, showing a larger definitional gap than for bachelor's degree enrollees.

> "52% of community college enrollees enrolled in college at the third semester and only 38% remaining continuously enrolled through semester four (a difference of 14 percentage points; see Figure 9)."

### Mahaffie 2024 (3)

Mahaffie, S., Nagaoka, J., & Usher, A. (2024). Measuring Patterns of Immediate and Continued College Enrollment among CPS Graduates. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research and the To&Through Project. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/measuring-patterns-of-immediate-and-continued-college-enrollment-among-CPSs-graduates

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative analysis of five persistence definition variants (Table 1) for CPS graduates, assessing predictiveness of four-year and six-year completion. The definitions showed "relatively similar levels of predictiveness", so the definition choice rested on timeliness, clarity, and alignment rather than predictive advantage.

> "All five definitions resulted in indicators with relatively similar levels of predictiveness of four -year and six-year completion for bachelor’s degree granting institution enrollees, and rates calculated using all five definitions showed similar trends over time."
