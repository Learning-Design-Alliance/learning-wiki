Multi-dimensional annotation quality analysis (Table 1) of 10,845 dataset entries across category, difficulty, and Bloom level dimensions. The analysis reports "97% effective uti- lization (5.84/6 levels)" for Bloom levels with concentration index 0.011, and 91% utilization across four difficulty levels.
