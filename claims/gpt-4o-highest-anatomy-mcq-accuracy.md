## Evidence

### Bolgova Olena 2025

Bolgova Olena, Mavrych Volodymyr (2025). Evolution of AI in anatomy education study based on comparison of current large language models against historical ChatGPT performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-22437-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Results of the three-attempt benchmark over 325 MCQs, reporting mean accuracy per model with standard deviations as printed. GPT-4o leads with "92.9 ± 2.5%" correct answers.

> "The best results were shown by GPT-4o (92.9 ± 2.5%), followed by Claude (76.7 ± 5.7%), Copilot (73.9 ± 11.9%), and Gemini (63.7.5 ± 6.5%)."

### Bolgova Olena 2025 (2)

Bolgova Olena, Mavrych Volodymyr (2025). Evolution of AI in anatomy education study based on comparison of current large language models against historical ChatGPT performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-22437-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pearson chi-square pairwise comparisons between chatbots (Table 1), e.g. GPT-4o vs. Gemini χ2 = 150.53; the article reports all pairwise differences as highly statistically significant.

> "All p-values were extremely small (much smaller than 0.05 or even 0.001), indicating that the performance differences between all model pairs are highly statistically significant."
