# Girls in Playworks schools had significantly higher accelerometer intensity counts and spent more time in vigorous physical activity than girls in control schools

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a 29-school randomized trial, girls in Playworks schools had significantly higher accelerometer intensity counts and more vigorous physical activity than girls in control schools. [→ Martha Bleeker 2015](#martha-bleeker-2015)

## Evidence

### Martha Bleeker 2015

Martha Bleeker, Nicholas Beyler, Susanne James-Burdumy, Jane Fortson. (2015). The Impact of Playworks on Boys' and Girls' Physical Activity During Recess. Journal of School Health, vol. 85, no. 3. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-impact-of-playworks-on-boys-and-girls-physical-activity-during-recess

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized evaluation in which 29 schools were assigned to Playworks or control for one school year; postintervention activity was measured with accelerometers and impacts estimated separately by gender using regression models. The article reports a statistically significant increase for girls with no printed effect size.

> "Girls in Playworks schools had significantly higher accelerometer intensity counts and spent more time in vigorous physical activity than girls in control schools."
