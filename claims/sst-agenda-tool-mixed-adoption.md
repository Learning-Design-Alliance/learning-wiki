`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case-study report of the district rollout of the AI-enabled SST agenda tool in November 2025. The study states "school staff usage of the tool across grade levels was mixed" and later that "overall usage analytics fell short of expectations," while meetings co-facilitated by the SEL/MTSS Coordinator showed enthusiasm.

> "The SST agenda development tool was available for district-wide use in November 2025. Despite district leadership excitement at the outset, school staff usage of the tool across grade levels was mixed."
