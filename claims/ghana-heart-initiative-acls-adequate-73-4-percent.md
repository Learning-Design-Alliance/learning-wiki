Cross-sectional, training-based assessment of 124 ACLS trainees immediately after training with a standardized questionnaire and skills drills per 2018 and 2020 AHA guidelines; 73.4% adequate. No effect size printed.

> "The majority (91/124, 73.4%) showed adequate knowledge and skills in ACLS. Statistically significant differences in ACLS knowledge and skills scores were observed based on gender, profession, and facility (P<.05; see Table 3)."
