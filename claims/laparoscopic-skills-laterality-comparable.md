`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Numerical comparison from the laparoscopy simulator phase of the study, in which all students trained three skills for 3 h. The peg-transfer comparison favored LH students descriptively but was "p- = 0.825". Clipping (p = 0.288) and cutting (p = 0.20) were likewise statistically insignificant.

> "The total Peg transfer time was shorter among LH medical students as compared to RH medical students [129.85 ± 80.873 s) vs. (135.52 ± 104.815 s), (CI –56.792–45.459) p- = 0.825]"
