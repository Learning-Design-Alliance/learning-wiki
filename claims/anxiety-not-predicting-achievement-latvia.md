## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In two-point cross-sectional fully adjusted multiple linear regression models, no association was observed between anxiety level and achievement in any subject for semester or final state exam. [→ Zile Inta 2021](#zile-inta-2021)

## Evidence

### Zile Inta 2021

Zile Inta, Bite Ieva, Krumina Indra, Folkmanis Valdis, Tzivian Lilian. (2021). Association between Anxiety, Quality of Life and Academic Performance of the Final-Year-Students in Latvia. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18115784

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis in the same 287-student cohort; anxiety coefficients were non-significant in all models (e.g., final mathematics exam β = 0.05 [−0.02; 0.12], p = 0.16). Sensitivity analysis with dichotomized anxiety did not change predictor significance. No standardised effect size printed.

> "In two-point cross-sectional analysis using fully adjusted multiple linear regression models, we did not observe any association between the level of anxiety and studentsŌĆÖ academic achievement in subjects considered either for the semester or for the final state exam."
