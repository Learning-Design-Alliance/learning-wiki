`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of the 11-surgeon simulation plotting each participant's SD of time-points against years of surgical experience (Fig. 3): SD correlated negatively with experience (right hand "r=-0.248"; left hand "r=-0.491"), and the most experienced surgeon showed no right-left SD gap.

> "The SD was also larger in less-experienced participants (right hand, r=-0.248; left hand, r=-0.491). Participants with 24 years of surgical experience did not have any appreciable difference in the SD of the time-points between the right and left hands."
