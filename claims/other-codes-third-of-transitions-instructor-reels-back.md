## Evidence

### Deepa N. Chari 2019

Deepa N. Chari, Hai D. Nguyen, Dean A. Zollman, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2019). Student and instructor framing in upper-division physics. [doi:10.1119/1.5120392](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5120392)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of the subset of "other" codes (administrative activities or socializing) in the transition matrices from the 50 episodes. The authors interpret this as students being off-topic, possibly because they are stuck, and the instructor reeling them back in with new ideas. Conversely, her off-topic responses tend to finish episodes more often than not.

> "Overall, they are involved in about a third of all transitions as either the preceding frame, the following frame, or both. We notice that when the students are engaged in an other activity, the instructor's most common response is to respond in a frame related to problem solving (ﬁrst row, ﬁgure 9)."
