q2 · i? · causal · r1

Exploratory topic-wise paired analyses (Table 5), 10 items per topic, Holm-adjusted across six topics per centre. The quoted sentence prints percentage gains and p-values but no effect size, so impact is uncoded. At Centre 1 the largest improvements were HIV (12.9%) and HTN (8.8%).

"At Centre 2, improvements were seen across most topics (e.g., CVD 16.1%, Holm-adjusted p = 0.022; HIV 15.8%, Holm-adjusted p = 0.003), while IDA showed no meaningful change (0.3%, Holm-adjusted p = 0.947)."
