Carlos González, Daniel Ponce, Violeta Fernández. (2023). Teachers' experiences of teaching online during COVID-19: implications for postpandemic professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10200-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Results-section interview findings on assessment: the study reports rote-memorization exams were "no longer feasible" online; the passage continues that several teachers became aware of the need for change and deployed practices resembling authentic assessment, while others complained of open cheating.

> "In a situation in which it was impossible to control the materials students had at hand while completing exams, traditional exams focused on rote memorization, which had previously been widely used, were no longer feasible."
