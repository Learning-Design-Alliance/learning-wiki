## Evidence

### Fuchs et al. 2016

Lynn S. Fuchs, Robin F. Schumacher, Jessica Long, Jessica Namkung, Amelia S. Malone, Amber Wang, Carol L. Hamlett, Nancy C. Jordan, Robert S. Siegler, and Paul Changas. (2016). Effects of Intervention to Improve At-Risk Fourth Graders' Understanding, Calculations, and Word Problems with Fractions. Elementary School Journal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566704

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVAs on each fraction outcome in a randomized trial of 213 at-risk fourth graders assigned to M-WP intervention, A-WP intervention, or control. The article reports "Posttest scores on number line, NAEP, and calculations, controlled for pretest scores, were stronger for each of the two intervention conditions compared to the control group," with printed effect sizes in the medium-to-large range: ES = 0.44 to 1.22 favoring M-WP over control and ES = 0.33 to 1.70 favoring A-WP over control.

> "Posttest scores on number line, NAEP, and calculations, controlled for pretest scores, were stronger for each of the two intervention conditions compared to the control group. Effects sizes (ESs; difference between adjusted means, divided by the pooled unadjusted SD) favored M-WP over control group and ranged from 0.44 to 1.22; ESs favoring A-WP over control ranged from 0.33 to 1.70."
