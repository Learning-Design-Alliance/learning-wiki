## Evidence

### Matthew Richard 2022

Matthew Richard, Jennifer Delgado, Sarah LeGresley, Christopher J. Fischer. (2022). Implementing Competency-Based Grading Improves the Performance of Women and First Generation Students in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.10574](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.10574)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Grade-distribution analysis of 727 enrolled students across Spring 2015–Spring 2019 (Figure 1, right panel). The article prints these percentages descriptively, with no statistical test or effect size. The authors note the A-fraction increase is consistent with Carroll's model predictions.

> "the fraction of students earning a grade of “A ” or “B” increased from 68% pre-ALPaCA to 75% post-ALPaCA, and the fraction of students earning a grade of “A ” increased from 30% pre-ALPaCA to 50% post-ALPaCA."
