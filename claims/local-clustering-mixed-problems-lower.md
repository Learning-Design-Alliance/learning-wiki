OLS regression model 2 (Table 1) on local clustering, a measure of network cohesion, with 77 observations and R2 = 0.112. The Class 2 coefficient was −0.174** in Table 1 (text reports −0.169), and the Class 3 coefficient was −0.008, non-significant.
