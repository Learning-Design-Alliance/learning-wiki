## Evidence

### Aaron Cochrane and C. Shawn Green 2021

Aaron Cochrane and C. Shawn Green. (2021). Trajectories of performance change indicate multiple dissociable links between working memory and fluid intelligence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00111-w

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of time-sensitive WM modeling in 87 participants. Rate of change related to Gf with a printed correlation of r = −0.30 (CI = [−0.49, −0.08], BFlog3 = 2.05); the same pattern held using Spearman ρ.

> "both rate of change on the WM task ( r(85) = −0.30, CI = [−0.49, −0.08], BFlog3 = 2.05) and ﬁnal accuracy ( r(85) = 0.33, CI = [0.11, 0.52], BF log3 = 2.99) showed evidence of relations to Gf"

### Aaron Cochrane and C. Shawn Green 2021 (2)

Aaron Cochrane and C. Shawn Green. (2021). Trajectories of performance change indicate multiple dissociable links between working memory and fluid intelligence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00111-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of the same 87-participant experiment confirmed the two links' independence: "each remained reliable when controlling for the other", with printed b and ΔR2 values. Results persisted under robust regression and with starting accuracy as covariate.

> "a regression model was run containing main effects for both predictors (rate of change and ﬁnal accuracy), and each remained reliable when controlling for the other (rate of change b = −0.111, CI = [−0.213, −0.008], ΔR2 = 0.046; ﬁnal accuracy b = 0.428, CI = [0.112,.745], ΔR2 = 0.073"
