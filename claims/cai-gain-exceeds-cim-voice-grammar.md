## Evidence

### R. Ramesh & B. Sumathi 2010

R. Ramesh & B. Sumathi. (2010). CAI in Learning Voice in English Grammar among Eighth Standard Students – An Experimental Study. li-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102768

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental within-and-between group pre-test/post-test study of 89 eighth-standard students randomly allotted to CIM (n = 43) and CAI (n = 46) groups; Table 5 reports mean gain scores of 4.7 (CIM) and 8.92 (CAI) with t = 4.58, "statistically significant at 0.01 level". No effect size is printed.

> "From Table 5 it is shown that there is a mean gain score difference in learning Voice in English Grammar among the students between the CIM and CAI group. Further, the mean score differences are also statistically significant at 0.01 level."
