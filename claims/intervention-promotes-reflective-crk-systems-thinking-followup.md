### Rosenkränzer 2016

Rosenkränzer, F., Kramer, T., Hörsch, C., Schuler, S., & Rieß, W. (2016). Promoting Student Teachers' Content Related Knowledge in Teaching Systems Thinking: Measuring Effects of an Intervention through Evaluating a Videotaped Lesson. Higher Education Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v6n4p156

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study with three treatment courses and a control group (108 student teachers), measuring reflective CRK via a videotaped lesson at three time points. The follow-up test showed "a significant improvemen t", F(3,103)=4.916, p=.003, η2=.125.

> "In follow up test M=1.67 (SD=1.14), a significant improvemen t in student teachers CRK for teaching systems thinking was measured, F (3,103)=4.916, p=.003, η2=.125."
