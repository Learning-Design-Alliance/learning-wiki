# SFA® shows positive effects on alphabetics for beginning readers in grades K–4

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across eight studies meeting WWC group design standards, SFA® had a positive and statistically significant effect on alphabetics outcomes in four studies, with no contrary evidence. [→ Success for All® 2017](#success-for-all-2017)

## Evidence

### Success for All® 2017

Success for All®. (2017). WWC Intervention Report: Beginning Reading. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/672

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The WWC systematic review's alphabetics domain synthesis, based on eight studies including 7,957 students in 137 schools. The review found the impact "positive and statistically significant for four studies" and no studies showed significant negative effects, yielding a positive-effects rating.

> "In the eight studies that reported findings, the estimated impact of the intervention on outcomes in the alphabetics domain was positive and statistically significant for four studies, two of which meet WWC group design standards without reservations."
