Evidence¶

Mutluhan Yilmaz 2026¶

Mutluhan Yilmaz, Andrea Gauthier, Mutlu Cukurova. (2026). Supporting online learners' regulation skills with the help of learning analytics and generative artificial intelligence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10595-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Precision, Recall and F1 were computed against LA-based standardised scores as ground truth for both feedback sources across five SRL pillars. In the SE positive category, "GenAI’s F1 score is remarkably higher than that of the tutor, with scores of 0.64 compared to 0.22". Tutor feedback frequently omitted aspects: 27 positive pieces overlooked help-seeking and 46 overlooked self-evaluation.

"Notably, in the SE positive feedback category, GenAI’s F1 score is remarkably higher than that of the tutor, with scores of 0.64 compared to 0.22."
