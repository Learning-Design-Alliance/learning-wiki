## Evidence

### Ying Xu 2023

Ying Xu, Kunlei He, Julian Levine, Daniel Ritchie, Zexuan Pan, Andres Bustamante, and Mark Warschauer. (2023). Artificial Intelligence Enhances Children's Science Learning from Television Shows. https://osf.io/j7upd

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Multi-level logistic regression on the 156 children in interactive (n = 79) and pseudo-interactive (n = 77) conditions; printed odds ratios are OR = 1.026 and OR = 0.96, both negligible in magnitude. The article concludes "contingent interaction led to children steadily increasing their verbal engagement, whereas pseudo-interaction led to a steady decrease in engagement" (Figure 4).

> "A multi-level logistic regression analysis revealed that in the interactive condition, for each additional question, the odds of a child verbally responding increased by 2.6% (OR = 1.026, p = .01). In contrast, in the pseudo-interactive condition, for each additional question the odds of a child responding decreased by 1.4% (OR = 0.96, p = .005)"
