Evidence¶

Wisniewski B 2020¶

Wisniewski B, Zierer K and Hattie J (2020) The Power of Feedback Revisited: A Meta-Analysis of Educational Feedback Research. Front. Psychol. 10:3087. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Mixed-effects moderator analysis by research design (Table 3). Pre-post studies (k = 244) average d = 0.63 [0.56–0.69] versus controlled studies (k = 713) d = 0.42 [0.37–0.46]; the authors note pre-post designs are "likely to be inﬂuenced by confounding variables".

"Controlled study 713 0.42 [0.37 – 0.46] 3321.86 78.6% Pre-post study 244 0.63 [0.56 – 0.69] 2317.88 89.5%"

Mixed-effects moderator analysis by publication type (Table 3). Journal articles (k = 843) average d = 0.49 versus dissertations (k = 116) d = 0.36; the authors note "Feedback eﬀects seem to be less likely to be published when they are low or even negative."

"Journal article 843 0.49 [0.45 – 0.53] 5176.67 83.7% Dissertation 116 0.36 [0.25 – 0.46] 522.72 78.0%"
