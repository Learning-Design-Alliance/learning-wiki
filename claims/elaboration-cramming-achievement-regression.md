## Evidence

### Rebecca Hey · Mark McDaniel · Flaviu A. Hodis 2024

Rebecca Hey · Mark McDaniel · Flaviu A. Hodis. (2024). How undergraduate students learn: uncovering interrelationships between factors that support self-regulated learning and strategy use. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09389-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression of self-reported achievement on rehearsal, elaboration, spacing, cramming, and self-testing (Table 6) in the NZ undergraduate survey; the article reports elaboration (Beta = 0.24) and cramming (Beta = -0.30) as the significant predictors, R2 = .18, and states "for every one standard deviation increase in elaboration, there is a 0.24 standard deviation increase in achievement".

> "These results suggest that (all else being the same) for every one standard deviation increase in elaboration, there is a 0.24 standard deviation increase in achievement."
