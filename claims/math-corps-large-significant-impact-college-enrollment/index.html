Barbara Harris 2022¶

Barbara Harris, Lindsay Fox. (2022). Participation in Math Corps Increases College Enrollment. Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/participation-in-math-corps-increases-college-enrollment

q2 · i? · causal · r2

A quasi-experimental retrospective analysis by Mathematica of long-term outcomes for students who attended Math Corps from 2004 to 2009 found "a large and statistically significant impact on college enrollment." No effect size is printed in the brief.
