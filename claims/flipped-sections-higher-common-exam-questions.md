## Evidence

### Riedl Ann 2021

Riedl Ann, Yeung Fan, Burke Tina. (2021). Implementation of a Flipped Active-Learning Approach in a Community College General Biology Course Improves Student Performance in Subsequent Biology Courses and Increases Graduation Rate. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-07-0156

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 16 flipped vs. 17 non-flipped sections of General Biology I (n = 378 vs. 410) over two semesters, assessed by percent correct on common multiple-choice questions. The article reports "students from flipped sections scored significantly higher" on common unit and final exam questions.

> "students from flipped sections scored significantly higher on the five common exam questions per chapter for units 2 through 5 and on the 50 common comprehensive final exam questions"

### Riedl Ann 2021 (2)

Riedl Ann, Yeung Fan, Burke Tina. (2021). Implementation of a Flipped Active-Learning Approach in a Community College General Biology Course Improves Student Performance in Subsequent Biology Courses and Increases Graduation Rate. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-07-0156

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Factorial analysis of variance of common unit exam questions across 33 sections, with factors of type of instruction, exam, and semester. The article reports a significant section-by-unit interaction, F(4, 468) = 5.53, p = 0.00023, alongside significant main effects for teaching method and unit.

> "Significant interaction effects between section and unit were also observed, F(4, 468) = 5.53, p = 0.00023."
