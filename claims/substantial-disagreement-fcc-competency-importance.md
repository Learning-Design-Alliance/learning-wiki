Quantitative post hoc analysis of the Q-sort data, comparing explained variance across subgroups defined by country (Australia vs Netherlands) and professional group (nurses, lecturers, researchers, policy makers), including leave-one-out analyses. The article reports no subgroup's two factors exceeded 29% explained variance.

> "In none of the subgroups the explained variance of two factors exceeded 29%, suggesting general lack of agreement on importance of competencies between and within our groups of professionals and countries."
