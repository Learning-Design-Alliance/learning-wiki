## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a cross-sectional assessment of 98 proctology videos, peer-reviewed videos achieved a median total score of 16.0 versus 10 for non-peer-reviewed videos, a statistically significant difference. [→ Mirdha S 2025](#mirdha-s-2025)

## Evidence

### Mirdha S 2025

Mirdha S, Tokidis E, Le Blevec L, Wilson T. (2025). Should online, free proctology videos be used for self‐directed post‐graduate learning? A proposed evaluation using a colorectal video assessment framework. Colorectal Disease. https://doi.org/10.1111/codi.70313

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational cross-sectional study of 98 proctology videos (65 peer-reviewed, 33 not) scored by two researchers on a 9-item tool adapted from LAP-VEGaS. "This difference was statistically significant (Mann–Whitney U = 2024.0, p < 0.001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The median total score for peer‐reviewed videos was 16.0 [interquartile range (IQR) 13.0–17.0] compared to 10 (IQR 8.0–12.0) for the non‐peer‐reviewed videos. This difference was statistically significant (Mann–Whitney U = 2024.0, p < 0.001)."
