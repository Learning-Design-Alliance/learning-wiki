## Evidence

### Clements 2013

Clements, D. H., Sarama, J., Wolfe, C. B., & Spitler, M. E. (2013). Longitudinal Evaluation of a Scale-Up Model for Teaching Mathematics With Trajectories and Technologies: Persistence of Effects in the Third Year. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831212469270

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null moderator result from the intent-to-treat HLM models (N = 1,079); the article reports no statistically significant gender-by-treatment interaction and no effect size for this null finding.

> "Gender was not found to be a significant moderator within any of these comparisons. That is, within the ITT sample, treatment was found to be equally effective for children identifying with either reported gender."
