# No significant group differences in exploration diversity or ease: POIs visited and 2D map openings did not differ between GNSS and RTK groups

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The number of POIs visited did not differ significantly between the GNSS and RTK groups. [→ J. Mercier 2023](#j-mercier-2023)

## Evidence

### J. Mercier 2023

J. Mercier, N. Chabloz, G. Dozot, C. Audrin, O. Ertz, E. Bocher, D. Rappo. (2023). Impact of Geolocation Data on Augmented Reality Usability: A Comparative User Test. [doi:10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploration diversity indicator from in-app user-triggered events, normalized for 15 minutes. No statistically significant difference between groups; equivalence was not tested.

> "The GNSS group visited an average of M = 21.09 (SD = 4.02) POIs, whereas the RTK group visited an average of M = 19.29 (SD = 5.87). The results do not show any significant difference (t(51) = 1.30, p = 0.199)."

### J. Mercier 2023 (2)

J. Mercier, N. Chabloz, G. Dozot, C. Audrin, O. Ertz, E. Bocher, D. Rappo. (2023). Impact of Geolocation Data on Augmented Reality Usability: A Comparative User Test. [doi:10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploration ease indicator from logged map-open events. Not significant; the authors suggest the RTK group could have had more ease exploring given its lower average map use.

> "The GNSS group opened the 2D map M = 2.83 (SD = 2.24) times in average, whereas the RTK group opened it M = 1.91 (SD = 2.41) times. The results do not show any significant difference (t(51) = 1.44, p = 0.157)."
