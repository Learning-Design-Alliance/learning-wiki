## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Intraindividual correlations over six weeks of daily measurements (N = 72) revealed that for many emotions relations were homogeneous across persons, but daily anxiety related differently to motivation in different students, positively for some and negatively for others. ## Evidence

### Reinhard Pekrun 2002

Reinhard Pekrun, Thomas Goetz, Wolfram Titz, & Raymond P. Perry. (2002). Academic Emotions in Students' Self-Regulated Learning and Achievement: A Program of Qualitative and Quantitative Research. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326985ep3702_4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Diary study with daily measurements over six weeks before and during final university exams. The distribution of intraindividual anxiety-motivation correlations (Figure 1) centered near r = -.01, spanning negative and positive values across individuals.

> "we used data from our diary study (Pekrun & Hofmann, 1999) on exam-related emotions to conduct idiographic analyses by calculating intraindividual correlations within individual students over time (N = 72 students, daily measurements over 6 weeks before and during final university exams)."
