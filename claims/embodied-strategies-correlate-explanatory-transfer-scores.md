## Evidence

### Polly K. Lai 2026

Polly K. Lai. (2026). Enhancing learning of nanoscale phenomena: the role of agent-based models and embodied reasoning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09776-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of reasoning strategies against post-test scores (Table 8) in the same 27-student study; "generating causal inferences was positively correlated with higher post-test explanatory knowledge scores (r =.447, p =.019)" and retrieval was negatively correlated. No strong correlations with declarative scores were found.

> "Additionally, generating causal inferences was positively correlated with higher post-test explanatory knowledge scores (r =.447, p =.019), while relying on information retrieval was strongly negatively correlated with scores (r = -.467, p =.014)."

### Polly K. Lai 2026 (2)

Polly K. Lai. (2026). Enhancing learning of nanoscale phenomena: the role of agent-based models and embodied reasoning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09776-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of transfer-problem scores with strategy use showed "a strong positive correlation" for perspective shifting (r =.548, p =.003) and for gesture use (r =.538, p =.004); handwriting/drawing use was negatively linked to transfer scores (r = -.567, p =.002).

> "There was a strong positive correlation between students who used a first-to-third-person perspective to explain ideas and their scores on transfer problems (r =.548, p =.003)."
