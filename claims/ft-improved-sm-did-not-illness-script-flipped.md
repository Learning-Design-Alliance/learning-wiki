## Evidence

### Si Jihyun 2024

Si Jihyun. (2024). Fostering clinical reasoning ability in preclinical students through an illness script worksheet approach in flipped learning: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05614-9

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Dependent t-tests on the 36 students' pre- and post-DTI subscale scores. The article reports FT improved with "a moderate level of effect size" d = 0.63; SM showed no significant difference (t (35) = 0.81, p = .43, d = 0.13).

> "There were significant statistical differences between the pre- and post-assessment in the total DTI and FT, revealing a high level of effect size in the total DTI, t (35) = 5.89, p < .001, d = 0.98, and a moderate level of effect size in the FT, t (35) = 3.78, p = .001, d = 0.63."
