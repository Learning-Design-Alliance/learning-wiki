## Evidence

### Zhou Quan 2024

Zhou Quan, Gidziela Agnieszka, Allegrini Andrea G., Cheesman Rosa, Wertz Jasmin, Maxwell Jessye, Plomin Robert, Rimfeld Kaili, Malanchini Margherita. (2024). Gene-environment correlation: the role of family environment in academic development. Molecular Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-024-02716-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Structural equation modelling mediation analyses in the TEDS twin sample (5151 individuals) tested whether family environments mediated PGS prediction of achievement at ages 7, 9, 12 and 16. The study reports "The strongest indirect effects were found for SES", with a supportive home environment at age 9 (ß = 0.05) also substantial (~¼ of total prediction).

> "The strongest indirect effects were found for SES at age 7 (ß = 0.07 [95% CI, 0.06–0.08]) and age 16 (ß = 0.11 [95% CI, 0.09–0.13]), when SES mediated nearly 1/3 of the EA PGS prediction."
