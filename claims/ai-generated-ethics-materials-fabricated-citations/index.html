Evidence¶

Yusuf Pisan 2026¶

Yusuf Pisan. (2026). Teaching Intro AI When the Tools Can Do the Homework: A Course Redesign and a Student Bill of Rights. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05175 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.05175

q1 · i? · design · r2

The instructor's post-hoc verification of the three citations embedded in the Gemini-generated ethics exercises. He reports that a survey by Chan and Hu was cited for a finding it does not make, that a Washington OSPI H-AI-H framework does not exist in the actual guidance, and that a Cornell item could not be found.

"None of the three reproduces as cited. I treat this as an exhibit rather than an aside: a tool used to generate ethics materials about AI inserted references that range from misattributed to apparently fabricated, inside an exercise whose own subject was the reliability of AI in teaching."
