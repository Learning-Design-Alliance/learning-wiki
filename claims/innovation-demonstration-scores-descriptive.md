`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert-panel assessment: three nursing innovation experts independently scored each of the 10 group projects on a 10-dimension, 100-point score sheet (Cronbach's α = 0.912). The article states these descriptive scores characterise the presentations but do not establish course effectiveness.

> "The mean total score for the 10 group innovation project presentations was 80.57 ± 11.29 out of 100, equivalent to 80.57% of the maximum possible score (Table 6). Among the 10 assessment dimensions, teamwork had the highest percentage of the maximum score (90.00%)."
