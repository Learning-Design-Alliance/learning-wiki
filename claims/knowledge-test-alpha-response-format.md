# Open response format is associated with higher Alpha than multiple choice in knowledge tests

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Open response knowledge tests show higher meta-analytic Alpha (0.89) than multiple choice tests (0.79). [→ Peter A. Edelsbrunner 2025](#peter-a-edelsbrunner-2025)

## Evidence

### Peter A. Edelsbrunner 2025

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Bianca A. Simonsmeier, Michael Schneider. (2025). The Cronbach’s Alpha of Domain‑Specific Knowledge Tests Before and After Learning: A Meta‑Analysis of Published Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09982-y

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression from Table 2 controlling for log-centered test length. Open answer tests (Alpha = .89, 90% CI [.85, .92]) showed higher Alpha than multiple choice (Alpha = .79, 90% CI [.75, .83]), p = .001. Table 1 summarizes: "Open response highest."

> "Multiple choice 33 99 .79 [.75, .83] [.29, .94] ref 0.68 98.56 Open answer 19 99 .89 [.85, .92] [.61, .97] .001 0.70 98.25"
