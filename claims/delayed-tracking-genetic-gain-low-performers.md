## Evidence

### Antonie Knigge 2022

Antonie Knigge, Ineke Maas, Kim Stienstra, Eveline L. de Zeeuw and Dorret I. Boomsma. (2022). Delayed tracking and inequality of opportunity: Gene-environment interactions in educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 7:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00122-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 2 moderation analysis (N = 8847) plotting unique genetic, shared and non-shared environmental variances of attainment by CITO performance for each tracking group (Fig. 2). The article reports the genetic difference is "absent for high performers but much larger for low performers", contrary to H2a and in line with H2b.

> "The top and bottom left graph show that the difference in unique genetic effects on attainment between immediate and delayed tracking is absent for high performers but much larger for low performers."
