## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A small significant interaction of self-efficacy with both well-being measures on math scores (0.05–0.06 SD) appears without covariates but is not significant for reading and becomes insignificant when covariates are added. [→ Akhmedjanova Diana 2026](#akhmedjanova-diana-2026)

## Evidence

### Akhmedjanova Diana 2026

Akhmedjanova Diana, Kanonire Tatjana, Zakharov Andrey. (2026). Moderation analysis of subjective well-being, self-efficacy, and academic performance of 4th grade children in Russia. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0341318

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction terms added to OLS models of the whole sample (n = 1962). The article states that "When adding covariates, the interaction effect becomes insignificant across the models" for both math and reading.

> "With no control for demographic characteristics (Tables 2 and 3, Column 3), the results indicate a small statistically significant interaction effect with both affect toward school (0.06 SD, p < 0.05, ΔR2 = 0.003, S1) and satisfaction with school (0.05 SD, p < 0.05, ΔR2 = 0.003, S3), but only for math."
