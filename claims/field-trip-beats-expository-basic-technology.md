## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Amosa 2013

Amosa, A. G. A., Ogunlade, O. O., & Atobatele, A. S. (2013). Effect of Field Trip on Students' Academic Performance in Basic Technology in Ilorin Metropolis, Nigeria. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 3(2). https://www.mojet.net

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental, non-equivalent pre-test post-test control group study of 50 upper basic students in two Ilorin schools, randomly assigned to field trip (n=25) or expository (n=25) groups. Descriptive post-test means show "The mean score (45.040) of the students taught using field trip was higher" than the control mean of 35.240.

> "The mean score (45.040) of the students taught using field trip was higher than the mean score (35.240) of the students taught using expository method."

### Amosa 2013 (2)

Amosa, A. G. A., Ogunlade, O. O., & Atobatele, A. S. (2013). Effect of Field Trip on Students' Academic Performance in Basic Technology in Ilorin Metropolis, Nigeria. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 3(2). https://www.mojet.net

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on post-test scores of the experimental and control groups, as reported in Table 3 of the Results section. The article reports the value produced and rejects hypothesis one, though the printed F and significance figures are presented in an unconventional format.

> "From Table 3, it is shown that at 0.05 significant levels, the value produced F = (2,22)3.440 >0.109."
