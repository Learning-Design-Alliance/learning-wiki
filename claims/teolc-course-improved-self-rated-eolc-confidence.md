## Evidence

### Selman L 2016

Selman L, Robinson V, Klass L, Khan S, George R, Shepherd K, Burman R, Koffman J. (2016). Improving confidence and competence of healthcare professionals in end-of-life care: an evaluation of the 'Transforming End of Life Care' course at an acute hospital trust. BMJ Support Palliat Care. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjspcare-2015-000879

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-and-after service evaluation: 236 staff completed matched pre–post questionnaires; mean change scores and paired t tests with Bonferroni adjustment (p<0.0036) showed "Significant improvement (p<0.001) was identified in all self-assessment topics".

> "Significant improvement (p<0.001) was identified in all self-assessment topics, with the highest improvement seen in items related to Fast Track discharge (mean improvement 3.26–3.34)"
