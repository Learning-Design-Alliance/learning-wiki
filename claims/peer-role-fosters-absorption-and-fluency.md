## Evidence

### Zhengtao Xu 2026

Zhengtao Xu, Junti Zhang, Anthony Tang, and Yi-Chieh Lee. (2026). Who You Explain To Matters: Learning by Explaining to Conversational Agents with Different Pedagogical Roles. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790298

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on the FSS Learning Absorption subscale (α = .708) in the N=96 study. Post-hoc tests showed the Peer role fostered significantly deeper immersion than the Tutee role, and both Peer and Challenger exceeded Control.

> "For learning absorption, an ANOVA revealed a significant main effect,𝐹( 3, 92)= 6.86,𝑝<. 001. This corresponds to a large effect size (𝜂2𝑝 = 0.18)"

### Zhengtao Xu 2026 (2)

Zhengtao Xu, Junti Zhang, Anthony Tang, and Yi-Chieh Lee. (2026). Who You Explain To Matters: Learning by Explaining to Conversational Agents with Different Pedagogical Roles. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790298

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on the FSS Learning Fluency subscale (α = .882). Only a trend-level post-hoc effect (𝑝=.080) emerged between Peer and Tutee; Peer (𝑝=.005) and Challenger were significantly higher than Control.

> "For learning fluency, a significant main effect was found,𝐹( 3, 92)= 4.89,𝑝=. 003, with a large effect size (𝜂2𝑝 = 0.14)"
