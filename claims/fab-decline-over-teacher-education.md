### Asbury 2026

Asbury, K., Carstensen, B., & Klusmann, U. (2026). Changing perspectives: The development of preservice teachers' field‐specific ability beliefs across academic disciplines. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 337–355. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70019

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent growth modelling of four annual waves (2020–2023) from the StePs panel of 1734 preservice teachers showed "FABs changed over time, with five of the six subject groups showing a decline." The χ²-difference test (χ²(5) = 9.59, p = .088) indicated the slope model fit better than the intercept-only model.

> "FABs changed over time, with five of the six subject groups showing a decline. Mathematics‐intensive subjects and physical education exhibited the highest initial FAB levels."
