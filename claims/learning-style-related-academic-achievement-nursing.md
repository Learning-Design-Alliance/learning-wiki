## Evidence

### Shirazi 2019

Shirazi, F., & Heidari, S. (2019). The relationship between critical thinking skills and learning styles and academic achievement of nursing students. The Journal of Nursing Research, 27(4), e38. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000307

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey; Kolb's Learning Styles Inventory and previous-semester GPA. The study reports "a significant relationship between learning styles and academic achievement" (p < .001 per the abstract), with GPA ranked accommodating, diverging, converging, assimilating. No effect size printed.

> "There was a significant relationship between learning styles and academic achievement, with academic achievement (represented by GPA) highest in the accommodating learning style subgroup followed by the diverging, converging, and assimilating learning-style subgroups."
