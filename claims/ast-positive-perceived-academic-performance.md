## Evidence

### Jussila Juuso J 2023

Jussila Juuso J, Pulakka Anna, Halonen Jaana I, Salo Paula, Allaouat Sara, Mikkonen Santtu, Lanki Timo. (2023). Are active school transport and leisure-time physical activity associated with performance and wellbeing at secondary school? A population-based study. The European Journal of Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/eurpub/ckad128

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional logistic regression analysis of 34,103 Finnish adolescents from the 2015 School Health Promotion study, adjusted for sociodemographic, environmental, lifestyle covariates and leisure-time MVPA, found "the highest odds of high performance (OR 1.30, 95% CI 1.21–1.40)" for the 10–30 min active school transport group, with OR 1.26 for >30 min and OR 1.14 for <10 min.

> "Compared with non-active transportation, walking and cycling to school for 10–30 min a day was associated with the highest odds of high performance (OR 1.30, 95% CI 1.21–1.40), followed by more than 30 min (OR 1.26, 95% CI 1.14–1.40) and less than 10 min (OR 1.14, 95% CI 1.05–1.23)."
