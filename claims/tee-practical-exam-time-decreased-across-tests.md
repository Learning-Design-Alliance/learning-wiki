## Evidence

### Weber U 2019

Weber U, Zapletal B, Base E, Hambrusch M, Ristl R, Mora B, Schaller B. (2019). Resident performance in basic perioperative transesophageal echocardiography: Comparing 3 teaching methods in a randomized controlled trial. Medicine 98(36):e17072. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000017072

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive time-on-task measure reported across the four practical examinations in the randomized trial, within a 10-minute time limit per exam. The article interprets the shorter times as suggesting growing confidence; no inferential test or effect size is printed for this measure.

> "The average time to complete the practical examination decreased from the first practical test (6.54 ± 2.15 minutes) throughout the second (5.53 ± 1.86 minutes), the third exam (5.21 ± 2.03 minutes), and the follow up (5.11 ± 1.86 minutes) in all groups."
