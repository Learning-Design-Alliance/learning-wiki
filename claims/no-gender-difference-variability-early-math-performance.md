## Evidence

### Kersey 2018

Kersey, A. J., Braham, E. J., Csumitta, K. D., Libertus, M. E., & Cantlon, J. F. (2018). No intrinsic gender differences in children's earliest numerical abilities. npj Science of Learning, 3, 12. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0028-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Variance equality was tested across all tasks using Levene's test. In the infant numerosity sample, "Levene's test of Equality of Variances revealed no signiﬁcant differences in variance between girls and boys" (F(1, 78) < 0.01, p = .94). Similar null results held for all other tasks (e.g., TEMA-3: p = 0.99).
