# Online peer assessment activities account for about 12.8% of variance in project performance, with feedback activities adding 14.2% variance change

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' online collaboration and evaluation activities explained about 12.8% of project performance variance, and peer feedback activities added a significant 14.2% variance change beyond controls and grading. [→ Jingyan Lu 2012](#jingyan-lu-2012)

## Evidence

### Jingyan Lu 2012

Jingyan Lu, Nancy Law. (2012). Online peer assessment: effects of cognitive and affective feedback. Instructional Science 40:257–275. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-011-9177-2

`q2 · i?`

Hierarchical multiple regression (N = 181) with prior Humanities and Computer Literacy scores as controls (ΔR² = 0.037, p< 0.05). The article reports online activities "accounted for about 12.8% of project performance variance" and feedback added adjusted DR2 = .142, p\ .01.

> "The multiple squared correlation coefﬁcient was .128 indicating that students’ online collaboration and evaluation activities accounted for about 12.8% of project performance variance."
