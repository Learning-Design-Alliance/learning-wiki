### Matthew E. Brock and Erik W. Carter 2017

Matthew E. Brock and Erik W. Carter. (2017). A Meta-Analysis of Educator Training to Improve Implementation of Interventions for Students With Disabilities. Remedial and Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0741932516653477

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-regression model across all 12 group-design studies testing performance feedback as a hypothesized moderator. It was "not a significant predictor of effect size" (p = .14) but explained R2 = 19.53% of random variance.

> "Performance feedback alone was not a significant predictor of effect size, β = .68, t(10) = 1.62, p = .14, although it did explain random variance in the model (R2 = 19.53%)."
