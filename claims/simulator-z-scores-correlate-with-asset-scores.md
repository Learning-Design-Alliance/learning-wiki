## Evidence

### Rahm Stefan 2018

Rahm Stefan, Wieser Karl, Bauer David E., Waibel Felix WA, Meyer Dominik C., Gerber Christian, Fucentese Sandro F. (2018). Efficacy of standardized training on a virtual reality simulator to advance knee and shoulder arthroscopic motor skills. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-018-2072-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis relating simulator-recorded metric data (time, camera and instrument pathway) to blinded ASSET ratings for the same tasks. The article reports a "good correlation" with r = 0.688, p = 0.013, a printed correlation effect size the authors interpret as supporting metric data as a skill measure.

> "There was a good correlation (correlation coefficient = 0.688; p = 0.013) between the overall z-score and the overall ASSET score."
