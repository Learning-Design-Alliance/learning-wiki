Evaluating a preclinical orthodontic training using haptic-enhanced VR simulation system: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07036-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Open-ended feedback survey completed by all 26 participants after training, with three forced-choice and free-text questions. The article reports "most students (n = 18) chose the offline training" for three-dimensional adjustment and instant feedback.
