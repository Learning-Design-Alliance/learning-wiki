## Evidence

### Means 2020

Means, B., Peters, V., Neisler, J., & Griffiths, R. (2020). STEM Courses During the COVID Pandemic: Lessons from Spring 2020. Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7900cb79-6ea5-44d0-aad6-34aa81e9bf4b

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

National probability-based survey (Ipsos KnowledgePanel) of 620 undergraduates taking STEM courses that shifted fully online in spring 2020. The report states "46% of STEM students" had connectivity issues at least occasionally and "15%" often or very often; 23% reported hardware or software problems at least occasionally.

> "Internet connectivity issues were serious enough to interfere with students’ ability to attend or participate in their STEM courses at least occasionally for 46% of STEM students, with 15% of students reporting that they experienced such problems often or very often."
