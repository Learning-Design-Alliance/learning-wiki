## Evidence

### Adrienne L. Traxler 2015

Adrienne L. Traxler. (2015). Community structure in introductory physics course networks. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.078](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.078)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre-post network analysis of the two small sections (N ≤ 30, high interactive engagement) found "both density and number of communities stayed effectively constant"; Table II shows non-significant density changes (section C t = -0.45, p = 0.67; section D t = -1.01, p = 0.84).

> "In the smaller sections , on the other hand, both density and number of communities stayed effectively constant."
