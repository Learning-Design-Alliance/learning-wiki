## Evidence

### Yeomans 2018

Yeomans, M., Stewart, B. M., Mavon, K., Kindel, A., Tingley, D., & Reich, J. (2018). The Civic Mission of MOOCs: Engagement across Political Differences in Online Forums. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 28, 553–589. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0161-0

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of forum activity among U.S. students who reported ideology (39% of posters, 42% of focal-thread activity). Effect sizes negligible: "rank order correlation: rτ = −.019" overall, with sub-correlations of −.024, −.015, and .007 for replies, comments, and upvotes respectively; all effectively zero.

> "This analysis found essentially no relationship between political ideology and total forum activity (rank order correlation: rτ = −.019), and this result was consistent when we looked separately at replies (rτ = −.024), comments (rτ = −.015), and upvotes (rτ = .007)."
