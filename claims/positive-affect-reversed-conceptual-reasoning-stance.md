## Subclaims
`q1 i?` After successfully reasoning informally about waveforms and feeling satisfied, Judy called conceptual reasoning 'very useful', reversing her earlier stance that it was unproductive. [→ Ayush Gupta 2010](#ayush-gupta-2010)

## Evidence

### Ayush Gupta 2010

Ayush Gupta, Brian A. Danielak, Andrew Elby. (2010). Understanding students' difficulties in terms of coupled epistemological and affective dynamics. 40th ASEE/IEEE Frontiers in Education Conference. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1003.4238](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1003.4238)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Disconfirmatory Episode A from the clinical interview: pressed to reason through a wave-comparison question without her book, Judy produced informal area-under-the-curve reasoning and expressed satisfaction. The authors report this reversal as evidence an affect-free toy model cannot predict.

> "Though Judy says of her solution, "it's not very formal"—it's the kind of qualitative reasoning she earlier labeled unproductive—in this moment, feeling good about her solution, she calls this kind of conceptual reasoning "very useful.""
