Rachel E. Scherr. (2007). Gesture analysis for physics education researchers. Accepted to Physical Review Special Topics – Physics Education Research, May 2007. https://journals.aps.org/prper/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0712.0581](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0712.0581)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative video analysis of four students working on a Newton's third law tutorial about a truck ramming a parked car, across three videos. Gestural shifts are read as evidence that ideas moved from newly constructed to familiar, consistent with prior gesture research the article cites.

> "In Video 2, Alan's gestures are prolific, detailed, large, and strongly animated relative to his gestures in later videos, suggesting that he is explaining ideas that he considers to be new, either to him or to his listeners"
