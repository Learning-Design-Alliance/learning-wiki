# RL-timed GenAI access yields higher objective post-test scores than unrestricted access in higher education students

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Participants under the RL access policy scored significantly higher on the unassisted post-test than participants with always-allowed GenAI access (d = 0.59). [→ Janne Rotter 2026](#janne-rotter-2026)

## Evidence

### Janne Rotter 2026

Janne Rotter, Pau Benazet i Montobbio, Davinia Hernández-Leo. (2026). Access Timing as Scaffolding: A Reinforcement Learning Approach to GenAI in Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.15850](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.15850)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Controlled lab experiment, N=105 university students in a 3x1 between-subject design comparing RL, Never, and Always GenAI access conditions during text-comprehension tasks. The RL vs Always contrast showed "t(71) = 2.51,p= .014,d= 0.59" on the 0-8 point unassisted post-test.

> "participants in the RL condition significantly outperformed those in the Always condition regarding objective post-test scores,t(71) = 2.51,p= .014,d= 0.59"
