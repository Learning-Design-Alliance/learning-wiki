## Evidence

### Justyna P. Zwolak 2018

Justyna P. Zwolak, Michael Zwolak, and Eric Brewe. (2018). Educational commitment and social networking: The power of informal networks. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010131)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression using first-midterm scores as a proxy for final grade within the intermediate category (N = 56). The article reports no statistically significant correlation (P = 0.06); equivalence was not tested and no standardised effect size is printed.

> "there is no statistically signiﬁcant correlation between midterm scores and persistence within the intermediate group (logistic regression: OR = 1.06; B = 0.20, SE = 0.11, P = 0.06; N = 56)"
