Evidence¶

Tripathi 2026¶

Tripathi, P., & Kapralos, B. (2026). AI-Enabled Serious Games: Integrating Intelligence and Adaptivity in Training Systems. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21962 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.21962

q1 · i? · review · r2

The chapter identifies three failure scenarios as analytical risks, citing prior work: an RL policy may learn to present repeated similar scenarios because learners achieve high accuracy through pattern matching; a policy may present easier, more engaging content rather than challenging content producing learning gains; and extensive hints may create dependency where learners perform well with support but fail without it.

"If these are defined poorly, RL produces adaptive behavior that appears intelligent while being pedagogically hollow."
