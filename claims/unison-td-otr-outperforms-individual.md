# Class-wide unison TD-OTRs produce more positive academic and behavioral outcomes than individual TD-OTRs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Verbal and nonverbal choral (unison) response opportunities yield more positive outcomes than individual responding for students with and without disabilities. [→ MacSuga-Gage 2015](#macsuga-gage-2015)

## Evidence

### MacSuga-Gage 2015

MacSuga-Gage, A. S., & Simonsen, B. (2015). Examining the Effects of Teacher-Directed Opportunities to Respond on Student Outcomes: A Systematic Review of the Literature. Education and Treatment of Children, 38(2), 211-240. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070193

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of modality studies states "class-wide unison TD-OTRs" result in more positive academic and behavioral outcomes than individual TD-OTRs; one study (Wolery et al., 1992) found no functional relation between response type and outcomes, so the evidence base is not unanimous.

> "Results of this review indicate that providing students with class-wide unison TD-OTRs (i.e., verbal and nonverbal choral response opportunities) results in more positive academic and behavioral outcomes for students with and without disabilities than individual TD-OTRs."
