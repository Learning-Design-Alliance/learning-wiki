## Evidence

### Simkins 2008

Simkins, D. W., & Steinkuehler, C. (2008). Critical ethical reasoning and role-play. Games and Culture. https://doi.org/10.1177/1555412008317313

`q2 · i?`

Content analysis of the 10 interviews: each interview received a detailed content log, thematically coded with drift calibration. The article reports "Forty-six codes were generated from the coding process," with 25 preference codes covering 86 statements (mean 3.44 instances per code, median 2). Descriptive counts only; no effect size.

> "Forty-six codes were generated from the coding process. Of these, 25 were "preferences" expressing how the participant believed the game ought to be or what they like about games, shown in Table 1. Eighty-six statements were coded as preferences; the mean number of instances per code was 3.44, and the median was 2."
