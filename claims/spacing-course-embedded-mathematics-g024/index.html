Evidence¶

Murray 2023¶

Murray, E., Horner, A. J., & Göbel, S. M. (2023). A Meta-analytic Review of the Effectiveness of Spacing and Retrieval Practice for Mathematics Learning. https://osf.io/

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of the course-embedded subset (17 studies, 26 effect sizes), mostly classroom or homework settings in the USA. The weighted mean was "g = 0.24 (se 0.038)"; removal of no single study dropped the lower confidence limit below zero.

"The overall weighted mean effect size of spaced versus massed practice for cumulative material is g = 0.24 (se 0.038) (95% confidence interval [0.155, 0.324])."
