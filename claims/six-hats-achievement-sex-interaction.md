## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The interaction between teaching method and sex significantly affected achievement (F = 8.63, p = .004), with adjusted posttest means of 31.3 for experimental females versus 30.4 for experimental males and 20.9 versus 18.1 in the control group. [→ Alkhateeb 2015](#alkhateeb-2015)

## Evidence

### Alkhateeb 2015

DR.PH omar salem alkhateeb. (2015). The Effect of the Six Hats Based on Program in the Development of the Pivotal Thinking of Islamic Concepts Students in Hussein University. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083817

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Covariance analysis (Table 2) of the same 216-student quasi-experiment reports the Group×sex interaction term with F = 8.63 and significance .004. Table 3 adjusted means show female experimental students at 31.3 and male experimental students at 30.4.
