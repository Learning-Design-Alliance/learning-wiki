## Evidence

### Marcus 1998

Marcus, N. (1998). Understanding Instructions. PhD thesis, University of New South Wales. https://hdl.handle.net/1959.4/70247

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The thesis reports, citing Ericsson, Chase, and Faloon (1980), a case study in which a runner recoded numbers as meaningful running times. The thesis notes his underlying memory store had not increased; with other materials such as letters his performance remained normal, at about seven items.

> "They showed that a subject with a normal digit span of 7 could increase his digit span to about 80 digits."
