### Saleh 2018

Saleh, M., Prahmana, R.C.I., Isa, M., & Murni. (2018). Improving the Reasoning Ability of Elementary School Student through the Indonesian Realistic Mathematics Education. Journal on Mathematics Education, 9(1), 41-54. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1173650

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis of the same two-school quasi-experiment, reported in the results and Table 2. For the intermediate and low initial-ability categories the article reports "no significant difference" in achievement between PMRI and conventional learning; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, the achievement of students in the category of intermediate initial mathematics ability gave no significant difference. The same phenomenon was also shown by the students in the category of low initial mathematics ability."
