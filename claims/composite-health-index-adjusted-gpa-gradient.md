`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable negative binomial regression (Table 4, N = 47,011) on the Spring 2023 ACHA-NCHA III, adjusted for year in school, sex/gender, race/ethnicity, and BMI with standard errors clustered by institution; average marginal effects are reported as printed. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "students who reported an A GPA had, on average, 1.02 (p < .0001) more positive health behaviors than students reporting a D/F GPA, students with a B GPA had 0.72 more positive health behaviors (p < .0001), and students reporting a C GPA had an average of 0.32 more healthy behaviors (p = .005)"
