# In the perceptual-cue reanalysis, the only reliable forgetting and slip deviations were a small forgetting reduction under incongruent color and a modest slip reduction under congruent spacing

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Posterior contrast analysis (Table 7) from the same Multi BKT MCMC reanalysis of the perceptual cue study, comparing each congruency level to its neutral baseline. "The only reliable deviations" were the incongruent-color forgetting effect and the congruent-spacing slip effect; most effects for P(F) and P(S) were described as weak or uncertain.

> "The only reliable deviations are a small reduction in the forgetting rate for the incongruent color condition (-0.012, 95% CrI [-0.023,-0.003]) and a modest reduction in slip under the congruent spacing condition (-0.030, 95% CrI [-0.059,-0.001])."
