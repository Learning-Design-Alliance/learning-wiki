Talia Isaacs and Pavel Trofimovich. (2012). DECONSTRUCTING COMPREHENSIBILITY: Identifying the Linguistic Influences on Listeners' L2 Comprehensibility Ratings. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 34, Issue 3. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263112000150

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-methods study correlating 19 quantitative speech measures of 40 native French learners of English with scalar comprehensibility judgments from 60 native English listeners, plus three ESL teachers' introspective reports. The article reports that "five speech measures were identified that clearly distinguished between L2 learners at different comprehensibility levels."
