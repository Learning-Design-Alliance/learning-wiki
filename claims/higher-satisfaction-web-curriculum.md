## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Intervention PCPs rated the curriculum more highly (p < .0001), were more likely to recommend it (p < .0001), and 78% preferred the web-based program to reading materials or attending a CME class. [→ Wilkes Michael S. 2017](#wilkes-michael-s-2017)

## Evidence

### Wilkes Michael S. 2017

Wilkes Michael S., Day Frank C., Fancher Tonya L., McDermott Haley, Lehman Erik, Bell Robert A., Green Michael J. (2017). Increasing confidence and changing behaviors in primary care providers engaged in genetic counselling. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0982-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-curriculum survey of the Intervention Group comparing the interactive tool with traditional curricula across 10 goals (Table 5); differences significant for demonstrating communication styles (p = 0.0001), engaging content (p = 0.0001), and others. The article reports 78% preferred the web-based program; no effect sizes printed.

> "Compared to the Control Group, intervention PCPs were significantly more likely to rate the curriculum highly (p < .0001). They were also significantly more likely to recommend the curriculum to colleagues (p < .0001)."
