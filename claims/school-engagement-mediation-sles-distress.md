Connecting the dots: Examining stressful life events, campus climate, and school engagement on academic achievement and psychological distress in a predominantly Latine sample. Journal of Research on Adolescence, 35, e70096. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.70096

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the same path analysis (N = 293), the direct effect of SLEs on psychological distress was nonsignificant (β = .07, p = .177), while the indirect effect through school engagement was significant, "indicating school engagement mediated the relationship between SLEs and psychological distress."

> "SLEs had a significant positive indirect effect on psychological distress through school engagement (B = 0.18, β = .05, SE = 0.07, p = .014), indicating school engagement mediated the relationship between SLEs and psychological distress"
