### Nicy Scaria 2026

Nicy Scaria, Silvester John Joseph Kennedy, and Deepak Subramani. (2026). Learning in Blocks: A Multi Agent Debate Assisted Personalized Adaptive Learning Framework for Language Learning. Preprint, to appear in International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Education (LNAI). [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29755-6_27](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29755-6_27)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Benchmarking of four scoring methods on 150 CEFR A2 conversations transcribed with Whisper V3 Turbo and scored by two ESL experts on 0-5 rubrics; "model heterogeneity in HeteroMAD achieved a de- gree of variation of 0.23" versus 0.41-0.45 for non-MAD methods.

> "Role specialization in HomoMAD yielded only a marginal im- provement (0.43), whereas model heterogeneity in HeteroMAD achieved a de- gree of variation of 0.23."
