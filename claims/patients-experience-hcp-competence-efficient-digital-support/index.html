Kaihlaniemi 2025¶

Kaihlaniemi, J., Suonnansalo, P., Kääriäinen, M., Kaakinen, P., Litendahl, M., Paukkonen, L., Laukkonen, K., & Oikarinen, A. (2025). Patients' Experiences of Healthcare Professionals' Competence in Digital Counselling in Healthcare Settings—A Qualitative Systematic Review. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16663

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's meta-aggregation of qualitative findings (Table 5) reports that patients valued HCP competence in providing digital support, including familiarity with platforms (e.g., 'Zoom functions'), use of checklists, demonstrations, repetition, and written materials.

"Patients experienced that HCPs need competence in providing digital support to them, in addition to solving technological problems"

Kaihlaniemi 2025 (2)¶

Kaihlaniemi, J., Suonnansalo, P., Kääriäinen, M., Kaakinen, P., Litendahl, M., Paukkonen, L., Laukkonen, K., & Oikarinen, A. (2025). Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16663

The review's meta-aggregation (Table 5) identified 'Technology disruptions' and 'Collective action' as findings under the category 'Competence in solving technological problems', indicating patients valued HCPs' ability to manage technical issues collaboratively.
