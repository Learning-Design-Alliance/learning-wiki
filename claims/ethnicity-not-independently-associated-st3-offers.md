## Evidence

### Seehra Jaspreet Kaur 2025

Seehra Jaspreet Kaur, Ellis Ricky, Doleman Brett, McLarty Esther, Lund Jonathan. (2025). Fair access to higher surgical training in the UK: an equity, diversity and inclusion analysis of national selection in 2024. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-106487

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariable DAG-adjusted models across six surgical specialties using ONS ethnicity categories, with White as reference. The article states ethnicity "was not a significant factor in chances of an offer being made"; no effect size is printed for this finding.

> "However, lower odds of appointment are unrelated to ethnicity, which was not a significant factor in chances of an offer being made."
