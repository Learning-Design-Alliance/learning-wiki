## Evidence

### Hazzam 2023

Hazzam, J., & Wilkins, S. (2023). The influences of lecturer charismatic leadership and technology use on student online engagement, learning performance, and satisfaction. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104809

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 659 US higher education students analyzed with covariance-based SEM; path analysis shows charismatic leadership significantly predicts all three engagement dimensions, with "behavioral engagement (β = 0.25, p < .01)" weakest.

> "The findings of path analysis indicate that lecturer charismatic leadership relate positively and significantly to student's cognitive (β = 0.42, p < .001), emotional (β = 0.46, p < .001) and behavioral engagement (β = 0.25, p < .01), supporting H1a, H1b and H1c."
