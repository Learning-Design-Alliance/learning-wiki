## Subclaims
`q3 i2` High school students who received iSTART training produced higher average self-explanation scores at posttest than no-training controls. [→ McCarthy 2017](#mccarthy-2017)

## Evidence

### McCarthy 2017

McCarthy, K. S., Jacovina, M. E., Snow, E. L., Guerrero, T. A., & McNamara, D. S. (2017). iSTART therefore I understand: But metacognitive supports did not enhance comprehension gains. Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Education, Wuhan, China: Springer. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED577122

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA controlling for pretest self-explanation score compared iSTART (N=116) to no-training control (N=118) high school students. The article reports "F(1, 231) = 29.78, p < .001, η2p = .11".

> "this analysis indicated that those who received iSTART training received higher average self-explanation scores than those in the no training control condition, F(1, 231) = 29.78, p < .001, η2p = .11"
