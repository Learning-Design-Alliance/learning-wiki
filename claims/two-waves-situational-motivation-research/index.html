Evidence¶

Törmänen 2025¶

Törmänen, T., Ketonen, E., Lehtoaho, E., Turunen, M., Zabolotna, K., Shubina, T., & Järvenoja, H. (2025). Situational Motivation in Academic Learning: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10036-0

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review (PRISMA, Scopus, ProQuest, EBSCO; 80 studies, 2013–2023) identifying two waves: "motivation measured as a situation-specific construct without focusing on dynamic changes (n = 62)" versus momentary online measurement (n = 18).

"two research waves were identified: 1) motivation measured as a situation-specific construct without focusing on dynamic changes (n = 62), and 2) motivation measured repeatedly during the learning process to capture momentary changes (n = 18)."

Törmänen 2025 (2)¶

Törmänen, T., Ketonen, E., Lehtoaho, E., Turunen, M., Zabolotna, K., Shubina, T., & Järvenoja, H. (2025). Situational Motivation in Academic Learning: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10036-0

q3 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of measurement methods across the 80 included studies found that "all the included studies used self-reports" and "only 19% (n = 15) of the studies used an additional complementary data channel," showing dominance of offline self-report over online measures.

"Methodologically, all the included studies used self-reports and only 19% (n = 15) of the studies used an additional complementary data channel."
