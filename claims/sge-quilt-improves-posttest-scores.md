## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students' Understanding of Quantum Mechanics via the Stern-Gerlach Experiment. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Preliminary evaluation in two junior-senior level classes (22 and 13 students, different instructors); students received traditional instruction, took a pre-test, worked on the QuILT, then took a post-test. The first class averaged 53% pre-test and 92% post-test.

> "The average pre-test score for this class was 53% and the average post-test score was 92%."

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016 (2)

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students' Understanding of Quantum Mechanics via the Stern-Gerlach Experiment. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Second class of 13 students with randomly administered test versions A and B as pre-test and the other version as post-test after the QuILT; the article reports an average pre-test of 37% and post-test of 84%. Except for question (1), performance improved on all questions.

> "The average pre-test score for this second class was 37% and the average pos t-test score was 84%."
