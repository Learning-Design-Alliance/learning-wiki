Within the 91-student theoretical-learning comparison, 17 of 20 items favored the intervention but three did not differ significantly, as printed. A second entry point: observation time (P = 0.502) and oral feedback time (P = 0.063) showed no significant difference between the two skills groups.

> "the remaining three demonstrated similar results, including the following topics: "expanding sphere of knowledge" (P = 0.066), "the difficulty of the cases being moderate" (P = 0.107), and "cases being interesting to arouse interest in learning" (P = 0.225)"
