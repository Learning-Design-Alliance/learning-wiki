# Flipped-format students score significantly higher on exams than standard-format students, by an average of 11.6% across three exams in an upper-division physical chemistry course

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Averaged across three exams, flipped-class students outperformed standard-class students by 11.6% on exam scores after controlling for GPA. [→ Gross D 2015](#gross-d-2015)

## Evidence

### Gross D 2015

Gross D, Pietri ES, Anderson G, Moyano-Camihort K, Graham MJ. (2015). Increased Preclass Preparation Underlies Student Outcome Improvement in the Flipped Classroom. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-02-0040

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-model 2 (class type) × 3 (exam) ANCOVA over five cohorts totaling 464 students, controlling for GPA, comparing three standard-format years with two flipped-format years of the same course. The printed effect size is partial η² = 0.12 (partial η2 = 0.12), a medium effect, with F(1440) = 59.91, p < 0.001, for "an average 11.6% improvement".

> "Averaging across all three exams, the flipped class shows an average 11.6% improvement when compared with the standard course (F(1440) = 59.91, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.12)."
