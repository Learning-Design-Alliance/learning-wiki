Conceptual analysis of the psychometric score-generation process in large-scale assessments. The article states these 'PVs are a special case of multiple imputation, where the latent proficiency is treated as a missing value and is imputed repeatedly using a conditioning model'.

> "The PVs are random draws from the posterior distribution of each students' ability based on their item responses and numerous of their other observed background characteristics (Wu, 2005)."
