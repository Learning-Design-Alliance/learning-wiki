Tutal 2023¶

Tutal, Ö (2023). Does Context-Based Learning Increase Academic Achievement and Learning Retention?: A Review based on Meta-Analysis. Journal of Practical Studies in Education, 4(5), 1-16. https://doi.org/10.46809/jpse.v4i5.71

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Between-group heterogeneity test in the moderator analysis of 54 achievement effect sizes; group size (total students in experimental and control groups) was the only significant moderator. Printed subgroup effect sizes were strong: g=1.116 (≤50), g=1.096 (>50,<100), versus modest g=0.498 (≥100).
