Chi-square goodness-of-fit comparison of study-with-friends frequency from Week 0 to Week 10 in a first-quarter introductory biology course. "χ2 = 27.88, p < 0.001" with regular use rising to 19% and rare/never use falling to 37% by Week 10.

> "There was a significant increase in studying with friends by the end of the term (Figure 2, full contingency table in Supplemental Table S23, χ2 = 27.88, p < 0.001), with 19% saying they regularly study with friends, 45% saying they occasionally studied with friends, and 37% saying they rarely or never studied with friends at Week 10."
