`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey item on longitudinal confidence development in the 144-student pilot; four responses missing. The article reports "63.9% who reported confidence growth" descriptively, with no effect size or significance test.

> "Seventy students (48.6%) agreed, and 22 students (15.3%) strongly agreed with this statement, yielding a combined rate of 63.9% who reported confidence growth. Thirty-nine students (27.1%) remained neutral about whether their confidence had increased, while nine students (6.3%) disagreed or strongly disagreed."
