# Students in active classrooms scored higher on tests of learning than students in passive lectures (0.46 SD higher, P < 0.001)

### Deslauriers L. 2019

Deslauriers L., McCarty L. S., Miller K., Callaghan K., Kestin G. (2019). Measuring actual learning versus feeling of learning in response to being actively engaged in the classroom. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1821936116

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Fixed-effects regression (model 2) on pooled data from a randomized crossover experiment in two large-enrollment introductory physics courses with 149 individually randomized students. Active instruction raised test-of-learning performance, with students in active classrooms scoring "almost half an SD (0.46) higher on the examination" (P < 0.001); the article prints no standardized effect size for this finding.

> "In this case, students in active classrooms scored almost half an SD (0.46) higher on the examination. These results are highly significant (P < 0.001)."
