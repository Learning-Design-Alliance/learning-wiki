The article reports, citing the authors' prior publication on CTT metrics applied to SPRUCE data, that "at the couplet, AO, and whole-assessment level that SPRUCE does have evidence of both validity and reliability", with discrimination index, difficulty, and Pearson coefficient at couplet and AO levels, and difficulty, Ferguson's delta, Cronbach's alpha, test-retest stability, and split-halves reliability at the assessment level. The underlying study is reported second-hand here; no effect sizes are printed in this article.
