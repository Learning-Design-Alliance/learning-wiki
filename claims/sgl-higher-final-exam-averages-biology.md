## Evidence

### Gaudet Andrew D. 2010

Gaudet Andrew D., Ramer Leanne M., Nakonechny Joanne, Cragg Jacquelyn J., Ramer Matt S. (2010). Small-Group Learning in an Upper-Level University Biology Class Enhances Academic Performance and Student Attitudes Toward Group Work. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0015821

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of final exam grades across five course terms (Individualistic n=61; Initial SGL n=76; Refined SGL n=82), using the individually-written final exam as the outcome. The article reports "Refined SGL: 79±1%" versus 70±2% and 74±1%, with Kruskal-Wallis ANOVA and Dunn's test; no effect size is printed.

> "In the Refined SGL environment, the class average on the final exam was increased relative to both Individualistic and Initial SGL environments (Individualistic: 70±2%; Initial SGL: 74±1%; Refined SGL: 79±1%)"
