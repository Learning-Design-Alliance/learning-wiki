Garver 2023¶

Garver, K., Weisenfeld, G. G., Connors-Tadros, L., Hodges, K., Melnick, H., & Plasencia, S. (2023). State preschool in a mixed delivery system: Lessons from five states. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/387.446

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Cross-case analysis in the Governance section. The report explains that over 200 LEAs approved since 2018 "do not fall under the Supreme Court mandate for mixed delivery and have expressed reluctance to collaborate with non-LEAs"; descriptive trend, no effect size.

"Thus, as state-funded preschool enrollment grows in the state, the percentage of children served in non-LEAs is declining."
