Evidence¶

Wang 2020¶

Wang, S., & Li, S. (2020). Calibration of Spanish MAP Growth math tests. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/calibration-of-spanish-map-growth-math-tests/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Authors' explanation in the conclusion for the status 10 proportion observed in the calibration (Table 2.3 shows 1,166 of 3,955 collected items at status 10). Items are labeled status 10 when calibration sample size is less than 1,000. This is the article's own interpretation of the adaptive response distribution; no effect size is printed.

"For adaptive item calibration, due to student ability difference, the distribution of the number of student responses collected for a pool items is a uniform distribution as in a linear test. This is why about 1/3 calibrated Spanish math items have calibration status 10"
