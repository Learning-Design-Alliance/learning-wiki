Expósito 2020¶

Expósito, A., Sánchez-Rivas, J., Gómez-Calero, M. P., & Pablo-Romero, M. P. (2020). Examining the use of instructional video clips for teaching macroeconomics. Computers & Education, 143, 103705. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103709

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study of 120 undergraduates at the University of Seville comparing an experimental group given access to instructional videos with a control group; the abstract reports "the superiority of using instructional video-clips to achieve higher test scores."

"The findings confirm the superiority of using instructional video-clips to achieve higher test scores."
