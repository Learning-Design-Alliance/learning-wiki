## Evidence

### Moore 2018

James Christopher Moore. (2018). Efficacy of Multimedia Learning Modules as Preparation for Lecture-Based Tutorials in Electromagnetism. Educ. Sci. 2018, 8, 23. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8010023

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

CSEM normalized gain data from the two non-MLM semesters (Fa10 and Sp11). The author found this below-average performance surprising given the use of TIP and JiTT pedagogies, and attributes it possibly to the population's response to reformed instruction.
