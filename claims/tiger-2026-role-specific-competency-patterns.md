`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of the survey's role-specific relevance ratings revealed distinct competency profiles. The authors note that "basic computer literacy/digital literacy, and data protection and privacy stood out as they were represented among the top 10 in all five roles."

> "For Clinical Nursing, competencies related to patient safety were rated the most relevant, followed by competencies in nursing documentation and basic computer literacy/digital literacy. In both the Nursing Management and IT Management in Nursing roles, basic computer literacy/digital literacy (lead) emerged as the most relevant competency domain."
