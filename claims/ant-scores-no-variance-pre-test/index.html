Evidence¶

Killingsworth 2015¶

Killingsworth, S. S., Clark, D. B., & Adams, D. M. (2015). Self-Explanation and Explanatory Feedback in Games: Individual Differences, Gameplay, and Learning. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology, 3(3), 162-186. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1066360

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Regression analysis with collapsed physics pre-test score as dependent variable and ANT network scores (executive, orienting, alerting) and ANT neutral RT as predictors. The model was collapsed across conditions because game condition was not a significant predictor of pre-test performance.

"Regression analyses showe d that none of the ANT network scores or neutral RT predicted significant variance in pre-test scores. Additionally, the overall model including all predictors did not reach significance, F(4, 77) = .548, p = .701, R2 = .028."
