Bracco 2014¶

Bracco, K. R., Klarin, B., Broek, M., Austin, K., Finkelstein, N., Bugler, D., & Mundry, S. (2014). Statewide networks: Connecting education systems and stakeholders to support college readiness. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559537

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Cross-state qualitative analysis of AD interviews and collaborator surveys in Year Three of the grant. The report observes that "state strategies around Core to College priority goals focused primarily on informing and networking activities." No effect size is printed.

"The interactions of the Core to College networks are primarily based on information sharing and making connections between K–12 and higher education. Across the board, state strategies around Core to College priority goals focused primarily on informing and networking activities."
