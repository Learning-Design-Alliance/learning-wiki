### Zahradníková Petra 2025

Zahradníková Petra, Babala Jozef, Lindák Martin, Pechanová Rebeka, Hnilicová Silvia, Molnár Marián, Nedomová Barbora, Poš Lucie, Dotlačil Vojtěch, Kučerová Barbora. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review following PRISMA guidelines, registered in PROSPERO, searching PubMed/MEDLINE, SCOPUS, Web of Science and Cochrane from January 2010 to June 2024. The review reports that "no universal MIS training model exists for neonate/infant patients" despite promising results from 3D-printed, animal-based, and hybrid models.

> "Out of 1084 identified records, 72 studies met the inclusion criteria and were analysed across general, gastrointestinal, thoracic, and urological MIS specialties. Recent efforts have led to the development of 3D-printed, animal-based, and hybrid models several of which showed high fidelity, skill differentiation, and educational value. Despite promising results, no universal MIS training model exists for neonate/infant patients"
