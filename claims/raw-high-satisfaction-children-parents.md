Anonymous end-of-program satisfaction questionnaires completed separately by participants and parents after the 14-week program. The article reports "81 % of the participants and 99 % of the parents liked R@W" and that 85% of participants felt they had learnt something or quite a lot.

> "Overall, 81 % of the participants and 99 % of the parents liked R@W. Time investment was just fine or too short for 83 % of the participants."
