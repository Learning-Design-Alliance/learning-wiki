### St-Onge C 2026

St-Onge C, Boulais I, Bergeron L, Dion V, Le Sieur I, Marceau M, Poitras M-E, Dubé T, Schumacher DJ. (2026). Patients’ Voices and the Assessment of Medical Trainees: A Discourse Analysis. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2588

`q2 · i3` · `qualitative · r3`

The discourse analysis quotes this reviewed study's comparison of rater groups: "nurse and physician ratings were significantly correlated (r = 0.562, P = 0.01)" while parent ratings were unrelated to physician and nurse ratings. The article notes such low correlation was sometimes used to question patients as assessors.

> "When we compared the association between mean ratings among pairings of the three groups, nurse and physician ratings were significantly correlated (r = 0.562, P = 0.01), but parent mean ratings were unrelated to attending physician (r = –0.174) and nurse (r = 0.002) mean ratings"
