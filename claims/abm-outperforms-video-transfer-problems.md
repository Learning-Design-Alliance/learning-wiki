## Evidence

### Polly K. Lai 2026

Polly K. Lai. (2026). Enhancing learning of nanoscale phenomena: the role of agent-based models and embodied reasoning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09776-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way between-group ANCOVA with pre-test scores as covariate, in the same randomized experiment, showed "the ABM group outperforming significantly on the transfer problems compared to the visualization video group", partial η2 =.269; transfer problems asked students to apply size-dependent concepts to novel contexts.

> "After adjusting for pre-test scores, there was a significant difference between the groups on post-test transfer scores, with the ABM group outperforming significantly on the transfer problems compared to the visualization video group (F(1, 24) = 83.841, p =.007, partial η2 =.269; see Fig. 5)."
