## Evidence

### Setyoningrum Retno Asih 2026

Setyoningrum Retno Asih, Susilo Astrid Pratidina, Hasan Helmia, Rehatta Nancy Margarita. (2026). Effect of structured preceptorship on clinical reasoning of pediatric residents in Indonesia: a randomized controlled trial. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.130

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 41 pediatric residents (21 intervention, 20 control) at a single Indonesian hospital; between-group SCT comparison by Mann-Whitney U test showed a significant difference only for hematology & oncology, with "a median SCT score of 14.28" vs 12.75 (p=0.037).

> "For hematology & oncology topic, the intervention group recorded a median SCT score of 14.28 (IQR, 12.78–15.08) compared with a median of 12.75 (IQR, 11.49–13.63) in the control group (p=0.037)."
