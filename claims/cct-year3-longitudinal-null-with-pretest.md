# In the year 3 longitudinal comparison, the CCT effect was not significant with pretest controlled (ES = 0.13) but significant without it (ES = 0.19)

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` With pretest and teaching experience controlled, Treatment 2.3 showed no significant improvement over Control 2.1 (p = .190, ES = 0.13). [→ Irving 2016](#irving-2016)

## Evidence

### Irving 2016

Irving, K. E., Pape, S. J., Owens, D. T., Abrahamson, L., Silver, D., & Sanalan, V. A. (2016). Classroom Connectivity and Algebra 1 Achievement: A Three-Year Longitudinal Study. Journal of Computers in Mathematics and Science Teaching, 35(2), 131-151. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1095322

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal HLM (model 5p) comparing Cohort 2 teachers' year 3 treatment data with their year 1 baseline, with pretest and experience controlled. The article reports the printed effect size ES = 0.13, γ20 = 0.69, p = .190; equivalence was not tested.

> "when controlling for the pretest and years of teaching experience, the treatment group showed no significant improvement (γ20 = 0.69, p = .190) with a very small effect size (ES = 0.13)."

### Irving 2016 (2)

Irving, K. E., Pape, S. J., Owens, D. T., Abrahamson, L., Silver, D., & Sanalan, V. A. (2016). Classroom Connectivity and Algebra 1 Achievement: A Three-Year Longitudinal Study. Journal of Computers in Mathematics and Science Teaching, 35(2), 131-151. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1095322

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal HLM (model 5np) for Cohort 2 year 3 without the pretest covariate. The article reports the printed effect size of 0.19, γ10 = 1.30, p = .046, with treatment students scoring about 1.3 points higher; teaching experience was non-significant in both models 5p and 5np.

> "when the pretest was removed as a covariate, the comparison produced a significant difference between treatment and control (γ10 = 1.30, p = .046) and an effect size of 0.19."
