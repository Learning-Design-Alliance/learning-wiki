## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Xia 2016

Xia, M., Fosco, G. M., & Feinberg, M. E. (2016). Examining Reciprocal Influences Among Family Climate, School Attachment, and Academic Self-Regulation: Implications for School Success. Journal of Family Psychology, 30(4), 442-452. https://doi.org/10.1037/fam0000141

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lag results from the longitudinal SEM showed adolescents with higher school attachment reported ASR increases over each of the three time points, while ASR predicted school attachment only from W2 to W3, supporting a unidirectional pattern.

> "School attachment also was consistently linked with increases in ASR over time; however, ASR was not a consistent predictor of change in school attachment, as was true with family climate."
