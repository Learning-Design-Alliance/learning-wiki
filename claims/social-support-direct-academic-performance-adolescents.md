## Evidence

### Zhang Xiangping 2024

Zhang Xiangping, Qian Wensheng. (2024). The effect of social support on academic performance among adolescents: The chain mediating roles of self-efficacy and learning engagement. PLoS One 19(12): e0311597. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311597

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (2000 resamples, Amos 24.0) of the survey of 265 middle school students in Shandong, China, testing the direct effect of social support on standardized final-exam scores in Chinese, Math and English. The finding of a "significant direct effect" and a "significant total effect" comes from the printed confidence intervals.

> "At a 95% confidence level, the Bias-Corrected and Percentile methods’ confidence intervals do not include zero, indicating that social support has a significant total effect (bias-corrected CI [0.394, 0.673], percentile CI [0.393, 0.672], P < 0.01) and a significant direct effect (bias-corrected CI [0.152, 0.440], percentile CI [0.153, 0.441], P < 0.01) on academic performance."
