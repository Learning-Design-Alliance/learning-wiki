## Evidence

### Hibbert Emily J 2013

Hibbert Emily J, Lambert Tim, Carter John N, Learoyd Diana L, Twigg Stephen, Clarke Stephen. (2013). A randomized controlled pilot trial comparing the impact of access to clinical endocrinology video demonstrations with access to usual revision resources on medical student performance of clinical endocrinology skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled pilot trial: 2nd-year medical students randomized to video access or revision as usual after endocrinology tutorials, assessed in blinded OSCEs. The reported contrast was "91.7% (n = 11/12) of students randomized to the video" versus 40% (n = 4/10) of controls, with a printed group difference of 51.7% (95% CI 17.6-85.8%).

> "For the LLE, 91.7% (n = 11/12) of students randomized to the video were rated globally as competent at the skill compared with 40% (n = 4/10) of students not randomized to the video (p = 0.024)."
