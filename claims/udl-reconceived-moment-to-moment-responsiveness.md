### Rachel Lambert 2026

Rachel Lambert, Kara Imm. (2026). Teachers rehumanizing mathematics for students with disabilities; the role of empathy. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09753-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Cross-data-source finding from the pre/post surveys and focus groups of the professional-development teaching experiment, reported by the authors as documented shifts for a majority but not all participants. Educators connected this dynamic view of UDL to empathic relationships with students.

> "Initially viewed as a static and inflexible framework, UDL evolved in educators’ understanding to represent a dynamic “way of thinking,” emphasizing moment-to-moment responsiveness to students’ access needs rather than adhering to a fixed methodology"
