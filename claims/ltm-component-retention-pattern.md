Independent sample t-tests on the four retention components (Table 9), n = 60 per group. Means show Traditional higher on knowledge (1.0308 vs .8712) and Laboratory higher on comprehension (1.2492 vs .5306), application (1.0875 vs .3403) and skills (4.5333 vs .6833). No effect sizes printed.
