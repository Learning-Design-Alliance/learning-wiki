## Evidence

### Mishra J 2016

Mishra J, Sagar R, Joseph A A, Gazzaley A, Merzenich M M. (2016). Training sensory signal-to-noise resolution in children with ADHD in a global mental health setting. Translational Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/tp.2016.45

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline and post-intervention computerized cognitive testing in 11 ONTRAC completers vs 7 controls, analyzed by repeated-measures ANOVA with post hoc t-tests; the article reports F(1,16)=5.98, P=0.026 and F(4,64)=3.87, P=0.007.

> "This ANOVA showed an effect of intervention arm (F(1,16)=5.98, P=0.026) and an assessment × intervention arm interaction (F(4,64)=3.87, P=0.007). Between-arm results for each assessment were further parsed using two-tailed t-tests, showing P⩽0.05 significance in ONTRAC vs control post–pre change comparisons for the tests of response inhibition and Stroop interference."
