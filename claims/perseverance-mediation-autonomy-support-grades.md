### Huéscar Hernández E. 2020

Huéscar Hernández E., Moreno-Murcia J. A., Cid L., Monteiro D., Rodrigues F. (2020). Passion or Perseverance? The Effect of Perceived Autonomy Support and Grit on Academic Performance in College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17062143

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r1`

SEM specific indirect-path analysis (Table 3) in the same sample; indirect effects counted significant when the 95% CI excluded zero. The perseverance chain AS→BPNS→IM→PER→AP showed β = 0.09 (CI 95%: 0.00–0.11); the passion chain β = 0.00 (CI 95%: −0.01 to 0.02).

> "Accounting for indirect paths, all were significant via grit-perseverance, but not via grit-passion (Table 3). These findings reinforce previous direct effect analysis, showing that grit-passion seems to now stand as a significant predictor of academic performance nor does it seem to play a mediating role between autonomy support, BPN satisfaction, and intrinsic motivation on academic performance."
