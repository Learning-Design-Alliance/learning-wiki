### Stuij SM 2018

Stuij SM, Labrie NHM, van Dulmen S, Kersten MJ, Christoph N, Hulsman RL, Smets E. (2018). Developing a digital communication training tool on information-provision in oncology: uncovering learning needs and training preferences. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1308-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Facilitating-factors theme from the focus groups and interviews: participants saw creating workplace awareness, training in their own time, and accreditation points as facilitators; one participant said accreditation points "would push it to the top of my list".

> "there was consensus that any training should offer continuing medical education accreditation points."
