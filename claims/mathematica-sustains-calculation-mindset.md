### Thomas J. Bing and Edward F. Redish 2007

Thomas J. Bing and Edward F. Redish. (2007). Symbolic Manipulators Affect Mathematical Mindsets. https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.1187 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0712.1187](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0712.1187)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video case study of two students in a second-semester undergraduate quantum mechanics class working problem 6.32b from Griffiths. The authors report the students' "thinking was drawn into this Mathematica calculation" for fifteen minutes, and they never connected the result to <V> as the question required.

> "Their thinking was drawn into this Mathematica calculation, which sustained itself for fifteen straight minutes even when difficulty arose. Making the calculation work became a goal in and of itself, irrespective of the original homework question."
