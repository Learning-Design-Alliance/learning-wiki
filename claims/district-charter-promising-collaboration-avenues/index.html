McCullough 2015¶

McCullough, M., Heinkel, L., & Keating, B. (2015). District-Charter Collaboration Grant Implementation: Findings from Interviews and Site Visits. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/districtcharter-collaboration-grant-implementation-findings-from-interviews-and-site-visits

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Implementation study based on semistructured interviews with central office administrators and school principals, teacher focus groups, and observations across grantee districts. The report identifies these three activity types as "among the most promising avenues for cross-sector collaboration to date."

"School co-locations or partnerships, intensive programs for aspiring leaders, and shared preparation for implementing Common Core standards have been among the most promising avenues for cross-sector collaboration to date."
