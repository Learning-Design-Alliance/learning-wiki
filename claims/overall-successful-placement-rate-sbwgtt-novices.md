Successful placement assessment in a crossover randomized cadaver study with 30 veterinary students. The results section reports that "38 of 60 (63%) SBWGTT insertions in the current study were successful" with no difference between groups.

> "Overall, 38 of 60 (63%) SBWGTT insertions in the current study were successful, with 16 (53%) being landmark ‐guided and 22 (73%) being POCUS ‐guided. There was no difference in successful placement between groups (LPE, LPN, UPE, UPN)."
