## Evidence

### Henning Colin 2021

Henning Colin, Summerfeldt Laura J., Parker James D. A. (2021). ADHD and Academic Success in University Students: The Important Role of Impaired Attention. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547211036758

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on CAARS mean-item scores from 3,688 first-year undergraduates (1,024 men, 2,664 women). The gender × symptom-type interaction showed "men to have higher levels of inattention and impulsivity symptoms than women"; the article reports all significant effects were small.

> "The interaction between type of ADHD symptomatology and gender was significant, F(2, 7368) = 39.28, p < .001, ηp2 = .01, with planned comparisons showing men to have higher levels of inattention and impulsivity symptoms than women, but no gender difference for hyperactivity symptoms."
