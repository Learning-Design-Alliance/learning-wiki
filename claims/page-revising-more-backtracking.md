## Evidence

### Annie Piolat 1997

Annie Piolat, Jean-Yves Roussey and Olivier Thunin. (1997). Effects of screen presentation on text reading and revising. International Journal of Human-Computer Studies. https://doi.org/10.1006/ijhc.1997.0145

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 revising task with 26 randomly assigned undergraduates using a minimal word processor; navigation-file counts showed Page writers moved around the text more, F(1, 24) = 6.25, p < 0.02.

> "In line with Prediction 1, writers using Page did more backtracking than those using Scroll (7.85 vs. 4.07; F(1, 24) = 6.25, p < 0.02)."

### Annie Piolat 1997 (2)

Annie Piolat, Jean-Yves Roussey and Olivier Thunin. (1997). Effects of screen presentation on text reading and revising. International Journal of Human-Computer Studies. https://doi.org/10.1006/ijhc.1997.0145

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 revising-time measure: Page 1496 s vs. Scroll 1731 s, F(1, 24) = 1.13, reported as not significantly different.

> "The mean time taken by writers to modify the text with Page and Scroll did not differ significantly (Page = 1496 sec.; Scroll = 1731 s; F(1, 24) = 1.13)."
