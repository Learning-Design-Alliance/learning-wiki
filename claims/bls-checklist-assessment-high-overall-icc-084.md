Beck Stefanie, Ruhnke Bjarne, Issleib Malte, Daubmann Anne, Harendza Sigrid, Zöllner Christian. (2016). Analyses of inter-rater reliability between professionals, medical students and trained school children as assessors of basic life support skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0788-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of 889 summative assessments collected at school CPR-training events in Hamburg, using a two-way-random ICC model in SPSS. The article reports "0.84 (n = 889, 95 % CI: 0.82 to 0.86)", rated strong-to-optimal by the authors' own ICC thresholds.

> "Overall inter-rater reliability (Table 4) according to average measure of intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC) was 0.84 (n = 889, 95 % CI: 0.82 to 0.86)."
