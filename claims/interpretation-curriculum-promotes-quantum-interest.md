## Evidence

### Charles Baily 2015

Charles Baily, Noah D. Finkelstein. (2015). Teaching Quantum Interpretations: Revisiting the goals and practices of introductory quantum physics courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020124](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020124)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post matched-response analysis of the statement "I think quantum mechanics is an interesting subject" (Fig. 11). The article reports post-instruction distributions differ (p < 0.00001) though pre-instruction ones do not (p = 0.06). It adds that "virtually every INT-1 student agreed at the end of the semester that quantum mechanics is interesting."

> "Pre-instruction responses for the four courses regarding student interest are not signiﬁcantly diﬀerent (p = 0.06), but the post-instruction distributions are ( p< 0.00001)."
