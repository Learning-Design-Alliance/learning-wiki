## Evidence

### Meyer 2013

Meyer, A., Meyer-Ahrens, I., & Wilde, M. (2013). The Beneficial Effects of Non-received Choice: A Study on Intrinsic Motivation in Biology Education. European Journal of Educational Research, 2(4), 185-190. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086332

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation (type e) in the Discussion section, not a directly tested contrast. They argue the difference between choice and no choice "cannot entirely be attributed to the congruence of the chosen topic and individual interests".

> "Their higher motivation seems attributable to the voting process itself. Further, no frustration was found when choice was not realized."
