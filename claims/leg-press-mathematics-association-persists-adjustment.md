# Laboratory-based lower-limb muscular strength (1-RM leg press) is associated with mathematics skills and persists after adjustment for BMI and cardiorespiratory fitness

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` 1-RM leg press was associated with mathematics (β=0.251) and calculation skills (β=0.306) in Model 1, and remained significant after additional adjustment for BMI and cardiorespiratory fitness (mathematics β=0.316; calculation skills β=0.383). [→ Cadenas-Sanchez 2020](#cadenas-sanchez-2020)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional regression in 106 children with overweight/obesity (1-RM subsample n=88). The laboratory tests "only showed significant associations between the lower-limbs muscular strength (i.e.1-RM leg press) and mathematics skills measured by Woodcock-Muñoz (all β≥0.251, all p<0.01)"; Table 3 prints Model 2 mathematics β=0.316 and calculation skills β=0.383, the only association surviving Model 2 adjustment.
