## Evidence

### Chelagat T. 2020

Chelagat T., Kokwaro G., Onyango J., Rice J. (2020). Effect of project-based experiential learning on the health service delivery indicators: a quasi-experiment study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-020-4949-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study comparing 15 intervention project teams with 14 matched comparison institutions; paired-sample t-tests were run in SPSS with a .05 significance level. The article states "the trained teams' indicators improved significantly as compared to the non-trained teams."

> "The p values for the intervention group comparing the; baseline to endline was (0.017), and (0.061) for the endline to post-training. For the comparison group, the p values for the baseline to the endline was (0.173), and (0.095) for the endline to post-training."
