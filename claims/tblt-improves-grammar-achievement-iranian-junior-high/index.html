Evidence¶

NamazianDost et al. 2017¶

Islam NamazianDost, Ghassem Bohloulzadeh, Abdolreza Pazhakh. (2017). The Effect of Task-Based Language Teaching on Motivation and Grammatical Achievement of EFL Junior High School Students. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.2p.243

q2 · i? · causal · r1

80 female pre-intermediate EFL students aged 16–17 at one junior high school in Ahvaz, Iran (selected from 100 by the Oxford Quick Placement Test), in two classes divided into experimental and control groups. Independent-samples t-test on posttest scores of 40 experimental and 40 control students after 12 sessions; the article reports "a p value of .000" and a mean difference of 5.20 points favoring the experimental group. No effect size is printed.

"Comparing the performances of the two groups on posttest, the results of t -test suggest a p value of .000 which is smaller than the significance level set for the study (0.05), hence a significance difference is suggested."
