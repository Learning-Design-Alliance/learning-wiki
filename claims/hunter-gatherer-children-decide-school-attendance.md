## Evidence

### Ninkova 2025

Ninkova, V., Hays, J., Lavi, N., Ali, A., Lopes da Silva Macedo, S., Davis, H. E., & Lew-Levy, S. (2025). Hunter-Gatherer Children at School: A View From the Global South. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241255614

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of 82 ethnographic publications on hunter-gatherer children and schooling reports that "children themselves who decide whether they will participate in schooling," and that many parents do not force attendance despite continuous pressure from school authorities and governments. No effect sizes are printed; the support is a narrative synthesis of qualitative studies.

> "The most obvious impact of this autonomy is that it is generally children themselves who decide whether they will participate in schooling—or not. This is contrary to many legal frameworks, in which laws dictate that parents ensure that their children attend."
