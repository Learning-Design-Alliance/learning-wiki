## Evidence

### Aljadani Ahmed H. 2021

Aljadani Ahmed H., Alsolami Ahmed, Almehmadi Samiah, Alhuwaydi Ahmed, Fathuldeen Anas. (2021). Epidemiology of Burnout and Its Association with Academic Performance Among Medical Students at Hail University, Saudi Arabia. Sultan Qaboos University Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.18295/squmj.2021.21.02.011

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The multivariate logistic regression analysis of the 218-student cross-sectional survey shows the GPA 3.51–4.00 group was "considerably less prone to burnout compared to students with GPAs ≤2.0", with AOR = 0.17 (95% CI 0.03–0.91, P = 0.039).

> "Students with GPAs ranging from 3.51–4.00 (44%) were considerably less prone to burnout compared to students with GPAs ≤2.0 (AOR = 0.17, 95% CI = 0.03–0.91; P = 0.039)."
