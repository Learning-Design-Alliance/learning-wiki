Murray, D. W., Rabiner, D. L., & Carrig, M. M. (2014). Grade Level Effects of the Incredible Years Teacher Training Program on Emotion Regulation and Attention. SREE Spring Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562822

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors' summary interpretation of the moderated findings across emotion regulation, prosocial behavior, and reading outcomes in the randomized trial, contrasting with the counter-to-hypothesis second-grade findings.

> "In general, intervention effects for students were moderated by grade level and free/reduced lunch status in the direction predicted. That is, significant benefit was obtained for kindergarteners and 1st graders on social-emotional outcomes and for 1st graders in reading."
