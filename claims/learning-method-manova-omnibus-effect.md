MANOVA summary at 5% error level (Table 8) testing learning method on conceptual understanding and independence learning together. The article reports F = 324.865, Sig. 0.000, and R2 = 0.917, stating "91.7% both of conceptual understanding and students’ independent learning are influenced by learning methods".
