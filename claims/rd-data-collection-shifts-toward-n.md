# After instruction in a traditional lab course, point-like responses on the RD (data collection) probe decreased significantly without a significant increase in set-like responses, shifting students toward the N classification

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On the RD probe, the significant post-instruction decrease in point-like responses was not accompanied by a significant increase in set-like responses, so more responses were coded N after instruction. [→ H. J. Lewandowski 2018](#h-j-lewandowski-2018)

## Evidence

### H. J. Lewandowski 2018

H. J. Lewandowski, Robert Hobbs, Jacob T. Stanley, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, and Benjamin Pollard (2018). Student reasoning about measurement uncertainty in an introductory lab course. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.056](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.056)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post coding of open-ended RD probe responses from the PMQ administered in PHYS 1140. The article reports "the significant decrease in P responses" was not matched by a significant S increase; no effect size is printed. RD showed low fractions of both S and P with plurality N.

> "the signiﬁcant decrease in P responses after instruction was not accompanied by a signiﬁcant increase in S responses, meaning that a larger fraction of student responses fell into the N paradigm after instruction than before."
