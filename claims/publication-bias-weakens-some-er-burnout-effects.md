# Publication-bias adjustments render the ER difficulties–lack of efficacy and adaptive ER–emotional exhaustion effects non-significant

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Trim-and-fill adjustment reduced the ER difficulties–lack of efficacy effect to non-significance (r = 0.08) and the adaptive ER–emotional exhaustion effect to r = 0.00. [→ Ioana Alexandra Iuga · Oana Alexandra David 2024](#ioana-alexandra-iuga-oana-alexandra-david-2024)

## Evidence

### Ioana Alexandra Iuga · Oana Alexandra David 2024

Ioana Alexandra Iuga · Oana Alexandra David. (2024). Emotion Regulation and Academic Burnout Among Youth: a Quantitative Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09930-w

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Publication-bias assessment via funnel plots, Duval and Tweedie's trim-and-fill, and Egger's tests across outcomes; results varied, with no bias detected for adaptive ER–school burnout or the ER difficulties–overall burnout, cynicism, and exhaustion analyses.

> "For ER difficulties and lack of efficacy, the adjustment for missing studies in the trim-and-fill analysis led to a non-significant effect. Additionally, adaptive ER and emotional exhaustion displayed a similar pattern with the trim-and-fill method leading to a lower, non-significant effect size."
