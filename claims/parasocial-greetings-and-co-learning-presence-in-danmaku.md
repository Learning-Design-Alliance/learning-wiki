# Danmaku learners engage in parasocial greetings and compliments to the instructor and seek co-learning presence, phenomena absent on the discussion board

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Danmaku learners greeted and complimented the pre-recorded instructor one-way, generating co-monologue streams, and used four strategies to establish co-learning presence that received tens of peer replies; neither pattern was found on the discussion board. [→ Bo Yang 2024](#bo-yang-2024)

## Evidence

### Bo Yang 2024

Bo Yang. (2024). Combining Danmaku and Discussion Boards: Toward A Scalable and Sociable Environment for Mass Collaboration in MOOCs. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09426-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of socio-emotional behaviors in danmaku found learners "engaged in parasocial interactions" with the unresponsive instructor, cultivating co-monologue and co-learning-presence phenomena; a question such as "Is anyone watching this lecture?" could quickly receive tens of answers in a row, creating a co-learning presence.

> "Learners engaged in parasocial interactions by offering such one-way greetings and compliments to the instructor."
