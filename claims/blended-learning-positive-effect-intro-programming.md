# Blended learning shows a positive effect on teaching introductory programming and is perceived to support learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The vast majority of reviewed studies report that blended learning has a positive effect on teaching introductory programming, with students identifying blended courses as effectively supporting learning. [→ Ali Alammary 2019](#ali-alammary-2019)

## Evidence

### Ali Alammary 2019

Ali Alammary. (2019). Blended learning models for introductory programming courses: A systematic review. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0221765

`q2 · i?`

Narrative synthesis of 38 studies in higher education introductory programming contexts. The review reports that "the vast majority of studies included in this review shows that blended learning has a positive effect on teaching"; no pooled effect size is printed.

> "The vast majority of studies included in this review shows that blended learning has a positive effect on teaching, with students also identifying that blended courses effectively support learning."
