Standardized path coefficients from the bootstrap SEM in Mplus 8.3 with gender, age, school, and grade as controls. The study reports teacher support was "negatively associated with mathematics anxiety (β = − 0.428)" and "positively associated with learning engagement (β = 0.214)".
