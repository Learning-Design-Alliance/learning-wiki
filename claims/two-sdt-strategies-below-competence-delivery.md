## Evidence

### Hurley DA 2019

Hurley DA, Keogh A, Mc Ardle D, Hall AM, Richmond H, Guerin S, Magdalinski T, Matthews J. (2019). Evaluation of an E-Learning Training Program to Support Implementation of a Group-Based, Theory-Driven, Self-Management Intervention For Osteoarthritis and Low-Back Pain: Pre-Post Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/11123

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Intervention-specific SOLAS scale (7-point Likert) ratings of audio-recorded Session 4 delivery across five sites. The article reports "only 2 of the 15 self-determination theory strategies were delivered below the competence level"; medians are descriptive with no effect size.

> "The median scores of only 2 of the 15 self-determination theory strategies were delivered below the competence level during both the education and exercise components of the intervention (ie, use support and encouragement rather than pressurising behaviors and acknowledge patient’s feelings and perspectives)."
