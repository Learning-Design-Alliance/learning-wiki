University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-delivering-dream-college-graduation

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The report identifies a policy-research tension: CPS retained test-score benchmarks (SAT 1010, previously ACT 20) despite evidence favoring GPA. Even a 4.0-GPA graduate has just a 50 percent chance of enrolling in and graduating from a four-year college in four years; a 26-ACT graduate has a one-in-three chance.

> "there is a substantial disconnect between a reliance on standardized test scores as indicators of college readiness, and the research evidence that a student’s GPA is a better predictor of college graduation than test scores."
