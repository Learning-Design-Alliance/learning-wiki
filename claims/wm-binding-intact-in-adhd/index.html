Evidence¶

Ortega 2020¶

Ortega, R., López, V., Carrasco, X., Escobar, M. J., García, A. M., Parra, M. A. & Aboitiz, F. (2020). Neurocognitive mechanisms underlying working memory encoding and retrieval in Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. Scientific Reports 10:7771. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-64678-x

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Behavioral and ERP comparisons of Shape-Only vs Color-Shape conditions in the 18-vs-18 adolescent sample: "we did not find significant difference between groups regarding the binding condition", neither behaviorally nor electrophysiologically; no effect size is printed for the null interactions.

"In the current work we did not find significant difference between groups regarding the binding condition (Shape-Only Vs. Color-Shape), neither behaviorally nor electrophysiologically. Even though, the Color-Shape condition resulted in larger reaction times in all participants."

Related Claims¶

  • Adolescents with ADHD respond more slowly than non-ADHD peers across all conditions, and color-shape binding slows responses in both groups
  • Adolescents with ADHD show poorer accuracy than non-ADHD peers on all conditions of a visual short-term memory task, with the largest drop on trials requiring WM updating — related
  • Retrieval P3 discriminates Same from Different trials only in non-ADHD adolescents, indicating a failure of post-selection prioritization in ADHD — reports the opposite
  • Encoding P3 amplitude is reduced in ADHD adolescents during visual WM encoding, in both early and late time windows — reports the opposite
