Subgroup analysis of the pilot by self-reported experience level (beginner n= 9, intermediate n= 8, advanced n= 10). The article reports "Beginners showed the largest gains (+1.18)" and that all three subgroups reached p <0.001; no standardized effect size is printed for the subgroup contrast.
