# Presenting an evaluative test as having numerus clausus reduces short-term learning compared with presenting it as criterion-based, mediated by self-efficacy (Study 3)

## Evidence

### Sommet Nicolas 2013

Sommet Nicolas, Pulfrey Caroline, Butera Fabrizio. (2013). Did My M.D. Really Go to University to Learn? Detrimental Effects of Numerus Clausus on Self-Efficacy, Mastery Goals and Learning. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0084178

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Online experiment randomly assigning 212 polytechnical university students to a norm-based selection or criterion-based framing of a graph-theory test; the manipulation effect on learning had partial eta-squared η2 = .02, and "In the presence of numerus clausus, participants obtained a lower learning score".

> "Results showed a significant negative effect of the experimental variable, B = -0.62, F(1, 206) = 4.75, p < .04, η2 p = .02 (c path): In the presence of numerus clausus, participants obtained a lower learning score (M = 21.17, SE = 0.21) than in the absence of numerus clausus (M = 21.79, SE = 0.19)."

### Sommet Nicolas 2013 (2)

Sommet Nicolas, Pulfrey Caroline, Butera Fabrizio. (2013). Did My M.D. Really Go to University to Learn? Detrimental Effects of Numerus Clausus on Self-Efficacy, Mastery Goals and Learning. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0084178

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

Mediation analysis of the same experiment: the manipulation effect on self-efficacy had partial eta-squared η2 = .02, and "in the presence of numerus clausus, participants reported less self-efficacy"; the direct effect on learning became marginal and the bootstrap indirect effect was significant.

> "the experimental variable negatively predicted self-efficacy, B = -0.44, F(1, 206) = 5.83, p < .02, η2 p = .02 (a path). In support of hypothesis 1, in the presence of numerus clausus, participants reported less self-efficacy (M = 3.92, SE = .14) than in the absence of numerus clausus (M = 4.37, SE = .12)."
