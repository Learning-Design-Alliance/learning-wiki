Evidence¶

Means 2009¶

Means, Toyama, Murphy, Bakia, & Jones. (2009). Evaluation of Evidence-Based Practices in Online Learning: A Meta-Analysis and Review of Online Learning Studies. U.S. Department of Education. https://www.ed.gov/about/offices/list/opepd/ppss/reports.html

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analyses within the meta-analysis testing 13 online learning practices against the 51 contrasts. No effect sizes are printed for the individual nonsignificant practice variables, so impact is unquantified here.

"Of those variables, (a) the use of a blended rather than a purely online approach and (b) the expansion of time on task for online learners were the only statistically significant influences on effectiveness. The other 11 online learning practice variables that were analyzed did not affect student learning significantly."
