## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental single-group pretest–posttest study (Hawsawi et al. 2025, Saudi Arabia) using a lecture plus an evacuation disaster simulation scenario of a hospital fire on the third floor with practical small-group simulation, in 119 fourth-year undergraduate nursing students. The review reports "p = 0.000"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Significant improvement pre–post in all subcategories: Time 1.24 ± 1.49 → 3.74 ± 1.92; Process 2.23 ± 1.05 → 3.13 ± 1.17; Transportation 3.26 ± 1.41 → 4.13 ± 1.28; overall correct answers increased 48.5% → 72.1%; highly significant improvement (p = 0.000) and post‐intervention total score reported as 39.66 (p = 0.000)."
