### Joachim Wirth 2020

Joachim Wirth, Ferdinand Stebner, Melanie Trypke, Corinna Schuster, & Detlev Leutner. (2020). An Interactive Layers Model of Self-Regulated Learning and Cognitive Load. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09568-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review attributing this to Koriat et al. (2002) on judgments of learning. The review reports the "underconfidence-with-practice" (UWP) effect arises because learning performance increases strongly with restudying while JOLs increase only slightly; no effect size is printed.

> "learners overestimate their learning after studying the material once but underestimate their learning when studying the learning material a second, third, or fourth time"
