## Evidence

### Ebrahim Sumayyah 2024

Ebrahim Sumayyah, Kinoo Suman Mewa, Naidoo Maheshwar, Van Wyk Jacqueline Marina. (2024). The use of household items to support online surgical knot-tying skills training: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05549-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of three virtual focus group discussions with a total of 20 purposively sampled students. Participants highlighted the self-paced, readily accessible activity that could be practiced with items available at home as advantages.

> "For most, this was the first time they were engaging with online skills training; it was a practical, experiential learning process and they valued the social aspects of learning via the Flipgrid application."
