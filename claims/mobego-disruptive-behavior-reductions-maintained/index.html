Evidence¶

Allison Bruhn 2022¶

Allison Bruhn, Joseph Wehby, Lesa Hoffman, Sara Estrapala, Ashley Rila, Eleanor Hancock, Alyssa Van Camp, Amanda Sheaffer, and Bailey Copeland. (2022). A Randomized Control Trial on the Effects of MoBeGo, a Self-Monitoring App for Challenging Behavior. Behavioral Disorders, 48(1), 29–43. https://doi.org/10.1177/01987429221093987

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Planned contrast in the same RCT's multilevel model of disruptive behavior. The article reports the intervention-to-postintervention change "did not differ by group and was not significant within the treatment group"; the baseline-to-postintervention group contrast was marginal (p = .052, d = −0.28).

"The change in DB from intervention to postintervention did not differ by group and was not significant within the treatment group, indicating maintenance of the intervention effect."
