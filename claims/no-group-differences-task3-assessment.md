## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the Task 3 (analyzing, evaluating, creating) assessment, differences between the four groups were not statistically significant, and the control group had the best performance. [→ Mahir Akgun 2025](#mahir-akgun-2025)

## Evidence

### Mahir Akgun 2025

Mahir Akgun, Sacip Toker. (2025). Short-Term Gains, Long-Term Gaps: The Impact of GenAI and Search Technologies on Retention. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-99264-3_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99264-3_6)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVA on Task 3 assessment scores in the randomized four-group experiment; F(3, 119) = .768, p = .514, no significant differences. Descriptively the control group scored highest (M = 46.0).

> "The univariate ANOV A results for Task 3 did not show significant differences between groups, F(3, 119) = .768, p = .514. The results indicated that the control group had the best performance, while the E-textbook, Google, and ChatGPT groups had lower scores compared to the control group (see Figure 4)."
