## Evidence

### Binisha Marahatta and Matthew D. Sievert 2026

Binisha Marahatta and Matthew D. Sievert. (2026). Adapting Public Exams for Introductory Physics: A Mixed-Methods Investigation of Student Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.03847](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.03847)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of grade and engagement data from 42 consenting students in one introductory algebra-based physics course, with slope uncertainty from leave-one-out refitting. The authors report a "statistically significant positive correlation" and compute p= 3.1×10 −7 from the slope uncertainty; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "In this way, we obtain a slope and associated uncertainty m= 0.142±0.028, which suggests a statistically significant positive correlation between participation rate in the public exams revision process and exam grades."
