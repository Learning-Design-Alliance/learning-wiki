## Evidence

### Rae 2017

Rae, M. G., & McCarthy, M. (2017). The impact of vodcast utilisation upon student learning of Physiology by first year graduate to entry medicine students. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 17(2), 1-23. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v17i2.21125

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same quasi-experimental design as the CA analysis but using the EOM exam taken one week after module completion. No significant difference was detected; the article reports no effect size.

> "The results, illustrated in figure 4, show no significant difference between the results obtained by the 13-14 versus the 12-13 class in the 16 identical questions posed in each respective GM1001 EOM exam (65.2±3.0% in 12 -13 vs 64.9±2.8% in 13 -14, n=16, P=0.5, Student's paired t-test)."
