## Evidence

### Ming Fai Pang 2011

Ming Fai Pang, Lo Mun Ling. (2011). Learning study: helping teachers to use theory, develop professionally, and produce new knowledge to be shared. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-011-9191-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Post-lesson diagnostic test in class 4A of the secondary four chemistry learning study (VITAL project). The lesson 'basically reﬂects generally accepted science teaching strategies' with active experimentation, yet the counter-intuitive volume aspect was not learned; the authors explain this via the pattern of variation enacted.

> "However, they did badly in part C (which tested their understanding of the effect of volume on the rate) as the number of students who answered this item correctly in the post-test fell by 6% in com- parison with the pre-test."
