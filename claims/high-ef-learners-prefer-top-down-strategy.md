## Evidence

### Huang 2026

Huang, S. C.-F., Chen, Z.-H., Lin, Y.-T., & Yeh, M. K. (2026). Bridging strategy gaps in programming: the role of visual scaffolding for learners with low cognitive ability. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10684-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within-group paired-sample t-tests on eye-tracking data of 18 high-EF learners during three programming tasks. The article reports "fixation duration (t (17) = 9.689, p <.01, Hedges' g = 2.175" and comparable large effects for fixation count (g = 1.800) and visit count (g = 2.173).

> "the use of the top-down tracking window is significantly higher than that of the bottom-up window across all measures: fixation duration (t (17) = 9.689, p <.01, Hedges' g = 2.175"
