## Evidence

### Wang Guoxia 2025

Wang Guoxia, Zhang Qin, Wang Lumeng, Liu Yi. (2025). The impact of teachers’ autonomy support on students’ academic performance from the perspective of hyperscanning: the mediating role of autonomy need satisfaction. Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsaf115

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Time-course analysis aggregating INS per second across the 878-second teaching stage for the two style groups. The article reports that "from the 206th second, time-cumulative INS significantly increased in the teacher autonomy support group compared to the teacher control group, P < .05"; no effect size is printed.

> "Time course analysis showed that, from the 206th second, time-cumulative INS significantly increased in the teacher autonomy support group compared to the teacher control group, P < .05 (Fig. 6a)."

### Wang Guoxia 2025 (2)

Wang Guoxia, Zhang Qin, Wang Lumeng, Liu Yi. (2025). The impact of teachers’ autonomy support on students’ academic performance from the perspective of hyperscanning: the mediating role of autonomy need satisfaction. Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsaf115

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correlations between time-cumulative lPFC INS at each time point and behavioral outcomes during the teaching stage. The article reports positive correlations "with autonomy need satisfaction from the 384th second (P < .05), and positive emotions from the 534th second (P < 0.05)"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "time-cumulative INS in lPFC was positively correlated with autonomy need satisfaction from the 384th second (P < .05), and positive emotions from the 534th second (P < 0.05; Fig. 6b)."
