## Evidence

### Haleem Abdul 2016

Haleem Abdul, Khan Muhammad Khalil, Sufia Shamta, Chaudhry Saima, Siddiqui Muhammad Irfanullah, Khan Ayyaz Ali. (2016). The role of repetition and reinforcement in school-based oral health education-a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-2676-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized controlled trial of 935 adolescents in Karachi, Pakistan. GEE analysis showed oral health knowledge composite scores rose significantly after six months of repeated and reinforced OHE (p < 0.001) across dentist-led, teacher-led, and peer-led strategies.

> "The OHK, OHB and OHS indices increased significantly 6 months after RR-OHE than the initial scores (p < 0.001) irrespective of OHE strategy."

### Haleem Abdul 2016 (2)

Haleem Abdul, Khan Muhammad Khalil, Sufia Shamta, Chaudhry Saima, Siddiqui Muhammad Irfanullah, Khan Ayyaz Ali. (2016). The role of repetition and reinforcement in school-based oral health education-a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-2676-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of knowledge retention between intervention types using baseline as reference. RR-OHE knowledge gains were 168% sustainable over six months versus 24% for one-time OHE.

> "the sustainability of the knowledge gain resulting from RR-OHE was 168 % higher compared to 24 % sustainability in case of one-time OHE over the period of six-month"
