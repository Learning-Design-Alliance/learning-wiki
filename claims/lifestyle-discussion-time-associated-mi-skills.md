## Evidence

### Jansink Renate 2013

Jansink Renate, Braspenning Jozé, Laurant Miranda, Keizer Ellen, Elwyn Glyn, Weijden Trudy van der, Grol Richard. (2013). Minimal improvement of nurses’ motivational interviewing skills in routine diabetes care one year after training: a cluster randomized trial. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-14-44

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel regression on one-year follow-up consultation data (n = 175 consultations) linking consultation characteristics to MI skill ratings. The article reports B-estimate=4.28 (s.d.=0.53) for lifestyle discussion time on the additional MI items, and Table 3 reports B=8.98 (s.d.=0.71, p=0.000) for the BECCI.

> "The time lifestyle was discussed was also positively associated with the additional MI items (B-estimate= 4.28; s.d.= 0.53) as well as the patients readiness to change (B-estimate= 1.41; s.d.= 0.58)."
