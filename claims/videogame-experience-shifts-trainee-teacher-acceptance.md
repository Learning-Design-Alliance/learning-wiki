## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After experimenting with the videogame Lemmings, most students fully agreed that the game helps develop the curriculum in a crosswise manner, whereas most had disagreed before. [→ Marín Díaz 2014](#marin-diaz-2014)

## Evidence

### Marín Díaz 2014

Marín Díaz, V.; Martín-Párraga, J. (2014). Can videogames be used to develop the infant stage educational curriculum? New Approaches in Educational Research, 3(1), 20-25. https://doi.org/10.7821/naer.3.1.20-25

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post survey of 65 students in the Media Education course at the University of Córdoba. After the Lemmings experience, "76% of students fully agree with the idea that this video game helps to develop the curriculum in a crosswise manner", reversing pre-test disagreement.

> "Before using the videogame, 89% of students claimed to completely disagree with item 1, 6% of them said that they were indifferent, and the remaining 5% agreed with it. After experiencing the game, the results of the second questionnaire (post-test) show that 76% of students fully agree with the idea that this video game helps to develop the curriculum in a crosswise manner"
