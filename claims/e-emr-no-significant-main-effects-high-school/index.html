Evidence¶

Duong et al. 2022¶

Mylien T. Duong, Larissa M. Gaias, Eric Brown, Sharon Kiche, Lillian Nguyen, Catherine M. Corbin, Cassandra J. Chandler, Joanne J. Buntain-Ricklefs, Clayton R. Cook. (2022). A Cluster Randomized Pilot Trial of the Equity-Explicit Establish-Maintain-Restore Program among High School Teachers and Students. School Mental Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12310-022-09516-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Cluster-randomized pilot trial with 94 ninth-grade teachers and 412 students in six high schools; longitudinal models controlling for baseline and FRL status tested E-EMR effects on student self-report outcomes. The article reports that "none of the main effects reached statistical significance".

"Table 2 summarizes the main effects of E-EMR and average scores at pre- and post-intervention for students in E-EMR and control conditions separately. As shown, none of the main effects reached statistical significance, although changes were in the expected direction."
