## Evidence

### McLin 2013

McLin, K. M., Cominsky, L. R., Metevier, A. J., Coble, K., Bailey, J. M. (2013). Using the Big Ideas in Cosmology to Teach College Students. 4th Fermi Symposium, Monterey, CA. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1303.1768](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1303.1768)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Observational finding from the five-semester studies at the urban minority-serving institution, where students' poor math skills were seen as a hinderance to mastering new cosmological concepts. The article reports that "helping students attain these, especially in terms of working with graphical data and using simple proportional relationships to guide their reasoning, can pay large dividends over the course of the semester."
