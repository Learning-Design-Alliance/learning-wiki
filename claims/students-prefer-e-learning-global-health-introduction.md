## Evidence

### Gruner D 2015

Gruner D, Pottie K, Archibald D, Allison J, Sabourin V, Belcaid I, McCarthy A, Brindamour M, Augustincic Polec L, Duke P. (2015). Introducing global health into the undergraduate medical school curriculum using an e-learning program: a mixed method pilot study. BMC Medical Education 15:142. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0421-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of four post-intervention focus groups (24 students, 14 e-learning, 10 PDF) coded in NVivo-9 identified facilitators including "learning style", personal interest, ease of access, interactive content and curriculum integration. This is a qualitative pilot finding with no effect size.

> "Participants highlighted their preference for e-learning to introduce new global health knowledge and as a repository of resources. They also mentioned personal interest in global health, online convenience and integration into the curriculum as incentives to complete the e-learning."
