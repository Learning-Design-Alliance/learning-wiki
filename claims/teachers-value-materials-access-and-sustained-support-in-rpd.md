### Talafian 2025

Talafian, H., Lundsgaard, M., Mahmood, M., Shafer, D., Stelzer, T., & Kuo, E. (2025). Responsive professional development: A facilitation approach for teachers' development in a physics teaching community of practice. Teaching and Teacher Education, 153, 104812. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tate.2024.104812

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenological interview study with 13 high school physics teachers from the first two IPaSS cohorts. Coding of interview segments showed "access to instructional materials (22 segments from 11 teachers)" was most frequent, with sustained support second. These codes were not exclusive to a particular group of teachers.

> "Teachers most frequently remarked on the access to instructional materials (22 segments from 11 teachers) provided by the program and sustained support from the university team (12 segments from 5 teachers)."
