Li, H. and Zhang, W. (2026). Empowerment over enforcement: unpacking the psychological drivers of AI-assisted deep revision in EFL writing. Front. Psychol. 17:1871022. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1871022

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural model assessment in a cross-sectional survey of 327 Chinese university EFL learners analyzed with PLS-SEM; the APL→PC path was significant (β = 0.636, t = 24.387, p < 0.001), with APL also showing "substantial positive associations with Intrinsic Motivation (IM)" (β = 0.439) and Psychological Safety (β = 0.657).

> "AI Prompting Literacy (APL) and Perceived Competence (PC) were found to be significantly positively correlated, supporting hypothesis H1 (β = 0.636, t = 24.387, p < 0.001)."
