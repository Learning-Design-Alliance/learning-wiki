## Evidence

### Yun Heoncheol 2021

Yun Heoncheol, Kim Seon, Chung Eun-Kyung. (2021). Exploring the structural relationships between medical students’ motivational regulation, cognitive learning, and academic performance in online learning. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2021.193

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling of survey data from 510 pre-medical and medical students in partial online education; the structural model fit adequately (CFI=0.916, RMSEA=0.044). The path from MRS to CLS was "strongly influential to CLS (β=0.802, p<0.001)".

> "First, MRS is strongly influential to CLS (β=0.802, p<0.001). That is, the more frequently students use MRSs, the more rigorously student tend to use CLSs."
