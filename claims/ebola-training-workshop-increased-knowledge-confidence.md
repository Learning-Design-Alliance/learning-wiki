## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre–post training evaluation study without control group (Carlos et al. 2015, Philippines) of a 3-day workshop collaboratively developed by DOH and WHO, consisting of 18 lectures and 10 practical/small-group sessions including repeated PPE donning/doffing practice, in 364 health professionals. The review reports "p < 0.009" and "p = 0.018"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Knowledge increased significantly (p < 0.009) from median 7/10 (IQR 6–8) pre‐workshop to median 9/10 (IQR 8–9) post‐workshop. The proportion answering all 10 questions correctly increased from 2.8% to 22.5%. Confidence in safely caring for an EVD patient increased significantly (p = 0.018); post‐training 87.2% agreed/strongly agreed they could be safe caring for an EVD patient, and 96.4% reported feeling more prepared to screen/manage EVD."
