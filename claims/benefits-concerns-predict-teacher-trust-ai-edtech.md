## Evidence

### Viberg 2025

Viberg, Olga; Cukurova, Mutlu; Feldman-Maggor, Yael; Alexandron, Giora; Shirai, Shizuka; Kanemune, Susumu; Wasson, Barbara; Tømte, Cathrine; Spikol, Daniel; Milrad, Marcelo; Coelho, Raquel; Kizilcec, René F. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey regression (Model 1) of 508 K-12 teachers in six countries, with trust regressed on its two antecedents. The article reports the two antecedents are "significant predictors of trust in AI-EdTech" explaining 49% of variance; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Model 1 confirmed that perceived benefits of AI-EdTech and concerns about AI-EdTech are indeed significant predictors of trust in AI-EdTech, and they explain 49% of variance in trust."
