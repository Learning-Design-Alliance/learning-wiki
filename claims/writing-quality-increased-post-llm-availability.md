`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational regression analysis of LMS forum submissions at a four-year public US university, using linear models with student, course, and year-of-study fixed effects and LDA assignment-topic controls. Estimates are in standard deviations; Table S1 reports Phase 2 coefficients of 0.162, 0.129, and 0.086.

> "Figure 2 shows that across the three language proficiency indices, student writing quality was slightly higher (0.009 to 0.039 standard deviation) than the pre-LLM era in Phase 1 and substantially higher (0.086 to 0.162 standard deviation) in Phase 2"
