`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Case study of Elliott Elementary (Lincoln, Nebraska), a Title I PK–5 school where just over 90% of students participate in free or reduced price lunch. As a Cohort 1 grantee under the SIG transformation model, the long-time principal was replaced; despite leadership and staff turnover, Elliott built a culture of achievement sustained after the grant.

> "Elliott used SIG funds as an opportunity to reset its course, use data to improve student performance, and launch a pre-kindergarten (PK) program to better meet students' needs."
