Methodological quality assessment across the included evaluations used the Cochrane EPOC 2 tool for RCTs and ROBINS-I for quasi-experimental designs; the review reports that "randomised studies generally exhibited lower risk of bias" while quasi-experimental studies varied more in quality.

> "Risk of bias was assessed using the Cochrane EPOC 2 tool (Higgins and Green 2011) for randomised controlled trials and ROBINS‐I (Sterne et al. 2016) for quasi‐experimental designs. Randomised studies generally exhibited lower risk of bias, while quasi‐experimental studies showed greater variability in quality."
