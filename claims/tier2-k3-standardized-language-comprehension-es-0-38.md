## Evidence

### Wanzek 2016

Wanzek, J., Vaughn, S., Scammacca, N., Gatlin, B., Walker, M. A., & Capin, P. (2016). Meta-analyses of the effects of tier 2 type reading interventions in grades K-3. Educational Psychology Review, 28, 551-576. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED579894

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 31 studies with standardized language/comprehension outcomes, most assessing reading comprehension. The article reports "a mean effect size estimate of 0.38," described as "a small to moderate positive effect of intervention on students' language/comprehension."

> "The 31 studies that included standardized measures of language and comprehension had a mean effect size estimate of 0.38 (p = 0.005; 95% CI = 0.25, 0.51), indicating a small to moderate positive effect of intervention on students' language/comprehension."
