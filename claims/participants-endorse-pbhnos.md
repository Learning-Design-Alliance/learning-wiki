## Evidence

### Ayşe Sert Çıbık 2016

Ayşe Sert Çıbık. (2016). The Effect of Project-Based History and Nature of Science Practices on the Change of Nature of Scientific Knowledge. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(4), 453-472. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1094587

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of a researcher-developed questionnaire administered to all 41 experimental-group participants after the post-test; 92.65% (f=38) answered Yes on benefit. Frequencies and percentages are descriptive.

> "The findings of the first item indicate that 92.65% of the pre -service teachers (f=38) replied as “Yes” and 7.3% (f=3) gave the response “Not sure”."

### Ayşe Sert Çıbık 2016 (2)

Ayşe Sert Çıbık. (2016). The Effect of Project-Based History and Nature of Science Practices on the Change of Nature of Scientific Knowledge. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(4), 453-472. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1094587

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Second item of the same post-treatment questionnaire; 73.14% (f=30) answered Yes, 12.18% No and 14.61% Not sure. Descriptive statistics only.

> "The responses given to the second item were “Yes” by 73.14% of the participants (f=30), “No” by 12.18% of them (f=5) and “Not sure” by 14.61% (f=6)."

### Ayşe Sert Çıbık 2016 (3)

Ayşe Sert Çıbık. (2016). The Effect of Project-Based History and Nature of Science Practices on the Change of Nature of Scientific Knowledge. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(4), 453-472. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1094587

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Third item of the same questionnaire; 78.03% (f=32) answered Yes. The article concludes project activities positively affected most participants' views of the course. Descriptive statistics only.

> "The responses were determined as “Yes” by 78.03% of the pre -service teachers (f=32), “No” by 14.62% of them (f=6) and lastly “Not sure” by 7.31% of the participants (f=3) for the third item."
