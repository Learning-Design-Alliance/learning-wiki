## Evidence

### Faure Theresa 2024

Faure Theresa, Weyers Imke, Voltmer Jan-Bennet, Westermann Jürgen, Voltmer Edgar. (2024). Test-reduced teaching for stimulation of intrinsic motivation (TRUST): a randomized controlled intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05640-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the longitudinal mixed model of the seven-item WIRKSTUD self-efficacy scale, the group effect was significant and IVFF exceeded CG; the article prints test statistics only, no effect size.

> "Likewise, self-efficacy was also significantly affected by study group (F(2, 166.57) = 3.76, p < 0.05, ICC(Model) = 0.646). Specifically, students in the IVFF had significantly higher scale scores than those in the CG (t(168) = -2.69, p < 0.05)."

### Faure Theresa 2024 (2)

Faure Theresa, Weyers Imke, Voltmer Jan-Bennet, Westermann Jürgen, Voltmer Edgar. (2024). Test-reduced teaching for stimulation of intrinsic motivation (TRUST): a randomized controlled intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05640-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The group-by-time interaction qualified the effect: post hoc tests showed the IVFF advantage from t2 to t6, while the stress-management group's higher scores were explicitly descriptive and untested.

> "The effect of study group differed between timepoints (F(16, 1111.56) = 3.44, p < 0.01): Perceived self-efficacy was significantly higher in the IVFF than in the CG at t2 - t6 (see also Additional file 2). Descriptively all scores of the IVSM were higher than those in the CG but these differences were not significant."
