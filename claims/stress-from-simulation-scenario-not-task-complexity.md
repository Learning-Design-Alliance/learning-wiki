`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Conclusions of the quasi-experimental before-after study, resting on the pattern that anxiety rose and cortisol output increased in both simulation types despite differing workload ratings. The authors state more studies are needed to confirm this trend.

> "The simulations were stressful for our students and according to our results both the psychological and the biological aspects were modified due to simply being in a simulation scenario than to the intrinsic complexity of tasks required to be performed."
