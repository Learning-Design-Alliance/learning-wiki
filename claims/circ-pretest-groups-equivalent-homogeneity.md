## Evidence

### Erlidawati & Syarfuni 2018

Erlidawati & Syarfuni. (2018). The Effect of Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition on Reading Comprehension of IAIN Lhokseumawe, Indonesia. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 9(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.9n.4p.153

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Homogeneity-of-mean test on the pre-test data of the two units: F = 3.959 with significance 0.051, so the null hypothesis was accepted and the groups were treated as having the same ability. Levene's test on post-test variances gave sig. 0.607 and Kolmogorov-Smirnov gave 0.056 and 0.157, both above 0.05.

> "Form the table above F score obtained 3.959 with significance of 0.051 and the significance ≥ is 0,050.The null hypothesis is accepted. It can be concluded that the data was homogeneous, and it meant two groups had the same ability."
