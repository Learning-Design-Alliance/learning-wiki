# Co-design sessions with seven teachers reveal four distinct aspects of how teachers want to spend, invest, avoid, and lack time for orchestration of social transitions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Thematic analysis of three co-design sessions with seven teachers (147 notes) grouped teacher concerns about social transitions into four aspects: how teachers want to spend their time, how they are willing to invest their time, how they do not want to spend their time, and for what they do not feel they have sufficient time. [→ Jennifer K. Olsen & Nikol Rummel & Vincent Aleven 2021](#jennifer-k-olsen-nikol-rummel-vincent-aleven-2021)

## Evidence

### Jennifer K. Olsen & Nikol Rummel & Vincent Aleven 2021

Jennifer K. Olsen & Nikol Rummel & Vincent Aleven. (2021). Designing for the co-Orchestration of Social Transitions between Individual, Small-Group and Whole-Class Learning in the Classroom. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 31:24–56. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00228-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis (affinity diagramming) of three two-hour co-design sessions with seven teachers, yielding 147 notes. The authors report that 'Our results grouped naturally into four different aspects' concerning teacher time use around social transitions. No effect sizes are printed for this qualitative finding.

> "Our results grouped naturally into four different aspects: how teachers wanted to spend their time, how teachers were willing to invest their time, how teachers did not want to spend their time, and for what they did not feel they had sufficient time."
