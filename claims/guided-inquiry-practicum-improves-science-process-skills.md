## Evidence

### Albertus Hariwangsa Panuluh 2022

Albertus Hariwangsa Panuluh. (2022). Improving the Science Process Skills of Physics Education Students by Using Guided Inquiry Practicum. Proceedings The 2017 International Conference on Research in Education - Sanata Dharma University. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2211.04006](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2211.04006)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired sample t-test analysis (SPSS 22) comparing Likert-scale questionnaire scores given after three practicums and after six practicums to the same 15 students; the mean rose "from 76,33 to 82,40 after posttest" and the article reports a significant improvement in Table 2 (Sig. .037, df 14).
