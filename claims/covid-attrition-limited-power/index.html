O'Reilly 2026¶

O'Reilly, F., Loeb, S., Farrell, A., & Scerif, G. (2026). Supporting Parents to Support Children: A U.K. Randomized Controlled Trial Testing a Text Message Intervention to Cultivate the Home Learning Environment. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000973

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Attrition analysis in the RCT: nationwide school closures in early 2021 prevented postintervention YARC collection for most of the randomized sample. The authors report missingness checks (logistic regressions on school and pupil characteristics, balance across arms) and state the retained sample was comparable to the original U.S. study.

"Due to COVID-19, postintervention data collection faced approximately 70% attrition on the primary outcome measure, resulting in a final analytical sample of n = 753."
