Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-and-principal-leadership-chicago-ongoing-analyses-preparation-programs

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of CCSR 2007 teacher survey essential supports measures comparing schools with teams of Fund-supported principals or assistant principals plus at least three NBCTs to other schools, controlling for student and school characteristics.

> "Regarding evidence of best practices, schools with teams did not look very different from other schools, after accounting for differences in student and school characteristics. Elementary school teams were stronger than other schools in only two areas: parent involvement and innovation. No differences were found at the high school level."
