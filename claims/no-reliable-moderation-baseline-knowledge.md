## Evidence

### Vangelis Tsiligkiris 2026

Vangelis Tsiligkiris. (2026). What students ask matters: LLM interaction depth, task quality, and immediate recall in higher education. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00617-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Moderation analysis adding Pre × Depth interaction terms to task-quality, post-test, and gain models for 22 students. The article states these patterns "should be interpreted as exploratory and require replication" given the small sample.

> "No interaction reached conventional significance (all p > .11). Simple-slopes probing suggested a pattern in which the estimated Depth effect on task quality was larger at lower baseline knowledge (Pre = −1 SD: β = 9.58, p = .050) than at the mean (β = 4.75, p = .104), and it was negligible at higher baseline knowledge (Pre = +1 SD: β = 0.00, p = .989)."
