# Students perceived facilitating conditions for GAI tool use to a greater extent than staff in the surveyed School

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Student respondents rated facilitating conditions supporting their use of GAI tools significantly higher than staff respondents (means 4.94 vs 4.15). [→ Kahn P 2026](#kahn-p-2026)

## Evidence

### Kahn P 2026

Kahn P, Carrigan M, Wyman I, Liu R, Smith P, Andonegui AR, Murtagh L and Song F (2026) Still emerging: understanding Generative AI use in Higher Education. Front. Educ. 11:1885253. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1885253

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey in one School of a research-intensive UK university (86 complete student responses, 45 complete staff responses) with validated Facilitating Conditions scales for both groups. The t-test reached p-value=0.001, with the student mean of 4.94 compared to 4.15 for staff, showing "students perceived to a greater extent than staff" that conditions were in place.

> "A t-test (with p-value=0.001) indicated that students perceived to a greater extent than staff that facilitating conditions to support their use of GAI tools were in place. The mean for student responses was 4.94 compared to 4.15 for staff."
