## Evidence

### Zhu Jing 2025

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects pooling of two studies (175 participants) using continuous satisfaction measures, reported in Table 5 with I² = 0. The pooled effect size was SMD = 0.94 (95% CI 0.63–1.26), favoring the BOPPPS group.

> "Two studies employed continuous variables, indicating a substantial improvement within the BOPPPS group concerning student satisfaction when compared to the control group (SMD 0.94, 95% CI 0.63–1.26; P < 0.001; Table 5)."

### Zhu Jing 2025 (2)

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects pooling of three studies (929 participants) reporting satisfaction as dichotomous variables, with effect size OR = 2.31 (95% CI 1.18–4.49) and I² = 0.42, favoring the BOPPPS group.

> "Three studies with dichotomous variables also showed statistically significant differences between the two groups (OR 2.31, 95% CI 1.18–4.49; P = 0.01; Table 5)."
