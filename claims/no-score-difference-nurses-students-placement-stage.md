## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mann–Whitney U tests found no significant difference in sub-total and global scores between nurses and students, nor between students early or late in placement. [→ Moore Malcolm 2017](#moore-malcolm-2017)

## Evidence

### Moore Malcolm 2017

Moore Malcolm, Roberts Chris, Newbury Jonathan, Crossley Jim. (2017). Am I getting an accurate picture: a tool to assess clinical handover in remote settings? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1067-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparisons in the cross-sectional study of 55 calls (37 by students, 18 by nurses). The study found "no significant difference in the means of sub-total scores and global scores" between the caller groups or placement stages; equivalence was not tested.

> "Medical students and nurses showed no significant difference in the means of sub-total scores and global scores. There was no significant difference in scores of medical students making calls early or late in their clinical placement."
