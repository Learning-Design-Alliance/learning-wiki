Lemke 2025¶

Lemke, M., Faulkner-Bond, M., LeBeau, B., Soo Ping Chow, A., & Quesen, S. (2025). Early literacy performance in Massachusetts: Results of ongoing analysis of literacy screening assessments (2023/24 data). WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677571

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Report's key findings summary of 2023/24 screening benchmark analysis covering 388,204 records from 137,159 students. It reports that "between 16 and 19 percent were significantly below benchmark in all three time periods" and that these rates have been consistent over 3 years of analysis.

"About one quarter to one third of students were identified as significantly below benchmark at any given time period (beginning, middle, or end of year), and between 16 and 19 percent were significantly below benchmark in all three time periods. These rates have been consistent over 3 years of analysis."
