## Evidence

### Abhilash Nair 2018

Abhilash Nair, Paul Irving, and Vashti Sawtelle. (2018). Using disciplinary perspectives to refine conceptions of the "real world". arXiv:1808.10429. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.062](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.062)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

This is the authors' interpretation (discussion of Interview 1 and 2 case data) of Beverly, a pre-med human biology major whose unfavorable survey-type responses could be read as believing physics has little relation to experience outside the classroom. They argue her statements are about "the practical ne-cessity of using physics," not about how much she values thinking about physics.

> "One issue is the difference between Beverly recognizing that physics can be used in the real world and her believing that she doesn’t need to bring in physics in those experiences."
