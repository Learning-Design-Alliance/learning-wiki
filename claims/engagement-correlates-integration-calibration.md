### Lucia Mason 2022

Lucia Mason, Christian Tarchi, Angelica Ronconi, Lucia Manzione, Natalia Latini, Ivar Bråten. (2022). Do medium and Context Matter when learning from multiple complementary Digital texts and videos? Instructional Science (2022) 50:653–679. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09591-8

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Zero-order correlational analysis on the full sample (descriptive statistics in Table 1). The authors interpret that "participants who invested more time and effort in the task were also more likely to gain more integrated understanding and be better calibrated"; these are correlational, not causal, findings.

> "integration of information was correlated positively with both processing time (r = .46, p < .01) and response length ( r = .63, p < .01), and negatively with calibration error (r = − .81, p < .01)."
