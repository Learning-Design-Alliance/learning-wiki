Quasi-experimental comparison of retention rates; Table 1 reports 21.05% DFW in the comparison group (n=57) vs 12.50% in the treatment group (n=32). The article attributes the non-significance to group sizes being too small to reach the needed t-score.

> "The results indicate an 8.55% difference in the percent of students who dropped, failed, or withdrew between the comparison course and the treatment, which, while not significant, shows some improvement in raw numbers, though the groups are to small to reach the needed t-score"
