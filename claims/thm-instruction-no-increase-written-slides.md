## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lautrette Alexandre et al 2020

Lautrette Alexandre et al. (2020). Impact of take-home messages written into slide presentations delivered during lectures on the retention of messages and the residents' knowledge: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02092-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Result from the randomized controlled study of 12 ICU lectures with lecturers randomized 1:1 two months before the module. The reported comparison of written THMs showed no significant difference, "77% (n = 20/26), 95% CI 56–91 vs 64% (n = 18/28), 95% CI 44–81".

> "The number of written THMs incorporated into the slide presentations did not differ between the intervention and control groups (77% (n = 20/26), 95% CI 56–91 vs 64% (n = 18/28), 95% CI 44–81, P = 0.31)."
