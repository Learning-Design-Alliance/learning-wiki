## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Teams with a higher JVA ratio, computed automatically from camera video, scored higher on the team post-test (r(30) = 0.50, p < 0.005, r2 = 0.25, large effect size). [→ Guo 2020](#guo-2020)

## Evidence

### Guo 2020

Guo, Z., & Barmaki, R. (2020). Deep neural networks for collaborative learning analytics: Evaluating team collaborations using student gaze point prediction. [doi:10.14742/ajet.6436](https://doi.org/10.14742/ajet.6436)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of 30 two-person teams in a collaborative muscle-painting lab activity, relating the automatically extracted JVA ratio to team post-test scores. The printed effect size is "𝑟(30) = 0.50" (a large correlation, |r| ≥ .37), with r2 = 0.25 and p < 0.005.

> "The Pearson correlation measure indicates a significant positive linear association (Zou et al., 2003) with a strong relationship between the JVA ratio and team post-test scores (𝑟(30) = 0.50, 𝐹(1,28) = 9.33, 𝑝 < 0.005, 𝑟2 = 0.25 (large effect size))."
