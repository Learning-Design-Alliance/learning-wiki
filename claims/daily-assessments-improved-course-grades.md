## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who took daily assessments earned higher grades than students who completed daily homework assignments. [→ Bush 2011](#bush-2011)

## Evidence

### Bush 2011

Bush, H. F., & Walsh, V. K. (2011). Today‟s Program Is Brought To You By The Letters – Debit And Credit And By The Number Income. American Journal of Business Education, 4(5). https://clutejournals.com/index.php/AJBE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental comparison of grade distributions between an experimental group (n=52, daily assessments) and a historical control group (n=151, daily homework) from the previous three years. The article reports that "the cumulative percentages of the experimental group outpace the grades earned by the control groups" with "a higher percentage in each of the categories" for A, B, and C.

> "In the highest three grade (A, B, and C) not only did the cumulative percentages of the experimental group outpace the grades earned by the control groups, there actual was a higher percentage in each of the categories."
