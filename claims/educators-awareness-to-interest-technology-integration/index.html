q2 · i? · design · r2

Interview with health and physical education teacher Mr. Castelli in the Awareness section: he said the way the studio "was presented to me on how I could use it, that was the motivating factor." He planned to infuse new technologies into his Blue Zones lesson.

"The way [the Learning Studio] was presented to me on how I could use it, that was the motivating factor. Because I can help some students engage in some things that they're interested in."

educator-vargas¶

Wortman, A., and Schiner, N. (2024, June). Access to powerful technology as a catalyst for career pathway engagement [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/217

q2 · i? · design · r2

Physics teacher Mr. Vargas recounted that a prior 3D modeling attempt ended when "the computer crashed and they had to restart the whole thing." Knowing the Z Series workstations could handle sophisticated graphics, he moved from awareness to interest in using the studio.

"We tried 3D modeling once and [the students] found that super enjoyable because the 3D modeling program we use is kind of like Minecraft. Essentially, they kind of build together."
