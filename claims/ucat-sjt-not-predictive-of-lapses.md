### Sahota G 2026

Sahota G, Tiffin PA, Smith D, Tyrrell E, Hampshire M, Taggar J. (2026). Situational judgement testing at different stages of undergraduate medical training and the risk of professionalism lapses: A cohort study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.70020

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariable and multivariable logistic regression in the 705-student cohort for the pre-admission UCAT SJT; the study reports OR 0.85 (p = 0.34) unadjusted and OR 0.92 (p = 0.66) adjusted, with no statistically significant prediction of lapses.

> "The UCAT SJT scores were not statistically significantly predictive of professional lapses, on univariable (OR 0.85, p = 0.34) or multivariable analysis (OR 0.92, p = 0.66)."
