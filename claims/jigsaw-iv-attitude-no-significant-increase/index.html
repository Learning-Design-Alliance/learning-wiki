Evidence¶

Esin Özdemir 2016¶

Esin Özdemir, Ali Arslan. (2016). The Effect of Self-regulated Jigsaw IV on University Students' Academic Achievements and Attitudes towards English Course. Journal of Education and Training Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i5.1453

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-test on attitude gain scores in the same quasi-experiment (t = -2.838, p = 0.008; experimental gain 5.00, control -6.84). Table 1 shows the experimental group's attitude increase was "slight and insignificant" (pre 80.40 to post 85.40, paired t p = 0.076).

"From the point of attitude gain scores; the significant difference between the means may seem as in favor of the experimental group, but the reason for this can be explained by the decrease in the control group students' attitude gain scores."
