### Weber U 2019

Weber U, Zapletal B, Base E, Hambrusch M, Ristl R, Mora B, Schaller B. (2019). Resident performance in basic perioperative transesophageal echocardiography: Comparing 3 teaching methods in a randomized controlled trial. Medicine 98(36):e17072. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000017072

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up test three months after training, comprising a multiple-choice test and a practical examination on the simulation mannequin; residents were asked not to access additional learning resources in between. No statistics or effect sizes are printed for the retention comparison.

> "This follow up demonstrated that both theoretical and practical test scores decreased only slightly and that the scores were still higher than the initial scores."
