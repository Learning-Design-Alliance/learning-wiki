# All three teaching methods (live, flipped video, interactive flipped) produced statistically significant pre-to-post knowledge gains in a single dental lecture

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Each of the three teaching methods showed significant improvement in post-intervention scores (p < 0.001), with mean improvements of 27.69 (live), 27.30 (flipped video), and 27.11 (interactive flipped). [→ Al-Karadsheh O 2025](#al-karadsheh-o-2025)

## Evidence

### Al-Karadsheh O 2025

Al-Karadsheh O, Abutayyem H, Saidi A, Shqaidef A. (2025). Knowledge acquisition and student perceptions of three teaching methods: a randomized trial of live, flipped, and interactive flipped classrooms. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07156-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 156 fourth-year dental students, 52 per group, paired t-tests within groups. The live lecture group's mean difference was 27.69 (95% CI 21.20–34.18, "t (51) = 8.56, p < 0.001"); the flipped video (27.30) and interactive flipped (27.11) groups were also significant at p < 0.001.

> "The Live Lecture Group had a mean difference of 27.69, with a 95% confidence interval between 21.20 and 34.18 (t (51) = 8.56, p < 0.001)."
