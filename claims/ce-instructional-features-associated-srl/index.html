Evidence¶

Yvonne M. Hemmler 2024¶

Yvonne M. Hemmler, Dirk Ifenthaler. (2024). Self-regulated learning strategies in continuing education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100629

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Narrative synthesis of the review's included primary studies on CE-related factors. The review reports that "Several instructional methods such as peer interaction... feedback... self-assessments, and flipped classroom were positively associated with SRL strategies," attributing each to the cited primary studies.

"Several instructional methods such as peer interaction (Haemer et al., 2017), feedback (Kittel et al., 2021), self-assessments (Janakiraman et al., 2018), and flipped classroom (Hosseini et al., 2020) were positively associated with SRL strategies in the included primary studies."
