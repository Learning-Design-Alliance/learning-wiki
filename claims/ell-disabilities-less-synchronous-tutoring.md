## Evidence

### Burch 2016

Burch, P., Good, A., & Heinrich, C. (2016). Improving Access to, Quality, and the Effectiveness of Digital Tutoring in K–12 Education. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373715592706

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-group mean comparison tests, cross-tabulations with chi-square tests, and logistic regression on Dallas ISD data (11,111 students matched with 35 digital providers, 2011–2012). Logistic regressions confirmed these associations: odds of Hispanic students receiving synchronous tutoring were 34% lower, 17% lower for ELLs, and 20% lower for students with disabilities.

> "In addition, these descriptive tests showed that English language learners (p < .0001) and stu- dents with disabilities (p = .0250), as well as stu- dents of Hispanic origin (p < .0001), were significantly less likely to receive OST tutoring in synchronous formats."
