## Evidence

### Briseño-Garzón 2016

Briseño-Garzón, A., Han, A., Birol, G., Bates, S., & Whitehead, L. (2016). Faculty perceptions of challenges and enablers of effective teaching in a large research-intensive university: Preliminary findings. Collected Essays on Learning and Teaching, 9, 133-144. https://celt.uwindsor.ca/ceelt/vol9/brisenogarzon

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey participants overwhelmingly endorsed active learning (72% strongly agree, 24% agree), but open-ended responses identified class size, lack of supporting technology, theatre-style lecture hall seating, and content coverage requirements as barriers to implementing active learning practices.

> "Participants also overwhelmingly agreed (72% strongly agree and 24% agree) with the importance of encouraging "students to be active participants in learning". However, in responses to open-ended questions, participants reported class size, lack of technology to support active learning, theatre style seating in lecture halls, and the amount of content required to be "covered" as barriers to accomplishing active learning"
