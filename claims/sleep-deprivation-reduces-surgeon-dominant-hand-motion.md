## Evidence

### Bereuter Jean-Paul 2026

Bereuter Jean-Paul, Fries Alexa, Grummt Isabell, Geissler Mark Enrik, Geissler Rona Berit, Schmidt Sofia, Buck Nathalie, Weiß Juliane, Krause-Jüttler Grit, Weitz Jürgen, Distler Marius, Oehme Florian, von Bechtolsheim Felix. (2026). Impact of acute sleep deprivation on laparoscopic performance: a prospective, randomized crossover trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12872-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Crossover trial surgeon cohort (n = 20) tested during a real combined day and night shift, measured with the ForceSense device. Dominant-hand speed fell markedly in all tasks, e.g. "control: 7.4 cm/s vs. sleep-deprived: 3.1 cm/s, p = 0.003" in peg transfer. No effect sizes printed.

> "surgeons showed a significant reduction under sleep-deprivation conditions in the peg transfer (control: 7.4 cm/s vs. sleep-deprived: 3.1 cm/s, p = 0.003), circle cutting (control: 7.1 cm/s vs. sleep-deprived: 3 cm/s, p < 0.001), and suture and knot (control: 5.2 cm/s vs. sleep-deprived: 2.8 cm/s, p < 0.001)"

### Bereuter Jean-Paul 2026 (2)

Bereuter Jean-Paul, Fries Alexa, Grummt Isabell, Geissler Mark Enrik, Geissler Rona Berit, Schmidt Sofia, Buck Nathalie, Weiß Juliane, Krause-Jüttler Grit, Weitz Jürgen, Distler Marius, Oehme Florian, von Bechtolsheim Felix. (2026). Impact of acute sleep deprivation on laparoscopic performance: a prospective, randomized crossover trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12872-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same surgeon-cohort motion analysis (Table 4). Dominant-hand motion volume decreased significantly in all tasks, while non-dominant volume rose in suture and knot ("control: 6.1 cm3 vs. sleep-deprived: 7.7 cm3, p = 0.022"). No effect sizes printed.

> "The volume of motion of the dominant hand reduced significantly under sleep deprivation in all tasks, whereas the volume of motion of the non-dominant was significantly higher under sleep deprivation in the suture and knot task (control: 6.1 cm3 vs. sleep-deprived: 7.7 cm3, p = 0.022)"
