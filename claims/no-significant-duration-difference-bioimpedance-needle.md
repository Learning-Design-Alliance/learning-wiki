## Evidence

### Lilja Helmiina 2024

Lilja Helmiina, Talvisara Maria, Eskola Vesa, Heikkilä Paula, Sievänen Harri, Palmu Sauli. (2024). Novice providers' success in performing lumbar puncture: a randomized controlled phantom study between a conventional spinal needle and a novel bioimpedance needle. BMC Medical Education 24:520. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05505-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled phantom trial, procedure duration was measured from needle penetration to first fluid drop or stopping; successful procedures averaged "3:51 (SD 3:43)" versus "1:59 (2:25)", a non-significant difference (p = 0.068).

> "The mean duration of a successful procedure was 3:51 (SD 3:43) with the conventional needle and 1:59 (2:25) with the bioimpedance needle (p = 0.068)."
