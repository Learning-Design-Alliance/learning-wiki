### Paul Justice 2026

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Supporting physics instructors to use a variety of evidence-based approaches to improve student learning: An example from quantum mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.00329](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.00329)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational comparison within class B after the ILM pedagogy: 12 of 19 students corrected their low-stakes posttest for up to half the lost quiz points; correctors averaged 85% and non-correctors 71% on the midterm exam question on the same concepts. No effect size or significance test is printed for this contrast.

> "After corrections, students who corrected their mistakes on the posttest obtained an average score of 85% on this problem. Meanwhile, students who  did not correct the ir posttest obtained an average score of 71%"
