## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Walsh Jason L 2018

Walsh Jason L, Harris Benjamin H L, Denny Paul, Smith Phil. (2018). Formative student-authored question bank: perceptions, question quality and association with summative performance. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2017-135018

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Observational correlation analysis of two undergraduate medical cohorts (n=603) over three academic years, correlating PeerWise activity frequencies with mean summative exam percentage. Writing showed the strongest correlation: "R=0.24, 0.13 and 0.15, respectively".

> "There were significant correlations between writing, answering and commenting frequency with summative examination performance (p<0.001, R=0.24, 0.13 and 0.15, respectively)."
