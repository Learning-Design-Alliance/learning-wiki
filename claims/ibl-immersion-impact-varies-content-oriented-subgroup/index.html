Evidence¶

Voet 2018¶

Voet, M. & De Wever, B. (2018). Effects of immersion in inquiry-based learning on student teachers' educational beliefs. Instructional Science, 46(3), 383-403. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-017-9439-8

q3 · i? · causal · r1

The multivariate multilevel model of difference scores found significant between-student variance in belief change, but no significant variance at the dyad or school levels. No effect size printed.

"the model indicates that, for all scales together, there is significant variance on the student level (X2=246.19, df=1, p<.001), but not on the dyad level (X2=1.46, df=1, p=.23), or school level (X2=0.82, df=1, p=.37)."
