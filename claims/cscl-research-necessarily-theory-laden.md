Theoretical argument in the editorial, grounded in philosophy of science: unreflective CSCL research inherits folk-theory assumptions, e.g. "what a subject says in an interview or a survey directly represents what the researcher was looking for".

> "The philosophy of science has shown convincingly that research is necessarily theory-laden. Those who do not reflect on their theoretical footing simply adopt the assumptions of common sense, known as ‘folk theories’."
