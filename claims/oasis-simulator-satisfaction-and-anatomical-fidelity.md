## Evidence

### Knobel Roxana 2018

Knobel Roxana, Volpato Lia Karina, Gervasi Liliam Cristini, Viergutz Raquel de Almeida, Trapani Alberto Júnior. (2018). A Simple, Reproducible and Low-cost Simulator for Teaching Surgical Techniques to Repair Obstetric Anal Sphincter Injuries. RBGO Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0038-1668527

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire responses from 20 residents and 4 expert obstetricians (one resident did not respond) in classroom simulations at two public hospitals in southern Brazil. Open-question content analysis found "greater security and confidence" most cited. No effect size printed.

> "All the participants were satisfied with the training and considered that the simulation improved their knowledge and skills for correction of severe perineal lacerations. In the open questions, greater security and confidence in the case of necessity to perform the suture were the most cited categories."

### Knobel Roxana 2018 (2)

Knobel Roxana, Volpato Lia Karina, Gervasi Liliam Cristini, Viergutz Raquel de Almeida, Trapani Alberto Júnior. (2018). A Simple, Reproducible and Low-cost Simulator for Teaching Surgical Techniques to Repair Obstetric Anal Sphincter Injuries. RBGO Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0038-1668527

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive questionnaire result on perceived anatomical fidelity of the low-cost model; participants noted the "thickness of the layers" was reliable but the model was "very simplified". No effect size printed.

> "The majority (78%) of the participants considered that the simulator was effective in replicating the anatomical structures, with inherent limitations to the model."
