## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Among 19 STEM students divided into experts and novices, model representations correlated strongly and significantly positively with expertise in electricity (r=0.63, p=0.004). [→ D. Sonntag 2021](#d-sonntag-2021)

## Evidence

### D. Sonntag 2021

D. Sonntag, O. Bodensiek. (2021). EYE-TRACKING-BASED DESIGN OF MIXED REALITY LEARNING ENVIRONMENTS IN STEM. Technische Universität Braunschweig. [doi:10.21125/inted.2021.1990](https://doi.org/10.21125/inted.2021.1990)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Qualitative study of N=19 university STEM students split by a paper-pencil conceptual knowledge test into 10 experts and 9 novices; comparing mental models of experts and novices yielded "a strong significant positive correlation (r=0.63, p=0.004)".

> "By comparing the mental models of experts and novices, respectively, we found a strong significant positive correlation (r=0.63, p=0.004)."
