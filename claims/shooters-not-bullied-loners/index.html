Evidence¶

Langman 2018¶

Langman, P., Petrosino, A., & Persson, H. (2018). Five misconceptions about school shootings. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/five-misconceptions-about-school-shootings/

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief reports Langman's research finding that many shooters were not bullied and bullied shooters rarely attacked their tormentors; the most common targets were administrators who had disciplined them, teachers who had failed them, and peers who had rejected them. Shooters often had friends, dated, and participated in school activities.

"Langman's research, for example, found that many of the shooters were not bullied, and those who were bullied rarely aimed their attacks at the particular students who had picked on them"
