The author's observational assertion about Misconception 3 (every particle has its own wave function), again noting no research documents its prevalence. He attributes reinforcement of it to teaching atomic physics in shell language, and recommends students work with entangled two-particle wave functions to combat it.

> "I routinely see a look of surprise on students' faces when they learn otherwise, and I have even encountered this misconception among PhD physicists."
