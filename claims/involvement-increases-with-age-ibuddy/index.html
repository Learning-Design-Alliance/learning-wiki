Descriptive analysis of the end-of-session survey (N = 978) across school levels, reported in Table 3 and Fig. 7. The article prints the means and SDs and offers an explanation: younger students own fewer personal devices and perceive third-party data use as "normal".

"The global average score for involvement is lower, while standard deviation is higher (M = 7.09; SD = 2,18), and personal involvement increases with the age of the participants, from 6.15 in the secondary school (11–12y) to 8.65 in higher education."
