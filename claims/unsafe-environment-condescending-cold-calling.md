### Yangqiuting Li and Chandralekha Singh 2023

Yangqiuting Li and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). The impact of perceived recognition by physics instructors on women's self-efficacy and interest. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020125)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of the 38 interviews (11 of 38 students). Students described fear of looking like a complete idiot at office hours, harsh responses to questions, and being called on randomly; the article adds that "cold calling may increase students' anxiety, particularly if they are from marginalized groups".

> "In our analysis, 29% of the interviewed female students reported experiences that were coded under the subtheme feeling that the physics learning environment is unsafe, which includes two codes: condescending or intimidating behavior and cold calling."
