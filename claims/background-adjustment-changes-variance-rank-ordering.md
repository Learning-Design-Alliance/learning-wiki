## Evidence

### Leckie 2023

Leckie, G., Parker, R., Goldstein, H., & Tilling, K. (2023). Mixed-Effects Location Scale Models for Joint Modeling School Value-Added Effects on the Mean and Variance of Student Achievement. [doi:10.3102/10769986231210808](https://doi.org/10.3102/10769986231210808)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Figure 6 scatterplots compare Model 7 (adjusting for student background) against Model 3 (ignoring background) school means and variances for 465 schools. The article reports both are "correlated 0.94 across the two models" but the rank ordering of schools whose social mix differs most markedly from the London-wide average still changes considerably. No standardised effect size is printed for this model-comparison correlation.

> "The plots show both the school means and the school variances are correlated 0.94 across the two models."
