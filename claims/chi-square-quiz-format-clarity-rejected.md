### Khandakar 2021

Khandakar, A., Chowdhury, M. E. H., Khalid, M. S., and Zorba, N. (2021). Case study of Innovative Teaching Practices and their Impact for Electrical Engineering Courses during COVID-19 Pandemic. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2107.00746](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2107.00746)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square analysis of the 33 students' survey responses at α = 5%, degrees of freedom = 4, critical value 9.49 (Table 3). The all-students statistic of 38.73 exceeded the critical value, so the null hypothesis of positive response was rejected for this question; the female statistic of 13.47 was also rejected, while the male statistic of 5 was not.
