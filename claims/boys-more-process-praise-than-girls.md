## Evidence

### Gunderson Elizabeth A. 2013

Gunderson Elizabeth A., Gripshover Sarah J., Romero Carissa, Dweck Carol S., Goldin-Meadow Susan, Levine Susan C. (2013). Parent Praise to 1-3 Year-Olds Predicts Children's Motivational Frameworks 5 Years Later. Child development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12064

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Gender comparison in the same observational sample (29 boys, 24 girls); the difference held as percent of total praise (t(51) = 3.20, p < .01), percent of total utterances (t(51) = 3.27), and raw count (t(51) = 3.35), and at each child age. Overall praise amounts did not differ by gender (p = .29). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Cumulatively across all time points, 24.4% of the praise that boys received was process praise (SD = 18.7%), whereas only 10.3% of the praise that girls received was process praise (SD = 7.9%)"
