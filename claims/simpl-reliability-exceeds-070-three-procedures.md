### Sun Ting 2024

Sun Ting, Kim Stella Yun, Smith Brigitte Kristin, Park Yoon Soo. (2024). Reliability test of a smartphone-based measurement tool for the United States general surgical trainees’ intraoperative performance using multivariate generalizability theory: a psychometric study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2024.21.26

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Decision-study analysis of retrospective national SIMPL data (2015–2023, 4,616 residents, 94 programs) estimated reliability coefficients via multivariate generalizability theory. The authors state that "with 3 procedures, the reliability of the assessment of surgical trainees’ intraoperative autonomy and performance exceeded 0.7". No effect size is printed.

> "Our finding revealed that with 3 procedures, the reliability of the assessment of surgical trainees’ intraoperative autonomy and performance exceeded 0.7, which, while not perfect, can be considered satisfactory."
