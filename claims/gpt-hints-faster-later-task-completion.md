## Evidence

### Maciej Pankiewicz & Ryan S. Baker 2023

Maciej Pankiewicz & Ryan S. Baker. (2023). Large Language Models (GPT) for automating feedback on programming assignments. Proceedings of the 31st International Conference on Computers in Education. [doi:10.58459/icce.2023.950](https://doi.org/10.58459/icce.2023.950)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Rank-based regression of task-solving times on the 8 tasks without GPT feedback, controlling for pretest score, in the randomized experiment; "the experimental group needing less time than the control group" was significant.

> "The group was a statistically significant predictor of the time needed to solve tasks, with the experimental group needing less time than the control group, t(48)=-2.25, p=0.029."

### Maciej Pankiewicz & Ryan S. Baker 2023 (2)

Maciej Pankiewicz & Ryan S. Baker. (2023). Large Language Models (GPT) for automating feedback on programming assignments. Proceedings of the 31st International Conference on Computers in Education. [doi:10.58459/icce.2023.950](https://doi.org/10.58459/icce.2023.950)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same rank-based regression's estimate for the no-GPT tasks: "students in the experimental group took, on average, 375 seconds (6.25 min.) less time than the control group".

> "For the set of these tasks results indicate that students in the experimental group took, on average, 375 seconds (6.25 min.) less time than the control group, controlling for the pretest score."

### Maciej Pankiewicz & Ryan S. Baker 2023 (3)

Maciej Pankiewicz & Ryan S. Baker. (2023). Large Language Models (GPT) for automating feedback on programming assignments. Proceedings of the 31st International Conference on Computers in Education. [doi:10.58459/icce.2023.950](https://doi.org/10.58459/icce.2023.950)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Rank-based regression on the 38 tasks with GPT feedback enabled, analyzing 1,784 successful submissions; "the group was not a statistically s ignificant predictor of the time needed to solve tasks".

> "For this set of tasks, the group was not a statistically s ignificant predictor of the time needed to solve tasks, t(89)=-0.24, p=0.811."
