## Evidence

### Alex Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Alex Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Should Students be Provided Diagrams or Asked to Draw Them While Solving Introductory Physics Problems?. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.04357](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.04357)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within the comparison group on problem 2, self-selected diagram-drawers outscored non-drawers descriptively, but the article reports no statistically significant difference; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "Even though the students from Group 3 who drew diagrams performed better than those who did not, we did not find a statistically significant difference between their scores."
