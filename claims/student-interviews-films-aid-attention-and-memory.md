### C. Efthimiou 2006

C. Efthimiou, R. Llewellyn, D. Maronde, T. Winningham. (2006). Physics in Films: An Assessment. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0609154](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0609154)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative group interviews conducted by the independent RITE office with students from several sections after course completion, covering their experience of the course and views of science. Students reported the films kept attention better than lecture and made concepts "easier to remember."

> "Along with several comments about the films keeping students' attention better than lecture and watching movies a preferable form of homework, a couple of students mention that learning the concepts as illustrated in films makes them easier to remember."
