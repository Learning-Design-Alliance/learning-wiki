# MR learners score significantly lower on nominal anatomy questions at immediate post-test than text-only and 3DM learners

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The three learning groups differed significantly on nominal question scores in anatomy test 1. [→ Wang Chris 2020](#wang-chris-2020)
`q3 i?` The MR group's average nominal score was significantly lower than both the text-only and 3DM groups. [→ Wang Chris 2020 (2)](#wang-chris-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Wang Chris 2020

Wang Chris, Daniel Ben Kei, Asil Mustafa, Khwaounjoo Prashanna, Cakmak Yusuf Ozgur. (2020). A Randomised Control Trial and Comparative Analysis of Multi-Dimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-62855-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised experiment with 52 second-year medical students assigned to text-only, 3DM, or MR groups; one-way ANOVA on anatomy test 1 found a difference "for only nominal type questions", F(2,49) = 13.722, p < 0.001; no effect size printed for this omnibus test.

> "A one-way ANOVA showed significant differences for only nominal type questions in anatomy test 1 scores when comparing the learning groups, F(2,49) = 13.722, p < 0.001."

### Wang Chris 2020 (2)

Wang Chris, Daniel Ben Kei, Asil Mustafa, Khwaounjoo Prashanna, Cakmak Yusuf Ozgur. (2020). A Randomised Control Trial and Comparative Analysis of Multi-Dimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-62855-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni post-hoc comparisons of anatomy test 1 nominal scores showed the MR group (M = 2.32, SD = 1.81) scored lower than the text-only group (M = 4.83, SD = 1.20, p < 0.001) and the 3DM group (M = 3.93, SD = 1.62, p = 0.008); no effect size printed.

> "Bonferroni post-hoc analyses indicated that the average number of correct answers was significantly lower in the MR group (M = 2.32, SD = 1.81) compared to the text-only group (M = 4.83, SD = 1.20, p < 0.001) and 3DM group (M = 3.93, SD = 1.62, p = 0.008)."
