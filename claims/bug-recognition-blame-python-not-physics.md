`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Screencast observation of the case-study group around 55 minutes into the geostationary-orbit problem, after an instructor intervention. The article states the simulation "clearly displayed the trajectory of the satellite falling into the Earth" rather than the expected geostationary orbit.

### Michael J. Obsniuk 2015 (2)

Michael J. Obsniuk, Paul W. Irving, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2015). A Case Study: Novel Group Interactions through Introductory Computational Physics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.055](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.055)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Audio-recorded group discussion during the bug-recognition phase of the case study. Student A said, "I don't think it's a problem with our understanding of physics, it's a problem with our understanding of Python," and the authors note the group instead seemed to place blame on the computational aspect of the task.

> "SA: That's the thing like, I don't think it's a problem with our understanding of physics, it's a problem with our understanding of Python."
