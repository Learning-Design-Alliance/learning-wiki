## Evidence

### Rikke Magnussen 2015

Rikke Magnussen, Sidse Damgaard Hansen, Tilo Planke, Jacob Friis Sherson. (2015). Games as a Platform for Student Participation in Authentic Scientific Research. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1511.01653](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1511.01653)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Written questionnaire administered after the Class 1 play sessions; only 7 of 20 students completed it. In that sample, "5 of the 7 of students in Class 1 answered “To participate in real scientific research”", while each other option was chosen by 2 students.

> "On the question “What is the most interesting aspect(s) of playing the game?” 5 of the 7 of students in Class 1 answered “To participate in real scientific research”, the other options where each chosen by 2 of the students, see figure 4."
