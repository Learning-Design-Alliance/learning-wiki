## Evidence

### Alvarez-Lopez F 2020

Alvarez-Lopez F, Maina MF, Saigí-Rubió F. (2020). Use of a Low-Cost Portable 3D Virtual Reality Gesture-Mediated Simulator for Training and Learning Basic Psychomotor Skills in Minimally Invasive Surgery: Development and Content Validity Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/17491

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Likert-scale survey of 30 participants (22 experienced surgeons, 8 residents) at three sites in Brazil, Spain, and Colombia, each performing the simulator tasks twice. The article reports "Likert score: 4.07-4.73" for fidelity to the criterion, with 73% rating ease of use as 5.

> "As per the survey conducted to assess the fidelity to the criterion, all 30 participants felt that most aspects of the simulator were adequately realistic and that it could be used as a tool for teaching basic psychomotor skills in laparoscopic surgery (Likert score: 4.07-4.73)."
