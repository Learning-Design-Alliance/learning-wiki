Administrative-data analysis (Figure 2) tracking reenrollment of K-7 students from the 47 closed schools. The report states the 94 percent rate was "comparable to previous years," when on average 94 percent of those schools' students continued in the district, though lower than other CPS elementary schools that year (96 percent).

"Of the students enrolled in grades K-7, 94 percent reenrolled in a CPS school by September 2013 ( see Figure 2 )."
