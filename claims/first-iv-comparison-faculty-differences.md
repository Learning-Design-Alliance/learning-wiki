# FIRST IV alumni differ from paired colleagues: lower knowledge-transmission beliefs and intentions, higher teaching-methods self-efficacy, and classroom infrastructure seen as more challenging

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` FIRST IV faculty scored significantly lower than comparison faculty on Knowledge Transmission beliefs (d = -0.599) and intentions (d = -0.526), with no difference on Learning Facilitation subscales. [→ Emery 2021](#emery-2021)
`q2 i2` FIRST IV faculty reported significantly higher self-efficacy for Teaching methods than comparison faculty (d = 0.561), with no differences on five other subscales. [→ Emery 2021 (2)](#emery-2021-2)
`q2 i2` FIRST IV faculty found classroom infrastructure significantly more challenging to implementing active learning than comparison faculty (d = 0.485). [→ Emery 2021 (3)](#emery-2021-3)

## Evidence

### Emery 2021

Emery, Nathan; Maher, Jessica Middlemis; Ebert-May, Diane. (2021). Environmental influences and individual characteristics that affect learner-centered teaching practices. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250760

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on the Teaching Beliefs and Intentions instrument for 40 paired faculty pairs. For Learning Facilitation there was no significant difference in Beliefs (P = 0.878) or Intentions (P = 0.312).

> "With regards to “Knowledge Transmission,” FIRST IV faculty had significantly lower scores for Beliefs (paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test, P = 0.0082, Cohen’s d = -0.599) and Intentions (paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test, P = 0.016, Cohen’s d = -0.526)."

### Emery 2021 (2)

Emery, Nathan; Maher, Jessica Middlemis; Ebert-May, Diane. (2021). Environmental influences and individual characteristics that affect learner-centered teaching practices. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250760

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on the self-efficacy instrument for the 40 pairs: Teaching methods, P = 0.0088, Cohen's d = 0.561, i.e. "significantly higher self-efficacy". No significant differences on the five other subscales.

> "FIRST IV faculty had significantly higher self-efficacy than comparison faculty with regards to “Teaching methods” (paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test, P = 0.0088, Cohen’s d = 0.561)."

### Emery 2021 (3)

Emery, Nathan; Maher, Jessica Middlemis; Ebert-May, Diane. (2021). Environmental influences and individual characteristics that affect learner-centered teaching practices. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250760

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Background-survey obstacle ratings on a 1–5 scale for the 40 pairs: "Classroom infrastructure" was the only obstacle with a significant group difference (P = 0.026, Cohen's d = 0.485).

> "FIRST IV faculty found this obstacle significantly more challenging than comparison faculty (paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test, P = 0.026, Cohen’s d = 0.485)."
