# Learners working with tree diagrams performed fewer manipulations in the simulations than learners in other conditions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The Diagram condition performed fewer manipulations than the Arithmetic and Text + Arithmetic conditions, with no difference in time-on-task. [→ Bas Kolloffel 2009](#bas-kolloffel-2009)

## Evidence

### Bas Kolloffel 2009

Bas Kolloffel, Tessa H. S. Eysink, Ton de Jong, Pascal Wilhelm. (2009). The effects of representational format on learning combinatorics from an interactive computer simulation. Instr Sci 37:503–517. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9056-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Log-based analysis of manipulations in the simulations; due to technical reasons data for 25 learners were unavailable and three outliers excluded, leaving 95 participants. The article reports "fewer manipulations" in the Diagram condition; no effect size printed. A separate ANOVA found no time-on-task differences, F(4,119) = 1.62, ns.

> "Post-hoc LSD analyses showed that participants in the Diagram condition performed fewer manipulations than participants in the Arithmetic condition (P\ .01) and participants in the Text + Arithmetic condition ( P\ .05)."
