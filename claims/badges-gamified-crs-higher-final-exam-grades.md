## Evidence

### López-Jiménez 2021

López-Jiménez, J. J., Fernández-Alemán, J. L., García-Berná, J. A., López González, L., González Sequeros, O., Nicolás Ros, J., Carrillo de Gea, J. M., Idri, A., & Toval, A. (2021). International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413210

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-hoc Mann–Whitney U comparisons of final exam grades across three academic-year cohorts (92 SIDRA, 90 R-G-SIDRA, 90 RB-G-SIDRA students) in a first-year anatomy course, "adjusted p = 0.001" for RB-G-SIDRA vs SIDRA. No effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Statistically significant differences were found between RB-G-SIDRA and SIDRA, U = 39.211 adjusted p = 0.001 and RB-G-SIDRA and R-G-SIDRA U = 31.157 adjusted p = 0.015."
