Evidence¶

Granić 2009¶

Granić, A., Mifsud, C., & Ćukušić, M. (2009). Design, implementation and validation of a Europe-wide pedagogical framework for e-Learning. Computers & Education, 53, 1052–1081. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2009.05.018

q1 · i? · design · r2

A preliminary SWOT analysis of e-Learning implementation cases, used as a checklist for scenario developers and in the PF implementation phase. The matrix concluded that "blended learning... is the ideal teaching concept for the future". This is an analytical planning result, not a tested comparison.

"Some of the conclusions drawn from the resulting SWOT matrix lay emphasis on the fact that (i) blended learning, which uses the best of traditional and e-Learning methods, is the ideal teaching concept for the future and that (ii) the implementation of the e-Learning system does not save time but does create new opportunities for cooperation at the national and international levels"
