### Gamboa Oscar Andrés 2018

Gamboa Oscar Andrés, Agudelo Sergio Iván, Maldonado María Jose, Leguizamón Diana C., Cala Sandra M. (2018). Evaluation of two strategies for debriefing simulation in the development of skills for neonatal resuscitation: a randomized clinical trial. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3831-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of the same randomized trial's checklist compliance percentages, separated into cognitive/technical and behavioral components. The printed coefficients were "− 6.34%" for technical and "− 0.19%" for behavioral skills, with confidence intervals crossing zero in both cases.

> "When comparing the development of technical and behavioral skills, no significant differences were found between the types of debriefing, with − 6.34% (95% CI − 19.93% to 7.25%) and − 0.19% (95% CI − 10.85% to 10.46%), respectively."
