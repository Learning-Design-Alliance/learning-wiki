### Duffy Barbara 2023

Duffy Barbara, Tully Roisin, Stanton Alice V. (2023). An online case-based teaching and assessment program on clinical history-taking skills and reasoning using simulated patients in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03950-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous voluntary 11-item 5-point Likert survey run by the RCSI Quality Enhancement Office; the response rate was 31%. Satisfaction with staff facilitation was 88% and perceived technology usability 89%. The authors note the low uptake means positive feedback may be subject to participation bias.

> "The majority of respondents agreed or strongly agreed that they had the opportunity to ask questions (93%), that the tutorials were interactive (86%) and that the tutorials increased their understanding of the course material (81%)."
