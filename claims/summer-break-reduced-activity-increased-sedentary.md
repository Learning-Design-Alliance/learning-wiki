The commentary's synthesis of observational studies reports "evidence from different countries" that children's physical activity across intensity levels drops during summer break, typically "accompanied by more time spent in sedentary behavior" including higher screen-based and mentally passive sedentary activity.

> "there is evidence from different countries (e.g., Australia, Japan, Slovenia, and the United States) that school-aged children during the summer break spent less time in total physical activity (Volmut et al., 2020), light-intensity physical activity (Brazendale et al., 2018; McCue et al., 2013; Tanaka et al., 2016)"
