# School-aged children show reduced physical activity and increased sedentary behavior during the summer break across multiple countries

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Observational studies from Australia, Japan, Slovenia, and the United States show children spend less time in total, light-, moderate-, moderate-to-vigorous-, and vigorous-intensity physical activity during summer break, accompanied by more sedentary behavior and screen time. The commentary's synthesis of observational studies reports "evidence from different countries" that children's physical activity across intensity levels drops during summer break, typically "accompanied by more time spent in sedentary behavior" including higher screen-based and mentally passive sedentary activity.
