Evidence¶

Arsal 2010¶

Arsal, Z. (2010). The effects of diaries on self-regulation strategies of preservice science teachers. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 5(1), 85-103. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ884413

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The pre-/post-questionnaire control group study found no significant differences in cognitive strategies between groups, indicating that "the diaries about cognitive strategies did not affect the cognitive strategies of the pre-service science teachers".

"Both the pre and post-questionnaire results in Table 1 illustrated that there was no significant difference between the experimental group and control group in terms of cognitive strategies such as rehearsal, elaboration, organization, critical thinking"
