Sánchez Gómez M, López López CA, Greif R, Semeraro F, Ocampo Cervantes AB, Pons Claramonte M, Nieto Caballero S, Alcazar Artero PM, Pardo Rios M, Guillén Martínez D. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-report Likert ratings collected from the 101 participants after the VR simulation. The study reports "mean scores higher than 4.7/5 in all the perceptions of usefulness"; realism received 8.87/10 (SD = 1.26).

> "Table 2 shows mean scores higher than 4.7/5 in all the perceptions of usefulness, with the ability to reproduce difficult clinical scenarios (M = 4.82, SD = 0.44) and increased motivation (M = 4.80, SD = 0.45) as the most highly valued aspects."
