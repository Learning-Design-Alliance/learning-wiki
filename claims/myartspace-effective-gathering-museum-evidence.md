### Vavoula 2009

Vavoula, G., Sharples, M., Rudman, P., Meek, J., & Lonsdale, P. (2009). Myartspace: Design and evaluation of support for learning with multimedia phones between classrooms and museums. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2009.02.007

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational data from the final full-scale user trial at the D-Day museum with a class of 23 Year 9 students. The article reports that "637 objects were collected or captured by the 23 students during this class's visit", demonstrating the service enabled extensive gathering of multimedia evidence.

> "In total, 637 objects were collected or captured by the 23 students during this class's visit: 364 photographs, 121 sound recordings, 77 written comments, and 75 collected objects."
