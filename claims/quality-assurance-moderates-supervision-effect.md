# Supervision alone is positively associated with clinical quality only in facilities with quality assurance activities

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In facilities lacking quality assurance activities, supervision alone was not significantly related to quality of care; in facilities implementing them, it was. [→ Li Mengyao 2026](#li-mengyao-2026)

## Evidence

### Li Mengyao 2026

Li Mengyao, Zhou Zhongliang, Mitchell Rebecca, Wang Dan, Boyle Brendan, Wang Wenhua. (2026). Enhancing health professional clinical performance through supervision strategies: from 5311 facilities across eight countries in sick child healthcare. Journal of Global Health. https://doi.org/10.7189/jogh.16.04123

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Subgroup multilevel regression comparing facilities that implemented quality assurance activities (n = 8764) with those that did not (n = 10315). The non-implemented subgroup showed a non-significant coefficient of −0.097, so the effect holds only in one subgroup.

> "However, in health facilities implementing quality assurance activities, supervision alone was positively associated with quality of care (coefficient = 0.290; 95% CI = 0.14, 0.44)."
