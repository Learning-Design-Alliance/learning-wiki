## Evidence

### Kula Yosef 2025

Kula Yosef, Horosov Roy, Gidron Yori, Ekshtein Aya, Gordon Barak, Iversen Zev, Tehori Omer, Ben-Ari Oded. (2025). Pre-training heart rate variability as a predictor of Air Force Academy completion. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327406

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Matched-sample analysis: candidates were paired and matched on the significant cofounders (intelligence testing score and selection formula), then HRV differences were examined with a paired t-test to increase statistical power beyond the effects of significant cofounders. The result is also shown in Fig 2.

> "the mean initial HRV of those who passed the pilot course (M = 121.30ms, s.d = 61.48ms) was significantly higher than that of those who failed (M = 84.31ms, s.d = 12.05ms) t(25)= −1.78, p < 0.05"
