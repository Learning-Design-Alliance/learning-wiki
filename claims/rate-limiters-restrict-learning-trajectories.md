## Evidence

### Brymer 2022

Brymer, E., & Schweitzer, R. D. (2022). Learning clinical skills: an ecological perspective. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 27(3), 691-707. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10115-9

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical exposition. The article gives examples: developmental status, emotional readiness, physical characteristics such as a petite therapist with a large client, and notes educators must judge whether constraints enhance learning or act as rate limiters.

> "rate limiters (in the sense of information that is restrictive to learning), impacting upon the learning trajectory may include environmental, task or individual factors that limit the rate of learning and development in an individual"
