## Evidence

### Bakri Awaji 2020

Bakri Awaji, Ellis Solaiman and Lindsay Marshall. (2020). Investigating the Requirements for Building a Blockchain-Based Achievement Record System. [doi:10.1145/3411681.3411691](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411681.3411691)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire item on checking CV validity received 136 answers. The most common method was requiring original certificates (81.62%), followed by contacting the issuer (25.74%); 16.91% required a sealed envelope and 5.88% used other methods like digital signatures.

> "In Figure 3, it can be seen that 81.62% require the original copy of certificates from the applicants. While 25.74% contact the issuer of the certificates by email, phone, etc."

### Bakri Awaji 2020 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

> "Figure 4 illustrates the distribution of responses from 131 participants. As the figure shows, on average, record validation takes 12 days."
