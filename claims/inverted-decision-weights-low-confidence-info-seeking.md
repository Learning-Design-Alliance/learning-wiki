## Evidence

### Lorenz Weise 2022

Lorenz Weise, Saskia D. Forster, Siegfried Gauggel. (2022). Reverse-correlation reveals internal error-corrections during information-seeking. Metacognition and Learning (2022) 17:321–335. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09286-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA (3 condition × 2 selection × 2 confidence) on decision weights from 27 participants. The article reports the three-way interaction with F(2, 52) = 24.79 and effect size generalized eta squared (ɳG2) = 0.08, driven by reversed weights in low-confidence info-seeking trials.

> "the significant three way interaction between condition, selection and confidence level ( F(2, 52) = 24.79, p < 0.001, ɳG2 = 0.08). This effect occur red because the decision weights were reversed for low-confidence, info-seeking trials"

### Lorenz Weise 2022 (2)

Lorenz Weise, Saskia D. Forster, Siegfried Gauggel. (2022). Reverse-correlation reveals internal error-corrections during information-seeking. Metacognition and Learning (2022) 17:321–335. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09286-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc t-tests on the low-confidence info-seeking trials of the reverse correlation analysis. The non-selected option showed a positive association (M = 0.07, Cohen's d = 0.56) and the selected option a negative association (M = -0.16, Cohen's d = -0.87).

> "for low-confidence, info-seeking trials, the non-selected stimulus option was indeed positively associated with choice (M = 0.07, t(26) = 2.89, p = 0.008, Cohen's d = 0.56) and t he selected option was negatively associated (M = -0.16, t(26) = -4.53, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = -0.87)."
