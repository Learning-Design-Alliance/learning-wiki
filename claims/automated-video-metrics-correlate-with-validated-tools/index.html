Evidence¶

Dick L 2024¶

Dick L, Boyle CP, Skipworth RJE, Smink DS, Tallentire VR, Yule S. (2024). Automated analysis of operative video in surgical training: scoping review. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrae124

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis across the included studies. The review reports five studies comparing automated metrics with expert-based tools and states that "All studies reported that the metrics produced from analysis correlated with the validated tool used"; no correlation coefficients are printed.

"In addition, five studies compared the metrics produced with an alternative assessment tool. These were based on expert assessment and included OSATS, Global Evaluative Assessment of Robotic Skills (GEARS), Objective Structured Assessment of Cataract Surgical Skill (OSACSS)"
