## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Volunteers who attempted a TeachNow session but failed to match due to random chance mostly went offline rather than doing other teaching activities, showing the system captures time that would otherwise go to waste. [→ Ali Malik 2024](#ali-malik-2024)

## Evidence

### Ali Malik 2024

Ali Malik, Juliette Woodrow, Chao Wang, and Chris Piech. 2024. TeachNow: Enabling Teachers to Provide Spontaneous, Realtime 1:1 Help in Massive Online Courses. In Proceedings of the 2024 Innovation and Technology in Computer Science Education V. 1 (ITiCSE 2024). https://doi.org/10.1145/3649217.3653629

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational log analysis (Figure 4) comparing webpage-location logs of volunteers who successfully matched versus those who failed to match due to random chance. Both groups behaved identically until the attempt; unmatched volunteers, per the article, showed "a majority of teachers going offline instead of doing an alternative activity".

> "For the other group however, we see a majority of teachers going offline instead of doing an alternative activity."
