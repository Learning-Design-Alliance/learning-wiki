## Evidence

### Cheng Luo Chih MD; Mao Wu Sheng MD; Kuei Chien Wen PhD; Sheng Huang Chen MD; Cheng Lin Wei MD; Chun Chang Yin MD 2016

Cheng Luo Chih MD; Mao Wu Sheng MD; Kuei Chien Wen PhD; Sheng Huang Chen MD; Cheng Lin Wei MD; Chun Chang Yin MD. (2016). Students' Interest in Surgery Affects Laparoscopic Practicing Performance. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2016.00039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-group comparison in the same simulator curriculum: group A (n = 30, interested in general surgery or a surgical subspecialty) versus group B (n = 20, not interested). The article reports "Significant differences were observed in PTMs between groups A and B (P < .001)"; no effect size printed.

> "Significant differences were observed in PTMs between groups A and B (P < .001); the total scores of the 2 groups were 718 (122) and 373 (138.3), respectively."

### Cheng Luo Chih MD; Mao Wu Sheng MD; Kuei Chien Wen PhD; Sheng Huang Chen MD; Cheng Lin Wei MD; Chun Chang Yin MD 2016 (2)

Cheng Luo Chih MD; Mao Wu Sheng MD; Kuei Chien Wen PhD; Sheng Huang Chen MD; Cheng Lin Wei MD; Chun Chang Yin MD. (2016). Students' Interest in Surgery Affects Laparoscopic Practicing Performance. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2016.00039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of variance with post hoc test across year level and interest group, reported in Results and Figure 1. The article states "the total PTM scores were higher in the 6th- and the 7th-year medical students in group A than were those in group B".

> "ANOVA with a post hoc test revealed no differences in the BMs between the 6th- and 7th-year medical students in both groups A and B, but the total PTM scores were higher in the 6th- and the 7th-year medical students in group A than were those in group B (Figure 1)."
