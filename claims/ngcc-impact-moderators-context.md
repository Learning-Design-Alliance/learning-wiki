Summary of moderator analyses across NGCC impact data sets, reporting that impacts were "positive for implementations in 4-year institutions and insignificant in 2-year colleges" and more positive in biology, psychology, and math/statistics. No effect sizes are printed.

> "On average, the courseware impact estimate was positive for implementations in 4-year institutions and insignificant in 2-year colleges. Courseware implementations in biology, psychology, and math/statistics had more positive impacts."

### Barbara Means and Julie Neisler 2004 (2)

Barbara Means and Julie Neisler. (2004). Continuous Improvement for Student Success. Digital Promise Global. https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2004/12/06/the-bell-curve

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

A second moderator finding from the same set of NGCC impact analyses: comparisons against face-to-face versions showed positive average impacts, while same-modality comparisons did not.

> "Positive impacts were found on average when the comparison version of the course was taught face-to-face but not when it was the same modality as the treatment (courseware) condition."
