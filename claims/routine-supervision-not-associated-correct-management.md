## Evidence

### Miller Nathan P 2016

Miller Nathan P, Amouzou Agbessi, Hazel Elizabeth, Legesse Hailemariam, Degefie Tedbabe, Tafesse Mengistu, Black Robert E, Bryce Jennifer. (2016). Assessment of the impact of quality improvement interventions on the quality of sick child care provided by Health Extension Workers in Ethiopia. Journal of Global Health. https://doi.org/10.7189/jogh.06.020404

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression from the cross-sectional survey of 103 health posts and 257 sick children in Oromia, Ethiopia, 2012. The article reports "OR = 0.63, 95% CI 0.23–1.72" with P = 0.369; 65.3% of 225 children in supervised health posts were correctly treated vs 62.1% of 29. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The HEW receiving supervision on iCCM (OR = 0.63, 95% CI 0.23–1.72) was not significantly associated with a child receiving correct care."
