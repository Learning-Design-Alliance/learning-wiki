### Grunspan Daniel Z.; Kline Michelle Ann; Brownell Sara E 2018

Grunspan Daniel Z.; Kline Michelle Ann; Brownell Sara E. (2018). The Lecture Machine: A Cultural Evolutionary Model of Pedagogy in Higher Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0287

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' interpretation drawn from the model (type e): the academic trajectory "creates multiple hurdles through career points at which individuals are likely to be penalized for teaching effort" and transmission pathways "dominated by the most frequent pedagogical practices, historically lecture". Teaching-focused institutions are the stated exception, yet cannot spread innovations via PhD production.

> "we fear that interventions focused on individual departments or particular institutions may fail to enact change in the broader system of higher education."
