Evidence¶

Canning 2018¶

Canning, E. A., Harackiewicz, J. M., Priniski, S. J., Hecht, C. A., Tibbetts, Y., & Hyde, J. S. (2018). Improving Performance and Retention in Introductory Biology with a Utility-Value Intervention. Journal of educational psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000244

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Binary logistic regression on course records from the same randomized experiment (N = 577), controlling for gender, prior GPA, and initial interest. The article reports "79.80% of students who wrote about UV at least once continued" versus 69.50% of controls; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Students in the UV condition were more likely to enroll in the second course of the sequence, Wald = 4.55, B = .30, p = .033. We found that 79.80% of students who wrote about UV at least once continued to the next semester, compared to only 69.50% in the control condition."
