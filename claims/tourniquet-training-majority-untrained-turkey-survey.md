Cross-sectional online survey of 300 orthopedic physicians across hospital types in Türkiye, administered via Google Forms between April 1 and May 1, 2025. The training-status item showed "164 participants (54.6%) reported no formal training".

> "A total of 107 participants (35.7%) reported having received both theoretical and practical training on pneumatic tourniquet use, while 29 (9.7%) reported receiving only theoretical training. The remaining 164 participants (54.6%) reported no formal training."
