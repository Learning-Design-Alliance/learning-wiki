Voight 2013¶

Voight, A., Austin, G., and Hanson, T. (2013). A climate for academic success: How school climate distinguishes schools that are beating the achievement odds (Report Summary). San Francisco: WestEd. http://www.wested.org/cs/we/view/rs/1310

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Classification of the 1,715-school sample based on the difference between actual state standardized test performance (2007/08 to 2010/11) and performance predicted from student demographics, requiring outperformance or underperformance in all four middle-school years or the latter three high-school years. The report states "The 40 BTO schools (2.4 percent of the sample) had substantially more positive school climates than both the 20 CU schools (1.2 percent) and all other secondary schools (96.4 percent)."
