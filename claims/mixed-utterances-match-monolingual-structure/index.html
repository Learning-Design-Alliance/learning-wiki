Evidence¶

Bernardini 2004¶

Bernardini, P., & Schlyter, S. (2004). Growing syntactic structure and code-mixing in the weaker language: The Ivy Hypothesis. Bilingualism: Language and Cognition, 7(1), 49-69. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1366728904001270

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Longitudinal study of five Swedish-French/Italian bilingual children aged 2-4, comparing constituent types in mixed versus pure utterances per recording. The authors report that "the code-mixed utterances reﬂected as much syntactic structure of each language as was used in monolingual utterances in the same recording."

"The parts of the code-mixed utterances reﬂected as much syntactic structure of each language as was used in monolingual utterances in the same recording of each child."
