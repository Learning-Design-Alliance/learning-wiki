`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Summary of the companion report's observational findings (Series Summary section). Eleven neighborhood high schools on probation in low-income areas increased "from 16.3 percent in the 1993–94 school year, to 30.1 percent in 1999–00," because fewer general education students were promoted under the gate.

> "The enrollment of students with disabilities in ninth grade increased substantially over the period of our study, from 11.5 percent in the 1993–94 school year, to 16.4 percent in 1999–00."
