## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Short-term operating skill scores were higher descriptively with SDTM (87.40 vs 85.69) but not statistically significant (P = 0.084). [→ Yang Liu 2022](#yang-liu-2022)

## Evidence

### Yang Liu 2022

Yang Liu, Li Wen, Zou Jian, An Junnan, Zeng Bin, Zheng Yitao, Yang Jiming, Ren Jia. (2022). The application of the spot the difference teaching method in clinical skills training for residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03612-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison on a 100-point introductory clinical operating skill checklist: SDTM 87.40 ± 3.895 vs control 85.69 ± 4.899, T = 1.747, P = 0.084. The article reports "no statistically significant difference"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Although the SDTM group’s score was higher than that of the control in the short-term results of operating skill training (87.40 versus 85.69), there was no statistically significant difference (P = 0.084) with a medium effect size (d = 0.39)."
