## Evidence

### Luthra 2025

Luthra, S., Luor, A., Tierney, A. T., Dick, F., & Holt, L. L. (2025). Statistical learning dynamically shapes auditory perception. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00328-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In Exp 2e's equiprobable first half, a Frequency × Duration interaction was observed (RT: p = .003, η² = 0.029; Accuracy: p = 3.738e-5, η² = 0.037). The bias was mirrored qualitatively in Exp 2d and observed in Exp 2a-c unimodal distributions (RT: p = 3.968e-6, η² = 0.020; Acc: p = .003, η² = 0.020).

> "duration decisions for lower-frequency tones (800, 920 Hz) are more accurate and faster for long (90 ms) compared to short (50 ms) tones whereas those for the highest frequency tone (1200 Hz) are more accurate and faster for short compared to long tones"
