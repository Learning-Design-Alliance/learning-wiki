Subgroup analysis of the pooled proportion by publication year in the random-effects meta-analysis; proportions rose from "30.93%" to "49.08%" across periods, with high heterogeneity in both subgroups. The increase is descriptive, not a tested contrast.

> "The pooled proportion for studies published between 2018 and 2020 was 30.93% (95% CI: 21.08–40.77, I 2 = 93.97%, p = 0.00), whereas for studies published between 2021 and 2024, the pooled proportion was 49.08% (95% CI: 39.11–59.06, I 2 = 92.54%, p = 0.00)"
