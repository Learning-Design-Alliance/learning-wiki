## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Ordinal IPAQ score correlates strongly and positively with GPA (r = 0.75), and PSQI global score correlates moderately and negatively with GPA (r = −0.29). [→ Alghadier Mshari 2026](#alghadier-mshari-2026)

## Evidence

### Alghadier Mshari 2026

Alghadier Mshari, Badcock Nicholas. (2026). Sleep, movement, and marks: exploring the relationship between sleep quality, physical activity, and academic performance in male university students. PeerJ 14:e21154. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21154

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis among GPA, PSQI global score, ordinal IPAQ score, age, and BMI in the 130-student sample; correlations with age (−0.16) and BMI (−0.03) were low.

> "When the IPAQ score was coded ordinally (low = 1, moderate = 2, high = 3), greater PA was strongly and positively associated with GPA (r = 0.75, p < 0.001). In contrast, higher PSQI global scores (worse sleep) were moderately and negatively associated with GPA (r = −0.29, p < 0.001)."
