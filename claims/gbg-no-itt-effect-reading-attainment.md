# The Good Behavior Game shows no intent-to-treat effect on children's reading attainment at post-test or 1-year follow-up in a cluster RCT

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After covariate adjustment, ITT analysis found no statistically significant effect of the GBG on reading scores at either post-test (T2) or 1-year follow-up (T3). [→ Ashworth Emma 2020](#ashworth-emma-2020)

## Evidence

### Ashworth Emma 2020

Ashworth Emma, Panayiotou Margarita, Humphrey Neil, Hennessey Alexandra. (2020). Game On—Complier Average Causal Effect Estimation Reveals Sleeper Effects on Academic Attainment in a Randomized Trial of the Good Behavior Game. Prevention Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-019-01074-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Two-level multiple linear regression ITT models in a 2-year cluster-randomized trial of 77 schools (N = 3084 children) found "no statistically significant effect of the GBG on children’s reading scores" at T2 or T3; baseline scores dominated prediction (β = 0.77 at T2, β = 0.75 at T3).

> "After controlling for student-level and school-level covariates, there was no statistically significant effect of the GBG on children’s reading scores at T2 (β = 0.26, p > .05) or T3 (β = 0.18, p > .05)."
