# Adults' past enjoyment of education-related epistemic freedom is between moderate and high, and their tendency toward it is high

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kartal 2018

Kartal, O. Y., Yazgan, A. D., & Avci, E. (2018). An Investigation into the Relationship between Adults' Levels of Education-Related Epistemic Freedom and Epistemic Violence. International Education Studies, 11(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n10p96

`q2 · i?`

Descriptive analysis of the two epistemic freedom dimensions of the scale with 129 adults. Past enjoyment of epistemic freedom was 47.3% moderate and 41.9% high, while tendency to epistemic freedom was "high" and "very high" for 59.7% and 24.0% of participants (mean X = 4.06).

> "In consideration of adults' paradigm tendencies to epistemic freedom, it can be observed that the one with a "high" and "very high" level of tendency to epistemic freedom were 59.7% and 24.0%, respectively."
