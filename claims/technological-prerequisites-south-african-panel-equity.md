## Evidence

### Mirata 2023

Mirata, V., & Bergamin, P. (2023). Role of organisational readiness and stakeholder acceptance: an implementation framework of adaptive learning for higher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10248-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative Delphi data; the article reports South African experts pointed out that "internet costs are too high" and that high costs linked to investments in technical infrastructure were the most common outcome of technological barriers.

> "all technology needed for adaptive learning should be provided, which will have a large burden on the budget"

### Mirata 2023 (2)

Mirata, V., & Bergamin, P. (2023). Role of organisational readiness and stakeholder acceptance: an implementation framework of adaptive learning for higher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10248-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative Delphi data from the South African panel; the article reports this link between technological determinants and broader sociocultural outcomes such as equity in education was identified solely within that panel.

> "Provision for access to technology would be crucial to prevent the increasing digital divide [between students] as a result of efforts to expand technology-enhanced learning"
