## Evidence

### Gambari 2014

Gambari, I. A., Balogun, S. A., & Alfa, A. S. (2014). Efficacy of Interactive Whiteboard on Psychomotor Skills Achievement of Students in Isometric and Orthographic Projection. Contemporary Educational Technology, 5(4), 316-330. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105500

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of posttest scores for male and female students in the IWB group (n = 39). The gender main effect was not significant, "F (1, 42) = 1.697, p = > .05". Female students' mean gain (44.22) slightly exceeded males' (43.05).

> "Table 5 shows that, main effect of treatment group (IWB) on gender produced an F (1, 42) = 1.697, p = > .05. This result was not significant at the 0.05 alpha level. This indicates no significant difference in the performance of male and female students exposed to IWB."
