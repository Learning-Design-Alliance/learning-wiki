# The adult-like retrieval bias for multiplication over addition on strategy selection is significant only in fourth grade, with retrieval used as frequently for both operations in second and sixth grade

## Evidence

### Ineke Imbo · André Vandierendonck 2008

Ineke Imbo · André Vandierendonck. (2008). Effects of problem size, operation, and working-memory span on simple-arithmetic strategies: differences between children and adults? Psychological Research (2008) 72:331–346. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-007-0112-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The grade × operation interaction on retrieval use, F(2, 57) = 5.1, showed a significant increase in retrieval use for multiplication between second and fourth grade, F(1, 57) = 6.1, and for addition between fourth and sixth grade, F(1, 57) = 8.6; only fourth graders showed the multiplication bias.
