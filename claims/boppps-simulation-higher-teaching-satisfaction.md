## Evidence

### Wu Bingshu 2025

Wu Bingshu, Chen Tianran, Mei Xiaobin, Yue Wenqin, Wang Dan, Xu Maojin, Dong Yuchao, Hu Zhenli, Ying Mingzhen. (2025). Application of scenario simulation based on boppps model in physical diagnostics——a practical exploration of integrated teaching mode for cardiac physical examination and electrocardiogram. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08244-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire survey of all 76 students using the Student Evaluation of Faculty Form and Learning Habits Questionnaire (Cronbach's α = 0.876), five-point items. The article states "there were statistically significant differences" across all five domains, with unadjusted p-values.

> "The results showed that there were statistically significant differences in teachers' teaching attitudes (P < 0.0001), teaching abilities (P = 0.0011), teaching content (P < 0.0001), teaching methods (P < 0.0001), and teaching effectiveness (P < 0.0001) (Table 3)."
