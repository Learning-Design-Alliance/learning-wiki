## Evidence

### Aaron D. Slepkov and Ralph C. Shiell 2014

Aaron D. Slepkov and Ralph C. Shiell. (2014). A comparison of integrated testlet and constructed-response question formats. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020120)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correlation-matrix analysis of the two final exams (Table V); item correlations range from 0.14 to 0.62. Median CR-CR, CR-IT, and IT-IT correlations were 0.41/0.39/0.29 (red) and 0.51/0.41/0.15 (blue). Total IT-CR score correlations were 0.70 and 0.83 (midterm) and 0.69 and 0.66 (final), reported descriptively.

> "Scores on ITs correlate more closely to those on CR questions than they do to other ITs."
