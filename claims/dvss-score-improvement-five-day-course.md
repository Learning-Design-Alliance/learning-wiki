### Rusch Peter 2017

Rusch Peter, Kimmig Rainer, Lecuru Fabrice, Persson Jan, Ponce Jordi, Degueldre Michel, Verheijen René. (2017). The Society of European Robotic Gynaecological Surgery (SERGS) Pilot Curriculum for robot assisted gynecological surgery. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-017-4612-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation of the four fellows' performance on standardized DaVinci training console (DVSS) exercises with Mimic software during the 5-day course at ORSI, Ghent; Table 1 reports baseline and post-training overall scores in percent for exercises such as Endo-wrist manipulation and Needle driving. No statistical test is reported.

> "All fellows showed improvement over the week regarding their overall score on the DVSS virtual module (Table 1). Some candidates started with high scores already with only marginal improvement, while others showed much steeper graphs due to lower starting scores."
