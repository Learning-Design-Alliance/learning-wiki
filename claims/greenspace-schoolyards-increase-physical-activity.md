## Evidence

### Vi Ly · Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick 2024

Vi Ly · Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick. (2024). Effects of School-led Greenspace Interventions on Mental, Physical and Social Wellbeing in Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09963-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports Raney et al.'s 16-month quasi-experimental follow-up of schoolyard greening (two primary schools, n = 330): "students in the experimental school had significantly higher PA than students in the control school". No effect size is printed.

> "At 16-month follow-up, students in the experimental school had significantly higher PA than students in the control school (Raney et al., 2021)."

### Vi Ly · Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick 2024 (2)

Vi Ly · Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick. (2024). Effects of School-led Greenspace Interventions on Mental, Physical and Social Wellbeing in Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09963-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports a null from Van Dijk-Wesselius et al.'s large quasi-experiment (n = 2031): "non-significant differences in PA levels" between greened and paved schoolyard schools across follow-ups, though girls in intervention schools increased PA to follow-up 1.

> "Van Dijk-Wesselius et al. (2018) found that from baseline to follow-up 1 and 2, there were non-significant differences in PA levels between students in five primary schools who greened their schoolyards, and students in four control schools with paved schoolyards."
