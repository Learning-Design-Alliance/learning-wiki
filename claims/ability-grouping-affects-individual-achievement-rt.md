Sumadi, I. N. S. Degeng, Sulthon, & Waras. (2017). Effect of Ability Grouping in Reciprocal Teaching Technique of Collaborative Learning on Individual Achievements and Social Skills. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education, 6(3), 216-220. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1157151

Quasi-experimental MANOVA comparing four grouping compositions among Primary School Teacher Education students; the F-test found significant achievement differences (F = 14.111, p = 0.00) between "high group of homogeneous, middle group of homogeneous, low group of homogeneous, and heterogeneous group".

> "F-test results shows coeficien of 14.111 and the significant value of 0.00. This shows that there are significant differences in students’ individual achievements between high group of homogeneous, middle group of homo geneous, low group of homogeneous, and heterogeneous group in reciprocal teaching of collaborative learning."
