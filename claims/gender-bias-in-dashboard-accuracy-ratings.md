## Evidence

### Sun 2026

Sun, C., Major, L., Daltry, R., Obradovic, L., & Friedberg, A. (2026). Enhancing literacy and numeracy through teacher dashboards and digital personalised learning: an A/B/C test in Kenyan early years education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10703-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analysis of the relationship between teacher accuracy ratings and learner gender across the two dashboard partitions. A significant dependence between gender and rating types was found, with male students' rankings perceived as more accurate.

> "Female students received significantly fewer “yes” ratings (χ2 (1, N = 15,609) = 6.34, p =.035) and more “no” ratings (χ 2 (1, N = 6,501) = 10.01, p =.005) than male students, indicating that teachers perceived the ranking of male students to be more accurate."
