## Evidence

### Schwill Simon 2020

Schwill Simon, Reith Dorothee, Walter Tobias, Engeser Peter, Wensing Michel, Flum Elisabeth, Szecsenyi Joachim, Krug Katja. (2020). How to ensure basic competencies in end of life care – a mixed methods study with post-graduate trainees in primary care in Germany. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-020-00540-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

McNemar-type comparison of intention to pursue the additional qualification in palliative medicine within the intervention group before and 6–8 months after the seminar. The authors interpret this as the intervention giving an overview that satisfied the need for further training, or as a realistic view of its demands.

> "Comparing T1 (28.5%, n = 61/219) and T2 (19.9%, n = 30/153), significantly less participants aimed to achieve the additional qualification (p < 0.05)."
