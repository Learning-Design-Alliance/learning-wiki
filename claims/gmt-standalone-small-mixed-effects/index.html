q2 · i0 · review · r2

Narrative review attributing this to Macnamara and Burgoyne (2023a): their meta-analysis of the GMT literature found a "mean effect size estimate (d) of 0.05", falling to 0.01 and 0.03 after publication-bias correction; best-practices studies showed no effect on academic performance.

"In their meta-analytic review of the GMT literature, Macnamara and Burgoyne (2023a) found a mean effect size estimate (d) of 0.05. When controlling for publication bias, the estimate dropped to 0.01 and 0.03 (i.e., no effect at all)."
