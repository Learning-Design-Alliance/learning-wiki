## Evidence

### Gupta P 2025

Gupta P, Sheth N, AlAhmadi R, Yao X, Heiferman MJ. (2025). The Effect of Experience on Visual Search Patterns in Retinal Imaging Analysis. Ophthalmic Surg Lasers Imaging Retina. https://doi.org/10.3928/23258160-20250228-03

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Saccade-length analysis in the five-group eye-tracking study: attendings "significantly lower than PGY2s' F(4,20) = 18.82, P < 0.0001"; regression r(3) = −0.93, P = 0.020. No standardized effect size printed.

> "PGY2s exhibited the longest saccades with an average length of 0.107° ± 0.011°, diminishing among PGY3s, PGY4s, and fellows. Attendings demonstrated the shortest average saccade length of 0.066° ± 0.0058°, significantly lower than PGY2s' F(4,20) = 18.82, P < 0.0001. The linear regression analysis supported these findings and presented a negative correlation between saccade length and experience level r(3) = −0.93, P = 0.020 (Figure 2J)."
