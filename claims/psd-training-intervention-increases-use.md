## Evidence

### Costantini 2025

Costantini, A., Bakker, A. B., & Scharp, Y. S. (2025). Playful Study Design: A Novel Approach to Enhancing Student Well-Being and Academic Performance. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10022-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

As reported in the article's abstract, Study 3 was a quasi-experimental evaluation of a multi-module PSD training with 28 students, comparing an intervention group to a control group; trained students "increased their use of fun and competition when studying" and showed greater active learning, proactivity and absorption over time.

> "In Study 3 (N = 28 students), we conducted a quasi-experimental evaluation of a training intervention to demonstrate that students who learned to playfully redesign their study tasks increased their use of fun and competition when studying."
