## Evidence

### James T. Laverty and Marcos D. Caballero 2017

James T. Laverty and Marcos D. Caballero. (2017). What are we assessing? An analysis of the most common concept inventories in physics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.03191](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.03191)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Point-weighted 3D-LAP coding summarized in Fig. 2 shows the majority of tasks can elicit core ideas, while "scientiﬁc practices are almost never assessed." The authors interpret this as assessing knowledge about concepts but not the ability to do physics with them.

> "Crosscutting concepts are assessed signiﬁcantly less frequently than the core ideas and scientiﬁc practices are almost never assessed by these concept inventories."
