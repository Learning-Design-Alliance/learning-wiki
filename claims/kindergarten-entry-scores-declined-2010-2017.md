# Kindergarten-entry math and reading scores were mostly flat 2010-2014 then slightly declined by 2017, with median math down about 0.24 SD and reading down 0.14 SD

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Median math scores at kindergarten entry dropped approximately four RIT points (0.24 SDs) and median reading scores dropped two RIT points (0.14 SD) between 2010 and 2017, with scores slightly lower at all five selected percentiles in 2017 than 2010. [→ Kuhfeld 2019](#kuhfeld-2019)

## Evidence

### Kuhfeld 2019

Kuhfeld, Megan, Soland, James, Pitts, Christine, & Burchinal, Margaret. (2019). Trends in Children's Academic Skills at School Entry: 2010 to 2017. EdWorkingPaper No. 19-137, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. http://www.edworkingpapers.com/ai19-137

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of weighted fall MAP Growth K-2 scores from over two million kindergarteners entering school 2010-2017, reported by percentile. The study finds "the median math score has dropped approximately four RIT points" and reading dropped two RIT points, with similar declines across the 10th, 25th, 50th, 75th, and 90th percentiles.

> "In total, the median math score has dropped approximately four RIT points, which corresponds to 0.24 standard deviations (SDs), and the median reading score has dropped two RIT points (0.14 SD) during the 8-year time span."
