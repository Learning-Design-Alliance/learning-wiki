Table 4 entry for Ee et al. (2018), a cross-over trial in boys aged 10–11 using standing desks for alternating 21-school-day blocks across an academic year, measured with the Nordic Musculoskeletal Questionnaire. Neck discomfort was lower in the intervention group (p = 0.005).

"Neck discomfort: IG < CG (p = 0.005) Other body parts: no significant"

Table 4 entry for Parry et al. (2019), a cross-over trial in boys aged 9–11 with alternating 21-day standing and traditional desk blocks. Neck (p = 0.004) and shoulder (p < 0.001) discomfort rose from pre to post.
