# Academically prepared female students were around 4.98 times more likely than male students to take the introductory Basic Concepts of Physics course before AP1, consistent with lower confidence

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Among academically prepared students (GPE above three), gender was significantly related to taking Basic Concepts of Physics before AP1, with female students around 4.98 times more likely to do so. [→ Steven Turnbull 2017](#steven-turnbull-2017)

## Evidence

### Steven Turnbull 2017

Steven Turnbull, Dion R. J. O'Neale, Frederique Vanholsbeeck, S. Earl Irving, Thomas Lumley. (2017). A Leaky Pipe Dream? A Study of Gender Differences in Undergraduate Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1702.06249](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1702.06249)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square test on 2×2 contingency tables of academically prepared students (GPE above three) taking Basic Concepts of Physics before AP1: "χ2(1,N = 810) = 7.03,p =.008"; the printed odds ratio was 4.98, i.e. female students were "around 4.98 times more likely" to take it beforehand.

> "The relationship between gender and taking Basic Concepts of Physics was significant, χ2(1,N = 810) = 7.03,p =.008. Female students were around 4.98 times more likely to take Basic Concepts of Physics before taking AP1 (Table VIII)."

### Steven Turnbull 2017 (2)

Steven Turnbull, Dion R. J. O'Neale, Frederique Vanholsbeeck, S. Earl Irving, Thomas Lumley. (2017). A Leaky Pipe Dream? A Study of Gender Differences in Undergraduate Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1702.06249](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1702.06249)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Same contingency-table procedure for academically unprepared students (GPE equal to or below three): the relationship "tended towards significance, χ2(1,N = 609) = 3 .69,p = .054"; the printed odds ratio was 1.74, with females "around 1.74 times more likely" to take Basic Concepts first.

> "the relationship between gender and taking Basic Concepts of Physics tended towards significance, χ2(1,N = 609) = 3 .69,p = .054. Females students were around 1.74 times more likely to take Basic Concepts of Physics before AP1"
