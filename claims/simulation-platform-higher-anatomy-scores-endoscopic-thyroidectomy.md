# Trainees trained on an animal-experiment-based simulation platform achieved significantly higher anatomy scores than control trainees (d = 0.68)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Experimental-group trainees scored higher on post-training anatomy assessment than control-group trainees (81.5 ± 7.6 vs 76.7 ± 6.3, P = 0.01, Cohen's d = 0.68). [→ Yan Haichao 2025](#yan-haichao-2025)

## Evidence

### Yan Haichao 2025

Yan Haichao, Zhu Ruiying, Yue Ning, Han Zhenyi, Yu Xiaobo, Tanu Cheerly, Wang Ping, Yu Xing. (2025). Utilization of animal experiment-based simulation surgery platforms in the education of endoscopic thyroidectomy: a preliminary study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07935-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 60 trainees (n = 30 per group) at one thyroid surgery department; anatomy scores compared between groups after one month of training. The simulation group scored "significantly higher anatomy scores (81.5 ± 7.6)" with Cohen's d = 0.68 reported in Table 1.

> "the experimental group, which trained with the animal- experiment-based simulation platform, achieved significantly higher anatomy scores (81.5 ± 7.6) compared to the control group (76.7 ± 6.3) (P = 0.01)"
