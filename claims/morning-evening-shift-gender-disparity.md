Descriptive analysis of shift-disaggregated fall-semester enrollment records. "Evening programs remained strongly male-dominated," enrolling only 500–600 women annually, while morning-shift women grew from about 5,200 to more than 8,200.

> "In morning programs, women comprised 40–50% of students in most faculties, and their numbers increased from about 5,200 to more than 8,200. Evening programs remained strongly male-dominated, enrolling only 500–600 women annually and rarely exceeding one-quarter women in any faculty"
