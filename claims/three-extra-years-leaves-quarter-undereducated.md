## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Margaret “Macke” Raymond 2022

Margaret “Macke” Raymond, Ph.D. (2022). The Pace of Recovery after COVID. Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Hoover Institution. https://www.eduref.org/

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

Simulation-style projection over sixteen states using Spring 2022 assessment-based POL estimates, reported in Table 1 for reading. The no-loss baseline shows "about 68 percent of students end their K-12 years having met the benchmark"; 60-day and 90-day loss scenarios yield 65 and 64 percent, rising to 72 and 71 percent with three added years.

> "Adding three years of additional learning for the “no loss” students raises the proportion meeting the 12th Grade benchmark to 74 percent, an increase of six percentage points."
