## Evidence

### Nurwijayanti 2019

Nurwijayanti, A., Budiyono, & Fitriana, L. (2019). Combining Google SketchUp and Ispring Suite 8: A breakthrough to develop geometry learning media. Journal on Mathematics Education, 10(1), 103-116. https://jme.ejournal.uns.ac.id/index.php/jme

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Product-trials effectiveness test using a static group comparison design with four classes from two schools selected by stratified cluster random sampling. After normality and homogeneity tests, the t-test rejected the null hypothesis and the media group had better achievement.

> "The average score of experiment class was 67.63 and control class was 63.22."
