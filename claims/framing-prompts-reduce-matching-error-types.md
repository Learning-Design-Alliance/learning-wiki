## Evidence

### Sally P.W. Wu 2019

Sally P.W. Wu, Jainaba Corr, Martina A. Rau. (2019). How instructors frame students' interactions with educational technologies can enhance or reduce learning with multiple representations. Computers & Education 128 (2019) 199–213. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.09.012

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA on Chem Tutor log data from N = 180 students in the model and draw conditions, with pretest as covariate. The article reports a significant main effect of condition on error type, F(2, 176) = 4.969, p = .008, and a draw-error effect, F(2, 176) = 3.097, p = .048.

> "There was a significant main effect of condition on error type,F(2, 176) = 4.969,p = .008, such that students in model condition made fewer model-errors and students in the draw condition made fewer draw-errors, F(2, 176) = 3.097,p = .048."
