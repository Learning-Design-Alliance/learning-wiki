Evidence¶

ref-feedback¶

Jeongmin Rhee, Changhee Lee, Hyunwoo Kim, Kiroong Choe, Bohyoung Kim, Sungahn Ko, and Jinwook Seo. 2026. Penquiry: A Pen-based Interactive In-situ Q&A System Leveraging LLMs. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19870 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.19870

q3 · i? · design · r3

Qualitative findings from Study 1 (N=16) interviews; P14 reported an underline producing an irrelevant answer, and P1, P15 and P16 reported misaligned referential intent. The authors conclude explicit visual grounding is essential for verifying captured targets.

"Partici- pants noted a critical lack of visual feedback to confirm whether their intended targets were accurately captured, occasionally re- sulting in irrelevant system responses."

express-cost¶

Jeongmin Rhee, Changhee Lee, Hyunwoo Kim, Kiroong Choe, Bohyoung Kim, Sungahn Ko, and Jinwook Seo. 2026. Penquiry: A Pen-based Interactive In-situ Q&A System Leveraging LLMs. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19870

q3 · i? · design · r3

Study 1 qualitative finding on the Expressive Barrier in pen-based inquiry: participants formulating Knowledge-based questions reported physical and cognitive burden of handwriting plus recognition anxiety that the system might fail to digitize their text.

"Those who engaged in complex inquiry reported that hand- writing lengthy, structured queries was both physically burdensome and cognitively demanding."
