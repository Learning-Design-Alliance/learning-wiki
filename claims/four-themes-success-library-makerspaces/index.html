Evidence¶

Kailea Saplan · Sam Abramovich · Peter Wardrip 2024¶

Kailea Saplan · Sam Abramovich · Peter Wardrip. (2024). Analyzing properties of success for assessment development in maker-based learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10286-1

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative domain-analysis study with semi-structured interviews of 17 library staff and maker educators in two public library systems, coded through multiple rounds of descriptive, concept, sub-, and pattern coding with member checking. The article reports that analysis "revealed that library staff and educators spoke about four key themes."

"Our analysis revealed that library staff and educators spoke about four key themes when describing success and evidence of success in their makerspaces. They discussed who is expected to demonstrate success, how success is demonstrated, what makes success visible, and with whom instances of success must be shared."
