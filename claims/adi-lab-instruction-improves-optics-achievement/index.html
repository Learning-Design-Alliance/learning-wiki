Evidence¶

Tuba Demircioglu 2015¶

Tuba Demircioglu, Sedat Ucar. (2015). Investigating the Effect of Argument-Driven Inquiry in Laboratory Instruction. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.1.2324

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent groups study of 79 pre-service science teachers (ADI n=41, traditional n=38). ANCOVA on Optical Achievement Test post-test scores corrected for pretest showed a significant difference in favor of the ADI group with printed effect size ŋ 2 = .26, which the article calls a large effect describing 26% of the variance.

"According to the ANCOV A results, when the OAT pretest scores of the experimental and control groups were analyzed, a significant difference between their corrected post-test scores (F (1-76) = 26.33, p = .00) was found. The effect size of the applied method is ŋ 2 = .26"
