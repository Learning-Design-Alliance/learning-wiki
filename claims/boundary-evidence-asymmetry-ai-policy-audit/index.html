Evidence¶

Kai Yao 2026¶

Kai Yao. (2026). What Does the Credential Still Certify? Cognitive Stewardship for AI-Mediated Education. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19988 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.19988

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Policy audit of public generative AI assessment guidance from 30 universities across five national systems, scored by four open-weight LLM coders under a pre-specified codebook. It found "22 policies scored higher on boundary than evidence, 3 tied, and only 5 reversed the gap."

"24 reached a delegation-boundary score of at least 2, and the mean boundary score was 2.47/4. The weaker element was the evidence standard. Its mean was 1.89/4; 22 policies scored higher on boundary than evidence, 3 tied, and only 5 reversed the gap."
