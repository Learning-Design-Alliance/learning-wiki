## Evidence

### Pei Wang 2026

Pei Wang, Xueting Ju, Jiali Liu, Qingyuan Hou & Guomei Jiang. (2026). Assessing nurses' knowledge and confidence in adult sepsis: evidence from two hospitals in eastern China. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53878-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey comparison of trained (n = 47) vs untrained nurses; the study reports "significantly higher confidence levels" but only marginal knowledge differences, diverging from prior research the authors cite.

> "While nurses who had recently undergone sepsis training reported significantly higher confidence levels, the improvement in their knowledge scores was only marginal, diverging from previous research that demonstrated substantial enhancements in both attitudes and knowledge following structured educational programs"
