# Instructional support improves quality of feedback provision (g = 0.64) and quality of feedback reception (g = 0.49) but not subject-matter-related knowledge

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q4` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Instructional support has a medium-sized positive effect on the quality of feedback provision (g = 0.64). [→ Hornstein 2025](#hornstein-2025)
`q4 i2` Instructional support has a positive effect on the quality of feedback reception (g = 0.49). [→ Hornstein 2025 (2)](#hornstein-2025-2)
`q4 i?` Instructional support shows no significant effect on subject-matter-related knowledge (g = 0.25, p = 0.05). [→ Hornstein 2025 (3)](#hornstein-2025-3)

## Evidence

### Hornstein 2025

Hornstein, J., Keller, M. V., Greisel, M., Dresel, M., & Kollar, I. (2025). Enhancing the Peer-Feedback Process Through Instructional Support: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10017-3

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 18 studies (k = 92 effect sizes) on quality of feedback provision: g = 0.64, 95% CI [0.30, 0.98], p < 0.01 (Table 2).

> "Results revealed a positive, medium-sized effect for peer-feedback with instructional support vs. without instructional support on the quality of feedback provision and a small positive effect on the quality of feedback reception"

### Hornstein 2025 (2)

Hornstein, J., Keller, M. V., Greisel, M., Dresel, M., & Kollar, I. (2025). Enhancing the Peer-Feedback Process Through Instructional Support: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10017-3

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 18 studies (k = 40 effect sizes) on quality of feedback reception: g = 0.49, 95% CI [0.05, 0.92], p < 0.05 (Table 2).

> "Results revealed a positive, medium-sized effect for peer-feedback with instructional support vs. without instructional support on the quality of feedback provision and a small positive effect on the quality of feedback reception"

### Hornstein 2025 (3)

Hornstein, J., Keller, M. V., Greisel, M., Dresel, M., & Kollar, I. (2025). Enhancing the Peer-Feedback Process Through Instructional Support: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10017-3

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 17 studies (k = 60 effect sizes) on subject-matter-related knowledge: g = 0.25, 95% CI [-0.00, 0.51], p = 0.05 — not significant. Effects across dependent variables did not differ significantly (F(2,187) = 1.83, p = 0.23).

> "For subject-matter-related knowledge, we found no significant effect (see Table 2). We also tested whether the effects regarding the different dependent variables were significantly different from each other. This was not the case (F(2,187) = 1.83, p = 0.23)."
