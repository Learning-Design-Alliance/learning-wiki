## Evidence

### Mary Nguyen 2012

Mary Nguyen, Erin Ward Bibo, and Jennifer Engle. (2012). Advancing to Completion: Increasing degree attainment by improving graduation rates and closing gaps for Hispanic students. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED535510

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the 2004–2010 IPEDS/College Results Online sample, reported in Figure 2. The report finds Hispanic students "disproportionately represented among institutions that made no improvements," and notes rates declined at one of three private nonprofits, which educate 40 percent of Hispanic students at private colleges.

> "more than 40 percent of Hispanic students are attending institutions that did not make any improvements in their graduation rates. Considering the fact that these institutions made up only 35 percent of all schools, Hispanic students are thus disproportionately represented among institutions that made no improvements."
