Survey participants graded severity of experienced adverse events using the Society of International Radiology Adverse Event Severity Scale; the reported severity distribution shows type 3 and 5 perforations with "a greater degree of injury" and 9.2%/10.4% mortality versus <3% for several other scenarios.

> "In contrast, type 3 and 5 coronary perforations were associated with a greater degree of injury, with 11.8%/11.0% sustaining a life‐threatening complication and mortality of 9.2%/10.4%, respectively."
