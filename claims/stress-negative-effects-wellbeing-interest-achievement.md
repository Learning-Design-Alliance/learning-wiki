### Wijaya Tommy Tanu 2022

Wijaya Tommy Tanu, Rahmadi Imam Fitri, Chotimah Siti, Jailani Jailani, Wutsqa Dhoriva Urwatul. (2022). A Case Study of Factors That Affect Secondary School Mathematics Achievement: Teacher-Parent Support, Stress Levels, and Students’ Well-Being. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192316247

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural paths in the 531-student PLS-SEM model show stress significantly and negatively related to all three outcomes; the smallest path (achievement, p = 0.043) is near the 0.05 threshold. Stress's total effect on achievement is −0.268 (Table 9).

> "This study found that stress significantly reduced well-being (β = −0.323, t = 6.156, p = 0.000), interest in learning (β = −0.145, t = 4.243, p = 0.000), and students’ mathematics achievement (β = −0.063, t = 1.858, p = 0.043)."
