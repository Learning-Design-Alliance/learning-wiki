Evidence¶

Pang Xuefei 2026¶

Pang Xuefei, Wang Huilin, Li Jinggui, Gui Haijun, Cao Xia, Cai Ming. (2026). The Impact of Early Clinical Exposure on Competency Development in Dental Students: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109483

q4 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of 27 studies (2995 students; 1787 ECE, 1211 control) pooling standardised mean differences. The pooled effect was "SMD = 1.25, 95% CI = 0.83-1.66" with I² = 96.6%. GRADE certainty was rated low (serious inconsistency and imprecision).

"Meta-analysis of the 27 included studies showed that students who received ECE achieved significantly higher overall competency scores compared to those in traditional teaching groups (SMD = 1.25, 95% CI = 0.83-1.66, I² = 96.6%, P < .001)"
