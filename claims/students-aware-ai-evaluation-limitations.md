`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theme 2 of the thematic analysis of 13 reflections; Table 1 records 7 explicit plus 2 partial articulations. One participant noted the AI "spelt my last name wrong" due to handwriting; another said "the AI doesn't know [the instructor's] rating system."

> "These limitations fell into two categories: technical and contextual. At the technical level, several participants raised concerns that ChatGPT could misinterpret handwriting, writing intent, or contextual meaning, especially because the writing was handwritten and later scanned."
