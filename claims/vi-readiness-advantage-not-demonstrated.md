## Evidence

### Wijnen-Meijer M 2015

Wijnen-Meijer M, ten Cate O, van der Schaaf M, Burgers C, Borleffs J, Harendza S. (2015). Vertically integrated medical education and the readiness for practice of graduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0514-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors' overall conclusion from the 59-candidate comparative UHTRUST assessment: despite "large differences in the curriculum they had followed", only few performance differences emerged, and equivalence was not tested.

> "This study has been unable to demonstrate that vertically integrated curricula prepare their graduates better for clinical practice. But there is no reason to jump to the conclusion that the trend towards vertical integration is not the right direction."
