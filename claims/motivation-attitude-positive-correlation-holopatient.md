### Kang Yun 2025

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis in the same post-test survey (n = 100). The article prints r = .78 for the strongest pair; correlations of this magnitude exceed the r ≥ .37 threshold for a large association.

> "All dimensions of learning attitude towards HoloPatient-based simulation showed significant positive correlations with dimensions of learning motivation, with correlation coefficients ranging from r =.59 to r =.78 (all p <.001). The strongest correlation was observed between satisfaction (LM4) and study attitude (LA2) (r =.78, p <.001)"
