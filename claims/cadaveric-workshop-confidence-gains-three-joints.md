## Evidence

### Zure Mert 2025

Zure Mert, Topaloğlu Mahir, İş Enes Efe, Sezikli Selim, Sarıkaya Deniz, Dıraçoğlu Demirhan. (2025). Advancing skills and confidence in ultrasound-guided injection techniques: the impact of a cadaveric workshop. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07270-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective cross-sectional pre/post survey of 109 attendees across three one-day workshops, analyzed with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Confidence was self-reported on a 5-point Likert scale; "significantly improved" gains were 2.12 (hip), 1.24 (knee), and 1.87 (ankle) points.

> "Pre- versus post-course confidence scores were significantly improved for hip (mean 2.12 points, P-value < 0.001), knee (mean 1.24 points, P-value < 0.001), and ankle (mean 1.87 points, P-value < 0.001) joints (Table 3)."

### Zure Mert 2025 (2)

Zure Mert, Topaloğlu Mahir, İş Enes Efe, Sezikli Selim, Sarıkaya Deniz, Dıraçoğlu Demirhan. (2025). Advancing skills and confidence in ultrasound-guided injection techniques: the impact of a cadaveric workshop. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07270-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of the pooled pre/post results, attributing the largest hip gain to greater technical challenges and anatomical complexity; no effect size is printed for the between-joint contrast.

> "In contrast, the hip joint demonstrated the largest improvement in confidence, reflecting the greater technical challenges and anatomical complexity associated with hip injections [29]."
