Evidence¶

Chris Impey 2024¶

Chris Impey, Matthew Wenger, Nikhil Garuda, Shahriar Golchin, Sarah Stamer. (2024). Using Large Language Models for Automated Grading of Student Writing about Science. Preprint. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3962175/v1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Individual-student comparison across all twelve assignments for ten students per course (Figure 1); the article reports analogous results for astrobiology (-0.02 ± 0.17 vs -0.09 ± 0.20) and history and philosophy (-0.09 ± 0.24 vs -0.23 ± 0.30), concluding GPT-4 performs better than peer grading.

"For the introductory course, the mean instructor minus GPT-4 score is -0.01 ± 0.15, compared to the mean instructor minus peer grading score of -0.07 ± 0.26. The LLM grade is very close to the instructor grade, with a smaller dispersion than the peer grade."
