## Evidence

### Abegglen S 2020

Abegglen S, Krieg A, Eigenmann H, Greif R. (2020). Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing (OSAD) in simulation-based medical education: Translation and validation of the German version. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244816

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-version agreement analysis in which three different psychology students rated the same 57 debriefings with the original English OSAD for comparison with G-OSAD ratings. The article reports "ICC = 0.586" and considers the two versions' ratings comparable. No effect size printed.

> "The agreement between the average total G-OSAD ratings and the average total original English OSAD ratings was ICC = 0.586 (95% confidence interval, 0.297–0.756; p = 0.001)."
