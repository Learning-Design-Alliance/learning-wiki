Rodríguez Rincón 2025¶

Rodríguez Rincón, Y., Munárriz, A., Campión Arrastia, M. J., & Goicoechea López-Vailo, M. I. (2025). Instructional design for tutoring on interactive platforms: creating educational interventions overcoming the digital gap. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10516-8

Literature-based analysis in the online personalized learning systems section identifies the two barriers. The article cites Shin (2022) and Basilotta Gómez-Pablos et al. (2022) noting these skills are not widespread among university instructors, so universities outsource ITS services at high cost.

"Both the lack of programming knowledge and digital competences of instructors and the high cost of implementing and maintaining ITS are the two most relevant barriers to access to these tools or to implement them."
