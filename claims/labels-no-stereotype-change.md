### Kashikar 2025

Kashikar, L., Lüke, T., & Grosche, M. (2025). Effects of Diagnostic Labels for Students With Learning Problems on Teachers' Stereotypes and Performance Expectations. Journal of Learning Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/00222194251315187

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on warmth ratings from 413 teachers in the vignette experiment found no significant label-group, profession, or interaction effects (ηp2 = .001–.004); the competence ANOVA was similarly null (ηp2 = .002–.004). The discussion states "the SLN and LD labels did not significantly alter teachers' perceived warmth or competence."
