## Evidence

### Teodóra Vékony 2022

Teodóra Vékony, Claire Pleche, Orsolya Pesthy, Karolina Janacsek and Dezso Nemeth. (2022). Speed and accuracy instructions affect two aspects of skill learning differently. npj Science of Learning 7:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00144-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The Epoch × Group interaction on explicit sequence reports only "approached significance", with a printed partial eta-squared of 0.06 (a medium-range effect): the Accuracy Group reported better in the first epoch (86.4% vs. 74%) but performed similarly by the last epoch.

> "However, the interaction between the Epoch and Group factors approached significance [Epoch × Group interaction: F(2.22, 102.09) = 2.94, p = 0.05, ηp 2 = 0.06, BFexclusion = 0.81]."

### Teodóra Vékony 2022 (2)

Teodóra Vékony, Claire Pleche, Orsolya Pesthy, Karolina Janacsek and Dezso Nemeth. (2022). Speed and accuracy instructions affect two aspects of skill learning differently. npj Science of Learning 7:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00144-9

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U test on sequence report scores in the Same Instruction Phase found the groups performed "similarly" with a negligible printed effect size rRB = 0.08 and both groups near 97–98% correct.

> "the two groups performed similarly in terms of explicit sequence report scores, M Accuracy Group = 97.2%, M Speed Group = 97.9%, U = 309, p = 0.53, rRB = 0.08"
