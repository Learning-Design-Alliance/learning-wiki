## Evidence

### Dikshit et al. 2013

Jyotsna Dikshit, Suresh Garg, Santosh Panda. (2013). Pedagogic Effectiveness of Print, Interactive Multimedia, and Online Resources: A Case Study of IGNOU. International Journal of Instruction, 6(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085398

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on the post-test Achievement Test scores (40 questions, total score 100) of the three groups of 20 students each: between-groups SS 1634.7, F-ratio 8.53, P<.01, as printed in Table 5. The article reports "The F-ratio being significant at .01 level".

> "The F-ratio being significant at .01 level suggested that there existed significant independent and interaction effects of three modes of learning on achievement test scores of the students."
