### Stacey M. Davis and Scott V. Franklin 2006

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Multiple interviews across one quarter with a student whose BE scores indicated low compatibility with small groups and aversion to auditory learning; the authors observed such students 'appeared to succeed in the seemingly discordant SCALE-UP envi-ronment'.
