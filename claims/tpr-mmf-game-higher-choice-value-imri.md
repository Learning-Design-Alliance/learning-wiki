## Evidence

### Hsu 2026

Hsu, T.-C., Zhang, Z., Chen, Z., Liu, Y., & Liu, Y. (2026). Designing More Engaging Serious Games to Support Students' Mental Health: A Pilot Study Based on A CBT-Informed Design Framework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.15662](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.15662)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Same pilot RCT (N=28, 14 per group, 20-minute play session, post-session IMI). Independent-samples t-tests showed "significant advantages in perceived choice" (d=1.31) and value/usefulness (d=1.72) for the TPR-MMF game group.

> "the experimental group also showed significant advantages in perceived choice (E_Group M=5.20, SD=0.54; C_Group M=4.58, SD=0.40; p=.002, d=1.31) and value/usefulness (E_Group M=5.93, SD=0.37; C_Group M=5.35, SD=0.30; p<.001, d=1.72), with relatively large effect sizes in all these areas"
