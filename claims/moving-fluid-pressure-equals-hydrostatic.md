## Evidence

### Alvaro Suarez 2017

Alvaro Suarez, Sandra Kahan, Genaro Zavala, and Arturo C. Marti. (2017). Student Conceptual Difficulties in Hydrodynamics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Error analysis of 600 written midterm and final exam responses from Physics 2 students at the Faculty of Engineering, Montevideo, categorized recurrent errors (at least 5% of students). In the February 2016 exam, "27% of the students assumed that the pressures in the first section of pipes 1 and 2 were equal to the hydrostatic".

> "In the analysis of the written tests, 27% of the students assumed that the pressures in the first section of pipes 1 and 2 were equal to the hydrostatic, that is, to P0 +ρgh in pipe 1 and toP0 +ρgH in pipe 2, disregarding the velocity contributions."
