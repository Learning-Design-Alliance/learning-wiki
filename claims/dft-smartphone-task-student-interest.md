P. Pirinen, P. Klein, S. Z. Lahme, A. Lehtinen, L. Rončević, and A. Susac. (2023). Learning digital signal processing using an interactive Jupyter notebook and smartphone accelerometer data. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad0790](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad0790)

Questionnaire evaluation at the University of Jyväskylä on a graduate-level mathematical method course on integral transforms; four responses were received from physics majors on average in their 4th year. Students "rated the use of digital technology for data collection and analysis as positive" and found the task "a good demonstration of how the mathematical method can be used in practice".
