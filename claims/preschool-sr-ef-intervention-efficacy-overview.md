## Evidence

### Rosalyn A. Muir · Steven J. Howard · Lisa Kervin 2023

Rosalyn A. Muir · Steven J. Howard · Lisa Kervin. (2023). Interventions and Approaches Targeting Early Self-Regulation or Executive Functioning in Preschools: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09740-6

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of 85 studies (12,595 children aged 3–6) following PRISMA methodology. The review reports "60 (70.6%) of the studies" achieved significance for at least one outcome, and across 208 evaluated effects, 102 (49.0%) were significant immediately post-intervention. Effect sizes ranged from small to large; only four studies included follow-up assessments.

> "Statistically significant results (for at least one outcome) were reported in 60 (70.6%) of the studies; 43 (72.8%) EF interventions, 11 (64.7%) SR interventions and 6 (66.7%) of the combined SR/EF interventions."
