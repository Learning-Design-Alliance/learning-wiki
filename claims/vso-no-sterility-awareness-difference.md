### Wang Jiaxue 2025

Wang Jiaxue, Jiang Yunshui, Yang Jun, Zhou Qian, Hu Hui, Chen Yan, Jiang Jinqin. (2025). Application of virtual simulations in the practical teaching of clinical microbiology laboratory courses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07603-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same quasi-experiment, both groups had a median sterility awareness score of 92.00; the difference of 0.51 (−1.78–2.81, Z = −1.64, P = 0.101) was not statistically significant. The authors note the VSO emphasized biosafety conditions rather than explicit aseptic awareness design.

> "However, the sterility awareness assessment showed no significant difference between groups, with an average score difference of 0.51 (–1.78–2.81, Z = − 1.64) (P = 0.101)"
