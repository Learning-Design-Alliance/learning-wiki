### Lefèvre 2024

Lefèvre, E., Law, J. M., Prado, J., Anders, R., & Cavalli, E. (2024). Reading Comprehension Resiliency in Adolescents with and without Dyslexia relates to Vocabulary, Listening Comprehension and Socioeconomic Status. https://osf.io/fzyeh/?view_only=84fc51248a0d48ba9a505835e525efdc

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Group comparison in a cross-sectional study of 56 adolescents with dyslexia and 39 typical readers, mean age about 16. The dyslexic group showed "a greater RCR index compared to typical readers", with d = 1.03 as printed.

> "There was a significant difference between individuals with dyslexia and typical readers with respect to the RCR index (t(93) = 4.89, p < .001, d = 1.03)."
