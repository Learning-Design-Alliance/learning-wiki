## Evidence

### Katherine N. Quinn 2018

Katherine N. Quinn, Carl Wieman, and N.G. Holmes. (2018). Interview validation of the Physics Lab Inventory of Critical thinking (PLIC). [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.076](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.076)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview observation: ten of twelve students critically evaluated questions and selected only a small subset of options; five discerning students changed their initial judgment after justifying it. The authors argue this variability allows the PLIC to assess the quality of students' critical thinking as intended.

> "This demonstrates that seeing the answer options affects students' responses, but we argue that this triggers students to think critically."
