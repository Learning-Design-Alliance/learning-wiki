Evidence¶

Övez 2018¶

Filiz Tuba Dikkartin Övez. (2018). The Impact of Instructing Quadratic Functions with the Use of Geogebra Software on Students’ Achievement and Level of Reaching Acquisitions. International Education Studies, 11(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n7p1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' observation of control group classroom applications during the experiment, reported in the Result and Discussion section as a qualitative contrast with the experimental group's behavior.

"In the study during the control group applications it was seen that the students usually laid stress on algebraic operations and formulas and that they mostly memorized them and that they could not establish a relation between the graphs and algebraic expression of function."

Övez 2018 (2)¶

Filiz Tuba Dikkartin Övez. (2018). The Impact of Instructing Quadratic Functions with the Use of Geogebra Software on Students’ Achievement and Level of Reaching Acquisitions. International Education Studies, 11(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n7p1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' interpretation of the experimental group's performance, attributed to guided discovery revealing existing relations and to students constructing their own examples with GeoGebra; no separate statistic is printed for this contrast.

"Test group students were able to establish the relation between the algebraic expression and the graphs better and that they became more successful in interpreting them."
