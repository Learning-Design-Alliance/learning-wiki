Payne 2005¶

Payne, Charles. (2005). Still Crazy after All These Years: Race in the Chicago School System. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Illinois at Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/still-crazy-after-all-these-years-race-chicago-school-system

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Interpretive conclusion drawn from a case vignette of a white Comer-project facilitator in a South Side school who openly confronted parental racial hostility, asked for six months to do her job, and a year later was arguably the most socially central person in the building.

"Race is influential but not determinative. It slows the process down, it frustrates and complicates, but it does not have to dictate unless we somehow allow it to."
