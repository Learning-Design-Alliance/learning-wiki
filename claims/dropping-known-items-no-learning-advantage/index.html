Yael Sidi · Rakefet Ackerman 2024¶

Yael Sidi · Rakefet Ackerman. (2024). Opting Out as an Untapped Resource in Instructional Design: Review and Implications. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09879-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Kornell and Bjork (2008), that English-speaking participants learned Swahili vocabulary with translation cards, some conditions allowing "drop items" of known material; the dropping strategy "did not confer any sig- nificant advantage for learning outcomes". No effect size is printed.

"The results revealed that the dropping strategy did not confer any sig- nificant advantage for learning outcomes; in fact, it was sometimes mildly detri- mental."
