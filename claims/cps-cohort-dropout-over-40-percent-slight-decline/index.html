Evidence¶

Elaine Allensworth and John Q. Easton 2001¶

Elaine Allensworth and John Q. Easton. (2001). Calculating a Cohort Dropout Rate for the Chicago Public Schools: A Technical Research Report. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/calculating-cohort-dropout-rate-chicago-public-schools

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cohort analysis of CPS administrative records following students defined by age 13 through age 19. The report states "41.8 percent of the 1994 cohort had dropped out" versus 44.3 percent for the 1991 cohort; no effect size or significance test is printed.

"By the fall of 2000, 41.8 percent of the 1994 cohort had dropped out of school. This compares with a dropout rate of 42.9 percent for the 1993 cohort, 43.6 percent for the 1992 cohort, and 44.3 percent for the 1991 cohort."
