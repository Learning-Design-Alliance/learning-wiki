## Evidence

### Usta Sıla Nur 2025

Usta Sıla Nur, Silva Emmanuel João Nogueira Leal, Keskin Cangül, Tekkanat Hilal, Liukkonen Mikko, Felszeghy Szabolcs. (2025). A comparison of traditional and virtual reality haptic simulator approaches in preclinical endodontic training: Impacts on skill acquisition, confidence and stress. International Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.14236

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-confidence questionnaire responses (Table S2) collected after preclinical training from the two randomized groups of 20 students each; the article reports Group 1 "consistently reported higher levels of agreement across key areas". No effect size is printed.

> "Group 1 consistently reported higher levels of agreement across key areas, particularly regarding confidence in performing cavity preparation, the adequacy of anatomy teaching in meeting clinical needs, and the overall quality of endodontic training."
