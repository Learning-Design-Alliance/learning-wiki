Evidence¶

Mitrovic 2015¶

Mitrovic, A., & Ohlsson, S. (2015). Implementing CBM: SQL-Tutor After Fifteen Years. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0049-9

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The article reports a study by Mitrovic and Martin (2000) investigating the effectiveness of various levels of feedback in SQL-Tutor. It states the results showed principle-level feedback "was superior to other feedback levels". No effect sizes are printed.

"the results showed that feedback presenting information about the violated domain principles (e.g., Hint and All errors) was superior to other feedback levels, and resulted in faster and more effective learning."
