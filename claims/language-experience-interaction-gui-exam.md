# Programming language and prior experience interact on GUI-programming exam success: novices do better with the blocks-based language, experienced students with the text-based language

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A statistically meaningful two-way interaction between the Experienced/Novice factor combination and the programming language used affects Practical Exam scores (F(1,44)=10.58, p=.00). [→ Mihci 2017](#mihci-2017)

## Evidence

### Mihci 2017

Mihci, C. & Ozdener Donmez, N. (2017). Teaching GUI-programming concepts to prospective K12 teachers: MIT App Inventor as an alternative to text based languages. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 3(2), 543-559. https://doi.org/10.21890/ijres.327912

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

2x2 factorial ANOVA on Practical Exam scores of Phase 1 subgroups (novice and experienced factor combinations in both language groups), meeting normality and Levene requirements as printed; the interaction was significant at p = .00.

> "there is a statistically meaningful two -way interaction between the effects of the ―Experienced/Novice‖ dichotomous factor combination and the programming language used in the Programming Languages 2 course, upon the Practical Exam scores (F(1,4 4) = 10,58, p = .00)."

### Mihci 2017 (2)

Mihci, C. & Ozdener Donmez, N. (2017). Teaching GUI-programming concepts to prospective K12 teachers: MIT App Inventor as an alternative to text based languages. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 3(2), 543-559. https://doi.org/10.21890/ijres.327912

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up independent samples t-tests on Practical Exam means: novices BBL M=34.58 vs TBL M=20.00; experienced TBL M=67.00 vs BBL M=46.36; both contrasts statistically significant as printed.

> "there are statistically significant differences between both novice (t(22)= -2.20, p<.05) and experienced (t(19)= 2,35, p<.05) students based on the programming language they have used in the GUI -programming course, with novices being more successful using the BBL and experienced students more successful in the TBL."
