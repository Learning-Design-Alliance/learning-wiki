The overview's synthesis of 41 systematic reviews of professional behavior change interventions reports that "passive approaches are generally ineffective and unlikely to result in behavior change." No effect size is printed for this summary judgment.

"In general, passive approaches are generally ineffective and unlikely to result in behavior change."
