# AVT learners were more motivated to prepare and found the experience more enjoyable than learners who did a practice exam

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The fraction of AVT participants who spent more than 1 hour preparing for their session was almost twice as high as in the PracticeExam group, and learners rated AVT sessions as more enjoyable; the AVT group gave an average recommender rating of 4.2 out of 5. [→ Ali Malik 2024](#ali-malik-2024)

## Evidence

### Ali Malik 2024

Ali Malik, Juliette Woodrow, and Chris Piech. 2024. Learners Teaching Novices: An Uplifting Alternative Assessment. In Proceedings of the 55th ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education V. 1 (SIGCSE 2024). https://doi.org/10.1145/3626252.3630887

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exit-survey data from the RCT comparing the AVT group (n=15) and PracticeExam group (n=14) on preparation time and enjoyability (Figure 3). The article also reports the AVT group's average recommender rating of 4.2 out of 5 and positive qualitative feedback from learners.

> "We can see this fraction is almost twice as high as for the AVT group, suggesting that AVT participants took their preparation more seriously. We also tried to measure how enjoyable AVT was for learners. Figure 3 (right) shows that leaners found the AVT sessions more enjoyable compared to those who did a practice exam."
