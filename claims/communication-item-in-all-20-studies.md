## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Steiner-Hofbauer Verena 2017

Steiner-Hofbauer Verena, Schrank Beate, Holzinger Anita. (2017). What is a good doctor? Wiener Medizinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10354-017-0597-8

`q2 · i?`

Occurrence analysis across the 20 included studies and questionnaires (Table 3). The review reports that 19 of the studies/questionnaires named an item of general interpersonal qualities and medical competence, 18 included ethics, and questions on medical management or teaching/research/continuous education were "found only in half of the studies/questionnaires".

> "Most of the included articles and questionnaires include more than one category but all 20 included studies or questionnaires contained at least one item or question of the category “communication and patient involvement”."
