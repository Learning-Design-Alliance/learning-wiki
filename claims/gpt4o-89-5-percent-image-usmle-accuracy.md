# GPT-4o answered image-based USMLE sample items with 89.5% accuracy versus GPT-4's 73.4%, a numerically higher but not statistically significant difference

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On 38 image-based USMLE sample questions, GPT-4o achieved 89.5% accuracy (95% CI 74.4–96.1%) and GPT-4 achieved 73.4% (95% CI 57.0–85.5%), with no statistically significant difference (P = 0.137). [→ Xiang Y 2025](#xiang-y-2025)

## Evidence

### Xiang Y 2025

Xiang Y, Wei C. (2025). The performance of ChatGPT on medical image-based assessments and implications for medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07752-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Evaluation of all 38 publicly available image-based USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK sample items, each submitted once per model via the ChatGPT web interface with the prompt "Provide only an answer choice without explanation"; accuracy compared by chi-square/Fisher exact test. The article reports "no statistically significant difference (P = 0.137)".

> "GPT-4 achieved an accuracy of 73.4% (95% CI, 57.0% to 85.5%), while GPT-4o outperformed with an accuracy rate of 89.5% (95% CI, 74.4% to 96.1%), with no statistically significant difference (P = 0.137)"
