## Evidence

### He Xuecheng MD 2025

He Xuecheng MD, Gao Tao MD, Tang Jianfei MD, Jiang Zengxin MD, PhD, Zhang Zeng MD, PhD, Lin E. MD, Yuan Hengfeng MD, PhD. (2025). Assessment of simulation training efficacy in improving microsurgical skills: a retrospective analysis. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000002259

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective cohort of 232 patients (1191 screened); Table 2 reports Zone 1A digits: survival 81.8% vs 66.7% (P = 0.029), replantation time 94.2 (6.5) vs 126.5 (11.9) min (P < 0.01), and "a lower rate of arterial insufficiency" (10.2% vs 23.2%, P = 0.028). Venous congestion, cold intolerance, pulp atrophy, pain and neuroma did not differ (P > 0.05).

> "Trained microsurgeons accomplished replantation in less time, and the trained group saw a lower rate of arterial insufficiency, compared with the control group (9 [10.2%] vs. 16 [23.2%], P = 0.028, RD [CIs]: −25.1%, 1.3%)."
