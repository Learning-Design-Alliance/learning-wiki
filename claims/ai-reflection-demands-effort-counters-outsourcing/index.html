Related Claims¶

  • Students' generative AI use may function either as a scaffold supporting thinking or as a shortcut bypassing essential cognitive processes — related
  • In interviews with 25 engineering students at one Swedish university, students described GenAI as acceptable when it stimulates reflection and directly copying its outputs as cheating, and one used its fallibility as a prompt to double-check — related
  • Students' main AI challenges are content reliability and depth, and most expect AI to serve as tutor, creative partner, and critical thinking partner rather than mere content generator — related
  • Nearly all students valued learning through effort while feeling drawn toward AI, and split into those who limited use and those whose use conflicted with stated values — related
  • Student engagement with generative AI in unsupervised assessments forms a spectrum bounded by cognitive outsourcing and cognitive reallocation — related
