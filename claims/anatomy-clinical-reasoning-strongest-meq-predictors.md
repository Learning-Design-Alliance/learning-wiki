## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The five MEQ* subjects together explained 53.5% of MEQ-1 variance; anatomy alone explained 36.2% and added clinical reasoning raised this to 47.4%. [→ Büssing Oliver 2021](#bussing-oliver-2021)

## Evidence

### Büssing Oliver 2021

Büssing Oliver, Ehlers Jan P., Zupanic Michaela. (2021). The prognostic validity of the formative for the summative MEQ (Modified Essay Questions). GMS Journal for Medical Education 38(6), Doc99. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001495.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis using the five subjects common to MEQ* and MEQ-1 (N=88). The article reports the subjects "explained 53.5% of the variance in the MEQ-1", with anatomy alone explaining 36.2% and anatomy plus clinical reasoning 47.4% total; all but radiology were significant predictors.

> "Scores in the subjects of anatomy, physiology, clinical reasoning, biochemistry, and radiology in the MEQ* explained 53.5% of the variance in the MEQ-1"
