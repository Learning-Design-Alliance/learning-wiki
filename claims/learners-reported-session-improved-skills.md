`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Postsession survey of 32 learners. The article reports 94% (30 of 32) improved confidence in post-ROSC care, 84% (27 of 32) improved communication skills, and 75% (24 of 32) improved leadership skills.

> "Ninety-four percent of learners (30 of 32) reported that the session improved their confidence in caring for patients in the post-ROSC period. A majority of interns reported that they thought the session improved their communication skills (27 of 32, 84%) and leadership skills (24 of 32, 75%)."
