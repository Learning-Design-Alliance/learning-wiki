## Evidence

### Gökhan Özdemir and M. Can Çiftçibaşı 2017

Gökhan Özdemir and M. Can Çiftçibaşı. (2017). Effect of pop music on students’ attitudes to music lessons. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2017.3321

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest-posttest experimental study of 85 experimental-group students taught the syncopation/offbeat topic with Turkish pop songs via karaoke recordings for four weeks; t tests per item showed significant increases, e.g. item 3 t(168) = 9.07, p = 0.00, and item 12 t(168) = 5.63, p = 0.00. "positive significant difference" was found for all other items.

> "As there was a positive significant difference observed between pretest and posttest attitude points for all other items , the use of pop music samples in song learning during music lessons may be said to positively affect the attitudes of students to music lessons."
