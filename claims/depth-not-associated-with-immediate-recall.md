## Evidence

### Vangelis Tsiligkiris 2026

Vangelis Tsiligkiris. (2026). What students ask matters: LLM interaction depth, task quality, and immediate recall in higher education. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00617-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ANCOVA and gain-score OLS regressions on 22 students' immediate post-test scores. No interaction feature predicted recall or gains; the article reports no statistically significant association and equivalence was not tested. Power was limited (approximately 0.27 to detect r = 0.30).

> "Across both ANCOVA (Post-test as outcome) and gain-score specifications, Depth, Volume, and Pacing did not predict post-test scores or gains once baseline knowledge was included (all p ≥ .469; Depth: β = −0.014, p = .728)."
