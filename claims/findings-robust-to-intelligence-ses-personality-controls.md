## Evidence

### Wang Song 2019

Wang Song, Zhao Yajun, Li Jingguang, Wang Xu, Luo Kui, Gong Qiyong. (2019). Brain structure links trait conscientiousness to academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-48704-1

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Supplemental analyses adding general intelligence, family SES (SSS) and the other four NEO-FFI traits as covariates in the same sample. The printed partial correlations under intelligence control were r = 0.33 and r = −0.31; analogous results held for SES (partial r = 0.32 / −0.28) and other Big Five traits (partial r = 0.31 / −0.27).

> "trait conscientiousness was still correlated with the GMV in the bilateral SPL (partial r = 0.33, p < 0.001) and right MFG (partial r = −0.31, p < 0.001), even after controlling for general intelligence, gender, age and total GMV."
