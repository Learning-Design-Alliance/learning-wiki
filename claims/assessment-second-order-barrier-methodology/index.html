Donnelly 2011¶

Donnelly, D., McGarr, O. and O'Reilly, J. (2011). A Framework for Teachers' Integration of ICT Into Their Classroom Practice. Computers and Education;57(2), 1469-1483. https://hdl.handle.net/10344/2340

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview data from the initial study with Irish science teachers. One teacher explained that "they're measured in September by the results", so teachers see no need to change methodology when traditional approaches deliver exam points, illustrating a second-order barrier.

"They know they don't have to, why change your teaching methodology when at the end of the day you can turn out the results? And they're measured in September by the results."
