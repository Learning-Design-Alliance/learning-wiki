Evidence¶

Maass 2016¶

Maass, J.K. and Pavlik Jr., P.I. (2016). Modeling the Influence of Format and Depth during Effortful Retrieval Practice. Proceedings of the 9th International Conference on Educational Data Mining. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED592715

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory affect modeling within the PFA-based logistic regression: confusion (averaged before/after practice) was the only affect improving model fit, and the Prior Incorrect x Confusion parameter (estimate -0.18, Z = -3.78) was retained. The authors call this work exploratory.

"when a learner answers more questions incorrectly, higher confusion predicts a much larger negative effect than if a learner has higher confusion but is still having mostly successful practice."
