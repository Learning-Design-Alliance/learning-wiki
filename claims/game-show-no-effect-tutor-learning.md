## Evidence

### Matsuda 2013

Matsuda, N., Yarzebinski, E., Keiser, V., Raizada, R., Stylianides, G. J., & Koedinger, K. R. (2013). Studying the Effect of a Competitive Game Show in a Learning by Teaching Environment. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0009-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on post-test procedural scores with pre-test as covariate in the randomized classroom study; despite a trend favoring Game Show after adjustment, the condition effect was not reliable. Conceptual knowledge also showed no condition difference.

> "an ANCOV A using the pre-test score as a cova riate did not confirm a statistically reliable condition difference on the post-test of the procedural skill test; F(1,66)=0.23, p=0. 63"
