# Flipped classroom students achieve significantly higher post-test knowledge scores than lecture-based students in practical gastrointestinal anatomy (Cohen's d = 0.87)

## Evidence

### Mohammadi Shabnam 2026

Mohammadi Shabnam, Mastour Haniye, Dadgar Moghadam Maliheh, Emadzadeh Ali. (2026). Changing the landscape of medical anatomy education: impact of flipped classroom pedagogy on learning outcomes and perceptions of medical students in practical gastrointestinal anatomy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09522-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of flipped (n=28) vs. lecture-based (n=35) groups using a 30-item blueprint-based multiple-choice post-test. The flipped group's mean post-test score was significantly higher, with "Cohen's d = 0.87".

> "students in the flipped classroom group achieved significantly higher post-test scores than those in the control group (mean ± SD: 20.34 ± 3.10 vs. 17.42 ± 3.22; t₆₁ = 3.65, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 0.87)"
