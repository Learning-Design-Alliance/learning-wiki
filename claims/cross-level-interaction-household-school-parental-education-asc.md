## Evidence

### Horoz 2025

Horoz, N., van Atteveldt, N. M., Oude Groeniger, J., Koot, J. R., ..., & Buil, J. M. (2025). Household- and school-level parental education and academic self-concept development in elementary school. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00354-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple-slope probing of a significant cross-level interaction (interaction B = 0.003, p = 0.015) in the multilevel model showed the household-education association significant only in higher parental education schools. No standardized effect size is printed; the authors call the effects small.

> "children of lower-educated parents reported less positive ASC levels than children of higher-educated parents in higher parental education schools (B = −0.056, S.E. = 0.025, p = 0.026, CI [−0.106, −0.007]), while in lower parental education schools, there was no signi ﬁcant association between household-level parental education and child-level ASC (B = −0.027, S.E. = 0.020, p = 0.174, CI [−0.067, 0.012])."
