Evidence¶

Hosna Hosseini & Zargham Ghabanchi 2014¶

Hosna Hosseini & Zargham Ghabanchi. (2014). The Effect of Portfolio Assessment on EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension and Motivation. English Language Teaching, 7(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n5p110

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Mann-Whitney U test on motivation questionnaire posttest scores of the same 65-student quasi-experiment; the article reports "a significant difference between the groups (u=403.500, P=0.049<0.05, two tailed)". Pretest motivation did not differ (U=557.000, p=0.815).

"The findings obtained from the Mann-Whitney Utest revealed a significant difference between the groups (u=403.500, P=0.049<0.05, two tailed). The participants in the experimental group enjoying portfolio-based instruction ranked higher in the motivation test than the control group."
