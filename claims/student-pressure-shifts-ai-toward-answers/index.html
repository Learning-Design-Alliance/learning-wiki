Alex Liu 2026¶

Alex Liu, Min Sun, Lief Esbenshade, Victor Tian, Zachary Zhang, and Kevin He. (2026). Teacher-Authored Prompts for Configuring StudentAI Dialogue: K12 Classroom Implementation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16738 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.16738

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

Analysis of AI behavioral adherence under explicit student pressure (e.g., requests to just tell the answer or rewrite work). The article reports r = 0.14, p<.001, a small printed correlation, and notes the AI maintained scaffolded interaction in the majority of cases.

"the likelihood of the AI providing a direct answer increased significantly (26.1% vs. 12.5% baseline;r= 0.14,p<.001), representing a 13.6 percentage point rise."
