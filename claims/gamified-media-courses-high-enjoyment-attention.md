## Evidence

### Bajko et al. 2016

Robert Bajko, Jaigris Hodson, Katie Seaborn, Pamela Livingstone, Deborah Fels. (2016). Edugamifying Media Studies: Student Engagement, Enjoyment, and Interest in Two Multimedia and Social Media Undergraduate Classrooms. Information Systems Education Journal 14(6). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135365

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey data from the study's 37-question online instrument administered in week seven and week twelve of two gamified courses. "91% reported that they found the course enjoyable or very enjoyable" at midpoint, with 88% at term end; no inferential test is reported.

> "At the midpoint, 91% reported that they found the course enjoyable or very enjoyable while 88% reported the same findings at the end of the term."

### Bajko et al. 2016 (2)

Robert Bajko, Jaigris Hodson, Katie Seaborn, Pamela Livingstone, Deborah Fels. (2016). Edugamifying Media Studies: Student Engagement, Enjoyment, and Interest in Two Multimedia and Social Media Undergraduate Classrooms. Information Systems Education Journal 14(6). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135365

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same two-wave descriptive survey of the gamified CMN 450 and ITM 445 courses. "88% and 85% respectively disagreed" that the course did not hold their attention; percentages are descriptive with no effect size or significance test printed.

> "At the midpoint and at the end of the term, 88% and 85% respectively disagreed that the course did not hold their attention at all."
