`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article cites the TIMS video study as evidence that no schools showed high cognitive activation with low classroom management ratings, while the reverse pattern occurred frequently, supporting the asymmetrical interdependence of the two dimensions.

> "In the TIMS video study (Klieme et al., 2001), there were no schools in the sample with high cognitive activation ratings and low CM ratings, while the opposite was often the case."
