Thematic results on skill assessment from the teacher focus groups. Teachers described assessment formats ranging from script writing and oral presentations to video-based and performance-based tasks, but "Teachers noted the difficulty of assessing subt leties such as tone, audience alignment, or emotional intelligence in standardized formats ( COM16)." Digital literacy assessments were described as difficult to design in ways capturing critical reasoning and ethical tool use.
