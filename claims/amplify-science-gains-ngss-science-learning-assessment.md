# 1st graders in Amplify Science intervention classrooms significantly outperformed comparison classrooms on an NGSS-focused three-dimensional science learning assessment (g = 0.24)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q4` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q4 i1` Students in intervention classrooms scored significantly higher on the NGSS-focused science learning assessment, an effect of 0.24 standard deviation units (Hedges's g, p < .001). [→ Harris 2023](#harris-2023)

## Evidence

### Harris 2023

Harris, C. J., Murphy, R., Feng, M., & Rutstein, D. (2023). Supporting science learning and literacy development together: Initial results from a curriculum study in 1st grade classrooms. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638494

`q4 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Group-randomized controlled trial across 40 schools and 2,035 1st graders, analyzed with multilevel linear regression controlling for school and student characteristics and prior reading achievement. The intervention effect was "statistically significant (p < .001)" with "an effect size of 0.24 standard deviation units (Hedges's g)."

> "The estimated impact on performance on the science learning assessment was statistically significant (p < .001) and corresponds to an effect size of 0.24 standard deviation units (Hedges's g)."
