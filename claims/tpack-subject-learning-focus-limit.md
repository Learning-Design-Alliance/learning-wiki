### Garry Falloon 2020

Garry Falloon. (2020). From digital literacy to digital competence: the teacher digital competency (TDC) framework. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09767-4

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Literature-based evaluation of the TPACK model in the article's framework-review section. The author credits TPACK as a more comprehensive, holistic (non-hierarchical) model than SAMR, but argues its subject-learning focus limits it for an expanded view of digital competence.

> "it theorises the relationship between, and blending of pedagogical, content and technological knowledge to enhance the learning performance of students in, and sometimes across, subject disciplines. While valuable for this purpose, TPACK in its original form is limited in its usefulness for building a broader-based conceptualisation of teacher digital competence."
