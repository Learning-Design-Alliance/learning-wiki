This is an author-interpretation section of the perspective, not an empirical result. The authors argue the article as version of record creates friction and information loss, and that 'missing structure and context increase the risk of misinterpretation and distortion' in AI-mediated research.

> "PDFs remain effective for reading, but they constrain how research can be validated, reused, evaluated, and acted upon. Data are often separated from ﬁgures, ﬁgures are locked as static imag es, and methods, intermediate results, and negative ﬁndings are compressed or omitted due to format constraints."
