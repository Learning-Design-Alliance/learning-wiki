### Jionghao Lin 2025

Jionghao Lin, Jiarui Rao, Sandy Yiyang Zhao, Yuting Wang, Ashish Gurung, Amanda Barany, Jaclyn Ocumpaugh, Ryan S. Baker, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2025). Automatic Large Language Models Creation of Interactive Learning Lessons. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17356 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2506.17356](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2506.17356)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consensus ratings across three generated lessons per segmentation approach (Table 2): Scenario 1 Feedback scored 1/3 for one- and two-segment approaches versus 3/3 for three segments. The authors attribute the improvement to decomposition enabling the LLM to allocate more attention to feedback content.

> "The provision of feedback (codeFeedback) showed notable improvements in prompts using three or more segments compared to the one- or two-segment approaches."
