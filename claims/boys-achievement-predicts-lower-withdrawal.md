## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Boys' academic achievement at age 8 predicted decreased social withdrawal from age 8 to 10 (β = −.22), and achievement at age 12 similarly predicted attenuated withdrawal from age 12 to 14 (β = −.22). [→ Stenseng 2022](#stenseng-2022)

## Evidence

### Stenseng 2022

Stenseng, F., Tingstad, E. B., Wichstrøm, L., & Skalicka, V. (2022). Social withdrawal and academic achievement, intertwined over years? Bidirectional effects from primary to upper secondary school. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 92, 1354–1365. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12504

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Random intercept cross-lagged panel modelling of biannual teacher ratings in the Trondheim Early Secure Study community sample (n = 845), ages 6–14. The within-person analysis found "Boys’ AA at age 8 predicted decreased SW from age 8 to age 10 (β = −.22, p = .04)" with a similar age-12 effect.

> "Boys’ AA at age 8 predicted decreased SW from age 8 to age 10 (β = −.22, p = .04), and a similar effect was observed for higher AA at age 12, predicting attenuated SW from age 12 to age 14 (β = −.22, p = .005)."
