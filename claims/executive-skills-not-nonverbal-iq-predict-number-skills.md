## Evidence

### Moll K. 2015

Moll K., Snowling M. J., Göbel S. M., Hulme C. (2015). Early language and executive skills predict variations in number and arithmetic skills in children at family-risk of dyslexia and typically developing controls. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.03.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the preliminary path model, nonverbal IQ (WPPSI-III Block Design and Object Assembly composite) showed no direct or indirect effect on arithmetic and was removed. Executive functions (Go/No-Go, HTKS, Visual Search composite) predicted counting and number knowledge at t2.

> "A preliminary model included nonverbal IQ at t1 as a domain-general predictor of all t2 measures (counting skills, number knowledge and phonological awareness) but because nonverbal IQ did not have any direct or indirect effect on arithmetic skills, it was dropped from the model."
