### M. Tuveri 2025

M. Tuveri, V. Fanti (2025). The role of natural language in understanding the universe: a teaching-learning sequence for high school students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.19812](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.19812)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of students' crossroads definitions in the TLS. The authors report that "Misconceptions were common, particularly regarding gravity", giving two example group definitions, and separately that the Big Bang was often called a 'primordial big explosion'. These are qualitative observations; no test or effect size is reported.

> "Misconceptions were common, particularly regarding gravity, with one group defining it as "what keeps bodies from floating," and another describing it as a force perpendicular to the plane."
