## Evidence

### Santana‐Monagas 2025

Santana‐Monagas, E., Núñez‐Regueiro, F., & Núñez, J. L. (2025). Does motivation lead to academic success, or conversely? Reciprocal relations between autonomous and controlled motivation, and mathematics achievement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 513–529. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12736

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-person (trait) associations estimated in the RI-CLPMs of 1042 students. The article reports the positive random-intercept correlations (ψModel1 = .180; ψModel2 = .163) and separately that "trait factors between students' controlled motivation and their achievement levels correlated weakly and negatively (ψModel1 = − .126, p = .054; ψModel2 = − .123, p = .060)". No standardized effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Finally, random intercepts for students' autonomous motivation and achievement correlated positively (ψModel1 = .180, p = .000; ψModel2 = .163, p = .002), indicating that higher levels of motivation were systematically related to higher levels of achievement throughout the year"
