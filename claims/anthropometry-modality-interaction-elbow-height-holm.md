### Amirthanayagam Anumithra 2026

Amirthanayagam Anumithra, Zecca Massimiliano, Chitrakar Anisha, Chavda Vijay, Moss Esther L. (2026). Looks like a surgeon? Impact of anthropometry on the kinematics of surgical novices: counterbalanced cross-over design study of minimally invasive surgery. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-04077-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects model analysis of 643 upper-arm observations from 54 surgical novices, with random intercepts for participant and task bout and Holm correction across eight interaction tests. The model revealed "a significant Anthropometry × Modality interaction for elbow height" with the printed coefficients and p values; no standardised effect size was printed.

> "Linear Mixed-Effects Models (controlling for participant random effects and sequence/period order) revealed a significant Anthropometry × Modality interaction for elbow height (angular velocity: −0.55 °/s per 10 cm, 95% CI − 0.83 to − 0.28, p < 0.001; jerk: −0.142 m/s³ per 10 cm, 95% CI − 0.240 to − 0.044, p = 0.005), which remained significant after Holm correction and in all sensitivity analyses."
