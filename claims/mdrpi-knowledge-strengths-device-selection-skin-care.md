Aydinli Ayşe; Deniz Doğan Sevgi. (2025). Investigation of Nurses' Level of Knowledge on Medical Device‐Related Pressure Injuries: A Descriptive and Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70776

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Item‐level descriptive analysis of correct responses in the 217‐nurse survey (Table 3). The authors call these rates satisfactory given the total score and suggest nurses pay attention to device selection and skin care under devices; percentages are descriptive.

> "the three items with the highest correct response rates were as follows: “The appropriate medical device with the right size should be chosen for the patient to avoid MDRPIs” (94.9%), “The skin under the medical device should be maintained clean and dry” (94.9%), and “Critical patients, newborns, and the elderly are the high‐risk groups for MDRPIs” (92.2%)."
