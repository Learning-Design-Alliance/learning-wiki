Rupprecht, S., Paulus, P. & Walach, H. (2017). Mind the teachers! The impact of mindfulness training on self-regulation and classroom performance in a sample of German school teacher. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(4), 565-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.4.565

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test results in the waitlist-controlled study; Table 3 reports Cohen's d of 1.09 (tendency to resign), 0.36 (inner calm), 1.08 (teacher self-efficacy), 0.55 (general self-efficacy) and 0.77–0.85 for emotion-regulation scales. The article also reports "Both general and teacher self -efficacy improved statistically significantly in the intervention group".
