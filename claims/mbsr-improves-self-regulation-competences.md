# In one small non-randomised waitlist-controlled study, MBSR improved teachers' self-reported self-efficacy and emotion regulation, with mixed effects across self-regulation competences

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a non-randomised waitlist-controlled study of 32 German teachers, the MBSR group reported statistically significant within-group improvements on self-report scales for tendency to resign, inner calm, general and teacher self-efficacy, and emotional competence; between-group effects at post-test were large for reduced resignation and teacher self-efficacy, medium for general self-efficacy, small for inner calm, and near zero for proactive problem-solving. ## Evidence

### Rupprecht 2017

Rupprecht, S., Paulus, P. & Walach, H. (2017). Mind the teachers! The impact of mindfulness training on self-regulation and classroom performance in a sample of German school teachers. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(4), 565-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.4.565

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot non-randomised waitlist-controlled study of 32 self-selected German school teachers (intervention n=18, control n=14; groups assigned by age and school type), with the quantitative outcomes below measured by teacher self-report questionnaires (the study also ran qualitative interviews). Post-test results: Table 3 reports Cohen's d of 1.09 (tendency to resign), 0.36 (inner calm), 1.08 (teacher self-efficacy), 0.55 (general self-efficacy) and 0.77–0.85 for emotion-regulation scales, and 0.03 for proactive problem-solving, where neither group changed significantly. The effects on self-regulation competences are therefore mixed rather than uniformly medium to large. The article also reports "Both general and teacher self -efficacy improved statistically significantly in the intervention group".

> "Teachers of the intervention group reported statistically significant improvements in the tendency to resign and the ability to find inner calm and bal ance while non -significant changes were observed in the control group."
