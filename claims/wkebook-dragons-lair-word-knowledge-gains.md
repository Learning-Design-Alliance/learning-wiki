### Stephanie L. Day 2024

Stephanie L. Day, Jin K. Hwang, Tracy Arner, Danielle McNamara, Carol M. Connor. (2024). Choose Your Own Adventure: Interactive E-Books to Improve Word Knowledge and Comprehension Skills. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In this feasibility study, all 421 participating students read Dragon's Lair for 20 minutes per session three days a week. A paired samples t-test showed the gain was "significant (t(420) = 29.702, p < .001)". Means in Table 1 rose from 12.21 at pretest to 13.34 at posttest; no effect size was reported.

> "To determine whether students had significant gains between the Dragon’s Lair Word Knowledge Task pretest and posttest, we first ran a paired samples t-test which was significant (t(420) = 29.702, p < .001)."
