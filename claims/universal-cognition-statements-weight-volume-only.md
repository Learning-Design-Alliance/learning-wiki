# Children's statements about universal properties of cognition emerged only in weight and volume measurement activities, not in length or area activities

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across the 16 activities, children made statements judging cognitive difficulty or ease only in weight and volume activities; no such statements occurred in length or area activities. [→ Ebru Aydın 2022](#ebru-aydn-2022)

## Evidence

### Ebru Aydın 2022

Ebru Aydın, Çağlayan Dinçer. (2022). “I did it wrong, but i know it”: Young children’s metacognitive knowledge expressions during peer interactions in math activities. Thinking Skills and Creativity 45 (2022) 101104. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2022.101104

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative finding from the full set of 16 activities: universal-property statements (e.g., judging whether something is cognitively hard or easy to understand) appeared exclusively in weight and volume tasks. Examples include Michael's "it is soft and light" in the Bags activity.

> "According to the findings from the 16 activities, the children did not make any statements about general cognitive structures in activities based on length and area measurement skills; they only made such statements in activities based on weight and volume measurement skills."
