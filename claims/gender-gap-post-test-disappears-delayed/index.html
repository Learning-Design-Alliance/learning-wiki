Evidence¶

van Hove 2017¶

van Hove, S., vanderhoven, E., & Cornillie, F. (2017). The tablet for Second Language Vocabulary Learning: Keyboard, Stylus or Multiple Choice. Comunicar, 50, XXV. https://doi.org/10.3916/C50-2017-05

q3 · i? · causal · r2

A t-test of total mean scores in the quasi-experimental study found girls (M=72.14%) scored higher than boys (M=63.65%) on the post-test, a difference that was no longer significant at delayed post-test (p=.074).

"girls (M=72.14%; Sd=19.27%) achieved higher results than boys (M=63.65%; Sd=21.28%) on the post-test (t(245)=-2.406; p=.017). This significant effect disappeared when taking the delayed post-test into account (t(220)=-4.797; p=.074)"
