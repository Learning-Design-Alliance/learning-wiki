## Evidence

### Koponen 2017

Koponen, T., Georgiou, G., Salmi, P., Leskinen, M., & Aro, M. (2017). A Meta-Analysis of the Relation Between RAN and Mathematics. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(7), 977-992. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000182

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Null results on design and sample-type moderators; no effect sizes printed. The article attributes the sample-type null to heterogeneous atypical populations and states the study "did not provide any evidence that there are differences among these groups."

> "Although there was a trend showing that concurrent correlations are higher than longitudinal correlations, the difference was not significant."
