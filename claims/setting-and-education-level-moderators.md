## Evidence

### Adesope 2017

Adesope, O. O., Trevisan, D. A., & Sundararajan, N. (2017). Rethinking the Use of Tests: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Testing. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654316689306

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analyses of study setting and educational level (Table 3): classroom k = 30 (g = 0.67), laboratory k = 223 (g = 0.62); primary g = 0.64, secondary g = 0.83, postsecondary g = 0.60, between-levels QΒ(4) = 50.50, p < .001, with secondary significantly higher than postsecondary.
