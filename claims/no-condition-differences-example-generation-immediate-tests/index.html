Evidence¶

Esther Ziegler 2021¶

Esther Ziegler, Dragan Trninic, Manu Kapur. (2021). Micro productive failure and the acquisition of algebraic procedural knowledge. Instructional Science 49, 313–336. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09544-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Univariate ANOVA on the ratio of correctly solved problems averaged over the six immediate learning tests (process measure 4); the article reports F(1,77) = 0.95, p = 0.333, η2p = 0.01, nonsignificant, with descriptively higher PS-I means.

"but no significant effect on immediate learning tests, albeit the means were, on average, still higher for the PS-I condition, F(1,77) = 0.95, p = 0.333, η2p = 0.01."
