Zhorabekova A 2026¶

Zhorabekova A, Duisenova M, Kurmasheva M and Yesnazar A (2026). Acceptance of AI-gamified adaptive learning in EFL: bifactor evidence for essential unidimensionality and the limits of subscale scoring. Front. Educ. 11:1929123. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1929123

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Full-sample (N = 401) ML confirmatory factor analysis comparing one-factor, four-factor, second-order, and bifactor models. The article reports the bifactor model best [χ2(52) = 151.9; CFI = .979] and lowest AIC (9,866.0) and BIC (10,021.8).

"The one-factor model fitted poorly [χ2(65) = 555.1; RMSEA = .137, 90% CI [.127, .148]; CFI = .895; SRMR = .054], so a strictly unidimensional first-order representation is inadequate despite the parallel-analysis result."
