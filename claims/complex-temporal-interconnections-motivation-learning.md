### Törmänen 2025

Törmänen, T., Ketonen, E., Lehtoaho, E., Turunen, M., Zabolotna, K., Shubina, T., & Järvenoja, H. (2025). Situational Motivation in Academic Learning: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10036-0

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative synthesis across both waves reports "the momentary wave had most of the studies (78%, n = 14)" showing temporal interconnections, including links between task values, effort, engagement, expectancies, costs, and outcomes such as exam performance and course dropout.

> "The situation-specific wave had 29% (n = 19) of the studies and the momentary wave had most of the studies (78%, n = 14) that showed evidence on how different motivational constructs relate to each other over time and in different situations."
