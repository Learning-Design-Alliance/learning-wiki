Evidence¶

Xin et al. 2015¶

Huaibo Xin, Monica Kempland, Faustina H. Blankson. (2015). ADAPTABILITY AND REPLICABILITY OF WEB-FACILITATED, HYBRID, AND ONLINE LEARNING IN AN UNDERGRADUATE EXERCISE PSYCHOLOGY COURSE. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology – January 2015, volume 14 issue 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057333

q3 · i? · causal · r1

One-way ANOVA results for these measures: mid-term exam F = .38, p = .68; journal entries F = .62, p = .54; combined total F = 2.22, p = .11. No statistically significant difference was detected; equivalence was not tested.

"The statistically significant differences among the means of three sections or between the means of any two of the three sections for the mid-term exam, the total of four online journal entries, and the combined total of the mid-term, final exams, and four journal entries were not revealed."
