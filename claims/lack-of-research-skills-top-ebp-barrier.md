`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported ranking of barriers from the cross-sectional survey of Colombian physical therapists. "a lack of research skills was the most important barrier to using EBP" for 41%; the second and third barriers were lack of understanding of statistical analysis and inability to apply research findings to individual patients with unique characteristics; English comprehension was a significant barrier for 21.9%.

> "Forty-one percent of those surveyed stated that a lack of research skills was the most important barrier to using EBP."
