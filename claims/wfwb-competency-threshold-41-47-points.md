`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Following Gallagher et al.'s proficiency-based progression guidelines, "the mean rubric score and completion time of experts were defined as the competency thresholds" from the 41-participant simulation study data.

> "Based on these data, the competency threshold for the rubric score was determined to be 41/47 (87.2%) elements correctly completed. Competency threshold was set at 33 min 27 s (i.e., 1 standard deviation above the mean completion time for experts), reflecting our focus on accuracy over completion time."
