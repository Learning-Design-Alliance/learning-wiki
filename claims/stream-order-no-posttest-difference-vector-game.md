`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of the two level sequences assigned to teams sharing a river map. The KS test gave p = 0.64; equivalence was not tested beyond this non-significant result. Stream type was negatively correlated with growth in attempts (ρ = −0.54, p <10−4).

> "The Kolmogorov-Smirnov test on pre- and post-test results does not show a difference between the two game streams – starting with level A (Stream 1) or starting with level B (Stream 2), p = 0.64 did not seem to influence the learning."
