# Learning conditions did not differ in total questions attempted, ruling out differential effort quantity as the source of create-question differences

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Total questions generated (create and non-create combined) did not differ across the notetaking, explaining, and teaching groups. [→ Sarah Shi Hui Wong 2026](#sarah-shi-hui-wong-2026)

## Evidence

### Sarah Shi Hui Wong 2026

Sarah Shi Hui Wong, Stephen Lim. (2026). The Powerful Teacher: A Power Hypothesis for the Benefits of Learning-by-Teaching. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10097-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Manipulation check in the same experiment: a 3 × 2 ANOVA on total questions attempted showed no group difference, so differences in create question generation "cannot be attributed to more (or fewer) attempts in any particular learning condition."

> "there was no significant difference across the notetaking (M = 5.51, SD = 1.95), explaining (M = 5.83, SD = 2.51), and teaching (M = 5.47, SD = 2.75) groups in their total questions generated, F(2, 236) = 0.52, p=.60"
