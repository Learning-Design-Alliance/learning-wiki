Birgül et al. 2017¶

Arzu Ergişi Birgül, Elvan Şahin Zeteroglu, Meral Taner Derman. (2017). The Effect of the Activities Enhanced Concerning Time Concept on Time Concept Acquisition of Children. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(12A): 8-12. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051302

Pretest–posttest control-group experiment with 13 experimental and 16 control children at Uludağ University nursery schools; Mann Whitney U Test on Pictorial Form posttest scores. The article reports "a significant difference between the posttest score averages of the experimental and the control group". No effect size is printed.
