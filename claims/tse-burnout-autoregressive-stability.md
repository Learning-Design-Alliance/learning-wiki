`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Autoregressive paths from the final cross-lagged path models with covariates. The article reports "current TSE levels predicted future TSE levels" (β = .299 to .626) and stability paths for exhaustion and disengagement, supporting H9 and H10.
