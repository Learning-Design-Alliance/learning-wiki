`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same cross-sectional institutional-data analysis compared post-remote and pre-remote course grades, reporting that post-remote grades tended to be either the same or a fraction of a letter grade lower than pre-remote grades, with the comparison shown in Figure 1 and appendix tables.

> "Additionally, post-remote grades tended to be either the same or a fraction of a letter grade lower than pre-remote grades, which can be seen in either Figure 1 or in Table 3-A in the appendix."
