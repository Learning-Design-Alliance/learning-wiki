In its career pathways section, the scan reports the BLS projection that programmer employment will "decline by 10% from 2021 to 2031" as automation takes over repetitive tasks such as code formatting. This is a reported projection, not a tested effect.

Carter, B., Shah, Z., Tinsley, B., LeGrand-Dunn, J., Luke Luna, C. (2023, August). Understanding the supports and skills that enable successful pathways for Black learners and workers into non-four-year degree technology careers: A landscape scan. `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The scan's key idea, attributed to the Kapor Center, warns that apprenticeship programs placed in "career pathways that are highly at risk for displacement caused by automation" contribute to industry disparities, and urges sustainable, equitable post-program opportunities.

> "many companies contribute to technology industry disparities by setting up apprenticeship programs within career pathways that are highly at risk for displacement caused by automation"
