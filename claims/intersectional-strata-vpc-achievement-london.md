## Evidence

### Lucy Prior 2022

Lucy Prior, Clare Evans, Juan Merlo and George Leckie. (2022). Sociodemographic inequalities in student achievement: An intersectional multilevel analysis of individual heterogeneity and discriminatory accuracy (MAIHDA) with application to students in London, England. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2211.06321](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2211.06321)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Unadjusted random-intercept models (Model 1) fitted to Department for Education National Pupil Database records for two London cohorts show the variance partitioning coefficient was "around 30%" at age 11 and 25% at age 16, indicating substantial between-strata clustering.

> "In Model 1, the VPC statistics show a large proportion of the overall variance in student achievement positioned between intersectional strata at age 11 (around 30%), and a slightly smaller though still substantial amount at age 16 (25%)."
