## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin 2016

Shih-Yin Lin, Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Student Difficulties in Translating between Mathematical and Graphical Representations in Introductory Physics. Originally published 1997. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.05466](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.05466)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

A quasi-experimental comparison of three recitation groups of 95 calculus-based introductory physics students on a quiz. T-tests showed Group 1 outperformed Group 3, "in part (a) (ii) (p = 0.022, Cohen’s d = 0.628)" and in part (a)(iii).

> "T-tests on data in Table  2 reveal that students in Group 1 outperformed students in Group 3 in part (a) (ii) (p = 0.022, Cohen’s d = 0.628) and in part (a) (iii) (p = 0.019, Cohen’s d  = 0. 654)."
