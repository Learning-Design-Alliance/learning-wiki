Evidence¶

Chen 2010¶

Su-Chang Chen, National Penghu University of Science and Technology, Taiwan. (2010). Instructional Experiment Of Practical Competencies-Oriented Teaching Materials In Technical Universities. American Journal of Business Education – September 2010 Volume 3, Number 9. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1058552

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment with 31 experimental (Applied Foreign Languages) and 46 control (Marketing and Logistics Management) freshmen; post-test means were 39.371 vs 32.989, t = 2.655, p = 0.010. The article reports "the learning achievement of the experimental group is significantly better than the control group".

"According to Table 2, a t test showed that the learning achievement of the experimental group is significantly better than the control group and it reaches significance level (0.01)."
