Evidence¶

Mettler et al. 2011¶

Everett Mettler, Christine M. Massey, Philip J. Kellman. (2011). Improving Adaptive Learning Technology through the Use of Response Times. Proceedings of the 33rd Annual Meeting of the Cognitive Science Society. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED549436

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

The online learning company's researchers compared treatment students (n=41) with control students (n=31) receiving standard math lessons, matched for prior performance on standardized tests; the article reports effect sizes of .49 for accuracy and 1.29 for fluency, p < .01.
