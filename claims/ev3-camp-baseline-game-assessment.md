`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-camp assessment in which the 9 students played a 14-level Scratch-based game requiring control-flow instructions; results were recorded per student, with most students needing hints and many unable to use repeat statements. Descriptive only, no effect size.

> "Out of the 9 students who played the game, 3 students could not exceed Level 4, 2 students crossed all the levels without any hints and the rest landed up some-where in the range of 5 − 8."
