## Evidence

### Burston 2018

Burston, J., & Arispe, K. (2018). Looking for a Needle in a Haystack: CALL and Advanced Language Proficiency. CALICO Journal, 35(1). https://doi.org/10.1558/cj.31594

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The authors coded the pedagogical focus of the 47 studies. They report that "nearly 81% of AL2 studies focused on the written language" and that aural-oral skills were targeted in only 16% of the studies.

> "Lastly, in all, nearly 81% of AL2 studies focused on the written language (Figure 3), of which presentational and interpersonal writing accounted for 63% and interpretive reading 18%. In contrast, presentational and interpersonal speaking and interpretive listening together were targeted in only 16% of the studies."
