Phase 1 consensus conference: the same 173 mentors rated each competency's significance on a 5-point scale. Most relevant components included openly communicating concerns (mean 4.8) and nonjudgmental learning from incidents (mean 4.8).

> "the significance assigned by mentors to the different psychological safety competencies was high, with mean scores ranging from 4.4 to 4.8 points (global mean 4.6, SD 0.6; knowledge mean 4.5, SD 0.6; attitude mean 4.6, SD 0.6; skills mean 4.6, SD 0.7)"
