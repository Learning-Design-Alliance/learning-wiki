## Evidence

### O'Connor 2014

O'Connor, E. E., McCormick, M. P., Cappella, E., & McClowry, S. G. (2014). Getting a Good Start in School: Differential Effects of INSIGHTS on the Behaviors and Engagement of Children with Challenging Temperaments. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562775

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three-way interaction (Treatment x time x high maintenance temperament) in Model 2 growth models (Table 4, N = 435). The Tx x time x high maintenance coefficients were -0.12* for behavior problems and -0.02** for off-task behaviors; the engagement growth interaction was non-significant (0.02).

> "In addition, we found significant growth effects of INSIGHTS on reducing behavior problems (γ = -.12, p = .04, E.S. = .58) and off-task behaviors (γ = -.02, p = .04, E.S. = .67) for students with high maintenance temperaments."
