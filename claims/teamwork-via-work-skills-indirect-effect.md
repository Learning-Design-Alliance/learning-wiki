## Evidence

### Clemente Rodríguez-Sabiote 2022

Clemente Rodríguez-Sabiote, Eva María Olmedo-Moreno and Jorge Expósito-López. (2022). The effects of teamwork on critical thinking: A serial mediation analysis of the influence of work skills and educational motivation in secondary school students. Thinking Skills and Creativity. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2022.101063

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

PROCESS model 6 mediation analysis with 10000 bootstrap samples on the 1159-student sample. The first indirect effect through work skills yielded "a standardised coefficient of β = .053" with a 95% CI of .0312-.0717 that does not cover zero, so it is statistically significant.

> "With regards to the first indirect effect ([a1b1] (ind1): X→M1→Y), a standardised coefficient of β = .053 was obtained associated with a standard error of seboot= .0104"

### Clemente Rodríguez-Sabiote 2022 (2)

Clemente Rodríguez-Sabiote, Eva María Olmedo-Moreno and Jorge Expósito-López. (2022). The effects of teamwork on critical thinking: A serial mediation analysis of the influence of work skills and educational motivation in secondary school students. Thinking Skills and Creativity. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2022.101063

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Total and direct effect regressions from the same PROCESS model 6 analysis. The article reports that "both the total effect (β (c)=.55 (p<.001)) and direct effect (β (c´)=.44 (p<.001)) were also statistically significant", indicating partial mediation through work skills.

> "both the total effect (β (c)=.55 (p<.001)) and direct effect (β (c´)=.44 (p<.001)) were also statistically significant"
