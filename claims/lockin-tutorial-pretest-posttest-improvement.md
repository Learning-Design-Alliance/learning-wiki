## Evidence

### Seth DeVore 2015

Seth DeVore, Alexandre Gauthier, Jeremy Levy, and Chandralekha Singh. (2015). Improving Students' Understanding of Lock-In Amplifiers. [doi:10.1119/1.4934957](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4934957)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

A pretest/posttest study of 21 physics graduate students, none involved in developing the tutorial, using matched pairs of lock-in puzzles. The article reports that performance was "roughly 40% across all pretest questions" and "improved to over 80% across all corresponding posttest questions."

> "Student performance on these questions before exposure to the tutorial is roughly 40% across all pretest questions that asked the student to predict either the input or output. This average improved to over 80% across all corresponding posttest questions."
