### Kassaw Chalachew 2023

Kassaw Chalachew, Demareva Valeriia. (2023). Determinants of academic achievement among higher education student found in low resource setting, A systematic review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294585

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional study at Hawassa health college (n = 280) synthesized in the review: students with "Grade Point Average of below 60%" had higher odds of mental distress (AOR = 4.69; 95% CI 2.46–8.96). Similar associations were reported at Aksum and Gondar universities.

> "Students who had Grade Point Average of below 60% were 4.69 times more likely to be mentally distressed as compared to those students who scored Grade Point Average of greater or equal to 60% (AOR = 4.69; 95% CI, [2.46, 8.96)."
