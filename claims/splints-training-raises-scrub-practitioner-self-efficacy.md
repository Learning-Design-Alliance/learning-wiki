## Evidence

### Mohammadi Masoumeh 2025

Mohammadi Masoumeh, Omid Athar, Tarrahi Mohammad Javad, Ghadami Ahmad. (2025). The impact of intraoperative non-technical skills training on scrub practitioners' self-efficacy: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07042-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 30 intervention and 30 control scrub practitioners at two Iranian public hospitals; intervention received two two-hour lecture-plus-video-scenario sessions. Independent t-test comparison of overall self-efficacy showed "no significant difference ... before the training (P = 0.513)" and a significant post-training difference. No effect size printed.

> "The independent t-test revealed no significant difference in overall self-efficacy between the intervention and control groups before the training (P = 0.513). However, after the training, a statistically significant difference was observed (P = 0.025)."
