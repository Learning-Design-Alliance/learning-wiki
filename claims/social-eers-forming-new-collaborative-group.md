### Ying Cao 2025

Ying Cao, Tong Wan, Andrew Burns, Ethan Cusack, and Pierre-Philippe A. Ouimet. (2025). Emergent Explicit Regulation in College Students' Collaborative Scientific Inquiry Learning—Framework and A Case Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.09923](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.09923)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative video case study of one four-student group on Day 4 of the 2015 IMPRESS program. The authors report that 'These social EERs directed the four students to form and maintain a group for collaboration,' and journals from Ashley, Grace, and Jill expressed positive feelings about the new group.

> "Ashley's regulatory move was a jumpstart, breaking the silence and asking everyone's name by signing—initially interpreted by the interpreter, then directly signed between the group members, and lastly conveyed again by the interpreter."
