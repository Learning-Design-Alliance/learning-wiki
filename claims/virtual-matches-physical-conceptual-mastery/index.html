q3 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of single-modality comparisons in the review corpus. The review reports equivalence dominating, citing Husnaini and Chen (2019): a virtual lab "equal to a physical one on simple concepts but superior on difficult concepts and inquiry self-efficacy". Papalazarou et al. (2024), Evangelou and Kotsis (2019) and Oymak and Ogan-Bekiroglu (2021) found no significant differences.

"Husnaini and Chen (2019) found a guided-inquiry virtual laboratory equal to a physical one on simple concepts but superior on difficult concepts and inquiry self-efficacy, while the physical laboratory was superior to inquiry activities requiring learners to plan and refine an experiment."
