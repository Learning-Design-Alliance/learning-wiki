### Martinez 2017

Liza E. Martinez. (2017). Adult Hispanic ESL Students and Graded Readers. The CATESOL Journal, 29(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1164286

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of treatment-group Vocabulary Size Test pre- and posttest raw scores (Table 1). The article notes that all treatment participants except one ran out of time and two arrived late, so they lacked the full 40 minutes; the printed means were 7,308 (pretest) and 6,120 (posttest).

> "For the posttest, the mean was 19% lower than the pretest while the median was 24% lower."
