# Confirmation of expectations has a large, significant positive influence on perceived usefulness, satisfaction, and perceived enjoyment in CSCL

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Confirmation positively and significantly influences perceived usefulness (β = 0.558, ƒ2 = 0.452), satisfaction (β = 0.563, ƒ2 = 0.394), and perceived enjoyment (β = 0.685, ƒ2 = 1.129) in CSCL. [→ Pablo-César Muñoz-Carril 2021](#pablo-cesar-munoz-carril-2021)

## Evidence

### Pablo-César Muñoz-Carril 2021

Pablo-César Muñoz-Carril, Nuria Hernández-Sellés, Eduardo-José Fuentes-Abeledo, Mercedes González-Sanmamed. (2021). Factors influencing students' perceived impact of learning and satisfaction in Computer Supported Collaborative Learning. Computers & Education, 174. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104310

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

PLS structural model on questionnaire data from 701 master's students in online collaborative courses; bootstrapping with 5000 subsamples. The article reports confirmation's paths "with a large effect size", printing ƒ2 = 0.452, 0.394, and 1.129.

> "our results showed that this construct had a significant positive influence, with a large effect size, on perceived usefulness (H4a; β = 0.558; p < 0.001; ƒ2 = 0.452), satisfaction (H4b; β = 0.563; p < 0.001; ƒ2 = 0.394) and on perceived enjoyment (H4c; β = 0.685; p < 0.001; ƒ2 = 1.129)"
