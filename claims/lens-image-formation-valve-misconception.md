## Evidence

### M. Risch 2010

M. Risch. (2010). Investigations about Science Misconceptions. ArXiv.org. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1009.5524](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1009.5524)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Optics questions 6a-c on image formation by a converging lens, answered by 941 students; in question 6b (half lens covered) "image becomes half" drew 618 answers (65.7%), and in 6a 34.3% answered the image vanishes when the lens is removed.

> "Students seem to imagine a lens visually as a kind of valve letting light through, rather than deflecting rays, which combine to form an image."
