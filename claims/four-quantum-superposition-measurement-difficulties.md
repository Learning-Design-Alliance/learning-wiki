The article's barrier-selection section identifies four recurrent difficulties from the physics-education literature, citing prior studies for each: superposition as physical splitting, basis and measurement-context errors, probability-frequency confusion, and notation ambiguity. The article reports no new empirical measurement of these difficulties.

> "The module is organized around four recurrent learner difficulties drawn from the physics-education literature. Barriers were included when they are documented in prior work and can be addressed directly through a two-level Hamiltonian, its eigenstates, and computational-basis measurement."
