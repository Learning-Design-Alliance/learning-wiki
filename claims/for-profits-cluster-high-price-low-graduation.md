## Evidence

### Mamie Lynch 2011

Mamie Lynch, Jennifer Engle, and José L. Cruz. (2011). Priced Out: How the Wrong Financial-Aid Policies Hurt Low-Income Students. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED520199

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of the Landscape of Opportunity scatterplot (Figure 6) plotting net price against six-year graduation rate for the 1,186-institution sample, with bubble size showing Pell concentration. The report states "more than half of for-profit institutions and 94 percent of low-income students at for-profits are concentrated in the upper leftmost corner" and that 65 percent of these institutions are part of the University of Phoenix system.

> "In fact, more than half of for-profit institutions and 94 percent of low-income students at for-profits are concentrated in the upper leftmost corner. This region of the graph shows institutions that require students from families making less than $30,000 per year to contribute more than 100 percent of their average household income (about $17,000). Yet, these higher priced institutions provide students with less than a 1-in-4 chance at graduating."
