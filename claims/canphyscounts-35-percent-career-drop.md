National survey of the Canadian physics community (CanPhysCounts 2020, N=2532, shown in Fig. 2 across academic positions). The article reports the 35% undergraduate-to-career drop and the "almost 60%" drop for BIPOC women and gender diverse physicists. No effect size is printed.

> "From the 2020 CanPhysCounts survey, we see that the percentage of women and gender diverse physicists drops by around 35% between undergraduate students to those in a physics career. This decline is even more notable among BIPOC women and gender diverse physicists, whose representation drops by almost 60% [1]."
