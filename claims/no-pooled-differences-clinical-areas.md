## Evidence

### Hamamoto Filho PT 2021

Hamamoto Filho PT, Lourenção PLTA, Abbade JF, Cecílio-Fernandes D, Caramori JT, Bicudo AM. (2021). Exploring pooled analysis of pretested items to monitor the performance of medical students exposed to different curriculum designs. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0257293

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Fixed-effects pooled analyses of the clinical content areas comparing 2019 and earlier first-year cohorts. Internal medicine showed no significant difference (OR = 0.93, p = 0.544); the article likewise reports null pooled results for pediatrics, surgery, and obstetrics and gynecology.

> "In internal medicine, there were differences on two items: one result favored the new curriculum and the other, the former curriculum. However, the pooled analysis showed no statistically significant difference: OR = 0.93 (CI95% CI 0.735–1.176, p = 0.544)."
