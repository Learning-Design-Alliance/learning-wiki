## Evidence

### Zhang Luocheng 2025

Zhang Luocheng, Patterson Freyr, Penman Adriana, Forbes Roma. (2025). Understanding simulation-based learning for health professions students from culturally and linguistically diverse backgrounds: a scoping review. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10384-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Mixed-methods study (N=53) reported in the review: "international students showed a significantly greater increase in confidence regarding foundational clinical skills" after simulation.

> "Rossiter et al. (2023) found that both international and domestic allied health students reported improved confidence from simulation. However, international students showed a significantly greater increase in confidence regarding foundational clinical skills."

### Zhang Luocheng 2025 (2)

Zhang Luocheng, Patterson Freyr, Penman Adriana, Forbes Roma. (2025). Understanding simulation-based learning for health professions students from culturally and linguistically diverse backgrounds: a scoping review. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10384-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental study (N=50) reported in the review found "greater improvement in communication performance during clinical procedures, as measured by the Binary Checklist score" among international students.

> "Similarly, Nagy et al. (2021) found that international medical students demonstrated greater improvement in communication performance during clinical procedures, as measured by the Binary Checklist score, following simulation training than their domestic counterparts."
