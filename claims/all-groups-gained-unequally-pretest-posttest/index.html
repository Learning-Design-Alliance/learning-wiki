Sarı et al. 2017¶

İrfan Sarı, Nadir Çeliköz, Süleyman Ünal. (2017). The Effect of Peer Support on University Level Students’ English Language Achievements. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131776

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Paired T-tests on all four subgroups (each n = 200): Supported rose 42.00 to 48.38 (t = -8.40, p = .000), Not Supported 41.41 to 43.02 (t = -2.76, p = .006), Assistant 86.75 to 87.78 (p = .008), Not Assistant 86.12 to 87.61 (p = .000). The article notes "learning was occurred but in different levels".

"The Null Hypothesis can be falsified that each group increased their scores at the end of the semester and there is a statistically significant difference (p<0.05) bet ween groups’ pretest and posttest scores."
