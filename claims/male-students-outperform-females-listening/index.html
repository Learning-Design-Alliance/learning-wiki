Tekin and Parmaksız 2016¶

İnan Tekin, Ramazan Şükrü Parmaksız. (2016). Impact of Video Clips on the Development of the Listening Skills in English Classes: A Case Study of Turkish Students. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i9.1698

Independent-samples t-tests across drama, horror, and adventure categories with 72 girls and 54 boys; adventure showed significant male advantage on short, long, and total scores, horror on long clips only, and drama on long and total scores.

"there was a significant difference between the male and female participants under the Adventure category in favor of male participants (t(124)= -2.724; p>0.05)."
