# According to the study's mapping onto Barnett's framework, SBL in nursing education can promote only the 2 lowest levels of criticality (critical skills and reflexivity), not the highest levels.

> "When using SBL as a learning method in nursing education, the findings indicate that, in accordance with Barnett’s framework, only the 2 lowest levels of CT can be promoted."
