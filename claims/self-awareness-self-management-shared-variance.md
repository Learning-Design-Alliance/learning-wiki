## Evidence

### Carpendale 2025

Carpendale, E. J., Green, M. J., White, S. L. J., Williams, K. E., Tzoumakis, S., Watkeys, O. J., Harris, F., O'Hare, K., Carr, V. J., & Laurens, K. R. (2025). Promoting effective transitions: Primary school social–emotional competencies predict secondary school reading and numeracy achievement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 496–512. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12735

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Supplementary multivariable MSEMs placing Self-Awareness and Self-Management in a single model per outcome (Tables S6, S7): each competency "remained significantly associated with reading and numeracy", but the combined-model R² gain was under 1%, indicating overlapping variance.

> "the two competencies explained shared variance in students' academic performance, as indicated by the increase of <1% in R 2 for the combined models relative to R 2 values from the bivariate Self‐Awareness models"
