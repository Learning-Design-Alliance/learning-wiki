### Jiang Luyang 2023

Jiang Luyang, Yang Qingyue, Li Qingyue, Jiang Bailin, Laba Ciren, Feng Yi. (2023). Optimal Duration of High-Fidelity Simulator Training for Bronchoscope-Guided Intubation: A Noninferiority Randomized Trial. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000739

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc ancillary analysis using a generalized linear model fitting intubation time in patients with group, stratification variable, and all baseline characteristics; none reached significance (age P = 0.31, sex P = 0.39, previous experience P = 0.31, residence P = 0.09).

> "the intubation time was not significantly influenced by any of the baseline characteristics, including age (P = 0.31), sex (P = 0.39), previous experience (P = 0.31), and residence (P = 0.09)."
