## Evidence

### Kula Yosef 2025

Kula Yosef, Wacht Oren, Ben Shlomo Izhar, Gitler Asaf, Gidron Yori. (2025). Does heart rate variability predict and improve performance in pediatric CPR?—a simulation study. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-025-01209-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design repeated-measures ANOVAs in the randomized simulation study tested Time by Group on outcomes measured three times. All interactions were insignificant; for verbal fluency the interaction was also not significant (F(1,55) = 0.003, p = 0.95).

> "The Time by Group interaction was insignificant in relation to perceived stress (F(2,100) = 1.18 P = 0.311). It was also insignificant concerning RMSSD (F(2,72) = 0.3 P = 0.737). Finally, it was insignificant in relation to SDNN (F(2,72) = 1.71 P = 0.19)."
