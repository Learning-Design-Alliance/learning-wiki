## Evidence

### Meyers AB 2026

Meyers AB, Morgan B, Smallheer B. (2026). Enhancing ICU-OR Handoff Communication Through Simulation-Based Education in Nurse Anesthesiology Training. Critical Care Nursing Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1097/CNQ.0000000000000627

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Postsimulation SET-M survey of 15 second-year doctoral nurse anesthesia students in a high-fidelity ICU-OR handoff simulation. The article reports "88%, N = 14" strongly agreed they felt empowered to make clinical decisions, with 2 participants not answering this item.

> "Nearly all participants (88%, N = 14) strongly agreed that after the simulated use of the structured communication handoff tool, they felt empowered to make clinical decisions during the experience"

### Meyers AB 2026 (2)

Meyers AB, Morgan B, Smallheer B. (2026). Enhancing ICU-OR Handoff Communication Through Simulation-Based Education in Nurse Anesthesiology Training. Critical Care Nursing Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1097/CNQ.0000000000000627

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same SET-M post-simulation survey. The article reports "94%, N = 15" strongly agreed the scenario allowed opportunities to practice clinical decision making, with one SRNA disagreeing.

> "Regarding perceived opportunities to practice clinical decision making, most SRNAs (94%, N = 15) strongly agreed that the scenario allowed them to do so, while one SRNA reported they did not agree."
