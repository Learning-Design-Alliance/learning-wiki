### Emily M. Levesque (chair) 2025

Emily M. Levesque (chair), Rachel Ivie, Christopher Johns-Krull, Grace Krahm, Laura A. Lopez, Meredith A. MacGregor, Sebastian Monzon, Daniel R. Piacitelli, Seth Redfield, and Tom Rice. (2025). AAS Graduate Admissions Task Force: Final report and recommendations. American Astronomical Society. [doi:10.3847/25c2cfeb.53ec1fbc](https://doi.org/10.3847/25c2cfeb.53ec1fbc)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Program survey of 60 astronomy-related graduate departments conducted via SurveyMonkey in fall 2024, collecting trend data back to 2018. The report states "PhD applications increased by an average of 62%", with 17% of respondents reporting decreases and the largest increases 193% and 195%.

> "Between the 2018-2019 and 2023-2024 application years, PhD applications increased by an average of 62%, and the average increase has been steady with time."

### Emily M. Levesque (chair) 2025 (2)

Emily M. Levesque (chair), Rachel Ivie, Christopher Johns-Krull, Grace Krahm, Laura A. Lopez, Meredith A. MacGregor, Sebastian Monzon, Daniel R. Piacitelli, Seth Redfield, and Tom Rice. (2025). AAS Graduate Admissions Task Force: Final report and recommendations. American Astronomical Society. [doi:10.3847/25c2cfeb.53ec1fbc](https://doi.org/10.3847/25c2cfeb.53ec1fbc)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same program survey's offer and yield trends: offers stayed between 23 and 25 on average, and average yields stayed roughly constant, with "a low of 42% in 2020-2021 and highs of 46%".

> "By contrast, the average number of admissions offers did not change at all during this time, ranging from a low of 23 in 2019-2020 to highs of 25 in both 2018-2019 and 2022-2023."
