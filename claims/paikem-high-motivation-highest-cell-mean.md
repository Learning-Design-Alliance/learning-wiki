## Evidence

### Priyono 2018

Priyono. (2018). The Implementation of PAIKEM (Active, Innovative, Creative, Effective, and Exiting Learning) and Conventional Learning Method to Improve Student Learning Results. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 9(2), 124-137. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181986

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive cell means from Table 10 for the four model-by-motivation groups: PAIKEM high 86.462, conventional high 79.273, PAIKEM low 73.286, conventional low 72.353. These are descriptive comparisons; the article reports no pairwise significance test for them.

> "implementing PAIKEM method for students with high motivation resulted in higher learning results than implementing conventional learning method for the same group of students. However, implementing conventional learning for students with high motivation gave better learning results than implementing PAIKEM learning for students with low motivation."
