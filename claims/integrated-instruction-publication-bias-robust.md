## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Although Egger's regression tests indicated potential publication bias for vocabulary and comprehension, sensitivity analyses showed no possible level of bias could attenuate the effect sizes to zero. [→ HyeJin Hwang 2021](#hyejin-hwang-2021)

## Evidence

### HyeJin Hwang 2021

HyeJin Hwang, Sonia Q. Cabell & Rachel E. Joyner (2021). Effects of Integrated Literacy and Content-area Instruction on Vocabulary and Comprehension in the Elementary Years: A Meta-analysis. Scientific Studies of Reading. https://doi.org/10.1080/10888438.2021.1954005

`q3 · i?`

Publication-bias analyses for the vocabulary effect using Egger's regression and the PublicationBias R package (Mathur & VanderWeele, 2020). The sensitivity analysis found it "Not Possible" to attenuate the estimate to zero; a parallel analysis for comprehension reached the same conclusion.

> "the Egger's regression test suggested possible publication bias, t(34) = 4.60, p < .001. However, the publication bias was not large enough to be a concern. The sensitivity analysis reported Not Possible for the effect size estimate and 15.72 for the lower 95% confidence interval bound of the estimate"
