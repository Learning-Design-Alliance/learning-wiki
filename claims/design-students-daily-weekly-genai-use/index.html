Stefana Broadbent 2026¶

Stefana Broadbent. (2026). A study of GenAI usage by Design Students: Analysis of Survey Results and Journals of AI practices at the Politecnico di Milano in 2025/2026. Department of Design, Politecnico di Milano. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17094

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Self-report survey of design students at the Politecnico di Milano School of Design (English version, n=223 of 280 respondents). The frequency table shows University Studies use at 29% daily and 42% weekly. The article reports "daily or weekly basis (71%) for academic purposes".

"the majority of respondents reported engaging with GenAI tools on a daily or weekly basis (71%) for academic purposes, and regularly for personal use (59%)."

Related Claims¶

  • Journal analysis shows heavy GenAI reliance in secondary research and transcription but student autonomy in planning and interpretation of primary research — related
  • Only about a third of design students report reduced creativity or project ownership from GenAI use — related
  • Design students report low trust in GenAI outputs and systematically modify and verify them, with inaccuracy the main deterrent — related
  • Design students' GenAI use concentrates on research, writing and brainstorming tasks, with low use for image, media and code generation — related
  • Prototype generation is the only GenAI activity significantly associated with perceived loss of creativity or ownership — related
  • Curiosity about AI shifted between pre- and post-survey, from 71% to 50%, which the authors interpret as a move from diffuse curiosity to concrete critical engagement — related
