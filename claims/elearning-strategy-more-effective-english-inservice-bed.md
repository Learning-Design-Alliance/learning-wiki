## Evidence

### Singaravelu 2011

G. Singaravelu. (2011). E-Learning Strategy for Earning Learners. i-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology, Vol. 6 No. 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102735

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Parallel-group experimental study of sixty in-service B.Ed teachers (thirty per group) selected as low scorers in English at a distance-education PCP centre. The article reports the post-test means and concludes "acquiring the competencies by using e-learning strategy is more effective than conventional methods".

> "Achievement mean scores of the learners in post-test of control group is 10.88 and the achievement mean scores of the learners post-test of Experimental group is 17.70."
