`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic covariate-combination results from the German samples quantify incremental validity of CP beyond IP; the article also reports the full covariate battery did not raise explained variance beyond IP in the prior research review.

> "In all grade levels, CP explained additional variance in student achievement over and above IP. On average, incremental gains were largest in lower secondary school (+5%) and smallest in upper secondary school (+2%)."
