Brezovnik 2015¶

Brezovnik, A. (2015). The Benefits of Fine Art Integration into Mathematics in Primary School. c e p s Journal, 5(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1128967

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Subgroup analysis of the pedagogical experiment restricted to students with a previous-year fine art mark of 5 (59 control, 68 experimental). The multivariate test was statistically significant, and between-subject tests were significant for all four topics (e.g., Powers F = 44.84).

"There was a statistically significant difference in the effect of fine art: F (4. 122) = 16.15, p < 0.0005, V = 0.346."
