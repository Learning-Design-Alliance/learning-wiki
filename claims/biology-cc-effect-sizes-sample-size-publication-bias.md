### Aleknavičiūtė 2023

Aleknavičiūtė, V., Lehtinen, E., & Södervik, I. (2023). Thirty years of conceptual change research in biology – A review and meta-analysis of intervention studies. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100556

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic finding from the review of 33 studies: the authors report that "the effect sizes were strongly influenced by the number of participants in the samples, as well as publication bias". Moderator analyses classified samples as small (below 60), moderate (61–120) and large (above 121).

> "It was found that the effect sizes were strongly influenced by the number of participants in the samples, as well as publication bias."
