`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Exploratory subgroup analysis of the primary objective AP meta-analysis stratified by adherence instrument. MEDAS-14 studies showed a smaller, significant estimate with lower heterogeneity; KIDMED studies showed a larger, non-significant estimate with I2 = 98.3%; the between-subgroup difference was not significant (p = 0.219).

> "the association remained significant among studies using the MEDAS-14 (SMD = 0.20; 95% CI: 0.01 to 0.39; I2 = 69.5%; prediction interval: −0.37 to 0.77) but not among those using the KIDMED (SMD = 0.67; 95% CI: −0.05 to 1.39"
