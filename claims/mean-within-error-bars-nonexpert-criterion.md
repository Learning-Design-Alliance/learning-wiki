## Evidence

### Gayle Geschwind 2023

Gayle Geschwind, Michael Vignal, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2023). Representational differences in how students compare measurements. [doi:10.1119/perc.2023.pr.geschwind](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2023.pr.geschwind)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Individual answer-option analysis of the post-test responses (N = 670, Table III with 95% confidence intervals). Selection percentages include B at 66 ± 4% (NRI) and 79 ± 3% (PRI); the pattern shows students "frequently... require one of the means to be within the error bars of another measurement."

> "For both the PRI and the NRI, students most commonly select B, in which the means of both measurements lie within each other’s error bars. The second most common choices were A and D, in which the error bars of only one of the measurements overlaps with the mean of the other measurement."
