Evidence¶

Mehmet Bicakci 2026¶

Mehmet Bicakci, Heidrun Stoeger, Diana Wengler, Albert Ziegler. (2026). Recalibrating impact in STEM education: Empirical benchmarks for interpreting intervention effects. Educational Research Review 52. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2026.100803

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup analysis of the umbrella dataset by STEM field (Table 2) reports that "science showed the largest quartiles, whereas mathematics showed the smallest". Printed science raw quartiles are 0.36, 0.69, 1.16; mathematics 0.16, 0.38, 0.73.

"Among STEM fields, science showed the largest quartiles, whereas mathematics showed the smallest; multidisciplinary and technology-related studies occupied intermediate positions."
