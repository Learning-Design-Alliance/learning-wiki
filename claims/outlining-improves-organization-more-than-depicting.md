## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants' organization of ideas was rated higher after outlining training (mean 3.6) than after depicting training (mean 3.4), a statistically significant difference. [→ Hung 2018](#hung-2018)

## Evidence

### Hung 2018

Hung, B. P., & Van, L. T. (2018). Depicting and Outlining as Pre-writing Strategies: Experimental Results and Learners' Opinions. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 451-464. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11231a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same quasi-experiment; Table 2 reports Organization means of 3.4 (depicting, SD .6) versus 3.6 (outlining, SD .6), independent-samples t-test t = 2.1, df = 19, p = 0.04.

> "The p values from the independent samples t-test showed that the differences in performance between the two strategies were significant (p<0.05)."
