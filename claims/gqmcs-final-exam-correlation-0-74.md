### L. D. Carr and S. B. McKagan 2008

L. D. Carr and S. B. McKagan. (2008). Graduate quantum mechanics reform. https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.2628 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0806.2628](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0806.2628)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r3`

Correlational analysis across the three course years shown in Fig. 3, with a printed effect size of r = 0.74 ("Pearson correlation coeﬃcient of 0 .74") despite a final that was 75% calculations. The authors state this reinforces other research demonstrating that conceptual knowledge can improve students' ability to perform calculations.

> "performance on the GQMCS was a very good indicator of performance on the ﬁnal, with a Pearson correlation coeﬃcient of 0 .74, despite the fact that the ﬁnal was 75% calculations."
