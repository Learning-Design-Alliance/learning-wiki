## Evidence

### Steinmann JJ 2025

Steinmann JJ, Otto I, Born MP, Derous E, Woltman AM, van den Broek WW, Stegers-Jager KM. (2025). Adjustment of Assessors’ First Impressions Differs by Student Ethnicity. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2196

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Online experiment with 81 physicians in an incomplete block design; LMM analysis of first impression ratings after 60 seconds of scripted videos, controlled for script differences. The ascending-performance difference was "not statistically significant, F(1,83) = 2.68, p = .11"; the descending-performance difference was likewise non-significant, F(1,79) = 2.11, p = .15.

> "Looking at the first impression ratings of the ascending performance (poor start), mean scores for ethnic minority students were 4.91 (SD = 0.13) and for ethnic majority students 5.14 (SD = 0.13); this difference was not statistically significant, F(1,83) = 2.68, p = .11"
