`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-study synthesis of beneficial design features charted from 97 included studies; the review reports that only "52.6% (n = 51) of studies reported incorporating feedback as a feature", with a Fisher exact test finding no temporal association (p = 0.79) across publication eras.

> "Despite feedback being a beneficial feature, only 52.6% (n = 51) of studies reported incorporating feedback as a feature in the AI generated virtual patients that were developed."
