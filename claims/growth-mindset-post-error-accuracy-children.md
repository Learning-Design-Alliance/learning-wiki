## Evidence

### Schroder Hans S. 2017

Schroder Hans S., Fisher Megan E., Lin Yanli, Lo Sharon L., Danovitch Judith H., Moser Jason S. (2017). Neural evidence for enhanced attention to mistakes among school-aged children with a growth mindset. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2017.01.004

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Behavioral analysis of the go/no-go data from the same 123-child sample. When mindset was added as covariate, the Accuracy x Mindset interaction on post-error accuracy was marginal (F(1, 120) = 3.45, p = 0.07, η2p = 0.028); "higher growth mindset endorsement was significantly related to higher post-error accuracy".

> "Correlations revealed that higher growth mindset endorsement was significantly related to higher post-error accuracy (r(120) = 0.23, p = 0.010), but was not significantly related to post-correct accuracy (r(120) = 0.11, p = 0.24)."
