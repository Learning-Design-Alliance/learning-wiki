## Evidence

### Kumar Manish 2025

Kumar Manish, Mittal Mahima, Chavan Seema Shankarsingh, Krishnan S Vimal, Gilbert John H.V., Sanatombi Devi Elsa. (2025). Interprofessional simulation training in acute pediatrics using rapid cycle deliberate practice (ip-star). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08302-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison of TPOT scores (n = 12 sessions, Wilcoxon signed rank test) rated by two trained observers. Table 2 shows each domain improving from a pre-intervention median of 1 to post-intervention medians of 2.75–3.5, each with p = 0.002; no effect size is printed.

> "There was a significant change in domain specific TPOT scores, with greatest gains in team structure and leadership."
