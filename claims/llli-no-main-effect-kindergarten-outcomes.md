## Evidence

### Megan Johanson 2016

Megan Johanson, Laura M. Justice, and Jessica Logan. (2016). Kindergarten Impacts of a Preschool Language-Focused Intervention. Applied Developmental Science, 20(2), 94-107. https://doi.org/10.1080/10888691.2015.1074050

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

HLM analysis of the RCT follow-up (247 children nested in 49 classrooms) tested treatment differences on three kindergarten outcomes controlling for fall preschool language composite; the article reports the main effects were in the desired direction but "none of the effects were found to be statisti- cally signiﬁcant ( ps > .20)."
