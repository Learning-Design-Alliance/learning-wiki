## Evidence

### Dong Mohan 2024

Dong Mohan, Liu Changhao, Yan Junfang, Zhu Yong, Yin Yutian, Wang Jia, Zhang Ying, Wei Lichun, Zhao Lina. (2024). A survey on brachytherapy training of gynecological cancer focusing on the competence of residents in China. Radiation Oncology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13014-024-02433-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional anonymous questionnaire of 132 senior residents from 28 Chinese training bases, analyzed with a 5-Likert-type self-reported competence scale. The survey found "for interstitial implantation, the percentage was only 50.03% (70/132)" versus higher rates for other GBT types.

> "The percentage of senior residents who were confident to complete the GBT independently was 78.03% (103/132) for intracavitary and 75.00% (99/132) for vaginal stump brachytherapy respectively. However, for interstitial implantation, the percentage was only 50.03% (70/132)."
