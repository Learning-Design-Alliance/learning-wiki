Bell 1921¶

Bell, J. C. (1921). Group tests of intelligence: An annotated list. Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(2), 103–108. doi:10.1037/h0073682

q2 · i? · review · r1

Annotated-list entry describing Mrs. Sidney L. Pressey's Mental Survey Tests, Primer Scale. The author reports the four named subtests and states the tests "give a correlation of .58 with teachers' estimates"; no sample size is printed.

"A group of four tests (dot pattern, classification, picture form board and picture absurdities) designed for grades 1-3. The tests involve neither reading nor writing, enlist the active co-operation of the children, and give a correlation of .58 with teachers' estimates."
