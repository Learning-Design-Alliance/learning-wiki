# The positive association between moderate and minimal physical activity and academic performance is concentrated in the lower half of the APS distribution

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zhang Yunting 2015

Zhang Yunting, Zhang Donglan, Jiang Yanrui, Sun Wanqi, Wang Yan, Chen Wenjuan, Li Shenghui, Shi Lu, Shen Xiaoming, Zhang Jun, Jiang Fan. (2015). Association between Physical Activity and Teacher-Reported Academic Performance among Fifth-Graders in Shanghai: A Quantile Regression. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115483

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quantile regression with school fixed effects on 1470 observations. The article reports significant positive associations only "if the students were in the lower half of the APS distribution (10% and 25%)".

> "There was a statistically significant and positive association between MoIPA and APS and between MiIPA and APS if the students were in the lower half of the APS distribution (10% and 25%), while this was not the case any more for students who belonged to the top 50% of APS distribution in our sample (50%, 75% and 90%)."

### Zhang Yunting 2015 (2)

Zhang Yunting, Zhang Donglan, Jiang Yanrui, Sun Wanqi, Wang Yan, Chen Wenjuan, Li Shenghui, Shi Lu, Shen Xiaoming, Zhang Jun, Jiang Fan. (2015). Association between Physical Activity and Teacher-Reported Academic Performance among Fifth-Graders in Shanghai: A Quantile Regression. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115483

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quantile regression estimates for walking frequency show predicted APS increases of "0.24 points (1.0%) and 0.17points (0.7%)" at the 10th and 25th percentiles respectively.

> "The quantile regression coefficient of MiIPA showed that at the 10th and 25th percentiles of APS, a one-day increase in walking for at least 10 minutes per week, predicted an increase in the academic scores by 0.24 points (1.0%) and 0.17points (0.7%) respectively, holding all else constant in the model."

### Zhang Yunting 2015 (3)

Zhang Yunting, Zhang Donglan, Jiang Yanrui, Sun Wanqi, Wang Yan, Chen Wenjuan, Li Shenghui, Shi Lu, Shen Xiaoming, Zhang Jun, Jiang Fan. (2015). Association between Physical Activity and Teacher-Reported Academic Performance among Fifth-Graders in Shanghai: A Quantile Regression. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115483

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-estimation tests across the quantile equations (bootstrapped standard errors, 100 replications) gave F = 7.02, P = 0.01 and F = 4.41, P = 0.04 for the lower-quantile versus 90th-percentile MiIPA coefficients.

> "And for MiIPA, the coefficients at the 10th and 25th percentile were different from that at the 90th percentile (F = 7.02, P = 0.01 and F = 4.41, P = 0.04)."
