## Evidence

### Bo Yang 2024

Bo Yang. (2024). Combining Danmaku and Discussion Boards: Toward A Scalable and Sociable Environment for Mass Collaboration in MOOCs. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09426-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative content analysis coded all messages with a scheme built from Rourke et al.'s template plus a co-learning-presence category (Cohen's kappa 0.84 and 0.88). The analysis found "more than 70% of the interaction in danmaku is in the socio-emotional dimension," and that danmaku lacked relationship development while the board lacked cohesive responses and co-presence categories.

> "more than 70% of the interaction in danmaku is in the socio-emotional dimension, while there is slightly more cognitive interaction than socio-emotional interaction on the discussion board."
