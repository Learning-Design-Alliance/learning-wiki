Implementation report from the course-design section: a voluntary fall 2018 pilot in which "17 students chose the computational essay option, working singly or in pairs to produce 11 computational essays", followed by a mandatory fall 2019 iteration for all students.

> "This pilot was voluntary; students had the choice to either write and present a computational essay or present a report-style oral presentation on a chosen physics topic (a standard part of the course prior to that semester). 17 students chose the computational essay option, working singly or in pairs to produce 11 computational essays."
