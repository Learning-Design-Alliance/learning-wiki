### Martín‐Parrilla 2025

Martín‐Parrilla, M. Á., Durán‐Gómez, N., Montanero‐Fernández, J., Casas‐Méndez, C., López‐Jurado, C. F., & Cáceres, M. C. (2025). International Journal of Mental Health Nursing 34(5), e70151. https://doi.org/10.1111/inm.70151

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests in the control group (n = 107) receiving traditional classroom instruction. The article reports "none of these differences was statistically significant", with printed d values of 0.130, 0.013 and 0.004.

> "A paired t‐test for the control group indicated that none of these differences was statistically significant. Exhaustion showed a mean change of 0.160 (p = 0.092; Cohen's d = 0.130), cynicism had a negligible difference of 0.01 (p = 0.448; Cohen's d = 0.013), and reduced efficacy showed a slight mean increase of 0.01 (p = 0.484; Cohen's d = 0.004)."
