## Evidence

### Pantziaras I 2015

Pantziaras I, Fors U, Ekblad S. (2015). Training With Virtual Patients in Transcultural Psychiatry: Do the Learners Actually Learn? Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3497

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective single-subject-design study in which 32 resident psychiatrists completed identical 11-item pre- and post-interaction knowledge tests around one virtual patient session, analyzed with the matched-pair t test. The post-interaction mean of 8.47 showed an "improvement of 1.03 points", significant overall (P<.001) and by gender (male P=.03, female P=.002).

> "The mean post-interaction score was 8.47, demonstrating an improvement of 1.03 points, which was highly statistically significant overall (P<.001) and by gender (male: P=.03, female: P=.002)."
