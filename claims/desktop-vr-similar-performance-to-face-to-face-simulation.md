## Evidence

### Liaw Sok Ying 2023

Liaw Sok Ying, Sutini, Chua Wei Ling, Tan Jian Zhi, Levett-Jones Tracy, Ashokka Balakrishnan, Te Pan Terry Ling, Lau Siew Tiang, Ignacio Jeanette. (2023). Desktop Virtual Reality Versus Face-to-Face Simulation for Team-Training on Stress Levels and Performance in Clinical Deterioration: a Randomised Controlled Trial. Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-022-07557-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Performance was assessed after training with a video-recorded simulation-based assessment rated with a modified Rescuing A Patient In Deteriorating Situations tool (inter-rater ICC 0.91). No significant between-group differences were found ("t = 0.75, p = 0.45" for medical students).

> "an independent sample t-test showed no significant difference in the simulation performance scores between participants in the VR and SP groups for both medical (t = 0.75, p = 0.45) and nursing students (t = 0.08, p = 0.94), and overall students (t = 0.61, p = 0.54)"
