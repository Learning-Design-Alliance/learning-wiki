## Evidence

### Gehua Tong 2023

Gehua Tong, Rishi Ananth, John Thomas Vaughan, Jr., Sairam Geethanath. (2023). Virtual Scanner Games: expanding access to Magnetic Resonance (MR) education through interactive web tutorials. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.05582](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.05582)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey of five beta testers (three doctoral students, one postdoctoral researcher, one faculty member) plus student players via a Google Form. The article reports "the average score for recommendation was 4.8/5 for high school students and college students" and 5/5 for MR educators; no effect sizes were printed.

> "Among beta testers, the average score for recommendation was 4.8/5 for high school students and college students, 4.6/5 for science/engineering graduate students, and 5/5 for MR educators."

### Gehua Tong 2023 (2)

Gehua Tong, Rishi Ananth, John Thomas Vaughan, Jr., Sairam Geethanath. (2023). Virtual Scanner Games: expanding access to Magnetic Resonance (MR) education through interactive web tutorials. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.05582](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.05582)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot deployment survey of five high school to early college students over a month via the web link. Scores were descriptive means from the feedback form; "the average recommendation scores were 3.8/5 for high school students" with MR educators at 4.8/5. No statistical tests were reported.

> "In deployment, the average recommendation scores were 3.8/5 for high school students, 4.2/5 for college students, 4/5 for science/engineering graduate students, and 4.8/5 for MR educators."
