Supplemental analysis using employment and college enrollment data tracked three graduating cohorts (2003-2005) with very low qualifications in the year after graduation; enrollees were unlikely to persist and workers were substantially underemployed.

> "In the fall after graduation, the most common outcome for CPS students who have very low college qualifications was to be neither working nor in school."
