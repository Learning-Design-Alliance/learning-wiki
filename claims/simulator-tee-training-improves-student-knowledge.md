### Beres Adam 2026

Beres Adam, Ramagiri Lahari, Zhou Jonathan, Prats Michael I, Bahner David. (2026). Transesophageal Echocardiography – Student Early Engagement in Didactic Sonography (TEE-SEEDS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09600-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Phase 1 paired t-test analysis of 10 second-year medical students' pre- and post-training knowledge quiz scores. Students averaged "13.1/15 correct responses post-intervention", a significant improvement (p = 0.006).

> "The second-year students significantly improved their knowledge scores by 37.9% (p = 0.006, Table 1), averaging 13.1/15 correct responses post-intervention."

### Beres Adam 2026 (2)

Beres Adam, Ramagiri Lahari, Zhou Jonathan, Prats Michael I, Bahner David. (2026). Transesophageal Echocardiography – Student Early Engagement in Didactic Sonography (TEE-SEEDS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09600-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

> "The fourth-year students also significantly increased their test scores by 34.0% to 13.4/15 (p = 0.01)."
