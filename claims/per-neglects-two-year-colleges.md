## Evidence

### Stephen Kanim and Ximena C. Cid 2017

Stephen Kanim and Ximena C. Cid. (2017). The demographics of physics education research. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.02598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.02598)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of the three-journal PER corpus (1970–2015). The authors report about 161,000 students took physics at two-year colleges in 2011 versus about 450,000 at 4-year institutions, yet two-year college students are 0.3% of research subjects.

> "In our study we found only 6 papers (2 PRPER, 4 AJP, and 0 TPT) reporting on two-year college students in the United States, with a total student research population of 701 students (380 PRPER, 321 AJP, and 0 TPT). Although about one-quarter of all college physics students are enrolled in these institutions, only 0.3% of the total number of students studied by PER are from two-year colleges."
