# Students risk acquiring the misconception that all multidimensional wave functions are separable when taught only separable solutions

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Teaching only separable solutions in multidimensional quantum mechanics leads students to believe all multidimensional wave functions are separable, a misconception the author reports encountering though no physics education research documents its prevalence. [→ Daniel V. Schroeder 2017](#daniel-v-schroeder-2017)

## Evidence

### Daniel V. Schroeder 2017

Daniel V. Schroeder. (2017). Entanglement isn't just for spin. [doi:10.1119/1.5003808](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5003808)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The author's expert assertion, offered without physics education research data: he states he is not aware of any research documenting the misconception's prevalence but that most teachers of quantum mechanics have encountered it, since "few students will spontaneously realize that these are not all the allowed wave functions."

> "as long as there is enough symmetry for separable solutions to exist, few students will spontaneously realize that these are not all the allowed wave functions."
