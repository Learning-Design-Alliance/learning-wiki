`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The second-order meta-analysis (Sala, Aksayli, Tatlidil, Tatsumi, et al., 2019) reports key results distinguishing near from far transfer: near transfer survives placebo controls, whereas far transfer is null after corrections.

> "First, near transfer occurs even when placebo effects are controlled for and seems to be moderated by the age of the participants. Second, far transfer is negligible (uncorrected overall effect) or null (when placebo effects and publication bias are ruled out)."
