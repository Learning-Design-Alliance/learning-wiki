## Evidence

### Emily M. Stump 2023

Emily M. Stump, Mark Hughes, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Comparing intro and beyond-intro students' reasoning about uncertainty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020147](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020147)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Subgroup analysis holding institution constant by examining only Cornell University data, the sole institution with sufficient sample sizes at both levels. The article reports "the same trends" were observed, suggesting institution differences do not exclusively explain the findings.

> "With this subset of the data, we observed the same trends between the intro and beyond-intro students' responses identified in Sec. III A (see Fig. 9 in the appendix)."

### Emily M. Stump 2023 (2)

Emily M. Stump, Mark Hughes, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Comparing intro and beyond-intro students' reasoning about uncertainty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020147](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020147)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Comparison of intro physics majors to intro non-physics majors across the five survey questions. The article reports majors "may partly explain the lower rate of point reasoning" but "cannot explain the higher rate of set reasoning" in beyond-intro students.

> "These results indicate that the differences in majors between the intro and beyond-intro students may partly explain the lower rate of point reasoning in beyond-intro students' responses but cannot explain the higher rate of set reasoning in beyond-intro students' responses."

### Emily M. Stump 2023 (3)

Emily M. Stump, Mark Hughes, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Comparing intro and beyond-intro students' reasoning about uncertainty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020147](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020147)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Comparisons of beyond-intro students' responses based on whether they had taken a beyond-intro lab course and based on experimental research experience. The article reports "no evidence" that either experience alone could explain the differences.

> "We found no evidence, however, that either of these educational experiences alone could explain the differences in intro and beyond-intro students' reasoning."
