## Evidence

### McWayne 2016

McWayne, C. M., Melzi, G., Limlingan, M. C., & Schick, A. (2016). Ecocultural Patterns of Family Engagement among Low-income Latino Families of Preschool Children. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0040343

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Auxiliary-variable analyses linking LPA profiles to PLS-4 language scores for 179 directly assessed children. In the English subsample only the high engagement group scored at the norm; the other two profiles were about one standard deviation below the norm.

> "In sum, higher family engagement was related to higher language scores, regardless of primary home language, but this distinction was most pronounced among the English subsample."
