## Evidence

### Alex Liu 2026

Alex Liu, Min Sun, Lief Esbenshade, Victor Tian, Zachary Zhang, and Kevin He. (2026). Teacher-Authored Prompts for Configuring StudentAI Dialogue: K12 Classroom Implementation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16738 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.16738](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2604.16738)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of demonstrated versus targeted DOK across 1,362 linked conversations coded by LLM with human validation. The article reports "37.7% of conversations showed students performing below target DOK" and a mean gap of +0.39 (SD = 0.81).

> "37.7% of conversations showed students performing below target DOK, while 52.9% achieved alignment and 9.4% exceeded targets (Appendix Figure 7). The mean DOK gap was +0.39 (SD = 0.81)"

### Alex Liu 2026 (2)

Alex Liu, Min Sun, Lief Esbenshade, Victor Tian, Zachary Zhang, and Kevin He. (2026). Teacher-Authored Prompts for Configuring StudentAI Dialogue: K12 Classroom Implementation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16738

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Stratified analysis by teacher target DOK level (Table 4). The article reports 46.0% under-target for DOK 3 targets (N=824) and a negative mean gap of −0.58 for DOK 1 targets (N=67), indicating students exceeded recall targets.

> "However, when teachers targeted DOK 3 (strategic thinking), nearly half of conversations (46.0%) failed to reach that level with many students demonstrating only DOK 2 engagement (Appendix Figure 8)."
