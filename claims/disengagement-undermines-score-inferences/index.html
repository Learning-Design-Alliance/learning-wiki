Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness 13(1), 147-175. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/using-assessment-metadata-to-quantify-the-impact-of-test-disengagement-on-estimates-of-educational-effectiveness/

This is the article's stated motivating premise, offered as background knowledge rather than a tested result: disengagement "could undermine the inferences drawn from observed scores". The article offers no effect size for this premise in the available text.

"Educational stakeholders have long known that students might not be fully engaged when taking an achievement test and that such disengagement could undermine the inferences drawn from observed scores."
