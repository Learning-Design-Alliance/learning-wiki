## Evidence

### Napamon P 2025

Napamon P, Jirapongsuwan A, Kalampakorn S, Siri S. (2025). The effect of strengthening nurse practitioners’ competency in occupational health services for agricultural workers exposed to pesticides in primary care units, Thailand: a before-and-after study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.14

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way repeated measures ANOVA across 3 time points for 60 NPs. Table 2 reports significant group×time interactions for all four outcomes (e.g., knowledge F=36.41, P<0.001; self-efficacy F=15.26, P<0.001); Greenhouse-Geisser correction applied for professional attributes.

> "The mean scores for NPs’ competency, including occupational health service professional attributes, knowledge, and skills, and their perceived self-efficacy in delivering in-house occupational health services showed significant differences between the experimental and comparison groups across different time points (P<0.05)."
