q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports a Ugandan study in which CMDs stated that "community support in the form of feedback and rewards had a greater influence on their performance than that from the health system". This is a self-reported comparison; no effect size is printed.

"Community medicine distributors (CMDs) in Uganda reported that community support in the form of feedback and rewards had a greater influence on their performance than that from the health system (Kalyango et al. 2012)."
