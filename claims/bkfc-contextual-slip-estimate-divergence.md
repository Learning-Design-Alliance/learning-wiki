## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pairwise Spearman correlations show BKFC carelessness estimates correlate negatively with both BKT-based and ML contextual slip estimates. [→ Jiayi Zhang 2025](#jiayi-zhang-2025)

## Evidence

### Jiayi Zhang 2025

Jiayi Zhang, Ryan S. Baker, Namrata Srivastava, Jaclyn Ocumpaugh, Caitlin Mills, Bruce M. McLaren. (2025). Carelessness Detection using Performance Factor Analysis: A New Operationalization with Unexpectedly Different Relationship to Learning. arXiv. [doi:10.1109/cste64638.2025.11092064](https://doi.org/10.1109/cste64638.2025.11092064)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pairwise Spearman correlation analysis of carelessness estimates for 4,880 incorrect answers in the Decimal Point dataset; Table II reports BKT contextual slip ~ BKFC ρ = -.21 and ML contextual slip ~ BKFC ρ = -.23, both p < .001, while the two contextual slip variants correlated at ρ = .849.

> "However, carelessness detected by the BKFC model is negatively correlated with carelessness detected by either the BKT-based or ML contextual slip model."
