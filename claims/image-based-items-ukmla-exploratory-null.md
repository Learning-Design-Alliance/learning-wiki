## Evidence

### Vakili B 2025

Vakili B, Ahmad A, Zolfaghari M. (2025). Performance of GPT-4o and o1-Pro on United Kingdom Medical Licensing Assessment-style items: a comparative study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.30

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Separate analysis of 20 image-based items (clinical photographs, radiology, ECG, histopathology): "o1-Pro achieved an accuracy of 90.0%, while GPT-4o reached 75.0%". McNemar's test gave P=0.25; corrected OR 7.00 with wide 95% CI 0.36–135.52 due to a zero cell.

> "o1-Pro achieved an accuracy of 90.0%, while GPT-4o reached 75.0% (Fig. 3). The contingency table showed that both models answered 15 items correctly, with o1-Pro uniquely answering an additional 3 items that GPT-4o missed."
