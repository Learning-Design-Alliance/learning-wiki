## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Deaf and hard-of-hearing students reported a statistically significantly higher response to the Likert item on how much they learned about science than hearing students, with a Z-test p-value of 0.001. [→ Nordhaus 2020](#nordhaus-2020)

## Evidence

### Nordhaus 2020

Nordhaus, J., Campanelli, M., Bochner, J., Warfield, T., Bischof, H.-P., & Noel-Storr, J. (2020). AstroDance: Engaging Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Students in Astrophysics via Multimedia Performances. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.10543](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.10543)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of the audience survey's three five-point Likert scales, decomposing respondents into hearing and DHH student populations by an age restriction of 22 years. A Z-test on the difference in means for the learning item yielded "a p-value of 0.001".

> "the deaf students reported a statistically signiﬁcant higher response to “How much did you learn about science?”. To quantify the statistical signiﬁcance of the difference in the means, we performed a Z-test and report a p-value of 0.001."
