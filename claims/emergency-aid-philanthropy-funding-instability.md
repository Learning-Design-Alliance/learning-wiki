## Evidence

### Sandra Perez 2026

Sandra Perez. (2026). How to Strengthen the Emergency Safety Net for Today's Students. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682437

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the brief's funding findings, interviewees described funds that "run out quickly," year-to-year uncertainty causing institutions to hold back resources, and reliance on philanthropy that "creates uneven program capacity across campuses."

> "Most practitioners reported that their emergency aid programs rely primarily on private donations, alumni contributions, or foundation grants"

### Sandra Perez 2026 (2)

Sandra Perez. (2026). How to Strengthen the Emergency Safety Net for Today's Students. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682437

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The brief's institutional barriers section reports emergency aid is often a side project layered onto unrelated roles, leaving practitioners overextended; reporting requirements and data collection further take time from direct student support.

> "Many frontline practitioners reported being the sole staff member (or part of a very small team) responsible for running their campus emergency aid program in addition to their primary job duties"
