# Agent pedagogical role shapes engagement in learning-by-explaining: Tutee and Challenger roles elicit longer explanations, and the Tutee role drives more frequent and longer material review than the Peer role

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Tutee and Challenger groups wrote significantly more words than Peer and Control groups (large effect). [→ Zhengtao Xu 2026](#zhengtao-xu-2026)
`q2 i2` Tutee group reviewed learning materials significantly more often and for longer than the Peer group (medium effects). [→ Zhengtao Xu 2026 (2)](#zhengtao-xu-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Zhengtao Xu 2026

Zhengtao Xu, Junti Zhang, Anthony Tang, and Yi-Chieh Lee. (2026). Who You Explain To Matters: Learning by Explaining to Conversational Agents with Different Pedagogical Roles. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790298

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Behavioral log analysis across the four conditions in the crowdsourced between-subjects study (N=96, n=24 per group). Dunn's post-hoc tests showed the Tutee and Challenger groups each wrote significantly more than Peer and Control; the effect was large, "Rank𝜂 2 = 0.34".

> "a Kruskal-Wallis H test indicated a significant difference in word counts across the four groups, 𝐻( 3)= 34.06,𝑝<. 001. The effect size was large (Rank𝜂 2 = 0.34)"

### Zhengtao Xu 2026 (2)

Zhengtao Xu, Junti Zhang, Anthony Tang, and Yi-Chieh Lee. (2026). Who You Explain To Matters: Learning by Explaining to Conversational Agents with Different Pedagogical Roles. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790298

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis tests on log data for frequency and duration of material review. Frequency difference had "Rank𝜂 2 = 0.08"; duration was also significant (𝐻(3)=11.67, 𝑝=.009, Rank𝜂2=0.09), with the Tutee group exceeding the Peer group on both.

> "the analysis revealed a significant difference across the groups,𝐻( 3)= 10.17,𝑝=. 017, with a medium effect size (Rank𝜂 2 = 0.08). Post-hoc test showed that the Tutee group revisited the materials significantly more often than the Peer group"
