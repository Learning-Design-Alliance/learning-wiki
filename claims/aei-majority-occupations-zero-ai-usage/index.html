Analysis of the Anthropic Economic Index, which tracks how AI models are used on the job task by task, mapped to standard occupational classifications. The brief reports "54% of the 756 occupations AEI tracks show zero observed AI usage," concentrated in hands-on physical occupations. Heartland-common occupations including electricians, welders, and nursing assistants show minimal current AI usage share on this measure.

"As of its most recent release, 54% of the 756 occupations AEI tracks show zero observed AI usage; these tracks are concentrated almost entirely in hands-on physical occupations: skilled trades, transportation, agriculture, and food service"
