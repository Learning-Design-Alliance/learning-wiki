Analysis of the ramp posttest in the middle-school experiment found a "significant association between using either inquiry strategy (GPS or CVS) and drawing the correct conclusion"; Table 1 reports 76% (CVS, N=50), 73% (GPS, N=15), and 49% (Neither, N=35) choosing the relevant factor.
