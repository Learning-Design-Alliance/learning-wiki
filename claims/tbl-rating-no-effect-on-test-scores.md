# Student perception of TBL did not affect knowledge acquisition: no difference in iRAT or end-of-session test scores between students rating TBL 1–2 versus 4–5.

## Evidence

### Jabeen Kauser 2026

Jabeen Kauser, Rehman Javeria, Fatima Syeda Sadia, Ahmed Imran, Ghias Kulsoom. (2026). Does medical students’ perception of team-based learning have an impact on achieving planned learning outcomes?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09185-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Whole-cohort comparison of test scores grouped by overall TBL rating (1–2 vs 4–5) among 96 students. The study reports "no difference in the iRAT (p-value 0.478) or end-of session test scores (p-value < 0.664)"; Pearson correlations with rating were 0.05 (iRAT) and -0.01 (end-of-session test).

> "There was no difference in the iRAT (p-value 0.478) or end-of session test scores (p-value < 0.664) between students who gave an overall rating of 1 or 2 versus 4 or 5 to the sessions."
