# Numerical metacognition, but not emotion metacognition, predicts children's standardized mathematics achievement

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Children's numerical metacognitive sensitivity correlated with TEMA-3 mathematics test scores, with a printed correlation of r(30) = .54. [→ Vo 2014](#vo-2014)
`q3 i?` Emotion-domain metacognition did not significantly correlate with mathematics scores (r(30) = .26, p = .17). [→ Vo 2014 (2)](#vo-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Vo 2014

Vo, V. Y., Li, R., Kornell, N., Pouget, A., & Cantlon, J. F. (2014). Young Children Bet On Their Numerical Skills: Metacognition in the Numerical Domain. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614538458

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

PCA summary scores of metacognitive sensitivity were correlated with standardized test scores. Number-task metacognition related to TEMA-3 math scores with a printed effect size of r(30) = .54, p = .001; the emotion condition showed no significant correlation (r(30) = .26, p = .17).

> "Metacognitive sensitivity on the number task was significantly related to math ability (r(30) = .54, p = .001). A correlation was not observed for the emotion condition (emotion: r(30) = .26, p = .17)."

### Vo 2014 (2)

Vo, V. Y., Li, R., Kornell, N., Pouget, A., & Cantlon, J. F. (2014). Young Children Bet On Their Numerical Skills: Metacognition in the Numerical Domain. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614538458

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Metacognition measures were not correlated with KBIT-2 general intelligence (emotion r(30) = .11, p = .55; number r(30) = .13, p = .46), which the authors cite as further evidence of domain-specificity.

> "No measure of metacognition was significantly correlated with general intelligence scores as measured by the KBIT-2 (emotion r(30) = .11, p = .55, number r(30) = .13, p = .46)."
