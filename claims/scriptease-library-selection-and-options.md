## Evidence

### Carbonaro 2008

Carbonaro, M., Szafron, D., Cutumisu, M., & Schaeffer, J. (2008). Computer-game Construction: A Gender-neutral Attractor to Computing Science. Informing Science? No—venue not printed.

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Machine-counted analysis of student modules found "98% of the students learned a valuable Computing Science skill" and "96% (48/50) were successful" at selecting and customizing patterns.

> "We can conclude that 98% of the students learned a valuable Computing Science skill: how to select an appropriate computational artifact from a library. Only one of the students who successfully selected a single pattern did not set the options properly, so 96% (48/50) were successful in learning how to select an artifact from a library and customize the artifact by setting options (parameters) appropriate to the problem being solved."
