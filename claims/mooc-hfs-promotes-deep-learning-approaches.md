## Evidence

### Yang K 2026

Yang K, Kanwen W, Jing C, Zidan G, Chenggong J, Xue L. (2026). A MOOC-based and high-fidelity surgical simulation-integrated clinical internship mode in optometry teaching for ophthalmology graduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09610-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

R-SPQ-2F assessing learning approaches with Deep and Surface dimensions, each with motive and strategy subscales (Cronbach's α 0.86 Deep, 0.79 Surface). The experimental group showed "significantly higher Deep Motivation" and Deep Strategy, with printed effect sizes r = 0.53 for Deep Motivation and r = 0.49 for Deep Strategy, indicating a shift toward deep learning approaches.

> "The experimental group showed significantly higher Deep Motivation (Z = 3.90, P < 0.001) and Deep Strategy (Z = 3.56, P < 0.001), with moderate effect sizes."

### Yang K 2026 (2)

Yang K, Kanwen W, Jing C, Zidan G, Chenggong J, Xue L. (2026). A MOOC-based and high-fidelity surgical simulation-integrated clinical internship mode in optometry teaching for ophthalmology graduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09610-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Surface Motivation (Z = 0.95, P = 0.34, r = 0.13) and Surface Strategy (Z = 1.24, P = 0.22, r = 0.17) showed no significant between-group differences, indicating the blended model promoted deep learning without reducing surface approaches.

> "There were no significant between-group differences in Surface Motivation or Surface Strategy."
