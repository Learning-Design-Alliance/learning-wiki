### Meagan Sundstrom 2025

Meagan Sundstrom, Justin Gambrell, Adrienne L. Traxler, and Eric Brewe. (2025). Evaluating recognition and recall formats of social network surveys in physics education research. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sundstrom](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sundstrom)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations between alphabetized roster position and number of peer selections were computed for each of 54 surveys (pre- and post-semester in 27 courses at 24 institutions, response rates 55% to 100%, average 79%). No effect size is printed, only significance of each correlation.

> "For 48 of the 54 recognition surveys, there is no significant correlation between students’ roster position and how many of their peers selected their name on the survey (black circles in Fig. 3)."
