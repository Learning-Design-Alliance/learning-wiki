## Evidence

### Yu Lu 2019

Yu Lu, Zhu Xiaoqin, Shek Daniel T. L., Zou Xiao-Bing, Deng Hong-Zhu, Au Yeung Peter W. H. (2019). Validation of the Simplified Chinese Psychoeducational Profile Third Edition in Mainland China. Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders, 49(4), 1599-1612. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-018-3827-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis based on the full ASD sample (N = 554) across six age intervals, reported in the Results. The article reports alphas of "from 0.77 to 0.96 for the Performance subtests" and 0.94–0.98 for composites; the Characteristic Verbal Behavior alpha was 0.59 in age group 2.

> "For the whole sample of children with ASD, including all age groups, the Cronbach’s alpha coefficients ranged from 0.77 to 0.96 for the Performance subtests, from 0.80 to 0.86 for the Caregiver Report subtests, and from 0.94 to 0.98 for the composites."
