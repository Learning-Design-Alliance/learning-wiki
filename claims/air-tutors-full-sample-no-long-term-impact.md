# One year after tutoring, full-sample Air Tutors participation shows no statistically significant impact on statewide math assessment scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Students who attended at least one Air Tutors session scored slightly lower than matched comparison students on the spring 2025 statewide math assessment, but the difference was not statistically significant (effect size = –0.035, p = .332). [→ Fong 2026](#fong-2026)

## Evidence

### Fong 2026

Fong, A., Chen-Gaddini, M., & Huang, M. (2026). An Evaluation of the Air Tutors Program: A 1-Year Follow-Up on Math Achievement. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681370

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental matched comparison (Mahalanobis distance matching) of 926 treatment students who attended at least one Air Tutors session against 1,791 comparison students, on spring 2025 statewide math assessments. The combined effect size was "–0.035, and this difference was not statistically significant (p = .332)". District-specific estimates were also small, negative, and non-significant.

> "The combined effect size across District A and District B was –0.035, and this difference was not statistically significant (p = .332; see Table 2 for details)."
