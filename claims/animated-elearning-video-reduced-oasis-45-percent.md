## Evidence

### Ali-Masri Hadil 2018

Ali-Masri Hadil, Hassan Sahar, Fosse Erik, Zimmo Kaled M., Zimmo Mohammed, Ismail Khaled M. K., Vikanes Åse, Laine Katariina. (2018). Impact of electronic and blended learning programs for manual perineal support on incidence of obstetric anal sphincter injuries: a prospective interventional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1363-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective interventional quality-improvement study in two Palestinian maternity units; phase-2 (n = 626) followed launch of the self-directed animated e-learning video. The logistic-regression analysis, adjusted for parity, birthweight and second-stage duration, gave "aOR: 0.56, CI; 0.35–0.91, p = 0.018".

> "Compared to Phase-1, OASIS incidence was reduced by 45% (12.2 to 6.7%, aOR: 0.56, CI; 0.35–0.91, p = 0.018) and 74% (12.2 to 3.2%, aOR, 0.29, CI; 0.17–0.50, p < 0.001) in phases-2 and 3, respectively."
