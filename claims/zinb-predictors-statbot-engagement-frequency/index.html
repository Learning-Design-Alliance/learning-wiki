Evidence¶

Yuan-Hsuan Lee and Jiun-Yu Wu 2026¶

Yuan-Hsuan Lee and Jiun-Yu Wu. (2026). Differential engagement with generative artificial intelligence in higher education: Gender, motivation, and achievement trajectories. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00615-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

ZINB count-component regression of 97 graduate students' semester-long voluntary StatBot use found males used the chatbot less often than females, with the printed coefficient b = -0.57, p < .05, corresponding to an IRR of about 0.57. Autonomous motivation was also a significant positive predictor (b = 0.52, p < .05).

"female students were more likely than males to use StatBot frequently. The coefficient for Male (vs. Female) was significant and negative ( b = -0.57, p < .05)."

Yuan-Hsuan Lee and Jiun-Yu Wu 2026 (2)¶

Yuan-Hsuan Lee and Jiun-Yu Wu. (2026). Differential engagement with generative artificial intelligence in higher education: Gender, motivation, and achievement trajectories. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00615-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

In the same ZINB count model of StatBot use, prior knowledge carried a negative coefficient (b = − 0.02, p < .001) and final course grades a positive one (b = 0.04, p = .004), so students with stronger initial understanding used the chatbot less while higher final performers used it more.

"prior knowledge was negatively associated with usage ( b = − 0.02, p < .001), implying that students with stronger initial understanding had an expected usage frequency about 2% lower per unit increase in baseline knowledge"
