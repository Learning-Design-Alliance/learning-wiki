John T. E. Richardson. (2017). Student Learning in Higher Education: a Commentary. Educational Psychology Review, 29, 353–362. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9410-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The commentary reports Richardson's (2013) cross-sectional study using data from 3861 distance-learning students across the adult life span: age differences in deep, strategic, and surface approaches were highly significant but only small effects on Cohen's benchmarks, so age is not an important determinant. No effect size value is printed here.

> "He found age differences in the students ’ use of a deep approach, a strategic approach, and a surface approach that all achieved a high level of statistical significance. Nevertheless, on Cohen ’s benchmarks, these variations only constituted small effects of little or no theoretical or practical importance"
