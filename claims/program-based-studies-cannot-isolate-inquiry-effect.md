# Program-based studies cannot isolate the effect of inquiry-based instruction because they vary many program elements simultaneously

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r3` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Because inquiry programs incorporate many instructional elements (technology, teacher professional development, extra resources), favorable findings capture the impact of the program as a whole and cannot support claims that inquiry-based approach caused the gains. [→ Lin Zhang 2022](#lin-zhang-2022)

### Lin Zhang 2022

The commentary's methodological argument, illustrated with the LeTUS program evaluation (Geier et al., 2008) and the KGS program (Songer et al., 2002), which used pre-post or business-as-usual designs without isolating the inquiry component. This is an analytical argument, not a new empirical result.

> "the favorable findings capture only the impact of this program as a whole over the counterpart and cannot support the claim that students' outstanding performance was caused by the inquiry-based approach used"
