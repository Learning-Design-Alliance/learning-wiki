## Evidence

### Amanda Matheson 2018

Amanda Matheson, Laura Wood, and Scott Franklin. (2018). Guided and Unguided Student Reflections. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.061](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.061)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of twenty journals each from the 2014 (unguided) and 2015 (partially guided) IMPRESS summer programs, coded with a new rubric (average Kappa 0.82 for 2014, 0.63 for 2015). The 2015 journals were "shorter and more focused," with the printed Narrative fractions 47% vs. 65%.

> "In addition to being shorter, the 2015 journals contained a statistically signiﬁcant smaller proportion coded statements. The fraction of Narrative statements in the 2015 journals is also lower than that in the 2014 journals (47% vs. 65%)."
