## Evidence

### Aulakh Jasmine 2025

Aulakh Jasmine, Wahab Hana, Richards Christine, Bidaisee Satesh, Ramdass Prakash V. A. K. (2025). Self-directed learning versus traditional didactic learning in undergraduate medical education: a systemic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06449-0

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup meta-analyses comparing theoretical and practical SDL against TDL; the review reports "The overall mean difference in exam scores for the subgroup analysis for theoretical SDL was 2.667, 95% CI [0.009–5.325]" while the practical subgroup interval crosses zero.

> "The overall mean difference in exam scores for the subgroup analysis for theoretical SDL was 2.667, 95% CI [0.009–5.325], and I2 = 98.76%, shown in Fig. 3. The overall mean difference in exam scores for the subgroup analysis for practical SDL was 1.339, 95% CI [−1.591–4.268], and I2 = 91.76%, shown in Fig. 4."
