## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2013

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Benjamin D. Geller, Julia Gouvea, Vashti Sawtelle, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2013). Negative Energy: Why Interdisciplinary Physics Requires Multiple Ontologies. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.020](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.020)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interview data from the NEXUS/Physics course on a task similar to Phillip's. The authors argue that the substance metaphor may encourage thinking of more negative energies as larger, while the location metaphor may encourage thinking of them as lower, and that depending on context one type of reasoning may be productive and the other misleading.

> "The bar chart represen- tation may contribute to the idea that more negativ e energies are “more,” since the size of the (negativ e) bar is larger."
