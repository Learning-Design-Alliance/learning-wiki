Evidence¶

Emily Buchanan 2015¶

Emily Buchanan, Jack Worth, Helen Aston. (2015). Tutor Trust Primary: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581540

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Qualitative process evaluation interviews with 13 senior leaders, 7 classroom teachers, 37 tutored pupils and 10 tutors across six primary and five secondary schools. Staff were described as believing "the quality of tutors greatly influenced the impact of the programme".

"Tuition was perceived to be most effective when tutors possessed strong pedagogical skills and subject knowledge, and were able to engage and interact successfully with pupils and target sessions appropriately."
