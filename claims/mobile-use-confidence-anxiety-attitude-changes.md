## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on matched pre-post surveys showed confidence increased (p = 0.041) and anxiety decreased (p = 0.020). [→ Jabbour 2014](#jabbour-2014)
`q2 i?` No significant pre-post change was found for liking (p = 0.182) or usefulness in general (p = 0.058). [→ Jabbour 2014 (2)](#jabbour-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Jabbour 2014

Khayrazad Kari Jabbour. (2014). An Analysis of the Effect of Mobile Learning on Lebanese Higher Education. Informatics in Education, 13(1), 1–15. https://informatica.vu.lt/journal/informatics-in-education

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post matched attitude surveys of the experimental section analysed with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test on matched participant IDs; the printed P-values are 0.041 for confidence and 0.020 for anxiety, based on N of 12 matched pairs shown in Table 4.

> "The P-value for the confidence (0.041) and anxiety (0.020) are less than 0.05 which indicate that participants confidence increased, also the participants' anxiety decreased at the end of the term."

### Jabbour 2014 (2)
