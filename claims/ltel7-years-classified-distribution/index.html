Distribution analysis shown in Figure 7 of CALPADS enrollment data for 2022–23. The report states "more than half" were in their 7th–9th year, 25% in their 10th year, and "more than 20,000" for 12 or more years.

"more than half of the 330,733 students who were designated as LTEL7 were in their 7th, 8th, or 9th year of being classified an EL. Another 25% were experiencing their 10th year as an EL. And more than 20,000 students designated LTEL7 had been classified as an EL for 12 or more years"
