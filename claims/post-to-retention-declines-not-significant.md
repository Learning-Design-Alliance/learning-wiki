## Evidence

### Cortés-Rodríguez 2024

Cortés-Rodríguez, López-Rodríguez, Roman, Granero-Molina, Fernández-Sola and Hernández-Padilla. (2024). Effect of two teaching methods on nursing students' acquisition of patient-centered communication competence in older people care: a cluster randomized trial. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1510620

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

McNemar tests comparing post-test and retention-test benchmark attainment in the standardized patient simulation group found decreases that were "not statistically significant (p > 0.05)"; the role-play group showed similar non-significant declines in self-efficacy, skills, and overall competence.

> "The McNemar's test results showed a decrease in knowledge and skills, as well as on overall competence in the standardized patient simulation group, although these differences were not statistically significant (p > 0.05)."
