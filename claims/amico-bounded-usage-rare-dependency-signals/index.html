Descriptive analysis of interaction logs and risk-signal monitoring across the 20 bounded pilot sessions. The authors interpret this as suggesting the implemented safeguards contributed to maintaining the system within its intended pedagogical scope.

"Risk signals related to dependency —such as repeated attempts to extend interaction beyond suggested closure points—were rare and, when present, were effectively addressed through system prompts encouraging human contact."
