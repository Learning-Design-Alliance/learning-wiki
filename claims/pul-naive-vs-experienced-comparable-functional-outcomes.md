### Hsieh Tsung-Yi 2025

Hsieh Tsung-Yi, Hong Jian-Hua, Chueh Jeff Shih-Chieh. (2025). Comparing the clinical outcomes and learning curves of prostatic urethral lift performed by experienced versus naïve urologists. World Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-025-05895-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective comparison of 67 consecutive PUL patients at one tertiary center (40 experienced-urologist, 27 naïve-urologist cases). The study reports "no significant differences were found between the two groups" on all four functional measures; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "However, no significant differences were found between the two groups in the magnitude of change in IPSS total, QOL, Qmax, or PVR (Table 2)."
