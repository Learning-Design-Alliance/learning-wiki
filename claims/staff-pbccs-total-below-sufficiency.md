## Evidence

### Garavito C 2026

Garavito C, Karki S, Rose SJ, Hartnett J. (2026). Bereavement practices and staff competencies on perinatal loss at a community-based teaching hospital. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0347348

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey (n = 46) using the modified 32-item PBCCS; subscale and total mean sum scores were computed and compared against the 80% sufficiency cutoffs. All four subscales and the total of 116 fell below their printed cutoffs, which the article calls "an inadequate level of confidence".

> "Results showed low levels of bereavement support knowledge (46.45 ± 13.04), bereavement support skills (23.07 ± 7.31), self-awareness (24.85 ± 7.54) and organizational support (21.73 ± 6.08). The total mean sum score of 116 showed an inadequate level of confidence among the staff."
