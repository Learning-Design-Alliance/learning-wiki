# Parenting self-efficacy shows a small to medium positive correlation with parents' contributions to the home-school partnership among primary school families

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Across 50 studies and 185 effect sizes, parenting self-efficacy correlates positively with home-school partnership outcomes (r = .189). [→ Tianyi Ma 2024](#tianyi-ma-2024)

## Evidence

### Tianyi Ma 2024

Tianyi Ma, Cassandra L. Tellegen, Julie Hodges, Matthew R. Sanders. (2024). The Associations Between Parenting Self-Efficacy and Parents' Contributions to the Home-School Partnership Among Parents of Primary School Students: a Multilevel Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09937-3

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Multilevel meta-analysis (M1) of 185 effect sizes from 50 independent samples (N = 20,043 children), using a three-level random effects model. The pooled correlation was "small to medium Pearson's r correlation at .189" with significant heterogeneity (Q = 1262.50, p < .001).

> "A three-level random effect model (M1) was estimated to calculate the average correlation between parenting self-efficacy and home-school partnership. A small to medium Pearson's r correlation at .189 was found (Z r = .192 [95% CI: .110, .273])."
