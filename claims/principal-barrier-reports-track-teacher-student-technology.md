### Vanessa Coca 2007

Vanessa Coca, Elaine M. Allensworth. (2007). Trends in Access to Computing Technology and Its Use in Chicago Public Schools 2001–2005. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/trends-access-computing-technology-and-its-use-chicago-public-schools-2001-2005

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of principals' perceived barriers against teacher and student survey reports (Table 2). Correlations were negative and significant in all years, strongest for teacher access (-.47*** in 2001, -.46*** in 2003) and -.36*** for student access in 2005.

> "In schools where principals reported fewer barriers, students were more likely to report high levels of access to and use of computers at school"
