### Anan Schütt 2024

Anan Schütt, Tobias Huber, Jauwairia Nasir, Cristina Conati, Elisabeth André. (2024). Does Difficulty even Matter? Investigating Difficulty Adjustment and Practice Behavior in an Open-Ended Learning Task. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1145/3636555.3636876](https://doi.org/10.1145/3636555.3636876)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Benjamini-Hochberg-corrected pairwise Mann-Whitney U-tests on NLG between the five behavior clusters. Cluster 4 significantly outperforms clusters 0, 1, and 3 with the printed U, p, and probability-of-superiority values.

> "we obtain three significant pairs: Clusters 0-4 (MW, U = 103.5, p = 0.0007, PS = 0.784), 1-4 (MW, U = 62.0, p = 0.005, PS = 0.779), and 3-4 (MW, U = 27.5, p = 0.006, PS = 0.828)"
