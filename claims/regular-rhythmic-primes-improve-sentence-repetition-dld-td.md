# Regular rhythmic primes improve sentence repetition performance compared to irregular rhythmic primes in children with DLD and TD

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Sentence repetition scores were higher after regular than irregular rhythmic primes, d = 0.44, across both DLD and TD groups. [→ Fiveash 2023](#fiveash-2023)

## Evidence

### Fiveash 2023

Fiveash, A., Ladányi, E., Camici, J., Chidiac, K., Bush, C. T., Canette, L.-H., Bedoin, N., Gordon, R. L. & Tillmann, B. (2023). Regular rhythmic primes improve sentence repetition in children with developmental language disorder. npj Science of Learning 8:23. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00170-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Within-subjects experiment: 15 children with DLD and 18 TD children heard 32-s regular or irregular rhythmic primes before repeating sentences with relative clauses; cumulative link mixed models showed "higher performance after regular" primes, estimate = 0.46, d = 0.44, 95% CI [0.10, 0.79].

> "revealed higher performance after regular, emmean = 2.67, SE = 0.45, 95% CI [1.78, 3.55] than irregular, emmean = 2.20, SE = 0.45, 95% CI [1.33, 3.08] primes, estimate = 0.46, SE = 0.18, z-ratio = 2.551, p = 0.01, d = 0.44"
