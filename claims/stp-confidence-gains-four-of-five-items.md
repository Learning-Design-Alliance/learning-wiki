## Evidence

### Temehy Basema 2025

Temehy Basema, Soundy Andrew, Rosewilliam Sheeba. (2025). Evaluating an online stroke training programme for Saudi Arabian physiotherapists for improving their knowledge and confidence in long-term stroke care: a pilot mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06837-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post confidence ratings (0-10) from the same 26-participant questionnaire phase, Wilcoxon tests. Outcome measures confidence rose from 7.15 (SD 2.68) to 9.46 (SD 0.86) (p = 0.0001); "goal-setting showed the greatest improvement post-training, rising from 6.73 (SD 2.24) to 9.46 (SD 0.95) (p = 0.0001)".

> "While participants' confidence in goal-setting was the lowest pre-training, goal-setting showed the greatest improvement post-training, rising from 6.73 (SD 2.24) to 9.46 (SD 0.95) (p = 0.0001)."

### Temehy Basema 2025 (2)

Temehy Basema, Soundy Andrew, Rosewilliam Sheeba. (2025). Evaluating an online stroke training programme for Saudi Arabian physiotherapists for improving their knowledge and confidence in long-term stroke care: a pilot mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06837-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same questionnaire phase, transition item. The highest pre-training confidence item showed the smallest, non-significant change: mean 8.58 (SD 1.68) to 9.19, p = 0.095, so no significant gain was detected for this item.

> "Pre-training, confidence in providing patients with the necessary support prior to discharge scored the highest, as the mean confidence score was 8.58 out of 10 (SD 1.68), and demonstrated the lowest change post-training, at 9.19 (p = 0.095)."
