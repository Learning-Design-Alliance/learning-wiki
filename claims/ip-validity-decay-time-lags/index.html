Stallasch 2024¶

Stallasch, S. E., Lüdtke, O., Artelt, C., Hedges, L. V., & Brunner, M. (2024). Single- and Multilevel Perspectives on Covariate Selection in Randomized Intervention Studies on Student Achievement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09898-7

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Quantitative research review of previous studies on validity degradation, meta-analytically integrated by the authors; the review reports single-level IP values dropping "from Pooled R2T∣IP−1 = 0.63 to Pooled R2T∣IP−7 = 0.36".

"Multilevel Perspective The few existing studies on multilevel design parameters addressing the temporal validity degradation of covariates attested a notable decre- ment of explanatory power of IP at L1; Pooled R2 L1 /uni007C.varIP−1 = 0.50 declined to Pooled R2 L1 /uni007C.varIP−3 = 0.35 ."
