Thiyagarajan D, Wheatley C, Salva CR, Lerner V, Saab SS. (2025). Development and validation of a pudendal nerve block simulation‐based educational intervention. Int J Gynecol Obstet. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70012

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Post-intervention satisfaction survey among the 40 subjects who completed the intervention in its entirety; thematic analysis of open-ended responses identified "ability to receive faculty feedback easily," "hands‐on approach," and the "realistic nature" of the model as favorite features.

> "Participant's five‐point Likert scale mean rating of the usefulness of the simulation was 4.32 (standard deviation [SD]: 0.11), and satisfaction with the model was 4.18 (SD: 0.13)."
