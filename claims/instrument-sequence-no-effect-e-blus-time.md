## Evidence

### Uysal Daniel 2020

Uysal Daniel, Gasch Claudia, Behnisch Rouven, Nickel Felix, Müller-Stich Beat Peter, Hohenfellner Markus, Teber Dogu. (2020). Evaluation of new motorized articulating laparoscopic instruments by laparoscopic novices using a standardized laparoscopic skills curriculum. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-08086-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Group comparison in the randomized cross-over study: "it did not matter which instrument was used first, as both groups showed no difference (AC: 4:49 vs. CA: 5:02 min; p = 0.46)". Median errors were also similar between groups.

> "When comparing the mean time needed to complete all exercises of CA and AC, it did not matter which instrument was used first, as both groups showed no difference (AC: 4:49 vs. CA: 5:02 min; p = 0.46)."
