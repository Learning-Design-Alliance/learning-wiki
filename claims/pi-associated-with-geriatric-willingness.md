## Evidence

### Liao Ting 2026

Liao Ting, Zou Jinmei, Wang Min, Song Yuqing, Cai Li, Yin Jin, Chen Yao, Wang Yating. (2026). Professional Identity, Educator Competence Perception and Willingness to Engage in Geriatric Nursing Among Nursing Students: A Cross‐Sectional Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70804

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of 842 Chinese vocational college nursing students surveyed online via the Wenjuanxing platform, with covariates regressed on mediator and outcome. The article reports standardised path coefficients β = 0.70 and β = 0.33, both p < 0.001; no effect size beyond these coefficients is printed.

> "After adjusting for age, gender, and academic year, PI was positively associated with educator competence perception (β = 0.70, p < 0.001), and educator competence perception was positively associated with willingness to engage in geriatric nursing (β = 0.33, p < 0.001)."
