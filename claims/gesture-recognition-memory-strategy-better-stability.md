## Evidence

### Hsien-Sheng Hsiao 2025

Hsien-Sheng Hsiao, I-Hsiung Chang, Yi-Hsuan Chen, & Jyun-Chen Chen. (2025). Using gesture recognition with the memory strategy to improve preschoolers’ learning performance, motor skills, and executive function. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 1791–1809. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10471-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the same quasi-experiment, the stability subtest of the motor skills test (based on the Preschooler Gross Motor Quality Scale) showed "the EG achieved significantly better scores than the CG" with η² = 0.116, a medium effect.

> "The ANCOVA results showed that the EG achieved significantly better scores than the CG (F = 8.40, p = 0.005 < 0.05). The pretest score as the covariate was 4.02, and the adjusted mean scores were 7.60 for the EG and 7.03 for the CG. Moreover, the η 2 value was provided as a substitute for effect size (η 2 = 0.116)"
