## Evidence

### Gjersoe NL 2013

Gjersoe NL, Hood B. (2013). Changing Children’s Understanding of the Brain: A Longitudinal Study of the Royal Institution Christmas Lectures as a Measure of Public Engagement. PLoS ONE 8(11): e80928. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0080928

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures McNemar change test on surveys returned at Times 1 and 2 (169 of 232 Time 1 respondents). Responses were coded binary pass/fail with Bonferroni corrections; the quote shows the primary cohort's significant χ2 results for Questions 1–3.

> "This analysis revealed there were significant increases in the proportion of correct answers on all questions (Primary - Question 1: χ2(84)=12.8, p < 0.001, Question 2: χ2(84)=59.16, p<0.001, Question 3: χ2(84)=44.46, p < 0.001"
