## Evidence

### Bello Ajediran I 2014

Bello Ajediran I, Ofori Eric K, Alabi Oluwasegun J, Adjei David N. (2014). Assessment of the level of agreement in the interpretation of plain radiographs of lumbar spondylosis among clinical physiotherapists in Ghana. BMC Medical Imaging. [doi:10.1186/1471-2342-14-13](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2342-14-13)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 41 clinical physiotherapists in Ghana who interpreted three plain film radiographs and were scored against a radiologist's 24-point assessment guide; one-sample t-test. The mean score of "agreement (12.7 ± 2.6)" was "significantly lower (p = 0.001)" than the gold standard of 24. No effect size is printed.

> "The mean score of agreement (12.7 ± 2.6) for the physiotherapists was significantly lower (p = 0.001) than the gold standard point of 24 (Radiologist's score)."
