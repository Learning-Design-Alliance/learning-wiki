## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For students who attended the cardiovascular TBL before the dkTest, permanent teams tended to perform worse than temporary teams on cardiovascular items (p = 0.06). [→ Heber Stefan 2024](#heber-stefan-2024)

## Evidence

### Heber Stefan 2024

Heber Stefan, Wagner-Menghin Michaela, Volf Ivo, Slak Rupnik Marjan, Schmid Diethart, Marz Richard, Fischer Michael J.M. (2024). Short-term cognitive learning outcomes in team-based learning: is the permanent team important? Medical Education Online, 29(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2397864

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the randomized trial comparing permanent versus temporary teams on cardiovascular dkTest items among students whose preceding TBL covered cardiovascular topics; the tendency favored temporary teams but was not statistically significant.

> "For 'cardiovascular' TBL prior to the dkTest, students from permanent teams tended to perform worse than students from temporary teams in the respective topic (p = 0.06, Figure 2B)."

### Heber Stefan 2024 (2)

Heber Stefan, Wagner-Menghin Michaela, Volf Ivo, Slak Rupnik Marjan, Schmid Diethart, Marz Richard, Fischer Michael J.M. (2024). Short-term cognitive learning outcomes in team-based learning: is the permanent team important? Medical Education Online, 29(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2397864

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Subgroup analysis of the randomized trial comparing permanent versus temporary teams on metabolism dkTest items among students whose preceding TBL covered metabolism; no benefit of permanent teams was detected.

> "For 'metabolism' TBL prior to the dkTest, there was no evidence of a benefit of permanent teams (p = 0.10, Figure 2C)."
