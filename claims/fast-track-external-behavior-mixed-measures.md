### Fast Track: Elementary School 2014

Fast Track: Elementary School. (2014). WWC Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/628

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

From the WWC confirmation of the 1999a RCT findings in the external behavior domain after one year of implementation. Average improvement index +4 (range –4 to +14) for 860 students; measures with no significant difference included the CBCL Externalizing Scale, HIWC Hostile Attributions, Observed Acts of Aggression, PDR, Peer Nominations, TOCA-R Teacher Rating, and TRF Externalizing Scale. No standardized effect size is printed.
