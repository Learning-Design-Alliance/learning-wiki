Related Claims¶

  • Quality of written MSF feedback comments varies widely, with a substantial portion lacking actionable information and global judgements predominating — related
  • Negative emotions are perceived to have either positive or negative impacts on learning, with faculty more likely than students to recognize their functional benefits — related
  • Positive academic emotions correlate positively with interest, effort, flexible learning strategies, and perceived self-regulation, while boredom and hopelessness correlate negatively with all of these motivational variables — related
  • The type and intensity of emotions students experience in feedback situations depend on feedback focus (task vs person) and valence, in interplay with context and student characteristics — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
