Evidence¶

Luke McCrone & Martyn Kingsbury 2024¶

Luke McCrone & Martyn Kingsbury. (2024). An ecological approach to understanding transitions and tensions in complex learning contexts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00267-1

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Ethnographic observations and field interviews found students described the breakout space as a place where they "can talk here and make some noise" while feeling "free to do anything". Teachers concurred that answering one-on-one questions was easier in this informal setting, and observations showed renewed student-directed questioning during off-ramp transitions.

"The fixed power dynamic and potential fear of judgement often implicit in'typical'lecture theatre interactions seemed to emphasise a more democratic power dynamic in the breakout space, such that students felt emancipated to direct questions and take more risks in their learning in this adjacent space."
