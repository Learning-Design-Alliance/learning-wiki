`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

This is the article's own CoRP-lens re-analysis of published mathematics education case studies, including a case study of algebra student Ana on a bottle-filling task and Carlson et al.'s report on 20 college-level calculus students on the same task variant. The authors characterize Ana's piecewise-linear graphing as proto-expert-like use of regions of consistent behavior and early neighborhood analysis.

> "From this review of math students’ covariational reason- ing, we identify facility with linear functions as a reliable re- source."
