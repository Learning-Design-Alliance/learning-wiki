> "Robinson, Lee, Dearing, & Rogers (2018) did a replication study in 10 urban, suburban, and rural elementary districts in a large California county. Students determined to be at risk for absenteeism (n=10,504) were randomly assigned to receive Absence Reports, or to a control group. Effects on reducing absences were statistically significant (ES=+0.10)."
