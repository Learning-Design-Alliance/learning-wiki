## Evidence

### Casey Inez Canfield & Baruch Fischhoff & Alex Davis 2019

Casey Inez Canfield & Baruch Fischhoff & Alex Davis. (2019). Better beware: comparing metacognition for phishing and legitimate emails. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09197-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pooled analysis of two experiments (152 mTurk and 98 SBO participants; 250 total) scoring detection judgments with the Brier decomposition. The article reports "aggregate overconfidence of 18%" and states calibration was generally good while "resolution was very poor."

> "Across all questions and individuals, mean confidence was 85% ( SD =8 % ) . Mean accuracy was much lower at 67% ( SD = 11%), making for aggregate overconfidence of 18%"
