## Evidence

### Fernández-Lao Carolina 2016

Fernández-Lao Carolina, Cantarero-Villanueva Irene, Galiano-Castillo Noelia, Caro-Morán Elena, Díaz-Rodríguez Lourdes, Arroyo-Morales Manuel. (2016). The effectiveness of a mobile application for the development of palpation and ultrasound imaging skills to supplement the traditional learning of physiotherapy students. BMC Medical Education 16:274. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0775-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled study, the m-learning group rated global satisfaction with the mobile application at 8.200 ± .767 on an 11-point scale ranging from 10 = totally satisfied to 0 = totally unsatisfied.

> "Finally, the results from the post-program survey assessing the global satisfaction from the m-learning group with the mobile application were high (8.200 ± .767), on an 11 numeric point rating scale."
