# Idea unit standards do not enhance judgment accuracy in evaluating self-generated examples

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Idea unit standards showed no statistically significant main effect on bias, absolute accuracy, or relative accuracy, conceptually replicating Zamary et al. (2016). [→ Linda Froese 2022](#linda-froese-2022)

## Evidence

### Linda Froese 2022

Linda Froese, Julian Roelle. (2022). Expert example standards but not idea unit standards help learners accurately evaluate the quality of self-generated examples. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09293-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the N = 131 factorial experiment, the ANOVA on overall bias found no significant main effect of idea unit standards; the article reports the same null pattern for absolute accuracy and relative accuracy, with no printed effect size for the null findings.

> "In terms of overall bias, a 2×2-factorial ANOVA revealed no statistically significant main effect for idea unit standards, F(1, 127) < 0.01, p = .977, ηp2 < .01."
