## Evidence

### Campos 2024

Campos, D. G., Fütterer, T., Gfrörer, T., Lavelle-Hill, R., Murayama, K., König, L., Hecht, M., Zitzmann, S., & Scherer, R. (2024). Screening Smarter, Not Harder: A Comparative Analysis of Machine Learning Screening Algorithms and Heuristic Stopping Criteria for Systematic Reviews in Educational Research. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Retrospective screening simulation of 27 education and educational psychology abstract collections in ASReview, evaluating ten learning algorithms across 270 runs. The article reports "a 58% (SD = 19%) reduction in the screening workload of irrelevant records" and 1.66 days saved on average.

> "The results showed, on average, a 58% (SD = 19%) reduction in the screening workload of irrelevant records when using learning algorithms for abstract screening and an estimated time savings of 1.66 days (SD = 1.80)."
