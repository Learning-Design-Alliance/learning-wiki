# Ego orientation and need for cognition did not significantly contribute to predicting gaming engagement

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Ego scores and need for cognition scores were dropped from the regression model due to lack of significant F change, so neither significantly predicted engagement. [→ Bobby Hoffman 2010](#bobby-hoffman-2010)

## Evidence

### Bobby Hoffman 2010

Bobby Hoffman, Louis Nadelson. (2010). Motivational engagement and video gaming: a mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev 58:245-270. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-009-9134-9

`q2 · i?`

Null result from the stepwise regression with 189 participants: two candidate predictors failed to add significant F change and were removed. The article reports no effect size for these dropped predictors, so the finding indicates non-detection, not a measured small effect.

> "Ego scores and need for cognition scores were dropped from the analysis due to lack of signiﬁcant F change to the overall regression model."
