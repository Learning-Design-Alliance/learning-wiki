### Gikandi MI 2020

Gikandi MI, Singh BS, Shuchi S, Shailja V, Megha S, Vishal D, Tamhankar AJ, Stålsby Lundborg C, Pathak A. (2020). Identifying the know-do gap in evidence-based neonatal care practices among informal health care providers—a cross-sectional study from Ujjain, India. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-020-05805-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' discussion of the survey findings among 830 IHCPs; the article reports an "obvious know-do gap" between awareness of weight-based dosing and the practice of weighing neonates.

> "However, an obvious know-do gap existed among IHCPs who responded that they do not weigh the neonates despite being aware that dosages of drugs should be based on the weights of the children."
