## Evidence

### UMANODAN Rino 2014

UMANODAN Rino, SHIMAZU Akihito, MINAMI Masahide, KAWAKAMI Norito. (2014). Effects of Computer-based Stress Management Training on Psychological Well-being and Work Performance in Japanese Employees: A Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial. Industrial Health 52(6). https://doi.org/10.2486/indhealth.2013-0209

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Full-sample mixed-model repeated measures ANOVA in the cluster randomized trial (intervention n=142, wait-list control n=121). The article reports no significant group × time interaction on the other variables (p>0.05), though small non-significant effects (e.g., Cohen's d 0.24 for work performance at T1) were noted descriptively.

> "However, there was no significant group × time interaction on any of the other variables assessed (p>0.05)."
