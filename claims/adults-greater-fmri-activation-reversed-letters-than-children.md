## Evidence

### Blackburne LK 2014

Blackburne LK, Eddy MD, Kalra P, Yee D, Sinha P, Gabrieli JDE. (2014) Neural Correlates of Letter Reversal in Children and Adults. PLoS ONE 9(5): e98386. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0098386

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Whole-brain random-effects fMRI analysis of the letter reversal task in 15 children (mean age 9.5) and 15 adults (mean age 22.3), thresholded at voxel-wise p <.01 with FDR cluster correction p <.05, with motion and artifact covariates. The direct group comparison showed "adults exhibited significantly greater activation" for reversed letters; children did not exhibit greater activation than adults in this contrast. No effect size was printed.

> "Direct comparison of children and adults showed that adults exhibited significantly greater activation for the reversed . typical letters contrast than children did in multiple regions, including the left ventral visual stream and bilateral parietal cortices (Figure 3)."
