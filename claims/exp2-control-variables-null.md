## Evidence

### Manuela Glaser 2025

Manuela Glaser, Laura Hug, Stephan Werner, Stephan Schwan. (2025). Spatial versus normal audio guides in exhibitions: Cognitive mechanisms and effects on learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10424-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Two-tailed control-variable analysis in Experiment 2 (n = 123); externalization means were 1.11 (close), 1.28 (distant I) and 1.31 (distant II), supporting that externalization was kept constant across conditions as designed.

> "A Kruskal–Wallis test showed no significant differences of externalization between the conditions, H(2) = 4.05, p = .132, r = .37. As expected, externalization was perceived to be similar in the condition with audio explanations perceived to be located within the corresponding portraits (close; M = 1.11, SD = .46)"

### Manuela Glaser 2025 (2)

Manuela Glaser, Laura Hug, Stephan Werner, Stephan Schwan. (2025). Spatial versus normal audio guides in exhibitions: Cognitive mechanisms and effects on learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10424-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-tailed Kruskal–Wallis tests on perceived direction (H(2) = 1.15, p = .563) and plausibility of audio-room congruency (H(2) = 2.97, p = .227) in Experiment 2; all control variables were statistically similar across the three conditions.

> "Also, no significant differences regarding the perceived direction of the audio explanations were found between the conditions, H (2) = 1.15, p = .563, r = .10."
