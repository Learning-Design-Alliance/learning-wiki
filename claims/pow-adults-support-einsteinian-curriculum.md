# Adults attending a public outreach workshop showed near-universal support for teaching Einsteinian physics in school, with 100% agreement that everyone should have the chance to know about gravity and light

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Self-selected adult participants at a two-hour Public Outreach Workshop gave universally positive responses to statements supporting teaching modern gravity and light concepts in the curriculum. [→ Foppoli 2018](#foppoli-2018)

## Evidence

### Foppoli 2018

Foppoli, A., Choudhary, R., Blair, D., Kaur, T., Moschilla, J. and Zadnik, M. (2018). Public and Teacher Response to Einsteinian Physics in Schools. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aae4a4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aae4a4)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Attitudinal post-questionnaire (Likert-type scale) from 25 adults attending the Public Outreach Workshop at the Gravitational Wave Research Facility, analysed in Excel as distributions. Table 1 shows 100% agreement on statements (j) and (k), e.g. "I think everyone should have the chance to know about gravity and light".

> "Universally positive responses to both statements suggest that all members of the self- selected group strongly support teaching modern physics to a wider audience and would also like these concepts to become a part of the school curriculum."
