# Sense of belonging to math declined only in the problem-solving control condition, with no condition main effect on belonging

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` A significant condition x time interaction on sense of belonging reflected a decline in the Problem-solving Control condition (d = .31) while the Incorrect and Correct example conditions did not decline. [→ Barbieri 2016](#barbieri-2016)

## Evidence

### Barbieri 2016

Barbieri, C. & Booth, J.L. (2016). Support for struggling students in algebra: Contributions of incorrect worked examples. Learning and Individual Differences, 48, 36–44. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Support+for+struggling+students+in+algebra%22+Barbieri+Booth+incorrect+worked+examples. [doi:10.1016/j.lindif.2016.04.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2016.04.001)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

3x2 RMANOVA on Time 1 and Time 2 sense of belonging showed a significant Condition x Time interaction (F(2,122) = 3.802, p = .025); Bonferroni-corrected paired t-tests showed the decline only in the problem-solving control condition, with d = .31.

> "the Problem-solving Control condition reported a reduced sense of belonging from Time 1 (M = 5.03, SD = .87) to Time 2 (M = 4.72, SD = 1.10; t(40) = 2.397, p = .021, d = .31)"
