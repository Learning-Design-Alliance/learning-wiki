## Evidence

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2014

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2014). Ontological Flexibility and the Learning of Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1409.8499](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1409.8499)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Aggregate analysis of online survey responses from modern physics courses at the University of Colorado, combining the atomic-electrons Likert statement with the double-slit essay question grouped responses (Fig. 4). The authors conclude students "can be influenced by explicit instruction in a measurable way" yet their perspectives are not stable across contexts.

> "Such findings indicate that students can be influenced by explicit instruction in a measurable way, but that their ontological perspectives on electrons are not necessarily stable across contexts."
