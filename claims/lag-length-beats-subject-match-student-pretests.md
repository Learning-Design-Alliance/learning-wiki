## Evidence

### Jessaca Spybrook 2016

Jessaca Spybrook, Carl D. Westine, Joseph A. Taylor. (2016). Design Parameters for Impact Research in Science Education: A Multistate Analysis. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858415625975

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Comparison of most recent student-level pretest covariates across subjects with varying lags (1 to 4 years) in two- and three-level models. Because science is not tested annually, student-level science pretests are often unavailable or long-lagged; in Michigan and Texas Grade 8 the first science lag is 3 years while mathematics and reading are available at 1 year.

> "In the majority of the cases, the 1-year lag mathematics or reading pretest options explained more variation at the student, school, or district level than the 3-year lag science pretest."
