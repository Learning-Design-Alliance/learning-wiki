## Evidence

### Chenxi Liu 2025

Chenxi Liu, Ana-Paula Correia, Young Min Kim. (2025). Determining mobile learning acceptance outside the classroom: an integrated acceptance model. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10514-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis within the SEM decomposed the total PEOU→BI effect (0.701) into a direct effect (0.596) and an indirect effect through PU (0.104); both were significant, supporting partial mediation.

> "While the total effect of PEOU was estimated at 0.70 (p < 0.001), the amount of indirect effect was 0.10 (p < 0.05), and the amount of direct effect was 0.60 (p < 0.001). Both the indirect and direct effects were found to be statistically significant. Thus, the effect of PEOU was partially mediated by PU."
