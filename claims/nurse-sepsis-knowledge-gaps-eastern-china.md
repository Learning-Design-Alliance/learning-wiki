### Pei Wang 2026

Pei Wang, Xueting Ju, Jiali Liu, Qingyuan Hou & Guomei Jiang. (2026). Assessing nurses' knowledge and confidence in adult sepsis: evidence from two hospitals in eastern China. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53878-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 355 registered nurses using a 15-item multiple-choice knowledge assessment aligned with Sepsis-3 guidelines; the study reports "a mean of 10.04 ± 3.17 out of a maximum of 15" with notable gaps in early recognition and septic shock management.

> "Sepsis knowledge scores among participants ranged from 3 to 15, with a mean of 10.04 ± 3.17 out of a maximum of 15."
