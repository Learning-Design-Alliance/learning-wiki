## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Michael Vignal 2023

Michael Vignal, Gayle Geschwind, Marcos D. Caballero, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2023). Overview of couplet scoring in content-focused physics assessments. arXiv:2307.03099. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.03099](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.03099)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

A worked demonstration (not piloted with students) applying a post hoc couplet scheme to FCI item 18, using Stewart et al.'s four theoretical principles as AOs. The authors state the scheme "produces five different results for the five different answer options" where dichotomous scoring would not. No effect size is reported.

> "This scoring scheme produces five different results for the five different answer options, while dichotomous scoring would yield the same score for all distractors, which we believe represents a loss of potentially actionable information."
