## Evidence

### Alibali and Goldin-Meadow 1993

Martha Wagner Alibali and Susan Goldin-Meadow. (1993). Gesture-Speech Mismatch and Mechanisms of Learning: What the Hands Reveal about a Child's State of Mind. Cognitive Psychology 25, 468-523. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/001002859390017M

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The article reports, citing Goldin-Meadow, Nusbaum, Garber and Church (1993), a dual-task study in which children solved equivalence problems while remembering word lists; "the discordant children recalled the word lists significantly less well". No effect size is printed.

> "the discordant and concordant children produced the same number of correct solutions on the math task (virtually none), yet the discordant children recalled the word lists significantly less well than the concordant children"
