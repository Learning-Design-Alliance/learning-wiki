## Evidence

### Edward et al. 2019

Chamila Nishanthi Edward, David Asirvatham & Md Gapar Md Johar. (2019). The Impact of Teaching Oriental Music Using Blended Learning Approach. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 16(1), 81-103. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1219911

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

True experimental pretest-posttest control-group study with random assignment of 9 Colombo-district schools; 120 experimental and 240 control students. Independent samples t-test showed a significant difference in mean marks between groups (t = 5.278, df = 358, p = .000), described as "a significant difference in mean marks between the experimental and control group".

> "The mean post-test of the experimental group was 71.75 which is significantly higher than the mean control group which was 52.07. The mean difference was 19.68 ±1.91."
