## Evidence

### Husbands Adrian 2015

Husbands Adrian, Rodgerson Mark J., Dowell Jon, Patterson Fiona. (2015). Evaluating the validity of an integrity-based situational judgement test for medical school admissions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0424-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Gender comparison of SJT scores among the analysed sample of 198 participants using a t-test. The article reports "A medium Cohen's d effect size of 0.46" and notes such differences are consistent with prior findings for MMIs and other SJTs.

> "Female participants (M = 35.0, SD = 7.4) scored significantly higher than males (M = 31.7, SD = 7.0) on the SJTs, t(196) = 3.21, p < .01. A medium Cohen's d effect size of 0.46 was associated with this comparison."
