Evidence¶

Fong 2022¶

Fong, A., Porterfield, A., Skjoldhorne, S., & Hadley, L. (2022). Expanding the Expository Reading and Writing Curriculum: An evaluation of an Investing in Innovation Validation grant. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/expanding-the-erwc-evaluation/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Student-level randomized controlled trial in 17 schools in the 2020/21 Evaluation Year, with grade 11 students randomized by WestEd researchers to ERWC or a comparison English curriculum. The report states the impact was "positive and statistically significant" on the Non-PT ICA only; no effect size is printed in the quoted finding.

"The results of the grade 11 impact evaluation found that assignment to the ERWC has a positive and statistically significant impact on student achievement as measured by the Non-PT ICA; however, no statistically significant impact was detected with the Summative Assessment study sample."
