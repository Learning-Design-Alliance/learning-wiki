Schenke, K., Nguyen, T., Watts, T. W., Sarama, J. H., & Clements, D. H. (2017). Differential effects of the classroom on African American and non-African American’s mathematics achievement. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(6), 794–811. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000165

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Parametric bootstrap test (20,000 draws) of the indirect treatment effect through expectations and responsiveness to developmental needs for African American students "only approached statistical significance"; for non-African American students mediation was not significant (b = −0.01, p = .33).

> "However, the parametric bootstrap test for mediation only approached statistical significance (b = 0.05, p = .07, 95% CI [0.002, 0.12])."
