### Rebecca Y. Bayeck 2026 (2)

Rebecca Y. Bayeck. (2026). Exploring learning in classic African board games: a review of recent literature. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10733-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review describes songoputation as a new concept from Bayeck (2024): a culturally informed way of collaboratively and computationally solving issues, involving audience and players in computational thinking during competitive play.

> "This research also introduced a new concept, “songoputation,” which is defined as “the shared involvement of the audience and players in different aspects of computational thinking in a non-collaborative and competitive board game” (p. 271)."
