The study reports these three potentially biological etiologies "were not found to be associated with lower academic performance," with the printed odds ratios and confidence intervals above; no standardised effect size is printed and equivalence was not tested.

> "enrolment CD4+ cell count (OR 0.75 [95% CI 0.13, 4.18]), detectable pregnancy viral load (OR 1.06 [95% CI 0.54, 2.06]) and absence of receiving a three‐drug ART regimen in pregnancy (OR 0.88 [95% CI 0.44, 1.77]) were not found to be associated with lower academic performance."
