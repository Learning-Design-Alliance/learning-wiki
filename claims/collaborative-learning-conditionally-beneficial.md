### Andrew C. Butler & Shana K. Carpenter 2015

Andrew C. Butler & Shana K. Carpenter. (2015). Separating Myth from Reality in Education: Introduction to the Special Issue. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-015-9344-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editorial reports, citing Nokes-Malach et al. (2015), that despite the popular appeal of students working together, the review identifies "several individual and situational factors" that influence whether individual or group work is more effective.

> "their review of the evidence suggests that collaborative learning can be both beneficial and detrimental depending on the circumstances"
