In an exploratory six-fold participant-independent analysis of 18 coded participants, an ordinal calibrator with definition–score interactions reached 71.5% balanced 1-off accuracy and 1.124 macro-MAE versus 64.6% and 1.216 for matched score calibration, a 6.9-point fold-average improvement. → Sidharth Anupkrishnan 2026

Exploratory RQ2 calibration analysis: within six participant-grouped folds, four frozen per-modality engagement scores plus EDA site were compared with arms adding cognitive/behavioral/emotional definition codes and twelve interactions. The reported difference is descriptive, with no significance test.

"The interaction model reaches71 .5%balanced 1-off accuracy and1 .124 macro-MAE, compared with64.6%and1 .216for score calibration. The fold-average improvement is 6.9 percentage points in balanced 1-off accuracy."
