Experience report based on two university courses (CSE490Q at University of Washington 2019, CSYE6305 at Northeastern 2020). The author states the harness approach "significantly reduc- ing the workload on the instructors and the teaching assistants" and that harnesses shared with students provide immediate feedback. No quantitative evaluation is printed.

> "This approach to programming assignments allows the in- structors to automate the grading process, significantly reduc- ing the workload on the instructors and the teaching assistants."
