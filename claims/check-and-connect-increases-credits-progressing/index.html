Dropout Prevention intervention report: Check & Connect 2015¶

Dropout Prevention intervention report: Check & Connect. (2015). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/78

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

The 1998 randomized trial in Minneapolis public high schools (92 students in the analytic sample for this outcome; credits data were unavailable for two comparison students). WWC table reports means of 12.13 vs 6.63 credits, mean difference 5.50, effect size 0.83, improvement index +30, p < .01.

"ninth-grade students enrolled in Check & Connect accrued statistically signifi- cantly more credits at the end of the first follow-up year (corresponding to the end of ninth grade) than comparison group students"
