## Evidence

### Purwoko Haryadi Santoso 2024

Purwoko Haryadi Santoso, Bayu Setiaji, Wahyudi, Johan Syahbrudin, Syamsul Bahri, Fathurrahman, A. Suci Rizky Ananda, Yusuf Sodhiqin. (2024). Exploring gender differences in the Force Concept Inventory using a random effects meta-analysis of international studies. https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07190

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random effects meta-analysis of 38 PER studies reporting mean FCI scores by gender, using Hedges' g on pre-instruction FCI data. The pooled estimate was "0.61, 95% CI [0.51, 0.70]", a moderate effect size (g = 0.61), significant with z = 13.0013, p < .05, favoring male students.

> "The average gender difference in the mean FCI scores is 0.61, 95% CI [0.51, 0.70] with higher performance in FCI test should be gained by male than female students. This summarized gender difference was significant, 𝑧 = 13.0013, 𝑝 < .05."
