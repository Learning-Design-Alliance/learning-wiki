Pásztor et al. 2010¶

Attila PÁSZTOR, Róbert PAP-SZIGETI, Erika LAKATOS TÖRÖK. (2010). Effects of Using Model Robots in the Education of Programming. Informatics in Education, 9(1), 133–140. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2010.09

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pre/post comparison of a collapsed six-item programming self-concept factor (KMO = 0.87, %point scale) in both sub-samples; the experimental rise from 47.2%p to 52.2%p is reported significant, with no effect size printed.

"We found stable programming self-concept in case of the control group ( xpre =46.3%p; xpost =4 4.1%p; t = −0.45; p =0 .66), while the experimental group's programming self-concept developed signiﬁcantly ( xpre =4 7 .2%p; xpost =5 2 .2%p; t = −2.60; p =0 .01)."
