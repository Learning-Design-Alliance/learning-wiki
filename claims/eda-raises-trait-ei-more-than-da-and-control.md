## Evidence

### Parisa Abdolrezapour 2013

Parisa Abdolrezapour, Mansoor Tavakoli, Saeed Ketabi. (2013). Enhancing learners' emotions in an L2 context through emotionalized dynamic assessment. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 3(2), 213-243. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135884

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-treatment-posttest study with three intact classes (EDA n = 14, DA n = 17, control n = 19). One-way ANOVA on posttest TEIQue-ASF scores yielded F = 13.742, p = .000, and Sidak post-hoc comparisons showed "EDA > DA, EDA > CG" and "DA > CG" (all p < .05).

> "the EDA condition was found to be significantly different from the other conditions, with EDA > DA, EDA > CG. The DA condition differed significantly from the CG, with DA > CG."
