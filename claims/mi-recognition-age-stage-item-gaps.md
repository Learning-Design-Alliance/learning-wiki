`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unadjusted ANOVA and t-tests on the 500-student survey (Table 7): MI knowledge rose across age groups from 9.89 ± 2.54 (<20) to 12.00 ± 2.42 (24–25), p < 0.001; clinical students 12.14 ± 2.34 versus pre-clinical 10.12 ± 2.92, p < 0.001; gender difference negligible (p = 0.845).

> "In unadjusted analyses, MI knowledge varied by age group (Figure 4) and was higher amongst clinical-year students, with minimal differences by gender (Table 7)."

Assessment of knowledge and awareness of cardiopulmonary resuscitation and heart attack symptoms amongst medical students in Syria - a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08800-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-level analysis of the 18-item MI score (Table 8, n = 500): max time to benefit from CPR 15.6% correct, initial ACS treatment (aspirin) 20.8%, 'golden hour' 36.4%, abdominal pain as non-symptom 40.2% — all rated High priority by the authors.

> "The weakest items related to time-critical recognition and early actions in suspected acute coronary syndrome (Table 8)."
