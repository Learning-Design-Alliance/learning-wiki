`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SEM Model 1 of the Born in Bradford estimation sample (n = 4201) reported the "total effect estimate was B = 0.259 [0.195 to 0.320]" (p < 0.001) and a direct effect of "B = 0.152 [0.089 to 0.215]"; Model 2 showed a similar pattern (total B = 0.258, direct B = 0.158, 61%).

> "the total effect estimate was B = 0.259 [0.195 to 0.320]. The majority of this was represented in the direct effect between socioeconomic status and educational achievement at B = 0.152 [0.089 to 0.215] (mediation ratio = 58%)"
