Evidence¶

Yussof et al. 2013¶

Yusfarina Mohd Yussof, Abdul Rasid Jamian, Zaitul Azma Zainon Hamzah, Samsilah Roslan. (2013). Students’ Reading Comprehension Performance with Emotional Literacy-Based Reading Comprehension Strategy. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies 1(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.1n.1p.82

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent group pre-post study of 90 Form Two students (45 per group) in Hulu Langat, Selangor. The mixed-design ANOVA shows a "significant interaction" with Wilk's Lambda 0.525, F=(1,88)=79.7, p=0.000, and a printed effect size of "partial eta value=0.475".

"The Anova (Mixed Design) analysis administered with the two groups (control and experimental) shows a significant interaction. The obtained Wilk's Lambda value is 0.525 with the significant value(p)=0.000 (lesser than α value), F=(1,88)=79.7 and partial eta value=0.475."
