q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Colorado interviews with state administrators. A state-level administrator emphasized the need for disaggregation, noting a 33 percent foster graduation rate could mask widely different rates across settings such as detention facility programs versus traditional day programs.

"When “out-of-home placement” spans diverse settings (facility schools, youth corrections, alternative campuses, traditional schools), it is difficult to interpret outcome trends and target supports."

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Washington interviews. A child welfare agency administrator reports that formal education-child welfare data agreements make identification significantly easier, with data updated nightly to give districts and partners real-time access for timely support.

"“It’s significantly easier for districts and agencies to identify youth and determine who should be responsible for providing services to them,” a state-level child welfare agency administrator shared. Data is updated nightly,"
