## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The effect of revision on text generation was only marginally significant in both samples (ps > .08) and revision skills were not related to writing quality. [→ Limpo 2013](#limpo-2013)

## Evidence

### Limpo 2013

Limpo, T., & Alves, R. A. (2013). Modeling writing development: Contribution of transcription and self-regulation to Portuguese students' text generation quality. Journal of Educational Psychology, 105, 401-413. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031391

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the SEM of both grade groups, the revision-to-text-generation path was retained only because it was "marginally significant in both samples (ps > .08)"; the authors report revision skills were not related to writing quality. No effect size is printed.

> "Although students' transcription fluency predicted students' skills to revise meaning errors, these skills were not related to writing quality."
