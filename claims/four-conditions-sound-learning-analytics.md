Editorial summary of Ifenthaler and colleagues' Delphi study reported in the special issue: experts agreed on "four essential conditions for sound and effective use of learning analytics", with strategies and actions proposed for each area. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The authors conducted a Delphi study in which experts agreed upon four essential conditions for sound and effective use of learning analytics in educational practice: (1) development of data literacy in all stakeholders; (2) updating of guiding principles and policies of educational data; (3) establishing standards for ethical practices with data quality assurance, and (4) flexible user-centered design for a variety of users of analytics."
