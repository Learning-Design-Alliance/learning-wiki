Evidence¶

Fong 2025¶

Fong, A., Chen-Gaddini, M., & Huang, M. (2025). An evaluation of the Air Tutors Program: Findings from an Arnold Ventures grant. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673538

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis of student tutoring attendance data collected from Air Tutors for the 2023–24 evaluation year (482 students in District A, 728 in District B attended at least one session). The report contrasts the two districts' threshold attainment: "93 percent of Air Tutors students met or exceeded the 24-hour threshold" in District A versus 16 percent in District B.

"In District A, 93 percent of Air Tutors students met or exceeded the 24-hour threshold. In contrast, only 16 percent of students in District B reached this level."

Fong 2025 (2)¶

Fong, A., Chen-Gaddini, M., & Huang, M. (2025). An evaluation of the Air Tutors Program: Findings from an Arnold Ventures grant. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673538

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive attendance analysis for District B showing most participants fell well below the intended dosage: 450 students (62 percent) received 15 or fewer hours and 144 students (20 percent) received 10 or fewer hours. The report attributes low participation to budget constraints, scheduling challenges, late start dates, and frequent cancellations.

"a substantial portion—450 students (62 percent)—received 15 or fewer hours of tutoring, and 144 students (20 percent) received 10 or fewer hours of tutoring"
