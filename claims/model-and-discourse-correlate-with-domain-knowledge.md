### Dina Soliman 2026

Dina Soliman, Xueqi Feng, Marlene Scardamalia, Carol Chan. (2026). Knowledge building–modeling: Grade 5 students creating models, using discourse analytics, and advancing systems thinking. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-026-09493-8

`q2 · i3` · `design · r3`

Correlational analysis (RQ2) among domain knowledge, discourse scores, and model sophistication in the same 43-student class. Printed effect sizes: final model scores r = 0.541 (p < 0.001) and ST-3 discourse r = 0.428 (p = 0.008); correlations the article reports as significant.

> "Table 6 presents a significant correlation between post-domain knowledge and final model scores (r(37) = 0.541, p < 0.001), and with systems thinking discourse (ST-3) focusing on interconnections and feedback (r(36) = 0.428, p = 0.008)."
