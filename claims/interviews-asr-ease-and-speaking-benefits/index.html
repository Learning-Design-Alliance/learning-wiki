Semi-structured interviews with the 17 participants were coded for positive, negative, neutral, or mixed perceptions (Saldaña, 2009). The quoted sentence lists the appreciated affordances participants named.

"Interviews revealed that participants appreciated the ease of producing texts without having to worry about orthography and grammar, the possibility to improve their pronunciation and self-efficacy when speaking, and the user-friendly voice commands."
