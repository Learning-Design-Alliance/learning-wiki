### Mohamed A. Ahmed 2014

Mohamed A. Ahmed, Khaled A. El-Beh, Talal A. Mohammed, Dina F. Mansour, Mohammed Y. Ezz-Eldine. (2014). Learning Disabilities in Different Types of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorders and its Relation to Cortical and Brainstem Function. Case-control comparison of 40 ADHD children (mean age 7.68 ± 1.64 years) versus 20 normal controls using the modified Myklebust learning disability scale (cut point 1.98). Patients scored "2.81" (SD 0.51) versus control "3.52" (SD 0.39), P < 0.000.
