## Evidence

### Nees Lisa Katharina 2021

Nees Lisa Katharina, Grozinger Philipp, Orthmann Natalie, Deutsch Thomas Maximilian, Hennigs André, Domschke Christoph, Wallwiener Markus, Rom Joachim, Riedel Fabian. (2021). Evaluating the influence of music at different sound pressure levels on medical students' performance of standardized laparoscopic box training exercises. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02627-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized trial of 87 Heidelberg medical students performing three Luebecker Toolbox exercises, assigned to music at 50–80 dB, earplugs, or control. The results section reports "best absolute mean performance was measured under exposure at 70 dB (121 s)" versus control 157 s (p = 0.007); exercise (c) showed 115 s vs 150 s (p = 0.010).

> "In exercise (a) best absolute mean performance was measured under exposure at 70 dB (121 s), which was significantly better than the control group (157 s; p = 0.007)."
