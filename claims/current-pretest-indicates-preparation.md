## Evidence

### Charles Baily 2017

Charles Baily, Qing X. Ryan, Cecilia Astolfi and Steven J. Pollock. (2017). Conceptual Assessment Tool for Advanced Undergraduate Electrodynamics. PHYS. REV. – PHYS. ED. RES. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020113)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r3`

Correlational analysis of the optional 3-question pre-test (average 54.3% ±0.3%, N=298). Pre-test scores also correlate with post-test scores (r=0.55, p<0.01, N=298), and different populations showed widely different pre-test scores (e.g., 63.4% vs 45.8% at two non-CU institutions).

> "Pre-test scores are found to correlate well with a variety of measures, such as post-test scores on the CUE diagnostic [5] (r=0.59, p<0.001, N=150); and GPA in prior physics courses (r=0.42, p<0.001, N=175)."
