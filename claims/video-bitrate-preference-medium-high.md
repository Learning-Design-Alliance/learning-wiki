Audit of video bit-rate selection across all year levels in 2010, reported as proportions with 95% CIs. Medium quality was "accessed 45% (1614/3560; 95% CI 43-47) of the time" and high quality 39%; the article prints both 3590 accesses and denominators of 3560.

> "Across all year levels (1-4), there were 3590 video accesses. Low quality (56 kbit/s or 150 kbit/s depending on the program) video was accessed 16% (558/3560; 95% CI 13-19) of the time, medium quality (256 kbit/s or 400 kbit/s) was accessed 45% (1614/3560; 95% CI 43-47) of the time, and high quality video (512 kbit/s or 700 kbit/s) was accessed 39% (1388/3560; 95% CI 36-41) of the time."
