Lecce 2026¶

Lecce, S., Stagnitto, S. M., Mascheretti, S., Lampis, V., & Devine, R. T. (2026). Children's Theory of Mind and Academic Achievement: a Meta-analysis on Reading Comprehension and Math Skills. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10185-w

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Publication-bias assessment of the cross-sectional three-level model: visual funnel-plot inspection showed no clear asymmetry, but the multi-level Egger's test was significant ("B = 0.48, SE = 0.07, p < .001"). No effect size is printed for this test.

"the Egger’s test on the model intercept was significant, B = 0.48, SE = 0.07, p < .001, indicating evidence of asymmetry bias."
