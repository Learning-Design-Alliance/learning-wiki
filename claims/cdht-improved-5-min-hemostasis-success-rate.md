### Hou Qin 2025

Hou Qin, Zhou Bin, Kang Jing-Ping, Zuo Yun-Xia. (2025). Competency-driven hemostasis training reduces vascular complications in transradial hemodynamic monitoring: a self-controlled trial with early-career residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08048-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-controlled before-after comparison in which 10 residents performed radial artery compression on 93 patients before and 94 patients after CDHT. The article reports the success rate "increased to 93.6%" with P = 0.05; no effect size is printed.

> "Before training, the 5-min primary hemostasis success rate was 84.9%, while after training, the 5-min primary hemostasis success rate increased to 93.6% (P = 0.05, Table 2)."
