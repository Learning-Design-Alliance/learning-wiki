Pilot survey administration at the end of the spring semester in the introductory algebra-based lab for non-majors. The article reports "roughly 650 students" were surveyed, "nearly 500 responses" were received, and each of the 108 questions drew between 40-60 individual responses. No statistical validation was attempted.

> "This was accomplished by distributing the survey to the students in the introductory algebra-based lab for non-majors at the end of the spring semester, roughly 650 students. With nearly 500 responses to the survey we received between 40-60 individual responses to each of the 108 questions."
