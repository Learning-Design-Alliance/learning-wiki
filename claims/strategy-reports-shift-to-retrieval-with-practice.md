## Evidence

### Tenison 2015

Tenison, C., & Anderson, J. R. (2015). Modeling the distinct phases of skill acquisition. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of concurrent strategy assessments collected in blocks 2, 4, and 6 (Table 1). For practiced problems, retrieval reports rose from 63% to 94% while calculate fell from 15% to 3%; novel problems stayed near 83% calculate.

> "This shows an increase in reports of retrieval and a decrease in partial and calculate strategy for trained problems over the course of the experiment. On the other hand, novel problems show relatively little change in reports."
