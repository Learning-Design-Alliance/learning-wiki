Evidence¶

Hanna Onyi Yusuf 2013¶

Hanna Onyi Yusuf. (2013). Impact Of Using Joint Productive Activity Approach On Second Language Learners' Performance In Reading Comprehension At The Basic Education Level In Nigeria. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 4(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.4n.1p.51

q1 · i? · causal · r1

The authors' observational interpretation from the JPA lessons that were observed: students and teachers discussed passages before reading, and each student was eager to share experiences. This is an interpretation, not a tested result, and no measure or statistic is reported.

"There is a general increase in the level of involvement and participation of the second language learners on the JPA in the experimental group probably because of the activities students were engaged in."
