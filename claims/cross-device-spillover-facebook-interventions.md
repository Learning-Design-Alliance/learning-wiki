Survey item analysed after the intervention block (RQ3 follow-up): only the printed percentages and χ2 = 65.19, p < 0.001 are reported; no standardised effect size is printed, so impact is not coded. Interviews indicate participants used the smartphone deliberately to sidestep downsides while keeping benefits.

> "When asked if the interventions changed how they used Facebook on smartphone vs laptop, 86% of participants in Cgoal and 57% in Cno-feed answered 'Yes' (compared to 6% in Ccontrol, χ2 = 65.19, p < 0.001)"
