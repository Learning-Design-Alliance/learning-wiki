Exploring effects of a booster workshop on progression and retention of resuscitation skills of residents when added to regular low-dose simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06705-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of pre-test scenario scores between experience subgroups (77 residents with fewer than 5 events, 12 with more than 6) reported in Results. The article prints medians and p = 0.01 with no effect size.

> "Significant higher patient safety scores were observed in group of residents with more than 6 times participating resuscitation experience compared to those with less than 5 times (Median 153.75, Q1: 141.13, Q3: 168.75 vs. Median 141.00, Q1: 133.25, Q3: 151.00; p = 0.01)."
