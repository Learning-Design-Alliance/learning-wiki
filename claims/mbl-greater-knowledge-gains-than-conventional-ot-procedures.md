## Evidence

### Ahmed Dev Ahrar 2024

Ahmed Dev Ahrar, Rai Kanika, Sharma Amoldeep, Sarin Jyoti. (2024). Advancement in knowledge and skills of nursing students in operation theatre procedures with mobile based learning. Investigacion y Educacion en Enfermeria. https://doi.org/10.17533/udea.iee.v42n2e15

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study with 64 nursing students per group; unpaired t-test on post-test knowledge showed a significant between-group difference, with post-test means of 19.23±5.37 versus 14.11±3.36. The article reports "p<0.001" and no effect size.

> "For comparing the mean difference between the experimental and comparison group, unpaired t-test was applied, and the calculated t value of 6.46 (t (126) =1.98; p<0.001) was found to be statistically significant in the post-test."
