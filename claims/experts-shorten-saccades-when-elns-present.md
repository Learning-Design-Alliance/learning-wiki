# Experts shorten saccades selectively in video sections where enlarged lymph nodes are present

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In ELN videos, the Expertise × ELN presence interaction was highly significant for saccadic amplitude: experts reverted to much shorter saccades when ELNs were present, while other groups did not adjust. [→ Bertram Raymond 2013](#bertram-raymond-2013)

## Evidence

### Bertram Raymond 2013

Bertram Raymond, Helle Laura, Kaakinen Johanna K., Svedström Erkki. (2013). The Effect of Expertise on Eye Movement Behaviour in Medical Image Perception. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0066169

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Local LME analysis of feasible ELN videos (P2, P3): experts' saccades averaged 1.51 degrees in ELN-present sections versus 2.26 degrees elsewhere; semi-experts and naïve participants did not adjust saccadic amplitude as a function of ELN presence.

> "The interaction reflects that experts reverted to much shorter saccades in those sections of the videos where ELNs were present. In these sections, experts made saccades of on average 1.51o, whereas their saccadic amplitude was on average 2.26 o when ELNs were not present."
