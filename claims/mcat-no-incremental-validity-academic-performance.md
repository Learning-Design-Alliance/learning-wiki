## Evidence

### Michele Cherfane 2026

Michele Cherfane, Marc Ghanem, Georges Choueiry, Selim M Nasser, Tamina Elias-Rizk, Sola Aoun Bahous. (2026). Evaluating the Influence of MCAT Scores on Medical Student Selection and Performance Using a Machine Learning Approach: Retrospective Cohort Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/94197

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective cohort analysis of 349 admitted students with MCAT scores at a single institution. Pearson correlations compared admissions composites with and without MCAT against average final grades; the Steiger Z test showed no significant difference (Steiger Z=0.48, P=.63).

> "The correlation between the admissions composite score and the average final grade (an aggregate of Med 1‐4 performance) was 0.63 (95% CI 0.56-0.69) when the MCAT was included, compared to 0.62 (95% CI 0.55-0.68) without it, and this difference was not statistically significant (Steiger Z=0.48, P=.63)."
