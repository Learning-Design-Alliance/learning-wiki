## Evidence

### Moon 2026

Moon, Eunkyung and Shin, Won Sug. (2026). Exploring synchronous, asynchronous, and conventional online courses in higher education. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0345955

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Latent mean analysis across all three modality pairs with rotated reference groups; 313 students reported Syn experience, 316 Asyn, 166 COC. The pairwise Syn/Asyn contrast shows "higher mean values for SQ, SE, and CI than Syn", with printed CI d = 0.204 and TP d = −0.056.
