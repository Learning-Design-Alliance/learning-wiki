# Interns learning blood cell morphology with an interactive multimedia eBook achieved significantly better post-test scores than interns learning with a traditional PowerPoint atlas

`q3 i?` In a randomized controlled trial, the interactive multimedia eBook group achieved significantly better post-test scores than the PowerPoint group (mean 103.2 versus 70.6, p < 0.001). [→ Hsiao Chih-Cheng 2016](#hsiao-chih-cheng-2016)

## Evidence

### Hsiao Chih-Cheng 2016

Hsiao Chih-Cheng, Tiao Mao-Meng, Chen Chih-Cheng. (2016). Using interactive multimedia e-Books for learning blood cell morphology in pediatric hematology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0816-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 51 interns allocated to a traditional PowerPoint atlas (n = 25) or an interactive multimedia eBook atlas (n = 26) for a 2-week pediatric hematology course. The eBook group "achieved significantly better scores in the posttest" with means of 103.2(13.6) versus 70.6(13.7).

> "the interns in the interactive multimedia eBook group achieved significantly better scores in the posttest than the ones in the PowerPoint group with a mean score and standard deviation of 103.2(13.6) and 70.6(13.7), respectively (p < 0.001)"
