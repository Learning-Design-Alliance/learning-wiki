de la Torre 2021¶

de la Torre, M., Freire, S., & Blanchard, A. (2021). English Learners in Chicago Public Schools: An exploration of the influence of pre-k and early grade years. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/English-learners-in-Chicago-Public-Schools-an-exploration-of-the-influence-of-pre-K

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The report finds "screener data were, in general, not related to attendance" while low starting English proficiency related to lower performance on standardized test scores and grades. This mixed pattern means educators can identify students needing academic support as early as pre-k, but not attendance risk, from screener scores.

"Starting school with low levels of English proficiency was related to lower academic performance, measured by standardized test scores and grades; however, screener data were, in general, not related to attendance."
