Evidence¶

Qin Yongjie 2016¶

Qin Yongjie, Wang Yungui, Floden Robert E. (2016). The Effect of Problem-Based Learning on Improvement of the Medical Educational Environment: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Medical Principles and Practice. https://doi.org/10.1159/000449036

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Random-effects meta-analysis of six RCTs (pooled n = 673; PBL 335, LBL 338), all conducted in China between 2006 and 2013, using Revman 5.3. The pooled weighted mean difference favored PBL: "WMD 9.10, 95% CI 5.98–12.21, p < 0.00001". Moderate heterogeneity (I2 = 61%, p < 0.10) was reported.

"Compared with the control LBL group, the pooled effect size showed a significant difference in medical educational environment scores (WMD 9.10, 95% CI 5.98–12.21, p < 0.00001) in favor of the PBL group"
