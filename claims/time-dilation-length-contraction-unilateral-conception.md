## Evidence

### Paul Alstein 2021

Paul Alstein, Kim Krijtenburg-Lewerissa, and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2021). Teaching and learning special relativity theory in secondary and lower undergraduate education: A literature review. Physical Review Physics Education Research 17, 023101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.17.023101

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Selçuk, an analysis of written questionnaires and interview sessions with preservice teachers (N = 185) finding that "students fail to recognize the symmetry of time dilation and length contraction". No effect size is printed.

> "It was found that students fail to recognize the symmetry of time dilation and length contraction, considering instead that they are unilateral phenomena that occur only in the moving frame of reference."
