### Boxiong Wei 2025

Boxiong Wei. (2025). Performance Evaluation and Implications of Large Language Models in Radiology Board Exams: Prospective Comparative Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/64284

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective comparative analysis testing five LLMs on 150 image-free radiology board-style multiple-choice questions, with each model's first response recorded. The article reports GPT-4 "significantly outperforming all other models", with Claude at 62% (93/150; P<.001) and Bard at 54.7% (82/150; P<.001).

> "GPT-4 achieved the highest accuracy (83.3%, 125/150), significantly outperforming all other models. Specifically, Claude achieved an accuracy of 62% (93/150; P<.001), Bard 54.7% (82/150; P<.001)"
