Wu Yaqiu 2026¶

Wu Yaqiu, Cheng Meixiong. (2026). Effectiveness of the flipped classroom in medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09526-8

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Synthesis-wide finding from the review's discussion: heterogeneity exceeded I² 90% across all five outcome domains, and prespecified subgroup analyses mostly failed to explain it; the authors list instructional design granularity, facilitation skills, implementation fidelity, assessment validity, and prior study habits as potential effect modifiers.

"A major finding of this review was the consistently high heterogeneity across outcomes (I² > 90%). Although prespecified subgroup analyses by teaching format, learner group, and course type were conducted, most did not explain the observed variability"
