## Evidence

### Cao 2023

Cao, C., Ding, Z., Lee, G.-G., Jiao, J., Lin, J., & Zhai, X. (2023). Elucidating STEM Concepts through Generative AI: A Multi-modal Exploration of Analogical Reasoning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2308.10454](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2308.10454)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case evaluation of the third phase, evolving static visual analogies into dynamic ones. The authors report difficulty devising suitable transitions or motions to articulate the analogy dynamically, using the water-tank example.

> "we found it difficult to generate a visual representation that effectively conveyed the concept of "water flowing from the tank with more water to the tank with less water.""
