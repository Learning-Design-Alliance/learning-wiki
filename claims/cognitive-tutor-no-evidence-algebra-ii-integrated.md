Scope statement from the WWC systematic review: of 22 eligible studies identified, only those of Algebra I and Geometry met group design standards. Table 1 lists "No evidence" for Algebra II and Integrated Math I, II, and III, so no effectiveness rating is reported for those courses.

> "The findings in this report pertain only to Cognitive Tutor® Algebra I and Cognitive Tutor® Geometry. No studies that examine Cognitive Tutor® Algebra II or Cognitive Tutor® Integrated Math I, II, and III fall within the scope of the Secondary Mathematics review protocol and meet WWC group design standards."
