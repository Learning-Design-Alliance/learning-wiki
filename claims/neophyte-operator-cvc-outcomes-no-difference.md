## Evidence

### De Cassai Alessandro 2021

De Cassai Alessandro, Geraldini Federico, Pasin Laura, Boscolo Annalisa, Zarantonello Francesco, Tocco Martina, Pretto Chiara, Perona Matteo, Carron Michele, Navalesi Paolo. (2021). Safety in training for ultrasound guided internal jugular vein CVC placement: a propensity score analysis. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-021-01460-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup analysis comparing matched neophyte-operator and proficient-resident cohorts (224 procedures each after matching). Matched outcomes showed failure 1.8 vs 2.2% (p = 0.736), hematoma 1.8 vs 2.2% (p = 0.736), malposition 3.6 vs 3.1% (p = 0.792), pneumothorax 1.3 vs 0.9% (p = 0.652), arterial puncture 0.4 vs 2.7% (p = 0.056), none significant.

> "A subgroup analysis was conducted analyzing the PO group with a neophyte operators (NO) subgroup defined as operators with less than 2 years of experience and who performed 20 or less catheterizations."
