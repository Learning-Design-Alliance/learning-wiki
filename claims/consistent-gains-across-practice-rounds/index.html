Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • KT4EQG's education-value objective produces an adaptive KC-selection policy: weaker students are given better-mastered concepts and stronger students progressively more challenging ones — related
  • Providing baselines with oracle knowledge-concept selection substantially improves their performance, identifying concept selection as a key bottleneck, though baselines with oracle KCs still trail KT4EQG — related
  • KT4EQG achieves the highest exam score across all knowledge modules on both XES3G5M and MOOCRADAR, outperforming all question-generation baselines — related
  • Without alignment training, generated questions collapse to similar content regardless of the intended knowledge concept, whereas KT4EQG questions spread across many verified KCs — related
  • Reinforcement learning with a learned alignment reward substantially improves question-concept alignment after supervised fine-tuning, while answerability remains high throughout training — related
  • KT4EQG remains competitive when evaluated with alternative knowledge tracers (BKT and DKT), though its advantage varies across modules — related
