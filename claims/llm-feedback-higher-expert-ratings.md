## Evidence

### Yijun Liu 2026

Yijun Liu, Yifan Song, John Gallagher, Sarah Sterman, Tal August. (2026). FOXGLOVE: Understanding Goal-Oriented and Anchored Writing Feedback from Experts and LLMs on Argumentative Essays. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06271

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Expert quality-rating study (§5.1): two blind college-level English instructors rated 105 comments (61 LLM, 44 human) on 5-point scales across seven dimensions, yielding 1,430 ratings with Cohen's κw = 0.36 for sentence-level feedback. The article reports "the highest difference being in specificity (∆ = +1.85)".

> "For sentence level feedback, LLM feed- back scored higher than human feedback on six out of seven dimensions: with the highest difference being in specificity (∆ = +1.85), followed by ac- tionability (+1.40). The only dimension models did not perform better on was tone (−0.19)."
