Evidence¶

Xinming Yang 2025¶

Xinming Yang, Haasil Pujara, Jun Li. (2025). Learning by Teaching: Engaging Students as Instructors of Large Language Models in Computer Science Education. Published as a conference paper at COLM 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05979 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.05979

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of an after cohort (N approximately 49 to 80) against historical cohorts (N = 133 - 237) in a Computer Organization and Assembly Language course, using only unchanged coursework. The article reports "a statistically significant improvement in student performance on both Assignments (p = 0.028) and Projects (p = 0.018)"; no effect size is printed.

"Applying our approach led to a statistically significant improvement in student performance on both Assignments (p = 0.028) and Projects (p = 0.018)."

Xinming Yang 2025 (2)¶

Xinming Yang, Haasil Pujara, Jun Li. (2025). Learning by Teaching: Engaging Students as Instructors of Large Language Models in Computer Science Education. Published as a conference paper at COLM 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05979

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Same quasi-experimental cohort comparison, exam measure. The article reports the exam increases were "not statistically significant" (p = 0.693) and suggests the four-assignment dosage "had a more localized effect that did not register as strongly on broader measures." No effect size is printed.

"Conversely, the observed score increases for Exams (p = 0.693) were not statistically significant."
