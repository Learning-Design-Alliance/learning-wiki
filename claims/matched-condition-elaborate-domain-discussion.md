## Evidence

### B. Slof 2012

B. Slof, G. Erkens, P. A. Kirschner, J. Janssen, J. G. M. Jaspers. (2012). Successfully carrying out complex learning-tasks through guiding teams' qualitative and quantitative reasoning. Instr Sci 40:623–643. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-011-9185-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multilevel analysis of event-level chat codes also found a significant category effect for relations (b = 16.47, P < 0.05), with matched learners discussing more and different kinds of relationships than mismatched learners; no effect sizes printed.

> "First, a marginally signiﬁcant category effect for concepts (b = 11.91, P = 0.07) was found; learners in the matched condition discussed more concepts than learners in the mismatched condition."

### B. Slof 2012 (2)

B. Slof, G. Erkens, P. A. Kirschner, J. Janssen, J. G. M. Jaspers. (2012). Successfully carrying out complex learning-tasks through guiding teams' qualitative and quantitative reasoning. Instr Sci 40:623–643. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-011-9185-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of domain discussion codes showed matched learners discussed more mathematical relationships than causal-only and mismatched learners; only b coefficients and p-values are printed, no standardized effect sizes.

> "MLAs also revealed that learners in the matched condition discussed more mathematical relationships than learners in both the causal-only (b = 4.96, P\ 0.05) and mismatched ( b = 6.36, P\ 0.05) conditions."
