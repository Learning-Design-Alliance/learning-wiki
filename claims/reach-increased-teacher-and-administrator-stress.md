## Evidence

### Sporte 2016

Sporte, S.E., & Jiang, J.Y. (2016). Teacher Evaluation in Practice: Year 3 Teacher and Administrator Perceptions of REACH. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/page/teacher-evaluation

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Summary section reporting Year 3 survey results from the MVMS teacher survey and Consortium Administrator Survey on self-reported stress and anxiety attributable to the evaluation process.
