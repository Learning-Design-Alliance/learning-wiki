# High-school students rate multi-sensory astronomy exploration as highly engaging and report increased confidence that astronomy is accessible for blind and visually impaired people

## Evidence

### Rachele Toniolo 2026

Rachele Toniolo, Anita Zanella, Andrea Cottinelli, Giovanni Liuzzi, Sara Ricciardi, Massimo Grassi, Stefano Delle Monache. (2026). AMACA: Astronomy education with a Multi-sensory, Accessible, and Circular Approach. International Journal of Science Education, Part B: Communication and Public Engagement. https://doi.org/10.1080/21548455.2025.2594048

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Self-assessment questionnaires on 5-point scales collected from 39 HS students during 2023 after the Festival. On the accessibility question, "The large majority of the students (79.5%) answered the last question with" the more-confident option; 12.8% said their opinion had not changed.

> "Focusing on the first question, most of the students chose 5 (53.8%) and 4 (38.5%), indicating that they found the possibility of using multiple senses to learn about astronomy highly engaging."
