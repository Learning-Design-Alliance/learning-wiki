## Evidence

### Yang Ningxi 2018

Yang Ningxi, Xiao Han, Cao Yingnan, Li Shiyue, Yan Hong, Wang Yifang. (2018). Does narrative medicine education improve nursing students’ empathic abilities and academic achievement? A randomised controlled trial. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060518781476

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA of empathy at six time points (T1 January 2015 to T6 July 2017) in the cluster randomised trial. "Empathic ability increased rapidly after T3" in Group 3, with T1 versus T3–T6 differences significant (P<0.05); means rose from 104.42 (T1) to about 110 (T3–T6).

> "For Group 3, empathic ability increased rapidly after T3 (after the two-term intervention), and the differences in empathic ability between T1 and T3, T4, T5 and T6 were statistically significant (P<0.05)."
