## Evidence

### Pynoo 2012

Pynoo, B., Tondeur, J., Van Braak, J., Duyck, W., Sijnave, B. & Duyck, P. (2012). Teachers' acceptance and use of an educational portal. Computers & Education, 58(4), 1308-1317. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2011.12.026

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A two-step cluster analysis on five usage parameters from 919 teachers' portal logs identified four user types. The abstract reports that "four types of portal-users are distinguished: ‘new’ (N=37), ‘light’ (N=641), ‘medium’ (N=201), and ‘heavy’ (N=40)."

> "Based on the observed use data at T1, four types of portal-users are distinguished: ‘new’ (N=37), ‘light’ (N=641), ‘medium’ (N=201), and ‘heavy’ (N=40)."
