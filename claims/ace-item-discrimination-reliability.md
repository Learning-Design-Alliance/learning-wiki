## Evidence

### Ilic Dragan 2014

Ilic Dragan, Nordin Rusli Bin, Glasziou Paul, Tilson Julie K, Villanueva Elmer. (2014). Development and validation of the ACE tool: assessing medical trainees' competency in evidence based medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-114

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Individual item analysis across the 342-participant cross-sectional sample. All 15 items were positively indexed and exceeded the satisfactory 0.20 threshold, described as "excellent". No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The ability of the ACE tool to discriminate between participants with high versus low overall scores (IDI) was excellent, with items ranging from 0.37 to 0.84."

### Ilic Dragan 2014 (2)

Ilic Dragan, Nordin Rusli Bin, Glasziou Paul, Tilson Julie K, Villanueva Elmer. (2014). Development and validation of the ACE tool: assessing medical trainees' competency in evidence based medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-114

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item-total correlations ranged from 0.14 to 0.20 for most items; items 5, 6 and 8 (0.03, 0.04, 0.06) fell below the 0.15 acceptability threshold. These items address critical appraisal, specifically selection and performance bias.

> "Internal reliability (ITC) was acceptable for the majority of items apart from three (item 5, item 6 and item 8)."
