Evidence¶

Joseph Antonides 2022¶

Joseph Antonides, Michael T. Battista. (2022). Towards an Elaboration of Concreteness Fading: Reflections on a Constructivist Teaching Experiment. Proceedings of the forty-fourth annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED630441

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

One-on-one constructivist teaching experiments with two preservice middle school teachers (DC and NK) enumerating permutations as cube towers. The authors report that students' reasoning "shifted from operating on towers to operating primarily on symbolic representations", matching CF's enactive-iconic-symbolic stages.

"Gradually, the students' reasoning shifted from operating on towers to operating primarily on symbolic representations, which they could later use to reason in novel situations."
