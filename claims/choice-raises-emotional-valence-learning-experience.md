## Evidence

### Clément 2024

Clément, B., Sauzéon, H., Roy, D., & Oudeyer, P.-Y. (2024). Improved Performances and Motivation in Intelligent Tutoring Systems: Combining Machine Learning and Learner Choice. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.01669](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.01669)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA (algo x choice) on the summative Emotional Scale Score from the RCT; marginal means show Choice: EmoScale mean = 426.05 (sd : 183.58) versus No Choice: 338.86 (sd : 226.96), which the authors say suggests greater satisfaction.

> "The main significant effect revealed a difference for the Emotional Scale score between students who have choices and student without choice (Choice, [F (1, 261) = 12.060, p − value = 0.001, η2 = 0.044])."
