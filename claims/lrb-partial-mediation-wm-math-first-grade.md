# LRBs partially mediate the association between working memory and first-grade math achievement, with a significant direct effect remaining

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In first graders, the indirect path from working memory through LRBs to math was significant, and the direct effect of WM on math remained significant, indicating partial mediation. [→ Rivella 2025](#rivella-2025)

## Evidence

### Rivella 2025

Rivella, C., & Viterbori, P. (2025). Executive functions and school achievement: The mediating role of learning‐related behaviour in primary school children. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70053

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation model (Model 1a) with the WM factorial score as predictor, teacher-rated LRB as mediator, and math as outcome in a correlational study of 91 first graders, covariates included; the "indirect path was significant" with CI excluding zero and the direct effect retained. No effect size is printed for the indirect effect.

> "The mediation hypothesis was confirmed, as the indirect path was significant (CI [.033, .204]). The direct effect of WM on Math was also significant (p = .046, CI [.011, 1.306]), indicating partial mediation."
