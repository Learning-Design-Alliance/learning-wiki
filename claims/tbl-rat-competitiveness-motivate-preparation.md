## Evidence

### Burgess A. 2018

Burgess A., Roberts C., Ayton T., Mellis C. (2018). Implementation of modified team-based learning within a problem based learning medical curriculum: a focus group study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1172-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group thematic analysis; students attributed preparation to peer accountability and "friendly competitiveness", and reported the smaller groups (five versus ten students) increased dialogue and motivation to prepare.

> "In TBL, the individual and team tests appeared to be an important influence in ensuring students came to class prepared relative to the rest of their team. Reasons given by the students included not wanting to let their team down, and feeling a sense of friendly competitiveness."
