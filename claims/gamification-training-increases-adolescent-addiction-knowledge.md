## Evidence

### Taghipour Esmaeel 2023

Taghipour Esmaeel, Vizeshfar Fatemeh, Zarifsanaiey Nahid. (2023). The effect of gamification-based training on the knowledge, attitudes, and academic achievement of male adolescents in preventing substance and internet addiction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04858-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study with 114 male adolescents aged 13–15 assigned to intervention or control groups; post-tests immediately and one month later. Intervention-group knowledge of substance abuse rose from a pre-test mean of 4.40 ± 2.20 to "9.04 ± 1.21" post-test versus "4.82 ± 2.03" in controls (P = 0.000).

> "However, following gamification-based training, there was a significant increase in the mean knowledge scores on substance and internet addiction in the intervention group (P < 0.001). This increase in addiction knowledge persisted immediately after training and one month later (P < 0.001)"
