Evidence¶

Wang 2026¶

Wang, Y., Tang, X., Wu, S., & Sun, X. (2026). Growth mindsets are less endorsed and less associated with academic performance in non-WEIRD cultures. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00406-w

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Three-level multilevel mixed-effects model (Model B) in Mplus with sampling weights, students nested in schools nested in countries, controlling for gender, age, social class, GDP per capita and population density. Printed coefficients: "reading (b = 18.94, β = .21"; the association is associational, not causal.

"significant main effects were found that a growth mindset was associated with higher performance in reading (b = 18.94, β = .21, se = 2.58, p < .001), science (b = 15.82, β = .20, se = 2.55, p < .001), and math (b = 12.62, β = .16, se = 2.09, p < .001, Table 1)."
