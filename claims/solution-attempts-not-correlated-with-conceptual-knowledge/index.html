Christian Hartmann 2021¶

Christian Hartmann, Tamara van Gog, Nikol Rummel. (2021). Preparatory effects of problem solving versus studying examples prior to instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09528-z

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Within the PF condition (n = 55), correlations between solution attempt measures (coded 0–4 canonical components; number of solutions, M = 2.95, SD = 1.25) and conceptual knowledge were non-significant: "Neither solution quality, r(55) = 0.10, p = 0.453, nor quantity, r(55) = 0.12, p = 0.374".
