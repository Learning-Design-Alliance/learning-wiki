## Evidence

### Krautter Markus 2017

Krautter Markus, Diefenbacher Katja, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Maatouk Imad, Herrmann-Werner Anne, Koehl-Hackert Nadja, Herzog Wolfgang, Nikendei Christoph. (2017). Physical examination skills training: Faculty staff vs. patient instructor feedback—A controlled trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0180308

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with 14 PIs and 14 physicians giving feedback to 40 final-year students; feedback content-analyzed against a video-assessor gold standard. The article reports "no statistical significant difference concerning overall matching" (p = .219).

> "There was no statistical significant difference concerning overall matching of physician or PI feedback with gold standard ratings by video assessment (p = .219)."
