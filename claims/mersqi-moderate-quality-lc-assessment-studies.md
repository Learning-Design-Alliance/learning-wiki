`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The methodological-quality section reports MERSQI (maximum 18) scores for all 60 studies with a "median of 12.5 (IQR 11.5–13.5)". Outcomes were limited to performance-level measures in 58/60 (96.7%) of studies; methodological quality was broadly similar across the three modalities.

> "Across the 60 included studies, total scores ranged from 9.5 to 14.5, with a median of 12.5 (IQR 11.5–13.5)."

### Mainwaring Elizabeth M. A. 2026 (2)

Mainwaring Elizabeth M. A., Guidozzi Nadia, James Jonnie P., Pantling Jai, Menon Nainika, Salem Riadh, Markar Sheraz R. (2026). Objective technical skills assessment in laparoscopic cholecystectomy: a systematic review of manual, kinematic, and AI-based tools. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-13309-z

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

In the risk-of-bias results, "QUADAS-2 identified patient selection as the principal source of potential bias" in AI studies, while risk was low for the index test in 12/13 (92.3%). Overall, 3/13 (23.1%) AI studies were low risk across all domains. The discussion links this to generalisability concerns.

> "Among AI studies, QUADAS-2 identified patient selection as the principal source of potential bias: 4/13 (30.8%) were high risk and 6/13 (46.2%) unclear"
