### Lin Peixin 2026

BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10160-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hybrid thematic analysis of 14 in-depth interviews with purposively sampled experimental-group students; participants perceived narrative cases as supporting meaning-making and empathy awakening within the integrated model.

> "Participants reported that case-based storytelling made knowledge "easier to accept" (P9) and helped them transcend viewing patients as "cold cases" to seeing them as "real, living people" (P10)."

### Lin Peixin 2026 (2)

Lin Peixin, Yin Xiaolin, Zheng Xuankun, He Sijia, Cai Yuexin, Deng Xiaozheng, Chen Tongtong, Liang Weiquan, Liang Suqiong, Liang Yuanke, Lu Yiwen, Lin Haoyu. (2026). Exploring core competence in first-year medical students through narrative medicine and peer-assisted learning: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10160-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the same 14-interview qualitative phase, students described a safety-participation-practice dynamic in which the relaxed peer-led space allowed asking questions and receiving immediate corrections without fear of mistakes.

> "Students described the peer-led environment as "very open" (P14) and "less stressful" (P7) than traditional teacher-led classes, noting that the "group format forced me to speak" (P9)."
