## Evidence

### Behmanesh Fereshteh 2025

Behmanesh Fereshteh, Gholamnia-Shirvani Zeinab, Ghaffarifar Saeideh, Nikbakht Hossein-Ali, Nazmi Sana. (2025). Designing, implementing, and evaluating a gamification approach in childbirth training for midwifery students using computer and mobile application. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07370-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Performance was evaluated individually at the mother's bedside with a checklist based on the Ministry of Health's childbirth protocol. The gamified group scored 8.23 ± 1.38 versus 5.43 ± 1.65 for lecture, with a standardized mean difference of 1.83 (1.25 to 2.39). The text states the gamification group mean was "nearly three times lower" at four weeks, an apparent wording error since its mean was higher.

> "However, four weeks after the study, the average difference between the gamification and traditional education groups was 2.80 (95% CI: 2.06–3.54), which was statistically significant (p = 0.001)."
