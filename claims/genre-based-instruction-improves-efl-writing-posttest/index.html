Kalali and Pishkar 2015¶

Nazanin Naderi Kalali, Kian Pishkar. (2015). Genre Analysis and Writing Skill: Improving Iranian EFL Learners Writing Performance through the Tenets of Genre Analysis. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 6(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.6p.119

Independent-samples t-test on holistic post-writing scores of 15 experimental vs 15 control Iranian EFL learners after sixteen hours of genre-based vs traditional instruction. The article reports "significant differences between the performances of the two groups" on the post-writing test.
