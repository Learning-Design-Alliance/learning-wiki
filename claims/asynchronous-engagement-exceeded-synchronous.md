Matteo Luca Ruggiero and Lorenzo Galante. (2020). Online Tutoring in Introductory Physics Courses: a Lockdown Experience. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2011.13315](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2011.13315)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Attendance and platform-usage records from the tutoring programme; the article reports an average weekly synchronous attendance of 385 students (about 43 per group) and that "by the end of the summer exam session, we had registered about 9000 students on the Moodle platform", who kept using recordings and questionnaires after tutoring ended in June.
