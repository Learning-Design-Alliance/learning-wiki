## Evidence

### Hassan Ahmed A. 2023

Hassan Ahmed A., Elbashir Mustafa I., Al-Nafeesah Abdullah, AlEed Ashwaq, Adam Ishag. (2023). Association between Serum 25-Hydroxyvitamin D Concentrations and Academic Performance among Adolescent Schoolchildren: A Cross-Sectional Study. Nutrients 15(21):4552. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15214552

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the cross-sectional study of 241 schoolchildren; each additional year of age was associated with decreased odds of poor performance ("AOR = 0.59, 95% CI 0.48–0.72"). Linear regression also printed age coefficient 3.23, p < 0.001.

> "age (AOR = 0.59, 95% CI 0.48–0.72), 25(OH)D levels (AOR = 0.97, 95% CI 0.95–0.99) were associated with decreased odds of poor academic performance"
