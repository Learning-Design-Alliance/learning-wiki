Thomas Weymuth and Markus Reiher. (2021). Immersive Interactive Quantum Mechanics for Teaching and Learning Chemistry. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.2533/chimia.2021.45](https://doi.org/10.2533/chimia.2021.45)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Demonstration task in section 3.3: interactive exploration of electrophilic aromatic substitution of aniline with Br+ using real-time quantum chemistry. The article reports about 230 kJ/mol released for ortho and para products versus about 40 kJ/mol for meta, and about 170 versus 130 kJ/mol for the methyl-substituted case. These are simulation outputs, not learner measurements.

> "both the ortho- and the para-substituted derivative can be created easily, and both are energetically strongly favored; about 230 kJ/mol are released in both cases"
