## Evidence

### Mollborn 2014

Mollborn, S., Lawrence, E., James-Hawkins, L., & Fomby, P. (2014). When Do Socioeconomic Resources Matter Most in Early Childhood? Advances in Life Course Research. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.alcr.2014.03.001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Weighted descriptive statistics from ECLS-B (Table 1) documented change in SES resources across waves. Income rose "from 290% of the federal poverty line … in infancy to 351% in the fall of kindergarten," assets rose slightly (0.69 to 0.73), and maternal education rose by more than a third of a year.

> "Average household income increased from 290% of the federal poverty line (adjusted for household size) in infancy to 351% in the fall of kindergarten. This increase was concentrated in the prekindergarten year."
