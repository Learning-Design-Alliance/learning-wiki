## Evidence

### Hannan 2026

Hannan, M., Rainier, S., & Perry, R. (2026). Beyond curriculum adoption: What district leaders are saying about using Illustrative Mathematics. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681318

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview finding from the seven-district cross-case study: half of leaders reported initial teacher resistance; several felt teachers would have benefited from professional learning on the curriculum's rationale.

> "Half the district leaders who were interviewed for this study reported initial teacher resistance, with concerns centered on autonomy, “productive struggle,” and the question of whether the curriculum would meet their students’ needs."

### Hannan 2026 (2)

Hannan, M., Rainier, S., & Perry, R. (2026). Beyond curriculum adoption: What district leaders are saying about using Illustrative Mathematics. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681318

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic finding from the semistructured interviews: leaders combined learning walks and professional learning to reinforce expectations, citing Magnolia's instructional rounds and Maple's coaching-communicated fidelity guidance as spotlights.

> "Leaders described two key mechanisms for reinforcing expectations: (a) using classroom visits, which they call “learning walks,” to signal that they expected to see the adopted curriculum in use and (b) relying on professional learning opportunities to keep curriculum use a regular topic of conversation."
