## Evidence

### Dutt Deepaysh DCS 2025

Dutt Deepaysh DCS, Hohnen Harry, Kulshrestha Subham, Razavi Hessom. (2025). Simulation models in direct ophthalmoscopy education: a systematic review. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.79989

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Feasibility study of 10 non-medical students reviewed within the systematic review, comparing HTC Vive, Valve Index and Microsoft HoloLens interaction methods. Appendix A reports that "Simulation models with a physical controller allows has a higher ease of use and lower cognitive load than hand tracking gestures (limited to pinching)."

> "Chan et al.61 compared the use of the HTC Vive controller-based examination, Valve Index “Knuckle” controller-based examination, Microsoft HoloLens gesture-based examination, and noted that participants found it difficult to familiarise with operation of the three different simulations, and that headsets were heavy, and voice commands were inaccurate."
