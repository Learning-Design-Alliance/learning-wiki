Perveen 2014¶

Shaheen Perveen. (2014). Study of the Effectiveness of Multi-cultural Education on the Attitude Towards National Integration of High School Students. li-manager's Journal of Psychology, Educational, Vol. 8 No. 2, August-October 2014. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Study+of+the+Effectiveness+of+Multi-cultural+Education+on+the+Attitude+towards+National+Integration%22+Perveen

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Controlled comparison of attitude towards cultural diversity between control and experimental high school groups, analysed with t-test. Table 1 reports control mean 66.65 (SD 5.35), experimental mean 63.94 (SD 7.80), t-value 0.95, interpreted as insignificant.

"the obtained t - value is less than 1.96 for attitude towards cultural diversity and hence is insignificant at 0.01 level. Hence, the null hypothesis is accepted."
