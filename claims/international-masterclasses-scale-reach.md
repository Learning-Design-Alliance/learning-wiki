Descriptive programme report: a day-long programme in which students "become a particle physicist for a day by analysing real data collected by the experiments at the CERN LHC and other particle physics facilities", ending with a videoconference hosted at CERN or Fermilab to combine and discuss results.

> "The International Masterclasses (IMC) run every Spring and attract  each year more than 1 3 000 high school students in 60 countries."
