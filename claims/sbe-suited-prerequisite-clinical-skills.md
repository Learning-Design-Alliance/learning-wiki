(2025). “It’s Still Exposure Just in a Slightly Different Way”—Understanding the Contribution of Simulation to Developing Physical Therapist Skills in Ireland: An Interpretive Description Study. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding from the Priming for Clinical Environments theme: participants identified basic safety, manual handling equipment, and understanding the multidisciplinary team as beneficial SBE content because clinical educators lack time for basics. Documentation and subjective assessment structure were also named.

> "Using SBE to practice “basic safety” (P03), “manual handling equipment” (P06) and understand “what the MDT [multidisciplinary team] do” were identified as beneficial as “educators don’t really have the time to be going back to basics."
