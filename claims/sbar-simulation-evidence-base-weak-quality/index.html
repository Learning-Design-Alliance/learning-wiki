Evidence¶

Yun Jungmi 2023¶

Yun Jungmi, Lee Yun Ji, Kang Kyoungrim, Park Jongmin. (2023). Effectiveness of SBAR-based simulation programs for nursing students: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04495-8

q3 · i? · review · r3

EPHPP quality appraisal of the 12 included studies by two independent reviewers. The review reports "nine studies were classified as weak, three as moderate, and none were classified as strong," with most weakness in selection bias (11 of 12) and blinding (nine studies).

"Of the 12 studies included in the present systematic review, nine studies were classified as weak, three as moderate [22, 24, 30], and none were classified as strong."
