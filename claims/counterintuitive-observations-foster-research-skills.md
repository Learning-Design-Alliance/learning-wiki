`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

The authors' argument in the section on gaining a feeling for sciences: students can learn important lessons about the nature of science when they discover contradictions between textbooks and their own experimental results, and counter-intuitive observations hone research skills better than predictable experiments.

> "Counter-intuitive observations, like the attraction of equal magnetic poles if a coin is placed between them [15], are often more eﬀective to hone students’ research skills than experiments with predictable outcome."
