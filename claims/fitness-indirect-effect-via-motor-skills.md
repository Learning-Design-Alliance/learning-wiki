## Evidence

### Syväoja 2019

Syväoja, H. J., Kankaanpää, A., Joensuu, L., Kallio, J., Hakonen, H., Hillman, C. H., & Tammelin, T. H. (2019). The Longitudinal Associations of Fitness and Motor Skills with Academic Achievement. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise. https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000002031

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Indirect effects analysis in the 954-student cohort found borderline significant positive indirect effects of aerobic fitness (B = 0.005, P = 0.029) and muscular fitness (B = 0.003, P = 0.028) on GPA through motor skills.

> "A positive predictive effect of aerobic fitness on GPA through motor skills was borderline significant (B = 0.005, SE = 0.002, P = 0.029). Likewise, a positive predictive effect of muscular fitness on GPA through motor skills was borderline significant (B = 0.003, SE = 0.001, P = 0.028)."
