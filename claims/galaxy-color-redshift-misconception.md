### Hyunju Lee & Stephen E. Schneider 2015

Hyunju Lee & Stephen E. Schneider. (2015). Using Astronomical Photographs to Investigate Misconceptions about Galaxies and Spectra: Question Development for Clicker Use. Physical Review Special Topics Physics Education Research. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.11.020101

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Survey 1 open-ended responses to images of the two major galaxy types (Figure 4): students attributed the color difference to Doppler shift, illustrating how they connect new ideas to prior knowledge; other students cited distance, black holes, or camera differences.

> "Another interesting misconception was that the colors were interpreted as spectrally red-shifted or blue-shifted, with the type A galaxy moving away and the type B galaxy coming toward us"
