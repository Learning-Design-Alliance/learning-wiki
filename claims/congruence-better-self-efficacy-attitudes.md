## Subclaims
`q2 i2` ANCOVA with pretest as covariate showed significant between-group differences favouring the congruent group in SENI self-efficacy and ANI attitudes under the PCS method, with ANI at η2=0.05. [→ Yeh Ting-Kuang 2016](#yeh-ting-kuang-2016)

## Evidence

### Yeh Ting-Kuang 2016

Yeh Ting-Kuang, Huang Hsiu-Mei, Chan Wing P, Chang Chun-Yen. (2016). Effects of congruence between preferred and perceived learning environments in nursing education in Taiwan: a cross-sectional study. BMJ Open 6(5): e009925. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-009925

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Univariate ANCOVA on adjusted post-test SENI and ANI scores with pretest as covariate, comparing PrAPc_PCS and PrDPc_PCS groups; ANI showed "medium effect size using the eta square statistic (η2=0.05)" and SENI was described as a large effect size.

> "The ANCOVA analysis showed a significant between-group difference in SENI scores (large effect size) and ANI scores (medium effect size using the eta square statistic (η2=0.05)); (table 3)."

### Yeh Ting-Kuang 2016 (2)

Yeh Ting-Kuang, Huang Hsiu-Mei, Chan Wing P, Chang Chun-Yen. (2016). Effects of congruence between preferred and perceived learning environments in nursing education in Taiwan: a cross-sectional study. BMJ Open 6(5): e009925. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-009925

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA comparison of adjusted post-test SENI and ANI scores between the PCF congruent group (n=73) and discordant group (n=51); "There were significant differences in SENI... and ANI... scores" favouring the congruent group, described in qualitative effect-size terms only.

> "There were significant differences in SENI (medium effect size) and ANI (medium-to-large effect size) scores between the PrAPc_PCF and PrDPc_PCF groups."
