## Evidence

### Reiff FS 2024

Reiff FS, Bischoff C, Woelfler H, Roehling S. (2024). Influence of clinical expertise and practical experience on transfer accuracy in guided dental implant placement - an in vitro study. Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10006-024-01269-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Region-wise comparison within the in vitro experiment (72 implants, three expertise groups) using Wilcoxon signed rank tests; the free-end situation showed "larger than in the inter-dental gap situation" angular deviations in every group.

> "When comparing the mean values of TA regarding the angular deviation it was noticeable that for all three groups of operators the deviations in the free-end situation were larger than in the inter-dental gap situation. These differences turned out to be statistically significant in all three groups (DOC p = 0.046, TEC p = 0.029, OFC p = 0.006)."
