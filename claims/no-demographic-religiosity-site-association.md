## Evidence

### Shaddeau Angela 2015

Shaddeau Angela, Nimz Abigail, Sheeder Jeanelle, Tocce Kristina. (2015). Effect of novel patient interaction on students’ performance of pregnancy options counseling. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.29401

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary exploratory analyses in the randomized trial comparing outcomes across demographic variables, religiosity, and clerkship sites, including 31 students at a Catholic site versus 104 at secular institutions. The article reports no significant association and equal excellence percentages.

> "Additional analyses of the achievement of ‘excellence’, the ‘unsatisfactory performances’, and the completion of specific portions of options counseling between both groups showed no significant association with any demographic variables, religiosity, or clerkship sites."
