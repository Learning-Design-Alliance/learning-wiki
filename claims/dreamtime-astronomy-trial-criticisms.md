`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Program evaluation section: feedback from the four-school trial listed criticisms including that "The planisphere could be improved by using local Aboriginal astronomy content (Sydney region)", disappointment at not using the planetarium, signal sticks being least relevant, and extreme disappointment at not looking through a telescope.

> "There were a few major points of criticism from the teachers, students, and guides: 1) The planisphere could be improved by using local Aboriginal astronomy content (Sydney region) rather than content from western Victoria or other parts of Australia."
