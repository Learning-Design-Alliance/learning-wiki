## Evidence

### Yue Li 2025

Yue Li, Mikael Johansson & Andrey R. Nikolaev. (2025). Hierarchical event segmentation of episodic memory in virtual reality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00321-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Planned comparison controlling for inter-object intervals: the levels with the shortest and longest intervals were excluded, and the reduced GLMM "showed signiﬁcantly higher accuracy in the within-Mission/across-Spatial" condition, ruling out temporal-gap explanations.

> "The reduced model, including only levels with comparable inter-object intervals ( within-Mission/across-Spatial level and across-Mission/within-Spatial level), showed signiﬁcantly higher accuracy in the within-Mission/across-Spatial than in the across-Mission/within-Spatial level (estimate = 0.28, Std.Error = 0.1, Z-value = 2.7, p = 0.007)."
