### Campos 2024

Screening Smarter, Not Harder: A Comparative Analysis of Machine Learning Screening Algorithms and Heuristic Stopping Criteria for Systematic Reviews in Educational Research. Exploratory Spearman correlations of mixed-strategy sensitivity with dataset characteristics across the 27 collections. Group A showed "a positive correlation with the number of abstracts in an abstract collection" (ρ = 0.76, 95% CI [0.50, 0.89]).

> "the sensitivity achieved under each mixed-based heuristic stopping criteria had a positive correlation with the number of abstracts in an abstract collection in groups A, D, and G (group A: ρ  = 0.76, 95% CI [0.50, 0.89]"
