# PBL students achieve significantly higher excellent-rate proportions on EBERs-ISH theoretical assessments than LBL students, with no significant differences in good or fail rates

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The PBL group consistently achieved a significantly higher proportion of excellent theoretical scores (>=80 points) than the LBL group from 2023 onward (e.g., 100% vs. 27.3% in 2023, P < 0.001). [→ Zeng Meichun 2026](#zeng-meichun-2026)
`q3 i?` No significant differences were observed between PBL and LBL groups in good or failed theoretical score rates. [→ Zeng Meichun 2026](#zeng-meichun-2026)

## Evidence

### Zeng Meichun 2026

Zeng Meichun, Jia Qingjun. (2026). Application of problem-based learning in EBERs-ISH theoretical and practical teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08864-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in a pathology training program comparing PBL (n=29) and LBL (n=32) on theoretical test score categories across 2022-2025. The study reports "100% vs. 27.3% in 2023 (P < 0.001)" for excellent rates, with 75% vs. 0% in 2024 and 83.3% vs. 0% in 2025; good and failed rates showed no Bonferroni-corrected significance.

> "the PBL group consistently achieved a significantly higher proportion of “excellent” scores (≥ 80 points) compared to the LBL group. The annual excellent rates for the PBL versus LBL groups were 100% vs. 27.3% in 2023 (P < 0.001)"
