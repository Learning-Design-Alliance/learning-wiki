## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On the pretest, students often gave responses to nearly identical questions (e.g., Questions 1 and 5, and Questions 3, 4 and 7) that did not match each other. [→ DeVore 2016](#devore-2016)

## Evidence

### DeVore 2016

DeVore, S. and Singh, C. (2016). An Interactive Learning Tutorial on Quantum Key Distribution. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.01301](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.01301)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of written pretest responses from the three course years found inconsistent reasoning, e.g., between Questions 1 and 5 and among Questions 3, 4 and 7, which test the same transmission-probability content. Interviews suggest conceptual (no-equation) questions were more likely to elicit alternative models than mathematical ones.

> "Despite these two questions being nearly identical t o each other in subject matter, students often selected inconsistent responses to these questions"
