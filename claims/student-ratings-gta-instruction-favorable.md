## Evidence

### Reeves Todd D. 2018

Reeves Todd D., Hake Laura E., Chen Xinnian, Frederick Jennifer, Rudenga Kristin, Ludlow Larry H., O'Connor Clare M. (2018). Does Context Matter? Convergent and Divergent Findings in the Cross-Institutional Evaluation of Graduate Teaching Assistant Professional Development Programs. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-03-0044

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-semester student ratings of instruction from 1665 undergraduates at Universities A and B on 19 researcher-developed items; the overall mean was 4.25 (SD = 0.74).

> "All mean agreement ratings were between “agree” (4) and “strongly agree” (5), indicating favorable perceptions of the GTAs overall at the two universities."

### Reeves Todd D. 2018 (2)

Reeves Todd D., Hake Laura E., Chen Xinnian, Frederick Jennifer, Rudenga Kristin, Ludlow Larry H., O'Connor Clare M. (2018). Does Context Matter? Convergent and Divergent Findings in the Cross-Institutional Evaluation of Graduate Teaching Assistant Professional Development Programs. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-03-0044

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Fixed-effect ANOVAs compared student ratings between Universities A and B; the overall measure showed only a marginally significant difference (p = 0.06), and the largest item-level ηp2 estimate was 0.017.

> "there were statistically significant differences between GTAs at the two institutions for 10 of the 19 individual items. For nine of those 10 items, GTAs at University A were rated higher than GTAs at University B"
