Conceptual argument in Section 4.4. The article elaborates contestability (questioning and cross-checking outputs, requiring explainability), provenance (institutions describing training-data sources and bias), and non-delegation of moral and intellectual credit, citing Khosravi et al. (2022), Holzinger et al. (2025), and Bao & Zeng (2026). No empirical data.
