Document analysis of a published problem and solution from a popular calculus-based physics text (unidentified by the author to avoid singling out its authors). The Model-Visualize-Solve-Assess rubric is executed formally, yet the answer ignores that gases fill the room, illustrating that tying analysis to a formal rubric does not by itself activate intuition.

> "The difficulty is that the answer in the solution manual fails to treat the problem intuitively – to tell the story of the problem. The steps of the rubric are gone through formally – but without the activation of a sense-making intuition about molecules"
