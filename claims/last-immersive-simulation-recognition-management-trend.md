## Evidence

### Griesmer Katherine B. MD 2025

Griesmer Katherine B. MD, MPH, Thompson Maxwell MD, Miller Briana MD, Zhai Guihua PhD, Raper Jaron MD, Bloom Andrew MD. (2025). Combining Immersive Simulation with a Collaborative Procedural Training on Local Anesthetic Systemic Toxicity and Fascia Iliaca Compartment Block: A Pilot Study. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.25020

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same pilot, 19 EM residents completed a 30-minute immersive LAST case with structured debrief; pre/post surveys were compared with the Stuart-Maxwell test. Management perception followed "a similar trend with 3.0 (2.0–6.0) pre-simulation to 6.0 (6.0–7.0) post-simulation," with P = 0.12 and P = 0.08; no effect size was printed.

> "Recognition of LAST increased from 5.0 (3.0–6.0) to 6.0 (6.0–7.0) from pre- to post-simulation, respectively, with 7.0 representing strong agreement with a statement."
