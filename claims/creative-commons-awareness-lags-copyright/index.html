q2 · i? · associational · r?

Faculty licensing-awareness survey conducted in 2014 as part of the report's OER study with the Hewlett Foundation. Almost all faculty reported awareness of copyright licensing of classroom content and public domain licensing.

"Less than two-thirds of faculty reported that they are at least somewhat aware of Creative Commons licensing."

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Academic leader licensing-awareness results for 2015, mirroring the previous year. Awareness drops for Creative Commons, but nearly 80% claim some level of awareness; a majority reported being aware or very aware of open textbooks.

"over 95% percent reporting that they are aware of both copyright and public domain licensing."
