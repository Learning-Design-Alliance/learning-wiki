The review's conceptual argument, citing prior work, that literacy functions as a bridge between environmental demands and regulatory action but does not ensure regulation. The article argues this limitation is "especially pro - nounced in digital environments that repeatedly reactivate attention" via habit-forming design features.

"However, literacy alone does not guarantee regulation (Demirbag & Bahcivan, 2021; Nigg, 2017). This limitation may be especially pro - nounced in digital environments that repeatedly reactivate attention through algorith- mic recommendations, notifications, social feedback, and other habit-forming design features"
