## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After lab perceptual training, autistic children produced narrow and contrastive on-focus words with significantly longer duration than broad focus words, a pattern absent before training. [→ Chen 2026](#chen-2026)
`q3 i?` After lab perceptual training, contrastive on-focus words showed significantly higher mean intensity than broad focus words, a pattern not observed pre-training. [→ Chen 2026 (2)](#chen-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Chen 2026

Chen, S., Wang, B.X., Cheung, J.C.-W. et al. (2026). Lab perceptual training and robot-assisted training in improving speech prosody of autistic children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00425-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Pre/post speech production comparison within the lab perceptual training group of the randomized training study. Post-hoc LMM comparisons showed on-focus durational expansion emerged only after training; the article reports no effect size.

> "For the lab perceptual training group, participants produced both narrow and contrastive on-focus words with significantly longer duration than broad focus words in post-training sessions, while this pattern is absent in pre-training sessions."

### Chen 2026 (2)

Chen, S., Wang, B.X., Cheung, J.C.-W. et al. (2026). Lab perceptual training and robot-assisted training in improving speech prosody of autistic children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00425-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Pre/post LMM post-hoc comparisons of mean intensity in the lab perceptual training group; the significant post-training contrastive on-focus vs broad focus difference was absent pre-training. No effect size printed.

> "for the lab perceptual training group, the narrow and contrastive on-focus words had higher mean intensity than broad focus words with a significant difference between contrastive on-focus and broad focus words in post-training sessions, while this pattern is not observed in pre-training sessions."
