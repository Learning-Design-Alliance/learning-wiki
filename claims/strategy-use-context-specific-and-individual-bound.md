Vermunt, J. D. & Vermetten, Y. J. (2004). Patterns in Student Learning: Relationships Between Learning Strategies, Conceptions of Learning, and Learning Orientations. Educational Psychology Review. https://dspacelibrary.uu.nl/handle/1874/11958

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review reports Vermetten et al. (1999b) asked Law students from two cohorts about their strategies during four different courses; analyses of variance showed course-to-course variation and correlations showed consistency. No effect sizes are printed for this finding.

> "However, correlations showed that students were also consistent in their strategy use over different courses. This points to an individual-bound component in the use of learning strategies."
