## Evidence

### Fries 2020

Fries, L., Son, J. Y., Givvin, K. B., & Stigler, J. W. (2020). Practicing Connections: A Framework to Guide Instructional Design for Developing Understanding in Complex Domains. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09561-x

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument reviewing expertise research: adaptive experts' knowledge is organized in fewer, more interconnected schemas encoding abstract relations rather than discrete facts, which the authors argue renders knowledge transferable. The article offers this as a research-based characterization, not a new empirical test.

> "understanding is characterized by the ability to perceive and make explicit the underlying structure of a domain, its connections and relations in the form of coherent mental schemas, which allows for transferable and flexible application of domain principles."
