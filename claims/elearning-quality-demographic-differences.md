## Evidence

### Zarifsanaiey N 2024

Zarifsanaiey N, Farrokhi MR, Karimian Z, Hoseini S, Chahartangi F, Shahraki HR. (2024). Lesson learned from assessing teachers’ and students’ perspectives regarding the quality of e-learning in medical education during the pandemic: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05160-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 400 students analyzed with independent T-test and one-way ANOVA. Table 2 shows female mean 2.86 ± 0.52 vs male 2.62 ± 0.59, and nursing students highest at 3.04 ± 0.53 while dentistry students lowest at 2.38 ± 0.65.

> "There was a significant relationship between participants’ scores and age, gender, grade, year of study, and faculty (< 0.001). The female perspective mean score was significantly higher among male students. Furthermore, the mean score of the views decreased significantly with age."
