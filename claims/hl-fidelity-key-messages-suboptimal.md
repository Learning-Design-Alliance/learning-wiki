Observation-based fidelity assessment of 15 sessions; the article reports activities "were implemented as planned 80% of the time" and that key messages were emphasized in nine sessions but not mentioned in the remaining six.

> "Of the 15 sessions observed, overall, the HL program activities were implemented as planned 80% of the time; 20% of the time activities were implemented partially as intended."
