### Magsoodi Fahimeh 2026

Magsoodi Fahimeh, Ghaffari Fatemeh (2026). Development and Psychometric Evaluation of the Critical Reflection Competency in Delirium Care Scale (CRCDCS) Among Iranian ICU Nurses. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70818

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Quantitative face validity assessed with 10 ICU nurses who met inclusion criteria but did not participate in the main psychometric study. Impact Score formula applied as printed with threshold of 1.5.

> "Items with an impact score below 1.5 or judged irrelevant by participants were revised or removed (Polit and Beck 2006). At this stage, two items were removed."

### Magsoodi Fahimeh 2026 (2)

Magsoodi Fahimeh, Ghaffari Fatemeh (2026). Development and Psychometric Evaluation of the Critical Reflection Competency in Delirium Care Scale (CRCDCS) Among Iranian ICU Nurses. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70818

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative and quantitative face validity assessment with 10 ICU nurses. Two items removed for the reasons printed: inconsistent interpretations, partial overlap, and difficulty standardising interpretation.

> "These items were primarily removed because participants demonstrated inconsistent interpretations of the abstract concepts embedded within them, the items showed partial conceptual overlap with other ethical-reflection items, and their interpretation appeared difficult to standardise across nurses with diverse clinical backgrounds."
