# Knowledge gains over time are group dependent: significant time × group interaction favoring the virtual simulation group

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` MANOVA showed a significant time × group interaction (partial eta squared=.186), indicating knowledge differences across time are group dependent. [→ José Miguel Padilha 2019](#jose-miguel-padilha-2019)
`q3 i?` Within the experimental group, gains were significant from A0 to A1 and A0 to A2 but not A1 to A2; the control group showed no significant changes. [→ José Miguel Padilha 2019 (2)](#jose-miguel-padilha-2019-2)

## Evidence

### José Miguel Padilha 2019

José Miguel Padilha, Paulo Puga Machado, Ana Ribeiro, José Ramos, Patrício Costa. (2019). Clinical Virtual Simulation in Nursing Education: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/11529

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA across the 3 measurement points in the RCT; the time effect was also significant (Pillai Trace; F2,39=13.4, P<.001, partial eta squared=.407). The interaction effect size partial eta squared=.186 is large by the article's Cohen criteria.

> "the interaction term time x group (F2,39=4.45, P=.02, partial eta squared=.186), indicating that there are differences in the students’ levels of knowledge across time and that those differences are group dependent"

### José Miguel Padilha 2019 (2)

José Miguel Padilha, Paulo Puga Machado, Ana Ribeiro, José Ramos, Patrício Costa. (2019). Clinical Virtual Simulation in Nursing Education: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/11529

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni post hoc tests across measurement points: the experimental group improved significantly for A0-A1 (P<.001) and A0-A2 (P=.02) but not A1-A2 (P>.99), while the control group showed no significant differences at any pair of moments. No effect size is printed for these pairwise contrasts.

> "No significant differences were obtained for the control group (A0-A1: P=.44, A0-A2: P=.99, A1-A2: P>.99)."
