Cassie Pickens Jewell 2014¶

Cassie Pickens Jewell, Libby Makowsky, Kristin Hallgren, Jacob Hartog. (2014). Changing Education Agencies from the Inside Out: Year 2 Report on the Strategic Data Project and Education Pioneers. Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/changing-education-agencies-from-the-inside-out-year-2-report-on-the-strategic-data-project

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Key finding from the Year 2 descriptive implementation study, based on longitudinal, in-depth case studies of agencies employing SDP and EP fellows. The report states agencies "continue to refine and build on what they have learned and implemented." No effect sizes are reported; the study is descriptive.

"Partnerships with Strategic Data Project (SDP) and Education Pioneers (EP) have enabled education agencies to develop new and meaningful practices for using data, and many agencies continue to refine and build on what they have learned and implemented."
