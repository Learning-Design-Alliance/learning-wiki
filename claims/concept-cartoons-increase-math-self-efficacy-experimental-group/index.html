Evidence¶

Sare Şengül 2011¶

Sare Şengül. (2011). Effects of Concept Cartoons on Mathematics Self-Efficacy of 7th Grade Students. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 11(4), 2305-2313. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/estp

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Dependent-group t-test on pre/post scores of the 46-student experimental group taught with concept cartoons for four weeks; the article reports a significant difference, t(45)= -7.566, p<.05, and concludes cartoons had "a positive effect on students' mathematics self-efficacy perceptions". No effect size is printed.

"Results of the dependent group t-test conducted on the mathematics self-efficacy perception scale pre-test and post-test scores of the experimental group showed significant difference at a statistical level of significance of 0.05. [t(45)= -7.566, p<.05]."
