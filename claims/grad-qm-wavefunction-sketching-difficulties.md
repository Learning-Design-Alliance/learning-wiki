## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Student understanding of quantum mechanics at the beginning of graduate instruction. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06660](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06660)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey Question 2(e) asked students to plot the wavefunction in position space right after an energy measurement yielding the first excited state. "the most common incorrect response, provided by 14% of the students, was drawing too many zero crossings" despite many having written φ2(x) correctly in 2(d).

> "In Question 2(e), the most common incorrect response, provided by 14% of the students, was drawing too many zero crossings in the wavefunction."
