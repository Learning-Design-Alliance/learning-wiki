Evidence¶

Sutcher 2016¶

Sutcher, L., Darling-Hammond, L., & Carver-Thomas, D. (2016). A coming crisis in teaching? Teacher supply, demand, and shortages in the U.S. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/coming-crisis-teaching

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of the Civil Rights Data Collection, a universal survey of all public schools and districts, found "schools in the top quartile of minority student enrollment had four times as many uncertified teachers as schools in the bottom quartile," with gaps of 12, 8, and 5 times in Maryland, Louisiana, and Colorado.

"In 2013–14, on average, schools in the top quartile of minority student enrollment had four times as many uncertified teachers as schools in the bottom quartile of minority student enrollment."

Sutcher 2016 (2)¶

Sutcher, L., Darling-Hammond, L., & Carver-Thomas, D. (2016). A coming crisis in teaching? Teacher supply, demand, and shortages in the U.S. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/coming-crisis-teaching

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Nationally representative Schools and Staffing Survey (2011–12) analysis: "underprepared teachers were 61% more likely to be employed in a high-poverty, high-minority school than in low-poverty, low-minority schools (8.3% vs. 5.2%)"; inequities were more intense in cities.

"The combined categories of underprepared teachers were 61% more likely to be employed in a high-poverty, high-minority school than in low-poverty, low-minority schools (8.3% vs. 5.2%)."
