## Evidence

### Altunay 2016

Esen Altunay. (2016). The Effect of Training with TQM on the Perceptions of Teachers about the Quality of Schools. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2126-2133. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040925

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed rank test comparing pre-test and post-test scores of the 25-person experimental group; Z = -4.171, p = .000, effect size r = 0.83, which the article classifies as a large effect. Post-test perception scores decreased after training.

> "According to Cohen's d (T=3,75,  z=-4.171, p=0.00, r=0,83) effect size is large level (0.75 < r < 1.10). Post -test scores of the perceptions of participants in the experimental group about the applicabi lity of TQM principles decreased."
