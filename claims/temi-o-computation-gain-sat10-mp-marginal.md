# Treatment students significantly outperformed comparison students on TEMI-O Mathematics Computation (g* = .44), with a marginal, non-significant-after-adjustment effect on SAT-10 Mathematics Procedures (g* = .23)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` On distal whole-number computation measures, the treatment group significantly outperformed the comparison group on TEMI-O Mathematics Computation (p = .001; g* = .44); the SAT-10 Mathematics Procedures difference (p = .05; g* = .23) was not statistically significant after Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment. [→ Bryant 2011](#bryant-2011)

## Evidence

### Bryant 2011

Bryant, D. P., Bryant, B. R., Roberts, G., Vaughn, S., Pfannenstiel, K. H., Porterfield, J., & Gersten, R. (2011). Early numeracy intervention program for first-grade students with mathematics difficulties. Exceptional Children, 78(1), 7-23. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED577119

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of distal outcome measures in the same randomized design (139 treatment, 65 comparison). The article reports TEMI-O Computation as significant with printed effect size g* = .44, and SAT-10 Mathematics Procedures as p = .05 with g* = .23 but not significant after Type I error adjustment.

> "there were statistically significant differences on TEMI-O Computation {p = .001; g* = .44) and on SAT-10 Mathematics Procedures {p = .05; g* = .23), though this latter difference was not statistically significant after Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment"
