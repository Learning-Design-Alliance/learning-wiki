Pediatric in-hospital emergencies: real life experiences, previous training and the need for training among physicians and nurses. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-019-4051-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square comparison of answer categories from the survey's previous-training section, reported in Fig. 1. Course attendance was "distinctly more common among nurses for acquiring both knowledge and skills (76% and 68%, respectively)".

> "Self-directed learning played the major role in knowledge acquisition for physicians, whereas it was negligible for nurses, see Fig. 1. Attendance of practical courses for acquiring skills related to pediatric emergencies was low among physicians; only 32% had previously attended one or more courses."
