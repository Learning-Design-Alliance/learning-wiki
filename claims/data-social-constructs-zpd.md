### Peter Smagorinsky 1995

Peter Smagorinsky. (1995). The Social Construction of Data: Methodological Problems of Investigating Learning in the Zone of Proximal Development. Review of Educational Research, 65(3), 191-212. https://doi.org/10.2307/1170682

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument of the essay: the article contends that because development is inherently socially mediated, researcher intrusion cannot contaminate a natural process, and 'data are social constructs' produced jointly by researcher, participants, context, and collection means. No empirical data are offered; the support is the argument itself.

> "From a neo-Vygotskian perspective, data are social constructs developed through the relationship of researcher, research participants, research context (including its historical antecedents), and the means of data collection."
