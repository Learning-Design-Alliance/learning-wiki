Exploratory joint PCA of participant and AI-only essay embeddings (Gemini Embedding 2, L2-normalized); PC1 explained 17.91% of variance with an unadjusted omnibus F(4, 493)= 255.62, p<.001. The article labels these analyses exploratory and notes dispersion was descriptively similar across human conditions.

"Planned pairwise contrasts (Holm correction,𝑚= 2per component) showed that Engage-to-Unlock differed significantly from Standard Chatbot along PC1 (𝑝Holm < .001), whereas the contrast between Engage-to-Unlock and Time-Matched Unlock was not statistically significant (𝑝Holm =. 104)."
