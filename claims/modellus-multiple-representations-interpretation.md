## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In the motion and parametric equations activities, students who were not at ease distinguishing trajectories from coordinate graphs came to recognise that modelling with Modellus was more effective in helping them manipulate, distinguish and correctly interpret these different representations. ## Evidence

### Rui Gomes Neves 2010

Rui Gomes Neves, Jorge Carvalho Silva & Vítor Duarte Teodoro. (2010). Computational Modelling with Modellus: An Enhancement Vector for the General University Physics Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1006.4662](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1006.4662)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reported classroom observation in the section on motion and parametric equations activities, in which students explored rectilinear and uniform motions with parametric equations, coordinate graphs, and particle animations. The authors report this as students' recognised benefit, not as a measured comparison.

> "After completing the proposed tasks these and the other students recognized that, for allowing the possibility to visualise at the same time trajectories and diﬀerent coordinate graphs, modelling with Modellus was indeed more eﬀective in helping them to manipulate, distinguish and correctly interpret these diﬀerent representations of the model."
