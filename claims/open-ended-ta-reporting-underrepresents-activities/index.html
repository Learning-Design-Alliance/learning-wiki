Evidence¶

National Charter School Resource Center 2022¶

National Charter School Resource Center. (2022). How 2017-2019 State Entity Grantees Are Using Technical Assistance Set-Aside Funds. Bethesda, MD: Manhattan Strategy Group. https://www.qualitycharters.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/CorePerformanceFrameworkAndGuidance.pdf

q1 · i? · associational · r1

Methodological consideration stated by the report authors: applicant and authorizing activities were chosen "from a menu of options during NCSRC's data collection process," while all-student activities came from open-ended responses, so "data related to meeting the needs of all students may be an under-representation."

"As such, data related to meeting the needs of all students may be an under-representation of the activities in which SE grantees are engaged."
