# Studies showing positive academic performance effects used longer weekly intervention times than studies with null or negative effects

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean intervention time was 170.9 min/week in studies with positive effects on academic performance versus 118.3 min/week in studies with no significant effect, about 44.6% longer. [→ Sember Vedrana 2020](#sember-vedrana-2020)

## Evidence

### Sember Vedrana 2020

Sember Vedrana, Jurak Gregor, Kovač Marjeta, Morrison Shawnda A., Starc Gregor. (2020). Children's Physical Activity, Academic Performance, and Cognitive Functioning: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2020.00307

`q2 · i?`

Descriptive comparison across the 44 included interventions (Table 2 statistics). The article reports mean intervention time of "170.9 min/week" in positive-effect studies versus 118.3 min/week in null-effect studies, and average study duration 4.9% longer in positive studies.

> "Duration of intervened time/week in studies with positive effect on AP was 170.9 min/week and 118.3 min/week in studies with no significant effect on AP. Duration of the average intervention time/week was 44.6% longer in studies with positive effect"
