Open coding of student projects from the Spring 2024 and 2025 Ideas in Quantum Science and Technology course (12 students, no prerequisites); the article reports "seven of the twelve (58%)" did medicine-related projects. A caveat notes 2025 projects were group-based and three students might otherwise have chosen non-medical topics.
