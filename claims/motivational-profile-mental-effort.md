# Motivational profiles were associated with mental effort while studying the video modeling examples, but not with posttest mental effort

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Profile groups differed significantly in mental effort experienced while studying the videos (η2p = .056), with poor-quality and moderately negative profiles reporting more effort. [→ Lisette Wijnia 2021](#lisette-wijnia-2021)
`q2 i?` Profile groups did not differ significantly on posttest mental effort when controlling for pretest effort (p = .059). [→ Lisette Wijnia 2021 (2)](#lisette-wijnia-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Lisette Wijnia 2021

Lisette Wijnia, Martine Baars. (2021). The role of motivational profiles in learning problem-solving and self-assessment skills with video modeling examples. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09531-4

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

ANOVA on 9-point mental effort ratings collected after studying the videos, with Games-Howell post hoc tests; poor-quality reported more effort than good-quality (p = .027) and moderately negative more than good-quality (p = .014) or moderately positive (p = .009). "an ANOVA indicated statistically significant differences among the motivational profile groups, F(3, 338) = 6.74, p < .001, η2p = .056"

> "an ANOVA indicated statistically significant differences among the motivational profile groups, F(3, 338) = 6.74, p < .001, η2p = .056"

### Lisette Wijnia 2021 (2)

Lisette Wijnia, Martine Baars. (2021). The role of motivational profiles in learning problem-solving and self-assessment skills with video modeling examples. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09531-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA on posttest mental effort with pretest effort as covariate; the group effect was not statistically significant. "motivational profile groups did not differ significantly on mental effort on the posttest, taking into account mental effort on the pretest, F (3, 337) = 2.51, p = .059, η2p = .022." Equivalence was not tested.

> "motivational profile groups did not differ significantly on mental effort on the posttest, taking into account mental effort on the pretest, F (3, 337) = 2.51, p = .059, η2p = .022."
