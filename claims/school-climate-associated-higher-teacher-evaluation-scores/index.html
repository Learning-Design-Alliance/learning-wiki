Jennie Y. Jiang 2016¶

Jennie Y. Jiang, Susan E. Sporte. (2016). Teachers with lowest evaluation scores are overrepresented in high-poverty schools. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/page/teacher-evaluation

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Observational finding from the REACH Students report: "teachers in schools with better organizational and learning climates tend to have higher value-added and observation scores," holding when comparing demographically similar schools. The release prints no effect size.

"The report also finds teachers in schools with better organizational and learning climates tend to have higher value-added and observation scores, and these differences remain significant when comparing schools with similar student characteristics, including poverty level."
