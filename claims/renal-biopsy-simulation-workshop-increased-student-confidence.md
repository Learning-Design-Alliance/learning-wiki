## Evidence

### Onishi Yasuhiro 2026

Onishi Yasuhiro, Tsuji Kenji, Nakanoh Hiroyuki, Uchida Naruhiko, Fukushima Kazuhiko, Mise Koki, Takeuchi Hidemi, Tanaka Keiko, Tanabe Katsuyuki, Morinaga Hiroshi, Uchida Haruhito A., Kitamura Shinji, Wada Jun. (2026). Educational impact of a simulation-based renal biopsy workshop for medical students during nephrology clerkship. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09805-4

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Prospective single-center pre-post educational intervention with 113 fifth-year medical students; paired Wilcoxon signed-rank analysis of the primary confidence outcome. The "median difference, 69 percentage points [95% CI, 60—70]" with "effect size r = 0.87" was significant at p < 0.0001.

> "The self-reported estimate increased significantly from a median of 0% [Interquartile range (IQR) 0—5] before the workshop to 70% [IQR 70—80] after the workshop (median difference, 69 percentage points [95% CI, 60—70]; p < 0.0001; effect size r = 0.87)"
