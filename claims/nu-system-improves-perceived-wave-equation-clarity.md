### Alisher Sanetullaev 2026

Alisher Sanetullaev. (2026). A Pedagogical MKS-based Electromagnetic Unit Convention with ε0 = µ0 = 1/c. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.26056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.26056)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two anonymous, unpaired online surveys in one undergraduate physics course: Survey A baseline (N1 = 46) before instruction, Survey B (N2 = 39) after a lecture and reading guide on the nu-system. Between-survey comparisons used the Mann–Whitney U test; the clarity difference was "statistically signiﬁcant ( U = 1146, p = 0 .005)". No effect size is printed.

> "After instruction in the nu-system, the mean perceived clarity of the wave equation derivation was ¯x = 3 .51 ( s = 0 .91), compared with ¯x = 2 .91 ( s = 1 .04) at baseline. A Mann–Whitney U test conﬁrmed that this diﬀerence is statistically signiﬁcant ( U = 1146, p = 0 .005)"
