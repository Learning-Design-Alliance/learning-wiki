## Evidence

### Rotgans 2011

Rotgans, J. I., & Schmidt, H. G. (2011). Cognitive engagement in the problem-based learning classroom. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 16(4), 465–479. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9272-9

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Repeated measures one-way ANOVA with 208 polytechnic students measured at five phases of a one-day PBL event. The article reports "F (4,207) = 47.53, p < .01 (eta-squared = .19)", i.e. the effect size η² = .19, a large effect, with means rising from 3.33 to 3.66; the hypothesized wave-like pattern was not supported.

> "The repeated measures one-way ANOVA revealed that there were significant differences between the five situational cognitive engagement measurements in absolute sense: F (4,207) = 47.53, p < .01 (eta-squared = .19)."
