### barrier-themes

White, L. P. and Merritt Johnson, A. (2024, May). Realizing District-Community Driven Outcomes Through Inclusive Innovation. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/214

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of interviews across the pilot districts identified four barrier themes. Students wanted advance notice and clarity on scheduling and roles; adults cited project management and logistics coordination; participants stated that with more teacher engagement "the outcomes could have been better."

> "The turnover of school and district leadership was noted as a disruptive force. This lack of continuity of administration support was seen as a barrier to implementing the solutions."
