## Evidence

### Gautam Yadav 2024

Gautam Yadav, Paulo F. Carvalho, Elizabeth A. McLaughlin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2024. Beyond Repetition: The Role of Varied Questioning and Feedback in Knowledge Generalization. In Proceedings of the Eleventh ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3657604.3664688

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of performance differences between unique and repeated attempts among the 32 students. Students "scored higher, at 71.31%" on repeated attempts than on unique attempts, showing repetition improves immediate performance even as it does not aid generalization.

> "Our analysis showed that students had an average performance of 64.59% on unique attempts, whereas on repeated attempts, they scored higher, at 71.31% (p < .001)."

### Gautam Yadav 2024 (2)

Gautam Yadav, Paulo F. Carvalho, Elizabeth A. McLaughlin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2024. Beyond Repetition: The Role of Varied Questioning and Feedback in Knowledge Generalization. In Proceedings of the Eleventh ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3657604.3664688

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis between frequency of question repetitions and average quiz scores across the course, with a printed correlation of r = -0.59 (p < .001). The negative correlation is consistent with the course policy allowing retakes, "such that lower scores yield more repetition".

> "We found a significant negative correlation (r = -0.59, p < .001) indicating that students who tend to score lower grades on the quizzes frequently repeat questions."
