### Park Hyangyu 2026

Park Hyangyu, Han Yejin, Cho Eunbyul. (2026). Toward a sustainable model for novice ultrasound training: mixed-methods analysis of student portfolios and objective outcomes in Korean Medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09583-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis in the portfolio study of 105 students found "none of the variables was significantly associated with OSCE performance"; all r values were near zero (e.g., self-assessment r = −.09, p = .383). Spearman correlations showed a consistent non-significant pattern (all ρ ≤ |0.08|, p > .43).

> "The correlation analysis showed that none of the variables was significantly associated with OSCE performance: self-assessment (r = − .09, p = .383), post-practice self-efficacy (r = .01, p = .934), self-efficacy change (r = .02, p = .822), and self-video activity (r = .003, p = .972)"
