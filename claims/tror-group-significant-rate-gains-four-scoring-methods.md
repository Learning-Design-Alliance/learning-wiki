# The TROR group (timed reading plus repeated oral reading with chunking and prosody) made statistically significant reading rate gains of about 13 to 27 swpm across four scoring methods

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The TROR group's reading rate increased significantly under all four scoring methods, with gains of about 15, 13, 27, and 21 swpm and small effect sizes. [→ Shimono 2018](#shimono-2018)

## Evidence

### Shimono 2018

Shimono, T. R. (2018). L2 reading fluency progression using timed reading and repeated oral reading. Reading in a Foreign Language, 30(1). http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t tests on treatment-passage rates for the TROR group (n = 20, lower-proficiency classes) using four scoring methods with Holm-Bonferroni adjustment; the average method showed a "significant reading rate increase of about 15 swpm" with d = -0.71. All four methods reached significance.

> "Evaluating the TROR group in terms of the average scoring method showed a significant reading rate increase of about 15 swpm, from 84 to 99 swpm, t(19) = -3.18, p = .005. The standardized effect size index, Cohen’s d, was -0.71, which constitutes a small effect size."
