Evidence¶

Carver-Thomas 2018¶

Carver-Thomas, D. (2018). Diversifying the teaching profession: How to recruit and retain teachers of color. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/diversifying-teaching-profession

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing a reanalysis of Tennessee STAR class size study test score data, that race-matched Black students scored "3 to 6 percentile points higher" and that gains accumulated with each year of assignment. No standardized effect size is printed.

"One reanalysis of test score data from the Tennessee STAR class size study found that Black elementary students with Black teachers had reading and math test scores 3 to 6 percentile points higher than students without Black teachers and that gains in test scores accumulated with each year students were in a class with a race-matched teacher."

Carver-Thomas 2018 (2)¶

Carver-Thomas, D. (2018). Diversifying the teaching profession: How to recruit and retain teachers of color. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/diversifying-teaching-profession

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes this long-term finding to a second reanalysis of the same Tennessee STAR data. The printed magnitudes are "15% less likely to drop out of high school" and "10% more likely to take a college entrance exam"; no standardized effect size is reported.

"That study found that Black k–3 students assigned to a Black teacher in their first year of the STAR program were 15% less likely to drop out of high school and 10% more likely to take a college entrance exam."
