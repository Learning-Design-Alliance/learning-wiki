Evidence¶

Alicja Piotrkowicz 2021¶

Alicja Piotrkowicz, Kaiwen Wang, Jennifer Hallam, Vania Dimitrova. (2021). Data-driven Exploration of Engagement with Workplace-based Assessment in the Clinical Skills Domain. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 31:1022–1052. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00264-0

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Scattertext word-frequency comparison (Fig. 5) of concatenated short student reflections from primary vs secondary care WBAs across three year groups; free text was very short (average 25 tokens for student reflection), limiting analysis type.

"All three year groups shared similar words that are mentioned most frequently both in primary and secondary placements, i.e. “practice”, “confident”, “competent”."

Alicja Piotrkowicz 2021 (2)¶

Alicja Piotrkowicz, Kaiwen Wang, Jennifer Hallam, Vania Dimitrova. (2021). Data-driven Exploration of Engagement with Workplace-based Assessment in the Clinical Skills Domain. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 31:1022–1052. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00264-0

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Scattertext contrast of reflection keywords by placement setting and cohort (Fig. 5). The article also reports "History taking" and "consultation" shifting from secondary (2016/17) to primary placements (2017/18, 2018/19), consistent with the choice-of-skills findings.

"in secondary placements of 2018/19 cohort, infection control was one of the key focuses of students’ reflection, with “dry”, “hands” being one of the most frequently mentioned words."
