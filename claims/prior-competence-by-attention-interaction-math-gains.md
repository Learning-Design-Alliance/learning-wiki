### Geary 2021 (2)

Geary, D. C., Hoard, M. K., Nugent, L., & Scofield, J. E. (2021). In-Class Attention, Spatial Ability, and Mathematics Anxiety Predict Across-Grade Gains in Adolescents' Mathematics Achievement. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(4), 754-769. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000487

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple-slope breakdown of the competence-by-attention interaction printed unstandardized slopes of b = 1.08 (high), 0.85 (middle), and 0.63 (low attention); the high-versus-low contrast was significant.

> "The interaction was strongest in the high-attention group [b = 1.08, SE = 0.19], followed in turn by the middle- [b = 0.85, SE = 0.13] and low-attention [b = 0.63, SE = 0.11] groups (ps < .05)."
