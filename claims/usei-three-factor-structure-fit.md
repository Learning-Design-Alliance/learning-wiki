### Kassab Salah Eldin 2024

Kassab Salah Eldin, Rathan Ramya, Taylor David C. M., Hamdy Hossam. (2024). The impact of the educational environment on student engagement and academic performance in health professions education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06270-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis (maximum likelihood) on student responses to the 15-item USEI in the 554-student sample, testing the three latent engagement constructs; all printed fit indices met the article's stated thresholds.

> "applying the CFA using maximum likelihood estimation demonstrated acceptable fitness indices with the three-factor model (χ2 = 315.47, df = 84 (P = .000), χ2/df = 3.75, CFI = .96, TLI = 0.94, RMSEA = .07, SRMR = .05, and AIC = 270.00)."
