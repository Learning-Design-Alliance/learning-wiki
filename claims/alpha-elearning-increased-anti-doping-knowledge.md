## Evidence

### Deng Zhangyan 2022

Deng Zhangyan, Guo Jinyang, Wang Dong, Huang Tao, Chen Zuosong. (2022). Effectiveness of the world anti-doping agency's e-learning programme for anti-doping education on knowledge of, explicit and implicit attitudes towards, and likelihood of doping among Chinese college athletes and non-athletes. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13011-022-00459-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 64 Chinese young adults (32 college athletes) with a no-treatment control group; repeated-measures ANOVA on the 12-item ALPHA test showed a main effect for time with η2 = 0.20 and an interaction with η2 = 0.21, "driven by a significant increase in knowledge test scores in the intervention group (p < 0.01) compared with the control group."

> "A significant main effect for time (F(1, 60) = 14.49, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.20) and an interaction effect for group × time (F(1, 60) = 15.90, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.21) were observed on knowledge test scores."
