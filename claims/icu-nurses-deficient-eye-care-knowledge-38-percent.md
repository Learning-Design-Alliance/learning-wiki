### Y. Zhang 2025

Y. Zhang, C. Li, Y. Zhou, and X. Yu. (2025). Knowledge, Attitudes and Practices of Critical Care Nurses Regarding Eye Care Across 17 Hospitals: A Multicentre Cross‐Sectional Survey. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70120

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the 1947-nurse cross-sectional survey show the knowledge mean was 6.90 ± 2.51 with an "average correct rate of 38.3%". The lowest-answered items concerned preventing corneal abrasion (8.37% correct), tape direction for eyelid closure (13.92%) and the key objective of eye care (15.36%).

> "In the knowledge dimension, the score was 6.90 ± 2.51, with an average correct rate of 38.3%. Only 14.23% of nurses reached the intermediate level (≥ 10 points), and merely two attained the excellent level (≥ 14 points)."
