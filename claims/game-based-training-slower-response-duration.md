### Manuel Ninaus 2023

Manuel Ninaus, Rodolpho Cortez, Izabel Hazin, Kristian Kiili, Silke M. Wortha, Elise Klein, Elisabeth M. Weiss, Korbinian Moeller. (2023). The added value of game elements: better training performance but comparable learning gains. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10263-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed model predicting response duration (time from item onset to first answer) across five training sessions, 85 participants. The article reports "participants in the game-based condition took more time to respond" (p = 0.040) with Std. beta = − 0.46; no standardized effect size such as d was printed.

> "the effect of condition was statistically significant (beta = − 421.25, 95% CI [− 822.39, − 20.10], t(417) = − 2.06, p = 0.040; Std. beta = − 0.46, 95% CI [− 0.83, − 0.09]) and indicated that participants in the game-based condition took more time to respond"
