## Evidence

### Yao Qianyin 2025

Yao Qianyin, Zhu Peiyi, Zou Zixi, Cheng Yatao, Liang Yaojian, Huang Wenyu. (2025). Synergising flipped classroom and case-based learning in resource-intensive anatomy education in China: a quasi-experimental study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-102001

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 64 FCCL versus 64 traditional classroom students in a Human Structure course; Mann-Whitney U testing of the laboratory course examination showed "this 3% difference was statistically significant" with r=0.36, p=0.004.

> "In the 20 items assessed in the LCE, the average score for FCCL students was 60% (SD±16%), compared with 57% (SD±17%) for TC students. The Mann-Whitney U test indicated that this 3% difference was statistically significant (U=1442.500, p=0.004, r=0.36)"
