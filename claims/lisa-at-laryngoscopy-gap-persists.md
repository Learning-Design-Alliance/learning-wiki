## Evidence

### Breindahl Niklas 2025

Breindahl Niklas, Bay Emma Therese, Heiring Christian, MacLean-Nyegaard Ingrid Rose, Gudiksen Amalie, Frithioff Andreas, Henriksen Tine Brink, Tolsgaard Martin Grønnebæk, Aunsholt Lise. (2025). Assessing competency in less invasive surfactant administration: simulation-based validity evidence for the LISA-AT scores. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-025-03868-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-mastery comparison (Table 4) of novices' second-round performances (N = 95) against experts' first-round performances (N = 15). Laryngoscopy time "remained different from the experts’ performances" (36 s vs 20 s, p < 0.001), and the laryngoscopy metric also differed significantly (p = 0.001 adjusted).

> "There was no improvement in the duration of laryngoscopy during the second round with feedback, which remained different from the experts’ performances (median [IQR] for the novices was 36 s [27–47] compared to 20 s [15–26] for the experts, p < 0.001, Table 4)."
