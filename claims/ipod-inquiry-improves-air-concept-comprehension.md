## Evidence

### Lai 2016

Lai, C. (2016). Third graders' understanding of air concepts facilitated by the iPod inquiry teaching method. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 2(1), 1-9. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105183

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 53 experimental and 53 control third graders taught by a single science teacher, with pre-test scores as covariate in ANCOVA. Post-test means were 27.09 (SD 5.09) versus 21.58 (SD 5.67); the article reports the experimental group was "significantly better than" the control group.

> "after excluding the influence of the pre -test scores, the post -test scores of the experimental group for the Air Concept Comprehension Test were significantly better than for those of the control group ( F = 48.496, p <.001)"
