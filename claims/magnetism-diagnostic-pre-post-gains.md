## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2017

Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Improving Students' Understanding of Magnetism. Originally published 2007. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1701.01523](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1701.01523)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Pre/post administration of the 30-item magnetism diagnostic as recitation quizzes in calculus-based introductory physics courses; the article reports "28% on the pre-test" with 127 students total and "50% on the post-test", noting students still struggled after instruction.

> "The average score on the last version of the magnetism test (including all 30 questions) from calculus-based introductory physics was 28% on the pre-test (administered to two sections of the course with 127 students total) and 50% on the post-test"

### Chandralekha Singh 2017 (2)

Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Improving Students' Understanding of Magnetism. Originally published 2007. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1701.01523](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1701.01523)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Psychometric analysis of the post-test administration: the article reports the reliability coefficient alpha "is greater than 0.8 on the post-test", which it considers good by the standards of test design, and point biserial discrimination above 0.3 for 26 items.

> "The reliability coefficient, alpha, which is a measure of the internal consistency of the test, is greater than 0.8 on the post-test"
