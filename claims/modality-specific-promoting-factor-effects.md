## Evidence

### Tolonen M 2024

Tolonen M, Arvonen M, Renko M, Paakkonen H, Piippo-Savolainen E. (2024). The effect of promoting factors on learning by four different delivery modes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05864-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group multivariate linear regression (Table 6), adjusted for student status, gender and prior education. The article reports concentration significant "in the Podcast group only" with the printed B, St.B and p values; no standardized effect size beyond St.B is given.

> "When analysed separately within each teaching modality group, concentration was related to better test scores both in the short-term (B 3.594, St.B 0.321, p = 0.01) and in long-term tests (B 3.588, St.B 0.394, p = 0.028) but significantly in the Podcast group only."

### Tolonen M 2024 (2)

Tolonen M, Arvonen M, Renko M, Paakkonen H, Piippo-Savolainen E. (2024). The effect of promoting factors on learning by four different delivery modes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05864-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group multivariate linear regression (Table 6). The article reports anticipation significant only in the Vodcast long-term test with "B 6.770, St.B 0.476, p = 0.01" and that no other within-group associations were found.

> "Anticipation showed a significant association with better scores in the long-term test (B 6.770, St.B 0.476, p = 0.01) of the Vodcast group. No other associations were found between individual learning-promoting factors and the test results."
