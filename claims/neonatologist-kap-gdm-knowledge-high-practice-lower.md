### Yang Yi 2025

Yang Yi, Yang Yao. (2025). Assessment of neonatologists' competency in managing gestational diabetes complications: a cross-sectional analysis from China. Frontiers in Endocrinology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2025.1574480

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 1,614 board-certified neonatologists in Hubei Province using a 28-item KAP questionnaire with 80%-of-maximum competency thresholds. The results section reports "1,437 (89%) participants as knowledgeable, 1,513 (94%) with positive attitudes, and 1,165 (72%) demonstrating good practice patterns". Mean practice scores showed greater variability than knowledge or attitude scores.
