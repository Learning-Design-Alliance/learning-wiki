The article proposes a prediction that "the cognitive offloading group would perform better than the no cognitive offloading group when materials are high in element interactivity." This is a theoretical prediction for future research rather than an empirical finding.

"the cognitive offloading group would perform better than the no cognitive offloading group when materials are high in element interactivity (due to learners being less knowledgeable), whereas, when materials are low in element interactivity (due to learners being more knowledgeable), the advantage of cognitive offloading may be reduced"
