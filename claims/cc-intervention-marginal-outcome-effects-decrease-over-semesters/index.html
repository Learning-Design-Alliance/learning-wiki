Evidence¶

Michael J. Weiss 2023¶

Michael J. Weiss, Marie-Andrée Somers, & Colin Hill. (2023). Empirical Benchmarks for Planning and Interpreting Causal Effects of Community College Interventions. Journal of Postsecondary Student Success 3(1). https://doi.org/10.33009/fsop_jpss132759

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

FIRC-based secondary analysis of the THE-RCT RCTs, Results section, marginal outcomes. The article states "The mean (across interventions) effect on marginal enrollment and credits earned decreases over time" and that the spread of effects is widest in semester 1.

"the mean effect on credits earned starts at 0.48 credits in semester 1, and decreases to 0.43, 0.25, 0.17, 0.05, and 0.02 credits in semesters 2– 6 , respectively"
