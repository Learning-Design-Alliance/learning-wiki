## Evidence

### Könen 2015

Könen, T., Dirk, J., & Schmiedek, F. (2015). Cognitive benefits of last night's sleep: daily variations in children's sleep behavior are related to working memory fluctuations. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 56(2), 171–182. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.12296

`q3 · i?`

Multilevel analysis of the afternoon (around 3:00 pm) measurement occasion in the 31-day smartphone study. The fixed negative effect of tiredness was supported by a likelihood ratio test, but its random effect was not, indicating no reliable differences between children in this coupling.

> "In the afternoon, 5% of the children’s daily performance ﬂuctuations could be explained. Only tiredness predicted perfor- mance ﬂuctuations over and above daily trends."
