### F. Egert 2018

F. Egert, R.G. Fukkink, A.G. Eckhardt. (2018). Impact of In-Service Professional Development Programs for Early Childhood Teachers on Quality Ratings and Child Outcomes: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317751918

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Same nine-study meta-analytic subset (486 teachers, 4,504 children) with paired quality and child data. The printed effect size at classroom level is ES = 0.45 (SE = 0.11, p < .001), reported as "a medium effect at the corresponding classroom level" alongside the smaller child-level effect.

> "they showed a small effect at child level (ES = 0.14; SE = 0.02, p < .001) and a medium effect at the corresponding classroom level (ES = 0.45, SE = 0.11, p < .001)"
