### Özkan Çiğdem Gamze; Demırbağ Birsel Canan 2025

Özkan Çiğdem Gamze; Demırbağ Birsel Canan. (2025). First year nursing students’ knowledge, skill levels and learning persistence in nursing practices: a comparison between classical lecture and flipped classroom. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07574-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intra-group Wilcoxon comparisons in the quasi-experiment (Table 3): BGM t = -2.883, p = 0.007; NTI t = -3.106, p = 0.004; DP z = -2.870, p = 0.004, all favoring persistence follow-up over first follow-up.

> "The mean scores of the nursing students in the experimental group in the persistence follow-ups of the BGM, NTI and DP applications were statistically significantly higher than the mean scores of the first follow-up (p < 0.05)"
