Evidence¶

Susanne Carroll 1992¶

Susanne Carroll, Merrill Swain, and Yves Roberge. (1992). The role of feedback in adult second language acquisition: Error correction and morphological generalizations. Applied Psycholinguistics, Volume 13, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0142716400005555

q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the experimental study, 79 adult native speakers of English learning French were individually trained on two suffixation rules; experimental subjects received correction for erroneous responses. Analyses showed "differences in favor of the experimental groups" on feedback responses. No effect size is printed.

"Analyses of feedback responses indicated differences in favor of the experimental groups, but comparisons of guessing responses between experimental and comparison groups showed no evidence of learned generalizations."
