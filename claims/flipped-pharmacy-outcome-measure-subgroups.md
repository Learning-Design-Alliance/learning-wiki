## Evidence

### Cui He 2023

Cui He, Xie Xinyu, Wang Boyang, Zhao Yuan. (2023). Effectiveness of flipped classroom in pharmacy education – a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04865-2

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis by outcome measure (Table 3). Printed effect sizes: exam scores SMD = 1.82 (1.71,1.93), course grade SMD = 0.25 (0.13,0.38), OSCE SMD = 0.98 (0.77,1.20), each with I2 above 96%. The article reports "significant improvements for flipped classroom in exam scores, course grades, and Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OCSE) separately."
