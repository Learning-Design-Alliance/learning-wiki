## Evidence

### Hatzigianni 2014

Hatzigianni, M. (2014). 'I like computers but my favourite is playing outside with my friends': Young children's beliefs about computers. International Research in Early Childhood Education, 5(1). https://www.education.monash.edu.au/irecejournal/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 24 intervention and 28 non-intervention children, with responses analysed as frequencies and percentages pre- and post-intervention. The intervention group's 'very good at' computers percentage doubled (16.66% to 33.33%) versus a slight non-intervention increase (10.71% to 14.28%).

> "The percentage of children in the intervention group who believed they were ' very good at' computers doubled in post -intervention (from 16.66% to 33.33%), while the percentage of children from the non-intervention groups increased slightly (from 10.71% to 14.28% - Table 1)."

### Hatzigianni 2014 (2)

Hatzigianni, M. (2014). 'I like computers but my favourite is playing outside with my friends': Young children's beliefs about computers. International Research in Early Childhood Education, 5(1). https://www.education.monash.edu.au/irecejournal/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same descriptive pre-post comparison of intervention versus non-intervention groups. Post-intervention, no intervention child nominated computers as an activity they were 'not very good at', whereas the non-intervention percentage rose from 10.71% to 17.85%.

> "Consistent with this finding were the responses to the opposite question, activities children were 'not very good at', where computers were not nominated by any of the children from the intervention groups in post-intervention."
