## Evidence

### Van der Wath 2015

Van der Wath, A.E. & Du Toit, P.H., 2015, 'Learning end-of-life care within a constructivist model: Undergraduate nursing students' experiences', Curationis 38(2), Art. #1537. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v38i2.1537

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative descriptive analysis of 62 written reflections from second-year nursing students after four experiential learning opportunities in an end-of-life care module; open coding produced the sensory themes "'I saw', 'I heard' and 'I did'" illustrating students' sensory experiences during the opportunities.

> "The themes 'I saw', 'I heard' and 'I did' that emerged from participants' reflective narratives illustrate their sensory experiences during the learning opportunities."
