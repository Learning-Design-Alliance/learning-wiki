`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Longitudinal interview and survey study of students from the 2010 summer program across their first and second years at Berkeley. The article reports that "12 students spoke about community, friendship, or meeting people" and 6 about academic or scientific support, plus contact and study-group frequencies. Counts are descriptive; no statistical test is reported.

> "12 students spoke about community, friendship, or meeting people when asked what was important to them about the project, and 6 students spoke about academic or scientific support. Additionally, 13 students reported seeing each other at least once per week (9 had daily contact), 8 studied together, and 9 lived with another Compass student."
