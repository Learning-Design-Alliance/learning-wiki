## Evidence

### Van Der Maas et al. 2006

Han L. J. van der Maas, Conor V. Dolan, Raoul P. P. P. Grasman, Jelte M. Wicherts, Hilde M. Huizenga, and Maartje E. J. Raijmakers. (2006). A Dynamical Model of General Intelligence: The Positive Manifold of Intelligence by Mutualism. Psychological Review. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.113.4.842

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Simulation with four group factors of four variables each, using strong interaction (.08) within groups and weak interaction (.02) between groups. The zero-, one-, two-, and three-factor models were rejected (p ⬍ .05) while the four-factor model was accepted; the quoted hierarchical model fit is reported. No effect size is printed.

> "A full hierarchical confirmatory factor model with four group factors and one general factor also fits the data well; ␹2(100, N ⫽ 1,000) ⫽ 93.2, p ⫽ .67."
