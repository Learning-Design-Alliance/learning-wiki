`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of two-stage exam data from 218 students (44 teams) in introductory mechanics at Harvard, plotting team scores after the first collaboration against member scores. The printed effect size was r = 0.70 with p < 0.0001.

> "In Figure 3(b), the best of member performance in the individual round increases with the team outcome after the first collaboration in the team round (𝑟=0.70,𝑝<0.0001)."
