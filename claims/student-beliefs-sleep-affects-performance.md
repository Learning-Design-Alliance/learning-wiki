Self-reported questionnaire items in the cross-sectional survey (Table 1); these are perceived, not objectively measured, performance changes. On the belief item, "56.8% strongly agreed and 31.4% agreed that their sleep pattern affects their academic performance".

> "When asked whether they noticed any change in their academic performance during periods of sleep disturbance, 85.7% reported a decrease in performance, 1.6% reported an increase, and 12.7% stated that it did not change."
