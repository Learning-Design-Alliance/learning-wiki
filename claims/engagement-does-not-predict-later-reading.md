## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Cross-lagged paths from first-grade behavioral engagement to third-grade reading and from third-grade behavioral engagement to fifth-grade reading were non-significant, and adding these paths did not improve model fit. [→ Guo 2015](#guo-2015)

## Evidence

### Guo 2015

Guo, Y., Sun, S., Breit-Smith, A., Morrison, F. J., & Connor, C. M. (2015). Behavioral engagement and reading achievement in elementary-school-age children: A longitudinal cross-lagged analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(2), 332–347. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/a0037638

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the cross-lagged SEM analysis of 1,160 NICHD SECCYD children (Model 2), neither cross-lagged path from engagement to later reading was significant; the chi-square difference test showed "Model 1 and Model 2 performed equally (⌬␹2 ⫽ 6.15, ⌬df ⫽ 2, p ⫽ .05)."

> "the standardized path coefficients were ⫺.12 (p ⫽ .439) for first-grade behavioral engagement to third-grade reading achievement and ⫺.01 (p ⫽ .18) for third-grade behavioral engagement to fifth-grade reading achievement"
