# Sketching and viewing animations produced the best understanding of a geoscience text, while the photograph condition produced the worst

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Actual understanding differed significantly across the six instructional conditions, with sketching and animation conditions performing best and the photograph condition worst. [→ Wiley 2019](#wiley-2019)

## Evidence

### Wiley 2019

Wiley, J. (2019). Picture this! Effects of photographs, diagrams, animations, and sketching on learning and beliefs about learning from a geoscience text. Applied Cognitive Psychology, 33, 9-19. https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.3495

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment with 120 undergraduates randomly assigned to six conditions reading a ~1500-word text on climate change. The ANOVA on the composite outcome measure showed "a significant main effect of condition on performance" with η² = .19, F (5, 114) = 5.16, p = .01.

> "An ANOVA with condition as a between-subjects factor and the composite outcome measure as the dependent variable demonstrated a significant main effect of condition on performance, F (5, 114) = 5.16, p = .01, partial eta squared = .19."
