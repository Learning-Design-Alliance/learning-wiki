## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Challenges experienced in transformed college physics courses are sometimes significantly different from those in mindset studies when they are interactional, tedious, and/or related to students' personal goals and histories. [→ Little 2018](#little-2018)

## Evidence

### Little 2018

Little, A. J., Humphrey, B., Green, A., Nair, A., & Sawtelle, V. (2018). Exploring Mindset's Applicability to Students' Experiences with Challenge in Transformed College Physics Courses. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1807.11062](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1807.11062)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding development across three interview sets (5, 3, and 13 students) in introductory physics for life sciences courses at a large research university and a liberal arts college. The discussion of evidence-for-challenge codes concludes that "college physics course challenges are sometimes significantly different from challenges previously described in the mindset literature". No effect size is reported.

> "Our finalized codes suggest the following result: college physics course challenges are sometimes significantly different from challenges previously described in the mindset literature, particularly when those challenges are interactional, tedious, and/or related to students’ personal goals and histories."
