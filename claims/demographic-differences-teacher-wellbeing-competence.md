### Yang Bin 2025

Yang Bin, Song Shoujun, Zhang Lili, Xu Chunze. (2025). The relationship between resilience and professional well-being among early childhood teachers: the chain mediating role of teacher pedagogical competence and self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03227-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA/t-test comparisons across demographic groups (Table 3) in the 1,377-teacher survey: the article reports "age was found to be significantly different in teachers' occupational well-being (F = 2.96, p < 0.05)"; teaching experience and prior child-teaching experience also showed significant differences; gender and training participation did not.
