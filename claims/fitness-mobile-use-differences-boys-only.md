## Evidence

### Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo 2021

Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo, Morán-García Javier, Abián Pablo, Abián-Vicén Javier. (2021). Association of the Use of the Mobile Phone with Physical Fitness and Academic Performance: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18031042

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc comparisons within the boys' group of the cross-sectional study showed LMP above HMP on four tests and MMP above HMP on three, while "for the girls' group, no differences among mobile-use groups were found (p > 0.05)". The effect holds only for boys, so evidence is mixed.

> "Within the boys' group, intermobile phone-use differences were observed in the PF performance being higher in the values of LMP group than HMP group for the abdominals test (p = 0.005), medicine-ball throw (p = 0.047), broad jump (p = 0.009) and trunk-flexion test (p = 0.047)."
