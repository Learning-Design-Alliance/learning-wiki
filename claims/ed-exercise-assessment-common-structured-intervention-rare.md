`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey descriptive results on clinical practices among 102 Swedish ED clinicians. The article reports "Maladaptive exercise was largely assessed at the units (80%)" and 77% address it when salient, yet controlled physical activity was rarely offered (27%).

> "Maladaptive exercise was largely assessed at the units (80%), and most assessed frequency, intensity, and/or duration (80%). Most (77%) reported that they address physical activity within treatment if they consider it to be a salient symptom"
