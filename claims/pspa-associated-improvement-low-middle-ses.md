## Evidence

### Delgado-Floody P 2024

Delgado-Floody P, Cristi-Montero C, Jerez-Mayorga D, Ruiz-Ariza A, Guzmán-Guzmán IP, Álvarez C, Gómez-López M, Carter-Thuillier B, Caamaño-Navarrete F. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression on the 4,990-school sample relating PSPA classification to changes in academic achievement (2017 vs 2018) by SES group; the article reports the printed odds ratios and confidence intervals for maths and reading.

> "PSPA showed a positive association with improvements in maths in low-SES schools (OR; 1.80, 95%CI; 1.45–2.23, p < 0.001). It was also linked to improvements in reading in middle SES (OR; 1.45, 95%CI; 1.03–2.02, p = 0.029) and low-SES schools (OR; 1.47, 95%CI; 1.18–1.83, p < 0.001)"
