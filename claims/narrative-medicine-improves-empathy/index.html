Evidence¶

Dai Dongjun 2026¶

Dai Dongjun, Fan Yuzhi, Zhang Wenbao, Wei Qichun. (2026). The effect of narrative medicine-based education on academic performance and humanistic communication in residency training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53552-x

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis of 14 randomized controlled studies (408 vs 410 residents), empathy mainly assessed with the Jefferson Scale of Physician Empathy. The pooled effect size SMD = 1.51 (95% CI 0.76 to 2.26) was significant (P < 0.001) with high heterogeneity (I2 = 88.9%).
