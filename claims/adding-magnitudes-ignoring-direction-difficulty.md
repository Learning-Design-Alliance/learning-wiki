`q2 i?` Some students treat the net field magnitude as the sum of the magnitudes of the fields due to each charged object, ignoring vector direction. [→ Jing Li 2023](#jing-li-2023)

## Evidence

### Jing Li 2023

Jing Li, Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Helping students apply superposition principle in problems involving spherical, cylindrical and planar charge distributions. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2308.02003](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2308.02003)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difficulty documented on pre-test question (2) using the line-plus-cylinder geometry, supported by think-aloud interviews. An interviewed student acknowledged the field is a vector but "added the magnitudes of the electric field contributions" because he was asked for the magnitude. Qualitative evidence; no effect size.

> "some students simply added the magnitudes of the electric field due to the inﬁnite non -conducting cylinder and the inﬁnite line"
