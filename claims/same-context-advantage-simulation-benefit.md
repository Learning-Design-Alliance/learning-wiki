The review cites Koens and colleagues describing same-context advantage, whereby environmental cues facilitate memory recall in trial participants, as a possible explanation for the benefit shown by simulation groups.

> "Koens and colleagues describe a form of bias known as same-context advantage, whereby environmental cues facilitate memory recall in trial participants, which could account for some of the benefit demonstrated by the simulation groups in these studies."
