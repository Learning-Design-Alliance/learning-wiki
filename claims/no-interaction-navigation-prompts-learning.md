# Navigation features and self-explanation prompts do not interact in their effect on video learning

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The combination of navigation features and self-explanation prompts showed no interaction effect on retention or comprehension, contrary to the hypothesis that navigation would help most with prompts. [→ Beege 2025](#beege-2025)

## Evidence

### Beege 2025

Beege, M., & Ploetzner, R. (2025). Learning from interactive video: the influence of self-explanations, navigation, and cognitive load. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09693-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The 2×2 experiment's omnibus interaction test found "no interaction effect" on the learning outcomes, with BF10 = 0.04 for both retention and comprehension; an additional MANOVA on prompt-close vs. not-close comprehension items was likewise nonsignificant.

> "A MANOV A with retention and comprehension scores as depended variables revealed no interaction effect (Wilk' s Λ = 0.98, F(2, 123) = 1.27, p = .283). Bayes factors indicated support for the null-hypothesis regarding retention (BF10 = 0.04) and comprehension (BF10 = 0.04)"
