DRRW probit regression with inverse probability weighting controlling for demographic covariates and school dummies, estimating a "16%" increase in probability of pursuing higher education among intensive AP participants.

"Table 8 shows that participating in at least two semesters of AP workshops increases the probability of pursuing higher education (college, community college, etc.) after ﬁnishing high school by 16%."

Related Claims¶

  • Participation in at least two AP workshops produced significant GPA improvements of 0.55 sd overall, 0.61 sd language, 0.36 sd math, and 0.33 sd art — related
  • Program impacts scaled with exposure intensity, with the most positive and significant results among students who attended at least two semester-long workshops — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • AP participation increased time spent on cultural activities including watching films at home and creating cultural goods such as handicrafts, poetry, and theatre — related
  • Dual enrollment participation is associated with an approximately 8.5 percentage point increase in college enrollment probability, with larger gains for students from low-income and first-generation backgrounds — related
  • The art-based program studied by Egana-delSol is a high-school program, not a middle-school program, per the published correction — related
