## Evidence

### Silverthorn 2017

Silverthorn, N., DuBois, D. L., Lewis, K. M., Reed, A., Bavarian, N., Day, J., Ji, P., Acock, A. C., Vuchinich, S., & Flay, B. R. (2017). Effects of a School-Based Social-Emotional and Character Development Program on Self-Esteem Levels and Processes: A Cluster-Randomized Controlled Trial. SAGE Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2158244017713238

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Growth-curve analysis of the cluster-randomized trial found a significant linear interaction and a marginal quadratic interaction for maladaptive processes. Both PA and control students declined overall, with PA students showing a slight increase toward endpoint; "the resulting group difference at the final wave, however, was small in magnitude (ES = −.09)".

> "Maladaptive self-esteem processes showed a more complex pattern, with a significant Linear time × Condition interaction (B = −0.07, p = .038) and a Quadratic condition × Time interaction (B =.01, p = .054)"
