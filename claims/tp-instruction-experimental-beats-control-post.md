### Jing Wei 2017

Jing Wei. (2017). Effects of Instruction on Chinese College Students' Use of Thematic Progression in English Essays. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.8. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1139060

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`
