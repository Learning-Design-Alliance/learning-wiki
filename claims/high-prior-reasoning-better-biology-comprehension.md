## Evidence

### Wu 2016

Wu, H.-L., Weng, H.-L., & She, H.-C. (2016). Effects of scaffolds and scientific reasoning ability on web-based scientific inquiry. International Journal of Contemporary Educational Research, 3(1), 12-24. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1216018

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental MANCOVA with 138 seventh-grade students, pretest as covariate. High reasoning students scored 4.04 points higher on retention biology test (mean difference = 4.04). The "partial η 2 = 0.15" indicates a large effect.

> "MANCOVA results which are presented in Table 3 showed significant differences between the reasoning groups on the combined dependent variables, F(2, 104) = 9.30, p < 0.01, Wilk's λ= 0.85, partial η 2 = 0.15."
