### Yangqiuting Li 2020

Yangqiuting Li, Kyle Whitcomb, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). How learning environment predicts male and female students' physics motivational beliefs in introductory physics courses. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.li](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.li)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SEM path analysis results presented in Figure 2 of the survey study. The model with only perceived recognition explained 74% of the variance of identity (R² = 0.74 in Table III), and adding peer interaction and belonging did not explain further variance in identity.

> "Although identity is predicted by self-efficacy, interest and perceived recognition, perceived recognition, which is the only environmental factor of these three, is the largest predictor"
