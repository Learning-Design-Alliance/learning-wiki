## Evidence

### Ruppert Johannes 2025

Ruppert Johannes, Krüger Rebecca, Göbel Sebastian, Wolfhard Susanna, Lorenz Liv-Annebritt, Weimer Andreas Michael, Kloeckner Roman, Waezsada Elias, Buggenhagen Holger, Weinmann-Menke Julia, Weimer Johannes Matthias. (2025). The effectiveness of e-learning in focused cardiac ultrasound training: a prospective controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07409-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup analysis of post-test scores by self-reported e-learning time. The reference-group advantage held "up to 4 h" (p < 0.01) but not at 4.5–6 h (p = 0.79); no effect size printed.

> "The time to prepare (“How much time did you spend e-learning?”) data collected suggested that the reference group could achieve significantly better results than the study group with a preparatory period of any length of up to 4 h (p < 0.01). This trend was not evident with a preparatory period of 4.5–6 h (p = 0.79)."
