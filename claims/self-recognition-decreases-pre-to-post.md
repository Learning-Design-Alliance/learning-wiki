## Evidence

### Michael F. J. Fox and Taylor O. Pomfret 2025

Michael F. J. Fox and Taylor O. Pomfret. (2025). Self-Evaluated Expertise in experimental physics: a measure of students' physics self-recognition. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.07885](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.07885)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics on the filtered public E-CLASS dataset (19,209 responses): pre-test mean "−15.67 ± 0.08", post-test mean "−16.90 ± 0.08" (Fig. 3 and Fig. 4). No standardised effect size is printed for the pre-to-post shift, so impact is unquantified.

> "The mean of the total SEE score across all students for the pre-test is −15.67 ± 0.08, therefore indicating that on average students position themselves approximately 0.5 points on the 5-point scale below what they view an expert experimental physicist would respond."
