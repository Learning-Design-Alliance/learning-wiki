### Alexis Olsho 2022

Alexis Olsho, Charlotte Zimmerman, and Suzanne White Brahmia. (2022). A framework for characterizing covariational reasoning in physics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2022.pr.olsho](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2022.pr.olsho)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article reports results of the Zimmerman et al. study of individual think-aloud interviews with 20 physics experts (graduate students and faculty) on graphing tasks designed to elicit covariational reasoning, such as relating a Ferris wheel cart's height and distance traveled. The study found experts' reasoning was "consistently woven into physical sensemaking" and not fully captured by the mathematics framework.

> "Physics experts engaged in patterns of covariational reasoning and modeling that were not well described by the mathematics covariational reasoning framework summarized in Table I [10]."
