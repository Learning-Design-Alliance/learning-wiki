Authors' proposal in this Perspective, not a tested result. The modification merges the two original feedback-related microskills into one step, and the acronym drops the temporal 'One-Minute' framing so steps are completed 'in a time-flexible manner'.

> "We propose condensing it slightly from five to four steps, summarized by the descriptive acronym DEFT (Diagnosis, Evidence, Feedback, Teaching), by combining two of the original microskills ('reinforce what was done right' and 'correct mistakes' — now summarized as 'feedback')."
