## Evidence

### Sukkurwalla 2024

Sukkurwalla, A., Zaidi, S.J.A., Taqi, M., Waqar, Z., & Qureshi, A. (2024). Exploring medical educators’ perspectives on teaching effectiveness and student learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06465-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 25 interviews; theme 'Curriculum Changes and Faculty Development'. One participant stated "Effective horizontal or vertical integration requires equipment, teamwork, and coordination among different specialists."

> "They emphasised the insufficient coordination and integration among subjects, along with a deficiency in fundamental professionalism knowledge such as ethics."

### Sukkurwalla 2024 (2)

Sukkurwalla, A., Zaidi, S.J.A., Taqi, M., Waqar, Z., & Qureshi, A. (2024). Exploring medical educators’ perspectives on teaching effectiveness and student learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06465-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding: all interviewed teachers emphasised faculty development necessity, and each attended "two or more short courses". Change is self-reported retrospective perception, not measured.

> "Each participant participated in two or more short courses, from paper-setting workshops to lecture development sessions. They explicitly stated that their teaching methods and assessment approaches underwent noticeable changes after participating in the course."
