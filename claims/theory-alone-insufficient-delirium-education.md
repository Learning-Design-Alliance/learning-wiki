### Kılıç Gülşen 2026

Kılıç Gülşen, Işık Sevcan Avcı, Çevik Banu. (2026). Nursing students’ first clinical encounters with delirium: a qualitative study of experiential learning and professional development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09980-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Students reported that "the theory remains somewhat abstract" and that seeing delirium "is much more effective than just hearing about it," with direct exposure perceived as essential for clinical understanding.

> "Students highlighted that purely theoretical delirium education is insufficient for clinical readiness. Classroom knowledge often remains abstract until validated by real-life encounters, which provide the necessary context to make learning meaningful."
