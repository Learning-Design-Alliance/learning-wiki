## Evidence

### Wengaard E 2017

Wengaard E, Kristoffersen M, Harris A and Gundersen H (2017) Cardiorespiratory Fitness Is Associated with Selective Attention in Healthy Male High-School Students. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 11:330. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2017.00330

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model covariate analysis of reaction times in the Posner cue task (n = 54). The article reports "Time-point of cognitive testing was the only covariate that contributed significantly to the model ( F = 14.14, p < 0.001) with significantly slower RT in the morning (Estimate = −12.32, SE = 3.28, p < 0.001)".
