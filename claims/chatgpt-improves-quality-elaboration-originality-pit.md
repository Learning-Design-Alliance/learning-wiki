# ChatGPT use improves the quality, elaboration, and originality of university students' solutions to a complex ill-defined problem-solving task

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students solving the Product Improvement Task with ChatGPT produced solutions of higher quality than controls, F(1,142) = 19.07, η2p = .12. [→ Urban 2024](#urban-2024)
`q3 i2` ChatGPT-assisted solutions were better elaborated, F(1,142) = 14.87, η2p = .10, and more original, F(1,142) = 12.56, η2p = .08, than control solutions. [→ Urban 2024](#urban-2024)
`q3 i2` A significant group-by-task interaction on originality indicated a moderate improvement when ChatGPT was used, with no pre-test difference between groups. [→ Urban 2024 (2)](#urban-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Urban 2024

Urban, M., Děchtěrenko, F., Lukavský, J., Hrabalová, V., Svacha, F., Brom, C., & Urban, K. (2024). ChatGPT Improves Creative Problem-Solving Performance in University Students: An Experimental Study. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105031

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

ANCOVAs (with prior ChatGPT experience as covariate) in a randomized experiment with 68 control and 77 experimental university students. The article reports a "strong impact on the quality of the solutions" with η2p = .12, elaboration η2p = .10, originality η2p = .08.

> "Use of ChatGPT had a strong impact on the quality of the solutions, F(1,142) = 19.07, p <.001, η2p = .12, a moderate impact on elaboration, F(1,142) = 14.87, p = <.001, η 2p = .10, and a moderate impact on originality, F(1,142) = 12.56, p < .001, η2p = .08."

### Urban 2024 (2)

Urban, M., Děchtěrenko, F., Lukavský, J., Hrabalová, V., Svacha, F., Brom, C., & Urban, K. (2024). ChatGPT Improves Creative Problem-Solving Performance in University Students: An Experimental Study. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105031

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Mixed within-between ANCOVA comparing pre-test Unusual Uses Task (solved without ChatGPT in both conditions) with the experimental task. Pre-test groups did not differ, F(1,142) = 0.01, and the interaction term reached η 2p = .05.

> "However, the significant interaction term, F(1,142) = 7.79, p = .006, η 2p = .05, indicated a moderate improvement in originality scores when ChatGPT was used."
