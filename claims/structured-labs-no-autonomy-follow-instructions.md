## Evidence

### N.G. Holmes and Carl E. Wieman 2016

N.G. Holmes and Carl E. Wieman. (2016). Examining and contrasting the cognitive activities engaged in undergraduate research experiences and lab courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020103](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020103)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group interview analysis: students contrasted structured labs, where they "simply had to follow the instructions provided," with UREs where they could fiddle around with equipment and design labs where they decided next steps. Students also described not paying attention to the process due to the amount of information given.

> "In contrast, students explicitly discussed how the structured lab courses did not allow autonomy. They saw no decisions to be made; they simply had to follow the instructions provided."
