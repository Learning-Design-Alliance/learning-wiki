# The GEARS-case volume correlation is driven by practicing surgeons with more than 50 cases and is absent among resident/fellow surgeons and those with fewer than 50 cases

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Among practicing surgeons with > 50 cases, correlations were r = 0.90 (RAC) and r = 0.68 (RIHR). [→ Laverty Robert B. 2025](#laverty-robert-b-2025)

## Evidence

### Laverty Robert B. 2025

Laverty Robert B., Chesnut Charles H., Karam Joseph R., L'Huillier Joseph C., Bonte Alexander, Clanahan Julie M., Park Jisuk, Yoon Brian, Krell Robert W. (2025). Evaluating the evaluators: does C-SATS measure up? Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12150-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup analysis in the Results section of the same 48-surgeon video-review cohort reports "r = 0.90, p = 0.0004 for RAC; r = 0.68, p = 0.003 for RIHR" among practicing surgeons with > 50 cases.

> "The statistical significance of the association was driven by practicing surgeons with > 50 cases (r = 0.90, p = 0.0004 for RAC; r = 0.68, p = 0.003 for RIHR)"

### Laverty Robert B. 2025 (2)

Laverty Robert B., Chesnut Charles H., Karam Joseph R., L'Huillier Joseph C., Bonte Alexander, Clanahan Julie M., Park Jisuk, Yoon Brian, Krell Robert W. (2025). Evaluating the evaluators: does C-SATS measure up? Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12150-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The same Results section reports non-significant correlations for resident/fellow physicians and surgeons with < 50 historic case volume; no effect size is printed for these null findings, so equivalence was not tested.

> "In contrast, statistical significance was not achieved for correlations among resident/fellow physicians (r = 0.39, p = 0.11 for RAC; r = 0.22, p = 0.49 for RIHR) or those with < 50 historic case volume (r = 0.14, p = 0.55 for RAC; r = 0.21, p = 0.54 for RIHR)"
