# Post-exam analyses found no evidence of significant collusion: paired tests of identical incorrect answers were below the 0.05 significance threshold at every false discovery rate, and the Wilcoxon signed-rank test on first-versus-last-20-question correctness yielded p = 0.3133.

## Evidence

### Mengzhou Li 2021

Mengzhou Li, Lei Luo, Sujoy Sikdar, Navid Ibtehaj Nizam, Shan Gao, Hongming Shan, Melanie Kruger, Uwe Kruger, Hisham Mohamed, Lirong Xia and Ge Wang. (2021). Optimized collusion prevention for online exams during social distancing. npj Science of Learning 6:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00083-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-exam analysis of the 78 students' answers testing whether pairs of students gave the same incorrect answer more often than the 1/4 chance rate expected under independence; all q values fell below the 0.05 significance level (Fig. 2d).

> "The results of the hypothesis for testing aspect (i) confirm that the corresponding values for each false discovery rate is below the significance of 0.05 (Fig. 2d). Therefore, the empirical evidence does not support that there was an abnormal number of student pairs who consistently gave identical incorrect answers"

### Mengzhou Li 2021 (2)

Mengzhou Li, Lei Luo, Sujoy Sikdar, Navid Ibtehaj Nizam, Shan Gao, Hongming Shan, Melanie Kruger, Uwe Kruger, Hisham Mohamed, Lirong Xia and Ge Wang. (2021). Optimized collusion prevention for online exams during social distancing. npj Science of Learning 6:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00083-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Test of whether correctness increased in the latter half of the exam, where collusion would be more likely, based on the 78 students' answers; the null of identical averages for the first and last 20 questions could not be rejected.

> "we utilized the non-parametric Wilcoxon signed-rank test for paired observations49, which yielded a p value of 0.3133. Based on the evidence, we cannot reject the hypothesis that the average numbers of correct answers to the first and the last 20 questions are identical."
