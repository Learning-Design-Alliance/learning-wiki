## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Three themes emerged from fall class discussions: students wanted the TtR started earlier to avoid rushing, believed they recalled more information, and enjoyed interacting with classmates. [→ Divoll 2010](#divoll-2010)

## Evidence

### Divoll 2010

Divoll, Kent and Browning, Sandra (2010) "The "Classroom Ticket" to Concept Retention," International Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning: Vol. 4: No. 2, Article 10. https://doi.org/10.20429/ijsotl.2010.040210

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of class discussions about the TtR conducted during the 15-week fall semester courses. Students also said "We got to interact with our classmates, that never happens in some classes" and complained about feeling rushed and missing assignment instructions announced during the TtR.

> "Second, the students suggested that they did recall more information than they would have without the TtR. The common theme was that they "enjoyed the TtR because it really helped to learn information and share ideas""
