### Choi 2026

Choi, B., Lee, H., Kuhn, L., Kim, J., Hong, S.-J., Di Martino, A., Gozzi, A., Lombardo, M. V., Morrison, F. J., Lord, C., & Kim, S. H. (2026). Executive function predicts academic and social skills in autistic kindergartners based on a multimodal approach. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, and Allied Disciplines. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70038

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis among the three EF measures at kindergarten entry. The article reports the HTKS–BRIEF correlation of r = −.413; EF Touch was not significantly correlated with BRIEF (r = −.38), which the authors attribute partly to the small subset size.

> "At kindergarten entry, higher HTKS scores (more advanced skills) were significantly correlated with lower BRIEF scores (less impairment; r = −.413, p = .023)."

### Choi 2026 (2)

Choi, B., Lee, H., Kuhn, L., Kim, J., Hong, S.-J., Di Martino, A., Gozzi, A., Lombardo, M. V., Morrison, F. J., Lord, C., & Kim, S. H. (2026). Executive function predicts academic and social skills in autistic kindergartners based on a multimodal approach. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, and Allied Disciplines. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70038

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis among the three EF measures at kindergarten exit. The article reports the HTKS–BRIEF correlation disappeared while EF Touch and HTKS became significantly correlated (r = .539), suggesting stronger alignment between performance-based measures over time.

> "By the end of kindergarten, HTKS was no longer correlated with the BRIEF, while EF Touch scores were significantly correlated with HTKS (r = .539, p < .001)."
