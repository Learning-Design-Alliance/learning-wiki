## Evidence

### Bi S 2021

Bi S, Liu R, Li J, Gu J. (2021). The effectiveness of problem-based learning in gynecology and obstetrics education in China: A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000024660

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 10 randomized controlled trials reporting clinical practice (318 PBL vs 312 traditional participants), after excluding the three most heterogeneous studies. Clinical practice covers medical history collecting, physical examination, and case presentation. Pooled effect size was SMD = 2.17 (95% CI: 1.63, 2.71) with significant heterogeneity (I2 = 77%).

> "Compared with the traditional teaching model, the PBL teaching model significantly increased the clinical practice score (SMD: 2.17, 95% CI: 1.63, 2.71, Fig. 6). The heterogeneity was significant across the pooled effect result (I2 = 77%, P < .01)"
