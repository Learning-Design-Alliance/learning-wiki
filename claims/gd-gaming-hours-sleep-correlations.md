## Evidence

### Hawi Nazir 2024

Hawi Nazir, Samaha Maya. (2024). Relationships of gaming disorder, ADHD, and academic performance in university students: A mediation analysis. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0300680

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis in the descriptive results of the 348-student survey. The DGG averaged 3.6 hours (SD = 2.7) gaming on university days and 7.7 hours (SD = 4.8) on non-university days. The discussion suggests free time may exacerbate gaming behaviors.

> "A Pearson’s correlation analysis revealed a moderate positive relationship between GD and average gaming hours on university days (r = .365, p < .001), and a strong positive relationship on non-university days (r = .487, p < .001)."

### Hawi Nazir 2024 (2)

Hawi Nazir, Samaha Maya. (2024). Relationships of gaming disorder, ADHD, and academic performance in university students: A mediation analysis. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0300680

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis in the descriptive results section. The same section reports the DGG's average sleep duration was 6.1 hours (SD = 1.3), with 64.3% experiencing nocturnal awakenings to game, versus higher sleep averages in the non-DGG.

> "A small negative relationship was found between IGD and average sleep hours (r = -.151, p < .001)."
