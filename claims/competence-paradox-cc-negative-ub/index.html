q2 · i? · associational · r2

Explanatory qualitative follow-up interviews with 20 purposively selected students from anomalous survey profiles. Higher perceived-CC participants described T2I as a phased execution tool and tended "to conﬁne T2I to early ideation stages and low-risk tasks".

"Among participants with higher perceived CC, stronger capability was not necessarily associated with greater reliance on T2I. Instead, it often coincided with more critical judgment,"

Related Claims¶

  • Performance expectancy, social influence, novelty value, and creative competence positively predict art and design students' behavioral intention to use T2I GenAI tools — related
  • Exploratory multi-group analyses suggest competence-related paths differ by educational level and gender, with heterogeneity concentrated downstream of intention — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Effort expectancy and facilitating conditions negatively predict behavioral intention, contradicting conventional UTAUT assumptions in studio-based creative education — related
  • Metacognition may limit creativity during the generative stage but facilitate it during the exploratory stage — related
  • Behavioral intention translates only weakly into self-reported use behavior, and the extended UTAUT model explains intention formation better than enactment — related
