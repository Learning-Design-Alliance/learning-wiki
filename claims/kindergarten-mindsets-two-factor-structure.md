## Evidence

### Compagnoni 2019

Compagnoni, M., Karlen, Y., & Maag Merki, K. (2019). Play it safe or play to learn: mindsets and behavioral self-regulation in kindergarten. Metacognition and Learning, 14, 291–314. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09190-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis of the newly developed mindset scale administered to 147 kindergarteners yielded a 2-factor solution explaining "54.32% EV", with the two factors correlating at .23 (p = .005).

> "EF A (Table3) with oblimin rotation yielded a 2-factor solution (54.32% EV), with acceptable item loadings from .59 to .87."

### Compagnoni 2019 (2)

Compagnoni, M., Karlen, Y., & Maag Merki, K. (2019). Play it safe or play to learn: mindsets and behavioral self-regulation in kindergarten. Metacognition and Learning, 14, 291–314. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09190-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis comparing a two-factor model against a unidimensional composite model; the Satorra-Bentler scaled χ2 difference test showed the two-dimensional model fit significantly better (Δχ2(1) = 11.86, p < .001).

> "A CFA with two first-order latent factors immediately fit the data well ( χ2 (13) = 10.49, p = .653, RMSEA = .000, CFI = 1.00, SRMS = .034), in contrast to a model with one factor"
