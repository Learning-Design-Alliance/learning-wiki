Sensitivity analysis (Figure 4) and funnel-plot asymmetry tests (Figure 5) performed in the meta-analysis. The article reports the pooled results were stable and "did not show evidence of publication bias in exam scores".

> "The Begg's test of SMD relative to SMD standard error did not show evidence of publication bias in exam scores (P = .302) (Figure 5). The results of the Egger test were similar (P = . 926)."
