Michael Hardy 2026¶

Michael Hardy, Yunsung Kim. (2026). Knowledge without Wisdom: Measuring Misalignment between LLMs and Intended Impact. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00883

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of 16 LLMs' ordinal ratings of 479 lesson transcripts from 311 classrooms using bias-corrected squared distance correlation, summarized in Fig. 3. The article finds "LLM-LLM agreement is consistently higher than LLM-human agreement."
