## Evidence

### Qiao J 2025

Qiao J, Bajpai M, Metcalf M, Yin R, Huang S, Wen C, Bai X, Li S, Wang S, Wang X, Yang D. (2025). Exploring the Application Capability of ChatGPT as an Instructor in Skills Education for Dental Medical Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/68538

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Median split on the Purdue Spatial Visualization Test: Rotations divided participants into high and low spatial ability groups; independent samples t tests compared modes within each group. The low-ability contrast was statistically significant (P<.001, df 91 per Table 2).

> "With regard to those with low spatial abilities, the operational scores achieved by the ChatGPT group (mean 70.20, SD 10.71) were found to be higher than those attained by the control group (mean 55.41, SD 13.31)."

### Qiao J 2025 (2)

Qiao J, Bajpai M, Metcalf M, Yin R, Huang S, Wen C, Bai X, Li S, Wang S, Wang X, Yang D. (2025). Exploring the Application Capability of ChatGPT as an Instructor in Skills Education for Dental Medical Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/68538

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the high spatial ability subgroup (n=94 per Table 2), the operational performance difference between ChatGPT (mean 76.58, SD 9.23) and control (mean 73.89, SD 11.75) was not statistically significant (P=.22); equivalence was not tested.

> "there was no compelling evidence to support the notion that learning mode had a different impact on high spatial ability learners"
