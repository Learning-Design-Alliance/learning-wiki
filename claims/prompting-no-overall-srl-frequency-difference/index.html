Evidence¶

Rebecca Pape 2026¶

Rebecca Pape, Joachim Thomas. (2026). Exploring young learners’ prompted and unprompted SRL think-alouds. Learning and Instruction 102 (2026) 102294. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102294

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Frequency analysis of coded think-aloud data (8349 coded activities) from the 33-participant experiment; the t-test showed "no statistically significant dif- ference" in overall metacognitive SRL activity frequency (t(31) = .71, p = .484).

"a t- test for independent variables showed no statistically significant dif- ference between the control group (MCG = 263.25; SDCG = 94.96) and the experimental group (MEG = 243.35; SDEG = 64.24) with regard to metacognitive SRL activities (t(31) = .71, p = .484)"
