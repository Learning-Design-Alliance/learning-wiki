## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Interactive, media-enhanced internet-based programmes teaching basic CBT skills were readily used and acceptable, produced knowledge acquisition, and in one study produced greater knowledge acquisition than a two-day training workshop while being comparable on a simple clinical skill. ## Evidence

### Fairburn Christopher G. 2011

Fairburn Christopher G., Cooper Zafra. (2011). Therapist competence, therapy quality, and therapist training. Behaviour Research and Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2011.03.005

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The essay reports, citing Dimeff et al. (2009) and Weingardt et al. (2009), that two studies used interactive internet-based programmes with text, graphics, clinical vignettes and acted therapist–patient scenarios completed in trainees' own time by geographically dispersed trainees.

> "In both studies it resulted in knowledge acquisition, and in the study by Dimeff et al. (2009) this was greater than that obtained following a two-day training workshop although the methods were comparable with regard to the performance of a simple clinical skill."
