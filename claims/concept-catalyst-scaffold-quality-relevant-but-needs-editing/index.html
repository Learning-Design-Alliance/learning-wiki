Gennie Mansi 2026¶

Gennie Mansi, Sunni Newton, Roxanne Moore, Meltem Alemdar, Mark Riedl. (2026). Concept Catalyst: Exploring Scrutable Interfaces to Structure K-12 Teacher Interactions with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30590

q2 · i? · design · r2

Quality findings from the affinity-diagrammed WoZ interviews: all 10 teachers judged the ChatGPT-generated scaffolding questions interesting, relevant to the design challenge, and "similar in quality to those they write", and felt the questions could support students' critical thinking by prompting more writing.

"All teachers voiced that the questions were interesting and relevant to the design challenge. They felt questions were similar in quality to those they write."

Gennie Mansi 2026 (2)¶

Gennie Mansi, Sunni Newton, Roxanne Moore, Meltem Alemdar, Mark Riedl. (2026). Concept Catalyst: Exploring Scrutable Interfaces to Structure K-12 Teacher Interactions with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30590

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

In the same WoZ study, a subset of teachers found the generated wording overly ornate or lengthy; teachers still preferred more output rather than less and requested features such as color-coding to help "massage out" what was needed.

"Some teachers (P2-P4, P6) felt questions were written in “fancy talk” (P2) or were too long (P3) and needed modification."
