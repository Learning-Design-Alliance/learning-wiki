## Evidence

### Hang Su 2023

Hang Su, Borislav Dzodzo, Changlun Li, Danyang Zhao, Hao Geng, Yunxiang Li, Sidharth Jaggi, and Helen Meng. (2023). Learning Analytics from Spoken Discussion Dialogs in Flipped Classroom. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2301.12399](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2301.12399)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of the 90 group discussions. The article reports that the sum of dialog turns is positively correlated with group learning outcome, indicating the degree of information exchange positively correlates with outcome, and that variance of the cohesion score is negatively correlated with outcome, indicating stably high context relevance is positively correlated.

> "the mean of the cohesion score in an entire discussion dialog is positively correlated with the group learning outcome, which indicates that high context relevance discussions are associated with higher group learning outcome"
