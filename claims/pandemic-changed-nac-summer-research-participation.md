Self-report data from the NAC Alumni Survey, adapted from the "Staying in Science" Alumni Survey and run September-December 2020 via Qualtrics. The survey received 47 responses from 72 alumni, 44 complete; respondents reported pandemic effects including that "3 respondents had a summer internship/job cancelled".

> "15 out of 44 respondents reported that their ability to participate in summer research had changed due to the COVID-19 pandemic. 3 respondents had a summer internship/job cancelled, and 14 had a summer internship/job moved online."
