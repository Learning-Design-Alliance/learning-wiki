`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary between-intervention comparison in the randomized crossover study, analyzed with independent t tests comparing pre-post mean difference scores. The article reports the difference "was 0.36 points lower for AI-CST than AB-CST" with P=.04; no standardized effect size is printed. Sensitivity analyses using only matched pre-post surveys found no change to the primary findings.

> "The comparison of interventions found that the difference in aggregated mean difference scores was 0.36 points lower for AI-CST than AB-CST (95% CI −0.60 to −0.13; P=.04)."
