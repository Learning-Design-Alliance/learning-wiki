## Evidence

### Perez Kathryn E. 2010

Perez Kathryn E., Strauss Eric A., Downey Nicholas, Galbraith Anne, Jeanne Robert. (2010). Does Displaying the Class Results Affect Student Discussion during Peer Instruction? CBE Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.09-11-0080

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational analysis of the 629-student sample relating clicker participation on the 15 study questions to final course grade. The study reports "an average participation on clicker questions that was twice that of students who received grades of "D" or "F""; the discussion adds A students averaged about 80% participation and D/F students about 42%.

> "Students who received "A's" in the class had an average participation on clicker questions that was twice that of students who received grades of "D" or "F" (Figure 4)."
