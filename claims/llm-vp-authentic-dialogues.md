## Evidence

### Coristine A 2025

Coristine A, Li D, Foroutan DB, Ojo TF, Menon NP, Aditya I, Cook DA, Overgaard J, Pankratz VS, Del Fiol G, Aakre CA. (2025). Virtual Patients Using Large Language Models: Scalable, Contextualized Simulation of Clinician-Patient Dialogue With Feedback. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/68486

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Intrinsic evaluation of 60 VP-clinician conversations rated by 3 blinded physicians and GPT. Final human ratings of dialogue authenticity averaged 4.6-5.0 on a 6-point scale, described as a "very good approximation of a real conversation" with "easily overlooked flaws."

> "Mean final ratings ranged from 4.6 to 5.0 for authenticity, 4.6 to 4.9 for experience, and 4.5 to 4.9 for feedback"
