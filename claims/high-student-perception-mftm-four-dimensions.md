## Evidence

### Thai and Yasin 2016

Liong Kon Thai & Mohd. Hanafi Mohd Yasin. (2016). Magic Finger Teaching Method in Learning Multiplication Facts among Deaf Students. Journal of Education and Learning, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v5n3p40

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of a 20-item questionnaire (Cronbach Alpha 0.941) administered to the 70 respondents; all four dimensions and the whole scale were rated at a high level.

> "The level of deaf students’ perceptions towards MFTM as a whole is higher (mean = 75.63, s.d. = 10.38) namely the interest towards MFTM (mean = 23.14, s.d. = 3.26), self-confidence (mean = 22.71, s.d. = 3.49), persistence (mean = 15.14, s.d. = 2.68) and motivation (mean = 14.63, s.d. = 3.14)"
