`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-survey of 19 Cameroonian doctors (10 specialists, 9 general physicians; 82.6% response rate) on a 5-point Likert scale. The article reports infectious disease "scored the highest in the domain of infectious disease (mean ± SD, 4.6 ± 0.4)"; disaster, abuse and assault, and psychiatric and behavior disorder each had a mean of 2.8.

> "the Cameroonian healthcare professionals scored the highest in the domain of infectious disease (mean ± SD, 4.6 ± 0.4), followed by obstetrics and gynecology (4.2 ± 0.6) and hematology and oncology (4.2 ± 0.5)"
