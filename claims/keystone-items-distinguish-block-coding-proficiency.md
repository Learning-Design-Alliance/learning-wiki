## Evidence

### Yong Oh Lee 2026

Yong Oh Lee, Byeonghun Bang, Sejun Oh. (2026). LLM-Driven Rubric-Based Assessment of Algebraic Competence in Multi-Stage Block Coding Tasks with Design and Field Evaluation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.3390/app162110595](https://doi.org/10.3390/app162110595)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Field study analysis of keystone block-coding items ([Seg 3-2] and [Seg 5-1]) that directly assess applied programming competency among 42 middle school students. The difference was significant, "t = 3.42, p = 0.0045"; no effect size is printed.

> "Students who solved both items successfully (n = 12) attained significantly higher Rubric 3 scores (M = 85.0, SD = 23.97) compared to others (n = 29, M = 55.3, SD = 20.32), as shown in Figure 8b ( t = 3.42, p = 0.0045)."

### Yong Oh Lee 2026 (2)

Yong Oh Lee, Byeonghun Bang, Sejun Oh. (2026). LLM-Driven Rubric-Based Assessment of Algebraic Competence in Multi-Stage Block Coding Tasks with Design and Field Evaluation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.3390/app162110595](https://doi.org/10.3390/app162110595)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Field study applying a stricter combined criterion requiring both ≥70% overall scenario success and correct keystone solutions. Welch's t-test again confirmed the difference; the article reports the group means and SDs but no effect size.

> "the path-completed group (n = 10) achieved the highest Rubric 3 mean score ( M = 84.0, SD = 23.97), whereas the remainder (n = 32) averaged substantially lower (M = 55.3, SD = 20.32), as shown in Figure 8c."
