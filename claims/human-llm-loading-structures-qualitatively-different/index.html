Alona Strugatski 2026¶

Alona Strugatski, Licol Zeinfeld, Giora Alexandron. (2026). Do Assessment Instruments Measure the Same Thing for Humans and LLMs? A Latent Structure Analysis. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15630

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Qualitative visual analysis of EFA loading structures (Figure 1) for both instruments under the Kaiser solution; RMSEA values are printed for each group (e.g., chemistry human 0.019, LLM 0.045). The article states parallel-analysis solutions also revealed dissimilar item-factor structure.

"For instance for Chemistry, the third LLM factor, Lk3 (L: LLMs, K: Kaiser, Factor: 3), has loadings larger than 0.5 on Q5, Q16, and Q19. On the human side, these items are loaded on two different factors: Hk1 and Hk2."
