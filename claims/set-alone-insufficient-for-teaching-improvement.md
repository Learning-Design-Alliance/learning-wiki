### Wibbecke G 2015

Wibbecke G, Kahmann J, Pignotti T, Altenberger L, Kadmon M. (2015). Improving teaching on the basis of student evaluation: Integrative teaching consultation. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000944](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000944)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article's introduction, citing empirical literature, states that neither numerical evaluation data nor free-text comments provide a sound basis for improvement: "SET feedback does not lead to better evaluation results". The authors attribute this to prior studies rather than testing it themselves.

> "SET feedback does not lead to better evaluation results [13], [14] or to a perceived change in teaching quality [15]."
