Evidence¶

J. A. Anguera 2013¶

J. A. Anguera, J. Boccanfuso, J. L. Rintoul, O. Al-Hashimi, F. Faraji, J. Janowich, E. Kong, Y. Larraburo, C. Rolle, E. Johnston & A. Gazzaley. (2013). Video game training enhances cognitive control in older adults. Nature 501. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12486

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Experiment 1: cross-sectional assessment of 174 participants (about 30 per decade, ages 20–79) on a diagnostic NeuroRacer version after adaptive thresholding to ~80% accuracy on each component task. Costs increased linearly by decade (F(1,5) = 135.7, P < 0.00001; individually F(1,173) = 42.8, r = 0.45, P < 0.00001), described as "in a linear fashion".

"Costs across the lifespan (n 5 174) increased (that is, a more negative percentage) in a linear fashion when participants were grouped by decade ( F(1,5) 5 135.7, P , 0.00001) or analysed individually (F(1,173) 5 42.8, r 5 0.45, P , 0.00001"
