## Evidence

### Jayatilleke Achini Chinthika 2015

Jayatilleke Achini Chinthika, Yoshikawa Kayoko, Yasuoka Junko, Poudel Krishna C, Fernando Nilani, Jayatilleke Achala Upendra, Jimba Masamine. (2015). Training Sri Lankan public health midwives on intimate partner violence: a pre- and post-intervention study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-1674-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

McNemar's test comparison of pre- and post-intervention practices among 408 PHMs. Table 2 additionally shows advising tolerance fell from 117 (58.2%) to 5 (1.3%), and asking sufferers to seek help from family/friends rose from 29.4% to 77.1%.

> "At post-intervention, 96.5% (n = 387) of the PHMs discussed IPV with identified sufferers and suggested solutions; only 67.3% (n = 201) did so at the pre-intervention (p < 0.001)."
