# Training leads residents to initiate the simulated relative's first turn of speech about the bad news more often and earlier, during the pre-delivery phase

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After training, the simulated relative's first turn of speech regarding the bad news was more frequently initiated by residents (RR=19.17; P<0.001) and occurred more frequently during the pre-delivery phase (RR=6.68; P=0.008). [→ Merckaert I 2013](#merckaert-i-2013)

## Evidence

### Merckaert I 2013

Merckaert I, Liénard A, Libert Y, Bragard I, Delvaux N, Etienne A-M, Marchal S, Meunier J, Reynaert C, Slachmuylder J-L, Razavi D. (2013). Is it possible to improve the breaking bad news skills of residents when a relative is present? A randomised study. British Journal of Cancer. https://doi.org/10.1038/bjc.2013.615

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GEE analysis of blinded relative-inclusion coding in the randomised simulated triadic consultation study (n=95). The article reports the first turn about the bad news was "more frequently initiated by residents" and "occurred more frequently during the pre-delivery phase" after training.

> "after training, the simulated relative's first turn of speech regarding the bad news delivered was more frequently initiated by residents (RR=19.17; P<0.001) and occurred more frequently during the pre-delivery phase (RR=6.68; P=0.008) according to GEE analysis"

### Merckaert I 2013 (2)

Merckaert I, Liénard A, Libert Y, Bragard I, Delvaux N, Etienne A-M, Marchal S, Meunier J, Reynaert C, Slachmuylder J-L, Razavi D. (2013). Is it possible to improve the breaking bad news skills of residents when a relative is present? A randomised study. British Journal of Cancer. https://doi.org/10.1038/bjc.2013.615

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomised GEE analysis (Table 2, n=95): turns of speech by the simulated relative initiated by residents rose from a mean of 4 to 11 in the training group versus no change in the waiting list group, RR=2.74; P<0.001.

> "A significant increase in the number of turns of speech by the simulated relative initiated by trained residents was observed (RR=2.74; P<0.001)."
