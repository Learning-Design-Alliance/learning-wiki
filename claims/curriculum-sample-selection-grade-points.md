## Evidence

### de Visser M 2016

de Visser M, Fluit C, Fransen J, Latijnhouwers M, Cohen-Schotanus J, Laan R. (2016). The effect of curriculum sample selection for medical school. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-016-9681-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression on year-1 theoretical exam grade points (first attempt only) in the three-cohort study; the overall group difference was F(3,936) = 102.60, p ≤ 0.001.

> "Selected students had a higher GPA in year one than lottery-admitted students (6.9 vs 6.5, p = .001)."

### de Visser M 2016 (2)

de Visser M, Fluit C, Fransen J, Latijnhouwers M, Cohen-Schotanus J, Laan R. (2016). The effect of curriculum sample selection for medical school. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-016-9681-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression on years 2–3 grade points for the 2010–2011 cohorts (overall F(3,559) = 37.93, p ≤ 0.001); the pu-GPA-adjusted contrast with lottery admissions was not significant.

> "Lottery-admitted students had a lower average grade point in year 2 and 3 than selected students (7.0 vs 6.7, p = .001). Adjusted for pu-GPA, no differences remained (p = .12)."
