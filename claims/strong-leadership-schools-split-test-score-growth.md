`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Overview of the study's contrasting case design: interviews at four of 12 Chicago schools visited in 2013-14, selected because teachers reported strong principal instructional leadership on 5Essentials surveys from 2011-13. The article reports that "in half of the schools, student test scores were continuously growing, while in the other half, test scores were not improving."

> "Although teachers in each of these schools reported their principal was a strong instructional leader, in half of the schools, student test scores were continuously growing, while in the other half, test scores were not improving during the same time period."
