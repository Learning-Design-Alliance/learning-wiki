## Evidence

### Innab Adnan 2026

Innab Adnan, Hakamy Essa, Abdulgafor Nedaa, AlOtaibi Nora Ghalib. (2026). The influence of self-efficacy and satisfaction with e-learning on academic performance among nursing students: a multisite study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09236-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent samples t-test comparing male (n=73) and female (n=90) nursing students across three outcome measures. All three differences significant: satisfaction p=.002, self-efficacy p=.021, performance p=.004.

> "The results indicated that female nursing students had greater satisfaction with e-learning (M = 3.04, p<.01), greater self-efficacy (M = 27.3, SD = 4.69, p < .05), and higher perceived academic performance (M = 3.47, SD = 0.69) comparing to male nursing students."
