### Kron Frederick W. MD 2017

Kron Frederick W. MD, Fetters Michael D. MD, MPH, MA, Scerbo Mark W. PhD, White Casey B. PhD, Lypson Monica L. MD, MHPE, FACP, Padilla Miguel A. PhD, Gliva-McConvey Gayle A., Belfore Lee A. Patient Education and Counseling. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pec.2016.10.024

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on the global composite of four OSCE rating items (coefficient alpha = 0.82) in the multisite RCT, with blinded standardized patient instructors rating students several days after training. MPathic-VR students scored "significantly higher (M=.806, SD=.201), than CBL students (M=.752, SD=.198)".

> "This analysis indicated a main effect for module, F(1, 414)=6.09, p=.0140, η2 =0.0145. Thus, OSCE evaluators rated the communication skills of MPathic-VR-trained students significantly higher (M=.806, SD=.201), than CBL students (M=.752, SD=.198)."
