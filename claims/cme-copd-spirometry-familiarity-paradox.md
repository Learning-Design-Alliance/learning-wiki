## Evidence

### Drexel Carole 2011

Drexel Carole, Jacobson Anne, Hanania Nicola A, Whitfield Ben, Katz Jay, Sullivan Thomas. (2011). Measuring the impact of a live, case-based, multiformat, interactive continuing medical education program on improving clinician knowledge and competency in evidence-based COPD care. International Journal of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease. https://doi.org/10.2147/COPD.S18257

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-rated familiarity item in the matched survey comparison. The article reports participants' mean familiarity was lower ("5.9 versus 6.9, P = 0.014"), with more participants describing themselves as not at all or only somewhat familiar; no effect size is reported.

> "Participants reported a significantly lower mean level of familiarity than nonparticipants (5.9 versus 6.9, P = 0.014)."

### Drexel Carole 2011 (2)

Drexel Carole, Jacobson Anne, Hanania Nicola A, Whitfield Ben, Katz Jay, Sullivan Thomas. (2011). Measuring the impact of a live, case-based, multiformat, interactive continuing medical education program on improving clinician knowledge and competency in evidence-based COPD care. International Journal of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease. https://doi.org/10.2147/COPD.S18257

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Barrier-severity ratings on a 1-to-10 scale in the matched comparison. The article reports participants rated the spirometry barrier more severe ("5.4 versus 4.4, P = 0.029"), with 27% versus 12% calling it very significant; no effect size is reported.

> "participants assigned a higher mean severity score to "difficulty in obtaining spirometry results" than nonparticipants (5.4 versus 4.4, P = 0.029, Figure 2)."
