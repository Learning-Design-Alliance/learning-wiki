# Greater hours of engagement in doctoral teaching development are linearly associated with greater college teaching self-efficacy in every dimension except mastering subject knowledge

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Greater TD engagement (cumulative hours) is linearly associated with greater self-efficacy in every dimension except mastering subject knowledge. [→ Connolly 2018](#connolly-2018)
`q2 i?` Approximately 50 hours of TD engagement corresponds to a 0.16 SD increase in confidence on course planning and teaching methods. [→ Connolly 2018 (2)](#connolly-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Connolly 2018

Connolly, M. R., Lee, Y.-G., & Savoy, J. N. (2018). The Effects of Doctoral Teaching Development on Early-Career STEM Scholars' College Teaching Self-efficacy. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-02-0039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

OLS regression of the 2011 survey data (N reported per model 1243–1273) on engagement hours (range 0–400 hours; mean 39.4 hours). "Greater engagement in TD was linearly associated with greater self-efficacy in every dimension except mastering subject knowledge." Only coefficients and p-values are printed; no standardized effect size.

> "We found that greater engagement in TD was linearly associated with greater self-efficacy in every dimension except mastering subject knowledge, even after accounting for relevant covariates."

### Connolly 2018 (2)

Connolly, M. R., Lee, Y.-G., & Savoy, J. N. (2018). The Effects of Doctoral Teaching Development on Early-Career STEM Scholars' College Teaching Self-efficacy. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-02-0039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Numerical result from Table 1 OLS regression (Model 1 and 2): "0.16 SD increase in confidence on course planning and teaching methods" for approximately 50 hours, with coefficients 0.032 and 0.033 per 10 hours, both p < 0.001.

> "Spending approximately 50 hours on TD activities during doctoral training is associated with 0.16 SD increase in confidence on course planning and teaching methods (0.032, p < 0.001 in M1, and 0.033, p < 0.001 in M2, Table 1"

### Connolly 2018 (3)

Connolly, M. R., Lee, Y.-G., & Savoy, J. N. (2018). The Effects of Doctoral Teaching Development on Early-Career STEM Scholars' College Teaching Self-efficacy. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-02-0039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Supplementary interpretation of Models 3 and 5 in Table 1 (both coefficients 0.013, p < 0.05). The authors state the overlapping confidence intervals "reflect very small effect sizes, calling into question their practical significance."

> "the low slopes and largely overlapping 95% confidence intervals in creating learning environment and interacting with students (see M3 and M5 in Figure 1) reflect very small effect sizes, calling into question their practical significance, even though they are statistically significant."
