## Evidence

### Goodrich J. Marc 2017

Goodrich J. Marc, Lonigan Christopher J. (2017). Language-Independent and Language-Specific Aspects of Early Literacy: An Evaluation of the Common Underlying Proficiency Model. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000179

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of item-level data from 858 Spanish-speaking language-minority preschoolers. For blending, "The bifactor model provided significantly better fit to the data than did the two-factor model" (Table 2 fit statistics; bifactor diff test 772.32***).
