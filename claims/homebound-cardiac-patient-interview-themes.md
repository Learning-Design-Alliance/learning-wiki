## Evidence

### Feinberg JL 2017

Feinberg JL, Russell D, Mola A, Bowles KH, Lipman TH. (2017). Developing an Adapted Cardiac Rehabilitation Training for Home Care Clinicians. Journal of Cardiopulmonary Rehabilitation and Prevention. https://doi.org/10.1097/HCR.0000000000000228

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study: nine patients (mean age = 85 years) with HF, CAD, MI, or atrial fibrillation interviewed at home for an average of 45 minutes; transcripts analyzed with the constant comparative method by three authors. Patients "expressed confusion about their discharge instructions" and only 1 patient was familiar with CR services.

> "Patients often did not recognize feelings of discomfort as a symptom of their disease. However, when patients denied experiencing feelings of discomfort, they connected this lack of feelings with not having a heart problem."
