Kyriakoulis Konstantinos 2016¶

q3 · i? · review · r3

Pre-post evaluation of a one-day multifaceted EBM workshop for pharmacy students (n=37) in Japan. Overall EBM test scores improved from 11.4 (0.29) to 12.6 (0.22), P<0.0001, as reported in the review's synthesis.

"Another study conducted in Japan among pharmacy students was designed as one-day MI (students’ presentations, lectures, and small group discussions) and findings showed a significant improvement in the overall scores on the EBM test: 11.4 (0.29) vs. 12.6 (0.22), P<0.0001) [17]."
