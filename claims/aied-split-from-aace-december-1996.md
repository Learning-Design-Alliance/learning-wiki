`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

The memoir recounts that after a vigorous email debate in 1996, in which AACE asserted the AIED Society was formed by AACE and questioned the need for its executive committee, the committee "voted to split from AACE". At the split the new society had no members, since AACE held the contact details and funds of the old society.

> "The committee duly voted to split from AACE and to set up a new Society, Journal and Conference - to be called the International AIED Society, the International JAIED and the International AIED Conference."
