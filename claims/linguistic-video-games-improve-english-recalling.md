## Evidence

### Jalalian 2018

Jalalian, F. (2018). Study on the Role of Video Educational Games with a Linguistic Approach in English Language Education of the 2nd Grade High School Students. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 9(2). https://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.9n.2p.59

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study of 90 male students aged 16-17 in three groups of 30, analyzed by ANCOVA omitting the pretest covariate impact. The article reports "a significant difference exists between the mean scores of the three groups in English language posttest" (F = 16.244, p = 0.000); no effect size is printed.

> "F value of the impact of independent variable is 16.244, which is significant because its probability (0.000) is smaller than the significance level (0.05). This means that a significant difference exists between the mean scores of the three groups in English language posttest."
