Evidence¶

Muslem 2017¶

Muslem, A., Mustafa, F., Usman, B., & Rahman, A. (2017). The Application of Video Clips with Small Group and Individual Activities to Improve Young Learners' Speaking Performance. Teaching English with Technology, 17(4), 25-37. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1159095

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive comparison of mean pre-test/post-test scores per speaking aspect (rubric adapted from Brown, 2000), scored by two evaluators. SGA changes ranged from 1.00 (fluency) to 1.57 (vocabulary); IA changes ranged from 0.50 to 0.68. The article reports the SGA increases as significantly higher; no effect size was printed.

"Table 4 shows that the mean scores for the SGA gro up were higher than the means for those in the IA group in all 5 aspects of speaking skills measured namely vocabulary, fluency, grammar, pronunciation and comprehension."
