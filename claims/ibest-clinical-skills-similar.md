`q3 i?` No statistically significant difference in assessed clinical skills (CTS-R-Pain) between training groups (MD 0.17, 95% CI −0.2, 0.54), assessed in only 5 iBeST and 7 workshop participants. [→ Richmond Helen 2016](#richmond-helen-2016)

## Evidence

### Richmond Helen 2016

Richmond Helen, Hall Amanda M., Hansen Zara, Williamson Esther, Davies David, Lamb Sarah E. (2016). Using mixed methods evaluation to assess the feasibility of online clinical training in evidence based interventions: a case study of cognitive behavioural treatment for low back pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0683-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Clinical skills were assessed via audio-recorded treatment sessions using the 15-item CTS-R-Pain in only the 12 therapists who delivered BeST in practice (5 iBeST, 7 workshop); "no statistically significant difference between training groups". Assessment was by an unblinded rater, with 25% doubly assessed by a blinded rater.

> "Of these participants, we found no statistically significant difference between training groups (MD 0.17, 95 % CI −0.2, 0.54)."
