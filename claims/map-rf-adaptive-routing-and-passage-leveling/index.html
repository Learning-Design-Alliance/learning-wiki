NWEA 2024¶

NWEA. (2024). English MAP Reading Fluency Technical Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/english-map-reading-fluency-technical-report/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Design description from the Executive Summary's Test Design section. The report explains that students who pass the Sentence Reading Fluency measure continue to the Oral Reading Fluency track with passages, whereas those who do not pass continue to Foundational Skills.

"On the Adaptive Oral Reading form, students are routed to either Oral Reading Fluency or Foundational Skills based on their performance at the beginning of the assessment."

NWEA 2024 (2)¶

NWEA. (2024). English MAP Reading Fluency Technical Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/english-map-reading-fluency-technical-report/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Design rationale from Section 1.3.3 on gauging improvements in oral reading. The passage leveling adapts on comprehension rather than rate alone, so that a student who understands what they read aloud is challenged with passages at a higher level of text complexity.

"MAP Reading Fluency also adjusts the level of text complexity across multiple passages presented, adapting based on comprehension to find a maximum text level at which a student is showing understanding of what they read."
