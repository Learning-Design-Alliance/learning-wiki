Kaiyue Wu 2025¶

Kaiyue Wu, Xinsheng Wang, Linbo Sun, Jiayi Feng, Changping Song. (2025). Nurse Competency in Providing Spiritual Care and Associated Influencing Factors: A Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70192

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review reports, citing Seid et al. (2022), a decrease of 0.026 units per unit increase in age, and attributes the opposite direction to Manookian et al. (2023). These are second-hand findings narrated by the review.

"A study by Seid et al. (2022) has shown that for every unit increase in age, the corresponding level of spiritual care ability decreases by 0.026 units. However, Manookian et al. (2023) believe that with the increase of age, an individuals' cognition of spiritual care becomes clearer and more complete"
