`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Operative-time comparison by surgeon level in the retrospective multicenter cohort (Table 5). Residents generally required equal or longer operative duration across BCS alone, BCS with SLNB, and BCS with ALND; significant differences appeared only in the SLNB subgroup for MSL and WGL.

> "The median operative duration was significantly shorter for attending surgeons in the BCS with SLNB subgroup for both MSL (55 min, IQR 20, [18−180], vs 60 min, IQR 31, [37-98] p = 0.02) and WGL (90 min, IQR 40, [40−225] vs 100 min, IQR 30, [55−210], p = 0.02) techniques."
