### Amanda Matheson 2018

Amanda Matheson, Laura Wood, and Scott Franklin. (2018). Guided and Unguided Student Reflections. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.061](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.061)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Breadth-of-coding comparison between the 2014 and 2015 IMPRESS journals and the classroom GRFs. The printed counts show journals spanning more coding categories than GRFs, which the authors attribute to explicit prompts and classroom framing making GRF reflections "more constrained and less personal."

> "More than 80% of journals (in both sets) contained statements that were coded in four of the ﬁve ﬁve categories, and 60% had statements that encompassed all ﬁve categories. Conversely, only 50% of GRFs had statements in at least three of the four cat-egories, and only 4% had statements in all four."
