## Evidence

### Pendry Patricia 2020

Pendry Patricia, Carr Alexa M., Gee Nancy R., Vandagriff Jaymie L. (2020). Randomized Trial Examining Effects of Animal Assisted Intervention and Stress Related Symptoms on College Students’ Learning and Study Skills. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17061909

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison in the randomized trial showed at-risk students (above-the-mean on depression, anxiety, worry, or perceived stress indicators; N1 = 146) scored lower on all three LASSI composites; the article states "LASSI scores for students identified as at-risk were lower compared to those identified without the presence of mood related symptoms".

> "As expected, at baseline, LASSI scores for students identified as at-risk were lower compared to those identified without the presence of mood related symptoms on WILL, F(1, 281) = 47.45, p < 0.001, SELFREGULATION, F(1, 281) = 47.45, p < 0.001, and SKILL, F(1, 281) = 47.45, p < 0.001."
