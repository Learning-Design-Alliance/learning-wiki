`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Time-on-task comparison during training (Table 2) in the 24-resident RCT: the RCDP group had "more frequent feedback sessions" and longer instructor contact, while repeated-operation counts did not differ significantly. The authors interpret the feedback difference as part of RCDP's feedback mechanism rather than training intensity.

> "the RCDP group reported more frequent feedback sessions (3.58 ± 1.31 vs. 2.67 ± 0.65, P = 0.020) and longer instructor contact minutes (4.08 ± 1.16 vs. 3.75 ± 0.54, P = 0.020) compared to the TTM group. However, there was no significant difference in the number of repeated operations between the two groups (2.58 ± 0.67 vs. 2.67 ± 0.65, P = 0.814)"
