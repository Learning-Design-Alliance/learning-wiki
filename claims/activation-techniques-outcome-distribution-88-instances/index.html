Evidence¶

Courtney Hattan 2023¶

Courtney Hattan, Patricia A. Alexander, and Sarah M. Lupo. (2023). Leveraging What Students Know to Make Sense of Texts: What the Research Says About Prior Knowledge Activation. Review of Educational Research. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Leveraging+What+Students+Know+to+Make+Sense+of+Texts+Prior+Knowledge+Activation

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review's frequency coding of all included studies using a first-cycle, magnitude-coding procedure, with interrater agreement of 98% on 13 (24%) of articles. From the 88 techniques, "30 unique knowledge activation techniques were identified" and grouped inductively into eight categories.

"Across the 54 articles and 68 studies (14 articles included 2 studies), 88 prior knowledge activation techniques were investigated."

Courtney Hattan 2023 (2)¶

Courtney Hattan, Patricia A. Alexander, and Sarah M. Lupo. (2023). Leveraging What Students Know to Make Sense of Texts: What the Research Says About Prior Knowledge Activation. Review of Educational Research. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Leveraging+What+Students+Know+to+Make+Sense+of+Texts+Prior+Knowledge+Activation

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review's outcome coding of each technique against the study's other conditions, coding positive, negative, neutral, or mixed effects. Eight cases reported no significant outcomes, eight mixed outcomes, and six were qualitative or exploratory studies without quantitative assessment. Effect sizes were reported for only 20 of the 88 instances.

"For the 88 documented instances, 51 resulted in positive effects on readers' learning or comprehension, whereas 15 demonstrated negative outcomes."

Courtney Hattan 2023 (3)¶

Courtney Hattan, Patricia A. Alexander, and Sarah M. Lupo. (2023). Leveraging What Students Know to Make Sense of Texts: What the Research Says About Prior Knowledge Activation. Review of Educational Research. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Leveraging+What+Students+Know+to+Make+Sense+of+Texts+Prior+Knowledge+Activation

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review's timing coding, developed a priori from Kintsch's Construction-Integration Model, found activation was initiated solely during reading in 19 techniques, during-reading scaffolds added in 28, post-reading activation alongside before or during reading in 17, and solely after reading in only one instance.

"47 of the 88 techniques solely addressed knowledge activation before reading, while 65 of the techniques included a prereading prompt, even if it was combined with during or after reading activation."
