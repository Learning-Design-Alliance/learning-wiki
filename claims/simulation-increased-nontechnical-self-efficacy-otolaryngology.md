## Evidence

### Neevel Andrew J. MD 2026

Neevel Andrew J. MD, Ramazani Fatemeh MD, Vance Kaitlin NP, Leginza Marie NP, Casper Keith A. MD, Thorne Marc C. MD, MPH, Kupfer Robbi A. MD. (2026). Postsurgical Otolaryngology Emergencies: A Simulation to Improve Multidisciplinary Patient Care During Rare, Critical Situations. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11612

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-/postsimulation survey study of 62 participants (51 nurses, 6 medical students, 5 otolaryngology residents) using paired t tests. The mean overall nontechnical score rose from 1.8 (95% CI: 1.8-1.9) to 2.1 (95% CI: 2.0-2.1), a "0.3 points on a 4-point scale" increase, P < .001.

> "Mean nontechnical self-efficacy scores (assessing situational awareness, decision-making, communication/teamwork, and leadership) increased by 0.3 points on a 4-point scale postsimulation (P < .001)."
