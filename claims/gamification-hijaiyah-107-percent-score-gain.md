## Evidence

### Wisnu Uriawan 2026

Wisnu Uriawan, Denis Firmansyah, Devi Mulyana, Dika Haekal Firza Pratama, Adly Juliarta Lerian, Fajar Satria Wiguna. (2026). Gamification-Based Learning Method for Hijaiyah Letters. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.03851](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.03851)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A pre-test/post-test experimental design involving 50 elementary school students (grades 1–3, aged 6–9 years) over a 4-week intervention period. The mean score increased significantly from a baseline of 42.8 to 88.6, with the SD decreasing from 12.4 to 8.1.

> "The mean score increased significantly from a baseline of 42.8 to 88.6 (p <0.001). Notably, the Standard Deviation (SD) decreased from 12.4 in the pre-test to 8.1 in the post-test."

### Wisnu Uriawan 2026 (2)

Wisnu Uriawan, Denis Firmansyah, Devi Mulyana, Dika Haekal Firza Pratama, Adly Juliarta Lerian, Fajar Satria Wiguna. (2026). Gamification-Based Learning Method for Hijaiyah Letters. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.03851](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.03851)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Effect size computed for the same pre-test/post-test study (N = 50). The article reports Cohen's d = 4.87, described as a "very largepractical effect size", exceeding the d = 0.8 to 1.8 range it cites for recent gamification studies.

> "The calculated Cohen's d value of 4.87 indicates a very largepractical effect size. This figure far exceeds the typical effect sizes reported in recent gamification studies for Arabic/Hijaiyahlearning, which generally range betweend= 0.8and1.8"
