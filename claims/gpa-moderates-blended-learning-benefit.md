Milic Natasa M., Trajkovic Goran Z., Bukumiric Zoran M., Cirkovic Andja, Nikolic Ivan M., Milin Jelena S., Milic Nikola V., Savic Marko D., Corac Aleksandar M., Marinkovic Jelena M., Stanisavljevic Dejana M. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sensitivity analysis dividing the 440-student sample into high (≥8) and low (<8) GPA strata to explore how GPA relates to learning method; only descriptive means and p-values were printed, with no effect size.

> "There was a difference in the mean final scores for students with a low GPA between the on-site and blended learning groups (81.31±8.46 vs. 85.50±6.18, p = 0.030), but not for students with a high GPA (89.87±6.32 vs. 90.58±6.29, p = 0.428)."
