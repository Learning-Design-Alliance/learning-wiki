Evidence¶

Schleepen TMJ 2016¶

Schleepen TMJ, Van Mier HI, De Smedt B (2016) The Contribution of Numerical Magnitude Comparison and Phonological Processing to Individual Differences in Fourth Graders' Multiplication Fact Ability. PLoS ONE 11(6): e0158335. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0158335

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Two additional hierarchical regressions (N = 63) entered the comparison tasks in separate steps. Symbolic added 11.2% (F(1,57) = 12.2, p < .01); nonsymbolic added 3.7% (F(1,57) = 4.0, p = .049), so symbolic comparison contributed more to individual differences.

"This analysis showed that symbolic magnitude comparison explained an additional 11.2% of the variance over and above nonsymbolic magnitude comparison, which was statistically significant, F(1,57) = 12.2, p < .01."
