## Evidence

### Debrenti 2015

Debrenti, E. (2015). Visual Representations in Mathematics Teaching: An Experiment with Students. Acta Didactica Napocensia, 8(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1064387

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive timing data from the same 57-student experiment. The card group's longer average reflects that all 16 students solved the problem, whereas other groups' averages include only successful solvers.

> "Students from group II took a bit longer, almost 44 minutes (dispersion almost 20 minutes), however everyone in this group solved the problem, thus it reflects the avarage time for the whole group."
