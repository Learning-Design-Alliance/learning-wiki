## Evidence

### Kang Sung-Yoon 2018

Kang Sung-Yoon, Kim Sae-Hoon, Kwon Yong-Eun, Kim Tae-Bum, Park Hye-Kyung, Park Heung-Woo, Chang Yoon-Seok, Jee Young-Koo, Moon Hee-Bom, Min Kyung-Up, Cho Sang-Heon. (2018). The virtual asthma guideline e-learning program: learning effectiveness and user satisfaction. The Korean Journal of Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.3904/kjim.2017.017

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post questionnaire study of 158 physicians-in-training at six tertiary hospitals who used VLCAM for 2 weeks; paired t test comparing mean total awareness scores before and after. The mean total score rose from 80.3 to 85.1, "significant improvements were observed" at p < 0.001. No effect size is printed.

> "After the 2-week program, significant improvements were observed in the mean total score from 80.3 to 85.1 out of 100 (p < 0.001)."
