## Evidence

### Murphy 2020

Murphy, V. A., Arndt, H., Briggs Baffoe-Djan, J., Chalmers, H., Macaro, E., Rose, H., Vanderplank, R., & Woore, R. (2020). Foreign language learning and its impact on wider academic outcomes: A rapid evidence assessment. University of Oxford Department of Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED612981

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesis finding from the executive summary covering the updated Fitzpatrick et al (2018) and Harris and Ó'Duibhir (2011) seed reviews. The review states FonF effectiveness "very much depends on characteristics of both the teacher ... as well as the proficiency of the learner".

> "there are numerous studies indicating that within this meaning-oriented approach there is scope for careful attention to specific linguistics features - often referred to as Focus on Form (FonF). These approaches need to be strategically employed"
