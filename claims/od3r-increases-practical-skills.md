## Evidence

### Yunita Arian Sani Anwar 2018

Yunita Arian Sani Anwar, Senam, & Endang W Laksono. (2018). Meaningful Biochemistry Learning Using the Orientation-Decision-Do-Discuss-Reflect (OD3R) Method. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 17-30. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.1132a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same one-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment, practical skills were measured with a rubric of six categories (procedural, manipulative, observation, drawing, interpreting, reporting). The article reports the average rose from 49.5 to 74, described as a significant increase; no effect size is printed.

> "Before it, average student practice skill is in average at 49.5. After the implementation, the practice skills increased significantly by 74. Each category of practice skills improved after the OD3R method implementation"
