Ribeiro MAL 2026¶

Ribeiro MAL, Hohenfeld DWA, Tavares TMA, Araújo-Neto FC, Mesquita AR, Brito GC, Lyra Jr DP. (2026). Characterization of the learning environment for teaching pharmaceutical care: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08876-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

Table 4 of the review lists the theoretical reference adopted by each included study, including Vygotsky's sociocultural theory, constructivist theory, social constructivism, relational pedagogy, and Kolb's experiential learning theory; several studies reported no theoretical reference.

"The included studies based their learning environment on different learning theories, which highlights a transition from traditional models of teaching, teacher-centered, to student-centered pedagogical approaches"

Ribeiro MAL 2026 (2)¶

Ribeiro MAL, Hohenfeld DWA, Tavares TMA, Araújo-Neto FC, Mesquita AR, Brito GC, Lyra Jr DP. (2026). Characterization of the learning environment for teaching pharmaceutical care: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08876-7

q1 · i? · review · r2

The review's discussion asserts, without new data, that "No theory isolated is sufficient to explain the complexity of learning" and that complementary use of theories can facilitate curricular changes and training of pharmacists who are caregivers.

"No theory isolated is sufficient to explain the complexity of learning, therefore it is necessary to consider them in a complementary way [50, 51]."
