## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Sense of belonging did not differ significantly across androgyny discrepancy groups, while gender stigma consciousness did. [→ Li 2026](#li-2026)

## Evidence

### Li 2026

Li, Y., & Leibnitz, N. (2026). Gender expression appraisals in introductory physics courses: A cross-institutional replication. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA comparing belonging and stigma consciousness across androgyny discrepancy groups (Fig. 6) in the 1845-student survey. Stigma consciousness differed significantly, F(2, 1684) = 52.30, p < 0.001, with the positive group higher than zero (p < 0.001) and negative (p = 0.008) groups.

> "A one-way ANOVA indicated no significant group differences in sense of belonging, F(2, 1676) = 1.11, p = 0.330."
