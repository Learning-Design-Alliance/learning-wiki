# Undergraduate students' self-rated perceived level of competence rose from a median of 2 to 4 on a 5-point scale from pre-test to post-test in one uncontrolled pre-post study of VR pediatric emergency training, for both scenarios

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After VR sessions, students' perceived competence in managing pediatric status epilepticus and anaphylaxis scenarios increased from a median of 2 (IQR 1–3) to 4 (IQR 3–4) on a 5-point Likert scale (p < 0.001, Wilcoxon test for paired samples). [→ Savino Sandro 2023](#savino-sandro-2023)

## Evidence

### Savino Sandro 2023

Savino Sandro, Mormando Giulia, Saia Giorgia, Da Dalt Liviana, Chang Todd P., Bressan Silvia. (2023). SIMPEDVR: using VR in teaching pediatric emergencies to undergraduate students—a pilot study. European Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-023-05254-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot pre-post study of 45 last-year medical and nursing students at the University of Padova, each managing two pediatric emergency VR scenarios. Self-rated competence was collected before and after via anonymous identifier codes; the article reports "The perceived level of competence increased from 2 (IQR 1–3) to 4 (IQR 3–4)" with p < 0.001. No effect size is printed.

> "The perceived level of competence increased from 2 (IQR 1–3) to 4 (IQR 3–4) on a 5-point Likert scale, for both scenarios (p < 0.001, Wilcoxon test for paired samples)."
