Post-activity survey item on willingness to recommend the tool. In Zaragoza nearly 94% rated the tool very useful (53.59%) or useful (41.15%); in Huesca nearly 93% gave positive ratings. These are descriptive self-report proportions.

> "The results showed a strong consensus across both campuses: 97.62% of students in Huesca and 94.82% in Zaragoza answered affirmatively, with only a small minority responding neutrally (2.38% in Huesca and 5.18% in Zaragoza)."
