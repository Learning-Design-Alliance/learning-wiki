## Evidence

### Hardaway 2020

Hardaway, C. R., Sterrett-Hong, E. M., De Genna, N. M., & Cornelius, M. D. (2020). The Role of Cognitive Stimulation in the Home and Maternal Responses to Low Grades in Low-Income African American Adolescents’ Academic Achievement. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-020-01217-x

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis (Model 2) of 226 low-income African American mother–child pairs assessed at ages 14 and 16, controlling age 14 achievement and maternal education. The article reports that "age 14 cognitive stimulation in the home remained positively related to age 16 academic achievement (β = 0.14, p < 0.05)"; β = 0.14 was the strongest involvement predictor that survived the prior-achievement control.
