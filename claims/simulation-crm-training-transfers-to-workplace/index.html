Evidence¶

Boet Sylvain 2014¶

Boet Sylvain, Bould M. Dylan, Fung Lillia, Qosa Haytham, Perrier Laure, Tavares Walter, Reeves Scott, Tricco Andrea C. (2014). Transfer of learning and patient outcome in simulated crisis resource management: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Anaesthesia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4028539/

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review synthesis of four studies measuring transfer of simulation-based CRM learning into the clinical setting (Kirkpatrick Level 3), reporting that "simulation-enhanced CRM training was found significantly more effective than no intervention or didactic teaching" in three of them. No meta-analysis was performed because of heterogeneity of study design and outcome measures; a narrative summary was conducted instead.

"Four studies measured transfer of simulation-based CRM learning into the clinical setting (Kirkpatrick Level 3). In three of these studies, simulation-enhanced CRM training was found significantly more effective than no intervention or didactic teaching."
