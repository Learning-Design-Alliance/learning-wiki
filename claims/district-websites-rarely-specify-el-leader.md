# District websites rarely specify which department or administrator oversees services for English Learners

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a national protocol-driven search of district websites, the responsible department or administrator for English Learner services was rarely specified, requiring searches across several departments. [→ Palacios 2022](#palacios-2022)

## Evidence

### Palacios 2022

Palacios, M., Kibler, A., Farren Burho, J., Garrett Sandstead, M., Wiger, S., & Woodruff, K. (2022). Who is in charge of English Learner services? Lessons learned from a national co-teaching and collaboration study. WestEd: National Research & Development Center to Improve Education for Secondary English Learners. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED625797

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Protocol-driven website review of 1,934 districts sampled via the Common Core of Data. The review found that "District websites rarely specified which department or administrator oversaw services for English Learners"; contacts were eventually found in departments such as Curriculum and Instruction, ESL, Federal Programs, and Multilingual Services.

> "District websites rarely specified which department or administrator oversaw services for English Learners. In order to locate the relevant person in charge of English Learner services, we often had to look through the webpages of several different district departments."
