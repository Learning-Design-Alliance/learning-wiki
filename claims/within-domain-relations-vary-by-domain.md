## Evidence

### Goetz et al. 2007

Thomas Goetz, Anne C. Frenzel, Reinhard Pekrun, Nathan C. Hall, and Oliver Lüdtke. (2007). Between- and Within-Domain Relations of Students' Academic Emotions. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.715

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel three-level analysis of within-domain homogeneity of five emotion scales in each of four domains (542 students). Printed h(e) values: Grade 8 mathematics .56, physics .49, German .21, English .38; Grade 11 mathematics .59, physics .49, German .16, English .40.

> "In both samples, the strongest within-domain relations were found for mathematics, followed by physics, English, and German. Within-domain relations in German were by far the weakest."
