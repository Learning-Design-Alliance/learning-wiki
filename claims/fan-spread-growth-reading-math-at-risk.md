## Evidence

### Erbeli 2021

Erbeli, F., Shi, Q., Campbell, A. R., Hart, S. A., & Woltering, S. (2021). Developmental Dynamics Between Reading and Math in Elementary School. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.13004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate dual change score models fit separately to reading and math W-scores (grades 1–4, residualized on age). Reading showed "a fan-spread growth pattern" with rR0,1 = 0.644 and βR = −0.406; math showed parallel directionality with rM0,1 = 0.336 and βM = −0.177.

> "The positive correlation between the intercept and slope (rR0,1 = 0.644) indicated a fan-spread growth pattern, where, on average, children who were below the mean in the first grade grew more slowly across the span of first through fourth grade than children who were above the mean."
