International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315604

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Conclusion drawn from the cross-sectional spline analyses of the 2019 YRBSS sample, in which optimal grades were associated with 7–9 h/night of sleep and 5–7 days/week of activity, ranges the discussion describes as deviating slightly from national recommendations.

> "adherence to physical activity and sleep-specific public health guidelines may not be necessary for optimal academic achievement, but rather, amounts slightly below the recommendations may be sufficient."
