## Evidence

### Amin et al. 2018

Bunga D. Amin, Helmi Abdullah and Jasruddin D. Malago. (2018). Sketch strategy of knowledge in physics learning and its influence on metacognitive. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2017.3293

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results analysis of the level-1 PPM test, where both classes averaged 3 with 100% above 75%. The authors state level-1 "only measured the ability to remember and use" concepts, formulas and procedures; no effect size is printed.

> "Therefore, the effect of the use of sketch knowledge strategy and variables target strategy to complete the test items PPM level -1 does not have a significant difference in students."

### Amin et al. 2018 (2)

Bunga D. Amin, Helmi Abdullah and Jasruddin D. Malago. (2018). Sketch strategy of knowledge in physics learning and its influence on metacognitive. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2017.3293

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results analysis of Table 1 scores: class-A averaged 3.65 (level-2) and 5.65 (level-3) versus 2.75 and 2.375 for class-B. The authors attribute the advantage to the deeper thought required to link concepts across schemes; no per-level effect size is printed.

> "From the research results, it was obtained that teaching the concept using sketch knowledge strategy is better than the variable target strategy, especially in solving problems in PPM level -2 and level -3."
