## Evidence

### Bidault-Jourdainne Valeska 2024

Bidault-Jourdainne Valeska, Breaud Jean, Ali Liza, Talon Isabelle, Becmeur François, Ghoneimi Alaa El, Peycelon Matthieu. (2024). Contribution of simulation in paediatric laparoscopic surgery: improving suturing skills before performing a laparoscopic procedure in children. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06346-6

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of the 43-trainee cohort relating Peg Transfer time at the start of training to 5-mm knot outcomes. The article reports a "moderate correlation, ρ = 0.58" in session 1 and a "strong correlation, ρ = 0.78" in session 2; OSATS at session 2 was inversely correlated (ρ = -0.61, p < 10−5).

> "there was a significant correlation between the knot completion time and PT time both at the end of session #1 (moderate correlation, ρ = 0.58, 95%CI [0.31;0.76], p < 0.0002) and session 2 (strong correlation, ρ = 0.78, 95%CI [0.62;0.87], p < 10− 7)"

### Bidault-Jourdainne Valeska 2024 (2)

Bidault-Jourdainne Valeska, Breaud Jean, Ali Liza, Talon Isabelle, Becmeur François, Ghoneimi Alaa El, Peycelon Matthieu. (2024). Contribution of simulation in paediatric laparoscopic surgery: improving suturing skills before performing a laparoscopic procedure in children. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06346-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same cohort, 3-mm instrument results (Table 2): correlations with PT time were non-significant (e.g. OSATS session 2 ρ = 0.05, p = 0.75). The article reports "no significant correlation between OSATS score or knot completion time and PT time"; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant correlation between OSATS score or knot completion time and PT time, or between OSATS scores at the end of the two sessions."
