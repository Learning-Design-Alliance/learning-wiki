### McLin 2013

McLin, K. M., Cominsky, L. R., Metevier, A. J., Coble, K., Bailey, J. M. (2013). Using the Big Ideas in Cosmology to Teach College Students. 4th Fermi Symposium, Monterey, CA. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1303.1768](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1303.1768)

Open-ended questionnaires administered prior to instruction with a total of 1270 students participating, part of the project's published survey study. The finding reports students' cosmological knowledge: "Fewer than a third of the students thought that the age of the Universe was measured in billions of years."
