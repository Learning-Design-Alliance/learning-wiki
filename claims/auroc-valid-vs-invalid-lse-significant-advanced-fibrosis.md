## Evidence

### Armstrong M J 2013

Armstrong M J, Corbett C, Hodson J, Marwah N, Parker R, Houlihan D D, Rowe I A, Hazlehurst J M, Brown R, Hübscher S G, Mutimer D. (2013). Operator training requirements and diagnostic accuracy of Fibroscan in routine clinical practice. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2012-131640

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ROC analysis in the 153-patient biopsy subgroup comparing areas under the curve for valid versus invalid LSEs in predicting significant fibrosis (Ishak 3–6); the article reports "0.83 vs 0.66; p=0.048".

> "The AUROC for significant fibrosis (Ishak 3–6) was significantly greater with a valid LSE than an invalid LSE (0.83 vs 0.66; p=0.048)."

### Armstrong M J 2013 (2)

Armstrong M J, Corbett C, Hodson J, Marwah N, Parker R, Houlihan D D, Rowe I A, Hazlehurst J M, Brown R, Hübscher S G, Mutimer D. (2013). Operator training requirements and diagnostic accuracy of Fibroscan in routine clinical practice. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2012-131640

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same ROC analysis for advanced fibrosis (Ishak 5–6) found "0.87 vs 0.76; p=0.361", which the article reports as not significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in the AUROC for advanced fibrosis (Ishak 5–6) between a valid LSE and an invalid LSE (0.87 vs 0.76; p=0.361)."
