## Evidence

### Costas Efthimiou 2011

Costas Efthimiou, Dan Maronde, Tim McGreevy, Enrique del Barco, Stefanie McCole. (2011). Implementing Elements of The Physics Suite at a Large Metropolitan Research University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1103.3729](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1103.3729)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Volunteer student interviews conducted in focus group settings after the Spring 2008 semester (28 students: 8 from Physics I, 20 from Physics II) and Spring 2009, without monetary or grade compensation. The majority gave positive feedback and "many students claimed to have had preconceptions modified" by RTP experiments.

> "On major items, such as 'Do you like the format?', 'Have the experiments changed misconceptions?', and 'Do the experiments help you learn physics?', the majority of students gave positive feedback."
