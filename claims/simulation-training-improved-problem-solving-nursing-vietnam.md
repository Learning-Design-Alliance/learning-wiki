## Evidence

### Oanh Tran Thi Hoang 2024

Oanh Tran Thi Hoang, Thuy Luu Thi, Huyen Ngo Thi Thu. (2024). The effect of simulation-based training on problem-solving skills, critical thinking skills, and self-efficacy among nursing students in Vietnam: a before-and-after study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2024.21.24

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with 173 second-year nursing students at a Vietnamese university, analyzed with paired-sample t-tests. The posttest PSI mean of 127.24±12.11 was significantly lower than the pretest 131.42±16.95, "with a statistically significant difference (t172=2.55, P=0.011)"; lower PSI scores indicate better problem-solving skills.

> "the average problem-solving score (127.24±12.11) was lower than before (131.42±16.95), with a statistically significant difference (t172=2.55, P=0.011)"
