Evidence¶

Elise Lavoué 2019¶

Elise Lavoué, Maedeh Kazemitabar, Tenzin Doleck, Susanne P. Lajoie, Rubiela Carrillo, et al.. (2019). Towards emotion awareness tools to support emotion and appraisal regulation in academic contexts. Educational Technology Research and Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09688-x

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Quantitative descriptive analysis of retrospective interviews with university students about their self-reported emotion grids. The analysis coded "the focus of their emotions (retrospective, activity, or prospective)" plus attributed causes and value/control appraisals. No effect sizes or test statistics are printed in the provided material.

"Student transcripts were analyzed based on the focus of their emotions (retrospective, activity, or prospective), the causes they attribute to their emotions (agent or external circumstances) and how they appraised the situation in which they experienced the emotions (value and control)."
