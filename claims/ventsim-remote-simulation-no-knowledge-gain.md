## Evidence

### Ippolito M. 2022

Ippolito M., Simone B., Safadi S., Spinuzza E., Catania T., Ingoglia G., Milazzo M., Raineri S.M., Giarratano A., Gregoretti C., Cortegiani A. (2022). Effectiveness of a remote simulation training in mechanical ventilation among trainees. Pulmonology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pulmoe.2022.05.007

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 183 residents randomized by strata based on previous ICU rotation, analyzed by intention to treat. The primary outcome comparison showed "28.6 ± 8.6 vs. 27.6 ± 9; P = 0.49", i.e. no statistically significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "The overall number of correct answers was similar between the VentSim© group and control group (28.6 ± 8.6 vs. 27.6 ± 9; P = 0.49)."
