## Evidence

### Kelly Caitlin 2026

Kelly Caitlin, Bell Emily, Brown Megan E. L., Stelling Heidi. (2026). Gynaecology Teaching Associates in Medical Education—A Scoping Review. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70460

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Descriptive findings of the scoping review of 83 sources: 44 from North America, 33 from Europe, 9 Oceania, 2 Asia; publication dates 1975–2023, with about 70% published from 2000 onwards. The review reports the absence of direct patient perspectives as striking.

> "Across included studies, the perspectives of GTAs, students and faculty were variably represented, with GTA and student voices more prominent than those of educators and the patient voice notably absent."
