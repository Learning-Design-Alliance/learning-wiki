## Evidence

### Wick Katharina 2018

Wick Katharina, Faude Oliver, Manes Susanne, Zahner Lukas, Donath Lars. (2018). I Can Stand Learning: A Controlled Pilot Intervention Study on the Effects of Increased Standing Time on Cognitive Function in Primary School Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15020356

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Accelerometer-based comparison in the same two-class pilot. Table 2 reports sitting time lesson difference 12.7 min (95% CI 1.6; 23.9), t(36) = 2.32, p = 0.03, d = 0.75, with IG 172.1 min vs CG 184.9 min per day.

> "Compared to the CG, the IG sat 13 min less per day (65 min per week). Effect sizes ranged between 0.75 and 1.04."
