# Students in an Astro 101 mega-course using Lecture-Tutorials achieved an average normalized gain of <g> = 0.39 on the Star Properties Concept Inventory, higher than the <g> = 0.25 calculated for the SPCI validation-study sample

## Evidence

### Colin S. Wallace and Edward E. Prather 2018

Colin S. Wallace and Edward E. Prather. (2018). Lecture-Tutorials in Introductory Astronomy. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.00452](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.00452)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post concept-inventory study of the spring 2011 Astro 101 mega-course at the University of Arizona; matched pre- and post-instruction SPCI responses from N = 307 students yielded "an average normalized gain of <g> = 0.39", within Hake's medium-gain region. The comparison sample's <g> = 0.25 was calculated by the authors from Bailey et al.'s printed pre/post averages.

> "On the SPCI, the spring 2011 mega-course achieved an average normalized gain of <g> = 0.39 with a standard deviation of σ<g> = 0.19 from N = 307 matched pre- and post-instruction responses."
