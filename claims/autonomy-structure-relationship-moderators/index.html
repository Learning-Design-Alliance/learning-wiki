Moderation analyses in the meta-analysis tested data collection method (teacher report, student report, observation), school level, school subject, and cultural individualism as moderators of the autonomy support–structure correlation. The article reports the relationship "was moderated by the data collec- tion method and school level" and appears universal across contexts.
