Jonna Malmberg, Eetu Haataja, Tapio Seppänen, Sanna Järvelä. (2019). Are we together or not? International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning 14:467–490. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-019-09311-4

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis contrasted individual EDA peak counts (detected at minimum amplitude 0.05 μS, range 118–601, M = 471) with monitoring frequency for 12 students. The article reports "a significant correlation ( r =. 6 6 3 ,p < .005) between the students ' monitoring activities and EDA peaks". r = .663 exceeds .37, coded at the top of the scale.

> "The results show a significant correlation ( r =. 6 6 3 ,p < .005) between the students ' monitoring activities and EDA peaks, such that the students who were most active in monitoring also had the most EDA peaks."
