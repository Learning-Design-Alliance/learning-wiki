Evidence¶

Frank Reichert 2020¶

Frank Reichert, Nancy W. Y. Law, Gary K. W. Wong, Jimmy de la Torre. (2020). Exploring the structure of digital literacy competence assessed with authentic software applications. Education Tech Research Dev, 68, 2991–3013. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09825-x

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Reliability analysis of the bifactor model in Results (Table 6): general DL ω = .93, ωH = .58; "only 62.2% of the reliable variance in the total scores was due to general DL"; online information seeking was less reliably measured (ω = .59).

"The ratio of ω H to ω also shows that only 62.2% of the reliable variance in the total scores was due to general DL, aiding the interpretation of DL as a multidimensional (bifactor) construct."
