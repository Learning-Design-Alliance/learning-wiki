## Evidence

### Kreiter Clarence D 2013

Kreiter Clarence D. (2013). A measurement perspective on affirmative action in U.S. medical education. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v18i0.20531

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review summarizes meta-analytic and validity-generalization research on admissions measures, reporting that "undergraduate GPA and MCAT are clearly shown to be useful predictors" of medical school performance. No effect size is printed in the quoted summary.

> "when validity generalization and meta-analytic methods are used to correct for criterion unreliability and other attenuating influences, undergraduate GPA and MCAT are clearly shown to be useful predictors of both intellectual and clinical performance throughout the medical school years"
