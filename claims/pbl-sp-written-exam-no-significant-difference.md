## Evidence

### Wang An-Yu 2025

Wang An-Yu, Zhao Hao Cheng, Song Yue Wei, Xiong Bing Lang, Guo Zi Yan, Sun Xu Dong, Pan Ya Xin, Sun Wei Dong. (2025). Application of PBL in combination with the SP teaching method in the clinical teaching of orthopedics and traumatology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07658-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Final written exam (60% of total score, same closed-book paper for both groups) compared by independent t test: study 82.63 ± 4.49 versus control 80.66 ± 4.49 (t = -1.692, P = 0.096). The article printed no effect size.

> "Although the research group had a higher average score on the written exam, the difference was not statistically significant."
