## Evidence

### Emilio Ferrer 2023

Emilio Ferrer, Bennett A. Shaywitz, John M. Holahan and Sally E. Shaywitz. (2023). Early reading at first grade predicts adult reading at age 42 in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00205-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression of the adult ART-2 accuracy outcome onto the growth curve intercept in an epidemiologic longitudinal sample (N Typical = 246, N Dyslexic = 66). The article reports "β0 = 0.980 and 0.473, for typical and dyslexic readers, respectively" (standardized coefficients; p = 0.000).

> "the intercept from the growth curve model (reading level at 1 st grade) was predictive of accuracy scores in adulthood for both typical and dyslexic groups, although more strongly for typical readers (β0 = 0.980 and 0.473"
