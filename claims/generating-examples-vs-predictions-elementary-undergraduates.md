## Evidence

### Garvin Brod 2021

Garvin Brod. (2021). How Can We Make Active Learning Work in K–12 Education? Considering Prerequisites for a Successful Construction of Understanding. Psychological Science in the Public Interest. https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100621997376

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Experimental study (Breitwieser & Brod, 2021) in which late-elementary children and undergraduates studied science facts via generating a prediction or an example: "elementary-school children learned less when they generated examples than when they generated predictions." The difference was related to children's analogical reasoning abilities.

> "results showed that elementary-school children learned less when they generated examples than when they generated predictions, whereas undergraduates learned comparably well in both conditions (Breitwieser & Brod, 2021)"
