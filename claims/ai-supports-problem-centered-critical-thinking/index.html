Evidence¶

Jinhee Kim 2026¶

Jinhee Kim, Xi Lin, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick. (2026). Adult learners' perspectives of AI applications in supporting andragogy. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10621-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Problem-centered theme from the 20-participant interviews: AI tools remind learners of relevant background knowledge and diagnose misconceptions (P13); one said "AI helps me remember what I've learned before starting something new" (P20).

"ALs perceive that AI can support problem-centered learning by activating background knowledge and providing data-driven insights that help them tackle authentic tasks."

Jinhee Kim 2026 (2)¶

Jinhee Kim, Xi Lin, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick. (2026). Adult learners' perspectives of AI applications in supporting andragogy. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10621-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Critical-thinking theme: participants wanted AI to challenge them to ask better questions and serve as a verification tool, noting "it's better when AI makes me question my own answer" (P3) and that mapped ideas reveal what is missing (P19).

"ALs also expect AI to strengthen their critical thinking by helping them hone questioning skills, verify information, and visually organize ideas through concept mapping."
