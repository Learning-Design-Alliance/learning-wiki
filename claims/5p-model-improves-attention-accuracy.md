## Evidence

### Sanit Srikoon 2017

Sanit Srikoon, Tassanee Bunterm, Teerachai Nethanomsak & Tang Keow Ngang. (2017). A comparative study of the effects of the neurocognitive-based model and the conventional model on learner attention, working memory and mood. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 14(1), 83-110. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1150726

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA multivariate test in a true experimental pre-test/post-test control group design with 76 randomly assigned Grade 9 students (5P n = 38, 5E n = 38). The article reports the model effect was significant, "could explain the variance of 48.6 percent of attention accuracy" (partial eta-squared = 0.486).

> "the instruction models had significantly influenced attention accuracy at a 0.05 significance level (F(14,61)=4.124, p=0.000) and could explain the variance of 48.6 percent of attention accuracy"
