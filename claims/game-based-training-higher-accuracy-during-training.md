## Evidence

### Manuel Ninaus 2023

Manuel Ninaus, Rodolpho Cortez, Izabel Hazin, Kristian Kiili, Silke M. Wortha, Elise Klein, Elisabeth M. Weiss, Korbinian Moeller. (2023). The added value of game elements: better training performance but comparable learning gains. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10263-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed model predicting PAE across five training sessions with condition as fixed factor, 85 participants (game n = 41, non-game n = 44). The article reports "the effect of the condition was statistically significant" (p = 0.007) with Std. beta = 0.59 favoring the game-based condition; no standardized effect size such as d was printed.

> "the effect of the condition was statistically significant (beta = 0.66, 95% CI [0.18, 1.14], t(417) = 2.71, p = 0.007; Std. beta = 0.59, 95% CI [0.26, 0.92]) reflecting that participants in the game-based condition committed smaller PAE during the training"
