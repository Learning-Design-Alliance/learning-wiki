Evidence¶

Sarani et al. 2014¶

Abdullah Sarani, Esmail Zare Behtash and Saieed Moslemi Nezhad Arani. (2014). The Effect of Video-Based Tasks in Listening Comprehension of Iranian Pre-intermediate EFL Learners. Gist Education and Learning Research Journal, No. 8, pp. 29-47. https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1692-5777

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Control group of 20 learners received ten sessions of audio-only instruction. The paired-samples t-test yielded a Sig. (2-tailed) of .279, higher than .05, so "the SPSS computation did not detect any significant difference"; the post-test mean was lower than the pre-test mean.

"the mean score for pre-test of listening comprehension is known as 16.5000, and the same score for post-test shows 15.2500"
