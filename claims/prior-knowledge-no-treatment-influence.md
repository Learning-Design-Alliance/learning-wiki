## Subclaims
`q3 i?` ANCOVAs controlling for prior knowledge revealed no significant influence of previous knowledge on treatment-dependent learning achievement for either rote or conceptual learning. [→ Hofferber 2014](#hofferber-2014)

## Evidence

### Hofferber 2014

Hofferber, N., Eckes, A., & Wilde, M. (2014). Effects of Autonomy Supportive vs. Controlling Teachers' Behavior on Students' Achievements. European Journal of Educational Research, 3(4), 177-184. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085991

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

An ANCOVA with prior knowledge as covariate found no significant influence on rote learning treatment effects (η2 = 0.000); a parallel ANCOVA for conceptual knowledge was also non-significant (F1, 84 = 0.216; p = ns; η2 = 0.003).

> "There was no significant influence of previous knowledge on a possible treatment-dependent learning achievement in the students (F1, 84 = 0.002; p = ns; η2 = 0.000)."
