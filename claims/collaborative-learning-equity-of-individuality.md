## Evidence

### Van Dusen 2019

Van Dusen, B., & Nissen, J. (2019). Equity in College Physics Student Learning: a Critical Quantitative Intersectionality Investigation. California State University Chico. [doi:10.1002/tea.21584](https://doi.org/10.1002/tea.21584)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The gain HLM accounting for course type (153 collaborative courses with 11,740 students; 34 lecture courses with 2,117 students) predicted "gains that are 3.06 percentage points larger in collaborative courses than lecture-based courses" for every demographic group, which the authors interpret as achieving Equity of Individuality.

> "The model predicts that all demographic groups will have gains that are 3.06 percentage points larger in collaborative courses than lecture-based courses."
