# Under normal conditions children with ADHD show deficits in vigilance, visual search and divided attention relative to TD children

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Group membership differentiated all controlled attention indicators under normal conditions, with medium-to-large effects. [→ Sebastian Skalski 2021](#sebastian-skalski-2021)

## Evidence

### Sebastian Skalski 2021

Sebastian Skalski, Grzegorz Pochwatko, Robert Balas. (2021). Impact of Motivation on Selected Aspects of Attention in Children with ADHD. Child Psychiatry & Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-020-01042-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Simple main effects from the mixed-design MANOVA comparing 30 ADHD and 30 TD children under normal conditions; the article also reports visual search RT r = 0.56, RT slope r = 0.44, single-tasking r = 0.41 and multi-tasking r = 0.50.

> "The group membership factor differentiated all of the controlled attention indicators under normal conditions: omission errors ( t = 3.81, p < 0.001, r = 0.45), commission errors (t = 2.66, p = 0.010, r = 0.33)"
