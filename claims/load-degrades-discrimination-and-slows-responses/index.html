q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed-effects behavioral analysis with load as fixed factor, subject random, gender and age covariates. Found "a lower Pr (indicating less accurate discrimination) at higher levels of working memory load" [F(2,606)=469.83; p<0.001].

"As expected, subjects had a lower Pr (indicating less accurate discrimination) at higher levels of working memory load [F (2, 606)= 469.83; p<0.001)]."
