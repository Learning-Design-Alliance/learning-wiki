Evidence¶

Madkour and Mohamed 2016¶

Magda Madkour & Rafik Ahmed Abdel Moati Mohamed. (2016). Identifying College Students’ Multiple Intelligences to Enhance Motivation and Language Proficiency. English Language Teaching, 9(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n6p92

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental post-test comparison between group 1 (traditional memorization of rules) and group 2 (multiple intelligences instruction). The article reports the test score of group 2 at 89% versus 44% for group 1, "showing a difference of 45%." No effect size or test statistic is printed.

"the test score of group 1 of the participants who studied English using the traditional method of memorizing rules was 44% while the test score of group 2 who learned English using multiple intelligences was 89%, showing a difference of 45%."
