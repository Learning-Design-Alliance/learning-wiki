q2 · i? · design · r2

Internal consistency analysis of the Spanish MAP Growth Reading pilot administrations (Fall 2018, Winter 2019, Spring 2019). The report states the "overall marginal reliabilities for all grades are in the 0.90s," interpreted as high internal consistency; no per-grade coefficients are quoted here.

"The overall marginal reliabilities for all grades are in the 0.90s, which suggests that the tests have high internal consistency."
