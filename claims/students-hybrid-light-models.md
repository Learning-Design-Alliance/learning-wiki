Potsdam physics education research on model construction (citing Horn et al. 2002) found students in optics "tend to construct hybrid models", e.g. describing sinusoidal trajectories of light particles, mixing particle paths with wave descriptions.

> "Especially in optics, the students investigated often show a ten-dency to construct hybrid models. In their des-criptions, an appropriate und necessary distinction between different models of light is lacking"
