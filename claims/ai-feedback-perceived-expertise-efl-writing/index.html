Evidence¶

Serpil 2026¶

Serpil, H., & Mor, M. K. (2026). Bounded reliance: A source credibility perspective on EFL students' engagement with AI-generated writing feedback. Journal of Language Pedagogy and Linguistics, 1(1), 1-27. https://doi.org/10.0.20.161/zenodo.22828386

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Reflexive thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 17 undergraduate translation students after a 15-week GROK-supported writing course. Students reported improvements in vocabulary, grammar, and coherence; one noted the AI could "correct my mistakes immediately." Perceived efficacy only, not an independent textual-quality measure.

"The participants consistently described AI-generated feedback as an effective learning resource that supported multiple aspects of their writing development."
