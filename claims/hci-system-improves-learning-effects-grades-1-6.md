## Evidence

### Li Mengru 2025

Li Mengru, Lv Yang, Pu Yongming, Wu Min. (2025). Design and evaluation of children’s education interactive learning system based on human computer interaction technology. Scientific Reports 15, 6135. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-90800-y

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Statistical analysis of the randomized experiment's pre/post learning-effect improvement (Table 3). The t-test p-values are all less than 0.01; for grades 1–3 science the article reports "effect size for science (d = 0.67) is large" with a 95% confidence interval of [22.1, 34.1].

> "In the grade groups 1–3, the effect size for science (d = 0.67) is large, suggesting that the learning system has a significant impact on improving learning effects in this subject."

### Li Mengru 2025 (2)

Statistical analysis of the same randomized experiment for grades 4–6 (Table 3). The article reports "effect size for social science (d = 0.62)" exceeding mathematics (d = 0.58) and English (d = 0.50), all with p-values less than 0.01.

> "In the grade groups 4–6, the effect size for social science (d = 0.62) is slightly higher than that for mathematics (d = 0.58) and English (d = 0.50)."
