### Dures Emma 2014

Dures Emma, Hewlett Sarah, Ambler Nicholas, Jenkins Remona, Clarke Joyce, Gooberman-Hill Rachael. (2014). Rheumatology clinicians’ experiences of brief training and implementation of skills to support patient self-management. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2474-15-108

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 1 finding from semi-structured interviews with 16 rheumatology clinicians about brief training in self-management facilitation. The article reports that “The opportunity to practice specific skills and get feedback was highly valued,” and that case studies and vignettes exemplified the theoretical approaches.

> "The opportunity to practice specific skills and get feedback was highly valued. The use of case studies and vignettes to exemplify the theoretical approaches to self-management helped participants to think about ways of taking these different approaches into practice."
