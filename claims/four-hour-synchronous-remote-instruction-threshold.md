# During remote learning, students at all grade levels spent about four hours per day in synchronous online instruction regardless of district requirements

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students in grades K-12 averaged roughly 240 minutes of daily synchronous instruction via Google Meets during fall 2020, regardless of grade-level requirements, with kindergarteners and first-graders averaging 199 and 217 minutes. [→ Gwynne 2024](#gwynne-2024)

## Evidence

### Gwynne 2024

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., Young, C., & Hart, H. (2024). Student experiences through the COVID-19 pandemic: Attendance, online learning platform use, and annual survey reports. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/district-data/metrics/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of Google Meets login minutes for 270,061 students in grades K-12 enrolled at least 20 days in fall semester 2020-21 in regular district schools. The report found "students at all grade levels were engaged in synchronous instruction for about four hours a day (240 minutes)" despite differing requirements.

> "But regardless of requirements, students at all grade levels were engaged in synchronous instruction for about four hours a day (240 minutes)."
