## Evidence

### Abbasi Ebrahim 2026

Abbasi Ebrahim, Abbasifar Mahboubeh, Bazrafkan Lila. (2026). Relationship between midwifery educators’ quality of life and students’ clinical training quality. BMC Medical Education, 26, 1278. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09637-2

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional descriptive-analytical study using Spearman's non-parametric correlation on institution-level aggregated scores from 183 students and 87 teachers at seven universities in Fars Province. The correlation was positive and significant, with "r = 0.219, p = 0.042".

> "according to the correlation coefficient, there is a positive and significant correlation between the variable of midwifery students’ clinical training and the level of quality of life of their educational instructors (r = 0.219, p = 0.042)"
