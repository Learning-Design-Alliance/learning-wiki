## Evidence

### Benjamin O. Tayo and Ananda A. Jayawardhana 2018

Benjamin O. Tayo and Ananda A. Jayawardhana (2018). Grade inflation in introductory physics – the influence of out-of-class assignments. Pittsburg State University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1804.05084](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1804.05084)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantile-quantile analysis of the same 8-semester dataset compares observed versus expected percentiles calculated using the normal distribution. The article concludes "the proctored plus out-of-class scores are significantly biased due to the contribution from out-of-class assignments."

> "Figure 2 shows clearly that the proctored exam scores are distributed according to the normal distribution as most of the data points fit nicely with the identical line. However, for the proctored plus out-of-class scores, the expected percentiles do not correlate well with the observed percentiles."
