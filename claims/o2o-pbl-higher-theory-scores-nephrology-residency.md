## Evidence

### Wu Junxia 2024

Wu Junxia, Ke You, Chen Zhida, Alhendi Mhd Alaa, Zhu Lina, Ma Kunling. (2024). Online-to-offline combined with problem-based learning is an effective teaching modality in the standardized residency training of nephrology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05675-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of test scores between two demographically matched groups of 30 nephrology residency trainees each, one taught with O2O/PBL and one with LBT. The reported theoretical score difference was "statistically significant (P < 0.05)", with t = 2.581 and P = 0.013 in Table 2.

> "The theoretical score of O2O/PBL group was 81.233 ± 9.156, and that of the LBT group was 75.800 ± 7.009."
