### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Student difficulties with representations of quantum operators corresponding to observables. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.049](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.049)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Open-ended question Q3, administered to 62 UG and 68 graduate students, required writing ⟨p|p̂|p′⟩ without Dirac notation; only 27% of UG students wrote the correct expression, while 84% of graduate students did (Table III).

> "Table III shows that, in response to question Q3,  only 27% of the UG students wrote the correct expression, ⟨𝑝|𝑝̂|𝑝′⟩ = 𝑝′𝛿(𝑝 − 𝑝′)."
