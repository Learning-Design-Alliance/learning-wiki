Stefana Broadbent. (2026). A study of GenAI usage by Design Students: Analysis of Survey Results and Journals of AI practices at the Politecnico di Milano in 2025/2026. Department of Design, Politecnico di Milano. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17094

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey task checklist (n=231 respondents). Research activities such as finding information, summarising notes and brainstorming were each selected by more than two thirds; the article notes creative-media and code tasks were "unexpectedly selected by less than one in three respondents".

"tasks involving the generation of images, audio, music, video, games or code, were unexpectedly selected by less than one in three respondents."
