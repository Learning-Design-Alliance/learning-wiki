q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA of post-intervention OSCE scores by teaching method controlled for years of ED experience (reported range one to six years). The authors report the analysis 'showed no effect due to the years of ED experience', attributing this to the small sample.

"Due to small sample size of our experiment, plotting the experience against the OSCE result showed no effect due to the years of ED experience (Table 3)."
