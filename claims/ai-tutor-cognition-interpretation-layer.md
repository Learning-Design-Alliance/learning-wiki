A conceptual design claim from Section 5.2, not an empirical test. The article specifies that AI Tutors observe behaviours such as "revisions, hesitation sequences, questioning strategies" and interpret them as signals of tension, interpretive instability, or identity cognition, with temporal continuity across episodes.
