## Evidence

### Schwartz 2011

Schwartz, D. L., Chase, C. C., Oppezzo, M. A., & Chin, D. B. (2011). Practicing versus inventing with contrasting cases: The effects of telling first on learning and transfer. Journal of Educational Psychology. http://aaalab.stanford.edu/transfer.html. [doi:10.1037/a0025140](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0025140)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Coding of correct answers on the four contrasting-case worksheets in Experiment 2; disaggregation showed only the gold (continuous-density) worksheet differed significantly, F(1, 113) = 5.33, p = .023.

> "An ANOVA compared the average percentage correct per worksheet and demonstrated a significant effect of condition; MICC = 84.7% (SD = 22.7%), MT&P = 91.8% (SD = 12.2%), F(1, 113) = 4.5, p = .037."
