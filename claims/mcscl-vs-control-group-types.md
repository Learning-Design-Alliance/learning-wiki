# mCSCL outperforms individual learning and non-mobile collaborative learning, but shows no significant advantage over desktop CSCL

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` mCSCL effect sizes were largest against non-CL controls (g = 0.587), smaller against non-computer CL (g = 0.472), and not significant against desktop CSCL (g = 0.201). [→ Sung Yao-Ting 2017](#sung-yao-ting-2017)

## Evidence

### Sung Yao-Ting 2017

Sung Yao-Ting, Yang Je-Ming, Lee Han-Yueh. (2017). The Effects of Mobile-Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning: Meta-Analysis and Critical Synthesis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317704307

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects moderator analysis of control group types across the 48 included studies. Table 4 reports g = 0.587 for non-CL (k = 31), g = 0.472 for CL without computers (k = 11), and g = 0.201 for CSCL without mobile devices (k = 6), the latter not significant; QB = 3.282 was not significant.

> "The mCSCL studies using non-CL as their control group demonstrated the largest ES, followed by control groups of CL that did not use computers and control groups of CSCL that used desktops but not mobile devices."
