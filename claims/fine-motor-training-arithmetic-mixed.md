# Fine motor skills training improves children's arithmetic skills in some trials but not others

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r3` · `q2`–`q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized controlled trial, FMS training improved both FMS and arithmetic skills, with improvements the review describes as medium to large effect sizes without printed values. [→ Maëlle Neveu 2023](#maelle-neveu-2023)
`q2 i?` Piano training improved FMS and arithmetic jointly in one study, but two other FMS training studies reported no arithmetic improvement despite gains in FMS, self-esteem, or visuospatial working memory. [→ Maëlle Neveu 2023 (2)](#maelle-neveu-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Maëlle Neveu 2023

Maëlle Neveu, Marie Geurten, Nancy Durieux, Laurence Rousselle. (2023). Finger Use and Arithmetic Skills in Children and Adolescents: a Scoping Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09722-8

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Randomized controlled trial (Asakawa et al., 2019) in which school-aged TD participants were trained in fine motor skills; training improved FMS and arithmetic, unlike controls, with improvements the review describes as "medium to large effect sizes" but no numeric value printed.

> "The results showed that children in the training group improved not only their FMS but also their arithmetic skills, unlike those in the control group. The improvements had medium to large effect sizes."

### Maëlle Neveu 2023 (2)

Maëlle Neveu, Marie Geurten, Nancy Durieux, Laurence Rousselle. (2023). Finger Use and Arithmetic Skills in Children and Adolescents: a Scoping Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09722-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Non-randomized trials of FMS training (piano lessons for TD children; training for children with DCD) reported no arithmetic improvement despite enhancement in FMS, self-esteem, or visuospatial working memory.

> "Conversely, two other studies reported no improvement in arithmetic skills following FMS training in children with TD (Costa-Giomi, 2004; piano lessons) or DCD (Alloway & Warner, 2008), but there was an enhancement in FMS, self-esteem, or visuospatial working memory."
