### Collie 2022

Collie, R.J. (2022). Perceived social-emotional competence: A multidimensional examination and links with social-emotional motivation and behaviors. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101656

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`
