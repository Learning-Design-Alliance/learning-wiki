Robbrecht M, Van Winckel M, Mulder A, Embo M. (2025). What is the learning effect of video review in postgraduate medical education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06515-7

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's level 3 synthesis reports six studies on behaviour change. For example, Parker et al.'s study "reveal[ed] a significant decrease of errors post-video review"; Kava et al. found the video review group exhibited improved leadership scores in a second resuscitation whereas the control group did not; Birnbach et al. found consistently positive significant score improvements in the video review group.

> "Parker et al. [35] measured medication prescribing errors in the period before and after video feedback and self-assessment sessions with surgical residents, revealing a significant decrease of errors post-video review."
