Marginal homogeneity tests compared baseline and 6-month confidence questionnaires in the trial; more students rated themselves confident or fully confident at 6 months, while depth (p=0.139), rate (p=0.435), and hand position (p=0.056) showed no significant differences.

> "In the confidence test, significant differences were observed for the following items: "Overall, how confident are you in your CPR skills?" (p=0.007) and "Total chest recoil" (p=0.019)."
