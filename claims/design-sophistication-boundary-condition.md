### Juliette C. Désiron 2026 (2)

Juliette C. Désiron, Julien Venni, Mireille Bétrancourt. (2026). Mapping the Heterogeneity of Emotional Design Features in Multimedia Research: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10138-3

`q1 · i?` · `review · r3`

Authors' theoretical argument in the Discussion, not an empirical test. The review argues sophistication "constitutes a boundary condition for emotional design effects" depending on how elaborately features are designed.

> "The present review argues that the sophistication of visual design constitutes a boundary condition for emo- tional design effects. Even when emotional design features are present, their potential to elicit affective, motivational, or cognitive benefits may depend on how elaborately these features are designed."
