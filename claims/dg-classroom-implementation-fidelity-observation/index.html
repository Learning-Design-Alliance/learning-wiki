Evidence¶

Jiang et al. 2015¶

Zhonghong Jiang, Edwin Dickey, Alexander White, Ewelina McBroom, M. Alejandra Sorto, & Alana Rosenwasser. (2015). A Dynamic Geometry-Centered Teacher Professional Development Program and Its Impact. Proceedings of the 37th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education, East Lansing, MI: Michigan State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED584322

q3 · i? · causal · r2

DGOP/CGOP observations in 16 classrooms (8 per group), each visited by two observers four or five times across the school year, analyzed with mixed-effect ANOVA (Table 1). Good lesson design: 2.81 vs 1.85, p = .032; Use of dynamic features: 2.75 vs 0.70, p = .000; overall DGOP 2.28 vs 1.68, p = .088.

"Results confirm the efficacy of the DG treatment by showing significant differences in the two aspects related to the intention to implement a dynamic lesson. As a whole, lessons in the DG group had a significantly better design aligned with the DG teaching approach"
