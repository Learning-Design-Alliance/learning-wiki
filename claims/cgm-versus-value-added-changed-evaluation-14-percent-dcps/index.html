Evidence¶

Elias Walsh 2015¶

Elias Walsh, Eric Isenberg. (2015). How Does Value Added Compare to Student Growth Percentiles? Statistics and Public Policy, vol. 2, issue 1. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/how-does-value-added-compare-to-student-growth-percentiles

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Observational analysis of DCPS teacher evaluation data comparing a typical value-added model with the Colorado Growth Model. The study reports that "use of growth percentiles in place of value added would have altered evaluation consequences for 14% of DCPS teachers."

"Using data from the District of Columbia Public Schools (DCPS), we examine changes in evaluation scores across the two methods for all teachers and for teacher subgroups. We find that use of growth percentiles in place of value added would have altered evaluation consequences for 14% of DCPS teachers."
