## Evidence

### López-Brotons Maria 2026

López-Brotons Maria, Javaloy-Ballestero Sergio, Ramos-Rincón José-Manuel. (2026). Evaluation of a peer-assisted, simulation-based clinical skills training program in Spain: a prospective single-group before-and-after study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2026.23.15

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Overall pooled analysis of both academic years by workshop type, reported in Results and Table 4; all workshops showed significant pre–post gains, with "Cohen’s d=1.41" for pulmonary auscultation.

> "the greatest increase in knowledge was observed in the pulmonary auscultation workshop (Δ=3.1 points; Cohen’s d=1.41; 95% CI, 2.48–3.72), followed by the neurological examination workshop (Δ=2.9 points; Cohen’s d=1.38; 95% CI, 2.40–3.40). The smallest increase was observed in the rectal and breast examination workshop (Δ=1.9 points)"
