Non-randomized controlled trial (Table 3) with 18 credentialed gynaecological surgeons naive to RAS; the intervention group completed 10 dVSS tasks to proficiency plus standardized pig laboratory training before robotic supracervical hysterectomy on human patients. The article reports "IG significantly outperformed CG in terms of operative time and EBL" with no significant GOALS difference.
