### Orland 2010

Orland, M., Hoffman, A., & Vaughn, III, E. S. (2010). Evaluation of the Comprehensive School Reform Program Implementation and Outcomes: Fifth-Year Report. U.S. Department of Education. https://www.ed.gov/about/offices/list/opepd/ppss/reports.html#title

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of model adoption using CSRQ Center ratings covering the 31 most widely adopted CSR models. The report finds that "only one-third of CSR schools adopted models that later received ratings from CSRQ that indicated they had a scientific research base." Most schools chose unrated models.
