## Evidence

### Noritake 2025

Noritake, K., Yamaguchi, K., Kikukawa, M., Kanamori, Y., & Nitta, H. (2025). Characteristics of Good Clinical Teachers: Insights From Japanese Clinical Trainee Dental Residents. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13086

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content-analysis study recruiting CTDRs and dentists within a year of clinical training via maximum variation sampling across five Japanese institutions until thematic saturation. The study reports "a total of nine interviews involving 38 participants".

> "Between October 2019 and December 2021, we conducted a total of nine interviews involving 38 participants, comprising CTDRs and dentists."

### Noritake 2025 (2)

Noritake, K., Yamaguchi, K., Kikukawa, M., Kanamori, Y., & Nitta, H. (2025). Characteristics of Good Clinical Teachers: Insights From Japanese Clinical Trainee Dental Residents. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13086

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the results section reporting theme frequencies across the 376 extracted statements: the top theme, "Provides advice at the level of CTDRs", was mentioned 45 times, with residents valuing teachers who "assessed our initial capabilities and then decided our next steps based on our abilities".

> "The most frequently mentioned theme was ‘Provides advice at the level of CTDRs’ (T, 45 mentioned, ‘The teacher assessed our initial capabilities and then decided our next steps based on our abilities, saying, “Now that you can do this much, let's move on to the next level.” I'm grateful for that'.)"
