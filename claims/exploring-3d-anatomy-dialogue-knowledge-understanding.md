## Evidence

### Zhang J 2026

Zhang J, Sachdeva M, McCaig C, Gordon E, Shapiro L, Keenan ID. (2026). Exploring 3D anatomy: A free online course supporting novice learner inquiry and spatial conceptualization. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70288

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theme constructed from combined interpretative phenomenological analysis of focus group transcripts. One participant said discussion boards are "a really good place to learn mnemonics and acronyms," while another stated "I don't think [discussion boards are useful], because for anatomy it's like it's either right or isn't," though allowed that discussion of planes on a 3D object could be helpful with a larger user group.

> "This theme focuses on disparate views of anatomical education in terms of the focus and value placed on dialogue around the memorization of facts (knowledge) versus holistic and conceptual comprehension (understanding) in the context of learner reflection and discussion."
