q3 · i? · review · r2

Mapping question analysis of publication counts across the 2011–2022 review period, shown in Fig. 2, reports that "AIED research began to experience exponential growth, although there was a small retraction in 2022". The review attributes the surge to rapid AI technological advance and mainstream media attention to AI ethics.

"However, starting in 2019, there was a noticeable shift in the trajectory. AIED research began to experience exponential growth, although there was a small retraction in 2022, with a remarkable surge in the number of articles in 2021."
