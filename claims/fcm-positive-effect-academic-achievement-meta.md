# The flipped classroom model has a positive effect on academic achievement, with a random-effects mean Hedges' g of 0.462

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Meta-analysis of 71 effect sizes from 64 studies found FCM positively affects academic achievement, with a REM effect size of 0.462 (95% CI 0.341–0.583). [→ Talan 2020](#talan-2020)

## Evidence

### Talan 2020

Talan, T. & Batdi, V. (2020). Evaluating the Flipped Classroom Model through the Multi-Complementary Approach. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 21(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1269613

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 64 studies (71 effect size comparisons) on FCM versus traditional instruction, analysed with CMA and MetaWin software using a random effects model and Hedges' g. The REM mean effect size was 0.462 with a 95% confidence interval whose "lower limit was 0.341" and upper limit 0.583, interpreted by the authors as a positive effect.

> "Therefore, in the calculations made according to REM, the standard error was calculated as 0.062, the upper limit of the 95% confidence interval was 0.583 and the lower limit was 0.341."
