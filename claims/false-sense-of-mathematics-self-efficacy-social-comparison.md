Christopher T. Stripling and T. Grady Roberts. (2013). Effects of Mathematics Integration in a Teaching Methods Course on Self-Efficacy of Preservice Agricultural Education Teachers. Journal of Agricultural Education, 54(2), 114-129. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2013.02114

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion section, offered as an explanation for high self-reported efficacy despite prior research showing a lack of mathematics proficiency among Florida preservice agricultural education teachers; no new data test this explanation.

> "The social nature of self -efficacy may have led  the preservice teachers to believe that they were as competent in mathematics and the teaching of contextualized mathematics as their peers, resulting in a disconnect between perceived and actual ability ."
