## Evidence

### Scicluna Helen A 2012

Scicluna Helen A, Grimm Michael C, O’Sullivan Anthony J, Harris Peter, Pilotto Louis S, Jones Philip D, McNeil H Patrick. (2012). Clinical capabilities of graduates of an outcomes-based integrated medical program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-23

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of hospital-based supervisor PRF-ETSA ratings with junior doctors' self-ratings on the same form. Supervisors "consistently rated their junior doctors more capable than the junior doctors’ self-ratings", which the authors read as validating the self-perception data.

> "Moreover, supervisors consistently rated their junior doctors more capable than the junior doctors’ self-ratings (Figure 4), indicating that self-perceptions do not represent over-estimations of clinical competence."
