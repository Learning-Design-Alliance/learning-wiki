Tithi 2026¶

Tithi, S.D., Alam, N., Yasir, T., Shi, Y., Tian, X., Chi, M., Barnes, T. (2026). Adaptive Scaffolding for Cognitive Engagement in an Intelligent Tutoring System. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07308

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Null finding on the rule-accuracy component of the composite posttest score in the N=113 study; means were 72.1 (Control), 74.8 (BKT), and 74.7 (DRL) with no significant differences. Equivalence was not tested beyond the non-significant test.

"There were no significant differences in rule accuracy across groups (Control(mean): 72.1,BKT(mean): 74.8,DRL(mean): 74.7;p>0.05)."
