The authors report, from their own earlier longitudinal study of engineering students, that after controlling for credits earned "black transfer students are more likely to persist in engineering than non-transfer black students," a difference specific to black students. No effect size is printed.

> "In an earlier study, after controlling for credits earned, we found that black transfer students are more likely to persist in engineering than non-transfer black students—a finding that does not hold for students of other ethnicities."
