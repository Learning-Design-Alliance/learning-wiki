Survey results on highest training level completed (Figure 1) and reasons for not progressing. The article reports "financial constraints (n = 16; 28.1%), limited opportunities (n = 13; 22.8%) and time constraints (n = 12; 21.1%)" as the leading barriers; some participants preferred alternative systems such as SASL.

> "Slightly over half of the participants (n = 30; 52.6%) indicated they would like to complete further Makaton training. Reasons for not progressing to higher levels included financial constraints (n = 16; 28.1%), limited opportunities (n = 13; 22.8%) and time constraints (n = 12; 21.1%)."
