## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Fourth-year dental students who received 10 hours of HVRS training before clinical practice achieved significantly higher access cavity preparation scores on real patients than students who received routine training only (Mann–Whitney U Z = -2.052, p = 0.040, Cohen's d = 0.854). ## Evidence

### Şimşek Emine 2026

Şimşek Emine, Satar Selimcan. (2026). The effect of haptic virtual reality simulation training before clinical endodontic practice on student performance and perception. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09577-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled parallel-group study of 30 fourth-year dental students (15 per group) performing access cavity preparation on real patients. The experimental group's mean cavity score of 3.47 ± 0.83 was significantly higher than the control group's 2.73 ± 0.88, with Cohen's d = 0.854 (95% CI 0.097–1.596).

> "the mean score of the experimental group (3.47 ± 0.83) was found to be higher than that of the control group (2.73 ± 0.88). Statistical analysis revealed that the difference between the groups was significant (Z = -2.052, p = 0.040)"
