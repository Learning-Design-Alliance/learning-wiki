## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The proportion of turns in which the agent's response to raw ASR input differed from its response to the true input was not significantly correlated with post-test scores controlled by pre-test scores (r = .246, p = .142). [→ Yuya Asano 2023](#yuya-asano-2023)

## Evidence

### Yuya Asano 2023

Yuya Asano, Diane Litman, Mingzhi Yu, Nikki Lobczowski, Timothy Nokes-Malach, Adriana Kovashka, and Erin Walker. (2023). Impact of Experiencing Misrecognition by Teachable Agents on Learning and Rapport. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-36336-8_94](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-36336-8_94)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis of 38 undergraduate students who taught ratio word problems to the robot Emma via spoken dialogue for 30 minutes over Zoom, using partial correlation for post-test scores controlled by pre-test scores. Overall misrecognition showed "learning ( r = .246, p = .142)", which was not significant.

> "Overall misrecognition is not correlated with either rapport ( r = −.016, p = .922) or learning ( r = .246, p = .142)."
