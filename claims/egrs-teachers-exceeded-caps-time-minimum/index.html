Brahm Fleisch and Kerryn Dixon 2019¶

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative case-study lesson observations in Grade 1 and 2 Setswana Home Language classrooms across the two case study teams. The article reports that "teachers taught longer than the minimum time of 7 hours per week" and frames this as a shift, since prior studies found time on reading below the PIRLS mean.

"Observations revealed that teac hers taught longer than the minimum time of 7 hours per week for Home Language (HL) lessons prescribed by the Curriculum Assessment and Policy Statement (CAPS)"
