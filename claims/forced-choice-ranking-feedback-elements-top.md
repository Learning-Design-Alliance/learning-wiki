## Evidence

### Engerer Cosima 2016

Engerer Cosima, Berberat Pascal O., Dinkel Andreas, Rudolph Baerbel, Sattel Heribert, Wuensch Alexander. (2016). Integrating 360° behavior-orientated feedback in communication skills training for medical undergraduates: concept, acceptance and students’ self-ratings of communication competence. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0792-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Forced-choice ranking of nine didactic elements (1 'very important' to 9 'less important') completed by the 34 participants after the workshop; items were ranked in ascending order based on mean rank. The article reports "theoretical introduction, rank 7; memory card, rank 9".

> "The forced choice ranking placed all feedback elements at the top of the list (feedback (FB) by SPs, rank 2; FB by trainer, rank 3; FB by colleagues, rank 4), whereas theoretical elements were at the bottom (theoretical introduction, rank 7; memory card, rank 9)."
