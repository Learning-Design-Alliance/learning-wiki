## Evidence

### Amin Aspanani 2023

Amin Aspanani, Hosein Sadeqhi, Omid Athar. (2023). The relationship between visual memory and spatial intelligence with students’ academic achievement in anatomy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04327-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent t-tests on dental students (Table 1: N=38 male, N=47 female) and medical students (N=78 male, N=70 female) found no significant sex differences; medical P-values were 0.08 (visual memory) and 0.78 (spatial intelligence). No effect sizes printed; equivalence not tested.

> "Independent t-test showed that the mean scores of visual memory (P-value=0.14) and spatial intelligence (P-value =0.66) in dental students were not significantly different between men and women."
