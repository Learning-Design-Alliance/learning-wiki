## Evidence

### Hao 2025

Hao, Z., Jiang, J., Yu, J., Liu, Z., & Zhang, Y. (2025). Student-AI Interaction in an LLM-Empowered Learning Environment: A Cluster Analysis of Engagement Profiles. arXiv:2503.01694v3. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational cluster contrast from the discussion section: the stagnating interactors (Cluster 2, n=60) sent many messages (M=6.53, SD=9.56) yet had a low completion rate (M=0.77, SD=1.00). The authors interpret their frequent interactions as reflecting curiosity about the novel format rather than course content.

> "Students in Cluster 2 showed a high volume of interaction with the AI agents but did not complete the course. This suggests that a high message count may not always signify productive engagement; instead, their frequent interactions might reflect a curiosity about the novel learning format itself, rather than a genuine interest in the course content."

### Hao 2025 (2)

Hao, Z., Jiang, J., Yu, J., Liu, Z., & Zhang, Y. (2025). Student-AI Interaction in an LLM-Empowered Learning Environment: A Cluster Analysis of Engagement Profiles. arXiv:2503.01694v3. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA on coded message proportions across Clusters 2, 3, and 4 found significant differences in content-unrelated messages, F(2,205)=6.10, p=.003, with Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparisons showing Cluster 2 highest.

> "Pairwise comparisons with Bonferroni adjustments demonstrated that Cluster 2 had higher unrelated contents (M= 0.38 , SD= 0.41 ) than Cluster 3 (M= 0.23 , SD= 0.35 , t= 2.67 , p=.02 ) and Cluster 4 ( M= 0.19 , SD= 0.21 , t= 3.35 , p=.003 )."
