## Evidence

### Surman Craig B. H. 2013

Surman Craig B. H., Biederman Joseph, Spencer Thomas, Miller Carolyn A., McDermott Katie M., Faraone Stephen V. (2013). Understanding deficient emotional self-regulation in adults with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: a controlled study. Attention deficit and hyperactivity disorders. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4009378/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of ODD–DESR overlap among ADHD probands. The study reports "past oppositional defiant disorder (ODD) was significantly associated (p<0.01) with extreme (>95th percentile of control subjects) DESR scores, and current ODD was not," with 71.4% of current-ODD and 69.4% of past-ODD probands showing extreme DESR.

> "Among ADHD probands, past oppositional defiant disorder (ODD) was significantly associated (p<0.01) with extreme (>95th percentile of control subjects) DESR scores, and current ODD was not; 71.4 % of ADHD probands with current ODD, and 69.4 % of ADHD probands with past ODD had extreme DESR scores."
