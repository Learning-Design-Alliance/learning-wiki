## Evidence

### Mahmoud M.A. 2020

Mahmoud M.A., Laws S., Kamal A., Al Mohanadi D., Al Mohammed A., Mahfoud Z.R. (2020). Examining aptitude and barriers to evidence-based medicine among trainees at an ACGME-I accredited program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02341-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 80 internal medicine trainees at Hamad Medical Corporation, Doha, Qatar, using the validated ACE tool (15 binary questions). The study reports an average score of "8.9 ± 1.6" and "59.3%" correct answers.

> "Overall, participants' scores on the ACE tool ranged from 5.0 to 12.0 (out of 15) with an average of 8.9 ± 1.6 and a median of 9.0. This means that participants were able to correctly answer, on average, only 59.3% of the ACE questions."
