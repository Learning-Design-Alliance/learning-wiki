## Evidence

### Schenke 2017

Schenke, K., Nguyen, T., Watts, T. W., Sarama, J. H., & Clements, D. H. (2017). Differential effects of the classroom on African American and non-African American’s mathematics achievement. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(6), 794–811. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000165

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational results (Table 2) from the TRIAD evaluation sample reporting associations among classroom observation dimensions and posttest REMA achievement, including the null correlation for support for mathematical discourse (r = −.03, p = .29).

> "Across the whole sample, teacher confidence and enthusiasm was statistically significantly correlated with post mathematics achievement (r = .18, p < .001), as was expectations and responsiveness to developmental needs (r = .09, p < .01), but, support for mathematical discourse and posttest mathematics achievement were not significantly correlated (r = −.03, p = .29)."
