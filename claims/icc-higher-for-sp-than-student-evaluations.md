Intraclass correlation coefficients measured rating consistency among raters in the validation phase. The study reports "higher reliability" for SP evaluations (ICC = 0.588, 0.594) than student evaluations (ICC = 0.469, 0.34), indicating greater variability in how students were rated.

> "Evaluation of SPs by raters demonstrated higher reliability, with SP 1 (ICC = 0.588) and SP 2 (ICC = 0.594), suggesting good consistency in SP assessments compared to that in student evaluations."
