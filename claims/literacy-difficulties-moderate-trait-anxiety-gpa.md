## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The interaction of trait anxiety × literacy difficulties significantly predicted GPA (β = –0.15); trait anxiety was more strongly negatively associated with GPA for students with higher literacy difficulties (β = −0.40) than lower (β = −0.07, ns). [→ Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa 2025](#wang-li-chih-chung-kevin-kien-hoa-2025)

## Evidence

### Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa 2025

Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa. (2025). Relationships of trait anxiety, test anxiety, and academic performance of Chinese undergraduates with typical developments and high- and typical-functioning specific learning disabilities. Annals of Dyslexia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-025-00328-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Moderation analysis using Hayes' PROCESS macro (model 1) with GPA as outcome, literacy difficulties as moderator, age and gender as covariates. The article reports the "interaction term (trait anxiety × literacy difficulties) was also significant, β = –0.15"; simple slopes were β = −0.40 (p < 0.001) at higher difficulties and β = −0.07 (p = 0.38) at lower.

> "Crucially, the interaction term (trait anxiety × literacy difficulties) was also significant, β = –0.15, SE = 0.05, t = –3.00, p < 0.01, 95% CI [− 0.25, − 0.05]."
