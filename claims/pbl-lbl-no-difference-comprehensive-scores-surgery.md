## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There is no statistically significant difference between PBL and LBL on comprehensive scores in surgical education (SMD = 0.26, P = 0.421); equivalence was not tested. [→ Zheng Qi-Ming 2023](#zheng-qi-ming-2023)

## Evidence

### Zheng Qi-Ming 2023

Zheng Qi-Ming, Li Yuan-Yuan, Yin Qing, Zhang Na, Wang Ye-Peng, Li Guo-Xiang, Sun Zhi-Gang. (2023). The effectiveness of problem-based learning compared with lecture-based learning in surgical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04531-7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of three articles (7 studies) of comprehensive scores, 332 cases (118 PBL, 214 LBL), random-effects model due to heterogeneity (I²=85.8%). The pooled difference was not significant: "SMD = 0.26, 95% CI: -0.37 ~ 0.89, P = 0.421".

> "The results showed that there was no significant difference in comprehensive scores between students in PBL group and LBL group (SMD = 0.26, 95% CI: -0.37 ~ 0.89, P = 0.421) (Fig. 3a)."
