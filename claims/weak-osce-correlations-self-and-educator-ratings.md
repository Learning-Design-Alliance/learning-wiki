## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Akan Hülya 2025

Akan Hülya, Delibaş Hamza Yasin, Koran Serhat, Yiğit Pakize, Tanriöver Özlem. (2025). Enhancing self-regulated learning: a comparison of medical students’ self-assessment and faculty evaluations on communication skills in simulated patient interviews. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07794-4

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Spearman rank correlation analysis relating the 2023–2024 final OSCE (six stations, one communication) to educators' evaluations and students' self-assessments; correlations are weak (r = 0.271, r = 0.237) but statistically significant.

> "The results revealed a weak, statistically significant, and positive correlation between the OSCE score and educators’ evaluation (r = 0.271; p < 0.001). A weak, statistically significant, and positive correlation is found between the students’ self-assessment and the educators’ (r = 0.237, p < 0.001)."
