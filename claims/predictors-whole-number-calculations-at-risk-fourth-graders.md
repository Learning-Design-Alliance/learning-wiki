## Evidence

### Namkung 2016

Namkung, J. M., & Fuchs, L. S. (2016). Cognitive Predictors of Calculations and Number Line Estimation with Whole Numbers and Fractions among At-Risk Students. Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(2), 214-228. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000055

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model in which seven cognitive predictors and incoming calculation skill predicted latent whole-number and fraction calculation variables in 139 at-risk fourth graders. The model fit adequately (RMSEA = .055, CFI = .953) and "accounted for 51%" of whole-number calculation variance; no standardized effect sizes for individual paths are printed in the quoted text.

> "The model accounted for 51% and 32% of the variance in whole-number calculations and fraction calculations, respectively. For whole-number calculations, significant predictors were processing speed, attentive behavior, and incoming calculation skill."
