Ajmi Soffien, Kurz Martin, Lindner Thomas Werner, Dalen Ingvild, Ersdal Hege Langli. (2024). Does clinical experience influence the effects of team simulation training in stroke thrombolysis? A prospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-086413
