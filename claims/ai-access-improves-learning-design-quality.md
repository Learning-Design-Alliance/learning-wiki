### Pishtari 2027

Pishtari, G., Gnadlinger, F., Ley, T. (2027). When Teachers Use AI Chatbots and Are Trained for It: Impact on Learning Design Quality and Cognitive Effort. In: Blanchard, E.G., Chen, G., Chi, M., Isotani, S. (eds) Artificial Intelligence in Education. AIED 2026. Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol 16585. Springer, Cham. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_28

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects study of thirteen higher education teachers designing learning activities across three conditions; design artifacts were evaluated for "attainment of predefined pedagogical goals". The article reports AI access improved design quality; no effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Results show that AI access improved design quality and significantly lowered perceived cognitive effort."
