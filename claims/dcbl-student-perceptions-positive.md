# Most surveyed dermatology students reported positive perceptions of the DCBL model, with 82.62% satisfied or highly satisfied and over 89% expecting improvements across learning dimensions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an online survey, 82.62% of 129 dermatology students were satisfied or highly satisfied with the DCBL model, and majorities expected improvements in attention (92.2%), learning interest (89.9%), self-directed learning ability (89.9%), and dynamic understanding of lesions (96.9%). ## Evidence

### Li Yidan 2025

Li Yidan, Lei Li, Jiang Ling, Mao Ping, Fu Chuhan, Chen Jing, Zeng Qinghai. (2025). The dynamic case-based learning model: conducive to enhancing the practical learning effectiveness of dermatology for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07474-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Online questionnaire survey of 129 undergraduate medical students (54 males, 75 females) conducted April–May 2024, before any DCBL implementation. Students reported expected outcomes: "with respective percentages being 92.2%, 89.9%, 93%, 89.9%, 94.6%, and 96.9%" for attention, interest, perception, self-directed learning, lesion recognition, and dynamic understanding. These are perceptions, not measured outcomes.

> "Among the participants, 82.62% of students were satisfied or highly satisfied with the new model, with 44.96% indicating high satisfaction and 35.66% indicating satisfaction."
