My Math Academy evidence summary 2022¶

My Math Academy evidence summary. (2022). Age of Learning. https://www.ageoflearning.com/schools/systemcheck/

q2 · i? · review · r1

The summary reports two moderator patterns from the two efficacy studies: largest gains for students with moderate initial math knowledge, and a positive relationship between number of games played and learning gains. No effect sizes are printed for these patterns.

"My Math Academy produced the greatest learning gains in students who began with a moderate level of math knowledge and the more games students played, the greater the learning gains they experienced."
