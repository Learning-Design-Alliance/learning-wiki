`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adherence reporting in the Results section of the crossover study. The article reports 87% (346/396) AB-CST completion versus 69% (272/396) AI-CST completion as intended, and 88% (348/396) completing at least one AI-CST conversation; mean AI-CST time spent was 1 hour 53 (SD 45) minutes against a nominal 3-hour timetable slot.

> "For AB-CST, 50 participants did not complete the intervention due to absence from teaching, meaning 87% (346/396) completed the intervention as intended. For AI-CST, 69% (272/396) completed the intervention as intended (at least 6 total VPS conversations), with 88% (348/396) completing at least 1 conversation."
