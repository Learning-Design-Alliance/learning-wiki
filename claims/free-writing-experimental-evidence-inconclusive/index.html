Smagorinsky 1992¶

The review's synthesis of experimental studies of free writing reports that some studies "achieved significant gains through treatments involving free writing" while many others found no significant improvement over controls. No effect sizes are printed for either set of findings.

"achieved significant gains through treatments involving free writing, yet a great number of studies has found no significant improvement between treatments involving free writing and control treatments"
