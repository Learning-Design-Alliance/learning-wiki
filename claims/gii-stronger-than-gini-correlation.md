## Evidence

### Li Zhang 2024

Li Zhang, Baolige Chao, Yan Gao, Wenjing Wang, Yingzi Yuan, Chuangsheng Chen & Ziqiang Xin. (2024). Gender Inequality is negatively associated with academic achievement for both boys and girls. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00261-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Fisher's Z comparisons of country-level Pearson correlations show GII correlations "signiﬁcantly greater than those between the Gini coefﬁcient and academic achievement" in all three cycles (2012: Z = 1.95, P = 0.025; 2015: Z = 3.53, P < 0.001; 2018: Z = 2.25, P = 0.012); no effect size is printed for this comparison.

> "when Fisher ’s Z test was used to compare correlation coefﬁcients, we found that the co rrelations between GII and academic achievement were signiﬁcantly greater than those between the Gini coefﬁcient and academic achievement (overall mean scores of the three tested subjects)"
