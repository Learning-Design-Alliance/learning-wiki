q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Model-stratified subgroup analysis within the meta-analysis of 39 studies. Copilot pooled accuracy was 75.25% (95% CI: 14.01%-100%, I²=24.9%) and ChatGPT-4 was 72.97% (95% CI: 67.01%-78.57%, I²=87.2%); the between-group test was significant (Q-between = 24.44, P = .011).

"Subgroup analyses stratified by architecture demonstrated statistically significant differences in performance (Q-between = 24.44, P = .011). Copilot (GPT family interface) and ChatGPT-4 (OpenAI interface) achieved the highest pooled estimates (Copilot: 75.25%; 95% CI: 14.01%-100%; ChatGPT4: 72.97%; 95% CI: 67.01%-78.57%)"
