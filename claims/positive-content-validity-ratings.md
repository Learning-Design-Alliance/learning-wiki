## Evidence

### De Witte Benjamin 2021

De Witte Benjamin, Barnouin Charles, Moreau Richard, Lelevé Arnaud, Martin Xavier, Collet Christian, Hoyek Nady. (2021). A haptic laparoscopic trainer based on affine velocity analysis: engineering and preliminary results. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01128-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content evidence validation using a 25-item questionnaire on a 10-point rating scale completed after the two exercises by experts, intermediates and inexperienced interns; ANOVA gave p = 0.13 (NS). "The internal consistency of the content evidence questionnaire revealed a high Cronbach alpha coefficient (0.87)."

> "All participants positively rated the simulator whatever the group of inclusion, with mean (±SD) scores being 7.25 ± 0.8, 7.74 ± 0.6, and 7.72 ± 0.7 for the expert, intermediate and inexperienced levels, respectively."
