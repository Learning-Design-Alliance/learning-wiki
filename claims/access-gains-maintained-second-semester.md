`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Discussion of the two-semester RCT design: between-group effect sizes on self-reported academic skills increased from post-treatment to post-maintenance, indicating continued growth during the faded-support maintenance phase.

> "ratings of self-reported academic skills demonstrated small to moderate between-group differences at immediate post-treatment (ds from .12 to .46) that continued to increase in the following semester with moderate to large between-group differences in skills at the post-maintenance assessment (ds from .43 to .70)."
