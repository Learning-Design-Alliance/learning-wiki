## Evidence

### Lima Rodrigo A. 2020

Lima Rodrigo A., Stodden David F., Pfeiffer Karin A., Larsen Lisbeth R., Barros Mauro V. G., Bugge Anna, Andersen Lars B. (2020). Dynamic Balance, but Not Precision Throw, Is Positively Associated with Academic Performance in Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17082790

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sex-stratified SEM in the same longitudinal sample. The article reports the girls' direct, mediated and total coefficients with confidence intervals and states "waist circumference mediated 20% of the total association between balance and academic performance".

> "For girls, balance presented direct (β = 0.098; 95% CI: 0.053 to 0.144), mediated (β = 0.025; 95% CI: 0.008 to 0.042—via waist circumference) and total association with academic performance (β = 0.123; 95% CI: 0.061 to 0.186). Thus, waist circumference mediated 20% of the total association between balance and academic performance."
