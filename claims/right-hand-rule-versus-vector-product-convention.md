Observation from the written survey: most American students talked about finding direction using the right-hand rule, and the authors report not seeing one statement talking about the vector product among them.

> "It seems that American students are used more to the right hand rule, while Romanian students more to the vector product. None of the Romanian students had any statement related to the right hand rule."
