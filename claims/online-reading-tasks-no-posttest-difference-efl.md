### Altay 2017

Altay, İ. F., & Altay, A. (2017). The impact of online reading tasks and reading strategies on EFL learners' reading test scores. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 13(2), 136-152. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1159168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample t-test comparing post-test scores of the 25-student experiment group and 26-student control group in the quasi-experiment. The article reports no significant difference between "experiment group (M=20.72, SD=3.82) and control group (M=19.73, SD=4.99)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "It was found that there was not a statistically significant difference between the post-test scores of experiment group (M=20.72, SD=3.82) and control group (M=19.73, SD=4.99); t(46.71)=0.796, p=0.430."
