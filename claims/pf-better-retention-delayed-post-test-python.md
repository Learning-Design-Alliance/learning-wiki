## Evidence

### Hussel Suriyaarachchi 2025

Hussel Suriyaarachchi, Paul Denny, and Suranga Nanayakkara. 2025. Investigating the Use of Productive Failure as a Design Paradigm for Learning Introductory Python Programming. Proceedings of the 56th ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education V. 1 (SIGCSE TS 2025). https://doi.org/10.1145/3641554.3701911

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-week delayed post-test in a controlled study (DI n=9, PF n=7): three DI students failed the heart-rate programming task, whereas "All students in the experimental group (PF) showed greater persistence in maintaining their initial success on the heart-rate data problem" (Table 1: PF 7/7).

> "A total of three out of nine students failed to reproduce the expected solution for the task."
