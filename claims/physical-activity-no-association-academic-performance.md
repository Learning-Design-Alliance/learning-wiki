# Moderate-to-vigorous physical activity shows no significant association with academic performance in this sample

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No significant association was detected between moderate-to-vigorous physical activity and academic performance, neither by ANOVA nor in the regression model. [→ Heller Sebastian 2024](#heller-sebastian-2024)

## Evidence

### Heller Sebastian 2024

Heller Sebastian, Reichel Jennifer L., Mülder Lina M., Schäfer Markus, Schwab Lisa, Werner Antonia M., Letzel Stephan, Rigotti Thomas, Dietz Pavel. (2024). The association between health behaviours and academic performance moderated by trait mindfulness amongst university students: an observational study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1340235

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA (N = 1,049) and stepwise linear regression (Model 1, Step 4) both found no significant association between moderate-to-vigorous physical activity and academic performance. The ANOVA yielded p = 0.565, η2 = 0.002. The authors suggest the sample was quite physically active (57.1% highly active).

> "Interestingly, no significant association (not even a single association by ANOVA) between moderate-to-vigorous physical activity and academic performance was detected in this study, in contrast to previous studies that reported strong associations between these constructs."
