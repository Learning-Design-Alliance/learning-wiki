Wetzel N (2014) Development of control of attention from different perspectives. Front. Psychol. 5:1000. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.01000

`q2 · i?`

The editorial reports, citing Heim et al. (2013), a rapid serial visual presentation paradigm measuring accuracy of target detection in early adolescence. Age-related differences in distraction effects and capacity limits are discussed in terms of alternative cognitive strategies.

> "The authors conclude from results that processing of affective stimuli is prioritized and interferes with subsequent target processing during the critical attentional blink interval."
