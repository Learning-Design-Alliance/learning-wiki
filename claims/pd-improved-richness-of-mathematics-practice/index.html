Evidence¶

Garet 2016¶

Garet, M. S., Heppen, J. B., Walters, K., Parkinson, J., Smith, T. M., Song, M., Garrett, R., Yang, R., & Borman, G. D. (2016). Focusing on mathematical knowledge: The impact of content-intensive teacher professional development, executive summary (NCEE 2016-4009). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

q4 · i?

Spring 2014 MQI scoring of video-recorded lessons (79 treatment teachers with 158 lessons; 86 control teachers with 172 lessons). The report states this 17 percent difference corresponds to an improvement of 23 percentile points, significant at the 0.05 level.

"An average treatment teacher demonstrated Richness of Mathematics at a mid or high level during 63 percent of a typical lesson, compared with 46 percent for an average control teacher"

q4 · i?

Same spring 2014 MQI video analysis; the report notes a negative impact on Errors is expected because it corresponds to a decrease in Errors and Imprecision. No effect sizes beyond improvement indices are printed.

"The impact on Student Participation in Mathematics and Errors and Imprecision in the spring was in the expected direction but not statistically significant (improvement of 6 and −9 percentile points"

q4 · i? · causal · r2

Fall 2013 MQI video analysis from the same RCT, measured after Intel Math but before the Mathematics Learning Community and Video Feedback Cycles. The report also states impacts on practice did not vary by teachers' prior math knowledge.

"We also observed a statistically significant impact on Student Participation in Mathematics in the fall (after Intel Math but before the other two PD components) corresponding to an improvement index of 11 percentile points, but it was not sustained into the spring"

Related Claims¶

  • A 93-hour content-intensive math PD program improved fourth-grade teachers' math content knowledge — related

  • Content-intensive math PD produced a statistically significant, sustained gain in grade 4 teachers' math content knowledge (21 percentile points in spring) — related
  • The 93-hour PD was implemented with high fidelity, with 96 percent of Intel Math hours and 100 percent of the school-year components delivered — related
  • Teacher knowledge and most instructional-practice dimensions were not correlated with student achievement; only Errors and Imprecision was, at −0.20 — related
  • Despite gains in teacher knowledge and practice, the PD did not improve student math achievement; treatment students scored 2 percentile points lower — related
