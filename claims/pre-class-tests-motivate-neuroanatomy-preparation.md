## Evidence

### Ekpenyong 2026

Ekpenyong, E. E., Ikechukwu, M. S., Adediran, F. A., Kelechi, C. F., Eniola, A.-I. O., & Oloruntoba, A. A. (2026). Optimising pre-class tests to enhance student engagement in neuroanatomy: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education, 26, 1036.

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 144 third-year students in a neuroanatomy course at a Nigerian university. Descriptive Likert-scale results show "80.5% who reported increased motivation"; no inferential test or effect size is reported.

> "When asked whether the tests motivated them to prepare more thoroughly for each class, 52 students (36.1%) strongly agreed, and 64 students (44.4%) agreed, yielding a combined rate of 80.5% who reported increased motivation."
