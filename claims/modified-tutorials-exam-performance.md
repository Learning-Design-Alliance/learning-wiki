Michael C. Wittmann, Richard N. Steinberg, Edward F. Redish. (2002). Investigating student understanding of quantum physics: Spontaneous models of conductivity. American Journal of Physics 70:3, 218-226. Originally published 2001. [doi:10.1119/1.1447542](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1447542)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of three consecutive Physics 420 classes (N=12 traditional, N=24 modified 1, N=12 modified 2) on an identical final exam question (Fig. 7). Modified 2 scored 100% band diagram, 83% Drude, 83% both; modified 1 scored 46/33/8. All classes spent roughly the same class time on conductivity.

> "in the traditional class, 58% answered only the band diagram part correctly, 33% answered only the Drude model part correctly, and 25% answered both correctly."
