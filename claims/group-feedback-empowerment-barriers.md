## Evidence

### Berger B 2010

Berger B, Steckelberg A, Meyer G, Kasper J, Mühlhauser I. (2010). Training of patient and consumer representatives in the basic competencies of evidence-based medicine: a feasibility study. BMC Medical Education 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-16

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative content analysis of Metaplan card group-based feedback from all 14 courses, one of six reported achievements alongside higher self-confidence and empowerment through statistical literacy.

> "Participants realised that EBM is a method for critical appraisal of scientific studies which can be used to a certain extent even by laypersons."

### Berger B 2010 (2)

Berger B, Steckelberg A, Meyer G, Kasper J, Mühlhauser I. (2010). Training of patient and consumer representatives in the basic competencies of evidence-based medicine: a feasibility study. BMC Medical Education 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-16

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Barrier reported in the follow-up interview content analysis. Other stated barriers included lack of further support, limited possibilities to exchange experiences, language barriers, and limited access to publications.

> "Several self-help group members felt discouraged to use their new skills because they had experienced negative reactions from professionals if they raised critical questions concerning therapeutic issues."
