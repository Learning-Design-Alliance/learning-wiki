`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Review examination of study characteristics regarding participant age. The review reports that although some authors argue younger children may not be vulnerable, "studies including participants in middle childhood still found medium and large effects of SLD stigma on psychological adjustment," and the single adult-out-of-school study (Abraham et al., 2002) found large stigma–self-esteem effects.

> "This trend was not borne out in the present review, however—studies including participants in middle childhood still found medium and large effects of SLD stigma on psychological adjustment (Chan et al., 2017; Heyman, 1990; Maki, 2021)."
