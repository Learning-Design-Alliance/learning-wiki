## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across six applicant cohorts, the NCM required a higher minimum UMAT score and ATAR mark for invitation to interview than the CM. [→ Shulruf Boaz 2020](#shulruf-boaz-2020)

## Evidence

### Shulruf Boaz 2020

Shulruf Boaz, O'Sullivan Anthony, Velan Gary. (2020). Selecting top candidates for medical school selection interviews- a non-compensatory approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02031-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Threshold comparison of the two ranking models applied to six consecutive cohorts of eligible UNSW Medicine applicants. The CM threshold was "UMAT ≥53.67 and ATAR ≥95.9", whereas the NCM "set the threshold much higher (UMAT ≥57.67 and ATAR ≥98.2)". Thresholds are reported descriptively; no effect size or significance test is printed for this contrast.

> "Across the entire six cohorts, the threshold for selection for interview set by the CM was UMAT ≥53.67 and ATAR ≥95.9, whereas applying the NCM model set the threshold much higher (UMAT ≥57.67 and ATAR ≥98.2)."
