## Evidence

### Menzies et al. 2016

Victoria Menzies, Adetayo Kasim, Dimitra Kokotsaki, Catherine Hewitt, Nasima Akhter, Clare Collyer, Kirsty Younger, Andy Wiggins and Carole Torgerson. (2016). Hallé SHINE on Manchester: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581134

`q4 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Three two-armed cluster RCTs (pilot 361 pupils in 18 schools; main trial 2,306 pupils in 38 schools) with random allocation at year-group level, analysed by multi-level modelling on intention-to-treat. Year 1 effect size was "0.03 (-0.27, 0.34)"; Year 2 -0.10; Year 3 0.10, all with confidence intervals spanning zero.

> "There is no evidence that the Hallé Shine on Manchester programme had an impact on the reading attainment of children in the trial. This finding was consistent across all age groups, for pupils eligible for free school meals, and for all three years of the trials."
