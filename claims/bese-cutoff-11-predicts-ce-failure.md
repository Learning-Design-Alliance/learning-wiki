## Evidence

### Wallihan Rebecca 2018

Wallihan Rebecca, Smith Keely G., Hormann Mark D., Donthi Rajesh R., Boland Kimberly, Mahan John D. (2018). Utility of intermittent online quizzes as an early warning for residents at risk of failing the pediatric board certification examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1366-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ROC analysis in Results using ABP CE failure as the outcome, with predictive values computed at hypothetical prevalences (Table 2). The PGY-1 AUC was 0.77 and the composite AUC 0.69; the ≤11 cutoff gave 54% sensitivity and 80% specificity.

> "The area under the curve (AUC) for PGY-1 Score was 0.77 (p = 0.0011), indicating fair accuracy in predicting ABP CE failure. The AUC for Composite Score was 0.69 (p = 0.013), indicating poor accuracy. A cutoff score of 11 was chosen as this is nearest to the 25th percentile of all composite scores (11.02)."
