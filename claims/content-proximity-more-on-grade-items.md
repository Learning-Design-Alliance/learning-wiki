`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results from the spring 2022 pilot study comparing pilot and propensity-score-matched comparison groups showed significantly more on-grade items; the percentage of on-grade items in math "increased by at least 20% in grade 3-8" and in reading in all grades.

> "There were significantly more on-grade items in the pilot study group. The percentage of on-grade items in math increased by at least 20% in grade 3-8. The percentage of on-grade items in reading increased by at least 20% in all grades."
