## Evidence

### Bakhos David 2020

Bakhos David, Galvin John, Aoustin Jean-Marie, Robier Mathieu, Kerneis Sandrine, Bechet Garance, Montembault Norbert, Laurent Stéphane, Godey Benoit, Aussedat Charles. (2020). Training outcomes for audiology students using virtual reality or traditional training methods. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0243380

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Four-item VR quality survey (Fig 4) administered to both groups after VR training using a 5-point Likert scale; large majorities rated VR as more realistic and immersive than theoretical courses and fully complementary to them.

> "Compared to theoretical courses, students agreed (a rating of 4) or strongly agreed (a rating of 5) that the VR training was more realistic (93%) and immersive (100%) than theoretical course work."
