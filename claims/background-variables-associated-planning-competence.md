## Evidence

### Karsikas 2025

Karsikas, E., Meriläinen, M., Koivunen, K., & Kanste, O. (2025). Health and social care managers' self‐assessed competence in knowledge management: A descriptive cross‐sectional study. Journal of Advanced Nursing, 81, 787–797. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16237

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann–Whitney U test on survey data (N=116): healthcare setting was significantly associated with the planning competence development and cooperation dimension at p = .036; medians were 2.938 (specialized) vs 2.438 (primary).

> "The managers from public specialized medical care, such as university hospitals, demonstrated higher competence compared to those in public primary health and social care settings, such as health clinics (p = .036)."

### Karsikas 2025 (2)

Karsikas, E., Meriläinen, M., Koivunen, K., & Kanste, O. (2025). Health and social care managers' self‐assessed competence in knowledge management: A descriptive cross‐sectional study. Journal of Advanced Nursing, 81, 787–797. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16237

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis test on survey data (N=116): number of units managed was significantly associated with the planning dimension at p = .035; median for ≥3 units was 3.094.

> "Additionally, managers responsible for three or more units exhibited higher competence compared to those managing only one or two units (p = .035)."

### Karsikas 2025 (3)

Karsikas, E., Meriläinen, M., Koivunen, K., & Kanste, O. (2025). Health and social care managers' self‐assessed competence in knowledge management: A descriptive cross‐sectional study. Journal of Advanced Nursing, 81, 787–797. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16237

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann–Whitney U test on survey data (N=116): number of direct staff was significantly associated with the planning dimension at p = .028; medians 2.563 (≤50) vs 3.000 (>50).

> "Those with 50 or fewer direct staff demonstrated weaker competence in planning competence development and cooperation compared to those with over 50 direct staff (p = .028)."
