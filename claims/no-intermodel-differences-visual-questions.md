Results-section analysis of the 22 visually-based items: "Cochran's Q was not significant (p = 0.104), so no individual contrasts survived adjustment". The authors note few exclusive correct responses by any one model on OT-specific visuals, aligning with this null.

> "For the visually-based questions, the ranking changed little, Gemini 2.5 68.2%, OpenAI o3 63.6%, GPT-4.5 54.6%, Claude 3.7 50.0%, but Cochran's Q was not significant (p = 0.104), so no individual contrasts survived adjustment (Tables 2 and 3)."
