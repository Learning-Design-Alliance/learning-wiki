# More solutions are generated in the OC condition than the AU condition, and adults generate more answers than adolescents in general, with no condition-specific age difference

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` A mixed-model ANOVA revealed an interaction of condition × number with more solutions in OC than AU (η2 = 0.56), and an interaction of number × age group with more answers by adults (η2 = 0.12). [→ Kleibeuker 2013](#kleibeuker-2013)
`q3 i?` No significant condition × number × age group interaction, indicating adults were not specifically more creative but generated more answers in general. [→ Kleibeuker 2013 (2)](#kleibeuker-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Kleibeuker 2013

Kleibeuker, S. W., Koolschijn, P. C. M. P., Jolles, D. D., De Dreu, C. K. W., and Crone, E. A. (2013). The neural coding of creative idea generation across adolescence and early adulthood. Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 7:905. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2013.00905

`q3 · i3`

Mixed-model ANOVA on scanner task performance with age group as between-subjects factor. The study reports "more solutions generated in the OC condition compared to the AU condition" (η2 = 0.56) and "more answers generated by adults than by adolescents" (η2 = 0.12).

> "an interaction effect of condition × number with more solutions generated in the OC condition compared to the AU condition [F(3, 123) = 58.81, p < 0.001; η2 = 0.56], and an interaction effect of number × age group [F(3, 123) = 5.44, p = 0.009; η2 = 0.12] with more answers generated by adults than by adolescents"

### Kleibeuker 2013 (2)

Kleibeuker, S. W., Koolschijn, P. C. M. P., Jolles, D. D., De Dreu, C. K. W., and Crone, E. A. (2013). The neural coding of creative idea generation across adolescence and early adulthood. Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 7:905. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2013.00905

`q3 · i?`

The three-way interaction of condition × number × age group was not significant (F(3, 123) = 0.64, p = 0.56, η2 = 0.02), which the authors interpret as adults generating more answers in general rather than being specifically more creative.

> "There was no signiﬁcant condition x number x age group interaction effect [F(3, 123) = 0.64, p = 0.56, η2 = 0.02], indicat- ing that adults were not speciﬁcally more creative, but generated more answers in general."
