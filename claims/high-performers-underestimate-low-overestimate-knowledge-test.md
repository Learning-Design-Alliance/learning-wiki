## Evidence

### Mehdizadeh Leila 2014

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Myers Gil, Khatib Yasmin, Dacre Jane. (2014). How well do doctors think they perform on the General Medical Council's Tests of Competence pilot examinations? A cross-sectional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2013-004131

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Tertile analysis (top, middle, bottom) of estimated versus actual knowledge test scores among the 524 participating doctors, analysed with t tests. The article reports the highest performers underestimated by an average of 8 marks (t (196)=−7.70, p<0.01) and lower performers overestimated by an average of 7 marks (t (172)=6.09, p<0.01).

> "The highest performers significantly underestimated their knowledge test scores by an average of 8 marks (t (196)=−7.70, p<0.01) and the lower performers significantly overestimated by an average of 7 marks (t (172)=6.09, p<0.01)."
