## Evidence

### Ferdinand 2024

Ferdinand, R., Malanchini, M. & Rimfeld, K. (2024). Mathematics interest, self-efficacy, and anxiety predict STEM career choice in emerging adulthood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00275-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sex-stratified logistic regressions in TEDS. The article reports unadjusted male OR = 0.62 [0.42, 0.89] vs female OR = 0.88 [0.67, 1.16]; the Wald test (X2 = 2.26, p = 0.13) indicates the difference between sexes was not significant.

> "A Wald Chi-square test showed that maths anxiety was not a signi ﬁcantly better pre- dictor of STEM career choice in the male sample than in the female sample (X2 = 2.26, p = 0.13)"
