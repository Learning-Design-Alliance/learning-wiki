## Evidence

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2010

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2010). Refined Characterization of Student Perspectives on Quantum Physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.6.020113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.6.020113)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Aggregate analysis of end-of-term online survey responses across four courses with differing instructor stances. The authors conclude "instructor practices do influence student thinking," while noting a large fraction of all courses agreed atomic electrons have definite but unknown positions.

> "Students from the Matter-Wave course overwhelmingly preferred a wave-packet (Quantum) description of electrons in the double-slit experiment (each electron passes through both slits and interferes with itself), while responses from the three Copenhagen/Agnostic courses were more varied."
