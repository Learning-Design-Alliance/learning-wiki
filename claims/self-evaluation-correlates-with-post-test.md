## Evidence

### Cheng Meng 2025

Cheng Meng, Wang Xinle, Zhang Man, Li Sainan, Geng Cuizhi. (2025). Video based on the combination of animation and modules is effective in teaching suture technologies to novices without faculty involvement: a new surgical video course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06915-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Consistency test using Spearman's rank correlation between pre/post-course self-assessment and post-course test scores (Table 3). Correlations ranged up to ρ = 0.743 (HS) and ρ = 0.612 (LS), with all coefficients significant at the 0.05 or 0.01 level; the largest printed value is 0.743.
