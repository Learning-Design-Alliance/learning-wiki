### Brady D. Lund and Zoë Abbie Teel 2026

Brady D. Lund and Zoë Abbie Teel. (2026). AI Literacy: An Exercise in Power-Knowledge. University of North Texas. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27547

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument acknowledging AI's genuine democratizing potential: expert knowledge such as legal research, medical information, and literature synthesis is now accessible with modest resources. The authors contrast a researcher accelerating sophisticated knowledge production with a limited-education user getting answers without expanded capacity to produce or contest knowledge.

> "The productive capacity of AI is a multiplier – it amplifies the existing capabilities of those who can fully access and interrogate it, while offering more modest gains to those who cannot."
