Descriptive comparison of disciplinary acts by ELL program status in the district dataset, shown in Figure 3. Non-ELL students averaged 0.04 suspensions and 0.13 referrals; ELL students averaged 0.01 and 0.04 respectively.

"English Language Learners (ELLs) had significantly fewer disciplinary acts compared to non-ELLs. Students in the non-ELL group averaged 0.04 suspensions and 0.13 referrals per student, while students in the ELL group had a lower average of 0.01 suspensions and 0.04 referrals."
