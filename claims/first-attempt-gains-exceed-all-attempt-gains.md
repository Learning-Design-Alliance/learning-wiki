## Evidence

### Devang Kulshreshtha 2022

Devang Kulshreshtha, Muhammad Shayan, Robert Belfer, Siva Reddy, Iulian Vlad Serban, Ekaterina Kochmar. (2022). Few-shot Question Generation for Personalized Feedback in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv. [doi:10.3233/faia220062](https://doi.org/10.3233/faia220062)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison across the Korbit ITS deployment in Table 6, where every feedback model shows higher first-attempt gains (e.g., 66.67 vs 52.27 for question-based feedback) than all-attempt gains. The authors offer a knowledge-gaps explanation, which is interpretation rather than a tested result.

> "For all models, we observe that learning gains for 'First Attempt' are more than 'All Attempts'. This is likely because students who require many hints to solve an exercise may have knowledge gaps to solve exercises."
