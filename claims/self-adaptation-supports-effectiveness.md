App-level factor synthesis across eleven articles coding self-adaptation: the review reports positive association with effectiveness, while Foster et al. (2018) and Parks & Tortorelli (2021) argued that "without such adaptive learning designs, the effectiveness of digital mathematical games may become insignificant".
