## Evidence

### Ricky Ellis 2022

Ricky Ellis, Peter A Brennan, Amanda J Lee, Duncan SG Scrimgeour, Jennifer Cleland. (2022). Differential attainment at MRCS according to gender, ethnicity, age and socioeconomic factors: a retrospective cohort study. Journal of the Royal Society of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1177/01410768221079018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariate logistic regression in a retrospective cohort of 5780 UK MRCS Part A candidates (final model N = 1880) linked to UKMED data, adjusting for EPM decile scores and all sociodemographic factors. The article reports "being male (OR 2.34, 95% CI 1.87–2.92)" as an independent predictor of Part A success; no standardised effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "being male (OR 2.34, 95% CI 1.87–2.92) or a non-graduate (OR 1.98, 95% CI 1.44–2.74) were found to be the only factors that independently predict MRCS Part A success after adjusting for all sociodemographic factors."
