`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test (5,000 samples, 95% CI) on the 1,193-student survey found the indirect effect through class environment and mastery approach goal was 0.11, 95% CI[0.09, 0.13], the largest of the four goal-specific indirect effects in Table 4.

> "class environment is significantly positively related to mastery approach goal (β = 0.36, p < 0.001), and mastery approach goal is significantly positively related to learning engagement (β = 0.64, p < 0.001). The indirect effect was 0.11, 95% CI[0.09, 0.13]."
