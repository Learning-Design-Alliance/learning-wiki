`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomized trial, Results section. The dichotomized HIV transmission risk score (score >1 vs ≤1) combined unprotected contacts, viral load and STI diagnosis. Quote reports "ratio of ORs 9.01, CI: 1.78 to 45.71; P = 0.008"; nonsignificant at 6 months (ratio of ORs 1.31; P = 0.67).

> "Using "lower transmission risk" as outcome variable yielded a significant difference between the 2 groups, 3 months after the intervention. The OR of success at 3 months compared with baseline was 1.28 in the control group and 11.53 in the CISS group (ratio of ORs 9.01, CI: 1.78 to 45.71; P = 0.008)."
