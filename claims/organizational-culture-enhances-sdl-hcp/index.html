Berger-Estilita 2025¶

Berger-Estilita, Joana; Linda, Krista; Gogollari, Artemisa; Schmitz, Felix; Elfering, Achim; Guttormsen, Sissel (2025). Self-directed learning in health professions: A mixed-methods systematic review of the literature. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0320530

The review's narrative synthesis of 34 studies involving over 5,700 healthcare professionals, including nurses, pharmacists, and physicians, reports that "supportive organizational cultures significantly enhance SDL practices, whereas restrictive policies hinder their effectiveness."
