Evidence¶

Patsy M. Lightbown and Nina Spada 1990¶

Patsy M. Lightbown and Nina Spada. (1990). Focus-on-Form and Corrective Feedback in Communicative Language Teaching: Effects on Second Language Learning. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 12, Issue 4. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263100009517

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Analysis of a large corpus of classroom observation data from four intact classes in Quebec intensive ESL programs; the authors report only "some evidence" that accuracy differences were "due to differences in teachers' form-focused instruction". No effect size is printed.

"There was some evidence that these differences (which were not correlated with performance on listening comprehension tests) were due to differences in teachers' form-focused instruction."
