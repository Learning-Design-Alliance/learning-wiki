## Evidence

### Hao 2025

Hao, Z., Jiang, J., Yu, J., Liu, Z., & Zhang, Y. (2025). Student-AI Interaction in an LLM-Empowered Learning Environment: A Cluster Analysis of Engagement Profiles. arXiv:2503.01694v3. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694)

Within-cluster paired comparisons of pre- and post-course questionnaires on attitudes toward AI. Only z statistics and p-values are printed; no effect size is reported, so no magnitude is asserted.

> "The Wilcoxon test indicated a significant increase in attitudes towards AI in Cluster 4 (z= 2.31 , p=.02 ) and a potentially significant increase in Cluster 3 (z= 1.93 , p=.05 )."

### Hao 2025 (2)

Hao, Z., Jiang, J., Yu, J., Liu, Z., & Zhang, Y. (2025). Student-AI Interaction in an LLM-Empowered Learning Environment: A Cluster Analysis of Engagement Profiles. arXiv:2503.01694v3. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.01694)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-cluster ANCOVA models on post-course non-cognitive measures with pre-questionnaire data as covariate found no significant differences among clusters; the null results do not establish equivalence.

> "the ANCOV A models indicated that there were no significant differences among clusters across Motivation (F(2,104) = 0.22, p=.80 ), Academic Self-Efficacy (F(2,104) = 0.99, p=.37 ) and Attitudes towards AI (F(2,104) = 0.63,p=.53)."
