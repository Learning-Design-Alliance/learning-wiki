## Evidence

### Kjellenberg Karin 2025

Kjellenberg Karin, Helgadóttir Björg, Ekblom Örjan, Nyberg Gisela. (2025). Fitness and Screen Time at Age 13 Relates to Academic Performance at Age 16. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.70024

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel logistic regression (schools as clusters) in a longitudinal cohort of 1139 Swedish adolescents, adjusted for gender, parental education, parental country of birth and age-13 grades; the article reports "odds ratio: 1.038" per 1 mL fitness increase, p = 0.003 in Table 3 (n = 914).

> "Fitness level at age 13 was significantly related to academic performance at age 16 across the entire sample. More specifically, for each 1 mL increase in fitness, the odds of receiving grade A–D at age 16 was 4% higher (odds ratio: 1.038)."
