`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Within the same observational study of 40 faculty surgeons, per-module paired hand comparisons are reported in Results. For Ring Transfer, mean times were 117.5 (SD 26.7) s dominant vs 151.3 (SD 38.1) s nondominant, and the article reports "dominant and nondominant hand performance differed significantly (P<.001)". Similar hand differences are printed for Maze (P=.02) and Suture Passing (P<.001).

> "dominant and nondominant hand performance differed significantly (P<.001), and the benchmark was <134 seconds with no more than 1 dropped ring."
