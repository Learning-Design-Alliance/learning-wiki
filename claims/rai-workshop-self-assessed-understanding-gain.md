## Evidence

### Julia Stoyanovich 2025

Julia Stoyanovich, Rodrigo Kreis de Paula, Armanda Lewis, Chloe Zheng. (2025). Using Case Studies to Teach Responsible AI to Industry Practitioners. [doi:10.1609/aaai.v39i28.35177](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v39i28.35177)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre-/post-workshop self-assessment of RAI knowledge on a 10-point scale; 38 completed the pre-workshop survey and 16 completed both. The article reports "a weighted average of 4.88" pre versus 6.94 post, with 77% reporting knowledge very to substantially improved and 100% motivated to apply content.

> "Participants rated their post-workshop understanding of RAI concepts and techniques significantly higher, on average, than their pre-workshop understanding: a weighted average of 4.88 (for all participants, n = 38) or 4.84 (for participants who submitted both surveys, n = 16) versus 6.94."
