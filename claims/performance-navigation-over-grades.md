### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026

Arya VarastehNezhad, Fattaneh Taghiyareh. (2026). Behavioral and Performance Indicators of Depression and Anxiety in Electronic Learning Systems. Department of Computer Engineering, University of Tehran. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Academic-performance analysis in Group 2: both differences did not reach conventional significance; "Anxious students had a mean project score of 98.42 compared with 81.44" (d=0.622) and depressed students 97.07 vs 80.05 (d=0.627), indicating no poorer project performance.

> "Anxious students had a mean project score of 98.42 compared with 81.44 for non -anxious students (p=0.0852, d=0.622), while depressed students had a mean project score of 97.07 compared with 80.05 for non -depressed students (p=0.0696, d=0.627)."

### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026 (2)

Arya VarastehNezhad, Fattaneh Taghiyareh. (2026). Behavioral and Performance Indicators of Depression and Anxiety in Electronic Learning Systems. Department of Computer Engineering, University of Tehran. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Group 1 Spearman correlation analysis of performance-related LMS navigation: "lower engagement with quiz -related pages" among higher-anxiety students, plus negative correlations between depression and homework grade-announcement and final-project page visits (e.g., ρ=-0.334, p=0.0091).

> "higher anxiety was associated with lower engagement with quiz -related pages, especially quiz -attempt-report pages, with negative correlations for both anxiety score (ρ=-0.458, p=0.00023) and anxiety status (ρ=-0.377, p=0.0030)"

### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026 (3)

Arya VarastehNezhad, Fattaneh Taghiyareh. (2026). Behavioral and Performance Indicators of Depression and Anxiety in Electronic Learning Systems. Department of Computer Engineering, University of Tehran. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Group 2 navigation analysis: "a lower proportion of exam -related activity" with higher depression, alongside positive correlations of depression score with homework-solution-page visits (ρ=0.390, p=0.0169) and grade-announcement visits (ρ=0.357, p=0.0299).

> "Higher depression was linked to a lower proportion of exam -related activity, as shown by negative correlations for both depression score (ρ=-0.391, p=0.0166) and depression status (ρ=-0.361, p=0.0282)."
