## Evidence

### Ekmark Sergel S 2026

Ekmark Sergel S, Lundberg M, Magnusson C, Ådén U, Bergman G, Siljehav V. (2026). Academic Achievements in Adolescents With Congenital Heart Disease: A Total Population‐Based Cohort Study. Journal of the American Heart Association. https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.126.049927

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Total-population Swedish register cohort of 1,800,477 singletons born 1987–2005 with 16,075 CHD cases, analyzed with multivariable Poisson regression adjusted for sex, birth year, parental age, education, birth country, and family income. The Results report that qualification for USS "demonstrated a linear association with the complexity of CHD (Figure 1)" with major noncardiac anomalies excluded.

> "The primary outcome measure, qualification for USS, in children without noncardiac MCAs, under the assumption that those without any grade were deemed ineligible nonqualified, demonstrated a linear association with the complexity of CHD (Figure 1)."
