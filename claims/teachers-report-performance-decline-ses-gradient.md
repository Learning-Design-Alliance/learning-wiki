### Aymee Alvarez-Rivero 2023

Aymee Alvarez-Rivero, Candice Odgers and Daniel Ansari. (2023). Elementary school teachers' perspectives about learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. npj Science of Learning 8:40. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00191-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on overall student performance during the pandemic; the article reports the proportion of teachers perceiving worse performance and states there were no country differences (X2 = 2.97, p < 0.227). No effect size is printed for the overall drop.

> "More than 50% of teachers indicated that children in their class performed worse than in previous years (Fig. 1a)."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`
