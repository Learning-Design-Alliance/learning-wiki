Gao Junwei 2020¶

Gao Junwei, Yang Ling, Zhao Jinghui, Wang Lian, Zou Jiao, Wang Chunxiang, Fan Xiaotang. (2020). Comparison of problem-based learning and traditional teaching methods in medical psychology education in China: A systematic review and meta-analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0243897

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Sensitivity analysis removing each study sequentially to assess its influence on the pooled Hedges' g. The article reports no single study affected the significant difference; no effect size is printed for this analysis.

"The results showed that no single study could affect the statistically significant difference in total examination scores between students in PBL groups and controls"
