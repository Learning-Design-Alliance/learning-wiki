## Evidence

### Lieberoth 2015

Lieberoth, A., Pedersen, M. K., & Sherson, J. F. (2015). Play or Science? A Study of Learning and Framing in Crowdscience. Aarhus University, Centre for Community Driven Research (CODER). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1510.06841](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1510.06841)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlation matrix entering GUI-description categories and precision alongside the likert scales from the 38-student post-play survey. Autonomy "was very highly correlated with the proportion of correct physics descriptions given, r = .61**"; physics interest correlated r = .58** with the general number of correct descriptions.

> "autonomy showed up as the only interesting factor: It was very highly correlated with the proportion of correct physics descriptions given, r = .61**, while physics interest was only correlated with the general number of correct descriptions given r = .58**."
