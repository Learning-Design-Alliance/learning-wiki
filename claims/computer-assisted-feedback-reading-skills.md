The review reports, citing Murphy (2010), a study of 267 first-year university EFL majors in Japan using web-based collaborative reading exercises with computer-mediated feedback. The review states computer-assisted elaborative feedback "helped the students sharpen their reading skills"; no effect size is printed.

> "In a study of 267 ﬁrst-year university EFL majors in Japan, Murphy (2010) found that by enabling the students to self-check their mistakes and exchange ideas between partners, computer-assisted elaborative feedback helped the students sharpen their reading skills, and produce more quality interaction between pairs."
