## Evidence

### Isik U. 2018

Isik U., Wilschut J., Croiset G., Kusurkar R.A. (2018). The role of study strategy in motivation and academic performance of ethnic minority and majority students: a structural equation model. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-018-9840-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM with clerkship performance as outcome (n = 618); the authors attribute the null to measurement scope: the SRQ-A measured motivation for studying medicine in general and the SPQ does not measure study strategy in a clinical setting. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The findings showed that autonomous motivation, controlled motivation, and study strategy were not associated with clinical performance."
