### Joshua S. Cetron 2020

Joshua S. Cetron, Andrew C. Connolly, Solomon G. Diamond, Vicki V. May, James V. Haxby and David J. M. Kraemer. (2020). Using the force: STEM knowledge and experience construct shared neural representations of engineering concepts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0065-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same multivariate intersubject correlation and RSA methods applied to the fourth and final fMRI run in the same N = 31 sample showed fewer unique informational networks per group and convergence almost entirely localized to vOT. Convergent representations were "no longer as strongly re ﬂective of mechanical category information" but remained as strongly reflective of visual feature information as at run 1.

> "Finally, results from the RSA for run 4 revealed that those convergent representations that did emerge for each group were no longer as strongly re ﬂective of mechanical category information (but remained as strongly reﬂective of visual feature information as at run 1; Supplementary Fig. 3)."
