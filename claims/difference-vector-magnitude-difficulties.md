## Evidence

### Wilcox 2013

Wilcox, B. R., Caballero, M. D., Rehn, D. A., & Pollock, S. J. (2013). ACER: An Analytic Framework for Students' Use of Mathematics in Upper-Division Physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exam analysis (N=172) of element CC4. The article reports "86% of 172, N=148" attempted the difference vector and "47% of 148" failed; common errors were using a ring-of-charge magnitude (39%), setting it to the distance to the source or field point (25%), or giving no expression (12%) (Table II).

> "The magnitude of the diﬀerence vector, |⃗r|, must also be expressed such that it is consistent with the speciﬁc charge distribution (element CC4), and most students (86% of 172, N=148) attempted to do so. About half of these (47% of 148) were unable to produce a correct formula for |⃗r|."
