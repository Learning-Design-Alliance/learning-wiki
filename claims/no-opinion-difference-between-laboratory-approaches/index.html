Ataman Karacop 2017¶

Ataman Karacop, Emine Hatun Diken. (2017). The Effects of Jigsaw Technique Based on Cooperative Learning on Prospective Science Teachers' Science Process Skill. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.6. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1133003

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Post-test only comparison on the 16-item Student Opinion Scale (reliability 0,94) administered after the laboratory practices; experimental mean 54,92, control mean 50,00, out of a maximum of 80. Equivalence was not tested.

"independent samples t-test, which was done to see whe ther there was a statistically significant difference be tween the average SOS scores of the groups, showed that no statistically significant difference existed betw een the average SOS scores of the control and experim ental groups (t=1,276; p=0,208)"
