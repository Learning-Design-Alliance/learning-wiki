Laakkonen Noora, Jarva Erika, Hammarén Mira, Kanste Outi, Kääriäinen Maria, Oikarinen Anne, Mikkonen Kristina. (2024). Digital Competence among Healthcare Leaders: A Mixed-Methods Systematic Review. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/8435248

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of cross-sectional studies included in the review; the Adatara et al. study found gender differences in ICT use. The review reports "females' computer knowledge was lower than that of males" and "males' ICT ability was higher than females".

> "It was evident that females' computer knowledge was lower than that of males, and the proportion of females not knowing how to use computerised patient monitoring systems was higher than that of males. Therefore, males' ICT ability was higher than females [43]."
