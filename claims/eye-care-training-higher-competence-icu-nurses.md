## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Nurses with prior eye care training experience scored significantly higher than untrained nurses on all ECCI dimensions in univariate analysis, and training experience remained significant in multivariate regression. [→ Y. Zhang 2025](#y-zhang-2025)

## Evidence

### Y. Zhang 2025

Y. Zhang, C. Li, Y. Zhou, and X. Yu. (2025). Knowledge, Attitudes and Practices of Critical Care Nurses Regarding Eye Care Across 17 Hospitals: A Multicentre Cross‐Sectional Survey. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70120

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate stepwise linear regression on survey data from 1947 ICU nurses across 17 Chinese hospitals found training experience significantly affected the knowledge dimension, with the attitudinal dimension "associated with gender, training experience and material consultation". Univariate t-tests showed trained nurses scored 85.04 ± 9.22 versus 78.91 ± 11.77 on total ECCI (t = 10.538, p < 0.001).

> "The results showed that only training experience and material consultation significantly affected the knowledge dimension. The attitudinal dimension was associated with gender, training experience and material consultation."
