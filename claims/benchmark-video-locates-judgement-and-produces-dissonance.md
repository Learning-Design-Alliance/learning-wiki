# Scoring a concrete benchmark video and comparing with expert panel scores lets examiners locate their judgement, producing reassurance when aligned and dissonance when discrepant

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Observing and scoring a concrete video benchmark with panel information lets examiners identify scoring differences and sense their degree of alignment with the expert panel. [→ Edwards 2025](#edwards-2025)
`q2 i?` When examiner and panel judgements are discrepant, examiners feel uncomfortable dissonance and seek to resolve it by adjusting their perspective or dismissing the benchmark. [→ Edwards 2025 (2)](#edwards-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Edwards 2025

Edwards, R. J., Yeates, P., Lefroy, J., & McKinley, R. (2025). Understanding contexts and mechanisms through which video based benchmarking promotes alignment of examiners' scoring in objective structured clinical exams. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10454-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the stimulated realist interviews, examiners watched a station-specific video, scored it, and compared with the expert panel's agreed score and justification; this "gave participants a concrete example of performance" enabling comparison and reflection.

> "Observing and scoring a video-based benchmark performance gave participants a concrete example of performance on the station for which they had prepared."

### Edwards 2025 (2)

Edwards, R. J., Yeates, P., Lefroy, J., & McKinley, R. (2025). Understanding contexts and mechanisms through which video based benchmarking promotes alignment of examiners' scoring in objective structured clinical exams. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10454-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same interview study: most participants reported discomfort at discrepant scores, which they attributed to internal, external or combined loci, then either adjusted toward the panel or dismissed the benchmark.

> "Conversely, most participants felt uncomfortable when participant and expert panel judgements were discrepant because it may have implied they had erred"
