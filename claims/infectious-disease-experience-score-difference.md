`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariate t-test comparison in the 2055-nurse survey (t2053=3.333, P=.001). In the regression, the experience coefficient was not significant (B=0.599, P=.71), and the authors note experience is "no longer the primary influencing factor" after the pandemic.

> "397 (19.32%) had experience in responding to infectious diseases, scoring significantly higher (mean 146.00, SD 27.00) than those without such experience (mean 140.70, SD 28.26; P=.001)"
