## Evidence

### Bueckle 2022

Bueckle, Andreas; Buehling, Kilian; Shih, Patrick C.; Börner, Katy. (2022). Optimizing Performance and Satisfaction in Matching and Movement Tasks in Virtual Reality with Interventions Using the Data Visualization Literacy Framework. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.3389/frvir.2021.727344](https://doi.org/10.3389/frvir.2021.727344)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

RUI VR study, Plateau phase: 2D Desktop users saw a line graph of distance and angular difference from their Ramp-Up tasks during the Reflective phase. Results report "a significantly higher rotation accuracy" for the experiment group (median 5.806 degrees vs. 34.98 degrees, p = 0.031). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "we found a significantly higher rotation accuracy (median experiment group: 5.806 degrees, IQR: [2.216 - 9.949], median control group: 34.98 degrees, IQR: [12.109 - 88.457], Mann-Whitney-U-Test, p = 0.031)"
