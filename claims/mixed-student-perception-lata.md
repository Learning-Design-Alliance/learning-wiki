Three 5-point Likert perception items from the anonymous post-term survey (N=159), reported as stacked percentages in Figure 4. The quote carries the printed means, SDs, and positive-response percentages.

> "The LaTeX submission workflow itself was the most positively received component, with a mean of M= 3.64 (SD = 1.13) and 59.7% of respondents selecting 4 or 5; the feedback content (M= 3.26 , SD = 0.98; 42.1% positive) and overall sentiment toward LaTA (M= 3.16 , SD = 1.09; 40.3% positive) both sat slightly above the neutral midpoint but below the LaTeX-workflow item."
