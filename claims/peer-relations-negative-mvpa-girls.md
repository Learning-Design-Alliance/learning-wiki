## Evidence

### Byman 2026

Byman, A., Ulmanen, S., Pyhältö, K., Syväoja, H., Kulmala, J., & Tammelin, T. H. (2026). Gendered patterns in the associations between in-school physical activity, classroom peer ecology, and academic achievement among primary school students. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0351971

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multigroup SEM of 517 Finnish primary students (276 girls, 241 boys) with accelerometer-measured in-school MVPA; the girls' path was significant (p = .023) while the boys' peer-relations path was not (p = .411). The article reports "classroom peer relations were directly and negatively associated with in-school MVPA (−0.19)" among girls.

> "Among girls, classroom peer relations were directly and negatively associated with in-school MVPA (−0.19). Among boys, in-school MVPA was positively associated with GPA (0.15). No other paths between the study variables reached statistical significance"
