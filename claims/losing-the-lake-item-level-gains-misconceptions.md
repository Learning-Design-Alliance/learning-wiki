## Evidence

### E. Michael Nussbaum 2015

E. Michael Nussbaum, Marissa C. Owens, Gale M. Sinatra, Abeera P. Rehmat, Jacqueline R. Cordova, Sajjad Ahmad, Fred C. Harris, Jr., Sergiu M. Dascalu. (2015). Losing the Lake: Simulations to Promote Gains in Student Knowledge and Interest about Climate Change. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijese.2015.277a

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item analysis of the experimental group's posttest responses in the randomized trial; the authors report these three misconceptions persisted and conclude supplementary instruction is needed to reinforce these aspects of the game.

> "However, there were three popular misconceptions that were not effectively addressed, relating to casinos and resorts wasting a lot of water (untrue), the necessity of the greenhouse effect for human survival (effect significant at posttest but not maintained at delayed posttest), and that melting snowpack will cause lake levels to rise."
