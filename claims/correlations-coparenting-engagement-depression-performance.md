Descriptive correlation analysis (Table 2) in the 1989-dyad CFPS sample. The printed correlations are r = −0.069 (p < 0.01), r = −0.127 (p < 0.001), and performance was "positively related to academic engagement (r = 0.253, p < 0.001)".

> "academic performance was negatively correlated with co-parenting conflict (r = −0.069, p < 0.01) and depression (r = −0.127, p < 0.001), and positively related to academic engagement (r = 0.253, p < 0.001)."
