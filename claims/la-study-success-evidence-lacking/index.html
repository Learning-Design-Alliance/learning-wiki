Evidence¶

Dirk Ifenthaler 2020¶

Dirk Ifenthaler, Jane Yin-Kim Yau. (2020). Utilising learning analytics to support study success in higher education: a systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09788-z

q2 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review following eight standardised steps; an initial set of 6220 articles was identified and the final sample includes 46 key publications. The review reports promise but states that "rigorous, large-scale evidence of the effectiveness of learning analytics in supporting study success is still lacking".

"The findings obtained in this systematic review suggest that there are a considerable number of learning analytics approaches which utilise effective techniques in supporting study success and students at risk of dropping out. However, rigorous, large-scale evidence of the effectiveness of learning analytics in supporting study success is still lacking."
