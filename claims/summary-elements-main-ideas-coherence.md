## Evidence

### Pečjak and Pirc 2018

Sonja Pečjak, Tina Pirc. (2018). Developing Summarizing Skills in 4th Grade Students: Intervention Programme Effects. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, June 2018, Volume 10, Issue 5, 571-581. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2018541306

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Element-level ANOVAs on Summ_2: coherence F = 15.80, p = .000, η² = .080 (moderate); main ideas F = 6.17, p = .014, η² = .033; unimportant ideas F = 8.26, p = .005, η² = .043 — "significantly more coherent" in EG.

> "Students in EG compared to their peers in CG stated significantly more important ideas and included fewer unimportant ideas in the summary, which was significantly more coherent."
