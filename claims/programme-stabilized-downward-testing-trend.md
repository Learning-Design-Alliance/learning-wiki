## Evidence

### Joore Ivo Kim 2018

Joore Ivo Kim, van Bergen Jan E. A. M. J, ter Riet Gerben, van der Maat A., van Dijk N. (2018). Development and evaluation of a blended educational programme for general practitioners' trainers to stimulate proactive HIV testing. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-018-0723-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive trend analysis of six-monthly laboratory counts for 11 intervention and 624 control GPs. The article reports the pre-programme annual decreases of "9.1%" and "13.0%" and post-programme decreases of "2.3% and 1.8%", indicating a stabilized, higher-level trend.

> "between 2010 and 2013, the mean annual number of laboratory-documented HIV tests decreased by 9.1% for the 624 control GPs, and by 13.0% for the 11 intervention GPs. After the programme, the annual decreases were 2.3% and 1.8%"
