Bridge to Reading evidence summary 2025¶

Bridge to Reading evidence summary. (2025). https://www.heggerty.com

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental comparison in a rural Georgia district during the first half of 2023-2024: 247 first graders from 3 Bridge to Reading schools versus 736 first graders from 17 control schools with "similar demographics and pretest scores." Effect size was +0.16.

"After one semester, students using Bridge to Reading significantly outperformed students in control schools on MAP Growth Reading scores (effect size = +0.16), earning the program a Moderate rating."
