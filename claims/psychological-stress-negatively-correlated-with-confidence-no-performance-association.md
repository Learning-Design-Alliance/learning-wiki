## Evidence

### Liaw Sok Ying 2023

Liaw Sok Ying, Sutini, Chua Wei Ling, Tan Jian Zhi, Levett-Jones Tracy, Ashokka Balakrishnan, Te Pan Terry Ling, Lau Siew Tiang, Ignacio Jeanette. (2023). Desktop Virtual Reality Versus Face-to-Face Simulation for Team-Training on Stress Levels and Performance in Clinical Deterioration: a Randomised Controlled Trial. Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-022-07557-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlational analysis of post-test scores in the RCT found a significant negative correlation between psychological stress and confidence overall ("r = −0.43, p < 0.01"), and no significant correlation for other study variables including performance.

> "The Pearson correlation results demonstrated a significant negative correlation between the psychological stress and confidence level post-test score in the VR (r = −0.58, p < 0.001) and SP (r = −0.35, p < 0.01) groups, and overall (r = −0.43, p < 0.01) groups. No statistically significant correlation was found for the other study variables."
