## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Yoga teachers used a risk-averse, inclusive modification approach in groups, while yoga therapists preferred goal-focused modifications in one-to-one sessions. [→ Biswas I 2025](#biswas-i-2025)

## Evidence

### Biswas I 2025

Biswas I, Adebusoye B, Lewis S, Chattopadhyay K (2025). Clinical knowledge, experiences, and perceptions of yoga providers in arthritis treatment: a UK-based qualitative study. Rheumatology International 45(4):88. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00296-025-05843-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the 20 interviews showed teachers kept sessions "inclusive by modifying yoga using a risk-averse approach", using props and attendee feedback, whereas therapists strongly preferred goal-focused one-to-one delivery.

> "Yoga teachers shared their experiences of how they tried to keep their sessions inclusive by modifying yoga using a risk-averse approach to meet the needs of all attendees"
