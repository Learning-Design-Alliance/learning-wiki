### Binisha Marahatta and Matthew D. Sievert 2026

Binisha Marahatta and Matthew D. Sievert. (2026). Adapting Public Exams for Introductory Physics: A Mixed-Methods Investigation of Student Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.03847](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.03847)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic coding of open-ended responses in the NMSU physics course. The authors report that "Some students explicitly suggested to provide complete information about the problem in the Public Exams, but with a change in numerical values in the actual exam", and that students also suggested providing the equation for each problem.

> "However, they also expressed dissatisfaction about the withheld information on Public Exam questions and were unable to anticipate what would be asked in the real exam."
