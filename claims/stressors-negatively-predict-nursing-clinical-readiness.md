## Evidence

### Ozdemir Nur Guven 2025

Ozdemir Nur Guven, Dalcali Berna Kokturk, Berse Soner. (2025). The effects of stressors on readiness for clinical practice in novice nursing students: a path analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08079-6

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis of the hypothesized model on a cross-sectional survey of 553 first-year nursing students at three Turkish public universities. This path shows "education-related stressors" significantly and negatively predicting readiness, with β = −0.280.

> "The effect of education-related stressors on readiness for clinical practice was statistically significant (β= − 0.280, p < 0.001), supporting H1."
