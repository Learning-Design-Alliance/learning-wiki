## Evidence

### Angxuan Chen 2024

Angxuan Chen, Yuang Wei, Huixiao Le, Yan Zhang. (2024). Learning-by-Teaching with ChatGPT: The Effect of Teachable ChatGPT Agent on Programming Education. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15226 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2412.15226](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2412.15226)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on an adapted MSLQ-based SRL questionnaire administered pre and post; the experimental group "achieved significantly higher scores in self-efficacy (F = 37.26, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.75), and cognitive strategies (F = 18.97, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.61)".

> "The ANCOV A results (See Table 4) demonstrated that the experimental group, which utilized the ChatGPT teachable agent, achieved significantly higher scores in self-efficacy (F = 37.26, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.75), and cognitive strategies (F = 18.97, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.61) compared to the control group."
