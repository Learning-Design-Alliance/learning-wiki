q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Program evaluation comparing eMINTS schools with traditional control schools across rural Missouri over 3 years, covering 3,895 students. The summary reports "statistically significant outcomes on state math measures, with an effect size of +0.15", qualifying eMINTS for the "Strong" category.

"A large 3-year study in schools across rural Missouri evaluated reading and math outcomes of eMINTS in comparison to traditional control schools. No differences were found in reading, but there were statistically significant outcomes on state math measures, with an effect size of +0.15."
