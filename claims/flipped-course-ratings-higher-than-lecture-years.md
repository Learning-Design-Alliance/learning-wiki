## Evidence

### Galway Lindsay P 2014

Galway Lindsay P, Corbett Kitty K, Takaro Timothy K, Tairyan Kate, Frank Erica. (2014). A novel integration of online and flipped classroom instructional models in public health higher education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-181

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

University-collected overall course ratings compared across years: 2013 flipped students (n=9 completing the standard course review) against 130 students from 2009–2012 lecture-based offerings. No statistical test or effect size is reported for this comparison.

> "On a scale of 1–5 (1 = lowest rank; 5 = highest rank), the mean overall rating for the course in 2013 was 4.7/5 vs. prior years’ overall ratings of 3.7 (2012), 4.3 (2011), 4.1 (2010), and 3.9 (2009)."
