NWEA Research 2016¶

NWEA Research. (2016). Students improve even amid evaluation controversy. Phi Delta Kappan 98 (2), 58-65. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/students-improve-even-amid-evaluation-controversy/ doi:10.1177/0031721716671910

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The article reports positive student achievement and growth results for students in New York under the APPR system, stating these results "suggest that improvements to the teacher evaluation process" emphasizing strong procedures and data use "have promise for improving educator effectiveness." No effect sizes or test statistics are printed in the available text.

"Positive student achievement and growth results for students in New York suggest that improvements to the teacher evaluation process that emphasize the importance of strong evaluation procedures, the systematic collection of evidence of teacher performance, and the use of data to inform the process, have promise for improving educator effectiveness"
