## Evidence

### F. Egert 2018

F. Egert, R.G. Fukkink, A.G. Eckhardt. (2018). Impact of In-Service Professional Development Programs for Early Childhood Teachers on Quality Ratings and Child Outcomes: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317751918

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analytic subset analysis of nine studies including 486 teachers and 4,504 children that provided data on both quality ratings and child development. The printed effect size is ES = 0.14 (SE = 0.02, p < .001), reported as "a small effect at child level".

> "a subset of nine studies (including 486 teachers and 4,504 children) that provided data on both quality ratings and child development were analyzed, and they showed a small effect at child level (ES = 0.14; SE = 0.02, p < .001)"
