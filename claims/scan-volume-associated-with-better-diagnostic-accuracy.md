Observational subgroup comparison reported in Results (Figure 3 shows accuracy by individual practitioner and professional category). The article hedges with "appeared to have better"; scan volume was not experimentally manipulated, and the number of scans per practitioner "varied considerably and was very low in some cases".

> "Those who performed >200 scans (n=5) appeared to have better sensitivity (76.1% [95% CI, 64.1–85.7]) and specificity (80.5% [95% CI, 78.4–82.5]) compared with those with fewer scans (sensitivity 65.3% [95% CI, 53.5–76.0]; specificity 74.4%; 71.4–77.1)."
