## Evidence

### Preston Elisabeth 2012

Preston Elisabeth, Ada Louise, Dean Catherine M, Stanton Rosalyn, Waddington Gordon, Canning Colleen. (2012). The Physiotherapy eSkills Training Online resource improves performance of practical skills: a controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-119

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomised controlled trial of graduate-entry physiotherapy students (experimental n = 35, control n = 24) in consecutive semesters; the experimental group received the online resource plus usual teaching. The article reports "1.6 points out of 25 (95% CI −0.1 to 3.3)" higher total marks; no effect size or p-value is printed.
