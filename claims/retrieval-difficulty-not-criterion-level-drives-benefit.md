### Mary A. Pyc 2009

Mary A. Pyc, Katherine A. Rawson. (2009). Testing the retrieval effort hypothesis: Does greater difficulty correctly recalling information lead to higher levels of memory? Journal of Memory and Language 60 (2009) 437–447. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2009.01.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 hierarchical regression analyses (Table 5) across the four groups: criterion level alone accounted for significant variance in final test performance, but its contribution became non-significant once first key press latency was entered, providing converging evidence that retrieval difficulty drives the benefit.

> "However, when criterion level was entered after first key press latency (step 2 in Table 5), criterion level no longer accounted for a significant amount of variance in final test performance."
