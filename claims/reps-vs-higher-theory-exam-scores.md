## Evidence

### Hu Jia 2025

Hu Jia, Wang Xiuqing, Chen Ruonan, Lu Guoying, Zhang Ming, Huang Xiaojing. (2025). Use of virtual simulation for regenerative endodontic training: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06808-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 62 experimental vs 61 control fourth-year undergraduates on the post-intervention theory exam (Score Two). The article reports the experimental group total of "82.10 ± 14.50" was "significantly higher than the control group (p = 0.000)"; no effect size is printed.

> "the total score of the theory exam (Score Two) in group A after REPs-VS learning was 82.10 ± 14.50, which was significantly higher than the control group (p = 0.000)"

### Hu Jia 2025 (2)

Hu Jia, Wang Xiuqing, Chen Ruonan, Lu Guoying, Zhang Ming, Huang Xiaojing. (2025). Use of virtual simulation for regenerative endodontic training: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06808-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline check on the same randomized sample: after both groups attended the same two-hour didactic lecture, Score One totals (71.45 ± 13.65 vs 70.82 ± 10.05) showed "the same baseline level for the two groups (p = 0.771)".

> "The first test was taken after the didactic lecture (Score One), and the scores were comparable between the two groups in decision-making (Dimension 1) and operative procedures (Dimension 2), indicating the same baseline level for the two groups (p = 0.771)"
