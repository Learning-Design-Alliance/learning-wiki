## Evidence

### Nameera Akhtar 1991

Nameera Akhtar, Frances Dunham and Philip J. Dunham. (1991). Directive interactions and early vocabulary development: the role of joint attentional focus. Journal of Child Language, Volume 18, Issue 1, pp. 41-49. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305000900013283

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of maternal speech from twelve mother-child dyads videotaped at age 1;1, with 100 maternal utterances coded per mother. The article reports that "Only the frequency of mothers' FOLLOW-prescriptives correlated significantly" with productive vocabulary at 1;10, and that the correlation was "high and positive". No effect size coefficient is printed in the available text.

> "Only the frequency of mothers' FOLLOW-prescriptives correlated significantly with a productive vocabulary measure taken at 1;10. This correlation was high and positive"
