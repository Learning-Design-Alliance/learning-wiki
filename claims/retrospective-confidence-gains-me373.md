# Retrospective pre/post confidence rose on all four course learning objectives with large rank-biserial effects (N=159)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` On all four ME 373 learning objectives, retrospective pre/post confidence rose by more than one Likert point, with Mann–Whitney U p < 10−27 and rank-biserial effect sizes rrb from 0.688 (PDEs) to 0.825 (BVPs). [→ Jesse A. Rodríguez 2026](#jesse-a-rodriguez-2026)

## Evidence

### Jesse A. Rodríguez 2026

Jesse A. Rodríguez. (2026). LaTA: A Drop-In, FERPA-Compliant Local-LLM Autograder for Upper-Division STEM Coursework. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05410 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.05410](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.05410)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Anonymous post-term Canvas survey of N=159 students (~80% response rate) using a retrospective pre/then-post design; pre/post distributions treated as independent for Mann–Whitney U testing. Printed deltas were +1.57 to +1.88 Likert points.

> "All four comparisons achieved Mann–Whitney U p <10 −27 uncorrected (well below the threshold any standard multiple-comparisons correction would impose at q= 0.05 across only four tests) with rank-biserial effect sizes rrb ranging from 0.688 (PDEs) to 0.825 (BVPs), conventionally "large" effects."
