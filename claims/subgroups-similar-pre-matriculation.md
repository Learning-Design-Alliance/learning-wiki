## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Chi-square analyses found no significant differences among FYS/LC, FYS, and control groups on SAT score, Pell eligibility, ethnicity, or high school graduation percentile. [→ Tampke 2013](#tampke-2013)

## Evidence

### Tampke 2013

Tampke, D. R., & Durodoye, R. (2013). Improving Academic Success for Undecided Students: A First-Year Seminar/Learning Community Approach. Learning Communities Research and Practice, 1(2), Article 3. http://washingtoncenter.evergreen.edu/lcrpjournal/vol1/iss2/3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square analysis comparing FYS/LC (n=165), FYS (n=69), and control (n=109) undecided FTIC students on SAT score, Pell grant eligibility, ethnicity, and high school graduation percentile. All comparisons non-significant (e.g., SAT: χ²=7.3283, df=4, p<0.120; Pell: χ²=3.6910, df=2, p<0.158).

> "Despite the numerical differences, the chi-square an alysis suggests that the three groups— FYS/LC, LC, and Control—did not di ffer significantly across the at-risk categories included in the analysis."
