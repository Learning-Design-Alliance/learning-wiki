The preface reports, citing Kremer et al. (2013) in Science's Grand Challenges in Science Education special issue, that tailoring instruction to students' knowledge levels is a key challenge; the preface attributes this, and the underlying studies were not read here.

"As one of the key challenges that “could play an important role” in improving current practice, it identified the development of “programs that seek to tailor teaching to children’s learning level … and educational technology that tailors instruction to students’knowledge levels”(Kremer et al. 2013)."
