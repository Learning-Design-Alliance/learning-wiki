## Evidence

### Hou Qin 2025

Hou Qin, Zhou Bin, Kang Jing-Ping, Zuo Yun-Xia. (2025). Competency-driven hemostasis training reduces vascular complications in transradial hemodynamic monitoring: a self-controlled trial with early-career residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08048-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-controlled before-after comparison of 10 residents' cases; the article reports the rate "decreased to 13.8%" with χ² = 6.42, P = 0.01. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Before CDHT, the 24-h postoperative hematoma/bruise incidence was 29.0% among 93 patients. Following hemostasis training, this rate decreased to 13.8% in 94 consecutive cases (χ² = 6.42, P = 0.01, two-sided; Table 2)."

### Hou Qin 2025 (2)

Hou Qin, Zhou Bin, Kang Jing-Ping, Zuo Yun-Xia. (2025). Competency-driven hemostasis training reduces vascular complications in transradial hemodynamic monitoring: a self-controlled trial with early-career residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08048-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive severity analysis in the same trial: all observed hematomas were grade I (≤ 5 cm) in both phases, so training changed incidence, not severity.

> "A consistent finding was that the size of the hematoma remained within the same range before and after training, with all patients exhibiting a diameter of less than 5 cm (Table 2)."
