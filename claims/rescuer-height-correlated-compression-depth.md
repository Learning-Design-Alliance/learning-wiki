`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation in the 55-subject randomized mannequin simulation between measured subject height (63–76 inches) and mean compression depth, yielding "Pearson correlation coefficient −r=0.560, p<0.0001" — a large positive correlation.

> "The height and compression depth were strongly, positively correlated (Pearson correlation coefficient −r=0.560, p<0.0001). As height increases, compression depth also increases."
