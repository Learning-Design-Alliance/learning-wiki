Interview analysis: Student I alternated between saying the electron and the probability density went through both slits, later clarifying they were essentially one and the same for him. Four other students distinguished intuitive conceptions from what they learned; three were explicit about not thinking of an electron as either particle or wave.

> "Six of the interviewed students bridged between particle and wave descriptions of electrons in the double-slit experiment"
