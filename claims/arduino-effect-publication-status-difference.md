## Evidence

### Lee 2020

Lee, E. (2020). A meta-analysis of the effects of Arduino-based education in Korean primary and secondary schools in engineering education. European Journal of Educational Research, 9(4), 1503-1512. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.9.4.1503

`q3 · i?`

Meta-ANOVA on publication status in the 16-study meta-analysis. Academic papers showed a larger effect size than dissertations, and the article reports the difference was statistically significant (Qb = 6.00, p < .05).

> "The effect sizes were 1.258 (large) for academic papers and 0.567 (medium) for dissertations. From the ANOVA, the significance level was p < .05, and the effect sizes based on publication status were statistically different."
