## Evidence

### Nilsson Maria Skyvell 2010

Nilsson Maria Skyvell, Pennbrant Sandra, Pilhammar Ewa, Wenestam Claes-Göran. (2010). Pedagogical strategies used in clinical medical education: an observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' interpretive discussion of the observational data: these three strategies were read as instances of Ramsden's teaching-as-organizing-student-activity perspective, where "the focus is moved from the teacher toward the student's activity". This is interpretation, not a tested comparison.

> "By Piloting, Prompting and Supplementing the teacher supported the student in taking the role of an active doctor handling the clinical situation and guided the students on how to react."
