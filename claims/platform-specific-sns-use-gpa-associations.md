# Instagram, Snapchat, and Facebook use, but not TikTok, Twitter, or WhatsApp use, are significantly negatively associated with adolescent GPA

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Greater use of Instagram (β = −0.12), Snapchat (β = −0.10), and Facebook (β = −0.10) is significantly associated with lower GPA, while TikTok, Twitter, and WhatsApp show no significant association. [→ Aquino-Blanco A 2025](#aquino-blanco-a-2025)

## Evidence

### Aquino-Blanco A 2025

Aquino-Blanco A, Quiroz-Cárdenas F, Montenegro-Espinosa JA, Martín-Calvo N, Yañéz-Sepúlveda R, Gutiérrez-Espinoza H, Olivares-Arancibia J, Rahmati M, Stubbs B, Smith L, López-Gil JF, Jiménez-López E. (2025). Social networks, messaging apps, addictive use, and academic performance in adolescents: findings from the EHDLA study. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-025-02180-8

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Platform-specific generalized linear models in the same 583-adolescent EHDLA sample, adjusted for the same covariates. The article reports significant negative associations for Instagram, Snapchat, and Facebook, and states "The association of TikTok, Twitter, and WhatsApp use with GPA were not significant."

> "A statistically significant negative association was found between GPA and Instagram (p = 0.010; β = -0.12), Snapchat (p = 0.001; β = -0.10), and Facebook (p = 0.014; β = -0.10) use, suggesting that greater use of these three SNs is associated with a lower GPA. The association of TikTok, Twitter, and WhatsApp use with GPA were not significant."
