## Evidence

### Harris E 2026

Harris E, Collis A, Carlbom D, Johnson N, Town J, Raikhel V. (2026). Post In-Hospital Cardiac Arrest Simulation to Improve Medical Decision Making and Communication in Internal Medicine Residents. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11642

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post survey of 32 of 34 learners over 2 academic years; chi-square analysis of correct/incorrect responses. The article reports a statistically significant increase in correct responses to all 3 knowledge questions, with no effect size printed.

> "We observed a statistically significant increase in correct responses to all 3 multiple-choice knowledge-based questions, which included the recommended partial pressure of carbon dioxide in arterial blood (PaCO2), mean arterial pressure (MAP), and peripheral oxygen saturation (SpO2) for patients in the post-ROSC setting"
