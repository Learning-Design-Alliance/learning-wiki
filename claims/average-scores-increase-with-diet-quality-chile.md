## Evidence

### Correa-Burrows P 2016

Correa-Burrows P, Burrows R, Blanco E, Reyes M, Gahagan S. (2016). Nutritional quality of diet and academic performance in Chilean students. Bulletin of the World Health Organization. https://doi.org/10.2471/BLT.15.161315

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison across diet categories in the cohort (n = 395), shown in Table 1 and Fig. 1 with one-way ANOVA and Bonferroni adjustment: language p = 0.007, mathematics p = 0.010, GPA p = 0.001. Unhealthy-diet language mean was 430.6 versus 465.2 for healthy diet.

> "Average scores in language, mathematics and GPA increased with higher quality of diet (Table 1 and Fig. 1)."
