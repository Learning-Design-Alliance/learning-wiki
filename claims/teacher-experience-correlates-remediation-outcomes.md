## Evidence

### Winston Kalman A 2013

Winston Kalman A, Van Der Vleuten Cees PM, Scherpbier Albert JJA. (2013). Remediation of at-risk medical students: theory in action. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-132

`q2 · i3` · `qualitative · r2`

Observational analysis of outcome data for students taught by nine teachers on the ELLS remediation course, reported in Table 1 and Results. The article prints the correlation coefficient r = 0.81 between context experience and long-term pass rate, suggesting 51%–65% of outcome variance could be due to teacher experience.

> "The correlation between context experience, operationalized as number of years teaching at this school, and long-term pass rate is 0.81."

### Winston Kalman A 2013 (2)

Winston Kalman A, Van Der Vleuten Cees PM, Scherpbier Albert JJA. (2013). Remediation of at-risk medical students: theory in action. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-132

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Same observational analysis of teacher experience and student outcomes. The article prints the correlation coefficient r = 0.72 between total teaching experience and long-term pass rate, and notes many other factors must also be involved.

> "The correlation between total teaching experience and long-term pass rate is 0.72."
