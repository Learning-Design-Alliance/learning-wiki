## Evidence

### Mayrhofer A 2016

Mayrhofer A, Goodman C, Smeeton N, Handley M, Amador S, Davies S. (2016). The feasibility of a train-the-trainer approach to end of life care training in care homes: an evaluation. BMC Palliative Care 15:11. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-016-0081-z

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview and focus group findings from trainers, educators/facilitators and managers across three study sites. The article reports "a greater likelihood of staff engagement and discussion" when teaching fit working patterns; managerial trainers had to create time because routine encounters were hard to use.

> "Where teaching could be integrated with patterns of working there was a greater likelihood of staff engagement and discussion."
