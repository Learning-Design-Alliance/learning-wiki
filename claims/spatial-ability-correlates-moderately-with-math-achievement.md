# Spatial ability correlates moderately with mathematics achievement (r = .25), less strongly than verbal ability

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · review `r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium · n=30 meta-analyses, 66 correlations from 13,853 effect sizes (~4.66 million participants)

Spatial ability correlates r = .25 with mathematics achievement (Xie et al. 2020, k = 263, n = 14,766), with subtypes from .21 to .35. These are correlations compiled across meta-analyses: they describe how strongly the characteristic goes with mathematics achievement, not what causes it.

## Evidence

### Breit et al. 2025

Breit, M., Schneider, M., & Preckel, F. (2025). Mathematics achievement and learner characteristics: A systematic review of meta-analyses. *Learning and Individual Differences, 118*, 102621. [doi:10.1016/j.lindif.2024.102621](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2024.102621)

`q3 · systematic review of meta-analyses` · `i2 · medium effect, r = .25 (k = 263)` · `n=30 meta-analyses, 66 correlations from 13,853 effect sizes (~4.66 million participants)` · `review · r3`

The review compiles zero-order correlations from published meta-analyses (each with at least ten effect sizes, the largest one kept where several covered a variable), found by a systematic PsycINFO search in July 2024. It pools nothing further and controls for nothing: many of these characteristics correlate with one another, so a correlation here says how strongly the characteristic goes with mathematics achievement, not that it causes it. Overall spatial ability r = .25; intrinsic-dynamic .27 (k = 89), visual-spatial memory .26 (k = 114), intrinsic-static .23, extrinsic-static .21, and extrinsic-dynamic .35 on only 11 effect sizes with a wide interval (±.42). Age did not significantly moderate the correlation.

## Discussion

The authors suggest spatial representation is one of several routes to solving mathematics problems and can be compensated by others, and that spatial ability may matter more at high levels of mathematics, where it marks talent. See [Spatial training improves math performance](spatial-training-improves-math-performance.md) for the intervention evidence.
