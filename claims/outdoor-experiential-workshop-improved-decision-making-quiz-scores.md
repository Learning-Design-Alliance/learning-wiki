## Evidence

### Petelinsek S 2025

Petelinsek S, Zhang P, Colbert-Getz J, Hartridge T, Furman N, Awad E, Kelner R, Hughes PG. (2025). Getting lost to find insights - a quasi-experimental evaluation of outdoor experiential learning as a method to teach clinical decision-making and decision science concepts. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07533-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study of 41 first-year medical students who completed both quizzes. "Results of the paired sample t-test indicated a statistically significant improvement in scores" with t(40) = 7.64, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 1.19; mean scores rose from 51.83% to 74.09%, a 22.26% increase (95% CI: 16.37–28.14).

> "Results of the paired sample t-test indicated a statistically significant improvement in scores from pre- to post-quiz, t (40) = 7.64, p < 0.001. The effect size, calculated using Cohen's d, was 1.19, indicating a large effect."
