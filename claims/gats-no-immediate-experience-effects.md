## Evidence

### experience-null

Yuri Noviello, Naaz Sibia, Anastasiia Birillo, Thomas Overklift Vaupel Klein, Michael Liut, and Gosia Migut. 2026. AI-Generated Traces for Novice Programmers: Learning Effects and Learner Differences in a Multi-Institutional Study. Proceedings of the 31st ACM Conference on Innovation and Technology in Computer Science Education V. 1 (ITiCSE 2026). https://doi.org/10.1145/3803400.3809346

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Likert self-report instruments (Paas mental effort, NASA-TLX, IMI items) analyzed with linear mixed models at both sites; e.g., UofT pooled frustration Δ=-0.135, p=0.043, padj=0.086, and TU Delft pooled situational interest Δ=0.240, p=0.079, padj=0.196. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Across both deployments, pooled effects on frustration, cognitive load, and situational interest were small and did not survive correction."
