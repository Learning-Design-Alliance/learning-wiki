## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Verbal learning performance showed no significant main effect of background presence or stimulus group and no significant interaction, with negligible printed effect sizes. [→ Jäncke L 2010](#jancke-l-2010)

## Evidence

### Jäncke L 2010

Jäncke L, Sandmann P. (2010). Music listening while you learn: No influence of background music on verbal learning. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-6-3

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

A 2-way repeated-measures ANOVA on verbal learning data from 75 subjects randomly assigned to five background-stimulus groups, comparing learning with (background+) and without (background-) acoustic background. The article reports "no significant main effect" with η² = 0.001 for Background and η² = 0.049 for the interaction.

> "revealed no significant main effect (Background: F(1,70) = 0.073, p = 0.788, eta2 = 0.001; Group: F(4,70) = 1.42,p = 0.235, eta2 = 0.075) nor a significant interaction (F(4,70) = 0.90, p = 0.47, eta2 = 0.049)"
