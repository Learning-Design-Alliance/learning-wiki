## Evidence

### Koretsky 2018

Koretsky, M., Keeler, J., Ivanovitch, J., & Cao, Y. (2018). The role of pedagogical tools in active learning: a case for sense-making. International Journal of STEM Education 5:18. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-018-0116-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Think-aloud interview analysis of two selected engineering GIWs (one conceptual, one computational; 20 questions analyzed). Printed percentages are descriptive; the instructor also intended students "to reference previous knowledge and information presented in lecture (85%)".

> "the engineering instructor intended students to spend the majority of time engaged in quantitative reasoning (60%) with only a small amount of time in conceptual reasoning (5%). There is also less metacognitive thinking in these activities (10%) than that in the biology course."
