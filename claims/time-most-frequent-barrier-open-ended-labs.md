### Talafian 2024 (2)

University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Department of Physics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04275](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04275)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Open-response survey segments assigned multiple codes revealed "the interwoven nature of multiple structural and/or perception-related barriers in teaching labs"; the article's Table 3 shows "the three most common stacking of barriers," such as time with populous classes or with teachers' perceived content-knowledge gaps.
