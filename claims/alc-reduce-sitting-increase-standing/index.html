Sánchez-López M 2025¶

Sánchez-López M, Violero-Mellado J, Martínez-Vizcaíno V, Laukkanen A, Sääkslahti A, Visier-Alfonso ME. (2025). Impact and perceptions of Active Learning Classrooms on reducing sedentary behaviour and improving physical and mental health and academic indicators in children and adolescents: A scoping review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0317973

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis of six accelerometer-based studies of ALCs; the review reports that four "reported a decrease in sitting time and an increase in standing time". No pooled effect size is printed.

"Of the six studies that examined the association or effect of ALCs with movement behaviours [17–19, 30, 34, 35], four reported a decrease in sitting time and an increase in standing time [17–19, 30], three an increase in step time and number of steps [17–19]"
