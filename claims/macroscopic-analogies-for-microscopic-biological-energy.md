## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2011

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Edward F. Redish, and Jessica Watkins. (2011). Students' Views of Macroscopic and Microscopic Energy in Physics and Biology. PERC 2011. [doi:10.1063/1.3680024](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680024)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the interview data, when explaining how energy is stored in ATP the student invoked pushing magnets together and compressing a spring, saying "you have a whole lot of potential energy." The authors treat this as analogy rather than a claim about physical identity. No effect size is reported.

> "Dennis sees the repulsive force between magnets and the elastic potential energy in a spring as productive analogies for understanding the mechanism for energy changes in a chemical reaction."
