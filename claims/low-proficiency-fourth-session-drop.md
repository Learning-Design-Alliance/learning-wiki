## Evidence

### Lin 2014

Lu-Chun Lin. (2014). Learning Word Meanings from Teachers' Repeated Story Read-Aloud in EFL Primary Classrooms. English Language Teaching, 7(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n7p68

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample analysis of the low-proficiency group (n = 24) in the quasi-experiment, showing a significant decline in picture identification scores from the third to the fourth session ("t (23) = -3.31, p = .00, d = -0.46").

> "By the fourth session, however, the high-proficiency children scored significantly higher than the less-proficient children, who showed a significant drop in scores on the fourth picture identification test from the third test (paired-sample t (23) = -3.31, p = .00, d = -0.46)."
