A descriptive statement about the published US News ranking formula, quoted from the author's commentary. The author interprets that this weighting can lead administrators to conclude rankings rise if they admit only students with perfect GRE scores.

> "In the US News formula, the weight given to the mean GRE score is 12% (6% each to Verbal and Quantitative). The acceptance rate weighting of 6% is added to give "student selectivity" a total weight of 18%."
