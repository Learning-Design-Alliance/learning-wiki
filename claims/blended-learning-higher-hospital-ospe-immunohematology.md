## Evidence

### Saniee Nadia 2026

Saniee Nadia, Seidi Masoumeh, Amiri Fatemeh. (2026). Blended learning in laboratory science education: comparison of flipped classroom and traditional lectures in immunohematology training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09985-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Parallel-group randomized controlled study over five semesters with 196 students. Between-group comparison of hospital OSPE scores (internship durability assessed the next spring semester) found a significant difference favoring the intervention, with "effect size r = 0.550".

> "The mean ± SD hospital OSPE score was 5.319 ± 0.818 in the control group and 6.304 ± 0.749 in the intervention group. This difference was also statistically significant (P-value < 0.001)."
