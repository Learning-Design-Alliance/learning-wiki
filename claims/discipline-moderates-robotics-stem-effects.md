## Evidence

### Fan Ouyang and Weiqi Xu 2024

Fan Ouyang and Weiqi Xu. (2024). The effects of educational robotics in STEM education: a multilevel meta-analysis. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-024-00469-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Omnibus moderator analysis across 30 effect sizes in the multilevel model. Discipline was significant; subgroup estimates showed technology at g = 0.947 (p < 0.01) and cross-disciplinary at g = 0.414 (p < 0.05), versus non-significant mathematics (g = 0.376) and negative science (g = −0.613). Engineering was excluded for fewer than three effect sizes.

> "Significant difference was detected between the weighted effect sizes of different disciplines (F(3, 25) = 3.9205, p < 0.05), which indicated that discipline significantly moderated the effects of educational robotics on STEM learning."
