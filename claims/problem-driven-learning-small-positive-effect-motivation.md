## Evidence

### Wijnia 2024

Wijnia, L., Noordzij, G., Arends, L. R. A., Rikers, R. M. J. P., & Loyens, S. M. M. (2024). The Effects of Problem-Based, Project-Based, and Case-Based Learning on Students' Motivation: a Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09864-3

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Three-level random-effects meta-analysis of 139 subsamples (N = 20,154) and 288 effect sizes comparing PBL, PjBL, and CBL with teacher-centered instruction. The pooled effect was "d = 0.498 (SE = 0.07, 95% CI [0.354, 0.641]), p < 0.001". Heterogeneity was high: Q(287) = 1092.98, p < 0.001, with I2 Level 3 = 75.12% between-cluster.

> "The pooled effect size based on the three-level meta-analytic model was d = 0.498 (SE = 0.07, 95% CI [0.354, 0.641]), p < 0.001; indicating a small, positive effect."
