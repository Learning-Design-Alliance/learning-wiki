# Vietnamese EFL grade-11 learners report above-average overall metacognitive strategy use in reading

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants' overall metacognitive strategy use mean was significantly above the accepted average of 3.0 on the 5-point scale. [→ Thao M. T. Nguyen and Lap Q. Trinh 2011](#thao-m-t-nguyen-and-lap-q-trinh-2011)

## Evidence

### Thao M. T. Nguyen and Lap Q. Trinh 2011

Thao M. T. Nguyen and Lap Q. Trinh. (2011). Learners' Metacognitive Strategy Use and Reading Comprehension: Insights from a Vietnamese Context. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 1, January - March 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071083

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-sample t-test on questionnaire data from 76 valid MARSI-based responses (α = .87) in a case study of grade-11 EFL learners; the mean "M= 3.29, SD= 0.55" exceeded the accepted average of 3.0, indicating above-average reported strategy use.

> "The sample mean (M= 3.29, SD= 0.55) was significantly different from 3.0- the average point (t= 4.53, df= 75, p= .00). This means that participants' use of metacognitive strategies in reading is above average."
