## Evidence

### Levitan 2024

Levitan, S., & Munyan-Penney, N. (2024). Meaningful Assessment Reporting: Making Assessment Reports More Meaningful for Students & Families. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED658190

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Recent EdTrust focus groups with families, described in the introduction, found that "online portals were difficult to navigate" and reports lacked explanations of scale scores and percentile ranks, supporting the brief's claim that reports are not accessible or culturally affirming.

> "Participants shared that online portals were difficult to navigate, reports were often not in a caregiver's native language, and/or the information lacked clear explanations about what the scale scores or percentile ranks mean"
