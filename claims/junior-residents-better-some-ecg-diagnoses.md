## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Residents aged 29 years or younger more often correctly diagnosed sinus rhythm (p = 0.017), and year I and II residents more often correctly diagnosed sinus arrhythmia (p = 0.001) and SVT (p = 0.018) than their senior counterparts. [→ Mintesnot Helen 2024](#mintesnot-helen-2024)

## Evidence

### Mintesnot Helen 2024

Mintesnot Helen, Tadele Henok, Ahmed Hayat, Moges Tamirat, Gedlu Etsegenet. (2024). Electrocardiography interpretation competency among pediatric and child health residents at Addis Ababa University, Ethiopia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06614-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square/Fisher's exact analyses in the cross-sectional study of 95 residents. The printed p-values are 0.017 for age with sinus rhythm and 0.001 for residency year with sinus arrhythmia; no effect sizes are printed.

> "A resident’s correct diagnosis of sinus rhythm was associated with the age of the resident with more correct diagnosis among those aged 29 years or younger, p = 0.017. The diagnosis of sinus arrhythmia was associated with the year of residency with more correct diagnoses by year I and II residents, p = 0.001."
