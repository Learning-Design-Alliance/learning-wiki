## Evidence

### Serçe Sibel 2025

Serçe Sibel, Ovayolu Özlem, Ovayolu Nimet. (2025). The effect of simulator-assisted application on anxiety, satisfaction, and self-confidence level of students taking internal medicine nursing course: a randomized controlled and experimental trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08202-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled experimental trial in which 85 intervention students received simulation scenarios alongside clinical practice and 98 controls received clinical practice only. The intervention group's satisfaction/self-confidence mean rose from "3.8 ± 0.6 before the simulation" to 4.4 ± 0.6 after (p < 0.05 reported in the abstract); intra-group p = 0.001 and inter-group p = 0.001 were printed in Table 3 text.

> "The mean score of the students in the intervention group on student satisfaction and self-confidence in learning scale was 3.8 ± 0.6 before the simulation and increased to 4.4 ± 0.6 after the simulation"
