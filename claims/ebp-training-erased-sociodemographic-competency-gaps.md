## Evidence

### Akhter N 2026

Akhter N, Zhou X, Elhabashy S, Huq KATE, Okamoto M, Latif MA, Babaita AO, Moriyama M. (2026). Effect of evidence-based education program with mentoring system for prevention of ventilator-associated pneumonia on nurses' competency to improve quality care for patients in the intensive care unit: a quasi-experimental study in a tertiary hospital in Bangladesh. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1674223

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple regression of competency scores on age, education, total work experience and ICU experience at three time points in the 52-nurse study. After training and implementation, effects on all components were "no longer statistically significant"; at baseline, older age (B = 0.582, p = 0.023) and education (B = 6.264, p = 0.003) predicted skill, and longer work experience negatively predicted motivation (B = −1.938, p = 0.046).

> "Following the training intervention and implementation phases, the effects of age, education, work experience, and ICU experience on all measured components were no longer statistically significant"
