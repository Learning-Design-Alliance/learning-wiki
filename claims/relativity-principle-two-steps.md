# The principle of relativity enters Lorentz-transformation derivations through two distinct steps: round-trip identity and equivalence of v and −v

Analytical derivation summarized in Section VIII, restating the two-step decomposition developed in Section III, where step 1 gives α(v)α(−v) = β(v)β(−v) = 1 and step 2 gives α(−v) = β(v), together yielding α·β = 1 and full Minkowski length invariance.

> "the application of the prin- ciple of relativity comprises two steps: the combination of forward and backward transformation must yield the identity transformation (step 1) and forward and back- ward transformation are identical apart from the diﬀerent sign of the velocity (step 2)"
