## Evidence

### Breindahl Niklas 2025

Breindahl Niklas, Bay Emma Therese, Heiring Christian, MacLean-Nyegaard Ingrid Rose, Gudiksen Amalie, Frithioff Andreas, Henriksen Tine Brink, Tolsgaard Martin Grønnebæk, Aunsholt Lise. (2025). Assessing competency in less invasive surfactant administration: simulation-based validity evidence for the LISA-AT scores. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-025-03868-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Internal-structure analysis of the first simulation round (33 participants, two attempts each), with onsite and video ratings compared. The article reports "high test-retest reliability" (rho = 0.87), internal consistency alpha = 0.88, and inter-rater alpha = 0.82 / ICC = 0.71.

> "The LISA-AT had a high test-retest reliability (Spearman’s rho = 0.87 and ICC [IQR] = 0.81 [0.63-0.90]), high internal consistency (Cronbach’s alpha [IQR] = 0.88 [0.84–0.91]), and moderate-good inter-rater reliability (Cronbach’s alpha [IQR] = 0.82 [0.68-0.97] and ICC [IQR] = 0.71 [0.38–0.88])."
