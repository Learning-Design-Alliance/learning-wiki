## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Individual exam scores correlate moderately and positively with subsequent cumulative scores (exam 3 r = 0.571; exam 4 r = 0.546, both p < 0.001), whether the previous exam was taken individually or collaboratively. [→ Leight H. 2012](#leight-h-2012)

## Evidence

### Leight H. 2012

Leight H., Saunders C., Calkins R., Withers M. (2012). Collaborative Testing Improves Performance but Not Content Retention in a Large-Enrollment Introductory Biology Class. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-04-0048

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of the cross-over study (Figures 6 and 7): "a moderate positive correlation between individual exam scores and subsequent cumulative scores" (r = 0.571 and r = 0.546, both p < 0.001). Subgroup correlations within each format were also positive.

> "We found a moderate positive correlation between individual exam scores and subsequent cumulative scores on both exam 3 (Figure 6A, r = 0.571, p < 0.001) and exam 4 (Figure 6B, r = 0.546, p < 0.001) regardless of whether students took the previous exam individually or collaboratively."
