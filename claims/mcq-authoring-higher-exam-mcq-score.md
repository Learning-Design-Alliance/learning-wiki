## Evidence

### Enrique Abad 2015

Enrique Abad, Pilar Suárez, Julia Gil. (2015). A game-based educational method relying on student-generated questions. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.06439](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.06439)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U-test comparing final-exam MCQ test scores of a control group (N=13, traditional teaching) and an experimental group (N=11, MCQ authoring), reported in the statistical-analysis section. The comparison shows a statistically significant difference, with "p < 0.021" for the MCQ test; no effect size is printed.

> "for a signiﬁcance level equal to 0.05 the null hypothesis must be rejected in the comparison between the MCQ test results for both groups (p < 0.021), implying that the observed diﬀerences for the mean scores are statistically signiﬁcant"
