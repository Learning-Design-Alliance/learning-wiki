### Johnkutty Meenu 2025 (2)

Johnkutty Meenu, Kuperstein Harry, Koroma Fatima, Chen Jessi, Mattson James, Ahmad Sahar. (2025). A novel simulation paradigm for medical ICU cardiopulmonary arrest training: the in situ mirror simulation. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2528355

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' Findings and future direction section contrasts flexible with fixed skills; no test statistics or effect sizes are printed for the fixed-skill null findings, so this is a reported non-significant result, not evidence of equivalence.

> "In contrast, fixed technical skills such as knowledge of ACLS protocol and medications and fixed non-technical skills such as performing as team leader or assigning roles did not lead to statistically significant changes in confidence."
