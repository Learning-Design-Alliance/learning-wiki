## Evidence

### Singh 2016

Singh, C. and Schunn, C. D. (2016). Connecting Three Pivotal Concepts in K-12 Science State Standards and Maps of Conceptual Growth to Research in Physics Education. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.06024](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.06024)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Clement (1983) asked first-year pre-engineering college students to draw a force diagram of a coin just after it was tossed in the air, on paper before and after instruction and in interviews. A common incorrect response was that the force of the hand must exceed gravity while the coin rises, consistent with an impetus view; the article states the confusion "was pervasive even after instruction".

> "This confusion between velocity and net force was pervasive even after instruction. Clement extracted a number of characteristics from student responses and labeled them the “motion implies force” preconception."
