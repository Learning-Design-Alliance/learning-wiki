Ian Beatty. (2005). Transforming Student Learning with Classroom Communication Systems. EDUCAUSE Center for Applied Research (ECAR) Research Bulletin. Originally published 2004. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508129](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508129)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Challenges-section assertion about content coverage: CCS courses 'rarely cover as long a list of topics as traditional courses.' The bulletin argues this is not less learning, since much lecture content is lost shortly after the last exam, and that efficiency 'depends on whether one is measuring what students learn or what they’ve been exposed to.'

> "instructors using a CCS will encounter conflict with existing notions of how much subject material ought to be covered in a course, and at what rate. CCS-based courses rarely cover as long a list of topics as traditional courses."
