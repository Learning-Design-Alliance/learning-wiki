From A Systematic Investigation of Faculty-Produced Think-Pair-Share Questions to Frameworks for Characterizing and Developing Fluency-Inspiring Activities. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Design description of the Doppler shift Student Representation Task, in which the information about a star's color, distance, and position is "deliberately varied in order to provide student groups with suﬃcient contrasting cases" targeting common Doppler-shift reasoning difficulties; the final graphing part elevates the activity to QCR = 4.

> "The sequence of prompts in the Doppler shift SRT asks students to ﬁrst create sketches of dark line absorption spectra, then make a data table, and ﬁnally sketch energy output vs. wavelength graphs."
