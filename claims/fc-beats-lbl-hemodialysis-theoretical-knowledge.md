### Lu Zihua 2024

Lu Zihua, Zhu Jiangtao, Chen Cheng. (2024). Performance of flipped classroom teaching method during hemodialysis training of nurses. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2024-0276en

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 46 inexperienced hemodialysis nurses randomly allocated into FC and LBL groups of 23 each. In the reported comparison, the "FC group exhibited significantly higher scores in objective and subjective questions, as well as the overall theoretical knowledge"; the exam had a maximum score of 100 with subjective (40) and objective (60) components. No effect size printed.

> "FC group exhibited significantly higher scores in objective and subjective questions, as well as the overall theoretical knowledge, compared to LBL group (Figure 1)."
