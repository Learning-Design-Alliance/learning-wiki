## Evidence

### Omarchevska 2022

Omarchevska, Y., Lachner, A., Richter, J., & Scheiter, K. (2022). Do Video Modeling and Metacognitive Prompts Improve Self-Regulated Scientific Inquiry? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09652-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Randomized contrast analysis (N = 127; VMP n = 43, VM n = 43, control n = 41) comparing the two video modeling conditions against unguided inquiry on coded hypothesis quality (0–4). The contrast was significant: "t(124) = 2.60, p = 0.010, d = 0.49", a medium effect.

> "Contrast 1 showed that the VMP and VM conditions had higher hypothesis quality, t(124) = 2.60, p = 0.010, d = 0.49"
