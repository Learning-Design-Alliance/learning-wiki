## Evidence

### Cai Bohong 2025

Cai Bohong, Wang Zihan, Lyu Xi, Qiao Yiran, Liu Yuhan, Wu Danting, Li Sihang, Yao Qi, Chen Baixiang, Fu Fujian, Jiang Wei. (2025). Surgeon perceptions of essential skills and simulation for percutaneous transforaminal endoscopic discectomy training. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34397-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey analysis of 154 valid responses from surgeons with PTED experience, rating 21 specific skills grouped into three categories. The anatomical-identification category received the highest mean rating (4.646 ± 0.521), and "There were no significant differences in the ratings of these skill categories based on surgeon experience level."

> "Welch ANOVA and Tamhane T2 test indicated that skills pertaining to the identification of important anatomical structures were rated as the most critical. There were no significant differences in the ratings of these skill categories based on surgeon experience level."
