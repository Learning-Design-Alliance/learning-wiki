Zawacki-Richter 2019¶

Zawacki-Richter, O., Bond, M., & Gouverneur, F. (2019). Systematic review of research on artificial intelligence applications in higher education – where are the educators? International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-019-0171-0

q3 · i? · review · r3

The systematic review coded all 146 articles for presence of critical reflection on challenges and benefits. Only Li (2007) on privacy and Welham (2008) on costs addressed risks substantively.

"A stunningly low number of authors, only two out of 146 articles (1.4%), critically reflect upon ethical implications, challenges and risks of applying AI in education."
