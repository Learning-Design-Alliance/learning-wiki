Evidence¶

Catherine P. Bradshaw 2012¶

Catherine P. Bradshaw, Tracy E. Waasdorp, Philip J. Leaf. (2012). Effects of School-Wide Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports on Child Behavior Problems. Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2012-0243

q3 · i0 · causal · r3

Multilevel HLM in the 4-year group RCT showed children in the intervention schools "had better emotion regulation than those in the control" (ES = –0.11), based on teacher TOCA-C ratings over 5 time points.

"A similar effect was observed on emotion regulation (g = 0.03, t= 2.30, P , .05, ES = –0.11), such that children in the intervention schools had better emotion regulation than those in the control"
