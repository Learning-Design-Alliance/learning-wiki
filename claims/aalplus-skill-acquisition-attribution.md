## Evidence

### Schwill Simon 2020

Schwill Simon, Hundertmark Jan, Fahrbach-Veeser Johanna, Eicher Christiane, Tonchev Pencho, Kurczyk Sonia, Szecsenyi Joachim, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). The AaLplus near-peer teaching program in Family Medicine strengthens basic medical skills—A five-year retrospective study. PLoS ONE 15(5): e0233748. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233748

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-report item in the five-year participant survey asking whether each skill was learned within AaLplus or elsewhere; the study reports "90.3% (of n = 1391)" for anamnesis and 91.0%–98.5% for physical examination skills.

> "On average, 90.3% (of n = 1391) reported that they had learned to perform a structured anamnesis through AaLplus."

### Schwill Simon 2020 (2)

Schwill Simon, Hundertmark Jan, Fahrbach-Veeser Johanna, Eicher Christiane, Tonchev Pencho, Kurczyk Sonia, Szecsenyi Joachim, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). The AaLplus near-peer teaching program in Family Medicine strengthens basic medical skills—A five-year retrospective study. PLoS ONE 15(5): e0233748. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233748

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same self-report survey item for practical skills; the study prints "56.8% of n = 1383" for hand disinfection, "44.8% of n = 1371" for blood pressure measurement, and 73.7% for venepuncture.

> "More than half of the all participants reported this to be the case for hand disinfection (56.8% of n = 1383), less than half for blood pressure measurement (44.8% of n = 1371), and 73.7% (of n = 1382) of participants for venepuncture."
