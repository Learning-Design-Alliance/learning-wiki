## Evidence

### Kiver Benjamin 2025

Kiver Benjamin, Berberat Pascal O., Gartmeier Martin. (2025). The relation between teaching-related self-efficacy and general job-related well-being – a cross-sectional study among young physicians. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001777

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

T-test comparison in the cross-sectional survey between the 78 physicians with prior didactics training and the 204 without, recorded by a dichotomous yes/no item; trained physicians "rated themselves significantly more self-efficacious" (t(282)=2.684, p=0.008; M=2.96, SD=0.32 vs M=2.84, SD=0.35).

> "They rated themselves significantly more self-efficacious (t(282)=2.684, p=0.008) in teaching (M=2.96, SD=0.32) than the comparison group who had not previously attended such training (M=2.84, SD=0.35)"
