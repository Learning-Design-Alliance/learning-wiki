## Evidence

### Plana et al. 2012

Gutiérrez-Colon Plana, M., Gallardo Torrano, P. and Grova, M. E. (2012). SMS as a learning tool: an experimental study. The EUROCALL Review, 20(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1064974

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test/post-test comparison (Table 3) of the experimental group and a control group enrolled in the same course; pre-test means were 30.3 (68.8%) vs 29.5 (67%), post-test means 10.5 (52.5%) vs 7.95 (39.75%). No statistical test is reported.

> "From these results, we can safely conclude that the experimental group has outperformed the control group."
