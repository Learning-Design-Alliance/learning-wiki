# Teachers' operational knowledge and non-traditional textbook practice format do not relate to students' equivalence understanding

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Şimşek 2021

Şimşek, E., Xenidou-Dervou, I., Hunter, J., Gillon Dowens, M., Pang, J., Lee, Y., McNeil, N., Kirkland, P., & Jones, I. (2021). Factors Associated with Children's Understanding of Mathematical Equivalence: An Investigation across Six Countries. https://figshare.com/s/78625a6b0061200db6b7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel SEM (Model 1) found no significant relation between teachers' operational knowledge and overall equivalence, nor between non-traditional textbook practice and overall equivalence. No effect sizes printed for these null paths.

> "At the class level, we found a significant positive relation between overall equivalence and teachers' relational knowledge, whereas neither teachers' operational knowledge nor non-traditional practice in textbooks related to overall equivalence."
