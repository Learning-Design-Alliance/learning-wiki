## Evidence

### Aaron Cochrane and C. Shawn Green 2021

Aaron Cochrane and C. Shawn Green. (2021). Trajectories of performance change indicate multiple dissociable links between working memory and fluid intelligence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00111-w

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in the same 87-participant experiment found a printed correlation of r = 0.96 (CI = [0.93,0.97], BF log3 = 89.19) between overall and final WM accuracy. The authors state this "statistical near-isomorphism" supports interpreting ending accuracy as an index of stable performance.

> "the relation between overall and ﬁnal WM accuracy: r(85) = 0.96, CI = [0.93,0.97], BF log3 = 89.19). This statistical near-isomorphism supports the interpretation that, throughout our results, ending accuracy is an index of stable performance and can be interpreted as a proxy for the standard overall-accuracy measure"
