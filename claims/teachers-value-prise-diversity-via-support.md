## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2020

Martin O. Archer. (2020). Schools of all backgrounds can do physics research: On the accessibility and equity of the PRiSE approach to independent research projects. arXiv. [doi:10.5194/gc-2020-37](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-2020-37)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Thematic analysis of open-question teacher questionnaires and informal comments gathered across the programme; teachers "value the diversity within the programme" and attribute the equity to the exceptionally high level of support provided by PRiSE.

> "Qualitative feedback from teachers have shown that they value the diversity within the programme, seeing the ability of students from different schools and backgrounds to interact as equals at PRiSE conferences as a positive aspect."
