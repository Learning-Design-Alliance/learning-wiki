# Only one of 10 identified studies of InsideTrack© Coaching met WWC group design standards without reservations

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### study-screening

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2019, November). Supporting Postsecondary Success intervention report: InsideTrack© Coaching. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/696

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The report's research summary section describes its literature screening under WWC Standards version 3.0 and the Supporting Postsecondary Success protocol version 3.0. Seven studies failed because "equivalence of the analytic intervention and comparison groups is necessary and not demonstrated"; two were ineligible for using an ineligible design.

> "The WWC identified 10 studies that investigated the effectiveness of InsideTrack© Coaching (Figure 1): • 1 study meets WWC group design standards without reservations • 0 studies meet WWC group design standards with reservations • 7 studies do not meet WWC group design standards • 2 studies are ineligible for review"
