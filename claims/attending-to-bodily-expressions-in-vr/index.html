Evidence¶

Kenneth Silseth 2024¶

Kenneth Silseth, Rolf Steier, Hans Christian Arnseth. (2024). Exploring students’ immersive VR experiences as resources for collaborative meaning making and learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09413-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Analysis of a second group's dialogue about the scene with a mother telling the story of her son's death. The authors report that "the students attend to bodily expressions as reflections of people's emotional states," placing students in a community of listeners as the film's bystanders pay attention.

"The students attend to bodily expressions as reflections of people’s emotional states, and these reflections become a resource to engage with the story of the mother."
