`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Authors' reported instructional experience in a graduate health sciences evidence analysis course, offered as a worked applied example; no student-level data were gathered. The article states students "are more likely to understand and appreciate the quality of each evidence analysis step" under step-linking requirements.

> "We have found that students are more likely to understand and appreciate the quality of each evidence analysis step if they are required to link the product of a discrete step with earlier and subsequent steps"
