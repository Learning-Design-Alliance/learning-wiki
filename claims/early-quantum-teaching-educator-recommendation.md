## Evidence

### Apekshya Ghimire and Chandralekha Singh 2025

Apekshya Ghimire and Chandralekha Singh. (2025). Reflections of quantum educators on strategies to diversify the second quantum revolution. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1119/5.0223983](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0223983)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative think-aloud interviews with five college quantum educators from four institutions; this is an educator recommendation, not a tested intervention. One educator argued that with statistics, data science, coding and linear algebra emphasis, "once you've got that, you can teach any quantum you want…even in high school".

> "Some educators emphasized the importance of teaching quantum concepts early, e.g., in high school and early in college to students from diverse backgrounds. This exposure is important because targeting diverse high schools has the potential to introduce exciting quantum concepts and careers to students from diverse backgrounds"
