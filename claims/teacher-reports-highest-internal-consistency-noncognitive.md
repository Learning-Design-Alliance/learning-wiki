### Shuaizhang Feng 2022

Shuaizhang Feng, Yujie Han, James J. Heckman, Tim Kautz. (2022). Comparing the Reliability and Predictive Power of Child, Teacher, and Guardian Reports of Noncognitive Skills. The Proceedings of the National Academy Sciences, vol. 119, issue 6. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/comparing-the-reliability-and-predictive-power-of-child-teacher-and-guardian-reports-of-noncognitive

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

A longitudinal survey compared children's noncognitive skills as reported by children, guardians, and teachers. The study reports that "teacher reports have the highest internal consistency" among the three respondent types.

> "Although reports from all three types of respondents are correlated with each other, teacher reports have the highest internal consistency and are the most predictive of children's later cognitive outcomes and behavior in school."
