# Solving the easier problem (INC) with the multimodal system produced more favorable engagement, memorability and confidence ratings than solving the harder problem (ANA) with it

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mann-Whitney U tests showed favorable MuDoC ratings when INC was solved with it, while ANA-group ratings were not significantly better than neutral on overall experience, confidence or trustworthiness. [→ Karan Taneja 2025](#karan-taneja-2025)

## Evidence

### Karan Taneja 2025

Karan Taneja, Anjali Singh, and Ashok K. Goel. (2025). Towards a Multimodal Document-grounded Conversational AI System for Education. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98462-4_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98462-4_12)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Order-impact analysis comparing response distributions via Mann-Whitney U tests. For the ANA group (n=15), one-sample Wilcoxon tests found ratings not significantly better than neutral for overall experience (T=22.0, p=0.095), confidence (T=33.5, p=0.222) and trustworthiness (T=17.0, p=0.076).

> "When participants solved the easier problem (INC) using MuDoC, it led to favorable ratings for MuDoC in terms of being engaging (U=66.0, p=0.047), memorable (U=65.0, p=0.042), and perceived confidence (U=50.5, p=0.009)."
