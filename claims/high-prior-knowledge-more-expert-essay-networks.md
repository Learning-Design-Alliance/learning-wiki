`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-study essay task with 68 Study 2 students (34 high-scoring, 34 low-scoring on the prior final exam); essays were transformed into networks with ALA-Reader and tested nonparametrically because data were not normal. "H-group students' essay network overlap were structured much more like the expert network"; no effect size printed.
