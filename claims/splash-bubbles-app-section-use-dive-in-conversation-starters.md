# Families used multiple sections of the Splash and Bubbles for Parents app, with Dive In the most used and Let's Talk About It conversation starters rated most helpful within it

## Evidence

### Leones 2021

Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Huynh, K., Gedney, M., & Dominguez, X. (2021). Splash and Bubbles for Parents App: Station Study Report [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/120

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey data on which app sections families used. The app includes three main sections: Dive In, Ocean Guide, and Activities. Nearly all families (92.86%) indicated using Dive In.

> "Most families used at least two of the app sections described above (78.57%) while the remaining families used only one section (21.43%). Of the six families that used only one section, two thirds reported using Dive In with the remaining third using Activities."

### Leones 2021 (2)

Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Huynh, K., Gedney, M., & Dominguez, X. (2021). Splash and Bubbles for Parents App: Station Study Report [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/120

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey ratings among 26 families using Dive In. Most rated the Let's Talk About It prompts a 4 (57.7%) or 5 (26.9%) on a 1-5 helpfulness scale, with a smaller percentage rating them a 3 (15.4%).

> "Of the 26 families that reported using the Dive In section, most indicated the Let's Talk About It section (73.08%) as the most helpful section followed by Characters (15.38%), Clips (7.69%), and We'll Learn About It (3.85%)."
