Evidence¶

Mukherjee 2023¶

Mukherjee, S., Rohles, B., Distler, V., Lenzini, G., & Koenig, V. (2023). The effects of privacy-non-invasive interventions on cheating prevention and user experience in unproctored online assessments: An empirical study. Computers and Education, 207, 104925. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104925

q3 · i? · causal · r2

OLS regressions in the between-subjects experiment (N = 997) showed no significant effect of any intervention on UEQ-S pragmatic or hedonic UX dimensions. Similarly, PANAS positive (p = 0.559) and negative (p = 0.283) emotions showed no significant effects.

"The overall impact of the interventions was small and statistically non-significant on both pragmatic (p = 0.373) and hedonic qualities (p = 0.743)."
