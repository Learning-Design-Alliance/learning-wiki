# Self-efficacy correlated positively and test anxiety negatively with FCI post-test and exam scores

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Self-efficacy factor scores correlated significantly with FCI post-test (r = .42) and exam (r = .41). [→ Jared B. Stang 2020](#jared-b-stang-2020)
`q2 i2` Test anxiety factor scores correlated significantly but negatively with FCI post-test (r = −.24) and exam (r = −.24). [→ Jared B. Stang 2020 (2)](#jared-b-stang-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Jared B. Stang 2020

Jared B. Stang, Emily Altiere, Joss Ives, and Patrick J. Dubois (2020). Exploring the contributions of self-efficacy and test anxiety to gender differences in assessments. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.stang](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.stang)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of survey factor scores against assessment scores in the introductory physics course: self-efficacy correlated with both outcomes, with effect size r = .42 for FCI post-test and r = .41 for the exam ('r(385) =.42, p < .001'; 'r(436) =.41, p < .001').

> "Self-efﬁcacy was signiﬁcantly correlated with FCI post-test, r(385) =.42, p < .001, and the exam, r(436) =.41, p < .001."

### Jared B. Stang 2020 (2)

Jared B. Stang, Emily Altiere, Joss Ives, and Patrick J. Dubois (2020). Exploring the contributions of self-efficacy and test anxiety to gender differences in assessments. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.stang](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.stang)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in the same course: test anxiety correlated negatively with both outcomes, with effect size r = −.24 for FCI post-test and r = −.24 for the exam ('r(385) =−.24, p < .001'), 'though with a smaller effect size than self-efﬁcacy'.

> "Test anxiety was signiﬁcantly correlated with FCI post-test, r(385) =−.24, p < .001, and exam, r(436) =−.24, p < .001, though with a smaller effect size than self-efﬁcacy."
