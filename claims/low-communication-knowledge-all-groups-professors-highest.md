### Moezzi Masoumeh 2024

Moezzi Masoumeh, Rasekh Sara, Zare Elahe, Karimi Masoud. (2024). Evaluating clinical communication skills of medical students, assistants, and professors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-05015-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 289 participants (51 professors, 72 assistants, 76 interns, 90 externs) at Iranian teaching hospitals using a researcher-made 10-item knowledge questionnaire; one-way ANOVA gave F = 5.064, P = 0.002, with professor mean 4.04 versus extern mean 3.21. The article reports "the knowledge mean scores of all groups participating in the study were in the low category."

> "the knowledge mean scores of all groups participating in the study were in the low category. The highest knowledge score was related to professors, significantly different from other studied groups (P = 0.002)."
