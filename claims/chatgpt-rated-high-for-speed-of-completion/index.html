Elizondo-García ME 2025¶

Elizondo-García ME, Hernández-De la Cerda H, Benavides-García IG, Caratozzolo P and Membrillo-Hernández J (2025) Who is solving the challenge? The use of ChatGPT in mathematics and biology courses using challenge-based learning. Front. Educ. 10:1417642. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2025.1417642

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Item-level analysis of the concluding survey (Likert scale 1–10) administered after the ChatGPT-assisted activities in both courses. The high scores in this dimension suggest, in the article's words, that "students perceived ChatGPT as a helpful and user-friendly tool for completing tasks."

"Q1, which evaluated whether ChatGPT allowed students to complete activities more quickly, received some of the highest scores across both courses (9.36 ± 2.01 in “Fundamentals of Biological Systems” and 9.76 ± 0.76 in “Mathematics and Data Science for Decision Making”)."
