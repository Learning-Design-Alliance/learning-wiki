## Evidence

### Vinesh Rajpaul 2014

Vinesh Rajpaul, Saalih Allie, Sarah-Louise Blyth. (2014). The introductory astronomy course at the University of Cape Town: probing student perspectives. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020126](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020126)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-course SKA question answered by 73 students and post-course three-option question answered by 91 students. The article reports 76 (84%) post-course chose that "radio telescopes collect a particular type of electromagnetic wave", against 16 (22%) pre-course giving radio/EM-wave answers.

> "This is, perhaps, the reason for the reduction in responses in the post-course answers referring to the SKA collecting sound waves, since this misconception was directly addressed in the course material."
