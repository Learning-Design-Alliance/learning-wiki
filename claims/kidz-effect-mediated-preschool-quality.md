# The association between KiDZ attendance and age-12 mathematics is mediated by preschool process quality

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Treatment effects on age-12 mathematics diminish when controlling for preschool quality, and the indirect effect of project participation via preschool quality is significant. [→ Lehrl 2017](#lehrl-2017)

## Evidence

### Lehrl 2017

Lehrl, Simone, Kluczniok, Katharina, Rossbach, Hans-Guenther, & Anders, Yvonne (2017). Long term persistence of preschool intervention on children's mathematical development: Results from the German model project "Kindergarten of the Future in Bavaria." Global Education Review, 4(3), 70-87. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1158198

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pathway modeling testing indirect effects within the same longitudinal quasi-experiment; the article reports the indirect effect "b = .20, p < .05" and that treatment coefficients become negative and marginal (-.20, p < .10) once quality is in the model (Table 2, models 2-4).

> "As treatment effects diminish when controlling for preschool quality, indirect effects of project participation via preschool quality was tested via pathway modeling and showed significant effects (b = .20, p < .05)."
