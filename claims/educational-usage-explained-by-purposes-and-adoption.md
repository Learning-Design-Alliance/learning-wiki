## Evidence

### Arteaga Sánchez 2015

Arteaga Sánchez, R., Cortijo, V., & Javed, U. (2015). Students' perceptions of Facebook for academic purposes. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2013.08.012

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

SEM analysis of the 214 valid survey responses; the article reports adoption's indirect effect on educational usage via purposes of usage, with 54.4% of variance explained.

> "Facebook adoption had a significant positive indirect effect on educational usage of Facebook (β = 0.66; p < .01) and the total variance explained in educational usage of Facebook either directly by purpose of Facebook usage or indirectly by Facebook adoption is 54.4%"
