The article's conceptual analysis enumerates three challenge domains, citing prior work for each: "epistemic monoculture" and synthetic misinformation for knowledge; cognitive offloading degrading critical thinking for process; and environmental costs plus Global North-South dependencies for impact. No empirical data are offered in this article itself.

"The challenges are exponential and multi -dimensional, extending across three interrelated domains: knowledge itself; the epistemic processes through which knowledge is acquired; and the broader socio-environmental impacts of knowledge production."
