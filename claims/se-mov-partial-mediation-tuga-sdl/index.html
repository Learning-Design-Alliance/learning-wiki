Hui Zhao & Huijuan Gu 2026¶

Hui Zhao & Huijuan Gu. (2026). Thoughtless use of generative artificial intelligence and college students’ self-directed learning: a multi-group SEM analysis of gender differences. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-54337-y

Bootstrapping mediation analysis within PLS-SEM (Table 8). All three indirect paths from TUGA to SDL were significant, with the sequential SE–MOV path at "β = −0.20, t = 2.75, p = .003".

"the indirect effects through MOV (β = −0.37, t = 5.52, p < .001), SE (β = −0.09, t = 2.50, p = .012), and the sequential path of SE and MOV (β = −0.20, t = 2.75, p = .003) were all significant."

Same mediation analysis (Table 8, all paths labelled PM, partial mediation). Because the direct effect stayed significant, the article concludes the mediators "partially mediate" the TUGA–SDL relationship.

"At the same time, the direct effect remained significant (β = −0.42, t = 6.76, p < .001). These results indicate that SE, MOV , and the sequential SE–MOV pathway partially mediate the relationship between the TUGA and SDL."
