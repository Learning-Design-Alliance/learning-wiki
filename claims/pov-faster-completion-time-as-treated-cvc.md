### Liao Po-Hsiang 2026 (2)

Liao Po-Hsiang, Chen Chung-Ting, Lee Yi-Tzu, Lee Cheng-Hao, How Chorng-Kuang, Huang Hsien-Hao, Hsu Teh-Fu. (2026). Educational benefits of pre-class videos and viewing perspectives on ultrasound-guided central venous catheterization training: a prospective randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08870-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

As-treated analysis reclassifying participants who failed the cartoon-character video-completion check into control. The quote reports "a statistically significant reduction in the POV group compared to Control (p = 0.047)".

> "The results showed median (IQR) times of 22.09 (18.74–24.79), 19.71 (17.42–23.50), and 18.22 (15.78–21.85) minutes for the Control, F-t-F video, and POV video groups, respectively, with a statistically significant reduction in the POV group compared to Control (p = 0.047)."
