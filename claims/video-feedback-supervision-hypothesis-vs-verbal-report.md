### Kühne Franziska 2020

Kühne Franziska, Heinze Peter Eric, Weck Florian. (2020). Standardized patients in psychotherapy training and clinical supervision: study protocol for a randomized controlled trial. Trials 21, 276. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-020-4172-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Hypothesis in the protocol's Objectives section for the second experiment: direct, behaviorally based supervision in which supervisees show their role-play videos to a supervisor versus verbal reporting of experiences (supervision as usual). A pre-registered directional hypothesis with no outcome data yet.

> "Second, we expect that post-supervision, psychology students randomly assigned to supervision based on treatment videos will display more therapeutic competencies than the students who described their treatment verbally to the supervisor (hypothesis 3)."

### Kühne Franziska 2020 (2)

Kühne Franziska, Heinze Peter Eric, Weck Florian. (2020). Standardized patients in psychotherapy training and clinical supervision: study protocol for a randomized controlled trial. Trials 21, 276. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-020-4172-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Durability hypothesis for the supervision comparison, stated in the protocol's Objectives section; sessions are videotaped at baseline, post, and 3-month follow-up and rated by two blinded independent raters. No results are reported in this protocol.

> "Again, we expect the competence differences between the two groups to persist at the 3-month follow-up (hypothesis 4)."
