Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Phonological Awareness Training. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q2 · i? · causal · r?

A quasi-experimental study of 19 three- to five-year-old children at a private suburban Ohio preschool, comparing sound box and sound sort techniques on four measures. The report states "The average improvement index for phonological processing is +11 percentile points, with a range of –10 to +29 percentile points across findings."

"The findings favored the concrete representation of the sounds in words (sound box) condition over the categorization of words based on sounds alone (sound sort) condition."
