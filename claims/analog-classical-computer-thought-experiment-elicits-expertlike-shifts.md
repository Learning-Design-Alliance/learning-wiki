Investigating student interpretations of the differences between classical and quantum computers: Are quantum computers just analog classical computers?. Interview exchanges in Fall 2021 graduate and Spring 2022 undergraduate interviews show the thought experiment prompting more expertlike responses; the authors state it was "a powerful tool for helpin g students develop a more expertlike perspective."

> "as soon as the interviewer brought up the existence of an analog classical computer, Brandon al- most immediately made the connection back to a prior prob- lem he had solved regarding entanglement and identiﬁed en- tanglement as an essential quantum property"
