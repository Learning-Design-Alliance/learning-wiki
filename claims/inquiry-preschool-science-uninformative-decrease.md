# Percentage of uninformative explorations decreased from pre-test to post-test in the intervention group but not controls

> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2016 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` The interaction of pre-test/post-test with group for uninformative explorations was significant, with the percentage decreasing only for experimentals. [→ Dejonckheere 2016](#dejonckheere-2016)

`q2 i2` The proportion of uninformative (confounding) experiments decreased from pretest to post-test in experimentals but not controls, with a significant interaction (partial η² = 0.110). [→ Dejonckheere 2016](#dejonckheere-2016)

## Evidence

### Dejonckheere 2016

Dejonckheere, P. J. N., De Wit, N., Van de Keere, K., & Vervaet, S. (2016). Exploring the classroom: Teaching science in early childhood. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 8(4), 537-558. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1109852.

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same sample, the ratio of uninformative to total explorations was analyzed. The article reports "the interaction of pre-test/post-test with group (experimentals vs. controls) for the number of uninformative explorations was significant, F(1, 49)= 5.57; p<0.022".

> "However, the interaction of pre-test/post-test with group (experimentals vs. controls) for the number of uninformative explorations was significant, F(1, 49)= 5.57; p<0.022 (see Figure 5)."
