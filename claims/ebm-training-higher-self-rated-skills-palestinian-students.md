## Evidence

### Hamza K 2026

Hamza K, Qawasmeh H, Ahmad Rasha K, Alkhateeb S, Awawdeh F, Douden B, Zrineh A, Akwan R. (2026). Knowledge, attitudes, and skills towards evidence-based medicine among Palestinian undergraduate medical students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08750-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 709 undergraduate medical students at five Palestinian universities; skills self-rated on a 5-point Likert scale and compared by prior EBM training using Mann-Whitney U tests. The study reports "higher mean scores across all skill domains" with p < 0.001 for all comparisons; no effect sizes printed.

> "Students with prior EBM training reported higher mean scores across all skill domains compared with those without prior training (p < 0.001 for all comparisons)."
