## Evidence

### Lydon Sinéad 2019

Lydon Sinéad, Reid McDermott Bronwyn, Ryan Ethel, O’Connor Paul, Dempsey Sharon, Walsh Chloe, Byrne Dara. (2019). Can simulation-based education and precision teaching improve paediatric trainees’ behavioural fluency in performing lumbar puncture? A pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1553-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intervention phase of the pilot study: 10 paediatric senior house officers trained on the LumbarPunctureBaby task trainer until they met the fluency criterion of "100% accuracy within 19 mins and 53 s achieved in two consecutive trials". The article reports the trial and time means descriptively; no effect size is printed for this outcome.

> "Participants required a mean of five trials (SD = 1.2) to achieve fluency and mean total training time was 95 mins, 24 s (SD = 31 mins, 6 s)."
