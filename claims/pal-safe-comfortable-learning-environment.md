## Evidence

### Carr Sandra E. 2016

Carr Sandra E., Brand Gabrielle, Wei Li, Wright Helen, Nicol Pam, Metcalfe Helene, Saunders Julie, Payne John, Seubert Liza, Foley Laurie. (2016). “Helping someone with a skill sharpens it in your own mind”: a mixed method study exploring health professions students experiences of Peer Assisted Learning (PAL). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0566-8

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of student quotes across settings; students described peer learning as “less intimidating for students…more comfortable to voice opinions and ask questions” and less stressful because it was formative.

> "One of the most noticeable shared themes to emerge from the data was that PAL provided a safe comfortable learning environment where the students felt they could ask questions and ‘felt safe to make mistakes’"
