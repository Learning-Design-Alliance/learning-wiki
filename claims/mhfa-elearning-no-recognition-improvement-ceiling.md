## Evidence

### Davies E. Bethan 2018

Davies E. Bethan, Beever Emmeline, Glazebrook Cris. (2018). A pilot randomised controlled study of the mental health first aid eLearning course with UK medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1154-x

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

McNemar's tests on vignette recognition in the pilot RCT found no change in either arm. The discussion attributes this to "a ceiling effect as found in other studies", since baseline recognition was already high.

> "In both the ITT and non-ITT analyses, there were no pre-post changes in identification of the mental health condition (McNemar's test p = .125 for control, p = 1.00 for intervention)."
