## Evidence

### Auerbach A. J. 2018

Auerbach A. J., Higgins M., Brickman P., Andrews T. C. (2018). Teacher Knowledge for Active-Learning Instruction: Expert–Novice Comparison Reveals Differences. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-07-0149

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Count-based generalized linear model comparison of lesson-analysis survey responses from 14 experts and 29 novices; the estimate for this component was 4.7 (95% CI 2.1–11.4), a statistically significant expert advantage after multiple-comparison adjustment.
