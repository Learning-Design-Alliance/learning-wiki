# A year of Learning through REAL Projects had no clear impact on Year 7 pupils' literacy attainment

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Pupils in intervention schools showed no detectable literacy benefit relative to control pupils; the printed effect size of -0.13 was small and its confidence interval crossed zero. [→ Menzies 2016](#menzies-2016)

## Evidence

### Menzies 2016

Menzies, V., Hewitt, C., Kokotsaki, D., Collyer, C. and Wiggins, A. (2016). Project Based Learning: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/public/files/Projects/EEF_Project_Protocol_ProjectBasedLearning.pdf

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A two-armed cluster RCT with 12 intervention and 12 control schools measured literacy with the Progress in English 12 assessment after one year of delivery. Table 1 prints an effect size of -0.13 (95% CI -0.31, 0.05), equivalent to -2 months' progress, so no literacy benefit was detected.

> "The trial results did not find that the PBL progr amme had an impact either on the pupils’ literacy performance (as measured by Progress in Eng lish 12 tests), engagement, or attendance."
