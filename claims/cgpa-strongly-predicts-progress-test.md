## Evidence

### Dabaliz Al-Awwab 2017

Dabaliz Al-Awwab, Kaadan Samy, Dabbagh M. Marwan, Barakat Abdulaziz, Shareef Mohammad Abrar, Al-Tannir Mohamad, Obeidat Akef, Mohamed Ayman. (2017). Predictive validity of pre-admission assessments on medical student performance. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5a10.04e1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A linear regression predicted PT performance from cGPA in the retrospective dataset; the overall equation was significant (F(1,387)=59.006, p<0.0001, R2 of 0.132). The article states "on average, one-point increase in cGPA predicts for almost 11 points increase in the PT."

> "The regression further revealed that the cGPA was a strongly significant predictor of students' PT performance (p<0.0001, B=10.904, t=7.682)"
