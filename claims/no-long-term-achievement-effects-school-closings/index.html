Evidence¶

Marisa de la Torre 2009¶

Marisa de la Torre, Julia Gwynne. (2009). When Schools Close: Effects on Displaced Students in Chicago Public Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago Urban Education Institute. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/when-schools-close-effects-displaced-students-chicago-public-schools

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Long-run trajectory analysis on a reduced sample of students with data three years after closings (Figure 2): reading -1.49 at two years and +1.41 at three years, math -0.32 and +0.21, none statistically significant. Equivalence was not formally tested.

"In reading, displaced students were about one-and-a-half months behind in learning two years after their schools closed; however, this difference between their actual learning and their expected learning is not statistically significant."
