## Evidence

### Rebecca 2017

Rebecca, Ufonabasi Etiubon & Nsimeneabasi Michael Udoh. (2017). Effects of Practical Activities and Manual on Science Students’ Academic Performance on Solubility in Uruan Local Education Authority of Akwa Ibom State. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131863

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of posttest scores with pretest as covariate in a non-randomized pretest–posttest quasi-experiment with two intact classes (n=104). The main effect of instructional materials was not significant (p=.602), so "the null hypothesis one is accepted". Mean posttest scores were 68.70 (practical activities) and 68.10 (practical manual).

> "In Table 4, the calculated Probability value (P-value) .602 of the main effect of instructional materials is greater than the declared Probability value (alpha level) .05. Therefore, the null hypothesis one is accepted."
