## Evidence

### Qarareh 2016

Qarareh, A. O. (2016). The Effect of Using the Constructivist Learning Model in Teaching Science on the Achievement and Scientific Thinking of 8th Grade Students. International Education Studies, 9(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n7p178

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 136 eighth graders; post scientific thinking means were 15.35 (constructivist) vs 11.90 (traditional), with a significant teaching-method effect per ANCOVA.

> "there is statistically significant difference at (α= 0.05) for the effect of the constructivist Learning model on the achievement and scientific thinking in favor of experimental group"
