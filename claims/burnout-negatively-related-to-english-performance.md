### Pham Thi Thuy Dung 2024

Pham Thi Thuy Dung, Duong Nam Tien. (2024). Investigating learning burnout and academic performance among management students: a longitudinal study in English courses. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01725-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

SEM path analysis; current English performance regressed on Time-2 burnout gave a significant negative path (-0.092, t = -2.296), and Table 3 reports H7 as Supported. Performance was standardized to T-scores (mean 50, SD 10) across schools.

> "There is a significant negative relationship between English learning performance and burnout."
