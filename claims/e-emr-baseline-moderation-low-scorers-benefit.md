`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal moderation models in the cluster-randomized pilot trial tested condition × centered baseline interactions for each outcome. The article reports the three significant interaction coefficients with their B, SE, and p values as printed.

> "There was a two-way interaction between condition and baseline scores for three variables: STR (B = − 0.16(0.08), p = 0.05), conduct problems (B = − 0.24(0.10), p = 0.02), and total problems (B = − 0.15(0.07), p = 0.047)."
