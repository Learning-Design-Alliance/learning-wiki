Arthur Heche. (1909). A Report on the Teaching and Practice of Hygiene in the Public Normal Schools of the United States. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducreducpsyc

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire returns on sex hygiene from 84 schools. Twelve schools report a course, but several cases are described as doubtful. Only 27 answered affirmatively on using nature study and biology as an avenue to sex hygiene.

> "Twenty-nine of these do not touch the problem of sex hygiene either in direct instruction or in incidental treatment in other courses."
