Related Claims¶

  • Students perceive peer interaction—companionship, support as a social buffer, and cooperation toward shared goals—as a key element of fun in learning — related
  • Cooperation compared with individualistic efforts typically results in higher achievement, greater retention, and greater social competence and self-esteem — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Peer victimization is associated with more school absences and lower grades and achievement among school-aged students — related
  • Cooperative learning reduced emotional problems and increased peer relatedness for all students, not only marginalized students — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Cooperative learning significantly reduced bullying among marginalized (low-engagement) middle school students in a cluster randomized trial — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Cooperative learning significantly reduced perceived stress among marginalized middle school students — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
