## Evidence

### Pečjak and Pirc 2018

Sonja Pečjak, Tina Pirc. (2018). Developing Summarizing Skills in 4th Grade Students: Intervention Programme Effects. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, June 2018, Volume 10, Issue 5, 571-581. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2018541306

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group paired t-tests on summarizing achievement from pretest to follow-up: EG t(101) = 5.975, p < .001; CG t(68) = 1.901, p = .061, a smaller and non-significant gain.

> "EG made an improvement from Summ_1 to Summ_3 by 1.92 points on average, which shows a significant progress (t(101)= 5.975; p< .001). There was also an improvement from Summ_1 to Summ_3 in CG, but it was smaller (0.79 point on average) and not significant (t(68)= 1.901; p= .061)."
