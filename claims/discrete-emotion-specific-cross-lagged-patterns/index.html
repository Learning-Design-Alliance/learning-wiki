Goetz 2021¶

Goetz, T., Bieleke, M., Gogol, K., van Tartwijk, J., Mainhard, T., Lipnevich, A. A., & Pekrun, R. (2021). Getting along and feeling good: Reciprocal associations between student-teacher relationship quality and students' emotions. Learning and Instruction, 71, 101349. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101349

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Model 9 estimated all 24 domain-by-emotion combinations in the sample of 535 students, plotted with 95% confidence intervals. The article reports significant relationship-to-emotion paths "mainly in the domain of positive emotions" and states "No such associations were observed for anxiety and boredom."

"better initial relationships were associated with more enjoyment in math, German, and French classes and with more pride in German and French classes one year later."

Related Claims¶
