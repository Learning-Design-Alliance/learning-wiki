## Evidence

### Hooper 2013

Hooper, S. R., Costa, L.-J. C., McBee, M., Anderson, K. L., Yerby, D. C., Childress, A., & Knuth, S. B. (2013). A written language intervention for at-risk second grade students: a randomized controlled trial of the process assessment of the learner lesson plans in a tier 2 response-to-intervention (RtI) model. Ann. of Dyslexia, 63, 44-64. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-011-0056-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Contrast analysis within the Low-g latent class of the RCT sample; pre-treatment means did not differ significantly so no adjustment was needed, and the treated group significantly outscored the untreated group with Cohen's d=0.83.

> "For the fall of third grade, however, the treated group ( Mest =8.26) significantly outscored the untreated group ( Mest =5.23), t (474)=3.11, p=.002, with a large effect size being noted (Cohen 's d=0.83)."

### Hooper 2013 (2)

Hooper, S. R., Costa, L.-J. C., McBee, M., Anderson, K. L., Yerby, D. C., Childress, A., & Knuth, S. B. (2013). A written language intervention for at-risk second grade students: a randomized controlled trial of the process assessment of the learner lesson plans in a tier 2 response-to-intervention (RtI) model. Ann. of Dyslexia, 63, 44-64. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-011-0056-y

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted contrast within the Specific-Deficit latent class, where randomization failed to produce baseline equivalence and scores were adjusted by the pre-treatment mean difference before comparison; the printed effect size was Cohen's d adj=0.42.

> "For the last measurement point in third grade, the adjusted mean for the treated group ( Mest, adj=13.5 7) was significantly larger than the mean for the untreated group (Mest=11.55) by a larger margin, t (474)=2.82, p=.005, with a small to moderate effect size being present (Cohen 's dadj=0.42)."
