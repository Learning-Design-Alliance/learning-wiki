# The integrated model explains substantially less variance in science achievement at eleventh grade (17%) compared to fifth (41%) and eighth (38%) grades

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The proportion of achievement variance explained by the model decreases from 41% at fifth grade to 38% at eighth grade to 17% at eleventh grade. [→ Mason 2012](#mason-2012)

## Evidence

### Mason 2012

Mason, L., Boscolo, P., Tornatora, M. C., & Ronconi, L. (2012). Besides knowledge: a cross-sectional study on the relations between epistemic beliefs, achievement goals, self-beliefs, and achievement in science. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-012-9210-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM results across three grade levels showed R² for achievement of 0.41 (fifth grade, n = 213), 0.38 (eighth grade, n = 202), and 0.17 (eleventh grade, n = 281). The authors note this is a much smaller proportion at eleventh grade.

> "This model explained 17 % of achievement in science variance, which is a much smaller proportion compared with the previous models (R2 = 0.17)."
