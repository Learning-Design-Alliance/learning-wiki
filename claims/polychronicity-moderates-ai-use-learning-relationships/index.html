Evidence¶

Gao L 2026¶

Gao L, Sun Y and Khan SU (2026) From enhancement to over-reliance: a mixed-method study of generative AI and sustainable learning performance. Front. Psychol. 17:1847369. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1847369

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Moderation effects were estimated in the PLS-SEM model via interaction terms; the study reports that "polychronicity moderates key relationships". The hypothesized directions were weaker benefits and stronger over-reliance for high-polychronicity students.

"Furthermore, polychronicity moderates key relationships, indicating that multitasking tendencies shape both AI dependency and learning eﬀectiveness."
