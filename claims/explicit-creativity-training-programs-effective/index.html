Related Claims¶

  • Implicit pedagogical approaches—inquiry, open-ended tasks, cross-disciplinary and student-centered learning—contribute significantly to creativity development — related
  • Ideation-based interventions are a robustly implemented explicit approach that improves creative performance — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Systemic barriers—rigid curricula, limited teacher training, and innate-creativity misconceptions—marginalize creativity in mainstream education — related
  • Creativity-supportive school climates combine autonomy, psychological safety, teacher encouragement, and rich resources — related
  • Most reviewed studies defending domain-generality are minimalist, and none treats creativity as an innate faculty — related
