### Bustamante-Balén M 2019

Bustamante-Balén M, Satorres C, Puchades L, Navarro B, García-Morales N, Alonso N, Ponce M, Argüello L, Pons-Beltrán V. (2019). Non-guided self-learning program for high-proficiency optical diagnosis of diminutive and small colorectal lesions: A single-endoscopist pilot study. World Journal of Gastroenterology. [doi:10.3748/wjg.v25.i10.1278](https://doi.org/10.3748/wjg.v25.i10.1278)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Discussion analysis of the predefined 6-mo stopping period in the single-endoscopist assessment phase; the article reports specificity was "the most affected parameter" needing 200 lesions to recover.

> "Following the stopping period (which was not followed by a refresher course), almost all performance parameters dropped significantly. Specificity was the most affected parameter, and it took 200 lesions to reach previous levels."

### Bustamante-Balén M 2019 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same stopping-period analysis in the Discussion section, reporting the printed accuracy and NPV declines following the 6-month non-practice interval.
