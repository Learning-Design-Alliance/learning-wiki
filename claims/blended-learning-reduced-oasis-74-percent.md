## Evidence

### Ali-Masri Hadil 2018

Ali-Masri Hadil, Hassan Sahar, Fosse Erik, Zimmo Kaled M., Zimmo Mohammed, Ismail Khaled M. K., Vikanes Åse, Laine Katariina. (2018). Impact of electronic and blended learning programs for manual perineal support on incidence of obstetric anal sphincter injuries: a prospective interventional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1363-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Phase-3 (n = 692) followed the blended learning package combining the animated video with structured face-to-face training. The comparison of phase-3 against phase-2 shows a "significant reduction in OASIS incidence by 52%"; against baseline the adjusted odds ratio was 0.29 (CI 0.17–0.50, p < 0.001).

> "There was also a significant reduction in OASIS incidence by 52% from phase-2 to phase-3 (6.7% (42/626) to 3.2% (22/692), p = 0.003)."
