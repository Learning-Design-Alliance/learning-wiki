## Evidence

### Mehdizadeh Leila 2015

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Dacre Jane. (2015). Are the General Medical Council’s Tests of Competence fair to long standing doctors? A retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0362-x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation in the retrospective cohort of 95 general practitioners under investigation, shown in scatter plots (Figures 1 and 2). The OSCE association was "moderate positive" (r = 0.37, p < 0.001), with doctors qualified longest scoring less than more recent graduates.

> "There was a significant positive relationship between year of qualification and knowledge test score in doctors under investigation (r = 0.48, p < 0.001) (Figure 1)."
