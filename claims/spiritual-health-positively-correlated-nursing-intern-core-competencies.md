### Zhang 2026

Zhang, Limei; Zhong, Xiaoying; Liu, Jing; Yuan, Tingting; Yang, Wenli; Wang, Meng; Yuan, Ping; Ding, Xing. (2026). Analysis of Correlation Between the Core Competency Status and Spiritual Health of Nursing Intern Students. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/3522503

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 312 nursing intern students from five tertiary hospitals in Sichuan, China, using Pearson correlation between SHS-SF spiritual health and CINS core competency scores. The printed effect size for this finding is r = 0.687: the study reports "spiritual health was significantly correlated with core competencies (r = 0.687, p < 0.01)".

> "As shown in Table 3, spiritual health was significantly correlated with core competencies (r = 0.687, p < 0.01). All of the subdomains of spiritual health had a significantly positive correlation with core competencies."
