Evaluation of an online training tool for scoring programmed cell death ligand-1 (PD-L1) diagnostic tests for lung cancer. Diagnostic Pathology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13000-020-00953-9

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation offered in the Discussion, not a tested result: they hypothesise the variability "is probably reflective of the greater degree of effort necessary for accurate counting" of total viable and PD-L1-stained tumour cells, leading pathologists to rely on pattern-recognition 'guestimates'.

> "In our experience, the higher the threshold used for categoric scoring, the greater the likelihood of the level of variability observed with respect to the raw scores derived for these cases."
