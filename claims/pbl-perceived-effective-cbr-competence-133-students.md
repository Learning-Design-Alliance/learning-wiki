## Evidence

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-course questionnaire with closed- and open-ended items administered in the case study evaluation (Part II); the internal consistency Cronbach's alpha was 0.916. Responses were descriptive means on a 4-point scale, with "PBL was perceived by students as an effective teaching and learning method". No inferential test or effect size was reported. The text gives the questionnaire sample as n = 133, but Table 7 states "No. of participants = 113"; the article does not reconcile the two.
