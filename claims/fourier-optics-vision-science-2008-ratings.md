`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

2008 AO Summer School learner feedback survey ratings for the Fourier Optics and Vision Science stations, reported in Section 5 and plotted in Figure 4. Both activities were rated positively, though the Fourier Optics "mean content rating was 3.89 out of 5" and Vision Science 3.98, below the Demonstrator's 4.42.

> "For Fourier Optics, the mean content rating was 3.89 out of 5; 64% of respondees rated the level of challenge as “just right;” and 50% rated the activity as “very valuable” or higher. For Vision Science, the mean content rating was 3. 98 out of 5; 65% of respondees rated the level of challenge as “just right;” and 32% rated the activity as “very valuable” or higher."
