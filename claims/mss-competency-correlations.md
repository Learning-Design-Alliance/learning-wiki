## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Attitudes and beliefs correlated positively with overall MSS knowledge (r = 0.28, p < 0.001) and skills (r = 0.15, p < 0.01); malnutrition collaboration correlated with attitudes (r = 0.22, p < 0.001) and combined knowledge (r = 0.15, p < 0.01). [→ Netzer R. 2025](#netzer-r-2025)

## Evidence

### Netzer R. 2025

Netzer R., Levi N., Ganchrow K., Naan Y., Elboim-Gabyzon M. (2025). Clinical Competency in Managing Malnutrition–Sarcopenia Syndrome Among Physical Therapists: A Cross-Sectional Survey. Nutrients 17(2), 281. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu17020281

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Bivariate Spearman correlation analyses (Table 4) among the 337-PT survey's continuous outcome scores. The article reports attitudes and beliefs correlating with overall MSS knowledge at "r = 0.28, p < 0.001" and with the combined knowledge score at "r = 0.28, p < 0.001"; other printed correlations were smaller (r = 0.15–0.22).

> "Significant positive correlations were observed between the attitudes and beliefs scores and various knowledge and skill scores, including the combined sarcopenia and malnutrition knowledge score (r = 0.28, p < 0.001) and the overall MSS knowledge score (r = 0.28, p < 0.001)"
