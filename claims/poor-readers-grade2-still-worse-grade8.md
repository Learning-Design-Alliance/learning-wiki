## Evidence

### Wolff 2016

Ulrika Wolff. (2016). Effects of a Randomized Reading Intervention Study Aimed at 9-Year-Olds: A 5-Year Follow-up. Dyslexia, 22, 85–100. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1529

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests compared 66 typical readers with the grade-2-identified poor readers in grade 8 (Table 1); all differences significant at p = .001, with d = 1.16 word decoding, 0.86 reading comprehension, 1.54 spelling, 0.76 vocabulary, 1.10 working memory.

> "the typical readers’ performance was superior to that of the students identiﬁed as poor readers in grade 2 on all measures they were tested on, that is word decoding, reading comprehension, spelling and vocabulary. Cohen’s d ranged from 1.54 to 0.76, with the highest effect size on spelling and the lowest on vocabulary."
