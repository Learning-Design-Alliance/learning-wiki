## Evidence

### Lien Wan-Ching 2022

Lien Wan-Ching, Lin Phone, Chang Chih-Heng, Wu Meng-Che, Wu Cheng-Yi. (2022). The effect of e-learning on point-of-care ultrasound education in novices. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2022.2152522

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of image quality scores (5-point Likert, blinded instructor review) among PGY-1 users (3 [3–3.2]), non-users (3 [2.9–3]), and PGY-2 (3 [2.7–3]); the article reports "the e-learning users had better image quality than non-users and PGY-2" with p = 0.044 in Table 1.

> "the e-learning users had better image quality than non-users and PGY-2. There were no significant differences in the diagnostic accuracy between the PGY-1 and PGY-2."

### Lien Wan-Ching 2022 (2)

Lien Wan-Ching, Lin Phone, Chang Chih-Heng, Wu Meng-Che, Wu Cheng-Yi. (2022). The effect of e-learning on point-of-care ultrasound education in novices. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2022.2152522

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression on PGY-1 FAST image quality with covariates age, sex, prior US experience, and OSCE score; the article reports "using e-learning remained significant to be associated with better image quality (coefficient, 0.21 ± 0.09, p = 0.014)".

> "After adjusting age, sex, prior US experience, and OSCE scores, using e-learning remained significant to be associated with better image quality (coefficient, 0.21 ± 0.09, p = 0.014)."
