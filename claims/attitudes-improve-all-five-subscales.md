# Medical students' self-rated diabetes attitude subscale scores rose on all five subscales from pre-test to post-test in one uncontrolled pre-post study, with the largest change in the psychosocial impact of diabetes subscale

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` All five self-rated DAS-3 subscale scores rose from pre-test to post-test in one uncontrolled pre-post study; the largest magnitude of change was in the Psychosocial impact of diabetes subscale (Cohen's d = 0.60). [→ Beverly Elizabeth A. 2019](#beverly-elizabeth-a-2019)

## Evidence

### Beverly Elizabeth A. 2019

Beverly Elizabeth A., Ritholz Marilyn D., Rennie Rochelle G., Mort Sophia C. (2019). A brief interactive training with medical students improves their diabetes knowledge about hypoglycemia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1615-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post DAS-3 administration (n = 212) in the same feasibility study. All five subscales improved significantly (e.g., Psychosocial impact mean improvement = 0.27, t-value = 9.249, p < 0.001), with "a Cohen's d of 0.60 indicating a medium effect" on the psychosocial subscale.

> "We observed the largest magnitude of change with the "Psychosocial impact of diabetes" subscale, with a Cohen's d of 0.60 indicating a medium effect (see Table 3)."
