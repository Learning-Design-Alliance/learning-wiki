q3 · i2 · causal · r?

In the second RCT (45 rural schools), direct classroom observations complemented teacher ratings and showed "significant advantages for the TAPP students" on on-task and prosocial behavior, with effect sizes from +0.28 to +0.46.

"In contrast, direct classroom observations conducted showed significant advantages for the TAPP students who exhibited more on-task behavior, greater prosocial classroom behavior, and less off-task motor and passive behavior, with effect sizes ranging from +0.28 to +0.46."
