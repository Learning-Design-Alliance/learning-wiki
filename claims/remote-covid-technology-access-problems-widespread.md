## Evidence

### Means 2021

Means, B., Peters, V., Neisler, J., Wiley, K., & Grifths, R. (2021). Lessons from remote learning during COVID-19. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

National probability-based KnowledgePanel survey of undergraduates whose in-person courses went fully remote in spring 2020; final sample of 1,008. The survey found "44% of students" faced connectivity issues interfering at least occasionally, and separately almost a quarter (23%) reported hardware or software problems at least occasionally.

> "Internet connectivity issues were serious enough to interfere with students’ ability to attend or participate in their courses at least occasionally for 44% of students, with 16% of students reporting that they experienced such problems often or very often."
