q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic methodological review screening all articles in five journals (BJEP, CEP, JEP, JXE, Learning and Instruction) 2016–2022; 2,237 empirical articles classified by method. The review reports "Most of these articles used primarily quantitative methods (94.9%, n = 2123/2237)" and 57 mixed methods articles (2.55%).

"Most of these articles used primarily quantitative methods (94.9%, n = 2123/2237). A much smaller number of these articles used primarily qualitative methods (2.55%, n = 57/2237) or mixed methods (2.55%, n = 57/2237)."
