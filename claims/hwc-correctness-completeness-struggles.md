## Evidence

### Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox 2025

Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2025). Motivating reflection in problem solving: homework corrections in upper-division physics courses. [doi:10.1103/ntvp-spkc](https://doi.org/10.1103/ntvp-spkc)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Within-case analysis of Ari, who completed HWCs on every assignment and left 158 comments averaging about 14 per assignment, with only approximately half coded as within the HWCs framework. The case summary states he "sometimes struggles to produce HWCs that are both correct and complete."

> "While he makes many reflective comments on his assignments, he sometimes struggles to produce HWCs that are both correct and complete. In many cases he writes that he understands his error after looking at the solutions, but his corrections sometimes lack a complete explanation of his issue."
