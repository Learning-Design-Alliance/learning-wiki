## Evidence

### Gifford 2026

Gifford, K., Ehrlich, G. S., Bumbacher, E., & Kuo, E. (2026). “I forgot the formula:” How students can use coherence to reconstruct a (partially) forgotten equation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/yfxm-m4sw](https://doi.org/10.1103/yfxm-m4sw)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focal case from the clinical interviews: Gabi, a rising junior physics major, reconstructed the RC time constant by chaining qualitative dependencies ("more capacitance→more charge→greater discharge time") rather than deriving it from Kirchhoff's laws. Tom, a graduate student, similarly used the dependencies "more resistance→greater discharge time" and "more capacitance→greater discharge time" to select RC over 1/RC. The article notes this strategy "was supported by partially recalling its form and only considering possibilities that had distinct qualitative dependencies."

> "Gabi used an alternative approach: determining that the time constant isRC, and not 1/RC, by talking through expected qualitative dependencies of how resistance and capacitance relate to the discharge time."
