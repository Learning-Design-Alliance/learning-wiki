## Evidence

### Le-Thi 2017

Le-Thi, D., Rodgers, M. P. H., & Pellicer-Sánchez, A. (2017). Teaching Formulaic Sequences in an English-Language Class: The Effects of Explicit Instruction Versus Coursebook Instruction. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(3), 111–139. http://dx.doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1276

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Classroom quasi-experiment with 60 Vietnamese EFL students comparing experimental groups receiving explicit teaching plus regular coursebook instruction against a control group receiving coursebook instruction only. The authors state "Explicit teaching combined with incidental exposure to formulaic sequences in the coursebook was superior to the traditional coursebook instruction approach in the classroom setting," supported by large ANCOVA effect sizes (partial η² .443–.679).
