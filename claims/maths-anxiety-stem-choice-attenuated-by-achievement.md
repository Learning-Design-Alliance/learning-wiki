## Evidence

### Ferdinand 2024

Ferdinand, R., Malanchini, M. & Rimfeld, K. (2024). Mathematics interest, self-efficacy, and anxiety predict STEM career choice in emerging adulthood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00275-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second logistic regression model in the TEDS cohort adjusting maths anxiety, self-efficacy and interest for GCSE maths achievement. The article states maths anxiety "does not independently predict" STEM career choice once achievement is controlled; ORs only slightly attenuated (0.79–0.81).

> "Results from the second model indicate that maths anxiety does not independently predict STE M career choice when the effects of maths achievement are controlled. However, effect sizes in the adjusted models were only slightly attenuated compared to the unadjusted model (ORs ranging between 0.79 and 0.81)"
