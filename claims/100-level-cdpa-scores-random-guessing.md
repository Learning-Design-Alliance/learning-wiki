Observational analysis of CDPA scores for the two first-year courses across four years. The authors explain that in these courses students are introduced to uncertainties for the first time and apply them only in simple mechanics problems without deeper conceptual understanding, in agreement with the original CDPA paper.

> "The data presented in Figure 2 shows that for the two lower-level courses, PHYS 101 and PHYS 131, the overall CDPA score can be understood as being comparable to random guessing."
