Zhongyu Hu & Jiongjiong Yang 2025¶

Zhongyu Hu & Jiongjiong Yang. (2025). Timing of reward cues alters the interaction between reward and interest on memory over time. npj Science of Learning 10:82. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00374-7

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Experiment 2 (42 participants analyzed) presented reward cues after clips and ratings; ANOVA showed a significant reward-by-interest interaction (F(1, 41) = 4.56, p = 0.04, ηp2 = 0.10) and a significant three-way interaction (F(1, 41) = 6.64, p = 0.01, ηp2 = 0.14), with "the reward effect on memory was signiﬁcant only for high-interest clips at 1 day".

"Post-hoc analysis revealed that the reward effect on memory was signiﬁcant only for high-interest clips at 1 day (p < 0.001)"

Related Claims¶

  • Rewards decreased the interest effect on memory when cued before clips but increased it when cued after clips, at 1 day but not 10 minutes — a broader claim this one bears on
  • The timing of reward cues moderates the reward-by-interest interaction on memory over time (four-way interaction), with effects emerging only after consolidation — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Reward cues and delay conditions did not influence participants' interest ratings, confirming the independence of the reward manipulation from interest — related
