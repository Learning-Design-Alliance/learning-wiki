## Evidence

### Said-Metwaly 2019

Said-Metwaly, S., Fernández-Castilla, B., Kyndt, E., Van den Noortgate, W., & Barbot, B. (2019). Does the Fourth-Grade Slump in Creativity Actually Exist? A Meta-Analysis of the Development of Divergent Thinking in School-Age Children and Adolescents. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Does+the+Fourth-Grade+Slump+in+Creativity+Actually+Exist

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Publication-bias analysis in Results using funnel-plot inspection and a three-level extension of Egger's regression test. The overall analysis showed significant bias (t = -2.33, p = .02); the indicator-level tests were non-significant. No effect size is printed.

> "The Egger’s test showed that publication bias was significant for the overall analysis (t = -2.33, df = 140, p = .02), but not for fluency (t = -0.91, df = 39.10, p = .37), flexibility (t = -1.48, df = 36.90, p = .15), or originality (t = -0.57, df = 40.50, p = .57) taken separately."
