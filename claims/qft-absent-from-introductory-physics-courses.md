## Evidence

### Ian H. Redmount 2019

Ian H. Redmount. (2019). Music of the Spheres: Teaching Quantum Field Theory at the Introductory Level. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1908.01579](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1908.01579)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The author's survey-style assessment of introductory physics instruction, stated in the abstract and elaborated in the introduction, which notes that even calculus-based university physics and modern physics texts seldom explain QFT. The author calls the omission "striking" and comparable to omitting DNA from introductory biology.

> "Yet it is rarely taught, or even mentioned, in introductory physics—from high school, college, and university survey courses through upper-division “modern physics” courses."
