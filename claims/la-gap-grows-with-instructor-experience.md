## Evidence

### Daniel Caravez 2018

Daniel Caravez, Angelica De La Torre, Jayson Nissen, and Ben Van Dusen. (2018). Longitudinal associations between learning assistants and instructor effectiveness. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.015](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.015)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-based predictions from the HLM analysis, illustrated in Figure 2, show the gap favoring LA-supported courses grows with instructor experience; at 6 terms the predicted advantage is 10.3% on posttests.

> "When instructors have no prior experience teaching that course, our model predicts that the students in LA-supported courses will outperform their peers in courses without LAs by 3.1%. As the instructors gain experience the predicted gap in student posttest scores grows."
