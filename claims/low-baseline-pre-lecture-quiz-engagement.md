Eilidh Jack, Craig Alexander, Elinor M Jones. (2024). Exploring the impact of gamification on engagement in a statistics classroom. Information and Inference: A Journal of the IMA. [doi:10.1093/teamat/hrae009](https://doi.org/10.1093/teamat/hrae009)

Observational analysis of Moodle activity logs for the level 1 statistics courses shows the baseline problem: "the average completion of pre-lecture quizzes in advance of the active learning session taking place was 6.88%" in semester 1 of 2022-23, a pattern persistent over 2020-21, 2021-22 and semester 1 of 2022-23.
