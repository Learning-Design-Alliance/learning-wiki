## Evidence

### Leinonen J 2020

Leinonen J, Laitala M‐L, Pirttilahti J, Niskanen L, Pesonen P, Anttonen V. (2020). Live lectures and videos do not differ in relation to learning outcomes of dental ergonomics. Clin Exp Dent Res. 2020;6:489–494. https://doi.org/10.1002/cre2.300

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the cross-over trial with 45 third-year dental students, questionnaire scores improved significantly after the first teaching session in both the lecture-first and video-first groups (p < .05); the article also reports overall improvement significant at p < .001.

> "After the first teaching session in both groups, the improvement in knowledge was significant (p < .05)."
