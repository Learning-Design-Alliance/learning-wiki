## Evidence

### Schapiro and McClelland 2009

Anna C. Schapiro, James L. McClelland. (2009). A connectionist model of a continuous developmental transition in the balance scale task. Cognition 110, 395–411. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2008.11.017

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analysis of hysteresis test patterns in Jansen and van der Maas (2001) Experiment 1 (314 children) and 1680 model test sessions. The most common sudden jump for both was shifting from balance to the distance-based response between distance differences of one and two.

> "cases in which the ‘sudden jump’ occurred at a distance difference of four or ﬁve only occurred in a total of 3.503% of children (11/314)."
