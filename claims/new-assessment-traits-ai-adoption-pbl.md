### Chengbo Zheng 2024

Chengbo Zheng, Kangyu Yuan, Bingcan Guo, Reza Hadi Mogavi, Zhenhui Peng, Shuai Ma, and Xiaojuan Ma. (2024). Charting the Future of AI in Project-Based Learning: A Co-Design Exploration with Students. [doi:10.1145/3613904.3642807](https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3642807)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the ideal-student activity, participants proposed traits valued due to AI adoption. Efficacy in using AI was "the most frequently mentioned trait", with ideal students using AI with clear purposes and clearly communicating intents to AI. Other traits: leadership in project direction, symbiotic learning, judgment and discernment.

> "This trait is the most frequently mentioned trait, through which participants highlight that an ideal student should use AI with clear purposes (P06, P10, P16), clearly communicate intents to AI (P11, P13, P15)"
