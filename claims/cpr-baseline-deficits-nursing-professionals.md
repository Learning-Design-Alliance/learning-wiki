`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test assessment of the 56 nursing professionals before the first training, using the same 25-item theoretical questionnaire and 18-item practical checklist. The article reports the theoretical pre-test mean of 13.3 (±4.5) and states that "before the first training, insufficient theoretical knowledge and motor skills was observed, in view of the average of correct answers in the tests".
