## Evidence

### Xie 2024

Xie, C., Zhang, S., Qiao, X., & Hao, N. (2024). Transcranial direct current stimulation targeting the bilateral IFG alters cognitive processes during creative ideation. npj Science of Learning, 9, 75. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00285-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects analysis of CRA accuracy and reaction time in a randomized, sham-controlled tDCS experiment with 81 undergraduates; the Results report nonsignificant group main effects (F2,75 = 1.15, p = 0.33 for accuracy; F2,69.5 = 1.83, p = 0.17 for reaction time), i.e. "no signiﬁcant difference in the overall performance".
