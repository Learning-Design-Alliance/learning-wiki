## Evidence

### Wang Siqi 2025

Wang Siqi, Yang Di, Zhao Wenbo, Yan Xiaoyuan, Zhang Xiyue, Gao Wenqing, Guo Jiajie. (2025). The combination of a SPOC with evaluative interactive learning in dental adhesion education: a comparison with a lecture-based learning mode. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07261-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire survey (13 items, five dimensions, Cronbach's α = 0.916) one week after class. Table 6 shows active learning satisfaction of "4.54 ± 0.58" in EIL vs 4.18 ± 0.84 in LBL, P = 0.000; other dimensions were not significant.

> "a comprehensive analysis of satisfaction with educational quality indicated that students in the EIL group outperformed those in the LBL group in terms of active learning (Table 6)."

### Wang Siqi 2025 (2)

Wang Siqi, Yang Di, Zhao Wenbo, Yan Xiaoyuan, Zhang Xiyue, Gao Wenqing, Guo Jiajie. (2025). The combination of a SPOC with evaluative interactive learning in dental adhesion education: a comparison with a lecture-based learning mode. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07261-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same questionnaire: sense-of-burden scores were 2.85 ± 1.37 (LBL) vs 2.90 ± 1.24 (EIL), P = 0.797; preferences did not differ significantly (P = 0.900).

> "neither the LBL nor the EIL methods place additional burdens on students, and no significant differences were observed in terms of students' effective learning, learning flexibility or preferences for the two learning approaches"
