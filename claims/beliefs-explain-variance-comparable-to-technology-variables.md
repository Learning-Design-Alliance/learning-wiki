## Evidence

### Hermans 2008

Hermans, R., Tondeur, J., van Braak, J., & Valcke, M. (2008). The impact of primary school teachers’ educational beliefs on the classroom use of computers. Computers & Education, 51(4), 1499–1509. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2008.02.001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multilevel modelling compared the variance explained by each variable subset. Adding beliefs raised R² by 7.3% at teacher level and 9.7% at school level, versus 7.2% and 9.8% respectively for the technology-related variables — a comparable contribution.

> "Correspondingly, by adding teacher beliefs to Model 2b, the results show an increase of 7.3% at teacher level and 9.7% at school level (Model 3)."
