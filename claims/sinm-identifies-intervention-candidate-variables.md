## Evidence

### M.S.M. Sachisthal 2019

M.S.M. Sachisthal, B.R.J. Jansen, T.T.D. Peetsma, J. Dalege, H.L.J. van der Maas, M.E.J. Raijmakers. (2019). Introducing a Science Interest Network Model to Reveal Country Differences. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Based on network analyses of PISA 2015 data from the Netherlands and Colombia, the authors report identifying "variables... of structural importance within the interest construct". The article frames these as candidates to be tested, not as tested intervention targets.

> "we could also make predictions about which variables are of structural importance within the interest construct and as such worthwhile to test as potential candidates for intervention"
