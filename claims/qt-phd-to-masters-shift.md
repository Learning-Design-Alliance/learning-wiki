## Evidence

### Franziska Greinert 2024

Franziska Greinert, Malte S. Ubben, Ismet N. Dogan, Dagmar Hilfert-Rüppell, Rainer Müller. (2024). Advancing quantum technology workforce: industry insights into qualification and training needs. arXiv. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-024-00294-2](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-024-00294-2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview analysis (Sec. 3.2) reports this shift as companies and QT departments grow and technology readiness increases, with a growing need for junior professionals; the vast majority of people needed are engineers who do not require a high level of qualification in quantum physics.

> "There is a shift over time, especially in start-ups, from requiring a high level of expertise and experience, i.e. a PhD in a related field, to opening up to masters and the like."
