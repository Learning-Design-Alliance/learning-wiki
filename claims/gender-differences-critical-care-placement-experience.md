`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Comparative thematic analysis of journals by student gender found "female students reflected on a much more intense and negative emotional experience", while male students' satisfaction related to seeing emergency and ICU as an employment option and to individual and organizational learning aspects.

> "Female students reflected on a much more intense and negative emotional experience, linked to the environment of critical care practices with critically ill patients or by identification with young patients."
