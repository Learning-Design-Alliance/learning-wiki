## Evidence

### Connell 2016

Connell, G. L., Donovan, D. A., & Chambers, T. G. (2016). Increasing the Use of Student-Centered Pedagogies from Moderate to High Improves Student Learning and Attitudes about Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0062

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two sections of Biology 101 taught by the same instructor in the same quarter (n = 174 Extensive, 163 Moderate completing all three exams); mean exam scores compared with a Student's t test, showing "t = 6.57, p < 0.001". The article also reports an odds ratio of passing of 2.78 for the Extensive section versus 1.00 for the Moderate section.

> "Students in the Extensive section had significantly higher mean exam scores (grand mean of 72.0%) compared with students in the Moderate section (grand mean of 63.6%; t = 6.57, p < 0.001)."
