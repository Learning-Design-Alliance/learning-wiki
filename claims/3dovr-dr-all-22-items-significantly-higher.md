## Evidence

### Im Ji-Eun 2025

Im Ji-Eun, Gu Ja-Young, Bae Jung-Hee, Lee Jae-Gi. (2025). Development and user experience of a three-dimensional object-based virtual reality-simulation tool for dental radiography training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06623-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U tests on the 22-item survey compared the 3D-object VR group (n=40) with the 360° video group (n=39). "The mean and median values for all the questionnaire items were higher in the experimental group"; per-item P values ranged from 0.022 to below 0.001, and Cronbach's alpha was 0.953.

> "The mean and median values for all the questionnaire items were higher in the experimental group than in the control group (Table 3; Fig. 4). Statistical analysis yielded P values less than 0.05, indicating significant differences between the groups."
