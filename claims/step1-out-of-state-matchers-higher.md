### Gauer JL 2017

Gauer JL, Jackson JB. (2017). The association of USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK scores with residency match specialty and location. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2017.1358579

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent-samples t-test comparing Step 1 scores of in-state versus out-of-state matchers in the retrospective dataset (N = 1054). The article reports the printed means and "t(1052) = 2.215, p = 0.027" with no effect size.

> "We found a significant difference between those who stayed in-state (M = 225.24, SD = 19.084) and those who went out-of-state (M = 227.83, SD = 18.668) at the p < 0.05 level on USMLE Step 1 scores (t(1052) = 2.215, p = 0.027)."
