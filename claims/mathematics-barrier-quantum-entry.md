## Evidence

### Nita 2021

Nita, L., Mazzoli Smith, L., Chancellor, N. and Cramman, H. (2021). The challenge and opportunities of quantum literacy for future education and transdisciplinary problem-solving. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1080/02635143.2021.1920905](https://doi.org/10.1080/02635143.2021.1920905)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is an argumentative claim made in the article's discussion of mathematics as a barrier to entry, not an empirical test. The authors argue that 'Mathematical Dirac notation equations relay quantum states and concepts precisely, yet this is beyond the reach of most people,' making inclusion issues pertinent.

> "Access to a particularly difficult notation language in mathematics effectively functions as a powerful and exclusive form of knowledge that is only accessible to those who have achieved well in it."
