## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In multiple-group analyses, the path from fathers' growth mindset intercept to children's intercept differed by child gender (χ2(1) = 4.154, p < 0.042): significant for girls (β = 0.253, p = 0.014) but not boys (β = −0.038, p = 0.709). ## Evidence

### Jiao Chen and Chunhui Liu 2023

Jiao Chen and Chunhui Liu. (2023). The longitudinal association between children’s growth mindset in senior primary school and their parents’ growth mindset. Front. Psychol. 14:1110944. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1110944

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multiple-group analysis by child gender with Wald tests of eight paths; only the father-intercept-to-child-intercept path differed by gender. The effect held for daughters but not sons, contrary to same-gender modeling expectations the article cites.

> "paternal growth mindset in the first test predicted girls’ growth mindset in the fifth test (β = 0.253, p = 0.014) but not boys’ growth mindset (β = −0.038, p = 0.709)"
