## Evidence

### Esther Tan 2021

Esther Tan, Jacob Gerolf de Weerd, Slavi Stoyanov. (2021). Supporting interdisciplinary collaborative concept mapping with individual preparation phase. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09963-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests (alpha 0.05, Levene test showed no variance-equality violation) compared mean CCM scores of the eight groups on four outcome variables from the Cañas et al. (2015) scoring scheme. "no significant difference for concepts"; descriptive means were higher for WIP on all variables.

> "There was no significant difference for concepts (t (6) = 1.83, p = 0.12), domains (t (6) = 0.67, p = 0.53) and cross-links (t (6) = 1.77, p = 0.13) between the two experimental conditions."
