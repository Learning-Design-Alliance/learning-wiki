## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Aktas 2016

Aktas, S., & Aydin, A. (2016). The effect of the smart board usage in science and technology lessons. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 64, 125-138. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.64.7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-samples t-test within the experimental 7th-grade class comparing its post-test mean (19.11) with its permanence test mean (18.81). The article reports no significant difference (t=0.938, p>.05) and interprets the information as more permanent.

> "According to the results of the t -test for dependent groups, there is no significant difference between the post -test and permanence test scores of the experimental group (t=0.938; p>.05). As a result, the information seems to be more permanent in the experimental group."

### Aktas 2016 (2)

Aktas, S., & Aydin, A. (2016). The effect of the smart board usage in science and technology lessons. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 64, 125-138. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.64.7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-samples t-test within the control class comparing post-test (16.63) and permanence test (16.26) means. The article reports a significant difference (t=2.217, p=.033) while noting the means are quite similar; no effect size is printed.

> "there is a significant difference between the post -test and permanence test scores of the control group (t=2.217, p<.05), but while the average post -test score for the control group was X̅ = 16.63, the average score of the permanence test was X̅ = 16.26, which is quite similar."
