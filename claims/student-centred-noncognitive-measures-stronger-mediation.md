### Quan Zhou 2026

Quan Zhou, Liao Wangjingyi, Allegrini Andrea G., Rimfeld Kaili, Wertz Jasmin, Morris Tim T., Raffington Laurel, Plomin Robert, Malanchini Margherita. (2026). Non-cognitive skills mediate education-related polygenic score associations with academic achievement across development. Nature Communications. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72838-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Fine-grained mediation analyses in TEDS examined individual non-cognitive measures as mediators of polygenic score associations with academic achievement. "Mediation effects were stronger for student-centred non-cognitive variables" such as academic interest and self-concept compared to learning-environment measures. By age 16, academic self-concept mediated 45% and maths interest 32% of the EA polygenic score association.

> "Mediation effects were stronger for student-centred non-cognitive variables such as academic interest, academic perceived ability, self-concept and curiosity, as compared to measures capturing broader aspects of the learning environment, such as classroom satisfaction and peer support."
