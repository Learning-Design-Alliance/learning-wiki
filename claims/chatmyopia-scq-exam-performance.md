## Evidence

### Wu Y 2025

Wu Y, Chen X, Zhang W, Liu S, Sum WMR, Wu X, Shang X, Kee C, He M, Shi D. (2025). ChatMyopia: An AI Agent for Pre-consultation Education in Primary Eye Care Settings. [doi:10.1016/j.isci.2025.113768](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2025.113768)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

SCQ examination comparing ChatMyopia with five general ECPs and two specialists on 150 questions from Chinese national examination materials, analyzed by RM-ANOVA (group P=0.011; exam P=0.997). The quote prints the post-hoc scores and p-values; no effect size printed.

> "Post hoc comparisons indicated that ChatMyopia outperformed general ECPs (C-MISS-R score = 80 vs. 67.07, p = 0.029) and performed comparably to specialists (78.67, p = 0.243)."

### Wu Y 2025 (2)

Wu Y, Chen X, Zhang W, Liu S, Sum WMR, Wu X, Shang X, Kee C, He M, Shi D. (2025). ChatMyopia: An AI Agent for Pre-consultation Education in Primary Eye Care Settings. [doi:10.1016/j.isci.2025.113768](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2025.113768)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the same SCQ exams by question type. The quote names both sides of each comparison with printed percentages and p-values; no effect size printed.

> "ChatMyopia significantly outperformed general ECPs in both knowledge-based questions (82.20% vs. 72.14%, p=0.017) and scenario -based questions (75.76% vs. 53.33%, p=0.049), while performing similarly to specialists in both categories (p=0.208, p=0.529)."
