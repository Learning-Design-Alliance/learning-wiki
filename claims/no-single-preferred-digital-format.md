`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Rank-point survey (1st = 3 points) of preferred digital format among the 30 completers, reported with Fig. 2. "The educational material with the highest ranking was the video podcast"; animation and lecture-style presentation tied for second.

### Janssen J 2025 (2)

Janssen J, Lackenbauer W, Gasselich S, Lickel ME, Schabel L, Beikircher R, Keip C, Wieser M, Mazuquin B, Yeowell G. (2025). Improving physiotherapists’ ability to recognise the presence of a serious pathology with a digital educational training: a mixed methods feasibility study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08101-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Discussion of the format results: the animation video was polarising, "it also received the most votes for first and last place in the quantitative data", and qualitative comments on animations diverged between participants.

> "Despite the fact that the animation video reached the second place overall, it also received the most votes for first and last place in the quantitative data. The qualitative data confirmed this ambiguity."
