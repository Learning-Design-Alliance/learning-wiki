Evidence¶

Bahadır ERİŞTİ – Mustafa POLAT 2017¶

Bahadır ERİŞTİ – Mustafa POLAT. (2017). THE EFFECTIVENESS OF SYNECTICS INSTRUCTIONAL MODEL ON FOREIGN LANGUAGE VOCABULARY TEACHING. International Journal of Languages’ Education and Teaching, Volume 5, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.18298/ijlet.1753

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

True-experimental pretest-posttest study with two experimental and two control groups (39 experimental, 43 control students) in a state university English prep class; posttest covered 24 targeted words. The article reports means of 20.71 vs 14.04 and "Cohen’s d formula" value d=1.87, described as a powerful effect.

"the mean of the experimental groups is ( =20.71) and the mean of the control groups is ( =14.04). An independent t -test shows that the difference between the means of the groups is statistically significant in favor of the experi mental groups (t=8.73, p<.00)."
