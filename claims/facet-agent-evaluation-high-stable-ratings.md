## Evidence

### Jana Gonnermann-Müller 2026

Jana Gonnermann-Müller, Jennifer Haase, Konstantin Fackeldey, Sebastian Pokutta. (2026). FACET: Teacher-Centred LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems—Towards Personalized Educational Worksheets. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1080/13511610.2026.2727110](https://doi.org/10.1080/13511610.2026.2727110)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Agent-based (LLM evaluator) internal-validity evaluation of worksheets generated for four learner profiles, ten iterations each, on an inverted 6-point scale. The evaluator returned "consistently high ratings across all four profiles and ten iterations"; didactical structure (M=4.12, SD=0.22) and creativity (M=3.94, SD=0.26) were lower but favorable.

> "The strongest scores were observed for suitability of the task (M= 4.78, SD= 0.06) and clarity of the task (M= 4.76,SD= 0.12) for all four learner personas, indicating very high internal validity."
