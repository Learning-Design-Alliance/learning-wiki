`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Phase 1 of the three-phase validation assessed AI scoring reliability using the intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC) based on 20 records blindly evaluated by two experts. The ICC between AI scores and the mean expert score was 0.893 (95% CI: 0.713–0.959), indicating "high consistency with expert assessments".
