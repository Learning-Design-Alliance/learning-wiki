Evidence¶

Darmawansah 2025¶

Darmawansah, D., Hwang, G.-J., Lin, C.-J., & Febiyani, F. (2025). An artificial intelligence-supported GFCA learning model to enhance L2 students' role-play performance, English speaking and interaction mindset. Education Tech Research Dev (2025) 73:1451–1479. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10453-6

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Mann–Whitney tests of expert-identified L2 errors (morpho-syntactic, lexical, phonological) in role-play task T1; morpho-syntactic errors favored AI-GFCA with "r = 0.30", and lexical errors showed no significant difference.

"students in the AI-GFCA group (Mdn = 0, 1) produced fewer morpho-syntactic errors and phonological errors compared to students in the AI-C group (Mdn = 2, 1) with U = 165.50, z = − 2.068, p = 0.039, and r = 0.30, and U = 157.00, z = − 2.535, p = 0.019, and r = 0.35, respectively."

Darmawansah 2025 (2)¶

Darmawansah, D., Hwang, G.-J., Lin, C.-J., & Febiyani, F. (2025). An artificial intelligence-supported GFCA learning model to enhance L2 students' role-play performance, English speaking and interaction mindset. Education Tech Research Dev (2025) 73:1451–1479. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10453-6

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Mann–Whitney tests of L2 errors in role-play task T2; morpho-syntactic errors favored AI-GFCA with "r = 0.38", while phonological errors showed no significant difference.

"students in the AI-GFCA group (Mdn = 1, 2) produced fewer morpho-syntactic errors and lexical errors compared to students in the AI-C group (Mdn = 0, 1) with U = 145.00, z = − 2.569, p = 0.01, and r = 0.38, and U = 158.00, z = − 2.396, p = 0.017, and r = 0.35, respectively."
