## Evidence

### Hu 2022

Hu, P., Li, Y., & Singh, C. (2022). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with measurement uncertainty of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac9ba3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac9ba3)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study in a junior-/senior-level QM course, online (N=27) and in person (N=23) in consecutive years. The summary reports that "post-test scores improved for every question following the administration of the CQS , with the exception of Q3 in the online implementation," which had an exceptionally high pre-test score. Tables II-III list per-question normalized gains and Cohen's d values; no pooled effect size is printed for this overall finding.

> "Post-test scores improved for every question following the administration of the CQS , with the exception of Q3 in the online implementation; this question tested students on whether energy can be well-defined for various Hamiltonians, and had an exceptionally high pre-test score."
