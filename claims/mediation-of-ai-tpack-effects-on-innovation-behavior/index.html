Bai X and Hsieh T-S 2026¶

Bai X and Hsieh T-S (2026) AI teaching innovation behavior among college teachers: a structural equation modeling analysis based on the AI-TPACK framework, teaching self-efficacy, professional identity, and AI literacy. Front. Psychol. 17:1834827. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1834827

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bootstrap mediation analysis (5,000 resamples) in PLS-SEM with the 898-teacher sample evaluated indirect effects via 95% confidence intervals. The article reports "the indirect effects through PI and AIL were significant while the corresponding direct effects on AI-TIB also remained significant, indicating partial mediation."

"For most AI-TPACK dimensions, the indirect effects through PI and AIL were significant while the corresponding direct effects on AI-TIB also remained significant, indicating partial mediation."

Bai X and Hsieh T-S 2026 (2)¶

Bai X and Hsieh T-S (2026) AI teaching innovation behavior among college teachers: a structural equation modeling analysis based on the AI-TPACK framework, teaching self-efficacy, professional identity, and AI literacy. Front. Psychol. 17:1834827. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1834827

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The same bootstrap mediation analysis distinguished mediation types across paths. The article reports "AI-TK showed indirect-only mediation through PI and AIL, whereas AI-TPACK showed indirect-only mediation through TSE, PI, and AIL," and states the indirect effect through TSE was not supported.

"Specifically, AI-TK showed indirect-only mediation through PI and AIL, whereas AI-TPACK showed indirect-only mediation through TSE, PI, and AIL."
