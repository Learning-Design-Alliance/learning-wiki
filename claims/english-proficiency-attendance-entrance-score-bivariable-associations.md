## Evidence

### Tadese Mesfin 2022

Tadese Mesfin, Yeshaneh Alex, Mulu Getaneh Baye. (2022). Determinants of good academic performance among university students in Ethiopia: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03461-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariable chi-square analysis in the 615-student study showed English proficiency associated with performance (p = 0.002). Class absenteeism (p = 0.038) and entrance exam score (p < 0.002) were also bivariably significant; none remained significant in the multivariable model.

> "About 46.2% of students who had a pre-intermediate level of English proficiency were poor in academics, whereas 80.6% of students with an advanced level of proficiency were good in academics (chi2 p = 0.002)"
