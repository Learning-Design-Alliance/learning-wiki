### Woodham Luke A. 2019

Woodham Luke A., Round Jonathan, Stenfors Terese, Bujacz Aleksandra, Karlgren Klas, Jivram Trupti, Riklefs Viktor, Poulton Ella, Poulton Terry. (2019). Virtual patients designed for training against medical error: Exploring the impact of decision-making on learner motivation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0215597

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-institution t-tests within the repeated study design. At HUMP the branched group's means exceeded the linear group's significantly on Intrinsic Goal Orientation, Task Value, Critical Thinking and Help Seeking, "with p-values ranging from .001 to .031"; Control of Learning Beliefs also favoured the branched group there.

> "the exception was HUMP, which reported that the mean in the Branched group was significantly higher than that of the linear group, with p-values ranging from .001 to .031"
