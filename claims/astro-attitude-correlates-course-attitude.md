## Evidence

### Adam S. Trotter 2019

Adam S. Trotter, Daniel E. Reichart, Aaron P. LaCluyzé & Rachel Freed. (2019). Factors Contributing to Attitudinal Gains in Introductory Astronomy Courses. Robotic Telescopes, Student Research and Education (RTSRE) Proceedings, Vol. 1, No. 1. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.32374/rtsre.2019.014](https://doi.org/10.32374/rtsre.2019.014)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simultaneous multiple linear regression on the full survey data set (N = 749). Course Attitude Index was listed first, in decreasing order of correlation coefficient, among variables exhibiting significant correlation; Table 4 prints coefficient 0.25 and p-value 2.5E-26.

> "Course Attitude Index (Q48 in original survey; see Appendix): measures a student's attitude to the course as a whole, based on an average of responses to 10 statements, scaled to -1 = strongly disagree to +1 = strongly agree. Positively correlated."
