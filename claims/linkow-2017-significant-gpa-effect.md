## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2020

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020). Success Boston Coaching (WWC 2021-001). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/708

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

QED with 610 intervention and 1,380 comparison students matched on baseline characteristics. Table 4 reports GPAs of 2.45 (SD 0.92) versus 2.26 (SD 1.08), effect size 0.18, improvement index +7, p < .01; the quoted finding sentence prints no effect size.

> "The study showed evidence of a statistically significant positive effect in the academic achievement domain. This finding is based on an outcome analysis that includes 1,990 students."
