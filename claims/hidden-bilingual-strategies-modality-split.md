Interview findings (n = 29) revealing practices invisible in log data: learners reported "listening to the course content in Leb-Lango via radio broadcasts" while responding in English on phones; one learner registered two SIM cards to access both languages.

> "learners showed a preference for listening to the course content in Leb-Lango via radio broadcasts and interacting with the course content and responding to questions in English on their mobile phones"
