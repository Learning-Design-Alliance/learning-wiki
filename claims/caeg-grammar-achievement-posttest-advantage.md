### Adil Kayan & İbrahim Seçkin Aydın 2020

Adil Kayan & İbrahim Seçkin Aydın. (2020). The Effect of Computer-Assisted Educational Games on Teaching Grammar. World Journal of Education, 10(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v10n1p117

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest nonequivalent-group study with two classes of 35 six-grade students; Mann Whitney U test on posttests. The grammar achievement posttest difference was significant (U=362.500, p<0.05), with mean ranks of 42.64 versus 28.36 favoring the experimental group.

> "Table 4 illustrates that there is a statistically significant difference in the scores of grammar achievement posttest (U=362.500, p<0.05), grammar attitude posttest (U=247.500, p<0.05) and Turkish course attitude posttest U=334.500, p<0.05) of the student s between the experimental group and the control group after the practices of the computer-assisted educational games."
