### Puchalski Ritchie Lisa M. 2016

Puchalski Ritchie Lisa M., van Lettow Monique, Barnsley Jan, Chan Adrienne K., Schull Michael J., Martiniuk Alexandra L. C., Makwakwa Austine, Zwarenstein Merrick. (2016). Lay Health Workers experience of a tailored knowledge translation intervention to improve job skills and knowledge: a qualitative study in Zomba district Malawi. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0580-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of semi-structured interviews with 36 lay health workers (45 interviews over three time blocks in one year) in Zomba district Malawi. The study reports "Without exception participants described the intervention as valuable" and improved confidence and ability; no harms or detrimental effects were reported.

> "Without exception participants described the intervention as valuable. They reported improved confidence and ability to perform their role as TB care providers and adherence supporters, as a result of the knowledge and skills acquired and use of the point-of-care tool."
