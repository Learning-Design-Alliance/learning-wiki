## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Population attributable risk fractions for SEN were 3.44% (DOB matched), 3.73% (EDD matched) and 1.94% (EDD and school year matched), which the authors summarise as early school attendance accounting for about 1 in 60 infants needing SEN. [→ Odd David 2016](#odd-david-2016)

## Evidence

### Odd David 2016

Odd David, Evans David, Emond Alan. (2016). Preterm Birth, Age at School Entry and Long Term Educational Achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0155157

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Population attributable risk calculation within the ALSPAC cohort analysis, using the odds ratio from the final adjusted model and initial population prevalences. The printed SEN fractions were "3.44%", "3.73%" and "1.94%" across the three matching strategies.

> "The population attributable risk fraction for a SEN score in the DOB matched analysis was 3.44%, in the EDD matched analysis was 3.73% and in the EDD and school year matched analysis was 1.94%."
