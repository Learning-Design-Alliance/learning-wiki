## Evidence

### Howie Emma E. 2024

Howie Emma E., Ambler Olivia, Gunn Eilidh G.M., Dias Roger D., Wigmore Stephen J., Skipworth Richard J.E., Yule Steven J. (2024). Surgical Sabermetrics: A Scoping Review of Technology-enhanced Assessment of Nontechnical Skills in the Operating Room. Annals of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLA.0000000000006211

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Descriptive synthesis of the 81 included studies' settings. The review reports that of 81 studies, 48 were in simulation and 32 in real surgery, and that eye-metrics and EEG were used far less in live operating, stating a need to translate these metrics into clinical practice.

> "For example, eye-metrics were only used in 4 (5%) of the live setting compared to 21 studies overall. Similarly, EEG was only used 5 times in live surgery versus 18 overall. From the simulation studies, there is a clear need for these metrics, which must now be translated into clinical practice."
