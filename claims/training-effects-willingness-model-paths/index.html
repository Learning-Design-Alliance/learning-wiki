Donath 2025¶

Donath, J. L., Lüke, T., Tran, U. S., Botes, E., & Goetz, T. (2025). The Transfer of Teacher Training to Inclusive Classroom Practice: A Meta-Analytic SEM Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10081-9

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r?

Path estimates from the main MASEM analysis of the Willingness-to-Change model (Fig. 4). The article reports standardized path coefficients with 95% CIs, including "β = 0.81" for training to knowledge, β = 0.37 for willingness to change, β = 0.43 for skills, and β = 0.25 for beliefs.

"Participation in teacher training had a large effect on teachers’ professional knowledge (β = 0.81, 95% CI [0.64, 0.97]) and moderate effects on teachers’ willingness to change (β = 0.37, 95% [0.23, 0.51])"
