## Evidence

### Alaee 2023

Alaee, D. Z., & Zwickl, B. M. (2023). Challenges and outcomes in remote undergraduate research programs during the COVID-19 pandemic. arXiv. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative longitudinal phenomenographic interview study (94 interviews with ten mentees and eight paired mentors across six remote REU programs). Mentees reported difficulty with software installation and access, "partly due to the lack of resources and partly due to the lack of in-person contacts who could provide technical support."

> "Several mentees mentioned they had a hard time accessing the right technologies, partly due to the lack of resources and partly due to the lack of in-person contacts who could provide technical support."
