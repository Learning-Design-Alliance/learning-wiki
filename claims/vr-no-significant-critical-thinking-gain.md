## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Meta-analysis of three RCTs found no significant difference in critical thinking improvement between VR and control teaching (SMD = 0.79, 95% CI [-0.05, 0.46], p = 0.07). [→ Liu Kai 2023](#liu-kai-2023)

## Evidence

### Liu Kai 2023

Liu Kai, Zhang Weiwei, Li Wei, Wang Ting, Zheng Yanxue. (2023). Effectiveness of virtual reality in nursing education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04662-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of three randomized controlled trials of VR for critical thinking; heterogeneity was high (P < 0.001, I2 = 93%). The pooled "SMD = 0.79" was not statistically significant (p = 0.07) and the confidence interval crossed zero.

> "The combined results showed no significant difference in the improvement of satisfaction in nursing education with VR technology compared to the control teaching modality (SMD = 0.79, 95% CI [-0.05, 0.46], p = 0.07, Fig. 8)."
