## Evidence

### Jing Li and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Jing Li and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Students' Difficulties with Equations involving Circuit Elements. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.04872](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.04872)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multiple-choice quiz administered in recitations to 237 calculus-based introductory physics students who had received traditional lecture-based instruction. The printed response distribution shows "54% of the students answered the question about resistance correctly" with the stated incorrect-response percentages.

> "It shows that 54% of the students answered the question about resistance correctly; 25% incorrectly claimed that the resistance depends on only the current and voltage ; and 12% thought that the resistance depends on all of the factors given."
