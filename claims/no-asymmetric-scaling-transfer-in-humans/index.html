Yinqi Huang 2026¶

Yinqi Huang, Yilin Cai, Mengyao Li, Yue Chen & Robert C. Wilson. (2026). Human learning dynamics during teleoperation of surgical robots. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00442-6

Human retention/transfer LME analysis across training ratios 1:0.2 and 1:0.6 tested at 1:0.4; the article reports "the observed effect of motion scaling ratio was small" and no clear asymmetric transfer pattern. No effect size for the ratio effect is printed.

"In contrast, the observed effect of motion scaling ratio was small, and we did not observe a clear asymmetric transfer pattern in humans. Thus, the human results do not support Hypothesis 3 as ori- ginally stated."
