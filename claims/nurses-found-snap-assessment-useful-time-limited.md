### Chan Bibiana C 2013

Chan Bibiana C, Jayasinghe Upali W, Christl Bettina, Laws Rachel A, Orr Neil, Williams Anna, Partington Kate, Harris Mark F. (2013). The impact of a team-based intervention on the lifestyle risk factor management practices of community nurses: outcomes of the community nursing SNAP trial. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-13-54

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of four focus groups (31 of 71 trained nurses) and semi-structured interviews; one manager said it "just formalised something that was part of our everyday practice".

> "It was acknowledged that the intervention was “an extension” to the work that these nurses routinely perform."

### Chan Bibiana C 2013 (2)

Chan Bibiana C, Jayasinghe Upali W, Christl Bettina, Laws Rachel A, Orr Neil, Williams Anna, Partington Kate, Harris Mark F. (2013). The impact of a team-based intervention on the lifestyle risk factor management practices of community nurses: outcomes of the community nursing SNAP trial. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-13-54

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Focus group theme; nurses reported being "not really given enough time" amid a very big caseload, and some preferred spreading lifestyle discussion over several visits rather than the first meeting.

> "Time constraints and a large caseload were identified by both nurses and managers as a major barrier to conducting brief lifestyle interventions:"
