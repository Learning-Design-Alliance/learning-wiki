### Rhee Jiyoung 2025

Rhee Jiyoung, Park Hyun Soo. (2025). Standardized learner simulation for debriefer training through video conference. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.321

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed linear model of OSAD ratings (session as fixed effect; random effects for trainees and raters) on video-recorded simulations rated one month after the course by two CHSE-certified trainers. Table 2 reports session means from 28.2±3.61 to 33.9±3.15 and p-value for trend 0.006; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The mixed linear models revealed a statistically significant effect of SL simulation course on OSAD scores (p=0.006)."
