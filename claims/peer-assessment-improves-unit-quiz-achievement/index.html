Evidence¶

Sun DL 2015¶

Sun DL, Harris N, Walther G, Baiocchi M (2015) Peer Assessment Enhances Student Learning: The Results of a Matched Randomized Crossover Experiment in a College Statistics Class. PLoS ONE 10(12): e0143177. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0143177

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Matched randomized crossover RCT in a large introductory statistics course; 299 students analyzed for quiz scores after excluding non-compliers. Peer assessment yielded "Cohen's d = .115, t(298) = 2.92, p = .002" versus control students graded by instructors.

"The students who participated in peer assessment during a given unit performed significantly better on the unit quizzes (Cohen's d = .115, t(298) = 2.92, p = .002), as compared with students who did not."
