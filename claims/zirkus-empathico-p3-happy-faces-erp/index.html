Evidence¶

Naumann 2023¶

Naumann, S., Bayer, M., Kirst, S., van der Meer, E. & Dziobek, I. (2023). A randomized controlled trial on the digital socio-emotional competence training Zirkus Empathico for preschoolers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00169-8

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Post-training EEG ANOVAs (Zirkus Empathico N = 33; Controls N = 34) on P3 amplitudes with facial expression as within factor. The "interaction with training" (p = 0.04) showed the training group had larger P3 amplitudes for happy vs. neutral (p = 0.01) and happy vs. angry (p = 0.02) faces.

"The facial expressi on main effect was quali ﬁed by an interaction with training ( d = 0.11 [<0.01, 0.30], p = 0.04). The Zirkus Empathico group showed larger P3 amplitudes for happy vs. neutral faces ( p = 0.01) and happy vs. angry faces ( p = 0.02)."

Naumann 2023 (2)¶

Naumann, S., Bayer, M., Kirst, S., van der Meer, E. & Dziobek, I. (2023). A randomized controlled trial on the digital socio-emotional competence training Zirkus Empathico for preschoolers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00169-8

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Post-training EEG ANOVAs on early ERP components. Contrary to hypothesis, "N170 amplitudes were neither modulated by training"; for P1 there was a main effect of facial expression (larger for happy vs. neutral, p = 0.008) but no training effect (d = 0.19, p = 0.43).

"N170 amplitudes were neither modulated by training ( d = 0.08 [<0.01, 0.12], p = 0.73), facial expression ( d = 0.07 [<0.01, 0.10], p = 0.66), nor their interaction"
