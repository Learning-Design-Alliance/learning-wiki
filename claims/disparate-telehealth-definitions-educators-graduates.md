## Evidence

### Jones 2025

Jones, C. and Cominos, N. (2025). The Future of Healthcare—Simulation‐Based Learning Curricula and Preparing Future Midwives for Effective Rapport‐Building in Telehealth Settings. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16927

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative semi-structured interviews with three educators and seven midwifery graduates in a South Australian Bachelor of Midwifery simulation-based telehealth curriculum, analysed with reflexive thematic analysis. Interview data showed "two clinical educators considered telephone calls to be part of routine practice" and three graduates also initially excluded telephones, with telehealth seen as a second option.

> "There was broad agreement between the educators and graduates that telehealth is a medium that involves audio and visual platforms to enable individuals to access health care, however, two clinical educators considered telephone calls to be part of routine practice"
