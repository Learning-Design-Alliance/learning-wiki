Session II per-task analysis (Figure 9) showing quiz-based groups consistently performed better on novel tasks P12–P14; e.g., on P12 success rates were 22.7% (None), 29.5% (Code-Rec), 38.1% (Code-Quiz), 35.4% (Plan-Quiz). The article reports the trend "though no individual comparison reached significance."
