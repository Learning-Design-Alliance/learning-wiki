Descriptive analysis of the postexercise learning-preference survey (117 respondents) comparing VR modules with conventional lectures and synthetic-bone practical exercises (Table 6).

> "Only 3.4% (4/117) chose VR modules alone as their preferred method of learning, 89% (104/117) preferred VR as an adjunct to conventional methods, and only 8% (9/117) believed VR added no value (Table 6)."
