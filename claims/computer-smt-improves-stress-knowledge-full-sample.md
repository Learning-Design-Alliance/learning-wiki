## Evidence

### UMANODAN Rino 2014

UMANODAN Rino, SHIMAZU Akihito, MINAMI Masahide, KAWAKAMI Norito. (2014). Effects of Computer-based Stress Management Training on Psychological Well-being and Work Performance in Japanese Employees: A Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial. Industrial Health 52(6). https://doi.org/10.2486/indhealth.2013-0209

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized controlled trial of 12 work units (N=263) in a manufacturing company, analyzed with mixed-model repeated measures ANOVA adjusting for job demands. The article reports "a statistically significant group × time interaction on knowledge about stress management (F=6.028, p=0.003)"; knowledge scores increased from T0 to T1 in the intervention group (p<0.001).

> "A repeated-measures mixed-model ANOVA revealed a statistically significant group × time interaction on knowledge about stress management (F=6.028, p=0.003)."
