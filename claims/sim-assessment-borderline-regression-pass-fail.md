### Toale Conor 2024

Toale Conor, Morris Marie, Roche Adam, Voborsky Miroslav, Traynor Oscar, Kavanagh Dara. (2024). Development and validation of a simulation-based assessment of operative competence for higher specialist trainees in general surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11024-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consequences-domain analysis generating per-station pass scores via borderline regression on the 13 candidates' scores (total passing score 44.82, total passing rate 69.23% per Table 5); pass rates did not differ between compensatory and conjunctive models (9/13 both ways).

> "Either method resulted in all ‘senior’ trainees passing and 2/6 of ‘junior’ trainees passing. Only one trainee passed all eight stations."
