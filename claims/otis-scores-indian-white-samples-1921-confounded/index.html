Evidence¶

Gates 1921¶

Gates, A. I. (1921). Educational Psychology at the Chicago Meetings of Scientific Societies. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(2). https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q1 · i? · associational · r1

Conference report of a group-testing study: Hunter tested 715 American Indians and 1366 native whites of equal age, reporting medians of 82.6 and 122.6 respectively; with comparable groups intelligence reportedly varied with white blood degree. No effect size is printed.

"Walter S. Hunter, Kansas University, measured 715 American Indians of full and partial breed, finding a median Otis score of 82.6, as compared to a score of 122.6 for 1366 native whites of equal age."

Gates 1921 (2)¶

Gates, A. I. (1921). Educational Psychology at the Chicago Meetings of Scientific Societies. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(2). https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q1 · i? · associational · r1

Conference report of a second testing study of 371 Indians finding a similar relation, with the article itself stating the confounding variable of educational advantages "was unable to entirely eliminate" between groups. No effect size is printed.

"Thomas R. Garth, University of Texas, in tests of 371 Indians, found a similar relation of intelligence to blood, but was unable to entirely eliminate variations in educational advantages among the groups."
