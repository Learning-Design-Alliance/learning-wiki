## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Al Essa A 2026

Al Essa A, Schmidt HG, Mamede S, Knežević K, Zwaan L, Magzoub M. (2026). Premature closure underlies bias in medical diagnosis in students: A randomised controlled experiment. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.70229

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed modelling of total processing time per word in the same experiment; mean time was 0.436 s/word under SDF-at-the-beginning versus 0.490 under No-SDF, with "Time spent while the SDF appeared at the end did not significantly differ from the No‐SDF condition".

> "Only early presentation of SDFs was associated with reduced processing time per word (F2,636 = 4.799, p < 0.01), consistent with premature closure."
