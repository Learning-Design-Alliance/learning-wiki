Evidence¶

Christopher T. Stripling and T. Grady Roberts 2014¶

Christopher T. Stripling and T. Grady Roberts. (2014). Effects of Mathematics Integration in a Teaching Methods Course on Mathematics Ability of Preservice Agricultural Education Teachers. Journal of Agricultural Education, 55(3), 1-16. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2014.03001

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive statistics from the quasi-experimental study (Table 1): experimental group pretest M = 38.31 (SD = 11.03), posttest M = 45.71; the control group's mean decreased 0.32%. The authors state this "may suggest that the MTIS treatment is effective at providing some remediation to preservice teachers with lower mathematics ability scores".

"The experimental group’s mathematics ability mean increased 7.40% from week 2 to week 16 of the teaching methods course."
