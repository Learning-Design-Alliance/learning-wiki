Imagine IM: Evidence Rating 2024¶

Imagine IM: Evidence Rating. (2024). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/imagine-learning-illustrative-mathematics/

q2 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r1

Summary synthesis of four qualifying studies (three prospective quasi-experimental evaluations and one retrospective matched study) involving 8,720 students, reporting "an average effect size of +0.11 across studies" and a Moderate evidence rating.

"Overall, findings consistently favor Imagine IM, with an average effect size of +0.11 across studies, supporting a Moderate evidence rating."

Related Claims¶

  • In a Maryland rural fringe district study of over 700 students in grades K-5, Imagine IM showed significant impacts on Acadience Math (+0.26) and Grade 2 NWEA MAP Growth Math (+0.39), with positive but non-significant effects on the state MCAP assessment — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • In a small urban district in Iowa with over 4,400 students in grades K-5, Imagine IM showed directionally positive impacts on FastBridge earlyMath (+0.10) and aMath (+0.04), with significant subgroup effects and no meaningful differences on the state math assessment — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • A small retrospective matched study of 502 students in grades K-4 found positive impacts on Acadience (+0.13) and Grade 2 MAP Growth (+0.42), with no differences for 4th grade students on a state math assessment — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • In a matched suburban-district study, Imagine IM showed no differences on GCAS for grades 3-5 but significant positive impacts on the MAP (effect size = +0.38), strongest for students with disabilities and economically disadvantaged students — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
