## Evidence

### Desvergez A 2019

Desvergez A, Winer A, Gouyon J-B, Descoins M. (2019). An observational study using eye tracking to assess resident and senior anesthetists’ situation awareness and visual perception in postpartum hemorrhage high fidelity simulation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0221515

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-hoc analysis of fixation counts by region of interest in the video-viewing eye-tracking phase. The perineal region sits at the screen center; the authors offer center bias, greater peripheral-vision use by experts, and more efficient information extraction as possible explanations.

> "The post-hoc analysis of the interaction effect between the Group and ROI factors (F (16, 2754) = 1.82, p <0.05) only revealed that Experts fixed the perineal region significantly more often than the Novices (16.7 ± 0.44 vs. 14.9 ± 0.45, p <0.05), no other ROI were statistically more observed by Experts compared to Novices."
