Operational comparison of two OCR approaches across the exam corpus. The GPT-4V-only approach "exhibited strong variability in the accuracy of text interpretation, occasionally producing erroneous interpretations and extrapolations."

> "Overall, it was found that MathPix with GPT-4V sup- port was more reliable and reproducible than using only GPT-4V."
