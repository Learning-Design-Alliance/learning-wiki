`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 20 quantum industry managers at 17 companies, conducted December 2024 to April 2025. The theme of "a workforce spanning a range of educational levels" appeared in 17 of 20 interviews; coders showed 95% agreement on a subset.

> "The need for a workforce spanning a range of educational levels to support the scaling of the quantum industry is a theme that was identified in 17 out of 20 of the quantum industry manager interviews."
