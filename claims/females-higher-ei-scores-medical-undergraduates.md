Emotional intelligence and academic performance of medical undergraduates: a cross-sectional study in a selected university in Sri Lanka. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1018-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 130 Sri Lankan medical undergraduates using the Genos EI inventory; independent-sample t-test comparing female (n = 80) and male (n = 50) mean total EI scores, with females scoring higher (p = 0.045).
