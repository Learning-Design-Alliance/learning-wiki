## Evidence

### van der Zanden 2020

van der Zanden, P. J. A. C.; Meijer, P. C.; Beghetto, R. A. (2020). A review study about creativity in adolescence: Where is the social context? Thinking Skills and Creativity, 38, article 100702. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2020.100702

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of questionnaire studies in the review's sample (e.g., Erbas and Bas, 2015; Hong et al., 2014). The review reports openness to experience "positively associated with creativity in studies in our sample," with Hong et al. (2014) finding it related to accomplishments in music, writing and arts but not science and technology.

> "Openness to experience, a factor frequently cited in the creativity studies literature as a key correlate of creativity (see Feist, 1998), was also reported as being positively associated with creativity in studies in our sample"

### van der Zanden 2020 (2)

van der Zanden, P. J. A. C.; Meijer, P. C.; Beghetto, R. A. (2020). A review study about creativity in adolescence: Where is the social context? Thinking Skills and Creativity, 38, article 100702. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2020.100702

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The review attributes this to a questionnaire study (N = 439, China) reported in its Table 1. The review reports that "creative self-efficacy and intrinsic motivation contributed to creative activities and accomplishments" across the named domains; personality or motivation variables were not related to technology activities.

> "Hong et al. (2014) found that creative self-efficacy and intrinsic motivation contributed to creative activities and accomplishments in the domains of music, writing, arts, and/or science."
