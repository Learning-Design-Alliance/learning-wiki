## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Interactive tutorial to improve student understanding of single photon experiments involving a Mach-Zehnder Interferometer. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/37/2/024001](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/37/2/024001)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Table 1 of the pretest/posttest evaluation lists common difficulty Q5 with percentages 41 (UG pretest, N = 44) and 40 (G pretest, N = 45), falling to 0 (UG) and 7 (G) on the posttest. This difficulty concerns an additional detector placed in the L path (Fig. 5).

> "Q5 A photo-detector placed in the U or L path may absorb photons but does not affect whether interference is observed if photons arrive at detectors D1 and D2"
