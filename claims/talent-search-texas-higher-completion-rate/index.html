Evidence¶

Talent Search 2006¶

Talent Search. (2006). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. http://www.ed.gov/programs/triotalent/index.html

q2 · i1 · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental Texas study of 10 Talent Search projects: 4,027 participants propensity-matched on 18 characteristics to 30,842 nonparticipants from the same high schools; effective sample size 8,054 after weighting. Completion was "86% compared with 77%", effect size 0.37, statistically significant, improvement index +14.

"The Texas study indicated that Talent Search participants completed school at a significantly higher rate than comparison group students—86% compared with 77%."
