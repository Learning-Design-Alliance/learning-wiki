`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive referral findings from the survey of 89 dental professionals: "Many respondents had never received a referral from a nephrologist (n = 56, 62.92%)", and most reported no confirmed direct communication with a nephrologist about a shared patient.

> "Many respondents had never received a referral from a nephrologist (n = 56, 62.92%), and 70.79% (n = 63) reported no confirmed direct communication with a nephrologist regarding a shared patient."

### Chau Reinhard Chun Wang 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square analysis of referral frequency across private practice, academic medical centre, and public/government settings (n = 86 after excluding three other-setting respondents) found "no statistically significant variation in referral frequency across the three settings (χ² = 11.23, P = .08, Cramér's V = 0.26)"; no standardised effect size (d, g, r) was printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "Chi-square analysis revealed no statistically significant variation in referral frequency across the three settings (χ² = 11.23, P = .08, Cramér's V = 0.26), though a trend towards higher referral volumes among academic centre respondents was observed"
