## Evidence

### Kumar and Raja 2012

S. Praveen Kumar & B. William Dharma Raja. (2012). Remedial Instruction to Enhance Mathematical Ability of Dyscalculics. i-manager's Journal on Educational Psychology, Vol. 6 No. 1 May - July 2012. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102291

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group experimental study of 46 dyscalculic Standard V students given one week of remedial instruction; dependent t-test on mathematical ability test scores. The article reports a significant pre-post difference, with means rising from 30.83 to 48.07, "the students of treatment group scored better after remedial instruction".

> "Since p value is lesser than 0.01, the null hypothesis is rejected at 1% level of significance. Hence it is concluded that there is significant difference between the scores of the treatment group before and after remedial instruction."
