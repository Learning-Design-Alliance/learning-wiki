Rathleff Camilla Rams 2025¶

Rathleff Camilla Rams, Vergel John, Ryberg Thomas, Kristensen Jette Kolding, Telléus Patrik Kjaersdam. (2025). Medical students’ evaluation of digital problem-based learning: a mixed-methods systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07823-2

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Thematic synthesis of 195 student quotes categorized under epistemic design found that "Tasks within the digital PBL context gave the students a sense of Ownership" due to flexibility allowing greater autonomy in when and where to engage.

"Tasks within the digital PBL context gave the students a sense of Ownership. This was explained by the flexibility in the design of the task which allowed students greater autonomy in deciding – such as when and where to engage with the learning activities"
