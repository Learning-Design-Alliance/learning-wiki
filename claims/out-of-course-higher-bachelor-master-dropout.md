## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among Bachelor graduates at the School of Economics of Sapienza, students who do not complete their studies on time have a higher probability of withdrawing after the Bachelor degree than regular students, across quantile levels. [→ Cristina Mollica and Lea Petrella 2015](#cristina-mollica-and-lea-petrella-2015)

## Evidence

### Cristina Mollica and Lea Petrella 2015

Cristina Mollica and Lea Petrella. (2015). Bayesian binary quantile regression for the analysis of Bachelor-Master transition. [doi:10.1080/02664763.2016.1263835](https://doi.org/10.1080/02664763.2016.1263835)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian BQR analysis of administrative data on 2655 Bachelor graduates in Economics (a.y. 2009-2013); the out-of-course indicator's posterior interval estimates lie above zero across quantiles, showing "a higher probability to retire from the institution" for late graduates.

> "students who do not complete their studies on time have a higher probability to retire from the institution, as indicated by the interval estimates largely above the dashed line in the corresponding plot of Figure 5."
