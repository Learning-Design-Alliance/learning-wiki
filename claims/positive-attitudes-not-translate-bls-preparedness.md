### Khattak Osama 2026

Assessment of clinical readiness, knowledge and attitude, regarding Basic Life Support (BLS) and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) skill among dentists practicing in Saudi Arabia. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21098

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Attitude section of the survey (four dichotomous Agree/Disagree items). The article reports 85.5% agreement that CPR is critical, 90.5% feeling morally obligated, and 74.0% supporting mandatory licensure training; no effect size is printed for these percentages.

> "The majority agreed that CPR is critical for dental professionals (85.5%), that they feel morally obligated to help during emergencies (90.5%), and that BLS training should be mandatory for licensure (74.0%)."

### Khattak Osama 2026 (2)

Khattak Osama, Ehsan Aliya, Khalid Ayesha Akbar, Iqbal Azhar, Chaudhary Farooq Ahmad, Baig Muhammad Nadeem, Alsharari Thani, Memon Muhammad Rizwan. (2026). Assessment of clinical readiness, knowledge and attitude, regarding Basic Life Support (BLS) and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) skill among dentists practicing in Saudi Arabia. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21098

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of the same 400-dentist cross-sectional survey, contrasting the positive attitude percentages with the low knowledge (26.0% good) and confidence figures. This is the authors' stated reading, not a tested comparison; no effect size is printed.

> "However, positive attitudes alone did not translate into adequate preparedness, as knowledge and confidence remained limited."
