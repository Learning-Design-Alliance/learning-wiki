Evidence¶

Brookman-Byrne A 2018¶

Brookman-Byrne A, Mareschal D, Tolmie AK, Dumontheil I (2018) Inhibitory control and counterintuitive science and maths reasoning in adolescence. PLoS ONE 13(6): e0198973. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198973

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Mixed-model ANOVA on the misconceptions task (N = 87) showed a main effect of Age group on accuracy, F(3, 83) = 5.61, p = .001, ηp2 = .169 (means 65.3% to 72.8%); "Accuracy increased with age". There was no main effect of Age group on RT, p = .631.

"Accuracy increased with age and the pattern of age increase differed between science and maths (see S1 File for a description of Age group effects on accuracy)."
