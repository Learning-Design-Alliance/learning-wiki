## Evidence

### Turnbull 2017

Turnbull, B., & Sweetnam Evans, M. (2017). The effects of L1 and L2 group discussions on L2 reading comprehension. Reading in a Foreign Language, 29(1), 133–154. http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Duration and word-count comparisons across all four texts (Tables 1 and 2) show group 3 totalled 0:21:50 and 3037 words versus group 2's 0:11:55 and 810 words; the authors attribute this to freedom from limited L2 competence.

> "Participants in group 3 discussed the texts for a longer period of time than did participants in group 2"
