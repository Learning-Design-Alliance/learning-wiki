Thematic synthesis of qualitative extracts. Visual aids were mentioned in ten studies, including visual schedules for learners with autism and FASD; teachers used concrete objects to convey abstract concepts, taught step by step at a slower pace, and organized simulations, debates, games, storytelling, and peer tutoring pairing one pupil "who understands and one who doesn't yet".

> "Some of the most promising whole-class strategies used by teachers shared the aim to communicate and teach content in concrete and relatable ways, with simple communication, teaching aids, and selected teaching activities."
