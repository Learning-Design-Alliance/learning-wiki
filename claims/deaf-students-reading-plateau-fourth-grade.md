`q1 i?` Deaf students reach a plateau at fourth-grade reading skill even when they stay in school until age 19 or 20, contrasting with typical high school graduates reading at twelfth-grade level. [→ Neiburg 1997](#neiburg-1997)

## Evidence

### Neiburg 1997

Neiburg, Michael S. (1997). Following Native Language Acquisition. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the Japan Association for Language Teaching (23rd, Hamamatsu, Japan, October 11, 1997). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED417613

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing a study in Gibson and Levin's The psychology of reading, that "deaf students seem to reach a plateau atfourth-grade reading skill" despite schooling to age 19 or 20. The article treats this as evidence that hearing disadvantage limits literacy growth; no effect size is printed.

> "deaf students seem to reach a plateau atfourth-grade reading skill, even though they may stay in school until they are 19 or 20 yearsold."
