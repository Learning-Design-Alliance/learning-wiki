## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Schripsema NR 2016

Schripsema NR, van Trigt AM, van der Wal MA, Cohen-Schotanus J. (2016). How Different Medical School Selection Processes Call upon Different Personality Characteristics. PLoS ONE 11(3): e0150645. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0150645

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA of NEO-FFI conscientiousness across four admission groups (F3, 1014 = 12.78; p<0.001); post hoc tests showed the selection group "had a higher mean conscientiousness score than both lottery groups" (MD 1.73–3.02, p<0.01).

> "Bonferroni post hoc multiple comparison analyses showed that the group that was accepted in the multifaceted selection process had a higher mean conscientiousness score than both lottery groups (MD: 1.73–3.02; SE:0.47–0.48; p<0.01)."
