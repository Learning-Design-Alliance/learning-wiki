## Evidence

### Cassandra Paul 2026

Cassandra Paul, David J. Webb, and Mary Kate Chessey. (2026). Missing Data on Physics Exams: Demographic Patterns, Course-Level Predictions, and Implications for Equity. [doi:10.1103/b4q5-dkn5](https://doi.org/10.1103/b4q5-dkn5)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of CourseI student-level grade variables (Table II): CourseGrade with FracAs r = 0.827, FracDsOrFs r = -0.737, FracBlanks r = -0.308, all p < 10^-4. The caption states the fraction of A-grades "has a much weaker correlation with the fraction of answers left blank," suggesting leaving a blank is not simply showing the student cannot work the problem.

> "The fraction of A-grades is, as expected, strongly correlated with the course grade and the fraction of low grades (Ds or Fs) but has a much weaker correlation with the fraction of answers left blank."
