## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized controlled pilot study, VR trauma training was technically feasible: only one premature termination occurred and all remaining intervention participants correctly stabilised the virtual patient. [→ Birrenbach T 2024](#birrenbach-t-2024)

## Evidence

### Birrenbach T 2024

Birrenbach T, Stuber R, Müller CE, Sutter P-M, Hautz WE, Exadaktylos AK, Müller M, Wespi R, Sauter TC. (2024). Virtual reality simulation to enhance advanced trauma life support trainings – a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 24:666. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05645-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Feasibility analysis of the VR intervention group (n = 27 completing) in a randomized controlled pilot study. Participants completed an average of 2.4 trials (SD 0.6) and "this took an average of 19.3 min (SD 8.2)" to stabilise the patient.

> "All students were able to stabilize the patient at least once, and this took an average of 19.3 min (SD 8.2)."
