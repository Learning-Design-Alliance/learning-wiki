## Evidence

### Grant Shannan M. 2011

Grant Shannan M., Wolever Thomas M. S. (2011). Perceived Barriers to Application of Glycaemic Index: Valid Concerns or Lost in Translation? Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu3030330

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional postal survey of Canadian Registered Dietitians (DC and OPDQ members; 6060 contacted). The review reports 40% users vs 60% non-users, and that among non-users working with diabetes clients, "57% felt the GI concept was too complex for patients to understand" and 46% reported lack of education resources.

> "Of the total respondents (n = 2857), 40% (n = 724) identified as users, while 60% (n = 1,081) identified as non-users."
