## Evidence

### Ansari 2018

Ansari, A. (2018). The persistence of preschool effects from early childhood through adolescence. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000255

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Mediational path models with autoregressive and cross-lagged pathways (CFIs > 0.95, RMSEA < 0.07) in the matched ECLS-K sample decomposed preschool's long-term academic association, with printed indirect effects ranging from 8–16% of a SD; the corresponding direct pathways were no longer statistically significant.

> "the long-term associations between preschool enrollment and children’s academic functioning were almost entirely due to children’s earlier skill development, with indirect effects ranging from 8–16% of a SD"
