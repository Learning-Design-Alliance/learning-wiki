## Evidence

### Bardach 2024

Bardach, L., Bostwick, K. C. P., Fütterer, T., Kopatz, M., Memarpour Hobbi, D., Klassen, R. M., & Pietschnig, J. (2024). A Meta-Analysis on Teachers' Growth Mindset. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09925-7

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level multilevel meta-analysis of 80 effect sizes from 52 independent samples (N = 19,555 teachers and preservice teachers), synthesizing correlations between teachers' growth mindset and all considered outcomes. The pooled effect was "r = .111, p < .001" with I² = 79.47% between-study heterogeneity.

> "We observed a small positive effect when all effects of teachers' growth mindset were synthesized across all available outcomes (r = .111, p < .001)."
