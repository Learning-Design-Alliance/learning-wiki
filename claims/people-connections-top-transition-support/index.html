q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Thematic analysis of interviews (n=31) and co-design workshops (n=27-28) with HSE learners and workers grouped over 80 supports used during transitions into eight categories; "People and connections were the learning transition supports used" ranked first, ahead of services, self-qualities, tools, and financial resources.

"People and connections were the learning transition supports used, with participant-advisors mentioning support from friends, family members, peers, mentors, people who had gone through transitions, and their network."
