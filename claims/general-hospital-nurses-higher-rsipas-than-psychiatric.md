## Evidence

### Farokhzadian Jamileh 2025

Farokhzadian Jamileh, Sabzi Amirreza, Farmitani Zakieh. (2025). A comparative study of nurses’ competencies in integrating religion/spirituality into patient care. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12613-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 200 nurses (100 per hospital) selected by quota sampling in two Iranian hospitals, analyzed with independent samples t-test. The general hospital mean was "significantly higher than that of nurses in the psychiatric hospital (p = 0.001)"; no effect size was printed.

> "The competencies of nurses in integrating patients’ religion/spirituality into nursing practice were found to be moderate in both the general hospital (122.39 ± 19.40) and the psychiatric hospital (110.82 ± 25.63). However, the competency score of nurses in the general hospital was significantly higher than that of nurses in the psychiatric hospital (p = 0.001)"
