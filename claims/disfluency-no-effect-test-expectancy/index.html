Evidence¶

Bjork 2016¶

Bjork, R. A., & Yue, C. L. (2016). Commentary: Is disfluency desirable? Metacognition Learning, 11, 133–137. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-016-9156-8

q3 · i? · review · r2

Eitel and Kuhl's experiment (as summarized in this commentary) had participants read a passage in fluent or disfluent font with static images, with half told to expect a test. Test expectancy improved retention and transfer; disfluency showed no effect.

"Eitel and Kuhl found that while high text expectancy led to improved scores on both retention and transfer tests, the disfluency manipulation had no effect."
