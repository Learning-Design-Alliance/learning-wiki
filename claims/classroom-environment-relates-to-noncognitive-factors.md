# Classroom environment is positively associated with students' self-reported motivation and other noncognitive factors within students

## Evidence

### Camille A. Farrington 2019

Camille A. Farrington, Shanette Porter, Joshua Klugman. (2019). Do Classroom Environments Matter for Noncognitive Aspects of Student Performance and Students' Course Grades? University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/do-classroom-environments-matter-for-noncognitive-aspects-of-student-performance-and-students-course-grades

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-student multilevel analysis of BEL-S data (8,318 students, 25 schools) relating other-student-mean classroom environment scores to self-reported noncognitive factors in each target class. The article reports a "significant, positive, and moderate within-student association between classroom environment and motivation (β= 0.23)" and smaller significant associations for the other factors (Table 4).
