# Low teacher–student contact combined with low family SES amplified negative motivational effects during pandemic closures

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students with low teacher–student contact and below-average SES suffered more disadvantage in writing intrinsic value (b = 0.24, p = 0.024) during the pandemic compared with students from high SES families; for math self-concept the pattern was reversed (b = −0.16, p = 0.041). ### Golle 2026

COVID-19-related school closures and students' achievement and motivation: Did teacher support make a difference. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00439-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Interaction between the low-contact indicator and family SES in the multiple regression with all pretest controls; printed interaction coefficient b = 0.24 (ES in SDY) for writing intrinsic value. The authors report the reversed pattern for math self-concept (b = −0.16, p = 0.041), depicted in Figs. 3 and 4.

> "students with low contact with their teachers (i.e., occasionally or never) and who came from families with below-average SES suffered more dis- advantages during the pandemic in terms of intrinsic value in writing compared with students from high SES families, bwrite ¼ 0:24; p ¼ 0:024"
