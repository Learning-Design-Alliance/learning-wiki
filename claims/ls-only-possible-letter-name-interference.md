## Evidence

### Piasta 2010

Piasta, S. B., Purpura, D. J., & Wagner, R. K. (2010). Fostering Alphabet Knowledge Development: A Comparison of Two Instructional Approaches. Reading and Writing, 23(6), 607-626. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-009-9174-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the experimental results: LS-only children "performed worse on letter name measures than children in the treated control", suggesting the sound-only labels may have caused confusion or interference in alphabet learning.
