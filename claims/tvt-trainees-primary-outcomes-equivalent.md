## Evidence

### Nüssler EK 2021

Nüssler EK, Löfgren M, Lindkvist H, Idahl A. (2021). The tension‐free vaginal tape operation: Is the inexperienced surgeon a risk factor? Learning curve and Swedish quality reference. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14033

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Population-based observational cohort of 2804 trainee operations (163 trainees) and 3482 experienced-surgeon operations (40 surgeons) from the Swedish GynOp register, 2009–2017. "There were no statistically significant differences between experienced surgeons and trainees at any time" for both primary outcomes.

> "There were no statistically significant differences between experienced surgeons and trainees at any time for our primary outcomes: bladder perforations (3.8% vs 4.6%. P = .178) and urinary incontinence after 1 year (21.5% vs 21.0%, P = .784)"
