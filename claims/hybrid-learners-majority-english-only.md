Descriptive log analysis of instructional language use among the 1,270 Hybrid learners, finding "57.8% (n = 734) of Hybrid learners used English exclusively". Overall 68% did not explore both languages.

> "57.8% (n = 734) of Hybrid learners used English exclusively as an instructional language, 10.6% (n = 135) used only Leb-Lango, and 31.6% (n = 401) explored the use of both languages."
