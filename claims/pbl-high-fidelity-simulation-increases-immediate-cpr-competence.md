## Evidence

### Berger C 2019

Berger C, Brinkrolf P, Ertmer C, Becker J, Friederichs H, Wenk M, Van Aken H, Hahnenkamp K. (2019). Combination of problem-based learning with high-fidelity simulation in CPR training improves short and long-term CPR skills: a randomised single blinded trial. BMC Medical Education 19:180. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1626-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised single-blinded trial during a curricular anaesthesiology course; manikin-pair skill assessment two hours after training. The intervention group outperformed the control group "fourfold (51.9% vs. 12.5%)" on the primary endpoint of patient evaluation followed by sufficient chest compression.

> "“Guideline conform CPR” was performed significantly more in the intervention group as compared to the control group (51.9% vs. 12.5%, p = 0.007; Fig. 1)."
