# Teachers and education leaders differ in which OpenSciEd challenges they prioritize: teachers rate student engagement higher, leaders rate materials adaptation and teacher capacity higher.

## Evidence

### McElhaney 2024

McElhaney, K., Urban, R., Kamdar, D., Baker, A., Tsai, K., and Roschelle, J. (2024). Practitioner-reported Needs for Enacting, Implementing, and Adopting OpenSciEd Curriculum Materials [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/215

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup comparison within the 155-respondent survey between 113 classroom teachers and 42 education leaders. The report states "Teachers identified sustaining student engagement as a priority challenge with higher frequency (59%) than leaders did (38%)." Leaders also rated materials adaptation (33% vs 18%) and teacher capacity (33% vs 14%) higher.
