`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Design-comparison study of the DC OSSE online school report card assessing usability, understanding, findability, and recommendation. The study found that "moving the link to details of the district's School Transparency and Reporting (STAR) framework from the top of the page to beneath the STAR score improved the site's usability."
