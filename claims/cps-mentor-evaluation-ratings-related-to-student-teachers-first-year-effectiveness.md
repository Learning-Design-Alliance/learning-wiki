# Mentor teachers' own evaluation ratings were positively related to their student teachers' first-year effectiveness as teachers-of-record

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` Mentor years of experience, tenure, and NBCT status were unrelated to student teachers' first-year performance. [→ Gordon 2018 (2)](#gordon-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Gordon 2018

Gordon, M.F., Jiang, J.Y., Kapadia Matsko, K., Ronfeldt, M., Greene Nolan, H.G., & Reininger, M. (2018). On the path to becoming a teacher: The landscape of student teaching in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/surveys

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

For 2014-15 student teachers hired by CPS, mentors' own ratings of teaching effectiveness were "positively related to their student teachers' evaluation ratings the next year" on the REACH system.

> "Mentor teachers' own ratings of teaching effectiveness were positively related to their student teachers' evaluation ratings the next year, when the student teachers became teachers-of-record."

### Gordon 2018 (2)

Gordon, M.F., Jiang, J.Y., Kapadia Matsko, K., Ronfeldt, M., Greene Nolan, H.G., & Reininger, M. (2018). On the path to becoming a teacher: The landscape of student teaching in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/surveys

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same hired-cohort analysis found mentor qualifications "including their years of teaching experience, tenure, or NBCT status—were unrelated to student teachers' performance in their first year of teaching."

> "Other mentor teacher qualifications—including their years of teaching experience, tenure, or NBCT status—were unrelated to student teachers' performance in their first year of teaching."
