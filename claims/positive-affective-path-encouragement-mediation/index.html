Same mediation model; perceived independence did not significantly predict post-test (B = 0.14, p = .59) and its indirect effect was non-significant. The authors attribute this possibly to its correlation with perceived encouragement.

"However, there is no significant positive indirect effect through perceived independence (Ind= 0.05,SE= 0.10,p=.61,95%CI[−0.126,0.253])."
