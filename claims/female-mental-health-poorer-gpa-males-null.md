## Evidence

### Cen Shanduo 2025

Cen Shanduo, Zhao Miaomiao, Wang Feng, Tang Lei. (2025). Gender differences in the relationship between mental health and academic performance among undergraduate students at a medical school in Shanghai: a cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-21697-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression analysis of 6,923 students (2,153 male, 4,770 female) at a Shanghai medical school, using SCL-90 screening and GPA records from the university academic affairs office, adjusted for demographic covariates. The study reports "aOR = 1.183, 95% CI: 1.035–1.353" for females; for males "aOR = 1.138, 95% CI: 0.950–1.364, p > 0.05".

> "Among female students, those with positive mental health were more likely to underperform academically (aOR = 1.183, 95% CI: 1.035–1.353, p < 0.05)."
