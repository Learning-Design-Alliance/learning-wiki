## Evidence

### Vega-Hernández 2018

Vega-Hernández, M.-C., Patino-Alonso, M.-C., & Galindo-Villardón, M.-P. (2018). Multivariate characterization of university students using the ICT for learning. Computers & Education, 121, 124–130. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.03.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cluster analysis of 2117 students using coordinates from a three-factor principal components solution, refined by K-means with Euclidean distance. The article reports significant differences among the four resulting conglomerates.

> "For the selection of the conglomerate type, has been applied a hierarchical cluster and the solution has been reﬁned by the non-hierarchical K-means method and as a measure of the Euclidean distance."
