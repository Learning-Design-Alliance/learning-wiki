## Evidence

### Lesselroth Blake 2025

Lesselroth Blake, Senst Benjamin, Hellaby Mark, Viriyopase Atthaphon, Narajeenron Khuansiri. (2025). Correlation Between Electroencephalogram Brain-to-Brain Synchronization and Team Strategies and Tools to Enhance Performance and Patient Safety Scores During Online Hexad Virtual Simulation-Based Interprofessional Education: Cross-Sectional Correlational Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/69725

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of scaled TeamSTEPPS scores from 2 trained raters per profession across the SIMBIE sessions (Table 1). The article reports the group "performed worst in mutual support and best in situation monitoring across the 20 sessions"; descriptive means only, no inferential test.

> "In both analyses, the lowest group Scaled AVG value was observed in mutual support, while the highest was observed in situation monitoring for the 20 sessions and in team structure for the 9 sessions. This indicated that the group performed worst in mutual support and best in situation monitoring across the 20 sessions."
