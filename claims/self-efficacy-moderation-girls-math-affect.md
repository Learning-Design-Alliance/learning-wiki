## Evidence

### Akhmedjanova Diana 2026

Akhmedjanova Diana, Kanonire Tatjana, Zakharov Andrey. (2026). Moderation analysis of subjective well-being, self-efficacy, and academic performance of 4th grade children in Russia. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0341318

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Gender-subsample OLS analyses (girls n = 988). The satisfaction-with-school interaction for girls' math was "small (0.09 SD) and significant (p < 0.01) only when covariates are not added".

> "The interaction effect of affect toward school and self-efficacy is statistically significant (p < 0.05) both without and with control for other variables and the school fixed effect (0.1 and 0.06 SD, respectively; Table 6, Columns 3–4), again indicating a moderate effect size for the school fixed effect (ΔR2 = 0.337)."
