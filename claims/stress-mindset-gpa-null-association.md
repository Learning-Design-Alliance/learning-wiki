## Evidence

### Jenkins Anna 2021

Jenkins Anna, Weeks Molly S., Hard Bridgette Martin. (2021). General and specific stress mindsets: Links with college student health and academic performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256351

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of self-reported cumulative GPA in the 498-participant study; analyses were repeated excluding first-year students, and the general-mindset association remained non-significant (r = .10, p = .096). GPA was self-reported.

> "Controlling for sample and gender, the relationship between mindset and GPA was no longer statistically significant for general mindsets (r = .09, p = .056) or for specific stress mindsets."
