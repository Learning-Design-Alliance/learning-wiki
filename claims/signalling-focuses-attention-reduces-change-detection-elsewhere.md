## Evidence

### Mutlu-Bayraktar 2018

Mutlu-Bayraktar, D. & Bayram, S. (2018). Effects of cueing and signaling on change blindness in multimedia learning environment. World Journal on Educational Technology: Current Issues, 11(1), 128–139. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1205386

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Small-group case study of 21 undergraduates studying a multimedia animation on computer components, with eye movements, mouse movements and voice recorded. The conclusion reports that participants "focused more on the objects being signalled" and missed changes elsewhere.

> "It appears that the participants focused more on the objects being signalled and less detected the changes in other areas of the screen."
