Krajewski 2009¶

Krajewski, K., & Schneider, W. (2009). Exploring the impact of phonological awareness, visual-spatial working memory, and preschool quantity-number competencies on mathematics achievement in elementary school: Findings from a 3-year longitudinal study. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology, 103(4), 516-531. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2009.03.009

In the reduced final structural equation model (fit: Chi²[4] = 0.75, p = .95), the visual-spatial sketch pad's path to QNC Level II/III became significant at p < .01, whereas in the initial model it "did not significantly predict QNC Level I ( = .17, p > .05)".

"Interestingly, the paths from phonological awareness to QNC Level I and from visual-spatial sketch pad to QNC Level II/III became significant (p < .01) in this restricted model."
