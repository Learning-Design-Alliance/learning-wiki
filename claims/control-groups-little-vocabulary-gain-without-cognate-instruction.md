## Evidence

### Kasap 2018

Kasap, S. (2018). The role of cognate English–Kurdish words in teaching English. African Educational Research Journal, 6(4): 213-217. https://doi.org/10.30918/AERJ.64.18.046

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control bilingual group (N = 32) received the same slides but no cognate instruction. Table 3 prints pre-test M = 20.28, post-test M = 22.44, Sig. .004; the author calls this a "slight difference" and reports a paired-sample correlation analysis found it meaningful.

> "As seen in Table 3, there is a slight difference between the pre-test (M = 20.28) and post-test (22.44) and the std. deviation for the tests is about 2 points."
