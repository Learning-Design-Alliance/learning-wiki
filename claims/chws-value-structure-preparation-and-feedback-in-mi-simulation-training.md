## Evidence

### Chance-Revels 2026

Chance-Revels, R., Phan, Q., Crawford, K., Steiger, L., & Swan, B. A. (2026). Enhancing Community Health Worker Communication: Evaluating Simulation-Based Training in Motivational Interviewing. Journal of Community Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10900-025-01543-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inductive grounded-theory coding of open-ended survey responses from CHW trainees identified structure and preparation as a major theme. Trainees reported that pre-briefing, clear expectations, and psychologically safe environments encouraged engagement and learning.

> "Trainees also highlighted the importance of clear expectations, supportive facilitators, and time to prepare before starting the simulations. These teaching elements helped create a psychologically safe environment that encouraged CHW engagement and learning."
