# Students with low achievement can assume high-level responsibility for collectively accomplishing knowledge-building discourse in a supportive knowledge-building environment.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a supportive knowledge-building environment, students with low achievement appeared able to assume high-level responsibility for collectively accomplishing knowledge-building discourse. [→ Yang 2016](#yang-2016)

## Evidence

### Yang 2016

Yang, Y., van Aalst, J., Chan, C.K.K., & Tian, W. (2016). Reflective assessment in knowledge building by students with low academic achievement. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-016-9239-1

`q2 · i?`

Qualitative analysis of 148 notes across nine inquiry threads from 20 Grade 11 low-achieving students, coded using van Aalst's framework. The authors conclude students "appeared to be able to assume the high-level responsibility for collectively accomplishing knowledge-building discourse."

> "Overall, the above results suggest that in a supportive knowledge-building environment, students in this class appeared to be able to assume the high-level responsibility for collectively accomplishing knowledge-building discourse."
