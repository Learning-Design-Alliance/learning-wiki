Maier 2026¶

Maier, S., Gunzenhäuser, M., Schweisthal, J., Schneider, M., & Feuerriegel, S. (2026). A meta-analysis of the effect of generative AI on productivity and learning in programming. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04779

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis of 11 effect sizes from 10 studies comparing exam performance after instruction with versus without GenAI access. The article reports "a small but statistically non-significant pooled effect" (g = 0.14, 95% CI [-0.18, 0.47], SE = 0.17, p = 0.389) with I² = 86% and τ² = 0.25 (Q(10) = 54.96, p < 0.001).

"Overall, we find a small but statistically non-significant pooled effect of GenAI assistance on learning outcomes (Hedges’g= 0.14,95%CI:[−0.18,0.47],SE = 0.17,p= 0.389)."
