## Evidence

### Skjold-Ødegaard B 2024

Skjold-Ødegaard B, Ersdal HL, Assmus J, Søreide K. (2024). Internal and external factors affecting the performance score of surgical trainees doing laparoscopic appendectomy: a prospective, observational cohort study in a structured training programme. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11007-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective observational cohort of 142 supervised laparoscopic appendectomies; logistic mixed-effects model with random intercepts for trainees and instructors. The final model printed OR 34.51 (95% CI 6.75, 176.51), p < 0.001 for experienced trainee, showing "the experienced trainees were more likely to be proficient".

> "The trainee’s prior experience was correlated with achievement of proficiency as the experienced trainees were more likely to be proficient."
