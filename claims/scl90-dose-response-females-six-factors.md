## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Cen Shanduo 2025

Cen Shanduo, Zhao Miaomiao, Wang Feng, Tang Lei. (2025). Gender differences in the relationship between mental health and academic performance among undergraduate students at a medical school in Shanghai: a cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-21697-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Severity-level logistic regression (Fig. 3) categorizing the ten SCL-90 factors into four severity levels, adjusted for demographic covariates. The study reports "aOR ranging from 1.518 for slight evidence to 2.974 for clinical symptoms" for SOM among females, with all trends significant (p < 0.05); "no such dose-dependent relationships were observed among male students".

> "This dose–response pattern was most pronounced for somatization (SOM; aOR ranging from 1.518 for slight evidence to 2.974 for clinical symptoms), interpersonal sensitivity (INTS; aOR from 1.308 to 1.598), depression (DEPR; aOR from 1.437 to 1.891), paranoid ideation (PARI; aOR from 1.396 to 1.926), psychoticism (PSY; aOR from 1.537 to 2.191), and sleep and diet issues (ADD; aOR from 1.452 to 1.843)."
