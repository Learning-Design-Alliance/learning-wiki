### Buizza Chiara 2023

Buizza Chiara, Ferrari Clarissa, Sbravati Giulio, Dagani Jessica, Cela Herald, Rainieri Giuseppe, Ghilardi Alberto. (2023). Impact of COVID-19 Pandemic on Well-Being, Social Relationships and Academic Performance in a Sample of University Freshmen: A Propensity Score Match Evaluation Pre- and Post-Pandemic. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20156485

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multivariable logistic regression on the post-pandemic survey (Table 3) for the outcome 'improved examination grade' (60% yes). The model retained venting, sedatives, intrinsic regulation, and SOGS total score; a unit increase in venting/sedatives "increased the probability by 15%".

> "The results showed that a unit increase in venting and in sedatives score increased the probability by 15% (OR = 1.15, 95%CI: 1.03, 1.29; 95%CI: 1.05, 1.31, respectively) of saying that the pandemic has improved the examination grade compared to saying that it has had no effect."
