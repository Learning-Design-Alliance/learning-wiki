### Pedigo Ryan MD 2020

Pedigo Ryan MD, Tolles Juliana MD, MHS, Watcha Daena MD, MS, Kaji Amy H. MD, PhD, MPH, Lewis Roger J. MD, PhD, Stark Elena MD, PhD, Jordan Jaime MD. (2020). Teaching Endotracheal Intubation Using a Cadaver Versus a Manikin-based Model: a Randomized Controlled Trial. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2019.10.44522

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Satisfaction was assessed on a 5-point Likert-type scale after the 30-minute training session in the randomized trial. "The median subject satisfaction with the training exercise was higher for the cadaver-training group" (median difference = 0.5 points; p = 0.002; 95% CI, 0–1), analyzed with the Wilcoxon rank-sum test.
