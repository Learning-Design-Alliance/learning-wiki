## Evidence

### Paulo F. Carvalho 2020

Paulo F. Carvalho, Faria Sana and Veronica X. Yan. (2020). Self-regulated spacing in a massive open online course is related to better learning. npj Science of Learning 5:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0061-1

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Regression predicting quiz performance from spacing, number of activities completed, and their interaction, with covariates and random factors. The article reports "a significant spacing by activities interaction, β = −0.03"; marginal effects are plotted in Fig. 3b.

> "In this analysis (see Supplementary Table 5), we found a significant spacing by activities interaction, β = −0.03, SE = 0.01, t(6415) = −2.71, p = 0.007."
