## Evidence

### Shen Jun 2025

Shen Jun, Zhang Shaolin, Sun Dongdong, Ge Ruixiang, Chen Sansong, Fang Jincheng, An Qian. (2025). Comparison of face-to-face teaching and online teaching in neurosurgery education for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06817-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparative study of 87 face-to-face and 84 online fourth-year medical students at Wannan Medical College; both groups took the theory test offline. The article reports "no statistically significant difference was observed (P = 0.760)"; equivalence was not tested, and the authors note the small sample may limit statistical power.

> "The scores on the theory test were 75.51 ± 12.78 for the face-to-face group and 74.08 ± 12.32 for the online group, no statistically significant difference was observed (P = 0.760)."
