Kelly C. Dreger 2022¶

Kelly C. Dreger, Ed.D. and Steve Downey, Ph.D. (2022). Reinforcement Practicality for Middle School Students: A Meta-Analysis. Valdosta State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1355660

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator testing within each category for the 31 studies. The article reports the survey/questionnaire moderator at "p = .007", plus outcome type (behavior p = .021, performance p = .048), effect size direction (p = 0.025), year (p = 0.047), and variance (p < .05) as significant moderators.

"A moderator existed where studies that had surveys or questionnaires had a significantly higher effect size during treatment and control phases (p = .007)."
