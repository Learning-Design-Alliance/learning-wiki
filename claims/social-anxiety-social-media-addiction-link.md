## Evidence

### Mou Qiaoxing 2024

Mou Qiaoxing, Zhuang Jie, Wu Qunhong, Zhong Yaqin, Dai Qianqian, Cao Xin, Gao Yuexia, Lu Qingyun, Zhao Miaomiao. (2024). Social media addiction and academic engagement as serial mediators between social anxiety and academic performance among college students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01635-7

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis in the serial mediation model (R² = 0.140, F = 108.457, p < 0.001) showed "social anxiety showed a positive correlation with social media addiction (β = 0.364, p < 0.001)". The authors interpret this via the Social Compensation Hypothesis and Compensatory Internet Use Theory.

> "Furthermore, social anxiety showed a positive correlation with social media addiction (β = 0.364, p < 0.001), implying that social media addiction would increase 0.364 SD per one SD increase in social anxiety."
