## Evidence

### Williams Adrienne E. 2026

Williams Adrienne E., Denaro Kameryn. (2026). Studying with Friends in Introductory Biology Is Associated with Low Impact Study Strategies and No Improvement of Exam Scores. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-04-0074

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Overall F tests with Tukey pairwise comparisons of percentage of study time per strategy across solo, occasional, and regular group studiers. "7.4 and 10.9% of study time for group studiers compared with 2.7%" listening; solo studiers reread more (15.2% vs 10.4% and 8.3%). No difference on high-impact strategies.

> "Both occasional and regular group studiers spend, on average, more time having someone explain concepts to them (7.4 and 10.9% of study time for group studiers compared with 2.7% of study time for solo studiers). Solo studiers, compared with both occasional group studiers and regular group studiers, spend more time re-reading notes (15.2% of time for solo studiers vs. 10.4 and 8.3% for occasional and regular group studiers, respectively)"
