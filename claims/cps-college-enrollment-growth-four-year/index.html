Nagaoka 2017¶

Nagaoka, J., Seeskin, A., & Coca, V.M. (2017). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2016. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/data

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of National Student Clearinghouse enrollment among CPS graduates (Figure 4; 2015 N=20,267). The report states the increase “was largely driven by growth in four-year college enrollment,” with two-year rates relatively flat at 16 to 19 percent.

"This increase was largely driven by growth in four-year college enrollment—44 percent in 2015 vs. 33 percent in 2006, an 11 percentage point increase ( Figure 4 )."
