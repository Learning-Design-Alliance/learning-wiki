## Evidence

### Burn H 2020

Burn H, Puri L, Roshan A, Singh SK, Burton MJ. (2020). Primary Eye Care in Eastern Nepal. Ophthalmic Epidemiology 27(3):165-176. https://doi.org/10.1080/09286586.2019.1702217

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Equipment and medication audit during visits to 35 health posts (Table 5): distance vision chart in 2 (12.5%) Siraha posts, working torch in 11/16, 8/13 and 1/6 posts by district, ciprofloxacin eye drops nearly universal, fluorescein absent everywhere. These direct observations were consistent with qualitative reports of lacking resources.

> "Working torches were found in about two thirds of HPs in the terai and hills, but only in one HP in the mountains. Nearly all HPs had stocks of ciprofloxacin eye drops. None of the centres had fluorescein or other ophthalmic medications"
