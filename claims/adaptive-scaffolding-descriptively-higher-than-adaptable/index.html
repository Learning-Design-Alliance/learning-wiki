Evidence¶

Chernikova 2025¶

Chernikova, O., Sommerhoff, D., Stadler, M., Holzberger, D., Nickl, M., Seidel, T., Kasneci, E., Küchemann, S., Kuhn, J., Fischer, F., & Heitzmann, N. (2025). Personalization through adaptivity or adaptability? A meta-analysis on simulation-based learning in higher education. Educational Research Review 46 (2025) 100662. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100662

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analysis of N = 143 studies coded for scaffolding personalization; Table 1 reports adaptive scaffolding g = 0.98 (N = 25) and adaptable scaffolding g = 0.75 (N = 12), with the difference "did not reach statistical significance (p > .05)". No effect size for the contrast is printed.

"While the differences in the effect sizes between strategies did not reach statistical significance (p > .05), descriptive values indicate that adaptable scaffolding seems to be in general slightly less beneficial than adaptive scaffolding"
