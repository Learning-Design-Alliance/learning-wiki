## Evidence

### Harris Rebecca B. 2019

Harris Rebecca B., Grunspan Daniel Z., Pelch Michael A., Fernandes Giselle, Ramirez Gerardo, Freeman Scott. (2019). Can Test Anxiety Interventions Alleviate a Gender Gap in an Undergraduate STEM Course? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-05-0083

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression of exam points on gender controlling for GPA within the control section of the quasi-randomized course. The article reports women "did not differ from men in exam points" (β = −1.191, p = 0.572); equivalence was not tested, and no effect size is printed.

> "Controlling for college GPA at the start of the quarter, women in the control group did not differ from men in exam points (β = −1.191, p = 0.572)."

### Harris Rebecca B. 2019 (2)

Harris Rebecca B., Grunspan Daniel Z., Pelch Michael A., Fernandes Giselle, Ramirez Gerardo, Freeman Scott. (2019). Can Test Anxiety Interventions Alleviate a Gender Gap in an Undergraduate STEM Course? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-05-0083

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same control-section regression applied to low-stakes non-exam points: "women earned significantly more points than men" (β = 5.99, p = 0.002); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "In terms of non-exam points, we found that women earned significantly more points than men (β = 5.99, p = 0.002)."
