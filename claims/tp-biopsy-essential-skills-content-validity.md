Ong Kelly, Bald Philip, Dryhurst David, Ahmed Saif, Yusuf Gibran Tim, Lunawat Rahul, Sriprasad Seshadri, Thapa Gauri, Tirnoveanu Alice, Webb Richard, Rahman Eqram. (2023). The design and validation of a low-cost trans perineal (TP) prostate biopsy simulator for training: improving trainees’ confidence and cognitive targeting skills. World Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-023-04387-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validity was assessed by a questionnaire disseminated to 5 consultants and 3 trainees in TP prostate biopsy, whose consensus identified six skills (accurate zone sampling, lesion location, target sampling, maximum cancer length, and timed template/target sampling). "All participants (100%) agreed" zone identification was most important; 87.5% rated target biopsy accuracy as important.

> "All participants (100%) agreed that the most important skill in TP prostate biopsy is accurately identifying the anterior, mid, and posterior zones of the prostate on ultrasound."
