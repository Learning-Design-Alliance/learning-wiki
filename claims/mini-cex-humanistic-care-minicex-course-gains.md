`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the quasi-experimental study, Mini-CEX assessments (seven domains, 9-point scale, ICC 0.83) were conducted every three weeks and contributed 20% of the final grade. The intervention group's "Mini-CEX score (53.67 ± 5.81 vs. 50.40 ± 5.12, t = 4.140, P < 0.001)" was higher; comprehensive course score was also higher (87.03 ± 3.21 vs 84.51 ± 4.00, P = 0.040).
