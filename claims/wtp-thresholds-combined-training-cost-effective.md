## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For capital cost, if WTP per skill transfer score is above $22,500, combined virtual reality and wet laboratory training always has higher probability of cost-effectiveness; below that, wet laboratory phacoemulsification alone is favored. [→ Ng DS 2023](#ng-ds-2023)

### Ng DS 2023

Ng DS, Yip BHK, Young AL, Yip WWK, Lam NM, Li KK, Ko ST, Chan WH, Aryasit O, Sikder S, Ferris JD, Pang CP, Tham CC. (2023). Cost-effectiveness of virtual reality and wet laboratory cataract surgery simulation. Medicine 2023;102:40(e35067). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000035067

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Net benefit regression model generated cost-effectiveness acceptability curves (Fig. 4A,B) estimating the probability each strategy is cost-effective across WTP values; Table 4 translates the curves into practical decision scenarios.

> "However, if WTP is above $22,500, then the combination of virtual reality simulator and wet laboratory phacoemulsification training will always have higher probability of cost-effectiveness (Fig. 4A, Group A vs C)."

### Ng DS 2023 (2)

Ng DS, Yip BHK, Young AL, Yip WWK, Lam NM, Li KK, Ko ST, Chan WH, Aryasit O, Sikder S, Ferris JD, Pang CP, Tham CC. (2023). Cost-effectiveness of virtual reality and wet laboratory cataract surgery simulation. Medicine 2023;102:40(e35067). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000035067

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Recurring-cost CEAC (Fig. 4B) shows the combined strategy favored at WTP of $1850 and above, while wet laboratory phacoemulsification without the simulator is favored below $1400 per skill transfer score.

> "If WTP value per skill transfer score is slightly higher ($1850 and above), the combination of virtual reality simulator and wet laboratory phacoemulsification will achieve a higher probability of cost-effectiveness (Fig. 4B, Group A vs B and A vs C)."
