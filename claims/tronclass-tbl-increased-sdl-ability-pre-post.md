Hu Longyi, Li Siqi, Zhou Leshan. (2024). Effect of Tronclass combined with team-based learning on nursing students’ self-directed learning and academic performance: a pretest-posttest study. BMC Medical Education, Volume 24, Article 752. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05741-3
