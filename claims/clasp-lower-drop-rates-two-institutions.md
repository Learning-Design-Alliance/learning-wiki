## Evidence

### Cassandra A. Paul 2025

Cassandra A. Paul, David J. Webb. (2025). Course Deficit Model and the CLASP curriculum: Examining equity and graduation rates at two institutions. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Transcript-based logistic regression comparing regular-term students at two universities (NU 2010-2017, N = 3219; OU 1993-2000, N = 8235); all group-level odds ratios below one with P < 10^-3, showing "significantly higher" drop odds in NonCLASP courses.

> "For all students, the odds of dropping a NonCLASP course are significantly higher than the CLASP course at both universities."

### Cassandra A. Paul 2025 (2)

Cassandra A. Paul, David J. Webb. (2025). Course Deficit Model and the CLASP curriculum: Examining equity and graduation rates at two institutions. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Odds-ratio fits per demographic group (Table III) show the smallest ratios for male historically marginalized students, "at NU they had eight times" the drop odds in NonCLASP versus CLASP, and five times at OU — similar results 20 years apart.

> "at NU they had eight times the odds of dropping NonCLASP as of dropping CLASP and at OU five times the odds of dropping NonCLASP compared to dropping CLASP."

### Cassandra A. Paul 2025 (3)

Cassandra A. Paul, David J. Webb. (2025). Course Deficit Model and the CLASP curriculum: Examining equity and graduation rates at two institutions. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

χ2 equity analysis at NU under an Equity of Parity assumption found NonCLASP courses inequitable for marginalized students (P = 0.02) while CLASP drop fractions across groups fell "within a standard error of the overall average".

> "in the NU CLASP courses there was less than one extra marginalized student per year dropping the course and a χ2 test gives us P = 0.31"
