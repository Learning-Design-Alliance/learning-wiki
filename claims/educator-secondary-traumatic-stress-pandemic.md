# Educators during the pandemic may experience secondary traumatic stress, compassion fatigue, and community trauma alongside their own individual trauma

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · theoretical `r1`–`r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Educators may experience secondary traumatic stress from knowing about trauma experienced by students, and compassion fatigue from wanting to help traumatized students. [→ Browning 2020](#browning-2020)
`q1 i?` Everyone in a school, adults included, may be affected by community trauma such as grief and collective hardship from economic and social instability during the COVID-19 pandemic. [→ Browning 2020 (2)](#browning-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Browning 2020

Browning, A., & Romer, N. (2020). To create safe and healthy schools during a pandemic, prioritize educator wellbeing. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED610071

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The brief states, citing endnote 3 (Hydon et al., 2015), that educators may experience "secondary traumatic stress" and "compassion fatigue" related to student trauma. This is an attributed conceptual assertion; no data are reported in the brief.

> "educators may experience secondary traumatic stress associated with knowing about trauma experienced by students, as well as compassion fatigue that can result from wanting to help traumatized students"

### Browning 2020 (2)

Browning, A., & Romer, N. (2020). To create safe and healthy schools during a pandemic, prioritize educator wellbeing. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED610071

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r1`

The brief asserts that community trauma, including "grief and collective hardship" from pandemic-related instability, affects all members of a school including adults. No empirical data are presented for this statement in the brief.

> "everyone in a school (adults included) may be affected by community trauma such as the grief and collective hardship resulting from additional economic and social instability as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic"
