## Evidence

### Andrew J. Elliot · Nicolas Sommet 2023

Andrew J. Elliot · Nicolas Sommet. (2023). Integration in the Achievement Motivation Literature and the Hierarchical Model of Achievement Motivation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09785-7

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports Cury et al.'s (2006) first study with middle school students measuring implicit theories for math at semester start, goals three weeks later, and grade data from school records. Mastery-based goals 'did not have any intermediary role in predicting performance.'

> "entity theory facilitated performance-approach goals, and these goals in turn facilitated math performance; performance-approach goals served as suppressors of the direct negative relation between entity theory and math performance"
