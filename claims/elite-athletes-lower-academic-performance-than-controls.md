## Evidence

### Pinto-Escalona T. 2022

Pinto-Escalona T., Valenzuela P.L., Esteban-Cornejo I., Martínez-de-Quel Ó. (2022). Sport Participation and Academic Performance in Young Elite Athletes. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315651

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective analysis of all last-Baccalaureate students of the Spanish Elite Sport High School 2010–2019 (483 elite athletes, 643 controls), using one-way ANCOVA adjusted for sex, type of Baccalaureate, UEE call and academic year. Elite athletes scored lower on both outcomes, e.g. Baccalaureate GPA "6.98 ± 1.1" vs "7.21 ± 1.2" (Table 1).

> "Elite athletes attained a lower overall academic performance (average of all subjects) than control students when attending to both the Baccalaureate GPA and the UEE examination (p < 0.001, Figure 1; Table 1)"
