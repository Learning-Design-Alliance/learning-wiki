## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kyong YI 2020

Kyong YI, Jeong KH, Cheon LK, Hyea LJ, Kee MS, Yeop KJ. (2020). Comparison of McGrath, Pentax, and Macintosh laryngoscope in normal and cervical immobilized manikin by novices: a randomized crossover trial. European Journal of Medical Research 25:35. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40001-020-00435-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same 35-participant randomized crossover manikin trial, the cervical immobilization scenario produced significantly shorter intubation time and "the success rate with the first attempt was significantly higher" for both VLs versus Macintosh.

> "In the cervical immobilized manikin, the intubation time was shorter (p = 0.012), and the success rate with the first attempt was significantly higher (p < 0.001) when using McGrath VL and Pentax AWS compared with Macintosh laryngoscope."

### Kyong YI 2020 (2)

Kyong YI, Jeong KH, Cheon LK, Hyea LJ, Kee MS, Yeop KJ. (2020). Comparison of McGrath, Pentax, and Macintosh laryngoscope in normal and cervical immobilized manikin by novices: a randomized crossover trial. European Journal of Medical Research 25:35. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40001-020-00435-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section of the 35-novice crossover manikin trial: first-attempt success in the cervical immobilized condition was 100% for both VLs versus 90.5% for Macintosh, "p < 0.001"; no effect size was printed, so no magnitude is asserted.

> "success rates during the first attempt varied significantly between the groups (100%, 100% and 90.5% in McGrath, Pentax and Macintosh groups, respectively, p < 0.001) and first attempt failures included 10 cases in the Macintosh group."
