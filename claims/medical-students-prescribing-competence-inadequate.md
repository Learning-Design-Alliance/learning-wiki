Donker, E. M., Piët, J. D., Brinkman, D. J., Richir, M. C., Papaioannidou, P., Likic, R., Sanz, E. J., Christiaens, T., Costa, J. N., De Ponti, F., Böttiger, Y., Kramers, C., van Agtmael, M. Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeutics. https://doi.org/10.1002/cpt.70063

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of 3109 participating students across 16 schools. The article reports pass rates on the knowledge part of "26.6% and 8.1%" and school-level medians ranging from 49.3% to 73.0%, interpreted as indicating inadequate prescribing competence.

> "The median scores were 66.2% (IQR 55.4%–74.3%) and 58.9% (IQR 52.1%–65.8%) in Examinations 1 and 2, respectively."
