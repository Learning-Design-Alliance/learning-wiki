# HFS added to frontal lessons improves knowledge of ALS algorithms and of team working/early warning scores/communication, but not technical skills

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The HFS group answered significantly more algorithms questions correctly (p < 0.0001, 95% CI of difference 2.22 to 5.99). [→ Cortegiani A 2015](#cortegiani-a-2015)
`q3 i?` The HFS group answered significantly more team working/early warning scores/communication questions correctly (p = 0.0060, 95% CI 1.13 to 6.53). [→ Cortegiani A 2015 (2)](#cortegiani-a-2015-2)
`q3 i?` No significant difference between groups was found for technical skills questions (p = 0.67). [→ Cortegiani A 2015 (3)](#cortegiani-a-2015-3)

## Evidence

### Cortegiani A 2015

Cortegiani A, Russotto V, Montalto F, Iozzo P, Palmeri C, Raineri SM, Giarratano A. (2015). Effect of High-Fidelity Simulation on Medical Students' Knowledge about Advanced Life Support: A Randomized Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0125685

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Category-level secondary outcome from the same randomized post-test comparison (Table 3 reports Welch-test p < 0.0001 for algorithms correct answers); the quote names group S versus group C on algorithms knowledge.

> "A significantly higher number of correct answers was reported in group S than in group C for questions investigating knowledge of algorithms (p = 0.0001; 95% C.I 2.22–5.99)"

### Cortegiani A 2015 (2)

Cortegiani A, Russotto V, Montalto F, Iozzo P, Palmeri C, Raineri SM, Giarratano A. (2015). Effect of High-Fidelity Simulation on Medical Students' Knowledge about Advanced Life Support: A Randomized Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0125685

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome on the communication category from the randomized two-group post-test comparison; the quote reports p = 0.0060 with 95% CI 1.13–6.53 for group S versus group C.

> "A significantly higher number of correct answers was reported in group S than in group C for questions investigating knowledge of algorithms (p = 0.0001; 95% C.I 2.22–5.99) and team working/early warning scores/communication strategies (p = 0.0060; 95% C.I 1.13–6.53)."

### Cortegiani A 2015 (3)

Cortegiani A, Russotto V, Montalto F, Iozzo P, Palmeri C, Raineri SM, Giarratano A. (2015). Effect of High-Fidelity Simulation on Medical Students' Knowledge about Advanced Life Support: A Randomized Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0125685

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null secondary outcome from the same randomized comparison: Table 3 shows technical skills correct answers p = 0.67 (95% CI -1.59 to 2.46); equivalence was not tested.

> "A significantly higher number of correct answers were reported in group S than in group C for questions investigating the knowledge of algorithms and communication strategies, whereas it was not possible to reject the null hypothesis of equal scores in both groups for the category of technical skills (Table 3)."
