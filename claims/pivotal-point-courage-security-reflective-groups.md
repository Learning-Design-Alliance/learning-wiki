## Evidence

### Lutz G 2017

Lutz G, Pankoke N, Goldblatt H, Hofmann M, Zupanic M. (2017). Enhancing medical students’ reflectivity in mentoring groups for professional development – a qualitative analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0951-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview analysis of mentee and mentor accounts. A precondition was "an atmosphere of security and interest in each other, which allows the individuals to open up and speak about personally challenging topics." Two conditions were named: group bonding and trust, and students daring to address meaningful challenges.

> "The moment when participants seem to be able to find the courage and open up for personally meaningful topics was described as a pivotal point in starting a meaningful reflective group process and learning professionally."
