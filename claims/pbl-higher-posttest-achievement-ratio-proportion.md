### Özdemir et al. 2015

Ahmet Şükrü Özdemir, Filiz Yıldız and Sevda Göktepe Yıldız. (2015). The effect of project based learning in “ratio, proportion and percentage” unit on mathematics success and attitude. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, Vol. 3, No. 1. https://ojs.uef.fi/ejsme

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre/post control-group study of 70 7th graders (36 experimental, 34 control) taught the same unit over 7 weeks. The unrelated t-test found a meaningful difference in favor of the experimental group, whose post-test mean was 16.47 versus 14.15 for the control group.
