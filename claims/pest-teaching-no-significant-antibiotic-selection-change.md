# Interns' antibiotic selection on five clinical vignettes did not significantly differ from pre-test to post-test in a single-group pre-post study of a one-hour PEST teaching session (null result; equivalence not tested)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among 27 interns assessed before and after a PEST teaching session, differences in correct antibiotic selection on five vignette questions were not statistically significant at either site. [→ Davar Kusha 2023](#davar-kusha-2023)

## Evidence

### Davar Kusha 2023

Davar Kusha, Vijayan Tara. (2023). The PEST (Pathology, Epidemiology, Severity, Treatment) approach to optimizing antimicrobial therapy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04286-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post teaching study at two sites with 27 interns completing free-text responses to five vignette questions; per-question Fisher's exact tests found no significant differences in antibiotic selection. The authors note "None of the interns answered all the antibiotic selection questions correctly (i.e., earned 100%) at either site both pre- and post-intervention."

> "The differences in the pre and post intervention tests were not significant (Fig. 2)."
