## Evidence

### Lee Rebecca 2025

Lee Rebecca, Dhami Neil, Gibson William, Hamza Deena M., Oswald Anna E., Moffat Mandy. (2025). Student Perceptions of Narrative Feedback in Entrustable Professional Activities. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70089

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive thematic analysis of free-text survey responses from 35 students; subthemes included being helpful for learning, being an unhelpful burden, and a role in forcing programme improvements to supervision levels. Students noted EPAs "helpful to identify gaps and reinforce positives".

> "This study demonstrates that students have mixed feelings as to whether EPAs help support their clinical development."
