## Evidence

### Alkhammash L 2025

Alkhammash L, Saraco AN, Goes Schaurich C, Lo BSY, Krebs C. (2025). The impact of an immersive virtual reality anatomy lab for informal science education. Anat Sci Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70087

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation between Mental Rotation Test and Vividness of Visual Imagery Questionnaire scores (n = 82 for the psychometric comparison). The article reports "zero correlation (r = 0.01)" confirmed significant, prompting the tri-tile phenotype approach.

> "we performed a correlation calculation for MRT scores and VVIQ scores, which yielded a result of zero correlation (r = 0.01) and was confirmed to be statistically significant with a Student's t‐test (p < 0.05)"

### Alkhammash L 2025 (2)

Alkhammash L, Saraco AN, Goes Schaurich C, Lo BSY, Krebs C. (2025). The impact of an immersive virtual reality anatomy lab for informal science education. Anat Sci Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70087

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-way independent ANOVAs with MRT and VVIQ as continuous predictors of survey scores. The article reports "neither MRT nor VVIQ significantly affected the survey scores with the exception of a significant effect of VVIQ on extraneous CL"; this null contradicted the authors' original hypothesis about visual aptitude.

> "A series of 2‐way ANOVAs was performed and found that neither MRT nor VVIQ significantly affected the survey scores with the exception of a significant effect of VVIQ on extraneous CL (p < 0.05)"
