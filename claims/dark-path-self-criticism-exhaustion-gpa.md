# In the dark path, exhaustion does not mediate the self-criticism–GPA relationship, but higher initial self-criticism links to higher initial exhaustion and lower GPA, and decreasing exhaustion links to rising GPA

## Evidence

### Consiglio P 2026

Consiglio P, Fokkens-Bruinsma M, Opdenakker M-C, Jansen EPWA, Fleer J. (2026). Dual Pathways From Self-Compassion and Self-Criticism to Academic Achievement: The Roles of Engagement and Exhaustion. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1821

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Dark-path parallel process latent growth curve model (χ2 = 53.41, df = 32, TLI = .91, CFI = .95, RMSEA = .07) in 117 medical students: significant intercept-to-intercept path from self-criticism to exhaustion; coefficient unstandardized, no effect size printed.

> "self-criticism showed a positive association with exhaustion (β = 0.64, p < .01), indicating that higher initial levels of self-criticism were linked to higher initial levels of exhaustion."

### Consiglio P 2026 (2)

Consiglio P, Fokkens-Bruinsma M, Opdenakker M-C, Jansen EPWA, Fleer J. (2026). Dual Pathways From Self-Compassion and Self-Criticism to Academic Achievement: The Roles of Engagement and Exhaustion. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1821

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same dark-path PP-LGCM: self-criticism intercept negatively associated with GPA (β = –0.18, p < .05), an unstandardized coefficient; the authors caution that the GPA growth model fit was not fully adequate.

> "self-criticism was significantly and negatively correlated with GPA (β = –0.18, p < .05), suggesting that higher initial levels of self-criticism were associated with lower GPA."

### Consiglio P 2026 (3)

Consiglio P, Fokkens-Bruinsma M, Opdenakker M-C, Jansen EPWA, Fleer J. (2026). Dual Pathways From Self-Compassion and Self-Criticism to Academic Achievement: The Roles of Engagement and Exhaustion. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1821

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Slope-to-slope association in the dark-path PP-LGCM: exhaustion slope positively associated with GPA slope (β = 0.13, p < .05), unstandardized; interpreted as students whose exhaustion decreased being more likely to achieve higher GPA over time.

> "the only significant path identified was between exhaustion and GPA (β = 0.13, p < .05). This finding suggests that a decrease in exhaustion over time was positively associated with an increase in GPA throughout the Bachelor's studies."

### Consiglio P 2026 (4)

Consiglio P, Fokkens-Bruinsma M, Opdenakker M-C, Jansen EPWA, Fleer J. (2026). Dual Pathways From Self-Compassion and Self-Criticism to Academic Achievement: The Roles of Engagement and Exhaustion. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1821

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Indirect effects tested with 5000 bootstrap samples and 95% corrected confidence intervals in the dark-path model; the indirect path was not significant and no effect size was printed.

> "The results showed that exhaustion did not mediate the relationship between self-criticism and GPA."
