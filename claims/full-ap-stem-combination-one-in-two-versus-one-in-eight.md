Final comparison in Results & Discussion of schools offering the full AP STEM combination (Calculus BC, AP Biology, AP Chemistry, at least one AP Physics). The article reports "one-in-two" of Cohort 1 schools offer it versus "one-in-eight" of Cohort 2 schools; Table I gives 51.4% vs 12.8%.

> "Roughly one-in-two of the <50% Hispanic, <50% SED schools offer this combination, contrasted with only about one-in-eight of the >50% Hispanic, >50% SED schools, a strikingly inequitable result correlated to demographics."
