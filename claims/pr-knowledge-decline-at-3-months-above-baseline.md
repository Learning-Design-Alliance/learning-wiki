## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Median knowledge score at 3 months (9, IQR 7.75,10) was lower than post-training (p = 0.04) but higher than baseline, with the pre-to-3-month improvement significant (p = 0.01). [→ Walsh Jessica A. 2026](#walsh-jessica-a-2026)

## Evidence

### Walsh Jessica A. 2026

Walsh Jessica A., Dale Marita T., McKeough Zoe J., Alison Jennifer A., Dennis Sarah M. (2026). Changes in knowledge, skills, and confidence following a training program for primary care physiotherapists and exercise physiologists in pulmonary rehabilitation: a pre-post study. Primary Health Care Research & Development, 27, e74. https://doi.org/10.1017/S146342362610139X

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Of 17 completers, 13 (76%) completed the 3-month follow-up. Wilcoxon signed-rank testing showed the follow-up score was "lower than post-training (z = −2.05, p = 0.04)", though the Friedman test across all three timepoints was significant (p < 0.001).

> "At 3-month follow-up, the median (IQR) knowledge score was 9 (7.75, 10), which was lower than post-training (z = −2.05, p = 0.04)."
