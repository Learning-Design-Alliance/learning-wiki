`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Analytical derivation in the connection-excitation-gap-to-speed-of-sound section: the authors compute the work of making a cubic hole via six configurational changes, obtaining "Eroton = mc²/π", and compare with the neutron-scattering value at 1.2 K. A worked instructional estimate, not new experimental data.

> "Using c = 237.4 m/s [16] (Fig. 1), we obtain Eroton = 11.92 x 10−23J = 0.744 meV. The value that has actually been measured by means of neutron scattering [3] is 0.743 meV."
