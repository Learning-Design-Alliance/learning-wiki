## Evidence

### Lisa Bardach 2023

Lisa Bardach, Claudia Neuendorf, Kou Murayama, Thorsten Fahrbach, Michel Knigge, Benjamin Nagengast, and Ulrich Trautwein. (2023). Does students' awareness of school-track-related stereotypes exacerbate inequalities in education? npj Science of Learning 8:59. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00203-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Multigroup univariate growth curve models on the longitudinal sample showed significant positive slopes in every track; the article prints the effect sizes "Glass's Δ amounted to 0.306, 0.447, and 0.371", indicating increases of roughly one third of a standard deviation across 4 years.

> "Glass's Δ amounted to 0.306, 0.447, and 0.371 for the lowest track, the higher track, and the combined track, respectively (all ps < 0.001)"
