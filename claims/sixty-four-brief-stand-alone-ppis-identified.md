Evermore Wellbeing for Teens: A Scoping Review of Stand-alone and Brief Positive Psychology Interventions for Potential Use in Educational Settings. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10033-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Scoping review screening of 1367 interventions from Carr et al.'s (2023) mega-analysis, with two-stage study- and intervention-level screening by independent reviewers. The review reports that "Sixty-four interventions met our criteria" spanning gratitude, Best Possible Self, movement, and reminiscence domains.

> "Sixty-four interventions met our criteria for brevity (5–10 min), stand-alone implementation, and evidence-based effectiveness. These interventions span diverse domains including gratitude practices, Best Possible Self exercises, movement-based activities, and positive reminiscence"
