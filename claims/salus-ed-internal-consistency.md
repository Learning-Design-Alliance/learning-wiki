## Evidence

### Quintana-Alonso R 2026

Quintana-Alonso R, García JAC, Chamorro-Rebollo E, Redondo EG, Rebordinos-Hernando FJ, Fuster-Linares P. (2026). Construction and validation of the Salus-Ed questionnaire for measuring teaching competencies among nurse educators. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09009-w

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cronbach's α coefficients were calculated to assess reliability of the total questionnaire and its competencies among 242 nurse educators. The article reports that 'the overall internal consistency was excellent (α = 0.95)' and that all subscale coefficients met recommended thresholds for new instruments.

> "The internal consistency of the questionnaire was high, as indicated by a general Cronbach's α coefficient of 0.95."
