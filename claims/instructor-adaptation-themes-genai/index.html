Haifang Liu 2026¶

Haifang Liu. (2026). AI-enhanced legal pedagogy: a case study of generative AI integration in property law courses. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10698-9

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Thematic analysis of in-depth interviews with three law lecturers who delivered the experimental instruction; the article explicitly cautions this purposive sample of three limits transferability, so the themes are illustrative rather than representative, and no effect size is printed.

"Since routine explanations and feedback can be done by the iFlytek Spark teaching assistant, I am now able to devote my time to high-order tasks such as preparing students to analyze the fallacies involved in AI’s reasoning processes"
