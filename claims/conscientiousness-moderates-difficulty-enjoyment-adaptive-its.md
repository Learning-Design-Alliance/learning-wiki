# Conscientiousness exploratorily moderated the effect of ITS condition on subjective difficulty and enjoyment

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students with lower conscientiousness tended to report higher perceived difficulty and higher enjoyment in the adaptive condition than in the control condition, with differences smaller and partly reversed at higher conscientiousness. [→ Sibley 2026](#sibley-2026)

## Evidence

### Sibley 2026

Sibley, L., Berner, T., & Schmalfeldt, T. (2026). Effectiveness of adaptive versus non-adaptive intelligent tutoring systems in early primary mathematics. Computers and Education Open 11. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100420

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory moderation analyses in the N = 132 field experiment with conscientiousness as moderator of condition effects on subjective perceptions. The article reports η²p = .04 and η²p = .05, small-to-medium effects, based on visual inspection of interactions shown in Fig. 2.

> "conscientiousness moderated the effect of condition on subjective difficulty, F(1, 115) = 4.39, p = .038, η²p = .04, and on enjoyment, F(1, 114) = 5.63, p = .019, η²p = .05."
