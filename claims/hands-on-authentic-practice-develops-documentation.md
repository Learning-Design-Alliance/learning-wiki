## Evidence

### Jacob T. Stanley and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Jacob T. Stanley and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Lab notebooks as scientific communication: investigating development from undergraduate courses to graduate research. arXiv preprint arXiv:1604.00403. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview coding across three environments: undergraduate research experiences, stand-alone Master's programs, and current graduate research groups. The predominant experience was "extended periods of trial and error," a sentiment the article says was universally described.

> "interviewees described learning how to use a lab notebook mostly through an informal self-guided process involving a substantial amount of hands-on practice in documenting authentic scientific records."

### Jacob T. Stanley and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (2)

Jacob T. Stanley and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Lab notebooks as scientific communication: investigating development from undergraduate courses to graduate research. arXiv preprint arXiv:1604.00403. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview coding of self-evaluations of current practice. Nine interviewees judged their documentation sufficient, while six felt their current practice still requires improvement.

> "nine (I01, I02, I05–I07, I09–I11, I13) felt that their current documentation practice was suﬃcient for their research"
