Synthesis of NTS assessment practices across the 203 included studies. The review reports "Self-assessment in various forms was the most frequently used form of assessment (n = 78)", most commonly self-reported confidence on Likert scales in pre- and post-evaluations. A total of 72 studies used tools with strong validity evidence for both TS and NTS.
