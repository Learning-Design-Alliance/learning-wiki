The editorial reports, citing Ke, Chavez, Causarano and Causarano, a study of identity presence in online discussion forums and collaborative knowledge building. The editorial adds that identity expressions "are unlikely to produce the 'highest levels' of knowledge building by themselves".

> "They document how disclosing personal histories related to course topics tends to lead to longer and deeper discussion threads, especially when such forms of presence are encouraged by instructors."
