## Evidence

### Chittleborough 2014

Chittleborough, C. R., Mittinty, M. N., Lawlor, D. A., & Lynch, J. W. (2014). Effects of Simulated Interventions to Improve School Entry Academic Skills on Socioeconomic Inequalities in Educational Achievement. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12309

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Standard regressions of poor educational achievement on socioeconomic index and school entry assessment, each adjusted for measured confounders, in the imputed ALSPAC sample (n = 11,764). Descriptively, 78.2% of children with low school entry scores had poor achievement versus 24.2% of those with high scores.

> "Table3 shows the total effects model of low socioeconomic position (RR = 2.99, 95% CI [2.76, 3.23]) and low school entry scores (RR = 3.01, 95% CI [2.81, 3.21]) on risk of poor educational achievement."
