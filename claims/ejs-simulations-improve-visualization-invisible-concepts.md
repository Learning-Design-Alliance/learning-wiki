Qualitative survey excerpts and informal interviews from the pilot classroom lessons, reported under Feedback from Students. One student said the "3D simulation allows me to learn better" for satellite motion not visible in real life; no quantitative measures are reported.

> "“The lessons allow me to understand the movement of a satellite which we c annot see normally in real life and are unable to comprehend from the 2D [textbook] diagram. Thus, the 3D simulation allows me to learn better.”"
