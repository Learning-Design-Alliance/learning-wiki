Evidence¶

Pau Benazet i Montobbio 2026¶

Pau Benazet i Montobbio, Janne Rotter, Davinia Hernández-Leo. (2026). Experiential Versus Instructional Approaches for Eliciting Metacognitive Awareness in AI-Assisted Learning: A Short-Term Longitudinal Study. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20047 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.20047

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Within-group trajectory analysis across T0, T1 and T5 in the longitudinal quasi-experiment. The experiential group's MRC improved reliably (∆M = +0.16; p = .002; d = 0.37; BH p = .048); the instructional group's MRC remained flat after a small immediate gain.

"The instructional group (G1) shows a small immediate gain that is followed by a plateau. In contrast, the experiential group (G2) displays an immediate gain which is followed by continued growth."
