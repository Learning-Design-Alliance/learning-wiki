## Evidence

### Hoffman M 2014

Hoffman M, Wilkinson JE, Xu J, Wiecha J. (2014). The perceived effects of faculty presence vs. absence on small-group learning and group dynamics: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0258-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the paired survey analysis (n = 114 pairs), students 'were less likely to respond that they used the facilitator’s knowledge or relied upon the facilitator when the faculty member was absent' (facilitator knowledge 4.2 vs 3.5, p <.001; dependence on faculty leader 3.3 vs 2.3, p <.001); chaotic/disorganized ratings showed only a trend (1.4 vs 1.6, p = 0.06).
