## Evidence

### Akgül Nilgün 2025

Akgül Nilgün, Yilmaz Ebru, Akkurt Onur. (2025). Evaluation of Nursing Students' Knowledge on Dental Injuries. Dental Traumatology. https://doi.org/10.1111/edt.13046

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on avulsion first-aid approach; Table 1 reports 48.8% would refer immediately, 26.9% lacked knowledge, 22.9% would use a moist cloth, and 1.3% would reinsert. The article states knowledge of avulsion injuries was "inadequate regardless of their year of study."

> "In response to the question, “What would your approach be when encountering someone with an avulsed tooth?” participants answered, in order from most to least, “I would refer them to a dentist immediately,” “I would rinse the patient's mouth and place the tooth in a moist cloth,” and “I would reinsert the tooth and refer them to a dentist.”"
