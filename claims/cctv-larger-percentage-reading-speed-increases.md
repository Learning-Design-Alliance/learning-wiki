### Leat et al. 2017

Susan J. Leat, Francis Fengqin Si, Deborah Gold, Dawn Pickering, Keith Gordon, and William Hodge. (2017). The Experience of a Randomized Clinical Trial of Closed-Circuit Television Versus Eccentric Viewing Training for People with Age-Related Macular Degeneration. Journal of Visual Impairment & Blindness. https://clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/NCT00971464

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' discussion of the trial's descriptive percentage changes, noting the CCTV gains "are similar to those found by Peterson et al. (2003)"; the article also reports a 16% non-significant increase with eccentric viewing training for 1.3M print.

> "compared to an 86% increase with CCTV for 1.3M and 74% for 1M print. These increases with CCTV are similar to those found by Peterson et al. (2003), who reported that reading with a stand-mounted CCTV was 73% faster than with an optical aid."
