Merueña 2023¶

Merueña, J. A. A. (2023). Dynamic Language Learning Framework (DL2F) Instructional Model. EPRA International Journal of Research and Development (IJRD). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED637673

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the Theory of Learning section: the author roots DL2F in social and cognitive constructivism, experiential learning (Kolb), situated learning (Lave and Wenger), and task-based language teaching, positioning learners as active participants. No data are reported.

"Rooted in Vygotsky's social constructivism, the framework emphasizes collaborative learning through group activities and discussions, reflecting the social aspect of knowledge construction (Vygotsky, 1978)."
