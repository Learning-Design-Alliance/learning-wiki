`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Documentary analysis of the Polish Act on Certain Medical Professions adopted in 2023. The article reports that dietitians lack the legal recognition, accountability mechanisms, and professional autonomy granted to other regulated medical professions in Poland.

> "The Act enumerates 15 medical professions, establishing standards for education, eligibility, professional rights, and responsibilities. Notably, the profession of dietitian was not explicitly included among the regulated categories."
