## Evidence

### Şengör K 2025

Şengör K, Alptekin HM, Kara Ö, Görücü R, Ayoğlu T, Akyüz N. (2025). Assessment tool for surgical hand washing skills: a scale development study. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-03192-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analysis relating demographic characteristics to total ATSHWS scores in the 105-student sample using t-tests, one-way ANOVA, and Pearson correlation. The article reports only course repetition was significant, with repeaters (n = 28) scoring 24.57 ± 1.36 versus 25.54 ± 1.01 for non-repeaters; no effect size is printed.

> "It was determined that students who had to repeat a course had significantly lower surgical handwashing skill levels (t = 3.440; p = 0.001)"
