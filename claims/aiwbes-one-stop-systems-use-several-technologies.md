### Peter Brusilovsky 2003

Peter Brusilovsky, Christoph Peylo. (2003). Adaptive and Intelligent Web-based Educational Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00197315v1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The authors' comparative analysis of AIWBES versus pre-Web systems, illustrated by ELM-ART: "While ELM-PE (Weber, & Möllenberg, 1995), the prototype of ELM-ART was a purely problem solving support system, ELM-ART driven by WBE needs became a very versatile system supporting nearly all the needs of students and teachers."

> "While almost all pre-Web systems have focused on one specific need championing and extending one of the known technologies, almost all AIWBES use several technologies and become more complete as “one-stop” educational systems."
