Mamie Lynch 2011¶

Mamie Lynch, Jennifer Engle, and José L. Cruz. (2011). Priced Out: How the Wrong Financial-Aid Policies Hurt Low-Income Students. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED520199

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Institution-by-institution analysis of IPEDS net-price data for the 50 flagships (Figure 8, Table 4). The report states "None of these five flagships serves low-income students at or above the national average of 30 percent" and that the five most expensive flagships expect low-income students to finance more than $11,600.

"Because they are comparatively rich, with far more resources than other public institutions, all 50 flagships should be affordable. In reality, as revealed in Figure 8, all but five of them—University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, Louisiana State University, University of Florida, Indiana University at Bloomington, and University of Virginia—charge net prices above $4,600."
