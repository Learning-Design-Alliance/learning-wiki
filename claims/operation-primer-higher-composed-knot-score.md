# Adding a nodal point operation primer to video-assisted self-educational training yields a higher composed laparoscopic knot score than video-assisted learning alone in surgical novices

> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` First-year medical students trained with video plus operation primer scored significantly higher on the composed knot score (53.3 ± 8.4) than students trained with video alone (46.5 ± 13.6). [→ Axt Steffen 2018](#axt-steffen-2018)

## Evidence

### Axt Steffen 2018

Axt Steffen, Storz Pirmin, Ehrenberg Carolin, Falch Claudius, Immenroth Marc, Kirschniak Andreas, Muller Sven. (2018). Evaluation of self-educational training methods to learn laparoscopic skills - a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 18:85. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1193-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 45 first-year medical students randomized 1:1 after pretesting; the 23 primer-group participants outperformed the 22 video-only participants on the composed knot score (p = 0.016), a result the trial reports as "significantly more total points than group A".

> "For the primary outcome measure composed knot score group B (n = 23) achieved significantly more total points than group A (n = 22) (53.3 ± 8.4 versus 46.5 ± 13.6 points respectively, p = 0.016)."
