## Evidence

### Han 2022

Han, K.-T., Ruan, L.-W., Liao, L.-S., & Tchounwou, P. B. (2022). Effects of Indoor Plants on Human Functions: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analyses. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 19(12), 7454. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19127454

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Narrative synthesis across the 42 records covering health-related and behavioral functions; the review separately reports higher academic achievement and better performance in various cognitive tasks when plants were present.

> "In health-related functions, with exposure to indoor plants, participants less frequently took sick leave [54,55,65,67], consumed fewer pain killers [61,63,64], and had fewer hospitalization days [64] than participants in environments where indoor plants were absent."
