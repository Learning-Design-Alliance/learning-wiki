Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report 2024¶

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report. (2024). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA242-1.html

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Annual team connections survey analyzed with social network measures in Research Question 1. The report also states almost 40 percent of schools did not report connecting to their intermediary and on average 16 percent of possible connections were realized.

"We found that 61 percent of schools connected with their intermediary in the 2022-23 school year, compared to 56 percent in the 2020-21 school year. Fifty-four percent of schools connected with another school in the 2022-23 school year, compared to 39 percent in the 2020-21 school year."
