## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Experimental group reading comprehension post-test scores significantly exceeded pre-test scores after twelve weeks of explicit metacognitive strategy instruction, while control group scores did not improve. [→ Habibian 2015](#habibian-2015)

## Evidence

### Habibian 2015

Habibian, M. (2015). The Impact of Training Metacognitive Strategies on Reading Comprehension among ESL Learner’s. Journal of Education and Practice, 6(28). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1081318

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study with 48 Malaysian ESL undergraduates divided into experimental (n=24) and control (n=24) groups. The experimental group's mean reading score rose from 10.27 to 14.02, a significant t-test difference at .001; the control group showed no significant change.

> "Table 3 indicates that in experimental group the overall mean score of the post-test (14.02) was much higher than the pre-test (10.27). Moreover, the significant difference between the two tests is shown in the analysis of the t- test (at the level of .001)."
