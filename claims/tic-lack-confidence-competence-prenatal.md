Itani Mohammad S., Shankar Megha, Koenig Mary Dawn, Johnson Sara, Goldstein Ellen. (2026). Prenatal clinicians’ perspectives on trauma-informed care competency and training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09347-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 23 prenatal clinicians found that "some clinicians expressed initially feeling as though they lacked the confidence, knowledge, and comfort to formulate the questions" needed to discuss trauma with patients. One participant noted "I’m still not comfortable all the way".
