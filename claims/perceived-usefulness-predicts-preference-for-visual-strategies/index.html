Evidence¶

Nahari 2016¶

Nahari, A. A., & Alfadda, H. A. (2016). From Memorising to Visualising: The Effect of Using Visualisation Strategies to Improve Students' Spelling Skills. English Language Teaching, 9(6), 1. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n6p1

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Pearson correlation analysis of questionnaire responses found r = .994 (p = 0.001) between belief in the superiority of visual strategies and preference for using them, suggesting perceived usefulness drives method preference.

"the correlation between students' attitude towards visual strategies being better than the traditional method of teaching and students' preference of using visual strategies was found to be very high"
