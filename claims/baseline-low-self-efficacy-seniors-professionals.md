# Before the intervention, ML self-efficacy was low among last-semester students and experienced professionals, and did not differ between them

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Professionals with ≥5 years of clinical experience scored below 3 (the neutral point) on both PTP-ML parts and did not differ from last-semester students, indicating low self-efficacy despite conventional ML education. [→ Vaz DV 2021](#vaz-dv-2021)

## Evidence

### Vaz DV 2021

Vaz DV, Ferreira EMR, Palma GB, Atun-Einy O, Kafri M, Ferreira FR. (2021). Testing a new active learning approach to advance motor learning knowledge and self-efficacy in physical therapy undergraduate education. BMC Medical Education 21:62. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02486-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline between-groups comparison (Kruskal-Wallis with Mann-Whitney U pairwise tests) of PTP-ML scores among 28 third-semester students, 40 first-semester students, 31 last-semester students and 29 professionals. No effect size is printed for this contrast, so no magnitude is reported.

> "the self-efficacy of professionals with ≥5 years of clinical experience (2.83 for part A and 2.25 for part B) did not differ from that of last-semester students; it was equivalently low (below 3, indicating low self-efficacy)."
