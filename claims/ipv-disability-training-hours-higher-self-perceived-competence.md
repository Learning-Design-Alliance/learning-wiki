# More hours of IPV-and-disability training are associated with higher self-perceived competence among service providers, with the highest competence among those with more than 20 hours of training

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Self-perceived competence rose progressively with training hours, and providers with more than 20 hours of training demonstrated the highest self-perceived competence after adjusting for gender and occupation. [→ Anyango Cartrine 2025](#anyango-cartrine-2025)

## Evidence

### Anyango Cartrine 2025

Anyango Cartrine, Häggström Gunfridsson Erling, Namatovu Fredinah. (2025). Service providers’ self-perceived competence in supporting women with disabilities subjected to intimate partner violence: insights from a Swedish survey. Global Health Action. https://doi.org/10.1080/16549716.2025.2476822

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression (Model 1) in a cross-sectional survey of 1,151 Swedish service providers. The article reports a progressive increase with training hours, e.g. "34% for more than 20 hours (β = 0.34 CI: 0.27, 0.41)"; the β coefficients are unstandardized, so no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Notably, service providers who reported more than 20 hours of training demonstrated the highest self-perceived competence, after adjusting for gender and occupation, as shown in Model 1."
