`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within the multifactorial SEM fitted to the total sample, the three NIH Toolbox EF indicators loaded on the EF latent variable. The article states "working memory was the most important predictor (β = 0.52, p < 0.001), followed by inhibitory control (β = 0.42, p < 0.001)"; these are standardized loadings, not outcome effect sizes.

> "Among the variables included in EFs, working memory was the most important predictor (β = 0.52, p < 0.001), followed by inhibitory control (β = 0.42, p < 0.001)."
