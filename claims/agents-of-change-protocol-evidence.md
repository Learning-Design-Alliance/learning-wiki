## Evidence

### Cappetta 2013

Cappetta, R.W., and Zollman, A. (2013). Agents of Change in Promoting Reflective Abstraction: A Quasi-Experimental Study on Limits in College Calculus. REDIMAT – Journal of Research in Mathematics Education, 2(3), 343-357. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2013.35

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative protocol analysis of artifacts from the three-tiered comparison subgroup; the article concludes the experimental curriculum promoted reflective abstraction through all four agents, while the traditional section's instructor regularly initiated it in lectures and peers had no opportunity to work together.

> "Items from these protocols were coded based on the construct of reflective abstraction (interiorization, coordination, generalization, reversal, and encapsulation) and the agent of change (curriculum, peer, instructor, individual)."
