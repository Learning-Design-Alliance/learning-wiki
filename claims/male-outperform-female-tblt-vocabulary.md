## Evidence

### Abdullah Sarani & Leila Farzaneh Sahebi 2012

Abdullah Sarani & Leila Farzaneh Sahebi. (2012). The Impact of Task-based Approach on Vocabulary Learning in ESP Courses. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p118

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent-sample t-test comparing post-test scores of the 10 male and 15 female students in the task-based experimental group. The article reports means of 25.80 (males) and 22.80 (females) and a significant difference; no effect size is printed.

> "As Table 7 depicts, the t-value with its 21 degrees of freedom was -3.664. In fact the  t-observed (-3.664) was more than t-critical (2.80), at 0.05 level of significance. Therefore, the significance of differences between male and female was proved. That means male learners outperformed female learners."
