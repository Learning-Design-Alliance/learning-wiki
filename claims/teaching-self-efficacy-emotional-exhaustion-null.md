Kiver Benjamin, Berberat Pascal O., Gartmeier Martin. (2025). The relation between teaching-related self-efficacy and general job-related well-being – a cross-sectional study among young physicians. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001777

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of physicians in a university didactics training program; the four-item Maslach-derived emotional exhaustion scale (Cronbach's alpha 0.82) showed "no significant correlation" with teaching-related self-efficacy (r=-0.087; p=0.147; N=281).

> "Contrary to the postulate in hypothesis 1, there was no significant correlation (r=-0.087; p=0.147; N=281) between the teaching-related self-efficacy of physicians and their emotional exhaustion at work"
