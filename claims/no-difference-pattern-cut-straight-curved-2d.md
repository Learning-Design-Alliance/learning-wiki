`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized trial's 2D straight-vs-curved comparison, "No significant differences could be found in the Pattern Cut exercise"; the authors describe this relatively static cutting task as one where curved scissors could be advantageous, yet found no difference.

> "No significant differences could be found in the Pattern Cut exercise. Only for the number of errors at test time T5 a tendency for the advantage of straight instruments can be derived (0.58 ± 1.17 vs. 1.25 ± 0.97, p = 0.06)."
