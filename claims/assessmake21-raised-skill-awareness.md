Qualitative case-study findings from teacher and student interviews in six makerspaces, part of Theme 1 on awareness of 21st-century skills development. Teachers reported the tool "increased students' awareness of 21st-century skills" and helped them identify such skills; log files showed students completing reflection challenges such as uploading collaboration examples.
