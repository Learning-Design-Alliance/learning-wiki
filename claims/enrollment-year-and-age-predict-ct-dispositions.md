### Xiaoxia Wang 2018 (2)

Xiaoxia Wang, Xiaoxiao Sun, Tianhao Huang, Renqiang He, Weina Hao, Li Zhang. (2018). Development and validation of Critical Thinking Disposition Inventory for Chinese medical college students (CTDI-M). Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.11428](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.11428)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Secondary correlational analysis in the GLM comparison among age groups. The GLM itself showed age did not significantly predict CT dispositions (P=0.404, partial η2=0.004), while major and subgroup significantly predicted them.

> "However, Pearson correlation between age and “Open - mindedness” revealed significant negat ive associations (r=0.06, P =0.02)."
