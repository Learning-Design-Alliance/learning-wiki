## Evidence

### Morimoto Y 2025

Morimoto Y, Shikino K, Nomura Y, Ito S. (2025). Trends in the Japanese National Medical Licensing Examination: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/78214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Manual content classification of 2228 of 2518 non-public-health items across six NMLE sessions, analyzed with Cochran-Armitage trend tests. The article reports the "overall proportion of pathophysiology items declined significantly" with printed percentages and P<.001; no effect size is printed.

> "Over 23 years, the overall proportion of pathophysiology items declined significantly, from 51.6% (237/459) to 33.4% (98/293; P<.001)."

### Morimoto Y 2025 (2)

Morimoto Y, Shikino K, Nomura Y, Ito S. (2025). Trends in the Japanese National Medical Licensing Examination: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/78214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same content classification analysis, reporting significant upward trends for all three practical categories with printed percentages and P values; no effect size is printed.

> "From 2001 to 2024, the proportions increased from 21.6% (99/459) to 26.3% (77/293) for clinical reasoning (P=.01), 20.7% (95/459) to 28.7% (84/293) for primary emergency care (P<.001), and 6.1% (28/459) to 11.6% (34/293) for continued care (P<.001; Figure 3B)."
