## Evidence

### Sawdon Marina 2020

Sawdon Marina, McLachlan JC. (2020). '10% of your medical students will cause 90% of your problems': a prospective correlational study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-038472

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression of PSA scores (relative to the pass mark) on average CI scores, shown in figure 4, gave R=0.249, R2=0.062, B=0.343 (p<0.001, n=462); the first-CI-decile t-test against the other nine deciles gave p<0.001 (n=463). No standardised effect size for the decile contrast is printed.

> "Linear regression analysis shows R=0.249, R2=0.062, B=0.343, p<0.001 and n=462. T-test showed a statistically significant difference between PSA scores of students scoring in the first decile of the CI and the other nine deciles (p<0.001, n=463)."
