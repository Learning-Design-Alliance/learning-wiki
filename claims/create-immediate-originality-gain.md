## Evidence

### Nurit Paz-Baruch 2025

Nurit Paz-Baruch, Guy Grovas, Zemira R. Mevarech. (2025). The effects of meta-creative pedagogy on elementary school students' creative thinking. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09412-6

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the London Bridge Task total originality score: Time × Intervention interaction F(1, 98) = 14.81, p < .001, ηp² = 0.13. The originality per response interaction was F(1, 98) = 13.46, ηp² = 0.12, and the rarest originality interaction F(1, 98) = 11.61, ηp² = 0.11.

> "Thus, at Time 2, students in the experimental group scored significantly higher on the total originality score than students in the control group (p < .001)."
