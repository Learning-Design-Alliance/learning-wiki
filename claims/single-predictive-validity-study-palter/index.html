Pinar Sedat Alp 2025¶

Pinar Sedat Alp, Morrow Joseph J., Kong Chia Yew. (2025). A systematic review of the use of human body donor models for postgraduate laparoscopic surgical training. Anatomical Science International. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12565-025-00872-z

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports this as the only included study of predictive validity (Palter and Grantcharov 2012, 24 surgical residents, 18 post-intervention): a curriculum with VR simulator, cognitive training and body donor lab sessions versus the conventional residency pathway, with blinded senior-surgeon assessment on live operations. No effect size printed.

"trainees in the specific curriculum also performed significantly better in laparoscopic right hemicolectomy procedures on real patients, as assessed by senior surgeons during live operations that were blinded as to the randomisation of the residents."
