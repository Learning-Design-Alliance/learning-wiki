## Evidence

### Nie J 2026

Nie J, Zhang Z, Lu X, Zhang Y and Zhang M (2026) Development and validation of the generative AI assessment literacy scale for higher education students: psychometric evidence and associations with feedback engagement and academic integrity. Front. Educ. 11:1934632. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1934632

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Study 1 exploratory factor analysis with 486 students using principal axis factoring with oblimin rotation. Sampling adequacy was high: "The KMO value was .931", and Bartlett's test was significant, χ2(153) = 4,862.17, p < .001. Factor loadings ranged from .63 to .84.

> "Exploratory factor analysis supported a five-factor solution corresponding to the hypothesized dimensions. The final 18 items explained 67.82% of the total variance."
