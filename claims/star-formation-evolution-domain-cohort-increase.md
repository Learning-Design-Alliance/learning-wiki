`q2 i2` Domain 2 shows F(2, 363) = 28.0, p < 0.001, a medium-to-large overall effect (partial eta squared = 0.13), with pairwise effect sizes d = 0.52 (cohorts 1-2), d = 0.55 (cohorts 2-3), and d = 1.07 (cohorts 1-3). [→ Philipp Bitzenbauer 2023](#philipp-bitzenbauer-2023)

## Evidence

### Philipp Bitzenbauer 2023

Philipp Bitzenbauer, Sarah Navarrete, Fabian Hennig, Malte S. Ubben, and Joaquin M. Veith. (2023). A cross-age study on secondary school students' views of stars. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020165](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020165)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Cross-age ANOVA on domain 2 (formation and evolution of stars) responses; cohort means of scientifically accurate answers are 42.9%, 53.8%, and 65.4%. The text notes "a steady increase of scientifically accurate views across secondary education."

> "Likewise, all between group comparisons show highly statistical significance ( p <0.01) with effect sizes ranging from d = 0.52 between cohort 1 and 2, d = 0.55 between cohort 2 and 3 as well as d = 1.07 between cohort 1 and 3. Hence, not only do the groups differ highly significantly, but medium to high effect sizes can be associated with each of the pairwise comparisons, indicating a steady increase of scientifically accurate views across secondary education."
