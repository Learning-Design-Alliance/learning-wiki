Tamara van Gog & Liesbeth Kester & Kim Dirkx & Vincent Hoogerheide & Joris Boerboom & Peter P. J. L. Verkoeijen. (2015). Testing After Worked Example Study Does Not Enhance Delayed Problem-Solving Performance Compared to Restudy. Educ Psychol Rev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-015-9297-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 3, a randomized 2x2 experiment with 129 Belgian university students who studied four examples before testing (isomorphic or identical problems) or restudying (isomorphic or identical examples). The delayed-test ANOVA "showed no main effect of testing/restudy,F(1,122)<1, p=0.37 7,ηp2=0.006"; the testing/restudy interaction with task format was also non-significant.

> "A 2×2 ANOV A on performance on the final delayed test showed no main effect of testing/restudy,F(1,122)<1, p=0.37 7,ηp2=0.006"
