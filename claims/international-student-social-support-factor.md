## Evidence

### Jiang Qinxu 2022

Jiang Qinxu, Horta Hugo, Yuen Mantak. (2022). International medical students’ perspectives on factors affecting their academic success in China: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03597-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 40 international MBBS students (18 high achievers, 22 low achievers). "All the participants agreed" on the role of support; support included emotional, financial and daily-life help from both China and home countries.

> "All the participants agreed that social support from family, friends, peers, seniors, and even others with the same nationality played a significant role in helping them overcome difficult times regarding academic, sociocultural, emotional wellbeing, and other challenges in China."
