Motivating Students Through Positive Learning Experiences: A Comparison of Three Learning Designs for Computer Programming Courses. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group interview findings for the PBL design. Students highlighted "the trial -error learning process" and found collaborative interactions with co-students and teacher guidance "interesting, challenging and fun".

> "The PBL groups enjoyed the dynamic interplay between lecture and group work and the collaborative interactions with the professor in class. The interactive learning style in class provided a feeling of security, and the students felt comfortable and prepa red for the project work."
