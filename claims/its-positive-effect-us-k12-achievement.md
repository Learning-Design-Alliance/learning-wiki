## Evidence

### Walter L. Leite 2025

Walter L. Leite, Huibin Zhang, Shibani Rana, Yide Hao, Amber D. Hatch, Lingchen Kong, Huan Kuang. (2025). Do intelligent tutoring systems benefit K-12 students? A meta-analysis and evaluation of heterogeneity of treatment effects in the U.S. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04997

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Multivariate multilevel meta-regression of 77 effect sizes from 18 experimental studies meeting WWC standards, with random effects of studies and fixed effects of ITS names. The pooled estimate was "g=0.271, SE=0.011 p=0.001", a statistically significant positive effect. No standardized effect-size magnitude beyond g is printed for interpretation.

> "Overall, there was a statistically significant positive effect of ITS on U.S. K-12 students’ learning outcomes (g=0.271, SE=0.011 p=0.001) after accounting for the random effect of studies and the fixed effect of ITS names."
