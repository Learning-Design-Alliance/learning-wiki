## Evidence

### Sijing Zhou 2024

Sijing Zhou, Gavin R. Slemp & Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick. (2024). Factors Associated with Teacher Wellbeing: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09886-x

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Results of the psychometric meta-analysis: "Neuroticism emerged as the strongest negative predictor of hedonic (ρ = −.46), eudaimonic (ρ = −.46), and overall wellbeing (ρ = −.48)" with disengagement coping strongest for general and occupational wellbeing.

> "Neuroticism emerged as the strongest negative predictor of hedonic (ρ = −.46), eudaimonic (ρ = −.46), and overall wellbeing (ρ = −.48), whereas disengagement coping was strongly associated with both general wellbeing (ρ = −.46) and occupational wellbeing (ρ = −.48)."
