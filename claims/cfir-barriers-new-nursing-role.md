### Silies K 2025

Silies K, Huckle T, Pohontsch N, Jarchow A-M, Schütz K, Müller M, Lühmann D, Balzer K. (2025). A new role profile for nurses with expanded competencies promoting person-centered care in long-term care: a mixed-methods intervention development study. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-025-06086-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Eight stakeholders anonymously voted on implementation barriers organized by the five CFIR domains during the second workshop. The authors report that "Participants rated as most important barriers: unclear relative advantage and lacking adaptability (domain innovation characteristics)" among barriers in every domain.

> "Participants rated as most important barriers: unclear relative advantage and lacking adaptability (domain innovation characteristics), lacking peer pressure and supportive external policy and incentives (domain outer setting); lack of leadership engagement and resources (domain inner setting); individual stage of change (domain characteristics of individuals), and lack of support from opinion leaders and key stakeholders (domain process)."
