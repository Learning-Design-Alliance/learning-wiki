### Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes 2026

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes. (2026). Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08849

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Belief elicitation from the Session Two exit survey of the randomized experiment, in which students estimated how much AI access changed (or would have changed) their test scores. Open-ended accounts were coded as causal graphs from AI use to learning, with most students naming both a helping and a harming channel.

> "Both treated and control students expect positive effects, but only the treated group’s prediction comes close to our estimate; the control group overestimates it."
