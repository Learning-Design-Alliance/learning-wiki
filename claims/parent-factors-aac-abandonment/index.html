Byrnes C 2026¶

Byrnes C, Moran G, Clarke MT, Caron J, Smith EM, Galvin R, Gallagher AL. (2026). Experiences of Augmentative and Alternative Communication use in School: A Qualitative Evidence Synthesis Protocol. HRB Open Research. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13550906/

q2 · i? · review · r?

Narrative background synthesis of parent-perception studies cited in the protocol introduction. The protocol states parents "have described their experience in terms of a lack of emotional readiness and of feeling overwhelmed by implementation of the use of AAC with their child in the home". No effect size is printed; the abandonment outcome is reported as a cited qualitative finding.

"Unrealistic expectations and frustration with regards to the ongoing efforts required to implement AAC in the home can result in device abandonment."
