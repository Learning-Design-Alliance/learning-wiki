## Evidence

### Liu Guoyong 2026

Liu Guoyong, Guo Bin, Yu Junli, Xu Qian, Wang Yirong, Si Peiran, Gong Yizhuo, Huo Huanhuan, Ma Xiaohui, Hu Yuanyuan, Bi Hongsheng. (2026). Correlation analysis between visual factors and academic performance in Chinese children. Scientific Reports 16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-46397-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null finding from the same cross-sectional GLM analysis (N = 1,766); Table 3 shows cycloplegic SE univariate β = 0.20, 95% CI −0.11 to 0.52, P = 0.203, and refractive-status categories non-significant. The article reports no association; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "Consistent with previous studies5,8, we did not find an association between children’s academic performance and refractive status or cycloplegic SE."
