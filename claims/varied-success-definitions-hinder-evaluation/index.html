Qualitative analysis found that while all districts intended to use pilot data to evaluate product effectiveness, the types of data emphasized and analyses varied widely; "Districts also defined success differently," complicating standard evaluation.

"Districts also defined success differently; with varied definitions of success, it is difficult to determine an accurate standard for evaluating the effect of products on student improvement, both within and across districts."
