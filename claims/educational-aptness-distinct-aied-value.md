### Agarwal 2022

Agarwal, B., Urlings, C., van Lankveld, G., & Klemke, R. (2022). Identifying the Ethical Values and Norms of Artificial Intelligence in Education: a Systematic Literature Review. Open University of the Netherlands. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7347-8465

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Discussion section of the review comparing its six main values against the frameworks its sources drew on (Floridi et al. 2018; Dignum 2019; Jobin et al. 2019). The review reports the overlap holds "except for 'Educational aptness'" and concludes AIED ethics needs an education-related theme, as recommended by Holmes et al. (2021).

> "Additionally, the main EVs found as an answer to RQ1 overlap with the ethical values seen in these AI ethical principles or frameworks, except for 'Educational aptness'. Only one article provides a definition of an EV in this theme, implying limited attention to the educational implications and goals behind AIED."
