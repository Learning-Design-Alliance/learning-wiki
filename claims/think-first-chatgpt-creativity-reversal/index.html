q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The paper reports this secondhand, citing Wong and Qiu (2026): university students improved a creative product alone, with free ChatGPT, or under a think-first regimen; on the assisted task the free-ChatGPT group produced the most creative work and the think-first group showed no advantage.

"On a later task with no ChatGPT, the pattern reversed: the free-ChatGPT group fell back to the level of those who had worked alone, while the think-first group produced more creative responses than the other groups (Wong & Qiu, 2026)."
