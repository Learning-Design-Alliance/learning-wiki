## Evidence

### Kelkay Jenberu Mekurianew 2025

Kelkay Jenberu Mekurianew, Wubante Sisay Maru, Anteneh Deje Sendek, Takilo Mitiku Kassaw, Gebeyehu Chernet Desalegn, Alameraw Temesgen Ayenew, Gashu Kassahun Dessie. (2025). Intention to use eLearning-based continuing professional development and its predictors among healthcare professionals in Amhara region referral hospitals, Ethiopia, 2023: using modified UTAUT-2 model. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12317-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple-group analysis by gender within the SEM; the article reports the habit path was "significantly stronger for male respondents (β = 0.838, p-value < 0.001" than for females. Other predictors' gender moderation was not supported.

> "habit's positive effect on the intention to use eLearning-based continuing professional development was positively moderated by gender and significantly stronger for male respondents (β = 0.838, p-value < 0.001 compared to the relationship for female respondents (β = 0.721, p-value = 0.202)"

### Kelkay Jenberu Mekurianew 2025 (2)

Kelkay Jenberu Mekurianew, Wubante Sisay Maru, Anteneh Deje Sendek, Takilo Mitiku Kassaw, Gebeyehu Chernet Desalegn, Alameraw Temesgen Ayenew, Gashu Kassahun Dessie. (2025). Intention to use eLearning-based continuing professional development and its predictors among healthcare professionals in Amhara region referral hospitals, Ethiopia, 2023: using modified UTAUT-2 model. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12317-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple-group analysis by age (<30 vs ≥30) within the SEM; the habit path was "significantly stronger for ≥30 years (old age group) respondents (β = 0.857, p-value < 0.001". Moderation confirmed via chi-square difference (ΔX² = 5.029, p = 0.045 in Table 8).

> "habit's positive effect on the intention to use eLearning based on continuing professional development was positively moderated by age and significantly stronger for ≥30 years (old age group) respondents (β = 0.857, p-value < 0.001 compared to the significant relationship of <30 years of respondents (β = 0.672, p-value = 0.100)"
