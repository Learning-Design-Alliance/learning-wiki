### Noguera 2017

Noguera, P. (2017). Taking deeper learning to scale. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/deeper-learning-to-scale

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Mixed-methods equity study of OUSD combining quantitative achievement analysis, review of prior district reports, stakeholder interviews (N = 40), and school site reviews with 545 classrooms observed over 6 months. It found "a lack of clear and consistent focus on how to deliver high-quality instructional support to all students."

> "Most importantly, the equity study found that there was a lack of clear and consistent focus on how to deliver high-quality instructional support to all students."
