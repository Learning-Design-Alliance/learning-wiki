### Luchini 2025

Luchini, S. A., Kaufman, J. C., Goecke, B., Wilhelm, O., Kenett, Y. N., Lei, D., Benedek, M., van Hell, J. G., & Beaty, R. E. (2025). Creativity supports learning through associative thinking. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00334-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory paired-samples analysis in Study 2 compared verb generation performance on previously studied LET nouns versus non-studied nouns; the article describes this as exploratory and suggests "the prior study of nouns may have interfered with the ability to generate novel word associations".

> "non-studied nouns (M = 0.78, SD = 0.06) displaying higher semantic distance than LET nouns ( M =0 . 7 6 ,SD = 0.06), t(142) = 4.2, p < 0.001, d = 0.25, with no effect on free association"
