The authors' qualitative observation in both implementations: the teams "found the grid helpful for enhancing accuracy of recall." The biology team used indirect indicators as temporal markers, and one respondent corrected a previously mentioned event from summer to fall after cross-referencing the grid. No effect size is reported.
