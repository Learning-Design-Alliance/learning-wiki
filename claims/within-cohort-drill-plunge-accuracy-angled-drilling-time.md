## Evidence

### Hagen M. S. 2026

Hagen M. S., Dunahoe J., Parker K. M., Kweon C. Y., Kennedy S. A. (2026). Longitudinal Pilot Study on the Use of Low-Cost Modules to Improve Junior Orthopaedic Resident Surgical Skills. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-25-00257

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t tests on eight PGY1 residents' objective measures (Table 1); only drill plunge accuracy and angled drilling time reached significance; depth gauge accuracy (P = 0.879) and other tasks did not. No effect sizes printed.

> "Task accuracy in drill plunge significantly improved from the beginning to the end of the year, from 13.7 ± 8.6 to 5.7 ± 2.1 mm (P = 0.034, Table 1). Resident task efficiency in angled drilling significantly improved from beginning to the end of the year, from 37.6 ± 22.5 to 12.0 ± 3.6 seconds (P = 0.017, Table 1)."
