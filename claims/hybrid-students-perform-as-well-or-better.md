## Evidence

### Joyce P 2025

Joyce P, Cunningham M, Alexander L. (2025). Evaluation of a hybrid model of a Physician Associate Studies programme: students, teachers, and examiner perspectives. Irish Journal of Medical Science 194(2):431-438. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11845-025-03918-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Focus group interviews with lecturers and clinical staff plus a one-to-one interview with the external examiner, thematically analysed; the study reports consensus that there was "no difference in the hybrid students’ academic or clinical exam performance". Clinical teachers attributed observed differences to student backgrounds, not delivery mode.

> "There was consensus from lecturers and the external examiner that there was no difference in the hybrid students’ academic or clinical exam performance in the programme."

### Joyce P 2025 (2)

Joyce P, Cunningham M, Alexander L. (2025). Evaluation of a hybrid model of a Physician Associate Studies programme: students, teachers, and examiner perspectives. Irish Journal of Medical Science 194(2):431-438. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11845-025-03918-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Programme outcomes comparison under the Product component of the CIPP evaluation framework, between current on-campus students and hybrid students across both programme years. The article adds that after the second-year group took the national PA exam, "there has been no significant difference in the hybrid graduates’ results to the on-campus students."

> "Comparing student outcomes (Product of CIPP model), between on-campus and hybrid students shows that the hybrid students did as well or better across both first and second years."
