## Evidence

### Adamson 2014

Adamson, D., Dyke, G., Jang, H., & Rosé, C. P. (2014). Towards an Agile Approach to Adapting Dynamic Collaboration Support to Student Needs. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0012-6

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

The Dyke et al. (2013) study used a 2×2 between-subjects factorial design manipulating Revoicing and APT Feedback with 9th grade biology students in a Diffusion Lab task. The study found "a significant main effect of Revoicing" with "an effect size of 0.34 standard deviations."

> "Students showed significant learning gains in all conditions, and there was a significant main effect of Revoicing such that students in the Revoicing condition learned significantly more between Pretest and Posttest, with an effect size of 0.34 standard deviations."
