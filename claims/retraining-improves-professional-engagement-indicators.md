## Evidence

### He Peipei 2026

He Peipei, Yin Xiaohong, Xu Ying, Yang Tingjun. (2026). The construction and implementation of a clinical nursing experts-oriented retraining program for peritoneal dialysis specialist nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09379-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post paired comparison of 36 nurses using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test for variables that did not follow a normal distribution. Popular science works increased from 2(1,2) to 5(3,6.75) every 3 years (Z = -5.032, P < 0.001).

> "These included the development and publication of science communication materials, provision of nursing consultations, delivery of lectures at the hospital level or higher, leadership of quality improvement initiatives, implementation of new technologies, publication of research articles, approval of hospital-level or higher research projects, and receipt of hospital-level or higher awards. All of these indicators increased significantly from pre-retraining levels (all P < 0.001)"
