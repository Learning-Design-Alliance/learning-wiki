### Gankin 2026

Gankin, K., Venkatesh, R., Khokhlovich, E., & Vyshedskiy, A. (2026). Receptive and expressive language phenotyping in over 62,000 autistic individuals. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00445-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Joint UHCA and PCA of the 15 comprehension and 11 expressive items (Figure 4). The six clusters comprise the three comprehension clusters (Command, Modifier, Syntactic) and three expressive clusters (Single-Word, Single-Sentence, Multi-Sentence); the article reports stability "across multiple seeds and across different time points".

> "The PCA of the same items (Figure 4B) clarifies the existence of six clusters of items. These clusters are identical to those detected by separate clustering of receptive and expressive items"
