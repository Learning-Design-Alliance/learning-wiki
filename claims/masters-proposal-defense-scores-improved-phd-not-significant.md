### Xing Wenjing 2026

Xing Wenjing, Ju Huanyu, Pei Chunying, Guo Yongli, Tian Linlu, Ling Hong, Guo Tieyun, Cao Bo, Ma Jian. (2026). Research topic–driven curriculum content reform in graduate immunology to strengthen students' research and innovation competence. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08711-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Historical cohort comparison of thesis proposal defense scores (Fig. 3D reports t = 5.743, p = 0.000001 for master's students, n = 17 pre-reform and n = 25 post-reform). The article reports a "significant improvement in their final research proposal defense scores".

> "master's students from the 2022, 2023, and 2024 cohorts who underwent the research-driven curriculum showed a significant improvement in their final research proposal defense scores, increasing from 82.706 ± 2.257 (pre-reform) to 86.800 ± 2.243 (post-reform)"

### Xing Wenjing 2026 (2)

Research topic–driven curriculum content reform in graduate immunology to strengthen students' research and innovation competence. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08711-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

PhD subgroup of the same proposal defense comparison (Fig. 3D: n = 4 pre-reform, n = 10 post-reform, t = 1.766, p = 0.23). The article reports the change "did not reach statistical significance"; no effect size is printed.

> "although PhD students' scores increased from 86.750 ± 2.754 (pre-reform) to 88.990 ± 3.057 (post-reform), this change did not reach statistical significance"
