Evidence¶

Pang 2016¶

Pang, P. T., Nagappan, G., Guo, W. & Lu, B. (2016). Extracellular and intracellular cleavages of proBDNF required at two distinct stages of late-phase LTP. npj Science of Learning 1, 16003. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.3

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Electrophysiological rescue experiment applying tPA to tPA−/− hippocampal slices at the two stages; stage II application left fEPSP at 113.4 ± 12.0%, P = 0.840, versus stage I rescue to 150.3 ± 9.0%.

"Perfusion of tPA (500 ng/ml) at stage I (from 30 min before to 30 min after l-TBS) completely rescued L-LTP (Figure 2a, tPA − / − , 110.4 ± 6.8%; tPA − / − +tPA, 150.3 ± 9.0%, P o0.05), whereas application of tPA at stage II (30 –90 min after l-TBS) failed to restore L-LTP in tPA − / − slices"

Pang 2016 (2)¶

Pang, P. T., Nagappan, G., Guo, W. & Lu, B. (2016). Extracellular and intracellular cleavages of proBDNF required at two distinct stages of late-phase LTP. npj Science of Learning 1, 16003. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.3

q2 · i? · causal · r1

BDNF perfusion at stage II left fEPSP at 109.8 ± 9.6%, P = 0.960. Similar experiments in plasminogen−/− mice showed BDNF rescued at stage I but not stage II (data not shown).

"Application of BDNF (200 ng/ml) to the tPA − / − slices at stage I completely reversed the L-LTP de ﬁcit (Figure 2c, tPA − / − , 110.4 ± 6.8%; tPA − / − +BDNF, 154.0 ± 10.0%, P o0.005). In contrast, application of BDNF at stage II had no effect"

Related Claims¶

  • BDNF is required at two temporally distinct stages of L-LTP, with neither stage alone sufficient for rescue — related
  • BDNF signals through MAP kinase at stage I, whereas all three TrkB pathways (MAPK, PLC-gamma, PI3K) are required at stage II — related
  • Extracellular cleavage of proBDNF (blocked by PAI-1) is required at stage I, and intracellular cleavage by furin/PC1 (blocked by FIN II) at stage II, of L-LTP — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Activity induces surface proBDNF shortly after tetanus and surface mBDNF 3 h later, and blocking intracellular but not extracellular cleavage prevents mBDNF appearance at stage II — related
  • BDNF sustains protein synthesis-dependent L-LTP at stage II but not stage I, converting E-LTP to L-LTP only when applied after tetanus
