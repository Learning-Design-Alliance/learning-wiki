## Evidence

### Mayrhofer A 2016

Mayrhofer A, Goodman C, Smeeton N, Handley M, Amador S, Davies S. (2016). The feasibility of a train-the-trainer approach to end of life care training in care homes: an evaluation. BMC Palliative Care 15:11. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-016-0081-z

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Trainer interview data on learner readiness: some less experienced staff found it difficult to watch emotionally challenging content about death and dying alone and preferred group work with immediate debriefing. A trainer is quoted that "some emotional issues were dealt with during training".

> "…some emotional issues were dealt with during training (in relation to talking about death); this was an opportunity to discuss how they could/would best support each other…"

### Mayrhofer A 2016 (2)

Mayrhofer A, Goodman C, Smeeton N, Handley M, Amador S, Davies S. (2016). The feasibility of a train-the-trainer approach to end of life care training in care homes: an evaluation. BMC Palliative Care 15:11. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-016-0081-z

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interview and focus group findings on the continuing relationship between trainer, learner and educator/facilitator until newly trained staff reach confidence in administering EoL care. The article reports post-training support was seen as "key to its success".

> "Both trainers and EFEs stated that post training support was one of the most important aspects of the Train the Trainer model and key to its success."
