## Evidence

### Giacomelli G 2019

Giacomelli G, Ferré F, Furlan M, Nuti S. (2019). Involving hybrid professionals in top management decision-making: How managerial training can make the difference. Health Services Management Research, 32(4), 168–179. https://doi.org/10.1177/0951484819844778

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same structural equation model of 3084 clinical directors, the article reports a direct MT→CDI estimate of 4.03 with a 95% CI of [2.0080 – 6.0518], and states that the direct effect "remains significant after adding the mediators". No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The direct effect of the independent (MT) on the dependent variable (CDI) confirms H3, and the fact that it remains significant after adding the mediators enabled us to identify a partial mediation effect."
