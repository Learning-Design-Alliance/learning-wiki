### Sarli Leopoldo 2022

Sarli Leopoldo, Artioli Giovanna, Bogotto Sonia, Labelli Elsa, Pittella Francesco, Guasconi Massimo, De Simone Rosangela, De Luca Enrico, Rossi Sandra, D'Apice Clelia. (2022). From classroom training to e-learning: a journey through the quality of learning life of nurse students in post-graduate education - A longitudinal qualitative study. Acta Bio Medica : Atenei Parmensis. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9534212/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Fourth theme of the longitudinal qualitative interview study, "Factors favoring a new quality of educational life". Students reported it was easier to fit lectures and work without travelling to Parma, and at T1 some argued e-learning should not be abandoned after the pandemic.

> "This made it possible to improve didactic continuity (T0 cod. 13.6), considering it advantageous to be able, to reread the e-presentations and review the recorded video contents (T0 cod. 1.11)."

### Sarli Leopoldo 2022 (2)

Sarli Leopoldo, Artioli Giovanna, Bogotto Sonia, Labelli Elsa, Pittella Francesco, Guasconi Massimo, De Simone Rosangela, De Luca Enrico, Rossi Sandra, D'Apice Clelia. (2022). From classroom training to e-learning: a journey through the quality of learning life of nurse students in post-graduate education - A longitudinal qualitative study. Acta Bio Medica : Atenei Parmensis. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9534212/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Second sub-theme of the fourth theme in the same qualitative study. At T1 this perception evolved to include reduced stress related to traffic and car use, saving money and time, and being able to listen to lectures anywhere using technology.

> "even at T0, it was noted that for students online teaching has made possible to reduce the expenses related to the attendance of the course of study in the presence (being away from home) (T0 cod. 2.3), as well as saving time when traveling."
