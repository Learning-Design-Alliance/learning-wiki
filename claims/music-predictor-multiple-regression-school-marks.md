# Musical training remains a significant predictor of overall marks in multiple regression with income and grade, which have higher partial correlations

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Grade (partial correlation -.466), parent's income (.395) and music (.339) each significantly predicted overall average marks; music retained significance (t=3.950, p<.001) with other predictors held constant. [→ Olive Emil Wetter 2009](#olive-emil-wetter-2009)
`q2 i?` After excluding non-significant gender and handicraft, the three significant predictors accounted for 43% of the variance of overall average marks. [→ Olive Emil Wetter 2009](#olive-emil-wetter-2009)

## Evidence

### Olive Emil Wetter 2009
