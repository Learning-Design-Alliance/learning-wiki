Powell 2007¶

Powell, S. R., & Seethaler, P. M. (2007). Grade One: Math Computation (Math Case Study #1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED502453

q1 · i? · design · r1

The case study describes three goal-setting procedures: end-of-year benchmarks (e.g., 20 digits for grade 1 computation), an intra-individual framework in which baseline slope from at least eight CBM points is multiplied by 1.5 and projected forward, and national slope norms (grade 1 computation slope .35).

"There are three options for setting IEP goals. The first option is end-of-year benchmarking. For typically developing students at the grade level where the student is being monitored, identify the end-of-year CBM benchmark."
