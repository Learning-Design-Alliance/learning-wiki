### Bansbach Joachim 2024

Self-assessment and learning motivation in emergency point-of-care ultrasound: an online pilot investigation in German physicians. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-024-01154-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Dependent-samples t-test comparing one's own ratings with colleague ratings in the multicenter survey; the article reports "t (85) = 1.93, p = 0.05" and the rejection of hypothesis 1a. No effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "we found no overplacement effects towards their group (for one's competencies: mean (M) = 3.52, standard deviation (SD) = 0.56, and for other physicians' competencies: M = 3.40, SD = 0.62, t (85) = 1.93, p = 0.05). Therefore, hypothesis 1a, the presence of overplacement, was rejected."
