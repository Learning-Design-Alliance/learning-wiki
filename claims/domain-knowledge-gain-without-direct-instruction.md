## Evidence

### Eva Erdosne Toth 2000

Eva Erdosne Toth, David Klahr, and Zhe Chen. (2000). Bridging Research and Practice: A Cognitively Based Classroom Intervention for Teaching Experimentation Skills to Elementary School Children. Cognition and Instruction, 18(4), 423–459. https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci1804_1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post forced-choice domain knowledge test in the classroom study (which variable level makes the ball roll farther). The article reports 79% perfect scores before and 100% after instruction, t(71) = 4.3, p < .0001, with no direct instruction on the ramps variables.

> "Whereas 79% of the students provided correct answers to all three questions on the domain knowledge test prior to CVS instruction, 100% of the students got the maximum score after instruction,t(71) = 4.3, p < .0001."
