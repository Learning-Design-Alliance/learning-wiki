`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Annual questionnaire survey of programme participants (students n = 150, teachers n = 42 from 2016 onwards) rating overall happiness on a 5-point Likert scale. The article reports 91 ± 3% of students and 95 ± 5% "rated their experience as positive (scores of 4–5)"; teacher mean was 4.50 ± 0.09 vs student 4.17 ± 0.05.
