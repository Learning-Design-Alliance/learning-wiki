Evidence¶

Gilbert 2023¶

Gilbert, S. J., Boldt, A., Sachdeva, C., Scarampi, C., & Tsai, P.-C. (2023). Outsourcing Memory to External Tools: A Review of 'Intention Offloading'. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-022-02139-4

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Tsai et al. (2022), which used the optimal reminders paradigm with older and younger adults; older adults' higher reminder use reflected greater need, not greater preference. No effect size is printed.

"Results showed that even though older adults set numerically more remind- ers, they were in fact less biased towards external reminders than younger adults."

Gilbert 2023 (2)¶

Gilbert, S. J., Boldt, A., Sachdeva, C., Scarampi, C., & Tsai, P.-C. (2023). Outsourcing Memory to External Tools: A Review of 'Intention Offloading'. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-022-02139-4

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Scarampi and Gilbert (2021), who administered the intention offloading task to younger (18–30 years) and older (65–84 years) participants; older adults were overconfident and did not fully compensate. No effect size is printed.

"older adults still performed significantly worse on the task, despite the option to set reminders as a compensatory strategy"
