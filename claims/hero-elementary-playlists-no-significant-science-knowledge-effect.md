# The Hero Elementary playlist intervention showed no statistically significant effect on K-2 students' science knowledge (effect size 0.10, p ~ 0.38)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who received the playlist intervention did not significantly exceed comparison peers on ScienceQuest post-test scores (adjusted difference 0.41, effect size 0.102, CI [-0.51, 1.33]). [→ Li 2019](#li-2019)

## Evidence

### Li 2019

Li, L., Maher, B., Weiser, G., Orenstein, A., Chow, E., & Atienza, S. (2019). Hero Elementary playlist pilot study in after-school programs. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED608115

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pilot with 161 K-2 students in four after-school programs; multivariate regression with pre-tests as covariates. The intervention exceeded comparison peers with a "point estimate of 0.41" and effect size 0.102, not significant at alpha 0.05 (p ~ 0.38).

> "Adjusted mean differences on the post-test measure of ScienceQuest showed that students who received the intervention exceeded their comparison peers in total score (point estimate of 0.41) which corresponded to an effect size of 0.102 but which had a confidence interval of [-0.51, 1.33]."

### Li 2019 (2)

Li, L., Maher, B., Weiser, G., Orenstein, A., Chow, E., & Atienza, S. (2019). Hero Elementary playlist pilot study in after-school programs. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED608115

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline imbalance study note: the treatment group scored higher on ScienceQuest at pre-test (means 18.64 vs 16.62, SMD 0.48), which the regression model was used to adjust for.

> "However, the treatment group had higher pre-test scores on ScienceQuest (Table 6)."
