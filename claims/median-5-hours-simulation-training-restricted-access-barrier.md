Descriptive survey result: "respondents reported a median of 5 h (interquartile range, IQR 0–20, range 0–80) of simulation training on a robotic console." Restricted access to robotic consoles for simulation was also rated a significant barrier.

> "Overall, respondents reported a median of 5 h (interquartile range, IQR 0–20, range 0–80) of simulation training on a robotic console, whilst 48% (39/82) had attended a formal course in robotic surgery."
