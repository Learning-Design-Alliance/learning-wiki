# LDA topic modelling of 6,087 written student feedback responses yields six SET-related topics

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Applying LDA topic modelling to 6,087 written student feedback responses from a Finnish university produced a six-topic model covering themes such as good content, dissatisfaction, workload and good teaching methods. [→ Hujala 2020](#hujala-2020)

## Evidence

### Hujala 2020

Hujala, M., Knutas, A., Hynninen, T., & Arminen, H. (2020). Improving the quality of teaching by utilising written student feedback: A streamlined process. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103965

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of written student feedback collected at a Finnish university during the 2016-17 and 2017-18 academic years, restricted to responses written in Finnish, yielding a sample of 6,087 responses. The number of topics was selected by examining "local maximums in the semantic coherence results".

> "After examining the local maximums in the semantic coherence results, we proceeded with a topic model with six topics."
