`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Principal component analysis (Varimax rotation) of the four predictor measures in the pooled European sample yielded two components: "phoneme deletion and digit span loading on the first component" (phonology factor) and "the two RAN measures loading on the second component" (RAN factor).

> "The results showed again a two component solution with phoneme deletion and digit span loading on the first component (phonology factor: eigenvalue = 1.1 3 ; factor loadings = .8 1 and .77, respectively), and the two RAN measures loading on the second component (RAN factor: eigenvalue = 1.6 4 ; factor loadings =.8 7 and .83, respectively)."
