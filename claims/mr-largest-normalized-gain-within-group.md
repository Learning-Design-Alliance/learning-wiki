## Evidence

### Sharmin Akter 2026

Sharmin Akter, Mohammad Abu Nasir Rakib, Eshwara Prasad Sridhar, Somik Biswas, Mahmudur Rahman, and Md Rassel Raihan. (2026). Post-Lecture Interactive Environments for Conceptual Learning: A Randomized Comparison of Mixed Reality and Tangible Instruction in Undergraduate STEM Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.03896](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.03896)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive normalized-gain analysis in the pilot randomized study, reported as supporting analysis because gain is sensitive to baseline scores. The ET group's higher baseline left less room to improve within the normalized-gain formula.

> "Mixed Reality showed the largest normalized gain (M = 0.57, SD = 0.31), indicating strong conceptual improvement. Engineering Toolkit showed a smaller positive gain (M = .16, SD = .82), while the Traditional condition showed no average gain (M = -.11, SD = .50)."

### Sharmin Akter 2026 (2)

Sharmin Akter, Mohammad Abu Nasir Rakib, Eshwara Prasad Sridhar, Somik Biswas, Mahmudur Rahman, and Md Rassel Raihan. (2026). Post-Lecture Interactive Environments for Conceptual Learning: A Randomized Comparison of Mixed Reality and Tangible Instruction in Undergraduate STEM Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.03896](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.03896)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary within-group paired t-tests with Wilcoxon signed-rank checks in the same pilot study. The Traditional group (t(7) = -0.60, p = .580) and ET group (t(7) = 1.30, p = .230) did not show reliable improvement.

> "Only the MR group showed reliable improvement from baseline to post-test, t(7) = 4.70, p = .002, with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test supporting the same result, p = .016."
