### Grant Shannan M. 2011

Grant Shannan M., Wolever Thomas M. S. (2011). Perceived Barriers to Application of Glycaemic Index: Valid Concerns or Lost in Translation? Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu3030330

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Grant et al. (2011), a study using low-GI education as a supplement to standard care in women with gestational diabetes mellitus and impaired glucose tolerance, finding energy and macronutrient intake matched to controls. It also cites Frost et al. (1994) finding participants on the low-GI diet consumed less dietary fat and more fibre.

> "Addition of low-GI education did not result in divergence from current dietary recommendations in this group; energy and macronutrient intake was matched to controls post intervention."
