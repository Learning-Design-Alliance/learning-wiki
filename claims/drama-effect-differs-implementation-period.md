## Evidence

### Batdi 2020

Batdi, V. & Elaldi, S. (2020). Effects of drama method on social communication skills: A comparative analysis. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 6(3), 435-457. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1258528

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Mixed-effect moderator analysis of the 24 studies grouped into five implementation-period categories (Table 5). The homogeneity hypothesis was rejected (Q = 13.831 exceeded the critical value of 9.488 with 4 degrees of freedom), indicating "a significant difference amongst the inter groups" (Z=7.784, p=0.008).

> "While the “not specified” group had the lowest effect size ( g=0.252), the “2-4 week” period had the highest one (g= 1.193)."
