This is a conceptual assertion by the authors in a commentary, reported without supporting data or citations. It responds to a concern that discussing effects of constructivist learning environments implies such environments are superior to traditional ones.

> "We acknowledge that learning in lec- tures can be a constructive process, leading to meaningful learning. We do not claim that meaningful learning exclusively occurs in a learning environment that is designed explicitly based on constructivist theories of learning."
