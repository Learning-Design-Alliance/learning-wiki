Evidence¶

van Gog 2015¶

van Gog, T., & Sweller, J. (2015). Not New, but Nearly Forgotten: the Testing Effect Decreases or even Disappears as the Complexity of Learning Materials Increases. Educational Psychology Review, 27, 247–264. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-015-9310-x

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review narrates Tran et al.'s experiments in which students studied premise scenarios, then restudied or practiced retrieval, before multiple-choice transfer questions requiring deductive inferences: "they found no evidence that having engaged in retrieval practice improved the ability to make inferences", though premises showed a testing effect.

"they found no evidence that having engaged in retrieval practice improved the ability to make inferences, although their fourth experiment did show a testing effect with regard to the premises"
