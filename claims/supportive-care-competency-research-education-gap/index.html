Yoshimatsu Yuki 2023¶

Yoshimatsu Yuki, Ohtake Yoichi, Ukai Mamiko, Miyagami Taiju, Morikawa Toru, Shimamura Yoshinosuke, Kataoka Yuki, Hashimoto Tadayuki. (2023). “Diagnose, Treat, and SUPPORT”. Clinical competencies in the management of older adults with aspiration pneumonia: a scoping review. European Geriatric Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41999-023-00898-4

q1 · i? · review · r2

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion: although studies exist on individual supportive topics, "there are few studies conducted in the context of AP", particularly for underlying condition management, oral management, rehabilitation, multidisciplinary team, decision making, prevention and palliative care. This is the authors' stated research-agenda conclusion, not a tested result.

"There is also a need to determine how to train and assess healthcare professionals in these competencies."

Related Claims¶

  • A preliminary literature phase defined aspiration pneumonia as pneumonia occurring in the context of aspiration or dysphagia — related
  • A scoping review of 99 studies identified 12 clinical competencies for managing older adults with aspiration pneumonia — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Discussion of the 12 competencies in the literature is uneven: five competencies appear in 15 or more studies while seven supportive-care competencies appear in only a few — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Scoping review data extraction renamed three competencies and added prevention, prognosis, and palliative care to the preliminary Delphi list — related
