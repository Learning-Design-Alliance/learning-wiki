## Evidence

### Linares M 2026

Linares M, Grimaldi C, Palma N, Vintimilla B, Candal S, Estrella D, Saps M. (2026). Knowledge Does Not Translate Into Diagnostic Restraint: Application of Rome‐Based IBS Diagnosis Among Medical Students in Latin America. Neurogastroenterology and Motility. https://doi.org/10.1111/nmo.70335

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional vignette survey of 238 clinical-year medical students from 45 Latin American universities. In the adult Rome IV–consistent IBS vignette, "167 (70.2%) ordered diagnostic testing" despite most rating the case as likely functional; selected tests included blood analyses, ultrasound, CT, and colonoscopy.

> "At the initial stage, 180 students (75.6%) rated the presentation as likely functional. Despite this, 167 (70.2%) ordered diagnostic testing."
