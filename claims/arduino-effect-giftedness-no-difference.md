## Subclaims
`q3 i?` ANOVA found no statistically significant difference between gifted class (0.785) and general class (0.613) effect sizes (p > .05). [→ Lee 2020](#lee-2020)

## Evidence

### Lee 2020

Lee, E. (2020). A meta-analysis of the effects of Arduino-based education in Korean primary and secondary schools in engineering education. European Journal of Educational Research, 9(4), 1503-1512. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.9.4.1503

`q3 · i?`

Meta-ANOVA on giftedness across the 31 effect sizes. Both subgroups showed medium effect sizes; the article reports the difference was not statistically significant.

> "The effect sizes were 0.785 (medium) for gifted class and 0.613 (medium) for general class. From the ANOVA, the significance level was p > .05, and the effect sizes based on giftedness were not statistically different."
