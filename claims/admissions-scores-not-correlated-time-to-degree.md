## Evidence

### Walters T 2022

Walters T, Abeyta A, Bean AJ, Wilson MA. (2022). The impact of holistic review on correlations between doctoral student outcomes, and GPA and GRE scores in the biomedical sciences. PLOS ONE 17(12): e0279258. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0279258

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective Pearson correlation analysis of 286 doctoral records (2007–2012) at GSBS plotted normalized admissions scores against time to degree; the article reports "no significant correlation" for all students and each demographic subgroup (all-students r = -0.05634, p = 0.3373).

> "We observed no significant correlation between admissions committee scores and TTD among all students in our analysis (Fig 1A). There was also no significant correlation when analyzed by gender (Fig 1B), race/ethnicity (Fig 1C), or citizenship status (Fig 1D)."
