## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The interaction term of training regimen and fluid intelligence accounted for a significant amount of variance in Space Fortress training gain (ΔR2 = .046, p = .034). [→ Lee Hyunkyu 2015](#lee-hyunkyu-2015)

## Evidence

### Lee Hyunkyu 2015

Lee Hyunkyu, Boot Walter R., Baniqued Pauline L., Voss Michelle W., Prakash Ruchika Shaurya, Basak Chandramallika, Kramer Arthur F. (2015). The Relationship between Intelligence and Training Gains Is Moderated by Training Strategy. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0123259

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression on the full sample of 50 participants, with regimen, fluid intelligence, baseline performance and gender as predictors (R2 = .533), then the centered interaction term added. The article reports "ΔR2 = .046, ΔF (1, 44) = 4.76, b = -43.98, t(44) = -2.184, p = .034".

> "The interaction term was added to the regression model and accounted for a significant amount of variance in training gain, ΔR2 = .046, ΔF (1, 44) = 4.76, b = -43.98, t(44) = -2.184, p = .034."
