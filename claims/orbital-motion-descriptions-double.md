### Corey McInerney and Phil Sutton 2023

Corey McInerney and Phil Sutton. (2023). Einsteinian gravitational concepts throughout secondary school. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad001a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad001a)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of open-ended Q4 responses on how objects move in gravitational fields. The authors link the shift to the spacetime simulator demonstration and note added post-only terms such as 'oval' and 'spirals'.

> "Positively, phrases such as 'they fall ', 'towards ground/source' and ' pushed/pulled' decrease post-intervention, whereas the number of participants describing orbital motion doubles."
