Duane E. Wallace & Alec M. Bodzin 2017¶

Duane E. Wallace & Alec M. Bodzin. (2017). Developing Scientific Citizenship Identity Using Mobile Learning and Authentic Practice. Electronic Journal of Science Education, 21(6). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1187996

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Two-way mixed ANOVA on the Ideas about Careers subscale (8 items) in the quasi-experiment with 39 students per group. The printed effect size for the interaction is partial eta 2 = 0.063 (η² = 0.063), a medium-small effect; Table 3 lists a subscale effect size of 1.80.
