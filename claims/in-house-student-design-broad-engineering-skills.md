`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive account from the paper's Design Philosophy section of the student-led PULSE-A CubeSat program. The authors report that systems are "fully designed, integrated, and tested by students," enabling practice across engineering disciplines; no outcome measures are reported.

> "All of the mission's systems and the vast majority of subsystems (the Payload, OGS, RFGS, and most Bus subsystems) are fully designed, integrated, and tested by students. This has allowed PULSE-A's members to practice skills across a wide range of engineering disciplines."

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Optical System section of which skills the Payload and OGS design challenges demand of undergraduates, naming "optical simulations, FPGA engineering, and closed-loop feedback sequences"; no assessment data are reported.

> "These challenges force our team members to adapt by learning new skills which they would otherwise not encounter in their time as undergraduate students, including optical simulations, FPGA engineering, and closed-loop feedback sequences for modulation and tracking."
