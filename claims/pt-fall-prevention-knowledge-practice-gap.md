Assessing physical therapists' knowledge in fall prevention among older adults in Saudi arabia: a cross-sectional study. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-025-06668-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive cross-sectional survey of 384 licensed physical therapists in Saudi Arabia using a validated online questionnaire. The article reports a "substantial knowledge-practice gap": median knowledge 63% versus 14.8% consistent tool use and 30.5% rare or no use.

> "The study revealed a substantial knowledge-practice gap. While participants demonstrated acceptable knowledge (median 63%), only 14.8% consistently used standardized assessment tools, with 30.5% rarely or never employing them."
