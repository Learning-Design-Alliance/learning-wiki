### Rosemary Luckin & Benedict du Boulay 2016

Rosemary Luckin & Benedict du Boulay. (2016). Reflections on the Ecolab and the Zone of Proximal Development. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0072-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Empirical evaluation study with learners aged between 9 and 11 years using moEcolab (mastery goal orientation) and poEcolab (performance goal orientation) versions providing self-referenced or norm-referenced assessment respectively.

> "An empirical evaluation study with learners aged between 9 and 11 years found evidence that performance goal oriented feedback provided by the system affected the way children interacted and learned from the system, in particular there was a trend in the data that suggested that correctly matching the goal orientation of the child to the goal orientation provided by the system improved learning gains."
