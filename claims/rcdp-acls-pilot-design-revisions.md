## Evidence

### Blanchard E 2022

Blanchard E, Booker D, Peterson DT, Carter T. (2022). Rapid Cycle Deliberate Practice: Application to Adult Advanced Life Support. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11269

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot phase of the curriculum development involving the same level, type, and number of learners as later offerings. The authors report that "we incorporated a post-RCDP debriefing at the end of each case in subsequent sessions" in response, and also adjusted case order. No quantitative pilot data were reported.

> "A discovery during this pilot was that learners wanted an opportunity to briefly discuss their reactions and synthesize findings, as well as to ask any remaining questions. As a result, we incorporated a post-RCDP debriefing at the end of each case in subsequent sessions."
