Evidence¶

Mendonça PC 2026¶

Mendonça PC, Quintal F, Figueiredo M and Mendonça F (2026) Perceived usefulness and intention to use large language model-generated feedback across three educational levels: a user-centred study in programming. Front. Educ. 11:1934069. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1934069

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Standardised OLS regression on report-level ratings (n = 140) with Johnson's relative weights; the model accounted for 53% of variance [R2 = 0.53, F(5, 134) = 30.46, p < 0.001]. Motivation accounted for 31.9% and personalisation for 23.2% of explained variance; "The standardised coefficients of Motivation [β = 0.38, 95% CI (0.14, 0.57)] and Personalisation [β = 0.28, 95% CI (0.06, 0.59)] had bootstrap CIs that excluded zero".
