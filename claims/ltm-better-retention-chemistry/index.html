The Effect of Laboratory Training Model of Teaching and Traditional Method on Knowledge, Comprehension, Application, Skills-Components of Achievement, Total Achievement and Retention Level in Chemistry. i-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology, Vol. 7, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102743

Delayed post-test administered after a one-month gap to both groups; independent sample t-test (Table 8) prints means of 56.2958 (Laboratory) vs 27.2583 (Traditional), n = 60 per group. No effect size printed.
