## Evidence

### Kate M. Xu 2024

Kate M. Xu, Sarah Coertjens, Florence Lespiau, Kim Ouwehand, Hanke Korpershoek, Fred Paas, David C. Geary. (2024). An Evolutionary Approach to Motivation and Learning: Differentiating Biologically Primary and Secondary Knowledge. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09880-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-tests on nine-point cognitive-load ratings (Paas, 1992) after each list in the same experiment. Participants "experienced less difficulty" (d = -0.96) and "less effort" (d = -0.78) for primary words, both p < 0.001.

> "experienced less difficulty (t = -11.48, p < 0.001, d = -0.96; M = 4.89, SD = 1.41 for primary knowledge words; M = 5.65, SD = 1.49 for secondary knowledge words) and less effort (t = -9.35, p < 0.001, d = -0.78"
