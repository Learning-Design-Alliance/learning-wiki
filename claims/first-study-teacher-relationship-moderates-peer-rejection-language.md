# The study is the first to examine whether the teacher-child relationship moderates the link between peer rejection and language competence

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' statement of the study's novelty in the introduction, noting that prior moderation research focused on peer rejection and social-emotional outcomes instead of academic outcomes such as language competence. This is an author assertion, not a tested result.
