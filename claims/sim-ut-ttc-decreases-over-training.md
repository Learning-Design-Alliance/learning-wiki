## Evidence

### Freundt Paula 2023

Freundt Paula, Nourkami-Tutdibi Nasenien, Tutdibi Erol, Janzing Paul, von Ostrowski Thomas, Langer Martin, Zemlin Michael, Steinhard Johannes. (2023). Controlled Prospective Study on the Use of Systematic Simulator-Based Training with a Virtual, Moving Fetus for Learning Second-Trimester Scan: FESIM III. Ultraschall in der Medizin. https://doi.org/10.1055/a-1984-8320

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated standardized tests at 2, 4, and 6 weeks in the prospective trial, analyzed with Friedman and Wilcoxon tests, showed the trial group "recorded a significant decrease in TTC between each test".

> "The score of 1238±283 seconds in the first test after two weeks of SIM-UT decreased to 620±189 seconds (average time per plane 27 seconds) after six weeks of training (p overall <0.001)."
