`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reflexive thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with seven experimental-group students (69 codes, 262 coded references) found the score triggered concrete action, yet all seven were coded under Selective Inspection, giving sub-scores only "a glanced" look, and causal analysis was rare (Unsystematic Reflection).

> "Within Execution and Monitoring, 18 references from six of the seven students described the score as the trigger for concrete action"

### Chen C and Liu T 2026 (2)

Chen C and Liu T (2026) Impact of automated scoring on interpreting performance and self-regulated learning: evidence from a pedagogical experiment. Front. Psychol. 17:1867463. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1867463

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-learning Planning was the least coded dimension, with competing coursework and unstable routines cited. Emotionally, "Anxiety moved in both directions": two students felt more exposed while three felt less pressure than under teacher-collected submission, and three deferred engagement after a low score.

> "Where goals were set, they were derived from the previous session's score rather than from analysis of the upcoming task (S3), were sometimes equated with the score itself (S2), or were deliberately kept low (S3)."
