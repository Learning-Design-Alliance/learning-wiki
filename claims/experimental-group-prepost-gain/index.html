Seval Deniz KILIÇ & Hüseyin ALKAN 2018¶

Seval Deniz KILIÇ & Hüseyin ALKAN. (2018). The Effects of Activities and Approachments Intended Performance Improvement on the Students' Performances in Mathematics. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n5p146

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Paired comparison of the experimental group's PÖÖ1 and PÖÖ2 means (3.72 vs 6.31, n = 29) using a t-test: t = 5.075, p = 0.000. The authors argue the methods and approaches used in the application produced the difference.

"That is, there is a significant difference between the pretest and posttest mean scores of the experimental group students in favor of the posttest mean score. Thus, it can be argued that the methods and approaches used in the application led to a significant difference."
