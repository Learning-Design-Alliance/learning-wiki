## Evidence

### Heskin L. 2021

Heskin L., Simms C., Traynor O., Galvin R. (2021). Designing a synthetic simulator to teach open surgical skills for limb exploration in trauma: a qualitative study exploring the experiences and perspectives of educators and surgical trainees. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01417-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 11 educators and 11 trainees experienced in surgical simulation; Theme 1 reports that participants viewed simulation as "integral, relevant, intuitive" and supported deliberate practice and warmup.

> "There was an overwhelmingly positive response to the use of simulation among all participants, some regarding it as integral, relevant, intuitive and that it should be mandatory."
