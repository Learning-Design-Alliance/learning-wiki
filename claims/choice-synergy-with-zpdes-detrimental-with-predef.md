# Adding learner choice strengthens learning under ZPDES but is detrimental to learning under the predefined linear curriculum

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The three-way algo x choice x pre/post interaction is significant: choice increases the learning benefit of ZPDES but produces the opposite (detrimental) effect for the Predefined condition. [→ Clément 2024](#clement-2024)

## Evidence

### Clément 2024

Clément, B., Sauzéon, H., Roy, D., & Oudeyer, P.-Y. (2024). Improved Performances and Motivation in Intelligent Tutoring Systems: Combining Machine Learning and Learner Choice. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.01669](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.01669)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Three-way mixed ANOVA in the 265-child RCT reports the significant three-way interaction; pairwise comparisons show PCO vs ZCO post-test differences significant by LSD (p − value = 0.014) and only marginal by Bonferroni (p − value = 0.08).

> "the three-way interaction is significant (algo x choice x pre/post effect [F (1, 261) = 17.319, p − value = 0.049, η2 = 0.015]), the learning benefit from ZPDES condition is increased by the choice condition, whereas we observe the opposite for Predefined condition"
