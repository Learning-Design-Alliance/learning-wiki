`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome from the randomized trial's debrief survey on a 5-point Likert scale; the article reports "average perceived improvement scores of 4.31 (SOPHIE) vs. 3.83 (control)" with p <0.01. No standardized effect size is printed.
