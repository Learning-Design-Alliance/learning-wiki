# Novice surgeons trained with the VR simulation-based curriculum committed fewer total errors across their first 20 tunnel-construction procedures than conventionally trained surgeons, though the group effect was not statistically significant for total errors

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a multicenter RCT, the adjusted mean total number of errors over the first 20 procedures was 9.25 in the simulation group versus 17.56 in the control group, a 47% reduction, with the group effect not statistically significant (P = 0.05). [→ Nair Akshay Gopinathan et al 2021](#nair-akshay-gopinathan-et-al-2021)

## Evidence

### Nair Akshay Gopinathan et al 2021

Nair Akshay Gopinathan et al. (2021). Effectiveness of simulation-based training for manual small incision cataract surgery among novice surgeons: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-90410-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multicenter investigator-masked RCT; 10 trainees in the simulation group and 9 in the control group were analyzed by intention to treat, with outcomes ascertained by masked video review of the first 20 procedures. Adjusted estimates show "the total number of errors was 17.56 (95% CI 6.63–28.49) in the control group and 9.25 (95% CI 0–18.95) in the simulation group"; the article reports a 47% reduction but P = 0.05 was not statistically significant.
