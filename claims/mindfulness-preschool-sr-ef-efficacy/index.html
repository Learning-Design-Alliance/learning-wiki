Evidence¶

Rosalyn A. Muir · Steven J. Howard · Lisa Kervin 2023¶

Rosalyn A. Muir · Steven J. Howard · Lisa Kervin. (2023). Interventions and Approaches Targeting Early Self-Regulation or Executive Functioning in Preschools: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09740-6

q3 · i? · review · r3

Synthesis of 12 mindfulness intervention studies in the review (all conducted since 2015; median dose 10 h; mostly teacher-led with a mean of 16 h training). The review reports "10 (83.3%) of the studies" had significant outcomes and 17 of 26 evaluated outcomes (65.4%) were significant; four interventions were classified as high efficacy.

"Significant outcomes were reported in 10 (83.3%) of the studies. Across the interventions, 26 outcomes were evaluated, of which 17 (65.4%) were significant."
