## Evidence

### Burn H 2020

Burn H, Puri L, Roshan A, Singh SK, Burton MJ. (2020). Primary Eye Care in Eastern Nepal. Ophthalmic Epidemiology 27(3):165-176. https://doi.org/10.1080/09286586.2019.1702217

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative component: three focus group discussions (five PHCWs each) and semi-structured interviews with seven ophthalmic assistants and one district health manager, thematically analysed with triangulation. Participants stated "all PHCWs stated they would be willing to receive training and that they had the time to take on this additional role".

> "The OAs, PHCWs and district health manager all agree that PEC training is required in order to reduce the burden of blindness in their communities and all PHCWs stated they would be willing to receive training and that they had the time to take on this additional role"
