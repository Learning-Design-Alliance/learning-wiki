Evidence¶

Saunders 2024¶

Saunders, R., Fitz, J., DiNapoli, M. A., Jr., & Kini, T. (2024). Teacher residencies: State and federal policy to support comprehensive teacher preparation. Learning Policy Institute & EdPrepLab. https://doi.org/10.54300/358.825

q2 · i? · review · r2

A 2015 study of the Urban Teacher Residency partnership between New Visions for Public Schools, Hunter College, and the NYC Department of Education found "students of UTR residents and graduates outperformed a matched group of peers" on 16 of 22 Regents exam comparisons; a 2018 supplemental study confirmed the tendency in 8 of 9 comparisons.

"students of UTR residents and graduates outperformed a matched group of peers (controlling for students’ prior achievement, gender, special education status, and English learner status) taught by other novice teachers on 16 out of 22 (73%) comparisons of state Regents exam scores."

Saunders 2024 (2)¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

A 2012 study of Boston Teacher Residency graduates found first-year teachers as effective at raising ELA scores as other Boston Public School teachers with the same experience; "Although residency graduates were initially less effective in math, they improved more rapidly than other teachers," outperforming same-experience and veteran teachers by years four and five.

"Although residency graduates were initially less effective in math, they improved more rapidly than other teachers during their first 5 years in the classroom."
