## Evidence

### Benjamin D. Geller 2022

Benjamin D. Geller, Jack Rubien, Sara M. Hiebert, and Catherine H. Crouch. (2022). The impact of introductory physics for the life sciences in a senior biology capstone course. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Within the same capstone diffusion task data, the non-IPLS 1 group was subdivided into students with no college physics and students with non-IPLS college physics; the Wilcoxon comparison found no statistically significant difference, supporting the authors' statement that non-IPLS physics offered "minimal, if any, benefit to students". No effect size was printed.

> "We found no statistically significant difference in performance on these IPLS 1-specific elements of the code between these two groups (Wilcoxon p = 0.87, Fig. 1b)."
