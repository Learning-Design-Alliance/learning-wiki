q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Comparative analysis of five studies (four RCTs and one quasi‐experimental study) comparing e‐learning with face‐to‐face lectures or written handouts. The review reports "The direction of effect was mixed"; an SMD meta‐analysis was not feasible because only one study reported standard deviations.

"The direction of effect was mixed, with three studies reporting slightly higher gains in the e‐learning groups [27, 35, 41] while two marginally favored the control groups [8, 32]."
