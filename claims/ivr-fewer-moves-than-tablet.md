## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among children who solved the problem, those in the IVR condition used fewer moves than those using the tablet. [→ Araiza-Alba 2021](#araiza-alba-2021)

## Evidence

### Araiza-Alba 2021

Araiza-Alba, P., Keane, T., Chen, W. S., & Kaufman, J. (2021). Immersive virtual reality as a tool to learn problem-solving skills. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.104121

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis test on number of moves among successful solvers showed condition differences: "Children in the IVR condition used fewer moves to solve the game than the children using the tablet".

> "A Mann-Whitney test subsequently revealed a significant difference in the distribution of the number of moves between IVR and tablet conditions (U = 76.50, p =.001). Children in the IVR condition used fewer moves to solve the game than the children using the tablet"
