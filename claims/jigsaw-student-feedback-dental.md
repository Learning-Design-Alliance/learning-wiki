### Haider Ambreen Khurshid 2025

Comparative study of the effect of two small group discussion teaching methods (Tutorial vs Jigsaw) on academic motivation and achievement of undergraduate dental students – a preliminary study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07244-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-study session evaluation form (7 items, 5-point Likert) completed by participants: "most 75% of the students reported that the jigsaw method has enhanced their knowledge and understanding of the concepts taught".

> "In our study, most 75% of the students reported that the jigsaw method has enhanced their knowledge and understanding of the concepts taught."

### Haider Ambreen Khurshid 2025 (2)

Haider Ambreen Khurshid, Rehman Javeria, Ali Syeda Kauser. (2025). Comparative study of the effect of two small group discussion teaching methods (Tutorial vs Jigsaw) on academic motivation and achievement of undergraduate dental students – a preliminary study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07244-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same feedback form, jigsaw-group items on discussion conditions: participants reported adequate time "available for discussion" (70.83%) and being able to contribute effectively (62.5%); these are descriptive percentages, not effect sizes.

> "A large number of participants (70.83%) in the jigsaw group stated that they had adequate time available for discussion and 62.5% of the students in the jigsaw group reported being able to contribute effectively to the discussion."
