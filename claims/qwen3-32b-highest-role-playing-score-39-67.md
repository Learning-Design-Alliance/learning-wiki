`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Role-playing competency was assessed by 5 attending physicians using an 8-item scale across 3 dimensions on dental case dialogues. The "Qwen3-32B–based framework achieved a significantly higher total score (mean 39.67, SD 0.71)". Overall, a statistically significant difference in total role-playing competency scores among the 4 models was observed (χ²3=16.6; P<.001).

> "the Qwen3-32B–based framework achieved a significantly higher total score (mean 39.67, SD 0.71) compared to the GPT-4o–based framework (P=.008) and the single-LLM baseline (P=.04)"
