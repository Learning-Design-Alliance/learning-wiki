## Evidence

### Yang S‐Y 2021

Yang S‐Y. (2021). Effectiveness of neonatal emergency nursing education through simulation training: Flipped learning based on Tanner's Clinical Judgement Model. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.748

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent control group pre/post study with 65 South Korean senior nursing students (35 experimental, 30 control). Clinical judgement was rated with the Korean Lasater rubric by two expert observers; ANCOVA showed "statistically significant mean differences (F = 6.76, p < .001)". No effect size printed.

> "The clinical judgement ability score of the experimental group increased from pre‐intervention (20.12 SD 5.52) to postintervention (27.51 SD 3.98), while that of the control group decreased from pre‐intervention (19.86 SD 3.65) to postintervention (23.81 SD 3.12). ANCOVA using the prior clinical judgement ability score as a covariate revealed statistically significant mean differences (F = 6.76, p < .001) between the two groups."
