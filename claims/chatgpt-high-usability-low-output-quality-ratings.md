## Evidence

### Stefan Küchemann 2023

Stefan Küchemann, Steffen Steinert, Natalia Revenga, Matthias Schweinberger, Yavuz Dinc, Karina E. Avila, and Jochen Kuhn. (2023). Physics task development of prospective physics teachers using ChatGPT. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020128)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-task survey of intervention-group participants with ten System Usability Scale and six TAM2 items on 5-point Likert scales: "On average, participants rated system usability at 4.1", indicating agreement that the system is easy to use.

> "On average, participants rated system usability at 4.1, indicating agreement that the system is easy to use, functions are well-integrated, and they were conﬁdent in using it."

### Stefan Küchemann 2023 (2)

Stefan Küchemann, Steffen Steinert, Natalia Revenga, Matthias Schweinberger, Yavuz Dinc, Karina E. Avila, and Jochen Kuhn. (2023). Physics task development of prospective physics teachers using ChatGPT. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020128)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same survey, perceived usefulness for developing physics tasks was rated neutral, and "participants rated ChatGPT’s output quality as neutral but with the lowest average value of 2.76", indicating participants tended to have difficulties with the system's output.

> "Additionally, participants rated ChatGPT’s output quality as neutral but with the lowest average value of 2.76, indicating that participants tended to have diﬃculties with the system’s output."
