Cross-segment comparison from the audit Results section across 48 platforms, finding significant overall segment differences; K-12 platforms scored significantly higher on children's consent, the dimension governed by COPPA and FERPA, with no comparable advantage on AI disclosure or accountability.

"US K -12 platforms scored highest overall (M=7.47), followed by US higher education/adult learning (M=5.50) and India K -12/exam preparation (M=4.77); differences were significant (Kruskal -Wallis H=20.634, p<0.001)."
