Evidence¶

Dawid Ratajczyk 2026¶

Dawid Ratajczyk, Agata Dymarska, Adam Matłoka, Michał Tomczyk, Małgorzata Wiącek. (2026). Thinking With AI, Reasoning Without It: Cognitive Offloading to Generative AI Weakens Effort Regulation. Preprint. https://osf.io/59qtm/ doi:10.31234/osf.io/83jum_v1

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Stage 1 completion time in the preregistered lab experiment, analysed with a 2×2 ANOVA on fourth-root-transformed times. The quoted test shows incentives significantly increased completion time, η²p = .060; incentive-condition participants averaged 1262.17 s versus 1077.48 s without incentives.

"However, there was a significant main effect of incentives on completion time (𝐹(1,153) = 9.82,𝑝=.002,𝜂 2 𝑝 =.060)."
