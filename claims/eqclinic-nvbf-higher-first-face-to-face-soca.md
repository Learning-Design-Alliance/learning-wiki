# Medical students who completed an EQClinic teleconsultation and reviewed the automated nonverbal behavior feedback scored significantly higher on the first face-to-face SOCA than students who had not used the platform

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Group A students who reviewed the nonverbal behavior feedback before their first face-to-face consultation scored significantly higher on SOCA total score than group B students who had completed only course curriculum components (P=.04). [→ Liu C 2016](#liu-c-2016)

## Evidence

### Liu C 2016

Liu C, Lim RL, McCabe KL, Taylor S, Calvo RA. (2016). A Web-Based Telehealth Training Platform Incorporating Automated Nonverbal Behavior Feedback for Teaching Communication Skills to Medical Students: A Randomized Crossover Study. J Med Internet Res 18(9):e246. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6299

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized crossover trial of 268 second-year medical students allocated to staggered EQClinic exposure. In the first face-to-face consultation, group A + NVBF (mean 13.21) scored higher than group B (mean 12.58): "significantly higher on face-to-face SOCA total score in group A + NVBF (t58.25=2.13, P=.04)".

> "Mean SOCA scores were significantly higher on face-to-face SOCA total score in group A + NVBF (t58.25=2.13, P=.04) than in group B."
