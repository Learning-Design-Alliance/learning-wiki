## Evidence

### Ayush Gupta 2008

Ayush Gupta, David Hammer, Edward F. Redish. (2008). The Case for Dynamic Models of Learners' Ontologies in Physics. Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/hlns20

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The authors cite a plasma physics text (Chen, 1977) and parallel examples from nuclear theory (liquid drop versus nucleons) and quantum mechanics (light as waves or photons) to show experts explicitly choose among ontological models. They conclude physics concepts cannot be boxed in a single ontological category.

> "depending on the situation, physicists might conceptualize a plasma as made up of fluids, or of discrete particles, and in some cases as a combination of both."
