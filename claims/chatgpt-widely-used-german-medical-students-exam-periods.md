## Evidence

### Fußhöller Anna 2025

Fußhöller Anna, Lechner Fabian, Schlicker Nadine, Muehlensiepen Felix, Mayr Andreas, Kuhn Sebastian, Hirsch Martin Christian, Knitza Johannes. (2025). Perceptions, Usage, and Educational Impact of ChatGPT Among Medical Students in Germany: Cross-Sectional Mixed Methods Survey. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/81484

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey (May–August 2024) of medical students at Philipps University Marburg; 84 fully completed surveys of 315 started (26.7% response rate). Descriptive results show "76.2% (64/84)" had used ChatGPT for medical education.

> "Of all participants, 76.2% (64/84) stated that they have used ChatGPT for medical education purposes. The highest proportion of students used it once per week"

### Fußhöller Anna 2025 (2)

Fußhöller Anna, Lechner Fabian, Schlicker Nadine, Muehlensiepen Felix, Mayr Andreas, Kuhn Sebastian, Hirsch Martin Christian, Knitza Johannes. (2025). Perceptions, Usage, and Educational Impact of ChatGPT Among Medical Students in Germany: Cross-Sectional Mixed Methods Survey. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/81484

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired within-subject comparison of semester versus exam-period usage frequency in the same survey sample; the Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed significantly more frequent use during exam preparation (P=.003). No effect size printed.

> "A Wilcoxon signed-rank test indicated that students used ChatGPT significantly more often during exam preparation periods than during regular semester periods (P=.003)."
