# Instructor-led facial joy mimicry predominated: 58% of dyads were instructor-led and the critical lag window's midpoint was around +500 msec after the instructor's expression

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Frenzel 2024

Frenzel, A. C., Dindar, M., Pekrun, R., Reck, C., & Marx, A. K. G. (2024). Joy is reciprocally transmitted between teachers and students: Evidence on facial mimicry in the classroom. Learning and Instruction, 91, 101896. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101896

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-recurrence analysis of 69 instructor–student dyads compared sums of recurrences at negative versus positive lags to classify each dyad as student-led or instructor-led. The article reports "29 student-led and 40 teacher-led dyads" and 58% instructor-led.

> "There were 29 student-led and 40 teacher-led dyads, so on average, 58% of the dyads were characterized as instructor-led, implying a trend for instructors to lead the joy mimicry."
