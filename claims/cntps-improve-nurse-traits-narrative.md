`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Narrative analysis of four studies excluded from meta‐analysis because different traits were assessed; Chang et al. reported significant empowerment and productivity effects, while Okamoto et al.'s professional development effect was "not observed in the follow‐up evaluation (p > 0.100)".

> "Chang et al. (2008) found that nurses in the experimental group had significantly higher psychological empowerment (F (1,47) = 5.09, mean square error (MSE) = 3.25, p = 0.001, n 2 = 0.18) and job productivity (F (1,47) = 4.88, MSE = 5.18, p = 0.002, n 2 = 0.19) than those in the control group."
