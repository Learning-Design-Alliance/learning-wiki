## Evidence

### Shazia Rafiullah Miller 2002

Shazia Rafiullah Miller, Elaine M. Allensworth, Julie Reed Kochanek. (2002). Student Performance: Course Taking, Test Scores, and Outcomes — The State of Chicago Public High Schools: 1993 to 2000. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/student-performance-course-taking-test-scores-and-outcomes

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort analysis following Chicago students from age 13 until exit, tracking the 1992–93 through 1995–96 cohorts to graduation. The unadjusted rate rose from 40.6 to 44.3 percent, but "an increase of only 1.6 percent" remained after adjusting for incoming student composition.

> "With an increase of only 1.6 percent, the upward trend in adjusted graduation rates by age 18 is substantially smaller than the unadjusted one."
