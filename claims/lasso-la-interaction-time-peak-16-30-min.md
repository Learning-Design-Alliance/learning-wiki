## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen 2015

Ben Van Dusen, Laurie Langdon, and Valerie Otero. (2015). Learning Assistant Supported Student Outcomes (LASSO) study initial findings. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.081](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.081)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

HLM student interaction-time model (model 5), each category compared against 0 min/week; the table prints n of 280 students at 0 min, 184 at 16-30 min, and 203 at 30+ min. The 16-30 min category (0.873) was the only one significantly different from 0 min (0.369).

> "Model 5 shows that students' average effect size increases with the amount of time they spend with LAs. The trend peaks at 16-30 minutes/week, which is also the only category to be statistically significant from the 0 minutes/week category."
