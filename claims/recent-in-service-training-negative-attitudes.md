## Evidence

### Ni Cheng-Hua 2020

Ni Cheng-Hua, Guo Shu-Liu, Chao Chia-Yu, Wang Chia-Hui, Susanty Sri, & Chuang Yeu-Hui. (2020). Nurses’ Late-Life Depression Knowledge and Attitudes Toward Depression: A Cross-Sectional Study. Inquiry: A Journal of Medical Care Organization, Provision and Financing. https://doi.org/10.1177/0046958020945179

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey (N = 307); the regression prints B = −2.993 (β = −0.218, p < .001) for past-year in-service training, and the discussion states the surprise finding verbatim. No effect size is printed.

> "Surprisingly, nurses who had never received in-service training on depression in the recent 1 year had more positive attitudes toward depression in hospitals."
