`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Categorical analysis of the thirteen groups' submitted prompt records in the same take-home assignment. The author reports the majority adopted the probing approach, in which every prompt posed a different question, re-phrased for specificity or clarification, and one group masked ChatGPT as DAN.

> "Majority of the group adopted the probing approach to effectively use ChatGPT in their take-home assignment. Interestingly, one such group masked ChatGPT as DAN ('Do Anything Now' ) assuming it would enhance the response to their questions."
