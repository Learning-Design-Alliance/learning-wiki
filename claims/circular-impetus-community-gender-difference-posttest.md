### James Wells 2019

James Wells, Rachel Henderson, John Stewart, Gay Stewart, Jie Yang, and Adrienne Traxler. (2019). Exploring the Structure of Misconceptions in the Force Concept Inventory with Modified Module Analysis. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020122](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020122)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Post-test prevalence analysis (Table III) of non-blocking communities with t-tests by gender and Cohen's d: the circular impetus community averaged 14% for men versus 5% for women (p < 0.001, d = 0.39), while largest-force-determines-motion showed 42% versus 37% (d = 0.11).

> "6A, 7A 14 ± 35 5 ± 21 < 0.001 0.39 Circular impetus 17A, 25D 42 ± 49 37 ± 48 < 0.001 0.11 Largest force determines motion"
