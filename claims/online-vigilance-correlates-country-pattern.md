## Evidence

### le Roux 2021

le Roux, D.B., Parry, D.A., Totolo, A., Iyawa, G., Holloway, J., Prenter, A., Botha, L. (2021). Media Multitasking, Online Vigilance and Academic Performance among Students in three Southern African Countries. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.104056

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlations (Holm–Bonferroni adjusted) between online vigilance and academic performance across country samples. The article reports the full-sample association was small (ρ = −.17) and that "in the Namibia sample this association was moderate" (ρ = −.51), while absent in South Africa and Botswana samples.

> "A small association was found in the full sample (ρ =−.17,p<. 001), with no association in the South Africa and Botswana samples. However, in the Namibia sample this association was moderate ( ρ = −.51,p < .001)."

### le Roux 2021 (2)

le Roux, D.B., Parry, D.A., Totolo, A., Iyawa, G., Holloway, J., Prenter, A., Botha, L. (2021). Media Multitasking, Online Vigilance and Academic Performance among Students in three Southern African Countries. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.104056

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of the three Online Vigilance Scale subscales against academic performance among Namibian students (n = 402). Each subscale correlated significantly and negatively, with printed correlations r = −0.48, r = −0.42 and r = −0.47, described as showing negligible differences between their magnitudes.

> "We found signiﬁcant correlations between each of the subscales and academic performance, with negligible diﬀerences between their magnitudes (monitoring: r =−0.48,p < .001, salience: r =−0.42,p < .001, reacting: r =−0.47,p<. 001)."
