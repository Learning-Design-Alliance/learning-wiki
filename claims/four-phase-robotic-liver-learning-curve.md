## Evidence

### Rahimli M 2025

Rahimli M, Gumbs AA, Perrakis A, Al-Madhi S, Dölling M, Stelter F, Lorenz E, Andric M, Franz M, Arend J, Croner RS. (2025). Learning curve analysis of 100 consecutive robotic liver resections. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11551-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Phase boundaries were derived by cumulative average analysis of operation time and Iwate difficulty score in the single-surgeon series of 100 consecutive robotic liver resections, with linear regression fit per phase; no effect size is printed for the phase structure itself.

> "While this model typically includes three phases, we identified four distinct phases in our analysis based on trends in cumulative average operation time, Iwate difficulty scores, and statistical breakpoints: (1) Competency (Cases 1–20), (2) Proficiency (Cases 21–30), (3) Early Mastery (Cases 31–65), and 4) Full Mastery (Cases 66–100)."
