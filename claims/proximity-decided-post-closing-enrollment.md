### Sebring 2021

Sebring, Sall, Kheraj, Davis, Allensworth, de la Torre, & Beechum. (2021). Research and Engagement Agenda, 2021–25. UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.5-essentials.org/cps/5e/2018/s/610268/essentials/families/#trends?c=district&o=-change

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The agenda reports, citing its study of school closings (de la Torre et al. 2015), that "most families based their decision first and foremost on location"; students traveled about the same distance regardless of school rating. Families also defined quality as afterschool programs, curricula, small classes, welcoming environments, and one-on-one attention.

> "Whether they enrolled in a designated welcoming school, a higher-rated school, or a lower-rated school, most families based their decision first and foremost on location."
