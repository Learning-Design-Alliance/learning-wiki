## Evidence

### Elizondo-García ME 2025

Elizondo-García ME, Hernández-De la Cerda H, Benavides-García IG, Caratozzolo P and Membrillo-Hernández J (2025) Who is solving the challenge? The use of ChatGPT in mathematics and biology courses using challenge-based learning. Front. Educ. 10:1417642. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2025.1417642

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Sentiment polarity analysis using Python's TextBlob library on open-ended responses from both courses, scored from −1 (negative) to 1 (positive) with 0 neutral. The authors interpret the high neutral share as indicating students "might still lack familiarity with ChatGPT’s full capabilities or harbor uncertainties about its potential."

> "The sentiment distribution revealed that 90% of the responses were neutral, while only 2% were positive and 8% negative (Figure 2A)."
