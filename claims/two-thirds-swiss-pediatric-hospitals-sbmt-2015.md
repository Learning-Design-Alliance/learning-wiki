### Stocker M 2017

Stocker M, Laine K, Ulmer F. (2017). Use of simulation-based medical training in Swiss pediatric hospitals: a national survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0940-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

National survey of medical education representatives of all 31 FMH-recognized Swiss pediatric hospitals; 30 responded. The article reports "66.6% (20 out of 30) offering SBMT" and a surge from 3 institutions in 2009 to 20 of 31 in 2015.

> "Thirty Swiss pediatric hospitals and health care departments answered our survey (response rate 96.8%) with 66.6% (20 out of 30) offering SBMT."
