### Mühlberg Theres 2026

Mühlberg Theres, Bachner Joachim, Mess Filip, Schmid-Ellinger Jan. (2026). Observing teaching principles in school - Development and Validation of an Observation Protocol (OPTIS). PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0355710

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Expert review process across five rounds including schoolteachers, university teachers, seminar leaders, educational researchers and observation specialists. The article reports "18 experts" participated and content validity was considered given based on their feedback.

> "To ascertain whether OPTIS really assesses instructional quality within the framework of a constructivist approach of learning, and to enhance and deliberate upon the content validity of OPTIS, 18 experts reviewed the observation protocol at different time points during the developmental process."
