Sergey Sosnovsky 2025¶

Sergey Sosnovsky, Peter Brusilovsky, Andrew Lan. (2025). Intelligent Textbooks. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00451-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editorial summarizes the special-issue study by Sovrano et al. (2024), which extracts a knowledge graph from a collection of textbooks and other learning resources to provide on-demand explanations, combining knowledge graphs with generative question-answering models. No effect size is printed in the editorial.

"The authors conducted an experiment in the context of legal writing education to show that the enriched textbook leads to better learning outcomes than the baselines"

Sergey Sosnovsky 2025 (2)¶

Sergey Sosnovsky, Peter Brusilovsky, Andrew Lan. (2025). Intelligent Textbooks. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00451-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editorial summarizes Blobstein et al. (2024), which leverages students' emoji and hashtag annotations of course readings as a fourth-generation interaction-data source and also explored the feasibility of using LLMs to predict student annotations.

"The paper demonstrated that aggregating student annotation data could help teachers understand which parts of the text have been challenging or interesting"
