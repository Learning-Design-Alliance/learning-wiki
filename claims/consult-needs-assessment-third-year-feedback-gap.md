## Evidence

### Carter Keme MD 2015

Carter Keme MD, Golden Andrew BS, Martin Shannon MD, Donlan Sarah MD, Hock Sara MD, Babcock Christine MD, Farnan Jeanne MD, Arora Vineet MD. (2015). Results from the First Year of Implementation of CONSULT: Consultation with Novel Methods and Simulation for UME Longitudinal Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2015.9.25520

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Targeted needs assessment: an anonymous eight-question paper survey of third-year medical students at the conclusion of clerkships, with 57 of 96 eligible responding. The survey found "53 (93%) of third-year students completing the survey reported calling a consultation" while "Thirteen students (24%) reported receiving feedback on their ability to call consultations."

> "53 (93%) of third-year students completing the survey reported calling a consultation during any of their third-year rotations. Thirteen students (24%) reported receiving feedback on their ability to call consultations"

### Carter Keme MD 2015 (2)

Carter Keme MD, Golden Andrew BS, Martin Shannon MD, Donlan Sarah MD, Hock Sara MD, Babcock Christine MD, Farnan Jeanne MD, Arora Vineet MD. (2015). Results from the First Year of Implementation of CONSULT: Consultation with Novel Methods and Simulation for UME Longitudinal Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2015.9.25520

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same needs assessment survey of 57 third-year students. "Forty-one students (72%) reported receiving instruction on how to call a consult," and of those, almost all (40/41, 98%) "described informal instruction by a resident during their third-year rotations."

> "Forty-one students (72%) reported receiving instruction on how to call a consult, and of those, almost all (40/41, 98%) described informal instruction by a resident during their third-year rotations."
