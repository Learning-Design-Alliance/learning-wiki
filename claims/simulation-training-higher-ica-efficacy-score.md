## Evidence

### Oliveira-Santos Manuel 2026

Oliveira-Santos Manuel, Guerreiro Cláudio, Lobo Bárbara, Marinho Ana Vera, Borges-Rosa João, Guardado Jorge, Santos Eduardo Oliveira, Costa Marco, Gonçalves Lino, Bogle Richard, Dudek Dariusz, Pires-Morais Gustavo, Marques João Silva, Khokhar Arif A. (2026). Simulation training for invasive cardiovascular procedures: the Heart-SIMS-1 randomized trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08831-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot parallel-group randomized trial of 29 final-year medical students after a common one-hour lecture; the simulation group (n = 14) had a 20-minute mentored hands-on session and the conventional group (n = 15) watched a 20-minute video. The "efficacy score 91.5 ± 3.8% vs. 64.6 ± 8.3%" was graded on a 13-step checklist in a blinded simulated ICA assessment.

> "Both elements of the co-primary endpoint were superior in the simulation group: efficacy score 91.5 ± 3.8% vs. 64.6 ± 8.3% (mean difference 95% CI [20.8, 30.8]) and safety score 100.0% (100.0-100.0%) vs. 62.5% (20.8–79.2%) (median difference 95% CI [20.8, 79.2]), p < 0.001 for both."
