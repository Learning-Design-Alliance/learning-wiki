# Adjusting MAP Growth K–2 item difficulties in the 2020 scale alignment study improved item fit, with the largest gains in outfit statistics for difficult Mathematics items

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` New outfit statistics are closer to 1 than old outfit statistics for difficult items in both Reading and Mathematics, indicating improved model-data fit after difficulty adjustment. [→ He 2020](#he-2020)
`q2 i?` For Mathematics items with RIT above 220, new infit statistics appear closer to 1 after adjustment, while Reading infit statistics show no noticeable change. [→ He 2020](#he-2020)

## Evidence

### He 2020

He, W. (2020). MAP Growth K–2 item fit analysis: A follow-up study. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-k-2-item-fit-analysis-study-a-follow-up-study/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Scatterplot-based numerical analysis (Figures 2.1 and 2.2) of new versus old infit and outfit statistics against item difficulty for the AllYr sample. The report states the adjustment of item difficulties "has helped with the improvement of the item fit."

> "However, for Mathematics, the new infit statistics for those difficult items (i.e., item RIT>220) appear to have been brought closer to 1 with the adjusted item difficulties. The new outfit statistics are closer to 1 compared to their old counterparts for those difficult items in both content areas."

### He 2020 (2)

He, W. (2020). MAP Growth K–2 item fit analysis: A follow-up study. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-k-2-item-fit-analysis-study-a-follow-up-study/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Summary statistics in Table 2.2 for both samples: mean absolute distances of outfit statistics from 1.0 shrink after adjustment, most markedly for Mathematics (0.25 to 0.07), which the report reads as improved model-data fit.

> "the average distances from the expected value of 1.0 for the old (i.e., abs(Outfitold-1)) and new statistics (i.e., abs(Outfitnew-1)) are 0.05 and 0.04 for Reading and 0.25 and 0.07 for Mathematics."
