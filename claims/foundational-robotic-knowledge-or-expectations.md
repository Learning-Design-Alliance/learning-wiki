### Greenberg AL 2022

Greenberg AL, Syed SM, Alseidi A, O'Sullivan PS, Chern H. (2022). Robotic training for medical students: feasibility of a pilot simulation curriculum. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01508-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

First of four themes from the focus-group thematic analysis; students described acquiring robot knowledge piecemeal from unstructured sources and one noted "We get better reviews" when they already knew how to help. Qualitative perception data, n = 13 focus group participants.

> "Regarding expectations of students in OR, students revealed their perception of the importance of having foundational robotic skills and being able to help with robotic cases."
