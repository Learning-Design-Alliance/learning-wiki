## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the three-level multilevel model, the school mean pretest is predictive of posttest achievement, with a one standard deviation increase in school pretest associated with a 0.89 increase in outcome scores. [→ Vaden-Kiernan 2016](#vaden-kiernan-2016)

## Evidence

### Vaden-Kiernan 2016

Vaden-Kiernan, M., Borman, G., Caverly, S., Bell, N., Ruiz de Castilla, V., Sullivan, K., & Fleming, G. (2016). Findings from a Multi-Year Scale-Up Effectiveness Trial of Open-Court Reading (Imagine It!). SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567512

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel (student, school, district) ITT model with grand-centered school-mean GRADE pretest scores; Table 2 prints a Pretest Mean coefficient of 0.51 (SE 0.05, ***) in Year 1 and 0.62 (SE 0.08, ***) in Year 2. This is an unstandardized coefficient, not a standardized effect size.

> "The multilevel model results indicate that the schools mean pretest is predictive of the posttest achievement (see Table 2). A one standard deviation increase in school pretest score is associated with a 0.89 increase in outcome scores."
