### Jibeom Seo 2024

Jibeom Seo, Jun Cho, Sukyung Han, Meesoon Ha. (2024). Semantic Network Analysis of Achievement Standards in Physics of 2022 Revised Curriculum. arXiv. [doi:10.3938/npsm.74.944](https://doi.org/10.3938/npsm.74.944)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of 243 physics terms with 852 word-to-word connections from the achievement standards; distributions are right-skewed like the all-words network but with the larger exponent.

> "Notably, a power-law model analysis (p ∼ k−γ) of the node degree distribution yielded a relatively large exponent γ of 4.08. This suggests that the physics-term network more closely resembles a random network than a scale-free network, indicating the absence of prominent hub nodes."
