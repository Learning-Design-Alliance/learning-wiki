# Only 78% of Kentucky AP course completers took the AP exam in 2021-22, and test-taking growth did not extend to Black or Latino students

### Kristen Hengtgen 2023

Kristen Hengtgen, Hector Biaggi. (2023). Increasing Access to Advanced Coursework in Kentucky. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641971

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of AP course completers tested, by race/ethnicity, from the Kentucky School Report Card. The report prints that "only 78% of students who completed their AP course went on to take the AP test in 2021-22" and that growth bypassed Black and Latino students.

> "Overall, only 78% of students who completed their AP course went on to take the AP test in 2021-22. While there was a 3-percentage point growth in AP test-taking from 2019 to 2022, that growth did not extend to Black or Latino students, who continue to have lower test-taking rates."
