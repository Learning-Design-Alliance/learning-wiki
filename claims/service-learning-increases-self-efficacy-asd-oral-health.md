`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired t-tests on a 6-item 0–100 self-efficacy scale (Cronbach's α = 0.88) in the same pretest-posttest study; the largest change was in understanding of ASD oral health status, described as having "rose dramatically".

> "Self-rated understanding of the oral health status of children with autism, which rose dramatically from 32.28 ± 27.86 to 75.34 ± 17.77 (mean difference = 43.06, 95%CI: 37.01 to 49.12, p < 0.001)."
