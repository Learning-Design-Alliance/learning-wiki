## Evidence

### Rowe David 2023

Rowe David, Garcia Alejandra, Rossi Benito. (2023). Comparison of virtual reality and physical simulation training in first‐year radiography students in South America. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.639

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Statistical comparison of two stratified-randomised cohorts of 94 first-year radiography students each, assessed in a blinded OSCE after 25 weeks of matched tuition. The printed F ratio of 232.075 with P < 0.000 shows "OSCE time is significantly higher in the physical simulation cohort"; descriptive means were 912.245 s (VR) versus 961.47 s.
