## Evidence

### Dai Dongjun 2026

Dai Dongjun, Fan Yuzhi, Zhang Wenbao, Wei Qichun. (2026). The effect of narrative medicine-based education on academic performance and humanistic communication in residency training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53552-x

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 9 randomized controlled studies (321 vs 322 residents) of theoretical knowledge examination scores. The pooled SMD = 1.46 (95% CI − 0.72 to 3.64) was not significant (P = 0.19), with extreme heterogeneity (I2 = 95.3%); no effect size conclusion is warranted.
