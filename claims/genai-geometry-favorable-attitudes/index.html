Davor I 2026¶

Davor I (2026) Generative AI-supported instruction as a cognitive scaffold: effects on senior high school students' geometric reasoning and proof construction. Front. Educ. 11:1869961. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1869961

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive statistics on the attitude subscale among experimental-group students only. The highest-rated item was "Using generative AI during geometry lessons improves my understanding" (M = 4.47, SD = .631); the lowest was confidence in using technological tools (M = 3.91, SD = .840).

"The overall mean attitude score of 4.13 indicates that students who participated in the AI-supported instructional condition generally reported favorable attitudes toward the learning experience."
