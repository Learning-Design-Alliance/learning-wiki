Law Marcus, Rapoport Mark J., Seitz Dallas, Davidson Marla, Madan Robert, Wiens Andrew. (2015). Evaluation of a National Online Educational Program in Geriatric Psychiatry. Academic Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40596-015-0377-y

Post-test-only self-report survey of perceived effectiveness of the five instructional methods in the 2012–2013 OSG, analyzed descriptively. "short-answer questions being most popular and journal club being least popular (17 % stated it was not at all effective)". No effect size printed.
