Evidence¶

Mingming Zhou 2022¶

Mingming Zhou, Hao Mou. (2022). Tracking public opinion about online education over COVID-19 in China. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10080-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

LDA content analysis of the post-pandemic Weibo subset, with the optimal number of topics set to 7 when the weight stopped decreasing at 0.020. The article reports the seven topics were of an even bigger variety, spanning political, pedagogical, personal, and technological themes.

"After the pandemic, despite the decreasing number of microblogs, the topics that emerged from the Weibo were of an even bigger variety. Among the seven topics, Topic 1 and 4 were more political than other topics as shown in the key term of "resisting", "education bureau" and "resistance"."
