Language Preferences and Practices in Multilingual EdTech: Flexible Primary Language Use with Secondary Language Support. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Log-based analysis of registration choices among 2,931 learners enrolled in the remote course, showing "43% (n = 1,270) of students selecting the Hybrid condition". Interviews suggested learners valued both languages for comprehension and daily communication.

> "a majority of students opted for the Hybrid condition of the course, with 43% (n = 1,270) of students selecting the Hybrid condition, 31% (n = 903) selecting English, and 26% (n = 758) selecting Leb-Lango."
