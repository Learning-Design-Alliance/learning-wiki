# Shifts from the blue to the green behavioral cluster correspond to shifts from completing the worksheet to discussing ideas

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · qualitative `r3` · `q1`–`q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2007 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` In analyzed episodes, abrupt behavioral shifts from the blue cluster (leaning over papers, quiet recitation) to the green cluster (sitting up, eye contact, loud original speech, prolific gesture) coincide with shifts in the substance of reasoning from worksheet completion to discussing physics ideas. [→ Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer 2007](#rachel-e-scherr-and-david-hammer-2007)

`q1 i?` In group A's Newton's third law tutorial, an abrupt shift from blue to green behavior coincided with a shift from reading worksheet questions to discussing physics ideas. [→ Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer 2007](#rachel-e-scherr-and-david-hammer-2007)

`q1 i?` In the second episode, the transition to green-cluster behavior coincided with discussion of ideas rather than consultation about answers and reference to authority. [→ Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer 2007 (2)](#rachel-e-scherr-and-david-hammer-2007-2)

## Evidence

### Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer 2007

Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer. (2007). Student Behavior and Epistemological Framing: Examples from Collaborative Active-Learning Activities in Physics. Submitted to Cognition and Instruction. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Student+Behavior+and+Epistemological+Framing

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative episode analysis of group A working on the Newton's third law tutorial. Behavior and speech content jointly support the interpretation that students "shift from framing their activity as completing the worksheet to framing it as discussing the ideas."

> "The changes in behavioral cluster from blue to green correspond with changes in the substance of the conversation. These two types of evidence together support our identification of the students' epistemological framing: They shift from framing their activity as completing the worksheet to framing it as discussing the ideas."
