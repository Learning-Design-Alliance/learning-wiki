Evidence¶

Xia 2016¶

Xia, M., Fosco, G. M., & Feinberg, M. E. (2016). Examining Reciprocal Influences Among Family Climate, School Attachment, and Academic Self-Regulation: Implications for School Success. Journal of Family Psychology, 30(4), 442-452. https://doi.org/10.1037/fam0000141

q3 · i? · associational · r3

In the four-wave cross-lag model, family climate predicted ASR increases in all three intervals; the authors report only one of three reverse paths from ASR to family climate was significant, indicating "the direction of effects between family climate and ASR was unidirectional".

"More cohesive, more organized, and less conflictual family climates were associated with increases in ASR across all three time intervals."
