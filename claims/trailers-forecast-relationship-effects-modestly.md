# Students' reactions to 6-min teaching trailers forecast relationship effects on live-lecture evaluations, but with small unique accuracy (7% of variance, R = .27)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Relational teaching evaluations and positive affect in response to trailers significantly forecast evaluations of live lectures, with most predictive accuracy shared between the two predictors. [→ Gross Jennifer 2015](#gross-jennifer-2015)

## Evidence

### Gross Jennifer 2015

Gross Jennifer, Lakey Brian, Lucas Jessica L, LaCross Ryan, R Plotkowski Andrea, Winegard Bo. (2015). Forecasting the student–professor matches that result in unusually effective teaching. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12049

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression on relationship scores (N = 483 student–professor dyads) from 145 students. Positive affect uniquely forecast live-lecture ratings (β = .18; p < .05; ΔR2 = .02) but trailer evaluations did not (β = .11; n.s.).

> "In multiple regression analyses, teaching evaluations and positive affect in response to the trailers forecasted 7% (R = .27) of the variance in live lectures."
