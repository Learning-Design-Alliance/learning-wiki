`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective comparison on eCREST Case 1: students changed diagnoses 2.8 times on average, and 203 of 213 (95%) changed diagnosis at least once, with no significant difference between groups (P=.98 for changed at least once).

> "We found no evidence of differences between physician assistant students and medical students in the number of times they changed diagnoses (P=.99)."
