### Frith Chris D 2012

Frith Chris D. (2012). The role of metacognition in human social interactions. Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2012.0123

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Haggard et al.'s finding (as cited) that voluntary actions show intentional binding, an compression of subjective interval between action and outcome, absent for involuntary actions. The review links this to experience of agency and sense of responsibility.

> "actions and their consequences are experienced as closer together in subjective time than in objective time. Such intentional binding does not occur when the action is involuntary"
