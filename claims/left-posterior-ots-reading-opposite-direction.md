## Evidence

### Roell M. 2022

Roell M., Bellon E., Polspoel B., Declercq M. & De Smedt B. (2022). Investigating the association between variability in sulcal pattern and academic achievement. Scientific Reports 12, 12323. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-15335-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA on the Dutch One-Minute reading test in 66 grade 4 children controlling for sex, dataset and IQ. The left posterior OTS association reached significance ("F(1,44) = 6.15, p = 0.01", BF10 = 5.39); the other three OTS regions showed no significant association. No effect size printed.

> "The left posterior OTS was significantly associated with reading ability F(1,44) = 6.15, p = 0.01 and evidence for this association was substantial, BF10 = 5.39. Left anterior OTS was not significantly associated with reading ability, F(1,44) = 1.16, p = .28"
