### Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage 2016

Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage. (2016). Developing an Action Concept Inventory. arXiv:1605.01786. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Statistical analysis of ACI post-test response data from the 2014 and 2015 Physics 2 classes (N=76 and N=87) identified the four quantum-concept questions as particularly difficult with low discrimination; the quote names "complex amplitude, superposition principle, and probability" as the concepts affected.

> "Questions with particularly low discrimination, and diﬃculty indices - that is, particularly diﬃcult questions - were four questions testing three of the quantum mechan- ical concepts (Q14, Q15, Q17, Q18 [6]), namely: complex amplitude, superposition principle, and probability."
