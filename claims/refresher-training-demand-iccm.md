## Evidence

### Rabbani Fauziah 2016

Rabbani Fauziah, Perveen Shagufta, Aftab Wafa, Zahidie Aysha, Sangrasi Kashif, Qazi Shamim Ahmad. (2016). Health workers’ perspectives, knowledge and skills regarding community case management of childhood diarrhoea and pneumonia: a qualitative inquiry for an implementation research project “Nigraan” in District Badin, Sindh, Pakistan. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-016-1699-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Across FGDs and IDIs, training was named a major motivating factor; participants said "Training is very important, if we forget anything training helps to recall and stimulates our memories" and wanted supervisors trained to clinically mentor LHWs.

> "The last training for most LHWs and LHSs was conducted several years ago, but they desired additional observation-based training to enhance their knowledge and skills."
