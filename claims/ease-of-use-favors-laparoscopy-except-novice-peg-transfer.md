# After the tasks, inexperienced surgeons' ease-of-use ratings did not differ between conventional laparoscopy and SPIDER for peg transfer, while conventional laparoscopy was rated easier for pattern cutting; experienced surgeons rated conventional laparoscopy easier for both tasks

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2015 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among inexperienced surgeons, ease-of-use ratings did not differ between devices for the peg transfer task (median 3 vs. 3; p = 0.093), but conventional laparoscopy was rated easier for pattern cutting (median 3 vs. 2; p = 0.015). [→ Giannotti Domenico 2015](#giannotti-domenico-2015)
`q3 i?` Experienced surgeons rated conventional laparoscopy easier than SPIDER for both tasks (Task 1: median 5 vs. 3, p = 0.004; Task 2: median 5 vs. 3, p = 0.002). [→ Giannotti Domenico 2015 (2)](#giannotti-domenico-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Giannotti Domenico 2015

Giannotti Domenico, Casella Giovanni, Patrizi Gregorio, Di Rocco Giorgio, Castagneto-Gissey Lidia, Metere Alessio, Bernieri Maria Giulia, Vestri Anna Rita, Redler Adriano. (2015). Spider surgical system versus multiport laparoscopic surgery: performance comparison on a surgical simulator. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-015-0038-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-task questionnaire on a six-point Likert scale administered to the 10 PGY1 residents after each task. The study reports ease of use "did not differ in Task 1 (median 3 vs. 3; p = 0.093)" but Task 2 "proved to be simpler with conventional laparoscopic instruments"; no effect size is printed.

> "Among the inexperienced group, the ease of use of conventional laparoscopy and SPIDER surgical system did not differ in Task 1 (median 3 vs. 3; p = 0.093). Task 2 proved to be simpler with conventional laparoscopic instruments as opposed to the SPIDER system (median 3 vs. 2; p = 0.015)."

### Giannotti Domenico 2015 (2)

Giannotti Domenico, Casella Giovanni, Patrizi Gregorio, Di Rocco Giorgio, Castagneto-Gissey Lidia, Metere Alessio, Bernieri Maria Giulia, Vestri Anna Rita, Redler Adriano. (2015). Spider surgical system versus multiport laparoscopic surgery: performance comparison on a surgical simulator. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-015-0038-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-task Likert questionnaire administered to the 10 experienced laparoscopists after each task. The study reports they "found it easier to execute both tasks through conventional laparoscopy rather than with the SPIDER system"; no effect size is printed.

> "Furthermore, experienced laparoscopic surgeons found it easier to execute both tasks through conventional laparoscopy rather than with the SPIDER system (Task 1: median 5 vs. 3 p = 0.004. Task 2: median 5 vs. 3 p = 0.002)."
