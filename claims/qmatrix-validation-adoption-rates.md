Applying cognitive diagnostic models to mechanics concept inventories. Physical Review Physics Education Research 21, 010103. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.21.010103

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Q-matrix validation step applying the proportion of variance accounted for method to DINA analyses of the three RBAs; suggested changes were reviewed by the three coders and adopted when the majority agreed. Per-assessment adoption rates were 64% (FCI), 36% (FMCE), and 100% (EMCS).

> "Overall, only 8.5% of the codings (26 of 306) were identified for reexamination by this analysis. Of the 26 proposed changes, 13 were adopted across the 3 assessments, yielding an overall adoption rate of 50%."
