Perez Arnaldo 2023¶

Perez Arnaldo, Green Jacqueline, Moharrami Mohammad, Gianoni-Capenakas Silvia, Kebbe Maryam, Ganatra Seema, Ball Geoff, Sharmin Nazlee. (2023). Active learning in undergraduate classroom dental education- a scoping review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0293206

q3 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of 76 student reaction evaluations in the review; satisfaction was the dominant perceived outcome, and in fifty-nine studies active learning was perceived as superior to lectures, 5 found no differences, and 1 reported lectures as superior.

"active learning was perceived to improve satisfaction in 66 studies (86.8%) and knowledge acquisition in 4 studies (5.3%)"

Perez Arnaldo 2023 (2)¶

Perez Arnaldo, Green Jacqueline, Moharrami Mohammad, Gianoni-Capenakas Silvia, Kebbe Maryam, Ganatra Seema, Ball Geoff, Sharmin Nazlee. (2023). Active learning in undergraduate classroom dental education- a scoping review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0293206

q3 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of learning-level evaluations: 48 of 57 studies compared active learning and lectures; 39 found active learning superior in knowledge acquisition and 9 reported no difference between active learning and traditional lectures.

"All studies in which learning was directly measured (n = 57) found that active learning was effective to improve knowledge acquisition largely based on test scores"

Related Claims¶

  • Flipped learning, PBL, group discussion, and TBL were the most frequently evaluated active learning strategies, each showing superiority to lectures in direct-measure studies — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Student preference for CBL, PBL or TBL over traditional teaching was reported in 15 of 22 studies (68.1%), but only two studies used a controlled comparison with lecture — related
  • TBL in medical ethics education improves knowledge retention compared to lectures — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • PBL in family medicine residency programs is associated with high learner satisfaction, though not always rated above lectures — related
  • TBL is associated with higher satisfaction, positive attitudes and motivation versus lecture, but preferences are mixed — related
  • AI-powered PBL/CBL outperforms traditional lectures for knowledge acquisition, but shows no significant advantage over AI-free PBL/CBL — related
  • PBL is associated with improved trainee satisfaction in PGME (8 of 12 reaction studies reported high satisfaction) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Perceived VDT utility did not always correspond to measurable academic advantage — related
