Jacob Feinleib, Matthew Dew, and N.G. Holmes. (2026). Share, Rotate, Split: The Effects of Group Work Role Distributions on Student Outcomes. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/1n3c-v2fj](https://doi.org/10.1103/1n3c-v2fj)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison of pre- and post-survey score distributions (Fig. 4) across the three courses. The article reports PLIC raw gains of 0.01 to 0.07 and perceived agency raw gains of 0.02 to 0.09 out of 1, and notes clear ceiling effects on self-efficacy and perceived agency scores.

> "We found that students' scores increase from pre- to post-survey for all constructs, though the size of the increases varied by course and construct."
