Evidence¶

Browning M. H. E. M. 2019¶

Browning M. H. E. M., Rigolon A. (2019). School Green Space and Its Impact on Academic Performance: A Systematic Literature Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16030429

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review (PRISMA) of 13 peer-reviewed articles identified via Scopus, Web of Science, and ERIC searches, totaling 574 inferential analyses. The review reports "78 (64%) were null, another 34 (28%) were positive" and ten (8%) negative; no meta-analysis was conducted due to inconsistent designs.

"Of 122 entries, 78 (64%) were null, another 34 (28%) were positive [28,48,52,54,55,59,60,61], and ten (8%) were negative"

Related Claims¶

  • Positive green space associations concentrate in college preparatory exams and end-of-semester grades more than math, reading, or writing test scores
  • Green space measured within 2000 m of schools shows more positive associations, while 3000 m or greater buffers show mostly null or negative findings
  • Positive associations are limited to greenness, tree cover, and green land cover; agriculture, grass, and shrub cover show no positive findings
  • SES moderation of the green space–achievement relationship shows mixed direction across studies, and gender and urbanization moderation findings are largely non-significant
