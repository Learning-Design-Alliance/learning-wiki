Evidence¶

Wiggins et al. 2017¶

Meg Wiggins, Constanza Gonzalez Parrao, Helen Austerberry and Anne Ingold. (2017). Foreign Language Learning in Primary School: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581156

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis by FSM eligibility in the cluster RCT. The executive summary table reports English Language FSM pupils effect size -0.11 (-0.23 to 0.02), -2 months' progress, p value 0.09 — not statistically significant.

"Children in FLL classes who had ever been eligible for free school meals made 2 months’ fewer progress compared to other ever-eligible children. However, we have very low confidence that this result was not affected by other factors."
