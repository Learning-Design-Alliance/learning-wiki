Evidence¶

Hartatiana 2018¶

Hartatiana, Darhim & Elah Nurlaelah. (2018). Improving Junior High School Students’ Spatial Reasoning Ability Through Model Eliciting Activities with Cabri 3D. International Education Studies, 11(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p148

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of 143 junior high school students divided into two groups (MEAC, n = 72; MEA, n = 71), with a spatial reasoning test given before and after instruction. The t'-test gave t' = -4.181, Sig (2 tailed) 0.000, with mean 0.447 for MEAC versus 0.315 for MEA; the article reports "significant difference" in improvement.

"As shown in Table 3, the p value is lower than 0.05 so that H 0 is rejected. Thus, it can be concluded that there is significant difference students’ spatial reaoning ability improvement between students who are taught by using MEAC and those who are taught by using MEAC."
