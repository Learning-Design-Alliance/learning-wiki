## Evidence

### Dixon and Wu 2014

L. Quentin Dixon & Shuang Wu. (2014). Understanding Language Learning: Review of the Application of the Interaction Model in Foreign Language Contexts. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 11, 23-39. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137251

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review compares two cited studies: Qin's Grade 7 beginning EFL learners in China and Song and Suh's university-age intermediate Korean EFL learners. Output-based instruction "performed better on the production post-test" only in the latter; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Qin (2008) found that input-based instruction (processing instruction) and output-based instruction (dictogloss tasks) ended up with the same results after one month. However, Song and Suh (2008) reported a different result: participants with more opportunities to produce output performed better on the production post-test."
