## Evidence

### Wilton Alexander Awil 2022

Wilton Alexander Awil, Pananwala Hasitha. (2022). Publication in the Australian medical student journal is associated with future academic success: a matched-cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03607-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective matched-cohort study of 55 AMSJ first authors (2010–2015) versus 110 university- and year-matched controls, with outcome data from public sources. The quote reports that "37 of the 55 (67%)" authors achieved subsequent PubMed-indexed publications; the univariate analysis gave OR 3.43 (95% CI 1.74–6.77, P < 0.001).

> "At the time of data collection, 37 of the 55 (67%) of student authors had achieved subsequent PubMed indexed publications. There was an average of 4.35 (1–19) PubMed indexed publications per author."
