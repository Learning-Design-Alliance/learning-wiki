## Evidence

### Zhanni Luo 2024

Zhanni Luo. (2024). Factors contributing to teachers' acceptance intention to gamified EFL tools: a scale development study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10249-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

EFA results from the 512-teacher survey (PCA with Varimax rotation). The article reports the factor extraction "statistically regarded" the paired constructs as single factors, which the authors interpret as a face-validity problem. No effect size printed.

> "It worth noticing that the factor extraction results indicated that the PU and perceived enjoyment were statistically regarded as one factor, as well as the constructs PEoU and perceived risks."
