# More experienced clinicians answered transducer-height and phlebostatic-axis questions more accurately

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Respondents with more than 5 years of experience answered the transducer-height-effect question significantly more often correctly (94% vs. 76%, p < 0.001) and identified the phlebostatic axis more often (30% vs. 54%, p = 0.002). [→ O. Coolens 2026](#o-coolens-2026)
`q2 i?` Experienced professionals more often named the supine position as the valid reference for the phlebostatic axis (9% vs. 23%, p = 0.003). [→ O. Coolens 2026 (2)](#o-coolens-2026-2)
