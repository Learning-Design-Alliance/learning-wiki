## Evidence

### Ajitoni 2015

Ajitoni, S. O., & Gbadamosi, T. V. (2015). Community-Based Instructional Strategies, School Location, and Primary School Pupils' Environmental Knowledge. JISTE Vol. 19, No. 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1177058

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of post-test Environmental Knowledge Test scores with pretest as covariate, in a pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with 264 primary-5 pupils in 12 schools. The article reports "F(2, 251 )  =29.98, p = 0.00" and an effect size of η² = 0.489 (printed as n2 = 0.489), so hypothesis 1 was rejected.

> "There was a effect of treatment on pupils' environmental knowledge :     F(2, 251 )  =29.98, p = 0.00; n2 = 0.489 ). This means that the adjusted post -test scores of pupils in the two experimental groups and control are significantly different."
