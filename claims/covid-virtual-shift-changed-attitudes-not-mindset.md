Moire K. M. Prescott, Laura Madson, Sandra M. Way, Kelly N. Sanderson. (2024). Prevalence of a growth mindset among introductory astronomy students. arXiv:2407.06147 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010140)

Semester-to-semester comparison of presurvey scores (Figure 1) spanning the pandemic transition; the changes are described in words without printed effect sizes or test statistics.

> "students began the Fall 2020 and Spring 2021 semesters expressing significantly lower affin- ity for astronomy, somewhat lower levels of perceived competence, and slightly higher levels of perceived difficulty than during the previous few semesters."

### Moire K. M. Prescott 2024 (2)

Moire K. M. Prescott, Laura Madson, Sandra M. Way, Kelly N. Sanderson. (2024). Prevalence of a growth mindset among introductory astronomy students. arXiv:2407.06147 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010140)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same five-semester comparison shows mindset beliefs stayed within uncertainties during COVID-19 while other attitudes varied, emphasizing mindset beliefs as "a particularly stable aspect of students' mental landscape."

> "Yet this same time period seemed to have a negligible impact on students' learning mindset, as measured using standard growth mindset questions."
