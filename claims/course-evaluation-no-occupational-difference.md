`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Course evaluation (13 items, 6-point Likert) collected immediately after the workshop and compared between occupational groups with Kruskal-Wallis tests. The article reports "no statistically significant difference was found in evaluation scores across occupational groups (H = 4.381, p = 0.223)". No effect size printed.

> "The average course evaluation score was 63.26 (SD = 5.33). Social workers rated the course highest (mean = 65.33, SD = 3.50), followed closely by registered nurses (mean = 64.60, SD = 2.41). However, no statistically significant difference was found in evaluation scores across occupational groups (H = 4.381, p = 0.223)."
