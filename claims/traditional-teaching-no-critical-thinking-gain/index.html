Zhou 2018¶

Zhen Zhou. (2018). An Empirical Study on the Influence of PBL Teaching Model on College Students’ Critical Thinking Ability. English Language Teaching, 11(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n4p15

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples T test of the control group receiving conventional classroom teaching (Table 2). The article reports all significance values "larger than .05"; no effect size is printed and equivalence was not tested.

"the statistical results (Table 2) demonstrated that the significant values of the control group in all dimensions of critical thinking temperament and critical thinking skills and CET4 scores are all larger than .05, which means that there is no significant difference."
