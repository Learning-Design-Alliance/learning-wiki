Evidence¶

Shahin Hossain 2026¶

Shahin Hossain. (2026). Four Types of LLM Reliance and Their Predictors Among Undergraduate Writers: A Mixed-Methods Study at a Minority-Serving R1 University. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28749 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.28749

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Quantitative finding from the main survey study (N=382): the most cognitively sophisticated reliance type scored lowest on the six self-reported AI-mediated writing outcome indicators. No effect size is printed for this inversion in the supplied text.

"Strategic users, those who are deliberate, critically evaluative, and academically principled, consistently score lowest on all outcome measures."

Shahin Hossain 2026 (2)¶

Shahin Hossain. (2026). Four Types of LLM Reliance and Their Predictors Among Undergraduate Writers: A Mixed-Methods Study at a Minority-Serving R1 University. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28749

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Structural analysis of existing LLM outcome instruments: the authors argue such measures capture AI-attributed attainment, so principled restraint produces lower scores by design. This is the authors' interpretation supporting the measurement-artifact claim.

"existing outcome instruments in the LLM literature predominantly assess statements such as “I use generative AI tools to achieve X, ” thereby measuring AI usage rather than writing quality or cognitive development."
