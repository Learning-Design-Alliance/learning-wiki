Kosmas 2025¶

Kosmas, P., Nisiforou, E. A., Kounnapi, E., Sophocleous, S., & Theophanous, G. (2025). Integrating artificial intelligence in literacy lessons for elementary classrooms: a co-design approach. Education Tech Research Dev (2025) 73:2589–2615. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10492-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Student questionnaire data (n = 62) on perceived AI impact on text comprehension. The article reports "nearly 60% of students (38) finding it helpful", 16.1% unsure, and 22.6% finding AI unhelpful (14.5% limited benefit, 8.1% no improvement).

"Student responses in Fig. 8 reveal a positive trend in AI’s impact on text comprehension, with nearly 60% of students (38) finding it helpful. Among them, 25.8% reported significant improvement in their understanding ("Very Much"), and 35.5% experienced moderate benefits ("Somewhat")."
