## Evidence

### Armitage 2023

Armitage, KL; Suddendorf, T; Bulley, A; Bastos, APM; Taylor, AH; Redshaw, J. (2023). Creativity and Flexibility in Young Children's Use of External Cognitive Strategies. Developmental Psychology, 59 (6), pp. 995-1005. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001562

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the same preregistered study's transfer phase, children who had used the demonstrated strategy at least once were introduced to a conceptually similar but structurally distinct task; the article reports many 4-year-olds and older "spontaneously devised more than one previously unseen reminder-setting strategy."

> "By contrast, many children aged 4 years and older spontaneously devised more than one previously unseen reminder-setting strategy across the six transfer trials, with this tendency increasing with age."
