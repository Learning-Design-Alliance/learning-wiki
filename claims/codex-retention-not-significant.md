> "learners from the Codex group on average scored 9.0% higher on authoring tasks (Codex: M=59.1%, SD=34.8%; Baseline: M=49.8%, SD=32.4%; t(67)=1.13, p=.262, d=0.28), and 12.7% higher on modifying tasks (Codex: M=47.5%, SD=31.6%; Baseline: M=34.8%, SD=29.0%; t(67)=1.70, p=.092, d=0.41), but neither of these reached statistical significance"
