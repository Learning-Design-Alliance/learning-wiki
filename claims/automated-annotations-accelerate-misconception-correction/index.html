Evidence¶

Matey Yordanov 2026¶

Matey Yordanov, Mikhail Bychkov, and Andrei Kuchma. (2026). The Effect of High-Frequency, Automatically-marked Formative Assessments on Student Outcomes in A-Level Sciences. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23566

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative thematic analysis of student portfolios tracking conceptual error decay over the term. The article describes "a rapid phenomenon of ''error-type decay''" attributed to step-by-step marking; no effect size is reported.

"Because the Cortex algorithm assesses logic step-by-step (e.g., appending a ! at the precise locus of failure), students in the intervention cohort rectified fundamental procedural misconceptions (e.g., the misapplication of the chain rule in calculus) weeks in advance of their control group peers."
