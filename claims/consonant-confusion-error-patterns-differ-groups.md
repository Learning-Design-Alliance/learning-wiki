# Consonant-confusion error patterns in artificial script reading differ between dyslexic and typical readers

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In the letter task, typical readers with errors showed significantly more similar than dissimilar consonant confusions (d = .62). [→ Cao 2026](#cao-2026)
`q3 i2` In the syllable task, dyslexic readers made significantly more errors for dissimilar than similar consonants (d = -.63), with no difference in the letter task. [→ Cao 2026 (2)](#cao-2026-2)
`q3 i3` Among participants with confusion errors, TD participants showed a significantly higher similar error rate on the letter task than DD participants (d = .86). [→ Cao 2026 (3)](#cao-2026-3)

## Evidence

### Cao 2026

Cao, Y., Zhang, M., Gentile, F. & Bonte, M. (2026). Dynamic behavioral and neural correlates of letter-speech sound learning in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00410-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests on consonant substitution errors among TD participants who made errors (n = 19 syllable, n = 16 letter task): "significantly more confusions for similar than dissimilar consonants in the letter task" (d = .62), but no syllable-task difference.

> "Paired-samples t-tests showed no significant difference between error types for TD in the syllable task (t(18) = -.816, p > .05, d = -0.19), but significantly more confusions for similar than dissimilar consonants in the letter task (t(15) = 2.474, p = .026, d = .62)."

### Cao 2026 (2)

Cao, Y., Zhang, M., Gentile, F. & Bonte, M. (2026). Dynamic behavioral and neural correlates of letter-speech sound learning in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00410-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests among DD participants who made errors (n = 21 both tasks): "significantly more errors for dissimilar than similar consonants in the syllable task" (d = -.63), no letter-task difference (d = -.21).

> "Dyslexic readers instead showed significantly more errors for dissimilar than similar consonants in the syllable task ( t(20) = -2.904, p = .009, d = -.63) but no significant difference in the letter task ( t(20) = -.972, p > .05, d = -.21)."

### Cao 2026 (3)

Cao, Y., Zhang, M., Gentile, F. & Bonte, M. (2026). Dynamic behavioral and neural correlates of letter-speech sound learning in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00410-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing similar-error rates in the letter task: the 16 of 31 TD participants with confusion errors "showed a significantly higher similar error rate compared to the 21 (out of 31) DD participants" (d = .86).

> "the 16 (out of 31) TD participants with confusion errors on the letter task, showed a significantly higher similar error rate compared to the 21 (out of 31) DD participants with confusion errors on this task (t(35) = 2.604, p = .013, d = .86)."
