q2 · i? · associational · r2

Questionnaire findings from the national study; the table shows stress selected by 516 (55%) and anxiety by 504 (53%). Students attributed anxiety and stress to factors including feeling loss of control, isolation and lack of motivation.

"Over 50% of the questionnaire respondents identified “stress’, “depression”, “lack of motivation”, “mental health”, and “anxiety” as key challenges they faced while studying online during the lockdown."
