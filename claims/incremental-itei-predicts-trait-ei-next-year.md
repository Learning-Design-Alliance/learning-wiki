## Evidence

### Ana Costa 2023

Ana Costa, Luísa Faria. (2023). Implicit Theories of Emotional Intelligence and Students’ Emotional and Academic Outcomes. Psychological Reports. https://doi.org/10.1177/00332941231183327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis of three-wave longitudinal survey data from 222 Portuguese secondary students, using maximum-likelihood estimation in AMOS. The final model showed that "ITEIs in 10th grade (T1) positively predicted students’ perceptions of competence" for trait EI dimensions in 11th grade; standardized path coefficients were significant at p < .05.

> "The results indicated that ITEIs in 10th grade (T1) positively predicted students’ perceptions of competence in perceiving and understanding emotion and managing and regulating emotion (trait EI, T2; see Figure 3)"
