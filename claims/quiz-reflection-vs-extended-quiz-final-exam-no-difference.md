## Evidence

### Clinton 2018

Virginia Clinton. (2018). Reflections versus Extended Quizzes: Which is Better for Student Learning and Self-Regulation? Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v18i1.22508

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on cumulative final exam scores (40 multiple-choice items) for the 112 students who chose to take the optional final, covarying unit exam performance. The article reports "Again, there was no difference between conditions on exam performance, F(1, 110) = .20, p = .65".

> "a one-way ANCOVA was conducted with condition as the independent variable, the number of items answered correctly on the final exam as the dependent variable, and number of items answered correctly on the three previous unit exams as the dependent variable. Again, there was no difference between conditions on exam performance, F(1, 110) = .20, p = .65"
