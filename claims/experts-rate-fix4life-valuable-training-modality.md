## Evidence

### Rahimi A. Masie 2026

Rahimi A. Masie, van Emden Michael, Hardon Sem F., Simons Maarten, Bonjer H. Jaap, Horeman Tim, Daams Freek. (2026). Assessing the realism and face validity of Fix For Life: an embalmed human cadaver model for high-fidelity laparoscopic training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12550-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Face validation by ten board-certified surgeons who supervised the procedures and reviewed edited procedure videos. For the appendectomy, "The value as a training modality was rated as “Very Good”" (median 5.00, IQR 1.00); the cholecystectomy value was also scored Very Good.

> "The value as a training modality was rated as “Very Good” (5.00 Median, IQR 1.00)."

### Rahimi A. Masie 2026 (2)

Rahimi A. Masie, van Emden Michael, Hardon Sem F., Simons Maarten, Bonjer H. Jaap, Horeman Tim, Daams Freek. (2026). Assessing the realism and face validity of Fix For Life: an embalmed human cadaver model for high-fidelity laparoscopic training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12550-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Friedman comparison of expert ratings across the three procedures found one nominally significant difference for lifelike manipulation of tissue (p = 0.019), but "there were no significant differences" after Bonferroni adjustment.

> "demonstrated significant differences for lifelike manipulation of tissue (p = 0.019), however when performing the sub-analysis with a Bonferroni adjustment for multiple testing, there were no significant differences"
