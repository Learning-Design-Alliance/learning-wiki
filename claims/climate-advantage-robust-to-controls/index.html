Evidence¶

Voight 2013¶

Voight, A., Austin, G., and Hanson, T. (2013). A climate for academic success: How school climate distinguishes schools that are beating the achievement odds (Full Report). San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559741

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Multivariate regression models of 12 school climate outcomes with BTO and CU indicators, controlling five student-demographic and six personnel-resources variables; the report states differences "remained large and significant" after adjustment.

"These differences were attenuated, but remained large and significant, even after adjusting for schools’ student characteristics and personnel resources."
