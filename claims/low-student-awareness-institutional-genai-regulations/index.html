Lasse Bischof 2026¶

Lasse Bischof, Eva-Maria Schön, Maria Rauschenberger, Michael Neumann. (2026). Knowing the Rules Is Not Enough: Student Regulatory Awareness and Use of GenAI in Higher Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12436 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.12436

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive analysis of an anonymous online survey of 151 undergraduates in BIS and EGOV programs, conducted November–December 2024. The article reports "only 39 students (25.83%)" were aware of the regulations and "94 students (62.25%)" were not; program-level breakdowns showed only minor differences.

"Across the full sample of 151 respondents, only 39 students (25.83%) reported that they were aware of such regulations. In contrast, 94 students (62.25%) stated that they were not aware of the regulations, while 18 respondents (11.92%) abstained from answering this question."
