## Evidence

### Ajredini 2013

Ajredini, F., Izairi, N., Zajkov, O. (2013). Real Experiments versus PhET Simulations for Understanding Electrostatic Charging. European J of Physics Education, 5(1). https://ejpe.phywe.de

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Overall conclusion of the three-group quasi-experiment comparing Real (n=86) and Sim (n=81) groups against a traditionally taught control group (n=58) on a paper-and-pencil conceptual test. The article states "there is no significant difference" between the two experimental approaches.

> "This research shows that there is no significant difference between the knowledge acquired through learning supported by real experiments and the one through learning supported by computer simulated experiments."
