## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Logistic regression models built separately for each course ranged from failed to poor predictive accuracy (AUC 0.51–0.69), despite statistically significant omnibus tests. [→ Orr Robin M. 2020](#orr-robin-m-2020)

## Evidence

### Orr Robin M. 2020

Orr Robin M., Cohen Bruce S., Allison Stephen C., Bulathsinhala Lakmini, Zambraski Edward J., Jaffrey Mark. (2020). Models to predict injury, physical fitness failure and attrition in recruit training: a retrospective cohort study. Military Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40779-020-00260-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression modelling in the retrospective cohort, with eight predictor variables (age, sex, height, weight, BMI, initial MSFT, sit-ups, push-ups), evaluated by ROC/AUC. The models retained "1 to 6 predictors" with "AUCs ... ranging from 0.51 ... to 0.69". Nagelkerke R2 values were low (0.01–0.10); all Hosmer-Lemeshow tests were nonsignificant.

> "The models created for each course separately retained 1 to 6 predictors, with AUCs for the associated ROC curves ranging from 0.51 (predicting attrition in the 80-day course) to 0.69 (predicting stress fracture in the 80-day course). All models were statistically significant with omnibus tests of coefficients ≤0.028."
