## Evidence

### D. J. Webb 2012

D. J. Webb. (2012). A class where qualitative discussions, coming weeks before computationally complicated practice, helps students' problem solving abilities. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1210.3386](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1210.3386)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled comparison of two sections with the same instructor, textbook, homework, lecture questions, discussion questions, and labs, differing only in content organization. The exam was written by other instructors and blind to the author; the analysis controlled for GPA, entering FCI, and previous physics classes, finding an "exam gain by the treatment group (section I)" of 6.2 ± 2.3 of 120 points.

> "the treatment group performed better on the final exam. The exam gain by the treatment group (section I) was found to be 6.2 ± 2.3 points out of the 120 total points on the final exam."
