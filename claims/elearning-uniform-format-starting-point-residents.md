Qualitative template analysis of semi-structured interviews with GP residents and clinical teachers in the mixed-methods study. Residents reported the content was "uniform and set a basic level for everyone" and provided a starting point for learning.

> "The content provided by the E-learning programme was considered to be uniform and set a basic level for everyone. GP residents indicated to be easily overwhelmed by the many textbooks that are available for traditional education and often did not know where to start."
