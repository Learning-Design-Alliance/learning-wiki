Evidence¶

Liu YJ 2026¶

Liu YJ, Ma HZ, Luo HY, Xiong YX, Qu FW, Xie JP, Guo Y. (2026). From traditional classroom to AI-enhanced flipped classroom: a three-year pedagogical evolution for international students in pharmacology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09608-7

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Three-year quasi-experiment with 68 international clinical medicine undergraduates assigned by academic year (2023 n=21 traditional, 2024 n=16 flipped, 2025 n=31 AI-enhanced). Classroom interaction was recorded via a real-time response system; the AI-enhanced group showed "significantly higher" response rates on both contrasts.

"the response rate in the AI-enhanced flipped classroom group was significantly higher than that in the traditional classroom group ( p < 0.05) and the flipped classroom group (p < 0.01)"
