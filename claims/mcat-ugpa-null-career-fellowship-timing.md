## Evidence

### Bills James L. 2015

Bills James L., VanHouten Jacob, Grundy Michelle M., Chalkley Roger, Dermody Terence S. (2015). Validity of the Medical College Admission Test for predicting MD–PhD student outcomes. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9609-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression analyses across all 153 graduates found "no significant association of either MCAT score or uGPA with career outcome for any cohort" (p = 0.30–0.91); the Results section likewise reports no significant associations for fellowships, time-to-defense, Step I, or Step III. No effect sizes were printed; equivalence was not tested.
