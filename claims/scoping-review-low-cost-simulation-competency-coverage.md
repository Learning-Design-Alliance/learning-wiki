The review's competency synthesis reports that "Thirty-one studies (79.5%) focused on specific areas", with neonatal, paediatric and obstetric areas the largest group and transversal skills addressed by eight studies. Most studies addressed obstetric and neonatal emergency management and resuscitation (n=10, 25.6%).

> "Thirty-one studies (79.5%) focused on specific areas: 11 (28.2%) on critical care and emergency, and 16 (41%) on neonatal, paediatric and gynaecological-obstetric areas. Additionally, eight studies (20.5%) covered transversal skills applicable across multiple settings."
