## Evidence

### Guerrero 2023

Guerrero, J. G., Rosales, N. S., & Castro, G. M. T. (2023). Impact of high‐fidelity simulation exposure of nursing students with their objective structured clinical examination: A quasi‐experimental study. Nursing Open, 10, 765–772. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1343

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi‐experimental comparison using total population sampling of 192 female BSN students across four courses at one college; the HFS group's combined midterm and final OSCE mean was 92.58 (8.40) versus 82.66 (7.66) without HFS, and the 'mean difference between the groups of nursing students was 9.92%' with a p‐value of less than .01.

> "The overall mean (SD) midterm and final OSCE results of the group of nursing students with HFS exposure and those without HFS exposure were 92.58 (8.40) and 82.66 (7.66), respectively, and the mean difference between the groups of nursing students was 9.92% supported with the p‐value of less than .01."
