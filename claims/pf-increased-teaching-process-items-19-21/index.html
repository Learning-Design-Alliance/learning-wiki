Evidence¶

Safak 2016¶

Safak, P., Yilmaz, H. C., Demiryurek, P., & Dogus, M. (2016). The effect of performance feedback provided to student-teachers working with multiple disabilities. European Journal of Educational Research, 5(3), 109-123. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.5.3.109

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Item-level TSOF analysis of Item 19 across 11 student teachers' video-recorded lessons; descriptive counts reported without a test statistic.

"While only 7 student teachers performed the Item 19, which says “presents in line with predetermined teaching method,” before the feedback, all the student teachers lectured in compliance with the teaching method determined in the plan after getting feedback."

Safak 2016 (2)¶

Safak, P., Yilmaz, H. C., Demiryurek, P., & Dogus, M. (2016). The effect of performance feedback provided to student-teachers working with multiple disabilities. European Journal of Educational Research, 5(3), 109-123. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.5.3.109

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Item-level TSOF analysis of Item 21 across 11 student teachers' video-recorded lessons; descriptive counts reported without a test statistic.

"While before the PF, neither student teacher checked other students while engaging a student one to one, after the PF, 6 student teachers ensured the active participation of other students during one -to-one engagement or instructing the assistant student teacher."
