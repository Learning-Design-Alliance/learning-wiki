Evidence¶

Cowhy 2023¶

Cowhy, J.R., Gordon, M.F., & de la Torre, M. (2023). Forced to choose: Lessons learned from families of students within special education. Educational Policy. https://doi.org/10.1177/08959048231198814

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview findings from 29 parents whose schools closed in 2013 report that "many parents of students with IEPs did not have viable options to engage in school choice." Parents perceived the short list from school- or district-based officials, including the welcoming school, as the only schools available.

"Yet many parents of students with IEPs did not have viable options to engage in school choice."
