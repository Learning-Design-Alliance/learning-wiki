## Evidence

### Amogh Sirnoorkar and N. Sanjay Rebello 2026

Amogh Sirnoorkar and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Feedback That Clicks: Introductory Physics Students’ Valued Features in AI Feedback Generated From Self-Crafted and Engineered Prompts. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/v3pj-8491](https://doi.org/10.1103/v3pj-8491)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spreadsheet-based trend analysis of students’ rankings of the three feedback types; the article reports "a substantial section (91.1%) of students preferred feedback generated using structured prompts (combined first choices across B and C)".

> "Across 1235 responses, around 61.4% of students ranked Feedback C as the most useful, followed by B (29.7%) and A (8.9%)."

### Amogh Sirnoorkar and N. Sanjay Rebello 2026 (2)

Amogh Sirnoorkar and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Feedback That Clicks: Introductory Physics Students’ Valued Features in AI Feedback Generated From Self-Crafted and Engineered Prompts. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/v3pj-8491](https://doi.org/10.1103/v3pj-8491)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second-choice analysis given first choice (Fig. 5); among students ranking A first, two-thirds chose C next, and among those ranking B first, 90% chose A as second preference.

> "Finally, 95.5% of students who preferred Feedback C, selected B as their second choice followed by mere 4.5% for A."
