## Evidence

### Hesser 2016

Hesser, T. L., & Gregory, J. L. (2016). Instructional Support Sessions in Chemistry: Alternative to Remediation. Journal of Developmental Education, 39(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130190

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of mean correct responses on the 20 open-ended final exam questions, analyzed without the assumption of equal variances: underprepared M = 15.62 (SD = 4.88, N = 69) vs college-ready M = 14.00 (SD = 6.29, N = 249), t(137.1) = 2.294, p < .05. Results were recorded on a dichotomous scale for consistency.

> "students identified as underprepared had a significantly higher mean percent correct than college-ready students"
