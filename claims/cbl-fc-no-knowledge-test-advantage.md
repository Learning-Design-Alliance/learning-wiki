Hu Bibi, Wang Li, Wu Junxia, Zhu Lina, Chen Zhida. (2024). A combination of case-based learning with flipped classroom improved performance of medical students in nephrology bedside teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05973-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized controlled study of 92 nephrology-rotation students, the post-intervention general knowledge multiple-choice test showed no statistically significant difference between groups; the article reports only means and a P value, with no effect size.

> "In the general knowledge test, the average score in the CBL/FC group was 94.07 ± 4.86, while the score was 93.04 ± 5.33 in the LBT group, revealing a similar performance in both groups (P > 0.05, Fig. 3A)."
