Proceedings of the 12th International Conference of the Learning Sciences (ICLS 2016). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06963](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06963)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Thematic analysis of video records, field notes, student artifacts and interviews from the first modeling cycle, in which students measured Lego Mindstorms robots moving at constant speed. The class critiqued a video of one group's measurement, and the discussion "led to the first student talk about error".

> "The ensuing discussion led to the first student talk about error: “It’s not that the robot was moving differently, it’s that we were making mistakes!”"
