## Evidence

### Sarican 2018

Sarican, G., Akgunduz, D. (2018). The impact of integrated STEM education on academic achievement, reflective thinking skills towards problem solving and permanence in learning in science education. Cypriot Journal of Educational Sciences, 13(1), 94-113. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1196043

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent t-test on AAT permanence-test scores one year after the last test in the 44-student quasi-experiment: t = −1.569, p = 0.124. The article states "the group with integrated STEM education received higher scores than the constructivist approach group" but the difference was not significant.

> "Since the p value was 0.124 ( p > 0.05), there was no significant difference between the two groups’ AAT PT scores."
