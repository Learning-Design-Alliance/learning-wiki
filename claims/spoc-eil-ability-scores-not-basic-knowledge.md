## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The EIL group scored significantly higher than the LBL group on the ability analysis test and total scores, while basic knowledge scores did not differ significantly. [→ Wang Siqi 2025](#wang-siqi-2025)
`q3 i?` Pass rates on the ability knowledge test (55% vs 35.9%) and total scores (82.5% vs 56.4%) favored the EIL group over the LBL group. [→ Wang Siqi 2025 (2)](#wang-siqi-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Wang Siqi 2025

Wang Siqi, Yang Di, Zhao Wenbo, Yan Xiaoyuan, Zhang Xiyue, Gao Wenqing, Guo Jiajie. (2025). The combination of a SPOC with evaluative interactive learning in dental adhesion education: a comparison with a lecture-based learning mode. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07261-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Written examination one week after class in the randomized study (n=79): 10 objective questions (50 points) and 3 ability analysis questions (50 points). Basic knowledge scores "did not differ significantly"; ability and total scores were significantly greater in EIL.

> "The basic knowledge scores obtained by students in the LBL group and those in the EIL group did not differ significantly (Fig. 4A). However, the ability knowledge test and total scores obtained by the students in the EIL group were significantly greater than those obtained by the students in the LBL group were"

### Wang Siqi 2025 (2)

Wang Siqi, Yang Di, Zhao Wenbo, Yan Xiaoyuan, Zhang Xiyue, Gao Wenqing, Guo Jiajie. (2025). The combination of a SPOC with evaluative interactive learning in dental adhesion education: a comparison with a lecture-based learning mode. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07261-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pass-rate analysis in the same randomized study: EIL pass rates of "55%" on the ability knowledge test and "82.5%" on total scores exceeded LBL's 35.9% and 56.4%; basic knowledge pass rates were 85% vs 82%.

> "the EIL group achieved values of 55% and 82.5%, respectively, both of which exceeded the corresponding values for the LBL group (i.e., 35.9% and 56.4%, respectively"
