Same 60-article review. The seven factors printed with article counts include self-concept (n=7), depressive symptoms/low mood (n=7), anxiety symptoms (n=5), resilience/mental toughness (n=3), coping (n=5), attitudes towards school (n=9), and loneliness (n=2).
