`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the null cluster-RCT findings: the PA arms showed no significant differences from placebo on any outcome. The article states the substitution "was neither detrimental to children's cognitive performance nor academic achievement".

> "substituting academic classes with 2 weekly PA sessions was neither detrimental to children's cognitive performance nor academic achievement, but at the same time, reduced sedentary behavior and raised consciousness for an active and healthy lifestyle"
