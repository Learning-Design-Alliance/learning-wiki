Evidence¶

Janos Perczel 2025¶

Janos Perczel, Jin Chow, and Dorottya Demszky. (2025). TeachLM: Post-Training LLMs for Education Using Authentic Learning Data. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05087 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2510.05087

q2 · i? · design · r2

Automated multi-turn evaluations (Fig. 7) of fine-tuned Gemini 2.5 Flash and GPT-4o-2024-08-06 against the human training-data reference line. The article reports that "fine-tuning improves model performance on every benchmark," with simpler conversational metrics converging faster than complex pedagogical ones; no effect sizes are printed.

"We see that fine-tuning improves model performance on every benchmark, bringing the resulting values closer to the human training data benchmarks."

Related Claims¶

  • A fine-tuned student model paired with GPT-4 produces conversational statistics closer to human-AI dialogues than two prompt-engineered base models — related
  • Frontier LLMs achieve the best balance between detecting misconceptions in correct-answer cases and not over-flagging correct students, while fine-tuned local models achieve under 58% TM recall — related
  • Education-oriented instruction tuning consistently improves curriculum grounding, K-12 problem solving, and pedagogical tutoring at every evaluated model scale — related
