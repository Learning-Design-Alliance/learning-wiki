Validity analysis of the measurement model in the 533-student sample; Table 7 reports the offloading–critical thinking Pearson correlation as −.345***, and HTMT values ranged between 0.514 and 0.755, below the 0.90 threshold, supporting distinct constructs. No standardized effect size is printed in the quoted finding sentence, so no magnitude is asserted here.
