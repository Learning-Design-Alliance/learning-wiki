Gholam Reza Haji Pour Nezhad & Nasrin Shokrpour. (2012). The Impact of Task Type and Cognitive Style on Vocabulary Learning. English Language Teaching, 5(9). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n9p17

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Tukey HSD comparisons among the four subgroups of the randomized controlled study showed significant differences within the Dynamic group (p = .002) and within the Static group (p = .000); the article also reports FI students marginally better than FD students overall (27.977 vs 27.357).

> "FD dynamic students performed significantly better than FI dynamic students, who, in turn, performed significantly better than FD Static students."
