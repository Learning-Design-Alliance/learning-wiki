### Gutersohn Caterina 2025

Gutersohn Caterina, Schweingruber Sandra, Haudenschild Maximilian, Huber Markus, Greif Robert, Fuchs Alexander. (2025). Self-directed learning versus traditional instructor-led learning for education on a new anaesthesia workstation: a noninferiority, randomised, controlled trial. BJA: British Journal of Anaesthesia 135: 990-996. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bja.2025.03.043

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat analysis of the randomised trial's primary endpoint at the objective structured clinical examination-style assessment station: '100% of participants (n=108/108, 95% confidence interval [95% CI]: 96.6–100%) passed the test' in the instructor-guided group versus 99.1% (n=113/114) in the self-directed group.

> "In the instructor-guided group, 100% of participants (n=108/108, 95% confidence interval [95% CI]: 96.6–100%) passed the test vs 99.1% of participants (n=113/114, 95% CI: 95.2–100%) in the self-directed group."

Self-directed learning versus traditional instructor-led learning for education on a new anaesthesia workstation: a noninferiority, randomised, controlled trial. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Noninferiority test of the primary outcome in the randomised trial: the success-rate 'difference in the success rate between the instructor-guided and self-directed group was −0.9% (90% CI: −3.8%–1.7%)', within the prespecified margin, demonstrating noninferiority.
