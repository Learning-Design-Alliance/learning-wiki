# Teacher empathy shows no consistent relationship with classroom management or instructional support

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In seven of ten studies there was no statistically significant relationship between teachers' empathy and classroom management (83% of effects not significant). [→ Karen Aldrup · Bastian Carstensen · Uta Klusmann 2022](#karen-aldrup-bastian-carstensen-uta-klusmann-2022)
`q3 i?` Instructional support showed similarly weak links, consistent with the review's theoretical argument that these domains depend more on specific knowledge than on empathy. [→ Karen Aldrup · Bastian Carstensen · Uta Klusmann 2022 (2)](#karen-aldrup-bastian-carstensen-uta-klusmann-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Karen Aldrup · Bastian Carstensen · Uta Klusmann 2022

Karen Aldrup · Bastian Carstensen · Uta Klusmann. (2022). Is Empathy the Key to Effective Teaching? A Systematic Review of Its Association with Teacher-Student Interactions and Student Outcomes. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09649-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Vote-counting synthesis: 83% of classroom-management effects were not statistically significant. Exceptions occurred mainly when both predictor and outcome were teacher self-reported, e.g., Hu et al. (2018) found self-reported emotional perception linked to self-reported (r = .38) but not observer-rated management (r = .03).

> "In seven of ten studies spanning early childhood to tertiary education, there was no statistically significant relationship between teachers’ empathy and classroom management"

### Karen Aldrup · Bastian Carstensen · Uta Klusmann 2022 (2)

Karen Aldrup · Bastian Carstensen · Uta Klusmann. (2022). Is Empathy the Key to Effective Teaching? A Systematic Review of Its Association with Teacher-Student Interactions and Student Outcomes. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09649-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Theoretical and empirical synthesis: the review argues classroom management and instructional support also require classroom management and pedagogical content knowledge, so empathy should play a less central role than for emotional support; the printed results (Table 5) support this weaker expectation.

> "classroom management includes several facets that go beyond empathy, for example, productive use of time and establishment of rules"
