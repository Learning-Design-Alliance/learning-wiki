## Evidence

### Radkowitsch A 2020

Radkowitsch A, Fischer MR, Schmidmaier R, Fischer F. (2020). Learning to diagnose collaboratively: validating a simulation for medical students. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001344](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001344)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

One-sample t-tests contrasting the mean of two measurement times against a threshold of 3.0 among practitioners with high prior knowledge (N=25). The article reports means of 3.89 and 3.57 and the printed t values with p<.01; no effect size is printed.

> "Both authenticity ratings are significantly above the threshold of 3 (t(24)=4.9, p<.01 and t(24)=3.14, p<.01)."
