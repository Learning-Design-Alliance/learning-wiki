`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group pre-post comparison on the 50-question quiz in the randomized controlled trial: "The improvement in the e-learning group was a mean of 3.2 points (range 2.0-4.3) and of 2.1 points (range 1.2-3.1) in the video group".

> "Both learning methods had a positive impact on knowledge acquisition as both groups improved their scores. The improvement in the e-learning group was a mean of 3.2 points (range 2.0-4.3) and of 2.1 points (range 1.2-3.1) in the video group."
