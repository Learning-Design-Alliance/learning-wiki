### Adrienne L. Traxler 2020

Adrienne L. Traxler, Tyme Suda, Eric Brewe, and Kelley Commeford. (2020). Network positions in active learning environments in physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reciprocity statistics from Table II of the network descriptive results. ISLE reciprocity rises from 0.50 to 0.93; context-rich problems from 0.42 to 0.62; the text reports "the week 1 reciprocity rate is lower at 50%, but in week 10 is the highest, at 90%" for ISLE.

> "In ISLE, the week 1 reciprocity rate is lower at 50%, but in week 10 is the highest, at 90%. Context-rich problems show a smaller increase, with the lowest starting reciprocity but a week 10 value comparable to Peer Instruction and SCALE-UP."
