Evidence¶

Chen Minjian 2018¶

Chen Minjian, Ni Chunhui, Hu Yanhui, Wang Meilin, Liu Lu, Ji Xiaoming, Chu Haiyan, Wu Wei, Lu Chuncheng, Wang Shouyu, Wang Shoulin, Zhao Liping, Li Zhong, Zhu Huijuan, Wang Jianming, Xia Yankai, Wang Xinru. (2018). Meta-analysis on the effectiveness of team-based learning on medical education in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1179-1

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis pooling theoretical examination scores from 13 studies (1545 participants) comparing TBL with LBL in Chinese medical education, using a random-effects model. The pooled estimate was "SMD = 2.46, 95% CI: 1.53–3.40" with very high heterogeneity (I2 = 98.0%).

"We found that TBL significantly increased students’ examination scores when compared with LBL in random-effects model (SMD = 2.46, 95% CI: 1.53–3.40, I2 = 98.0%, Pheterogeneity < 0.001)"
