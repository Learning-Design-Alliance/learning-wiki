# Systematic role rotation structured pre-service educational psychologists' engagement in AI-supported case-based learning by distributing problem-framing, analytical, intervention-oriented, and reflective responsibilities

## Evidence

### Kenzhebayeva Z 2026

Kenzhebayeva Z, Matayev B, Sarsembayeva E, Kolyukh O, Assenova N and Ospanova A (2026) Designing an AI-integrated role-rotation pedagogical model to support competence-related learning in pre-service educational psychologists. Front. Educ. 11:1905781. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1905781

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reflexive thematic analysis of four triangulated qualitative datasets (62 journals, 18 interviews, 24 field records, 62 product sets) from an eight-week DBR intervention with 62 undergraduates. The results section reports that "role rotation emerged as a mechanism that structured participants' engagement" by distributing responsibilities; each role elicited a distinct form of engagement.

> "role rotation emerged as a mechanism that structured participants' engagement in AI-supported case-based learning by distributing problem-framing, analytical, intervention-oriented, and reflective responsibilities across successive learning cycles."
