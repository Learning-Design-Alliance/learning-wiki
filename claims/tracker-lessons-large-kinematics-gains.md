## Evidence

### Loo Kang WEE 2015

Loo Kang WEE, Kim Kia TAN, Tze Kwang LEONG, & Ching TAN. (2015). Using Tracker to understand 'toss up' and free fall motion: a case study. Physics Education, 50(4), 436-442. [doi:10.1088/0031-9120/50/4/436](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9120/50/4/436)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case study of N=123 express-pure physics students in three classes, each co-taught by a pair of teachers, using an 8-item multiple-choice pre-posttest. The article reports "Cohen's effect size d = 0.79" and "< g > total = 0.42", interpreted as large effect and medium gain above the traditional baseline.

> "We found within experimental group gains with Cohen's effect size d = 0.79 0.23 (large effect) and normalized gains with a gradient of < g > total = 0.42 0.08 (medium gain) above the traditional baseline value"
