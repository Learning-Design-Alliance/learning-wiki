## Evidence

### Janeczko 2026

Janeczko, J. M., Lehmkuhl, A., Junker, R., Zucker, V., Meschede, N., & Holodynski, M. (2026). Promoting preservice teachers' multiperspective professional vision in virtual learning environments – On the effects of modeling videos and prompts during video-based lesson analysis. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10546-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Process data from the module on interrelationships between classroom management and instructional support showed that the enriched VLE group needed fewer practice analyses to reach the 66% agreement threshold and achieved a higher efficiency score (M = 6.31 vs. M = 5.79).

> "the IG+MP worked on significantly fewer video analyses with a closed response format (M = 6.83, SD = 0.87) than the IG–MP (M = 7.14, SD = 0.96), with nine analyses being the maximum, t(102) = 1.71, p =.045."
