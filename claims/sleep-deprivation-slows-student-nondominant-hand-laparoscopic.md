### Bereuter Jean-Paul 2026

Bereuter Jean-Paul, Fries Alexa, Grummt Isabell, Geissler Mark Enrik, Geissler Rona Berit, Schmidt Sofia, Buck Nathalie, Weiß Juliane, Krause-Jüttler Grit, Weitz Jürgen, Distler Marius, Oehme Florian, von Bechtolsheim Felix. (2026). Impact of acute sleep deprivation on laparoscopic performance: a prospective, randomized crossover trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12872-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized crossover trial in the Surgical Skills Lab Dresden: 55 proficiency-trained students performed three tasks sleep-deprived and rested, measured with a ForceSense device. Mean non-dominant-hand speed fell in all tasks, e.g. peg transfer "control: 3.4 cm/s vs. sleep-deprived: 3.1 cm/s, p < 0.001". No effect sizes were printed.

> "sleep-deprived participants of the student cohort, showed a significantly lower mean instrument speed of the non-dominant hand in the peg transfer task (control: 3.4 cm/s vs. sleep-deprived: 3.1 cm/s, p < 0.001), the circle cutting task (control: 2.1 cm/s vs. sleep-deprived: 2 cm/s, p = 0.01), and the suture and knot task (control: 2.2 cm/s vs. sleep-deprived: 2.1 cm/s, p = 0.029)"
