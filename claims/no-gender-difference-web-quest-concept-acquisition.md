## Subclaims

## Evidence

### AL-Edwan 2014

AL-Edwan, Zaid Suleiman. (2014). Effectiveness of Web Quest Strategy in acquiring geographic concepts among eighth grade students in Jordan. International Journal of Education and Development using Information and Communication Technology, 10(4), 31-46. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu/viewarticle.php?id=1874

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two Way ANCOVA on post-test scores of the same 119-student two-group quasi-experiment, with gender (male, female) as a second independent variable. The gender effect was non-significant; descriptive post-test means were close across gender in both groups (e.g., 19.67 vs 20.14 in the control group).

> "There are no differences with statistical significance at the significance level ( α = 0 . 0 5 ) between the means of the eighth grade students’ test scores on acquiring the geographic concepts attributed to the gender variable. F- Value reached (1.097) at significance level (0.492)."
