# Vocabulary test scores correlated positively with reading comprehension in both groups, with stronger correlations in the hierarchy group

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Immediate vocabulary scores correlated positively with reading comprehension in the experimental group (r = .608, p = .001) and the control group (r = .544, p = .002). [→ Lin 2013](#lin-2013)
`q2 i3` Delayed vocabulary scores correlated even more strongly with reading comprehension in the experimental group (r = .767, p = .000) and the control group (r = .593, p = .001). [→ Lin 2013 (2)](#lin-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Lin 2013

Lin, Ching-Ying & Hsu, Wei Shu. (2013). Effects of Hierarchy Vocabulary Exercises on English Vocabulary Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 6(9). [doi:10.5539/elt.v6n9p110](https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n9p110)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of the same 56 students' immediate vocabulary and reading comprehension scores. Both correlations were significant at the .01 level; r² was .370 in the experimental group and .296 in the control group, with "r = .608" in the experimental group.

> "the results indicated positive linear relationships  between the scores of the immediate vocabulary test and the reading comprehension test in the experimental group (r = .608, p = .001) and in the control group (r = .544, p = .002)."

### Lin 2013 (2)

Lin, Ching-Ying & Hsu, Wei Shu. (2013). Effects of Hierarchy Vocabulary Exercises on English Vocabulary Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 6(9). [doi:10.5539/elt.v6n9p110](https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n9p110)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of delayed vocabulary and reading comprehension scores. The article reports r² of .589 in the experimental group versus .352 in the control group, suggesting vocabulary scores predict reading comprehension more strongly after hierarchy exercises.

> "The re sults revealed that they we re significantly correlated in the experimental group (r = .767, p = .000) and in the control group (r = .593, p = .001). The r value was higher in the experimental group than in the control group."
