## Evidence

### Pathblazer evidence summary 2020

Pathblazer evidence summary. (2020). https://www.edgenuity.com/support/technical-requirements/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A cluster randomized controlled trial over one semester in 2019–2020 in Floyd County, Georgia, with approximately half of each 3rd–5th grade teacher's classes assigned to Pathblazer reading. The trial reported a statistically significant winter reading advantage, "controlling for fall achievement, other student characteristics, and classroom and teacher effects"; effect size +0.07 across 1,524 students. No standardized effect beyond the printed +0.07 is stated, so impact is null.

> "Pathblazer had a positive and statistically significant impact on student reading achievement in winter, controlling for fall achievement, other student characteristics, and classroom and teacher effects."
