`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive frequency analysis across the three surveyed groups. The article reports these qualities averaged one vote or less per group, and separately states "A conspicuous omission in all the efficiency records obtainable is a sense of humor."

> "Some of the qualities which received an average of one vote or less in each of the three groups were health (offset by one boy who stated that the best teacher he ever had was ill half the time), being up-to-date, well-dressed, refined, practical, definite, natural, self-confident, broad-minded, systematic, experi-enced, faithful"
