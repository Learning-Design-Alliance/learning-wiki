Moganadas SR, Marín-González F, Nun SH and Gan CL (2026) Generative AI as a didactic-pedagogical mediator: rethinking human roles and pedagogical design in higher education. Front. Educ. 11:1856839. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1856839

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article argues, within its literature synthesis, that assessment is more than a mechanism for evaluating final outcomes: it also shapes whether students use GenAI to bypass cognitive effort, support drafting and reflection, compare explanations, verify claims, or make reasoning visible. This is a conceptual claim with no empirical effect size reported.

> "Assessment design, therefore, influences the quality of learner agency in GenAI-mediated environments because it determines what forms of judgment, evidence use, revision, and disclosure are expected and rewarded."
