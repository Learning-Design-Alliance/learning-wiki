### Kafagi Abdul Rhaman; Atassi Nazih; Kafagi Abdul-Hadi 2026

Kafagi Abdul Rhaman; Atassi Nazih; Kafagi Abdul-Hadi. (2026). Performance of large language models (GPT-5.2, Gemini 3 Pro, Claude Sonnet 4.6 and Grok 4.1) on the Fellowship of The Royal College of Surgeons Urology Part A examination. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-108775

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Controlled comparative evaluation of four LLMs on 240 FRCS (Urol) Part A questions across five independent runs per model, accessed via consumer-facing interfaces. The abstract reports the printed means, SDs and 95% CIs, including that Grok 4.1 failed "with its entire CI below 74%".

> "Three of four models exceeded the indicative 74% pass threshold: Gemini 3 Pro (82.4%±0.9%; 95% CI 81.3 to 83.6%), Claude Sonnet 4.6 (79.3%±1.1%; 95% CI 77.9 to 80.6%) and GPT-5.2 (76.1%±2.4%; 95% CI 73.1 to 79.1%). Grok 4.1 failed (70.4%±0.6%; 95% CI 69.6 to 71.2%), with its entire CI below 74%."
