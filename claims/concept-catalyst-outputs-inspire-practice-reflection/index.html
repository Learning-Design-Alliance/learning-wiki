Gennie Mansi 2026¶

Gennie Mansi, Sunni Newton, Roxanne Moore, Meltem Alemdar, Mark Riedl. (2026). Concept Catalyst: Exploring Scrutable Interfaces to Structure K-12 Teacher Interactions with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30590

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Post-generation reflection findings from the WoZ study: generated scaffolds inspired new content and activities, and reading outputs led teachers to reflect on the kinds of questions they asked, existing activity designs, student-interest connections, and in-class assessments.

"P4, P6, P7, and P9 all highlighted that the learning scaffolds written by the AI system helped them think of new content to include in their classroom."

Gennie Mansi 2026 (2)¶

Gennie Mansi, Sunni Newton, Roxanne Moore, Meltem Alemdar, Mark Riedl. (2026). Concept Catalyst: Exploring Scrutable Interfaces to Structure K-12 Teacher Interactions with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30590

q2 · i? · design · r2

Author interpretation from the Discussion of the same study: most teachers began skeptical of AI, but after using the tool many wanted their students to use it too; several credited the tool with helping them practice the EDP themselves.

"The teachers in our study not only felt encouraged to use AI via Concept Catalyst, many wanted their students to use it, too—a significant shift from the initial hesitancy we observed at the start of the interview"
