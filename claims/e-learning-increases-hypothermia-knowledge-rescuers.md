Podsiadło Paweł, Kosiński Sylweriusz, Darocha Tomasz, Sałapa Kinga, Sanak Tomasz, Brugger Hermann. (2018). The Use of E-Learning in Medical Education for Mountain Rescuers Concerning Hypothermia. High Altitude Medicine & Biology. https://doi.org/10.1089/ham.2018.0050

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest–post-test comparison in a study of 187 Polish mountain rescuers (136 volunteers, 51 professionals) completing a six-lesson e-learning course; Table 1 reports post-test means of 85.5 ± 11.5 for professionals and 84.8 ± 11.4 for volunteers, with pretest/post-test p < 0.001 in both groups.
