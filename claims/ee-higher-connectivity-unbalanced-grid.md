Numerical comparison of the three themes networks (Fig. 3, Table 1). Table 1 prints mean in-degree 5.22(1) for EE versus 4.28(1) for EP and 4.14(1) for EC. The article attributes EE's richer prerequisite network to its placement of Electromagnetism and Thermodynamics in the same semester and Modern Physics immediately after Electromagnetism.
