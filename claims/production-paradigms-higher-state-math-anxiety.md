## Evidence

### Yao 2026

Yao, X., Huber, J.F., Li, Z., Findik, Y., Nuerk, H.-C. & Artemenko, C. (2026). The dynamics of state math anxiety vary by paradigm and timing during arithmetic. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00398-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LMM (Model A) on state math anxiety in the preregistered study of 65 adults solving two-digit arithmetic in three decision and three production paradigms. The main effect of paradigm was significant (p < .001), "with an estimated increase of 0.29" for production over decision paradigms; conditional R² = .87.

> "The main effect of paradigm indicates that state math anxiety is higher in production paradigms compared to decision paradigms, with an estimated increase of 0.29."

### Yao 2026 (2)

Yao, X., Huber, J.F., Li, Z., Findik, Y., Nuerk, H.-C. & Artemenko, C. (2026). The dynamics of state math anxiety vary by paradigm and timing during arithmetic. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00398-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same LMM (Model A, Table 2) testing the paradigm × trait math anxiety interaction on state math anxiety. The interaction was significant (p < .001), "by an estimate of 0.24", so higher trait math anxiety amplified the production-paradigm increase in state math anxiety.

> "The interaction of paradigm and trait math anxiety indicates that the effect of trait math anxiety on state math anxiety is larger in production than in decision paradigms, by an estimate of 0.24."
