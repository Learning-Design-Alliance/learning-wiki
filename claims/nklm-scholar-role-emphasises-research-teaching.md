## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Compared to 13 international frameworks, the NKLM Scholar role stresses research and teaching competencies while clinical application plays a comparatively subordinate role. [→ Hautz SC 2015](#hautz-sc-2015)

## Evidence

### Hautz SC 2015

Hautz SC, Hautz WE, Keller N, Feufel MA, Spies C. (2015). The scholar role in the National Competence Based Catalogues of Learning Objectives for Undergraduate Medical Education (NKLM) compared to other international frameworks. GMS German Medical Science. [doi:10.3205/000224](https://doi.org/10.3205/000224)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative text analysis comparing the NKLM Scholar role with 13 international outcome frameworks found the NKLM "betont die zum Forschen und Lehren notwendigen Kompetenzen" while clinical application is comparatively subordinate. The English abstract states the same contrast.

> "Im Unterschied zu den verglichenen Rahmenwerken betont der NKLM die zum Forschen und Lehren notwendigen Kompetenzen, während die klinische Anwendung eine vergleichsweise untergeordnete Rolle spielt."
