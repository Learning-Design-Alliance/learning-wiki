## Evidence

### Erdoğan 2016

Yavuz Erdoğan. (2016). An Investigation of the Effectiveness of Concept Mapping on Turkish Students' Academic Success. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 4(6). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i6.1380

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis combining 71 experimental studies with control groups conducted in Turkey between 2000 and 2015; random effects model applied after heterogeneity was found. The article reports "ES=1,119, z=15,019, p<0,05", a very large effect per Thalheimer & Cook's classification.

> "The resu lts indicate that concept mapping instructional strategy has a positive effect on the Tur kish students’ academic success (ES=1,119, z=15,019, p<0,05)."
