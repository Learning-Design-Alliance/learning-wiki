## Evidence

### Pardos 2019

Pardos, Z. A., & Horodyskyj, L. (2019). Analysis of Student Behaviour in Habitable Worlds Using Continuous Representation Visualization. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.18608/jla.2019.61.1](https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2019.61.1)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of clickstream sequences from 778 students in two offerings of Habitable Worlds, visualized via a skip-gram model trained with pass/no-pass prefixed elements and reduced with t-SNE (Figure 6). The visualization shows that "Passing students show a linear progression through content" while failing students converge on quiz clusters. No effect sizes are reported; patterns are qualitative.

> "Passing students show a linear progression through content, with quizzes/applications appearing sequentially after their associated training activities. Failing students show a hub-and-spoke pattern for most units, with training activities converging onto a cluster of quiz/application activities for that unit."
