## Evidence

### Lu et al. 2016

Xiaoye Lu, Xin Cheng, Ke Li, Kenneth Ka Ho Lee & Xuesong Yang. (2016). Integration of Histology Lectures and Practical Teaching in China. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(4). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n4p157

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of end-of-semester histology exam scores between 24 volunteer integrated-course participants and 45 traditionally taught controls, graded by the same teachers on the same papers; the article reports the difference as significant (p=0.012), with "75 ± 3.46 and 63.96 ± 2.53".

> "The results showed that the average test scores for participants of the integrated teachin g and non -participants were 75 ± 3.46 and 63.96 ± 2.53, respectively."
