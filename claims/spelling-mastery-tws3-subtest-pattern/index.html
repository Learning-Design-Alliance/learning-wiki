tws3-subtests¶

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2014, January). Students with Learning Disabilities intervention report: Spelling Mastery. Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/579

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Supplemental subtest analysis within the Darch et al. (2006) RCT of 42 students. Appendix D prints a TWS-3 Predictable Words mean difference of 3.24 (effect size 0.57, p = 0.04 as reported) and an Unpredictable Words mean difference of 0.71 (effect size 0.11, p = 0.16); after the WWC difference-in-differences adjustment the Predictable Words result was not statistically significant.

"A difference-in-differences adjustment was needed and resulted in a WWC-computed p-value of 0.07 for the TWS Predictable Words subtest; therefore, the WWC does not find the result to be statistically significant."
