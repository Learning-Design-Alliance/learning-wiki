### Eric Brewe 2012

Eric Brewe, Laird Kramer, and Vashti Sawtelle. (2012). Investigating student communities with network analysis of interactions in a physics learning center. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 8, 010101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.8.010101

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Hierarchical multiple regression models 3–6 added time per week, ethnicity, introductory course type, and gender; z tests showed none significantly improved the correlation over model 2 (e.g., time per week improved it by only 0.03 standard deviations). No effect sizes are printed for these null results.

> "This pattern indicates that the other variables (gender, ethnicity, intro- ductory course type) do not improve prediction of central- ity over the days per week in PLC and major variables."
