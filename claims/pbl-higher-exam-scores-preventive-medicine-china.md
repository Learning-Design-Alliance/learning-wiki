## Evidence

### Ding Xiaojie 2014

Ding Xiaojie, Zhao Liping, Chu Haiyan, Tong Na, Ni Chunhui, Hu Zhibin, Zhang Zhengdong, Wang Meilin. (2014). Assessing the Effectiveness of Problem-Based Learning of Preventive Medicine Education in China. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep05126

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis pooling 15 studies involving 2,061 subjects published 2006–2013 comparing PBL with LBL in preventive medicine education in China. The pooled estimate showed "a significant increase in students' examination scores" with SMD = 0.62 (95% CI 0.41–0.83) and substantial heterogeneity (I² = 80.1%).

> "When all the eligible data were pooled into the meta-analysis, PBL was associated with a significant increase in students' examination scores (SMD = 0.62, 95% CI = 0.41–0.83, Pheterogeneity < 0.001)"
