## Evidence

### Ji Y 2026

Ji Y, Zhang X, Han X, Xiong G, Chen X, Lu Y, Zhang Q, Sui S, Yang Z, Yu S, Xu W, Zheng G, Zhang W, Liu Y, Xin J. (2026). Enhancing nuclear medicine residency standardization training: a comprehensive exploration of integrating a flipped classroom and case-based learning for effective instruction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09808-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course feedback questionnaires (Forms B and C) completed by 36 of 40 trainees. Universal satisfaction was reported, with lower consensus on pre-class study time appropriateness (4.38 ± 0.31) and broader knowledge acquisition (72.22% agreement).

> "Students universally expressed satisfaction with the instructional model of “flipped classroom combined with CBL.” High levels of agreement were observed regarding satisfaction with the teaching faculty’s competence. However, there was relatively lower consensus on two aspects: the appropriateness of the “length of pre-class study time” and the acknowledgment that “students consulted more than 5 literature references before class.”"
