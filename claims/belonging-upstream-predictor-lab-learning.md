## Evidence

### Marcus Kubsch 2026

Marcus Kubsch, Natasha G. Holmes, Antti Lehtinen. (2026). Multidimensional Profiles of Critical Thinking in Physics Labs: Latent Structure, Instructional Change, and Connections to Physics Identity. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.11598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.11598)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lagged panel model (lavaan, MLR estimation) on pre/post measures of belonging, recognition, self-efficacy, agency, and Profile 2 posterior membership probability. The article reports standardized cross-lagged coefficients of β=.107 (recognition), β=.102 (self-efficacy), β=.068 (agency), and β=.071 (profile membership), all p< .001; no standardized effect size beyond these path coefficients is printed.

> "Pre-belonging significantly predicted post-recognition (β=.107,p< .001), post-self-efficacy (β=.102,p< .001), post-agency (β=.068,p< .001), and post-Profile 2 membership (β=.071,p< .001). Belonging thus exerted broad prospective influence across both identity-related and epistemic outcomes."
