`q2 i?` Positive correlations were found between academic achievement (language and mathematics) and all executive functions — inhibition, cognitive flexibility and working memory — in both boys and girls. [→ Visier-Alfonso ME 2020](#visier-alfonso-me-2020)

## Evidence

### Visier-Alfonso ME 2020

Visier-Alfonso ME, Sánchez-López M, Martínez-Vizcaíno V, Jiménez-López E, Redondo-Tébar A, Nieto-López M. (2020). Executive functions mediate the relationship between cardiorespiratory fitness and academic achievement in Spanish schoolchildren aged 8 to 11 years. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231246

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Partial correlation analysis by gender, controlling for age and mother educational level, found "positive correlations were found between academic achievement and all executive functions" in both boys and girls, covering inhibition, cognitive flexibility and working memory. The text says all p < 0.001, but Table 2 marks boys' working memory with language achievement (r = .220) at p < 0.05 only.

> "In addition, positive correlations were found between academic achievement and all executive functions in both boys and girls (all p < 0.001)."
