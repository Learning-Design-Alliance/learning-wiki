# SFAILQ shows scalar measurement invariance across adolescents, young adults, and middle-aged adults

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Multi-group CFA supported configural, metric, and scalar invariance across the three age groups (12–17, 18–40, 41–60), with scalar ΔCFI = 0.008 and ΔRMSEA = 0.007 within thresholds. [→ Liu Qingqi 2026](#liu-qingqi-2026)

## Evidence

### Liu Qingqi 2026

Liu Qingqi, Miao Wenjiao, Li Jingjing, Lei Yuju. (2026). The Six-Facet Artificial Intelligence Literacy Questionnaire (SFAILQ): Assessing AI Literacy in Adolescents, Young Adults, and Midlife Adults. Behavioral Sciences, 16(7), 1110. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071110

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multi-group confirmatory factor analyses across the three age groups tested progressively constrained models (configural, metric, scalar). The metric model showed ΔCFI = 0.005 and ΔRMSEA = 0.005; the scalar model values are quoted here, supporting equivalent measurement of the construct across ages.

> "The scalar model yielded ΔCFI = 0.008 and ΔRMSEA = 0.007, also within the acceptable range."
