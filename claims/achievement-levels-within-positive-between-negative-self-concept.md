## Evidence

### Wolff et al. 2020

Fabian Wolff, Fabio Sticca, Christoph Niepel, Thomas Götz, Jan Van Damme, and Jens Möller. (2020). The Reciprocal 2I/E Model: An Investigation of Mutual Relations Between Achievement and Self-Concept Levels and Changes in the Math and Verbal Domain Across Three Countries. https://osf.io/7f6gb/

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Study 1 (N = 1,508 German students, Grades 4–8, grades as achievement indicators), 2I/EM sub-model results. All six 2I/EM hypotheses found support, with standardized effect sizes β = .63 to .66 within subjects and β = –.22 to –.20 between subjects. Similar patterns held in Studies 2 and 3.

> "The effects of achievement levels on self-concepts were strongly positive within subjects (all .63 ≤ β ≤ .66, all p < .001) and moderately negative between subjects (all –.22 ≤ β ≤ –.20, all p < .001)."
