### Arsalidou 2016

Arsalidou, M. & Pascual-Leone, J. (2016). Constructivist developmental theory is needed in developmental neuroscience. npj Science of Learning 1, 16016. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.16

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review reports, citing a mathematical-achievement study, this predictive finding. It interprets it as evidence that learning relies on non-content-specific coordinating brain regions, since parietal areas are typically engaged during mathematical problem solving.

> "individual responses to 8 weeks of training can be predicted by brain anatomy and functional connectivity measures recorded before training; this is the connectivity and cortical indices of hippocampus and basal ganglia, but not of parietal areas"
