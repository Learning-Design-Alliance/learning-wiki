## Evidence

### Milička J 2025

Milička J, Marklová A, Drobil O, Pospíšilová E. (2025). Humans can learn to detect AI-generated texts, or at least learn when they can't. Preprint. Manuscript submitted to PLOS One. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.01877](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.01877)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed effects logistic regression on register-dimension differences in the 254-participant experiment. The article reports significant Dim1–Dim4 effects in the no-feedback group (Dim2 p=0.012; Dim3 p=0.024; Dim4 p=0.017) and interprets that participants without feedback assumed AI texts are static, prepared, highly cohesive, and polythematic.

> "For Dim1 difference, the effect was highly significant (p < 0.001; estimate: -0.30; CI: -0.47 – -0.13). That indicates that when the AI-generated text was more static than the original human-written continuation, participants were more likely to identify it correctly."
