### Buljan Ivan 2021

Buljan Ivan, Marušić Matko, Tokalić Ružica, Viđak Marin, Peričić Tina Poklepović, Hren Darko, Marušić Ana. (2021). Cognitive levels in testing knowledge in evidence-based medicine: a cross sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02449-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired comparison within the overall cross-sectional sample (N = 203) of summed lower-level versus higher-level scores: "All participants had higher scores on lower cognitive levels" with a median difference of 9 versus 7 points, P < 0.001 (Wilcoxon paired samples test).

> "All participants had higher scores on lower cognitive levels (Remembering, Understanding, Applying) (Md = 9 on 0–18 theoretical range, 95% CI = 9 to 9,) compared to the higher levels (Analysing, Evaluating, Creating) (Md = 7, 95% CI = 7 to 7) (P < 0.001, Wilcoxon paired samples test)."
