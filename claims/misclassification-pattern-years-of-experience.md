## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The four experts misclassified as novices had fewer years of experience (9.0 ± 4.3 years) than the group average (13.3 ± 5.0 years) but more than the novice-group average (5.8 ± 5.0 years); the two misclassified novices averaged 2.5 ± 0.7 years, which the authors say does not explain their classification as experts.
