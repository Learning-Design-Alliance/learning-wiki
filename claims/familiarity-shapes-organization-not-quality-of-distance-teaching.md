## Evidence

### Fütterer 2023

Fütterer, T., Hoch, E., Lachner, A., Scheiter, K., & Stürmer, K. (2023). High-quality digital distance teaching during COVID-19 school closures: Does familiarity with technology matter? Computers & Education, 199, 104788. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104788

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

T-tests comparing organizational characteristics between familiarity groups. The study reports "a statistically significantly higher percentage of digital distance teaching" in the high familiarity group with d = 0.44, and similarly for synchronous phases (d = 0.35).

> "students in the high familiarity group reported a statistically significantly higher percentage of digital distance teaching (M =85.73%, SD = 14.96) than students in the low familiarity group (M =79.02%, SD = 16.52), t(354) = −5.08, p < .001, d = 0.44"
