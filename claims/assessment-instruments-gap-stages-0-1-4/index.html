Evidence¶

Liu 2026¶

Liu, J. P., & Levy, R. (2026). Beyond Tool Adoption: A Practical Five-Stage Developmental Continuum for AI Literacy in Higher Education. North Carolina State University. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00038 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.00038

q2 · i? · design · r2

Analytical mapping of six published assessment instruments onto continuum stages (Table 3), finding SNAIL and MAILS primarily assess Stages 2–3. The authors conclude "performance-based assessments are needed to diagnose the full range of the continuum."

"Critically, Stages 0 and 1 are poorly served by existing instruments, and Stage 4 competencies fall outside the scope of most current instruments."
