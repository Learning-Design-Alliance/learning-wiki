## Evidence

### Edda Sant 2019

Edda Sant. (2019). Democratic Education: A Theoretical Review (2006–2017). Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654319862493

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The review's analysis of deliberative educational policy reports Gutmann's two principles guaranteeing inclusive deliberation: nonrepression, and nondiscrimination, where "Citizens cannot be denied participation in deliberative processes on the basis of group differences (Fraser-Burgess, 2012; Meens & Howe, 2016; Perry, 2009)." It links the second principle to the need for a democratic threshold, illustrated by the Mozert v. Hawkins case.
