### Eamonn Corrigan 2023

Eamonn Corrigan, Martin Williams, and Mary A. Wells. (2023). High School Enrolment Choices — Understanding the STEM Gender Gap. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/s42330-023-00285-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42330-023-00285-y)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed effects regression on Ontario enrolment data (Table II: 12U Biology male estimate +0.749 with female-as-reference interaction −1.189). The article reports 12U Biology among courses with an average decline in male continuation, with the printed β1 = −0.44 ± 0.08 and p = 1.79 × 10−7.

> "12U Biology (β1 = −0.44 ± 0.08; p = 1.79 × 10−7) have all seen an average decline in male continuation over the eleven years of available data."

### Eamonn Corrigan 2023 (2)

Eamonn Corrigan, Martin Williams, and Mary A. Wells. (2023). High School Enrolment Choices — Understanding the STEM Gender Gap. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/s42330-023-00285-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42330-023-00285-y)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same mixed effects model, sex-by-year interaction for 12U Biology (Table II: −1.189, p < .001), the largest gap in ∆SCR between the sexes among the courses, described as appearing in biology stream courses.

> "the interaction term which calculated the average difference in ∆SCR between male and female students was −1.19 ± 0.09 percentage points per year."
