## Evidence

### Saubern R. 2020

Saubern R., Urbach D., Koehler M. & Phillips M. (2020). Describing increasing proficiency in teachers’ knowledge of the effective use of digital technology. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103784

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Qualitative comparison of TPACK Confidence construct map bands derived from the Rasch analysis. The authors state that Band 4 teachers "are more confident in their skills to support students to use technology to develop deep understanding of the curriculum area" than Band 3 teachers.

> "one thing that distinguishes them from the more confident teachers in Band 4 is that those teachers are more confident in their skills to support students to use technology to develop deep understanding of the curriculum area"

### Saubern R. 2020 (2)

Saubern R., Urbach D., Koehler M. & Phillips M. (2020). Describing increasing proficiency in teachers’ knowledge of the effective use of digital technology. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103784

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Qualitative comparison of TPACK Usefulness construct map bands. The authors state that Band 4 teachers differ from Band 3 in having "a stronger belief in the value of technology to facilitate students to develop deep understanding and construct multidisciplinary knowledge."

> "one aspect of what distinguishes those teachers from the teachers in Band 4 is a stronger belief in the value of technology to facilitate students to develop deep understanding and construct multidisciplinary knowledge"
