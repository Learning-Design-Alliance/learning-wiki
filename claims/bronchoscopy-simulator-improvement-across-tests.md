### Bjerrum Anne Sofie 2016

Bjerrum Anne Sofie, Eika Berit, Charles Peder, Hilberg Ole. (2016). Distributed practice. The more the merrier? A randomised bronchoscopy simulation study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.30517

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA across pre-test, three post-tests and a 4-week retention test in the 20-resident randomised simulator study. The article reports "a significant performance improvement from pre-test to post-test and retention test" with only F and p values printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "A significant main effect of the test was found for all measures (F(2,72) > 11.11, p<0.0001) except for the variable 'wall collisions', which indicates a significant performance improvement from pre-test to post-test and retention test."
