Lorenzo G. Rimoldini and Chandralekha Singh. (2005). Student understanding of rotational and rolling motion concepts. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Written-test analysis of rolling and friction questions: "Question (14) was the most diﬃcult question on the test for both int roductory and upper-level students (apart from the honor class)", probing the role of friction in rolling without slipping of a rigid wheel on a rigid horizontal surface. In test-based interviews students claimed kinetic friction was relevant and a constant force was needed to keep a wheel rolling.
