# In one uncontrolled pre-post study, participants' self-rated confidence in foot assessment rose from before to after the workshop, while the self-reported belief that only a podiatrist can stratify foot risk fell

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Self-rated confidence in doing a diabetic foot assessment rose from 34 % pre-training to 95 % immediately after the intervention, in one uncontrolled pre-post study (p < 0.001). [→ Schoen Deborah E. 2016](#schoen-deborah-e-2016)
`q2 i?` The share of participants who self-reported believing that only a podiatrist can stratify foot risk fell from 31 % to 19 %, in one uncontrolled pre-post study (p < 0.001). [→ Schoen Deborah E. 2016 (2)](#schoen-deborah-e-2016-2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

### Schoen Deborah E. 2016 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`
