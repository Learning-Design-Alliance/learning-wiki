### Salomon F. Itza-Ortiz 2003

Salomon F. Itza-Ortiz, N. Sanjay Rebello and Dean Zollman. (2003). Students' Models of Newton's Second Law in Mechanics and Electromagnetism. Pre-print, Kansas State University. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/25/1/011](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/25/1/011)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of interview transcripts across the study's contexts found the non-Newtonian models were "mostly triggered by questions relating to double the force, double the mass or double the speed", an interpretation the authors link to the proportionality cue in the wording.

> "The Aristotelian and hybrid models were mostly triggered by questions relating to double the force, double the mass or double the speed; perhaps because of the word "double" which indicated proportionality."
