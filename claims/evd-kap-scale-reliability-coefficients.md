`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability assessment within the formal multi-center survey of 841 neurosurgical nurses. The article reports "The total questionnaire's Cronbach's α was 0.871", with attitude and practice dimension α values of 0.872 to 0.873 and split-half reliability of 0.82 for the total scale.

> "The total questionnaire's Cronbach's α was 0.871, with attitude and practice dimensions ranging from 0.872 to 0.873."

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Test-retest substudy with 20 nurses recruited two weeks after the formal survey. The article reports "The test-retest reliability of the questionnaire was 0.853".

> "Two weeks after the formal survey, we recruited 20 nurses meeting the predefined inclusion/exclusion criteria for test-retest reliability evaluation. The test-retest reliability of the questionnaire was 0.853, indicating good reliability."
