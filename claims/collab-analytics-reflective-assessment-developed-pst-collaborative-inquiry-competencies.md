## Subclaims
`q2 i?` PSTs in the analytics-enhanced knowledge building class developed collaborative inquiry competencies for community knowledge advancement, outperforming a comparison class using portfolio-based reflective assessment. [→ Yang 2022](#yang-2022)

## Evidence

### Yang 2022

Yang, Y., Zhu, G., Sun, D., & Chan, C. K. K. (2022). Collaborative analytics-supported reflective Assessment for Scaffolding Pre-service Teachers’ collaborative Inquiry and Knowledge Building. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09372-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

An 18-week quasi-experimental study comparing a class of 40 PSTs using a "knowledge building design enhanced by collaborative analytics-supported reflective assessment" with a class of 28 PSTs taught by the same instructor using portfolios on the same inquiry topics. Discourse was coded from Knowledge Forum notes (1,406 experimental, 707 comparison) covering epistemic, social, and metacognitive dimensions.

> "The analysis of the PSTs’ Knowledge Forum discourse showed that collaborative analytics-supported reflective assessment helps PSTs develop collaborative inquiry competencies for community knowledge advancement."
