# VIQ moderated SCI-A response: lower-VIQ students benefited on communication, while higher-VIQ students showed worse outcomes on social cognition, communication, and motivation

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The VIQ × condition interaction was significant for Social Cognition, Communication, Motivation, and Restricted/Repetitive Behavior subscales; at lower VIQ, SCI-A positively impacted social communication (d = −0.50). [→ Stichter 2018](#stichter-2018)
`q3 i3` At higher VIQ, SCI-A students had higher SRS-2 scores than BAU students on social cognition (d = 1.02), communication (d = 0.67), and motivation (d = 1.02). [→ Stichter 2018](#stichter-2018)

## Evidence

### Stichter 2018

Stichter, J. P., Herzog, M. J., Kilgus, S. P., & Schoemann, A. M. (2018). Exploring the Moderating Effects of Cognitive Abilities on Social Competence Intervention Outcomes. Behavior Modification, 42(1), 84-107 (published online 2017). https://doi.org/10.1177/0145445517698654

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel moderation analysis probing simple slopes at VIQ 85 and 115 in the CRT. Interactions were significant for four subscales (p = .004–.050); low-VIQ simple slopes favored SCI-A (d = −0.50 for communication), high-VIQ slopes favored BAU (d up to 1.02).

> "Examination of effect sizes of simple slopes indicated that for groups characterized by lower VIQ, SCI-A students had lower SRS-2 scores relative to BAU students, indicating SCI-A had positive impact on social communication (d = −0.50) for this group."
