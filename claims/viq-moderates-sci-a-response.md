## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The VIQ × condition interaction was significant for Social Cognition, Communication, Motivation, and Restricted/Repetitive Behavior subscales; at lower VIQ, SCI-A positively impacted social communication (d = −0.50). [→ Janine P. Stichter 2018](#janine-p-stichter-2018)

## Evidence

### Janine P. Stichter 2018

Janine P. Stichter, Melissa J. Herzog, Stephen P. Kilgus, and Alexander M. Schoemann. (2018). Exploring the Moderating Effects of Cognitive Abilities on Social Competence Intervention Outcomes. Behavior Modification. https://doi.org/10.1177/0145445517698654

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel moderation analysis probing simple slopes at VIQ 85 and 115 in the CRT. Interactions were significant for four subscales (p = .004–.050); low-VIQ simple slopes favored SCI-A (d = −0.50 for communication), high-VIQ slopes favored BAU (d up to 1.02).

> "Examination of effect sizes of simple slopes indicated that for groups characterized by lower VIQ, SCI-A students had lower SRS-2 scores relative to BAU students, indicating SCI-A had positive impact on social communication (d = −0.50) for this group."
