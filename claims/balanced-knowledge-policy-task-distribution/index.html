Evidence¶

Tsompanoudi et al. 2015¶

Despina TSOMPANOUDI, Maya SATRATZEMI, Stelios XINOGALOS. (2015). Distributed Pair Programming Using Collaboration Scripts: An Educational System and Initial Results. Informatics in Education. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2015.17

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Task-distribution analysis in the 24-pair evaluation counted tasks per student per learning goal and calculated the difference from an even distribution. The article concludes "the balanced distribution of tasks among pairs achieved symmetry in knowledge acquisition".

"For all learning goals (summative for all projects) there was a greater difference in the control group compared to the experimental group. A Mann-Whitney test revealed that the average difference between the two groups was also statistically significant (U = 25.5, p = 0.007), meaning that system's interventions during DPP had a positive effect on task distribution."
