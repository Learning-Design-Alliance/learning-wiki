Isiguzel 2014¶

Isiguzel, B. (2014). The blended learning environment on the foreign language learning process: A balance for motivation and achievement. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 15(3), Article 10. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1043673

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control group study with 62 students (35 experimental, 27 control) over 10 weeks; two-way ANOVA for mixed measures. The interaction was significant: "[F (1-60)= 7.292, p< .005]". Means rose from 33.08 to 69 (experimental) and 30.40 to 57.18 (control).
