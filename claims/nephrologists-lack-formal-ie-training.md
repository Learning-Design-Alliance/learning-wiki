Cross-sectional email survey (Results) of nephrologists registered with the Brazilian Nephrology Society found that 'did not receive information about the importance IE during their formal training' was reported by 172 (65.5%), while 242 (92.4%) recognized exercise as beneficial. No effect size is printed for this descriptive finding.
