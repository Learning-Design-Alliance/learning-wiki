## Evidence

### Egger Fabienne 2019

Egger Fabienne, Benzing Valentin, Conzelmann Achim, Schmidt Mirko. (2019). Boost your brain, while having a break! The effects of long-term cognitively engaging physical activity breaks on children's executive functions and academic achievement. PLoS ONE 14(3): e0212482. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0212482

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVAs on pre-post data from a 20-week three-group cluster-randomized classroom intervention (N = 142 children aged 7–9) with pre-test values as covariates. The group contrast showed "shifting differed significantly between the groups (F(3, 138) = 4.68, p = .011, ηp2 = .064)"; post-hoc tests favored combo over aerobic.

> "However, shifting differed significantly between the groups (F(3, 138) = 4.68, p = .011, ηp2 = .064). Post-hoc analysis revealed a significantly greater improvement in shifting performance in the combo group than in the aerobic group (p = .003), but no significant difference between the combo group and the cognition group (p = .176)."
