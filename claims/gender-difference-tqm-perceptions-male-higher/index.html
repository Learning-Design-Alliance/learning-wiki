Altunay 2016¶

Esen Altunay. (2016). The Effect of Training with TQM on the Perceptions of Teachers about the Quality of Schools. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2126-2133. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040925

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Mann-Whitney U tests on post-test scores by gender in the experimental group (11 female, 14 male as printed in Table 7); all four dimensions were significant, e.g. management structure Z = -3.146, p = .003. No effect sizes are printed.

"Accordingly when was considered gender the perceptions of the participants who were male about the applicability of TQM principles were higher than female in all dimensions."
