## Evidence

### Vidal-Abarca 1998

Vidal-Abarca, E., & Sanjosé, V. (1998). Levels of comprehension of scientific prose: The role of text variables. Learning and Instruction, 8(3), 215-233. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959475297000200

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment with 67 tenth graders randomly assigned to four text versions; 2x2 ANCOVA (coherence: low, high x linking: present, absent) with prior knowledge as covariate on the main-ideas test. The article reports "Only themaineffectofcoherencewasreliable, F(1,62)=17.010, MSE=20.023, p<.001".
