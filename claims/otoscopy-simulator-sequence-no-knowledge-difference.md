## Evidence

### Xu J 2018

Xu J, Campisi P, Forte V, Carrillo B, Vescan A, Brydges R. (2018). Effectiveness of discovery learning using a mobile otoscopy simulator on knowledge acquisition and retention in medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-018-0317-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A two-group randomized controlled trial of 34 pre-clerkship medical students using a 2x2 mixed effects ANCOVA with pre-test scores as covariate. The study found "improvement in scores of both groups and no significant effects of group (F1,31 = 0.53, p = 0.47)."

> "Analysis of knowledge acquisition and retention showed improvement in scores of both groups and no significant effects of group (F1,31 = 0.53, p = 0.47)."
