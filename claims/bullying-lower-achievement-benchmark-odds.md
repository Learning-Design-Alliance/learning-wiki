## Evidence

### Rothon Catherine 2011

Rothon Catherine, Head Jenny, Klineberg Emily, Stansfeld Stephen. (2011). Can social support protect bullied adolescents from adverse outcomes? A prospective study on the effects of bullying on the educational achievement and mental health of adolescents at secondary schools in East London. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adolescence.2010.02.007

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Prospective school-based survey (RELACHS) of 2790 adolescents in 28 East London comprehensive schools; achievement measured two years after baseline bullying reports via Key Stage 3 or GCSE benchmarks. Crude analysis found bullied pupils "approximately half as likely to achieve the benchmark (OR = 0.46, 95% CI 0.29, 0.72)"; adjustment for confounders left OR 0.46 (0.28, 0.76).

> "Those that had been bullied in the last term were approximately half as likely to achieve the benchmark (OR = 0.46, 95% CI 0.29, 0.72)."
