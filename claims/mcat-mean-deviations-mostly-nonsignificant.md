### H. Sabbagh 2025

H. Sabbagh, T. Ribnishki, L. Hötzel, L. von Bronk, Y. Khazaei, and A. Wichelhaus. (2025). An Open‐Source, AI‐Supported Teaching Tool in Orthodontic Education—Assessment of Acceptance and Effectiveness. Journal of Dental Education 89(12): 1753–1764. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13916

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of end-of-course manual tracing deviations (19 variables, three radiographs) between S1 (N = 47) and pooled S2/S3. Table 2 reports, e.g., ML-NSL Ceph1 p = 0.034 and NSp/SpMe Ceph3 p = 0.035; most variables show p above 0.05. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Significant differences between the control (S1) and intervention groups (S2 and S3) were observed in specific landmarks such as ML‐NSL and SGo/NMe for Ceph1 and NSp/SpMe for Ceph3."
