## Evidence

### Van Iddekinge 2024

Van Iddekinge, C. H., Arnold, J. D., Krivacek, S. J., Frieder, R. E., & Roth, P. L. (2024). Making the Grade? A Meta-Analysis of Academic Performance as a Predictor of Work Performance and Turnover. Journal of Applied Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/apl0001212

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analytic estimate for academic performance as a predictor of training performance across eight independent samples (N = 10,624). The article states the training CI [.32, .37] "did not overlap with the CI for job performance [.18, .24]", supporting the stronger prediction of training performance.

> "Across eight independent samples, the mean observed r between AP and training performance was .31, and the mean corrected r was .34."
