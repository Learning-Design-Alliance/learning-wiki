## Evidence

### Dunn 2019

Dunn, L., & Ambroso, E. (2019). Balancing act: State and district roles in school improvement under ESSA. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED602966

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview finding from the 10-SEA study. Louisiana's example includes requiring identified schools to choose high-quality curricula and a professional development vendor, publishing guidance on LEA options, and competitively allocating its 7% school-improvement set-aside.
