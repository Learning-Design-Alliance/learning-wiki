Yanhong Zuo 2026¶

Yanhong Zuo, Xiatinghan Xu, Leigh Ann Dunning. (2026). “Will AI steal my glory?”: Power relations perceived by college instructors when grappling with Generative AI. Journal of Applied Learning and Teaching, 9(1), 31-50. https://doi.org/10.37074/jalt.2026.9.1.8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

The article's instrumental case study of college instructors used inductive analysis of instructor perceptions of Generative AI. The abstract reports that the analysis "identifies four centers that have shaped the college instructors' perceptions," naming the global impact of AI, the university and department, colleagues, and students. No effect sizes or participant counts are printed in the available text.
