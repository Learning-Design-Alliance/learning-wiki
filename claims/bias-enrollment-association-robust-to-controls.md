## Evidence

### Park Yeji 2026

Park Yeji, Riddle Travis, Sinclair Stacey. (2026). Associations between county-level racial attitude bias and disparities in Advanced Placement participation. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2512555123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up multilevel regression analyses adding two controls: a county-level correlation index capturing racial disparities in AP course offerings, and county-level Black-White achievement disparities on standardized tests in grades 3 to 8 from the Stanford Education Data Archive. Robustness checks using alternative operationalizations of AP access disparities produced consistent results.

> "Even after controlling for disparities in AP access and prior achievement, county-level implicit racial bias remained associated with greater Black–White disparities in AP course enrollment across all 3 y (Ps < 0.001; SI Appendix, Fig. S13)."
