## Evidence

### Reavley Nicola J. 2018

Reavley Nicola J., Morgan Amy J., Fischer Julie-Anne, Kitchener Betty, Bovopoulos Nataly, Jorm Anthony F. (2018). Effectiveness of eLearning and blended modes of delivery of Mental Health First Aid training in the workplace: randomised controlled trial. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-018-1888-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Objective course-website usage data from the RCT; the blended group's online completion rate exceeded the eLearning group's, with trends toward more completed modules and more time on course, though one third of participants spent less than two hours online in total.

> "It is notable that participants in the blended MHFA group were significantly more likely to complete the online course (64.8% vs 55.3%) and there were also trends towards a higher number of completed modules and more time spent on the course overall among participants in that group."
