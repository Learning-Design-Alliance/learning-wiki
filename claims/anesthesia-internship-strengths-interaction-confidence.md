`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis (SWOT-directed, Graneheim & Lundman coding) of interviews with 7 participants at a Dezful hospital; the strengths subcategory's most repeated codes concerned interaction and anxiety reduction. Participants stated the plan "improved the students’ interaction with staff and patients".

> "Most participants stated that the implementation of the internship plan improved the students’ interaction with staff and patients, leading to a reduction in their anxiety, enhanced self-confidence, independence, and responsibility."
