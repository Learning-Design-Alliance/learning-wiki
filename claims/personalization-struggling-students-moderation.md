### Walkington 2013

Walkington, C. A. (2013). Using adaptive learning technologies to personalize instruction to student interests: The impact of relevant contexts on performance and learning outcomes. Journal of Educational Data Mining, 5(1). https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM. [doi:10.1037/a0031882](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031882)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis using curriculum progress as achievement proxy. 25 students identified as struggling (13 control, 12 experimental). The combined odds ratio was "odds = 1.64 * 2.56 = 4.20, p = 0.037". Control success rate 24.69% versus 57.90% experimental.

> "low achievement students in the personalization condition greatly outperformed low achievement students in the control condition on hard KCs in Unit 6 (odds = 1.64 * 2.56 = 4.20, p = 0.037)"
