## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Bifactor indices show the general factor explains most common variance (ECV = .822) and total-score variance (ωh = .927), so the scale can be scored as essentially unidimensional. [→ Zhorabekova A 2026](#zhorabekova-a-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhorabekova A 2026

Zhorabekova A, Duisenova M, Kurmasheva M and Yesnazar A (2026). Acceptance of AI-gamified adaptive learning in EFL: bifactor evidence for essential unidimensionality and the limits of subscale scoring. Front. Educ. 11:1929123. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1929123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bifactor CFA on the full sample (N = 401) with ML estimation. The article reports "ECV = .822" and "ωh = .927", exceeding conventional benchmarks (ECV > .70, ωh > .80, PUC > .70), with several specific-factor loadings very small (e.g., EFF1 = .06).

> "Bifactor-based indices confirmed a dominant general factor: it explained most of the common variance (ECV = .822) and of the variance in total scores (ω = .971; ωh = .927), with PUC = .808."
