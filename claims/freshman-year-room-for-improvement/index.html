Freshman Year: The Make-it or Break-it Year 2007¶

Freshman Year: The Make-it or Break-it Year. (2007). Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. http://ccsr.uchicago.edu

Descriptive statistics in the brief state that "More than half of Chicago freshmen fail a course," that about 40 percent miss more than a month of school in the first year including class cutting, and that the average GPA is lower than a C. These are descriptive rates, not tested comparisons.
