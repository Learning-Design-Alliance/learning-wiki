## Evidence

### Hernández-González Samuel 2026

Hernández-González Samuel, Gómez-Climent María Ángeles, Clemente-Soriano Albert, Alba Robles Jose Luis, Caurín-Alonso Carlos, Solbes Jordi (2026). Reduced cognitive empathy and differences in EEG activity in low-achieving adolescents. PLOS One 21(10): e0361520. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0361520

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mixed-design repeated-measures ANOVA on the FER task with group as between-subjects factor and emotion category as within-subject factor in 40 students. Group effect p = 0.079 and interaction p = 0.251 were not significant; equivalence was not tested — the distinctive term recognition accuracy appears inside.

> "For recognition accuracy, neither the main effect of group (F(1, 38) = 3.252, p = 0.079, η2p = 0.079) nor the group-by-emotion interaction (F(6, 228) = 1.316, p = 0.251, η2p = 0.033) was statistically significant."

### Hernández-González Samuel 2026 (2)

Hernández-González Samuel, Gómez-Climent María Ángeles, Clemente-Soriano Albert, Alba Robles Jose Luis, Caurín-Alonso Carlos, Solbes Jordi (2026). Reduced cognitive empathy and differences in EEG activity in low-achieving adolescents. PLOS One 21(10): e0361520. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0361520

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Mixed-design repeated-measures ANOVA in the same 40 students: the emotion-category main effect was highly significant for both accuracy and reaction time, with performance varying independently of group membership — the distinctive term facial-expression category appears inside.

> "A significant main effect of facial-expression category was observed for recognition accuracy (F(6, 228) = 61.855, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.619) and reaction time (F(6, 228) = 9.347, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.197), indicating that performance varied across emotion categories independently of group membership."
