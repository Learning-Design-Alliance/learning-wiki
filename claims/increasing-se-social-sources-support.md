## Evidence

### Peura 2026

Peura, P., Koponen, T., Aro, T., Viholainen, H., & Aro, M. (2026). Dynamics of change in math self-efficacy, sources of self-efficacy, and math skills. Journal of Educational Psychology, 118(2), 209-223. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001006

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

BCH comparisons of latent growth factors for each source of self-efficacy across the four trajectories (LR tests significant at p < .01). The Increasing trajectory differed in direction and rate of change in verbal persuasion and vicarious experiences from the other trajectories, with effect sizes d = 0.46–4.87 across source comparisons.

> "children in the Increasing SE trajectory reported increasing verbal persuasion, vicarious experiences, and mastery experiences over the year, whereas in other trajectories, the change trends in these sources were decreasing"
