### Beaugé Yvonne 2026

Beaugé Yvonne, Kusch Simon, Thorsson Lukas, Zentgraf Michael, Schauwienold Julian, Muenzberg Matthias, Verboket Rene, Lefering Rolf, Schweigkofler Uwe, Faul Philipp, Sterz Jasmina, Ruesseler Miriam. `q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

RM-ANOVA on team-level Task Responsibility Similarity Scores in the cluster-randomized study of 29 teams. The time effect was significant, F(1,27) = 166.18, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.86, while the training group effect was not (F = 0.07, p = 0.80, partial η² = 0.00). Means rose from about 61–62% to 71–74% agreement (Table 2).
