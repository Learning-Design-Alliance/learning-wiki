### Chiu 2024

Chiu, T. K. F. (2024). A classification tool to foster self-regulated learning with generative artificial intelligence by applying self-determination theory: a case of ChatGPT. Education Tech Research Dev, 72, 2401–2416. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10366-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consensus ratings of the 20 activities from the Delphi panel, reported in the Results section: "the majority of learning activities (11 out of 20) could better satisfy students’ basic needs for competence," with autonomy next and relatedness fewest. No effect sizes were reported.

> "Overall, the majority of learning activities (11 out of 20) could better satisfy students’ basic needs for competence, followed by learning activities that better satisfy students’ needs for autonomy. The fewest activities are in favor of relatedness."
