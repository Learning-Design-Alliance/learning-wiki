## Evidence

### Tejinder Kaur 2017

Tejinder Kaur, David Blair, Ron Burman, Warren Stannard, David Treagust, Grady Venville, Marjan Zadnik, Warwick Mathews and Dana Perks. (2017). Evaluation of 14 to 15-Year-Old Students' Understanding and Attitude towards Learning Einsteinian Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1712.02063](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1712.02063)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Five-question views questionnaire given only at the end of the programme (Table IV). For EQ 4, 70% agreed they would like to learn more about Einsteinian physics while only 7% disagreed; for EQ 5, 63% agreed and 5% disagreed.

> "The analysis revealed that 88% of students responded positively to the use of hands-on activities (EQ 1). They agreed that hands-on activities helped to clarify Einsteinian concepts."
