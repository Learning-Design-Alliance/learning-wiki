Noughabi 2017¶

Mostafa Azari Noughabi. (2017). The Effect of Meaning-focused Listening Input on Iranian Intermediate EFL Learners' Productive Vocabulary Size. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(15). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1143810

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative phase of the study: six EFL teachers participated in semi-structured interviews transcribed and analyzed with MAXQDA version 12; the analysis found teachers "believed in the beneficiary role" of meaningful listening input but considered it "time-consuming a nd too demanding".

"The qualitative analysis o f the data suggested that teacher participants believed in the beneficiary role of sufficient amount of listening to meaningful input; however, they find this innovativ e method of vocabulary instruction time-consuming a nd too demanding."
