# Experimental designs and study-specific measures are associated with larger program effects, while peer-reviewed status shows no indication of publication bias

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Studies with experimental designs yielded larger effects (d = 0.98 overall) than quasi-experimental studies (d = 0.32). [→ Suzanne Fikrat-Wevers 2021](#suzanne-fikrat-wevers-2021)
`q3 i3` Study-specific instruments yielded larger effects (d = 0.84 overall) than preexisting standardized instruments (d = 0.37). [→ Suzanne Fikrat-Wevers 2021 (2)](#suzanne-fikrat-wevers-2021-2)
`q3 i?` Peer-reviewed publication source was a nonsignificant moderator, indicating no publication bias. [→ Suzanne Fikrat-Wevers 2021](#suzanne-fikrat-wevers-2021)

## Evidence

### Suzanne Fikrat-Wevers 2021

Suzanne Fikrat-Wevers, Roel van Steensel, Lidia Arends. (2021). Effects of Family Literacy Programs on the Emergent Literacy Skills of Children From Low-SES Families: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654321998075

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis of immediate posttests found experimental studies averaged d = 0.98 (SE = 0.22) overall versus d = 0.32 (SE = 0.06) for quasi-experimental; Q(1) = 8.76** overall. Peer-reviewed status was nonsignificant (Q(1) = 0.01).

> "Research design was a significant moderator of effects on overall scores (k = 48) and comprehension-related skills (k = 38). Studies with an experimental design yielded larger effects than studies using a quasi-experimental design."

### Suzanne Fikrat-Wevers 2021 (2)

Suzanne Fikrat-Wevers, Roel van Steensel, Lidia Arends. (2021). Effects of Family Literacy Programs on the Emergent Literacy Skills of Children From Low-SES Families: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654321998075

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis found study-specific measures averaged d = 0.84 (SE = 0.14) overall versus d = 0.37 (SE = 0.08) for existing standardized measures; Q(2) = 8.61* overall, significant for all three outcomes.

> "This variable was a significant moderator of all three outcomes (koverall = 48, k comprehension = 38; k code = 22): In all cases, effects were larger for study-specific than for study-independent instruments."
