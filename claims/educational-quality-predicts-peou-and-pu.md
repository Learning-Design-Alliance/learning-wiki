## Evidence

### Patience E. Idoga 2022

Patience E. Idoga, Dokun Iwalewa Oluwajana, Ibrahim Adeshola. (2022). Educational quality and technological complexity on recognition of enhanced learning platform in developing countries using PLS-SEM in a post COVID era. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10150-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural model results from the PLS-SEM analysis of 299 instructor responses; hypotheses H3 and H4 were empirically supported with the printed path coefficients. The authors state the findings are "consistent with those of similar studies" (Hassanzadeh et al., 2012; Al-Azawei et al., 2017).

> "instructors educational quality shows a positive impact on perceived ease of use (β = 0.272, p < 0.01) and instructors’ educational quality shows a positive impact on perceived usefulness (β = 0.299, p < 0.01)"
