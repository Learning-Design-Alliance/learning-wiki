### Amit Dutta 2026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled experiment with 30 university students randomly assigned 15 per condition; mean post-test score was 74.3% for RAFT versus 61.4% for RoR, and "a large effect size (d >2)" was reported for the between-condition difference.

> "Students in the RAFT condition achieved significantly higher post-test scores than students in the RoR condition. A two-tailedt-test found a significant between-condition difference (p <0.0001), with a large effect size (d >2)."
