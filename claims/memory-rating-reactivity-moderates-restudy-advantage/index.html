Evidence¶

Higham 2023¶

Higham, P. A., Fastrich, G. M., Potts, R., Murayama, K., Pickering, J. S., & Hadwin, J. A. (2023). Spaced Retrieval Practice: Can Restudying Trump Retrieval? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09809-2

q2 · i? · causal · r3

Experiment 2 manipulated whether the post-feedback memory rating was required, testing whether rating-induced reactivity interfered with retrieval and/or boosted restudy performance. Per the article's summary of the results, "both interference on retrieval trials and facilitation on restudy trials" contributed; the abstract adds the pattern was only descriptively reversed without ratings. No effect size was printed in the supplied text.

"The results sug- gested that both interference on retrieval trials and facilitation on restudy trials contrib- uted to the superiority of the restudy condition in Experiment 1."
