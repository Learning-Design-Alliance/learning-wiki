Stress and fatigue results from the ten-surgeon crossover simulation study, measured pre- and post-task via heart rate, salivary amylase, Chalder Fatigue Scale, and visual analog scale. Only p values and medians are printed; no effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Changes in physiological and subjective stress indicators (ΔSAA, ΔHR, ΔVAS, and ΔCFS) did not differ significantly between modalities. However, the median ΔHR and ΔSAA values were lower in the Robo group compared with the Lap group (ΔHR: 12 vs 23.5, p = 0.14; ΔSAA: 0 vs 17, p = 0.41)"
