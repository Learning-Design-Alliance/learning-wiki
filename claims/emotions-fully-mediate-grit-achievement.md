## Evidence

### Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang 2026

Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang. (2026). Unpacking grit in exam-oriented education: divergent roles of perseverance and consistency in foreign language achievement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04312-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bias-corrected bootstrapping with 5,000 resamples in the CB-SEM model (N = 310) tested indirect effects. The COI→FLB→FLA indirect effect was significant; the article reports this mediation as partial, since the total effect of COI on FLA was negative but modest.

> "COI demonstrated a significant negative indirect association with FLA via FLB (β = − 0.063, 95% CI [− 0.137, − 0.021]). No significant indirect effect through FLE was observed."

### Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang 2026 (2)

Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang. (2026). Unpacking grit in exam-oriented education: divergent roles of perseverance and consistency in foreign language achievement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04312-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bootstrapped indirect effects in the same SEM. The authors interpret the pattern, following MacKinnon et al., as "competitive suppression", and translate the total indirect effect into an estimated 12.1-point increase on the 150-point exam per one-SD increase in POE.

> "Although the total effect of POE on achievement was positive and significant (β = 0.213, p < .01), the direct effect became non-significant after the inclusion of FLE and FLB in the model."

### Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang 2026 (3)

Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang. (2026). Unpacking grit in exam-oriented education: divergent roles of perseverance and consistency in foreign language achievement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04312-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct-effect estimates (Table 5) from the SEM with N = 310; FLE's direct association with FLA was non-significant (p = 0.052, H2a rejected), while boredom retained a significant negative path. The model accounted for 81.1% of FLE variance, 35.5% in FLB, and 14.3% in FLA.

> "Among the emotion variables, FLB showed a significant negative association on achievement (β = −0.210, p < .01)."
