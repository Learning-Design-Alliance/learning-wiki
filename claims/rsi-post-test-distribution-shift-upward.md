Descriptive comparison of pre-test and post-test RSI boxplots (Figure 7) with quartile values in Table 4 (pre-test median 0.22, upper whisker 0.39; post-test median 0.28, upper whisker 0.67). No significance test attached to this comparison.

> "we point out a couple of interesting things, as all points in Q4 of the post-test are higher than the upper whisker of the pre-test, except for the outliers, and the median in the post-test (0.28) is higher than the median in the pre-test (0.22)"
