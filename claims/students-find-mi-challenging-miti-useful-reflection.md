`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Summative content analysis of one five-student focus group and eleven reflective assignments. A physiotherapy student reported that "Transcribing was hardest to do, although it made me much more aware of the interview"; other students found rating elements like simple versus complex reflection and evocation confusing.
