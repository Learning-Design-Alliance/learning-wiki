## Evidence

### Russell AE 2015

Russell AE, Ford T, Russell G (2015) Socioeconomic Associations with ADHD: Findings from a Mediation Analysis. PLoS ONE 10(6): e0128248. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0128248

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unadjusted logistic regressions in ALSPAC (N = 8,132): single parent OR 1.70 (95% CI 1.09–2.66), council/HA rent OR 1.84 (1.22–2.76), maternal age OR 0.96 (0.93–0.99), financial difficulties OR 2.23 (1.57–3.16). The article reports these as "significant predictors of ADHD".

> "As shown in Table 2, in unadjusted logistic regression significant predictors of ADHD were housing tenure, marital status, mothers ’age at birth and financial difficulties"
