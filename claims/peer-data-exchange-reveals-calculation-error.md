Classroom observation of the peer data-exchange step of the lab. The article reports that when teams examine each other's raw data, "the other people’s data gives the examining students’ initial results," showing both groups' issue is in the velocity calculation.

> "The students should be encouraged to email each other their raw position vs. time data, so each team can closely examine the other’s work. The students are in for a surprise, the other people’s data gives the examining students’ initial results!"
