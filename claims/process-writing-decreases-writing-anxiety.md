## Evidence

### Bayat 2014

Nihat BAYAT. (2014). The Effect of the Process Writing Approach on Writing Success and Anxiety. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 14(3). https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2014.3.1720

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on Writing Apprehension Test scores in the same 74-student quasi-experiment; adjusted means were 64.00 for the experimental group and 76.56 for the control group, with higher scores indicating "an increased level of anxiety". No effect size is printed.

> "As a result of the analysis, it was ascertained that there was a statistically significant difference between the experimental group and control group participants' mean anxiety scores on the posttest adjusted according to pretest anxiety scores (F (1,73)=19,661; p=.000)."
