## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A one-way ANOVA showed a significant effect of condition on confrustion (F(3, 187) = 14.01, p < .001) and gaming (F(3, 187) = 10.07, p < .001); Tukey tests showed PS-only students had significantly lower levels of both, with no differences among the erroneous-example conditions. ## Evidence

### Bittencourt Ig Ibert 2020

Bittencourt Ig Ibert, Cukurova Mutlu, Muldner Kasia, Luckin Rose, Millán Eva, Mogessie Michael, Richey Elizabeth J., McLaren Bruce M., Andres-Bray Juan Miguel L., Baker Ryan S. (2020). Confrustion and Gaming While Learning with Erroneous Examples in a Decimals Game. Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-52240-7_38

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA across the four conditions (PS, ErrEx, ErrEx/PS, PS/ErrEx) with 191 students. The Tukey contrast shows "students in the PS-only condition experienced significantly lower levels of confrustion"; the same pattern held for gaming (ps < .001, other conditions ps > .91).

> "Posthoc (Tukey) tests indicated that students in the PS-only condition experienced significantly lower levels of confrustion (ps < .001), while there were no differences among the other conditions (ps > .97)."
