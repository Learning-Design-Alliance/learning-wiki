# Within test sections, students performed significantly better on spirometry concepts than on other midterm concepts when data were pooled across semesters

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pooled across four semesters, test-section students scored higher on spirometry concepts than on other concepts (76.5% vs 70.9%, p<0.05), though per-semester differences were individually non-significant. [→ Wolf et al. 2015](#wolf-et-al-2015)

## Evidence

### Wolf et al. 2015

Alex M. Wolf, Carlos Liachovitzky & Abass S. Abdullahi. (2015). Active Learning Improves Student Performance in a Respiratory Physiology Lab. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, 4(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.5430/jct.v4n1p19

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-section analysis comparing test-section performance on spirometry concepts versus all other concepts on the midterm. Pooled data showed "76.5±4.3% vs. 70.9±1.5%", p<0.05; individual semester comparisons were not significant (p values 0.09, 0.78, 0.33, 0.15); no effect size was printed.

> "When data of all four semesters are combined, students performed better on the spirometry concepts than on other concepts assessed on the midterm. (76.5±4.3% vs. 70.9±1.5%, p<0.05)"
