### Holley 2017

Elizabeth A. Holley. (2017). Engaging Engineering Students in Geoscience Through Case Studies and Active Learning. Journal of Geoscience Education. https://doi.org/10.5408/16-145.1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey item B5, "I could explain some of the big-picture geological concepts that I have previously learned," improved from premedian 3.5 to postmedian 4 (Z = 4.04, p < 0.01). The one-group pretest–posttest survey matched 30 students' anonymous responses via self-assigned PINs.
