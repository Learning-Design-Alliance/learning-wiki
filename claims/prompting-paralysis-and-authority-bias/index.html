Evidence¶

Deliang Wang and Cunling Bian 2026¶

Deliang Wang and Cunling Bian. (2026). Scaffolding Critical Engagement with GenAI: Transforming Ethnic Minority Preparatory Students’ Collaborative Discourse in Prompt Engineering Tasks. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28630 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.28630

q2 · i? · design · r2

Thematic analysis of students’ reflective essays from Week 3 identified prompting paralysis and epistemic asymmetry as drivers of cognitive offloading; students positioned GenAI as an epistemic authority to be deferred to rather than interrogated, creating a psychological barrier to critical engagement.

"Students explicitlyacknowledged that they tended to accept AI outputs because they perceived them as “more professional and organized” than their own writing, or because, under time pressure, the AI’s responses appeared “good enough.”"
