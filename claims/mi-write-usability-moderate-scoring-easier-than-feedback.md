## Evidence

### Wilson 2024

Wilson, J., Zhang, F., Palermo, C., Cordero, T. C., Myers, M. C., Eacker, H., Potter, A., & Coles, J. (2024). Predictors of middle school students’ perceptions of automated writing evaluation. Computers & Education, 211, 104985. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104985

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from a survey of 1,299 middle school students using MI Write during the 2021–2022 school year. Students rated usability on a 0–3 scale, with a mean of 1.88. The article notes students found scoring easier to understand than feedback.

> "Overall, students rated MI Write as moderately easy to use (M = 1.88; SD = 0.49; mode = 2)."
