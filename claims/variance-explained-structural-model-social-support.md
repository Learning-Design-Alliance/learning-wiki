## Evidence

### Zhang Xiangping 2024

Zhang Xiangping, Qian Wensheng. (2024). The effect of social support on academic performance among adolescents: The chain mediating roles of self-efficacy and learning engagement. PLoS One 19(12): e0311597. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311597

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Coefficient-of-determination reporting from the SEM of the 265-student sample; Table 5 prints R2 of 0.141 (self-efficacy), 0.259 (learning engagement) and 0.474 (academic performance). The text separately reports model fit including χ2/df = 1.511, RMSEA = 0.044 and CFI = 0.976.

> "Table 5 shows that the path coefficient value R2 shows that only 14.1% of the total variance was explained by social support, learning engagement and academic performance. Furthermore, R2 shows that 25.9% and 47.4% of the total variance in learning engagement and academic performance were explained by social support."

### Zhang Xiangping 2024 (2)

Zhang Xiangping, Qian Wensheng. (2024). The effect of social support on academic performance among adolescents: The chain mediating roles of self-efficacy and learning engagement. PLoS One 19(12): e0311597. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311597

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Structural model path coefficients from Fig 2 of the same study: social support→self-efficacy 0.441 (P < 0.001), social support→learning engagement 0.424 (P < 0.001), self-efficacy→learning engagement 0.155 (P < 0.01), predicting academic performance with standardized coefficients of 0.298, 0.292 and 0.218.

> "The study found that the social support construct explains 14% of the variance in self-efficacy, with a standardized regression value of 0.441 (P < 0.001). The social support and self-efficacy constructs together explain 26% of the variance in the learning engagement construct, with standardized regression coefficients of 0.424 (P < 0.001) and 0.155 (P < 0.01, respectively)."
