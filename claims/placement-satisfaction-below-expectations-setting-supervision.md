## Evidence

### Golos Anat 2019

Golos Anat, Tekuzener Esti. (2019). Perceptions, expectations and satisfaction levels of occupational therapy students prior to and after practice placement and comparison of practice placement models. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1762-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Paired t-test comparing all 155 students' pre- and post-placement scores on the placement setting and supervision category of the questionnaire. The article reports "a medium effect size" (Cohen's d = .44), with pre-scores M = 4.37 (SD = .45) higher than post-scores M = 4.12 (SD = .68).

> "the results of the paired t-test indicated significant differences with a medium effect size (t[df] = 3.960[154], p < .001, Cohen's d = .44) between the pre- and post-placement scores"
