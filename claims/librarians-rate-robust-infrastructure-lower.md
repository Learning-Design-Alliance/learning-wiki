# School librarians rated Robust Infrastructure significantly lower than both teachers and administrators

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The Robust Infrastructure construct showed significant librarian–teacher (p = .010) and librarian–administrator (p = .033) differences, with librarians rating themselves lowest (M = 2.04); the administrator–teacher comparison was not significant (p = .940). ### Felicia Warden · Andrew A. Tawfik 2026

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the survey (N = 186), the Robust Infrastructure omnibus test reached significance (p = .007); "a statistical significance between the school librarian and teacher roles (p =.010), and between the administrator and school librarian roles (p  =.033)". Librarians' mean was 2.04 versus teachers' 2.57.

> "post-hoc testing via pair-wise analysis and Tukey HSD identified a statistical significance between the school librarian and teacher roles (p =.010), and between the administrator and school librarian roles (p  =.033) on the construct of Robust Infrastructure"
