# A subset (16%) of introductory biology students report moderately high general classroom anxiety, and anxiety levels differ among the three classes

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 16% of surveyed introductory biology students had higher-than-average general class anxiety (mean ≥ 5 on a 1–7 scale). [→ England Benjamin J. 2017](#england-benjamin-j-2017)

## Evidence

### England Benjamin J. 2017

England Benjamin J., Brigati Jennifer R., Schussler Elisabeth E. (2017). Student anxiety in introductory biology classrooms: Perceptions about active learning and persistence in the major. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182506

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Online survey of 327 students across three introductory biology lecture classes using a 7-item general class anxiety sub-scale (α = 0.937); the distribution shows "16% of the total sample had higher than average general anxiety."

> "The distribution of general class anxiety means shows that 59 students had means between 1–1.99, 75 students had means from 2–2.99, 79 students had means from 3–3.99, 56 students had means from 4–4.99, 33 students had means from 5–5.99, 15 students had means from 6–6.99, and 2 students had means of 7."

### England Benjamin J. 2017 (2)

England Benjamin J., Brigati Jennifer R., Schussler Elisabeth E. (2017). Student anxiety in introductory biology classrooms: Perceptions about active learning and persistence in the major. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182506

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA with Tukey's post-hoc comparing mean general class anxiety among OEB (2.85 ± 0.14), CMB 1 (4.12 ± 0.20), and CMB 2 (3.21 ± 0.11); the printed effect size is Cohen's f = 0.32, a medium-to-large effect.

> "CMB 1 student general class anxiety was significantly higher than for students in the other two classes, which were not different from each other (one-way ANOVA followed by Tukey's post-hoc, p < 0.001). Effect size (Cohen's f) was calculated as 0.32, indicating a medium-to-large effect."
