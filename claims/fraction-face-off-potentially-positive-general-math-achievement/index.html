naep-1¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, March). Fraction Face-Off!. Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/702

q4 · i2 · causal · r3

Findings from the one RCT meeting WWC standards without reservations, using a collection of 19 researcher-selected items from the 1990-2009 NAEP fourth-grade assessment with 1,152 fourth-grade students. The intervention group mean was 14.83 versus 12.36 for comparison, effect size 0.64, p < .01.
