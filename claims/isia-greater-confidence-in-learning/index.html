Evidence¶

Fan et al. 2018¶

Xinxin Fan, David Geelan and Robyn Gillies. (2018). Evaluating a Novel Instructional Sequence for Conceptual Change in Physics Using Interactive Simulations. Education Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8010029

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA on the five-point confidence rank survey appended to the conceptual test at post-test; the experimental group mean (M = 75.65, SD = 8.88) exceeded the control mean (M = 67.60, SD = 12.57).

"There was a statistically signiﬁcant difference at the p < 0.05 level in scores for the two groups: F (1, 115) = 15.65, p = 0.000. The effect size, calculated using eta squared, was 0.12."
