### Gerd Kortemeyer 2024

Gerd Kortemeyer, Julian Nöhl, Daria Onishchuk. (2024). Grading Assistance for a Handwritten Thermodynamics Exam using Artificial Intelligence: An Exploratory Study. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020144](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020144)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis of TA versus AI part grades across all four problems (WF2, 252 exams). Graphical parts 2a (T-s diagram, 8 points) and 4a (p-T diagram, 4.5 points) showed no TA-AI correlation.

> "The pairs of vertices for the graphical problems 2a and 4a show no correlations (for 2a, the vertices are at opposite ends of the graph, while for 4a, the TA-vertex is completely disconnected)."
