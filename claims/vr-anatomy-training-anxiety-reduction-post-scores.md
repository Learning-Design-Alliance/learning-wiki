`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post questionnaire comparison in the study of 179 Tunisian medical students; the article reports the descriptive means and SDs and calls the shifts statistically significant, but prints no effect size, so none is reported here.

> "The mean score for “Anxiety before exams” (M = 2.70, SD = 1.10) is lower than “Reduction of anxiety” (M = 4.11, SD = 0.87), suggesting that VR training might help alleviate students’ pre-exam anxiety."
