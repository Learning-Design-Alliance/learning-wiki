## Evidence

### Rocha de Macedo Bruno 2025

Rocha de Macedo Bruno, Lima Carolina Saldanha, Haydar Ahmed, Holanda Marcelo Alcantara, Hayashi Fatima Kiyoko, Ferreira Juliana Carvalho. (2025). Impact of a Competency-based Mechanical Ventilation Course Using Virtual Simulation. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.34197/ats-scholar.2024-0083OC

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple linear regression on the 81 completing residents' virtual SBA scores, adjusted for post-graduate year and baseline MCE score. The β-coefficient for baseline MCE was 0.27 (P = 0.02); unstandardized regression coefficients are not treated as effect sizes, so no impact magnitude is assigned.

> "In the multiple linear regression, the baseline MCE score was significantly associated with the virtual SBA score with a β-coefficient of 0.27 (95% CI, 0.04–0.51; P = 0.02), whereas post-graduate year was not (β-coefficient, 0.02; 95% CI, −0.65, 0.69; P = 0.9)."
