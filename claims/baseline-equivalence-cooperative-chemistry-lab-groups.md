## Evidence

### Tawfik A. Saleh 2011

Tawfik A. Saleh. (2011). Statistical Analysis of Cooperative Strategy Compared with Individualistic Strategy: An Application Study. The Journal of Effective Teaching, 11(1), 19-27. https://www.uncw.edu/jet/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-application analysis of report scores during the first three experiments, when both groups were taught individualistically; Table 2 reports means of 80.67 and 80.33 with SDs of 7.30 and 7.40, and the null hypothesis was accepted at alpha = 0.05. Equivalence was not tested beyond this non-significant t-test.

> "The mean difference of 0.34, t = -0.277, P = 0.808 indicated that the difference of the results between the control group and the experimental group was not significant ( p > 0.05)."
