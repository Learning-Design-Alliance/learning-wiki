`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of child-initiated conversations coded during Cohort 1 in-person sessions, used as a measure of children's science talk. The report states "25% of the intervention families’ after episode viewing prompts were found to address science, while none of the prompts from comparison families were science related," with stronger differences after than before viewing.
