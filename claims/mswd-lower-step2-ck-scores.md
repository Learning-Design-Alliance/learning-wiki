### Nguyen Mytien 2026

Nguyen Mytien, Kim Michael, Pereira-Lima Karina, Hodgens Tiffany, Juliao Jordan, Holman Elizabeth, Sangsland J. T., Boatright Dowin, Meeks Lisa M. (2026). USMLE Step 2 Clinical Knowledge performance by disability, race, and ethnicity in U.S. medical students: A multi-site study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0349774

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional analysis of the Pathways II dataset (nine U.S. MD-granting schools, 2020–2023 graduates), with students with disabilities matched 1:2 to nondisabled peers by institution, gender, cohort, and MCAT score; multivariable linear regression adjusting for MCAT found "medical students with disabilities scoring 7-points lower than nondisabled students."

> "medical students with disabilities scoring 7-points lower than nondisabled students (239.6 vs 246.7; adjusted β: −7.02; 95%CI: −8.76 to −5.28)"
