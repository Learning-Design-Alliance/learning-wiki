# Special issues of IJAIED in 1997–2001 played a significant part in developing AIED sub-fields such as collaborative learning and modelling teaching

## Evidence

### John Self 2015

This is the editor's retrospective assessment of the journal he edited. He reports special issues on "authoring systems, collaborative learning, modelling teaching, educational dialogues and web-based teaching", many derived from AIED conference sessions or workshops, and states they played a significant part in developing those sub-fields; the assessment is an untested personal judgement.

> "In the years 1997 –2001 we had a number of special issues on topics such as authoring systems, collaborative learning, modelling teaching, educational dialogues and web-based teaching, all of which I feel played a significant part in developing those sub-fields of AIED."
