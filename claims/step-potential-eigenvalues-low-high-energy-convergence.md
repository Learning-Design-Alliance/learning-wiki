## Evidence

### F. Marsiglio 2008

F. Marsiglio. (2008). The harmonic oscillator in quantum mechanics: A third way. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.3051 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0806.3051](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0806.3051)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Numerical eigenvalue study of the step potential (Fig. 3, v0 = 4000) shows the particle "ﬁnds itself in an essentially inﬁnite square well of width a/2" at low energies. The article adds: "Beyond quantum number n = 80 the eigenvalues, with a simple accounting for the step v0/2, agree with those of an inﬁnite square well."

> "For low-lying eigenvalues the agreement is very good. In other words, the particle ﬁnds itself in an essentially inﬁnite square well of width a/2."
