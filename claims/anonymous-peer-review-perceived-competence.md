### Petersen 2021 (2)

Proceedings of the 1st Virtual Conference on Implications of Information and Digital Technologies for Development, 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.09955](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.09955)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

From the same case study's perceived-competence theme on eLearning-system skills: platform difficulties hindered completion, though some students resolved them with lecturer assistance; the exercise was also perceived as confusing and uncomfortable by some students.

> "A finding that negatively impacted student engagement was that the majority of students found that they could not complete the anonymous peer review due to issues related to the Sakai platform used."
