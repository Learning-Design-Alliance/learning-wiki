## Evidence

### Tejinder Kaur 2017

Tejinder Kaur, David Blair, Rahul Kumar Choudhary, Yohanes Sudarmo Dua, Alexander Foppoli, and Marjan Zadnik. (2017). Gender response to Einsteinian physics interventions in School. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1712.06323](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1712.06323)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired sample t-tests comparing pre and post conceptual test scores in two Australian Year 9 interventions and one Indonesian Year 10 intervention. The article reports that "conceptual understanding towards Einsteinian physics improved significantly" in all three interventions, with no students decreasing their scores.

> "For intervention 3, it was calculated as t(44) = 20.1, p < .05, for intervention 4, it was calculated as  t(56) = 30.3, p < .05, while for intervention 7, it was t(30) = 19.98, p < .05"
