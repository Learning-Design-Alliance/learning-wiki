### Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin 2026

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive/bivariate analysis of the N = 644 sample: "moderately correlated" per the article; r = 0.29 and r = 0.26, both p < 0.001.

> "Poorer M1-grades are moderately correlated with lower school performance (r = 0.29, p < 0.001) and higher age at enrolment (r = 0.26, p < 0.001)."

### Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin 2026 (2)

Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin. (2026). Student background, admission routes, and academic success: a structural mediation analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09068-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate Mann-Whitney U-tests on Table 1 means (AQ 1.51, SQ 1.91, WQ 2.17); the article notes WQ students still achieved a solid "good" M1-grade on average.

> "Students admitted via AQ achieve significantly better average M1 grades than those in SQ (p < 0.001, Mann-Whitney U-test), who in turn outperform students admitted via WQ (p = 0.001)."
