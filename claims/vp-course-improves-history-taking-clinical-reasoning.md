## Evidence

### Isaza-Restrepo Andrés 2018

Isaza-Restrepo Andrés, Gómez María Teresa, Cifuentes Gary, Argüello Arturo. (2018). The virtual patient as a learning tool: a mixed quantitative qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1395-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental before-and-after study with 20 voluntary undergraduate medical students without prior semiology training; performance with an actor patient was videotaped and scored 1-3 by three evaluators using a validated matrix, analyzed with the Wilcoxon signed rank test. The article reports "significantly improved after taking the course" with Table 3 printing z and p values (e.g., interview evaluator 1: z = −2.37 p = 0.01).

> "The student skills of history taking and clinical reasoning significantly improved after taking the course (Table 3)."
