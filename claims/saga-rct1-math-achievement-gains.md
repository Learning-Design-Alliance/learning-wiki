# Saga tutoring significantly raises standardized math achievement for at-risk 9th and 10th graders in the first Chicago RCT (ITT +0.09, TOT +0.18)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Students randomized to Saga scored significantly higher on standardized math tests than controls receiving routine CPS services, with an ITT effect size of +0.09 and a TOT effect size of +0.18. [→ Saga Math (Evidence Summary) 2020](#saga-math-evidence-summary-2020)

## Evidence

### Saga Math (Evidence Summary) 2020

Saga Math (Evidence Summary). (2020). Evidence for ESSA program evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/saga-math/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

First RCT (2013-14, 12 high-poverty Chicago high schools) randomized over 2,000 at-risk male 9th and 10th graders to Saga or routine CPS services; after one year the ITT analysis showed Saga students "scoring significantly higher on standardized math achievement test scores (effect size = +0.09)"; the printed TOT effect size was +0.18.

> "the intent-to-treat (ITT) analysis, which includes students assigned to Saga tutoring regardless of any uptake, resulted in Saga students scoring significantly higher on standardized math achievement test scores (effect size = +0.09)"
