## Evidence

### Remy Dou and Justyna P. Zwolak 2019

Remy Dou and Justyna P. Zwolak. (2019). Practitioner’s guide to social network analysis: Examining physics anxiety in an active-learning setting. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020105](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020105)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Permutation simple linear regressions on end-of-semester network data tested indegree, outdegree, and closeness at four α values. The article reports that after "adjusted for false positives (type I error)", "only out- degree remains signiﬁcant (for all α)"; no effect size is printed for closeness.

> "Out- degree (regardless of α) and closeness ( α > 0) are sig- niﬁcant predictors of normalized anxiety shift. However, when adjusted for false positives (type I error), only out- degree remains signiﬁcant (for all α)."
