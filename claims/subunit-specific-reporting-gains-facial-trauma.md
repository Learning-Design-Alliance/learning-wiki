## Evidence

### Malouf William T. 2024

Malouf William T., Kondaveeti Geeth, Phillips Jacline G., Patel Kunjan, Hall Justin A., Fourrey Torrey L., May Nelson, Meegalla Nuwan T., Reger Kevin J., Runyan Christopher M., Hiatt Kevin D. (2024). Facial trauma education in radiology: using surgeon feedback as the benchmark for success. Emergency Radiology 31(6):807-814. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10140-024-02288-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blinded surgeon-graded pre/post assessment of 26 participants' reports found significant gains for Le Fort, nasoseptal, and NOE subunits but not for upper face, ZMC, or mandible; the article calls the unimproved covered subunits "unexpected shortcomings" of the intervention.

> "In subunit analysis, there were significant improvements in reporting Le Fort, nasoseptal, and nasoorbitoethmoid fractures. No significant improvements occurred in the reporting of upper face, zygomaticomaxillary complex, or mandibular fractures."

### Malouf William T. 2024 (2)

Malouf William T., Kondaveeti Geeth, Phillips Jacline G., Patel Kunjan, Hall Justin A., Fourrey Torrey L., May Nelson, Meegalla Nuwan T., Reger Kevin J., Runyan Christopher M., Hiatt Kevin D. (2024). Facial trauma education in radiology: using surgeon feedback as the benchmark for success. Emergency Radiology 31(6):807-814. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10140-024-02288-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-assessment grading by otolaryngology evaluators showed the lowest mean scores for ZMC and NOE subunits, concordant with prior literature the authors cite; the survey separately found 88.5% of participants named NOE as their least correctly reported subunit.

> "On analysis of assessments graded by the evaluators, the ZMC and NOE subunits had the lowest mean scores on pre-assessments."
