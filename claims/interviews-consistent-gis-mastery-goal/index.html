Evidence¶

Soon Singh Bikar Singh 2016¶

Soon Singh Bikar Singh, Balan Rathakrishnan, Sabariah Sharif, Rosy Talin, Oliver Valentine Eboy. (2016). The Effects of Geography Information System (GIS) Based Teaching on Underachieving Students' Mastery Goal and Achievement. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 15(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117621

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Thematic analysis of standardized open-ended interviews with five experiment group students, conducted after the eight-week intervention; all interviewed experiment participants (100%) said they had achieved their main goal in the class.

"The experiment group student interviews revealed they were excited to learn about GIS. This was because they participated actively in every activity conducted by the teache r in the classroom."

Soon Singh Bikar Singh 2016 (2)¶

Soon Singh Bikar Singh, Balan Rathakrishnan, Sabariah Sharif, Rosy Talin, Oliver Valentine Eboy. (2016). The Effects of Geography Information System (GIS) Based Teaching on Underachieving Students' Mastery Goal and Achievement. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 15(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117621

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Interviews with five control group students selected for highest post-test scores; the article states this indicated traditional teaching methods had a moderate effect on control participants' mastery goal. Two interviewees reported difficulty understanding lessons.

"However, two of the participants confessed that they were unable to achieve their learning goal because they were unable to understand the lessons delivered in the classroom."
