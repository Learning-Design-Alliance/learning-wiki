Evidence¶

Ryan S. Baker 2016¶

Ryan S. Baker. (2016). Stupid Tutoring Systems, Intelligent Humans. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0105-0

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The author's observational analysis of the ITS field compares deployed systems (SQL-Tutor, ALEKS, Cognitive Tutor, ASSISTments) against the original research vision, noting that "many systems in wide use depend on simple heuristics to assess student mastery, such as whether the student gets three right in a row."

"In fact, the most widely used intelligent tutoring systems are in some ways the furthest from the initial vision of researchers like Carbonell and Self."
