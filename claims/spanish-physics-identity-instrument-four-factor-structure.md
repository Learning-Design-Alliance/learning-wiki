## Evidence

### O. I. González-Peña 2024

O. I. González-Peña, G. Morán-Soto, and B. M. Rodríguez-Lara. (2024). Decoding physics identity: A Spanish-language adaptation on an instrument and its correlation with STEM achievement. [doi:10.1057/s41599-024-04112-z](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-024-04112-z)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Split-half exploratory factor analysis of the adapted 29-item instrument with 167 Mexican STEM students (KMO=0.88), using ordinary least squares estimation and promax rotation. The authors report the "four-factor model ... accounted for 54% of the variance".

> "The four-factor model used for the EF A accounted for 54% of the variance for the analysed items, which is within the accepted ranges for new instruments in social science research"
