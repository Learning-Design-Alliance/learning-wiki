Fives 2017¶

Fives, Helenrose, Barnes, Nicole, Buehl, Michelle, Mascadri, Julia, & Ziegler, Nathan. (2017). Teachers’ epistemic cognition in classroom assessment. Educational Psychologist, 52(4), pp. 270-283. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2017.1323218

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the section on epistemic cognition in teaching: teachers extend consideration of a topic like formative assessment beyond personal learning to its use for teaching particular content to particular students, "thereby adding a layer of epistemic cognition that is not present for learners" (Fives & Buehl, 2008, 2010, as cited by the authors).

"This requires teachers to integrate multiple bodies of knowledge (e.g., knowledge of pedagogy, subject matter, and students) and to manage learning goals for both their students"
