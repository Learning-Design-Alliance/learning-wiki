Spearman correlation analysis of the survey's three dimension scores. All three pairwise correlations are positive and statistically significant, with the strongest pair being attitude and practice at "r = 0.450, p < 0.001".

> "Spearman correlation analysis revealed positive relationships between knowledge and attitude (r = 0.243, p < 0.001), knowledge and practice (r = 0.319, p < 0.001) and attitude and practice (r = 0.450, p < 0.001)."
