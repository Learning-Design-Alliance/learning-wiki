## Evidence

### N. M. Chase 2024

N. M. Chase. (2024). Survey on the Teacher-Learner Collaboration. MCPHS University. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2401.02964](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2401.02964)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bar-graph analysis (Figures 6-8) of pooled survey responses from 650 students over 12 semesters of PHY 270 and PHY 274. The exam-too-difficult group also tended to report not feeling encouraged to ask questions, weaker math preparedness, lower GPAs, and lower overall course ratings.

> "Students who reported that they found exams too difficult (only 7.3%) tended  to find the material too difficult."
