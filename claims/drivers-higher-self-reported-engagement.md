## Evidence

### Qi Zhou 2024

Qi Zhou, Wannapon Suraworachet, and Mutlu Cukurova. 2024. Harnessing Transparent Learning Analytics for Individualized Support through Auto-detection of Engagement in Face-to-Face Collaborative Learning. In The 14th Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3636555.3636894

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

T-test comparison of self-reported engagement across the two automatically detected engagement clusters from 76 valid post-session questionnaire responses collected over twenty collaborative sessions in a 10-week postgraduate module. Drivers scored higher (“Mean=3.86, SD=.356”) than passengers, with “p=.021, Cohen’s d=.420”. The authors state this shows alignment between detected and self-reported engagement.

> "“drivers” (n=28, Mean=3.86, SD=.356) had signi￿cantly higher self-reported engagement scores than “passengers” (n=48, Mean =3.60, SD=.707), p=.021, Cohen’s d=.420."
