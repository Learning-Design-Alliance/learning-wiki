`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results of the console phase of the 30-resident study, analyzed with t-tests and reported in Table 6. Table 6 also prints mean total console scores of 15.33 (Group A) versus 14.40 (Group B), p = 0.3801, and the endoscope-use exercise showed no significant difference (p = 0.5309). No effect sizes are printed.
