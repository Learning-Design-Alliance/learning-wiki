# Pilot test events represented fewer grade levels overall, with more grade levels represented for low- and high-performing students, while the comparison group showed the opposite pattern

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In grade 3 math, only two grade levels were represented in the pilot group versus six in the comparison group; the middle 80% of pilot students had items spanning three grade levels versus six for comparison students. [→ J. Patrick Meyer 2023](#j-patrick-meyer-2023)
`q2 i?` More grades were represented for the lower and upper 10% of pilot students than for the middle 80%, supporting the off-grade adaptation claim; the comparison group showed the opposite pattern. [→ J. Patrick Meyer 2023 (2)](#j-patrick-meyer-2023-2)

## Evidence

### J. Patrick Meyer 2023

J. Patrick Meyer, Ann Hu, Sylvia Li. (2023). Content Proximity Spring 2022 Pilot Study Research Brief. Psychometric Solutions, NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/content-proximity-spring-2022-pilot-study-research-report/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of Table 2.1 (number of item grades represented with 5% or more items, by decile groups) showed pilot grade 3 math items spanned "only two grade levels" versus six in the comparison group.

> "For example, only two grade levels were represented by items in grade 3 math pilot group, but 6 grade levels were represented by items in the comparison group (relevant numbers marked in Table 2.1 with superscript 1)."

### J. Patrick Meyer 2023 (2)

J. Patrick Meyer, Ann Hu, Sylvia Li. (2023). Content Proximity Spring 2022 Pilot Study Research Brief. Psychometric Solutions, NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/content-proximity-spring-2022-pilot-study-research-report/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 2.1 analysis by ability decile groups showed more grades represented for the lower and upper 10% of pilot students than the middle 80%, while "the middle 80% tended to have more grades represented" in the comparison group, the opposite of expectations.

> "It indicates that more grades were represented by items for in the lower and upper pilot study groups than for the middle 80%. In the comparison group, the middle 80% tended to have more grades represented than either the lower or upper 10%."
