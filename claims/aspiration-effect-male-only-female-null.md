## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kamimura Go 2025

Kamimura Go, Maeda Koki, Aoki Masaya, Imamura Satomi, Morizono Shoichiro, Tokunaga Takuya, Umehara Tadashi, Harada-Takeda Aya, Nagata Toshiyuki, Ueda Kazuhiro. (2025). Importance of future motivation for skill acquisition among medical students undergoing endoscopic forceps training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07048-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sex-stratified ANCOVA of skill-acquisition rate in the same 98-student cohort; the effect held among males (p = 0.004) but not females (p = 0.68), which the article attributes to the small number of female aspiring surgeons.

> "Although aspirations to become a surgeon significantly impacted the rate of skill acquisition among males (p = 0.004), it showed no significant impact in females, possibly due to the small number of female aspiring surgeons (p = 0.68)."

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`
