Archibald D, Macdonald Colla J, Plante J, Hogue RJ, Fiallos J. (2014). Residents' and preceptors' perceptions of the use of the iPad for clinical teaching in a family medicine residency program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-174

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Non-parametric Mann–Whitney tests compared resident and preceptor survey ratings in the pilot. On the curriculum-confidence item residents' mean (SD) was 3.2 (0.58) versus preceptors' 2.76 (0.89), p = 0.04; all other items were not significantly different.

> "Non-parametric Mann–Whitney tests showed that perceptions between residents and preceptors were not significantly different, except for the item labeled "Helped me develop confidence in my understanding of the FM curriculum" (residents's mean (SD) = 3.2 (0.58), preceptors' mean (SD) = 2.76 (0.89), p = 0.04)"
