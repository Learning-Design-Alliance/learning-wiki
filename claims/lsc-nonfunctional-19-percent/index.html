Bryk 1993¶

Bryk, A. S., Easton, J. Q., Kerbow, D., Rollow, S. G., & Sebring, P. A. (1993). A View from the Elementary Schools: The State of Reform in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/view-elementary-schools-state-reform-chicago

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey-indicator analysis of LSC functioning. Because the Consortium data archive lacked LSC survey data, the report could measure only whether the LSC was a functional working group, using seven indicators such as meeting at least once a month and having at least one subcommittee; "19 percent of the LSCs fall into this nonfunctional category."

"When the LSC indicators are examined for all Chicago public elementary schwls, we find that 19 percent of the LSCs fall into this nonfunctional category."
