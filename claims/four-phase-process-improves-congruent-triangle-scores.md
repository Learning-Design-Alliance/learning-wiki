# A four-phase teaching process for congruent triangles significantly improves grade 8 students' test scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the four-phase intervention, the experimental class's mean score rose from 5.7 to 6.55, a statistically significant difference on a paired-samples t-test. [→ Uyen 2021](#uyen-2021)

## Evidence

### Uyen 2021

Uyen, B. P., Tong, D. H., & Tram, N. T. B. (2021). Developing mathematical communication skills for students in grade 8 in teaching congruent triangle topics. European Journal of Educational Research, 10(3), 1287-1302. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.10.3.1287

`q2 · i?`

Single-group pre-test/post-test experiment with 40 grade 8 students in Vietnam, analyzed with a paired sample t-test in SPSS 25. The article reports "a sig value of 0.000 < 0.05", so the null hypothesis of no difference was rejected; means were 5.7 (pre) and 6.55 (post).
