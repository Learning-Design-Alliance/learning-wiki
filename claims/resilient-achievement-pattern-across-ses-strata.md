## Evidence

### Rémeau Margot 2026

Rémeau Margot, Raffy Gaël, Borst Grégoire. (2026). Academic Resilience in Contexts of Inequality: Motivational and Self‐Efficacy Profiles of Disadvantaged High Achievers. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1002/jad.70190

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Holm‐adjusted post hoc comparisons on the academic achievement index across nine learner profiles showed that "low‐SES high achievers (academically resilient learners) significantly outperformed all low‐ and mid‐achieving profiles across SES strata (all ps < 0.001)," yet "their academic achievement remained similar to that of high‐achieving students from mid‐SES backgrounds and lower than that of high‐SES high‐achieving students."

> "low‐SES high achievers (academically resilient learners) significantly outperformed all low‐ and mid‐achieving profiles across SES strata (all ps < 0.001), including high‐SES low achievers. However, their academic achievement remained similar to that of high‐achieving students from mid‐SES backgrounds and lower than that of high‐SES high‐achieving students, indicating that socioeconomic advantage continues to confer an additional performance benefit even among high achievers."
