## Evidence

### Nair Akshay Gopinathan et al 2021

Nair Akshay Gopinathan et al. (2021). Effectiveness of simulation-based training for manual small incision cataract surgery among novice surgeons: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-90410-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted analysis from the same RCT (10 simulation vs 9 control trainees) using an F-test from type II sums of squares. The article reports the group effect was "statistically significant for major errors (P = 0.02)", with adjusted means of 4.86 (95% CI 0.13–9.59) in the simulation group and 10.09 (95% CI 4.76–15.41) in the control group, a 52% reduction. No effect size is printed.

> "The effect of the group variable was not statistically significant for total and minor errors (P = 0.05 and 0.16, respectively), and statistically significant for major errors (P = 0.02)."
