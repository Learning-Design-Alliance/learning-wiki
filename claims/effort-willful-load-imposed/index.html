Tino Endres 2025¶

Tino Endres, Lisa Bender, Stoo Sepp, Shirong Zhang, Louise David, Melanie Trypke, et al. (2025). Developing the Mental Effort and Load–Translingual Scale (MEL‑TS) as a Foundation for Translingual Research in Self‑Regulated Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09978-8

q2 · i? · design · r3

Qualitative interview analysis of bilingual participants' reflections on effort and load concepts. The article reports effort was "described as an active investment that is made willfully and controllably", and separately that mental load was described as "something imposed or even forced that cannot be controlled easily (English and French)". Descriptive qualitative finding; no effect size.

"Overall, effort was described as an active investment that is made willfully and controllably and depends on motivation."
