Theoretical argument from the paper's framing of the field. The author asserts that the field's instrumental orientation has produced "a theoretical misalignment between contemporary AI systems... and established pedagogical understandings of collaborative learning." No empirical data are offered for this characterization.

"Research on Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED) has predominantly conceptualized AI as a tool for automation, personalization, or instructional efficiency, often privileging individual learning outcomes over social and relational processes."
