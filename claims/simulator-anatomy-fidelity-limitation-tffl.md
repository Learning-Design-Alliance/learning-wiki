Use of a Low-fidelity simulator to improve trans-nasal fibre-optic flexible laryngoscopy in the clinical setting: a randomized, single-blinded, prospective study. `q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

This is the authors' interpretation in the Discussion, not a tested result; the article offers it as a possible explanation for the null simulation effect, alongside the challenges of live-patient endoscopy.

> "Based on our observations, the most challenging aspect of the endoscopy was navigating the nasal cavity. This anatomy was not reflected in the design of the simulator. It may be that, in this case, a simulator that better reflected nasal anatomy would be more beneficial."
