## Evidence

### Walck-Shannon 2021

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Rowell, S. F., & Frey, R. F. (2021). To What Extent Do Study Habits Relate to Performance? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-05-0091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression in a large introductory biology course (exam 1 n = 422; exam 2 n = 456), controlling for ACT math, pretest, absences, and total study hours. The article reports "ΔR2 = 0.024" for exam 1 and "ΔR2 = 0.066" for exam 2, with each additional active strategy worth 1.9% and 2.8% of exam score.

> "the number of active strategies students used added significant additional predictive value for exam 1, (F(1, 416) = 33.698, p < 0.0001 ΔR2 = 0.024; and exam 2, F(1, 450) = 91.083, p < 0.0001, ΔR2 = 0.066."
