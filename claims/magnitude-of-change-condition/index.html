q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical prediction in the reply, illustrated with emotional design: Endres et al. (2024) found stronger effects when implementation included a narrative frame (stronger manipulation) rather than only warm colors and background music (weaker manipulation).

"yielding the new testable prediction that a stronger manipulation in terms of a stronger increase in reward (e.g., by increasing more value facets, or value facets to a stronger degree) of an instructional technique should yield significant learning gains with a higher likelihood."
