Evidence¶

Eddery R. 2026¶

Eddery R., Leith R. (2026). Practitioner-related factors that may influence knowledge and confidence of TDI management in children; a scoping review. European Archives of Paediatric Dentistry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40368-026-01162-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review synthesis of the three included confidence studies (self-reported Likert-scale confidence). The review reports longer-qualified GDPs showed greater confidence than those with less experience. No effect sizes printed.

"Matoug-Elwerfelli et al. (2022) reported that GDPs qualified more than 5 years prior showed greater confidence in managing TDIs affecting the permanent dentition than those with less years of experience. Bani-Hani et al. (2024) found that GDPs with 5–10 years of experience were most confident in TDI management."

Eddery R. 2026 (2)¶

Eddery R., Leith R. (2026). Practitioner-related factors that may influence knowledge and confidence of TDI management in children; a scoping review. European Archives of Paediatric Dentistry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40368-026-01162-6

q1 · i? · review · r2

Authors' interpretation across the review's findings: recently qualified GDPs have the highest knowledge but the least confidence, an association the review frames as a possibility rather than a tested result.

"Our results suggest the possibility of an interesting inverse relationship between knowledge and confidence of TDI management with regard to years of experience."
