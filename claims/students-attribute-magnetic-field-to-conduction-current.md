### Álvaro Suárez 2024

Álvaro Suárez, Arturo C. Marti, Kristina Zuza, Jenaro Guisasola. (2024). Learning difficulties among students when applying Ampère-Maxwell's law and its implications for teaching. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010143](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010143)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenographic analysis of written answers to question 3, which describes field lines of cylindrical symmetry detected in an empty region. "Category C (66,2%) includes the students' reasoning when they associate the magnetic field with conduction currents"; two out of three answers fell in this category.

> "Category C (66,2%) includes the students' reasoning when they associate the magnetic field with conduction currents."

### Álvaro Suárez 2024 (2)

Álvaro Suárez, Arturo C. Marti, Kristina Zuza, Jenaro Guisasola. (2024). Learning difficulties among students when applying Ampère-Maxwell's law and its implications for teaching. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010143](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010143)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Written-test category analysis for question 3: only "category A (9.2%)" answers "recognise the presence of a variable electric field associated with the magnetic field"; a further 9,2% associated the field with a static electric field.

> "The explanations included in category A (9.2%) are based on arguments that recognise the presence of a variable electric field associated with the magnetic field demonstrated in the question."
