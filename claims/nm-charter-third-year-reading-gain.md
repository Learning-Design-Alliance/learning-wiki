### Charter School Performance in New Mexico 2019

Charter School Performance in New Mexico. (2019). Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-new-mexico-2/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Matched VCR analysis (Figure 6) limited to students first enrolling in a charter between 2014-15 and 2016-17, grouped by first, second, and third year of enrollment; reading advantage in year three of 124 days.

> "they outpace the average TPS VCRs in reading in the third year of charter enrollment and their advantage is equivalent to 124 extra days of learning."

### Charter School Performance in New Mexico 2019 (2)

Charter School Performance in New Mexico. (2019). Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-new-mexico-2/

Same years-of-enrollment analysis (Figure 6): math growth does not differ significantly from TPS VCRs in any of the first three years.

> "In math, the growth of new charter school students is not significantly different from that of average TPS VCRs throughout the first three years of charter school enrollment."
