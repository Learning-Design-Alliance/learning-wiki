Scientific Reports 13, 11413. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-38455-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Generalised estimating equations analysis of repeated age-for-grade measures among 982 children aged 6–13 in the Karonga HDSS cohort, adjusted for ten baseline confounders including SES and HIV status. The article reports "some evidence" with "aOR = 0.82; 95%CI = 0.64–1.06"; the confidence interval crosses 1.

> "adjusted results provided some evidence that children exclusively breastfed for 6 months were less likely to be over-age for grade than those exclusively breastfed for less than 3 months (aOR = 0.82; 95%CI = 0.64–1.06)."
