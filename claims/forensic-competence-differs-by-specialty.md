## Evidence

### Özkök Alper 2025

Özkök Alper, Saçal Seda, Şimşek Ümit, Terece Cem, Helvacı Özant, Akar Taner, Demirel Birol. (2025). Residents’ perspectives on forensic issues and the impact of postgraduate forensic medicine education on perceived competence and attitudes: a multidisciplinary pre- and post-test study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08259-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interspecialty comparison of pre-training scores using Kruskal–Wallis tests with Bonferroni-adjusted Mann–Whitney U pairwise comparisons (Table 2). "the differences were due to higher scores given by EM residents and lower scores given by OG residents" on the two forensic-report knowledge propositions. No effect size reported.

> "Pairwise comparisons revealed that, for the first two propositions, the differences were due to higher scores given by EM residents and lower scores given by OG residents."

### Özkök Alper 2025 (2)

Özkök Alper, Saçal Seda, Şimşek Ümit, Terece Cem, Helvacı Özant, Akar Taner, Demirel Birol. (2025). Residents’ perspectives on forensic issues and the impact of postgraduate forensic medicine education on perceived competence and attitudes: a multidisciplinary pre- and post-test study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08259-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interspecialty descriptive comparison of pre-training concern and reporting-preference items (Table 2). GS residents "expressed the highest concern about making mistakes in forensic reports (mean: 4.29)" and were "the most likely to avoid issuing final reports (mean: 1.57)". Means are descriptive; no effect size reported.

> "It was notable that GS residents, who expressed the highest concern about making mistakes in forensic reports (mean: 4.29), were also the most likely to avoid issuing final reports (mean: 1.57)."
