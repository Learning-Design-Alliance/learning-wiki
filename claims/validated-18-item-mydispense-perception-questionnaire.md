### Palanisamy A 2024

The effectiveness of a structured validated questionnaire to assess student perception with virtual pharmacy simulation in pharmacy practice experiential education. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0314117

Instrument validation with 200 students at one pharmacy faculty using content and construct validity procedures. Reliability coefficients exceeded 0.8 in all four domains; CFA fit indices were "χ2/df (2.16), RMSEA (0.076), SRMR (0.045), CFI (0.938), and TLI (0.927)". The article prints no effect sizes.
