Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, San Antonio, TX. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/robust-irt-scaling-considerations-in-constructing-item-bank-from-tests-across-years/

Results section comparison of mean scale scores across 45 administrations (Figure 4; Tables 2-3). For example, 1992F summed-scoring means were 513.72 (CH_S), 514.37 (SM_S), and 424.92 (CR_S), showing the CR means were "consistently lower."

"With the CR method, the score means across years were relatively less fluctuated and consistently lower, more so with the older tests, than those from the separate calibration methods."
