Alizadeh Maryam, Masoomi Rasoul, Mafinejad Mahboobeh Khabaz, Parmelee Dean, Khalaf Rusul Jasim, Norouzi Ali. (2024). Team-based learning in health professions education: an umbrella review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06147-x

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Umbrella review synthesis of 23 reviews: 17 reported cognitive outcomes, 13 of which compared TBL with lecture-based learning; students "performed better in theoretical exams, including final exams, midterm exams, and national board exams" under TBL.

> "The evidence consistently supported the superiority of TBL in enhancing students’ cognitive performance. Among the 23 reviews analyzed, 17 reviews (74%) reported on the cognitive outcomes of TBL."
