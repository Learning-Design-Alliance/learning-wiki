## Evidence

### Mohammad Saber Khaghaninezhad & Ghasem Jafarzadeh 2014

Mohammad Saber Khaghaninezhad & Ghasem Jafarzadeh. (2014). Investigating the Effect of Reduced Forms Instruction on EFL Learners' Listening and Speaking Abilities. English Language Teaching, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n1p159

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-test on a two-minute recorded speaking task scored by the number of reduced forms produced; the experimental mean rose from 0.60 to 1.96. The control group's change from 0.68 to 0.72 was not significant (p = .161).

> "Table 10 indicates that the experimental group made a significant improvement in the "reduced forms" production post-test (p= .000 < .05) which suggests the "reduced forms" instruction was effective in improving participants' "reduced forms" production."

### Mohammad Saber Khaghaninezhad & Ghasem Jafarzadeh 2014 (2)

Mohammad Saber Khaghaninezhad & Ghasem Jafarzadeh. (2014). Investigating the Effect of Reduced Forms Instruction on EFL Learners' Listening and Speaking Abilities. English Language Teaching, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n1p159

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test comparing both groups (25 each) on the production post-test; experimental mean 1.96 versus control mean 0.72. The article concludes reduced-forms practice increased learners' ability to produce reduced forms in daily conversations.

> "A third independent-samples t-test depicted that there was statistically significant difference between these two group's performances in the "reduced forms" production post-test (p = 0.000 < .05)."
