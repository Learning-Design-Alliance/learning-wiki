## Evidence

### Louise M Blakemore 2020

Louise M Blakemore, Sarah E M Meek, Leah K Marks. (2020). Equipping Learners to Evaluate Online Health Care Resources: Longitudinal Study of Learning Design Strategies in a Health Care Massive Open Online Course. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/15177

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Quasi-experimental longitudinal comparison across 8 runs of a 6-week FutureLearn MOOC, using mean references per summary assignment as a proxy for digital literacy skills. The article reports "significantly higher (3.5) than in Run 1 (1.8)" with P=.001 (t test, df=186).

> "The average number of references cited in Run 8 was significantly higher (3.5) than in Run 1 (1.8) of the MOOC (P=.001)."
