Evidence¶

Xin Sun 2023¶

Xin Sun, Owen Norton and Shaylene E. Nancekivell. (2023). Beware the myth: learning styles affect parents', children's, and teachers' thinking about children's academic potential. npj Science of Learning 8:46. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00190-x

q3 · i3 · causal · r3

Pre-registered Hypothesis 2A analysis in Experiment 3 tested grade predictions across four core subjects. The visual learner was rated higher in language arts, math, and social studies (d = 1.02, 1.07, 0.97), "but not science", where the difference was non-significant (d = 0.12).
