## Evidence

### Kim 2005

Kim, K. H. (2005). Can Only Intelligent People Be Creative? A Meta-Analysis. The Journal of Secondary Gifted Education, XVI(2/3), 57-66. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Can+Only+Intelligent+People+Be+Creative

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis across 186 male and 180 female correlation coefficients. Neither the post hoc contrast (p = .736) nor the a priori χ2 test (χ2 = .614, p = .433) was significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "Post hoc contrasts revealed no statistically significant differences (p = .736) between males and females. On an a priori basis, males (r = .149) and females (r = .159) were not statistically different either, χ2 = .614, p = .433."
