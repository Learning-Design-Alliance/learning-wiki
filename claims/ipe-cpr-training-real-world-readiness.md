### Fenzi Giulio 2024

Fenzi Giulio, Alemán-Jiménez Carolina, López-Ferrándiz Lucia, Leal-Costa Cesar, Díaz-Agea José Luis. (2024). Enhancing Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Training: An Interprofessional Approach With Undergraduate Medicine and Nursing Students Using Self-Learning Methodology in Simulated Environments (MAES)—A Qualitative Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/9470402

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group findings from the same qualitative study. A student noted neither profession had trained together before: "I liked sharing the experience between medicine and nursing students, because in a few months we will be working together" (S12); another called it "very enriching on a personal and human level" (S13).

> "Interprofessional training fosters collaboration between medical and nursing students, enriching their perspectives, enhancing real-world readiness, and improving skills crucial for their future work environments."
