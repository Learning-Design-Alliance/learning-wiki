`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Descriptive analysis of per-item agreement in the cross-sectional validation study with 31 participants rating one test study. Highest agreement items were "16 Planned delivery (94 %), 14 Modifications (84 %), 17 Actual schedule (81 %) and 8 Instructors (81 %)"; lowest agreement items were Steps of EBP, Environment and Adaptations.

> "The consistency of agreement between the participant ratings for completeness of reporting with the consensus criterion ratings (the participants ratings and the consensus criterion ratings were exactly the same) ranged from 19 % for item 4 Steps of EBP to 94 % for item 16 Planned delivery."
