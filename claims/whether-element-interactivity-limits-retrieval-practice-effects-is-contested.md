# The argument that retrieval practice effects do not occur with materials high in element interactivity is contested, and the chapter judges its research base not convincing

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The chapter reports that van Gog and Sweller (2015) argued retrieval practice effects do not occur with materials high in element interactivity. [→ Karpicke 2017](#karpicke-2017)
`q2 i?` The chapter reports, citing de Jonge et al. (2015), retrieval practice effects with a randomly ordered text but not with an intact text. [→ Karpicke 2017 (2)](#karpicke-2017-2)
`q2 i?` The chapter reports, citing Chan (2009), robust retrieval practice effects for both intact and randomly ordered texts. [→ Karpicke 2017 (3)](#karpicke-2017-3)
`q2 i?` The chapter concludes, citing Karpicke and Aue (2015) and Rawson (2015), that there is a wealth of evidence retrieval practice enhances learning of complex educational texts. [→ Karpicke 2017 (4)](#karpicke-2017-4)
