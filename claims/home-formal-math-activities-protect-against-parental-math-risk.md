## Evidence

### van Viersen 2026

van Viersen, S., Psyridou, M., & Torppa, M. (2026). General introduction to the special issue on resilience in learning. Learning and Instruction, 101, Article 102254. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102254

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editors report, citing Torppa et al. (2025), latent interaction models on longitudinal data "spanning age 2 to Grade 2" showing formal home math activities mitigated the risk of parental math difficulties. No effect sizes are printed in this introduction.

> "Testing of latent interaction models, based on longitudinal data spanning age 2 to Grade 2, showed that formal math activities in the home environment mitigated the risk of parental math difficulties for children’s math development, making it a protective factor for familial risk."
