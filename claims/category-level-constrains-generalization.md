## Evidence

### David Menendez 2023

David Menendez. (2023). Cues to generality: Integrating linguistic and visual information when generalizing biological information. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000812

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Internal meta-analysis of Studies 2-6 found a "reliable effect of category level, d = 1.242", indicating greater generalization to the specific category, though heterogeneity was substantial (I2 = 91.2%).

> "I also found a reliable effect of category level, d = 1.242 [1.113, 1.370], but there was substantial heterogeneity, I2 = 91.2%."

### David Menendez 2023 (2)

David Menendez. (2023). Cues to generality: Integrating linguistic and visual information when generalizing biological information. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000812

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The meta-analysis found reliable category-level × text-type interactions (d = 0.811 and d = 0.609); as Figure 3 shows, "the difference in statement type where present for the specific category but not the broader category."

> "I also found reliable interactions between category level and the generic vs. non-generic contrast, d = 0.811 [0.651, 0.972], I2 = 0.2%, between category level and the exemplified generic vs. non-generic contrast, d = 0.609 [0.453, 0.765], I2 = 0.0%."
