## Evidence

### McQuistan 2026

McQuistan, M. R., Young, L. B., & Pendleton, C. (2026). Poverty Simulations: Are the Learning Outcomes Consistently and Uniformly Positive? Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.70090

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired-samples t-test on 154 students with paired T2–T3 evaluation data. The article reports a mean T2 evaluation score of 23.6 (SD = 5.5) and a non-significant T3–T2 difference of −0.5 (p = 0.146), stating "a decline in post‐simulation evaluations between T2 and T3 was not found."

> "Regarding CAPS evaluations, the mean T2 evaluation score was 23.6 (SD = 5.5) of a possible 8 to 32 points. Unlike the changes in attitude scores, a decline in post‐simulation evaluations between T2 and T3 was not found, so H2 was not supported."
