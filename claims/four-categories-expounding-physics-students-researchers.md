`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phenomenographic analysis of semi-structured interviews (45 minutes to two hours, audio- and video-recorded) with 6 fourth-year students and 10 researchers. The authors report that interviews were read repeatedly, categories 'formed and reformed', and an outcome space presented at the collective level.

> "Analysis of the interviews with students revealed an outcome space of four categories of description which can be logically and hierarchically related to each other"
