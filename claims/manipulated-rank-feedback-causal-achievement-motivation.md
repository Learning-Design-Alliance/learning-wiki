## Evidence

### Vu 2025

Vu, T., Meeter, M., Hofman, A., Jansen, B., Magis‐Weinberg, L., van Triest, E., & van Atteveldt, N. (2025). Academic motivation–achievement cycle and the behavioural pathways: A short‐timeframe experiment with manipulated perceived achievement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 683–722. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12731

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Registered experiment in which 309 participants received manipulated rank feedback midway through the task; the article states the manipulation "partially supported a causal interpretation" of the achievement→motivation link. No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Our experimental manipulation partially supported a causal interpretation of the unilateral achievement→motivation pathway."

### Vu 2025 (2)

Vu, T., Meeter, M., Hofman, A., Jansen, B., Magis‐Weinberg, L., van Triest, E., & van Atteveldt, N. (2025). Academic motivation–achievement cycle and the behavioural pathways: A short‐timeframe experiment with manipulated perceived achievement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 683–722. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12731

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Design description of the manipulation: participants saw their actual score plus a rank among 20 'participants'; the first round always showed above-average rank and the second below-average rank, creating a perceived setback independent of performance.

> "The first round consisted of blocks that ended with ‘above‐average’ rank and the second round consisted of blocks with ‘below‐average’ rank."
