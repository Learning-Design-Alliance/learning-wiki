## Evidence

### Oquendo Yousi A. 2017

Oquendo Yousi A., Riddle Elijah W., Hiller Dennis, Blinman Thane A., Kuchenbecker Katherine J. (2017). Automatically rating trainee skill at a pediatric laparoscopic suturing task. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-017-5873-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Repeated-trial analysis of the 31 subjects who completed two trials in the custom box trainer. First and second trial summed scores correlated highly ("R = 0.89"), and subjects first scoring between 10 and 15 out of 20 showed the most improvement.

> "The Pearson product-moment correlation coefficient between these two metrics is R = 0.89, indicating a high correlation. Five participants (16.1%) showed a slight decrease in performance on the second trial, four (12.9%) had no change in performance, and 22 (71.0%) improved their summed score."
