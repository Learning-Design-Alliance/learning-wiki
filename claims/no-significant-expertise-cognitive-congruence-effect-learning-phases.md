## Evidence

### Chng Esther 2011

Chng Esther, Yew Elaine H. J., Schmidt Henk G. (2011). Effects of tutor-related behaviours on the process of problem-based learning. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9282-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null ANCOVA finding from the same 223-student, one-day PBL study with GPA as covariate: neither subject-matter expertise nor cognitive congruence significantly predicted concept recall after any phase. The article prints no effect size, so the null is reported as a non-significant test, not as equivalence.

> "No significant effects were found of subject-matter expertise and cognitive congruence of the tutor on each of the learning phases in the PBL process."
