## Evidence

### Shinohara Eriko 2022

Shinohara Eriko, Ohashi Yukiko, Hada Ayako, Usui Yuriko. (2022). Effects of 1-day e-learning education on perinatal psychological support skills among midwives and perinatal healthcare workers in Japan: a randomised controlled study. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-022-00832-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial with 115 participants randomised to intervention (n = 58) and control (n = 57); two-way ANOVA on the video-viewing test showed a significant group × timing interaction, and "both the ECS-V and ECS-P scores were higher among the intervention group participants at the post-test occasion".

> "For the primary outcome, the scores of the ECS-V (EUIP) showed the main effects of the group (F(1) = 4.302, p < 0.05), timing (F(1) = 21.967, p < 0.001) as well as the interactive effect (F(1,1) = 25.157, p < 0.001)."
