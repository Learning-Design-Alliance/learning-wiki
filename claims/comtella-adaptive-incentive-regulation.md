### Vassileva 2015

Vassileva, J., McCalla, G. I., & Greer, J. E. (2015). From Small Seeds Grow Fruitful Trees: How the PHelpS Peer Help System Stimulated a Diverse and Innovative Research Agenda over 15 Years. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0073-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A one-term evaluation in an Ethics and IT class, reported by the article as Cheng & Vassileva (2006). Participants neither over-contributed nor produced substandard (fake) contributions, matching the instructor's predefined level for timing, quantity and quality; no effect size is printed.

> "The mechanism was incorporated into Comtella and evaluated during one term in a class on Ethics and IT, and the results showed that it regulated in a sustainable way the contributions in an online community (Cheng & V assileva, 2006)."
