Trotti 2017¶

ANCOVA of phonics post-test with phonics pre-test as covariate in the same three-group pre-K experiment. The article reports "F(2, 158) = 5.883, p = 0.003" and "eta squared = 0.069" considered moderate; post-test means favor both CAI groups slightly.
