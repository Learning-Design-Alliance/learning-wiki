Evidence¶

Anna Riedmann 2025¶

Anna Riedmann, Philipp Schaper, Birgit Lugrin. (2025). Reinforcement Learning in Education: A Systematic Literature Review. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00494-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review assessed reward-function elements of the 18 papers whose RL approaches statistically outperformed one or more baselines. Accuracy (often combined with time or consecutive incorrect attempts) appeared in three approaches; two defined rewards from learning gain plus engagement, one solely on engagement.

"Learning gain, particularly normalized learning gain, which quantifies students' progress regardless of their initial competence, appeared to be an effective reward source and was used in over half of the papers (n = 10)."
