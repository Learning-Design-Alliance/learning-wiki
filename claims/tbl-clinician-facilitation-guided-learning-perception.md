## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students preferred the standardisation of the TBL approach and valued clarification and guidance from a clinician facilitator, feeling it focused sessions on relevant basic science. [→ Burgess A. 2018](#burgess-a-2018)

## Evidence

### Burgess A. 2018

Burgess A., Roberts C., Ayton T., Mellis C. (2018). Implementation of modified team-based learning within a problem based learning medical curriculum: a focus group study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1172-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group study of 14 of 20 first-year medical students after two TBL sessions replacing PBL in a cardiovascular block; thematic analysis mapped to constructivist theory. Students reported "students preferred the standardisation of the TBL approach" and valued tutor clarification.

> "Consequently, students preferred the standardisation of the TBL approach, and points of clarification from the tutor were important to their learning, particularly around the depth and breadth of basic science relevant to a clinical problem."
