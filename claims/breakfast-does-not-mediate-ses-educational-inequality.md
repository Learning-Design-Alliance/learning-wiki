## Evidence

### Littlecott Hannah J 2015

Littlecott Hannah J, Moore Graham F, Moore Laurence, Lyons Ronan A, Murphy Simon. (2015). Association between breakfast consumption and educational outcomes in 9–11-year-old children. Public Health Nutrition. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1368980015002669

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel models comparing SES coefficients before and after adding dietary behaviour terms. Table 2 prints individual FSM OR=0·38 (0·31, 0·46) and school-level FSM OR=0·99 (0·98, 1·00) with dietary terms included.

> "Associations of school- and individual-level measures of SES with education remained unchanged after inclusion of dietary variables, providing no evidence that their association with educational performance was mediated by SES differences for any outcome."
