## Evidence

### Tefera Aklile Semu 2024

Tefera Aklile Semu, Melaku Ermiyas Endewunet, Urgie Besufekad Mulugeta, Hassen Erzik Muhammed, Tamene Tilahun Deresse, Gebeyaw Enguday Demeke. (2024). Barriers to implementing problem-based learning at the school of medicine of Debre Berhan University, Ethiopia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05252-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative cross-sectional study at Debre Berhan University medical school (Feb 2022) using semi-structured interviews with 15 staff and FGDs with 16 year 1 and 2 students. Participants reported that students "encountered difficulties transitioning from a passive to an active learning environment" and that reading a large volume of information in a short period was very difficult.

> "Students encountered difficulties transitioning from a passive to an active learning environment and expressed fear of being challenged by tutor questions."
