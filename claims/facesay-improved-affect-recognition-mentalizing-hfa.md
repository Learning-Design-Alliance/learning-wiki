## Evidence

### DiPietro Joan 2019

DiPietro Joan, Kelemen Arpad, Liang Yulan, Sik-Lanyi Cecilia. (2019). Computer- and Robot-Assisted Therapies to Aid Social and Intellectual Functioning of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina55080440

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Controlled study of FaceSay software with 31 elementary school HFA children in California, sessions conducted by para-educators. Affect Recognition post-test favored the clinical group (adj M 12.59 vs 8.50); mentalizing post-test also favored clinical (12.39 vs 16.85 control, F (1.28) = 37.35, p < 0.001). No significant teacher-reported peer interaction differences.

> "Results demonstrated significant differences between groups post-test in Affect Recognition score. The adjusted average (adj M) for the clinical group was 12.59, while the control group's adjusted average was 8.50 [F (1.28) = 20.45, p < 0.001]."
