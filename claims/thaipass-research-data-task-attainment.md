`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Task-completion observation during the 2018-19 ThaiPASS schools: students replicated colour-magnitude diagrams of open and globular clusters from the SDSS SEGUE catalogue. "All participants being able to produce their own colour magnitude plot", with varying help. Descriptive only.

> "Students took to this task well, with all participants being able to produce their own colour magnitude plot from the data provided, though varying degrees of help were required."

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Task-completion observation for the Forward Euler solver task in both ThaiPASS'18 and '19: "Most students succeeded, with assistance either from peers or the teaching staff", while the full orbit-modelling extension was completed by only a few students and is judged university-level difficulty.

> "Most students succeeded, with assistance either from peers or the teaching staﬀ, in the programming of the forward Euler solver, a particularly impressive feat considering the diﬃculty. The full task of modelling the orbits of an example planet was completed only by a few students, however the diﬃculty of this task is more appropriate for a university level course"
