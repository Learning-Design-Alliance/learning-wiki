Analysis of incorrect pre-/posttest responses in the 72-student dataset. On item 1, "19 out of 36 students who claimed that the expectation value is 'time independent' incorrectly stated that the system was in a stationary state"; on item 2, 18 of 25 gave non-commutation-based reasoning.
