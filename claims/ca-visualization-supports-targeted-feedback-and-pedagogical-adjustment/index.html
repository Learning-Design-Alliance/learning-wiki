Evidence¶

Mónica Hernández-Campos 2026¶

Mónica Hernández-Campos, Isabel Hilliger and Francisco José García-Peñalvo. (2026). Assessing 21st Century Competencies: A Two-Case Study on Curriculum Analytics to Inform Continuous Improvement in Higher Education. Journal of Learning Analytics 13(1). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9131

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Cross-case qualitative finding (RQ1-1) from interviews, cognitive walkthroughs, and improvement-plan documents at two universities, with n = 46 extracts (42.6%) at U1 and n = 62 (57.4%) at U2. The finding states the CA tool makes "student performance more transparent and actionable."

"The assessment and visualization of LOs through the CA tool facilitate the provision of targeted feedback to students and enable instructors to adjust their pedagogical practices based on evidence."

Related Claims¶

  • LO assessment supported by CA tools prompted faculty toward more authentic, participatory, and reflective teaching practices — related
