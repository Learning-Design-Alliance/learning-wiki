### Amirthanayagam Anumithra 2026

Amirthanayagam Anumithra, Zecca Massimiliano, Chitrakar Anisha, Chavda Vijay, Moss Esther L. (2026). Looks like a surgeon? Impact of anthropometry on the kinematics of surgical novices: counterbalanced cross-over design study of minimally invasive surgery. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-04077-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of upper-arm sensor data from 54 surgical novices performing three tasks on LAP and da Vinci Xi platforms, comparing dominant and non-dominant arms within each modality. The analysis found "Angular velocity was higher for the dominant than for the non-dominant arm in both LAP and RA" with the printed p values; no standardised effect size was printed.

> "Angular velocity was higher for the dominant than for the non-dominant arm in both LAP and RA (p < 0.001; Fig. 2), and in every task except Task 1 during RA (p = 0.547; Fig. 3)."
