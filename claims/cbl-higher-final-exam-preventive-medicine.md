## Evidence

### Liu Zhipeng 2024

Liu Zhipeng, Na Guanqiong, Liu Lihua, Tian Sicong, Shan Yujuan. (2024). Investigation and implementation of case-based learning in the sino-foreign joint program of preventive medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06372-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational comparison reported in the Discussion: the 79 CBL joint-program students (final average 77.62 ± 8.07) were compared with 1716 other medical students taught traditionally, whose average was 69.2, a difference the authors attribute to CBL. The comparison was not randomized and no effect size or significance test is printed, so the impact is reported as untested. The article notes "it was not possible to directly compare the results with earlier results."

> "when compared to students who used the traditional teaching method during the same time period, the average final exam score of other medical students (n = 1716) in Preventive Medicine was 69.2 points. The average final exam score of Sino-foreign joint training students was still 7.8 points higher"
