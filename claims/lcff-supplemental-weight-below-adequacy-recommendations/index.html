Across five state adequacy studies (2016–2023), recommended weights ranged from 15% to 40% for English learners and 30% to 81% for at-risk students; California's 20% weight is at the low end or below, translating to about $2,100 per student versus nearly $6,500 or more than $9,900 recommended elsewhere. → Kaplan 2025

q2 · i? · review · r2

Cross-state comparison of five adequacy studies commissioned by the District of Columbia, Maryland, Michigan, Nevada, and Delaware, conducted 2016–2023 and analyzed by LPI. Recommended weights "translated into additional funding of nearly $6,500 per pupil in the District of Columbia, and more than $9,900 per pupil in Delaware and Maryland."

"California's 20% supplemental grant weight translated into additional funding of about $2,100 per student, substantially below the level of additional funding recommended by studies in these other states."
