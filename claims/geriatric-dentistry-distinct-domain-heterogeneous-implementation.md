Synthesis of 27 studies (18 selected after de-duplication) covering curricula in countries including the USA, Canada, India, South Korea, Japan, Australia, Germany, Switzerland, the UK, Malaysia, and Latin American nations.

> "Collectively, these studies indicate that geriatric dentistry is increasingly recognized as a distinct educational domain rather than merely a component of special care dentistry. However, curricular implementation remains heterogeneous across regions, and nationally standardized competency-based frameworks are still limited."
