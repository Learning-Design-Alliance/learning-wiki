# A preparatory web course before CPR training does not improve 13-year-old students' practical CPR skills at 6 months or directly after training

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students who took the Help-Brain-Heart web course before practical CPR training did not score significantly differently on the modified Cardiff test of CPR skills, either directly after training or at 6 months. [→ Nord Anette 2017](#nord-anette-2017)

## Evidence

### Nord Anette 2017

Nord Anette, Svensson Leif, Claesson Andreas, Herlitz Johan, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). The effect of a national web course “Help-Brain-Heart” as a supplemental learning tool before CPR training: a cluster randomised trial. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-017-0439-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomised trial of 13-year-olds; 432 students analysed for practical skills directly after training and 399 at 6 months. Both groups scored "34 points (SD 4.0; 61% of the maximum score)" after training; at 6 months scores were 33 (SD 4.2) vs 32 (SD 3.9), not significant.

> "There was no significant difference between the two groups with regard to total score for the modified Cardiff test. Directly after CPR training, the intervention group scored 34 points (SD 4.0; 61% of the maximum score) and the control group scored 34 points (SD 4.4; 61% of maximum score)."
