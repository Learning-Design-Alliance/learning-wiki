## Evidence

### Seitz T. 2017

Seitz T., Turk B. R., Löffler-Stastka H. (2017). Can we still stop the migration of physicians from Austria? An evaluation of clinical internships by students of the Medical University of Vienna. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-016-1095-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of survey ratings (Table 5): general assessment of clinical training correlated at r = 0.71 with "Integration into the clinical team" and r = 0.7 with "Supervision by medical professionals and physicians" (both p = 0.0001); interest in the specific field correlated at r = 0.33.
