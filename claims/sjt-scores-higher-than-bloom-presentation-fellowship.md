## Evidence

### Prakasam Ruby Kala 2026

Prakasam Ruby Kala, Tripathi Anjali, Kapoor Aditya, Bejjanki Kavya M, Pathengay Avinash, Snigdha Snigdha. (2026). Use of situational judgment test for evaluating nonacademic attributes in ophthalmology fellowship selection. Indian Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4103/IJO.IJO_2484_25

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective analysis of 68 candidates interviewed in August 2024 for ophthalmology fellowship. Box-plot comparison of percentage scores showed "The median score for SJT (85.4%, IQR = 25) was greater than the median score of the presentations (61.1%, IQR = 35)"; no effect size was printed.

> "The median score for SJT (85.4%, IQR = 25) was greater than the median score of the presentations (61.1%, IQR = 35). This difference was statistically significant (Wilcoxon signed rank test, z = −5.72, P < 0.001)."
