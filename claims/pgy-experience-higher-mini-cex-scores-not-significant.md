## Evidence

### Liu Yueh-Ping 2019

Liu Yueh-Ping, Jensen Dana, Chan Cho-yu, Wei Chung-jen, Chang Yuanmay, Wu Chih-Hsiung, Chiu Chiung-hsuan. (2019). Development of a nursing-specific Mini-CEX and evaluation of the core competencies of new nurses in postgraduate year training programs in Taiwan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1705-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of three training-duration groups (18 trainees 4–12 months, 6 at 13–18 months, 8 at 19–24 months) using the Kruskal-Wallis test; domain p values in Table 3 ranged from 0.08 to 0.71. No statistically significant difference was found; equivalence was not tested.

> "On average, trainees with 19–24 months of PGY training obtained higher scores than less-experienced trainees in all domains except for “counseling skills.” However, no statistically significant difference was indicated between the performances of the four groups"
