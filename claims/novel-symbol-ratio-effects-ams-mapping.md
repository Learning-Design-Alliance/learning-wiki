## Evidence

### Amy Bennett 2018

Amy Bennett, Matthew Inglis and Camilla Gilmore. (2018). The Cost of Multiple Representations: Learning Number Symbols with Abstract and Concrete Representations. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000318

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

A by-items linear regression in Experiment 1 predicted the proportion of participants answering each trial correctly from the trial's ratio, showing "a significant effect of ratio, β = -.744". Overall accuracy also correlated with arithmetic fluency, r = .391.

> "This revealed a significant effect of ratio, β = -.744, p < .001, R2 = .554. Overall accuracy was correlated with performance on the Woodcock Johnson arithmetic fluency test, r = .391, p = .001"
