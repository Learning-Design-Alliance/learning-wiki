## Evidence

### Lisabeth Marie Santana and Chandralekha Singh 2024

Lisabeth Marie Santana and Chandralekha Singh. (2024). How the physics culture shapes the experiences of undergraduate women physics majors: A comparative case study of three physics departments. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.12995](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.12995)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured empathetic interviews with 16 undergraduate women physics and astronomy majors at a large research university, of which 6 interviews were analyzed due to the range of experiences. A senior student describes a male peer who ignored her input and mansplained concepts; other women described female friend groups as their rocks and support.

> "I would say something and he'd ignore it and I would end up being right and he wouldn't acknowledge that"
