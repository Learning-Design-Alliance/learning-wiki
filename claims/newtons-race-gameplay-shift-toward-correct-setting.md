## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Anne van der Linden 2022

Anne van der Linden, Ralph F. G. Meulenbroeks and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2022). Learning Newtonian mechanics with an intrinsically integrated educational game. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.11627](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.11627)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Log-data analysis of chosen force settings across key levels 2, 3 and 5, with chi-square tests per level (e.g. Level 2: χ2(2, N = 222) = 20.900, p <.000). In all three levels the setting F=0N was chosen significantly more often than F>friction, showing gameplay matched expected learning.

> "In all three levels the setting F=0N, the scientifically correct setting, is chosen significantly more often than the setting F>friction. Also, a significant shift in the settings is found."

### Anne van der Linden 2022 (2)

Anne van der Linden, Ralph F. G. Meulenbroeks and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2022). Learning Newtonian mechanics with an intrinsically integrated educational game. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.11627](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.11627)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Log-data analysis of completed attempts per key level in the same study. Of 507 completed attempts, 368 used F = 0N; the chi-square test printed χ2(1, N = 506) = 354.710, p <.000, indicating the correct setting was most chosen among completions.

> "The key levels were completed 507 times, of which 368 times with the setting F = 0N. A chi-square test determined that this setting was chosen significantly more often than the other two settings; χ2(1, N = 506) = 354.710, p <.000."
