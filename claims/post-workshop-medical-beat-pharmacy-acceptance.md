# After the workshop, medical students show significantly higher SAIL-10 scores than pharmacy students, though the two groups did not differ beforehand

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Before the workshop there is no significant difference in total SAIL-10 score or either domain between medical and pharmacy students. [→ Chua Siew Siang 2019](#chua-siew-siang-2019)
`q2 i?` After the workshop medical students show significantly higher total SAIL-10 and both domain scores than pharmacy students. [→ Chua Siew Siang 2019 (2)](#chua-siew-siang-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Chua Siew Siang 2019

Chua Siew Siang, Lai Pauline Siew Mei, Sim Si Mui, Tan Choo Hock, Foong Chan Choong. (2019). Acceptance of interprofessional learning between medical and pharmacy students in a prescribing skills training workshop: pre-post intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1525-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Phase 2 pre-workshop comparison of 175 medical and 95 pharmacy students using the Mann-Whitney U-test; Table 4 reports pre-workshop total-score z = −0.381 (p = 0.703). Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "There was no significant difference in the total SAIL-10 score, as well as for both domains, between medical and pharmacy students before attending the prescribing skills workshop."

### Chua Siew Siang 2019 (2)

Chua Siew Siang, Lai Pauline Siew Mei, Sim Si Mui, Tan Choo Hock, Foong Chan Choong. (2019). Acceptance of interprofessional learning between medical and pharmacy students in a prescribing skills training workshop: pre-post intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1525-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Phase 2 post-workshop Mann-Whitney comparison (175 medical vs 95 pharmacy): Table 4 reports post-workshop total score z = −3.220 (p = 0.001), Domain 1 z = −2.186 (p = 0.029), Domain 2 z = −3.019 (p = 0.003).

> "Medical students showed significantly higher total SAIL-10 score, as well as for both domains, than pharmacy students after they completed the workshop (Table 4)."
