## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Tina Seufert 2020

Tina Seufert. (2020). Building Bridges Between Self-Regulation and Cognitive Load—an Invitation for a Broad and Differentiated Attempt. Educational Psychology Review, 32, 1151–1162. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09574-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing van Gog, Hoogerheide, and van Harsel, a series of studies training learners to combine actual performance and mental-effort appraisals for task choice; the video-modeled algorithm "improved learners monitoring accuracy and the task selection accuracy and above all also improved learning outcomes."

> "This algorithm was conveyed by a video modeling example, and it actually improved learners monitoring accuracy and the task selection accuracy and above all also improved learning outcomes."
