## Evidence

### Li Xingqiao; Ye Yinghua 2025

Li Xingqiao; Ye Yinghua. (2025). Mediating role of online academic emotions between online presence and learning performance in blended learning environments. Scientific Reports 15, 29875. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-15947-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of a stratified-sample questionnaire survey of 971 college students in Hangzhou (Table 1). Online presence correlated r = .67 with positive-high arousal and r = .71 with positive-low arousal emotions, and negatively with negative emotions, "significantly and positively correlated with positive-high arousal academic emotions (p < 0.01)".

> "Online presence was significantly and positively correlated with positive-high arousal academic emotions (p < 0.01) and positive-low arousal academic emotions (p < 0.01) and significantly and negatively correlated with negative high-arousal academic emotions (p < 0.01)"
