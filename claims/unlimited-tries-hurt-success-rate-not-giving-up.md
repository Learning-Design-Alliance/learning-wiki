## Evidence

### Gerd Kortemeyer 2014

Gerd Kortemeyer. (2014). An Empirical Study of the Eﬀect of Granting Multiple Tries for Online Homework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.2276](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.2276)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exponential fit to the first 20 attempts of the 99-tries semester (Fig. 4). The article reports λa of 0.265 for giving up and λs of 0.335 for solving, interpreting unlimited tries as having "no eﬀect on the rate of students giving up on problems" while hurting the success rate per attempt.

> "the decay constant λs of 0.335 for succeeding in solving the problem follows the trend of less and less successful problem solving per attempt with increasing number of allowed attempts."
