## Evidence

### Burn H 2020

Burn H, Puri L, Roshan A, Singh SK, Burton MJ. (2020). Primary Eye Care in Eastern Nepal. Ophthalmic Epidemiology 27(3):165-176. https://doi.org/10.1080/09286586.2019.1702217

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Clinical skills test in which each of 107 PHCWs viewed colour clinical images with brief scenarios for four common eye conditions and scored points for diagnosis, management and urgency. Corneal ulcer had "the lowest recognition score (27%)"; Table 3 prints diagnosis rates of 27.1%, 83.2%, 75.7% and 54.2%.

> "In all three districts we found that corneal ulcer was the condition with the lowest recognition score (27%) and only 58.8% could explain the correct management. Similarly, the scores for the diagnosis and management of ophthalmia neonatorum were also poor. The recognition rates for both cataract and conjunctivitis were somewhat better"
