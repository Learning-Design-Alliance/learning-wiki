## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Posttest scores after traditional instruction are low (37%) and Hake normalized gains are small (g = 0.11 calculus-based, g = 0.10 algebra-based). [→ Benjamin Brown and Chandralekha Singh 2021](#benjamin-brown-and-chandralekha-singh-2021)

## Evidence

### Benjamin Brown and Chandralekha Singh 2021

Benjamin Brown and Chandralekha Singh. (2021). Development and Validation of a conceptual survey instrument to evaluate introductory physics students' understanding of thermodynamics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre/post administration of the final STPFaSL in calculus-based (N=705 pre, 507 post) and algebra-based (N=218 pre, 382 post) courses at six institutions. Table 3 shows posttest totals of 37% with normalized gains of 0.11 and 0.10; the article states students "did not improve much from pretest to posttest."

> "Table 3 also lists Hake normalized gain g deﬁned as g = ( post% − pre%)/(100% − pre%) for introductory courses [14] for which both pretest and posttest data are available. The normalized gains show that introductory physics students did not improve much from pretest to posttest."
