`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Design-by-treatment inconsistency assessment of the under-18 primary NMA. Restriction to under-18 school-PE studies "reduced heterogeneity and aligned the analysis with the stated population, but it did not establish transitivity or eliminate inconsistency."

> "The 13-study under-18 primary network retained substantial heterogeneity (tau-squared = 0.1531; I-squared = 65.67%). The between-design component of the design-by-treatment decomposition was statistically significant (Q = 12.91, df = 2, p = 0.0016)."
