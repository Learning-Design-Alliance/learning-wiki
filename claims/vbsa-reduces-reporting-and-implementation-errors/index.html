Related Claims¶

  • The voice-based synthetic assistant interface showed very low false-positive rates in interaction with trainees, suggesting reliable rejection of unwanted commands — related
  • Novice trainees trained with a voice-based synthetic assistant achieved significantly higher performance scores than conventionally trained trainees on the third test of a hemorrhage treatment scenario — related
  • Training with the voice-based synthetic assistant caused no statistically significant loss in task execution time and no significant time difference between groups across testing scenarios — related
  • Repetitive training with the synthetic assistant produced statistically significant performance growth across successive tests, whereas the control group's growth was largely insignificant — related
