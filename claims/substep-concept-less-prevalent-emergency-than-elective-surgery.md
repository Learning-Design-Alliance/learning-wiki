## Evidence

### Wachter N. 2024

Wachter N., Güsgen C., Geis C., Penzkofer L.S., Oldhafer K., Willms A.G., Huber T. (2024). Status quo of operative training in emergency surgery in Germany – results of a survey. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03360-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Nationwide anonymized online survey of DGAV members conducted November 2021–January 2022; 184 complete questionnaires analyzed descriptively with Chi-square tests. Participants reported sub-step assistance significantly less often in emergency surgery, "this percentage was significantly lower at 38%".

> "In response to the question of whether sub-steps are assisted during elective operations, 44% of participants answered “completely true” or “rather true”. For the assistance of sub-steps in emergency surgery, this percentage was significantly lower at 38% (Fig. 2)."
