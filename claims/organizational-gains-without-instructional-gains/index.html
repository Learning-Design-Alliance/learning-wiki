Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/trends-chicagos-schools-across-three-eras-reform-full-report

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Trend analysis of CCSR survey measures of school leadership, professional capacity, parent/community ties, learning climate, and instruction across the three eras (Chapter 7). Organizational improvements at era starts were often unsustainable and did not improve overall instructional quality. No effect size is printed.

"These improvements in overall school organization did not, however, translate into better overall instructional quality in classrooms."

Luppescu 2011 (2)¶

Luppescu, S., Allensworth, E. M., Moore, P., de la Torre, M., Murphy, J., with Jagesic, S. (2011). Trends in Chicago's Schools Across Three Eras of Reform. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/trends-chicagos-schools-across-three-eras-reform-full-report

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey trend analysis of student-reported climate measures (Chapter 7). The report states that improvements in instruction and student supports were not sustained, with substantial post-2005 declines in student-reported teacher relationships and support. No effect size is printed.

"after 2005 there were substantial declines in students’ reports of their relationships with teachers and the support they received from them"
