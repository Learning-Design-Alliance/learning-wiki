Murphy et al. 2017¶

Richard Murphy, Felix Weinhardt, Gill Wyness and Heather Rolfe. (2017). Lesson Study: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581145

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Summary of the RCT's secondary outcome analyses, assessed with the same intention-to-treat methodology as the primary outcome. The report "found no impact" on any listed secondary measure for either cohort; science was cohort 1 only.

"Twelve months after the end of the Lesson Study programme, this evaluation found no impact of Lesson Study on KS2 reading and maths attainment, nor for any of the secondary outcomes (maths and reading individual scores; spelling, punctuation and grammar (SPAG) scores; and science scores)"
