## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Studies with training of 11 days or more showed stronger pooled associations than shorter training for vaccinations (RR = 3.62 vs 1.12), nutrition counseling (RR = 6.05 vs 3.00), and caregiver instruction (RR = 2.63 vs 1.87). [→ Nguyen Duyen Thi Kim 2013](#nguyen-duyen-thi-kim-2013)

## Evidence

### Nguyen Duyen Thi Kim 2013

Nguyen Duyen Thi Kim, Leung Karen K., McIntyre Lynn, Ghali William A., Sauve Reg. (2013). Does Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI) Training Improve the Skills of Health Workers? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0066030

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Stratified meta-analysis by training length found stronger associations among studies with training of 11 days or more. The review reports "a longer training duration (RR = 3.62, 95% CI: 1.15–11.41)" for vaccinations, with broad and frequently overlapping confidence intervals between strata.

> "a stronger performance was seen in vaccinations among studies describing sufficient medical equipment for IMCI (RR = 1.10, 95% CI: 1.02–1.19), and a longer training duration (RR = 3.62, 95% CI: 1.15–11.41)."
