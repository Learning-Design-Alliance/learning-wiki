## Evidence

### Aline Godfroid 2017

Aline Godfroid, Jieun Ahn, Ina Choi, Laura Ballard, Yaqiong Cui, Suzanne Johnston, Shinhye Lee, Abdhi Sarkar and Hyung-Jo Yoon (2017). Incidental vocabulary learning in a natural reading context: an eye-tracking study. Bilingualism: Language and Cognition, Volume 21, Issue 3. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1366728917000219

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Growth curve analysis of eye-tracking data from native and non-native English speakers reading an authentic English novel showed reading times on unfamiliar words decreased non-linearly, "following an S shaped curve". No effect size is printed for the curve shape.

> "Growth curve modeling revealed a non-linear decrease in reading times that followed an S shaped curve"
