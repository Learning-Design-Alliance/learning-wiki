## Evidence

### Sejer EPF 2024

Sejer EPF, Ladelund AK, Bruun FJ, Slavensky JA, Mortensen EL, Kesmodel US. (2024). Preterm birth and subsequent intelligence and academic performance in youth: A cohort study. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14796

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive comparison of grade averages across gestational age groups in the Danish cohort (Table 2), with p < 0.001 for LSE total, mathematics, Danish, English, and core subject averages. The article reports "0.71 grade points higher for term vs. extremely preterm" in mathematics and notes the English difference (0.03 grade point) was not considered clinically relevant.

> "Statistically significant differences were also observed in LSE for total GPA and core subject GPA with higher grades in the term group. The largest disparities between the groups were seen in mathematics (0.71 grade points higher for term vs. extremely preterm)."
