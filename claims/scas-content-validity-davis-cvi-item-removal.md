## Evidence

### KOÇAK Cemal 2025

KOÇAK Cemal, GÜVERCİN Hande, ESEN Ecem, ÇÖL Meltem. (2025). Development study of scientific competence and activity scale in medical faculty research assistants. Turkish Journal of Medical Sciences. https://doi.org/10.55730/1300-0144.6026

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content validity phase in which 7 experts (epidemiology, public health, internal medicine, general surgery, medical biochemistry) rated the 41-item draft. The article reports items with CVI 0.43 (e.g., foreign language skills, institutional scientific culture) and 0.71 (e.g., systematic review knowledge) were removed; the pilot with 15 assistants removed no further items, leaving 36.

> "Items with a content validity index below 0.80 were excluded from the study [16]."
