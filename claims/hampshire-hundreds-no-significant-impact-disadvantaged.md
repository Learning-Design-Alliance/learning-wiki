## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sandra McNally 2014

Sandra McNally, Amy Challen, Gill Wyness, Anne West, Philip Noden. (2014). Hampshire Hundreds Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581163

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomised controlled trial with wait-list control; intention-to-treat analysis of the primary outcome (combined InCAS reading and maths, restandardised to mean 0, sd 1) for 924 disadvantaged pupils across 24 schools. Table 6a reports an effect size of 0.026 (SE 0.054, 95% CI [-0.086, 0.138]); the finding was "not statistically significant".

> "The intervention showed an effect size on the disadvantaged pupils of 0.03. Similar effect sizes were found for children eligible for free school meals. This effect size is very small. However, the confidence intervals are very wide."
