Robert Linquanti and Alison L. Bailey 2014¶

Robert Linquanti and Alison L. Bailey. (2014). Reprising the Home Language Survey: Summary of a National Working Session on Policies, Practices, and Tools for Identifying Potential English Learners. CCSSO. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED565756

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

The paper reports, citing the Bailey and Kelly review, four common state HLS practices, including that "23 states and Washington, DC have created a single HLS form and mandate its use in schools statewide." The remaining counts (17, six, four states) are as printed in the review's summary.

"In their review, Bailey and Kelly (2010; 2013) identified four HLS practices common in the states: 1) 23 states and Washington, DC have created a single HLS form and mandate its use in schools statewide; 2) 17 states mandate use of an HLS"
