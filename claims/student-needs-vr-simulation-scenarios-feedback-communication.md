## Evidence

### Jeon Jaehee 2020

Jeon Jaehee, Kim Jin Hee, Choi Eun Hee. (2020). Needs Assessment for a VR-Based Adult Nursing Simulation Training Program for Korean Nursing Students: A Qualitative Study Using Focus Group Interviews. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17238880

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme cluster 'Using desired scenarios during VR-based simulation training' from the FGIs; participants also wanted a ward-like environment caring for several patients as the nurse in charge.

> "they wanted the simulation to feature varying levels of difficulty and to allow them to go on to the next step only when the correct treatment was given, preferably by giving immediate feedback, such as what happens in computerized games."

### Jeon Jaehee 2020 (2)

Jeon Jaehee, Kim Jin Hee, Choi Eun Hee. (2020). Needs Assessment for a VR-Based Adult Nursing Simulation Training Program for Korean Nursing Students: A Qualitative Study Using Focus Group Interviews. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17238880

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same theme cluster; one participant said it is important that the simulation enables students "to talk to patients as much as to judge the situation and deliver care."

> "Particularly, they wanted to experience a variety of cases that they would not often encounter in clinical settings and learn how to deal with emergencies."
