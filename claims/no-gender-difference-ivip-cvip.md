## Evidence

### Gambari 2016

Gambari, A. I., Shittu, A. T., Daramola, F. O., & James, M. (2016). Effects Of Video-Based Cooperative, Competitive And Individualized Instructional Strategies On The Performance Of Senior Secondary Schools Students In Geometry. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences, 4(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1116321

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

t-test comparing 15 male and 15 female students in the individualized group; means were 71.20 (male) and 72.13 (female). The article reports no significant difference (t = 0.754, df = 28, p = 0.457); no effect size is printed.

> "This indicates that there is statistically no significant difference between the male and female students taught with IVIP, (t = 0.754, df = 28, p = 0.457)."

### Gambari 2016 (2)

Gambari, A. I., Shittu, A. T., Daramola, F. O., & James, M. (2016). Effects Of Video-Based Cooperative, Competitive And Individualized Instructional Strategies On The Performance Of Senior Secondary Schools Students In Geometry. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences, 4(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1116321

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

t-test comparing 15 male and 15 female students in the conventional control group; means were 66.07 (male) and 65.27 (female). The article reports no significant difference (t = 0.334, df = 28, p = 0.825); no effect size is printed.

> "This indicates that there is statistically no significant difference between the male and female students taught with CVIP, (t = 0.334, df = 28, p = 0.825)."
