## Evidence

### Oxenford K 2017

Oxenford K, Daley R, Lewis C, Hill M, Chitty LS. (2017). Development and evaluation of training resources to prepare health professionals for counselling pregnant women about non-invasive prenatal testing for Down syndrome: a mixed methods study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1315-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Questionnaire study using the 8-item true/false/unsure knowledge test (KR-20 reliability 0.81) comparing Q1 and Q3 with Wilcoxon signed-rank test; the article reports a statistically significant difference in actual knowledge. No effect size was printed.

> "There was a statistically significant difference in actual knowledge between Q1 (Mdn = 2, IQR = 4) and Q3 (Mdn = 6, IQR = 2), Z = 9.142, p < 0.001"

### Oxenford K 2017 (2)

Oxenford K, Daley R, Lewis C, Hill M, Chitty LS. (2017). Development and evaluation of training resources to prepare health professionals for counselling pregnant women about non-invasive prenatal testing for Down syndrome: a mixed methods study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1315-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of individual items in the knowledge test at the one-month follow-up (Table 4); the article names the four weak areas, e.g. only 39.4% correctly answered that the three-week turnaround statement was false. No effect size printed.

> "There were four knowledge areas where fewer than 65% of people indicated the correct response following training; test turn-around time; false positive rates; cell free DNA originates from the placenta; and cell free DNA concentration increases with gestation."
