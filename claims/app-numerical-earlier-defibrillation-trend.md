`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Ventricular fibrillation scenario with 10 teams per arm. "Time to first defibrillation did not differ clearly between intervention and control teams" (p = 0.224); authors note the numerical trend may be explainable by chance given limited statistical power.

> "Time to first defibrillation did not differ clearly between intervention and control teams (112.8 ± 40.9 s vs. 133.3 ± 31.1 s, p = 0.224), although a numerical trend toward earlier defibrillation was observed in the intervention group."
