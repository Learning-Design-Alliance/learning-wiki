### Bhuvaneswari S 2026

Bhuvaneswari S, Bamini Devi N, Helen Shaji J C, Lakshmi L. (2026). Voices of learners: a qualitative exploration of nursing students’ satisfaction with simulation-based basic life support training. BMC Medical Education, 26(1). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09260-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic finding from focus group discussions with 40 nursing students. Despite overall positive perceptions, students identified peer observation anxiety, limited practice time, and large group sizes as barriers, with one stating "I felt nervous performing in front of my classmates."

> "Peer observation during simulation sometimes caused performance anxiety. Limited time for repeated practice and large group sizes were also perceived as barriers. Participants suggested smaller groups and additional practice sessions to enhance learning and satisfaction."
