## Evidence

### Steegen 2018

Steegen, A.; Hasendonckx, F.; Cock, M. (2018). Can an Interactive Learning Path on A Tablet PC Counter Misconceptions on the Formation of Clouds and Wind? Review of International Geographical Education Online (RIGEO), 8 (1), 53-73. http://www.rigeo.org/vol8no1/Number1Spring/RIGEO-V8-N1-3.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-point Likert scale student experience questionnaire administered in the posttest. Students rated innovativeness 4.02/5, agreed experiments illustrate learning content (4.29/5), and rated exercises as obliging them to think (3.38/5). The experiment lesson was most positively evaluated.

> "Although students were positive about the innovative character  of the tablet pc based lesson (4.02 on 5), they also indicated that it was difficult to hold attention to this lesson and that they probably won't remember the learning content for a long time."
