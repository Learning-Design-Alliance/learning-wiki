## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In binomial generalized linear models, 10 of 19 belonging items significantly increased with attendance; the strongest was making new friends, with a 23.3% predicted increase from attending one versus all 10 sessions. [→ Mueller 2026](#mueller-2026)

## Evidence

### Mueller 2026

Mueller, L. O., Bamrah, M., Petrie, K. L., Tour, E., McDonnell, L., Lo, S. M., & Meaders, C. L. (2026). The Fourth Credit Hour: How do we Teach in Biology Discussion Sections? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0185

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binomial generalized linear regression predicting each belonging item from number of discussions attended (1–10), Holm corrected. Each session attended raised the likelihood of making a friend by over 2%; two practice-sensitive items were not predicted by attendance and three attendance-sensitive items were not practice-sensitive.

> "Of the 19 survey items, 10 were found to significantly increase with increased attendance (Table 2), the strongest signal being the survey item: “I made new friends in this course” with a 23.3% predicted increase in likelihood of agreement"
