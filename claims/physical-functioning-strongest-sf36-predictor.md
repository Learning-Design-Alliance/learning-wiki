Multiple regression analysis with simultaneous entry tested the predictive power of the eight quality-of-life components of educators for students' clinical education quality. Physical performance was the only significant contributor with "a beta coefficient of 0.807"; other dimensions did not contribute significantly.

> "the dimension of lack of physical performance with a beta coefficient of 0.807 is in the first degree of importance in terms of predicting the quality of clinical education and this value is at the alpha level. (0.01) was significant"
