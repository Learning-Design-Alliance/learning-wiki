### Angela Zavaleta-Bernuy 2022 (2)

Angela Zavaleta-Bernuy, Ziwen Han, Hammad Shaikh, Qi Yin Zheng, Lisa-Angelique Lim, Anna Rafferty, Andrew Petersen, and Joseph Jay Williams. 2022. How can Email Interventions Increase Students’ Completion of Online Homework? A Case Study Using A/B Comparisons. In LAK22: 12th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK22), March 21–25, 2022, Online, USA. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3506860.3506874

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Semi-structured interviews with 9 students recruited after the course. The article reports 6/9 students found the reminders "helpful for spurring homework com- pletion", 3/9 were neutral, and some reported feeling patronized when reminders arrived after they had finished. Descriptive counts only; no effect size.

> "Some students (6/9) reported in the interviews that they liked receiving the re- minder messages as they were helpful for spurring homework com- pletion."
