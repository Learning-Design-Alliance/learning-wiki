Sensitivity analyses using sequentially adjusted ANCOVA models (Model 1 through Model 4, adding training year, rotation block, and baseline academic indicators) showed the "adjusted mean difference remained statistically significant across all models"; no effect size was printed for the sensitivity range.

> "The adjusted mean difference remained statistically significant across all models (range, 6.48–6.85 points; all p < 0.001), indicating that the findings were robust to adjustment for residency training year, specialty, rotation block, and baseline academic performance"
