## Evidence

### Peters Philipp 2023

Peters Philipp, Lemos Martin, Bönsch Andrea, Ooms Mark, Ulbrich Max, Rashad Ashkan, Krause Felix, Lipprandt Myriam, Kuhlen Torsten W., Röhrig Rainer, Hölzle Frank, Puladi Behrus. (2023). Effect of head-mounted displays on students' acquisition of surgical suturing techniques compared to an e-learning and tutor-led course: a randomized controlled trial. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000464

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Computerized optical-flow analysis (MATLAB script, 1 Hz sampling) of the RCT's >26 h of test videos; the article states OF and suture counts can be used as additional measures to GRS.

> "In an additional computerized analysis, we extracted the OF from the recorded videos, revealing a linear correlation between the GRS and the sutures performed in each test (R 2=0.73, P<0.001), as well as a weak correlation with the OF (R 2=0.16, P<0.001)."

### Peters Philipp 2023 (2)

Peters Philipp, Lemos Martin, Bönsch Andrea, Ooms Mark, Ulbrich Max, Rashad Ashkan, Krause Felix, Lipprandt Myriam, Kuhlen Torsten W., Röhrig Rainer, Hölzle Frank, Puladi Behrus. (2023). Effect of head-mounted displays on students' acquisition of surgical suturing techniques compared to an e-learning and tutor-led course: a randomized controlled trial. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000464

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Algorithm-based evaluation (Fig. 6C): movement per suture declines with rising GRS. The article cautions this alone would not be enough to replace detailed feedback.

> "Interestingly, there was a strong decrease in OF per suture performed (R 2=0.61, P<0.001), starting with an increase in the GRS (Fig. 6)."
