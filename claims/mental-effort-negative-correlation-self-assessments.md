## Evidence

### Waldeyer 2024

Waldeyer, J., Endres, T., Roelle, J., Baars, M., & Renkl, A. (2024). How to Optimize Self-Assessment Accuracy in Cognitive Skill Acquisition When Learning from Worked Examples. Educational Psychology Review, 36, 103. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09944-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 correlational analysis (N = 129) testing whether learners used cognitive load as a self-assessment cue. A "statistically significant negative correlation between mental effort and self-assessments" appeared in both groups (r = − 0.29 and r = − 0.27); a Fisher's z-test showed the correlations did not differ (z = 0.17, p = 0.431).

> "For both learners with and without cue information before problem-solving, there was a statistically significant negative correlation between mental effort and self-assessments, r = − 0.29, p = 0.012, and r = − 0.27, p = − 0.048."
