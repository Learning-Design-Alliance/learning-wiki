# ACE scores show a graded positive association with internalizing behavior trajectories from age 3 to 14

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children with more ACEs showed higher internalizing scores at age 3, with adjusted coefficients of 0.057 (1 ACE), 0.108 (2 ACEs), and 0.202 (3+ ACEs), and differences persisted through age 14. [→ Leonardo Bevilacqua 2021](#leonardo-bevilacqua-2021)

## Evidence

### Leonardo Bevilacqua 2021

Leonardo Bevilacqua, Yvonne Kelly, Anja Heilmann, Naomi Priest, Rebecca E. Lacey. (2021). Adverse childhood experiences and trajectories of internalizing, externalizing, and prosocial behaviors from childhood to adolescence. Child Abuse & Neglect 112 (2021) 104890. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chiabu.2020.104890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear mixed effects models on 8,571 MCS children tested ACE scores against log-transformed SDQ internalizing trajectories across five waves (ages 3-14), adjusted for gender, ethnicity, and poverty. The article reports "a graded positive association between the number of ACEs and the number of internalizing symptoms reported at age 3" that "remained through to age 14".

> "ACE scores showed a graded association with internalizing (ACE score of 1: β= 0.057; ACE score of 2: β= 0.108; ACE score of 3: β= 0.202)"
