### Nourian 2026

Understanding How International Students in the U.S. Are Using Conversational AI to Support Cross-Cultural Adaptation. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15127 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.15127](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.15127)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Semi-structured interview study with 14 international students, analysed via reflexive thematic analysis. Participant P14 illustrates academic use of AI for communication: "let the AI to correct me, let the AI tell me if I have any grammar mistakes." Qualitative interview data; no effect size reported.

> "When I was doing teamwork, [and] communicate with somebody online, I will firstly, tell the AI what kind of thing that I want to say for my own sentence, and send it to AI, and let the AI to correct me, let the AI tell me if I have any grammar mistakes."
