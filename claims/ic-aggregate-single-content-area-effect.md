### Portes 2016

Portes, P. R., Gonzalez Canche, M. S., & Stollberg, R. (2016). Early RCT Findings for ELL Elementary Student Learning Outcomes After a Two-Year Pedagogical Intervention. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567010

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OLS analyses of standardized CRCT scores across five content areas (ELA, Math, Reading, Science, Social Studies) for the aggregate sample of 1,527 students. The article reports a significant difference in "one content area exam"; Tables 3 through 6 show non-significant treatment coefficients for Math, Reading, Science, and Social Studies.
