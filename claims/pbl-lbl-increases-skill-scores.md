## Evidence

### Jia Xue MM 2018

Jia Xue MM, Zeng Wen MM, Zhang Qin MM, Tarantino. Giovanni. (2018). Combined administration of problem- and lecture-based learning teaching models in medical education in China: A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000011366

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 13 RCTs reporting skill scores (651 and 649 students in the PBL+LBL and LBL groups, respectively), random effects model due to higher heterogeneity (P < .00001, I2 = 97%).

> "The meta-analysis of the skill scores found that the PBL+LBL teaching model significantly increased skill scores by a mean of 2.11 compared with the LBL teaching model (95% CI, 0.51–3.71; P = .01)."
