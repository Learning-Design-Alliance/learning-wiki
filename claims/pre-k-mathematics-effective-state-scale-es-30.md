# Pre-K Mathematics remains effective at the state scale, with a positive and statistically significant effect of .30 on the ECLS-B Mathematics Assessment

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a state-level cluster randomized controlled trial across heterogeneous California pre-K programs, Pre-K Mathematics produced positive, statistically significant effects on children's math ability (ES = .30, p < .01). [→ Jaime Thomas 2018](#jaime-thomas-2018)

## Evidence

### Jaime Thomas 2018

Jaime Thomas, Thomas D. Cook, Alice Klein, Prentice Starkey, Lydia DeFlorio. (2018). The Sequential Scale-Up of an Evidence-Based Intervention: A Case Study. Evaluation Review, vol. 42, no. 3. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-sequential-scale-up-of-an-evidence-based-intervention-a-case-study

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

Cluster randomized controlled trial of the Pre-K Mathematics intervention scaled to the state level across pre-K programs representing low-income families in California. The study reports the intervention "remains effective at the state level, with positive and statistically significant effects" with an effect size of .30 (p < .01) on the ECLS-B Mathematics Assessment.

> "We find that Pre-K Mathematics remains effective at the state level, with positive and statistically significant effects (effect size on the Early Childhood Longitudinal Study, Birth Cohort Mathematics Assessment = .30, p < .01)."
