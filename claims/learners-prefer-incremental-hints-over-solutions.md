Participant quote from the four-week study's feedback data. Early in the study, participants explicitly requested more guided hints instead of full solutions, with one suggesting the system let users try multiple times and provide a small relative hint on request.

> "The GPT also asked me if I wanted a hint or if I wanted to do the problem on my own. . . which was a nice option."
