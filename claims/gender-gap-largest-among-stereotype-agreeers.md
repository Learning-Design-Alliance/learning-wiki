### Alexandru Maries 2017

Alexandru Maries, Nafis I. Karim, and Chandralekha Singh. (2017). The impact of stereotype threat on gender gap in introductory physics. 2017 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.059

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Table II comparisons of FCI performance between female and male students who agreed with the stereotype, in calculus-based pre-test (p < 0.001, effect size 1.36), calculus-based post-test (p = 0.007, effect size 1.02), and algebra-based pre-test (effect size 1.78). The article states the gender gap is largest among agreeers.

> "Three out of the four comparisons (calculus-based pre-test and post-test, algebra-based pre-test) resulted in large effect sizes (1.36, 1.02, and 1.78, respectively)."
