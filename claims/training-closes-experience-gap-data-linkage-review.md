## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Before training, participants who had never reviewed a data linkage application reported lower understanding than experienced reviewers, but after training there was no difference between the groups on any question. [→ Tan Kate M 2015](#tan-kate-m-2015)

## Evidence

### Tan Kate M 2015

Tan Kate M, Flack Felicity S, Bear Natasha L, Allen Judy A. (2015). An evaluation of a data linkage training workshop for research ethics committees. BMC Medical Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12910-015-0007-y

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Between-group (Mann Whitney U) and within-group comparisons of participants with and without prior review experience. Pre-training medians differed (e.g. "median scores 2 vs 3" for questions 1, 6 and 8, all p < 0.05); post-training medians were 4 for both groups with between-group p values such as 0.761 and 0.883.

> "Following the training there was no difference in all questions between those who had or had not previously reviewed an application (p > 0.05)."
