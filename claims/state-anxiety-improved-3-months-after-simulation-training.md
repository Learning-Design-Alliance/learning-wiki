## Evidence

### Klein Thomas 2025

Klein Thomas, Kimmoun Antoine, Granier Guillaume, Hani Hind, Levy Bruno, Viaux Alain, Zuily Stéphane, Valentin Simon. (2025). Reduced symptoms of anxiety among professional caregivers facing cardiac arrest after simulation based training. Scientific Reports 15, 9406. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-93932-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group comparison in the trained group in this prospective pilot case-control study; the article reports state anxiety "significantly improved" at 3 months with the printed means. No effect size reported.

> "State anxiety of participants was significantly improved 3 months after the training session versus baseline (55.4 ± 9.1 points vs. 41.2 ± 7.4 points; p < 0.0001)."

### Klein Thomas 2025 (2)

Klein Thomas, Kimmoun Antoine, Granier Guillaume, Hani Hind, Levy Bruno, Viaux Alain, Zuily Stéphane, Valentin Simon. (2025). Reduced symptoms of anxiety among professional caregivers facing cardiac arrest after simulation based training. Scientific Reports 15, 9406. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-93932-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline between-group comparison from the same pilot study: controls scored lower than participants on state anxiety before the session (p = 0.045), which the authors attribute partly to some controls' CPR experience.

> "Controls had a lower score than participants before the session (48.6 ± 14.5 points vs. 55.4 ± 9.1 points; p = 0.045) (Panel B, Fig. 2)."
