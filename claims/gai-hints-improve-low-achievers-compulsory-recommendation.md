# In Experiment 1, compulsory GAI-powered personalized recommendation with heuristic hints significantly improved physics performance for low-achieving students (d = 0.673)

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Low-performing students randomized to receive GAI-powered heuristic solution hints within a personalized recommendation system significantly improved standardized physics exam performance by 0.673 SD (d = 0.673). [→ Xusheng Dai 2025](#xusheng-dai-2025)

## Evidence

### Xusheng Dai 2025

Xusheng Dai, Zhaochun Wen, Jianxiao Jiang, Huiqin Liu, Yu Zhang. (2025). How Students Use AI Feedback Matters: Experimental Evidence on Physics Achievement and Autonomy. Preprint, under review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.08672](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.08672)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup regression analysis in Experiment 1, a five-week randomized trial in a Chinese high school (121 valid participants; bottom-third pretest subgroup), controlling for pretest score, pretest SRL, and class fixed effects. The GAI-hint condition showed a "significantly improved performance by 0.673 SD (p<0.05)" (d = 0.673); the workbook-only condition was marginally significant at 0.743 SD (p=0.051).

> "subgroup analyses revealed that for low-performing students, the GAI-powered recommendation significantly improved performance by 0.673 SD (p<0.05), and traditional recommendations had a marginally significant effect of 0.743 SD (p=0.051)"
