### P. Arun 2008

P. Arun, Kuldeep Kumar & Mamta. (2008). Considerations for Anderson-Bridge Experiment. https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.3932 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0804.3932](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0804.3932)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Test experiment in which one careful undergraduate, given basic instructions and Yarwood's book, performed the Anderson bridge experiment; Tables 1-3 list the results. The authors note "the results are inaccurate and are scattered and deviated from t he known value" and that the minimum could not be resolved for a range of r values.

> "the results are inaccurate and are scattered and deviated from t he known value. Also, notice that the student was not able to resolve the mini mum for a range of ‘r’ values (of the order of 50Ω)."
