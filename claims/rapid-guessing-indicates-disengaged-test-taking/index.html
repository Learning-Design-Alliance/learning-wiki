Steven Wise 2017¶

Steven Wise. (2017). Rapid‐guessing behavior: Its identification, interpretation, and implications. Educational Measurement Issues and Practice 36 (4) 52-61. https://doi.org/10.1111/emip.12189

q2 · i? · theoretical · r?

The article's abstract reports prior research showing that "very short response time—termed rapid guessing—has been shown to indicate disengaged test taking" across both high-stakes and low-stakes contexts. No effect size or study sample is printed in the available text.

"In particular, very short response time—termed rapid guessing—has been shown to indicate disengaged test taking, regardless whether it occurs in high‐stakes or low‐stakes testing contexts."
