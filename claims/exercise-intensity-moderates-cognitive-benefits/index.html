Related Claims¶

  • Acute exercise produces an overall small positive effect on cognition, with executive functions benefiting most and effects lasting up to two hours post-exercise — related
  • Acute moderate aerobic exercise speeds children's reaction times without improving accuracy, with greater speed gains on choice than simple reaction time tasks — related
  • Duration and intensity of PA bouts may moderate attention effects, consistent with an inverted-U hypothesis — related
  • Type of PA may moderate acute effects: coordinative exercises outperformed a normal PE lesson of the same intensity — related
  • Aerobic exercise improves executive functioning, most notably inhibitory control, in typically developing children, though evidence is tentative and one week-long study found no main effect — related
  • A 10-minute bout of moderate-intensity exercise improves Stroop inhibitory control with increased left dorsolateral prefrontal activation in young adults — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Effortful regulation is maximized at moderate levels of emotional and physiological reactivity and declines at very low or very high levels — related
  • Five of six laboratory studies found significant effects of acute PA on cognitive control of attention, mainly on accuracy not reaction time — related
  • Reported benefits of physical activity programs on executive functions depend on program duration and the executive domain analyzed — related
  • The reward effect on inhibitory control is significant across all six IC task types, but task type significantly moderates the effect, with the largest effect in Stroop tasks — related
  • Physical well-being — exercise, sleep, and nutrition — impacts learning — related
  • Self-chosen exercise intensity produced the greatest mood benefits in a small study, suggesting enjoyable workouts may best improve mood — related
  • Executive functions in childhood benefit from moderate-to-vigorous intensity and aerobic physical activity interventions — related
  • Mathematics outcomes benefit from PA with academic instruction of 6–10-week duration at moderate or moderate-to-vigorous intensity, and language outcomes from enriched PA programmes — related
