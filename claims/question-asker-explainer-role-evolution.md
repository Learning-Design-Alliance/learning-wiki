Jessica R. Hoehn, Julian D. Gifford, and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2020). Epistemic stances toward group work in learning physics: Interactions between epistemology and social dynamics in a collaborative problem solving context. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2005.02425](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2005.02425)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Discourse analysis of Episode 1 identifies the exchange about the number of nodes in the n=7 wave function as the turning point. The authors note these "positioning and role-taking tendencies ... continue to evolve and begin to solidify as the group continues to engage in collective problem solving." Qualitative; no effect size.

> "This exchange represents a turning point in the overall dynamic of the group, where Penny begins to assume the role of "question asker" and Cam and Morgan begin to assume roles as "explainers.""
