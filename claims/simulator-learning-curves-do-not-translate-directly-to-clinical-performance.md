### Post JH 2023

Post JH, Teslak KE, Tolsgaard MG, Rasmussen S, Friis ML. (2023). Cost of simulation-based mastery learning for abdominal ultrasound. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04919-5

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Author interpretation in the Discussion, attributing to prior gynecological ultrasound training studies that trainees still required high supervision over their first 6 months, though "substantially less" than trainees without simulation-based training.

> "the learning curves demonstrated in the simulated setting do not translate to directly clinical performances."

### Post JH 2023 (2)

Post JH, Teslak KE, Tolsgaard MG, Rasmussen S, Friis ML. (2023). Cost of simulation-based mastery learning for abdominal ultrasound. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04919-5

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Narrative comparison in the Discussion attributing shorter learning curves to prior cited studies; the authors attribute the difference to their program's more comprehensive diagnostic abdominal ultrasound skills versus simpler procedures.

> "Previous studies focusing on simulation-based ultrasound training have reported significantly shorter learning curves before attaining mastery learning levels – for example, between three to 4 hours for simulation-based obstetric [15], gynecological ultrasound [16], or FAST examinations [5]."
