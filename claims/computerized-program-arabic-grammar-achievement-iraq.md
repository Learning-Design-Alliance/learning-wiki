Quasi-experimental pre/post two-group study of 120 intermediate first-grade students in Diqar, Iraq (60 experimental, 60 control), six weeks of instruction; ANCOVA on post-test with pre-test covariate gave F = 101.657, p = 0.000 for teaching method, "in favor of the experimental method".
