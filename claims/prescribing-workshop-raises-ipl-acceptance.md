## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-workshop self-rated total SAIL-10 scores were significantly higher than pre-workshop scores in one uncontrolled pre-post study (Wilcoxon signed ranks test). [→ Chua Siew Siang 2019](#chua-siew-siang-2019)
`q2 i?` Both self-rated domain scores were significantly higher after the workshop in one uncontrolled pre-post study, although the median facilitators domain score rose and the median groups domain score did not change. [→ Chua Siew Siang 2019 (2)](#chua-siew-siang-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Chua Siew Siang 2019

Chua Siew Siang, Lai Pauline Siew Mei, Sim Si Mui, Tan Choo Hock, Foong Chan Choong. (2019). Acceptance of interprofessional learning between medical and pharmacy students in a prescribing skills training workshop: pre-post intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1525-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post study of 270 students (73.4% response rate) completing SAIL-10 before and after the workshop; Table 3 reports total score z = −9.950, p < 0.001. The article reports the scores as "significantly higher after the participants attended".

> "The total SAIL-10 score, as well as the scores for both domains were significantly higher after the participants attended the prescribing skills workshop (Table 3)."

### Chua Siew Siang 2019 (2)

Chua Siew Siang, Lai Pauline Siew Mei, Sim Si Mui, Tan Choo Hock, Foong Chan Choong. (2019). Acceptance of interprofessional learning between medical and pharmacy students in a prescribing skills training workshop: pre-post intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1525-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 3 of the Phase 2 pre-post assessment (Wilcoxon signed ranks test) reports post-workshop z-values of −10.310 for Domain 1 and −4.471 for Domain 2, both p < 0.001; median Domain 1 rose from 25 to 28 and Domain 2 from 12 to 12 (IQR 4 to 5).

> "Pre- prescribing skill workshop 270 72 (18) 81.66 25 (8) 89.58 12 (4) 96.67 Post- prescribing skill workshop 270 82 (22) 131.93 28 (7) 124.27 12 (5) 100.12"
