The review's synthesis of feedback modalities across included studies reports that "human supervision was often deemed superior in guiding skill acquisition". This is a qualitative comparative judgement, not a meta-analytic estimate.

"While automated feedback ensured immediacy, human supervision was often deemed superior in guiding skill acquisition."
