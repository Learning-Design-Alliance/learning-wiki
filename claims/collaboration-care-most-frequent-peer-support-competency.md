`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of 41 RCTs of peer support interventions for adults with serious mental health challenges; the authors estimated the utilization frequency of each competency across all trials, with the denominator being the number of eligible trials. The review reports "collaboration and care (90.2%)" as the most frequent competency.

> "The most frequently demonstrated peer support competency across all trials (N = 41) was collaboration and care (90.2%), followed by providing support (82.9%); communication (73.2%); recovery planning (70.7%); and skill teaching (68.3%)."

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Exploratory subgroup analysis of competency frequencies grouped by three serious mental health challenge categories across the 41 included RCTs. Frequencies were similar across subcategories, with collaboration and care most demonstrated in each.

> "collaboration and care was the most demonstrated peer support competency for interventions targeting schizophrenia and other psychotic disorders (87.1%, n = 31), bipolar disorder (91.2%, n = 24), and anxiety and depression (89.3%, n = 28)"
