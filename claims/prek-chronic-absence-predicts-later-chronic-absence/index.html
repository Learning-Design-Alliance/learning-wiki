q2 · i? · associational · r?

Longitudinal observational analysis following pre-k students into later grades; the study reports chronically absent pre-k students "were more likely to be chronically absent in subsequent grades." No effect size or test statistic is printed.

"Chronically absent pre-kindergarten students—those who were absent 10 percent or more of their enrolled days—displayed lower levels of academic and behavioral kindergarten readiness and were more likely to be chronically absent in subsequent grades."
