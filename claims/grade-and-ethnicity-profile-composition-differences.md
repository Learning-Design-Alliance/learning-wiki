## Evidence

### Hernández 2024

Hernández, M. M., Kornienko, O., Figueroa, J. M., Coker, M., Paredes, K., Toth, C., Carrillo, J. P., Rogers, A. A., & Ha, T. (2024). Identifying Culturally Relevant School Support Profiles and Links to Academic Functioning in Adolescents. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-024-02098-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple-group measurement equivalence testing of the LPA across grades. With profile means and variances equal between grades, "the low general, devoid cultural & critical (#1) and moderate general, moderate cultural, & devoid critical (#2) support profiles had proportionally more 9th graders than 6th graders"; the two high-general profiles had proportionally more 6th graders.

> "Based on evidence of dispersion similarity between grades (i.e., the profile means and variances were equal between sixth and ninth graders), the low general, devoid cultural & critical (#1) and moderate general, moderate cultural, & devoid critical (#2) support profiles had proportionally more 9th graders than 6th graders."

### Hernández 2024 (2)

Hernández, M. M., Kornienko, O., Figueroa, J. M., Coker, M., Paredes, K., Toth, C., Carrillo, J. P., Rogers, A. A., & Ha, T. (2024). Identifying Culturally Relevant School Support Profiles and Links to Academic Functioning in Adolescents. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-024-02098-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Measurement equivalence testing of the four-profile LPA across ethnoracial background. "There was evidence for distributional similarity across ethnicity-race", with means, variances, and profile proportions equal between minoritized and White youth.

> "There was evidence for distributional similarity across ethnicity-race (i.e., the profile means, variances, and distributional sample sizes were equal between ethnoracially minoritized and White youth)."
