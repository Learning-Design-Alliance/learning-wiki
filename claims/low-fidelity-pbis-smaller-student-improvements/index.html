Authors' discussion interpretation (type e) of the fidelity findings: low-fidelity teachers may conclude PBIS does not work, and without considering fidelity it is difficult to ascertain whether the intervention enhanced or constrained outcomes.

"despite almost a full year of training on PBIS, teachers implementing with less skill (i.e., low fidelity) tended to not experience large improvements in student behavioral functioning commensurate with their colleagues implementing with high levels of skill (i.e., high fidelity)."
