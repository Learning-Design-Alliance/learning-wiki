## Evidence

### Bagheri Majid 2025

Bagheri Majid, Shiryani Morteza, Mehralizade Sara, Basirinezhad Mohammad Hasan, Bagheri Hossein, Abbasi Ali. (2025). The effect of educational intervention using surgery preference cards on anxiety and clinical competence among operating room nursing students in general surgeries: a randomized controlled trial study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07930-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison in the randomized trial (30 per group) measured with the Persian PPCS-R two weeks after the intervention: the intervention group scored higher, but "this difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.076)". Equivalence was not tested; no effect size printed.

> "although the mean clinical competence score was higher in the intervention group compared to the control group, this difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.076)."

### Bagheri Majid 2025 (2)

Bagheri Majid, Shiryani Morteza, Mehralizade Sara, Basirinezhad Mohammad Hasan, Bagheri Hossein, Abbasi Ali. (2025). The effect of educational intervention using surgery preference cards on anxiety and clinical competence among operating room nursing students in general surgeries: a randomized controlled trial study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07930-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intragroup paired comparison in the same trial: the intervention group's mean clinical competence rose significantly (p < 0.001), while the control group's change was not significant (p = 0.196). No effect size printed.

> "in the intervention group, we observed a significant increase in the mean clinical competence score after the surgical preference card training, which was statistically significant (p < 0.001)."
