In the randomized controlled trial, satisfaction was measured with a single-item 0-10 Numerical Rating Scale; the chatbot group's post-test mean rose to 7.42 ± 1.59 while the control group's fell to 5.55 ± 1.74. The authors attribute satisfaction to chatbots allowing conversations "free from time and space constraints" and reduced tension and stress.
