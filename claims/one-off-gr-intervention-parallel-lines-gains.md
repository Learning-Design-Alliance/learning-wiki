### Corey McInerney and Phil Sutton 2023

Corey McInerney and Phil Sutton. (2023). Einsteinian gravitational concepts throughout secondary school. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad001a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad001a)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional pre/post questionnaire at one English grammar school (183 participants, Years 7-13). The results section reports that 'yes' responses to Q1 rose from 6.2% to 44.2% and that "all year groups showed an increase in 'yes' responses for both Q1 and Q2 with Yr 9, 11, and 12 having the highest increase in percentage".

> "The total number of ' yes' responses moves from 6.2% to 44.2% after the intervention."
