The letter reports, citing VanLehn (2011), a survey finding that step-based ITSs were "nearly as effective as average human tutors", less effective than expert human tutors, and better than untutored domain content. No effect sizes are printed in the letter.

> "VanLehn (2011) found that the effectiveness of the Bstep-based^ intelligent tutoring systems he surveyed were nearly as effective as average human tutors, and that neither of these were as effective as expert human tutors"
