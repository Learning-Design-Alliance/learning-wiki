Sarah Herrera 2021¶

Sarah Herrera, Beth M. Phillips, Yi-Chieh Newton, Jennifer L. Dombek, and James A. Hernandez. (2021). Effectiveness of Early Literacy Instruction: Summary of 20 Years of Research. Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast, REL 2021-084. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Weighted synthesis of 13 interventions teaching phonological awareness and print knowledge but not decoding. These interventions "significantly improved decoding performance (weighted effect size 0.22)," suggesting decoding can improve without direct decoding instruction.

"Thirteen other interventions taught phonological awareness and print knowledge but not decoding and were evaluated in studies that included decoding outcomes; these interventions significantly improved decoding performance (weighted effect size 0.22; see table B15 in appendix B)."

Sarah Herrera 2021 (2)¶

Sarah Herrera, Beth M. Phillips, Yi-Chieh Newton, Jennifer L. Dombek, and James A. Hernandez. (2021). Effectiveness of Early Literacy Instruction: Summary of 20 Years of Research. Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast, REL 2021-084. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Figure 7 comparison across the 4 interventions teaching all three domains versus 13 teaching phonological awareness and print knowledge only. The weighted effect size for decoding reached 0.52 when all three domains were taught, though the review notes the two effect sizes were not significantly different.

"However, when phonological awareness, print knowledge, and decoding instruction co-occurred, the weighted effect size for decoding increased to 0.52."
