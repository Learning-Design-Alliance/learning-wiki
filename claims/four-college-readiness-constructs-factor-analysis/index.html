Factor analysis of roughly 40 variables identified as strongly predictive by random forest and lasso models; the bifactor model fit best (chi-square 2403, RMSEA .056) among candidate models compared on fit statistics.

"Beyond the general factor, the variables load on roughly four factors. The first of these factors is associated primarily with test scores and other measures of academic preparation like grades."
