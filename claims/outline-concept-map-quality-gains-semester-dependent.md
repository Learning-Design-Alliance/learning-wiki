# Quality of outlines and concept maps rose on seven of nine metrics in Fall, while outline scores rose and concept map scores did not change in Spring, in single-group pre-post studies with no comparison groups

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Fall 2014, students' scores on seven of nine outline and concept map quality metrics rose from the first to the last graded assignment in a single-group pre-post study with no comparison group, including outline content (t53 = 3.96, p = 0.0002) and concept map organization (t53 = 2.89, p = 0.0056). [→ Hoskins Tyler D. 2017](#hoskins-tyler-d-2017)
`q2 i?` In Spring 2015, students' outline scores rose from the first to the last graded assignment in a single-group pre-post study with no comparison group (content t43 = 4.17, p < 0.0001; organization t43 = 3.15, p = 0.0029), while scores on every concept map metric did not change. [→ Hoskins Tyler D. 2017 (2)](#hoskins-tyler-d-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Hoskins Tyler D. 2017

Hoskins Tyler D., Gantz J. D., Chaffee Blake R., Arlinghaus Kel, Wiebler James, Hughes Michael, Fernandes Joyce J. (2017). Effectiveness of a Low-Cost, Graduate Student–Led Intervention on Study Habits and Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0004

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t tests on triplicate-graded first and last assignments from Fall 2014. Outline content improved significantly, outline organization only marginally, and concept map metrics improved except number of nodes (t53 = 1.63, p = 0.1087).

> "Students improved on seven of the nine metrics we examined for the quality of outlines and concept maps (Figure 2). Student outlines improved for content scores (t53 = 3.96, p = 0.0002; Figure 2), but showed only marginal improvement on organization (t53 = 1.87, p = 0.0670; Figure 2)."

### Hoskins Tyler D. 2017 (2)

Hoskins Tyler D., Gantz J. D., Chaffee Blake R., Arlinghaus Kel, Wiebler James, Hughes Michael, Fernandes Joyce J. (2017). Effectiveness of a Low-Cost, Graduate Student–Led Intervention on Study Habits and Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0004

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t tests on Spring 2015 graded assignments showed outline gains on both scored components but no significant change in any concept map metric, a direct contrast to the Fall pattern.

> "Student outlines improved over the duration of the sprint course both in terms of organization (t43 = 3.15, p = 0.0029; Figure 2) and content (t43 = 4.17, p < 0.0001; Figure 2). Conversely, we did not observe changes in scores on concept maps in any metric analyzed"
