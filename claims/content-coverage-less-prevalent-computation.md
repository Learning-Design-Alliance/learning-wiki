## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ashleigh Leary 2019

Ashleigh Leary, Paul W. Irving, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2019). The difficulties associated with integrating computation into undergraduate physics. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.leary](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.leary)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview thematic analysis found this force weakened for computation: an interviewee described TA pushback that students still needed to learn to add vectors and that time should go to upcoming exams, not computation.

> "Expectations of Content Coverage: was not as prevalent in the context of computation. It was not a question of what computational material should be covered, it was more an issue of time."
