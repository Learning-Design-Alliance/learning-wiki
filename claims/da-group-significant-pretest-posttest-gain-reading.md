## Evidence

### Mohammad Reza Amirian 2014

Mohammad Reza Amirian, Mohammad Davoudi, Mohsen Ramazanian. (2014). The Effect of Dynamic Assessment on Iranian EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 5(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.3p.191

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-test on the 14 experimental-group learners' pretest and post-test scores on the same Objective PET reading test. The article reports "t(13)= 3.79, P=.002 < .05" and eta squared of 0.54, a large effect size.

> "Results of paired-samples-t-test of the pretest and post-test in the experimental group showed that there was a significant difference between scores from pretest (M=22.35, SD= 3.79) to post-test (M=26.85, SD=2.50), t(13)= 3.79, P=.002 < .05 (two-tailed). The eta-squared statistic (0.54) indicated a large effect size based on Cohen (1988)."
