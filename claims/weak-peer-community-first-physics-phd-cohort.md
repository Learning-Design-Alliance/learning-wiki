Focus group theme from the first-year Physics Ph.D. cohort. A participant noted the focus group itself was "more direct interaction with my peers than is otherwise fostered within the department." Students also perceived Astrophysics students as having a stronger social network aided by shared classes.

> "This is why I'm advocating for more events. Outside of class time or grad seminar, this interview is more direct interaction with my peers than is otherwise fostered within the department, and I would like to change that."
