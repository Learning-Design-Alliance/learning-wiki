## Evidence

### Pablo Flores Romero 2025

Pablo Flores Romero, Kin Nok Nicholas Fung, Guang Rong & Benjamin Ultan Cowley. (2025). Structured human-LLM interaction design reveals exploration and exploitation dynamics in higher education content generation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00332-3

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of 25 structured GPT interactions in a doctoral AIEd course. Kendall's tau showed a significant positive correlation with the printed effect size τ = 0.57 (p = 0.0008), described as "a significant positive correlation between the frequency of exploration prompts and the diversity of keywords used by participants".

> "there is a signiﬁcant positive correlation between the frequency of exploration prompts and the diversity of keywords used by participants (τ = 0.57, p = 0.0008)"
