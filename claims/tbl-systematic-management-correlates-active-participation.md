The moderating effect of team-based learning participation frequency on the relationship between medical students’ perception of systematic team-based learning class management and active class participation. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.346

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of feedback questionnaire data from 575 medical students in TBL courses at one Korean medical college. The study reports a "significant positive correlation" between perception of systematic management and active participation, r=0.762, p<0.01; the correlation is observational and causal direction was not tested.

> "The Pearson correlation analysis showed a significant positive correlation between students’ perception of systematic TBL class management and their active class participation (r=0.762, p<0.01), suggesting that higher perceptions of systematic management are associated with higher levels of active participation."
