# Elementary students' perceived science ability significantly increased after the Dark Skies, Bright Kids astronomy program, while creativity and science aspirations did not change significantly

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' perceptions of their science ability significantly increased after program participation (Wilcoxon signed-rank test, p = 0.0034). [→ Finn 2023](#finn-2023)
`q2 i?` After the program, all but three of the 74 paired respondents answered positively to feeling good at science. [→ Finn 2023 (2)](#finn-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Finn 2023

Finn, M. K., Mazzei, R., Drechsler, B., Telkamp, Z., Rao, M., Agrawal, P., and McAlister, A. (2023). How Informal Science Education Influences Elementary Students’ Perceptions of Science and Themselves. Journal of STEM Outreach. https://doi.org/10.15695/jstem/v6i1.10

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Matched before- and after-club survey responses from elementary DSBK participants, analyzed with a non-parametric Wilcoxon signed-rank test. The article reports perceived science ability "significantly increased after participation in our program at the 95% confidence threshold (p = 0.0034)".

> "For the question "Do you feel like you are good at science?", students' perceptions of their science ability significantly increased after participation in our program at the 95% confidence threshold (p = 0.0034)."

### Finn 2023 (2)

Finn, M. K., Mazzei, R., Drechsler, B., Telkamp, Z., Rao, M., Agrawal, P., and McAlister, A. (2023). How Informal Science Education Influences Elementary Students’ Perceptions of Science and Themselves. Journal of STEM Outreach. https://doi.org/10.15695/jstem/v6i1.10

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive tally of the 74 matched before/after survey pairs reported in the discussion: "only three students responded to "Do you feel like you are good at science?" with "Maybe" at the end of the program", with all other responses positive.

> "Of the 74 paired before and after responses, only three students responded to "Do you feel like you are good at science?" with "Maybe" at the end of the program, and all other responses were "Yes" or "Definitely yes"."

### Finn 2023 (3)

Finn, M. K., Mazzei, R., Drechsler, B., Telkamp, Z., Rao, M., Agrawal, P., and McAlister, A. (2023). How Informal Science Education Influences Elementary Students’ Perceptions of Science and Themselves. Journal of STEM Outreach. https://doi.org/10.15695/jstem/v6i1.10

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on matched before/after responses found "the before and after responses did not change significantly over the course of our programs" for the creativity and scientist-aspiration questions.

> "the before and after responses did not change significantly over the course of our programs, at the 95% confidence threshold for the questions "Do you feel like you are creative?" (p = 0.096) and "When you grow up, do you want to be a scientist?" (p = 0.082)."
