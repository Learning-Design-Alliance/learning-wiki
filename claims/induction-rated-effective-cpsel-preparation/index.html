Evidence¶

Wechsler 2024¶

Wechsler, M. E., Patrick, S. K., Thompson, C., & Levin, S. (2024). On the path to leadership: California's administrator induction programs. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/636.224

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Statewide completer survey analysis (6,812 completers, 2017–2021) reports that "over 90% of California administrators" rated their program effective or very effective; the authors suggest this may reflect the state's high program standards. No effect size is printed.

"Over 90% of California administrators on the statewide survey rated their induction program as effective or very effective at developing the knowledge and skills needed to become an education leader, which may reflect California's high program standards."

Wechsler 2024 (2)¶

Wechsler, M. E., Patrick, S. K., Thompson, C., & Levin, S. (2024). On the path to leadership: California's administrator induction programs. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/636.224

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The same completer survey reports CPSEL-aligned preparedness ratings, with "at least 85% of survey respondents" reporting they were well or very well prepared on every leadership skill. These are descriptive percentages; no inferential test or effect size is printed.

"Across every leadership skill aligned with the California Professional Standards for Education Leaders (CPSEL), at least 85% of survey respondents reported being well or very well prepared for that leadership skill."
