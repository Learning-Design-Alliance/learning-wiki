Evidence¶

Sorkkila 2026¶

Sorkkila, M., Malmberg, L.-E., Pöysä, S., Kiuru, N., Lerkkanen, M.-K., & Pakarinen, E. (2026). Change and transmission of students' and teachers' lesson-specific emotions. Learning and Instruction, 102, Article 102298. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102298

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-classified model (M2) of student lesson-reports. The article reports no effect for the positive-emotion path and states "There were no effects of teachers' emotions on students' negative emotions." Equivalence was not tested.

"As shown in Fig. 3 (M2), no effect was identified for positive emotions."

Sorkkila 2026 (2)¶

Sorkkila, M., Malmberg, L.-E., Pöysä, S., Kiuru, N., Lerkkanen, M.-K., & Pakarinen, E. (2026). Change and transmission of students' and teachers' lesson-specific emotions. Learning and Instruction, 102, Article 102298. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102298

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-classified model (M2) of teacher lesson-reports nested in 20 teachers. The article reports no credible path from either student emotion to teacher positive emotions; equivalence was not tested.

"As shown in Fig. 4 (M2), neither of students' emotions predicted teachers' positive emotions."
