### Wu Jr-Wei 2022

Wu Jr-Wei, Cheng Hao-Min, Huang Shiau-Shian, Liang Jen-Feng, Huang Chia-Chang, Shulruf Boaz, Yang Ying-Ying, Chen Chen-Huan, Hou Ming-Chih, Huey-Herng Sheu Wayne. (2022). Correlational analysis in the derivation cohort of 50 NYMU graduates (2018) examined associations between grades and national OSCE performance. The study reports "Pearson r = 0.34, p = 0.017" for medical school grades versus OSCE scores above the qualification standard.

> "medical school grades were associated with the performance of national OSCEs (defined as the percentage of scores above the qualification standard) (Pearson r = 0.34, p = 0.017)"
