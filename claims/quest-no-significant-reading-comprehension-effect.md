## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Quest: Evaluation report and Executive summary 2015

Quest: Evaluation report and Executive summary. (2015). Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581287

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomised efficacy trial with regression and robust standard errors; 2,083 pupils analysed. Table 3.9 reports Hedges g = -0.04 (95% CI -0.14, 0.07), p = 0.48, adjusted post-test means of -0.06 (intervention) vs -0.03 (control).

> "A small negative effect size was found but was not statistically significant. In addition, none of the interaction terms were significant."
