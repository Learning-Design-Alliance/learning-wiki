Posttest t-test analysis of the same two intact sections (control N 26, treatment N 28). Two-tailed p-value was 0.0415, reported as "Statistically significant", with observed t-value 2.1492 at df 25; posttest means were 5.31 (control) and 9.04 (treatment).
