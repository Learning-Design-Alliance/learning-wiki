## Evidence

### Devine 2025

Devine, R. T., Gray, L., Edwards, M., Jess, M., Dempsey, C., Heng, J., Mehrotra, M., D'Souza, H., Fink, E., & Hughes, C. (2025). Changes in Children's Well-Being and Mental Health Across the Early School Years: Links With Academic and Social Competence. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001962

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis comparing competing measurement models in a 1-year longitudinal study of 252 children (Mage = 5.40 years), fit with WLSMV in Mplus. The article reports that "a three-factor model was the best fitting model at Time 1 and Time 2" at both waves.

> "A three-factor model was the best fitting model at Time 1 and Time 2, indicating that children's well-being and mental health were distinct but related constructs (Figure 2)."
