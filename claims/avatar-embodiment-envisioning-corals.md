## Evidence

### Paolo Boffi 2026

Paolo Boffi, Alberto Gallace, Pier Luca Lanzi. (2026). An Investigation of the Relation Between Immersion and Learning Across Three Domains. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A within-subjects lab study of Envisioning Corals with 31 participants experiencing all three avatars in randomized order found a main effect of avatar on embodiment, with "the hermit crab revealing significantly higher scores than the coral", driven by the Agency subscale.

> "A Linear Mixed Model (LMM) on overall embodiment revealed a main effect of the avatar (χ 2(2) = 7.47,p=.023), with the hermit crab revealing significantly higher scores than the coral (t(58.1) =−2.57,p=.034)"

### Paolo Boffi 2026 (2)

Paolo Boffi, Alberto Gallace, Pier Luca Lanzi. (2026). An Investigation of the Relation Between Immersion and Learning Across Three Domains. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In Envisioning Corals study 2 (27 participants, IVR vs DVR, hermit crab avatar only), embodiment questionnaire scores were significantly higher for IVR, with differences in Agency, Tactile Sensation, Location, and Appearance subscales but not Body Ownership or Response.

> "Embodiment scores (Figures 6b–6d) were evaluated through Student's t-test, revealing significantly higher scores for the IVR group (t= 5.19,p < .001;M IV R = 2.63,M DV R =1.39)"

### Paolo Boffi 2026 (3)

Paolo Boffi, Alberto Gallace, Pier Luca Lanzi. (2026). An Investigation of the Relation Between Immersion and Learning Across Three Domains. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Electrodermal activity recorded around three events in study 2 showed "significantly higher IVR activations at the Embodiment" and at the Intro-End event, while no differences occurred at the Env-Actions event; Response and Body Ownership subscales did not correlate with final-event EDA.

> "Wilcoxon test-based analyses revealed significantly higher IVR activations at the Embodiment (W= 137,p=.025;M IV R = 0.164,MDV R =−0.411)"
