## Evidence

### Rathmann Katharina 2018

Rathmann Katharina, Herke Max, Bilz Ludwig, Rimpelä Arja, Hurrelmann Klaus, Richter Matthias. (2018). Class-Level School Performance and Life Satisfaction: Differential Sensitivity for Low- and High-Performing School-Aged Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15122750

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 4 of the NEPS multilevel analysis (n = 5196 students, 478 classes, 250 schools) added a cross-level interaction term: −0.19 (SE 0.05, p < 0.001). Simple slopes showed a significant negative slope for class-level performance only for students at +1 SD individual performance (estimate −0.257, p < 0.010).

> "Students with high performance show significantly lower life satisfaction when placed in high-performing classes, and higher life satisfaction when placed in low-performing classes."
