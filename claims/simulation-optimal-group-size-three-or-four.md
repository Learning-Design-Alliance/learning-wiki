## Evidence

### Jibeom Seo 2025

Jibeom Seo, Beom Jun Kim. (2025). Opinion dynamics model of collaborative learning. [doi:10.1016/j.physa.2025.130670](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physa.2025.130670)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Simulation of final group performance versus group size N for initial conditions NH, EH, and SH (T = 10, tf = 100), shown in Fig. 6. The article reports that "the final group performance displays a clear maximum around N = 3 or N = 4."

> "for the SH initial condition, the final group performance displays a clear maximum around N = 3 or N = 4. Additionally, we find that the group performance for N = 2 is worse than N = 3, implying that too small group size hinders the group of students from finding the correct answer"
