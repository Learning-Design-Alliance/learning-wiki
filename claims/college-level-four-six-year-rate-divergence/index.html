Nagaoka 2023¶

Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Arneson, A. (2023). The four years fallacy: Four-year vs. six-year bachelor's degree completion rates. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/the-four-years-fallacy

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of the 41 colleges most commonly attended by 2012–14 CPS immediate enrollees (25,847 students), plotted in Figure 3. The report contrasts College C and College D, whose four-year rates differ by "a difference of 27 percentage points" despite similar six-year rates above 80%. No effect size is printed.

"College C has a four-year completion rate of 44% and College D has a four-year completion rate of 71%—a difference of 27 percentage points that would not be reflected on most college search tools for students considering both colleges."
