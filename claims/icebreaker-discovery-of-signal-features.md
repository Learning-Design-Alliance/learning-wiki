## Evidence

### Michelle B. Larson 2006

Michelle B. Larson, Louis J. Rubbo, Kristina D. Zaleski and Shane L. Larson. (2006). Science Icebreaker Activities: An Example from Gravitational Wave Astronomy. Originally published 2005. [doi:10.1119/1.2353577](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.2353577)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' facilitation guidance reports, from implementing the activity with students and adults, that participants with minimal instruction discover matching criteria themselves. This is the authors' implementation experience, not a controlled study; no effect size is reported.

> "The participants, so me with encouragement, will discover the need to use the axes as reference lines, and to look carefu lly at details such as amplitude, period and initial phase diﬀerences, or other signiﬁcant features i n the signals."
