## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wiggins Benjamin L. 2017

Wiggins Benjamin L., Eddy Sarah L., Wener-Fligner Leah, Freisem Karen, Grunspan Daniel Z., Theobald Elli J., Timbrook Jerry, Crowe Alison J. (2017). ASPECT: A Survey to Assess Student Perspective of Engagement in an Active-Learning Classroom. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0244

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Repeated-measures comparison in which the same students completed ASPECT after a short-activity day (clicker questions) and a long-activity day (group worksheet). Linear mixed-effects models with student random effects, selected by AIC backward selection, showed discrimination on Value and IC "but not the PE students put into the activity."

> "We found that the ASPECT survey distinguishes between activity types, student populations, and student populations performing different activity types both in the Value students place on the activity and in the IC students perceive, but not the PE students put into the activity (Table 3)."
