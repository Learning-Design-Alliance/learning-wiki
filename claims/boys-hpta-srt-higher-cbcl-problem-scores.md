## Evidence

### Reijers Stefanie N.H. 2025

Reijers Stefanie N.H., Vroegop Jantien L., Jansen Pauline W., Kremer Bernd, van der Schroeff Marc P. (2025). The Impact of Slight to Mild Hearing Loss on Academic Performance and Behavior of 9–15‐Year‐Olds. The Laryngoscope. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.31908

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multiple linear regression on CBCL T-scores, stratified after a significant hearing-acuity-by-sex interaction (boys n = 2301), showed "β = 0.01; 95% CI [0.00, 0.02]" per dB HPTA for total problems; the article states every 1 dB corresponded with a 0.10 point higher score.

> "Among boys, both elevated HPTA in the better‐ and worse‐hearing ear, and SRT, were associated with the total problem score (per 1 dB HPTA: β = 0.01; 95% CI [0.00, 0.02] and per 1 dB SRT: β = 0.04; 95% CI [0.01, 0.07])."
