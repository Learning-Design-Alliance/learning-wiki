## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Across two years of implementation, the authors observed that students struggled to distinguish coding errors from physics errors and that long gaps between VPython exposures forced relearning. [→ Caballero 2012](#caballero-2012)

## Evidence

### Caballero 2012

Caballero, M.D.; Burk, J.B.; Aiken, J.M.; Thoms, B.D.; Douglas, S.S.; Scanlon, E.M.; Schatz, M.F. (2012). Integrating Numerical Computation into the Modeling Instruction Curriculum. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1207.0844](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1207.0844)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Authors' reflection on two years of teaching the course (15-18 students per year) in a private school setting. They attribute the debugging difficulty to "the somewhat loose integration of computational modeling in their physics course." No measures are reported.

> "Students find debugging their programs difficult; that is, they have trouble determining whether they have made a coding error or a physics error and how to deal with that issue. This is likely due to the somewhat loose integration of computational modeling in their physics course."
