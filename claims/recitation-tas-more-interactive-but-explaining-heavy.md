## Evidence

### Eric M. Hickok and Cassandra A. Paul 2025

Eric M. Hickok and Cassandra A. Paul. (2025). Examining teaching assistant pedagogies in traditional laboratories and recitations. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.09136](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.09136)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational RIOT analysis of roughly seven hours of recitation observations across three TAs teaching three sections. TAs split time nearly evenly between whole class (23.3%), small groups (26.0%), individuals (20.9%), and not interacting (29.8%); "During the Recitations, a large percentage of total time (33.4%) is spentExplaining Content."

> "Recitations are more interactive than labs in that only 29.8% of total time is spentNot Interacting."
