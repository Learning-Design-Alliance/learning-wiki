## Evidence

### Lee Nam-Ju 2016

Lee Nam-Ju, Chae Sun-Mi, Kim Haejin, Lee Ji-Hye, Min Hyojin Jennifer, Park Da-Eun. (2016). Mobile-Based Video Learning Outcomes in Clinical Nursing Skill Education: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Computers, Informatics, Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1097/CIN.0000000000000183

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same RCT, knowledge (13 researcher-developed items) and skill performance (15-item checklist on a catheter model, blind evaluators) showed no statistically significant between-group differences; "scores for knowledge and skill performance ... were higher in the intervention group."

> "In addition, scores for knowledge and skill performance, markers of fundamental nursing competency, were higher in the intervention group than in the control group, but there were no statistically significant differences."
