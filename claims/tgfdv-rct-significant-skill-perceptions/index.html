What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Too Good for Drugs and Violence. https://www.mendezfoundation.org

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial (Bacon, 2001a) with 303 students in grades 9-12 across five Florida high schools, classrooms randomly assigned. WWC-calculated effect size for perceptions of social and resistance skills was 0.52 ("0.52 Statistically significant"); emotional competence had effect size 0.50, also statistically significant.
