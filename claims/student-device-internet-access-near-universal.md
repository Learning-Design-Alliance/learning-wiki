Descriptive survey tracking of student access to devices and internet from March 2020 to March 2021 (Figure 17). Initial access ranged from 45 percent in New York to 73 percent in Washington; internet access rose from 58 percent to nearly 95 percent by May 2020.

> "By March 2021, schools across all states reported that at least 98 percent of students had access to devices."
