### Wonde 2024

Wonde, S. G., Tadesse, T., Moges, B., & Schauber, S. K. (2024). Experts’ prediction of item difficulty of multiple-choice questions in the Ethiopian Undergraduate Medicine Licensure Examination. *BMC Medical Education*. [doi:10.1186/s12909-024-06012-x](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06012-x)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Domain-level comparison of mean rater predictions versus actual performance across the fourteen UGMLE domains; maximum differences occurred in domains thirteen (30.4%), seven (26.4%) and eleven (25.5%), minimum in domains four (0.1%), one (1.2%) and two (2.4%).

> "in exam domain thirteen, the mean actual performance (Mean = 41.74, SE = 6.19) was 30.4% lower than the mean rater prediction (Mean = 59.93; SE = 1.71). Whereas in domain three the mean actual performance (Mean = 70.99, SE = 4.34) was greater than the mean rater prediction (Mean = 64.46; SE = 1.14) by 10.1%."
