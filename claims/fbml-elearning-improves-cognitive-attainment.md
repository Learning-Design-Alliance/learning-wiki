## Evidence

### Amasha 2014

Amasha, M., & Alkhalaf, S. (2014). The Effect of Using Facebook Markup Language (FBML) for Designing an E-Learning Model in Higher Education. International Journal of Research in Computer Science, 4(5), 1-9. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.05576](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.05576)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A quasi-experimental study of 58 SLRD students randomly divided into experimental (n=29) and control (n=29) groups compared cognitive attainment using a t-test. The experimental group mean was 45.41 (SD 3.96) versus control 17.79 (SD 5.79), described as "a statistically significant distinction between the mean of the students' degrees."

> "Results indicate that there is a statistically significant distinction between the mean of the students' degrees of the experimental group in favor of the control in the students' cognitive attainment after teaching for knowledge, t(56, N = 29) = 21.173; p = .05."
