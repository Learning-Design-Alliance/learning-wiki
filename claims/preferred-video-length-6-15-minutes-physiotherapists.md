### Janssen J 2025

Janssen J, Lackenbauer W, Gasselich S, Lickel ME, Schabel L, Beikircher R, Keip C, Wieser M, Mazuquin B, Yeowell G. (2025). Improving physiotherapists’ ability to recognise the presence of a serious pathology with a digital educational training: a mixed methods feasibility study. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Closed-question survey of the 30 completers on preferred length of digital educational material. "the majority of participants (n = 16, 53%) preferred a length of 6–10 min" and nine (30%) preferred 11–15 minutes.

> "The preferred length for digital formats ranged between 3 and 5 and 16–20 min. However, the majority of participants (n = 16, 53%) preferred a length of 6–10 min. With nine participants (30%) indicated that they preferred a length of 11–15 min."
