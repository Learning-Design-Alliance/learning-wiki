`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Narrative synthesis of affective outcomes assessed by one or two studies each, not meta-analyzed due to insufficient data. Emotional intelligence showed significantly higher post-test scores in the IVR group (M = 5.48 vs. M = 4.19; p < 0.001); cognitive load showed numerically higher but nonsignificant levels in the IVR group (p = 0.21).

> "For communication competency, two studies yielded contrasting results: Lee et al. [40] found no significant differences between IVR and conventional education (Hedges’ g = −0.06, 95% CI: −0.54 to 0.42) [40], while Mayor Silva et al. (2023) demonstrated a large significant effect favoring IVR (Hedges’ g = 1.25, 95% CI: 0.82–1.68)"
