## Evidence

### Moura Sofia 2014

Moura Sofia, Fançony Cláudia, Mirante Clara, Neves Marcela, Bernardino Luís, Fortes Filomeno, Sambo Maria do Rosário, Brito Miguel. (2014). Impact of a training course on the quality of malaria diagnosis by microscopy in Angola. Malaria Journal, Volume 13, Article 437. https://doi.org/10.1186/1475-2875-13-437

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Expert-rated quality of 973 slides (475 pre-course, 498 post-course) classified as good, satisfactory or bad. Pooled total P values were 0.003 (thick) and <0.001 (thin) by Pearson chi-square; good thick smears rose from 9.1% to 16.3%.

> "Considering all laboratories there was a significant increase in quality of both smears."

### Moura Sofia 2014 (2)

Moura Sofia, Fançony Cláudia, Mirante Clara, Neves Marcela, Bernardino Luís, Fortes Filomeno, Sambo Maria do Rosário, Brito Miguel. (2014). Impact of a training course on the quality of malaria diagnosis by microscopy in Angola. Malaria Journal, Volume 13, Article 437. https://doi.org/10.1186/1475-2875-13-437

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-laboratory analysis of slide quality. Bengo's thin smear improvement was significant (P = 0.003); Luanda's decrease was non-significant (P = 0.724 thick, 0.073 thin), which the authors attribute to post-course reorganisation of the laboratory.

> "Benguela registered a significant (P < 0.001) increase in quality of both smears. Luanda had a non-significant decrease in the quality of both smears."
