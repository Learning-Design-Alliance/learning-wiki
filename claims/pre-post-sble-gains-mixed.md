# Pre-post evaluations in reviewed SBLE studies report significant gains in EOL knowledge and attitudes, but not uniformly across studies

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Several reviewed pre-post studies reported statistically significant improvements in EOL knowledge, attitudes, or confidence (e.g., Fluharty et al.: improved EOL care knowledge scores, P < .000), while at least one (Bodine and Miller) found improvement in EOL communication for the simulation group nonsignificant. [→ Smith Madison B. RN 2018](#smith-madison-b-rn-2018)
