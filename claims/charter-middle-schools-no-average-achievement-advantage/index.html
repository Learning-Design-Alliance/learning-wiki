Evidence¶

Philip Gleason 2010¶

Philip Gleason, Melissa Clark, Christina Clark Tuttle, Emily Dwoyer. (2010). The Evaluation of Charter School Impacts. Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The report's headline experimental finding compares lottery-based charter middle schools with traditional public schools, stating they are "neither more nor less successful than traditional public schools in improving student achievement". No effect size is printed on this page.

"On average, charter middle schools that hold lotteries are neither more nor less successful than traditional public schools in improving student achievement, behavior, and school progress"
