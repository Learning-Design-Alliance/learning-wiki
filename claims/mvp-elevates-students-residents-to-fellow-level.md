`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Between-group comparisons across training levels in the 62-trainee pre/post study, using t-tests or one-way ANOVA. Fellows' pre-test advantage over "medical students" and "residents" disappeared on the post-test; the article reports no effect size.

> "Fellows had higher pre-test scores overall, p < 0.001, when compared to medical students, p = 0.007 or residents, p = 0.001. This difference was no longer reflected in the post-test scores."
