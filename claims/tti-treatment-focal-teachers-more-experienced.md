Randomized comparison of focal teachers filling treatment and control vacancies (Table ES.2, N=63 treatment, 41 control) reports "13 years versus 8 years" experience and advanced-degree rates of "48 percent versus 21 percent," both statistically significant.

> "The five-year difference in average experience in teaching (13 years versus 8 years) was statistically significant. Treatment focal teachers were also more likely than control focal teachers to have an advanced degree (48 percent versus 21 percent)."
