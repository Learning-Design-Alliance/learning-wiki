Fyfe Emily R., Borriello Giulia, Merrick Megan. (2023). A developmental perspective on feedback: How corrective feedback influences children's literacy, mathematics, and problem solving. Educational Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2022.2108426

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Moderator synthesis across 25 papers examining individual differences: "children with low prior knowledge to benefit more from feedback relative to children with high prior knowledge" in 10 of 14 prior-knowledge papers; some high-knowledge children did better without feedback.
