## Evidence

### Bülbül and Izgar 2018

Ayşe Eliüşük Bülbül, Gö khan Izgar. (2018). Effects of the Patience Training Program on Patience and Well-Being Levels of University Students. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i1.2900

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of post-test Patience Scale scores between the experimental and control groups (15 students each, non-parametric tests used because n<30). All three patience sub-dimensions differed significantly, e.g. "Patience in everyday life (U=168.5, p<.001)".

> "the difference between the Patience scale sub-dimension scores of Patience in everyday life (U=168.5, p<.001), İnterpersonal patience (U=127, p<.001), Patience in life difficulties (U=135, p <.001) is significant for the post -test results of the control and experimental groups."
