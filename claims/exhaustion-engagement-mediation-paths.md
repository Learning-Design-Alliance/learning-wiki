# Exhaustion mediates links of demands and both grit facets to depression and life satisfaction; engagement mediates perseverance of effort to life satisfaction

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Seven of 18 presumed mediated paths were significant, including demands → exhaustion → depression (B = 0.13) and perseverance → engagement → life satisfaction (B = 0.08). [→ Teuber Ziwen 2021](#teuber-ziwen-2021)

## Evidence

### Teuber Ziwen 2021

Teuber Ziwen, Nussbeck Fridtjof W., Wild Elke. (2021). The Bright Side of Grit in Burnout-Prevention: Exploring Grit in the Context of Demands-Resources Model among Chinese High School Students. Child Psychiatry and Human Development, 52(3), 464–476. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-020-01031-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bootstrapped indirect effects (5000 samples) in the cross-sectional SEM of N = 1527 students. No mediated path to academic achievement was significant. The authors stress mediation "could not be strictly tested due to the cross-sectional data" and results cannot be causally interpreted.

> "seven of the presumed 18 mediated paths were statistically significant: Engagement mediated the relationship between perseverant grit and life satisfaction (1); exhaustion mediated the relationships between demands and life satisfaction (2), between demands and depression (3)"
