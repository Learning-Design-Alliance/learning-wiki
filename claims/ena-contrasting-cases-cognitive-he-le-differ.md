Valentina Nachtigall, David Williamson Shaffer, Nikol Rummel. (2022). Stirring a Secret Sauce: A Literature Review on the Conditions and Effects of Authentic Learning. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:1479–1516. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09676-3

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

Epistemic Network Analysis of contrasting cases comparing 26 high-effectiveness with 16 low-effectiveness studies (cognitive outcomes); a t-test on network positions on the x dimension yields "Cohen’s d = 0.78".

> "These differences are supported by a t-test revealing a statistically significant difference between HE and LE studies on the first (x) dimension with a moderate effect (MHE = − 0.23, MLE = 0.38; t = − 2.23, p = 0.04, Cohen’s d = 0.78)."
