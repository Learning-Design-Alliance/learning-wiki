`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the same retrospective cohort (n=77; operative time reported for n=71), Table 2 prints median operative time of 40 min for specialists versus 45 min for trainees, p=0.037, median difference +5 min (+1 to +14). The article reports this as "a median increase of 5 min".
