## Evidence

### Tanya Chichekian 2026

Tanya Chichekian, Maria Cutumisu, Annie Savard and Yi-Mei Zhang. (2026). Profiling Pre-service Teachers' Computational Thinking: The Role of Metacognition and Coding Experience. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1), 31–41. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9077

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis in Table 1 among 128 pre-service teachers. The article reports metacognitive strategies correlating with problem-solving skills at "r = .45, p < .001", which the authors read as underscoring reflective thinking's role in CT.

> "Metacognitive strategies (MetaCog) were strongly correlated with problem-solving skills (CT_PS, r = .45, p < .001), underscoring the role of reflective thinking in CT."

### Tanya Chichekian 2026 (2)

Tanya Chichekian, Maria Cutumisu, Annie Savard and Yi-Mei Zhang. (2026). Profiling Pre-service Teachers' Computational Thinking: The Role of Metacognition and Coding Experience. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1), 31–41. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9077

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of Qualtrics trace data and task scores. The article reports clicks correlating with task performance at "r = –.51, p < .001" and playground time at "r = .30, p < .01"; the efficiency and practice-benefit readings are the authors' interpretations.

> "Behavioural metrics showed that more clicks (L_Clicks) negatively correlated with task performance (L_Score, r = –.51, p < .001), reflecting greater efficiency among higher performers, whereas playground time (PG_Time) positively correlated with L_Score ( r = .30, p < .01), highlighting practice benefits."
