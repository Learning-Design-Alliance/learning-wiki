## Evidence

### Schwarz G 2022

Schwarz G, Bleiner D, Günther D. (2022). On video lectures during remote teaching and beyond. Analytical and Bioanalytical Chemistry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00216-022-03983-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Special survey at the end of the spring 2021 semester asking students about their experiences with synchronous and asynchronous lectures; the article reports the preference percentage descriptively with no test statistic. Students "felt that live lectures offered more incentives and allowed for more interaction."

> "Surveys conducted during and at the end of the spring 2021 semester ultimately revealed that a large majority of 80% of survey participants preferred synchronous lectures."

### Schwarz G 2022 (2)

Schwarz G, Bleiner D, Günther D. (2022). On video lectures during remote teaching and beyond. Analytical and Bioanalytical Chemistry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00216-022-03983-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous learning-management-system survey on preferred lecture format for spring 2021; only 18 students responded, with a ten-to-six majority for synchronous lectures and two expressing no preference. No inferential statistics are reported.

> "Although only 18 students participated, possibly indicating that the others had no particular preference, a majority of ten to six chose to switch to synchronous lectures via videoconferencing; two participating students expressed no preference."
