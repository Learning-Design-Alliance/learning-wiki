## Evidence

### Wang Gang 2026

Wang Gang, Liu Yingying, Wang Shengyun, Gao Silong, Lin Hui, Wu Qian, Huang Debin, Pan Yueshuai, Zhao Yuxiao, Wei Huimin, Wei Lili. (2026). Factors Influencing ICU Nurses’ Proficiency in Point‐of‐Care Gastrointestinal Ultrasound: A National Cross‐Sectional Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/3178958

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression in the 2009-nurse cross-sectional survey. Billability was an organizational predictor, with "OR for nonbillable vs. billable = 0.456" (OR = 0.456); 84.4% of units could not bill for GI-POCUS.

> "Nurses working in departments where GI‐POCUS examinations were billable were more likely to report “good” proficiency than those in nonbillable settings (OR for nonbillable vs. billable = 0.456, 95% CI: 0.296–0.702, p < 0.001)"
