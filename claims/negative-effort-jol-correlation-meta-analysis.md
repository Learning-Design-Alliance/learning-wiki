# Effort ratings correlate negatively with judgments of learning (r = −0.355), but the correlation disappears under a goal-driven interpretation of effort

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Across studies, learners' effort ratings correlate negatively with judgments of their learning during a learning task (r = −0.355), consistent with a data-driven easily-learned-easily-remembered heuristic. [→ Anique B. H. de Bruin 2023](#anique-b-h-de-bruin-2023)
`q2 i?` In the subset of studies where learners adopted a goal-driven interpretation of effort, no negative correlation between effort and learning was observed; this approach is less common than the data-driven approach. [→ Anique B. H. de Bruin 2023 (2)](#anique-b-h-de-bruin-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Anique B. H. de Bruin 2023

Anique B. H. de Bruin, Felicitas Biwer, Luotong Hui, Erdem Onan, Louise David, Wisnu Wiradhany. (2023). Worth the Effort: the Start and Stick to Desirable Difficulties (S2D2) Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09766-w

`q3 · i2` · `review · r2`

Meta-analytic synthesis reported in the review: the correlation was "weaker for prospective than for retrospective judgments, and stronger for problem-solving than for paired associates tasks."

> "a recent meta-analysis (Baars et al., 2020) revealed that learners' effort ratings correlate negatively with judgments of their learning when engaging in a learning task (r =  − 0.355)."

### Anique B. H. de Bruin 2023 (2)

Anique B. H. de Bruin, Felicitas Biwer, Luotong Hui, Erdem Onan, Louise David, Wisnu Wiradhany. (2023). Worth the Effort: the Start and Stick to Desirable Difficulties (S2D2) Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09766-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports this moderator from the same meta-analysis: under goal-driven interpretation, higher effort leads to higher perceived learning and more use of desirable difficulties; the article notes this approach is less common among learners.

> "the subset of studies where learners adopted a goal-driven interpretation of effort showed no negative correlation between effort and learning."
