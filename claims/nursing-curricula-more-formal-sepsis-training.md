### Roye-Green Karen J. 2025

Roye-Green Karen J., Vickers Ivan, Priestley Sharon, Walker Jerome, Willis Rohan. (2025). Knowledge, practice and attitudes of healthcare students to sepsis management in Jamaica. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07122-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Sepsis-training analysis within the cross-sectional survey of 292 students. "A majority of nursing students (55/82, 67.0%) had received sepsis training compared to only 41/210 (19.5%) medical students (p < 0.001)"; medical students' trained proportion rose from 0% in Y1 to 28.6% by Y5.
