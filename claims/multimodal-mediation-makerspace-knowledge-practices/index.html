Evidence¶

Anu Kajamaa & Kristiina Kumpulainen 2020¶

Anu Kajamaa & Kristiina Kumpulainen. (2020). Students' multimodal knowledge practices in a makerspace learning environment. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning (2020) 15:411–444. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-020-09337-z

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative video-based analysis of 350 min of transcribed video from two student groups working on FUSE STEAM challenges, using sociocultural discourse analysis complemented with multimodal interaction analysis. Findings show "the physical space with its arrangements mediated the students' knowledge practices" alongside talk, materials and embodied action. No quantitative effect sizes are reported.

"Our findings show how discourse, digital and other "hands on " materials, embodied actions, such as gestures and postures, and the physical space with its arrangements mediated the students' knowledge practices."
