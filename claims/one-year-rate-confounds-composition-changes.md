## Evidence

### Elaine Allensworth and John Q. Easton 2001

Elaine Allensworth and John Q. Easton. (2001). Calculating a Cohort Dropout Rate for the Chicago Public Schools: A Technical Research Report. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/calculating-cohort-dropout-rate-chicago-public-schools

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Methodological argument comparing the CPS one-year rate (15–17 percent of high school students per year, Table 3) with cohort rates. The one-year rate's advantage of being up to date is outweighed, the authors argue, by these confounds; no statistical test is involved.

> "These trends are mixed together in the one-year dropout rate because it does not distinguish between new and continuing students."
