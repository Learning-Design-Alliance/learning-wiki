`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Sample-size and power section of the protocol, using expected 20 students per group and CITO distribution data for reading comprehension, arithmetic and orthography. The article states "at maximum a difference between the mean scores of both groups of around 10% will be accepted"; if the experimental mean is more than 10% lower, non-inferiority will not be accepted.

> "This means that for accepting non-inferiority in case μ = 0 or μ > 0, at maximum a difference between the mean scores of both groups of around 10% will be accepted."
