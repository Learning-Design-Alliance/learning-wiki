# Teacher–student interpersonal neural synchronization in the left prefrontal cortex is higher under autonomy-supportive teaching than under a controlling style, with significant task-related INS only in the autonomy-support group

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Task-related INS in the lPFC was significantly higher in the teacher autonomy support group than in the teacher control group (d = 0.86, FDR corrected). [→ Wang Guoxia 2025](#wang-guoxia-2025)
`q3 i3` Within the autonomy-support group, task-related lPFC INS exceeded zero (d = 1.58), while the control group showed no significant task-related INS. [→ Wang Guoxia 2025 (2)](#wang-guoxia-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Wang Guoxia 2025

Wang Guoxia, Zhang Qin, Wang Lumeng, Liu Yi. (2025). The impact of teachers’ autonomy support on students’ academic performance from the perspective of hyperscanning: the mediating role of autonomy need satisfaction. Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsaf115

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

fNIRS hyperscanning during simulated teaching; wavelet-coherence INS in the 0.02–0.04 Hz band, Fisher z-transformed, compared between groups. The article reports lPFC INS "significantly higher than the teacher control group, t (40) = 2.78, P = .0082, d = 0.86, FDR corrected"; no rTPJ group difference is reported.

> "Second, in the lPFC, task-related INS in the teacher autonomy support group was significantly higher than the teacher control group, t (40) = 2.78, P = .0082, d = 0.86, FDR corrected."

### Wang Guoxia 2025 (2)

Wang Guoxia, Zhang Qin, Wang Lumeng, Liu Yi. (2025). The impact of teachers’ autonomy support on students’ academic performance from the perspective of hyperscanning: the mediating role of autonomy need satisfaction. Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsaf115

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-sample t-tests against zero on task-related INS in the lPFC for each group separately. In the autonomy-support group "task-related INS was found in the lPFC, t (19) = 3.44, P = .0027, d = 1.58"; the control group showed no significant task-related INS.

> "First, one-sample t-tests showed that, in the teacher autonomy support group, task-related INS was found in the lPFC, t (19) = 3.44, P = .0027, d = 1.58, FDR corrected. In the teacher control group, there was no significant task-related INS."
