Evidence¶

McNally et al. 2016¶

Sandra McNally, Jenifer Ruiz-Valenzuela and Heather Rolfe. (2016). ABRA: Online Reading Support — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN18254678

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Finding from the qualitative process evaluation of six intervention schools. In practice TAs grouped pupils into groups of three to four guided by ability, behaviour, SEN status, and personality. The report hedges with "may be best delivered".

"The process evaluation found that both the ICT and non-ICT interventions may be best delivered in groups of similar rather than mixed ability."
