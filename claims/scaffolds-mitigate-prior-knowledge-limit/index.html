General additive model results (Table 4) for the control condition smooth term of prior knowledge on posttest irrelevant-evidence reasoning, edf = 1.93, F = 4.39, p < 0.001; the corresponding experimental-condition row reads "Prior knowledge (experimental condition) 1.1.0 0.4 0.054", indicating no significant prior-knowledge effect under scaffolding.

Hanna Grimm · Peter A. Edelsbrunner · Kornelia Möller 2023 (2)¶

Hanna Grimm · Peter A. Edelsbrunner · Kornelia Möller. (2023). Accommodating heterogeneity: the interaction of instructional scaffolding with student preconditions in the learning of hypothesis-based reasoning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09601-9

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Latent transition analysis of the 70 control-condition students: students in the low profile mostly moved to intermediate (13%, n = 9) or stayed (9%, n = 6), and higher-profile students were static — consistent with prior knowledge limiting gains without the additional scaffold.

"Finally, all students (46%; n = 32) who already showed the intermediate profile or the full profile before the instruction stayed in their initial profiles."
