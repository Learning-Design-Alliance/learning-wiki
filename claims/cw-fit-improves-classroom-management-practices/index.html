Evidence¶

Debra Kamps 2015¶

Debra Kamps, Howard Wills, Harriett Dawson-Bannister, Linda Heitzman-Powell, Esther Kottwitz, Blake Hansen, and Kandace Fleming (2015). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams "CW-FIT" Efficacy Trial Outcomes. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300714565244

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Eight-item classroom management checklist (modified from the Classroom Atmosphere Rating Scale) completed during each observation. CW-FIT "improved 84%" of effective management practices with little change in comparison classes.

"Both groups averaged use of about 50% (of the total possible points) of good management practices in place during baseline. The comparison group remained at similar levels in Baseline 2 (52%), whereas the CW-FIT implementation improved 84%."
