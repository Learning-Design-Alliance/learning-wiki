## Evidence

### Suacamram 2019

Suacamram, M. (2019). Using the C-K theory to develop student's creativity: A Case Study of Creative University. International Journal of Instruction, 12(4), 719-732. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12446a

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A pretest-posttest control group design compared 14 students taught with C-K theory lesson plans against 11 students taught with regular lesson plans. Repeated measures analysis showed "significant difference in their creativity development (F = 15.462 P = 0.001)".

> "The student groups demonstrated significant difference in their creativity development (F = 15.462 P = 0.001) To conclude, the students who were taught the C-K theory were able to achieve higher scores in all of the five assignments than those whose weren't taught the C-K theory."
