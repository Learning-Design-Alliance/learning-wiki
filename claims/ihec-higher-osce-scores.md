## Evidence

### Xie Dili 2026

Xie Dili, Sun Xuechun, Guo Jianshu, Liu Peng, Yang Junling, Zhong Ping. (2026). Integrating iHEC into standardized residency training for chronic disease management: a structural equation analysis of competency outcomes via digital health platforms. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1926948

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA comparison of post-rotation OSCE scores between the conventional (n = 118) and iHEC (n = 127) cohorts in a retrospective sequential cohort study, adjusted for baseline score, training year, specialty, and calendar time, showing an "Adjusted mean difference, 6.85 points" with d = 0.82.

> "OSCE score: Adjusted mean difference, 6.85 points (95% CI, 5.12–8.58; p < 0.001; Cohen’s d = 0.82), indicating a large effect size."
