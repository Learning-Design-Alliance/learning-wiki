## Evidence

### Corley E 2023

Corley E, Fahey L, Fitzgerald J, Holleran L, Walton E, Morris DW, Donohoe G. (2023). The impact of early adversity and education on genetic and brain morphological predictors of cognitive ability. Genes, Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1111/gbb.12850

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modelling in a UK Biobank sample (N = 5237; age 38–72) with a latent cognitive ability factor built from four tests. The article reports the model "explaining ~15% of variation in cognitive ability", with the largest path for total GMV (β = 0.327, p < 0.001) followed by IQ-PGS (β = 0.181, p < 0.001).

> "Common genetic variation, grey matter volume and early life adversity were each significant predictors in the model, explaining ~15% of variation in cognitive ability."
