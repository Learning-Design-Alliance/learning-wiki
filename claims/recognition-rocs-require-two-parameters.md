## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Empirical recognition ROCs are curvilinear and their shapes change across conditions, requiring no fewer than two independent memory parameters. [→ Yonelinas 2002](#yonelinas-2002)
`q2 i?` Item recognition ROCs are curvilinear whereas associative recognition ROCs are often much more linear, indicating different processes contribute to the two test types. [→ Yonelinas 2002](#yonelinas-2002)

## Evidence

### Yonelinas 2002

Andrew P. Yonelinas. (2002). The Nature of Recollection and Familiarity: A Review of 30 Years of Research. Journal of Memory and Language. https://doi.org/10.1006/jmla.2002.2864

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of confidence-response ROC studies reports that empirical ROCs "require no less than two functionally independent memory parameters", and that associative ROCs are often more linear than item ROCs. No effect sizes are printed.

> "the observed empirical functions are curvilinear and their shapes change across conditions such that they require no less than two functionally independent memory parameters to describe them"
