## Evidence

### Markus Huff 2025

Markus Huff, Natalia Gagarina, Ekaterina Varkentin, Irina R. Brich. (2025). Education, Not Age, Linked to Narrative Comprehension. https://aspredicted.org/WT9_TK6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the preregistered representative-sample experiment (N = 1487), age was treated as a centered continuous predictor; the age effect was not significant: "narrative comprehension did not decline with increasing age, χ2(1) = 2.14, p = .143." No effect size was printed.

> "Not confirming Hypothesis 2, narrative comprehension did not decline with increasing age, χ2(1) = 2.14, p = .143."

### Markus Huff 2025 (2)

Markus Huff, Natalia Gagarina, Ekaterina Varkentin, Irina R. Brich. (2025). Education, Not Age, Linked to Narrative Comprehension. https://aspredicted.org/WT9_TK6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Bayesian model comparison in the same experiment provided "at least substantial evidence for the codality-only model and absence of age effects" (mean BF = 129.81), explicitly supporting the null hypothesis of no age effect.

> "The mean BF of this codality-only model over the main effects model was 129.81 (range = 7.36-3.70 x 105), thus, providing at least substantial evidence for the codality-only model and absence of age effects"
