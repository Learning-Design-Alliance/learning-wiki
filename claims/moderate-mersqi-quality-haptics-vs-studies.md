`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's systematic quality assessment of all 51 included studies using the MERSQI instrument. The review reports "The mean MERSQI score across all papers was 12.82 (1.59)" and identifies sample size and instrument validity as the lowest-scored categories.

> "The mean MERSQI score across all papers was 12.82 (1.59). This score represents a moderate rating across studies. 32 The lowest scored categories were related to sample size and validity of the evaluation instruments utilized."

### Sayed Azher 2024 (2)

Sayed Azher, Aralia Mills, Jinzhi He, Taliah Hyjazie, Junko Tokuno, Andrea Quaiattini, and Jason M. Harley. (2024). Findings Favor Haptics Feedback in Virtual Simulation Surgical Education: An Updated Systematic and Scoping Review. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/15533506241238263

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's critical appraisal of RCTs and quasi-experimental studies using the CASP and JBI checklists. The review reports that "many studies fell short in achieving blinding of participants and researchers".

> "the CASP RCT Standard Checklist and the JBI Critical Appraisal Checklist for Quasi-Experimental Studies observed that many studies fell short in achieving blinding of participants and researchers, a practical challenge often encountered in the context of medical training."
