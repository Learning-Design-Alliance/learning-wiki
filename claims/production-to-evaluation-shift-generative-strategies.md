Conceptual analysis of the eight generative learning strategies under AI mediation. The article argues the shift is not inherently negative but that "production generally yields stronger learning than evaluation or comprehension" per the generation effect literature, so evaluation must be designed to demand production-like processing.

> "Across multiple strategies (summarizing, mapping, self-explaining), AI reconfigures the learner's role from producer of meaning to evaluator of AI-generated meaning. This shift is not inherently negative."
