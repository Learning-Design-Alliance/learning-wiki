## Evidence

### Peng 2020

Peng Peng, Kievit Rogier A. (2020). The Development of Academic Achievement and Cognitive Abilities: A Bidirectional Perspective. Child Development Perspectives. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdep.12352

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

A meta-analysis of 680 studies spanning ages 3–80 found that reasoning–reading/mathematics correlations increased with age after controlling for confounds; the article notes "correlations increased by about .03 for every decade" — a small effect with no standardized effect size printed.

> "In a meta-analysis of 680 studies on the relations between nonverbal reasoning and reading/mathematics across a wide range of age groups (3–80 years old; Peng, Wang, Wang, & Lin, 2019), these relations increased with age, even after controlling for confounding variables"
