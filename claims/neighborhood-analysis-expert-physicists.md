## Evidence

### Charlotte Zimmerman 2019

Charlotte Zimmerman, Alexis Olsho, Michael Loverude, Suzanne White Brahmia. (2019). Identifying Covariational Reasoning Behaviors in Expert Physicists in Graphing Tasks. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1911.02044](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1911.02044)

`q2 · i?`

Qualitative analysis of the two interview rounds. The authors report that "when unable to relate a compiled relationship to the quantities in question, expert physicists often turned to neighborhood analysis," citing a Ferris Wheel participant who used velocity vectors and a Drone participant who compared two representative neighborhoods.

> "Neighborhood analysis refers to when participants draw their graphs by identifying physically significant points, considering the slope of the graph around those points (in the points’ “neighborhood”), and then connecting those slopes to form a smooth curve."
