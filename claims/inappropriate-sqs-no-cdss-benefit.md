## Evidence

### Yanagita Yasutaka 2025

Yanagita Yasutaka, Shikino Kiyoshi, Yokokawa Daiki, Ishizuka Kosuke, Tsukamoto Tomoko, Li Yu, Uehara Takanori, Ikusaka Masatomi. (2025). Appropriate semantic qualifiers increase diagnostic accuracy when using a clinical decision support system: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07294-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome of the randomized controlled trial on the 172 inappropriate-SQ diagnoses: 48 correct (55.2%) in the CDSS group versus 45 correct (52.9%) in the control group; the article reports "no significant difference". Equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "there was no significant difference in diagnostic accuracy between the CDSS and control groups (χ2(1) = 8.62 × 10–2, p = 0.769)"
