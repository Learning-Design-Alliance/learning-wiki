## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2013

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Benjamin D. Geller, Julia Gouvea, Vashti Sawtelle, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2013). Ontological metaphors for negative energy in an interdisciplinary context. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020108)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Class statement and follow-up interview with one student (pseudonym Anita) reflecting on a bond-energy discussion. She reported using the potential energy graph and gravitational hill intuitions, and the article states "Switching to a blended substance/location ontology has helped her develop a more correct understanding of chemical bond energy."

> "According to Anita’s self-report, her initial exclusive use of the substance metaphor led her to claim incorrectly that energy is released when bonds are broken."
