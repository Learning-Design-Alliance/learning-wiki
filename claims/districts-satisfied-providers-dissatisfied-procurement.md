## Evidence

### Morrison 2014

Morrison, J. R., Ross, S. M., Corcoran, R. P., & Reid, A. J. (2014). Fostering Market Efficiency in K-12 Ed-tech Procurement. Center for Research and Reform in Education, Johns Hopkins University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563768

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey and interview data from 54 districts in 31 states and 47 ed-tech providers. The report states "the majority views it as working sufficiently in most respects" for districts, while providers "are extremely dissatisfied, with close to three-fourths conveying negative views."

> "Results indicate that while few district stakeholders are satisfied with the efficiency and success of the procurement process, the majority views it as working sufficiently in most respects for acquiring the selected ed-tech products."
