## Evidence

### Geoffrion R 2024

Geoffrion R, Koenig NA, Cundiff GW, Flood C, Hyakutake MT, Schulz J, Brennand EA, Lee T, Singer J, Todd NJ. (2024). Procedure-specific simulation for vaginal surgery training: A randomized controlled trial. Acta Obstetricia et Gynecologica Scandinavica. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14810

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted regression analysis in the same randomized trial, reported per index procedure. The VH contrast favored intervention ("12.0% mean difference, 95% CI: 1.8–22.3, p = 0.023"), while the AR and PR contrasts did not reach significance. The authors attribute the VH advantage to its fairly standard operative technique.

> "GRS scores were statistically significant after adjusting for potential confounders, overall (8.2% mean difference, 95% CI: 0.2–16.1, p = 0.044) and for VH (12.0% mean difference, 95% CI: 1.8–22.3, p = 0.023) but not for AR or PR"
