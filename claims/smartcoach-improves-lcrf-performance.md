`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons of LCRF from LC1 to LC5 in the multicenter RCT (nine surgeons per arm). The article reports "significant improvement" in the coaching group and "no significant difference" in the self-learning group; only medians and P-values are printed.

> "In the coaching group, participating surgeons showed a significant improvement in their last LC surgery compared to their first (median, 31 vs 40, P=0.008). However, no significant difference was observed in the self-learning group (median, 34 vs 38, P=0.138)."
