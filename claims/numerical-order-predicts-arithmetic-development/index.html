Review synthesis of behavioural research on numerical order processing, reported as part of an enumerated list of findings including explaining unique variance in children's arithmetic abilities and mediating the association between quantity processing and arithmetic; no effect sizes are printed.

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt 2021 (2)¶

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt. (2021). Developmental brain dynamics of numerical and arithmetic abilities. npj Science of Learning (2021) 6:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00099-3

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review notes only a handful of neuroimaging studies have directly explored numerical order processing in children; it reports "an age-dependent increase in brain activation in the left IPS" in response to order processing, plus associations with the semantic control network; no effect sizes are printed.

"The results of these studies have demonstrated an age-dependent increase in brain activation in the left IPS in response to numerical order processing"
