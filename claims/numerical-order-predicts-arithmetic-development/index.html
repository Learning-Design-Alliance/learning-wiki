The review notes only a handful of neuroimaging studies have directly explored numerical order processing in children; it reports "an age-dependent increase in brain activation in the left IPS" in response to order processing, plus associations with the semantic control network; no effect sizes are printed.

Related Claims¶

  • Both number system knowledge and estimation uniquely account for variance in all three third-grade arithmetic skills independently of each other and of domain-general factors
  • Preschool counting skills and number knowledge directly predict formal arithmetic skills in primary school, together accounting for 40–46% of variance
  • Symbolic numerical magnitude processing is a powerful unique predictor of arithmetic fact retrieval
  • Both numerical magnitude processing tasks correlate positively with arithmetic fact retrieval, more strongly for symbolic
  • Numerical magnitude comparison explains significant unique variance in multiplication fact performance over and above phonological processing, intellectual ability, and general reaction time
