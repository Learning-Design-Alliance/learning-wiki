## Evidence

### Öcal Mustafa 2025

Öcal Mustafa. (2025). The role of video and direct laryngoscopy in medical student intubation training: a comparative study on success rates and learning curves. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07239-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mannequin-based study of 130 medical students with no prior intubation training, randomized to starting scenario. Success rates rose across attempts for both methods: DL reached "104 students (80%) on the third attempt" while VL reached "116 students (89.23%) on the third attempt".

> "48 students (36.92%) achieved successful intubation on the first attempt using DL, 88 students (67.69%) on the second attempt, and 104 students (80%) on the third attempt. In contrast, with VL, 82 students (63.07%) were successful on the first attempt, 111 students (85.38%) on the second attempt, and 116 students (89.23%) on the third attempt."
