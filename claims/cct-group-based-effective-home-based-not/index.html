Evidence¶

Lampit A 2014¶

Lampit A, Hallock H, Valenzuela M (2014) Computerized Cognitive Training in Cognitively Healthy Older Adults: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Effect Modifiers. PLoS Med 11(11): e1001756. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1001756

q4 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Mixed-effects subgroup meta-analysis of delivery format across all cognitive outcomes. Group-based CCT was effective (g = 0.29) while home-based was not (g = 0.09); the between-group Q test was significant (p = 0.007), with low within-subgroup heterogeneity for group-based studies.

"there was a significant difference in the efficacy of group-based training (g = 0.29, 95% CI 0.21 to 0.38, p,0.001) compared to home-based administration (g = 0.09, 95% CI 20.02 to 0.21, p = 0.11; Q statistic for between-group heterogeneity = 7.183, df = 1, p = 0.007)."
