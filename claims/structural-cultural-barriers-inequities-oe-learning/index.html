Stelling H 2026¶

Stelling H, Burford B, Park S, McGurk L, Brown M E L, Vance G. (2026). How do doctors build knowledge around obstetric emergencies? A scoping review. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2026-119725

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review authors' synthesis in the Discussion: "Inequalities permeated all domains, driving inequities in outcomes". Limited access to care, transport, facilities and continuing education with poor staffing and resourcing created cycles where the most disadvantaged communities received the poorest care.

"Barriers to learning were both structural, such as access to training, knowledge and resources, and cultural, including blame, punitive attitudes and entrenched hierarchies. Inequalities permeated all domains, driving inequities in outcomes."
