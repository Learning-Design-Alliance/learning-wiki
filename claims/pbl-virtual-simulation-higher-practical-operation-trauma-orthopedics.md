## Evidence

### Xue Gang 2026

Xue Gang, Zhang Xin, Ji Shengyi, Qi Jin. (2026). Integrating problem-based learning with virtual simulation in trauma orthopedic education: effects on learning outcomes and student satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09558-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same assessor-blinded RCT of 50 clinical medical students. Clinical skills were assessed on case analysis, radiographic interpretation, and surgical planning, scored independently by two blinded instructors. The experimental group's post-teaching practical operation score was significantly higher, with a "mean difference = 9.76" and "p < 0.01". The article reports Cohen's d = 2.97 for this outcome in the Results section.
