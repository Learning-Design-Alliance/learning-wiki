## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Participants' willingness to attempt US-guided cannulation independently before calling a senior for help increased from 7.5 to 10 (P < 0.001, effect size r = 0.75). [→ Lin Xu-Nian 2026](#lin-xu-nian-2026)

## Evidence

### Lin Xu-Nian 2026

Lin Xu-Nian, Lu Cheng-Wei, Lin Tzu-Yu, Chou San-Fang, Chang Ya-Ying. (2026). Rapid skill acquisition through task-specific ultrasound training for peripheral cannulation in medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09935-9

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test on paired pre/post ratings from 30 participants. Willingness increased from a median of 7.5 to 10, with effect size r = 0.75 and P < 0.001.

> "To what extent do you agree with this statement: "I would consider attempting to use US to IV cannulation before calling a senior for help in the future"? 7.5 (2) 10 (1) 1.5 (1.0-2.5) 4.08 0.75 < 0.001*"
