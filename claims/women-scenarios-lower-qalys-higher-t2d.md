Miners Alec, Harris Jody, Felix Lambert, Murray Elizabeth, Michie Susan, Edwards Phil. (2012). An economic evaluation of adaptive e-learning devices to promote weight loss via dietary change for people with obesity. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-12-190

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Base case results across patient starting characteristics in the e-LEEM discrete event simulation (Table 3): scenario D (female) yielded 11.703 QALYs on CC versus 12.527 in scenario A (male), with ICERs of £125,891 and £102,112 respectively.

> "Scenarios containing women were associated with lower QALYs compared with men (e.g. scenario D compared with A) because lower rates and time spent with CVD were more than offset by higher rates and time spent with T2D."
