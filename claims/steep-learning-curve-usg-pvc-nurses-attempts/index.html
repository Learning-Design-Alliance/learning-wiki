Evidence¶

Jørgensen Rasmus 2021¶

Jørgensen Rasmus, Laursen Christian B., Konge Lars, Pietersen Pia Iben. (2021). Education in the placement of ultrasound-guided peripheral venous catheters: a systematic review. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-021-00897-z

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of learning-curve studies in nurses; the review reports "after four to nine real world attempts a nurse's probability of success was over 70% on average". Descriptive percentages only.

"Two studies found a relatively steep learning curve after initial training, and that after four to nine real world attempts a nurse's probability of success was over 70% on average [41, 42]."

Jørgensen Rasmus 2021 (2)¶

Jørgensen Rasmus, Laursen Christian B., Konge Lars, Pietersen Pia Iben. (2021). Education in the placement of ultrasound-guided peripheral venous catheters: a systematic review. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-021-00897-z

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports pediatric learning-curve results: "a success rate of 67% after ten supervised attempts" rising to 83% after ten further unsupervised attempts. Ault et al. found the learning curve flattened after ten successful attempts.

"Likewise, results were seen in paediatric patients with a success rate of 67% after ten supervised attempts, and an increase after ten additional unsupervised attempts to 83% [27]."
