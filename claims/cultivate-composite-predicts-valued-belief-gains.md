# Within-year improvements in overall learning conditions predict large gains in students' I am Valued beliefs

## Subclaims

Predictive validity analysis of fall-to-spring Cultivate responses; models controlled for fall learning condition and belief values, student-level covariates (e.g., race/ethnicity, gender, EL status), school-level covariates, and a school random effect, with all results significant at p<.001. Table 2 shows the composite predicts 19.4 (middle grades) and 20.5 (high school) percentile point increases in I am Valued.
