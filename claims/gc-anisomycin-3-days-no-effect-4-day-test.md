# Anisomycin infusion into the GC 3 days after CTA acquisition does not impair memory when tested 4 days later, under both standard and weak protocols

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After standard CTA, anisomycin infused 3 days post-acquisition produced no significant difference in aversion when tested 4 days later; extinction was intact in both groups. [→ Levitan 2016](#levitan-2016)
`q3 i?` After weak CTA (0.05 mol/l LiCl), the same 3-day anisomycin infusion likewise showed no significant difference from vehicle at the 4-day test. [→ Levitan 2016 (2)](#levitan-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Levitan 2016

Levitan, D., Gal-Ben-Ari, S., Heise, C., Rosenberg, T., Elkobi, A., Inberg, S., Sala, C. & Rosenblum, K. (2016). The differential role of cortical protein synthesis in taste memory formation and persistence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Standard-protocol experiment: anisomycin (n = 9) or vehicle (n = 11) was infused into the GC 3 days after CTA acquisition and memory was tested 4 days later, with "no signi ﬁcant difference between groups" (Mann–Whitney Z = − 0.113; P = 0.94). No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "When tested 4 days later, both groups showed high aversion toward saccharin with no signi ﬁcant difference between groups ( n =9 –11, Mann –Whitney: Z = − 0.113; P = 0.94)."

### Levitan 2016 (2)

Levitan, D., Gal-Ben-Ari, S., Heise, C., Rosenberg, T., Elkobi, A., Inberg, S., Sala, C. & Rosenblum, K. (2016). The differential role of cortical protein synthesis in taste memory formation and persistence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Weak-protocol replication: anisomycin or vehicle was infused 3 days after weak CTA (0.05 mol/l LiCl) and tested 4 days later, showing "no signi ﬁcant difference compared with animals receiving vehicle infusion" (t20 = − 0.585; P = 0.56). No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "When tested 4 days later, animals receiving anisomycin showed no signiﬁcant difference compared with animals receiving vehicle infusion ( n =9 –11; t20 = − 0.585; P = 0.56, t test)."
