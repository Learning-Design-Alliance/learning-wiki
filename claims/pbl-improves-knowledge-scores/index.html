Evidence¶

Luke Alexander Maniangat 2021¶

Luke Alexander Maniangat, Mathew Simy, Kuriadom Sam Thomas, George Jeny Mary, Karobari Mohmed Isaqali, Marya Anand, Pawar Ajinkya Mansing. (2021). Effectiveness of Problem-Based Learning versus Traditional Teaching Methods in Improving Acquisition of Radiographic Interpretation Skills among Dental Students—A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/9630285

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Random-effects meta-analysis of four studies (116 PBL and 117 TT students) assessing knowledge scores. The forest plot "demonstrates a significant difference in mean knowledge score between the PBL group and the TT group in all included studies".

"The implant mean difference in the knowledge score for 116 and 117 students in the PBL and TT groups, respectively, in the included four studies [20, 22, 24, 25] was 4.15 with a minimum and maximum of -0.35 and 8.65, respectively."
