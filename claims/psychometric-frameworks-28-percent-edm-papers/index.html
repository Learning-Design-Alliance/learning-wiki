Ryan S.J.D. Baker and Kalina Yacef 2009¶

Ryan S.J.D. Baker and Kalina Yacef. (2009). The State of Educational Data Mining in 2009: A Review and Future Visions. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Article 1, Vol 1, No 1, Fall 2009. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/8

q2 · i? · review · r2

Trend analysis in Section 5.2 of framework prominence across EDM's history. The review reports these methods "were found in 28% of the papers in EDM2008 and EDM2009" and attributes the increase to integration of psychometrics and student modeling researchers.

"Another key trend is the increase in prominence of modeling frameworks from Item Response Theory, Bayes Nets, and Markov Decision Processes. These methods were rare at the very beginning of educational data mining, began to become more prominent around 2005"
