Discussion interpretation of the interview data: the authors contrast interns' perceptions with cited evidence that lymphoedema is common across healthcare settings, often co-morbid with other chronic conditions, and a risk factor for serious and costly complications.

> "Many interns in this study justified the lack of lymphoedema training by the perceived low prevalence and ‘seriousness’ of lymphoedema. However, these attitudes contrast with evidence that lymphoedema is common across a variety of healthcare settings"
