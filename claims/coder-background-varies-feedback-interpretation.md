## Evidence

### Seiyon M. Lee 2025

Seiyon M. Lee, Sami Baral, Hongming Chip Li, Li Cheng, Shan Zhang, Carly S. Thorp, Jennifer St. John, Tamisha Thompson, Neil Heffernan, Anthony F. Botelho. (2025). Developing Feedback Taxonomy for Math: A Synergy of Perspectives through Data Mining Methods. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 17, No 2. https://osf.io/puhyr/?view_only=514d8534e9be41d9a57ad82e9efb3249

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Fleiss' kappa computed over six coders (three researchers, three mathematics teachers with over 20 years of experience) coding 100 feedback messages. The article reports weak combined agreement "below 0.30" for the named codes and strong agreement for Emoji and Positive Affect; no effect size is printed.

> "while the research team generally showed higher agreement than the teacher experts, we found weak agreement with combined kappa values below 0.30 for certain codes, such as "Specific," "Learning Strategy Suggestion," and "Hints.""

### Seiyon M. Lee 2025 (2)

Seiyon M. Lee, Sami Baral, Hongming Chip Li, Li Cheng, Shan Zhang, Carly S. Thorp, Jennifer St. John, Tamisha Thompson, Neil Heffernan, Anthony F. Botelho. (2025). Developing Feedback Taxonomy for Math: A Synergy of Perspectives through Data Mining Methods. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 17, No 2. https://osf.io/puhyr/?view_only=514d8534e9be41d9a57ad82e9efb3249

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Spearman's rank correlation analysis of coding by researchers versus teachers on the 100-message sample. The article reports group-specific correlation patterns, e.g. researchers linking Specific with Correction and Explanation, teachers linking Mathematical Suggestions with Specific and Explanation; no effect size printed.

> "Overall, the findings suggest that each group's interpretative lens is shaped by their professional backgrounds and experiences. For researchers, the emphasis was on the diagnostic function of feedback, while for teachers, it centered on pedagogical design underlying the feedback."
