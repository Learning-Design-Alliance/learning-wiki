Evidence¶

J. Patrick Meyer 2023¶

J. Patrick Meyer, Ann Hu, Sylvia Li. (2023). Content Proximity Spring 2022 Pilot Study Research Brief. Psychometric Solutions, NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/content-proximity-spring-2022-pilot-study-research-report/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of extant pilot test events against blueprints showed in K-2 math "over 95% of test events achieved the target number items per instructional area"; in math, 2-5 and 6+ bands had 90% or more of test events within 2 items of target. Reading tests in the 2-5 and 6+ bands varied more: 65% to 80% of test events were within two items of target per instructional area (81% to 93% within three).

"In the K-2 band for math, over 95% of test events achieved the target number items per instructional area and 100% were within 1 item of the target number."

J. Patrick Meyer 2023 (2)¶

J. Patrick Meyer, Ann Hu, Sylvia Li. (2023). Content Proximity Spring 2022 Pilot Study Research Brief. Psychometric Solutions, NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/content-proximity-spring-2022-pilot-study-research-report/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Reading passage counts per test event showed "90% to 97% of pilot group test events on the 2-5 test had two reading passages" versus 71-91% in the comparison group; for 6+ tests, 95-97% of pilot events had three passages.

"A total of 90% to 97% of pilot group test events on the 2-5 test had two reading passages. Only 6% of events had no reading passages. In the comparison group, the percentages of test events with two reading passages were lower and ranged from 71 to 91."
