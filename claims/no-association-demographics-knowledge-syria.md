### Ataya J 2026

Ataya J, Ismail A, Kousa A, Shahoud Z, Alhalbi R, Ghanem R, Ranjous Y, Zaitouna M. (2026). Assessing theoretical knowledge related to surgical and clinical skills in Syrian medical students: A cross-sectional study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000051241

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests across 500 participants found no significant association for any of these four sociodemographic variables with knowledge level. Equivalence was not tested.

> "No significant association was observed for academic year (Monte Carlo P = .188), marital status (Monte Carlo P = .093), working status (P = .436), or financial status (Monte Carlo P = .280)."
