Second randomized evaluation: 61 New York City public elementary schools and 2,242 parents responding to the NYC School Survey during the intervention period, with "significant positive effects on all three measures (effect size = +0.17 for each measure)".

"Family engagement was assessed using measures of Inclusive Leadership, Trust in School Leader, and Communication and Trust in Teacher, with significant positive effects on all three measures (effect size = +0.17 for each measure)."
