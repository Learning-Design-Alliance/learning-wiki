Evidence¶

Finkelstein 2011¶

Finkelstein, N., Hanson, T., Huang, C.-W., Hirschman, B., and Huang, M. (2011). Effects of Problem Based Economics on high school economics instruction (NCEE 2010-4002rev). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED533126

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Sensitivity analyses re-estimated the impact models with different combinations of baseline covariates and analytic samples. The report states "effect sizes ranged from 0.17 to 0.42 across all the models estimated", with variation largely due to intervention-control differences on the teacher baseline TEL measure.

"Although the impact estimates on TEL scores varied, effect sizes ranged from 0.17 to 0.42 across all the models estimated to assess the sensitivity of results. The sensitivity tests therefore are consistent with the key study finding that students in PBE classrooms outperformed their counterparts in control classrooms."
