## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional correlational study with one-group posttest-only design (Alzahrani and Al‐Moteri 2022, Saudi Arabia) of 213 ER healthcare practitioners (45 physicians, 168 registered nurses) during the 2020 peak of the first COVID-19 wave. The review reports associations with "p = 0.01" through "p = 0.04"; no standardized effect size is printed, and the design is correlational.

> "Participation in the triage training program was significantly associated with higher scores across all domains of the Triage Decision‐Making Inventory, including cognitive abilities (p = 0.01), critical thinking (p = 0.03), confidence (p = 0.01), and intuition (p = 0.02). Participation in the Disaster Preparedness Program was significantly associated only with cognitive abilities (p = 0.04). Involvement in mock drills was significantly associated with higher confidence scores (p = 0.03)."
