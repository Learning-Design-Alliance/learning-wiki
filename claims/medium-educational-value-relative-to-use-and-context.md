Theoretical synthesis attributed to the long trajectory of studies centered on the didactic objective of media education, from the early 20th century to today; the author reports these studies corroborated the initial results. No individual study data are reported.

> "The educational value of a medium is always relative, it can be extremely beneficial but at the same time, if the usage and the context are not favorable for it, it can even be counterproductive."
