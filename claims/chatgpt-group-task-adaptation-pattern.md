### Stefan Küchemann 2023

Stefan Küchemann, Steffen Steinert, Natalia Revenga, Matthias Schweinberger, Yavuz Dinc, Karina E. Avila, and Jochen Kuhn. (2023). Physics task development of prospective physics teachers using ChatGPT. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020128)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Adaptation analysis of the intervention group's developed tasks found "participants in the ChatGPT group used ChatGPT for 50 tasks (98% of all tasks in this group)" and adapted ChatGPT tasks in 12 cases (24% of the group's tasks).

> "participants in the ChatGPT group used ChatGPT for 50 tasks (98% of all tasks in this group) and adapted ChatGPT tasks in 12 cases (24% of all tasks in this group)."

### Stefan Küchemann 2023 (2)

Stefan Küchemann, Steffen Steinert, Natalia Revenga, Matthias Schweinberger, Yavuz Dinc, Karina E. Avila, and Jochen Kuhn. (2023). Physics task development of prospective physics teachers using ChatGPT. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020128)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Counting improvements across seven categories (Fig. 2), the article reports that "participants working with ChatGPT made a greater number of improvements compared to those in the textbook group", which the authors expected because textbook participants primarily developed tasks independently.

> "Fig. 2 shows that participants working with ChatGPT made a greater number of improvements compared to those in the textbook group."
