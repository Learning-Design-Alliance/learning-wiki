Evidence¶

Ben Clarke 2016¶

Ben Clarke, Christian Doabler, Keith Smolkowski, Evangeline Kurtz Nelson, Hank Fien, Scott K. Baker & Derek Kosty (2016). Testing the Immediate and Long-Term Efficacy of a Tier 2 Kindergarten Mathematics Intervention. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2015.1116034

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Follow-up analysis of the randomized trial using the SESAT at kindergarten posttest and the SAT-10 approximately six months into first grade, tested with analysis-of-covariance models using ASPENS and TEMA as pretest covariates. The article reports no statistically significant differences between conditions on either standardized measure.

"We did not detect statistically signiﬁcant differences between conditions in gains on the NSB nor differences between con- ditions on the SESAT or SAT-10; both tested with the ASPENS and TEMA as pretest covari- ates."
