## Evidence

### Kaluschke Maximilian 2023

Kaluschke Maximilian, Yin Myat Su, Haddawy Peter, Suebnukarn Siriwan, Zachmann Gabriel. (2023). The effect of 3D stereopsis and hand-tool alignment on learning effectiveness and skill transfer of a VR-based simulator for dental training. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0291389

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Suitability-for-assessment analysis in the randomized controlled study measured transfer from real-world pre-testing to first-session simulator performance; stereoscopic 3D produced significantly lower initial simulator error than monoscopic 3D, p < 0.05.

> "The stereoscopic 3D condition resulted in significantly lower initial simulator error (M = 1.99, SD = 0.73) compared to the monoscopic 3D condition (M = 2.48, SD = 0.92) (t(36.09) = 1.86, p < 0.05)."

### Kaluschke Maximilian 2023 (2)

Kaluschke Maximilian, Yin Myat Su, Haddawy Peter, Suebnukarn Siriwan, Zachmann Gabriel. (2023). The effect of 3D stereopsis and hand-tool alignment on learning effectiveness and skill transfer of a VR-based simulator for dental training. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0291389

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of the four condition groups in the same study; group 1 showed a moderate significant correlation (R = 0.41, p < 0.05) while group 4 showed a moderate negative correlation (R = −0.39, p = 0.067).

> "group 1 (stereoscopic 3D & hand-tool alignment) shows by far the strongest skill transfer correlation, with a moderate, significant correlation (R = 0.41, p < 0.05) between pre-training error and simulator error."
