### Sutcher 2017

Sutcher, L., Podolsky, A., & Espinoza, D. (2017). Supporting principals' learning: Key features of effective programs. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/supporting-principals-learning

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report's narrative review of in-service learning describes networks and professional learning communities as replacing one-time workshops, citing San Diego Unified's web of learning supports including case studies, school visits, and peer coaching, which principals reported as "intensive in its hands-on nature" and helpful for school improvement work.

> "Practicing principals who are part of networks or well-functioning professional learning communities that meet regularly and work on common problems of practice can provide opportunities for principals to share best practices, develop a shared orientation toward instruction, and problem solve"
