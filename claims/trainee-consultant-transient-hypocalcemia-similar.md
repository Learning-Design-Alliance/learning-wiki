## Evidence

### Ambe Peter C. 2016

Ambe Peter C., Wassenberg Dirk R. (2016). Is sutureless thyroid surgery safe in the hands of surgical trainees. A single centre retrospective study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-016-1940-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results-section rates from the retrospective 208-patient series: transient hypocalcemia in "21.7 % (31/147)" consultant versus 19.7% (12/61) trainee cases; the two permanent cases were both consultant cases. Printed rates are descriptive; no effect size or test reported.

> "The corresponding incidences were 21.7 % (31/147) and 19.7 % (12/61) in consultant group and trainee group respectively. Permanent hypocalcemia developed in two cases (0.9 %), both from consultant group."
