Observational analysis of 2,874 student turns from 221 completed sessions with a Socratic AI tutor in an introductory mechanics course. The article reports "The top 25 categories cover∼52% of all messages" and that categories with five or more messages cover 86%. No effect size is printed.

> "Out of 357 consolidated categories the LLM identified in the data, the top 25 cover roughly half of all student turns, and categories with five or more messages cover 86%."
