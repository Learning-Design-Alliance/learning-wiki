## Evidence

### Charles Henderson 2006

Charles Henderson, Andrea Beach, and Michael Famiano. (2006). Promoting Instructional Change via Co-Teaching. Manuscript submitted to American Journal of Physics (Physics Education Research Section). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0608294](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0608294)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Outsider observation study of the Fall 2005 co-taught introductory physics course: three observations of each instructor scaffolded by the RTOP instrument plus field notes. Similar RTOP scores suggested both instructors were 'working appropriately within the interactive class structure'.

> "Both MF and CH received similar scores on the RTOP instrument for each class session as well as similar scores to one another [47]."

### Charles Henderson 2006 (2)

Charles Henderson, Andrea Beach, and Michael Famiano. (2006). Promoting Instructional Change via Co-Teaching. Manuscript submitted to American Journal of Physics (Physics Education Research Section). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0608294](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0608294)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-study observation data from the co-teaching semester: the authors interpret the observations as showing the course-structure scaffolding worked 'right from the start, in helping MF teach in a non-traditional way,' with no implementation dip in teaching performance.

> "These observations suggest that the scaffolding provided by the course structure was effective, right from the start, in helping MF teach in a non-traditional way. There was no “implementation dip” present in MF’s teaching performance."
