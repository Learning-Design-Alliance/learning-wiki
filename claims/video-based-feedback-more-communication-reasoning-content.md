# Video-based feedback sessions address more communication skills and clinical reasoning elaboration items than direct observation feedback, independent of feedback length

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After adjusting for feedback length, supervisors addressed more communication skills items (11.29 vs. 7.71, p=0.002) and more clinical reasoning elaboration items (3.71 vs. 2.04, p=0.010) in video-based than direct observation sessions. ## Evidence

### Junod Perron Noëlle 2016

Junod Perron Noëlle, Louis-Simonet Martine, Cerutti Bernard, Pfarrwaller Eva, Sommer Johanna, Nendaz Mathieu. (2016). Feedback in formative OSCEs: comparison between direct observation and video-based formats. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.32160

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Audiotaped content analysis of 48 feedback sessions (2 direct and 2 video-based per supervisor, 12 supervisors) coded with a 21-item feedback scale and compared via MANOVA with supervisor and duration effects. The video-based format "were higher in the video-based format (11.29 vs. 7.71, p=0.002" for communication skills.

> "When adjusted for the length of feedback, there were significant differences in terms of content and process between both formats; the number of communication skills and clinical reasoning items addressed were higher in the video-based format (11.29 vs. 7.71, p=0.002 and 3.71 vs. 2.04, p=0.010, respectively)."

### Junod Perron Noëlle 2016 (2)

Junod Perron Noëlle, Louis-Simonet Martine, Cerutti Bernard, Pfarrwaller Eva, Sommer Johanna, Nendaz Mathieu. (2016). Feedback in formative OSCEs: comparison between direct observation and video-based formats. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.32160

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 1 reports mean number of items addressed per session; the elaboration on communication and professionalism row shows "4.29 (1.68)	2.21 (1.38)" in favor of video-based format, significant both unadjusted and adjusted to feedback length (p<0.001).

> "Elaboration on communication – professionalism	4.29 (1.68)	2.21 (1.38)	2.08 (0.40)	<0.001	1.12 (0.76)	<0.001"
