# No robust link between Dark Triad traits and giftedness: the selected sample was lower only in narcissism (ES = .01) and did not differ in Machiavellianism or psychopathy

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Likhanov 2021

Likhanov, M. V., Tsigeman, E. S., Papageorgiou, K. A., Akmalov, A. F., Sabitov, I. A., & Kovas, Y. V. (2021). Ordinary extraordinary: Elusive group differences in personality and psychological difficulties between STEM‐gifted adolescents and their peers. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12349

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariate ANOVAs on the Dark Triad scales in the 2 × 2 design (Table 3). The article reports the selected sample "was lower in narcissism (ES = .01)" with no difference in Machiavellianism or psychopathy; the printed partial η² for narcissism (.013) is below the negligible threshold used here.

> "In line with results of the meta‐analysis (O’Boyle et al., 2013), our data showed no robust link between Dark Triad personality traits and giftedness. The selected sample was lower in narcissism (ES = .01), but did not differ from unselected students in Machiavellianism and psychopathy."
