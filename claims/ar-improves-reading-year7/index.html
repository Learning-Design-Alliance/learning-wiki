Evidence¶

Gorard et al. 2015¶

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui, Beng Huat See. (2015). Accelerated Reader Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.gl-assessment.co.uk/products/new-group-reading-test/test-detail

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Two-arm post-test-only RCT across four secondary schools; 175 AR pupils versus 164 controls analysed intention-to-treat on NGRT 3A. The report states the treatment group is ahead "by about one quarter of a standard deviation", with printed effect sizes of +0.24 (overall score) and +0.26 (SAS).

"Whether considered in terms of the NGRT overall reading score or the SAS, it is clear that AR has had a modest impact on the treatment group (Tables 4 and 5). The treatment group is ahead of the control by about one quarter of a standard deviation, equivalent to around an extra three months' reading age."
