## Evidence

### V.O.M. Lara 2014

V.O.M. Lara, D. F. Amaral, D. Faria, and L. P. Vieira. (2014). Demonstrations of magnetic phenomena: Measuring the air permeablity using tablets. https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.6581

`q2 · i1`

Data fit under the magnetic-dipole model with b imposed at −3, using the coil's mean radius, turns and current; the fit returned a′ = 4240± 25.81 with standard deviation less than 1%, and unit conversion gave "µair∼= 1.298× 10−6Hm−1".

> "Converting all the units to their SI values, the result leads to µair∼= 1.298× 10−6Hm−1, in good agreement with the expected value of µ0 ≡ 4π× 10−6 ≈ 1.2566370614× 10−6Hm−1."

### V.O.M. Lara 2014 (2)

V.O.M. Lara, D. F. Amaral, D. Faria, and L. P. Vieira. (2014). Demonstrations of magnetic phenomena: Measuring the air permeablity using tablets. https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.6581

`q2 · i1`

A more sophisticated error analysis replacing each Table II coil data point in equation 2, with stated measurement uncertainties and the variance formula; Fig. 6 shows the per-distance µair values deviating relatively little from the expected value.

> "The uncertainty σµair = 0.1× 10−6Hm−1 tell us that the experiment enables the evaluation of µair with 2 signiﬁcant ﬁgures."
