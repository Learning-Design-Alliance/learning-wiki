Rachel Lambert 2026¶

Rachel Lambert, Kara Imm. (2026). Teachers rehumanizing mathematics for students with disabilities; the role of empathy. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09753-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Post-course Qualtrics survey completed by 33 of the 45 course participants, rated on a none/some/definite growth scale with open-ended questions coded qualitatively. Participants described shifts "from a medical model towards a social model, in the context of their own past deficit thinking, and related to the larger political struggle of disabled people."

"the course outcome that participants reported the most growth on was “we rethink our ideas about disability” with 27 participants noting “definite growth,” and none “no growth.” Seventeen participants noted that they had shifted away from deficit thinking because of the course."
