## Evidence

### Kim Young-Suk Grace 2022

Kim Young-Suk Grace, Wolters Alissa, Mercado Janet, & Quinn Jamie. (2022). Crosslinguistic Transfer of Higher Order Cognitive Skills and Their Roles in Writing for English-Spanish Dual Language Learners. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000516

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of writing-sample language use by instructional program (Table 1 proportions). The article attributes the pattern to program design: dual program students receive literacy instruction in both languages whereas English immersion students receive instruction only in English.

> "Many students in the English immersion program wrote in English when prompted to write in Spanish. For those in the Spanish-English dual program, many students were able to write in both languages while some students in Grade 1 wrote in Spanish when prompted to write in English."
