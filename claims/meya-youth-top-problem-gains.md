### Wood Jeffrey J. 2024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Caregiver-rated Youth Top Problems totals compared from last baseline session to end of MEYA for six children, with printed percent reductions of 58.3%, -4.5%, 38.4%, 7.4%, 13.1%, and 48.9%. The article calls these outcomes "limited in interpretability due to the variability in MEYA sessions when final ratings were taken".

> "there was evidence of gains in personalized goals for most child participants"
