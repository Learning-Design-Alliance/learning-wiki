# Austin CMO-affiliated charters outperformed independent Austin charters in both subjects

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r?` · `q2` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Austin CMO charters outgrew the state average (reading 0.1 SD/60 days; math 0.11 SD/65 days, both p<0.01) while independent charters fell below it (reading -0.06; math -0.10), and CMOs outperformed independents by 0.16 SD (96 days) in reading and 0.21 SD (124 days) in math.
