### Douglas Biber 2011

Douglas Biber, Tatiana Nekrasova, and Brad Horn. (2011). The Effectiveness of Feedback for L1-English and L2-Writing Development: A Meta-Analysis. ETS Research Report RR-11-05. https://www.ets.org/toefl

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The meta-analysis screening (Section 4.1) required publication between 1982 and 2007, quantitative measures with an experimental or quasi-experimental design, feedback-characteristic independent variables, and writing-behavior outcome indicators: "112 studies were identified as potentially relevant for the meta-analysis." Reported exclusions include 24 correlational designs, 24 with incomplete statistical reporting, 16 addressing different questions, 14 with no comparison basis, and 11 with multiple treatments and a single posttest.

> "Based on these criteria, 112 studies were identified as potentially relevant for the meta-analysis. These studies were then subjected to a second round of closer scrutiny to determine if the design and reporting standards were in fact adequate for our purposes here."
