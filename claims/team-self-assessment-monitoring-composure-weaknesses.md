## Evidence

### Chiu Po-Wei 2025

Chiu Po-Wei, Chu Shao-Chung, Yang Chia-Han, Lee Huan-Fang, Hung Hsuan-Man, Hsu Hsiang-Chin. (2025). Peer-assisted learning in critical care: a simulation-based approach for postgraduate medical training. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2497333

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same-day self-assessment by all 29 trainees using the TEAM scale (1–5 range), reported descriptively with box-and-whisker plots. The article notes "higher scores were observed in team communication (mean = 3.72, SD = 0.65), leadership (mean = 3.82, SD = 0.70), and team morale (mean = 3.89, SD = 0.68)". Descriptive only; no inferential test.
