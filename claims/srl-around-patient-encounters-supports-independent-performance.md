### Sagasser MH 2016

Sagasser MH, Kramer AWM, Fluit CRMG, van Weel C, van der Vleuten CPM. (2016). Self-entrustment: how trainees' self-regulated learning supports participation in the workplace. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-016-9723-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative non-participant observational study in seven general practices; two observation days per supervisor-trainee pair covering 112 patient encounters, debriefing sessions, educational meetings and interviews, analysed phenomenologically. The article reports that "Self-regulated learning supported trainees to increasingly perform independently".

> "Self-regulated learning supported trainees to increasingly perform independently. They engaged in self-regulated learning before, during and after encounters."
