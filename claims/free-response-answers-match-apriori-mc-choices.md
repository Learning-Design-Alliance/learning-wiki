### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Can Multiple-Choice Questions Simulate Free-Response Questions? Physics Education Research Conference proceedings (AIP Conference Proceedings). Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.07910](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.07910)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Binning analysis of free-response answers from 153 students in course A, matching each answer to the choices of the corresponding MC question (Table 4). The authors report "84% and 88% of students’ free-response answers could be matched" for questions 1 and 2.

> "We find that out of the 153 students involved, 84% and 88% of students’ free-response answers could be matched to the a priori choices in the multiple-choice questions 1 and 2, respectively."
