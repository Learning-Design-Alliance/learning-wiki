Janssen, Verena Ruf, David Dzsotjan, Christian De Schryver, Jakob Karolus, Stefan Ruzika, Karen Joisten, Paul Lukowicz, Jochen Kuhn, Norbert Wehn, Stefan Küchemann. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey analysis within the winter semester 2022/23 usability evaluation at RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau and LMU Munich. The article reports "a significant linear relation of 0.42 ( p = 0.0014)" between summarized intention of use and frequency of use among users; no standardized effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded here.

> "By numbering each answer option for the frequency of use starting with 1 for “daily” and ending with 7 for “Once a semester”, we find a significant linear relation of 0.42 ( p = 0.0014) between the summarized intention of use (shown in Fig. 5) and the frequency of use (shown in Fig. 3)."
