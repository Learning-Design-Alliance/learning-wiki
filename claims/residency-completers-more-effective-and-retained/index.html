Evidence¶

Wojcikiewicz 2024¶

Wojcikiewicz, S. K., & Wei, W. (2024). Investing in Texas teacher preparation: Key features of successful residency programs [Brief]. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/texas-teacher-preparation-brief

q2 · i? · review · r1

The brief's synthesis of prior research (citing Saunders et al. 2024, Kirksey & Gottlieb 2024, and Marder et al. 2022) reports that "residency program completers are more effective, and more likely to stay in teaching" than other-pathway entrants. No effect sizes are printed in the brief.

"Growing evidence indicates that residency program completers are more effective, and more likely to stay in teaching, than teachers who enter through other pathways or without preparation, contributing to student learning and workforce stability."

  • Residency-prepared teachers remain in teaching and in their districts at higher rates than other novice teachers, typically 80–95% after 3 years — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Across CPS preparation pathways, traditional and residency student teachers completed more methods coursework, had lengthier programs, and planned more years in teaching than alternative-pathway student teachers — related
  • Teachers of color disproportionately enter through alternative certification pathways, which predict higher turnover — related

  • Grow Your Own program graduates remain in teaching longer than typical beginning teachers and teach in high-need schools at high rates — related
