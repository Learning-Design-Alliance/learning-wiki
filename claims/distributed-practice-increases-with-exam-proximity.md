## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The likelihood of engaging in distributed practice was significantly higher in weeks 3 and 4 compared to week 1, across both groups. [→ David 2026](#david-2026)

## Evidence

### David 2026

David, L., Biwer, F., Crutzen, R., & de Bruin, A. (2026). Understanding and supporting university students' use of distributed practice via implementation intentions. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00448-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Generalized linear mixed-effects model with week as categorical predictor on daily ESM data: "signiﬁcantly higher likelihood of engaging in distributed practice in week 3" and week 4 relative to week 1, with no significant group × week interactions. The authors link this convergence to exam proximity and possible measurement reactivity.

> "Compared to week 1, participants showed a signiﬁcantly higher likelihood of engaging in distributed practice in week 3 (β =0 . 6 8 , S E=0 . 2 4 ,p = 0.004, 95% CI [0.22; 1.14]) and week 4 ( β = 1.68, SE = 0.33, p < 0.001; 95% CI [1.03; 2.32])."
