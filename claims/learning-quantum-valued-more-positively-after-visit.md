## Evidence

### Tania S. Moraga-Calderón 2020

Tania S. Moraga-Calderón, Henk Busiman and Julia Cramer. (2020). The relevance of learning quantum physics from the perspective of the secondary school student: A case study. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.30935/scimath/9545](https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9545)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding of open-question answers from the 45 students, coded inductively into value categories. The article also reports that no student expressed a more negative view of learning quantum after the visit, and only two initially negative students remained negative.

> "The coding of the category “Value” (see Table 3 ) of section C ‘Why do I learn quantum physics? ’ shows that students describe ‘learning quantum’ as more useful, interesting or important after the visit (Figure 3)."
