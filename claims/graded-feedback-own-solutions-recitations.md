### P. Klein 2021

P. Klein, L. Ivanjek, M.N. Dahlkemper, K. Jeličić, M.-A. Geyer, S. Küchemann, and A. Susac. (2021). Studying physics during the COVID-19 pandemic: Student assessments of learning achievement, perceived effectiveness of online recitations, and online laboratories. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ANOVA comparing students grouped by experienced recitation format (submission of own solutions versus no submission) among recitation attendees. The article reports "F (1, 393) = 6.74, p = 0.01" with no significant differences for other group splits; no effect size is printed.

> "It was observed that students who sent their own solution to be rated ( N = 168) assessed the eﬀectiveness of the recitations signiﬁcantly higher than students who did not (N = 227), F (1, 393) = 6.74, p = 0.01."
