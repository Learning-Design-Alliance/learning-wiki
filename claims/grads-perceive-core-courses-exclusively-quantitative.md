# Graduate students perceive the physics core courses as focused exclusively on quantitative problem solving with little focus on concepts

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Alexandru Maries 2012

Chandralekha Singh and Alexandru Maries. (2012). Core Graduate Courses: A Missed Learning Opportunity?. [doi:10.1063/1.4789732](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789732)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Individual discussions with physics graduate students enrolled in the core courses about what they learned and what the courses achieved. Students reported that 'the focus of the core courses was exclusively quantitative' and that they therefore did not process the material conceptually.

> "Some of the graduate students specifically noted that the focus of the core courses was exclusively quantitative and they felt bogged down with the mathematical manipulations introduced at a tremendous pace in all of their core courses."
