# Learning gains correlate with in-app progression and motivation only under LP-based personalization conditions, most fully when choice is added

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Only in the ZCO condition are learning score, Kidlearn progression and motivation score all positively interrelated (r = .21 and r = .27); ZPDES shows a learning-score/progression correlation (r = .32); PCO shows none. [→ Clément 2024](#clement-2024)

## Evidence

### Clément 2024

Clément, B., Sauzéon, H., Roy, D., & Oudeyer, P.-Y. (2024). Improved Performances and Motivation in Intelligent Tutoring Systems: Combining Machine Learning and Learner Choice. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.01669](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.01669)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Bravais-Pearson inter-correlations computed per condition in the RCT (Table 1); ZPDES also shows a learning-score/progression correlation (r = .32, P = .005); in the Predef condition learning score correlates with motivation (r = .29) but not progression.

> "the ZCO condition is the only condition where it is possible to observe positive relations between the learning score and the learning experience with the ITS in terms of both kidlearn progression and the motivation state (respectively r = .21 and r = .27)."
