Evidence¶

Amanda Vite 2024¶

Amanda Vite, Erika A. Patall, Man Chen. (2024). Relationships Between Experiences of Autonomy and Well(Ill)-Being for K-12 Youth: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review (2024) 36:127. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09967-x

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Mixed-effects meta-regression within the K-12 meta-analysis testing study region (USA/Canada, Asia, Europe, Australia/New Zealand, Middle East) as a moderator with publication status and both scale alphas as covariates; the region effect was significant.

"Overall, analyses revealed that the region a study was conducted in was a significant moderator of the ANS and well-being correlation, controlling for publication status, predictor reliability, and outcome reliability."
