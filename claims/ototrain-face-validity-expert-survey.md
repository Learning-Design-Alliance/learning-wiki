## Evidence

### Wickens Brandon 2015

Wickens Brandon, Lewis Jordan, Morris David P, Husein Murad, Ladak Hanif M, Agrawal Sumit K. (2015). Face and content validity of a novel, web-based otoscopy simulator for medical education. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-015-0060-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert survey of the OtoTrain simulator: 11 otoscopy experts (5 Neurotologists, 5 Pediatric Otolaryngologists, 1 Audiologist) rated face validity on a 7-point Likert scale. Mean scores were 2.7 (SD 1.5) for external auditory canal anatomy and 2.3 (SD 1.3) for tympanic membrane anatomy, described as "an acceptable realistic representation of the relevant anatomy".

> "The mean scores across both study domains measured at ≤ 3, falling between the “Agree”, “Mostly Agree”, and “Strongly Agree” categories. This was considered to be an acceptable realistic representation of the relevant anatomy."
