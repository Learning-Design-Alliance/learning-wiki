### Foppoli 2018

Foppoli, A., Choudhary, R., Blair, D., Kaur, T., Moschilla, J. and Zadnik, M. (2018). Public and Teacher Response to Einsteinian Physics in Schools. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aae4a4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aae4a4)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of 49 direct reader comments on two Conversation articles (viewed 5,700 times with 22 comments, and 18,700 times with 27 comments), categorised as strong support, qualified support, too complex, opposed, or not relevant (Table 7).

> "The responses to Article 1 show support from 86% of the comments. None were opposed, while 14% of comments were irrelevant. In the case of the second article, 19% of recipients were opposed, with most comments indicating confusion between mathematical tools and concepts."
