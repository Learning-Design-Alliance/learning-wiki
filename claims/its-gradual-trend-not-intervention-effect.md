# Interrupted time series analysis found tutor quality improved gradually over time (β=0.01, p=.022) with no immediate level change or slope change at the training intervention

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` An interrupted time series analysis of average transcript score showed a small but significant linear trend over time (β=0.01, p=.022), with no immediate level change at the intervention point (post: p=.246) and no slope change (time×post: p=.479). [→ Thomas 2026](#thomas-2026)
`q3 i?` A logistic ITS model of opportunity likelihood showed a significant positive pre-training trend (OR=1.07, p<.001), a non-significant negative level change (post OR=0.63, p=.111), and a significantly attenuated post-training slope (time×post OR=0.99, p=.028). [→ Thomas 2026 (2)](#thomas-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Thomas 2026

Thomas, D. R., Kamikazi, M. C. A., Brandt, C., Borchers, C., & Koedinger, K. R. (2026). AI-Driven Assessment of Human Tutors: Linking Training Performance to Real-Life Practice. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18617

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interrupted time series analysis of average transcript scores across the three-phase within-subjects design. Variation was largely attributable to stable differences between tutors (ICC=.27), with fixed effects explaining little variance (marginal R²=.01).

> "An interrupted time series analysis of average transcript score showed a small but significant linear trend over time (β= 0.01,p=.022), indicating gradual improvement in tutor quality independent of the training intervention."

### Thomas 2026 (2)

Thomas, D. R., Kamikazi, M. C. A., Brandt, C., Borchers, C., & Koedinger, K. R. (2026). AI-Driven Assessment of Human Tutors: Linking Training Performance to Real-Life Practice. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18617

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Logistic mixed-effects ITS model of opportunity likelihood, with substantial heterogeneity across tutors and skills remaining (ICC=.35). The post-training slope was significantly attenuated (time×post OR=0.99, p=.028).

> "A logistic mixed-effects interrupted time series model predicting the likelihood of observing an opportunity to apply a target tutoring skill revealed a significant positive pre-training trend (odds ratio, OR = 1.07 per unit time,p<.001)"
