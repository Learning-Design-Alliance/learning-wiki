## Subclaims
`q3 i?` GPT-3.5 achieved 57% accuracy on English-speaking countries' examinations versus 48% on non-English-speaking countries' examinations, with a meta-regression coefficient of 0.46 (P = .011). [→ Liu Mingxin 2025](#liu-mingxin-2025)

## Evidence

### Liu Mingxin 2025

Liu Mingxin, Okuhara Tsuyoshi, Huang Wenbo, Ogihara Atsushi, Nagao Hikari Sophia, Okada Hiroko, Kiuchi Takahiro. (2025). Large Language Models in Dental Licensing Examinations: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2024.10.014

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis of GPT-3.5 performance by examination origin country using a random effects model. The review reports "The P value indicating significant difference was 0.05, suggesting a borderline level of significance".

> "The integrate accuracy rate of GPT-3.5 in non-English-speaking countries' examinations was 48% (95% CI: 42%-54%), while in English-speaking countries' examinations, it was 57% (95% CI: 50%-64%). The P value indicating significant difference was 0.05, suggesting a borderline level of significance"
