## Evidence

### Fehervari Matyas 2022

Fehervari Matyas, Das Bibek, Soleimani-Nouri Payam, Ahmad Manal, Fadel Michael G., Deputy Mohammed, Morgan Catrin, Burke Joshua R., Mason John D., Nott David, Spalding Duncan. (2022). Can surgical skills be taught using technological advances online? A comparative study of online and face-to-face surgical skills training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-022-09170-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Primary-endpoint analysis of the validation study: demonstrators rated whether delegates met each Dave's-taxonomy domain with 'Yes' or 'No' after online breakout-room or face-to-face small-group teaching; development to "the level of articulation" showed no between-format difference. No effect sizes printed.

> "The development of suturing skills, tendon repair and vascular anastomosis to the level of articulation were no different between delegates participating in breakout rooms compared to face-to-face sessions (p = 0.841, p = 0.792 and p = 1.00, respectively)."
