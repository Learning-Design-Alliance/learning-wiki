### Srakoopun Chalinee 2025

Srakoopun Chalinee, Suebnukarn Siriwan, Haddawy Peter, Kaluschke Maximilian, Weller Rene, Yin Myat Su, Aguilar Panuroot, Phumpatrakom Panupat, Pinchamnankool Kriangkrai, Budsaba Kamon, Zachmann Gabriel. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Split-plot ANOVA on procedural error score differences in the RCT (n = 30) found no significant main effects, and the interaction was also non-significant (F = 0.42, p = 0.52), with changes "comparable across all groups." Expert raters showed a Cohen's kappa of 0.924.

> "The analysis revealed no significant main effect of training method, F(1, 28) = 1.20, p = 0.28, and no significant main effect of access cavity preparation technique, F(1, 28) = 1.02, p = 0.31."
