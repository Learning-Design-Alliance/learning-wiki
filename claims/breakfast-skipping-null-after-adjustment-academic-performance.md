# Skipping breakfast is associated with low academic performance in univariate analysis but not after adjustment for other lifestyle factors

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Skipping breakfast was significantly associated with low academic performance in univariate analysis (OR 1.70) but the association disappeared in multivariate analysis (OR 1.10, not significant). [→ Yamada Masaaki 2019](#yamada-masaaki-2019)

## Evidence

### Yamada Masaaki 2019

Yamada Masaaki, Sekine Michikazu, Tatsuse Takashi, Asaka Yukiko. (2019). Association between lifestyle, parental smoke, socioeconomic status, and academic performance in Japanese elementary school children: the Super Diet Education Project. Environmental Health and Preventive Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12199-019-0776-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the cross-sectional survey, univariate analysis showed skipping breakfast "OR 1.70; 95% CI, 1.09–2.65" but multivariate analysis showed OR 1.10 (95% CI, 0.68–1.78), not significant. No statistically significant difference was detected after adjustment; equivalence was not tested.

> "Although there was a significant association in the univariate analysis, the association disappeared in the multivariate analysis."
