# Hippocampal lesions made before training prevent cued recall of flavor-place paired associates in rats

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Sham-lesioned rats showed preferential digging at the cued location in a nonrewarded probe test, whereas hippocampal-lesioned rats were at chance. [→ Tse et al. 2007](#tse-et-al-2007)

## Evidence

### Tse et al. 2007

Dorothy Tse, Rosamund F. Langston, Masaki Kakeyama, Ingrid Bethus, Patrick A. Spooner, Emma R. Wood, Menno P. Witter, Richard G. M. Morris. (2007). Schemas and Memory Consolidation. Science. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1135935

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 1, neurotoxic hippocampal lesions made before training in the paired-associate event-arena task. After 13 sessions, sham animals improved while lesioned animals did not; in the probe test "the hippocampal-lesioned animals were at chance". Lesions were extensive, leaving minimal residual hippocampal tissue.

> "The sham-lesioned animals spent significantly more time digging at the cued location than at the other five incorrect sand wells, whereas the hippocampal-lesioned animals were at chance"
