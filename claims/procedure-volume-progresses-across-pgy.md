### Tejos R 2019

Tejos R, Avila R, Inzunza M, Achurra P, Castillo R, Rosberg A, Cordero O, Kusanovich R, Bellolio F, Varas J, Martínez J. (2019). IMPACT OF A SIMULATED LAPAROSCOPIC TRAINING PROGRAM IN A THREE-YEAR GENERAL SURGERY RESIDENCY. Arquivos Brasileiros de Cirurgia Digestiva. https://doi.org/10.1590/0102-672020190001e1436

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of 10,415 abdominal interventions by 28 trainees: "a progression in the number of procedures performed as the primary surgeon concerning to the PGY of residency" with 1,702 (22.1%), 2,788 (36.3%) and 3,198 (41.6%) across PGY1-3. Descriptive percentages; no effect size.

> "There is a progression in the number of procedures performed as the primary surgeon concerning to the PGY of residency, with a total of 1,702 (22.1%), 2,788 (36.3%) and 3,198 (41.6%) for PGY1, PGY2, and PGY3 respectively"
