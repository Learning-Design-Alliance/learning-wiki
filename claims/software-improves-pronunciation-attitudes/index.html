Evidence¶

Yang Gao and Barbara E. Hanna 2016¶

Yang Gao and Barbara E. Hanna. (2016). Exploring optimal pronunciation teaching: Integrating instructional software into intermediate-level EFL classes in China. CALICO Journal. https://doi.org/10.1558/cj.v33i2.26054

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-samples t test on attitude survey scores for Group 2 (n = 20): presurvey M = 40.00 (SD = 3.29) vs postsurvey M = 43.75 (SD = 3.68), "t (19) = −9.46, p = 0.000". For this group the attitudinal change could only be attributed to the software.

"A paired-samples t test displayed a significant difference at a significance level of 0.01 between scores of pre-survey and scores of postsurvey (t (19) = −9.46, p = 0.000) (Table 10) for Group 2, implying that the software was very helpful for improving users’ attitudes."
