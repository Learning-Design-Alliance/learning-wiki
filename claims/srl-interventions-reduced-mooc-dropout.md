# SRL intervention groups had significantly lower dropout than control in a MOOC, with no difference between the two intervention modes

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Retention was statistically significantly higher after both the personalized and the general intervention than in control; retention was 87.5% personalized, 80.0% general, 62.5% control. [→ Psathas 2023](#psathas-2023)

## Evidence

### Psathas 2023

Psathas, G., Tegos, S., Demetriadis, S. N., & Tsiatsos, T. (2023). Exploring the impact of chat-based collaborative activities and SRL-focused interventions on students’ self-regulation profiles, participation in collaborative activities, retention, and learning in MOOCs. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn 18:329–351. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09394-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square test of homogeneity on dropout across the three 120-student conditions; of 360 students, "84 (23.3%) of the students dropped out". Post hoc z-tests of two proportions with Bonferroni correction found both interventions retained significantly more users than control (p < 0.01); no effect size was printed.

> "a chi-square test of homogeneity revealed a statistically significant difference between the three conditions (χ 2 = 22.08, p < 0.01)."
