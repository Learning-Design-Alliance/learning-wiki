## Evidence

### Navarro Juan-José 2015

Navarro Juan-José, García-Rubio Javier, Olivares Pedro R. (2015). The Relative Age Effect and Its Influence on Academic Performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0141895

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

One-way ANOVA with Tamhane post hoc comparisons on the SIMCE global score of 15,234 Chilean eighth graders; Table 2 reports F = 1372.85, p < .001, with means of 307.08 (private), 269.41 (subsidised), and 246.91 (public). No standardised effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "in relation to the type of institution, results also reveal significant differences among the three groups referred to: private schools who obtain higher scores, followed by subsidised and, finally, public."
