This is the authors' interpretation in the General Discussion, not a separately tested manipulation: no feedback-absent condition existed in either experiment. They argue corrective feedback may have helped refine emerging representations and, in the varied retrieval condition, supported comparison across instances.

> "Thus, the benefits observed in the present study reflect retrieval practice with feedback rather than retrieval alone."
