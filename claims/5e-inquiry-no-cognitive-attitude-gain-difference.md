### Senol Sen & Ozge Ozyalcin Oskay 2016

Senol Sen & Ozge Ozyalcin Oskay. (2016). The Effects of 5E Inquiry Learning Activities on Achievement and Attitude toward Chemistry. Journal of Education and Learning, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v6n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on cognitive attitude gain scores (posttest-pretest) of the ASCI in the same quasi-experiment; the article reports "no significant differences" (t (32) = -.877, p = .387). Gain scores were used because groups differed on cognitive attitude at pre-test.

> "it was found that there were no significant differences between the students’ cognitive gain scores (MExp. = 1.11, SD = 2.81; MCont. = 2.19, SD = 4.28; t (32) = -.877, p = .387, two-tailed)"
