## Evidence

### Patrick Albus 2023

Patrick Albus, Tina Seufert. (2023). The modality effect reverses in a virtual reality learning environment and influences cognitive load. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09611-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on intrinsic cognitive load measured with the Differentiated Cognitive Load Questionnaire immediately after the VR learning session. No significant difference between conditions; equivalence was not tested.

> "no significant difference was found between the conditions for ICL (H4: F(1,59) = 2.69, p = .106, ηp2= 0.044)"
