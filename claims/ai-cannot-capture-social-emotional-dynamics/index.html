Theme summary from Section 4.2 of the speed-dating study; teachers reacted negatively, for example, to a virtual hand-raise feature, with one teacher calling it "kind of artificial in a group situation like this."

"Teachers, unlike students, were also concerned about a possible decrease in interpersonal interactions and communications between teachers and students."
