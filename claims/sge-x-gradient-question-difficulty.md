### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students' Understanding of Quantum Mechanics via the Stern-Gerlach Experiment. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Written survey of more than 200 graduate students at the beginning of instruction and 35 undergraduates from two classes after traditional SGE instruction; the article reports 23% and 39% correct respectively. The most common difficulty was assuming no splitting since the spin state is |↑⟩z.

> "Only 23% of the more than 200 graduate students in a survey at the beginning of instruction provided the correct response. The performance of 35 undergr aduate students from two diﬀerent classes who were given this question immediately after traditional instruction in SGE was only somewhat better (39%)."
