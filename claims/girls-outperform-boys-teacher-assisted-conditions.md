# Girls outperformed boys on knowledge tests in the teacher-assisted conditions, and this gender advantage persisted at retention.

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In both CALT and TAL, girls gained significantly higher sum scores than boys on the post-test (CALT: Z = −2.385, p = 0.017; TAL: Z = −3.784, p < 0.001). [→ Conradty 2016](#conradty-2016)
`q3 i?` The gender advantage persisted at the six-week retention test (CALT: Z = −3.777, p < 0.001; TAL: Z = −2.339, p = 0.019). [→ Conradty 2016 (2)](#conradty-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Conradty 2016

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U tests comparing girls' and boys' post-test (K2) knowledge sum scores within the CALT and TAL treatment groups in the quasi-experimental study.
