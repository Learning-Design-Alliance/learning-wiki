### Sarah Almy and Melissa Tooley 2012

Sarah Almy and Melissa Tooley. (2012). Building and Sustaining Talent: Creating Conditions in High-Poverty Schools That Support Effective Teaching and Learning. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED543216

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Annual district working-conditions survey results reported in the case study show “teachers at 13 out of 14 Strategic Staffing schools were more satisfied with their school leader’s effectiveness and support than were teachers at a set of comparison schools.”

> "On the most recent survey, teachers at 13 out of 14 Strategic Staffing schools were more satisfied with their school leader’s effectiveness and support than were teachers at a set of comparison schools in the district."

### Sarah Almy and Melissa Tooley 2012 (2)

Sarah Almy and Melissa Tooley. (2012). Building and Sustaining Talent: Creating Conditions in High-Poverty Schools That Support Effective Teaching and Learning. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED543216

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The case study reports achievement gains in the initiative’s first two cohorts, citing the district’s 2011 evaluation: “schools increasing the percentage of proficient students by 20 to 30 percent over the course of two years,” with 70 percent outpacing comparison growth in 2009-10.

> "All schools in the first two cohorts made gains in reading and math, and in some cases, those gains were dramatic, with schools increasing the percentage of proficient students by 20 to 30 percent over the course of two years."
