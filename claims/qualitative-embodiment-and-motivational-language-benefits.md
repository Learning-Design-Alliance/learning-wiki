### Sonlu 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative analysis in §5.2–5.4: embodied variations were "slightly more beneficial and interesting", with high-trait variations described as motivational by participants; low-trait agent movements were called monotonic and the graphics unhelpful by some.

> "Participants found variations with embodied agents slightly more beneficial and interesting. Especially, they reported a positive influence of having an embodied human agent for the high-trait variations."
