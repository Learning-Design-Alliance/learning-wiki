## Evidence

### Gimeno A. 2026

Gimeno A., Alberola‐Zorrilla P., Blasco‐Serra A., Sánchez‐Zuriaga D., Zaragozá R. (2026). Online anatomical images are an effective resource to improve knowledge acquisition and practical exam performance in gross anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70254

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational comparison of quiz participants (both quizzes n=45; at least one n=48) versus non-participants (n=87); the article reports a Hodges–Lehmann median difference of 1.000 favoring both-quiz takers. Association, not causal assignment.

> "The Kruskal–Wallis test revealed significant differences in practical exam scores between students who did not engage with the online quizzes and those who participated in at least one quiz (Figure 2B)."
