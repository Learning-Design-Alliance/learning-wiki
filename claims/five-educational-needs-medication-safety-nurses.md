Jung Sua, Park Jinkyung. (2025). Educational needs for medication safety competence among nurses by clinical ladder stage. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0319483

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative focus group study, April 2022, with twenty nurses in four stage groups of five each (novice to proficient); transcripts were analyzed by two researchers using qualitative content analysis until data saturation. The study identified "crucial educational needs for medication safety" across five themes (Table 2).

> "We identified the following as crucial educational needs for medication safety: self-reflective attitude toward medication safety, effective interaction skills, medication practice knowledge, ability to manage safety risk situations, and establishment of a continuous education system for medication safety (Table 2)."
