# Flexibility showed a different pattern: the H-H group scored highest, but the H-N group outperformed the N-H group

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Flexibility was significantly related to exam performance with the H-H group highest, but unlike other components the H-N group scored better than the N-H group. [→ Hsieh Shu-Shih 2018](#hsieh-shu-shih-2018)

## Evidence

### Hsieh Shu-Shih 2018

Hsieh Shu-Shih, Tsai Jia-Ren, Chang Shao-Hsi, Cheng Chih-Fu, Sung Yao-Ting, Hung Tsung-Min (2018). The Relations between 3-year Changes in Physical Fitness and Academic Performance in Nationally Representative Sample of Junior High School Students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-34370-2

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted ANOVA in the nationwide sample compared four flexibility-change groups on BCTJH scores. The article reports "Results for flexibility were quite different from the other three components", with the H-H group highest and the H-N group beating the N-H group in post hoc order.

> "Results for flexibility were quite different from the other three components. Specifically, while this component was significantly related to exam performance, F(3,382252) = 466.62; p < 0.05; ηp2 = 0.004), and the H-H group (281.0 ± 93.2) had higher scores compared with the H-N (270.8 ± 95.8; d = 0.11), N-H (268.3 ± 96.5; d = 0.14), and N-N (266.4 ± 95.7; d = 0.15) groups"
