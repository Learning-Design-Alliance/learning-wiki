Evidence¶

Linda Castañeda 2024¶

Linda Castañeda, Victoria I. Marín, Daniel Villar-Onrubia. (2024). Relational topologies in the learning activity spaces: operationalising a sociomaterial approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10296-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Bubble diagrams from an online questionnaire in which RICT students reported the device they regularly use to access each tool from each space. The article reports students "privilege the use of Instagram and WhatsApp on their mobile phones" and prefer laptops and tablets for Canva, MIRO and Blogger.

"Figure 11 shows how the RICT students privilege the use of Instagram and WhatsApp on their mobile phones. CANVA, MIRO and Blogger are preferably used from their laptops and tablets, but the use of the LMS among the three devices is very similar compared to the other tools."

Linda Castañeda 2024 (2)¶

Linda Castañeda, Victoria I. Marín, Daniel Villar-Onrubia. (2024). Relational topologies in the learning activity spaces: operationalising a sociomaterial approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10296-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Per-device, per-space bubble diagrams placed in the multi-spatial learning context matrix, from the same RICT self-report data. The article reports laptop-based content creation occurred in spaces dedicated to learning, with laptop use on public transportation "overall much more limited" in intensity and variety.

"RICT students used their laptops to create content (with CANVAS, Blogger, and MIRO) in the spaces specifically dedicated to learning (the classroom and their study spaces at home). The bubbles show few differences among spaces regarding the use of laptops, except in the case of public transportation, where the intensity and variety of uses are overall much more limited."
