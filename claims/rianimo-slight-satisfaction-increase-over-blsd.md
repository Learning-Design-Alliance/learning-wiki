### Palmisano Francesco 2024

Palmisano Francesco, Santuari Nadia, Moletta Cristina, Ambrosi Elisa, Rizzoli Andrea. (2024). The effect of a team-based blended simulation training program on cardiopulmonary resuscitation on healthcare professionals' perception, performance, and costs: a mixed-method study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06543-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective cohort study comparing 845 BLSDr HCPs (2019) with 1,167 riAniMO HCPs (2022); satisfaction was a dichotomized 4-point Likert item. The article reports "a slight increase in the number of HCPs satisfied" (99.9% vs 99.4% satisfied in Table 5); no effect size or significance test is printed.

> "In the riAniMO group, there was a slight increase in the number of HCPs satisfied with retraining compared to the BLSD group."
