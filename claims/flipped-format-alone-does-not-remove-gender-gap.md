## Evidence

### David J. Webb and Wendell H. Potter 2021

David J. Webb and Wendell H. Potter. (2021). Gender-grade-gap zeroed out under a specific intro-physics assessment regime. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2102.10451](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.10451)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Footnoted secondary analysis of one class taught by an instructor who had used the flipped format with online lectures but usual GRC grading, showing a gender gap of -0.22 ± 0.07, matching the usual-classes gap. The article attributes the gap to the assessment/grading scheme.

> "One instructor had used the ﬂipped-class format with the usual grading methods. This class had a gender gap of −0.22 ± 0.07. So, the gender gap seems more related to the assessment/grading scheme that to the ﬂipped-class issue."
