How Arizona's Dropout Crisis Affects Communities 2014¶

How Arizona's Dropout Crisis Affects Communities, Creates Economic Losses for the State of Arizona. (2014). A Project of WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559628

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The report's Section 5.2 presents lifetime present values at age 20 for disconnected youth aged 16-24, combining losses during youth years ($155,470 social) and adulthood ($539,620 social). Across the cohort of 183,200 youth, the social loss is $127.3 billion and fiscal loss $43.0 billion.

"Adding together the full loss during the youth years and the adult loss, the total social loss per disconnected youth is $695,090. The total fiscal loss is $234,480."
