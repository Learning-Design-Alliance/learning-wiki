## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students taught with the ISIA scored significantly higher on the inquiry process skills survey at post-test, with a large effect size. [→ Fan et al. 2018](#fan-et-al-2018)

## Evidence

### Fan et al. 2018

Xinxin Fan, David Geelan and Robyn Gillies. (2018). Evaluating a Novel Instructional Sequence for Conceptual Change in Physics Using Interactive Simulations. Education Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8010029

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way between-groups ANOVA on the 13-item inquiry process skills survey at post-test in the same quasi-experimental trial; the article reports "η2 = 0.38, a large effect size" favouring the ISIA group.

> "There was a statistically signiﬁcant difference at the p < 0.05 level in scores for the two groups: F (1, 115) = 71.36, p = 0.000, η2 = 0.38, a large effect size."
