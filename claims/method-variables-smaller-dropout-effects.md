`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-regression across the coded studies (Table 2). The article reports unpublished status (p = .00) and method quality (p = .06) as method variables associated with smaller treatment differences; routine practice status was not significant (p = .94).

> "The results of the regression analyses on the studies coded thus far find that unpublished research and higher quality methods produce significantly smaller treatment differences."
