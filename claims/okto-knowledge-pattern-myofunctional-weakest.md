## Evidence

### Cheung Chiyuen 2026

Cheung Chiyuen, Lu Hongfei, Tu Shaoqin, Chen Xinyuan, Feng Yi, Shao YiTing, Liu Yuyao, Mai Zhihui, Ai Hong, Chen Zheng. (2026). Development and application of a multidimensional instrument to evaluate competency discrepancies in orthodontic practice. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08237-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

OKTO correct-answer percentages per domain in the 434-participant survey show a distribution that differs from SCS/CPBS: TMJ knowledge was adequate despite low TMJ confidence and practice compliance, while myofunctional knowledge was weakest. The authors attribute the TMJ knowledge pattern to increased emphasis on TMJ knowledge education in recent years.

> "orthodontists demonstrated better knowledge mastery in dental (82.63%) and temporomandibular joint (82.58%) fields, but showed relatively weaker performance in myofunctional (68.26%) and periodontal (73.56%) areas"
