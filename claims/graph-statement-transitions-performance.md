# Frequent eye transitions between graph and problem statement were marginally associated with correct answers

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Transitions between the graph and the problem statement were marginally higher for students who answered correctly; no other transition types differed. [→ Ögren 2017](#ögren-2017)

## Evidence

### Ögren 2017

Ögren, M., Nyström, M., & Jarodzka, H. (2017). There's more to the multimedia effect than meets the eye: is seeing pictures believing? Instructional Science, 45, 263–287. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9397-6

`q2 · i?`

Multi-level logistic regression (Table 7) on transition counts between AOIs; the effect was marginally significant (z = 1.734, p = 0.0829). No significant differences were found for input–graph or input–statement transitions.

> "there is a marginally signiﬁcant effect (p ¼ 0:08) that the number of transitions between the graph and the problem statement were higher for students that answered a problem correctly"
