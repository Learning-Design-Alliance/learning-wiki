Evidence¶

Smagorinsky 1992¶

Smagorinsky, P., & Smith, M. W. (1992). The Nature of Knowledge in Composition and Literary Understanding: The Question of Specificity. Review of Educational Research, 62(3), 279-305. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543062003279

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes to Hillocks's meta-analysis of exemplary experimental composition studies conducted from 1963-1983 the conclusion that an inquiry focus—involving students in inductive analysis of concrete materials to develop task-specific strategies—was "the most effective of the focuses contrasted." No effect size is printed in the review.

"Hillocks concludes from his meta-analysis of exemplary experimental composition studies conducted from 1963-1983 (1984, 1986a, 1987) that an instructional focus that teaches writers task-specific composing procedures is the most effective of the focuses contrasted."
