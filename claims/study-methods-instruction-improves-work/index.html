q2 · i? · causal · r?

Conference report of A. S. Edwards' study with school children; improvement in work is reported without test statistics or effect sizes. A course of study in how to study was being constructed for use in the grades.

"A. S. Edwards, University of Georgia, found that instruction of school children in methods of study resulted in improvement in then- work. Such instruction must be specific and apply directly to the tasks then being undertaken in the school room."
