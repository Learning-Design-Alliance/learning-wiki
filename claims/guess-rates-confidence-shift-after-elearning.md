### Leopold Simma 2025

Leopold Simma, Schneeberger Maurice Henri, von Felten Stefanie, Seiler Michelle, Ramantani Georgia, Bölsterli Bigna Katrin. (2025). E-Learning for Pediatric Emergency Department Staff in Point-of-Care Electroencephalogram Interpretation: Prospective Cohort Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/69395

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of guess rates and Likert confidence items across the three tests; the article reports "an improvement in confidence was observed across all occupational groups during the posttest" that largely remained at retention, with SMS consistently most confident.

> "Completion of the e-learning module was followed by a notable reduction in guess rates across all groups, with some senior staff not guessing any. However, at the 3-month retention test, guess rates increased again for all groups compared with posttest."
