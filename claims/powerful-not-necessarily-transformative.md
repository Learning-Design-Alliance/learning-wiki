## Evidence

### Bearman Margaret 2019

Bearman Margaret, Greenhill Jennene, Nestel Debra. (2019). The power of simulation: a large‐scale narrative analysis of learners’ experiences. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13747

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Discussion of the 327-narrative analysis: disorienting dilemmas from mistakes were common, but the authors report that "not many narratives contained the type of epistemic shift" associated with transformation; only 25/327 (8%) were coded as transformation narratives. The authors offer three possible explanations for the small number. No effect sizes are printed.

> "However, although disorienting dilemmas as a consequence of mistakes and failures were common, not many narratives contained the type of epistemic shift that Kegan suggests is associated with transformative experiences."
