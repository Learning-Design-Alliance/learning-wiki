`q2 i?` Applying a flavour-changing transformation such as s→u in Λ→Pe−ν̄ decay to a partial-symmetric Λ state produces a vector outside the Pauli-allowed Hilbert space; imposing complete symmetry of the wavefunction cures this and leads to the generalized Pauli principle.
