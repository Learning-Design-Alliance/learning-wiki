### Xu Linfang 2026

Xu Linfang, Zeng Lan, Liu Chunjuan, Zhu Jiayi, Qiu Mengying, Zhang Fengying. (2026). The effects of reciprocal peer practice with digital platform on undergraduate nursing students’ operational skills, critical thinking, and self-efficacy: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09169-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dimension-level analysis of the 32-item nasogastric feeding checklist in the quasi-experiment. The article reports significant between-group differences in four dimensions and "no statistically significant difference" for patient assessment and general evaluation; the authors note both groups scored near the upper limit on patient assessment at pretest.

> "Post-intervention comparisons showed statistically significant differences between the two groups in Dimension 1 (Material Preparation), Dimension 3 (Operational Skills), Dimension 4 (Humanistic Care), and Dimension 5 (Critical Thinking) (p < 0.05)."
