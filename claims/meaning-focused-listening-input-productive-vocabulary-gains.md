## Evidence

### Noughabi 2017

Mostafa Azari Noughabi. (2017). The Effect of Meaning-focused Listening Input on Iranian Intermediate EFL Learners' Productive Vocabulary Size. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(15). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1143810

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test comparing posttest Lex30 scores of the experimental group (meaning-focused listening input) and control group (traditional instruction) after 12 treatment sessions in a randomized pretest-treatment-posttest design. The article reports "significant differences were found between posttest scores"; no effect size is printed.

> "As Table 5 shows, with regard to productive vocabulary size, significant differences were found between posttest scores of the control group and ex perimental group; p=0.00<0.05, T(61)= -16.90."

### Noughabi 2017 (2)

Mostafa Azari Noughabi. (2017). The Effect of Meaning-focused Listening Input on Iranian Intermediate EFL Learners' Productive Vocabulary Size. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(15). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1143810

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest equivalence check in the same randomized experiment: control mean 30.38 (SD=1.94), experimental mean 30.56 (SD=2.10); the t-test showed "no significant difference betwe en the performance" of the groups at pretest.

> "As Table 3 suggest, no significant difference betwe en the performance of control group and experimental group in Lex30 at pretest phase of the study is seen; T(61)= -0.343, p= 0.73>0.05."
