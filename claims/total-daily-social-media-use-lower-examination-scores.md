`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mutually adjusted multivariable linear regression of self-reported examination percentage in the same cross-sectional sample of 364 students; the model explained 25.4% of variance (F(13, 350) = 9.17, p < 0.001). The article notes the linear-per-category specification implies an approximately 7.0-point five-category contrast that should not be read as a literal hours contrast.

> "each increment in the total daily use category was associated with examination scores 1.40 percentage points lower (B = −1.404, 95% CI − 2.053 to −0.756, p < 0.001, β = −0.282), the largest standardized association in the model"
