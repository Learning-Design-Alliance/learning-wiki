Evidence¶

Léonard Boussioux* 2026¶

Léonard Boussioux, Ziyi Zhao, and Kanghyun Cho*. (2026). One Tool, One Taste? How Vibe Coding Trades Collective Diversity for Individual Creativity. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38183 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.38183

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Perception-layer analysis joining post-task surveys from 58 of 73 builders (agency item n = 57) to artifact-layer originality scores. The article reports no detectable association between felt authorship, satisfaction, or perceived quality and embedding-based cohort atypicality, despite the rubric explicitly rewarding original design; equivalence was not tested.

"Among survey respondents, perceived human control, satisfaction, and perceived quality show no detectable association with embedding-based cohort atypicality."
