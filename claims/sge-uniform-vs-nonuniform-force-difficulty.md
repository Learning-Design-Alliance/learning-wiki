## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Written responses and interviews suggest many students do not realize that in a uniform external magnetic field the spin only precesses, whereas in the non-uniform SGE field a force can spatially separate angular momentum components; some also treat spin as an orbital degree of freedom. ## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Students’ Understanding of Stern Gerlach Experiment. arXiv:1602.06374 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interview evidence: one student drew a molecular-orbital diagram and another drew Larmor precession without mentioning the force due to the non-uniform field, believing the pattern would be a circle due to precession. The authors state responses "suggest that many students were unclear" about the uniform-versus-non-uniform field distinction and that "many students have difﬁculty realizing that spin is not an orbital degree of freedom."

> "Written responses and interviews suggest that many students were unclear about the fact that in a uniform external magnetic ﬁeld, the spin will only precess (if not in a stationary state) but in a non-uniform magnetic ﬁeld as in the SGE, there will be a force"
