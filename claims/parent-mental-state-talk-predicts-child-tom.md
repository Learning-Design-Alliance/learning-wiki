`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Adrián et al. (2007), a longitudinal finding that parents' frequent and elaborated mental state talk during shared book reading predicts children's later ToM performance; a meta-analysis ranks shared book reading among the most effective contexts for stimulating young children's ToM.

> "For example, Adrián et al. (2007) showed that mothers' mental state talk in shared book readings predicted their 3- to 7-year-old children's ToM-performance one year later, while the reverse relation was not supported."
