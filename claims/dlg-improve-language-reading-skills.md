# DLG implementation is associated with improved language and reading skills and more positive attitudes towards learning and reading

## Evidence

### Ruiz-Eugenio 2023

Ruiz-Eugenio, L., Soler-Gallart, M., Racionero-Plaza, S., & Padrós, M. (2023). Dialogic literary gatherings: A systematic review of evidence to overcome social and educational inequalities. Educational Research Review, 39, 100534. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100534

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

Implementation of DLGs with family involvement in a Mexican primary school in a highly underprivileged urban area preceded a "drastic increase in academic performance in language": basic reading proficiency rose from 17% to 55% and writing from 10% to 19%, and "In 2018, 23% (6% before DLG) of students obtained satisfactory or excellent results in language and communication, exceeding the national average by five points".

> "In 2018, 23% (6% before DLG) of students obtained satisfactory or excellent results in language and communication, exceeding the national average by five points"

### Ruiz-Eugenio 2023 (2)

Ruiz-Eugenio, L., Soler-Gallart, M., Racionero-Plaza, S., & Padrós, M. (2023). Dialogic literary gatherings: A systematic review of evidence to overcome social and educational inequalities. Educational Research Review, 39, 100534. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100534

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Díez-Palomar et al. (2020) analyzed attitudes towards learning of 418 students aged 7–11 (251 in DLGs) in UK and Italian schools using an attitude test; "78.3% of the students also responded that they could learn more when they listen to the ideas of others".

> "78.3% of the students also responded that they could learn more when they listen to the ideas of others"
