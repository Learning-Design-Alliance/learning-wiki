## Evidence

### Serçe Hüseyin 2026

Serçe Hüseyin, Aydoğmuş Mevlüt, Şentürk Cihad. (2026). Psychological predictors of professional readiness: modeling the effects of preservice teachers' 21st-century skills on self-efficacy and professional attitudes. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05039-7

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same SEM of 401 preservice teachers' self-reports; the path from 21st-century skills to attitudes toward the teaching profession was significant, "the predictive effect of twenty-first century skills on attitudes towards the teaching profession is 0.39" (β = 0.39, a moderate effect) with R2 = 0.15.

> "the predictive effect of twenty-first century skills on attitudes towards the teaching profession is 0.39 (95% CI [0.21; 0.65], p < 0.001). It is also seen that twenty-first century skills explain 15% of the variance in attitudes towards the teaching profession (R2 = 0.15)."
