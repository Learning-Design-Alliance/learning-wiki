### Wczysla Karolina 2024

Wczysla Karolina, Sparn Moritz, Schmied Bruno, Hahnloser Dieter, Bischofberger Stephan. (2024). There is a need for a paradigm shift in laparoscopic surgical training: results of a nationwide survey among teaching hospitals in Switzerland. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05209-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of Swiss teaching-hospital department heads (48 respondents). The Results section states "There is a mandatory training curriculum in 7 (15%) institutions" and 15 institutions (31%) offer structured assessment, with assessments varying greatly in form.

> "There is a mandatory training curriculum in 7 (15%) institutions; in 10, there is a defined practice plan for some specific skills, and in another 10, some unstructured exercises are available. Fifteen institutions (31%) offer a structured assessment of the training."
