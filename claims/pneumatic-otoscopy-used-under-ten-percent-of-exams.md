## Evidence

### Higgins Joyce Alanna 2019

Higgins Joyce Alanna, Raman Maya, Beaumont Jennifer L., Heiman Heather, Adler Mark, Schmidt Suzanne M. (2019). A survey comparison of educational interventions for teaching pneumatic otoscopy to medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1507-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-clerkship survey across the three randomized groups. Self-reported pneumatic otoscopy use was under 10% of ear exams for 68% of students, with group rates of 62%, 71%, and 70% and no significant difference (p = 0.612).

> "However, most students (68%) said they used pneumatic otoscopy in less than 10% of their ear exams (62% task trainer, 71% peer-to-peer, 70% video; p = 0.612)."
