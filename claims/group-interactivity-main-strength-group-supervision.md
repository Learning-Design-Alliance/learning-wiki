## Evidence

### Burke 2025

Burke, K. L. A., Snowdon, D. A., Milne, S. C., & Farlie, M. K. (2025). Exploring Physiotherapists' Perspectives of Group Clinical Supervision Effectiveness for Aquatic Physiotherapy Skill Development: A Qualitative Study. Physiotherapy Research International. https://doi.org/10.1002/pri.70044

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group findings on the group interactivity theme: participants valued "the diversity of opinions and ideas that the group shared" and learning from colleagues with varied aquatic experience, contrasting with the isolation of pool practice.

> "The main strength of group supervision sessions reported was the engaging, highly interactive environment in which participants could learn and develop skills in a clinical area that was otherwise very isolating."
