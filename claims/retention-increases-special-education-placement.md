## Evidence

### Nagaoka 2004

Nagaoka, J., & Roderick, M. (2004). Ending Social Promotion: The Effects of Retention. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/ending-social-promotion-effects-retention

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Tracking of retained cohorts under the CPS promotional policy shows an 18 percent special education placement rate within two years, "a rate three times that of low-achieving students prior to the introduction of the policy" and nearly three times higher than promoted low achievers.

> "On average, 18 percent of third and sixth graders retained in the 1997-1998, 1998-1999, and 1999-2000 school years were placed in special education within two years of the retention decision—a rate three times that of low-achieving students prior to the introduction of the policy"
