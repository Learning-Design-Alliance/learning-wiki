## Evidence

### Alsadi 2025

Alsadi, M., Alosta, M. R., Othman, E. H., Obiedallah, H., & Nashwan, A. J. (2025). Optimising Educational Practices in Simulation‐Based Nursing Education: Results of A National Survey. Nursing Open, 12(4), e70172. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70172

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey results from 966 Jordanian undergraduate nursing students responding to the 20-item NLN Simulation Design Scale on a 5-point Likert scale; the article reports the dimension means, including "the highest score (M = 3.67 ± 1.3)" for support.

> "Receiving “Support” during the simulation‐based learning had the highest score (M = 3.67 ± 1.3), then having clear “Objectives and Information” (M = 3.66 ± 1.22), “Fidelity” also had a high score (M = 3.64 ± 1.18)"
