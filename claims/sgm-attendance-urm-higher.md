Active Learning Outside the Classroom: Implementation and Outcomes of Peer-Led Team-Learning Workshops in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-01-0051

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparison of attendance distributions over eight semesters of voluntary-attendance workshops, reported in the Results section. Median attendance was three SGMs overall and four for URM and non-AP subsets; rates for non-AP versus AP groups were not different.

> "The frequency of SGM attendance by students from URM groups exceeded that of non-URM students (Figure 1A; chi-square = 13.8, df = 5, p = 0.02)."
