Evidence¶

Jerrim et al. 2015¶

John Jerrim, Helen Austerberry, Cosette Crisan, Anne Ingold, Candia Morgan, Dave Pratt, Cathy Smith, Meg Wiggins. (2015). Mathematics Mastery: Secondary Evaluation Report. Education Endowment Foundation. https://doi.org/10.1186/ISRCTN70922140

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Process evaluation using baseline and final questionnaires (214 then 138 teacher responses), focus groups, lesson observations and telephone interviews in five focus schools. It found wide variation in school responses but a shift in most schools towards problem-solving pedagogy.

"Nevertheless, there was evidence of a shift in most schools away from the teaching of procedures towards a problem-solving approach, involving increased use of discussion, objects, and diagrams."
