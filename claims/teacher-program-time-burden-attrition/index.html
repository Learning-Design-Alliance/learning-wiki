Joanne R. Beames 2023¶

Joanne R. Beames, Samantha Spanos, Anna Roberts, Lauren McGillivray, Sophie Li, Jill M. Newby, Bridianne O'Dea, Aliza Werner-Seidler. (2023). Intervention Programs Targeting the Mental Health, Professional Burnout, and/or Wellbeing of School Teachers: Systematic Review and Meta-Analyses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09720-w

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Descriptive review findings on program delivery demands across the 88 studies: programs were typically face-to-face, in groups, with external accredited facilitators, and followed structured manualised protocols.

"Most programs included 1–8 sessions that lasted between 1 and 4 h each, delivered over 1–12 weeks, in teachers' own time."

Joanne R. Beames 2023 (2)¶

Joanne R. Beames, Samantha Spanos, Anna Roberts, Lauren McGillivray, Sophie Li, Jill M. Newby, Bridianne O'Dea, Aliza Werner-Seidler. (2023). Intervention Programs Targeting the Mental Health, Professional Burnout, and/or Wellbeing of School Teachers: Systematic Review and Meta-Analyses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09720-w

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Attrition reported by 65 of the reviewed studies (73.86%). The authors note they could not formally test whether attrition was higher in more demanding programs due to small meta-analytic sample sizes.

"Our review showed that attrition rates reached as high as 66.67% in the program groups, with an overall mean of 12.69%."
