## Evidence

### AL-Edwan 2014

AL-Edwan, Zaid Suleiman. (2014). Effectiveness of Web Quest Strategy in acquiring geographic concepts among eighth grade students in Jordan. International Journal of Education and Development using Information and Communication Technology, 10(4), 31-46. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu/viewarticle.php?id=1874

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Semi-experimental two-group study of 119 eighth-grade students in Al-Zarqa, Jordan: 58 taught the Natural Resources unit via Web Quest Strategy, 61 via the traditional method for two months. Two Way ANCOVA controlling pre-test scores gave F = 6.408, significant at α = 0.05, "in favor of the experimental group".

> "Table (3) shows that there are differences with statistical significance at the significance level (α =0.05) between the means of the eighth grade students’ scores on the test of geographic concepts’ acquisition attribute to the method of teaching variable, F -value reached (6.408)"
