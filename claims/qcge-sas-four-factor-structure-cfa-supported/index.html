Evidence¶

Loris T. Jeitziner 2025¶

Loris T. Jeitziner, Lisa Paneth, Oliver Rack, Susanne Bleisch, Carmen Zahn. (2025). Measuring the quality of collaborative group engagement: Development and validation of the QCGE self-assessment scale (QCGE-SAS). Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09445-8

q3 · i? · design · r2

Confirmatory factor analysis (MLM robust estimator, N = 243 after three participants were excluded for missing responses) of the QCGE-SAS items, with the RMSEA of "0.069 with a 90% confidence interval of 0.051–0.088" indicating acceptable fit; CFI = 0.933, TLI = 0.911, SRMR = 0.062 were also reported.

"The robust root mean square error of approximation (RMSEA) was RMSEA = 0.069 with a 90% confidence interval of 0.051–0.088, indicating an acceptable fit (Hu & Bentler, 1999)."

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The authors' summary of the same confirmatory factor analysis, stating the "four-factor structure of the QCGE-SAS fits well", interpreted against the chi-squared test of 140.51 (df = 48) and the complementary fit indices reported in the Results section.

"In summary, the CFA shows that the four-factor structure of the QCGE-SAS fits well."
