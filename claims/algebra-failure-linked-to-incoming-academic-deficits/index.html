Evidence¶

Rickles 2016¶

Rickles, J., Heppen, J., Taylor, S., Allensworth, E., Michelman, V., Sorensen, N., Walters, K., & Clements, P. (2016). Getting Back on Track: Who Needs to Recover Algebra Credit After Ninth Grade? (Research Brief 3). American Institutes for Research & University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.air.org/CreditRecovery

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Descriptive analysis of composite middle school math and reading achievement test scores for the 36,423-student CPS cohort. Failed-Algebra students entered at the 39th math percentile versus the 53rd for those who passed, and 25% versus 19% were in the bottom reading quintile.

"a higher percentage of students who failed Algebra I than students who passed entered high school with math achievement in the bottom fifth of the district distribution (30% vs. 17%). On average, students who failed Algebra I entered ninth grade at the 39th percentile in math and students who passed Algebra I entered at the 53rd percentile."
