Prothero 2026¶

Prothero, L., Abdulmohdi, N., Shaw, S., Edmonds, M., and Meads, C. (2026). The Effectiveness of Simulation‐Based Versus Patient‐Based Clinical Education in Nursing: A Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis. Nursing Open 13, no. 9: e70689. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70689

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Outcome-assessment analysis across all included studies: every study used "one or more independent assessments of nursing ability, such as exams, OSCEs, knowledge tests, externally rated performance scores and clinical placement grades", and none assessed Kirkpatrick Level 4 type outcomes.

"No studies assessed patient‐based clinical outcomes, patient acceptability of nursing care given or staff resource time for teaching of the students."
