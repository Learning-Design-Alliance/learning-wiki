Shaun Bangay 2024¶

Shaun Bangay, Adam P.A. Cardilini, Sophie McKenzie, Maria Nicholas and Manjeet Singh. (2024). Review Ecosystems to access Educational XR Experiences: a Scoping Review. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17243

The scoping review reports, citing its included sources, that reviews of eduXR experiences "reveal human in- terface issues, motion sickness and problems with interaction affordances". This is a narrative synthesis statement attributed to source [40], not a new empirical measurement by this article.
