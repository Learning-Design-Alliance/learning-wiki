## Evidence

### Dreyer Tom 2025

Dreyer Tom, Papadopoulos Symeon, Wiesner Rudolf, Karay Yassin. (2025). Classroom teaching versus online teaching in physiology practical course – does this lead to different examination results? GMS Journal for Medical Education, 42(1), Doc8. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2025-42/zma001732.shtml

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of exam test scores per topic between the face-to-face group (FtFgroup) and the online Zoom group (Ogroup) among 198 randomly assigned fourth-semester students, using a two-sided t-test. The FtFgroup mean exceeded the Ogroup mean, "The effect size was low (Cohen's d=0.135)".

> "In comparison with the Ogroup (M=7.02), the FtFgroup (M=7.38) achieved a significantly higher test score on average. The effect size was low (Cohen's d=0.135)."
