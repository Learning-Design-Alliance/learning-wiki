# Class-average achievement shows a small negative indirect effect on subsequent mathematics achievement mediated through mathematics self-concept

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In an exploratory mediation analysis, the effect of class-average achievement on subsequent mathematics achievement through mathematics self-concept was small, negative, and statistically significant in all three countries. [→ Televantou 2023](#televantou-2023)

## Evidence

### Televantou 2023

Televantou, I., Marsh, H. W., Xu, K. M., Guo, J., & Dicke, T. (2023). Peer Spillover and Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effects with SIMS80: Revisiting a Historical Database Through the Lens of a Modern Methodological Perspective. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09816-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Exploratory supplementary path analysis with SIMS80 data modeling mathematics self-concept as mediator. The restricted multi-group estimate was βmed = -.052 (SD = .005, p < .001), suggesting "at least part of the effect of class-average achievement is mediated via mathematics self-concept."

> "We found a small, statistically significant negative mediation effect for all three countries (Table 4). When equality restrictions of compositional and mediation effects were imposed for the three countries, the mediation effect was estimated as negative and statistically significant (βmed = − .052, SD = .005, p < .001)."
