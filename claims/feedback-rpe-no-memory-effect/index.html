Evidence¶

Maria A. Woitow 2026¶

Maria A. Woitow, Anthony I. Jang, Ben Eppinger, Matthew R. Nassar, Marcel Brass & Julia M. Rodriguez Buritica. (2026). Shared effects of one's own and others' experiences during reinforcement learning on episodic memory. npj Science of Learning 11:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00409-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-sample and paired-samples t-tests on feedback RPE slopes in 79 participants found no effect of current or previous feedback on memory in either condition (ps ≥ 0.054, corrected α = 0.0125); unsigned feedback RPEs and surprise/uncertainty parameters also showed no effect.

"We found no effect of the current (t) or previous trial ’s( t - 1 ) feedback on memory in either condition (Fig. 4c; ps ≥ 0.054; αcorrected= 0.0125, Supplementary Table 16)"
