Evidence¶

Yang Gao and Barbara E. Hanna 2016¶

Yang Gao and Barbara E. Hanna. (2016). Exploring optimal pronunciation teaching: Integrating instructional software into intermediate-level EFL classes in China. CALICO Journal. https://doi.org/10.1558/cj.v33i2.26054

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Planned contrasts on attitude-change scores (N = 60): Group 3 vs Group 2 "t(57) = 2.09, p < 0.05 (one-tailed)" and Group 3 vs Group 1 "t(57) = 1.8, p < 0.05 (one-tailed)"; Group 3's mean change was 5.3 (SD = 1.42).

"planned contrasts (Table 25) revealed that combined instruction significantly improved attitudes towards pronunciation compared to instructional software, t(57) = 2.09, p < 0.05 (one-tailed), and human-aided instruction, t(57) = 1.8, p < 0.05 (one-tailed)."

Yang Gao and Barbara E. Hanna 2016 (2)¶

Yang Gao and Barbara E. Hanna. (2016). Exploring optimal pronunciation teaching: Integrating instructional software into intermediate-level EFL classes in China. CALICO Journal. https://doi.org/10.1558/cj.v33i2.26054

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Repeated-measures one-way ANOVA on attitudinal surveys: the instruction-type effect on attitude change was "non-significant... F(2, 57) = 2.570, p > 0.05", though attitudes changed significantly over time (p < 0.05) and Levene's test showed equal variances.

"although Group 3 experienced the largest degree of mean changes, there was a non-significant effect of instruction types on changes of students’ attitudes towards pronunciation, F(2, 57) = 2.570, p > 0.05."
