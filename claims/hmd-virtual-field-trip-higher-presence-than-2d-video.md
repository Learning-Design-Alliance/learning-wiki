## Evidence

### Guido Makransky · Richard E. Mayer 2022

Guido Makransky · Richard E. Mayer. (2022). Benefits of Taking a Virtual Field Trip in Immersive Virtual Reality: Evidence for the Immersion Principle in Multimedia Learning. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:1771–1798. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09675-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Independent-samples t-test in the between-subjects experiment (49 HMD, 53 2D video students) testing Hypothesis 1. The "HMD group reported (M = 4.18, SD = 0.74) significantly higher presence than the video group", with d = 1.43, supporting Hypothesis 1.

> "the HMD group reported (M = 4.18, SD = 0.74) significantly higher presence than the video group (M = 2.91, SD = 1.01), t(100) = 7.171, p < 0.001, d = 1.43"
