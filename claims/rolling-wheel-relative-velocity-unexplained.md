## Evidence

### Lorenzo G. Rimoldini and Chandralekha Singh 2005

Lorenzo G. Rimoldini and Chandralekha Singh. (2005). Student understanding of rotational and rolling motion concepts. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview task on a rigid wheel rolling without slipping on a horizontal surface: "None of the students were able to explain the velocity of the top and bottom points relative to the ground." The authors attribute this to discomfort with linear relative motion concepts and lack of understanding that rolling without slipping means the bottom point is instantaneously at rest relative to the ground.
