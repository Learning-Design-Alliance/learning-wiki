## Evidence

### Veloso Sérgio Geraldo 2019

Veloso Sérgio Geraldo, Pereira Gabriel Santos, Vasconcelos Nathália Nascimento, Senger Maria Helena, de Faria Rosa Malena Delbone. (2019). Learning by teaching basic life support: a non-randomized controlled trial with medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1500-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-report questionnaire applied at different times (case after teaching, control before): 100% of case students versus 73% of controls felt able to apply BLS; on ability to teach others, "there was a great difference between case (97.1%) and control (62.2%)". Descriptive percentages, no inferential test reported.

> "As for the ability to apply knowledge and skills about BLS in a real situation, 94.6% of community participants felt capable after attending the course, as did 100% of students in the case group. In the control group, with only classroom learning and answering the question about 60 days after learning BLS and before teaching, only 73% felt able to perform these techniques."
