Narrative review statement in the brief's universal supports section. It notes that common MBI practices include deep breathing, body-centered awareness, mindful movement, pausing before speaking, compassion practice, and guided relaxation, but that "there is, however, a lot of variability in implementation of MBIs, such as in terms of program length."

> "The evidence base for MBIs with children and youths is not developed enough to provide definitive recommendations about specific practices within mindfulness approaches and programs."
