The article reports, citing Reif et al. [7], a survey of Physics Professors at the University of California, Berkeley asked to explain how acceleration changes along a simple pendulum trajectory; "approximately half of them noticed" the omission upon reconsideration, the other half did not.

> "A surpris ingly large fraction of professors incorrectly noted that the acceleration at the lowest point of the t rajectory is zero because they did not account for the centripetal acceleration."
