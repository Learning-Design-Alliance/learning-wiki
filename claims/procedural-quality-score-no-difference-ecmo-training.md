## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bouras Marwan 2026

Bouras Marwan, Senage Thomas, Meule Emma, David Charles-Henri, Guimbretière Guillaume, Cadiet Julien, Bazin Olivier, Rozec Bertrand, Lejus-Bourdeau Corinne, Vourc’h Mickael. (2026). Full-Scale high-fidelity simulation compared to procedural simulation workshop for veno-arterial ECMO cannulation during refractory cardiac arrest: a randomized controlled trial. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized trial, blinded video-based assessment using a Delphi-derived 17-point procedural quality grid found no significant between-group difference in total score (13.0/17 vs 12.0/17, "p = 0.15").

> "The procedural simulation workshop group achieved a median score of 13.0/17 (IQR 12.0–13.0), compared to 12.0/17 (IQR 11.5–12.5) in the high-fidelity simulation group (p = 0.15)"
