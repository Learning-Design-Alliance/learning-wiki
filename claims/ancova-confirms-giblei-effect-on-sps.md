## Evidence

### Chengere Ashebir Mekonnen 2025

Chengere Ashebir Mekonnen, Bono Beyene Dobo, Zinabu Samuel Assefa, Jilo Kedir Woliy. (2025). Enhancing secondary school students’ science process skills through guided inquiry-based laboratory activities in biology. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0320692

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on post-test SPS scores controlling for pretest scores in the quasi-experimental design. The article reports the significant effect (F = 12.36, p = .001) and concludes the difference was "likely due to the Guided Inquiry-Based Laboratory Experiments Enriched Instruction (GIBLEI) intervention".

> "the ANCOVA results revealed a significant effect of the intervention on SPS post-test scores, with an F-value of 12.36 and a p-value of.001 (p <  0.05)."
