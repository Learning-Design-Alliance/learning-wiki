Evidence¶

Schnaubert 2021¶

Schnaubert, L., Krukowski, S., & Bodemer, D. (2021). Assumptions and confidence of others: the impact of socio-cognitive information on metacognitive self-regulation. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09269-5

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Within-subject ANOVA with rank transformation on competence ratings across three partner confidence levels (uncertain, medium, certain). After Huynh–Feldt correction the effect was "highly significant effect of the partners' confidence level on the competence estimation ( F(1.79,105.32) = 73.49, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.56)"; the planned linear contrast also fit (F(1,59) = 109.39, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.65).

"we conducted Huynh–Feldt corrections on the within-subjects effect and found a highly significant effect of the partners' confidence level on the competence estimation ( F(1.79,105.32) = 73.49, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.56)."
