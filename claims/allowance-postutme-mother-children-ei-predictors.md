## Evidence

### Falade Joshua 2025

Falade Joshua, Adebayo Ezekiel Taiwo, Eegunranti Benjamin Adekunle, Fatusi Adesegun Olayiwola. (2025). Connecting Emotional Intelligence with Academic Performance and Family Dynamics among Fresh Undergraduates in a Health Sciences University: Preliminary Findings. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences 35(1). https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v35i1.7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression (Table 4) in the 560-respondent census: monthly allowance OR = 1.000 (p = 0.034), Post UTME OR = 0.889 (95% CI 0.842–0.938), mother's children OR = 0.481 (95% CI 0.319–0.725). The authors link allowances to reduced stress and smaller families to more individualized maternal attention.

> "The odds of having above-average EI increased by one unit with each additional scale of monthly allowance (p = 0.034). However, the odds decreased by 0.889 for each increase in Post UTME score (p < 0.001). Furthermore, the odds of above-average EI decreased by 0.481 for each additional child from the mother (OR = 0.481, p < 0.001)."
