# The CMS has a one-factor structure with five items for both metacognition and self-judgment accuracy scales

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Exploratory factor analysis of the CMS metacognition and self-judgment accuracy scales revealed a one-factor solution, with five retained items for metacognition (polite, serious, kind, affable, graceful) and ten for self-judgment accuracy. [→ dos Santos Kawata KH 2021](#dos-santos-kawata-kh-2021)
`q2 i?` Confirmatory factor analysis showed acceptable fit for the one-factor models for both CMS scales. [→ dos Santos Kawata KH 2021](#dos-santos-kawata-kh-2021)

## Evidence

### dos Santos Kawata KH 2021

dos Santos Kawata KH, Ueno Y, Hashimoto R, Yoshino S, Ohta K, Nishida A, Ando S, Nakatani H, Kasai K and Koike S (2021) Development of Metacognition in Adolescence: The Congruency-Based Metacognition Scale. Front. Psychol. 11:565231. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.565231

`q2 · i?`

Exploratory factor analysis (maximum likelihood estimation with Promax rotation) on data from 633 child-parent pairs identified a one-factor solution for both CMS scales. The retained metacognition items were five positive adjectives: "polite, " "serious, " "kind, " "affable, " and "graceful."

> "The second EFA for the CMS metacognition and self-judgment accuracy scales revealed one factor solution (eigenvalues = 2.13 and 2.67) explaining 35.4% and 26.7% of the variance, respectively."

### dos Santos Kawata KH 2021 (2)

dos Santos Kawata KH, Ueno Y, Hashimoto R, Yoshino S, Ohta K, Nishida A, Ando S, Nakatani H, Kasai K and Koike S (2021) Development of Metacognition in Adolescence: The Congruency-Based Metacognition Scale. Front. Psychol. 11:565231. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.565231

`q2 · i?`

Confirmatory factor analysis tested the one-factor model fit for the selected items. For the CMS metacognition, SRMR, GFI, and AGFI indicated good fit while CFI and RMSEA were slightly outside reference values; for self-judgment accuracy, RMSEA = 0.07 was acceptable fit.

> "For the CMS metacognition, SRMR = 0.05, GFI = 0.97, and AGFI= 0.92 were considered as good fit, while CFI = 0.89 and RMSEA= 0.11 were slightly below and above the reference, respectively."
