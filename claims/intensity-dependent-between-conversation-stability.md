# Between-conversation persona stability is intensity-dependent: high-intensity ADHD personas cluster tightly while moderate-intensity personas show the highest aggregate variability

## Evidence

### Jana Gonnermann-Müller 2026

Jana Gonnermann-Müller, Jennifer Haase, Nicolas Leins, Thomas Kosch, Sebastian Pokutta. (2026). LLM-Based Educational Simulation: Evaluating Temporal Student Persona Stability Across ADHD-Related Persona Specifications. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06307 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.06307](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.06307)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment I pooled distributions across 50 independent runs, five models, and three prompt formats in the education scenario. Moderate-intensity self-reports showed higher variability (M = 18.0, SD = 5.04, 14.0% of scale); observer ratings showed the same pattern (high M = 21.1, SD = 2.57; moderate M = 16.6, SD = 4.78). Variability is descriptive; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "High-intensity personas produced tight clustering across the pooled observations (education:M = 30.2, SD = 1.79)."
