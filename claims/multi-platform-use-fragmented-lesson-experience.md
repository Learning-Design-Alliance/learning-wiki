`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation from the pilot study of two ninth-grade classes using five tools concurrently: the article reports that switching between Miro, Wizer.me, Formative.com, PhET, and Google Classroom "was found to be confusing for both students and teachers". The discussion attributes fragmentation to cognitive overload, particularly in younger learners.

> "The switching between tools was found to be confusing for both students and teachers and reduced the overall effectiveness of the digital lesson flow."
