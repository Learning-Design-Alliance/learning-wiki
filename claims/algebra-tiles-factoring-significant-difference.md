## Evidence

### Larbi and Mavis 2016

Ernest Larbi, Okyere Mavis. (2016). The Use of Manipulatives in Mathematics Education. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.36. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1126428

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

T-test comparison of posttest factorisation scores in the same quasi-experiment (n=56). The article reports the difference as significant with "eta squared=0.285", stating 28.5% of score variation was explained by the instructional strategy; no control student scored above five while 28% of the experimental group did.

> "a significant difference in the mean score of the experimental group (M = 3.96, SD = 3.21) and the control group (M = 0.84, SD = 1.13); t (54) = 4.64, p= 0.00"
