# Mastery aims are strongly bound to autonomous reasons and only weakly to controlled reasons

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r1` · `q3` · `i1`–`i3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Mastery aims correlate strongly with autonomous reasons (r = 0.43 pooled; r = 0.60 in unanchored measurement) and weakly with controlled reasons (r = 0.17). [→ Janke 2024](#janke-2024)

## Evidence

### Janke 2024

Janke, S. (2024). Dynamic or Static Goal Regulation: Implications of Weak and Strong Bonds Between Autonomous/Controlled Reasons and Aims for Achievement Goal Striving. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09968-w

`q3 · i3` · `review · r3`

Meta-analytic pooling of Sommet and Elliot's (2017) three studies, which measured generalized reasons detached from aim items. The pooled "r = 0.60" exceeded the r = 0.43 found in anchored studies, implying roughly 36 percent shared variance between mastery aims and autonomous reasons.

> "The mean association between autonomous reasons and mastery aims found in their three studies with 1295 participants reached r = 0.60 (95% CI [0.54, 0.66], Q(2) = 6.08, p = 0.048, I2 = 67.61%)."

### Janke 2024 (2)

Janke, S. (2024). Dynamic or Static Goal Regulation: Implications of Weak and Strong Bonds Between Autonomous/Controlled Reasons and Aims for Achievement Goal Striving. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09968-w

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random-effects meta-analysis of five anchored goal-complex studies (n = 1510) pooling mastery-aim/controlled-reason correlations. The printed pooled value was "r = 0.17", far below the corresponding autonomous-reason association.

> "The mean association between mastery aims and controlled reasons reaches r = 0.17 (95% CI [0.08, 0.27], k = 5, n = 1510, Q(4) = 14.03, p = 0.007, I2 = 72.02%)"
