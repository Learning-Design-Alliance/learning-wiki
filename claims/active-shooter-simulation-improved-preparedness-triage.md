# An active shooter simulation with mass casualty triage improved nursing students' preparedness and triaging skills

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a quasi-experimental pre–post one-group study of 88 senior-level baccalaureate nursing students, a high-fidelity active shooter simulation with mass casualty triage improved post-intervention scores across most domains, with correct responses on ASE knowledge increasing from 57% to 74% and small-to-medium effect sizes observed. ## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre–post one-group study (Levey and Montenegro‐Montenegro 2025, United States) of a high-fidelity active shooter simulation followed by a mass casualty triage scenario including run–hide–fight training, public safety involvement, standardized victims, moulage, and structured debriefing, in 88 senior-level baccalaureate nursing students. The review reports "57% to 74%" and small-to-medium effect sizes without printing their values.

> "Post‐intervention scores improved across most domains. Correct responses on ASE knowledge (IS‐907) increased from 57% to 74%. Small‐to‐medium effect sizes observed for self‐regulation, ICS, triage, communication, confidence, and readiness. The intervention significantly improved students’ preparedness and triaging skills."
