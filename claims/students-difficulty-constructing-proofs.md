The review reports Senk's large-scale study asking 1520 high school geometry students to prove four theorems, two requiring only a single deduction beyond given information. Only 30% could prove three of the four; the review attributes similar results to other studies.

> "She found that only 30% of the sample were able to prove three of these four theorems and 29% of the sample were unable to produce a single proof."
