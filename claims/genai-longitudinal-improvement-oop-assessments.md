`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Longitudinal comparison reusing the same programming tests and exam questions as the 2025 evaluation of ChatGPT and Copilot. ChatGPT improved from below the lower quartile of the student distribution to exceeding the upper quartile.

> "Compared with the previous year's evaluation [4], substantial improvements were observed, particularly across ChatGPT and Copilot. Several recurring errors identified prev iously—including difficulties with queue -related tasks, inheritance hierarchies, constructor invocation, and object -oriented program structure —were no longer observed."
