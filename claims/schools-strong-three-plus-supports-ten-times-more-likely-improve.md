### Ehrlich 2016

Ehrlich, S.B., Pacchiano, D.M., Stein, A.G., & Luppescu, S. (2016). Essential organizational supports for early education: The development of a new survey tool to measure organizational conditions. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The brief reports, citing Bryk et al. (2010), that responses to the K-12 5Essentials surveys "strongly predicted which schools were most and least likely to improve over time." This is prior research reported as background for the early education adaptation, not a new analysis in this brief.
