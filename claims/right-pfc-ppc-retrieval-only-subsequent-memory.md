## Evidence

### Liu Xiaonan L. 2014

Liu Xiaonan L., Liang Peipeng, Li Kuncheng, Reder Lynne M. (2014). Brain Correlates of Testing Effect. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0092025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ROI analysis of initial-study activation for pairs later correct versus incorrect on Test 1, involving data from 15 participants. The article reports "Significant subsequent memory effects were found in left PFC, t(14) = 4.37, p = .001" and the other listed regions, with no effects in right PFC or right PPC.

> "Significant subsequent memory effects were found in left PFC, t(14) = 4.37, p = .001, left PPC, t(14) = 3.84, p = .001 and bilateral hippocampus, left, t(14) = 2.66, p = .01; right, t(14) = 2.12, p = .027.There were no significant effects in right PFC or right PPC, t's <1"
