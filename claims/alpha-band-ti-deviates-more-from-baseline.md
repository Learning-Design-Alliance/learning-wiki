Lesselroth Blake, Senst Benjamin, Hellaby Mark, Viriyopase Atthaphon, Narajeenron Khuansiri. (2025). Correlation Between Electroencephalogram Brain-to-Brain Synchronization and Team Strategies and Tools to Enhance Performance and Patient Safety Scores During Online Hexad Virtual Simulation-Based Interprofessional Education: Cross-Sectional Correlational Study. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison of communication TI statistics across professions and brain areas in the virtual SIMBIE sessions. The article states these results "suggest that the alpha-band communication TI of a student deviated more from baseline" than the all-frequency-band TI; no inferential test is reported.

> "The absolute values of the alpha-band communication TI of a student were larger than those of all frequency bands in most cases: 78% (14/18) during the 30 sessions and 67% (12/18) during the 9 sessions across the 6 professions and 3 brain areas."
