## Evidence

### Wassenaar T. M. 2021

Wassenaar T. M., Wheatley C. M., Beale N., Nichols T., Salvan P., Meaney A., Atherton K., Diaz-Ordaz K., Dawes H., Johansen-Berg H. (2021). The effect of a one-year vigorous physical activity intervention on fitness, cognitive performance and mental health in young adolescents: the Fit to Study cluster randomised controlled trial. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-021-01113-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Structural equation modelling in the cluster RCT tested the intervention effect on an executive function latent variable defined by colour-shape switching, Flanker and two-back accuracy. The article reports "no significant difference in EF skills" with SMD = 0.017 (95% CI: -0.14, 0.17).

> "The intervention group showed no significant difference in EF skills compared to the control group at posttest (SMD = 0.017, 95% CI: -0.14, 0.17)."

### Wassenaar T. M. 2021 (2)

Wassenaar T. M., Wheatley C. M., Beale N., Nichols T., Salvan P., Meaney A., Atherton K., Diaz-Ordaz K., Dawes H., Johansen-Berg H. (2021). The effect of a one-year vigorous physical activity intervention on fitness, cognitive performance and mental health in young adolescents: the Fit to Study cluster randomised controlled trial. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-021-01113-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ITT analysis of the cluster RCT's computer-based cognitive battery. The article reports no significant between-group differences in relational memory (SMD = -0.1) or processing speed (SMD = 0.04), though both improved across groups from pre to post-intervention.

> "However, no significant difference was observed in relational memory performance (SMD = -0.1, 95% CI: -0.25, 0.02) or processing speed (SMD = 0.04, 95% CI: -0.07, 0.15) between the intervention and control group at posttest."
