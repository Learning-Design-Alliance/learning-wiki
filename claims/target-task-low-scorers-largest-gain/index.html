Olaniyan 2015¶

Olaniyan, A. O., & Omosewo, E. O. (2015). Effects of a Target-Task Problem-Solving Model on Senior Secondary School Students' Performance in Physics. Science Education International, 25(4), 522-538. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086539

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA of post-test PTCE scores for the 60 experimental-group students by scoring level (N = 12 high, 18 medium, 30 low). Scoring Level F = 92.018, Sig. .000. Table 3 printed mean gains of 29.35 (low), 25.33 (high), 15.27 (medium); the text states "low scoring students perform better than high scoring students".

"Table 4 showed that the P value (0.000) is less than the P alpha level of 0.050 (P<0.050); therefore the null hypothesis is rejected."
