# Participants valued the AI as a relational buffer that softened interpersonal pressure when disagreement required concession, without expecting it to resolve conflict

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants considered AI intervention valuable when disagreement required someone to reconsider or concede a position, but did not expect relational buffering to eliminate disagreement or resolve conflict. [→ relational-1](#relational-1)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative workshop finding: participants described the AI as "a lubricant for team emotions" and reported that intervention from a non-judgmental AI could make concession feel less like interpersonal pressure; one participant said it "does help people become more willing to concede". Descriptive; no effect size printed.
