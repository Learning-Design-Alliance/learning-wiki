## Evidence

### Baran L 2025

Baran L, Öztürk H. (2025). The effects of video-based and blended learning on medication dosage calculation skills of paramedic students: A randomized, quasi-experimental study. Medicine 104(31):e43651. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000043651

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the same 2-group quasi-experiment, both groups rose from low pretest means (BL "31.54 ± 19.92", VBL "26.12 ± 16.18") to high posttest means, supporting hypothesis H1 that both methods positively affect MDC skill development.

> "According to pre- and post-MDCST scores, both BL and VBL techniques helped EMTP students improve their MDC skills. The mean score of the pretest was 31.54 ± 19.92 in the BL group and 26.12 ± 16.18 in the VBL group"
