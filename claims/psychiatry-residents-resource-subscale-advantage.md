## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Aydin Zeynep 2025

Aydin Zeynep, Borlu Arda, Durmuş Hasan, Çeti̇nkaya Fevziye. (2025). Mental health literacy among resident physicians in Türkiye: a neglected competency in medical training?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08243-y

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Univariate t-test in the cross-sectional survey comparing 13 psychiatry specialists with 292 others on the resource subscale. Table 7 prints Cohen's d = 0.82 for this contrast while the text reports d = 0.32; both values are printed in the article.

> "Psychiatry residents, including child and adolescent psychiatry, scored significantly higher in the resource subscale only (Table 7, MD = 0.96, 95% CI: 0.34–1.58, p = 0.010, d = 0.32)."
