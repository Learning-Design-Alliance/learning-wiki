## Evidence

### Cragg J 2021

Cragg J, Mushtaq F, Lal N, Garnham A, Hallissey M, Graham T, Shiralkar U. (2021). Surgical cognitive simulation improves real-world surgical performance: randomized study. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrab003

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial with complete objective data for seven intervention and seven control trainees; blinded assessors graded videos of laparoscopic cholecystectomy on porcine specimens before and 4 months after training. The study reports "procedure times (–15.98 min in intervention group versus –1.14 min in control group; P = 0.024)"; Table 2 prints Cohen's d = 0.1060 for time.
