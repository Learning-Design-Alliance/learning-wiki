## Evidence

### Freytag J 2021

Freytag J, Stroben F, Hautz WE, Penders D, Kämmer JE. (2021). Effects of using a cognitive aid on content and feasibility of debriefings of simulated emergencies. GMS J Med Educ 38(5):Doc95. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001491.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section (3.3) of the randomised trial: the CRM principles listed in TeamTAG were discussed more consistently in the intervention condition; groups discussed "a median of 5 (min=4; max=6) of the six TeamTAG principles" versus a median of 3 in control.

> "In the intervention condition, the groups discussed a median of 5 (min=4; max=6) of the six TeamTAG principles; in the control condition, a median of 3 (min=2; max=5) principles."

### Freytag J 2021 (2)

Freytag J, Stroben F, Hautz WE, Penders D, Kämmer JE. (2021). Effects of using a cognitive aid on content and feasibility of debriefings of simulated emergencies. GMS J Med Educ 38(5):Doc95. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001491.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Figure 3 heatmap analysis of repetition patterns across debriefings: control groups repeated topics more often, particularly "the principles 4, 7 and 12", while intervention groups mostly discussed new topics each debriefing (except instructed repetition in case 6).

> "The control groups, on the other hand, more frequently repeated CRM principles, especially the principles 4, 7 and 12 (role of leadership or team member, safe communication and re-evaluating situations), which are also mentioned in TeamTAG."
