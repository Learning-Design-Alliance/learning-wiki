The report's landscape analysis describes CalSAAS, created by Assembly Bill 1219 in 2019 for annual monitoring of certificated educator assignments, and identifies the exclusion of turnover and retention data as a gap. The future C2C Teacher Training and Retention Dashboard is delayed with no launch date set.

> "However, they exclude key data needed for workforce planning, including teacher turnover and retention data, because those data are not captured in the data-sharing agreement."
