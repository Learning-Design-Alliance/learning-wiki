# Learning emerges under interacting individual, environmental and task constraints

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Constraints — individual, environmental and task — are the boundaries that shape the emergence of attitudes, qualities and behaviours during learning, and their interaction forces learners toward stable, effective behaviour patterns. [→ Brymer 2022](#brymer-2022)
`q1 i?` Small changes in one part of the learner-environment system can lead to large-scale changes in behaviour, so educator manipulations can produce disproportionate learning effects. [→ Brymer 2022](#brymer-2022)

## Evidence

### Brymer 2022

Brymer, E., & Schweitzer, R. D. (2022). Learning clinical skills: an ecological perspective. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 27(3), 691-707. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10115-9

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical exposition drawing on Newell's (1986) classification of constraints into individual, environmental and task groups, applied to clinical pedagogy. The article adds that "A small change in one part of the learner-learning environment system can lead to large scale changes in behaviour".

> "The word constraint is a technical term used to define the boundaries which shape the emergence of attitudes, qualities and behaviours during learning and performance"
