### Jingyan Lu 2012

Jingyan Lu, Nancy Law. (2012). Online peer assessment: effects of cognitive and affective feedback. Instructional Science 40:257–275. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-011-9177-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the hierarchical multiple regression (N = 181), entering peer grading measures in step 2 added no significant variance, whereas peer feedback activities added significant variance ("adjusted DR2 = .142, p\ .01").
