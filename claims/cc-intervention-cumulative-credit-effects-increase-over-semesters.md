### Michael J. Weiss 2023

Michael J. Weiss, Marie-Andrée Somers, & Colin Hill. (2023). Empirical Benchmarks for Planning and Interpreting Causal Effects of Community College Interventions. Journal of Postsecondary Student Success 3 (1). https://doi.org/10.33009/fsop_jpss132759

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

FIRC reanalysis of MDRC's THE-RCT student-level RCT data (39 interventions, up to 65,604 students) estimating the cross-intervention distribution of true effects by outcome and semester. The article reports the mean cumulative-credit effect "increases to 0.89, 1.14, 1.43, 1.70, and 2.46 credits" across semesters 2–6.

> "the mean effect on cumulative credits earned starts at 0.47 credits in semester 1, and increases to 0.89, 1.14, 1.43, 1.70, and 2.46 credits, in semesters 2- 6, respectively."
