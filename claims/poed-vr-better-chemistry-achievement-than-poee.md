## Evidence

### Kuan‑Fu Chen 2025

Kuan‑Fu Chen, Gwo‑Jen Hwang, Mei‑Rong Alice Chen. (2025). Precision diagnosis in virtual learning contexts: a predict‑observe‑explain‑diagnose‑based approach to scientific inquiry. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10527-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 55 eighth graders (28 experimental, 27 control) learning factors affecting chemical reaction rate; ANCOVA with pretest as covariate. The article reports "the experimental group significantly outperformed the control group (F = 7.19, p = 0.01, η2 = 0.12)".

> "The results showed that the experimental group significantly outperformed the control group (F = 7.19, p = 0.01, η2 = 0.12), indicating that the POED-based VR approach could effectively enhance students' learning achievement in chemistry."
