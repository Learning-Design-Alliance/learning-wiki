Analysis of CPS student record files for fall 1999 cross-referenced with magnet school neighborhood zones, reported by race/ethnicity (Figure 4). The report prints the percentages 24, 28, 10, and 14 for multiple-school access among African-American, Asian, Latino, and white students respectively.

> "Approximately one-quarter of African-American and Asian students, however, live within the neighborhood zones of more than one magnet school, while only 10 percent of Latino students, and 14 percent of white students, live within the neighborhood zones of more than one magnet school."
