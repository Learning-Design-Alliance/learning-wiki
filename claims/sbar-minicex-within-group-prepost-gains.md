# Within the observation group, clinical communication, clinical thinking and mini-CEX ratings rose from pre-test to post-test in a pre-post comparison

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the observation group, clinical communication ability rose from 67.96 ± 7.89 to 75.19 ± 8.05, clinical thinking from 80.52 ± 8.91 to 84.60 ± 8.56, and mini-CEX rating from 86.34 ± 8.61 to 88.37 ± 8.57 (all p < 0.05). [→ Xu Bu 2025](#xu-bu-2025)

## Evidence

### Xu Bu 2025

Effect of the situation-background-assessment-recommendation model combined with the mini-CEX assessment on the clinical communication, clinical thinking and comprehensive clinical abilities of nursing interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08022-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group pre–post comparison in the observation group (n = 204) using paired t-tests; Table 6 prints t = −9.165 (p = 0.000), −4.716 (p = 0.000) and −2.392 (p = 0.017) for the three measures. No effect sizes are printed; no control-group pre–post comparison is reported.

> "The intra-group comparison also showed that in the observation group, the clinical communication, clinical thinking and mini-CEX assessment ratings of nursing interns were improved after intervention compared with those before intervention (p < 0.05)."
