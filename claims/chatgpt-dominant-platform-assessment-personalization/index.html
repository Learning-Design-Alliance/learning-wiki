AlSheikh MH 2026¶

AlSheikh MH, Zaini R, ALmulhem MA and Ahmad S (2026). Mapping artificial intelligence integration in higher education: a systematic review using the FACETS and SAMR frameworks. Front. Educ. 11:1871468. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1871468

q3 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of the 22 included studies' use cases. The review identifies assessment automation and personalized learning support as the dominant use cases, and separately reports "the current review identifies ChatGPT as the most widely adopted tool".

"Artificial Intelligence (AI) has predominantly been employed in tasks related to assessment (such as plagiarism detection, automated grading, and formative feedback) as well as in personalized learning support (including adaptive pathways and recommender systems)."
