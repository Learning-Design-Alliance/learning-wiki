Onwards and upwards: The development, piloting and validation of a new measure of academic tenacity- The Bolton Uni-Stride Scale (BUSS). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0235157

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Test-retest reliability sub-study within Study 2 using a small sample of 26 students retested after three weeks. The authors report "a test retest value of 0.70 at p < .001 was obtained for Tenacity and 0.77 at p < .001" for Self-composure.

> "Test-retest reliability was assessed at three weeks using Pearson correlation coefficients through a sample of 26 students. A test retest value of 0.70 at p < .001 was obtained for Tenacity and 0.77 at p < .001 was obtained for Self-composure for a period of 3 weeks."
