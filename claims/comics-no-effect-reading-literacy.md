## Evidence

### Kerneža and Košir 2016

Maja Kerneža and Katja Košir. (2016). Comics as a Literary-Didactic Method and Their Use for Reducing Gender Differences in Reading Literacy at the Primary Level of Education. c e p s Journal, Vol. 6, No. 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1129008

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 143 Slovenian pupils (73 experimental, 70 control) in grades 2–5, analysed with four-way repeated measures ANOVA. The article reports the measurement × inclusion interaction was not significant for either literacy measure: "did not significantly improve their result in reading comprehension and incoherence perception tests."

> "Compared to the pupils in the control group, the pupils in the experimental group did not significantly improve their result in reading comprehension and incoherence perception tests."
