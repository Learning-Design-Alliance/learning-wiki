# LLM-generated short-form reels improved quiz performance compared to long-form videos in a between-subjects study of 62 university students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students in the LLM group scored significantly higher on a 6-item comprehension quiz than students in the long-form video group. [→ Lazaros Stavrinou 2025](#lazaros-stavrinou-2025)

## Evidence

### Lazaros Stavrinou 2025

Lazaros Stavrinou, Argyris Constantinides, Marios Belk, Vasos Vassiliou, Fotis Liarokapis, and Marios Constantinides. 2025. The Reel Deal: Designing and Evaluating LLM-Generated Short-Form Educational Videos. CHIGreece 2025. https://doi.org/10.1145/3749012.3749048

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects experiment with 62 university students randomly assigned to long-form or ReelsEd reels conditions. The LLM group scored "M = 93.85%" versus 79.72% for the No-LLM group; a Mann-Whitney U test found the difference statistically significant (U = 736.50, p = 0.0001). No effect size is printed.

> "The mean quiz score for the LLM group was M = 93.85%, SD = 7.89, compared to M = 79.72%, SD = 16.98 for the No-LLM group, as illustrated in Figure 3 (left). A Mann-Whitney U test indicated that this difference was statistically significant, U = 736.50, p = 0.0001"
