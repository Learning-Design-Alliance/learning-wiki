## Evidence

### Olds T; Maher C; Blunden S; Matricciani L 2010

Olds T; Maher C; Blunden S; Matricciani L. (2010). Normative data on the sleep habits of Australian children and adolescents. SLEEP. https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/33.10.1381

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three-factor factorial ANOVA of 48h MARCA recall data from 4032 Australians aged 9-18 found age, day type and sex significantly associated with sleep duration; regression against age gave the printed rates, with "12 (95% confidence interval 10-13) min/ night per year of age on school days".

> "Sleep duration declined with age at the rate of 12 (95% confidence interval 10-13) min/ night per year of age on school days, but at only 4 (2-6) min/ year of age on non-school days."
