## Evidence

### Yi Da 2025

Yi Da, Xu Xing, Zhang Qingqi, Ma Ai, Liu Xiaoqian, Lian Cheng. (2025). The moderating role of academic achievement attribution in shaping the impact of negative life events on adolescent problem behaviors and school dropout tendency. BMC Psychology 13. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03650-8

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of the 1,012-student survey found both indirect paths significant: "β = 0.545" via internalizing and "β = 0.459" via externalizing behaviors, with the direct path non-significant, indicating full mediation.

> "negative life events affected dropout tendency through internalizing problem behaviors (β = 0.545, SE = 0.069, 95% CI [0.410, 0.680], p < 0.001) and through externalizing problem behaviors (β = 0.459, SE = 0.067, 95% CI [0.327, 0.591], p < 0.001), supporting Hypotheses 2a and 2b."
