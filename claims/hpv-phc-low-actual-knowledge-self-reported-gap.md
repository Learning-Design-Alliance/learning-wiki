### Nilsen Karin 2017

Nilsen Karin, Aasland Olaf Gjerløw, Klouman Elise. (2017). The HPV vaccine: knowledge and attitudes among public health nurses and general practitioners in Northern Norway after introduction of the vaccine in the school-based vaccination programme. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2017.1358433

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study among public health nurses and GPs in Northern Norway, one year after the HPV vaccine entered the school-based programme. The discussion reports self-reported knowledge exceeded actual knowledge and that "barely half of these PHC professionals knew that HPV infection causes cervical cancer".

> "Interestingly, self-reported knowledge was much higher than actual knowledge in both professional groups, and barely half of these PHC professionals knew that HPV infection causes cervical cancer."
