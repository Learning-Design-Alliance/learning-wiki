Descriptive comparison of incoming achievement across CPS program types. The report also finds these students do not necessarily come from more advantaged families: IB students have "strikingly similar demographic and socioeconomic characteristics to other students in their school."

"Students in selective enrollment high schools have much higher eighth-grade test scores than students in IB and AP programs in neighborhood high schools. Thus, AP and IB programs seem to be filling an important gap in neighborhood high schools for students who have higher-than-average achievement but still may not be able to gain admission to highly competitive selective enrollment high schools."
