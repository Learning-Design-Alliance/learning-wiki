Descriptive statistics comparing families of children with a DAWBA research diagnosis of ADHD at age 7 (175 children, 2.13% of the sample) with those without; the ADHD group also had 83.9% boys vs 50.5% and more council/HA housing (17.8% vs 10.1%). These are descriptive percentages with no test reported.
