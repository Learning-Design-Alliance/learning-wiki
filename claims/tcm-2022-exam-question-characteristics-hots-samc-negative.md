Tseng Liang-Wei, Lu Yi-Chin, Tseng Liang-Chi, Chen Yu-Chun, Chen Hsing-Yu. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT-4 on Taiwanese Traditional Chinese Medicine Licensing Examinations: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/58897

Descriptive characterization of 476 questions (480 minus 4 image-related) from the 2022 examination using modified Bloom's taxonomy, Webb's DOK, question type, vignette style, and polarity, classified independently by two reviewers with consensus resolution. Table 1 reports SAMC at 92.2% and negative-choice at 62.2%.
