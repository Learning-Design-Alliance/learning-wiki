Bardin's content analysis of pre-simulation expectation narratives (Table 2) yielded categories including high expectations, communication development and humanization, reflection, practice opportunity, and "anxiety and nervousness, with feelings of self-doubt and insecurity".

> "The main categories show students with high motivation and acknowledgment of the potential learning with simulations as well its importance for personal development. On the other hand, anxiety and nervousness, with feelings of self-doubt and insecurity also came as a theme."
