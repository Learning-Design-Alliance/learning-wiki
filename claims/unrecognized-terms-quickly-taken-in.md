Informal observational report from game sessions at the summer school; the authors describe noticing rapid uptake of unfamiliar terms by group members. No assessment data, counts, or tests are printed.

> "Sometimes, a specific term may not be recognized by some of the members of a certain group. However, we noticed that the said term is quickly taken in by those members, as evidenced by their future recognition of that, as well as similar terms."
