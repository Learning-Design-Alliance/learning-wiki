Person-centered and qualitative approaches to network analysis in physics education research. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020132)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review describes a mixed-methods egocentric study of medical students entering clinical clerkships, with interviews and participant sociograms at the beginning and four-month point. It reports "the networks did not significantly grow" while role composition shifted toward doctors and same-cohort peers.

> "The analysis compared network size and role composition between time points and found that the networks did not significantly grow, but the distribution of roles changed, with ties shifting away from nurses and near-peers (students in other years) and toward doctors and peers (students in the same cohort)."
