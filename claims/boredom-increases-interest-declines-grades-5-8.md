# Latent boredom means increased and latent interest means declined from grade 5 to grade 8 in both domains

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Estimated latent means showed boredom steadily inclining and interest declining from T1 to T4, with higher values in German than mathematics. [→ Katharina Luisa Boehme 2025](#katharina-luisa-boehme-2025)

## Evidence

### Katharina Luisa Boehme 2025

Katharina Luisa Boehme, Thomas Goetz, Markus Feuchter, Franzis Preckel. (2025). Is Boredom the Opposite of Interest? A Longitudinal Reciprocal Effect Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09991-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent means from the combined longitudinal measurement invariance model (N = 1421). Boredom rose in German from M = 2.43 to 3.04 and in mathematics from 0.80 to 1.53; interest declined in German from 5.46 to 4.86 and in mathematics from 2.63 to 1.74. No effect size was printed for these trends.

> "Our combined measurement invariance model for both domains showed tendencies for subject-specific boredom to increase, and subject-specific interest to decrease over time."
