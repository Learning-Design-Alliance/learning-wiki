## Evidence

### Naghshi Maryam 2024

Naghshi Maryam, Janati Ali, Raoofi Samira, Khodayari-Zarnaq Rahim. (2024). Exploring the Challenges of Implementing Managers' Competency Assessment Center in the Health System: A Phenomenological Study. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v34i6.2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the extra-organizational challenges sub-theme of the interview study, a participant (M. 3) described the center's committee as temporary and lacking an organizational chart, making it an unstable mechanism; another (M. 10) said foreign models are copied without considering local needs and requirements.

> "The committee for this task is temporary and has no organizational chart. This makes it an unstable mechanism."

### Naghshi Maryam 2024 (2)

Naghshi Maryam, Janati Ali, Raoofi Samira, Khodayari-Zarnaq Rahim. (2024). Exploring the Challenges of Implementing Managers' Competency Assessment Center in the Health System: A Phenomenological Study. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v34i6.2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the political challenges sub-theme, a participant (M. 4) described political culture enabling position acquisition based on outside connections and views. The sub-theme also includes political instability in the country and failure to assess senior managers.

> "Our political culture makes some people have connections with people outside the organization and get positions based on their views, and senior managers are not introduced to the organization."
