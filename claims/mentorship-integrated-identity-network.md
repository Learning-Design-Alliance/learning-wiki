Behdokht Kiafar and Roghayeh Leila Barmaki. 2018. Understanding Computing Identity Development Through Mentorship and Epistemic Network Analysis. In Proceedings of the ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education (SIGCSE TS 2027). Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.16904](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.16904)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ENA network modeling of coded open-ended survey responses from 37 computing-field participants, grouped by mentorship status. The Mentored group's network showed its strongest connections among the four supportive constructs, and "Competence was also the largest node in this network". SVD1 explained 49.4% and SVD2 33.2% of variance.

> "In the Mentored network, the strongest connections appeared among Interest, Competence, Recognition, and Sense of Belonging. Competence was also the largest node in this network, indicating that it appeared most frequently in the Mentored group's responses."
