Human emergent coding of 167 modern physics syllabi from 127 US research-intensive institutions identified topic frequency. The coding scheme encompassed sixteen topics including "thermal physics, relativity, quantum mechanics, atomic physics, nuclear physics" among others, with quantum, atomic, and relativity most frequent.

> "The results of the syllabi analysis concluded that quantum (94%), atomic (83%), and relativity (70%) were the most commonly taught topics in modern physics within the US, as shown in Figure 1."
