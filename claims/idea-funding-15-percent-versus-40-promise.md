## Evidence

### Krausen 2020

Krausen, K., Caparas, R., Ripma, T., & Willis, J. (2020). Strategic resource allocation for the 21st century: How state leaders can address the silent recession. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/strategic-resource-allocation/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report's federal funding section states that when IDEA was enacted in 1975 Congress promised funding equal to 40 percent of the national average per-pupil expenditure, but "Congress has provided the equivalent of only about 15 percent of the national average per-pupil expenditure," citing the U.S. Department of Education (2018a) and Griffith (2015).

> "However, since then, Congress has provided the equivalent of only about 15 percent of the national average per-pupil expenditure — just over a third of the initial promise"
