## Evidence

### Ginsburg S 2014

Ginsburg S. (2014). Duty hours as viewed through a professionalism lens. BMC Medical Education 14(Suppl 1):S15. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-S1-S15

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the Coverdill study of surgical residents under duty-hour restrictions: only a minority thought it acceptable to pass off work to night teams, and few reported receiving guidance on how to make stay-or-go decisions. Residents wanted to rely on their own discretion as part of developing professionalism. No effect size is printed.

> "However, Coverdill and colleagues found that, more than five years later, residents still felt that working longer hours was more professional."
