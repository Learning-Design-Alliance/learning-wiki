Narrative review synthesising the few evaluations of PBL implementation in dietetic education (refs 61–65), reporting perceived outcomes attributed to those studies; perceptions, not tested performance outcomes, and no effect sizes are printed.

> "These studies generally found that PBL students perceived that they developed stronger thinking and problem‐solving skills, more effective communication skills and a greater sense of personal responsibility and collaborative learning than the students who received didactic instruction."
