## Evidence

### Nilsson C. 2017

Nilsson C., Sorensen J. L., Konge L., Westen M., Stadeager M., Ottesen B., Bjerrum F. (2017). Simulation-based camera navigation training in laparoscopy—a randomized trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-016-5210-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized single-center superiority trial with 36 randomized surgical novices assessed during an outpatient laparoscopic cholecystectomy using an OSA-CNS tool; the transfer test found no significant difference between the three groups (p = 0.279).

> "No significant difference in total score on the transfer test was found between the groups (p = 0.279). The mean total score was 14.0 (95% CI, 11.9–16.1) for the camera group, 12.3 (95% CI, 10.7–14.0) for the procedure group, and 14.3 (95% CI, 11.9–16.6) for the control group."
