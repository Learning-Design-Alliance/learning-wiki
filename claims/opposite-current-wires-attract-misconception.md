## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2017

Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Improving Students' Understanding of Magnetism. Originally published 2007. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1701.01523](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1701.01523)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Lecture-demonstration based interviews about the force between two parallel wires carrying opposite currents: "many students explicitly predicted that the wires would attract and come closer to each other", and none could reconcile prediction and observation after seeing repulsion.

> "Even in the individual lecture-demonstration based interviews, many students explicitly predicted that the wires would attract and come closer to each other."

### Chandralekha Singh 2017 (2)

Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Improving Students' Understanding of Magnetism. Originally published 2007. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1701.01523](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1701.01523)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview probing of students who predicted repulsion correctly: "none of the interviewed students" could explain the observation "based upon the force on a current carrying wire", even when explicitly asked to do so.

> "none of the interviewed students who correctly predicted that the wires carrying current in opposite directions would repel could explain this observation based upon the force on a current carrying wire"
