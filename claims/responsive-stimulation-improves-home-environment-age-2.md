## Evidence

### Finch 2016

Finch, J. E., Obradović, J., & Yousafzai, A. (2016). Home environment quality mediates the effects of an early intervention on children's social-emotional development in rural Pakistan. SREE Spring 2016 Conference abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567225

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Path analysis in Mplus 7.3 of the PEDS trial follow-up, with 1302 children clustered within 80 Lady Health Workers, controlling for family wealth, food insecurity, child gender, maternal education, family size, and the enhanced nutrition intervention. The intervention showed a significant positive path to home environment quality at 24 months, "β = 0.385, SE = 0.028, p <.001".

> "Controlling for all covariates, the responsive stimulation intervention was significantly associated with children's home environment quality at age 2 (β = 0.385, SE = 0.028, p <.001)"
