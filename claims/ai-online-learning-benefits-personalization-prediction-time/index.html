q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of benefits across cited studies (Osorio et al., 2024; Singh et al., 2025), naming "personalization of learning, predictive analytics, and potential reduction in instructor time on tasks." This is the review's narrative synthesis, not a pooled quantitative estimate.

"The review identified key benefits when using AI in online learning include personalization of learning, predictive analytics, and potential reduction in instructor time on tasks, which can allow instructors to focus on addressing students' higher-order learning needs"

Jennifer Lock 2025 (2)¶

Jennifer Lock, Soraya Arteaga, Carol Johnson. (2025). Integrating AI in Online Learning in Higher Education: A Literature Review. International Journal on Innovations in Online Education, 9(1). https://doi.org/10.1615/IntJInnovOnlineEdu.2026060433

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing González-Rico & Lluch Sintes (2024), that students using ChatGPT alongside teacher tutoring experienced a "greater perception of learning and improvement of their skills" than ChatGPT alone. Reported as the reviewed study's finding; no effect size is printed.

"students who used ChatGPT alongside teacher tutoring experienced a "greater perception of learning and improvement of their skills" than those merely using ChatGPT by itself"
