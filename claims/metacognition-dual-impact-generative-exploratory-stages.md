# Metacognition may limit creativity during the generative stage but facilitate it during the exploratory stage

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Metacognition might limit creativity during the generative stage by constraining spontaneous new associations, while facilitating creativity during the exploratory stage by enabling assessment of appropriateness. [→ Preiss David D 2022](#preiss-david-d-2022)

## Evidence

### Preiss David D 2022

Preiss David D. (2022). Metacognition, Mind Wandering, and Cognitive Flexibility: Understanding Creativity. Journal of Intelligence. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9500634/

`q1 · i?`

Theoretical argument in the review: metacognition requires cognitive control and might set limits for creativity by not giving enough room for the mind to wander, while metacognition might be necessary for creativity during the exploratory stage and the evaluation of creative products. The article offers this as a reasoned hypothesis, not a tested result.

> "on the one hand, there may exist trade-offs between metacognition and creativity during generation of ideas and, on the other hand, there may exist metacognitive facilitation of creativity during the exploration of ideas."
