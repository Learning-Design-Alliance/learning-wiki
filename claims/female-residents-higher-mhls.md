## Evidence

### Aydin Zeynep 2025

Aydin Zeynep, Borlu Arda, Durmuş Hasan, Çeti̇nkaya Fevziye. (2025). Mental health literacy among resident physicians in Türkiye: a neglected competency in medical training?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08243-y

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Univariate t-test comparison in the cross-sectional survey (n = 305; 187 female, 118 male). The article reports "d = 0.31" for the total scale and, in Table 5, d = 0.29 for the knowledge subscale (p = 0.016).

> "Female residents scored on average 0.89 points higher than males on the MHLS total scale (95% CI: 0.20–1.58, p = 0.009, d = 0.31)."
