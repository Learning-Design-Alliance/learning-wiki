## Evidence

### Kölbel Benny 2024

Kölbel Benny, Ragnitz Julian, Schäle Kevin, Witzenhausen Moritz, Axt Steffen, Beltzer Christian. (2024). 3D vs. 2D-4 K: Performance and self-perception of laparoscopic novices in a randomized prospective teaching intervention using standard tasks and box trainers. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03515-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized two-arm teaching intervention (n = 19 per arm), the ANOVA on failure rates showed the groups "did not differ significantly" with F (1, 36) = 3,32, p = 0.077, partial η2 = 0.09. The authors note a larger sample might have shown differences.

> "The intervention groups did not differ significantly regarding failure rates for tasks 1 and 2, F (1, 36) = 3,32, p = 0.077, partial η2 = 0.09."
