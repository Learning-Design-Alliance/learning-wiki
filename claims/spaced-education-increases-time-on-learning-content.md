## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Third-year dental students receiving spaced education e-mails spent significantly more time on the learning content (216.2 ± 123.9 min) than the traditional group (58.4 ± 94.8 min, p < .0005). [→ Nkenke Emeka 2012](#nkenke-emeka-2012)

## Evidence

### Nkenke Emeka 2012

Nkenke Emeka, Vairaktaris Elefterios, Bauersachs Anne, Eitner Stephan, Budach Alexander, Knipfer Christian, Stelzle Florian. (2012). Spaced education activates students in a theoretical radiological science course: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-32

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized pilot study of 42 dental students assigned to a traditional or spaced education group; self-documented time on learning content compared with the Mann–Whitney-U test, showing the spaced group "spent significantly more time (216.2 ± 123.9 min)".

> "During the theoretical radiological science course the spaced education group spent significantly more time (216.2 ± 123.9 min) on keeping busy with the learning content compared to the traditional group (58.4 ± 94.8 min, p < .0005)."
