Michael Hardy 2026¶

Michael Hardy. (2026). Autoscoring Anticlimax: A Meta-analytic Understanding of AI's Short-answer Shortcomings and Wording Weaknesses. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04820

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Bayesian re-estimation of the maximal item-varying random-effects meta-regression on 890 QWK observations. The gpt coefficient is "negative in the Bayesian specification (posterior mean≈−0.39 , with the 95% credible interval excluding 0 in the provided fit)". The abstract states decoders underperform encoders by 0.37.

Related Claims¶

  • Mid-range degradation is conditioned on task-specific adaptation: it is most severe for few-shot LLMs with few examples, decreases with more task-specific data, and is mildest for the fine-tuned encoder — related
  • LLMs cannot effectively perform in-context learning for short answer scoring, but cross-prompt pre-finetuning substantially boosts their zero-shot performance — related
  • Human-perceived item difficulty (human-human QWK) does not reliably predict LLM scoring difficulty on the same items — related
  • Item-by-implementation instability is substantial: a model can be reliable on some items and unreliable on others, varying across studies and training regimes — related
