## Evidence

### Sagasser MH 2016

Sagasser MH, Kramer AWM, Fluit CRMG, van Weel C, van der Vleuten CPM. (2016). Self-entrustment: how trainees' self-regulated learning supports participation in the workplace. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-016-9723-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Observation of debriefing sessions (26 min on average) plus interviews in the seven-practice study. Sessions exchanged contextual and medical knowledge, provided reassurance trainees explicitly sought ("resolving uncertainty"), and let supervisors gauge trainees' reasoning and ensure patient safety.

> "The debriefing sessions, which followed shortly after the encounters, fulfilled three purposes. First, they served to respond to informative questions from both trainees and supervisors."
