The article reports, citing Barbera (2013), an observational example of implausible order-of-magnitude judgments: the reported share is "75% of first year undergraduate chemistry students"; the article notes the answer does not make sense since a mole is 12 gr of carbon.

> "75% of first year undergraduate chemistry students after course in general chemistry thought the number of carbon atoms in a line across a punctuation dot being 6⋅1023, i.e. the number of atoms in a mole"
