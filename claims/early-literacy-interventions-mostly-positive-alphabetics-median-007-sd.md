Elias Walsh, John Deke, Silvia Robles, Andrei Streke, and Dan Thal. (2023). Using Bayesian Meta-Analysis to Explore the Components of Early Literacy Interventions. What Works Clearinghouse, NCEE, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Publication/WWC-2023008

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Bayesian meta-analysis of 100 findings from 29 WWC-reviewed studies of K-3 early literacy interventions, using independently developed alphabetics measures and adjustments for sampling error, design bias, and publication bias. The report states "about 72 percent of the impacts of early literacy interventions on independent alphabetics outcomes were positive" with a median of 0.07 SD, described as a 3-percentile-point gain for a median student.

> "about 72 percent of the impacts of early literacy interventions on independent alphabetics outcomes were positive, as shown in Figure 5. The median effect size was  0.07 standard deviations ."
