Gedamu Tigist Enyew. (2025). The impact of nutritional and socioeconomic status on academic performance of primary urban school children in northern Ethiopia: a cross-sectional study. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-025-02175-5

In the multivariate model of the 399-child cross-sectional study, father head of household versus mother head of household yielded an "AOR = 2.79, 95% CI: 1.55–5.04" for good academic performance, significant at p ≤ 0.05.

> "Being father of head of house (AOR = 2.79, 95% CI: 1.55–5.04) almost increased the academic achievement by three fold."
