The brief reports the new Parent PLUS caps and attributes to PEER the estimate that "29% of parent borrowers took out more debt than the new limits will allow." Percentages and dollar figures are printed; no effect size.

> "Parent PLUS loan borrowers will only be able to borrow $20,000 per year per student, and borrowing is capped at $65,000 per student.
