Zhou 2018¶

Zhen Zhou. (2018). An Empirical Study on the Influence of PBL Teaching Model on College Students’ Critical Thinking Ability. English Language Teaching, 11(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n4p15

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired samples T test of the experimental group before and after the 12-week PBL experiment, reported in Table 3. The article reports that for analysis, openness and justice the significance values are "less than .05, which means that there are significant differences"; no effect size is printed.

"In the analysis, the values of openness and justice are less than .05, which means that there are significant differences. The experimental group was taught by the PBL teaching model, and the three critical thinking temperaments are significantly promoted."
