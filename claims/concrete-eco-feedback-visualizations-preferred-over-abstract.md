Descriptive analysis of Likert attention, satisfaction, and preference ratings for four visualization formats (detailed metrics, relative savings, real-world equivalents, trade-off graph). "the more interpretive and outcome-oriented visualizations were better received than purely descriptive metrics"; 80.9% reported at least some attention.

> "the more interpretive and outcome-oriented visualizations were better received than purely descriptive metrics. Within the Your Impact visualization, real-world analogies framing carbon savings as everyday activities (e.g., driving a gasoline car or running a light bulb) were rated more favorably than abstract units"
