### Hollands 2020

Hollands, F., & Escueta, M. (2020). How research informs educational technology decision-making in higher education: the role of external research versus internal research. Education Tech Research Dev, 68, 163–180. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09678-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study coding thirteen types of activities named as research (Table 2). Both top activities were "internally conducted by personnel within the interviewees' own institutions," per the article.

> "The most frequently mentioned activities that educational technology decision-makers counted as research were conducting student, staff, and faculty interviews, surveys, or focus groups about educational technology issues, mentioned in 40% of interviews, followed by looking at student outcomes after implementing an educational technology strategy or product, mentioned in 38% of interviews."
