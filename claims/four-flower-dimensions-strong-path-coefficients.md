# Four FLOWER dimensions (open resources and information, leverage to remain motivated, working together, reflection and knowledge construction) have high positive and significant path coefficients on the e-learning experience

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In an SEM of survey data from 1200 medical students, working together, open resources and information, reflection and knowledge construction, and leverage to remain motivated each had high positive, statistically significant path coefficients on the e-learning experience. [→ Elshaer Ibrahim A. 2022](#elshaer-ibrahim-a-2022)

## Evidence

### Elshaer Ibrahim A. 2022

Elshaer Ibrahim A., Sobaih Abu Elnasr E. (2022). FLOWER: An Approach for Enhancing E-Learning Experience Amid COVID-19. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health 19(7), 3823. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19073823

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of an online survey of medical students at three Saudi public universities (n = 1200) using Blackboard. Path coefficients were "high positive and significant": working together β = 0.47, open resources and information β = 0.44, reflection and knowledge construction β = 0.43, leverage to remain motivated β = 0.41, all with p < 0.001.

> "However, four of the six FLOWER dimensions to improve the e-learning experience were found to have high positive and significant path coefficients: Leverage to remain motivated (β = 0.41, t-value = 8.987 with p < 0.001); open resources and information (β = 0.44, t-value = 9.753 with p < 0.001); working together (β = 0.47, t-value = 12.345 with p < 0.001); and reflection and knowledge construction (β = 0.43, t-value = 9.938 with p < 0.001)."
