## Evidence

### Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong 2020

Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong. (2020). The social brain of language: grounding second language learning in social interaction. npj Science of Learning 5:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0068-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review reports Legault et al.'s iVR study in which participants wore head-mounted displays to view and interact with objects/animals in a kitchen or zoo. Vocabulary attainment was significantly better than word-to-word association, kitchen words beat animal words (presumably due to more direct object manipulation), and struggling learners gained most. No effect size is printed.
