## Evidence

### Brechter Anna Maria 2026

Brechter Anna Maria, Schwab Roxana, Dold Christoph, Skala Christine, Schröder Maria, Schiestl Lina, Gillen Katharina, Brenner Walburgis, Hasenburg Annette, Schmidt Mona Wanda. (2026). Developing a Realistic and Cost-Effective Training Model (MaiSurge) for Laparoscopic Hysterectomies to Train and Assess Surgical Skill: Prospective Nonrandomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/66369

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Primary outcome of the prospective, nonrandomized, rater-blinded controlled trial: 2 trained blinded raters scored video-recorded performances using a 21-item modified H-OSATS with a maximum of 105 points, "with an ICC of 0.91" for interrater reliability.

> "The expert group obtained significantly higher procedure-specific modified H-OSATS scores compared to the novices (mean 74.0, SD 12.9 vs mean 60.3, SD 14.9; P=.049)."
