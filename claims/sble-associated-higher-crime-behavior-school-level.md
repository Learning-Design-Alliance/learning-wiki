### Fisher 2022

Fisher, B., & Petrosino, A. (2022). What a systematic review of 32 evaluations says about the impact of school-based law enforcement. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED621156

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic synthesis of 27 school-level studies of SBLE and crime/behavior outcomes. The review reports higher crime and behavior levels with SBLE, with the increase "driven by an increase in the use of exclusionary discipline, such as suspension." No effect size is printed in the brief.

> "Among the 27 studies that examined crime and behavior outcomes using schools as the unit of analysis, SBLE presence was associated with higher levels of crime and behavior . This result appears to have been driven by an increase in the use of exclusionary discipline, such as suspension."
