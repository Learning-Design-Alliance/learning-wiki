Evidence¶

Hauk and Matlen 2017¶

Shandy Hauk, Bryan Matlen. (2017). Exploration of the Factors that Support Learning: Web-based Activity and Testing Systems in Community College Algebra. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567766

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Updated analysis of post-semester MDTP post-test scores from the multi-site cluster randomized trial's practice semester, correcting for instructor clustering and algebra readiness pre-test scores. Treatment (adjusted M = 23.80, N = 80) exceeded control (adjusted M = 22.45, N = 147), but the difference was "not statistically different (Estimate = 0.93, p = 0.62)" with "Hedges' g = .12".

"students in the treatment condition (adjusted M = 23.80, unadjusted SD = 6.67, N = 80) performed higher on their post-test than students in the control condition (adjusted M = 22.45, unadjusted SD = 8.27, N = 147), albeit these mean scores, at about 1 point difference, were not statistically different (Estimate = 0.93, p = 0.62). The effect size for this difference was Hedges' g = .12"
