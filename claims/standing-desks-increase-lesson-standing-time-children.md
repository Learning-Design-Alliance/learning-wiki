## Evidence

### Wick Katharina 2018

Wick Katharina, Faude Oliver, Manes Susanne, Zahner Lukas, Donath Lars. (2018). I Can Stand Learning: A Controlled Pilot Intervention Study on the Effects of Increased Standing Time on Cognitive Function in Primary School Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15020356

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Accelerometer (ActiGraph wGT3X-BT) comparison over five schooldays in a non-randomized controlled pilot with two classes (IG n = 19, CG n = 19). Table 2 reports standing time lesson "t-Value (df)" −3.06 (36), p = 0.004, "Effect Size d" 0.99, with IG 60.5 min vs CG 47.1 min.

> "The measured sitting and standing time of the IG during lessons differed in comparison to the CG."
