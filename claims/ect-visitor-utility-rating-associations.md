`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression of ECT visitor overall utility ratings (33.8% rated very useful; n = 696 observations). Modality was not significant univariably or multivariably, though video/phone showed some evidence of lower ratings (OR 0.55, p = 0.07). Odds ratios printed; no standardised effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Multivariable associations of ECT visitor educational utility ratings ("5-Very useful") were the ECT visitor having previous experience as a GP supervisor (OR: 2.55, 95%CI: 1.27 to 5.13), ECT visitor gender (female, OR: 0.51, 95%CI: 0.29 to 0.91) and discussion of organisation and flow (OR: 1.53, 95%CI: 1.07 to 2.18)"
