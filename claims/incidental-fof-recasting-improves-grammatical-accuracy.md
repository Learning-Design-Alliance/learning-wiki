# Incidental focus on form via recasting during jigsaw retelling tasks improves EFL learners' overall grammatical accuracy

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group receiving incidental focus on form via recasting significantly outperformed the control group on overall grammatical accuracy as measured by a 50-item grammatical judgment test. [→ Abdollahzadeh 2015](#abdollahzadeh-2015)

## Evidence

### Abdollahzadeh 2015

Abdollahzadeh, S. (2015). The Effect of Incidental Focus on Form on EFL Learners’ Grammatical Accuracy. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(5). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.5p.40

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control group study of 80 female EFL learners aged 16-18 found that the experimental group, which received recast feedback during jigsaw retelling tasks over eight sessions, showed a "positive effect of incidental focus on form on grammatical accuracy of L2 learners" compared to the control group, which received no feedback.

> "The results at the end supported the hypotheses of the study and positive effect of incidental focus on form on grammatical accuracy of L2 learners."
