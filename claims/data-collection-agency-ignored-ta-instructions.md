## Evidence

### Anna McLean Phillips 2021

Anna McLean Phillips, Meagan Sundstrom, David G. Wu, and N. G. Holmes. (2021). Not engaging with problems in the lab: Students’ navigation of conflicting data and models. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phase 1 timing-of-data-collection coding (Fig. 6) showed most groups collected data throughout the session despite TA discouragement, while Group F collected the smallest amount of data in TA-suggested rounds and Group E took no multiple trials. The authors interpret this as differential uptake of agency.

> "The fact that most groups ignored the TA’s instructions is evi- dence that these groups took up agency that the Quiet Group E and the Uncertain Group F did not."
