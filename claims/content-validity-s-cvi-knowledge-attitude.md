# Expert panels rated the questionnaire's content validity as good, with S-CVI of 0.98 (knowledge) and 0.93 (attitude)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Nine independent experts rated content relevance on a four-point scale, yielding S-CVI 0.98 (I-CVI 0.89-1) for knowledge and S-CVI 0.93 (I-CVI 0.78-1) for attitude items. [→ Luo Taizhen 2025](#luo-taizhen-2025)

## Evidence

### Luo Taizhen 2025

Luo Taizhen, Gong Yang, Dai Sidi, Xia Hua-an, Jiang Zhiyan, Gao Lingling. (2025). Questionnaire development: midwives' knowledge and attitude on liberal birthing position in 2nd stage. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07466-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validity testing (Step 4) with 9 experts from Guangdong, Shanghai, Zhejiang, and Hong Kong rating each item's relevance on a four-point ordinal scale; the article reports the S-CVI and I-CVI values above and notes "Experts did not propose any changes."

> "The S-CVI for the knowledge section was 0.98, I-CVI was 0.89-1; The S-CVI for the attitude section was 0.93, I-CVI was 0.78-1. It means that the content validity is better [18]. Experts did not propose any changes."
