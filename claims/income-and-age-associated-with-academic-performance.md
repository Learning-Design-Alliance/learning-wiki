## Evidence

### Asmare Biachew 2018

Asmare Biachew, Taddele Mekuanint, Berihun Sileshi, Wagnew Fasil. (2018). Nutritional status and correlation with academic performance among primary school children, northwest Ethiopia. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3909-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on the 436-child cross-sectional sample identified age and monthly income as significant factors alongside nutritional indicators (WAZ and HAZ), all at p < 0.05. The article reports the printed AORs and CIs quoted here; no standardised effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "Age of the child (AOR = 0.177, 95% CI 0.07, 0.4), Monthly income less < 1000.00 birr (AOR = 0.05, 95% CI 0.02, 0.15), stunted children (AOR = 0.21, 95% CI 0.10, 0.43) and under-weight (AOR = 0.63, 95% CI 0.26, 0.84) were significantly associated with academic performance."
