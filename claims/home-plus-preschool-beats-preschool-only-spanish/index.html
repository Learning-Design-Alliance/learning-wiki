Evidence¶

Baker 2016¶

Baker, D. L., Mogna, V., Rodriguez, S., Farmer, D., & Yovanoff, P. (2016). Building the Oral Language of Young Hispanic Children Through Interactive Read Alouds and Vocabulary Games at Preschool and at Home. Journal of International Special Needs Education, 19(2), 81-94. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1195411

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Gain-score comparison within the treatment group of Study 1 between 6 children in the home plus preschool condition and 25 children in the preschool-only condition. The article reports d = 0.91 on Spanish RNA and d = 0.79 on Spanish RVK, both significant; English RNA and RVK gain comparisons were not significant.

"Differences on the Spanish RNA were 1.39 points, favoring the home plus preschool intervention group ( d ¼ 0.91). Differences on the Spanish RVK were 2.39 points favoring the home plus preschool intervention ( d ¼ 0.79)."
