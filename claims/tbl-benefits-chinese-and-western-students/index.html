Evidence¶

Xie Zhi-Bo 2025¶

Xie Zhi-Bo, Cheng Xin-Yu, Li Xiao-Yan, Zhang Yi-Fan. (2025). Team based learning pedagogy enhances the education quality: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07175-x

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup analysis of 6 studies focused on Chinese students and 20 studies on Western country students. Both groups showed significant improvements, and "Western students appeared to benefit more from the TBL method compared to Chinese students".

"Results showed that both groups benefited from the TBL class, with Chinese students (SMD = 0.58, 95%CI 0.23 to 0.93, I2 = 67.0%, random-effects model) and Western students (SMD = 1.07, 95%CI 0.76 to 1.38, I2 = 94.7%, random-effects model) showing significant improvements"
