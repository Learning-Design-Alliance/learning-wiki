Design-based research study with two macro cycles of design, experiment, and retrospective analysis. Participants completed surveys and interviews; qualitative data were coded using a grounded theory design with a constant comparative method, and "100% agreement was achieved" among the four researchers on revisions.

> "Conducted over two iterative macro cycles, this unique study engaged 114 participants, including faculty from a range of disciplines and institutional types, accreditation organizations and coordinators, and education and technology organizations."
