## Evidence

### Mohebi Meysam 2025

Mohebi Meysam, Moosavi Soolmaz, Hasandoost Fateme, Forouzanfar Mohammad Mehdi. (2025). Impact of an advanced trauma life support (ATLS) simulation program on nursing students’ clinical decision-making in trauma care: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08210-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive results from the quasi-experimental study (intervention n = 31, control n = 35): intervention group mean rose from 40.81 ± 3.22 to 52.24 ± 2.04; control from 39.06 ± 2.27 to 48.09 ± 1.97, "with a greater increase observed in the intervention group (Table 2)".

> "Descriptive results revealed that mean scores increased from pre- to post-intervention in both groups, with a greater increase observed in the intervention group (Table 2)."
