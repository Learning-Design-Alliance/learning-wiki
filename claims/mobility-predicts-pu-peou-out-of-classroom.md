## Evidence

### Chenxi Liu 2025

Chenxi Liu, Ana-Paula Correia, Young Min Kim. (2025). Determining mobile learning acceptance outside the classroom: an integrated acceptance model. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10514-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling of survey data from 639 valid MTurk responses tested the proposed model. The path analysis shows mobility significantly predicts PEOU (β = 0.59, p < 0.001) and PU (β = 0.37, p < 0.001), supporting H6a and H6b.

> "The latent path analysis supports variation of perceived ease of use (PEOU) is related to MO and Compatibility (COM) (β = 0.59, p < 0.001; β = 0.30, p < 0.01, respectively). Also, expected change of PU is associated with MO, COM, service quality (SQ), PEOU (β = 0.37, p < 0.001; β = 0.39, p < 0.001; β = 0.26, p < 0.001; β = 0.13, p < 0.05, respectively)."
