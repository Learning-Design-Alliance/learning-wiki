`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument from the section on training scientific generalists; the author states the program "should be thought of as being the ﬁrst step in a career-long trajectory" and calls for supporting postdoctoral infrastructure and special postdoctoral programs with generous several-year funding.

### Gopal P. Sarma 2017 (2)
