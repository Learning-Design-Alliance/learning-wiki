Sensitivity analysis recomputing the random-effects mean after excluding the five studies without known risk-of-bias domains. The restricted estimate "g = 1.14; 95% CI = [0.45, 1.83]" exceeded the all-studies mean, indicating inclusion of biased studies deflated the estimate.

"The random-effects mean effect size of only the five studies without known risks of bias (as identified through the Cochrane collaboration tool; see Table 1) was actually greater than the mean effect size that included all studies (g = 1.14; 95% CI = [0.45, 1.83])."
