## Evidence

### Peiris D. L. I. H. K. 2022

Peiris D. L. I. H. K., Duan Yanping, Vandelanotte Corneel, Liang Wei, Yang Min, Baker Julien Steven. (2022). Effects of In-Classroom Physical Activity Breaks on Children’s Academic Performance, Cognition, Health Behaviours and Health Outcomes: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19159479

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of a single study (Egger et al., two intervention arms, n = 188 students) evaluating spelling. The pooled SMD = 2.13 was statistically significant (p = 0.03) with low heterogeneity (I2 = 11%); the review hedges that breaks "might have an impact".

> "The result with a statistically significant, large, pooled effect estimate (SMD = 2.13, 95% CI [0.21 to 4.05], p = 0.03; I2 = 11%, p = 0.45) confirmed that the classroom-based physical activity breaks might have an impact on primary school kids’ spelling performance"
