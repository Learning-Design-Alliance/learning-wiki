Evidence¶

Alexander S. Browman 2025¶

Alexander S. Browman. (2025). Centering Social Fit, Self-Concept Fit, Goal Fit, and Resource Fit as Core Elements of Students' Experiences of Belonging at School. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10009-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of qualitative studies (e.g., Faircloth 2009 with ethnically diverse ninth-graders; Matthews et al. 2021 with Black and Latinx fifth- through twelfth-graders; Bettencourt 2021 and Fernández et al. 2023 with working-class university students) concludes "students, including those from a number of systematically marginalized groups" report belonging hinges on identity connection. One student stated, "In order to 'make it' at this school, I would have to dress differently and be someone that I'm not."

"students, including those from a number of systematically marginalized groups, are more likely to feel like they belong at their school when they feel able to maintain connections to their most valued identities or selves, both in the classroom and in the school environment more generally."
