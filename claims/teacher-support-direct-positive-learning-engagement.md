### Liu Zihan 2026

Liu Zihan, Tang Wenguang, Tang Liping, Ma Yankun. (2026). The relationship between perceived teacher support and learning engagement of Chinese adolescents: a moderated chain mediation model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03990-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of questionnaire data from 1,193 junior high students in Guangzhou, using PROCESS Model 6 with gender, grade, and class cadre status controlled, showed perceived teacher support significantly positively related to learning engagement (β = 0.17, p < 0.001).

> "The results (Fig. 2) showed that perceived teacher support is significantly positively related to classroom environment (β = 0.47, p < 0.001), mastery approach goal (β = 0.29, p < 0.001), and learning engagement (β = 0.17, p < 0.001)."
