Qualitative focus groups and a follow-up survey conducted in Phase 1 with CCC students, staff, and instructors to identify procurement pain points and priorities. Among the "26 students who participated in focus groups and a subsequent survey", students expressed desires for transparency, input on vetting criteria, equity centering, and ongoing involvement such as product testing.
