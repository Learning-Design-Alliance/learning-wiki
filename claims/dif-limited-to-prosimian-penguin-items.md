Federer Meghan Rector, Nehm Ross H., Pearl Dennis K. (2016). Examining Gender Differences in Written Assessment Tasks in Biology: A Case Study of Evolutionary Explanations. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

DIF analysis using the Mantel-Haenszel procedure and independent t tests of Rasch item difficulties (Table 5) found KC difficulty higher for males on two items; Table 5 prints Cohen's d of 0.18 (Prosimian) and 0.15 (Penguin), both at p < 0.05.

> "Independent analysis of KC and NI scores across the 12 ACORNS items revealed that the vast majority of items did not function differently across genders. However, measures of KCs for two items suggested possible performance differences between males and females: Prosimian (ELSP strand) and Penguin (SRPE strand; see Table 5)."
