## Evidence

### Kilgour 2016

Kilgour, J. M., & Saadia, T. (2016). An investigation into the optimal number of distractors in single-best answer exams. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 21(3), 571-585. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9652-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simulation analysis of point-biserial correlation coefficients across three exam paper models. Changes were statistically significant but negligible in magnitude, with some papers showing increases (Y04: 0.14 to 0.17) and others decreases (Y03: 0.16 to 0.15).

> "Mean item discrimination (Rpbis) changed in a less linear fashion than exam difficulty. For the year three paper, discrimination was equivalent across the five-option and four-option exam models at 0.16, but it decreased by a statistically significant degree to 0.15 for the three-option model (p < 0.017 five-option to three, p < 0.004 four-option to three; upper 95% CI of the difference for both −0.01)."
