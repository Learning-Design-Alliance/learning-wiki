Evidence¶

Herbert W. Marsh 2023¶

Herbert W. Marsh, Jiesi Guo, Philip D. Parker, Reinhard Pekrun, Geetanjali Basarkod, Theresa Dicke, et al. (2023). Peer Victimization: an Integrative Review and Cross-National Test of a Tripartite Model. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09765-x

q3 · i? · associational · r3

The authors' own substantive-methodological synergy analysis compared five structural models (one-, two-, and three-component CFA and ESEM) in PISA2018 data and reports "the superior explanatory power of the three-component model" across fit, differentiation, and discriminant validity with gender, attitudes, and well-being. No effect size is printed for the model comparison.

"We demonstrate the superior explanatory power of the three-component model—goodness-of-fit, component differentiation, and discriminant validity of the three components concerning gender differences, paradoxical anti-bullying attitudes"

Herbert W. Marsh 2023 (2)¶

Herbert W. Marsh, Jiesi Guo, Philip D. Parker, Reinhard Pekrun, Geetanjali Basarkod, Theresa Dicke, et al. (2023). Peer Victimization: an Integrative Review and Cross-National Test of a Tripartite Model. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09765-x

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Methods section: the empirical test drew on "nationally representative samples from 77 countries (594,196 fifteen-year-old students)", a two-stage sampling design with survey weights; three countries lacking victimization data were excluded.

"The PISA2018 data are based on nationally representative samples from 77 countries (594,196 fifteen-year-old students)."
