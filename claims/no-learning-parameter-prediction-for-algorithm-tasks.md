## Evidence

### Marinelli CV 2021

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regressions for algorithm/rule-based tasks (pseudo-word spelling, number judgment, transformation into digits, number order, dictation of numbers, computation strategies): "none of the multiple regression analyses were significant, as expected", overall R2 0.006–0.064.

> "In the case of tasks for which a relationship was not predicted, none of the multiple regression analyses were significant, as expected (the overall R 2 ranging from 0.006 to 0.064)"

### Marinelli CV 2021 (2)

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Control tests mapping non-verbal intelligence and short-term memory: "none of the models with the control tests (Raven Matrices and Digit span) proved significant", overall R2 0.005–0.092, and no individual parameter showed a significant contribution.

> "Finally, none of the models with the control tests (Raven Matrices and Digit span) proved significant (with overall R2 ranging from 0.005 to 0.092)"
