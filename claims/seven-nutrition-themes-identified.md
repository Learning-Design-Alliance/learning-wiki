`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive thematic content analysis of nutrition-related learning objectives extracted from Polish educational standards. Two researchers independently coded the objectives and grouped codes into seven main themes.

> "The following main themes were identified: (1) nutritional knowledge, (2) food technology, (3) nutritional assessment, (4) impact of diet on therapy, (5) medical nutrition therapy (MNT), (6) nutritional counselling, (7) prophylaxis."
