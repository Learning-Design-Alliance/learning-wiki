## Evidence

### P. Spangenberger 2025

P. Spangenberger, N. Matthes, F. Kapp, L. Kruse, J. L. Plass. (2025). Orchestrating iVR technology in an authentic classroom setting and its effects on factual knowledge, comprehension and transfer. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10409-2

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA on a six-item intrinsic motivation scale (ωpre = 0.803; ωpost = 0.832) in the classroom study. No significant interaction, F (1, 27) = .640, p = .431, η p2 = .023; Wilcoxon tests showed no significant within-group changes.

> "The analysis did not show an interaction effect for time and condition (F (1, 27) = .640, p = .431, η p2 = .023), indicating that increases in motivation did not differ between groups"
