## Evidence

### Arthur 1998

Arthur, W., Jr., Bennett, W., Jr., Stanush, P. L., & McNelly, T. L. (1998). Factors That Influence Skill Decay and Retention: A Quantitative Review and Analysis. Human Performance, 11(1), 57-101. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327043hup1101_3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis categorizing retention intervals into eight groups (Table 2) and computing corrected mean ds per interval (Table 3). The interval-vs-d correlation was "based on only eight data points" and equaled -0.51.

> "Furthermore, although it was based on only eight data points, the correlation between retention interval and corrected mean d (6) was -0.51. Consistent with the hypothesis, studies with longer retention intervals reported more skill loss."
