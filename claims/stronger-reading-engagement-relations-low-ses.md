## Evidence

### Guo 2015

Guo, Y., Sun, S., Breit-Smith, A., Morrison, F. J., & Connor, C. M. (2015). Behavioral engagement and reading achievement in elementary-school-age children: A longitudinal cross-lagged analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(2), 332–347. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/a0037638

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

A multigroup cross-lagged model compared low-SES (n = 234) and mid/high-SES (n = 853) children; the unconstrained model fit significantly better than the constrained model (⌬␹2 = 56.16, ⌬df = 11, p < .001), and four parameters differed across groups, including the preschool reading path.

> "Four parameters were identified as different across groups: the cross-lag path coefficient from preschool reading to first-grade behavioral engagement (⌬␹2 ⫽ 6.52, ⌬df ⫽ 1, p ⫽ .01)"
