Heterogeneity assessment in the re-analysis of Leary et al. (2013) data across 353 outcomes. The article reports the overall effect as "the overall effect size (g = 0.27)" with "I2 = 80.0%" of variability attributed to heterogeneity, warranting additional analyses of study differences.

"the overall heterogeneity is statistically significant, Q(352) = 1756.83, p = 0.01. It is quite large (Tau2 = 0.13) relative to the overall effect size (g = 0.27), and a large portion of that observed variability (I2 = 80.0%) can be attributed to heterogeneity"
