## Evidence

### Ma Irene W Y 2015

Ma Irene W Y, Pugh Debra, Mema Briseida, Brindle Mary E, Cooke Lara, Stromer Julie N. (2015). Use of an error-focused checklist to identify incompetence in lumbar puncture performances. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.12809

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

ROC analysis on the same 18 simulator LP performances. The article reports the conventional checklist was "high (AUC 0.89, 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.72–1.00)" for competence, while the error-focused tool was poor (AUC 0.15).

> "The accuracy of the conventional checklist in identifying competence was high (AUC 0.89, 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.72–1.00) in comparison with that of the error-focused checklist (AUC 0.15, 95% CI 0.00–0.33)."
