Binning N = 2118 v3.0 responses into 2-minute intervals (Fig. 4) showed score and alpha vary with time on task only at very low times; analyses retained students with at least 10 minutes and 5 or fewer unanswered items (N = 1612 for v3.0).

> "These data suggest that, for students who spend fewer than 10 minutes engaging with the test, there is a strong relationship between time on task and student score, as well as a distinct relationship between time on task and Cronbach's α for very low times."
