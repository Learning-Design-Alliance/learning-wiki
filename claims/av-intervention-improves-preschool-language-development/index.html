Evidence¶

Roul 2014¶

Sushanta Kumar Roul. (2014). Language Development of the Preschool Children: The Effects of an Audio-Visual Intervention Program in Delhi. International Journal of Instruction, 7(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085265

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Post-test comparison of an experimental group (mean 100.50 on language development total) and control group (mean 54.16), each of 50 children from one Delhi preschool, using the investigator-developed Language Development Test. The t-ratios were "12.69, 16.41 and 16.42" for listening comprehension, vocabulary and total.

"The t-ratio between the groups comes out to be 12.69, 16.41 and 16.42. It is significant beyond 0.01 as well as 0.05 level of significance. It indicates that the preschool children of the two groups differ significantly on language development in general."
