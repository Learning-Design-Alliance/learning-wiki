### Okabayashi 2022

Okabayashi, S., Kitazawa, K., Kawamura, T., & Nakayama, T. (2022). E-Learning Material of Evidence-Based Medicine for Laypersons. HLRP: Health Literacy Research and Practice, 6(4), e290-e299. https://doi.org/10.3928/24748307-20221113-01

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative semi-structured focus group interviews with the same 25 participants, divided into four to five interviewees per group; transcripts were coded and triangulated among authors, yielding "Ten categories and twenty-six codes" (Table 4).

> "The effectiveness of the e-learning material was heavily influenced by the story, characters, and whether the contents of the material were consistent with interviewees' daily interests."
