## Evidence

### Xiao Meixia 2026

Xiao Meixia, Gao Yujian, Wang Yashu, Lei Ziwei, Shi Shengming. (2026). A study of the effectiveness of the BOPPPS model combined with FC-CBL in the standardized training of general medicine residents. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1856743

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with 38 general practice residents (19 per group) over 12 weeks. Independent samples t-test showed the BOPPPS/FC-CBL group scored higher on theory tests, with "d = 0.76" reported as the effect size.

> "The BOPPPS/FC-CBL group demonstrated significantly higher scores than the LBL group in theory tests (82.46 ± 8.34 vs. 76.82 ± 7.13, p = 0.03, d = 0.76)"
