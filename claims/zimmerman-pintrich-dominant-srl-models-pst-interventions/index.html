q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

RQ1.1 analysis of SRL models across the 66 interventions, coded against Panadero's (2017) classification: 6 studies explicitly framed their intervention in one model, 34 referred without explicit anchoring, and 26 did not refer to a model at all.

"Zimmerman’s (2000) model emerged as the most frequently cited model (74%), closely followed by Pintrich’s (2000) model (67.4%). This suggests that the theoretical model followed by the researchers did not affect the areas promoted within the interventions."
