Authors' interpretive conclusion (type e), not a tested result. They acknowledge it takes lots of time to build, test, revise and rebuild physical objects, and that this process does not always align with science learning outcomes, but they encourage teaching students to solder.

> "Particularly because the discussion of “defective” solar panels is so linked to Kirchhoﬀ voltage loops, we feel this project is a meaningful addition to our course’s ≈ 2 − 3 week discussion of voltage, current, power, and electric circuits."
