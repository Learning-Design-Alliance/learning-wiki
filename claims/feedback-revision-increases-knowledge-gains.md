## Evidence

### Sisson SD 2012

Sisson SD, Rastegar DA, Hughes MT, Bertram AK, Yeh HC. (2012). Learner feedback and educational outcomes with an internet-based ambulatory curriculum: a qualitative and quantitative analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-55

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Before-and-after comparison of average score change (posttest minus pretest) on the same 19 modules across 56,254 modules before and 72,596 modules after feedback-based revision; the article reports the gain rose from "17.0 %" to "20.2 %" (p < 0.001). No effect size is printed.

> "In the “before” group, the average improvement in scores was 17.0 %. In the “after” group, the average improvement in scores was 20.2 %. In comparing these two groups of changes in scores, these differences were statistically significant (p < 0.001)."
