## Evidence

### Maureen A. Conroy 2019

Maureen A. Conroy, Kevin S. Sutherland, James Algina, Crystal Ladwig, Brittany Werch, Jose Martinez, Gabriel Jessee, Maria Gyure. (2019). Outcomes of the BEST in CLASS Intervention on Teachers' Use of Effective Practices, Self-Efficacy, and Classroom Quality. School Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.17105/SPR-2018-0003.V48-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Teacher-report analysis in the cluster randomized trial (n = 185 for TEBS) using Mplus 8.0 with BHFDR correction; the article prints treatment effect sizes ".50 or larger for all four TEBS variables", and adjusted means e.g. "AMB = 3.67 and AMC = 3.41" for Maintaining Positive Classroom Climate.

> "Effect sizes for treatment were .50 or larger for all four TEBS variables (see Table 3)."

### Maureen A. Conroy 2019 (2)

Maureen A. Conroy, Kevin S. Sutherland, James Algina, Crystal Ladwig, Brittany Werch, Jose Martinez, Gabriel Jessee, Maria Gyure. (2019). Outcomes of the BEST in CLASS Intervention on Teachers' Use of Effective Practices, Self-Efficacy, and Classroom Quality. School Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.17105/SPR-2018-0003.V48-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The MPCC subscale was the only TEBS variable with a significant C × T interaction; the estimated treatment effect was .26 at the grand mean pretest (3.36) and larger below it. Accommodating Individual Differences and Monitoring and Feedback for Learning effects were moderated by research site.

> "Therefore, BEST in CLASS resulted in higher teacher self-efficacy for MPCC, and the effect was larger for teachers who had lower levels of self-efficacy at pretest."
