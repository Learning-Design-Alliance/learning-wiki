Plan implementation fidelity was assessed by consultant classroom observations on four occasions during active intervention, using checklists tied to each student's plan summary form. Sixty-seven percent of plan steps were delivered at levels deemed highly effective; descriptive only.

> "Average overall implementation quality was 1.56 (SD = 0.38) with a maximum of 2.0 indicting that the plan steps were implemented by teachers with high quality."
