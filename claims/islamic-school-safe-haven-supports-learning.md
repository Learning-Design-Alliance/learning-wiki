## Evidence

### Ian Robottom and Sharifah Norhaidah 2008

Ian Robottom and Sharifah Norhaidah. (2008). Western science and Islamic learners: When disciplines and culture intersect. Journal of Research in International Education 7(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/1475240908091302

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

From student interviews in the Australian mixed-method study in two Victorian Islamic schools. Students said that in an outside school they would feel abandoned and picked on, while at the Islamic school they had a support group and could focus on study rather than worrying.

> "The students at the Islamic college feel more insulated from some of the pressures in Australian society – pressures associated with recent international events, tensions and world terrorism – and that the school is a kind of safe haven within which students can just be themselves and concentrate on their learning."
