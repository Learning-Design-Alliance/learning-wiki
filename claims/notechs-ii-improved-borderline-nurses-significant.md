## Evidence

### Robertson Eleanor 2015

Robertson Eleanor, Morgan Lauren, New Steve, Pickering Sharon, Hadi Mohammed, Collins Gary, Rivero Arias Oliver, Griffin Damian, McCulloch Peter. (2015). Quality Improvement in Surgery Combining Lean Improvement Methods with Teamwork Training: A Controlled Before-After Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0138490

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Non-technical skills of operating teams were rated with the Oxford NOTECHS II behavioural scale (three sub-teams scored 1-8 on four behavioural parameters; theoretical maximum 96) during observed operations before and after the intervention. The active group's "Mean Oxford NOTECHS II score increased from 69.81 before to 75.56 after the intervention"; the control group was static and the change difference was of borderline significance (p = 0.058).

> "Mean Oxford NOTECHS II score increased from 69.81 before to 75.56 after the intervention in the active group (difference = 5.75; 95% confidence interval 0.68 to 10.82)"

### Robertson Eleanor 2015 (2)

Robertson Eleanor, Morgan Lauren, New Steve, Pickering Sharon, Hadi Mohammed, Collins Gary, Rivero Arias Oliver, Griffin Damian, McCulloch Peter. (2015). Quality Improvement in Surgery Combining Lean Improvement Methods with Teamwork Training: A Controlled Before-After Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0138490

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sub-team analysis of the same NOTECHS II ratings compared before/after change between active and control groups for each of the nursing, surgical and anaesthetic sub-teams. Between-group differences were non-significant for surgeons (p = 0.101) and anaesthetists (p = 0.876) but "statistically significant for nurses (difference 2.22; 95% CI 0.22 to 4.24; p = 0.016)".

> "statistically significant for nurses (difference 2.22; 95% CI 0.22 to 4.24; p = 0.016)"
