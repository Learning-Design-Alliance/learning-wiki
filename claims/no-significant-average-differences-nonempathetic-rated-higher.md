# Average check-in and weekly ratings show no significant differences between chatbot conditions, with the Non-Empathetic chatbot often rated as more engaging and useful

## Evidence

### Li Siyan 2026

Li Siyan, Kai-Hui Liang, Shopnil Shahriar, Yilin Ye, Shiyoh Goetsu, Wei-Wei Du, Masahiro Yoshida, Tsunayuki Ohwa, Xuhai Xu, and Zhou Yu. (2026). Invisible Impact of Empathy on Behavioral Change: Isolating the Effect of Empathy in Long-term Physical Activity Coaching Chatbot Interactions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26641

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive averages of post-conversation check-in form items across the three chatbot conditions in the six-week within-subject study (N = 13). The article states the "uniformly high ratings limit statistical power" and that "there are no significant differences between the averages"; the printed means are descriptive, so no effect size is available.

> "The Non-Empathetic chatbot is associated with higher intention to follow and perceived usefulness, whereas the Standard chatbot shows higher average step count and self-efficacy."
