`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of Questionnaire Part 3 responses on perceived difficulty of root canal stages with artificial versus extracted teeth (Table 3). Respondents also reported artificial teeth were identical to the original anatomy 60.2% of the time and 58.9% softer than extracted teeth. Percentages are descriptive perceptions, not tested contrasts.

> "Access cavity preparation (71.9%), pulp chamber and canal orifice localisation (76.3%), working length determination (75.6%), instrumentation (73.6%), irrigation (60.7%), master cone selection (61.5%), and obturation (63.6%) procedures were easier for artificial teeth."
