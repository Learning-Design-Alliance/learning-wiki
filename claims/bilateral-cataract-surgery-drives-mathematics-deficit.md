## Evidence

### Al-Bakri Moug 2023

Al-Bakri Moug, Skovgaard Anne Mette, Bach-Holm Daniella, Larsen Dorte Ancher, Siersma Volkert, Kessel Line. (2023). School performances in children with cataract: results from a population-based cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-072984

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Subgroup analysis of pooled adjusted mathematics scores by operation status. The adjusted mean difference for bilateral surgery was "mean difference=−8.98" (p value=0.004); unilateral and unoperated subgroups were not significantly different. The mean difference is unstandardised, so no effect size is coded.

> "The lower test scores in mathematics were driven by those with bilateral surgery (mean difference=−8.98, 95% CI: −15.07 to −2.89, p value=0.004), while no significant difference was found for children with unilateral surgery or unoperated cataract compared with the control cohort."
