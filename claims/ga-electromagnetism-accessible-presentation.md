James M. Chappell, Azhar Iqbal, Derek Abbott. (2010). A simplified approach to electromagnetism using geometric algebra. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1010.4947](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1010.4947)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' stated rationale in the abstract for the GA-based curriculum: it avoids vector calculus and tensor index notation, "producing a much more accessible presentation for students." No empirical evaluation of student learning is reported in the article.

> "A new simpliﬁed approach for teaching electromagnetism is presented using the formalism of geometric algebra (GA) which does not require vector calculus or tensor index notation, thus pro- ducing a much more accessible presentation for students."
