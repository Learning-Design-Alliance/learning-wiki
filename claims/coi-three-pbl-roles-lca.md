# Three emergent roles — leader, social mediator, and peripheral explorer — were identified in online PBL discussions from CoI-coded discourse using latent class analysis.

## Evidence

### Ramy Elmoazen 2025

Ramy Elmoazen, Mohammed Saqr, Laura Hirsto, Matti Tedre. (2025). Capturing temporal pathways of collaborative roles: A multilayered analytical approach using community of inquiry. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09431-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Modeling of 24632 CoI-coded annotations from 9301 discussion-board posts by 135 first-year dental students across four courses. LCA in the glca R-package selected three classes "where BIC has the largest decrease" with entropy 0.9 and posterior probabilities 0.98/0.95/0.92.

> "The best CA model suggested three roles, which were given the labels of leader, social mediator, and peripheral explorer according to COI indicators"
