Evidence¶

Sungeun An 2025¶

Sungeun An, Jennifer Hammock, Ashok Goel. (2025). How Online Learners Engage in Self-Directed Modeling: A Behavioral Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00506-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Markov chain analysis of state sequences from 822 models, clustered via Jensen-Shannon divergence over transition probabilities, separately for original (N=351) and copied (N=456) models. Table 1 shows clusters 1-3 predominantly Observation, clusters 4-6 predominantly Construction, and cluster 9 only 29.67% Exploration.

"The first three of these Markov clusters (1, 2, and 3) were predominantly associated with the Observation behavior type, with 115, 148, and 31 models in each cluster, respectively."
