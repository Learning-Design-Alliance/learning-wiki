# Writing anxiety can constrain or paralyze adult writers, with recognizable signs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Writing anxiety, arising from causes such as low self-esteem, past failure, and misconceptions about writing, can constrain or even paralyze writers, and shows recognizable signs such as physical tension and repeated false starts. [→ Coombs 1987](#coombs-1987)

## Evidence

### Coombs 1987

Coombs, Lucinda H. (1987). The GED Writing Skills Test Essay Teacher Training Manual. Maine Dept. of Educational and Cultural Services / University of Maine. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED324536

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The manual's writing-process section lists eight causes of writing anxiety and four signs of a blocked writer, based on the author's practitioner experience with adult learners rather than a formal study. No effect sizes are reported.

> "in some cases, writing anxiety can constrain or even paralyze a writer. Some signs of a blocked writer are: 1. physical tension, evidenced by handwriting and body language 2. repeated false starts due to premature editing or censoring"
