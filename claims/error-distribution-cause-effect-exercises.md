`q2 i?` Across 7,000 incorrect solutions, incorrect cause with incorrect effect is the most frequent error, followed by missing cause with correct effect; correct cause with incorrect effect occurs rarely. [→ Devang Kulshreshtha 2022](#devang-kulshreshtha-2022)

## Evidence

### Devang Kulshreshtha 2022

Devang Kulshreshtha, Muhammad Shayan, Robert Belfer, Siva Reddy, Iulian Vlad Serban, Ekaterina Kochmar. (2022). Few-shot Question Generation for Personalized Feedback in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv. [doi:10.3233/faia220062](https://doi.org/10.3233/faia220062)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis running the cause-effect extractor over 7,000 incorrect student solutions in Korbit ITS (Figure 2 shows the error distribution for one exercise, with incorrect-cause-correct-effect at 36.21% and incorrect-cause-incorrect-effect at 56.21% for that exercise). The ranked frequencies are reported as the authors' finding.

> "We find that most frequent student error is'incorrect cause incorrect effect'followed by 'missing cause correct effect'. The error type 'Correct cause incorrect effect' occurs rarely as students usually know the main answer if they know the explanation behind it."
