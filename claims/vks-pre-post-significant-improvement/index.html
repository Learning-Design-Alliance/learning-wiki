Evidence¶

Kabooha and Elyas 2018¶

Raniah Kabooha & Tariq Elyas. (2018). The Effects of YouTube in Multimedia Instruction for Vocabulary Learning: Perceptions of EFL Students and Teachers. English Language Teaching, 11(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n2p72

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-sample t-test across all 100 students comparing Vocabulary Knowledge Scale scores before and after the seven-week module. The article reports 'a significant improvement' with t(99)=-17.021, p<0.05; no effect size is printed. This analysis pools both groups.

"there was a significant improvement ( t (99) = -17.021, p < 0.05) in vocabulary recognition and understanding among the students when comparing the scores of the students on the tests carried out before (M= 22.8, SD= 7.7) and after (M= 42.7, SD= 10.9)"
