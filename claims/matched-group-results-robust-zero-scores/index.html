Chapman 1921¶

Chapman, J. Crosby and Dale, A. Barbara. (1921). A Further Criterion for the Selection of Mental Test Elements. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org/search?query=A+Further+Criterion+for+the+Selection+of+Mental+Test+Elements

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Zero scores occurred only in the older group (four in Test 4, six in Test 5); removing them and their paired papers left 41 pairs, and the article reports Table IV was too similar to previous tables to need further comment.

"This reduced the number of pairs to 41 and the process of calculating the central tendencies, etc. was repeated for these pairs."

Chapman 1921 (2)¶

Chapman, J. Crosby and Dale, A. Barbara. (1921). A Further Criterion for the Selection of Mental Test Elements. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org/search?query=A+Further+Criterion+for+the+Selection+of+Mental+Test+Elements

q3 · i? · associational · r1

As confirmatory evidence, three additional matched pairs of 20 cases each were analyzed; the printed ratios were I 0.92, II 1.04, III 0.97, IV 1.44, V 0.78, described as "the same general figures".

"for three other pairs of 20 apiece, selected without eliminating the language question, the same general figures are obtained."
