## Evidence

### Yanase Yukiko 2026

Yanase Yukiko, Hiragi Saki, Kawahara Yasuhiro. (2026). Effects of generic medical communication training on physiotherapy students’ ability to communicate in movement-based clinical scenarios: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08560-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correspondence analysis (KH Coder) of transcribed participant utterances during CWM tasks, based on 100 of 1,145 distinct words appearing at least 10 times. The scatterplot (Fig. 1) positioned identification and politeness words toward the learned group and "dull" toward the PT group, which the authors link to lay-language explanation of test results.

> "Characteristic words identified in the direction of each group from the origin included “date of birth,” “name,” and “answer please” for the learned group; “muscle” and “inside” for the unlearned group; and “rise,” “let see,” and “dull” for the PT group."
