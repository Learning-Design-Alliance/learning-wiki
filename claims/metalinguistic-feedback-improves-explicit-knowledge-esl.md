## Evidence

### Ebadi et al. 2013

Rohollahzadeh Ebadi, M., Mohd Saad, M. R., & Abedalaziz, N. (2013). Explicit Form Focus Instruction: The Effects on Implicit and Explicit Knowledge of ESL Learners. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Science, 2(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086281

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA in the same randomized pretest-treatment-posttest experiment, with explicit knowledge measured by UGJT (ungrammatical items) and MKT posttest scores. The printed effect size is eta square = .78, which the article calls a moderate to large effect size.

> "after controlling for the effect of the pretest, there is a significant difference between the experimental group  and control group in explicit knowledge, (F (1, 88) = 313.00, p =.00, eta square =.78)"
