Seashore 1922¶

Seashore, C. E. (1922). Elementary tests in psychology. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Kwei Tan's training experiment took thirteen advanced students through repeated intensity-of-sound discrimination measurements; six continued for twenty hour-periods each and six for thirteen to seventeen periods. The article reports "no evidence of improvement with -practice" despite individual fluctuations in the practice course.

"where we had control of the conditions and there were no disturbing influences present, this amount of training yielded no evidence of improvement with -practice."
