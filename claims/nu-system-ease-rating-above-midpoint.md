Post-instruction Survey B (N2 = 39) Likert item on ease of using the nu-system in derivations, tested against the scale midpoint with a one-sample t-test. Mean perceived usefulness was also above midpoint (3.51, s = 0.97). No effect size is printed.

> "The mean ease rating (B1) of ¯x = 3.46 was signiﬁcantly above the scale midpoint of 3 (one-sample t-test: t(38) = 2.52, p = 0 .016), indicating that students found the nu-system derivations modestly easier to follow than their SI counterparts on average."
