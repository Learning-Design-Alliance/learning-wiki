Evidence¶

Ganz 2022¶

Ganz, J. B., Pustejovsky, J. E., Reichle, J., Vannest, K. J., Foster, M., Fuller, M. C., Pierson, L. M., Wattanawongwan, S., Bernal, A. J., Chen, M., Haas, A. N., Skov, R., Smith, S. D., & Yllades, V. (2022). Augmentative and Alternative Communication Intervention Targets for School-Aged Participants with ASD and ID: a Single-Case Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Review Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders. doi:10.1007/s40489-022-00326-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Multilevel meta-analysis of Tau effect sizes from 114 single-case studies with 330 participants and 767 effects, using cluster-robust standard errors. The article reports "The overall average Tau was 0.719" with substantial study-level heterogeneity, indicating much variation in efficacy from study to study.

"The overall average Tau was 0.719, 95% CI (0.670, 0.768), with substantial heterogeneity at the study level"

Ganz 2022 (2)¶

Ganz, J. B., Pustejovsky, J. E., Reichle, J., Vannest, K. J., Foster, M., Fuller, M. C., Pierson, L. M., Wattanawongwan, S., Bernal, A. J., Chen, M., Haas, A. N., Skov, R., Smith, S. D., & Yllades, V. (2022). Augmentative and Alternative Communication Intervention Targets for School-Aged Participants with ASD and ID: a Single-Case Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Review Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders. doi:10.1007/s40489-022-00326-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

LRR analysis of 93 studies across 239 participants using 516 effects, after excluding data series with near-zero baseline levels. The article reports "The overall average LRR was 1.857" with very high contrast-level heterogeneity, aligning with the Tau results.

"The overall average LRR was 1.857, 95% CI (1.581, 2.133), with substantial heterogeneity at the study level"
