## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Non-USIMGs had higher test scores (mean 197 vs 190) and slightly higher patient volume than USIMGs; both differences were statistically significant. [→ Norcini JJ 2014](#norcini-jj-2014)

## Evidence

### Norcini JJ 2014

Norcini JJ, Boulet JR, Opalek A, Dauphinee WD. (2014). The Relationship Between Licensing Examination Performance and the Outcomes of Care by International Medical School Graduates. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000000310

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison of the 2,152 non-USIMGs and 373 USIMGs in the study (Table 1): Step 2 CK means 197 (SD 32) vs 190 (SD 30), P < .001 by two-sided Student t test; both effects "statistically significant."

> "The non-USIMGs had slightly higher patient volume during the period of study than the USIMGs. The non-USIMGs also had higher test scores than the USIMGs. Both of these effects were statistically significant."
