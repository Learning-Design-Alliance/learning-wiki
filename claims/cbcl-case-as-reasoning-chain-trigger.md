### Montague Steven 2026

Montague Steven, Khokhar Aditi, DeWeese Brandy, Elkins Jeananne, Otto Morkel Jacques, Ramos Ana Amelia Bartolamei, Pepes Julia Priscila Ferraz, Mello Rosiane Guetter, Ortiz Orlando, Marques Camila Aparecida Moraes. `q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive account of Case 1 (discitis-osteomyelitis), a 55-year-old man with neck pain, fever, and inflammatory markers, used as an introductory guided review. The authors describe the case as "functioning instead as a trigger for the clinical-radiological reasoning chain."

> "This design allowed the case to go beyond the simple question of “which test to order,” functioning instead as a trigger for the clinical-radiological reasoning chain: diagnostic suspicion, clinical-laboratory correlation, selection of imaging modality, interpretation of findings, and understanding of the underlying pathophysiological mechanisms."
