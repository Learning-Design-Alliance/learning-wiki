## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Interviews revealed ambiguity in asking about beliefs about experimental physics because lab courses and professional research share vocabulary, leading to paired personal/expert wording. [→ Benjamin M. Zwickl 2012](#benjamin-m-zwickl-2012)

## Evidence

### Benjamin M. Zwickl 2012

Benjamin M. Zwickl, Noah Finkelstein and H. J. Lewandowski. (2012). Development and Validation of the Colorado Learning Attitudes about Science Survey for Experimental Physics. [doi:10.1063/1.4789747](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789747)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cognitive-interview validation with ten students on an early version and nine students on the revised version (four female, five male; spanning pre-lab, intro lab, and advanced lab), who took the survey and explained their answers and defined technical terms. Interviews "resulted in substantial revisions of the survey."

> "One signiﬁcant change that came about through student interviews was the choice to change from a single statement asking about the student’s personal beliefs to the personal/expert pairs shown in Fig. 2."
