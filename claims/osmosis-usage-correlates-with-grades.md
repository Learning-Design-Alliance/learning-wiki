## Evidence

### Hoyt Garrik 2024

Hoyt Garrik, Adegboyega Samuel, Constantouris Gus, Basu Paramita. (2024). Study of the impact of introducing a multimedia learning tool in podiatric medical courses. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1002/jfa2.12018

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of treatment-group platform usage metrics (median 34 videos, 30 questions, 37 flashcards per week) against average final grades over the two courses. The authors state these "results suggest that the more students used the tool, the higher their grades tended to be," but only the questions-answered correlation reached significance.

> "There was a moderate positive correlation between the number of questions answered and final grades (r = 0.279, p < 0.05) as well as a weak positive correlation between the number of flashcards answered and final grades (r = 0.151, p = 0.169), as well as number of videos watched and final grades (r = 0.140, p = 0.202)."
