## Evidence

### Bird 1921

Bird, Grace E. (1921). The Devious Path of Slow Work. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ123456

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same experiment with one hundred college students, introspective reports after rapid adding showed only five individuals recording "conscious distractions of any kind," whereas during slow adding all but three mentioned distractions including imagery and emotional disturbances.

> "Only five individuals recorded conscious distractions of any kind. The others stated as their central thought a desire to get the answer, or to add as rapidly as they were supposed to."
