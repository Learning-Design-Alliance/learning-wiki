### Tekian A 2019

Tekian A, Park YS, Tilton S, Prunty PF, Abasolo E, Zar F, Cook DA. (2019). Competencies and Feedback on Internal Medicine Residents' End-of-Rotation Assessments Over Time: Qualitative and Quantitative Analyses. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000002821

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative constant-comparative analysis of 828 narrative comments from 1,869 end-of-rotation assessments on one residency cohort (2013–2016); the article reports "the most prevalent theme in the qualitative analysis, present across all training years (usually as praise), was the subtheme of work ethic." No effect size is printed.
