## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The effect of fact retrieval tutoring on fact retrieval skill depends on whether students have MD alone or concurrent math and reading difficulties. [→ Powell 2009](#powell-2009)

## Evidence

### Powell 2009

Powell, S. R., Fuchs, L. S., Fuchs, D., Cirino, P. T., & Fletcher, J. M. (2009). Effects of Fact Retrieval Tutoring on Third-Grade Students with Math Difficulties with and without Reading Difficulties. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice, 24(1), 1-11. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-5826.2008.01272.x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 139 third graders blocked by site and MD subtype into four tutoring conditions; two-way ANCOVA with pretest covariate found the tutoring condition by MD subtype interaction significant, "F(3,127) = 2.90, p < .0375", subsuming main effects.

> "These effects, however, were subsumed by the interaction of tutoring condition with MD subtype, which was also significant, F(3,127) = 2.90, p < .0375."
