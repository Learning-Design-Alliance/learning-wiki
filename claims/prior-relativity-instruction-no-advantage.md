Observational comparison within the 2012 ANU Physics 2 class. The article reports that although "approximately a third of the class claimed to have had prior formal instruction in rela tivity at secondary school, those students did not per form better" on the RCI pre/post-tests or the exam question; no effect size is printed.

> "Interestingly, although approximately a third of the class claimed to have had prior formal instruction in rela tivity at secondary school, those students did not per form better in either the RCI pre or post-tests, or in the exam relativity question."
