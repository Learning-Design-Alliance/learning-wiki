`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Personal-context theme from the qualitative interviews (n = 11). The article contrasts this with anesthesiology participants' reported surgical skill gains and quotes one participant calling it "more of a refresher course".

> "Although participants with a background in anesthesiology indicated they gained several surgical skills (e.g., chest tube placement or performing a thoracotomy), most participants did not experience a substantial increase in their domain-specific knowledge and skills."
