## Evidence

### Korucu and Cakir 2018

Agâh Tuğrul KORUCU, Hasan ÇAKIR. (2018). The Effect of Dynamic Web Technologies on Student Academic Achievement in Problem-Based Collaborative Learning Environment. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, Volume 6, Issue 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165485

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation offered in the findings discussion, not a separately tested result: they argue the DWT environment enabled constant teacher-student-environment interaction, more effort meeting weekly work-plan requirements, and more active studying time for the experimental group.

> "This difference between the experimental and control group students' "academic achievement scores" at the end of the research process emerges because the environment developed with DWT provides continuous interaction with lecturers and other colleagues in their group, classmates and developed environments independent of time and space limitations and active participation both in and out of the school constantly for experimental group students."
