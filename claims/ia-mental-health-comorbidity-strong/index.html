q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's narrative synthesis reports, citing Sayed (2022), that Egyptian pharmacy students' IA "was strongly correlated with higher levels of depression, anxiety, and stress"; Indian dental students with depression were six times more likely to have IA, and Thai medical students with probable IA had 1.58 times higher likelihood of depression.

"Among Egyptian pharmacy students, IA was strongly correlated with higher levels of depression, anxiety, and stress [11]. This strong, bidirectional relationship with depression [15, 26] suggests IA may be both a cause and a consequence of psychological distress"
