## Evidence

### Jiang and Xiao 2014

Lin Jiang & Hailing Xiao. (2014). The Efficacy of Written Corrective Feedback and Language Analytic Ability on Chinese Learners’ Explicit and Implicit Knowledge of English Articles. English Language Teaching, 7(10). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p22

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analyses between language analytic ability test scores and posttest scores for 92 learners. The relationship was much stronger for the DOC group than for the DMC group; DOC group posttest correlations reached p < .05 while DMC group correlations did not.

> "In this study, students’ posttests scores were positively related with their language analytic ability scores for the DOC group but not for the DMC group, suggesting that language analytic ability mediated the effectiveness of the DOC rather than that of the DMC."
