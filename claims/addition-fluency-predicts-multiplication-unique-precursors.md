### Rautiainen 2026

Rautiainen, J., Aro, T., Aro, M., Tolvanen, A., & Koponen, T. (2026). Hierarchical Nature of Arithmetic Skills: Similarities and Differences Between Arithmetic Operations. Journal of Educational Psychology, Early online. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001066

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Addition fluency latent SEM model in the longitudinal study (n = 207), contrasting the addition fluency model against the baseline model. The article states "working memory’s significant association with multiplication skill fluency was revealed" once covariance with addition fluency was captured; the model explained R2 = .40 (R2 = .48 prior residualization).

> "Contrary to the baseline model, working memory’s significant association with multiplication skill fluency was revealed after controlling for the covariance with the hierarchically lower addition fluency skill. The addition model explained a good proportion of multiplication fluency (R2 = .40, prior residualization R2 = .48)."
