## Evidence

### Vinesh Rajpaul 2014

Vinesh Rajpaul, Saalih Allie, Sarah-Louise Blyth. (2014). The introductory astronomy course at the University of Cape Town: probing student perspectives. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020126](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020126)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Pre-course open question on reasons for enrolling, answered by all 79 students; most students stated more than one reason. The article reports 75 out of 79 (95%) mentioned interest-type motivations and only 2 mentioned an impression the course would be an easy elective.

> "75 out of 79 (95%) of the responses mentioned as part of the reason for doing the course an interest of some sort – whether an interest in a specific topic, or in astronomy in general – or a desire to pursue further studies, or indeed a career, in astronomy."
