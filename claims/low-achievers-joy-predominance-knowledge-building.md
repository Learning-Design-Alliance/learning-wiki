## Evidence

### Yuqin Yang · Gaoxia Zhu · Carol K. K. Chan 2022

Yuqin Yang · Gaoxia Zhu · Carol K. K. Chan. (2022). Evolution of the academic emotions of academically low-achieving students in knowledge building. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09380-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Content analysis of 1,565 Knowledge Forum notes coded into seven academic emotions plus none, from 120 low-achieving students in four classes. These results suggest students manifested a predominately higher percentage of positive emotions such as "joy and curiosity" than negative emotions in the Knowledge Building process.

> "The most frequently manifested emotion in the Knowledge Building inquiry threads across the four classes was joy (53.16%), followed by confusion (15.53%), anxiety (10.93%) and curiosity (9.07%). Surprise (2.17%) and boredom (1.41%) were less frequently expressed."
