## Evidence

### Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer and Jérôme Prado 2023

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer and Jérôme Prado. (2023). Arithmetic skills are associated with left fronto-temporal gray matter volume in 536 children and adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00201-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

The introduction, citing Peters and de Smedt's review, reports structural BWAS sample sizes "ranged from n = 11 to n = 59, with an average of n = 24", and states that underpowered BWAS yield inflated effects. The authors' own study (n = 536) is presented as the response to this problem.

> "BWAS with such relatively small sample sizes may be characterized by effect sizes that are inflated and difficult to replicate because studies are underpowered to detect functional or structural brain-behavior associations."
