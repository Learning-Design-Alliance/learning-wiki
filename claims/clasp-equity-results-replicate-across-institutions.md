`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Side-by-side analysis of CLASP at OU (three-quarter series, N = 8235) and NU (two-semester series, only Physics A reformed, N = 3219) with different HM fractions (0.13 vs 0.28) and STEM-major fractions shows "similar" equity patterns.

> "It is also rather striking that these two results show up in a similar way for two very different universities 20 years apart."
