## Evidence

### Bassah Nahyeni 2016

Bassah Nahyeni, Cox Karen, Seymour Jane. (2016). A qualitative evaluation of the impact of a palliative care course on preregistration nursing students’ practice in Cameroon. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-016-0106-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative evaluation using three focus groups with 23 participants and 10 individual critical incident interviews with preregistration nursing students who attended a palliative care course in Cameroon, analysed thematically using the framework approach. The analysis yielded "26 critical incidents, with an average of 2 incidents per student". Students described palliative care encounters initiated through compassion, delegated care, family reports and deliberate patient selection.

> "The results suggest that nursing students in receipt of palliative care education can transfer their learning to practice. Students reported recognizing patients with palliative care needs, providing patients with physical, psychosocial and spiritual support and communicating patient information to the wider care team."
