## Evidence

### Thacker 2025

Thacker, B., Wang, J., Zhang, Y., Tran, Q. B., Vemula, D. S., & Kushimo, T. (2025). From Research to Resources: Assessing Student Understanding and Skills in Quantum Computing. NSF grant DUE 2235464. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ae21a4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ae21a4)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview evidence from a single Proficient-level F23 student who, while prompted with the S-gate matrix, wrote the output in Dirac notation and analyzed what the matrix could do to |0> and |1>. The article states the mini-tutorial "may have positively impacted" his learning.

> "The mini-tutorial about gates may have positively impacted F23_2_b’s learning by engaging him in all three representations, facilitating their integration. The Lower Proficiency students,  F23_3_d and F21_3_d, did not demonstrate an operational understanding of the 𝑆 and 𝑆!gates"
