`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Grounded theory analysis of six focus groups (46 students, Years 2 and 4 at one graduate-entry medical school) after students completed two authored VP cases. The analysis "identified a central core phenomenon designated 'learning from the VP'" with the four named categories, illustrated with in vivo codes in Tables 1–3.

> "We identified a central core phenomenon designated 'learning from the VP'. This had four categories: VP Construction; External Preconditions; Student–VP Interaction, and Consequences."
