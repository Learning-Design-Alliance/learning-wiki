## Evidence

### Rudolph 2012

Rudolph, A. L., Lamine, B., Joyce, M., Vignolles, H., & Consiglio, D. (2012). Introduction of interactive learning into French university physics classrooms. Physical Review Special Topics – Physics Education Review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1311.3622](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1311.3622)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA of CSEM normalized gain across three interactivity groups in the second-year E&M classes: highly interactive (N=42, <g>=0.286), somewhat interactive (N=124, <g>=0.233), non-interactive (N=86, <g>=0.101). Table II reports an F-statistic of 9.28, p < 0.01.

> "clearly the normalized gain increased as interactivity increased.  To test for statistical significance of this result, we compared the normalized gain for these three groups using an analysis of variance (ANOVA) test, and found that the results were highly statistically significant (p < 0.01)."
