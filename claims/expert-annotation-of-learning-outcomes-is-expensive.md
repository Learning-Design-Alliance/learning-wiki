### Dhivya Chandrasekaran and Vijay Mago 2022

Dhivya Chandrasekaran and Vijay Mago. (2022). Automating Transfer Credit Assessment in Student Mobility - A Natural Language Processing-based Approach. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.32604/cmc.2022.027236](https://doi.org/10.32604/cmc.2022.027236)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' argument in the Challenges section (type e, not a tested result): learning outcomes are treated as instructors' intellectual property, many are "vague or don't follow the structural requirements," and expert annotation costs far more than ordinary sentence annotation — the article cites $0.04 versus $0.65 per annotation on a crowdsourcing platform.

> "one of the popular crowdsourcing platforms charges $0.04 for annotators with no speciﬁcation and $0.65 for an annotator with at least a Master's degree."
