## Evidence

### Tokuda Yasuharu 2020

Tokuda Yasuharu, Matayoshi Tetsutaro, Nakama Yasunori, Kurihara Masaru, Suzuki Tomoharu, Kitahara Yusuke, Kitai Yuya, Nakamura Takashi, Itokazu David, Miyazato Tatsuya. (2020). Cardiac auscultation skills among junior doctors: effects of sound simulation lesson. International Journal of Medical Education 11:107-110. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5eb6.70c6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive pre-lesson test results (Table 2 and Results) for the 50 junior doctors; Table 3 prints AS at 41 (82%) and MS at 0 (0%). The authors interpret this as easier comprehension of systolic murmurs and tougher identification of diastolic murmurs.

> "At pre-lesson test, the highest diagnostic accuracy was obtained in auscultation of aortic stenosis (systolic ejection murmur), while accuracy was none for mitral stenosis (opening snap plus diastolic rumble)."
