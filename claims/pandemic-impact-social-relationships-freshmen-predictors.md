`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate and stepwise multivariable logistic regression on the post-pandemic survey (Table 2): the best model retained gender, nationality, USS, instrumental support, and external regulation. The article reports being female "increased the probability by 69% (OR = 1.69, 95%CI: 1.09, 2.62)".

> "The results showed that being female, compared to being male, increased the probability by 69% (OR = 1.69, 95%CI: 1.09, 2.62) of saying that the pandemic has had an effect on social relationships compared to saying that it has had no effect."
