## Evidence

### Changuiti O 2026

Changuiti O, Mahou FZ, Decormeille G, Koraich N, El Hsaini H. (2026). Hybrid simulation approach enhances technical and non-technical skills in therapeutic pharmacology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09983-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observation of 78 nursing students using an SBAR-inspired 10-item grid (three-point ordinal scale) during role-play sessions, reported in Results (Table 3). The study reports "Higher improvements were noted in areas such as asking for the patient's name (0.87 to 1.8)" with p-values < 0.001 except item 4 (p = 0.008).
