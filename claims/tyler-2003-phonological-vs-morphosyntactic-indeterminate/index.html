Phonological Awareness Training 2012¶

Phonological Awareness Training. (2012). WWC Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial comparing phonological awareness training (target-sound awareness, differences and similarities, production practice) with a morphosyntactic intervention, both with weekly individual and group sessions over a 12-week period. Per WWC criteria the effect is indeterminate.

"Tyler et al. (2003) found, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant differences between the intervention and comparison groups."
