## Evidence

### Keller 2020

Keller, L., Preckel, F., & Brunner, M. (2020). Nonlinear Relations Between Achievement and Academic Self-Concepts in Elementary and Secondary School: An Integrative Data Analysis Across 13 Countries. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000533

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Integrative data analysis of representative TIMSS and PISA samples applying quadratic regression models per country and cycle, with coefficients integrated by three-level random-effects meta-analysis. The mean quadratic coefficient was "mean β = 0.12" for both older groups, significant with 95% CIs excluding zero.

> "we observed positive quadratic relations in the group of 15-year-old students (mean β = 0.12, 95% CI [0.10, 0.14]) and Grade 8 students (mean β = 0.12, 95% CI [0.08, 0.15]), implying that the increase in students’ mathematics self-concept was weaker for lower achieving students than for higher achieving students."
