## Evidence

### Li Lan 2026

Li Lan, Yeonhee Seo, Fan Qianqian. (2026). Enhancing psychological well-being through embodied bilingual teaching: the mediating role of basic psychological needs and learning motivation. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04185-2

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test (5,000 resamples) in the SEM of 518 students. The article reports "β = 0.028, 95% CI [0.004, 0.064]" and characterizes the chain effect as "a small effect size" functioning as a supplementary mechanism.

> "Chain mediation: teacher support had a positive indirect effect on psychological well-being through basic psychological needs and learning motivation (β = 0.028, 95% CI [0.004, 0.064], P = 0.029, supporting H9)"
