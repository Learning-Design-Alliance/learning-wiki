Evidence¶

Fatollahi 2016¶

Moslem Fatollahi. (2016). Applying Sight Translation as a Means to Enhance Reading Ability of Iranian EFL Students. English Language Teaching, 9(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n3p153

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental posttest comparison of two intact classes (N = 35 each) after eleven treatment sessions, analyzed with an Independent Samples T-test in SPSS 22; the printed Sig(2-tailed) is 0.040. The article reports "a significant difference between the reading scores of the two groups".

"result of the critical T-test score for equality of means is 0.04 which does not approve the null hypothesis of the posttest at a 0.05 level of confidence. In other words, as the first null hypothesis of this study is not approved, it is revealed that there is a significant difference between the reading scores of the two groups."
