Evidence¶

Taylor et al. 2018¶

Jonte C. Taylor, Ching-mei Tseng, Brian Hand, Angelique Murillo, William Therrien. (2018). Using Argument-based Science Inquiry to Improve Science Achievement for Students with Disabilities in Inclusive Classrooms. Journal of Science Education for Students with Disabilities, Vol. 21, No. 1. http://scholarworks.rit.edu/jsesd/vol21/iss1/2

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Between-groups post-test t-test analysis of ITBS science standard scores for 208 treatment and 199 comparison students with IEPs in inclusive classrooms. The treatment group out-performed the comparison group significantly, "t(407) = 4.0189, p < 0.001". No effect size is printed for this specific between-group t-test in the quoted passage.

"When comparing post-test differences only between groups, there is a significant differ- ence at the 0.05 alpha level. The treatment group out-performed the comparison group significantly as indicated by the t-test results [t(407) = 4.0189, p < 0.001]"
