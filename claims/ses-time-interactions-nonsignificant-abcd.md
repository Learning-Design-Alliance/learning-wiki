`q3 i?` In post hoc ordinal regression models including SES-by-time interactions, interaction terms Timepoint*Education (OR 1.04, p = 0.42), Timepoint*ITN (OR 0.99, p = 0.86), and Timepoint*ADI (OR 0.99, p = 0.76) were not statistically significant, while the timepoint main effect was significant (OR 0.82, p < 0.001).
