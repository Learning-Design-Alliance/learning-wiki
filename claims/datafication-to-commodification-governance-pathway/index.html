q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Analytical result of the article's value-critical synthesis. The article states the pathway "is not technologically inevitable but institutionally mediated" and is constrained by robust public-oriented governance frameworks including transparent data governance, limits on extractive data practices, and accountability mechanisms for algorithmic decision-making.

"A second major result identifies a governance pathway through which AI-driven digitalization can shift education from a value-based practice toward a commodified service: datafication → platform governance → market-aligned accountability → commodification."
