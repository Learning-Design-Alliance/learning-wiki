Julia Lademann, Jannik Henze, and Sebastian Becker-Genschow. (2024). Building Bridges – AI Custom Chatbots as Mediators between Mathematics and Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.15747](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.15747)

In the randomized controlled study, self-efficacy expectation regarding independent solving of a topic-related task was measured with five items after the learning phase. The EG scored higher, with a printed effect size of d = 0.63 ("medium effect size").

> "The values of the EG were found to be significantly higher than those of the CG  ( 𝑝𝑝 = 0.00001, 𝑑𝑑 =  0. 63, medium effect size)."
