Stoet G 2013¶

Stoet G, Geary DC (2013) Sex Differences in Mathematics and Reading Achievement Are Inversely Related: Within- and Across-Nation Assessment of 10 Years of PISA Data. PLoS ONE 8(3): e57988. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0057988

q3 · i? · associational · r2

PISA secondary analysis across the 33 countries in all four assessments. The printed increase was from "32.0 points in 2000 to 38.8 points in 2009, t(32) = 26.25, p,.001"; only the t statistic is printed, so no effect size is coded.

"Not only was the sex difference in reading relatively large, the overall average difference increased from 32.0 points in 2000 to 38.8 points in 2009, t(32) = 26.25, p,.001."
