### Setyoningrum Retno Asih 2026

Setyoningrum Retno Asih, Susilo Astrid Pratidina, Hasan Helmia, Rehatta Nancy Margarita. (2026). Effect of structured preceptorship on clinical reasoning of pediatric residents in Indonesia: a randomized controlled trial. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.130

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Psychometric validation of a newly developed pediatric SCT using aggregate scoring with a 12-expert panel; item reduction yielded a final instrument with "20 items per topic with Cronbach’s α values ranges from 0.60 to 0.85".

> "Inter-rater reliability of the panelists’ responses by topic was high: intraclass correlation coefficients (average measures) ranged from good to excellent (0.75–0.95) and the F test for each topic was significant (p<0.001)."
