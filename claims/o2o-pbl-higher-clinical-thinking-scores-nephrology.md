## Evidence

### Wu Junxia 2024

Wu Junxia, Ke You, Chen Zhida, Alhendi Mhd Alaa, Zhu Lina, Ma Kunling. (2024). Online-to-offline combined with problem-based learning is an effective teaching modality in the standardized residency training of nephrology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05675-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Clinical thinking examination involved randomly selected case studies requiring trainees to summarize medical histories, give diagnoses and differential diagnoses, and propose treatment strategies. Difference was statistically significant with t = 2.105 and P = 0.04; no effect size printed.

> "The clinical thinking score of the O2O/PBL group was 88.933 ± 4.473, and that of the LBT group was 86.667 ± 3.844."
