## Evidence

### Ilhan 2018

Ilhan, M., & Guler, N. (2018). A Comparison of Difficulty Indices Calculated for Open-Ended Items According to Classical Test Theory and Many Facet Rasch Model. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 75. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181454

`q3 · i?`

Correlational analysis of difficulty indices computed for ten open-ended items from ratings of 375 eighth graders by three teachers; CTT indices via Excel, MFRM via FACETS. The chart reports "r = .999, p<.001".

> "Spearman’s correlation coefficient shown at the bottom of Chart 1 suggests that there is a positive and perfect correlation between item difficulty indices estimated in CTT and MFRM."
