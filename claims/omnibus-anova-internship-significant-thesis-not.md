## Evidence

### Yang Ningxi 2018

Yang Ningxi, Xiao Han, Cao Yingnan, Li Shiyue, Yan Hong, Wang Yifang. (2018). Does narrative medicine education improve nursing students’ empathic abilities and academic achievement? A randomised controlled trial. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060518781476

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus one-way ANOVA across the three groups (n=163 completing all stages) on internship score, graduation thesis achievement and empathic ability. Internship scores differed significantly (F=4.175, P=0.018); thesis achievement (P=0.076) and empathy (P=0.067) did not, as "the results in Table 2 show".

> "The results in Table 2 show significant differences in internship scores (P<0.05) but not in graduation thesis achievement and empathic ability among the three groups."
