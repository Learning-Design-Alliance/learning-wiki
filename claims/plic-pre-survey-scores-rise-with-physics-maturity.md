## Evidence

### Cole Walsh 2019

Cole Walsh, Katherine N. Quinn, C. Wieman, and N.G. Holmes. (2019). Quantifying critical thinking: Development and validation of the Physics Lab Inventory of Critical thinking (PLIC). [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010135)

`q3 · i3` · `design · r3`

Concurrent validity analysis of matched pre-survey scores from 1558 FY students, 353 BFY students, and 78 experts. The ANOVA gave F(2, 1986) = 203, p < 0.001, "with a large eﬀect size ( η2 = 0.170)"; experts averaged 7.6 ± 0.2.

> "An ANOVA comparing pre-survey scores across physics maturity (FY, BFY, expert) indicates that physics maturity is a statistically signiﬁcant predictor of pre-survey scores (F (2, 1986) = 203, p < 0.001) with a large eﬀect size ( η2 = 0.170)."
