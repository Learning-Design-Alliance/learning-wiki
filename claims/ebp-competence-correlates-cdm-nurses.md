### Sarıköse Seda 2026

Sarıköse Seda, Sevim Fatma, Alık Şebnem, Karaçay Pelin, Yaşar Özgen. (2026). Integrating Insights: A Mixed‐Methods Approach to Nurses' Competencies in Evidence‐Based Practice and Clinical Decision‐Making. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70525

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 387 nurses using Pearson's correlation between scale totals. The article reports "a statistically significant and highly positive relationship" with r = 0.719, p < 0.001; subscale correlations are given in Table 2.

> "There was a statistically significant and highly positive relationship between EBP‐COQ and CDMNS total scores of nurses (r = 0.719; p < 0.001)."
