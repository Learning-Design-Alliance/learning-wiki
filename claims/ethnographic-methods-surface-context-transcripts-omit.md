Qualitative case study in one 8th-grade math classroom using seven participant observations, four interviews, two focus groups, and four member checks with four focal students; field notes were thematically grouped into contextual factors LLM-based measures miss.

> "By using ethnographically-oriented methods, we recover much of what transcripts lose by treating student talk as a situated, embodied, social, and cultural experience."

### Liliana Santos-Deonizio 2027 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Observation-based analysis contrasting two students: Carlos rarely spoke but worked diligently, while Drake was charismatic and frequently contributed; interpreted only through spoken-talk codes they look like opposite ends of a participation scale, though the difference may reflect preferred mode of engagement.

> "Coding schemes built around spoken academic talk risk mischaracterizing those differences as gaps in ability or attention."
