## Evidence

### Lwin Ei Zar 2025

Lwin Ei Zar, Watthanakulpanich Dorn, Phetrak Athit, Soonthornworasiri Ngamphol, Prangthip Pattaneeya. (2025). Factors influencing secondary school students' nutrition, mindfulness, and academic performance in Nan Province, Thailand. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0308882

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 350 students in grades 8–11 at Bo Kluea School, Thailand; multiple logistic regression on GPA category. The article reports mindfulness "significantly influenced academic performance (p < 0.001, AOR = 50.777, 95%CI = 23.086–111.686)"; no standardised effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Normal BMI was associated with better academic performance (AOR = 2.027), and mindfulness significantly influenced academic performance (p < 0.001, AOR = 50.777, 95%CI = 23.086–111.686)."
