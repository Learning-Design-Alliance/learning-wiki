q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Finding from teacher focus groups conducted as part of the implementation study across grantee districts. Teachers were "receptive to collaboration on average," while the share of teachers involved in grant activities was limited in every district studied.

"Teachers interviewed in all sectors are receptive to collaboration on average, but the proportion of teachers that grantee districts have involved in collaboration grant activities has been limited across each district."
