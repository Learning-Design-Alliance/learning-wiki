Evidence¶

Jinhee Kim 2026¶

Jinhee Kim, Xi Lin, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick. (2026). Adult learners' perspectives of AI applications in supporting andragogy. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10621-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Relevance theme from the interviews: AI tracks progress against objectives (P18), keeps learners on track (P16), and breaks down larger goals, with one participant saying "it helps me see the next steps instead of feeling overwhelmed" (P1).

"ALs perceive that AI can enhance the relevance of their learning by supporting goal orientation through real-time monitoring, personalized recommendations, and goal breakdowns."

Jinhee Kim 2026 (2)¶

Jinhee Kim, Xi Lin, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick. (2026). Adult learners' perspectives of AI applications in supporting andragogy. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10621-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Personal/professional relevance theme: human-like AI assistants and coaches offer tailored career-planning advice (P5), and recommender systems match individuals by learning profiles and career goals, so "the system connects me with people who share my goals" (P12).

"Participants also expect AI to support their personal and professional relevance by acting as a career companion and serving as a networking platform that provides meaningful connection suggestions."
