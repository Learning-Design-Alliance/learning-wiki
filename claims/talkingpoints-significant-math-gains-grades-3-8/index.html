A retrospective difference-in-differences quasi-experiment in a large urban school district using Smarter Balanced Assessment results. The article reports an "8.7-point increase (effect size = +0.08)" in math for grades 3-8.

"Results for students in grades 3-8 showed significant improvements in math scores, with an 8.7-point increase (effect size = +0.08)."
