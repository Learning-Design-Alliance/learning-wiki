Claims

Numerical cognition assessed at end of first grade statistically mediates associations between early processing speed, working memory, calculation skill, word-problem solving, and attentive behavior and later calculations, word-problem solving, and reading

In a longitudinal path model of 370 first graders, end-of-first-grade numerical cognition statistically mediated associations between early cognitive and academic skills and later calculations, word-problem solving and reading; the observational design cannot show cause.
