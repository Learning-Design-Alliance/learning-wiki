Repeated fixed-instance trial series: for each of three AI-deterrence-modified Qiskit packages, the same student-visible instance was tested in 50 separate ChatGPT sessions under a minimally engaged student workflow, and "all 150 passed." The study reports Ca = Na = 50 and Ra = 0 per package.

"All 150 final artifacts were executed and evaluated with the corresponding grader, and all 150 passed."
