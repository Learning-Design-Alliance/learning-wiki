## Evidence

### Lu Chen 2025

Lu Chen, Gong Bin, Zhang Xiaochen, Wu Chengcheng, Yang Jin. (2025). Effectiveness of video-based peer feedback in self-training for skill acquisition among surgical residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08029-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation (Discussion) of their RCT results: simple interrupted suturing had "the lowest average score and pass rate among all evaluated skills" (pre-assessment pass rate 33.3%), and VPF showed no advantage there. This is an interpretation, not a separately tested result.

> "These findings suggest that when task complexity exceeds a certain threshold - as appears to be the case with simple interrupted suturing for our cohort - the effectiveness of peer feedback may be compromised."
