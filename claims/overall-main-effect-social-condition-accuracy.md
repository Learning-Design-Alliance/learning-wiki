# Across all age groups, relational reasoning accuracy is lower in the friend-present condition than in the experimenter-present condition overall

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wolf L.K. 2015

Wolf L.K., Bazargani N., Kilford E.J., Dumontheil I., Blakemore S.-J. (2015). The audience effect in adolescence depends on who's looking over your shoulder. Journal of Adolescence, 43, 5–14. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adolescence.2015.05.003

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus mixed-design ANOVA on accuracy across all 62 analysed participants. The social condition main effect was significant (ηp2 = 0.06) and the friend-versus-experimenter pairwise contrast reached r = 0.34; the other post-hoc comparisons were not significant (ps > 0.1).

> "The accuracy data revealed a main effect of social condition (F(2,118) = 3.49, p = 0.034, ηp2 = 0.06) with participants responding less accurately in the friend-present condition than the experimenter-present condition overall (t(61) = 2.78, p = 0.006, r = 0.34)."
