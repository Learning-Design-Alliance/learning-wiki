q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the randomized comparison of the 100 elementary schools, teacher survey reports showed treatment-group teachers reported "less frequent instructional support and feedback than teachers whose principals did not receive the professional development." No effect size is printed.

"In fact, teachers whose principals received the professional development reported receiving less frequent instructional support and feedback than teachers whose principals did not receive the professional development."
