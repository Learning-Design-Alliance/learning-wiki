## Evidence

### Andersson ML 2023

Andersson ML, Duch P, Bessmann EL, Lundstrøm LH, Ekelund K. (2023). Preparing for obstetric anaesthesia—An educational randomised controlled trial comparing e‐learning to written course material. Acta Anaesthesiol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aas.14148

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Explorative post hoc subgroup analysis comparing the two e-learning modules (22 vs 23 participants); pre-course knowledge 30.6 (EL-PE) vs 33.2 (EL-PPH), p < .001; 4- and 12-week knowledge also favoured EL-PPH (p = .016 each).
