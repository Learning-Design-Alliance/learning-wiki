## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2010

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2010). Using Analogy to Solve a Three-Step Physics Problem. PERC Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3515228](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515228)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-study comparison with the authors' companion paper: although the companion problems required Newton's 2nd Law with centripetal acceleration (generally considered more difficult), students transferred well there, but struggled with the added step here. The authors attribute the difference to difficulty decomposing problems and connecting sub-problems.

> "A comparison with the result in the companion paper indicates that it is much harder for students to extend what they learned from a two-step problem to solve a three-step problem than simply going from a two-step problem to another two-step problem."
