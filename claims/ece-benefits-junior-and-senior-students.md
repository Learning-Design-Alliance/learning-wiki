## Evidence

### Pang Xuefei 2026

Pang Xuefei, Wang Huilin, Li Jinggui, Gui Haijun, Cao Xia, Cai Ming. (2026). The Impact of Early Clinical Exposure on Competency Development in Dental Students: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109483

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Student-type subgroup analysis of the meta-analysis, defining junior as second year and below and senior as third year and above. Both subgroups were significant; heterogeneity was very high in the junior subgroup.

> "Significant benefits were observed for both junior preclinical students (second year and below, SMD = 1.58, 95% CI = 0.79-2.37, I² = 97.5%, P < .001) and senior preclinical students (third year and above, SMD = 0.82, 95% CI = 0.44-1.21, I² = 87.6%, P < .001)"
