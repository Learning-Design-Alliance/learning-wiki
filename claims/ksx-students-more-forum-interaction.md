### Heusler Andreas 2019

Heusler Andreas, Molitor Dominik, Spann Martin. (2019). Knowledge Stock Exchanges increase student success in Massive Open Online Courses. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0223064

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison of forum activity (Table 3) across the two groups; the printed averages show stronger interaction for the dual-platform group on both the MOOC and KSX forums. No inferential test is reported for this contrast.

> "By comparing between MOOC-only students (2.71 comments on average) and MOOC ∩ KSX students (6.14 MOOC comments and 12.12 KSX comments on average), we observe considerably stronger forum interactions for the latter group on both platforms."
