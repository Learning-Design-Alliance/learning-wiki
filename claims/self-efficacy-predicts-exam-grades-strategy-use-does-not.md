## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a regression on first-exam grades, neither self-reported cognitive nor metacognitive strategy use predicted learning outcomes, while self-efficacy did. [→ Nadja M. Gentner 2024](#nadja-m-gentner-2024)

## Evidence

### Nadja M. Gentner 2024

Nadja M. Gentner, Lisa Respondek, Tina Seufert. (2024). Effects of short- and long-term prompting in learning journals on strategy use, self-efficacy, and learning outcomes. Instructional Science (2024) 52:919–950. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09671-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Multiple regression for hypothesis H3 with cognitive strategy use, metacognitive strategy use, and self-efficacy reported at t4 entered as predictors of first-exam grade. "Learners’ self-efficacy predicted students’ grade in the first exam"; strategy use did not.
