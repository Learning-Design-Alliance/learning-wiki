# Groups entering Just Math hold expectations that they must do math, that doing math is solitary, and that off-topic talk is permissible

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Once a group enters Just Math, observable expectations include that proceeding requires doing math, that doing math is a solitary activity, and that silly off-topic talk can accompany math processing. [→ Steven F. Wolf 2014](#steven-f-wolf-2014)

## Evidence

### Steven F. Wolf 2014

Steven F. Wolf, Leanne Doughty, Paul W. Irving, Eleanor C. Sayre and Marcos D. Caballero. (2014). Just Math: A New Epistemic Frame. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.065](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.065)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Expectations were inferred from behaviors and discourse before, during, and after candidate episodes in the homework help sessions. Prior discussion scaffolds a mathematical procedure until each member is comfortable to proceed, after which the group enters Just Math.

> "The main expecta- tion being that to proceed with the problem they need to “do math”. Students also have the expectation that “do- ing math” is a solitary activity."
