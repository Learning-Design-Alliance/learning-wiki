Adjusted multivariable linear regression of counseling scores in the cross-sectional survey of 133 physicians. The article states this "should be interpreted cautiously" given the self-reported, cross-sectional design.

> "Interestingly, those who reported having no specific training in STD counseling also had significantly higher scores than their counterparts with formal training (β = 0.594, 95% CI: 0.030 to 1.159, p = 0.0391)."
