The article's synthesis of prior literature on LMS decision processes, citing [19]. It states students are often excluded even though the level of success of the LMS is determined in part by the frequency of student use and its impact on educational outcomes. No effect size is printed.

> "Typically, the university management, the chief information officer (CIO) and faculty representatives make the administrative decision to implement a new LMS or transition from one LMS to another [19] without any input from the students."
