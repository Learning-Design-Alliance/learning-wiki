## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The treatment effect on WIAT-II Written Expression was significant on the quadratic (acceleration) component of the slope (B=1.18, p<.006), and by the start of third grade the treated group's growth rate significantly exceeded the untreated at-risk group's (B=2.79, p<.003). ## Evidence

### Hooper 2013

Hooper, S. R., Costa, L.-J. C., McBee, M., Anderson, K. L., Yerby, D. C., Childress, A., & Knuth, S. B. (2013). A written language intervention for at-risk second grade students: a randomized controlled trial of the process assessment of the learner lesson plans in a tier 2 response-to-intervention (RtI) model. Ann. of Dyslexia, 63, 44-64. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-011-0056-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel growth model fit to WIAT-II Written Expression raw scores for typical, at-risk untreated, and at-risk treated groups in the RCT; the quote reports the significant quadratic contrast and its interpretation as "acceleration in the rate of writing skill acquisition". No effect size was printed for this contrast.

> "the treatment effect was significant only on the quadratic component of the slope ( B Estimate=1.18, p<.006). The quadratic component represents an acceleration parameter, indicating that the treatment induced acceleration in the rate of writing skill acquisition for treated participants."

### Hooper 2013 (2)

Hooper, S. R., Costa, L.-J. C., McBee, M., Anderson, K. L., Yerby, D. C., Childress, A., & Knuth, S. B. (2013). A written language intervention for at-risk second grade students: a randomized controlled trial of the process assessment of the learner lesson plans in a tier 2 response-to-intervention (RtI) model. Ann. of Dyslexia, 63, 44-64. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-011-0056-y

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Contrast analysis of model-implied means in the fall of third grade after constant adjustment for pre-treatment differences; the article characterizes the printed effect size dadj=0.36 as small but increasing over time.

> "the adjusted model-implied mean for the treatment group ( Mest, adj =11.54) was larger than the mean for the untreated at-risk group ( Mest=9.93), and the comparison was statistically significant, t (478)=2.89, p=.004, Cohen 's dadj=0.36."
