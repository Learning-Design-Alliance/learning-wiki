Evidence¶

Roderick 2000¶

Roderick, M., Nagaoka, J., Bacon, J., & Easton, J. Q. (2000). Update: Ending Social Promotion: Passing, Retention, and Achievement Trends Among Promoted and Retained Students 1995-1999. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Three-year tracking of the additional 13 percent of 1997 sixth graders who met cutoffs after Summer Bridge. The brief interprets this as "a one time increase in their test scores that allowed these students to remain on track."

"Over the three years between fifth and eighth grade, these students also increased their ITBS scores 3.0 GEs, compared to 2.7 GEs for students in 1995 who were socially promoted."
