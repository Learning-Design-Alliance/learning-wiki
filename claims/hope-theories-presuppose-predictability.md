`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument from the narrative review's introduction: the article argues that cognitive hope models assume individuals can "pursue clearly defined goals" and that this assumption "breaks down in contexts where established frameworks of meaning and social order are disrupted." No empirical data are offered for this conceptual claim.

> "Cognitive approaches rely on a shared assumption: that individuals can envision future states, identify viable pathways, and pursue clearly defined goals (Snyder, 2002). These theories implicitly presuppose a temporal structure characterized by predictability and control."
