Analysis of Kentucky Department of Education School Report Card data on AP and DC enrollment for SY 2018-19 and 2021-22, reported by race/ethnicity in state figures. The report states "enrollment in DC rose, consistent with the state's emphasis on DC" while AP fell for all groups.
