# Micro-flexions (>1 Hz) occur more frequently in the immobile condition than the expressive condition

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Phase counts above 1 Hz are significantly higher when players are instructed to stay still (χ2 = 31.49, p < 0.001). [→ Moura 2023](#moura-2023)

## Evidence

### Moura 2023

Moura, N., Vidal, M., Aguilera, A. M., Vilas-Boas, J. P., Serra, S. & Leman, M. (2023). Knee flexion of saxophone players anticipates tonal context of music. npj Science of Learning 8:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00172-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Poisson-regression ANOVA on weighted phase counts comparing immobile and expressive conditions; the article reports "χ2 = 31.49, p < 0.001". A Wilcoxon signed-rank test on phase changes per second confirmed it (p < 0.001).

> "The analysis of the phase counts revealed that for the IMO condition countings were significantly higher than for the EXP condition ( χ2 = 31.49, p < 0.001, using ANOVA via Poisson regression with phase counts weighted according to performance duration)."
