# Underprepared students scored significantly lower on early-semester common questions, validating their need for support

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On Group 1 common questions given early in the semester, underprepared students scored significantly lower than college-ready students. [→ Hesser 2016](#hesser-2016)

## Evidence

### Hesser 2016

Hesser, T. L., & Gregory, J. L. (2016). Instructional Support Sessions in Chemistry: Alternative to Remediation. Journal of Developmental Education, 39(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130190

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample t-test on Group 1 common questions (Chapters 1, 2, and 4) given early in the Fall 2014 semester: underprepared M = 2.91 (SD = 1.43, N = 69) vs college-ready M = 3.66 (SD = 1.18, N = 249), t(316) = -4.43, p = .000. The authors state this "validates these students' need for added instructional support."

> "students identified as underprepared had significantly lower mean correct responses than college-ready students on Group 1 common questions"
