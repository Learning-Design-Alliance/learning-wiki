## Evidence

### Haywood Xonné 2021

Haywood Xonné, Pienaar Anita Elizabeth. (2021). Long-Term Influences of Stunting, Being Underweight, and Thinness on the Academic Performance of Primary School Girls: The NW-CHILD Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health 18(17):8973. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178973

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Time-point t-tests and repeated measures ANOVA in the NW-CHILD study comparing underweight (n = 21 in longitudinal analysis) and normal weight (n = 112) girls on language, mathematics, and GPA. Descriptive means sometimes favoured the underweight group, but "None of these percentage differences... were, however, significant"; equivalence was not tested.

> "None of these percentage differences between the underweight and normal weight groups were, however, significant at any time point or over time."
