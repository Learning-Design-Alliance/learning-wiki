The review reports that "Doig et al. found that the reliability of the OSCE exam results were not significantly associated with the clinical background of examiners", station organization, or time of exam.

"Doig et al. found that the reliability of the OSCE exam results were not significantly associated with the clinical background of examiners (senior medicine residents, family physicians, other specialists), the organization of the OSCE station and the time of the exam (morning versus afternoon session) [76]."
