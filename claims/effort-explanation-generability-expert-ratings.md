# Experts rate the Bloch sphere as differing in effort in explanation from the Circle Notation and Qake, while generability shows no significant pairwise differences after correction

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Expert ratings of effort in explanation differed significantly between representations, specifically between the Circle Notation and the Bloch sphere (d = 0.55) and between Qake and the Bloch sphere (d = 0.56). [→ Qerimi 2025](#qerimi-2025)

## Evidence

### Qerimi 2025

Qerimi, L., Malone, S., Rexigel, E., Mehlhase, S., Kuhn, J., Küchemann, S. (2025). Exploring the mechanisms of qubit representations and introducing a new category system for visual representations: Results from expert ratings. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1)

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Expert rating of the effort required to explain each representation; the Friedman test was significant, with differences "specifically between the Circle Notation and the Bloch sphere (d = 0.55, p = .034)". Per the article's scoring, a high mean value means less effort in explanation.

> "The data indicated significant differences between the representations in terms of effort in explanation ( χ2(2) = 8 .9333, p < .01) specifically between the Circle Notation and the Bloch sphere (d = 0.55, p = .034) and between Qake and the Bloch sphere (d = 0.56, p = .032)."

### Qerimi 2025 (2)

Qerimi, L., Malone, S., Rexigel, E., Mehlhase, S., Kuhn, J., Küchemann, S. (2025). Exploring the mechanisms of qubit representations and introducing a new category system for visual representations: Results from expert ratings. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert rating of how readily learners can reproduce each representation; after a significant omnibus Friedman test, pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni correction showed "no significant differences between the individual representations (p > .05)".

> "However, the subsequent pairwise comparisons using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test with Bonferroni correction showed no significant differences between the individual representations (p > .05)."
