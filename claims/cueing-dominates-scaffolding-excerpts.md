Categorisation of 164 excerpts from 18 included articles in this scoping review. The results report that "59.76% of the analysed excerpts described actions characterised by cueing"; separately, 40 excerpts (24.39%) contained active verbal chunking, 20 (12.20%) active nonverbal cueing, and 28 (17.07%) combined verbal-nonverbal cueing.

> "Within expert-novice interactions, 59.76% of the analysed excerpts described actions characterised by cueing."
