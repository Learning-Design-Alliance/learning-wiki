### McCoy C. Eric MD 2019

McCoy C. Eric MD, MPH; Rahman Asif MD; Rendon Juan C. MD; Anderson Craig L. PhD, MPH; Langdorf Mark I. MD, MPHE; Lotfipour Shahram MD, MPH; Chakravarthy Bharath MD, MPH. (2019). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized parallel-arm trial (70 analyzed), compression fraction — the proportion of time CPR was delivered while the patient was without a perfusing rhythm — was higher in the SIM group. The article reports "The compression fraction was 0.724" for SIM versus 0.679 for STD, p=0.01. No effect size was printed.

> "The compression fraction was 0.724 (95% CI [0.699 – 0.751]) for the SIM group and 0.679 (95% CI [0.655 – 0.702]) for the STD group, p=0.01"
