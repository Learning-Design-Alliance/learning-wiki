`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In reviewing how the 8 prominent measures were developed, the author reports that five appear to have been developed by generating an item pool and using factor analysis to narrow it, and that "many of these measures also appear to have been initially validated using small, non-representative, primarily White and middle-class samples" with no demographic factor-structure tests.

> "many of these measures also appear to have been initially validated using small, non-representative, primarily White and middle-class samples, with none appearing to test for differences in factor structure based on race-ethnicity, socioeconomic status (SES), or other potentially meaningful demographic or cultural group indicators."
