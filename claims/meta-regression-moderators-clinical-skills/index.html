Random-effects meta-regression (REML estimation with Knapp-Hartung modification) of clinical-skills effect sizes across seven covariates, after removing two collinear covariates (mean VIF = 132.79 in the initial model). Residual heterogeneity remained moderate (residual I² = 66.90%).

"these covariates jointly explained a substantial proportion of between-study variance (Adjusted R² = 75.59%), with significant moderating effects identified for participants' country (P = 0.001), outcome type (P = 0.009), grade (P = 0.026), and sample size (P = 0.022)"
