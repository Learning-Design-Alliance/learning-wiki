## Evidence

### Lixiang Yan 2026

Lixiang Yan, Yueqiao Jin, Xibin Han, Dragan Gasevic. (2026). Socially fluent AI decouples conversational signals from source identity in online interaction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23426 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.23426](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.23426)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RSA across 1,148 targets with complete data showed "interactional cue structure was strongly aligned with ground-truth identity" (ρ = .455, permutation p < .001), while judgment structure bore no relation to cues (ρ ≈ 0, p = .924) and aligned with humanness and trust ratings (ρ = .247).

> "interactional cue structure was strongly aligned with ground-truth identity (Spearman 𝜌 = .455, 95% bootstrap CI [.453, .456], permutation 𝑝 < .001), indicating that AI and human teammates occupied systematically separable regions in cue space"
