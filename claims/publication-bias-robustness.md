## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Funnel plots, Egger's test, publication-status moderation, and fail-safe N analysis showed limited evidence of publication bias. [→ Tianyi Ma 2024](#tianyi-ma-2024)

## Evidence

### Tianyi Ma 2024

Tianyi Ma, Cassandra L. Tellegen, Julie Hodges, Matthew R. Sanders. (2024). The Associations Between Parenting Self-Efficacy and Parents' Contributions to the Home-School Partnership Among Parents of Primary School Students: a Multilevel Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09937-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three publication-bias tests were applied. Published studies had larger effect sizes than unpublished (Fisher's Z = .222 vs .149) but not significantly (p = .124); fail-safe N analysis suggested at least 2797 null effect sizes would be needed to reduce the pooled effect to non-significance.

> "Funnel plot (see Fig. 2) and Egger's test of heterogeneity suggested that the distribution of effect sizes was largely symmetrical, thus showing limited evidence of publication bias, t(18) = 0.111, p = .913."
