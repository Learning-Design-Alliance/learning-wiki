## Evidence

### Mayrhofer A 2016

Mayrhofer A, Goodman C, Smeeton N, Handley M, Amador S, Davies S. (2016). The feasibility of a train-the-trainer approach to end of life care training in care homes: an evaluation. BMC Palliative Care 15:11. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-016-0081-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of learners trained per home across 17 participating care homes, using the Mann–Whitney U test on the number of learners trained. The article reports "Significantly more learners were trained in stable care homes (median = 10.5)" with p = 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "Significantly more learners were trained in stable care homes (median = 10.5) than in those that were not stable (median = 4.0) (p = 0.001, Mann–Whitney U test)."
