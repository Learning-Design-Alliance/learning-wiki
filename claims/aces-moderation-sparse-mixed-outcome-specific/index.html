Evidence¶

Stoddard 2026¶

Stoddard, S. A., Moore, C. R., Polick, C. S., & Moss, J. M. (2026). Cumulative Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs) and School Outcomes During Adolescence: A Scoping Review. The Journal of School Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/josh.70209

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of five studies assessing moderators. The review reports school connection marginally buffered some associations, income moderated disengagement, a suspension-by-ACE interaction was significant only for Black students, and two studies found no significant interactions. No effect sizes are printed.

"Together, these findings suggest that moderation in the ACE–school outcome association may be outcome-specific and context-dependent."
