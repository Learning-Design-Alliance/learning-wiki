Structural equation modeling on 457 Mexican high school students tested one model per subject with satisfaction and boredom as exogenous variables. The path diagram showed "the models which best predict school satisfaction are SP (γ = 0.80), MATH (γ = 0.64), and ENG (γ = 0.50)"; no standardized effect size beyond these path coefficients is printed.
