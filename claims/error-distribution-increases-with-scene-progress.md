> "As shown in Table 3, the number of detected parts was high in Scenes 3 and 4. This is thought to be attributed to the fact that as the surgery progresses, the treatment area moves to the back and is closer to important organs, while the range of movement of the surgical instrument becomes narrower, which increases the level of difficulty."
