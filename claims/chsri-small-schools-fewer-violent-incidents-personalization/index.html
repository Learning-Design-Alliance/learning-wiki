Sporte 2004¶

Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/notes-ground-teachers-principals-and-students-perspectives-chicago-high-school-redesign

Interview finding from the 2004 study of 11 small schools; a principal reported a metal-detector sweep yielded "zero expellable offenses" compared with a minimum of six previously.

"There seem to be fewer violent incidents at small schools. We haven't had a ﬁght, which is good… Principal We just had a metal detector search."

Sporte 2004 (2)¶

"Many students and staff described their school as providing a personalized environment with academic support.7"
