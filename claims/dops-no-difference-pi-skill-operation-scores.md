## Evidence

### Zhang W 2024

Zhang W, Peng Y, Zhuang H, Yu H, Liu Q, Gu Y, Yao J. (2024). Application of direct observation of operational skills in nursing skill evaluation of pressure injury: A randomized clinical trial. Int Wound J. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.14498

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 82 nurses (41 per group) after a six-month PI training program; three skill operations scored by two assessors in each group. The article reports "no statistically significant difference (p > 0.05)"; both groups achieved a 100% pass rate. Equivalence was not tested.

> "When comparing the study group to the control group in terms of the three PI skill operation scores, there was no statistically significant difference (p > 0.05)."
