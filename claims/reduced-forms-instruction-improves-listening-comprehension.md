## Evidence

### Mohammad Saber Khaghaninezhad & Ghasem Jafarzadeh 2014

Mohammad Saber Khaghaninezhad & Ghasem Jafarzadeh. (2014). Investigating the Effect of Reduced Forms Instruction on EFL Learners' Listening and Speaking Abilities. English Language Teaching, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n1p159

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 50 Iranian EFL learners (25 per group) at a private language institution; the experimental group received 10 weeks of reduced-forms instruction while the control continued regular classes. The post-test means were 10.36 versus 8.90, and the article reports the experimental group "significantly outperformed the control on this test".

> "Table 12 reveals that there was a significant difference between these two group's performances in the "general listening comprehension" post-test (p = 0.003 < .05) i.e., the experimental group significantly outperformed the control on this test."
