# HRV improvement predicts academic grades, working memory, and self-pressure, but not Stroop cognitive flexibility

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In linear regression, HRV explained 48.5% of the variance in academic grades (F(1, 48) = 45.19, p < 0.001), 30.5% in working memory, and 21.5% in self-pressure, but did not significantly predict Stroop performance (p > 0.05). [→ Latino Francesca; Tafuri Domenico; Tafuri Francesco 2025](#latino-francesca-tafuri-domenico-tafuri-francesco-2025)
`q3 i?` HRV was not significantly correlated with cognitive flexibility on the Stroop Task (r = 0.10, p = 0.32), though it correlated positively with self-pressure (r = 0.32), well-being (r = 0.33), working memory (r = 0.31), and grades (r = 0.30). [→ Latino Francesca; Tafuri Domenico; Tafuri Francesco 2025 (2)](#latino-francesca-tafuri-domenico-tafuri-francesco-2025-2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple linear regression models (Figure 2) testing whether HRV change predicted academic grades, working memory, self-pressure, and Stroop performance, controlling for baseline scores, age, sex, and physical activity level where appropriate.

> "F(1, 48) = 45.19, p < 0.001 → The model is highly significant. R2 = 0.485 → 48.5% of the variance in academic grades is explained by HRV"

### Latino Francesca; Tafuri Domenico; Tafuri Francesco 2025 (2)

Latino Francesca; Tafuri Domenico; Tafuri Francesco. (2025). Academic Self-Pressure and Physiological Responses in Adolescents: A Pilot Experimental Study on the Moderating Role of an Escape Room-Based Physical Activity Intervention on Cognitive and Academic Outcomes. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22060948

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis (Figure 1) of HRV with outcome measures in the full sample; positive correlations with self-pressure (r = 0.32, p = 0.024), well-being (r = 0.33, p = 0.023), Digit Span (r = 0.31, p = 0.029), and grades (r = 0.30, p = 0.033) were reported.

> "No significant association was found between HRV and cognitive flexibility (Stroop Task) (r = 0.10, p = 0.32), indicating that other factors might influence this relationship"
