### Turan D 2026

Turan D, Pekmezci H. (2026). Simulation-based education in paramedic training: effects of perceived competence and practice frequency on clinical skills. BMC Medical Education, 26:487. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08541-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on self-reported perceived competence (15 items, Cronbach's alpha 0.922) of 60 paramedic students before and after the training program; overall medians moved from 1.00 to 5.00 (z = -6.81, p < 0.001).
