`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the findings section of this interview study of 13 engineering majors, six students reported that awareness of a faster AI alternative made effort feel less worthwhile. P4 said "I'm spending all this time doing it, and then an AI could do it in, like, 3 seconds."

> "Several students (n=6) who expressed strong baseline motivation described how knowing that AI could complete the assignment changed how worthwhile their effort felt."
