# Redundancy effect on comprehension: TP and TO conditions outperformed the TPO condition in a mobile learning experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Fifth-grade students in the TP condition and the TO condition each scored significantly higher on the leaf-morphology comprehension test than students in the TPO condition. [→ Liu 2011](#liu-2011)

## Evidence

### Liu 2011

Liu, T.-C., Lin, Y.-C., Tsai, M.-J., & Paas, F. (2011). Split-attention and redundancy effects on mobile learning in physical environments. Computers & Education. https://ro.uow.edu.au/edupapers/784

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Planned contrasts in the randomized three-condition experiment (n=81) showed TP (d=0.56) and TO (d=0.57) each "performed significantly better than the TPO condition on the comprehension test"; TPO mean was 7.22 versus 8.81 (TP) and 8.74 (TO).

> "the TP condition (t (78) = 2.05, p < 0.05, Cohen’s d= 0.56) and TO condition (t (78) = 2.10, p < 0.05, Cohen’s d= 0.57) performed significantly better than the TPO condition on the comprehension test."
