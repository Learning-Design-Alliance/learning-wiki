`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square subgroup analysis of log codes by postgraduate year across the 10,574-log cohort. The article reports "a significant difference in these logs between PGY (P < 0.001)" and a trend where increasing PGY was associated with decreased chance of a log changing future patient care. No effect size is printed.

> "Postgraduate year subgroup analysis found first-year residents recorded the most logs (602, 23.3%) that indicated research would lead to a future change in patient management (all codes including 1 as the second digit). There was a significant difference in these logs between PGY (P < 0.001)"
