## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Residents found recording and watching themselves on video stressful and uncomfortable, and residents reported critical challenges for facilitator skills in connecting feedback with pertinent theoretical frameworks. [→ Dohms M. C. 2020](#dohms-m-c-2020)

## Evidence

### Dohms M. C. 2020

Dohms M. C., Collares C. F., Tibério I. C. (2020). Video-based feedback using real consultations for a formative assessment in communication skills. BMC Medical Education 20:57. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1955-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of the qualitative questionnaires after the VF sessions in the pre/post controlled study. Two residents reported discomfort, e.g. "I don't like to see myself on video", while still enjoying the group discussion and colleagues' videos.

> "Some challenges related to the intervention: two residents reported that the experience of being videotaped and later watching themselves with the group was uncomfortable."
