## Evidence

### Kalantari Reza 2021

Kalantari Reza, Zamanian Zahra, Hasanshahi Mehdi, Faghihi Seyed Aliakbar, Jamali Jamshid, Niakan Hadi. (2021). The Effect of Educational Intervention on the Improvement of Nontechnical Skills in Circulating Nurses. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/5856730

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon within-group tests in the intervention group showed significant improvement in all domains; Table 5 reports improvement percentages of 30.9% task management, 42.5% teamwork, 16.7% situational awareness, 26.6% communication, 21% leadership, and 27.5% overall. The abstract states "the highest and lowest improvements were observed in teamwork (42%) and situational awareness (16.7%)".

> "The results revealed a significant improvement in the scores of all domains of nontechnical skills in the intervention group (p < 0.05). The highest and lowest improvements were observed in teamwork (42%) and situational awareness (16.7%), respectively."
