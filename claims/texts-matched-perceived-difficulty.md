### Sackstein S 2015

Sackstein S, Spark L & Jenkins A. (2015). Are e-books effective tools for learning? Reading speed and comprehension: iPad vs. paper. South African Journal of Education, 35(4), Art. #1202. https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v35n4a1202

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-tests on perceived difficulty ratings confirmed no significant differences between Text 1 and Text 2 for School A (p = .110), School B (p = .539), or university students (p = .292).

> "Across all three experimental groups , students did not perceive either text to be at a greater level of difficulty, and therefore , it can be seen that the texts and their respective questions were well matched in terms of difficulty"
