## Evidence

### Luo Dingyuan 2025

Luo Dingyuan, Liu Mengke, Yu Runyuan, Liu Yulian, Jiang Wenjun, Fan Qi, Kuang Naifeng, Gao Qiang, Yin Tao, Zheng Zuncheng. (2025). Evaluating the performance of GPT-3.5, GPT-4, and GPT-4o in the Chinese National Medical Licensing Examination. Scientific Reports 15, 14119. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-98949-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative evaluation of 600 NMLE questions from 2020 and 2021 answered by the three models via the OpenAI chat interface, with chi-square tests comparing accuracy. GPT-4o scored "84.2%" (2020) and "88.2%" (2021); differences were statistically significant (P < 0.001).

> "In the 2020 NMLE test, the overall accuracy rates of GPT-3.5, GPT-4, and GPT-4o were 50.5%, 74.7%, and 84.2%, respectively. The accuracy rates for the 2021 NMLE test were 50.8%, 73.2%, and 88.2%."
