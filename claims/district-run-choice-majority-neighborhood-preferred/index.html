q2 · i? · associational · r?

Descriptive analysis of NCES survey data reported in the Tapestry of Public Education section. The report states 37.3% of parents said choice was available and 30.5% considered other schools, and that "More than three fourths of parents said their children's current school was their first choice."

"According to the National Center for Education Statistics, in 2012, the most recent year for which data are available, 37.3% of parents said public school choice was available in their district, and 30.5% said they considered other schools beyond those their children were slated to attend."
