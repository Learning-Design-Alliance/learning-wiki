Evidence¶

Avivit Arvatz 2025¶

Avivit Arvatz, Roee Peretz, Yehudit Judy Dori. (2025). Self-regulated learning and reflection: a tool for teachers and students. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09415-3

q2 · i? · design · r2

Chi-square test of association between tertile-binned overall SRL score and externally graded reflection level (N = 154); the heat chart showed the highest concentration of participants (21%) at SRL Level 2 and Reflection Level 1, and half of the highest-SRL participants (11% of 22%) showed the highest reflection level.

"we performed a Chi-Square test on the aggregate sample of TG1 and TG2 combined (N = 154), which established the significance of the association between the SRL level and the reflection level χ 2(1, N = 154) = 4.72, p < 0.03."
