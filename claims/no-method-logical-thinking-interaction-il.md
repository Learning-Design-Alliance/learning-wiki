# No statistically significant interaction effect between instructional method and logical thinking level on information literacy skill (F=1.638, p=0.198)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The instructional method x logical thinking level interaction on information literacy skill did not reach statistical significance, indicating HLT and LLT students benefited similarly across the three methods. [→ Ismail et al. 2018](#ismail-et-al-2018)

## Evidence

### Ismail et al. 2018

Mohd Nasir Ismail, Nurfaezah Mamat, Adnan Jamaludin. (2018). The Effects of Webopac Self Training Tool with Guided Exploration on Information Literacy Skills among First Year Degree Students. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165734

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVA of the interaction term in the 3x2 factorial design (n=150). The article prints F=1.638, p=0.198, effect size 0.022, observed power 0.341 in Table 3, and marks the interaction Not Sig. Equivalence between methods was not formally tested.

> "There is an inter action effect between the instructional method and the student’s logical thinking level on information literacy skills across the three groups (F-value= 1.638,  p-value= 0.198)."
