## Evidence

### Cai Bohong 2025

Cai Bohong, Wang Zihan, Lyu Xi, Qiao Yiran, Liu Yuhan, Wu Danting, Li Sihang, Yao Qi, Chen Baixiang, Fu Fujian, Jiang Wei. (2025). Surgeon perceptions of essential skills and simulation for percutaneous transforaminal endoscopic discectomy training. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34397-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on duration of received simulated training among 154 surgeons; overall mean was 22.797 ± 43.572 hours (Table 6). The ANOVA on log-transformed hours was non-significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "a one-way ANOVA on logarithmically transformed training hours revealed no statistically significant difference in the total duration of simulation training across experience levels. Despite this, descriptive data showed a trend wherein junior surgeons tended to report longer overall training durations than senior surgeons."
