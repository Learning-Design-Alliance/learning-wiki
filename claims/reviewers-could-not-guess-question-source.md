## Evidence

### Chauhan 2025

Chauhan, A., Khaliq, F., & Nayak, K. R. (2025). Assessing Quality of Scenario-Based Multiple-Choice Questions in Physiology: Faculty-Generated vs. ChatGPT-Generated Questions among Phase I Medical Students. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 35, 2315–2344. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00471-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Blind-source judgment task during the development phase, in which five independent external reviewers rated 120 coded, randomized SBMCQs and guessed each item's source. The article reports no reviewer reached a 90% correct guess rate; no effect size is printed.

> "the study's results indicated that there was no correlation between the guesses made by the independent faculty reviewers on the source of SBMCQ by faculty or ChatGPT. None of the five reviewers achieved a correct guess rate of 90% or above."
