Sarah Herrera 2021¶

Sarah Herrera, Beth M. Phillips, Yi-Chieh Newton, Jennifer L. Dombek, and James A. Hernandez. (2021). Effectiveness of Early Literacy Instruction: Summary of 20 Years of Research. Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast, REL 2021-084. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic review using WWC-modeled standards of 132 interventions from 109 high-quality experimental or quasi-experimental studies published 1997-2017. The review reports "38 demonstrated effectiveness in at least one language or literacy outcome domain relative to the comparison group"; all others except one showed inconclusive effects.

"Of the 132 interventions evaluated by high-quality impact studies, 38 demonstrated effectiveness in at least one language or literacy outcome domain relative to the comparison group."
