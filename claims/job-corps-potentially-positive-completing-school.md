# Job Corps has potentially positive effects on completing school: more youth without credentials earned a diploma or GED

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Among youth who entered without a high school diploma or GED, 43% of Job Corps students earned one by the 48-month follow-up, compared with 26% of control students — a statistically significant and substantively important difference (Cox effect size 0.33, improvement index +13). ## Evidence

### Job Corps 2008

Job Corps. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Job Corps. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://jobcorps.dol.gov/about.htm

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial, completing school domain, restricted to respondents without a high school credential at entry (n = 8,597). The WWC appendix reports group rates of 47.3% vs 34.4%, mean difference 12.9, effect size 0.33, statistically significant, improvement index +13 percentile points.

> "among those who entered the program without a high school diploma or GED certificate, 43% of Job Corps students earned one by the end of the 48-month follow-up period, compared with 26% of control group students."
