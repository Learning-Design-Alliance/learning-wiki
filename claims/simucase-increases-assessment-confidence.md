### Elliott 2021

Elliott, Haley and Brumbaugh, Klaire (2021). Student Perceptions of a Simulated Clinical Experience: A Pilot Study. Teaching and Learning in Communication Sciences & Disorders, 5(1), Article 7. https://doi.org/10.30707/TLCSD5.1.1624982519.538001

`q2 · i?`

Focus-group perceptions from the same 10-participant qualitative pilot. Students practiced protocol completion and scoring within each case and used terms like "felt comfortable" and "confident" about selecting, administering, scoring, and interpreting assessments. Perceptions only; no performance measure.

> "The participants agreed that their confidence in testing procedures increased following the Simucase course experience. Each assigned case involved the completion of a part-task trainer or a full assessment."
