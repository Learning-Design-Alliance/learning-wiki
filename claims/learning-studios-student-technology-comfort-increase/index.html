Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot 2017¶

Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot. (2017). Digital Promise Global. https://global.digitalpromise.org/learning-studios

q2 · i? · design · r2

Exploratory pilot-year research in the Learning Studios using pre/post/follow-up student surveys plus interviews and focus groups. The report states student comfort "teaching others the technology, and troubleshooting technology issues increased significantly." No effect size is printed.

"Student comfort while using technology (specifically the Sprout computer and 3D printer), teaching others the technology, and troubleshooting technology issues increased significantly."
