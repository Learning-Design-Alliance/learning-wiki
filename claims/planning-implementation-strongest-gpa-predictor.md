## Evidence

### Boadum O 2026

Boadum O, Wilson TD, Thompson K, Robertson EM, Notebaert A. (2026). Self-directed learning readiness in preclinical medical education: Patterns, variation, and academic outcomes from a mixed longitudinal and cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09867-4

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression with cluster-robust standard errors predicting self-reported cumulative GPA from standardized SDLI subscale scores (Year 1 n = 218; Year 2 n = 328; combined n = 546). The standardized β for planning and implementation was small (β = 0.154 in the combined model), with FDR correction applied.

> "Across all models, PI was the strongest positive predictor (Year 1: β = 0.142, 95% CI 0.045–0.238, p = .004; Year 2: β = 0.137, 95% CI 0.060–0.214, p < .001; Combined: β = 0.154, 95% CI 0.094–0.215, p < .001)."

### Boadum O 2026 (2)

Boadum O, Wilson TD, Thompson K, Robertson EM, Notebaert A. (2026). Self-directed learning readiness in preclinical medical education: Patterns, variation, and academic outcomes from a mixed longitudinal and cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09867-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same within-year OLS regression models on the pooled multi-cohort sample. Variance explained fell from R² = 0.222 in Year 1 to R² = 0.090 in Year 2; R² is not treated here as a standardized effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Within-year regression models indicated that the four SDL-R domains collectively explained 22.2% of GPA variance in Year 1 (R² = 0.222, adjusted R² = 0.207, F = 15.18, p < .001) and 9.0% in Year 2 (R² = 0.090, adjusted R² = 0.079, F = 8.03, p < .001)."
