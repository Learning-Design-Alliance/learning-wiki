Evidence¶

Valentín 2013¶

Valentín, A., Mateos, P. M., González-Tablas, M. M., Pérez, L., López, E., & García, I. (2013). Motivation and learning strategies in the use of ICTs among university students. Computers & Education, 61, 52–58. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2012.09.008

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Factor analysis of the ICT-use survey responses of 543 first- and second-year undergraduates at the University of Salamanca. The article reports the analysis "establish four factors ... explaining 57.4% of the total variance."

"we performed a factor analysis of principal components with Varimax rotation on the use of ICTs. This allowed us to establish four factors (Table 2 ), with eigenvalues greater than one and explaining 57.4% of the total variance."
