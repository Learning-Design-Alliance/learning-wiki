## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Instructor motion quantity was greater under explanation than scaffolding, while dyadic and learner motion showed no significant condition differences. [→ Yafeng Pan 2022](#yafeng-pan-2022)

## Evidence

### Yafeng Pan 2022

Yafeng Pan, Suzanne Dikker, Yi Zhu, Cuirong Yang, Yi Hu and Pavel Goldstein. (2022). Instructor-learner body coupling reflects instruction and learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00131-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Individual-level linear mixed-effects model on instructor motion energy in the dyadic experiment showed a main effect of Instructional Approach (β = −0.20, p = 0.01, R²β = 0.25); learner models showed no significant effects. The authors conclude "instructors moved more when using an explanation-based instructional approach than when using a scaffolding approach".

> "indicating that the instructors moved to a larger degree when using an explanation strategy (0.97 ± 0.06) vs. a scaffolding strategy (0.77 ± 0.06)"
