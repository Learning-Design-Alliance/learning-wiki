Evidence¶

Early Risers 2012¶

Early Risers. (2012). WWC Intervention Report: Children Classified as Having an Emotional Disturbance. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.med.umn.edu/psychiatry/research/earlyrisers/home.html

q3 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r1

Randomized effectiveness study in two neighborhood family centers and 10 elementary schools in a large midwestern city, with kindergarten and first-grade students randomized to intervention or comparison. The WWC confirmed "no statistically significant difference between the intervention and comparison groups on the Internalizing Problems Parent Report and Internalizing Problems Teacher Report measures"; the domain average effect size was 0.18.

"August et al. (2003) found, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant difference between the intervention and comparison groups on the Internalizing Problems Parent Report and Internalizing Problems Teacher Report measures."
