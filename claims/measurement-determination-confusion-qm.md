# Students conflated measurement with determination of Dynamical Variables, and one student treated the uncertainty principle as a classical uncertainty

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students often referred to determination of Dynamical Variables as measurement, failing to distinguish arranging/executing a measurement from being in an eigenstate knowing a specific value. [→ Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017](#glauco-cohen-ferreira-pantoja-2017)
`q2 i?` One student (Sean) treated the uncertainty principle as a classical uncertainty, associating uncertainties in momenta with accelerations (attribute b.X.VI). [→ Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017 (2)](#glauco-cohen-ferreira-pantoja-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017

Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja. (2017). A STUDY ON PRE-SERVICE PHYSICS TEACHERS' CONCEPTUALIZATION ON ELEMENTARY QUANTUM MECHANICS. Investigações em Ensino de Ciências – V22 (2), pp. 120-138. https://doi.org/10.22600/1518-8795.ienci2017v22n2p120

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Predicative knowledge analysis of the intervention. The authors note this confusion (attribute b.2.V) persisted despite the difference being stressed in instruction, and that it appeared more frequently in their prior study (Pantoja et al., 2012).

> "Students often refer to determination of Dynamical Variables as measurement. This difference has been stressed, but somehow the students couldn't understand the difference between arranging and executing a measurement (to measure) and being in an eigenstate of a given operator"

### Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017 (2)

Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja. (2017). A STUDY ON PRE-SERVICE PHYSICS TEACHERS' CONCEPTUALIZATION ON ELEMENTARY QUANTUM MECHANICS. Investigações em Ensino de Ciências – V22 (2), pp. 120-138. https://doi.org/10.22600/1518-8795.ienci2017v22n2p120

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same predicative analysis, identifying a specific noticeable problem in understanding: a single student's classical reading of the uncertainty principle, evidenced in his written answers.

> "The feature b.X.VI reveals some confusion in conceiving the uncertainty principle. One student (Sean) treats it as a classical uncertainty, since he associates the uncertainties in momenta, as accelerations."
