### Jina R 2013

Jina R, Jewkes R, Christofides N, Loots L. (2013). Knowledge and confidence of South African health care providers regarding post-rape care: a cross-sectional study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-13-257

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional pre-training questionnaire study of 124 providers (81.6% of those trained) from eight provinces. The knowledge assessment used 66 multiple choice questions; the median score was "37.3%" and only a quarter scored 50% or more.

> "The levels of knowledge were low with a median percentage score on the knowledge assessment of 37.3% and a range of 0% to 65.3%. A quarter of the providers (n=29) scored 50% or more."
