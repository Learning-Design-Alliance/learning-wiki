# Subjective learner-reaction measures (Kirkpatrick level 1) were used in 22 of 51 studies, all reporting positive effects, mostly with non-validated questionnaires

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Twenty-two of 51 studies measured impact only by subjective learner reaction/opinion; all reported a positive effect, but most questionnaires were purpose-designed and not formally validated. [→ James 2019](#james-2019)

## Evidence

### James 2019

James, H. K., Chapman, A. W., Pattison, G. T. R., Griffin, D. R. and Fisher, J. D. (2019). Systematic review of the current status of cadaveric simulation for surgical training. The British Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1002/bjs.11325

`q2 · i?`

Review synthesis of Kirkpatrick level 1 studies: 21 were non-comparative descriptive studies (OCEBM level 3) and one a comparative cohort study. The review reports all 22 used participant questionnaires, "most of which were purpose-designed and not validated formally".

> "All 22 studies used participant questionnaires to assess learner reaction, most of which were purpose‐designed38 and not validated formally."
