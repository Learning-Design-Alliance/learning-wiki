### Aljamal Husam 2025

Aljamal Husam, Alawneh Rama, Derbas Afnan, Edaibes Mohammad, Ahmed Aya, Amer Lama, Alzoubi Hiba, Serhan Hashem Abu Habuser. (2025). Efficacy of mind maps and concept maps in enhancing academic performance among undergraduate medical students in the preclinical stage: a systematic review. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10437-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review synthesis of two included RCTs (Mukhopadhyay et al. 2019; Wickramasinghe et al. 2007). Mukhopadhyay reported no significant difference in post-test scores (p = 0.8682); Wickramasinghe found no difference between mind map (31.3%) and control (37.6%) groups. No effect sizes printed.

> "Two studies found no significant differences in performance (p > 0.05), but students consistently expressed a preference for these methods due to their utility in understanding and summarizing information."

### Aljamal Husam 2025 (2)

Efficacy of mind maps and concept maps in enhancing academic performance among undergraduate medical students in the preclinical stage: a systematic review. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10437-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Perception data from the Wickramasinghe et al. (2007) RCT as reported in the review: despite no significant performance difference, "97.1% of students in the mind map group found the method helpful for summarizing information" and wanted to continue using it.

> "Despite this, 97.1% of students in the mind map group found the method helpful for summarizing information and expressed a strong interest in continuing its use."
