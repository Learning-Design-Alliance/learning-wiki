Evidence¶

Rothlind Erica 2020¶

Rothlind Erica, Fors Uno, Salminen Helena, Wändell Per, Ekblad Solvig. (2020). The informal curriculum of family medicine – what does it entail and how is it taught to residents? A systematic review. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-020-01120-1

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review assessment of 11 included interventions using Kirkpatrick's hierarchy and the BEME classification scale (Table 1): 10 curricula at level 2, 1 at level 3, 0 at levels 1 and 4; BEME strength ratings of 3, 3, and 5 studies at levels 1–3 respectively. No effect size printed.

"There was only one study where a change in behavior could be observed [47], the remaining studies only dealt with learning in terms of perceived modification of attitudes/perceptions/knowledge or skills [17, 43–46, 48–52]. The mean score was 2, and the median was 2."
