## Evidence

### Mengjia Liu 2026

Mengjia Liu, Harwati Hashim, Nur Ainil Sulaiman. (2026). A systematic review of emerging technology applications for teaching English as a foreign language across different educational levels. Educational Research Review 52 (2026) 100795. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2026.100795

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 33 experimental and quasi-experimental studies (N = 3181) using Hedges' g. The review reports "a small-to-moderate positive overall effect" with substantial heterogeneity; 27 of 33 studies (81.8%) showed positive effects, 18 (54.5%) reaching statistical significance.

> "The random-effects meta-analysis of all 33 studies revealed a small-to-moderate positive overall effect of emerging technologies on TEFL outcomes (Hedges’ g = 0.38, 95% CI [0.26, 0.50], p < 0.001), with substantial heterogeneity (Q(32) = 324.56, p < 0.001; I2 = 90.14%)."
