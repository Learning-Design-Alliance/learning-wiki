Post hoc McNemar pairwise comparisons among chatbots. On Day-0, significant differences were found between GPT-4o mini and GPT-4o (p = 0.008), GPT-4o mini and CoPilot Pro (p = 0.001), and GPT-4o mini and Gemini Advance (p = 0.008); on Month-1, p = 0.017, 0.001, and 0.001 respectively. No effect size is printed.
