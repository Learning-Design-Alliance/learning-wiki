`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM result from the 206-student survey. "Higher baseline relatedness was negatively associated with perceived relatedness satisfaction after AI chatbot use (β=−. 259,p=. 014)"; the authors describe a relatedness-specific diminishing-returns pattern.
