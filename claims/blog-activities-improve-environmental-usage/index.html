Saltan 2017¶

Saltan, F., & Divarci, O. F. (2017). Using Blogs to Improve Elementary School Students’ Environmental Literacy in Science Class. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(3), 347-355. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.3.347

Repeated-measures t-test on the usage subscale for 56 students. Post-test mean (70.73) exceeded pre-test mean (66.57), a statistically significant difference (t(55) = -3.36, p = 0.001).

"It has been realized that there is a crucial difference between the students’ pre -test (m=66, 57, sd=9, 39) and post -test (m=70,73 ; sd=8,81) use of environment scores (t(55)=-3,36, p=0.001)."
