Evidence¶

Walter R. Miles and Julian E. Butterworth 1912¶

Walter R. Miles and Julian E. Butterworth. (1912). A Tentative Standardization of a Completion Test. The Journal of Educational Psychology, pp. 329-336. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Tentative+Standardization+of+a+Completion+Test%22

q2 · i? · design · r2

Combined introspective ratings from the 25 subjects produced these pooled frequencies; the authors then reasoned that "the very difficult elisions are 11.1 times as difficult as words of average difficulty" and reduced the proportions to fractions of unity (.048, .427, .533).

"83.2% of the elisions were marked as of average difficulty. 9.3% of the elisions were marked as of more than average difficulty. 7.5% of the elisions were marked as very difficult."
