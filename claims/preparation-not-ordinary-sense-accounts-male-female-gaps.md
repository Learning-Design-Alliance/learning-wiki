## Evidence

### M. B. Weissman 2020

M. B. Weissman. (2020). Comment on "Demographic gaps or preparation gaps?: The large impact of incoming preparation on performance of students in introductory physics". Department of Physics, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2011.06678](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2011.06678)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The comment's interpretive argument: since males and females go to the same schools and took the same nominal courses, the same-coursework finding undermines the preparation explanation specifically for the male/female gap. This is the comment's reasoning, not a new data analysis.

> "Thus the results provide strong evidence that it is not preparation, at least  in the ordinary sense of the word, that accounts for the male/female differences in both precollege and college exams."
