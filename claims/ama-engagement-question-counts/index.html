Quantitative analysis of interaction logs from the 63-student school study measured engagement by questions posed: "The majority (37 of 63 students) showed moderate interaction (1 to 3 questions)", with 13 students asking 4-6 and a standout first-grader asking 21 questions (Figure 4).
