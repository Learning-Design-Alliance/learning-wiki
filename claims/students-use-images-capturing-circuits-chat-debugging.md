### John Hu and Andrew J. Ash 2026

John Hu and Andrew J. Ash. (2026). Chat Debugging: An Exploratory Study of Human-AI Collaboration to Debug Analog Circuits. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02955

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of voluntarily shared chat logs from a 30-minute timed hands-on lab final exam found an image-based usage pattern the authors had not expected: "2 out of 5 chat logs in Spring 2025 and 5 out of 12 chat logs in Fall 2025 followed this usage pattern." The authors attribute it to exam time pressure and the convenience of photos.
