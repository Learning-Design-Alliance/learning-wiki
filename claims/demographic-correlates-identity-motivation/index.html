Evidence¶

Jamaal S. Matthews 2014¶

Jamaal S. Matthews, Meeta Banerjee, Fani Lauermann. (2014). Academic Identity Formation and Motivation Among Ethnic Minority Adolescents: The Role of the “Self” Between Internal and External Perceptions of Identity. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12318

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Initial descriptive correlational analyses (Table 1) of the 600-adolescent sample; the quote also reports adolescents in higher grades reported somewhat lower value (r = −.14), belonging (r = −.10), and self-regulation (r = −.20), all ps < .05. All are small bivariate correlations.

"males reported somewhat lower mastery orientation (r = /C0 .09), self-efﬁcacy ( r = /C0 .11), and self-regula- tion ( r = /C0 .11) than females; Latino adolescents reported somewhat lower sense of school belonging relative to African American adolescents ( r = /C0 .13)"
