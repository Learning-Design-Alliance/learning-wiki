Once Early-Reading Program: Evidence Rating Summary. (2025). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/once-early-reading-program/

q2 · i2 · associational · r?

A secondary dosage analysis within the 2024-2025 Tennessee quasi-experimental study compared students completing 80 or more sessions with other program students. The printed effect size is +0.46; the comparison is observational within the study, not a randomly assigned contrast.

"The study also found that students who completed 80 or more sessions demonstrated meaningfully larger gains (effect size +0.46), suggesting that program dosage is a key driver of outcomes."
