## Evidence

### Jaehn M 2025

Jaehn M, Hissbach J, Frickhoeffer M, Weppert D, Zimmerhofer A, Hampe W, Kadmon M, Becker N. (2025). Predictive validity of admission tests and educational attainment on preclinical academic performance – a multisite study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07974-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Nationwide survey study of 2113 medical students from all 38 German medical schools, matching self-reported PCGPA and M1 results with official admission test scores, with multivariate correction for range restriction. The article reports "correlation coefficients ranged between 0.23 and 0.48"; no standardized effect size beyond the correlations is printed.

> "In our study, we found significant associations of admission test scores with academic performance in preclinical years. The magnitude of correlation coefficients ranged between 0.23 and 0.48 and is thereby in line with results of the meta-analysis by Schult et al. [18]."
