## Evidence

### Swire-Thompson 2025

Swire-Thompson, Briony, Butler, Lucy, & Rapp, David N. (2025). The truth sandwich format does not enhance the correction of misinformation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 117(6), 851–862. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000961

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Pre-registered within-subjects experiment in which participants received truth sandwich corrections, bottom-loaded refutations, and uncorrected control claims for false cancer statements. Compared to control, "both the bottom–loaded refutation and truth sandwich reduced belief in the misinformation" (both ps < .001).

> "Compared to the control, both the bottom–loaded refutation and truth sandwich reduced belief in the misinformation, t(516) = 28.59, p < .001; t(516) = 29.61, p < .001 respectively."
