# Sensitivity reanalyses (outlier removal and sample-standardized age) confirmed that PGS is significantly associated with WM change (all p < 0.01) with no PGS × group interaction.

## Evidence

### Bruno Sauce 2021

Bruno Sauce, John Wiedenhoeft, Nicholas Judd and Torkel Klingberg. (2021). Change by challenge: A common genetic basis behind childhood cognitive development and cognitive training. npj Science of Learning 6:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00096-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robustness check on the combined 344-participant dataset with two modifications applied separately and combined: removing five participants with WM change scores beyond three SD from the mean, and standardizing age within each sample. All reanalyses showed the association with p < 0.01 and no PGS × group interaction.

> "Reanalyses with these modi ﬁcations (separately and combined) conﬁrmed our main ﬁnding: that PGS has a signi ﬁcant association to change in WM (all p < 0.01), but there was no interaction between PGS and group."
