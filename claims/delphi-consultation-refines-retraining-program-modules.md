`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Delphi study assessing inter-rater reliability and consensus among 22 experts in two rounds of consultation. Response rates were 100% in the first round and 95.45% in the second, with authority coefficients of 0.90 and 0.89.

> "The Kendall's W coefficients were 0.273 (P < 0.001) and 0.249 (P < 0.001), indicating a significant level of agreement among experts."

### He Peipei 2026 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Delphi expert consultation with 22 experts refined the preliminary framework of 9 learning modules and 38 learning directories into a final program of 11 learning modules and 38 learning directories, comprising 28 theoretical courses and 10 practical components.

> "After two rounds of Delphi consultation, the final retraining program was expanded to include 11 learning modules and 38 learning directories, consisting of 28 theoretical courses and 10 practical components."
