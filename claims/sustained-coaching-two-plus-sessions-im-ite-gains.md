# Two or more coaching meetings are associated with significant gains in both IM-ITE percent correct and percentile scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2` · `i2` medium
> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Residents meeting a coach two or more times improved IM-ITE percent correct (p = 0.015, ES = 0.788) and percentile (p = 0.003, ES = 0.999) versus those meeting once or not coached. [→ Burtson Kathryn M. MD 2025](#burtson-kathryn-m-md-2025)

## Evidence

### Burtson Kathryn M. MD 2025

Burtson Kathryn M. MD, Wilson Kelsey R. DO, Kiger Michelle E. MD, Jung Eulho PhD, Hartzell Joshua D. MD, Meyer Holly PhD. (2025). Academic Coaching to Promote Self-Directed Learning in Graduate Medical Education. Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-025-09424-7

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Same pre-test post-test analysis of 51 residents with paired IM-ITE data: met ≥2 times (n=14) vs met once or not coached (n=37), independent-samples t-tests. Both outcomes significant with printed effect sizes "ES = 0.788" and "ES = 0.999".

> "residents who met with a trained coach at least twice had statistically significant improvements in their IM-ITE percent correct compared to residents who did not participate in the coaching program, p = 0.015, ES = 0.788, and percentile score compared to residents who did not participate in the coaching program, p = 0.003, ES = 0.999"
