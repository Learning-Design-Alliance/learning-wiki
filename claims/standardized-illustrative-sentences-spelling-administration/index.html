Evidence¶

David Spence Hill 1917¶

David Spence Hill. (1917). Standardized Illustrative Sentences for the Springfield Spelling List. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org

q2 · i? · design · r2

The author describes his own large-scale testing procedure in New Orleans during the spring of 1916, in which "the writer in advance wrote all illustrative sentences" used by all examiners. The examiner pronounced the word, spoke the sentence, and children wrote the word only.

"However, in the administration of a well-known list of words to about 25,000 children in 78 schools, the writer in advance wrote all illustrative sentences, and these were used by all examiners on the day of the test."
