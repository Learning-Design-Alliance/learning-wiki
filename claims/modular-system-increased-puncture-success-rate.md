# Success rate of ultrasound-guided vascular puncture rose significantly from 79% pre-test to 94% posttest after a 2-hour hands-on seminar using the competency-based modular teaching system, in a single-group pre-test/posttest study with no comparison group

## Evidence

### Kikuchi M 2021

Kikuchi M, Asao T, Tokumine J, Lefor AK, Matsushima H, Andoh H, Tanaka K, Kanamoto M, Ideno Y. (2021). A novel system for teaching the in-plane vascular access technique: a simulation study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000027201

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pre-test/posttest simulation study with 47 participants (15 senior physicians, 27 residents, 5 nurses), all novices in the in-plane technique. Success rose from 37 (79%) pre-test to 44 (94%) posttest, "P = .04" by McNemar test; posterior wall puncture fell from 13% to 2.1%.

> "Success rates significantly increased after simulation training (Table 1). Forty-two participants’ ultrasound images were analyzed for the puncture accuracy (15 senior doctors, 27 residents)."
