## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Chuey 2021

Chuey, A., McCarthy, A., Lockhart, K., Trouche, E., Sheskin, M. & Keil, F. (2021). No guts, no glory: underestimating the benefits of providing children with mechanistic details. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00108-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Part-movement task analyzed with linear regression across the three conditions (180 children, 60 per condition). Immediate (M = 3.12) and delayed (M = 2.77) did not differ, "t(174) = 1.63, p = 0.21"; both video conditions exceeded control (p < 0.001). Memory was consistent across ages, p = 0.43.

> "memory was comparable in the immediate (M = 3.12, SD = 0.90) and delayed test conditions (M = 2.77, SD = 1.29), t(174) = 1.63, p = 0.21, while delayed test participants performed better than those in the control condition (M = 1.95, SD = 1.32), t(174) = 3.81, p < 0.001"
