Digital Promise 2018¶

Digital Promise. (2018). Meeting Learners Where They Are: Using Microsoft Forms to Drive Improvement in Learning Outcomes. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8a12967d-b67d-404f-875f-3dc61d0cc32e

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview study of 22 teachers; the report states Forms supports real-time data for “in-the-moment” instructional adjustments, and teachers described unit-start comprehension checks and question-by-question class review of results.

"Technology tools like Forms can helps educators make “in-the-moment” instructional adjustments by providing them with real-time data on student performance."
