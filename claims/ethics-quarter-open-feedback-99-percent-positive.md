Laukkanen L, Suhonen R, Löyttyniemi E, Leino-Kilpi H. (2022). The usability, feasibility and fidelity of the Ethics Quarter e-learning intervention for nurse managers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03241-w

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inductive content analysis of one open question on EQ content, answered by 75% (n=71) of the 95 respondents. Positive feedback described contents as "good, necessary, time-saving, practical and research knowledge-based".

> "The responses were positive in 99% (n = 70) of cases and negative in 1% (n = 1). Of the responses, 11% (n = 8) also included development propositions."
