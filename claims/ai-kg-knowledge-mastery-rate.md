## Evidence

### Yuanyuan Yu 2026

Yuanyuan Yu, Ying Yuan. (2026). Application and effectiveness analysis of AI Intelligent tutoring system combined with knowledge graph in basic medical pathology teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09348-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mastery rates were computed by the AI system from 5–10 automatically administered quiz questions per knowledge point, updated daily. The experimental group's overall mastery rate was "14.5% points higher" than the control group's, with the largest difference on complex mechanism knowledge points.

> "The average knowledge point mastery rate of the experimental group was 90.3%, 14.5% points higher than that of the control group (75.8%); among them, the difference in complex pathological mechanism knowledge points (such as metastasis pathways of malignant tumors and pathophysiological changes of liver cirrhosis) was the most significant, with the experimental group having a mastery rate of 87.6% and the control group only 65.2% (P < 0.001)"
