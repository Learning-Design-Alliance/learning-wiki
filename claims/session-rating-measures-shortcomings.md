# Session-rating measures of therapy quality have unsatisfactory inter-rater reliability, unestablished validity, and narrow content

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Therapy quality rating scales such as the CTS and CTS-R have proved difficult to use reliably, their validity has not been established, and they assess generic CBT style rather than disorder-specific strategies central to a treatment's mode of action. [→ Fairburn Christopher G. 2011](#fairburn-christopher-g-2011)

## Evidence

### Fairburn Christopher G. 2011

Fairburn Christopher G., Cooper Zafra. (2011). Therapist competence, therapy quality, and therapist training. Behaviour Research and Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2011.03.005

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Conceptual critique in the essay's evaluation of the therapy quality method, citing prior work (e.g. Jacobson & Gortner, 2000) on reliability and noting that validity of the scales and their threshold scores has not been demonstrated.

> "it has proved difficult to define, operationalise and demarcate the aspects of treatment of interest with the result that inter-rater reliability has been less than satisfactory"
