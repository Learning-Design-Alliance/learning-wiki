## Evidence

### Ehsanzadeh Seyed Jafar 2025

Ehsanzadeh Seyed Jafar, Dwyer Eric, Barzegar Mohammad, Hamidi Hadi. (2025). Teaching medical terminology: comparing the effectiveness of lecture-based and corpus-assisted methods. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-025-07584-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 40 EFL medical students in four groups (n = 10 each) taught eight cardiovascular terms; Tukey's HSD post-hoc tests on VLT retention scores showed "concordancing’s retention remained significantly higher than lecture-based at all intervals (p <.001)". Table 2 shows lecture-based means falling from 5.4 (1.0) at T1 to 2.7 (1.2) at T3.

> "Tukey’s HSD post-hoc tests confirmed that concordancing’s retention remained significantly higher than lecture-based at all intervals (p <.001), with frequency and collocation showing moderate advantages over lecture-based, particularly at T3."
