## Evidence

### Gonzalez-Sanz Juan D. 2026

Gonzalez-Sanz Juan D., Teresa-Morales Cristina, Rodríguez-Pérez Margarita, Torres-Enamorado Dolores, Feria-Ramírez Carmen, Urcola-Pardo Fernando. (2026). Nursing students’ evaluations of the flipped classroom using the FLAS scale: a quantitative analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08625-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-level descriptive analysis of Dimension 3 (comparison with traditional methodology) among 432 nursing students. The article reports that on item 10 "only 56.7% (255 participants) assigned it a score of 4 or more", with means slightly below 3.5 in both year groups; no effect size is printed.

> "Conversely, when assessing whether FC facilitates concentration more than TM (item 10), it received the lowest score: only 56.7% (255 participants) assigned it a score of 4 or more. Of these participants, 55.5% (86) were in their first year and 57.4% (159) were in their third year."
