## Evidence

### Joore Ivo Kim 2018

Joore Ivo Kim, van Bergen Jan E. A. M. J, ter Riet Gerben, van der Maat A., van Dijk N. (2018). Development and evaluation of a blended educational programme for general practitioners' trainers to stimulate proactive HIV testing. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-018-0723-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Zero-inflated negative binomial regression of six-monthly laboratory data (2010–2015) comparing 11 intervention GPs with 624 control GPs. The article reports "twice as many laboratory-documented HIV tests as the controls (IRR 2.05; 95% CI 1.35–3.12; p = 0.001)"; before the programme the IRR was 1.50 (p = 0.211).
