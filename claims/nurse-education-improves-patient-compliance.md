## Evidence

### Lee Yoo Jin MD; Kim Eun Soo MD 2015

Lee Yoo Jin MD; Kim Eun Soo MD, PhD; Park Kyung Sik MD; Cho Kwang Bum MD, PhD; Jang Byoung Kuk MD, PhD; Chung Woo Jin MD, PhD; Hwang Jae Seok MD, PhD; Somchai Amornyotin. (2015). Education for Ward Nurses Influences the Quality of Inpatient's Bowel Preparation for Colonoscopy. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000001423

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interview-based compliance assessment by an unblinded-to-groups trained nurse in a controlled trial. "Preparation instructions" compliance 92.2% vs 60.8% and dietary compliance 83.5% vs 35.3% (both P < 0.001); water intake 63.0% vs 26.0%.

> "Preparation instructions were also more closely followed in the educated ward than in control (92.2% vs 60.8%, P < 0.001). Similarly, the proportion of patients who complied with the dietary instructions was higher in the educated ward than in control (83.5% vs 35.3%, P < 0.001)."
