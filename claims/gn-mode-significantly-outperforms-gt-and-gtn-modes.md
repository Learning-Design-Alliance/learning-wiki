# Students using the Graphic novel and Narration (GN) mode performed significantly better on critical thinking posttest scores than those in the Graphic novel and Text (GT) mode and the Graphic novel, Text and Narration (GTN) mode.

## Evidence

### Hii 2013

Hii, S. C., & Fong, S. F. (2013). Effects of Multimedia-Based Graphic Novel Presentation on Critical Thinking among Students of Different Learning Approaches. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018032

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 291 Secondary Two students in three Malaysian secondary schools. One-way ANCOVA controlling for pretest scores found a significant main effect of treatment mode on posttest scores, F(2, 287) = 18.408, p = 0.000, with the GN mode outperforming both GT and GTN modes. The "students who were exposed to the Graphic novel and Narration (GN) mode performed significantly better" than those in the other two modes.

> "The results showed that students who were exposed to the Graphic novel and Narration (GN) mode performed significantly better than their counterparts in the Graphic novel and Text (GT) mode and the Graphic novel, Text and Narration (GTN) mode."
