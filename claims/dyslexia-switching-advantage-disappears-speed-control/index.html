Doyle C 2018¶

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Case-control ANOVA on switching z-mean reaction time cost composites (Number-Letter and Phoneme switch tasks) in the dyslexia vs control sample. Dyslexia showed "a significantly lower switching z-mean reaction time cost score," Cohen's d = −0.60.

"At the composite level, dyslexia is characterized by a significantly lower switching z-mean reaction time cost score than control participants [ F(1, 54) = 5.03, p = 0.029, Cohen'sd = −0.60]."
