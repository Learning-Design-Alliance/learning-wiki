## Evidence

### Lin Rongmao 2024

Lin Rongmao, Chen Yanping, Shen Yilin, Hu Ting, Huang Ying, Yang Yishan, Yu Xueting, Ding Jinliang. (2024). Academic grit scale for Chinese middle- and upper-grade primary school students: testing its factor structure and measurement invariance. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01622-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Parallel-model analysis in the same sample of 1,894 students: the one-factor parallel model fit well, "indicating that composing a total score in AGS is reasonable". The three-factor parallel model fit poorly (CFI = 0.560, RMSEA = 0.176), so factor scores were not supported.

> "The one-factor parallel model had good fit indices (S-B χ2 = 363.687, df = 53, CFI = 0.948, TLI = 0.956, SRMR = 0.056, RMSEA = 0.061), indicating that composing a total score in AGS is reasonable."
