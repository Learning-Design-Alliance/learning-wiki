## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Most surveyed students named two or more different resources used to write their MCQs; only 15% (9/59) identified a single source. [→ Grainger R 2018](#grainger-r-2018)

## Evidence

### Grainger R 2018

Grainger R, Dai W, Osborne E, Kenwright D. (2018). Medical students create multiple-choice questions for learning in pathology education: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1312-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Free-text survey responses analysed by thematic content analysis among 59 respondents. The authors report "51% (30/59), identified two sources" and 22% three sources; the most popular sources beyond lectures were the set textbook, e-learning tutorials and an additional recommended text.

> "Fifteen percent (9/59) of students identified a single source used to complete the task, 51% (30/59), identified two sources, 22% (13/59) identified three sources and 12% (7/59) more than three sources."
