`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory Mann-Whitney analysis of change scores by sex in the same cohort, which included only 6 men and 31 women. The article states these results "should be considered exploratory and should be interpreted with caution" given the small male subsample; no effect size is printed.

> "The results indicate significant differences in the two-point discrimination tests of the thumb ND (Male 0.33 ± 0.52, Female − 0.42 ± 0.62; p <.05), middle ND (Male 0.33 ± 0.52, Female − 0.45 ± 0.62; p <.05), and middle ring ND (Male 0.33 ± 0.52, Female − 0.48 ± 0.63; p <.05)."
