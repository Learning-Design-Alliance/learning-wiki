Non-randomized before-after comparison across three course instances (baseline n=85, step I n=96, step II n=93) using an anonymous voluntary 20-question case-based SBA test. Step II students scored "14 (12–16) answers correct" with 69% at ≥65%, P < 0.0001 versus baseline and step I.

> "Students taking the test after step II got 14 (12–16) answers correct; 64 (69%) had ≥65% correct answers (P < 0.0001 versus both baseline and step I)."
