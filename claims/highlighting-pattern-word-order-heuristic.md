### Fries 2017

Fries, M.-H. (2017). Teaching compound nouns in ESP: insights from cognitive semantics. In C. Sarré & S. Whyte (Eds), New developments in ESP teaching and learning research (pp. 93-107). Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2017.cssw2017.747

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative error analysis of the slideshow corpus from both groups. The author concludes the highlighting pattern "seems to be representative enough to be used as a teaching heuristic for CNs in S&T", with figurative compounds based on metaphor or metonymy as exceptions.

> "On the whole, mistakes in word order are infrequent in the slideshow corpus: only two in the experimental group (“area electrolysis” and “energy household”) and three in the control group (“estimate energy losses”, “storage ammonia”, and “unit production”)."
