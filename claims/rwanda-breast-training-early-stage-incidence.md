`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary AJCC-staged analysis of cancers among intervention versus control catchment populations found "3.3 per 100,000 in intervention areas and 0.7 per 100,000 in control areas (P = .048)"; patients lacking required metastatic imaging were classified as unknown stage.

> "In our primary analysis, the incidence of early-stage breast cancer was 3.3 per 100,000 in intervention areas and 0.7 per 100,000 in control areas (P = .048; Table 4)."

### Pace LE 2019 (2)
