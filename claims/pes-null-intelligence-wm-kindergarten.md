# Post-error slowing in EF shows no significant association with intelligence or WM in kindergarten children

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2`–`r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` PES in either the incongruent or mixed block of the HF task was not associated with intelligence or WM (all ps > .056), even after controlling for age, and hierarchical regressions confirmed null predictions. [→ Ger Ebru 2023](#ger-ebru-2023)
`q3 i?` The trajectory of PES across a block did not vary as a function of intelligence: neither the three-way interaction nor the block × index interaction was significant. [→ Ger Ebru 2023 (2)](#ger-ebru-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Ger Ebru 2023

Ger Ebru, Roebers Claudia M. (2023). The Relationship between Executive Functions, Working Memory, and Intelligence in Kindergarten Children. Journal of Intelligence. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10143737/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pre-registered correlational analyses in the N = 147 sample found no PES–intelligence or PES–WM associations. Pre-registered hierarchical regressions predicting PES (Table 3) added no variance: intelligence step ΔR2 = −0.006 in the mixed-block model.

> "Regarding cognitive control, PES in either block of EF was not associated with intelligence or WM (all ps > .056), even after controlling for age"

### Ger Ebru 2023 (2)

Ger Ebru, Roebers Claudia M. (2023). The Relationship between Executive Functions, Working Memory, and Intelligence in Kindergarten Children. Journal of Intelligence. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10143737/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Exploratory mixed linear regression on trial-level RTs tested whether PES magnitude across errors varied with intelligence. Neither the three-way interaction nor the two-way interaction was significant, so the PES trajectory was independent of intelligence.

> "Model comparisons revealed neither a significant three-way interaction (Χ2(3) = 0.46, p = .927) nor a two-way interaction between the block and index (Χ2(3) = 0.48, p = .487)"
