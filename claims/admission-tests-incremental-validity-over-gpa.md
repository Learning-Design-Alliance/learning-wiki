## Evidence

### Jaehn M 2025

Jaehn M, Hissbach J, Frickhoeffer M, Weppert D, Zimmerhofer A, Hampe W, Kadmon M, Becker N. (2025). Predictive validity of admission tests and educational attainment on preclinical academic performance – a multisite study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07974-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression analyses on the TMS and HAM-Nat samples controlling for age and gender, with values corrected for range restriction. The article reports the models-3 increment "between 2.9% and 15.0% of additional variance explained" over GPA-only models; the reverse increment (GPA over tests) is described only as small, with no effect size printed.

> "Adding both high-school GPA and admission test scores to the prediction of academic performance (cf. models 3) resulted in a significant improvement from models 2a with between 2.9% and 15.0% of additional variance explained."
