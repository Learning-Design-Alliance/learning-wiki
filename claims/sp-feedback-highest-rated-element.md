Evaluation questionnaire results (Table 1) from the 34 participants, 6-point scale from 1 'very good' to 6 'insufficient'. The article reports "‘feedback of the SPs’ (M = 1.12, SD = 0.33)" as the highest-rated specific element.

> "Interestingly, the didactic tools ‘practical training with SPs’ (M = 1.18, SD = 0.46) and ‘feedback of the SPs’ (M = 1.12, SD = 0.33) were rated highest."
