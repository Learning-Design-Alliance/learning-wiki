`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation from the introduction, based on their experience with introductory-level students, many of whom they describe as still in the concrete Piagetian stage; no empirical test of this assertion is reported.

> "a plot of kinetic energy is not as persuasive as a computer gen- erated temperature that corresponds to an actual ther- mometer measurement, taken in person. Conservation laws are beautiful, but our beginning students don’t be- lieve them."
