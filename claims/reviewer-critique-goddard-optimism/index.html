q1 · i? · review · r?

The reviewer's own critical assessment of the Goddard volume, noting that "Technical matters, such as the validity of the Intelligence Quotient, are omitted" and questioning whether the constant-growth impression is literally meant. The review concludes the book is a clear, interesting account of intelligence testing.

"Technical matters, such as the validity of the Intelligence Quotient, are omitted. Certain charts and statements give the impression that the writer considers all individuals to have a constant growth"
