`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomised controlled trial assessing 214 children across nine primary schools at pre- and post-test. The article reports a significant effect on "fundamental movement skills (d = 0.65)".

> "There was a significant effect of MAST on language (d = 0.2) and fundamental movement skills (d = 0.65), but not effect on self-regulation."
