> "In a meta-analysis of eight studies8,33,37–39,42,44,50, 115 participants were randomized to VR training and 102 to NAT. Statistical heterogeneity was substantial (I2 = 52%). Using a random-effects model, the combined weighted effect favoured VRT (MD=−8.35 min, 95 per cent c.i. 13.10 to 3.60, P = <0.001)"
