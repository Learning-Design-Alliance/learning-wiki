Frequency analysis across 69 course offerings found "closeness and betweenness showed negative correlation with performance in around 4% of courses." Degree centralities showed no negative correlations.

> "In summary, closeness and betweenness showed negative correlation with performance in around 4% of courses, while eigenvector centrality in 1%, and degree centralities showed no negative correlations."
