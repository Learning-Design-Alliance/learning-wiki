### Blohm M. 2015

Survey data on tutor training from 22 of 33 contacts. The article reports "a significant variation" in basic training scope, most frequently 10–20 hours but varying from about 3 to 50 hours, often differentiated into didactic and technical training, usually offered once per semester.

> "Results revealed a significant variation in the later: the most frequent entries on the scope of basic training ranged from 10-20 hours but varied from about 3 to 50 hours."
