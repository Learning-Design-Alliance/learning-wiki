# I-BEST has no discernible effects on credit accumulation

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` One quasi-experimental study (42,894 students) found an indeterminate effect of I-BEST on the percentage of students earning more than 12 credits, with effect size -0.03 and improvement index -1. [→ credacc-1](#credacc-1)

### credacc-1

In the Anderson et al. (2017) QED (Accelerating Opportunity, propensity score matched, 42,894 students), 45.7% of the intervention group versus 46.9% of the comparison group earned more than 12 credits, effect size -0.03, improvement index -1, p .13 — not statistically significant.
