q2 · i? · review · r3

The chapter reports Batsell et al. (2016), comparing two simultaneously taught introductory psychology sections: "Students who took daily quizzes performed better on classroom exams", including completely new questions.

"Students who took daily quizzes performed better on classroom exams that included questions that were identical to the quizzed questions, similar to quizzed questions (i.e., questions that covered topics assessed on the quiz), and completely new questions that were not directly related to topics covered on quizzes."
