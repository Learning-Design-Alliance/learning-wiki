# Under high support and practice, reflective judgment shows developmental spurts between ages 18-20 and 23-25/26, but the hypothesized 14-15 spurt appears only in stage-specific and highest-stage data

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The PRJI at Time 2 showed significant 1- and 2-year increases at ages 18-19, 17-19, 18-20, and 23-25/26, supporting spurts at about 18-20 and 23-25 years. [→ Kitchener 1993](#kitchener-1993)
`q2 i?` Stage-specific PRJI data show Stage 5 tasks first passed at age 15 (37.5%) and rising sharply at 19-20, consistent with a 14-15 spurt not visible in the composite score. [→ Kitchener 1993 (2)](#kitchener-1993-2)

## Evidence

### Kitchener 1993

Kitchener, K. S., Lynch, C. L., Fischer, K. W., & Wood, P. K. (1993). Developmental Range of Reflective Judgment: The Effect of Contextual Support and Practice on Developmental Stage. Developmental Psychology, 29(5), 893-906. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/dev/29/5/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVAs on RJI Time 1 and PRJI Time 2 scores (age effects significant, F(12, 90) = 8.01 and F(12, 91) = 9.58) with Waller-Duncan tests of adjacent ages found spurts only on the PRJI at the ages quoted; the RJI showed one 2-year increase (21-23) attributed to possibly low 21-year-old scores.

> "The PRJI condition at Time 2 showed a significant 1-year increase between ages 18 and 19 and significant 2-year increases between ages 17 and 19, 18 and 20, and 23 and 25/26."

### Kitchener 1993 (2)

Kitchener, K. S., Lynch, C. L., Fischer, K. W., & Wood, P. K. (1993). Developmental Range of Reflective Judgment: The Effect of Contextual Support and Practice on Developmental Stage. Developmental Psychology, 29(5), 893-906. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/dev/29/5/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Stage-specific analysis of percentage of tasks passed at each age on PRJI Time 2 (graphed in Figures 2 and 3): Stage 5 tasks rose from 37.5% at age 18 to 75% at 19 and 87.5% at 20; Stage 6 tasks first passed at 19 (31%) and reached 100% at 25/26; Stage 7 rose from 12.5% to 62.5% by 25/26.

> "For Stage 5, no PRJI tasks were passed at age 14, but at age 15, 37.5% of the tasks were passed"
