# Teacher roles vary by orchestration type, with content-related and ethical roles prominent when the teacher intervenes in AI-generated feedback, and a knowledge-transmitter role emerging in explain-the-screen

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Content and ethical roles appeared together in AI-correcting and AI-mediating episodes, where the teacher judged AI feedback against curriculum standards and fostered students' critical acceptance. [→ Yun Hwa Noh & Hee-jeong Kim 2026](#yun-hwa-noh-hee-jeong-kim-2026)

## Evidence

### Yun Hwa Noh & Hee-jeong Kim 2026

Yun Hwa Noh & Hee-jeong Kim. (2026). Exploring teachers' instrumental orchestration and roles in high school mathematics classes using AI-based digital tools. The Mathematical Education 65(2), 309-335. https://doi.org/10.63311/mathedu.26.6525

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Directed content analysis coding teacher roles in each orchestration episode using lesson utterances, actions, and post-lesson interview data, with the Yoon and Kwon (2024) AI-TPACK-based framework as initial categories. Pedagogical and AI-technological roles also appeared in monitoring and support types.

> "셋째, 내용적 역할과 윤리적 역할은 'AI-수정하기'와 'AI-매개하기'에서 공통적으로 나타났는데, 이는 두 유형 모두 AI가 생성한 피드백에 대해 교사가 교육과정 기반의 판단을 내리고 학생에게 비판적 수용 태도를 형성시키는 실천을 포함하기 때문이다."

### Yun Hwa Noh & Hee-jeong Kim 2026 (2)

Yun Hwa Noh & Hee-jeong Kim. (2026). Exploring teachers' instrumental orchestration and roles in high school mathematics classes using AI-based digital tools. The Mathematical Education 65(2), 309-335. https://doi.org/10.63311/mathedu.26.6525

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive coding of roles not captured by the existing framework found that the teacher's direct mathematical concept and content delivery in explain-the-screen episodes did not map onto the AI-focused subcategories, so it was categorized as a knowledge-transmitter role.

> "다만, '화면-설명하기' 유형에서 교사가 수행한 수학적 개념 및 내용 전달은 AI 기반 교수・학습에 초점을 맞춘 기존 분석틀의 하위 범주와 직접적으로 대응되지 않았으며, 이를 '지식 전달자'로 범주화하였다."
