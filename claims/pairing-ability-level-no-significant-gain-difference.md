## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Impact of peer interaction on conceptual test performance. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07661](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07661)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The 148 IG-protocol students were divided into categories A (≥70%), B (50–70%), and C (≤50%) by initial score, and second individual scores were compared across all nine pair types (Tables V and VI). The article reports no significant differences in gains across pairing types.

> "We found no signiﬁcant diﬀerences between individual gains regardless of whether a student with a high individual score was paired with another student who had a high or a low individual score."
