## Evidence

### Vy Le 2025

Vy Le, Jayson M. Nissen, Xiuxiu Tang, Yuxiao Zhang, Amirreza Mehrabi, Jason W. Morphew, Hua Hua Chang, and Ben Van Dusen. (2025). Applying cognitive diagnostic models to mechanics concept inventories. Physical Review Physics Education Research 21, 010103. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.21.010103

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Consensus coding of FCI items by three researchers, refined through DINA-based Q-matrix validation. The FMCE quote states "All 47 items assessed conceptual relationships skill" and the EMCS "included 2 items that assessed the algebra skill."

> "Eighteen items assessed apply vector skill, 17 assessed conceptual relationships skill, 1 assessed visualizations skill, and 0 assessed algebra skill."

### Vy Le 2025 (2)

Vy Le, Jayson M. Nissen, Xiuxiu Tang, Yuxiao Zhang, Amirreza Mehrabi, Jason W. Morphew, Hua Hua Chang, and Ben Van Dusen. (2025). Applying cognitive diagnostic models to mechanics concept inventories. Physical Review Physics Education Research 21, 010103. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.21.010103

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Distribution of FCI items across the number of skills they assess (Table V); the FMCE had 13 items (28%) single-skill and 31 items (66%) two-skill, and the EMCS 20 items (80%) single-skill.

> "Twenty-four items (80%) assessed a single skill, 6 items (20%) assessed two skills, and 0 items assessed three skills (Table V)."
