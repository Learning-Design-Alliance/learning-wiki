## Evidence

### Valentín 2013

Valentín, A., Mateos, P. M., González-Tablas, M. M., Pérez, L., López, E., & García, I. (2013). Motivation and learning strategies in the use of ICTs among university students. Computers & Education, 61, 52–58. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2012.09.008

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression analysis (n = 543) using MSLQ-derived motivation and learning-strategy scales to predict EPU. The article reports "Elaboration (b ¼ .269, p ¼ .000)" as the sole learning-strategy predictor, alongside two motivational predictors.

> "Three predictors proved to be signi ﬁcant: Elaboration (b ¼ .269, p ¼ .000), as the only Learning Strategy, and Intrinsic Goals (b ¼ .126, p ¼ .007) and Task Value ( b ¼ .111,p ¼ .018), among the motivational components."
