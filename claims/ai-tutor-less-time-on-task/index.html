Kestin 2025¶

Kestin, G., Miller, K., Klales, A., Milbourne, T. & Ponti, G. (2025). AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning: an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational setting. Scientific Reports 15:17458. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Platform usage tracking in the crossover RCT measured AI-group time on task, shown in Fig. 2; "The median time on task for students in the AI group was 49 minutes" versus an assumed 60 in-class minutes.

"70% of students in the AI group spent less than 60 minutes on task, while 30% spent more than 60 minutes on task. The median time on task for students in the AI group was 49 minutes."
