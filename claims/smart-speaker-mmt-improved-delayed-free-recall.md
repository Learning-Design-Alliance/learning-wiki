## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Delayed free recall scores were significantly higher in the training group after 8 weeks, with a large paired effect (Cohen d=0.84). [→ Kim J 2021](#kim-j-2021)

## Evidence

### Kim J 2021

Kim J, Shin EJ, Han KH, Park S, Youn JH, Jin G, Lee JY. (2021). Efficacy of Smart Speaker–Based Metamemory Training in Older Adults: Case-Control Cohort Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/20177

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Per-protocol analysis of the case-control cohort (MMT n=29 vs control n=31) reports delayed free recall improvement for the MMT group at "P<.001; t=0.50; Cohen d=0.84"; the control group's delayed cued recall change was not significant (P=.62, Table 2).

> "Figure 2 Box and scatter plots of cognitive function changes in metamemory training and control groups during the 8 weeks of the study. (A) Delayed free recall (P<.001; t=0.50; Cohen d=0.84)."
