# Early precursors predict adolescent N2 directly, not indirectly through childhood inattention symptoms

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Path analysis found no indirect paths from effortful control or paternal inattention through childhood inattention symptoms to N2 amplitude (β = −0.06 and β = 0.03, both non-significant), while the model explained 29% of N2 variance with good fit. [→ Einziger Tzlil 2021](#einziger-tzlil-2021)

## Evidence

### Einziger Tzlil 2021

Einziger Tzlil, Ben-Shachar Mattan S., Devor Tali, Shmueli Michael, Auerbach Judith G., Berger Andrea. (2021). "My Brain Can Stop": An ERP Study of Longitudinal Prediction of Inhibitory Control in Adolescence. Brain Sciences, 11(1), 100. https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci11010100

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis (lavaan) in the longitudinal sample testing direct and indirect routes to adolescent N2. The article reports "No indirect paths through inattention symptoms were found" with β = −0.06 and β = 0.03; the full model explained 29% of N2 variance (CFI = 1, RMSEA < 0.01).

> "No indirect paths through inattention symptoms were found; β = −0.06, z = −0.86, p = 0.40 and β = 0.03, z = 0.82, p = 0.41 for childhood effortful control and paternal inattention, respectively."
