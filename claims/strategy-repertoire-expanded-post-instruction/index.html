Ebadi and Pourzandi 2015¶

Saman Ebadi, Mahsa Pourzandi. (2015). The Effects of Explicit and Implicit Pragmatic Instruction on the Development of Compliments and Compliment Responses. li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 5, No. 4, October - December. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=The+Effects+of+Explicit+and+Implicit+Pragmatic+Instruction+on+the+Development+of+Compliments+and+Compliment+Responses

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Figure-reported distribution of compliment strategies across pre-test and post-test in this quasi-experimental study, described in the accompanying results text. The printed percentages (nearly 60%, less than 50%) are descriptive; no effect size is printed.

"All three groups mainly used three strategies in pre-test, while all 8 possible strategies were used after they received instructions. For instance, nearly 60% of the learners used explicit complimenting strategy in pre-test while it decreased to less than 50% after they received their special instructions."
