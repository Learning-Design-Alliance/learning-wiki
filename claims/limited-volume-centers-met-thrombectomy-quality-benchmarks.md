`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quality surveillance of clinical practice using Norwegian Stroke Register data for 191 patients at SUS (n=125), SSK (n=27), and AHUS (n=39), compared against multisociety consensus guidelines (>70% reperfusion, >30% independence, <10% sICH).

> "The clinical reperfusion rates (mTICI ≥ 2b/3) were 85% or higher, proportions of patients achieving functional independence (mRS 0–2) were 44% or higher, and complication rates (sICH) were 5% or lower."
