## Evidence

### Sturgiss Elizabeth 2017

Sturgiss Elizabeth, Haesler Emily, Elmitt Nicholas, van Weel Chris, Douglas Kirsty. (2017). Increasing general practitioners' confidence and self-efficacy in managing obesity: a mixed methods study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014314

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative pro forma interview data from the end of the 6-month pilot, transcribed and thematically reviewed by two authors for confidence, self-efficacy and practice change. GPs gave examples such as taking more waist circumferences and weighing more patients in general practice.

> "Additionally, some GPs reported that they had already changed their practice with other patients who were not engaged in the pilot trial."
