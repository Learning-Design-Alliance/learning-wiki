Luke Heinkel 2015¶

Luke Heinkel, Kristin Hallgren, Brian Gill, Megan Shoji. (2015). Making Effective Use of Postsecondary Data in K-12 Education Settings. Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/making-effective-use-of-postsecondary-data-in-k-12-education-settings

The brief's introduction states that while services now let K-12 agencies examine college enrollment, persistence, and completion of former students, "many officials, however, are unsure of the best ways to use these data." This is the authors' motivating observation, not a survey finding with reported statistics.
