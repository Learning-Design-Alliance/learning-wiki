# Students fluent in C-style structured programming struggle to accept object-oriented ideas when first using an object-oriented language

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Because students learned C first and became familiar with structured programming, they find it difficult to accept the ideas and methods of object-oriented programming. [→ Guowu Yuan 2021](#guowu-yuan-2021)

## Evidence

### Guowu Yuan 2021

Guowu Yuan, Bing Kong, Haiyan Ding, Jixian Zhang, Yang Zhao. (2021). Teaching Reform and Exploration on Object-Oriented Programming. https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2021.3068404

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Instructor report from the paper's problem analysis of its own course at Yunnan University: students took C in semester 1 and data structures in semester 2, and the authors state they "are difficult to accept the idea and method of object-oriented". No data or test is reported.

> "With a large number of programming with C language, they have been familiar with the process of structured programming. When they use object-oriented programming language, they are difficult to accept the idea and method of object-oriented."
