## Evidence

### Alyahya D 2025

Alyahya D, Alharbi A, Kashoo F, Alsaheli S, AlMubarak FM, Aljuhni R. (2025). Knowledge and competence in vestibular rehabilitation: a cross-sectional study of physical therapy interns. PeerJ 13. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.20213

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 233 PT interns across 19 Saudi universities; the results section reports descriptive mean scores. The academic test mean was "4.17 out of 20 (28%)"; the internship test mean was 2.43 out of 20 (17%), also described as low.

> "The academic test assessing knowledge of essential vestibular rehabilitation (VR) test maneuvers learned during academic coursework had a mean score of 4.17 out of 20 (28%), indicating low knowledge acquisition."
