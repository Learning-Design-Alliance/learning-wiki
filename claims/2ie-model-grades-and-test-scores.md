Studies 1 and 2 tested the 2I/E model with both grades and test scores as achievement indicators in samples of German students (Study 1: Grade 4–6, math and German; Study 2: Grade 6–8, three subject combinations). The article reports the relations "were replicated with both grades and standardized test scores as achievement indicators."

> "Furthermore, the relations of the 2I/E model were replicated with both grades and standardized test scores as achievement indicators (Study 1 and Study 2)."
