`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Scoring-inference analysis of the pilot SJT completed by 303 respondents (192 medical students and 111 resident doctors). Difficulty was computed as the proportion answering each item correctly. The text says "the majority of the items (76%)" fell in the 0.30-0.80 band, but the 50 values in Table 3 give 36 (72%) in that band, 10 below and 4 above, matching the text's 20% and 8%; the 76% is inconsistent with the table. No effect size is printed.

> "Table 3 show that the majority of the items (76%) have a difficulty index that falls within the range deemed appropriate for a good question (0.30–0.80). Few questions, 20% have a difficulty index below 0.30 (too difficult questions) and 8% have a difficulty index above 0.80 (too easy questions)."
