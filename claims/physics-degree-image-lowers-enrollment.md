Qualitative interpretation of student comments the author reports hearing over a decade of teaching, including comments that physics is difficult, that mathematics is the barrier, and that the physics job market is weak. The author concludes "one of the causes of lower enrollment in Physics is the image of the degree itself." No quantitative measure is printed.

> "These comments clearly indicate that one of the causes of lower enrollment in Physics is the image of the degree itself. Basically, the image of physics as a subject is not spoiled in a day."
