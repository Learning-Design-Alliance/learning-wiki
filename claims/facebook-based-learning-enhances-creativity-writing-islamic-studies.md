## Evidence

### Alias et al. 2013

Norlidah Alias, Saedah Siraj, Mohd Khairul Azman Md Daud & Zaharah Hussin. (2013). Effectiveness of Facebook Based Learning to Enhance Creativity Among Islamic Studies Students by Employing Isman Instructional Design Model. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008868

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

An experimental t-test comparison of post-test achievement in creativity in writing between a treatment group (n = 40) using Facebook-based learning and a control group (n = 40) using traditional classroom texts. The treatment group scored higher, with the difference in mean score between the treatment and control groups reported as 4.90.

> "Table 2 shows that achievement mean score for the control group (n = 40) is 29.25 (SD = 9.37), while that of the  treatment group (n = 40) indicates higher achievement, at 34.15 ( SD = 9.78)."
