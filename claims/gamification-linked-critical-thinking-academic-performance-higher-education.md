Systematic review using the PRISMA method across Scopus, WOS and Scielo, with a final corpus of 124 articles published 2022–2024. The review's own synthesis states "gamification is linked to the generation of critical thinking and the improvement of academic performance". No effect sizes are reported.

> "In the more in-depth results, it can be demonstrated that gamification is linked to the generation of critical thinking and the improvement of academic performance in the context of higher education."
