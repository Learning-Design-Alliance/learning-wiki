## Evidence

### Mason Ron 2020

Mason Ron, Doherty Kathleen, Eccleston Claire, Winbolt Margaret, Long Marita, Robinson Andrew. (2020). Effect of a dementia education intervention on the confidence and attitudes of general practitioners in Australia: a pretest post-test study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033218

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test results for the 332 registrars on the Attitude to Care subscale in the pretest post-test study. The overall score rose from 4.35 to 4.70 with t=17.6, p<0.001, mean difference 0.34 and Cohen's d = 0.840.

> "Overall score	GPR (n=332)	4.35	4.30 to 4.39	4.70	4.65 to 4.74	17.6	<0.001	0.34	0.840"

### Mason Ron 2020 (2)

Mason Ron, Doherty Kathleen, Eccleston Claire, Winbolt Margaret, Long Marita, Robinson Andrew. (2020). Effect of a dementia education intervention on the confidence and attitudes of general practitioners in Australia: a pretest post-test study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033218

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prose summary of the Attitude to Care results in the pretest post-test study of 332 registrars and 114 supervisors; the article characterises the increases as "moderate effect sizes" and reports the between-group postworkshop difference. Supervisors' tabled d was 0.570.

> "Overall mean scores for Attitude to Care (table 3) were equivalent for supervisors and GPRs prior to the workshops and increased significantly for both GPRs and supervisors following the workshop, with moderate effect sizes for the increases. GPRs scored significantly higher than supervisors postworkshop (t(420)=2.463; p=0.014)."
