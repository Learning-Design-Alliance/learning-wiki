Bjorklund 2022¶

Bjorklund, D.F. (2022). Children's Evolved Learning Abilities and Their Implications for Education. Educational Psychology Review, 34, 2243-2273. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09688-z

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

This is the article's own stated recommendation set in its abstract: the author lists four ways educators can "take advantage of children's evolved learning abilities," the fourth being "taking advantage of stress-adapted children's "hidden talents."" No effect sizes are reported in the available text.

"I then suggest some ways educators can take advantage of children's evolved learning abilities to minimize the effects of evolutionary mismatches, including (1) following developmentally appropriate practices (which are also evolutionarily appropriate practices), (2) increasing opportunities for physical activities, (3) increasing opportunities to learn through play, and (4) taking advantage of stress-adapted children's "hidden talents.""
