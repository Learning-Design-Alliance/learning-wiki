Related Claims¶

  • Conjunction learning transfers partially to new spatial locations, with color showing larger transfer indices than orientation, indicating pure selectivity neurons also participate alongside mixed selectivity — related
  • Global conjunction-level feedback yields faster and greater conjunction learning than feature-specific feedback in orientation-color conjunction training — related
  • Orientation learning with salient task-irrelevant color (Experiment 4) is smaller than orientation learning under global feedback (Experiment 1) or attended single-feature training (Experiment 3), but not smaller than feature-specific feedback (Experiment 2) — related
  • Months after conjunction training, the global-feedback group showed significantly larger non-linear mixing on an XOR transfer task than the feature-specific group, though average XOR accuracy did not differ between groups — related
  • Conjunction benefit of global feedback is not due to higher cognitive load of dual feature updates: post-cued single-feature training (Experiment 3) does not match global-feedback conjunction learning — related
  • A mixed-channel reinforcement learning model better explains choices after global feedback, and a separate-channel model better explains choices after feature-specific feedback — related
