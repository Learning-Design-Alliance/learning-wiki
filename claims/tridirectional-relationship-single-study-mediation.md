`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Narrative synthesis of the single included study (Kayani et al., cross-sectional, 358 undergraduates) that modeled all three variables. The review reports it as "the only one to show a mediating effect of stress on the relation of PA and AP", with higher PA linked to lower stress and higher stress to lower AP.
