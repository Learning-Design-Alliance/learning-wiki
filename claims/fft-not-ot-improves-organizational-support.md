## Evidence

### Burn M. 2019

Burn M., Tully L. A., Jiang Y., Piotrowska P. J., Collins D. A. J., Sargeant K., Hawes D., Moul C., Lenroot R. K., Frick P. J., Anderson V., Kimonis E. R., Dadds M. R. (2019). Evaluating Practitioner Training to Improve Competencies and Organizational Practices for Engaging Fathers in Parenting Interventions. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-018-0836-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study 1 paired t-test comparing pre-training and 2-month follow-up ratings of organizational support for father-inclusive practice after face-to-face training; the improvement was significant, t (118) = − 2.19, p = 0.030. No effect size is printed.

> "In addition, a paired samples t-test revealed significant improvements from pre-training (M = 3.72, SD = 0.89) to follow-up (M = 3.89, SD = 0.88) in ratings of organizational support for father-inclusive practice, t (118) = − 2.19, p = 0.030."

### Burn M. 2019 (2)

Burn M., Tully L. A., Jiang Y., Piotrowska P. J., Collins D. A. J., Sargeant K., Hawes D., Moul C., Lenroot R. K., Frick P. J., Anderson V., Kimonis E. R., Dadds M. R. (2019). Evaluating Practitioner Training to Improve Competencies and Organizational Practices for Engaging Fathers in Parenting Interventions. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-018-0836-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study 2 paired t-test comparing pre-training and follow-up ratings of organizational support after online training; "no significant changes" were found, t(109) = 0.27, p = 0.78. Equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "However, a paired samples t-test revealed no significant changes from pre-training (M = 3.63, SD = 0.86) to follow-up (M = 3.61, SD = 0.78) in ratings of organizational support for father-inclusive practice, t(109) = 0.27, p = 0.78."
