Bochatay N 2025¶

Bochatay N, Ju M, O'Brien BC, van Schaik SM. (2025). A Scoping Review of Interprofessional Simulation-Based Team Training Programs. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000792

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of outcome timing across included articles; most assessments occurred immediately after training in simulated settings.

"Collaborative behaviors were observed before and after simulations in 49 (84.5%) articles. Thirty-nine (67.2%) of these articles reported some improvement in collaborative behaviors assessed and 10 (17.2%) articles did not report any change."

The review reports few longer-term measurements; of 15 articles including clinical outcome measures, 11 showed improvements and 4 showed no impact.

"Eight (13.8%) articles measured longer-term outcomes, either through simulation56,58–60 and/or in clinical settings.38,56,61–63 Fifteen (25.9%) articles included measures of the impact of the training on clinical outcomes, such as patient safety."
