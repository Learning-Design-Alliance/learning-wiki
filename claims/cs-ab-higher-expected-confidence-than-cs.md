# The CS+AB agent version produced significantly higher expected confidence than the CS-only version

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After interacting with the CS+AB version, learners' expected confidence was significantly higher than that of learners who interacted with the CS-only version (p = 0.002). [→ Emmanuel Ayedoun 2018](#emmanuel-ayedoun-2018)

## Evidence

### Emmanuel Ayedoun 2018

Emmanuel Ayedoun, Yuki Hayashi, Kazuhisa Seta. (2018). Adding Communicative and Affective Strategies to an Embodied Conversational Agent to Enhance Second Language Learners' Willingness to Communicate. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0171-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A pilot study with 40 Japanese undergraduates and graduate students assigned three groups to CS+AB, CS-only, or AB-only agent versions; a one-way ANCOVA on posttest expected confidence controlling for pretest found F(2, 36) = 7.139, p < .05, with Group 1 (CS+AB) significantly higher than Group 2 (CS-only), "p = 0.002". No effect size is printed.

> "There was a significant difference in participants ' expected confidence [F(2, 36) = 7.139, p < .05] among the three groups. The post-hoc Tukey Kramer tests (Table 6) showed that Group 1 's confidence was significantly higher than that of Group 2 ( p = 0.002)."
