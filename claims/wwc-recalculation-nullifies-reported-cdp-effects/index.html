What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2007). Caring School Community™ WWC Intervention Report, Character Education. U.S. Department of Education. http://www.devstu.org/csrd/cdp_index.html

q2 · i? · causal · r2

WWC re-analysis of the Six-District quasi-experimental study of CDP, which examined altruistic behavior and 19 indicators of problem behavior and victimization. The report states "none of these outcomes were statistically significant as calculated by the WWC" after corrections for clustering and multiple comparisons.

"The study reported statistically significant effects for two outcomes (use of alcohol and use of marijuana). However, none of these outcomes were statistically significant as calculated by the WWC."
