## Evidence

### Tianlong Zu 2026

Tianlong Zu, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Transition Matrix Analysis: Analyzing Students' Use of Cognitive Resources in Physics. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.00798](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.00798)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired samples t-tests comparing class transition matrix elements between pretest and posttest (Table 4 reports t(132) values, e.g. TSS t = 2.613, p = .010; TR1R1 t = -3.060, p = .003). The authors state these results "provide evidence for the relative stability of the activation of unproductive cognitive resources".

> "We found that, compared to pretest, students in the posttest retained significantly less productive cognitive resource in the group session (𝑇𝑆𝑆). Students retain their unproductive cognitive resources in the group session on significantly more questions in the posttest compared to pretest (𝑇𝑅1𝑅1 and 𝑇𝑅2𝑅2)."
