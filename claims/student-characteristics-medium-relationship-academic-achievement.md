## Evidence

### Erdem Cahit 2026

Erdem Cahit, Kaya Metin, Polat Mustafa, Eğmir Eray. (2026). Thinking and feeling matter: a second-order meta-analysis of student characteristics across developmental domains and their effect on academic outcomes. BMC Psychology, 14, 919. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05035-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Second-order meta-analysis synthesizing 29 first-order meta-analyses meeting R-AMSTAR quality criteria, yielding 71 effect sizes transformed to r and analyzed with a random-effects model in CMA 2.0. The mean effect size was "r = 0.26 (LL = 0.238 UL = 0.288)", interpreted as a medium-level relationship.

> "The data set included 71 effect sizes. The effect sizes ranged between r = 0.46 and r = 0.01. The mean effect size was r = 0.26 (LL = 0.238 UL = 0.288)."
