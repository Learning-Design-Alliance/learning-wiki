T-test for dependent samples on the experimental group's reading comprehension scores (n = 50), within a randomized control group pretest-posttest design. Means rose from 15.90 at pretest to 23.04 at posttest, which the article reports as significantly improved "at the 0.01 level".

> "The data presented in Table 4 indicates that the experimental group showed significantly improved English reading comprehension at the 0.01 level, after being taught through the DR-TA using tales of virtue based on the King’s teaching concepts(X = 15.90(Pretest),23.04(Posttest), S.D =4.87(Pretest), 4.50(Posttest), t = 15.87**)."
