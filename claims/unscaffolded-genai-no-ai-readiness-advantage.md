## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zhang Jun 2026

Zhang Jun, Peng Yuting, Deng Xinyue, Zeng Qin, Wang Kai. (2026). The Impact of Unscaffolded GenAI Use on Pre-Service Teachers’ AI Readiness, Self-Regulated Learning, Critical Thinking, and Instructional Design Performance: A Quasi-Experimental Study. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071114

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA on AI readiness (18-item scale, four dimensions) with pretest as covariate in the quasi-experiment of 52 pre-service chemistry teachers; adjusted means were 4.07 (experimental) and 4.11 (control). The article reports "F = 0.198, p = 0.658, η2 = 0.004, with a negligible effect size".

> "The ANCOVA analysis showed no significant difference between the experimental and control groups, F = 0.198, p = 0.658, η2 = 0.004, with a negligible effect size."
