## Evidence

### Kevin L. Van De Bogart 2015

Kevin L. Van De Bogart, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, H. J. Lewandowski, and MacKenzie R. Stetzer. (2015). The role of metacognition in troubleshooting: an example from electronics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.080](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.080)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' conclusion from a preliminary analysis of one coded episode from a set of 8 task-based interviews; the article states the framework is "suitable for and useful in the analysis of student troubleshooting."

> "We have illustrated that the Socially Mediated Metacognition Framework is both suitable for and useful in the analysis of student troubleshooting of an electronic circuit."

### Kevin L. Van De Bogart 2015 (2)

Kevin L. Van De Bogart, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, H. J. Lewandowski, and MacKenzie R. Stetzer. (2015). The role of metacognition in troubleshooting: an example from electronics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.080](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.080)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of transcript lines 14-36 reports a "cyclic interaction" between the pair in which ideas, measurements, and re-evaluations alternate; the authors state this mutuality is "captured in our coding of back and forth other-monitoring exchanges."

> "This mutual attempt to understand each other's thinking is captured in our coding of back and forth other-monitoring exchanges."
