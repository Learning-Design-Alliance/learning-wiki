## Evidence

### van Tetering AAC 2025

van Tetering AAC, de Vries EL, Ntuyo P, van den Heuvel ER, Fransen AF, van der Hout-van der Jagt MB, Namagembe I, Byamugisha J, Oei SG. (2025). Mono-Professional Simulation-Based Obstetric Training in a Low-Resource Setting: Stepped-Wedge Cluster Randomized Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/54911

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stepped-wedge cluster randomized trial at Mulago National Referral Hospital, Uganda, with 17,496 births across 7 clusters of senior house officers. The intervention group showed a combined mortality rate of "9.05% (95% CI 8.37%‐9.77%)" versus 8.73% in controls; the OR of 0.98 (P=.81) was not statistically significant, and equivalence was not tested.

> "The combined mortality rate was 9.05% (95% CI 8.37%‐9.77%) in the intervention group, and 8.73% (95% CI 8.21%‐9.28%) in the control group (odds ratio [OR] 0.98, 95% CI 0.86‐1.12; P=.81)."
