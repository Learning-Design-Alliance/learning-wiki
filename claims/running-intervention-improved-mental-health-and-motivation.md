## Evidence

### Ma Dongmin 2025

Ma Dongmin, Akram Huma, Li Shengji. (2025). Assessing the role of physical activity in shaping students’ academic motivation: the mediating role of mental health. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-25541-8

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Two-way repeated measures ANOVA in a quasi-experimental study of 450 undergraduates running 30 minutes daily for four months, assessed at pre-, mid- and post-test. The article reports the main effect with "F(2, 898) = 41.23, p = < 0.001, η² = 0.32".

> "Main effects revealed significant improvements across all constructs from pre- to post-intervention, F(2, 898) = 41.23, p = < 0.001, η² = 0.32."
