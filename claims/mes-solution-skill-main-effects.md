## Evidence

### Darie S. 2026

Darie S., Wainman B.C., Wohl G.R., Wang A.Y., Zhang B.Z., Li A., Ahmad E., Palombella A.S., Rockarts J.L., de SA D.L., Nguyen L.N., DeCarlo B., Downer N., Brewer‐Deluce D. (2026). Preliminary insights on high‐fidelity embalming solutions for surgical skills training—An evaluation using the McMaster Embalming Scale and mechanical tests. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70185

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Two-way MANOVA of MES ratings across the five skill-dependent domains, with solution and skill as independent variables, from a fully crossed double-blind study in which 23 surgical residents rated four embalmed donors. The article reports "a significant main effect of solution" (partial η 2 = 0.042) and of skill (partial η 2 = 0.077), with no significant interaction (p > 0.05).

> "There was both a significant main effect of solution (F(15, 828) = 2.414, p = 0.002, partial η 2 = 0.042, Figure 3) and skill (F(10, 550) = 4.564, p < 0.001, partial η 2 = 0.077, Figure 4) with no significant interaction (p > 0.05)."
