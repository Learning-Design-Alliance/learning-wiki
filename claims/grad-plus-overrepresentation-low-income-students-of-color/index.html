Descriptive shares reported in the brief's Grad PLUS section comparing representation among graduate students versus Grad PLUS borrowers; percentages are printed directly and no effect size is given.

"Graduate degree completers with incomes below $30,000 account for 46% of graduate students but 68% of Grad PLUS borrowers. Black, Latino, and Native American, Alaska Native, Native Hawaiian, and Pacific Islander students represent 12%, 11%, and 7% of graduate students respectively, but 16%, 12%, and 8% of Grad PLUS borrowers."
