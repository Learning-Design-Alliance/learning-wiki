## Evidence

### Yu Zizhong 2025

Yu Zizhong, Zhao Zhao, Chen Xinyue, Shi Jiayi, Liang Yiting, Zhang Xicheng, Qu Mengjiao, Huang Meiyue, Li Panpan, Li Dan, Li Man, He Jinbo. (2025). Effects of standardised patients (SP) combined with case-based learning (CBL) in Chinese clinical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-095705

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs (n=543) using a fixed effects model after finding no heterogeneity (I2=0.0%). The article reports an odds ratio of 7.19 for teaching satisfaction, an odds ratio rather than a standardised effect size, so no standardised magnitude is printed.

> "The meta-analysis revealed that, compared with traditional teaching, the application of SP within the context of CBL significantly improved students’ and resident physcians’ satisfaction with teaching (OR 7.19, 95% CI 3.80 to 13.60, p<0.001)"
