Evidence¶

Merging Cognitive Science and Curriculum to Strengthen Middle School Math 2014¶

Merging Cognitive Science and Curriculum to Strengthen Middle School Math. (2014). WestEd R&D Alert, Vol. 15, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559573

q1 · i? · design · r1

The authors' interpretation of cognitive science research as applied in the bumper car floor plan revision, where a photograph was replaced with a sketch of three possible floor plans including one that does not meet the requirements. No new empirical data are reported for this specific change.

"Schneider explains that pictures like the one of an amusement park are not just filling space unnecessarily but actually distract students because such pictures are not directly related to the math of the problem."
