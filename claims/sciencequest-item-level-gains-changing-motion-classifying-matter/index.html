Evidence¶

Li 2019¶

Li, L., Maher, B., Weiser, G., Orenstein, A., Chow, E., & Atienza, S. (2019). Hero Elementary playlist pilot study in after-school programs. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED608115

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Exploratory differential item analysis of ScienceQuest post-test improvement by treatment condition using Pearson's chi-squared statistics; four items reached the .1 level and one item (H00X23) reached p = 0.006. The article labels these results as promising rather than confirmatory.

"Five items, corresponding to the playlists "Changing Motion" and "Classifying Matter", saw intervention-receiving students improve beyond their comparative peers."
