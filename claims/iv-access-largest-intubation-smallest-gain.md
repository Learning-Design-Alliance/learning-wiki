Quantitative results from the same paired pre/post survey (N = 31). The article reports the IV access mean difference of 5.74 (95% CI 4.84 to 6.64) as the largest and the intubation mean difference of 4.55 (95% CI 3.62 to 5.48) as the smallest; these are raw mean differences, not standardized effect sizes.

> "The largest improvement in confidence was seen in the peripheral vein IV access station, where we observed a mean difference between presession and postsession confidence scores of 5.74 (95% CI 4.84 to 6.64)."
