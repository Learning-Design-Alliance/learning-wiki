# Basic obstetric ultrasound knowledge scores rose from pre-test to post-test among medical students and junior house officers in a low-resource setting after a combined didactic and hands-on ultrasound teaching session, in one single-group pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Medical students and junior house officers showed a statistically significant increase in pre-post test scores after the didactic and hands-on teaching session (p<0.0001). [→ Ahmadzia Homa 2018](#ahmadzia-homa-2018)

## Evidence

### Ahmadzia Homa 2018

Ahmadzia Homa, Cigna Sarah, Namagembe Imelda, Macri Charles, Galerneau France, Magriples Urania. (2018). Teaching obstetric ultrasound at Mulago Hospital - Kampala, Uganda. African Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ahs.v18i1.21

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post evaluation of a teaching intervention at Mulago Hospital with 40 participants (12 medical students, 23 JHOs, 5 SHOs), analyzed with paired t-tests. The article reports "mean difference in raw score was 5.3 and 4.9 points, respectively (p <0.0001)" for medical students and JHOs.

> "There was a significant difference between pre- and post-test scores in the medical student and JHO groups; mean difference in raw score was 5.3 and 4.9 points, respectively (p <0.0001)."

### Ahmadzia Homa 2018 (2)

Ahmadzia Homa, Cigna Sarah, Namagembe Imelda, Macri Charles, Galerneau France, Magriples Urania. (2018). Teaching obstetric ultrasound at Mulago Hospital - Kampala, Uganda. African Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ahs.v18i1.21

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary stratified analysis of the same pre-post survey, splitting scores into Section 2 (basic definitions) and Section 3 (practical knowledge). Both sections retained significance (p<0.0001) for medical students and JHOs, e.g., "stratified into the basic definitions and practical skills sections of the survey (p <0.0001)".

> "Statistical significance was retained in the medical student and JHO group score differences when the results were stratified into the basic definitions and practical skills sections of the survey (p <0.0001)"
