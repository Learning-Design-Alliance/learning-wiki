### Koney Nii Koney‐Kwaku 2026

Koney Nii Koney‐Kwaku, Omenako Smart Nana, Adjei David Nana, Arko‐Boham Benjamin, Hortsch Michael. (2026). Introducing virtual microscopy at the University of Ghana during the COVID‐19 pandemic—Identifying and overcoming obstacles in a low‐resource learning environment. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70276

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional Qualtrics survey of 553 valid responses from University of Ghana medical and dental students (levels 200–600), analyzed with Pearson's chi-square test. The article reports a statistically significant increase in VM awareness by class level, with "χ 2 = 81.6; p‐value < 0.0001".

> "Subsequent classes (200, 300, and 400) took histology classes during or past the pandemic and stated an increased awareness (chi‐squared test: df = 4; χ 2 = 81.6; p‐value < 0.0001) and use of VM"
