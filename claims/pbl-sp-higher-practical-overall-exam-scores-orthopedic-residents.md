## Evidence

### Wang An-Yu 2025

Wang An-Yu, Zhao Hao Cheng, Song Yue Wei, Xiong Bing Lang, Guo Zi Yan, Sun Xu Dong, Pan Ya Xin, Sun Wei Dong. (2025). Application of PBL in combination with the SP teaching method in the clinical teaching of orthopedics and traumatology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07658-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 59 TCM orthopedic residents (control n = 29, study n = 30) across three training bases; final exam compared via independent sample t tests. The study reported "statistically significant improvements in practical exam scores and overall scores (P < 0.05)"; no effect size printed.

> "Compared with the control group, the research group showed statistically significant improvements in practical exam scores and overall scores (P < 0.05)."
