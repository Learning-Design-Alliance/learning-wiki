Stevens, Slavin, and Farnish (1989), describing the same trial as Stevens et al. (1991), reported ITBS Reading Achievement results without inferential contrasts; WWC calculations on 320 students across 20 classes found an effect size of 0.02 and improvement index of +1, p > 0.05.
