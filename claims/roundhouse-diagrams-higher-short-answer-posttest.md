## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental group showed a higher level of conceptual achievement than the control group on the posttest of the Force and Motion Subject Short-Answer Questions Test (t=4.264, p=0.001). [→ Kocakaya and Gönen 2014](#kocakaya-and-gonen-2014)

## Evidence

### Kocakaya and Gönen 2014

F. Kocakaya, S. Gönen. (2014). Influence of Computer-Assisted Roundhouse Diagrams on High School 9th Grade Students’ Understanding the Subjects of “Force and Motion”. Science Education International, 25(3), 283-311. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1044458

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent sample t-test of posttest scores on the 20-item short-answer test (15 fill-in-the-blank, 5 true-false with 'because' justifications): experimental mean 30.65 (SD 16.980, n=30) vs control mean 12.18 (SD 7.064, n=17), t=4.264, p=0.001. No effect size printed for this comparison.

> "At the end of the study, when the scores of the Force and Motion Short-Answer Questions Test applied to the experimental and control groups were compared, it was seen that the experimental group had a higher level of conceptual achievement."
