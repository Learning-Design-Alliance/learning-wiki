# EBM workshop attendance, prior research experience, and high facilitating factors are associated with higher EBM knowledge scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After adjusting for years of service, doctors who attended EBM workshops (mean 76.3 vs 70.3), had research experience (76.1 vs 70.5), and had high facilitating factors (78.1 vs 72.5 moderate, 69.3 low) had significantly higher knowledge scores (all P < 0.001). ## Evidence

### Albart Stephenie Ann 2025

Albart Stephenie Ann, Looi Irene. (2025). Knowledge, practice, and facilitating factors of evidence-based medicine among Malaysian doctors: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06865-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-factorial MANCOVA of the 409-doctor survey, adjusted for years of service. Workshop attendance, research experience, and facilitating-factor level each showed significant knowledge differences (all P < 0.001); the quote gives the workshop contrast, and the text similarly reports research experience (76.1 vs 70.5) and high facilitating factors (78.1 vs 69.3 low, P < 0.001).

> "The mean knowledge score for those who attended the EBM workshop (mean = 76.3, SE = 1.32) was statistically significantly higher than that of those who never attended (mean = 70.3, SE = 1.17)."
