## Evidence

### Dziadzko Mikhail 2024

Dziadzko Mikhail, Varvinskiy Andrey, Di Loreto Rodolphe, Scipioni Hugues, Ateleanu Bazil, Klimek Markus, Berger-Estilita Joana. (2024). Examiner workload comparison: three structured oral examination formats for the European diploma in anaesthesiology and intensive care. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2364990

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results-section analysis linking NASA-TLX scores to marking agreement and per-SOE scores across all three formats. The article reports no relations; the discussion adds that no U-shape patterns were found when exploring workload and examiner performance.

> "There were no relations between NASA TLX score and kappa agreement nor examination score for all type of examinations."

### Dziadzko Mikhail 2024 (2)

Dziadzko Mikhail, Varvinskiy Andrey, Di Loreto Rodolphe, Scipioni Hugues, Ateleanu Bazil, Klimek Markus, Berger-Estilita Joana. (2024). Examiner workload comparison: three structured oral examination formats for the European diploma in anaesthesiology and intensive care. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2364990

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Agreement analysis of examiner pairs using kappa per SOE paper, covering 380 paired examinations from 120 examiners. The median kappa was 0.55 [IQR 0.29 to 1], statistically different across formats but described as without meaningful significance.

> "Kappa statistics calculation was available for 380 paired SOE (papers) examinations (190 examinees), which included 120 examiners, with a median value of 0.55 [IQR 0.29 to 1], with statistically different but without meaningful significance across types of examination"
