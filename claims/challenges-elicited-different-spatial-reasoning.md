# Different FUSE challenges facilitated different types of spatial reasoning, with CAD-software challenges eliciting relatively high intrinsic-dynamic and extrinsic-dynamic reasoning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` FUSE challenges involving CAD software showed relatively high frequencies of both intrinsic-dynamic and extrinsic-dynamic spatial reasoning, indicating that particular technology tools facilitate particular types of spatial reasoning. [→ Ramey 2019](#ramey-2019)

## Evidence

### Ramey 2019

Ramey, K. E., Stevens, R., & Uttal, D. H. (2019). In-FUSE-ing STEAM Learning with Spatial Reasoning: Distributed Spatial Sensemaking in School-based Making Activities. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000422

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Analysis of the relative frequency of spatial-reasoning types across challenges (Figure 2 and Appendix) found high intrinsic-dynamic and extrinsic-dynamic reasoning in "FUSE challenges involving CAD software". Frequencies are descriptive; no statistical test is reported.

> "First is the relative frequency of both intrinsic-dynamic and extrinsic-dynamic reasoning in FUSE challenges involving CAD software (e.g., 3D You, Keychain Customizer, Print My Ride, Eye Candy, Dream Home, and Dream Home 2).  This indicates the importance of particular technology tools in facilitating particular types of spatial reasoning."
