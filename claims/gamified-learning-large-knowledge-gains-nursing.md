# Gamified clinical skill learning produces a large positive effect on knowledge acquisition in undergraduate nursing students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q4 i3` Meta-analysis of 17 RCTs (n=1,505) shows gamified learning significantly improves knowledge acquisition over traditional teaching (SMD=0.81, 95% CI [0.55, 1.07]). [→ Chen W.-L. 2026](#chen-w-l-2026)

## Evidence

### Chen W.-L. 2026

Chen W.-L., Winitchayothin S., Cheng C.-H., Tonapa S.I., Chou F.-H., & Zimmerman P.-A. (2026). A systematic review and meta-analysis of the effect of gamified clinical skill learning on learning outcomes in undergraduate nursing education. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000772

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 17 randomized controlled trials in undergraduate nursing education pooling 1,505 students. The review reports a large pooled effect, SMD = 0.81, described as "a pooled standardized mean difference of 0.81 (95% CI [0.55, 1.07], p<.001)". GRADE certainty was moderate.

> "A meta-analysis of 17 studies comprising 1,505 students was conducted to examine the effects of gamified clinical skill learning on knowledge acquisition. The random-effects model revealed a pooled standardized mean difference of 0.81 (95% CI [0.55, 1.07], p<.001)"
