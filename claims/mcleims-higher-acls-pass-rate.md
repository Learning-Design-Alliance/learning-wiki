`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Prospective non-randomized controlled observational study comparing ACLS outcomes by timing of mCLEIMS participation among 213 SY-CC students. The mCLEIMS-first group passed at "96.8% [61/63] vs. 84.7% [127/150], p = 0.012". No effect size printed.

> "Participants who completed mCLEIMS before ACLS training and assessment had a significantly higher ACLS pass rate than those who had not completed mCLEIMS beforehand or had completed it afterward (96.8% [61/63] vs. 84.7% [127/150], p = 0.012; Fig. 3A)."
