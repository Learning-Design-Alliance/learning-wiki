## Evidence

### Lee Jea Woog 2022

Lee Jea Woog, Park Sung Je, Kim Soyeon, Chung Un Sun, Han Doug Hyun. (2022). The Association Between Temperament and Characteristics, Smartphone App Use Patterns and Academic Performance of University Students. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2022.37.e143

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis (maximum likelihood) on the full sample with academic performance split into theoretical and sports performance. "serious app use pattern showed significant relations with theoretical performance" and sports performance, with the printed t values; model fit indices (NFI = 0.945, CFI = 0.970) satisfied stated criteria.

> "In addition, serious app use pattern showed significant relations with theoretical performance (t = 11.495, P = 0.010) and sports performance (t = 10.348, P = 0.010)."

### Lee Jea Woog 2022 (2)

Lee Jea Woog, Park Sung Je, Kim Soyeon, Chung Un Sun, Han Doug Hyun. (2022). The Association Between Temperament and Characteristics, Smartphone App Use Patterns and Academic Performance of University Students. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2022.37.e143

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis of TCI factors onto the two performance outcomes. "Theoretical performance showed statistically significant relationships with reward dependence" and persistence, while "Sports performance showed statistically significant relationships with novelty seeking" and persistence; the printed β values are path coefficients, not standardised effect sizes.

> "Theoretical performance showed statistically significant relationships with reward dependence (t = 8.214, P = 0.010) and persistence (t = 5.837, P = 0.013). Sports performance showed statistically significant relationships with novelty seeking (t = 6.967, P = 0.011) and persistence (t = 6.226, P = 0.012)."
