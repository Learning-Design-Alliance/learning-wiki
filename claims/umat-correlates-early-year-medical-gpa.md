## Subclaims
`q2 i3` UMAT Total and section scores showed positive correlations with GPA in the first two year-levels, peaking at r = 0.41 (Total) and r = 0.48 (Logical Reasoning, Year 3) at Institution A, with weaker correlations at the other institutions. [→ Edwards Daniel 2013](#edwards-daniel-2013)

## Evidence

### Edwards Daniel 2013

Edwards Daniel, Friedman Tim, Pearce Jacob. (2013). Same admissions tools, different outcomes: a critical perspective on predictive validity in three undergraduate medical schools. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-173

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Pearsons r) of admissions variables against GPA across three Australian medical schools using two entry cohorts (2006 n=224, 2007 n=426). Printed effect sizes: r = 0.41 (UMAT Total, Years 2-3) and r = 0.48 (Logical Reasoning, Year 3) at Institution A; "These levels of correlation were not as strong in the other institutions".

> "In Institution A UMAT was shown to correlate strongly with Total score of 0.41 with 2nd and 3rd Year results and up to 0.48 with Logical Reasoning in Year 3. These levels of correlation were not as strong in the other institutions"
