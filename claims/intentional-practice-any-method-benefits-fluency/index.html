Letwinsky 2017¶

Letwinsky, K. M., & Berry, M. D. (2017). Technology Integration and the Effect on Mathematics Fact Fluency in the Middle East. Journal of International Education and Leadership, 7(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1153618

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation in the Results and Implications section, not a separately tested result. It rests on the within-group growth of both groups and is consistent with Longnecker's (2013) IXL findings the authors cite as showing no significant effect.

"As both the control and the treatment groups improved in time and accuracy, it seems as though interacting with basic facts in any capacity is sufficient for improving basic multiplication fact fluency."
