`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive trends in self-efficacy survey means plotted in Figure 1; the article also reports an increase in self-efficacy and decrease in test anxiety going from Physics 1 post to Physics 2 pre, hypothesized to follow final grades exceeding expectations.

> "Overall, students' self-efficacy decreased from Physics 1 pre to post, which can be seen in Figure 1. For men, the average self-efficacy score remained fairly stable from the end of Physics 1 to the end of Physics 2."
