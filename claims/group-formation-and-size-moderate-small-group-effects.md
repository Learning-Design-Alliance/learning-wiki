# Self-selected and randomly formed small groups show larger achievement effects than ability-based grouping, and groups of three to five students outperform pairs

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Self-selected groups (d = 0.51) and random selection (d = 0.46) produced higher effect sizes than ability grouping by GPA and SAT scores (d = 0.21). [→ Kalaian 2018](#kalaian-2018)
`q3 i1` Studies with groups of three to five students produced larger effect sizes (d = 0.49) than studies with two-student groups (d = 0.31). [→ Kalaian 2018 (2)](#kalaian-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Kalaian 2018

Kalaian, S. A., Kasim, R. M., & Nims, J. K. (2018). Effectiveness of Small-Group Learning Pedagogies in Engineering and Technology Education: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Technology Education, 29(2). https://scholar.lib.vt.edu/ejournals/JTE/

`q3 · i2`

Subgroup analysis of group-placement moderator (Table 3): self-selected groups (11 effect sizes, d = 0.51, p = 0.00), random selection (11, d = 0.46, p = 0.001), ability grouping by GPA and SAT (4, d = 0.21, p = 0.29, nonsignificant).

> "the primary studies that placed the students into small groups by students' selecting their own groups produced much higher effect sizes (d = 0.51) than the studies that placed students in the groups by random selection (d = 0.46) and much less than the studies that placed the students in groups based on abilities"

### Kalaian 2018 (2)

Kalaian, S. A., Kasim, R. M., & Nims, J. K. (2018). Effectiveness of Small-Group Learning Pedagogies in Engineering and Technology Education: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Technology Education, 29(2). https://scholar.lib.vt.edu/ejournals/JTE/

`q3 · i1`

Subgroup analysis of group size (Table 3): two-student groups (6 effect sizes, d = 0.31, p = 0.22, nonsignificant) versus three (12, d = 0.48), four (6, d = 0.49), and five-student groups (2, d = 0.49), all statistically significant.

> "the primary studies with small groups of three to five students produced larger effect sizes (d = 0.49) than the studies with groups of two students (d = 0.31)"
