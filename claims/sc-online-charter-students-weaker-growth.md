# Online charter school students show substantially weaker growth than both TPS peers and brick-and-mortar charter students in reading and math

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### CREDO 2019

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in South Carolina. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-south-carolina-2019-2/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Delivery-system comparison across roughly 10,000 online charter students in six online campuses versus brick-and-mortar charters and statewide average TPS VCRs; online students also lagged brick-and-mortar peers by 41 days in reading and 89 in math.

> "students attending online charter schools have weaker growth in both reading and math compared to the average TPS VCRs. The gap translates to 35 and 118 fewer days of learning for online charter students in reading and math, respectively."
