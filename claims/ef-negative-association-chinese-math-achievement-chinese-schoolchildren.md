# Children's executive function scores are negatively associated with their Chinese and mathematics achievement, with inhibition control a stronger predictor than working memory

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In hierarchical regressions controlling for demographics, Chinese performance was negatively associated with working memory (β = −0.129) and inhibition control (β = −0.191); CHEXI scores are reverse-scored, so higher EF means better performance. [→ Wang Lijing 2025](#wang-lijing-2025)

## Evidence

### Wang Lijing 2025

Wang Lijing, Sun Jiancui, Wang Lijuan, Zheng Danheng, Zhou Yulan. (2025). Fundamental motor skills, executive functions, and academic achievement of Chinese schoolchildren: Testing a mediating model. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0336470

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical regression analysis (Model 3, N = 733) predicting Chinese performance after controlling for age, gender, family income, and mother's education. The model explained "17.6% of the variance in the model". CHEXI is reverse-scored, so negative coefficients indicate higher EF goes with better performance.

> "Academic performance in Chinese had a negative association with working memory (β = −0.129, p = 0.005) and inhibition control (β = −0.191, p < 0.001) but a positive association with object control (β = 0.198, p < 0.001), thus explaining 17.6% of the variance in the model"

### Wang Lijing 2025 (2)

Wang Lijing, Sun Jiancui, Wang Lijuan, Zheng Danheng, Zhou Yulan. (2025). Fundamental motor skills, executive functions, and academic achievement of Chinese schoolchildren: Testing a mediating model. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0336470

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression analysis (Model 3, N = 733) predicting mathematics performance with the same demographic controls, explaining "14.7% of the variance in the model". Working memory was not significant in this model (p = 0.071 per Table 4).

> "Academic performance in mathematics had a negative relationship with inhibition (β = −0.246, p < 0.001) but had a positive relationship with object control (β = 0.095, p < 0.01) and stability skills (β = 0.096, p < 0.05), thus explaining 14.7% of the variance in the model"
