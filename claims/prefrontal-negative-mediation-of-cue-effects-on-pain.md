## Evidence

### Atlas Lauren Y 2022

Atlas Lauren Y, Dildine Troy C, Palacios-Barrios Esther E, Yu Qingbao, Reynolds Richard C, Banker Lauren A, Grant Shara S, Pine Daniel S. (2022). Instructions and experiential learning have similar impacts on pain and pain-related brain responses but produce dissociations in value-based reversal learning. eLife 11. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73353

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel voxelwise mediation of current cue contingency effects on medium-heat pain (Figure 5, fMRI sample). Path a effects were positive in bilateral anterior insula, left dorsal posterior insula, dACC, and right anterior prefrontal cortex, and negative in left sgACC.

> "we observed significant negative mediation by the right VMPFC/OFC, left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC), the dorsomedial prefrontal cortex (DMPFC), and the sgACC"

### Atlas Lauren Y 2022 (2)

Atlas Lauren Y, Dildine Troy C, Palacios-Barrios Esther E, Yu Qingbao, Reynolds Richard C, Banker Lauren A, Grant Shara S, Pine Daniel S. (2022). Instructions and experiential learning have similar impacts on pain and pain-related brain responses but produce dissociations in value-based reversal learning. eLife 11. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73353

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same mediation analysis; the authors conclude dynamic effects of predictive cues and reversals on pain-related brain responses are similar whether individuals learn through instruction or experience, despite stronger cue effects on subjective pain in the Instructed Group.

> "we did not observe significant moderation by Group in any of the paths at FDR-corrected thresholds"
