Preregistered 2×2 ANOVA on fourth-root-transformed Stage 2 completion times in the lab experiment. The quoted interaction, η²p = .028, shows incentive effects on time investment depended on prior genAI access; post hoc tests showed incentives slowed only no-genAI participants.

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory linear mixed-effects regression of Stage 2 per-question completion time with participants and items as random effects. The quoted three-way interaction shows the genAI × incentive pattern was significant only at high question difficulty; no standardised effect size is printed.
