## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Scores on the 18-question protoPIQL are fairly normally distributed with an average of 11 out of 18 correct, SD 3.0, and Cronbach's alpha of 0.67, below the commonly accepted 0.7 threshold for group measurements. [→ Trevor I. Smith 2019](#trevor-i-smith-2019)

## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2019

Trevor I. Smith, Suzanne W. Brahmia, Alexis Olsho, Andrew Boudreaux, Philip Eaton, Paul J. Kelly, Kyle J. Louis, Mitchell A. Nussenbaum, and Louis J. Remy. (2019). Developing a reasoning inventory for measuring physics quantitative literacy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1901.03351](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1901.03351)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Whole-test analysis of the protoPIQL administered to 1,076 introductory physics students who answered at least 2/3 of questions. Scores averaged "11 out of 18 correct" with SD 3.0; internal reliability was Cronbach's alpha = 0.67, below the 0.8 and 0.7 thresholds, possibly due to the instrument measuring two constructs.

> "Overall, scores are fairly normally distributed (see Fig. 1), with an average (mean, median, and mode) of 11 out of 18 correct, a standard deviation of 3.0, and small but negative values of both skewness and kurtosis (-0.3 and -0.2, respectively)."
