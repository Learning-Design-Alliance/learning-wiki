## Evidence

### Jiang Nana 2025

Jiang Nana, Li Haijun, Ju Soon-Yew, Kong Lai-Kuan, Li Jing. (2025). Pre-service teachers' empathy and attitudes toward inclusive education—The chain mediating role of teaching motivation and inclusive education efficacy. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0321066

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM structural model on the same 480-respondent survey; the article also reports teaching motivation → attitudes (β = 0.333, t = 6.911, p < 0.01). It notes "empathy has a low explanatory power for teaching motivation" because R2 = 0.059 for H2.

> "empathy has a positive effect on pre-service teachers' teaching motivation (β=0.244, t = 4.650, p < 0.01, LL = 0.142, UL = 0.343)"

### Jiang Nana 2025 (2)

Jiang Nana, Li Haijun, Ju Soon-Yew, Kong Lai-Kuan, Li Jing. (2025). Pre-service teachers' empathy and attitudes toward inclusive education—The chain mediating role of teaching motivation and inclusive education efficacy. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0321066

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bootstrapped indirect effect in the same PLS-SEM analysis (10000 resamples). The article reports the indirect path Empathy→TM→ATIE as significant, supporting H4.

> "Teaching motivation mediates the relationship between empathy and pre-service teachers' attitudes toward inclusive education (β=0.081, t = 3.756, p < 0.01, LL = 0.023, UL = 0.090)"
