Thematic analysis of think-aloud and observation data from the case study. The article also reports that 'The AI didn't recognize the text for some people,' requiring a log-out and repeat, and that three observers noted the AI took a few minutes to generate feedback.

"The vocabulary level of the AI Helper feedback was challenging for some students to comprehend. Teacher Five noted, “[Student Five] wants the language of the AI Helper to be simplified, avoiding some difficult words.”"
