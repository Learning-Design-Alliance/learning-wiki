### Gutersohn Caterina 2025

Gutersohn Caterina, Schweingruber Sandra, Haudenschild Maximilian, Huber Markus, Greif Robert, Fuchs Alexander. (2025). Self-directed learning versus traditional instructor-led learning for education on a new anaesthesia workstation: a noninferiority, randomised, controlled trial. BJA: British Journal of Anaesthesia 135: 990-996. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bja.2025.03.043

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary endpoint asked at the end of the competency test in the randomised trial: '83.3% (n=90/108) in the instructor-guided treatment arm favoured traditional teacher-led workshop' versus 60.5% (n=69/114) of the self-directed group (P<0.001).

> "Teaching-method preferences differed significantly: 83.3% (n=90/108) in the instructor-guided treatment arm favoured traditional teacher-led workshop; 60.5% (n=69/114) of the self-directed workshop favoured the traditional teacher-led workshop (P<0.001)."
