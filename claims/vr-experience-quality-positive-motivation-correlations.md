## Evidence

### Alkhammash L 2025

Alkhammash L, Saraco AN, Goes Schaurich C, Lo BSY, Krebs C. (2025). The impact of an immersive virtual reality anatomy lab for informal science education. Anat Sci Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70087

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of opinion-survey topics (Experience, PPI, PLO) against IMMS, CLQ, IPQ, and SSQ scores, with 39 correlations performed and significance confirmed by Student's t-test. The article reports "positive correlations observed between the perceived quality of the VR experience" and motivation measures; Pearson coefficients for these links are not printed in the available text.

> "positive correlations observed between the perceived quality of the VR experience reported by the learners and the learners’ motivation to learn, attention, confidence, satisfaction, and potential positive impact on their education (p < 0.05)"
