## Evidence

### Mohamad Nawal 2025

Mohamad Nawal, Rousseau Kim-Louise, Dowlut Fatimah, Gering Milton, Thomas Kevin G. F. (2025). Symptoms of ADHD and Other Common Mental Disorders Influence Academic Success in South African Undergraduates. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547241310659

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Linear regressions (Tables 6 and 7) on the Time 1 sample predicted weighted first-semester GPA from ASRS Inattention versus Hyperactivity-Impulsivity subscales with age, Grade 12, and NBT controls; the printed inattention effect was ηp2 < .01.

> "Analyses indicated that inattention symptoms were better predictors of academic performance than hyperactivity-impulsivity symptoms (p = .04, ηp2 < .01 versus p = .21, ηp2 < .01)."

### Mohamad Nawal 2025 (2)

Mohamad Nawal, Rousseau Kim-Louise, Dowlut Fatimah, Gering Milton, Thomas Kevin G. F. (2025). Symptoms of ADHD and Other Common Mental Disorders Influence Academic Success in South African Undergraduates. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547241310659

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Regressions (Tables 6 and 7) on the three-wave completers showed inattention predicted both adjustment outcomes with printed effect sizes partial eta squared ηp2 = .10 and ηp2 = .02, whereas the article reports "analyses detected no significant association between hyperactivity-impulsivity symptoms" and either adjustment outcome (p = .29; p = .49).

> "There was also a significant association between inattention scores and (a) magnitude of academic adjustment, p < .001, ηp2 = .10 (specifically, higher symptom counts predicted smaller magnitude of academic adjustment), and (b) academic adjustment at the end of the semester, p = .04, ηp2 = .02."
