## Evidence

### Segura-Fornieles 2026

Segura-Fornieles, A. J., Márquez-Hernández, V. V., García-Viola, A., Rodríguez-García, M. C., & Garrido-Molina, J. M. (2026). Improving operational preparedness for armored vehicle casualty rescue: effectiveness of simulation-based training with a novel extrication device. Frontiers in Public Health, 14. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1910210

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest study (N = 156); Table 3 reports ability to teach others rose from 32.7% to 96.8% and readiness to use the device from 43.6% to 100%, both p < 0.001.

> "Ability to teach others how to use the device 51 (32.7%) 105(67.3%) 151(96.8%) 5(3.5%) <0.001 Readiness to use the device 68 (43.6%) 88 (56.4%) 156 (100%) - <0.001"

### Segura-Fornieles 2026 (2)

Segura-Fornieles, A. J., Márquez-Hernández, V. V., García-Viola, A., Rodríguez-García, M. C., & Garrido-Molina, J. M. (2026). Improving operational preparedness for armored vehicle casualty rescue: effectiveness of simulation-based training with a novel extrication device. Frontiers in Public Health, 14. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1910210

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same study (N = 156); willingness to recommend the device self-rated on a 0–10 scale. Statistically significant improvement reported with no printed effect size.

> "statistically significant differences were found when comparing the recommendation of device use (p < 0.001) between the pre-intervention moment (M = 7.87, SD = 1.71) and post-intervention (M = 9.05, SD = 0.83)."
