## Evidence

### Saniee Nadia 2026

Saniee Nadia, Seidi Masoumeh, Amiri Fatemeh. (2026). Blended learning in laboratory science education: comparison of flipped classroom and traditional lectures in immunohematology training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09985-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Parallel-group randomized controlled study with 196 students. Satisfaction measured via an 18-item questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha = 0.89) on a six-point Likert scale found significantly higher satisfaction in the intervention group, with effect size r = 0.850.

> "the mean (± SD) scores were 42.780 ± 5.004 and 81.729 ± 5.873 for the control and intervention groups, respectively. The mean satisfaction score was significantly higher in the intervention group (P-value < 0.001)."
