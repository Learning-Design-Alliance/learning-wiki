## Evidence

### Smaragdi Loutou 2018

Smaragdi Loutou, Nikolaos Tselios, Panagiota Altanopoulou. (2018). The effect of twitter-mediated activities on learning outcome and student engagement: A case study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1803.01343](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1803.01343)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of 17 students' Twitter logs and course grades in a one-group pretest-posttest case study. The article reports the exam grade was "highly correlated with the total comments on microactivity", with Pearson's r = 0.66, p = 0.00. The correlation is observational, not causal.

> "As to the degree of final examination appear to be highly correlated with the total comments on microactivity (Pearson's r = 0,66, p = 0,00 <0,05, s)."

### Smaragdi Loutou 2018 (2)

Smaragdi Loutou, Nikolaos Tselios, Panagiota Altanopoulou. (2018). The effect of twitter-mediated activities on learning outcome and student engagement: A case study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1803.01343](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1803.01343)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Same correlational analysis of Twitter logs and grades. The article reports total Twitter activity "shows a high correlation with the degree of the final exa ms", with Pearson's r = 0.59, p = 0.01. Small single-cohort case study; no control group.

> "Finally, the total activity on Twitter seems to confirm the above, since it shows a high correlation with the degree of the final exa ms (Pearson's r = 0,59, p = 0,01 <0,05, s)."
