Androni Alexandra, Fleer Joke, Smids Laura R., van der Wouden Joke M., Jaarsma A. Debbie C., Schönrock-Adema Johanna. (2024). Identifying Behaviours Representative of Agentic Engagement in Pre-clinical Medical Education PBL Groups Based on Literature and Observations. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1414

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The authors' interpretation of their observational findings in pre-clinical medical PBL groups, where the expression of agentic engagement "differed notably from secondary education contexts for which agentic engagement was originally operationalized".

> "Our findings indicate that agentic engagement cannot be generalized universally across all situations or environments. Instead, it is shaped and defined by the particular context in which it takes place."
