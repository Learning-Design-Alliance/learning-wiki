## Evidence

### Bekaert 2020

Bekaert, H., Van Winckel, H., Van Dooren, W., Steegen, A., & De Cock, M. (2020). Design and validation of an instrument to test students' understanding of the apparent motion of the Sun and stars. Physical Review Physics Education Research, 16(2), 020135. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.16.020135

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

First pilot study with 42 Flemish 5th-year secondary school students who took the test after a geography curriculum covering Earth's rotation, revolution, and seasons. The mean score on all questions was 40%, with Sun questions (M = 46%) outperforming star questions (M = 34%). No inferential statistic is reported for the Sun-star contrast.

> "The mean score for all participants on all questions was M = 40%, SD = 14% (n = 42). On average the seven Sun questions (M = 46%, SD = 20%) are answered more correctly than the seven star questions (M = 34%, SD = 15%)."
