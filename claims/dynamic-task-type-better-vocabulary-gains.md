## Evidence

### Gholam Reza Haji Pour Nezhad & Nasrin Shokrpour 2012

Gholam Reza Haji Pour Nezhad & Nasrin Shokrpour. (2012). The Impact of Task Type and Cognitive Style on Vocabulary Learning. English Language Teaching, 5(9). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n9p17

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Four-month randomized controlled study: 84 intermediate EFL learners matched into two groups of 42, taught identical reading passages twice weekly; ANOVA on the Nelson post-test "reject the equality of means", and the dynamic group mean of 28.735 exceeded the static group mean of 26.600.
