# Both AI-supported and Excel-based data analysis produced significant intragroup learning gains, with domain-specific patterns across pendulum physics and data evaluation

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The AI group showed significant pre-to-post gains in the spring pendulum and data evaluation domains with very large effect sizes, and a borderline-significant gain in the thread pendulum domain. [→ Henze J 2026](#henze-j-2026)
`q3 i3` The Excel group showed a statistically significant gain only in the data evaluation domain, with a large effect size, while the thread and spring pendulum domains showed no significant change. [→ Henze J 2026 (2)](#henze-j-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Henze J 2026

Henze J, Lademann J, Becker-Genschow S and Bresges A (2026). AI-supported data analysis boosts student motivation and reduces stress in physics education. Front. Educ. 11:1719670. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1719670

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on pre-post scores of the randomly assigned AI group (n = 25) showed "signiﬁcant gains in both the spring pendulum and data evaluation domains, with very large eﬀect sizes"; the printed rank-biserial correlations were r = -0.8 (SP) and r = -0.86 (DE), and thread pendulum was borderline significant (p = 0.05, r = -0.53).

> "By contrast, the AI group exhibited signiﬁcant gains in both the spring pendulum and data evaluation domains, with very large eﬀect sizes. The thread pendulum domain reached a borderline signiﬁcance threshold, suggesting a possible eﬀect that warrants further investigation."

### Henze J 2026 (2)

Henze J, Lademann J, Becker-Genschow S and Bresges A (2026). AI-supported data analysis boosts student motivation and reduces stress in physics education. Front. Educ. 11:1719670. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1719670

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on pre-post scores of the randomly assigned Excel group (n = 25) showed that "only the data evaluation domain showed a statistically signiﬁcant improvement, with a large eﬀect size"; the printed rank-biserial correlation was r = -0.78 (DE), with the pendulum physics domains non-significant.

> "In theExcel group, only the data evaluation domain showed a statistically signiﬁcant improvement, with a large eﬀect size. The other two domains showed no signiﬁcant change."
