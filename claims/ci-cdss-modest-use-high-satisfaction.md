### JaKa Meghan M. 2025

JaKa Meghan M., Rossom Rebecca C., Crouse Bethany, Crain A Lauren, Gustafson Sally K., Ekstrom Heidi L., Zibley Laura J., Borson Soo, O'Connor Patrick J., Werner Ann M., Appana Deepa, von Sternberg Thomas L., Hanson Leah R. BMC Primary Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-025-03053-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up survey of 159 CI-CDSS arm clinicians; 51 (32%) remembered the tool and 33 reported using it. Table 2 shows 91% agreed it helped diagnose patients and 97% agreed "CI-CDSS helped provide better CI care"; 88% said diagnosis took the same or less time.

> "Of those reporting use of the tool (n = 33), most thought it helped them better diagnose and care for patients with CI, took the same amount of time or less to diagnose with the tool, and intended to continue using the tool."
