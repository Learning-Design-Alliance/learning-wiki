Finding from the Quint, Bos, and Polit (1997) randomized controlled trial as summarized in the WWC report's completing school findings section: the completion gain was driven entirely by GED receipt.

> "This positive effect on completion came entirely from New Chance 's positive and statistically significant effect on the likelihood of receiving a GED certificate."

### What Works Clearinghouse 2008 (2)

What Works Clearinghouse. (2008). New Chance: WWC Intervention Report, Dropout Prevention. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/336

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Finding from the same randomized controlled trial, reported in the WWC completing school findings section: a small, statistically significant negative effect on earning a high school diploma, contrasting with the positive GED effect.

> "New Chance was found to have a small, but statistically significant, negative effect on the likelihood of earning a high school diploma."
