# The mediation pattern from online class-related enjoyment to achievement is moderated by gender but not by major or grade

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Multi-group SEM showed the structural paths differed significantly between male and female groups (Δχ2(6) = 14.564, p < .05) but not between major groups (Δχ2(6) = 10.427, p > 0.05) or grade groups (Δχ2(18) = 37.060, p > 0.05); the LE→AA path was significant in females (0.313) but not in males (-0.012). ## Evidence

### Zeng Youlai 2023

Zeng Youlai, Zhang Wenting, Wei Jiaxin, Zhang Wen. (2023). The association between online class-related enjoyment and academic achievement of college students: a multi-chain mediating model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-023-01390-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square difference test between unconstrained and constrained multi-group SEM models using the full 1294-student sample. Table 4 reports the LE→AA path as -0.012 for males (non-significant) and 0.313 for females (significant at 0.01).

> "the restriction of equal structural weight between male and female groups had a significant effect on the model (Δχ2 (6) = 14.564, p < 05), but there were no significant effect on model in different professional groups (Δχ2 (6) = 10.427, p > 0.05) and grade groups (Δχ2 (18) = 37.060, p > 0.05)."
