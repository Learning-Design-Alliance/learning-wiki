## Evidence

### Kabooha and Elyas 2018

Raniah Kabooha & Tariq Elyas. (2018). The Effects of YouTube in Multimedia Instruction for Vocabulary Learning: Perceptions of EFL Students and Teachers. English Language Teaching, 11(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n2p72

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-test on pre-test Vocabulary Knowledge Scale scores before the module began, establishing that 'both groups were at the same level of proficiency before the module began'. No effect size is printed; equivalence was not formally tested beyond the non-significant t-test.

> "there was no significant difference (t (98) = -.495, p > 0.05) between the pre-test scores of the experimental group (M=22.6, SD=7.5) and the pre-test scores of the control group (M=23.4, SD=8.3)"
