## Evidence

### Reubenson Alan A. 2025

Reubenson Alan A., Riddell Hugh, Brewer Margo L., Ng Leo, Gucciardi Daniel F. (2025). Individual differences and self-regulatory factors are credible determinants of physiotherapy student performance on clinical placement: Insights from a measurement burst design study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10453-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-placement (level 3) predictors in multilevel SEM across 97 students' four placements, using a study-specific stressor count/frequency measure. The article reports stressors "predicted lower performance for both clinical and professional dimensions"; only unstandardised estimates and credible intervals printed.

> "Experiencing a greater number of and more frequent academic stressors week-to-week while on a placement predicted lower performance for both clinical and professional dimensions for that placement compared to a student's other placements."
