Evidence¶

Katharina Scheiter 2025¶

Katharina Scheiter. (2025). Commentary: How can We Come to Terms when Discussing the Role of Effort from the Perspective of Cognitive Load Theory and Theories of Self-regulated Learning? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10037-z

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument contrasting the two traditions' accounts of what causes effort investment; the commentary notes CLT's predominant notion of effort remains a data-driven one because motivational influences cannot be modelled in CLT.

"According to CLT, a high task difficulty will increase the demands required by a task and cause a high mental load, which may lead learners to invest more mental effort into the task"

Katharina Scheiter 2025 (2)¶

Katharina Scheiter. (2025). Commentary: How can We Come to Terms when Discussing the Role of Effort from the Perspective of Cognitive Load Theory and Theories of Self-regulated Learning? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10037-z

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The commentary reports, citing Abel et al. (2024), an experiment on learners' preferences for interleaved versus blocked study sequences when learning to categorize easily confused objects; motivation induction alone did not shift strategy choice.

"higher motivation – experimentally induced through highlighting the relevance of goal attainment – was not sufficient to make learners choose a more demanding cognitive strategy; rather, learners needed to be additionally informed about the effectiveness of the strategy"
