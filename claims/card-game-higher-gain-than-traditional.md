## Evidence

### Gutierrez 2014

Gutierrez, A. F. (2014). Development and Effectiveness of an Educational Card Game as Supplementary Material in Understanding Selected Topics in Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.13-05-0093

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A pretest–posttest quasi-experiment with two intact biology classes (n = 40 analyzed per group) at Bulacan State University–Sarmiento Campus, matched on mean IQ. A t test for independent means compared mean gain scores, and the result "favors the experimental group."

> "The computed t value of 2.508 is higher than the critical t value of 1.96. This would mean there is a significant difference between the two mean gain scores. The result of the test favors the experimental group with its higher mean gain score of 12.675 compared with the 9.225 mean gain score of the control group."
