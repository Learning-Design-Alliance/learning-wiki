## Evidence

### Simper Trevor 2020

Simper Trevor, Agley Jon, DeSalle Mallori, Todd Jennifer, Dutta Tapati. (2020). Pilot study of the influence of self-coding on empathy within an introductory motivational interviewing training. BMC Medical Education 20:43. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1956-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pilot randomized trial with 53 students in the analytic sample; repeated-measures ANOVA within-subjects time effect. The printed effect size partial ƞ2 = .685 shows a large increase in mean therapeutic empathy from pre to post training.

> "A mixed ANOVA using the general linear model found a significant main effect for the MI training program across all students (F1,51 = 110.83, p < .001). The partial ƞ2 statistic (.685, LL90%CI = .554, UL90%CI = .757) suggested that the training resulted in a large increase in mean therapeutic empathy for all students, in aggregate."
