## Evidence

### Karalar 2016

Karalar, H., & Korucu, A. T. (2016). Effects of Semantic Web Based Learning on Pre-service Teachers' ICT Learning Achievement and Satisfaction. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 4(8). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i8.1676

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 98 pre-service teachers (52 SWBL, 46 TT) in an ICT course; one-way ANCOVA used pre-test as covariate. The article prints an effect size of η² = 0.369 and reports the SWBL group's adjusted post-test score of 33.11 was "significantly higher than the 26.52 score of the control group".

> "the effects of the independent variables on the dependent variables were as follows: F = 55.627, p = .000 < 0.05 and ƞ2 = 0.369 , which reached significance."
