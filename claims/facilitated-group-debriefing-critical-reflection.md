## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Learners need strong guidance from peers and facilitators to truly reflect on self, and must feel psychologically safe to challenge their own beliefs and habits. [→ Stocker Martin 2014](#stocker-martin-2014)

## Evidence

### Stocker Martin 2014

Stocker Martin, Burmester Margarita, Allen Meredith. (2014). Optimisation of simulated team training through the application of learning theories: a debate for a conceptual framework. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-69

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument in Debate 3 drawing on Jarvis's modification of Kolb's cycle, Sandars's reflection strategies, Schön's reflection-on-action, and Miettinen's analysis of Dewey. The article argues debriefing should let the group talk and critically explore. No empirical data are reported.

> "It is highly unlikely that an individual would be able to observe unbiased experiences, reflect openly on these, conceptualise new ideas and principles, and apply these new concepts actively, without the pressure of inadequacy and facilitation through others."
