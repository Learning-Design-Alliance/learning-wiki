## Evidence

### Hung Yin-Ling 2026

Hung Yin-Ling, Zhou Yi-Chao, Feng Hsin-Pei, Lin Chia-Huei, Chien Wu-Chien, Tzeng Wen-Chii. (2026). Spherical Video‐Based Virtual Reality Training for Nurses’ Delirium Care Competence: A Randomized Controlled Trial With Qualitative Evaluation. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/8355655

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

GEE analysis of the RCT using the 10-item attitude domain of the Nurse's Assessment Ability Questionnaire in Delirium Subtypes (5-point Likert, range 10–50). Attitude scores declined at 1-month follow-up in both groups; no effect size is printed for the group contrast.

> "Attitude showed a significant overall improvement at T1 (B = 2.43, 95% CI [1.41, 3.45], p < 0.001), whereas the group‐by‐time interactions at T1 and T2 were not statistically significant."
