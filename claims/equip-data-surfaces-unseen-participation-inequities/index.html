Cunningham 2024¶

Cunningham, J., Takahashi, S., & Hirschboeck, K. (2024). Using the EQUIP observation tool to advance equity in math classrooms. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655697

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Case report of a 1st-grade teacher in the ARISE partnership during the 2021/22 school year whose video-recorded math lesson was coded with EQUIP. The data report showed "only 3 of the 9 girls (33%)" participated versus 5 of 12 boys (42%), and she had called on fewer than half of her students.

"Sophia was particularly interested in increasing participation for girls because only 3 of the 9 girls (33%) present that day participated, compared to 5 of the 12 boys (42%)"

Cunningham 2024 (2)¶

Cunningham, J., Takahashi, S., & Hirschboeck, K. (2024). Using the EQUIP observation tool to advance equity in math classrooms. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655697

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Interview statement by Haley McNamara, a researcher in the PIRT study, describing the effect of EQUIP data reports on instructors' awareness and calling practices; offered as an interpretive observation, not a measured result.

"It illuminated patterns [teachers] weren't aware of and changed who they called on."
