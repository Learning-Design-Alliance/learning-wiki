`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Per-language stepwise regressions for spelling (same covariate structure as the reading models) showed the proximal predictors "accounted for clearly higher amounts of variance in English (34.7 %) than in all other orthographies (8.9 to 16.2 %)".

> "Table 5 shows that the proximal predictors (phonological awareness, memory and RAN) accounted for clearly higher amounts of variance in English (34.7 %) than in all other orthographies (8.9 to 16.2 %)."
