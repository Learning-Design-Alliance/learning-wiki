## Evidence

### Paul Alstein 2021

Paul Alstein, Kim Krijtenburg-Lewerissa, and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2021). Teaching and learning special relativity theory in secondary and lower undergraduate education: A literature review. Physical Review Physics Education Research 17, 023101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.17.023101

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis statement of the review over the multimedia-support studies. The review reports realistic virtual environments "were shown to be successful in advancing motiva- tional and attitudinal objectives" while not yet proven to enhance conceptual understanding. No effect sizes are printed.

> "students’ everyday experience, realistic virtual environ- ments were shown to be successful in advancing motiva- tional and attitudinal objectives."

### Paul Alstein 2021 (2)

Paul Alstein, Kim Krijtenburg-Lewerissa, and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2021). Teaching and learning special relativity theory in secondary and lower undergraduate education: A literature review. Physical Review Physics Education Research 17, 023101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.17.023101

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Croxton, a scan of community-posted gameplay videos (N = 20) for segments addressing educational objectives, attitude, and engagement. Engagement was enthusiastic but the game's objective "was not reached". No effect size is printed.

> "While players and viewers were found to engage enthusiastically with the concepts of SRT, the objective of the game, namely, to make SRT less paradoxical and more intuitive, was not reached."
