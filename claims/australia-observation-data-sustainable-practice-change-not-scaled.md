# Australian case: classroom observation data collection was sustainable and scalable, but resulting changes in teachers' technology integration were not scaled

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participatory classroom observation using a web-enabled observation kit was sustained over four years and scalable, but the teachers' practice changes were not captured or scaled because professional learning was not built into the innovation. [→ Howard 2021](#howard-2021)

## Evidence

### Howard 2021

Howard, S. K., Schrum, L., Voogt, J., & Sligte, H. (2021). Designing research to inform sustainability and scalability of digital technology innovations. Educational Technology Research and Development, 69(4). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09913-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case study of participatory research with a Sydney secondary school using continuous video, audio and log data collection. Teachers changed practice based on analyses, but the article notes the absence of built-in professional learning and suggests interviews and questionnaires could have documented implementation.

> "Therefore, while collecting data was sustainable and scalable, the resulting changes in teachers' technology integration were not necessarily being sustained or able to be scaled to other contexts."
