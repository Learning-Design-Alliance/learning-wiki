Early Intervention in Reading® 2008¶

Early Intervention in Reading®. (2008). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.earlyinterventioninreading.com

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The WWC report describes the Taylor et al. (1991) randomized controlled trial of first-grade teachers in two Midwestern suburban schools. The report states the study authors found, and the WWC confirmed, "statistically significant positive effects on both measures" of alphabetics; no effect size appears in this sentence.

"When the EIR® group was compared with the comparison group, the study authors found, and the WWC confirmed, statistically significant positive effects on both measures."

Early Intervention in Reading® 2008 (2)¶

Early Intervention in Reading®. (2008). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.earlyinterventioninreading.com

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

WWC calculations for the Taylor et al. (1991) randomized controlled trial (Appendix A3.1) report a segmentation-and-blending mean difference of 3.89 favoring EIR®, an "effect size" of 0.80, statistically significant, with an improvement index of +29.

"Segmentation and blending Grade 1 12/56 14.30 (4.09) 10.41 (5.41) 3.89 0.80 Statistically significant +29"
