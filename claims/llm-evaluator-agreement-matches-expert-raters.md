### Globerson 2026

Globerson, A., Keeling, A., Choudhury, A., et al. (2026). Towards Scalable Measurement of Durable Skills. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15864

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Evaluation of the Vantage collaboration protocol with 188 Prolific-recruited participants generating 373 conversations, rated by two NYU pedagogical raters and a Gemini-based AI Evaluator. Cohen's Kappa inter-expert agreement was "in the range of 0.45-0.64", and LLM-expert agreement was similar, supporting automatic rating as a scalable alternative.

> "Agreement between the human expert raters was moderate, with Kappa in the range of 0.45-0.64 [28]. Agreement between the LLM and the human experts was similar to that between the two experts."
