`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Students completed an online satisfaction survey at week 6 and week 12 of the semester using a 7-point Likert scale from '1 = Strongly disagree' to '7 = Strongly agree'. The mean of 5.05 indicates agreement that system-generated scores represented their approach to learning.

> "Overall, participants reported in the online survey that they agreed that the Learning Analytics SRL rubric scores they received represented well their approach to learning in their subject (n = 17, M = 5.05, SD = 0.75)."
