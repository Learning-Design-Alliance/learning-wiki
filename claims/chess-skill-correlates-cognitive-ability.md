## Evidence

### Sala Giovanni 2017

Sala Giovanni, Gobet Fernand. (2017). Does Far Transfer Exist? Negative Evidence From Chess, Music, and Working Memory Training. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721417712760

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analytic review of comparison and correlational studies of the cognitive correlates of expert performance (Sala et al., 2017, as reported here): chess players outperformed non-chess players on overall cognitive ability by half a standard deviation, described as "a moderately large effect."

> "we found that chess players’ overall cognitive ability was superior to that of non-chess players by half a standard deviation, a moderately large effect."

### Sala Giovanni 2017 (2)

Sala Giovanni, Gobet Fernand. (2017). Does Far Transfer Exist? Negative Evidence From Chess, Music, and Working Memory Training. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721417712760

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of studies correlating chess skill with cognitive outcomes reported statistically significant correlations with fluid intelligence, processing speed, short-term and working memory, and comprehension knowledge; no effect sizes are printed here.

> "The second meta-analysis (Burgoyne et al., 2016) reported statistically significant correlations between chess skill and four broad measures of cognitive ability: (a) fluid intelligence"
