## Evidence

### Seidel 2015

Seidel, S. B., Reggi, A. L., Schinske, J. N., Burrus, L. W., & Tanner, K. D. (2015). Beyond the Biology: A Systematic Investigation of Noncontent Instructor Talk in an Introductory Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of transcripts from 29 recorded class sessions of a single semester-long, cotaught introductory biology course with about 270 students. The study reports quotes per session were "ranged from a low of six to a high of 68" with an average of 26 quotes per session (SEM = 2.9); no effect size is printed for this descriptive finding.

> "The number of quotes ranged from a low of six to a high of 68 within a single class session. On average, there were 26 quotes per class session, with an SEM = 2.9."
