## Evidence

### Hale Caleb 2021

Hale Caleb, Crocker Jonathan, Vanka Anita, Ricotta Daniel N, McSparron Jakob I, Huang Grace C. (2021). Cohort study of hospitalists' procedural skills: baseline competence and durability after simulation-based training. BMJ Open. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8370503/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pre/post cohort study of hospitalists at a large urban academic medical centre; simulator performances were videotaped and scored with published checklists (LP max 21, MPS 18; AP max 25, MPS 21). The Results report "only 3 (16%) and 6 (32%) hospitalists achieved MPS" at baseline. No effect size printed.

> "At baseline, only 3 (16%) and 6 (32%) hospitalists achieved MPS for LP and AP, respectively. Participants scored on average 15 out of 21 items on the LP checklist and 17 out of 25 on the AP checklist."
