## Evidence

### Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich 2023

Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Seifert Laura, Kohlhas Laura, Schmidt Mona Wanda, Ali Seher, Fan Carolyn, Köppinger Karl Felix, Müller-Stich Beat Peter, Nickel Felix. (2023). Video-based training of situation awareness enhances minimally invasive surgical performance: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-023-10006-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective, single-center, two-arm, blinded parallel-group randomized trial of 61 laparoscopy-naïve medical students; the intervention group viewed LC videos with interposed questions, controls identical videos without. The regression model gave a group β-Coefficient of 9.5 (p value 0.034).

> "The SA intervention resulted in a significant improvement in performance measured with the OSATS score (SA: 67.0 ± 11.5 versus control: 59.1 ± 14.0, p value = 0.034) as the primary outcome"
