Analytical mapping argument, not an empirical test: the authors argue materials "support children’s Attention by each isolating a single quality" (e.g., Red Rods isolate length; Number Rods' alternating red and blue sections isolate quantity through counting the sections).

"The Montessori materials support children’s Attention by each isolating a single quality. With just one feature highlighted, each set of materials allows children to focus on one new thing at a time."
