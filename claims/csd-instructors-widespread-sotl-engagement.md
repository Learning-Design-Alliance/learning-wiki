## Evidence

### Friberg 2023

Friberg, Jennifer; Hoepner, Jerry K.; Sauerwein, Allison M.; and Mandulak, Kerry (2023) "The Integration of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning into the Discipline of Communication Sciences and Disorders," Teaching and Learning in Communication Sciences & Disorders: Vol. 7: Iss. 1, Article 10. https://doi.org/10.30707/TLCSD7.1.1675490380.873306

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey respondents (n = 120) selected all applicable engagement levels on a continuum from no engagement to reading, sharing, and conducting SoTL. Only one respondent reported no engagement; 80 of 120 reported using SoTL to inform teaching.

> "The vast majority of survey respondents reported reading/accessing SoTL at least occasionally with two-thirds of respondents actively using SoTL to inform their teaching. Close to one-third of respondents reported conducting a SoTL study of their own."
