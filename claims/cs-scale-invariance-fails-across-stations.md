## Evidence

### Piumatti Giovanni 2021

Piumatti Giovanni, Cerutti Bernard, Perron Noëlle Junod. (2021). Assessing communication skills during OSCE: need for integrated psychometric approaches. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02552-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group confirmatory factor analysis with maximum likelihood estimation tested configural, metric, scalar and strict invariance across stations and across groups of stations (147 fourth-year students across six stations; 149 third-year students across three stations). The article reports the minimum requirement "was not reached".

> "we cannot safely assume that the calibration of these items is similar across stations. This implies that the meaning of the obtained score from this scale is not the same across stations"
