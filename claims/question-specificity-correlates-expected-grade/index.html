Evidence¶

Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi 2025¶

Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi, N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Analyzing Undergraduate Problem-Solving in Physics Through Interaction With an AI Chatbot. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14778 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.14778

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis linking each student's question specificity (fraction of questions coded specific) to self-reported expected course grade in Week 9. The article reports "Pearson r ≈ 0.43, p < 0.0001" and notes considerable scatter, with some high-specificity students expecting only mid-range grades.

"A mild positive correlation was observed (Pearson r ≈ 0.43, p < 0.0001). In general, students who engaged in a higher proportion of specific, detailed questioning during the chatbot session reported higher expected grades."
