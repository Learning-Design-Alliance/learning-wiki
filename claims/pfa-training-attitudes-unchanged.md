### He Yazhou 2026

He Yazhou, Ding Yulan, Yan Jiaqin, Xiong Xiaoning, Huang Shan, Li Sijian. (2026). Evaluation of the Effectiveness of Psychological First Aid Training for Community Nurses in China: A Quasi‐Experimental Study on Knowledge and Skills Enhancement. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70497
