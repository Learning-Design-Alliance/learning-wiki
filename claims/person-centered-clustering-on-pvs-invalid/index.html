Dumas 2026¶

Dumas, D., Goecke, B., Kagan, S., & Weiss, S. (2026). Zooming Out On Education: Making Valid Psychological Inferences From Large-Scale Assessment Data. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10110-7

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Conceptual argument about valid analytic uses of PVs. The article states such methods 'are outside the valid use of PVs' because they go beyond population-level distributions, while clustering sampling-frame units 'can be done validly'.

"More sophisticated but still on epistemically shaky ground are person-centered clustering methodologies that seek to sort students into underlying groups."
