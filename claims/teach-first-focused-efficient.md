# Teachers perceived the Teach-First Approach as more focused and a more efficient approach to learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers perceived that beginning with the teacher-facilitated lesson produced more on-task behaviour and more efficient use of lesson time than beginning with the challenge. [→ Russo and Hopkins 2017](#russo-and-hopkins-2017)

## Evidence

### Russo and Hopkins 2017

James Russo, Sarah Hopkins. (2017). How does lesson structure shape teacher perceptions of teaching with challenging tasks? Mathematics Teacher Education and Development, 19(1), 30-46. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1152737

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Rachel's interview comment from the Addition Unit; she also reported that under the Task-First Approach some students ran out of time before finding success, whereas with the lesson first they knew what to do and used the time well.

> "I think they were more on task in the lesson first scenario. I think some of them, with the challenge first, when they didn’t know what to do, they just got distracted, and just started mucking around a little bit."
