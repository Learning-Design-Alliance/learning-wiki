### Vadla MS 2023

Vadla MS, Mduma ER, Kvaløy JT, Mdoe P, Hhoki BH, Sarangu S, Michael P, Oftedal B, Ersdal H. (2023). Increase in Newborns Ventilated Within the First Minute of Life and Reduced Mortality After Clinical Data-Guided Simulation Training. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Primary outcome measured by research assistants observing all births with stopwatches and recording times in the Liveborn app. Median time "decreased from median 101 (quartiles 72–150) to 55 (45–67) seconds (P < 0.001)" during the campaign year.

> "Time to first ventilation decreased from median 101 (quartiles 72–150) to 55 (45–67) seconds (P < 0.001), before increasing to 67 (49–97) and 85 (57–133) seconds after intervention."
