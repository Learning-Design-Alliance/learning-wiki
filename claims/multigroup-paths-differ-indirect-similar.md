# Structural paths differ between undergraduate and postgraduate students, but the indirect mediating effects do not differ significantly

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A Wald χ2 test found significant group differences in path coefficients between undergraduates and postgraduates, while indirect effects did not differ significantly between groups. [→ Shengyao Ye 2024](#shengyao-ye-2024)

## Evidence

### Shengyao Ye 2024

Shengyao Ye, Xuefen Lin, Jenatabadi Hashem Salarzadeh, Samsudin Nadia, Chunchun Ke, Ishak Zahari. (2024). Emotional intelligence impact on academic achievement and psychological well-being among university students: the mediating role of positive psychological characteristics. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01886-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multigroup SEM comparing 323 undergraduates and 189 postgraduates rejected equality of path coefficients; the article reports the Wald χ2 test result as significant (χ2 = 65.33, p = 0.005), and Table 5 shows no significant group differences for any indirect effect.

> "This substantial outcome resulted in the rejection of the null hypothesis that the route coefficients are similar between the undergraduate and postgraduate groups."
