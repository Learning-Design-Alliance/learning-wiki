Evidence¶

Brunner 2007¶

Brunner, M.; Artelt, C.; Krauss, Stefan; Baumert, J. (2007). Coaching for the PISA test. Learning and Instruction 17 (2), S. 111-122. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2007.01.002

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Research question from the abstract: the study investigates whether performance on the OECD PISA reading and mathematics assessments, described as a "low-stakes test" with no personal relevance for students, can be fostered by coaching and pretesting.

"we explore whether student performance on the reading and mathematics assessments of the OECD's Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA) can be fostered by coaching (and administering a pretest)."

Related Claims¶

  • Only the combined effects of pretesting and coaching have substantial positive effects on student performance on the low-stakes PISA assessments — related
  • Coaching and pretest effects on PISA performance were studied separately for each content domain (reading and mathematics) — related
  • Differential coaching effects across academic tracks were examined by sampling German Hauptschule and Gymnasium students — related
  • Coaching is known to improve student performance on high-stakes tests, motivating the question for low-stakes tests — related
