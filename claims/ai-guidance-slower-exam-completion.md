## Evidence

### Karni Ofri 2025

Karni Ofri, Shitrit Itamar Ben, Perlin Amit, Jedwab Roni, Wacht Oren, Fuchs Lior. (2025). AI-enhanced guidance demonstrated improvement in novices' Apical-4-chamber and Apical-5-chamber views. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06905-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome in the same randomized trial of 44 novices: the non-AI group completed the exam about one minute faster than the AI-enhanced group, a difference the authors say was anticipated because handling the probe and AI instructions takes longer.

> "the scanning times were approximately one minute faster in the non-AI group compared to the AI-enhanced group during the proficiency exam, as anticipated [mean 401 s (± SD 51 s) vs. 348 s (± SD 81 s) respectively; p = 0.038; refer to Table 2; Fig. 5)."
