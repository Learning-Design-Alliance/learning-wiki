## Evidence

### Sutcher 2017

Sutcher, L., Podolsky, A., & Espinoza, D. (2017). Supporting principals' learning: Key features of effective programs. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/supporting-principals-learning

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report's review cites studies of successful programs at Bank Street College, Delta State University, the University of Connecticut, and the University of San Diego, finding recruits were more likely to have been instructional coaches and teacher mentors and "more likely to become and remain principals."

> "Recruits in these programs were also more likely to become and remain principals, to feel competent, and to plan to stay in their jobs than their counterparts across the nation."
