## Evidence

### Serpil 2026

Serpil, H., & Mor, M. K. (2026). Bounded reliance: A source credibility perspective on EFL students' engagement with AI-generated writing feedback. Journal of Language Pedagogy and Linguistics, 1(1), 1-27. https://doi.org/10.0.20.161/zenodo.22828386

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview-based thematic analysis in the same 17-student case study. One participant stated, "I am hesitant to share my personal work with AI platforms because they can use our data in any way they want"; another said, "I find AI demotivating; I would prefer not to use it at all."

> "Several students expressed hesitation about uploading their written work to AI platforms, emphasizing uncertainties regarding data privacy and the potential use of their personal information."
