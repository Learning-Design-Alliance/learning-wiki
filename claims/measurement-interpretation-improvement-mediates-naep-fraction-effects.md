## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a path analytic mediation analysis, the indirect effect of intervention versus control on NAEP via number line improvement was significant (0.14, CI = .0557 to .2811), and the direct effect was no longer significant, indicating full mediation. [→ Fuchs et al. 2016](#fuchs-et-al-2016)

## Evidence

### Fuchs et al. 2016

Lynn S. Fuchs, Robin F. Schumacher, Jessica Long, Jessica Namkung, Amelia S. Malone, Amber Wang, Carol L. Hamlett, Nancy C. Jordan, Robert S. Siegler, and Paul Changas. (2016). Effects of Intervention to Improve At-Risk Fourth Graders' Understanding, Calculations, and Word Problems with Fractions. Elementary School Journal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566704

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bootstrapped path analytic mediation (5000 draws) contrasting combined intervention against control, with number line improvement as mediator and NAEP as outcome. The article concludes "improvement in measurement understanding fully mediated the effect of intervention versus control on NAEP."

> "The coefficient for the indirect effect (the c' path) of 0.14 (S.E. = .05) was significant (CI = .0557 to .2811). Thus, improvement in measurement understanding fully mediated the effect of intervention versus control on NAEP."
