## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Adding redundant onscreen text to audio and image (AIT mode) did not improve, and statistically reduced, post-test performance relative to AI mode (p = 0.000). [→ Aldalalah and Fong 2010](#aldalalah-and-fong-2010)

## Evidence

### Aldalalah and Fong 2010

Osamah (Mohammad Ameen) Ahmad Aldalalah & Soon Fook Fong. (2010). Effects of Modality and Redundancy Principles on the Learning and Attitude of a Computer-Based Music Theory Lesson among Jordanian Primary Pupils. International Education Studies, 3(3). https://www.ccsenet.org/ies

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA post hoc pairwise comparison with pre-test covariate among the three treatment groups (N = 405 third-grade pupils). The AI group (Mean = 25.17, SD = 3.35, n = 128) significantly outperformed the AIT group (Mean = 16.63, SD = 4.33, n = 136), reported as "p = 0.000" (LSD post hoc comparison); no standardised effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "The AI (Mean = 25.17, SD = 3.35,) pupils’ learning significantly outperformed the TI (Mean = 16.82, SD = 3.77) and the AIT (Mean = 16.63, SD = 4.33)."
