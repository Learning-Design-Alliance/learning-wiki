q3 · i? · review · r3

Review reports a national difference-in-differences study of CEP exposure and graduation rates; the printed estimate was 1.506%pt (p = 0.024) and effects were "positive but diminishing returns" with each additional year of exposure.

"One study showed that CEP exposure was associated with improved graduation rates at schools with the largest increases in free meal access by 1.506%pt (p = 0.024) [39]. Each additional year of CEP exposure yielded positive but diminishing returns."
