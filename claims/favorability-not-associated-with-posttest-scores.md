Mediation analysis testing whether posttest performance influenced favorability (measured after the posttest), in the same dataset. The association was non-significant: "t(51) = 1.67,p=.101". Equivalence was not tested; no effect size printed.

> "We did not find any evidence that the favorability scores were associated with posttest scores,t(51) = 1.67,p=.101, suggesting that the favorability scores are likely to measure a general favorability toward the practice method."
