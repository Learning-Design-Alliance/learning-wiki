## Evidence

### Hecht 2023

Hecht, C. A., Murphy, M. C., Dweck, C. S., Bryan, C. J., Trzesniewski, K. H., Medrano, F. N., Giani, M., Mhatre, P., & Yeager, D. S. (2023). Shifting the mindset culture to address global educational disparities. npj Science of Learning 8:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00181-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

A categorization experiment asked college students to sort 119 specific teaching practices as indicating a growth or fixed instructor mindset; growth-categorized practices were endorsed "by ≥99% of students." Findings build on earlier focus groups and a survey of instructor perceptions.

> "expressing a belief that anyone could be a top student in the class, discussing ways for students to improve their grades over the course of the semester, and encouraging students to ask questions to improve their understanding were all categorized as indicating a growth mindset by ≥99% of students."
