## Evidence

### Tadese Mesfin 2022

Tadese Mesfin, Yeshaneh Alex, Mulu Getaneh Baye. (2022). Determinants of good academic performance among university students in Ethiopia: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03461-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression among 615 Hawassa University students in an institution-based cross-sectional study found non-smokers had higher odds of good performance, with the article reporting "AOR = 3.15, 95% CI = 1.21-7.30" (p = 0.025). No standardised effect size printed.

> "Students who didn’t smoke cigarettes were three times more likely to register good academic grades compared to those who smoke (AOR = 3.15, 95% CI = 1.21-7.30)."
