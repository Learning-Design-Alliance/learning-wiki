## Evidence

### Mason Ron 2020

Mason Ron, Doherty Kathleen, Eccleston Claire, Winbolt Margaret, Long Marita, Robinson Andrew. (2020). Effect of a dementia education intervention on the confidence and attitudes of general practitioners in Australia: a pretest post-test study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033218

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive percentages reported in the Results of the pretest post-test study; the article states that "Before the workshop, only 13.8% of GPRs" were confident to diagnose. No effect size is printed for this percentage change.

> "Before the workshop, only 13.8% of GPRs were 'confident (either strongly agreed or agreed) in their ability to diagnose' compared with 44.2% of supervisors, rising to 60.4% for GPRs postworkshop (62.6% post for supervisors)."
