## Evidence

### Justyna P. Zwolak and Eric Brewe 2015

Justyna P. Zwolak and Eric Brewe. (2015). The impact of social integration on student persistence in introductory Modeling Instruction courses. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Information-theoretic threshold analysis on the same MI network data, reported in Table III; the table prints thresholds including total degree θ = 1 (χ2 = 11.05, p < 10−3) and indegree θ = 1 (χ2 = 7.37, p = 0.007) for persistence in physics, and closeness θ = 0.022 (χ2 = 4.53, p = 0.033) for persistence in MI.
