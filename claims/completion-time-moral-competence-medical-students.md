## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Medical students completing the MCT in 15 minutes scored about d ≈ 0.35 higher than those completing in 5 minutes, controlling for trait anxiety. [→ Zielina M 2025](#zielina-m-2025)
`q3 i0` Among psychology students no such association was observed (d ≈ 0.06, not significant). [→ Zielina M 2025 (2)](#zielina-m-2025-2)
