Open Court Reading© 2014¶

Open Court Reading©. (2014). WWC Intervention Report: Beginning Reading, Updated October 2014. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/347

q2 · i? · review · r3

A systematic review of the intervention literature against the Beginning Reading topic-area protocol. The article reports that 27 studies failed group design standards, one failed single-case standards, and 155 did not meet eligibility criteria; the two included studies covered 1,113 beginning readers in six states.

"The WWC identified 185 studies that investigated the effects of Open Court Reading© on the reading skills of beginning readers."

Open Court Reading© 2014 (2)¶

Open Court Reading©. (2014). WWC Intervention Report: Beginning Reading, Updated October 2014. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/347

q2 · i? · review · r3

Domain-coverage finding from the review's effectiveness summary: the two qualifying studies reported findings only in general reading achievement and comprehension, leaving alphabetics and reading fluency without studies meeting standards.

"There were no studies that meet standards in the two other domains, so this intervention report does not report on the effectiveness of Open Court Reading© for those domains."
