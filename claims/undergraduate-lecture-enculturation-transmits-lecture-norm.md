A theoretical-argument element of the model (Transmission: Undergraduates Learn to Learn via Lecture). The authors support it with cited studies showing one's own learning experiences often inform future teaching decisions, calling it "an early experience of enculturation".

> "A continued tradition of teaching undergraduates via straight lecture perpetuates an early normative belief into the population of potential future graduate students that lecture is both the preferred and accepted way to teach."
