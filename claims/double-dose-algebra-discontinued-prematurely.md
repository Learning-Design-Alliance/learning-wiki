The article's narrative of the Algebra Initiative reports that the double-dose intervention, which gave at-risk freshmen a support period plus algebra, was dropped before outcomes appeared; the authors cite Nomi, Raudenbush, & Smith (2021) for later-documented long-term benefits.

> "Double-dose algebra was jettisoned after a few years, however, because improved academic outcomes had not been documented yet, and administrators complained that it complicated course scheduling rooted in established teacher preferences."
