## Evidence

### Ramadanov N 2026

Ramadanov N, Heinz M, Hable R, Becker R, Banke IJ. (2026). Surgeon experience in hip arthroscopy improves operative efficiency and reduces conversion to total hip arthroplasty: A meta‐analysis. Knee Surgery, Sports Traumatology, Arthroscopy. https://doi.org/10.1002/ksa.70431

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of four studies reporting conversion to total hip arthroplasty, where odds ratios consistently favoured late cases, ranging from 0.01 to 0.31. The estimate showed "a statistically significant reduction in the risk of conversion to THA for late cases", with moderate heterogeneity (I² = 49%).

> "The pooled random‐effects estimate demonstrated a statistically significant reduction in the risk of conversion to THA for late cases (OR 0.10; 95% CI 0.01 to 0.98)."
