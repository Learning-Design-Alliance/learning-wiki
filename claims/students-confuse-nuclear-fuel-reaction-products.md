## Evidence

### Mirofora Pilakouta and John Sinatkas 2020

Mirofora Pilakouta and John Sinatkas. (2020). Surveying Undrergraduare Greek Student's Knowledge on Some Issues of Radiations and Nuclear Energy. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.03549](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.03549)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey question B4 on radioactive elements released after a nuclear power plant accident. The article reports 10% correct (cesium and iodine), 50% choosing plutonium and uranium, and 24% answering don't know; the authors conclude students "confuses the nuclear fuel with the reaction products."

> "Only 10% answered correctly (cesium and iodine). Half of students (50%), table 2, have the wrong perception that Plutonium and Uranium are the radioactive elements that are mainly released in the environment after a nuclear accident."
