Easton 2023¶

Easton, J.Q. & Diaz, B. (2023). Lasting differences: Math grades and gender. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/lasting-differences

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of CPS administrative records for 29,229 first-time ninth-graders in algebra and geometry across 2016-17 and 2017-18. The report states "the difference between young women and young men in each racial/ethnic group ranged from a low of 3.2 among AAPI students, to a high of 4.5 among Latine students."

"Young men earned fewer points than young women in all racial/ethnic groups; the difference between young women and young men in each racial/ethnic group ranged from a low of 3.2 among AAPI students, to a high of 4.5 among Latine students."
