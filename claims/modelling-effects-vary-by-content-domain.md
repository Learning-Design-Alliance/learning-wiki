## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Probability and statistics generated the highest subgroup effect (ES = 3.11, SE = 1.17), but removing the Eysink et al. outlier of 4.49 reduced it to ES = 0.21 (SE = 0.17); calculus produced the lowest effect (ES = 0.38, SE = 0.09). [→ Andrzej Sokolowski 2015](#andrzej-sokolowski-2015)

## Evidence

### Andrzej Sokolowski 2015

Andrzej Sokolowski. (2015). The Effects of Mathematical Modelling on Students’ Achievement-Meta-Analysis of Research. The IAFOR Journal of Education, Volume III - Issue I - Winter 2015. https://journals.iafor.org/index.php/joe

`q3 · i3`

Content-domain moderator analysis across algebra, calculus, probability and statistics, and geometry subgroups. The article reports "ES = 3.11, SE = 1.17" for probability and statistics, an outlier of 4.49, and ES = 0.21 with the outlier removed; calculus was the lowest at ES = 0.38 (SE = 0.09).

> "According to computations, probability and statistics, generated the highest effect size (ES = 3.11, SE = 1.17).  The magnitude of this effect size was inevitably affected by an outlier of 4.49 (Eysink et al., 2009). If this study were re moved, the effect size would have been ES = 0.21, with SE = 0.17."
