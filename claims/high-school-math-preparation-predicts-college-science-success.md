The chapter reports, citing Sadler & Tai (2007), that "students’ preparation in high school mathematics – not science (!) – courses" correlates more strongly with college science success, and that more math courses relate to higher college persistence and completion rates.

> "Perhaps somewhat surprisingly, it is students’ preparation in high school mathematics – not science (!) – courses that correlates more strongly with success in college science courses (Sadler & Tai , 2007)."
