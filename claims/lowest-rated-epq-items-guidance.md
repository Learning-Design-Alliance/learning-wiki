## Evidence

### Zalewska Katarzyna 2022

Zalewska Katarzyna, Zarzycka Danuta. (2022). Best Educational Techniques in High-Fidelity Simulation According to Nursing Students—Adaptation and Validation of the Educational Practices Questionnaire (EPQ). International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192214688

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive item-level results from the Polish sample (Table 6): question six on "receiving guidance at the appropriate time" and question eight on discussing ideas with the instructor were the two lowest-rated EPQ-PO items, both M = 4.1, SD ± 1.0.

> "The question with the lowest rating among the respondents concerned receiving guidance at the appropriate time (for the student) during simulations (question six) (M = 4.1, SD ± 1.0). The second-to-lowest rated item was the question related to discussing the student’s ideas and concepts with the instructor during the simulation (question eight) (M = 4.1, SD ± 1.0)."

### Zalewska Katarzyna 2022 (2)

International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192214688

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the EPQ-IO section, question ten on productive use of simulation time had the lowest rating (M = 4.1, SD ± 1.0), while instructor commentary (question five, M = 4.4) was rated highest in that section.

> "On the other hand, the statement that time spent on simulations was more productive (question ten) had the lowest rating (M = 4.1, SD ± 1.0)."
