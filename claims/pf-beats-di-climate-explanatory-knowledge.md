## Subclaims
`q2 i2` A significant main effect of treatment group favored PF on climate explanatory knowledge (η² = .044), with no significant teacher experience effect or interaction. [→ Jacobson 2017](#jacobson-2017)

## Evidence

### Jacobson 2017

Jacobson, Michael, Markauskaite, Lina, Portolese, Alisha, Kapur, Manu, Lai, Polly, & Roberts, Gareth (2017). Designs for learning about climate change as a complex system. Learning and Instruction, 52, pp. 1-14. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.03.007

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on climate explanatory knowledge in the same quasi-experiment (110 Year Nine students). The treatment main effect favored PF (η² = .044); teacher experience and interaction were not significant. Pre-test could not be used as covariate because homogeneity of regression slopes was violated.

> "For climate explanatory knowledge, a two-way ANOVA revealed that there was a significant main effect of treatment group (F(1,106) = 4.852, p = .015, one-tailed, = .044)."
