Hossain Sheikh Jamal, Tofail Fahmida, Sujan Hasan Mahmud, Arifeen Shams El, Hamadani Jena. (2021). Factors associated with school achievement of children aged 8–10 years in rural Bangladesh: Findings from a post hoc analysis of a community-based study. PLoS ONE 16(7): e0254693. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254693

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted multiple linear regression (Table 5) comparing school types in the 372-child rural cohort. The private-school advantage in reading was 10.28 points (5.05, 15.51) and in spelling 6.22 points (1.31, 11.13); the madrasa contrast was not statistically significant.
