## Evidence

### Gökalp Parasız 2017

Gökalp Parasız, M. Hanifi Kervancıoğlu. (2017). The Usability of Erzurum Folk Songs in Viola Education. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.8. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1139085

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive content analysis of the three randomly selected songs for modal structure and playing techniques; the analysis identified maqams (Nikriz, Rast), rhythms such as 4/4 Sofyan and 9/8 Aksak, and techniques including Legato, Staccato, Detache and Martele.

> "The song “Aya Bak Nice Gider” is sol decisive in tr aditional Turkish Art Music and harmonious with Nik riz maqam for its trend character, decision and tempora ry stays. The song “Bir Gül Ektim Duvara” in tradit ional Turkish Art Music is sol decisive and harmonious wi th Rast maqam due to its characteristic of melodic cruise, decision and temporary stays."
