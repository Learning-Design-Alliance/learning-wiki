## Subclaims
`q1 i?` SBE constructs another space for learning, distinct from university-based education and clinical placements, which may increase the theory-practice split because students must negotiate different social contexts and actor expectations across sites. [→ Allan 2026](#allan-2026)

## Evidence

### Allan 2026

Allan, H. T., and M. O'Driscoll. (2026). Using Recontextualisation Theory to Understand Learning Across Multiple Sites in Simulation‐Based Nurse Education. Journal of Advanced Nursing 82, no. 5: 5513–5520. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70163

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the Findings section of this critical discussion paper, based on a literature search and the authors' research experience. The authors argue SBE "is a third learning space" requiring students to negotiate different social contexts and expectations of lecturers, assessors, clinical staff and patients. No empirical effect size is offered.

> "Using recontextualization theory, we argue that SBE has constructed yet another theory‐practice split which is unexplored in the literature; SBE is a third learning space, distinct from both university‐based education and learning on clinical placements."
