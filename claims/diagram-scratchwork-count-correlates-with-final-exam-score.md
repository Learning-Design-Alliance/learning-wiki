## Evidence

### Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Using Reflection with Peers to Help Students Learn Effective Problem Solving Strategies. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02944](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02944)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of final-exam scores against the number of problems with diagrams and with scratchworks, tested separately for the traditional and PR groups with null hypothesis of no correlation. The article reports significance but no printed correlation coefficient or effect size.

> "We ﬁnd that, for both the traditional group and the PR group, the students who had more problems with dia- grams and scratchworks were statistically (signiﬁcantly) more likely to perform well on the ﬁnal exam."
