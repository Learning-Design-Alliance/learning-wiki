## Evidence

### Adusumilli 2026

Adusumilli, S. J., Giri, D., Vaswani, P., Singh, P., Moncy, M., Pulavarthy, L. P., & Purkayastha, S. (2026). Preference vs. Performance: EEG-Based Classification of Learner Engagement in Multimodal Instruction. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1109/icalt68022.2026.00126](https://doi.org/10.1109/icalt68022.2026.00126)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Behavioral analysis in a quasi-experimental classroom MMLA study (N=37) found no significant overall pre-to-post gain ("W= 189.5, p= 0.536"); 37.84% improved, 21.62% maintained, and 40.54% declined. Equivalence was not tested.

> "A Wilcoxon signed-rank test confirmed no statistically significant overall pre-to-post difference (W= 189.5, p= 0.536)."
