## Evidence

### Silverthorn 2017

Silverthorn, N., DuBois, D. L., Lewis, K. M., Reed, A., Bavarian, N., Day, J., Ji, P., Acock, A. C., Vuchinich, S., & Flay, B. R. (2017). Effects of a School-Based Social-Emotional and Character Development Program on Self-Esteem Levels and Processes: A Cluster-Randomized Controlled Trial. SAGE Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2158244017713238

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Growth-curve analyses of the cluster-randomized trial tested eight self-esteem measures; significant Time × Condition interactions emerged "for four of the eight measures", with no group differences on global, family, appearance, or sports self-esteem. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Students in PA schools were found to have higher self-esteem in the domains of school and peers, but the remaining domains, as well as global self-esteem, showed no group differences."
