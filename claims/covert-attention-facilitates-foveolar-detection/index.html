Evidence¶

Poletti Martina 2017¶

Poletti Martina, Rucci Michele, Carrasco Marisa. (2017). Selective attention within the foveola. Nature Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4622

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Experiment 2 used gaze-contingent retinal stabilization to present targets at 10′ eccentricity within the foveola in five observers. The article reports the foveola valid-invalid difference as "22 ms ± 5 ms; parafovea: 25 ms ± 10 ms; two-tailed paired t-test, p=0.31" and accuracy slightly increased in valid trials (F(2,4) = 9.6; p=0.008).

"observers were faster at detecting targets presented at the attended location (ANOVA F(2,4) = 25.5; p=0.0003). Notably, the effect of attention did not change with retinal distance."
