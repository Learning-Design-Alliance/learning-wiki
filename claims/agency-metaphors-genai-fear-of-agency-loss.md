## Evidence

### Carmen Vallis 2025

Carmen Vallis, Stephanie Wilson, Alison Casey. (2025). Fear and Awe: Making Sense of Generative AI Through Metaphor. Journal of Interactive Media Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/jime.972

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of workshop metaphors. Beneath the dread was fear of GenAI as a "job rival" and "competitor"; replacement metaphors reflected sociocultural anxiety about professional obsolescence, alongside awe at superhuman qualities like operating 24/7 and never tiring.

> "GenAI was often attributed agency and volition, with a sense of dread. Participants conceptualised agency as finite or limited, a zero-sum resource, with GenAI "stealing" and "taking agency" from teachers and students by being a crutch and laziness promoter."
