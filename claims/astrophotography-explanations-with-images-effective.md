### Simone Iovenitti 2021

Simone Iovenitti, Chiara Righi, Stefano Orsenigo and Riccardo Sgarro. (2021). Astro-photography as an effective tool for Outreach and Education: IACT in exposition. 37th International Cosmic Ray Conference (ICRC 2021). [doi:10.22323/1.395.1380](https://doi.org/10.22323/1.395.1380)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Authors' conclusion based on visitor feedback and observed engagement, not a controlled comparison. They report positive comments at the venue and on social media, and that teachers said students continued to elaborate on the topics.

> "accurate scientiﬁc explanations are more attractive to the general public, when conveyed with astonishing astronomical images. For this reason, we can say that astrophotography, as we adopted it, is deﬁnitely validated as a very eﬀective outreach and educational tool."
