Cress 2018¶

Cress, U., Stahl, G., Rose, C., Law, N., & Ludvigsen, S. (2018). Forming social systems by coupling minds at different levels of cognition: Design, tools, and research methods. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 13, 235–240. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9284-z

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Editorial summary of Matuk and Linn's study of middle school students using the idea manager in WISE, where students sort, share, and copy ideas between private and shared baskets. The relationship between idea sources and "the quality of students' scientific explanations" was, as the authors note, perhaps surprising in direction.

"Students who collected more ideas that were not already represented in their private idea collections tended to write poorer explanations, whereas students who generated their own redundant ideas tended to write better explanations."
