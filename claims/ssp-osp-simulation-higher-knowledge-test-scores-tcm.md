# SSP-TCM and OSP-TCM simulation training both produced higher systematic knowledge test scores than traditional didactic training among TCM undergraduates

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Trainees in the SSP-TCM and OSP-TCM groups scored significantly higher than TM trainees on the 50-item systematic knowledge test in each of 2018, 2019, and 2020, with no significant difference between the two SP groups. [→ Zeng Jinhao 2023](#zeng-jinhao-2023)

## Evidence

### Zeng Jinhao 2023

Zeng Jinhao, Liang Shuang, Fu Xiaoxu, Guo Jing, Bai Yaolin, Zhou Shan, Du Quanyu, Wang Zhenxing, Zhang Xiyu, Peng Sihan, Wen Lijuan, Li Wenyuan, Li Bin, Yang Han, Zhang Yi. (2023). Student standardized patients versus occupational standardized patients for improving clinical competency among TCM medical students: a 3-year prospective randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04198-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three-group randomized trial (147 fourth-year TCM undergraduates across 2018–2020) with a 50-item online knowledge test. One-way ANOVA showed group differences (F = 4.484/3.338/3.386), and post-hoc tests showed "a statistically significant difference in the overall grading between the SSP-TCM and TM groups" and between OSP-TCM and TM groups; SSP vs OSP differences were not significant (all Pc > 0.05).

> "The results of one-way ANOVA for comparing the differences among the three groups (2018/19/20, F = 4.484/3.338/3.386, P = 0.017/0.044/0.042), along with the post-hoc analysis suggested a statistically significant difference in the overall grading between the SSP-TCM and TM groups"
