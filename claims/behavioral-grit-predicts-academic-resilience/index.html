Evidence¶

Chen 2024¶

Chen, D. L., Ertac, S., Evgeniou, T., Miao, X., Nadaf, A., & Yilmaz, E. (2024). Grit and academic resilience during the COVID-19 pandemic. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00265-3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Non-parametric binned scatterplots (Figs. 2 and 3) of change in overall test scores against pre-Covid behavioral grit show a significant predictive association for both subjects, and the authors report the pattern indicates the correlation's direction and strength.

"A signiﬁcant predictive association for behavioral grit can be seen in binned scatterplots in Fig. 2 (math) and Fig. 3 (science), which condense the information from a scatterplot by partitioning the x‐axis into bins and calculating the mean of y within each bin."
