## Evidence

### Lin 2025

Lin, T., Aafjes‐van Doorn, K., Heckman, T. G., Antebi‐Lerman, E., & Anderson, T. (2025). Are Therapists Less Skilful in Teletherapy Than In‐Person Therapy Scenarios? A Latent Profile Analysis of Facilitative Interpersonal Skills. Clinical Psychology & Psychotherapy. https://doi.org/10.1002/cpp.70047

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA and chi-square predictor analyses in the same 223-participant study; the latent profiles were also not associated with years of practice, licensure status or hours of delivering teletherapy. No effect sizes were printed, so the null is reported without one.

> "No differences were found across the three subgroups on age, gender, clinical setting, theoretical orientation, acceptance for teletherapy and professional self‐doubt (all p > 0.05)."
