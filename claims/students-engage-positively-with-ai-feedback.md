`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Thematic analysis of think-aloud sessions and observations during the AI Helper implementation at a rural Alaska middle school. Observers and teachers reported that students "appreciated the ability to engage directly with the AI tool," with one student conversing with the AI for further explanation.

> "students we observed appreciated the ability to engage directly with the AI tool. Teacher Three noted, “[Student Three] used the conversational box beneath the criteria feedback and found that particularly helpful."
