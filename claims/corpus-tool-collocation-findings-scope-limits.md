### Kartal and Yangineksi 2018

Galip Kartal, Gonca Yangin Eksi. (2018). The Effects of Using Corpus Tools on EFL Student Teachers' Learning and Production of Verb-Noun Collocations. PASAA, Vol. 55. https://so04.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/pasaa

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' stated limitations: the study was conducted in one setting (an ELT department of a state university in Turkey) with a limited number of participants; participants' computer and Internet capabilities might have affected ease of corpus use; only verb-noun collocations were studied.

> "this study dealt with only verb-noun collocations. The results may not apply to other types of collocations (e.g. adjective-noun, noun-noun, and verb-adverb collocations)."
