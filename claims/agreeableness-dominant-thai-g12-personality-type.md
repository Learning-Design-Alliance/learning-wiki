Kaoropthai Chayaporn, Dryver Arthur. (2025). A multivariate analysis of the relationships among the Big Five personality traits, activity-oriented learning styles, and academic performance of Grade 12 students in Thailand. BMC Psychology, 13, 1074. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03387-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of IPIP Big Five responses from 1,109 of 1,358 Grade 12 students in three Thai government secondary schools, reported in Results Table 4. The distribution shows "personality type A (Agreeableness) at 59.7%" as the dominant type; no effect size is printed.

> "Table 4 on personality types reveals that the highest percent was personality type A (Agreeableness) at 59.7%, followed by C (Conscientiousness) at 12.2%, N (Neuroticism) at 10.3%, Ties (Multimodal) at 10.2%, O (Openness) at 4.8%, and finally E (Extraversion) at 2.9%."
