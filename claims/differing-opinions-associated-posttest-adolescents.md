### Howe 2017

Howe, Christine; Zachariou, Antonia. (2017). Small-group collaboration and individual knowledge acquisition: The processes of growth during adolescence and early adulthood. Learning and Instruction. [doi:10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.10.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.10.007)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The reported study of 46 pairs of students aged 10 to 22 working on a computer-presented trajectories task found that "Associations between group dialogue and post-test performance confirmed a key role for differing opinions". No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Associations between group dialogue and post-test performance confirmed a key role for differing opinions while collaborative constructions turned out to have little relevance."
