## Evidence

### Drost RMWA 2016

Drost RMWA, Paulus ATG, Jander AF, Mercken L, de Vries H, Ruwaard D, Evers SMAA. (2016). A Web-Based Computer-Tailored Alcohol Prevention Program for Adolescents: Cost-Effectiveness and Intersectoral Costs and Benefits. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.5223

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Base-case cost-effectiveness analysis of the Alcoholic Alert cluster RCT (n=690 analyzed at follow-up: 387 intervention, 303 control), using nonparametric bootstraps with 5000 simulated replications. The article reports "€40 and €79 from the health care perspective, and €62 and €144 from the societal perspective" per unit effect.

> "ICERs differed for both perspectives, namely €40 and €79 from the health care perspective, and €62 and €144 from the societal perspective per incremental reduction of one glass of alcohol per week and binge drinking occasion per 30 days, respectively."
