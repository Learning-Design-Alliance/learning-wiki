Evidence¶

Ramyar O. Qader and Fadime Yalcin Arslan 2019¶

Ramyar O. Qader and Fadime Yalcin Arslan. (2019). THE EFFECT OF FLIPPED CLASSROOM INSTRUCTION IN WRITING: A CASE STUDY WITH IRAQI EFL LEARNERS. Teaching English with Technology, 19(1), 36-55. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1204642

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Questionnaire (ten three-point Likert items, previously used by Mireille 2014) administered to the 34 experimental-group students after the program; percentages were calculated per item, e.g. "about 68% of students believed that FCI allowed them to prepare for their class in adva nce" and almost 71% reported better writing strategies with more class application time.

"when responding to Item 1, about 68% of students believed that FCI allowed them to prepare for their class in adva nce"

Ramyar O. Qader and Fadime Yalcin Arslan 2019 (2)¶

Ramyar O. Qader and Fadime Yalcin Arslan. (2019). THE EFFECT OF FLIPPED CLASSROOM INSTRUCTION IN WRITING: A CASE STUDY WITH IRAQI EFL LEARNERS. Teaching English with Technology, 19(1), 36-55. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1204642

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Semi-structured interviews with 10 experimental-group volunteers, analysed by inductive content analysis; themes included providing easily accessible learning (F=8), being interesting and motivating (F=6), and students describing themselves as active, motivated, and self-independent. These are self-reported perceptions, not tested outcomes.

"53% of students rejected the view that FCI had not helped them at all , which means that more than half of students' writings had been improved due to FCI"
