Binotti Marco, Genoni Giulia, Rizzollo Stefano, De Luca Marco, Carenzo Luca, Monzani Alice, Ingrassia Pier Luigi. (2019). Simulation-based medical training for paediatric residents in Italy: a nationwide survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1581-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 1 reports technical skills' teaching methods by the 274 respondents, showing SBMT, bedside, other methods and not taught/not evaluated percentages for ten procedures; e.g. difficult airway management was not taught or not evaluated for 67% (183).

> "Compared to bedside practice and other teaching methodologies like frontal lessons, SBMT was the preferred method used to teach chest compressions (45%), cardioversion/defibrillation (36%), endotracheal intubation (33%), difficult airway management (21%) and intraosseous access (30%)."
