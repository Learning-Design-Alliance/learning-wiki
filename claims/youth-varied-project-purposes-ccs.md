### Erin Bridges Bird · Heidi L. Ballard · Margaret Harte 2023

Erin Bridges Bird · Heidi L. Ballard · Margaret Harte. (2023). Data to decision-making: how elementary students use their Community and Citizen Science project to reimagine their school campus. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09612-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Ethnographic case study of ten focal student-ornithologists in an after-school bird-monitoring CCS club, drawing on interviews and field observations (Findings, project purpose). The analysis found layered, socially and ecologically framed purposes; the article adds that "all youth expressed interest in and action towards the project purpose of helping the birds."

> "Each student spoke about and acted on at least a couple of their collective project purposes, which they saw as a means to help birds, attract birds, conduct more research, teach others, and learn more about birds and plants."
