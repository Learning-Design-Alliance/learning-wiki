`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group data from the Round 1 pilot revealed scoring opacity: one participant noted "The points deducted for hints or time loss should be clear at every stage." Another requested feedback explaining why answers were wrong.

> "During the pilot application, students were unaware that a scoring system was in place or that they would lose points if they used hints in the game. They thought they were merely racing against time."
