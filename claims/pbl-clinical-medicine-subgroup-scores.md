## Evidence

### Gao Junwei 2020

Gao Junwei, Yang Ling, Zhao Jinghui, Wang Lian, Zou Jiao, Wang Chunxiang, Fan Xiaotang. (2020). Comparison of problem-based learning and traditional teaching methods in medical psychology education in China: A systematic review and meta-analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0243897

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup meta-analysis of six clinical medicine studies (327 cases, 327 controls) under a random effect model. The subgroup estimate was "Hedges’ g = 2.086", significantly favoring PBL, though heterogeneity was not obviously decreased.

> "The total examination scores of clinical medicine students were significantly higher in the PBL group than in the control group (Hedges’ g = 2.086, 95% CI 0.997–3.174, p<0.001)"
