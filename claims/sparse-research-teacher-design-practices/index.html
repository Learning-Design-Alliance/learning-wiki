Evidence¶

Peter Goodyear 2015¶

Peter Goodyear. (2015). Teaching as design. HERDSA Review of Higher Education. https://www.herdsa.org.au/herdsa-review-higher-education-vol-2/27-50

q2 · i? · review · r?

Narrative review of the design praxiology literature, citing Ertmer, Parisio and Wardak (2013) as a recent overview and studies comparing university teachers with professional educational designers.

"Within this pool of empirical material, there is actually very little work that provides a close look at how higher education teachers engage in design processes, particularly when they are doing so in their habitual ways, rather than for the purposes of an experiment."

Peter Goodyear 2015 (2)¶

Peter Goodyear. (2015). Teaching as design. HERDSA Review of Higher Education. https://www.herdsa.org.au/herdsa-review-higher-education-vol-2/27-50

q2 · i? · review · r?

The review reports, citing Ellis et al (2009) and Bennett et al (2015), that interview-based studies of teachers' design activities align with earlier course-planning research; it notes these studies are subject to the usual restrictions of first-person accounts.

"thoughts are focussed by teachers' beliefs about their students, what their students will and won't enjoy, and by pragmatic constraints of the teaching and learning environment"
