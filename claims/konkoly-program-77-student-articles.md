### László Molnár 2023

László Molnár, László L. Kiss, and Róbert Szabó. (2023). Educated towards research: the first five years of the undergraduate mentoring program at Konkoly Observatory. [doi:10.1556/2065.184.2023.1.12](https://doi.org/10.1556/2065.184.2023.1.12)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective publication audit by the program organizers of articles listing program students as authors, counting articles published since the demonstrator application through two years after completion. The audit found "a total of 77 articles" with 708 citations, equating to almost two papers and 16 citations per student, though the distribution is far from even.

> "We identiﬁed a total of 77 articles, 63 of which were published in peer-reviewed journals, with the remaining 12 being conference proceedings and other smaller publications. These received a total of 708 citations by the time of writing this manuscript"
