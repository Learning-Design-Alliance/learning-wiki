## Evidence

### Usichenko Taras 2020

Usichenko Taras, Wenzel Anna, Klausenitz Catharina, Petersmann Astrid, Hesse Thomas, Neumann Nicola, Hahnenkamp Klaus. (2020). Auricular stimulation vs. expressive writing for exam anxiety in medical students – A randomized crossover investigation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238307

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired comparisons from the crossover trial show state anxiety "was higher than at baseline after both expressive writing" with "MD = 5.0; 95% CI 2.1, 8.2; t36 = 3.4; P = 0.003; d = 0.5" and likewise after NI, i.e. EW did not lower anxiety.

> "State anxiety on the day of exam immediately before exam (Time III) was higher than at baseline after both expressive writing: MD = 5.0; 95% CI 2.1, 8.2; t36 = 3.4; P = 0.003; d = 0.5 and after no intervention condition: MD = 4.0; 95% CI 2.4, 6.8; t32 = 4.3; P < 0.001; d = 0.5"
