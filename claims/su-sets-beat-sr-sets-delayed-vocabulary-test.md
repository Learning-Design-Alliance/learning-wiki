## Evidence

### Wang 2015

Wang, X. (2015). The Impacts of Presenting New Words in Semantically-Related Sets on Vocabulary Learning. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(6). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.6p.111

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with two intact Grade-2 classes (19 per group) taught the same 54 GRE words in four 20-minute lessons; SR group received words grouped by semantic set, SU group received the same words spread across lessons. Delayed L2-L1 translation test showed "significant difference (t=-2.381, p=.023<.05)".

> "The results of the LT test show that the mean score of SR group (m=14.2632, std=9.35618) is lower than that of SU group (m=22.5789, std=12.00609) and significant difference (t=-2.381, p=.023<.05) is found between the two groups."
