## Evidence

### Kvintová Jana 2025

Kvintová Jana, Lacková Lucia, Liu Hongyang, Váchová Lucie, Potmesil Milon, Růžičková Veronika, Kopecký Kamil, Regec Vojtech. (2025). Resilience displays similar associative patterns with academic achievement regardless of the personality and mental health profile of future teachers. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03697-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-sample multiple regression in the same 793-student cross-sectional survey, with the AAQ social adaptation subscale as dependent variable. The model explained 30% of variance; PACSR had the most pronounced effect (R2 = 0.10, p < 0.001), and Control and PCHST also contributed significantly.

> "The regression model for social adaptation was the strongest among the three subscales, achieving significance (F[5, 787] = 69.95, p < 0.001) and explaining 30% of the variance (adjusted R2 = 0.30). Positive Acceptance of Change and Secure Relationships (PACSR) had the most pronounced effect (R2 = 0.10, p < 0.001)"
