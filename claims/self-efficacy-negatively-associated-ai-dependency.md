### Huang Q 2026

Huang Q, Tu S, Lin J, Lu L and Lv C (2026). The dark side of AI in education: AI dependency as a mediator linking academic self-efficacy and teacher support to learning burnout among university students. Front. Psychol. 17:1889053. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1889053

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Regression Model 1 in a cross-sectional online questionnaire study of 276 university students in Chengdu, China. Academic self-efficacy was "signiﬁcantly and negatively associated with AI dependency" with β = −0.424, R2 = 0.180, f 2 = 0.220.

> "Academic self-eﬃcacy was signiﬁcantly and negatively associated with AI dependency ( β = −0.424, p < 0.001, 95% CI [ −0.603, −0.359]), explaining 18.0% of the variance in AI dependency ( R2 = 0.180, f 2 = 0.220)."
