q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in a position paper: the authors state the vision should not be read as a rejection of GenAI/LLM technology, listing engaging language ability, democratization, and semantic-sensor concept fusion as properties serving the vision. They pose asking what is the smallest LLM that can fulfill the functions needed, likely a locally-run small open model.

"its ability for language is inherently engaging (which could help overcome hurdles like the friction of reflection and deeper learning), and it can be used as a force for democratization"
