## Subclaims

## Evidence

### van Leest 2021

van Leest, A., Hornstra, L., van Tartwijk, J., & van de Pol, J. (2021). Test‐ or judgement‐based school track recommendations: Equal opportunities for students with different socio‐economic backgrounds? The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 91(1), 193–216. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12356

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

School‐level mediation models showed significant positive indirect SES effects through prior performance for test‐based recommendations via reading comprehension and the school leavers' test.

> "the multilevel mediation models presented significant positive indirect effects (path ab) of students' SES through students' prior performance on test‐based (reading comprehension: b = .09, p = .044; school leavers' test: b = .37, p < .001)"

### van Leest 2021 (2)

van Leest, A., Hornstra, L., van Tartwijk, J., & van de Pol, J. (2021). Test‐ or judgement‐based school track recommendations: Equal opportunities for students with different socio‐economic backgrounds? The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 91(1), 193–216. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12356

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same school‐level models showed indirect effects for judgement‐based recommendations via reading comprehension and mathematics, while the test‐based mathematics indirect effect 'was not statistically significant (b = .00, p = .885)'.

> "and judgement‐based recommendations (reading comprehension: b = .26, p < .001; mathematics: b = .20, p < .001). For test‐based recommendations, the indirect effect of students' prior mathematics performance was not statistically significant (b = .00, p = .885)."
