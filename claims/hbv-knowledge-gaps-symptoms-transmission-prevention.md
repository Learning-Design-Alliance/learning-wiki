## Evidence

### Chao Jonathan 2010

Chao Jonathan, Chang Ellen T, So Samuel KS. (2010). Hepatitis B and liver cancer knowledge and practices among healthcare and public health professionals in China: a cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-10-98

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey results (Table 2) from the 250-respondent questionnaire; the printed item percentages show 33.7% incorrect/missing on symptoms and 28.9% on complications. The article reports that "34% of respondents did not know that chronic HBV infection does not usually have symptoms".

> "For example, 34% of respondents did not know that chronic HBV infection does not usually have symptoms and 29% did not know that chronic HBV infection can lead to liver cirrhosis, liver cancer, and premature death."

### Chao Jonathan 2010 (2)

Chao Jonathan, Chang Ellen T, So Samuel KS. (2010). Hepatitis B and liver cancer knowledge and practices among healthcare and public health professionals in China: a cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-10-98

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Knowledge-section results from the same survey; the modes-of-transmission item showed 34.1% incorrect/missing. The article also reports 30% did not know vaccination and avoiding needle reuse are among the best prevention methods.

> "Thirty-four percent were unaware that contaminated blood, contaminated needles, unprotected sex with an infected person, and birth to an infected mother are all modes of HBV transmission."
