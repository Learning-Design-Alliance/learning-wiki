The authors' experiential impression from years of running Bonn Physikshows for general audiences and school classes; no survey data or statistics are printed. They add that such adults "often suspect that advanced secondary school physics might be a prerequisite and they don't come".

> "our impression is that adults with little or no background in physics feel more welcome to a show which is advertised for kids 10 years and older, than 15 years and older."
