# Indiana's FAFSA requirement coincided with a smaller completion decline (-2.9%) than the national average (-10%) during the chaotic 2024-25 FAFSA rollout

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sayda Martinez-Alvarado 2025

Sayda Martinez-Alvarado. (2025). A Scan of State Universal FAFSA Policies. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED672497

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

EdTrust's analysis of Indiana public high school FAFSA rates for the class of 2024, the first cohort under Senate Bill 167: "only a 2.9% decrease in FAFSA completions compared to the national average decrease of 10%." The report frames this as a possible, not established, explanation.

> "Indiana's high school FAFSA requirement could explain why the state experienced only a 2.9% decrease in FAFSA completions compared to the national average decrease of 10% by July 31."
