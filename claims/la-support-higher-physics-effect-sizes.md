# Physics courses supported by Learning Assistants show course-level Cohen's d effect sizes on average 1.4 times higher than courses without LA support

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The presence of LAs in physics courses is associated with significantly higher mean course effect sizes than courses without LAs. [→ Jada-Simone S. White 2016](#jada-simone-s-white-2016)

## Evidence

### Jada-Simone S. White 2016

Jada-Simone S. White, Ben Van Dusen, & Edward A. Roualdes. (2016). The Impacts of Learning Assistants on Student Learning of Physics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.091](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.091)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Welch two-sample t-test comparing course mean Cohen's d effect sizes from paired pre/post concept inventory data (3,740 usable pairs, 69 courses, 17 institutions): 18 no-LA courses vs 51 LA-supported courses. The article reports "1.4 times higher" with t28.03=-2.7125, p=0.01; no standardized effect size for the group contrast is printed.

> "On average, the effect size of courses supported by LAs was 1.4 times higher than the effect sizes of courses lacking LA support (Fig. 1: t-test, t28.03=-2.7125, p=0.01; Levene's Test: F1,67=7e-4, p=0.98)."
