The review reports Santos et al.'s 2026 FSE Companion study, finding disallowed-use descriptions clustered around routine coursework, "with time pressure and unclear expectations playing an important role", supporting integrity as partly a design problem.

"students’ descriptions of inappropriate or disallowed LLM use clustered around programming assignments, routine coursework, and documentation, with time pressure and unclear expectations playing an important role."

Qusay H. Mahmoud 2026 (2)¶

Qusay H. Mahmoud. (2026). Judgment-Centred Software Engineering Education: A Post-Hype Review and Framework for AI-Augmented Learning. Preprint, September 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29473

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review cites cross-course and cross-national evidence that interpretations of AI use and rules vary across courses and between students and educators, making detection an unstable organizing strategy for assessment.

"Students and educators differ in how they interpret AI use and its impact on assessment [Zastudil et al., 2023, Kizilcec et al., 2024], and students encounter different rules across courses [Keuning et al., 2024, Yabaku and Ouhbi, 2024]."
