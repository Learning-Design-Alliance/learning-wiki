The report's interdistrict choice section cites a recent review of metropolitan desegregation choice plans, including Massachusetts METCO and a similar Minnesota program; an evaluation of the Minnesota program found positive achievement outcomes for both transfer groups compared to non-participants.

"A recent review of research on these programs found that they not only decrease segregation, they also help close racial achievement gaps, while improving racial attitudes, especially among Whites, and leading to long-term mobility and further education for the students of color who participate."
