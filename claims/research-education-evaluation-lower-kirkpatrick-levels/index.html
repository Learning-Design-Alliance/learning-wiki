Evidence¶

King 2022¶

King, O., West, E., Lee, S., Glenister, K., Quilliam, C., Wong, S. A., & Beks, H. (2022). Research education and training for nurses and allied health professionals: a systematic scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03406-7

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of evaluation approaches across included programs, mapping reported outcomes to the modified Kirkpatrick typology. Outcomes were mostly satisfaction, knowledge and confidence, with "few evaluated objectively using traditional research milestones".

"Outcome measures largely reflected lower levels of Barr and colleagues' modified Kirkpatrick educational outcomes typology (e.g., satisfaction, improved research knowledge and confidence), with few evaluated objectively using traditional research milestones (e.g., protocol completion, manuscript preparation, poster, conference presentation)."

King 2022 (2)¶

King, O., West, E., Lee, S., Glenister, K., Quilliam, C., Wong, S. A., & Beks, H. (2022). Research education and training for nurses and allied health professionals: a systematic scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03406-7

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review reports that organisational and practice outcomes were rarely measured and that evaluation methods were "poorly described" across the included program evaluations.

"Few programs were evaluated using organisational and practice outcomes. Overall, evaluation methods were poorly described."
