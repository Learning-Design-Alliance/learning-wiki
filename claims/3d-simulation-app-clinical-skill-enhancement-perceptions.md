`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive Likert results (Q4–Q7) from the same 25-participant post-training questionnaire. "96% of respondents agreed" that 3D training improved skills to inject real patients; 84% agreed on skin condition analysis and 80% on injection treatment planning; 12% disagreed on understanding injection procedures.

> "96% of respondents agreed, with the vast majority (80%) strongly agreeing with the assertion that the 3D training positively improved their skills to inject real patients. However, 4% disagreed with this statement"
