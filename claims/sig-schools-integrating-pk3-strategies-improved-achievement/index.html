Evidence¶

Connors-Tadros 2015¶

Connors-Tadros, L., Dunn, L., Martella, J., & McCauley, C. (2015). Incorporating early learning strategies in the School Improvement Grants (SIG) program: How three schools integrated early childhood strategies into school turnaround efforts to improve instruction for all students. New Brunswick, NJ: National Institute for Early Education Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562080

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Cross-case synthesis of three case studies of SIG Cohort 1 elementary schools selected for growth in preschool populations and improved achievement data, based on literature and data reviews plus interviews with school, district, and state leaders. The authors state "the improved student achievement in each of these schools speaks to the promise" of aligned early learning strategies.

"However, the improved student achievement in each of these schools speaks to the promise of implementing aligned early learning strategies as part of an overall school turnaround plan."
