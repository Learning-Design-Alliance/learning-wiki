## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Task D (involvement load 4) yielded significantly lower gains than Tasks A–C, and Task A did not differ from B or C. [→ Snoder 2017](#snoder-2017)

## Evidence

### Snoder 2017

Snoder, P. (2017). Improving English Learners’ Productive Collocation Knowledge: The Effects of Involvement Load, Spacing, and Intentionality. TESL Canada Journal, 34(3). https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1277

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA with LSD post-hoc comparisons of four tasks (N = 59) in a within-subjects classroom experiment. Task D vs A–C differences significant (p = .006, .000, .014); Tasks A–C did not differ pairwise. Quote fragment: "significantly lower learning gains".

> "the task with the highest involvement load generated significantly lower learning gains than the three other tasks with relatively lower involvement loads, which runs counter to the predictions of the ILH"
