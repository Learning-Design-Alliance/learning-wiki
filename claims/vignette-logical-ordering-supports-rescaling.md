Coenen Johan, Golsteyn Bart H. H., Stolp Tom, Tempelaar Dirk. (2021). Personality traits and academic performance: Correcting self-assessed traits with vignettes. PLoS ONE 16(3): e0248629. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248629

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of vignette ordering in the survey of first-year Dutch economics students (Table 1: Risk Preference 94.4% set A and 93.4% set B; Emotional Stability only 56.65% completely correct). The article reports the rescaling technique uses the ordering of answers relative to vignettes.

> "Using the strictest definition of logical order, 80.6% of the respondents ordered the vignettes for the Conscientiousness item "Always prepared" in a logical way."
