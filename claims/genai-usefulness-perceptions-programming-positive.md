## Evidence

### Kohen-Vacs 2025

Kohen-Vacs, D., Usher, M., & Jansen, M. (2025). Integrating Generative AI into Programming Education: Student Perceptions and the Challenge of Correcting AI Errors. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00496-4

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

One-sample t-tests against the neutral midpoint (3) from an online questionnaire completed in March 2024 by 64 Israeli undergraduates; the study found perceptions "significantly positive, exceeding the neutral midpoint" with d = 0.87; overall usefulness mean was 3.63 (SD = 0.87).

> "Results indicated that students perceptions of GenAI's usefulness were significantly positive, exceeding the neutral midpoint (t(63) = 5.76, p <.001, d = 0.87)"

### Kohen-Vacs 2025 (2)

Kohen-Vacs, D., Usher, M., & Jansen, M. (2025). Integrating Generative AI into Programming Education: Student Perceptions and the Challenge of Correcting AI Errors. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00496-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive statistics from the same questionnaire: the highest-rated item was "GenAI enhances my efficiency in learning programming" (M = 3.88, SD = 1.03) and the lowest was "GenAI enhances the effectiveness of my learning process" (M = 3.27, SD = 1.10), indicating uncertainty about learning quality beyond efficiency.

> "As shown in Table 2, students reported generally favorable perceptions of GenAI's usefulness in programming education, with an overall mean score of 3.63 (SD = 0.87)"
