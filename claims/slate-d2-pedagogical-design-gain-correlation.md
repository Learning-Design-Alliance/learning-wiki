Spearman rank correlations across the 10 evaluated generation systems (Figure 5), relating multi-agent D1/D2 judge scores to D4 gain. The article reports "ρ= 0.72, p= 0.019" for D2 versus "ρ= 0.38, p= 0.28" for D1.

> "Within the evaluated systems, D2 (pedagogical design) shows a stronger positive association with gain (ρ= 0.72, p= 0.019) than D1 (knowledge validity;ρ= 0.38, p= 0.28 )."
