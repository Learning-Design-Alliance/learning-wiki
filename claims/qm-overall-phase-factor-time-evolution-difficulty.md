## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2016

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2016). Review of Student Difficulties in Upper-Level Quantum Mechanics. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020117)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Study reviewed from seven universities (Ref. [24]): students were given the initial superposition √2/7φ1(x) + √5/7φ2(x) for an electron in a one-dimensional infinite square well and asked to find Ψ(x,t). Interviews indicated difficulty differentiating time-dependence of stationary and non-stationary states.

> "Instead of the correct response, Ψ(x,t) = √2/7φ1(x)e−iE1t/¯h + √5/7φ2(x)e−iE2t/¯h, in which the ground state wave function is φ1(x) and the first excited state wavefunction is φ2(x), approximately one-third of students wrote common phase factors for both terms"
