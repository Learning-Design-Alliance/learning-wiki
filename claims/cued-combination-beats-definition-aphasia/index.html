Wells 1917¶

Wells, F. L. (1917). Aphasic performance in the Terman vocabulary test. Journal of Educational Psychology, 8(8), 483–487. doi:10.1037/h0073107

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Single-case administration on March 14, repeating the complete vocabulary list by a method of "cued combination" in which a short context sentence cues the tested word. The examiner reports the patient "did very much better" and failures were more normally distributed.

"With this method, the patient did very much better, and the failures were more normally distributed."
