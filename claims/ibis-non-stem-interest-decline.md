## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Within each year some students became less interested in learning about biology (p < 0.05 for all years); e.g., in 2014 disagreement with interest in biology rose from 10% pre-course to 24.4% post-course, mostly among non-STEM-intending students. ## Evidence

### Shaw Tarren J. 2019

Shaw Tarren J., Yang Suann, Nash Troy R., Pigg Rachel M., Grim Jeffrey M. (2019). Knowing is half the battle: Assessments of both student perception and performance are necessary to successfully evaluate curricular transformation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210030

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Attitudinal Likert items on the pre/post concept survey tracked interest in biology across four cohorts, visualized with network diagrams of attitude migration. The article reports the 2014 increase from 10% to 24.4% disagreeing, and that very few STEM majors reduced interest.

> "in 2014, 10% of students either Disagreed or Strongly Disagreed with the statement "I am interested in learning more about biology" prior to taking the course. On the post-course survey, this increased to 24.4% (Fig 5)."
