Robinson Katie 2023¶

Robinson Katie, Riley Nicholas, Owen Katherine, Drew Ryan, Mavilidi Myrto F., Hillman Charles H., Faigenbaum Avery D., Garcia-Hermoso Antonio, Lubans David Revalds. (2023). Effects of Resistance Training on Academic Outcomes in School-Aged Youth: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Sports Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-023-01881-6

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analysis of intervention type within the primary meta-analysis (five studies, 20 effect sizes for resistance training; five studies, 33 effect sizes for concurrent training). Printed effect sizes: resistance training SMD = 0.26 (95% CI 0.10–0.42) versus concurrent training SMD = 0.11 (95% CI −0.05 to 0.28); intervention type explained R2 = 0.40 of between-study variance. The concurrent training CI crosses zero.

"Resistance training significantly improved cognition and academic outcomes (SMD 0.26, 95% CI 0.10–0.42), while the result for concurrent training was less effective (SMD 0.11, 95% CI − 0.05 to 0.28)."
