## Evidence

### Odd David 2016

Odd David, Evans David, Emond Alan. (2016). Preterm Birth, Age at School Entry and Long Term Educational Achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0155157

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sensitivity analysis of the ALSPAC cohort splitting exposure into very preterm (<32 weeks), moderate preterm (32–36 weeks) and term, using the conditional regression models. The fully adjusted odds ratio for very preterm infants was "OR 1.84 (1.20–2.96)" in the initial analysis, attenuating to OR 1.63 (0.95–2.78) when restricted to the same year of schooling.

> "Very preterm infants had increased risk of a low KS4 score (fully adjusted OR 1.84 (1.20–2.96)) in the initial analysis, which persisted in the EDD matched model but attenuated substantially when restricting to the same year of schooling (fully adjusted OR 1.63 (0.95–2.78))."
