### Kevin Coldren and Diana Sachmpazidi 2026

Kevin Coldren and Diana Sachmpazidi. (2026). Data-Driven by Design: Building a Reflective Physics Graduate Program. [doi:10.1103/g497-h3zw](https://doi.org/10.1103/g497-h3zw)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group theme on required coursework. A participant complained "noneof them are related to what I'm doing" and that core courses resemble "a slight upgrade from undergrad courses." Students also valued the flexibility of the four elective courses, which aligned better with research interests.

> "You have to pick four out of five courses, which noneof them are related to what I'm doing, and all of them are like a slight upgrade from undergrad courses."
