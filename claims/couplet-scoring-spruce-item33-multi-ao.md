## Evidence

### Michael Vignal 2023

Michael Vignal, Gayle Geschwind, Marcos D. Caballero, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2023). Overview of couplet scoring in content-focused physics assessments. arXiv:2307.03099. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.03099](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.03099)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Design analysis of SPRUCE item 3.3, a multiple-choice item on the period of a mass on a spring, scored as two couplets (H1: propagate uncertainties using formulas; H2: report with correct significant digits). The authors argue proper significant-digit reporting requires coordinating with the uncertainty response, so splitting the item would not meaningfully measure H2. This is a design argument, not an empirical comparison; no effect size is reported.

> "In these ways, couplet scoring allows these two AOs to be targeted in ways that two separate, traditionally scored items could not."
