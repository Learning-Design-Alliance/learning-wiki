## Evidence

### Punch 2011

Punch, R., & Hyde, M. B. (2011). Communication, Psychosocial, and Educational Outcomes of Children with Cochlear Implants and Challenges Remaining for Professionals and Parents. International Journal of Otolaryngology. https://doi.org/10.1155/2011/573280

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey comparison of parents' retrospective preimplant expectations with current experiences across five outcome domains (communication, social, wellbeing, academic, future life) in a study of 247 parents. "approximately four fifths of the parents indicated that their expectations had been met", while a tenth reported unmet expectations.

> "In most outcome domains, there was no statistically significant difference between parents' expectations and subsequent experiences of their children's outcomes with the CI."
