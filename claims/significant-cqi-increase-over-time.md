## Evidence

### Olson Jonathan R. 2020

Olson Jonathan R., Coldiron Jennifer Schurer, Parigoris Ryan M., Zabel Michelle D., Matarese Marlene, Bruns Eric J. (2020). Developing an Evidence-Based Technical Assistance Model: a Process Evaluation of the National Training and Technical Assistance Center for Child, Youth, and Family Mental Health. The Journal of Behavioral Health Services & Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11414-020-09686-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Repeated-measures t tests compared SAIS subscale scores from 18 grantee sites of the 2014 cohort between 2016 baseline and 2018 follow-up (107 respondents, 73% response rate, aggregated by grantee). The article reports "a significant increase in CQI"; only the t statistic is printed.

> "Results of paired samples t tests indicate that grantees reported a significant increase in CQI (operationalized as accountability routines, and data collection and use) over time (t = 2.91, p = 0.01)."

### Olson Jonathan R. 2020 (2)

Olson Jonathan R., Coldiron Jennifer Schurer, Parigoris Ryan M., Zabel Michelle D., Matarese Marlene, Bruns Eric J. (2020). Developing an Evidence-Based Technical Assistance Model: a Process Evaluation of the National Training and Technical Assistance Center for Child, Youth, and Family Mental Health. The Journal of Behavioral Health Services & Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11414-020-09686-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same repeated-measures analyses found the management domain trended toward significance (p = 0.07) and the governance, service delivery, and geographic area subscales remained relatively unchanged. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Furthermore, the management domain increased over time, with the result trending toward but not reaching statistical significance (t = 1.93, p = 0.07). The three other subscales remained relatively unchanged."
