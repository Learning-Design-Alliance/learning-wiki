## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Most experimental students said they learned by having fun because activities were associated with daily life, and large majorities credited group work with stronger solutions and motivation. [→ Yenmez 2017](#yenmez-2017)

## Evidence

### Yenmez 2017

Yenmez, A. A., Özpinar, İ., & Gökçe, S. (2017). Use of WebQuests in Mathematics Instruction: Academic Achievement, Teacher and Student Opinions. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(9). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050913

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Written opinion forms from all experimental-group students plus semi-structured interviews with six students chosen across achievement categories. Percentages printed include 72% learning by having fun, 51% learning from each other, 80% different perspectives strengthening solutions, and 85% increased motivation through mutual support.

> "72% of them stated that they learned by having fun because they associated the activity with daily life. They also mentioned that this environment supported their cooperative learning. They highlighted the group works and reported that the group work was efficient because they learned something from each other (51%)."
