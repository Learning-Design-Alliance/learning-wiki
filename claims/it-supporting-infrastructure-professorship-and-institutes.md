## Evidence

### Pak-Lok Poon 2026

Pak-Lok Poon, Sau-Fun Tang, Santoso Wibowo, & Srimannarayana Grandhi. (2026). Information technology curriculum: General or specialized? An Australia's census study. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Census analysis of regular full professors' research expertise coverage of offered IT majors/specializations (Table 16); overall mean coverage was 44.6% with a range of 0.0-100.0% and standard deviation of 31.6%.

> "the statistics show that Go8 universities have a much higher mean percentage of coverage (72.0%) than non-Go8 universities (37.6%)."

### Pak-Lok Poon 2026 (2)

Pak-Lok Poon, Sau-Fun Tang, Santoso Wibowo, & Srimannarayana Grandhi. (2026). Information technology curriculum: General or specialized? An Australia's census study. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Census of research institutes relevant to MA-degrees or SP-degrees across the 39 offering universities (Table 17); overall mean was 1.23 institutes per university (total 48), with a range [0, 6] and standard deviation of 1.54.

> "the mean number of research institutes per each university of Go8 (=1.54) is larger than non-Go8 (=1.50). In effect size analysis, Cohen's d is 0.54, indicating only a medium difference."
