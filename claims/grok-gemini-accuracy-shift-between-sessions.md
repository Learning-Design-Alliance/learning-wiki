`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Second-round reassessment of the same five models on the identical 150 MCQs ten days later, reported in the Results and Table 1/Fig. 1. The quote reports the per-model shifts; figures are descriptive counts and percentages with no effect size printed.

> "Grok XI improved its performance slightly, answering 139 questions correctly (92.7%), and Gemini showed a modest improvement with 133 correct responses (88.7%). Qwen 2.5 remained consistent with 134 correct answers (89.3%), whereas DeepSeek showed a slight decline, answering 130 questions correctly (86.7%)."
