## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). To Use or Not to Use Diagrams: The Effect of Drawing a Diagram in Solving Introductory Physics Problems. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.05467](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.05467)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of diagram detail on the two-dimensional problem, given in the second semester of the course after instruction involving significant vector addition. Fewer than 30% of students drew the electric force components, which the article calls an indication of a lack of transfer.

> "These students had done problems where they had to find the net force in Newtonian mechanics, and still less than 30% realized that they should draw the components of the electric force in the 2D problem presented here."
