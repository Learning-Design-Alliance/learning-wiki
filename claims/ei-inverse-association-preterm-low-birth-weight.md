## Evidence

### Stingone JA 2026

Stingone JA, McVeigh KH, Lednyak L. (2026). Early Intervention Developmental Programming and Childhood Academic Outcomes. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.55890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Subgroup analyses within the matched LSED cohort for preterm and low-birth-weight strata. The article reports inverse estimates and states "difficulty in identifying comparators suggests caution when interpreting these results"; very low birth weight is an autoeligible condition, and privately accessed interventions would not appear in these records.

> "We observed lower test scores among preterm children (estimate for math, −0.108 [95% CI, −0.158 to −0.057]; estimate for ELA, −0.043 [95% CI, −0.092 to 0.006]) and those with low birth weight (estimate for math, −0.228 [95% CI, −0.331 to −0.125]; estimate for ELA, −0.104 [95% CI, −0.205 to −0.004]) who received EI, although difficulty in identifying comparators suggests caution when interpreting these results."
