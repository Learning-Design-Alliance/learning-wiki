Lacoe 2019¶

Lacoe, J., & Steinberg, M. P. (2019). Do Suspensions Affect Student Outcomes? Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, vol. 41, issue 1. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/do-suspensions-affect-student-outcomes

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Student panel data analysis from the School District of Philadelphia using student fixed effects and instrumental variable strategies finds that "A suspension decreases math and reading achievement for suspended students." No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"Using student panel data from the School District of Philadelphia, we implement student fixed effects and instrumental variable (IV) strategies to examine the consequences of suspensions for offending students and their peers. A suspension decreases math and reading achievement for suspended students."
