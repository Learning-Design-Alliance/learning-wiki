## Evidence

### Pishghadam 2011

Pishghadam, R., & Ghadiri, S. (2011). Symmetrical or asymmetrical scaffolding: Piagetian vs. Vygotskyan views to reading comprehension. Journal of Language and Literacy Education [Online], 7(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097243

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent t-test within the asymmetrical group (n=28), pre-test mean 25.0357 to post-test mean 30.1786. The article reports "a significant difference between pre and posttests in the AS group (t=9.4, p<.05)"; no effect size is printed.
