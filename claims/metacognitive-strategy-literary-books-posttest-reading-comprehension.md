## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The experimental group's post-test reading comprehension scores were significantly higher than the control group's, with a very large effect (Cohen's d = 3.10, p < .001). [→ Erkan Çer 2016](#erkan-cer-2016)

## Evidence

### Erkan Çer 2016

Erkan Çer, Ertuğrul Şahin. (2016). Improving Reading Comprehension Skills with Children's Books through Metacognitive Strategy: The Turkish Context. Journal of Education and Training Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i9.1566

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample t-tests on pre- and post-test reading comprehension scores in a quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control group study of 65 fifth-graders (35 experimental, 30 control). The post-test contrast favored the experimental group: "t(63): 13.89, Cohen's d: 3.10, p<.001".
