Evidence¶

tzirides¶

Jacob, S., Miceli, H., & Schneider, H. (2026, May). Landscape of emerging technologies in higher education: A review. Digital Promise & The American Association of Colleges & Universities. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/293

q2 · i? · review · r?

The review reports, citing Tzirides et al. (2024), graduate-level seminars with "hands-on experience with generative AI" in which students used an AI writing reviewer and image generator; participants "showed greater ability to assess AI in a critical manner" after using and evaluating AI tools in a course. No effect size is printed.

"This design helped students learn through direct interaction and reflection, resulting in increased comfort with AI and improved ability to critically assess AI outputs by course end."
