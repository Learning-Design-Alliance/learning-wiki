He Peipei, Yin Xiaohong, Xu Ying, Yang Tingjun. (2026). The construction and implementation of a clinical nursing experts-oriented retraining program for peritoneal dialysis specialist nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09379-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Rate of advancement from peritoneal dialysis SN to clinical nurse specialist calculated among 36 trained nurses using the formula: Number of nurses meeting the progression criteria / Total number of trained nurses. The progression criteria pilot test involved 23 SNs with Cronbach's α coefficient of 0.749.

> "As of September 2025, the proportion of peritoneal dialysis SNs progressing to the level of clinical nurse specialist reached 88.89%, representing a substantial increase compared with the baseline rate of 2.78% before retraining (P < 0.001)."
