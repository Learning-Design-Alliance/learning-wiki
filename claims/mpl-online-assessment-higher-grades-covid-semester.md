## Evidence

### Khandakar 2021

Khandakar, A., Chowdhury, M. E. H., Khalid, M. S., and Zorba, N. (2021). Case study of Innovative Teaching Practices and their Impact for Electrical Engineering Courses during COVID-19 Pandemic. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2107.00746](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2107.00746)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of letter-grade distributions between the 2019-Pre-COVID semester and the 2020 In-COVID MPL semester of the same course taught by the same instructor (Figure 5). The authors report "A higher number of students got better grades (A, B+, and B)" and no D or F grades; a z-test accepted the hypothesis that A and B+ probability was larger among MPL participants.

> "It can be seen that there has been positive effect of the MPL and online assesment approach on the students’ grades in the ‘In -COVID semester’. A higher number of students got better grades (A, B+, and B)."
