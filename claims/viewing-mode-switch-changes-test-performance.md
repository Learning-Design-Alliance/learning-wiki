# Switching viewing mode between training and testing changes test performance: 2D→3D transfer outperforms 2D-only control, while 3D→2D transfer underperforms 3D-only control

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large
> **Currency** · newest study 2021 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Among 2D-trained groups, the 2D → 3D transfer group performed significantly better at test than the 2D → 2D control group; among 3D-trained groups, the 3D → 2D transfer group performed significantly worse than the 3D → 3D control group (overall η2 = 0.29). [→ Beattie 2021](#beattie-2021)

## Evidence

### Beattie 2021

Beattie, K. L., Hill, A., Horswill, M. S., Grove, P. M., & Stevenson, A. R. L. (2021). Laparoscopic skills training: the effects of viewing mode (2D vs. 3D) on skill acquisition and transfer. Surgical Endoscopy, 35(8), 4332–4344. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-07923-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Testing-phase post hoc comparisons (same training, different testing) on total test performance score: 2D → 3D vs 2D → 2D p = 0.008 and 3D → 2D vs 3D → 3D p = 0.005, both "2D > 3D" in the direction of the test mode advantage; the omnibus effect had η2 = 0.29.

> "Among the groups that trained in 2D, the 2D → 3D transfer group performed significantly better than the 2D → 2D control group during testing. Among those that trained in 3D, the 3D → 2D transfer group performed significantly worse compared to the 3D → 3D control group"
