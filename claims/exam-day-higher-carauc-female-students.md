## Evidence

### Losiak Wladyslaw; Losiak-Pilch Julia 2020

Losiak Wladyslaw; Losiak-Pilch Julia. (2020). Cortisol Awakening Response, Self-Reported Affect and Exam Performance in Female Students. Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10484-019-09449-9

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA in a study of 25 female first-year students comparing exam-day and neutral-day saliva samples. The article reports a significant CARauc difference with "eta-squared = 0.365", a large effect.

> "there was a significant difference in integrated volume of cortisol over the waking period (CARauc – F (1,24) = 13.788, p = .001, eta-squared = 0.365). The integrated volume of cortisol was higher on the day of the exam than on the neutral day."
