## Evidence

### Sun and Wu 2015

Sun and Wu. (2015). Analysis of Learning Achievement and Teacher–Student Interactions in Flipped and Conventional Classrooms. http://www.oeconsortium.org/about-oec/

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semi-structured interviews with the instructor, TAs, and learners from both groups, conducted at the end of the course to supplement the quantitative data. Learner A reported asking the TA in real time to "clarify my misconception and get a deeper understanding of the topics."

> "In contrast, OCW integrated with a conventional classroom allowed the learners to ask their peers for help whenever they encountered any questions; however, their time management was poor, and they tended to cram for the biweekly tests."
