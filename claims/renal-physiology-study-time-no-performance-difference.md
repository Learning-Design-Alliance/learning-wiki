## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among survey respondents, exam scores did not differ significantly between cohorts spending more versus similar or less time on renal physiology (p = 0.69). [→ Heltne 2024](#heltne-2024)

## Evidence

### Heltne 2024

Heltne, Sofie Fagervoll; Hovdenakk, Sigrid; Kvernenes, Monika; Tenstad, Olav. (2024). Study preferences and exam outcomes in medical education: insights from renal physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05964-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of MED4 students (n = 72 respondents) correlating reported study time with exam scores. Cohort means were 69.6% ± 12.4%, 72.2% ± 9.9%, and 73.4% ± 11.0%, with "(p = 0.69)", i.e. no significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "the average exam score for respondents who spent considerably more time on renal physiology was 69.6% ± 12.4%, compared to 72.2% ± 9.9% for those who spent more time, and 73.4% ± 11.0% for those who spent a similar amount or less time studying the subject (p = 0.69)."
