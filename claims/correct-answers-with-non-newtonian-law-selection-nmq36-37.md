# Students who answer an FCI action-reaction item correctly often report using Newton's first law, and reported first-law use falls after instruction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among students correctly answering NMQ 36, sole use of Newton's first law fell from 13.6% pre-test to 7.3% post-test and combined use from 50.2% to 34.2%, while exclusive third-law use rose from 35.0% to 55.3%. [→ Ashutosh Kumar Pathak 2026](#ashutosh-kumar-pathak-2026)
`q2 i?` Correct answers paired with first-law reasoning suggest students may confuse equal-and-opposite reaction pairs with balanced forces on a single object. [→ Ashutosh Kumar Pathak 2026](#ashutosh-kumar-pathak-2026)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Law-selection analysis of Type 2 sub-item NMQ 37 responses among students who correctly answered NMQ 36 (pre-test N = 243, post-test N = 219, University A only), shown as UpSet plots. Percentages are descriptive; no statistical test is reported.

> "35 .0% and 55 .3% of students, in the pre-test and post-test respectively, make exclusive use of Newton’s third law to answer NMQ 36, and 72 .0% and 83 .6% of students, in the pre-test and post-test respectively, make use of Newton’s third law in combinatio n with other reasoning."
