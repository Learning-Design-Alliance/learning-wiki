## Evidence

### Dominik M. Piehlmaier & Dee Warmath 2025

Dominik M. Piehlmaier & Dee Warmath. (2025). The challenge of explicit learning in life skill education. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00375-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian proportion tests on intertemporal choice in the five-week RCT. Strategic thinking's delayed-payment posterior proportion was 0.883 versus 0.647 for financial education; the group difference was ΔpM 23.6% with 95% HPD [4.8%; 42.8%].

> "participants who were randomly allocated to the Strategic Thinking condition substantially out- performed their counterparts in the Financial Education arm (ΔpM: 23.6%; pSD: 9.8%; 95% HPD: [4.8%; 42.8%]; p( ΔpM > 0) > 99%)"
