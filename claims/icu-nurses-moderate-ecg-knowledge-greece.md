`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional web-based survey of 100 ICU nurses in Greece (October 2024–February 2025) using 10 true/false knowledge items; the article reports "The median composite knowledge score was 6.0 (IQR: 2.0) of 10" with Cronbach's α = 0.81. No effect size applies to this descriptive result.
