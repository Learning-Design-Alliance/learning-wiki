Historical argument quoting Piaget's own recollection (in Broughton and Freeman-Moir 1982) that "Pierre Janet, whose courses I took in Paris, cited him [Baldwin] constantly." The paper adds Wozniak's point that Baldwin's works were published concurrently in French and English, so Piaget could not have avoided them.

> "...Pierre Janet, whose courses I took in Paris, cited him [Baldwin] constantly and had been equally very influenced by him ..."
