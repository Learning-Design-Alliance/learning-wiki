## Evidence

### Hossain 2012

Hossain, M.A., & Tarmizi, R.A. (2012). Gender-related effects of group learning on mathematics achievement among the rural secondary students. Egitim Arastirmalari-Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 47, 1-22. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057355

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-test comparing female students across conditions (44 experimental, 41 control); pre-test means were equivalent (25.22 vs 26.46, not significant). The article reports the post-test difference favored "the female students in experimental group."

> "the experimental female students scored a mean of 47.95 (SD = 2.36) which is higher than the conventional female students with post -test mean score of 35.37 (SD = 1.64). The difference between these two post -test means was significant [t (76.71) = 28.68, p < 0.05] in favor of the female students in experimental group"
