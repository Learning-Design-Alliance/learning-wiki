Aggregate construct-level analysis of classified comments from the four-year Copilot activity (Table 1). The article reports the two construct exceptions to What-comment dominance and states this "suggests that when logic involved multiple steps or repeated actions, students shifted from describing intended outcomes to describing the procedure".

"The exceptions wereMulti-Step-Block, where 94.3% of comments were classified asHow,andLoop-Iteration, where 57.3% were classified asHow."
