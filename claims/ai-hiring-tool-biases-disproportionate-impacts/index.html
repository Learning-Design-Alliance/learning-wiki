The brief states, without printed statistics or cited study designs, that AI hiring tools have "demonstrated concerning biases" in posting visibility and application selection and ranking, with named groups bearing disproportionate negative impacts. Transparency and accountability gaps compound these problems.

"Additionally, AI tools used in hiring processes have demonstrated concerning biases, affecting which jobseekers even see job postings and how applications are selected and ranked, with women, individuals with disabilities, and underrepresented ethnic and racial groups bearing disproportionate negative impacts"
