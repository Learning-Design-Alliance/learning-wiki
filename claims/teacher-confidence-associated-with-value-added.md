`q2 i?` In a regression of teacher value-added on teacher survey items, only confidence in implementing the program had a significant positive association with student outcomes, with several other covariates marginally significant. [→ Newman 2016](#newman-2016)

## Evidence

### Newman 2016

Newman, D., Lazarev, V., & Zacamy, J. (2016). Comparing a Program Implemented Under the Constraints of an RCT and in the Wild. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567633

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory linear regression with teacher value-added (teacher fixed effects from an ETS study-administered pretest/posttest) as outcome; Table 2 prints Confidence level estimate 0.223, p = 0.019; model selection maximized adjusted R-squared.

> "Our analysis of the correlates of student outcomes (teacher value added) shows that only teachers' confidence in their ability to implement the program in the classroom has a significant (at 0.05 level) positive association with student outcomes and several others are marginally significant (see Table 2, Appendix B)."
