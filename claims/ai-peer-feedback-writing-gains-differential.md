# AI-peer integrated feedback yields significant gains on all four writing dimensions, with the group × dimension interaction showing differential improvement favoring task achievement and lexical resource over coherence and cohesion

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The group × writing dimension interaction was significant (partial η2 = 0.024), and the mixed-design ANCOVA also showed a significant main effect of group (partial η2 = 0.26), indicating the intervention's effectiveness varied across the four writing criteria. [→ Liu W 2026](#liu-w-2026)

## Evidence

### Liu W 2026

Liu W (2026) AI-peer integrated feedback in second language writing classes: exploring students' engagement and writing performance. Front. Psychol. 17:1871951. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1871951

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

A 2 (group) × 4 (writing dimension) mixed-design ANCOVA with composite pretest writing score as covariate; the omnibus interaction tested whether intervention effectiveness varied across the four IELTS-based writing criteria. The main effect of group was also significant (partial η2 = 0.26).

> "Most importantly, group × writing dimension interaction reached statistical significance (F(2.85, 342.00) = 2.93, p = 0.036, partial η2 = 0.024, 95% CI [0.001, 0.07]), justifying follow-up simple-effect analyses"

### Liu W 2026 (2)

Liu W (2026) AI-peer integrated feedback in second language writing classes: exploring students' engagement and writing performance. Front. Psychol. 17:1871951. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1871951

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Bonferroni-adjusted follow-up simple-effect analyses on the mixed-design ANCOVA. Task achievement and lexical resource gains significantly exceeded coherence and cohesion gains (p = 0.023 and p = 0.031); coherence and cohesion showed "the smallest yet still significant gains".

> "All four dimensions showed significant between-group differences favoring the experimental group, with effect sizes descending as follows: task achievement (d = 1.41, 95% CI [1.04, 1.77]), lexical resource (d = 1.28, 95% CI [0.92, 1.63])"
