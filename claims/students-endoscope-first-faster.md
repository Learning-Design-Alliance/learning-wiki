Anschuetz Lukas, Stricker Daniel, Yacoub Abraam, Wimmer Wilhelm, Caversaccio Marco, Huwendiek Sören. (2019). Acquisition of basic ear surgery skills: a randomized comparison between endoscopic and microscopic techniques. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1803-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Effect of randomization order (endoscope first versus microscope first) on surgical time in the randomized crossover study, reported as a significant interaction between randomization group and training status depicted in Figure 5. The article reports ηp2 = 0.208 for students beginning endoscopically.

> "Students beginning with the endoscopic technique showed an overall significantly lower amount of time to complete the tasks (F2, 27 = 3.538, p = 0.043, ηp2 = 0.208)."
