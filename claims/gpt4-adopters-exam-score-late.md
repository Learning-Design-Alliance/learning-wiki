`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Causal (LATE) estimation with cross-fitting and imputation for missing exam scores in the RCT, using the Bansak (2020) effect-size proposal for compliers. "The estimated LATE is positive, at∆=6.86" with ES=0.40; the ignoring-missingness estimate was smaller (∆=4.49, ES=0.23).

> "The estimated LATE is positive, at∆=6.86. Though the difference is not statistically significant after adjusting for multiple hypothesis testing (BCa 90%CI=[0.30, 14.13], ES=0.40), the estimated effect size is near medium3, at 0.40"

### Allen Nie 2024 (2)

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Hypothesis test comparing exam scores of control students versus experiment-group students who did not use GPT-4: "Cohen’sd=0.03", not significant, suggesting advertisement alone had little effect on scores for non-users.

> "the difference between the two groups is very small and not statistically significant when we ignore missingness (∆=0.54pp, SE=0.81, 90% CI=[-0.79, 1.87], Cohen’sd=0.03, P>.99, Unadjusted P=.504)"
