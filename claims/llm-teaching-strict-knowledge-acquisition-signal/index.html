q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Domain-specific supplementary random-effects meta-analysis of 5 studies restricted to outcomes judged to represent stricter post-course knowledge acquisition. The printed effect size was SMD 0.80 (95% CI 0.07 to 1.53, I² = 88.6%); GRADE-informed certainty was low.

"The strict knowledge acquisition sensitivity analysis included 5 studies (164 vs. 164 participants; Fig. 4) and yielded a pooled effect of SMD 0.80 (95% CI 0.07 to 1.53; I² = 88.6%)."
