The article reports that "In a total of 45 single-unit in-course evaluations, pretest-to-posttest learning gains averaged about 18 percentage points" for the Genetics Tutor across post-secondary and high school levels.

"In a total of 45 single-unit in-course evaluations, pretest-to-posttest learning gains averaged about 18 percentage points (equivalent to almost 2 letter grades) across topics at both the post-secondary and high school levels."
