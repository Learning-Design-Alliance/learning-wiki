CHENG Kai Wen 2011¶

CHENG Kai Wen. (2011). When Creative Problem Solving Strategy Meets Web-Based Cooperative Learning Environment in Accounting Education. New Horizons in Education, 59(1), 106-118. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ955493

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Author's observational interpretation from the learning processes of the two experimental groups, where each member took turns being group leader across the six units. No effect size is reported.

"It also could be discovered from the learning processes of the two experimental groups that group discussion and interactions were deeply affected by the leader. An encouraging attitude of the leader could induce better group discussions."
