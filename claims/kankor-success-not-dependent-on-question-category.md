### Abdul Rahman 2016

Abdul Rahman, Sherzad. (2016). Using Descriptive Analytics for the Improvement of National University Entrance Exam: A case study in the Context of Kankor in Afghanistan. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1612.01378](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1612.01378)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Assessment of the Detailed Kankor Results Data (2004-2006, about 120,000 records) with subject-wise scores. The author states one should get high scores in all categories to be admitted into a favorite institution; no effect size is printed.

> "from another assessment and evaluation done on the DKRD, it can be concluded that a successful Kankor is not dependent on any particular category of questions i.e. Mathematics, Natural, and Social Sciences, etc."
