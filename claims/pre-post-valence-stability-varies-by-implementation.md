`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Two-level bivariate Kendall's τb correlations (Table 2) in the flipped sample (n = 35) show state-level valence stability within students did not replicate the lecture-based pattern, while stable between-student differences remained weak and positive, with printed effect size "τb = 0.12, p = .028".

> "In the flipped learning group, the within-student association between pre- and post-event valence was not statistically significant, whereas the between-student association was weak and positive (τb = 0.12, p = .028)"
