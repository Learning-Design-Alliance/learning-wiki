## Evidence

### Nicolas Sommet 2022

Nicolas Sommet, David L. Weissman, and Andrew J. Elliot. (2022). Income Inequality Predicts Competitiveness and Cooperativeness at School. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000731

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Confirmatory factor analyses per country comparing a two-factor (instrumental vs. intrinsic) model against a one-factor baseline; "The two-factor model was found to ﬁt the data better" in 95% (Study 2a) and 97% (Study 2b) of countries, showing the constructs are operationally distinct.

> "The two-factor model was found to ﬁt the data better than the one-factor model in 95% of the countries of Study 2a and 97% of the countries of Study 2b"

### Nicolas Sommet 2022 (2)

Nicolas Sommet, David L. Weissman, and Andrew J. Elliot. (2022). Income Inequality Predicts Competitiveness and Cooperativeness at School. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000731

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Multilevel models using the instrumental-minus-intrinsic cooperativeness difference score, consistent with nonpreregistered Hypothesis 2B′ (b = .10, p < .001, bc = 0.12, pc < .001); replicated in Study 2b (b = .08, bc = 0.17, both pc < .001).

> "the higher the income inequality, the more students favored domain-speci ﬁc instrumental cooperativeness over domain-speci ﬁc intrinsic coop- erativeness, b = .10 [0.06, 0.15], p , .001, and bc = 0.12 [0.06, 0.17], pc , .001"
