Provider-failure and recovery results of the 120-episode synthetic rehearsal, shown in Figure 5 with 30 scripted episodes per condition. The report states the absence of hints in failure conditions "was the intended outcome"; no effect size is printed.

"The valid and reentrant conditions each stored 30 synthetic hints. The exception and empty conditions each recorded 30 unavailable outcomes and stored zero hints (Figure 5). Across all conditions, 120 callbacks were invoked and 60 hints were retained."
