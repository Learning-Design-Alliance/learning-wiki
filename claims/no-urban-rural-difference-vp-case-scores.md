## Evidence

### Nyiringango Gerard 2024

Nyiringango Gerard, Fors Uno, Forsberg Elenita, Tumusiime David K. (2024). Enhancing clinical reasoning for management of non-communicable diseases: virtual patient cases as a learning strategy for nurses in primary healthcare centers: a pre-post study design. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05440-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of 56 participants (14 urban, 42 rural) comparing scores by workplace area; Table 3 post-test p-values ranged from 0.414 to 0.946 across the four illness areas. The authors note tablets and portable internet equipment were lent so participants "used similar devices with similar internet capabilities in both urban and rural areas".

> "When it comes to participants' scores according to their area of work (urban or rural), the T-test analysis indicates no significant statistical difference based on participants' working area, see Table 3."
