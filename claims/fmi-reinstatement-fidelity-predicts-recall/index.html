In the fMRI experiment (n = 22 analysed, all dual-context), a representational similarity analysis quantified context reinstatement per trial; "high representational ﬁdelity trials (0.50 ± 0.17)" showed better recall than low-fidelity trials, with a printed partial eta-squared of ηp2 = 0.395 (F(1, 21) = 13.712, p = 0.001).
