### Habiyaremye HT 2022

Habiyaremye HT, Ntivuguruzwa C, Ntawiha P. (2022). From pedagogical toward technological pedagogical content knowledge frameworks and their effectiveness in teaching mathematics: A mapping review. F1000Research 11:1029. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.125073.1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Pre- and post-testing study (Lim-Teo et al., 2007) in which an MPCK instrument was given to 113 of 261 entering student teachers in July 2005 and re-administered in February 2006; the review reports "a weighty development in all areas of the MPCK".

> "The results show that teachers at the establishment of their education are poor in their knowledge of the pedagogical content of mathematics. There was a weighty development in all areas of the MPCK after the accomplishment of the pedagogy course."
