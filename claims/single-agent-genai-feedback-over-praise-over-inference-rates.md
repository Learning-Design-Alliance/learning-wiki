## Evidence

### Guo 2024

Guo, S., Latif, E., Zhou, Y., Huang, X., & Zhai, X. (2024). Using Generative AI and Multi-Agents to Provide Automatic Feedback. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.07407](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.07407)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Human coding of feedback generated by a single GPT-4o agent for 240 balanced student responses to a middle school science modeling item; two raters reached 100% inter-rater agreement on a 30% subsample. The article reports "15.42% ... over-praise, while 27.20% was labeled as over-inference" and 9.58% with both issues.

> "This study found that 15.42% of the automatically generated feedback provided by a single GenAI agent was labeled as over-praise, while 27.20% was labeled as over-inference. Additionally, 9.58% of the feedback exhibited both over-praise and over-inference."
