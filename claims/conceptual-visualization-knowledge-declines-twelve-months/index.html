Evidence¶

Francisco José Jiménez-Hornero 2026¶

Francisco José Jiménez-Hornero, Eduardo Gutiérrez de Ravé. (2026). Conceptual and procedural knowledge retention in data visualization: a longitudinal study of doctoral learning and instructional design implications. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09808-0

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Longitudinal LMM analysis of 40 doctoral students' rubric scores at T0, T1, and T2, with time as fixed effect and participant as random intercept. Means fell from 3.82 to 3.32; "the effect of time was F(2, 78) = 32.6, p < 0.001". The β = −0.25 coefficient indicates gradual decline.

"For the conceptual-understanding rubric, the effect of time was F(2, 78) = 32.6, p < 0.001. Predicted marginal means decreased from T0 (M = 3.82, 95% CI [3.70, 3.94]) to T1 ( M = 3.56, 95% CI [3.44, 3.68]) and T2 ( M = 3.32, 95% CI [3.19, 3.45])."
