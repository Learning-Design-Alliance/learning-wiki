## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Residents who underwent repeated 3D-model training showed no significant difference in operation score or time on porcine models compared with intermediates who had no such training (P > 0.05). [→ Xia Jianfu 2025](#xia-jianfu-2025)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre- and post-training porcine-model comparison among 12 surgeons (three experts, three untrained intermediates, six trained surgeons), with performance recorded and MBOSATS-scored by 2 experienced surgeons (Figures 3F-G). The test found "no significant differences... between residents who underwent repeated training and intermediates who did not (P>0.05)"; equivalence was not separately tested.

> "No significant differences were observed in operation score or time between residents who underwent repeated training and intermediates who did not (P>0.05)."

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same porcine-model transfer comparison, restricted to repeatedly trained intermediates versus experts. The article reports matched operation scores (P=0.557) but significantly longer operation times for trained intermediates (P=0.002), indicating score convergence without time convergence.

> "Repeated-training intermediates showed no significant differences with experts in operation score (P=0.557), but prolonged operation time (P=0.002)."
