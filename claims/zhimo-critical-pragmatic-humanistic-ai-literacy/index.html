Evidence¶

Zhaozhe Wang 2025¶

Zhaozhe Wang, Chaoran Wang. (2025). A posthumanist approach to AI literacy. Computers and Composition 76 (2025) 102933. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compcom.2025.102933

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Case-study analysis of Zhimo's interview and reflection data. He used metaphors such as "search engine," "machine," and "tool," and described an interactive philosophy of letting AI refine grammar while keeping his own logic intact as his authentic literacy agency.

"Zhimo perceives and conceptualizes AI technologies as a sophisticated, linguistically and rhetorically capable tool that can be “调教 (“tiao jiao”: trained and manipulated)” to perform a set of distinct, human-articulated writing tasks."

Zhaozhe Wang 2025 (2)¶

Zhaozhe Wang, Chaoran Wang. (2025). A posthumanist approach to AI literacy. Computers and Composition 76 (2025) 102933. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compcom.2025.102933

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Analysis of Zhimo's 1.5-hour interview shows his pragmatism was characterized by a pursuit of efficiency; the authors report he mentioned the notion eight times and evaluated AI as an external force that accelerates human-environment interaction.

"During the 1.5-hour interview session, Zhimo mentioned the notion of 高效 (“gao xiao”: efficiency) eight times, suggesting his internalized axiological disposition toward the purpose of AI assistance"
