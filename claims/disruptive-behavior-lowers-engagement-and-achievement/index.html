Evidence¶

Oliver 2011¶

Oliver, R. M., Wehby, J. H., & Reschly, D. J. (2011). Teacher classroom management practices: Effects on disruptive or aggressive student behavior. https://www.sree.org. doi:10.4073/csr.2011.4

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Background statement in the review's context section, attributed to Dolan et al. (1993); the review reports this second-hand and no effect size is printed. The review also cites Greer-Chase et al. finding aggressive students in disruptive classrooms more likely to be aggressive later.

"Students who are disruptive experience less academic engaged time, tend to have lower grades, and perform worse on standardized tests compared to students in well managed classrooms (Dolan, Kellam, Brown, Werthamer-Larsson, Rebok, Mayer, et al., 1993)."
