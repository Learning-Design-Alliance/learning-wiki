### Zosia Krusberg 2025

Zosia Krusberg. (2025). Where's the Line? A Classroom Activity on Ethical and Constructive Use of Generative AI in Physics. The University of Chicago. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.00229](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.00229)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Theme from students' reflective writing and discussion across cohorts. Students distinguished AI use supporting comprehension, such as prompting explanation or self-testing, from use bypassing intellectual effort, and connected this to "increased awareness of prompt design".

> "A central theme is a shift from viewing AI as a shortcut toward understanding it as a learning tool whose value depends on how it is used."
