## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Konkoor total score correlates weakly-to-moderately with CPIE scores (r = 0.326), and no combination of preadmission variables explains more than 12.6% of CPIE variance. [→ Farrokhi-Khajeh-Pasha Yasin 2012](#farrokhi-khajeh-pasha-yasin-2012)

## Evidence

### Farrokhi-Khajeh-Pasha Yasin 2012

Farrokhi-Khajeh-Pasha Yasin, Nedjat Saharnaz, Mohammadi Aeen, Rad Elaheh Malakan, Majdzadeh Reza, Monajemi Farshid, Jamali Ehsan, Yazdani Shahryar. (2012). The validity of Iran’s national university entrance examination (Konkoor) for predicting medical students’ academic performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-60

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis (models B1-B8) of the national cohort (CPIE scores for 1886 of 2126 matriculants) found preadmission variables explained no more than 12.6% of CPIE variance; Konkoor total score alone correlated r = 0.326 with CPIE.

> "The CPIE was the least predictable of the outcome variables. Different combinations of preadmission variables could explain, at most, 12.6% of the variance in CPIE scores."
