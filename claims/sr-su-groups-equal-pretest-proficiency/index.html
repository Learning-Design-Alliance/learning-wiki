Wang 2015¶

Wang, X. (2015). The Impacts of Presenting New Words in Semantically-Related Sets on Vocabulary Learning. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(6). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.6p.111

The pretest was the students' mid-term English exam with a total of 150 marks, used to check the rough equality of the two intact classes before treatment. The article reports the groups were "not significantly different (t=1.910, p=.064>.05)".

"the mean score of SR group (m=137.1579, std=5.38788) and the mean score of SU group (m=133.8947, std=5.14128) are not significantly different (t=1.910, p=.064>.05)."
