Evidence¶

Podiya Jayalaxmi Kaniyagundi 2025¶

Podiya Jayalaxmi Kaniyagundi, Navaneetham Janardhana, Bhola Poornima. (2025). Influences of school climate on emotional health and academic achievement of school-going adolescents in India: a systematic review. BMC Public Health 25, 54. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-21268-0

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review of eleven Indian studies (3,711 participants, grades 8–10) identified via PRISMA screening of 383 records; the review's synthesis reports that positive climate characteristics were "significantly associated with reduced symptoms of depression, stress, improved self-esteem, and academic performance". No pooled effect size was computed.

"positive school climate characteristics such as student-teacher relationships, peer support, a sense of connectedness, school belongingness, methods of learning, and teaching were significantly associated with reduced symptoms of depression, stress, improved self-esteem, and academic performance among high school students"
