## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Means differ statistically between matched and unmatched datasets at both pre- and post-survey, with the matched dataset biased toward higher scores. [→ Cole Walsh 2019](#cole-walsh-2019)

## Evidence

### Cole Walsh 2019

Cole Walsh, Katherine N. Quinn, C. Wieman, and N.G. Holmes. (2019). Quantifying critical thinking: Development and validation of the Physics Lab Inventory of Critical thinking (PLIC). [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010135)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Partial-sample reliability comparison of 1911 matched respondents against students completing only one closed-response survey (1376 pre, 797 post). Matched pre-average was 5.25 ± 0.05 versus 5.15 ± 0.06 unmatched; post-average 5.52 ± 0.05 versus 5.22 ± 0.09 unmatched.

> "Means are statistically diﬀerent between the matched and un- matched datasets for both the pre- and post-survey."
