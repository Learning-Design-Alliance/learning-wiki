## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There is a statistically significant main effect of treatment (ICT vs conventional) on participants' learning outcomes in economics, F = 6.42, P < 0.05. [→ Egaga 2015](#egaga-2015)

## Evidence

### Egaga 2015

Egaga, Patrick I.; Aderibigbe, S. Akinwumi (2015). Efficacy of Information and Communication Technology in Enhancing Learning Outcomes of Students with Hearing Impairment in Ibadan. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.30. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1081324

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of pretest-posttest control-group quasi-experiment with 30 purposively sampled students with hearing impairment (15 males, 15 females); the article reports "a significant main effect of treatment" (F=6.42, P <0.05), so the null hypothesis was rejected.

> "Table 1 shows that there is a significant main effect of treatment participants' learning outcomes in economics (F=6.42, P <0.05)."
