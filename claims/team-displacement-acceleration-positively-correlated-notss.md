## Evidence

### Harari Rayan Ebnali PhD 2024

Harari Rayan Ebnali PhD, Dias Roger D. MD PhD, Kennedy-Metz Lauren R. PhD, Varni Giovanna PhD, Gombolay Matthew PhD, Yule Steven PhD, Salas Eduardo PhD, Zenati Marco A. MD. (2024). Correlation analysis in the same 30-case cross-sectional dataset, with acceleration computed as frame-to-frame differences in team member speed from neck keypoints. The printed "acceleration: r = 0.48; P = .008" reached the study's significance threshold.

> "The average trajectory and acceleration of team displacement showed a positive correlation with overall NOTSS scores (trajectory: r = 0.51; P = .005; acceleration: r = 0.48; P = .008)."
