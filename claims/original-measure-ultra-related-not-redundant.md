## Evidence

### Limeri 2025

Limeri, Lisa B. (2025). Intelligence in Context: A Context-specific Mindset Measure Better Predicts Outcomes for Science and Math Undergraduates. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 24(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-09-0229

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Latent factor correlations from the measurement model fit to the 1537-student dataset. The measures overlap substantially (r = 0.48, p < 0.001) but, per the author, "are not redundant, but are both approximating the same construct."

> "the original mindset measure positively correlates with the fixed factor of ULTrA (r = 0.48, p < 0.001; Table 4) and negatively correlates with the growth factor of ULTrA (r = −0.23, p < 0.001; Table 4). These results are consistent with the expectation that the original mindset measure and ULTrA are not redundant, but are both approximating the same construct."
