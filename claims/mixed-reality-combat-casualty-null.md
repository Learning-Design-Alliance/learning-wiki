`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Trial by Tadlock et al. (2022): 55 surgeons and 14 non-surgical physicians assigned to 69 combat casualty care procedures (38 mixed reality, 31 audio). The review reports "Mixed reality shows no differences in procedural flow or advance planning (3.67 vs. 3.28; p = 0.21)."

> "Mixed reality shows no differences in procedural flow or advance planning (3.67 vs. 3.28; p = 0.21), nor in the probability of correctly performing individual procedural steps (4.12 vs. 3.59; p = 0.06)."
