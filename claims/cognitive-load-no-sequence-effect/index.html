Evidence¶

Eman Ganaiem 2025¶

Eman Ganaiem, Ido Roll. (2025). Investigating the order of example-problem sequences when learning experimental design and graphing competencies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09750-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Of 113 experimental design participants, 94 had complete Paas 9-point mental-effort ratings; ANCOVA found no condition effect (p = 0.175), contradicting the hypothesis that PS-first raises load.

"A one-way ANCOV A, controlling for pre-test, shows no significant effect for condition: F (2, 93) = 1.776, P = 0.175 and no significant condition-pre-test interaction: F (2, 93) = 0.378, P = 0.686."

Eman Ganaiem 2025 (2)¶

Eman Ganaiem, Ido Roll. (2025). Investigating the order of example-problem sequences when learning experimental design and graphing competencies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09750-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Of 120 graphing participants, 108 had complete cognitive load measures; ANCOVA found no significant condition effect (p = 0.607).

"A one-way ANCOV A, controlling for pre-test, found no significant effect for condition: F (2, 102) = 0.501, P = 0.607."
