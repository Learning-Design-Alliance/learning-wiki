## Evidence

### Gupta Chandni 2022

Gupta Chandni, Henein Christin, Ashton Christopher, Makuloluwa Achini, Mathew Rashmi G. (2022). Development of virtual ophthalmic surgical skills training. Eye. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41433-021-01896-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-report questionnaire data from the same 20-student virtual session. At baseline only 40% agreed they felt confident using instruments and suturing, and 30% felt confident tying reef and slip knots; these are perceived confidence gains, not assessed competency.

> "Overall, 75% perceived an improvement in their confidence in instrument handling, 80% in cable knot tying and 70% in suture tying."
