# The Text + Arithmetic format specifically improved procedural knowledge (executing action sequences to solve problems)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A significant main effect of representational format on procedural knowledge was found, with Text + Arithmetic outperforming Diagram and Diagram + Arithmetic. [→ Bas Kolloffel 2009](#bas-kolloffel-2009)

## Evidence

### Bas Kolloffel 2009

Bas Kolloffel, Tessa H. S. Eysink, Ton de Jong, Pascal Wilhelm. (2009). The effects of representational format on learning combinatorics from an interactive computer simulation. Instr Sci 37:503–517. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9056-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on procedural post-test items (solving combinatorial problems) in the five-condition experiment. The article reports "a signiﬁcant main effect of representational format" with F(4,119) = 2.52; no effect size was printed.

> "Analysis of procedural knowledge revealed a signiﬁcant main effect of representational format, F(4,119) = 2.52, P\ .05. A post-hoc LSD analysis showed that participants in the Text + Arithmetic condition outperformed participants in the Diagram condition (P\ .01), and participants in the Diagram + Arithmetic condition ( P\ .05)."
