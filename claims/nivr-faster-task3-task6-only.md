## Evidence

### Zhang D 2025

Zhang D, Fu M, Zhang J, Li Y, Chen L, Chen Y, Zhong Z, Zhang Y. (2025). Evaluating Whether Nonimmersion Virtual Reality Simulation Training Improves Nursing Competency in Isolation Wards: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/63131

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Per-task OSCE timing analysis in the randomized trial of 90 nurses; the intervention group beat the control group on task 3 and task 6 with moderate effect sizes (Cohen d=0.68 and 0.64).

> "Among the 6 work tasks, the intervention group completed task 3 (patient reception inwards) and task 6 (leaving the isolation area) significantly faster than the control group (t88=3.22, P=.002, Cohen d=0.68; t88=3.03, P=.003, Cohen d=0.64; Table 3)."

### Zhang D 2025 (2)

Zhang D, Fu M, Zhang J, Li Y, Chen L, Chen Y, Zhong Z, Zhang Y. (2025). Evaluating Whether Nonimmersion Virtual Reality Simulation Training Improves Nursing Competency in Isolation Wards: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/63131

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same per-task analysis found "no differences in completion times for the remaining 4 tasks between both groups", so the total-time advantage was confined to two of the six tasks.

> "In comparison, there were no differences in completion times for the remaining 4 tasks between both groups (P>.05; Table 3)."
