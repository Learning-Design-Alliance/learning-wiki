Correlational analysis relating time-taken-to-learn in the AV task to linguistic and cognitive measures (Figure 5); correlations were not corrected for multiple comparisons. The null phonological-awareness result matches prior adult findings and supports the expectation that phonological skills are not directly related to learning efficiency in this task.
