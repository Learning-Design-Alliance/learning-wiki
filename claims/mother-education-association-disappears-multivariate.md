### Boonyapison 2025

Boonyapison, K., Sittironnarit, G., & Rattanaumpawan, P. (2025). Association between the big five personalities and academic performance among grade 12 students at international high school in Thailand. Scientific Reports. [doi:10.1038/s41598-025-01038-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-01038-7)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the questionnaire study, univariate comparison showed "91.2% vs. 81.2%; p-value = 0.04" for mothers with a bachelor's degree or higher in the AA- vs. AN-group, but the association "disappeared in the multivariate model", attributed to low heterogeneity in parental education.

> "However, the positive association between the mother’s educational level and academic achievement disappeared in the multivariate model. Given that the majority of mothers in both groups graduated with a bachelor’s degree or higher (> 80%), it would be difficult to confirm the significant association after adjusting for other factors."
