Hampton et al. 2020¶

Lauren H Hampton, Ann P Kaiser and Elizabeth A Fuller. (2020). Multi-component communication intervention for children with autism: A randomized controlled trial. Autism, 24(8), 2104–2116. https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361320934558

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial with 68 children ages 3–5 with autism randomized to intervention (34) or comparison (34); linear regression controlling for autism severity and pretest scores. The discussion reports the joint attention effect as 'a moderate to small effect size (d = 0.312)'.
