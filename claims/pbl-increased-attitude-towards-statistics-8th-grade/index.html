Evidence¶

Koparan 2014¶

Koparan, T., & Güven, B. (2014). The Effect on the 8th Grade Students' Attitude towards Statistics of Project Based Learning. European Journal of Educational Research, 3(2), 73-85. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086040

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre/post study; 35 8th-grade students taught statistics via student-defined projects for about one month. The article reports the dependent t-test on Rasch logits: "t(34) = -3.553, p < .05", with means .51 to 1.18.

"Looking at the measures of the intervention groups the average score of students at the pre -test was .51 (SD = .64), while the average score at post was 1.18 ( SD = .87). Results from a dependent t-test indicate that this difference was significant, t(34) = -3.553, p < .05."
