## Evidence

### Campbell 2018

Campbell, A. R., Bowman-Perrott, L., Burke, M. D., & Sallese, M. R. (2018). Reading, Writing, Math, and Content-Area Interventions for Improving Behavioral and Academic Outcomes of Students With Emotional and Behavioral Disorders. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 16(2), 119-138. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1194543

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meta-analysis of 16 studies (Losinski et al., 2014), including 12 single-case and 4 randomized controlled trial designs, covering K-12 students; the review reports large effect sizes across three dependent variables.

> "Results indicated that SRSD interventions had large effect sizes across three independent variables (essay elements, quality, and word count)."

### Campbell 2018 (2)

Campbell, A. R., Bowman-Perrott, L., Burke, M. D., & Sallese, M. R. (2018). Reading, Writing, Math, and Content-Area Interventions for Improving Behavioral and Academic Outcomes of Students With Emotional and Behavioral Disorders. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 16(2), 119-138. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1194543

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of 13 studies (Sreckovic et al., 2014), 10 single-case and 3 group designs, evaluating SRSD for writing in grades 2-11 using Horner et al. (2005) and Gersten et al. (2000) quality standards.

> "In all 13 studies, a functional relationship was established between the introduction of SRSD and changes in student performance according to visual inspection and small to large effect size magnitudes."
