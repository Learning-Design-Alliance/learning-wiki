Evidence¶

Julian F. Lohmann 2025¶

Julian F. Lohmann, Nils Machts, Jens Möller, Steffen Zitzmann. (2025). A More Comprehensive, More Reliable Multilevel Approach for Assessing and Modeling Teacher Judgment Accuracy Using Latent Variables. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10029-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Simulation study 2 used a 2 × 3 design (N Level-2 = 50/200; N Level-1 = 10/30/50) with 500 datasets per condition and a large moderation effect of ± 0.5, comparing ML-LVM and PS detection of moderation effects via 95% credible intervals versus lm-based p-values. The article reports "consistent performance advantages in terms of true-positive rates occurred for the ML-LVM approach".

"Comparing the PS method and the ML-LVM, consistent performance advantages in terms of true-positive rates occurred for the ML-LVM approach across conditions and components except in the first condition."
