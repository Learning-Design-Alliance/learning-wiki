## Evidence

### de Leng W E 2018

de Leng W E, Stegers-Jager K M, Born M Ph, Themmen A P N. (2018). Integrity situational judgement test for medical school selection: judging 'what to do' versus 'what not to do'. Medical Education. [doi:10.1111/medu.13498](https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13498)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational validation study of the Integrity SJT administered to 402 prospective applicants at Erasmus MC, with correlations meta-analytically merged across two test versions. Correlations with integrity measures indicated "a moderate effect size (−0.22 ≤ r ≤ 0.40)"; self-efficacy correlations were non-significant.

> "All SJT scores (i.e. total, HH-based and CD-based) correlated significantly with the four external integrity-related measures (Table3). The correlations were in the expected direction and indicated a moderate effect size (−0.22 ≤ r ≤ 0.40)."
