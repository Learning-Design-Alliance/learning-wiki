### Kurup Chanchal 2025

Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2025). Exploring the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Analysis of Recruiting Managers' View Points. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70184

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke's 6-stage inductive framework) of semi-structured interviews with eight recruiting managers, conducted January to March 2023. The analysis "constructed from the data" the three named themes, with subthemes covering matching, barriers, and advancement strategies.

> "Three themes regarding recruiting managers' perspectives about the specialty skill transfer of IQNs to the Australian health system were constructed from the data: Theme 1: Right person, right opportunity; Theme 2: Barriers to skill alignment; and Theme 3: Advancing opportunities"
