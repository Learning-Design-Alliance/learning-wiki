### Bösner S 2015

Bösner S, Pickert J, Stibane T. (2015). Teaching differential diagnosis in primary care using an inverted classroom approach: student satisfaction and gain in skills and knowledge. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0346-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of a focus group discussion and free-text questionnaire answers from all 17 participants, conducted after the 8th of 14 sessions. One student noted that "The things (seminar content) that are in the videos can be outsourced very easily".

> "The blended learning design of the seminar using an inverted classroom approach was appreciated by all students, as it gave more time during the seminar to concentrate on interactive and practice based learning."
