# Graduate students with hybrid researcher-teacher identities hold more synergistic RTN perceptions than those with researcher identities

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` RTN perception scores differ significantly by academic identity, with hybrid researcher-teacher and teacher-researcher identities scoring higher than researcher identity. [→ Reid Joshua W. 2020](#reid-joshua-w-2020)

## Evidence

### Reid Joshua W. 2020

Reid Joshua W., Gardner Grant E. (2020). Navigating Tensions of Research and Teaching: Biology Graduate Students' Perceptions of the Research–Teaching Nexus within Ecological Contexts. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-11-0218

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis test on survey data (χ2(3) = 8.21, p = 0.04) comparing RTN perception scores across academic identities; post hoc tests show "a statistically significant difference between researcher identity and researcher-teacher identity (p = 0.006)". Mean rank scores: researcher 1.36, researcher-teacher 1.96, teacher-researcher 1.98, teacher 1.33.

> "Post hoc analysis revealed a statistically significant difference between researcher identity and researcher-teacher identity (p = 0.006) as well as between researcher identity and teacher-researcher identity (p = 0.028)"
