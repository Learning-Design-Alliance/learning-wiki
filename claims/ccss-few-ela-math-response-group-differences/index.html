The inset box describes CPS's two implementation strategies: the Literacy department used "early-adopter schools" to design professional learning and materials, while the Mathematics department partnered with two local universities and built an online repository of standards-aligned materials. Despite these differences, the brief reports few ELA-versus-math response differences.

"The Department of Literacy relied on early-adopter schools—CPS schools that had already shown evidence of using instructional practices aligned with the goals of the new ELA standards—to help design professional learning and develop curriculum and materials"
