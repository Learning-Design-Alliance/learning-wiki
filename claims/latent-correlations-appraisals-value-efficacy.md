### The appraisal of fear appeals as a challenge or a threat influences a subsequent decline or increase in attainment value and academic self-efficacy 2014

The appraisal of fear appeals as a challenge or a threat influences a subsequent decline or increase in attainment value and academic self-efficacy. (2014). https://eric.ed.gov/?q=appraisal+of+fear+appeals+challenge+threat+attainment+value+academic+self-efficacy

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent bivariate correlations reported in Table 1 from the preliminary measurement-model analyses. Challenge and threat were positively intercorrelated, as were academic self-efficacy and attainment value at both waves; no standardised effect sizes beyond the correlations themselves are printed here.

> "A challenge appraisal was positively correlated with, and a threat appraisal was negatively correlated with, academic self-efficacy (at both T1 and T3). A challenge appraisal was positively correlated with attainment value at both T1 and T3. However, a threat appraisal was positively correlated with attainment value at T1 but negative correlated with attainment value at T3."
