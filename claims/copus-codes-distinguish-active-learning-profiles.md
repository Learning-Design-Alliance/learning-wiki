### Kelley Commeford 2022 (2)

Kelley Commeford, Eric Brewe, and Adrienne Traxler. (2022). Characterizing active learning environments in physics using latent profile analysis. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010113)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

> "Proﬁle 2 (blue boxes), which holds all of the Modeling Instruction observations, is the only pedagogy that used student presentations (S.SP) and whole class discussions (S.WC)."
