# Teachers disagree about whether thermal energy can be useful, revealing that energy's usefulness depends on the objects included in the scenario

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In one episode, teachers offer conflicting definitions of useful energy (heat as wasted entropy vs. heat from coal as useful vs. controllability as the criterion), failing to reach consensus. [→ Daane 2014](#daane-2014)

## Evidence

### Daane 2014

Daane, Vokos, and Scherr. (2014). Goals for teacher learning about energy degradation and usefulness. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 10, 020111. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.10.020111

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Episode analysis of secondary teachers discussing Energy Theater and Energy Cubes: Irene calls heat 'wasted, part of entropy,' Kate counters that coal-burning heat is useful, and Abdul adds controllability. The article attributes the disagreement to teachers imagining different scenarios.

> "In this episode, teachers do not reach consensus about what useful energy is and what defines less useful or wasted energy. The lack of agreement highlights that whether energy is free or degraded depends on the scenario."
