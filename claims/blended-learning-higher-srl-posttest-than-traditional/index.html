Evidence¶

Uz 2018¶

Uz, R., & Uzun, A. (2018). The influence of blended learning environment on self-regulated and self-directed learning skills of learners. European Journal of Educational Research, 7(4), 877-886. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.7.4.877

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent pre-test post-test control group study of 167 undergraduates; Kruskal-Wallis on post-test SRL scores gave chi-square = 12.315, p = .002, and Mann-Whitney U follow-ups showed the "experimental group and two control groups" differed significantly (p = .004 and p = .002) while Control 1 vs Control 2 did not (p = .713).

"Table 7 shows that the difference between self -regulated learning post -test scores of the experimental group and two control groups was statistically significant. But the difference between self -regulated learning post -test scores of two control groups was not statistically significant."
