## Subclaims
`q2 i2` A modest-to-moderate positive correlation (r = 0.341) existed between improvement in computational thinking and improvement in poetic thinking, supported at greater than 90% confidence (p = 0.082). [→ Jenkins 2015](#jenkins-2015)
