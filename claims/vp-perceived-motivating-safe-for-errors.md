## Evidence

### Isaza-Restrepo Andrés 2018

Isaza-Restrepo Andrés, Gómez María Teresa, Cifuentes Gary, Argüello Arturo. (2018). The virtual patient as a learning tool: a mixed quantitative qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1395-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative component using student focus groups, professor field journals, and ethnographic observation coded in Atlas.ti under four analytical categories. The use-of-the-tool category yielded advantages including "making mistakes with no consequences" and review and reassessment of information; disadvantages included monotony as a sole methodology and inability to replace real patient interaction.

> "participants perceived a motivating and easy-to-use tool, which allowed for making mistakes with no consequences over the integrity of a real patient."
