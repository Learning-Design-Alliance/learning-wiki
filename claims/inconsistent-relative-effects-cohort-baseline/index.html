Evidence¶

Foorman 2017¶

Foorman, B., Herrera, S., Dombek, J., Schatschneider, C., & Petscher, Y. (2017). The relative effectiveness of two approaches to early literacy intervention in grades K–2 (REL 2017–251). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

Subgroup probes of treatment interactions in the hierarchical linear models; the FRA Word Reading outcome was "higher in embedded intervention schools than in standalone intervention schools" in grade 1 cohort 1, with a printed effect size of 0.29 standard deviation (22 points, 11 percentile rank points). The report describes the overall cross-cohort pattern as inconsistent.

"In grade 1 the FRA Word Reading outcome was higher in embedded intervention schools than in standalone intervention schools among students in cohort 1."
