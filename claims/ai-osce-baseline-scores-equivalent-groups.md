# Baseline OSCE scores on the generative AI platform did not differ between the podcast and control groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Cumulated baseline OSCE scores did not differ between the podcast group (23.8 ± 3.9) and the control group (23.3 ± 5.3; p = 0.77), which the authors interpret as supporting the platform's reliability in a randomized cohort. [→ Dupont V 2025](#dupont-v-2025)

## Evidence

### Dupont V 2025

Dupont V, Guerrot D, Bouazzi L, Figueres L, Pers Y-M, Guenou E, Barbe C, Yauy K. (2025). Evaluating podcasts as a tool for OSCE training: a randomized trial using generative AI-powered simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06675-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled trial's baseline assessment, the first three OSCE stations on DocSimulator produced no statistically significant difference between groups, with means of "23.8 ± 3.9 vs. 23.3 ± 5.3; p = 0.77". Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "The cumulated note at baseline did not differ between the two groups (23.8 ± 3.9 vs. 23.3 ± 5.3; p = 0.77)."
