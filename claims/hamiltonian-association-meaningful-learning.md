## Evidence

### Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017

Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja. (2017). A STUDY ON PRE-SERVICE PHYSICS TEACHERS' CONCEPTUALIZATION ON ELEMENTARY QUANTUM MECHANICS. Investigações em Ensino de Ciências – V22 (2), pp. 120-138. https://doi.org/10.22600/1518-8795.ienci2017v22n2p120

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Predicative knowledge analysis of the six-student intervention. The authors state this association "represents an extended combination of the concepts of Dynamical Variable and Energy" new to students, and that despite being erroneous "this might be evidence of Meaningful Learning" requiring negotiation.

> "Another feature that should be discussed is the consideration of the Hamiltonian as a Dynamical Variable identical to the Energy (d.1.V.), an association made by three students."

### Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017 (2)

Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja. (2017). A STUDY ON PRE-SERVICE PHYSICS TEACHERS' CONCEPTUALIZATION ON ELEMENTARY QUANTUM MECHANICS. Investigações em Ensino de Ciências – V22 (2), pp. 120-138. https://doi.org/10.22600/1518-8795.ienci2017v22n2p120

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Same predicative analysis. For conservative systems students related the Hamiltonian to interaction via potential energy; the authors argue this is evidence of Meaningful Learning because students base it on prior knowledge of potential energy.

> "The last feature we want to point out, because it's relatively shared by the students, is the one that considers the Hamiltonian gives information on the interactions occurring in the Physical System (d.2.V.)."
