Questionnaire evaluation of the AR learning environment with 188 high school students using the System Usability Scale. The average score was "80.18 (SD: 12.06)", which the authors associate with an "excellent" rating. No learning outcome measures were collected in this evaluation.

> "We evaluated the usability of the AR learning environment by handing out questionnaires to 188 high school students, using the system usability scale from [30]. The scale led to an average score of 80.18 (SD: 12.06) which can be associated with an assessment of "excellent" according to [31]."
