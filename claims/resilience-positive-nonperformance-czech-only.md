## Evidence

### Kvintova Jana 2024

Kvintova Jana, Novotny Jan Sebastian, Liu Hongyang, Vachova Lucie, Kantor Jiri. (2024). Path analysis reveals cross-country differences between Czech and Chinese university students in effect of internet and smartphone addiction, mental health, and personality traits on academic achievement in the post-pandemic era. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02069-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Czech path model: resilience positively predicted coping with study demands (B = 0.07 [0.01, 0.13], P = 0.029) and social adaptation (B = 0.25 [0.19, 0.3], P < 0.001); no resilience path appears in the Chinese model. The article describes "the protective role of resilience against academic challenges". No standardised effect size is printed for this finding.

> "the predicted positive effect was confirmed for resilience, but only for Czech students."
