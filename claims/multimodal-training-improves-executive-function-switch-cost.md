## Evidence

### Zwilling 2019

Zwilling, C. E., Daugherty, A. M., Hillman, C. H., Kramer, A. F., Cohen, N. J. & Barbey, A. K. (2019). Enhanced decision-making through multimodal training. npj Science of Learning 4:11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0049-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus ANCOVA on the set shifting task switch cost measure in the RCT. Table 2 reports Cohen's d effect size of 0.68 (fitness), 0.29 (fitness plus cognitive), and 0.31 (fitness plus cognitive plus meditation) relative to active control; groups did not differ at pre-intervention (F = 0.75, p = 0.52).

> "The ANCOVA model testing the post-intervention switch cost measure of executive function, while controlling for the pre-intervention switch cost measure, was signi ﬁcant ( F = 4.17, p = 0.0072, df = (4,155), Table 2)."
