### Renato P. dos Santos 2014

Renato P. dos Santos. (2014). Big Data as a Mediator in Science Teaching: A Proposal. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.2441534](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.2441534)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Interpretive caveat the article draws, citing Lazer et al. (2014): Google and its derivatives 'constantly changing their algorithms' prevents replication and casts doubt on whether users of these data sources are watching robust patterns or evanescent trends. No effect size is printed.

> "Moreover, this change prevents the replication of results and consequently compromises the empirical research of the scientists who use these data sources over time and casting doubt if they are watching robust patterns or trends evanescent."

### Renato P. dos Santos 2014 (2)

Renato P. dos Santos. (2014). Big Data as a Mediator in Science Teaching: A Proposal. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.2441534](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.2441534)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Interpretive caveat the article states, citing Mohebbi et al. (2011): 'the underlying cause of search behavior can never be known' and the correspondence may not hold in the future; the article adds that Google users do not represent a random sample of the population. No effect size is printed.

> "one must be aware that the underlying cause of search behavior can never be known, that this correspondence may not hold in the future due to changes in user behavior, which are unrelated to the target behavior"
