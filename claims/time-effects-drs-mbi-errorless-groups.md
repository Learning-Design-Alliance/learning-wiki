### Lee Grace Y 2013

Lee Grace Y, Yip Calvin CK, Yu Edwin CS, Man David WK. (2013). Evaluation of a computer-assisted errorless learning-based memory training program for patients with early Alzheimer's disease in Hong Kong: a pilot study. Clinical Interventions in Aging. https://doi.org/10.2147/CIA.S45726

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Friedman's repeated-measures analysis across pretest, post-test, and three-month follow-up in each arm of the pilot trial. For DRS the article reports increases of 4.67 in CELP, 10.5 in TELP, and 0.86 in the waiting-list control group; no effect sizes are printed.
