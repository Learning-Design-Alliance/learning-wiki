## Evidence

### Eroğlu Haktanır A 2026

Eroğlu Haktanır A, Çelebi A. (2026). Achalasia: Physician practices and knowledge in Türkiye. Turk J Gastroenterol. https://doi.org/10.5152/tjg.2026.25713

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results (n = 675); 75.4% self-reported adequate knowledge yet 78.1% acknowledged gaps, which the authors call a paradox in which "cognitive familiarity does not consistently translate into clinical practice".

> "Only 21.8% routinely inquired about dysphagia, regurgitation, or chest pain, whereas over half did so rarely or never."

### Eroğlu Haktanır A 2026 (2)

Eroğlu Haktanır A, Çelebi A. (2026). Achalasia: Physician practices and knowledge in Türkiye. Turk J Gastroenterol. https://doi.org/10.5152/tjg.2026.25713

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results on referral patterns; physicians identified lack of awareness (66.6%) as the leading cause of diagnostic delay, followed by patient noncompliance (47.0%) and late presentation (46.6%).

> "Regarding patient referral for achalasia, 44.0% of respondents had referred at least 1 patient, while 56.0% reported never having referred a patient for further evaluation or management."
