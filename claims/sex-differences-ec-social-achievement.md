## Evidence

### Valiente Carlos 2011

Valiente Carlos, Eisenberg Nancy, Haugen Rg, Spinrad Tracy L., Hofer Claire, Liew Jeffrey, Kupfer Anne. (2011). Children's Effortful Control and Academic Achievement: Mediation Through Social Functioning. Early Education and Development, 22(3), 411-433. https://doi.org/10.1080/10409289.2010.505259

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

T tests in the longitudinal sample (N=214 at T1) showed girls higher on all three EC measures; at T2 teachers rated girls higher on social competence and lower on externalizing problems, Fs(1, 178)=7.57 and 9.28, ps<.01; at T3 girls' achievement was higher, ts(121 and 134)=2.43 and 4.15.

> "At T1, girls were higher on parent- and teacher-rated EC and observed EC than boys, ts(207, 193, 210)=3.26, 3.35, and 3.20, respectively, ps<.002."
