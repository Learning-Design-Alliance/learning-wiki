### Auerbach Anna Jo and Schussler Elisabeth 2017

Auerbach Anna Jo and Schussler Elisabeth. (2017). A Vision and Change Reform of Introductory Biology Shifts Faculty Perceptions and Use of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0258

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Null result from the same repeated-measures ANOVA analyses of observations of 10 instructors: "no significant differences" for GALO or any AL event type across the 3 years. GALO typically comprised 13–21% of total class time; no effect sizes were printed for these tests.

> "There were no significant differences when comparing the use of GALO or any of the types of AL events (i.e., clickers, verbal questions, and activities) in class across the 3 years."
