### Gifford 2026

Gifford, K., Ehrlich, G. S., Bumbacher, E., & Kuo, E. (2026). “I forgot the formula:” How students can use coherence to reconstruct a (partially) forgotten equation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/yfxm-m4sw](https://doi.org/10.1103/yfxm-m4sw)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of 18 one-on-one clinical interviews in which students answered qualitative E&M questions; researchers reviewed The Four Circuits Problem across all interviews and identified 12 instances of partially or totally forgetting an equation or dependence, four of which involved mathematical sensemaking reconstruction. The authors note "In four of these instances, the interviewees (Gabi#1, Tom, Gabi#2, and Rene) attempted to reconstruct the missing equation or dependence using mathematical sensemaking."
