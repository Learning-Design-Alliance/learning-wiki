`q2 i?` Experts noticed instructor behaviors that hold students accountable 5.8 times more often than novices (p < 0.01, adjusted). [→ Auerbach A. J. 2018](#auerbach-a-j-2018)

## Evidence

### Auerbach A. J. 2018

Auerbach A. J., Higgins M., Brickman P., Andrews T. C. (2018). Teacher Knowledge for Active-Learning Instruction: Expert–Novice Comparison Reveals Differences. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-07-0149

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Quantitative comparison of qualitative content-analysis counts from a lesson-analysis survey completed by 14 experts and 29 novices; the generalized linear model estimate for this component was 5.8 (95% CI 2.2–17.9), interpreted by the authors as experts noticed accountability "5.8 times more often than did novices."

> "Holding students accountable	instructor behaviors that impact students’ motivation to engage and work in class by holding them accountable."
