### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2016

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2016). Student difficulties with determining expectation values in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Open-ended question Q3 on the continuous-eigenvalue case, administered to 32 UG and 62 G students after traditional instruction and graded with the same rubric as the discrete case. The article reports "only 22% of the UG students and 53% of the G students" correct.

> "Table II shows that only 22% of the UG students and 53% of the G students were correctly able to obtain an expression for expectation value 𝑄̂ for state |Ψ〉 using a basis of eigenstates |𝑞〉 and eigenvalues 𝑞."
