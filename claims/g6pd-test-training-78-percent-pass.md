Summary analysis of a label-comprehension questionnaire administered after training to 45 health workers recruited as a convenience sample at four sites in Brazil, Ethiopia, and India; "35 out of 45 participants (78%)" passed the unweighted 85% criterion.

> "The summary results of the unweighted analysis by participant show that in total, 35 out of 45 participants (78%) met the predetermined acceptance criteria of a minimum of 85% correct responses."
