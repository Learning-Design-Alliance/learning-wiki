The essay's Lesson Six argument, citing Polya (1945) and Schoenfeld (1985). Named heuristics include "drawing new diagrams, comparing a problem to one we have solved before, or explaining it aloud"; unconscious-engaging examples are brainstorming, taking a break, and sleeping on the problem.
