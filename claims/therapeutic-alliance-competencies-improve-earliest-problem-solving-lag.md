`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of mean ENACT-E item scores grouped by domain (Table 2, Figs 1–4) across training and pilot months 5–11. Therapeutic alliance items "saw the earliest and greatest improvements"; by months 5–11 problem solving scored 2.45, coping mechanisms 2.50 and giving advice 2.77.

> "In general, therapeutic alliance competencies saw the earliest and greatest improvements (see Fig 1). Items relating to personal factors (e.g., substance use and physical health) and external factors (e.g., assessing social networks) were initially rated lower, but scores improved during the pilot."
