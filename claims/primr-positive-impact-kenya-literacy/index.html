Evidence¶

Piper 2015¶

Piper, B., Bulat, J., & Johnston, A. (2015). Reading Skill Transfer Across Languages: Outcomes from Longitudinal Bilingual Randomized Control Trials in Kenya and Haiti. SREE Spring 2015 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562423

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Findings from the PRIMR randomized control trial, in which clusters of Kenyan schools were randomly assigned to treatment or delayed-control conditions and pupils were assessed with EGRA-based tasks in English and Kiswahili at three time points. The article reports "a positive impact on the literacy outcomes of interest."

"Analysis shows that PRIMR had a positive impact on the literacy outcomes of interest, including but not limited to letter sound identification fluency, oral reading fluency, the percentage of pupils who read at the MOE's bench mark, and reading comprehension, although not on all combinations of language, grade, and school type."
