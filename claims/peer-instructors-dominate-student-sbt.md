## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students report peers as their main simulator instructors far more often than physicians do, and formal assessment of simulator learning success is frequently absent. [→ Pillong Lukas 2025](#pillong-lukas-2025)

## Evidence

### Pillong Lukas 2025

Pillong Lukas, Sprengart Franziska Marietta, Recker Florian, et al. (2025). Reflecting realities: gauging the pulse of simulator-based training in medical minds—resonance of simulator-based ultrasound training in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07198-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Questionnaire items on instructor roles in the 343-participant survey: "64% of PJ students and 42% of third-year students cited peers in this role, compared to only 13% of physicians". On learning-success assessment, 55% of PJ students, 32% of third-year students, and 34% of physicians reported no formal assessment (p = 0.0017).

> "When asked about instructors for simulator-based training, 64% of PJ students and 42% of third-year students cited peers in this role, compared to only 13% of physicians (p < 0.001). Meanwhile, physicians were named by 62% of PJ students and 27% of both third-year students and physicians themselves (p < 0.001)."
