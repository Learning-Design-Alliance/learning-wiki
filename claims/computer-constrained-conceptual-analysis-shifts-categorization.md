# Novices' categorization tendency shifted toward categorizing problems by principle rather than surface feature from pre-test to post-test after brief exposure to computer-constrained conceptual analysis, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Novices' categorization tendency showed significant shifts from pre-test to post-test after brief exposure to a computer-based program requiring expert-like conceptual analysis before problem solving, in one uncontrolled pre-post study, without instructional feedback. [→ Gerace 2007](#gerace-2007)

## Evidence

### Gerace 2007

Gerace, W. J. (2007). Contributions from Cognitive Research to Mathematics and Science Education. In D. Grayson (Ed.), Proceedings of the Workshop on Research in Science and Mathematics Education, Pietermaritzburg, South Africa. Originally published 1992. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0708.2496](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0708.2496)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study by the author's group in which novices were constrained by a computer-based program to perform expert-like conceptual analysis before solving problems. The article reports "significant shifts" in categorization tendency; no effect size is printed.

> "After only a rather brief exposure to this device, which incidentally was completely devoid of any instructional feedback, students displayed significant shifts in their tendency to categorize problems by principle rather than by surface feature."
