q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Outcome-specific synthesis of three studies (Gutiérrez-Fresneda, 2017, 2018; Rolla et al., 2006) measuring concepts of print using shared reading, textbook-based teaching, and family/tutor/classroom interventions. The article reports no effect size, only negative nonsignificant impacts.

"Impacts on concepts of print outcomes were negative and nonsignificant in these three studies. Future research is needed to provide evidence about this instructional practice on reading outcomes."
