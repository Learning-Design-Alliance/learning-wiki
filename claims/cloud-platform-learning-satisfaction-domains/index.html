Evidence¶

Shih 2013¶

Shih, R.-C., Cho, C.-L., Tsai, C.-C., & Lou, S.-J. (2013). Employing Microsoft Live@Edu Cloud Platform to Assist in Teaching Chinese Reading for Junior High School Students. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018038

q2 · i? · causal · r1

One-sample t-tests (test value=3, df=31) on the learning satisfaction questionnaire showed all domains significantly above midpoint: teaching method (t=10.17), quality of learning (t=8.11), learning interaction (t=6.65), learning assessment (t=5.58), learning reflection (t=6.68), overall performance (t=8.45).

"All the five domains obtained p value of .000<.05, indicating the students have made a significant progress in areas of teaching method, quality of learning, learning interaction, learning assessment, learning reflection, and overall performance."
