## Evidence

### Peter A. Edelsbrunner 2025

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Bianca A. Simonsmeier, Michael Schneider. (2025). The Cronbach’s Alpha of Domain‑Specific Knowledge Tests Before and After Learning: A Meta‑Analysis of Published Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09982-y

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Between-studies heterogeneity was very large: τ² = 0.73, I² = 98.68%. The authors state this "makes it difficult to predict the Alpha for future studies" and suggest the PI range as a guideline for tests with 20 items.

> "The 90% PI for the overall Alpha was [0.35, 0.96]. There was an estimated between-studies variance of τ2 = 0.73, which made up for an estimated proportion of I2 = 98.68% of the total variability."
