## Evidence

### Cherry et al. 2014

Stephen M. Cherry, Amy Lucas, Kim A. Case, and William T. Hoston. (2014). Who is White? Assessing Students' Perceptions of Whiteness through Active-Learning Effectiveness and the Construction of Racial Doubt. The Journal of Effective Teaching, Vol. 14, No.3, 2014, 68-83. http://www.understandingrace.org/home.html

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment in two undergraduate race and ethnicity courses (N = 97) taught in consecutive spring semesters; only one course received the doubt-raising materials. Post-lesson survey showed treated students "overwhelmingly likely to be 'unsure' about racial classifications"; Figure 2 notes significance at the 0.01 level.

> "Student in the treated class identified, on average, approximately two of the 27 nationalities as White, four as non -White, and 21 as "n ot sure." Conversely, students in the non-treated class were far more certain in their identifications."
