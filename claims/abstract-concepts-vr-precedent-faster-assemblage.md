## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In the two-player demonstration, Player 2, who interacted with geometric abstract concepts, reproduced the assemblage in significantly shorter time and reported higher understanding and motivation than Player 1. [→ Hassan Anifowose 2022](#hassan-anifowose-2022)

## Evidence

### Hassan Anifowose 2022

Hassan Anifowose, Wei Yan, Manish Dixit. (2022). Using Game Engine for Visualization in Architectural & Construction Education. ACADIA 2021. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2201.09954](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2201.09954)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In-lab demonstration with two players; Player 2 experienced all of Player 1's features plus interacting with the geometric abstract concepts. The authors report Player 2 had "significant shorter time to reproduce the assemblage" and higher understanding and motivation; no effect size or test statistic is printed.

> "Player 2 had significant shorter time to reproduce the assemblage while also reporting a higher level understanding and motivation for learning and reproducing the building than Player 1."
