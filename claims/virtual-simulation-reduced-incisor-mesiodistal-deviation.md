## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Dental interns trained with virtual simulation showed significantly smaller median mesiodistal deviation than traditionally trained interns for incisor bracket placement (0.174 mm vs 0.233 mm, P < .05). [→ Lai Danlin 2026](#lai-danlin-2026)

## Evidence

### Lai Danlin 2026

Lai Danlin, Lai Baoliang, Xu Liang, Huang Xiaohong. (2026). Virtual Simulation Versus Traditional Training for Orthodontic Bracket Positioning: A Pilot RCT. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109530

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot RCT: 20 orthodontically inexperienced dental interns randomized to traditional (Group A) or virtual simulation (Group B) training, then bracket placement on 3D-printed models measured in Geomagic Studio. Incisor mesiodistal medians were "0.174 mm for Group B and 0.233 mm for Group A (P < .05)"; no effect size printed.

> "the virtual simulation group (Group B) showed a significant reduction in mesiodistal deviation compared with the traditional training group (Group A). The values were 0.174 mm for Group B and 0.233 mm for Group A (P < .05)."
