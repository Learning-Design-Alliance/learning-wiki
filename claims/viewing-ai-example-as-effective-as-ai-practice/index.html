Evidence¶

Benjamin Lira 2025¶

Benjamin Lira, Todd Rogers, Daniel G. Goldstein, Lyle Ungar, and Angela L. Duckworth. (2025). Coach not crutch: Evidence that AI can improve writing skill despite reducing effort. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02880

q4 · i0 · causal · r3

Pre-registered replication and extension (N=2,003, Prolific) replacing no-practice with an example-only condition. Participants who merely saw an example "improved as much as participants who had practiced with the AI tool" (d=.03, p=.883).

"they improved as much as participants who had practiced with the AI tool (and could edit its output,d= .03,p= .883)"

Benjamin Lira 2025 (2)¶

Benjamin Lira, Todd Rogers, Daniel G. Goldstein, Lyle Ungar, and Angela L. Duckworth. (2025). Coach not crutch: Evidence that AI can improve writing skill despite reducing effort. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02880

q4 · i0 · causal · r3

One-day follow-up subsample (n=633 of n=800 recontacted). Both AI conditions "continued to outperform" practice without AI, and the example-only and AI-practice conditions did not differ from each other (d=.02, p=.830). Condition × time interactions were non-significant (ps>.96).

"Participants who had practiced with the AI tool (d= .29,p= .006) and participants who had simply seen an AI example (d= .32,p= .003), both continued to outperform those who had practiced without the tool"
