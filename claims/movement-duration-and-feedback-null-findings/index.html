Sattelmayer Martin 2016¶

Sattelmayer Martin, Elsig Simone, Hilfiker Roger, Baer Gillian. (2016). A systematic review and meta-analysis of selected motor learning principles in physiotherapy and medical education. BMC Medical Education, 16, 15. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0538-z

Meta-analysis of four RCTs of mental practice on movement duration, 181 participants, post-acquisition tests. The pooled estimate was 0.00 SMD (95 % CI -0.29 to 0.30) and not statistically significant (p: 0.98), with low heterogeneity (I2: 0 %).

"In total 181 participants were analysed for this outcome. The pooled estimate was 0.00 SMD with a 95 % CI between -0.29 and 0.30. The result was statistically not significant (p: 0.98) and heterogeneity was low (I2: 0 %)."
