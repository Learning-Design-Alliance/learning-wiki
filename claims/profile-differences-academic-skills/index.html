Amzalag M 2026¶

Amzalag M, Zviel-Girshin R and Beimel D (2026) The illusion of cognitive independence in the age of generative AI: unpacking GenAI literacy and learner profiles. Front. Educ. 11:1861548. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1861548

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

One-way ANOVA across the three learner profiles on self-perceived academic skills. The article reports Cluster 1 "reported significantly higher perceived enhancement in problem-solving skills" with η2 = 0.08; outcomes are self-reported perceptions.

"For problem solving and task execution [F(2, 820) = 35.00, p < .001, η2 = 0.08], Cluster 1 (M = 3.57) reported significantly higher perceived enhancement in problem-solving skills compared to Cluster 2 (M = 3.26) and Cluster 3 (M = 3.29)."

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

One-way ANOVA on perceived cognitive endurance across clusters, with Cluster 1 significantly outperforming Clusters 2 and 3 (η2 = 0.05). The measure is self-perceived skill change, not an objective performance test.

"Cluster 1 (M = 3.36) demonstrated the highest levels of cognitive endurance [F(2, 820) = 21.40, p < .001, η2= 0.05], significantly outperforming Clusters 2 and 3."
