Evidence¶

Dana Opre 2023¶

Dana Opre, Andrei Costea, Răzvan Jurchiș, Romiță Iucu. (2023). Enhancing Self-Regulated Learning: Immediate and Follow-up Effects of Cognitive Prompts. Journal of Educational Sciences, XXIV, 2(48). https://doi.org/10.35923/JES.2023.2.16

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation of the Experiment B follow-up data: even high-quality deep strategies had no consistent long-term effect. They attribute this to retention requiring processes such as memory and fluency, supported by spacing and retesting, whereas their prompts mainly supported understanding and sense-making.

"These findings indicate that prompting strategies for activating deep learning would not be sufficient for preserving a learning advantage in the long run."
