Meta-analysis covering the two studies reporting neonatal hypoxic ischemic encephalopathy. Certainty of evidence was downgraded to very low because of imprecision and inconsistency; the two studies differed in design and intervention.

> "In the one RCT 16 an OR of 3.20 (95% CI 0.77–13.30) was found, whereas in the one observational study 19 an OR of 0.50 (95% CI 0.26–0.96) was reported."
