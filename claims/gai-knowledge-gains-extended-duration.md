### Li Juan 2025

Effectiveness of generative artificial intelligence-based teaching versus traditional teaching methods in medical education: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07750-2

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Predetermined subgroup analysis of knowledge scores by learning duration across the included RCTs. The ≥1 week subgroup showed a pooled effect size of SMD = 0.64 (95% CI 0.09–1.19, p = 0.02), versus −0.34 (95% CI −1.26 to 0.58) for <1 week; subgroup-difference p = 0.07.
