SSQ administered after the lab session; Table I reports nausea 3.18 (SD 4.77), oculomotor 17.69 (SD 17.78), disorientation 10.83 (SD 15.21), total "12.88" (SD 13.5). Descriptive scores only; no effect size is printed.
