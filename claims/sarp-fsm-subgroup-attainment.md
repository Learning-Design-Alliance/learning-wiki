## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Both FSM (g = +0.22) and non-FSM (g = +0.08) subgroups made more progress than controls, but neither effect was statistically significant and the FSM*intervention interaction was not significant for the primary outcome. [→ Maxwell et al. 2014](#maxwell-et-al-2014)

## Evidence

### Maxwell et al. 2014

Dr Bronwen Maxwell, Professor Paul Connolly, Sean Demack, Dr Liam O'Hare, Anna Stevens, Lucy Clague. (2014). Summer Active Reading Programme: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581463

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the primary reading comprehension outcome in the multilevel models: FSM subgroup effect +0.22 (-0.24, +0.69) and non-FSM +0.08 (-0.23, +0.40), both reported as not statistically significant in the executive summary table.

> "Whilst the FSM*intervention interaction term was not found to be s tatistically significant for the primary outcome, it was significant for the both of the secondary outcomes."
