Theoretical argument in the article's literature review, attributed to MacWilliam (1986): the priority stressed is "that of listening (input), over that of speaking (output)". The article offers no new data for this position.

> "as viewed by some persons, the phase of speaking should stay delayed until the listening comprehension has finished taking place (MacWilliam, 1986). The Priority that tends to be stressed here happens to be that of listening (input), over that of speaking (output)."
