Review synthesis of facilitation reporting across the 41 included studies. The review concludes facilitation is an important but under-recognized aspect of IPE and that future descriptions of educational interventions should provide specific details to guide best practices.

> "Only nine of the 41 studies documented the numbers of facilitators involved in training sessions and the participants’ perspectives on the importance of facilitators [13, 17, 18, 41, 47]."
