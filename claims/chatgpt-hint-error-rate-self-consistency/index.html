Evidence¶

Pardos ZA 2024¶

Pardos ZA, Bhandari S (2024) ChatGPT-generated help produces learning gains equivalent to human tutor-authored help on mathematics skills. PLoS ONE 19(5): e0304013. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0304013

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Application of self-consistency (10 samples per question, modal answer kept) evaluated by the same rater procedure. The article concludes "self-consistency helps reduce ChatGPT's error rate, with algebra topics reaching close to 0% error rates"; no effect size is printed.

"This approach, called self-consistency and detailed in the Methods section, resulted in an Elementary Algebra error of 0%, Intermediate Algebra error of 2%, College Algebra error of 2%, and Statistics error of 13%."
