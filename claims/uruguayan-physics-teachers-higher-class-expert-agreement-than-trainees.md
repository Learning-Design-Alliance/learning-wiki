Cross-sectional CLASS survey of Uruguayan secondary physics teachers and first-year physics teacher-training students, analyzed by categorized responses. The article reports that "The average of categorized responses is 80% for teachers and 67% for students", with teachers' category averages spanning a narrower range than students'.
