## Evidence

### Wee H.G. 2022

Wee H.G., Yang Z., Xing L., Tsang K.K.-T., Ruan H.D., Li Y. (2022). Nighttime Sleep Awakening Frequency and Its Consistency Predict Future Academic Performance in College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19052933

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of consistency of daily self-reported sleep quality (0-10 scale) in the 45-student sample. The printed effect size was r = ŌłÆ0.323 with future GPA; inconsistent sleep quality went with lower future GPA and "is not associated with lower immediate academic achievement, but long-term academic achievement". The correlation remained significant controlling for current GPA (r = ŌłÆ0.326, p = 0.031).

> "we found that self-reported sleep quality was negatively correlated with the future GPA (r = ŌłÆ0.323, p = 0.031; Figure 3), but not with the current GPA (r = ŌłÆ0.237, p = 0.117), indicating that inconsistent sleep time in a period is not associated with lower immediate academic achievement, but long-term academic achievement."
