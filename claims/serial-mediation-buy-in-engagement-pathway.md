# A significant serial mediation pathway runs from exposure to active learning to engagement via persuasion, identification, and commitment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Exposure predicted persuasion (b = 0.70, p < 0.001), persuasion predicted identification (b = 0.52, p < 0.001), identification predicted commitment (b = 0.57, p < 0.001), and commitment predicted engagement, with an indirect exposure→engagement path (b = 0.05, LLCI = 0.009, ULCI = 0.089). [→ Cavanagh A. J. 2016](#cavanagh-a-j-2016)

## Evidence

### Cavanagh A. J. 2016

Cavanagh A. J., Aragón O. R., Chen X., Couch B., Durham M., Bobrownicki A., Hanauer D. I., Graham M. J. (2016). Student Buy-In to Active Learning in a College Science Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 15(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-07-0212

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the same course survey sample, a serial mediation model (Hayes, 2013) tested the four EPIC stages sequentially. The indirect exposure→engagement pathway "did not cross zero" (b = 0.05, LLCI = 0.009, ULCI = 0.089), and each stage's main effect was reported significant.

> "Serial mediation analyses did reveal a significant pathway from exposure to engagement via persuasion, identification, and commitment. The lower and upper limits of the confidence interval for the exposure→engagement pathway did not cross zero (b = 0.05, lower level confidence interval [LLCI] = 0.009, upper level confidence interval [ULCI] = 0.089), indicating a statistically significant pathway."
