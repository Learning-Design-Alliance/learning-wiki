## Evidence

### Bruijns BA 2022

Bruijns BA, Vanderloo LM, Johnson AM, Adamo KB, Burke SM, Carson V, Heydon R, Irwin JD, Naylor P-J, Timmons BW, Tucker P. (2022). Change in pre- and in-service early childhood educators’ knowledge, self-efficacy, and intentions following an e-learning course in physical activity and sedentary behaviour: a pilot study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-022-12591-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post pilot study of the ~5 h TEACH e-Learning course; knowledge measured with a 30-item questionnaire before and immediately after course completion, analyzed with paired samples t-tests. The article reports both groups "significantly increased their total knowledge scores" with p < .05; no effect size is printed.

> "Both pre- (n = 32) and in-service (n = 121) ECEs significantly increased their total knowledge scores from pre- to post-course completion (p < .05*)."
