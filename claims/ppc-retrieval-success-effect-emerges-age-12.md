# A graded left superior parietal retrieval-success effect emerges by age 12 but is absent in 8-year-olds

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a DRM-paradigm fMRI study, graded activation in left superior parietal cortex (BA 7) during retrieval was observed among 12-year-olds and adults but not among 8-year-olds. [→ Ghetti Simona; Bunge Silvia A 2012](#ghetti-simona-bunge-silvia-a-2012)

## Evidence

### Ghetti Simona; Bunge Silvia A 2012

Ghetti Simona; Bunge Silvia A. (2012). Neural Changes Underlying the Development of Episodic Memory During Middle Childhood. Developmental cognitive neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2012.05.002

`q2 · i?`

Developmental fMRI study using the Deese-Roediger-McDermott paradigm (Paz-Alonso et al., 2008, as reviewed) comparing 8-year-olds, 12-year-olds, and adults. The authors interpret the absence in 8-year-olds as consistent with increasing specialization of episodic-retrieval regions during childhood.

> "we showed a retrieval success effect with a pattern of graded activation in left superior parietal cortex (BA 7) among 12-year olds and adults, but not among 8-year-olds."
