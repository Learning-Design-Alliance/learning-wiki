### Initial Accomplishments of Implementation Networks: A Baseline Technical Report of the Chicago Annenberg Research Project 1998

Initial Accomplishments of Implementation Networks: A Baseline Technical Report of the Chicago Annenberg Research Project. (1998). https://consortium.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Principal survey measure of access to resources for school improvement. The report found "about 40 percent report that participation has provided few if any useful resources to their schools," while most reported moderate or high access to network-provided resources and partner in-kind services.

> "While a majority of principals report that participation in implementation networks has provided useful resources for school improvement, about 40 percent report that participation has provided few if any useful resources to their schools."

Principal survey item on the place of Annenberg among school programs. "Approximately three-quarters of principals indicated that Annenberg is only one of many other programs they have at their schools," a condition the authors say creates potential for fragmentation and competition for teachers' time and attention.
