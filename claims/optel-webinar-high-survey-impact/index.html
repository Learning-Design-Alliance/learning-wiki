Strengthening English Learner Reclassification in California 2024¶

Strengthening English Learner Reclassification in California. (2024). Region 15 Comprehensive Center at WestEd, U.S. Department of Education grant #S283B190053. https://nces.ed.gov/programs/coe/indicator/cgf

q2 · i? · design · r2

Feedback survey results collected as part of a five-year evaluation of R15CC technical assistance by the Utah Education Policy Center. The evaluation reports that "92 percent of participants reported increased knowledge" after the statewide webinars. Percentages are descriptive self-reports; no effect size is printed.

"Post-session surveys showed high impact: 92 percent of participants reported increased knowledge, 91 percent found the webinars beneficial, and 88 percent felt they were well-designed."
