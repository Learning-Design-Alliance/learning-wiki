# Students most preferred the student-prepared aids testing condition

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an end-of-semester preference questionnaire, most students indicated they preferred the student-prepared aids condition (n = 36, 38.3%), followed by instructor-prepared aids (n = 23, 24.5%) and open textbook (n = 20, 21.3%); the no-aid condition was least preferred (n = 15, 16%).
