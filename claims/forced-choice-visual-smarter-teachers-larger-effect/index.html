Xin Sun 2023¶

Xin Sun, Owen Norton and Shaylene E. Nancekivell. (2023). Beware the myth: learning styles affect parents', children's, and teachers' thinking about children's academic potential. npj Science of Learning 8:46. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00190-x

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Experiment 2 mixed binary logistic regression with 79 parents and 94 teachers making forced-choice smarter/sportier selections. "The main effect of the question type was signi ﬁcant", odds ratio = 4.99; participants picked the visual learner as smarter and the hands-on learner as sportier.

"The main effect of the question type was signi ﬁcant, b = 1.61, 95% CI = [1.29, 1.92], z = 9.95, p < 0.001, odds ratio = 4.99."

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Same Experiment 2 model. "a signi ﬁcant interaction between the question and group", odds ratio = 0.65; as shown in Fig. 2, teachers showed a larger effect than parents. There was no main effect of group (p = 0.126).

"But there was a signi ﬁcant interaction between the question and group, b = –0.43, 95% CI = [–0.74, –0.11], z = –2.63, p = 0.008, odds ratio = 0.65."
