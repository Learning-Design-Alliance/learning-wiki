## Evidence

### Shewach 2019

Shewach, O. R., Sackett, P. R., & Quint, S. (2019). Stereotype Threat Effects in Settings With Features Likely Versus Unlikely in Operational Test Settings: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Applied Psychology, 104(12), 1514–1534. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/apl0000420

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis using Nguyen and Ryan's typology of subtle, moderately explicit, and blatant activation cues. The article states "Both moderately explicit and blatant cues had a significantly stronger threat effect than subtle cues"; no test statistic for the contrast is printed in the quoted passage.

> "In the overall sample, subtle cues produced the weak- est threat effect (d ⫽ ⫺.17, k ⫽ 63, N ⫽ 4,149, SD␦ ⫽ .30), followed by moderately explicit cues (d ⫽ ⫺.38, k ⫽ 51, N ⫽ 2,766, SD␦ ⫽ .48), with blatant cues producing a comparable threat effect as moderately explicit cues (d ⫽ ⫺.41, k ⫽ 62, N ⫽ 3,371, SD␦ ⫽ .31)."
