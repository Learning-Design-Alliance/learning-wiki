Related Claims¶

  • Reviewed studies consistently raise concerns about AI accuracy, overreliance, academic integrity, and reduced critical engagement in Arab EL2 contexts — related
  • Across 11 reviewed Arab-university EL2 studies (2023–2025), learners and educators report overwhelmingly positive perceptions of AI-assisted language learning — related
  • Empirical gains from AI tools in Arab EL2 classrooms are most consistent for surface-level outcomes, while higher-order writing quality and speaking proficiency show mixed results contingent on teacher mediation — related
  • The evidence base on AI in Arab EL2 classrooms is limited by perception-based designs, varied samples, and insufficient reporting of AI versions and implementation — related
  • Technology implementation in higher education depends on individual initiative rather than institutional systems, with governance structures rarely including those responsible for implementation — related
  • Institutions have expanded student access to generative AI tools faster than they have developed policies, guidance, and shared learning goals for their use — related
