The article's project-experience account (type e) states that hemisphere models with stars in relief and constellation lines serve this function for BVI users, while also helping sighted viewers "to identify and to put them in the context of the whole sky". No measured outcome is reported.

> "These models help BVI to establish a spatial mental image of the distribution of these elements on the sky, but can also help people able to see the same figures projected onto a screen"
