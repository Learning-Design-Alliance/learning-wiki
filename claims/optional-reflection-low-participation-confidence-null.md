Participation and self-confidence analysis in Study 2's CS1 field experiment: of 1068 enrolled students, 112 clicked the optional link. The article reports "We did not find any differences in self-confidence before and after the reflection exercise", attributing this possibly to insufficient students per condition.

> "The engagement rate with the reflection component was low. Only 10.48% of the students who were enrolled at the time clicked on the link to participate in self-reflection."
