Evidence¶

Monika Geist 2017¶

Monika Geist. (2017). Noticing grammar in L2 writing and problem-solving strategies. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 7(3), 471-487. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2017.7.3.6

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative think-aloud study of ten German teenage learners composing an English paragraph; coding of language related episodes found "the majority (119) were related to lexis whereas only 36 were related to grammar."

"Of the 188 LREs produced by the ten participants, the majority (119) were related to lexis whereas only 36 were related to grammar."

Related Claims¶

  • Review-reported experiment found output performed noticing and hypothesis-testing functions but no apparent metalinguistic function in L2 writing — the general claim this finding bears on, with the studies behind it
  • While producing L2 paragraphs, an EFL learner's self-noticed problems were overwhelmingly lexical (about 79%) rather than grammatical (about 21%) — related
  • Notetaking as a feedback tool facilitates resolution of lexical problems but leaves grammatical problems unresolved, suggesting reformulation as an alternative — related
  • Comparing original texts to model paragraphs prompted noticing that was again focused on lexical features — related
  • Learners resolve grammar-related noticing with intuition, reasoning, rephrasing and explicit rules rather than external resources — related
  • Learners' grammar noticing centers more on syntax than morphology, with syntax issues encountered by all participants — related
  • Syntactical noticing focuses mainly on sentence length and ways of connecting clauses — related
  • Morphological noticing focuses mainly on verb forms, especially modals, and on preposition choice — related
  • Rewriting after noticing gaps and comparing to models produced modified output that solved more lexical gaps than grammatical problems — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Whether conceptual discrepancies are noticed (or not) and how they are resolved can be traced through concurrent think-aloud protocols and retrospective interviews — related
