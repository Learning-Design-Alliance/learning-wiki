`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Corpus-configuration analysis for RQ2 across 59 reports. The review found collaboration "treated collaboration as an explicit design component" in 9 of 59 reports and a collaborative outcome measured in 5; a sensitivity audit with a wider term list gave nearly identical figures (16.2%/13.6% designed, 8.1%/9.1% measured).

> "only nine of the 59 reports (15.3%) treated collaboration as an explicit design component through role structures, collaboration scripts, positive-interdependence tasks, or a collaborative problem-solving model, and only five (8.5%) measured a collaborative or communicative outcome."
