`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

CSAC recipient survey (Figure 3) reports self-reported perceptions: "Nearly 3 in 4 (74%) respondents said the grant was influential or very influential in their decision to pursue teaching", with 93% citing importance for completing preparation and 69% for teaching in a priority school.

> "Nearly 3 in 4 (74%) respondents said the grant was influential or very influential in their decision to pursue teaching.
