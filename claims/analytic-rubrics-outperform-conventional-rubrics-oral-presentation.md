## Evidence

### Rob J. Nadolski 2021

Rob J. Nadolski, Hans G. K. Hummel, Ellen Rusman, Kevin Ackermans. (2021). Rubric formats for the formative assessment of oral presentation skills acquisition in secondary education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10030-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures mixed ANOVA with 158 first-year Dutch secondary students randomized by class to three conditions. Between-subjects tests showed both experimental groups outperforming the control group, with "F(2, 156) = 118.97, p < 0.01, ηp2 = 0.59". A Bonferroni-corrected post-hoc analysis confirmed experimental groups significantly (p < 0.01) outperformed the control group at both moments.

> "Tests of between-subjects effects showed significant differences between conditions, with F(2, 156) = 118.97, p < 0.01, ηp2 = 0.59, and both experimental groups outperforming the control group as expected (so we could accept H1)."
