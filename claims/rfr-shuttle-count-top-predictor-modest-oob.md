### Bielik Viktor 2025

Bielik Viktor, Nosáľ Vladimír, Nechalová Libuša, Špánik Milan, Žilková Katarína, Grendar Marian. (2025). The prediction model of academic achievement based on cardiorespiratory fitness and BMI status for ninth-grade students. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-024-05353-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Random Forest Regression trained on the entire dataset of 6,530 ninth-graders from 341 Slovak schools, with VIMP used to rank predictors. The article reports the number of shuttles as the top predictor, with out-of-bag R2 of 0.12 for Mathematics and 0.13 for Slovak language, described as modest.

> "The RFR algorithm has identified the number of shuttles as the most important predictor of the academic achievement both in Mathematics and Slovak language (Fig. 1)."
