Evidence¶

Szucs Denes 2013¶

Szucs Denes, Devine Amy, Soltesz Fruzsina, Nobes Alison, Gabriel Florence. (2013). Developmental dyscalculia is related to visuo-spatial memory and inhibition impairment. Cortex, 49(10). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cortex.2013.06.007

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Across five experiments (animal Stroop, numerical and physical size Stroop, non-symbolic magnitude comparison, Stop-signal task) in 12 DD vs 12 controls, DD showed larger congruency/facilitation effects and poorer correct rejections, interpreted as "failure to inhibit the task-irrelevant stimulus dimension".

"Overall, our data from five different experiments suggests that DD were more susceptible to the effect of task-irrelevant information than controls."
