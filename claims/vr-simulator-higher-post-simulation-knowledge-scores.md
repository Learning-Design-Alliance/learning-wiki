## Evidence

### Gosavi A 2025

Gosavi A, Kanneganti A, Khoo ET, Singh K, Shen L, Rauff M, Liu C, Choolani M. (2025). Virtual reality simulation training for childbirth: A cluster randomized crossover study. Int J Gynecol Obstet. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70053

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized crossover trial of 111 fourth-year medical students in 20 clinical groups; two-sample t-test compared improvement in pre- and post-simulation percentage correct scores between modalities. The VR group scored "79.0% vs. 54.0%; P < 0.001". No effect size was printed.

> "After phase 1, the VR simulation group had a significantly higher post‐simulation mean percentage correct score than the mannequin simulation group (79.0% vs. 54.0%; P < 0.001) with an average improvement in mean percentage correct scores of 40.8% versus 15.1% (P < 0.001)."
