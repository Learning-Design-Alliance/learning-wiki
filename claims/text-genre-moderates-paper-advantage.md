## Evidence

### Delgado 2018

Delgado, P., Vargas, C., Ackerman, R., & Salmerón, L. (2018). Don't throw away your printed books: A meta-analysis on the effects of reading media on reading comprehension. Educational Research Review, 25, 23-38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2018.09.003

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

ANOVA moderator analysis of text genre on between-participants effect sizes (informational k = 34, narrative k = 7, mixed k = 10). Printed effect sizes were Hedge's g = -0.27 (informational), Hedge's g = -0.30 (mixed), and Hedge's g = 0.01 (narrative-only, no effect). Genre explained 31% of the mean effect size variance (R2 = .31).

> "Comparisons conducted with informational texts or a combination of informational and narrative texts showed significant mean effect sizes favouring paper-based reading over digital-based reading (Hedge's g = -0.27 and -0.30, respectively), whereas comparisons conducted only with narrative texts showed no effect of media (Hedge's g = 0.01)"
