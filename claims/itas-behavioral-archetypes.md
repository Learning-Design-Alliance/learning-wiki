Qualitative archetype analysis of pilot deployment data across video playback, code execution, chat, checkpoint, and session events. The article describes the self-directed, tutor-reliant, late engager, and passive consumer archetypes; the passive consumer never signals confusion and was detected only by the analytics agent.

> "A controlled demonstration cannot produce a late engager. A single-session test cannot produce a passive consumer detectable only through longitudinal data."
