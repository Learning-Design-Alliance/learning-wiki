# Six months after training, retention of knowledge and clinical skills did not differ significantly between the 7-day and 11-day IMNCI groups

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Knowledge decline from post-training to six-month re-assessment did not differ significantly between the 7-day group (−12.4, 95% CI −18.5–6.4) and the 11-day group (−6.4, 95% CI −10.5–2.3), p = 0.094. [→ Ariff Shabina 2021](#ariff-shabina-2021)
`q2 i?` Clinical skills decline from post-training to six-month re-assessment did not differ significantly between the 7-day group (−6.3, 95% CI −11.3–1.3) and the 11-day group (−9.1, 95% CI −11.5–6.6), p = 0.308. [→ Ariff Shabina 2021 (2)](#ariff-shabina-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Ariff Shabina 2021

Ariff Shabina, Sadiq Kamran, Jiwani Uswa, Ahmed Khalil, Nuzhat Khadija, Ahmed Shakeel, Nizami Qamruddin, Khan Iqtidar A., Ali Nabeela, Soofi Sajid Bashir, Bhutta Zulfiqar A. (2021). Evaluation the Effectiveness of Abridged IMNCI (7-Day) Course v Standard (11-Day) Course in Pakistan. Maternal and Child Health Journal. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10995-021-03276-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Six-month re-assessment of the same quasi-experimental cohorts; knowledge retention (post-training minus re-assessment) was analysed for 27 versus 30 providers. Table 4 reports declines of "− 12.4 (15.2)" versus "− 6.4 (10.9)", p = 0.094, not significant.

> "Retention Knowledge 27 − 12.4 (15.2) − 18.5–6.4 30 − 6.4 (10.9) − 10.5–2.3 0.094"

### Ariff Shabina 2021 (2)

Ariff Shabina, Sadiq Kamran, Jiwani Uswa, Ahmed Khalil, Nuzhat Khadija, Ahmed Shakeel, Nizami Qamruddin, Khan Iqtidar A., Ali Nabeela, Soofi Sajid Bashir, Bhutta Zulfiqar A. (2021). Evaluation the Effectiveness of Abridged IMNCI (7-Day) Course v Standard (11-Day) Course in Pakistan. Maternal and Child Health Journal. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10995-021-03276-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Six-month re-assessment; clinical skills retention was analysed for 26 versus 31 providers. Table 4 reports a decline of "− 6.3 (12.4)" in the 7-day group versus "− 9.1 (6.7)" in the 11-day group, p = 0.308, not significant.

> "Clinical skills 26 − 6.3 (12.4) − 11.3–1.3 31 − 9.1 (6.7) − 11.5–6.6 0.308"
