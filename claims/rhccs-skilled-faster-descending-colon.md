## Evidence

### Rohr A. 2022

Rohr A., Perrenot C., Pitta A., Celerier I., Labrousse M., Renard Y., Cadiot G., Brugel M. (2022). Reperfused human cadaver as a new simulation model for colonoscopy: a pilot study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-022-09763-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective monocentric feasibility study; 24 participants (11 skilled, 5 intermediates, 8 novices) each performed one colonoscopy on the cadaver model. The Results section reports descending colon reaching rates and times by group, with "significantly shorter for skilled participants (both p < .01)".

> "They reached DC more frequently (100% for skilled, 80% for intermediates and 50% for novices, p = 0.018). The median time to reach RSJ (59, 272 and 686 s for skilled, intermediates and novices group, respectively) and DC (90, 534 and 1360 s for skilled, intermediates and novices) was significantly shorter for skilled participants (both p < .01)."
