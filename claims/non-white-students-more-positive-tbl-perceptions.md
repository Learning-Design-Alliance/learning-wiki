## Evidence

### Drovandi Aaron 2026

Drovandi Aaron, Sheader Elizabeth, Kingston Margaret. (2026). Integrated Team-Based Learning in a UK Undergraduate Medical Programme. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70470

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional survey comparing Likert-scale ratings between participant ethnicity using the Mann–Whitney U nonparametric test with Bonferroni adjustments (n = 253). Findings show 'non-White' students had more positive perceptions of TBL overall (p = 0.008) and across several domains.

> "‘non‐White’ students had more positive perceptions of TBL overall (p = 0.008), resource relevance to sessions (p = 0.001), clarity of requirements before attending the Friday session (p = 0.007), the value of varied teaching sessions (p = 0.003) and understanding materials to the depth required (p < 0.001)."
