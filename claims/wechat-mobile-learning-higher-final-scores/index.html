Dai et al. 2018¶

Guiyu Dai, Yang Liu & Shanmeng Cui. (2018). A Study on the Mobile Learning of English and American Literature Based on Wechat Public Account. English Language Teaching, 11(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n6p47

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The second control experiment (final test) compared the same two groups. The experiment group's mean of 91.112 exceeded the control group's 88.167, and the t-test confirmed a statistically significant difference at the 0.05 level.

"The 2-tailed significance of the t-test for Equality of Means is 0.041, less than 0.05 (95% Confidence Interval), which shows in the Table 6."
