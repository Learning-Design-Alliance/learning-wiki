# Confirmatory factor analysis in an independent sample supports the five-factor correlated model over collapsed and one-factor alternatives

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In Study 2 (n = 798), the five-factor correlated CFA model showed acceptable fit (CFI = .954, RMSEA = .047) and fit substantially better than the three-factor collapsed and one-factor models. [→ Nie J 2026](#nie-j-2026)

## Evidence

### Nie J 2026

Nie J, Zhang Z, Lu X, Zhang Y and Zhang M (2026) Development and validation of the generative AI assessment literacy scale for higher education students: psychometric evidence and associations with feedback engagement and academic integrity. Front. Educ. 11:1934632. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1934632

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Study 2 confirmatory factor analysis with 798 students using robust maximum likelihood in Mplus 8.3. Model comparison showed the "three-factor collapsed model and the one-factor model fitted the data substantially less well"; the one-factor model reached CFI = .738.

> "The hypothesized five-factor correlated model showed good fit to the data, χ2(125) = 344.72, χ2/df = 2.76, CFI = .954, TLI = .944, RMSEA = .047, and SRMR = .042."
