# In Experiment 2, actual regulation of adaptable heuristic worked examples showed no significant correlation with self-regulation skills or MAS outcomes

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Self-regulation skills correlated non-significantly positively with frequency of choosing maximal worked examples (r = 0.12). [→ Vogel 2022](#vogel-2022)
`q2 i0` Adaptation frequency showed no significant correlation with pre- or post-test socio-discursive or domain-specific MAS. [→ Vogel 2022 (2)](#vogel-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Vogel 2022

Vogel, F., Kollar, I., Fischer, F., Reiss, K., & Ufer, S. (2022). Adaptable scaffolding of mathematical argumentation skills: The role of self-regulation when scaffolded with CSCL scripts and heuristic worked examples. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09363-z

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations in Experiment 2 between frequency of adapting to maximum worked examples (M=8.72, SD=4.34, range 1–17) and measures. Self-regulation correlation "non-significant" (r=0.12, p=0.51), a negligible magnitude.

> "The correlation analyses revealed a non-significant positive correlation between self-regulation skills and learners' actual regulation of the adaptable heuristic worked examples (r = 0.12, p = 0.51)."

### Vogel 2022 (2)

Vogel, F., Kollar, I., Fischer, F., Reiss, K., & Ufer, S. (2022). Adaptable scaffolding of mathematical argumentation skills: The role of self-regulation when scaffolded with CSCL scripts and heuristic worked examples. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09363-z

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 correlation analyses between adaptation frequency and MAS measures showed negligible, non-significant domain-specific correlations (pre-test r=0.03, post-test r=-0.08); socio-discursive correlations (r=-0.02; r=0.09) were also non-significant.

> "Likewise, for the domain-specific MAS the adaptation showed no significant correlation with the pre-test (r = 0.03, p = 0.89) nor the post-test (r = -0.08, p = 0.66)."
