Evidence¶

Hosna Hosseini & Zargham Ghabanchi 2014¶

Hosna Hosseini & Zargham Ghabanchi. (2014). The Effect of Portfolio Assessment on EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension and Motivation. English Language Teaching, 7(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n5p110

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pretest-treatment-posttest study of 65 female translation students in two intact classes; the experimental group (n=33) received portfolio-based instruction. Posttest means were 14.09 (experimental) vs 10.68 (control), and the article reports the difference as "statistically significant (T=5.225, P=0.000<0.05)".

"The independent t-test was run to compare the mean scores of the experimental and the control groups. As shown, the difference between the two groups was statistically significant (T=5.225, P=0.000<0.05)."
