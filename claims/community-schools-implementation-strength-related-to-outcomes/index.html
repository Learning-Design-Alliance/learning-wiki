Germain 2025 (2)¶

Germain, E., Maier, A., Espinoza, D., & Oakes, J. (2025). Community schools certification: An approach for implementation. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/220.412

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The research review reports that recent research finds "longer and more sustained implementation is associated with more significant outcomes," noting early impacts like attendance can grow over time while academic impacts emerge later. No effect sizes are printed.

"Recent research provides additional insight into the relationship between community schools implementation and outcomes, finding that longer and more sustained implementation is associated with more significant outcomes."
