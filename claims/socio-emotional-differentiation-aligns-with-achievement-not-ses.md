## Evidence

### Rémeau Margot 2026

Rémeau Margot, Raffy Gaël, Borst Grégoire. (2026). Academic Resilience in Contexts of Inequality: Motivational and Self‐Efficacy Profiles of Disadvantaged High Achievers. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1002/jad.70190

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

The fine‐grained SES × achievement classification across nine profiles in the French panel showed that "within each SES tertile, high‐achieving students, regardless of their socioeconomic background, exhibited higher levels of self‐efficacy and intrinsic motivation than their lower‐achieving peers," indicating socio‐emotional differentiation tracks achievement more than SES.

> "Within each SES tertile, high‐achieving students, regardless of their socioeconomic background, exhibited higher levels of self‐efficacy and intrinsic motivation than their lower‐achieving peers. Resilient learners clustered with high achievers rather than socioeconomically similar lower‐achieving peers, supporting the view of resilience as the alignment of strong performance and adaptive motivation."
