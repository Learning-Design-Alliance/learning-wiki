## Evidence

### Lortie-Forgues 2017

Lortie-Forgues, Hugues and Siegler, Robert S (2017) Conceptual knowledge of decimal arithmetic. Journal of Educational Psychology. pp. 374-391. ISSN: 0022-0620. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000148

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In Study 1, computation accuracy was analyzed alongside direction of effect judgments; "Most students (14 of 19, 74%) who correctly solved both multiplication computation problems" with decimals below one still erred on both parallel direction of effect judgments (division: 9 of 14, 64%).

> "Most students (14 of 19, 74%) who correctly solved both multiplication computation problems involving decimals below one were incorrect on both of the direction of effect judgments on parallel problems."
