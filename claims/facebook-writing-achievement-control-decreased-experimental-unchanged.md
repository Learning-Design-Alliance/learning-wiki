# Writing achievement decreased significantly in the control group but did not change significantly in the Facebook experimental group over the 14-week course

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The control group's writing achievement mean fell from 51.22 to 47.12, a statistically significant decrease. [→ Altunkaya and Topuzkanamis 2018](#altunkaya-and-topuzkanamis-2018)
`q2 i?` The experimental group's writing achievement mean fell from 47.12 to 44.79, with no statistically significant change. [→ Altunkaya and Topuzkanamis 2018 (2)](#altunkaya-and-topuzkanamis-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Altunkaya and Topuzkanamis 2018

Hatice Altunkaya, Ersoy Topuzkanamış. (2018). The Effect of Using Facebook in Writing Education on Writing Achievement, Attitude, Anxiety and Self-efficacy Perception. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2133-2142. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061010

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dependent samples t-test on the control group's essays (n=48), scored with the Writing Expression Evaluation Scale, in this pretest-posttest quasi-experiment. The article reports "a significant difference between these two scores" and concludes achievement decreased in the control group.

> "the control group pre -test mean score was 51.22, and the posttest mean score was 47.12. It was observed that there was a significant difference between these two scores [t(47) = 2.041; p <.05]"

### Altunkaya and Topuzkanamis 2018 (2)

Hatice Altunkaya, Ersoy Topuzkanamış. (2018). The Effect of Using Facebook in Writing Education on Writing Achievement, Attitude, Anxiety and Self-efficacy Perception. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2133-2142. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061010

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dependent samples t-test on the Facebook experimental group's essays (n=48) in the same quasi-experiment. The article reports no significant difference and concludes writing achievement did not change in the experimental group.

> "the experimental group writing achievement pre -test mean score was 47.12, and the posttest mean score was 44.79. It was observed that there was no significant difference between these two scores [t(47)=1.070;p>.05]"
