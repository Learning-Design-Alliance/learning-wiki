## Evidence

### Bordbar Shima 2025

Bordbar Shima, Mirzaei Saeid, Bahmaei Jamshid, Atashbahar Omolbanin, Yusefi Ali Reza. (2025). Predicting students' academic performance based on academic identity, academic excitement, and academic enthusiasm: evidence from a cross-sectional study in a developing country. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07374-6

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression (Enter method) on data from 290 students; the article reports these five standardized β coefficients, all with P < 0.001 in Table 5, as the identity predictors of academic performance.

> "the most important dimensions of academic identity predicting academic performance were academic commitment (β = 0.648), future orientation (β = 0.643), personal agency (β = 0.638), belonging academic (β = 0.631), and self-concept (β = 0.629), respectively"
