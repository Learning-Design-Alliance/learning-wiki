Sound Partners 2010¶

Sound Partners. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Sound Partners. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.wri-edu.org/partners/sound-partners.htm

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The WWC synthesis reports that "the average improvement index for reading fluency is +19 percentile points across the seven studies"; three studies, two with strong designs, found positive and statistically significant fluency effects.

"The average improvement index for reading fluency is +19 percentile points across the seven studies, with a range of +6 to +33 percentile points across findings."
