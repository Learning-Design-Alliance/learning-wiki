Secondary Pearson correlation analysis between number of messages and number of coded mistakes per problem among initially incorrect prompts. Printed coefficients include negligible values such as "r= .01" and weak values up to r = .19; three were non-significant.

> "The results were non-significant, B1-2 (r= .01), B1-3 (r= -.02), and B2-3 (r= .10), or weak, B1-1 (r= .19,p< .05), B2-1 (r= .17,p< .01), and B2-2 (r= .17,p< .05), indicating little evidence to support the number of mistakes influencing the number of prompts needed."
