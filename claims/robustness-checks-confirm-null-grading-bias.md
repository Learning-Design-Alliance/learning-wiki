## Evidence

### Bischoff Carina Saxlund 2021

Bischoff Carina Saxlund, Ejrnæs Anders, Rubin Olivier. (2021). A quasi-experimental study of ethnic and gender bias in university grading. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254422

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Ordered logistic regression addressing the non-equidistant Danish grading scale, plus examiner dummies for the three examiners grading both years (subsample n = 458), robust standard errors, and a 1,000 bootstrap sample; all confirmed the null findings.

> "The results of the analysis confirmed the findings reported above. None of the interaction terms used to detect gender or ethnic bias were significant (see S4 Table)."
