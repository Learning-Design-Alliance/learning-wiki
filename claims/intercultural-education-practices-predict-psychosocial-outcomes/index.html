Evidence¶

Basarkod 2024¶

Basarkod, G., Dicke, T., Allen, K. A., Parker, P. D., Ryan, M., Marsh, H. W., Carrick, Z. T. & Guo, J. (2024). Do intercultural education and attitudes promote student wellbeing and social outcomes? An examination across PISA countries. Learning and Instruction, 91. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101879

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Multilevel linear regressions on PISA 2018 data (students nested in schools and countries) examine associations between intercultural factors and psychosocial outcomes; the article reports that "Student-reported intercultural education practies positively predicted" these four outcomes. No effect sizes are printed.
