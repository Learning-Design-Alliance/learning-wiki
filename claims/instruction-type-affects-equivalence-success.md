## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Instruction had a significant effect on success on both explanation and paper-and-pencil posttests, with analogy and procedure groups succeeding on more problems than the feedback group. [→ Alibali 1999](#alibali-1999)

## Evidence

### Alibali 1999

Alibali, M. W. (1999). How children change their minds: Strategy change can be gradual or abrupt. Developmental Psychology, 35(1), 127-145. https://doi.org/10.1037/0012-1649.35.1.127

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A between-participants experiment with 143 children randomly assigned to five intervention groups. ANOVA showed significant instruction effects on both posttests. Dunnett's tests showed analogy and procedure groups exceeded feedback group on both tests.

> "Instruction had a significant effect on success, F(4, 138) = 22.97, p < .001, for the explanation test; F(4, 138) = 11.77, p < .001, for the paper-and-pencil test"
