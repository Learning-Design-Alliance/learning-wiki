Evidence¶

Jo Tondeur 2025¶

Jo Tondeur, Ottavia Trevisan, Sarah K. Howard, Johan van Braak. (2025). Preparing preservice teachers to teach with digital technologies: An update of effective SQD-strategies. Computers & Education 232. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105262

q3 · i? · review · r2

Meta-aggregative synthesis for the Collaborative Design statement, aggregating six sub-categories including Instructional Design Models (n=23 studies), many focused on the TPACK framework. The review reports studies emphasised understanding TPACK components and their interplay in instructional design.

"Instructional design models provide preservice teachers with frameworks and tools for pedagogical guidance."

Jo Tondeur 2025 (2)¶

Jo Tondeur, Ottavia Trevisan, Sarah K. Howard, Johan van Braak. (2025). Preparing preservice teachers to teach with digital technologies: An update of effective SQD-strategies. Computers & Education 232. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105262

q3 · i? · review · r2

The Collaboration category (C4) was identified in 20 studies, comprising group work, partnership and digital communication sub-categories; the review reports partnerships with mentors fostered deeper engagement with technology integration.

"All these sub-categories highlight the importance of collaboration among preservice teachers."
