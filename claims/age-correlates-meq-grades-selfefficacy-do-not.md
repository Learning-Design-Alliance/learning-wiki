## Evidence

### Büssing Oliver 2021

Büssing Oliver, Ehlers Jan P., Zupanic Michaela. (2021). The prognostic validity of the formative for the summative MEQ (Modified Essay Questions). GMS Journal for Medical Education 38(6), Doc99. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001495.shtml

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of the combined two-cohort sample (N=88). Age showed a "moderate positive correlation (r=.306, p=.004)" with the MEQ* and r=.307 with the MEQ-1; the article reports no significant correlations for high school grade or self-efficacy (all p>.050).
