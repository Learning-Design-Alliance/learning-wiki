## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Data triangulation with four pupils and four teachers found multimedia was perceived as effective for vocabulary achievement, with two prerequisite factors: adequate facilities and appropriate materials. [→ Jingjit 2015](#jingjit-2015)

## Evidence

### Jingjit 2015

Mathukorn Jingjit. (2015). The Effects of Multimedia Learning on Thai Primary Pupils' Achievement in Size and Depth of Vocabulary Knowledge. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.32. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083500

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative triangulation interviews with four pupils and four English teachers of Thai primary school. Respondents named facilities (computer rooms, computers, competent resources) and materials drawn from the school curriculum as prerequisites; perceptions only, no effect size.

> "However, this study reveal that two prerequisite factors are exists to be met , so that multimedia learning effectively could be applied in the classroom to improve pupils' academic achievem ent in vocabulary knowledge."
