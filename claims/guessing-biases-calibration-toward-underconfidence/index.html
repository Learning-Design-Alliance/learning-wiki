Evidence¶

Vuorre 2021¶

Vuorre, M., & Metcalfe, J. (2021). Measures of relative metacognitive accuracy are confounded with task performance in tasks that permit guessing. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09257-1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Analytical argument in the simulation study's discussion section: because guesses artificially inflate performance while mean judgments track knowledge, a perfectly calibrated person will appear underconfident; the authors illustrate with a chance-level 4AFC participant showing 25% observed underconfidence. They suggest estimating knowledge via Abbott's formula as one remedy.

"Guessing will bias measures of calibration towards underconfidence, such that the lowest-performing individuals will have the strongest guessing-induced underconfidence bias."
