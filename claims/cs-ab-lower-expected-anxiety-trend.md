### Emmanuel Ayedoun 2018

Emmanuel Ayedoun, Yuki Hayashi, Kazuhisa Seta. (2018). Adding Communicative and Affective Strategies to an Embodied Conversational Agent to Enhance Second Language Learners' Willingness to Communicate. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0171-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The ANCOVA on expected anxiety in the pilot study's Phase 1 did not reach the conventional significance threshold: the article reports only "a trend towards significant difference" with F(2, 36) = 3.472, p < .1, and a marginal pairwise difference (p = 0.072). No effect size is printed.

> "There was a trend towards significant difference in participants ' expected anxiety [F(2, 36) = 3.472, p < .1] among the three groups. The post hoc tests showed (Table 7) that Group 1 's anxiety tended to be significantly lower than Group 2 's( p = 0.072 )."
