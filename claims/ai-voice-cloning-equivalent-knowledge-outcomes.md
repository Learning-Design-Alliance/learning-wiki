## Evidence

### Kanzow Philipp 2026

Kanzow Philipp, Fukuzawa Fumitoshi, Wilkinson Jenny, Gavoille Antoine, Subtil Fabien, Nourredine Mikaïl. (2026). Voice Cloning Using AI vs Traditional Audio Recording for Prerecorded Courses in Medical Pedagogy: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/86569

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized controlled trial with 88 fourth- and fifth-year medical students randomized 1:1 to AI voice cloning or traditional audio recording. The primary outcome was total knowledge test score, showing "no significant difference between the 2 groups" (P=.60).

> "The mean total score on the 2 knowledge tests was 51.2 (SD 4.9) points in the AI voice cloning group and 51.8 (SD 6.9) in the audio recording group, with no significant difference between the 2 groups (adjusted mean difference −0.9, 95% CI −2.7 to 4.4; P=.60)."
