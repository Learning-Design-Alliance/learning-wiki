## Evidence

### Brener A 2020

Brener A, Lewnard I, Mackinnon J, Jones C, Lohr N, Konda S, McIntosh J, Kulinski J. (2020). Missed opportunities to prevent cardiovascular disease in women with prior preeclampsia. BMC Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12905-020-01074-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second retrospective chart review using the same inclusion criteria and data collection tool, covering 164 women seen at the same clinics from 12/5/16 to 4/19/17 after teaching sessions and emailed slides. The abstract reports "Education interventions did not significantly change rates of screening documentation (p = 0.36)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "When comparing all clinics pre-intervention and post-intervention (in-person and email), there was no significant difference (all p-values > 0.05)."
