### Taşpınar and Kaya 2016

Şeyda Eraslan Taşpınar, Ali Kaya. (2016). Painting with the Multiple Intelligences: Defining Student Success and Permanence in Art Class. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i7.1684

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Delayed posttest: the same success test was reapplied three weeks after instruction to both groups (experimental n=32, control n=31). Means were 15.87 versus 8.54 and the difference was "statistically significant (t=12.79; P<0.05)"; no effect size is printed.

> "It can be seen when the table is considered that mean permanence score of the students in experimental and control groups is X = 15.87 and X = 8.54, respectively. It was determined according to the results obtained that the difference between experimental and control group is statistically significant (t=12.79; P<0.05)."
