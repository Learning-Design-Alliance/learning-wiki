## Evidence

### O. Coolens 2026

O. Coolens, R. Dubb, A. Kaltwasser, U. Lorenzen, and P. Nydahl. (2026). INPRESS: INvasive PRESSure Monitoring Knowledge and Practice Survey. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70443

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey (convenience sample, Germany, May 2025) of 303 clinicians using a 26-item questionnaire; "n = 21 (7%) answered all questions correctly". Correct-answer rates were similar across experience groups (7% vs. 6%).

> "In total, n = 21 (7%) answered all questions correctly. Participants with more than 5 years of experience answered n = 16 (7%) of all questions correctly, vs. n = 5 (6%) of participants with less than 5 years of experience."
