# Yeager et al. (2016) and Fink et al. (2018) found indeterminate effects on enrollment, credit completion, and chemistry final exam scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r?` · `q3`–`q4`

## Subclaims
`q4 i?` Yeager et al. (2016), an RCT with 3,837 students in the analytic sample, showed indeterminate effects on full-time enrollment (effect size 0.06, p = .32) and completing 12 or more credits (0.06, p = 0.35). [→ Growth Mindset Interventions 2022](#growth-mindset-interventions-2022)
`q3 i?` Fink et al. (2018), a compromised RCT with 565 students, showed an indeterminate effect on General Chemistry final exam score (effect size 0.05, p = .57). [→ Growth Mindset Interventions 2022 (2)](#growth-mindset-interventions-2022-2)
