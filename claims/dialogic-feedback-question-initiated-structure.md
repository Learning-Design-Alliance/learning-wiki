## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Dialogic feedback sequences are initiated by a PD question, after which the resident narrates and the PD elicits further elaboration with in-depth questions. [→ Duitsman 2019](#duitsman-2019)

## Evidence

### Duitsman 2019

Duitsman, M. E., van Braak, M., Stommel, W., ten Kate-Booij, M., de Graaf, J., Fluit, C. R. M. G., & Jaarsma, D. A. D. C. (2019). Using conversation analysis to explore feedback on resident performance. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-019-09887-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Conversation-analytic analysis of dialogic feedback fragments in the eight recorded meetings. The article defines the format: "Dialogic activities are characterized by an initial question, followed by in-depth questions of the program director"; extracts show the open question eliciting elaborate resident responses with minimal PD contributions, closed by a PD-initiated summary.

> "Dialogic activities are characterized by an initial question, followed by in-depth questions of the program director to ask the resident about past performance."
