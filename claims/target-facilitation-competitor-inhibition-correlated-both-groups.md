# Target facilitation and competitor inhibition are strongly correlated in both groups

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Residual target facilitation and competitor inhibition were positively correlated in bilinguals (r = .5, p = .003) and monolinguals (r = .6, p = .001). [→ Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica 2011](#blumenfeld-henrike-k-marian-viorica-2011)

## Evidence

### Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica 2011

Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica. (2011). Bilingualism influences inhibitory control in auditory comprehension. Cognition. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3582323/

`q3 · i3`

Correlation analysis of priming-probe latencies in the same study. "A strong relationship between response latencies on competitor priming probes ... and response latencies on target priming probes ... was found in both bilinguals (r = .5, p = .003) and monolinguals (r = .6, p = .001)".

> "A strong relationship between response latencies on competitor priming probes (measuring residual inhibition of preceding competitors) and response latencies on target priming probes (measuring residual activation of preceding targets) was found in both bilinguals (r = .5, p = .003) and monolinguals (r = .6, p = .001)"
