# School absence mediates health–attainment associations, least for ADHD (7.1%) and most for migraine (72.0%) and BMI (39.9%) (ALSPAC cohort)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mediation analysis in ALSPAC found associations between health and attainment were mediated by school absence for all conditions except asthma, with the mediated percentage lowest for ADHD and highest for migraine and BMI. [→ Hughes 2021](#hughes-2021)

## Evidence

### Hughes 2021

Hughes, A., Wade, K. H., Dickson, M., Rice, F., Davies, A., Davies, N. M. & Howe, L. D. (2021). Common health conditions in childhood and adolescence, school absence, and educational attainment: Mendelian randomization study. npj Science of Learning 6:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00080-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analysis using Stata's paramed package in ALSPAC, decomposing total associations into direct and indirect (via absence) effects. The article reports "Percent of associations mediated was lowest for ADHD" and highest for BMI and migraine, with asthma not mediated.

> "Percent of associations mediated was lowest for ADHD (7.1% at age 10, 8.4% at 13) and highest for BMI (39.9% at age 10, 32.6% at 13) and migraine (72.0% at age 10)."
