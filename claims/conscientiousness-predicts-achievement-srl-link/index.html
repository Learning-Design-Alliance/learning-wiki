Evidence¶

Mathias Allemand 2026¶

Mathias Allemand, Brent W. Roberts, Ulrich Trautwein. (2026). Integrating Insights from Volitional Personality Change Interventions to Improve Self-Regulated Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10127-6

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review attributes this to prior meta-analytic work (Mammadov, 2022; Poropat, 2009), reporting that conscientiousness "reflects a propensity toward self-control, goal orientation, planning, and adherence to rules" and exhibits the strongest conceptual and empirical links to SRL among the Big Five.

"Conscientiousness is also a robust and consistent predictor of academic performance and achievement, even after accounting for cognitive ability (Mammadov, 2022; Poropat, 2009), underscoring its value as a key target for educational intervention efforts."
