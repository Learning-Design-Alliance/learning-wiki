Evidence¶

Westphal 2024¶

Westphal, A., Richter, E., Lazarides, R., & Huang, Y. (2024). More I-talk in student teachers’ written reflections indicates higher stress during VR teaching. Computers & Education, 212, 104987. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.104987

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal regression (Model 4a) controlling for prior physiological stress and class size found that subjective pronoun use significantly predicted physiological stress in the second session (β = 0.18, p = .040), meaning students who "used more subjective first-person singular pronouns... experienced a stronger increase in physiological stress".

"Thus, student teachers who used more subjective first-person singular pronouns in their written reflections of the first VR teaching session experienced a stronger increase in physiological stress during the second VR teaching session."
