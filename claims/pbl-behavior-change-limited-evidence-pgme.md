`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesis of three studies assessing subjective behavior change. Holmes et al. found most participants implemented committed changes at three to six months; Benson et al. found referral and risk-assessment changes that were not statistically significant; Agrawal et al. found drops in pharmaceutical marketing tool use (25% fewer gifts accepted, 17% fewer sponsored CME events) that were not statistically significant (p > 0.05).
