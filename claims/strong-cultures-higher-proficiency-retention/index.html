Sarah Almy and Melissa Tooley 2012¶

Sarah Almy and Melissa Tooley. (2012). Building and Sustaining Talent: Creating Conditions in High-Poverty Schools That Support Effective Teaching and Learning. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED543216

The report cites one study finding “a difference of 23 points in math and 14 points in reading” in proficiency between schools with the strongest and weakest teacher-perceived cultures.

"schools with strong school cultures, based on teacher perceptions, have higher student proficiency rates than those with the weakest cultures — a difference of 23 points in math and 14 points in reading, according to one study."
