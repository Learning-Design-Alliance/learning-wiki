## Evidence

### Steven J. Pollock and Noah D. Finkelstein 2008

Steven J. Pollock and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2008). Sustaining Educational Reforms in Introductory Physics. Physical Review: Special Topics in Physics Education Research. https://journals.aps.org/prper/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0805.0277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0805.0277)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of BEMA pre/post matched scores for two semesters of the second-semester course (Fa04 initial, Sp05 hand-off), both led by PER faculty with nearly identical course structures. The article reports "the posttest score averages were the same, 59%" and normalized gains of 44% ± 1% versus 43% ± 1%.

> "The posttest score averages were the same, 59%, and the normalized learning gains indistinguishable 44% ± 1% (standard error on the mean) for the initial implementation and 43% ± 1%, secondary implementation."
