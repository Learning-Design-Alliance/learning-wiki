The review documents Vanderbilt's quantitative rationale for disabling Turnitin's AI detector in 2023, citing reliability, false-positive risk at scale, and lack of transparency. The figure is the vendor's stated rate applied to the institution's submission volume, not a measured error rate.

"applying the vendor's then-stated 1% false-positive rate to approximately 75,000 papers submitted through Vanderbilt's instance in 2022 would imply around 750 papers potentially misidentified as AI-written"
