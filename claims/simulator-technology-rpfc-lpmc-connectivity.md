## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Anil Kamat 2022

Anil Kamat, Basiel Makled, Jack Norfleet, Steven D. Schwaitzberg, Xavier Intes, Suvranu De, Anirban Dutta. (2022). Directed information flow during laparoscopic surgical skill acquisition dissociated skill level and medical simulation technology. npj Science of Learning 7:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00138-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

The same repeated-measure two-way MANOVA (13 subjects, both simulators) tested simulator technology effects on directed connectivity; the article reports the significant RPFC-to-LPMC effect with F(1,15)=6.002, p=0.027, "partial η2 = 0.286". The authors interpret higher VR flow as possibly increased attentional processes.

> "Additionally, two-way MANOVA found a statistically signi ﬁcant effect of the simulator technology (physical simulator, VR simulator) on the interregional directed functional connectivity from the RPFC to LPMC ( F (1, 15) = 6.002, p = 0.027; partial η2 = 0.286)"
