Schochet, Peter Z. (2013). What is the Minimum Information Needed to Estimate Average Treatment Effects in Education RCTs? SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. http://www.whitehouse.gov/sites/default/files/omb/memoranda/2013/m-13-17.pdf

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Descriptive background statement in this methods abstract about the data-access context for opportunistic experiments. The author reports that some states release identifiable data with sufficient security protections and IRB approval, while others will not.

> "The federal Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) prohibits the disclosure of personally identifiable information from education records without written consent, except under certain exceptions. States interpret FERPA in different ways, however, to protect the confidentiality of their data. States also have their own privacy laws, which vary and in some cases are highly restrictive."
