`q2 i3` The average convergent validity coefficient between any pair of randomly sampled methods was .42; observation-notebook correlations were high (r = .84, .71) but correlations with computer simulation and short-answer were moderate to low. [→ Shavelson 1993](#shavelson-1993)

## Evidence

### Shavelson 1993

Shavelson, R. J., Gao, X., & Baxter, G. P. (1993). Sampling Variability of Performance Assessments. National Center for Research on Evaluation, Standards, and Student Testing. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED359229

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

A p x t x m G study in which 186 students were tested on two tasks by each of four methods (observed, notebook, computer, short-answer). The article reports r = .84 (Electric Mysteries) and r = .71 (Bugs) for observation-notebook, and moderate-to-low correlations with the other two methods.

> "For the particular tasks (Electric Mysteries and Bugs), the average convergent validity coefficient between any pair of randomly sampled methods was .42."
