## Evidence

### Sebring 1995

Sebring, P. A., Bryk, A. S., Luppescu, S., & Thum, Y. M. (1995). Charting Reform in Asbury High School: Results of Student and Teacher Surveys. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-asbury-school-results-student-and-teacher-surveys

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey report summary of the eleven-item Parents' Involvement in Students' Learning scale for top-quartile schools, with printed percentages of one in five, about 40, and almost two-thirds. Bottom-quartile schools show about 45 percent limited and 15 percent minimal support.

> "One in every five students in the top quartile schools reports a great deal of support from parents (scores of 9-10). About 40 percent of the students in these schools report moderate support (scores of 6-8). Thus, almost two-thirds of the students fall in these top two categories."
