## Evidence

### Akçay 2016

Nilüfer Okur Akçay. (2016). Implementation of Cooperative Learning Model in Preschool. Journal of Education and Learning, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v5n3p83

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analysis of the same study comparing female (n=20 pooled pre/post groups as printed) and male students' scores. The posttest gender comparison was "not significant (t(36)=1.51; p>0.05)"; no effect size is printed.

> "It was determined that the arithmetic mean of the posttest results of the female students was 6.65 and standard deviation was 2.15; the arithmetic mean of the male students was 5.50 and the standard deviation was 2.52, and the difference between them was not significant (t(36)=1.51; p>0.05)"
