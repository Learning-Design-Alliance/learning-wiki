Frey 2015¶

Frey, A. J., Small, J. W., Feil, E. G., Seeley, J. R., Walker, H. M., & Forness, S. (2015). First Step to Success: Applications to Preschoolers at Risk of Developing Autism Spectrum Disorders. Education and Training in Autism and Developmental Disabilities, 50(4), 397–407. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1082560

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Baseline comparison (Table 1) of the 34 children identified as at risk for ASD against the 92 non-identified children from the original trial. Parent-reported differences were expected since the subsample was identified using parent-reported ECI data; teacher-reported SSiS Empathy showed t = 2.19, p = .030.

"The two samples did not differ signiﬁcantly on any of the teacher-reported outcome measures with the exception of a signiﬁcantly lower SSiS Empathy score for the subsample."
