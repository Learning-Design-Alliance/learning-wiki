Mapping of the 24 bachelor's-level positions to the four experimental skill categories (Table VI) shows communication and collaboration skills in every position. The authors state this "underscores the importance of collaborative work in quantum industry contexts."

> "Across all role types, communication and collaboration is the only experimental skill category that appears in every position in our dataset. As shown in Table VI, all bachelor's-level positions include at least one skill related to communication and collaboration."
