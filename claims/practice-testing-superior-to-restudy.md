## Evidence

### Thomas M. Cavanagh 2023

Thomas M. Cavanagh, Christa Kiersch. (2023). Using commonly-available technologies to create online multimedia lessons through the application of the Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10181-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports two recent meta-analyses (Adesope et al., 2017; Rowland, 2014) confirming "practice testing is superior to both no-activity controls and restudy conditions", with format and feedback-timing moderators; no effect sizes are printed for these contrasts.

> "Two-recent meta-analysis have confirmed earlier results, finding specifically that practice testing is superior to both no-activity controls and restudy conditions (Adesope et al., 2017), that short answer and free recall practice tests are more effective than multiple-choice or recognition tests, that feedback increases the size of the effect, and that delayed feedback is more effective than immediate feedback (Rowland, 2014)"
