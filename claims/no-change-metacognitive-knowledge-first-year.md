## Evidence

### Hong Wei Han 2015

Hong Wei Han, Vadivelu Jamunarani, Daniel Esther Gnanamalar Sarojini, Sim Joong Hiong. (2015). Thinking about thinking: changes in first-year medical students' metacognition and its relation to performance. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.27561

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired sample t-test on 159 first-year UMMP students' MSI knowledge subscale scores at year start and end; the article reports t(158)=1.59, p=0.114, with means of "M=77.18, SD=10.79" and "M=75.87, SD=9.81". No effect size is printed.

> "The paired sample t-tests showed there was no significant difference in the scores for the dimension of knowledge of metacognition scores in first-year medical students at the beginning of the academic year (M=77.18, SD=10.79) and at the end of the academic year (M=75.87, SD=9.81)"
