Evidence¶

McInerney Niall 2022¶

McInerney Niall, Nally D., Khan M.F., Heneghan H., Cahill R.A. (2022). Performance effects of simulation training for medical students – a systematic review. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2022-39/zma001572.shtml

q3 · i? · review · r2

Narrative systematic review per PRISMA of Ovid MEDLINE, EMBASE and PubMed (2010–2020); nineteen articles including nine randomised trials concerning 2459 students (median 79/study) were selected. The review reports that "All groups reported a performance benefit to students associated with simulation training." No pooled effect size was computed because meta-analysis was prohibited by heterogeneity.

"All groups reported a performance benefit to students associated with simulation training. Simulation training was shown to have a positive effect when used across a broad range of medical and surgical specialities, in acute and non-acute scenarios."
