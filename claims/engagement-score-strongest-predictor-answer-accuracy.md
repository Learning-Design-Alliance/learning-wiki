## Evidence

### Sun Shiying 2026

Sun Shiying, Xu Shenglin, Wang Xue. (2026). Classroom behavioral indicators predict academic performance in large-class clinical instruction: a cohort study using real-time behavioral data. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09340-2

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Individual-level univariate linear regression pooling all classes (n = 1,048 students with analyzable records) in a retrospective observational study of a fourth-year obstetrics and gynecology course delivered via Rain Classroom. The correlation of "student engagement score (r = 0.5095, df = 1046, p < 0.0001)" with answer accuracy was significant and stronger than quiz completion rate (r = 0.3497).
