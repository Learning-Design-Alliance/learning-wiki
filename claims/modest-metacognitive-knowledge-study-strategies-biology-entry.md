### Williams Adrienne E. 2026

Williams Adrienne E., Denaro Kameryn. (2026). Studying with Friends in Introductory Biology Is Associated with Low Impact Study Strategies and No Improvement of Exam Scores. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-04-0074

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of first-year introductory biology students (n = 502) at a large public R1 institution at Week 0, asking students to rate 11 study strategies for research-supported effectiveness on a 0–100 scale. Mean scores were tightly clustered, "ranging from 56 (SD = 28) to 77 (SD = 20)", and students ranked answering practice problems lower than re-watching videos or summarizing notes.

> "When students were asked to rank, from 0 to 100, which strategies researchers consider to be the most effective, they gave all study strategies very similar mean scores, ranging from 56 (SD = 28) to 77 (SD = 20)."
