## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2020

Martin O. Archer. (2020). Schools of all backgrounds can do physics research: On the accessibility and equity of the PRiSE approach to independent research projects. arXiv. [doi:10.5194/gc-2020-37](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-2020-37)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survival analysis of school retention across intervention stages for academic years 2017/18 and 2018/19 (n = 81 school-year participations per Fig. 4), showing a "fairly uniform drop-off rate between the kick-off – visit – comments stages at23 ± 7%".

> "Overall there is a fairly uniform drop-off rate between the kick-off – visit – comments stages at23 ± 7% with a slightly higher drop-off from assignment to kick-off ( 33 ± 5%)."

### Martin O. Archer 2020 (2)

Martin O. Archer. (2020). Schools of all backgrounds can do physics research: On the accessibility and equity of the PRiSE approach to independent research projects. arXiv. [doi:10.5194/gc-2020-37](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-2020-37)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Logrank tests comparing survival distributions across years and across the four PRiSE projects found no overall differences (χ2 (1) = 0.60, p = 0.441 by year; χ2 (3) = 4.28, p = 0.232 by project).

> "No overall differences across survival distributions in a logrank test (Machin et al., 2006) appear present by year (χ2 (1) = 0.60,p = 0.441) or project (χ2 (3) = 4.28,p = 0.232)."
