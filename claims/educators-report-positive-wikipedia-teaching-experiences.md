## Evidence

### Shani Evenstein Sigalov 2025

Shani Evenstein Sigalov, Piotr Konieczny. (2025). Writing for the greater good: what do educators think about using Wikipedia as a teaching tool? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10505-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire survey of 222 educators who used Wikipedia assignments, open May–August 2020, with purposive sampling through Wikimedia and Wiki Education channels. In the teaching-experience section, "83% of respondents rated the experience as either 4 or 5"; only 3.2% rated it 1 or 2.

> "83% of respondents rated the experience as either 4 or 5 (36.9% and 46.4%, respectively). Only 13.5% rated it as a 3, and just 3.2% rated their experience as either 1 or 2."
