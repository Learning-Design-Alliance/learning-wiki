### Philipp Scheiger 2023

Philipp Scheiger, Holger Cartarius, Ronny Nawrodt. (2023). Why and how to implement worked examples in upper division theoretical physics. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/5.0105612](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0105612)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Instructor-reported experience across several university courses (mathematics refresher to seminars in classical mechanics, electrodynamics, quantum theory, thermodynamics/statistics); the authors state they "observed strong interest of the students and their active participation". No systematic evaluation was conducted.

> "We used this or a similar structure of worked exam- ples in a set of different courses from mathematics re- fresher courses to seminars accompanying classical me- chanics, electrodynamics, quantum theory, and thermo- dynamics/statistis lectures. In all these setups we ob- served strong interest of the students and their active participation."
