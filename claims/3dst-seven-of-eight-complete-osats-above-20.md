## Evidence

### Molinari Giulia 2025

Molinari Giulia, Emiliani Nicolas, Cercenelli Laura, Bortolani Barbara, D'Azzeo Rossana, Burato Arianna, Presutti Livio, Molteni Gabriele, Marcelli Emanuela (2025). A Novel 3D Printed Multi‐Material Simulator for Endoscopic Stapes Surgery: The “3D Stapes Trainer”. The Laryngoscope 135(9): 3356–3363. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.32168

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational performance evaluation during the Bologna Oto Summer School: eight course participants performed endoscopic stapes surgery on the 3DST, recordings were scored by two raters using modified OSATS scales. "Seven out of 8 (87.5%) participants managed to complete the endoscopic SS" with mean time 24 min (range 14.3–36.3 min).

> "Seven out of 8 (87.5%) participants managed to complete the endoscopic SS. The mean surgical time to complete the task was 24 min (range 14.3–36.3 min)."

### Molinari Giulia 2025 (2)

Molinari Giulia, Emiliani Nicolas, Cercenelli Laura, Bortolani Barbara, D'Azzeo Rossana, Burato Arianna, Presutti Livio, Molteni Gabriele, Marcelli Emanuela (2025). A Novel 3D Printed Multi‐Material Simulator for Endoscopic Stapes Surgery: The “3D Stapes Trainer”. The Laryngoscope 135(9): 3356–3363. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.32168

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

OSATS scoring of recorded simulations from the same course cohort: "75% of the participants received a cumulative score higher than 20" on the general OSATS global rating scale (maximum 30, per Figure 4).

> "According to the general OSATS, 75% of the participants received a cumulative score higher than 20."
