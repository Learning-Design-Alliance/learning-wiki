## Evidence

### Rouis Sana 2025

Rouis Sana, Chouchene Imed, Gaddas Meriem, Naija Walid, Ben Saad Helmi, Lassoued Latifa. (2025). Residents' contribution to teaching at a Tunisian faculty of medicine. The Libyan Journal of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/19932820.2025.2597563

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reported fields of teaching in the cross-sectional survey (n = 105): e.g. the relevance of collateral contacts taught frequently by 65.7%, essential elements of clinical examination by 46.6%, while empathy was 20% and professionalism 14.3%. The article states these are "areas that require more focus".

> "Residents frequently teach essential clinical skills but place less emphasis on professionalism, empathy, and communication with patients' families, areas that require more focus to enhance teaching outcomes"
