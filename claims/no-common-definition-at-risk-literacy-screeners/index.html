Lemke 2023¶

Lemke, M., Murphy, D., Soo Ping Chow, A., Spencer, H., & Zhang, A. (2023). Comparing early literacy screening assessment benchmarks in Massachusetts. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638508

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive review (Table 1) of risk-level documentation for the state-approved screeners (DIBELS 8th Edition, mCLASS, FastBridge aReading, i-Ready, Lexia RAPID, MAP Growth, Star Reading), showing levels from two to five per assessment, set by percentile bands or grade-level labels; the authors note 'These levels differ across assessments in how they were determined and in what they represent.'

"Most of the approved screening assessments, however, do not simply identify risk using a single cut score, instead providing several benchmarks or risk levels (e.g., low risk, some risk, high risk; see Table 1)."
