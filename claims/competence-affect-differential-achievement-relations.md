## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Schneider 2020

Rebecca Schneider, Jörn R. Sparfeldt. (2020). Academic competence and affect self-concepts in elementary school students: Social and dimensional comparisons. Social Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11218-019-09532-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

GI/E path analyses in grades 2–4 (N = 858) comparing corresponding-domain paths for competence versus affect self-concept factors. The study reports affect paths were "numerically lower and of moderate size" than the competence paths; the contrast is descriptive, no significance test of the difference is printed.

> "For the affect self-concept factors, path coefficients were numerically lower and of moderate size, both for mathematics (.35 ≤ β ≤ .48) and for German (.20 ≤ β ≤ .32)."
