Sibuma 2018¶

Sibuma, B., Wunnava, S., John, M.-S., Anggoro, F., & Dubosarsky, M. (2018). The Impact of an Integrated Pre-K STEM Curriculum on Teachers' Engineering Content Knowledge, Self-Efficacy, and Teaching Practices. 2018 IEEE Integrated STEM Conference (ISEC). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED585271

q1 · i? · causal · r?

This is the study's stated hypothesis, not a tested result: the paper prints no post-intervention outcome statistics. Analysis was planned via "a general random effects regression model" with matching variables as covariates, with results to follow post-intervention (about June 2018).

"We hypothesized that teachers who implement the integrated STEM curriculum will have significantly higher engineering pedagogical content knowledge and self-efficacy than teachers in the comparison group."
