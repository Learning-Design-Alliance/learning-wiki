## Evidence

### Rebecca Pape 2026

Rebecca Pape, Joachim Thomas. (2026). Exploring young learners’ prompted and unprompted SRL think-alouds. Learning and Instruction 102 (2026) 102294. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102294

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttest analysis of the 10-min recall knowledge test after the 30-min CBLE session; the Mann-Whitney U test found "no significant difference between the groups" (U = 145.50, Z = .343, p = .736).

> "The statistical analysis showed, using a Mann-Whitney U test, no significant difference between the groups (U = 145.50, Z = .343, p = .736). Hence, the metacognitive prompts did not significantly support the students’ recall learning performance."
