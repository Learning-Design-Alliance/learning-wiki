Manifest content analysis of open-ended questionnaire replies from all 274 participants, guided by the research question on what enables students to treat patients with medications. The article reports "Four themes emerged" and that 171 students (62%) mentioned clinical placements.

> "Four themes emerged from the content analysis: Curriculum, Clinical placement, Theoretical teaching and Student responsibility (Fig. 1)."
