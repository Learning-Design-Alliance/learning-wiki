Evidence¶

Katharina Spintzyk 2016¶

Katharina Spintzyk, Friederike Strehlke, Stephanie Ohlberger, Bernd Gröben, and Claas Wegner. (2016). An Empirical Study Investigating Interdisciplinary Teaching of Biology and Physical Education. Science Educator, Vol. 25, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1110252

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental test/control group study with 141 German sixth-form students taught three hours weekly for six weeks; ANOVA with repeated measurements on knowledge-test total scores, analyzed with SPSS 21 at a 5% significance level. The printed effect size for the interaction is η²p = 0.43, reported in the article as "high I2 p=0.43".

"The evaluation showed a high signiﬁ cance for the time of measurement ((F(1, 113.24)=126, p<0.01) and the treatment ((F(1, 94.12)=126, p<0.01) in favor of the test group. The interaction effect of this signiﬁ cant developmental differen- tial between the test and control group is high I2 p=0.43"

Katharina Spintzyk 2016 (2)¶

Katharina Spintzyk, Friederike Strehlke, Stephanie Ohlberger, Bernd Gröben, and Claas Wegner. (2016). An Empirical Study Investigating Interdisciplinary Teaching of Biology and Physical Education. Science Educator, Vol. 25, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1110252

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive pre-/post-test values from the knowledge test: at t2 the test group (N=64) scored M = 12.42, SD = 4.2 versus the control group (N=64) at M = 6.31, SD = 2.64; at t1 the groups differed only slightly (M = 6.67 vs M = 6).

"At the time of measurement t 2, the test group N=64 achieved better average val- ues (M = 12.42, SD = 4.2) than the con- trol group N=64 (M = 6.31, SD = 2.64)."
