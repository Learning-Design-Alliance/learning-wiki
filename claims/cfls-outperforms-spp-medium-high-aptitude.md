# CFLS planner outperforms the SPP for medium and high aptitude learners within the success triangle despite lacking prerequisite knowledge

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the simulation comparison, the CFLS planner outperformed the Simple Prerequisite Planner on expertise in all cells within the success triangle, and on coverage in all but five cells, for medium and high aptitude learners — without access to prerequisite information. [→ Stephanie Frost · Gord McCalla 2020](#stephanie-frost-gord-mccalla-2020)

## Evidence

### Stephanie Frost · Gord McCalla 2020

Stephanie Frost · Gord McCalla. (2020). A Planning Algorithm to Support Learning in Open-ended, Unstructured Environments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00221-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison against the SPP baseline (100% coverage, expertise 0.619–0.714 by aptitude). CFLS exceeded SPP on coverage in the success triangle except at b=2,f=3; b=3,f=3; b=2,f=4; b=3,f=4; and b=4,f=4 where coverage was marginally below 100%. Low aptitude learners only outperformed SPP at b=1, f=1.

> "for both medium and high aptitude groups, the CFLS planner outperformed the SPP on expertise in all cells within the success triangle"
