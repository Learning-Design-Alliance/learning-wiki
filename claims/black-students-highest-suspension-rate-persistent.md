# Black students are suspended at the highest rate of any racial group, at 12% in 2017–18, with persistent disparities across all years analyzed

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Leung-Gagné 2022

Leung-Gagné, M., McCombs, J., Scott, C., & Losen, D. J. (2022). Pushed out: Trends and disparities in out-of-school suspension. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/235.277

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CRDC analysis of suspension rates by race and ethnicity (Figure 1). The report states Black students' 12% rate was "9 percentage points higher than the rate at which white students were suspended (3%)"; Native American students had the second-highest rate at 7%.

> "Black students continue to be excluded from school at the highest rate, with more than 1 in 8 Black students (12%) receiving one or more out-of-school suspensions in 2017–18."
