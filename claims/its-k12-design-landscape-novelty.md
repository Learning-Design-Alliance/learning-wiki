## Evidence

### Létourneau 2025

Létourneau, A., Martineau, M. D., Charland, P., Karran, J. A., Boasen, J. & Léger, P. M. (2025). A systematic review of AI-driven intelligent tutoring systems (ITS) in K-12 education. npj Science of Learning 10:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00320-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's analysis of the 28 included studies (Fig. 1) found quasi-experimental pre/post designs dominated, most studies were in STEM (82%), and interventions were often very short. The review notes such short durations may amplify effect sizes via novelty.
