`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre- and post-workshop questionnaire means from the two-day remote co-design study with fifteen teachers. Only the two familiarity means and the confidence mean are printed; no standard deviations or effect sizes accompany them, so no impact magnitude can be reported.

> "Participants came into the workshop rating their own familiarity with AI an average of 4.8 out of 7, and finished the workshop with an average rating of 5.8 out of 7. Teachers also rated their confidence about integrating AI into their own curriculum with an average rating of 5.6 out of 7."
