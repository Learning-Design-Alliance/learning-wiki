# Prior research on task familiarity and negotiation of meaning is inconsistent, with some studies finding more negotiation for unfamiliar tasks and one finding no difference

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The review of prior work reports that Robinson (2001) and Hardy and Moore (2004) found unfamiliar-task or unsupported students negotiated more, while Yule, Powers and McDonald (1992) reported no significant difference between groups. [→ Arslanyilmaz 2010](#arslanyilmaz-2010)
