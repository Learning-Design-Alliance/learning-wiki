## Evidence

### Barrat 2015

Barrat, V. X., Berliner, B., & Felida, N. J. (2015). Arizona's Invisible Achievement Gap: Education Outcomes of Students in Foster Care in the State's Public Schools. San Francisco: WestEd. http://www.azed.gov/research-evaluation/files/2013/11/2013-a-f-technical-manual.pdf

Observational analysis of school enrollment by A–F Letter Grade Accountability System rating in 2012/13 (figure 6). Students in foster care (17 percent) and low-SES students (16 percent) attended A-rated schools at nearly half the percentage of all students (30 percent).

> "43 percent of students in foster care and 45 percent of low-SES students attended public schools that the state rated as C, D, or F, compared to 33 percent of all students statewide."
