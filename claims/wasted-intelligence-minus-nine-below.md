### Pintner 1920

Pintner, R., and Marshall, H. (1920). A Combined Mental-Educational Survey. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=A+Combined+Mental-Educational+Survey+Pintner+Marshall

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

From the frequency distribution of Differences of 4303 cases, the authors identify the band of minus 9 and below (19.0 plus 2.5 per cent) as wasted intelligence and state that finding the reasons and stimulating these pupils is the school's business. No effect size is reported.

> "The 21.5 per cent having Differences of minus 9 or below are obviously marked cases of wasted intelligence. They have ability to do nruch better and for some reason or other this ability is not being Titilized."
