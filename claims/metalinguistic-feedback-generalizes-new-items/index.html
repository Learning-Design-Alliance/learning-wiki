Evidence¶

Rod Ellis 2006¶

Rod Ellis, Shawn Loewen and Rosemary Erlam. (2006). Implicit and Explicit Corrective Feedback and the Acquisition of L2 Grammar. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 28, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263106060141

q3 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA on oral imitation new-item scores (verbs not receiving feedback) at delayed posttest, n = 34. "the metalinguistic group is significantly more accurate than both the recast and control groups"; GJT new items also favored metalinguistic over recast (F = 4.295, p < .05).

"there were differences on the delayed posttest, F (2, 34) = 8.943, p < .01. The post hoc contrasts show that the metalinguistic group is significantly more accurate than both the recast and control groups."
