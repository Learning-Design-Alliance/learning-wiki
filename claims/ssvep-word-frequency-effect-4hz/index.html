Evidence¶

Fang Wang 2025¶

Fang Wang, Elizabeth Y. Toomarian, Radhika S. Gosavi, Blair Kaneshiro, Anthony M. Norcia & Bruce D. McCandliss. (2025). Steady-state EEG captures how elementary classroom instruction drives plasticity for novel visual words. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00371-w

q3 · i? · causal · r2

EEG oddball analysis in the same sample (N = 28): significant responses at first and fourth harmonics for HFW-PW (all pFDR < 0.01) but only the first harmonic for MFW-PW (pFDR < 0.001). The authors state this is the first examination of the word frequency effect using an SSVEP paradigm.

"In comparison with the MFW vs. pseudowords contrast, the HFW vs. pseudowords contrast elicited larger amplitude responses, particularly at the fourth harmonic (i.e., 4 Hz), suggesting facilitated access to lexical representations compared to MFW44,45."
