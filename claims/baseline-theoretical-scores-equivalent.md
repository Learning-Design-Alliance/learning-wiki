`q3 i?` ANOVA showed no significant difference in average first theoretical scores among the V, J, V-J, and J-V groups (P > 0.05). [→ Sheng Jie 2022](#sheng-jie-2022)

## Evidence

### Sheng Jie 2022

Sheng Jie, Zhang Congdi, Gao Zhengkun, Yan Yimin, Meng Yucheng, Ren Shiqi, Liu Bin, Zhang Baoping. (2022). Virtual versus jaw simulation in inlay preparation preclinical teaching: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03930-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA of the first theoretical examination, administered after the shared theoretical lecture and before group-specific training, in the RCT with 76 undergraduates. The article states this indicated "the same degree of students' learning ability after the theoretical teaching".

> "An ANOVA was performed, and it showed no significant difference in the average scores among the V, J, V-J, and J-V groups (54.5 ± 2.35, 55.2 ± 2.21, 56.3 ± 4.27, 55.5 ± 1.77, respectively) (P > 0.05)"
