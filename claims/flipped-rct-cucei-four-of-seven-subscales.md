Bonferroni-corrected (alpha 0.007) follow-up ANOVAs on the CUCEI in the RCT. Table 3 prints partial η² of 0.871 (Involvement), 0.711 (Satisfaction), 0.783 (Task Orientation), 0.898 (Innovation); non-significant subscales had η² of 0.098, 0.001, and 0.009.

> "Based on this corrected criterion, the flipped classroom group scored significantly higher than the lecture-based learning group on four of the seven subscales: Satisfaction, Involvement, Task Orientation, and Innovation. The remaining three subscales—Personalization, Cohesiveness, and Individualization—did not reach statistical significance after the correction."
