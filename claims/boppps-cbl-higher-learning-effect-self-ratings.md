## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Self-evaluations of learning effects were significantly higher in the experimental group than in the control group, including understanding and mastery of knowledge (r = 0.24) up to stimulation of learning interest and initiative (r = 0.68). [→ Lu Jiaxuan 2025](#lu-jiaxuan-2025)

## Evidence

### Lu Jiaxuan 2025

Lu Jiaxuan, Wu Ruixue, Zhu Ling, Zhao Linlin, Zhao Wei, Ma Lan. (2025). Application of the BOPPPS combined with CBL method in clinical clerkship of pediatric dentistry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07967-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Learning-effects dimension of the post-clerkship questionnaire (71 respondents). All five items favored the experimental group; printed effects range from "understanding and mastery of knowledge (P < 0.05, r = 0.24)" to r = 0.68 for stimulation of learning interest and initiative.

> "self-evaluations of learning effects, including learning efficiency (P < 0.01, r = 0.31), understanding and mastery of knowledge (P < 0.05, r = 0.24), practical application skills (P < 0.001, r = 0.60), self-study ability (P < 0.001, r = 0.44) and stimulation of learning interest and initiative (P < 0.001, r = 0.68), were significantly increased in the Exp group than in the Con group"
