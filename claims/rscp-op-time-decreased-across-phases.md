## Evidence

### Park Kena 2024

Park Kena, Kwon Ji Young, Yoo Eun-Hee, Lee Seon Hwa, Song Jeong Min, Pyeon Seung Yeon. (2024). Application of CUSUM analysis in assessing learning curves in robot-assisted sacrocolpopexy performed by experienced gynecologist. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-024-02691-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA comparison across the three CUSUM-defined phases in the 50-case retrospective series showed mean operative times of 338.1, 213.0, and 179.9 minutes; "A significant decrease in op time was observed between three phases (p = 0.000)", largest between Learning and Proficiency.

> "The specific mean op time in the Learning, Proficiency, and Competency phases are 338.1 ± 57.4 min, 213.0 ± 34.3 min and 179.9 ± 28.0 min, respectively. A significant decrease in op time was observed between three phases (p = 0.000), with a larger discrepancy between the Learning and Proficiency phase."
