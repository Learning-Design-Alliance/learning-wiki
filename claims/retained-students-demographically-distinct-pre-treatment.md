## Evidence

### Hwang 2016

Hwang, S., Cappella, E., & Schwartz, K. (2016). Examining Multidimensional Middle Grade Outcomes after Early Elementary School Grade Retention. SREE Spring 2016 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567416

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Descriptive pre-treatment comparison in the ECLS-K analytic sample of 5,586 students, reported in Table 1 and the accompanying text; the article notes "22% of retained students were classified as having a disability" versus 13% of promoted students.

> "the retained group consists of a higher proportion of males (58% versus 48%) and lower proportion of white students (38% versus 61%) as compared to the promoted group."
