## Evidence

### Husain et al. 2016

Fatima Husain, Nico Jabin, Sarah Haywood, Adetayo Kasim, Jonathan Paylor. (2016). Parent Academy: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN42518213

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Finding from the three-arm randomised controlled trial comparing incentivised and unincentivised treatment arms. The report also notes that "Six in ten parents (60%) who were offered the Parent Academy did not take part in any sessions and only around one in ten (11%) attended all twelve sessions."

> "Offering financial incentives improved attendance  at Parent Academy, suggesting this may be an effective way to engage and retain parents in interventions of this type."
