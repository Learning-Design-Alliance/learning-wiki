### Dowlati 2016

Dowlati, E., Musick, D. W., Zhang, L., Thornton, K., & Carvalho, H. (2016). Use of Dramatization to Teach Cardiac Cycle Physiology to Medical Students. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i9.1603

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pearson and point-biserial correlation procedures examined the association between individual assessment items, total scores, and student confidence levels. Only the Group A immediate posttest correlation reached significance ("r= 0.59 (df = 11, p-value = 0.035)"); the other five correlations ranged from r=-0.34 to r=0.44 and were non-significant.
