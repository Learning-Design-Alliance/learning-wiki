## Evidence

### Shen 2013

Shen, F.-Y. (2013). Using Group Discussion with Taiwan’s EFL College Students: A Comparison of Comprehension Instruction for Book Club, Literature Circles, and Instructional Conversations. English Language Teaching, 6(12), 58-. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n12p58

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on beyond-lesson-text comprehension test scores (maximum 26 points) across four conditions. The article reports "a main effect of approach, F (3, 141) = 5.03, p = .002" with post-hoc tests showing each experimental group exceeded control.

> "The ANOV A revealed that there was a main effect of approach, F (3, 141) = 5.03, p = .002. Scheffe post-hoc tests on the scores indicated that the experimental groups significantly outperformed the control group, meaning that discussion-based approaches helped stude nts transfer reading skills to their own reading more effectively than did the basal comprehension approach."
