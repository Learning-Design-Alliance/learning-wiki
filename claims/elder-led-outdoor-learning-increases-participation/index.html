Evidence¶

Acharibasam 2021¶

Acharibasam, J. B., & McVittie, J. (2021). The use of a two-eyed seeing approach to include Indigenous Knowledge in Early Childhood Care and Development in Ghana. International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 20(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1329976

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Researcher participant observation during weekly Elder-led outdoor activities (January–March 2020) at Boania Primary School. The authors report "more participation in outdoor learning activities even from students who did not participate much in class," and offer three possible explanations without testing them.

"The researchers observed more participation in outdoor learning activities even from students who did not participate much in class. The researchers observed that children who normally did not speak or answer questions in class were more engaged during the outdoor learning activities with the Elders."
