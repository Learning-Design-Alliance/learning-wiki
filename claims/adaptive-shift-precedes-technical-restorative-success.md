## Evidence

### Trout 2021

Trout, L. (2021). The toolkit before the toolkit: Centering adaptive and relational elements of restorative practices for implementation success. [Center to Improve Social and Emotional Learning and School Safety at WestEd]. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED615970

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Practitioner-guide assertion (expert interpretation, no empirical study reported) explaining failed implementations: schools may run circles with technical skill while devaluing the tenets behind the process. The brief adds that without adaptive shifts, implementing technical pieces becomes exhausting and educators dismiss restorative practices.

> "Many districts and schools lose out on the full impact of their technical work with restorative practices because the adaptive components — values, mindsets, and beliefs — that imbue those practices have not shifted."
