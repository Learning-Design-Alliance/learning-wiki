## Evidence

### Eiring Lisa 2026

Eiring Lisa, Benson Sven, Fandrey Joachim, Prost-Fingerle Katrin, Winning Sandra. (2026). M1ündlich – S(t)imulation of competency-oriented learning in physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09661-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of ~190 third-semester medical students participating in two rounds of peer-teaching-based exam training. The abstract reports a shift toward more favorable self-assessment ratings after participation, with "more students rating themselves higher and fewer reporting poor performance".

> "After participation students reported higher self-assessment scores, with more students rating themselves higher and fewer reporting poor performance."

### Eiring Lisa 2026 (2)

Eiring Lisa, Benson Sven, Fandrey Joachim, Prost-Fingerle Katrin, Winning Sandra. (2026). M1ündlich – S(t)imulation of competency-oriented learning in physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09661-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey data from the first training round (T1) of the quasi-experimental study. A majority of students (61.9%) reported that PT-BET strengthened their confidence in their own abilities, with the proportion increasing to 71.9% in the second round (T2).

> "A total of 61.9% of students in T1 (n = 189) stated that participating in the PT-BET had strengthened their confidence in their own abilities (categories 1 and 2)."
