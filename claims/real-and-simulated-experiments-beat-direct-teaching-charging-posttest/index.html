Evidence¶

Ajredini 2013¶

Ajredini, F., Izairi, N., Zajkov, O. (2013). Real Experiments versus PhET Simulations for Understanding Electrostatic Charging. European J of Physics Education, 5(1). https://ejpe.phywe.de

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental posttest comparison among three groups (Real n=86, Sim n=81, control n=58) of 10th-grade gymnasium students in Macedonia after teaching the unit Electrical charging. The experimental groups clearly exceeded the control group: "the control group has only 43 % correct answers".

"While 75 % of the students in the Real group gave correct answers, the Sim group has slightly lower result of 66 % correct answers and the control group has only 43 % correct answers."
