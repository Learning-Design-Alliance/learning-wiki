## Evidence

### Liu Mei-Yuan 2025

Liu Mei-Yuan, Liao Li-Ling, Huang Yu-Ting, Lee Yi-Chen, Lai I-Ju. (2025). Effectiveness of a scenario-based simulation course on improving the clinical communication skills of dietetic students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06684-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA comparison of the experimental (n = 58) and control (n = 51) groups in a non-randomized pretest–posttest design, controlling for pre-test scores. The experimental group's post-test scores were "significantly higher (p < 0.001)" on all three self-reported scales; Table 1 reports F = 15.053, 17.988, and 15.035.

> "Regarding communication confidence, self-efficacy, and competence, the post-test scores of the experimental group were significantly higher (p < 0.001) than those of the control group for each of the three aspects, as determined by ANCOVA analysis."
