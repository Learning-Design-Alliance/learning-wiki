Observational analysis of drop rates by major from ten cohorts of institutional records, plotted in Fig. 3a with standard errors. The article reports physics, mathematics, and chemistry students are the most likely to drop, while computer science, non-STEM, and psychology students are the least likely.

> "The overall attrition rate in physics is the worst of all majors and shows that more than one third of the students drop the major after declaring it."
