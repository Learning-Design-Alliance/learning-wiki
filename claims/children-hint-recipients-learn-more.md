## Evidence

### Sierksma 2023

Sierksma, J. (2023). Children perpetuate competence-based inequality when they help peers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00192-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Study 1 (N = 253, ages 6–9, science museum) compared children's inferences to chance (0.50) after watching videos of groups receiving hints vs. correct answers. Children picked hint recipients as learning more ("d = 0.90, 95% CI = [0.68, 1.11]") and as smarter ("d = 0.45").

> "children more often picked the group that received a hint (vs. the correct answer) when asked who was smarter ( t(133) = 5.21, p < .001, d = 0.45, 95% CI = [0.27, 0.63]) or which group would learn more ( t(118) = 9.78, p < .001, d = 0.90, 95% CI = [0.68, 1.11])"
