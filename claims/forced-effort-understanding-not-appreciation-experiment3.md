## Evidence

### Jin et al. 2026

Yimeng Jin, Chunyu Ma, Danyang Liu, Junpeng You, Johan Lauwereyns. (2026). Effort enhances understanding, while appreciation drives engagement in Cross-sentence semantic processing. Acta Psychologica. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2026.107927

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 3 LMMs with Bonferroni-corrected EMM comparisons (43 Japanese-speaking students; Forced-effort trials required think-aloud regardless of choice); imposed effort benefited understanding only, as "Forced-effort Trials received significantly higher Sense Scores than Low-effort Trials".

> "Forced-effort Trials received significantly higher Sense Scores than Low-effort Trials, whereas Forced-effort and Low-effort Trials did not differ significantly in Interest or Like Scores."

### Jin et al. 2026 (2)

Yimeng Jin, Chunyu Ma, Danyang Liu, Junpeng You, Johan Lauwereyns. (2026). Effort enhances understanding, while appreciation drives engagement in Cross-sentence semantic processing. Acta Psychologica. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2026.107927

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations computed within Forced-effort trials only (n = 42, excluding one participant who never experienced Forced-effort Trials); the Sense-Like correlation was not significant, unlike in Chosen-effort and Low-effort trials where all pairs correlated positively.

> "only a slight positive correlation was found between Sense Score and Interest Score (r = 0.350, p = .023). Notably, no significant correlation was found between Sense Score and Like Score (r = 0.288, p = .064)."
