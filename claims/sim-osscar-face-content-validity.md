## Evidence

### Dean William H. FRCOphth 2019

Dean William H. FRCOphth, Murray Neil L. MD, Buchan John C. FRCOphth, Golnik Karl MD, Kim Min J. MSc, Burton Matthew J. PhD. (2019). Ophthalmic Simulated Surgical Competency Assessment Rubric for manual small-incision cataract surgery. Journal of Cataract and Refractive Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcrs.2019.04.010

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Closed-question 5-point Likert evaluation by 12 international expert SICS surgeons from 8 countries, half involved in initial revision. Face validity was rated "4.60 ± 0.52 out of 5" and content validity 4.5 after iterative amendment.

> "In response to the question, “Do you think the OSSCAR represents the surgical techniques and skills upon which trainees should be assessed?,” all respondents either agreed or strongly agreed. Face validity was rated as 4.60 (out of 5.0)."
