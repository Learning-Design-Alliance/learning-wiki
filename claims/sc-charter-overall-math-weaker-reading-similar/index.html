CREDO 2019¶

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in South Carolina. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-south-carolina-2019-2/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Statewide quasi-experimental analysis comparing matched charter students' one-year learning gains with Virtual Control Records built from feeder TPS students; the math disadvantage equals "53 fewer days of learning" while the reading difference was not statistically significant.

"in a typical school year, charter students in South Carolina experience lower academic progress than their TPS peers in mathematics. The disadvantage for charter students is equivalent to 53 fewer days of learning in mathematics in a 180-day school year."
