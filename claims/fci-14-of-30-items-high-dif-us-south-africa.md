G-DINA DIF analysis of FCI pre-test responses from the US LASSO cohort (N = 4,750) and UJ cohort (N = 1,016), using item-level Wald tests with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction and Cohen's h severity thresholds. The analysis found "14 exhibited High DIF (|h|≥0.50)".

> "Out of 30 items, 14 exhibited High DIF (|h|≥0.50), 9 exhibited Moderate DIF (0.20≤|h|<0.50), and 7 exhibited Negligible DIF (|h|<0.20)."
