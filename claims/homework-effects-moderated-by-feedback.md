### Müller 2018

Müller, A. (2018). (Science) Teacher Education in the Knowledge Society: Good Practice at the University of Geneva. University of Geneva. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1807.00974](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1807.00974)

`q2 · i3` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing work on classroom instruction, that homework effects are "between small and medium size" (d from 0.36 to 0.65), but feedback as co-factor yields "a large contrast between strong and weak effects" (d = 0.83 vs. d = 0.28). Reported as an example of how co-factors must be taken into account in meta-analysis.

> "overall effects are between small and medium size2) (d from 0.36 to 0.65), but a much better image is obtained when taking account of feedback as co-factor, which when given, or not, leads to a large contrast between strong and weak effects (d = 0.83 vs. d = 0.28; [26])."
