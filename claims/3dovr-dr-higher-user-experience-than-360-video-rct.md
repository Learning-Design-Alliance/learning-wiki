## Evidence

### Im Ji-Eun 2025

Im Ji-Eun, Gu Ja-Young, Bae Jung-Hee, Lee Jae-Gi. (2025). Development and user experience of a three-dimensional object-based virtual reality-simulation tool for dental radiography training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06623-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in which 79 dental hygiene students were assigned to a control group using a 360° VR video (n=39) or an experimental group using 3DOVR-DR (n=40) over six weeks, then surveyed with a 22-item questionnaire. "User-experience ratings were significantly higher in the experimental group (4.35±0.47)"; the Mann–Whitney U test gave U=324.00, Z=−4.474, P<0.001.

> "User-experience ratings were significantly higher in the experimental group (4.35±0.47) compared to the control group (3.63±0.66; P < 0.001)."
