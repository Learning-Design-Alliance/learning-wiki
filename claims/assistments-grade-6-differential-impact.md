## Evidence

### Feng 2025

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments companion report: Implementation analysis of the ASSISTments intervention. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677784

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Grade-level subgroup analysis of the quasi-experimental sample (predominantly 7th graders, N = 2,121; 6th graders N = 383; 8th graders N = 351). The article suggests the intervention "may be particularly beneficial when introduced earlier in middle school."

> "The analysis revealed that students in 6th grade significantly outperformed their comparison group (p = 0.026; effect size = 0.07), whereas no evidence of significant differences was detected for students in 7th grade (p = 0.204) or 8th grade (p = 0.594)"
