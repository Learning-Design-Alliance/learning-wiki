### Hsu 2026

Hsu, T.-C., Zhang, Z., Chen, Z., Liu, Y., & Liu, Y. (2026). Designing More Engaging Serious Games to Support Students' Mental Health: A Pilot Study Based on A CBT-Informed Design Framework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.15662](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.15662)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the experimental group's (N=14) post-play qualitative survey answers (three questions per level, five levels), coded independently by three researchers. Themes for the Metaphorical Understanding dimension matched the CBT objectives, e.g., the Perfectionism level was "primarily interpreted as “Don’t persue perfection” (70%)".

> "The “Perfectionism” level was primarily interpreted as “Don’t persue perfection” (70%) and “Don’t be tempted”(15%)."
