## Evidence

### Miners Alec 2012

Miners Alec, Harris Jody, Felix Lambert, Murray Elizabeth, Michie Susan, Edwards Phil. (2012). An economic evaluation of adaptive e-learning devices to promote weight loss via dietary change for people with obesity. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-12-190

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

One-way sensitivity analyses on the scenario A base case (Table 5). With "£0 initial cost for e-learning devices" the e-LD was dominant (Dom, marked with 59% probability cost-effective at £30,000 per QALY); the base-case fixed cost was £854 per patient from McConnon's trial. Other ICERs ranged from £20,053 to £232,911.

> "Only when the fixed cost of the e-LD was removed, or substantially lowered, did e-learning devices appear to be cost-effective."
