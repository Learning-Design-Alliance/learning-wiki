# SDTM residents scored higher than control residents on the departmental rotation examination (d = 0.50)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` First-year residents trained with SDTM achieved significantly higher total departmental rotation examination scores than controls (97.60 vs 95.13, d = 0.50). [→ Yang Liu 2022](#yang-liu-2022)

## Evidence

### Yang Liu 2022

Yang Liu, Li Wen, Zou Jian, An Junnan, Zeng Bin, Zheng Yitao, Yang Jiming, Ren Jia. (2022). The application of the spot the difference teaching method in clinical skills training for residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03612-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 42 SDTM and 39 control first-year residents on the end-of-rotation departmental examination (max 115 points): 97.60 ± 4.762 vs 95.13 ± 5.424, T = 2.179, P = 0.032, "a medium effect size (d = 0.50)".

> "The total SDTM score was significantly higher than that of the control (P <0.005) with a medium effect size (d = 0.50)."
