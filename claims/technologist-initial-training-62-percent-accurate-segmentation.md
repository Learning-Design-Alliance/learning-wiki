## Evidence

### Suman Garima 2020

Suman Garima, Panda Ananya, Korfiatis Panagiotis, Edwards Marie E., Garg Sushil, Blezek Daniel J., Chari Suresh T., Goenka Ajit H. (2020). Development of a volumetric pancreas segmentation CT dataset for AI applications through trained technologists: a study during the COVID 19 containment phase. Abdominal Radiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00261-020-02741-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of the first segmentation batch in this IRB-approved study: 22 technologists segmented 188 CTs with freehand tools on enterprise software after initial training, and two radiologists reviewed all cases. "117 (62%) were deemed accurate by radiologists"; DSC was 0.63 ± 0.15 and JC 0.48 ± 0.15.

> "Of the initial batch of 188 segmentations, 117 (62%) were deemed accurate by radiologists and 71 (38%) had to be repeated due to segmentation errors."
