## Evidence

### Si Qingzong 2026

Si Qingzong, Li Siqi, Cai Lulu, Huang Qian, Huang Chupeng, Zhang Xinyue, An Xiaoli. (2026). Experiential teaching centered on science popularisation videos: a randomised controlled trial on the impact on learning outcomes and core competencies of dental preventive medicine students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09910-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-tests on course feedback items Q3 knowledge translation, Q4 planning and design, Q5 health communication confidence, and Q7 career development benefit (df = 70). Q7 compared 4.56 ± 0.56 vs 3.33 ± 0.48 with Cohen's d of 2.35.

> "The results showed that the scores of the intervention group on the four core items were extremely significantly higher than those of the control group (all P < 0.001). Cohen’s d effect size ranged from 0.992 to 2.35, all ranging from large to extremely large effects."
