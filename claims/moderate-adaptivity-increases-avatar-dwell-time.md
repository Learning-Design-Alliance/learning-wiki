Kruskal–Wallis analysis of eye-tracking dwell time on the avatar in the 54-participant between-subjects VR study found "a significant effect of adaptivity condition, 𝐻(2) = 10.11,𝑝 = .006,𝜀 2 = .15". Median percentages were No 7.20%, Moderate 14.47% and High 9.49%; Dunn's post-hoc tests (Holm–Bonferroni) showed a significant difference only between No and Moderate Adaptivity (p = .005).
