Evidence¶

Michael Pin-Chuan Lin 2025¶

Michael Pin-Chuan Lin, Daniel H. Chang, Philip H. Winne. (2025). A proposed methodology for investigating student-chatbot interaction patterns in giving peer feedback. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10408-3

q2 · i? · design · r2

Analysis of 23 undergraduates' chatbot interaction logs in a first-year educational psychology course tutorial; the authors interpret the pathway data as showing misjudged understanding, quoting "over-confident in their judgement". No effect size is printed.

"Our preliminary findings suggest participants were over-confident in their judgement as they often misjudged their understanding of how to provide feedback."
