Evidence¶

Farrokhnia 2026¶

Farrokhnia, M., Latifi, S., Papadopoulos, P. M., Hogenkamp, L., Gijlers, H., Khosravi, H., & Noroozi, O. (2026). Generative AI offers more, but students revise less: comparing the effects of teacher and AI feedback on student essay revisions. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00579-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Pearson bivariate correlation analysis for RQ2 in the Zero-shot condition (n = 24) found a moderate significant association between feedback quality and initial essay quality, "indicating a significant reliance on the quality of the initial essays".

"A moderate correlation was shown between GenAI feedback generated using Zero-shot prompt and initial essay quality (r = 0.53, p < .01), indicating a significant reliance on the quality of the initial essays for generating feedback."

Farrokhnia 2026 (2)¶

Farrokhnia, M., Latifi, S., Papadopoulos, P. M., Hogenkamp, L., Gijlers, H., Khosravi, H., & Noroozi, O. (2026). Generative AI offers more, but students revise less: comparing the effects of teacher and AI feedback on student essay revisions. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00579-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Pearson correlation for the CoT condition (n = 22) showed a moderate significant association (r = 0.42), weaker than in the Zero-shot condition, suggesting somewhat less dependence on initial draft quality.

"Similarly, a moderate correlation was observed between GenAI feedback generated using a CoT prompt and initial essay quality (r = 0.42, p < .05), though this correlation was weaker compared to the Zero-shot prompt condition"

Farrokhnia 2026 (3)¶

Farrokhnia, M., Latifi, S., Papadopoulos, P. M., Hogenkamp, L., Gijlers, H., Khosravi, H., & Noroozi, O. (2026). Generative AI offers more, but students revise less: comparing the effects of teacher and AI feedback on student essay revisions. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00579-9

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Pearson correlation for the teacher condition (n = 24) found a weak, non-significant association (r = 0.21, p > .05), suggesting teacher feedback quality is less dependent on initial essay quality.

"a weak and statistically non-significant correlation between teacher feedback and initial essay quality ( r = 0.21, p > .05), suggesting that the quality of teacher feedback is less dependent on the initial quality of essays"
