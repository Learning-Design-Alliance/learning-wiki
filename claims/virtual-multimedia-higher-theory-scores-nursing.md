## Evidence

### Mosalanejad 2012

Mosalanejad, L.; Shahsavari, S.; Sobhanian, S.; & Dastpak, M. (2012). The effect of virtual versus traditional learning in achieving competency-based skills. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 13(2). https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojde

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 43 freshman nursing students in a virtual learning program with a matched group of 43 sophomores taught conventionally, same content, instructor and final evaluation. Table 1 reports theory means of 13.83±1.67 (virtual) versus 12.43±2.02 (traditional), P=0.001, described as "higher mean scores".

> "A significant difference in theoretical scores of the two groups was found; students who were taught by interactive multimedia and virtual simulation had higher mean scores in comparison with traditional teaching group."
