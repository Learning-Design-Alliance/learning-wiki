# Teaching physics in a science teaching laboratory practice course via the Jigsaw method based on cooperative learning yields higher achievement than the confirmatory laboratory method

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Prospective science teachers taught with the Jigsaw cooperative learning method scored significantly higher on the SLPAT than those taught with the confirmatory laboratory method, after controlling for GPA. [→ Karacop 2017](#karacop-2017)

## Evidence

### Karacop 2017

Ataman Karacop. (2017). The Effects of Using Jigsaw Method Based on Cooperative Learning Model in the Undergraduate Science Laboratory Practices. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(3): 420-434. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050314

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental post-test-only control group study of 48 prospective science teachers (JCLG n=25, CG n=23); ANCOVA on SLPAT scores with GPA as covariate found a significant group difference, F(1,45)=6.215; p=0.016, with adjusted means favoring JCLG ("XJCLG = 60.953; XCG= 52.008"). No standardized effect size is printed for this contrast in the quoted finding.

> "The results of the analysis in Table 3 showed a significant difference among the SLPAT corrected average scores of the students in JCLG and CG (F(1,45)=6.215; p=0.016; p<0.05)."
