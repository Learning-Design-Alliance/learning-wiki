### Annoh R 2023

Annoh R, Buchan J, Gichuhi S, et al. (2023). The impact of simulation-based trabeculectomy training on resident core surgical skill competency. Journal of Glaucoma. https://doi.org/10.1097/IJG.0000000000002114

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-arm Wilcoxon signed rank analysis in the control arm of the GLASS trial (Table 1): median rose from 3.25 at baseline to 11.33 at intervention, "p=0.00001", maintained at fifteen months with p=0.0156; n=24 at intervention.

> "On receiving the intervention after one year of conventional training, median scores in the control arm increased to 11.33 (IQR 10.67-12.50; p=0.00001). The increase was maintained at fifteen months (median 11.00, IQR 8.17-14.00; p=0.0156)."
