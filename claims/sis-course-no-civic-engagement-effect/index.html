Evidence¶

Schutte et al. 2018¶

Ingrid Schutte, Elanor Kamans, Marca Wolfensberger and Wiel Veugelers. (2018). Effects of an International Undergraduate Honors Course on Awareness of Global Justice. Educ. Sci. 2018, 8, 82. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8020082

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-posttest with control group using SFS civic engagement (N participants = 18, N control = 16) and speaking up and acting out (N participants = 18, N control = 16) subscales; the valuing social action subscale was excluded due to low posttest reliability. No effect size was printed.

"With respect to civic engagement and speaking up and acting out the analyses did not show that the course had an impact on these forms of motivation to contribute to a more just world. There were no interaction effects Fs < 1, nor did simple effect analysis indicate an increase in civic engagement or political actions, all Fs < 1."
