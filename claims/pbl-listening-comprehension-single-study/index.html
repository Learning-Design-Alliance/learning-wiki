Narrative report of Study 27 (Lin et al.), a mixed-design study with first-year Malaysian undergraduates where both groups took the same listening post-test. The review reports "a notable improvement in listening comprehension scores" in the experimental group.

"The results indicated a notable improvement in listening comprehension scores among students in the experimental group compared to those in the control group, suggesting the potential effectiveness of PBL in enhancing receptive language skills, particularly in listening comprehension."
