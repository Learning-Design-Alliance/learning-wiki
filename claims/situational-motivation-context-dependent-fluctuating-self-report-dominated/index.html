Joni Lämsä 2026¶

Joni Lämsä, Susanne de Mooij, Martine Baars, Inge Molenaar, Sanna Järvelä. (2026). Self-Regulated Learning, Multimodal Data, and Analysis Grid: Where Are We Now and Where Are We Going? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10113-4

q2 · i? · review · r2

The commentary summarizes Törmänen et al.'s (2025) systematic review of 80 empirical studies framed within five motivation theories, including self-determination and expectancy-value theory. The review found "situational motivation is highly context-dependent" and that most studies relied on self-report measures.

"The findings reveal that situational motivation is highly context-dependent, fluctuates within and between learners, and is shaped by complex, temporally embedded interactions among motivational constructs."
