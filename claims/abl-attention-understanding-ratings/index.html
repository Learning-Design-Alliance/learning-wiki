Evidence¶

Anwar 2019¶

Anwar, F. (2019). The effect of activity-based teaching techniques on student motivation and academic achievement. Journal of Education and Educational Development, 6(1), 154-170. https://journals.iobmresearch.com/index.php/JEED

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Post-lesson survey items on attention and understandability. The article also reports "46.67% students found extremely understandable concepts" in the experimental group versus 33.33% average and 20% a little bit in the control group.

"In experimental group, 40% students chose that activity based teaching held their attention level and 40% chose average indicator; while 26.6% students opted a little bit option, 26.6% selected average option and 20% selected not at all in control group."
