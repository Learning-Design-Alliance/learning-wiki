### Liang 2026

Liang, S., Yau, K. W., Meng, H., Chiu, T. K. F., King, I., Yam, Y., & Chai, C. S. (2026). From disengaged to self-determined: A latent transition analysis of students' AI learning motivation. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-13998-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Conditional LTA models regressed transition paths on gender, grade level, and prior AI learning experience (Table 8). The quote reports the prior-experience effect; Table 8 shows a coefficient of −0.50* (p <.05) for the Disengaged no-transition path, with gender dummy coded Male = 0, Female = 1.

> "Among students initially classified as Disengaged, those with greater prior AI learning experience were more likely to transition to the Developing and Self-Determined profiles."
