Evidence¶

Wang 2024¶

Wang, V., Leung-Gagné, M., Melnick, H., & Wechsler, M. E. (2024). Universal prekindergarten expansion in California: Progress and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/597.103

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive survey results on facility needs. The report states the majority (82%) indicated adequate space, with medium-sized LEAs less likely to say so (77%, versus 84% of small and 83% of large LEAs), and notes these classrooms are in addition to 15,425 existing UPK classrooms.

"The 249 LEAs that stated they did not have enough classroom space indicated needing a total of 946 more classrooms across California to meet projected Tk enrollment by 2025–26."

Wang 2024 (2)¶

Wang, V., Leung-Gagné, M., Melnick, H., & Wechsler, M. E. (2024). Universal prekindergarten expansion in California: Progress and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/597.103

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Open-ended survey findings on implementation challenges. The report states staffing recruitment and retention was the second most commonly cited challenge after facilities, that costs for staffing, facilities upgrades, and curriculum development often exceeded state funding, and that 37% of LEAs used grant funds for facility updates.

"Over one tenth of LEAs noted that securing sustainable funding and resources for UPk implementation was one of the biggest challenges they faced in the 2022–23 school year."
