Evidence¶

Denton Carolyn A 2012¶

Denton Carolyn A. (2012). Response to Intervention for Reading Difficulties in the Primary Grades: Some Answers and Lingering Questions. Journal of Learning Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022219412442155

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes to the Wanzek and Vaughn (2007) synthesis the finding of "larger effects for intervention provided in Grades K–1 than in Grades 2–5." No effect sizes are printed in the review; the review notes remediation in Grades 3–5 is possible but typically more challenging.

"For example, Wanzek and Vaughn (2007) found larger effects for intervention provided in Grades K–1 than in Grades 2–5."
