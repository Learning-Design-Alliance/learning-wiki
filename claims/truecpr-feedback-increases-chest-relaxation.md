## Evidence

### Majer Jolanta 2019

Majer Jolanta, Jaguszewski Miłosz J., Frass Michael, Leskiewicz Marcin, Smereka Jacek, Ładny Jerzy R., Robak Oliver, Szarpak Łukasz. (2019). Does the use of cardiopulmonary resuscitation feedback devices improve the quality of chest compressions performed by doctors? A prospective, randomized, cross-over simulation study. Cardiology Journal. https://doi.org/10.5603/CJ.a2018.0091

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized cross-over simulation study, complete chest relaxation was "95% (IQR 76–99)" with TrueCPR vs 33% (IQR 29–38) without, p < 0.001, measured with SimPad PLUS on the Laerdal simulator.

> "The degree of complete chest relaxation with the TrueCPR device was 95% (IQR 76–99) and without a device it was 33% (IQR 29–38). The difference in the degree of correctly performed chest relaxation between the tested devices was statistically significant"
