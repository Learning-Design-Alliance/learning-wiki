## Evidence

### Kasper J 2017

Kasper J, Liethmann K, Heesen C, Reissmann DR, Geiger F. (2017). Training doctors briefly and in situ to involve their patients in making medical decisions—Preliminary testing of a newly developed module. Health Expectations. https://doi.org/10.1111/hex.12565

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same one-cohort pre-post pretest (N=40 consultations from 10 doctors), Wilcoxon signed-rank tests comparing baseline with the fourth consultation. Neither patient scale showed statistically significant improvement; the article prints no effect size, so no magnitude is asserted, and equivalence was not tested.

> "No improvement was observed in the patients’ active involvement (MAPPIN‐Opatient: P=.109); in addition, the patients’ evaluation did not indicate improvement of the communication during SDM training (MAPPIN‐Qpatient: P=.145)."
