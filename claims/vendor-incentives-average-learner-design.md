### Sierra Noakes 2019

Sierra Noakes. (2019). Market Signals for Research-Based Innovations. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7c36b6fa-e221-437f-92d5-f4d3a89c0a1f

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Third listening-tour challenge, reported from conversations with district leaders and product developers. The report adds these design decisions "miss critical opportunities to leverage rich learning sciences research to truly personalize learning experiences"; no quantitative data are reported.

> "Edtech vendors lack incentive to design for unique learner needs. Instead, many education leaders and product developers shared a concern that vendors' focus on profits pushes many in the field to develop the broadest edtech tool on the market, designed for a mythical average learner, that appeals to the most possible buyers."
