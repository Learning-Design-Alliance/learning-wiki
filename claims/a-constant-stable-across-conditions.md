### Bilicioglu Gunes Ayse 2026

Bilicioglu Gunes Ayse, Zeybekoglu Yesil Serife. (2026). Examining the joint impact of missing data mechanisms and item parameter drift on the accuracy of item response theory-based test equating. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0353665

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simulation results across IPD-only (K1–K5) and missing-data (K6–K9) conditions. The article reports "no clear difference was observed between MCAR and MAR conditions in terms of the A constant", with the highest combined RMSE of 0.0661 in K21. Bias showed no meaningful variation across combined conditions.

> "For the A constant (see Table 2), bias values ranged between −0.0043 and 0.0013, and no meaningful bias was observed in any condition. RMSE of constant A increased from 0.0512 in K1 to 0.0634 in K5; however, this increase remained limited."
