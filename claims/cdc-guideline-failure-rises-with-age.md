Olds T; Maher C; Blunden S; Matricciani L. (2010). Normative data on the sleep habits of Australian children and adolescents. SLEEP. https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/33.10.1381

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparison of guideline adherence in the 4032-participant cohort; the article reports failure rising "from 3% at age 9 to 36% at age 17 on school days" (both P < 0.0001), with no boy-girl difference and 27% inadequate on Sundays (P = 0.006).

> "Overall, 17% of school days and 20% of non-school days failed to meet the American Centers for Disease Control and Prevention sleep duration guidelines."
