## Subclaims
`q2 i3` ARPS correlates positively with attention, relevance, confidence, and satisfaction as measured by RIMMS, with Pearson r values of .646, .614, .616, and .608 respectively (all p < .001). [→ Enrico Gandolfi · Richard E. Ferdig 2025](#enrico-gandolfi-richard-e-ferdig-2025)

## Evidence

### Enrico Gandolfi · Richard E. Ferdig 2025

Enrico Gandolfi · Richard E. Ferdig. (2025). Exploring the relationship between motivation and augmented reality presence using the augmented reality presence scale (ARPS). Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10446-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of ARPS and RIMMS scores from the same 90-participant study; Table 5 prints effect sizes r = .646 (attention), r = .614 (relevance), r = .616 (confidence), r = .608 (satisfaction), all Sig. (2-tailed) < .001. Post hoc power exceeded 0.93.

> "Correlations were found to be significant and positive for all the four RIMMS subfactors (see Table 5). A post hoc power analysis of the correlation coefficient, using an alpha level of 0.05, yielded a statistical power of over 0.93."
