# A two-step screening method using 'Four questions in 90 seconds' followed by the HDRS improved the efficiency and acceptability of depression screening

> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Using a short screening tool first and a more detailed tool (HDRS, validity 0.65–0.90) if positive improved screening efficiency and acceptability, per follow-up interviews with nurses. [→ Tan Jing 2023](#tan-jing-2023)

## Evidence

### Tan Jing 2023

Tan Jing, Brancatisano Olivia, Cadilhac Dominique A., Deng Bo, Wu Yanni, Li Yan, Liu Ning. (2023). Screening, prevention, and management of patients with poststroke depression in a tertiary hospital in China: a best practice implementation project. JBI Evidence Implementation. https://doi.org/10.1097/XEB.0000000000000377

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative follow-up interviews with nurses after implementation of the two-step method: the Chinese 'Four questions in 90 seconds' first, then the HDRS if positive. The article reports nurses said the combined use of the two scales "helped nurses to quickly and accurately screen patients".

> "Results from the follow-up interviews with nurses indicated that the combined use of the two scales helped nurses to quickly and accurately screen patients with stroke for depression upon admission."
