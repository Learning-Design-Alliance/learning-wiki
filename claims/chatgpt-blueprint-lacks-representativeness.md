## Evidence

### Fąferek J 2025

Fąferek J, Kononowicz AA, Bogutska N, Da Silva Domingues V, Davydova N, Frankowska A, Iguacel I, Mayer A, Morin L, Pavlyukovich N, Popova I, Shchudrova T, Sudacka M, Szydlak R, Hege I. (2025). Applying ChatGPT to plan and create a realistic collection of virtual patients for clinical reasoning training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08006-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of a ChatGPT-4o-generated blueprint for 200 VPs, assessed against the iCoViP blueprint approach for representativeness of diagnoses, key symptoms, and sociodemographics, and internal logic. The authors found it "did not meet our expectations in terms of quality or representativeness" with repetitive patterns and atypical outlines.

> "The generated blueprint did not meet our expectations in terms of quality or representativeness and showed repetitive patterns and an unusually high number of atypical VP outlines."
