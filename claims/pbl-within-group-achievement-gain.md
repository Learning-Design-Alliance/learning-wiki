## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Experimental group pre-test to post-test scores differed significantly in favor of the post-test (p = 0.000). [→ Özdemir et al. 2015](#ozdemir-et-al-2015)
`q2 i?` Control group pre-test to post-test scores showed no meaningful difference (p = 0.353). [→ Özdemir et al. 2015 (2)](#ozdemir-et-al-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Özdemir et al. 2015

Ahmet Şükrü Özdemir, Filiz Yıldız and Sevda Göktepe Yıldız. (2015). The effect of project based learning in “ratio, proportion and percentage” unit on mathematics success and attitude. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, Vol. 3, No. 1. https://ojs.uef.fi/ejsme

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Related t-test on the experimental group's achievement scores (pre-test mean 13.75, post-test mean 16.47, N = 36). The article reports a positive increase in success for the PBL group.

> "According to related the t-test resu lts which was used to compare pre-test and post-test scores of the experimental group students that were shown at Table 1  (p = 0,000 < 0,05) a meaningful difference in favour of post-test scores"

### Özdemir et al. 2015 (2)

Ahmet Şükrü Özdemir, Filiz Yıldız and Sevda Göktepe Yıldız. (2015). The effect of project based learning in “ratio, proportion and percentage” unit on mathematics success and attitude. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, Vol. 3, No. 1. https://ojs.uef.fi/ejsme

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Related t-test on the control group's achievement scores taught with traditional methods (pre-test 13.56, post-test 14.15). No statistically significant difference was detected; equivalence was not tested.

> "According to related the t-test resu lts which was used to compare pre-test and post-test scores of the control group students (Pre-test: 13.56, Post-test: 14.15, p = 0,353 > 0,05) it can be seen that there is not a meaningful difference"
