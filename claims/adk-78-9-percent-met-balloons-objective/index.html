Johnson 1994¶

Johnson, Jessie; And Others. (1994). Language Development Component: All Day Kindergarten Program 1992-1993. Ohio Disadvantaged Pupil Program Fund. Final Evaluation Report. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED379098

q2 · i2 · design · r2

Posttest-only program evaluation of the ADK language development component in Columbus Public Schools, 1992-93. The evaluation sample comprised pupils attending at least 80% of program days with a valid Balloons posttest; "195 (78.9%) sample pupils" met the objective, and the desired outcome was achieved.

"The data indicated of the 247 in the evaluation sample, 195 (78.9%) sample pupilssuccessfully completed 12 of 17 items on the concepts about print test (Balloons); 47 (19.0%) ofthis number had all 17 items correct."
