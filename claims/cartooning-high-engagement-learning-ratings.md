## Evidence

### Bahsoun Soukaina 2026

Bahsoun Soukaina, Morris Mhairi, Akam Elizabeth. (2026). Cartooning in Biological Learning. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70040

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous 18-question survey (5-point scale and free-form comments) completed via Microsoft Forms by the 79 participating students. Learning-outcome questions on improved understanding, critical thinking and identifying main points "scored on average 4.2, 4, and 4.3 respectively"; the abstract reports engagement averaged 4.8 and learning 4.5. Only descriptive averages are printed, no effect sizes.

> "The other three questions relating to improved understanding, critical thinking, and identifying the main points in the taught topic scored on average 4.2, 4, and 4.3 respectively (Questions 3, 4 & 5—Figure 5)."
