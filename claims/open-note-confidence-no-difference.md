## Evidence

### Sato 2015

Sato, B. K., He, W., Warschauer, M., & Kadandale, P. (2015). The Grass Isn't Always Greener: Perceptions of and Performance on Open-Note Exams. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0121

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-point Likert self-report ("I am very confident I understand the paper being tested") collected on the paper quiz in the same randomized comparison; means in Table 1 were 3.65 (closed) vs. 3.63 (open). No effect size printed.

> "confidence in understanding, like performance, did not vary whether or not the student was allowed to bring the article to the exam (Figure 2C)."
