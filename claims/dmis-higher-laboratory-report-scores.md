# Medical students using the 5G digital microscopy interactive system earned significantly higher laboratory report scores than controls

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The DMIS group scored significantly higher than the control group on laboratory reports assessing microscope operation proficiency and result analysis (P<.001). [→ Xu J 2025](#xu-j-2025)

## Evidence

### Xu J 2025

Xu J, Sha J, Jia S, Li J, Xu L, Shao Z. (2025). Effectiveness of a 5G Local Area Network–Based Digital Microscopy Interactive System: Quasi-Experimental Design. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/70256

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 39 medical freshmen (20 DMIS, 19 control) in a biology course; laboratory reports were scored on a rubric emphasizing "microscope operation proficiency and accuracy in result analysis," with the DMIS group scoring 90.33 (SD 2.63) versus 80.53 (SD 3.52).

> "For the performance of laboratory reports, the DMIS group scored 90.33 (SD 2.63) versus 80.53 (SD 3.52) for the control group (P<.001; see Figure 3A)."
