Evidence¶

Alexandra O. Cohen 2020¶

Alexandra O. Cohen, Kate Nussenbaum, Hayley M. Dorfman, Samuel J. Gershman and Catherine A. Hartley. (2020). The rational use of causal inference to guide reinforcement learning strengthens with age. npj Science of Learning 5:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00075-3

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The authors' interpretation of the combined attribution and modeling findings: children's explicit attributions matched task structure, yet their choices followed a one-learning-rate model. The article argues different learning mechanisms may underlie acquiring causal understanding versus using it for policy selection.

"Our results demonstrate that individuals can learn about and gain an explicit understanding of the causal structure of their environments but fail to use that knowledge to guide action selection"
