Evidence¶

Bautista 2015¶

Bautista, R. G. (2015). Students' affordance of teleologic explanations and anthropomorphic language in eliciting concepts in physics. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 5(1), 31-40. https://doi.org/10.3926/jotse.137

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Dependent t-test comparison of 48 students' explanations of 15 abstract physics concepts (Position and Speed, Velocity and Acceleration, Forces and Motion, Momentum, Thermodynamics) before and after classroom interventions; all 15 t-values significant at .05, e.g. concept 15 t = 12.573, df = 47, p = .000.

"Using dependent t-test, it shows that anthropomorphic explanatons of students to abstract concepts difer signifcantly before and afer the classroom treatment. It shows that the t-values of the 15-stems made a signifcant diference at .05 level of signifcance."
