van Viersen 2026¶

van Viersen, S., Psyridou, M., & Torppa, M. (2026). General introduction to the special issue on resilience in learning. Learning and Instruction, 101, Article 102254. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102254

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editors report, citing Kleemans et al. (2025), a study of children with developmental language disorder in which group-level analyses found no protective effects while individual-level analyses showed strong verbal reasoning partly compensated math delays. The effect held at one level of analysis but not the other.

"Analyses at the group level did not reveal any protective effects, but analyses at the individual level indicated that children with DLD and strong verbal reasoning could partly compensate delays in mathematics."
