## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Model-based predictions showed adolescents with fitness above the mean who received an E at age 13 had an 18–19% predicted probability of reaching A–D at age 16, versus 11% for those below the mean; corresponding F-grade probabilities were 4% versus 2%. ## Evidence

### Kjellenberg Karin 2025

Kjellenberg Karin, Helgadóttir Björg, Ekblom Örjan, Nyberg Gisela. (2025). Fitness and Screen Time at Age 13 Relates to Academic Performance at Age 16. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.70024

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Stata margins predictions from the fitted multilevel model (results not shown in tables), holding confounders at average values; the article reports "predicted probability to improve their grades to an A–D at age 16 were 18% among girls" and 19% among boys, versus 11% below the mean.

> "Among adolescents with fitness above the mean, who received of E at age 13, their predicted probability to improve their grades to an A–D at age 16 were 18% among girls (99% CI: 8%–28%) and 19% among boys (99% CI: 9%–29%)."
