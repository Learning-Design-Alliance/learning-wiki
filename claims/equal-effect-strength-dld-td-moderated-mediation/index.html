Evidence¶

Kleemans 2025¶

Kleemans, T., Vissers, C. T. W. M., & Segers, E. (2025). Linguistic precursors of sixth-grade geometric and fraction skills in children with and without Developmental Language Disorder. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102019. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102019

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Moderated mediation analysis (Hayes, 2018) testing Group (DLD vs. TD) as moderator for each of seven predictors of geometric skills; no interactions reached significance, so the strength of direct and indirect effects was similar for both groups.

"The moderated mediation analyses for each predictor variable as an independent measure did not reveal significant interactions in predicting arithmetic skills and sixth-grade geometric skills (all p's > .082)."

Kleemans 2025 (2)¶

Kleemans, T., Vissers, C. T. W. M., & Segers, E. (2025). Linguistic precursors of sixth-grade geometric and fraction skills in children with and without Developmental Language Disorder. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102019. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102019

q3 · i? · associational · r2

For the fraction-skills mediation model, moderated mediation showed no Group differences in direct or indirect effects (all p-values >.059), supporting a shared underlying process across DLD and TD children.

"Similar to the previous analyses, the results of the moderated mediation analyses did not show differences in direct and indirect effects between groups (all p-values >.059); the strength of the direct and indirect effects was the same for children with and without DLD."
