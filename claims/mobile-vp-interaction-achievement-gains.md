## Evidence

### Çetinkaya Levent 2024

Çetinkaya Levent, Keser İlke, Yildirim Serkan, Keser Hafize. (2024). The effect of case-based mobile virtual patient application on students’ academic achievement in clinical reasoning skills. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2322223

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-factor mixed-measures ANOVA in a quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study of 52 physiotherapy students; the Group*Measurement interaction was significant. The article reports "the combined effects of being in different learning environments and repeated measures factors on achievement were significant"; no effect size is printed in this prose sentence.

> "from pre-experiment to post-experiment, the success of the students differed significantly, in other words, the combined effects of being in different learning environments and repeated measures factors on achievement were significant (F(1–50) = 8.34, p = .006)"
