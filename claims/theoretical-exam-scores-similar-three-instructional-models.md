### Koçyiğit A 2025

Koçyiğit A, Cavlak U. (2025). Digitalization through the use of Ren’Py-Based interactive learning experiences in physiotherapy and rehabilitation education: a randomised, controlled, single-blind study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08474-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on the 20-item multiple-choice theoretical exam in the three-arm randomized trial found no significant difference (F = 0.487, p = 0.616); equivalence was not tested. The authors conclude "there is no substantial difference between instructional methods in terms of theoretical knowledge acquisition."

> "The theoretical exam scores were similar among all groups (degrees of freedom (df) = 2; mean square (MS) = 110.209; F = 0.487; CI(95%) = 0.00 to 0.07; p = 0.616)."
