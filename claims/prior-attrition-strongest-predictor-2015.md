This finding comes from the implications discussion of the longitudinal LSAGS analysis across the 2012-13 and 2015 surveys. The article reports that "The strongest predictor of working out of field in 2015 was working out of the field in 2012," and interprets that the likelihood of return is low.

> "The strongest predictor of working out of field in 2015 was working out of the field in 2012. In other words, once trained astronomers leave, the likelihood of their returning is low."
