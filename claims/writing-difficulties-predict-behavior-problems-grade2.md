## Evidence

### Kulakow 2023

Kulakow, S., Mahlau, K., & Kocaj, A. (2023). The longitudinal relationship between internalizing and externalizing behavioral problems with academic achievement in elementary school. https://osf.io/cyqak/?view_only=37eade9dd7c142e88fe9bef9451c125e

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

In the writing-domain CLPM (N = 461), "writing difficulties at T1 strongly predicted emotional problems at T2 (β = 0.15)" and the other three facets (β = 0.16–0.18); behavioral problems did not predict writing at any point.

> "writing difficulties at T1 strongly predicted emotional problems at T2 (β = 0.15), conduct problems at T2 (β = 0.16), hyperactivity at T2 (β = 0.16), and problems with peers at T2 (β = 0.18)"
