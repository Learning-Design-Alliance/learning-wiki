Between-subjects ANOVA on the three-item indispensability measure (α = .66) across the two group work conditions, plus a second ANOVA on residuals controlling for social comparison; neither showed a difference. The measure's internal consistency was low.

> "A between‐subjects ANOVA revealed no significant difference in perceived indispensability between conjunctive and additive group work."
