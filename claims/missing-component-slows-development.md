## Evidence

### Dixon and Wu 2014

L. Quentin Dixon & Shuang Wu. (2014). Understanding Language Learning: Review of the Application of the Interaction Model in Foreign Language Contexts. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 11, 23-39. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137251

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of the operational process of the interaction model, illustrated by Nabei and Swain's case study in which a Japanese college student's teacher's recasts were not necessarily translated into immediate learning because the student failed to notice them. The review concludes learners "may experience a slowdown in their language development" when a component is missing.

> "With the absence of any one of these three components "input", "interaction" and "output", learners may experience a slowdown in their language development."
