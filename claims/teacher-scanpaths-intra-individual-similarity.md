# Teachers' gaze scanpaths are more similar within an individual than between individuals, in both attentional and communicative gaze

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` During attentional gaze, intra-individual scanpath similarity was significantly greater than inter-individual similarity (M = 0.43 vs M = 0.38). [→ Nora A. McIntyre & Tom Foulsham 2018](#nora-a-mcintyre-tom-foulsham-2018)
`q3 i3` During communicative gaze, intra-individual similarity (M = 0.40) exceeded inter-individual similarity (M = 0.37). [→ Nora A. McIntyre & Tom Foulsham 2018 (2)](#nora-a-mcintyre-tom-foulsham-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Nora A. McIntyre & Tom Foulsham 2018

Nora A. McIntyre & Tom Foulsham. (2018). Scanpath analysis of expertise and culture in teacher gaze in real-world classrooms. Instructional Science 46, 435-455. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-017-9445-x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on string-edit-distance similarity scores from 40 eye-tracked teachers during attentional (questioning) episodes. Within-person comparisons exceeded between-person, "intra-individual similarities were signiﬁcantly greater (M = 0.43)", η²p = 0.43.

> "intra-individual similarities were signiﬁcantly greater (M = 0.43) than inter-individual similarities ( M = 0.38), F(1,39) = 29.14, p \\ 0.001, g2p = 0.43"

### Nora A. McIntyre & Tom Foulsham 2018 (2)

Nora A. McIntyre & Tom Foulsham. (2018). Scanpath analysis of expertise and culture in teacher gaze in real-world classrooms. Instructional Science 46, 435-455. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-017-9445-x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same repeated-measures ANOVA design applied to communicative (lecturing) episodes of the same 40 teachers. Intra-individual similarity exceeded inter-individual, "intra-individual ( M = 0.40) than in inter-individual ( M = 0.37)", η²p = 0.63.

> "( M = 0.40) than in inter-individual ( M = 0.37) comparisons, F(1,36) = 61.34, p \\ 0.001, g2p = 0.63"
