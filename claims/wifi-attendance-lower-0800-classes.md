## Evidence

### Yeo Sing Chen 2023

Yeo Sing Chen, Lai Clin K. Y., Tan Jacinda, Lim Samantha, Chandramoghan Yuvan, Tan Teck Kiang, Gooley Joshua J. (2023). Early morning university classes are associated with impaired sleep and academic performance. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01531-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Observational analysis of Wi-Fi connection logs from 23,391 students across 337 large lecture courses, with course-level effects and demographic variables adjusted. The article reports attendance at 08:00 was "about ten percentage points lower compared with lecture classes that started at 10:00 or later"; no standardised effect size accompanies this between-group contrast.

> "The Wi-Fi-confirmed attendance rate for lecture classes at 08:00 was about ten percentage points lower compared with lecture classes that started at 10:00 or later, adjusting for course-level effects and demographic variables"

### Yeo Sing Chen 2023 (2)

Yeo Sing Chen, Lai Clin K. Y., Tan Jacinda, Lim Samantha, Chandramoghan Yuvan, Tan Teck Kiang, Gooley Joshua J. (2023). Early morning university classes are associated with impaired sleep and academic performance. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01531-x

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Within-student comparisons in the same Wi-Fi attendance dataset (23,391 students) compared each student's attendance in 08:00 classes versus their later-starting classes. The article reports "a medium effect size of starting class at 08:00" with "Cohen's d: 09:00, 0.53" and d = 0.48 for 10:00 classes.

> "In within-student comparisons of Wi-Fi-confirmed attendance rates, there was a medium effect size of starting class at 08:00 relative to other class start times (start time, Cohen's d: 09:00, 0.53, 95% CI = 0.38 to 0.68;"
