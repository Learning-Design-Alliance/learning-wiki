Evidence¶

Chainikova 2018¶

Chainikova, G.R., Zatonskiy, A.V., Mitiukov, N.W., Busygina, H.L. (2018). Development of Foreign Language Lexical Competence on the Basis of a Learner’s Terminological Thesaurus and Dictionary. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2018.1.51

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-strata Fisher exact tests in the same pedagogical experiment (17 experimental, 15 control students) gave non-significant p-values for each assignment type, e.g. "Fisher test p-value: 0.6454394" for lexical assignments without a communication situation; the authors conclude there is "almost no difference in the proportions within strata".
