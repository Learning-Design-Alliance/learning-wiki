### Lek M 2026

Lek M, Stenfors T, Palmgren PJ. (2026). Unpacking the teaching–research nexus: a phenomenographic analysis of student experiences in a medical university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10653-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenographic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 20 students from five programmes at Karolinska Institutet, following Dahlgren and Fallsberg's seven steps. The article reports that "Analysis resulted in four distinct categories of understanding research, presented in an order that reflects increasing complexity."

> "Analysis resulted in four distinct categories of understanding research, presented in an order that reflects increasing complexity: (1) “Research is a spark of curiosity,” (2) “Research is exploring a knowledge gap,” (3) “Research is examining and providing new knowledge,” and (4) “Research is a continuous practice.”"
