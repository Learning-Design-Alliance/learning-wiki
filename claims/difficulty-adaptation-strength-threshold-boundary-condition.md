## Evidence

### Koskinen 2023

Koskinen, A., McMullen, J., Hannula-Sormunen, M., Ninaus, M., & Kiili, K. (2023). The strength and direction of the difficulty adaptation affect situational interest in game-based learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104694

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the discussion of the within-subject game study, the authors state "a boundary condition for the effective utilization of the adaptation of difficulty" based on the pattern that only major adaptations changed situational interest and perceived difficulty.

> "Crucially, our results show that there is a boundary condition for the effective utilization of the adaptation of difficulty. The strength of difficulty adaptation needs to surpass a certain threshold to influence motivational outcomes."
