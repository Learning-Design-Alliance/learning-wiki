# Scenario-based tutor lessons produced a significant pooled 7.4% learning gain, driven by three lessons with established construct validity while three newer lessons showed no significant gains

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pooled across all lessons, tutors showed a statistically significant 7.4% pretest-to-posttest learning gain (F=27.50, p<.001). [→ Thomas 2026](#thomas-2026)
`q3 i?` REACT_ERRORS gained 25.2% (F=30.10, p<.001), DETERMINE_KNOW 14.2% (p=.006), and GIVE_PRAISE 8.1% (p=.009), while AFFIRM_CORRECT, GUIDE_THINKING, and PROMPT_EXPLAIN showed non-significant gains of 1.4% to 2.1% (all p>.05), attributed to a ceiling effect. [→ Thomas 2026 (2)](#thomas-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Thomas 2026

Thomas, D. R., Kamikazi, M. C. A., Brandt, C., Borchers, C., & Koedinger, K. R. (2026). AI-Driven Assessment of Human Tutors: Linking Training Performance to Real-Life Practice. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18617

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Univariate ANOVAs on pretest-posttest lesson scores across 433 pooled lesson completions by 86 tutors. The article reports "a statistically significant learning gain of 7.4% (F=27.50,p<.001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Despite the flat growth in these newer lessons, the aggregate analysis on all lessons (pooled) revealed a statistically significant learning gain of 7.4% (F=27.50,p<.001), supporting overall content validity."

### Thomas 2026 (2)

Thomas, D. R., Kamikazi, M. C. A., Brandt, C., Borchers, C., & Koedinger, K. R. (2026). AI-Driven Assessment of Human Tutors: Linking Training Performance to Real-Life Practice. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18617

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-lesson ANOVAs with time as factor (post vs. pre) on tutors' pretest-posttest scores; Table 4 prints per-lesson means and F-statistics. The three newer lessons had pretest scores already ranging from 0.92 to 0.98 out of 1.0, which the authors attribute to a ceiling effect.

> "REACT_ERRORS, achieving a 25.2% gain (F=30.10,p<.001), followed by DETERMINE_KNOW (14.2% gain,p=.006) and GIVE_PRAISE (8.1% gain,p=.009)."
