### Frank V. Kowalski and Susan E. Kowalski 2014

Frank V. Kowalski and Susan E. Kowalski. (2014). Embedded Formative Assessment in the Undergraduate Engineering Classroom. [doi:10.1109/tale.2014.7062618](https://doi.org/10.1109/tale.2014.7062618)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive report of a follow-up in-class graphical formative assessment question in the same sixty-student electromagnetics course, submitted minutes after the derivation question. The authors note "how many responses ignore the result they just derived" and the substitution of the familiar conducting-sphere solution.

> "It is surprising how many responses ignore the result they just derived ! The solution that is sometimes chosen is one they are familiar with (a charged conducting sphere) rather than the one they just solved"
