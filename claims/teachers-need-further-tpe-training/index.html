Slotnik 2015¶

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2015). Change in Practice in Maryland: Student Learning Objectives and Teacher and Principal Evaluation. Mid-Atlantic Comprehensive Center (WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED568386

Statewide survey capacity-building items comparing 2014 and 2015 teacher responses (teacher N 2014=14,905; 2015=17,344). Need decreased year by year on items such as receiving SLO feedback (down 7 percentage points for teachers).

"More teachers in 2015 compared to 2014 say they are receiving the support they need to implement TPE. Despite these improvements, approximately 45% of teachers indicate that they need further training."
