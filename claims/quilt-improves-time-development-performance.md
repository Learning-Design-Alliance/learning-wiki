# The measurement QuILT improved student performance on questions about time development of the wavefunction after measurement beyond what concept tests alone achieved

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On questions related to time development of the wavefunction after a measurement (Q6 in both tests), post-test performance after the QuILT exceeded pre-test performance after concept tests only (e.g., 93% vs 70% on Test A Q6). [→ Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016](#guangtian-zhu-and-chandralekha-singh-2016)

## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students Understanding of Quantum Measurement Part 2: Development of Research-based Learning Tools. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06926](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06926)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Per-question comparison on consecutive energy measurement questions: Test A Q6 rose from 70% (concept tests) to 93% (QuILT post-test); Test B Q6 rose from 66% to 93%. Descriptive percentages only; no statistical test is reported.

> "For the questions related to the time development of the wavefunction after a measurement, e.g., Q6 in both Test A and B, the QuILT led students to a better performance compared to when they used the concept tests only."
