## Evidence

### Yurniwati 2020

Yurniwati, & Soleh, D. A. (2020). The Effectiveness of Computer-Based Problem Solving to Improve Higher Order Thinking Skills on Prospective Teachers. International Journal of Instruction, 13(2), 393-406. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13227a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with pre-test/post-test control group design; 18 prospective teachers per group studied linear equation and function for five weeks, experimental group using CBPS with GeoGebra, control using expository learning. ANOVA on post-test gave p = 0.0004, described as "statistically significant the difference of HOTS".

> "Table 7 indicates that p value = 0.0004 <0.05 is statistically significant the difference of HOTS between Experiment and control group. The post-test mean scores of the experimental group was higher than the post-test mean of the control group."
