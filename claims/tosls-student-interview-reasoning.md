## Evidence

### Gormally C. 2012

Gormally C., Brickman P., Lutz M. (2012). Developing a Test of Scientific Literacy Skills (TOSLS): Measuring Undergraduates' Evaluation of Scientific Information and Arguments. CBE—Life Sciences Education 11(4):364–377. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-03-0026

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Think-aloud cognitive interviews during Fall 2011 with 16 undergraduates (n = 10 at semester start, n = 6 at semester end), conducted by a doctoral research assistant using a rubric of correct, vague, and incorrect reasoning refined through iterative synchronous rating by two coauthors.

> "In total, students answered 81.25% of the multiple-choice questions correctly. In terms of the reasoning students provided for the correctly answered multiple-choice questions, 4.4% of responses were vague and 94.5% of responses provided correct reasoning."
