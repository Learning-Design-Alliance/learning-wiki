Qualitative analysis of students' written solving strategies across both cohorts, contrasting successful, neutral, and unsuccessful approaches. The article reports the lack of integration was "most evident in unsuccessful strategies"; no effect size is printed.

> "This lack of integration between mathematical reasoning and physical visualization was most evident in unsuccessful strategies, where conceptualization and computation were minimal or absent. As a result, the depth of the discussions was significantly restricted."
