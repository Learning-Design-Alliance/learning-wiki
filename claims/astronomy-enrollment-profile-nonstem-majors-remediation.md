### Zachary Richards and Angela M. Kelly 2023

Zachary Richards and Angela M. Kelly. (2023). Predicting Community College Astronomy Performance through Logistic Regression. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010119)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics section of the transcript analysis (N=1909). The sample's "average cumulative GPA for all astronomy students was 2.81" and mean mathematics GPA was 2.80; 58.1% were men and 26.0% qualified for a Pell Grant.

> "The data indicated that 96.0% of students were non-STEM majors, indicating most STEM majors did not take astronomy. In terms of mathematics remediation and performance, 28.0% of the students were required to register for some type of developmental mathematics course and 20.1% had to repeat at least one mathematics course."
