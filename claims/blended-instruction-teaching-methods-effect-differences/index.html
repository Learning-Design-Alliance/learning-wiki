Moderator analysis coding ten teaching methods across the pooled studies. Project-based learning, peer instruction, and learning by discussion exceed SMD 0.6; Q&A teaching (P > 0.05) shows no significant effect; the between-group test is p = 0.121 > 0.05.

"the effect sizes of blended instruction under task-driven learning, role-playing, inquiry-based teaching, and case-based teaching are all above 0.8, indicating these four teaching methods have substantial positive effects"
