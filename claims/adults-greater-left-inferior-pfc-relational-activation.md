## Evidence

### Dumontheil Iroise 2016

Dumontheil Iroise, Wolf Laura K., Blakemore Sarah-Jayne. (2016). Audience effects on the neural correlates of relational reasoning in adolescence. Neuropsychologia. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2016.05.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Voxel-wise analysis within the relational-integration network found only one region with an Age group × Task interaction, a left inferior lateral frontal cluster ([−54,17,16], k=43, Z=4.24, pFWE=0.032); no behavioural performance differences between age groups were found.

> "in adults activation in the Relational condition was significantly greater than in the Control condition (t(13)=6.14, p<0.001, ηp2=.133), while there was no significant difference in activation in adolescents (t(18)=1.66, p>0.1, ηp2=.744)"
