## Evidence

### Bisgaard Claus Hedebo 2018

Bisgaard Claus Hedebo, Rubak Sune Leisgaard Mørck, Rodt Svein Aage, Petersen Jens Aage Kølsen, Musaeus Peter. (2018). The effects of graduate competency-based education and mastery learning on patient care and return on investment: a narrative review of basic anesthetic procedures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1262-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of Kirkpatrick level-3 (behavior) outcomes across the 38 included studies. The review reports twelve studies failing to find lasting effects and six struggling to find an initial transfer effect; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Twelve of the reviewed studies for level 3 fail to find lasting effects [81, 82, 85–89, 92, 95–97], and six struggle to find an initial effect for the immediate skill transfer to patient care [70, 80, 93, 95, 96, 98]."

### Bisgaard Claus Hedebo 2018 (2)

Bisgaard Claus Hedebo, Rubak Sune Leisgaard Mørck, Rodt Svein Aage, Petersen Jens Aage Kølsen, Musaeus Peter. (2018). The effects of graduate competency-based education and mastery learning on patient care and return on investment: a narrative review of basic anesthetic procedures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1262-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review discussion of retention measurement. The review states retention intervals "ranges from 4 weeks to over 2 years" and that short intervals may miss competence decay, with results showing predominant skills decrease over time.

> "The interval for the evaluation of retention in the included studies is variable and ranges from 4 weeks to over 2 years after the completion of the educational intervention [91, 92]. Short retention intervals may be insufficient to capture competence decay over time"
