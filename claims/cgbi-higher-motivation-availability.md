## Evidence

### Yu Zhonggen 2014

Yu Zhonggen, Yu Wei Hua, Fan Xiaohui, Wang Xiao. (2014). An Exploration of Computer Game-Based Instruction in the "World History" Class in Secondary Education: A Comparative Study in China. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0096865

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Posttest feedback questionnaire with three motivational dimensions rated on 5-point scales by participants in both classes; overall motivation for CGBI (M = 22.34) significantly exceeded NCGBI (M = 21.37), reported as a "significant (p  =  .04) increase".

> "The general motivation towards CGBI (M  =  22.34) also showed a significant (p  =  .04) increase compared with NCGBI (M  =  21.37) at the significance level .05."

### Yu Zhonggen 2014 (2)

Yu Zhonggen, Yu Wei Hua, Fan Xiaohui, Wang Xiao. (2014). An Exploration of Computer Game-Based Instruction in the "World History" Class in Secondary Education: A Comparative Study in China. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0096865

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same motivational-dimension comparison; the availability of learning materials dimension favored CGBI (M = 7.71) over NCGBI (M = 6.83) significantly, described as "significantly (p  =  .00) greater".

> "the dimension "availability of learning materials" in CGBI (M  =  7.71) was significantly (p  =  .00) greater than that in NCGBI (M  =  6.83) at the significance level .05."
