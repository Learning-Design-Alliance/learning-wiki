## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Interventions limiting students' text choice (e.g., based on reading level) showed larger effects than interventions without limitation (d = 0.73 vs. d = 0.22; Q = 10.52). [→ Sangers 2025](#sangers-2025)

## Evidence

### Sangers 2025

Sangers, N. L., van der Sande, L., Welie, C., Dobber, M., & van Steensel, R. (2025). Learning a Language Through Reading: A Meta-analysis of Studies on the Effects of Extensive Reading on Second and Foreign Language Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10068-6

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator subgroup analysis over all effect measures combined (Table 3). Interventions with text limitation (k = 25) yielded d = 0.73 versus d = 0.22 without limitation (k = 57), a significant moderator (Q = 10.52**). The article notes most interventions did not limit text choice (k = 57, 70%).
