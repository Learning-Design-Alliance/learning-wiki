Evidence¶

Stern 2018¶

Stern, A., & Petrosino, A. (2018). What do we know about the effects of school-based law enforcement on school safety? San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/effects-of-school-based-law-enforcement-on-school-safety

q3 · i? · review · r2

The same systematic review of 12 quasi-experiments reported that "common non-curricular policing strategies have no overall effects on measures of crime or discipline in schools," and the brief adds that none of the multiple analyses indicated a positive impact on school safety outcomes.

"The review conducted by Petrosino and colleagues (forthcoming) also suggests that common non-curricular policing strategies have no overall effects on measures of crime or discipline in schools."
