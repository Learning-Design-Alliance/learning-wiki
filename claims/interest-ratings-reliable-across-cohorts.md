## Evidence

### Levy 2025

Levy, O., Shadi, T., Korisky, A., Bleichner, M. G., & Zion Golumbic, E. (2025). Differential effects of external noise and situational interest on neurophysiological responses during video based learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00392-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Lab study (n=32) and online screening (n=37) both rated 63 lecture segments for interest on a 1-7 scale. Segment-level variation in interest was highly correlated across cohorts ("Pearson's r = 0.827"), indicating group-level ratings capture common interest fluctuations.
