# One-sided help increases friendship initiation, but mutual help does not and may hamper it

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` One-sided help increased the likelihood of friendship initiation relative to no help. [→ van Rijsewijk 2019](#van-rijsewijk-2019)
`q2 i?` Mutual help showed a negative effect on friendship initiation versus one-sided help, based on an exceptional situation of about 20 pairs. [→ van Rijsewijk 2019 (2)](#van-rijsewijk-2019-2)

## Evidence

### van Rijsewijk 2019

van Rijsewijk, L. G. M., Snijders, T. A. B., Dijkstra, J. K., Steglich, C., & Veenstra, R. (2019). The Interplay Between Adolescents' Friendships and the Exchange of Help: A Longitudinal Multiplex Social Network Study. Journal of Research on Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.12501

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RSiena creation-function analysis of the same 41-classroom longitudinal network data. The article reports friendship initiation increased under "one‐sided help versus no help at all" (η = 1.26, SD = .18, p > .99).

> "the likelihood of friendship increased under the condition of one‐sided help versus no help at all (parameter 1; η = 1.26, SD = .18, p > .99)"

### van Rijsewijk 2019 (2)

van Rijsewijk, L. G. M., Snijders, T. A. B., Dijkstra, J. K., Steglich, C., & Veenstra, R. (2019). The Interplay Between Adolescents' Friendships and the Exchange of Help: A Longitudinal Multiplex Social Network Study. Journal of Research on Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.12501

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same RSiena analysis. The article reports a "negative effect of mutual help versus one‐sided help on friendship initiation" (η = −1.87, SD = .52, p < .01), and mutual help versus no help was not significant (η = −0.62, p = .88).

> "Surprisingly, there was a negative effect of mutual help versus one‐sided help on friendship initiation (parameter 3; η = −1.87, SD = .52, p < .01)."
