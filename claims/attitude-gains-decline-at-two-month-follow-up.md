## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mean poverty attitude scores declined by 2.3 points between T2 and T3 (p < 0.001), though they remained significantly improved over baseline. [→ McQuistan 2026](#mcquistan-2026)

## Evidence

### McQuistan 2026

McQuistan, M. R., Young, L. B., & Pendleton, C. (2026). Poverty Simulations: Are the Learning Outcomes Consistently and Uniformly Positive? Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.70090

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired-samples t-test on 153 students with paired T2–T3 data. The article reports a decline of 2.3 points (SD = 6.0, p < 0.001) and states "the T3 attitudes regressed toward the baseline mean, they remained significantly improved over baseline attitude scores."

> "Thus, H1 was supported: the improvement in poverty attitudes documented immediately after the simulation did change significantly 2 months (T3) later compared to T2. Although the T3 attitudes regressed toward the baseline mean, they remained significantly improved over baseline attitude scores."
