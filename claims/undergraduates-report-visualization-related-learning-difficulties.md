### Ivan Sudakov 2014

Ivan Sudakov, Thomas Bellsky, Svetlana Usenyuk, and Victoria Polyakova. (2014). Mathematics and Climate Infographics: A Mechanism for Interdisciplinary Collaboration in the Classroom. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1405.6435](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1405.6435)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 38 undergraduate mathematics and natural science majors in the United States and the Russian Federation. The authors state these difficulties "can be solved by using infographics," associating them with the need to improve visualization in classroom materials.

> "Surveyed students identiﬁed signiﬁcant diﬃculties in learning within undergraduate courses as: • too much information: 53%, • too few illustrative examples: 35%, and • diﬃculty in perceiving information solely from lecture: 35%."
