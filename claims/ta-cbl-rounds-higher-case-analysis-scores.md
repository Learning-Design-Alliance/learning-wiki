# New nurses in TA+CBL teaching rounds achieve higher case analysis scores than nurses in CBL-only rounds

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` New nurses trained with the think-aloud approach combined with case-based learning scored higher on a structured case analysis assessment than those trained with case-based learning alone. [→ Zhao Rui 2024](#zhao-rui-2024)

## Evidence

### Zhao Rui 2024

Zhao Rui, Ding TingTing, Meng JinPing, Lei Miao, Ma Huili. (2024). Think-aloud approach combined with case-based learning in nursing teaching round for new nurses in cancer hospital. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05891-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 50 control nurses (CBL only) and 48 observation nurses (TA+CBL) recruited by convenience sampling in 2020–2021, assessed one week post-training. The article reports the observation group scored "(95.49 ± 6.62)" versus "(90.33 ± 6.17)", t = 3.988, P < 0.001; no effect size printed.

> "The mean case analysis scores of new nurses in the control group (90.33 ± 6.17) lagged behind those in the observation group (95.49 ± 6.62), with a statistically significant difference observed (t = 3.988, P < 0.001)."
