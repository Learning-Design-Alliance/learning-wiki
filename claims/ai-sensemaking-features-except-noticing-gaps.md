### Amogh Sirnoorkar 2024

Amogh Sirnoorkar, Dean Zollman, James T Laverty, Alejandra J Magana, Sanjay Rebello, Lynn A Bryan. (2024). Student and AI responses to physics problems examined through the lenses of sensemaking and mechanistic reasoning. [doi:10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100318](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100318)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Quantitative distribution analysis of 16 student think-aloud interviews and 16 ChatGPT-3.5 zero-shot responses across both Gravitron formats, coded for five sensemaking elements. The article reports "Around 50% of the student responses evidenced the metacognitive feature of noticing inconsistencies" while no AI response did.

> "Unlike student solutions, all of the AI responses exhibited the sensemaking features with the exception of noticing “knowledge gaps”. Around 50% of the student responses evidenced the metacognitive feature of noticing inconsistencies in their knowledge systems"
