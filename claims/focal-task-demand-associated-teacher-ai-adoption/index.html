Holster 2026¶

Holster, J. (2026). Where Artificial Intelligence Enters Teacher Work. https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/talis-2024-database.html

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

Survey-weighted teacher-level logistic regression of TALIS 2024 adoption data for 56,669 teachers across 46 education-system samples, regressing AI adoption on mean focal-task demand with system indicators. The article reports "OR = 1.084, 95% CI [1.050, 1.120], p < .001".

"A one-point increase in average demand across the three matched domains was associated with 8.4% higher odds of reporting any AI use (OR = 1.084, 95% CI [1.050, 1.120], p < .001)."
