Evidence¶

Zhang Guoyong 2026¶

Zhang Guoyong, Sun Xianghong, Yang Dan, Shao Qinwen. (2026). Nursing students’ experiences in virtual simulation teaching: a meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1863553

q2 · i? · review · r3

Discussion-section comparison across the 17 included studies, of which 10 used computer-based VS and seven used more immersive VR technologies (headsets, smart glasses, or haptic simulators). The review attributes the differing limitations to each platform's respective technical constraints.

"the shortcomings of the computer-based simulation platform mainly lay in the insufficient timeliness of feedback within the computer program (9); Jeon (33), whereas the limitations of the VR platform centered on the physiological discomfort caused by VR glasses or headsets, such as dizziness and nausea (19, 34)."
