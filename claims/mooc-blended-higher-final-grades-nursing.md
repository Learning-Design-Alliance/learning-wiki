## Evidence

### Cao W 2021

Cao W, Hu L, Li X, Li X, Chen C, Zhang Q, Cao S. (2021). Massive Open Online Courses-based blended versus face-to-face classroom teaching methods for fundamental nursing course. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000024829

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized controlled trial of 181 third-year nursing undergraduates (blended n = 91, face-to-face n = 90) in the Fundamental Nursing II course; final grade combined daily performance (10%), operational performance (20%), and theoretical achievements (70%). The blended group scored higher on "operational performance (P = .001), theoretical achievements (P < .001), and final grades (P < .001)".

> "Overall, students in the MOOC-based blended teaching group were associated with higher scores for operational performance (P = .001), theoretical achievements (P < .001), and final grades (P < .001) compared to those in the face-to-face classroom group."
