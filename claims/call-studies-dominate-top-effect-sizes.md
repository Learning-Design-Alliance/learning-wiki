Post hoc analysis of the meta-analysis displaying the six largest effect sizes (Salem 3.48, Davis 1.90, Guidi 1.65, Yanguas 1.60, Stoehr 1.49, Goyette 1.33) by study type. The article reports Davis (1989) was the only traditional paper-based study among them; the rest were CALL.
