Evidence¶

Bakla A 2018¶

Bakla A. (2018). Learner-generated materials in a flipped pronunciation class: A sequential explanatory mixed-methods study. COMPUTERS & EDUCATION, 125, 14-38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.05.017

q2 · i? · design · r2

Post-instruction survey of 40 intermediate Turkish learners of English in a flipped pronunciation class; the study reports that "the learners reported mostly positive attitudes" toward flipped learning. No effect size is printed.

"although the learners reported mostly positive attitudes and that the higher and lower scorers (Moodle Scores) viewed the flipped learning model almost equally valuable"
