Evidence¶

Xin Liu 2025¶

Xin Liu, Benjamin Becker, Ya Jie Wang, Ying Mei, Haoran Dou & Yi Lei. (2025). A visual generalization gradient of conceptual stimuli based on fear acquisition in visual and auditory modalities. npj Science of Learning 10:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00318-1

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Repeated-measures ANOVA on US expectancy ratings in the acquisition phase of the fear-conditioning experiment (40 analyzed participants, 20 per group). The article reports a large effect for the CS+ vs CS− contrast: ηp2 = 0.943 (F1, 79 = 1312.167, p < 0.001).

"The main effect of stimulus type onUS expectancy ratings was signiﬁcant in the acquisition phase (F1, 79 = 1312.167,p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.943), with ratings for the CS+ being signiﬁcantly higher than those for the CS−."

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

SCR analysis of the same acquisition phase in the same 40-participant experiment; the CS+ main effect is printed as F1, 79 = 47.569, p < 0.001, with effect size ηp2 = 0.376.

"In the acquisition phase, the main e ffect of stimulus type was signi ﬁcant (F1, 79 = 47.569, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.376), with SCR being signiﬁcantly higher for the CS+ compared to the CS−."

Xin Liu 2025 (3)¶

Xin Liu, Benjamin Becker, Ya Jie Wang, Ying Mei, Haoran Dou & Yi Lei. (2025). A visual generalization gradient of conceptual stimuli based on fear acquisition in visual and auditory modalities. npj Science of Learning 10:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00318-1

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

fNIRS repeated-measures ANOVA on mPFC HbO during acquisition showed a stimulus-type main effect of F1,38 = 20.191, p < 0.001, with effect size ηp2 = 0.347; post-hoc tests show the CS+ > CS− difference "in both the unimodal group (p = 0.006) and the crossmodal group (p = 0.001)".

"The post-hoc test showed that mPFC activity was signiﬁcantly higher in responses to the CS+ than to the CS− in both the unimodal group (p = 0.006) and the crossmodal group (p = 0.001)."
