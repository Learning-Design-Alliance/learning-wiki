## Evidence

### Delaney 1998

Delaney, P.F., Reder, L.M., Staszewski, J.J., & Ritter, F. (1998). The strategy-specific nature of improvement: The power law applies by strategy within task. Psychological Science, 9(1). https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9280.00001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Trial-by-trial strategy classification from feeling-of-knowing button presses in the reanalyzed experiments. The article reports averages of "2.39 and 2.12 strategy shifts per problem" and 3.23 switches per problem, with back-and-forth shifting spanning large fractions of trials.

> "Participants frequently chose to calculate an answer after they had retrieved it on a prior trial, averaging 2.39 and 2.12 strategy shifts per problem in Reder and Ritter's (1992) experiments and 3.23 switches per problem in the Schunn et al. (1997) experiment."
