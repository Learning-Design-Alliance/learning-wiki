## Evidence

### Manuaba Ida Bagus Amertha Putra 2022

Manuaba Ida Bagus Amertha Putra, No Yi, Wu Chien-Chih. (2022). The effectiveness of problem based learning in improving critical thinking, problem-solving and self-directed learning in first-year medical students: A meta-analysis. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0277339

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of six articles evaluating critical thinking/knowledge in PBL versus conventional groups, using a random-effect model because heterogeneity p < 0.10; the authors report "no significant difference between PBL and conventional learning methods (p = 0.29)" and I2 = 93%, indicating good heterogeneity.

> "We found that for developing critical thinking, PBL was a better program. Unfortunately, there is no significant difference between PBL and conventional learning methods (p = 0.29) (Fig 2)."
