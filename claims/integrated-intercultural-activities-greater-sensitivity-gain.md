# In a quasi-experiment with pretest-posttest and control group, intercultural sensitivity development activities integrated with a scientific research methods course produced a significantly greater pretest-posttest change in intercultural sensitivity than the standard course

## Evidence

### Tuncel 2019

Tuncel, İ. (2019). The effectiveness of intercultural sensitivity development activities integrated with scientific research methods course. European Journal of Educational Research, 8(1), 301-312. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.8.1.301

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA for mixed measures on Intercultural Sensitivity Scale pretest-posttest scores of 43 experimental and 43 control students; the Group*Test interaction was significant, F(1, 84) = 48.84, p<.05. Table 6 separately lists a partial eta squared of .368 for this interaction.

> "It is ascertained that the change in the successive (iterative) measurements (pretest and posttest scores) is significant with regard to procedure groups (as experimental and control): F (1, 84) = 48.84, p<.05."
