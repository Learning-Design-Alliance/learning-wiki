### Daniel Kassler 2018

Daniel Kassler, Ira Nichols-Barrer, Mariel Finucane. (2018). Beyond "Treatment versus Control": How Bayesian Analysis Makes Factorial Experiments Feasible in Education Research. Working Paper 61. Simulation study: the authors "repeatedly simulate factorial experiments with a variety of sample sizes and numbers of treatment arms" to estimate the minimum detectable effect for classical and Bayesian methods.
