Pre/post study (N = 28): on the pre-test many students left question 6 blank and all multiple-choice options in question 7 were observed, with one or two bases most popular; after the tutorial the answer spread narrowed to the correct answer of one basis or two bases.

> "This appears to be challenging after traditional lecture-based instruction alone; however, on the post-test after the tutorial, nearly all students answered question 6 correctly, and much improvement was observed on question 7 as well, with three fourths providing the correct response (see Table 1)."
