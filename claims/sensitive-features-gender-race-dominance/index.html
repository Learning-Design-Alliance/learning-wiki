The review's analysis of sensitive features across the 12 eligible studies (Table 4). Notable exceptions were S4 and S7, which each examined four sensitive features including first-generation student status and financial need.

"This review reveals that most of the studies in the existing literature on education algorithmic fairness primarily concentrate on gender and race as sensitive features. As shown in Table 4, ten papers (S1, S2, S3, S4, S6, S7, S8, S9, S10, & S12) of the 12 papers reviewed used gender as sensitive feature while seven papers (S4, S5, S7, S8, S9, S10, & S12) used race as a sensitive feature."
