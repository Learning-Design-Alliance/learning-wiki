## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When the piston presses the volume, atoms bounce off it with higher velocity than before, increasing the gas energy, observable in the energy plot and velocity histogram; increasing the volume leads to the inverse effect. [→ M Mereš and B Tomášik 2008](#m-meres-and-b-tomasik-2008)

M Mereš and B Tomášik. (2008). A simulation of hard spheres gas for educational purposes. https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.0677 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0811.0677](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0811.0677)

Simulation demonstration in the pressing mode: one container side is turned into a piston moved by arrow buttons or dragging. The article reports atoms are "bounced oﬀ it with higher velocity than they had before", raising the gas energy; no effect size is printed.

> "One can see that when the piston is pressing the volume, atoms are bounced oﬀ it with higher velocity than they had before and this increases the energy of the gas."
