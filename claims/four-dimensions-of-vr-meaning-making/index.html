Evidence¶

Kenneth Silseth 2024¶

Kenneth Silseth, Rolf Steier, Hans Christian Arnseth. (2024). Exploring students’ immersive VR experiences as resources for collaborative meaning making and learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09413-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The interaction analysis of transcribed reflective dialogues used a review of the full corpus to identify recurring themes, then analyzed longer sequences in detail. The authors state they "identified four prominent dimensions that occurred across all groups" in how students characterized their meaning making.

"Looking for patterns across all eight groups, we identified four prominent dimensions that occurred across all groups in the way students talked about their experiences and characterized their meaning making: (1) the feeling of taking part"
