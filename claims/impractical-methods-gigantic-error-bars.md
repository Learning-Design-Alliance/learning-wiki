`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' classification of their Table 1 results: methods that could work only in a very idealised world requiring unreasonably precise measurements. For general relativity (#60), assuming a 1 ms minimum stopwatch difference over one hour leads to a minimum measurable height of 3000000 km. GPS altitude carries a typical 6–8-meter uncertainty.

> "A last group of experiments features gigantic error bars, several orders of magnitude higher than the actual height. This indicates that the method is not suited for the task at hands."
