## Evidence

### Hobbs F. Collin 2013

Hobbs F. Collin, Johnson Daniel J., Kearns Katherine D. (2013). A Deliberate Practice Approach to Teaching Phylogenetic Analysis. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe-13-03-0046

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Question-by-question logistic regression on the final laboratory exam comparing intervention (n = 134) and comparison (n = 124) groups. Significant differences appeared on questions 2a, 17a, and 20b (odds ratios 2.32, 3.49, 2.83; p < 0.05), which "required analysis or application of knowledge concerning the interpretation of evolutionary relationships."

> "we saw significantly different log odds only between comparison and intervention groups in three of the seven phylogenetics questions (Table 2). These three questions required analysis or application of knowledge concerning the interpretation of evolutionary relationships on phylogenetic trees."
