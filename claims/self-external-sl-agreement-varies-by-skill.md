## Evidence

### Zante Bjoern 2020

Zante Bjoern, Schefold Joerg C. (2020). Simulation training for emergency skills: effects on ICU fellows’ performance and supervision levels. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02419-4

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman rank correlation analysis comparing fellows' and consultants' supervision-level perceptions in the monocentric training-program assessment. The article reports correlations of "r = 0.65" and "r = 0.46" for chest tube insertion; pericardiocentesis and post-training cricothyroidotomy correlations were reported as n.s. without coefficients.

> "Self- and external assessed pre-training/post-training supervision levels were observed to correlate (r = 0.65, 95%-CI 0.37–0.82, p = 0.0002; r = 0.46, 95%-CI 0.1–0.71, p = 0.01, respectively)."
