### Nafis I. Karim 2018

Nafis I. Karim, Alexandru Maries, and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Exploring one aspect of pedagogical content knowledge of teaching assistants using the Conceptual Survey of Electricity and Magnetism. Physical Review Physics Education Research 14, 010117. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.14.010117

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative CSEM-related PCK task with TAs in the professional development course, scored against student post-test data. The article reports that on Q2 "roughly half the TAs expected that choice D would be the MCI, but this answer choice was only selected by 11% of students."

> "For example, on Q2 on the CSEM, roughly half the TAs expected that choice D would be the MCI, but this answer choice was only selected by 11% of students (see Table I)."
