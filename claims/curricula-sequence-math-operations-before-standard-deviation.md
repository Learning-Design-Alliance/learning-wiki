## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The Common Core Standards (USA), Rahmenlehrplan Sek I (Germany), and Kerndoelen Wiskunde (Netherlands) introduce addition/subtraction around first grade, mean/median/absolute values around sixth grade, and the standard deviation only at high school level. [→ Kok 2022](#kok-2022)

Document analysis of three middle school mathematics curricula used to classify the complexity of the uncertainty measures. The article reports the curricula share a similar structure, with the Common Core also mentioning IQR and MAD in sixth grade, while "The standard deviation, however, is introduced at the high school level".
