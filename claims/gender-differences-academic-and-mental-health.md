Agnafors S, Barmark M, Sydsjö G. (2021). Mental health and academic performance: a study on selection and causation effects from childhood to early adulthood. Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00127-020-01934-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive and model-based results from the SESBiC cohort; the article reports that "boys were more likely to perform below grade in reading at age 12 and to be non-eligible for higher education compared to girls", that 61% of non-eligible individuals were boys, and that girls had a fivefold increased risk for internalizing problems at age 20.

> "Interestingly, boys were more likely to perform below grade in reading at age 12 and to be non-eligible for higher education compared to girls."
