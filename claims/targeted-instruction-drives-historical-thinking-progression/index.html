Hardy 2021¶

Narrative synthesis of British cognitive-oriented empirical history education research (Schools Council History Project, CHATA) reported in the progression section; the review attributes the conclusion to researchers including Lee and Ashby, citing instruction that builds on prior understanding and aligns assessment to second-order concepts.
