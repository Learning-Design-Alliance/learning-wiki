### de Greeff 2018

de Greeff, J. W., Bosker, R. J., Oosterlaan, J., Visscher, C., & Hartman, E. (2018). Effects of physical activity on executive functions, attention and academic performance in preadolescent children: a meta-analysis. `q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis pooling 12 longitudinal physical activity program studies in children aged 6-12. The review reports "a positive effect on executive functions (g = 0.24; 95% CI = 0.09, 0.39; 12 studies)". The interval excludes zero; the effect is small.
