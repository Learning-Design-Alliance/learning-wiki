`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The synthesis reports, citing Cabell et al., a study of 492 preschool children in programs targeting at-risk enrollment. Kindergarten follow-up showed children with low preschool profiles continued to show lower language and literacy levels.

> "Using a person-centered approach to analyses, Cabell and her colleagues identified five different profiles that characterized children's early literacy and language competence."
