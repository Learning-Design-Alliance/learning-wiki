### Diego Dermeval 2018 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Overview classifying research methods by Easterbrook et al. categories plus two extra categories. The review reports illustrative scenarios as the majority (39.39%; 13 studies) and controlled experiments at 27.27% (9 studies), and states the number of papers without empirical evaluation "is still high and deserves attention by the community".

> "Fifteen papers (45.45%) are concerned in conducting empirical studies (i.e., controlled experiment, case study, and survey) on the applications of authoring tools ITS design."
