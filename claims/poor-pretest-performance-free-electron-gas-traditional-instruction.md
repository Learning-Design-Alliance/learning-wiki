### Paul Justice 2019

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Student understanding of Fermi energy, the Fermi-Dirac distribution and total electronic energy of a free electron gas. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In-class written pre/posttest in an upper-level undergraduate QM course graded with a rubric by two authors with inter-grader reliability better than 95%. The article reports that "student performance after traditional lecture -based instruction was poor on all questions" and posttest scores remained "at approximately 50% on many of the questions".

> "Table 2 shows that student performance after traditional lecture -based instruction was poor on all questions. After engaging with the CQS, although the average performance improved, it was still at approximately 50% on many of the questions."
