## Evidence

### Alias et al. 2013

Norlidah Alias, Saedah Siraj, Mohd Khairul Azman Md Daud & Zaharah Hussin. (2013). Effectiveness of Facebook Based Learning to Enhance Creativity Among Islamic Studies Students by Employing Isman Instructional Design Model. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008868

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

An experimental t-test comparison of post-test achievement in creativity in problem solving between a treatment group (n = 40) using Facebook-based learning and a control group (n = 40) using traditional classroom texts. The treatment group scored higher, with the difference in mean score between the treatment and control groups reported as 5.68.

> "Table 3 shows that the post test achievement mean score for creativity in problem solving for the control group (n = 40) is 19.48 ( SD = 12.13), while treatment group (n = 40) indicates higher achievement i.e 25.15 ( SD = 12.03)."
