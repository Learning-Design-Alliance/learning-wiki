Evidence¶

Parker 2010¶

Parker, D. R., Field Hoffman, S., Sawilowsky, S., & Rolands, L. (2010). An Examination of the Effects of ADHD Coaching on University Students' Executive Functioning. Journal of Postsecondary Education and Disability, 24(2), 115-132. https://www.edgefoundation.org

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interview responses to the third research question on well-being. All seven participants named specific stress-management gains; two students felt more coaching time was needed to answer fully. Sessions themselves were described as a welcome break from stress.

"As Parker and Boutelle (2009) found, coaching helped these students minimize the daily stress of college by enhancing their proﬁciency with managing their time, goal-directed behaviors, and emotions."
