## Evidence

### Graell M. et al 2026

Graell M. et al. (2026). Towards disentangling the polygenic contribution of dyslexia to school performance. Translational Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41398-026-04118-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Ordinal mixed-effect models in the INSchool cohort tested the concordant partition (variants with consistent direction of effect in dyslexia and EA) against school grades. The concordant partition was associated with better mathematics performance only, with no significant association in first or foreign language.

> "PGSDYS_concordant was associated with better performance in mathematics (OR = 1.10, 95% CI = 1.04–1.16), but displayed no significant association in language-related subjects"
