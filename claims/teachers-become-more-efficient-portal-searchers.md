## Evidence

### Pynoo 2012

Pynoo, B., Tondeur, J., Van Braak, J., Duyck, W., Sijnave, B. & Duyck, P. (2012). Teachers' acceptance and use of an educational portal. Computers & Education, 58(4), 1308-1317. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2011.12.026

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired samples t-tests compared per-session usage metrics between T1 and T2 for light and medium users. The results report that "these users become more efficient in searching for information", consistent with prior work cited by the authors.

> "Light (t(601) = -6.039, p<.001) and medium (t(192) = -2.013, p=.05) users download more per login and need to consult fewer pages per download at T2 (respectively t(505) = 6.769, p<.001; t(187) = 3.637, p<.001). This indicates that these users become more efficient in searching for information"
