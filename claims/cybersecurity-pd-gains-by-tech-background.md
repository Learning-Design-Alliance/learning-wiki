`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank subgroup analysis within the same 50-participant pre-post PD evaluation. The article reports the significant gain for participants "without a technology background" and the non-significant result for those with one; no effect sizes are printed.

> "participants without a technology background increased their knowledge significantly (V = 116.00, P = 0.016 < 0.05). Conversely, participants with a technology background did not (V = 28.50, P = 0.247 > 0.05)."
