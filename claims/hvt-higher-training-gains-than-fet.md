### Lee Hyunkyu 2015

Lee Hyunkyu, Boot Walter R., Baniqued Pauline L., Voss Michelle W., Prakash Ruchika Shaurya, Basak Chandramallika, Kramer Arthur F. (2015). The Relationship between Intelligence and Training Gains Is Moderated by Training Strategy. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0123259

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on training gain (post test block of session 15 minus pre test block of session 1) with regimen as between-subjects variable, gender and baseline performance as covariates, in the 50-participant experiment. The article reports "F(1,46) = 4.73, p <.05" favouring HVT.
