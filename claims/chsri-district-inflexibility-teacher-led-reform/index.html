q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview finding from the 2004 study; a principal contrasted the district's boss-model principalship with a facilitator role that empowers teachers to solve their own problems.

"Several principals and teachers felt that the district did not support leadership styles that em-phasize shared decision-making rather than a top-down approach. These teachers and principals stated that such leadership is essential in small-school reform."
