Evidence¶

Wanzek et al. 2016¶

Jeanne Wanzek, Yaacov Petscher, Stephanie Al Otaiba, Shawn C. Kent, Christopher Schatschneider, Martha Haynes, Brenna K. Rivas, Francesca G. Jones. (2016). Examining the Average and Local Effects of a Standardized Treatment for Fourth Graders with Reading Difficulties. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED577133

q2 · i1 · causal · r3

Exploratory quantile regression of GMRT posttest on the treatment dummy code, reported in Figure 1b. At the .40 quantile the intervention coefficient was 8.59 (t(1) = 8.59, p < .05), corresponding to g = 0.28; averaged across the .40 to .60 quantile range the mean effect size was g = 0.32 (SD = 0.05).

"The panel in Figure 1b illustrates that significant effects for the Passport to Literacy intervention were observed from approximately the .40 quantile to the .70 quantile. Effect sizes within this part of the distribution of the GMRT reading comprehension ranged from g = 0.23 at the .60 quantile up to g = 0.38 at the .50 quantile."
