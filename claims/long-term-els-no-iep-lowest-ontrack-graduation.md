Descriptive analysis of CPS cohorts. This group (4% of ninth-graders) had the lowest on-track rate and a graduation rate "10 percentage points lower than the district average" (74% vs. 84%).

> "Long-term English Learners without IEPs had the lowest Freshman OnTrack rate (77%), suggesting that this group was having a hard time passing their classes and earning credits in ninth grade, compared to other students."
