## Evidence

### Carpendale 2025

Carpendale, E. J., Green, M. J., White, S. L. J., Williams, K. E., Tzoumakis, S., Watkeys, O. J., Harris, F., O'Hare, K., Carr, V. J., & Laurens, K. R. (2025). British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 496–512. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12735

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bivariate multi-level SEM (Model 1) of Year 6 MCS-SEL Self-Management predicting Year 7 NAPLAN reading and numeracy in the 23,865-student cohort; Table 2 reports R² of .04 for both outcomes, above the 2% practical-significance threshold the study set.

> "Regarding Self‐Management effects, an increase of one SD equated to an increase of .21 SD in reading (8‐percentile gain) and .20 SD in numeracy (8‐percentile gain)."
