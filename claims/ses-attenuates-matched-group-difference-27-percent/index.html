Bailey 2015¶

Bailey, D. H., Nguyen, T., Jenkins, J. M., Domina, T., Clements, D. H., Sarama, J., Wolfe, C. B., & Spitler, M. E. (2015). Fadeout in an Early Mathematics Intervention: Same Old Schools or Underlying Skills? SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562173

Regression Model 1 added free/reduced price lunch and maternal education dummies to the age-adjusted matched-group model. The article reports the difference "was reduced by 27% after adjusting for socioeconomic status (Model 1)" but remained statistically significant.
