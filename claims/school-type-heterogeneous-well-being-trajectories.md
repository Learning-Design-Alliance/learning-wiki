International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17114012

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

School-type-stratified distributed fixed-effects models on the NEPS restricted sample (Table 3, Models 5-8; Figures 4e-h). The article reports no significant change for general school students at any observed time point and intermediate-school recovery to baseline after an immediate decrease.

> "In contrast, grammar school students (Figure 4h, Table 3: Model 8) are the only school type that benefits from the experience of grade retention and repetition in the short-term, middle-term and long-term."
