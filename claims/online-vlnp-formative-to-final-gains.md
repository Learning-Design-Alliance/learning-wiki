# Online VLNP test scores for etching, rinsing, bracket positioning and total score rose 3–17% from formative test to final online test

Within-system comparison of online formative versus final test results on the VLNP for 26 postgraduates, analysed with Student's t-tests. The article reports "an increase of 3–17%" for the four named items; other items improved descriptively but not significantly.

> "the scores for 'etching', 'rinsing', 'positioning bracket position', and the total score were significantly improved, with an increase of 3–17%, when comparing the formative test and the final test of online training (Student's t-tests, normal distribution, P < 0.05; Table 2)"
