## Evidence

### Feng Xiaosu 2025

Feng Xiaosu, Ren Shuanquan, Shi Peng. (2025). The relationship and mechanism of screen time and academic performance among adolescents: an empirical study based on CEPS. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1533327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Structural equation path analysis on the CEPS sample (CMIN/DF = 4.127, RMSEA = 0.013, CFI = 0.999) showed the screen time → cognitive performance path (β = −0.125) and cognitive performance → overall performance path (β = 0.403) were significant, with "the total effect size of screen time acting on overall performance through cognitive performance was −0.056".

> "the total effect size of screen time acting on overall performance through cognitive performance was −0.056, and the total effect size of screen time acting on adolescents’ overall performance was −0.209."
