## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2020

Trevor I. Smith, Kyle J. Louis, Bartholomew J. Ricci, IV, and Nasrine Bendjilali. (2020). Quantitatively ranking incorrect responses to multiple-choice questions using item response theory. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010107)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Anomalous-result analysis run on Rowan University's high-performance computing cluster: the same 1% of analyses provide the higher results for all parameters and all questions, with distributions of similar shape and relative location. For response A on question 19 the left distribution spans -4.2 to -1.9 and the right +1.3 to +2.3, leaving a gap of 3.2.
