A classroom double-rating exercise reported by Witham: after further study of the scale, students rated 32 seventh-grade compositions, exchanged sets, and rated again, so "each composition was rated by two students"; the two ratings were combined by whether marks were the same or differed by one or two steps.

> "After a further study of the scale these same students were given a set of 32 seventh grade compositions to rate. Each student had two compositions."
