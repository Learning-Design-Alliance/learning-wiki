Meta-analysis of performance growth trends for 10 U.S. cities (2014-15 to 2018-19), benchmarking city and sector growth against state averages with demographic controls. The report concludes there are "overall positive contributions that charter schools make to the learning growth of students they serve."

> "Despite the heterogeneous trends by city, the performance growth trends in many cities included in this study show that there are overall positive contributions that charter schools make to the learning growth of students they serve."
