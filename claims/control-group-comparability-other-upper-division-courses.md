## Evidence

### David J. Jones 2015

David J. Jones, Kirk W. Madison, Carl E. Wieman. (2015). Transforming a 4th year Modern Optics Course Using a Deliberate Practice Framework. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1502.02208](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1502.02208)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational tracking of ENPH students through two other traditionally taught upper-division courses (PHYS 301 and PHYS 350), measuring average standard deviations from the course average: CG was "higher than the average by 0 .25σ," EG1 0.08σ above, EG2 0.18σ below.

> "As s hown in Table V, the metric of CG was higher than the average by 0 .25σ from the average, while for EG1 it was only 0 .08σ above the average and for EG2 it was 0 .18σ below the average."

### David J. Jones 2015 (2)

David J. Jones, Kirk W. Madison, Carl E. Wieman. (2015). Transforming a 4th year Modern Optics Course Using a Deliberate Practice Framework. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1502.02208](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1502.02208)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors' conclusion from this comparability check: "the CG group to be at least as strong if not stronger than either ex perimental groups," making a weaker control-group background unlikely to explain the exam-score gains.

> "These results in fact show the CG group to be at least as strong if not stronger than either ex perimental groups in these other upper-division physics courses."
