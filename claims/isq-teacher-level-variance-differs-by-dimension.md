## Evidence

### Knol Mariska H. 2016

Knol Mariska H., Dolan Conor V., Mellenbergh Gideon J., van der Maas Han L. J. (2016). Measuring the Quality of University Lectures: Development and Validation of the Instructional Skills Questionnaire (ISQ). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149163

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the descriptive item variances (Table 3) from the multilevel analysis of 225 rated lectures, consistent across occasions. The article attributes the pattern to differences in difficulty between dimensions, noting it is harder to engage a large classroom than to structure subject matter.

> "For example, professors did not display much variance from one another in their degree of structuring (ISQ dimension: Structure). However, they varied substantially with respect to degree to which they stimulated and activated students (ISQ dimension: Stimulation and Activation)."
