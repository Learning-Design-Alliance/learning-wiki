## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Because numerical scores are assigned only after all responses are categorized, instructors can give more value to solutions of a given type after grading was completed but before scores were distributed, avoiding re-grading the whole set. [→ Cassandra Paul 2013](#cassandra-paul-2013)

## Evidence

### Cassandra Paul 2013

Cassandra Paul, Wendell H. Potter & Brenda Weiss. (2013). Grading by Category: A simple method for providing students with meaningful feedback on exams in large courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1304.0222](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1304.0222)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors describe the category M example: students who switched the vector definitions were initially considered to have made a major conceptual error, but after further thought the instructor decided the solutions showed enough evidence of understanding to indicate students just accidentally switched the labels.

> "this method allowed the instructor to give more value to solutions of this type AFTER the grading was completed, but before scores were distributed."
