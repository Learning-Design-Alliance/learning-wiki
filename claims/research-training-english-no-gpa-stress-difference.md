## Evidence

### Jiang Juan 2025

Jiang Juan, Deng Pengbo, Li Ruijia, Yang Qian, Hu Chengping, Li Yuanyuan. (2025). The effect of research training on research skills, academic performance, and stress level among medical undergraduates in China: a cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07809-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison in the same cross-sectional survey: trained students scored higher on the standardized medical English test (P = 0.023); GPA showed only a tendency (P = 0.073), and "There was no significant difference in the DASS-21 stress score" (P = 0.283).

> "the undergraduates in the trained group had higher scores on the standardized medical English test (78.6 [69.7–83.2] vs. 71.2 [65.7–78.3], P = 0.023) and tended to have better GPAs (3.70 [3.48–3.80] vs. 3.30 [3.00-3.78], P = 0.073) than did those in the untrained group. There was no significant difference in the DASS-21 stress score"
