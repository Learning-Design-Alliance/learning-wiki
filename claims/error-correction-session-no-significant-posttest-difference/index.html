Evidence¶

Karaazmak 2018¶

Fulda Karaazmak. (2018). The Effects of a Grammar Error Correction Session on Language Learners' Success. International Journal of Curriculum and Instruction 10(1), 61–70. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1207240

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-test comparing posttest scores of the experimental group (n=29) and control group (n=35) in a quasi-experimental study of 64 eleventh-grade students. The article reports "No significant difference ( p.951>.05)"; equivalence was not tested.

"No significant difference ( p.951>.05) was detected between the results of the post -test concerning the experimental ( M=48.86, SD=18.38) and the control group (M=49.14, SD=17.70) as reported in table 1."
