Theoretical argument (modus tollens) resting on the meta-analytic null far-transfer result: brain-plasticity, primitive-elements, and learning-to-learn accounts all predict far transfer that the evidence does not show.

> "Prima facie, this outcome indicates that theories based on general mechanisms, such as brain plasticity (Karbach & Schubert, 2013), primitive elements (Taatgen, 2013), and learning to learn (Bavelier et al., 2012), are incorrect when it comes to far transfer."
