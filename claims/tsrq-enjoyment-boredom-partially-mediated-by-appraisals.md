## Evidence

### Wu Qiong 2026

Wu Qiong. (2026). The impact of teacher-student relationship quality on achievement emotions in English as a foreign language within a Confucian heritage cultural context: an examination of the mediating roles of cognitive appraisals. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04561-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bootstrap mediation test in the same LSEM of 1103 Chinese college EFL learners. For boredom, indirect effects via self-concept (β = −0.070) and value appraisals (β = −0.182) were significant alongside a significant direct effect (β = −0.158), "indicating partial mediation".

> "For enjoyment, the indirect effects via EFL-related self-concept (β = 0.147, p < .001, 95% C.I. [0.110, 0.187]) and via value appraisals (β = 0.162, p < .001, 95% C.I. [0.127, 0.209]) were both significant. The direct effect of TSRQ on enjoyment also remained significant (β = 0.208, p < .001), indicating partial mediation."
