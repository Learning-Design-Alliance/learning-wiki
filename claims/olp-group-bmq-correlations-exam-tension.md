## Evidence

### Kleine-Borgmann Julian 2021

Kleine-Borgmann Julian, Schmidt Katharina, Billinger Marieke, Forkmann Katarina, Wiech Katja, Bingel Ulrike. (2021). Effects of open-label placebos on test performance and psychological well-being in healthy medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports 11:2130. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-81502-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis within the RCT, reported for Figure 3B: in the OLP group "r = 0.28, t(77) = 2.58, p = .011"; in the control group "r = − 0.03, t(73) = − 0.30, p = .766". Correlational, so no causal claim.

> "Correlation between the BMQ benefit score and the exam score in the OLP group (r = 0.28, t(77) = 2.58, p = .011, right) and control group (r = − 0.03, t(73) = − 0.30, p = .766, left)"

### Kleine-Borgmann Julian 2021 (2)

Kleine-Borgmann Julian, Schmidt Katharina, Billinger Marieke, Forkmann Katarina, Wiech Katja, Bingel Ulrike. (2021). Effects of open-label placebos on test performance and psychological well-being in healthy medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports 11:2130. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-81502-2

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis within the OLP group of the RCT. The article reports the significant negative association "r = − 0.24, t(71) = − 2.23, p = 0.028" between BMQ general benefits score and PSQ20 tension increase.

> "a correlation analysis revealed a significant negative association between the expectation (BMQ general benefits score) and the PSQ20 tension score, indicating that higher expectation scores were associated with smaller increase in tension (r = − 0.24, t(71) = − 2.23, p = 0.028)"
