## Evidence

### Dong Minghua 2026

Dong Minghua, He Huitao, Wei Bowen. (2026). When digital engagement becomes dysregulated: associations with aggression, achievement motivation, and academic performance in adolescents. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1896602

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Three-block hierarchical regression with classroom-clustered robust standard errors in 309 students across 12 classrooms. Block 3 entry of regulatory difficulty added ΔR² = 0.040 to a full-model R² of 0.344. The association was "negatively associated with GPA" and statistically significant.

> "In the final cluster-adjusted model, regulatory difficulty was negatively associated with GPA after adjustment for the demographic and design-related covariates, total aggression, and achievement motivation (B = −0.072, robust SE = 0.027, β = −0.214, 95% CI [−0.131, −0.013], p = 0.021)."
