## Evidence

### Mikami 2015

Mikami, A. Y., & Normand, S. (2015). The Importance of Social Contextual Factors in Peer Relationships of Children with ADHD. Curr Dev Disord Rep. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40474-014-0036-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports its small randomized pilot study of MOSAIC, in a 2-week summer camp with 24 children with ADHD and 113 typically developing peers using a repeated measures crossover design; results favored the MOSAIC condition on sociometric measures, supported by observations of peers behaving more positively.

> "children with ADHD were better liked and less disliked by peers, and had more reciprocated friendships, as assessed via sociometric measures, when they were in classrooms where MOSAIC was added to behavioral management and social skills training"
