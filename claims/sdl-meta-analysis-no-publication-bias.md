### Wilkes S. 2026

Wilkes S., Maggio L. A., Martin P. C., Melton J., Zheng B. (2026). Self-Directed Learning in Health Professions Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2128

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication-bias assessment of the 74 effect sizes using funnel plot inspection and Egger's regression test with a two-level random-effects model. The authors note the possibility of underreported null or negative findings remains.

> "The test result was non-significant (t(72) = 1.05, p = .297), indicating no statistical evidence of small-study effects or publication bias. The limit estimate as the standard error approaches zero was 0.03 (95% CI [–0.36, 0.41])"
