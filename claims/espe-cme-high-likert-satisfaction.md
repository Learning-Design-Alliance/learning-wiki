## Evidence

### van der Zwan YG 2026

van der Zwan YG, van Wijngaard-de Vugt C, Abu-Libdeh A, Kalaitzoglou E, Karabouta Z, Drop SLS, Boot AM, Ng SM, Idkowiak J. (2026). Global Learner Feedback on Continuing Medical Education–Accredited e-Learning Modules in Pediatric Endocrinology and Diabetes: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/67332

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of 5-point Likert responses from the mandatory postcourse surveys (PE n=283, RLS n=56, PD n=228), reported as medians (IQR) and means (SD) in Table 1; the article reports "median scores for the majority of questions were 4.0" with no effect size.

> "Across all survey items, overall learner satisfaction was high, with most responses falling within the agree or strongly agree categories (Table 1, Figure 3). The median scores for the majority of questions were 4.0, with means consistently above 4.0 for questions relating to content quality, clarity, motivation, competence, and perceived educational value."
