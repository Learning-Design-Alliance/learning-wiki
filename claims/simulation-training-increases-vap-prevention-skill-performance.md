# Scenario-based simulation training significantly increases nursing students' VAP prevention skill performance scores in a single-group pretest–posttest study

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Topbaş Eylem 2026

Topbaş Eylem, Sarmasoğlu Kılıkçıer Şenay, Tunçbilek Zahide. (2026). Prevention of Ventilator‐Associated Pneumonia in Intensive Care Units: The Effect of Scenario‐Based Simulation on Nursing Students' Skill Development Process. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70558

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pretest–posttest quasi-experiment with 36 undergraduate nursing students in an intensive care course in Türkiye; skill performance assessed by two independent observers before and after simulation training. Mean scores rose from "18.38 ± 5.06" to "38.38 ± 7.81" (Wilcoxon Z = 3.112, p = 0.002; rank-biserial r = 0.610).

> "The students' VAP prevention performance scores were significantly higher in the posttest than in the pretest (38.38 ± 7.81 vs. 18.38 ± 5.06, p = 0.002, Cohen's d = 1.25)."
