## Evidence

### Marisa de la Torre 2009

Marisa de la Torre, Julia Gwynne. (2009). When Schools Close: Effects on Displaced Students in Chicago Public Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago Urban Education Institute. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/when-schools-close-effects-displaced-students-chicago-public-schools

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of displaced students ages eight and older in 18 closing elementary schools against similar students in non-closing CPS schools, using pre- and post-closing ITBS scores to estimate expected learning trajectories. Figure 1 reports reading at -1.50** and math at -0.59** months (p < 0.01).

> "During this announcement year, reading achievement for students in schools slated for closing was about one-and-a-half months of learning below the expected level, and math achievement was more than half a month below the expected level."
