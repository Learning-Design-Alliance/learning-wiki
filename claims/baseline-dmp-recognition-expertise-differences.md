## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Before gAI, DMP recognition scores differed by expertise: JT versus T differed significantly in the infrapyloric area (p = 0.009, r = 0.22) and suprapancreatic area (p < 0.001, r = 0.48), and ST versus T also differed in the suprapancreatic area (p = 0.01, r = 0.31). `q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Baseline annotation of 48 suprapancreatic images per expertise group against EEC reference standards; printed effect sizes include "r = 0.48" (JT vs T) and "r = 0.31" (ST vs T). Pancreas Dice coefficients were comparable across groups.

> "In the suprapancreatic area, the median was 3 (IQR 2–5) in the JT group, 4 (IQR 3–5) in the ST group, and 5 (IQR 4–5) in the T group, with significant differences between the JT and T groups (p < 0.001, r = 0.48) and between the ST and T groups (p = 0.01, r = 0.31) (Fig. 3D)."
