Youth experiences in gender–sexuality alliances predict academic engagement but not disaffection through social–emotional wellbeing. Child Development, 96, 847–864. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.14209

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

This is the study's primary longitudinal finding from a three-wave path model among 627 youth in 51 GSAs, testing indirect effects of GSA experiences on academic engagement through wellbeing. The article reports that "Greater peer responsiveness in the GSA predicted increased academic engagement through increased wellbeing overall and specifically through hope and decreased negative affect."
