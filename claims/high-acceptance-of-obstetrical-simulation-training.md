## Evidence

### Monod Cécile 2013

Monod Cécile, Voekt Cora A., Gisin Martina, Gisin Stefan, Hoesli Irene M. (2013). Optimization of competency in obstetrical emergencies: a role for simulation training. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-013-3111-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Six-point Likert acceptance questionnaire (previously used by Blum et al.) completed directly after the course by 156 participants. The article reports mean scores of 5.61 for team training and 5.71 for patient safety, and 1.72 for preferring to train alone.

> "The participants considered simulation training for obstetrical emergencies as a useful method to train as a team for the management of emergency situations [5.61 (95 % CI: 5.49–5.74)] and to improve patient safety [5.71 (95 % CI: 5.60–5.83)]. When asked if they would have rather train alone, they strongly disagreed [Likert scale of 1.72 (95 % CI: 1.52–1.91)]"
