### Poon K 2018

Poon K (2018) Hot and Cool Executive Functions in Adolescence: Development and Contributions to Important Developmental Outcomes. Front. Psychol. 8:2311. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.02311

Hierarchical regression controlling general intellectual ability predicting the five SDQ parent-version domains. The article reports CGT risk adjustment as the only EF factor associated with emotional problems, accounting for an additional 10% of variance; no cool EF test associated with any other SDQ domain.
