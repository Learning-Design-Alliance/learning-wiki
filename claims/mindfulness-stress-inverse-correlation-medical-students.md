## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Elevated mindfulness was paralleled by reduced perceived stress, with significant negative correlations at T1 and T3. [→ Lampe Luisa Charlotte 2021](#lampe-luisa-charlotte-2021)

## Evidence

### Lampe Luisa Charlotte 2021

Lampe Luisa Charlotte, Müller-Hilke Brigitte. (2021). Mindfulness-based intervention helps preclinical medical students to contain stress, maintain mindfulness and improve academic success. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02578-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal observational analysis of the study cohort across first and third term using the PSS-10 and MAAS scales. The authors report "statistically significant correlations" of r = −0.4594 (T1) and r = −0.5300 (T3), both p < 0.0001.

> "Spearman rank correlation analyses performed for our study cohort revealed statistically significant correlations with coefficients of r = − 0.4594 at T1 (95% CI = − 0.5831 to − 0.3149, p < 0.0001) and r = − 0.5300 at T3"
