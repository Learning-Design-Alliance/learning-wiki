### Hensley Lauren 2021

Hensley Lauren, Kulesza Amy, Peri Joshua, Brady Anna C., Wolters Christopher A., Sovic David, Breitenberger Caroline. (2021). Supporting Undergraduate Biology Students' Academic Success: Comparing Two Workshop Interventions. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-03-0068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-report surveys administered before the intervention and at the end of the semester measured college commitments via the College Persistence Questionnaire; the Metacognition+TM group showed greater increases in degree commitment. No effect size is printed in the supplied text.

> "students who participated in the Metacognition+TM intervention experienced greater increases in their exam scores and degree commitment than those in the baseline intervention"
