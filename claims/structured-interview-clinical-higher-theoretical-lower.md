`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review synthesis of three structured-interview studies, all conducted at one medical school (University Sains Malaysia). The review reports structured-interview admits "scored higher on the clinical component of their final phase exam" but worse on the theoretical component; no effect sizes were printed.

> "Ultimately, relative to their peers without interviews, students admitted with structured interviews scored higher on the clinical component of their final phase exam (including an OSCE) but worse on the theoretical component (including a written exam and essay).28"
