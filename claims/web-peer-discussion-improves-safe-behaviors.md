## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group reported more safe behaviors (e.g., placing sharps in containers, wearing gloves) and fewer unsafe behaviors (e.g., recapping needles with both hands) than the control group at six-month follow-up (p < 0.05). [→ Chen Lulin 2026](#chen-lulin-2026)

## Evidence

### Chen Lulin 2026

Chen Lulin, Liang Danyan, Zhu Yuhuan, Huang Weihong, Li Chenglin, Liang Xiong, Liang Yutao, Lu Yunyun, Gan Yongjiang, Zhong Yao. (2026). The effect of web-based learning with peer-facilitated discussion on nursing students’ knowledge and practices in work-related biological exposure: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09333-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Six-month follow-up survey of 251 experimental and 259 control nursing students using a 6-item self-report behavior questionnaire with 'often', 'occasionally', or 'never' response options. Wilcoxon rank-sum tests compared groups.

> "The self-reported unsafe behaviors in the experimental group were lower, including removing or transferring contaminated needles and other sharp instruments bare-handedly and recapping the needle of a syringe with both hands after use (p < 0.05)."
