`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation (type e) in the Discussion: for drawing, every screen switch breaks the visual reference and forces mental re-alignment, whereas symbolic content can be held briefly in memory; hence "co-locating guidance matters most when the instructions and the task share a unified spatial frame". This is an interpretation of the study results, not a separately tested result.

> "As a result, co-locating guidance matters most when the instructions and the task share a unified spatial frame. Our findings imply that robot-mediated projection is not a general-purpose replacement for screen-based assistance, but a targeted one."
