Authors' interpretive recommendation in the Conclusion, not a tested result of this cross-sectional study; they cite Ghadamzan & Nazeri's finding that preschool painting education raised visual-spatial intelligence by up to 21%, and Wai et al. and Lubinski on spatial ability for STEM talent identification.
