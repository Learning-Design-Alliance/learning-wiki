## Evidence

### Bosma et al. 2017

Tirza Bosma, Claire E. Stevenson, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2017). Differences in Need for Instruction: Dynamic Testing in Children with Arithmetic Difficulties. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2326

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA with the three observed behavior scales (Confidence, Openness, Concentration & Cooperation) as dependent variables and instruction profile as between-subjects factor, with printed effect size partial η² = .19. Follow-up ANOVAs were significant for all three behaviors (Self Confidence p = .004, Openness p = .017, Concentration & Cooperation p = .004).

> "demonstrated significant differences in behavior among the children in the four profiles: Wilks' Λ = .535, F (3, 9) = 4.20, p <.001, partial η² = .19)."
