## Evidence

### Levitan 2016

Levitan, D., Gal-Ben-Ari, S., Heise, C., Rosenberg, T., Elkobi, A., Inberg, S., Sala, C. & Rosenblum, K. (2016). The differential role of cortical protein synthesis in taste memory formation and persistence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Time-course comparison of weak CTA memory: separate groups tested 1, 3, or 21 days after acquisition showed "no signi ﬁcant difference" (Kruskal–Wallis χ2 = 0.016; P = 0.99). The article concludes the enhancement was on the background of "a stable and non-declining memory". No effect size is printed.
