## Evidence

### Yeo Sing Chen 2023

Yeo Sing Chen, Lai Clin K. Y., Tan Jacinda, Lim Samantha, Chandramoghan Yuvan, Tan Teck Kiang, Gooley Joshua J. (2023). Early morning university classes are associated with impaired sleep and academic performance. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01531-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Six-week actigraphy study in 181 undergraduates; 6,546 sleep offset times from 174 students who reported commute time were sorted by first class of the day. Chi-squared tests gave "χ2(6) = 394.4, P < 0.001" for sleeping past class start and "χ2(6) = 487.5, P < 0.001" for waking too late to arrive on time; no standardised effect size was reported for this finding in the quote.

> "Students did not wake up in time for nearly one-third of classes that took place at 08:00, whereas they rarely slept past the start of classes that began at noon or later."
