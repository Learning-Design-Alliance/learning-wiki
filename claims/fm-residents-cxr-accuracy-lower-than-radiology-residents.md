## Evidence

### Al Shammari Malak 2021

Al Shammari Malak, Hassan Ali, AlShamlan Nouf, Alotaibi Sarah, Bamashmoos Manar, Hakami Amani, Althunyan Abdullatif, Basager Shymaa, Motabgani Sameerah, Aljubran Sawsan, Alsaif Hind S. (2021). Family medicine residents' skill levels in emergency chest X-ray interpretation. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-021-01390-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 205 physicians (34.2% response rate) with 10 chest X-ray emergency cases; Mann-Whitney comparison of family medicine residents (n=74) versus diagnostic radiology residents (n=21) showed "58.0% vs. 90.5%; U = 118; P < 0.001".

> "the diagnostic accuracy of family medicine residents was significantly less than that of the diagnostic radiology residents (58.0% vs. 90.5%; U = 118; P < 0.001)"
