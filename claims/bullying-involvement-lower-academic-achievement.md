# Bullying involvement is associated with lower academic achievement (pooled OR = 0.61)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Children involved in bullying behaviour are significantly more likely to have low academic achievement (k = 14, OR = 0.61, 95% CI 0.47-0.79, p = 0.000). [→ Muthanna 2021](#muthanna-2021)
`q3 i1` Victims only are also more likely to have low academic achievement (k = 10, OR = 0.62, 95% CI 0.47-0.83, p = 0.001). [→ Muthanna 2021 (2)](#muthanna-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Muthanna 2021

Samara, M., Da Silva Nascimento, B., El-Asam, A., Hammuda, S., & Khattab, N. (2021). How Can Bullying Victimisation Lead to Lower Academic Achievement? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of the Mediating Role of Cognitive-Motivational Factors. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 18(5), 2209. [doi:10.3390/ijerph18052209](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18052209)

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis across 14 effect sizes from the 11 included studies of children and adolescents. Bullying involvement was "significantly more likely to have low academic achievement" with OR = 0.61 and very high heterogeneity (I2 = 98.66%).

> "The combined pooled effect size showed that children who are involved in bullying behaviour were significantly more likely to have low academic achievement (k = 14) (OR = 0.61, 95% CI (0.47, 0.79), p = 0.000). The heterogeneity assessment was also significant (Q(13) = 974.27, p < 0.000, I2 = 98.66%)."

### Muthanna 2021 (2)

Samara, M., Da Silva Nascimento, B., El-Asam, A., Hammuda, S., & Khattab, N. (2021). How Can Bullying Victimisation Lead to Lower Academic Achievement? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of the Mediating Role of Cognitive-Motivational Factors. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 18(5), 2209. [doi:10.3390/ijerph18052209](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18052209)

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup meta-analysis restricted to studies of victims only (k = 10). Victims were "more likely to have low academic achievement" (OR = 0.62, p = 0.001) with heterogeneity I2 = 99.07%.

> "The results for victims only also showed significant results where victims were more likely to have low academic achievement (k = 10) (OR = 0.62, 95% CI (0.47, 0.83), p = 0.001) with a significant heterogeneity between groups (Q(9) = 966.67, p = 0.000; I2 = 99.07%)."
