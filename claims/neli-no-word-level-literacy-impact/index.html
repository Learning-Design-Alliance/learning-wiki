Evidence¶

Sibieta 2016¶

Sibieta, L., Kotecha, M., & Skipp, A. (2016). Nuffield Early Language Intervention: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects/language-for-learning/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Null finding from the same randomised controlled trial (350 pupils), measured on a secondary composite of word-level literacy (YARC letter knowledge, early word reading, and spelling). The report states it "provided no reliable evidence" of a positive impact for either version; no effect size is printed for this finding.

"However, this evalu ation provided no reliable evidence that either version of the programme had a positive impact on children’s word-level literacy skills in the short term."
