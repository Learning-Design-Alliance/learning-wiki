`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Automated benchmark evaluation of two frontier tutors over 4,529 usable post-pressure responses on the EDUFRAMETRAP test set, with two LLM judges and human adjudication of disagreements. The article reports "The aggregate adjudicated sycophancy rate is14.1%" and a two-judge disagreement rate of 11.7%.
