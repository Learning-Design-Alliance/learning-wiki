## Evidence

### Ding Xiaojie 2014

Ding Xiaojie, Zhao Liping, Chu Haiyan, Tong Na, Ni Chunhui, Hu Zhibin, Zhang Zhengdong, Wang Meilin. (2014). Assessing the Effectiveness of Problem-Based Learning of Preventive Medicine Education in China. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep05126

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Sensitivity analyses deleting one study at a time, plus Begg's funnel plot and Egger's linear regression test for publication bias. Funnel plots showed no obvious asymmetry and "no evidence of publication bias (P = 0.432)" was found.

> "Further exclusion of any single study got similar results with a range from 0.56 (95% CI = 0.37–0.75) to 0.67 (95% CI = 0.46–0.87), suggesting that the results of this meta-analysis are statistically reliable"
