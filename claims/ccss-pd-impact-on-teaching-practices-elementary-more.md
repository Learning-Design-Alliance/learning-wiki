`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

First-time 2016 survey questions asked the extent to which standards-related PD influenced teaching practices. "Between 70 and 80 percent of elementary teachers" reported strong impact on encouraging students to explain their thinking and on student communication time; 60-70 percent reported strong impact on challenging tasks and curricular coherence, with high school figures lower (around 60 percent and 40-60 percent).

> "Between 70 and 80 percent of elementary teachers in the ELA and math response groups described the impact that their standards-related professional development had on teachers' encouraging students to explain their thinking"
