### Navarro-Ibáñez 2026

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of six studies on AHS and academic performance; five reported positive associations. The review reports that Astatke found "a significant positive correlation between AHS and academic performance" and that Karabıyık identified adaptive help-seeking as a GPA predictor.

> "Astatke, (2018) reported a significant positive correlation between AHS and academic performance, while in Turkey, Karabıyık, ( 2020) identified adaptive help-seeking as a predictor of GPA among future English teachers."

### Navarro-Ibáñez 2026 (2)

Navarro-Ibáñez, M., Balladares, J., Sprovera, I., Manaut, C., & Infante, M. (2026). Help! I Need Somebody: A Systematic Review of Academic Help-Seeking Among Pre-Service Teachers. Educational Psychology Review, 38, 115. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10208-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of the countervailing finding: Schulz and Voermanek's log-data study found a negative relationship, but the review reports it "was largely explained by help-abuse", with only step-by-step hints detrimental and strategic help-seeking showing no negative association.

> "In contrast, Schulz & Voermanek, ( 2025) found a negative relationship between observed help-seeking and learning achievement in Switzerland. However, this effect was largely explained by help-abuse, as only the use of step-by-step hints was detrimental, whereas strategic help-seeking showed no negative association."
