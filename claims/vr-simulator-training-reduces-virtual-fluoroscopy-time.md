## Evidence

### Kronner 2025

Kronner, N., Strebl, M., Lenk, J., Gebauer, B., Schweizerhof, O., Frisch, A., Wintergerst, C., Uller, W., de Bucourt, M. & Can, E. (2025). Simulator training for enhanced interventional radiology education. Scientific Reports 15, 10861. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-95828-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory prospective study in which ten voluntarily enrolled medical students completed five angiographic sessions on an endovascular simulator. The abstract reports "The median virtual fluoroscopy time decreased from 19.3 min initially to 9.3 min (p = 0.007)"; results give the average final session time as 8.96 min (SD 2.56).

> "The median virtual fluoroscopy time decreased from 19.3 min initially to 9.3 min (p = 0.007), indicating enhanced procedural proficiency."
