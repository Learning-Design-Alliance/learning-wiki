Evidence¶

Lixiang Yan 2026¶

Lixiang Yan, Yueqiao Jin, Xibin Han, Dragan Gasevic. (2026). Socially fluent AI decouples conversational signals from source identity in online interaction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23426 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.23426

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Signal detection analysis of 1,572 post-interaction identity judgments from 786 participants in 471 groups showed "identity discrimination sensitivity collapses to chance levels" (d' = 0.117, 95% CI [−0.007, 0.257]); hit rate 0.317 closely matched false alarm rate 0.276.

"Across 1,572 identity judgments (685 AI targets; 887 human targets) provided by 786 participants nested within 471 collaborative groups, identity discrimination sensitivity collapses to chance levels. Signal detection analysis yielded a sensitivity of 𝑑′ = 0.117, 95% CI [−0.007, 0.257]"
