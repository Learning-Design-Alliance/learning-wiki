## Evidence

### Youqing Fang 2026

Youqing Fang, Yinhao Tang, Yanan Sun, Jiangning Liu, Ziyi Wang, Xun Zhao, Bin Liu, Weiming Zhang, Kuikun Liu, Wenwei Zhang, Kai Chen. (2026). MindCopilot: Towards Formalizing and Evaluating Granular Human-LLM Co-Writing. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23535 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.23535](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.23535)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled user study with 30 participants recruited from universities and technology companies, using an online interactive writing system instantiating all four paradigms with a unified GPT-5.1 backend. Likert-scale measures covered perceived workload, usefulness, customization, and adaptability (Figure 6).

> "Proactive paradigms (L1–L3) are preferred over user-initiated interac- tion (L0), with stateful and adaptive settings (L2, L3) achiev- ing the lowest perceived workload, highest usefulness, and strongest adaptability."

### Youqing Fang 2026 (2)

Youqing Fang, Yinhao Tang, Yanan Sun, Jiangning Liu, Ziyi Wang, Xun Zhao, Bin Liu, Weiming Zhang, Kuikun Liu, Wenwei Zhang, Kai Chen. (2026). MindCopilot: Towards Formalizing and Evaluating Granular Human-LLM Co-Writing. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23535

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interaction-log analysis in the same user study (Table 5) prints, for example, window switches falling from 15.76 (L0) to 8.15 (L3), editing time from 46.42 to about 31 minutes, and acceptance rate rising from 18.55 (L1) to 27.87 (L3).

> "Interaction logs further corroborate these perceptions: proactive collaboration substantially re- duces window switching and task completion time, while ac- ceptance rates increase from stateless to stateful and adaptive settings."
