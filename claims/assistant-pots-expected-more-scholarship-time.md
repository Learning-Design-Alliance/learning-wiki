## Evidence

### Paine A. R. 2025

Paine A. R., Wilton M., Solanki S. M., Ellefson M., Ferguson J. E., Lo S. M., Sato B. K. (2025). Reimagining the role of teaching-focused faculty in research-intensive universities: The evolution of scholarly expectations and departmental influence. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0334895

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 158 UC STEM PoTs (63% response rate of 473 invited), comparing Assistant vs Associate/Full PoTs with two-sample t-tests. Assistant PoTs expected 19.76% vs 16.08% of time on scholarship; "two-sample t-test, t(165.5) = 2.77, p = 0.006".

> "Assistant PoTs reported that they are expected to spend significantly more time on their scholarly activities, aligning with prior results (two-sample t-test, t(165.5) = 2.77, p = 0.006)."
