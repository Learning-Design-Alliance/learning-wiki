`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-tabulation of correct self-assessment, over- and underestimation by gender and semester (Table 1) in the N = 426 sample. The study reports "the percentage of female students assessing themselves realistically is higher than the percentage of their male peers" in every cohort. Table 1 shows, e.g., 19.6% of women versus 15.0% of men assessing accurately overall. No effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).
