## Evidence

### Almuzaini Ohood K. 2025

Almuzaini Ohood K., Wali Saad M., Harbi Maan H., Azhari Hala F. (2025). Evaluating the impact of a journal club activity on pharmacy students’ learning outcomes and professional skills development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07691-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fisher's Exact Test comparisons by gender within the learning outcomes domain (N = 128 male, N = 79 female); the printed proportions and p-values support each contrast. No effect sizes were printed.

> "A greater proportion of males (53.9%) reported improvement in critical thinking compared to females (39.2%, p = 0.046). Similarly, 71.9% of males reported improved presentation skills, while only 38.0% of females indicated the same (p < 0.001). For literature search skills, 70.3% of males noted improvement compared to 36.7% of females (p < 0.001)."

### Almuzaini Ohood K. 2025 (2)

Almuzaini Ohood K., Wali Saad M., Harbi Maan H., Azhari Hala F. (2025). Evaluating the impact of a journal club activity on pharmacy students’ learning outcomes and professional skills development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07691-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon rank-sum tests comparing median domain scores by gender; the printed medians and p-values support the subclaim. The general experience score difference by gender was not significant (p = 0.097).

> "Males had higher median scores compared to females in learning outcomes (8.5 vs. 5.0, p < 0.001), perception and attitude (4.0 vs. 2.0, p < 0.001), and feedback and future improvements (4.0 vs. 2.0, p < 0.001)."
