## Evidence

### Renaud Jean-Sébastien; Côté Luc 2017

Renaud Jean-Sébastien; Côté Luc. (2017). Validation of the 5-item doctor-patient communication competency instrument for medical students (DPCC-MS) using two years of assessment data. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1026-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of 4991 clinical rotation assessments from 634 students (2011–2013), analyzed separately for junior and senior students. The article reports "Cronbach’s alpha values, over .70, were acceptable", with all inter-item correlations positive and discrimination indices above .30 for all items.

> "Cronbach’s alpha values, over .70, were acceptable. Cronbach’s alpha was higher for senior (α = .83) than for junior students (α = .75)"
