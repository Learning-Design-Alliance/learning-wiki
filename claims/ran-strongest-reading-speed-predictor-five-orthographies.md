`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Stepwise regression analyses per language, controlling age and IQ in step 1 and entering phonological memory, phonological awareness and RAN simultaneously in step 2, showed RAN explained "clearly higher amounts of unique variance in reading speed" in English, French, German, and Hungarian (16.7 to 22.8%).

> "RAN explained clearly higher amounts of unique variance in reading speed than the two phonological predictor measures in English, French, German, and Hungarian (16.7 to 22.8%)."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple correlations based on grade-specific scores, tabulated separately for each language, showed reading speed had "higher associations with RAN than with the two phonological measures in all orthographies apart from Finnish".

> "Reading speed showed higher associations with RAN than with the two phonological measures in all orthographies apart from Finnish where the associations with the three predictor components were roughly equal."
