`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative analysis of course offerings from the UC Course List for 324 schools split into Cohort 1 (n=183) and Cohort 2 (n=141). The article reports that "Course offering parity between the cohorts declines with the popularity of the exams, from near parity for Calculus AB down to 50% and below for second-year AP courses."
