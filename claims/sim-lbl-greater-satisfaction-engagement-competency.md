## Evidence

### Li Jialan 2025

Li Jialan, Wu Ningling, Lu Jianyun, Tong Xiaoliang, Gao Lihua, Zeng Jinrong. (2025). Implementation and evaluation of a Sim + LBL hybrid teaching model in dermatology and venereology: a prospective study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08440-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous questionnaire with ten domains (Cronbach's alpha 0.83) completed by both cohorts. The article reports "74.1% of students in the Sim + LBL group rated Q6 at 4 or higher, compared to only 23.1% in the LBL group", plus higher Q3/Q4 satisfaction and Q9 competency gains (Fig. 2).

> "In terms of perceived learning outcomes (Fig. 2e), the Sim + LBL group outperformed the LBL group across all three dimensions: facilitation of knowledge mastery (Q6), stimulation of learning interest (Q7), and enhancement of classroom engagement (Q8)."
