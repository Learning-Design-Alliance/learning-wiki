### Dai 2026

Inquiry-based learning in STEM education: the impact of generative AI-based Chatbots on primary school students’ problem posing ability in science. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00631-0

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot test observation with 30 Grade 3 students from the same school but not in the study sample, conducted to check instrument feasibility; "more than half of the students were unable to complete all three items". The formal experiment subsequently used extended time.

> "First, the allotted time was too short: more than half of the students were unable to complete all three items, or produced only one response per item."
