### Siniarski Aleksander 2025

Siniarski Aleksander, Piątek Paweł, Karcińska Aleksandra, del Carmen Yika Alicia, Szostek Kamil, Dziwiński Tomasz, Stąpór Maciej, Golińska-Grzybała Karolina, Piórkowski Adam, Gackowski Andrzej. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blind qualitative evaluation by two independent experts of all 26 students' per-view graphs, graded on a −2 to +2 scale with consensus resolution of discordance; the printed average improvement is "+ 0.96 (SD = 0.73)". The grading scale is the authors' arbitrary global measure, not a standardized effect size.

> "It was demonstrated that the average improvement in the performance of the assumed simulated TEE examination was + 0.96 (SD = 0.73). Based on pre-established criteria, it was determined that 22 students enhanced the quality and speed of the TEE examination, 2 remained neutral, and 2 exhibited a decline in performance (Fig. 9)."
