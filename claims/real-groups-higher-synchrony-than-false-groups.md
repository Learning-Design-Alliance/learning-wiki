### Haataja 2021

Haataja, E., Malmberg, J., Dindar, M., & Järvelä, S. (2021). The pivotal role of monitoring for collaborative problem solving seen in interaction, performance, and interpersonal physiology. Metacognition and Learning, 17, 241–268. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09279-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Validation analysis comparing MdRQA synchrony measures between 19 real groups and randomly constructed false groups. "The Mann-Whitney U test showed a significant difference between the real and the false groups for percent recurrence" and also for %DET and ADL, confirming synchrony arose from collaboration rather than chance.
