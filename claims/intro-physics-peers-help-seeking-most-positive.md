### Pelakh 2025

Thematic analysis of student perceptions of resources and demands experienced in introductory physics. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.14692](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.14692)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same descriptive coding of 546 segments from 24 interviewed students. Table V reports peers at 34.1% positive and 32.9% negative (ratio 0.96), help-seeking 30.3% positive (1.56), and learning activity 26.6% positive (1.86). The abstract notes "only experiences with peers had more positive reports than negative."

> "The three lowest negative to positive ratios and the three lowest percentages of negative comments were PEERS, HELP-SEEKING, and LEARNING ACTIVITY."
