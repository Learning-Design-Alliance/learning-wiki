## Evidence

### Romanovska 2022

Romanovska, L., Janssen, R. & Bonte, M. (2022). Longitudinal changes in cortical responses to letter-speech sound stimuli in 8–11 year-old children. npj Science of Learning 7:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00118-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc paired-samples t-tests on individual beta values from the same longitudinal fMRI sample. With Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction, only the sessions 1–2 pre-CG contrast survived; sessions 1–3 pre-CG (q = 0.06) and sessions 1–2 left STG (q = 0.08) were uncorrected-level only.

> "pairwise session comparisons only yielded a significant difference between sessions 1 and 2 in the lateral inferior pre-CG cluster (t(17) = −3.038, p < 0.01; q = 0.04), while at uncorrected level, activation in this cluster also differed between sessions 1 and 3"
