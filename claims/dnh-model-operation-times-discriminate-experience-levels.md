## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Experts, attending physicians, and residents differed significantly in stepwise and total operation times on the DNH model, with residents taking the longest (total 63.00 ± 5.52 min vs experts 26.00 ± 2.74 min). [→ Zhao Fei 2025](#zhao-fei-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhao Fei 2025

Zhao Fei, Bai YunFang, Wu JunJie, Zhu ZhiHao, Mao JinLei, Aili Aikebaier, Gu HongRun, Yu ShiJie, Wang ZhiFei. (2025). A novel model of dual-network composite hydrogels for application in endoscopic submucosal dissection. BMC Gastroenterology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12876-025-04341-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-center simulation study in which 5 experts, 5 attending physicians, and 5 residents performed full ESD procedures on the DNH model with recorded times. Post hoc Tukey analysis found "statistically significant differences in stepwise operation times and total operation time between all pairs of physician groups" (p < 0.05); experts averaged 26.00 ± 2.74 min total, residents 63.00 ± 5.52 min.

> "Post hoc analysis revealed statistically significant differences in stepwise operation times and total operation time between all pairs of physician groups with different experience levels (p < 0.05, Fig. 6)."
