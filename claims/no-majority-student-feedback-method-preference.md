`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of students' stated preferences for giving placement feedback. The article reports "the most frequently occurring preference was for one-to-one feedback", while others cited "fear of repercussion"; anonymous online surveys were chosen most overall.

> "Free text comments reflected significant variability between students with respect to the preferred mechanism of feedback delivery. There was no single method of feedback cited by a majority of students as being the most useful or appropriate."
