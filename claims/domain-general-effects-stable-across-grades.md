## Evidence

### Geary David C. 2017

Geary David C., Nicholas Alan, Li Yaoran, Sun Jianguo. (2017). Developmental Change in the Influence of Domain-General Abilities and Domain-Specific Knowledge on Mathematics Achievement: An Eight-Year Longitudinal Study. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000159

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal mixed functional data analysis of 167 children followed from kindergarten through 8th grade, summing standardized beta estimates for intelligence, central executive, and prior reading at each grade. The article reports effects "ranging between .453 and .558" with no significant adjacent-grade differences.

> "The overall domain-general effects are relatively stable, ranging between .453 and .558. None of the adjacent grade comparisons differ significantly for these domain-general effects (ps > .126)"
