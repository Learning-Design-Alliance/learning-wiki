Community-area analysis of graduation and dropout trends across Chicago's 77 community areas. The report frames the South Side–North Side contrast as consistent with residential racial segregation; Figure 4 maps community areas by percent graduated by age 18.

"Consistent with Chicago's racially segregated neighborhoods, the report ﬁnds that communities on Chicago's mostly African-American South Side saw less improvement in graduation and dropout rates than did North Side communities."
