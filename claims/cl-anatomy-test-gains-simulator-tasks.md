# Medical students improved shoulder anatomy test scores after simulator tasks, with question-level improvement on the 3D-printed simulator only

## Evidence

### Massey Patrick A. 2025

Massey Patrick A., Scalisi Wayne, Barton Richard Shane, Hansen Austin, Montgomery Carver, Solitro Giovanni F. (2025). Validity Assessment of a Three-Dimensional Printed Arthroscopic Shoulder Simulator: An Experimental Evaluation of Construct Validity and Educational Value. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-25-00225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre- and posttask shoulder anatomy tests administered to medical students in the randomized trial. Average scores went "from an average of 52% to a 76% after performing the four tasks on the control and from 52% to 84% on the 3DP".

> "Medical students on their anatomy test went from an average of 52% to a 76% after performing the four tasks on the control and from 52% to 84% on the 3DP (Figure 5)."

### Massey Patrick A. 2025 (2)

Massey Patrick A., Scalisi Wayne, Barton Richard Shane, Hansen Austin, Montgomery Carver, Solitro Giovanni F. (2025). Validity Assessment of a Three-Dimensional Printed Arthroscopic Shoulder Simulator: An Experimental Evaluation of Construct Validity and Educational Value. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-25-00225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Question-level pre/post comparison of individual anatomy test items for medical students in the randomized trial. On the 3DP there was "a higher chance of medical students scored correctly on anatomy questions" (P = 0.03); on the control, no notable improvement (P = 0.14).

> "After performing tasks on the 3DP, a higher chance of medical students scored correctly on anatomy questions (P = 0.03), whereas after performing tasks on the control group, no notable improvement was found in correct answers (P = 0.14)."

### Massey Patrick A. 2025 (3)

Massey Patrick A., Scalisi Wayne, Barton Richard Shane, Hansen Austin, Montgomery Carver, Solitro Giovanni F. (2025). Validity Assessment of a Three-Dimensional Printed Arthroscopic Shoulder Simulator: An Experimental Evaluation of Construct Validity and Educational Value. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-25-00225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of overall pre/post anatomy test scores across both medical student groups in the randomized trial: "no notable difference was found in scores between students before and after tasks on the control or 3DP" (P = 0.13 and P = 0.06).

> "When comparing the overall scores of each group, no notable difference was found in scores between students before and after tasks on the control or 3DP (P = 0.13 and P = 0.06, respectively)."
