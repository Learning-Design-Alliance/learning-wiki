# Static trunk inclinations of over 20° held for more than 4 s fell by 22% from pre-test to 6-month follow-up after the training program, in one single-group pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The median proportion of shift time in static inclinations ≥20° held for >4 s fell from 4.4% to 3.6% (P < 0.001), a 22% relative reduction. [→ Kozak Agnessa 2017](#kozak-agnessa-2017)

## Evidence

### Kozak Agnessa 2017

Kozak Agnessa, Freitag Sonja, Nienhaus Albert. (2017). Evaluation of a Training Program to Reduce Stressful Trunk Postures in the Nursing Professions: A Pilot Study. Annals of Work Exposures and Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/annweh/wxw002

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

CUELA pre/post measurement: static postures, defined per DIN EN 1005-1 as trunk postures maintained >4 s at constant or slightly changing force, fell from a median 4.4% (IQR 3.0–6.7) to 3.6% (2.5–4.5) of shift time; the article reports "a significant reduction in static inclinations was also detected (22%; P < 0.001)".

> "A significant reduction in static inclinations was also detected (22%; P < 0.001), from 462 to 329 inclinations per shift (numbers of inclinations not in the table)."
