## Evidence

### Adeleke 2017

Adeleke, A. G. (2017). Effects of Geographic Information System on the Learning of Environmental Education Concepts in Basic Computer-Mediated Classrooms in Nigeria. IAFOR Journal of Education, 5(3), 125-136. https://iafor.org/archives/2017/ijedu/volume-5-issue-3/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive results from the retention test show the CM-EGIS group (N = 67) scored a mean of 88.17 (SD = 19.0249) versus the CM group's (N = 59) mean of 84.38 (SD = 17.8897). As the abstract states, "treatment groups retention mean was higher than contemporaries", though not significantly so.

> "No significant effect was found across the groups on pupils retention even though, treatment groups retention mean was higher than contemporaries."
