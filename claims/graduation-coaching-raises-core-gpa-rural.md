### Zeller 2013

Zeller, P. J., Carpenter, S., Lacefield, W. E., & Applegate, E. B. (2013). Graduation coaching in a rural district school. GEAR UP Learning Centers, Western Michigan University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1033208

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Causal-comparative analysis of 32 coached at-risk 9th graders versus 100 uncoached at-risk baseline students, using core-course GPA as the outcome. The planned contrast showed coached students "performed almost an entire letter grade better than expected" (Wald χ2 = 47.31, p < .0001).

> "Coached students performed almost an entire letter grade better than expected based on comparative student results from baseline studies (Wald χ2 df=1 = 47.31, p < .0001)."
