## Evidence

### Peters 2018

Peters, O., Körndle, H. & Narciss, S. (2018). Effects of a formative assessment script on how vocational students generate formative feedback to a peer's or their own performance. Eur J Psychol Educ, 33, 117–143. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10212-017-0344-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the two-factorial design, one factor compared students who generated peer feedback on a fictitious erroneous draft before internal feedback with students who generated internal feedback only; the sequence factor showed no effect, which the authors describe as unexpected.

> "Unexpectedly, generating peer feedback prior to generating internal feedback had no effects on the quality of assessors' subsequent internal feedback."
