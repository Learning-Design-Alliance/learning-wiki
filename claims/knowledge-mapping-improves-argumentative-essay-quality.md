## Evidence

### Mochizuki 2019

Mochizuki, T., Nishimori, T., Tsubakimoto, M., Oura, H., Sato, T., Johansson, H., Nakahara, J., & Yamauchi, Y. (2019). Development of software to support argumentative reading and writing by means of creating a graphic organizer from an electronic text. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09676-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Classroom design case study with 63 Japanese undergraduates (34 knowledge-mapping, 29 non-knowledge-mapping) reading a target text and writing essays. T tests showed the knowledge-mapping class scored higher on both measures; the article reports "a large effect size on the constructiveness score" (d = 1.073) and a medium effect on coherence (d = 0.766).

> "the average scores in the knowledge-mapping class were significantly higher than those in the non-knowledge-mapping class. The results showed a large effect size on the constructiveness score and a medium effect size on the logical coherence score."
