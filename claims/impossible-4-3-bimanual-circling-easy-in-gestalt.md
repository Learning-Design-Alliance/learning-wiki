# An otherwise impossible 4:3 bimanual circling frequency relationship can be performed comfortably when it serves visual symmetry of visible proxy flags

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants instructed to circle visible flags in symmetry, with a gear system making symmetric flag circling require a 4:3 hand-frequency relationship, could do so after brief training, implicitly performing the impossible hand pattern. [→ Dor Abrahamson · Franz Mechsner 2022](#dor-abrahamson-franz-mechsner-2022)

## Evidence

### Dor Abrahamson · Franz Mechsner 2022

Dor Abrahamson · Franz Mechsner. (2022). Toward Synergizing Educational Research and Movement Sciences: a Dialogue on Learning as Developing Perception for Action. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09668-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Apparatus experiment by Mechsner et al. (2001) in which unseen hands drive visible proxy flags, with a gear system making the right flag circle 4/3 times faster than the hand. Participants "circle the flags in symmetry" after a few minutes of training, implicitly enacting the 4:3 hand relationship. No effect size printed.

> "However, if participants are instructed to circle the flags in symmetry (after a few minutes of unimanual training with the RH handle–flag gear system), they are well able to do so (after a further few minutes of bimanual training)."
