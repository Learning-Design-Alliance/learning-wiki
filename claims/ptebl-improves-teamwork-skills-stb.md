## Evidence

### Zhao Wanchen 2024

Zhao Wanchen, Cao Yangbo, Hu Liangrong, Lu Chenxiao, Liu Gaoming, Gong Matthew, He Jinshen. (2024). A randomized controlled trial comparison of PTEBL and traditional teaching methods in “Stop the Bleed” training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05457-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 153 third-year medical students randomized 1:1 to PTEBL (n = 77) or traditional (n = 76) four-hour Stop the Bleed courses; post-course questionnaire showed "a statistically significant difference (P < 0.05)" in reported teamwork improvement between groups.

> "94.8% (73/77) of the PTEBL course participants believed their teamwork skills were improved, while 81.6% (62/76) of the traditional course participants believed their teamwork skills were improved, with a statistically significant difference (P < 0.05) observed."
