## Evidence

### Wu Zelin 2024

Wu Zelin, Gan Wenyi, Xue Zhaowen, Ni Zhengxin, Zheng Xiaofei, Zhang Yiyi. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT on Nursing Licensure Examinations in the United States and China: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/52746

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Venn diagram analysis (Figures 3 and 4) of which questions each LLM answered correctly or incorrectly in English input. The article reports ChatGPT 4.0's uniquely correct counts of 14, 33, and 26 and uniquely incorrect counts of 2, 6, and 7, while Google Bard had the most uniquely incorrect answers.

> "ChatGPT 4.0 had the highest number (n for NCLEX-RN MCQs=14; n for NNLE Theoretical MCQs=33; n for NNLE Practical MCQs=26) of uniquely correct answers and the lowest number (n for NCLEX-RN MCQs=2; n for NNLE Theoretical MCQs=6; n for NNLE Practical MCQs=7) of uniquely incorrect answers among the 3 engines"
