Ballard 2023¶

Ballard, H. L. (2023). Community and citizen science in schools: nested inquiries, productive tensions, and the role of communities. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09643-7

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary reports, citing Matuk et al. (this issue), a study in which U.S. high school students joined an open science community through a citizen science platform on human brain and behavior research with scaffolded, student-directed curriculum support. Findings are attributed to that study.

"In this study, authors found that students gained enhanced understanding of experimental design and research ethics; however, they struggled with quality peer review processes and aligning their methods to the research question."
