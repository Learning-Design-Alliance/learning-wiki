### Lenguerrand Erik 2020

Lenguerrand Erik, Winter Cathy, Siassakos Dimitrios, MacLennan Graeme, Innes Karen, Lynch Pauline, Cameron Alan, Crofts Joanna, McDonald Alison, McCormack Kirsty, Forrest Mark, Norrie John, Bhattacharya Siladitya, Draycott Tim. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

As-implemented analysis of the stepped-wedge trial modelling a categorical exposure-duration variable; all duration categories had confidence intervals crossing 1 and "overall LR test χ2=3.7, p=0.86", indicating no duration effect.

> "The duration of exposure to the intervention did not affect the outcome (ORexposed≤6months vs not exposed=1.08 (0.88 to 1.32), ORexposed6-12months vs not exposed=0.93 (0.73, 1.19), ORexposed12-18months vs not exposed=1.02 (0.75 to 1.37), ORexposed18-24months vs not exposed=1.18 (0.82 to 1.71), ORexposed≥24months vs not exposed=1.12 (0.66 to 1.87); overall LR test χ2=3.7, p=0.86)."
