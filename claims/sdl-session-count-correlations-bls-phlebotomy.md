## Evidence

### McGrath Deirdre 2015

McGrath Deirdre, Crowley Louise, Rao Sanath, Toomey Margaret, Hannigan Ailish, Murphy Lisa, Dunne Colum P. (2015). Outcomes of Irish graduate entry medical student engagement with self-directed learning of clinical skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0301-x

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlations between number of SDL sessions attended and OSCE performance in the 2010/11 examinations; the article reports a printed effect size of "rs = 0.3" for first-year BLS (p = 0.003) and rs = 0.30 (p = 0.006) for second-year phlebotomy, with no other significant correlations.

> "As the number of BLS sessions attended increased, the performance in the exam also tended to increase (Spearman’s correlation coefficient rs = 0.3, p = 0.003)"
