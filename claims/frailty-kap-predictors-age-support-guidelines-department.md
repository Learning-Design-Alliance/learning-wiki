## Evidence

### Huang Xueyan 2025

Huang Xueyan, Zhou Haifang, Feng Ying, Li Mengchi, Wang Rui, Fang Ge, Lu Lumeng, Chen Jian'ao, Jiang Wenhui. (2025). Knowledge, attitude, and practice of frailty management among clinical nurses: a cross-sectional study. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-025-05776-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple stepwise linear regression (model 5) on the 524-nurse cross-sectional survey; the article reports "five significant predictors that explained 26.4% of the KAP scores" with adjusted R2 = 0.264, F = 38.506, p < 0.001. The printed β values are unstandardized regression coefficients, so no standardized effect size is used here.

> "Among these variables, increasing age(β = 5.344), organizational support(β = 7.383), familiarity with relevant guidelines(β = 7.413), and familiarity with frailty(β = 4.139) were protective factors for the level of KAP in clinical nurses, while department(β=-0.866) was a risk factor."
