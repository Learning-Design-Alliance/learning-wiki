## Evidence

### Cotler 2017

Cotler, J. L., DiTursi, D., Goldstein, I., Yates, J., & DelBelso, D. (2017). A Mindful Approach to Teaching Emotional Intelligence to Undergraduate Students Online and in Person. Information Systems Education Journal, 15(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135358

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Eight multiple regressions run in R on Trait EI and Peer EI measures from the quasi-experiment. Intervention mode (face-to-face vs. online) was not statistically significant for either measure; the article reports no effect size for this null finding.

> "Examining the regressions using the Trait EI measure, the only independent variable that was statistically significant was the pre -test score. When looking at the Peer EI measure, individual independent variables were not statistically significant"
