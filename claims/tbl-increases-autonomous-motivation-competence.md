# In a one-group pretest–posttest study of physiotherapy students, self-reported intrinsic motivation, identified regulation, perceived competence, and perceived autonomy support rose from the lecture period to the TBL period

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Students' intrinsic motivation (d = −0.66), identified regulation (d = −0.53), and perceived competence (d = −0.35) significantly increased from the lecture period to the TBL period. [→ Jeno Lucas M. 2017](#jeno-lucas-m-2017)

## Evidence

### Jeno Lucas M. 2017

Jeno Lucas M., Raaheim Arild, Kristensen Sara Madeleine, Kristensen Kjell Daniel, Hole Torstein Nielsen, Haugland Mildrid J., Mæland Silje. (2017). The Relative Effect of Team-Based Learning on Motivation and Learning: A Self-Determination Theory Perspective. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-03-0055

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-samples t tests in a quasi-experimental one-group pretest–posttest study of 64 second-year physiotherapy students comparing four weeks of lectures (pretest) with four weeks of TBL (posttest). Figure 3 prints Cohen's d values: "intrinsic motivation, d = −0.66; identified regulation, d = −0.53"; "perceived competence, d = −0.35"; "autonomy support, d = −1.56".

> "the students’ intrinsic motivation, identified regulation, external regulation, perceived competence, engagement, autonomy support, needs satisfaction, and perceived learning, significantly increased from pretest to posttest. Further, students’ amotivation significantly decreased from pretest to posttest. The effect sizes for the mean differences in change scores are all large in magnitude."
