## Evidence

### Marchessault and Larwin 2013

Jennifer Kohart Marchessault, Karen H. Larwin. (2013). Structured Read-Aloud In Middle School: The Potential Impact On Reading Achievement. Contemporary Issues In Education Research, Volume 6, Number 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1073207

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-subjects effects analysis from the same Repeated Measures Factorial ANOVA of DORA subtest gains in the n = 70 control and n = 90 treatment groups. The article reports "significant main effect with comprehension (F = 7.496, p = .007)" and no significant main effect for vocabulary; equivalence was not tested.

> "Vocabulary and comprehension analysis reveal no significant main effect for vocabulary; however, there is significant main effect with comprehension (F = 7.496, p = .007)."
