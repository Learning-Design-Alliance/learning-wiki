## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Exposure to English language instruction correlated positively and exposure to reading instruction negatively with ROWPVT scores (standardized coefficients 0.034 and -0.032); no significant relationships with other outcomes. [→ Condelli 2010](#condelli-2010)

## Evidence

### Condelli 2010

Condelli, L., Cronen, S., Bos, J., Tseng, F., and Altuna, J. (2010). The Impact of a Reading Intervention for Low-Literate Adult ESL Learners (NCEE 2011-4003). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Non-experimental correlational analysis combining observed instructional emphasis with student attendance hours. "standardized coefficients in both cases were small (0.034 and –0.032, respectively)"; no significant relationships were found with any reading outcomes or the other English language outcomes.

> "However, the amount of exposure to English language instruction was positively and statistically significantly correlated with ROWPVT scores. The opposite pattern was found for reading instruction; exposure to reading instruction had a negative and statistically significant relationship with scores on the ROWPVT. However, the standardized coefficients in both cases were small (0.034 and –0.032, respectively)."
