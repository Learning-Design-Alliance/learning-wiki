## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a flipped classroom implementation with 20 senior students who read the module outside class, students demonstrated their understanding during activities, supporting the module's use as a standalone resource. [→ Ciaran Hughes 2020](#ciaran-hughes-2020)

## Evidence

### Ciaran Hughes 2020

Ciaran Hughes, Joshua Isaacson, Anastasia Perry, Ranbel Sun, and Jessica Turner. (2020). Teaching Quantum Computing to High School Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.07206](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.07206)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classroom implementation in which one author taught 20 senior students after AP Physics C in a flipped classroom active-learning paradigm; students chose their own topics in small groups. The judgment of standalone appropriateness is the authors' observational conclusion, not a tested comparison.

> "One motivation for this self-taught setup was to determine if the module was appropriate as a standalone resource. This proved to be true, as the students demonstrated their understanding when engaging in the activities and answering the questions."
