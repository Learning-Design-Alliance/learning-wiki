`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction test within the same univariate GLM of the ACHA-NCHA secondary analysis. The article reports the non-significant PA-by-sleep interaction (p = 0.267); equivalence was not tested.

> "The interaction between sleep and PA was also not significant, F(1, 1817) = 1.23, p = 0.267, partial η2 = 0.001. This suggests that PA and good sleep duration independently contribute to higher PWB, with no added interactive effect between the two variables."
