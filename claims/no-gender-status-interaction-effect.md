## Evidence

### Schwortz 2020

Schwortz, A. C., & Burrows, A. C. (2020). Authentic Science Experiences with STEM Datasets: Post-secondary Results and Potential Gender Influences. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.12448](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.12448)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way multivariate ANOVA of matched gains tested gender, undergraduate/science educator status, and their interaction across overall, recall/synthesis, and skills/content outcomes, finding "no additional interaction effect between gender and undergraduate/science educator status".

> "Two-way ANOVA indicated no additional interaction effect between gender and undergraduate/science educator status, indicating that gender does not have a significantly different effect on undergraduates than it does on science educators."
