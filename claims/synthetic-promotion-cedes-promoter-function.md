Conceptual analysis within Dialogical Self Theory: because the synthetic position is frictionless and always available, "consulting it is easier than convening the inner conversation", so the work of surveying and integrating voices migrates outward. This is a theoretical argument with no data.

> "When habitually enlisted, the synthetic position tends to absorb the integrative authorship the theory assigns to the meta-position and the promoter position, a process we term synthetic promotion, producing a condition we name authorial suspension."
