Survey of 105 SABER members responding to the prompt to list active-learning strategies they use in biology classrooms. The article reports "a list of 681 strategies from the responses, of which 201 were unique" and the category percentages shown in Figure 2B.

> "We collated a list of 681 strategies from the responses, of which 201 were unique. After categorizing these strategies, we found the most frequently represented strategy categories from the surveys were discussion (34%), group work (29%), and metacognition (45%; Figure 2B)."
