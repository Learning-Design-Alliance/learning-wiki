## Evidence

### Tan Kate M 2015

Tan Kate M, Flack Felicity S, Bear Natasha L, Allen Judy A. (2015). An evaluation of a data linkage training workshop for research ethics committees. BMC Medical Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12910-015-0007-y

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre-post evaluation of five one-day workshops with 98 attendees; 75 participants completed both pre- and post-test questionnaires. Median Likert ratings rose on all eight items, e.g. understanding of the data linkage process from 3 (2-4) to 4 (4-5), compared using the Wilcoxon Signed Rank test with "p < 0.001" on every item.

> "Overall there was a significant increase in participants self-evaluation of knowledge and understanding for all eight impact statements following the training (p < 0.001)."
