## Evidence

### Park Hyangyu 2026

Park Hyangyu, Han Yejin, Cho Eunbyul. (2026). Toward a sustainable model for novice ultrasound training: mixed-methods analysis of student portfolios and objective outcomes in Korean Medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09583-z

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Paired t-test analysis of 105 KM students' pre- and post-practice self-efficacy ratings (1-10 scale) in the portfolio study. Mean rose "from 2.98 (SD = 2.17) to 6.84 (SD = 1.86)", t(104) = 14.77, p < .001, Cohen's d = 1.44.

> "students' self-efficacy regarding ultrasound procedural performance increased significantly following the educational intervention, with the mean score rising from 2.98 (SD = 2.17) to 6.84 (SD = 1.86); mean difference = 3.86, 95% CI [3.34, 4.38], t(104) = 14.77, p < .001, Cohen's d = 1.44"
