Evidence¶

Doug Rohrer 2019¶

Doug Rohrer, Robert F. Dedrick, Marissa K. Hartwig, Chi-Ngai Cheung. (2019). A Randomized Controlled Trial of Interleaved Mathematics Practice. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://osf.io/pfeg4/

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Pilot study at a public middle school with two teachers and three classes each (n = 83), nearly identical to the main study apart from sample size. The printed effect size was d = 0.97, 95% CI (0.52, 1.43).

"On the test, the interleaved group (M = 0.51, SD = 0.32) outperformed the blocked group (M = 0.22, SD = 0.25). The effect size was large, d = 0.97, 95% CI = (0.52, 1.43)."
