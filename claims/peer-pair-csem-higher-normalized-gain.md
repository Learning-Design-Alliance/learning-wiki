# Students who worked in pairs on the CSEM showed significantly higher normalized gain on subsequent individual testing than a control group that took the test individually twice.

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a calculus-based introductory physics course, students who collaborated in pairs before individual testing achieved a normalized gain of 0.41 versus 0.14 for the no-intervention control group. [→ Chandralekha Singh 2016](#chandralekha-singh-2016)

## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Impact of peer interaction on conceptual test performance. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07661](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07661)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 148 IG-protocol students who worked in pairs against 178 control students from two courses who took the CSEM individually twice with no group intervention. The article reports "The normalized gain for the control group is 0.14" versus 0.41 for the paired students.

> "The normalized gain for the control group is 0.14, which is much less than the gain of 0.41 for students in th e IG protocol who worked in pairs before the individual testing two weeks later."
