## Evidence

### Pathmavarthini G 2026

Pathmavarthini G, Ughanthinii K, Shalini SB, Rubini SS, Trisha P, Devaashiny SV, Nursiati MT, Fatiah RMRZ, Dewi RR, Anwar ZK. (2026). Prediction of academic outcomes by serum cortisol, perceived academic stress, perceived memory performance, and physical activity among preclinical undergraduate medical students: a short-term observational study. BMC Psychology 14:557. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04327-6

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis H test of PRMQ scores across combined cortisol-tertile and stress-level groups in 106 students. The significant trend (p = 0.034) carries a printed effect size of η2 = 0.09, a medium effect by the article's own scale; post-hoc pairwise comparisons were not performed due to small subgroup sizes.

> "Poorer perceived memory performance (indicated by higher overall PRMQ scores) was significantly associated with higher perceived stress (moderate to high) in combination with elevated cortisol levels (2nd − 3rd tertile) (p for trend = 0.034, η2 = 0.09)"
