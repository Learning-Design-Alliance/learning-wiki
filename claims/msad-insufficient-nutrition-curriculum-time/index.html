Khiri Nasr and Kristy Howells 2025¶

Khiri Nasr and Kristy Howells. (2025). Nutritional Education in Medical Curricula and Clinical Practice: A Scoping Review on the Knowledge Deficit Amongst Medical Students and Doctors. Journal of Human Nutrition and Dietetics. https://doi.org/10.1111/jhn.70031

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing prior studies, that most UK medical students receive less than 2 h of nutrition education and that "75% of medical schools had no required clinical nutrition classes" in the USA. The review attributes these figures to the cited primary studies.

"Most UK medical students report receiving less than 2 h of nutrition education [8, 13]. This lack of time dedicated to nutrition education is like that found within USA. Krishnan et al. [14] most recently reported that 75% of medical schools had no required clinical nutrition classes."
