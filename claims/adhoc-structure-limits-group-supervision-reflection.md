# The ad hoc structure of group supervision sessions is perceived to limit reflective learning; participants want pre-determined structured content

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants reported that the ad hoc, unstructured content of group supervision sessions did not always facilitate reflective learning and would be enhanced by structured, pre-communicated content. [→ Burke 2025](#burke-2025)

## Evidence

### Burke 2025

Burke, K. L. A., Snowdon, D. A., Milne, S. C., & Farlie, M. K. (2025). Exploring Physiotherapists' Perspectives of Group Clinical Supervision Effectiveness for Aquatic Physiotherapy Skill Development: A Qualitative Study. Physiotherapy Research International. https://doi.org/10.1002/pri.70044

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group theme on structure and processes: while tailored ad hoc content suited current caseloads, participants wanted a guiding framework, pre-session preparation, and documented tasks to increase accountability for contributing.

> "The main shortcoming of group clinical supervision reported was a lack of structure and processes; participants believed that the ad hoc approach limited reflective practice and would be enhanced if content were structured and determined prior to the session to allow physiotherapists time to prepare for the sessions."
