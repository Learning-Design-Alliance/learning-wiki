## Evidence

### Pietsch Urs 2020

Pietsch Urs, Lischke Volker, Sollid Stephen J. M., Prückner Stephan, Theiler Lorenz, Greif Robert, Albrecht Roland. (2020). Efficacy and efficiency of indoor nighttime human external cargo mission simulation in a high-fidelity training Centre. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-020-00755-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' own estimate, based on approximately 260 kg fuel per flight hour and about 3 kg CO2 per kg jet fuel, applied to more than 45 h per year of required outdoor training; the article states emissions were "difficult to measure precisely".

> "In more than 45 h per year of real flight time for required outdoor training, this would be a savings of more than 35 tons of CO2 if HEC training were held indoors."
