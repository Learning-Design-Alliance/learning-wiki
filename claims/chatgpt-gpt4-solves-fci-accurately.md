## Evidence

### Fabian Kieser 2023

Fabian Kieser, Peter Wulff, Jochen Kuhn, and Stefan Küchemann. (2023). Educational data augmentation in physics education research using ChatGPT. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020150](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020150)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of N = 14 ChatGPT (GPT-4) response sets on the 30-item FCI with figures transcribed to text, reported in the Findings section on RQ1: "ChatGPT scored on average 83% in the FCI". Item-level variance was zero for most items.

> "We found that M =0.83 (SD=0.22), i.e., ChatGPT scored on average 83% in the FCI (with some transcribed items)."
