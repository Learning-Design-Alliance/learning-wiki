# MDETs use did not outperform conventional teaching on conceptual learning in a regular high-school mechanics course

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` ANCOVA found no significant effect of treatment (with vs. without MDETs) on the learning post-test. [→ A. Gasparini 2026](#a-gasparini-2026)
`q3 i?` An item-level ANCOVA with multiple-comparison correction also found no differences between control and treatment groups. [→ A. Gasparini 2026 (2)](#a-gasparini-2026-2)

## Evidence

### A. Gasparini 2026

A. Gasparini, F. Stern, M. Delaval, A. Müller. (2026). Using smartphones and tablets as experimental tools in the physics classroom: Effects on learning and motivation. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.10959](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.10959)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on the conceptual learning post-test in the main study (N = 111), with class group and learning pre-test as covariates, found no significant treatment effect. Equivalence between treatments was not tested; the result indicates no detected difference.

> "No significant effect was observed of the independent variable treatment (F(1, 109) = 0.026, p = 0.87)."

### A. Gasparini 2026 (2)

A. Gasparini, F. Stern, M. Delaval, A. Müller. (2026). Using smartphones and tablets as experimental tools in the physics classroom: Effects on learning and motivation. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.10959](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.10959)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A supplementary ANCOVA on the individual item level of the learning post-test, using the Benjamini-Hochberg correction, likewise found no control-treatment differences.

> "Taking into account the correction for multiple significance testing [107], no differences were found between the control and the treatment groups."
