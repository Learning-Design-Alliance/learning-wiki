## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu 2024

Guangtian Zhu, Jing Hu, Chun Du. (2024). Assisting Tibetan Students in Learning Quantum Mechanics via Mathematica. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.18810](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.18810)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured think-aloud interviews with six Tibetan and six Han students, none with prior Mathematica experience, solving a QM question and a calculus question on a laptop. The article reports "Four Han students were able to write the proper codes" versus two Tibetan students, and only one Tibetan student interpreted sample codes.

> "Four Han students were able to write the proper codes in Mathematica whereas only two Tibetan students successfully did so."
