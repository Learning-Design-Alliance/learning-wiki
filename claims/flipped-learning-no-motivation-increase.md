## Evidence

### Moll-Khosrawi Parisa 2021

Moll-Khosrawi Parisa, Zöllner Christian, Cencin Nadine, Schulte-Uentrop Leonie. (2021). Flipped learning enhances non-technical skill performance in simulation-based education: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02766-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome in the randomised controlled trial, measured with the German Situational Motivation Scale (intervention n = 36, control n = 40). The article reports "negligible and not significant" pre-post differences in autonomous and controlled motivation; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "In both groups, differences between the pre- and post-test measurements of motivation were negligible and not significant (Table 5)."
