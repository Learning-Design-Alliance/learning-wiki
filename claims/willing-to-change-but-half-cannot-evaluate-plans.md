## Evidence

### Stanton 2015

Stanton, J. D., Neider, X. N., Gallegos, I. J., & Clark, N. C. (2015). Differences in Metacognitive Regulation in Introductory Biology Students: When Prompts Are Not Enough. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0135

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of E1-SE assignments coded evaluating and planning for exam 2 (n = 245). The article reports 98.4% were willing to change, but "only 110 students out of 245 (44.9%) indicated that their selection was based on their exam 1 experiences (Table 2)"; 55.1% gave no evidence of reflection-based adjustment.

> "Surprisingly, 241 out of 245 students (98.4%) were willing to change their initial study plan. Only four out of 245 (1.6%) reported that they would not change their plans in any way."
