## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Schools allocated to TEEP showed no statistically significant difference in GCSE maths scores compared with control schools (Hedges g = -0.02). [→ Institute for Effective Education 2016](#institute-for-effective-education-2016)

## Evidence

### Institute for Effective Education 2016

Institute for Effective Education. (2016). The Teacher Effectiveness Enhancement Programme: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581110

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat multilevel analysis of GCSE maths scores from 45 schools controlling for individual KS2 scores. Table 7 reports Hedges g = -0.02 (-0.13, 0.10), p = 0.78, with 10,449 pupils in the model; the difference favoured control but was not significant.

> "In some analyses the effect sizes show a small negative effect in favour of the control group, but these differences were not statistically significant."
