Evidence¶

Farrokhnia 2024¶

Farrokhnia, M., Noroozi, O., Baggen, Y., Biemans, H., & Weinberger, A. (2024). Improving hybrid brainstorming outcomes with computer-supported scaffolds: Prompts and cognitive group awareness. Computers & Education 227 (2025) 105229. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105229

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Randomized between-subjects experiment with 94 students in triads; ANCOVA comparing first individual phase across control, prompts, and prompts+awareness conditions, adjusting for educational level and entrepreneurship attitude. The overall condition effect on originality was significant (F(2,89) = 10.56, p < .001, ηp² = 0.19), with "significantly higher (p ≤ .001)" originality in both experimental conditions.

"the average originality of ideas generated in experimental 1 (Adjusted M = 2.42, SE = 0.09) and 2 (Adjusted M = 2.54, SE = 0.09) conditions were significantly higher (p ≤ .001) than those in control condition (Adjusted M = 1.98, SE = 0.09)"
