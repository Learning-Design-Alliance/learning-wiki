# Children spell regular words more accurately than pseudowords and irregular words, and irregular word accuracy falls below pseudoword accuracy until Yr 5

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Regular word accuracy was significantly higher than pseudoword and irregular word accuracy, and pseudoword accuracy was significantly higher than irregular word accuracy. [→ Niolaki 2022](#niolaki-2022)
`q3 i?` Irregular word accuracy was significantly lower than pseudoword accuracy up to Yr 4, but significantly higher in Yr 5 and Yr 6. [→ Niolaki 2022 (2)](#niolaki-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Niolaki 2022

Niolaki, G., Vousden, J., Terzopoulos, A., Shepherd, D.-L., Debney, L., & Masterson, J. (2022). Spelling predictors; investigating the role of phonological ability and rapid naming in a large cross-sectional British study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101635

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bonferroni-corrected pairwise comparisons of word type in the mixed-model analysis of 641 children's spelling; regular words (M = 21.6) exceeded pseudowords (M = 18.2) and irregular words (M = 15.4), and pseudowords exceeded irregular words.

> "These revealed that accuracy for regular words was significantly (ps < .001) higher (M = 21.6, SD = 10.8) than for pseudowords (M = 18.2, SD = 8.9) or irregular words (M = 15.4, SD = 11.7)."

### Niolaki 2022 (2)

Niolaki, G., Vousden, J., Terzopoulos, A., Shepherd, D.-L., Debney, L., & Masterson, J. (2022). Spelling predictors; investigating the role of phonological ability and rapid naming in a large cross-sectional British study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101635

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Year-group pairwise comparisons in the same mixed-model analysis show the irregular-versus-pseudoword ordering reverses with experience: irregular accuracy is lower up to Yr 4 (apart from Yr 3) and higher in Yr 5 and Yr 6.

> "Irregular word accuracy was significantly lower than pseudoword accuracy up to Yr 4 (apart from Yr 3), but for Yr 5 and Yr 6, irregular word accuracy was significantly higher than pseudoword accuracy."
