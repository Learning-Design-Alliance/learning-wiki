## Evidence

### Haută Alexandra 2024

Haută Alexandra, Iacobescu Radu-Alexandru, Corlade-Andrei Mihaela, Nedelea Paul Lucian, Cimpoeșu Carmen Diana. (2024). Translating training to medical practice in trauma care, a literature review. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-024-02548-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Longitudinal multicenter cohort study at level III and IV trauma centers reported in the review's Table 1; 'time to transfer squad arrival was shorter in the cohort that received communication training'.

> "The time to transfer squad arrival was shorter in the cohort that received communication training (31.12 vs. 67.19 min for RTTDC cohort, P < 0.01)."

### Haută Alexandra 2024 (2)

Haută Alexandra, Iacobescu Radu-Alexandru, Corlade-Andrei Mihaela, Nedelea Paul Lucian, Cimpoeșu Carmen Diana. (2024). Translating training to medical practice in trauma care, a literature review. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-024-02548-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Retrospective pre/post-test study in three non-trauma rural hospitals cited by the review, showing reduced 'time to transfer acceptance and total transfer times (139.2 vs. 110 min, P = 0.003 and 257.3 vs. 219.2 min, P = 0.002, respectively)'.

> "A further study by Malekpour et al. has shown similar results regarding time to transfer acceptance and total transfer times (139.2 vs. 110 min, P = 0.003 and 257.3 vs. 219.2 min, P = 0.002, respectively) [40]."
