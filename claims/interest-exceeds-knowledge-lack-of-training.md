### Katy Klauenberg 2025

Katy Klauenberg, Peter Harris, Philipp Möhrke and Francesca Pennecchi. (2025). The three most common needs for training on measurement uncertainty. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.2478/msr-2025-0029](https://doi.org/10.2478/msr-2025-0029)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A questionnaire on 21 uncertainty topics answered by EA and Italian calibration laboratories and their assessors; the authors state the interest-knowledge gap "may be interpreted as a general lack of training on uncertainty topics".

> "In general, the level of interest is similar to the level of reported knowledge for the topics, but the average interest score is higher and less spread than the average knowledge score (see table 2). Again, this outcome may be interpreted as a general lack of training on uncertainty topics and confirms the need expressed above by standardization and accreditation bodies."
