### Wickens Brandon 2015

Wickens Brandon, Lewis Jordan, Morris David P, Husein Murad, Ladak Hanif M, Agrawal Sumit K. (2015). Face and content validity of a novel, web-based otoscopy simulator for medical education. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-015-0060-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative open-text component of the 11-expert survey. The most common suggested improvements were haptic feedback, greater emphasis on pneumatic otoscopy, and more eardrum pathologies such as "eardrums with hearing aid complications, post-surgical scars, and different skin pathologies".

> "The most common suggested improvements included:Need for haptic feedback (by using a device, such as a haptic arm, to simulate controlling and navigating the otoscope into the ear canal)"
