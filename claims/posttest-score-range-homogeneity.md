## Evidence

### Mokhtari and Talebinezhad 2014

Elahe Mokhtari, Mohammed Reza Talebinezhad. (2014). Using Supplementary Readings (Short Stories) in Increasing the Conceptual Fluency, the Case of Idioms in English. Advances in Language and Literary Studies. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.1p.134

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of score ranges from the post-test frequency distributions (Figures 3 and 4); the article states the experimental group's scores were "close to the mean which shows the homogeneity of the group in terms of conceptual fluency". These are descriptive statistics, not tested differences.

> "the lowest and highest scores of experimental group (18 & 28) are higher than that of control group (10 & 27.5). And on the other hand, the difference between the highest and lowest scores for experimental group (10) is less than that of control group (17.5)."
