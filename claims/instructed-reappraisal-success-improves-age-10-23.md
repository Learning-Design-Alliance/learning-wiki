# Instructed reappraisal success improves with age across ages 10-23, following linear and quadratic trends

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a lab-based reappraisal paradigm, regulation success, defined as percentage decrease in self-reported negative affect on decrease versus look trials for negative stimuli, improved with age across participants aged 10-23, following both linear and quadratic trends. ## Evidence

### Ahmed Saz P. 2015

Ahmed Saz P., Bittencourt-Hewitt Amanda, Sebastian Catherine L. (2015). Neurocognitive bases of emotion regulation development in adolescence. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6989808/

`q3 · i?`

A lab-based study of 44 participants aged 10-23 viewing negative and neutral IAPS pictures under look or decrease (reappraisal) instructions. The review reports regulation success improved with age with both linear and quadratic trends.

> "Regulation success was defined as percentage decrease in self-reported negative affect on ‘decrease’ trials relative to ‘look’ trials for negative stimuli, and was found to improve with age, following both linear and quadratic trends."
