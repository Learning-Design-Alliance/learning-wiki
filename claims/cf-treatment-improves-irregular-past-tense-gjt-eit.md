## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yusuke Sato 2023

Yusuke Sato. (2023). Interactions Among Declarative and Procedural Memory Systems, Different Linguistic Structures, and the Efficacy of Different Corrective Feedback Types. Journal for the Psychology of Language Learning, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.52598/jpll/5/1/4

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA over pretest, immediate and delayed posttests for the same 92 learners on irregular past tense. The article states "The large effect sizes showed that the three types of CF were beneficial for learning irregular past tense forms."

> "A repeated measures ANOVA confirmed that all the groups improved their GJT and EIT scores after they received the treatment, F (2, 182) = 32.0, p = .00, partial η² = .26; and F (2, 182) = 24.2, p = .00, partial η² = .21, respectively."
