## Subclaims
`q2 i?` IC group students achieved higher scores on specialty physical examination, admission note writing, and anatomy examinations than LC group students. [→ Su Xiaoqun 2026](#su-xiaoqun-2026)
`q2 i?` Theoretical examination scores did not differ significantly between the IC and LC groups. [→ Su Xiaoqun 2026 (2)](#su-xiaoqun-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Su Xiaoqun 2026

Su Xiaoqun, Hu Bing, Liu Chengcheng, Ren Hongmiao. (2026). Application of integrated whole-course clinical learning combined with case-based learning in undergraduate otolaryngology clerkships. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09871-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subject score comparison in the 79-student clerkship intervention; specialty physical examination mean difference 3.95 points, adjusted p < 0.01. Admission notes (t(73) = 5.84) and anatomy (t(59) = 4.58) also favored IC, both adjusted p < 0.01.

> "The students in the IC group achieved higher scores on specialty physical examination skills than those in the LC group did (IC-LC: 89.18 ± 4.26 vs. 85.22 ± 4.22; Welch’s t test: t(77) = 4.14, adjusted p < 0.01)."

### Su Xiaoqun 2026 (2)

Su Xiaoqun, Hu Bing, Liu Chengcheng, Ren Hongmiao. (2026). Application of integrated whole-course clinical learning combined with case-based learning in undergraduate otolaryngology clerkships. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09871-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Theoretical examination comparison in the same intervention; adjusted p = 0.07, no significant difference; equivalence was not tested. Authors attribute this to clerkships following completed theoretical coursework and large score variability.

> "In contrast, no significant difference was observed in the theoretical examination scores between the two groups (IC-LC: 88.92 ± 8.52 vs. 85.15 ± 9.97; t(76) = 1.81; adjusted p = 0.07)."
