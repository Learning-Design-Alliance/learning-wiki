# Check & Connect mentoring had no detectable impact on engagement, academic progress, dropout, or graduation for at-risk general education high school students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a three-year RCT with 553 at-risk general education students, assignment to Check & Connect produced no statistically significant impacts on engagement, course-taking and achievement, dropout, or four- and five-year graduation. [→ Heppen 2017](#heppen-2017)

## Evidence

### Heppen 2017

Heppen, J. B., Zeiser, K., Holtzman, D. J., O'Cummings, M., Christenson, S., & Pohl, A. (2017). Efficacy of the Check & Connect Mentoring Program for At-Risk General Education High School Students. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2017.1318990

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three-year randomized controlled trial in 10 urban high schools; 553 students randomly assigned within school to Check & Connect or business-as-usual control, analyzed intent-to-treat. The article reports the program had no detectable impact on key student outcomes, with no effect size printed.

> "Contrary to hypotheses and counter to the prior evidence, we found the program had no detectable impact on key student outcomes."
