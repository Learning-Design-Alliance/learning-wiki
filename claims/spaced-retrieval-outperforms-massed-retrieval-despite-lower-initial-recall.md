## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The chapter reports, citing Karpicke and Roediger (2007a, Experiment 3), that a small spacing cut initial recall by about 25% but enhanced retention on final tests 10 min or 2 days later. [→ Karpicke 2017](#karpicke-2017)
`q2 i?` The chapter reports, citing Karpicke and Bauernschmidt (2011), that massed repeated retrieval produced no benefit relative to dropping items after one recall on a 1-week test. [→ Karpicke 2017 (2)](#karpicke-2017-2)

### Karpicke 2017

Karpicke, J. D. (2017). Retrieval-Based Learning: A Decade of Progress. Learning and Memory: A Comprehensive Reference, 2nd edition, Volume 2. https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-809324-5.21055-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The chapter reports a vocabulary experiment without feedback comparing lag 0 and five intervening items: "inserting a small spacing produced a decrease in recall of approximately 25%", yet spaced retrieval enhanced final retention.

> "Items were recalled nearly perfectly immediately after presentation, while inserting a small spacing produced a decrease in recall of approximately 25%. However, spaced retrieval enhanced retention on the ﬁnal tests."

### Karpicke 2017 (3)

Karpicke, J. D. (2017). Retrieval-Based Learning: A Decade of Progress. Learning and Memory: A Comprehensive Reference, 2nd edition, Volume 2. https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-809324-5.21055-9
