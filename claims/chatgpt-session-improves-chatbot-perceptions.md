Holger Arndt. (2024). Economic and Financial Learning with Artificial Intelligence: A Mixed-Methods Study on ChatGPT. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.15278](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.15278)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest survey of 102 participants who learned chosen economic or business topics with ChatGPT in guided sessions averaging 52 minutes (SD = 17). Weighted mean differences on Likert items showed "competence using chatbots (+0.74)" and enjoyment of learning (+0.66); only descriptive gains are reported.
