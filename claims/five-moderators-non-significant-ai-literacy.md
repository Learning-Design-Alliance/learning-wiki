`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analyses of five non-significant factors in the meta-analysis. Descriptively, mixed pedagogies (g = 1.071), reflective pedagogy (g = 0.988), and GenAI (g = 1.140) showed numerically larger effects, but omnibus tests did not reach significance; equivalence was not tested.

> "Effect sizes did not differ significantly across classroom instruction approaches (F (3, 168) = 1.570, p = .198). Descriptive comparisons indicated that mixed instruction approaches (g = 1.071) and reflective pedagogy (g = 0.988) tended to produce larger effect sizes"
