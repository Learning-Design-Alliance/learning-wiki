A Dialogue-Based Framework for Correcting Multimodal Errors in AI-Assisted STEM Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04131 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.04131](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.04131)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline evaluation of GPT-5, Claude Sonnet-4.5, and Gemini-3 Pro Preview on 100 text-only OpenStax physics questions, answers manually verified against the OpenStax key. The article reports "an average accuracy of 96% (95% CI [0.93, 0.98])". Cochran's Q found no significant model differences (Q = 3.6, df = 2, p = 0.17).

> "On text-only physics problems (Table 1), all three models demonstrated strong performance with an average accuracy of 96% (95% CI [0.93, 0.98])."
