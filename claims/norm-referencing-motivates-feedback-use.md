## Evidence

### Burr Steven A 2013

Burr Steven A, Brodier Elizabeth, Wilkinson Simon. (2013). Delivery and use of individualised feedback in large class medical teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-63

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on learner motivation. Responders' adequate-performance standard was 63.1%, and 58% reported greater motivation when performing below the year average than at an absolute 55%, which the authors read as peer-comparison motivation.

> "the mean assessment grade students considered to reflect an adequate performance was 63.1%. 58% of responders then suggested they would be more motivated to improve and utilise the feedback report if they performed 10% lower than the year's average (calculated as 65%), rather than if they achieved 55%."
