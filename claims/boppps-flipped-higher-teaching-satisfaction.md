## Evidence

### Wei Linna 2025

Wei Linna, Yu Xinyang, Wang Yiming, Shan Nan. (2025). Application of BOPPPS and Flipped Classroom Joint Teaching Model into Clinical Practice Ability of Obstetrics and Gynecology Residents in Standardized Training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07246-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Satisfaction questionnaire (Cronbach α = 0.917, split-half = 0.819) administered after courses; 70 valid questionnaires were collected. Residents were "significantly more satisfied with the teaching than those in the control group" (P < 0.05). No standardized effect size is printed for these χ² comparisons.

> "The residents in the experimental group were significantly more satisfied with the teaching than those in the control group (P < 0.05, Table 4)."

### Wei Linna 2025 (2)
