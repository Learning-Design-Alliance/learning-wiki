`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Blinded video grading of four consecutive robotic HJ and PJ anastomoses per surgeon during LEARNBOT biotissue training, with errors categorized into technical, instrument-related, visualization, procedural, and equipment types. The article reports "0.175 errors decrease per attempt (ρ=−0.175, P=0.006)".

> "There was a significant decrease in the total number of errors made during consecutive attempts [0.175 errors decrease per attempt (ρ=−0.175, P=0.006)], although the median number of errors made in the last attempt of the HJ was 2.0 (IQR: 0.75–3.0), and for the PJ it was 6.0 (IQR: 3–9)"
