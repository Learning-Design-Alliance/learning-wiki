Evidence¶

Wise 2019¶

Wise, S. (2019). The Relationship Between Test-Taking Disengagement and Performance on MAP Growth Retests. (The Collaborative for Student Growth at NWEA Working Paper). https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/the-relationship-between-test-taking-disengagement-and-performance-on-map-growth-retests/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Large-scale observational analysis of MAP Growth test events comparing RIT scores of students retested within one day, across four disengagement levels on each test event. The article reports mean gains and declines exceeded 10 RIT points in several instances; no effect sizes are printed.

"regardless of disengagement on the initial test event, students who exhibited less rapid guessing during the retest tended to see score gains, while those who exhibited increased rapid guessing saw score declines. In several instances, the magnitude of these mean gains or declines exceeded 10 RIT points."
