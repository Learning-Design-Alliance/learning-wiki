# Weekly ultrasound-guided injection volume correlates moderately with pre-course confidence, weakly with post-course confidence, and negatively with confidence improvement

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Reported weekly number of injections correlates moderately with pre-course confidence scores (hip r = 0.548, knee r = 0.594, ankle r = 0.605). [→ Zure Mert 2025](#zure-mert-2025)
`q3 i2` A weaker correlation holds for post-course scores (hip r = 0.318, knee r = 0.376, ankle r = 0.417). [→ Zure Mert 2025 (2)](#zure-mert-2025-2)
`q3 i3` Reported weekly injections correlate negatively with improvement in reported scores (hip r=-0.400, knee r=-0.465, ankle r=-0.384). [→ Zure Mert 2025 (3)](#zure-mert-2025-3)

## Evidence

### Zure Mert 2025

Zure Mert, Topaloğlu Mahir, İş Enes Efe, Sezikli Selim, Sarıkaya Deniz, Dıraçoğlu Demirhan. (2025). Advancing skills and confidence in ultrasound-guided injection techniques: the impact of a cadaveric workshop. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07270-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis among the 109 workshop participants between reported weekly injection frequency and pre-course confidence; the article calls the correlations moderate (e.g., hip r = 0.548).

> "There was a moderate correlation between reported weekly number of injections and pre-course confidence scores (hip r = 0.548, knee r = 0.594, ankle r = 0.605)."

### Zure Mert 2025 (2)

Zure Mert, Topaloğlu Mahir, İş Enes Efe, Sezikli Selim, Sarıkaya Deniz, Dıraçoğlu Demirhan. (2025). Advancing skills and confidence in ultrasound-guided injection techniques: the impact of a cadaveric workshop. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07270-z

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Same correlation analysis for post-course confidence scores; the article describes the correlations as weaker (e.g., hip r = 0.318).

> "A weaker correlation was also observed for post-course scores (hip r = 0.318, knee r = 0.376, ankle r = 0.417)."

### Zure Mert 2025 (3)

Zure Mert, Topaloğlu Mahir, İş Enes Efe, Sezikli Selim, Sarıkaya Deniz, Dıraçoğlu Demirhan. (2025). Advancing skills and confidence in ultrasound-guided injection techniques: the impact of a cadaveric workshop. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07270-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Same correlation analysis for improvement scores: higher weekly injection volume was associated with smaller gains, with negative correlations (hip r=-0.400) the article calls weak to moderate.

> "As logically expected, there was a weak to moderate negative correlation between reported weekly injections and improvement in reported scores (hip r=-0.400, knee r=-0.465, ankle r=-0.384)."
