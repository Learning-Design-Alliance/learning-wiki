`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post quality improvement evaluation in three medical wards at Singapore General Hospital: 40 RNs completed the 20-item modified Palliative Care Quiz before and after lectures plus case-based discussions. The article reports "increased from 15% to 53%" with a printed chi-square test (X2=-0.375, p<0.01); no effect size is printed.

> "the results demonstrated a significant improvement in the EOL proficiency pass rate, which increased from 15% to 53% 3 months post-intervention."
