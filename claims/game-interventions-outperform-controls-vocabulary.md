Review's Table 1 entry for Ashraf et al. (2014), Iran: 24 participants aged 16–22, two 2-hour sessions weekly for 15 weeks; experimental group played an online game, controls used pen and paper; outcome was a written vocabulary recall test, with t = 2.40 reported.

> "The results show that the experiment al group outperform ed the co ntrol group (t = 2.40) and thus, that playing online games enhances vocabulary acquisition in language learning."
