Evidence¶

Molenaar 2010¶

Molenaar, I., van Boxtel, C. A. M., & Sleegers, P. J. C. (2010). Metacognitive scaffolding in an innovative learning arrangement. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-010-9154-1

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Planned comparison between the problematizing condition (19 triads) and the structuring condition (17 triads) on group paper quality. The article reports a significant medium positive effect (r = 0.28), confirming the hypothesis that problematizing scaffolds would outperform structuring scaffolds.

"the form of the scaffolds had a signiﬁcant effect on group performance as measured by the quality of the group paper, t (49) = 2.07; p\0.02 (one tailed) see Table 4. The effect size (r = 0.28) indicated a medium positive effect of the form of scaffolding on the quality of the paper."
