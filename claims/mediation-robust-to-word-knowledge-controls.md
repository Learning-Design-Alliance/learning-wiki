# The mediation of child-directed speech effects through processing efficiency holds after controlling for knowledge of the target words and excluding at-chance performers

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Weisleder Adriana 2013

Weisleder Adriana, Fernald Anne. (2013). Talking to children matters: Early language experience strengthens processing and builds vocabulary. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797613488145

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Control analysis addressing whether processing differences merely reflect target-word knowledge: parents reported whether children understood each target word (66% of children understood all words at 19 months, 72% at 24 months), non-understood trials were removed, and the mediation model was re-run with the same pattern of results.

> "the parameter estimate for the effect of CDS on vocabulary was significantly reduced from 12.61 to 8.75 when processing efficiency was included in the model"

### Weisleder Adriana 2013 (2)

Weisleder Adriana, Fernald Anne. (2013). Talking to children matters: Early language experience strengthens processing and builds vocabulary. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797613488145

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Final analysis including only children whose mean accuracy exceeded .50 at 19 months (n = 22), excluding at-or-below-chance performers. Mediation again held, with the CDS-to-vocabulary path "significantly reduced from 15.61 to 8.86" when processing efficiency was included.

> "This analysis revealed even stronger correlations between child-directed speech and processing efficiency (r = 0.58, p < 0.01), and between processing efficiency and later vocabulary (r = 0.62, p < 0.01)"
