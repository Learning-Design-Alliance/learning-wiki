# An interviewed student justified answering that the ATP bond breaking releases energy by appealing to the larger biological context of the hydrolysis reaction, not the raw misconception that bond breaking releases energy

## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2012

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Benjamin D. Geller, Vashti Sawtelle, Julia Svoboda, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2012). Students' Interdisciplinary Reasoning about "High-Energy Bonds" and ATP. [doi:10.1063/1.4789667](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789667)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured interview with Gregor, a pre-med student, conducted immediately after class on the day quizzes were returned, in which he spontaneously discussed the ATP quiz question. He explains that "in a larger biological context, that reaction does release energy," while recognizing the answer key's correctness.

> "I don't [argue] that breaking a bond r e- leases energy, but just like in a larger biological context, that reaction does release energy. So that's what me and apparently like 80% of the class was thinking."
