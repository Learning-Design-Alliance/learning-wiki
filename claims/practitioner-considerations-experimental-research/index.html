q1 · i? · design · r2

Advice surfaced in Office Hours conversations and echoed by the practitioner advisory board, which suggested researchers consider "the variability in training that occurs across schools or districts" based on available resources, expertise, or leadership. Practitioner input, not a tested result.

"Practitioners also addressed that it is important that experimental research not just look for causal effects but account for multiple variables in a classroom environment, such as class timeline, learner pace, curriculum, privacy, in-situ setup, and keeping human-in-the-loop when using technology."
