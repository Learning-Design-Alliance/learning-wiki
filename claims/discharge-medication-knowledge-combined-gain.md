## Evidence

### Wilkoff MH 2025

Wilkoff MH, Seltzer ES, Piniella NR, Seepersaud H, Loanzon P, Kurtz S, Salonia J, Jodorkovsky D. (2025). Acute Upper Gastrointestinal Bleeding: A Hands-On Simulation Case for Internal Medicine Residents Improves Knowledge and Confidence. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11541

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post survey analysis of the simulation cohort. The article reports a significant combined-cohort increase in discharge-medication knowledge (p = .01) while per-level analyses were not significant; no effect size is printed.

> "While individual resident levels did not show a statistically significant increase in their knowledge of discharge medications for EV and PUD, there was an overall significant increase with all residents combined (p = .01; Table 1)."
