Corpus analysis of 888 review-automation papers; publication counts plotted from 2010 onward and fitted by ordinary least squares on log count. The fit "corresponds to an estimated 4.7% monthly growth rate"; the 2026 continuation is described as a descriptive extrapolation, not a forecast.

"A log-linear fit to positive monthly counts from January 2020 through June 2025 corresponds to an estimated 4.7% monthly growth rate."
