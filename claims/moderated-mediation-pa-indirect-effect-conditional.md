# The indirect effect of RU2-Short on reading outcomes through phonological awareness is conditional on home environment: significant at low and medium parental education and low SES, but not at high parental education or high SES

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For word reading accuracy, the conditional indirect effect of RU2-Short through phonological awareness was significant when parental education was low and medium but not when parent education was high. [→ Li 2024](#li-2024)
`q3 i?` The conditional indirect effect was significant when SES was low but not when SES was high. [→ Li 2024](#li-2024)

## Evidence

### Li 2024

Li, M., DeMille, M. M. C., Lovett, M. W., Bosson-Heenan, J., Frijters, J. C., Gruen, J. R. & the GRaD Study Consortium. (2024). Phonological awareness mediates the relationship between DCDC2 and reading performance with home environment. npj Science of Learning 9, 36. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00247-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderated mediation models (Table 4) tested with PROCESS in the GRaD sample; the same pattern held for word reading fluency and reading comprehension, and for SES (low SES b = −2.91 vs. high SES b = −0.05, CI crossing zero).

> "When parental education was low and medium, there was a signiﬁcant indirect effect of RU2Short on word reading accuracy through phonological awareness (c’ path: b = −3.39, 95% CI [−5.01, −1.78]; b = −1.93, 95% CI [−3.01, −0.86]) but not when parent education was high (c’ path: b = −0.47, 95% CI [−2.01, 1.03])."
