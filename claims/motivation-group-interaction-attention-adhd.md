## Evidence

### Sebastian Skalski 2021

Sebastian Skalski, Grzegorz Pochwatko, Robert Balas. (2021). Impact of Motivation on Selected Aspects of Attention in Children with ADHD. Child Psychiatry & Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-020-01042-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-design two-way MANOVA (group × motivation) with 30 ADHD and 30 TD children; the omnibus interaction was significant with a printed effect size of η² = 0.48, and univariate interactions were confirmed for omission errors, visual search RT and RT slope, and single- and multi-tasking RT.

> "As a result of the analyses, a multidimensional interaction effect was obtained regarding group membership and motivation, F(6,53) = 8.13, p < 0.001, η² = 0.48."
