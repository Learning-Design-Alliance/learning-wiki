## Evidence

### Alonso-Carracedo 2026

Alonso-Carracedo, M., Fernandez-Boullon, R., Celard, P., Rodríguez-Martínez, F. J., Otero-Cerdeira, L. (2026). Automated grading of Linux/bash examinations using large language models: a four-level cognitive taxonomy approach. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02432

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Inter-rater reliability analysis of the three instructors' independent blind grades of the 1200 exam responses (Section 4.1.3). The article reports "ICC(2,1) = 0.949(95% CI[0.941,0.955],p < 0.001)", plus Pearson r = 0.949, Weighted Kappa 0.948, MAE 0.028, and Bland-Altman bias of −0.008 points.

> "The Intraclass Correlation Coefficient ICC(2,1) = 0.949(95% CI[0.941,0.955],p < 0.001) indicates excellent absolute agreement."
