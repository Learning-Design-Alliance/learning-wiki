The report's introduction states its central estimate: cumulative losses from start-up routines, special programs, holiday slowdowns, test preparation, and last-six-weeks dropoffs reduce actual instruction far below the official 900 hours, to "something much closer to 500 hours."

> "Indeed, this report suggests that many Chi- cago students experience not the intended 900 hours of annual instruc- tion, but something much closer to 500 hours."
