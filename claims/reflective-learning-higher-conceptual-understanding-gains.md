## Evidence

### Junsay 2016

Junsay, M. L. (2016). Reflective Learning and Prospective Teachers' Conceptual Understanding, Critical Thinking, Problem Solving, and Mathematical Communication Skills. Research in Pedagogy, 6(2), 43-58. https://doi.org/10.17810/2015.34

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental matched comparison of two intact classes (n=30 per group) reported in Table 5. The mean gain difference of 3.40 was significant, t(29)=2.15, p=.040, favouring the reflective learning group. No effect size was printed.

> "the mean gain scores of the prospective teachers in conceptual understanding exposed to the reflective learning approach (Mgain=15.03) was higher by 3.40 than the mean gain of the scores of prospective teachers exposed in the lecture-discussion approach (Mgain=11.63)"
