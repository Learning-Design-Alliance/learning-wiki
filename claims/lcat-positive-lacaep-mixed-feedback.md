`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of closed items from the built-in feedback survey completed by 34 participants for the LCAT and 18 for the LACAeP. The article also reports "The majority of participants (n = 14, 78%, Table 1) felt that the e‐learning package was useful" while 61% would be unlikely to recommend the training. Percentages are descriptive counts; no inferential statistics are reported.

> "Three‐quarters (n = 26, 76%, Table 1) of participants found the LCAT easy to use, approximately the same proportion (n = 25, 74%, Table 1) said that they would or would probably use the tool in their role, and two‐thirds (n = 23, 68%, Table 1) stated that they would be likely or very likely to recommend the tool to others."
