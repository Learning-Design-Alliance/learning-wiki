## Evidence

### Roszipal Benjamin 2025

Roszipal Benjamin, Szelesi Gabriella, Ernst Martin, Hoffelner Alexander, Wagner Michael. (2025). Competency development in early nursing training: a cross-sectional OSCE study of self-assessment versus examiner ratings. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08279-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple linear regressions in the OSCE cohort predicted domain discrepancy scores from age. The social-domain regression was significant (B = −0.009, p = .006), but the article notes this "R² = 0.07" is modest explained variance; R² from regression is not treated as a standardized effect size here.

> "For social/communication skills, age was a significant negative predictor, F(1, 102) = 7.73, p =.006, R² = 0.07, indicating that older students tended to show smaller discrepancies."
