## Evidence

### Rafael Padilla Perez 2025

Rafael Padilla Perez, Özgür Keleş. (2025). Immersive Virtual Reality Environments for Embodied Learning of Engineering Students. San Jose State University. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.16519](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.16519)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-/post-test student trial (Section 5): students from materials, mechanical, chemical, and aerospace engineering took a pre-test, did the self-paced VLE (~5 min per test), then a post-test. The article reports "a 49%, 133%, and 65% increase in scores"; no effect size or significance test is printed for this within-student comparison.

> "Students had higher scores for all the questions after the VLE experience. There was a 49%, 133%, and 65% increase in scores for questions 1, 2, and 3, respectively."
