### Sahin Turker MSc 2025

Sahin Turker MSc, MA; Korkmaz Ufuk Turan Kursat MD; Guven Hakan MD; Atasoy Mustafa Selcuk MD; Velioglu Yusuf MD; Yuksel Ahmet MD. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Second survey (Perfusion Safety and Accidents, 66 questions) answered by 109 perfusionists. Self-reported experience of equipment failures and accidents including monitor failure, oxygenator failure, and required oxygenator changes.

> "Forty-four percent of the participants encountered a monitor or screen failure in the heart-lung machine, 63.3% encountered oxygenator failure, and 73.4% had to change the oxygenator."
