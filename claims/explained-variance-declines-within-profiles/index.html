Dirk Tempelaar · Alexandra Niculescu 2026¶

Dirk Tempelaar · Alexandra Niculescu. (2026). Testing the Relative Universalism of Psychological Theories: A Person-Centered Analysis of Control-Value Theory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10155-2

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of standardized SEM results across full-sample, multigroup, and latent-profile models. The article reports "a median R 2 exceeding 30%" for grouping analyses versus below 15% for latent profiles, and states variance restriction does not fully explain the reduction; the discussion adds anxiety's decline is nearly two-thirds.

"The group -ing analyses yield a median R 2 exceeding 30%, whereas the latent profile models fall below 15%. Although variance restriction inherent in latent profile analysis may partly account for this reduction, it does not fully explain the pronounced weakening of the role of Academic Control."
