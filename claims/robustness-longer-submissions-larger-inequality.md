## Evidence

### Renzhe Yu 2024

Renzhe Yu, Zhen Xu, Sky CH-Wang, Richard Arum. (2024). Whose ChatGPT? Unveiling Real-World Educational Inequalities Introduced by Large Language Models. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2410.22282](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2410.22282)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robustness replication on a subsample of submissions to assignments with median length over 100 words (685,923 posts), plus an extended Fall 2019-Winter 2024 sample (3,365,845 posts) controlling for pandemic terms. The article reports the extended-sample SES gaps of 0.028 and 0.038 SD versus 0.025 and 0.015 in the main results.

> "For longer submissions, almost all the estimated gaps were larger than the main findings, reaffirming our claims about new digital inequalities introduced by LLM availability"
