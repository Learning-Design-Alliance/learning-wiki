Azimi 2026¶

Azimi, S. (2026). AI Literacy over Tool Design: A Mixed-Methods Study of Scaffolded versus Unrestricted Generative AI in Programming Education. Delft University of Technology. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16784

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive comparison in a seven-week pilot with 33 master's students randomly assigned to a Coach condition (n=16) or unrestricted AI (n=17); no inferential tests were run. The article reports "Gains on the concept inventory between week 2 and week 7 were similar in both groups" and equivalent rubric scores.

"The two conditions did not differ in assignment performance. Gains on the concept inventory between week 2 and week 7 were similar in both groups, and the rubric scores on the assignments were equivalent."
