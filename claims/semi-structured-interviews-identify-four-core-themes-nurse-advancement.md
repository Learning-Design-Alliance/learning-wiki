`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative analysis of semi-structured interviews with 10 peritoneal dialysis SNs and 6 SN preceptors from Zhejiang Province, all female, with a mean age of 42.81 ± 1.31 years. The four themes covered role differentiation, core responsibilities, priority competencies, and training needs.

> "Between June 1 and July 15, 2023, semi-structured interviews were conducted with 10 peritoneal dialysis SNs and 6 peritoneal dialysis SN preceptors from Zhejiang Province. All participants were female, with a mean age of 42.81 ± 1.31 years. Analysis of the interview data identified four core themes."
