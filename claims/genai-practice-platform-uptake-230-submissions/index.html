q2 · i? · design · r2

Platform analytics from a mid-semester voluntary deployment in a core electrical engineering subject on probability. The authors report that "34 went on to attempt at least one question, altogether generating 230 submissions", with a median of three and a maximum of 40 submissions; no inferential test is reported.

"Of the 95 students (out of the 157 enrolled in the subject) who registered for the platform, 34 went on to attempt at least one question, altogether generating 230 submissions. Among these active users, engagement varied widely. The median student made three submissions, but the most active made 40"
