Evidence¶

Sabirova 2016¶

Sabirova, E. G., Zakirova, V. G. & Masalimova, A. R. (2016). Development of Junior Pupils Research Skills in Interrelation with Universal Learning Activities. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(4), 505-514. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1094583

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of one control and two experimental groups of junior pupils in Kazan schools, using diagnostic measurements of research skills. The article reports level movement in "80% of pupils" on successfulness and 75% on mastery in experimental groups; control indices were 30% and 32% with no considerable changes.

"Movement to the new level (from low to intermediate, and from intermediate to higher level) according to successfulness index was revealed in 80% of pupils, and according to mastery index it was revealed in 75% of pupils in experimental groups."
