Moderator analyses in the HLM models (Table 5) added a Language × Treatment interaction; interactions were significant for LWID (coefficient .36, p = .04) and PC (.40, p = .03), and probing at mean ±1 SD showed "when students had initially high language skills (one standard deviation above the mean), they were found to signiﬁcantly beneﬁt from the treatment."

> "However, when students had initially high language skills (one standard deviation above the mean), they were found to signiﬁcantly beneﬁt from the treatment ( p ¼ .02 and .05 for Letter-Word Identiﬁcation and Passage Comprehension, respectively)."
