## Evidence

### Aksu Miray 2026

Aksu Miray, Varol Belgin, Özer Küçük Emine. (2026). Determination of Managerial Competence Levels and Training Requirements of New Nurse Managers for Role Transition: Empirical Research Quantitative. Nursing Open, 13(10). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70715

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal–Wallis comparison across acting-manager experience groups in the survey of 185 new nurse managers; Table 3 reports care management KW = 8.528, p = 0.014, η² = 0.046, a medium effect.

> "In terms of experiential factors, experience as acting manager showed a significant difference, especially in the sub-dimension of management of care (p = 0.014)"

### Aksu Miray 2026 (2)

Aksu Miray, Varol Belgin, Özer Küçük Emine. (2026). Determination of Managerial Competence Levels and Training Requirements of New Nurse Managers for Role Transition: Empirical Research Quantitative. Nursing Open, 13(10). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70715

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Mann–Whitney U comparison by appointment method in the same survey; Table 3 reports total score Z = 2.108, p = 0.035, r = 0.15 and personnel management Z = 2.539, p = 0.011, r = 0.19, small effects.

> "An exploratory association was noted, indicating that managers appointed with specific skills/competency criteria generally had higher scores in the total score (p = 0.035) and in the personnel management sub-dimension (p = 0.011)"
