## Evidence

### Andrew J. Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Andrew J. Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Surveying College Introductory Physics Students' Attitudes and Approaches to Problem Solving. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/37/5/055704](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/37/5/055704)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analysis of the AAPS survey administered to all surveyed groups (N=595). The article reports "αc = 0.84, which is reasonable from the standards of test-design", and content validity was supported by faculty near-unanimity and interviews used to refine item wording.

> "The Cronbach's alpha, αc, test was applied over all 33 questions for all groups (N=595), and αc = 0.84, which is reasonable from the standards of test-design."
