# A topic-matched online TBL session before the test improved topic-specific domain knowledge test scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Having the dkTest topic covered in an online TBL session beforehand was associated with a higher dkTest score by 0.33 points (p = 0.004). [→ Heber Stefan 2024](#heber-stefan-2024)
`q3 i?` Students who had cardiovascular TBL beforehand scored 0.45 points more on cardiovascular dkTest items than students who had metabolism TBL beforehand. [→ Heber Stefan 2024 (2)](#heber-stefan-2024-2)
`q3 i?` Students who had metabolism TBL beforehand scored 0.21 points more on metabolism dkTest items than students who had cardiovascular TBL beforehand. [→ Heber Stefan 2024 (3)](#heber-stefan-2024-3)

## Evidence

### Heber Stefan 2024

Heber Stefan, Wagner-Menghin Michaela, Volf Ivo, Slak Rupnik Marjan, Schmid Diethart, Marz Richard, Fischer Michael J.M. (2024). Short-term cognitive learning outcomes in team-based learning: is the permanent team important? Medical Education Online, 29(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2397864

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed linear model on the 464 students' dkTest scores, comparing students who had attended the topic-matched TBL with those who had not; the article reports this as a 3.3% relative improvement on the dkTest.

> "Having a topic in an online TBL session before the dkTest was associated with a higher score by 0.33 points (0.10–0.55, main effect 'taught or not' p = 0.004, least squares means, Figure 3A)."

### Heber Stefan 2024 (2)

Heber Stefan, Wagner-Menghin Michaela, Volf Ivo, Slak Rupnik Marjan, Schmid Diethart, Marz Richard, Fischer Michael J.M. (2024). Short-term cognitive learning outcomes in team-based learning: is the permanent team important? Medical Education Online, 29(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2397864

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the cardiovascular TBL arm of the same trial; the topic-matched advantage on cardiovascular dkTest problems was 0.45 points with a confidence interval excluding zero.

> "students who had an online TBL session with cardiovascular as topic prior to their dkTest achieved 0.45 points (95% CI 0.09–0.81) more on cardiovascular-related dkTest problems than students who had online TBL metabolism before the dkTest (Figure 3B)."

### Heber Stefan 2024 (3)

Heber Stefan, Wagner-Menghin Michaela, Volf Ivo, Slak Rupnik Marjan, Schmid Diethart, Marz Richard, Fischer Michael J.M. (2024). Short-term cognitive learning outcomes in team-based learning: is the permanent team important? Medical Education Online, 29(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2397864

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the metabolism TBL arm of the same trial; the topic-matched advantage on metabolism dkTest problems was 0.21 points with a confidence interval including zero.

> "The mean score of those students with metabolism as online TBL topic prior to their dkTest was 0.21 points (95% CI −0.10–0.51) above on metabolism-related dkTest problems compared to students who had online TBL cardiovascular before the dkTest (Figure 3C)."
