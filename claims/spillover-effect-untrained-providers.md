## Evidence

### Stone L 2021

Stone L, Puri MC, Guo M, Shah IH. (2021). Assessing knowledge, attitudes, and practice of health providers towards the provision of postpartum intrauterine devices in Nepal: a two-year follow-up. Reproductive Health 18, 43. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12978-021-01099-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary analysis of the 17 providers not trained by 24 months (Additional files 1–2); the article reports counseling "increased from 0 to 58.8%" and significant attitude changes (P < 0.05) towards recommending PPIUD to women under 20 and unmarried women.
