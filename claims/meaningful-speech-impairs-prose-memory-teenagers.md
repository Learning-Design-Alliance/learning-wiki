# Meaningful background speech impairs delayed memory for texts in teenagers more consistently than transportation noise

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 review `r2`–`r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Recall of prose paragraphs read under noise and tested later in silence was impaired by meaningful speech but not by meaningless speech. [→ Klatte M 2013](#klatte-m-2013)
`q2 i?` Results for transportation noise are inconsistent across studies, with some finding impairments from aircraft or road traffic noise and others finding none. [→ Klatte M 2013 (2)](#klatte-m-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Klatte M 2013

Klatte M, Bergström K and Lachmann T (2013) Does noise affect learning? A short review on noise effects on cognitive performance in children. Front. Psychol. 4:578. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2013.00578

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports teenage prose-memory studies: "Recall performance was impaired by meaningful speech... but not by meaningless speech", consistent with adult studies showing meaningful speech is more disruptive in verbal tasks.

> "Recall performance was impaired by meaningful speech (Hygge et al., 2003; Boman, 2004; Sörqvist, 2010 ), but not by meaningless speech ( Hygge, 2003 )."

### Klatte M 2013 (2)

Klatte M, Bergström K and Lachmann T (2013) Does noise affect learning? A short review on noise effects on cognitive performance in children. Front. Psychol. 4:578. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2013.00578

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports inconsistent transportation-noise findings across studies; Ljung et al. (2009) found no effect of road traffic noise or meaningful speech on 12- to 13-year-olds' online reading comprehension.

> "Hygge (2003) found impairments due to aircraft noise during encoding. Sörqvist (2010) used a within-subjects design and found no effect of aircraft noise, but severe impairments due to meaningful speech."
