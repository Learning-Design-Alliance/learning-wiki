The review's discussion of level 4a outcomes: interventions produced organisational changes including a teaching resident rotation with dual benefits, a train-the-trainer program, and a program attracting invitations for delivery to other areas of the organisation.

> "Studies did demonstrate that positive organisational change could occur due to the intervention (Kirkpatrick level 4a). One study created a teaching resident position that enabled the dual benefit to the resident and the students that the resident was teaching.30 Another created a “train the trainer” program which enabled the dissemination of teaching skills courses across the organisation.38"
