## Evidence

### Lee Hyo Jeong 2023

Lee Hyo Jeong, Kim Do-Hwan, Kang Ye Ji. (2023). Passivity, task segmentation, and relational capital: a study of interpersonal relationship formation among freshman medical students in team-based activities. Korean J Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2023.261

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group interviews with 11 students from a premedical team-based class, analyzed by constant comparison. Participants described conforming to majority views because "they did not want to stand out"; one reported stopping discussion when the team atmosphere was not receptive.

> "The participants primarily demonstrated a communication style characterized by actively agreeing with others’ opinions and conforming to the majority view."

### Lee Hyo Jeong 2023 (2)

Lee Hyo Jeong, Kim Do-Hwan, Kang Ye Ji. (2023). Passivity, task segmentation, and relational capital: a study of interpersonal relationship formation among freshman medical students in team-based activities. Korean J Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2023.261

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

FGI participants reported self-censorship in group chats: conversations felt "recorded" and lacked facial expressions and tone, so some reviewed each sentence before sending or withheld opinions entirely.

> "These characteristics were particularly prominent in “group chat,” where students often chose to review and refine their messages multiple times before sending them or even chose not to express their opinions if they found the process burdensome."
