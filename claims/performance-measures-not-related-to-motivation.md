### Marshall A. Baker & J. Shane Robinson 2017

Marshall A. Baker & J. Shane Robinson. (2017). The Effects of an Experiential Approach to Learning on Student Motivation. Journal of Agricultural Education, 58(3), 150-167. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2017.03150

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple correlation analysis (MCA) of successful-intelligence performance measures (creative, practical, analytical) against total IMMS motivation score in the same 87-student experiment. The R2 = .07 was non-significant (p = .12 per Table 4), so the system of measures did not significantly relate to motivation.

> "The MCA analysis for the second research question yielded a non-significant R2 value with only seven 7% of variance in motivation explained by performance measures"
