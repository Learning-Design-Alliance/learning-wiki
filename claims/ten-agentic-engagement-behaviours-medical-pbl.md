### Androni Alexandra 2024

Androni Alexandra, Fleer Joke, Smids Laura R., van der Wouden Joke M., Jaarsma A. Debbie C., Schönrock-Adema Johanna. (2024). Identifying Behaviours Representative of Agentic Engagement in Pre-clinical Medical Education PBL Groups Based on Literature and Observations. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1414

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Observational study of seven second-year pre-clinical PBL tutor groups at the UMCG, using fourteen video-recorded meetings totalling 16:34:28 of footage. From an initial draft list of 18 literature-sourced behaviours, the authors "retained 10 agentic behaviours that were representative of agentic engagement in medical education PBL classes".

> "Out of the initial 18 behaviours on our draft list of potential agentic engagement indicators, we retained 10 agentic behaviours that were representative of agentic engagement in medical education PBL classes (Table 2, Step 4)."
