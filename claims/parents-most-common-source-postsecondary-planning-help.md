`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Survey of Chicago public high school seniors in the 2005 Consortium biannual survey; seniors reported who helped them make postgraduation plans. The report states "parents were the most common source of assistance, with 79 percent of seniors reporting such help," with teachers at 51 percent and counselors at 43 percent.

> "Overall, parents were the most common source of assistance, with 79 percent of seniors reporting such help. School staff were less frequently cited as a source of help, with only 51 percent of seniors reporting that their teachers provided assistance, and only 43 percent reporting that counselors assisted in postsecondary planning."
