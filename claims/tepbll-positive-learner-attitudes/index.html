q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis in the Results section of the systematic review, based on the authors' coding of the 31 included empirical studies. The review reports that "learners had an overall positive attitude towards TEPBLL." No effect sizes are reported because a meta-analysis was not conducted.

"Overall, from the above results, it can be seen that the learners had an overall positive attitude towards TEPBLL and their learning outcomes were also satisfactory."
