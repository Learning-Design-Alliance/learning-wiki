## Evidence

### Angela Johnson 2020

Angela Johnson, Megan Kuhfeld. (2020). Fall 2019 to fall 2020 MAP Growth attrition analysis. NWEA Research. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/fall-2019-to-fall-2020-map-growth-attrition-analysis/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The authors' inference from the attrition analysis: since lower-achieving and minoritized students were more likely to be missing, observed fall 2020 results overstate the achievement of the full population, so COVID-19 impacts are "likely underestimated".

> "The systematic differences between attriters and students who were tested in fall 2020 mean that the impacts of COVID-19 on student achievement are likely underestimated."
