Item response theory (Rasch) residual correlation analysis of the post-test data. The article reports that "student ability, as modelled by item re- sponse theory, explains the correlations between the raw scores of conceptually unrelated questions"; residual correlations were approximately normally distributed with mean zero and standard deviation 0.02.

> "Hence student ability, as modelled by item re- sponse theory, explains the correlations between the raw scores of conceptually unrelated questions."
