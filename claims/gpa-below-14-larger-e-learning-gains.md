`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of learning-outcome differences across GPA categories in the comparative study; the difference between GPA categories was significant (P = 0.022), and "Students with a GPA of less than 14 experienced higher increments in their average scores after the e-learning compared to in-person education."

> "Moreover, the difference between students’ GPA categories among the two groups was significant (P = 0.022). Students with a GPA of less than 14 experienced higher increments in their average scores after the e-learning compared to in-person education."
