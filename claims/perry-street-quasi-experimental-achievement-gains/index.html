Evidence¶

Evan 2019¶

Evan, A. (2019). From the brink of closure: Key factors in one charter school's successful turnaround. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://dcpcsb.egnyte.com/dl/hdsUO9pDbh/

q3 · i? · causal · r?

A quasi-experimental impact study conducted by WestEd compared Perry Street students' achievement during the turnaround to similar students at other DC traditional and charter schools; the report states the difference was significant but prints no effect size.

"students attending Perry Street not only improved academically during the turnaround period, but they improved significantly more at Perry Street than they would have if they had attended a different traditional or charter school during that time."
