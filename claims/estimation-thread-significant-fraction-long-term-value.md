The author's observational report from his own classes comparing a full-course estimation thread with occasional exam use, based on student responses to an extra-credit question; no effect size or count is printed.

> "This is a much more successful approach than just asking them to do it occasionally on an exam! On my end-of-term-final-exam-extra-credit question ("Have you learned anything in this class that will be useful in five years?"), a significant fraction of my students now mention estimation as being of long-term value."
