## Evidence

### Andre L. C. Barczak 2023

Andre L. C. Barczak, Anuradha Mathrani, Binglan Han, Napoleon H. Reyes. (2023). Automated assessment system for programming courses: a case study for teaching data structures and algorithms. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10277-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous voluntary survey over two DSA course offerings yielded 46 responses from a combined class size of 100; seven blank responses were eliminated, leaving 39 valid responses. Students ranked assignments and overall experience, and the authors interpret the responses as showing the automarker "served as a formative instructional strategy".

> "Based on these responses, we find that the automarker served as a formative instructional strategy for experiential learning and honing students’ programming skills."
