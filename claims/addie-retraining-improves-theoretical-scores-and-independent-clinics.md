## Evidence

### He Peipei 2026

He Peipei, Yin Xiaohong, Xu Ying, Yang Tingjun. (2026). The construction and implementation of a clinical nursing experts-oriented retraining program for peritoneal dialysis specialist nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09379-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post paired comparison of 36 nurses in Zhejiang Province using the paired t-test for normally distributed data. The theoretical examination score improved significantly from 74.31 ± 12.14 to 89.31 ± 6.21 (t = -7.297, P < 0.001).

> "Following the retraining program, nurses achieved a mean score of 89.31 ± 6.21 on the specialized theoretical examination, representing a statistically significant improvement compared with pre-retraining scores (P < 0.001)."

### He Peipei 2026 (2)

He Peipei, Yin Xiaohong, Xu Ying, Yang Tingjun. (2026). The construction and implementation of a clinical nursing experts-oriented retraining program for peritoneal dialysis specialist nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09379-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Categorical variable reported as frequencies and evaluated using the McNemar test before and after retraining among 36 nurses. The number of independent clinics increased from 13 to 33 (χ² = 24.080, P < 0.001).

> "The number of independently operated peritoneal dialysis nursing clinics also increased from 13 before retraining to 33 after retraining (P < 0.001)."
