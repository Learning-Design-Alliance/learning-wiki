Rakesh, D., Sadikova, E., & McLaughlin, K. A. (2025). Associations among socioeconomic disadvantage, longitudinal changes in within-network connectivity, and academic outcomes in the ABCD study. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2025.101587

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear mixed effects models on residualized connectivity change in the ABCD sample (N = 4745) with all three SES indicators entered simultaneously; FDR-corrected p values were < 0.0001 (parent education, SMN hand), 0.022 (ADI, SMN hand), and 0.001 (parent education, SMN mouth).

> "Both higher neighborhood disadvantage and lower parent educational attainment were associated with greater increases in within-SMN(H) connectivity (Figs. 1E, 1F). Finally, lower parent educational attainment was associated with lower decreases in connectivity within the SMN(M) (Fig. 1G)."
