## Evidence

### Doorn Neelke N. 2011

Doorn Neelke N., Kroesen J. Otto. (2011). Using and Developing Role Plays in Teaching Aimed at Preparing for Social Responsibility. Science and Engineering Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11948-011-9335-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Returning qualitative discovery reported on the informative dimension in the Discussion section of the exploratory evaluation. The authors add that in the real world "decisions are often taken on the basis of fragmentary information and under time pressure"; one student asked for more time for an informed decision.

> "Via a role play, even more when it reflects a real political issue of the time, they discover that bare facts have little impact on the discussion conducted and decisions made."
