Secondary outcome in the three-group randomized trial: adherence to the correct guideline algorithm scored per procedural step and order. "ANOVA did not indicate statistical significance for the domain ‘adherence to correct algorithm’"; means were 88.7±10.3 (Vid), 93.3±6.7 (VPanim), 90.3±10.5 (VPstat), with no effect size for the omnibus test.
