### Djimeli Armand Duclaire Kemo 2026

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported utilization measured with two questionnaire items (use in the six months before the study and frequency: always, sometimes, or rarely) in the cross-sectional survey. The article reports "the magnitude of partograph use was 72.1%, 95% CI: 67.3 - 76.6".

> "the magnitude of partograph use was 72.1%, 95% CI: 67.3 - 76.6; that is, 269 (72.1%) of obstetric care providers systematically used the partograph, while 71 (19%) used it sometimes, 14 (3.8%) rarely used the partograph, and 19 (5.1%) reported never using the partograph."
