## Evidence

### Hammami Aya 2024

Hammami Aya, Ghali Hela, Elleuch Nour, Ben Saad Omar Khalil, Jaziri Hanen, Ksiaa Mehdi, Ali Habiba Sik. (2024). Enhancing proficiency in ascites puncture through procedural simulation: an audit type study investigating medium-term skill retention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05063-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pre-test/post-test comparison of five MCQ scores among 40 DCEM1 students after a 90-minute simulation session; the article reports the mean rose from "2.93. ± 0.71 out of 5 versus 4.21 ± 0.94" with p < 0.000. No effect size is printed.

> "We found a statistically significant improvement in the marks obtained after the training (2.93. ± 0.71 out of 5 versus 4.21 ± 0.94) (p < 0.000)"
