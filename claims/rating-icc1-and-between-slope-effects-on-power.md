## Evidence

### Steffen Zitzmann 2021

Steffen Zitzmann, Wolfgang Wagner, Martin Hecht, Christoph Helm, Christian Fischer, Lisa Bardach, Richard Göllner. (2021). How Many Classes and Students Should Ideally be Sampled When Assessing the Role of Classroom Climate via Student Ratings on a Limited Budget? An Optimal Design Perspective. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:511–536. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09635-4

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical optimization results shown in Fig. 2, with the same budget and cost assumptions as Fig. 1. Selecting a higher ICC(1) of the ratings increases the optimal number of classes and decreases the optimal number of students per class while increasing power.

> "unlike the ICC(1) of student achievement, a higher ICC(1) of students' ratings of classroom management will increase the power to detect the between slope."

### Steffen Zitzmann 2021 (2)

Steffen Zitzmann, Wolfgang Wagner, Martin Hecht, Christoph Helm, Christian Fischer, Lisa Bardach, Richard Göllner. (2021). How Many Classes and Students Should Ideally be Sampled When Assessing the Role of Classroom Climate via Student Ratings on a Limited Budget? An Optimal Design Perspective. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:511–536. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09635-4

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical optimization results shown in Fig. 3 for the standardized between slope (standardized according to Marsh et al.'s (2009) formula) under the same fixed budget and cost assumptions.

> "the larger this slope is chosen to be, the smaller the optimal number of classes will be, and the larger the optimal number of students per class will be. Moreover, a larger standardized between slope will increase the power to detect the between slope."
