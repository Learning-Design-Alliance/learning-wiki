### Albardía MS 2025

Albardía MS, Peña-Fernández S and Agirreazkuenaga I (2025) Technology, education and critical media literacy: potential, challenges, and opportunities. Front. Hum. Dyn. 7:1608911. https://doi.org/10.3389/fhumd.2025.1608911

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 141 university students reporting media use. The article states "social media (77.5%) and television (63%) are the primary sources of information"; only 6.5% listen to radio and 8.7% read print newspapers. 72.5% access news daily or almost daily, and 48.94% use three or more sources versus 17.73% using one.

> "The analysis of media consumption among university students shows that social media (77.5%) and television (63%) are the primary sources of information, followed by digital newspapers (47.1%) and general internet sources (42.8%) (Table 5)."
