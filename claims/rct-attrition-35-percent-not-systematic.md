# Attrition of 130 students (35%) between pretest and posttest was not systematically related to baseline characteristics

## Evidence

### Tim Fütterer 2026

Tim Fütterer, Lisa Bardach, Jochen Kuhn, Stefan Daniel Keller, Peter Gerjets. (2026). Enhancing School Students’ Self-Regulated Learning through Generative AI Support: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10133-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis comparing the 130 students (35%) who dropped out between T1 and T2 with those who remained, on gender, conscientiousness, openness, dispositional interest, ability self-concept, and growth mindset. "no statistically significant differences between dropouts and non-dropouts on any of these variables".

> "The analysis revealed no statistically significant differences between dropouts and non-dropouts on any of these variables, suggesting that attrition was not systematically related to students’ initial characteristics."
