## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Open classroom climate relates positively to enjoyment at both the within- and between-classroom level, negatively to anxiety at the within level and boredom at both levels, and shows no significant association with shame. [→ Graf 2023](#graf-2023)

## Evidence

### Graf 2023

Graf, E., Stempfer, L., Muis, K. R., & Goetz, T. (2023). Classroom emotions in civic education: A multilevel approach to antecedents and effects. Draft version 1.0, University of Vienna / McGill University (not peer-reviewed preprint). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101869

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Doubly-latent multilevel SEM on survey data from 1,117 students in 89 Austrian vocational-school classes. The analysis found "a positive relation between open classroom climate and enjoyment both on the within- and between-level" (R2w = .086, R2b = .840), significant negative associations with anxiety (within) and boredom (both levels), and no association with shame.

> "Our analysis revealed a positive relation between open classroom climate and enjoyment both on the within- and between-level. This means that the more open a classroom climate is experienced relative to one's classmates, the more enjoyment is experienced"
