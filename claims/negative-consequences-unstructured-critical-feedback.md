## Evidence

### Pulkki KH 2025

Pulkki KH, Pira S, Young M, Scott GM, Nhan C, Fung K, Le Blanc G, Nguyen LHP. (2025). Considering the potential unintended consequences of RateMDs: an exploratory study in one specialty. Canadian Medical Education Journal 16(2). https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.77821

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, citing prior literature on unstructured feedback harms; the abstract itself labels the confidence/self-worth effect speculative. No new empirical test and no effect size are printed.

> "Negative consequences of receiving unstructured feedback have been found to be psychologically and emotionally wounding in several ways, including conflict within self (self-esteem, motivation, satisfaction, professional identity), conflict in relationships (mistrust, betrayal, fear for safety), and conflict in professional identity (medico-legal issues, damage to one’s reputation, potential loss of employment)."
