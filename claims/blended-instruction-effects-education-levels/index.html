Evidence¶

Li 2022¶

Li, B., Yu, Q., & Yang, F. (2022). The effect of blended instruction on student performance: A meta-analysis of 106 empirical studies from China and abroad. Best Evidence in Chinese Education, 10(2):1395-1403. https://doi.org/10.15354/bece.22.ar018

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Moderator analysis of the meta-analysis comparing pooled effect sizes across five education levels: senior secondary, junior secondary, higher education, primary, and adult. All are above 0.5; the between-group test (p = 0.550 > 0.05) shows no significant differences, though the article notes an "exceptionally great positive effect at the senior secondary level".

"0.867, 0.758, 0.647, 0.566 and 0.565 respectively. The effect sizes at senior secondary and junior secondary levels are both above 0.75"
