## Evidence

### Davies 2013

Davies, P. L., Schelly, C. L., & Spooner, C. L. (2013). Measuring the Effectiveness of Universal Design for Learning Intervention in Postsecondary Education. Journal of Postsecondary Education and Disability, 26(3), 195-220. http://accessproject.colostate.edu/udl

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A priori comparisons in Table 2: Q34 control rose from 53.73 to 65.48 (t = 4.01, p < .002) while intervention change was NS (t = 0.39); the article notes the control group began significantly lower and improved but did not significantly surpass the intervention group's post rating.

> "The control group experienced more change pre- to post-questionnaire than the intervention group for only two questions. The two questions in this grouping were Q33 (the instructor expresses his or her personal enthusiasm) and Q34 (feedback provided on assignments is helpful and instructive)."
