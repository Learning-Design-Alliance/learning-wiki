## Evidence

### Rohr A. 2022

Rohr A., Perrenot C., Pitta A., Celerier I., Labrousse M., Renard Y., Cadiot G., Brugel M. (2022). Reperfused human cadaver as a new simulation model for colonoscopy: a pilot study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-022-09763-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Workload analysis using a modified NASA Task Load Index (with an added mental-disturbance item, 1–20 scale) administered to all 24 participants after their procedure, reported in the workload Results subsection.

> "Modified NASA-TLX survey showed that mental, physical, and technical demands were gradually higher the lower the level was (Supplementary material—Table S4)."

### Rohr A. 2022 (2)

Rohr A., Perrenot C., Pitta A., Celerier I., Labrousse M., Renard Y., Cadiot G., Brugel M. (2022). Reperfused human cadaver as a new simulation model for colonoscopy: a pilot study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-022-09763-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

> "Novices were significantly less disturbed working on human cadavers than intermediaries or skilled participants (p = 0.029)."
