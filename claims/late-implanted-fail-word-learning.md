# Early implanted children and their NH age-matched peers demonstrated word learning, while late implanted children did not unlike their peers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Paired t-tests showed significant target preferences for the early implanted group (Cohen's d = 1.41), their NH peers (d = .68), and the late group's NH peers (d = 1.17), but not for the late implanted group. [→ Derek M. Houston 2012](#derek-m-houston-2012)

## Evidence

### Derek M. Houston 2012

Derek M. Houston, Jessica Stewart, Aaron Moberly, George Hollich, and Richard T. Miyamoto. (2012). Word learning in deaf children with cochlear implants: effects of early auditory experience. Dev Sci. 2012 May ; 15(3): 448–461. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-7687.2012.01140.x

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Paired t-tests on mean looking time differences for early implanted, late implanted, and their NH age-matched groups. The early implanted group showed d = 1.41; the late implanted group did not differ from chance (t(7) < 1).

> "Paired t-tests revealed that the mean looking time differences between the target and nontarget was statistically significant for the early implanted group (t(11) = 4.85, p = .001, Cohen's d = 1.41), their normal-hearing peers (t(11) = 2.32, p = .04, Cohen's d = .68), and the normal-hearing peers of the late implanted group (t(7) = 3.14, Cohen's d = 1.17), but not for the late implanted group (t(7) < 1)."
