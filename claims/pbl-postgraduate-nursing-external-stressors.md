Wang Sixue, Yuan Yue, Zhou Chunyi, He Huijuan, Wang Yuncui. (2026). Postgraduate nursing students' experiences in a project-based learning model: a qualitative focus group study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08649-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative focus group study of 21 postgraduate nursing students; thematic analysis of four focus group interviews. Students described time conflicts with other coursework and clinical duties, and competency gaps in complex tasks such as judging peers' research proposals — "the dual pressures of time and capacity".

> "This theme reflected the dual pressures of time and capacity faced by postgraduate nursing students in the PBL course. While these pressures essentially highlighted the challenges of role balance and the rationality of project design"
