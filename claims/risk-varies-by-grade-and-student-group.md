# Risk identification varies by grade and student group: kindergarten and grade 3 students were less often at risk than grades 1–2, and economically disadvantaged, English learner, Hispanic, Black, and special education students were more often identified at risk

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Fewer kindergarten and grade 3 students were identified at any level of risk than grade 1 and 2 students, and more students in several disadvantaged groups were identified at risk than their peers. [→ Mariann Lemke 2023](#mariann-lemke-2023)
