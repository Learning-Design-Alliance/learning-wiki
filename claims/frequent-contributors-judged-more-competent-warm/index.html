Renoux 2024¶

Renoux, M., Goudeau, S., Alexopoulos, T., Bouquet, C. A. & Cimpian, A. (2024). The inherence bias in preschoolers' explanations for achievement differences: replication and extension. npj Science of Learning 9:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00218-w

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Study 1 one-sample t tests against the explicit midpoint (3 = same as peers) on competence and warmth items. Nicer: d = 0.45; liked by teacher: d = 0.42.
