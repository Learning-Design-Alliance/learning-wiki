## Evidence

### Lichand 2024

Lichand, G., Christen, J., & Van Egeraat, E. (2024). Neglecting students’ socio-emotional skills magniﬁed learning losses during the pandemic. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00235-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

OLS treatment-effect analysis of 2018, 2019 and 2021 State-wide school-level proficiency exams (340 school-grade-subject-year observations pooled). Learning deteriorated tremendously during the pandemic: average proficiency backtracked 2.84 years in math and 2.25 years in Portuguese against the pre-pandemic trend.

> "Nudges were able to prevent 24.2% of learning losses in Portuguese (column 2) and 7.5% of those in math (column 3), but these effect sizes are only imprecisely estimated: 90% CI"
