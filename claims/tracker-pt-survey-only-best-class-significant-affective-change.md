## Evidence

### Loo Kang Wee 2014

Loo Kang Wee, Tze Kwang Leong. (2014). Performance Task using Video Analysis and Modelling to promote K12 eight practices of science. GIREP-MPTL 2014 International Conference, Paper 294. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.01532](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.01532)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post Likert-scale perception survey (1-6 scale, middle 3.5) of N=273 Secondary Three students after the Tracker performance task, shown in Figure 7. The authors report "only the best class registered a significant positive change" in affective items like "I look forward to physics lessons", with the cohort showing smaller positive changes. No effect size is printed.

> "Our initial analysis of N=273 students’ pre-post perception survey on the experience of the performance task suggests  only the best class registered a significant positive change (Figure 7) in the affective domains"
