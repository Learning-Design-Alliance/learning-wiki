Preliminary validation of the assessment instruments before the trial, comparing expert and trainee performances; "The expert group achieved average scores of 39.2/40 on the OSATS grid and 39.7/41 on the checklist", significantly above student scores (P = 0.001 and P = 0.008).

> "The expert group achieved average scores of 39.2/40 on the OSATS grid and 39.7/41 on the checklist, while the student group obtained average scores of 31.4/40 and 32.3/41, respectively (P = 0.001 and P = 0.008, respectively)."
