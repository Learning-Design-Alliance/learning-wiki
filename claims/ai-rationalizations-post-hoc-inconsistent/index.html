Kim 2026¶

Kim, Jiyoon; Toyama, Kentaro; Kim, Sangmi; Carroll, John. (2026). "It's OK Because...": The Wild West of Student Rationalization of AI Use in Academic Writing. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29090 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.29090

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 20 undergraduates who self-reported AI use for writing, triangulated with chat logs and syllabi. The article reports that "Among the 20 participants, 15 exhibited this pattern" of evolving, contradictory reasoning, and that some participants only considered the ethics when interview questions raised it.

"Their thinking was fluid, unfinished, and some- times internally contradictory. Among the 20 participants, 15 exhibited this pattern."
