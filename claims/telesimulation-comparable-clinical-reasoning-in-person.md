### Marshall J 2025

Marshall J, Shiu C, Raatz M, Penman A, Beak K, Clarke S, Ward EC. (2025). Telesimulation for Training in Infant Feeding: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Dysphagia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00455-024-10746-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-groups comparison in the same randomized controlled trial of 39 speech pathologists (17 in-person, 22 telesimulation). The telesimulation group showed "a greater extent of change overall in clinical reasoning than the in-person group" with partial ƞ2=0.12, which the authors did not consider statistically significant.

> "Between groups, the telesimulation group demonstrated a greater extent of change overall in clinical reasoning than the in-person group (Mean difference telesim = 2.23; Mean difference in-person = 1.71; F1,37=4.935; p = 0.033; partial ƞ2=0.12) (Table 2) although this difference was not considered statistically significant."
