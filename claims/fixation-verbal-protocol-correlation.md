## Evidence

### Mike Stieff 2011

Mike Stieff, Mary Hegarty, Ghislain Deslongchamps. (2011). Identifying Representational Competence with Multi-Representational Displays. Cognition and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2010.507318

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative comparison between the independent eye-fixation and protocol analyses (conducted by two researchers) in the ten-participant interview study. The printed correlations were large: "correlations ranged from .63 to .96".

> "the amount of attention to the different representations, as measured by total fixation duration, was highly correlated with how often they were mentioned in the verbal protocols (correlations ranged from .63 to .96)"
