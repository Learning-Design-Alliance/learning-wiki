## Evidence

### Janneke van de Pol 2024

Janneke van de Pol, Eleanor Rowan, Eva Janssen, Tamara van Gog. (2024). Effects of availability of diagnostic and non-diagnostic cues on the accuracy of teachers' judgments of students' text comprehension. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09383-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel regression in the same within-subjects experiment (N = 33 teachers) found "judgment accuracy was also significantly higher" with only diagnostic cues than with a mix; the article explains teachers still used non-diagnostic cues to a limited extent in the mixed condition.

> "judgment accuracy was also significantly higher in condition 2 (diagnostic cues only) than condition 3 (mixed cues) (B = 0.23, SE = 0.08, p = 0.003; R2 = 0.606)"
