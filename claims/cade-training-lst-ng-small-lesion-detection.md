The implementation of computer-aided detection in an initial endoscopy training improves the quality measures of trainees' future colonoscopies: a retrospective cohort study. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary LST-morphology analysis in the 6000-patient retrospective cohort: the AI-trained group detected 10 LST-NG lesions of 10–20 mm versus 3 in the conventional group, a 0.2% difference with intermediate data quality and 41% power; the LST missing-data rate was 0%. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "AI-assisted endoscopy training significantly improved the detection of LST-NG lesions smaller than 20 mm by 0.2% (CI 95%: 0.1–0.4%)."
