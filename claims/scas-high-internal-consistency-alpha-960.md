## Evidence

### KOÇAK Cemal 2025

KOÇAK Cemal, GÜVERCİN Hande, ESEN Ecem, ÇÖL Meltem. (2025). Development study of scientific competence and activity scale in medical faculty research assistants. Turkish Journal of Medical Sciences. https://doi.org/10.55730/1300-0144.6026

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the SCAS administered to 299 research assistants at one medical faculty. The article reports "the Cronbach’s alpha coefficient for the 29 items was calculated as 0.960" and that no item-total correlation fell below 0.30; subscale alphas ranged 0.761–0.955 (Table 4).

> "The Cronbach’s alpha coefficient for the 29 items was calculated as 0.960, and the item-total correlation coefficients were observed to exceed 0.3."
