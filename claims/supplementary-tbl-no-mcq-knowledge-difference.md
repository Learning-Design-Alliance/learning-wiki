## Evidence

### Meike Jost 2017

Meike Jost, Peter Brüstle, Marianne Giesler, Michel Rijntjes, Jochen Brich. (2017). Effects of additional team-based learning on students’ clinical reasoning skills: a pilot study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-017-2614-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of the mandatory 40-question MCQE closing the neurology course (n = 11 TBL, n = 13 non-TBL; Mann–Whitney-U tests). Table 1 reports overall means of 35.0 (TBL) versus 36.0 (non-TBL), p = 0.303, with subgroup p values of 0.473 and 0.518; no effect sizes are printed.

> "There was no statistically significant difference between the TBL-group and the non-TBL-group in the overall mean score as well as in the two subgroups of question referring to the 4 topics of the seminars/TBL (six questions) and to the other neurological topics (34 questions) (Table 1)."
