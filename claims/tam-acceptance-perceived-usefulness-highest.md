Reaction-level technology-acceptance survey using a 21-item questionnaire adapted from the TAM (seven domains, five-point Likert, cutoff 3). All domains scored above cutoff; the highest were "Perceived usefulness (4.06 ± 0.88)", "Concentration (3.96 ± 0.83)", and "Attitude towards using (3.95 ± 0.90)".

> "Based on the findings of Table 2, the highest mean scores belong to the components Perceived usefulness (4.06 ± 0.88), Concentration (3.96 ± 0.83), and Attitude towards using (3.95 ± 0.90), and in all components, the scores are above the cutoff point."
