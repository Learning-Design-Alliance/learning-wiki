Evidence¶

Nader Haghani & Samira Kiani 2018¶

Nader Haghani & Samira Kiani. (2018). On the Efficiency of Text Production in Vocabulary Learning: An Empirical Study on Iranian GFL-Learners. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n3p176

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Two-way ANOVA for the 20–30-year age group (CG n = 15, EC n = 21); the article reports "F (1, 34) = .80, p = .379" for short-term retention and non-significant results at all other intervals. No effect size printed.

"The F-value for short-term retention is statistically insignificant (F (1, 34) = .80, p = .379). This also applies to the medium-term retention (F (1, 34) = 1.44, p = .239)"
