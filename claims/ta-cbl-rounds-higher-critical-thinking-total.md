## Evidence

### Zhao Rui 2024

Zhao Rui, Ding TingTing, Meng JinPing, Lei Miao, Ma Huili. (2024). Think-aloud approach combined with case-based learning in nursing teaching round for new nurses in cancer hospital. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05891-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training comparison on the Chinese version of the Critical Thinking Ability Scale (70 items, 6-point Likert, range 70–420; Cronbach's α 0.802). Table 3 reports total scores of "322.47 ± 37.78" for the observation group versus "289.98.±32.21" for controls, t = 4.573, P < 0.001; no effect size printed.
