Evidence¶

Liu Jingxia & Liu Li 2013¶

Liu Jingxia & Liu Li. (2013). An Empirical Study on the Application of Theme Theory in the Field of Writing Pedagogy. English Language Teaching, 6(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n5p117

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative sample analysis of two compositions titled "Reading Extensively": the good composition thematized "reading" in 40% of its themes and used 6 TP patterns, while the poor composition had 5 simple themes among 8 T-units and T-units "not involved in any progression".

"The above analysis has revealed that good writers are mo re skillful in organizing their compositions through the reasonable choices of themes and TP patterns."
