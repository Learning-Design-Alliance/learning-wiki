## Evidence

### Alba López-Moraga 2024

Alba López-Moraga, Laura Luyten & Tom Beckers. (2024). A history of avoidance does not impact extinction learning in male rats. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00223-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Simple-effects analysis of the significant group-by-day interaction on suppression of lever pressing in Experiment 1, where the platform was absent during extinction. The article prints the interaction effect size ηp2 = 0.13 (i = 2 by the code table) alongside Q(3, 10.61) = 7.14, p = 0.007.

> "revealing that Avoiders showed more suppression than Yoked animals on day 1 (V = 135,p < 0.001) and less than Yoked animals on day 2 ( V = 32.5, p = 0.024), suggesting steeper extinction learning in the Avoiders. Group differences leveled off by day 3."
