Scerif 2023¶

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of EF intervention studies and meta-analyses (e.g. Melby-Lervåg & Hulme 2013; Kassai et al. 2019), reporting "poor evidence of even near transfer" between EF components and limited transfer to mathematics.

"while evidence for the immediate efficacy of isolated EF training on the trained EF seems robust even in pre-schoolers, there is poor evidence of even near transfer (from one EF to another, for example, from inhibitory training to working memory outcomes; e.g. Thorell et al., 2009)."
