# The Tikichuela interactive audio math program raised preschool standardized math scores by 0.16 standard deviations in under five months

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Students in treatment schools scored 0.16 standard deviations higher than controls at endline, moving the average treated student from the 50th to the 57th percentile of the control distribution. [→ Naslund Hadley 2014](#naslund-hadley-2014)

## Evidence

### Naslund Hadley 2014

Naslund Hadley, Emma, Parker, Susan W. & Hernandez-Agramonte, Juan Manuel (2014). Fostering early math comprehension: Experimental evidence from Paraguay. Global Education Review, 1(4), 135-154. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1055163

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial of 265 school districts (131 treatment, 134 control schools; about 4,500 students), regressing endline on baseline standardized EGMA-adapted math scores. The fully controlled specification shows a treatment effect of "0.16 standard deviation" (2,800 observations), an effect size of 0.16 SD.

> "Students in the pilot program saw an increase in test scores (over those not in the program) of 0.15 standard deviation for column one and two, and 0.16 standard deviation for column three."
