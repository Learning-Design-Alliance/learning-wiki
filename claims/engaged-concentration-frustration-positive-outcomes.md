## Evidence

### Pardos 2014

Pardos, Z. A., Baker, R. S., San Pedro, M., Gowda, S. M., & Gowda, S. M. (2014). Affective States and State Tests: Investigating How Affect and Engagement during the School Year Predict End-of-Year Learning Outcomes. Journal of Learning Analytics, 1(1), 107-128. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2014.11.6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Longitudinal analysis of ASSISTments tutor log-data with automated affect detectors estimating engaged concentration and frustration probabilities per student action, related to end-of-year standardized exam performance. The article reports "Engaged concentration and, surprisingly, frustration are both associated with positive learning outcomes"; no effect sizes are printed in the available text.
