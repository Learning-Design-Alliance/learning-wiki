# People with MLD show a large pooled deficit in mathematics performance versus typically performing peers (g = 1.52)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across 283 effect sizes from 145 studies, MLD groups performed substantially worse than typically performing groups in mathematics, Hedges's g = 1.52, p < .001. [→ Kroesbergen 2023](#kroesbergen-2023)

## Evidence

### Kroesbergen 2023

Kroesbergen, E. H.; Huijsmans, M. D. E.; Friso-van den Bos, I. (2023). A Meta-Analysis on the Differences in Mathematical and Cognitive Skills Between Individuals With and Without Mathematical Learning Disabilities. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543221132773

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic unconditional model of 283 effect sizes from 145 studies comparing MLD and typically performing groups on mathematics. The pooled difference was "Hedges's g = 1.52", significant at p < .001, with 90% of variation attributed to true effect-size differences.

> "The weighted mean difference of Hedges's g = 1.52 demonstrated the expected significant differences between the typically performing groups and MLD groups, with p < .001."
