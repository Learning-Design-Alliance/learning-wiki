## Evidence

### Tomlin D 2025

Tomlin D, Bowers P, Zonca B, Graydon K, Phillips J, Rance G. (2025). Speech-in-noise recognition as a predictor of academic achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0324998

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 1) of Sound Scouts subtest scores and NAPLAN domains for all children. The printed correlation coefficient for reading and speech-in-noise was 0.19 (p < .05 threshold marked); the article reports the significant correlations with the printed p-values but no standardized effect size for the association.

> "A statistically significant correlation between reading scores and speech-in-noise (p = .020) as well as overall Sound Scouts score (p = .004) was found."
