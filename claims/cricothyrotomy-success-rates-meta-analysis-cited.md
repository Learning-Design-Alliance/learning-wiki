Furin Molly MD, MS, Kohn Melissa MD, MS, Overberger Ryan DO, MS, Jaslow David MD, MPH. (2016). Out-of-Hospital Surgical Airway Management: Does Scope of Practice Equal Actual Practice? Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2016.3.28729

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The review reports, citing Hubble et al, a meta-analysis of 56 studies in which "needle cricothyrotomy success rates of 65.8% and surgical cricothyrotomy success rates of 90.5%" and physician success at 97.1%. The article cautions that paramedic training and skills education were variable in many of these studies and results may not generalize to the broader group of ground paramedics. The review did not independently re-analyze these data.

> "A meta-analysis of 56 studies by Hubble et al reported needle cricothyrotomy success rates of 65.8% and surgical cricothyrotomy success rates of 90.5%, with the physician success rate slightly higher at 97.1%.17"
