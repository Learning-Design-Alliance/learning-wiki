## Evidence

### Emely Hoch 2023

Emely Hoch, Yael Sidi, Rakefet Ackerman, Vincent Hoogerheide, Katharina Scheiter. (2023). Comparing Mental Effort, Difficulty, and Confidence Appraisals in Problem-Solving: A Metacognitive Perspective. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09779-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Online experiment with 224 Prolific participants solving 30 missing tan task (MTT) items in four appraisal groups. ANOVA on within-participant appraisal-response time correlations showed a "significant medium effect of appraisal type" with η2 = 0.06; only the confidence–goal-driven effort contrast reached significance (p = 0.002).

> "There was a significant medium effect of appraisal type, F(3, 220) = 4.51, MSE = 0.08, p< 0.005,η2 = 0.06. Post hoc pairwise comparisons showed that the correlation with response time was significantly weaker for confidence than for goal-driven effort appraisals, p = 0.002."
