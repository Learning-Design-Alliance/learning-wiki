## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Time 1 inhibitory and attentional control deficit significantly predicted hyperactivity/impulsivity at Time 1 (ΔR² = 0.08) and Time 2 (ΔR² = 0.11) and inattention at Time 1 (ΔR² = 0.06), and remained significant when all tasks were considered simultaneously. ## Evidence

### Zheng Que 2022

Zheng Que, Cheng Yu Yan, Sonuga-Barke Edmund, Shum Kathy Kar-man. (2022). Do Executive Dysfunction, Delay Aversion, and Time Perception Deficit Predict ADHD Symptoms and Early Academic Performance in Preschoolers. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-022-00937-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regressions in a longitudinal sample of 131 preschoolers (96 at Time 2) predicting parent-rated SNAP-IV symptoms, with age, general ability, and treatment condition as covariates. The authors report IAC deficit was "the strongest among the three components".

> "Time 1 IAC deficit contributed significantly to HI symptoms at both Time 1 (ΔR 2 = 0.08, p = .02) and Time 2 (ΔR 2 = 0.11, p = .05), as well as IA symptoms at Time 1 (ΔR 2 = 0.06, p = .02)."
