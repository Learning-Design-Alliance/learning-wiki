Evidence¶

Rezaei and Dezhara 2011¶

Omid Rezaei, Salman Dezhara. (2011). An Investigation of the Possible Effects of Favored Contexts in Second Language Vocabulary Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 4(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v4n4p97

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Two paired t-tests (24 d.f.) on the experimental group's sport and geography pretest-posttest scores, both significant at the 0.05 level. The article reports t = -16 for sport and t = -18.3 for geography, concluding participants "have not performed better in sport passages than geography ones"; hypothesis 1 was rejected and hypothesis 2 confirmed.

"As it can be inferred from the difference between the t-value obtained from scores of sport pretest and posttest (-16) and t-value obtained from scores of geography pretest and posttest (-18.3), it could be concluded that the participants have not performed better in sport passages than geography ones."
