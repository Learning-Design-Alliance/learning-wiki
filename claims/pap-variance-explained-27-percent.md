## Evidence

### Izaguirre 2023

Izaguirre, L. A., Rodríguez‐Fernández, A., & Fernández‐Zabala, A. (2023). Perceived academic performance explained by school climate, positive psychological variables and life satisfaction. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 93, 318–332. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12557

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Explained-variance figures reported in the Results section for the final structural model with 1397 adolescents: 45% for resilience, 42% for life satisfaction, and 27% for perceived academic performance from all studied variables combined.

> "School climate and PEI were found to explain 45% of the variance observed in resilience. Similarly, school climate, PEI and resilience were found to explain 42% of life satisfaction, whereas together, all the variables studied explained 27% of PAP."
