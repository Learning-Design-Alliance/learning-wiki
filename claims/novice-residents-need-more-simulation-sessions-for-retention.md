`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of their own cohort findings; the article offers this as a possible explanation, not a tested result, and cites literature that skill decay timing varies with the specific skill, level of learning, and interval between teaching and assessment.

> "In the present study, residents participated in three interprofessional simulated scenarios, which may not have been sufficient to retain these skills. Furthermore, they are beginner learners, which may require more training sessions and more time to learn these skills."
