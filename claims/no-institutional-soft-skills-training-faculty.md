### Penha 2026

Serrano, S. P., Barlem, E. L. D., da Luz, E. M. F., Silva, K. L., & dos Santos Mendes, V. M. (2026). Nursing faculty perceptions of soft skills teaching in undergraduate nursing education: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education, 26(1), 1001. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09339-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview study of 26 nursing faculty; participant report of no training on soft skills. Another participant described communication training pursued independently as "not institutionalized."

> "I have never received any type of training regarding this [soft skills], but I consider them extremely important, because it is impossible to talk about nursing without talking about these skills."
