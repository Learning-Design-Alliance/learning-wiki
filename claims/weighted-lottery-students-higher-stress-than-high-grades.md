### Broks VMA 2023

Broks VMA, Stegers-Jager KM, Fikrat-Wevers S, Van den Broek WW, Woltman AM. (2023). The association between how medical students were selected and their perceived stress levels in Year-1 of medical school. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04411-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

In the same multilevel Model 2 analysis of 650 students, weighted-lottery-selected students showed a medium effect size of 0.5 for higher stress perception relative to high-grades-selected students; estimated stress for female lottery students was 29.7 versus 25.7 for high-grades students.

> "weighted lottery (B = 3.95 [1.49–6.42], ES = 0.5 (medium), p < .01) had higher stress perception levels compared to students selected by high grades"
