### P. Klein 2021

P. Klein, L. Ivanjek, M.N. Dahlkemper, K. Jeličić, M.-A. Geyer, S. Küchemann, and A. Susac. (2021). Studying physics during the COVID-19 pandemic: Student assessments of learning achievement, perceived effectiveness of online recitations, and online laboratories. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of the same five-university survey (RQ1), with significance level set by Bonferroni correction (p = 0.05/15 = 0.003). The article reports that learning success "is negatively correlated with students’ attitudes towards online learning"; Table III lists the attitude correlation but no standardized effect size is printed in the quoted finding.

> "It is negatively correlated with students’ attitudes towards online learning. In other words, students who have a positive attitude towards online learning also expect a higher learning achievement."
