## Evidence

### Hu Xiaolei 2019

Hu Xiaolei, Zhang Hengyan, Song Yuan, Wu Chenchen, Yang Qingqing, Shi Zhaoming, Zhang Xiaomei, Chen Weidong. (2019). Implementation of flipped classroom combined with problem-based learning: an approach to promote learning about hyperthyroidism in the endocrinology internship. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1714-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Domain-level analysis of the same randomized trial's quizzes. Quiz items were classified by Bloom's Taxonomy: "remember" and "understand" items formed basic theoretical knowledge; higher-level items formed clinical case analysis. No effect size is printed, so no magnitude is claimed.

> "Furthermore, there was no significant difference in basic theoretical knowledge between the TG and CG after classes (p > 0.05). However, the most improvement was observed in the CG regarding analyzing clinical cases (p < 0.001) (Fig. 2b)."
