## Evidence

### H. Asorey and A. López Dávalos 2011

H. Asorey and A. López Dávalos. (2011). Fermi Problem: Power developed at the eruption of the Puyehue-Cordón Caulle volcanic system in June 2011. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1109.1165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1109.1165)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive account from the abstract of the instructional implementation: hypotheses were built and evaluated across two consecutive lectures, then students performed the calculations. No learning outcome measures are reported.

> "These calculations have been done as a pedagogic exercise, and after evaluation of the hypothesis was done in the classroom, the calculations have been performed by the students."

### H. Asorey and A. López Dávalos 2011 (2)

H. Asorey and A. López Dávalos. (2011). Fermi Problem: Power developed at the eruption of the Puyehue-Cordón Caulle volcanic system in June 2011. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1109.1165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1109.1165)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Section 2, hypothesis 5: the mixture density was measured with a measuring cup and kitchen scale, giving ρ = 600 kg/m3. The authors note samples taken days later gave higher density values, probably because rain water dissolved or dragged away components.

> "The density of the sand and ash fell in Bariloche was determined using household items: a measuring cup used to measure the flour or sugar and a kitchen scale."
