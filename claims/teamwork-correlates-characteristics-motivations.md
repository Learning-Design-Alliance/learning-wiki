## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Among the four school creative climate subscales, only teamwork was correlated with a creative teaching behavior subscale: characteristics and motivations (r = 0.35, p < .01). [→ Liu Hsing-Yuan 2019](#liu-hsing-yuan-2019)

## Evidence

### Liu Hsing-Yuan 2019

Liu Hsing-Yuan, Wang I-Teng. (2019). Creative teaching behaviors of health care school teachers in Taiwan: mediating and moderating effects. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1641-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of subscale scores from the 53-teacher cross-sectional survey; teamwork was the only SCCS subscale correlated with a CTBS subscale, at r = 0.35, p < .01 (r = 0.35, an i=2-magnitude effect).

> "The teamwork (TW) subscale of the SCCS was moderately correlated with the CTBS subscale of characteristics and motivations (CM) (r = 0.35, p < .01), suggesting a relationship between these aspects of creative school climate and creative teaching behaviors, respectively."
