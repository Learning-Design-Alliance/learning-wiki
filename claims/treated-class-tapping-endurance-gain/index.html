Evidence¶

Emma Kohnky 1913¶

Emma Kohnky, M.A. (1913). Preliminary Study of the Effect of Dental Treatment upon the Physical and Mental Efficiency of School Children. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Results tables for the fatigue index of the tapping test (Whipple's Test 10), computed as the ratio of last-15-second decrease to first-15-second total over a 60-second trial. Left-hand medians fell from 17.5% to 13.5% in Room 21 versus 15.1% to 14% in Room 18. Percents are descriptive; no test statistic is printed.

"As with the right hand, although Room 21 shows greater fatigue in the early test, it shows .5% less in the second, and thus shows a gain in endurance of 2.9% over Room 18. As pointed out above, the fact that Room 21 leads in every case in total number of taps makes the lead in endurance the more conspicuous."
