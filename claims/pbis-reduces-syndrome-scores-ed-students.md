The Impact of Intensive Positive Behavioral Supports on the Behavioral Functioning of Students with Emotional Disturbance: How Much Does Fidelity Matter? Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Tests on TRF syndrome scores in the same pre-post study (N = 37) found significant reductions on three syndrome scales; other syndrome scales (e.g., Anxious/Depressed, Rule-Breaking) were not significant. No effect sizes printed.

> "Further, statistically significant reductions were found on the Thought Problems (p = .034), Attention Problems (p = .003), and Aggressive Behavior (p = .014) syndrome scores from pre- to post-test."
