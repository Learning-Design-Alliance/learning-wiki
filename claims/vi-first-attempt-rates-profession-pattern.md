Analysis of background website data from 163 health professionals completing all 10 VI questions between July 27 and November 14, 2015, analyzed with chi-square for trend (Table 3). The printed average first-attempt correct rates are 65%, 68%, and 82%.

> "The rate of correctly answered questions on first attempt was 65% for nurses, 68% for doctors, and 82% for pharmacists, significantly higher in the pharmacist group (P<.001)."
