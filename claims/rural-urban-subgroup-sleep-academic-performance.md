## Evidence

### Gu Linni 2025

Gu Linni, Zhu Rui, Tian Donghua. (2025). Body mass index mediates the association between sleep duration and academic performance: An evidence in Chinese adolescent students. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323969

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subsample OLS regression analysis on rural (n = 3,677) and urban (n = 3,541) CEPS students, comparing the sleep-duration coefficient across the two household-register groups; the article attributes the rural–urban difference to urban students' access to tutoring classes.

> "Subgroup analyses further demonstrated that a 10-minute increase in sleep duration led to an 8.5% decrease in academic performance for rural adolescent students, while urban students experienced a 6.7% decrease under similar circumstances."
