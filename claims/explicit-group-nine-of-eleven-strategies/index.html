Ebadi and Pourzandi 2015¶

Saman Ebadi, Mahsa Pourzandi. (2015). The Effects of Explicit and Implicit Pragmatic Instruction on the Development of Compliments and Compliment Responses. li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 5, No. 4, October - December. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=The+Effects+of+Explicit+and+Implicit+Pragmatic+Instruction+on+the+Development+of+Compliments+and+Compliment+Responses

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Post-test analysis of the explicit instruction group's DCT responses in this quasi-experimental study (n = 19 in the explicit group per the treatment description). The article reports the printed percentages 86.19% and 79.93% as descriptive results; no effect size or significance test is printed for this comparison.

"It was the explicit instruction group with the total percentage of 86.19% that deployed almost all strategies in both parts of the questionnaire. 79.93% of participants in explicit instruction group could use at least 9 strategies out of 11 strategies provided for both parts of the questionnaire."
