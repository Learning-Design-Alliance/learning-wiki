## Evidence

### Yamamoto R 2026

Yamamoto R, Nojiri T, Yoshida D, Oyamada Y, Fukutoku A, Kon K. (2026). Accuracy of dynamic computer-assisted implant surgery among novice operators using three different patient registration methods: an in-vitro study. International Journal of Implant Dentistry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40729-026-00666-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Implant-level analysis (n = 25 per group) of procedural time, defined as duration from completion of registration to final implant placement; means were 640.00 s (ND), 478.88 s (XC), 435.24 s (XM), with "no significant difference... between the XC and XM groups (adjusted p = 0.75)".

> "Post hoc analysis using Dunn's test with Bonferroni correction demonstrated that the ND group required significantly longer procedural times than both the XC group (adjusted p = 3.96 × 10−⁸) and the XM group (adjusted p = 1.53 × 10−11)."
