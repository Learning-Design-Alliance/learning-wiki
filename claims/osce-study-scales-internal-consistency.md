## Evidence

### Sarsak Hassan Izzeddin 2025

Sarsak Hassan Izzeddin. (2025). Professional Identity and Self-efficacy Among Occupational Therapy Students Engaged in Objective Structured Clinical Examination Utilizing Simulation Learning. Inquiry: A Journal of Medical Care Organization, Provision and Financing. https://doi.org/10.1177/00469580251315351

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cronbach's alpha analysis of the adapted scales (modified from nursing to occupational therapy wording, pilot tested with 20 students). The article reports "α = .844 before OSCE and α = .964 after OSCE" for professional identity.

> "The reliability coefficients for the 12 items of the professional identity scale were high, with α  =  .844 before OSCE and α  =  .964 after OSCE. Similarly, the 9 items of the self-efficacy scale also showed high reliability, with α  =  .906 before OSCE and α  =  .936 after OSCE"
