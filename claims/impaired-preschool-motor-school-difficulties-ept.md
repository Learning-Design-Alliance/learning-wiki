Five‐year neurodevelopmental assessment of extremely preterm or extremely low birthweight infants: Association with school performance. Acta Paediatr. 2025;114:131–140. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.17416

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression analysis of school difficulties in a nationwide cohort of 248 children born extremely preterm or with extremely low birthweight. The univariate odds ratio of 8.1 for impaired versus normal motor performance was printed; in the multivariate model the estimate fell to "1.7 (0.7–4.12)". No effect size beyond the odds ratio was printed.

> "Impaired motor performance was associated with school difficulties in the univariate analyses (OR 8.1, 95% CI 4.1–16.1) (Table 3)."
