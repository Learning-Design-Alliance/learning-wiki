### Zhu Xiaocheng 2026

Zhu Xiaocheng, Tang Anliu, Chin Munle, Chang Huan. (2026). Developing purposeful observation during early clinical exposure in anesthesiology: a cluster-randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10042-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized study of 154 second-year medical students in 12 clerkship groups; the structured group scored higher on the prespecified primary outcome, "learning objective clarity and self-reported purposeful observation" (22.55 ± 1.17 vs. 16.74 ± 1.36, P < 0.001).
