Fan 2025¶

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

ANOVA on a 10-item knowledge test on AI in education, pre and post, in the randomised four-group experiment. Neither pre-test nor post-test differed significantly, so knowledge gain did not differ despite the AI group's essay-score advantage.

"The ANOVA results indicated no signiﬁcant diﬀerences between the groups in terms of the pre-test score (F=1.294, p=0.281, η2=0.036) and post-test score (F=0.913, p=0.438, η2=0.030), which means no signiﬁcant diﬀerences in knowledge gain."

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

ANOVA on a 10-item transfer test on AI in healthcare in the same randomised experiment. The printed effect size was η2=0.000, indicating no detectable group difference in knowledge transfer.

"We compared the transfer test scores between the four groups, and ANOVA results showed that there were no signiﬁcant diﬀerences between the four groups (F=0.019, p=0.996, η2=0.000)."
