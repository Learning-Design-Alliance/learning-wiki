## Evidence

### Hardon Sem F. 2018

Hardon Sem F., Horeman Tim, Bonjer H. Jaap, Meijerink W. J. H. Jeroen. (2018). Force-based learning curve tracking in fundamental laparoscopic skills training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-6090-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Trial-count analysis of 1842 recorded performances (1594 successful) by six novices, tracking trials needed to meet expert-derived thresholds. The article reports "it takes a student on average between 11 and 36 trials to master a task" and concludes "Proficiency levels for all tasks can be reached in 2 weeks of at home training".
