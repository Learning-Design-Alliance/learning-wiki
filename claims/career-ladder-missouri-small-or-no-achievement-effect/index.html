Quasi-experimental matched comparison of 524 Missouri school districts over a nine-year period, using longitudinal data on district math and reading scores; "estimated effects range from small positive effects to no effect."

Kevin Booker 2009¶

Kevin Booker, Steven Glazerman. (2009). Does the Missouri Teacher Career Ladder Program Raise Student Achievement? Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/does-the-missouri-teacher-career-ladder-program-raise-student-achievement

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Statewide longitudinal analysis of district math and reading scores for 524 Missouri school districts over a nine-year period. The article reports that the estimated effects of the Career Ladder program "range from small positive effects to no effect in both math and reading."
