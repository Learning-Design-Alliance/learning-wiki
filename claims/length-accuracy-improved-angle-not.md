## Evidence

### Zhong Zixing 2025

Zhong Zixing, Lu Yijuan, Pu Yuqun, Luo Qiong, Zhao Baihui. (2025). Educational efficacy of the simulation-based episiotomy training: a multi-center observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06887-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Accuracy analysis (Table 3) of the 82-participant observational study, comparing valid length and angle counts before and after the course; total valid length rose from 47 (57.3%) to 62 (75.6%) and valid angle from 47 (57.3%) to 57 (69.5%), p = 0.144.

> "Overall, after the educational programme, the accuracy of the length significantly improved (p = 0.001); however, the accuracy of the angle showed no significant difference."
