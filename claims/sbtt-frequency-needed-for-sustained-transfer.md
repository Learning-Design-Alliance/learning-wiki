## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Finstad Anne Strand 2023

Finstad Anne Strand, Aase Ingunn, Bjørshol Conrad Arnfinn, Ballangrud Randi. (2023). In situ simulation-based team training and its significance for transfer of learning to clinical practice—A qualitative focus group interview study of anaesthesia personnel. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04201-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group interview 2 (six months after SBTT). Participants reported mixed transfer experiences and named "Frequency and participation from everyone (in the team)" as keys to success.

> "If it takes a year before next simulation training, then I can’t say it has any effect on my behaviour in clinical practice (No.7.3) … it is fresh produce (No.5.3)."
