### O’Brien A 2023

O’Brien A, Forde C. (2023). Health science staff and student experiences of teaching and assessing clinical skills using digital tools: a qualitative study. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2023.2256656

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study (reflexive thematic analysis of semi-structured group and individual interviews with 10 staff and 8 students across 11 health science disciplines); the article reports that "those who teach and those who learn were consistent in voicing a desire" for blended practical skill teaching. No effect size is reported.
