## Evidence

### Williams 2017

Williams, S. N., Thakore, B. K., & McGee, R. (2017). Providing Social Support for Underrepresented Racial and Ethnic Minority PhD Students in the Biomedical Sciences: A Career Coaching Model. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0021

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview study of 33 URM students; Table 2 codes 24 students receiving appraisal support from coaches. This quote shows a student using his experienced coach as a "marker of how I am progressing" in his career.

> "I use [my coach] as a marker of how I am progressing because he's done this for a long time, he's had students of his own and he's in I guess a higher position and in administration so he has an idea of how this works"

### Williams 2017 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same interview study; 23 students were coded as receiving appraisal support from peers. This quote illustrates validation through learning that others face the same graduate school difficulties.

> "When we started discussing different things that can happen during graduate school, and how to handle them, that was immensely helpful, especially in my personal setting … knowing that I'm not the only one this is happening to"
