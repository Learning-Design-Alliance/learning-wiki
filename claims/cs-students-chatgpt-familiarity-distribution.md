Descriptive survey result from Question 2 of the questionnaire completed by 430 MSc computer science students; the authors report the familiarity distribution "follows a rather normal distribution" and note the one-fifth unfamiliar proportion with surprise.

> "Around 12% of the students indicate that they are very familiar with the tool, whereas 19% of the students indicate that they are not familiar with the tool at all."
