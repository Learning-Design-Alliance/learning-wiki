## Evidence

### Cynthia J. Miller 2015

Cynthia J. Miller, Jeff C. Falcone & Michael J. Metz. (2015). A Comparison of Team-Based Learning Formats: Can We Minimize Stress While Maximizing Results? World Journal of Education, 5(4). http://dx.doi.org/10.5430/wje.v5n4p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results section comparing TBL section averages from 6 medical TBLs (161 students, 966 scores) and 2 dental pilot TBLs (119 students, 238 scores) via one-way ANOVA and Tukey post-hoc test; no significant differences on iRAT or tRAT were found.

> "The revised dental TBL format, compared to the original medical format, resulted in similar student performance during iRAT and tRAT sessions."
