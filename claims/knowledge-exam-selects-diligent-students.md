## Evidence

### Abbiati Milena 2016

Abbiati Milena, Baroffio Anne, Gerbase Margaret W. (2016). Personal profile of medical students selected through a knowledge-based exam only: are we missing suitable students? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.29705

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 311 first-year medical students at the University of Geneva; profiles derived by PCA from 15 measures, then compared between selected and non-selected students by multivariate ANOVA. The article reports a statistically significant difference with p=0.01; no effect size is printed.

> "Students demonstrating a profile of diligence with higher conscientiousness, deep learning approach, and task-focused coping were more frequently selected (p=0.01)."
