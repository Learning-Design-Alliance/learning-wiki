Muasaad Alrasheedi and Luiz Fernando Capretz. (2015). Determination of critical success factors affecting mobile learning: a meta-analysis approach. Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, Volume 14, Issue 2, pp. 41-51. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1095705

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis correlating aggregated CSF means with learner perceptions across nine studies using Microsoft Excel. The article reports "Both content and user friendly design have highly positive correlations with learner perceptions" (Pearson r = 0.92961 and 0.80454 in Table 5), while technical competence showed r = -0.5595.
