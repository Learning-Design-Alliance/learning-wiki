# In an authentic vocational classroom setting, a lesson orchestrated around an iVR serious game produced significantly stronger factual knowledge gains than a student video design control group

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Apprentices learning with the iVR game embedded in a hybrid teaching session showed significantly greater pre-to-post factual knowledge increases than the control group (ηp² = .295). [→ P. Spangenberger 2025](#p-spangenberger-2025)

## Evidence

### P. Spangenberger 2025

P. Spangenberger, N. Matthes, F. Kapp, L. Kruse, J. L. Plass. (2025). Orchestrating iVR technology in an authentic classroom setting and its effects on factual knowledge, comprehension and transfer. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10409-2

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA in the authentic vocational school evaluation study (iVR n=14, control n=15, randomized). The interaction was significant, F(1, 27) = 11.28, p = .002, ηp2 = .295, with post hoc analyses showing factual knowledge scores "were higher for the iVR group".

> "The analysis revealed a statistically significant interaction effect for time and condition (F(1, 27) = 11.28, p = .002, ηp2 = .295), indicating that increases in factual knowledge differed for the two groups."
