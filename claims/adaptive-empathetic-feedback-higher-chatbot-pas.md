## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The Adaptive condition outperforms the Fixed condition on all perceived affective support metrics, and both empathetic conditions beat None on encouragement and appreciation. [→ Li Siyan 2024](#li-siyan-2024)

## Evidence

### Li Siyan 2024

Li Siyan, Teresa Shao, Zhou Yu, Julia Hirschberg. (2024). EDEN: Empathetic Dialogues for English learning. arXiv:2406.17982v2 [cs.CL]. [doi:10.18653/v1/2024.findings-emnlp.200](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.findings-emnlp.200)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Preliminary IRB-approved user study with 31 native Mandarin speakers (17 None, 6 Fixed, 8 Adaptive after reassignment) conversing with EDEN. Post-survey PAS averaged 4.16 for Adaptive, 3.33 for Fixed, 3.78 for None; "Adaptive outperforms Fixed for all PAS-related metrics." No significance tests or effect sizes are printed.

> "Adaptive outperforms Fixed for all PAS-related metrics. This is expected, as a generic phrase is unlikely to elicit as much perceived empathy as a tailored, adaptive piece of feedback."
