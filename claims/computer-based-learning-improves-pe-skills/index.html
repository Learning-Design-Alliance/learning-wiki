Evidence¶

Moßhammer D 2017¶

Moßhammer D, Graf J, Joos S, Hertkorn R. (2017). Physical examination in undergraduate medical education in the field of general practice – a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1074-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports that in studies of computer and internet-based learning programs for clinical examination skills, "students were scored higher in exams" including OSCE "after using computer based learning programs compared to students without IT support".

"students were scored higher in exams (knowledge based and skill based assessment, p.e. OSCE) after using computer based learning programs compared to students without IT support [25, 28–30]."
