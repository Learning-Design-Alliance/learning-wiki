## Evidence

### Abhishek Kulkarni and Sharon Lynn Chu 2026

Abhishek Kulkarni and Sharon Lynn Chu. (2026). Designing AI Tutors for Interest-Based Learning: Insights from Human Instructors. [doi:10.1109/icalt68022.2026.00015](https://doi.org/10.1109/icalt68022.2026.00015)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From tutor post-design interviews and reflections in the qualitative study: with low hobby knowledge, examples could "feel forced and potentially distracting", and one tutor uncertain about football worried about coming up with the best examples.

> "Designing scenarios was harder when the interest and topic had limited conceptual overlap. In such cases, tutors broadened the interest; reflecting on a Maslow’s lesson tied to painting, one tutor noted,“I’m not really experienced with painting, that’s why I had to make it more broad"
