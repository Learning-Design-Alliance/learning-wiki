Evidence¶

Katherine L. Jones 2022¶

Katherine L. Jones, Mei Zhou and Dhanisha J. Jhaveri. (2022). Dissecting the role of adult hippocampal neurogenesis towards resilience versus susceptibility to stress-related mood disorders. npj Science of Learning 7:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00133-y

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative review summarising rodent studies across stress paradigms (chronic unpredictable mild stress, social defeat, restraint, corticosterone administration). The review reports that decreases are common but that "studies that show no effects of stress on hippocampal cell proliferation" exist, with some reporting increases.

"However, there have also been studies that show no effects of stress on hippocampal cell proliferation 48–51, with some even reporting an increase in hippocampal neurogenesis following stress52–54."
