### Student Growth through Design-Centered Learning: Report from the Learning Studios Pilot 2017

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student survey indicators of engagement and persistence (working beyond what was required, trying problems in multiple ways, persisting, curiosity, questioning) in the pilot. Survey, focus group and interview evidence suggested engagement and persistence were positively impacted. Descriptive percentages only; no effect size.

> "On average, 75 percent of students in all grades reported experiencing these markers during their time in the Learning Studio; among high school students, that average was 82 percent."
