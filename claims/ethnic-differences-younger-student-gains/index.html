Evidence¶

M. Danish Shakeel · Paul E. Peterson 2022¶

M. Danish Shakeel · Paul E. Peterson. (2022). A Half Century of Progress in US Student Achievement: Agency and Flynn Effects, Ethnic and SES Differences. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09657-y

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The article reports subgroup medians of change per decade by ethnicity (Tables 2 and 3), stating "Young white students progress more slowly" than the three other groups; except for Asians, these differences attenuate when students are tested at an older age.

"Young white students progress more slowly (m: 0.28sd, rd: 0.09sd) than Asian (m: 46sd rd: 0.28sd), black (m: 0.36sd, rd: 0.19sd), and Hispanic (m: 0.29sd, rd: 0.13sd) students."
