## Evidence

### Matteo Luca Ruggiero 2021

Matteo Luca Ruggiero, Sara Mattiello, Matteo Leone. (2021). Physics for the masses: teaching Einsteinian gravity in primary school. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac1ca3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac1ca3)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire study of 58 pupils aged 10–11 in three Turin primary classes; pre-questionnaire two weeks before and final questionnaire one month after a 15-hour Einsteinian gravity intervention. The authors report the shift from everyday-effect descriptions to interaction/deformation descriptions, noting "the concept of deformation is often implicit".

> "contrary to the pre-intervention answers, where 63% described gravity in terms of eﬀects on everyday life, in the post-intervention questionnaire students refer to gravity as an interaction or a force between bodies"
