## Evidence

### Zapata-Martínez I 2025

Zapata-Martínez I, Rius-Diaz F, Lorenzo-Álvarez R, De la Peña-Fernández L. (2025). Radiation Oncology Active Learning in Undergraduate Medical Education: The Usefulness of Kahoot and TikTok. Journal of Cancer Education 40(6):847–853. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-025-02583-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-cohort comparison in the 2022/23 experimental group (151 exam takers): 108 Kahoot participants vs 43 non-participants, means 7.47 ± 1.24 vs 6.93 ± 1.41, compared with Student's t-test, "scoring an average of 0.54 points higher" (p=0.021). No effect size printed.

> "108 took at least one Kahoot, scoring an average of 0.54 points higher on the exam than those who did not participate (7.47 points vs. 6.93 points, p = 0.021)"
