q2 · i? · associational · r3

Descriptive participation rates by economic indicators (Figure 3). The report adds that free or reduced-price lunch eligible students participated at 29% vs. 15%, Medicaid-qualified at 30% vs. 19%, and high-hardship community area residents at 32% vs. 21%; all remained statistically significant in multilevel models.

"the participation rate among students living in temporary situations was double that of students not living in temporary situations (57% vs. 27%)"
