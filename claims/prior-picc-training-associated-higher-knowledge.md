## Evidence

### Xu Binbin 2020

Xu Binbin, Zhang Jinghui, Hou Jianmei, Ma Mengdan, Gong Zhihong, Tang Siyuan. (2020). Nurses' knowledge of peripherally inserted central catheter maintenance and its influencing factors in Hunan province, China: a cross-sectional survey. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033804

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon rank-sum comparison in the 4110-nurse cross-sectional survey (Z=−17.850, p<0.001 in Table 4); trained nurses scored 77.14±13.73 versus 69.41±14.84 for untrained nurses. The authors conclude training is an effective way to enhance knowledge.

> "The difference in the knowledge score between nurses who had received PICC maintenance training previously and those who had not obtained training was statistically significant (p<0.01)."
