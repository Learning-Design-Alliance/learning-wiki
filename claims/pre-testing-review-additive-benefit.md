`q3 i?` Reviewing notes for 10 minutes just before testing raised accuracy from 71.7% to 77.4% averaged across conditions. [→ Hannon 2013](#hannon-2013)

## Evidence

### Hannon 2013

Brenda Hannon. (2013). The Influences of Pre-testing Reviews and Delays on Differential-associative Processing versus Conditions in which Students chose their Learning Strategy. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v1i2.225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-subjects main effect of pre-testing review in the same ANOVA; the article reports "F (1, 207) = 20.98" with "η2= .02", a small effect by its own Cohen-based guidelines, and no d for this effect.

> "There was also a main effect for review, F (1, 207) = 20.98, MSE = 65.79, η2= .02 such that more questions were answered in the review condition than in the no review condition, 77.4% versus 71.7% respectively"
