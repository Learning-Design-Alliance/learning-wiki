## Evidence

### Ding Chun 2019

Ding Chun, Li Shengguo, Chen Baihua. (2019). Effectiveness of flipped classroom combined with team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning on ophthalmology teaching for eight-year program students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1861-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-and-after self-evaluation using the clinical thinking ability scale and independent learning ability scale, analysed by paired t-test within each group of the comparative study. The article reports the FC-TCLEBL improvements as significant and no improvement in the LBC group.

> "The students in the FC-TCLEBL group improved their clinical thinking ability (56.84375 ± 10.98124 vs. 50.375 ± 8.870938, p = 0.01188) and independent learning ability (138.5 ± 17.40967 vs. 147.3125 ± 17.32132, p = 0.046666) after class. However, the students in the LBC group had no improvement in critical thinking, clinical thinking ability, or independent learning ability after class."
