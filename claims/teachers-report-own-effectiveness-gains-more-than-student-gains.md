# Chicago teachers are far more likely to report improvements in their own work than improvements in student academic performance

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sebring 1995

Sebring, P. B., Bryk, A. S., Easton, J. Q., Luppescu, S., Thum, Y. M., Lopez, W. A., & Smith, B. (1995). Charting Reform: Chicago Teachers Take Stock. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-chicago-teachers-take-stock

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of elementary and high school teachers in 266 elementary and 46 high schools, administered May-June 1994, covering 13 recent-change items. Teachers were far more positive about their own work than about student outcomes: "Only slightly more than one-third believe that student academic performance has gotten better."

> "Only slightly more than one-third believe that student academic performance has gotten better in the past three years and nearly one-quarter say that it has gotten worse."
