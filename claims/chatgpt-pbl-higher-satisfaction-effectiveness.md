## Evidence

### Tong Xiaoya 2025

Tong Xiaoya, Hu Ying, Long Yanjun, Zhang Qian, Yang Yao, Yuan Jing, Zha Yan. (2025). The application of problem-based learning (PBL) guided by ChatGPT in clinical education in the Department of Nephrology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07427-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous Likert-scale questionnaires designed by the researchers (ten-item satisfaction, five-item effectiveness self-assessment) completed by both groups; the experimental group's "45.92 ± 2.37" satisfaction and 23.18 ± 1.31 effectiveness ratings exceeded controls, P < 0.001. No effect size printed.

> "the experimental group received significantly higher evaluations in both teaching satisfaction and self-assessment of teaching effectiveness after the training (45.92 ± 2.37 vs 43.77 ± 1.53; 23.18 ± 1.31 vs 21.31 ± 1.54, P < 0.001)"
