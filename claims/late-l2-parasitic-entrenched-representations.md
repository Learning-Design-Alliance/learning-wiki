### Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong 2020

Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong. (2020). The social brain of language: grounding second language learning in social interaction. npj Science of Learning 5:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0068-7

`q1 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review reports computational models of bilingual lexical development that systematically manipulated the timing of adding L2 items to L1 structure during simultaneous or sequential learning. The models showed the printed AoA sensitivity; the review attributes parasitism to entrenchment of the early-established L1 lexical structure.

> "the L2 lexical organization is sensitive to AoA: the later L2 is learned, the less well organized and more fragmented the L2 representations are"
