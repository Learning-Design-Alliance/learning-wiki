`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the cross-sectional study; both birth-weight categories were significant (p = 0.048 and p = 0.024) with printed adjusted odds ratios OR: 0.34 and OR: 0.24.

> "Birth weights between 2.5 and 3.9 kg (OR: 0.34; 95% CI: 0.11–0.99) and ≥ 4.0 kg (OR: 0.24; 95% CI: 0.07–0.83) were also associated with reduced odds of good performance when compared with children weighing < 2.5 kg at birth."
