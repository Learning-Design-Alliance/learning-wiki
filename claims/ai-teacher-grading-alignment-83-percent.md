`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Visual analysis of isolated discrepancies between AI and teacher grades for Attempt I (Figure 4). One exception involved an AI higher grade traced by the teacher to an error from ambiguous learning materials, which the authors cite as showing the importance of human oversight.

> "The majority of cases show higher teacher grades (in red) and lower AI grades (in blue), suggesting a conservative tendency in the AI's evaluation algorithm."
