`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-subjects Hotelling T-squared tests of paired emotion differences (joy, confusion, frustration) across three action types in the CRYSTAL ISLAND study. Only the after-scan comparison was significant: "After Scan 59 9.61 (0.028)*", while during-book (p = 0.13) and after-submission (p = 0.12) tests were not.
