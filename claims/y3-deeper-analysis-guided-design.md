## Evidence

### Debra Bernstein 2022

Debra Bernstein, Gillian Puttick, Kristen Wendell, Fayette Shaw, Ethan Danahy, Michael Cassidy. (2022). Designing biomimetic robots: iterative development of an integrated technology design curriculum. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10061-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Y3 case study: the focal group sketched internal skeletal and muscular structures, articulated how structure position and movement contribute to digging, and produced a final robot mimicking the mole's structure and 'swimming' motion.

> "Using the revised Y3 version of the curriculum, students were able to conduct a more in-depth animal structure–function analysis and this helped guide their robot design."

### Debra Bernstein 2022 (2)

Debra Bernstein, Gillian Puttick, Kristen Wendell, Fayette Shaw, Ethan Danahy, Michael Cassidy. (2022). Designing biomimetic robots: iterative development of an integrated technology design curriculum. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10061-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The authors' interpretation attributes the Y3 group's success to thorough decomposition and abstraction of both external and internal animal structure, supported by vector arrows, internal-feature sketches, and comparative-anatomy conversations.

> "We believe that the additional focus on internal and external animal structure resulted in a robot that mimicked both animals structure and function because decomposition and abstraction of both external and internal animal structure was so thoroughly accomplished."
