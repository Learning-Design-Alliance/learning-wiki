### Zile Inta 2021 (2)

Zile Inta, Bite Ieva, Krumina Indra, Folkmanis Valdis, Tzivian Lilian. (2021). Association between Anxiety, Quality of Life and Academic Performance of the Final-Year-Students in Latvia. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18115784

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

McNemar comparison of the proportion of highly anxious students between time points; 170 of 228 students whose status was tracked (74.6%) did not change category between baseline and follow-up. No effect size printed.

> "Most of the children were not highly anxious either at baseline or follow-up (69.0% and 68.6%, respectively), without statistically significant difference between time points."
