Manav Esra Yildirim, Akbiyik Sema Yazici, Ceylan Aydan Büşra, Çakiroğlu Ayfer Ezgi Yilmaz, Tuncer Duygu. (2025). Effects of virtual reality and layered tooth model training on manual dexterity in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07622-9

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

This is the authors' interpretation offered in the Discussion, not a tested result; they cite simulation-based education literature reporting that increased cognitive load negatively impacted skill acquisition and transfer, and note the effect is especially relevant in compressed formats.

> "According to Cognitive Load Theory, exposing novice learners to multiple complex training modalities—such as VR and layered models—simultaneously may exceed their working memory capacity, thereby impairing learning efficiency."
