### Winch 1912

Winch, W. H. (1912). Mental fatigue in day school children as measured by immediate memory: Part I. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 3*(1), 18–28. [doi:10.1037/h0070728](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070728)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Gross weekly comparison of Groups A (morning) and B (afternoon) from Table II. Improvement by practice was still obvious in both groups, but "Group A is improving faster than Group B", and the difference is taken as a measure of the difference in mental energy between 9.45 a.m. and 4 p.m.

> "in the first week the morning group improved on its record in the preliminary tests-to the extent of 6.2fi per cent., calculated as a percentage on the average of the four preliminary tests; in the second week 11.3 per cent., and in the third week 14.9 per cent.; whilst the afternoon group improved on its pre- liminary record in the first week 2.2 per cent."
