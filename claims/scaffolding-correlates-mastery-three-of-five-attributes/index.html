Evidence¶

Jiaqi Yu 2022¶

Jiaqi Yu, Wenchao Ma, Jewoong Moon, Andre R. Denham. (2022). Developing a Stealth Assessment System Using a Continuous Conjunctive Model. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3), 11–31. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7639

q3 · i3 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis of 84 participants' scaffold-use counts and CCM-estimated mastery levels, with 999-bootstrap bias-corrected accelerated confidence intervals. Correlations were "significantly correlated with Attributes 1, 3, and 4" — r = 0.468, 0.537, 0.425 as printed in Table 8.

"the use of scaffolding is significantly correlated with Attributes 1, 3, and 4 at a .05 nominal level, but not significantly related with attributes 2 and 5."
