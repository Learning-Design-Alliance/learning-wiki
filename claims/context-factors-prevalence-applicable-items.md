## Evidence

### Essers Geurt 2013

Essers Geurt, Kramer Anneke, Andriesse Boukje, van Weel Chris, van der Vleuten Cees, van Dulmen Sandra. (2013). Context factors in general practitioner - patient encounters and their impact on assessing communication skills - an exploratory study. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-14-65

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational analysis of the 40-consultation sample found "the mean number of CFs per consultation was 6.5 (range 4 – 12)". As a consequence, the number of applicable items per consultation decreased from 12.2 to 11.8 (sub-items from 40.6 to 37.7).

> "In 38 out of 40 consultations, CFs prompted the application of the context-specific rating protocol. In two consultations there was no CF present that required deviating from the MAAS-Global Manual. The mean number of CFs per consultation was 6.5 (range 4 – 12)."
