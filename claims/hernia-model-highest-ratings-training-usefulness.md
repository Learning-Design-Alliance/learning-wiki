de Barros PHF, Borges CS, Ferreira CPdC, Hernani BL, Abreu IPN, Tastaldi L, Cavazzola LT. (2024). Hernia 3D training model: a new inguinal hernia 3D-printed simulator. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2024AO0620

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Results of the post-training Likert questionnaire (Table 2) completed by 28 surgeons after performing a simulated TAPP procedure. Training-usefulness items received medians of 5 (4–5), higher than items on realism and ease of use, which had medians of 4.

> "When asked about the model’s potential impact on education and training in inguinal herniorrhaphy, the surgeons completely agreed (with a median score of 5). Complete agreement (with a median score of 5) was also observed for resident training before entering the operating room."
