`q2 i?` A majority of interviewed peer teachers reported receiving no formal training in pedagogy or teaching methods, nor subject-specific training, prior to their peer teaching assignment. [→ George SS 2025](#george-ss-2025)

## Evidence

### George SS 2025

George SS, Bonnevier A, Stenfors T, Kenne E. (2025). Learning to teach by teaching your peers: exploring students’ needs for training in the undergraduate medical education curriculum. BMC Medical Education 25(1):414. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07022-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of focus groups and individual interviews with 16 peer teachers and 9 peer learners; the Results section reports that most PTs said they "had not received any formal training in pedagogy or teaching methods". Introductory information meetings were not perceived as training.

> "it was evident that a majority experienced that they had not received any formal training in pedagogy or teaching methods, nor any subject specific training prior to their PT-assignment."
