`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary-outcome comparison of BOSATS scores in the same randomized-controlled trial (n = 60). Initial BOSATS scores were comparable between groups; the printed means and p-values above show the pair-trained group significantly better at three of six repetitions. No standardized effect size printed.

> "However, the intervention group performed better than the control group in the second, fourth, and fifth MIS RYGB (91.1 ± 6.4 vs. 87.1 ± 7.0 points, p = 0.025; 104.0 ± 4.7 vs. 100.3 ± 6.1 points, p = 0.014; 106.2 ± 2.8 vs. 101.9 ± 5.8 points, p = 0.026), respectively."
