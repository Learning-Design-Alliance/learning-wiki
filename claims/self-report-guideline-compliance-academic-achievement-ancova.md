## Evidence

### Watson Amanda 2022

Watson Amanda, Dumuid Dorothea, Maher Carol, Olds Tim. (2022). Associations between meeting 24-hour movement guidelines and academic achievement in Australian primary school-aged children. Journal of Sport and Health Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2020.12.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional ANCOVA in the CheckPoint sample (n = 1270, mean age 11.99 years) comparing NAPLAN scores across all eight guideline-compliance combinations, adjusted for age, sex, SEP, and pubertal development. The article reports that achievement "significantly differed based on guideline compliance" for both domains; no effect size is printed for the omnibus tests.

> "When assessed based on self-reported movement behaviors, academic achievement in both literacy (F(7, 1258) = 3.08, p = 0.003) and numeracy (F(7, 1258) = 2.92, p = 0.005) significantly differed based on guideline compliance."
