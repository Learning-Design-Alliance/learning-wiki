### Farokhzadian Jamileh 2025

Farokhzadian Jamileh, Sabzi Amirreza, Farmitani Zakieh. (2025). A comparative study of nurses’ competencies in integrating religion/spirituality into patient care. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12613-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVAs and independent t-tests on the 200-nurse sample (Table 3) compared RSIPAS scores across sociodemographic groups; the listed factors reached p < 0.05. No effect sizes were printed.

> "The results indicated a significant difference in nurses’ competencies in integrating religion/spirituality based on factors such as age, work experience, degree of religiosity and spirituality, and the utilization of professionals for providing religious/spiritual care (p < 0.05)"
