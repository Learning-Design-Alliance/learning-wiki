`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear regression predicting CPR knowledge (0–11, n = 500). Witnessed/used AED versus not carried "adjusted B 0.63 (95% CI 0.10 to 1.15)" (p = 0.019); the MI model independently showed B 0.88 (95% CI 0.37 to 1.40) for the same predictor.

> "Prior AED exposure was also associated with higher CPR knowledge (adjusted B 0.63 (95% CI 0.10 to 1.15))."
