# Community-oriented PBL with real-patient videos: medical students' comprehension test scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in one uncontrolled pre-post study (r=0.528)

## Evidence

### Kiyoshi Shikino 2025

Kiyoshi Shikino, Kazuyo Yamauchi, Nobuyuki Araki, Ikuo Shimizu, Hajime Kasai, Tomoko Tsukamoto, Hiroshi Tajima, Yu Li, Misaki Onodera, Shoichi Ito. (2025). Understanding Community Health Care Through Problem-Based Learning With Real-Patient Videos: Single-Arm Pre-Post Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/68743

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comprehension test (5 multiple-choice questions on holistic medicine, patient-centered care, and ICF) administered before core time 1 and after core time 2. The article reports the posttest "median of 5 (IQR 4-5)" with "effect size r=0.528" (Wilcoxon signed rank test).

> "The total comprehension test scores of the students significantly improved after the community-oriented PBL intervention (P<.001, effect size r=0.528). The total pretest scores had a median of 4.0 (IQR 2.5-5.0), whereas the posttest scores improved to a median of 5 (IQR 4-5)."
