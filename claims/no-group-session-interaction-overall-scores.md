Thai NP, Hoang HT, Vu CTB, Nguyen ATN, Doan TM. (2026). Improvement of learner’s preclinical skill in occlusal rest seat preparation using digital and conventional training. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08402-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the linear mixed-effects model of 80 students' overall scores, the interaction between Group and Session was non-significant: "the improvement did not differ significantly between the two groups (p = 0.611)". The interaction fixed effect was negligible (Cohen's f = 0.06).
