# Class-level instructional quality (classroom management, cognitive activation, supportive climate) is positively related to student self-efficacy

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In classes where students rate their teacher higher on any of the three instructional quality dimensions, students report higher self-efficacy. [→ Burić 2020](#buric-2020)

## Evidence

### Burić 2020

Burić, Irena and Kim, Lisa (2020) Teacher self-efficacy, instructional quality, and student motivational beliefs: An analysis using multilevel structural equation modeling. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.101302

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Climate-effect analysis using doubly latent ML-SEM: aggregated student ratings of instructional quality predicted aggregated student self-efficacy, with standardized βs of .361, .312, and .384 across the three dimensions. All three dimensions were similarly relevant.

> "in classes of teachers with higher levels on the three instructional quality domains (i.e., classroom management, cognitive activation, and supportive climate), the students reported higher levels of SSE (β = .361, p = .001; β = .312, p = .016; and β = .384, p = .003, respectively)"
