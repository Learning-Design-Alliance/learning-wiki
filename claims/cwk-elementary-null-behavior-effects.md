# Connect with Kids shows no statistically significant effects on elementary school students' assessments of their own or classmates' behavior

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Elementary school student survey parts 1 and 2 showed no statistically significant differences between intervention and comparison groups (effect sizes 0.27 and 0.25, both ns). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2006](#what-works-clearinghouse-2006)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2006

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Connect with Kids. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/104

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of grades 3–5 in 24 classrooms. WWC table shows effect sizes of 0.27 and 0.25, both marked ns, with improvement indices of +10 for each elementary survey part; equivalence was not tested.

> "The study found no statistically significant effects on elementary school students’ assessments of their own or their classmates’ behavior (elementary school student survey part I and part II)."
