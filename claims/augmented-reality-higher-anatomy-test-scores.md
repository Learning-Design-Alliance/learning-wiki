## Evidence

### Triepels CPR 2019

Triepels CPR, Smeets CFA, Notten KJB, Kruitwagen RFPM, Futterer JJ, Vergeldt TFM, Van Kuijk SMJ. (2019). Does three‐dimensional anatomy improve student understanding? Three‐Dimensional Methods for Learning Anatomy. Clinical Anatomy 32:25–33. https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.23405

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Kucuk et al. (2016), that mobile augmented reality produced significantly higher test scores than 2D pictures, graphs, and text; only two AR studies were included, so support is limited.

> "the other study found that test scores of students who used mobile augmented reality were statistically significantly higher than those who used 2D pictures, graphs, and text (P < 0.05; Kucuk et al., 2016)."
