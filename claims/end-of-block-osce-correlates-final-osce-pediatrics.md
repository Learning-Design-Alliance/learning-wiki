# End-of-block OSCE performance is positively correlated with final OSCE performance in pediatrics clerks (r = 0.508)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across 286 students over three academic years, end-of-block OSCE scores correlate positively with final OSCE scores, r = 0.508, p < 0.001. [→ Al Rushood Maysoun 2020](#al-rushood-maysoun-2020)

## Evidence

### Al Rushood Maysoun 2020

Al Rushood Maysoun, Al-Eisa Amal. (2020). Factors predicting students’ performance in the final pediatrics OSCE. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0236484

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of score records from three consecutive classes (n = 286) using Spearman's rho. The end-of-block OSCE and final OSCE correlation was "a correlation coefficient r of 0.508" (r = 0.508), with per-year coefficients of 0.539, 0.434, and 0.536 (p < 0.001).

> "the results showed that there is a positive correlation between the performances on the end-of-block OSCE and the final OSCE with a correlation coefficient r of 0.508"
