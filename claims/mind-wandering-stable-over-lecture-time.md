## Evidence

### Wammes J.D. 2019

Wammes J.D., Ralph B.C.W., Mills C., Bosch N., Duncan T.L. & Smilek D. (2019). Disengagement during lectures: Media multitasking and mind wandering in university classrooms. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.12.007

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In Study 2, a repeated-measures ANOVA on mind wandering reports found "no evidence for a linear trend" across lecture quarters (p = .21), replicating prior live-lecture findings of stability over time.

> "While there was also a significant main effect of Lecture Quarter in the p roportion of mind wandering 3 reports, F(2.857, 197.132) = 3.60, MSE = .01, p < .001, ηp 2 =.05, there was no evidence for a linear trend, F(1, 69) = 1.63, MSE = .01, p = .21, ηp 2 =.02, and the only significant increase was from the first to the second quarter, t(69) = 3.44, SE = .01, d = 0.42, p = 0.001."
