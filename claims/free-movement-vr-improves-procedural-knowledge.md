### Medenitzki R 2026

Medenitzki R, Dubovi I. (2026). Bodily Engagement in Virtual Reality Nursing Simulation: Implications for Students' Declarative and Procedural Learning. Nurse Educator. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000002271

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study of 72 first-year nursing students found "higher procedural knowledge scores" in free-movement VR, while "declarative knowledge, cognitive load, and cybersickness did not differ significantly."

> "Free-movement students showed greater task-relevant movement and higher procedural knowledge scores. Declarative knowledge, cognitive load, and cybersickness did not differ significantly."
