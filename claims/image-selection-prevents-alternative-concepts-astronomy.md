Theoretical assertion in the article's introduction, attributed by the authors to Pena & Gil Quilez (2001). The article offers no new empirical data for it; it states that a growing number of images "are not always true" aids to learning and may "promote the development of alternative concepts".

> "In education the ideal role of images should be helping students form better mental representations and abstractions of the studied subject. To assure this goal and minimize undesirable alternatives concepts, the selection of appropriate images alongside detailed well written descriptions are crucial"
