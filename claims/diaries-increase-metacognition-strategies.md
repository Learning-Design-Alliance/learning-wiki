## Evidence

### Arsal 2010

Arsal, Z. (2010). The effects of diaries on self-regulation strategies of preservice science teachers. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 5(1), 85-103. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ884413

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A pre-/post-questionnaire control group study with 60 pre-service science teachers found that the experimental group, which kept diaries for fourteen weeks, scored higher on metacognition than the control group, with "significant difference between the experimental group and control group in terms of metacognition".

> "the post-questionnaire results showed that there was significant difference between the experimental group and control group in terms of metacognition (t=2.17, p=.034)"
