## Evidence

### Marco Lionel Di 2017

Marco Lionel Di, Venot Alain, Gillois Pierre. (2017). Does the acceptance of hybrid learning affect learning approaches in France?. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2017.14.24

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of the full year-2 midwifery class (n=38, 68.4% participation) using the validated French R-SPQ-2F. The article reports "higher scores for the deep approach than for the superficial approach" on strategy, motivations, and both, with the printed P-values; no effect size is printed, so no magnitude is claimed.

> "the mean scores on the R-SPQ-2F survey showed that our population had higher scores for the deep approach than for the superficial approach (maximum score, 25) concerning strategy (P< 0.05), motivations (P< 0.001), or both (P< 0.001)"
