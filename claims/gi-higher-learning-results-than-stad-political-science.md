# Students taught Basics of Political Science with the GI cooperative learning model achieved higher learning results than students taught with STAD

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Learning results in Basics of Political Science were higher for students taught with the GI cooperative learning model (mean 31.61) than for students taught with STAD (mean 29.28), F = 14.27 > F table = 3.98 at α = 0.05. [→ Sugiharto 2015](#sugiharto-2015)

## Evidence

### Sugiharto 2015

Sugiharto. (2015). The Effect of Cooperative Learning Model and Kolb Learning Styles on Learning Result of The Basics of Politics. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.21. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079121

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental 2x2 treatment-by-level study of 78 first-semester students at State University of Medan; class A (37 students) taught with GI, class C (41 students) with STAD. ANOVA gave "F calculate = 14.27 > F table = 3.98 with α = 0.05", so H0 was rejected and the GI advantage accepted.

> "Looking at the average of the learning results of the basics of political science students treated by  GI type (x ̅ = 31.61) was higher than the average learning outcomes treated by type of STAD (x ̅ = 29.28), it can be concluded that learning outcomes of students who learned with GI cooperative learning model is higher than that learned through type of STAD."
