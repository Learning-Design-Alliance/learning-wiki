## Evidence

### Dahlberg Johanna 2018

Dahlberg Johanna, Nelson Marie, Dahlgren Madeleine Abrandt, Blomberg Marie. (2018). Ten years of simulation-based shoulder dystocia training- impact on obstetric outcome, clinical management, staff confidence, and the pedagogical practice - a time series study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-018-2001-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective time series comparing 11, 20 and 29 shoulder dystocia deliveries across pre-, early and late post-training periods (2004-2015). Table 4 prints 8/11 vs 5/29 injuries, p = 0.001, and the authors call it "a significant reduction".

> "The rate of brachial plexus injuries in deliveries complicated with shoulder dystocia was 73% prePROBE compared to 17% in the late postPROBE group (Table 4), which was a significant reduction."
