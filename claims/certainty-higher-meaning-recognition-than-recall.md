# L2 learners report higher certainty for meaning recognition than for meaning recall or form recognition of newly encountered words

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` L2 participants' certainty for meaning recognition was significantly higher than for meaning recall and for form recognition. [→ Pellicer-Sánchez 2016](#pellicer-sanchez-2016)

## Evidence

### Pellicer-Sánchez 2016

Pellicer-Sánchez, Ana. (2016). Incidental L2 Vocabulary Acquisition From and While Reading: An Eye-Tracking Study. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, 38(1), 97-130. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263115000224

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Friedman test with Wilcoxon signed-rank post hoc comparisons (Bonferroni-adjusted alpha p < .017) on certainty ratings (1 = very uncertain, 4 = very certain) from 23 L2 participants across the three vocabulary tests; χ2(2) = 10.92, p = .004.

> "the difference between L2 participants’ certainty for meaning recognition was significantly higher than for meaning recall, Z = –3.45, p < .001, and for form recognition, Z = –3.48; p < .001"
