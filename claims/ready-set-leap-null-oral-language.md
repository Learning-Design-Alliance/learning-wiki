# Ready, Set, Leap!® has no significant effect on preschool oral language (domain average effect size 0.02)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2008

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Ready, Set, Leap!®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/430

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Findings from two RCTs in the oral language domain. The appendix table prints a domain average effect size of 0.02 (ns) across studies, with improvement index +1 percentile points. PCER also used the TOLD-P:3 Grammatic Understanding subtest (effect size –0.11, ns).

> "RMC Research Corporation (2003) reported no significant effect of Ready, Set, Leap!® on oral language as measured by the Peabody Picture Vocabulary Test-III (PPVT-III)."
