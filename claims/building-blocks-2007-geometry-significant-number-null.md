## Evidence

### SRA Real Math Building Blocks PreK 2007

SRA Real Math Building Blocks PreK. (2007). WWC Intervention Report. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.gse.buffalo.edu/org/buildingblocks/

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of four preschool teachers (53-61 children) from low-income families in New York State over 25 weeks. Appendix A3 reports Geometry means of 15.75 versus 10.64, effect size 1.40, statistically significant, improvement index +42.

> "Clements and Sarama (2007) found a statistically significant difference favoring children in the Building Blocks for Math group on one of the two outcome measures assessed in this domain (Building Blocks Assessment of Early Mathematics, PreK–K: Geometry section) and this effect was confirmed to be statistically significant by the WWC."
