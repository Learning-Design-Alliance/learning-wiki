## Evidence

### Mansouri Dana DH 2026

Mansouri Dana DH, Samuel T, Moise AC, Thamboo A. (2026). Effectiveness of a near-peer teaching model in an otolaryngology bootcamp for medical students: a pilot study. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.000126

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey of the same 16 participants using a 4-point Likert scale; paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. Cricothyrotomy confidence "increased from 1/4 to 3/4"; epistaxis (p = 0.003) and nasolaryngoscopy (p = 0.001) also improved. No standardized effect size printed.

> "Self-reported confidence for performing cricothyrotomy techniques increased from 1/4 to 3/4 (p = 0.0009; median change 2, 95% CI 1 to 2) (Figure 4)."
