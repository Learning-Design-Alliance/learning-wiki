# In catch trials, large numbers elicit greater right-hemisphere P300 positivity than small numbers

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A number x hemisphere interaction in the P300 band showed large numbers elicited greater positivity than small numbers in the right hemisphere, though simple effects were not significant. [→ Salillas E 2009](#salillas-e-2009)

## Evidence

### Salillas E 2009

Salillas E, Grana´ A, El-Yagoubi R, Semenza C (2009) Numbers in the Blind's ''Eye''. PLoS ONE 4(7): e6357. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0006357

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Catch-trial analysis (pink noise in both channels, no target) in the same ERP experiment with 7 blind and 7 sighted participants. The interaction "Large numbers elicited a greater positivity than small numbers in the right hemisphere", but the article states the simple effects for this interaction did not reach significance. No effect size is printed.

> "In the latency band of the P300 (300–420 ms.) a number 6 hemisphere interaction was found in the lateral electrodes (F(1,12) = 17.9, p = 0.001). Large numbers elicited a greater positivity than small numbers in the right hemisphere."
