## Evidence

### S. B. McKagan 2008

S. B. McKagan, K. K. Perkins, and C. E. Wieman. (2008). A Deeper Look at Student Learning of Quantum Mechanics: the Case of Tunneling. https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.3194 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0802.3194](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0802.3194)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Administration of the Figure 6 question in transformed engineering courses across formats (Table II). Scores rose from 37-41% on the QMCS to "58% answered correctly" as an essay and 90% after in-class review. The article attributes the discrepancy to students taking exams more seriously and explaining reasoning resolving confusion.

> "However, when we gave it instead as an essay question on the ﬁnal exam, asking students to “explain your reasoning,” 58% answered correctly. When we gave it as a multiple choice question on a ﬁnal exam after asking it on the QMCS and reviewing it in class, so that students were already familiar with it, 90% answered correctly."
