Evidence¶

Lai 2016¶

Lai, C. (2016). Third graders' understanding of air concepts facilitated by the iPod inquiry teaching method. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 2(1), 1-9. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105183

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Narrative literature review attributing these misconceptions to Lin (2006) for Taiwanese primary students and to Akbaş and Gençtürk (2011) for atmospheric-pressure misconceptions; the reviewed studies' designs are not described in this article. The article also reports Chang (2000) found the hardest air concept is that air expands when heated and contracts when cooled.
