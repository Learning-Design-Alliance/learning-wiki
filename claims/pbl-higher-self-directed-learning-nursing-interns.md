### Xue Huarong 2025

Xue, H., Lu, Y., & Liu, L. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning in enhancing health education skills, self-directed learning, and critical thinking among nursing interns: a prospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07936-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`
