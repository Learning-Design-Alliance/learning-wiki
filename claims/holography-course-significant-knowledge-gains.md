## Evidence

### Martin Erik Horn 2004

Martin Erik Horn, Antje Leisner, Helmut F. Mikelskis. (2004). Difficulties of Model Construction in Optics. German Physical Society Spring Conference Leipzig 2002. Originally published 2002. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0402080](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0402080)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r?`

Study overview of the holography dissertation project with 54 grade-13 students at five Brandenburg Gymnasium schools, evaluated with pre-, post- and long-term tests, concept maps, videotaping and interviews. The authors report "highly significant effects of comprehension" also in geometric optics and wave phenomena, though smaller.

> "The analysis of the results (first objective) demonstrated that the knowledge of the group of students concerning the basics of holography increased considerably. As it is assumed that the students have a low familiarity with the subject, this increase is highly significant and demonstrates a successful learning process."
