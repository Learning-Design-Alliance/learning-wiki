Evidence¶

Noetel 2021¶

Noetel, M., Griffith, S., Delaney, O., Sanders, T., Parker, P., del Pozo Cruz, B., & Lonsdale, C. (2021). Video Improves Learning in Higher Education: A Systematic Review. Review of Educational Research, 91(2), 204–236. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654321990713

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Publication-bias assessment via funnel plots and a three-parameter selection model across all included effect sizes. The review reports the bias pattern was significant, but "a strong publication bias would be required to nullify these effects" (10.48× for swapping; none plausible for supplemental video).

"The 3PSM likelihood ratio test indicated a pattern of effect sizes consistent with publication bias (χ2(7) = 35.50, p < 0.001), such that affirmative studies appeared more likely to be published."
