### Huang and Hsu 2011

Chin-Wen Huang, Chun-Pin Hsu. (2011). Using Online Games To Teach Personal Finance Concepts. American Journal of Business Education – December 2011 Volume 4, Number 12. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1056617

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' observational report from the game-based session, not a tested result. It illustrates the repeated-practice mechanism the authors attribute to games such as Refund Rush, where players encounter the same concept many times.

> "Some students reported difficulties answering the questions raised by the games on their first attempts. However, the students soon learned the new concepts and acquired the necessary knowledge by playing a given game repeatedly."
