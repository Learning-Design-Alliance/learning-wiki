## Evidence

### Cui Liping 2024

Cui Liping, Dong Yuting, Zhang Shan, Ma Wenxia, Li Min. (2024). Construction and application of procedural pathways combined with information management in nursing staff skills training system. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05593-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 300 experimental-group nurses (2021) against 267 control-group nurses (2020) trained traditionally in the same hospital. Monthly t-tests showed the experimental group's scores were "higher than those in the control group (P < 0.05)"; Table 3 reports means from 75.45 vs 92.18 in month 1 to 91.42 vs 93.27 in month 12 for theory.

> "the monthly theoretical and clinical nursing operation scores of nursing interns in the experimental group were higher than those in the control group (P < 0.05), and the group differences were statistically significant"
