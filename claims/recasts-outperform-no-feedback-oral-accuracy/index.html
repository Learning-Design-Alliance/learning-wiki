Evidence¶

Farahman Farrokhi 2012¶

Farahman Farrokhi, Mohammad Hassan Chehrazad. (2012). The Effects of Planned Focus on Form on Iranian EFL Learners' Oral Accuracy. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v2n1p70

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Dunnett t test comparing each experimental group against the control group across six sessions; no significant differences in sessions 1-5, and in session 6 the recast group beat the control group with "p-value = .040".

"in the session 6 the second group which received recast on the erroneous production outperformed the control group which received no feedback (p-value = .040 < =0.05)"
