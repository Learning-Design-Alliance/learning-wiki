## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 26 students constructing the matrix of S1z + S2z, about 1/4 incorrectly produced a two-dimensional matrix by adding the two subspace matrices, and more than half failed to see S1z is a 4×4 matrix in the product space. ## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving student understanding of addition of angular momentum in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06165)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Question administered to 26 students asking them to construct the matrix of S1z + S2z in a suitable basis. "about 1/4 of them incorrectly claimed that the resulting matrix is two dimensional"; three of the 26 multiplied the 2×2 subspace matrices for S1·S2, and more than half did not realize S1z is 4×4 in the product space.

> "when 26 students were asked to construct the matrix of ˆS1z + ˆS2z in a suitable basis, about 1/4 of them incorrectly claimed that the re- sulting matrix is two dimensional and they simply added up the matrices of the operators ˆS1z and ˆS2z"
