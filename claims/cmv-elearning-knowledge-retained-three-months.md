## Evidence

### Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al 2022

Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al. (2022). eLearning significantly improves maternity professionals' knowledge of the congenital cytomegalovirus prevention guidelines. Australian & New Zealand Journal of Obstetrics & Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13500

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Three-month follow-up analysis of limited available T3 data (n = 27, 7% of baseline) using the same mixed-effects model. The article reports the score "decreased between T2 and T3" yet stayed above pre-course levels.

> "the total mean score decreased between T2 and T3 (−1.71, p < 0.001); however, the mean T3 score remained significantly higher than prior to the course (20.8, +3.6, P < 0.001)"
