Sartain, L., Allensworth, E., & Porter, S. (2014). Suspending Chicago's Students: Differences in Discipline across Schools. UChicago Consortium. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/suspending-chicagos-students-differences-discipline-practices-across-schools

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Observational analysis linking Chicago discipline records with child welfare data for the first time. The report found "almost a third of the students with a history of abuse or neglect were suspended in the 2013-14 school year"; in almost all high-suspending high schools at least 10 percent of students had a documented abuse or neglect history.

> "For the first time, researchers were able to combine discipline data with child welfare data and found that almost a third of the students with a history of abuse or neglect were suspended in the 2013-14 school year."
