Narrative review synthesis of two longitudinal studies supporting cumulative models. The review reports a study from 18 months to mid-adulthood found "cumulative stress rather than physical abuse alone was predictive of adult depressive symptoms," and a birth-to-37 study found childhood stress interacted with current life stress to predict adult diurnal cortisol. No effect sizes are printed.

> "A recent longitudinal study from 18 months to mid- adulthood found that cumulative stress rather than physical abuse alone was predictive of adult depressive symptoms [ 40]. Another study, which followed children from birth to age 37 years, found that childhood stress interacted with current life stress, regardless of type of stressor, to predict diurnal cortisol patterns in adulthood"
