# In students with lower concentration scores, BALANCE showed larger gains on easy mathematics tasks than CARDIO, with MENTAL differences uncertain (exploratory subsample analysis)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the exploratory subsample with concentration scores at least 0.58 SD below the mean, BALANCE showed larger median improvement than CARDIO on the easy math section, while differences involving MENTAL had confidence intervals spanning zero. [→ Leukel Christian 2026](#leukel-christian-2026)
`q2 i?` In the same subsample, no meaningful group differences emerged for the difficult mathematics section or for accuracy. [→ Leukel Christian 2026 (2)](#leukel-christian-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Leukel Christian 2026

Leukel Christian, Lauber Benedikt, Leuders Juliane, Hertel Sven, Taube Wolfgang. (2026). Differential impact of various in-class physical exercise interventions on cognitive function and mathematics achievement in primary school children. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-45347-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory Kruskal–Wallis analysis of change scores in the post-hoc low-concentration subsample: "χ2(2) = 6.04, p = .04; ε2 = 0.11"; pairwise Hodges–Lehmann estimates showed BALANCE exceeded CARDIO (HL = 9.0, 95% CI [1.0, 17.0]), while BALANCE vs. MENTAL (HL = 0.0) and CARDIO vs. MENTAL intervals included zero.

> "the Kruskal-Wallis test indicated small-to-moderate overall group differences in change scores (χ2(2) = 6.04, p = .04; ε2 = 0.11)"

### Leukel Christian 2026 (2)

Leukel Christian, Lauber Benedikt, Leuders Juliane, Hertel Sven, Taube Wolfgang. (2026). Differential impact of various in-class physical exercise interventions on cognitive function and mathematics achievement in primary school children. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-45347-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

For the difficult math section in the low-concentration subsample, "no meaningful group differences in change scores (χ2(2) = 2.50, p = .287; ε2 = 0.05)"; the authors state no group gained on the difficult section, likely due to general difficulty mastering the material in five weeks.

> "the Kruskal-Wallis test revealed no meaningful group differences in change scores (χ2(2) = 2.50, p = .287; ε2 = 0.05)"
