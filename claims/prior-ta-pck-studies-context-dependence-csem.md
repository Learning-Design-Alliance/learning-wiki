## Evidence

### Liam Doyle 2026

Liam Doyle, Alexandru Maries, Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Exploring pedagogical content knowledge of physics teaching assistants using the Energy and Momentum Conceptual Survey. [doi:10.1103/fgqc-dfbz](https://doi.org/10.1103/fgqc-dfbz)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The article summarizes its three prior studies using the TUG-K, FCI, and CSEM, attributing these findings to those studies. The review reports that TAs' knowledge of alternate conceptions is context dependent and that TAs sometimes expect alternate conceptions to be common when they are not.

> "The TAs struggled more in identifying alternate conceptions in the context of the CSEM than in the context of the FCI or TUG-K. This may be due to the fact that many alternate conceptions in the context of mechanics come from people's experience with the real world and thus may be easier to predict than alternate conceptions with electricity and magnetism, where the concepts are more abstract."
