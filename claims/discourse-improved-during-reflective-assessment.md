### Yang 2016

Yang, Y., van Aalst, J., Chan, C.K.K., & Tian, W. (2016). Reflective assessment in knowledge building by students with low academic achievement. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-016-9239-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square test comparing note-category proportions before (weeks 6–8, 102 notes) and during (weeks 9–17, 59 notes) reflective assessment using KCA. The article reports "χ2(df =8 , N = 161) = 46.2, ϕ = .54" and calls it "a moderate to large effect."

> "The frequency distributions were significantly different for the two phases, χ2(df =8 , N = 161) = 46.2, ϕ = .54; this is a moderate to large effect."
