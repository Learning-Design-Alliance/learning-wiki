Katz 1993¶

Katz, I. R., Martinez, M. E., Sheehan, K. M., & Tatsuoka, K. K. (1993). Extending the Rule Space Model to a Semantically-Rich Domain: Diagnostic Assessment in Architecture. Educational Testing Service. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED366652 doi:10.21236/ada273789

q2 · i? · design · r2

Verbal protocol analysis of three think-aloud subjects, with categories defined from a pilot subject and inter-rater agreement of 82% on the doubly-scored portion. The authors report the model "accounted for 71% of the verbalizations"; no effect size is printed.
