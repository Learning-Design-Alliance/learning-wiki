## Evidence

### Alexandra Werth 2022

Alexandra Werth, Jessica R. Hoehn, Kristin Oliver, Michael F. J. Fox, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2022). Rapid Transition to Remote Instruction of Physics Labs During Spring 2020: Instructor Perspectives. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Closed-response survey analysis comparing PhET use across learning-goal categories; the article reports 47.5% versus 20.5%. Eleven instructors also reported using simulations beyond PhET, including SPICE, Simulink, and MultisimLive.

> "a larger percentage of courses emphasizing physics concepts as their primary learning goal after the transition to remote instruction used PhET simulations [33] (47.5%) than courses whose focus was learning lab skills during remote instruction (20.5%)."
