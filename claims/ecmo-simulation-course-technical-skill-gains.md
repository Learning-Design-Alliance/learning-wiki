## Evidence

### Brum Roberta 2015

Brum Roberta, Rajani Ronak, Gelandt Elton, Morgan Lisa, Raguseelan Nira, Butt Salman, Nelmes David, Auzinger Georg, Broughton Simon. (2015). Simulation training for extracorporeal membrane oxygenation. Annals of Cardiac Anaesthesia. https://doi.org/10.4103/0971-9784.154472

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Postcourse open-ended questionnaire responses from all 14 delegates attending three course deliveries. The authors report that "8 (57%) gained familiarity in troubleshooting an ECMO circuit"; these are self-reported perceptions, not tested performance.

> "For the technical skills, 8 (57%) gained familiarity in troubleshooting an ECMO circuit, 6 (43%) increased their familiarity with the ECMO pump and circuit and 3 (21%) improved their understanding of patient physiology and respective changes on ECMO."
