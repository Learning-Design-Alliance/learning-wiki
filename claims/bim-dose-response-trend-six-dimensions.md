Cross-sectional analysis across three BIM-frequency groups reported one-way ANOVA F values and a P for trend < 0.01 for these six dimensions; e.g. teamwork F = 25.72, information literacy F = 22.69. No standardized effect sizes were printed.

> "The scores of six dimensions like information literacy, summarization, expressive communication, self-directed learning, teamwork, and objective evaluation showed an upward trend as the number of interventions increased (P for trend < 0.05)."
