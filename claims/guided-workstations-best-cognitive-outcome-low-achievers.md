# Guided learning at workstations (G2) produced significantly higher post- and retention-test knowledge than conventional workstations (G1) and teacher-centered instruction (G3) in low achievers

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a quasi-experiment with low-achieving 8th graders, the student-centered guided workstation group scored significantly higher in post- and retention tests than both the conventional workstation group and the teacher-centered group. [→ Heyne 2012](#heyne-2012)
`q2 i?` All three instruction groups showed significant pre-to-post and pre-to-retention gains and significant forgetting, irrespective of teaching method. [→ Heyne 2012 (2)](#heyne-2012-2)

## Evidence

### Heyne 2012

Heyne, T., & Bogner, F. X. (2012). Guided Learning at Workstations about Drug Prevention with Low Achievers in Science Education. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v2n6p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 16 Bavarian classes (G1 n=72, G2 n=93, G3 n=84) taught cannabis-prevention content; Kruskal-Wallis tests differed at posttest and retention (Chi-Square(T-2) = 31.94; Chi-Square(T-3) = 18.43), and the article reports "significantly higher achievement scores for student-centered guided lessons (G2)". No effect size printed.

> "Subsequent pair-wise analyses of the three instruction groups (Mann-Whitney-U-Test) identified significantly higher achievement scores for student-centered guided lessons (G2) in post- and retention tests compared to G1 (student-centered) and G3 (teacher-centered)."

### Heyne 2012 (2)

Heyne, T., & Bogner, F. X. (2012). Guided Learning at Workstations about Drug Prevention with Low Achievers in Science Education. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v2n6p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon within-group comparisons across all three instruction groups (e.g., G2 Z = -8.389, p < .001 for T1 vs T2) showed learning and forgetting in every method; the article states students "learned and also forgot to a significant extent, irrespective of the teaching method used".

> "The in group comparison (Wilcoxon-Test) of the three instruction groups revealed significant differences between the pre- (T 1), post- (T 2) and retention test (T 3) (Table 2)."
