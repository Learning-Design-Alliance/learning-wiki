`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The author's reflective observation from editorial experience about research practice in the field: claims about learning, characterized as 'stable and persistent changes in what a person knows, believes, and can do', are rarely supported by longitudinal data, and many intervening factors complicate attribution.

> "Rarely do researchers collect data before, after, and long after to support claims about learning. Moreover, there are many factors other than an instructional design that intervene."
