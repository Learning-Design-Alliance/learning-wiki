Coppe, T., Thomas, L., Strijbos, J.-W., & Vanderlinde, R. (2026). The connection between the social network perspective and teacher professional development: A systematic literature review. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2026.100818

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis and historical analysis (citing Coppe, 2024) of how the field framed teacher networks. Table 4 shows the catalyst rationale appears in 15 studies, 11 of which conceptualize TPD as improvement of instructional practices, and its context is listed as "Only US". The review reports this was the field's earliest framing.

> "the earliest work in this field primarily framed teacher networks as a means to enable the enactment of externally introduced professional development initiatives"
