Veronika Hackl, Alexandra Elena Müller, Maximilian Sailer. (2025). The AI Literacy Heptagon: A Structured Approach to AI Literacy in Higher Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18900

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative expert-led curriculum mapping (collaborative profiling sessions) with two experts, one per program, assigning target proficiency levels per dimension. The authors report that "technical programs emphasize Technical Knowledge and Skills (TKS) and Application Proficiency (AP)"; profiles are illustrative, not measurements of student performance.

> "As expected, technical programs emphasize Technical Knowledge and Skills (TKS) and Application Proficiency (AP), while humanities-oriented programs focus more on Ethical Awareness and Reasoning (EAR) and Social Impact Understanding (SIU)."
