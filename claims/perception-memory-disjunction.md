### Kenny Yu 2024

Kenny Yu, Wolf Vanpaemel, Francis Tuerlinckx & Jonas Zaman. (2024). The representational instability in the generalization of fear learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00287-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis comparing predictive samples from individual perceptual and memory distributions, using the most recent perception before each memory task. The article reports significant individual disparities in the discrepancies between perception and perceptual memory.

> "While some individuals exhibit perceptualmemory that closely aligned with the most recent encoding of physical inputs, overall, the predictions of per- ceptual memory and perception do not align well."
