## Evidence

### Stolze Franka 2026

Stolze Franka, Lehm Manuel, Behme Daniel, Fuchs Erelle, Schwab Roland. (2026). Comparison of video-based and presentation-based self-directed instruction for learning basic neuroangiography. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10418-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-evaluation questionnaire item on detecting complications among the 10 experienced participants showed a non-significant trend (p = 0.071, VG median 2.5 vs PG median 4); the authors offer the authors' interpretation that videos omitted error management. No effect size printed.

> "However, experienced learners reported lower confidence levels in recognizing complications after video-based teaching. One possible reason for this could be the fact that the videos only focused on ideal scenarios and omitted error management, which is an important limitation of passive learning formats."
