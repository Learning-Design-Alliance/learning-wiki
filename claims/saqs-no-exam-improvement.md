## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The self-assessment questions introduced after 2022 were not followed by better exam performance in 2023; the authors could not determine whether they helped or hindered. [→ Korner 2025](#korner-2025)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Observational comparison of the 2022 and 2023 rounds, where SAQs were added only in 2023; the tutor reported the tutorial was not used in the intended way by the majority of students. No effect size or test statistic is printed.
