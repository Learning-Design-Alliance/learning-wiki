## Evidence

### Kölbel Benny 2024

Kölbel Benny, Ragnitz Julian, Schäle Kevin, Witzenhausen Moritz, Axt Steffen, Beltzer Christian. (2024). 3D vs. 2D-4 K: Performance and self-perception of laparoscopic novices in a randomized prospective teaching intervention using standard tasks and box trainers. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03515-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized parallel-group teaching intervention with 19 novices per arm performing box-trainer tasks of increasing difficulty. Repeated-measures ANOVA on completion times found "differed significantly regarding time for tasks 1 and 2" with F (1, 36) = 7.94, p = 0.008, partial η2 = 0.18.

> "The 3D and 2D intervention groups differed significantly regarding time for tasks 1 and 2, F (1, 36) = 7.94, p = 0.008, partial η2 = 0.18."
