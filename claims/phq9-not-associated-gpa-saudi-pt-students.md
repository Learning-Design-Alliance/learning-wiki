In the cross-sectional survey (N = 600), the chi-square test found no significant association between PHQ-9 severity categories and GPA; the ANOVA of PHQ-9 scores across GPA groups was also non-significant (P = .26). Mean PHQ-9 ranged from 5.5 to 11 across GPA categories.
