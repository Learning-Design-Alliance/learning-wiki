# Evidence on the policy's effects on 10th-grade geometry outcomes was inconsistent across groups and years

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Geometry GPAs and failure rates diverged between students just below and above the median in 2003, but there were no differences in 10th-grade course outcomes in 2004. [→ Takako Nomi and Elaine M. Allensworth 2008](#takako-nomi-and-elaine-m-allensworth-2008)

## Evidence

### Takako Nomi and Elaine M. Allensworth 2008

Takako Nomi and Elaine M. Allensworth. (2008). “Double-dose” Algebra as an Alternative Strategy to Remediation: Effects on Students’ Academic Outcomes. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. http://www.informaworld.com/smpp/content~db=all?content=10.1080/19345740802676739

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression discontinuity with time series applied to 10th-grade geometry grades and failure; the divergence appeared in 2003 only and did not persist in 2004. No effect size printed.

> "While geometry GPAs were unchanged and course failure increased for students just above the national median in 2003, students just below the national median showed slightly higher GPAs in 2003 and their failure rates did not increase."
