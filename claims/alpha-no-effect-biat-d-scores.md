## Evidence

### Deng Zhangyan 2022

Deng Zhangyan, Guo Jinyang, Wang Dong, Huang Tao, Chen Zuosong. (2022). Effectiveness of the world anti-doping agency's e-learning programme for anti-doping education on knowledge of, explicit and implicit attitudes towards, and likelihood of doping among Chinese college athletes and non-athletes. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13011-022-00459-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on BIAT D-scores in the 64-participant quasi-experiment; the intervention did not significantly change the behavioural implicit attitude measure (η2 = 0.01), despite neural changes in temporoparietal junction channels.

> "However, there was no significant intervention effect on the doping-related BIAT D-scores (-0.36 ± 0.50 vs. -0.42 ± 0.49, F(1, 60) = 0.66, p > 0.05, η2 = 0.01)."
