### Osama Swidan 2026

Osama Swidan. (2026). Responding to AI-generated emotional alerts: teachers' intervention and students' engagement in the mathematics classroom. ZDM-MathematicsEducation. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-026-01810-7

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of one episode in which the teacher watched Dema without speaking for about 15 seconds, then affirmed her careful dragging of point D; the author interprets this episode as "stabilization rather than branching" of a Get the Job Done structure. Dema's SRI reflection reported the encouragement strengthened her only momentarily.

> "This episode therefore exempliﬁes stabilization rather than branching: the motivating desire and cognitive orien- tation remained intact, while the affective tone shifted to- ward reassurance."
