Optimizing Peer Grading: A Systematic Literature Review of Reviewer Assignment Strategies and Quantity of Reviewers. Originally published 2025. `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative synthesis (RQ2) of competency-based systems across the reviewed studies. Reviewers are allocated using indicators such as academic performance, domain knowledge, past grading behavior, and calibration accuracy; systems grouped students into competency clusters (Haddidi et al., Maicus et al., Badea et al.) so each submission received reviewers of mixed levels. The review adds that diverse reviewers make feedback complementary and support learning.

> "While some mechanisms can be added to random assignment to mitigate its problems, competency-based reviewer assignment provides a more robust solution. This systematically mitigates skewed grading."
