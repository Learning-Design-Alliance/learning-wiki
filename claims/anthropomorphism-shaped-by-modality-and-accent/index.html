Synthesis of drawing and survey findings across the four conditions in the 33-teacher study. Voiceless, text-based participants "depictedPhoenix as technology(5 of 6)", and the article notes two respondents "assumedPhoenixwasWhite/European,suggestingpotentialdefault biases from text alone".

"Thus, thedegree of anthropomorphism attributed to Phoenix was shaped by both interaction modality (audio vs. text) and, within audio-based interactions, the accent of its voice."
