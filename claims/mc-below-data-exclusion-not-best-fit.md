`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Observation from the two-flavour disappearance analysis of the teaching dataset: the comparison of non-oscillated MC with data shows the prediction below data in nearly every bin, so the pedagogical goal shifts from fitting to excluding regions via chi-squared confidence-level contours. No effect size is printed.

> "When comparing the non-oscillated MC and data distributions, the MC prediction lies below the corresponding data histogram in nearly every bin, as shown in figure 5(c). Since muon-neutrino disappearance uniformly reduces the event weights in the MC sample, any oscillated MC distribution will also remain below the data."
