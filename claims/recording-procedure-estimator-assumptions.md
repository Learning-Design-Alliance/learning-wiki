## Subclaims
`q2 i?` CDR and MTS provide direct estimates of prevalence, so the POR can be estimated from phase means without further assumptions. [→ Pustejovsky 2013](#pustejovsky-2013)
`q2 i?` Event counting estimates incidence rather than prevalence, requiring an assumed known constant average event duration; partial interval recording additionally requires parametric assumptions about the IET distribution. [→ Pustejovsky 2013 (2)](#pustejovsky-2013-2)
