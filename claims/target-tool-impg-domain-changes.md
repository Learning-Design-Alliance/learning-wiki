### Weeldenburg 2024

Weeldenburg, G., Slingerland, M., Borghouts, L. B., Kromkamp, L., van Dijk, B., van der Born, E., & Vos, S. (2024). Evaluation of the digital teacher professional development TARGET-tool for optimizing the motivational climate in secondary school physical education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10379-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study with 10 PE teachers analyzed via the Framework Method against IMPG domains. Teachers reported new insights from student scans, experimentation with strategies (e.g., flexible grouping, student choice), positive student reactions, and changed convictions about self-regulation opportunities.

> "Using the Interconnected Model of Professional Growth as a theoretical basis, it was demonstrated how the use of the TARGET-tool engages teachers as active and reflective participants in their professional development and induces changes within the external domain, the domain of practice, the domain of consequences, and the personal domain."
