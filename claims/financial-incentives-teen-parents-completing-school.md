## Evidence

### Financial Incentives for Teen Parents to Stay in School 2006

Financial Incentives for Teen Parents to Stay in School. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Two randomized controlled trials (LEAP, n = 913 analyzed; Cal-Learn, n = 906 aged 18 and older). WWC effect sizes were 0.06 (LEAP) and 0.15 (Cal-Learn), both not statistically significant; the report states "Neither study reported effects on completion that were statistically significant."

> "The LEAP study reported 34% of LEAP teens completed high school or earned a GED compared with 31.9% of control group students. The Cal-Learn study reported 29.1% of treatment group members 18 years of age and older received a high school diploma or GED compared with 24.2% of similarly aged control group members."
