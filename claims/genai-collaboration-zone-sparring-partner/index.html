Falloon 2026¶

Falloon, G., & Jones, M. (2026). Human–machine knowledge building: reconceptualising knowledge building partnerships in the age of artificial intelligence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10605-2

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the article's 'Interpreting the model' section, proposing the Collaboration Zone as the site of greatest GenAI potential. The article offers no empirical data for this claim; it is a theorised proposition about where GenAI can best augment knowledge building.

"we suggest the greatest potential for GenAI to enhance human knowledge building exists in the Collaboration Zone, where it can act as a cognitive 'sparring partner', offering prompts, provocations, alternative perspectives, and refinements which could extend human thinking."
