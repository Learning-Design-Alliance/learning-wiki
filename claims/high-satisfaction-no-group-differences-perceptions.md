## Evidence

### Yu Huan 2026

Yu Huan, Li Jing, Jia Yan, Zhang Jiayang, Wang Zhitao, Zou Huiru. (2026). Enhancing online teaching of “subgingival scaling and root planing” through periodontal endoscopy assistance: a study on its application in a teaching-practicing module. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-07706-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire item Q14 on overall arrangement, answered by 147 valid respondents across the three groups; means of 4.93 ± 0.25, 4.88 ± 0.33 and 4.69 ± 0.42 with no statistically significant difference. The article prints the count as 127/14.

> "Specifically, 86.39% (127/14) of the students “strongly agreed”, and 13.61% (20/147) “agreed” that they were “satisfied with the overall arrangement of this teaching module”. The mean satisfaction scores for the three groups were 4.93 ± 0.25, 4.88 ± 0.33, and 4.69 ± 0.42, respectively. There was no statistically significant difference in the satisfaction scores among the three groups."

### Yu Huan 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the remaining summed questionnaire indicators (Q5–Q7, Q11–Q14, Q15–Q17) across the three groups; the endoscopy groups rated slightly higher but the differences were not statistically significant.

> "both PE in-person and online groups received slightly higher ratings compared to the TT group in terms of the self-reported general grasp of theories and techniques, mastery percentage of theories and techniques and satisfaction with the study module. However, these differences were not statistically significant."
