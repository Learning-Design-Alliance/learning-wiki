## Evidence

### Baleghizadeh 2014

Baleghizadeh, S., & Mortazavi, M. (2014). The impact of different types of journaling techniques on EFL learners’ self-efficacy. PROFILE Issues in Teachers’ Professional Development, 16(1), 77-88. https://doi.org/10.15446/profile.v16n1.37184

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Scheffe's test comparing the two collaborative conditions in the four-group experiment: "the mean difference between the students in the TFC and PFC was not detected as significant." The authors read this as evidence for the significance of feedback regardless of its source.

> "the mean difference between the students in the TFC and PFC was not detected as significant, indicating that collaborative journal writing in both forms (peer and teacher) were effective."
