## Evidence

### Abasi-amefon O. Affia 2022

Abasi-amefon O. Affia, Alexander Nolte, and Raimundas Matulevičius. (2022). Integrating Hackathons into an Online Cybersecurity Course. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3510456.3514151](https://doi.org/10.1145/3510456.3514151)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire analysis of the feedback intervention per grade group (Fig 5); all teams rated it above average. Feedback included online sessions during events and written feedback on submitted write-ups. No effect size was printed.

> "High grade teams (𝑀 = 4.13,𝐼𝑄𝑅 = 0.06, see Feedback Outcome in Fig 5) reported higher values of the feedback intervention than other teams followed by middle grade teams (𝑀 = 4.00,𝐼𝑄𝑅 = 0.00) then low grade teams (𝑀 = 4.00,𝐼𝑄𝑅 = 0.00)."

### Abasi-amefon O. Affia 2022 (2)

Abasi-amefon O. Affia, Alexander Nolte, and Raimundas Matulevičius. (2022). Integrating Hackathons into an Online Cybersecurity Course. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3510456.3514151](https://doi.org/10.1145/3510456.3514151)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Open-ended questionnaire responses on the feedback intervention; one student noted written feedback felt vague and rushed and required asking for clarification on every point, while online sessions allowed teams to double-check tasks and find errors in current work.

> "Student B01, however, preferred online feedback sessions, stating that “the opportunity to get explanations in real-time about the feedback was better” (B01)."
