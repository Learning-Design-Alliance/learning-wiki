Evidence¶

Livingston et al. 2018¶

Livingston, S., Toce, A., Casey, C., Montoya, F., Hart, B. R., & O'Flaherty, C. (2018). Effect of X-Word Grammar and Traditional Grammar Instruction on Grammatical Accuracy. English Language Teaching, 11(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n3p119

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Same quasi-experimental comparison of the two grammar approaches over three months, analyzing the first 300 words of essays for sentence-pattern errors. The X-Word group's error rate fell from 59.88% to 45.50% versus 51.60% to 44.10% for the traditional group, but the article reports the p-value as "0.244 which is not statistically significant" and attributes this partly to limited sentence-pattern data.

"The p-value for the sentence patterns comparison is 0.244 which is not statistically significant. This may be due in part to the limited amount of sentence pattern data available."
