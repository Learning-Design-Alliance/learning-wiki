### Sissi L. Li 2015

Sissi L. Li, Gabriela Serna, Jocelyn S. Read, Joshua R. Smith, Heather Chilton, Michael E. Loverude. (2015). A collaboration to support novice instructors in research-based astronomy teaching. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1411.5738](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1411.5738)

Peer-instructor and instructor observations during Lecture-Tutorials and lectures on moon phases: students attempted to memorize positions in the Earth-sun-moon diagram, and "many students struggle when we pose questions in which the arrangement of celestial bodies differs from the initial presentation."

### Sissi L. Li 2015 (2)

Sissi L. Li, Gabriela Serna, Jocelyn S. Read, Joshua R. Smith, Heather Chilton, Michael E. Loverude. (2015). A collaboration to support novice instructors in research-based astronomy teaching. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1411.5738](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1411.5738)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Instructor observations across course offerings on parallax (d = 1/p) and Kepler's second law: students reacted negatively to equations and struggled to accept that differently shaped triangles can have equal areas; parallax questions were "consistently among the most missed" on midterm and final exams.

> "It is diﬃcult to address this; we have not yet ﬁgured out a winning strategy. In the midterm and ﬁnal exams, questions related to parallax, are consistently among the most missed."
