# At 6 months, the full video and short answer questions groups retained knowledge significantly while the logic puzzles group did not, with no significant between-group difference in retention

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The full video and short answer questions groups demonstrated significant knowledge retention at 6 months, though less than their initial gains. [→ Lisa A DelSignore 2016](#lisa-a-delsignore-2016)
`q3 i?` The logic puzzles group did not demonstrate statistically significant knowledge retention at 6 months. [→ Lisa A DelSignore 2016 (2)](#lisa-a-delsignore-2016-2)
`q3 i?` Between groups, mean knowledge retention from pretest to retention test was comparable and not statistically significant. [→ Lisa A DelSignore 2016 (3)](#lisa-a-delsignore-2016-3)

## Evidence

### Lisa A DelSignore 2016

Lisa A DelSignore, Traci A Wolbrink, David Zurakowski, Jeffrey P Burns. (2016). Test-Enhanced E-Learning Strategies in Postgraduate Medical Education: A Randomized Cohort Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6199

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Of 49 residents, 35 (71%) completed 6-month retention testing. Mixed-model ANOVA showed "a significant degree of knowledge retention at 6 months" for full video (F1,32=22.08, P<.001) and short answer questions (F1,32=20.12, P<.001), to a lesser degree than initial gains.

> "Repeated measures mixed-model ANOVA demonstrated that the full video and short answer questions groups had a significant degree of knowledge retention at 6 months, although to a lesser degree than knowledge gained on initial mean posttest scores (full video: F1,32=22.08, P<.001; short answer questions: F1,32=20.12, P<.001)."

### Lisa A DelSignore 2016 (2)

Lisa A DelSignore, Traci A Wolbrink, David Zurakowski, Jeffrey P Burns. (2016). Test-Enhanced E-Learning Strategies in Postgraduate Medical Education: A Randomized Cohort Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6199

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same retention analysis of 35 residents, the logic puzzles group (n=11) showed no statistically significant retention (F1,32=2.44, P=.13); the authors attribute this most likely to small sample size.

> "The logic puzzle group did not demonstrate statistically significant knowledge retention at 6 months (F1,32=2.44, P=.13)."

### Lisa A DelSignore 2016 (3)

Lisa A DelSignore, Traci A Wolbrink, David Zurakowski, Jeffrey P Burns. (2016). Test-Enhanced E-Learning Strategies in Postgraduate Medical Education: A Randomized Cohort Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6199

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison of retention change across the three groups was not statistically significant (F2,32=2.77, P=.08); equivalence was not tested.

> "One-way ANOVA revealed that all 3 groups had comparable degrees of mean knowledge retention between the initial pretest and retention test (F2,32=2.77, P=.08)."
