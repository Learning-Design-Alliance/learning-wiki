# By the end of year 2, RAISE significantly improved science students' literacy assessment scores (ES = 0.32), with a non-significant meaningful effect in ELA (0.22) and no effect in history

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` RAISE had a positive, statistically significant impact on literacy achievement for students in biology classes (ES = 0.32). [→ Fancsali 2016](#fancsali-2016)
`q3 i?` ELA classrooms showed a meaningful but non-significant effect (ES = 0.22) and history classrooms a non-significant negative estimate (ES = -0.08). [→ Fancsali 2016](#fancsali-2016)

## Evidence

### Fancsali 2016

Fancsali, C., Abe, Y., & Pyatigorsky, M. (2016). Developing Content-Area Literacy: The Reading Apprenticeship Improving Secondary Education (RAISE) project: A Randomized Controlled Trial. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566986

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

Year-2 ETS online scenario-based literacy assessment impacts by subject (Exhibit 5): biology ES = 0.32 (p = .010, N = 4,360), ELA ES = 0.22 (p = .148, N = 2,936), history ES = -0.08 (p = .618, N = 3,449).

> "By the end of the second year of implementation, RAISE had a positive and statistically significant impact on student literacy in science classes. The effect size of the impact was 0.32."
