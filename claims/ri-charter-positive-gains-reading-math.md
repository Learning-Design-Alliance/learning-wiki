# Charter attendance in Rhode Island is associated with positive, significant one-year growth gains in reading (0.16 SD) and math (0.15 SD) relative to matched TPS controls

## Subclaims

### Charter School Performance in Rhode Island 2023

Impact tables print "All Students1 0.16** 94**" in reading and "0.15** 89**" in math, i.e. an effect size of 0.16 SD in reading and 0.15 SD in math.
