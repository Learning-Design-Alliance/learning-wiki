## Evidence

### N.G. Holmes 2015

N.G. Holmes, Carl E. Wieman, D.A. Bonn. (2015). Teaching critical thinking. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1505329112

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cohort equivalence check in the supplementary participant analysis: groups were also statistically equivalent on the Mechanics Baseline Test (t(288) = 1.21, p = 0.227) and the Brief Electricity and Magnetism Survey (t(177) = 1.96, p = 0.052). Equivalence was not formally tested beyond these comparisons; no effect sizes printed.

> "On entering the course, the two groups had statistically equivalent pre-test scores on the Force Concept Inventory [24]: Control, M = 77%,SE = 2%; Experiment, M = 76%,SE = 2%,t (266) = 0.20,p = 0.839."
