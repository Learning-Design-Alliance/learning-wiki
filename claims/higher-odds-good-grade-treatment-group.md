## Evidence

### D. J. Webb 2012

D. J. Webb. (2012). Improving student's problem-solving ability as well as conceptual understanding without sacrificing the physics content of a class. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1210.3385](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1210.3385)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Logistic regression on the same four-problem final-exam subset, controlling for the same factors as the linear models. The odds ratio was "2 .8 ± 0.8 times higher" for treatment students, using a cutoff of 58 out of 80 points set so about 21% of control students would reach A- level.

> "the odds of getting at least this score are 2 .8 ± 0.8 times higher for a student in the treatment group than for one in the control group made up of sections II&IV."
