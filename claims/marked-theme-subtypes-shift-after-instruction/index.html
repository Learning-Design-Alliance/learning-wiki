Evidence¶

Wei 2016¶

Wei, J. (2016). Effects of Instruction on Chinese College Students’ Thematic Choice in Academic Writing. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(14). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102949

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Analysis of marked Theme subtypes (temporal, spatial, manner, cause, contingency) in CE and CC pre- and post-writing, presented in Table 5. The quoted sentence reports the CE group's printed subtype proportions; the article also reports parallel changes in CC post-writing.

"They used less adjuncts expressing temporal (30% vs. 36%) and spatial (10% vs. 22%), and more adjunct for manner (22% vs. 13%) , cause (16% vs. 8%) and contingency (22% vs. 21) in their post-writing."
