## Evidence

### Kim T. Edward 2019

Kim T. Edward, Tsui Ban C.H. (2019). Simulation-based ultrasound-guided regional anesthesia curriculum for anesthesiology residents. Korean J Anesthesiol. https://doi.org/10.4097/kja.d.18.00317

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a randomized trial (Chuan et al.) comparing a porcine meat model with embedded bovine tendon (low-fidelity) against a fresh-frozen cadaveric upper limb (high-fidelity), each with a 45-minute deliberate practice session; it found "no differences in time to complete the simulated sciatic nerve block, errors committed, and ultrasound image quality".

> "Between the low- and high-fidelity groups, there were no differences in time to complete the simulated sciatic nerve block, errors committed, and ultrasound image quality of the nerve."

### Kim T. Edward 2019 (2)

Kim T. Edward, Tsui Ban C.H. (2019). Simulation-based ultrasound-guided regional anesthesia curriculum for anesthesiology residents. Korean J Anesthesiol. https://doi.org/10.4097/kja.d.18.00317

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review reports a study (Friedman et al.) of epidural catheter placement in actual patients comparing mannequin training with a virtual-reality display against a banana ('greengrocer's') model; "the two groups did not show a difference in skill acquisition" over six months.

> "Throughout a six-month period, the two groups did not show a difference in skill acquisition when assessed by a checklist and global rating scale (GRS)."
