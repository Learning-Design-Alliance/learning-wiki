## Evidence

### Yanagita Yasutaka 2024

Yanagita Yasutaka, Yokokawa Daiki, Fukuzawa Fumitoshi, Uchida Shun, Uehara Takanori, Ikusaka Masatomi. (2024). Expert assessment of ChatGPT's ability to generate illness scripts: an evaluative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05534-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluative study in which three board-certified physicians graded ChatGPT-4 illness scripts for 184 diseases on a three-tier scale; the Results section reports the rating distribution: "56.0% (103/184) were rated “A,” 28.3% (52/184) were “B,” and 15.8% (29/184) were “C”".

> "The distribution of the ratings among the evaluated scripts revealed that 56.0% (103/184) were rated “A,” 28.3% (52/184) were “B,” and 15.8% (29/184) were “C”."
