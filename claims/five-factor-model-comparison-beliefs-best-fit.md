## Evidence

### Boosting Students' Academic Self-Concepts 2024

Boosting Students' Academic Self-Concepts. (2024). https://osf.io/xbv4q/?view_only=62ce423079de44dba59a9e7b132417e6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Confirmatory factor analyses comparing one-, three-, and five-factor models at T1 and T2 with all participants (N = 857). The five-factor model showed the best fit (T1: RMSEA = .053, CFI = .960; T2: RMSEA = .042, CFI = .985), and the authors conclude the "beliefs are also empirically distinguishable".

> "Both the fit indices and the factor loadings show that the five-factor model fit the best. We can therefore assume that the beliefs are also empirically distinguishable."
