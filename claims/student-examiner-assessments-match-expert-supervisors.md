## Evidence

### Möltner Andreas 2020

Möltner Andreas, Lehmann Mirijam, Wachter Cornelia, Kurczyk Sonia, Schwill Simon, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). Formative assessment of practical skills with peer-assessors: quality features of an OSCE in general medicine at the Heidelberg Medical Faculty. GMS Journal for Medical Education 37(4), Doc42. [doi:10.3205/zma001335](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001335)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quality-assurance analysis of 135 random second assessments by five supervisors (medical staff) within the formative OSCE General Medicine 2018. Only one station, Anamnesis Abdomen, showed a statistically significant difference in the Wilcoxon signed-rank test.

> "Table 7 (Tab. 7) contains the correlations between examiners and supervisors at the stations, these ranged from 0.729 to 0.989."

### Möltner Andreas 2020 (2)

Möltner Andreas, Lehmann Mirijam, Wachter Cornelia, Kurczyk Sonia, Schwill Simon, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). Formative assessment of practical skills with peer-assessors: quality features of an OSCE in general medicine at the Heidelberg Medical Faculty. GMS Journal for Medical Education 37(4), Doc42. [doi:10.3205/zma001335](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001335)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Generalizability-theory analysis of all data with examiner group as fixed factor and separate variance components for the two examiner groups (Table 8). For the five supervisors, "no variance component other than zero can be demonstrated (p=0.117)" regarding strictness.

> "The supervisors give 0.568 points less than the student examiners, but the effect is not significant (p=0.152)."
