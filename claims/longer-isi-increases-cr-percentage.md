## Evidence

### Leila Etemadi 2023

Leila Etemadi, Dan-Anders Jirenhed and Anders Rasmussen. (2023). Effects of working memory load and CS-US intervals on delay eyeblink conditioning. npj Science of Learning 8:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00167-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed effects model comparing ISI groups (150, 250, 500 ms). The article reports that "Switching from a 150 ms ISI to a 500 ms ISI translates to a 0.17*350 = 59.5% increase in CR percentage", statistically significant; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "A 1 ms increase in the ISI results in a 0.17% increase in the percentage of CRs. Switching from a 150 ms ISI to a 500 ms ISI translates to a 0.17*350 = 59.5% increase in CR percentage. This effect is also signi ﬁcant ( t = 10.30, P = 1.74e–22***, CI = 0.13–0.20%)."
