Second moderated regression model on the 260-teacher survey, controlling for confidence, explained 74.2% of variance in attitudes (R2 = 0.742) with strong overall fit (F = 205.9, p < 0.001); the direct support-attitude path was not significant (V = 0.128, p = 0.106).

"The confidence had a strong and statistically significant positive effect on attitudes (V = 0.855, p < 0.001), indicating that teachers with higher confidence in using AI are more likely to adopt favorable attitudes."
