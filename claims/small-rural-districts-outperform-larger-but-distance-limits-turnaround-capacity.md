`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

The CST handbook chapter on 'Building Rural District Capacity for Turnaround', based on a survey of senior staff from 13 state education agencies and a research literature review, states that "smaller districts tend to outperform larger ones" while large geographic spread limits turnaround management capacity.

> "The handbook chapter notes that a smaller population can mean smaller classes or schools, and smaller districts tend to outperform larger ones. Yet, being spread over a large area may limit the district's capacity to manage turnaround efforts because staff must travel so far to reach each school."
