### Shinohara Eriko 2022

Shinohara Eriko, Ohashi Yukiko, Hada Ayako, Usui Yuriko. (2022). Effects of 1-day e-learning education on perinatal psychological support skills among midwives and perinatal healthcare workers in Japan: a randomised controlled study. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-022-00832-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result on the 9-point Counsellor Response Form attitude measure in the randomised trial (N = 115); the article reports no statistically significant effect and no effect size, so the finding is a failure to detect change, not evidence of equivalence.

> "For attitude, no main effect was observed for either group (F(1) = 2.998, NS) or timing (F(1,1) = 0.061, NS)."
