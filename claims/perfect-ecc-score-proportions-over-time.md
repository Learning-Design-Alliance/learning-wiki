Evaluation of the Effectiveness of Advanced Technology Clinical Simulation Manikins in Improving the Capability of Australian Paramedics to Deliver High-Quality Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation: Pre- and Postintervention Study. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive statistics from the pre-post study (Table 2) report perfect-score proportions; hand position and depth had the highest means across all time points, while adequate rate and full release were lowest (means of 85 or above).

> "The proportion of paramedics with a perfect overall compression score was 21.3% (n=20) at time 1, 28.7% (n=27) at time 2, and 7.1% (n=2) at time 3."
