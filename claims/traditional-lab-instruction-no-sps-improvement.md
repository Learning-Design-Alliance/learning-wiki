## Evidence

### Chengere Ashebir Mekonnen 2025

Chengere Ashebir Mekonnen, Bono Beyene Dobo, Zinabu Samuel Assefa, Jilo Kedir Woliy. (2025). Enhancing secondary school students’ science process skills through guided inquiry-based laboratory activities in biology. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0320692

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired samples t-test within the control group (N=29) taught with the TLEI approach for eight weeks. The article reports pretest M = 2.28 (SD = 1.56) and posttest M = 1.79 (SD = 1.70), a slight decrease, and "no significant improvement in SPS scores within the CG".

> "The results showed a t-value of 1.220 with a p-value of.233 (p >  0.05), indicating no significant improvement in SPS scores within the CG."
