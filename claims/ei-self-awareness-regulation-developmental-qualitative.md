`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative descriptive study of 24 purposively sampled undergraduate health sciences students at the University of Hail; reflexive thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews. Participants reported that regulation is "a skill developed progressively through academic exposure and experiential learning."

> "Importantly, emotional regulation was not described as an innate or fixed trait but rather as a skill developed progressively through academic exposure and experiential learning."
