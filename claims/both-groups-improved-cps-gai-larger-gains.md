# Both GAI-assisted and traditional DST groups improved in CPS skills over the 20-week program, with larger gains in the GAI group

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The experimental group showed a strong within-group CPS improvement (Cohen's d = 1.18) while the control group showed a moderate improvement (Cohen's d = 0.64). [→ Wei 2025](#wei-2025)

## Evidence

### Wei 2025

Wei, X., Wang, L., Lee, L.-K., & Liu, R. (2025). The effects of generative AI on collaborative problem-solving and team creativity performance in digital story creation: an experimental study. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 22:23. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-025-00526-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired sample t-tests on pre/post CPS scores for both groups (n = 30 each). The article reports "significant improvements in CPS skills for both" groups, with Cohen's d of 0.64 (control) and 1.18 (experimental), indicating moderate and strong effects respectively.

> "the paired sample t-test results showed significant improvements in CPS skills for both the control (t = 3.53, p < 0.01) and experimental groups (t = 6.44, p < 0.001). The effect sizes were 0.64 for the control group and 1.18 for the experimental group"
