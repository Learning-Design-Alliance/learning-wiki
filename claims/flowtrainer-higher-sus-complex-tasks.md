# FlowTrainer (SystemB) receives higher usability ratings than the baseline editor (SystemA) in the first and third study sessions, with the largest advantage in the most complex session

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` SystemB scored higher than SystemA on SUS in Session 1 (74.38 vs 72.19) and Session 3 (83.12 vs 73.44), though task complexity did not significantly impact usability ratings for either system. [→ Ananya Ipsita 2025](#ananya-ipsita-2025)

## Evidence

### Ananya Ipsita 2025

Ananya Ipsita, Ramesh Kaki, Mayank Patel, Asim Unmesh, Kylie A Peppler, and Karthik Ramani. (2025). Interactive authoring of outcome-oriented lesson plans for immersive Virtual Reality training. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.5574179](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5574179)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative within-subjects user study with 8 experienced welders, counterbalanced order. In Session 3 SystemB averaged SUS 83.12 (SD = 10.42) versus SystemA 73.44 (SD = 19.64); Session 1 showed a smaller advantage (74.38 vs 72.19).

> "In Session 3, SystemB had a significant advantage with a score of 83.12 (SD = 10.42), compared to SystemA with an average of 73.44 (SD = 19.64)."
