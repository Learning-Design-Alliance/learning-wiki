### Cheung Chiyuen 2026

Development and application of a multidimensional instrument to evaluate competency discrepancies in orthodontic practice. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08237-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multiple regression on the 434-participant cross-sectional sample with composite MDT score as dependent variable. The article reports the three predictors "collectively explained 14% of the variance" (adjusted R² = 0.134), with acceptable multicollinearity diagnostics (VIF < 1.02) and Durbin-Watson of 2.044.

> "Educational attainment emerged as the strongest predictor (β = 0.262, p < 0.001), followed by age (β = 0.201, p < 0.001) and frequency of continuing education participation (β = 0.127, p = 0.005)"
