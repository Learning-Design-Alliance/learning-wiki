### Noll C 2026

Noll C, Mikuteit M, Golon S, Louzek C, Afshar K, Just I, Steffens S. (2026). Feasibility and implementation of a final year Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) in a medical model curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10350-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Anonymous voluntary online questionnaire completed by students after the PJ-OSCE. The mean rating was "10.90/15 points (SD 2.54)" and "41% of students reported a large or very large subjective learning gain".

> "On a 15-point grading scale, students rated the PJ-OSCE with a mean score of 10.90/15 points (SD 2.54; n = 225). 41% of students reported a large or very large subjective learning gain."
