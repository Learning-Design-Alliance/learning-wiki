## Evidence

### Fernández-Lao Carolina 2016

Fernández-Lao Carolina, Cantarero-Villanueva Irene, Galiano-Castillo Noelia, Caro-Morán Elena, Díaz-Rodríguez Lourdes, Arroyo-Morales Manuel. (2016). The effectiveness of a mobile application for the development of palpation and ultrasound imaging skills to supplement the traditional learning of physiotherapy students. BMC Medical Education 16:274. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0775-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled study, the m-learning group rated the learning process more positively than the control group on five survey items, with no significant differences for theory-practice combination (p = .114) or preference for another group (p = .185).

> "There were higher ratings in the responses from the m-learning group compared with the control group for the items the teacher was competent (p < .001), lessons were interesting (p = .016), I was able to learn a lot (p = .022), the size of the groups was optimal (p = .002) and the teacher-student interaction was adequate (p = .008)."
