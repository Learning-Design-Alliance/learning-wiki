## Evidence

### King 2025

King, R. B., & Wang, F. (2025). The rich get richer: Socioeconomic advantage amplifies the role of growth mindsets in learning. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 792–810. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12755

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 2 interaction tests in the three-level PISA 2018 analysis, with school SES computed as the mean family SES within each school. The article reports the null achievement interaction and significant positive interactions for motivation and engagement.

> "The moderation effect between school SES and growth mindsets was not significantly related to academic achievement (b = −.16, SE = .62, p > .05, effect size = .00), supporting the universalist hypothesis. However, school SES was a significant moderator for intrinsic motivation (b = .03, SE = .00, p < .001, effect size = .06) and academic engagement (b = .03, SE = .01, p < .05, effect size = .04), supporting the rich‐get‐richer hypothesis."
