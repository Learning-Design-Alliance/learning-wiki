## Evidence

### Oquendo Yousi A. 2017

Oquendo Yousi A., Riddle Elijah W., Hiller Dennis, Blinman Thane A., Kuchenbecker Katherine J. (2017). Automatically rating trainee skill at a pediatric laparoscopic suturing task. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-017-5873-6

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feature-selection analysis of the TMVG model, in which LASSO regularization chose 97 of 280 features; none were time-based. The authors contrast this with previous studies that used task completion time to compare novice and intermediate surgeons to experts.

> "Interestingly, our best model did not select any features based solely on task completion time, which shows that completion time is not necessarily predictive when evaluating surgeons on a finer scale."
