Christina Clark Tuttle 2013¶

Christina Clark Tuttle, Brian Gill, Philip Gleason, Virginia Knechtel, Ira Nichols-Barrer, Alexandra Resch. (2013). KIPP Middle Schools: Impacts on Achievement and Other Outcomes (Executive Summary). Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/kipp-middle-schools-impacts-on-achievement-and-other-outcomes

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The executive summary states the evaluation "nearly doubled the size of the sample, to 43 KIPP middle schools", drawing on data from local districts or states for schools open at the study's 2010 start. No effect sizes are printed in the summary.

"For this phase of the study, we nearly doubled the size of the sample, to 43 KIPP middle schools, including all KIPP middle schools that were open at the start of the study in 2010 for which we were able to acquire relevant data from local districts or states."
