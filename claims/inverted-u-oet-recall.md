## Evidence

### Luke G. Eglington and Philip I. Pavlik Jr 2020

Luke G. Eglington and Philip I. Pavlik Jr. (2020). Optimizing practice scheduling requires quantitative tracking of individual item performance. npj Science of Learning 5:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00074-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Likelihood ratio test on Experiment 2 data comparing linear versus quadratic fits of OET condition value to final-test performance; the quadratic model was "a signiﬁcantly better ﬁt" and predicted declining performance near OET 1.

> "A likelihood ratio test indicated that the model with an additional quadratic term was a signiﬁcantly better ﬁt( ▫2 = 23.47, p < 0.001) and importantly predicted that performance would eventually decrease as the OET approached 1 (maximally easy)."
