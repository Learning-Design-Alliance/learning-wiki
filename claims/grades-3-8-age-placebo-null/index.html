Nick Huntington-Klein. (2026). Little Impact of ChatGPT on High School Test Scores. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08812

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Age-placebo analysis using SEDA cohort-standardized district scores for grades 3-8 in mathematics and reading, 2017-2019 and 2022-2024, over 9,877 districts. Estimates are "-0.0006 (0.0022)" and "0.0030 (0.0019)".
