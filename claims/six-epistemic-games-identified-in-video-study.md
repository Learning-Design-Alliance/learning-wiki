The article reports, citing Tuminaro's dissertation study of problem solving in algebra-based physics, that videotaped student groups were analyzed and six e-games identified. The review attributes this to J. Tuminaro (PhD dissertation, U. of Maryland, 2004); the underlying study's design is not described here beyond the video corpus.

> "In watching about 50 hours of video of student groups solving problems, Tuminaro identified six commonly used e-games: Mapping Meaning to Mathematics, Mapping Mathematics to Meaning, Physical Mechanism, Pictorial Analysis, Recursive Plug-and-Chug, and Transliteration to Mathematics"
