Evidence¶

Maaike E. Prangsma 2008¶

Maaike E. Prangsma, Carla A. M. Van Boxtel, Gellof Kanselaar. (2008). Developing a 'big picture': Effects of collaborative construction of multimodal representations in history. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-007-9026-5

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Post-test ANOVA follow-up in the same classroom experiment: the Visual group (n=31) versus Text group (n=30) contrast was non-significant; equivalence was not tested. The authors note the means pointed in the hypothesised direction.

"On average, the scores of the Visual group on the post-test were 1.75 higher than the Text group scores, but this difference was not significant (p = .58)."
