# For children enrolled two full years, the ECE PDI produced a statistically significant medium effect on social-emotional growth (δ = .60, p < .05) and a near-significant language effect

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Longitudinal growth modeling showed the ECE PDI lessened the gap in child social emotional development between intervention and comparison children with a medium effect size (δ = .60, p < .05). [→ Whalen 2016](#whalen-2016)

## Evidence

### Whalen 2016

Whalen, Samuel P. PhD; Horsley, Heather L. PhD; Parkinson, Kathleen K. MEd; and Pacchiano, Debra PhD (2016) "A Development Evaluation Study of a Professional Development Initiative to Strengthen Organizational Conditions in Early Education Settings," Journal of Applied Research on Children: Informing Policy for Children at Risk: Vol. 7 : Iss. 2 , Article 9. http://digitalcommons.library.tmc.edu/childrenatrisk/vol7/iss2/9

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal hierarchical linear growth models comparing growth rates for children with 2 full years of ECE enrollment (n = 358; 55 comparison, 301 intervention for Social Emotional) on GOLD Teaching Strategies measures. The article reports a statistically significant effect on the rate of social-emotional development, δ = .60, p < .05.

> "The ECE PDI model intervention lessened the gap in child social emotional development between the intervention and comparison center children with a medium effect size (δ = .60, p < .05)"

### Whalen 2016 (2)

Whalen, Samuel P. PhD; Horsley, Heather L. PhD; Parkinson, Kathleen K. MEd; and Pacchiano, Debra PhD (2016) "A Development Evaluation Study of a Professional Development Initiative to Strengthen Organizational Conditions in Early Education Settings," Journal of Applied Research on Children: Informing Policy for Children at Risk: Vol. 7 : Iss. 2 , Article 9. http://digitalcommons.library.tmc.edu/childrenatrisk/vol7/iss2/9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same 2-year cohort growth modeling on GOLD measures. Table 6 reports language δ = 0.43, p = 0.08; cognitive δ = 0.24, p = 0.23; literacy δ = 0.07, p = 0.71; mathematics δ = 0.20, p = 0.35. GOLD ELA was excluded due to small subgroup sample size.

> "Modeling the children's growth trajectories over the 2-year period yielded a statistically significant intervention effect on the children's rate of learning and development in the Social Emotional domain, with a near significant effect in the children's language development"
