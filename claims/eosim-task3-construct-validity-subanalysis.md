## Evidence

### Leijte Erik 2019

Leijte Erik, Arts Elke, Witteman Bart, Jakimowicz Jack, De Blaauw Ivo, Botden Sanne. (2019). Construct, content and face validity of the eoSim laparoscopic simulator on advanced suturing tasks. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-06652-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subanalysis of the anastomosis task (45 participants: 31 novices, 6 target, 8 experts), performed because the 16-stitch version was too difficult to complete within the time limit. The quote reports completion differences of "38% versus 0%" and "100% versus 35%", both p = 0.003; the subanalysis also found significant differences in number of stitches, time per stitch and distance per stitch (all p < 0.001).

> "This subanalysis shows the difference in the completion of the task between novices and experts for the 16 sutures (38% versus 0%, p = 0.003) and eight sutures (100% versus 35%, p = 0.003)."

### Leijte Erik 2019 (2)

Leijte Erik, Arts Elke, Witteman Bart, Jakimowicz Jack, De Blaauw Ivo, Botden Sanne. (2019). Construct, content and face validity of the eoSim laparoscopic simulator on advanced suturing tasks. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-06652-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Primary ANOVA on Task 3 raw parameters found no significant between-group differences; the descriptive expert-versus-novice contrasts (764 versus 1123 s, p = 0.092; 13.0 versus 22.8, p = 0.107) were not statistically significant. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Task 3, however, shows no significant differences in the system outcome parameters between the groups, although experts were faster compared to novices (764 versus 1123 s, p = 0.092) and had travelled a shorter distance with their instruments (13.0 cm versus 22.8 cm, p = 0.107)."
