## Evidence

### Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi 2025

Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi (2025). Consistency between self-reported and log data to understand students’ experience of learning in flipped classrooms. Scientific Reports 15, 34369. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17052-8

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Hierarchical cluster analysis of the proportions of five online event types in the LMS log data yielded an assessment-oriented cluster (n = 135) and a preparation-oriented cluster (n = 136); ANOVAs showed the largest difference on "post-lecture quizzes (F(1, 269) = 62.63, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.19)".

> "the two clusters differed significantly on pre-lecture readings (F(1, 269) = 8.42, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.03), pre-lecture videos (F(1, 269) = 6.14, p = 0.01, η2 = 0.02), pre-lecture quizzes (F(1, 269) = 24.14, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.08), and post-lecture quizzes (F(1, 269) = 62.63, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.19)"

### Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi 2025 (2)

Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi (2025). Consistency between self-reported and log data to understand students’ experience of learning in flipped classrooms. Scientific Reports 15, 34369. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17052-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results of the log-data cluster comparison report the direction of each contrast: "Cluster 2 students had higher proportions of pre-lecture readings, pre-lecture videos, and pre-lecture quizzes", while cluster 1 students had a higher post-lecture quiz proportion.

> "Cluster 2 students had higher proportions of pre-lecture readings, pre-lecture videos, and pre-lecture quizzes than cluster 1 students. But cluster 1 students had a higher proportion of post-lecture quizzes than cluster 2 students."
