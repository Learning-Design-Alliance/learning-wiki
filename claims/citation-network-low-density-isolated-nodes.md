Citation network analysis based on 686 citing–cited publication pairs, limited to the 943 publications (76.29%) with DOIs, using OpenAlex metadata. The article reports the printed density and isolation figures as evidence that even same-prompt-type studies often fail to cite each other.

> "Although the complete network encompassed 941 publications and 1,547 citation links, it showed a very low overall density (0.0017; i.e., 0.17% of all possible citation connections are actually present) and consisted of 366 isolated nodes (38.90% of the 941 papers)."
