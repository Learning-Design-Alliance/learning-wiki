## Evidence

### Benjamin D. Geller 2022

Benjamin D. Geller, Jack Rubien, Sara M. Hiebert, and Catherine H. Crouch. (2022). The impact of introductory physics for the life sciences in a senior biology capstone course. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of written responses from 64 biology majors in a senior capstone course, grouped by prior physics background; nonparametric Wilcoxon test on IPLS 1-specific emergent code elements, as the authors report that "IPLS 1 students scored significantly higher than non-IPLS 1 students (Wilcoxon p < 0.0001". No effect size was printed, so no impact magnitude is coded.

> "On elements of the code that relate directly to content and skills emphasized in IPLS 1, IPLS 1 students scored significantly higher than non-IPLS 1 students (Wilcoxon p < 0.0001, Fig. 1a)."
