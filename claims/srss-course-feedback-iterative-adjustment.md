## Evidence

### Farris David P. 2024

Farris David P., Lebo Rachael A., Price Carrie. (2024). Designing a framework for curriculum building in systematic review competencies for librarians: a case report. Journal of the Medical Library Association : JMLA. https://doi.org/10.5195/jmla.2024.1930

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Case-report description of MLA course feedback forms across eleven cohorts (2022–2024). The article reports the themes qualitatively; no ratings or statistics are printed.

> "Comments from the course feedback forms were consistently positive. Recurring themes were centered around 1) the instructors' abilities to curate content for both new and experienced searchers, 2) enthusiasm for the breakout rooms and the course activity, and 3) the ability to incorporate something new from the class into their professional work."

### Farris David P. 2024 (2)

Farris David P., Lebo Rachael A., Price Carrie. (2024). Designing a framework for curriculum building in systematic review competencies for librarians: a case report. Journal of the Medical Library Association : JMLA. https://doi.org/10.5195/jmla.2024.1930

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Authors' account of iterative course refinement across cohorts, including expanded concept-nesting instruction and lengthened small-group time after cohorts reported valuing it. The article states no feedback necessitated larger changes.

> "For each iteration of the course, the instructors consider previous cohorts' feedback and make small adjustments as necessary. For example, an early cohort disclosed that they did not understand the idea of concept nesting, so concept nesting instruction was provided in greater detail."
