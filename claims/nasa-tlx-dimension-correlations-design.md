# NASA-TLX workload dimensions were significantly intercorrelated, including mental demand with effort (r = 0.768) and performance negatively with frustration (r = −0.443)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Mental demand correlated positively with effort (r = 0.768, p < 0.05) and effort with temporal demand (r = 0.641, p < 0.05). [→ Christopher Nikulin 2019](#christopher-nikulin-2019)
`q2 i3` Performance correlated negatively with frustration (r = −0.443, p < 0.05), and the authors report a strong frustration–mental demand relationship (r = 0.509, p < 0.05). [→ Christopher Nikulin 2019 (2)](#christopher-nikulin-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Christopher Nikulin 2019

Christopher Nikulin, Gabriela Lopez, Eduardo Piñonez, Luis Gonzalez, Pia Zapata. (2019). NASA-TLX for predictability and measurability of instructional design models: case study in design methods. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09657-4

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of 133 NASA-TLX questionnaires covering 11 modules and three activities each; the authors report "mental demand and effort, r = 0.768, p < 0.05" and effort–temporal demand r = 0.641, with most dimensions correlated and significant.

> "several relationships emerged (e.g., mental demand and effort, r = 0.768, p < 0.05; effort and temporal demand, r = 0.641, p < 0.05)"

### Christopher Nikulin 2019 (2)

Christopher Nikulin, Gabriela Lopez, Eduardo Piñonez, Luis Gonzalez, Pia Zapata. (2019). NASA-TLX for predictability and measurability of instructional design models: case study in design methods. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09657-4

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Same correlation analysis of the 133 questionnaires; the authors report performance and frustration negatively correlated at "r = − 0.443, p < 0.05", and in the discussion a frustration–mental demand relationship of r = 0.509, p < 0.05.

> "However, negative relationships are strongly correlated to the performance and frustration: r = − 0.443, p < 0.05."
