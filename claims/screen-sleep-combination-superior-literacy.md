## Evidence

### Watson Amanda 2022

Watson Amanda, Dumuid Dorothea, Maher Carol, Olds Tim. (2022). Associations between meeting 24-hour movement guidelines and academic achievement in Australian primary school-aged children. Journal of Sport and Health Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2020.12.004

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Post-hoc t tests with sequential Bonferroni adjustment in the self-report model (n = 1270) compared each compliance category with the meets-no-guidelines condition. The article reports d = 0.9 for the screen+sleep combination and d = 0.7 for meeting all guidelines; Table 2 shows literacy means of 591 ± 47 and 566 ± 55 versus 536 ± 68 for none.

> "When assessed by self-report, there were significant differences in literacy achievement among those who met all guidelines (t(334) = -4.05, p = 0.0001, d = 0.7) or met the screen time and sleep guidelines in combination (t(125) = -5.02, p < 0.001, d = 0.9), compared with the "meets no guidelines" category."
