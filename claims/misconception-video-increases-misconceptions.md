## Evidence

### Shayan Majidy 2024

Shayan Majidy. (2024). Addressing misconceptions in university physics: A review and experiences from quantum physics educators. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.20923](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.20923)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment by Kulgemeyer and Wittwer dividing 149 physics learners into two groups viewing videos similar in comprehensibility and duration but differing in content accuracy about force. The misconception group showed "more misconceptions and less scientific knowledge" than controls.

> "The results revealed that the group exposed to misconceptions developed a comparable level of perceived understanding to the control group but acquired more misconceptions and less scientific knowledge."
