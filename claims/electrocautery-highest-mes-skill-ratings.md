## Evidence

### Darie S. 2026

Darie S., Wainman B.C., Wohl G.R., Wang A.Y., Zhang B.Z., Li A., Ahmad E., Palombella A.S., Rockarts J.L., de SA D.L., Nguyen L.N., DeCarlo B., Downer N., Brewer‐Deluce D. (2026). `q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Skill-specific post-hoc comparisons from the two-way MANOVA of MES ratings in the resident-rating study. The article reports "electrocautery was significantly higher ranked than the other skills (Figure 4, all p < 0.001)" with partial η² values of 0.062 to 0.113 across the four domains.

> "In terms of skill‐specific differences, electrocautery was significantly higher ranked than the other skills (Figure 4, all p < 0.001) across the appearance (partial η 2 = 0.062), texture (partial η 2 = 0.113), force (partial η 2 = 0.093), and suitability (partial η 2 = 0.076) domains."
