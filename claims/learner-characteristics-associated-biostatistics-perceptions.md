## Evidence

### Li Chen 2018

Li Chen, Wang Ling, Zhang Yuhai, Li Chanjuan, Xu Yongyong, Shang Lei, Xia Jielai. (2018). Assessment of a block curriculum design on medical postgraduates’ perception towards biostatistics: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1232-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Univariate analysis (Table 4) of the 499 block-teaching students linked background characteristics to perception section scores; logical thinking, mathematical basics and computer basics each showed significant differences (P < 0.05).

> "Moreover, students with sufficient logical thinking, mathematics, and computer basics were more positive than those with poor basics (P < 0.05)."

### Li Chen 2018 (2)

Li Chen, Wang Ling, Zhang Yuhai, Li Chanjuan, Xu Yongyong, Shang Lei, Xia Jielai. (2018). Assessment of a block curriculum design on medical postgraduates’ perception towards biostatistics: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1232-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate comparison within the block cohort between students with and without research experience on value, expectation and ability section means, all reported at P < 0.01.

> "In addition, the research experience aided the students in obtaining maximum course value (3.58 vs. 3.38, P < 0.01), positive expectations (4.35 vs. 4.20, P < 0.01) and superior applicability on academic knowledge (3.50 vs. 3.24, P < 0.01)."
