Evidence¶

Fancsali 2016¶

Fancsali, C., Abe, Y., & Pyatigorsky, M. (2016). Developing Content-Area Literacy: The Reading Apprenticeship Improving Secondary Education (RAISE) project: A Randomized Controlled Trial. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566986

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Authors' interpretation of the year-1-to-year-2 pattern in teacher survey impacts from the three-cohort RCT; the article prints no effect size for the year-1 comparison in this abstract.

"The size of the effects increased between year 1 and 2, suggesting that the additional professional development received by teachers in the summer following their first year of implementation, along with the subsequent on-site support during year 2, increased teachers' ability to implement RAISE."
