## Evidence

### Ong Kelly 2023

Ong Kelly, Bald Philip, Dryhurst David, Ahmed Saif, Yusuf Gibran Tim, Lunawat Rahul, Sriprasad Seshadri, Thapa Gauri, Tirnoveanu Alice, Webb Richard, Rahman Eqram. (2023). The design and validation of a low-cost trans perineal (TP) prostate biopsy simulator for training: improving trainees’ confidence and cognitive targeting skills. World Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-023-04387-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Face validity was collected via a standardised Likert questionnaire from 3 expert and 4 novice participants. Ultrasound realism was rated 4.14/5, needle navigation realism 4.29/5, procedural steps 4.14/5, and the simulator overall 4.57/5 (IQR 4.5–5); "There was no significant difference between the expert and novice (p = 0.09)". No effect size was printed.

> "The overall realism of the biopsy procedure was rated a mean of 4 out of 5 (IQR 3.5–4.5) by all participants."
