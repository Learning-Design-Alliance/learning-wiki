Pagnucci N, Carnevale FA, Bagnasco A, Tolotti A, Cadorin L, Sasso L. (2015). A cross-sectional study of pedagogical strategies in nursing education: opportunities and constraints toward using effective pedagogy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0411-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey results (Table 5) show the receptive architecture had the highest mean (4.10, s.d. 0.73) and the metacognitive architecture the lowest (2.09, s.d. 1.18); the student-focused meta-architecture mean was 2.65 versus 3.55 for teacher-focused. The quote names both sides of the comparison.

> "The more student-focused didactic architectures, which call for shared control of the methodology between students and teacher, and moreover view the student not only as more active in the learning process, but at its very centre (guided discovery, simulative, collaborative, exploratory and meta-cognitive activities), turned out to be less frequently adopted, on average, than the teacher-focused architectures (receptive and behavioural)"
