# Learning with 3D anatomy materials on a glasses-free 3D display does not significantly improve mean understanding of spatial arrangement compared with a 2D display

## Evidence

### Muro S 2025

Muro S, Miyosawa K, Yamaguchi K, Okamoto K, Okamoto S, Itamiya T, Akita K. (2025). Evaluation of glasses‐free 3D anatomy learning materials through a randomized control study with a puzzle‐like method. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70063

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the RCT comparing 2D versus 3D display groups (28 students each), Mann–Whitney U comparison of the total improvement index found no significant difference between groups; the article also reports "Dpre:p=0.62, Dpost:p=0.332" for pre/post between-group comparisons. No effect size is printed.

> "Regarding the improvement index ID, the mean was 0.37±0.21 and 0.39±0.14 for the 2D and 3D display groups, respectively, indicating no significant difference between the groups."
