### Bracco 2014

Bracco, K. R., Klarin, B., Broek, M., Austin, K., Finkelstein, N., Bugler, D., & Mundry, S. (2014). Statewide networks: Connecting education systems and stakeholders to support college readiness. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559537

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consideration drawn from the cross-state evaluation of the four state spotlights and AD interviews. The report states local and regional activities "led to more tangible outcomes such as course alignment, transition course development." No effect size is printed.

> "Cultivating activities in local nodes may be the most effective way to achieve and sustain goals. While statewide planning and networking was critical to the Core to College work, the activities at the local and regional level led to more tangible outcomes such as course alignment, transition course development, and implementation of teacher training and professional development."
