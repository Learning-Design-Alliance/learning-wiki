## Evidence

### James Soland 2019

James Soland. (2019). Can item response times provide insight into students' motivation and self-efficacy in math? An initial application of test metadata to understand students' social emotional needs. Educational Measurement Issues and Practice 38 (3) 86-96. https://doi.org/10.1111/emip.12258

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Analytic example examining whether item response times, after accounting for item difficulty and estimates of true achievement, relate to social-emotional constructs. The article reports that "the amount of time students spend on very difficult items is highly correlated with motivation and self-efficacy." No correlation coefficient is printed in the available text.

> "While results do not indicate a strong relationship between mean item durations and these constructs in general, the amount of time students spend on very difficult items is highly correlated with motivation and self-efficacy."
