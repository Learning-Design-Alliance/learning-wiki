# NRP trainees report significantly higher perceived usefulness for a VR simulation than for 360-degree NRP training videos

## Evidence

### Mustafa Yalin Aydin 2024

Mustafa Yalin Aydin, Vernon Curran, Susan White, Lourdes Peña-Castillo, Oscar Meruvia-Pastor. (2024). VR-NRP: A Virtual Reality Simulation for Training in the Neonatal Resuscitation Program. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.15598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.15598)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled crossover study of N=30 NRP trainees comparing the VR-NRP simulation with 360-degree NRP videos, questionnaire ratings showed "significantly higher perceptions of usefulness" for the VR simulation (FDR-corrected WMW test p=0.014). No effect size was printed.

> "Participants reported significantly higher perceptions of usefulness (false discovery rate (FDR)-corrected Wilcoxon–Mann–Whitney (WMW) test p=0.014) in particular regarding learning performance and the perceived learning effect of VR."
