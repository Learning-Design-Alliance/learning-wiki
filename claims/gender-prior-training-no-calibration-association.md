## Evidence

### Roszipal Benjamin 2025

Roszipal Benjamin, Szelesi Gabriella, Ernst Martin, Hoffelner Alexander, Wagner Michael. (2025). Competency development in early nursing training: a cross-sectional OSCE study of self-assessment versus examiner ratings. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08279-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-subjects t-tests within the OSCE cohort compared discrepancy scores by prior healthcare training (41.2% with training) and gender (69.6% female). All comparisons non-significant. The article notes the mostly female cohort reduces power to detect gender effects and heterogeneous prior training may limit transferability.

> "Independent-samples t-tests revealed no significant differences in discrepancy scores by prior healthcare training (professional: t(101)=–1.42, p =.160; methodological: t(101)=–0.16, p =.875; social: t(102)=–0.16, p =.876) or by gender (professional: t(101)=–1.14, p =.256; methodological: t(101)=–1.03, p =.306; social: t(102) = 0.22, p =.825)."
