## Evidence

### Zöll Lisa 2025

Zöll Lisa, Deschner James, Hollinderbäumer Anke, Nowak Thomas, Zajontz Tamara, Behling Lina, Fischbeck Sabine, Schmidt Uwe, Pfeiffer-Grötz Thekla J. (2025). The effects of communication training on communication competence – a 360° evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07676-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control group of 63 students received no intervention between T0 and T1, yet Wilcoxon tests showed significant self-assessment gains on questions 1.2–1.8 (p = 0.004 to p < 0.001). The authors attribute this partly to a possible Hawthorne effect.

> "The control group showed significant improvements in the students’ self-assessments of questions 1.2–1.8. (S1.2: p = 0.004; S1.3: p = 0.011; S1.4: p = 0.042; S1.5: p = 0.021; S1.6 p = 0.023; S1.7: p < 0.001; S1.8: p = 0.012)"

### Zöll Lisa 2025 (2)

Zöll Lisa, Deschner James, Hollinderbäumer Anke, Nowak Thomas, Zajontz Tamara, Behling Lina, Fischbeck Sabine, Schmidt Uwe, Pfeiffer-Grötz Thekla J. (2025). The effects of communication training on communication competence – a 360° evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07676-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of intervention and control group self-assessments at T1 found "no statistically significant differences between the two groups at this one point in time"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The statistical comparison revealed no statistically significant differences between the two groups at this one point in time."
