## Evidence

### Sahin Abdul Samet 2026

Sahin Abdul Samet, Yilmaz Mutlu, Imamoglu Melih, Beser Muhammet Fatih, Karaca Yunus, Pasli Sinan, Gunduz Abdulkadir. (2026). Effectiveness and retention of extremity and junctional tourniquet training among medical interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09111-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same 32-intern single-group study, junctional skill scores (max 12) rose from near-baseline 1.7 ± 2.1 to 10.8 ± 0.9 post-training and remained 10.4 ± 1.0 at 60 days; Bonferroni-corrected Wilcoxon tests showed no significant post-test to follow-up decline.

> "junctional tourniquet skill scores, in which participants had almost no prior familiarity, increased from 1.7 ± 2.1 to 10.8 ± 0.9 and these skill gains were statistically maintained at the two-month follow-up (10.4 ± 1.0)"
