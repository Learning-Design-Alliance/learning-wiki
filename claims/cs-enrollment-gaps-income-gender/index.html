Quinn Burke 2021¶

Quinn Burke, Jeremy Roschelle, Pati Ruiz, & Josh Weisgrau. (2021). Understanding and Supporting District Systems Change Around Computer Science Education. AERA Annual Conference, Division L Structured Poster Session. https://bit.ly/36szXLy

q2 · i? · design · r1

Self-reported enrollment figures quoted from one district's application to the partnership, comparing enrollment of more affluent students with students in poverty and reporting the share of female students. The article offers these as motivation for the district's equity focus, with no inferential test.

"Of the high school students currently enrolled in a computer science or engineering course, ● 16% of our more affluent students are enrolled, while only 4% of our students in poverty are enrolled. ● Only 30% of the students are female"
