## Evidence

### Zhang Yunyun 2026

Zhang Yunyun, Ye Jingying, Qin He. (2026). Application of VR technology in standardized residency training for endoscopic procedures in otorhinolaryngology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09576-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of the five examination domains showed VR advantages concentrated in two domains, with "No significant differences" in disease pathogenesis, clinical manifestations, and diagnostic reasoning. No effect sizes were reported.

> "In domain-specific comparisons, the VR group scored significantly higher in anatomical and physiological foundations and treatment principles of diseases (P < 0.001 for both). No significant differences were observed in the domains of disease pathogenesis, clinical manifestations of diseases, and diagnostic and differential diagnostic reasoning (P = 0.670, P = 0.198, and P = 0.195)."
