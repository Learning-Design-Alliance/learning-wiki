## Evidence

### Dowdall 2020

Dowdall, N., Melendez-Torres, G. J., Murray, L., Gardiner, F., Harrtford, L. & Cooper, P.J. (2020). Shared picture book reading interventions for child language development: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Child Development, 91(2). https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13225

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Robust variance estimation meta-analysis of six studies providing 15 effect sizes from 481 caregivers, using measures such as facilitating verbalizations, sensitivity and reciprocity. Found "a large sized effect in improving caregiver book sharing competence" with d=1.01, p=0.009.

> "The pooled result of the robust variance estimation meta-analysis provided evidence (p=0.009) for interactive book sharing interventions having a large sized effect in improving caregiver book sharing competence when compared to controls (d=1.01; CI: 0.40, 1.63; df: 4.79); tau-squared = 0.29."
