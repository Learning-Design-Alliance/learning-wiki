## Evidence

### Solaini L. 2023

Solaini L., Cavaliere D., Rocco G., Avanzolini A., Di Pietrantonio D., Ercolani G. (2023). Differences in the learning curve of robotic transabdominal preperitoneal inguinal hernia repair according to surgeon's robotic experience. Hernia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10029-023-02846-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CUSUM analysis of skin-to-skin operative time in a retrospective cohort of 124 robotic TAPP repairs (Forlì, 2020–2022) showed the trainee's learning phase ended at the "20th procedure", with improvement more evident from the 51st.

> "The surgeon in training with the robotic platform showed a learning phase till the 20th procedure followed by a gradual improvement in performances which are more evident from the 51st procedure"

### Solaini L. 2023 (2)

Solaini L., Cavaliere D., Rocco G., Avanzolini A., Di Pietrantonio D., Ercolani G. (2023). Differences in the learning curve of robotic transabdominal preperitoneal inguinal hernia repair according to surgeon's robotic experience. Hernia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10029-023-02846-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the same retrospective CUSUM analysis (61 procedures by the expert robotic surgeon), the learning phase lasted till the 35th procedure, "after which a constant decrease of operative time was recorded till the last procedure included".

> "Differently, the expert surgeon showed a learning phase till the 35th procedure after which a constant decrease of operative time was recorded till the last procedure included"
