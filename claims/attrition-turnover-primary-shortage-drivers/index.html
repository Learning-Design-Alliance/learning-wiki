Carver-Thomas 2020¶

Carver-Thomas, D., Kini, T., & Burns, D. (2020). Sharpening the divide: How California's teacher shortages expand inequality. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/sharp-divide-california-teacher-shortages

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The report's analysis of district turnover rates finds an association between turnover and student poverty; it also notes roughly 1 in 10 districts has turnover rates of 25% or more while nearly 1 in 10 has rates under 5%.

"Districts with higher turnover rates also have greater proportions of students from low-income families compared to those with the lowest turnover rates."
