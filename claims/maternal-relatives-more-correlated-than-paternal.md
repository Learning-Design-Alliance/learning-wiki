Comparison of maternal versus paternal relative categories in the national-test correlation dataset. The article reports "about 20% higher" for maternal half siblings consistently across all eight tests; 1st and 2nd cousin maternal differences are significant but smaller.

> "Consistently, across all the eight separate national tests, the correlations between maternal half siblings are about 20% higher than those for paternal pairs."
