## Evidence

### Hoyt-Austin A 2025

Hoyt-Austin A, Sadovnikova A, Muñoz S, Donohue LT, Tancredi DJ, Kair LR. (2025). High-fidelity telesimulation to teach lactation skills to primary care residents, a cluster randomized educational trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08118-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-differences analysis of mean change scores in the cluster randomized trial. Self-efficacy was rated on a 6-point Likert scale; all participants rose from a pre-test mean of 2.6 (±0.8) to about 4.1 at 3 months, described as "Somewhat confident".

> "Mean change scores from enrollment to 3 months showed an increase in clinical lactation self-efficacy for both groups with a gain of 1.6 (± 0.9) for control and 1.3 (± 0.8) for intervention; however, the effect size (per-protocol) of 0.3 (-0.5, 1.1) showed no significant difference between groups."
