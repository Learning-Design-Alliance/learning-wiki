`q2 i?` Pearson correlations between group membership (experimental=1, comparison=2) and the dependent variables were weakly negative: r = -.25 for knowledge test score and r = -.21 for perceived interest. [→ Slussareff 2016](#slussareff-2016)

## Evidence

### Slussareff 2016

Slussareff, M. and Boháčková, P. (2016). Students as Game Designers vs. 'Just' Players: Comparison of Two Different Approaches to Location-Based Games Implementation into School Curricula. Digital Education Review, 29, 284-297. http://greav.ub.edu/der

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis preceding the ANOVAs in the same quasi-experiment. The article reports "Weak negative correlations" with r = -.25 for knowledge test score and r = -.21 for subjectively apperceived interest; per-question coefficients ranged from negligible to strong negative relationships.

> "Weak negative correlations (experimental group labelled as 1 and comparison group labelled as 2) were found with Pearson coefficients (r) -.25 (for knowledge test score) and -.21 (for the subjectively apperceived interest), p < .00."
