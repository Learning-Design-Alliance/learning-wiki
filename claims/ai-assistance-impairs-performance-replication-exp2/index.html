Evidence¶

Grace Liu 2026¶

Grace Liu, Brian Christian, Tsvetomira Dumbalska, Michiel A. Bakker, Rachit Dubey. (2026). AI Assistance Reduces Persistence and Hurts Independent Performance. Published as a conference paper at COLM 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04721 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.04721

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Replication RCT (667 recruited, 585 analyzed) adding a pretest for exclusions and a control sidebar with pretest solutions to remove interface asymmetry. Solve-rate impairment replicated, "Cohen's d=− 0.19"; exclusion rates were similar across conditions (11.7% vs 12.9%).

"Participants in the AI condition had a lower solve rate (mean 0.71, s.d. 0.36) than participants in the control condition (mean 0.77, s.d. 0.32; t(583) =− 2.33, P= 0.020; Cohen's d=− 0.19, 95% CI [−0.36,−0.03])."
