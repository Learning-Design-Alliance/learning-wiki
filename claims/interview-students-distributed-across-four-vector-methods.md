## Evidence

### Jeffrey M. Hawkins 2009

Jeffrey M. Hawkins, John R. Thompson, and Michael C. Wittmann. (2009). Students’ Consistency of Graphical Vector Addition Method on 2-D Vector Addition Tasks. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings (venue not printed in text). [doi:10.1063/1.3266704](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266704)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview observation of the eight students' solutions across the ten gridded and non-gridded questions, categorized by method (Table 2 lists A-C head-to-tail, D-E components, F bisector, G general direction, H various). The head-to-tail users "used the grid to more accurately redraw the vectors into a head-to-tail arrangement."

> "Three students (A, B and C) used the head-to-tail method to answer all 10 questions. When the grid was added, the students all used the grid to more accurately redraw the vectors into a head-to-tail arrangement"
