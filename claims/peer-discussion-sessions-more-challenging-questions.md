## Evidence

### Lewin JD 2016

Lewin JD, Vinson EL, Stetzer MR, Smith MK. (2016). A Campus-Wide Investigation of Clicker Implementation: The Status of Peer Discussion in STEM Classes. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-10-0224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observer feedback survey administered in Spring 2015 for 41 of the 80 clicker class sessions; observers rated question difficulty from voting results. The finding is descriptive, based on observer judgment rather than a statistical test.

> "The survey responses suggested that Peer Discussion class sessions more commonly included questions that were challenging for students when compared with Individual Thinking and Alternative Collaboration class sessions."
