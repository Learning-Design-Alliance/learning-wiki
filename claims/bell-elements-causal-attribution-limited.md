> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Because the prior evaluation estimated overall program impacts only, the case study cannot identify which specific elements produced the positive effects. [→ Jeffrey Capizzano 2007](#jeffrey-capizzano-2007)

## Evidence

### Jeffrey Capizzano 2007

Jeffrey Capizzano, Kendra Bischoff, Nicola Woodroffe, Duncan Chaplin. (2007). Ingredients of a Successful Summer Learning Program: A Case Study of the Building Educated Leaders for Life (BELL) Accelerated Learning Summer Program. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/ingredients-of-a-successful-summer-learning-program-a-case-study-of-the-building-educated-leaders-for-life-bell-accelerated-learning-summer-program

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Authors' stated limitation of the evaluation design: impacts were estimated "of the program overall," so element-level causal attribution is not possible. This is the authors' own interpretive statement, not a tested result.

> "As with most evaluation studies our study only estimated impacts of the program overall. This means that we cannot distinguish which elements caused the positive impacts."
