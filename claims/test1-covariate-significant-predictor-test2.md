## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Noroozi 2012

Noroozi, O., Busstra, M. C., Mulder, M., Biemans, H. J. A., Tobi, H., Geelen, A., van't Veer, P., Chizari, M. (2012). Online discussion compensates for suboptimal timing of supportive information presentation in a digitally supported learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev (2012) 60:193-221. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-011-9217-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA in the same sequential experiment: test 1 mean quality score served as covariate and test 2 mean quality score as dependent variable. The article reports F (1, 79) = 27.20; p < 0.01 for the covariate, indicating strong stability of individual knowledge construction quality across phases.

> "The covariate, the quality of knowledge construction as measured by test 1, had a signiﬁcant effect on the quality of knowledge construction as measured by test 2: F (1, 79) = 27.20; p\ 0.01."
