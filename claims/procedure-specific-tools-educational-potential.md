### Tesfai FM 2024

Tesfai FM, Nagi J, Morrison I, Boal M, Olaitan A, Chandrasekaran D, Stoyanov D, Lanceley A, Francis N. (2024). Objective assessment tools in laparoscopic or robotic‐assisted gynecological surgery: A systematic review. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14840

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's conclusion section states "procedure‐specific tools hold great educational potential" relative to global tools. Supporting results: content validity was demonstrated in 12/13 (92.3%) procedure-specific tool studies versus 8/13 (61.5%) global tools, and four of 26 manual tools plus one APM provided benchmark scores, none applied for benchmarking or credentialing in training curricula.

> "While global assessment tools remain readily available, procedure‐specific tools hold great educational potential."
