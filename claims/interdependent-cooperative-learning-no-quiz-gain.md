## Evidence

### Premo Joshua 2018

Premo Joshua, Cavagnetto Andy, Davis William B. (2018). Promoting Collaborative Classrooms: The Impacts of Interdependent Cooperative Learning on Undergraduate Interactions and Achievement. CBE—Life Sciences Education 17(2). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-08-0176

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of weekly quiz scores (n = 435) from 20 laboratory classrooms; 18.3% of quiz variance was between classrooms, and TA identity, academic ability, and science interest accounted for most of it before condition was entered. The article reports that "condition did not predict significant variance in quiz scores."

> "With these controls in place, condition did not predict significant variance in quiz scores."
