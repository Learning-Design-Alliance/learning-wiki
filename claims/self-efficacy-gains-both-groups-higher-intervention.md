## Evidence

### Wilkes Michael S. 2017

Wilkes Michael S., Day Frank C., Fancher Tonya L., McDermott Haley, Lehman Erik, Bell Robert A., Green Michael J. (2017). Increasing confidence and changing behaviors in primary care providers engaged in genetic counselling. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0982-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial measuring self-efficacy (28 items, two domains, 1–5 scale) pre and post via linear mixed effects model. Table 3 shows clinical skills self-efficacy change 0.8 (intervention) vs 0.6 (control), between p = 0.02; genetic knowledge self-efficacy change 1.0 vs 0.8, between p = 0.02. No standardized effect size printed.

> "After participation, PCPs' self-efficacy improved in both groups for both domains (P < 0.01), with modest but significantly higher improvement in the Intervention Group (Table 3)."
