Pınar 2020¶

Pınar, E. & Yıldız, A. (2020). The Effect of Journal Writing, One of the “Writing to Learn” Activities, on Achievement and Science Attitude in the Fourth Grade Science Course. Journal of Current Researches on Social Sciences, 10(1), 179-196. https://doi.org/10.26579/jocress.354

Quasi-experimental comparison of 24 experimental and 20 control fourth-graders using Mann Whitney U on Science Course Achievement Test post-test scores; the article reports “a statistically significant difference in favour of experimental group students (U=129.50; p<.05)”.
