# Novice trainees' confidence in laparoscopic skill rose from pre-test to post-test within Group A after training with AEDS real-time feedback

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Group A's average confidence rose from 2.4 (SD = 1.05) to 3.6 (SD = 1.14), and the median rose from 3 to 4, after AEDS training (Wilcoxon p < 0.001). [→ Luo Yitian 2025](#luo-yitian-2025)

## Evidence

### Luo Yitian 2025

Luo Yitian, Wang Jingjie, Yan Zongting, He Jingjing, Fu Liye, Wang Shenghan, Han Ying, Fu Yaoyu, Wang Xiandi, Li Kang, Yin Rong, Pu Dan. (2025). Enhancing laparoscopic surgery training: a comparative study of traditional models and automated error detection system. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07242-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test on Group A's (n = 20) 5-point Likert confidence ratings, used because Shapiro-Wilk indicated non-normal scores (before: p = 0.0024, after: p = 0.0071). The proportion rating "Relatively confident" or "Very confident" rose from 5% to 50%. No effect size is printed.

> "the average confidence level of participants before the training was 2.4 (SD = 1.05), which increased to 3.6 (SD = 1.14) after the training sessions. This significant improvement (p < 0.001) in average confidence underscores the positive psychological impact"
