### Jung 2013

Jung, M., Hartman, P., Smith, T., & Wallace, S. (2013). The Effectiveness of Teaching Number Relationships in Preschool. International Journal of Instruction, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085361

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear model (Model 5) on the same 73-child sample testing gender as a moderator of the intervention effect, with pretest and age as covariates. No effect sizes were printed for these null results.

> "a linear model (Model 5 in Table 5) showed no statistically significant main effect for gender ( F(1, 67) = 0.10, p = .76) and no significant Treatment × Gender interaction effect (F(1, 67) = 0.28, p = .60)."
