## Evidence

### Watson Amanda 2022

Watson Amanda, Dumuid Dorothea, Maher Carol, Olds Tim. (2022). Associations between meeting 24-hour movement guidelines and academic achievement in Australian primary school-aged children. Journal of Sport and Health Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2020.12.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the accelerometry model (objectively measured MVPA and sleep plus self-reported screen time; n = 927), the numeracy omnibus ANCOVA was not significant (F(7, 915) = 0.80, p = 0.58), and no post-hoc literacy comparison reached significance. Equivalence was not tested; no effect sizes are printed for these null findings.

> "Post-hoc analyses showed when assessed by accelerometry, there were no differences in literacy achievement for any movement behavior combination when compared with the "meets no guideline" condition."
