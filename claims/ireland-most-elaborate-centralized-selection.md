## Evidence

### Hagelsteen K 2021

Hagelsteen K, Pedersen H, Bergenfelz A, Mathieu C. (2021). Different approaches to selection of surgical trainees in the European Union. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02779-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Observational study-visits (2018, 2019) to the Royal College of Surgeons Ireland observing selection procedures, with fieldnotes analysed by two sociologists and four surgeons. The article reports "around 100 candidates compete for 60 positions" and four eight-minute MMIs with two independent assessors rating on a Likert scale.

> "Ireland has the most elaborate centralized selection process, and the only country formally testing technical skills and aptitude."

### Hagelsteen K 2021 (2)

Hagelsteen K, Pedersen H, Bergenfelz A, Mathieu C. (2021). Different approaches to selection of surgical trainees in the European Union. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02779-5

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Attributed report from the RCSI program director via personal communication, not an independent measurement; the article also notes attrition rates are difficult to compare across systems due to lack of decentralized data.

> "A further systemic benefit according to the RCSI program director is that the attrition rate has been reduced substantially after this rigorous process for selection has been applied in tandem with follow-up on skills during the first two trainee years."
