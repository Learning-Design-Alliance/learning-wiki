# Open ChatGPT support increases college students' final decision confidence relative to independent decision-making, and reflection attenuates the boost

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students in the open ChatGPT condition reported higher final confidence than students in the independent condition, with reflection producing a smaller increase. [→ Ren S 2026](#ren-s-2026)

## Evidence

### Ren S 2026

Ren S (2026) College students’ metacognitive awareness of generative-AI reliance: an experimental study of decision confidence and attribution bias. Front. Psychol. 17:1926110. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1926110

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized three-condition experiment (114 per condition) on reasoning and information-evaluation tasks. The omnibus condition effect on final confidence was significant, F(2,339) = 10.34, p < 0.001, "η2p = 0.06", with the open ChatGPT condition exceeding the independent condition by Mdiff = 7.7 points.

> "The condition eﬀect on ﬁnal conﬁdence was signiﬁcant,F(2,339) = 10.34,p < 0.001, η2p = 0.06.Holm-adjustedpairwisecontrastsshowedthatstudentsintheopen ChatGPT condition reported higher ﬁnal conﬁdence than students in the independent condition (Mdiﬀ = 7.7, 95% CI [4.37, 11.03], adjustedp < 0.001)."
