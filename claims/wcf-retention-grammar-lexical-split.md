# Grammatical accuracy gains from written corrective feedback are retained at delayed post-test, but lexical accuracy gains are not

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Grammatical errors did not differ significantly between post-test (M=3.31) and delayed post-test (M=2.79) in the experimental group. [→ Al-Hazzani and Altalhab 2018](#al-hazzani-and-altalhab-2018)
`q2 i?` Lexical errors rose significantly from post-test (M=1.41) to delayed post-test (M=3.59) in the experimental group. [→ Al-Hazzani and Altalhab 2018 (2)](#al-hazzani-and-altalhab-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Al-Hazzani and Altalhab 2018

Nahla Al-Hazzani, Sultan Altalhab. (2018). Can Explicit Written Corrective Feedback Develop Grammatical and Lexical Accuracy of Saudi EFL Learners? International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.6n.4p.16

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired Sample t-test within the experimental group (n=29) on grammatical mistakes across post and delayed post-tests; the article reports t=1.192, p=0.238, which "is more than (0.05), indicating that this is not statistically significant". No effect size is printed.

> "regarding grammatical errors, no statistically significant differences between the errors of the experimental group students either in the post or the post-delayed tests were found, with an average number of errors amounting to (3.31) for the post-test and (2.79) for the post-delayed test."

### Al-Hazzani and Altalhab 2018 (2)

Nahla Al-Hazzani, Sultan Altalhab. (2018). Can Explicit Written Corrective Feedback Develop Grammatical and Lexical Accuracy of Saudi EFL Learners? International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.6n.4p.16

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired Sample t-test within the experimental group (n=29) on lexical mistakes: post M=1.41 (SD=1.40) versus delayed post M=3.59 (SD=2.63); the article reports t=-3.930, p=0.000, favoring the post-test. No effect size is printed.

> "The results in Table 4 indicated that there were statisti - cally significant differences for vocabulary used by the stu - dents in the experimental group, both in the post-test and the post-delayed test."
