Kingpum et al. 2015¶

Peerasak Kingpum, Chaiyot Ruangsuwan and Sumalee Chaicharoen. (2015). A development of a collaborative blended learning model to enhance learning achievement and thinking ability of undergraduate students at the Institute of Physical Education. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2350

Two-group time-series experiment with 30 experimental students; Table 2 reports pretest mean 29.67 (SD 3.77) and posttest mean 48.03 (SD 3.92), t = -17.93, p = .000. The article reports the posttest "was 48.03(SD = 3.92) at 80.05%".
