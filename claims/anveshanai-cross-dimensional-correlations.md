Cross-dimensional correlation analysis (Table 2 and Figure 7 heatmap) of the 10,845-entry dataset computing Cramér's V between annotation dimensions. The paper reports the strong category–difficulty value of 0.596 and weak Bloom-level correlations of "0.166-0.172", interpreting them as supporting independent cognitive assessment.

> "The strong category-difficulty correlation (0.596) validates systematic annotation patterns, demonstrating that certain subject domains naturally exhibit higher complexity. Conversely, the weak correlations between Bloom taxonomy and other dimensions (0.166-0.172)"
