## Evidence

### DiPietro Joan 2019

DiPietro Joan, Kelemen Arpad, Liang Yulan, Sik-Lanyi Cecilia. (2019). Computer- and Robot-Assisted Therapies to Aid Social and Intellectual Functioning of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina55080440

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Study at a Midwest university speech-language clinic of children with speech language impairments age 2–8 (six of 18 had ASD), comparing a computerized feedback system against a traditional noncomputerized pacing board and an active control. "58%, 60%, and 8–9 words, respectively, for the VocSyl group" exceeded both comparison arms.

> "Results demonstrated 58%, 60%, and 8–9 words, respectively, for the VocSyl group; 50%, 50%, and 7–8 words for the pacing board group; and 23%, 22%, and 3–4 words for the active control group."
