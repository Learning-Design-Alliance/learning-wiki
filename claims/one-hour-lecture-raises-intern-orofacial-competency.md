### Aljohani Suad 2026

Aljohani Suad, Alhamed Sana, Farag Arwa M, Aljohani Salha R, Jazzar Ahoud, AlDehlawi Hebah, Almohammadi Abdullah, Alghamdi Malik, Aljohani Khalid. (2026). BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-07662-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post-follow-up study of 91 dental interns (of 161 invited) completing a validated 40-item questionnaire at three timepoints. The article reports "a relative improvement of approximately 13%" with p < 0.001; no standardised effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Following the intervention, overall competency scores significantly increased (from 78% at baseline to 91% immediately after the lecture), with a relative improvement of approximately 13% being observed (p < 0.001); moreover, the scores remained 8.5% higher than those at baseline at the two-month evaluation."
