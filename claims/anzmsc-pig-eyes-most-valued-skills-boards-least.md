`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney non-parametric testing of station rankings from the University of Sydney Student Evaluation Forms completed by 227 ANZMSC students. The article reports "z = 6.06, p < 0.01" for the pig-eye versus skills-board contrast and no significant difference among the remaining seven stations.

> "The station ranked as being the most valuable learning experience was corneal suturing practise on pig eyes, and the least was the suturing on skills boards (z = 6.06, p < 0.01). There was no significant difference in the ranking of the remaining seven stations assessed."
