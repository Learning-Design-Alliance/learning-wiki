Meta-analysis of four RCTs (N = 274) found "3D + PBL teaching led to higher teaching satisfaction". GRADE certainty was very low; no significant heterogeneity was found (I2 = 49%).

> "The meta-analysis indicated that 3D + PBL teaching led to higher teaching satisfaction (SMD = 0.85, 95% CI: 0.49–1.22, P < 0.00001; see Fig. 8)."
