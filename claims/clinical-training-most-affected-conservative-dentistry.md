## Evidence

### Hattar Susan 2021

Hattar Susan, AlHadidi Abeer, Sawair Faleh A., Alraheam Islam Abd, El-Ma'aita Ahmad, Wahab Fouad Kadim. (2021). Impact of COVID-19 pandemic on dental education: online experience and practice expectations among dental students at the University of Jordan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02584-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multi-answer survey item on experiences most affected by quarantine (Fig. 1). "Clinical training was the most affected experience reported by students (86.5%)"; regular lectures were missed more by 4th years (p = 0.008), case presentations more by 5th years (p < 0.001).

> "Clinical training was the most affected experience reported by students (86.5%)."

### Hattar Susan 2021 (2)

Hattar Susan, AlHadidi Abeer, Sawair Faleh A., Alraheam Islam Abd, El-Ma'aita Ahmad, Wahab Fouad Kadim. (2021). Impact of COVID-19 pandemic on dental education: online experience and practice expectations among dental students at the University of Jordan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02584-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-answer survey item on subspecialties most negatively affected (Fig. 2). "Conservative dentistry (73.2%) followed by prosthodontics (69.4%)" ranked highest; several subspecialty differences varied significantly by year.

> "Conservative dentistry (73.2%) followed by prosthodontics (69.4%) were the two most negatively affected subspecialties while orthodontics (18.4%) and oral diagnosis (21.0%) were the two least negatively affected subspecialties."
