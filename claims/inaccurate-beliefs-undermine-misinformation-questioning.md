### Carita Kiili 2026

Carita Kiili, Ivar Bråten, and Julie Coiro. (2026). The framework of prior knowledge in online credibility evaluation. Educational Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2026.2717081

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Kulju et al. (2024), a study of second-year pre-service teachers in which stronger belief in learning styles made it harder to question online texts with learning-styles misinformation despite weak supporting evidence. No effect size is printed.

> "the more strongly pre-service teachers believed in learning styles–a persistent myth in education–the more difficult it was for them to question the credibility of online texts containing misinformation about learning styles, even though the evidence presented for the inaccurate claims about learning styles was weak."
