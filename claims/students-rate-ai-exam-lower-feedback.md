`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-point Likert questionnaire completed by all 126 students (63 per group); the five-dimension scale showed acceptable reliability (Cronbach's α > 0.7 per dimension) and structural validity (five factors explaining 70.45% of variance). No significant group differences appeared on Clarity of Language (p = 0.586) or Professionalism (p = 0.205).

> "The AI group rated significantly lower than the human group in "Difficulty Adaptability" (AI = 3.53 ± 1.03 [95% CI: 3.28–3.78]; Human = 4.19 ± 0.76 [95% CI: 4.00–4.38]), "Knowledge Coverage" (AI = 3.67 ± 0.89 [95% CI: 3.45–3.89]; Human = 4.19 ± 0.72 [95% CI: 4.01–4.37]), and "Learning Inspiration" (AI = 3.79 ± 0.90 [95% CI: 3.57–4.01]; Human = 4.25 ± 0.67 [95% CI: 4.09–4.41]; p < 0.001; Table 7)"
