## Evidence

### Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi 2025

Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi (2025). Consistency between self-reported and log data to understand students’ experience of learning in flipped classrooms. Scientific Reports 15, 34369. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17052-8

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical cluster analysis of self-reported deep and surface approach scale means among 271 undergraduates in a flipped computing course retained two clusters: "One cluster had 71 students, who reported using more deep but less surface approaches" and another of 199 students with the opposite profile; the deep-scale difference carried η2 = 0.31.

> "One cluster had 71 students, who reported using more deep but less surface approaches; whereas another cluster had 199 students, who reported using less deep but more surface approaches."

### Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi 2025 (2)

Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi (2025). Consistency between self-reported and log data to understand students’ experience of learning in flipped classrooms. Scientific Reports 15, 34369. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17052-8

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVAs in the same self-report cluster analysis confirmed significant differences between the two clusters on both scales, with η2 = 0.31 for the deep scale and η2 = 0.34 for the surface scale, both p < 0.001.

> "One-way ANOVAs found that students in the two clusters differed significantly on the deep (F(1, 269) = 117.38, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.31) and surface approaches (F(1, 269) = 141.01, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.34)."
