An MSU study cited in the report found that "Michigan districts use more than $500 per pupil from general education funds, on average" to cover special education costs, exceeding $1,000 per pupil in some districts. Under partial reimbursement, districts shoulder most special education funding responsibility.

"An MSU study found that in order to fully fund special education costs, Michigan districts use more than $500 per pupil from general education funds, on average."
