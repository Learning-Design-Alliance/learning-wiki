### Stathas SpiroAnthony 2024

Stathas SpiroAnthony, Baribeau Vincent, Kutch Maximillian, Zapata Isain, Thomson Andrew, Mason Nena Lundgreen. (2024). Teaching medical students ultrasound-guided needle aspiration of synthetic cysts: effect of a formalin-embalmed cadaver simulation model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05907-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Skills testing after a 20-min one-on-one workshop in a single-center non-randomized interventional study of 30 first-year medical students. Participants "took an average of 161.5 ± 12.4 s to independently image and fully drain a cyst"; the article separately reports two of the thirty students did not pass within the 5-min limit.

> "Participants took an average of 161.5 ± 12.4 s to independently image and fully drain a cyst during the skills assessment. Full drainage was defined as aspiration of approximately 15-20 cc of fluid by volume into the syringe as confirmed by the instructor administering the assessment."
