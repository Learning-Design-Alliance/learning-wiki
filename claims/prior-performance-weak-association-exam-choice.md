## Evidence

### Kozikowski A 2026

Kozikowski A, Bruza-Augatis M, Ritsema T, Sasek C, Jiang Y, Dallas A, Goodman J. (2026). Factors associated with PAs' choice of the traditional PANRE over the PANRE-LA. JAAPA. https://doi.org/10.1097/01.JAA.0000000000000411

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Independent-samples t-test comparison in the full cohort (Figure 2A); the printed means were 520.5 versus 512.6 (P < .001), a mean difference the discussion puts at "only 7.9 points". The difference was not statistically significant among PAs younger than 35. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Overall, PAs who chose the traditional PANRE scored slightly higher on their first attempt at the PANCE than those who chose the PANRE-LA (520.5 vs 512.6; P < .001)."
