## Evidence

### Jill S. Cannon 2006

Jill S. Cannon, Alison Jacknowitz, Gary Painter. (2006). Is Full Better than Half? Examining the Longitudinal Effects of Full-day Kindergarten Attendance. Journal of Policy Analysis and Management, Vol. 25, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.1002/pam.20174

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

County fixed effects models on ECLS-K math and reading scores, displayed for math and reading only because fixed-effects probits required dropping large portions of the sample. Dropping private school students made the third-grade math coefficient insignificant, suggesting potential selection into private schools.

> "the inclusion of the fixed effects in the whole sample does not change the kindergarten or the first grade results. The only coefficients to change are the coefficients on math scores in third grade, suggesting that students who attend a full-day kindergarten have lower scores in math than their half-day peers."
