Systematic review and directed content analysis of 46 experimental comparison studies of online motivational design strategies in health professions education. The review reports that "intrinsic value beliefs in 23 of the 46 (50%) studies" were the most frequently targeted construct, ahead of extrinsic value beliefs (n=9), competence and control beliefs (n=6), social connectedness (n=4), and autonomy (n=2).

> "Studies investigated motivational design strategies that targeted intrinsic value beliefs in 23 of the 46 (50%) studies, extrinsic value beliefs in 9 (20%) studies, competence and control beliefs in 6 (13%) studies, social connectedness in 4 (9%) studies, and autonomy in 2 (4%) studies."
