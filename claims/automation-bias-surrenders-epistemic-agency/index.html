Theoretical argument in the article's section on automation bias and the fluency heuristic: the authors argue that confident, fluent LLM output triggers a coherence heuristic in learners, and that 'the learner stops feeling responsible for verifying the truth of the output'. No empirical data are reported for this claim.

"This leads to a surrender of epistemic agency, where the learner stops feeling responsible for verifying the truth of the output."
