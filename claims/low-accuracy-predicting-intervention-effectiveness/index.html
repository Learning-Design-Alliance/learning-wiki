Evidence¶

Bosch 2021¶

Bosch, Nigel. (2021). Identifying Supportive Student Factors for Mindset Interventions: A Two-model Machine Learning Approach. Computers & Education, 167, 104190. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104190

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the NSLM secondary analysis, model 2's cross-validated accuracy for predicting intervention effectiveness was low: "Mean r was small (.094) but significantly above chance (p < .001)". The authors attribute this to the inherent difficulty of predicting a small intervention effect.

"Mean r was small (.094) but significantly above chance (p < .001), indicating that the variables considered in this study predicted intervention efficacy even after controlling for their relationships to GPA in the control condition."
