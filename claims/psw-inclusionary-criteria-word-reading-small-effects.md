## Subclaims
`q3 i1` For word reading, XBA normal-ability status and deficits in crystallized intelligence and short-term memory were significant predictors, with η2 range .008 to .018; no criterion predicted fluency or comprehension. [→ Miciak Jeremy 2016](#miciak-jeremy-2016)

## Evidence

### Miciak Jeremy 2016

Miciak Jeremy, Williams Jacob L., Taylor W. Pat, Cirino Paul T., Fletcher Jack M., Vaughn Sharon. (2016). Do Processing Patterns of Strengths and Weaknesses Predict Differential Treatment Response? Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(6), 898-909. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000096

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Dichotomized status on each inclusionary criterion entered into ANCOVAs with pretest covariate for 206 fourth graders. The article prints an effect size of "η2 range .008 to .018" for these word-reading predictors; elsewhere it states "The largest eta squared was .018 for crystallized intelligence deficits predicting word reading", versus 68.5% of variance explained by pretest.

> "For the XBA, “normal” ability status was a significant predictor after accounting for pretest performance. Deficits in crystallized intelligence and short-term memory were also significant predictors (η2 range .008 to .018)."
