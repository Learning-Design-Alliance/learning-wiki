# Structural quality of integration (GCsim) increases substantially at delayed writing three days later

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` GCsim was significantly higher at delayed writing than at after-reading or revised writing in both studies. [→ Ziqian Wei 2025](#ziqian-wei-2025)

## Evidence

### Ziqian Wei 2025

Ziqian Wei, Shuting Yin, Roy B. Clariana, Xuqian Chen. (2025). The influence of pre-reading purpose and extra-textual networks with summary writing on multiple document concept network integration: a replication of Wei et al. (2024). Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10508-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Study 1 found a significant main effect of writing stages on GCsim, F(2,196) = 140.33, p < .001, η² = 0.371, with delayed writing substantially higher than earlier stages.

> "Results of multiple comparisons under Bonferroni correction showed significantly higher GCsim in delayed writing (M = 0.48, SD = 0.28) than both after-reading writing (M = 0.20, SD =.08), t(98) = 11.89, p <.001, Cohen's d = 1.62, and revised writing (M = 0.20, SD =.08), t(98) = 11.82, p <.001, Cohen's d = 1.61"
