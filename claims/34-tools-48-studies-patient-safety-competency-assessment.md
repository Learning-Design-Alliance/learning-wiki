## Evidence

### Okuyama Ayako 2011

Okuyama Ayako, Martowirono Kartinie, Bijnen Bart. (2011). Assessing the patient safety competencies of healthcare professionals: a systematic review. BMJ Quality & Safety. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3203526/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review of six databases plus hand searches to December 2010; the review reports that "48 articles were selected, and 34 unique assessment tools were identified". Table 1 characterises the studies: 22 (46%) from the USA, 34 (71%) single-institution, and 45 of 48 (94%) published after 2005.

> "In total, 48 articles were selected, and 34 unique assessment tools were identified (table 1)."
