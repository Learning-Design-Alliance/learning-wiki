Helena Salminen, Nabil Zary, Karin Björklund, Eva Toth-Pal, Charlotte Leanderson. (2014). Virtual Patients in Primary Care: Developing a Reusable Model That Fosters Reflective Practice and Clinical Reasoning. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2616

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content analysis of the three group interviews (n=14) in the Technical Design theme. Students described the structure as "being easy to follow" and appreciated moving forward and backward; they also saw the model could, with adaptation, be used in teaching on all levels of the medical program.

> "The students described the model structure as being easy to follow. They appreciated the possibility to move both forward and backward in the model."
