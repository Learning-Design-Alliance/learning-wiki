## Evidence

### Antoniou Mark 2016

Antoniou Mark, Ettlinger Marc, Wong Patrick C. M. (2016). Complexity, Training Paradigm Design, and the Contribution of Memory Subsystems to Grammar Learning. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0158812

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise multiple regression on the Experiment 1 baseline group found that "for the complex grammar the only significant predictor was declarative memory"; the Experiment 2 no-feedback group replicated this (β = .690, p < .001). The authors link this to declarative memory storing morphophonological paradigms for analogy-based generalization.

> "for the complex grammar the only significant predictor was declarative memory, B = .837, β = .669, R2 = .448, adjusted R2 = .431, p < .001"
