### Pinto-Escalona Tania 2024

Effects of a school-based karate intervention on academic achievement, psychosocial functioning, and physical fitness: A multi-country cluster randomized controlled trial. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

> "a non-significant trend toward a beneficial effect was also observed for flexibility (B = 9.16, d = 0.24, p = 0.056). No significant between-group differences were found for the remaining outcomes."
