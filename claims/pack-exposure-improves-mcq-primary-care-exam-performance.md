## Evidence

### Mash R 2017

Mash R, Pather M, Rhode H, Fairall L. (2017). Evaluating the effect of the Practical Approach to Care Kit on teaching medical students primary care: Quasi-experimental study. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v9i1.1602

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 50 first-semester (control) versus 83 second-semester (intervention) sixth-year students using an independent samples t test on 30-item end-of-rotation MCQs aligned with PACK content. The article reports the mean grade was "significantly better (p = 0.03)" with PACK.

> "The mean grade for the MCQ examination was significantly better (p = 0.03) in those with the PACK (72.3%, SD 8.6) compared to those without (68.8%, SD 9.6)."
