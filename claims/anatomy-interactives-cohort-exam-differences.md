## Evidence

### Gentges Joshua 2026

Gentges Joshua, Pascoe Michael, Al-Redouan Azzat, Sumner Caroline BS, Case Sami L PhD, Franklin Samuel PhD, Platt Kristen PhD. (2026). Interactive, Image-Based Modules as a Complement to Prosection-Based Anatomy Laboratories: Multicohort Evaluation. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/85028

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Cohort comparison of MD examination scores (2023 cohort without modules, 2024 cohort with modules) analyzed with Mann-Whitney U tests; effect size assessed by rank-biserial correlation. Examination 1 written medians were 82% versus 86%, P<.001, with printed effect size rrb = .28; the article describes "moderate effect sizes for the written portions and total examination scores."

> "Written portion	82 (74-88)	86 (79-94)	<.001	15,130	.28"

### Gentges Joshua 2026 (2)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Same two-cohort Mann-Whitney comparison with rank-biserial effect sizes. Examination 3 written medians were 85% versus 92%, P<.001, printed effect size rrb = .41; total examination 3 difference also significant (P<.001, rrb = .30).

> "Written portion	85 (79.5-92)	92 (84-96)	<.001	12,420	.41"
