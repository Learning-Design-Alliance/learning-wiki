## Evidence

### Waddington 1995

Waddington, Tad S. H. (1995). Why Mastery Matters. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the American Educational Research Association. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED394982

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic techniques applied to Stevenson and Lee's multi-national battery of tests, collapsing data across operations and problem solving and across first and fifth grades, show a printed effect size of d = 0.78, i.e. "a standardized mean difference(d)of 0.78" favoring Japanese students.

> "Japanese students' math achievement has a standardized mean difference(d)of 0.78 better than their American counter-parts, with a 959s confidence interval of 0.70 to 0.90"
