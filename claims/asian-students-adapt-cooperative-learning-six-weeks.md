## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Tran 2012

Tran, V. D., & Lewis, R. (2012). The Effects of Jigsaw Learning on Students' Attitudes in a Vietnamese Higher Education Classroom. International Journal of Higher Education, 1(2), 9. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v1n2p9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The article draws this conclusion from the positive attitude findings across the six-week intervention, supporting prior studies (Biggs, 1996; Volet & Renshaw, 1996; Wong, 2004; Le, 2010; Thanh-Pham, 2011) reporting that Asian students are highly adaptive to different teaching styles. It argues that 'learning styles are not culturally-based but contextual.'

> "Despite the fact that all of these students were accustomed to the Confucian-heritage learning culture, or teacher-centered style of learning environment (Harman & Nguyen, 2010), they could adapt to this new style of learning in six weeks of instruction in an Asian learning context."
