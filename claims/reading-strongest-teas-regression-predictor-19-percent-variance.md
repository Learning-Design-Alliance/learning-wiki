## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Reading had the largest standardized regression coefficient (β = 0.193) and Math the smallest (β = 0.037), with all four β values significant at the .01 level and TEAS scores explaining about 19% of Fundamentals variance. [→ Yoo H 2025](#yoo-h-2025)

## Evidence

### Yoo H 2025

Yoo H, Zhou X, Phillips B. (2025). Predictive validity of preadmission assessment on early nursing academic readiness: impact of COVID-19. Nurse Educ. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000001891

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis with forward selection for the overall sample (Supplemental Digital Content, Table 1). Printed standardized coefficients: β = 0.193 for Reading and β = 0.037 for Math. The study reports "TEAS scores accounted for approximately 19% of the variance in the Fundamentals score," and Science added 6% additional variance at step 2 (ΔR2).

> "The β value was the largest for Reading (0.193), indicating that for each 1 percentage score increased in Reading, the predicted Fundamentals percentage score increased by approximately 0.193. Math had the least β value (0.037). Despite the differences across the 4 areas, all β values were significant at .01 level"
