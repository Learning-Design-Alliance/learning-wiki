Leckie, G., Parker, R., Goldstein, H., & Tilling, K. (2023). Mixed-Effects Location Scale Models for Joint Modeling School Value-Added Effects on the Mean and Variance of Student Achievement. [doi:10.3102/10769986231210808](https://doi.org/10.3102/10769986231210808)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model 7 adds student age, gender, first language, SEN, and FSM status to the mean and residual variance functions (ΔDIC = 7247 versus Model 3). The article reports variance is "higher for SEN and FSM students than for otherwise equal students" and lower for summer born, girls, Black, and Asian students, who "appear to perform in a more consistent fashion."

> "the residual variance and therefore variance in student adjusted achievement again increases with age 11 scores but is now also shown to be higher for SEN and FSM students than for otherwise equal students."
