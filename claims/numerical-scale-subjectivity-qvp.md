### Mårtensson S 2026

Mårtensson S, Eckerström J, Knez R, Sörman K, Larsson M. (2026). Tell us what you really think: undergraduate nursing students validation of the Questionnaire Virtual Patient - a qualitative think-aloud study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09738-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

During cognitive interviews, one student noted difficulty with 1–10 scales, saying a ten for her may not be a ten for someone else. The article reports this as "hesitation in assigning high numerical values despite positive experiences," identifying a limitation of fixed numerical scales.

> "the result provides a nuanced picture of the inherent subjectivity associated with such scaled response formats. For example, the hesitation in assigning high numerical values despite positive experiences, highlighting the limitations of quantitative scaling methods"
