## Evidence

### Karimi and Hamzavi 2017

Mehrnoosh Karimi, Raouf Hamzavi. (2017). The Effect of Flipped Model of Instruction on EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension: Learners' Attitudes in Focus. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 8(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.1p.95

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on posttest reading comprehension with pretest as covariate, 25 learners per group. Experimental mean was 21.20 (SD 3.58) versus control 12.72 (SD 1.98); the article reports a significant group effect with partial eta squared of .71, but no standardised effect size in d or g form is printed, so impact is coded null.
