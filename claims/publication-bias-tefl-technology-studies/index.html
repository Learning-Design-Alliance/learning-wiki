Publication bias assessment within the meta-analysis of 33 studies. Six studies showed negative effects, two reaching significance (Mohsen & Carol, 2021, g = −0.42; Vahid & Hassan, 2024, g = −0.31), both involving short durations (2–4 weeks) and small samples; outliers were retained and sensitivity analysis confirmed stability.
