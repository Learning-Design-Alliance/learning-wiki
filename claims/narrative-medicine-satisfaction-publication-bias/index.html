Evidence¶

Dai Dongjun 2026¶

Dai Dongjun, Fan Yuzhi, Zhang Wenbao, Wei Qichun. (2026). The effect of narrative medicine-based education on academic performance and humanistic communication in residency training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53552-x

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of 6 randomized controlled studies (192 vs 192 residents) reporting resident satisfaction as a binary outcome. The pooled effect size OR = 6.67 (95% CI 1.74 to 25.51) was significant (P < 0.001) with moderate heterogeneity (I2 = 50.9%).

"residents were more satisfied with the narrative medicine education than LBL (6 studies with 192 residents in narrative medicine group and 192 in LBL group, odds ratio [OR] = 6.67, 95% CI 1.74 to 25.51, P < 0.001; I2 = 50.9%, Supplementary Fig. 3)"
