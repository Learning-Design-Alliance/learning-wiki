Evidence¶

Muhamad Dah 2024¶

Muhamad Dah, N., Mat Noor, M. S. A., Kamarudin, M. Z., & Syed Abdul Azziz, S. S. (2024). The impacts of open inquiry on students' learning in science: A systematic literature review. Educational Research Review, 43. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100601

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Rahmat and Chanunan (2018), that "through open inquiry students gained higher metacognitive skills than through conventional models", with students defining problems and analysing work independently.

"According to Rahmat and Chanunan (2018), through open inquiry students gained higher metacognitive skills than through conventional models. As part of empowering their metacognitive skills, students had to define the problem observed and analyse what they had done independently"

Muhamad Dah 2024 (2)¶

Muhamad Dah, N., Mat Noor, M. S. A., Kamarudin, M. Z., & Syed Abdul Azziz, S. S. (2024). The impacts of open inquiry on students' learning in science: A systematic literature review. Educational Research Review, 43. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100601

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Yang et al. (2016), that students "significantly progressed in the performances of science inquiry, and divergent and convergent thinking"; the review presents this as empirical evidence for creative thinking through open inquiry.

"For example, in Yang et al.'s (2016) study, students significantly progressed in the performances of science inquiry, and divergent and convergent thinking due to open inquiry learning."

  • AI-supported inquiry-based learning did not produce a statistically significant improvement in students' critical problem-solving skills — related

  • Research-led creativity training for teachers enhances children's divergent thinking and moves teachers' conceptions closer to researchers' definitions — related
