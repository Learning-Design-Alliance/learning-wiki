## Evidence

### Ullah Himayat 2025

Ullah Himayat, Huma Sarwat, Naeem Lubna, Yunus Mohammed, Sarfraz Junaid. (2025). Bridging the gaps in problem-based learning: an evidence-based intervention in bachelor of medicine and surgery (MBBS) program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07003-2

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Pre-post comparison of an 18-question Quality Assessment Questionnaire completed by 129 students and 31 faculty before and after the intervention. The Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed a mean r value of -0.71 (P <.001), which the authors call a "huge effect size of the intervention".

> "The overall post-intervention QAQ score rose to 4.0 as compared to the pre-revision score, which was 2.7, indicating a 48.1% improvement in the PBL quality."
