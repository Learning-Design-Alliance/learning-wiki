## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Joyce Kelly Barreto Silva 2025

Joyce Kelly Barreto Silva, Thomaz Bittencourt Couto, Andreia Melo Coriolano, Alex Aquino, Júlio Cesar Martins Monte. (2025). Analysis of acquisition and retention of cardiopulmonary resuscitation skills according to training frequency. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2025AO1257

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Generalized estimation equation models (Gamma distribution) on the 43-student trial's longitudinal assessments; the printed mean ratios show "12% at 3 months (mean ratio [MR]=1.12; p=0.001)" and 10.1% at 6 months.

> "We observed an increase in overall performance with the use of a feedback-equipped device of 12% at 3 months (mean ratio [MR]=1.12; p=0.001) and 10.1% at 6 months compared to the baseline performance (MR=1.101; p<0.001)."

### Joyce Kelly Barreto Silva 2025 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same GEE analysis of the trial's longitudinal data; the compression-rate effect appeared at 6 months only, with "MR=1.086, p=0.026" at 6 months and a non-significant 6.6% increase at 3 months.

> "The compression rate showed a significant increase of 8.6% at 6 months compared to the baseline (MR=1.086, p=0.026); however, the increase of 6.6% at 3 months was not significant (MR=1.066, p=0.198)."
