## Evidence

### Chrispeels H. E. 2014

Chrispeels H. E., Klosterman M. L., Martin J. B., Lundy S. R., Watkins J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2014). Undergraduates Achieve Learning Gains in Plant Genetics through Peer Teaching of Secondary Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

2012 posttest comparison of MS (n = 19) and HS (n = 38) undergraduates via Mann-Whitney U-test with false-discovery rate control. The article reports "HS undergraduates scored significantly higher than MS undergraduates on the last two questions" on PCR and gel electrophoresis.

> "However, the HS undergraduates scored significantly higher than MS undergraduates on the last two questions, which related to PCR and gel electrophoresis, subjects that were part of the HS curriculum but not the MS curriculum (Figure 1B)."

### Chrispeels H. E. 2014 (2)

Chrispeels H. E., Klosterman M. L., Martin J. B., Lundy S. R., Watkins J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2014). Undergraduates Achieve Learning Gains in Plant Genetics through Peer Teaching of Secondary Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

2013 pre/posttest comparison of MS (n = 24) and HS (n = 28) undergraduates. The article reports HS undergraduates "scored significantly higher than the MS undergraduates on the HS-specific questions, despite all students learning about the content in the lecture portion of the course."

> "However, the HS undergraduates scored significantly higher than the MS undergraduates on the HS-specific questions, despite all students learning about the content in the lecture portion of the course."
