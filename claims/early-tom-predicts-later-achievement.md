## Evidence

### Lecce 2026

Lecce, S., Stagnitto, S. M., Mascheretti, S., Lampis, V., & Devine, R. T. (2026). Children's Theory of Mind and Academic Achievement: a Meta-analysis on Reading Comprehension and Math Skills. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10185-w

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of longitudinal partial correlations for reading comprehension (18 effect sizes controlling for verbal ability, 15 for executive functions). Printed partial effect sizes rVA = .12 and rEF = .15 remained statistically significant, though attenuated relative to zero-order effects.

> "The weighted mean remained statistically significant when controlling for both verbal ability, rVA = .12, 95% CI [.04, .21], p = .006, and executive functions, rEF = .15, 95% CI [.03, .25], p < .001."
