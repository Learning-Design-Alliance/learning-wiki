## Evidence

### Dixon and Wu 2014

L. Quentin Dixon & Shuang Wu. (2014). Understanding Language Learning: Review of the Application of the Interaction Model in Foreign Language Contexts. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 11, 23-39. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137251

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Birjandi and Tabatabaei (2009), that in Iran, where boys and girls are typically taught separately, learners "were more likely to become engaged in scaffolding conversations" with same-gender interlocutors. No effect size is printed.

> "the EFL learners were more likely to become engaged in scaffolding conversations when paired with interlocutors of the same gender (Birjandi & Tabatabaei, 2009)."
