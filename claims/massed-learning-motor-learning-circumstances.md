### Amabile Amy H. 2021

Amabile Amy H., Nixon-Cave Kim, Georgetti Larry J., Sims Ashley C. (2021). Front-loading of anatomy content has no effect on long-term anatomy knowledge retention among physical therapy students: a prospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02925-z

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Narrative synthesis by the authors of the limited studies they identified in which massed learning proved superior to spaced delivery; they report these studies "support the idea that in certain circumstances learning can be potentiated through the use of massed instruction". Attributed to their literature search, not tested here.
