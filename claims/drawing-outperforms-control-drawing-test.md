# All three drawing conditions scored better than the control condition on the drawing test and performed equally well

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The no support, global support, and local support conditions each scored better than the control condition on the drawing test (partial η2 = 0.06) and did not differ from each other. [→ Steffen P. Schmidgall 2020](#steffen-p-schmidgall-2020)

## Evidence

### Steffen P. Schmidgall 2020

Steffen P. Schmidgall, Katharina Scheiter, Alexander Eitel. (2020). Can we further improve tablet-based drawing to enhance learning? An empirical test of two types of support. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09513-6

`q3 · i2`

ANCOVA on drawing test performance (visuo-spatial knowledge) with prior knowledge as covariate, N = 153 after three participants terminated early. The article reports an overall effect with partial η2 = 0.06 and that the three drawing conditions "scored better than the control condition, thereby performing equally well (all ps > 0.68)".

> "For drawing test performance, an ANCOVA with the covariate prior knowledge revealed that the experimental conditions differed statistically significantly, F(3,148) = 2.88, MSE = 398.49, p < 0.05, partial η2 = 0.06. The no support (SE = 4.52, p = 0.03), global support (SE = 4.58, p = 0.02), and local support conditions (SE = 4.55, p = 0.01) scored better than the control condition, thereby performing equally well (all ps > 0.68)."
