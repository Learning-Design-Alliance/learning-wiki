## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Plebanek 2017

Plebanek, D. J., & Sloutsky, V. M. (2017). Costs of Selective Attention: When Children Notice what Adults Miss. Psychological Science, 28(6), 723-732. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797617693005

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Visual search accuracy in Experiment 2: both groups exceeded chance (children 75%, adults 89.2%), and "adults were unsurprisingly better at finding the target feature" (d = .9). This is the benefit side of mature selective attention.

> "While performance in both age groups was high (75% in children and 89.2% in adults, both above chance of 16.6%, ps < .001, ds > 6.47), adults were unsurprisingly better at finding the target feature, t(56) = 3.37, p = .001, d = .9, 95% CI [.062, .241]"
