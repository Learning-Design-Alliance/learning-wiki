### Jesse A. Rodríguez 2026

Jesse A. Rodríguez. (2026). LaTA: A Drop-In, FERPA-Compliant Local-LLM Autograder for Upper-Division STEM Coursework. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05410

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Regrade-request audit across the eight Winter 2026 assignments: the instructor classified each disputed rubric line item as valid or invalid. The quote carries the printed contested and confirmed per-item rates.

> "Against this denominator, the 5–10 regrade requests received per assignment correspond to a contested per-rubric-item rate of 0.04–0.08% and an instructor-confirmed per-rubric-item error rate (after the ∼50% valid filter) of approximately 0.02–0.04%."
