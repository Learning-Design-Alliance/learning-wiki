Evidence¶

Özek 2016¶

Müzeyyen Bulut Özek. (2016). The Effect of Creative Drama on Student Achievement in The Course of Information Technologies. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 4, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i6.1370

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-test/post-test experimental design with 89 sixth-grade students randomly assigned to three groups (28 creative drama, 30 computer-based, 31 control). ANCOVA on post-test with pre-test covariate gave F = 93.68, p < .001; "the corrected mean post-test score of the experimental 1 group ( 𝑋= 64.13) was higher than experimental 2 group ( 𝑋=50.57) and control group (𝑋=43.13)". The F is the omnibus test across the three groups; no pairwise or post hoc comparisons are reported, so whether creative drama exceeded each of the other two groups significantly is not established.

"According to Table 3, when the pre -test scores of the groups were controlled there appears a statistically significant difference between the post-test score of groups (f2.85= 93.68, p<.001)."
