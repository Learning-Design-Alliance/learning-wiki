## Evidence

### Brown 2025

Brown, Janie; Hu-Collins, Phoebe. (2025). Communication training for effective Goals of Patient Care conversations in acute care: An integrative review of the literature. Palliative & Supportive Care. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1478951525000264

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative interview study (30–45 minute semistructured interviews) with 23 clinicians one year after Serious Illness Communication Program implementation on general internal medicine wards. The review reports three themes including behavior-change supports and concerns about time, transient relationships, and conversation fluidity.

> "Changes in clinician behavior were supported by having a unit champion, interprofessional engagement, access to copies of the Serious Illness Conversation Guide, and documentation in the electronic medical record."
