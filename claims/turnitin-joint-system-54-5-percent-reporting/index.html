The paper reports, citing Perkins et al. (2024), an evaluation at a UK-curriculum business school of twenty-two experimental GPT-4 submissions marked by fifteen academic staff alongside 963 genuine student submissions. The tool 'identified only 54.8 per cent of the AI-generated content within those flagged submissions'.

"the tool identified only 54.8 per cent of the AI-gener -ated content within those flagged submissions, despite the entirety of each experimental submission having been AI-generated; faculty, even with the Turnitin AI score visible in the marking interface, formally reported only 54.5 per cent of the experimental submis -sions through the academic-misconduct process."
