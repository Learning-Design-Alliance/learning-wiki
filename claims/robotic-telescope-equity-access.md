## Subclaims

## Evidence

### E.L. Gomez & M.T. Fitzgerald 2017

E.L. Gomez & M.T. Fitzgerald. (2017). Robotic Telescopes in Education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1080/21672857.2017.1303264](https://doi.org/10.1080/21672857.2017.1303264)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review argument about access equity, not a tested comparison. The review states schools in less developed countries and lower socioeconomic areas gain "equal access to this instrumentation", and separately notes computer access helps students unable to reach eyepieces and the visually impaired using accessibility tools. No empirical measurement is reported.

> "By providing access to robotic and remote telescopes for free or very low cost, schools in less developed countries and/or in lower socioeconomic areas have equal access to this instrumentation [25]."
