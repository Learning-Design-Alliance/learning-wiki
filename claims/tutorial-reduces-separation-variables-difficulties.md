When Support Hides Progress: Insights from a Physics Tutorial on Solving Laplace's Equation Using Separation of Variables in Cartesian Coordinates. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.3390/educsci15101345](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci15101345)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Think-aloud interview data from 13 advanced students. The article separately reports that on the unscaffolded pretest "none of the students applied the Fourier trick", while on the unscaffolded posttest most wrote the general solution as a sum of separable solutions and applied the Fourier trick confidently.

> "During the interviews, in the unscaffolded pretest, only two out of thirteen students were able to obtain the separable solution for the given problem correctly."
