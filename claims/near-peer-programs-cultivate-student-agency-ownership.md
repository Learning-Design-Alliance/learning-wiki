Theoretical argument from the guide's introduction about what student-led near-peer programs add beyond belonging. The authors state, from practitioner experience, that "students with opportunities to design, rather than just experience" equitable spaces gain greater ownership of their learning; no data are reported.

> "These programs promote student ownership of learning environments by providing students with opportunities to design, rather than just experience, more equitable and accessible spaces in the physical sciences, which can feed back into greater ownership of their learning and further enhance the benefits described above."
