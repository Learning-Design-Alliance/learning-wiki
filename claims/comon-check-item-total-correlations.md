## Evidence

### Radziej K 2017

Radziej K, Loechner J, Engerer C, Niglio de Figueiredo M, Freund J, Sattel H, Bachmann C, Berberat PO, Dinkel A, Wuensch A. (2017). How to assess communication skills? Development of the rating scale ComOn Check. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2017.1392823

`q2 · i3` · `design · r3`

Item analysis of consensus ratings of 135 videotaped consultations. Item-total correlations were significant for items A1-E5 except A1 (printed coefficient 0.15, not significant); the reported range of significant coefficients is "0.31 (A2 – Patient's perception) to 0.78 (B2 – Structure in subchapters)", and item F correlated 0.91 with the sum score (r = 0.78 at the top of the significant range).

> "Pearson correlation coefficients ranged from 0.31 (A2 – Patient's perception) to 0.78 (B2 – Structure in subchapters). The accordance for item F (Global rating for total assessment) with the sum score was nearly perfect, with a correlation coefficient of 0.91."
