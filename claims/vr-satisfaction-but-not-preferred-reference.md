`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

User experience survey (CSQ-VR plus satisfaction items) completed by all 17 participants after the VR session. The article reports only 12% (n = 2) reported cybersickness-related symptoms and more than 88% agreed the tool enhanced their learning experience.

> "53% of participants indicated that they do not prefer VR over prosection, while 23.5% were neutral."
