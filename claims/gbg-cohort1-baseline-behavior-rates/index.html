Evidence¶

Kurki 2013¶

Kurki, A., Wang, W., Li, Y., & Poduska, J. (2013). Measurement of Child Behavior via Classroom Observations in the Good Behavior Game Professional Development Models Randomized Control Trial. SREE Fall 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563300

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis of fall baseline observations for Cohort 1 (18 schools, 71 classrooms, 1401 students). The article reports the printed percentages and adds that aggressive behavior was rare: "2.2 and 3.0 percent of students were identified as engaged in aggressive behaviors".

"Analysis regarding student behavior for baseline shows that 51.0 and 52.3 percent of students were observed as off-task, and 17.6 and 24.9 percent as disruptive during morning and afternoon observations."
