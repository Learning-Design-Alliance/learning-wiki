## Evidence

### Barış Eren Nadiye; Çiftçi Bahar 2025

Barış Eren Nadiye; Çiftçi Bahar. (2025). The Use of Puzzles as a Teaching Technique in Nursing Education: A Randomized Controlled Study. Journal of Evaluation in Clinical Practice. https://doi.org/10.1111/jep.70118

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the same RCT (n = 65), dependent-groups t-tests on IMTS totals showed the experimental group improved significantly while the control group's change was 'not statistically significant'; the between-group posttest comparison gave t = 2.419, p = 0.018, ES = 0.600.

> "The experimental group's mean score on the “Motivation Scale for Instructional Materials” significantly improved from 100.53 ± 32.80 to 128.84 ± 23.29 (t = −3.583, p = 0.001, d = 0.633). In contrast, the control group's mean score increased slightly from 103.64 ± 30.35 to 113.58 ± 27.37, but this change was not statistically significant (t = −1.313, p = 0.198, d = 0.573)."
