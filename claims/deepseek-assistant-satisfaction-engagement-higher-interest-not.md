## Evidence

### Fan Hao 2025

Fan Hao, Du Wenqin, Yan Jia, Jiang Hong. (2025). Application of DeepSeek-based AI teaching assistant in teaching anesthesiology theories. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08494-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 5-point Likert satisfaction survey was analyzed with the Mann-Whitney U test in the RCT; Table 3 reports engagement medians of 4.75 (4.75–5.0) vs 3.0 (3.0–4.0) and timeliness of problem resolution of 4.0 (4.0–5.0) vs 3.0 (3.0–4.0). Only significance is printed, no effect size.

> "In terms of learning engagement and responsiveness to questions, the experimental group achieved significantly higher scores than the control group (P < 0.05)."

### Fan Hao 2025 (2)

Fan Hao, Du Wenqin, Yan Jia, Jiang Hong. (2025). Application of DeepSeek-based AI teaching assistant in teaching anesthesiology theories. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08494-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same Mann-Whitney U analysis of Likert-survey dimensions found no significant differences in learning interest (medians 3.0 vs 4.0) or satisfaction (3.75 vs 4.0). No effect size is printed, so the direction of the raw medians should not be read as an AI advantage or deficit.

> "However, no significant differences were found between the two groups in dimensions such as learning interest and satisfaction (P > 0.05)."
