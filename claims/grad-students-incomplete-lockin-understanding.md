### Seth DeVore 2016

Seth DeVore, Alexandre Gauthier, Jeremy Levy, and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Development and evaluation of a tutorial to improve Students' Understanding of a lock-in amplifier. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.01314](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.01314)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Think-aloud interview study in which graduate students worked through the pretest, tutorial and posttest while verbalizing their reasoning. The article observed that 'most of the interviewed graduate students had an incomplete understanding of the intricacies of the lock-in's operation,' with difficulties spanning the time constant, mixer, phase, amplitude modulation, and multiple frequencies.
