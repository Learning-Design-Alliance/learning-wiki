Summer Learning and Beyond: Opportunities for Creating Equity 2021¶

Summer Learning and Beyond: Opportunities for Creating Equity. (2021). Spencer Foundation & Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/summer-learning-creating-equity

q2 · i? · review · r2

Design Principle 5 section, a narrative synthesis, states that "tutoring can be highly effective" and that cross-age peer tutoring benefits both tutors and tutees. No specific studies, samples, or effect sizes are printed.

"Research has demonstrated not only that tutoring can be highly effective but that hiring older peers as tutors, mentors, and coaches supports learning gains for both older and younger students."
