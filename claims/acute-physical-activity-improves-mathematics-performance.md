## Evidence

### Muntaner-Mas A 2024

Muntaner-Mas A, Morales JS, Martínez-de-Quel Ó, Lubans DR, García-Hermoso A. (2024). Acute effect of physical activity on academic outcomes in school-aged youth: A systematic review and multivariate meta-analysis. Scand J Med Sci Sports. https://doi.org/10.1111/sms.14479

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Univariate and multivariate random-effects meta-analysis of nine studies assessing mathematics via math tests, WIAT-III, WRAT-3/4 and the New York State Testing Program. Both analyses showed significant improvement, with the multivariate estimate "Hedge's g = 0.29, 95% CI, 0.16-0.42".

> "Our univariate and multivariate meta-analysis showed that acute physical activity interventions were associated with improved mathematics performance (univariate: Hedge's g = 0.31, 95% CI, 0.18-0.44, p < 0.001, I 2 = 0%; multivariate: Hedge's g = 0.29, 95% CI, 0.16-0.42, p < 0.001, I 2 = 28.92%)"

### Muntaner-Mas A 2024 (2)

Muntaner-Mas A, Morales JS, Martínez-de-Quel Ó, Lubans DR, García-Hermoso A. (2024). Acute effect of physical activity on academic outcomes in school-aged youth: A systematic review and multivariate meta-analysis. Scand J Med Sci Sports. https://doi.org/10.1111/sms.14479

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Sub-group meta-analysis by mathematics skill type. Math tests showed a significant increase (g = 0.34) while arithmetic tests did not (g = 0.18, p = 0.237); the between-group difference was not significant (p = 0.35).

> "a significant increase was observed in math tests (Hedge's g = 0.34, 95% CI, 0.19-0.48, p < 0.001, I 2 = 0%), but not in arithmetic tests (Hedge's g = 0.18, 95% CI, -0.12 to 0.48, p = 0.237, I 2 = 0%) (p = 0.35 between groups)"
