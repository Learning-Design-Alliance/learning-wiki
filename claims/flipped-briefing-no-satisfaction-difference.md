## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yang S‐Y 2021

Yang S‐Y. (2021). Effectiveness of neonatal emergency nursing education through simulation training: Flipped learning based on Tanner's Clinical Judgement Model. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.748

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same quasi-experiment (N = 65); satisfaction measured with the Korean version of the Simulation Experience Satisfaction Measurement Tool (α = 0.82 in this study). Both groups' satisfaction decreased slightly from pre- to postintervention.

> "ANCOVA using the prior score of learners’ satisfactions as a covariate revealed no significant mean differences (F = 0.94, p = .352) between the two groups. Thus, hypothesis 4 was rejected."
