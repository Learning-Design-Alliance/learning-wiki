Evidence¶

Shabani 2018¶

Karim Shabani. (2018). Group Dynamic Assessment of L2 Learners' Writing Abilities. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research 6(1), 129-149. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165517

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Microgenetic frequency analysis of reciprocating acts (Table 12) from the first to the last DA session, e.g. taking no action after receiving the first mediations fell from 42 to 2 and providing the correct response independently and with confidence rose from 2 to 23, as printed in the table. The article treats these as evidence of microgenetic development and increased self-regulated functioning; no effect size is printed.
