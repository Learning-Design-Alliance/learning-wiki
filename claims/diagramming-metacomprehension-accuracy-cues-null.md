## Evidence

### Joep van der Graaf 2023

Joep van der Graaf, Michelle Taub, Yizhou Fan. (2023). Introduction to special issue on facilitating self-regulated learning with scaffolds: Recent advances and future directions. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09364-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The introduction reports, citing Pijeira-Díaz et al. (this issue), a three-condition study with secondary students in a reading class comparing an empty diagram, a pre-structured diagram, and a pre-structured diagram with self-assessment cues. "diagramming conditions showed higher metacomprehension accuracy" while cue-use differences were null. No effect sizes are printed.

> "No differences were found between conditions regarding the cues they used to monitor comprehension, but diagramming conditions showed higher metacomprehension accuracy."
