### Yeager Amanda Love CNM 2025

Yeager Amanda Love CNM, DNP; Nypaver Cynthia CNM, PhD, WHNP‐BC. (2025). Evidence‐Based Suturing Education for Midwives. Journal of Midwifery & Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/jmwh.70018

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same program evaluation pilot (15 students): after the workshop, faculty assessed competence with an adapted OSCE, a performance examination evaluating clinical competence; no preworkshop competence score was collected because BSN-prepared students were not expected to enter with suturing skill. No effect size is printed.

> "Additionally, the average postintervention competence score reflected a level of suturing skill consistent with that of an advanced beginner. Our goal was to equip students with the knowledge and skills to begin performing perineal repair under the supervision of their preceptors at an advanced beginner level."
