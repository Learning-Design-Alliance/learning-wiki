Evidence¶

Jade Mai Cock · Ido Roll · Käser Tanja 2025¶

Jade Mai Cock · Ido Roll · Käser Tanja. (2025). Different Topics, Similar Patterns: The Consistency of Learners and Learning Across Interactive STEM Simulations. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00512-7

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cluster analysis (variable, relationship, and focus paradigms) of log data from 135 undergraduates using two PhET simulations found two clusters per lab, with two clusters per lab of similar sizes (e.g., 76 and 68 for Beer's Law Lab under the variable paradigm). The article reports "in both labs, students in the larger cluster demonstrated more systematic behavior".

"in both labs, students in the larger cluster demonstrated more systematic behavior while the smaller clusters contained students who explored the simulation in a more free manner."

Related Claims¶

  • Activity order influences strategy adoption: starting with the easier Beer's Law Lab doubles the rate of moving to a systematic strategy on the second simulation — related
  • Most learners (68%) maintain the same inquiry-strategy cluster across two different simulations, indicating stable rather than task-specific approaches — related
  • The three feature-abstraction paradigms yield largely overlapping inquiry clusters but capture systematic behavior from different angles, as shown by Rand indices — related
  • Prior knowledge influences which inquiry strategy learners adopt, with learners holding some knowledge more likely to behave systematically — related
  • Systematic inquiry behavior is associated with gaining quantitative understanding of simulation variables, while free exploration is associated with only qualitative understanding — related
  • The choice of normalization technique influences clustering outcomes, so multiple normalization approaches should be tested in cluster analysis — related
