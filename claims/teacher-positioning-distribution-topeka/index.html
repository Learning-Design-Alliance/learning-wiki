Qualitative interview study of 24 middle school language arts teachers using the Topeka AI writing platform in 2021-22. The Findings section reports the distribution: "12 (50%) positioned the AI as a teaching partner, 7 (29%) as a grading assistant, and 5 (21%) as a substitute teacher."
