## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wagner 2016

Wagner, M.-N. L., Donskaya, M. V., Kupriyanova, M. E., & Ovezova, U. A. (2016). Perspectives of Introduction of the Mobile-Assisted Language Learning (Mall) Technology. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(15), 8562-8571. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

An observational implementation study with 60 senior-year students at a university in Kazakhstan, using the Mobl21 platform for English learning; the authors report that use of the methodic support was "psychologically comfortable and facilitated the actualization of their learning potential." No effect size is printed.

> "Observation of the students’ activity during the process of the MALL technology implementation and conduction of interviews with them demonstrated that the use of methodic support of the English language learnin g process was psychologically comfortable and facilitated the actualization of their learning potential."
