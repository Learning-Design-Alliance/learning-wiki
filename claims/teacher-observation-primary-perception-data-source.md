Price, H., & Fitz, J. (2026). Cultivating math mindsets: Teaching practices that enable equitable classroom learning. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/810.406

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview and observation findings from the study's 10 focal middle school math teachers, who were nominated as early adopters of innovative and inclusive math practices. The report states their most important data sources were "based on their own observations—by noticing and interpreting student behavior and engagement," and that all teachers supplemented this informal data with formal student survey data.

> "The majority of teachers in this study indicated that their most important data sources for understanding students’ feelings about math and their classroom learning conditions were based on their own observations—by noticing and interpreting student behavior and engagement."
