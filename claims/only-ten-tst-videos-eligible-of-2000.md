Freely available, online videos to support neurological physiotherapists and students in task-specific training skill acquisition: a scoping review. `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Scoping review screening: Google Video and YouTube searches identified 2,000 videos, 191 were screened against eligibility criteria with 95% inter-reviewer agreement, and only ten were included. The article reports "0.4% of videos identified, 5% of videos bookmarked" met criteria.

> "Only ten videos fulfilled the eligibility criteria (0.4% of videos identified, 5% of videos bookmarked) and were included in the review, as represented in Fig. 1."
