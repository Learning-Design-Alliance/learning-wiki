## Evidence

### Lockwood 2017

Lockwood, J., & Mooney, A. (2017). Computational Thinking in Education: Where does it fit? A systematic literary review. Maynooth University, Ireland. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1703.07659](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1703.07659)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The review attributes this to Haddad & Kalaani's study of data from 982 students over two years taking a Computing for Engineers course, comparing GPA to CT skills (final course grade) and instructor teaching style. No correlation coefficient is printed.

> "They found that student's average GPA scores correlated with their CT performance. They also noted that the difference in performance was mainly due to how students perceived CT and their level of readiness."
