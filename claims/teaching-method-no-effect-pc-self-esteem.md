## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Self-esteem of the PC cluster increased irrespective of whether the preceding substance-specific lessons were student-centered or teacher-centered; no between-group differences were found. [→ Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X 2013](#heyne-thomas-bogner-franz-x-2013)
`q2 i?` Contrary to the fourth hypothesis, a self-esteem change occurred in the PC group between T2 and T3 under the teacher-centered method G3PC. [→ Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X 2013 (2)](#heyne-thomas-bogner-franz-x-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X 2013

Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X. (2013). Drug Prevention by Increasing Self-Esteem: Influence of Teaching Approaches and Gender on Different Consumption Groups. World Journal of Education, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v3n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the three preceding teaching approaches (G1PC student-centered, G2PC student-centered guided, G3PC teacher-centered) within the PC cluster; the results section reports no between-group differences (Kruskal-Wallis Chi-Square(PC-T1) = 0.884; df = 2; p = .643).

> "Self-esteem of the potentially curious cluster (PC) was increased, irrespective of the different preceding teaching methods."

### Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X 2013 (2)

Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X. (2013). Drug Prevention by Increasing Self-Esteem: Influence of Teaching Approaches and Gender on Different Consumption Groups. World Journal of Education, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v3n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Discussion-section interpretation of an unexpected late self-esteem change under the teacher-centered implementation; the authors suggest the external expert rather than the regular classroom teacher may explain it, citing Tobler and Stratton on external counsellors.

> "In addition to the retention-test timeframe effect and in contradiction of our fourth hypothesis, a change occurred in the PC consumption group between the T-2 and T-3 testing schedules under the implementation teacher-centered method G3 PC."
