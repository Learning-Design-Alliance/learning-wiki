Sebring 1995¶

Sebring, P. A., Bryk, A. S., Luppescu, S., & Thum, Y. M. (1995). Charting Reform in Asbury High School: Results of Student and Teacher Surveys. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-asbury-school-results-student-and-teacher-surveys

Survey report summary of the SIP Implementation scale (seven items) for teachers in bottom-quartile schools. The report states "the SIP is not being implemented" and gives the printed percentages 40, 50, and ten percent for negative, mixed, and integral ratings respectively.

"Responses from teachers in these schools reveal the SIP is not being implemented. Forty percent of the teachers respond negatively, including that they have no knowledge of the SIP. Another 50 percent of the teachers give mixed ratings to the SIP."
