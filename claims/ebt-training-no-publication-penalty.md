## Evidence

### Shortlidge Erin E. 2018

Shortlidge Erin E., Eddy Sarah L. (2018). The trade-off between graduate student research and teaching: A myth? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0199576

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Proportional log-odds model on the 338-student survey sample predicting publication category (0, 1, 2+). The article reports no significant relationship and a non-significant trend: "for each unit increase in a student’s average training in EBT practices, they were 1.04 times more likely to have at least one additional paper."

> "After controlling for whether a PhD student had previously earned a Master’s degree and their year in a PhD program, there was no evidence for a relationship between training in EBT and the number of papers a PhD student had published to date (β = 0.04 ± 0.032, t = 1.32, p = 0.19, Fig 3)."
