## Evidence

### Huynh G 2026

Huynh G, Tam J, Hashami HA, Tadros M, Wadhwa A. (2026). From Presentation to Prognosis: Case-Based Learning in Pediatric Bacterial Meningitis for Postgraduate Training. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11632

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired pre/post 10-item quiz evaluation with 49 learners completing matched quizzes using pseudonyms, analyzed with a paired t test. The article reports a "mean paired increase of 2.69 points" with t(48) = 8.13, P < .001; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Mean quiz scores improved from 5.2/10 to 7.9/10, with a mean paired increase of 2.69 points (SD of paired differences 2.32; 95% CI, 2.03–3.36). This represented a statistically significant improvement by paired t test, t(48) = 8.13, P < .001."
