### Trejo-Mejía Juan Andrés 2016

Trejo-Mejía Juan Andrés, Sánchez-Mendiola Melchor, Méndez-Ramírez Ignacio, Martínez-González Adrián. (2016). Reliability analysis of the objective structured clinical examination using generalizability theory. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.31650

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Numerical D-study projection based on the G-study variance components, not a new data collection; the article states the D study "predicts what would happen if the number of stations is increased".

> "In our study, an increase to 22 stations, with a duration of 2 h 24 min, would increase the G coefficient from 0.93 to 0.94. Versions and sites have a component of variance close to 0%; therefore, the increase in reliability would be marginal if they get changed."
