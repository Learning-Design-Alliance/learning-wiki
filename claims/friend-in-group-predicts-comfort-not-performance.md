## Evidence

### Theobald EJ 2017

Theobald EJ, Eddy SL, Grunspan DZ, Wiggins BL, Crowe AJ. (2017). Student perception of group dynamics predicts individual performance: Comfort and equity matter. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0181336

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Binomial and ordinal mixed models on the same course dataset. Friend was the single predictor of comfort in the final model; the performance models found "There was no impact of working with a friend on student performance (Table 2)". No standardized effect size printed for either finding.

> "Students who worked with friends were 425% (i.e. 5.25 times) more likely to report being comfortable compared to students who did not work with a friend"
