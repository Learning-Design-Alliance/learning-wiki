Wrzesińska Magdalena A., Rakoczy Jarosław, Binder-Olibrowska Katarzyna W., Kostyła Magdalena, Allott Vincent E.S., Harris Samuel R.L., Walsh Jason L., Handa Ashok, Harris Benjamin H.L. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Open-ended survey responses from 134 students were independently coded by two judges with a consensus coding frame. NT was described as "a more effective form of learning" and student-friendly; MCQ preferences centred on an "interesting and creative form of learning" and perceived exam-preparation effectiveness.

> "The qualitative analysis revealed two categories in relation to NT: effectiveness of the method and student-friendly method."
