# Enem 2011 item 70 (simple electric circuits) reveals lack of familiarity with conducting materials: only about 35% correct, 40% chose options connecting the wire to the bulb's glass, and the item has low discrimination

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` About 35% of students chose the correct answer, while 40% chose options involving connecting a terminal to the glass of the bulb, showing unfamiliarity with electric conduction. [→ Marta F. Barroso 2017](#marta-f-barroso-2017)
`q2 i?` The item has low discrimination (0.41) and low pseudo-chance (0.05), and the authors attribute the errors to the near-total absence of practical activities and school laboratory experience in Brazilian Physics teaching. [→ Marta F. Barroso 2017 (2)](#marta-f-barroso-2017-2)
