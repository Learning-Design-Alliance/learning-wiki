The model was significant, "F(8, 53) = 6.64, p < 0.001", with cross-validated R2 of 0.285; the table shows During Book Joy (t = 3.71**), After Scan Confusion (t = 2.07*), and Total Worksheet Submits (t = -5.37**) as significant predictors.
