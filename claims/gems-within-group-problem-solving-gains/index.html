Unal 2018¶

Unal, M., & Saglam, M. (2018). Examination of the effect of the GEMS program on problem solving and science process skills of 6 years old children. European Journal of Educational Research, 7(3), 567-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.7.3.567

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon Signed Rank tests on the 11 experimental-group children's pre- and post-test PSSSE scores; the article reports the difference was "in favor of the post-test score" and suggests GEMS education had an important effect on problem solving skills.

"the difference between Science and Nature Problems subscale (z: -2.51, p<.05), Material Usage Problems subscale (z: -2.51, p<.05) and total ( SNP + MUP) scores of the Problem Solving Scale in Science Education before and after GEMS education in children receiving GEMS education was significant (z: -2.83, p .05)"
