Evidence¶

Evans 1955¶

Evans, Richard I.; Roney, H. Burr; and McAdams, Walter J. (1955). An Evaluation of the Effectiveness of Instruction and Audience Reaction to Programming on an Educational Television Station. Journal of Applied Psychology, 39, 277-79. doi:10.1037/h0042123

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey of college and high-school groups evaluating educational music films, conducted at eight colleges or universities and 13 high schools in Illinois and Indiana. The article reports the use contrast between educators and specialists and that "Every group reported difficulty in finding suitable material"; no inferential statistics are printed.

"College music educators were making the most extensive use of audio-visual materials and college music specialists were making the least use of them. (c) Every group reported difficulty in finding suitable material."
