Evidence¶

Özgür Ulubey 2018¶

Özgür Ulubey. (2018). The Effect of Creative Drama as a Method on Skills: A Meta-analysis Study. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 6(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1172791

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis of 63 experimental studies comparing creative drama instruction with other methods, with posttest means and standard deviations for both groups. The article reports "a significant effect size was found (ES=1.177)", large by Cohen's classification, favoring experimental groups.

"Due to this heterogeneity, the effect size was calculated by means of the random effects model and a significant effect size was found (ES=1.177). This effect size is in favor of the experimental groups and according to Cohen’s (1998) classification, it is large."

Related Claims¶

  • The very large effect found here is consistent with prior meta-analyses of creative drama, which report medium-to-huge positive effects on achievement — related
  • Creative drama method has a very large positive effect on Social Studies course achievement in favor of experimental groups (random-effects d = 1.171) — related
  • Effect size of creative drama on skills varies significantly with duration of experimental application, with longer applications showing larger effects — related
  • Creative drama-based learning has a large positive effect on academic success in Turkish language instruction (REM ES = 1.712) — related
  • The drama approach has a strong positive effect on students' academic achievement in social studies courses compared with traditional teaching — related
  • Effect size of creative drama on skills does not vary significantly by practitioner (researcher vs teacher) — related
  • Effect of creative drama on skills varies significantly by level of schooling, highest at pre-school and lowest at high school — related
  • Effect size of creative drama does not vary significantly by skill type (social, basic language, higher thinking) — related
  • Creative drama has a large significant positive effect on academic achievement (overall weighted ES = 1.68 under a random effects model across 40 studies) — related
  • Several learning theories and teaching strategies have very large positive effects on academic achievement (e.g., creative drama ES = 1.453), while the surface approach to learning has a small negative effect (ES = -.366) — related
  • The authors interpret the drama method's effects through active, constructivist learning in which learners reconstruct and give new meanings to knowledge — related
