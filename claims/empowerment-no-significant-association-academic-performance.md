## Evidence

### Reyes Esteban G. 2026

Reyes Esteban G., Santos Matheus Pierini, Newball-Noriega Edda E., Huancahuire-Vega Salomón. (2026). Abuse and empowerment in the academic field on the academic performance of medical students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09378-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable Poisson regression with robust variance among 255 medical students. The article reports "academic empowerment did not show a statistically significant association with academic performance after adjustment"; autonomy (PR = 1.07) and responsibility (PR = 0.60) were also non-significant. Equivalence was not tested.

> "academic empowerment did not show a statistically significant association with academic performance after adjustment for potential confounders, including sex, age, nationality, and academic history"
