## Evidence

### Chen Shuya 2024

Chen Shuya. (2024). Structural modeling of Chinese students’ academic achievement identity and basic psychological needs: do academic self-efficacy, and mindfulness play a mediating role? BMC Psychology, 12. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01571-6

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

The SEM mediation test in the 355-student sample confirmed the indirect path through mindfulness ("β = 0.26, T = 2.20, P = 0.001").

> "Hypothesis 4 was confirmed, indicating that Basic Psychological Needs influence students' successful academic identity through the mediating variable of mindfulness (β = 0.26, T = 2.20, P = 0.001)."

### Chen Shuya 2024 (2)
