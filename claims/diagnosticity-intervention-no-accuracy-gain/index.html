Evidence¶

Janneke van de Pol 2024¶

Janneke van de Pol, Eleanor Rowan, Eva Janssen, Tamara van Gog. (2024). Effects of availability of diagnostic and non-diagnostic cues on the accuracy of teachers' judgments of students' text comprehension. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09383-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multilevel comparison of conditions 3 and 4 in the same experiment found no significant accuracy change; the authors attribute this to teachers already making reasonably accurate judgments (about a 15% deviation) because actual cue values were provided, so equivalence was not tested.

"teachers' judgment accuracy did not significantly differ before (condition 3) and after (condition 4) the cue diagnosticity intervention (B = -0.14, p = 0.09, SE = 0.08)"
