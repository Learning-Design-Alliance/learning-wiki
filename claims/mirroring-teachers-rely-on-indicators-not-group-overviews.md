`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory analysis of dashboard interaction in the between-subjects experiment (n = 53). A significant condition difference in indicator views, η2 = 0.167, and a mirroring-versus-alerting post hoc difference, d = 0.52; mirroring teachers looked at "the highest number of indicators, with an average of 5.70 indicators per vignette" and seemed to rely more on indicators than group overviews.
