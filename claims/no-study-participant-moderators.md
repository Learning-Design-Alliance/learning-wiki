### Dahl-Leonard 2024

Dahl-Leonard, K., Hall, C., & Peacott, D. (2024). A meta-analysis of technology-delivered literacy instruction for elementary students. Education Tech Research Dev (2024) 72:1507–1538. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10354-0

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Random-effects between-groups heterogeneity tests and meta-regression across the 53 studies (Table 5). The article reports no statistically significant moderators among these study and participant characteristics; no effect size is printed for the null tests.
