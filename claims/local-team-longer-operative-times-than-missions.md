`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Intraoperative comparison in the retrospective JKCI cohort: local team mean aortic cross-clamping 118.9 ± 44 min vs missions 83.0 ± 34.5 min (t=6.587, P=0.001); CPB 187.9 ± 76.2 vs 125.3 ± 46.3 min (t=7.086, P=0.001); operation time 6.4 ± 1 vs 5.4 ± 0.9 h (t=8.881, P=0.001). No effect sizes are printed.

> "An independent t-sample test was conducted to compare the intraoperative parameters such as number of grafts, duration of aortic cross-clamping, total duration of cardiopulmonary bypass time, total operation time, time interval to extubation, and total duration of ICU stay between the local surgical team and international missions, and it was found to have significant statistical difference (Table 2)."
