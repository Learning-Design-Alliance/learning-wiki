## Evidence

### Dong Xue 2026

Dong Xue, Chen Yihong, Tan Xin, Zeng Xi, Guo Tao. (2026). A feasibility and preliminary effectiveness study of a tiered gynecological laparoscopic training curriculum in southwestern China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09652-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-/post-training objective assessment of 37 participants on standardized tasks, scored by live consensus observation of three senior gynecological surgeons; suturing time fell from 690.00 to 470.00 s and quality score rose from 14 to 17 (both P < 0.001).

> "For the pattern cutting exercise, post-training completion times were significantly reduced compared to pre-training values (155.00 [54.00–270.00] vs. 230.00 [71.00–300.00], P < 0.001)."
