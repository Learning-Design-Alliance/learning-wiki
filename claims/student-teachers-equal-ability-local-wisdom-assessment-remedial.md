### Toharudin and Kurniawan 2017

Uus Toharudin, Iwan Setia Kurniawan. (2017). Values of Local Wisdom: A Potential to Develop an Assessment and Remedial. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.6, No.1. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of biology student teachers at Pasundan University; two-tailed t-test comparing 13 groups developing assessment with 13 groups developing remedial, both based on local wisdom. The article reports "a p -value of 0.635" and accepts H0, concluding equal ability.
