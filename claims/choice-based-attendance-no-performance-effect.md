### Kalani Hettiarachchilage 2023

Kalani Hettiarachchilage, Neel Haldolaarachchige. (2023). Effective Model with Personalized Online Teaching and Learning Science in the Era of ChatGPT. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.09545](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.09545)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of final lecture grade distributions across two semesters (Fall 22 mandatory vs Spring 23 choice-based attendance) in four online physics courses. In Spring 23, less than 10% of each class joined synchronously. The authors report descriptive grade distributions without statistical testing and note they cannot accurately discuss effects in most grade ranges.

> "Therefore, there is not much effect on the method of conducting problem-solving sessions on the student performances. And also overall grade distribution of all classes is good although the classes are online setting."
