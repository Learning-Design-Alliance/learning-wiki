q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative theme from interview accounts in the same study: some students found metaphors essential while others found even memory-view arrows overwhelming, despite identical designs and similar experience levels. Interpretive finding; no effect size.

"This variation reveals that what counts as "concrete" or "overwhelming" is not a property of the representation alone but depends on the learner's interpretive capacity, prior habits, and current goals."
