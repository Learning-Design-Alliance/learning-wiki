## Evidence

### Pöschel Katharina 2026

Pöschel Katharina, Geier Anne-Kathrin, Frese Thomas, Schmalz Gerhard, Schrimpf Anne Marie, Bleckwenn Markus, Ziebolz Dirk, Deutsch Tobias. (2026). Raising medical students’ awareness for the interdependence between oral health and systemic diseases - evaluation of a problem-based learning intervention: an exploratory pilot study. BMC Primary Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-026-03323-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post survey with comparison group: 125 of 348 Leipzig students completed both surveys; 188 of 217 Halle students served as comparison. Wilcoxon and Mann-Whitney tests showed significant score increases, with means as printed; no effect size reported.

> "Students achieved significantly higher scores after the intervention. This could be seen in pre – post comparison (pre: 4.1 ± 1.4 vs. post: 4.9 ± 1.5; p < 0.001) as well as in contrast to the comparison group (comparison: 3.9 ± 1.5 vs. post: 4.9 ± 1.5; p < 0.001)."
