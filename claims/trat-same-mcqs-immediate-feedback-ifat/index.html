Ahmad-Naik Danish, Webb Alexandra, Namboothiri Vaishnavi Krishnan, Valter Krisztina. (2026). The practices and strategies for implementing team-based learning in pre-clinical medical education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08713-x

Narrative synthesis of t-RAT practices across included studies (Fig. 4): teams retake "the same MCQs as the i-RAT" immediately, with team discussion fostering consensus building.

"The t-RATs, as shown in Fig. 4, almost always used the same MCQs as the i-RAT and followed it immediately [15, 33, 57]."

Ahmad-Naik Danish 2026 (2)¶

Ahmad-Naik Danish, Webb Alexandra, Namboothiri Vaishnavi Krishnan, Valter Krisztina. (2026). The practices and strategies for implementing team-based learning in pre-clinical medical education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08713-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports that facilitator-led clarification followed the t-RAT in many studies, while fewer studies described written or delayed faculty feedback; appeal processes were allowed in some programs.

"In addition, many studies reported brief facilitator-led clarification provided immediately following the t-RAT, often in the form of targeted discussion or short mini-lectures to address common misconceptions [35, 38, 47, 54]."
