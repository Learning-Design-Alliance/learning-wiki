## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Participants with plants had less response time than those without plants, but the large pooled effect was nonsignificant (SMD = −0.939, p = 0.170). [→ Han 2022](#han-2022)

## Evidence

### Han 2022

Han, K.-T., Ruan, L.-W., Liao, L.-S., & Tchounwou, P. B. (2022). Effects of Indoor Plants on Human Functions: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analyses. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 19(12), 7454. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19127454

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random-effect meta-analysis of three studies (749 participants; 374 control, 375 experimental) measuring response time in seconds or milliseconds; high heterogeneity (I2 = 96.144%). The confidence interval crosses zero.

> "The pooled effect size (SMD) was −0.939, and the 95% confidence interval ranged from −2.208 to 0.401. The results indicated that the group with plants had less response time than did the group without plants. However, the difference was not significant (p = 0.170)"
