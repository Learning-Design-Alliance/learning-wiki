# Teachers' job satisfaction is negatively associated with turnover intentions (pooled r = −0.46)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across 61 studies, teachers' job satisfaction correlates negatively with turnover intentions (r = −0.46). [→ Wartenberg 2023](#wartenberg-2023)

## Evidence

### Wartenberg 2023

Wartenberg, G., Aldrup, K., Grund, S., & Klusmann, U. (2023). Satisfied and High Performing? A Meta-Analysis and Systematic Review of the Correlates of Teachers' Job Satisfaction. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09831-4

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 61 studies (N = 41,622 teachers) pooling correlations between job satisfaction and turnover intentions. The pooled correlation was "r = − 0.46" with substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 96.19).

> "results indicated a significant negative association of teachers' job satisfaction with both turnover intentions (r = − 0.46, CIr = [–0.52, –0.40]) and absenteeism"
