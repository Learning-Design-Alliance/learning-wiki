## Evidence

### Jing Li and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Jing Li and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Developing a Magnetism Conceptual Survey and Assessing Gender Differences in Student Understanding of Magnetism. PERC Proceedings. Originally published 2009. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.04871](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.04871)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA of MCS post-test scores in the algebra-based classes (121 females, 110 males). The article reports means of 12.3 and 13.2 with p = 0.355 and states the students "do not have a significant difference in performance based on gender"; no effect size is printed.

> "It shows that the mean for the females is 12.3 compar ed to the mean for the males, 13.2. The p -value, 0.355, suggests that even on the post-test, the algebra-based students do not have a significant difference in performance based on gender."
