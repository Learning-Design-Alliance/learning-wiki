## Evidence

### Da-Wei Zhang 2024

Da-Wei Zhang, Melissa Boey, Yan Yu Tan & Alexis Hoh Sheng Jia. (2024). Evaluating large language models for criterion-based grading from agreement to consistency. npj Science of Learning 9:79. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00291-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Preliminary effectiveness assessment: one high school English teacher from China, without IELTS training or teaching experience, graded the same 31 essays. The article reports "the teacher’s grading demonstrated poor interrater agreement (ICC = 0.37)" and significantly lower consistency than the best LLM prompt.

> "In contrast, the teacher’s grading demonstrated poor interrater agreement (ICC = 0.37, 95% CI [0.02, 0.63]) and poor interrater consistency (ICC = 0.44, 95% CI [0.00, 0.72]). The interrater consistency was signiﬁcantly different from that generated by the best prompt condition in ChatGPT 3.5 (p < 0.05)."
