### Bhattacharya et al. 2016

The Effectiveness of Financial Literacy Instruction: The Role of Individual Development Accounts Participation and the Intensity of Instruction. Journal of Financial Counseling and Planning, Volume 27, Number 1, 2016, 20–35. https://doi.org/10.1891/1052-3073.27.1.20

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

First-stage probit of camp attendance (N = 74 to 65) from the Heckman procedure; PRESENT_BIAS was derived from parents' responses to a hypothetical $1,000 cash prize comparing immediate versus future amounts. The article reports coefficients of −1.11, −1.02, and −1.57 (p < .01); these are probit coefficients, not effect sizes.

> "It is interesting to note from the first-stage probit estimates reported at the bottom of Table 5 that the coefficient on PRESENT_BIAS is negative and statistically significant across all columns."
