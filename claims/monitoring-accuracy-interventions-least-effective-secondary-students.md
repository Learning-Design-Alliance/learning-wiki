## Evidence

### Noortje Janssen · Ard W. Lazonder 2024

Noortje Janssen · Ard W. Lazonder. (2024). Meta-analysis of Interventions for Monitoring Accuracy in Problem Solving. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09936-4

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Exploratory moderator analysis of school level in the 35-study meta-analysis; Table 3 reports elementary g = 0.45, secondary g = 0.12, adults g = 0.31. The pairwise comparisons favored elementary and adults over secondary students.

> "interventions were generally less effective for secondary school students than for elementary school students, z = − 4.75, p < 0.001, and adults, z = − 2.14, p = 0.049, while differences between elementary school students and adults were not significant, z = 1.28, p = 0.201."
