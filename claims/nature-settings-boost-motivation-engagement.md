## Evidence

### Kuo M 2019

Kuo M, Barnes M and Jordan C (2019) Do Experiences With Nature Promote Learning? Converging Evidence of a Cause-and-Effect Relationship. Front. Psychol. 10:305. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.00305

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that across prior reviews and recent studies, "students and teachers report strikingly high levels of student engagement and motivation" in nature activities, and that learning in and around nature is associated with intrinsic motivation, which is crucial for engagement and longevity of interest in learning.

> "students and teachers report strikingly high levels of student engagement and motivation, during both student-elected and school-mandated nature activities"
