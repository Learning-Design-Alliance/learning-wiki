Exploratory cross-tabulation (visualized as a heatmap in Fig. 1) of regulatory awareness against self-assessed rule-compliant use among the 127 GenAI users. The article reports uncertainty persisted "even among students who reported being familiar with the institutional rules."

> "students with limited awareness of institutional regulations are frequently unable to assess whether their use of GenAI tools is rule-compliant. However, the results also show that awareness does not automatically lead to actionable clarity."
