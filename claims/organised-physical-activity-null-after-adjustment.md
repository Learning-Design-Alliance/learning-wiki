## Evidence

### Kjellenberg Karin 2025

Kjellenberg Karin, Helgadóttir Björg, Ekblom Örjan, Nyberg Gisela. (2025). Fitness and Screen Time at Age 13 Relates to Academic Performance at Age 16. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.70024

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted multilevel logistic regression (n = 983) in the longitudinal cohort; the article reports OR 1.375, p = 0.152, and attributes the disappearance of the crude effect to adolescents with parents with long education participating to a greater extent.

> "we did not find participation in organised physical activity at age 13 to significantly predict academic performance in the adjusted model. Interestingly, in the crude models, this predictor was significant."
