Second theme of the longitudinal qualitative interview study (T0 and T1 interviews with post-graduate nursing students). Early difficulties were "of technological matter", such as discomfort in asking questions online with recorded lectures; at T1 the lack of a tutor figure and of being with other companions in person dominated.

> "At T1, the difficulty in having satisfying relationships prevails over technological discomfort."
