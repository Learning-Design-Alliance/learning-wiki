Nakamoto, J. & Rice, J. (2017). Network for Teaching Entrepreneurship (NFTE) 2016 Alumni Survey Findings. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED582976

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey analysis comparing respondents' self-employment rate with an external national figure the report cites (Hipple & Hammond, 2016). The report explicitly states it cannot say NFTE participation caused the high rates.

> "Of all NFTE alumni who responded to the survey and indicated they were employed, 16 percent either reported they were self-employed or reported they had a business open in the past year."
