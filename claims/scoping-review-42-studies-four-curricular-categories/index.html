q3 · i? · review · r2

PRISMA-ScR-guided scoping review searching Ovid-MEDLINE, Ovid-EMBASE, and the Cochrane Library. The article reports that "leaving 42 studies for final qualitative analysis" after full-text screening, with thematic synthesis producing four curricular categories.

"In total, 1403 articles were identified through the initial database search after duplicate removal. Following title and abstract screening, 162 articles were selected for full-text review. Of these, 120 were excluded based on eligibility criteria, leaving 42 studies for final qualitative analysis."
