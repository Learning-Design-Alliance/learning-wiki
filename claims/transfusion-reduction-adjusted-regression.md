### Egenberg 2017

Egenberg, S., Masenga, G., Bru, L. E., Eggebø, T. M., Mushi, C., Massay, D., & Øian, P. (2017). Impact of multi-professional, scenario-based training on postpartum hemorrhage in Tanzania: a quasi-experimental, pre- vs. post-intervention study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1478-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on transfusion need (dependent variable; n = 81) in the random samples; the printed table row gives adjusted OR 0.45 (95% CI 0.28–0.73, p < 0.01) for the post-training period.

> "Time periods of data collection (2012 vs. Nov 2013 to Oct 2014)	0.52	0.33–0.82	<0.01	0.45	0.28–0.73	<0.01"

### Egenberg 2017 (2)

Egenberg, S., Masenga, G., Bru, L. E., Eggebø, T. M., Mushi, C., Massay, D., & Øian, P. (2017). Impact of multi-professional, scenario-based training on postpartum hemorrhage in Tanzania: a quasi-experimental, pre- vs. post-intervention study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1478-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary comparison from the same random samples: cesarean section rose from 34.2% to 40.8% and vacuum delivery from 0.5% to 1.2% (both p < 0.01), yet transfusions fell despite CS being a PPH risk factor.

> "There were significant increases in the numbers of CS and vacuum deliveries (both p < 0.01) from the pre- to the post-training periods, but the numbers of episiotomies were similar between groups."
