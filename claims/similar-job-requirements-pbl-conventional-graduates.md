`q2 i?` Both groups rated job requirements similarly; the most required competencies were 'Independent learning/working' (5.19 ± 0.9), 'Practical medical skills' (5.17 ± 1.2) and 'Psycho-social competence' (4.89 ± 1.3). [→ Schlett Christopher L 2010](#schlett-christopher-l-2010)

### Schlett Christopher L 2010

Schlett Christopher L, Doll Hinnerk, Dahmen Janosch, Polacsek Ole, Federkeil Gero, Fischer Martin R, Bamberg Fabian, Butzlaff Martin. (2010). Job requirements compared to medical school education: differences between graduates from problem-based learning and conventional curricula. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of graduates of all German medical schools graduating 1996-2002, rating competencies required at day-to-day work on a 6-point Likert scale. Means are descriptive; no between-group test is reported for these three competencies.

> "The most required competencies at their day-to-day work were "Independent learning/working", "Practical medical skills", and "Psycho-social competence" (5.19 ± 0.9, 5.17 ± 1.2, and 4.89 ± 1.3, respectively; Table 2)"

### Schlett Christopher L 2010 (2)

Schlett Christopher L, Doll Hinnerk, Dahmen Janosch, Polacsek Ole, Federkeil Gero, Fischer Martin R, Bamberg Fabian, Butzlaff Martin. (2010). Job requirements compared to medical school education: differences between graduates from problem-based learning and conventional curricula. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-1

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Between-group comparison of required-competency ratings in the same graduate survey. The article reports Hedges' g of 0.6 for research competence and 0.4 for medical knowledge, both small to moderate.

> "Graduates of the PBL-based curriculum rated the "Research competence" as a significantly higher job requirement as compared to graduates of conventional curricula (3.78 ± 1.6 vs. 2.92 ± 1.5, p < 0.001; respectively)"
