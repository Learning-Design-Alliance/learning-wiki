### Shaeema Zaman Ahmed 2022

Shaeema Zaman Ahmed, Carrie A. Weidner, Jesper H. M. Jensen, Jacob F. Sherson, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2022). Student use of a quantum simulation and visualization tool. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac93c7](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac93c7)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Observation from the coded think-aloud interviews, reported in the discussion. Two documented misuse cases are given in the results: one student missed the Play button for time evolution in Q2, and another used the static rather than dynamic plot for Q4. No effect size.

> "we note, however, that when students use Composer, they can get the wrong answer, which, in the worst case, can facilitate the development of incorrect reasoning."
