Related Claims¶

  • In the practiced condition, 23 brain parcels showed genotype differences in exponential model fit, mostly favoring the Met group and concentrated in frontal and striatal regions — related
  • BDNF Val66Met genotype is associated with behavioral learning curves: Met-allele carriers show higher learning gain and, per the authors, less efficient early learning — related
  • Repeated procedural training produces exponential decay of brain activation in a distributed set of regions including fronto-striatal circuits, paralleling exponential improvement in task performance — related
  • Decision trees achieve competitive accuracy with black-box models (LR, RF, SVM), which performed slightly better but lack interpretability — related
