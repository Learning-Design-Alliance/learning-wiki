## Evidence

### Gentges Joshua 2026

Gentges Joshua, Pascoe Michael, Al-Redouan Azzat, Sumner Caroline BS, Case Sami L PhD, Franklin Samuel PhD, Platt Kristen PhD. (2026). Interactive, Image-Based Modules as a Complement to Prosection-Based Anatomy Laboratories: Multicohort Evaluation. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/85028

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

PA/DPT postcourse survey (N=31) on perceived module impact; the PA/DPT cohort also "felt more prepared for the examination when they had a laboratory module to support the curriculum." Perceptions are self-reported, with no test.

> "23 out of 31 (74%) PA/DPT students reported that the modules helped them on their written examination, and 26 out of 31 (84%) believed the modules helped them on their laboratory examination."

### Gentges Joshua 2026 (2)

> "The medical student survey revealed 80 out of 116 (69%) of that cohort believed their assessment scores reflected the time and effort they spent on modules."
