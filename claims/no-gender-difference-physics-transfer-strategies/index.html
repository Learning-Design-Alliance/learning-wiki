Akinbobola 2015¶

Akinbobola, Akinyemi Olufunminiyi. (2015). Enhancing Transfer of Knowledge in Physics through Effective Teaching Strategies. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.16. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079935

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Three t-test comparisons of male versus female transfer scores (Tables 5-7) in the same 278-student quasi-experiment; the guided discovery comparison shown here gave t = 0.02 against a critical value of 1.98, and the demonstration and expository comparisons were likewise not significant.

"The analysis in Table 5 shows that the calculated t -value of 0.02 is less than the critical t-value of 1.98 at p<.05. Therefore, the null hypothesis stating a non-signif icant effect of gender (male and female) on student s' transfer of knowledge in physics taught with guided discover y was retained."
