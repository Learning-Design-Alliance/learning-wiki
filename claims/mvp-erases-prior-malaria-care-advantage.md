## Evidence

### Jantausch Barbara A. 2023

Jantausch Barbara A., Bost James E., Bhansali Priti, Hefter Yosefa, Greenberg Isabella, Goldman Ellen. (2023). Assessing trainee critical thinking skills using a novel interactive online learning tool. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2023.2178871

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the 40 trainees with prior malaria patient care experience against the rest of the 62-trainee sample. The pre-test difference ("p = 0.007") disappeared post-test; the article reports no effect size for this contrast.

> "Trainees who had taken care of a patient with malaria (n = 40), had higher scores on the pre-test, than those who had not, p = 0.007. This difference was no longer reflected in the post-test scores."
