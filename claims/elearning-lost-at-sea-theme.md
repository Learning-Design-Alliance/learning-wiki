`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the focus-group analysis, the lost at sea theme encompassed feeling overwhelmed by the volume of lectures and lacking guidelines on pacing; students wanted to know which parts were examinable so they could target their study.

> "Not only was the volume of material an obstacle, but a perceived lack of direction as to how to approach the resource hindered participants. This could be described as a lack of signposting within the elearning resource."
