Evidence¶

Cortes 2022¶

Cortes, R. A., Peterson, E. G., Kraemer, D. J. M., Kolvoord, R. A., Uttal, D. H., Dinh, N., Weinberger, A. B., Daker, R. J., Lyons, I. M., Goldman, D., & Green, A. E. (2022). Transfer from spatial education to verbal reasoning and prediction of transfer from learning-related neural change. Science Advances. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abo3555

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Whole-brain Group-by-Time ANOVA in the 63 fMRI participants; the aIPS cluster overlapped the Neurosynth 'spatial' map (SpatialMap) and was retained, while the aIPS-SPL cluster did not overlap and was excluded. No changes were greater for controls within SpatialMap.

"This analysis revealed a Group-by-Time interaction, indicating longitudinal changes in activity during Reasoning that were greater for students who received the Geospatial curriculum than for control students in two regions of PPC: left anterior intraparietal sulcus (aIPS; Fig. 3A) and a cluster extending from left aIPS into left superior parietal lobule (aIPS-SPL; table S19)."

Cortes 2022 (2)¶

Cortes, R. A., Peterson, E. G., Kraemer, D. J. M., Kolvoord, R. A., Uttal, D. H., Dinh, N., Weinberger, A. B., Daker, R. J., Lyons, I. M., Goldman, D., & Green, A. E. (2022). Transfer from spatial education to verbal reasoning and prediction of transfer from learning-related neural change. Science Advances. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abo3555

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Representational similarity analysis in the fMRI subsample: nonspatial relation trials became more similar to spatial trials in aIPS, a large printed effect (d = 0.83). The Group-by-Time ANOVA confirmed the increase was greater in Geospatial students than controls (np2 = 0.072).

"Geospatial students showed increased similarity between nonspatial and spatial trials in aIPS (paired t test for T1 versus T2 similarity in Geospatial students: t = 3.43, P = 0.001, d = 0.83; Supplementary Materials)."

Cortes 2022 (3)¶

Cortes, R. A., Peterson, E. G., Kraemer, D. J. M., Kolvoord, R. A., Uttal, D. H., Dinh, N., Weinberger, A. B., Daker, R. J., Lyons, I. M., Goldman, D., & Green, A. E. (2022). Transfer from spatial education to verbal reasoning and prediction of transfer from learning-related neural change. Science Advances. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abo3555

q2 · i? · causal · r2

PPI-based functional connectivity analysis to a DLPFC seed from a meta-analysis of verbal syllogistic reasoning, masked with SpatialMap, in the fMRI subsample. No connectivity changes were greater for control students. A Group-by-Time-by-Gender interaction indicated female Geospatial students showed greater connectivity increases than males.

"Consistent with the MMT-based prediction, this analysis revealed that Geospatial students increased more than controls in connectivity of SpatialMap regions to DLPFC (Fig. 3C, fig. S10, and table S23)."

Cortes 2022 (4)¶

Cortes, R. A., Peterson, E. G., Kraemer, D. J. M., Kolvoord, R. A., Uttal, D. H., Dinh, N., Weinberger, A. B., Daker, R. J., Lyons, I. M., Goldman, D., & Green, A. E. (2022). Transfer from spatial education to verbal reasoning and prediction of transfer from learning-related neural change. Science Advances. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abo3555

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Regression in the fMRI subsample controlling for PSAT, GPA, and gender: connectivity change predicted reasoning improvement. Subsequent mediation indicated a medium indirect effect (14% of total variance) that was not significant (indirect effect, P = 0.088); only regression coefficients are printed, so impact is null.

"Increased connectivity of SpatialMap to DLPFC showed a medium effect on ΔReasoning (β = 0.309), and this association was significant (P = 0.016; table S30)."
