## Evidence

### Chyl 2021

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a longitudinal diffusion MRI study of at-risk children in which "children at risk for dyslexia who became good readers also showed a faster rate of FA development in the right superior longitudinal fasciculus" than those who became poor readers. The review interprets this as potential right-hemisphere compensation.

> "children at risk for dyslexia who became good readers also showed a faster rate of FA development in the right superior longitudinal fasciculus (SLF, connecting the parietal, occipital, and temporal lobes with ipsilateral frontal cortices) than those who developed into poor readers"
