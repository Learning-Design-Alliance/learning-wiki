### Hettiarachchi Manjula 2014

Hettiarachchi Manjula, Lakmal Fonseka Chathuranga, Gunasekara Priyanka, Jayasinghe Prasanjanie, Maduranga Dasun. (2014). How does the quality of life and the underlying biochemical indicators correlate with the performance in academic examinations in a group of medical students of Sri Lanka? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v19.22772

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the paired pre/post analysis of the 78-student cohort, cortisol and thyroglobulin were the parameters that did not differ significantly from baseline; Table 1 reports serum cortisol 16.51 (0.7) pre and 15.88 (0.7) post with p=0.41. Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "During this period, other than serum cortisol and Tg levels, all investigated parameters showed a significant difference when compared with the baseline value of each indicator."
