## Evidence

### Carrier A 2025

Carrier A, Beaudoin M, Éthier A, Bédard D. (2025). Acting as a change agent: longitudinal effects of a tailored training program aimed at developing the competencies of health and social service professionals. Canadian Medical Education Journal 16(4). https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.80292

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of planned actions (post-immediate) with actions actually taken (post-six-month) among 23 respondents, tested with exact binomial or McNemar tests. Tableau 2 shows significant declines for actions such as analyzing costs (p = 0,001) and communicating effectively (p = 0,002).

> "Immédiatement après la formation, plus des 75 % des professionnelles désirent se mettre en action (Tableau 2). Cependant, six mois après la formation, la proportion de participantes ayant effectivement déployé des actions diminue d'au moins 20,0% par action, et ce, de façon significative (Tableau 2)."
