Pedrollo Laysa Fernanda Silva; Silva Aline Conceição; Zanetti Ana Carolina Guidorizzi; Vedana Kelly Graziani Giacchero (2022). Creation and validation of a high-fidelity simulation scenario for suicide postvention. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.6034.3699

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validation of the postvention scenario by 10 expert judges (five postvention, five high-fidelity simulation specialists) using a three-point Likert-scale instrument, analyzed with the Content Validity Index. The authors report that "all the scenario items obtained values equal to or greater than 0.90", above the 0.80 cutoff.

> "In relation to acceptance and agreement (CVI), all the scenario items obtained values equal to or greater than 0.90, thus reaching the minimum approval criterion (CVI = 0.80) (Table 1)."
