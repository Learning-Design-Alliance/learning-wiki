## Evidence

### Kim Kyong-Jee 2021

Kim Kyong-Jee, Lee Yeon Ji, Lee Mi Jin, Kim Young Hyo. (2021). e-Learning for enhancement of medical student performance at the Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253860

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis in the survey sample (n = 35) relating perceptions of e-learning to readiness measured with a 12-item adaptation of Hung et al.'s three-domain scale (Cronbach's alpha 0.85). No correlation reached significance; the article reports "r = 0.20, p = 0.25" for the total score.

> "Students’ overall perceived effectiveness of e-learning was not associated with their total e-learning readiness scores (r = 0.20, p = 0.25) nor with that of each domain of student e-learning readiness, where p values ranged from 0.14 to 0.54."
