`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simplex analysis of belonging responses against perceived activity allocation (Table 2). Increases in group work, primarily at the loss of lecture time, were associated with making friends, joining study groups, and feeling respected; two lecture-associated items concerned relationships with instructors.

> "students who agreed held a significantly different average location in the simplex than students who did not on nine of the 19 sense of belonging questions. In all but two of these, higher group work was found in the agreement group"
