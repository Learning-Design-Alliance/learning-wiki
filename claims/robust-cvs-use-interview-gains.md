## Evidence

### Eva Erdosne Toth 2000

Eva Erdosne Toth, David Klahr, and Zhe Chen. (2000). Bridging Research and Practice: A Cognitively Based Classroom Intervention for Teaching Experimentation Skills to Elementary School Children. Cognition and Instruction, 18(4), 423–459. https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci1804_1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Individual interview measure from the classroom study (videotaped interviews with 43 permission students). Robust CVS use combines valid experiment design with a CVS-based rationale; means rose from .57/9 to 7.0/9, t(41) = 11.7, p < .0001.

> "increased from a mean of .57 out of 9 (6.3%) in the preinterview to a mean of 7.0 (78%) in the postinterview, t(41) = 11.7,p < .0001."

### Eva Erdosne Toth 2000 (2)

Eva Erdosne Toth, David Klahr, and Zhe Chen. (2000). Bridging Research and Practice: A Cognitively Based Classroom Intervention for Teaching Experimentation Skills to Elementary School Children. Cognition and Instruction, 18(4), 423–459. https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci1804_1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consistency analysis of the same interview measure: 0 of 21 preinstruction students versus 12 of 22 (55%) postinstruction students were consistent robust CVS users, χ2(1, N = 43) = 15.9, p < .0001.

> "None of the 21 students interviewed prior to instruction was a consistent robust CVS user (i.e., able to create controlled experiments and justify their designs with a CVS rationale on at least eight of nine trials). After instruction, 12 of the 22 students interviewed (55%) were consistent robust CVS users, χ2(1, N = 43) = 15.9, p < .0001"
