Evidence¶

Shannon R. Becker and Jessica L. Sturm 2017¶

Shannon R. Becker and Jessica L. Sturm. (2017). Effects of Audiovisual Media on L2 Listening Comprehension: A Preliminary Study in French. CALICO Journal. https://doi.org/10.1558/cj.26754

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Delayed listening posttest administered one week after the four-week learning phase in the same classroom quasi-experiment (n = 31 analyzed). The experimental group "continued to outperform the control group" by 8.19%; the article reports d = −0.40 and F(1, 21) = 1.17, p = .31, with degrees of freedom adjusted for unequal variances.

"On the delayed posttest one week after the initial posttest, the experimental group continued to outperform the control group, albeit by a smaller margin, at 8.19% (see Table 1)."
