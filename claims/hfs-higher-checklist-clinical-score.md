## Evidence

### Grissa Mohamed Habib et al 2023

Grissa Mohamed Habib et al. (2023). Comparison of simulation and video-based training for acute asthma. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04836-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary endpoint assessment: seven days after training, all students took a simulation scenario test on a different acute asthma case, scored by two independent senior physicians on a 15-item checklist (range 0–30). HFS scored "22.9 ± 4.8 vs 19.1 ± 3" (p < 0.001).

> "On the simulation scenario test, the HFS groups mean checklist clinical score was significantly higher than mean IVC group’s (22.9 ± 4.8 vs 19.1 ± 3; p < 0.001))"
