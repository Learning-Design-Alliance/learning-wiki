## Evidence

### C. E. Aguiar and M. M. Pereira 2009

C. E. Aguiar and M. M. Pereira. (2009). Using the Sound Card as a Timer. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.1437 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0908.1437](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0908.1437)

`q2 · i0` · `design · r2`

A scatter plot (Fig. 4) of ball speed vs. the kicker's height was examined for the 80-student sample. The article reports "the correlation coeﬃcient of the data is r2 = 0.09", a very weak correlation, and states that only very weak (if any) correlations were found for age, gender, height or mass.

> "Simple inspection of the plot suggests that the height of a student doesnt have much inﬂuence on how hard he kicks the ball. A more reﬁned analysis conﬁrms this—the correlation coeﬃcient of the data is r2 = 0.09."
