## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the final Cox models, overall GPA was significantly associated with first probation but not attrition, whereas undergraduate course withdrawals were significantly associated with both outcomes. [→ Waynick 2026](#waynick-2026)

## Evidence

### Waynick 2026

Waynick, J., Hussaini, S. S., Stonerook, E., Hiatt, T., Sisson, C., Peacock, B., O'Neal, S., McKinnond, A., Steele, L., & Gillette, C. (2026). Using academic admissions data to predict PA training attrition and academic difficulties: a survival analysis approach. Medical Education Online, 31(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2745370

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The Discussion summarizes the differential predictor patterns across the two Cox models (n = 591): "overall GPA only predicted 1st probation" while withdrawals predicted both. The attrition model's GPA estimate (aHR = 0.29, 95% CI 0.06–1.29) was not significant.

> "We found that non-science course withdrawals predicted both attrition and probation whereas overall GPA only predicted 1st probation."
