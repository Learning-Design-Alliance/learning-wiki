## Evidence

### Gedamu Tigist Enyew 2025

Gedamu Tigist Enyew. (2025). The impact of nutritional and socioeconomic status on academic performance of primary urban school children in northern Ethiopia: a cross-sectional study. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-025-02175-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression among 399 public primary school children found the adjusted odds ratio for good academic performance (above the mean of 71.65) was "AOR = 2.26, 95%CI: 1.39–3.67" for ages 7–10 versus 11–14 years, statistically significant at p ≤ 0.05. Descriptive means were 76.61 (±11.81) versus 69.84 (±12.45).

> "Being in age group of 7 to 10 years (AOR = 2.26, 95%CI: 1.39–3.67) increased academic performance almost by two fold."
