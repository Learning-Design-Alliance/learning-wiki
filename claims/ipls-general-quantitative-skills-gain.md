# Students who completed one or more IPLS courses scored significantly higher than non-IPLS students on general quantitative skill code elements, but the advantage was significant only for students who had taken both semesters.

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Benjamin D. Geller 2022

Benjamin D. Geller, Jack Rubien, Sara M. Hiebert, and Catherine H. Crouch. (2022). The impact of introductory physics for the life sciences in a senior biology capstone course. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Wilcoxon comparison of general quantitative skill code element scores (units reasoning, slope comparison, equation use) between the full capstone sample split by any-IPLS versus no-IPLS background; the article reports the IPLS group "scored significantly higher" with p = 0.03 and no effect size.

> "On elements of the code related to more general quantitative skills, students who completed one or more IPLS courses scored significantly higher than students who had not taken an IPLS course (Wilcoxon p = 0.03, Fig. 2a)."

### Benjamin D. Geller 2022 (2)

Benjamin D. Geller, Jack Rubien, Sara M. Hiebert, and Catherine H. Crouch. (2022). The impact of introductory physics for the life sciences in a senior biology capstone course. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The IPLS group was subdivided into one-semester and two-semester students and compared with the no-IPLS group using Kruskal-Wallis with Wilcoxon post-hoc tests; the article reports significance "only when we compared students who had completed both semesters" and that one semester versus none was not significant (p = 0.21). Only test statistics are printed.

> "The difference in performance on the general quantitative skill elements of the code was statistically significant only when we compared students who had completed both semesters of IPLS with those who had had no IPLS experience at all (Wilcoxon p = 0.01, Fig. 2b)."
