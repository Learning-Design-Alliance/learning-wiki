## Evidence

### Burkow TM 2015

Burkow TM, Vognild LK, Johnsen E, Risberg MJ, Bratvold A, Breivik E, Krogstad T, Hjalmarsen A. (2015). Comprehensive pulmonary rehabilitation in home-based online groups: a mixed method pilot study in COPD. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1713-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mixed-methods pilot with ten COPD patients in GOLD grades I–IV in two home-based online rehabilitation groups; semi-structured telephone interviews after the 9-week intervention showed the programme was “well accepted by the patients” and acceptance “seemed to be independent of disease severity.”

> "The mode of delivery and the components of the programme were well accepted by the patients, and the acceptance seemed to be independent of disease severity."
