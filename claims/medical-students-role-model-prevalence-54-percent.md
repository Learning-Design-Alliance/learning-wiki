## Evidence

### Spaans I 2025

Spaans I, Verbree A-R, de Kleijn R, Dilaver G. (2025). Role models in medicine and beyond: a survey study exploring the relationship between role models, academic performance and sense of belonging. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08257-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 3,474 Utrecht University students (478 medical) in Spring 2022. The article reports "54.4% (260/478) of the medical students in our sample reported having one or more role models in their study program"; a chi-square test across faculties gave χ² (6, N = 3474) = 27.50, p <.001.

> "Results show that 54.4% (260/478) of the medical students in our sample reported having one or more role models in their study program."
