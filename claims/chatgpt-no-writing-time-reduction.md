# ChatGPT assistance did not reduce essay writing time: both groups required a similar duration and writing duration was not a significant predictor of essay score

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The ChatGPT-assisted group (172.22 ± 31.59 minutes) and the control group (179.11 ± 31.93 minutes) took a similar amount of time, and writing duration did not significantly affect essay scores (P = 0.669). [→ Željana Bašić 2023](#zeljana-basic-2023)

## Evidence

### Željana Bašić 2023

Željana Bašić, Ana Banovac, Ivana Kružić, Ivan Jerković. (2023). Better by You, better than Me? ChatGPT-3 as writing assistance in students' essays. University Department of Forensic Sciences, University of Split. [doi:10.35542/osf.io/n5m7s](https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/n5m7s)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Writing time was recorded for each of the 18 students during the four-hour supervised task. The article reports the GTP-assisted group at "172.22 ± 31.59" minutes and the control at "179.11 ± 31.93 minutes"; no effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The duration of the essay writing for the GTP-assisted group was 172.22  ± 31.59, and for the control, 179.11 ± 31.93 minutes."

### Željana Bašić 2023 (2)

Željana Bašić, Ana Banovac, Ivana Kružić, Ivan Jerković. (2023). Better by You, better than Me? ChatGPT-3 as writing assistance in students' essays. University Department of Forensic Sciences, University of Split. [doi:10.35542/osf.io/n5m7s](https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/n5m7s)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same linear regression of overall essay score, writing duration was not significant (P = 0.669). The article prints only the p-value for this predictor, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "However, none of the predictors had a significant effect on the outcome: group (P  = 0.184), writing duration (P  = 0.669), module (P = 0.388), and GPA (P = 0.532)."
