## Evidence

### Bereuter Jean-Paul 2026

Bereuter Jean-Paul, Fries Alexa, Grummt Isabell, Geissler Mark Enrik, Geissler Rona Berit, Schmidt Sofia, Buck Nathalie, Weiß Juliane, Krause-Jüttler Grit, Weitz Jürgen, Distler Marius, Oehme Florian, von Bechtolsheim Felix. (2026). Impact of acute sleep deprivation on laparoscopic performance: a prospective, randomized crossover trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12872-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Manipulation check in the student cohort using PSQI, Epworth Sleepiness Scale, Stanford Sleepiness Scale and a self-designed tiredness questionnaire, plus vital parameters. SSS rose from "control: 2 vs. sleep-deprived: 4; p < 0.001"; heart rate was significantly lower when sleep-deprived. No effect sizes printed.

> "students had a significant higher score on the SSS (control: 2 vs. sleep-deprived: 4; p < 0.001) as well as higher levels of sleepiness (control: 0.47 vs. sleep-deprived: 1.47, p < 0.001) and tiredness (control: 0.55 vs. sleep-deprived: 1.33, p < 0.001) at the sleep-deprived measurement"
