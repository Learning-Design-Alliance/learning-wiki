## Evidence

### Kourmousi 2018

Kourmousi, N., Markogiannakis, G., Tzavara, C., Kounenou, K., Mandrikas, A., Christopoulou, E., & Koutras, V. (2018). Students’ Psychosocial Empowerment With The ‘Steps For Life’ Personal and Social Skills Greek Elementary Programme. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 10(5), 535-549. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2018541303

`q3 · i?`

Multiple linear regression models with change in each dimension as dependent variable, adjusted for gender, nationality, age, parental education, siblings, and school factors, found "significantly greater improvement of all their investigated skills". Adjusted β ranged from 0.17 (self-esteem) to 0.44 (emotions' identification and expression), all p < 0.001 except aggressiveness control (p = 0.001).

> "Regression analyses showed that children of the interven- tion group had significantly greater improvement of all their investigated skills post intervention as compared to controls."
