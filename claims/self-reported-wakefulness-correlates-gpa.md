## Evidence

### Wee H.G. 2022

Wee H.G., Yang Z., Xing L., Tsang K.K.-T., Ruan H.D., Li Y. (2022). Nighttime Sleep Awakening Frequency and Its Consistency Predict Future Academic Performance in College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19052933

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of daily self-report questionnaire data (whether participants struggled to keep awake during daytime) averaged across the tracking period in the 45-student sample. Printed effect sizes were r = 0.309 with current GPA and r = 0.359 with future GPA. Wakefulness was also negatively correlated with awakening frequency (r = ŌłÆ0.331, p = 0.026).
