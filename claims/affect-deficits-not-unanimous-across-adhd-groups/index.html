Evidence¶

Miller 2011¶

Miller, M., Hanford, R. B., Fassbender, C., Duke, M., & Schweitzer, J. B. (2011). Affect Recognition in Adults with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3950374/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' overall interpretation in the Discussion of the combined ANOVA and regression findings from the three-group comparison of 51 adults on the DANVA. "Thus, affect recognition deficits were certainly not found unanimously in the ADHD groups, suggesting a more nuanced view of affect recognition deficits in adults with ADHD." This reflects the pattern of significant fearful-affect differences only for the ADHD-I group and dimensional symptom associations only for sad affect errors.
