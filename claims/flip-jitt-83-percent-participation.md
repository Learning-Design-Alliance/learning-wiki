## Evidence

### Nathaniel Lasry 2012

Nathaniel Lasry, Michael Dugdale & Elizabeth Charles. (2012). Just in Time to Flip Your Classroom. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1309.0852](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1309.0852)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructor-reported participation tracking in a multi-section college Electricity & Magnetism course using LON-CAPA warm-ups. The authors report "a compliance rate of 83%" with 631 of 759 possible assignments graded across 11 warm-ups and 69 students. No comparison condition or effect size is reported.

> "Tracking student participation, we found a compliance rate of 83% (we assigned 11 warm-ups to 69 students; grades were assigned to 631 out of a total possible of 759 assignments for a participation rate of 83.1%)."
