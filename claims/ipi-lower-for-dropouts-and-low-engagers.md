### Sinha 2014

Sinha, T. (2014). "Your click decides your fate": Leveraging clickstream patterns from MOOC videos to infer students' information processing & attrition behavior. B.Tech project report, School of Computing Science and Engineering, VIT University, Chennai. [doi:10.3115/v1/w14-4102](https://doi.org/10.3115/v1/w14-4102)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Z tests of IPI means between student partitions in video lecture 4-6 of the Scala MOOC. The report finds "The average IPI is significantly higher" for high engagers and non-dropouts, and the histogram shows many non-dropouts with positive IPI.

> "The average IPI is significantly higher for students with “High" engagement (|z|=8.296, p<0.01) and “Non dropouts" (|z|=22.54, p<0.01)."
