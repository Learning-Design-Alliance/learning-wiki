## Evidence

### Kubsch 2025

Kubsch, M., Strauß, S., Grimm, A., Gombert, S., Drachsler, H., Neumann, K., & Rummel, N. (2025). Self-regulated Learning in the Digitally Enhanced Science Classroom: Toward an Early Warning System. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10011-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Intercoder reliability assessment on a randomly selected ~10% subsample of open-ended task responses, independently coded by two coders, with drift checks via regular reviews of coded data.

> "An average Spearman's rho of 0.97 with a minimum of 0.68 indicated substantial intercoder reliability. Across the different scores we found an average Spearman's rho of 0.94 for knowledge elements, an average Spearman's rho of 0.91 for engagement in scientific practices, and an average Spearman's rho of 0.94 for learning performances."
