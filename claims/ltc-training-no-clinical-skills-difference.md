## Evidence

### Li Y 2025

Li Y, Cheng S, Liu D, Jiang W. (2025). “Learning, Thinking & Conveying” training with experiential learning in the emergency department for undergraduate nursing students: A quasi-experimental study. Medicine 2025;104:28(e43231). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000043231

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of the practical exam results in the same 90-student quasi-experiment found no significant association with training or any demographic covariate; the printed means were 92.64 ± 3.255 (experimental) versus 92.80 ± 3.087 (control), t = -0.233, P = .817. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Neither univariate linear regression analysis nor mutual adjustment through multivariate linear regression revealed any category of factors that were significantly associated with practical exam results"
