Systematic review synthesis of 4 observational studies of grit and academic performance in graduate rehabilitation science students. The review reports "moderate, positive, significant correlations with academic performance" in the majority of studies, with grit the strongest of all relationships examined.

"Grit was examined in 4 included studies. The majority found that grit had moderate, positive, significant correlations with academic performance, the strongest of all relationships examined in this review."
