## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized trial (Triola et al. 2006, USA) in which 55 health care providers received live standardized patient cases or a mix including virtual patient simulations for psychosocial sequelae of disasters. The review reports equivalent improvements ("all p > 0.05") and "p = 0.79" for modality ratings; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "Both groups showed equivalent improvements in comfort level, preparedness to respond, screen, and care for patients (all p > 0.05). No significant differences were found in subjective ratings of effectiveness between VP and SP modalities (p = 0.79)."
