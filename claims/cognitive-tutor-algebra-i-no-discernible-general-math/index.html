Evidence¶

Cognitive Tutor® 2016¶

Cognitive Tutor®. (2016). WWC Intervention Report: Cognitive Tutor®, Secondary Mathematics. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.carnegielearning.com

q2 · i? · causal · r?

WWC review of one quasi-experimental study in six senior high schools in Miami–Dade County Public Schools, including 658 students in six schools. The finding was "no statistically significant difference," the effect size was below the 0.25 substantively-important threshold, and equivalence was not tested, yielding a no-discernible-effects rating with small extent of evidence.

"Shneyderman (2001) reported, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant difference between the Cognitive Tutor® Algebra I and comparison groups on the general mathematics achievement domain."
