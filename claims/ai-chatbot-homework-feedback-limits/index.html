Evidence¶

Perl-Nussbaum 2026¶

Perl-Nussbaum, D., & Finkelstein, N. D. (2026). A Workshop Series for Effective Use of AI in Uncertain Times: Building a Physics Faculty Learning Community. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23887 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.23887

q1 · i? · design · r2

Small-group hands-on testing during Session 3, in which faculty tried prompts on real anonymized student homework from a modern physics course (technical and conceptual items). The article reports feedback was "uneven and depended heavily on prompting" and needed rubric guidance to be reliable.

"AI easily solved every problem, but its ability to correct student work and give useful feedback was uneven and depended heavily on prompting"
