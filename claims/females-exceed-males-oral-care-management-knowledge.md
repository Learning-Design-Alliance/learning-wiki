Chi-square analysis of the same 506-participant cross-sectional survey (Table 3) comparing females (n = 271) with males (n = 235) on the 8 oral-care-management items; the article reports "58.9% vs. 47.4%, p < 0.05". No effect size is printed.

> "The Chi-square statistical test identified a significant gender difference in knowledge of oral care/health management for cancer patients, with a significantly higher proportion of female participants demonstrating good knowledge compared to males (58.9% vs. 47.4%, p < 0.05)."
