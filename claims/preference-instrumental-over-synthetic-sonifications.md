## Evidence

### Arcand KK 2024

Arcand KK, Schonhut-Stasik JS, Kane SG, Sturdevant G, Russo M, Watzke M, Hsu B and Smith LF (2024). A Universe of Sound: processing NASA data into sonifications to explore participant response. Front. Commun. 9:1288896. https://doi.org/10.3389/fcomm.2024.1288896

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation drawing on the Deep Field's wider enjoyment range (it was the shortest and contained synthetic sounds) and open-ended responses demonstrating participant preference for orchestral sounds; no effect size or test is printed for this preference.

> "One more minor but no less critical conclusion is that participants prefer instrumental soniﬁcations over synthesized sounds."
