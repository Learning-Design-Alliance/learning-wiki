## Evidence

### WANG Yibo 2024

WANG Yibo, MAO Yuanyuan, Lik-Hang Lee, NI Shi-ting, WANG Zeyu, GU Xiaole, HUI Pan. (2024). Metabook: A Mobile-to-Headset Pipeline for 3D Story Book Creation in Augmented Reality. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.13701](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.13701)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject study with 22 children comparing paper reading and 3D book experience; Smileyometer interest ratings favored the 3D book, with "3 out of 5 for the paper book reading mode and 4.5 out of 5 for the 3D book mode"; only the Wilcoxon statistic is printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "In the Smileyometer ratings for the level of interest, statistical significance is observed ( Z = −3.4078, p <0.001). Group A gave scores of 3 out of 5 for the paper book reading mode and 4.5 out of 5 for the 3D book mode."
