## Evidence

### Navarro Juan-José 2015

Navarro Juan-José, García-Rubio Javier, Olivares Pedro R. (2015). The Relative Age Effect and Its Influence on Academic Performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0141895

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical multiple regression on the SIMCE global academic performance score of 15,234 Chilean eighth graders. Model 3 added RAE after SES and institution type; the article reports "additionally explaining only the 0.1% of the model" with RAE β = -.025, t = -3.494, p < .001, a standardised coefficient of negligible magnitude.

> "For its part, the RAE, after controlling the effects of SES and the institution type, shows a statistically significant effect on academic performance, additionally explaining only the 0.1% of the model (Model 3)."
