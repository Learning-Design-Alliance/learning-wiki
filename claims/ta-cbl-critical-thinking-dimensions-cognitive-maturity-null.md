## Evidence

### Zhao Rui 2024

Zhao Rui, Ding TingTing, Meng JinPing, Lei Miao, Ma Huili. (2024). Think-aloud approach combined with case-based learning in nursing teaching round for new nurses in cancer hospital. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05891-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training comparison across the seven dimensions of the Critical Thinking Ability Scale. Table 3 shows cognitive maturity at 47.31 ± 4.83 (control) versus 49.17 ± 5.02 (observation), t = 1.868, P = 0.065, not significant; other dimensions reached P < 0.01. No effect sizes printed.

> "Subsequent to the teaching rounds, discernible discrepancies in critical thinking prowess emerged between the observation and control groups, save for the cognitive maturity dimension, where distinctions were statistically significant (P < 0.05), depicted in Table 3."
