# UCSMP Algebra has potentially positive effects on general mathematics achievement for secondary students, with a small extent of evidence

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In one quasi-experimental study of 189 students, UCSMP Algebra produced a statistically significant positive effect on general mathematics achievement (effect size 0.81, improvement index +29). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2016](#what-works-clearinghouse-2016)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2016

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse intervention report: University of Chicago School Mathematics Project (UCSMP). What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/641

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Matched-pairs quasi-experiment (Thompson et al., 2006) comparing UCSMP Algebra (second edition) to non-UCSMP textbooks in three high schools; on the UCSMP Algebra Test the intervention mean was "49.8 (16.3)" versus "37.3 (14.9)", effect size "0.81", improvement index "+29", p-value ".04".
