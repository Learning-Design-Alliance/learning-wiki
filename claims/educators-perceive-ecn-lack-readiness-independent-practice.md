### Alexander 2026

Alexander, L., Burdeu, G., Cappello, A., & Griffin, K. (2026). Qualified but Unprepared: The Readiness Paradox in Early Career Mental Health Nurses—A Descriptive Qualitative Study. International Journal of Mental Health Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/inm.70285

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 10 nurse educators (8 RN, 2 EN) at a large Melbourne tertiary health service, conducted April–May 2025. Educators described "a misalignment between training and clinical practice expectations" and inadequate placement models.

> "Findings indicate that graduates frequently lack readiness for independent practice and demonstrate a disconnect between expectations and the realities of mental health nursing."
