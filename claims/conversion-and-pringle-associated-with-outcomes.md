### van Dorst R. W. J. J. 2026

van Dorst R. W. J. J., Rinkes I. H. M. Borel, Gilg S., Molenaar I. Q., Hagendoorn J., Sparrelid E. (2026). Reaching proficiency in robotic liver surgery in two high-volume European HPB centers. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03372-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear regression on the bi-institutional cohort: conversion to open surgery was associated with "a substantial increase in blood loss" (β = 322.75 ml, p < 0.001) and longer operative time (β = 59.94 minutes, p<.017); unstandardised coefficients only.

> "Conversion to open surgery was associated with a substantial increase in blood loss (β = 322.75, [232.844-412.647], p = < 0.001) and operative time (β = 59.94, [11.031-108.853], p<.017)."

### van Dorst R. W. J. J. 2026 (2)

van Dorst R. W. J. J., Rinkes I. H. M. Borel, Gilg S., Molenaar I. Q., Hagendoorn J., Sparrelid E. (2026). Reaching proficiency in robotic liver surgery in two high-volume European HPB centers. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03372-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same regression analysis: Pringle maneuver use was "strongly associated with a reduction in blood loss" (β=-710.42, p < .001), while longer Pringle duration independently increased blood loss (β = 2.60, p = .004) and operative time (β = 1.79, p < .001); unstandardised coefficients only.

> "Usage of the Pringle maneuver was strongly associated with a reduction in blood loss (β=-710.42, [-940.453—480.379], p < .001), while longer Pringle duration was independently associated with increased blood loss (β = 2.60, [0.840 − 4.350], p = .004) and longer operative time (β = 1.79, [0.944-2.635], p < .001)."
