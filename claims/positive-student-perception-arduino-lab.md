## Evidence

### Eugenio Tufino and Giovanni Organtini 2023

Eugenio Tufino and Giovanni Organtini. (2023). Evaluation of the effectiveness of an introductory mechanics Lab with Arduino and smartphone. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.00606](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.00606)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous end-of-course questionnaire, developed by the authors following published guidelines, administered in the last week before the final exam. "The number of students who responded to the end -of-course questionnaire was 46", about 50 percent of those enrolled in the lab section.

> "The analysis of student feedback revealed that most students have a favourable perception of their laboratory experience and feel that it has been important for the development of their skills. Students generally responded positively to the introduction of Arduino boards"

### Eugenio Tufino and Giovanni Organtini 2023 (2)

Eugenio Tufino and Giovanni Organtini. (2023). Evaluation of the effectiveness of an introductory mechanics Lab with Arduino and smartphone. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.00606](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.00606)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Open-comment and closed items from the same end-of-course questionnaire of 46 respondents; some students expressed excitement about Arduino and interest in continuing to use it on their own.

> "some of them expressed excitement about the potential for the device and expressed interest in continuing to use Arduino in the future also on their own."
