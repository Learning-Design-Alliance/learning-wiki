## Evidence

### Ciaran Hughes 2020

Ciaran Hughes, Joshua Isaacson, Anastasia Perry, Ranbel Sun, and Jessica Turner. (2020). Teaching Quantum Computing to High School Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.07206](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.07206)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post survey of 45 students asked to list as many quantum mechanics or computing concepts as came to mind, before and after the course. The histograms in Fig. 2 show the after distributions "shifting to the right"; no effect size or test statistic is printed.

> "The top left and right histograms of Fig. 2 show that after taking the course, students signiﬁcantly improved their QM and QC knowledge base, indicated by the orange distributions shifting to the right."
