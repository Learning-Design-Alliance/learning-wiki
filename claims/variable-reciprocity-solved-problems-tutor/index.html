The commentary reports, citing Evans and Moore (2013), this role-assignment proposal within the OPAL system discussion; the article describes the proposal rather than presenting new data for it. Students who accurately solve a problem gain authority to be tutors for the problem.

"Evans and Moore (2013) proposed variable reciprocity in which students play the roles of tutor and tutee for various problems depending on whether or not they accurately solve the problems"
