Martinez-Roig 2026¶

Martinez-Roig, R., Aragonés-González, M., & Cazorla, M. (2026). Exploring the implementation of the Pepper social robot in formal education: a scoping review. Journal of New Approaches in Educational Research, 15, 22. https://doi.org/10.1007/s44322-026-00072-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

Scoping review synthesis of 13 peer-reviewed empirical studies on Pepper in formal education, selected via PRISMA-ScR from four databases (2014–2026). The review reports the methodological distribution verbatim: "eight studies (61.5%) adopted qualitative approaches" and "No large-scale longitudinal studies were identified."

"From a methodological perspective, eight studies (61.5%) adopted qualitative approaches, including case studies or interaction analysis. Three studies (23.1%) used mixed-methods designs and two (15.4%) employed predominantly quantitative experimental designs. No large-scale longitudinal studies were identified."
