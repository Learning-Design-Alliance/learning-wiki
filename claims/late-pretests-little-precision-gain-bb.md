## Evidence

### Unlu 2013

Unlu, F., Layzer, C., Clements, D., Sarama, J., & Cook, D. (2013). Approaches to Incorporating Late Pretests in Experiments: Evaluation of Two Early Mathematics and Self-Regulation Interventions. SREE Fall 2013 Conference. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2013f/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Precision comparison across estimators in the cluster RCT for the BB condition. The article reports standard errors "decrease by 10-20%" and concludes controlling for contaminated pretests is not worthwhile for BB.

> "adding contaminated pretests hardly improves the precision of estimated impacts (standard errors of impact estimates decrease by 10-20%), which may be due to sizeable early treatment effects."
