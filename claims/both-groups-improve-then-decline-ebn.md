## Evidence

### Chu Tsung-Lan 2019

Chu Tsung-Lan, Wang Jeng, Monrouxe Lynn, Sung Yu-Chih, Kuo Chen-li, Ho Lun-Hui, Lin Yueh-E. (2019). The effects of the flipped classroom in teaching evidence based nursing: A quasi-experimental study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210606

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Overall trajectory across the three time points (pre-course, post-course, one-month follow-up) in the 151-nurse quasi-experiment. GEE analysis confirmed significant learning effects for knowledge (posttest vs. pretest β = 9.73, p < 0.001) and self-efficacy (β = 18.53, p < 0.001) across all participants.

> "In both group the scores of the EBN knowledge and self-efficacy in practice improved after training. The scores of the experimental group increased significantly than in the control group. However, the scores declined in both groups one month after the course."
