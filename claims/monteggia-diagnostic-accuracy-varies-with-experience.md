### Choi Min Hyeok 2025

Choi Min Hyeok, Moon Min Hui, Kang Suk Woong, Kim Kyeong Baek, Ahn Tae Young, Bae Jung Yun. (2025). Challenges in Diagnosing Pediatric Monteggia Fractures: Role of Clinical Experience and Imaging. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina61081457

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective diagnostic-accuracy study in which six physician groups interpreted 120 radiograph folders (40 Monteggia, 80 other fractures) without clinical information. The article reports "first-year orthopedic residents exhibited the highest overall error rate (46.7%)" and pediatric radiologists the lowest (12.5%); Table 2 adds 87.5% vs 26.3% for first-year residents on Monteggia vs other fractures.

> "Among the physician groups, first-year orthopedic residents exhibited the highest overall error rate (46.7%), followed by general radiologists (40.0%). In contrast, pediatric radiologists demonstrated the lowest error rate (12.5%)."
