## Evidence

### Wojewodka G 2017

Wojewodka G, Hurley S, Taylor SJC, Noble AJ, Ridsdale L, Goldstein LH. (2017). Implementation fidelity of a self-management course for epilepsy: method and assessment. BMC Medical Research Methodology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12874-017-0373-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Fidelity evaluation of five SMILE(UK) courses (28% of those delivered) across three modules, using audio recordings scored by two independent raters. The article reports "> 50% of scored items received the maximum of 2 points" and group interaction "mean score: 1.9–2.0 out of 2".

> "We found that the courses were delivered with a good level of adherence (> 50% of scored items received the maximum of 2 points) and high competence. Groups were interactive (mean score: 1.9–2.0 out of 2) and the overall impression was on average assessed as “good”."
