# Physics teachers at the surveyed schools rely on static, non-interactive media and have not widely adopted interactive multimedia

## Evidence

### Serli Ahzari 2025

Serli Ahzari, Akmam Akmam. (2025). Analyzing Students’ Critical Thinking as a Basis for Developing Interactive Physics Multimedia with Generative Learning and Cognitive Conflict Strategies. Jurnal Pendidikan Fisika, 13(2), 163–176. https://doi.org/10.26618/jpf.v13i2.17702

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A questionnaire of three physics teachers plus field observations found teachers "primarily use simple media such as PowerPoint presentations, learning videos, audio, and real objects", which the article describes as tending not to meet diverse learning styles. Descriptive, no effect size.

> "Based on the questionnaire analysis administered to three physics teachers teaching 11th Grade Phase F in three different high schools in Lima Puluh Kota Regency, it was found that teachers primarily use simple media such as PowerPoint presentations, learning videos, audio, and real objects."

### Serli Ahzari 2025 (2)

Serli Ahzari, Akmam Akmam. (2025). Analyzing Students’ Critical Thinking as a Basis for Developing Interactive Physics Multimedia with Generative Learning and Cognitive Conflict Strategies. Jurnal Pendidikan Fisika, 13(2), 163–176. https://doi.org/10.26618/jpf.v13i2.17702

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Field observations at the surveyed schools identified the stated barriers: "time constraints for learning and developing new media", lack of technological facilities, and limited technical skills, leading teachers to use familiar simple media. Qualitative observational finding.

> "Field observations revealed that teachers have not widely adopted interactive multimedia due to time constraints for learning and developing new media, compounded by a lack of technological facilities in schools and teachers limited technical skills."
