## Evidence

### Mocanu (Dedu) Elena Mădălina 2017

Mocanu (Dedu) Elena Mădălina, Deaconu Alecxandrina. (2017). The use of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) as a teaching method in vocational education and training in tourism. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 10, Number 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1160585

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intragroup longitudinal analysis (Table 3 and Figure 11) comparing pretest and post-test averages in the tourism experiment; printed averages are pretest 6.88 (experimental) vs 6.83 (control) and post-test 8.33 vs 7.33. The article reports "a faster evolution" for the experimental sample.

> "the averages of the results of the two analyzed samples had an ascending evolution between the two moments, pretest - posttest, the results of the experimental sample having a faster evolution  compared to the control sample."
