`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same RCT, independent-samples t-tests compared theoretical knowledge, practical skills, innovative ability, and comprehensive scores between groups. The experimental group showed "significantly higher" practical skills and "markedly exceeded" innovative ability scores (P < 0.01), while theoretical knowledge showed no significant difference (P > 0.05).

> "Their practical skills score (94.25 ± 4.34) was significantly higher than that of the control group (84.31 ± 5.26) (P < 0.01), and their innovative ability score (92.43 ± 4.76) markedly exceeded that of the control group (70.35 ± 8.98) (P < 0.01)"
