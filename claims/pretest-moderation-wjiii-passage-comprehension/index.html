Evidence¶

Wanzek et al. 2016¶

Jeanne Wanzek, Yaacov Petscher, Stephanie Al Otaiba, Shawn C. Kent, Christopher Schatschneider, Martha Haynes, Brenna K. Rivas, Francesca G. Jones. (2016). Examining the Average and Local Effects of a Standardized Treatment for Fourth Graders with Reading Difficulties. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED577133

q2 · i? · causal · r3

Exploratory moderation analysis with simple slopes (Preacher, Curran, & Bauer methods) on the WJIII passage comprehension outcome; the authors describe the baseline moderation as marginal. With a mean pretest of 482, the treatment-posttest relation was positively moderated when pretest was at least 488. No moderation was found for GMRT comprehension.

"The test revealed that moderation existed when centered pre-test scores on the WJIII passage comprehension were greater than 5.70 (60th percentile)."
