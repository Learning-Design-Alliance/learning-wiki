# The supporting evidence comes from a cluster randomized controlled trial in 77 Colorado elementary schools with low attrition, meeting WWC group design standards without reservations

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Schools were randomly assigned to STeLLA® or a content-knowledge-focused comparison professional development program across two cohorts, with overall school attrition of 8.3% and differential attrition under 1 percentage point. [→ study-methods](#study-methods)

## Evidence

### study-methods

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021, May). Science Teachers Learning through Lesson Analysis (STeLLA®). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/716

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

WWC study-characteristics review of Taylor et al. (2017): 84 schools randomized (46 intervention, 38 comparison) with 3,710 students; analyzed sample was 2,823 students (1,485 intervention, 1,338 comparison) in grades 4–5; student nonresponse rate 24%, differential 2.3 percentage points.

> "This is a cluster randomized controlled trial with low cluster-level attrition and low individual-level nonresponse."
