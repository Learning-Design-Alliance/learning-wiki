## Evidence

### Zhao Fei 2025

Zhao Fei, Bai YunFang, Wu JunJie, Zhu ZhiHao, Mao JinLei, Aili Aikebaier, Gu HongRun, Yu ShiJie, Wang ZhiFei. (2025). A novel model of dual-network composite hydrogels for application in endoscopic submucosal dissection. BMC Gastroenterology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12876-025-04341-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Procedure-specific realism analysis (Table 3) from the expert evaluation, rating marking, submucosal injection, circumferential incision, and dissection. The authors attribute weaker injection performance to weaker simulation of fluid dynamics. No effect size is printed.

> "Procedure-specific analysis (Table 3) showed moderate effectiveness in the marking phase (M = 5.00, P25 = 4.00, P75 = 6.00), and circumferential mucosal incision (M = 5.00, P25 = 4.75, P75 = 6.00), while the submucosal Injection (M = 4.50, P25 = 3.00, P75 = 5.25) and the submucosal dissection (M = 4.50, P25 = 4.00, P75 = 5.00) procedure achieved lower effectiveness."
