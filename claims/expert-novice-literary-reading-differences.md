## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Goldman 2016

Goldman, S. R., Britt, M. A., Brown, W., Cribb, G., George, M., Greenleaf, C., Lee, C. D., Shanahan, C., & Project READI. (2016). Disciplinary Literacies and Learning to Read for Understanding: A Conceptual Framework for Disciplinary Literacy. Educational Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2016.1168741

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article's narrative review of talk-aloud protocol studies of expert and novice literary readers reports shared attention to plot and character but documented differences in depth of knowledge and conceptions of interpretation. Experts draw on intertextual links, warrant with literary theory, and construct multiple interpretations. No effect sizes are printed.

> "these studies also clearly document significant differences in the depth of knowledge expert versus novice readers bring, as well as differences in conceptions of what literature invites readers to do"
