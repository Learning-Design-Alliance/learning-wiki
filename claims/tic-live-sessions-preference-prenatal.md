`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 23 prenatal clinicians found that "participants expressed a preference for live, dynamic sessions over lengthy, mandatory online modules". One participant described clicking through videos without engagement: "I have 40 more videos to watch, click, click, click, click".
