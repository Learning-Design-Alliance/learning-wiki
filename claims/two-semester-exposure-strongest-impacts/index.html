Evidence¶

Pablo Egana-delSol 2023¶

Pablo Egana-delSol. (2023). The impacts of a high-school art-based program on academic achievements, creativity, and creative behaviors. npj Science of Learning 8:39. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00187-6

q2 · i? · causal · r2

PSM analysis comparing AP participants by exposure intensity against matched nonparticipant controls, balancing on demographics including sex, parental education, income, books at home, and prior artistic activity. The authors interpret the scaling relationship as "consistent with minimal sampling bias."

"The most positive and signi ﬁcant results were observed among the group that was treated more intensively (i.e., those who had attended at least two semesters of workshops)."
