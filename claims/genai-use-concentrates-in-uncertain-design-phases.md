Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with a focus group (N=6) in the third semester, comparing sixth-week (W6) and thirteenth-week (W13) coded design processes shown in Figure 7. The article reports the shift in usage across design phases; no effect size is printed.

> "However, after the workshops in the studio process, active GenAI usage is observed in more uncertain phases of the design (e.g. Problem Space, Figure 7b).
