## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Path analysis with good model fit showed the second VSAQ understanding level had a significant direct effect on MCQ score (β = 0.25, p = 0.033), whereas passing the second VSAQ had a total effect of −0.05 on MCQ score. [→ Lertsakulbunlue Sethapong 2024](#lertsakulbunlue-sethapong-2024)
