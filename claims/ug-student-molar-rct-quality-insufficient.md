## Evidence

### El-Ma’aita Ahmad M. 2024

El-Ma’aita Ahmad M., Mahasneh Sari A., Hamandi Maryam A., Al-Rabab’ah Mohammad A. (2024). Molar root canal treatment performed by undergraduate dental students; an observational study of procedural errors and student perception. BMC Medical Education 24. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05397-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational radiographic assessment of 213 post-obturation radiographs of molar RCTs performed by final-year dental students at the University of Jordan, evaluated against predefined criteria by calibrated assessors. The study found technical quality "deemed sufficient in 39.6% of the cases"; interrater reliability was excellent (Kappa statistic: 0.83).

> "The overall technical quality of RCTs was deemed sufficient in 39.6% of the cases."
