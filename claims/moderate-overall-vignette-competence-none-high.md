### Lohela Terhi Johanna 2016

Lohela Terhi Johanna, Nesbitt Robin Clark, Manu Alexander, Vesel Linda, Okyere Eunice, Kirkwood Betty, Gabrysch Sabine. (2016). Competence of health workers in emergency obstetric care: an assessment using clinical vignettes in Brong Ahafo region, Ghana. BMJ Open. [doi:10.1136/bmjopen-2015-010963](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-010963)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional vignette assessment of the most experienced delivery provider in each of 64 facilities, scored out of 20 points against WHO guideline actions. The Results report a median of 55%, low competence for 28% of respondents, and no respondent above 75%.

> "The median total vignette score was 55% of actions mentioned correctly with a range from 5% to 75%. For 28% of all respondents, competence was classified as low (<50%) and none of the respondents achieved a high score (>75%)."
