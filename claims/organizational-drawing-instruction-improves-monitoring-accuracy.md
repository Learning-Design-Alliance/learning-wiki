## Evidence

### Janneke van de Pol 2020

Janneke van de Pol, et al. (2020). Mapping and Drawing to Improve Students’ and Teachers’ Monitoring and Regulation of Students’ Learning from Text: Current Findings and Future Directions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09560-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Study of 5th-grade students drawing during reading under two instruction conditions. The review reports the contrast verbatim — "higher relative monitoring accuracy (G = 0.51) than the representational drawing condition (G = − 0.03)" — and notes the relational cue was only diagnostic of performance for the organizational condition, so only those students could benefit.

> "The organizational drawing condition showed higher relative monitoring accuracy (G = 0.51) than the representational drawing condition (G = − 0.03)."
