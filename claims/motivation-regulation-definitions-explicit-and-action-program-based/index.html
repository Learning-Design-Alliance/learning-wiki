Evidence¶

Maike Trautner 2025¶

Maike Trautner, Carola Grunschel, Malte Schwinger. (2025). Motivating Motivation Regulation Research—An Evidence and Gap Map Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10019-1

q3 · i? · review · r3

Quantitative content analysis of the definitions used in the included studies, coded with interrater reliabilities of 0.69 < κ < 1.00. The review reports "Out of the 289 included studies, 225 (77.9%) provided an explicit definition of motivation regulation"; the remaining 64 studies operationalized it via methods or subsumed it under umbrella constructs.

"Out of the 289 included studies, 225 (77.9%) provided an explicit definition of motivation regulation."

Maike Trautner 2025 (2)¶

Maike Trautner, Carola Grunschel, Malte Schwinger. (2025). Motivating Motivation Regulation Research—An Evidence and Gap Map Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10019-1

q3 · i? · review · r3

Same content analysis of definition wording across the 225 explicitly defining studies. The review reports that "most definitions (54.3%) mentioned complex action programs (e.g., strategies, tactics, competences)", with simpler circumscriptions such as actions, behaviors, or thoughts and beliefs also occurring frequently.

"First, regarding the question how motivation regulation is exerted, most definitions (54.3%) mentioned complex action programs (e.g., strategies, tactics, competences)."
