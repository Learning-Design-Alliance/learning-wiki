Descriptive regional fNIRS analysis comparing LPFC and VMPFC activation in the pilot study's game and video conditions. The article reports the "2.33 times higher" LPFC ∆HbO ratio for the game condition and notes video ∆HbR was slightly higher.

> "Interestingly, in the game condition, ∆HbO (M=0.28) in the LPFC is 2.33 times higher compared to the video condition (M=0.12). However, the ∆HbR value in the video condition (M=0.23) is slightly higher than in the game condition (M=0.21)."
