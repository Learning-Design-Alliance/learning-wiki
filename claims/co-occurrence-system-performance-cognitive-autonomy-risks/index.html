Espinoza-Celi V and Vargas-Saritama A (2026) Technical and pedagogical limitations of artificial intelligence in undergraduate EFL higher education: a systematic review. Front. Educ. 11:1948091. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1948091

q2 · i? · review · r2

Cross-study co-occurrence analysis across the 54 included studies counted each technical–pedagogical pairing once per study; the top pattern was "reported in 25 studies (46%)". The review explicitly states this indicates association, not causal direction.

"The most frequently observed pattern involved AI system performance limitations and learner cognitive and autonomy risks, reported in 25 studies (46%). Co-occurrence indicates that both categories were coded within the same study and does not establish directionality or causation."
