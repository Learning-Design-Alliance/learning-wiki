## Evidence

### Fouda Shaimaa M. 2025

Fouda Shaimaa M., Madi Marwa, Ellakany Passent, Aly Nourhan M., Bahgat Hala A., Elembaby Abeer E., Virtanen Jorma I., AlHumaid Jehan. (2025). The perception of dental students and educators about e-learning during COVID − 19 pandemic. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-06152-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression on the student survey found grade-level differences in overall satisfaction; the printed AORs for third, fourth and fifth grade versus the sixth/postgraduate reference are 0.09, 0.08 and 0.15. No effect size is printed for attitude, which was not significant.

> "Students from third to fifth grade showed significantly lower satisfaction than sixth grade and postgraduate students (AOR = 0.09, 95%CI = 0.02, 0.54, AOR = 0.08, 95%CI = 0.02, 0.39 and AOR = 0.15, 95%CI = 0.04, 0.62, respectively)."
