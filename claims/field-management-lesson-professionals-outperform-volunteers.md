## Evidence

### Podsiadło Paweł 2018

Podsiadło Paweł, Kosiński Sylweriusz, Darocha Tomasz, Sałapa Kinga, Sanak Tomasz, Brugger Hermann. (2018). The Use of E-Learning in Medical Education for Mountain Rescuers Concerning Hypothermia. High Altitude Medicine & Biology. https://doi.org/10.1089/ham.2018.0050

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Lesson-pass rates from Table 2 of the e-learning course study: 76.5% of professionals versus 52.2% of volunteers passed the field-management lesson on first attempt (p = 0.003), the only lesson with a statistically significant between-group difference.

> "only approximately half of the volunteers could correctly answer at least three of four questions concerning the lesson about the field management of hypothermia, in contrast to 76.5% of professional rescuers (p = 0.003)"

### Podsiadło Paweł 2018 (2)

Podsiadło Paweł, Kosiński Sylweriusz, Darocha Tomasz, Sałapa Kinga, Sanak Tomasz, Brugger Hermann. (2018). The Use of E-Learning in Medical Education for Mountain Rescuers Concerning Hypothermia. High Altitude Medicine & Biology. https://doi.org/10.1089/ham.2018.0050

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Lesson-pass rates for the post-traumatic hypothermia lesson in the same cohort: 56.9% of professionals and 50.7% of volunteers passed on first attempt, described by the authors as a difficult topic for all rescuers.

> "Regarding the lesson covering post-traumatic hypothermia, only half of the rescuers in each group achieved the sufficient number of correct answers to pass the lesson on their first attempt."
