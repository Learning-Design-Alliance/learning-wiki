Method section of this project report describes the decentralized technical arrangement: slides were scanned at 40× magnification with a Nanozoomer 2.0 RS or HT, and "the virtual slides are currently decentralized in two universities (Bordeaux and Paris 7)". Descriptive, no evaluation data.

> "Each university was responsible for its own slide scanning, image storage and online display with virtual slide viewers (currently, mScope, Aurora Interactive Ltd., Montreal, Canada)."
