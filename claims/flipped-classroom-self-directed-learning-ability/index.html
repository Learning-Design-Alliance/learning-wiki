Evidence¶

Wu Yaqiu 2026¶

Wu Yaqiu, Cheng Meixiong. (2026). Effectiveness of the flipped classroom in medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09526-8

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of four studies (intervention n = 550; control n = 561) pooling standardized mean differences for self-directed learning ability. The review reports SMD = 2.54 (95% CI 1.04–4.04) with I² = 98.8%.

"Pooled analysis showed a statistically significant improvement in self-directed learning ability with the flipped classroom compared with traditional lecture-based teaching (SMD = 2.54, 95% CI 1.04–4.04), with very high heterogeneity (I² = 98.8%, p < 0.0001)"

Wu Yaqiu 2026 (2)¶

Wu Yaqiu, Cheng Meixiong. (2026). Effectiveness of the flipped classroom in medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09526-8

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup analyses for self-directed learning ability (Fig. 4B–D) found no between-subgroup differences by teaching format, learner group, or course type; heterogeneity remained high within subgroups.

"No evidence of between-subgroup differences was observed by teaching format (p = 0.8683), learner group (p = 0.7025), or course type (p = 0.8683), while heterogeneity remained high within subgroups"
