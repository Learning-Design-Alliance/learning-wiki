Fidelity assessment based on site visits, interviews, and data review for each element, rated 1 (few requirements met) to 5 (almost all). The article reports an average of "3.9 or moderately high", with continuity and stability highest at 4.2 and aligned curriculum lowest at 3.3.

> "The average rating of implementation fidelity for year 1 across the six program elements was 3.9 or moderately high. The highest was continuity and stability (4.2) and the lowest aligned curriculum (3.3)."
