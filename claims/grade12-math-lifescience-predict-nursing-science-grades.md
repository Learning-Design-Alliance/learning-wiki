# Grade 12 mathematics and life science grades significantly predict first-year nursing science module performance, explaining 37.1%–38.1% of variance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a standard multiple linear regression with life science and mathematics as predictors, both were significant predictors (p < 0.001) of first-year science module grades, with the model explaining 37.1% to 38.1% of variance. [→ Mthimunye 2017](#mthimunye-2017)
`q3 i?` Each additional level in grade 12 life science corresponds to an expected average increase of 3.175% in first-year science module grades, and each additional level in mathematics to nearly 3% (β = 2.997, p < 0.001). [→ Mthimunye 2017](#mthimunye-2017)

## Evidence

### Mthimunye 2017

Mthimunye, K.D.T. & Daniels, F.M., 2017, ‘Performance in grade 12 mathematics and science predicts student nurses’ performance in first year science modules at a university in the Western Cape’, Curationis 40(1), a1776. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v40i1.1776

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 2 of the standard multiple linear regression analysis (Table 5) used grade 12 life science (β = 3.175, p < 0.001) and mathematics (β = 2.997, p < 0.001) to predict first-year science grades; "model 2 explains 37.1% to 38.1% of the variation".

> "The generalised R2 = 0.381 (p < 0.001) and the adjusted R2 of 0.371 (p < 0.001) suggest that model 2 explains 37.1% to 38.1% of the variation in the first year science grade distributions."
