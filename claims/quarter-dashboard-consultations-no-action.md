### Alina Hase 2024

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports this finding from one included observational study of 38 teachers consulting dashboards during math lessons. It balances the finding by noting that, regarding all studies, data use influenced the majority of teachers' daily teaching practice.

> "Finally, Molenaar and Knoop-van Campen (2018) report that no explicit teacher actions followed from about a quarter of the dashboard consultations (i.e., looking at the digital learning data)."
