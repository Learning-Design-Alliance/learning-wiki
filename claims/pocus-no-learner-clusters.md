## Evidence

### Bansbach Joachim 2024

Bansbach Joachim, Bentele Michael, Bollinger Matthias, Bentele Stefanie, Langenhan Ronny, Gerber Bianka, Trifunovic-Koenig Milena, Bushuven Stefan. (2024). Self-assessment and learning motivation in emergency point-of-care ultrasound: an online pilot investigation in German physicians. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-024-01154-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-step cluster analysis using log-likelihood distance with BIC and AIC criteria, including motivation, self-assessment, loop performance, and maximal risk as categorical variable. The Silhouette measure was "poor (< 0.25)" against a satisfactory threshold of 0.25.

> "the analysis showed an optimum of two clusters. However, the Silhouette measure of cohesion and separation was poor (< 0.25). In conclusion, physicians could not be grouped based on overestimation, motivation to learn, and risk perception. Hypothesis 3 was, therefore, also rejected."
