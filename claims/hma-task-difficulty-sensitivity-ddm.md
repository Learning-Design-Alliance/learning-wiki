### Jie Liu 2025

Jie Liu, Zhifeng Li, Jiawang Yang, Hao He & Fang Cui. (2025). The neurocognitive mechanism underlying math avoidance among math anxious people. npj Science of Learning 10:50. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00343-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Hierarchical Drift Diffusion Model regression fitted to choices and reaction times from the same fMRI study; drift rate was modulated by trial-by-trial task difficulty and reward. Bayesian posterior tests gave p (HMA < LMA) = 0.01 for the difficulty coefficient and p = 0.46 for reward.

> "The HMA group exhibited a signiﬁcantly elevated impact of task difﬁculty on drift rate compared to the low math anxiety (LMA) group (p (HMA < LMA) = 0.01, Fig. 3b). The coefﬁcient of reward did not show signi ﬁcant differences between the two groups (p (HMA < LMA) = 0.46, Fig.3c)."
