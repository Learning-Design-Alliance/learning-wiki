## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Medical students and tutors distinguished between behaviours that improve group interaction and behaviours that improve learning quality; the two impacts do not always coincide. [→ Iqbal M 2016](#iqbal-m-2016)

## Evidence

### Iqbal M 2016

Iqbal M, Velan GM, O’Sullivan AJ, Balasooriya C. (2016). Differential impact of student behaviours on group interaction and collaborative learning: medical students’ and tutors’ perspectives. BMC Medical Education 16(1):217. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0730-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of five focus group discussions with 22 first- and second-year medical students and eight semi-structured tutor interviews at UNSW Medicine. Participants distinguished behaviours affecting "group interaction" from those affecting "collaborative learning"; no effect sizes reported in this qualitative design.

> "An important finding was that some of these student behaviours were found to have a differential impact on group interaction compared with collaborative learning."
