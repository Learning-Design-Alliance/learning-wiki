### Muhammad Irfan Arif and Munawar S. Mirza 2017

Muhammad Irfan Arif and Munawar S. Mirza. (2017). Effectiveness of an Intervention Program in Fostering Academic Resilience of Students at Risk of Failure at Secondary School Level. Bulletin of Education and Research, 39(1), 251-264. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1210192

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest control group experiment with 64 non-resilient at-risk students (32 per group) in a public secondary school for boys in District Lahore; t-test on gain scores after three months of treatment. The experimental group's overall mean gain was 72.89 versus 12.06 for controls, with t = 166.62, df = 62, p = .001 — "a significant difference between the mean gain scores".
