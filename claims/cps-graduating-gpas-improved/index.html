Nagaoka 2017¶

Nagaoka, J., Seeskin, A., & Coca, V.M. (2017). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2016. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/data

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive analysis of unweighted core-course GPA distributions for CPS graduates (Figure 8; 2015 N=16,431). The report states the 3.0-plus share rose “from 21 percent to 32 percent between 2006 and 2015,” using 3.0 as a college-readiness benchmark.

"In particular, the percentage of students graduating with at least a 3.0 GPA increased from 21 percent to 32 percent between 2006 and 2015 ( see Figure 8 )."
