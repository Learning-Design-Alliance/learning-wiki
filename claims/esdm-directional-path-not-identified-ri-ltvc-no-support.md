## Evidence

### Núñez-Regueiro 2026

Núñez-Regueiro, F., Marsh, H. W., Pekrun, R., Lüdtke, O., & Guo, J. (2026). On Model Interpretability and Time-Reversal Testing in Analyses of Reciprocal Effects: Clarifying Modeling Issues in Sorjonen et al.'s (2025a, 2025b) Commentaries. Educational Psychology Review, 38(80). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10179-8

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical equivalence demonstration reported by the authors: because trait factors are time-invariant random intercepts, reversing or correlating the ESDM path yields "identical fit and parameter estimates", so the direction of the path is not empirically identified.

> "identical fit and parameter estimates are obtained if the effect is reversed ( ASC T rait → ACH T rait) or replaced by a correlation ( ACH T rait ↔ ASC T rait), as we showed before"

### Núñez-Regueiro 2026 (2)

Núñez-Regueiro, F., Marsh, H. W., Pekrun, R., Lüdtke, O., & Guo, J. (2026). Educational Psychology Review, 38(80). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10179-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-comparison result reported from the authors' prior response (Núñez-Regueiro et al., 2025): the random-intercept time-varying covariate model "had the second worst fit" and a 0% probability of being the true model, disfavouring the confounder account.

> "This RI-LTVC had the second worst fit to the data compared to other models and a 0% probability of reflecting the true relations between ASC and ACH"
