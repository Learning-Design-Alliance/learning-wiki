## Evidence

### Talan JW 2026

Talan JW, Adelman MH, Forster M, Reuland B, Kaufman B, Hafiz A, Nair SS, Kramer VE, Mendelson JS, Andriotis A. (2026). Virtual reality simulation for tracheostomy emergencies: a randomized educational intervention. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.1093/atsscholar/aapag023

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 19 critical care fellows (10 VR, 9 small group) assessed 6-8 weeks post-training on a novel task trainer by 3 blinded graders. The article reports "no significant difference" between "18.03 (3.39)" and "16.96 (4.41)".

> "Of a total possible 26 points on the checklist assessment, fellows in the VR group scored an average (SD) of 18.03 (3.39) compared to the small group score of 16.96 (4.41) (P = .558)."
