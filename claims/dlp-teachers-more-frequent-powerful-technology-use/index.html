Evidence¶

Bakhshaei 2018¶

Bakhshaei, M., Hardy, A., Francisco, A., Noakes, S., & Fusco, J. (2018). Fostering Powerful Use of Technology Through Instructional Coaching: Results from the Pilot Year of the Dynamic Learning Project. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/540c3325-fd5f-408e-a347-2409f57848dd

q2 · i? · design · r2

End-of-year survey comparison of the 50 DLP pilot schools. "86 percent of the DLP teachers" reported more frequent technology use versus 76 percent of non-participating teachers; the report attributes possible non-coached gains to culture shifts and coach access.

"At the end of the year, 86 percent of the DLP teachers stated that their technology use was more frequent this year than previous years . This was the case for 76 percent of teachers who didn't participate in the DLP"

Bakhshaei 2018 (2)¶

Bakhshaei, M., Hardy, A., Francisco, A., Noakes, S., & Fusco, J. (2018). Fostering Powerful Use of Technology Through Instructional Coaching: Results from the Pilot Year of the Dynamic Learning Project. q2 · i? · associational · r1

End-of-year survey of DLP and non-DLP teachers in pilot schools: "60 percent of the DLP teachers" reported considerable progress in how they use technology versus 46 percent of colleagues; only percentages are printed, no test statistic.

"At the end of the year, 60 percent of the DLP teachers reported that they had made considerable progress in how they use technology in their teaching practice, compared with 46 percent of their colleagues who didn't participate in the DLP"
