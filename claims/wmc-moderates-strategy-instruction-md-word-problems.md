## Evidence

### Swanson 2013

Swanson, H. L., Lussier, C. M., & Orosco, M. J. (2013). Cognitive Strategies, Working Memory, and Growth in Word Problem Solving in Children With Math Difficulties. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 48(4), 339–358. [doi:10.1177/0022219413498771](https://doi.org/10.1177/0022219413498771)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized 2-year intervention with 192 children in Grades 2 and 3, analyzed with mixed ANCOVA (HLM) with WMC as covariate. The article reports "treatment effects were significantly moderated by WMC" with outcomes higher at high WMC.

> "The intent-to-treat as well as the “as-treated” analyses showed that treatment effects were significantly moderated by WMC. In general, treatment outcomes were higher when WMC was set to a high rather than low level."
