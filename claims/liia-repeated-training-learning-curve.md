## Evidence

### Xia Jianfu 2023

Xia Jianfu, Wu Junjie, Chen Hao, Mao Jinlei, Xu Xiaodong, Zhang Jing, Yang Jin, Wang Zhifei. (2023). Assessment of laparoscopic intracorporeal intestinal anastomosis training using simulation-based 3D printed models: exploring surgical performance and learning curves. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000582

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Repeated-training study in which three intermediates and three novices completed eight LIIA training sessions on the model, with scores and times plotted against expert and intermediate averages (Figure 3). No effect size printed.

> "As the number of training increases, all surgeons presented gradual progress. The operation times and scores of novices close to the average of the intermediates, and the operation times and scores of intermediates close to the average of the experts."

### Xia Jianfu 2023 (2)

Xia Jianfu, Wu Junjie, Chen Hao, Mao Jinlei, Xu Xiaodong, Zhang Jing, Yang Jin, Wang Zhifei. (2023). Assessment of laparoscopic intracorporeal intestinal anastomosis training using simulation-based 3D printed models: exploring surgical performance and learning curves. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000582

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

CUSUM learning-curve analysis of the six trained surgeons; the authors report the intermediates' learning curve "reached a relatively stable plateau". No effect size printed.

> "The turning points in the learning curve for the three novices occurred at the 5th, 4th, and 5th cases, while for the three intermediates, they appeared earlier at the 2nd, 3rd, and 3rd cases (Figure 3C)."
