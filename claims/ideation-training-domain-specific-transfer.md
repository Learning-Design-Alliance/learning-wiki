`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review of Fink et al. (2020), a comparison of verbal and figural ideation training in primary school students. The review reports the asymmetric transfer pattern: 'verbal training led to significant increases in both verbal and figural creativity', while figural training improved only the figural task. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Their study revealed that verbal training led to significant increases in both verbal and figural creativity, but figural training only led to improvements in a figural task."
