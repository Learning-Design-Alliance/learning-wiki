### L. N. Simpfendoerfer 2024

L. N. Simpfendoerfer, Meagan Sundstrom, Matthew Dew, and N. G. Holmes. (2024). What topics of peer interactions correlate with student performance in physics courses?. arXiv. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad358b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad358b)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' comparison of the linear mixed model coefficients (Fig. 4) against code-frequency histograms (Fig. 3) for the lab courses, where small-group work appeared in about 60% of explanations and was the only significant topic.

> "the extent to which interaction topics correlate with final course grade (Fig. 4) mirrors the frequencies of the topics (Fig. 3) in the lab course, with more frequently mentioned topics generally having a higher correlation with final grade in this course"

### L. N. Simpfendoerfer 2024 (2)

L. N. Simpfendoerfer, Meagan Sundstrom, Matthew Dew, and N. G. Holmes. (2024). What topics of peer interactions correlate with student performance in physics courses?. arXiv. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad358b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad358b)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' observation for the lecture courses, where homework occurred in about 40% of explanations yet was only moderately correlated with grades, while concepts correlated most strongly.

> "In the lecture courses, on the other hand, the interaction topic frequencies do not closely mirror their relationship with student grades."
