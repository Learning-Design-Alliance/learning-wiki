## Evidence

### Raney Julia H. 2019

Raney Julia H., Morgan Melissa C., Christmas Amelia, Sterling Mona, Spindler Hilary, Ghosh Rakesh, Gore Aboli, Mahapatra Tanmay, Walker Dilys M. (2019). Simulation-enhanced nurse mentoring to improve preeclampsia and eclampsia care: an education intervention study in Bihar, India. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-019-2186-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 12 mentor interviews. Mentors described simulation as more memorable than didactics, clinical discussions and the two-challenge rule as fostering teamwork, and AMANAT workshops as improving physician leadership and support for mentees.

> "All mentors agreed that simulation training was an important enabler of high quality care. The majority felt that mentoring during live cases helped develop mentees' confidence, facilitating their ability to independently treat PE/E."
