## Evidence

### Sattler 2025

Sattler, Kierra; Font, Sarah; Panlilio, Carlomagno. (2025). Foster care entry and later academic achievement among infants involved with child protective services. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.70008

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Supplemental logistic regression (Table 5) constraining the comparison group to children with an opened in-home services case by age 5, to address selection of higher-risk children into removal. The article reports the odds ratios quoted; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "children who entered care during early infancy (OR = 1.34, p < .01) or toddlerhood (OR = 1.41, p < .001) had higher odds of demonstrating basic or above reading skills in grade 3. Children placed in foster care during early infancy (OR = .46, p < .001), toddlerhood (OR = .62, p < .001), or preschool (OR = .55, p < .05) all had lower odds of chronic absenteeism"
