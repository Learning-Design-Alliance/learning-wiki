## Evidence

### Günther V 2025

Günther V, Nees F, Maass N, von Otte S, Ruchay Z, Pape J, Ackermann J, Alkatout I. (2025). How effective and sustainable is proctoring in robotic surgery? A retrospective analysis based on interviews with surgeons. Surgical Endoscopy 39(3):1985–1995. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11503-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation between proctoring duration and questionnaire item 1.2 in the 40-surgeon interview study; the Results report the duration–learning-needs association with p = 0.009. Duration of proctoring averaged 5.6 (SD 3.4) hours; no effect size is printed.

> "the longer the duration of proctoring, the less appropriate it was deemed in terms of learning needs. In other words, the surgeons felt that their learning needs had not yet been met and would have liked to spend more time training (p = 0.009, Fig. 3)."
