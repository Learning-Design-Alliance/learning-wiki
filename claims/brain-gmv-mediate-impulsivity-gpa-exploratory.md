Post-hoc exploratory mediation analyses reversing the mediator and predictor (brain GMV as mediator between impulsivity and GPA), using the same 153-participant sample and residualized values. The study reports that "the GMVs of the identified regions (left cerebellum and right CN) played a mediating role in the relationship between impulsivity and school performance" with a negative, partial mediation effect (Fig. 4c, d).
