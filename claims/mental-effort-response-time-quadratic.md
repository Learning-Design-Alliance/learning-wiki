## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the reanalysis of Szulewski et al. (2017) data, a quadratic model for the mental effort–response time relation was preferred over linear, with a significant quadratic term. [→ Jimmie Leppink & Patricia Pérez-Fuster 2019](#jimmie-leppink-patricia-perez-fuster-2019)

## Evidence

### Jimmie Leppink & Patricia Pérez-Fuster 2019

Jimmie Leppink & Patricia Pérez-Fuster. (2019). Mental Effort, Workload, Time on Task, and Certainty: Beyond Linear Models. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-09460-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reanalysis of repeated-measurement data (16 questions per participant, 32 participants) from Szulewski et al. (2017) using two-level mixed-effects models; both AIC and BIC preferred the quadratic model, and the quadratic term was significant: "F1, 446.066 = 22.166, p <0 . 0 0 1 ,B = 0.514".

> "In Szulewski et al. (2017), we gain about 0.025 in R2M by going from Model 1 to Model 2, while the gain of adding further complexity is almost zero. In Model 2, we find for the quadratic term: F1, 446.066 = 22.166, p <0 . 0 0 1 ,B = 0.514, and 95% CI = [0.300; 0.728]."
