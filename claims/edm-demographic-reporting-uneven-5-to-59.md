# Reporting frequency of demographic categories in EDM publications is very uneven, ranging from 5% (SES) to 59% (education level)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across applicable EDM publications, education level was the most reported category (59%) and SES the least (5%), with the full range spanning 5% to 59%. [→ Paquette 2020](#paquette-2020)

## Evidence

### Paquette 2020

Paquette, Li, Baker, Ocumpaugh, & Andres. (2020). Who's learning? Using demographics in EDM research. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 12, No 3. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/404

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Coding of the 288 applicable publications shows uneven category reporting: education level 59%, geographic location 43%, gender 25%, age 19%, race 9%, SES 5%; the article notes "SES is almost always the least reported category".

> "Some categories, such as the education level of participants in the study, are often included (59% of the time), whereas others such as SES are much less common (5%)."
