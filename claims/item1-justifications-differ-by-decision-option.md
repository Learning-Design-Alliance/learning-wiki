# For item 1, the justifications students give differ significantly by decision option both before and after instruction, with medium effect sizes

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In the pre-test, students choosing the digital caliper tended to justify with personal considerations while those choosing the micrometer used data-quality and practical considerations; the difference across decision options is significant with medium effect sizes pre (w = 0 .42) and post (w = 0.45). ## Evidence

### Micol Alemani 2026

Micol Alemani, Karel Kok, Eva Philippaki. (2026). Students’ reasoning in choosing measurement instruments in an introductory physics laboratory course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.16638](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.16638)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Per-instrument coding of item 1 justifications (Fig. 4): pre-test caliper choosers used "justifications based on personal experience, easiness of use and personal biases" while micrometer choosers used data-quality and practical considerations; the printed effect sizes are w = 0 .42 (pre) and w = 0.45 (post).

> "We found for item 1 a statistical significant difference of medium effect between the justifications of the decisions options in both pre-test [χ2(6) = 44.76, p <0.0001, w = 0 .42] and post-test [ χ2(6) = 33 .73, p < 0.001, w = 0.45]."
