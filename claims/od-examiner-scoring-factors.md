## Evidence

### Régent Alexis 2023

Régent Alexis, Thampy Harish, Singh Mini. (2023). Assessing clinical reasoning in the OSCE: pilot-testing a novel oral debrief exercise. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04668-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reflexive thematic analysis of think-aloud transcripts and semi-structured interviews with sixteen OSCE examiners who scored three scripted OD videos. Examiners treated the OD as "a window into their clinical reasoning ability" and judged structured presentation, prioritisation and justification as markers of higher CR ability.

> "participants perceived CR was more strongly displayed when candidates successfully discriminated between relevant and less relevant information and in doing so, prioritised and justified their decisions."
