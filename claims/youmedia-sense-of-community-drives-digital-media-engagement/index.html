Teens 2013¶

Teens, Digital Media, and the Chicago Public Library. (2013). University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teens-digital-media-and-chicago-public-library

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Key finding from the Consortium report on YOUmedia Chicago. The report states the center is "cultivating a budding sense of community among teens who participate" and that this community sense and peer relationships drive new engagement with digital media. No effect sizes are printed.

"YOUmedia is cultivating a budding sense of community among teens who participate. This sense of community, along with peer relationships, serves as a potent force for driving teens to engage in digital media in new ways."
