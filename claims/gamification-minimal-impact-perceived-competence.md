## Evidence

### Liuyufeng Li 2024

Liuyufeng Li, Khe Foon Hew, Jiahui Du. (2024). Gamification enhances student intrinsic motivation, perceptions of autonomy and relatedness, but minimal impact on competency: a meta-analysis and systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10337-7

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Random-effects meta-analysis of the 12 independent studies reporting competence data. The article concludes "these gamification interventions yielded minimal effect in enhancing students' perceived competence," with a confidence interval barely above zero.

> "These data showed that the effect size of the influence of gamification on perceived competence was marginally significant (Hedges' g = 0.277, 95% CI [0.001, 0.553], p = 0.049)"
