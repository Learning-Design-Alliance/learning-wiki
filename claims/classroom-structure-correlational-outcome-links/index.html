Evidence¶

Patall 2023¶

Patall, E. A., Yates, N., Lee, J., Chen, S., Bhat, P., Lee, K., Beretvas, S. N., Guan, S., Cohen, M., Zhai, F., Brecht-Heitz, S., DeLorenzo, D., & Gonzales, K. (2023). A meta-analysis of teachers’ provision of structure in the classroom and students’ academic competence beliefs, engagement, and achievement. Educational Psychologist. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED631356

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

A quantitative meta-analysis of correlational studies of classroom structure and student outcomes pooled 191 samples from 165 studies, reporting "statistically significant correlations with achievement (.11), engagement (.28), and competence beliefs (.22)". The disengagement correlation (-.08) was not significant; equivalence was not tested. The printed correlations are small-to-small-to-moderate Pearson r values, not standardized effect sizes, so impact is coded as null.

"A meta-analysis of 191 samples from 165 correlational studies revealed statistically significant correlations with achievement (.11), engagement (.28), and competence beliefs (.22), and a statistically non-significant relationship with disengagement (-.08)."

Related Claims¶

  • Consistent with a dual process model of engagement, structure shows stronger associations with engagement than with disengagement — related
