## Evidence

### Innab Adnan 2026

Innab Adnan, Hakamy Essa, Abdulgafor Nedaa, AlOtaibi Nora Ghalib. (2026). The influence of self-efficacy and satisfaction with e-learning on academic performance among nursing students: a multisite study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09236-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent samples t-test comparing 135 students who attended e-learning training with 26 who did not. All three outcome comparisons non-significant (satisfaction p=.25, self-efficacy p=.06, performance p=.215).

> "However, there was no significant difference in satisfaction with e-learning, general self-efficacy, and perceived academic performance for those who did and who did not attend training courses regarding e-learning."
