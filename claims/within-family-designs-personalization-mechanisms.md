### von Stumm 2021

von Stumm, S. & Wertz, J. (2021). Who's learning? Using within-family studies to understand personalized learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00082-4

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

A conceptual argument from the commentary's section on contextual and instructor characteristics, proposing within-family studies as a route to understanding how children actively make their own environments in educational settings; no empirical data are reported.

> "Within-family studies can help here by elucidating how contextual characteristics, such as availability of resources in the home, affect the extent to which children personalize their home environment and evoke differential reactions from their parents."

### von Stumm 2021 (2)

von Stumm, S. & Wertz, J. (2021). Who's learning? Using within-family studies to understand personalized learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00082-4

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

A proposal, not a finding: the commentary suggests within-family designs could disentangle family-level drivers, e.g. socioeconomic status, from child-trait drivers of parents' differential responsiveness, and asks why some parents are more attuned to their children's needs than others.

> "Within-family designs enable testing to what extent parents' responsiveness may be driven by family-level characteristics (e.g., socioeconomic status) or by differences in their children's traits, and if parents' differential responsiveness occurs for some but not other trait domains."
