## Evidence

### Şahin Eda Adeviye 2026

Şahin Eda Adeviye, Şahin Turan, Ağlamış Savaş Özgür, Tekin Arzu Bilge, Şahin Hanifi. (2026). Outcomes of first 60 vaginal myomectomies by one gyn surgeon with 20 years of extensive vaginal surgical expertise. BMC Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12905-026-04715-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Chronological group comparison of 60 consecutive vaginal myomectomy cases at one center. The first 30 cases versus last 30 showed "the mean operative time decreased by 11.4 min (95% CI, 6.4–16.4 min)" with "a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.16)"; grouped ANOVA gave p = 0.001.

> "When the first 30 cases were compared with the last 30 cases, the mean operative time decreased by 11.4 min (95% CI, 6.4–16.4 min), corresponding to a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.16)."
