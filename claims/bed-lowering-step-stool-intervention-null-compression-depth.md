# In an intention-to-treat analysis, lowering the bed and pre-positioning a step stool did not significantly increase mean chest compression depth compared to control

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The intervention group (bed lowered, step stool prominently placed) achieved a mean compression depth not significantly different from the control group (p = 0.11). [→ Mayrand Kelsey P. 2015](#mayrand-kelsey-p-2015)

## Evidence

### Mayrand Kelsey P. 2015

Mayrand Kelsey P., Fischer Eric J., Ten Eyck Raymond P. (2015). A Simulation-based Randomized Controlled Study of Factors Influencing Chest Compression Depth. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2015.9.28167

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled mannequin simulation with 55 analyzed subjects (28 intervention, 27 control) allocated by block randomization; intention-to-treat comparison of mean compression depth. The intervention group mean was "39.3 (95% CI [35.4–43.2])mm" versus 34.6 mm in control, p = 0.11.

> "The intervention group achieved a mean compression depth of 39.3 (95% CI [35.4–43.2])mm compared to the control group 34.6 (95% CI [30.2–39.0])mm (p = 0.11)."
