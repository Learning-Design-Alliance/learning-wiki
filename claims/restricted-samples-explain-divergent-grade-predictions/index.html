The interview's key takeaways state that "some studies may find different conclusions" because of restricted samples or less-accurate weighted or self-reported GPAs, concluding that how a study is done and what data researchers use matters. No quantitative evidence is printed.

"Some studies may find different conclusions, often because they're looking at a restricted sample (e.g. students with high GPAs going to very selective schools) and/or using weighted or self-reported GPAs, which are less accurate."
