# Hispanic graduation rates at study institutions rose 3.5 percentage points from 2004 to 2010, roughly matching overall improvement

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mary Nguyen 2012

Mary Nguyen, Erin Ward Bibo, and Jennifer Engle. (2012). Advancing to Completion: Increasing degree attainment by improving graduation rates and closing gaps for Hispanic students. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED535510

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weighted analysis of IPEDS and College Results Online data for 391 four-year nonprofit institutions with at least 30 Hispanic students in both study years. The report states "Hispanic graduation rates increased by 3.5 percentage points, from 43.7 percent in 2004 to 47.2 percent in 2010."

> "from 2004 to 2010, Hispanic graduation rates increased by 3.5 percentage points, from 43.7 percent in 2004 to 47.2 percent in 2010. Similarly, the overall graduation rate improved by 3.3 percentage points, from 60.4 percent in 2004 to 63.7 percent in 2010."
