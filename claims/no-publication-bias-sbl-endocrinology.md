## Evidence

### Wu Zeyu 2024

Wu Zeyu, Huang Yiling, Lyu Ling, Huang Yu, Ping Fan. (2024). The efficacy of simulation-based learning versus non-simulation-based learning in endocrinology education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06010-z

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Publication-bias assessment of the knowledge and skill meta-analyses using funnel plots, Egger's regression and Begg's rank correlation in Stata 12.0. Neither test "revealed significant publication bias," though the theory-test Egger P of 0.051 is near the threshold.

> "Neither Egger’s test (theory test: P = 0.051; skill test: P = 0.662) nor Begg’s test (theory test: P = 0.108; skill test: P = 0.125) revealed significant publication bias."
