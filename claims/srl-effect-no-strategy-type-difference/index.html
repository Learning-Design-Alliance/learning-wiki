Evidence¶

Ergen 2017¶

Ergen, B., & Kanadli, S. (2017). The effect of self-regulated learning strategies on academic achievement: A meta-analysis study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 69, 55-74. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2017.69.4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Categorical moderator analysis under the random effects model comparing subgroups coded as cognitive (k=11), metacognitive (k=10), resource management (k=2), and motivational (k=4) strategies; Qbetween was not significant (p > .05).

"It was found in this study that the common effect size of self -regulated learning shows no significant difference according to self -regulated learning strategies (cognitive, metacognitive, motivational and resource management) (Qb=2.994, p>.05)."
