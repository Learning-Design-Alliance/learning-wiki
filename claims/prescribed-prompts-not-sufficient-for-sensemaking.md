## Evidence

### Martinuk 2010

Martinuk, M. and Ives, J. (2010). Do Prescribed Prompts Prime Sensemaking During Group Problem Solving? Physics Education Research conference proceedings style manuscript, University of British Columbia. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1111.5400](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1111.5400)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Audio-coded analysis of six group problem-solving episodes using a modified Scherr and Hammer epistemological framing scheme. The authors conclude that "the majority of segments fall well short of this upper limit" and that the prompts are "not, in general, effective in promoting sensemaking." No effect size is printed.

> "Treating the above benchmark as an estimate of the upper limit on the percentage of time spent in the Conceptual Discussion frame during quantitative group problem solving we see that the majority of segments fall well short of this upper limit."
