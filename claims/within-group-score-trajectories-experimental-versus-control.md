`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intra-group comparisons across the three assessments used one-way ANOVA or Kruskal-Wallis with Bonferroni-corrected post hoc tests. Table 2 prints experimental-group pairwise P values of 0.005, < 0.001 and < 0.001, and a control-group admission vs mid-term P of 0.643; no effect sizes are printed.

> "In the control group, no statistically significant difference was found between the admission and mid-term examination scores, while statistically significant differences were observed between the entrance and exit examination scores as well as between the mid-term and exit examination scores."
