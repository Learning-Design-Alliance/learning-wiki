## Evidence

### Nomura Roseli Mieko Yamamoto 2023

Nomura Roseli Mieko Yamamoto, Reis Fabiana Medeiros D., Gonçalves Amanda Menezes, de Proença Carolina Matos. (2023). Obstetric simulation for undergraduate medical education: how to improve students’ self-confidence and expectation according to gender. Revista da Associação Médica Brasileira. https://doi.org/10.1590/1806-9282.20221625

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of summed subscale scores (Table 2, n=115) using Wilcoxon paired tests. The article reports the expectation subscale sum fell (median 9 to 8, p=0.046) while "No difference was observed in the subscale "interest."" (p=0.234).

> "the median score was significantly higher on the subscales “comprehension,” “knowledge of procedures,” and “motivation,” but significantly lower on the subscale “expectation.” No difference was observed in the subscale “interest.”"
