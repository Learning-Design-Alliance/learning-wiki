## Evidence

### Chaumont Corentin 2024

Chaumont Corentin, Morgat Charles, Ollitrault Pierre, Brejoux Celia, Extramiana Fabrice, Milliez Paul, Savoure Arnaud, Al Hamoud Raphaël, Eltchaninoff Hélène, Anselme Frederic. (2024). How to improve medical students’ ECG interpretation skills ? Multicenter survey and results of a comparative study evaluating a new educational approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05929-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective multicenter comparative study, teaching method assigned nonrandomly by center (control n = 27, workshop n = 25). The workshop group's gain exceeded the control group's, "p < 0.001", on a 100-point 10-tracing test graded by a blinded instructor.

> "the improvement was significantly greater in the workshop group as compared to the control group (+ 26 ± 11 vs. + 11 ± 6, p < 0.001)"

### Chaumont Corentin 2024 (2)

Chaumont Corentin, Morgat Charles, Ollitrault Pierre, Brejoux Celia, Extramiana Fabrice, Milliez Paul, Savoure Arnaud, Al Hamoud Raphaël, Eltchaninoff Hélène, Anselme Frederic. (2024). How to improve medical students’ ECG interpretation skills ? Multicenter survey and results of a comparative study evaluating a new educational approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05929-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group paired t-tests on the 30-minute 10-tracing ECG test at baseline and 5 weeks showed significant improvement in both groups, each "p < 0.0001"; baseline scores were similar (p = 0.40).

> "The exam results significantly improved from baseline to 5-week assessment in both groups (control group: 33 ± 12 to 44 ± 12, p < 0.0001; workshop group: 36 ± 13 to 62 ± 12, p < 0.0001 in the)."
