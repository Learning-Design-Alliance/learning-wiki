Evidence¶

Morris 2025¶

Morris, W., Crossley, S., Holmes, L., Ou, C., Dascalu, M., & McNamara, D. (2025). Formative Feedback on Student-Authored Summaries in Intelligent Textbooks Using Large Language Models. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 35, 1022–1043. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00395-0

q2 · i3 · design · r2

Post-hoc concurrent-validity test on 94 professional section summaries from the OpenStax Macroeconomics 2e textbook, scored matched versus randomized-unmatched to their sections by the best-performing Longformer. Matched summaries "scored higher than average in wording and content"; the printed effect sizes were d = 1.56 for Content and d = 0.938 for Wording.

"The differences between matched and unmatched content were statistically significant ( p <.001) in both domains with large effect sizes, but Content reported a greater effect size (d = 1.56) than Wording (d = 0.938)."
