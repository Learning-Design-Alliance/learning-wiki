# Correct retrievals were faster and more accurate than restudied-only items at final test, but response speed did not differ between one and two prior tests

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` RTs for the second test in the STT-T condition were significantly faster than RTs for the first test in the SST-T condition (d = 0.60). [→ Liu 2016](#liu-2016)
`q3 i3` At Phase 4, RTs were faster for items recalled correctly at least once than for restudied-only items (d = 0.95 and d = 1.01), with no reliable difference between one and two prior tests (P = 0.33). [→ Liu 2016 (2)](#liu-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Liu 2016

Liu, X. L., & Reder, L. M. (2016). fMRI exploration of pedagogical benefits of repeated testing: when more is not always better. Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.476

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Reaction-time analysis of correct test trials in the within-subject behavioral experiment (n = 20). "RTs for the second test in the STT‐T condition were significantly faster than RTs for the first test in the SST‐T condition, t 19 = 2.21, P = 0.039, d = 0.60".

> "RTs for the second test in the STT‐T condition were significantly faster than RTs for the first test in the SST‐T condition, t 19 = 2.21, P = 0.039, d = 0.60."

### Liu 2016 (2)

Liu, X. L., & Reder, L. M. (2016). fMRI exploration of pedagogical benefits of repeated testing: when more is not always better. Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.476

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Phase 4 RT analysis in the same experiment. The article reports "a significant effect of type of learning condition, F 2,38 = 11.54, P < 0.001, ηp2=0.38", with faster RTs for items recalled at least once (d = 0.95; d = 1.01); the STT-T versus SST-T comparison was not reliable, P = 0.33.

> "there was a significant effect of type of learning condition, F 2,38 = 11.54, P < 0.001, ηp2=0.38, such that RTs were faster for items that had been recalled correctly at least once (STT‐T or SST‐T condition) compared with items that had only been restudied (SSS‐T condition), t 19 = 3.38, P = 0.003, d = 0.95; t 19 = 4.26, P < 0.001, d = 1.01."
