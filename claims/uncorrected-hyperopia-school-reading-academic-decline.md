### Kulp MT 2026 (2)

Kulp MT, Ciner EB, Candy TR, Ying G-s, Hall C, Quinn G, Moore B. (2026). Impact of uncorrected hyperopia on visual function and academic performance in preschool and school-age children. Optometry and Vision Science. https://doi.org/10.1002/ovs2.70018

`q2 · i3` · `review · r3`

The review attributes to Thurston a synthesis of 6 studies and 3082 children reporting "a clinically meaningful effect size of -0.85 in reduced reading for hyperopic children."

> "Thurston reported a clinically meaningful effect size of -0.85 in reduced reading for hyperopic children (6 studies and 3082 children)."

### Kulp MT 2026 (3)

Kulp MT, Ciner EB, Candy TR, Ying G-s, Hall C, Quinn G, Moore B. (2026). Impact of uncorrected hyperopia on visual function and academic performance in preschool and school-age children. Optometry and Vision Science. https://doi.org/10.1002/ovs2.70018

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The review reports the Baltimore Reading and Eye Disease Study, in which 321 elementary school children were assessed, finding children with uncorrected hyperopia >=1 D "did not perform as well on baseline reading assessments" as age-typical peers. No effect size is printed.

> "The Baltimore Reading and Eye Disease Study found that elementary school children (n = 321) with uncorrected hyperopia >=1 D did not perform as well on baseline reading assessments as children with age-typical refractive error."
