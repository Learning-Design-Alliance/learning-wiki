## Evidence

### Jiang Luyang 2023

Jiang Luyang, Yang Qingyue, Li Qingyue, Jiang Bailin, Laba Ciren, Feng Yi. (2023). Optimal Duration of High-Fidelity Simulator Training for Bronchoscope-Guided Intubation: A Noninferiority Randomized Trial. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000739

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Multicenter randomized noninferiority trial; 16 participants per group each performed one FOB intubation in a patient within 1 week of training. Mean intubation times were "81.9 (65.7–98.1) seconds in group New and 97.0 (77.4–116.6) seconds in group Reference"; noninferiority was claimed against a 10-s margin.

> "The mean intubation time (95% CI) in patients was 81.9 (65.7–98.1) seconds in group New and 97.0 (77.4–116.6) seconds in group Reference. The upper bound of the 1-sided 97.5% CI for the mean difference in intubation time in patients between group New and group Reference was 9.3 seconds"
