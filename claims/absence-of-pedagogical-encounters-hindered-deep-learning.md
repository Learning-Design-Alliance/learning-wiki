# Absence of pedagogical encounters with teachers and patients prevented students from placing presentation content in a larger context and hindered deeper learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students reported that lack of interaction with teachers and patients, and absence of follow-up discussion and feedback, hindered deeper learning of the presentation content. [→ Hyll Mary 2019](#hyll-mary-2019)

## Evidence

### Hyll Mary 2019

Hyll Mary, Schvarcz Robert, Manninen Katri. (2019). Exploring how medical students learn with the help of a digital presentation: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1569-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the interview analysis, students expressed that knowledge must be formed during patient contact; "Connecting knowledge to patients enabled them to put the information in a larger perspective, giving it meaning." They missed a real person to answer questions and preferred seeing the teacher's body language. Interestingly, students did not mention interaction with peer students.

> "There was no follow-up discussion in connection with the presentation, and this lack of feedback was also described as hindering deeper learning."
