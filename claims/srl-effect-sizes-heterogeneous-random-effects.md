## Evidence

### Guntur 2024

Guntur, M., & Purnomo, Y. W. (2024). A Meta-Analysis of Self-Regulated Learning Interventions Studies on Learning Outcomes in Online and Blended Environments. Online Learning Journal, 28(3). https://doi.org/10.24059/olj.v28i3.4025

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Heterogeneity test comparing the Q statistic to the χ2 table with df = n-1; because Q exceeded χ2 at p < .05, the fixed effects model was rejected and the random effects model used for pooling the 15 studies.

> "As the heterogeneity among the effect sizes of the studies was high (Q > χ2, p < .05), the common effect size was calculated following the random effects model."
