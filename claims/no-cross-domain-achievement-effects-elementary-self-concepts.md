## Evidence

### Lohbeck 2019

Annette Lohbeck. (2019). Social and dimensional comparison effects on academic self-concepts and self-perceptions of effort in elementary school children. Educational Psychology, 39:1, 133-150. https://doi.org/10.1080/01443410.2018.1527018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Path analysis (Model 2) with 200 fourth graders found positive within-domain grade-to-self-concept paths (e.g. MSC: b = 0.67, GSC: b = 0.68, p < .001 for competence; b = 0.61 and 0.58 for affective), while cross-domain paths were near zero, e.g. "there were no significantly negative cross-domain achievement effects".

> "In support of the I/E model's predictions, both self-reported grades revealed significantly positive effects on academic self-concepts of corresponding domains, irrespective of the component of academic self-concepts. However, there were no significantly negative cross-domain achievement effects on competence or affective self-concepts."
