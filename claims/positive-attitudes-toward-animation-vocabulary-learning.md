# Teacher observations and student opinions indicated relatively positive attitudes toward using animations in vocabulary learning

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The class teacher observed more enthusiasm in the animation-based class, and students in that class reported the animation was useful for learning and remembering words. [→ Kayaoğlu 2011](#kayaoglu-2011)

## Evidence

### Kayaoğlu 2011

Kayaoğlu, M. N., Dağ Akbaş, R., & Öztürk, Z. (2011). A Small Scale Experimental Study: Using Animations to Learn Vocabulary. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932222

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative teacher observation and student opinion data collected after the applications in both classes. The teacher reported "considerably more enthusiasm" in the animation-based class; students' quoted opinions emphasized seeing and hearing together aiding word learning.

> "When the teacher was asked his observations about both groups he stated that there was considerably more enthusiasm for the activity presented in animation-based method as compared to the one presented in traditionally paper-based method."
