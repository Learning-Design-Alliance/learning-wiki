Evidence¶

Roschelle 2020¶

Roschelle, J. & Hodkowski, N.M. (2020). Using Research on Homework to Improve Remote and Hybrid Learning [White Paper]. San Mateo, CA: Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/107

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review attribution: the paper reports, citing Fernández-Alonso et al. (2015), negative impacts from compliance-focused parenting; hedged as "some researchers."

"If parents and guardians focus on compliance (e.g., "Do your homework! Now!"), some researchers report negative impacts on student learning (Fernández-Alonso et al., 2015)."

Roschelle 2020 (2)¶

Roschelle, J. & Hodkowski, N.M. (2020). Using Research on Homework to Improve Remote and Hybrid Learning [White Paper]. San Mateo, CA: Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/107

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review attributions to Vazquez et al. (2020) on process-supportive parental help and Bergman & Chan (2019) on low-cost text reminders; no effect sizes printed.

"Conversely, when parents and guardians focus on supporting student effort and helping with the learning process, the effects can be very good (Vazquez et al., 2020). Studies are beginning to show that even simple text messages to parents to remind them when and how to help can be very effective (Bergman & Chan, 2019)."
