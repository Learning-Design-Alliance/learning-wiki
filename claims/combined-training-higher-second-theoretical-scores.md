## Evidence

### Sheng Jie 2022

Sheng Jie, Zhang Congdi, Gao Zhengkun, Yan Yimin, Meng Yucheng, Ren Shiqi, Liu Bin, Zhang Baoping. (2022). Virtual versus jaw simulation in inlay preparation preclinical teaching: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03930-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA of second theoretical examination scores in an RCT where 76 undergraduates were randomly assigned to four training groups of 19 each. The combined groups outscored the single-method groups; the article reports "Group V-J had the best results (P < 0.05)".

> "the results of Group V-J (63.5 ± 2.89) and Group J-V (60.5 ± 3.25) were higher than those of Group V (57.5 ± 3.13) and Group J (58.0 ± 3.67), among which Group V-J had the best results (P < 0.05)"
