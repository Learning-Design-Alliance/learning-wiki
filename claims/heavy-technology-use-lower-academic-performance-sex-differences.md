`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Ordered logistic regression analyses (Table 2, Model 2) adjusted for age, ethnoracial background, subjective SES, smoking, alcohol and cannabis use, stratified by sex because sex interactions were significant (p < 0.05). The article reports the adjusted odds ratios above for females and males relative to 2 h or less of daily use; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "Compared to technology use for 2 h or less per day, daily technology use of 3 to 4 h (AOR = 0.69, 95% CI = 0.52–0.91) or more than 5 h (AOR = 0.46, 95% CI = 0.34–0.61) was associated with lower academic performance among females, while only daily use of more than 5 h was associated with lower academic performance among males (AOR = 0.73, 95% CI = 0.55–0.98)."
