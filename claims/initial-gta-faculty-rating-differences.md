## Evidence

### Kendall 2013

Kendall, K. D., & Schussler, E. E. (2013). Evolving Impressions: Undergraduate Perceptions of Graduate Teaching Assistants and Faculty Members over a Semester. CBE—Life Sciences Education 12(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-07-0110

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on initial surveys from 255 undergraduates in eight biology lecture/laboratory courses found significant initial differences for confident (Z = −4.623, p = 0.000), engaging (Z = −2.998, p = 0.003), enthusiastic (Z = −4.343, p = 0.000), nervous (Z = −4.053, p = 0.000), and uncertain (Z = −3.828, p = 0.000), with "students perceived GTAs to be more nervous and uncertain, and faculty members to be more confident, engaging, and enthusiastic."

> "These ratings indicated that students perceived GTAs to be more nervous and uncertain, and faculty members to be more confident, engaging, and enthusiastic."
