# The second-order multidimensional IRT model for testlets is formally equivalent to the testlet model, and both are restricted bifactor models

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Algebraic manipulation of the second-order model's equations shows it is formally equivalent to the testlet model, which itself is a bifactor model with proportionality restrictions within each testlet. [→ Rijmen 2009](#rijmen-2009)

## Evidence

### Rijmen 2009

Rijmen, F. (2009). Three Multidimensional Models for Testlet-Based Tests: Formal Relations and an Empirical Comparison. ETS Research Report RR-09-37. http://www.ets.org/research/contact.html

`q2 · i?`

Analytical derivation comparing Equation 7 to the bifactor model's Equation 2 under standard normal latent variables. The derivation shows the second-order model is a restricted bifactor model with the same restrictions that yield the testlet model, and the article notes this equivalence had not been explicitly described before, to the knowledge of the author.

> "some further algebraic manipulations of Equation 7 clearly show that the second-order model is formally equivalent to the testlet model"
