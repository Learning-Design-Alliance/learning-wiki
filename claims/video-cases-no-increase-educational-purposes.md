# Video cases in a classroom management course do not increase the number of educational purposes linked to a case by pre-service teachers.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental group using video cases showed no pre-post increase in the number of educational purposes linked to the video case, while the control group showed a learning loss. [→ Geerts 2018](#geerts-2018)

## Evidence

### Geerts 2018

Geerts, W. M., Steenbeek, H. W., & van Geert, P. L. C. (2018). Effect of Video-Cases on the Acquisition of Situated Knowledge of Teachers. International Education Studies, 11(1), 64. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p64

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment in which 41 second-year Dutch pre-service teachers were randomly divided into a video-case course and a written-case course. In the experimental group there is no difference between the pre- and post-tes t; the control group showed a pre-post decline of d = -0.67, p<0.001, described as a moderately sized learning loss.

> "In the experimental group, there is no difference between the pre- and post-tes t, and consequentially there is no effect at all in this group."
