### Gwynne 2024

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., Young, C., & Hart, H. (2024). Student experiences through the COVID-19 pandemic: Attendance, online learning platform use, and annual survey reports. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/district-data/metrics/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of publicly available CPS district attendance data for students in preschool through twelfth grade, 2010-11 through 2022-23. Absence rates "increased considerably in all grade levels, by 2-7 percentage points" in the remote year and rose further in 2021-22; in 2022-23 rates were 3-6 points above 2018-19.

> "In the remote/ hybrid year (2020–21), official absence rates increased considerably in all grade levels, by 2-7 percentage points. They increased even further the next year (2021–22)"
