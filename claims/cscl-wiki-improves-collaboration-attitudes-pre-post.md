## Evidence

### Vassilios Giannakos & Maria Darra 2019

Vassilios Giannakos & Maria Darra. (2019). The Contribution of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning to the Development of Collaboration Between Students: Results of Pilot Implementation in Greek Secondary Education. International Education Studies, 12(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v12n3p158

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post comparison of the student collaboration self-assessment rubric completed by the 25 experimental-group students before and after the thirteen-hour wiki-supported intervention. The article reports the attitude toward collaboration score rose from 77 to 101 points, described as "+ 19%", while confidence decreased by 2%. No statistical test or effect size is printed.

> "the collaboration rate increases from 77 points to 101 points after the experiment (+ 19%), the effectiveness moves from 89 to 93 points (+ 3%), confidence in collaboration gathered 105 points before and 103 after the experiment (-2%)"
