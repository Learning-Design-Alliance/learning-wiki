### Burr 2023

Burr, E., Burho, J., & Perez, A. (2023). Pre-Referral Processes in California State Preschool Programs: How Practitioners Decide to Refer Multilingual Children for Special Education Evaluation. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655866

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview study of CSPP coordinators. A special education coordinator worried children needing services may be overlooked "because it's assumed that it's an EL issue"; others cited fear of over-identification and pandemic-era referral increases.

> "There's no standardized assessment for figuring out how good a 3-year-old's English is. It's not existing, nobody's doing it."
