q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Systematic review synthesis of three studies meeting WWC group design standards. The review concludes "one study showed a statistically significant positive effect, one study showed a substantively important positive effect, and one study showed an indeterminate effect," yielding the potentially positive effects rating.

"Thus, for the mathematics achievement domain, one study showed a statistically significant positive effect, one study showed a substantively important positive effect, and one study showed an indeterminate effect."
