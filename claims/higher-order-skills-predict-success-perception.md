Akçay Ahmet Oğuz, Karahan Engin, Kara Ahmet. (2026). The role of problem-solving and decision-making skills in linking critical thinking dispositions to perceptions of success. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04918-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis in the same sample; the three predictors together account for approximately 33% of perceived success. The article reports the printed t and p values for the decision-making and problem-solving paths.

> "a one-unit increase in decision-making skills in primary school students increases their perceptions of success by 0.20 (t = 3.215, p < 0.01). Similarly, a one-unit increase in problem-solving skills in primary school students increases their perception of success by 0.24 (t = 4.638, p<. 001)"
