### Tiffany D. Do 2025

Tiffany D. Do, Usama Bin Shafqat, Elsie Ling, and Nikhil Sarda. (2025). PAIGE: Examining Learning Outcomes and Experiences with Personalized AI-Generated Educational Podcasts. PREPRINT. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3706598.3713460](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713460)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the 3x3 user study of 180 U.S. college students, the Philosophy subject showed a significant main effect of content style on test score, and the post-hoc test revealed that "the Personalized condition (M=7.30, SD=1.45) had significantly higher scores than both the Generalized condition" and the Textbook condition. No effect size is printed.

> "a post-hoc test revealed that the Personalized condition (M=7.30, SD=1.45) had significantly higher scores than both the Generalized condition (M=5.85, SD=1.35),𝑝 < 0.01, and the Textbook condition (M=5.95, SD=1.57),𝑝 = 0.01."
