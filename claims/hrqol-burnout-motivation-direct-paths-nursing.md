## Evidence

### Torkani F 2025

Torkani F, Ahmadi S-H, Binazir A, Fath MM, Shafigh N, Asadiparvar-Masouleh H, Vasli P. (2025). The relationship between health-related quality of life and academic success in nursing students: the mediating role of academic burnout and academic motivation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06661-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct-path coefficients (Table 4, paths a1–a4) from the cross-sectional path analysis in 261 nursing students. The article reports significant unstandardized effects of 0.29, -1.02 and -0.14, and a non-significant effect on extrinsic motivation (B = 0.09, p = 0.56).

> "The results indicated that the direct paths from HRQoL to AB (a1, p ≤ 0.001), intrinsic motivation (a3, p ≤ 0.05), and amotivation (a4, p ≤ 0.05) were significant. This implies that an increase in one unit of HRQoL leads to an increase in intrinsic motivation by 0.29 and a decrease in AB and amotivation by 1.02 and 0.014, respectively. In contrast, the direct effect of HRQoL on extrinsic motivation was not significant (a2, B = 0.09, p = 0.56)."
