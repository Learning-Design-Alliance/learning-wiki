Evidence¶

Voet 2018¶

Voet, M. & De Wever, B. (2018). Effects of immersion in inquiry-based learning on student teachers' educational beliefs. Instructional Science, 46(3), 383-403. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-017-9439-8

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative analysis of posttest written comments, coded independently by two researchers (K=.81 for increased understanding of history's nature), found many student teachers described recognizing that sources can be interpreted in different ways. No effect size printed.

"In total, 109 students explicitly stated that the experience had improved their understand ing of how historical inquiry worked. In several cases, student teachers stressed that they now had a better understanding of history's interpretative nature."
