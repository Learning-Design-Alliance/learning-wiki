## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Students’ Understanding of Stern Gerlach Experiment. arXiv:1602.06374 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06374)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Preliminary classroom evaluation (n = 22 undergraduates): pre-test, then QuILT, then post-test. The article reports "The average pre-test score was 53% and the average post-test score was 92%", which the authors call encouraging; it also reports post-test performance "signiﬁcantly improved compared to pre-test performance" during individual administration. No effect size is printed.

> "We conducted preliminary evaluation of the QuILT in a class with 22 undergraduate students. Students had traditional instruction about the SGE, took the pre-test, then worked on the tutorial and then took the post-test (some questions from the pre-test and post-test are in the Appendix). The average pre-test score was 53% and the average post-test score was 92%, which is encouraging."
