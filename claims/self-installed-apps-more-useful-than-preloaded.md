## Evidence

### Archibald D 2014

Archibald D, Macdonald Colla J, Plante J, Hogue RJ, Fiallos J. (2014). Residents' and preceptors' perceptions of the use of the iPad for clinical teaching in a family medicine residency program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-174

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey and focus-group findings on apps and resources in the pilot. The article reports "preceptors found the self-installed apps more useful than pre-loaded apps", naming Show Me and iAnnotate; residents reported RxFiles most beneficial and preceptors favored LexiComp.

> "In most cases, preceptors found the self-installed apps more useful than pre-loaded apps. These included for example "Show Me" an app that displays a white board, audio and video, and "iAnnotate" which is an app that can edit PDFs."
