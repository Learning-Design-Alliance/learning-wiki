# Graduates from a vertically integrated curriculum receive higher physician scores for active professional development than non-VI graduates

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Physicians rated VI (Dutch) candidates significantly higher on the facet of competence active professional development, which includes reflection and asking for feedback. [→ Wijnen-Meijer M 2015](#wijnen-meijer-m-2015)

## Evidence

### Wijnen-Meijer M 2015

Wijnen-Meijer M, ten Cate O, van der Schaaf M, Burgers C, Borleffs J, Harendza S. (2015). Vertically integrated medical education and the readiness for practice of graduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0514-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional performance assessment (UHTRUST) of 30 Dutch VI and 29 German non-VI near-graduates, each scored by two physicians on seven facets of competence. Physicians rated the Dutch VI candidates higher on "active professional development" (Mann-Whitney r = .31).

> "Regarding the FOC "active professional development" the Dutch candidates (M = 3.55; SD = .77) received higher scores than the German candidates (M = 2.81; SD = 1.14; U = 278; z = -2.41; p = .02, r = .31)."

### Wijnen-Meijer M 2015 (2)

Wijnen-Meijer M, ten Cate O, van der Schaaf M, Burgers C, Borleffs J, Harendza S. (2015). Vertically integrated medical education and the readiness for practice of graduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0514-z

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Definitional statement of the facet: the article explains that this FOC covers "self-assessment, reflection, asking for feedback and setting and achieving learning goals", and links it to readiness for continuing education.

> "The complete description of this FOC is: "The physician aims for quality and professional development by means of a critical attitude towards himself and his environment, study, self-assessment, reflection, asking for feedback and setting and achieving learning goals."
