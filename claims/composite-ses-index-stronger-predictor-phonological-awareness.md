# Composite SES indices predict phonological awareness more strongly than single SES indicators

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` SES measurement type moderated the SES-to-phonological-awareness path, with the composite SES index a stronger predictor than a single index in accuracy (β = 0.05, p = 0.033) and fluency (β = 0.05, p = 0.03) models. [→ Li 2021](#li-2021)

## Evidence

### Li 2021

Li, J., Peng, P., Ding, N., Ma, X., & Zhao, J. (2021). How does family socioeconomic status influence children's reading ability? Evidence from meta-analytic structural equation modeling. https://www.frontiersin.org

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

OSMASEM moderation analysis restricted to the 100 articles reporting SES measurement. The omnibus moderation tests were only marginally significant; the specific SES-to-PA path moderation reached p = 0.033 and p = 0.03, explaining about 19.43% and 21.2% of heterogeneity.

> "the omnibus tests with SES measurement type in the reading accuracy and reading fluency models are marginally significant (reading accuracy: χ2(5) = 10.67, p = 0.058; reading fluency: χ2(5) = 10.33, p = 0.066)"
