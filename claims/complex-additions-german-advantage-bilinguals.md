# Complex additions are solved faster and more accurately in German than French across all five bilingual proficiency stages

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Complex additions were performed faster and more accurately when the task language was German than French, with large effects on RT and CR. [→ Van Rinsveld A 2015](#van-rinsveld-a-2015)

## Evidence

### Van Rinsveld A 2015

Van Rinsveld A, Brunner M, Landerl K, Schiltz C and Ugen S (2015) The relation between language and arithmetic in bilinguals: insights from different stages of language acquisition. Front. Psychol. 6:265. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00265

`q3 · i3`

In the complex-addition ANOVA of the 193-participant transversal study, the German task-language advantage was significant for both RTs and correct response rates, with η² = 0.522 for RT and η² = 0.381 for CR.

> "In general, complex additions were also performed faster and more accurately when the task language was German than when it was French, RT: F(1, 185) = 201. 922; p < 0. 001; η 2 = 0. 522 and CR: F(1, 185) = 113. 630; p < 0. 001; η 2 = 0. 381"
