Document-based comparative analysis of statutory curricula and examination specifications across fifteen countries, citing the Eurydice comparative analysis, finds that "in several systems a student may complete general secondary education" without programming exposure.

"programming instruction across European secondary systems ranges from compulsory standalone subjects to entirely optional or cross-subject integration, meaning that in several systems a student may complete general secondary education with digital tool literacy but no formal programming exposure"

"the MAV applies exclusively to the gymnasium pathway, attended by approximately one fourth of Swiss youth. The majority who enter the vocational dual-track system fall outside its scope"
