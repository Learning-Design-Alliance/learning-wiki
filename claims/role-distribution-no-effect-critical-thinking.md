### Jacob Feinleib 2026

Jacob Feinleib, Matthew Dew, and N.G. Holmes. (2026). Share, Rotate, Split: The Effects of Group Work Role Distributions on Student Outcomes. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/1n3c-v2fj](https://doi.org/10.1103/1n3c-v2fj)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical linear modeling of survey data from three introductory physics lab courses at two institutions, relating post-semester PLIC scores to reported role distributions while controlling for pre-test scores and course-semester clustering. The article reports no significant effect; no effect size is printed for this finding in the quoted text.

> "We found that a student's role distribution had no significant effect on their PLIC performance."
