## Evidence

### Feng Xiaosu 2025

Feng Xiaosu, Ren Shuanquan, Shi Peng. (2025). The relationship and mechanism of screen time and academic performance among adolescents: an empirical study based on CEPS. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1533327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the CEPS path analysis, 6 of 21 paths were non-significant, including screen time → BMI (p = 0.825) and screen time → subjective health perception (p = 0.467); the article states the pathway "through BMI and subjective health perceptions does not hold". Hypothesis H2b was only partially supported.

> "This suggests that the pathway of screen time acting on cognitive and academic performance through BMI and subjective health perceptions does not hold, the pathway of screen time acting on academic performance through mental health does not hold, and the pathway of screen time acting on academic performance through Pathway 10 does not hold."
