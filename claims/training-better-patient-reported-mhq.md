## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Total MHQ score at 6 months was 84.6 (15.5) for the trained group vs 77.0 (22.7) for controls (P < 0.001), with significant differences in work performance (P < 0.001), pain (P < 0.001), aesthetics (P = 0.002) and satisfaction (P = 0.042). [→ He Xuecheng MD 2025](#he-xuecheng-md-2025)
