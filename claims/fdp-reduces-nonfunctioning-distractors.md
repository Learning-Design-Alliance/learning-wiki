## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Abdulghani 2015

Abdulghani, H.M. et al. (2015). Faculty development programs improve the quality of Multiple Choice Questions items' writing. Sci. Rep. 5, 9556. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep09556

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square comparison of non-functioning distractors (options chosen by fewer than 5% of examinees) across 240 pre- and 240 post-training items. The article reports NFDs "less (n513)" post-training versus n=28 pre-training, χ²=6.00, p=0.02.

> "The numbers of non-functional distractors (NFDs) were also less (n513) in the year 2013–2014 (post-training) and it was more than double in case of the year 2012–2013 (pre-training) (n528) (x256.0, p50.02)."

### Abdulghani 2015 (2)

Abdulghani, H.M. et al. (2015). Faculty development programs improve the quality of Multiple Choice Questions items' writing. Sci. Rep. 5, 9556. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep09556

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-course ANOVA (Table 3) shows the NFD pre-post difference reached significance only for the renal block (F=8.566, p=0.004); respiratory (F=0.687, p=0.408) and cardiovascular (F=0.073, p=0.788) were non-significant.

> "Renal Difficulty Index (P) 7.852 0.006 Discrimination Index (DI) 0.422 0.517 Non-function distractors (NFD) 8.566 0.004"
