### Huang Meilan 2024

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Formative mini-CEX medical interview domain in the three-group RCT (n = 30 per group). The article reports the OSP-TCM group's "superior performance" with printed means, SDs, and pairwise p-values; the FSP–OSP contrast was not significant (Pc = 0.99).

> "The OSP-TCM group demonstrated superior performance compared to both FSP-TCM and TT groups in terms of medical interview scores, with significant difference between OSP-TCM and TT groups (TT: mean 6.43 SD 0.94, FSP-TCM: 7.03 SD 0.93, OSP-TCM: mean 7.07 SD 0.98, Pa = 0.04, Pb = 0.03)."
