## Evidence

### Möller 2020

Möller, J., Zitzmann, S., Helm, F., Machts, N., & Wolff, F. (2020). A Meta-Analysis of Relations Between Achievement and Self-Concept. Review of Educational Research, 90(3), 376–419. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654320919354

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Multivariate random-effects meta-analysis of the classic I/E model (k = 118 data sets, n = 213,121 participants) pooling path analyses of the six bivariate correlations. The authors report "an average path coefficient of β = .57" and β = .46, both strong positive social comparison effects.

> "We found an average path coefficient of β = .57 between mathematics achievement and mathematics self-concept and an average path coefficient of β = .46 between L1 achievement and L1 self-concept."
