## Subclaims
`q3 i0` For FSM pupils, effect sizes were -0.30 for Chatterbooks and -0.09 for Chatterbooks Plus, neither statistically significant, so results should be interpreted as chance findings. [→ Styles 2014](#styles-2014)

## Evidence

### Styles 2014

Styles, B., Clarkson, R., & Fowler, K. (2014). Chatterbooks: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581155

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Pre-specified subgroup analysis on the primary reading outcome using free school meals eligibility as a proxy for pupil premium. Both subgroup effect sizes had confidence intervals passing through zero and were marked "No" for statistical significance; the report notes subgroup analyses are always less secure than overall findings.

> "Chatterbooks (FSM pupils) 37 -0.30 (-0.63, 0.03) -4 No Chatterbooks Plus (FSM pupils) 40 -0.09 (-0.39, 0.21) -1 No"
