## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In BDNF+/− slices, exogenous BDNF applied at stage I or stage II alone failed to rescue L-LTP, but perfusion throughout the experiment completely rescued it, indicating two BDNF-dependent stages. [→ Pang 2016](#pang-2016)

## Evidence

### Pang 2016

Pang, P. T., Nagappan, G., Guo, W. & Lu, B. (2016). Extracellular and intracellular cleavages of proBDNF required at two distinct stages of late-phase LTP. npj Science of Learning 1, 16003. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Electrophysiological field recordings in hippocampal slices from BDNF+/− mice. BDNF applied 60 min before to 5 min after l-TBS (101.8 ± 8.6% vs 115.5 ± 12.7%, P = 0.408) or only after l-TBS (105.1 ± 5.8%, P = 0.788) "failed to rescue L-LTP", whereas whole-experiment perfusion rescued it.

> "Perfusion of BDNF throughout the entire experiment completely rescued L-LTP in BDNF+/− mice (Figure 1c, control, 101.8 ± 8.6%; +BDNF, 155.7 ± 9.7%, P o 0.005)."
