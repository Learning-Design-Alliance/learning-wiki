# Handwriting duration of the first letter improves massively between Grade 1 and Grade 3, then plateaus

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` First-letter writing duration decreased with grade, with 1st graders slower than 3rd and 5th graders, who did not differ from each other. [→ Danna 2021](#danna-2021)

## Evidence

### Danna 2021

Danna, J., Longcamp, M., Nalborczyk, L., Velay, J.-L., Commengé, C., & Jover, M. (2021). Interaction between orthographic and graphomotor constraints in learning to write. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3596-880X

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on first-letter duration in the main experiment with 65 students across Grades 1, 3 and 5 writing five-letter words on a digitizing tablet. The article reports "a main effect of Grade on mean duration, F(2, 62) = 63.20, p <.001. η²p = 0.67", with post-hoc tests showing 1st graders slower than the older groups.

> "The ANOVA also revealed a main effect of Grade on mean duration, F(2, 62) = 63.20, p <.001. η²p = 0.67."
