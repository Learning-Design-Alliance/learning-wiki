## Evidence

### Cheng-Han Yu and Angela Ebeling 2026

Cheng-Han Yu and Angela Ebeling. (2026). Active Learning with Bayesian Reasoning: A POGIL-Based Pedagogy in Introductory Statistics. arXiv:2606.08660. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.08660](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.08660)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Model-based results from the retained main-effects Bayesian bivariate GLM (M0) fitted to 94 complete student records (48 POGIL, 46 lecture) from four sections across back-to-back semesters. Posterior means for expected exam scores were 83.0 (POGIL) and 82.1 (Lecture) with wide 95% credible intervals; "the 95 percent credible interval included 0."

> "For teaching style, the posterior mean difference POGIL minus Lecture in expected exam points was small, and the 95 percent credible interval included 0."
