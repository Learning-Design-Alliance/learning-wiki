## Evidence

### Kidayi Paulo L. 2025

Kidayi Paulo L., Dausen Eyeshope J., Ndile Menti, Sixsmith Jane, Mawona Zuhura M., Berntsen Karin, Manangwa Saturini E., Smit Joya M., Rogathi Jane, de Zeeuw Janine. (2025). Development of a training programme to improve health literacy and respectful compassionate care competencies among undergraduate student nurses: a quantitative study. BMC Medical Education 25, 348. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06894-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest questionnaire survey of student nurses (n = 151) across three Tanzanian universities, analysed with paired t-tests. Health literacy skills rose "from 5.02 while after the training this was 5.79", significant at < 0.001; knowledge rose from 4.86 to 5.47 and attitudes from 3.29 to 3.53, both also significant.

> "For the health literacy skills, the mean before training was 5.02 while after the training this was 5.79 and this change was statistically significant (< 0.001)."
