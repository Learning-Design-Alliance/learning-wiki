Evidence¶

Wen Wu 2019¶

Wen Wu, Li Chen, Qingchang Yang, You Li. (2019). Inferring Students' Personality from Their Communication Behavior in Web-based Learning Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-00173-9

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Prediction experiment with 164 students, 90/10 train-test split with 10-fold cross-validation, comparing LASSO against a mean-value baseline using two-tailed paired t-tests. Statistics copied from the Prediction Results section.

"the prediction error of LASSO is significantly lower than that of baseline regarding MAE and RMSE (average MAE: 0.664 vs. 0.852, t=-8.8, p< 0.01; average RMSE: 0.842 vs. 0.900, t=-6.3, p< 0.01)."

Wen Wu 2019 (2)¶

Wen Wu, Li Chen, Qingchang Yang, You Li. (2019). Inferring Students' Personality from Their Communication Behavior in Web-based Learning Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-00173-9

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Spearman's rank correlation between predicted values and ground truth for the same LASSO evaluation; the article notes the closer to 1 the better. No standardized effect size printed.

"the average value of five personality traits generated by LASSO is 0.594 while the value of baseline is -0.198."
