## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Scenario had a statistically significant main effect on error rates (F3, 35 = 5.27, p = 0.004); the VT scenario had the highest error rate followed by the tricyclic scenario. [→ Hall Charlotte 2020](#hall-charlotte-2020)
