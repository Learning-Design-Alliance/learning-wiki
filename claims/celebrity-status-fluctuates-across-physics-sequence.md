### Meagan Sundstrom and Logan Kageorge 2024

Meagan Sundstrom and Logan Kageorge. (2024). Investigating peer recognition across an introductory physics sequence: Do first impressions last?. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010133)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Social network analysis of recognition surveys in a two-semester algebra-based introductory physics sequence at a mostly-women institution, with the same instructor and nearly identical enrollment. The scatterplot of fall versus spring indegree shows a positive trend with two exceptions: "Sam receives eight nominations in the fall" and only two in spring, while Bianca rose from five to ten.

> "Sam receives eight nominations in the fall (when she is a celebrity) and only two nominations in the spring (when she is not a celebrity) and Bianca receives five nominations in the fall (when she is not a celebrity) and 10 nominations in the spring (when she is a celebrity; Fig. 2)."
