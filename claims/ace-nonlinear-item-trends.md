## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Ten items showed unusual patterns, with novice groups outperforming intermediate, or intermediate outperforming advanced, contrary to the overall linear trend. [→ Ilic Dragan 2014](#ilic-dragan-2014)

## Evidence

### Ilic Dragan 2014

Ilic Dragan, Nordin Rusli Bin, Glasziou Paul, Tilson Julie K, Villanueva Elmer. (2014). Development and validation of the ACE tool: assessing medical trainees' competency in evidence based medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-114

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item-level analysis of the cross-sectional cohort showed the overall linear trend did not hold within 10 of 15 items. The authors attribute this to curriculum 'spot-lighting' of different EBM aspects at different training years. No effect size is printed.

> "A total of 10 items returned unusual presentations, with both the EBM-novice group demonstrating a larger effect than the EBM-intermediate group, and similarly the EBM-intermediate group demonstrating a larger effect than the EBM-advanced group."
