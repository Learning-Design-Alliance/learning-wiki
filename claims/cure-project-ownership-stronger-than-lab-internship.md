### Auchincloss LC 2014

Auchincloss LC, Laursen SL, Branchaw JL, Eagan K, Graham M, Hanauer DI, Lawrie G, McLinn CM, Pelaez N, Rowland S, Towns M, Trautmann NM, Varma-Nelson P, Weston TJ, Dolan EL. (2014). `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meeting report summarizes a linguistic-analysis study by Hanauer and colleagues (2012) comparing project ownership across a traditional laboratory course, a fieldwork CURE, and a research internship; the review reports that CURE students

> "students in the CURE reported a stronger sense of ownership of their research projects compared with students who participated in traditional lab courses and research internships"

### Auchincloss LC 2014 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The same linguistic-analysis study, as reported in this meeting report: CURE students with stronger ownership also reported higher persistence in science or medicine; the report calls the inferred relationship provisional.

> "these students also reported higher levels of persistence in science or medicine (Hanauer et al., 2012)"
