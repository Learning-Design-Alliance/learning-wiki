## Evidence

### Mohamad Nawal 2025

Mohamad Nawal, Rousseau Kim-Louise, Dowlut Fatimah, Gering Milton, Thomas Kevin G. F. (2025). Symptoms of ADHD and Other Common Mental Disorders Influence Academic Success in South African Undergraduates. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547241310659

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary correlational analysis (Table 4 and the Statistical Preparation section) of the Time 1 sample showed positive associations between inattention and both depression and anxiety symptoms; the authors therefore used ASRS total rather than subscale scores in the comorbidity models. No correlation coefficient was printed in the quoted sentence.

> "However, ASRS Inattention subscale scores were significantly positively associated with BDI-II and BAI total scores and hence the coefficient estimates of the models would be at risk of instability due to multicollinearity amongst predictor variables."
