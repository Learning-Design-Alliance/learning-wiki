## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Heusler Andreas 2019

Heusler Andreas, Molitor Dominik, Spann Martin. (2019). Knowledge Stock Exchanges increase student success in Massive Open Online Courses. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0223064

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Discrete-time survival analysis (logit) on 103,781 person-period observations clustered in 58,729 students; negative coefficients indicate risk-reducing effects, with IsStockTrader (KSX) at -1.539*** and IsDiscussionInitiator (KSX) at -1.325***.

> "With respect to the KSX platform, students that actively engaged in the trading of virtual knowledge stocks strongly increase the course completion likelihood, followed by students who initiated discussions on the KSX forum."
