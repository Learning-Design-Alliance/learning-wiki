## Evidence

### Liu Zihan 2026

Liu Zihan, Tang Wenguang, Tang Liping, Ma Yankun. (2026). The relationship between perceived teacher support and learning engagement of Chinese adolescents: a moderated chain mediation model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03990-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderated mediation analysis (PROCESS Model 84) controlling cadre status, gender, and grade: the support × future orientation interaction on mastery approach goal was statistically significant (β = 0.06, p < 0.01); simple slopes were β = 0.15 at low and β = 0.26 at high future orientation.

> "the interaction effect between perceived teacher support and future orientation on mastery approach goal was statistically significant (β = 0.06, p < 0.01), indicating that future orientation perspective may moderate the first stage of the mediation model involving mastery approach goal."

### Liu Zihan 2026 (2)

Liu Zihan, Tang Wenguang, Tang Liping, Ma Yankun. (2026). The relationship between perceived teacher support and learning engagement of Chinese adolescents: a moderated chain mediation model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03990-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderated mediation analysis (PROCESS Model 84) on the same sample: the interaction on performance approach goal was statistically significant (β = 0.064, p < 0.01); at low future orientation the support path was not significant (β = -0.001, p > 0.05), at high it was (β = 0.127, p < 0.05).

> "the interaction effect between perceived teacher support and future orientation on performance approach goal was statistically significant (β = 0.064, p < 0.01), indicating that future orientation moderated the first stage of the mediation model involving performance approach goal."

### Liu Zihan 2026 (3)

Liu Zihan, Tang Wenguang, Tang Liping, Ma Yankun. (2026). The relationship between perceived teacher support and learning engagement of Chinese adolescents: a moderated chain mediation model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03990-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderated mediation analysis (PROCESS Model 84) controlling cadre status, gender, and grade: the support × future orientation interaction on mastery avoidance goal did not statistically significant (β = -0.001, p > 0.05).

> "the interaction effect between perceived teacher support and future orientation on mastery avoidance goal did not statistically significant (β = -0.001, p > 0.05), indicating that future orientation did not moderated the first pathway of the mediation model involving mastery avoidance goal."
