q3 · i? · causal · r2

Headline finding of the school-level RCT, based on results for the 1,258 Year 5 pupils after two years, presented as progress scores from KS1 to KS2. The report states "2 additional months' progress in reading and maths compared to children in the other schools".

"Children in the CU schools made 2 additional months' progress in reading and maths compared to children in the other schools. The finding for maths has moderate security, and the finding for reading has low to moderate security."
