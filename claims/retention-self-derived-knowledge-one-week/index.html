Evidence¶

Bauer 2020¶

Bauer, P. J., Esposito, A. G., & Daly, J. J. (2020). Self-derivation through memory integration: A model for accumulation of semantic knowledge. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.101271

q3 · i2 · causal · r1

One-week retention test in the classroom experiment with 96 second-graders. Open-ended recall of self-derived integration facts rose from a mean of 1.53 at Session 1 to 1.95 at Session 2; "a statistically significant increase in performance from Session 1 by a mean of .42" (t(95) = 4.03, p < .001, d = .40). Combined recall/recognition score was 3.45 of 4.

"This represents a statistically significant increase in performance from Session 1 by a mean of .42, 95% CI[.21 to .62], t(95) = 4.03, p < .001, d = .40."
