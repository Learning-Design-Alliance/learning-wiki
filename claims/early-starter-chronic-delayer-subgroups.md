# Early starters (<5 min delay) show greater outcome growth while chronic delayers (>13 min) show negative trends relative to regular starters

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jordan Gutterman 2026

Jordan Gutterman, Ashish Gurung, Lee Branstetter, Kenneth Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Cross-Subject Predictive Validity for Learning Outcomes of Delayed Start Behavior. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25308

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regressions with sub-group dummies (Table 3) on the 711-student sample: early starters (19.7% of sample) β=.11 (p=.07) math, .15 (p=.02) ELA; chronic delayers (19.4%) β=-.13 (p=.05) math, -.11 (p=.11) ELA.

> "Early starters outperformed the baseline group of regular starters by .11 SD in math and .15 SD in ELA; the chronic delayers underperformed regular starters by .13 SD in math and .11 SD in ELA."
