## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Acaray Ali 2026

Acaray Ali. (2026). How digital stress affects academic procrastination and subjective well-being: mediating effect of ego depletion. BMC Psychology, 14, 449. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04164-7

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of survey data from 768 undergraduates in Rize, Türkiye. All five digital stress subdimensions showed small positive Pearson correlations with procrastination; e.g. availability stress "r = 0.18, p < 0.01" and online vigilance "r = 0.21, p < 0.01".

> "All digital stress dimensions were positively related to academic procrastination (availability stress (r = 0.18, p < 0.01); approval anxiety (r = 0.13, p < 0.01), FOMO (r = 0.14, p < 0.01); connection overload (r = 0.17, p < 0.01); online vigilance (r = 0.21, p < 0.01))."
