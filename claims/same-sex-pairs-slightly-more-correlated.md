Regression on the national-test correlations with an indicator for same-sex pairs, controlling for relative category and weighting by precision. The article reports "b = 0.009, P < 0.001"; the male-versus-female comparison was not significant (P = 0.45).

> "a regression analysis, detailed in Materials and Methods, showed that correlations for same-sex pairs are systematically higher than those for opposite sex pairs, albeit slightly (b = 0.009, P < 0.001)."
