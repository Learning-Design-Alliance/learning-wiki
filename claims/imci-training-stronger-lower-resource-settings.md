## Evidence

### Nguyen Duyen Thi Kim 2013

Nguyen Duyen Thi Kim, Leung Karen K., McIntyre Lynn, Ghali William A., Sauve Reg. (2013). Does Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI) Training Improve the Skills of Health Workers? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0066030

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Stratified meta-analysis by country Human Development Index found stronger training benefits in lower-resource settings. The review reports "stronger, significant associations seen in lower resource settings for nutrition counseling (RR = 5.11, 95% CI: 2.95–8.87)" with HDI accounting for all heterogeneity in vaccinations.

> "Similar patterns were observed in the analyses by HDI, with stronger, significant associations seen in lower resource settings for nutrition counseling (RR = 5.11, 95% CI: 2.95–8.87) and a trend towards significance for vaccinations (RR = 1.27, 95% CI: 0.98–1.63)."
