### Brechter Anna Maria 2026

Brechter Anna Maria, Schwab Roxana, Dold Christoph, Skala Christine, Schröder Maria, Schiestl Lina, Gillen Katharina, Brenner Walburgis, Hasenburg Annette, Schmidt Mona Wanda. (2026). Developing a Realistic and Cost-Effective Training Model (MaiSurge) for Laparoscopic Hysterectomies to Train and Assess Surgical Skill: Prospective Nonrandomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/66369

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subjective evaluation on a 5-point Likert scale comparing the PVA model, a silicone version, and a commercial model; experts rated MaiSurge consistency at a median of 3.5 (IQR 3.0-4.0) versus 4.0 for the commercial model, and rated its tear resistance best.

> "Overall, the newly developed model was rated better or equally well compared to the silicone model and the commercial uterus model with regard to realism, color, consistency, size, thickness, tensile strength, proportions, and suitability for training"
