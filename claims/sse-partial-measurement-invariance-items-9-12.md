## Evidence

### Polónyiová K. 2025

Polónyiová K., Teličák P., Vitovič P., Hnilicová S. (2025). Psychometric analysis of the Slovak version of the satisfaction with simulation experience scale (measurement invariance). Medical Education Online, 30(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2534055

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group measurement invariance testing across the two language groups (Table 5). The article reports a "significant chi-square difference, p < 0.001" at the scalar level; a partial scalar model releasing Items 9 and 12 improved fit (CFI = 0.949 vs 0.947).

> "Chi-square tests revealed that the fit remained nearly the same from Configural to Metric invariance (non-significant chi-square difference) but significantly worsened from Metric to Scalar invariance (significant chi-square difference, p < 0.001), indicating a decline in model fit when item intercepts were included."
