## Evidence

### Galvin Sheila 2026

Galvin Sheila, Lucitt Margaret. (2026). Student Learning Performance and Satisfaction With a Flipped Classroom in Undergraduate Dental Pharmacology Education. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70062

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous end-of-year Qualtrics survey with a trichotomous scale (good, satisfactory, non-satisfactory); response rates 78.34% (76/97) FC versus 62.79% (81/129) TC. Descriptively, "9% higher score rates in the FC (31.5%)" for the good rating, but the Chi-square test was not significant.

> "Despite this, a Chi‐square test of raw count responses did not show a significant difference (p = 0.3365) in the response satisfaction ratings when comparing the 2 teaching approaches."
