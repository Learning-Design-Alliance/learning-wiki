Evidence¶

Han Xue 2026¶

Han Xue, Cao Jing, Wang Zi, Luo Heng. (2026). The Effects of Agent Type and Feedback Style on Self-Directed Learning: A Mixed-Methods Study. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071069

q3 · i3 · causal · r?

Mixed-design ANOVA of coded human–AI interaction logs from 51 postgraduate students completing instructional design tasks, comparing customized vs. general-purpose agents. The article reports a significant main effect of agent type on feedback accuracy (η2p = 0.368) and specificity (η2p = 0.176). No interaction effects on feedback quality dimensions were found.

"Mixed-design ANOVA results showed a significant main effect of agent type on feedback accuracy (η2p = 0.368) and specificity (η2p = 0.176), indicating large and moderate effects."
