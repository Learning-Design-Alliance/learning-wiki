## Evidence

### Chesimet M. C 2016

Chesimet M. C, Githua B.N, Ng’eno, J. K. (2016). Effects of Experiential Learning Approach on Students’ Mathematical Creativity among Secondary School Students of Kericho East Sub-County, Kenya. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(23). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1112801

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental Solomon Four Non-Equivalent Control Group study of 168 Form Two students in four co-educational district secondary schools in Kericho East Sub-County; Tukeys post-hoc tests showed "students who were taught using ELA achieved significantly higher scores in MCT than those who were taught through the conventional methods".

> "The results of the post hoc comparisons confirmed that ELA had a positive effect on students’ mathematical creativity. The study found that students who were taught using ELA achieved significantly higher scores in MCT than those who were taught through the conventional methods"
