Sun 2025¶

Sun, Y., Xin, H., Li, S., Niu, Q., Chai, C. S., Huang, L., & Chen, G. (2025). Modeling AI-TPACK in Practice: Insights from Teachers' Multi-Agent Workflow Design. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13906

q2 · i? · associational · r2

K-means cluster analysis (k=3, average silhouette score 0.389) of clickstream logs from 61 teachers (8,718 actions) in a two-day AI professional development workshop. "Cluster 1 (n=18) was configuration-intensive" with Content Configuration at 32.50% of activity.

"Cluster 1 (n=18) was configuration-intensive: Content Configuration dominated their activity (32.50%), while Content Editing and Testing/Debugging rates were 3-4 times higher than other clusters."
