### Borman 2013

Borman, G. D., & Grigg, J. (2013). An Effort to Close Achievement Gaps at Scale through Self-Affirmation. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563077

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat analysis of a randomized trial in which roughly half of consented seventh graders in eleven Madison middle schools were assigned to self-affirmation writing; the article reports "we did not find an overall average effect of assignment to the self-affirmation condition."
