### Griffin Pitts 2026

Griffin Pitts, Neha Rani, Weedguet Mildort, and Eva-Marie Cook. (2026). Students' Reliance on AI in Higher Education: Identifying Contributing Factors. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-12773-0_9](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-12773-0_9)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of the same fifty-student controlled experiment. Post-task trust and satisfaction scales were each negatively correlated with the percentage of correctly followed helpful and rejected misleading recommendations, per the article's printed values "r = −0.303, p = .032" and "r = −0.292, p = .040".

> "Final trust in the chatbot was negatively correlated with appropriate reliance (r = −0.303, p = .032), as was satisfaction (r = −0.292, p = .040)."
