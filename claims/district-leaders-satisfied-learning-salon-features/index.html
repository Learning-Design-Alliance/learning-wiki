Descriptive post-engagement survey (15 Likert and open-ended items via Qualtrics) administered after the first three Learning Salons; 14 district leaders responded to satisfaction items measured from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 4 (very satisfied). The report states "100% of the district leaders that responded to our survey indicated that they were satisfied or very satisfied". No effect size is printed.

"Across all three Learning Salons, 100% of the district leaders that responded to our survey indicated that they were satisfied or very satisfied with the key features of the Learning Salon model."
