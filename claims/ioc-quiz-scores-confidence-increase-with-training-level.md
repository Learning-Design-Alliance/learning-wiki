### Hannah Emily M. 2025

Hannah Emily M., Bloom Matthew B., Schwaitzberg Steven D. (2025). Project interpret cholangiogram at the SAGES 2024 Education & Innovation Center: skill deficiency in bile duct injury recognition among surgical residents. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12145-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional quiz study of 88 participants (53 residents, 9 fellows, 26 attendings) stratified into four training-level groups; ANOVA and Fisher's exact tests showed the training-level differences, reported as "Overall quiz scores and confidence in interpreting IOC increased with level of training (p < 0.001)."
