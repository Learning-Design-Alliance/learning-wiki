## Evidence

### Wu Zeyu 2024

Wu Zeyu, Huang Yiling, Lyu Ling, Huang Yu, Ping Fan. (2024). The efficacy of simulation-based learning versus non-simulation-based learning in endocrinology education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06010-z

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 17 randomized controlled trials comparing SBL with NSBL in endocrinology education, pooled in Review Manager 5.4. The pooled estimate showed "a significant advantage of SBL over NSBL" with SMD = 1.00 (95% CI 0.68–1.32) and high heterogeneity (I2 = 89%).

> "Seventeen studies [14, 16–19, 21–31, 33] reported test scores of theoretical knowledge (811 subjects in the SBL cohort and 830 subjects in the NSBL cohort), demonstrating a significant advantage of SBL over NSBL (SMD = 1.00; 95% CI: 0.68–1.32, P < 0.00001, I2 = 89%)"
