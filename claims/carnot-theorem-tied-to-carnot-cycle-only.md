## Evidence

### Lilin Zhu and Gang Xiang 2021

Lilin Zhu and Gang Xiang. (2021). Investigating student understanding of heat engine: a case study of Stirling engine. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac342b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac342b)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of written answers and post-test interviews (each averaging 10 minutes) found very few students—two in Xiang class and one in Wu class—used Carnot's theorem to obtain the right efficiency answer, and only after getting stuck. No effect size is printed.

> "most of thestudentsonlycorrelateCarnot’stheoremwithCarnot cycle and not with other reversible cycles such as Stirling cycle. The very poor performance on Carnot’s theorem was somewhat unexpected, as explicit instruction on it was included in all classes."
