## Evidence

### Reviriego E 2014

Reviriego E, Cidoncha MA, Asua J, Gagnon MP, Mateos M, Gárate L, de Lorenzo E, González RM. (2014). Online training course on critical appraisal for nurses: adaptation and assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-136

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The pre-post study administered the 40-item Spanish SDLRSNE, rated on 5-point Likert scales, before and after the course. The post-course mean was 163.06 (SD 13.71), "more than 5 points higher" than pre-course, a statistically significant paired difference (p < 0.001); no effect size was printed.

> "The mean post-course score was 163.06 (SD 13.71), more than 5 points higher than the pre-course score, and this difference was statistically significant (Student's t-test for paired samples, p < 0.001)."

### Reviriego E 2014 (2)

Reviriego E, Cidoncha MA, Asua J, Gagnon MP, Mateos M, Gárate L, de Lorenzo E, González RM. (2014). Online training course on critical appraisal for nurses: adaptation and assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-136

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Reliability analysis of the SDLRSNE in this sample reported Cronbach's alpha of "0.884" for the complete questionnaire, with subscale coefficients of 0.821 (Self-management), 0.812 (Desire for learning) and 0.742 (Self-control).

> "The Cronbach's alpha for the complete questionnaire was 0.884, confirming the internal consistency of the scale."
