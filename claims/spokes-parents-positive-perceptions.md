Tracey, L., Chambers, B., Bywater, T., & Elliott, L. (2016). SPOKES Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581435

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Light-touch process evaluation using telephone interviews with a random sample of one third of intervention parents, plus weekly and end-of-programme evaluation forms analysed thematically; findings are parental perceptions, not attainment measures.

> "most of the intervention parents who were interviewed reported liking and benefitting from their participation in the SPOKES programme. At both immediate post-test and subsequent follow-ups they believed that it improved their children’s reading."
