## Evidence

### Maizel Julien 2014

Maizel Julien, Guyomarc'h Laurianne, Henon Pierre, Modeliar Santhi Samy, de Cagny Bertrand, Choukroun Gabriel, Slama Michel. (2014). Residents learning ultrasound-guided catheterization are not sufficiently skilled to use landmarks. Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/cc13741

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moving-window comparison of the last five UG procedures against the first five LM procedures per resident (Table 3). The article reports "the success rate was significantly lower (65% versus 93%; p = 0.01)" at the transition; no effect size was printed.

> "The success rate was significantly lower (65% versus 93%; p = 0.01) and the complication rate was significantly higher (33% versus 8%; p = 0.01) for the first 5 LM procedures."
