## Evidence

### QiRui Ju 2023

QiRui Ju. (2023). Experimental Evidence on Negative Impact of Generative AI on Scientific Learning Outcomes (A Pilot Research). The Samuel Dubois Cook Center on Social Equity, Duke University. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.4567696](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4567696)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression analysis of the pilot experiment (N = 32 college-educated Prolific participants, three reading-writing cycles) controlling background knowledge, reading difficulty, and demographics found "the accuracy of learning outcomes dropped by 25.1%" under full AI reliance, statistically significant.

> "when entirely relying on AI , the accuracy of learning outcomes dropped by 25.1% and when partly relying on AI, the accuracy dropped by 12%. Both drops are statistically significant."
