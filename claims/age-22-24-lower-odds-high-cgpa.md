Multivariate logistic regression in the Results section of the 1,209-student survey. The adjusted odds ratio of 0.6 (95% CI 0.4–0.9, p = 0.021) compares the 22–24 age group (67.7% of the sample) against the 18–21 reference group for the outcome cGPA > 3.5; "the lower the students' age, the higher their cGPA" in cited prior work.
