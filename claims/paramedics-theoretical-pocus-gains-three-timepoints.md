## Evidence

### Jonck Christopher 2024

Jonck Christopher, Weimer Andreas Michael, Fundel Beatrice, Heinz Wolfgang, Merkel Daniel, Fiedel Hendrik, Ille Carlotta, Kloeckner Roman, Buggenhagen Holger, Piepho Tim, Weimer Johannes. (2024). Development and evaluation of a point-of-care ultrasound curriculum for paramedics in Germany – a prospective observational study and comparison. BMC Medical Education 24:811. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05816-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective observational study of n = 72 paramedics tested at three time points (T1 before preparation, T2 after digital preparation, T3 after the on-site phase). The article reports a significant improvement, quoting "The results improved significantly (p < 0.001)" with means rising from 41.3 to 82.7; no effect size is printed.

> "The results improved significantly (p < 0.001) over the observation period from T1 (41.3 ± 16.0) over T2 (76.5 ± 14.5) to T3 (T3: 82.7 ± 9.7)."
