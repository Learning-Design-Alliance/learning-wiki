## Evidence

### C. Netzer and A. Mittelstädt 2021

C. Netzer and A. Mittelstädt. (2021). Incorporating microlearning videos, online exercises and assessments into introductory physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.01385](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.01385)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Grading analysis comparing the share of students versus relative points obtained in the final online exam (winter term 2020/21, Fig. 3) with the three preceding winter terms. The authors state the grades "did not deviate signiﬁcantly from preceding semesters".

> "The exam was neither showing a massive amount of best grades, which would be a possible indicator that many students cheated, nor a lot of failed exams, indicating that the online teaching itself failed."
