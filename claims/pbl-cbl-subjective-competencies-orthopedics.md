## Evidence

### Xiong Xianghua 2025

Xiong Xianghua, Xu Jiongnan, Luo Mingyan, Niu Dongsheng, Bi Qing, Wang Zhaofu, Zhang Jun. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning combined with case-based learning versus lecture-based learning in orthopedic education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07741-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 6 studies (296 students) on self-learning ability, a subjective outcome. Pooled risk ratio favored PBL-CBL: "RR = 1.55, 95% CI: 1.34–1.80, P < 0.0001" with no heterogeneity (I2 = 0%); no standardized effect size was printed.

> "The PBL-CBL group outperformed the LBL group in self-learning ability (RR = 1.55, 95% CI: 1.34–1.80, P < 0.0001, I2 = 0%)"

### Xiong Xianghua 2025 (2)

Xiong Xianghua, Xu Jiongnan, Luo Mingyan, Niu Dongsheng, Bi Qing, Wang Zhaofu, Zhang Jun. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning combined with case-based learning versus lecture-based learning in orthopedic education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07741-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 5 studies (308 students) on team collaboration ability. Pooled risk ratio favored PBL-CBL: "RR = 1.86, 95% CI: 1.54–2.25, P < 0.0001" with low heterogeneity (I2 = 16%); no standardized effect size was printed. The article also reports communication abilities RR = 1.41 favoring PBL-CBL.

> "The PBL-CBL group outperformed the LBL group in team collaboration ability (RR = 1.86, 95% CI: 1.54–2.25, P < 0.0001, I2 = 16%)"

### Xiong Xianghua 2025 (3)

Xiong Xianghua, Xu Jiongnan, Luo Mingyan, Niu Dongsheng, Bi Qing, Wang Zhaofu, Zhang Jun. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning combined with case-based learning versus lecture-based learning in orthopedic education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07741-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of 2 studies (162 students) on the critical thinking disposition inventory (CTDI-CV) total score. Pooled mean difference favored PBL-CBL: "MD = 31.25, 95% CI: 28.25–34.25, P < 0.0001"; no standardized effect size printed.

> "The PBL-CBL group had a higher CTDI-CV total score than the LBL group (MD = 31.25, 95% CI: 28.25–34.25, P < 0.0001, I2 = 39%)"
