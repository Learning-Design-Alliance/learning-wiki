## Evidence

### Miyagami Taiju 2025

Miyagami Taiju, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Yamamoto Yu, Shikino Kiyoshi, Kataoka Koshi, Kobayashi Hiroyuki, Naito Toshio, Tokuda Yasuharu (2025). A cross sectional study of the relationship between clinical scores and timing of guidance for resident physicians in outpatient settings. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-30748-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire of 4,662 Japanese residents taking the 2022 GM-ITE asked whether they received teaching across five outpatient stages. The article reports "the lowest frequency of teaching was observed during the medical interview stage (59.4%; Fig. 2)" and that 187 (4.0%) received no teaching.

> "Teaching was most frequently provided during the decision-making stage (90.4%), followed by the patient assessment stage (89.7%). Conversely, the lowest frequency of teaching was observed during the medical interview stage (59.4%; Fig. 2)."
