## Evidence

### Colin S. Wallace 2021

Colin S. Wallace, Edward E. Prather, John A. Milsom, Ken Johns, Srin Manne (2021). Students taught by a first-time instructor using active learning teaching strategies outperform students taught by a highly-regarded traditional instructor. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1080/0047231x.2021.12290517](https://doi.org/10.1080/0047231x.2021.12290517)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exam-level averages for both sections, calculated after removing items flagged as biased toward the reformed section (Figure 1). The article states "the difference between the sections was 13.2% in favor of the reformed section" on the cumulative final exam.

> "Notice that the average scores on exams 2 and 3 for the reformed section were higher than those of the traditional section (by 8.5% and 7.5%, respectively). On the final exam, the difference between the sections was 13.2% in favor of the reformed section."
