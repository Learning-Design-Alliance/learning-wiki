Jenny Nagaoka, Jonah Deutsch, Andy Brake, Melissa Roderick. (2007). Individual School Report: Responses from the 2007 AVID SURVEY. Consortium on Chicago School Research. http://ccsr.uchicago.edu/AVID

Summary of findings for students attending schools in the lowest overall category in the 2007 CPS AVID survey. The report states "70% agreed or strongly agreed that AVID gives them the support they need to be successful in high school", 79% agreed their teacher had earned their respect, and however, only 50% agreed they would recommend AVID to their friends.
