Preceptor questionnaire analysis: of 12 residents, eight questionnaires were considered, three excluded because one preceptor was absent or completion was poor. Table 4 reports preceptor-rated skill rising from 2.1 to 4.8, p=0.000, by the same two preceptors on first and last day.

> "In the analysis, there was a significant improvement in the laparoscopic skill of the residents when evaluated by the preceptors (preceptor 1 and preceptor 2) (Table 4)."
