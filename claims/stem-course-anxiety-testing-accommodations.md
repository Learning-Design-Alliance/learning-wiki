## Evidence

### Westley James 2019

Westley James, Kamryn Lamons, Roberto Spilka, Caroline Bustamante, Erin Scanlon and Jacquelyn J. Chini. (2019). Hidden walls: STEM course barriers identified by students with disabilities. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1909.02905](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1909.02905)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

IPA interview analysis: participants described debilitating anxiety episodes before chemistry exams, with one participant identifying feeling unprepared due to course barriers such as unexplained practice-problem answers as the cause.

> "Four participants identified frequent episodes of anxiety as a diagnosis characteristic and reported that STEM courses increased the frequency and intensity of these episodes ."

### Westley James 2019 (2)

Westley James, Kamryn Lamons, Roberto Spilka, Caroline Bustamante, Erin Scanlon and Jacquelyn J. Chini. (2019). Hidden walls: STEM course barriers identified by students with disabilities. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1909.02905](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1909.02905)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

IPA interview analysis of accommodation use: accommodations included extra test time, reduced-distraction environments, and make-up tests. One participant said "knowing I have that extra time makes me, makes my anxiety go down."

> "Eight out of nine participants made use of test accommodations, and seven out of eight of these individuals reported that test  accommodations played a significant role in reducing test anxiety."
