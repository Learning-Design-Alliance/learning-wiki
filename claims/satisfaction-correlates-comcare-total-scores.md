## Evidence

### Gärtner Julia 2021

Gärtner Julia, Prediger Sarah, Harendza Sigrid. (2021). Development and pilot test of ComCare – a questionnaire for quick assessment of communicative and social competences in medical students after interviews with simulated patients. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001464](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001464)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Pearson correlations of the general "satisfaction with the consultation" item with total scores showed r=.765 for ComCareP and r=.486 for ComCareD, both p<.001. The strongest ComCareP item correlates were "creating a comfortable atmosphere" (r=.650) and "showing sincere interest" (r=.633).

> "The general satisfaction with the consultation showed a significantly strong positive correlation with ComCareP’s total score mean (r=.765, p<.001) and a significantly medium positive correlation (r=.486, p<.001) with ComCareD’s total score."
