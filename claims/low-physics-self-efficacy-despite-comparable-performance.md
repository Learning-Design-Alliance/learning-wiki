## Evidence

### Danny Doucette and Chandralekha Singh 2020

Danny Doucette and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Why are there so few women in physics? Reflections on the experiences of two women. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/1.5145518](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5145518)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-study interview evidence: one student's self-report in a one-hour semi-structured interview. She said she "was very comparable to them" on tests yet felt not good at physics, contrasting with her confidence after a strong P-chem midterm.

> "There would be times when I would feel like I am not good at physics, I am not good at it. But we would get tests back… I was very comparable to them but I still felt like, oh, it is not my thing"
