## Evidence

### J. Fitzgerald et al 2014

J. Fitzgerald et al. (2014). Important Text Characteristics for Early-Grades Text Complexity. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037289

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

First substudy: 1,258 first and second graders from 10 U.S. states read a stratified random sample of 90 texts (one rejected) and completed a maze task; Rasch modeling assigned logits to the 89 texts with "reliability for the forms ranged from .85 to .96."

> "From the student responses, a logit scale was created, and the 89 texts were assigned logits for text-complexity level. Cronbach’s alpha estimates of reliability for the forms ranged from .85 to .96."
