Jina Kang, Chungsoo Na, Morgan Diederich, Hillary Swanson, Lili Yan. (2026). Cultivating science data literacy in K-16 science education through data practices: A systematic review. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09803-5

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Association analysis of data practice facets by grade level (Fig. 6), aggregating studies into K–12 (n = 19) and higher education (n = 21) with two mixed-level studies excluded. The review reports no overlapping association rules between the two categories and characterizes K–12 as emphasizing contextual understanding and hands-on approaches.

> "Notably, the results revealed distinct sets of co-occurring data practices in K–12 and higher education, with no overlapping association rules (i.e., no common combinations of practices) observed across the two categories. Higher education predominantly emphasized second-hand data analysis (e.g., using given data, applying statistics or computational methods, and selecting methods), while K–12 focused on first-hand data (i.e., producing their own data ) along with other data practices such as developing hypotheses, identifying variables , gathering information , making data-driven arguments , synthesizing results, and sharing results."
