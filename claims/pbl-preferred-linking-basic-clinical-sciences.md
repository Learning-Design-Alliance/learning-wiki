### Ibrahim Nahla Khamis 2014

Ibrahim Nahla Khamis, Banjar Shorooq, Al-Ghamdi Amal, Al-Darmasi Moroj, Khoja Abeer, Turkistani Jamela, Arif Rwan, Al-Sebyani Awatif, Musawa Al-anoud, Basfar Wijdan. (2014). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same 460-student cross-sectional survey, the knowledge-linkage item showed PBL mean 4.04 [1.13] versus lectures 3.29 [1.11], described as "a highly statistical significant difference (t test =10.15, P<.001)". Perceptions, not objective linkage measures.

> "However, students preferred PBL (mean score = 4.04 [1.13]) to traditional lectures (score=3.29 [1.11]) for a better linkage between knowledge of basic and clinical sciences. A highly statistical significant difference (t test =10.15, P<.001) was observed."
