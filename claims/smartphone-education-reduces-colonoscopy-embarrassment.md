## Evidence

### Wen MC 2020

Wen MC, Kau K, Huang SS, Huang WH, Tsai LY, Tsai TY, Tsay SL. (2020). Smartphone education improves embarrassment, bowel preparation, and satisfaction with care in patients receiving colonoscopy: a randomized controlled trail. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000023102

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GEE analysis of two-time-point embarrassment scores (colonoscopy embarrassment scale, Cronbach α = 0.95) in a double-blinded RCT with 70 patients per group; the interaction term "Time 2 × Intervention" reached significance. The B coefficient is unstandardized, so no effect size is printed.

> "the results of the interaction analysis indicated that subjects who received the smartphone education program (B = –2.78, P = .02) reported fewer embarrassment scores than those of the control group"
