Voight 2013 (2)¶

Voight, A. (2013). The Racial School-Climate Gap. A report from the Region IX Equity Assistance Center at WestEd. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED580366

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Two reports using Cal-SCHLS data from different years confirmed significant connectedness disparities with the ordering White > Asian > American Indian and Hispanic > African American. White students were most likely to report a caring adult relationship and high expectations; Hispanic students least likely.

"Two reports using data from different years confirmed that there were significant disparities in students’ sense of connectedness to school, with White students having the highest levels of connectedness, followed by Asian students, American Indian and Hispanic students, and, lastly, African American students."
