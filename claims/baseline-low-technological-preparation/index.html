Sidorov et al. 2018¶

Oleg V. Sidorov, Lyubov’ V. Kozub, Alexander V. Goferberg, Natalya V. Osintseva. (2018). Organization and Carrying out the Educational Experiment and Statistical Analysis of its Results in IHL. European Journal of Contemporary Education. https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2018.1.177

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Stating-stage questionnaire and testing of all 287 participants before the forming stage, establishing baseline technological preparation. The article also reports more than 92% (264 students; in Ishim 100%) unfamiliar with the equipment and 25% (71 students) at a low level on creative tasks. Descriptive percentages only.

"The analysis of the questionnaires showed that 85 % (243 students) had no sufficient knowledge for the realization of integration of the content of various disciplines. But 100 % (287 students) showed some interest in gaining the necessary knowledge."
