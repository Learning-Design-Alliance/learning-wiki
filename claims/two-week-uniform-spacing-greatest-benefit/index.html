John Duplice 2022¶

John Duplice. (2022). The GoldList Notebook Method: A Study on L2 Vocabulary Learning. IAFOR Journal of Education: Language Learning in Education, Volume 10 – Issue 1. https://iafor.org/journal/iafor-journal-of-education/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pairwise comparison of treatments using the lmer package with difflsmeans function, exclusive of the baseline comparison. The article reports "two weeks between review sessions throughout the study showed the greatest benefit" with nearly twice the baseline improvement.

"In a nutshell, two weeks between review sessions throughout the study showed the greatest benefit with nearly twice the improvement of not using the GoldList Notebook Method."
