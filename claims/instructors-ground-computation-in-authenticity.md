### W. Brian Lane and Cortney Headley 2022

W. Brian Lane and Cortney Headley. (2022). Student Representations of Computation in the Physics Community. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020110](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020110)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Findings from semistructured interviews with all three instructors of the computationally integrated courses, conducted to foreground the local community of practice. The instructors "identiﬁed professional authenticity and student career readiness as important reasons for integrating computation," and all three described using computation in their research and modeled course activities on those research practices.

> "These instructors identiﬁed professional authenticity and student career readiness as important reasons for integrating computation. They frequently referred to the demands for computational skills in graduate study and industry."
