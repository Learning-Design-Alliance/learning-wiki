## Evidence

### Tambunan 2019

Hardi Tambunan. (2019). The Effectiveness of the Problem Solving Strategy and the Scientific Approach to Students' Mathematical Capabilities in High Order Thinking Skills. International Electronic Journal of Mathematics Education, 14(2), 293-302. https://doi.org/10.29333/iejme/5715

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same quasi-experimental design (n = 138 experimental, n = 139 control). Creativity posttest mean was 75.6087 for the experimental group versus 70.4460 for the control group; the t test rejected the null hypothesis.

> "The results of the t test show that the value of t = 11.743, Sig. <0.025, the null hypothesis was rejected, and therefore, problem solving strategies are more effective than the scientific approach to students' abilities in mathematical creativity."
