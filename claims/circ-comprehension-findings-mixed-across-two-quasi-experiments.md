# Stevens and Slavin (1995) found statistically significant positive effects of CIRC on CAT Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary subtests, while Jewell (1994) found no statistically significant effects

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the Stevens and Slavin (1995) quasi-experiment, CIRC showed statistically significant positive effects on the Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary subtests of the California Achievement Test. [→ Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition® 2010](#cooperative-integrated-reading-and-composition-2010)
`q2 i?` In the Jewell (1994) quasi-experiment, effects on the Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary subtests of the Gates–MacGinitie Reading Test were not statistically significant, and the WWC-calculated average effect was below the 0.25 substantive-importance threshold. [→ Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition® 2010 (2)](#cooperative-integrated-reading-and-composition-2010-2)

## Evidence

### Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition® 2010

Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition®. (2010). WWC Intervention Report, Adolescent Literacy. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/111

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Findings section of the WWC review reporting the Stevens and Slavin (1995) matched quasi-experiment (411 treatment vs. 462 comparison students in Maryland); the WWC confirmed the reported significant positive effects on the named CAT subtests.

> "Stevens and Slavin (1995) reported, and the WWC confirmed, statistically significant positive effects of Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition ® on the Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary subtests of the California Achievement Test (CAT) for students in grades 2 through 6."

### Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition® 2010 (2)

Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition®. (2010). WWC Intervention Report, Adolescent Literacy. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/111

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

WWC review reporting the Jewell (1994) quasi-experiment (15 CIRC vs. 15 comparison classrooms) with non-significant effects; the printed average effect fell below the WWC's 0.25 substantive-importance threshold, coded i null as only the threshold is printed.

> "Jewell (1994) did not find statistically significant effects of Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition ® on the Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary subtests of the Gates–MacGinitie Reading Test for students in grades 2 through 6, and the WWC-calculated average effect across the two measures was not statistically significant or large enough to be considered substantively important"
