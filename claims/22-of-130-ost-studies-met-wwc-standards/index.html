The panel's systematic search of US OST research (1988-2008) screened studies against WWC standards. The guide reports that "22 met WWC standards or met the standards with reservations," constituting the strongest available evidence base for OST effectiveness.

"Of the 130 studies, 22 met WWC standards or met the standards with reservations. These 22 studies of 18 different OST programs represent the strongest evidence of the effectiveness of OST programs."
