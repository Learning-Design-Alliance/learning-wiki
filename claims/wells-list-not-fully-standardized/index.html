Evidence¶

Irving King and Hugo Gold 1911¶

Irving King and Hugo Gold. (1911). A Tentative Standardization of Certain "Opposites Tests". University of Iowa. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=A+Tentative+Standardization+of+Certain+Opposites+Tests

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Comparison of Wells' list against the authors' frequency table for 100 subjects: sick drew 12 healthy and 40 closed responses, open drew 57 shut, and glad drew 40 sad and 21 unhappy, while words like heavy, high, and wet drew near-unanimous correct opposites.

"From the above it appears that Wells is not wholly right in his expectation of uniform results from the lists which he proposes. If he should leave out the words sick, open, and glad he could reasonable expect fairly uniform results from the other ten words."
