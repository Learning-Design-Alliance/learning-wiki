Independent samples t-test comparing mean GEARS scores between XR-trained (n = 63) and no-training (n = 60) groups. The effect size was "strongly in favor of the VR trained group" at 0.964 with a 95% CI of [0.589, 1.336].

"The independent samples t test yielded an effect size strongly in favor of the VR trained group; 0.964 (95% CI 0.589 to 1.336). The threshold for a large effect size is 0.8"
