### Yarullina 2016

Yarullina, A. Sh., & Araslanova, A. T. (2016). Development of Subject Position as a Condition for Forming Communicative Competence of a Future Teacher. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(9), 2311-2322. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1114566

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Formative experiment with bachelor students of "Pedagogical education" of 2-4th courses, experimental (78 people) and test (72 people) groups identical in all tests. The article reports increases in high and normal subjective position levels in the experimental group; no effect size is printed.

> "The gained results enable us to make a conclusion, that in the process of experimental searching work in the experimental group the number of students with high (7.4%) showed the increase and normal (86.2%) level of subjective position development."
