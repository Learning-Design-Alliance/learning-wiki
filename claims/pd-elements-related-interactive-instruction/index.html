Evidence¶

Mark A. Smylie 2001¶

Mark A. Smylie, Elaine Allensworth, Rebecca C. Greenberg, Rodney Harris, Stuart Luppescu. (2001). Teacher Professional Development in Chicago: Supporting Effective Practice. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-professional-development-chicago-supporting-effective-practice

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Cross-sectional analysis of the 1997 and 1999 citywide teacher surveys (13,000 teachers in 1997; 9,900 in 1999) using three-level hierarchical linear models at the teacher level. The report states: "We found statistically significant, positive relation- ships between the frequency, content, and quality of professional development and teachers’ use of inter- active teaching methods." No effect sizes are printed.

"We found statistically significant, positive relation- ships between the frequency, content, and quality of professional development and teachers’ use of inter- active teaching methods (see Figure 2)."
