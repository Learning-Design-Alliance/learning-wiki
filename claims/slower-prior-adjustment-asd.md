## Evidence

### Sapey-Triomphe 2023

Sapey-Triomphe, L.-A., Sanchez, G., Hénaff, M.-A., Sonié, S., Schmitz, C., & Mattout, J. (2023). Disentangling sensory precision and prior expectation of change in autism during tactile discrimination. npj Science of Learning, 8:54. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00207-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Exp. III compared the accuracy-curve intercepts to the previous (30 Hz) versus current (26 Hz) context means. The intercept was "higher (i.e., closer to the mean stimulus frequency of the context-setting blocks)" in ASD in the first TOE block (d = 0.71), but not in the second, indicating both groups adjusted flexibly yet ASD more slowly.

> "This intercept was higher (i.e., closer to the mean stimulus frequency of the context-setting blocks) in the ASD group than in the NT group in the ﬁrst TOE block ( t(35) = 2.1, p = 0.045, d = 0.71, BF = 1.68)"
