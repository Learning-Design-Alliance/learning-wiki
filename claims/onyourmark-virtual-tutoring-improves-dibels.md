### OnYourMark 2023

OnYourMark. (2023). OnYourMark program description and evaluation summary. https://www.onyourmark.education

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Randomized study across 12 Texas schools in 2022-23 with 2,085 K-2 students assigned to 1:1 tutoring, 2:1 tutoring, or business-as-usual control. The article reports students assigned to tutoring "scored significantly higher than their peers in the control group on the DIBELS (effect size = +0.08)".

> "Overall, students who were assigned to receive OnYourMark tutoring scored significantly higher than their peers in the control group on the DIBELS (effect size = +0.08)"
