# Charter middle school students gain in math while multi-level charter students lag in both subjects relative to TPS peers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Charter middle school students show math growth equivalent to 53 additional days of learning versus TPS virtual twins, while multi-level charter students have 24 and 77 fewer days of learning in reading and math. [→ CREDO 2019](#credo-2019)

## Evidence

### CREDO 2019

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in South Carolina. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-south-carolina-2019-2/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Grade-configuration analysis comparing charter students in elementary, middle, high, and multi-level schools with their respective VCRs; elementary and high charter students showed similar growth to TPS in both subjects.

> "Inferior progress is found among charter students enrolled in multi-level schools. When compared to their TPS virtual twins multi-level charter students are found to have 24 and 77 fewer days of learning than TPS VCRs in reading and math, respectively."
