## Evidence

### Park Kye-Yeung 2019

Park Kye-Yeung, Park Hoon-Ki, Hwang Hwan-Sik. (2019). Group randomized trial of teaching tobacco-cessation counseling to senior medical students: a peer role-play module versus a standardized patient module. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1668-x

`q3 · i?`

Repeated-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni post-hoc comparisons across four evaluation periods over two to eight months. "students’ OSCE scores were highest before the KMLE-CSA in both groups"; the authors attribute this partly to self-directed learning before the licensing exam.

> "Among the four assessments—pre-intervention OSCE, post-intervention OSCE, post-clerkship OSCE, and OSCE before the KMLE-CSA—students’ OSCE scores were highest before the KMLE-CSA in both groups (p < 0.001 in both groups)."

### Park Kye-Yeung 2019 (2)

Park Kye-Yeung, Park Hoon-Ki, Hwang Hwan-Sik. (2019). Group randomized trial of teaching tobacco-cessation counseling to senior medical students: a peer role-play module versus a standardized patient module. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1668-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus repeated-measures ANOVA on OSCE scores across the four assessment periods found "no significance of group-by-time interaction" (p = 0.30); between-module post-clerkship (d 0.35, p = 0.06) and pre-KMLE (d 0.16, p = 0.38) differences were also non-significant.

> "There was no significance of group-by-time interaction in the OSCE score by repeated-measures ANOVA (p = 0.30)."
