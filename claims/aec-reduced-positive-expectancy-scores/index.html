Evidence¶

Pamela Portelli 2018¶

Pamela Portelli. (2018). A clustered randomized controlled trial for the prevention of alcohol misuse among Maltese teenagers. Special Issue Volume 10, Number 1, April 2018, pp 112-132. https://www.um.edu.mt/ijee

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Mann Whitney comparison of AEQ-A positive expectancy sub-scales immediately post-intervention in the cluster RCT. Scale 3 (p = 0.004) and Scale 7 (p < 0.001, r = 0.32) also differed significantly; Scale 6 did not (p = 0.13).

"Statistical analysis revealed a significant difference in Scale 2 AE scores in the intervention group ( Mdn = 6) when compared to the control group ( Mdn = 8.5), U = 1013, p <0.001, r = 0.40."

Pamela Portelli 2018 (2)¶

Pamela Portelli. (2018). A clustered randomized controlled trial for the prevention of alcohol misuse among Maltese teenagers. Special Issue Volume 10, Number 1, April 2018, pp 112-132. https://www.um.edu.mt/ijee

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Four-month follow-up Mann Whitney tests showed significant differences for Scales 2 (p = 0.03, r = 0.18), 3 and 7 (p = 0.01, r = 0.21); the hypothesis of lower AE in the intervention group was "partly supported".

"On a similar note, a significant difference in Scale 3 AE scores was observed between the intervention ( Mdn = 1) and the control group ( Mdn = 4), U= 876, p < 0.001, r = 0.32."
