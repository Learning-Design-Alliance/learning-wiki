## Evidence

### Netland 2025

Netland, Torbjörn; von Dzengelevski, Oliver; Tesch, Katalin. (2025). Comparing human-made and AI-generated teaching videos: An experimental study on learning effects. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105164

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Across-subject online experiment with 447 participants comparing AI-generated and human-made teaching videos; the article reports a "statistically significant but small advantage" in learning experience ratings for human-made videos.

> "The findings show that human-generated videos provided a statistically significant but small advantage to participants in terms of learning experience, indicating that the participants still prefer to be taught by human teachers."
