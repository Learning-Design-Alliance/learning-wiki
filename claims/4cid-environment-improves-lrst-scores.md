# A 4C/ID-based online learning environment improves upper secondary students' research skills test scores from pretest to posttest

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Students improved significantly on the LRST posttest compared to pretest, d = -0.567. [→ Louise Maddens 2023](#louise-maddens-2023)

## Evidence

### Louise Maddens 2023

Louise Maddens, Fien Depaepe, Annelies Raes, Jan Elen. (2023). Fostering students' motivation towards learning research skills: the role of autonomy, competence and relatedness support. Instructional Science 51, 165–199. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09606-4

`q2 · i2`

Paired samples t-test on the Leuven Research Skills Test in the quasi-experimental classroom study; scores are on a 0-1 range. The difference was significant with d = -0.567 as "A paired samples t-test reveals that" students improved.

> "A paired samples t-test reveals that, in general, students (n = 210) improved on the LRST-posttest (M = 0.57, SD = 0.16) compared to the pretest (M = 0.51, SD = 0.15)"
