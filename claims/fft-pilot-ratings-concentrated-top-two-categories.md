## Evidence

### Stephen Lipscomb 2015

Stephen Lipscomb, Jeffrey Terziev, Duncan Chaplin. (2015). Measuring Teachers' Effectiveness: A Report from Phase 3 of Pennsylvania's Pilot of the Framework for Teaching. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/measuring-teachers-effectiveness-a-report-from-phase-3-of-pennsylvanias-pilot-of-the-framework

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of pilot observation data from the 2012-2013 school year covering 6,676 teachers in 269 Pennsylvania districts. The report states "less than 13 percent of teachers received the top rating (distinguished)" and "almost 85 percent were rated in the second highest category (proficient)"; less than 0.1 percent were rated failing and around 2.6 percent needs improvement.

> "Based on these pilot data from the 2012–2013 school year, we estimate that, although less than 13 percent of teachers received the top rating (distinguished) for the overall PPR score, almost 85 percent were rated in the second highest category (proficient)."
