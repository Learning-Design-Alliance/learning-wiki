## Evidence

### Wu Yali 2020

Wu Yali, Gong Mingfu, Zhang Dong, Zhang Chun. (2020). Educational impact of the mini-Clinical Evaluation Exercise in resident standardization training: a comparative study between resident and professional degree postgraduate trainees. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060520920052

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective analysis of mini-CEX scores from 154 first-year resident standardization training trainees (57 PDPG, 97 RE) registered 2017-2019 at one hospital, compared with Student t-test. "The mean score (SD) of the PDPG group for overall performance was 84.18 (4.25)" exceeded the RE mean of 81.48 (3.35) at p < 0.05.

> "The mean score (SD) of the PDPG group for overall performance was 84.18 (4.25), which was higher than that of RE trainees (81.48 (3.35), p < 0.05)"
