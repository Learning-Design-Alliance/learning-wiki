q2 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

The review narrates the earlier Valdebenito et al. (2018) meta-analysis of 37 RCTs published from 1980 onwards, which reported SMD = 0.30 (95% CI [0.20, 0.41]) in the short term, with the effect "not sustained in longer‐term follow‐ups".

"this review identified a small but statistically significant reduction in school exclusion in the short term (SMD = 0.30; 95% CI [0.20, 0.41]; p < 0.001). However, the effect was not sustained in longer‐term follow‐ups."
