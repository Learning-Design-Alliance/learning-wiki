## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Four Chinese-language models scored lower on policies-and-ethics questions than on professional-knowledge questions, while the two English-language models (Llama and ChatGPT) showed the opposite pattern. [→ Yao Zhong 2025](#yao-zhong-2025)

## Evidence

### Yao Zhong 2025

Yao Zhong, Duan Liantan, Xu Shuo, Chi Lingyi, Sheng Dongfang. (2025). Performance of Large Language Models in the Non-English Context: Qualitative Study of Models Trained on Different Languages in Chinese Medical Examinations. JMIR Medical Informatics. https://doi.org/10.2196/69485

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the 600 CNMLE questions split by knowledge scope into 28 policies-and-ethics and 572 professional-knowledge questions. The article reports ChatGPT achieved the highest ethics accuracy (85.71%) while Baichuan led professional knowledge at 86.71%.

> "4 Chinese-language models (ERNIE Bot, Kimi, Baichuan, and ChatGLM) demonstrated lower accuracy on policies and ethics questions than on professional knowledge questions. In contrast, the 2 English-language models (Llama and ChatGPT) exhibited higher accuracy on policies and ethics questions compared to professional knowledge questions."
