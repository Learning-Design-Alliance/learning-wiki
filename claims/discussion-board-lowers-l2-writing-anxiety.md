## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Focus group participants reported feeling more confident expressing opinions via asynchronous discussion boards than in face-to-face classroom recitation. [→ Topacio 2018](#topacio-2018)

## Evidence

### Topacio 2018

Topacio, K. N. M. (2018). Exploring the use of online educational platform in teaching writing among ESL students. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(1), 86-101. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1175799

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative focus group interviews with randomly chosen participants in three smaller groups per condition. Students cited the "less personal feedback system" and ability to "think of grammar first before posting" as sources of confidence absent in classroom recitation.

> "There was a unanimous agreement during the interviews that the discussion board was helpful way for them to express their ideas without the fear of disagreement and criticism because of incorrect grammar"
