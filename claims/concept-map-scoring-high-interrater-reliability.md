## Evidence

### Hung Chia-Hui 2015

Hung Chia-Hui, Lin Chen-Yung. (2015). Using concept mapping to evaluate knowledge structure in problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0496-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reliability analysis in which two trained raters scored 29 concept maps drawn by participants using the Novak & Gowin quantitative scoring protocol; ICC(2,1) coefficients were computed for each scoring parameter.

> "The ICC coefficient for interrater reliability of the concept mapping scores indicated good agreement in concept relationships of 0.99 (95 % CI, 0.95–0.99); in the hierarchy, of 0.96 (95 % CI, 0.84–0.96); in levels, of 0.92 (95 % CI, 0.72–0.93); and in cross linkages, of 0.95 (95 % CI, 0.79–0.95)"
