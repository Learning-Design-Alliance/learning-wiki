Khodabakhshzadeh et al. 2017¶

Hossein Khodabakhshzadeh, Reza Zardkanloo, Iman Alipoor. (2017). The Effect of Mock Tests on Iranian EFL learners’ Test Scores. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies 5(3). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.5n.3p.47

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Baseline homogeneity check in the quasi-experiment: pretest scores of Group 1 (M=20.11, SD=2.898) and Group 2 (M=20.45, SD=3.011) compared via independent samples t-test; the article reports "non-significant difference between the groups was assumed." Equivalence was not formally tested beyond this comparison.

"As shown in Table 3, (t(49)=-4.87, p=.12 ≥ α= 0.05); therefore non-significant difference between the groups was assumed. In addition, the difference between Group 1 (M= 20.11, SD= 2.898) and Group 2 (M=20.45, SD=3.011) is not significant."
