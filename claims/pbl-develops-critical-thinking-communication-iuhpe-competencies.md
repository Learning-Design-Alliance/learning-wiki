### Leavy JE 2022

Leavy JE, Della Bona M, Nelson B, Leaversuch F. (2022). A comparison of face‐to‐face and fully online problem‐based learning: Student results and staff experiences, 2014–2020. Health Promot J Austral. https://doi.org/10.1002/hpja.636

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive qualitative analysis of five academic staff interviews (20–30 minutes, transcribed) and aggregated student evaluation comments. Although PBL scenarios were "not specifically designed to meet" IUHPE competencies, staff identified competencies including Enable Change, Evaluation & Research and Communication as inherent in the process. No effect size.

> "Staff and students described improved academic writing, critical thinking, problem solving and communication skills. A fully online student in 2016 commented "This unit helped me to further develop critical thinking and academic writing skills through the PBL process.""
