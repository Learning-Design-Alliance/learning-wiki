## Evidence

### Stolze Franka 2026

Stolze Franka, Lehm Manuel, Behme Daniel, Fuchs Erelle, Schwab Roland. (2026). Comparison of video-based and presentation-based self-directed instruction for learning basic neuroangiography. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10418-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of 10 experienced vs 10 inexperienced participants on the objective checklist; experienced participants used 35.41% less fluoroscopy time (r = -0.456) and scored higher on handling, systematic approach, and problem identification across catheters.

> "Experienced participants needed 35.41% less fluoroscopy time than inexperienced ones with the same catheter (U = 23.000, p = 0.043, r = -0.456, Table 2). They also demonstrated a significant superiority in handling the Vertebralis catheter (p = 0.019), a significantly better strategic approach (p = 0.017, Table 2), as well as better identification and resolution of problems (p = 0.007; p = 0.002, Table 2)."

### Stolze Franka 2026 (2)

Stolze Franka, Lehm Manuel, Behme Daniel, Fuchs Erelle, Schwab Roland. (2026). Comparison of video-based and presentation-based self-directed instruction for learning basic neuroangiography. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10418-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Simmons 2 catheter results from the same experienced-vs-inexperienced comparison: success rates (RICA p = 0.034, LICA p = 0.001), path deviations on all three vessels, and 47.91% less fluoroscopy time (p = 0.029) all favoured experienced participants.

> "Experienced participants had significantly higher success rates in catheterizing the RICA (p = 0.034) and the LICA (p = 0.001, Table 2). They had fewer path deviations for all blood vessels (RICA: p = 0.022, LICA: p = 0.003, LV: p = 0.042), and required 47.91% less fluoroscopy time (p = 0.029; Table 2)."
