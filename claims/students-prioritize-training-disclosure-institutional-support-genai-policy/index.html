Hingle 2025¶

Hingle, A., & Johri, A. (2025). A Guided Inquiry Approach to Students Co-Designing Generative AI Course Policies. https://www.google.com/search?q=A+Guided+Inquiry+Approach+to+Students+Co-Designing+Generative+AI+Course+Policies

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Hybrid inductive-deductive thematic analysis (Cohen's Kappa .84) of individual policy assignments and group discussions from 55 consenting undergraduates in a technology-ethics course, organized around the ED-AI Lit Framework. The study "showed that participants prioritized training for students and instructors, standardized procedures for disclosing AI use, and stronger institutional support."

"Analysis of student artifacts and group discussions showed that participants prioritized training for students and instructors, standardized procedures for disclosing AI use, and stronger institutional support."
