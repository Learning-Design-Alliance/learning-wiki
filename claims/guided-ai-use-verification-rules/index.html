Evidence¶

Liu YJ 2026¶

Liu YJ, Ma HZ, Luo HY, Xiong YX, Qu FW, Xie JP, Guo Y. (2026). From traditional classroom to AI-enhanced flipped classroom: a three-year pedagogical evolution for international students in pharmacology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09608-7

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Design description of the 2025 AI-enhanced cohort's guidance. Students used DeepSeek V3.1 and Kimi across pre-, in-, and post-class phases; no formal training or prompt templates were provided, by design, to observe natural self-directed AI use.

"Students were not allowed to submit AI-generated content directly. They had to verify, modify, and integrate AI outputs with authoritative sources, and they took full responsibility for the accuracy and originality of all submitted work."
