## Evidence

### Boelens 2018

Boelens, R., Voet, M., & De Wever, B. (2018). The design of blended learning in response to student diversity in higher education: Instructors' views and use of differentiated instruction in blended learning. Computers & Education, 120, 197–212. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.02.009

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview study of 20 instructors in two Flemish adult education centers; instructors self-reported strategies for differentiated instruction in semi-structured interviews. "interventions on the affect (n=8) and content (n=9) level were reported less frequently" than product and process strategies.

> "It turns out that instructors most frequently reported strategies for differentiated instruction on product (n=15) and process (n=13) level, while interventions on the affect (n=8) and content (n=9) level were reported less frequently by the instructors."
