## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a school-fixed-effects nonlinear regression on 12,960 adolescents, the linear physical activity coefficient was significantly positive and the square term significantly negative, satisfying the inverted U criteria with an inflection point of about 3.549 h/week. ## Evidence

### Wang Tianjiao & Guo Congbin 2022

Wang Tianjiao & Guo Congbin. (2022). Inverted U-Shaped Relationship between Physical Activity and Academic Achievement among Chinese Adolescents: On the Mediating Role of Physical and Mental Health. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19084678

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional secondary analysis of CEPS 2013–2014 baseline data (n = 12,960 after listwise deletion) using a nonlinear education production function with school fixed effects, individual and family controls. Model 2 in Table 2 shows a positive linear term and negative square term; the article reports the inflection point at "approximately 3.549 h/week".

> "It can be considered that there is an inverted U-shaped nonlinear relationship between the length of adolescent physical activity and academic achievement, and the inflection point was approximately 3.549 h/week."
