## Evidence

### Buss 2017

Buss, R. R., Lindsey, L., Foulger, T. S., Wetzel, K., & Pasquel, S. (2017). Assessing a technology infusion approach in a teacher preparation program. International Journal of Technology in Teaching and Learning, 13(1), 33-44. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Assessing+a+technology+infusion+approach+in+a+teacher+preparation+program

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative analysis of open-ended responses to how candidates hope to use technology-infused instruction in their own classrooms next year. The article reports results "are remarkably similar" to student-teaching use, with teacher-directed delivery, routine effective use, higher-level use, and continued interactive whiteboard use all represented.

> "the majority of respondents wrote about using technology and expecting to use technology in routine, non-transformative ways. Still others claimed they used and intended to use technology in more transformative fashion, albeit the number of these TCs was much smaller."
