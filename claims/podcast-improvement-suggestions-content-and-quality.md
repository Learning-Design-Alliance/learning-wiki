## Evidence

### Prakash S.S. 2017

Prakash S.S., Muthuraman N., Anand R. (2017). Short-duration podcasts as a supplementary learning tool: perceptions of medical students and impact on assessment performance. BMC Medical Education 17:167. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1001-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative coding of open-ended item A2 suggestions (N = 76) on the adapted coding scheme; learning-challenge scored −0.26 and engagement-technology −0.11, indicating needs for improvement. Sample comments included "include more concepts" and "make it easier to download".

> "In the learning category, the majority of students’ comments suggested improving the challenge component of 3MTLs by including more concepts and extending to other complex topics in biochemistry. Another suggestion was to increase the duration of podcasts."
