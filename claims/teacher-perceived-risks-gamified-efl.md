### Zhanni Luo 2024 (2)

Zhanni Luo. (2024). Factors contributing to teachers' acceptance intention to gamified EFL tools: a scale development study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10249-6

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Email-interview responses coded during item generation for the perceived-risks construct. The article counts 36 responses mentioning side effects, with distraction (n = 9) and eyesight damage (n = 8) most frequent. Descriptive counts only.

> "Thirty-six email-interview responses mentioned the side effects of using gamified learning tools, including "digital addiction" (n = 7), "distraction" (n = 9), "privacy risk" (n = 1), "eyesight damage" (n = 8)"

### Zhanni Luo 2024 (3)

Zhanni Luo. (2024). Factors contributing to teachers' acceptance intention to gamified EFL tools: a scale development study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10249-6

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Face-to-face and email interview responses analysed thematically during item generation. Teachers worried about students' self-control with devices, tools with fixed knowledge systems marginalising their role, and discipline management in large classes. Qualitative, no effect size.

> "The interview responses revealed that teachers expressed concerns regarding their control over the device, the learning process, and discipline management when using gamified EFL tools."
