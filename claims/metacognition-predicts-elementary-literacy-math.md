## Evidence

### Ha Cheyeon 2023

Ha Cheyeon, Roehrig Alysia D., Zhang Qian. (2023). Self-regulated learning strategies and academic achievement in South Korean 6th-graders: A two-level hierarchical linear modeling analysis. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0284385

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Initial 2-level HLM of KELS data (7,065 students, 446 schools) with cognitive strategies predicting literacy. The article reports "as metacognition increased, their literacy achievement tended to increase" within and between schools; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "for students in an average school, as metacognition increased, their literacy achievement tended to increase, holding constant other predictors (γ = 5.657, p < .001); also, schools with higher average levels of metacognition were associated with higher average levels of literacy achievement (γ = 13.470, p < .01)"

### Ha Cheyeon 2023 (2)

Ha Cheyeon, Roehrig Alysia D., Zhang Qian. (2023). Self-regulated learning strategies and academic achievement in South Korean 6th-graders: A two-level hierarchical linear modeling analysis. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0284385

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same HLM series with math as outcome. The article reports metacognition "increased, math achievement tended to increase" within and between schools; only unstandardized γ coefficients with p-values are printed, so no standardized effect size is available.

> "when their metacognition increased, math achievement tended to increase, holding constant other predictors (γ = 6.323, p < .001); also, schools with higher average levels of metacognition tended to show higher average levels of math achievement (γ = 20.234, p < .01)"
