## Evidence

### Artioli Giovanna 2019

Artioli Giovanna, Bedini Gabriele, Bertocchi Elisabetta, Ghirotto Luca, Cavuto Silvio, Costantini Massimo, Tanzi Silvia. (2019). Palliative care training addressed to hospital healthcare professionals by palliative care specialists: a mixed-method evaluation. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-019-0476-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training focus group finding: participants identified continued communication difficulties and, per the article, "the need to share personal aspects of their professional life" emerged only in the qualitative research (Theme 5).

> "They stressed that this issue remained a critical point despite having undergone the present training course and began planning further training that could help them address their communication needs towards a collaborative approach with PC professionals."

### Artioli Giovanna 2019 (2)

Artioli Giovanna, Bedini Gabriele, Bertocchi Elisabetta, Ghirotto Luca, Cavuto Silvio, Costantini Massimo, Tanzi Silvia. (2019). Palliative care training addressed to hospital healthcare professionals by palliative care specialists: a mixed-method evaluation. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-019-0476-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training focus groups on self-consideration of emotions: staff reported bearing emotional burden alone and proposed mutual-support moments for discussing cases and emotional reactions, coded as Theme 5.

> "Following training, professionals in the FGs recognized above all the need to receive psychological support themselves to face complex EoL situations."
