# Both PAS types correlate positively with adolescent outcomes, but companion coefficients are generally higher than manager coefficients

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jang Yujin 2021

Jang Yujin, Suh Youngmeen. (2021). Longitudinal Effects of Parental Academic Support on Academic Achievement in Korea: Will You Be a Companion or a Manager in Your Children’s Academic Support? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182010823

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of the KELS sample (Table 3): companion PAS correlates r = 0.16 with self-regulation and r = 0.10–0.17 with subject scores; manager PAS correlates r = 0.12 with self-regulation and r = 0.04–0.15 with subject scores; both correlate negatively with amotivation (−0.14 and −0.09). These printed r values are small (|r| = .10–.19 range for the significant ones).

> "Both PAS as a companion and PAS as a manager showed correlations with adolescents’ self-regulation and Korean, Math, and English grades; however, both types of PAS showed a negative correlation with amotivation."

### Jang Yujin 2021 (2)

Jang Yujin, Suh Youngmeen. (2021). Longitudinal Effects of Parental Academic Support on Academic Achievement in Korea: Will You Be a Companion or a Manager in Your Children’s Academic Support? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182010823

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Same correlation matrix (Table 3) in the KELS panel data: the companion type's coefficients exceed the manager type's for self-regulation, amotivation, Korean, and English; only the Math correlation reverses (r = 0.14 vs r = 0.15), both small printed correlations.

> "The correlation coefficients differed, and the coefficients were generally higher for PAS as a companion than for PAS as a manager, except for Math (PAS as a companion: r = 0.14, PAS as a manager: r = 0.15)."
