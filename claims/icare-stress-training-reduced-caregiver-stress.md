## Evidence

### Klimova Blanka 2019

Klimova Blanka, Valis Martin, Kuca Kamil, Masopust Jiri. (2019). E-learning as valuable caregivers’ support for people with dementia – A systematic review. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-019-4641-9

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in which 150 informal caregivers were randomly divided into the iCare Condition (ICC) or the Education/Information-Only Condition (EOC), using a 3-month iCare Stress Management e-Training Program; the review reports the printed p value of .017 for the between-condition contrast.

> "The main outcome was a change in perceived stress; it was significant for the ICC but not the EOC (p = .017)."
