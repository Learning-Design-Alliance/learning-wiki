## Evidence

### Carlo Maria Carbonaro 2020

Carlo Maria Carbonaro, Alessia Zurru, Viviana Fanti, Matteo Tuveri, Gianluca Usai. (2020). Cooperative Problem Solving: an experience of high-school teaching updating. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire with the 98 students participating in the CPS laboratories after solving text-enriched problems. The article reports "93.8% rated it >3 in a 1-5 scale" for interest and medium difficulty for 72.6%.

> "In general, the experience was evaluated as interesting (93.8% rated it >3 in a 1-5 scale) and with a medium level of difficulty (72.6% rated a level 2-3 in a 1-5 scale)."

### Carlo Maria Carbonaro 2020 (2)

Carlo Maria Carbonaro, Alessia Zurru, Viviana Fanti, Matteo Tuveri, Gianluca Usai. (2020). Cooperative Problem Solving: an experience of high-school teaching updating. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Open-comment question on which aspects helped most in finding the solution, answered by the participating students. The most appreciated aspect was "the discussion within the group and the sharing of knowledge", with role splitting and the resolving scheme also cited.

> "The most appreciated aspect was the discussion within the group and the sharing of knowledge. Other beneficial aspects were the splitting in roles and the resolving scheme."
