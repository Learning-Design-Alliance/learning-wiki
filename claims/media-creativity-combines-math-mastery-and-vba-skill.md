### Rohaeti 2019

Rohaeti, E.E., Bernard, M., & Primandhika, R.B. (2019). Developing interactive learning media for school level mathematics through open-ended approach aided by Visual Basic application for Excel. Journal on Mathematics Education, 10(1), 59-68. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1204890

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation from the analysis of the media created by student groups, not a tested comparison. They state the combination of mathematics mastery and VBA-aided media-making "is a good creative thinking process", citing prior work (Bernard & Rohaeti, 2016; Fatah et al., 2016; others).
