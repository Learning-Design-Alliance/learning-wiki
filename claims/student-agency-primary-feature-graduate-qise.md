Practitioner observation reported as the finding that emerged most consistently across the eighteen NRT programs. The article offers no outcome measures for this assertion; it is a synthesis of program experience, not a tested result.

> "student agency (defined as structured ownership by students of program elements that persist beyond their own tenure) is not a secondary feature of successful graduate QISE programs but a primary one, producing professional skills, community cohesion, and cross-disciplinary fluency that formal coursework alone does not reliably develop."
