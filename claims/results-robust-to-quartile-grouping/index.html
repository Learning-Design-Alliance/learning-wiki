Evidence¶

Steven Glazerman and Jeffrey Max 2011¶

Steven Glazerman and Jeffrey Max. (2011). Do Low-Income Students Have Equal Access to the Highest-Performing Teachers? NCEE Technical Appendix. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs/20114016/pdf/20114016.pdf

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Robustness analysis re-grouping schools into poverty quartiles, presented in Figure A.2 for elementary, middle school math, and middle school ELA. The figure note states the "Chi -square test of no relationship between quintile and percent highest -performing is rejected at the 0.05 level" for the starred panels.

"Grouping schools into quintiles was an arbitrary choice, so we also examined the results grouping schools into four instead of five equal -sized groups of schools. Figure A.2 shows the main results by grouping schools into quartiles instead of quintiles."
