## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jiang 2014

Jiang, J. (2014). Teacher Evaluation in Practice: Year Two Teacher and Administrator Perceptions of REACH. University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-evaluation-practice-year-2-teacher-and-administrator-perceptions-reach

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Survey of more than 19,000 teachers and nearly 800 principals and assistant principals in Chicago Public Schools in REACH's second year. "About two-thirds of teachers" and 89 percent of principals agreed REACH will improve instruction and outcomes; teacher satisfaction dropped from over 70 percent to 62 percent.

> "while the majority of teachers (62 percent) reported being satisfied with the evaluation process at their school, this is a decrease in satisfaction from the first year of REACH, when over 70 percent reported satisfaction with the process"
