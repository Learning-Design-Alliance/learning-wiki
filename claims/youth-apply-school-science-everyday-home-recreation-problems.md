## Evidence

### Gitari 2016

Gitari, W. (2016). The Application of School Science by Urban High School Youth through Problem-solving in Everyday Life. Science Education International, 27(3), 344-368. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1118432

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative focus group study of ten Ontario high school youth (two focus groups of five) reporting past non-guided problem-solving incidents. Events ranged "from baking a cake for New Year celebration to deterring boredom while on a cruise," and the analysis "detailed two settings, namely home and family related, and play and recreation situations."

> "Problem-solving events, involving the application of scientific knowledge, ranged from baking a cake for New Year celebration to deterring boredom while on a cruise."
