### Ozcelik 2013

Ozcelik, E., Ercil Cagiltay, N., & Sahin Ozcelik, N. (2013). The effect of uncertainty on learning in game-like environments. Computers & Education, 67, 12–20. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2013.02.009

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The LISREL path analysis of the game experiment data showed "motivation was positively related with accuracy" and "the response time to the questions", supporting hypotheses H2 and H3. As motivation increased, participants tended to spend more time answering questions and achieve higher accuracy.

> "Motivation was positively related with accuracy (t ¼ 3.82) and the response time to the questions (t ¼ 2.23), therefore, H2 and H3 were supported."
