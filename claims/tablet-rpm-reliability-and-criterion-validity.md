# A 3-minute tablet-based single-digit arithmetic fluency assessment is highly reliable and explains roughly 36% of variance in standardized math test scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Correct responses per minute (RPM) over the 3-minute tablet session was highly reliable (r = 0.97) and 3 minutes of single-digit fluency data explained roughly 36% of variance in SBAC standardized math scores. [→ Ethan Roy 2025](#ethan-roy-2025)

## Evidence

### Ethan Roy 2025

Ethan Roy, Mathieu Guillaume, Amandine Van Rinsveld, Project iLead Consortium & Bruce D. McCandliss. (2025). Tablet-based arithmetic fluency assessment reveals developments in math cognition and math achievement from childhood to adolescence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00314-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the tablet-based single-digit fluency module in the study's cohort of 3rd, 5th, and 7th graders. The article reports "RPM proved highly reliable (r = 0.97)" across the 3-min session, with r = 0.95 for 2 min and r = 0.92 for 1 min of data; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Across the entire 3-min session, RPM proved highly reliable (r = 0.97; Fig. 1, left panel)."

### Ethan Roy 2025 (2)

Ethan Roy, Mathieu Guillaume, Amandine Van Rinsveld, Project iLead Consortium & Bruce D. McCandliss. (2025). Tablet-based arithmetic fluency assessment reveals developments in math cognition and math achievement from childhood to adolescence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00314-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Criterion validity analysis predicting Smarter Balanced (SBAC) math scores from RPM. The article reports that "3 min of single-digit fluency served to explain roughly 36% of the variance in standardized test scores", with first-minute and 2-minute subsets explaining over 24% and 32% respectively; variance explained is not treated as an effect size.

> "We found that 3 min of single-digit fluency served to explain roughly 36% of the variance in standardized test scores (Fig.1, right panel)."
