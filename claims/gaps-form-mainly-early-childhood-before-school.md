Paul T. von Hippel, Caitlin Hamrock. (2019). Do Test Score Gaps Grow Before, During, or Between the School Years? Measurement Artifacts and What We Can Know in Spite of Them. Sociological Science 6: 43-80. https://doi.org/10.15195/v6.a3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article's synthesis across three longitudinal datasets (BSS, ECLS-K, GRD) concludes that "gaps are almost full ﬂedged by the time children begin elementary school" and that later changes are relatively small.

> "Once measurement artifacts are accounted for, the most-replicable result is that gaps are almost full ﬂedged by the time children begin elementary school. Any changes in gaps during later school years and summers are relatively small."
