# Annenberg students' ITBS achievement improved while student engagement and classroom behavior declined, mirroring outcomes across the Chicago system

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Student academic achievement on the ITBS improved in Annenberg schools, but student engagement and classroom behavior levels declined, in patterns mirroring student outcomes across the system. [→ Wenzel 2001](#wenzel-2001)

## Evidence

### Wenzel 2001

Wenzel, S. A., Smylie, M. A., Sebring, P. B., Allensworth, E., Gutierrez, T., Hallman, S., Luppescu, S., & Miller, S. R. (2001). Development of Chicago Annenberg Schools: 1996-1999. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED452313

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analyses of standardized ITBS scores and survey data compared Annenberg elementary schools with demographically comparable non-Annenberg schools from 1996-97 through 1998-99. The report found achievement "as measured by the ITBS improved, but student engagement and classroom behavior levels declined."

> "Likewise, we found that student achievement and social-psychological outcomes in Annenberg schools mirrored student outcomes across the system—students' academic achievement as measured by the ITBS improved, but student engagement and classroom behavior levels declined."
