Spearman correlations across the 400-student four-school comparative sample (Table 6, N=400); printed coefficients include r = .477 for demonstrative by ICTE and r = .402 for school outings, both large effect sizes significant at the .01 level, ranging down to -.122 (dogmatic). The interrogative method was "the only one that has not shown any significant difference in its use among the schools studied."

"the methods that are used with very significant differences between the schools studied are active dialogue, discovery method, student presentations, demonstrative methods, laboratory experiments and School outings. These are all active methods associated primarily with private school and to a lesser extent with the school located in downtown."
