Evidence¶

Mary Beth Calhoon 2013¶

Mary Beth Calhoon, Hollis S. Scarborough, Brett Miller. (2013). Interventions for struggling adolescent and adult readers: instructional, learner, and situational differences. Read Writ 26:489–494. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-013-9442-7

q2 · i? · review · r1

The editorial reports, from Calhoon and Petscher's investigation across three middle school reading projects using RAMP-UP, that latent change score analyses combined with individual gain classifications showed outcomes "strongly related to the modality in which reading instruction had been provided".

"Results from both analytic approaches indicated that reading outcomes for these struggling adolescent readers were strongly related to the modality in which reading instruction had been provided."
