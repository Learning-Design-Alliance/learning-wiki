## Evidence

### Södersved Källestedt 2025

Södersved Källestedt, M.-L., & Hidefjäll, P. (2025). A mixed methods study of self-directed learning in clinical practice using a mobile skills training system. BMC Medical Education, 25, 1515. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08127-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey of 78 nurses and assistant nurses before and after SDL skills training at a mid-sized Swedish regional hospital. The article reports a statistically significant shift toward higher self-assessed self-efficacy, with more "true" answers after training.

> "Following their skill-trained care situations with SDL, the healthcare professionals self-assessed their self-efficacy as higher than before using this learning method (see Table 2), with more "true" answers after training. The Wilcoxon test yielded p < 0.017."
