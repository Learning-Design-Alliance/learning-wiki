Descriptive concentration analysis of query volume across participants in the nine-week deployment record, reported as a Lorenz curve (Fig. 1) and Gini coefficient. The article reports a Gini of 0.447 across participants, with the least active half accounting for 20.0% of volume.

> "Across the 1,434 participants, mean demand is 2.85 queries, median 2 and maximum 36. The most active decile accounts for 34.6% of queries and the most active fifth for 51.9%. Exactly one query was raised by 42.9% of participants, and 4.0% raised ten or more."
