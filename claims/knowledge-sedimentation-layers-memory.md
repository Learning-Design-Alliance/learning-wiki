The article explicitly frames this as an assumption: "It is assumed that knowledge acquired in different phases form layers in memory." The sediments "remain available for use" when newer structures fail, completing the theory's account of earlier-phase knowledge.

> "It is assumed that knowledge acquired in different phases form layers in memory through a sedimentation process. These knowledge sediments, although usually not applied any more in subsequent phases in the development of expertise, remain available for use when ontologically more recently acquired structures fail to produce an adequate representation of a clinical problem."
