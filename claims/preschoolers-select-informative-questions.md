## Evidence

### Azzurra Ruggeri 2022

Azzurra Ruggeri. (2022). An Introduction to Ecological Active Learning. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/09637214221112114

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Study 1 from Ruggeri et al. (2017), described in the article: 4- and 5-year-olds heard a storybook about Toma's lateness under uniform or skewed conditions and chose which of two monster friends asked the more informative question. 'In both conditions, the majority of children correctly selected the monster asking the more informative question.' Percentages are descriptive; no effect size printed.

> "In the uniform condition, 70% of the children selected the monster who asked the constraint-seeking question, and in the skewed condition, 73% of the children selected the monster who asked the hypothesis-scanning question."
