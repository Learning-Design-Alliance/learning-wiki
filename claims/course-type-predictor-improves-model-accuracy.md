## Evidence

### Orr Robin M. 2020

Orr Robin M., Cohen Bruce S., Allison Stephen C., Bulathsinhala Lakmini, Zambraski Edward J., Jaffrey Mark. (2020). Models to predict injury, physical fitness failure and attrition in recruit training: a retrospective cohort study. Military Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40779-020-00260-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression models built on both courses combined, with course type (duration) as a potential ninth predictor, evaluated by ROC/AUC. The models retained "2 to 7 predictors" with AUCs from 0.59 to 0.78; the abstract reports two models with AUC of 0.70 and 0.78, considered fair predictive accuracy. Nagelkerke R2 ranged from 0.02 to 0.14.

> "The models created with the combined data retained 2 to 7 predictors, with AUCs for the associated ROC curves ranging from 0.59 (predicting attrition) to 0.78 (predicting stress fracture). Course type was retained as a predictor in all models except for that for attrition."
