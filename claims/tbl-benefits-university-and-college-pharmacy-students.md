# TBL benefits pharmacy students in both 4-year university and 3-year college programs, with a larger pooled effect in colleges

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` TBL was beneficial for university (4-year program) students (SMD = 1.69, 95% CI [1.10, 2.28]) and college (3-year program) students (SMD = 4.37, 95% CI [1.33, 7.40]). [→ Lang Bingchen 2019](#lang-bingchen-2019)

## Evidence

### Lang Bingchen 2019

Lang Bingchen, Zhang Lingli, Lin Yunzhu, Han Lu, Zhang Chuan, Liu Yantao. (2019). Team-based learning pedagogy enhances the quality of Chinese pharmacy education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1724-6

Subgroup analysis of theoretical test scores by education background, reported in the Results section. Both subgroups favored TBL with p < 0.00001; the college subgroup estimate ("SMD = 4.37") was larger but with a wide confidence interval.

> "TBL pedagogy was beneficial to both students who experienced 4-year program education from universities (SMD = 1.69, 95% CI [1.10, 2.28], p < 0.00001) and the ones who received 3-year program education from colleges (SMD = 4.37, 95% CI [1.33, 7.40], p < 0.00001)"
