## Evidence

### Oermann Marilyn H. 2020

Oermann Marilyn H., Krusmark Michael A., Kardong-Edgren Suzan, Jastrzembski Tiffany S., Gluck Kevin A. (2020). Training interval in cardiopulmonary resuscitation. PLoS ONE 15(1): e0226786. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0226786

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 475 nursing students assigned to daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly CPR training; linear mixed models on pretest scores showed gains in all intervals. The article reports "Overall compression scores improved from sessions 1 to 4 in all training intervals (all p < .001)". No effect size printed.

> "Overall compression scores improved from sessions 1 to 4 in all training intervals (all p < .001), but shorter intervals (daily training) resulted in larger increases in compression scores by session 4."
