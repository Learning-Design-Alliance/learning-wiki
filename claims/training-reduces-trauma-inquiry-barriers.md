### Lotzin Annett 2019

Lotzin Annett, Buth Sven, Sehner Susanne, Hiller Philipp, Pawils Silke, Metzner Franka, Read John, Härter Martin, Schäfer Ingo. (2019). Reducing barriers to trauma inquiry in substance use disorder treatment – a cluster-randomized controlled trial. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13011-019-0211-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial of 148 professionals in 27 German outpatient SUD centers, randomized by SUD organization to a one-day 'Learning How to Ask' training plus refresher or no training; primary outcomes analyzed with covariate-adjusted linear mixed models. The article reports "significant greater decreases in five of the six perceived barriers"; no standardized effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The trained group experienced significant greater decreases in five of the six perceived barriers to the inquiry of traumatic events from baseline to 6-month follow-up than the control group"
