Inductive content analysis of chapter 6 'Force and motion' of the textbook Rom Stoff Tid Fysikk 1, tracing which of the eight framework categories appear and in which order. The authors note the concept of force is given five substantially different definitions the reader must take into consideration: "the textbook include five out of eight categories".

> "It is important to note that the textbook include five out of eight categories, indicating a large diversity in how the concept of force is used in textbooks."
