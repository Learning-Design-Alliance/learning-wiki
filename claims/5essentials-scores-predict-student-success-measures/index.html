5Essentials Survey: New insights for school leaders 2026¶

5Essentials Survey: New insights for school leaders. (2026). University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The brief reports, citing University of Chicago Consortium re-examination studies of the 5Essentials Survey, that "strong scores and growth in measure scores" predict multiple student success measures, and states this holds "across elementary and high schools serving students with different backgrounds."

"strong scores and growth in measure scores on the 5Essentials Survey are both predictive of many student success measures, including GPA, attendance, test scores, and college enrollment."
