## Evidence

### Genevieve McArthur and Anne Castles 2017

Genevieve McArthur and Anne Castles. (2017). Helping children with reading difficulties: some things we have learned so far. npj Science of Learning (2017) 2:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0008-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The authors' synthesis of prior research on reading difficulty profiles, citing multiple studies of subtypes. They state poor readers are "highly heterogeneous" and describe distinct profiles: poor phonological recoding (detected by nonwords), poor sight word reading (exception words), poor fluency, and poor comprehension.

> "One thing that we have learned about poor readers is that they are highly heterogeneous; that is, they do not all display the same type of reading impairment"
