Fostering inclusion in EEG measures of pediatric brain activity 2024¶

Fostering inclusion in EEG measures of pediatric brain activity. (2024). npj Science of Learning 9, 27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00240-y

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review’s framing of the problem, based on the authors’ experience collecting pediatric EEGs with predominantly Black and/or Latinx families: “traditional methods of EEG data acquisition with standard equipment is often limited in some racial and ethnic groups due to common hair types and styles.”

"traditional methods of EEG data acquisition with standard equipment is often limited in some racial and ethnic groups due to common hair types and styles"

Fostering inclusion in EEG measures of pediatric brain activity 2024 (2)¶

Fostering inclusion in EEG measures of pediatric brain activity. (2024). npj Science of Learning 9, 27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00240-y

q2 · i? · review · r?

In the Equipment Considerations section the review states, as a descriptive claim about available hardware, that “the allowed space between the electrode and scalp is not wide enough to accommodate thick hair or raised hairstyles like braids,” noting many systems allow only a centimeter or two.

"The primary limitation for most caps and electrodes available on the market is that the allowed space between the electrode and scalp is not wide enough to accommodate thick hair or raised hairstyles like braids"
