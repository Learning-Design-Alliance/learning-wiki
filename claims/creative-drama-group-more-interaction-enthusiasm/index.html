Evidence¶

Özek 2016¶

Müzeyyen Bulut Özek. (2016). The Effect of Creative Drama on Student Achievement in The Course of Information Technologies. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 4, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i6.1370

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' observational interpretation from the discussion section, not a tested result: no instrument, measure or statistic is reported for these social-behavioral differences. The authors link it to Erbay & Doğru (2010) on social communication skills.

"In experimental 1 group where creative drama is employed it was detected that compared to experimental 2 and control group, students interact with each other more intensely, listen to one another more diligently, act in better harmony, exhibit more joyful and enthusiastic behavior."
