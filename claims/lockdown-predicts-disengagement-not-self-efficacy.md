# Lockdown was significantly associated with higher disengagement (β = 0.12) but not with self-efficacy among university students

## Evidence

### Martin Andrew J. 2023

Martin Andrew J., Ginns Paul, Collie Rebecca J. (2023). University students in COVID-19 lockdown: The role of adaptability and fluid reasoning in supporting their academic motivation and engagement. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101712

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model of 500 Australian undergraduates (fully-forward SEM, MLR estimator, students nested in 41 universities). The authors report lockdown "significantly predicted disengagement (β = 0.12, p = .01; medium effect)" while its path to self-efficacy was non-significant.

> "Findings showed that lockdown significantly predicted disengagement (β = 0.12, p = .01; medium effect), but it did not predict self-efficacy (the hypothesized mediator)."
