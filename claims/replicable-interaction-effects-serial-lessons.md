## Evidence

### Buchan 2020

Buchan, L., Hejmadi, M., Abrahams, L. & Hurst, L. D. (2020). A RCT for assessment of active human-centred learning finds teacher-centric non-human teaching of evolution optimal. npj Science of Learning 5:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00078-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simulation-based test following Sevdalis and Jacklin: true absolute interaction effect sums were compared against 10,000 random simulants per tranche in which SoW assignment was randomised (Table 7).

> "In both tranches, the true AIS is significantly greater than that expected by chance ( P ~ 1.00 × 10−4, one-tailed empirical P-value) with no simulant exceeding the observed interaction. This suggests the unique combinations of lessons in each SoW have meaningful impacts on scores."
