# Heterogeneity of outcome reporting across VRS simulation studies precluded quantitative meta-analysis

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Outcome measures were reported too heterogeneously across the included studies to permit quantitative analysis, limiting the strength of conclusions. [→ Rasmussen Rasmus Christian 2019](#rasmussen-rasmus-christian-2019)

## Evidence

The review's account of heterogeneity among the seven included studies: performance was reported variously as error scores, total scores across eleven or six modules, or time to completion. A qualitative analysis was therefore performed.
