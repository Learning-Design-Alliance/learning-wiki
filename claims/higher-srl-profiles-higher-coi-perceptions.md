## Evidence

### Chungsoo Na 2024

Chungsoo Na, Soojeong Jeong, Jody Clarke-Midura, Youngin Shin. (2024). Linking self-regulated learning to community of inquiry in online undergraduate courses: A person-centered approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10380-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA with SRL profile membership as independent variable and the three CoI presences as dependent variables, in the survey of 750 undergraduates. Follow-up analyses showed moderate and competent regulators perceived significantly higher presence than the other three profiles.

> "Significant differences were found across the five SRL profiles in the three-subscales for CoI (Pillai's Trace: 0.59, F (12, 2235) = 46.03, p < 0.001)."
