## Evidence

### Gross Jennifer 2015

Gross Jennifer, Lakey Brian, Lucas Jessica L, LaCross Ryan, R Plotkowski Andrea, Winegard Bo. (2015). Forecasting the student–professor matches that result in unusually effective teaching. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12049

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Variance-component analysis of 145 students' ratings of 10 professors. Table 1 prints professor variance proportions of .347 (trailer) and .359 (class) for teaching evaluations; the article reports no professor effects for negative affect or quiz scores.

> "There was a substantial professor effect (i.e., inter-rater agreement) on teaching effectiveness for both trailers and live lectures, accounting for about 35% of the variance (Table 1)."
