## Evidence

### Collins Simonne S.E. 2019

Collins Simonne S.E., Spencer-Smith M., Mürner-Lavanchy I., Kelly C.E., Pyman P., Pascoe L., Cheong J., Doyle L.W., Thompson D.K., Anderson P.J. (2019). White matter microstructure correlates with mathematics but not word reading performance in 13-year-old children born very preterm and full-term. NeuroImage: Clinical. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nicl.2019.101944

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression comparison of academic scores between the VPT (n = 114) and FT (n = 36) groups: mathematics mean difference was "−5.8 (−11.3, −0.3), p = .04" after adjustment. For reading, the VPT group was on average 4 points below the FT group, not significant (p ≥ .05).

> "VPT children performed below their FT peers in mathematics (Table 1), even after controlling for age and social risk [mean difference (95% confidence interval (CI)) = −5.8 (−11.3, −0.3), p = .04]."
