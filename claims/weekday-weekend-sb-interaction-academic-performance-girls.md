## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A significant interaction of SB with weekday/weekend on academic performance was observed in girls (p < 0.001), with no corresponding significant interaction reported for boys. [→ Huang Xin 2019](#huang-xin-2019)

## Evidence

### Huang Xin 2019

Huang Xin, Zeng Nan, Ye Sunyue. (2019). Associations of Sedentary Behavior with Physical Fitness and Academic Performance among Chinese Students Aged 8–19 Years. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16224494

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 4 model analysis of the 1040-student sample tested interactions between the weekday/weekend dichotomy and SB types. The interaction on academic performance was significant in girls (p < 0.001); no weekday or weekend SB associations with physical fitness were significant (p > 0.05).

> "after adjusting for age, school, grade, class, and SB relevant variables, significant interaction of SB with weekday/weekend on academic performance in girls was observed (p < 0.001), while no significant associations of week day or weekend SB (including screen-based SB, study-based SB, and other SB) with physical fitness were observed (p > 0.05)."
