## Evidence

### Wang Wei 2020

Wang Wei, Ma Hucheng, Ren Haozhen, Wang Zhongxia, Mao Liang, He Ningning. (2020). The Impact of Surgical Boot Camp and Subsequent Repetitive Practice on the Surgical Skills and Confidence of Residents. World Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00268-020-05669-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post confidence survey of the experimental group before and after the two-week boot camp, rated 1-5 on 21 tasks. Confidence "improved significantly after the boot camp" on all but two tasks (p < 0.05 for most items); no effect sizes are printed.

> "Our results (Table 3) showed that the confidence of residents in the experimental group improved significantly after the boot camp, except for two skills: "handle daily medical record writing (question 3)" and "perform a surgical puncture (question 9).""
