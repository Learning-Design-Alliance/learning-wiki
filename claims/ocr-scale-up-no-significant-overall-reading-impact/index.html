Evidence¶

Vaden-Kiernan 2016¶

Vaden-Kiernan, M., Borman, G., Caverly, S., Bell, N., Ruiz de Castilla, V., Sullivan, K., & Fleming, G. (2016). Findings from a Multi-Year Scale-Up Effectiveness Trial of Open-Court Reading (Imagine It!). SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567512

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Intent-to-treat analysis of the multi-site cluster randomized trial (49 schools, 7 districts; Year 1 analytical sample 4,392 students with valid Spring GRADE scores), using a three-level model with school-mean GRADE pretest as covariate. Table 2 prints effect sizes of 0.038 (Year 1) and -0.06 (Year 2), both negligible; equivalence was not tested.

"ITT analysis indicates that the OCR program does not have a statistically significant impact on students' reading achievement compared to the business as usual curriculum in place in control schools across either year."
