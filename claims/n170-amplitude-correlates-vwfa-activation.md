# The N170 reversed-minus-typical amplitude difference correlates with the VWFA reversed-minus-typical activation difference across participants, but not within either group

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across participants with both ERP and fMRI data (N = 17), the N170 amplitude difference at electrode P7 correlated significantly with the reversed > typical difference in the functionally defined VWFA; within each group separately there was no correlation. [→ Blackburne LK 2014](#blackburne-lk-2014)

## Evidence

### Blackburne LK 2014

Blackburne LK, Eddy MD, Kalra P, Yee D, Sinha P, Gabrieli JDE. (2014) Neural Correlates of Letter Reversal in Children and Adults. PLoS ONE 9(5): e98386. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0098386

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis combining ERP and fMRI data from 17 participants (10 adults, 7 children) who had both ERP data and a functionally defined VWFA. The P7 N170 difference correlated significantly with the VWFA difference (p = .004, two-tailed; the article notes the correlation is negative because the N170 is a negative going effect); PO7 was marginal (p = .05).

> "The mean amplitude difference on the N170 (computed by subtracting the amplitude of the response to typical letters from reversed letters) at electrode site P7 correlated significantly with the reversed letter . typical letter difference in the functionally defined VWFA (r(17) = 2.66, p = .004 (two tailed) (the correlation is negative because the N170 is a negative going effect)"
