Evidence¶

Nowbakht and Shahnazari 2015¶

Mohammad Nowbakht, Mohammadtaghi Shahnazari. (2015). The Comparative Effects of Comprehensible Input, Output and Corrective Feedback on the Receptive Acquisition of L2 Vocabulary Items. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.4p.103

q3 · i? · causal · r1

One-way ANOVA across three groups (control, 8 low-feedback receivers, 7 high-feedback receivers) was significant (F(2, 27) = 8.464, p = .001); post-test means were 21.571, 15.625 and 13.200 respectively, with the high-feedback group mean of "21.57" (SD = 1.98).

"The results of the Tukey post-hoc test display that the high-feedback group is significantly different in the post test from the low-feedback group (p= 0.04) and the control group (p= 0.001); however, the low-feedback group and the control group are not statistically different from each other ( p=0.43)."
