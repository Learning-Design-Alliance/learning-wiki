Longitudinal effect of early social media use on standardized learning outcomes during school career. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-026-02522-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

DiD estimates for English listening and reading (grade 8 only) show coefficients near zero with confidence intervals crossing zero for all three early-adopter groups (e.g. grade-6 reading −0.03, 95% CI −0.13, 0.07). The authors offer the hypothetical explanation that pervasive English-language content online may provide incidental practice. No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.
