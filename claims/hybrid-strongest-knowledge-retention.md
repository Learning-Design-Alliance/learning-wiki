> "CI decreased from 2.71 to 2.28 (84.13% retention; Hedges’ g = − 0.48, small–moderate decline), VR from 2.28 to 2.00 (87.72%; g = − 0.34, small decline), and CI + VR from 2.50 to 2.33 (93.20%; g = − 0.35, small decline), with CI + VR showing the smallest decline."
