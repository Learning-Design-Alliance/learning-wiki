### Niri 2026

Niri, G., Chiu, T. K. F., Ombid, A. M. O., Ybañez, D. L. J. B., Dennerlein, S. M., Zhou, X., & Lavicza, Z. (2026). STEAM education for AI literacy: a systematic literature review. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00629-8

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Disciplinary mapping of 31 unique subjects across the 39 studies, classified via ISCED-F 2013 and grouped into six STEAM clusters (Fig. 7). The review reports Computer Science and Data Science are "the most comprehensively covered, appearing across all five TIECD dimensions".

> "A cluster-level analysis reveals that the Technology domain is the most dominant across the TIECD frame - work (see Fig. 7). It shows particularly strong align - ment with the Technology dimension (AILE 1.1–1.3), with subjects like Computer Science (CS) and Data Sci - ence appearing frequently"
