Caballero-Julia 2024¶

Caballero-Julia, D., Martín-Lucas, J., & Andrade-Silva, L. E. (2024). Unpacking the relationship between screen use and educational outcomes in childhood: A systematic literature review. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105049

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Bautista et al. (2013) and Reid et al. (2023), that the digital native concept lacks empirical grounding; children handle devices easily but cannot distinguish benefits from pitfalls.

"some studies, such as those conducted by Bautista et al. (2013) and Reid et al. (2023) question the very concept of digital native because it lacks any empirical grounding and is overstated."
