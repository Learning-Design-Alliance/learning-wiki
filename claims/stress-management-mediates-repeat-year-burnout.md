## Evidence

### Molero Jurado María del Mar 2021

Molero Jurado María del Mar, Pérez-Fuentes María del Carmen, Martos Martínez África, Barragán Martín Ana Belén, Simón Márquez María del Mar, Gázquez Linares José Jesús. (2021). Emotional intelligence as a mediator in the relationship between academic performance and burnout in high school students. PLoS ONE 16(6): e0253552. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253552

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis with repeating a year as antecedent variable and the five EI factors as mediators (Table 5). Indirect paths Repeat → STR-M → EXH (0.060), → CYN (0.058) and → EFF (-0.024) were significant; direct effects of repeating were found only on cynicism and self-efficacy.

> "stress management is the emotional intelligence factor which could be mediating in the relationship between repeating a year and school burnout, and this path is significant for the three components (exhaustion, cynicism and self-efficacy)."
