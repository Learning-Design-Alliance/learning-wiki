Maria A. Woitow 2026¶

Maria A. Woitow, Anthony I. Jang, Ben Eppinger, Matthew R. Nassar, Marcel Brass & Julia M. Rodriguez Buritica. (2026). Shared effects of one's own and others' experiences during reinforcement learning on episodic memory. npj Science of Learning 11:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00409-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed-effects models on recognition data from 79 participants; the play-versus-pass choice effect on accuracy was significant (ß = 0.04, t = 2.26, p = 0.025) and "did not differ between learning conditions" (interaction ß = 0.01, p = 0.62); memory strength was likewise higher for play trials (ß = 0.07, t = 3.10, p = 0.002).
