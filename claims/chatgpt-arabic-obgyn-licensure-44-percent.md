## Evidence

### Cohen Adiel 2026

Cohen Adiel, Eliasi Elior, Meyer Raanan, Brezinov Yoav, Levin Gabriel. (2026). Performance of ChatGPT in Israeli Arabic-language OBGYN national medical licensure exam. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08753-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Performance study of a consecutive sample of text-based multiple-choice questions from authentic Arabic-language Israeli OBGYN licensure exams (2021 onward); a new ChatGPT-3.5 account answered each question in a separate session. "ChatGPT correctly answered 54 questions (43.9%, 95% CI: 35.1% – 52.7%)".

> "Overall, 123 authentic questions were analyzed. ChatGPT correctly answered 54 questions (43.9%, 95% CI: 35.1% – 52.7%) and reached a score below 50%."
