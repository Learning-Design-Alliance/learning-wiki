The playbook reports, citing Hill and Tyson (2009), a typical decline in family engagement across the elementary-to-middle transition. Attributed factors include children seeking more independence, schools providing fewer structured opportunities, and communication becoming less frequent or personalized.

> "Typically, there is a decline in family engagement from elementary to middle school, which can be attributed to several factors (Hill & Tyson, 2009)."
