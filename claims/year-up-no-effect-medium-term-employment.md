## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Based on one RCT meeting WWC standards, Year Up showed no discernible effects on medium-term employment three years after program completion. [→ medium-term-employment](#medium-term-employment)

## Evidence

### medium-term-employment

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021, November). Year Up. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/722.

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The WWC review of Roder & Elliott (2014), based on 143 students, rated medium-term employment as no discernible effects; the outcome average effect size was -0.02 with an improvement index of -1, not statistically significant. Employment three years after program completion was 82% versus 80% (p = 0.78) and average hours worked favored the comparison group (effect size -0.11, p = 0.55).

> "The WWC determined that one study that meets WWC group design standards without reservations (Roder & Elliott, 2014) showed evidence of an indeterminate effect of Year Up on medium-term employment."
