Evaluation of a peer-assisted, simulation-based clinical skills training program in Spain: a prospective single-group before-and-after study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2026.23.15

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis of the 425 paired pre–post attendances reported in the Results section; the article reports the gender contrast with "P=0.034" and Cohen's d values of 1.17 vs. 1.15.

> "Male students showed greater knowledge gain than female students (Δ=2.8 vs. 2.3; 95% CI, 2.17–3.43 vs. 1.91–2.69; Cohen’s d=1.17 vs. 1.15; P=0.034)"

### López-Brotons Maria 2026 (2)

Evaluation of a peer-assisted, simulation-based clinical skills training program in Spain: a prospective single-group before-and-after study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2026.23.15

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`
