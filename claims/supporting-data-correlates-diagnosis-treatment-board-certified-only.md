## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In board certified internists, supporting-data quality correlated significantly with leading diagnosis (r = 0.553) and treatment plan (r = 0.885) scores; no such correlation appeared in residents. [→ McBee Elexis 2017](#mcbee-elexis-2017)

## Evidence

### McBee Elexis 2017

McBee Elexis, Ratcliffe Temple, Picho Katherine, Schuwirth Lambert, Artino Anthony R. Jr., Yepes-Rios Ana Monica, Masel Jennifer, van der Vleuten Cees, Durning Steven J. (2017). Contextual factors and clinical reasoning: differences in diagnostic and therapeutic reasoning in board certified versus resident physicians. BMC Medical Education 17:211. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1041-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis by group on post-encounter form scores printed Spearman Rho correlations of r = 0.553 (p = .03) with leading diagnosis and r = 0.885 (p < 0.001) with treatment plan; the article states "quality of supporting data in the board certified internists significantly correlated to scores on leading diagnosis" and this pattern was absent in residents.
