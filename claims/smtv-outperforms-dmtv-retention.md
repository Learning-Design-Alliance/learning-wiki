`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Delayed/retention test administered with the same instrument to both groups to cover the interaction of time of measurement and treatment effects. t = 3.22, df = 46, p = .002; the retention null hypothesis was rejected.

> "The mean of the experimental group was found 61.00 and that of control group was 52.13. The standard deviations were 9.33 and 9.73 respectively."
