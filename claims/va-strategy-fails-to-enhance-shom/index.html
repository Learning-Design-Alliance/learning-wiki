Evidence¶

Çakiroğlu 2025¶

Çakiroğlu, Ü., Güven, O., Yaman, H., & Mazlum Güven, E. (2025). Scientific habits of arguing minds: enhancing online argumentation to foster SHOM. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10474-1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-sample t-test on SHOM Survey pre/post scores of 33 teacher candidates in a pre-experimental design; the article reports the decrease was "not statistically significant" with means 3.03 to 2.96. Equivalence was not tested.

"the SHOM scores of the teacher candidates were slightly decreased after the online argumentation implementation. The result of the t- test indicates that this decrease was not statistically significant (M = 3.03, SD = 0.21 for pre-test and M = 2.96, SD = 0.23 for post-test), t(31) = 3.21, p > 0.05."
