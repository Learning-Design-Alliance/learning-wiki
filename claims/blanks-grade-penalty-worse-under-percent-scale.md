### Cassandra Paul 2026

Cassandra Paul, David J. Webb, and Mary Kate Chessey. (2026). Missing Data on Physics Exams: Demographic Patterns, Course-Level Predictions, and Implications for Equity. [doi:10.1103/b4q5-dkn5](https://doi.org/10.1103/b4q5-dkn5)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical model fit (Eq. 1, Table III) to 107 classes and 27,008 students, controlling for FracAs (linear and quadratic), grade scale, and their interaction. The authors report that "under a percent-scale grading regime leaving answers blank resulted, on average, in over three times larger grade drop, about 0.3 grade points (for example from a B to a B-)."
