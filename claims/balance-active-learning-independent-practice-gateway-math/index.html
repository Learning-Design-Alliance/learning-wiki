Secondary qualitative finding from the gateway math study. The report states "students benefit from a balance of social, active learning and independent practice" and that productive struggle deepens mathematical reasoning.

"Across all environments, students benefit from a balance of social, active learning and independent practice. They also need to practice productive struggle, which is crucial for deepening their mathematical reasoning."
