## Evidence

### Jensen Jamie L. 2015

Jensen Jamie L., Kummer Tyler A., Godoy Patricia D. d. M. (2015). Improvements from a Flipped Classroom May Simply Be the Fruits of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0129

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two sections of a nonmajors biology course (n = 53 nonflipped, 55 flipped) taught by the same instructor with the same materials. ANOVA on all three unit exams found no significant differences; ANCOVA with LCTSR and BKA covariates confirmed this (p = 0.34, 0.09, 0.58).

> "An ANOVA was conducted, wherein each unit exam was compare between the nonflipped and flipped conditions. For all three unit exams, student scores were equivalent"
