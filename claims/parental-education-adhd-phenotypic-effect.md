## Evidence

### Fartein Ask Torvik 2020

Fartein Ask Torvik, Eilertsen Espen Moen, Tom A. McAdams, Kristin Gustavson, Henrik Daae Zachrisson, Ragnhild Brandlistuen, Line C. Gjerde, Alexandra Havdahl, Camilla Stoltenberg, Helga Ask, Eivind Ystrom. (2020). Mechanisms linking parental educational attainment with child ADHD, depression, and academic problems: a study of extended families in The Norwegian Mother, Father and Child Cohort Study. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.13197

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Biometric structural equation model fitting (extended children-of-twins model, OpenMx) on the MoBa extended-family sample; the best fitting model by AIC retained direct paths from both parents' education. The article reports the coefficients in T-points per level of education.

> "The best fitting model for child symptoms of ADHD included paths directly from maternal and paternal educational attainment (model 7). Higher maternal and paternal educational attainment was associated with fewer symptoms of ADHD (−0.65 and −0.42 T‐points per level of education)."
