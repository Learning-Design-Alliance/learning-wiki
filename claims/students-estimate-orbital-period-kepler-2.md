## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Reading the time between two consecutive transit minima on the MAST website, students obtained an orbital period of 2.201631±0.011335 days versus the literature value 2.204737±1.7·10−5 days. [→ Ollé 2022](#olle-2022)

## Evidence

### Ollé 2022

Ollé, H. and Kovács, T. (2022). What Hides within the Photograph: the Analysis of a Light Curve in the Classroom. Physics Education. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac68c3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac68c3)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Students adjusted the MAST view so two consecutive transits appeared and read the time between the minima; the estimate "2.201631 ± 0.011335 days" is close to the printed literature value of 2.204737±1.7·10−5 days.

> "read the time T between the two minima T1 and T2, which we ﬁnd 2.201631 ± 0.011335 days (Fig. 3). This is again a good estimate, as the literature value is 2.204737± 1.7· 10−5 days [12]."
