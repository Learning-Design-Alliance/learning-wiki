`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective interventional comparative study in an Indian teaching hospital: 30 undergraduates allocated by systematic sampling (15 per arm) to VD or SP teaching of spinal injury skills, then assessed on a 7-station OSCE. The SP group's mean of "18.27" exceeded the VD group's "15.47" (P=.004).

> "The mean overall OSCE score of the SP group (18.27) was notably higher than that of the VD group (15.47). This difference was statistically significant on unpaired 2-sided t test analysis (P=.004; Figure 2)."
