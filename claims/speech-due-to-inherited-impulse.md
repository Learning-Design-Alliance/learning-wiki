Theoretical argument from the article's section on the inherited system of factors; no data are reported. It holds that organic growth affords the basis for articulation, quoting that utterance 'may be said to be due to an inherited impulse'.

> "In the sense, therefore, that it is correlated with a particular phase of organic development which not only makes its appearance possible, but of which it is a spontaneous product, the articulate utterance of the child may be said to be due to an inherited impulse."
