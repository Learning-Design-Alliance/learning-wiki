## Evidence

### Daniel Dziob 2015

Daniel Dziob, Łukasz Kwiatkowski, Dagmara Sokołowska. (2015). Studying the impact of the class tournament as an assessment method on student achievements in physics courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.08663](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.08663)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Statistical analysis of one class tournament (n = 28 after two absentees excluded) comparing tournament (TNT) and former tests (FT) scores. Table II prints mean TNT 74.96 vs FT 48.39, TNT–FT mean 26.57; Table III prints t = 13.36, p = 1.02 × 10-13. The authors attribute the difference to "the team work and cooperation among the students".

> "the average TNT score is much higher as compared to FT, with the difference being statistically significant at any typical  level (i.e. {0.01, 0.05, 0.1}; see Tab. III)"
