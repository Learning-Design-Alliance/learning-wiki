The panel's synthesis of its literature search: of more than 490 citations, 64 used experimental, quasi-experimental, or single-subject designs, and "only six meet the causal validity standards of the What Works Clearinghouse". The panel reports it "cannot point to rigorous research that proves the practices do im- prove student achievement".

"given the status of the research, the panel does not have compel- ling evidence that these recommendations lead to improved student outcomes. As a result, all of the recommendations are sup- ported by low levels of evidence."
