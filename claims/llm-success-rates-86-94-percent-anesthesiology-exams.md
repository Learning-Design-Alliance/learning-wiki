## Evidence

### Stefan Andrei 2026

Stefan Andrei, Giet Thibault, Belouard Alexis, Stefan Mihai, Popescu Mihai, Tanaka Sébastien, Montravers Philippe, Gouel Aurélie. (2026). Performance and Hallucination Analysis of Large Language Models on European Anesthesiology Examinations: Cross-Sectional Comparative Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/95859

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Computational comparative study of 437 MCQs (1748 queries) from IADE, EDAIC, and OLA examinations, each submitted to 4 LLMs with item-level scoring. The article reports "Average success rates ranged from 86% (SD 18%) to 94% (SD 10%) across LLMs and examination types".

> "Average success rates ranged from 86% (SD 18%) to 94% (SD 10%) across LLMs and examination types, exceeding the EDAIC part I passing threshold, representing substantial improvement over previously reported GPT-3.5 performance."
